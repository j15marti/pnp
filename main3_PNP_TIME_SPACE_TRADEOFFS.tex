assumed. The complete user-directed source-to-output program is
\[
\text{source authentication}\to
\text{spanning forest and cycle basis}\to
\text{chosen exact evaluator}\to
\text{integer answer}\to\text{YES/NO}.

The prior checkpoint material below is historical work, not an

\paragraph{Named candidate and falsifiers.}
\textsf{C3-T5-FOREST-CYCLE-DUAL068} is a pair of uniform exact
algorithms selected after charged graph preprocessing. For every
finite digraph with $n$ explicitly named vertices, $m$ distinct
oriented arcs, $c$ weak components and cycle rank $r=m-n+c$, it
has two running-time options $2^{n-c}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ and

products, singleton components and easy templates are retained.

\paragraph{Source and baseline.}
For physical complexity we use a canonical binary source with
unary declarations of $n,m$ and fixed-width binary arc endpoints,
so $n,m\le N$. The implementation also accepts an ASCII
\texttt{n m} header followed by precisely $m$ endpoint pairs.
Malformed sources have an explicit rejection outcome; duplicate arcs
are harmless and removed, while loops and opposite arcs force NO.
A direct exhaustive counter examines $5^n$ assignments.
The fixed template is a loopless tournament with two directed
triangles $(0,1,2)$ and $(0,3,4)$. By the semicomplete-digraph
homomorphism dichotomy of Bang-Jensen, Hell and MacGillivray

\begin{theorem}[Binary forest and independent component shifts]
If $G$ has no self-loop and no pair of opposite arcs, then
\[
 \#\mathrm{Hom}(G,T_5)
 =5^c\sum_{\beta\in\{0,1\}^{n-c}}
 [\,\text{the forest assignment from }\beta
       \text{ satisfies all non-tree arcs}\,].
\]
One uniform blank-start deterministic locally headed implementation
computes this count and YES/NO in
$O((N+1)^6(1+2^{n-c}))$ bit time and
$O((N+1)^3)$ simultaneous writable bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a spanning forest of the undirected support. Anchor each
component root at colour zero. Each parent-child edge permits
exactly two child colours, obtained by adding $1$ or $3$ if the
source edge points toward the child and subtracting these increments
otherwise. Every choice of the $n-c$ binary decisions produces one
unique rooted forest labelling. Checking every remaining arc
establishes a bijection with rooted homomorphisms. Independently
adding any of five constants to every label of each component
preserves all constraints and changes each root. This action is
free, proving the factor $5^c$. The full locally headed machine
pays for reading and validating its source, an $O(n^2)$-bit
adjacency representation, forest discovery, each real recursive
branch and incident-edge check, binary counting, head movements,
and output. Its search tree has $O(n2^{n-c})$ nodes.
The conservative sixth-degree polynomial bounds those operations
on a fixed finite-tape implementation; all live objects fit the
stated polynomial space.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact cycle-space and polynomial-space Fourier counter]
Orient the $m$ arcs as given and let
$C\in\{-1,0,1\}^{r\times m}$ be the signed fundamental-cycle
matrix of a source-constructed spanning forest, interpreted modulo
five. Then
\[
 \#\mathrm{Hom}(G,T_5)
 =5^c\sum_{d\in\{1,3\}^m}[Cd=0\bmod 5].
\]
Writing $\zeta=\exp(2\pi i/5)$, the same integer equals
\[
 \frac{5^c}{5^r}\!
 \sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb Z_5^r}
 \prod_{e=1}^m
 \bigl(\zeta^{\langle\lambda,C_e\rangle}
       +\zeta^{3\langle\lambda,C_e\rangle}\bigr).
\]
An exact streaming realization has
$O((N+1)^8(1+5^r))$ time and
$O((N+1)^3)$ simultaneous writable bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every actual vertex assignment induces edge differences
$d_e=x_{v(e)}-x_{u(e)}$ and these telescope to zero on
fundamental cycles. Conversely, any $d\in\{1,3\}^m$ with
$Cd=0$ integrates uniquely on the forest after fixing component
roots to zero; the cycle equations verify every chord.
There are exactly $5^c$ original labellings per integrated $d$.

Character orthogonality over $\mathbb Z_5^r$ gives
$[Cd=0]=5^{-r}\sum_\lambda\zeta^{\langle\lambda,Cd\rangle}$.
Interchanging two finite sums produces the product formula.
Instead of storing or approximating complex numbers, evaluate it
using four signed integer coefficients in
$\mathbb Z[z]/(z^4+z^3+z^2+z+1)$, streaming one
$\lambda$ at a time. The final sum is a scalar divisible by $5^r$.
Each intermediate coordinate has $O(m+r)$ bits, because there are
at most $2^m$ terms per product and $5^r$ accumulated products;
fixed-degree reduction increases bit lengths by only $O(m)$.
Retain the source-derived $O(mr)$ cycle entries, one radix-five
address counter, four-coordinate intermediates, exact accumulator,
and the source/working heads. This proves the stated joint
polynomial-space ledger and polynomial-per-term time, with all
construction and failed arithmetic operations charged.
\end{proof}

uses polynomial physical time and space. A single deterministic

\[
 \boxed{T(N)=(N+1)^{O(1)}
       (1+\min\{2^{n-c},5^r\}),\qquad
       S_{\rm peak}(N)=(N+1)^{O(1)}.}
\]
The cycle-space table alternative stores $5^r$ exact integer counts,
taking $5^r\operatorname{poly}(N)$ space, and is \emph{not} silently
substituted for the streaming evaluator. Standard exact elimination

$5^{w+1}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time and
$5^w\operatorname{poly}(N)$ space.

\[
  \sum_{k=0}^a\sum_{\ell=0}^b
  {a\choose k}{b\choose\ell}
  [a-b+2(k-\ell)\equiv0\pmod5],

multiplied by $5$ for its root and by $2$ for every attached
tree vertex. In particular, a directed triangle has 15, a
transitive triangle has 5, and a directed five-cycle has 10

\section{New exact T5 reduction: degree-two cyclic weighted suppression (/069)}

\noindent\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT constructive upper bound;
independent mathematical review OPEN; finite tests COMPUTATIONAL.}
The named dependency \textsf{C3-T5-DEG2-SUPPRESSION069}
strengthens the source-authenticated /068 forest/cycle-rank solver.
Fix any explicit original directed graph $G$ on $n$ vertices, with
binary source length $N\ge n+m$. Reject malformed sources; a
self-loop or opposed arc pair yields exact count zero, while
duplicate copies of the same directed arc are redundant.
Define the undirected support 2-core $H_2$ by leaf peeling, and
let $h$ count its vertices with degree at least three.

\begin{theorem}[Five-coordinate degree-two suppression]
There is one uniform blank-start locally headed deterministic
algorithm that outputs the exact original-label count
$Z(G)=|\operatorname{Hom}(G,T_5)|$ and its zero/nonzero decision
with same-realization resource guarantees
\[
 T(N)=O((N+1)^8(1+5^h)),\qquad
 S_{\rm peak}(N)=O((N+1)^3)\ {\rm writable\ bits}.
\]
After the stated elimination the residual weighted support has
$k\le h$ vertices and $q$ nonempty components, and at most
$5^{k-q}$ anchored candidate assignments are tested.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent every real directed edge between $a<b$ by an integer
vector $W_{ab}(d)$, $d\in\mathbb Z_5$, where the target constraint
is $W_{ab}(x_b-x_a)$. For edge $a\to b$ it is the indicator of
$d\in\{1,3\}$ and for $b\to a$ it indicates
$-d\in\{1,3\}$. Every intermediate state maintains an
integer scalar $z$ and this \emph{exact} original count identity:
\[
 Z(G)=z\sum_{x\in\mathbb Z_5^{V_{\rm active}}}
               \prod_{\{a,b\}}W_{ab}(x_b-x_a).
\]
A degree-zero vertex multiplies $z$ by five.
For a degree-one vertex $v$ adjacent only to $u$,
sum over its colour, multiply $z$ by
$\sum_{d=0}^4W_{uv}(d)$, and remove that factor.
These sums are independent of $x_u$ by translation symmetry.
For a degree-two vertex $v$ with two distinct neighbours
$a<b$, orient its adjacent factors as $F(x_v-x_a)$ and
$J(x_b-x_v)$ and replace the two by the exact cyclic convolution
\[
 H(d)=\sum_{s=0}^{4}F(s)J(d-s\bmod5).
\]
If an edge factor already exists between $a,b$, combine it
with $H$ \emph{pointwise}, not by a second convolution.
The displayed identity follows by exchanging finite summations,
at each elimination, including all disconnected components.
Each remaining factor is translation-invariant.
The residual assignment sum fixes one colour to zero per
connected component and multiplies by $5^q$; the other
$k-q$ colours are exhaustively evaluated, retaining their
exact integer multiplicities.

Eliminate degree at most one until the ordinary 2-core remains;
then suppress degree at most two, always processing newly
created lower-degree vertices first. Deleting degree-two $v$
and adding an edge between its neighbours never increases either
neighbour's simple support degree. Hence a final vertex of
degree at least three had degree at least three in $H_2$:
$k\le h$. Every factor entry is a nonnegative count of some
eliminated original-variable completions with boundary colours
fixed, and is at most $5^n$. Thus all coefficients, scalars,
addresses and output integers have $O(n+1)$ bits.
At most $n(n-1)/2$ factors of five such integers, a source
buffer, an active-degree table, counters and final assignments
fit in $O((N+1)^3)$ simultaneous writable bits.
There are at most $n$ eliminations, each doing at most 25
products and polynomial sequential source/factor tape scans.
Each of at most $5^{k-q}$ final assignments scans only
polynomially many bounded-precision factors. Conservative
$O((N+1)^8)$ bit steps per iteration and for all preprocessing,
head travel, address arithmetic, multiplication, cache retention,
malformed-input handling and exact output suffice.
No implicit Oracle, shared free buffer or preloaded weighted
graph is assumed. The Python reference is not a native physical
microcode trace.
\end{proof}

\section{C3-T5-CORE-QUOTIENT: exact directed independent-twin kernel (/070)}

\noindent\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT restricted constructive counting theorem,

\paragraph{Chosen dependency and falsifiers.}
The new lemma closes a specific instance of
\textsf{C3-T5-CORE-QUOTIENT}: construct an exact source-derived
quotient for dense, nontrivial minimum-degree-three cores.
The intended counterchecks are (i) that in/out identical-neighborhood
classes might not be independent or might have nonuniform external arcs,
(ii) a forbidden complete $2$-by-$2$ biclique could exist in $T_5$,
(iii) counting a two-color support by $2^{m_i}-2$ could lose or
double-count original vertex assignments, and (iv) discovering supports,
enumerating states, integer precision or materialized source
might invalidate the same-machine ledger. All four are resolved in
the declared model; no arbitrary-solver lower bound is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[Exact directed independent-twin count]
Take every finite source-authenticated digraph $G=(V,E)$
with $n$ named vertices, and normalize repeated identical arcs.
A self-loop or a pair of opposite arcs has exact count zero.
For the other graphs, identify $u\sim v$ iff both their \emph{complete}
out-neighbor and in-neighbor sets are identical. Let $n_0$ be the
number of isolated original vertices, and let
$C_1,\ldots,C_q$ be the remaining equivalence classes,
with sizes $m_i=|C_i|$. Form the quotient oriented graph $Q$
in which $i\to j$ means that \emph{all} arcs $C_i\to C_j$
are present. Define
\[
{\cal S}=\{S\subseteq\mathbb Z_5:1\le|S|\le2\},
\qquad
a_i(S)=\begin{cases}
1,&|S|=1,\\
2^{m_i}-2,&|S|=2.
\end{cases}
\]
For every $G$, the exact homomorphism count is
\[
\boxed{
Z(G)=5^{n_0}
\sum_{(S_1,\ldots,S_q)\in{\cal S}^q}
\left(\prod_{i=1}^q a_i(S_i)\right)
\prod_{i\to j\in E(Q)}
   [\,S_i\times S_j\subseteq R\,].
}
\]
One uniform deterministic blank-start locally headed machine
constructs and evaluates this sum, correctly rejects malformed source
words, and computes both the count and its zero/nonzero answer
using the \emph{same complete execution} with
\[
 \boxed{
 T(N)=O((N+1)^8(1+15^q)),\qquad
 S_{\mathrm{peak}}(N)=O((N+1)^3)\ \text{writable bits},
 }
\]
for a canonical unary-count binary graph encoding of length
$N\ge n+m$. For $q=O(\log N)$ the time is polynomial.
No claim about a mandatory representation for arbitrary algorithms
is included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equality of both complete in/out neighborhood signatures is
an equivalence relation. Distinct equivalent vertices cannot
be adjacent: if $u\to v$, then $v\in N^+(u)$ but
$v\notin N^+(v)$ because the source has no loops.
Suppose $u\in C_i$ and $v\in C_j$ with $u\to v$.
All $u'\in C_i$ have the same outgoing neighborhood, hence
$u'\to v$, while all $v'\in C_j$ have the same incoming
neighborhood, hence $u'\to v'$. Thus every distinct
pair of classes has either no arcs or exactly one orientation of
all possible bipartite arcs.

Every color has precisely two outneighbors and two inneighbors in
$T_5$. If $C_i$ is incident with any quotient arc and
$S_i$ is its set of actually used colors in a satisfying original
assignment, then $S_i$ lies in the two-element
in- or out-neighbor set of some color at the opposite class.
Therefore $1\le |S_i|\le2$.
For two colors $a\ne a'$ the intersection
$N^+(a)\cap N^+(a')$ has at most one member:
$N^+(a)=\{a+1,a+3\}$ modulo five.
Consequently two adjacent quotient classes cannot
\emph{both} use two colors. This is a valid optional pruning
condition, not a needed premise of the formula.

A fixed nonempty support $S_i$ of size one admits exactly one
assignment of the $m_i$ labeled original vertices.
For a fixed support of size two there are $2^{m_i}$ maps
and exactly two fail to use both colors, leaving $2^{m_i}-2$.
All original assignments partition disjointly by their support
tuple. As each quotient arc is complete bipartite, all its
individual vertex arcs hold if and only if
$S_i\times S_j\subseteq R$. Isolated vertices contribute
five independent choices apiece. Multiplying the exact
onto-multiplicities and summing proves the identity.

To realize it physically, validate unary counts and every endpoint,
deduplicate arcs, reject loops/opposed arcs as zero and malformed
words as INVALID, build explicit in/out bit matrices, and compare
all pairs of signature vectors. Store exact class memberships,
the quotient graph, up to $q$ five-bit masks, at most $15q$
integer weights, physical addresses/counters and the output.
There are at most $15^q$ mask tuples and each checks at most
$q^2$ quotient arcs. Every nonnegative summand represents
a disjoint family of original assignments; consequently every
intermediate partial sum is at most $5^n$ and has
$O(n+1)$ bits. All retained buffers and work-head positions fit
$O((N+1)^3)$ writable bits. Conservatively charging locally
headed scans, membership tests, failed tuples, bit arithmetic
including exponentiation, multiplication, source acquisition,
authentication, discovery and final output gives
$O((N+1)^8)$ steps per tuple and for all preprocessing.
The same deterministic run meets both stated bounds.
\end{proof}

\section{Authenticated Circuit Value source}
For $n\geq1$, the binary source has unary $1^n0$, followed by $n$ gate
records. A record contains a two-bit opcode for constant zero, constant
one, AND or OR and two $n$-bit predecessor masks. Constants have zero
masks. For other gates each mask has exactly one bit set, naming a
strictly earlier gate. Repeated predecessors are allowed. The last gate
is the output. Thus the valid source length is
\[
N=2n^2+3n+1.
\]
Define $f(x)=0$ on malformed strings. A fixed total locally headed
multitape evaluator $A$ first validates the unary header and total length,
before allocating the VALUE prefix. It then rescans real source records,
reads predecessor values by a physical VALUE-tape scan, and writes each
new value. Induction over gate order proves both-label correctness.
Valid-source time is $O(nN\log(N+2)+N\log(N+2))$; a loose all-input
envelope is $O(N^2\log(N+2))$. Valid-source writable space is
$O(n+\log(N+2))$. Invalid length claims reject before large allocation.
Each individual tape head can be kept below $N+d$ for a sufficiently
large fixed constant $d$; separate fixed counter tapes hold addresses.
No input-dependent advice or graph is preloaded.

This grammar is P-complete under logspace reductions. Starting from
Circuit Value \cite{Ladner1975}, replace each gate with positive and
negative monotone rails. NOT swaps rails; AND and OR use their De Morgan
dual on the negative rail. Append an output-copy AND gate. A logspace
transducer prints the unary header and one-hot masks by counters and
rescans. The increase in length is polynomial, and the final bit is
unchanged. Membership follows by the actual evaluator $A$.

\section{A fully local public clock}
Choose an even fixed integer $C\geq2$ exceeding the cubic-time constant
of $A$, including the initial source-head return. Choose a fixed $d\geq8$
so that, for every $u\geq d$,
\[
2^u\geq(C+4)u^3.
\]
Such a $d$ is computable by finite search because $2^u/u^3$ increases
for $u\geq8$ and is unbounded. Put $b(N)=N+d$.

\begin{lemma}[Three bounded bouncing clocks]
There is a fixed finite-control, three-tape clock which, after an exact
$b$-step bootstrap on an $N$-bit source, runs for exactly $Cb^3$ steps.
Throughout, each clock head remains in cells $0,\ldots,b-1$.
At the end of the epoch all three heads are at cell $b-1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
During bootstrap, sweep the source and then $d$ blank positions. On each
clock tape write left and right endpoint symbols at cells $0$ and $b-1$.
The first $b-1$ steps move right; the final step stays while writing the
right endpoint. A fixed counter of $d$ states after the first source
blank locates this endpoint; no free length oracle is used.

An enabled clock head moves one cell in its current direction whenever
that move remains inside the endpoints. At an outward-facing endpoint,
it stays for one step, reverses its direction, and emits a pulse.
A half-sweep therefore takes exactly $b$ enabled steps. Enable the first
clock on every physical transition, the second only on first-clock
pulses, and the third only on second-clock pulses. The third clock emits
one pulse per $b^3$ transitions. Count $C$ such pulses in finite control.
Since $C$ is even, the numbers of half-sweeps on the three tapes are
$Cb^2$, $Cb$, and $C$, respectively, all even. Every head returns to its
initial right endpoint. Endpoint symbols and direction bits suffice for
each local transition. All initialization, moves, and endpoint holds are
paid transitions.
\end{proof}

\section{Complete block-respecting realization}
Construct one fixed machine $M_*$ with $A$'s tapes, the three clock tapes,
and a fresh MARK tape. All work tapes start blank and all heads at zero.
The source tape is never modified. The only nonblank initial tape is the
original binary input.

\paragraph{Bootstrap.}
Use the exact $b$-step procedure above. The original source head ends at
$b-1$, still inside its first tape block. MARK remains at zero. The
VALUE and counter tapes remain blank.

\paragraph{Synchronous evaluation.}
For the next $Cb^3$ global transitions run $A$ and the clock as a finite
multitape product. This includes $A$ returning to the start of the source,
parsing, validation, rereads, gate evaluation, and storing its Boolean
answer. After $A$ finishes, freeze its tape contents and head locations
while retaining $f(x)$ in control. The chosen constant $C$ guarantees
completion before the clock expires. This is one machine, not a network:
each product step reads and updates a fixed number of local heads.

\paragraph{Alignment and marker.}
At the end of the epoch all clock heads are at the right endpoint.
Freeze $A$ and clocks 2 and 3. Allow clock 1 one $b$-step half-sweep left.
MARK stays at zero. On the next rightward half-sweep, move MARK right on
each of the first $b-1$ steps. At the last endpoint-hold step, move MARK
right exactly if $f(x)=1$. Hence its final position is $b-1+f(x)$.
One additional paid read/output transition then halts.

The only possible tape-block crossing is the last MARK move, at absolute
time $Cb^3+3b$, an integer multiple of $b$. Every other head always stays
inside its first $b$-cell block. Thus authentication and initialization,
not merely the final suffix, obey the block-respecting condition.

\begin{theorem}[P-complete authentic first-visit query at legal width]
For the fixed machine $M_*$, let
\[
t(N)=Cb(N)^3+3b(N)+1,\qquad j(N)=Cb(N)^2+4.
\]
Then $b(N)\geq\lceil\log_2t(N)\rceil$, $b(N)=\Theta(t(N)^{1/3})$,
and the predicate
\[
E(x)=\bigl[((\mathrm{MARK},0,2),(1,j(|x|)))
           \in E(G_{M_*,x})\bigr]
\]
is P-complete under logspace reductions. It is exactly $f(x)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The partitioned elapsed time is
\[
b+Cb^3+b+b+1=t(N).
\]
The dummy read begins time block $j(N)$, since
$(j(N)-1)b+1=t(N)$. Before that read, MARK has only read cells in
block 1. At the read it is in block 2 if and only if $f(x)=1$.
Williams's first-visit edge definition therefore gives exactly $E(x)=f(x)$.
The specified source node denotes the initially blank second MARK block;
chronological edges are distinct from that source edge.

Since $t\leq(C+4)b^3\leq2^b$, the required logarithmic condition holds.
The cubic relation yields $b=\Theta(t^{1/3})$.
Both $b(N)$ and the binary index $j(N)$ can be calculated in $O(\log N)$
workspace with charged input-length acquisition and binary arithmetic.
Combining these with the logspace Circuit Value compiler proves hardness.
Actual simulation, or the original evaluator $A$, proves membership in P.
\end{proof}

\section{Graph guessing, FAIL checking, and the missing simultaneous time bound (TMR-3/061)}
\paragraph{Exact correction to the source-indexer discussion.}
Williams's Section~3.2 already bypasses authentic source-only graph
indexing: enumerate every candidate movement word
$g\in\{-1,0,+1\}^{pB}$, store its $O(pB)$-bit encoding, derive the
corresponding graph in logspace \emph{relative to that guess}, and
evaluate a source-authenticated implicit tree. Wrong head movements
return FAIL at the root; the correct guess returns the true decision
bit \cite{Williams2025}. Thus /059--/060's P-complete authentic
edge is not a counterexample to that published space simulation.
The unresolved cost is selecting the authentic graph in sufficiently
small \emph{simultaneous} time and workspace.

\paragraph{One complete cheap machine and exact enumeration workload.}
Fix a total deterministic blank-start local two-head machine $M_R$.
It starts with a binary source $x$ of length $N$ and a blank work tape.
For each real data bit it moves both heads right and ANDs that bit into
one finite-control register. At the first endmarker it stays, emits
$\bigwedge_{i=1}^{N}x_i$, and halts. A legal source-aware alternative
uses one streaming pass and $O(1)$ writable bits. Its authentic physical
execution takes $t=N+1$ transitions; the original $M_R$ has
$\Theta(N)$ visited work cells.

For any $b,B$ with $t=bB$ and $b\ge\lceil\log_2t\rceil$,
its block-boundary movement labels, in time-major order on two heads,
are $((+1,+1))^{B-1},(0,0)$. All positive crossings occur
at honest multiples of $b$, for both YES and NO inputs.
Take the specific lexicographic enumeration of all
$\{-1,0,+1\}^{2B}$ guesses in the order $-1<0<+1$, and
apply the sound FAIL-or-decision contract to every guess.
The authentic word's zero-based ternary rank is
$3^{2B}-5$, so the total number of candidate evaluations is exactly
\[
\boxed{Q_{\mathrm{guess}}(B)=3^{2B}-4.}
\]
Every earlier word has at least one incorrect movement label
and therefore returns FAIL. This counts distinct \emph{guess attempts}
even with hypothetical unit-cost evaluation. It is a lower bound
only for the stipulated dense, exhaustive, lexicographic algorithm,
not for arbitrary machine-aware orders or compressed representations.

At $t=m^3$, $b=m$ and $B=m^2$, for all sufficiently large $m$,
the block width is admissible, yet
\[
Q_{\mathrm{guess}}=3^{2m^2}-4
=2^{\Theta(t^{2/3})}.
\]
With near-minimum legal $b=\Theta(\log t)$ the same architecture has
$2^{\Theta(t/\log t)}$ attempts on a suitable cofinal input-length
subsequence. The trivial direct AND decider demonstrates why neither
cost is intrinsic decision hardness.

\paragraph{Two independent full-template resource gates.}
For $p$ constant tapes and $B=\lceil t/b\rceil$, let $Q_E$ be the
actual number of implicit-tree requests \emph{per candidate} and $A_E$
the maximum paid time per request, including original-source seeks,
graph reconstruction, precision and output. Let $I_E$ be remaining
per-candidate work and $S_E$ the evaluator's peak live workspace
excluding a retained candidate and necessary block buffers.
A sequential implementation that literally enumerates the dense words
has the conservative conditional bounds
\[
T_{\rm total}\le T_{\rm parse}
+O\!\left(3^{pB}(I_E+Q_E A_E+pB)\right),
\]
\[
S_{\rm peak}=O(pB+b+S_E+\log(t+N+2)).
\]
These bounds describe one implementation with its own charged costs;
$Q_E$ and $A_E$ are \emph{not} assumed polynomial.

The full movement-word representation by itself consumes
$\Theta(B)$ retained bits. Consequently, substituting an improved
height-sensitive evaluator of space $O(b+B^{1/2})$ into the
\emph{unchanged} full-word search does not yield
$O(t^{1/3})$ space: with $b=t^{1/3}$, its retained candidate
uses $\Theta(t^{2/3})$ bits. The unmodified dense-template
tradeoff is constrained by $b+t/b\ge2\sqrt{t}$.
These are \emph{representation-specific} costs, not a lower bound for
every possible compact graph description.

Moreover, for the specified exhaustive lexicographic search the
$Q_{\rm guess}$ candidate tests alone preclude polynomial simultaneous
