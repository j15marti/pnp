\section{Candidate and falsifiers}
The candidate replaces the width-one marker by a publicly aligned
terminal sweep in a fully charged block-respecting realization.
The four falsifiers are: an equivalent converter may erase a named head;
the query epoch may depend on the answer; the clock or original evaluator
may hide source accesses or retained space; and a claim for one realization
may be incorrectly strengthened to every converter or every admissible
block size. The construction and conclusion below explicitly address
these distinctions. No prescribed expensive DAG is made compulsory.

\section{Authenticated Circuit Value source}
For $n\geq1$, the binary source has unary $1^n0$, followed by $n$ gate
records. A record contains a two-bit opcode for constant zero, constant
one, AND or OR and two $n$-bit predecessor masks. Constants have zero
masks. For other gates each mask has exactly one bit set, naming a
strictly earlier gate. Repeated predecessors are allowed. The last gate
is the output. Thus the valid source length is
\[
N=2n^2+3n+1.
\]
Define $f(x)=0$ on malformed strings. A fixed total locally headed
multitape evaluator $A$ first validates the unary header and total length,
before allocating the VALUE prefix. It then rescans real source records,
reads predecessor values by a physical VALUE-tape scan, and writes each
new value. Induction over gate order proves both-label correctness.
Valid-source time is $O(nN\log(N+2)+N\log(N+2))$; a loose all-input
envelope is $O(N^2\log(N+2))$. Valid-source writable space is
$O(n+\log(N+2))$. Invalid length claims reject before large allocation.
Each individual tape head can be kept below $N+d$ for a sufficiently
large fixed constant $d$; separate fixed counter tapes hold addresses.
No input-dependent advice or graph is preloaded.

This grammar is P-complete under logspace reductions. Starting from
Circuit Value \cite{Ladner1975}, replace each gate with positive and
negative monotone rails. NOT swaps rails; AND and OR use their De Morgan
dual on the negative rail. Append an output-copy AND gate. A logspace
transducer prints the unary header and one-hot masks by counters and
rescans. The increase in length is polynomial, and the final bit is
unchanged. Membership follows by the actual evaluator $A$.

\section{A fully local public clock}
Choose an even fixed integer $C\geq2$ exceeding the cubic-time constant
of $A$, including the initial source-head return. Choose a fixed $d\geq8$
so that, for every $u\geq d$,
\[
2^u\geq(C+4)u^3.
\]
Such a $d$ is computable by finite search because $2^u/u^3$ increases
for $u\geq8$ and is unbounded. Put $b(N)=N+d$.

\begin{lemma}[Three bounded bouncing clocks]
There is a fixed finite-control, three-tape clock which, after an exact
$b$-step bootstrap on an $N$-bit source, runs for exactly $Cb^3$ steps.
Throughout, each clock head remains in cells $0,\ldots,b-1$.
At the end of the epoch all three heads are at cell $b-1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
During bootstrap, sweep the source and then $d$ blank positions. On each
clock tape write left and right endpoint symbols at cells $0$ and $b-1$.
The first $b-1$ steps move right; the final step stays while writing the
right endpoint. A fixed counter of $d$ states after the first source
blank locates this endpoint; no free length oracle is used.

An enabled clock head moves one cell in its current direction whenever
that move remains inside the endpoints. At an outward-facing endpoint,
it stays for one step, reverses its direction, and emits a pulse.
A half-sweep therefore takes exactly $b$ enabled steps. Enable the first
clock on every physical transition, the second only on first-clock
pulses, and the third only on second-clock pulses. The third clock emits
one pulse per $b^3$ transitions. Count $C$ such pulses in finite control.
Since $C$ is even, the numbers of half-sweeps on the three tapes are
$Cb^2$, $Cb$, and $C$, respectively, all even. Every head returns to its
initial right endpoint. Endpoint symbols and direction bits suffice for
each local transition. All initialization, moves, and endpoint holds are
paid transitions.
\end{proof}

\section{Complete block-respecting realization}
Construct one fixed machine $M_*$ with $A$'s tapes, the three clock tapes,
and a fresh MARK tape. All work tapes start blank and all heads at zero.
The source tape is never modified. The only nonblank initial tape is the
original binary input.

\paragraph{Bootstrap.}
Use the exact $b$-step procedure above. The original source head ends at
$b-1$, still inside its first tape block. MARK remains at zero. The
VALUE and counter tapes remain blank.

\paragraph{Synchronous evaluation.}
For the next $Cb^3$ global transitions run $A$ and the clock as a finite
multitape product. This includes $A$ returning to the start of the source,
parsing, validation, rereads, gate evaluation, and storing its Boolean
answer. After $A$ finishes, freeze its tape contents and head locations
while retaining $f(x)$ in control. The chosen constant $C$ guarantees
completion before the clock expires. This is one machine, not a network:
each product step reads and updates a fixed number of local heads.

\paragraph{Alignment and marker.}
At the end of the epoch all clock heads are at the right endpoint.
Freeze $A$ and clocks 2 and 3. Allow clock 1 one $b$-step half-sweep left.
MARK stays at zero. On the next rightward half-sweep, move MARK right on
each of the first $b-1$ steps. At the last endpoint-hold step, move MARK
right exactly if $f(x)=1$. Hence its final position is $b-1+f(x)$.
One additional paid read/output transition then halts.

The only possible tape-block crossing is the last MARK move, at absolute
time $Cb^3+3b$, an integer multiple of $b$. Every other head always stays
inside its first $b$-cell block. Thus authentication and initialization,
not merely the final suffix, obey the block-respecting condition.

\begin{theorem}[P-complete authentic first-visit query at legal width]
For the fixed machine $M_*$, let
\[
t(N)=Cb(N)^3+3b(N)+1,\qquad j(N)=Cb(N)^2+4.
\]
Then $b(N)\geq\lceil\log_2t(N)\rceil$, $b(N)=\Theta(t(N)^{1/3})$,
and the predicate
\[
E(x)=\bigl[((\mathrm{MARK},0,2),(1,j(|x|)))
           \in E(G_{M_*,x})\bigr]
\]
is P-complete under logspace reductions. It is exactly $f(x)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The partitioned elapsed time is
\[
b+Cb^3+b+b+1=t(N).
\]
The dummy read begins time block $j(N)$, since
$(j(N)-1)b+1=t(N)$. Before that read, MARK has only read cells in
block 1. At the read it is in block 2 if and only if $f(x)=1$.
Williams's first-visit edge definition therefore gives exactly $E(x)=f(x)$.
The specified source node denotes the initially blank second MARK block;
chronological edges are distinct from that source edge.

Since $t\leq(C+4)b^3\leq2^b$, the required logarithmic condition holds.
The cubic relation yields $b=\Theta(t^{1/3})$.
Both $b(N)$ and the binary index $j(N)$ can be calculated in $O(\log N)$
workspace with charged input-length acquisition and binary arithmetic.
Combining these with the logspace Circuit Value compiler proves hardness.
Actual simulation, or the original evaluator $A$, proves membership in P.
\end{proof}

\section{Same-execution resource ledger and meaning}
The three clocks use $3b$ visited writable cells; MARK uses $b+f(x)$.
All frozen VALUE and counter cells remain charged. Therefore
\[
S_{M_*}=4b+f(x)+O(n+\log(N+2))=\Theta(N),\qquad
T_{M_*}=Cb^3+3b+1=\Theta(N^3).
\]
The timing partition is disjoint. The steps of $A$ overlap with the
clock epoch and are not added a second time as elapsed time. Initialization,
all original-source accesses, output, and the final read are included.
A direct graph-index algorithm may simply evaluate $A$; its genuine
polynomial-time shortcut remains legal. Its separate space bound must
not be substituted for the original clocked machine's storage.

If a uniform source-only graph provider answers this single specified
query in polynomial time and $O(\log N)$ workspace on every source, then
Circuit Value is in L, so $\mathrm L=\mathrm P$. That is a conditional
consequence, not an unconditional lower bound. It neither precludes
$T^{1/3}$ space nor improves generic Tree Evaluation. The deliberate
clock padding is a normal-form device, not hardness amplification.

\section{Conversion scope and an exact failed transport}
The new machine is already block-respecting. Identity normalization,
with an idle extra tape if desired, is a valid witness to Williams's
Lemma 2.1 for it. A normalizer can special-case this one fixed program
and use HPV for all others. This establishes existence in the eligible
target class, not trace invariance for every equivalent compiler.

Mere coarsening of /059 does not work: for $b\geq2$, positions 0 and 1
are in the same tape block. Nor does semantic equivalence preserve a
marked edge: an output-equivalent compiler may discard a marker tape
which is not used to compute acceptance. There is an even sharper matched control. Define $M_0$ by changing only
that last conditional MARK move to a stay, retaining the circuit answer
and the same clocks, bootstrap, alignment, sweep and output. It has
identical $t(N)$ and $b(N)$, the same Boolean language, and at most one
fewer visited cell. Every tape now reads only its first block, so its
block graph is length-only and the specified first-visit bit is always
zero. Thus an output-equivalent, equally timed block-respecting
normalization can erase the hard graph bit without solving a new
problem. Computational information remains in node values, not the
graph positions. This symbolic control is distinct from the executed
clock checks.

The present proof does not
certify the literal folded-copy image of the old /059 program. It also
does not quantify over every admissible $b(N)$, especially logarithmic
$b$ with input distributed over many blocks. Those stronger statements
remain OPEN. The exact result already suffices to reject a free
logarithmic-space authentic-index assumption over the entire eligible
class unless the corresponding $\mathrm L=\mathrm P$ consequence is faced.
