\section*{Setup}
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$, with $R$ a fixed sound rule set whose derivations are checkable in time polynomial in $|\pi|$ (Cook--Reckhow). Strategy discovery is free.

\section*{Results}
\begin{thm}[T1, KNOWN] $\overline W_R$ polynomial for a Cook--Reckhow $R$ implies $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. Free choice of the minimizing derivation is exactly nondeterminism, so the measure is a coNP-side quantity.\end{thm}
\begin{thm}[T2, PROVED/EXACT] Let $B(R)$ be $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. If $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial, then $B(R)\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.\end{thm}
\emph{Proof.} The conclusion of $B(R)$ is false, so $B(R)$ is equivalent to the negation of its hypothesis. $\square$

Consequence: for Resolution, Cutting Planes, bounded-depth Frege (known exponential bounds), proving $B(R)$ is proving $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$. The bridge carries content only for $R$ with no known lower bound (Frege, Extended Frege).

\begin{thm}[T3, KNOWN, Cook/Krajicek] If some polynomial-time algorithm $A$ decides SAT and $\mathrm{PV}\vdash$ ``$A$ is correct'', then EF has polynomial-size refutations of every unsatisfiable $F$.\end{thm}
\emph{Sketch.} The translation of the PV proof gives poly-size EF proofs of the reflection tautologies; the run of $A$ on the fixed $F$ is a polynomial-size circuit evaluation. Combine. The gap is \emph{provability}: truth of $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$ does not give EF-provability.

\begin{thm}[T4, PROVED/EXACT, red team] The standard hard families fail against a strong fixed $R$: Tseitin and pigeonhole formulas have polynomial-size Frege/EF proofs, so they cannot witness $\overline W_{EF}$. Candidate families remaining: random $k$-CNF near threshold, and cryptographic-flavoured formulas (factoring-based), both open for Frege.\end{thm}
\begin{thm}[T5, KNOWN barrier] Feasible interpolation, the source of unconditional bounds for Resolution and CP, fails for EF under factoring hardness (Krajicek--Pudlak; Bonet--Pitassi--Raz). Any lower-bound technique for strong $R$ must avoid it.\end{thm}

\section*{Time--space variant}
With cost $=(\text{work},\text{space})$, a polynomial-time SAT algorithm gives a derivation of polynomial work and polynomial peak space (space$\le$time), subject to the same provability gap as T3. Hence the time--space bridge adds no new strength; unconditional time--space tradeoffs exist for Resolution (Ben-Sasson--Nordstr\"om; Beame--Beck--Impagliazzo) but are covered by T2.

\section*{Weakest sufficient bridge (assessment)}
T2 shows the tautological bridge is vacuous. The least vacuous candidates: (i) provable correctness of a SAT algorithm (T3); (ii) existence of an optimal proof system (open; follows from $\mathrm{NE}=\mathrm{coNE}$); (iii) advice-based optimal systems (Cook--Krajicek, CANDIDATE, statement not re-verified here). Each is itself open.
