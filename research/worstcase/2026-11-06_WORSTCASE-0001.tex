\title{Worst-case derivation cost: bridge analysis}\author{WORSTCASE-0001}\date{2026-11-06}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} Define $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$ and $\mathrm{Bridge}_R:\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\begin{prop}[Bridge collapse; PROVED/EXACT] If $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial, then $\mathrm{Bridge}_R\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$.\end{prop}
\begin{proof} The conclusion of the implication is false, so the implication holds iff its hypothesis is false.\end{proof}
Consequently the bridge matters only for systems with no known lower bound, and there it carries the full difficulty of a simulation theorem.

\paragraph{System $R_T$.} Fix a sound theory $T$ (e.g.\ PV, $S^1_2$, ZFC). A derivation of $\bot$ from $F$ is a triple $(e,p,t)$: $e$ a machine, $p$ a $T$-proof of $\mathrm{Snd}(e):\forall\varphi\,(M_e(\varphi)=\text{``UNSAT''}\Rightarrow\varphi\in\mathrm{UNSAT})$, and $t$ an accepting trace of $M_e$ on $F$ answering ``UNSAT''. Cost $=|p|+|t|$ (size), peak space $=\max(|p|,\text{space}(t))$ if desired. Soundness of $T$ makes $R_T$ a sound proof system (checking is polynomial).

\begin{thm}[PROVED/EXACT, hypothesis $H_T$] If some polynomial-time $e^*$ decides SAT and $T\vdash\mathrm{Snd}(e^*)$, then $\overline W_{R_T}(N)\le |p^*|+O(N^c)$, where $|p^*|$ is a constant independent of $N$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} For any UNSAT $F$ take $(e^*,p^*,t)$ with $t$ the run of $e^*$ on $F$, length $O(N^c)$.\end{proof}
Only soundness of the UNSAT answers is required; completeness is irrelevant.

\begin{thm}[PROVED/EXACT] If $\overline W_{R_T}$ is superpolynomial, then no polynomial-time machine that refutes UNSAT instances has a $T$-provable soundness statement. In particular $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$ follows only together with $H_T$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} Contrapositive of the previous theorem applied to any such machine (it need not decide SAT: a polytime sound refuter that is correct on all UNSAT inputs suffices).\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reading.} The quantifier order is the obstruction: $R$ is fixed before the algorithm $e^*$. A fixed $R$ with $\mathrm{Bridge}_R$ unconditional must simulate every polynomial-time SAT algorithm without a provability hypothesis; $R_T$ trades this for $H_T$. Giving the optimal derivation for free (no search charge) is consistent with all of the above since $\overline W$ is a pure minimum.

\paragraph{Literature (secondary sources, not re-verified against primaries).} Cook--Reckhow: polynomially bounded proof system $\iff$ NP$=$coNP; automatizability of all propositional systems $\iff$ P$=$NP; no Frege/EF lower bounds known; Alekseev (ToC 22, 2026) lower bound for PC with extension rule on the binary value principle; Krajíček--Pudlák: feasible interpolation fails for Frege/EF under cryptographic assumptions.
