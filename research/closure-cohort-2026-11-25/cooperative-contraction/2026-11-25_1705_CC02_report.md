## 2. Exact signed incidence-matching projection (new integration)
Let F be a finite list of signed NAE owners, each on >=2 distinct Boolean variables, of arbitrary explicitly listed arity, literals x_v+s_iv. Private eligible variable set X, protected exterior Y. Suppose a matching in the owner-variable incidence graph assigns every owner i a distinct pivot a_i in X. Put one directed arc a_i -> v for each other variable v in that owner, carrying b_iv=1+s_i,a_i+s_iv in F2. The owner is satisfied exactly when at least one outgoing equation x_a_i+x_v=b_iv holds: any nonconstant literal vector differs from its pivot at some other literal. Every unmatched variable (including all exterior) has no outgoing arcs.

Theorem A. The exact projection to ALL nonpivot variables is either empty or the entire cube. It is entire iff every sink SCC consisting of pivots has a simple directed circuit with XOR of arc demands zero.

Necessity: from a model select one true outgoing equation per pivot. Inside a closed all-pivot SCC, these functional edges necessarily contain a simple directed circuit. Summing its equations forces zero demand parity. A sink containing a nonpivot is a singleton and has no such obligation.

Sufficiency: choose one balanced circuit in each closed all-pivot SCC. Each has a consistent assignment (arbitrary starting bit). Use its vertices and all unmatched variables as roots. Every vertex reaches a root, because every finite condensation DAG reaches a sink and each closed SCC is strongly connected. From any nonroot choose an outgoing arc decreasing distance to roots. Assign arbitrary given nonpivot values unchanged; assign each circuit and then propagate x_v=x_w+b_vw along the chosen arcs in increasing distance. Every pivot has one selected satisfied equation (possibly additional true alternatives), so every owner is satisfied. No product of root assignments and no guard violation. This witness plan works for every boundary value. An external guard on nonpivot variables is therefore preserved pointwise. A guard coupling eliminated pivots is not admitted without recompilation/recertification.

Deterministic polynomial backend: replace an arc of demand 0 by a fresh length-two directed path; leave demand-1 arcs length one. Every simple directed circuit has even length in expanded graph iff its original demand sum is zero. Robertson, Seymour, Thomas, *Permanents, Pfaffian orientations, and even directed circuits*, Annals 150 (1999) 929–975, Section 9 and discussion pp.931,964–965, arXiv:math/9911268v1, establish deterministic polynomial even-directed-simple-circuit decision on finite digraphs. To acquire actual circuit, greedily delete expanded edges while maintaining YES using at most O(E) additional decision calls; the minimal retained edges form a single even simple circuit. Project through subdivisions. This is not shortest-even-cycle randomized PIT, not parity reachability allowing repeated vertices, and not a free SAT oracle. Standard matching, SCC and graph traversal finish the construction. Backend theorem imported, not implemented in the audit.

For an arbitrary signed connected graphic owner packet, its forbidden equations are edge differences on a connected support. Gaussian/graph consistency check drops inconsistent forbidden packet (always satisfied); otherwise choose a potential on the connected support so the forbidden system is exactly all shifted literals equal. This supplies the signed NAE representation. Rank-one connected packet is a two-literal NAE, or ordinary XOR. Do not apply to arbitrary affine planes, disjoint paired-edge owners, or after arbitrary guard transformations unless actual connected graphical representation is certified.

Corollary: any unguarded list of such connected graphic packets with max affine rank deficiency <=1 is polynomially decidable. For every nonempty owner subset S, r(S)=v(S)-c(S)<=v(S)-1, so |S|<=r(S)+1<=v(S); incidence Hall supplies the pivots. This includes strict deficiency-one signed NAE of arbitrary arities and each connected component of a signed three-perfect-partition NAE3 instance (or apply incidence Hall directly by counting its degree-three incidences). The latter also follows from literature on degree-bounded NAE and is not a novel general NAE-k-SAT-k complexity classification. CC01's candidate t* for a connected strict graphic core can now be decided by this terminal using its actual signed offsets. For closed matched SCCs the RST even-digraph/Pfaffian criterion also decides universal sign coverage, since arc bits out of each pivot can be prescribed independently by literal signs within its unique owner. This last criterion concerns ALL possible owner signs, not the same thing as detecting one balanced circuit for the supplied signs.

## 3. Exact incidence-deficiency core extension (new integration with CC01/AB06)
Define d_I(S)=|S|-|N_X(S)| and delta_I=max_S d_I(S), including empty. Supermodularity gives unique least maximizing C. Hall with dummy vertices gives maximum matching size m-delta_I, so obtain C by at most m+1 ordinary matching computations: remove an owner if remaining family retains maximum defect. Monotone deletion test suffices, since deleting more owners cannot increase maximal defect. For any T outside C, maximality yields |N_X(T) minus N_X(C)|>=|T|. Thus match all outside owners to distinct eligible variables not appearing in C. Run theorem A on that outside region. If its projection empty, whole input UNSAT; if full, delete outside owners and their pivots, keep core and all exterior guards on nonpivots unchanged. EVERY actual core solution lifts via the same plan, not merely some compatible solution. This generalizes Hall rank peeling for this model by permitting matched cyclic regions even if private affine rank is too small for one independent row per owner. C empty yields exact whole-instance decision, with no SAT calls. A supplied polynomial-length sequence of admitted nontrivial owner-deleting steps charges each plan once and reconstructs in reverse. No theorem that arbitrary high-deficiency sources reach such steps.

## 4. Exact full-core obstruction on the genuine source (new strengthening)
Let H be a connected k-perfect-partition 3-uniform hypergraph, k>=3 (linearity not needed in this paragraph). For proper owner subset S let components have v_j vertices and e_j owners, let v=sum v_j, c=#components. Rank of all pair difference rows for S equals v-c. Define incidence exposure b_j=k v_j-3e_j. Because each partition has disjoint triples, each partition contributes at least v_j mod 3 to b_j. If v_j divisible by 3, b_j is a multiple of 3; it is positive because zero exposure means an entire disconnected component of H (or full H with no owner omitted), impossible for proper S. Otherwise b_j>=k>=3. Thus b=sum b_j>=3c. For empty S use b=c=v=0.

Full rank defect d_full=m-n+1=(k-3)n/3+1. For proper S,
d_full-d(S)=(k-3)(n-v)/3+b/3+1-c >=1.
Hence the unique maximum affine rank-deficiency core is the ENTIRE connected source. For k=4 this gives exactly n/3+1, not just a lower bound. For k=3 it gives precisely strict deficiency one and theorem A decides all signings in polynomial time, independently of the two-type candidate theorem.

Under ANY linear observation restriction W while retaining the same labeled owner set, quotient rank r_W(S)=r(S)-dim(U_S intersect W), where U_S is the original dual span. Since U_S subset U_full, the gap d_W(full)-d_W(S) can only increase. Therefore merely imposing guards cannot expose a smaller maximum-defect core while all owners remain. Removing tautologies, forced owner replacement, nonlinear contraction, re-encoding or semantic deductions can bypass this result and are not ruled out. The raw incidence core is also full: d_I(full)-d_I(S)=[(k-3)(n-v)+b]/3>0 for k>3 and proper S; restricting eligible variables only increases this gap. Thus theorem A/core extension is complementary, not immediately a universal raw-source solver.

Local coordinate private regions: if p private vertices have all k=4 of their incident owners included in a region of e owners, 3e>=4p. Its private rank <=p, so max deficiency >=e-p>=p/3. If deficit<=D, necessarily p<=3D and e<=4D. Hence CC01's constant-deficit local coordinate projection cannot directly absorb an arbitrarily large raw region before semantic rewiring.

## 5. Exponential explicit chart-cover lower bound (new)
Let a least core have m rank-two output blocks, total private rank r=m-Delta, and max independent row representative count r by Rado. Let W=F2^(2m), P=im L, Q=W/P of dimension q=2m-r. Define T={H y: every two-bit block of y nonzero} in Q, H quotient. This is EXACT attainable syndrome support over ALL signings. Select r independent coordinate row representatives from distinct owners by ordinary MI. For uniformly random s in W, solve these r equations on private variables x to make their selected bit of Lx+s equal 1, choosing a fixed solution rule depending only on offsets of selected owners. Thus r owners are satisfied, and remaining Delta owners have independent uniform two-bit shifts conditional on the chosen x; each avoids zero with probability 3/4. Therefore
|T| / 2^q >= (3/4)^Delta.
For any one-covector-per-owner affine chart of CC01, Lambda is surjective on W, and its image consistency condition has codimension m-rank(Lambda L)>=Delta in Q. Every such chart contains <=2^(q-Delta) points. The union of B such actual charts cannot cover T unless
B >= (3/2)^Delta.
For the connected four-partition source, Delta=n/3+1, so an EXPLICIT global all-signing actual-chart cover of this type necessarily has exponentially many members (even allowing non-rank-full selections). This is a scoped representational obstruction, not a lower bound on deciding the single positive target t=0, symbolic shared representations, adaptive forget-and-join, complement representations, other types of affine flats, or arbitrary algorithms. CC01's upper bound for fixed Delta is unaffected. For mixed output ranks k_i the same proof gives B>=2^Delta product over unselected owners (1-2^(-k_i)), if the selected rank r is attainable by independent reps. The rank-two version suffices here.

## 6. No raw forest elimination on an infinite genuine SAT family (new source-specific AB07 obstruction)
For linear triples each dual observation plane consists of pair difference rows and zero; two different planes intersect trivially because no pair of variables repeats. Any direction v (binary flip word) touches owners whose literals are not all equal on v. For two touched owners their stacked observation map is surjective onto F2^4. Choose a word x at which the first owner's forbidden output occurs at one value of the private direction bit and the second owner's forbidden output occurs at the opposite value. Such x exists by surjectivity; projecting along v retains a consistent conflict packet. Thus the AB07 conflict graph is a complete graph on all touched owners, independent of signs, on the raw chart. It is a forest iff at most two owners touched.

For any integer l>=5 with distinct residues 0,2,4,6 mod l (e.g. all odd l>=5), construct n=3l tripartite vertices A_i,B_i,C_i, four partitions indexed j=0,1,2,3, owner (A_i,B_(i+j),C_(i+2j)) modulo l. Within each partition every vertex appears once, all variable pairs occur at most once, connected because any adjacent pair of partition indices joins all A_i cyclically via common B vertices and all B,C via their clauses. Set A=0,B=1,C=0 for actual SAT witness. Any nonconstant direction corresponds to a nontrivial vertex subset X. If |X| not divisible by 3, every partition contains a mixed triple, so at least four touched owners. If |X| divisible by 3, a partition containing any mixed triple contains at least two (one mixed triple alone contributes 1 or 2 mod3). Thus <=3 mixed owners would be confined to one partition, leaving three unbroken partitions including adjacent indices. Their union is connected, so X must be empty or full, contradiction. Minimum touched is exactly 4 by a singleton. Therefore every nontrivial direction modulo global complement has conflict graph at least K4. No AB07 forest step on this raw chart; initial four-port source elimination cannot rely on such admission universally. The family is easy SAT, so no runtime lower bound. Genuine positive UNSAT control: affine plane of order 3, its 12 lines split into four parallel partitions. In any two-coloring one color has >=5 points. If a five-point set contains no full line, each direction's three parallel lines contain at most two points, hence counts (2,2,1), giving just two pairs per direction and eight pairs total; the ten pairs actually lie on unique lines, contradiction. Minimum two monochromatic lines computationally observed; flipping one literal in each bad line gives a signed SAT neighbor on identical geometry (not a positive instance). The four-partition rank-core and chart-volume obstruction applies to both labels.

Initial unguarded source closure: each rank-two NAE forbidden indicator has three distinct quadratic monomials on its variable pairs. Linearity implies each such monomial occurs in only one owner. Therefore no nonzero linear combination of these original indicator equations cancels all degree-two terms. After a single coordinate pin, every owner still has a quadratic term on an unpinned pair unique to it; no rank-one owner or affine consequence arises by that same degree-two linear cancellation alone. Global complement symmetry rules out any forced individual bit on SAT unpinned inputs. This is a scoped check on elementary initial closure, not a claim about all sound inference. In the actual matching audit, local unit/contradiction propagation was checked on supplied boundary pins; full AB03 integration was not run.
