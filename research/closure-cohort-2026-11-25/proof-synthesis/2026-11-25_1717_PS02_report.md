## 2. Theorem A: paid recognition of all graphic-plane charts
All arithmetic GF(2). Input m labeled packets (a_i*x=s_i) OR (b_i*x=t_i), each a_i,b_i independent coefficient vectors after local affine normalization, explicit encoded length N. Define U_i=span(a_i,b_i) and completed ground set of all three nonzero vectors of every U_i, with owner IDs retained even if directions repeat. Let M be its binary vector matroid. Define d(S)=|S|-rank(sum_{i in S}U_i), Delta=max_S d(S) including empty.

**Claim.** If Delta<=1 and M is graphic, the supplied signed instance is decidable and an actual model acquired deterministically in polynomial N, without a supplied graph, repair chart, target assignment, or signed consistent transversal. All signings are covered. Recognition can also reject the chart promise in polynomial time, but non-graphicness returns UNKNOWN for SAT, not UNSAT.

Choose a basis of the coefficient span by Gaussian elimination. Express every labeled ground vector in that basis. Bixby-Wagner graph realization acquires a tree T whose r basis edges encode the independent coordinates and whose paths encode the other columns; add a labeled graph edge between each path's endpoints. Conversely if M is graphic, choose any representing graph and spanning forest corresponding to the given basis. Glue one root vertex of each component together if necessary to form a single tree; this preserves edge dependencies. Over GF(2), the unique expression of a nonbasis edge in the forest basis is its fundamental path. Thus the graph realization test is necessary and sufficient for a coordinate chart that realizes every completed row as an edge difference. A root-relative vertex assignment is equivalent to a choice of basis row evaluations; for any graph word solve those independent row equations in the original variables. Conversely any original word sets tree edge differences and hence every represented difference. No rank loss or extra semantic restrictions. Check the acquired tree/path representation explicitly in polynomial time.

For one owner the three distinct nonzero edge rows satisfy a+b+(a+b)=0. In a loopless graph a three-element circuit consists of three edges of a triangle (no parallel pair because independent distinct directions). The forbidden observation is (1+s_i,1+t_i); its third value is their XOR. Thus precisely one switching class of three vertex labels is forbidden, i.e. a signed NAE3 owner. For any triangle vertex chosen as pivot, use its two incident edges as alternatives with RHS complementary to their respective forbidden values. This is the same relation, not a replacement by only a sufficient condition.

For any nonempty owner subset S, its graphic rank is v(S)-c(S)<=v(S)-1. By Delta<=1, |S|<=rank+1<=v(S). Hall therefore assigns each owner a distinct incident vertex pivot by ordinary bipartite matching. This also works on disconnected graphs, though the glued tree construction is convenient. Pivot owner sends two arcs to its two other triangle vertices, carrying the corresponding required parity. Nonpivots have no outgoing arc.

Use CC02 Theorem A: every closed sink SCC of pivots must have a balanced simple directed cycle, since selecting one true outgoing edge per pivot from a model produces such a cycle. Conversely choose one balanced cycle in every such sink SCC and use all nonpivots as additional roots. Every vertex reaches a root. Choose an outgoing arc decreasing distance for every other pivot; assign root bits arbitrarily, assign cycle bits by its zero total parity, propagate outward along the chosen arcs. Each owner has one actual satisfied equation. Even-cycle recognition via RST is exact: replace every demand-0 arc by fresh two-edge directed path, demand-1 arc stays one edge. A simple directed cycle in expanded graph corresponds to an original simple cycle, with length parity equal to total original demand. No duplicate ordered pairs in matched triangle graph. If YES, greedily delete edges while retaining YES, at most polynomially many RST calls; an inclusion-minimal YES subgraph consists of one even simple cycle. Lift through subdivisions, then tree basis and original affine equations. Check every original owner. QED.

Important distinction: the matroid must include ALL THREE directions per owner, not merely the two originally offered edges. A disjoint paired-edge graph has graphic original edges but can fail this condition. K5 below illustrates that failure. This theorem combines an acquired coordinate chart with CC02, rather than assuming all strict geometric kernels are signed NAE in given coordinates. For general rank>=3 owner spaces the analogous all-nonzero completion contains a rank-three Fano restriction; no universal mixed-arity claim.

## 3. Theorem B: single-owner puncture preserves the admissible deficiency
Assume strict d1: total rank=m-1 and all proper subsets have rank>=their owner counts. Pick any owner i; choose one of its two original equations as a guard a*x=s and delete that owner. In the quotient by span(a), any subset S of remaining owners had rank>=|S|, so its new rank>=|S|-1. Thus maximum deficiency is <=1 in each child. This is independent of the guard value. Normalize exact affine residuals by Gaussian substitution and retain original guard and lift.

Paid forced-information saturation: remove satisfied constants and tautologies, reject an all-false packet; if residual packet becomes one nonzero equation, impose it and delete that owner. This never increases maximum deficiency. Proof: for any subset S of other owners, if the forced row a lies outside span(S), quotient rank of S unchanged. If a lies inside, quotient rank loses one, but before deletion S union forced-owner had the same rank as S and one more owner, so its previous deficiency already bounds the new one. A tautology deletion cannot increase max deficiency. Repeat until no independent forced row remains; rank-increasing guards bounded by input dimension. Recheck actual row ranks and preserve proofs in original coordinate system. This is syntactic affine forced closure, not semantic entailment oracle.

Consequently try each owner and both branches (<=2m child attempts), after root forced closure and conditional closure. If any admitted child YES, return actual lifted model. If for some owner both children are exact NO, return UNSAT. Otherwise UNKNOWN. When both children of at least one owner have graphic completed planes (or are locally discharged), the terminal is complete polynomially; include all failed attempts in cost. A single graphic child that is NO does not prove root NO. This closes a concrete acquisition and exception-decision class beyond direct CC02; it does not establish existence of a universally suitable owner.

K5 root (fix x0=0, remaining four coordinates) groups as in PS01: (1,6),(2,9),(4,10),(8,5),(3,12) in decimal binary row masks. All signs 1: UNSAT. Their completed planes partition all 15 nonzero vectors of F2^4. Not graphic: each rank-three hyperplane supplies seven distinct nonzero rows, while a simple graphic matroid of rank 3 has at most 6 edges (component rank sum 3, sum binom(r_j+1,2)<=6). But after deleting any owner and contracting any nonzero direction of that plane, the 12 remaining directions project to exactly six of the seven nonzero directions in F2^3. Indeed each quotient fiber has two vectors, the other two vectors of the removed plane comprise exactly one missing fiber. Any set of six nonzero rank-three directions is graphic K4 under a linear chart: GL(3,2) sends the missing vector to 111, leaving six weight-one/two vectors. Thus any owner puncture suffices, with all signs, even though no root graphic chart exists. Forced rank-one closure preserves this chart if needed (graphic matroids closed under contraction/deletion).

Also independently recover the primal rows of MFC45 five dual planes using a kernel basis of the 6-by-10 H with columns 15,32,13,17,14,36,11,53,10,39. The rank-four primal has nine distinct completed directions, is not graphic, but both branches of owner 0 admit the terminal for every one of the 1024 primal signings in the executed reference audit. This is a verified finite instance, not a universal theorem. K5 likewise all 1024 signs decided, 1008 SAT/16 UNSAT. Distinct syndrome counts differ from raw signs because equivalent affine translations duplicate targets.

## 4. Theorem C: strict deficiency-one chain with arbitrarily many surviving Fano restrictions
This refutes a natural proposed extension of theorem B, including ordinary forced-information closure, not the entire cooperative portfolio.

Take b independent copies of the rank-four K5 geometry above, in direct sum V_1+...+V_b. For each neighboring pair i,i+1, remove owner 2 (index1) of block i and owner 1 (index0) of block i+1; replace them by ONE cross owner consisting of the first original direction of each removed owner, namely (2_i=1) OR (1_(i+1)=1). Interior blocks lose two owners, endpoints lose one. All other owners remain. This is a paired-affine graphic-original-row instance, with physically separate graph components connected by group IDs; not the simple connected 4-regular P4CC promise of SW02. Number of groups 4b+1, full rank 4b because each block retains at least three distinct full K5 spread planes.

For any subset S of owners let l_i count retained original owners selected in block i plus included cross incidences at that block. Let e be number of included cross owners. Then sum l_i=|S|+e. At most two local owner planes are reduced to a singleton direction, all nonzero directions of different original planes distinct. For l<=2 the selected local directions have rank>=l. For l=3, at least one full plane and an outside direction guarantee rank>=3 (or two full planes give rank4). For l=4 or5, two full planes suffice to span four. Thus local rank>=min(l_i,4). Let f=#blocks with l_i=5. Full blocks have every incident cross included. For proper S each connected run of full blocks in the path has at least one included outgoing edge to a nonfull block, and internal edges are included; the number of included edges is therefore >=f (the runs' counted edges are distinct). Hence
  rank(S)>=sum l_i-f=|S|+e-f>=|S|.
Full S has rank 4b=m-1. Strict d1 proven.

All signs 1: each endpoint can satisfy its retained four owners only if its removed selected direction is false, because K5 is UNSAT. An interior block satisfying its three retained owners cannot have both selected removed directions true, since that would satisfy full K5. Hence at most b-2 endpoint contributions to the b-1 cross obligations, so UNSAT for b>=2 (b=1 original K5). Flip only the demand of leftmost cross alternative 2_1 to 0. An explicit actual witness in root-relative four-bit masks is x^(1)=13, x^(i)=14 for i>=2; all retained and cross owners verified directly, giving same-geometry SAT neighbor. Both labels also decided by two-state chain DP: enumerate 16 words per block, keep actual left/right boundary contribution bits, join adjacent OR and store witnesses. O(b) transitions with backpointers. This is an easy infinite family, not a runtime lower bound.

Every local block retains a rank-three Fano restriction in the full completed-direction matroid. Its surviving local rows include all 15 except the two unused directions in each removed plane (<=4 missing rows). Missing rows have no odd dependency: a 3-term relation would use two from one removed plane, summing to its selected direction which is not missing, and a 1-term relation impossible. Thus a linear functional h_i taking value 1 on every missing row exists (solve the consistent linear equations), choosing any nonzero functional if no missing rows. Its kernel has the seven nonzero vectors of a rank-three space, all retained. Each is a simple nongraphic rank-three restriction. Hence b disjoint independent local Fano restrictions, certified using only four-bit data. Any chart transformation preserves these row dependencies.

**Guard-envelope strengthening.** Consider a live branch using h explicit original-owner guards (one offered equation or an equivalent original direction per chosen owner) and deletion of those owners, interleaved with ordinary local affine closure as in section 3. Let B0 be blocks touched by these explicit owner IDs, at most 2h. A row chosen after substitution may be lifted to the original owner direction modulo previous guards; include that original direction in the accounting. Let E consist of B0 and their immediate path neighbors, so |E|<=3|B0|<=6h.

Define an envelope subspace as direct sum of full V_i for i in B0 and for a boundary neighbor with two neighbors in B0; for any neighbor with just one B0 neighbor include only the line of its selected original direction on that cross owner; elsewhere include zero. Every explicit guard lies in this envelope. Inductively every forced equation stays in it: a rank-two packet can become tautological, impossible or unary only when the accumulated guard span intersects its original plane nontrivially (otherwise its two observations remain independent). For retained local owners in a one-neighbor block, its plane is disjoint from that block's envelope line, since different spread planes intersect only at zero. For cross owners not incident to B0, their two local directions belong to unused distinct owner planes, hence neither lies on the relevant one-neighbor line; blocks having two B0 neighbors have no such outgoing cross. Direct-sum separation then gives zero intersection with envelope. All other owner planes are entirely inside envelope. Thus any new forced original direction can be represented inside envelope, and a packet wholly outside with rank unchanged cannot become tautological. Deleting an explicit or forced owner cannot remove a direction needed for a Fano restriction of a block outside E, since no owner touching that block is explicit or affected. Consequently every live branch retains >=b-6h Fano restrictions unchanged in its quotient after closure. In particular h<b/6 cannot normalize the SAT neighbor into a graphic completed-plane chart by these guards and this forced closure alone. Contradictory branches may terminate earlier; no claim about stronger semantic elimination, nonlinear contraction, proof systems or arbitrary algorithms. This counterexample deliberately has a complementary exact constant-interface DP terminal.
