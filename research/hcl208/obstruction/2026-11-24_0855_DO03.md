## 2. Exact tetrahedral cycle compiler (PROVED)
Let G=(V,E) be a connected simple graph, n=|V|, m=|E|, b=m-n+1. Identify the four colors with F2^2. For each edge choose independently a nonzero difference d_e in {01,10,11}. Let C be the b-by-m binary matrix of fundamental cycles of a spanning tree. The compatibility constraint is C d=0 in (F2^2)^b. Equivalently, in F2^(2b), the explicit factor R_e={ (C_e,0),(0,C_e),(C_e,C_e) }. The root target is zero; pinned edges contribute their actual fixed syndromes and shift the target. The exact row product is independent until the explicit global XOR equation. A coloring yields these rows. Conversely, integrate differences on the tree from root color zero, and fundamental cycle equations verify every non-tree edge. Nonzero differences enforce each original inequality. All source rows are acquired explicitly in polynomial time; no local semantic query or hidden witness is used. Each zero-syndrome tuple lifts to exactly four colorings. This gives source-to-output correctness for the ordinary graph domain.

## 3. Girth controls EVERY exact low-rank linear projection (PROVED)
Let g be girth, let P be any surjective linear map from the full 2b-dimensional syndrome space onto F2^r, and pin any p distinct edge rows to arbitrary admissible differences, with p<g. Choose the remaining edge differences independently uniformly from their three values. Define Y=P(Cd). For nonzero character a in F2^r the pullback P^T a is nonzero, and the two binary components give two cycle-space vectors, not both zero. Any nonzero cycle-space vector is an even-degree subgraph containing a cycle, hence its support size is at least g. Their union therefore has at least g active edges. On an unpinned edge with nonzero character coefficient, the three signs average -1/3 (a nonzero linear functional on F2^2 takes 0 once and 1 twice on the nonzero vectors). A pinned edge contributes only a sign. Other factors contribute 1. By independence, |E(-1)^(a dot Y)| <= 3^(-(g-p)).
Character orthogonality (sum of all characters equals 2^r on zero and zero otherwise) yields, for every t,
 Pr(Y=t) >= 2^(-r)[1-(2^r-1)3^(-(g-p))].
If 2^r-1 < 3^(g-p), the exact projected support is ALL F2^r, even for the UNSAT original graph, regardless of which projection or pins are chosen. This is not a linear relaxation: each projected value has actual independent edge-row witnesses, not necessarily actual global colorings. Adaptively selecting P and pins within the bound cannot yield a missing projected value. For any fixed p,r choose sufficiently large g; no uniform superpolynomial runtime follows. This is an analytic all-projection statement; finite tests sample projections, not prove the theorem.
A useful signed count identity at full rank is #colorings = 4 * 3^m / 2^(2b) times the sum over all pairs of cycle-space vectors of (-1/3) raised to the size of their union support. Exact global cancellation must occur on UNSAT. Evaluating this full sum is not established polynomial. A triangle-free graph g=4 permits r=6 at p=0, r=4 at p=1, r=3 at p=2 by the stated bound; at g=3 r=4 at p=0.

## 4. Certified unconditional and guard-local affine closure stagnates (PROVED)

## 5. All nonzero BA02 pair saturation spaces fail (PROVED)
Assume edge connectivity at least three. Every C_e is nonzero (no bridge); C_e=C_f iff e,f form a two-edge cut, since the orthogonal complement of the cycle space is the cut space. Therefore all C_e are distinct, and U_e cap U_f={0} over F2 (independent nonzero binary columns). Each three-row factor has odd size, so no nontrivial translation period.
For arbitrary nonzero quotient H, not merely the pair-span intersection, write h=dim H. If h=1, each three-point factor has a singleton coset fiber (odd size), hence the minimum nonempty fiber sum is 1+1=2, not >2. If h=2, a minimum fiber count >=2 requires all three points in one coset (any nontrivial partition has a singleton); then its entire difference plane lies in H. Because the two planes are distinct, at least one minimum is 1, and the other <=3, so sum <=4. If h>=3, sum <=6<=2^h. Thus BA02's strict minimum-fiber criterion cannot eliminate ANY nonzero subspace using two raw factors. Pinning fewer than g-1 edges and performing affine closure leaves all other three-row factors unchanged; the same result persists. Fixed singleton factors may be absorbed into the target without claiming a nonzero syndrome quotient. Pair merging remains legal; disjoint planes give nine distinct sums, odd cardinality and no period, but repeated merging/grouping is not ruled out. No assertion about other nonaffine sumset criteria or transformed representations.

## 6. Infinite adjacent SAT/UNSAT pairs and NP-complete promised source (PROVED)
By Erdos' large girth/large chromatic number theorem, for each fixed g>=3 take a minimal non-4-colorable subgraph H of girth >=g. Minimality is by vertices and edges, so H is connected, chi=5 (delete an edge then use an extra color if needed), min degree >=4. Every nontrivial cut has at least four edges: otherwise color both induced sides with four colors, randomly permute colors on one side; expected number of conflicting crossing edges <=3/4, so one permutation has no conflict, contradiction. Every edge deletion H-e is 4-colorable and remains at least 3-edge-connected, with girth >=g. Thus BOTH labels exhibit all the preceding raw barriers. For every SAT neighbor, every original edge row is actually supported: GL(2,2) permutes the three nonzero differences transitively while preserving proper colorings. The positive witness lift is genuine. On H, none of the actual rows has a global extension even though every rank-limited projection and affine test described above passes.
Stronger domain statement: for each FIXED g, 4-colorability remains NP-complete on connected simple graphs of girth >=g and edge connectivity >=3. Start with arbitrary 3-colorability and adjoin one universal vertex to obtain connected 4-colorability. Fix H as above and an edge uv. H-uv is a 4-colorable equality gadget between u,v: all its 4-colorings give equal port colors, otherwise H would be 4-colorable; every common color extends by a color permutation. It has edge connectivity >=3. Replace each source edge xy by three disjoint copies of this gadget with u identified with x, private v=z, and one attachment edge zy per copy. Otherwise copies lie on the side of their x, and any nontrivial cut separates original vertices; some original edge crosses and contributes all three attachment edges. Every cycle inside a gadget has length >=g; a cycle containing an attachment edge must travel between its two gadget ports (distance >=g-1), hence length >=g.
