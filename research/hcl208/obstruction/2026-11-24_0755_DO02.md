## 1. Weak vector equality gadget (PROVED)
Take the Fano plane on ports a,b,p and four private vertices 3,4,5,6. Delete line {a,b,p}; remaining six triples are {a,3,4},{a,5,6},{b,3,5},{b,4,6},{p,3,6},{p,4,5}. Every Boolean solution has all three ports equal: otherwise it would color the full Fano plane. The full Fano plane is non-2-colorable: the larger color class has >=4 points; any four-point set either contains a line or is the complement of a line (count the 28 four-sets formed by line plus outside point, no duplicates as two distinct lines have union of size 5, leaving seven complements of lines). Equal ports extend by assigning all four private vertices opposite. As a two-port gadget with p private, it is inclusion-minimal: each of six single-edge deletions admits both unequal port assignments. This last finite statement is certified by exhaustive 2^7 table below; it also follows by checking the six explicit triples. Each deletion table is included in raw results.

Assign unit real vectors A,B to a,b with A dot B >=4/5 and assign A to p. Define m=(A+B)/2 and s=||m||^2 >=9/10. Take q=(9/(10s))m+t, with t in a new private orthogonal direction and ||t||^2=9/10-81/(100s)>=0. Then ||q||^2=A dot q=B dot q=9/10. Choose four private mutually orthogonal directions u_j with norm squared 4/5, orthogonal to q and ports, and ubar=(sum u_j)/4. Set v_j=-(2/3)q+u_j-ubar. Each has norm one; distinct interiors have inner product 1/5; every port-interior inner product is -3/5. Therefore each of the six edge vector sums has squared norm 3+2(1/5-3/5-3/5)=1. The logical equality can thus transmit differing unit vector orientations at no local vector cost. All coordinates and diagonal metric entries rational when input port coordinates and metric are rational; zero private norm is allowed.

## 2. An exact critical counterexample (PROVED)
Let root vector A=(1,0). Build two chains of four independent copies of the above gadget, sharing only consecutive terminal vertices. On positive branch use endpoint sequence (4/5,3/5),(3/5,4/5),(0,1),(-3/5,4/5); use reflection in horizontal axis for negative branch. Consecutive dot products >=4/5. Final clause consists of root and the two chain endpoints. Boolean equality along both chains forces all three final bits equal, so UNSAT. Removing final clause permits all equal ports. Removing any gadget edge permits that gadget's two ports unequal; take one orientation switch along its chain and leave the other chain unchanged, fill every remaining gadget by its local table. Final triple then NAE. Hence edge-critical, with 49 vertices and 49 triples. Complement of each deletion witness gives the other monochromatic assignment on its deleted edge, so all 98 single-owner relaxations SAT.

Let B be monotone incidence matrix, D(q) weighted occurrence diagonal, M(q)=3B^T diag(q) B-D(q), BH01 convention. For a Gram matrix X of unit vectors, trace(M(q)X)=sum_e 3 q_e (||sum_{v in e} v||^2-1). The constructed X is PSD with all gadget terms zero and final term -72/25, since final vector sum=(-1/5,0). For every nonnegative q with positive final weight, trace(M(q)X)=-72 q_final/25<0. Therefore M(q) is not PSD; in particular every strictly positive weight choice is excluded, not just unweighted PD. Positive diagonal trace ensures indefiniteness when q is nonzero. For any diagonal subtraction diag(d) with sum d>0, trace((M(q)-diag(d))X)<0 even if q_final=0; thus diagonal-shift positive-margin certificates also fail.

Moreover let z be the real spin witness satisfying all gadget clauses and violating final clause (all ports equal). Its rank-one Gram has edge-sum squared norm 1 on gadgets and 9 at final. X'=(25/28)X+(3/28)zz^T is PSD with unit diagonal and EVERY edge-sum squared norm exactly 1. This is an exact feasible point for the basic NAE unit-vector SDP with per-clause equalities, not merely aggregate objective feasibility. No floating point is used. It need not satisfy all global triangle inequalities or stronger moment hierarchies. Any PSD M(q) with nonnegative weights must omit the final edge and annihilate the range of X (trace=0); its kernel constraints alone cannot imply real linear equalities between ports having distinct vectors in X. Further Boolean reasoning or other transformations may solve the instance.

## 3. Arbitrarily high girth while preserving the obstruction (PROVED)
For any fixed g>=2, DO01 supplies an edge-minimal non-2-colorable 3-uniform H of Berge girth >g. Remove one edge f={u,v,w}. All remaining Boolean models make f monochromatic, so u=v. Select an inclusion-minimal subset of remaining triples enforcing u=v. It is satisfiable (both equal values by complement); removing any of its edges admits unequal u,v (both orientations by complement). Delete isolates. Retain this fixed two-terminal equality gadget K_g. Any internal path from u to v in its incidence graph uses at least g hyperedges, because appending f would otherwise give a Berge cycle of length <=g in H. Its internal girth >g. Existence/selection is not claimed polynomial in g; one can enumerate finite hypergraphs and truth tables for fixed g.

For an arbitrary monotone NAE network G replace EACH incidence (v,e) by a fresh occurrence variable v_e in e and a private K_g linking v and v_e; no other overlaps between gadgets. All original assignments extend through gadget witnesses and all new solutions decode to original assignments. Each gadget transmits ANY unit vector exactly in the relaxed model: take one satisfying Boolean assignment with both ports 0, give its variables vector A or -A according to its bits. Every internal edge sum has norm 1. Set occurrence vector equal to original vector. A simple incidence cycle inside a gadget has girth >g; any cycle leaving a gadget and crossing it connects distinct ports and contains at least g gadget edges plus an outside edge. Every cycle either stays within a gadget or crosses one (the remaining triples use disjoint occurrence variables), hence stretched graph has Berge girth >g. Criticality is preserved from any edge-critical G: deleting an original copied triple uses its source deletion witness; deleting an internal equality edge on incidence (v,e) uses a source witness violating only e, flips only v_e using the unequal port witness, and fills every other gadget normally. That repairs e. This proves all single-owner neighbors SAT, with both assignments to deleted monochromatic edge by complement. Applying stretch to Section 2 gives, for arbitrarily large g, connected edge-critical monotone NAE UNSAT with feasible exact basic SDP and failure of EVERY strictly positive weighted BH01 PSD test. No extraction of a new critical core is needed after stretching.

## 4. Stronger reduction: fixed-g spectral-resistant promise retains NP-completeness (PROVED)
For ANY monotone NAE formula F, give every source variable A=(1,0); for each of its triple occurrences construct fresh weak equality chains from the source variable to a private final occurrence with respective terminal vector A, (-3/5,4/5), (-3/5,-4/5). Use one weak gadget with identical orientations for first occurrence, and the four-step chains above for other two. Each original triple becomes the triple of private occurrences. Both SAT directions exact by equality, witness decoding and local filling. Every wire edge has vector-sum norm squared 1, every source triple norm squared 1/25. An all-equal source Boolean assignment satisfies every gadget and violates all source triples, giving the same 25/28,3/28 Gram mixture with all edge equalities satisfied. Stretch all incidences using K_g as above. For each fixed g this is a polynomial-time reduction of monotone NAE-3SAT to monotone NAE-3SAT of girth >g with an explicitly constructible basic SDP feasible point and an explicit negative-trace witness against all strictly positive weighted PSD tests (nonempty inputs). All constants depend on g, not N. A fixed finite K_g and its equality/deletion witnesses can be hardcoded or acquired by exhaustive search once for fixed g; no uniform polynomial bound as g varies. Source SAT/UNSAT both preserved, optional rational Gram factor certificate polynomial bit size (constant rational metrics for fixed orientation list, O(N) axes; explicit dense encoding O(N^2) or sparse). Given certificates, their rational metric nonnegativity and edge equalities are polynomially checkable. NP membership by original Boolean witness; hence the corresponding certificate-augmented language is NP-complete, or NP-hard on the promise of the structural and spectral properties. This is a reduction, NOT a P!=NP result. For a critical source the weak-wire compiler is also critical: an edge removal in a wire uses the deletion witness of its associated source triple, switches the wire after the removed gadget and repairs that triple; deletion of a terminal triple lifts directly.
