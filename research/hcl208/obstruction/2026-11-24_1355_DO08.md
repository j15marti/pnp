## A. Effective local spaces and shared rank (PROVED)
For simple graph H, n vertices, m edges, write x_v in GF(2)^2. Factor f_e=1-[x_u+x_v=s_e]. Its observation plane P_e is spanned by the two coordinate differences. On the two-bit difference chart, f_e takes 0 on one word and 1 on the other three. All three nonzero unnormalized Walsh coefficients have absolute value 1. Thus BA07's minimum exact value observation space is P_e, also the minimum support space because this factor is Boolean. This remains true in any affine guard quotient q by a coefficient space L whenever q is injective on P_e: the two-bit chart remains surjective. No expensive semantic acquisition or cancellation of local factor rank needed. This does not assert minimum rank for merged products.

For connected bridgeless H, each edge is on a cycle, hence P_e lies in sum of the other planes; BA07 U=sum P_e has dimension 2(n-1). The two-coordinate kernel D of DP08 is the direct sum of two binary cycle spaces, delta=2(m-n+1). Its support components on factor IDs are exactly the edge blocks of the cycle matroid (bridges isolated): each cycle dependency is an inclusion-minimal support and hence lies within one component; conversely a cycle basis within each block spans all dependencies. In a 2-connected graph every pair of edges is on a common cycle, so one component. This uses the original unguarded planes. Do NOT assume every original circuit stays inclusion-minimal after a quotient.

## B. General width-to-dual-component inequality (PROVED)
For any explicit family of observation subspaces W_i let w be the minimum over subcubic event-leaf trees of the maximum cut intersection rank dim(sum_left W_i intersect sum_right W_i). Let D=ker(direct sum W_i -> sum W_i) and let C be any DP08 basis-support partition, with component nullity delta_C. Then
    max_C delta_C >= w.
Proof: DP08 component spans are jointly independent. Choose any binary trees inside each component and glue them into a binary tree on all factors. Every cut splits at most one component or separates whole components. In the former case the overlap rank equals its intra-component intersection rank, which is at most sum_{i in C} dim W_i - dim sum_{i in C} W_i = delta_C by rank subadditivity on both sides. In the latter case it is zero. For a singleton or empty family use width zero. Thus a high minimum overlap width rules out *all* DP08 support partitions of small maximum nullity, including ones obtained by recomputing the basis after guards. Global nullity alone would not establish this. BA07's global shared rank is also >= w: a cut intersection element decomposed into a left and right sum need not have each summand individually shared, but the combined linear relation implies every summand is in its own overlap I_i, so the element lies in U=sum I_i.
