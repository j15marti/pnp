## 2. DO12-A: true local semantic support and pivot-free groups (PROVED)
Let H be an explicit 3-uniform monotone NAE registry of n variables, m triples, maximum incidence degree D, Berge girth g. Each triple e={a,b,c} has true observation plane U_e=span(e_a+e_b,e_a+e_c) in the dual of F2^n. For any owner subset J of size <g its incidence graph is a forest. The 2|J| generating pair rows are independent: replace each triple by the two edges of its star centered at a; the resulting ordinary graph is a forest (equivalently each connected incidence tree with t triples has 2t+1 variable vertices). Thus dim sum U_e=2|J|.

Let f_J be the conjunction of the NAE predicates on J, without existential projection. Its true observation space is exactly V_J=sum U_e. Proof: a translation t constant on each triple preserves all factors. If t is nonconstant on e, choose the values on e equal to t|e; this is a legal local word, and after translating it becomes all zero. Extend the local word to all other triples by walking the incidence forest outward, assigning two new child variables to avoid monochromaticity. This produces a legal word whose translate is illegal, so t is not a period. The annihilator of the period space is the exact linear observation space. Additionally the satisfying set has full affine hull: for any variable v set the other two variables in every incident triple to opposite bits, and extend into their disjoint remaining subtrees. Both v values are legal with the same other coordinates. These coordinate difference witnesses span the whole scope. For nonempty J the support is proper (the zero word fails), hence nonaffine. This remains true under any surjective affine reparametrization of its scope.

Now permit r deleted or changed original owners (omit them ONLY from the rank argument unless exact removal is certified), and any consistent affine chart Q of final codimension h, including every acquired closure equation. Choose h pivot coordinates P such that restriction of guard rowspace H_Q to P is injective. Gaussian elimination constructs P. Every dual row supported outside P has injective image under the quotient by H_Q; all words on nonpivot coordinates are achievable on Q, by solving for pivot bits. At most Dh original triples touch P. Partition the retained original unmodified owners into disjoint groups of at most b triples, where 2b<g. Mark a group dirty if it touches P, or contains a changed/deleted original owner; equivalently first omit r owners and group the remainder. For the latter convention at most bDh further owners are lost. If mixed groups including changed owners are allowed, charge the entire contaminated groups in r, not just the changed factor. The rest are clean. Their actual guarded regional Boolean functions have the above exact semantic spaces projected injectively, and are nonaffine. Each pair of distinct clean regions has disjoint observation spaces because its union has <=2b<g triples. The sum of their ranks is 2 times the number of clean owners, at least 2(m-r-bDh). All statements hold for every affine RHS, adaptive partition and guard, not merely random guards.

A family of coordinate subspaces with pairwise zero intersections must be a direct sum. Thus S17 simultaneous common-basis recognition on the raw planes (b=1) cannot succeed, even with arbitrary basis change, whenever
    2(m-r-bDh)>n-h.
The same inequality excludes common-coordinate recognition of the exact clean regional observation spaces for b>1, although S17's affine-rejection-to-CNF converter does not itself apply to nonaffine conjunctions. Equivalently success requires (2bD-1)h >= 2(m-r)-n. This excludes the BA11 *minimal-intersection* direct-sum chart as well (pairwise intersections zero yet global dependency). It does NOT by itself exclude DP12's more permissive conservative edge blocks: e.g. three lines span(a), span(a+b), span(b) on a path have zero pair intersections but admit independent edge blocks a,b. Section 3 explicitly handles that escape.

## 3. DO12-B: arbitrary independent edge blocks imply a weighted bag bound (PROVED)
Use the exact DP12 adapter domain: arbitrary regional interaction graph G, independent coordinate blocks one per actual edge and private block per region, and all regional observations lie in their incident/private block sum. Blocks can be arbitrarily large and conservative, not necessarily intersections of minimal spaces. A supplied tree decomposition of G has bags Q_j with running intersection and edge coverage. Keep the original owner leaves of clean regions; for this width argument assign zero space to dirty regions (not a semantic solver reduction). Each clean group contains all its original plane images within its true observation space. Let t=max_j number of clean regions in Q_j.

Claim: the original clean owner planes, on the actual chart, admit a factor-leaf subcubic tree with maximal cut-overlap <=4bt. Proof: choose one occurrence bag for every clean region and attach its original owner leaves there. Refine the bag tree to a subcubic tree (replace high-degree nodes by binary trees), then prune unused branches and suppress degree-two vertices. Each cut is either across an original bag-tree edge or inside a refinement of a bag node B. Choose B as one endpoint in the first case. The regions outside B whose assigned leaves are on opposite sides cannot have an interaction edge: the bags containing either vertex form connected subtrees and, avoiding B, lie in different components after removing B. Hence their observation sums A,C lie in disjoint sets of independent edge/private blocks, so A intersect C=0. Edges to B may be used, but are distinct on either side. The remaining owner rows on either side lie in V_B=sum of clean region observation spaces in B, dim V_B<=2bt. Both cut spaces lie in A+V_B and C+V_B respectively. Dimension identity gives dim((A+V_B) intersect (C+V_B)) <=2 dim V_B, since A intersect C=0. Every cut is therefore bounded by 4bt. This proves the claim even for conservative interface blocks and large local packet coupling rank. If the graph is a forest, take edge bags of size <=2 (or singleton bags), so width <=8b.

Let w_* be optimal raw owner cut overlap of H. Removing at most r+bDh owners of rank two loses at most 2(r+bDh) in w_* (DO10 deletion lemma). Quotienting the clean owners is injective on their entire joint rowspace, so no additional overlap loss. Therefore every such supplied adapter/decomposition satisfies
    w_* <= 2r+2bDh+4bt.                         (1)
This is a restriction on the actual region group representation, not a runtime lower bound.

Every nonempty clean region has proper nonaffine Boolean support by section 2. Hence no single affine indicator packet represents it, and a single expanded signed separable character label cannot represent it either: its support is a Cartesian product of affine sets, hence affine. Therefore L'_i>=2 for every clean region in DP12's unprojected local Boolean label list (before summing private coordinates). Define Bcost=sum_bags product_{i in bag} max(1,L'_i). Then log2 Bcost>=t. Combining with (1):
    log2 Bcost >= max(0,(w_*-2r-2bDh)/(4b)).    (2)
For DO09/DO11's raw width w_*=Omega(n), bounded D, g=Omega(log n), choose b with 2b<g and b=O(log n). For h=o(n/b), r=o(n), every such bag representation has Bcost>=2^Omega(n/b), superpolynomial for polynomial source length. Even the forest case is impossible in this regime. Conversely any polynomial Bcost requires bDh=Omega(n) or deletion/change of linearly many owners, or larger grouping / an alternate representation. This falsifies a universal small-group/low-guard weighted-bag lemma in the fixed registry. It does not exclude global regrouping into larger regions, existential projections with new owner semantics, general overlapping regions, compressed bag contraction, special Fourier cancellation not represented by this bag budget, or other terminals. No lower bound on actual algorithm runtime is inferred merely from the budget expression.
