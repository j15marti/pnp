## Constructive route: signed planar dual T-join
For a signed NAE owner with distinct actual bits, take the three signed literal pairs and charge one unit for each equal literal pair. A satisfied owner contributes 1 and a violated owner 3, so the total pair cost is m+2V. Keep all physical parallel edges. Given a planar embedding of the resulting signed multigraph, encode preferred edge difference d_e and defect y_e = x_u XOR x_v XOR d_e. The possible defect vectors are exactly the vectors whose parity around each face equals the parity of d around that face: face boundary incidence spans the binary cycle space for a connected plane graph (bridges occur twice). In the dual, these are T-joins with T the odd-frustration faces. Minimum nonnegative-weight T-join: compute shortest-path distances on the dual, solve minimum-weight perfect matching on the even terminal set, take symmetric difference of the paths. Conversely every T-join decomposes into T-terminal paths plus cycles, and nonnegative weights justify optimality. Reconstruct primal x by spanning-tree integration and verify all edges and original owners. Handle disconnected graphs separately. All acquisition, matching, reconstruction and bit costs polynomial (standard deterministic weighted matching). This exact route bypasses DO23 signed exponential absolute cycle mass, and can accept arbitrary signs, degree, cycle rank and frustration. It is a standard planar Ising/MaxCut argument, not a general SAT solver.

## Topological crossover firewall (independent proof)
Let finite local energies f_S(x_S) be invariant under complement of all bits of S, with a disk-embedded primal interaction graph (each S induces a clique; ternary planar incidence may be converted by triangle replacement). Four distinct ports in cyclic order 1,2,3,4. Let E(a) be the exact minimum conditioned on four-bit word a. Let Z(a) be any nonnegative product-weight boundary partition function, with complement-symmetric local factors. For each word a and unit vectors e_i, write b=a XOR e_2 XOR e_4. Then
E(a)+E(b) >= min{E(a XOR e_2)+E(a XOR e_4), E(a XOR e_1)+E(a XOR e_3)}.
Z(a)Z(b) <= Z(a XOR e_2)Z(a XOR e_4)+Z(a XOR e_1)Z(a XOR e_3).

Proof: take ordered assignments x,y extending a,b, and induced disagreement graph D on vertices where they differ. Swapping x,y on a single connected component preserves sum energy / product weight, because all differing arguments of any local factor belong to one component. If ports 2,4 are disconnected in D, swap the component of port 2. Output extends a+e2 and a+e4. Otherwise complement y; the disagreement graph becomes the induced complement vertex set, with ports 1,3 therein. There cannot be both a 2-to-4 path in D and a disjoint 1-to-3 path in its complement in a disk (alternating endpoints, Jordan separation). Swap the component of port 1 in the new disagreement graph to produce a+e1 and a+e3. For minima select minimizers. For weights apply this to every ordered pair. Each of the two output classes has an injective map: disagreement set is unchanged by a component swap, so swap the specified component again, and in class two complement y back. The two output boundary pairs are distinct. Thus summing nonnegative products proves the second inequality, including zero/one feasibility counts. This works with arbitrary signs, weights, private size, and any planar complement-symmetric clique factors; no P vs NP assumption.

Corollary: the exact projected crossing relation x1=x3 and x2=x4 cannot be realized by any such disk network, even allowing arbitrary many private bits, copies, and exact optimum-preserving replacement rather than equality of all energies. Set a=0000,b=0101; both feasible, all four outputs infeasible. Likewise a common minimum on these four valid words and strict higher energy on all invalid words contradicts the minimum inequality. Independent crossing parity demands are equivalent by a fixed bit gauge. A noncrossing pairing has a trivial two-edge realization. This rules out a local exact planarization route to the dual-matching/cycle evaluator, not a general nonlocal normalization, a higher-arity nonplanar primal graph, a shared noncomplement-symmetric reference, or P=NP. Given two source witnesses, component recombination costs O(graph size + factor incidence) and preserves total pair energy, with direct source verification. Full SAT witness acquisition is not free.
