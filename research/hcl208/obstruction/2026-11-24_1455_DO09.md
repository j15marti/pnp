## 2. DO09-A: simultaneous bounded degree, logarithmic girth and linear treewidth (PROVED)
There are absolute positive constants D, alpha, eta, gamma and infinitely many edge-minimal non-2-colorable 3-uniform linear hypergraphs H on n vertices, max degree <=D, Berge girth >=gamma log n, and pair-graph treewidth >=eta n. Every one-owner relaxation is SAT, as in DO01. Pair graph joins each pair in a triple. n denotes the final critical core; use v for precursor size.

Proof. On v vertices independently include each triple with p=C/v^2 for fixed C=1000 and sufficiently large v. Every coloring has at least v^3/30 monochromatic candidate triples for sufficiently large v (balanced is minimal up to rounding). Its random monochromatic count has mean >=Cv/30. Chernoff lower tail at half the mean is <=exp(-Cv/240); union bound over 2^v colorings tends to zero. Thus with probability tending to one every coloring violates >=Cv/60 triples.

Small-set sparsity: put q=3/5. For a k-set, let l=ceil(q k). The probability that SOME k-set contains at least l triples is bounded by
  binom(v,k) binom(binom(k,3),l) p^l
  <= (ev/k)^k [e C k^3/(6 l v^2)]^l.
Choose fixed alpha>0 small enough that B(k/v)^2<=1 for all k<=alpha v, B=eC/(6q), and e B^q alpha^(2q-1)<=1/2. Then this probability is <=[e B^q (k/v)^(2q-1)]^k. Impossible l>binom(k,3) cases contribute zero. Summing k<=sqrt(v) gives o(1) by a geometric bound with ratio eB^q v^(-(2q-1)/2); for sqrt(v)<k<=alpha v sum <=v 2^(-sqrt(v))=o(1). Thus simultaneously every nonempty set S with |S|<=alpha v induces <q|S| triples.

High degree removal: vertex degree X has E X^2 <= C/2+(C/2)^2. Choose fixed integer D >8(C/2+(C/2)^2). Expected sum of degrees over vertices with degree>D is <=v E X^2/D <v/8, by X 1_(X>D)<=X^2/D. Markov gives probability >7/8 that deleting every edge incident to these vertices removes <=v edges.

Short-cycle removal: for ell>=2 the expected number of Berge ell-cycles is at most v^(2ell)p^ell=C^ell, by overcounting ordered distinct cycle vertices and one remaining vertex per triple (require actual distinct edges for the probability p^ell). Set g=floor((log_C v)/4). Expected number for 2<=ell<=g is O(C^g)=O(v^1/4). With probability tending to one there are <=v^1/2 such cycles. Delete one edge per short cycle; no new cycle is created. All events can hold simultaneously with positive probability by a union bound. Total deletions <=v+v^1/2, less than Cv/60 for large v, so the remaining bounded-degree, girth>g hypergraph is non-2-colorable. Small-set sparsity survives arbitrary edge/vertex deletion.

Extract an inclusion-minimal non-2-colorable edge set, remove isolated vertices. This is existential, not efficient generation. Core H is connected, every vertex degree >=2 (degree one could be extended after removing its edge), hence m>=2n/3. Small-set sparsity implies n>alpha v. Also n<=v, hence girth>g=Omega(log n), max degree<=D and n grows unboundedly. m<=Dn/3. Source encoding is polynomial in n (indeed sparse owner registry has O(n) occurrences).

Linear treewidth proof: let G be the pair graph, t=tw(G). A graph of treewidth t has a balanced vertex separator of size <=t+1 (use a centroid bag in a tree decomposition with each vertex assigned to one bag). Recursively separate components of size >alpha v, always balancing to at most half. At each depth at most 1/alpha such components exist, and after ceil(log_2(1/alpha))+1 depths none remain. Thus some set S of size <=K_alpha(t+1), for constant K_alpha=(ceil(1/alpha)+1)(ceil(log_2(1/alpha))+1), leaves all components of size <=alpha v. Every hyperedge avoiding S lies entirely within one component, since its three vertices form a clique. Those triples number <=q(n-|S|). At most D|S| triples meet S. Therefore
  2n/3 <= m <= q(n-|S|)+D|S| <= q n+D|S|.
  |S|>=n/(15D), t+1>=n/(15 D K_alpha).
Hence t=Omega(n). This is robust to critical extraction; it is not justified merely by precursor expansion. Choosing eta smaller absorbs the additive 1. Criticality gives a coloring of H-e for each e and this coloring must be monochromatic on e; complement chooses either allowed orientation. Relax exactly one owner of e (budget 2 to 3) and the coloring is a SAT witness. Same source incidence/weights, changed one budget. No efficient witness discovery or criticality recognition assumed.

## 3. DO09-B: arbitrary sparse background cannot beat the rank/width tradeoff (PROVED)
Let J be a symmetric pair-interaction matrix of BH08, G its nonzero off-diagonal support, with maximum degree Delta. For any symmetric rational/integer background A of rank r, set correction R=J-A off diagonal. In ANY vertex order let w be maximum number of assigned vertices with a future nonzero correction edge (adding hard relations only increases w).
For any prefix cut X|Y, let K be its correction frontier. On X\\K by Y, J=A. For a maximum crossing matching of size nu, at most |K| matched edges meet K. The remaining matching in the submatrix M has size nu0>=nu-|K| and rank(M)<=r.

Bound 1 (bounded degree, no girth required): greedily choose an induced submatching. Each chosen edge conflicts with at most 2(Delta-1) other matching edges via off-diagonal crossing adjacency. Thus at least nu0/(2Delta-1) edges survive. Their submatrix is diagonal nonzero, so rank(M)>=(nu-|K|)/(2Delta-1). Use max(1,2Delta-1) for empty graph.
Bound 2 (C4-free support, degree not needed): the submatrix indexed by the matching pairs has nonzero diagonal and at most one of each pair of opposite off-diagonal entries nonzero, since otherwise there is a C4. Its Hadamard product with its transpose is a nonsingular diagonal. The rank of a Hadamard product is at most the product of ranks (expand outer products), so nu0<=r^2. This is the classical fooling-set rank inequality, applied to actual matching pairs. Girth>4 and linearity of H imply C4-free pair graph by BH08, including after deleting a triple. Signs do not affect this argument.
Therefore for every cut
  nu <= |K|+min(r^2,(2Delta-1)r) [C4-free, Delta>=1].
Without C4-free retain the linear-in-r degree bound.
Use the caterpillar decomposition of the vertex order; all internal cuts are prefix cuts, singleton cuts have matching size <=1. Known maximum matching width inequality tw(G)+1<=3 mmw(G) (Jeong, Saether, Telle, arXiv:1507.02384, for nontrivial graphs) gives
  tw(G)+1 <= 3 max(1,w+min(r^2,(2Delta-1)r)).
For H of DO09-A, Delta<=2D and tw=Omega(n). Thus w+(4D-1)r=Omega(n) for EVERY A (including zeros, arbitrary sparse supports, arbitrary diagonal and rational entries) and EVERY order. Fixed-r backgrounds necessarily have linear correction frontier; log-width correction necessarily has linear background rank. BH08's explicit moment capacity sufficient condition cannot hold, even if optimal sparse backgrounds were given for free. This is a rank/width parameter obstruction, not a lower bound on actual surviving min-plus keys or arbitrary computation. A SAT neighbor removes one triple from J and retains a hard single-mask relation. Pair graph loses only its three edges, and adding two of the three vertices to all bags restores them, so treewidth drops by at most two; same conclusion. Protected arbitrary dense guards may change the source matrix; no universal claim after nonlinear transforms.
