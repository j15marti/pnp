## B. Low-weight guard localization (PROVED)
Let L be the coefficient space of G. Define S as the set of vertices touched by any weight-at-most-four vector of L in the ORIGINAL bit coordinates. Select a basis of the span of these low-weight words FROM the low-weight words themselves. At most p vectors, each with at most 4 bit positions, cover the support of the entire low-weight subspace. Hence |S| <=4p (even number of touched bits <=4p). Discovery can be charged polynomially by checking all O(n^4) masks against a Gaussian basis, but the proof does not need to acquire S in the evaluator.

Call an edge good if both endpoints outside S. At most 4pD original edges are not good. For a good edge e and any other distinct edge f, their two-dimensional observation planes are independent modulo L in the e direction: if nonzero u in plane(e) and v in plane(f) have u+v in L, this sum has weight <=4, and at least one endpoint of e not shared with f contributes a bit outside S. Contradiction. Simplicity of graph is crucial. In particular each good event remains codimension two, pairwise compatible with every other consistent event, and cannot be contained in another distinct event (if other event has positive codimension, its intersection imposes an additional equation). For earlier subguards L_old subset L, the same holds. Thus no good event is removed by the specified closure on a nonrejected path. A guard that acquires many new independent equations is not ruled out: p is the FINAL rank.

## C. DP07 lower bound (PROVED)
For any peeling order take the first good event. All other good events still remain and co-occur with it. Their combined coefficient rank is 2(n-kappa_good), where kappa_good counts components of the graph of good edges, including isolates; standard incidence rank by a spanning forest. Deleting <=4pD edges raises component count by at most that number. Therefore, for every order, and hence for the optimal DP07 bottleneck K*,
 K* >= max(0,min(m-4pD-1, 2(n-kappa-4pD)-p-2)).
When no good edge exists the right side is zero. This is the exact parameter's lower bound, NOT a lower bound on alternative count algorithms. DP07 arithmetic job credit has a term >=2^K*. Even arbitrary affine changes of basis cannot change these conditional ranks.

## D. DS06 nonlinear rank robustness (PROVED)
For any retained edge set of size m', let g_e be the bad indicator, a product of two affine forms (1+a_u+a_v+s_a)(1+b_u+b_v+s_b). For a subset T of edges, the quadratic polar form of their XOR has matrix [[0,L_T],[L_T,0]], with L_T the binary graph Laplacian sum of incidence outer products. The affine shifts do not affect this matrix. If the XOR becomes affine on G, the polar form vanishes on ker(G) x ker(G). In a basis extending ker(G), the complementary block has size p, so polar rank <=2p and rank(L_T)<=p.
Every row of L_T has support <=D+1. A basis of <=p actual rows covers every nonzero column, hence at most p(D+1) nonzero columns. By symmetry and simplicity every incident vertex of T has a nonzero column (off-diagonal entry for its edge). Thus |T|<=pD(D+1)/2. The kernel of the map from edge-combination coefficients to restricted nonlinear quadratic coefficients is a linear subspace whose EVERY word has weight at most that bound. Any dimension h binary subspace has a word of weight >=h by setting every systematic pivot coordinate to one. Therefore the kernel dimension is at most floor(pD(D+1)/2), and
 r_nonlinear >= m' - floor(pD(D+1)/2).
This bound includes arbitrary dense affine restrictions and arbitrary invertible input/output affine charts. At p=0, unique cross-edge monomials give rank m'. Discovery of coefficient ranks uses explicit polynomial ANF substitution or DS06 sparse orbit; never assume semantic satisfying-set hull for free.

## E. BH06 private-pivot obstruction (PROVED)
Each legal edge is an OR of two affine inequality tests. On any graph cycle, each of its edge difference forms is the XOR of the corresponding other edge forms. This identity survives any affine chart and restriction on coefficients. Within a remaining cycle, no edge has a private pivot annihilating all the others. In a fully private-pivot peelable subset of good edges the graph must therefore be a forest (consider first removal from a surviving cycle). At least m-n+kappa-4pD good edges must be set aside as exceptions. At p=0 the exact minimum is cycle rank beta=m-n+kappa: discard a feedback edge set to leave a forest, whose leaves supply private pivots. This applies to the BH06 two-affine-test presentation, not all possible feature representations or nonlinear projection.
