## A. Universal envelope on the fixed registry (PROVED)
Input n Boolean coordinates, m signed NAE triples of distinct variables, each with observation plane U_i=span(e_a+e_b,e_a+e_c), and one forbidden observation word. A consistent affine guard has arbitrary dense rowspace H_0, rank h, and actual RHS. Owner-local affine closure means enumerate the four possible observation words, Gaussian-test against current guard, discard forbidden word, reject if no legal word; otherwise insert one independent equation necessary on these legal words, restart all owner scans until stagnation. These are unconditional only within the actual chart, and guard RHS remains attached.

Construct a structural activation envelope independent of RHS/signs: start K=H_0, M=empty; while any unmarked plane meets K nontrivially, mark its owner and adjoin all of U_i to K. Repeat until none. At most m markings; choice of order cannot affect least fixed point by monotonicity and induction (any completed fixed point containing H_0 contains every increment). Each marking adds at most one independent row, so rank K<=h+|M|; sum_{i in M} U_i subset K.

If U_i meets current rowspace trivially, all four observation words remain attainable; the three accepted words have full affine hull in the plane. No new equation or contradiction can originate there. Any newly implied ambient affine equation of this single-owner restriction is also in current rowspace + U_i: directions in their common kernel preserve all accepted words. Thus all actual local insertions and any contradictory owner belong to the envelope; actual rowspace stays inside K by induction, including before a contradiction. This proof covers any order, arbitrary dense input guards, arbitrary signs and all consistent RHS. In particular every unmarked owner retains full nonaffine support on every surviving chart. K is a structural overapproximation ONLY, not a sound set of forced equations. Never impose the added envelope rows as constraints.

## B. Rank expansion bounds amplification (PROVED)
Suppose for every nonempty owner subset J of size <=t we have rank(sum U_i) >= (1+delta)|J| for some delta>0. If h<delta*t, the marking sequence cannot reach t owners: at that prefix the rank inequality gives (1+delta)t<=h+t, contradiction. Hence final q=|M|<t, and (1+delta)q<=h+q, yielding q<=h/delta and final actual rank h_final<=h(1+1/delta). Applies even to contradictory guarded branches up to their contradiction. A sharper finite-girth variant has delta=1 for t<g, since each such owner subset is an incidence forest.

DO09-A supplies existential critical NAE cores of unbounded n with max degree D, Berge girth g=Omega(log n), and every variable set of size <=alpha*n inducing fewer than 3|S|/5 triples (inherits from precursor). For t=floor(alpha*n/3), each subset of <=t owners has <=alpha*n incident variables. In each connected incidence component with j owners, rank of pair-incidence span is |V|-1. If j<g this equals 2j. Otherwise |V|>5j/3 and for g>=6, |V|-1>=3j/2. Summing gives delta=1/2. Consequently for ALL signs and every initial guard of rank h<t/2, q<=2h and h_final<=3h. The bound is uniform over adaptive choices of guard rowspace and RHS; previous externally acquired independent equations must be included in h, not treated as free. If h=o(n), actual owner-local closure cannot amplify to linear rank on these cores.

Counterexample outside hypothesis: triples {a,b,c_i} all share a,b. Rank-one guard a=b forces each c_i opposite under unsigned NAE. With m triples the rank grows to m+1, so a universal constant amplification without expansion is false. This non-linear family is easy, not a hard input.
