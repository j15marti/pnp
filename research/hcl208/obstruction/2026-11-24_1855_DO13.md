## A. Signed opposite-label neighbors on the exact same registry (PROVED)
Explicit finite monotone NAE-3SAT H, n used Boolean variables, m distinct triples, each with three distinct variables. Berge girth g means incidence graph has no cycle shorter than 2g. Let H be edge-critical non-2-colorable. For ANY owner e and ANY one of its three literal incidences a in e, flip that literal's sign, obtaining H^a. Every satisfying assignment of H-e must be monochromatic on e (or H would be SAT). It therefore satisfies the flipped NAE, and conversely every satisfying assignment of H^a satisfies H-e. Thus H UNSAT, every H^a SAT, with exactly #SAT(H-e) models. Global complement pairs these models, so their count is twice that of either one-sided CNF relaxation. This is a sign change rather than removal or addition of an owner, with no new variables or padding. The homogeneous observation planes U_e=span(e_u+e_v,e_u+e_w), incidence matching deficiency, primal graph and raw cut ranks are identical. RHS changes must NOT be discarded. DO09 gives an infinite ungrafted family with g=Omega(log n), raw width Omega(n), bounded degree (conditional on that peer theorem). DO11 adds an existential graft with Hall deficit >=2 for the root, hence >=2 for these sign neighbors too; its exact equality manifest is a legitimate escape. No efficient family generation is assumed.

For each sign assignment sigma, B_i(x) is the 0/1 indicator that the signed literals of owner i are all equal, x uniform on F2^n. For J a set of owners define M_sigma(J)=E prod_(i in J) B_i. Introduce an auxiliary bit z_i for the common signed value at each selected owner. The rejection conjunction is equivalent to the incidence equations x_v+z_i=sigma_iv. Every z_i is uniquely determined by x. If the selected incidence graph is a forest, it has 3|J| independent incidence equations on n+|J| variables, counting isolated original variables; consequently M_sigma(J)=2^(-2|J|)=4^(-|J|). For |J|<g all such graphs are forests, independent of signs. Inclusion-exclusion on any set of <g flags gives their full joint distribution as independent Bernoulli(1/4). In particular ALL indexed moments below order g match between H and H^a, although their zero-violation probabilities are 0 and >0.

No complete semantic closure is assumed. For any owner individually the accepting hull is full: fix the other two signed literals to opposite values and flip the remaining coordinate. The same holds for every conjunction of <g owners, by extending each local flip pair into disjoint incidence subtrees (DO12-A); signs only change prescribed polarities. This certifies stagnation of this owner-local or bounded-forest-group affine-hull closure at the unconditioned root and each signed neighbor. No stronger global deductions excluded.

## B. Falsify universal fixed-order moment extension (PROVED restricted)
Let P(B_1,...,B_m) be ANY real multilinear polynomial of degree k<g (coefficients arbitrary, not necessarily positive or uniform across instances). If P(0)=0 and P(b)>=1 for all nonzero Boolean b, then E_H P>=1 since H has no zero-violation word. By A, E_(H^a) P=E_H P>=1. Thus no estimator of that degree with universally valid Boolean-cube majorization can certify SAT by conditional expectation <1 on the unguarded signed SAT neighbor. This includes coefficients/forest/group selection adaptive to the signed source: apply the selected polynomial unchanged to the root only for the mathematical comparison. More generally validity on the union of the two actual flag supports suffices. A source-specific proof of positivity/zero-set restricted to the neighbor alone is NOT excluded. Any decision rule whose sole semantic input is the indexed moments of orders <=k and common topology (even adaptive queries and unlimited computation on returned data) cannot distinguish this pair. It is NOT a lower bound when the actual signs, guard probes or alternate source computations are allowed.

In BS12's unguarded forest special case p_i=1/4, q_ij=1/16 for all pairs if g>2. Every forest has at most m-1 edges; at the all-active pattern B_T >=(3m+1)/16. A path attains equality: its objective increases whenever a missing vertex is activated since degree<=2. Thus even the optimal forest cannot certify extension for m>=5. More generally group original owners into blocks of at most b; each group's rejection flag is OR of its member B_i, a multilinear polynomial of degree <=b. An estimator of degree t in those flags pulls back to degree <=bt on original flags. If bt<g and it majorizes the nonzero-rejection indicator on the entire cube, the same impossibility follows, even for adaptive overlapping groups whose union covers all owners. This blocks the direct bounded-order extension of BS12 on both DO09 and DO11 signed neighbors. It does not exclude genuine nonlocal projection, transformed charts, positive-hull payloads or source-specific positivity proofs. No full-portfolio obstruction.

## C. Exact first differing moment is polynomially discoverable (PROVED, constructive escape)
Let f=(a,e) be the changed incidence, and let L_f be the shortest cycle length in the bipartite incidence graph through f divided by two; infinity if no cycle. For arbitrary J, H's all-rejected system is consistent. For H^a the same equations have one RHS flipped if e in J. A parity-edge system is consistent iff every cycle has even XOR (assign potentials by spanning forest). Hence M changes precisely when the selected incidence graph contains a cycle through f. In that case the new moment is zero. Otherwise the number of components, and thus the number of solutions, is unchanged. In full generality for consistent selected signed system, intersection cardinality is 2^c, c number of components including unused original vertices, excluding unselected auxiliary owners. Every cycle through f contains exactly half its length in distinct owner vertices. Therefore the first differing degree is EXACTLY L_f, not just >=g. Remove f and BFS between its endpoints; shortest path length d gives L_f=(d+1)/2 and an explicit minimum distinguishing owner subset. Cost polynomial (linear graph scans for one f), no enumeration of all subsets. For a critical H no incidence is a bridge: if f were a bridge, flipping all variable and owner-potential coordinates on one side toggles only f's sign, a gauge equivalence preserving NAE feasibility, contradicting A. Alternatively all moments would coincide, including the full inclusion-exclusion count. So L_f is finite for every critical neighbor. This distinguishes a known pair, not an arbitrary SAT/UNSAT input: loose-cycle controls have arbitrarily large first differing order yet both labels SAT. A high moment degree cannot be interpreted as mandatory evaluation of binom(m,k) terms.

Exact count identity: with D_f={J: selected graph contains a cycle through f}, Z(H^a)-Z(H)=-sum_(J in D_f)(-1)^|J| 2^c(J). This follows by subtracting inclusion-exclusion expansions; it is not a polynomial counting algorithm, and no compulsory enumeration claim. A useful next target is compressed summation over these incidence-connectivity classes, using actual frontier or nonlocal projections, rather than expanding all monomials. A single arbitrary guard can break moment indistinguishability: fix x_a+x_b=0 on another literal b in e. Root's conditional owner rejection is 1/2, flipped owner's is zero.
