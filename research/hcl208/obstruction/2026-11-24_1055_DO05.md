## A. Exact opposite-label guards on the SAME source (PROVED)
For every ordinary simple edge-critical non-four-colorable graph H=(V,E), every edge e=uv, and each d in Q=GF(2)^2, put F_d = H-e with guard x_u+x_v=d. Each remaining edge rejects equality; colors are Q. Then F_0 is SAT and every F_d with d != 0 is UNSAT. A coloring of H-e exists by criticality, and necessarily x_u=x_v or it colors H. Conversely a coloring with nonzero difference would color H. Moreover each F_d, d != 0, is edge-minimal UNSAT relative to its guard: for every other edge f, any coloring of H-f satisfies e; an invertible linear map on Q takes its nonzero e-difference to d and preserves all ordinary inequalities. Thus deleting f gives an actual guarded witness. Gauge fixing x_u=0 by translation preserves both labels and all difference observations.

For DO04 H, these guarded neighbors have same retained graph with linear raw width, logarithmic girth and bounded degree. The guard is rank two. This is a strengthening of the neighboring construction to identical rejection-event structure with a different RHS, not a claim that guarded width itself equals raw width. Complete semantic closure of unguarded H-e would recover the forced equality x_u=x_v; that closure is not assumed free. Criticality is a promise for this mathematical family, not a freely supplied oracle or an efficiently recognized label certificate.

## B. Full low-order moment indistinguishability (PROVED)
Let g=girth(H). For any subset J of retained edges of size at most g-2, J union {e} is a forest. The map from vertex colors to the relative colors along these edges is surjective with fibers of equal size, by rooting each tree and assigning offsets along its edges. Conditional on any d, every word of relative colors on J occurs exactly 4^(n-1-|J|) times among 4^(n-1) guarded assignments. This holds for arbitrary signed rejection shifts on those retained edges (the opposite-label theorem specifically uses ordinary inequalities). It also holds after translation gauge x_u=0, with counts divided by 4. Thus the full joint distribution of ALL labeled rejection indicators on ANY such J is the product of Bernoulli(1/4), for every d. In particular every order <=g-2 intersection count agrees between the SAT and UNSAT guarded instances. The first-order individual-event affine closure sees only rank-two rejection events; their complements span the guard and add no affine facts. Same conclusion after pinning p additional distinct edge differences when p+|J|+1<g, provided the pins do not themselves enforce a violated constraint; this is a statement about affine counting, not global satisfiability under pins.

Corollary: any exact classifier whose sole input is (n,m, indexed moment table through order g-2) must give identical outputs on these guarded opposite-label instances. It cannot give correct decisions on both; a sound partial classifier must decline both. The classifier does NOT get the guard RHS or graph incidence beyond this table. An algorithm with original source access is not so restricted. Explicitly listing the full table can cost sum_{j<=g-2} binom(m-1,j), quasipolynomial for g=Theta(log n); the theorem is stronger than polynomial query indistinguishability only in this specified information channel, not a time lower bound. Connection: Whitney/chromatic-polynomial forest expansion, here stated for guarded full relative-color distributions rather than just coefficients.

## C. No DS04 tree even after disjoint logarithmic clusters (PROVED)
Partition retained edges into r nonempty disjoint clusters C_i, each of size <=b, and define the rejection event B_i = OR of the equalities in C_i. Assume r>=6 and max(2b,b+2)<=g-2. These cluster events are not generally affine; however their counts and pair intersections are exactly and cheaply computable by the forest theorem, so we grant an exact cluster acquisition backend rather than invoke a SAT oracle.

For every guard d, each B_i has relative mass p_i=1-(3/4)^|C_i|, and distinct B_i,B_j are independent because |C_i union C_j|<=2b. For any three distinct clusters i,j,h, pick one edge in i and one in j, assign both relative colors zero, assign every edge in h relative color 1, and retain e-difference d. This is a forest since its edge count <=b+3<g. It has an explicit assignment by forest propagation. Thus B_i and B_j occur while B_h does not. In ANY tree on r>=3 clusters choose a nonadjacent pair and an intermediate node h. The preceding assignment falsifies DS04's path condition. DS04's pointwise component-count proof applies to arbitrary events, so no tree is exact.

Even optimal Hunter dominance fails. Orient any tree from a maximum-p root; its sum of pair weights is at most p_max sum_{i != root}p_i, achieved by the star. Hence minimum upper bound U = p_max+(1-p_max)sum_{i != root}p_i >1, since p_i>=1/4 and r>=6. Lower second Bonferroni bound is <1: the independent product model with these marginals has nonzero probability all inactive, and satisfies the very same first and pair moments, so any valid lower bound from these moments is <=1-prod(1-p_i)<1. Both DS04 dominance tests and exact-tree certificate fail, on BOTH guard labels. The proof applies simultaneously to EVERY such disjoint partition and EVERY tree, not just the executed grouping. For DO04 asymptotic family can take b=floor((g-2)/2) for g>=6, which grows at least logarithmically; r>=6 eventually. Overlapping clusters, non-OR rewrites, clusters of greater size and other certificate types remain open. The result does not prohibit exact global coverage using higher-order or non-moment structure.

## D. MS10 uniform repair on the SAT neighbor is constrained by an e-cut (PROVED)
Let J be any nonempty subset of retained edges. Protect all outside relative-color maps P (as MS10 requires). If u,v are connected by an outside-edge path, then their difference is fixed by P. Pick f in J. A coloring w of H-f satisfies every outside edge and e, hence w supplies an actual possible outside word with nonzero e-difference. Any repair avoiding all J while preserving P would color H, impossible. Therefore the uniform existential projection of the SAT neighbor's group J onto all outside maps cannot be full unless J separates u and v. More generally, on the affine guard d!=0, every nonempty J has a forbidden fiber witnessed by a coloring of H-f normalized to d; this is criticality under guard, not just a failure of a numerical certificate. Even restricting external words to those satisfying outside edges cannot rescue the stated uniform-repair criterion.

DO04 established H has no edge cut of size <=3 (color both sides and translate one side to avoid at most three shifts); therefore every e-separating J in H-e has size >=3. All singleton and pair group deletions fail uniform repair on the unguarded SAT neighbor. This is not sufficient to characterize larger groups. On d=0 there may be uniform repair; no claim otherwise. The equality guard is explicitly protected. An algorithm may use the forced equality, an alternative projection, conditional repairs, or solve the target without uniform full-support recognition (S10). This is a stronger restriction than RT10's critical-UNSAT-only argument because it covers unguarded SAT neighbors for every group that does not disconnect the missing-edge endpoints.
