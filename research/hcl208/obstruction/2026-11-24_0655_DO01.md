## 2. Infinite connected exact-row family with SAT neighbors (PROVED)
For every g>=2 there exists a finite edge-minimal non-2-colorable 3-uniform hypergraph H of Berge girth >g. Here a 2-coloring forbids all-monochromatic hyperedges. Self-contained existence: take n vertices, independently include each triple with p=n^(-2+epsilon), epsilon=1/(2g). Every fixed binary coloring has Omega(n^3) monochromatic triples (balanced is asymptotically minimal), hence expected monochromatic included edges Omega(n^(1+epsilon)). Chernoff plus union bound over 2^n colorings shows with probability tending to one ALL colorings have Omega(n^(1+epsilon)) monochromatic included edges, with uniform constant. Expected number of Berge cycles of length ell in [2,g] is O_g(n^(2ell)*p^ell)=O_g(n^(epsilon ell)); choose ell vertices and at most one extra vertex per edge, overcounting harmless. Total expectation O_g(n^1/2). Markov gives at most n^3/4 short cycles with probability tending to one. Delete one hyperedge per short cycle; all colorings still fail. Extract an inclusion-minimal non-2-colorable subhypergraph and remove isolated vertices. This is an existential selection, NOT an efficient generator or solver.
The critical hypergraph is connected (else a proper component is noncolorable), every vertex has degree >=2 (a degree-one vertex can be colored after deleting its edge), and contains a Berge cycle (a Berge forest can be colored from a root outward). Hence its vertex count n'=n_H exceeds g, and its edge count m satisfies 3m>=2n'. Linearity follows from no length-two cycle. Thus arbitrarily large connected critical H exist. Relabel n'=n below; n means actual core vertices, not the random precursor.

For each hyperedge e={u,v,w} impose 1<=x_u+x_v+x_w<=2 on Boolean bits. This is exactly two distinct monotone clauses, one all positive and one all negative, with separate owner budgets 2 and unit occurrence costs x_v and 1-x_v. Apply RT05 path compiler: each vertex is one rank-one parity cycle with two private terminal-to-terminal paths, each of length deg(v), one edge per owner occurrence on the appropriate path. Parity forces exactly one whole path, giving bijective Boolean row choices. Cost of each owner is the corresponding literal-false count. No protected tags. Thus UNSAT. Deleting one hyperedge admits a coloring by edge minimality; it must be monochromatic on the deleted edge. By global complement symmetry one can arrange either monochromatic value. Relax ANY single owner of that edge from 2 to 3 and the corresponding coloring is a verified SAT lift. No topology or weights change. Witness existence is proved, not efficient discovery. All parameters below refer to explicit coefficient rows, not a strict wheel/physical matching compiler.
