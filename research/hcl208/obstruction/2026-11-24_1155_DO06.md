## A. Exact indexed-repair compiler (PROVED)
Let F(x) be a proper CNF, each clause nonempty of width <=3 on distinct variables, with n variables and m clauses. This covers ordinary proper 3SAT; remove tautological clauses or repeated literals in preprocessing, preserve declared variables for counts. Choose integer t>=2 polynomially bounded, introduce t protected switch bits s. Put q=n+t, r=ceil(log2(m+t)), L=2^r, and private binary index y in F2^r. List L slots: first m are source clauses, next t are singleton positive switch clauses, padding slots duplicate the last singleton. For each slot j and each literal ell in its clause, introduce one affine rejection event E_{j,ell}={y=j AND ell(x,s)=1}. All equations are explicit, no hidden constraints; each event rank r+1, external codimension one, pair external codimension <=2 when consistent, private rank r. Legal relation R is the complement of the union of events. Every event is rank>=2, so its complement has full affine hull.

For fixed y=j, legality is exactly falsifying the slot clause. Thus legal R is a disjoint union of L explicit nonempty affine cubes (each includes all r index pins). For z=(x,s), the entire fiber is missing iff every slot clause is true, i.e.
  NOT exists y R(x,s,y) iff F(x) AND s_1=...=s_t=1.
Both directions are immediate: a false clause gives its slot as a witness; every legal slot falsifies its clause. Consequently full protected support iff F is UNSAT. For every F, R itself is SAT, e.g. choose s_1=0 and its slot. Every missing word uniquely encodes an actual satisfying source assignment; #missing=#SAT(F), #distinct protected words=2^q-#SAT(F). Uniform repair recognition coNP-complete (a missing z verified by scanning all L slots); missing count #P-complete parsimoniously; projected count #P-complete under polynomial metric reductions by subtraction (in #P since each z has deterministic polynomial feasibility). This is not a hardness claim for R's ordinary decision.

Sparse compiler size O((m+t) log(m+t) log(n+t+r)) with indexed equations and explicit variables; dense representation remains polynomial. t may be 2 or e.g. n+2. r logarithmic in original encoded input. Event rank is logarithmic, NOT uniformly rank two. No strict E4/B4 or matching adapter is asserted.

## B. Complete affine closure and exact guarded counting remain polynomial (PROVED)
On ANY supplied affine guard G in (x,s,y), intersect G with each slot cube by Gaussian elimination. Sum 2^nullity over nonempty intersections, since index slots are disjoint. Keep one actual witness per nonempty intersection as needed. To compute exact affine hull of the union, take one anchor a, span all within-slot nullspace bases and all differences of slot anchors from a. Orthogonal complement gives every affine identity, not merely local propagation; empty union rejects. This is a paid complete affine forced-information closure and exact unary support; reapply on each new guard, retain the guard. No search branching is needed. The procedure also computes exact support/count for any prescribed external word, and lifts by one slot witness. If there is no guard, switch slot 1 and slot 2 give all external direction vectors in the combined span; every index slot nonempty, so the projection of the span to index space is full. Thus the full legal relation has full affine hull and no nontrivial unconditional affine identities even when projected support has holes.

For any rank k<t observation A z, every target in its affine image has a nonempty fiber meeting the legal projection. Otherwise that observation fiber, of dimension q-k, would be contained in s=all ones, of dimension q-t, impossible. Thus ALL k-bit affine observation supports are full for k<t, not only coordinate marginals. With t=n+2, this gives linear-order support indistinguishability. It does not imply equality of guarded counts. A guard s_i=0 immediately certifies uniform repair via its slot. Any prescribed z has a paid exact witness or a missing-fiber certificate. Full-support recognition across unrestricted z is still coNP-complete; full affine closure of R is not full semantic projection.

## C. Complete unconditioned rejection statistics identical across sign neighbors (PROVED)
For fixed slot j, its literal truth vector is independent uniform under uniform ambient x,s because distinct variable coordinates occur. Given y=j, every other event is false. The full indexed vector of all rejection indicators therefore has a distribution determined solely by slot lengths and fixed indexing, independent of source literal signs. All intersections of all orders and the entire distribution (at most 1+sum_j (2^|C_j|-1) distinct patterns) can be computed in polynomial time. Ordinary exact #R=sum_j 2^(q-|C_j|), independent of signs. Unconditioned distributions do not retain which external word produced them.

Take the eight 3-clauses covering the three-variable cube; F is UNSAT. Flip one literal of any one clause, leaving all indexed scopes unchanged: one original assignment now satisfies F, since the new clause duplicates another and removes one forbidden point. With t=2, both compiled R have 160 legal full assignments and identical complete raw joint rejection histogram, yet projected support sizes 32 and 31. All eight selected flips of first literal tested. More generally any proper minimally UNSAT CNF has SAT sign-flip neighbors by a deletion witness; RT11's growing critical family is eligible. On each pair, unconditioned statistics, exact affine hull, low-order projected support and source SAT label (R always SAT) are identical; uniform repair label differs. Infinite critical sign pairs follow e.g. RT11 clause replacement; no efficient recognition of minimal criticality assumed.

An exact classifier using ONLY the raw joint histogram, slot skeleton and full affine hull (not signs or external labels) cannot decide uniform coverage. A classifier with source signs or conditional external Walsh frequencies is not restricted to this information. In particular MS11 terms are cheaply acquired (external rank <=2) but their sufficient bound need not recognize all full-support instances. A complete such recognizer in this domain would solve coNP-complete UNSAT. No claim that every polynomial-time method must use this classifier.
