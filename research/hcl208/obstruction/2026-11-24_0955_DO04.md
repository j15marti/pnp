## A. Existence of a bounded-degree critical family (PROVED)
For all sufficiently large integers M, there exists a connected simple 5-critical graph H on n=Theta(M) vertices, maximum degree <=1000, girth Omega(log n), and treewidth Omega(n). Every H-e is four-colorable, has same vertices, girth at least as large, treewidth >=tw(H)-1, and maximum degree <=1000.

Proof. Use G(M,d/M), d=100, for M>d. Fix A=euler*(euler*d/3)^(3/2), alpha=min(1/10,1/(4A^2)). With probability tending to one no nonempty vertex set S of size s<=alpha M spans >=3s/2 edges. Indeed union bound for k=ceil(3s/2) gives binom(M,s)binom(binom(s,2),k)(d/M)^k <= [A sqrt(s/M)]^s, with impossible k cases contributing zero; using k>=3s/2 is valid because euler*d*s/(3M)<1. Sum for s<=sqrt(M) is o(1) by geometric bound A M^(-1/4); for larger s each term <=2^(-s). Also no independent set of size ceil(M/5) with probability tending to one, by 2^M exp(-d M/50+O(1)). Expected number of cycles of length <=g0=floor(log M/(4 log d)) is <=sum_{l=3}^{g0}d^l/(2l)=O(M^(1/4) log M); Markov gives at most M^(1/2) such cycles with probability tending to one. Expected number of vertices of degree >=1000 is <=M(euler*d/1000)^1000, hence with probability >0.99 fewer than M/20 such vertices. All properties coexist for sufficiently large M. Delete all these high-degree vertices and one vertex per short cycle. More than 4M/5 vertices remain, independence number <ceil(M/5) (indeed <=ceil(M/5)-1 < M/5). Four independent classes cannot cover remaining vertices. Select an inclusion-minimal non-four-colorable subgraph H and discard isolated vertices. H is 5-critical: every proper subgraph is four-colorable; adding a vertex back to a 4-coloring uses at most one new color. Minimum degree >=4 by greedy extension, connected by minimality. Local sparsity inherited; if n<=alpha M then 2n<=m<1.5n contradiction. Hence n>alpha M, n<=M, and 2n<=m<=500n. Girth >g0, so Omega(log n). This is an existential probabilistic proof, NOT an efficient graph-generation/criticality-discovery algorithm. No random host in the asymptotic parameter range was generated. Critical graph existence does not require solving its criticality in polynomial time for this counterexample to a universal structural assertion.

## B. Width lemma (PROVED, elementary)
For any graph H of minimum degree >=4 on >L vertices with e(S)<3|S|/2 for every nonempty |S|<=L, put k=floor(L/2)>=2. Then tw(H)+1 >= k/6.
Suppose a tree decomposition has bags of size q<k/6. Root its tree and assign each vertex to its highest occurrence bag (connectedness gives uniqueness). Take a deepest node v whose subtree has assigned weight >=k. Each child has weight <k; at most q assigned vertices lie in v itself, so total child weight >=k-q>k/2. Select child subtrees greedily until their assigned weight u is >=k/2; then u<3k/2. Let U be their assigned vertices and S the bag at v. U and S are disjoint; any neighbor of U outside U belongs to S by running intersection and edge coverage. Therefore all edges incident with U are in U union S, size <3k/2+k/6<=L. But 4u <=2 e(U union S)<3(u+|S|)<4u, contradiction. Apply L=floor(alpha M), tw(H)=Omega(M)=Omega(n). Adding one endpoint to every bag of a decomposition of H-e covers the deleted edge, so tw(H-e)>=tw(H)-1. No maximum-degree assumption needed for width lemma itself.

Both H and H-e are bridgeless: if H has an edge cut of size <=3, color each proper side and translate one entire side by GF(2)^2 to avoid at most three forbidden shifts, a contradiction. H-e thus has edge connectivity >=3. For bridgeless graphs graph branchwidth equals cycle-matroid branchwidth (Mazoit, Bandwidth of graphic matroids); treewidth+1 <= max(2, floor(3 branchwidth/2)) by standard middle-set bag construction. At a ternary node each vertex counted in the union of its three middle sets belongs to at least two, so bag size <=3bw/2; leaf bags cover endpoints. DA03's tensor observation width w=2(bw(matroid)-1) therefore Omega(n), for both labels, in both primal and dual representations. Under rank-r linear guard and DA03 local affine cascade, every nonrejected residual has w' >=w-2r. In particular all O(log N)-rank guards that survive that closure still have linear raw interface width (N explicit graph length O(n log n), or dense syndrome encoding polynomial in n). This is a structural width assertion, not a lower bound on number of reachable keys or arbitrary algorithms.

## C. Simultaneous barriers and exact neighboring deficiency (PROVED)
1. DO03's Fourier argument: any rank-r linear syndrome projection is fully supported for any p pinned distinct edges if 2^r-1<3^(g-p), even on H. Thus a range of ranks proportional to log n is excluded; NOT every O(log N) rank with arbitrary leading constant. Certified affine row-support closure is stagnant for p+1<g: every remaining row extends to the affine relaxation since remaining other columns span full syndrome. Arbitrary complete semantic closure not free.
2. DS03 full-span saturation of ANY nonempty original factor group is impossible on H by edge criticality. BA03 independent-switch saturation fails on both H and every H-e: for nonempty J, k'=components(G-J), rank C_J=|J|+1-k'. If k'=1 then 2rank>|J|. Otherwise H's >=4 edge boundaries imply at least 2k' original crossing edges; at most one deleted, so |J|>=2k'-1, hence 2rank-|J|>=1. Pairwise plane-intersection and period barriers of DO03 also hold for both. No claim that all other saturation certificates fail on H-e.

The deficit-zero transversal terminal cannot settle either side, though some SAT neighbors may admit an easier smaller positive-deficiency core. Unlike the Fano deficiency-one case, a single relaxation cannot produce zero deficiency on this family.
