## Theorem A: robust exact support spaces (PROVED)
Let f_i be explicit Boolean support predicates on d selector bits, each depending on a coordinate scope S_i of size <=s, each coordinate occurring in <=B scopes. Let W_i be its TRUE minimum linear observation space (span of its nonzero Walsh frequencies). Set r=max_i dim W_i <=s. Let Q=z0+ker H be ANY consistent affine chart, with h=dim H, arbitrary dense rows and arbitrary RHS. Let pi be quotient by H, equivalently restriction of linear functionals to ker H. Let V_i be the TRUE minimum support space of f_i restricted to Q (affine offsets understood).

Call i affected if W_i intersects H nontrivially; let b count affected factors. For every unaffected i, V_i=pi W_i: evaluations of W_i on Q are surjective onto the entire local observation space, and the same local truth function survives up to translation; its nonzero Fourier frequencies pull back injectively. This applies to nonlinear factors, not just affine ones. Always V_i subset pi W_i. To bound b, choose one nonzero row v_i in W_i intersect H for each affected i. Select a basis from these actual chosen rows; at most h basis rows, each with support size <=s. All chosen rows are supported on the union of these basis supports, of size <=sh. Each affected factor has a scope meeting that union. Thus b<=sBh. This does not assume sparse H itself.

For ANY factor cut L|R, define A=sum_L W_i, B0=sum_R W_i, and original overlap w(L)=dim(A intersect B0). Deleting affected factors from both sides loses at most r b in overlap: reducing A and B0 by codimensions a,b' reduces their intersection by at most a+b'. Then quotienting by H loses at most h in the intersection (new intersection may grow, not shrink further). Unaffected actual support spaces are exactly their images; adding back the affected restricted spaces can only increase overlap. Hence
  w_Q(L) >= w(L)-h-r b >= w(L)-(1+r s B)h.
This is for all cuts, simultaneously and every RHS. A restricted predicate can be constant zero, but this does not invalidate the width estimate for the entire retained registry; a certified zero factor is a paid UNSAT terminal and need not use a width algorithm.

Write w_* for minimum maximum overlap across all factor-leaf subcubic trees, and w_*(Q) for the analogous optimum of the ACTUAL conditioned support spaces. Applying the pointwise bound to any chosen conditioned tree proves w_*(Q)>=w_*-(1+r s B)h, with no common tree requirement. Deleting k arbitrary factors first lowers w_* by at most rk, whenever w_*>rk: extend any remaining tree by a subtree of the deleted leaves; each old cut increases by at most rk and each new internal deleted-subtree cut has overlap <=rk. Thus for all remaining trees,
  w_*(Q,remaining) >= w_*-rk-(1+r s B)h.
Degenerate empty/single-factor cases are vacuous if the right side is nonpositive. For raw full NAE planes an affected factor contains a selected pair-incidence row in H. In a linear hypergraph chosen edges are distinct, max degree <=D; incidence rank is sum of (vertices-1) over nontrivial components, at least number of selected edges / D. Thus b<=Dh, improving coefficient to 1+2D for the raw full registry (omit the one relaxed factor, charge it as deletion). This also covers RT15's intrinsic polar-plane control (all three possible nonzero directions), not merely RT14's fixed two factors: a common control hitting all m full planes needs h>=m/D.

## Theorem B: apply after EVERY independent-star choice (PROVED conditional on DO09/BH09)
DO09-A gives infinitely many linear critical NAE-3 hypergraphs, bounded degree D, Berge girth Omega(log n), primal treewidth Omega(n). Every one-owner relaxation SAT. BH09-C proves, for ANY independent center set S, exact star elimination yields full even-weight local support spaces on each surviving star boundary; untouched triple planes have true rank 2. Every factor tree has max actual overlap >=alpha n for some absolute alpha>0 for sufficiently large n. Its proof via clique-fill, graphic-matroid branchwidth and graph treewidth survives one owner relaxation by omitting at most one affected factor, losing O(D) in width. This omission is only for the rank lower bound, never a decision reduction. A projected factor has scope <=2D, and every surviving vertex appears in <=D projected scopes, since each can be charged to one of its original incident triples and buckets are disjoint. Thus s,r<=2D, B<=D (D>=2), giving C=1+4D^3. Uniformly over S, root or any single-owner neighbor, every choice of k anchor factors to remove, every consistent affine chart of rank h, every tree chosen after conditioning:
  w_*(Q,remaining) >= alpha n-2D k-C h-O(D).
The bound holds if cumulative local closure equations have already been included in H; it does not assert they cannot accumulate to linear rank, nor require them to be exported to another variant. No dense rows, different chart RHS, or adaptive tree ordering evade this inequality as long as actual retained factors are restrictions of this fixed projected registry. Further nonlinear projection or arbitrary factor replacement is outside its domain.

DS09 direct-sum decomposition: if quotient factors partition into jointly independent blocks, stitch their individual trees together; cross-block cuts have zero overlap, each intra-block cut has exactly its local overlap because outside blocks are independent of the whole block. Therefore some block has width at least the displayed lower bound; since each factor rank <=r, at least one block has >=(alpha n-2Dk-Ch-O(D))/r factors. This excludes the strategy 'small guard then all components small rank/width' on the SAME family, even allowing a distinct decomposition on every guard. Does not exclude a large component solved by a different paid terminal (packets, moment, matching, flow, special nonlinear structure).

## Theorem C: conditional width/cover tradeoff (PROVED)
Fix a nonempty anchor legal set A that is the product of k selected Boolean regional predicates, before the remaining factors are solved. It depends on at most rk observations; its density is >=2^(-rk) in the full live selector chart. Suppose an explicit family of affine charts covers A, and EVERY nonempty chart has an exact remaining-factor tree of width <=w (possibly different per chart). A chart of codimension h has density 2^(-h). From B each chart has h>=Hmin=max(0,ceil((alpha n-rk-O(D)-w)/C)). Union bound gives ell>=2^(Hmin-rk). Hence for k=o(n), w=o(n), there must be 2^Omega(n) explicit chart terms. This is a lower bound on this exact full-cover normalization, not on solving SAT. In particular an UNSAT branch can be discharged before it gets low width, a SAT branch can stop at its first witness, certified deductions can shrink the necessary set (the post-closure charts need not cover the entire anchor), and factored/signed covers or symbolic guard families need not enumerate terms. All these escapes remain legal. A low-rank input guard followed by large-rank closure is not ruled out: the theorem charges final accumulated rank, not just the initial anchor rank. No global semantic closure assumed.
