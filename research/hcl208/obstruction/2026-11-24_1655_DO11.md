## 2. DO11-A: unmatched-port extension lemma (PROVED)
Domain: finite explicit homogeneous signed Boolean NAE factors, each on distinct variables, no hidden guards or consumers. Let a SAT gadget G have an incidence matching M saturating all its factors. Every assignment to ANY subset of M-unmatched variables extends to a legal word of G.
Proof: take a satisfying word, and for each matched pivot u choose one other literal v with opposite signed truth. The chosen arcs u->v carry parity p=1 XOR s_u XOR s_v. Each matched vertex has one outgoing edge, each unmatched vertex none. Each functional component has a directed cycle (consistent under the original witness) or a unique unmatched sink. Keep cycle colors; independently set all unmatched sinks as requested; propagate colors backward on chosen edges. All selected parity relations and hence all factors hold. No two unmatched sinks can be in the same functional component. This proves surjective joint support on the entire unmatched set. Given a witness, propagation is polynomial; otherwise BH10 supplies a polynomial witness procedure on this domain. Never fix an arbitrary matched port or export such choices as unconditional deductions.

Corollaries for a SAT gadget: (i) if its projected two-port relation is exactly equality (or signed disequality), then any factor-saturating matching consumes at least one port; (ii) if a factor gadget has a proper nonempty relation on its ports, any saturating matching consumes at least one port. Otherwise every assignment to all those ports would extend. This is a semantic requirement on exact local source gadgets, not a syntactic counting assumption. Multiple equality ports cannot all be left unmatched: at most one may be unmatched if they are all synchronized. Dense additional affine guards, integer quotas, protected matched ports and arbitrary non-NAE factors are excluded unless separately encoded.

## 3. DO11-B: Hall deficit cannot decrease under tree-local splitting (PROVED)
Define delta(F)=#factors minus maximum incidence matching size, equivalently the maximum number of factors in excess of their neighbor set. Source F is any signed NAE formula with proper nonempty factor relations on distinct ports; one-sided clauses may be homogenized with ONE actual reference variable first (BH10). Consider any exact compiled signed NAE network G with the following disjoint interiors:
(a) each original factor has its own proper nonempty exact local replacement, with one distinct occurrence port per original incident variable;
(b) all copies of each original variable (possibly with extra relay hubs) are connected by a TREE of satisfiable two-port equality/disequality gadgets, whose port relation exactly synchronizes copies up to known signs; each occurrence port belongs to only its original variable tree. No other shared private variables or extra unregistered constraints.
Then delta(G) >= delta(F).

Proof: take ANY maximum matching of G. Mark a gadget damaged if at least one of its internal factor owners is unmatched. Let d_f,d_w count damaged factor and wire gadgets; d_f+d_w<=delta(G). Every clean source factor gadget uses at least one occurrence port internally (DO11-A); choose one such port per clean factor, all distinct by the matching. Every clean wire uses at least one of its two endpoint ports internally; these occupied ports are distinct and unavailable to chosen source factors. For a variable tree with t port/relay vertices and b damaged wires, the t-1-b clean edges consume >=t-1-b distinct vertices. At most b+1 vertices remain for chosen source factors. Keep at most one chosen factor at each original variable, discarding at most b. Across all variable trees this loses <=d_w clean factors. Discard all <=d_f damaged factor gadgets as well. Remaining chosen factor-variable edges are an original incidence matching of size at least m-d_f-d_w. Hence delta(F)<=d_f+d_w<=delta(G). Cyclic binary wire networks containing a spanning tree have the same bound by ignoring non-tree gadgets. For a single arbitrary multiport synchronization gadget of arity t per variable, damage can release at most t-1 additional chosen ports; for source max degree D, delta(G)>=delta(F)/max(1,D-1) by the analogous counting (when no extra occurrence copies are counted as source ports).

Thus merely copying wheels/variables and restoring equality through disjoint exact signed NAE gadgets CANNOT turn any Hall-deficient source into a Hall-saturable instance for BH10, regardless of private gadget size. This falsifies a local universal compiler lemma. It does not exclude globally coupled hub gadgets, changes of factor relation, nonaffine projected quotients, or another algorithm. If a binary wire admits a saturating internal matching leaving either endpoint free (Fano-minus-line is one), and factor gadgets are unchanged singleton factors, one can also lift any source matching to the tree: root the wire tree at the matched occurrence, match each wire using its endpoint farther from the root and private variables, leaving the root free, and match original selected factors there. Therefore equality holds for these transparent wires, including the executed K5 Fano-wire control. Check the required wire matching via ordinary bipartite matching, not semantic inference.

## 4. DO11-C: simultaneous high-girth, linear raw width, Hall-deficient critical family (PROVED conditional on prior DO09 theorem)
Dense equality gadget D on ports a,b and three private bits p,q,r: six NAE triples, each port with each of the three private pairs. Every assignment to p,q,r has a majority pair; both ports must be opposite that majority, hence equal. Equal ports extend by all private bits opposite. Deleting any one triple permits unequal ports: choose exactly its private pair as majority, remaining private bit opposite, set its port to majority and other port opposite. The deleted triple is the only monochromatic one. Complement gives either orientation. Thus D is minimal for equality, has n_D=5,m_D=6 and surplus m_D-(n_D-1)=2.

Let H be one DO09 critical core, n active vertices, m factors, bounded degree D0, girth g=Omega(log n), primal treewidth Omega(n). Its fused NAE event rows have total rank n-1 by connectedness. DY10's criticality theorem gives m>=n. Choose one edge e with terminals a,b,c; remove it and extract an inclusion-minimal subset K forcing a=b (DO02). This extraction is EXISTENTIAL, not an efficient generator. K is SAT, both equal words extend, deleting any K edge permits unequal ports in both orientations. Delete isolates; K is connected, since irrelevant components can be removed and different components containing a and b can be independently complemented. Its internal girth >=g; any path between a,b uses >=g-1 hyperedges, since adding e closes a Berge cycle. K plus the single rank-one NAE2(a,b) is minimally UNSAT, so DY10 gives m_K+1>=n_K; hence m_K-(n_K-1)>=0.

Stretch all 18 incidences of D using independent copies of K, replacing the variable at each incidence with a fresh occurrence linked by K. Call resulting two-port gadget D*. It remains exactly equality and inclusion-minimal for that relation: for a deleted copied D triple lift the corresponding D deletion witness; for a deleted internal K edge use the D witness violating only the associated triple and flip the corresponding occurrence via its broken-wire witness. All other K wires extend intact. No new variable identification except named ports. It has
 n_D* = 5+18(n_K-1), m_D* = 6+18m_K,
 m_D*-(n_D*-1) = 2+18[m_K-(n_K-1)] >=2.
All short cycles are eliminated: a cycle wholly in K has girth >=g; otherwise it must cross a K from port to port (copied D triples alone are disjoint), and use at least one additional hyperedge. Hence girth >=g. The degree bound is constant in D0.

Now graft D* into ONE incidence of H: replace that occurrence by a fresh variable and connect it to the original variable through D*. The new H' is UNSAT by exact equality decoding and edge-critical by the same deletion-witness argument. Every one-budget relaxation has a SAT witness of either formerly monochromatic orientation. Its sizes are
 n'=n+n_D*-1 <= 19n (since n_K<=n),
 m'=m+m_D*,
 m'-n' >= (m-n)+2 >=2.
Degrees remain bounded, girth >=g=Omega(log n'), and n' grows unboundedly. The unchanged H factors except one retain raw algebraic width Omega(n): from DO09/DO10, removing one rank-two factor reduces optimal cut width by at most 2. Restrict ANY factor-leaf decomposition of H' to those unchanged leaves; adding other factor spaces only increases cut intersection rank. Hence optimal actual raw width of H' is Omega(n'). Dropping one additional modified owner for a SAT neighbor loses at most another constant. Alternatively the original pair graph minus that triple remains a linear-treewidth subgraph up to constant additive width. DO10's bounded-scope conditioning inequality applies to this raw registry: sublinear cumulative guard rank preserves linear width. The one-budget neighbor retains all unaffected triples and the changed factor is rank three; omit it for the lower-bound calculation. No claim about all connected nonlinear projections or independent-star projections after the graft without new proof.

BH10's raw incidence matching now declines on BOTH labels: root delta>=m'-n'>=2; one-sided relaxation homogenized with one common reference bit has delta>=m'-(n'+1)>=1. BD10's full independent transversal also declines because rank <=n'-1 (root) or <=n' (neighbor) <m'. Root DY10 canonical core is full by edge criticality, not a claimed lower bound on its resolution. Neighbor core may peel and become easy. Each unconditioned full NAE triple (or homogenized relaxed NAE4) has full local affine hull, so the stated single-factor parity/affine closure repertoire is stagnant; no claim about stronger global closure, LP after graft, SDP, moments, all peer terminals or physical E4/B4. The family is existential, not a diagonal hard sequence.

Honest escape: if the exact equality manifest is supplied and paid, project D* to equality, merge the copies, and return to H. For m=n roots, BH10 solves H; for broken-wire neighbors one can use a source deletion witness, and BH10 can solve the relaxed H when its Hall promise holds. Even without the manifest, another terminal may discover these structures. Thus the theorem establishes a simultaneous RAW registry obstruction against width/conditioning and Hall-saturation, and a general local compiler invariant, not an unrestricted hardness claim.
