## Theorems
Let m owners each have rank-two observation plane U_i. Suppose for every owner set J with |J|<=s0, raw rank R(J)>=3|J|/2. RT26 supplies this with s0=floor(delta n) and s0/m bounded below: on every connected component of J of size t>=4, R=|V|-1>7t/4-1>=3t/2; components of size <=3 are incidence forests for sufficiently large girth and have R=2t. Sum over components.

For arbitrary dense affine guard with independent rowspace H rank r, let d_H(J)=|J|-dim((H+sum U_i)/H). It is supermodular and d_H(J)<=r-|J|/2 on small sets. Let k=max(0,2r-2), assume 2k<=s0 and s0>=1. A minimal positive-deficiency set D of size <=s0 has d_H(D)=1 (delete one owner, rank cannot increase), |D|<=k. These sets are precisely the circuits of the Rado owner matroid (independent owner subsets admit independent representative rows in the quotient, via matroid intersection). Their union K over all such small circuits has |K|<=k. Indeed add circuits one by one; if not contained, intersection is a proper subset of a circuit and has d<=0, so supermodularity preserves positive deficiency; size of the next union <=2k<=s0 and hence <=k. If none, K is empty. For any J of size<=s0 the DS15 smallest maximizing set C_J is the union of the matroid circuits in J: deletion decreases nullity iff the element is not a coloop, equivalently belongs to a circuit. Thus C_J subset K.

For any actual guard functional b on H, every small subregistry has a satisfying extension iff the single K has one. If K satisfiable, each C_J is satisfied by the same word and the DS15 independent representative lift keeps H and all C_J observations (rank-zero vacuous owners may be retained as loops in this combinatorial extraction; a forbidden whole fiber is a sound contradiction). K itself is small. This does not imply simultaneous extension to the full registry.

Discovery: if k=0 use empty K. Otherwise partition the m owner IDs into B=min(m,ceil(2mk/s0)) near-equal groups; enumerate unions of min(k,B) groups. Each union has size <=s0 (if B=m singleton groups; otherwise k ceil(m/B)<=s0/2+k<=s0). Every circuit of size<=k is contained in a union. Compute DS15 core in each union via at most |J|+1 deterministic matroid intersections and take their union. Forward follows from local feasibility; reverse pick an assignment satisfying K_H' under b, possible because K_H' is small; its H' word is in P_H'. Hence simultaneous affine-scope local supports survive arbitrary bounded-rank dense linear observations and restriction, not just coordinate scopes.
