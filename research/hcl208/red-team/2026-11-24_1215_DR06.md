## A. Precise falsification of arbitrary affine-guard transfer
M12 is correct for its fixed independent chart and prefix pins. We test a stronger proposed integration rule, not a claim M12 actually made. Let M be the number of words in the current affine chart, F_i the exact count of bad event E_i, and L_ij=F_i F_j/M if each shared free bit has a common monotone direction (or one event is irrelevant to it), otherwise max(0,F_i+F_j-M). Credit D=M-sum F_i+max_T sum L_ij where T ranges over forests. D>0 certifies a legal word; D<=0 is UNKNOWN. At root and after each reset the maximum is found by exact Kruskal on nonnegative scaled integer weights M L_ij.

Use six independent bits x0,...,x5. Seven bad events, each a conjunction of the displayed equalities:

## B. Exact signed forest credit is affine-chart independent (PROVED)
Fix a forest T over event IDs. For each source word x let J(x) be the set of active bad events. Define w_T(x)=1-|J(x)|+|E(T[J(x)])| = 1-number of components of T[J(x)]. Empty induced forest has zero components. For a legal word this equals 1; for an illegal word it is <=0. For any guard G,
 C_T(G)=|G|-sum_i |G intersect E_i|+sum_(ij in T)|G intersect E_i intersect E_j|
       =sum_(x in G) w_T(x) <= #legal(G).
For every disjoint exhaustive partition G=union G_b, C_T(G)=sum C_T(G_b), with no assumption of product measure or independent chart. Maximizing over forests on each child preserves >=. Moreover if certified closure removes only illegal words then C_T cannot decrease: every removed word had nonpositive weight. Thus a positive exact certificate survives sound pruning and has a positive child after every exhaustive disjoint split. This is a positive-witness certificate, NOT an upper work bound, and does not prove UNSAT when nonpositive.

Paid special domain: G and every E_i explicitly encoded affine systems over GF(2). Each event count and pair count is exactly zero if inconsistent, otherwise 2^(n-rank), by Gaussian elimination on stacked rows. No adaptive chart or assignment enumeration. The result also holds for events represented as authenticated polynomial-size DISJOINT unions of affine cubes: intersections of pieces from each pair are disjoint; sum their exact counts. Disjointness can be verified pairwise by Gaussian inconsistency. For a pair of general overlapping unions this simple sum is not justified. For implicit M12 bundles the adapter must be acquired and verified, not assumed. A hybrid forest can retain exact pair edges where cheap and Harris edges on fixed product-prefix refinements; for an arbitrary affine refinement the exact-edge transport proof does not extend to unrecertified approximate edges.

For the six-bit counterexample the optimal exact forest credit is 12 at root, 6 in each child, even retaining the same root exact tree. Exact count lower bound therefore restores a constructive lift without chart resets. This does not equal the actual count 14. At a positive guard choose one exact positive child under each coordinate pin, rerun sound closure, and verify a complete source word. At most n successful pins, two child count evaluations each, and at most n strict coordinate deductions across a witness path. Additional independent constraints must be included before the certificate is interpreted as a global witness.

## C. Product-subcube repair has an exact exponential capacity (PROVED)
Let G={x:Ax=b} nonempty, rank r, with t nonzero columns of A (in the OLD product chart). Every axis-aligned subcube contained in G must fix all t active coordinates: flipping an active free coordinate changes Ax. There are exactly 2^(t-r) distinct feasible assignments on those t coordinates, each admitting the entire free zero-column subcube. Therefore the minimum number of axis-aligned subcubes covering G is exactly 2^(t-r), even for overlapping covers. For a dense single parity equation on n bits this is 2^(n-1), despite rank one. Restoring a prefix-certificate proof by explicitly listing these subcubes is not universally polynomial. This is a representation bound, not a lower bound for Gaussian guard evaluation, symbolic transport, or unrestricted SAT. Small support t, or special paid event pairs, provides a valid escape; a positive global M12 root can also simply finish in its original chart before any unnecessary split.
