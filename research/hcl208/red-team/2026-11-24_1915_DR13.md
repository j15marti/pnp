## A. Signed one-incidence sensitivity (PROVED)
For every explicit nonempty 3-CNF F on n>=1 declared variables (clauses of length 1..3 allowed), construct two signed NAE-3SAT sources H and H' differing in exactly one literal sign, such that both have readily constructible satisfying assignments, their variable/owner registry is identical, their model sets are nested, and
  #H' - #H = 2 #F.
Here all physical NAE variables, including auxiliaries, are counted; no projection oracle. Empty inputs can be handled directly. Include unused declared variables in the count.

Construction: reference bit r, input relative bits x_i XOR r, and one fresh relative selector u. Relative literal truth uses r as zero; physical complement of all variables preserves every NAE owner. A fresh AND output z=a AND b is uniquely encoded by NAE(a,b,not z), NAE(not z,a,r), NAE(not z,b,r). When r=0, the last two are (not z OR a),(not z OR b), and the first forces z=1 when a=b=1 and z=0 when a=b=0. Mixed inputs force z=0. OR by De Morgan; NOT by literal polarity. If two input literals share a variable, use a fresh copy of one signed literal with NAE(a,not z,r), NAE(not a,z,r), uniquely forcing z=a relative to r. This ensures all NAE triples have distinct variable IDs. Build ordinary circuit evaluating F to a signed output literal t, without constraining t=1. Append b=(not t OR u) as a signed literal from a fresh gate. Include final owner NAE(u,b,r) in H, NAE(not u,b,r) in H'. For each r, x, u the gate registry has exactly one extension. H' accepts every such word because b>=u implies (not u OR b). H accepts all except u=0,t=1. Thus #H'=2^(n+2), #H=2^(n+2)-2#F. Set u=1 and any x, evaluate gates, choose either r for a common explicit SAT witness. H subset H' pointwise. The extra models encode F's models one-to-two exactly, not merely via a loose bound.

Consequences: exact delta is #P-hard via one metric reduction; nonzero delta and non-equivalence are NP-hard even with both labels and common witness supplied. Non-equivalence is in NP (exhibit an assignment violating equality); equivalence is coNP-complete on the constructed promise domain. The promise of nested known-SAT inputs is guaranteed by construction. No unconditional superpolynomial lower bound follows. #P-hardness does not mean that P=NP would automatically yield polynomial exact counting.

## B. Monotone-root strengthening (PROVED)
The same barrier applies to DO13's exact changed-incidence connectivity sum for arbitrary MONOTONE roots, not merely signed roots. Replace every negative occurrence of each physical variable p by one new variable v_p, constrained to be its complement using a disjoint-private constant gadget. Take Fano triples 123,145,167,246,257,347,356, delete 123 and identify 1=p,2=q,3=w, with q,w and 4..7 fresh. The six remaining triples force p=q=w: otherwise a satisfying assignment of the six would color all seven, impossible. For clarity, the Fano plane is not 2-colorable: a majority color of size >=5 has too many pairs to assign their third vertices to the <=2 minority vertices, each of which can pair at most floor(size/2) majority pairs. For size 4, its six pairs must use all six possible majority-pair slots at the three minority vertices; each majority vertex's three lines are then exhausted, so the three minority vertices form a line. Once p=q=w is fixed, all six pairs of the four internal vertices are constrained not to be both equal to p. Zero or one of those four may equal p, giving exactly 5 extensions. Add NAE(p,q,v_p), forcing v_p != p. Every fixed p has exactly five gadget extensions; v_p is unique. Private vertices of different gadgets are disjoint. All triples remain distinct-variable monotone NAE. If k complement gadgets are used, every original physical assignment has exactly 5^k lifts. Keep the final u incidence positive in H, flip only that incidence in H'. The output H0 is monotone, H1 is a one-sign neighbor, and
  #H1 - #H0 = 2*5^k*#F.
Both are easy SAT, nested, with polynomial witness reconstruction (choose u=1, evaluate gates, then a valid private word per gadget). k is explicitly known, and 5^k has O(k) bits. This construction is polynomial and all labels are authenticated. DO13's monotone-root signed inclusion-exclusion identity applies exactly: only subsets with a cycle through the changed incidence contribute to this delta, since all original rejection parity systems are consistent. Thus a universally polynomial evaluator for that connectivity sum on arbitrary monotone roots would yield FP=#P (for the usual counting class formulation), hence P=NP. Even deciding whether the sum vanishes is coNP-hard. This is NOT a hardness theorem for DO13's high-girth edge-critical UNSAT roots; the gadgets introduce short cycles and the roots here are SAT. No strict tetravalent physical compiler is used.
