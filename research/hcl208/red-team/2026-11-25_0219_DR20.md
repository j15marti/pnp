## PROVED: affine-slice mode-support hardness, even for nonnegative constant-volume factors
Given a 3-CNF F on occurring variables lambda_i, each clause of width k=3 has literal truth bits l_j(lambda) and falsity bits f_j=1+l_j. Homogenize f_j to a linear form f_j(lambda,s) by replacing its constant by s. For each clause introduce independent primal bits v_0,...,v_k and u_0,...,u_{k+1}. Set
Q_C(lambda,s;u,v) = u_0*s*(v_0+1) + sum_{j=1}^k u_j*(s*v_j+f_j(lambda,s)*v_{j-1}) + u_{k+1}*s*v_k
over F2. Q=sum_C Q_C is a quadratic primal polynomial, linear in (s,lambda), hence a coefficient family q_0,...,q_n in the exact GD27 format. For s=1, summing u independently yields 2^(k+2) times the number of v satisfying v_0=1, v_j=f_j*v_{j-1}, v_k=0. The triangular recurrence has exactly one v if the clause holds and none otherwise. Thus each local Gauss factor is 0 or 2^(k+2), and the full product on s=1 is 2^A [F(lambda)], A=sum_C(k+2). For s=0 the equations in v are homogeneous, so every factor is positive; there is no signed cancellation anywhere. In particular the exact slice sum divided by 2^A counts F; testing slice nonzero or finding a nonzero mode is NP-complete/search-SAT, and exact normalized slice sum is #P-complete. Membership uses one supplied mode evaluated by Gaussian elimination; the reductions are explicit and uniform. Every block has 2k+3=9 primal bits, at most four dual observations (s and three source coordinates), and the full factorization is supplied, not searched. The coefficient basis is independent if each variable occurs: each lambda_i has a private monomial u_j v_{j-1} in an occurrence, and s has u_0 v_0 unique to itself. All nonzero combinations have a quadratic term, so coefficient-span affine closure acquires no equation. The primal zero system is always SAT (all u=0), even with full affine hull: all v directions occur with u=0; for each isolated u coordinate set v_0=1 for u_0, set the two relevant v bits to zero for an interior u_j, or set v_k=0 for the final u. These are actual primal solutions; subtracting u=0 words gives every u direction. Consequently even complete semantic affine closure would not remove a dimension of this constructed primal system. The guard s=1 is a dual mode slice, not a primal pin; never confuse the two. No reduction of SAT decision to decision of the unshifted primal system is claimed. Indeed direct primal SAT is trivial. This establishes a barrier to universal polynomial *slice acquisition/aggregation*, not to every zero-preserving signed decomposition or unrestricted SAT. For completeness orthogonality gives Z(q0=0)-Z(q0=1)=2^(A-n) #F with q_i=0 for i>0 (interpret powers rationally if necessary); counting difference is not a decision reduction. No need to compute this difference in a decision-only solver.
