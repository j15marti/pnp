## 2. DR08-A: uniform disabled-fiber coverage (PROVED)
Let the mandatory directed arcs be fixed. Optional arc e is enabled iff its local word y_e in GF(2)^{a_e}, a_e>=1, equals h_e. All local words together must satisfy sum_e P_e y_e=sigma. Initially obtain P by Gaussian elimination on the common affine observation map (GD14); after reductions retain the exact affine syndrome equation, with its RHS. The allowed activated sets are downward-closed (in particular s-t nonreachability).
For a nonempty group J define P_J as horizontal concatenation, U_J=im P_J, r_J=rank P_J, and
    k_e=a_e+rank P_(J without e)-r_J, e in J.
Then k_e is the dimension of the projection onto block e of ker P_J: its image consists exactly of local vectors v with P_e v in im P_(J without e). Its kernel within the full kernel consists of vectors supported on the other blocks, so subtract their nullities to obtain the formula.
For every t in U_J, the fiber P_J y=t is nonempty and uniform. Its e-block marginal is uniform on an affine space of dimension k_e. Probability that e is enabled is either zero or 2^{-k_e}. Therefore if
    rho(J)=sum_{e in J}2^{-k_e}<1,
every syndrome t in U_J can be realized with ALL arcs of J disabled. Proof: union bound, strictly positive probability of no enabled arc. Equivalently the sumset of disabled syndrome images equals U_J. No statistical independence assumed. A singleton with nontrivial ker P_e is a special case. Two injective blocks with image intersection dimension >=2 are another; three blocks with k_e>=2 each also suffice.
Exact deterministic lift: count L of solutions of P_J y=t under any prefix via Gaussian elimination. For each e count L_e with y_e=h_e under the same prefix. Initially L-sum_e L_e>0. This integer credit splits additively into the two possible next-bit credits. Select one with positive credit at every bit. The final complete word has L=1, sum L_e=0. O(sum a_e) iterations, O(|J|) Gaussian counts per bit, exact integers of O(sum a_e) bits, no enumeration of local words. This is witness acquisition, not a global forced-information deduction.

## 4. Sharp failures and geometry (PROVED)
The strict inequality cannot be weakened to <=1. Take two parallel optional s-t arcs, P_1 columns (a,b), P_2 columns (b,c) for independent a,b,c, h_1=h_2=00. k_1=k_2=1, rho=1. For target b the only compatible local pairs are (00,10) and (01,00), with local order as written; one arc enabled in either pair. UNSAT. Target 0 is SAT via both blocks choosing their b-coordinate. Both rank-two guards remain surjective in their affine chart; GD14 singleton rank-one closure cannot resolve them. This is a counterexample to a proposed safe elimination, not a lower bound.
For an authentic unguarded signed four-color difference graph, a cut of k<=3 edges can be removed and reinserted: independently color both sides, translate all colors on one side by a common two-bit value, and avoid at most k forbidden translation values. This is also DR08-A: simultaneous two-bit shifts of cut-edge words lie in ker P_J (all other observed differences unchanged), so every k_e>=2 and rho<=k/4<1. For a protected affine chart the same proof requires that both independent side shifts remain in its homogeneous kernel and no protected integer/occupancy condition is changed. This generalizes the low-degree/bridge escape to authenticated small edge cuts. In an edge-critical non-four-colorable ordinary graph, any nontrivial edge cut has >=4 edges; otherwise proper subgraphs on each side are four-colorable and glue. Four parallel exclusions between a pair of vertices, forbidding all four differences, are UNSAT; replacing one forbidden color by a duplicate is SAT. Same syndrome rank 6, no group of <=3 passes (the proper-group kernel is zero); all four together have rho=1. Strict boundary is real. Parallel-edge signed example is not a simple high-girth critical graph. On DO08 large-width raw registry this excludes a class of small-cut obstructions, not the whole family.
Arbitrarily large raw delta can be irrelevant: two parallel s-t arcs with local widths d, P_1=P_2=I_d, h=0, sigma a fixed nonzero word. For d>=2, rho=2/2^d<1, both can be disabled; one group quotient reduces rank d to zero. This is a valid affine chart y_2=y_1+sigma. Tests through d=128. Does not show every raw high-rank family has such groups. DA08's multicolored-clique construction uses rank-one disjoint parity groups; within a parity group, two or more members yield k_e=1, while singleton k_e=0. No nonempty group can satisfy rho<1 (if any group has >=2 members it contributes >=1; else singleton contributes 1). Thus the rule does not bypass DA08-A's W[1]-hardness. Dependency components remain coupled by graph topology; DP08 product component credit cannot be transferred freely. BW08 protected zero-state requirements must be represented by their authenticated guarded predicates before this rule; never delete a shared hub or a nonzero pin by analogy. S13/BS07H exact quotas/matching tags remain outside the theorem unless a new exact joint lift proves preservation.

## 5. Diagonal and aggregate audit
For one fixed total exact portfolio A, not in polynomial time means forall K,c exists F with T_A(F)>K |F|^c. Since finitely many inputs have length <=L and finite runtimes, choose K large enough to exclude all such inputs and choose c=j; obtain increasing-length F_j with T_A(F_j)>|F_j|^j. This is an existential sequence, not an efficient sampler or a certificate of universal hardness. With fair charged simulation, a fixed terminal whose complete work (including compiler, discovery, verification and lifts) is bounded by a fixed polynomial on an input class cannot cover infinitely many sufficiently late sequence members. This conclusion applies to the new residual terminal only if it is actually scheduled with polynomial overhead, not merely mentioned in a report. In the declared domain, delta_res=O(1) or w_res+delta_res=O(log N) on an infinite subsequence would contradict simultaneous escape once implemented. Raw delta or local width does not suffice.
A fixed inefficient portfolio can fail even if P=NP: take a total exact brute-force solver delayed by 2^N steps on every input. Non-polynomial portfolio runtime does not quantify over all uniform solvers. Pointwise existence of a terminal exponent/index per input is vacuous; require fixed caps and acquisition or an explicitly uniform schedule with a proven aggregate bound. An enumerator of arbitrary candidate algorithms does not have a free correctness filter, especially for UNSAT. No diagonal contradiction established.
For a branching proof, an integer credit B=mu^c bounded by a fixed polynomial in original encoded length and sum child B<=B(parent)-1 bounds the number of charged tree jobs; each expansion and edge needs polynomial paid work, no state length blowup, and terminal reconstruction/aggregation separately reserved. DAG reuse only for identical authenticated contracts (including query and guards); consumers paid individually. A polynomial-bit integer may be exponential in numerical value. DP08's existing counterexample to weighted SAT-child contraction is not repaired by a unary deletion rule. Our new normalization has a polynomial unary budget and strictly sufficient terminal cap, not a universal weighted branch theorem. A complete proof of universal bounded residual capacity, or another paid terminal on every residual, remains OPEN.
