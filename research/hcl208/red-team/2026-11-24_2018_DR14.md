## A. Zero raw cut width but exponential joint negative support (PROVED)
For k independent systems B_i, give system i exterior bits a_i,b_i and private bits p_i,q_i. Its four rows are:
 a_i=1 => p_i=0; a_i=1 => p_i=1;
 b_i=1 => q_i=0; b_i=1 => q_i=1.
All private bits are distinct, all guards are rank-one, no zero private rows are needed. Let R_i(a_i,b_i)=exists p_i,q_i B_i. Exactly R_i=NOT a_i AND NOT b_i. Thus the simultaneous negative query is
 C_k(a,b)=AND_i NOT R_i=AND_i(a_i OR b_i).
Source length O(k log(k+2)) with binary variable IDs; pointwise fixed-fiber Gaussian checks and direct certificate reconstruction polynomial. No native four-valent compiler asserted.

Order the complete a-side events before the b-side events. At the central cut, raw all-primal observation spaces are disjoint (all a,p coordinates versus all b,q); intersection rank zero. Private syndrome intersections are also zero. If each half's two rows are contiguous, maximum raw rank over the entire order is at most two (the two rows' local guard and private bit), independent of k. Each system alone needs only GD20's single parity bit. The simultaneous negative query, however, has k independently required contradiction obligations.

Closure: the unpinned exterior support of C_k has full affine hull, since the all-ones word and every single-coordinate flip are accepted. Thus no nontrivial exterior affine equation is necessary, even semantically, and projected OR unit closure stagnates. Never impose the positive B_i's necessary equations in a negative query. In a guarded pinned chart, the binary OR owner forces its remaining bit true if the other is false, rejects if both false; restart after every deduction. These deductions are unconditional only relative to their supplied chart and cannot be exported across different pin guards.

## B. Exact fixed-cut lower bound including signed cancellation (PROVED)
Index prefix rows by subsets A of [k] where a_i=1. Index suffix columns by C=[k] minus the set of b_i=1. Then C_k(A,C)=1 iff C subseteq A. Consider ANY matrix M over ANY field with M[A,C] nonzero iff C subseteq A. Order row/column subsets by cardinality then the same tie-break. M is lower triangular (equal cardinality and distinct sets are incomparable) with nonzero diagonal. Therefore rank(M)=2^k.
Consequently every exact separable signed representation M[A,C]=sum_{j=1}^r f_j(A)g_j(C) must have r>=2^k. Arbitrary cancellation, nonuniform coefficients and unequal weights on accepting entries do not reduce this bound. This applies to a linear/bilinear flat interface of dimension r and exact nonzero support, not arbitrary symbolic circuits or nonlinear summaries.
A Boolean nondeterministic flat interface with r states induces a cover by r accepting rectangles. All 2^k diagonal entries must be covered. A rectangle containing (A,A) and (C,C) with A!=C would need both off-diagonal entries accepted, which implies A subseteq C and C subseteq A, impossible. Hence r>=2^k also in the Boolean rectangle model. Deterministic residual keys are at least as large (each A induces a distinct set of suffix completions).
These are dimension/state cardinality lower bounds, NOT 2^k bits of memory: k bits encode a key. Nor is a factorization with exponentially many implicit summands necessarily expensive to evaluate for a specialized consumer. Only an algorithm required to explicitly materialize all these distinct flat states/basis directions must pay their enumeration/storage costs.

## C. Exact repair and tight crossing-obligation profile (PROVED)
For any order of the 2k complete half-events, let c_i be the number of groups with exactly one half processed. If completed groups have been accepted, retain only the truth bit of their first half for each open group. A new group's first bit opens its slot; the second bit checks OR and closes the slot. Each step introduces at most two alternatives, each admitted state retains an actual history, and all correlation is preserved. There are at most 2^c_i keys. Tightness for full support follows by fixing completed and future-only groups to accepted values, and applying the same triangular submatrix argument to the c_i crossing groups. This establishes exact flat support rank at each cut for this family, even under signed cancellation. The analogous upper bound is an explicit sum over crossing assignments.
Interleave a_i,b_i by system ID: c_i<=1, at most two keys, O(k) elementary transitions after paid sorting/recognition. In fact this source's exact projection is ordinary disjoint binary OR, hence BS13/SCC or unit closure plus setting free variables to 1 solves it even more directly. For any full support demand a factored DAG of k ORs has O(k) nodes; the lower bound does not forbid this representation or its cheap decision. The raw grouped order and a valid alternative are both supplied by authenticated owner identity. This is a repair, not merely a negative example.
For general GD20 queries, the conservative interface width should include the number of unresolved negative obligations crossing the cut, rather than assume one extra bit total or rely only on raw primal width. A group may also have private syndrome dimensions, actual exterior correlation or restrictions; counting open groups is a conservative upper parameter, not a universal lower bound. Each group can open at its first event and enforce its odd parity at its last event. Raw private width and actual primal width still charged. Proof for this particular example only; general multinegative backend not executed.

## D. Resource/diagonal firewall (PROVED conditional accounting)
A paid potential is sufficient if there exists one uniform exact procedure on every residual with original length N, all intermediate encodings, adapter/guard acquisition, verification, closure, failed attempts, edge/consumer reads, reconstruction and per-node work bounded by a fixed polynomial in N (or explicitly prepaid), and nonnegative potential mu with mu(root)<=poly(N) and sum_child mu(child)^c <= mu(parent)^c-1 for one fixed c>0. Sum the inequalities over the occurrence tree: each expanded node consumes at least one unit of root credit, so the number of expansions is at most mu(root)^c. Leaves/edges must also be paid or bounded fanout; zero-credit terminal children cannot be emitted for free. Duplicate children count as separate occurrences unless the execution certifies exact reuse and charges each access; a unique-node ledger must prove its own global scheduling invariant. A bit-length encoding of an exponential reserve is not a polynomial numerical reserve. Taking mu from the optimal solver's unknown runtime merely restates the desired result.
The grouped C_k full-support table reserve is exponential, despite every individual negative obligation being inexpensive. The interleaved repair has additive O(k) transition credits and a fixed polynomial adapter/reconstruction cost. Thus this does not contradict DP14's paid schedule lemma or BD13's single-use linear context theorem; it falsifies the proposed transfer from individual cheap terminals to their arbitrary shared-order flat conjunction.
For a fixed deterministic total exact portfolio A with no uniform polynomial bound, for each integer j choose F_j with T_A(F_j)>(|F_j|+2)^j. Such choices exist, and their lengths tend to infinity because any fixed finite set of inputs has finite maximum runtime. A fixed terminal's admitted domain with a guaranteed paid polynomial runtime and a fair polynomial-overhead portfolio schedule can contain only finitely many of these F_j. For a finite list of such fixed terminals the sequence eventually escapes all their domains. This quantifier statement is not a uniform hardness certificate against arbitrary other algorithms, and the family C_k cannot serve as that sequence for a portfolio containing the cheap binary terminal. An existential good order or certificate with no paid acquisition guarantee does not yield a terminal domain exclusion. No claim that DO09/DO14 survives all nonlocal transformations.
Serial interleaving or OR decision: polynomial source encoding, parsing, grouping, guard checks, closure, actual witness reconstruction, and verification; T,S polynomial on the same realization; CM <= (T+1)S. Grouped explicit tables require 2^k states at central cut for full support, with explicit entries consuming at least one storage unit each if simultaneously retained; streaming/recomputation can alter peak space. No universal time-space product lower bound. Do not infer physical byte counts from logical state counts.
