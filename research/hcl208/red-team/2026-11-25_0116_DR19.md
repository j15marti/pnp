## Exact result and falsification
Full proofs: [shared manuscript §DR19](https://github.com/j15marti/pnp/blob/fc52c600c380c63051a26d78c1c3482e232877c5/research/final-proof/main.tex#L2265), labels lem:dr19-horn, lem:dr19-fill, lem:dr19-rename.

1. A Horn block with v internal bits, L literal occurrences and authenticated exterior-row literal pin sets can be eliminated into a polynomial-size monotone rejection circuit on positive and negative pin flags. Forward chaining for at most v rounds followed by goal/negative-pin checks is exact; a nonrejecting full exterior tuple lifts to the least model. Size O((v+1)(L+v+1)) gates plus pin acquisition, with polynomial bit costs. The consumer is NOT generally a pair relation; new internal guards require version/replay. S24's row/edge credit does not automatically pay for the circuit and future consumer calls. Retain these as explicit objects under a fixed original-N cap and prove aggregate dispatch before claiming polynomial completion.
2. k pairs of flags a_i,b_i with rules a_i→c_i, b_i→c_i, a conjunction chain over c_i and a final negative unit have exactly 2^k minimal inconsistent pin sets. The circuit uses 3k Horn clauses of width at most 3. Any CNF over flags alone needs at least 2^k clauses, since the meet of distinct minimal rejected assignments is accepted but a clause's false set is meet closed. This is an explicit representation lower bound only, not a general circuit or runtime lower bound.
3. Mixed-module universal tractability is not justified: any 3-CNF on n variables compiles into an independently tractable negative Horn block with two rails per variable, plus independent binary owner choices pinning one rail true. Each source clause forbids the simultaneous falsifying rails, and each pair of rails is mutually exclusive. The least model accepts a complete choice exactly when it satisfies the source. Each layer alone is easy and the composed problem is NP-complete. No enumeration of exterior choices has been proved polynomial. This is a reduction, not P!=NP; a universal efficient coupled-consumer theorem here would already solve SAT. Nonempty clauses may be assumed in the reduction (empty clause is a trivial reject).
4. For an explicit binary chart omit intra-owner at-most-one clauses in the row-selection CNF: positive coverage clauses plus negative cross-conflict clauses are equisatisfiable and allow an exact lift by selecting one row per owner. Renamable-Horn recognition is 2-SAT: every same-owner pair requires at least one flipped variable; every cross conflict permits at most one flipped variable. A K2,2 conflict is an exact certificate against all polarity choices of this encoding. Raw linear NAE intersecting owners have a K3,3 conflict, independent of signs. After AC, if renaming succeeds, choosing any flipped row per nonsingleton owner and each singleton gives a witness; thus the adapter is not a general UNSAT terminal after AC. Failure cannot exclude alternate auxiliary encodings or block transformations.
