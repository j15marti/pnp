## 2. DR12-A: equality-guard firewall (PROVED)
Take arbitrary strict monotone NAE-3SAT H on n used variables and m distinct-variable triples, an NP-complete language by Schaefer. Make one disjoint three-bit block y_i for each triple. The local predicate is NAE, given by 1-[000]-[111], or equivalently 1 minus the affine monochromatic event. Before external guards, the product of these independent predicates has exactly 6^m models, full accepting affine hull and no interregional edges. Every local packet has coupling rank zero when its three-bit block is treated as one coordinate block.
For each original variable, list its k occurrences in any order and impose y_occ_j+y_occ_(j+1)=0 for j<k. The guard matrix A consists of disjoint equality paths on occurrence vertices, with row weight exactly two, each occurrence in at most two guard rows, and independent row rank h=3m-n. All guard RHS are zero. The construction and rank verification are polynomial, and the guard is always consistent. Each satisfying original assignment has exactly one guarded occurrence word and vice versa; verification and reconstruction are linear in registry size given indices. Thus the reduction is parsimonious, unlike existential-only peel.
For every individual block, the projection of the full guard onto its three coordinates is surjective because the three original variable IDs are distinct. This remains true after ANY subset of these equality rows, in ANY order. The NAE accepting set has full affine hull in F2^3 (each coordinate has a flip pair holding the other two opposite). Therefore local accepting-affine-hull closure relative to the current guard adds no independent affine equation at any prefix. This is NOT a claim about complete semantic closure or multi-owner deductions. Single-CNF-clause relaxation of one NAE owner gives OR or NAND with full affine hull as well.
Consequences: exact decision under an arbitrary accumulated equality-path guard on disjoint constant-size factors is NP-complete; a polynomial general adapter would prove P=NP. Guard graph treewidth one, local packet coupling zero and guard row weight two do not imply a polynomial terminal. The failure is JOINT compatibility, not free local acquisition. Even an individually cheap count query for each row does not tell the count of their conjunction. A small explicit counterexample: independent fair bits a,b; guards a=0 and b=0 each halve mass, but a=0 and a=1 have the same individual masses and zero joint mass. The equality-path reduction is much stronger, with independent consistent rows. No claim that arbitrary guards inherently require enumeration or that P!=NP.
For full semantic affine closure of arbitrary guarded products, even distinguishing empty support from a nonempty affine hull already includes this decision problem. If a closure oracle omits emptiness, append a bit t and use one-sided SAT constructions only with a fully proved reduction; no such oracle assumed here. Certified previously derived necessary rows are different: adding them does not change support, and a fixed-point archive avoids recompiling them, as DP12 correctly notes.

## 3. DR12-B: exact guard-syndrome cut terminal (PROVED)
Domain: independent blocks y_i in F2^{d_i}, each authenticated Boolean f_i given by explicit integer signed affine packets on that block, and arbitrary explicit external affine equations A y=b. No nonlocal quota/tag semantics assumed. Write A_i for the columns on block i; U_i=im(A_i), P_i=sum_{j<=i} U_j, S_i=sum_{j>i} U_j, W_i=P_i intersect S_i and w_i=dim W_i. Verify source equivalence separately from Booleanity (BD11 second moment). For a supplied or chosen block order compute all spaces by Gaussian elimination; root target outside P_m implies immediate contradiction.
Every feasible prefix contribution s=sum_{j<=i} A_j y_j belongs to C_i=P_i intersect (b+S_i), an empty set or affine coset of W_i, of size <=2^w_i. Retain only these keys. Start at s=0 and finish at b. For each candidate adjacent key pair (s,t), compute exact local fiber mass
    Z_i(t+s)=sum_{y_i:A_i y_i=t+s} f_i(y_i).
For signed packets f_i=sum_j c_ij [L_ij y_i=h_ij], this is the signed sum of Gaussian intersection cardinalities. Its value is nonnegative after Booleanity authentication. This is independent of the actual prefix because blocks are disjoint. Sum-product recurrence M_i(t)=sum_s M_(i-1)(s) Z_i(t+s) counts every complete guarded word exactly once. It never counts signed labels as witnesses. No enumeration of the local block words is required in the symbolic theorem.
Acquire a local positive-fiber actual anchor and its full difference-span basis using local signed counts with additional linear tests on that block. Find a witness by coordinate self-reduction; test complements of basis equations of the current affine hull and find a witness outside it by prefix self-reduction, at most d_i expansions and O((d_i+1)^2) count queries. Local additional equations do not couple blocks. At a positive transition the prefix and local fiber supports form a Cartesian product on disjoint coordinates, so multiply counts, concatenate real anchors and take span of embedded difference bases. At a merge of two incoming transitions to the same key, their support sets are disjoint (a word uniquely determines its previous contribution and local word). Add counts; retain one anchor and add all incoming difference bases and the anchor difference. By the elementary affine-hull-of-union identity the resulting basis spans exactly the differences of the actual union. Induction gives count, actual witness and complete affine hull on the original chart at root. This extends M18's positive payload to signed local factors AFTER exact local fiber aggregation. Raw signed labels cannot carry this payload. A basis vector is not a witness.
For mandatory closure, before the terminal use paid owner-local accepting hull on actual guard (Gaussian projection + local signed counts), repeat after independent necessary equations and at stagnation. If a newly derived row would change the cut parameter, retain it as a verified consequence, not an external restriction on the original equivalent representation. Root payload is complete affine closure of actual support; adding any of its identities preserves support and hence the closure is already fixed. Empty root is contradiction, not an arbitrary identity. Conditional external equations stay in A,b throughout. This theorem does not claim free semantic closure for any source outside the domain.

## 4. Exact width identity on equality paths (PROVED)
For DR12-A, each variable with k occurrences has a separate (k-1)-row syndrome coordinate block. The k columns form the incidence vectors of vertices in a connected path (transpose of its edge-vertex incidence matrix); their sole nonzero binary dependency is the sum of all k columns, as follows from the equations x_j=x_(j+1). Every proper subset is independent. At any partition of the occurrence blocks, if all occurrences of a variable lie on one side, it contributes zero to W. If both sides contain occurrences, the ranks of the two column subsets sum to k while their union rank is k-1; contribution is exactly one. Different variables use disjoint syndrome coordinates. Thus
    w_i = # original variables with occurrences on BOTH sides of the cut.
This is the actual variable frontier, not guard graph pathwidth. The same proof works for arbitrary factor bipartitions. Equality duplication does not magically turn high frontier into low syndrome width. DO12 may still exclude fixed-registry approaches; this result allows other orders, different terminal representations and exact nonlocal elimination. It is not an unrestricted lower bound and does not claim equality of this frontier with every algebraic cut measure.

## 5. Charged work and diagonal quantifiers (PROVED restricted)
For explicit encoded length N, charge source authentication/compilation and all failed discovery in A_setup. Let J=1+sum_i |C_(i-1)| |C_i| (or upper bound 1+sum_i 2^(w_(i-1)+w_i)). Reserve polynomial-sized local jobs for Booleanity, signed counts, bit arithmetic, basis acquisition, payload merges, closure, all accesses and source verification. Each candidate pair costs poly(N) for the domain; coefficients and counts have polynomial bit lengths (count <=2^sum d_i, temporary signed sums bounded by explicit coefficient magnitudes). Candidate pairs are processed once. Rolling tables require S<=poly(N)(1+max_i 2^w_i), including source, spans, local scratch and payloads, provided local queries are streamed not cached exponentially. Total T<=A_setup+poly(N)J+paid closure; for same bounded-write serial execution CM<=(T+1)S, with setup memory included in S. If w=O(log N) and setup/closure polynomial, this is a deterministic polynomial terminal; cumulative guard rank h may be linear. A remaining transition-job reserve decreases by one per charged job and disjoint job partitions have c=1 contraction. This is a scheduler potential on certified domains, NOT a universal structural mu or an input-size-only guarantee. If a DAG shares nodes, count distinct reachable job keys AND consumer edges or charge their on-demand generation; summing child reserves can double count shared jobs, so use actual disjoint ownership or a union ledger. A small static circuit or number of node types is not a bound on the number of generated keys or edge inspections.
Diagonal audit: for fixed total exact A with no polynomial worst-case cap, choose growing lengths N_j and inputs F_j with T_A(F_j)>N_j^j, by finite exceptions absorbed in constants. Under a fixed fair scheduler with polynomial overhead for each fixed terminal and its verified cap, every fixed finite set of polynomial terminals must eventually fail to return a decision on this sequence within their guaranteed bounds. This does not imply any prescribed structural family lies on the sequence or that all possible polynomial solvers fail on one common sequence. A polynomial bound on just one label for a fixed exact decider would yield a different polynomial decider by cutoff and returning the other label; only under P!=NP (or an independently justified exclusion of the cutoff solver) can we infer superpoly sequences on both labels. A slow fixed portfolio alone does not imply P!=NP, nor that both labels must be slow. If P=NP, intentionally delayed exact deciders are counterexamples. No diagonal contradiction has been closed. A universal polynomially bounded mu with fixed exponent and paid local jobs would suffice by induction, but the coverage premise remains OPEN. Guard path simplicity cannot supply that premise unless P=NP is independently proved.
