## Explicit cubic source and pseudoexpectation
Fix D=16 and any integer m>2D+2. Construct G from K_m by replacing each vertex i with a cycle of m-1 vertices (i,j), j!=i in increasing j order; connect (i,j) to (j,i). Simple connected cubic graph, v=m(m-1), e=3v/2. Set a single vertex charge b_v=-1, others +1. For edge spins x_e require product of its three incident spins = b_v. Multiplying all vertex constraints gives 1=-1. Source odd parity is a polynomially verifiable UNSAT certificate.

For every cut of size <=D, at most D/2 cycles are split. If a,b are the numbers of full cycles on its two sides, ab<=D from cross edges and a+b>=m-D/2. If both positive, ab>=a+b-1>D, impossible. Hence a side has no full cycles and size <=(D/2)(m-1)<v/4. The small shore is unique. For any squarefree monomial x^S with |S|<=D, define E[x^S]=0 if S is not a cut, else product of charges over its unique small shore. In particular E[1]=1. For |A|,|B|<=D/2, group monomials by coset of the binary cut space. Across groups moment matrix entries are zero. Within a group, use a representative A0. The small shores for A xor A0 and B xor A0 have symmetric difference of size <v/2; this is the small shore for A xor B (since that cut also has <=D edges). Therefore E[x^(A xor B)] factors as E[x^(A xor A0)] E[x^(B xor A0)]. Each block is an outer product, proving positivity on squares through degree D/2. For S and S xor star(v) both <=D, cut membership is unchanged and, when cut, the small shores differ exactly by vertex v (size <v/4+1<v/2). Thus E[x^S product_star x]=b_v E[x^S]. No external SoS theorem or assumed oracle.

Graph minus any <=2 vertices remains connected: intact cycles are joined by their complete intercycle edges. If two removed vertices are on the same cycle, every surviving vertex on it has a cross edge to an intact cycle. If on different cycles, each remaining path has a cross edge to an intact cycle (m>=4). Consequently, on the union of any <=2 vertex stars the moment functional equals the uniform local distribution satisfying just those vertex parities. Indeed cuts supported on that union have a shore contained in the removed vertices (the complement graph minus those vertices is connected), hence their nonzero Fourier coefficients are exactly the vertex-equation subgroup. This also proves pairwise independence of distinct source edge spins on these scopes, since one star has size 3 and two distinct stars have symmetric difference size 4 or 6.
