## A. Opposite-label Prouhet guarded DAGs (PROVED)
For each integer k>=1, take 2k selector bits a_i,b_i, i=0..k-1, and let x_i=a_i XOR b_i, X=sum 2^i x_i, p=XOR_i x_i. Define nonnegative integer path counts
 P_even=2X+p, P_odd=2X+1-p.
As X runs over [0,2^k), P_even enumerates exactly the integers in [0,2^(k+1)) of even binary digit parity, P_odd enumerates the odd ones. Each occurs exactly 2^k times on the 2k-bit chart. Only even contains zero, at X=0. Thus even has 2^k legal words, odd none, where legal means no enabled contradiction path.

The signed exponential generating sum over the two unpadded count distributions is
 sum_z (exp(t P_even(z))-exp(t P_odd(z)))
 = 2^k product_{i=0}^k (1-exp(2^i t)).
Expand by binary subset choice. It has a zero of order exactly k+1 at t=0. Hence M_j(even)=M_j(odd) for ALL j=0..k, with M_0 defined as chart cardinality (including zero-count words), and M_(k+1) differs. Both labels have exponentially many distinct positive multiplicities: 2^k-1 and 2^k respectively. This is Prouhet's identity applied to path-count semantics, not a new number-theoretic identity.

Explicit graph: one source fans out to two tracks per i, for the two patterns (a_i,b_i)=(0,1),(1,0), plus parity state 0. In each of 2k selector layers, observe exactly one fresh bit; each digit track has its designated one-bit guard at its own two layers, otherwise mandatory edges. Simultaneously a two-state deterministic parity monitor toggles on each bit. After these layers, give each digit track 2^(i+1) paths using i+1 doubling stages of two distinct parallel mandatory edges, padded to k stages. Parity monitor gets one mandatory continuation. Merge tracks at the sink, adding one final edge from parity state 1 (even) or state 0 (odd). The two graphs differ by a single final indexed-edge replacement. Every path requires >=2 optional edges, all guards individually possible. Size O(k^2) indexed vertices/edges, polynomial encoded bit length; one may replace each parallel edge with distinct mandatory two-edge routes preserving path count and polynomial size. No physical E4/B4 source adapter claimed.

All observation rows belong to their own layer, so I=0 at EVERY cut and local rank<=1. GD16 closure (mandatory/possible reachability and a single optional rank-one edge completing a mandatory path) stagnates on both. No complete semantic affine closure claimed: even has necessary equalities a_i=b_i, obtainable by other paid reasoning. The zero set and both-label proof are independent of the checker.

## B. Same-pair simultaneous forward/reverse explicit frontier barrier (PROVED)
On 2k fresh bits u, define a neutral prefix N_k: four tracks per group i, each retained only under the corresponding two-bit word d_i in {0,1,2,3}. Observe each bit at one layer; all other track edges are mandatory. After all selector layers exactly one track per group survives. The 4^k distinct reachable sets are all size k, hence none contains another. Equal-key merging also cannot reduce them. Give track (i,d) precisely (d+1)4^i indexed paths through mandatory parallel edges (d+1 copies, then 2i doublings, padded). Merge at a single vertex. Then
 N_k(u)=sum_i (d_i+1)4^i = integer(u)+(4^k-1)/3 >0,
with exactly 4^k distinct values. The reversed neutral gadget has the same counts and creates the same reverse-frontier obstruction.

Concatenate on independent fresh coordinates:
 G_even = N_k(u) ; P_even(a,b) ; reverse N_k(v)
 G_odd  = N_k(u) ; P_odd(a,b)  ; reverse N_k(v).
Semicolon identifies sole sink with next sole source. All guards still have local rank<=1, interface rank zero. Both graphs still have O(k^2) edges and vertices, 6k selector bits, and differ by one edge replacement. Every full path still has >=2 optional edges; no mandatory path, possible path exists. Therefore specified closure stagnates before branching. No deduction is exported to the neighbor; each closure is independently run. At the forward prefix frontier and the reverse suffix frontier, both graphs require 4^k actual incomparable masks under GD16 literal subset dominance. The reverse and forward expensive cuts need not coincide; this is NOT a lower bound on a selected meet cut or on adaptive reordering/semantic simulation.

Counts factor pointwise: P_G=N_k(u) P_core(a,b) N_k(v). Therefore raw moments factor by independence, M_j(G)=M_j(N_k)^2 M_j(core). The two labels have exactly equal moments through k, first difference k+1. Fix any positive core word and one suffix word: varying u gives >=4^k distinct positive total counts in BOTH graphs. Even has 2^(5k) legal words, odd zero. For every fixed constant b, choose k>=2b+1. GD15/BA09 bounded positive-multiplicity annihilation then correctly declines both; moments up to any fixed order cannot by themselves determine coverage. Even an order increasing linearly with k does not suffice for this pair. No claim about arbitrary adaptive exact moment probes, signed/cross moments, or full descriptions. The GD15 exact theorem remains sound; its hypothesis fails.

Thus FALSIFIED: universal dichotomy "either literal explicit forward/reverse antichain stays polynomial or fixed positive-multiplicity degree suffices" on general explicit affine-guarded DAGs, even rank-zero interface. The claim is about prescribed full passes and this exact representation only. A forward-or-reverse decision procedure may short-circuit or exploit the middle cut. These are not DO09 physical high-girth cores.

## C. Paid positive escape, not a diagonal hard family (PROVED)
The neutral gadgets are semantically transparent. Their source/sink articulation cuts can be found by scanning the explicit layer widths; for the supplied construction every group has a complete two-bit partition and positive continuations, verified by inspecting four guards and mandatory paths for each group. This is a polynomial certificate of all-word reachability, not an invocation of arbitrary coverage. Both gadgets may be removed FOR DECISION; preserve independent arbitrary u,v choices for witness lift. Do not remove them for exact path counts. The core is a sum of nonnegative weighted XOR indicators and one parity indicator: P_even=0 iff all a_i=b_i, while P_odd=0 would force all a_i=b_i and p=1, impossible. These are exact Gaussian tests. Recover even witness e.g. all zero; evaluate original graph and verify no path. Polynomial work including decoding, verification and output. This is the constructive normalization escape, and shows why a simultaneous restricted failure is not a hard input for a fair portfolio containing these additional certified reductions.

Future-language dominance is another valid escape, but not free in general. In zero-interface layered clause-track DAGs (GD16-B), let one state at a cut have a mandatory path to sink for every suffix, another state reach sink iff an arbitrary 3-CNF is falsified (parallel tracks). A preceding fresh bit can select either singleton, so both are realizable in the same syndrome bucket. The first state's suffix language is included in the second iff the formula is UNSAT. Membership of a counterexample is polynomial. Thus exact directed future-language inclusion is coNP-complete even for reachable singleton states in this model. Sufficient syntactic/simulation dominance is not excluded. This result is symbolic, not separately executed.

## D. Aggregate potential and quantifier audit
For a mandated computation that materializes and discharges each of H distinct frontier states at least once, every job consumes at least one unit. If a nonnegative integer reserve Phi starts <=poly(N) and drops by >=1 for each charged job, it cannot cover H=4^k=superpoly(N) in these explicit encodings. Likewise a tree recurrence with weight Phi=mu^c, fixed c and mu polynomial, and sum child Phi <= Phi(parent)-1 gives <=Phi(root) processed nodes by telescoping (unit charge per internal node, leaves charged by positive terminal credit or separate bound). A DAG version must separately count outgoing occurrences/consumer work, not simply unique vertices. Assigning credit after the expensive frontier is discovered is circular unless its acquisition is independently paid. A cap-and-decline is valid, not a universal decision. This falsifies extending a reserved-budget proof to the prescribed full-frontier trace, not every possible potential or normalization. In the escape algorithm a polynomial reserve is immediate. No lower bound follows for general matching, packet algebra, DS09 anchors or BS09 bundles, which may change the registry.
