## Theorem 1 (coNP-complete fixed-interface identity under strong promises)
For any explicit 3-CNF F(x) with n variables, choose integers k>=1,t>=0 (explicit sizes). Introduce h=k+1 control bits p and l=t+1 mask bits y, q=XOR(y), branch bit b. Define legal residual
L_b(x,p,y) = (p != 1^h) OR ((q=b) AND F(x)).
The parent is this same predicate with b free. Its two guarded children retain b=0 and b=1; the common protected interface is (x,p,y) in fixed coordinates. Both children are always SAT. The complement is a union of h-control-gated affine rejection events: p=1^h with q!=b, and p=1^h with each falsified clause. Each event has rank at most h+3; for k=1, at most 5, and each can be expanded into a sequence of rank-one tests. Empty clauses can be handled by a direct rejection; the reduction from ordinary nonempty strict 3-CNF suffices.

Explicit deterministic acyclic graph: sequentially test each p bit, any zero goes to a noncontradiction sink; if all one test q=b, mismatch goes to contradiction; otherwise examine clauses sequentially, test literals until first true then advance to next clause, all false goes to contradiction; after last clause go to noncontradiction. Each test has complementary affine edges, and exactly one complete path to one sink for every word. Number of nodes/edges O(h+m), row storage polynomial in n+h+l and input length. If a strictly layered graph is required, pad forward edges with private unconditional chains at at most quadratic size. No independent selector copies: all edges observe the same word. Path count to contradiction P_b=1-L_b, always 0 or 1. No physical E4/B4 adapter claimed.

Let Z=#SAT(F), X=2^n, Q=2^l, H=2^h. Exact identities:
* #L_b=(H-1)XQ + ZQ/2, independent of b.
* Every ordinary path moment M_j(P_b), j>=1, equals XQ-ZQ/2, independent of b. M_0=HXQ. GD15 multiplicity support has at most one positive value.
* For every fixing of x,p and any proper subset of y coordinates, the conditional model counts agree: each parity value has the same number of extensions. Hence all coordinate marginal counts of order <=t agree, and even arbitrary x,p conditions plus <=t mask-coordinate pins agree. This is NOT a claim about all linear-character count marginals.
* The baseline p!=1^h spans the full ambient affine chart (contains zero and all unit vectors since h>=2). Moreover every consistent affine fiber of codimension <=k intersects the baseline: its dimension >=d-k exceeds the dimension d-h of the omitted subspace. Consequently all such affine SUPPORT queries succeed for both children. Complete unconditional affine closure is empty on the free chart; conditional guard b remains pinned in each branch. Local rejection-event complements also have full affine hull. The parent similarly has full hull.
* Exact symmetric difference D=sum (L_0-L_1)^2=ZQ: if p=1^h and F(x), exactly one child is legal for every y; otherwise children agree. Therefore fixed-interface semantic identity L_0=L_1 iff F is UNSAT. The problem is in coNP because an unequal interface word is checkable in polynomial time. The reduction preserves every promise above, and yields coNP-hardness even k=1,t=2. Arbitrary k,t polynomial in the source length give polynomial graph encodings with stronger marginal promises.

For exact substitution under every possible downstream conjunction on protected variables, equality is both sufficient and necessary: if the relations differ, a downstream conjunction of coordinate pins to a disagreement word distinguishes their feasibility. Thus the candidate universal rule 'equal counts + all bounded-order marginal counts + full affine closure + singleton multiplicity imply interchangeable cached relations' is FALSIFIED. Complete semantic identity acquisition on this class would give P=NP if polynomial. This does not forbid a sufficient syntactic key or a sound conservative cache.

## Theorem 2 (mixed cost and a positive escape)
A cross moment computes the hidden disagreement exactly: sum P_0 P_1=XQ-ZQ, so M_2(P_0)+M_2(P_1)-2 sum P_0P_1=ZQ. Also one specified Walsh frequency q has coefficients W_q(L_0)=ZQ/2 and W_q(L_1)=-ZQ/2: baseline cancels by parity; all gated legal points have sign (-1)^b. Computing that coefficient exactly is #P-hard under polynomial arithmetic reductions (standard #3SAT counting completeness), and zero/nonzero is coNP/NP-complete respectively. GD15's mixed-tensor and MS14/BD08 signed identity mechanisms remain exact; their required common overlap/discovery hypotheses cannot be dropped merely because separate moments agree or b=1. This result does not show their actual width is exponential on every instance. Likewise BA08 small-nullity conditions remain intact.

There IS an explicit cheap transport: flip any one y bit; this maps L_0 to L_1 bijectively. If the consumer is also transported and source witnesses reverse-lifted, this is a valid shared subproblem. If a fixed downstream consumer pins that bit, it may not be invariant. Do not treat this as a counterexample to symmetry-aware sharing or to bare decision-result reuse (both children SAT). The known transport is deliberate DISJ/easy-family control. The obstruction is to unlabeled identity/substitutability and summary-only merging under arbitrary fixed protected contexts, not to all DAG sharing. For mere decision, stop at p=0 with a verified witness; for counting or cross-interface reuse, charge the exact contract. DR08/BS08 existential deletion still valid on their stated domains.

## Diagonal and potential audit
Fix a total deterministic exact portfolio A with polynomial-overhead fair simulation and verified results, measured on finite binary encodings. If it has no polynomial worst-case bound, then for each j and length threshold L there is an input length >L with T_A(F)>|F|^j (otherwise finite exceptional inputs can be absorbed into a constant). Choose strictly increasing lengths. For each fixed terminal with fully charged polynomial completion on a domain, all sufficiently late hard-sequence members lie outside that domain, assuming portfolio scheduling actually grants that terminal its guaranteed resources, and any required source preprocessing has been included. No inference that the sequence is uniformly efficiently constructible, UNSAT-only, or hard for another solver. If terminal domains are dependent on optional earlier transformations, fair search over all transformation histories is not automatically polynomial overhead; must specify a uniform executed route. Baker-Gill-Solovay blocks a proof that simply relativizes to all oracles; our reduction uses explicit clause structure and does not provide a nonrelativizing separation.

Let root encoded size N, a polynomially computable integer potential mu<=N^a, and fixed positive c such that mu^c is a nonnegative integer credit or use an explicitly integer credit directly. Require at each node, including guards, paid poly(N) discovery/verification/terminal work and polynomial encoding lengths, either a paid terminal or sum over actually processed children of credit <= parent credit-1. Reserve 1 also for leaves (or charge one per outgoing edge; otherwise zero-credit terminal children need a separate polynomial fanout bound). Summing over the unshared execution tree telescopes, bounding total nodes by root credit (or polynomial fanout times root credit). Polynomial time, peak space bounded by total allocated bits, and CM <= (T+1)S on the same serial realization. For a shared DAG, count actual distinct computations and every consumer edge, guard/map validation, cache miss and source lift. A nominal unique-key count is insufficient if identity acquisition or edge discovery is uncharged. The above counterexample refutes a shortcut to exact cache identity, not the existence of another computable potential.
