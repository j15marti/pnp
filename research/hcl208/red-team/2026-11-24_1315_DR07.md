## 2. Exact guard transport and sharp degree budget (PROVED)
Let S be any set of actual Boolean chart words, R subset S, V_j(S)=span over GF(2) of evaluations of all squarefree monomials through degree j, including constant. For an affine guard H of coefficient rank p, its indicator I_H is the product of p affine match indicators after Gaussian consistency reduction, hence degree <=p (zero for inconsistent H). If V_(D+p)(R)=V_(D+p)(S), then V_D(R intersect H)=V_D(S intersect H). Proof: for each polynomial q of degree <=D vanishing on R intersect H, I_H*q vanishes on R, so by equality of higher feature spans it vanishes on S. Thus q vanishes on S intersect H. Equality of annihilators proves the result. The reverse containment is automatic. This is actual representative transport, not counting.

Sharpness: take a full cube on p+D coordinates (p>=1,D>=0), remove its all-ones word to form R. Every Boolean polynomial vanishing on R is either zero or the full product of all p+D coordinates, by uniqueness of squarefree multilinear representation. Thus V_(p+D-1)(R)=V_(p+D-1)(S). Guard the first p coordinates to 1. The degree-D monomial in the remaining coordinates vanishes on the restricted R but not restricted S. This includes D=0: even existential support is lost. Therefore an arbitrary rank-p affine guard can require p additional feature degrees; a rank-one restriction need not preserve a fixed-degree basis. For a chain use rank of the combined consistent guard rather than the sum of redundant ranks. Fixed total degree allows polynomial representative capacity, but growing degree does not have a universal polynomial bound. This does NOT contradict BH06's special one-sided extension theorem.

## 3. Easy root, NP-complete next-rank compatibility (PROVED)
Fix k>=1. For arbitrary proper 3-CNF F on n variables x, append k selector bits t. Let T(t)=product_i t_i and S_F={(x,t): T(t)=0 or F(x)=1}. Equivalently reject each affine event E_C=[all t_i=1 AND all three clause literals false]. These have rank k+3 for proper clauses. Explicit event construction is polynomial, deduplicate clauses and discard tautologies. Root is SAT with known witness t=0. The root contains every word of Hamming weight <=k-1; those words give an explicit degree-(k-1) basis, since the matrix indexed by monomials and supports ordered by cardinality is unit triangular by inclusion. Thus the full V_(k-1) is identical for every F, regardless of F's label. All these representatives can be constructed and verified in N^O(k) time for fixed k, with no SAT oracle.

Every consistent affine guard H of rank p<k contains a legal word: H cannot be contained in the trigger T=1, because its dimension is n+k-p>n. Scan i, solve H with t_i=0 by Gaussian elimination, and return the first consistent chart word. This is a uniform polynomial low-rank compatibility terminal even if all p<k guards are allowed adaptively. But the specific rank-k guard t=1 is feasible iff F is satisfiable. Taking k=D+1 proves failure of universal extrapolation from exact degree-D features and all rank<=D guard decisions to next-rank decisions. Root feature data alone is identical for opposite labels, so is not a sufficient cache key for arbitrary future guarded queries. It does not prevent a solver from using the actual source or additional state. For k>=2 even the complete root affine hull is full, no nontrivial affine forced information exists. The specified DP07 codim-one/containment closure cannot change the root guard: every distinct proper clause event has the same codimension k+3, so no strict containment; root closure only deduplicates. On the trigger branch, all guard rows are protected, and the remaining problem is the original F. Apply closure again; no claim that complete semantic closure is free.

## 4. Universal feature acquisition is equivalent to P=NP (PROVED implication and converse, fixed degree)
The exact dimension at degree k is M_k(n+k)-1 if F is UNSAT and M_k(n+k) if F is SAT, with M_j(d)=sum_{i<=j} binomial(d,i). Proof: any polynomial vanishing outside trigger T=1 is T(t)*q(x), by uniqueness of Boolean multilinear expansion (evaluate t one word at a time or use the indicator decomposition). If its degree is <=k, q is constant. The sole possible nonzero annihilator is T; one satisfying trigger word kills it. Thus exact acquisition even of just the rank at degree k, on always-SAT inputs with explicit root witness, decides arbitrary 3-SAT. For every fixed degree k>=1 this implication holds, including affine-hull rank k=1. Conversely under P=NP, on arbitrary explicitly represented affine-event residuals, find a legal word whose feature vector is outside the current span by NP search (membership, feature evaluation and Gaussian span test are polynomial for fixed k). Use standard decision-to-search; add at most M_k words, terminate on a negative query. Therefore a uniform polynomial exact feature-rank/actual-basis acquisition algorithm for arbitrary residuals exists iff P=NP. This is not a lower bound independent of P vs NP. Cardinality, bit-size, and checking that supplied vectors are legal and independent are easy; verifying their completeness is a different query.

Also root exact projected count = (2^k-1)*2^n + #SAT(F). Thus exact projected counting on this always-SAT family is #P-complete under polynomial metric reductions, even where low-degree span and every lower-rank guard decision are easy. Do not conflate count and decision.

## 6. Diagonalization and charged ledger
For one fixed total deterministic portfolio A, failure of every uniform polynomial time bound implies: for every exponent j and finite length threshold b, there exists F with |F|>b and T_A(F)>|F|^j (otherwise finitely many exceptions are absorbed into a constant). Choose strictly increasing lengths; fair polynomial-overhead simulation excludes each fixed fully paid polynomial terminal eventually, including its discovery and verification. A growing list of terminals is not automatically uniformly simulable at constant polynomial overhead: explicitly charge construction, scheduling/indexing and runtime cap. This diagonal sequence need not be computable by an efficient algorithm, nor need the same explicit family be hard for every solver. It is not a P!=NP proof. DR07 roots have an immediate SAT terminal and cannot be a hard sequence for a portfolio implementing it. The protected trigger residual is arbitrary SAT; no claim that these syntactic families evade all solvers.
