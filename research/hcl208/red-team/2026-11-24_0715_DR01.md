## 2. Closure forest lemma (PROVED)
Let H=(V,E) be a finite 3-uniform hypergraph of Berge girth >g (incidence graph has no cycle of length <=2g). Each edge e imposes signed NAE on its three distinct vertices, with arbitrary fixed occurrence signs. For S subset V define C(S) to be the least superset closed under adding the third vertex of an edge whenever two are already present. Compute by monotone scans. C is extensive, monotone, idempotent. For 1<=s=|S|<=g, |C(S)|<=2s-1 and the induced incidence graph on C(S) and all edges contained in C(S) is a forest.

Proof: if s additions occur, the first s distinct triggering edges lie within at most 2s vertices, so their incidence graph has 3s incidence edges and at most 3s nodes. It contains a cycle using at most s hyperedges, contradiction. Hence at most s-1 additions. If at least s hyperedges are contained in C(S), take s of them: 3s incidence edges and at most 3s-1 nodes imply a cycle of length <=2s. Thus fewer than s induced edges exist; a cycle among them also contradicts girth. C(empty)=empty. This proof works for all S, not merely connected scopes, and all signs.

## 3. Anchored extension and exact local family (PROVED)
If S subset T with |T|<=g, EVERY satisfying coloring alpha of all induced edges on C(S) extends to a satisfying coloring on C(T), preserving every bit of alpha. Further, if v in C(T) minus C(S) is designated, either prescribed value for v is possible.

Proof: every edge contained in C(T) but not C(S) has at most one old vertex, by closedness. Choose two distinct new vertices from each such edge and impose that their occurrence-signed literals are unequal. The graph of chosen pairs is a forest, since a cycle of chosen pairs (including parallel pairs) would lift to a Berge cycle in the induced incidence forest. Each pair gives a parity equation with a fixed constant determined by signs. Propagate along trees; each component has one free root bit and any one designated vertex can take either prescribed value. Unselected new vertices are free. New edges satisfied irrespective of old bits, old edges satisfied by alpha. This is a paid constructive local extension using polynomial scans and parity propagation, not global SAT. From empty anchor all small closures are satisfiable.

For every |S|<=g define P_S as the projection onto S of all satisfying colorings of induced edges on C(S). It is nonempty, includes only assignments satisfying all source constraints internal to S, is restriction-closed (C is monotone), and every member extends to a member of P_T for S subset T, |T|<=g. Exact tables are definitional witnesses; no free assumption of their polynomial-size discovery for variable g. For fixed s, C size <=2s-1, exhaustive acquisition <=2^(2s-1) poly(N) per S. Can also construct a lift by a forest algorithm.

For unpinned source, each P_S has full affine hull over GF(2)^S: the incidence forest's relation has full affine hull, by rooting at variables and stripping leaf edges; conditional on the parent bit, its two child bits have three allowable pairs (the square minus one point), spanning their full two-dimensional space, so coefficients of leaf bits in any affine identity must vanish; iterate and then vary each root. Projection preserves full affine hull. Same argument holds with a single fixed root vertex, for all remaining unpinned coordinates in its component. For |S|=2 every pair assignment is supported (forest closure contains at most one edge; any two pins extend). For S equal to an original edge, C(S)=S by linearity and P_S is exactly its six signed NAE rows. Thus the local family cannot force a unary bit, delete an original valid row, or imply a nontrivial unpinned affine identity at any small scope. Do not claim every derived nonaffine relation is trivial.

## 4. Failure of REL01 at every fixed width, even on critical UNSAT cores (PROVED)

## 5. Guarded two-value adversary and tree credit (PROVED restricted)
For a set A of free branch selections and one actual satisfying local coloring alpha on C(A), allow a guard G of pinned original variables entirely inside C(A), agreeing with alpha. For |A|+w<=g, use tables of free-variable scopes S of size <=w with the local family obtained by projecting satisfying colorings on C(A union S) extending alpha. These tables are nonempty, satisfy all clauses whose residual scopes lie in S after G is fixed, and have the restriction/extension property, by Section 3 applied to A union S. The proof of overlap invariance is unchanged. This handles explicit guard pins even when their count exceeds |A|. Guard-relative exclusions must stay conditional. If v outside C(A) is selected, both labels extend alpha to C(A union {v}) as long as |A|+1<=g. If v is inside C(A), preserve at least the alpha-consistent branch. Any forced pin certified by the above REL tables must agree with the anchor on old vertices; no vertex outside C(A) can be forced while |A|+w<=g (use a scope containing it and the two-value extension).

Consequently on a high-girth UNSAT core, every terminating disjoint coordinate-branch TREE whose only conflict terminals are original clause conflicts and width-w overlap closure (including their guard-relative supported pins) has at least 2^(g-w) distinct nodes. Start A empty; before |A|=g-w, follow anchor-consistent choices within C(A) and both values for every new outside selection. Neither an original clause conflict nor empty REL table is possible. With finitely many variables, the search must make another outside selection on each surviving path: otherwise it exhausts all variables inside C(A) without a permitted rejection, and if C(A)=V the anchor itself is a global model, contradicting UNSAT. Induct to obtain two disjoint descendants at each free-selection level, with |A| increased by one. This bound is on explicit nodes of a TREE, not distinct canonical DAG states or arbitrary solvers. It allows adaptive choice of branch variable; it does not allow non-coordinate partitions, unrestricted learned constraints across branches, certified representation changes, symmetry or DAG sharing. It is not a superpolynomial lower bound: on DO01 cores minimum variable degree >=2 and edge degree=3, a nonbacktracking incidence BFS out to distance g has no collisions (otherwise a cycle of length <=2g), and at least 2^floor(g/2) distinct variables at an even layer; hence g<=2 log2(n)+1. This particular exponential-in-g bound can be polynomial in n.

For an additive credit proof on that exact tree with unit debit, root mu^c must at least cover the actual expanded nodes (positive integer credit, terminal debit). Strict per-branch decrease alone does not imply the aggregate inequality. This lower bound does not falsify existence of polynomial credit, only any smaller credit on the stipulated execution.
