## PROVED: signed B4 alternating-path cube and affine-rank tradeoff
B4 means exactly two of four actual ports are 1; wires impose equality or complement, including signs. Exposed physical ports are distinct. For two feasible assignments a,b of any pure B4 network (fixed pins allowed), pair the 0-to-1 changed ports with the 1-to-0 changed ports at every vertex. Follow changed wire incidences and these local pairs. This decomposes all changed ports into cycles and paths with two changed boundary endpoints. Toggle any subset of components from a; every B4 remains balanced, every wire valid and every common pin fixed. Changes are disjoint, so actual port values are affine in switch bits. Given two paid witnesses, component acquisition and affine guard solving by Gaussian elimination are polynomial; exact within this cube, never complete outside it. A planar embedding permits noncrossing opposite-change pairings at every four-changed-port vertex (at least one adjacent pairing is valid). Hence in a disk two crossing equality wires cannot be the exact guard-free B4 boundary relation: compare 0000 and 1111, a noncrossing endpoint pair can be toggled alone and yields a forbidden word. The same argument for E4 (0000,1111) requires no planarity.

Let R_k consist of k disjoint exposed four-port groups, each constant. Suppose a pure B4 chart with an affine guard of rank r projects to a subset of R_k. For two accepted words differing on t groups, choose guarded physical lifts a,b. The changed boundary has 4t ports, yielding 2t paths; freeze cycle switches at zero. The affine guard on switch bits is homogeneous at a, rank <=r. Its kernel has dimension >=2t-r. Distinct path choices produce distinct boundary differences. They must lie in the t-dimensional space of whole-group toggles (other groups cannot change). Thus t<=r. Every chart has decoded Hamming diameter <=r and at most sum_{j=0}^{min(r,k)} binom(k,j) words (ball around any accepted word). Exact flat union needs >=2^k / this bound charts. For r=O(log k), this is 2^{k-O(log^2 k)}. Single-chart exact support needs r>=k, tight with two disjoint two-port B4 equality gadgets per group and one parity link between their bits (BW24 octahedron-minus-edge gadget). No claim on factored intersections/products, high-rank guards, source-aware decision, arbitrary SAT runtime, or P!=NP. This is an exact-support representation firewall, not a simultaneous hard sequence.
