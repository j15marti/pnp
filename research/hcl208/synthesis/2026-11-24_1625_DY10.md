## A. Canonical deficiency core (PROVED)
Input: nonempty authenticated affine chart G in GF(2)^d with equation rowspace H; complete exterior rowspace W. Each owner i in [m] rejects exactly on an explicitly supplied consistent affine event E_i defined by equations A_i z=b_i. Inconsistent events are dropped. An event covering G certifies UNSAT. A rank-one event modulo H forces the complement of one actual nonconstant equation on G; update G and repeat. Keep original source/owner/guard and lift registry. No hidden quota, physical occupancy or tag constraints; they must be represented in the exterior observation contract or retained in an exact adapter. Arbitrary positive escape unions are not silently treated as a single affine rejection. Work in V=GF(2)^d/(H+W), with U_i the image of event rowspace.

For J subset [m], let r(J)=dim sum_{i in J} U_i and D(J)=|J|-r(J). Set D=max_J D(J)>=0. Rank is submodular, hence D(J) is supermodular. Any two maximizers have intersection and union also maximizing D (sum inequality and both terms <=D). There is therefore a UNIQUE inclusion-minimum maximizer C, the intersection of all maximizers. Empty iff D=0.

For every T subset of complement K=[m] minus C,
 r(C union T)-r(C) >= |T|,
since otherwise D(C union T)>D. In V/sum_{i in C}U_i, Rado's independent-transversal condition holds on the entire K. Select one actual supplied equation from every K owner with rows independent modulo H+W+span_{i in C} A_i. Complement their RHS, as in BD10. On EVERY word satisfying G and the retained C factors, keep ALL W and C row evaluations unchanged; the new equations are consistent because their quotient rows are independent. Gaussian elimination returns a word avoiding all K rejection events while keeping C legal. Conversely every original legal word satisfies C. Thus exact projected support on W (indeed jointly on W and all C observations) is preserved. Source reconstruction stores the selected original equations and provenance. Counts need not be preserved. This is a stronger partial reduction than full-transversal deletion: it succeeds even when D>0 if C is proper. No independent selector copies or enumeration of exterior words.

## B. Polynomial core discovery (PROVED)
Tagged supplied basis rows form a ground set of length L, omitting rows zero in quotient. Use partition and linear matroid intersection with shortest augmenting paths, sources addable to partition, sinks addable to linear, outside->inside exchange in linear, inside->outside exchange in partition. Rado/Edmonds gives maximum cardinality m-D. A simple independently checkable algorithm recomputes maximum deficiency after excluding each owner; owner belongs to C iff that exclusion reduces the maximum. This uses <=m+1 intersection calls, plus one for K in the new quotient.

Faster implemented extraction: after the final failed augment search, let C consist of all unmatched owners (including those with no nonzero quotient row) plus the owners whose selected row is reachable from a source. All nonzero rows of a reached owner are reached (partition arcs); a reached outside row lies in span of selected rows, else it is a sink. Its unique representation uses only reached selected rows, since any selected row with nonzero coefficient is a linear exchange neighbor. Thus r(C)=number of selected owners in C and D(C)=total unmatched=m-|I|. Any maximizing J must contain every unmatched owner: D(J)<=number of unmatched owners in J<=m-|I|, with equality requiring span U_J equal span of selected rows of J. Hence J contains all reached owners by propagation along the same exchange arcs. C is the inclusion-minimum maximizer. Verify rank equality, selected owner uniqueness, and complement certificate before deletion. The failed search is charged. This is the affine observation analogue of matching-autarky/lean-kernel reasoning, not a claim of priority over matroid theory.

## C. Critical UNSAT tradeoff (PROVED)

By A, C must equal the full owner set: otherwise a SAT word of the proper core lifts to a SAT word of the full system. Consequently D(full)>D(J) for every proper J, D(full)>=1.
