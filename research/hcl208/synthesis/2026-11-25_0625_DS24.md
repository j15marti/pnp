## Exact modulo-four projection
For an integer balance system A x=b in bit variables, every Boolean solution solves A x=b modulo 4. Over Z/4Z, use odd (unit) coefficient pivots, normalizing and clearing each pivot column in all other rows. Remaining coefficient rows are all even. Reject an odd residual RHS; otherwise divide each residual row by two. The binary image of the modular solution set is exactly the affine system consisting of pivot rows reduced modulo two and these divided rows modulo two. Necessity follows by reduction. For sufficiency start from a binary representative y satisfying those rows; choose x=y+2z, with z on pivot coordinates correcting the residual discrepancy in the pivot equations; even rows cannot be changed by 2z and already hold. Costs O(m n (m+n)) bounded modular arithmetic (a looser polynomial bound is fine), plus binary Gaussian elimination. General integer equations permit arbitrary affine guards by adding 2Gx=2h modulo four. Pair relations translate to exact integer equations x_i-x_j=0 or x_i+x_j=1. Feed newly certified pairs to the integer system and repeat local owner closure. Conditional equations remain in their own guard. At most O(n²) distinct pairs; at every round recompute closure. Exact source verification is required for any candidate. A declined relaxation never means UNSAT. A full integer-lattice version uses polynomial-bit Smith/Hermite normal form with solution/kernel generators, whose reductions modulo two give the exact affine image of all integer solutions, not of Boolean solutions. General SNF was not implemented.

## Universal firewall, exact real/PSD lift
For any signed NAE owner with signed spins a,b,c, introduce a private spin t and impose a+b+c-t=0. The unique t is a sign exactly when the owner is satisfied. Thus the sum of squares of all such linear rows is a homogeneous PSD quadratic with explicitly supplied Gram factor, zero over signs iff the NP-complete source is SAT; polynomial source compilation, unique witness lift and reverse restriction. Over unrestricted integer bit variables, for every source bit word each private auxiliary bit has a unique integer value; it may be -1 or 2 if the source violates its owner. Consequently every source word extends to a real and integer solution, and to every modular relaxation. Even exact integer-lattice projection gives no restrictions on original source bits. Full binary image in source+aux coordinates is just the row parity graph: each private bit is a fixed affine parity of its triple. For distinct ternary scopes this graph has no forced pair, and every B4 owner has full affine hull in its parity hyperplane. Hence unguarded owner-local affine closure combined with arbitrary-modulus linear saturation alone cannot resolve this source. SAT and UNSAT exist with identical raw scopes.
