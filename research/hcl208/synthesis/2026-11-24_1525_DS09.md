## 2. DS09-A: heterogeneous affine-cover composition (PROVED)
For every explicit affine chart G in GF(2)^n, let authenticated anchor f0(z)=sum_{j=1}^ell c_j 1_{E_j}(z) with c_j>0 and explicit affine E_j. Other region factors f_i(z)>=0 are supported on authenticated affine observations W_i, i.e. their values depend only on W_i(z); no independent selector copies. They may be arbitrary nonlinear functions or existential support predicates of private regions with source-certified lift maps. All protected tags, quotas, shared private variables and guards must be retained in the observation contract or grouped in the same exact oracle; no unauthenticated loss of correlations. Source length N includes anchor, rows, region source encodings, guard registry and lift maps. The local support oracle for a block must return feasibility AND actual compatible observation values and private/source witness in paid time, or UNKNOWN; its domain is explicitly tested.

For each consistent Q_j=G intersect E_j, let H_j be its equation row span. Quotient the other W_i modulo H_j. Find a partition of region IDs into blocks B such that the block spans U_B=sum_{i in B} image(W_i) form a direct sum. The partition may vary with j. For each block call an exact local support oracle on Q_j, restricted to that block; it may be a different certified terminal (wheel escape, matching, flow with exact quota, packet mass, moment DP, syndrome tree, etc.). If any block is UNSAT, Q_j is rejected. If every block is SAT, their local observation functionals glue on the direct sum; extend the glued functional by Gaussian elimination to a word of Q_j. Each private witness reconstructs from this common word (it observes only the authenticated local rows). The anchor is positive on Q_j, so the lifted common word is SAT. Conversely any satisfying word lies in at least one E_j and yields feasible witnesses for every block on that term. Thus the entire original problem is SAT iff at least one term has all blocks feasible. Terms are NOT required to be disjoint; UNSAT requires all consistent terms to be rejected, not merely all attempted ones. Any remaining UNKNOWN with no positive witness yields UNKNOWN. This proves both directions and reconstruction, not a universal coverage oracle.

Proof of independence: write Q_j=z0+ker H_j. Its uniform linear evaluations onto the product of block observation spaces are surjective exactly when sum_B dim U_B=dim(sum_B U_B). Thus arbitrary compatible block observation functionals can coexist and the fibers have uniform size. This is a joint rank test; pairwise intersections alone are insufficient. No enumeration of the shared observation rank or the term's free variables is needed. Nontrivial affine restrictions in E_j can fix correlations without enumerating their individual RHS possibilities. Conditional closure facts stay local to Q_j unless independently proved on the whole parent. Any sound new root affine pin triggers reparameterization and renewed closure; a term-local pin may narrow Q_j only if necessary for its remaining product. Positive reconstruction choices are witness selection, not unconditional parent deductions.

### Automatic finest direct-sum block discovery (PROVED symbolically, not executed)
Compute quotient bases per region using Gaussian elimination. Form a maximal independent subset T of their union (retaining region IDs). For each nonbasis row v express it uniquely in T. Union its region ID with the IDs of all basis rows having nonzero coefficient in that expression. Initial rows of the same region share its ID. Each resulting component's span equals the span of its own subset of T, so different components have direct-sum spans. Moreover any independent partition must keep the regions of each such dependency together: a sum of a nonempty subset of independent basis rows from a different block cannot be zero. Hence this gives the finest partition of whole region IDs into direct-sum spans. All Gaussian work and union-find are polynomial. Zero quotient rows contribute no couplings. This is a paid syntactic independence test, not semantic equivalence acquisition. The checker instead tests singleton independence and, if it fails, conservatively merges all leaves; general finest-partition backend not executed.

## 3. DS09-B: signed anchor mass and exact counting firewall (PROVED)
Let c_j be signed rational/integer coefficients, but the complete anchor and all other factors are nonnegative pointwise by source contract. Define Q_j, direct-sum blocks as above. If for each block an exact MASS oracle supplies
  M_{jB} = sum_{z in Q_j} product_{i in B} f_i(z),
then uniform observation independence implies
  Z = sum_z f0(z) product_i f_i(z) = sum_j c_j |Q_j| product_B (M_{jB}/|Q_j|).
Inconsistent terms contribute zero. This formula is valid for arbitrary overlaps of anchor terms and different partitions per term. It is exact integer/rational arithmetic with polynomial bit length in explicit chart, source coefficient sizes and number of factors, provided each local value has polynomial bit length. For nonnegative original factors, Z>0 iff SAT; Z=0 iff UNSAT. Boolean source counts only if all weights are original Boolean indicators or a parsimonious source adapter has been proved. BW09 positive escape sums instead yield weighted feasibility mass. BS08/DA09 existential eliminations do not generally preserve original counts; their support is legal for DS09-A, not silently for DS09-B. Signed intermediate negative/zero terms cannot be treated as UNSAT. Signed anchor positive mass does not justify lifting an arbitrary positive term witness, since anchor cancellation may annihilate that point. Signed witness reconstruction is only conditional on paid closure under prefix restrictions or another certified lift; not claimed in general and not executed. For positive anchors the direct-sum lift in A is sufficient.

## 4. Aggregate cost / actual cooperative scheduler
For fixed exponent a, run a capped implementation on the original authenticated chart. First run polynomial scoped local forced-information closure (zero support, necessary pins) to a fixed point; after each deduction restart closure. For each of <=ell terms pay for guard consistency, conditional closure, quotient bases, partition discovery, local calls, failed candidates, and lift verification. Choose a polynomial input-length budget N^a for each local terminal call INCLUDING its source compilation, discovery, verification and output. Capped failures return UNKNOWN. Use one immutable guard+region-ID+representation+consumer contract per cache key. DR09 excludes summary-only merging; BD09/I15 may supply actual compatible tables. Serially charge all term/block consumers; do not multiply count copies or erase guarded conditional results. Do not require global exact affine closure for free; with explicit signed local factors local fiber counts are Gaussian and necessary coordinate deductions are paid. Complete local affine hull is optional with paid support/witness queries; not executed.
If ell and source size are polynomial, at most ell*m block calls plus polynomial closure/discovery and actual reconstruction. Hence T<=poly(N)+ell*m*N^a*poly(N); S<=poly(N)+max call space plus polynomial retained witness/guard registry, CM<=(T+1)S for this SAME serial realization. For polynomial-bit arithmetic the coefficient product has O(poly(N)) bits (each mass bounded by |Q_j| times product of local magnitudes). Credit C = number of outstanding reserved elementary jobs (including max charged attempts, each term/block call, closure rank decreases, verification and lift); allocate a polynomial budget at root for this fixed capped execution, decrement each used/discharged job. This is strict additive contraction for the bounded terminal, not universal SAT-child contraction. No assertion that a polynomially acquired anchor exists for arbitrary residuals. Positive anchor lift requires oracle witness guarantees; a support oracle returning only a Boolean is not enough unless its guarded self-reduction is paid.

For the full exact portfolio fix explicit programs and deterministic schedules (including a complete guarded fallback). Dovetail independent uniform root pipelines; if a module produces a sound unconditional deduction, share only through authenticated source contracts and include conversion/checking costs. A pipeline's guaranteed polynomial cap must cover its own actual preprocessing and closure, not a guessed sequence of transformations. A fixed polynomially successful route is simulated with polynomial overhead if it is given fair steps and all data accesses are charged. No claim that fairness over exponentially many transformation histories is free. Capped UNKNOWN never commits UNSAT. Minimal-counterexample spine: source authentication -> certified closure -> unary source-safe reductions (DA09, BS08) -> paid terminal routes (DS08, DP09, GD15, BD09, I15, new DS09-A/B) -> bounded aggregate search if no terminal -> verified lift. The first universal missing lemma remains: uniformly find a terminal or child list with polynomially bounded credit and paid discovery, such that sum C(child)<=C(parent)-1, and polynomial residual/edge lengths. This suffices by telescoping for finite positive credits, but DS09 only proves a new terminal domain. If a fixed total fair portfolio lacks any polynomial bound, choose strictly increasing lengths N_j and inputs F_j with T(F_j)>N_j^j (finite exceptions absorb into constants). Every fixed fully charged polynomial pipeline is eventually excluded on this sequence. ## 5. A same-instance high-rank positive/negative control (PROVED)
Let shared x have r>=2 bits, anchor f0 be indicator of the r+1 points 0,e_1,...,e_r. For each such mode j, a block rejects x=mode_j and imposes a private monotone NAE triple on disjoint y_j. Thus f_j=(1-1_{x=mode_j}) NAE(y_j), a constant number of signed affine terms. Raw anchor interface rank r and total observation dependency nullity r(r+1) (each of r+2 factors observes shared x; private triples independent). Each local factor has full affine hull on its own observations: point rejection has full hull for r>=2 and NAE has full three-bit affine hull; anchor points span GF(2)^r. Therefore even exact *local* affine-hull closure has no root pins. Each anchor term fixes the shared chart, all remaining blocks are independent. Root UNSAT; removing any one point-rejection component (retaining its private NAE) yields SAT with exactly 6^(r+1) models. Alternatively have every block reject the same non-anchor word 1^r; then all r+1 terms are feasible, mass=(r+1)6^(r+1). This demonstrates unbounded raw rank with ell=r+1, no root affine collapse, and no repeated global branch enumeration. These are explicit affine-factor controls, not DO09 high-girth critical cores or full physical E4/B4 source replays.
Hostile joint-rank control: x,y independent two-bit vectors, blocks reject x=0, y=0, x+y=0. Each pair of row spaces has zero intersection, but joint rank 4 rather than 6; true support mass 6 of 16, not 16*(3/4)^3. Joint test must merge or decline. Code checks UNKNOWN at merged rank cap zero and exact count six at cap four.

## 6. Falsify universal polynomial positive affine-cover acquisition by representation size
For k independent monotone NAE triples, the product relation has 6^k words and full affine hull. Any affine subspace A contained in it projects on each triple into an affine subspace of dimension <=2 (a dimension-three projection would include forbidden words). The map into the product of these projections is injective, so dim A<=2k and |A|<=4^k. Every exact union of positive affine subspaces representing the product therefore needs at least (3/2)^k terms, regardless of overlaps or choice of directions. This is an actual lower bound on this particular explicit positive-cover representation even after complete affine closure on the SAT product. It is NOT a lower bound on SAT runtime, or on finding a useful local anchor; independent factors solve immediately. Signed sums, structured circuits, matching, alternate trees and packet methods remain legal. On critical UNSAT roots the full legal relation is empty, but computing that fact is not a free cover. Hence the universal proposal 'flatten the global residual into a polynomial positive affine union and then apply A' is false as stated; a universal useful-anchor theorem remains open. The positive anchor source map must be proven, not acquired by uncharged semantic coverage (BW09 coNP barrier).
