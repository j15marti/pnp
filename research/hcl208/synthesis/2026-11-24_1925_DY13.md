## B. Rank-alone firewall (PROVED reduction; conditional consequence)
Reduce k-Multicolored Clique with k nonempty classes. One bit per vertex. Negative 2-CNF clauses forbid selecting two vertices in one class or any incompatible cross-class pair. For each class V_i require XOR_{v in V_i} x_v=1. Rows have disjoint nonempty supports, rank exactly k. At most one selected plus odd parity implies exactly one per class, hence a clique iff satisfiable. Polynomial construction/inverse and parameter-preserving. Multicolored Clique is W[1]-complete. Therefore universal f(k)N^C algorithm, including 2^O(k)poly(N), for general mixed domain implies FPT=W[1]. Conditional barrier, not P!=NP.
