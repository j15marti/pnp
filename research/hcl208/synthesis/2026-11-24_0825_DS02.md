## 1. Exact two-sided acquisition theorem (PROVED)
Fix an encoded guarded source F of length N. A verified partition of its constraints into regions has private variables occurring in only one region and boundary variables with globally authenticated identities. Additional global compatibility constraints may include exact owner budgets, signed XOR tags, guard equations or occupancy constraints, provided the exported row lists retain ALL needed observables and a positive row has an actual private witness. For each region i let D_i be a finite explicit candidate row universe (including all possible true rows), and R_i its exact supported subset. Let M=sum_i |D_i|. The outer decision functional J(A_1,...,A_t) is one iff a globally compatible tuple of rows exists in product A_i. It is monotone under coordinatewise inclusion, regardless of signs or costs. Assume J can be evaluated exactly, with witness tuple if true, in polynomial time for ALL subsets of the explicit universes used here. Sufficient examples: supplied checked running-intersection tree with polynomial-size tables (including adequate budget/tag state if present); logarithmically many total boundary bits; explicit bounded-width DP. The general theorem only needs monotone J and the ability to lift positive tuples. Merely having a compact symbolic graph is not this assumption.

Maintain L_i (certified positive rows with actual witnesses) and U_i (all rows not certified excluded). Invariant L_i subset R_i subset U_i. Then
  J(L) <= SAT(F) <= J(U).
If J(L)=1, glue stored private witnesses using the common boundary word and verify the original input. If J(U)=0, reject, even if many interior rows remain unqueried. Otherwise continue. If L=U in every region, the answer is exact. This is precisely the best possible conclusion based only on independent interval information: when J(L)=0 and J(U)=1 the abstract row completions L and U have opposite answers. They need not both be realizable by the SAME original source; a different source-aware deduction can resolve the gap.

An additional safe retirement rule: a candidate r in U_i with J(U restricted to r at i)=0 never occurs in any actual global model. Remove it from the global active upper domain and abandon its acquisition. Do NOT claim r is locally infeasible: r may be in R_i. After retirement maintain the invariant that every actual global model is represented in the active upper product, while active positive rows remain individually witnessed. Global incompatibility can only persist under further upper deletions. All facts are scoped to the fixed original constraints and current guard. A branch requires reauthentication or distinct key. Upper singleton/forced boundary identities are sound global deductions, not local identities. Run the finite certified closure at start, after deductions, and at stagnation before any further branch; each strict active-domain deletion consumes one of M credits. Pin tests or a complete outer support scan implement retirement at polynomial cost. At most M certified positive/negative status changes and M retirements; unsuccessful scans and restarts charged. Complete semantic closure is not assumed. Simultaneously acquired independent row results may be committed in a batch before restarting closure, since none weakens a sound result.

For running-intersection trees the outer witness is obtained by ordinary Boolean join-tree messages. Enumerate each pair of adjacent tables and test shared boundary equality; bottom-up messages and backpointers prove an explicit polynomial bound in M and N, even when the union of boundary variables is large. Full row universes may still be exponential; require M polynomial. If using explicit cost/syndrome tables, the same argument applies only to an actual sufficient separator representation, not arbitrary shared owners omitted from the tree. DP02 WHT counts can be used only when the exact nonnegative row semantics are preserved; signed terms are not lower/upper support bounds. S06/M08/I08 can serve as individual query channels, but arbitrary subsets of an implicit matching/flow domain are not necessarily matching/flow representable.

## 2. Decisive subset time theorem (PROVED)
There is a fixed finite library of uniform deterministic sound row-query channels A_j. Each can return YES with verifiable source witness, NO via a previously justified exact algorithm or checked exclusion proof, or UNKNOWN. A job is (source/version, region, entire guard and boundary row, channel). Include finite exhaustive fallback if completeness of every job is desired. A supplied manifest and its acquisition/verification are charged. Let t(r) be the minimum bit runtime of any channel actually certifying the true status of r, including output verification (infinity if none). For the true label z define a decisive subset C of row statuses as one such that after acquiring those statuses, J(L_C)=1 when z=SAT, or J(U_C)=0 when z=UNSAT. Define tau=min_C max_{r in C} t(r), infinity if no such set; empty C gives zero. This is an analytic parameter, NOT a cheaply computable potential. Both SAT and UNSAT may have small decisive subsets even if full table acquisition is slow.

Run every live job in a deterministic round robin on one machine, one simulated bit step per job per sweep, or restart all jobs with doubling time budgets (charge restarts). Whenever a certified status arrives, update bounds, run paid closure to fixed point, and check J(L),J(U). Retirement never delays a necessary certificate: globally irrelevant rows may be removed without their status, and doing so only strengthens upper rejection; for a lower witness its rows cannot have been retired while actual global witness exists. Thus by tau sweeps every necessary surviving certificate is available, and the answer is obtained without guessing the subset. With M rows and fixed K channels there are at most KM jobs. A straightforward deterministic bit-machine simulator incurs polynomial overhead in N+tau+M; outer closure/evaluation is polynomial in N+M, repeated at most O(M) times on actual changes (or at each sweep for a still-polynomial bound). A conservative bound is
  T <= discovery + poly(N+M+tau), S <= poly(N+M+tau), CM <= (T+1)S.
More explicitly the number of primitive simulated steps is <= KM(tau+1); administrative visits, checks, and table updates are separately charged. Store one exact key and witness per status; full comparison avoids hash collision assumptions. On a multitape bit machine copying/lookup overhead can be polynomial in total reached representation length; no arbitrary-cost RAM operations presumed. Both same-realization T and S use actual concurrent simulations and retained witness tables, not separately optimized implementations. Unknown jobs are discarded on termination. The fixed deadline h version admits explicit remaining credit KM*h + O(M) status/retirement credits (plus prepaid polynomial check overhead); every simulated step and actual state update consumes a credit. This is polynomial when h,M are polynomial; h can be doubled with geometric overhead, not presumed universally small. For recursive jobs use an already justified backend or an explicit well-founded decreasing task rank and full work ledger, not circular calls to this theorem on the original input. No exponential task tree is silently included in M.

Crucially, existence of a cheap decisive subset in a polynomially discoverable manifest is sufficient for a uniform scheduler; no algorithm for recognizing the optimal subset or tau is necessary. This weakens the universal target from full exact interface acquisition to only decisive acquisition. It does not evade DR02 on every source.

## 3. Shared-owner min bounds (PROVED corollary; not executed)

## 4. Sharp limitation and diagonal spine
DR02's switch H_F has equal-port support always and unequal-port support iff arbitrary F is SAT. Add an outer inequality a!=b. Then J on the remaining unknown rows encodes the original SAT problem; neither cheap equal-port witnesses nor the small affine boundary output settles it. Complement symmetry may reduce the two unknown unequal rows to one, but acquisition of that bit still has the original decision complexity. Therefore the universal existence of polynomially discoverable decisive certificates in the augmented fixed portfolio is at least as consequential as P=NP if it covers all encoded 3-CNF. This is not a superpolynomial lower bound. Conversely adding outer equality makes the hard rows irrelevant, and adding both equality and inequality permits rejection without solving ANY switch row to completion (can establish contradiction externally). This directly refutes a requirement that every regional support relation must be acquired before global decision.

Let A be the fixed exact portfolio: verify source compilation (BS01 explicit CNF/E4/B4 rows; strict physical transfer only where proved), paid sound closure, fair interleave certified direct terminals and manifest discovery, run selective bound acquisition for verified tractable outer instances, and interleave complete guarded fallback. Preserve all guards, row/owner IDs, cost floors, tags, source lifts. Fairness may be implemented by a global doubling budget on the full deterministic bounded job generator and each discovered manifest; at most polynomial overhead if discovery time, number of generated jobs, and successful certificate runtime are polynomial. Do not force an expensive channel to finish before another starts. Every partial answer is sound; fallback ensures completeness. If A has no uniform polynomial time bound, for each integer j choose increasingly large F_j with T_A(F_j)>|F_j|^j (otherwise eventual polynomial plus finite constants). For every fixed exponent d, sufficiently late F_j cannot have a decisive subset acquired within N^d on a verified manifest generated within N^d, with polynomially many jobs and outer cost bounded by N^d, because the actual fair schedule would answer in N^{O(d)}. In particular each such manifest's cheaply acquired lower join remains empty and upper join nonempty, or discovery/outer evaluation fails its paid deadline. This is stronger than listing individually stalled raw parameters and is the correct simultaneous obstruction. It concerns this fixed A, not every conceivable SAT algorithm. Neither the sequence label nor an unrestricted lower bound follows. If P!=NP were assumed, any exact solver with a polynomial bound on all UNSAT inputs would also imply P=NP by clocking and answering SAT on timeout; this does not follow from nonpolynomial A alone.

Minimal-counterexample spine: if for some fixed exponents a,c every residual under this charged model admits a discoverable decisive terminal OR a verified finite expansion of polynomial-size children with integer potential B=mu^c <= N^a and sum B(child)<=B(parent)-1, and per-node discovery, verification, acquisition, aggregation and reconstruction are polynomial in original N, then the least-credit unresolvable residual cannot exist by induction. Total expanded occurrences <= B(root), or actual distinct keys+attempted edges must be charged by a separate DAG ledger. Polynomial bound follows. First unproved universal lemma U-DS02: one such paid decisive terminal or contraction exists on every closed residual, including the DR02 unequal guard, DO01/DO02 critical cores after actual normalization, thin nonaffine flow blocks and joint guarded occupancy. Do not define mu from unknown true runtime. No universal coverage proof or disproof obtained. This is a concrete narrowed dependency, not a restatement as a free oracle.

Relativization: scheduling and credit arithmetic relativize, no claim to overcome Baker-Gill-Solovay; any future structural contradiction needs source-specific content.
