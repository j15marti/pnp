## 2. DY12-A: exact change of width under affine conditioning (PROVED)
Work in the dual observation space of the actual consistent affine chart. Fix a partition of owner IDs, with rowspace sums A and B and W=A intersect B, w=dim W. New independent affine equations have homogeneous rowspace H, on a nonempty chart; quotient map q into dual/H is surjective. Put A_H=q(A), B_H=q(B).
By dimension of sums and rank-nullity:
    w_H = dim(A_H intersect B_H)
        = w + dim(H intersect (A+B)) - dim(H intersect A) - dim(H intersect B).
Define k_H=dim(H intersect (A+B))-dim((H intersect A)+(H intersect B)) >=0.
Then
    w_H = w + k_H - dim(H intersect W).
This is an exact identity for raw quotient spaces, independent of RHS on consistent fibers. If true semantic spaces shrink further, it remains an upper bound on their overlap. The coupling term k_H measures the part of the guard that synchronizes otherwise separate observations; it need not be small merely because individual guard equations are sparse. In particular A=span(a), B=span(b), H=span(a+b) has w=0 and w_H=1. General bound w-rank(H)<=w_H<=w+rank(H). This explains DR12's guard-composition firewall without implying compulsory enumeration.

### Closure stability (PROVED)
If H is generated by rows each lying in one original owner space, H=(H intersect A)+(H intersect B) for EVERY owner bipartition; hence k_H=0 and w_H=w-dim(H intersect W)<=w. The same assertion holds for successive owner-local forced equations on evolving charts: if local factor f_i depends only on V_i, a necessary affine equation of its nonempty accepting support on the current guard has row in q(V_i). Indeed the support is invariant under ker q(V_i); any necessary row annihilates this kernel. Empty support is instead a contradiction. Lift that row to V_i plus existing guards, so the cumulative added guard modulo the initial chart is generated by owner-local rows. Induction preserves the exact identity for raw spaces and the monotone inequality for semantic shrinkage.
Thus a verified branch tree can be reused through arbitrary many paid owner-local closure deductions without widening any cut. This does NOT cover multi-owner deductions, arbitrary external guards, or freely discovering complete semantic closure. Those require new certification or archiving already proved consequences. Always keep RHS and actual conditional guards. Repeated necessary constraints do not change the global satisfying set.

## 3. DY12-B: subspace overlap tree with bundled leaves (PROVED)

Give a binary rooted tree whose leaves bijectively label actual owners. For node S (its leaf set), V_S=sum_{i in S}V_i; W_S=V_S intersect V_{outside S}. Let w_S=dim W_S. All these spaces and the tree leaf bijection are verifiable in polynomial time.

A word on V_S means a linear functional V_S -> F2. Every such word extends to the ambient chart, with 2^(d-dim V_S) preimages. Store M_S(lambda), the number of locally satisfying words on V_S with restriction lambda to W_S, plus one actual word when positive. At a leaf i, compute for each of 2^w_i possible lambda:
    M_i(lambda)=2^(-(d-dim V_i)) sum_t c_it # {z: packet_it(z), W_i z=lambda}.
Each affine intersection cardinality is zero for inconsistency or a power of two from Gaussian rank. Exact division accounts for unused degrees of freedom, never count signed labels as witnesses. Positive leaves obtain a real ambient representative by coordinate-prefix self-reduction using signed local counts; store its V_i word. Cost per candidate polynomial even for large local rank.

For internal S=A union B (disjoint child leaf sets, here A,B denote index sets), I=V_A intersect V_B is contained in both W_A and W_B. Also W_S subset W_A+W_B: for any u in W_S write u=a+b, a in V_A,b in V_B; since u lies in exterior V_C, a=u+b lies in V_B+V_C, and similarly b. Thus any two child keys agreeing on I glue uniquely on W_A+W_B and determine a parent key. Any two full satisfying child words of those keys extend uniquely to a word on V_A+V_B; proof: define evaluation of a+b by the sum, well-defined precisely by agreement on I. All such pairs are admissible and distinct, hence multiply exact positive child counts and add to the determined parent key. Different child-key pairs partition the parent support. An actual representative is glued by solving the child basis equations, not choosing an arbitrary signed packet. Empty table propagates contradiction. At root W=0, exact ambient count equals M_root(0) * 2^(d-dim V_all); positive count yields a verified witness. Both SAT directions and exact count established for this representation, unlike existential peel.

This is a standard linear-functional gluing DP specialized to signed-affine source packets, not a claimed new general branchwidth theorem. It combines DR12's bundled local fibers and M18's tree scheduling without requiring disjoint local variables or DP12's independent edge blocks. Width at most 2 for this three-leaf tree; exact compatibility is retained even though a simultaneous coordinate basis cannot represent all three as disjoint. For nonaffine predicates on these spaces, the same DP applies.
