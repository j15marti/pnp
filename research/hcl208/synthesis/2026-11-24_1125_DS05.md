## 2. DS05-A: exact forest terminal (PROVED)
For every explicit instance whose normalized incidence graph is a forest, a deterministic evaluator decides the guarded feasibility problem and reconstructs a source row selection in O(4^q poly(N)) bit time and O(2^q poly(N)) space, independent of numeric budget magnitudes except their binary bit lengths.

Root each tree at a component (isolated owner has no residual cost). Use exact messages indexed by subtree XOR word z.
Component i -> parent owner o: M_i(z) is minimum residual cost contributed to o among assignments satisfying ALL descendant owner budgets and with XOR z. If infeasible M_i(z)=infinity. For each row r, each child owner p supplies its table L_p; accept its word y iff L_p(y)+c_{ip}(r)<=K_p. Combine child words by XOR, with starting word g_i(r). For each reachable z take minimum c_{io}(r) over these rows. At a root replace parent cost by zero.
Owner o -> parent component i: L_o(z) is minimum sum of contributions to o from all its child component subtrees at XOR z. Compute iteratively by min-plus XOR convolution: (A*B)(z)=min_{a XOR b=z}(A(a)+B(b)), with optional truncation above K_o. The parent checks L_o(z)+its own contribution against K_o. Nonnegative costs justify truncation.
Proof: induction on oriented tree. Each subtree has disjoint source rows from the other child subtrees, and all interaction with its parent is one scalar threshold and the tag word. Lower cost weakly dominates higher cost at the SAME word, regardless of any parent continuation. At a component, fix the actual row before combining child messages, preserving every correlation between its costs at distinct owners and its nonlinear tag. At an owner, independent child subtrees sum costs exactly; minima attained. At roots, combine support across forest components by XOR; h is reachable iff original instance feasible. Backpointers (or copied actual witnesses) certify each finite entry. Verify original selected rows, all original costs and original guard; lift using paid component recipes.
Complexity: H=2^q, at most H entries per message. Each owner child convolution uses <=H^2 attempts. Each component row uses <=deg(i) such convolutions/filters. Final roots combine with <=H^2 attempts each. Every integer sum uses O(max input coefficient bits+log(number of rows)) bits. Retained messages, witness arrays, source IDs, reconstruction and even simple deterministic arrays/dictionaries all charged. Closure can be repeated at most original row count times per call. Thus time H^2 poly(N) and space H poly(N) including closure; no public vector enumeration, no assumption of exact weighted counts. CM<=(T+1)S for same serial evaluation. This is a decision-specific refinement of BS04, not a claim of min-plus validity for signed cancellation.
If q<=a log2(original N), fixed a, and local acquisition/verification/lift is polynomial in original N, this is a paid polynomial terminal. Applies when global owner product capacity is exponential even for unit residual costs.

## 3. DS05-B: discoverable forest backdoor and conserved branch credit (PROVED)

Removing C leaves a forest (remaining edges subset of original spanning forest). Let r_i=|R_i| at root closure and V=prod_{i in C} r_i. Discovery and exact bit computation of V are polynomial, no optimization of C required. One may also use a supplied certified smaller C, but charge acquisition.
Pin one row per component in C, retain guard and original costs; reclose after each pin, renormalize. Edges only disappear under row restrictions; after C is pinned graph is a forest. Stop early if forest or contradiction. Branch volume at most V; number of nodes <=(1+|C|)V including unary steps. For fixed remaining cutset choices let V(F)=product of remaining row counts for cutset factors; when splitting a nonsingleton coordinate into all individual row children, sum V(F_j)<=V(F), since closure only shrinks. A strict row deduction outside C is paid inside closure, not charged as a branch decrement. Terminal consumes its credit. ## 4. Two sharply scoped barriers (PROVED)
A. Replace forest by treewidth <=2: PARTITION with positive binary integers a_i and even sum 2B reduces to two public owners with caps (B,B), one component per a_i, rows (a_i,0) or (0,a_i), q=0. Feasible iff a subset sums to B. All local rows explicit, two each, zero base rank, no private constraints. Incidence K_{2,n} has treewidth at most 2 via bags containing both owners and one component. Odd total handled by fixed NO output. Membership in NP clear. Thus uniform poly for this more general binary-cost class would imply P=NP. This is weak NP-completeness, not strong/unit-cost hardness; numeric DP and special structure remain legal. Independent minima closure stagnates when max a_i<=min budgets. Same-structure controls a_i=2 (four components), caps (4,4) SAT and (3,5) UNSAT. Both are easy by parity; not an unrestricted hard family.
B. In DO01 raw paired-owner NAE representation, each hyperedge has two distinct owners incident to all three variables (both rows vary). Any factor-only forest deletion C must remove at least two variables per intact triple, since any two surviving variables plus its two owners make K2,2. Let k=|C| and I=V\C. Every surviving variable incident to an intact triple can be assigned the unordered pair of C vertices of such triple. Linearity of the hypergraph makes this map injective (two triples cannot share the same pair; one triple has at most one surviving variable). Hence n-k<=binom(k,2), k=Omega(sqrt(n)). On a single-owner relaxation, even if that nonbinding owner is discarded, at most 3 surviving variables lose the argument, so n-k<=binom(k,2)+3. DO01 cheap raw affordability closure stalls on both labels; XOR tags empty. This yields superpolynomial 2^k initial backdoor volume in n, and in explicit polynomial-size-in-n row encoding (m<=binom(n,2)/3). It does NOT prove actual branch execution exponential, nor exclude paired-owner fusion, Hall-core projection BH04, exact alternative transformations, early closure, or the unrestricted solver. For bounded maximum hypergraph degree Delta, counting intact triple incidences also gives k>=2(m-1)/Delta. This is a representation-specific parameter obstruction, not a lower bound.
