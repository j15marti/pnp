## 3. Theorem DS06-A (PROVED): exact nonlinear rank in polynomial acquisition time
Let c=g(0), A_i=g(e_i)+c (binary vectors), and let
 R(z)=g(z)+c+sum_i z_i A_i,
 U=span_GF(2){R(z):z in GF(2)^d}, r=dim U.
Then U is the smallest output subspace modulo which g is affine. Indeed R(0)=R(e_i)=0; if g mod W is affine, its values at 0 and e_i determine that affine map and imply R(z) in W for all z. Conversely g mod U is affine. Thus r is invariant under invertible affine reparameterization of z and invertible affine output-coordinate changes. Under affine restriction of the source chart, the minimal residual subspace is contained in U (in unchanged output coordinates). It may shrink.
Crucially compute U without enumerating 2^d words or expanding ANF after dense substitution. Take the union S of all numerator masks plus 0 and e_i. M=|S|. Let v(z)=(chi_s(z)) over Z/(2D). BD06-A's modular pivot orbit gives a list of at most MB actual generator words: starting at z=0, reduce v by pivots keyed by first nonzero coordinate and its lowest nonzero bit; queue each actual admitted word and explore its d bit flips. Each pivot subtraction progresses, at most MB pivots and MB subtractions per candidate. At most 1+dMB candidates; exhausted queue gives invariant module containing the entire orbit.
There is a module-linear map L taking v(z) to D R(z) modulo 2D in q coordinates: use A_j numerators, constant mask, and the identity D*z_i=(D/2)(1-chi_e_i). (Here A_i in the affine interpolant denotes binary columns, not the Walsh numerator.) Every output lies in the order-two subgroup {0,D}^q; under its identification with GF(2)^q the module span maps to the ordinary binary span. Hence computing R on the actual orbit generators and Gaussian-eliminating gives EXACT U, with all residual image directions witnessed by actual selector evaluations. No joint support or coverage recognition is inferred. Equivalently the affine hull of graph {(z,g(z))} is exactly {(z,c+A z+u):u in U}. Its rank is d+r. This is a graph-hull closure, NOT the affine hull of {z:g(z)=h}. The acquisition is polynomial in d,q,M,B and encoded coefficient length, even when r grows.
