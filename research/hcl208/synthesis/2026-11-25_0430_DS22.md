## PROVED: arbitrary-rank common adapted basis recognition
Input finite family of subspaces U_i given by binary bases. Let A=sum U_i, B initially empty. For each of at most dim A iterations start W=A. Scan all U_i in a fixed order, replace W by W intersect U_i iff the intersection is not contained in span B. Choose a basis vector of W outside span B and append to B. At end verify that B intersect U_i has cardinality dim U_i for every i. If it passes, B is an adapted basis. If any adapted basis exists, maintain an adapted basis C extending B. The chosen v has inclusion-maximal membership pattern among vectors outside span B whose membership contains its own: if i was rejected, W_final intersect U_i is contained in span B, since the intersection at the rejection was. In the C expansion of v any nonzero coefficient is on a vector in every U_i containing v (coordinate-subspace hypothesis); at least one such c is outside B. Its membership cannot strictly contain that of v, by maximality. Exchange c for v; they have identical memberships, so C remains adapted and extends the new B. Induction proves completeness of the algorithm. Gaussian intersection, span and containment tests give polynomial discovery for arbitrary ranks, with O(dim(A)*m) intersections; no enumeration of intersection lattices or vectors. BD22 rank-two criterion is a special case.

## PROVED: punctured-affine syndrome fusion
Represent a table by a+U with optionally one forbidden point p in a+U. Normalize empty and rank-one punctured cases (rank one becomes singleton). The exact pair sum of A=a+U minus optional p and B=b+V minus optional q, when W=U intersect V is nonzero, is a+b+(U+V), except if both punctured and dim W=1, in which case remove exactly p+q+w where w is the unique nonzero element of W. For any target s in the affine sum, all decompositions are a coset of W (choose u by Gaussian solve, add w to both summands). At most two decompositions are excluded, one by each puncture. For dim W>=2 there is always a choice; for dim W=1 both excluded iff s lies in p+q+W and is not p+q. If only one puncture, one exclusion cannot exhaust a nontrivial fiber. Reconstruction tries a fixed at most three distinct points of the fiber (if dim W=1 use both). No expanded truth table. Merge reduces sum of stored subspace ranks by dim W and number of owners by one.

Closure: for each table intersect with t+sum(other anchors)+sum(other spaces). ## PROVED: common-basis high-rank matching consumer

On d local bits append parity pad bit so total internal occupancy even. Affine table: clique on d+1 ports. Punctured table: same clique plus two compulsory private internal vertices, no edge between them, each adjacent to all ports. It has an internal matching covering both private vertices and precisely the occupied ports iff the occupied set is even and nonempty: pick two occupied ports for the vertices and pair remaining ports.
