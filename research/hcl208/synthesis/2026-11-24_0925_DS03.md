## 2. All-size group saturation is exactly group-colorability of a contraction (PROVED)
Let G be a finite connected loopless undirected graph, Q=GF(2)^2, C a binary fundamental-cycle matrix with b=m-n+1 rows. Work over Q coordinatewise. For each edge e, let d_e range over Q minus zero; define U_e={C_e d:d in Q}, R_e={C_e d:d nonzero}. Use actual edge IDs even when tags coincide. C has full row rank and ker C consists exactly of vertex differences, by tree integration. A signed graph with forbidden difference s_e on edge e is colorable iff C s lies in R_E=sum_e R_e: set d_e=s_e+x_u+x_v. This also proves both source directions and gives the witness reconstruction.

For a nonempty edge set S, set V_S=sum_{e in S} U_e and R_S=sum_{e in S} R_e. Let K_S be the multigraph formed by contracting the connected components of G-S and keeping all edges in S, including loops and multiplicity. Then

  R_S=V_S iff K_S is Q-group-colorable, meaning every assignment of a forbidden difference to its edges has a proper signed coloring.

Proof: any target in V_S is C_S a for some a in Q^S. A representation C_S d=C_S a with d nonzero coordinatewise exists iff the extension by zero of d+a lies in ker C, iff d_e+a_e=x_u+x_v for some vertex potentials constant on every component of G-S. This is precisely signed coloring of K_S with forbidden labels a_e. Gaussian solve supplies a and reverse potential integration supplies lift. Neither a given compact quotient nor a single unsigned coloring proves universal signed-colorability. Loops prohibit saturation (choose label zero). If K_S is loopless 3-degenerate counting parallel edges separately, reverse degeneracy order gives a proper signed coloring for every a (at most three forbidden colors at each step). Thus a supplied group can be certified and lifted without enumerating its sumset. Discovery of a globally helpful arbitrary S is NOT proved polynomial.

In particular deleting a cut with at most three cross-edge singleton exclusions between two unpinned translation-invariant subinstances is exact: solve both, translate one by a Q element avoiding the at most three bad shifts. Weighted extension: each crossing relative mask excludes a set of shifts, and sum of forbidden-mask cardinalities <=3 suffices. Four distinct parallel exclusions are a sharp hostile example. Extra global observations, pins, owner costs, or guards spanning the two sides must be kept or separately proved invariant; no silent use of independent gauge. This statement is decision and witness preservation, not model count or arbitrary pinned-row support.

## 3. All-factor and all-direction obstruction on critical graphs (PROVED)
Let H be connected edge-minimal non-four-colorable. For every edge e=uv choose a proper coloring of H-e, necessarily x_u=x_v. Its vertex-difference vector has syndrome zero and is nonzero everywhere except e. Replace d_e=0 by any nonzero value a. The resulting syndrome is exactly C_e a. Therefore
  (union_e U_e minus {0}) subset R_E, while 0 notin R_E.
In particular any linear quotient with kernel K intersecting any U_e nontrivially gives a FALSE positive for target zero. This rules out annihilating even one direction of one raw factor plane, including high-rank projections. Not all possible kernel directions need lie in a factor plane.

For any nonempty S, H-S is four-colorable by criticality. Choose its vertex differences on E-S (nonzero). Their syndrome belongs to V_S because full gradients have zero syndrome. If R_S=V_S we could repair with choices on S, making H colorable. Contradiction. Thus NO nonempty group of ANY cardinality has full-span saturation. This includes groups adaptively selected from the original raw factors, not arbitrary new representations or non-full-span fiber quotients.

SAT neighbor G=H-e0, e0=uv: suppose S nonempty and R_S=V_S for the cycle matrix of G. If u,v are connected in G-S, choose a proper four-coloring of H-S (SAT), including the retained e0. Its differences in G-S extend by saturation to a zero-syndrome word on G, preserving the differences along the path from u to v, hence preserving their disequality. This would color H, contradiction. Consequently every saturated group of G MUST separate u,v in G-S. This is a necessary condition, not sufficiency. High-girth critical graphs from DO03 inherit these two results; no high-girth construction executed here. Edge-minimality also implies no cut of <=3 edges by the gluing theorem. Its existence proof does not give uniform efficient critical-core discovery.

## 4. Full-puncture support for K5 and its closure under Hajós joins (PROVED)
Define property P(G): G is connected bridgeless, and its signed four-colorings exist exactly for unbalanced labels, where balanced means s is a vertex-difference vector (equivalently Cs=0). Equivalently R_E=Q^b minus {0}. Balanced signings gauge to ordinary coloring, which is impossible. This is substantially stronger than the DO03 low-rank obstruction.

Base K5: any unbalanced signing has a triangle with nonzero label sum (triangles span the cycle space). Pick triangle w,u,v, choose colors of u and v so they forbid the same color at w: x_v=x_u+s_uw+s_vw. Nonzero triangle sum ensures uv is satisfied. Color the other two vertices sequentially (at most three already colored neighbors). Finally w has at most three distinct forbidden colors among its four neighbors. This gives a constructive coloring of every unbalanced signing. Balanced signing is gauge-equivalent to unsigned K5, UNSAT.

Closure: let disjoint P-graphs G1,G2 have selected edges u1v1,u2v2. Delete the two edges, identify u1=u2=u, add v1v2. Each deleted-edge side remains connected. For arbitrary labels on that side, let D_i be its attainable relative boundary values x_ui+x_vi.
- If side labels are balanced, gauge them to zero. P implies unsigned G_i is UNSAT and deleting a nonbridge edge is SAT (perturb its forbidden label, use the unbalanced coloring). Thus D_i is exactly the singleton given by the side's potential difference.
- If side labels are unbalanced, restoring the deleted edge with ANY of the four possible forbidden differences still yields an unbalanced signing. P gives a proper coloring for each, so D_i has at least two elements. A singleton would be ruled out by the restored edge forbidding it.
Independent side witnesses can be translated to align u. The new cross edge is satisfied iff there exist d1 in D1,d2 in D2 with d1+d2 different from its forbidden label. If either side is unbalanced, this is always possible because at least one D_i has two elements. If both sides are balanced, the joined graph is balanced exactly when the cross label equals the sum of the two fixed boundary differences; this is exactly the case with no coloring. The joined graph is connected and bridgeless: any old cycle through a removed edge can replace it by the path through the other connected deleted-edge side and the new cross edge. The new edge also lies on a cycle. Hence P holds inductively.

For k copies of K5 joined this way, n=4k+1, m=9k+1, b=5k+1. Every such graph is edge-critical: perturb one edge label nontrivially; because it is not a bridge this signing is unbalanced, and its coloring satisfies every other original edge. Its ordinary coloring is impossible. These graphs contain triangles, not claimed high-girth.

**Exact projection impossibility.** For ANY nonzero kernel K <= GF(2)^(2b), the zero coset contains a nonzero attainable syndrome, so EVERY noninjective linear projection makes the UNSAT target feasible. This holds even for one-dimensional kernels and arbitrary adaptive selection of projection, with no constraints on the amount of preprocessing used to choose it. The statement applies only when retaining the original independent row factors and checking projected target equality; it does not forbid adding constraints, nonlinear transforms, or a different representation. In particular the full relation is the compact single puncture "syndrome != 0", expressible by one OR of its bits. This is a constructive symbolic multi-factor terminal for a verified Hajós certificate, not a hardness result. It also proves that lossless representation compression need not be linear dimension reduction. No claim that all critical graphs have full puncture.
