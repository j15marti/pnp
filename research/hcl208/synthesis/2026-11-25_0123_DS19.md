## Exact attainable veto signature composition (proved)
On a finite authenticated binary row chart, form one vertex per live row; cross-owner conflicts are edges, and same-owner rows cannot be jointly chosen. For a processed owner set A, a partial transversal P contains exactly one row per owner of A and satisfies all internal pairs. Its key is the union of its conflict neighborhoods intersected with live rows of exterior owners. All partial transversals with that key have IDENTICAL allowable exterior transversals, since compatibility across the cut is precisely absence of a selected exterior row in the key. Retain one actual representative (not a necessary row deduction). To add owner j, try every surviving row r not vetoed by the key V; next key is (V union N(r)) restricted to the new exterior. Merge identical masks. Induction proves exact completeness; empty exact table means UNSAT, and a final representative verifies SAT. This adapts established Boolean-width neighborhood-union DP to full owner transversals, not all subsets.

Cap K=(N+2)^c for fixed c with N the ORIGINAL encoded input length and a fixed polynomial cap on all acquired chart objects. Stop an attempted extension at K+1 distinct keys, discard it, and never infer UNSAT from overflow. At each stage test every remaining owner and choose the smallest completed table, deterministic ties. This is one exact adaptive order, not exhaustive order search. At most m(m+1)/2 attempted owner extensions, at most K*R row transitions per attempt (R registered rows), three simultaneous tables of at most K+1 keys plus representatives, polynomial dictionary/bit costs even with naive exact comparison. No recursive credit reset; closure initially and after each imported necessary deletion batch. Internal DP representative merging does not delete actual rows globally. A complete DP is a paid polynomial terminal; a capped failure consumes its paid attempt and must not be retried on the same chart for free. On success, verify reconstructed source, pay lifts; serial T,S polynomial, CM <= (T+1)S. Full portfolio remains unbounded. A binary join variant combines two internal representatives if neither vetoes the other, and takes restricted union; cross feasibility is class-invariant in each argument. Cap all table sizes and pay K^2 trials per tree node. If cross-conflict matrix rank over F2 is r, every union of chosen rows is union of a basis of their span (support of a linear combination is contained in union of basis supports), so at most the number of subspaces of F2^r, bounded by (r+1)2^(r^2). Thus r=O(sqrt(log N)) suffices on a supplied decomposition, not a free discovery theorem. This sufficient estimate is not necessary.

## Falsified: universal polynomial attainable-key bound even after complete pair closure
Take any bounded-degree edge-expander G on n vertices, one two-row owner per vertex, forbid only joint value 1 on adjacent vertices. All zeros is a model, each row and every allowed pair extends by setting every other coordinate zero. Thus even complete semantic singleton/pair closure cannot remove anything. For any balanced owner cut A|B, expansion gives Omega(n) crossing edges. Greedily select a crossing edge and remove every candidate having an endpoint adjacent or equal to either chosen endpoint. At most 2(D+1)D crossing edges disappear per step for maximum degree D; hence an induced matching of size Omega(n). For each subset of its A endpoints choose value 1 there, zero on the other A vertices. This is an actual internally feasible transversal. The veto at its corresponding B endpoint is present precisely for the selected A endpoint, by inducedness, giving 2^Omega(n) distinct attainable keys. Every owner order crosses a balanced cut; every binary owner decomposition has a cut with each side at least n/3. The explicit encoded pair chart is polynomial in n. No fixed polynomial key cap suffices on these unchanged registries for ALL orders/trees, even with exact pair closure. But the family is a trivial SAT/2-CNF terminal, so NOT simultaneous hardness, NOT P!=NP. This rules out using low Boolean-width as a universal invariant solely from closure. It does not rule out alternative symbolic representations, early witness detection or scope changes.
