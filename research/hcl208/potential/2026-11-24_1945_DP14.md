## A. Exact model and mixed-cut bound (PROVED)
Finite explicit loopless regional multigraph, labels t_v in [L_v], local signed rational coefficient a_v(t_v), each edge e=(u,v) carries exact factor K_e(t_u,t_v)=sum_{k=1}^{r_e} U_e(t_u,k)V_e(t_v,k). Orient by a supplied actual vertex order v_1,...,v_n; transpose factors if necessary. All label tables, factor entries and IDs explicit and polynomial in source length N, or acquired and charged before terminal launch. L_v,r_e>=1; an empty region or zero edge matrix gives zero sum. DP13 endpoint-span factorization provides probe-stable r_e under its supported local pins and parity-character weights; do not replace it by the unconditioned cross-rank. Sum Z=sum_t product_v a_v(t_v) product_e K_e(t_u,t_v). SAT iff Z>0 only with authenticated nonnegative Boolean source semantics; a general signed sum is an arithmetic value, not a verdict. Parallel edges have distinct IDs. For explicit ordinary 3-CNF/NAE, use one equality-copy region per variable with two labels (all its incidence bits equal), one clause region with at most eight legal local words, and one independent incidence bit per edge. Identity edge factor over its bit enforces actual variable equality; each source model has exactly one label lift. Unused variables contribute two choices. This is a polynomial both-label/count-preserving adapter, not a strict physical E4/B4 compiler. For general DP13 affine-character adapter acquisition remains charged.

After processing prefix P_i, for each u in P_i let E_i(u) be its crossing edges to future vertices, R_i(u)=product_{e in E_i(u)} r_e (empty product one). Choose to retain the single actual label t_u if L_u<R_i(u), otherwise replace it with the tuple of all crossing rank indices for this star. Define
 B_i=product_{u in P_i} min(L_u,R_i(u)).
This is an efficiently computable bound on the number of mixed boundary keys, not the rank of a semantic residual. It is no larger than either the pure label frontier or the pure rank frontier at the same cut. Every u's R_i(u) is nonincreasing in i, so a retained label switches to rank indices at most once. Empty star is forgotten. All its future edge factors are represented jointly; no independent choice of an original hub label per consumer.

Invariant: the table represents the partial sum over processed labels with unary coefficients and internal edge factors included once; for expanded crossing stars multiply their outgoing U_e factors and index each k_e, leaving the future V_e factors unevaluated. For retained stars leave crossing K_e unevaluated and preserve the actual t_u. Thus the contraction of the table with the future factors equals Z. At v=v_{i+1}, for each old key and t_v, multiply a_v(t_v). Each incoming edge from a retained u contributes full K_e(t_u,t_v); each from an expanded star contributes V_e(t_v,k_e), after which k_e is summed out. Merge identical remaining keys by exact addition. Temporarily retain t_v. Now each star that should switch replaces its single label by ALL of its remaining rank indices, multiplies by the product of their U_e(t_u,k_e), and sums over the old label. This is the distributive law exactly, including negative cancellation. Perform the switches sequentially. Forget labels with empty remaining stars. Every unprocessed edge still appears exactly once in the future representation. At root P_n all keys empty and the invariant gives Z. No semantic equivalence oracle. In a DAG-style implementation the same frontier cell can have many consumers; its outgoing transitions are explicitly charged, not treated as one operation. Original label multiplicity never reintroduced after a switch. No arbitrary nonlinear shared-subexpression unfolding permitted (BD13).

## B. Paid weighted potential (PROVED)
Let Lmax=max_v L_v. Immediately after processing v but before switches, key capacity <=B_i L_v: consumed rank indices only disappear. Each switch replaces a label domain L_u by a rank tuple of size R_{i+1}(u)<=L_u, so the capacity never increases through these microsteps. Enumerate each switch from at most B_i L_v keys, with at most Lmax emitted keys per input; there are <=n switches per event even with a conservative implementation. The total operation reserve is
 J_mix = 1 + sum_{i=0}^{n-1} (n+1) B_i L_{v_{i+1}} Lmax.
Polynomial factors cover key construction, all matrix entries, factor multiplications, exact additions and table lookups (deterministic trie or balanced tree suffices; JS Map is experimental). B_i products have polynomial bit length even if their values are large, and J can be computed exactly or capped before launch. Fixed graph/order and stable upper ranks make this reserve reusable under supported probes, including domain deletion. Charge order acquisition and unsuccessful orders; a small order's existence is not an efficient search algorithm. A fixed cap J<=N^C for a fixed C is a paid polynomial terminal. More tightly, each remaining microstep and each enumerated emission can be allotted its own job credit; processing one credit, skipping unused jobs or splitting a pre-reserved enumeration interval into disjoint subintervals gives sum child credits <=parent credit-1 with c=1. For q+1 actual queries preallocate q+1 copies of the fixed budget, and charge closure/reconstruction and all accesses separately or with a larger fixed polynomial factor. This is an execution-job potential, not a universal potential on arbitrary residual formulas. No independent branch duplication is allowed beyond the paid budget. The same table may be reused for exact identical certified keys; charge all consumer reads. Recomputing it is a new job.

For a serial evaluator T<=A+(q+1)poly(N)J_mix, S<=poly(N)(1+max_i B_i L_v), CM<=(T+1)S. Here A includes source acquisition, validation, adapter discovery/verification, stable-rank acquisition, graph/order construction, failed attempts, certified closure and reconstruction checks. For rational inputs clear denominators per factor table by positive common products; bit lengths stay polynomial. Each table entry is a sum of terms involving at most O(n+m) supplied factors, with at most product L_v product r_e terms (logarithm polynomial); exact integer arithmetic and final division therefore polynomial per entry. Same execution for T,S,CM. Two successive tables suffice; sharing source once does not erase repeated access charges. A SAT witness in a genuine Boolean source is found by sequential original-coordinate pins and exact nonnegative counts, at most one test per binary coordinate plus final verification. DP13 supports the relevant edge/private pins. In the explicit incidence adapter variable labels may be pinned directly. Pins in these witness queries are conditional, not exported necessities. An arbitrary signed internal state is not a witness.

Certified closure: the explicit label implementation removes a zero unary label or any label with no nonzero compatible neighbor in the current domains (test full K, not a rank component); all such removed labels contribute zero to the exact sum. Restart after each strict deletion and stop only at a fixed point, reject empty domains. For genuine Boolean tables this is certified local forced-information closure, including singleton pins, performed at root and for every guarded query. No full semantic affine hull, full Gaussian closure or general cooperative terminal was executed. In the general signed expansion this is a zero-contribution algebraic simplification, not necessarily a logical deduction about source variables. Stronger known source deductions can precede this adapter, followed by recertification of affected keys/ranks. Arbitrary dense guards that do not factor on DP13's blocks are outside the reuse claim.

Combine with DP13 block-cut tree: for a block use min of its paid feedback-slice budget and mixed-order budget (up to a polynomial factor for parent-label pins if necessary); evaluate exact block message once for every actual parent articulation label, exclude parent's original coefficient as in DP13. Child messages sharing an articulation multiply on the SAME label. Aggregate costs ADD across blocks, not a product. This combination is symbolic; checker executes the mixed frontier itself.

## C. Strict feedback separation and universal firewall (PROVED)
The 2 x k ladder (k>=2) is one biconnected block, n=2k,m=3k-2, so any spanning tree leaves k-1 feedback edges. Give each incident wire an independent bit and each vertex the even-parity relation on its incident bits, with optionally one flipped vertex syndrome. Local labels are parity words (2 at endpoints,4 interior); identity matching matrices have stable endpoint ranks 2. DP13 slice reserve R=2^(k-1). Column-by-column order, top then bottom, has at most three crossing edges at any cut, so B_i<=8. Mixed evaluator polynomial; even syndrome count 2^(k-1), odd syndrome count zero by graphic GF2 rank n-1. This strict difference concerns the two evaluation schedules, NOT a lower bound; Gaussian elimination also solves these easy controls. Demonstrates that a large biconnected block and large feedback product do not suffice for diagonal obstruction.

Universal low mixed frontier is FALSE on unchanged registries even with degree<=4. Take k x k even-sided grid with one region per vertex, two labels representing one bit copied to all its incident wires, each edge identity or fixed XOR permutation, stable rank2. At half-vertex cut of ANY order, >=k/4 rows are mixed or there exist both a completely full and completely empty row (if fewer than k/4 mixed rows, half selected and half unselected cannot fit in mixed rows). In first case at least k/4 horizontal crossing edges, in second every column has a vertical crossing edge. Each processed boundary vertex touches <=4 crossing edges; hence >=k/16 such vertices. Every such vertex contributes min(2,2^degree_cross)=2, so B_i>=2^(k/16) for all orders, superpolynomial in explicit grid encoding. All-equality root is SAT (two models); flip one interior edge on a cycle for UNSAT, same reserve. Local arc consistency leaves both labels everywhere, but global parity closure solves both; this is a raw-registry potential barrier ONLY, not a simultaneous post-closure hard family and not necessary work for any algorithm. The grid bound is symbolic, not benchmarked. A polynomial number of reachable states can still occur despite the large capacity bound; no lower bound on actual reachable keys is claimed.
