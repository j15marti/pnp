## 2. DP09-A sharp affine branch-credit law (PROVED)
Let A,B be the row spaces of local observations on either side of a fixed cut of a common free affine chart. Let k=dim(A intersect B). A rank-p independent affine guard H is a subspace of the chart dual, with one branch for each of the 2^p RHS words (each nonempty as a chart, not necessarily legal for the nonlinear factors). In the conditioned chart the observation spaces are images under q: V -> V/H. Write a=dim(H intersect A), b=dim(H intersect B), u=dim(H intersect (A+B)). By rank-nullity,
  k_H=dim(qA intersect qB)=k+u-a-b.
Hence the sum of nominal separator capacities over ALL chart children is
  sum_rhs 2^k_H = 2^k * 2^(p+u-a-b).
Since a,b<=u<=p, p+u-a-b>=0, with equality exactly when a=b=u=p, i.e. H subset A intersect B. When H subset A+B, multiplier is 2^(2p-a-b). This is independent of RHS and polynomial-bit computable by Gaussian elimination. It is a capacity identity before additional nonlinear simplification; it is not a lower bound on actual states, work, or all possible algorithms.

One-bit cases: shared row h -> k_H=k-1, both branch capacities sum to parent; row in exactly one side -> k_H=k, factor 2; row in A+B but neither side -> k_H=k+1, factor 4; row outside A+B -> k_H=k, factor 2. This last case is about chart labels, possibly completely irrelevant to the decision. Do not equate pinning an arbitrary chart row with DS08 coordinate-column deletion in the occurrence representation.

Hostile control: two independent monotone NAE triples on disjoint three-bit blocks, A and B their coordinate spans, k=0. Both local legal sets have full affine hull and contain six words. Split on h=x1+x4. Both branches have 18 source assignments, no forced affine relation beyond the protected guard (for each shared first-bit value, each remaining two-bit fiber has three points spanning F2^2; differences span the four private directions, and both shared values occur). k_H=1, child nominal capacities 2 and 2. No fixed positive exponent c makes 2^c+2^c<=1^c-1; even a shared-only split gives equality at c=1, not strict progress without a separately reserved internal job. p independent copies give 2^p nonempty explicit guard contexts, each with 18^p models, after the same restricted/full affine closure; this is a counterexample to universal cheap explicit branching, not hard SAT. A complete decision solver can choose a single witness or solve independent factors directly. No inference about unrestricted complexity.

## 3. DP09-B simultaneous separator-local guard multiplexing (PROVED)
Use DS08's authenticated independent local relations R_i and explicit syndrome columns P_i; normalize a consistent RHS with a linear baseline y^0. For an unrooted degree<=3 decomposition tree with factor leaves, every edge e induces J|Jc. Let I_e=im P_J intersect im P_Jc and k_e=dim I_e. Each side's shifted syndrome is in I_e for any compatible word, and the two sides must agree in GF(2). For a common observation chart this separator has dimension dim(A intersect B) by DS08. In that chart any h in A intersect B induces a linear functional on the shared syndrome plus the baseline affine constant: express h in local rows on J, and note it annihilates ker P_J (a compatible homogeneous change supported wholly in J, invisible on Jc). Therefore the value is determined by the syndrome and can be computed from any linear lift by Gaussian elimination, independently of the local nonlinear predicates. Obtain the same value from the opposite side. This applies to any affine row family H contained in the shared intersection.

More generally impose arbitrarily many explicit or polynomial-time evaluable masks M_e(s) on separator states, including nonaffine masks. The mask must be authenticated as a function of the actual shared syndrome, not merely some regional guard of comparable rank. Group the masks per edge, charge their evaluation and preserve original IDs. Masks may be placed simultaneously on all edges; total global guard rank need not be small. No new separator coordinates are required. A guard on a shared h has a mask selecting its desired RHS; an arbitrary h outside the intersection cannot be presumed to admit such a mask. In fact, on the unconstrained linear chart a row's value is determined by the separator alone iff it belongs to A intersect B (the annihilator of the sum of the two kernels of the side observation maps; chart-invisible directions included). Nonlinear support can permit additional dependencies but their discovery is not free.

Compute both directional messages m_(u->v)(s) for every edge; a message stores precisely the support of its side conditional on the boundary syndrome and all masks strictly internal to that side. At an interior degree-three vertex, combine the two incoming messages by XOR, check their edge masks, and filter the sum to the outgoing intersection. At a degree-two vertex pass through with the incoming mask. At leaves use DS08 paid affine-fiber oracle, or explicit local relation. An edge's full compatible support is m_(u->v) intersect m_(v->u) intersect M_e. Compute the two passes (or dependency-ordered memoized recursion); exactness by the same disjoint-block induction as DS08, since each interior region is a disjoint union of its incoming sides. Any retained edge state admits actual compatible lifts on both sides; do not independently select edge states from different marginals. A witness uses one retained state and follows stored compatible child choices, checking all source constraints and mask IDs. Optional exact counts multiply side counts and sum over compatible states when counting local tuples is intended; auxiliary cut-label/gauge choices can overcount projected source models. For general leaf support only use Boolean messages.

This is a batched replacement for separate branch evaluation: all possible labels of every single separator-supported affine query can be read from these messages with no rebuilding; report only explicitly requested labels or the factored mask support. Multiple simultaneously asserted edge-local guards remain in the same complexity bound, even if their choices would have 2^p combinations under naive enumeration. The construction does NOT list all combinations or evaluate arbitrary nonlocal guard predicates. A changed mask invalidates messages whose internal side contains it; do not reuse those under a new context without recomputation. Distinct leaf contracts, source IDs, guards and count semantics remain separate. This is exact tree message passing specialized to DS08, not a new claim about classical sum-product.

## 4. DP09-C paid shared arithmetic potential (PROVED restricted)
For each oriented tree edge a=(u->v), reserve
 B_a=1+2^k_a+ product_(b entering u from neighbors other than v) 2^k_b,
 with empty product 1. Reserve additional sum_e 2^k_e for all compatible-edge scans, plus polynomial setup, fixed mask checks (or multiply each job by polynomial in complete explicit mask size), original witness, closure and verification overhead. Every pair attempted including failures, each possible output state, lookups, consumer edges and stored witness/backpointer is charged. Let B=1+sum_a B_a+sum_e 2^k_e. Each directed message depends on smaller sides and is computed once for its fixed complete contract. A potential of outstanding reserved jobs decreases by at least one per scheduled job, including setup/aggregation jobs; unused credit released. Thus c=1, T<=poly(N)B, S<=poly(N)(1+sum_e 2^k_e), CM<=(T+1)S for one serial implementation. Exact integer tuple counts have polynomial bit length (at most sum local word lengths plus coefficient bits if explicitly counting nonnegative multiplicity). B polynomial-bit computable; for supplied verified k_max<=a log N, a fixed constant, B polynomial. This is a polynomial bound for the fully charged decomposition terminal, not a universal potential on arbitrary residuals. Polynomial closure can be prepaid per independent pin; after initial restricted closure, exact marginal deductions are unconditional in the retained guard, add them one at a time and reclose, at most polynomial many independent pins. Existing messages remain valid if deductions are certified redundant in that fixed representation; if chart or predicate changes, pay a rebuild. No assumed cheap optimum tree discovery.

A single separator query splits the existing table into disjoint cells: its label enumeration can use the ordinary exact trie credit 2^(p+1)-1 (p<=k_e), internal query nodes charged. Across multiple cuts the potential accounts for the union of directed jobs, not the sum of independently recomputed branches. Shared copies must have the same full contract; idempotence of Boolean predicates does not imply equality of physical count multiplicities. Unbounded context creation, canonicalization, mask acquisition, physical source adapter, matching quota and nonlocal guard discovery are not free.
