## Ordered predecessor avoidance and collective fusion
For forbidden affine events E_i on a consistent actual affine guard G, choose any total order and sets P_i of at most w earlier IDs. Define
f(x)=sum_i 1[E_i(x)] product_{j in P_i}(1-1[E_j(x)]).
It is nonnegative integer and is zero iff no event occurs: the earliest violated event has no violated predecessor. Thus E[f|G]<1 certifies SAT. Each term is an exact inclusion-exclusion sum over at most 2^w affine intersections. Gaussian elimination counts each intersection under a guard, exactly, with O(n)-bit counts. Fix the order and predecessor lists during reconstruction; choose a nonempty next bit side of no larger conditional expectation (weighted average identity). Final f<1 implies f=0; verify original events/guard. No independence needed. This is related to established cherry-tree and multitree union probability bounds, not a claim of novelty for the probability inequality itself. See Bukszar/Prekopa 2001 DOI 10.1287/moor.26.1.174.10596 and Bukszar 2001 DOI 10.1017/S0001867800010880 (abstracts inspected). For fixed w, acquire a BH22 maximum-weight spanning tree and orient/traverse from root; for each event retain its original tree predecessor or choose an earlier set of <=w IDs minimizing its term. This never worsens BH22, because choices are independent for the fixed order and w>=1. Search O(m^{w+1}2^w) Gaussian counts, lift O(nm2^w); all failures charged. No optimal global order needed or claimed.

Uniform fusion: for a punctured table sum with summation kernel K, if each projection onto {i} union P_i is surjective onto the product of the corresponding slot spaces, the term on every target fiber has expectation p_i product_{j in P_i}(1-p_j), p_i=2^{-dim U_i}. If sum<1, exact affine sum and polynomial reverse lift for every target follow. Surjectivity via Gaussian rank. Full affine tables can be omitted from the event list but kept in the kernel. With fixed w and specified polynomially many candidate groups, fuse only groups of >=2 slots, retain source IDs and map/lift; original W=sum ranks+table count decreases by >=1 per fusion because new rank <= sum old ranks. Necessary hull restrictions also strictly decrease W; no reserve reset, and polynomial total lift cost with a tree of disjoint merged owner groups.
