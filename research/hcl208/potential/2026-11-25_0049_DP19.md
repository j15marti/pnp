## PROVED: guarded row replacement (conditional domination)
Let D_i be live owner domains, E symmetric inter-owner compatibility, N(r) all live compatible rows from other owners. A feasible transversal selects one row per owner and all selected pairs in E. For r in D_i choose a different pivot owner j. For each a in D_j intersect N(r), supply s_a in D_i minus {r}, with a in N(s_a) and
    (N(r) minus N(s_a)) intersect N(a) = empty.
Then deleting r preserves existential feasibility, and indeed the exact projection onto *all exterior owners*. Proof: any feasible transversal using r selects one compatible a; every other selected row t belongs to N(r) intersect N(a), hence N(s_a), and a is compatible with s_a. Replace only r. Converse is subset inclusion. A completed check after arc closure includes every compatible pivot row; a failed or capped check gives no certificate. Global neighborhood inclusion is a special case, not necessary. Apply certified closure initially, after each accepted move, and at stagnation. Record that the new chart is a representative restriction under this guard, NOT that r is impossible. No all-model/count claim.

With R original registered rows, a naive scan over r,s,a and bit-vector containment costs O(R^4) bit operations, with polynomial storage; at most R deletions. Scanning all pivots adds no more than polynomial work (each a determines its owner). At fixed declared span r0 and fixed original-input row/edge cap B, h=live rows+live pair entries strictly decreases, so W=(B+1)(q-1)+h+1 with q=(1+r0)^4 contracts by >=1. Keep original declared coordinates and guards; no reset. All failed scans, closure, certificate copies, provenance, row/edge reads, and source witness verification/lifts paid. A serial implementation on admitted polynomial charts has polynomial time/space and CM<= (T+1)S. Other recursive occurrences still require DR18 base-credit contraction, and no guarantee of universal chart acquisition or folding eligibility follows.

## PROVED: guard transport firewall
A fold preserves projection on exterior rows, so commutes with any later guard depending only on those rows. It does not in general commute with new restrictions on the folded owner, nor license unconditional source equations or row deletions in a parallel unfurled chart. Any peer consuming representative facts must use the same representative state and guard, or prove an invariant/lift for the source fact. For SAT, every surviving transversal is already a legal pre-fold transversal; earlier GD24/BS17 lifts still apply. For UNSAT, induct exact projection. No need to reconstruct every removed witness. If each coordinate of the folded owner is observed by at least one other owner, every feasible exterior selection fixes its row uniquely: any single-owner replacement preserving exterior rows is impossible on a feasible original transversal. Hence such a fold can delete only genuinely infeasible rows in a raw all-shared coordinate chart; scope-changing or multiowner replacements are required to obtain true decision-only compression there.

## PROVED: raw fixed point on signed linear NAE
In an unpinned linear 3-uniform NAE registry of minimum variable degree 2, no row r has the certificate above. For any alternative s choose a coordinate v where r,s differ and another owner k containing v. If the pivot is k, a compatible a fixes v to r(v), excluding s. Otherwise choose a k-row t agreeing with r at v and with a on its at-most-one common coordinate. These pins are consistent (if the common coordinate is v, a already agrees with r) and at most two pins always extend to signed NAE. Linearity gives exactly the required pair compatibility; t is incompatible with s. Thus no replacement for any compatible a. AC/PC also stagnate on raw linear NAE by DS18. All Fano labels and high-girth raw controls satisfy this barrier, but other terminals can escape it. This rules out universal folding/credit progress, not general polynomial decision. No unrestricted lower bound, no relativization transfer.
