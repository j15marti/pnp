## A. Exact four-edge collision theorem (PROVED)
Q=GF(2)^2. Each signed edge forbids ONE relative color: x_u+x_v != s_e. Let A and B be disjoint regions with independent constraints invariant under uniform Q-translation; no omitted pins, external tags, budgets or cross constraints. Exactly four crossing incidences indexed i=1..4 have endpoints a_i in A, b_i in B and labels s_i. Repeated endpoints allowed; original edge IDs retained. For any fixed feasible A assignment x, let R_B be the set of feasible B port words y=(y_1,...,y_4). Define six 4-bit masks
    M_ij={y_i+y_j : y in R_B}, 1<=i<j<=4.
Each bit is acquired by an exact decision/witness query with one two-bit relative observation. For every x (even when its ports coincide),
    exists y in R_B, t in Q: all four crossing constraints hold after translating B by t
    iff exists i<j: x_i+x_j+s_i+s_j in M_ij.
Proof: for fixed x,y the forbidden translations are f_i=x_i+y_i+s_i. A legal translation exists iff these four values do not exhaust Q. Since there are exactly four, this is equivalent to a collision f_i=f_j for at least one pair. This equality is exactly the displayed mask membership. If a mask bit is witnessed, use ITS particular B solution and test four translations, not a mixture of different witnesses. Conversely any successful gluing yields a collision and its exact mask membership. Empty R_B means all masks empty and the message rejects. Relative queries are translation invariant. The message is decision/projected-extension exact for every fixed parent assignment, not exact original-row support, private multiplicity or model counting.

Only 24 exact bit queries suffice instead of acquisition of all 4^3=64 gauge-normalized boundary words. This is a bound on distinct demands, not a lower bound or necessarily optimal. No enumeration of the B interior is required IF the paid local terminal supports the probes. General <=3-edge cuts need only an unconditioned witness (DS03). For a four-edge cut, the message is an OR of six pair-mask conditions (each is a two-bit observation). To encode in BW03/BH03 affine-disjunction language, enumerate the 64 normalized parent-port words, retain rejected words as conjunctions of six affine equations; complement each rejection. Repeated endpoints can make some equations inconsistent; safely discard these. At most 64 such guards per interface, constant construction size apart from endpoint IDs.

## C. Counterexample to universal composition of easy regional terminals (PROVED)
Star of regions: arbitrary graph H as edgeless central region (one color variable per original vertex); for each source edge uv add a private leaf singleton w. Exactly four signed cross incidences: between u,w forbid shifts 1,2,3 separately; between v,w forbid shift 0. If x_u=x_v all four colors of w are forbidden. If x_u!=x_v at least two forbidden colors coincide and there is a legal w. Thus each leaf projects to the original constraint x_u!=x_v, independently, with explicit linear-time lift per source edge. Every isolated region has trivial SAT decision; leaf profiles are all trivial. Nevertheless augmented center is arbitrary four-colorability. The signed multigraph star domain with supplied partition, constant four-edge interfaces and individually edgeless regions is NP-complete (membership immediate; ordinary 4-colorability NP-complete).
Simple graph version: replace each w by four distinct private ports all forced equal using three ordinary K5-minus-edge equality gadgets sharing one reference port. Each equality gadget is 4-colorable iff its two tips equal, since the other three vertices form a triangle adjacent to both tips. Use distinct cross ports. This keeps each private region constant-size, all graph edges simple, exact same four singleton exclusions and both SAT directions. Symbolic, not executed. Strict all-vertex tetravalence is NOT asserted.

Therefore the sufficient condition "all base regions easy plus four-edge interfaces" is not a proved polynomial terminal; it would imply P=NP if asserted universally with paid augmentation. This is NOT a proof that the domain cannot be solved in polynomial time. It also shows why any potential solely counting unprocessed interfaces can hide a hard final local job. Count-volume, matching, Pfaffian or common-basis conditions must be retested on the actual augmented center. Ordinary local vertex domains can remain full by translation symmetry; absence of unary deductions does not decide SAT.

## D. Sharp five-edge limitation (PROVED)
For five ports, take normalized words y_1=0. R_bad = all words using all four colors, R_good = all words (256). Both have full pairwise difference masks at every pair: any chosen pair can coincide, using remaining three positions for other colors, or differ with all four colors still represented. With fixed parent word zero and labels zero, R_bad permits no translation, R_good does. Both unnormalized relations are translation invariant. Thus pair masks alone are not exact at five edges, even with full acquisition. This is an explicit relation counterexample, not claimed realization by a strict physical wheel. General k-edge repair: enumerate partitions into at most three color classes, or equivalently choose a spanning forest of k-3 collision equalities among k port positions (if k>=4). At most three distinct forbidden shifts iff such a forest exists with all its equalities satisfied. Each forest requires JOINT two-bit relative observations, never independently combine single-pair masks. For fixed k this is a constant-size exact demand interface, with new local probe costs; no universal polynomial bound when k grows. Not implemented beyond the 5-port counterexample.
