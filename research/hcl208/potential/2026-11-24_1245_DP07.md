## 3. DP07-A: Exact conditional-rank peeling
Fix any order pi of remaining events. For i at its deletion step let S be remaining event IDs including i; H_i=G intersect E_i. J_i(S)={j in S minus i: H_i intersect E_j nonempty}. Obtain adjacency by pair Gaussian consistency. Let L(A) be the row space of coefficient forms of affine system A. Define
  r_i(S)=dim(L(H_i)+sum_{j in J_i(S)} L(E_j))-dim L(H_i).
  k_i(S)=min(|J_i(S)|,r_i(S)).
The disjoint first-hit set is C_i=H_i minus union_{j in J_i(S)} E_j. Here use the LAST event in the order (later events excluded); the sets C_i partition the rejected words by their last active event. Pair nonneighbors cannot be active on H_i, but no pair approximation is made for neighbors. Exactly
  Z=|G|-sum_i |C_i|.
Compute each |C_i| by either (a) inclusion-exclusion over subsets of J, exact Gaussian counts of all intersections with H_i, or (b) extend a coefficient basis of H_i by independent forms from J, enumerate all 2^r assignments to these observations, and test each neighbor's equations within the resulting affine chart. Every observation word has exactly 2^(n-rank(H_i)-r) preimages; all neighbor predicates are constant on that fiber. Count the fibers satisfying no neighbor. Hence local work <=2^k poly(N), using the cheaper alternative. No omitted higher intersection and no signed approximate identity.
For BA06 multiple repair regions, intersect the exact legal chart with all acquired dual events from every region, using one common authenticated selector chart. This counts selectors feasible in ALL regions if the original repair variables are disjoint once the chart is fixed; shared repair variables require a new exact joint adapter. A global witness follows by positive chart count and local Gaussian repair reconstruction. Source count transfer still requires explicit multiplicity certification.

## 4. DP07-B: Greedy discovery of the optimal bottleneck
For fixed i, k_i(S) is nondecreasing with S: neighbors only added, quotient span only grows. Greedily peel an i of minimum k_i(S). Let K*=min_pi max_step k_i(S). For any S, take the first member of S in an optimal full order. All S members were present when it was removed there, so its cost on S <= K*. Therefore every greedy step costs <=K*, and the greedy maximum is exactly K*. This is a generalized smallest-last argument, proved here directly; no decomposition search oracle.
Define P_pi=sum_i 2^k_i(S). Greedy P<=m*2^K* <=m*min_pi P_pi. The greedy order is not asserted to minimize the sum exactly. All ranks, pair tests, greedy scans, and integer powers are computable in polynomial bit time, even if numerical P is exponential. For every fixed a, if ANY order has P<=N^a, greedy has P<=mN^a; equivalently K*=O(log N) is a polynomial terminal. A cap can be checked before exponential local acquisition.
With a fixed order and fixed explicit source, guard strengthening cannot increase costs: inconsistent events/edges vanish, remaining coefficient forms are a subset of original forms, and the denominator row space of H_i only grows. The quotient rank cannot increase. Containment deletion also only removes events. Restrict the original order to survivors; recomputing greedy preserves the bottleneck bound (not necessarily the exact original sum). This proves polynomial stability for all affine witness-conditioning queries in the declared domain.

## 5. Aggregate contraction, duplication and reuse
For one count, local jobs for distinct positions are a disjoint registry (each has 2^k candidate jobs, plus polynomial-factor per-candidate arithmetic). Define suffix credit P(S) by the fixed remaining order. The arithmetic recurrence subtracts the exact local contribution from the count with i removed; it is not a SAT OR branch. Charge the local job 2^k, and the continuation suffix P(S minus i). Reserve one extra job per event for aggregation: mu(S)=sum remaining (1+2^k). Then mu(local)+mu(continuation)=mu(S)-1 with local credit 2^k. Within each local job, sequential enumeration consumes one credit per candidate. Empty suffix is base count paid separately. This gives aggregate contraction c=1 for the actual arithmetic evaluation, not arbitrary SAT branches. The bounded n+1 witness queries are charged externally; or allocate (n+1) full capped-query budgets up front.
Safe exact reuse: in a local job with H_i, take a fixed ordered observation list L of all neighbor equation masks. Compute the affine image L(H_i) (here notation means image, not coefficient row span), with canonical equations in observation coordinates. The local predicates are equality tests of sublists with recorded RHS. Cache the normalized number of legal observation words only under exact image and predicate-list equality (or a verified coordinate bijection). Multiply by the fiber size 2^(dim H_i-r_i) per consumer. All image construction, comparisons, attempted edges and scaling are paid. No assumption that equal ranks or counts give a valid identity. This optional image-cache theorem is symbolic and was not implemented; witness pinning may recompute local jobs.
Falsified arbitrary pin contraction: take m=2^s mutually exclusive slot events E_i=(selector=i, x_i=y_i=0), s>=2, with independent payload pairs. Every k=0, so P=m, mu=2m. Pin x_0=1: E_0 vanishes and P=m-1. Pin x_0=0: E_0 survives with codimension s+1, P=m. No further closure rule applies, and even full relative affine hull is the whole guard: every unfixed payload can vary in a different slot and every slot has a legal word. Thus for every fixed c>0, sum child credits^c > parent^c-1, both for P and mu. Unaffected local counts can share normalized image jobs, but incidence/fiber scaling still charged. This disproves unrestricted pin contraction for this potential, not for every potential.
Raw bottleneck barrier: E_i=(z=0,x_i=0,y_i=0), i=1..m. Co-occurrence graph complete; on H_i, remaining event forms contribute two independent forms per neighbor. First cost min(m-1,2(m-1))=m-1 for every order; P=2^m-1. All legal words have full affine hull (z=1 frees all payloads, and legal z=0 exists). No root codim1/containment closure. Row spaces even share z. All nonempty subset intersections are distinct. Yet pinning the common hub z yields count 4^m+3^m directly by independent pair products. This is an explicit exponential raw-potential counterexample with an easy alternative, not an unrestricted lower bound or a diagonal-hard family.
