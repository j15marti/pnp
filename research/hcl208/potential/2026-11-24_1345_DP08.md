## 2. Representation and exact kernel formula (DP08-A)
After Gaussian parameterization of a consistent protected affine guard, let z range freely in GF(2)^d. Have k explicit nonnegative regional functions f_i(z), each a signed integer sum of affine-event indicators. Nonnegativity and source correspondence must be authenticated by construction; no generic positivity test is presumed free. Assume all private lifts independent conditional on z, with original maps retained. Boolean f_i give projected count; nonnegative integer f_i can represent independently realizable multiplicities. Syntactic observation space W_i is spanned by coefficient rows of all terms of factor i; optionally use BA07's minimum value space after paying its discovery and quotient rewrite. Let r_i=dim W_i, R=dim sum W_i, and
 D=ker[direct sum_i W_i -> sum_i W_i], (w_i) -> XOR_i w_i,
 delta=dim D=sum_i r_i-R.
Choose bases of W_i and use Gaussian elimination on their concatenation to find an explicit basis of D. All ranks and dependency supports polynomially discoverable. Define L_i(w) as the unnormalized local Walsh coefficient over the 2^r_i linear functionals on W_i. For a term c*1_{Az=b} of rank a whose row space V lies in W_i, its coefficient at w is zero if w outside V; otherwise it is c*2^(r_i-a)*(-1)^h where h is the implied RHS for w in the term's affine system. Inconsistent events vanish. Sum these coefficients; no spectrum enumeration. This is Gaussian elimination with exact signed integers.

By Fourier inversion f_i(z)=2^-r_i sum_{w in W_i} L_i(w)(-1)^(w dot z). Multiply all factors, sum z, use character orthogonality:
 Z = sum_z product_i f_i(z) = 2^(d-sum_i r_i) sum_{(w_i) in D} product_i L_i(w_i).
Equivalently Z=2^(d-R) [sum_D product_i L_i(w_i)/2^delta]. This counts exactly, including higher-order correlations; cancellations are exact. Products and total numerator have O(poly(N)) bits (sum local ranks/coefficient bit lengths plus delta); exact integer division at the end. Negative intermediate terms are not negative feasibility counts.

## 3. Component decomposition and polynomial numerical credit (DP08-B)
Take any computed basis of D. On factor IDs, union every set of factors touched by a single basis vector. Let C be the resulting components, including isolated factors. Each basis vector has support entirely within one component; every dependency decomposes as a sum of within-component basis combinations. Hence D=direct sum_C D_C; spans of the component observation spaces are jointly independent (a relation between them would give a cross-component dependency not decomposable into separately zero sums). This does not rely on pairwise intersections or on finding an optimum partition. A greedy fundamental-circuit basis is sufficient.
Let delta_C=dim D_C and R_C=rank of all rows in C. Then
 Z = 2^(d-sum_C R_C) product_C [2^-delta_C sum_{D_C} product_{i in C} L_i(w_i)].
Each bracket is an integer nonnegative local count, or signed mass for signed-only factors. The exact computational capacity is A=sum_C 2^delta_C (use 1+A for empty factors). It is polynomial-bit computable without enumeration. A bounded terminal checks A against N^a for fixed a before expansion. T<=poly(N)(1+A), S<=poly(N), CM<=(T+1)S in one serial enumerator. Each component enumerated once; all evaluations and zero contributions charged. A may be exponential numerically; no universal bound.

Unlike BA07's primal overlap rank, dual nullity can be constant at arbitrarily large joint overlap. For m>=3, choose independent forms a_1,...,a_(m-1), a_m=XOR of those forms, and private bits y_i. Reject E_i=[a_i=0,y_i=0]. Spaces W_i=<a_i,y_i>; R=2m-1, total local rank=2m, delta=1. All a_i lie in their respective overlap spaces; BA07 shared rank m-1. Exactly two kernel words: zero and the single full relation. Local L_i(0)=3 and L_i(a_i)=-1, so count (3^m+(-1)^m)/2. Each factor nonaffine; DP07 codim-one rule stagnates. m disjoint copies would give multiplicative naive enumeration, while component accounting adds. This is an upper bound on a selected terminal, not a lower bound on BA07 or DP07 or an all-algorithm separation.

## 4. Guard-safe acquisition and reconstruction (DP08-C)
For a local pin w dot z=b with nonzero w in one currently retained W_i, quotient all observation spaces by span(w). R drops one, sum r_i drops at least one. Thus delta'<=delta. Other original independent component spans are unaffected; keeping the original component registry gives delta'_C<=delta_C even if the component subsequently splits. Removing a factor or shrinking any observation space embeds the new dependency kernel into the old one, so also cannot increase nullity. A pin outside all local spaces can be harmless or create a relation; for arbitrary rank-p external guard delta'<=delta+p before closure, but no monotonicity. A source-consistent external guard must be normalized BEFORE establishing the initial budget.
For a positive root count, choose a nonzero observation row from one remaining factor. Compute the exact conditional count at bit zero; if positive keep zero, otherwise keep one (partition of actual chart, not approximate signed credit). Retain all previous guard rows, including certified forced rows, and reclose after each deduction. At most d independent pins, with at most two complete counts per pin if explicitly checking both labels; remaining factors then constant. Gaussian lift any chart word, verify original factors and separately paid source lifts. Keep fixed original components throughout to bound each query's aggregate capacity by A_root; if recomputing split components, <=k A_root suffices. This proves T,S,CM remain within the polynomial terminal for full reconstruction, not just decision. ## 5. Exact weighted job contraction, and counterexamples to SAT branching (DP08-D)
For each original component with nullity h reserve the full binary enumeration-tree node count B(h)=2^(h+1)-1, including internal arithmetic scheduling nodes. B(h)=1+2B(h-1) for h>=1. Signed summation over the two children is exact; they partition the dependency assignments, NOT the SAT assignments. A frontier potential is the sum of outstanding tree-node credits plus a reserved unit for each other paid polynomial job. Each expansion, leaf evaluation or aggregation job reduces reserved credit by at least one (reserve joins separately). For root decision plus reconstruction reserve 1+2d complete component jobs, using original h and registry, plus polynomial setup/lift/verification overhead. Unused credits can be released. This is c=1 aggregate contraction of an actual bounded arithmetic computation; polynomial per job bit work includes all failures. If keeping DAG sharing, a unique job is credited once only when its full guard/source/query contract agrees; every consumer edge, lookup, pin, canonicalization and aggregation remains separately charged. No free semantic-key acquisition or assumption of only polynomially many distinct guard requests.

Falsifier 1 (executed): two independent rank-two rejection events [a=b=0] and [c=d=0] have delta=0. An arbitrary cross guard a=c changes delta to 1. Guard strengthening does not universally lower dependency credit.
Falsifier 2 (executed): the m=8 one-relation example with TWO private bits per factor and bad event [a_i=y_i=z_i=0]. Pin y_1=0; the rank-three event becomes rank two, the connected dependency kernel remains one-dimensional, A_parent=A_zero=2. Pin y_1=1; first factor disappears, seven independent factors remain, A_one=7. Both children SAT, exact counts sum to parent, and any fixed c>0 violates A_zero^c+A_one^c<=A_parent^c-1. Even fixed registry credit stays unchanged on the zero child. This refutes universal weighted child contraction for that potential, not existence of a better transformation. Reconstruct along only one successful child with prepaid query count, rather than pretend both child costs vanish.
Falsifier 3 (symbolic): duplicating a factor with observation dimension r and synchronizing all its variables increases delta by r; for Boolean f, f*f=f, so certified exact syntactic idempotence can eliminate the duplicate after checking source lift contracts. For non-Boolean count factors f*f may differ, and different guards are not interchangeable. Independent copies instead permit additive component credits. Duplication never creates free information.
Falsifier 4 (symbolic, DS07 control): tagged complete-graph monotone 3CNF x_u OR x_v OR y_e has k=m, each factor rank3, global rank n+m, delta=2m-n, connected dependency component, no rank-one deductions. A exponential, but easy SAT by y_e=1 and symmetry-based exact count. Neither dual nor primal parameters alone are universal hardness measures.
