## 1. Local Fourier factorization (PROVED)
Work over F2. The actual consistent guarded chart is parametrized by independent coordinates, partitioned into disjoint blocks: one block Y_e per edge of a regional interaction forest, one private block Y_i per region, and an optional unused block. Each regional Boolean f_i depends only on its incident edge blocks and its private block and is given by an authenticated explicit integer signed affine indicator list. A supplied linear block adapter is verified by Gaussian elimination: rank equals the sum of block dimensions, actual source rows of each term lie in its allowed local block sum, and all external consumers and owner identities are preserved. BA11's semantic-space acquisition may propose such a chart; not assumed free. The theorem applies to actual factors and their original chart, not to arbitrary marginal support tables. Booleanity of each integer list is verified via BD11's sum(f_i^2-f_i)=0 on the guard, polynomially many Gaussian intersections; source equivalence is a separate obligation. Local private block may be empty.

For a nonempty affine packet A in product_j Y_j let A_j be its exact marginal affine image, obtained by eliminating the other coordinates. A lies in product A_j. Put
    k(A)=sum_j dim A_j - dim A >= 0.
Equivalently k is the full equation rank minus sum of the marginal equation ranks. Gaussian extension of the independent marginal rows to the full equation rowspace gives k additional rows l_h(y)=b_h, each l_h=sum_j l_hj(y_j). On the product of the marginals these rows are independent and impose exactly A. Therefore pointwise
    1_A(y)=2^(-k) sum_{u in F2^k} (-1)^(u dot b)
                 product_j [1_{A_j}(y_j) (-1)^(sum_h u_h l_hj(y_j))].
This follows from character orthogonality; a nonzero discrepancy has zero sum by pairing u with u+e_h at a discrepant coordinate. No assignment enumeration and no dropping of correlations. Empty packets are removed. All phases, marginals, coefficients and region/edge IDs are explicit. The number of separable signed-phase labels is exactly 2^k per uncoalesced packet. Let L'_i=sum_{A in list i}2^k(A), K_i=max_A k(A); multiply local coefficients by 2^K_i to keep integer arithmetic, dividing final count by 2^sum K_i. Coalesce only exact identical labels if desired, charging canonicalization. No equality of mere rowspaces implies equality of labels.

For two blocks the 2^k bound is sharp for the real matrix rank of the individual affine indicator: on their marginals, the residual coupling map on either block must have rank k (otherwise a nonzero combination would be an additional marginal-only equation). Both maps are surjective onto F2^k after affine anchoring. The matrix is a duplicated row/column permutation of the 2^k-by-2^k identity, hence rank 2^k. This lower bound is only for separable rank of that packet under that split, not decision complexity or even minimum rank of a full signed sum. For three or more blocks the bound need not be tight. This is a constructive relaxation of BA11's k=0 Cartesian promise, not a new generic tensor-contraction theorem.

## 3. Aggregate work potential (PROVED restricted)

## 4. Universal obstruction firewall (PROVED)
Without the forest/paid weighted-width condition, even k(A)=0 and bounded L_i do not imply tractability. Compile arbitrary 3-CNF into a graph with one Boolean block per literal occurrence, one variable node with factor equal to 1 iff all its occurrence bits are equal (two disjoint Cartesian point packets), one clause node with factor 1 minus the Cartesian point that falsifies its three literals. An edge is an actual occurrence bit; all blocks independent. Every factor has <=2 signed Cartesian packets, every k=0, every edge rank1. Joint satisfaction iff original SAT; witness maps both directions and construction is polynomial. Variable nodes of unbounded degree can optionally be replaced by a tree of arity<=3 equality factors with new occurrence bits, still <=2 packets. General graph can have cycles and unbounded treewidth. This is NP-completeness of the unrestricted interaction graph domain, not a lower bound on all algorithms, no claim that P!=NP, no strict physical E4/B4 compilation. BW11 already gives a complementary local-geometry hardness firewall; this one concerns packet-label aggregate potentials. It refutes the inference that only the maximum local coupling or packet count suffices for a universal paid terminal unless P=NP.

The two-block rank lower bound and graph reduction do not imply a compulsory expansion for all decisions. Other peers' Gaussian, matched-cycle, nonaffine elimination or compact circuits can bypass these representations. DP11's common guard-family lift and DY11 nonlinear guarded reconstruction remain separate existential support contracts; do not count via them without multiplicity proof.
