## Theorem 3: fixed-degree protected orbit reconstruction
For each feasible count vector choose anchor x0 with first k_j row IDs selected in each group. Let R_k be actual assignments in that fiber obtainable by at most k disjoint selected/unselected swaps, total across groups. Every degree<=k Boolean polynomial f on original row bits that vanishes on R_k vanishes on the entire fiber. Proof: for any other fiber assignment x, pair removed and added coordinates within each group; the pairs are disjoint. Introduce one Boolean switch per pair, with each affected original bit affine in its switch. The restriction of f to the resulting switch cube has degree <=k and vanishes at all switches of weight <=k, since those assignments lie in R_k. Boolean subset Mobius inversion makes every coefficient zero; evaluate at all switches one to obtain f(x)=0. This works over GF(2), and in fact any field. Thus exact degree-k feature span is generated by actual radius-k witnesses; at most sum_{i<=k} B^(2i) candidates, polynomial for fixed k. Enumerate pairs directly (rather than scan all assignments); verify each source row and merge via Gaussian elimination with constant feature. Full product orbit information preserved, including degree-k protected affine pullbacks; nonlinear physical pullbacks require a certified bounded degree. Exact identity inference only at original unguarded union or conditional within a box; no counts of physical witnesses inferred. RT06 affine swaps are k=1 special case; anchor alone loses even affine information, e.g. exactly one selected of two.
