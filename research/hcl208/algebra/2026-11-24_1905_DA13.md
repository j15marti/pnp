## A. Exact symplectic defect and isotropic coverage (PROVED)
G=(V,E) finite simple graph, n vertices, maximum degree Delta, c connected components including isolates. Full independent two-bit colors x_v in F2^2; actual source edge e=uv forbids x_u+x_v=s_e. Let d_e=e_u+e_v, U_e=span((d_e,0),(0,d_e)). Choose a nonzero character l_e in F2^2, r_e=d_e tensor l_e, S=span(r_e), R=dim S, r=n-c. On F2^(2n) define alternating bilinear form omega((a,b),(a',b'))=a dot b'+b dot a'. For a rowspace H define symplectic defect rho(H)=rank of this restricted alternating form (even). This is a new explicitly scoped parameter, NOT automatically the unstable syndrome rank of M17. No physical compiler or gauge invariance of this form under arbitrary charts is asserted.

For distinct edges, omega(r_e,r_f)=(d_e dot d_f) det(l_e,l_f). In a simple graph sharing exactly one endpoint gives dot=1; distinct nonzero characters have det=1. Thus if S is isotropic, every incident pair has identical characters. On each nontrivial connected component, all characters are uniform. Hence R=r and any selected independent isotropic escape family is a forest. Conversely, one uniform character on a forest is independent and isotropic. So the maximum number of original owners with independent isotropic representatives is EXACTLY r. The statement also holds for any subset of edges with its own graphic rank. It is independent of source signs but does not imply that non-independent escape equations are inconsistent or that an isotropic solver is impossible.

For unrestricted representatives, a supplied independent set can be converted to a two-forest assignment without decreasing its cardinality: for each mixed row (d,d), at least one of (d,0),(0,d) lies outside the span of all other chosen rows, since their sum already lies outside. Replace it by such a row; repeat. Two coordinate row sets are independent iff their edge subsets are forests. Conversely any two forests supply independent rows. Therefore maximum independent representative coverage equals the maximum cardinality of a union of two edge-disjoint forests, found by standard graphic matroid union, with polynomial rank tests. A full independent escape cover exists iff |J|<=2r(J) for every edge subset J (Edmonds matroid partition theorem). Once acquired, enforce r_e dot x = 1+l_e dot s_e; independence makes the right-hand sides consistent for every signing. Solve by Gaussian elimination and verify original edges. This is a SAT-only terminal, not an UNSAT test on failure; the independent choices are sufficient witness conditions, NOT unconditional forced-information closure. The general matroid-union backend was not implemented.

## B. Quantitative price of nonisotropy (PROVED)
Let K=(omega(r_e,r_f)) for the selected rows, whose rank equals rho(S) since the rows span S. Each row has at most D=2 max(0,Delta-1) nonzero entries. Choose rho(S) spanning rows of K; their supports cover all nonzero columns, hence by symmetry the number of nonzero rows is at most D rho(S). Delete those edge IDs, leaving an isotropic selected graph E0, uniform on each component. Let d=|E-E0|. Then R<=r(E0)+d<=r(E)+d, giving
  R-r <= D rho(S).
For an independent all-owner escape cover R=m this yields m-r<=D rho(S), and rho>=2 whenever a simple cycle is present. The same bound holds for any independent covered subset with its own cycle rank. For maximum degree four, D<=6.

For a sharper dense-registry estimate, let A=span{d_e}, t=number of even-order connected components (isolates odd). A is the even-weight subspace on each component; its dot-product radical is the span of the all-ones vectors on even components, dimension t. The restriction of omega to A x A has radical dimension 2t, rank 2(r-t). Project S into the nondegenerate symplectic quotient of dimension 2(r-t). Its image has dimension k>=R-2t. Since dim L^perp=2(r-t)-k, its restricted form has rank >=2k-2(r-t). Thus
  rho(S) >= max(0, 2(R-r-t)).
This bound applies to multigraphs too; the uniformity and sparse-defect bound above require simplicity. In particular any independent two-forest cover on a connected dense graph with m near 2(n-1) has large symplectic defect even though the escape equations themselves can be solved easily. No runtime lower bound from this rank.

Parallel-edge firewall: two vertices, two parallel edges, l=01 and 10. Their incidence dot product is zero (two common endpoints), so rho=0 and R=2>r=1. Uniformity, isotropic forest characterization and width equality do not extend to multigraphs. Native E4/B4 includes more than this simple graph domain.

## C. Two different cut-width prices (PROVED)
For cut E=X disjoint Y, let A_X,A_Y be incidence spans, lambda=dim(A_X intersect A_Y). Selected guard cut lambda_l=dim(S_X intersect S_Y). Deleting d edges can decrease the graphic cut by at most d (one deletion changes it by 0 or 1). The selected cut cannot decrease when rows are added back (intersection of enlarging subspaces). E0 has uniform directions on each component, so its two cut widths agree. Therefore
  lambda_l >= lambda - D rho(S).
DA12 also gives the often stronger lambda_l>=lambda-(R-r). The novelty is the explicitly quantified isotropy price and independent-escape obstruction, not a new unrestricted width lower bound.

NEW: consider ANY consistent affine guard with rowspace H containing one chosen row per owner, h=dim H, not necessarily spanned by the chosen rows or isotropic. Let B_X=A_X x A_X, B_Y=A_Y x A_Y, W=B_X intersect B_Y=(A_X intersect A_Y)^2. Let w_H be the cut dimension of the images of B_X and B_Y modulo H, keeping the original owner registry. Original width=2lambda. DY12 identity:
  w_H=2lambda+k_H-dim(H intersect W), k_H>=0.
Project the incidence space A onto A/(A_X intersect A_Y), dimension r-lambda. Choose r-lambda edge vectors whose projected incidence classes are independent. Their chosen tensor rows remain independent modulo W: apply the dual coordinate functionals to each nonzero tensor direction. Consequently dim H - dim(H intersect W)>=r-lambda. Combining,
  w_H >= lambda - (h-r).
This is a bound on the actual linear quotient of the two-channel observation spaces, unlike DA12's selected-row width. It applies even to dense extra guards. For an isotropic H, h<=n (nondegenerate ambient symplectic form), hence w_H>=lambda-c. For a connected simple graph, a minimum uniform all-hit guard S has w_S=lambda exactly (direct coordinate decomposition); in any larger isotropic all-hit H, w_H>=lambda-1. If H has large extra dimension, the bound can be vacuous. If owners are removed, become tautological, or their affine consequences are globally solved, recompute the actual registry. In particular all-hit residuals are affine and Gaussian-solvable despite large raw quotient cut width. This is NOT a runtime or terminal obstruction.

## D. Actual matching tag-cut identity (PROVED symbolic)
For M18's actual edge columns a_e=(d_e,g_e), define for any subset Z its cycle kernel C_Z=ker(D_Z) extended by zeros to E; let T map edge coefficients to the XOR of their tags, V_Z=T(C_Z), epsilon_Z=dim V_Z. Rank joint(Z)=r(Z)+epsilon_Z by the kernel of projection to occupancy. For cut X,Y define K=dim V_E - dim(V_X+V_Y); 0<=K<=lambda because C_E/(C_X+C_Y) has dimension lambda (rank-nullity, disjoint coordinates). Then exactly
  lambda_joint = lambda - K + dim(V_X intersect V_Y).
Here K is the cross-cycle tag rank modulo local cycle tag spaces. It measures how many shared occupancy dimensions are removed by actual cross-cycle distinguishing tags; common local tag variations add width instead. Vertex gauge changes g_e -> g_e+p_u+p_v vanish on every cycle, and transform joint columns by an invertible linear shear, so all these quantities are gauge invariant. Example: an ordinary cycle cut into two nonempty paths, lambda=1, V_X=V_Y=0. A single independent nonzero tag on one edge makes K=1 and joint cut width zero, while the original untagged cut has width one. This refutes the claim that appending tags must increase the actual shared cut width. All quantities are polynomial Gaussian jobs without enumerating matching words. This theorem is about raw M18 columns; feasible matching variations may be proper subspaces of cycle tag images. It does not replace M17's paid selector-conditioned matching-span acquisition, actual occupancy, integer quotas, or target compatibility. Not experimentally tested in this run.
