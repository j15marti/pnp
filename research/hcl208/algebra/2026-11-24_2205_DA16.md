## Theorem A (guard discharge, any affine order)
Work in the Boolean ring over F2, with squarefree polynomials; equations p=0, rules XOR and multiplication by one variable followed by multilinear reduction. Degree is the maximum reduced degree of any proof line. Let g be affine, and suppose P plus g=0 has a BPC refutation of degree D>=1. Multiply every line by 1+g. The g axiom maps to zero, all other axioms times 1+g are derived from their original versions by variable multiplication and XOR, and multiplication/linear combination commute with this map in the Boolean ring. The final 1 maps to 1+g. The derived equation 1+g=0 excludes the refuted branch, at degree <=D+1. The g=1 case is symmetric. This is an explicit proof transformation, not a SAT call.

For any finite binary affine case tree refuting P, every leaf has a refutation from P and its path equations of degree <=D (including all preprocessing consequences used in the proof). If there are L leaves, then P has a BPC refutation of degree <=D+floor(log2 L). Induct: discharge the smaller child first, whose leaf count <=L/2, then use the learned opposite guard as an already derived axiom in the larger child proof. The larger proof pays no additional degree. Affine branch forms may be dense and adaptively chosen; branch order and depths arbitrary. An ordinary (non-reduced) PC derivation simulates each BPC step with degree overhead at most one by subtracting Boolean axioms times monomials. The tree theorem controls degree, not expanded proof size; no claim about arbitrary DAGs with exponentially many unfolded paths. Valid same-context DAG reuse, non-PC terminals, new extension variables, witness-based support/flow certificates and nonlocal rewrites with no bounded-degree lift are not covered. A leaf's necessary deductions and coordinate elimination must have a certified proof in the original coordinate ring at the stated degree cap; we do not count unproved semantic closure or arbitrary transformations as degree-free.

## Theorem B (hard source family)
Use Mikša–Nordström, A Generalized Method for Proving Polynomial Calculus Degree Lower Bounds, Theorem 5.1 (https://doi.org/10.1145/3675668; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01358): CNF whose clause-variable graph is an (s,delta) boundary expander requires ordinary PC refutation degree >delta*s/2 over any field. Here each nonempty set of at most s clauses has at least delta times its size variables appearing exactly once. This includes F2. Use 6n independent random 3-clauses, each with distinct variables and uniform signs. Expected model count 2^n(7/8)^(6n)=o(1). For a t-clause set to have <=floor(7t/4) distinct neighbors, union bound gives binom(6n,t)binom(n,v)(v/n)^(3t) <= [C(t/n)^(1/4)]^t, v=floor(7t/4), absolute C. Choose alpha>0 with C alpha^(1/4)<1/2; split sum at sqrt(n) to see probability o(1). Every set of <=alpha*n clauses then has >7t/4 neighbors and >=2|neighbors|-3t>t/2 boundary variables. Thus UNSAT and PC degree Omega(n) coexist. Existence, not a certified efficient hard-instance generator. Convention x=1 means true; changing conventions is linear substitution.

Compile each clause l1 OR l2 OR l3 to NAE(l1,l2,y), NAE(1+y,l3,z), with one shared phase z (globally complement all source variable bits and y if z=1). At z=0 this is equisatisfiable, with y=(1+l1)(1+l2) a Boolean function of degree <=2. Under the substitution z=0 and this y, the first NAE violation polynomial becomes zero and the second exactly (1+l1)(1+l2)(1+l3), the original clause polynomial. Each rank-two NAE violation is the product of two equality factors. A degree-d BPC refutation maps to source BPC degree <=2d+2: substitute each line, simulate a variable multiplication by expanding the substitute (degree <=2) into monomials and multiplying termwise; intermediate products have degree <=2d+2 before reduction. Zero axioms can be ignored. Ordinary PC overhead <=1. Hence these NAE charts require BPC degree Omega(n).

To obtain linear max-degree-four NAE, use BH15's switched Fano-minus-line equality gadget (six lines, three terminals forced equal, constant-zero and constant-one assignments legal). Split occurrences into disjoint external triples. For a variable of t occurrences connect t occurrence leaves to a chain of t equality gadgets, consecutive gadgets sharing only one backbone vertex; each gadget has two consecutive backbone vertices and one occurrence leaf as its three terminals. Backbone degree <=4, leaf degree 3 including its external clause, internal degree 3. No two clauses share more than one vertex. Each assignment of the original variable extends with every copy and gadget bit equal to that variable; the gadget polynomials substitute to zero. Therefore the same degree simulation holds after the occurrence split, and size is O(n) variables/owners, O(n log n) bits using dense integer names. Both directions of the SAT lift are exact; discovery of the compiler is linear in occurrences, with polynomial bit costs. The input is UNSAT. ## Combined consequence for U-PROGRESS
On these strict linear NAE cores of O(n) size, every affine-branch proof tree using only BPC refutations of maximum degree D at leaves must have L >= 2^(Omega(n)-D). For every fixed d (or D=o(n)), polynomial tree credit is impossible for this restricted dispatch, even with global adaptive affine branches, not just DP16's separated guard schedule, and independently of which numerical potential is proposed. Fixed-degree saturation is itself polynomial: dimension M=sum_{j<=D} binom(n,j), maintain the Gaussian span and, for each variable, compute the subspace of products lying within the degree cap via high-coordinate cancellation, not only products of basis rows individually. At most M strict span increases, all matrix operations polynomial for fixed D. Polynomial for one chart does not imply polynomial aggregate tree. At D=Theta(n) the dense monomial method has exponential capacity; capacity alone is not an unrestricted cost lower bound. The bound is on unfolded leaf occurrences; a different factored evaluator may bypass them. It also does not impose PC on certificates from matching, flow, PSD, signed-basis, min-cut, or arbitrary decision-only proof systems. No unrestricted hard sequence or model transfer follows. If a portfolio is slow, diagonal lemma applies only to that fixed total portfolio. To invoke thm:aggregate one must either find a different non-PC exact contraction, lift a high-degree inference with actually polynomial bit work, or prove genuine global sharing without paying unfolded leaves. Avoid circular semantic keys (DR15).
