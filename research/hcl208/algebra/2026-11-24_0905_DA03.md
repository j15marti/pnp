## 1. Tensor-plane source and optimal simultaneous guard
Let c_e be any explicitly given nonzero binary columns in F2^d. The common source variables are (x,y) in F2^d x F2^d. Each indexed constraint forbids exactly one observation word q_e in F2^2 for (c_e dot x,c_e dot y). Its plane of linear forms is U_e = span((c_e,0),(0,c_e)). For a simple graph take c_uv=e_u+e_v. q=00 gives ordinary four-coloring; arbitrary q gives a signed forbidden difference. The graph and all words are explicit. These are observation planes on one common source, NOT DO03's independent edge-factor syndrome tags.

Let K be any subspace of linear guard forms, rank r. Each assignment to its basis defines a disjoint affine guard. A factor is hit iff U_e intersects K nontrivially. If hit, its local relation in that guard is affine or empty: restrict a three-out-of-four set to an affine space of dimension at most one. Conversely an unhit factor remains three-out-of-four in its independent observation coordinates. For any independent set of columns c_e, any choice of nonzero u_e in U_e is independent: apply the corresponding dual coordinate selector in both copies. Thus rank of hit columns <=r. Hitting all columns requires r>=rank(c_e), and choosing all (c_e,0) attains equality. For graph G, the minimum common-isotropic codimension is exactly n-components(G). This is an exact discovery result for this tensor family, stronger than DA02's general factor-two approximation. Given full guard word, every remaining constraint is affine or empty; Gaussian elimination gives a model or rejection. No logarithmic guarantee for connected large graphs.

## 2. Cascade conservation (PROVED)
Run the following certified closure before any external guard, immediately after introducing one, after each derived affine equation, and at stagnation. Maintain current consistent equations with span L, initially K; choose any remaining edge with U_e intersect L nonzero. Compute its actual allowed observation fiber (at most four rows, using Gaussian elimination for the restricted domain). If empty reject current guard; otherwise add all affine equations defining that fiber and remove the edge. This is exact: within the old affine domain, membership in the fiber equals the original edge predicate. Preserve the RHS, source edge ID and current guard. No unhit edge's full affine hull adds information.

Let W be span of columns of already removed edges, H=W x W, t=dim W. Inductively L subset K+H. If a new removed edge has c_e in W, H stays fixed. Otherwise U_e intersects H trivially, and a nonzero hit vector in U_e intersect L lies outside H but in K+H. In quotient by H this provides a nonzero vector simultaneously in the image of K and in the new two-dimensional plane. Enlarging H to H+U_e therefore decreases dim((K+H)/H) by at least one. Initially that dimension is r; consequently at most r independent column insertions can occur, regardless of closure order or sign words. This argument also includes an empty hit edge if desired, but no width bound is needed after contradiction.

Therefore t<=r, dim H=2t, dim(K+H)=2t+dim((K+H)/H)<=r+t<=2r, and rank L<=2r. Crucially the entire cascade, not merely the initial hit set, obeys the same budget. A derived affine form may trigger a new edge even though the initial K did not hit it. This theorem pays for all such steps without treating complete semantic closure as free. It does NOT constrain nonlocal deductions from other authenticated terminals, nonlinear substitutions, or unrelated relations. One can restart the argument with their independently justified equations included in K; charge rank and cost, and retain guards.

For graph columns the removed-edge graph has graphic rank t<=r; in each component choose all but one vertex to form a vertex cover S of the removed edges, |S|=t. All original edges of G-S therefore remain. If initial min degree delta>2, max degree Delta, and surviving raw graph has max degree <=2, each vertex outside S needs at least delta-2 original neighbors in S. Counting gives (delta-2)(n-|S|)<=Delta|S|, so r>=(delta-2)n/(Delta+delta-2). This rules out cheap transition into that specific raw degree-two domain, NOT arbitrary matching encodings. In particular no automatic extension of DA01 matching, S06 count or S08 slack recourse.

## 3. Dual exact separator width and guarded tradeoff
For a family of grouped columns U_e let lambda(A)=dim(span U_A intersect span U_complement). Width w is the minimum over binary factor trees of the largest cut lambda; use no +1 convention, zero for at most one leaf. For ordinary graph coloring, let B be incidence columns and C the fundamental-cycle matrix of DO03, so rowspace C=ker B. For any edge subset S and T=complement, rank C_S=|S|-rank B+rank B_T: projection of ker B onto S has kernel the cycles supported on T. Therefore lambda_C(S)=lambda_B(S) (one-bit columns). Duplicating both bit coordinates gives exactly 2 lambda_B on either side. This is an identity for every cut, not a heuristic relationship with topological separator width. For arbitrary signed q, column geometry is unchanged.

After closure in a consistent guard, let E'=remaining edges and let pi quotient forms by L. Define w' as the optimal factor-tree width of the remaining planes pi(U_e). For any residual cut A,B (A union B=E'), put X=span U_A and Y=span U_B. Original cut with all removed leaves on B side has intersection X intersect (Y+H). Under pi its dimension can drop by at most dim L. In quotient, adding pi(H) to pi(Y) increases intersection dimension with pi(X) by at most dim pi(H). Thus
 lambda_original(A) <= lambda_residual(A) + dim L + dim pi(H)
 = lambda_residual(A) + dim(L+H) <= lambda_residual(A)+2r.
Extend any residual tree by attaching a subtree containing all removed leaves at its root (subdivide an edge if necessary). Cuts internal to removed subtree or separating it have lambda<=dim H<=2r. Remaining cuts are the preceding bound. Empty/singleton residual handled directly. Hence
             w_original <= w' + 2r.
This holds after every full cascade and for every guard word not already rejected, including arbitrary adaptively selected K and signed forbidden words. Also the raw residual graph contains G-S, so tw(G)<=tw(G_remaining)+r by adding S to every bag of a decomposition of G-S. This last statement is a structural relation only.

For exact guarded evaluation, represent the affine domain after L by a particular point plus free basis. The map from free variables to all surviving local observation words has a binary linear image plus known offset. Its parity-check matrix is a basis of the kernel of the transpose of the observation map. Rank duality above applies to grouped columns of this matrix as well: exact syndrome cut widths equal residual observation cut widths. Local rows are explicit three-point sets with offset handled. Given a tree, the exact target-conditioned affine cut sets have at most 2^w' keys; enumerate pairs, retain one witness per reachable key and reconstruct all row choices, then Gaussian-lift a common source assignment and verify every original constraint. DP02/GD09 provide the exact join framework; no claim of polynomial tree discovery at logarithmic width. This gives a paid sufficient bound 2^r * max_guards[2^(2w') poly(N)+T_tree_discovery] for enumerating all guard words, plus compiler and lift. Early SAT/UNSAT or other terminals may avoid enumeration. The inequality is NOT a runtime lower bound for arbitrary algorithms or even for all implementations of the hybrid. It bounds the best possible widths in this specific representation.

## 4. Explicit falsification of a raw universal low-rank/low-width disjunction
In K_n any binary factor tree has an edge cut with both sides at least m/3 (leaf-weight centroid argument). In any partition of its edges at least one side's spanning graph is connected, since if one is disconnected all cross-component edges lie in the other. Rank of the smaller-rank side at this balanced cut is at least the least integer a with a(a+1)/2>=m/3: a graph with rank a has at most a(a+1)/2 edges, by summing over components and concentrating rank. Hence w_original >=2a=Omega(n). K_n for n>=5 is trivially UNSAT. K_{n,n} is trivially SAT and has K_{n+1} as a minor (contract n-1 disjoint cross edges). Graphic-matroid lambda width is minor-monotone: deleting e yields lambda'(A,B)<=lambda(A,B+e) by rank marginal monotonicity; contracting e yields the same by rank(A+e)-rank(A)<=rank(e). Restrict and suppress tree leaves; iterate. Thus K_{n,n} also has w_original=Omega(n). Both have connected guard optimum linear; both have delta=Delta growing, so reducing raw residual degree to 2 also requires linear guard rank. For any nonrejected guard of rank O(log N), w' remains Omega(n). On K_n no guarantee of nonrejected branches at large guard, but rank-zero root is nonrejected and width large; on K_{n,n}, every valid coloring selects a nonrejected word. These easy examples falsify the claim that the three raw structural parameters must trade off to make one small, but emphatically do not defeat clique or bipartite terminals. Not asserted for every high-girth DO03 instance; high girth alone does not establish a needed linear width. Large-width critical high-girth families and new exact nonlinear contractions remain separate questions.
