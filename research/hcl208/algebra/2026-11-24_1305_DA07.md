## 2. Exact private-tree collision theorem (PROVED)
Colors are F2^2, edge uv forbids x_u+x_v=s_uv. Let T be a connected induced tree of t private vertices. Each has its full four-color domain, total source degree exactly four (internal tree edges + exterior edges), and no extra observation, budget or guard involving its color. Exterior colors are explicit affine functions of chart z in F2^d, optionally restricted by explicit affine guard G. Multiple ports may meet the same exterior vertex; retain actual affine correlations. More generally, the following pointwise identity holds for any exterior assignment, even when other external constraints are nonaffine.

Root T. Each nonroot has exactly three incoming ports (external neighbors plus children); root has four. Define a formal message f_v(z) in F2^2 in postorder: external incoming word x_w(z)+s_vw; child incoming word f_w(z)+s_vw; f_v is XOR of all its incoming words. The root message is not consumed. At every vertex form a collision event for each pair of incoming words, i.e. their equality (two explicit affine equations). Number of events is 3(t-1)+6=3t+3. Boundary edge count =4t-2(t-1)=2t+2. Formal messages are affine even if they are not actual forced colors on a particular chart word.

Claim: T extends an exterior assignment iff AT LEAST ONE collision event holds. Proof: every nonroot subtree has a nonempty root-color support, by induction since an incoming subtree of size >=2 forbids no color and one of size1 forbids exactly one; at most three incoming sources. If all descendants have no collision, their messages are uniquely forced, and if the three local incoming words are distinct the unique remaining color is their XOR, since XOR of all four colors is zero. If there is a local collision, at least two colors remain. If a child already has >=2 supported colors, it imposes no restriction on the parent, which again has >=2 colors (nonroot). Thus the condition 'a collision occurs anywhere below or at this nonroot' is equivalent to support size >=2; otherwise the support is exactly {f_v(z)}. At root, a child with >=2 support leaves at most three exclusions; otherwise root extends iff its four incoming words are not all distinct. Hence the formal comparisons do not create false positives: a spurious formal value above an earlier collision is immaterial since that earlier collision already ensures extension. Both directions and reverse lift are exact.

If some vertex has total degree <4 and all have degree <=4, the entire connected region is universally extendable under every exterior assignment. After fixed exterior colors, remaining list sizes >=internal degree and at least one strict slack; greedily color in reverse spanning-tree order ending at a slack vertex. More general DP-degree extension below. If a private vertex has an extra pin/guard/owner, do NOT use the full-list projection without an exact adapter.

## 4. General degree-feasible nonlinear deletion and structural tradeoff (PROVED via known theorem; not replayed)
After fixing exterior colors a private region R has lists L(v) of allowed colors. If original lists have size at least total degree, then |L(v)|>=deg_R(v); an exterior edge removes at most one color. A connected component with strict slack is extendable by the reverse-tree greedy argument. More generally if any block of R is neither complete nor a cycle, BKP Theorem 9 ensures every degree-feasible list cover is colorable: our edge conflict pairs c -> c+s are matchings between lists. Thus such a region may be removed existentially for EVERY exterior word, irrespective of external interface width, if no internal guards/costs/observations remain. Actual witnesses can be recovered polynomially: on a degree-feasible list instance, connected components containing slack or a non-clique/non-cycle block are SAT; otherwise all blocks are cliques (size<=5) or cycles, use exact block-cut tree support DP with four-color articulation identities, enumerate clique tables and cycle paths. Isolates with empty lists reject. Pin and delete one color at a time, reduce neighboring lists, preserving list-size>=degree on remaining vertices; query same polynomial decision routine. At most 4n queries. This is decision only, not a count identity. It applies to explicit simple signed graph, NOT arbitrary four-valent E4/B4 constraint network.

A corollary: connected unguarded simple signed graph of maximum degree <=4 is SAT except potentially K5, which can be checked exhaustively at constant cost. With arbitrary local color lists, enumerate vertices B whose list size < degree, plus every vertex touched by any retained nonlocal guard (or a certified sufficient support); after pins, all remaining vertices have list size >= residual degree. This yields 4^|B| poly(N) for explicit graph + such guards (and all remaining constraints local). Initial certified singleton closure can shrink B, never requires complete semantic closure. For the proof the full B is fixed after root closure; all conditional pins retain guards. Not an executed generic backend. Small guard RANK does not imply small guard SUPPORT.

Critical graph tradeoff: for a connected vertex-minimal uncolorable simple signed graph with full lists and no extra guards, min degree>=4. Let L be degree-4 vertices and H vertices of degree>=5. Each component of G[L] must be a GDP tree (blocks clique or cycle), otherwise color its proper exterior by criticality and extend by the preceding theorem. If the component is the entire graph, the same theorem applies without exterior. For girth g>=4, the only blocks are bridges or cycles of length >=g. If l=|L|, kappa_L components, c cyclic blocks, then e(L)=l-kappa_L+c, c<= (l-kappa_L)/(g-1), so edges crossing from L to H >= (2-2/(g-1))l+(2+2/(g-1))kappa_L. For maximum degree Delta and alpha=2-2/(g-1), Delta|H|>=alpha|L| implies |H|>=alpha*n/(Delta+alpha). This is an application of correspondence-coloring/Gallai structure, not new unrestricted lower-bound theory. A bounded-degree high-girth critical graph therefore cannot be normalized by merely assuming few degree-exception vertices. DO07's guarded H-e neighbors require separate treatment because pins and guard support alter lists. No claim about a minimum isotropic backdoor or a matching adapter. ## 5. Guarded star compiler: exact limit of local gluing (PROVED)
For arbitrary proper 3CNF F(z), give each clause its own disjoint four-port star, no extra edges. Center has full color list, all shifts zero; leaf colors are chart functions. One leaf is 00. For three literals ell_i, set the other leaf colors to c_i*(1+ell_i), where c_1=01,c_2=10,c_3=11. Multiplication is by a Boolean scalar and hence affine. A false clause produces four distinct colors and no center color. Any true literal gives a duplicate 00, leaving an available color. Thus exists center color iff clause true, and all centers independently extend iff F(z). Source-to-star construction and verification O(poly(N)); explicit dense chart or use occurrence synchronization affine equations with isolated selector bits. The underlying signed graph is a disjoint forest of stars, physical graph treewidth 1, degree<=4, no cycle at all. All global complexity resides in the correlated exterior chart. It is an exact polynomial many-one reduction into this guarded feasibility domain, NP membership immediate. This does not contradict DA06 or the unguarded degree theorem. Count transfer is not parsimonious; different legal literal words allow different numbers of center colors.

Consequently, the lemma 'each region has a nonempty locally feasible chart, therefore the independently repaired union has a common selector' is false (complete clause cube or Fano). The stronger proposed universal polynomial gluing of arbitrary such local projections over the shared affine chart is equivalent to a polynomial 3SAT decision algorithm, not disproved merely by NP-completeness. All local collision events have codim<=2, each region has 6 events; these bounds are insufficient to obtain a polynomial bound on the standard explicit cross-product of region choices. With k regions and no other constraints beyond G, choose one collision per region, Gaussian test; exact T<=product_j(3t_j+3)*poly(N), polynomial for fixed k, not universally. Shared chart constraints cannot be dropped or counted twice. The open issue is nonlinear composition across many ORs of affine equalities; matching and BA06 repair directions can be used only after exact adapters, not from local rank alone. No proof of an unrestricted lower bound.
