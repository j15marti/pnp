## A. Paid discovery of all small connected two-port modules (PROVED)
Construct the primal graph, with an edge for every pair of variables occurring in a common factor. For each active vertex s, start C={s}, B=empty. While an unclassified neighbor v in N(C) minus (C union B) exists, choose the least such v by fixed order. Either include v in C (if |C|<k), or mark v in B (if |B|<2). When no neighbor remains, output C,B; necessarily B=N(C) minus C. Stop infeasible paths. Every connected nonempty C of size <=k with <=2 neighbors is found: start at any s in C, follow the include choice exactly for vertices in C and exclude choice for its neighbors. Connectivity prevents premature completion, and the true path respects both limits. Thus no supplied manifest, guess of optimal decomposition, or semantic oracle is needed.
Depth <= k+1. At depth d at most sum_{j=0}^2 binom(d,j) decision prefixes are possible (one deterministic include/exclude decision each step, at most two excludes). Sum over d gives O((k+2)^3) visited nodes per seed. At most O(n(k+2)^3) nodes overall. Each frontier scan and dedup by sorted C costs polynomial in N (charge all duplicates/failed searches). For general fixed b replace 2 by b and obtain O(n(k+b+1)^(b+1)) coarse bound; no logarithmic-b polynomial claim. This is an elementary bounded-frontier enumeration, not a new general treewidth algorithm.

For each found region take exactly all factors touching C. Every outside variable in those factors lies in B by graph construction. Compute exact table R_B={b: exists c satisfying all those factors}, by at most 2^(k+2) assignments, retaining a checked internal witness per feasible boundary row. No hidden protected tag may be omitted. If all regional factors are complement-invariant, R_B is complement-invariant. For |B|<=1 it is either empty or full. For |B|=2 it is exactly empty, full, equality or disequality. Empty rejects globally in the current guard; equality or disequality is a certified linear equation on the ports; full allows deleting the internal factors and variables with reverse witness table. With absolute pins or non-complement-symmetric factors use the full binary table (2SAT), not the complement classification. This latter extension and arbitrary protected tags not executed. Any local probe is conditional on the exact boundary tuple; only the projected relation or its universally entailed parity is exported. Overlapping regions may all provide implied equations but cannot all be deleted as independent products. For simultaneous full-relation deletion require pairwise disjoint interiors and no selected boundary in another selected interior (or do sequential deletion). Reverse records restore all original values; verify original source.
For a hidden two-terminal equality gadget with at most k private variables, no outside couplings except its two terminals, and satisfiable equal-port states, deletion of the ports may split the private part into several connected pieces. Their exact boundary relations intersect (with any port-only factors retained); each piece with two ports has empty/full/equal/unequal support, and a one-port nonempty piece is unrestricted. If equality comes from the private part, at least one piece forces equality; a port-only binary constraint is already direct closure. Thus all such hidden port equalities on a frozen source are discovered in one sweep. This applies to the DO02 Fano-minus-line modules with five private vertices. For fixed-girth DO02 substitutes K_g of bounded size, take k sufficient for their private vertices (or k=O(log N) on instances large enough); arbitrary growing-gadget or adaptive discovery coverage is not assumed.
Repeat exact repeated-literal/binary closure before each sweep, after each certified substitution and at stagnation; add every sound parity obtained on the frozen residual and close each substitution. When no new parity is available, project a disjoint family of full modules, with recorded lifts. Every successful iteration removes at least one active variable; at most n successful iterations and one final failed search. Work T<=2^k poly(N), polynomial storage for fixed k or k<=c log N (only up to four witnesses per module; seen keys and frontier scans charged), CM<=T S in a bounded-cell model. No unrestricted search branches; if residual nonempty with no module, return UNKNOWN. No assertion that all high-girth stretched families normalize under every greedy contraction order. ## B. Deterministic 2-approximation for common-isotropic linear guards (PROVED)
Let D be the binary dual vector space of linear forms on the free coordinates after exact affine elimination. For each nonaffine two-bit relative factor i, let U_i=span(a_i,b_i) be its two-dimensional observation plane. For a signed NAE triple, a_i=x_u+x_v and b_i=x_u+x_w, and signs merely shift the one forbidden pair. Its Boolean violation polynomial is a product of two affine forms; its polar alternating form is a_i tensor b_i + b_i tensor a_i. More generally every rank-two alternating form over GF(2) has this expression for a basis of its row plane. On a direction subspace K^perp, it vanishes iff the two forms become dependent when restricted there, i.e. dim(U_i mod K)<=1. This is precisely the common-isotropic condition for a family of rank-at-most-two polar forms. For any nonaffine forbidden-pair relation, rank two on a guard fiber retains three of four relative states and is not affine; rank <=1 guarantees every feasible fiber is affine. For general explicit relative tables this condition is sufficient, not always necessary. A general higher-rank polar form may cancel between different wedge summands, so its arbitrary rank decomposition is not covered by the approximation theorem.

Define b_* = min dim K such that K intersects every U_i nontrivially. Start H=0; while some U_i has two independent images modulo H, choose its basis a,b and add BOTH to H. Every iteration adds exactly two independent dimensions. For any optimal K, its intersection with U_i has a nonzero vector c. Since U_i intersects current H trivially, c is not in H, but is in new H and K. Thus dim(H intersect K) strictly increases by at least one on each iteration. There are at most b_* iterations and final dim H<=2 b_*. Polynomial greedy discovery and rank checks; no search over subspaces required. Final H satisfies the desired common-isotropic property. This also shows factor 2 may be achieved (single plane, optimum1, greedy2; finite tests confirm). For general rank>2 forms no approximation factor claimed.

Apply certified local affine support closure before greedy discovery: for each forbidden-pair constraint enumerate its four observation rows, intersect with current affine equations via Gaussian consistency; delete forbidden and infeasible rows. Empty rejects, support of size 1 or 2 adds its exact affine equations; support of size 3 gives no affine-hull information. Restart after every rank increase. Quotient by accumulated affine equations. Then enumerate all 2^dim H assignments to H, each retaining its explicit guard equations. On every guard fiber each original observation plane has rank <=1, so its exact feasible support (0,1 or2 rows) is affine and Gaussian closure is a COMPLETE decision method in that guard. Equivalently compute all restricted affine equations first and solve jointly. Empty guard rejects; a nonempty affine solution space gives witness by Gaussian back-substitution. Guard assignments are disjoint and exhaustive, so for this explicit original-variable model exact count is sum of 2^(n-rank_guard) over feasible guards. Do not import count semantics after nonparsimonious projection. For decision with regional modules, back-substitute retained source witnesses. All conditional equations remain in their guard, only root-certified equations are unconditional. All source tests, closure scans, guard edges, canonical equations, arithmetic bit lengths, and reconstruction charged. Streamed T<=2^dim H poly(N)<=4^b_* poly(N), S=poly(N), CM<=TS. Polynomial if discovered rank logarithmic; no universal such bound. Counts have at most n+1 bits. Full GD07 higher-rank quadratic lift, strict tetravalent acquisition and all-channel outer closure not executed. Test runner uses finite bit-mask guard enumerations only at small ranks; symbolic algorithm uses arbitrary-precision guard counters.

## C. Structural tradeoffs and falsifier
The common-pair family NAE(x0,x1,x_i) for i=2,...,n-1 has m=n-2 independent quadratic coefficient rows (each contains unique monomial x1*x_i), but one common form x0+x1 hits every plane. b_*=1, greedy rank2, four guards suffice, exact count 2^(n-1)+2 (x0 != x1 gives 2^(n-1); x0=x1 forces all others opposite). Thus raw GD07-type independent quadratic direction count can be large even with a paid isotropic terminal.
Conversely any disjoint collection of t ternary scopes forces b_*>=t: their U_i have disjoint coordinate supports, so any K meeting each contains t linearly independent vectors. If each variable occurs in <=Delta clauses, greedy disjoint clause packing has size at least m/(3Delta-2). In particular for connected simple cubic-dual NAE, Delta=2, b_*>=m/4, even though DA01 interval-degree matching solves these instances in polynomial time. This falsifies a UNIVERSAL logarithmic common-isotropic backdoor premise, not the combined portfolio; no unrestricted lower bound. The DO01 bounded-width local obstruction and DO02 spectral resistance cannot imply universal hardness either. Three-port complement-invariant supports need not be affine (three allowed relative states); merging them by affine hull is false. Two-port exact projection and common-isotropic guards are alternative structural contractions, not a universal nonaffine-to-affine rule.
