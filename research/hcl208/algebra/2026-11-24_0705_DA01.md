## Theorem A (exact signed degree-two factor)
Exhaust implemented certified interval closure before decomposition: substitute pins; clip bounds to [0,residual arity]; reject empty interval; delete tautologies; if upper=0 or lower=arity, pin one literal and restart. Conditional deductions retain guard. Repeat locally on each boundary assignment. No full semantic or all-channel closure asserted.
For an unpinned variable with one occurrence at row i, make an edge i--private leaf of allowed degree [0,1]. For two equal-sign occurrences at rows i,j, make one edge i--j; its selection equals the first literal. For opposite signs introduce a private vertex w of exact degree 1, edges i--w,w--j; exactly one edge selected. The original row vertices require degrees in their residual integer intervals. Every source assignment maps to a unique selected edge set, and conversely the first edge selection XOR first sign recovers its variable. Parallel edges kept by ID. Isolated unconstrained variables are free. Thus feasible signed rows with occurrence<=2 are exactly feasible interval-degree subgraphs.

Self-contained reduction to ordinary perfect matching: for each graph edge make two endpoint nodes linked by an edge. For vertex i create u_i slots, l_i mandatory, u_i-l_i optional. Connect each endpoint to every slot of its incident graph vertex. Put all optional slots in one clique; add one optional dummy iff sum u_i is odd. No mandatory slot has a clique edge. In a perfect matching each original edge either matches its endpoints to each other (unselected), or both endpoints to respective slots (selected). Mandatory slots enforce lower bounds and total slots enforce upper bounds. Conversely from any feasible degree subgraph assign distinct slots to selected endpoints covering mandatory slots first; unused optional slots plus the dummy have even cardinality since the number of selected endpoints is even. Pair them in the clique. This also works with empty bounds and nonbipartite graphs. Size O(N) vertices and O(N^2) edges, polynomial deterministic maximum-cardinality blossom gives exact decision. SAT lift is checked in source rows.
