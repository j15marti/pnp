## A. Exact connected projection (PROVED)
Let K=GF(2)^2, with a signed exclusion x_u+x_v != s_uv on each edge. A connected private component C consists of t>=1 disjoint simple K4 blocks linked by t-1 single bridges in a tree; each private vertex has total degree exactly four. Every remaining unused vertex port connects to a protected vertex by a single signed exclusion. Thus each private vertex has precisely one incident bridge or boundary port outside its K4. There are b=2t+2 boundary port occurrences (not necessarily distinct protected IDs), n_C=4t=2b-4, precisely the sharp family in DA09. All private lists initially full, no other private consumers, quotas or tags. External constraints remain intact. The claim is pointwise in the complete protected word. No strict physical E4/B4 source adapter presumed.

For any signed K4 with lists of size >=3, it is uncolorable iff its internal translations are balanced (there exists a gauge q_v with s_uv=q_u+q_v on every internal edge), and the gauge-transformed lists are all the same 3-set.

Proof of clique fact: if a list has size 4, greedily color that vertex last, after its three neighbors. Otherwise suppose no coloring, all lists size 3. Pin any color r in a chosen root list. Every remaining vertex must lose one available color: otherwise the residual triangle has lists >=2 and a slack list >=3 and admits greedy coloring (color slack last, orient the other two arbitrarily). Hence for every root color r and other vertex i, r+s_ri belongs to its original list, so after switching signs on edges incident to the root to zero, all four lists equal the root list L. For each r in L the residual triangle has the same two-element list L\\{r} on each vertex. If an internal triangle edge has a translation that does not preserve this 2-set, color its first endpoint with a color whose forbidden image misses the other endpoint's list, color the third vertex next, then the second endpoint last. This colors the triangle. Uncolorability therefore forces every internal edge translation to preserve every one of the three distinct two-element subsets of L. Their intersecting structure forces the translation to be zero (a nonzero translation of K pairs the four colors into two disjoint pairs). All internal signs are thus zero in this gauge. Conversely, ordinary K4 cannot be colored from one common 3-set. This proves the claim without relying on a black-box bad-cover recognition theorem.

Root the tree of cliques. A nonroot clique communicates the set S of feasible colors of the vertex incident to its parent bridge; S is nonempty since that vertex has full list and the others have lists of size >=3. A child whose S has at least two colors forbids nothing at the parent endpoint, since any parent color excludes at most one child color. A singleton S forbids precisely its translated color. A boundary port likewise forbids exactly one color. By the clique fact, the nonroot clique forces its parent endpoint to ONE color c iff the clique is balanced and all three incoming forbiddances exist and agree in its gauge. Indeed if S={c}, artificially exclude c at that endpoint; the resulting degree-feasible K4 must be uncolorable, yielding the stated conditions. Conversely under these conditions the three other vertices exhaust the common three-color set, forcing the missing color c in that gauge. Otherwise |S|>=2. At the root all four incoming constraints must supply forbiddances and be equal after gauge, and the root clique balanced, for failure. These messages are exact for each boundary word; no choices of distinct occurrences of a protected variable are made independently.

The bridge tree allows all locally balanced cliques to be put in one global gauge q. Thus if ANY internal signed cycle is unbalanced the entire component extends EVERY boundary word. Otherwise define c_p=x_{protected(p)}+s_p+q_{private(p)} for each port p. The complete rejection event is
    E: c_1=c_2=...=c_b.
Consequently the exact existential support is g=1-1_E (or identically 1 if unbalanced). Induction on the rooted clique tree proves both directions: a failure requires every child forced and all aligned incoming forbiddances equal, recursively equivalent to all normalized boundary words equal; the converse propagates the forced color and leaves the root K4 with only three colors. The proof includes a single clique. No preservation of original private multiplicity is asserted.

## B. Exact support, linearization and fiber tradeoff (PROVED)
For independent port words the event E has codimension 2(b-1), since all b colors are tied to a common two-bit color. On an arbitrary consistent affine chart, let the consistent nonempty rejection event have actual RREF rowspace U of rank r. Translation periods of its indicator, and hence of g, are exactly U^perp (for any direction outside this kernel translate a point of E out of E). So the minimum linear support rank is r, including r=0 for a constant predicate. In the independent-port family r=4t+2 grows linearly even though the exact signed affine-indicator representation uses at most TWO terms. BD09 Fourier packet count also at most two: delta at zero for 1 minus one affine phase packet on U (coefficient 2^-r). A nonempty E has affine indicator of Boolean algebraic degree r: change to independent affine coordinates and multiply the r equality indicators; the highest-degree monomial has coefficient 1 and invertible affine changes preserve degree. If K0 is any pinned observation rowspace, on a fiber meeting E the event has relative codimension r-dim(U intersect K0). On disjoint fibers the predicate is constant. Therefore the exact minimum dimension of a linear control space making g affine on EVERY fiber is max(0,r-1), attained by pinning any r-1 independent event rows. This is a higher-degree indicator-specific counterpart to RT15's rank-two polar-plane criterion; it is NOT an assertion about arbitrary alternative quadratic source encodings. In particular large intrinsic affine-control rank and large minimum support rank do not imply expensive local packet work.

Let W be the complete observation space of all outside consumers, after paid chart substitution; no private quota or hidden protected observation may be dropped. If E inconsistent, deletion is universal. Otherwise exact universal existential deletion over the outside fibers occurs iff
    dim(U+W)-dim W > 0.
Indeed if positive, every nonempty exterior fiber is either disjoint from E or intersects it in fraction 2^-delta<1; choose a point outside E by Gaussian lift forcing one independent event equation to fail. If delta=0, a word in E induces an entire forbidden exterior fiber, so universal deletion is impossible. If independent ports are all protected by outside consumers, delta=0 and the exact two-term packet must be retained. This identifies a precise geometry-specific tradeoff between exposed syndrome and affine-control rank, not a rank lower bound on arbitrary algorithms.

For a collection of disjoint-private clique trees with common selector chart, if every nonempty exterior fiber has sum_i 2^-delta_i<1 (omit inconsistent E_i), BS09's union-bound credit yields universal deletion of the collection. Indeed on every exterior fiber the number of safe words is >= fiber size minus sum of exact bad-event sizes, a positive additive integer credit. Conditional expectation by successive bit splits retains positive credit, then lift all private cliques independently on the SAME resulting chart assignment. This multi-region corollary and the general Fourier/rank/fiber algorithms are symbolic in this run, not executed. Failure of the sufficient union bound is UNKNOWN, not UNSAT.

## C. Global gluing counterexample and diagonal scope (PROVED)
For an arbitrary proper 3-CNF, compile each clause independently into one balanced K4 tree, with normalized port 1 fixed to 0, three other port words equal to the literal truth bits embedded as 00/01; all additional ports if any are fixed to zero. Affine chart synchronization identifies original Boolean variables and accounts for negations and source identities. A component rejects iff all three literals are false. Thus arbitrary 3-SAT compiles to this family even when each clique tree has constant size, every local support has two signed packets, each clause rejection has rank at most three, and the graph of private vertices has treewidth 3. More generally any polynomial number of clique blocks per owner can be used with redundant zero ports. The independent-region graph alone is not the chart dependency graph. The construction is polynomial, exact in both SAT directions, and reconstructs every private vertex by the above tables. An arbitrary global compatibility/coverage test for these components is SAT/coNP-complete respectively. This is an explicit extension of DA07's star compiler to DA09's sharp kernel family; not an unrestricted lower bound. A polynomial decision procedure for this full chart class would establish P=NP, not a consequence of the compact local representation.
