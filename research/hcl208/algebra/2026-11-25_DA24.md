## Exact theorem
Given an authenticated nonnegative integer pair energy E0 on a certified planar graph and an explicit rank-r binary affine observation map L, allow an arbitrary polynomially evaluable nonnegative integer correction h(Lx) and guard g(Lx). Assume total energy upper bound polynomial in original N (unit NAE pair costs meet this; binary huge weights do not automatically). The complete guarded energy histogram is obtainable in 2^r poly(N) bit operations. Set B=2^(n+1), F(y)=1_g(y) B^h(y), use the integer Walsh transform W_F, and calculate Z=2^-r sum_a W_F(a) sum_x (-1)^(a^T Lx) B^E0(x). Base-B digits are exact counts, with no carry. Intermediate signed values are never interpreted as counts before exact division/cancellation.

For any character c, expand each core edge weight as (A_e + D_e (-1)^(x_u+x_v))/2, where A_e=w0+w1, D_e=w0-w1. If c has odd total on a core component, the character sum is zero. Otherwise find a forest edge set T with boundary c by parity propagation. The expansion is 2^(n-|E0_edges|) times the weighted even-subgraph sum with A and D interchanged on T. This avoids dividing by individual weights and admits zero/negative weights.

Explicit polynomial reduction of the latter to a weighted planar perfect matching sum: split each degree-d vertex into an embedded path of d incident-port copies (one old edge each), put A=D=1 on new path edges; evenness uniquely determines path choices when old incident parity is even. Replace each resulting degree<=3 vertex by a clique on its ports. Match an external port edge exactly when its original edge is absent from the even subset. Remaining ports must be even in number (zero or two), and are matched uniquely internally. Give each external matching edge weight A; distribute D of each original edge to just one of its endpoints, and weight internal clique edge by the product of assigned endpoint factors. The perfect matching monomial is precisely the even-subgraph monomial. All-external matching exists as a combinatorial sign reference even with zero weights. A planar Pfaffian orientation makes all combinatorial matching signs identical, including with signed/zero numerical weights; calibrate the global sign by the reference rather than taking absolute value. Compute Pfaffian by exact rational elimination with row/column pivoting and polynomial bit size (minors bound), or exact integer fraction-free arithmetic. Isolated core vertices contribute through the original character sum, not through added artificial fields. Embedding, orientation, forest propagation and all arithmetic are paid.

Witness acquisition: repeatedly select a remaining core edge and query each possible parity by substituting x_v=x_u+b and contracting that edge, preserving planarity, moving loop costs into constants and transporting the affine map and guard. Keep a parity that has positive count at the requested target; rank never increases. When no core edge remains, enumerate the at most 2^r observation words and solve the affine lift by Gaussian elimination. At most n contractions, verify original source energy and guard. Outside-span probes add at most one extra independent affine row per call, giving full target-model hull using S29's O(n^2) probes and verified lifts, not by accumulating all probes as guards. Thus for r=O(log N), the admitted exact phase including hull, closure and failed probes has polynomial aggregate cost, peak and cumulative space (CM<=(T+1)S on same serial execution). For SAT use certified rank-one closure first, after deductions and at stagnation; for an optimum histogram, only optimum-preserving deductions are allowed. No free semantic closure.

## Universal firewall
On n actual coordinates with every pair coefficient nonzero in the real Fourier expansion of a quadratic signed energy, suppose it is represented pointwise as a planar pair energy plus ANY function of r affine binary observations. Fourier support of the correction lies in row space U of L, irrespective of its computational form. Every absent planar-core edge uv has e_u+e_v in U. Let H contain all absent pairs. Its edge-incidence span over F2 has rank n-c(H), where c counts isolated vertices too, so c>=n-r. Choose one vertex from each H-component: all intercomponent pairs are present in the planar core, forming a clique, hence c<=4. Thus r>=n-4. Affine translations/signs do not change this support. This refutes universal logarithmic patch rank on raw complete-pair charts (including S27), even for arbitrary nonlinear correction; not a barrier to nonlocal coordinate changes, auxiliary lifts, general nonplanar matching or decision-only algorithms. A two-hub planar core has rank n-3 for complete input, giving matching order of growth. This does not prove simultaneous hardness. An affine guard can reduce the actual geometry, so the bound is not asserted after arbitrary guard quotient.
