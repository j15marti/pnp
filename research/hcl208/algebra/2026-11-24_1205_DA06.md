## A. Exact conditional parity and one-defect quotient (PROVED)
Let color at vertex v be (a_v,b_v) in F2^2. An edge uv forbids difference (p_e,s_e). At a fixed first-bit word a, define A(a)={e:a_u+a_v=p_e}. Then all remaining conditions are precisely b_u+b_v=q_e=1+s_e on A(a); all other edges are tautologies. Forest propagation assigns b and checks every nonforest edge. A contradiction iff some active cycle has odd sum q. When consistent, the number of b-words is 2^c(V,A), including isolated vertices. Both directions and witness verification follow directly.

Choose p_e=0 on every edge and q_e=0 except a designated closing edge st with q=1. All nonclosing edges are 'good', enforcing b equality only if their endpoints' a bits agree. Pin a_s=a_t=0. Let H_a be the active good graph. A lift exists iff s and t are in different components of H_a. Necessity: an active good path forces b_s=b_t, conflicting with the closing edge. Sufficiency: set b=1 in t's good component and b=0 elsewhere. This pointwise reconstruction is linear in graph size (encoded-index costs charged). More generally after gauge switching, contract active q=0 components, retain all active q=1 edges, and test bipartiteness of the quotient (loops reject). An arbitrary q pattern is eligible after choosing a spanning forest gauge; no small exceptional-edge set assumed. Distinguish coefficient rank n-c(V,A) from number of nonzero q edges and cycle inconsistency. Our hard guarded examples have exactly one frustrated edge before conditioning, not necessarily small coefficient rank.

## B. Dual exact CNF compilers (PROVED)
Take any proper nonempty 3-CNF F(z), variables z_1,...,z_n, m clauses of three distinct literals (width <=3 also permitted). The guard on first bits is explicit: connectors a=0; occurrence a is z_j or 1+z_j. Thus guard acquisition and its entire affine chart are polynomial, with n free generator bits (unused declared variables restored), no hidden nonlinear condition.

PARALLEL compiler: each clause is a private path from its entry to exit through its three occurrence vertices; set a at occurrence equal to its literal truth, so path enabled iff clause false. Compose paths in a binary parallel tree: at each internal node make new entry s and exit t, connect s to each child entry and each child exit to t by good edges. All connector a=0. Append a single closing edge between root terminals, sign (p,s)=(0,0) (q=1); good edges have sign (0,1) (q=0). Root good connection iff at least one source clause false. Therefore
  exists b Legal_parallel(z,b) iff F(z).
This is an exact many-one reduction from 3SAT to *guarded* positive feasibility and pointwise witness reduction in both directions. Membership NP by the explicit graph and guard verification. For 3-clauses V=7m-2, E=8m-3. Degree <=3.

SERIES compiler: each clause is a private parallel bundle of three paths of length two, each via one occurrence with a=1+literal truth. It connects iff the clause is true. Keep separate entry/exit for every bundle and join consecutive bundles by one good connector edge. Append the same closing edge. Then
  exists b Legal_series(z,b) iff NOT F(z).
All-fiber coverage forall z exists b Legal_series iff F UNSAT, coNP-complete (a missing fiber is verified by the linear parity test). Positive feasibility for this compiler is easy for proper nonempty clauses: falsify one clause. For 3-clauses V=5m, E=7m. Degree <=4.
Both graphs are simple two-terminal series-parallel, including the closing edge as a parallel branch. A tree decomposition with terminal bag and width <=2 follows by induction: for a series join attach a bag of the three distinct boundary/join vertices to child terminal bags; for parallel join attach the two-terminal bag to both children. Hence treewidth <=2 before guard edges are incorporated. Good graph connected before conditioning, and every cycle containing the closing edge is odd, so the minimum number of second-channel sign changes to balance is exactly one. These bounds are structural, not empirical width estimates.

Every fixed guard word is cheaply evaluable and liftable; the PARALLEL existential question and SERIES uniform-coverage question remain respectively NP-complete and coNP-complete. DO06's indexed private-rank construction is thus complemented by a two-channel bounded-degree graph construction with rank-one frustration. The number of private b bits is O(m), not logarithmic; conditional repair is polynomial by parity, not by enumerating b. No unrestricted decision-hardness claim from the series compiler. Without the affine occurrence guard the graphs are trivially SAT (choose opposite a at closing endpoints and constant b elsewhere). The guard is essential.

## C. Rank and width tradeoff (PROVED)
Raw local forbidden-difference observation plane D_e=span{a_u+a_v,b_u+b_v}. For an all-fibers common linear guard K, every raw edge support becomes affine iff dim(K intersect D_e)>=1 for every edge (rank-two complement-of-point is nonaffine, residual rank <=1 is affine or empty). A spanning tree's edge planes are in direct sum. Hence in a connected graph the optimum common guard rank is exactly |V|-1, attained by all first-bit differences. This raw pre-projection invariant does not lower-bound any adaptive/semantic algorithm; after imposing the supplied guard, the free selector rank is only n and observation dependencies are changed. It explains why one frustrated cycle coordinate does not imply a small isotropic/backdoor rank. I11's rank-one matching result applies only after authenticating actual local supports; no automatic matching shortcut follows from the physical graph width.

For the supplied construction tree, associate to each region I the set X_I of source generator variables used by its occurrence leaves. The span of its nonconstant affine guard forms is the coordinate space on X_I (signs only change constants). Across a cut the exact guard interface rank is |X_I intersect X_out|. At a binary join define w_I as the number of source variables present in at least two of left child, right child and outside. Thus physical treewidth 2 does not bound this algebraic width; duplicate occurrence copies are NOT independent. The complete guarded CSP's width must include the occurrence correlations. E.g. a prescribed parallel tree whose two halves both contain all n generators has join rank n. No claim every tree has that width.

Constructive terminal: for each region keep a two-valued truth support (connectivity 0/1) per assignment to its boundary X_I intersect X_out. At a leaf enumerate at most 3 source bits. At a join enumerate the w_I shared assignments and the two possible truth values from each child; combine with AND or OR as dictated by series/parallel construction, existentially forget variables no longer needed outside. The fixed assignment to shared variables makes child interiors independent, so both soundness and completeness follow inductively. Archive a witness pointer for each retained entry. At root truth 0 gives a positive lift, truth 1 a missing fiber. Thus both existential and uniform decisions are paid T<=poly(N)(1+sum_I 2^w_I) (leaf constants included), S<=same bound, reconstruction polynomial in tree size and input. For an authenticated fixed tree use P=sum_I (1+2^w_I) as job credit, leaves w_I<=3; subtree job sets are disjoint, parent reserves >=1, so sum child P <= P-1 with original tree contexts retained. This is a restricted batch potential, not arbitrary assignment-branch contraction. Width discovery for THIS compiler follows from manifest, not optimal decomposition search. This DP theorem is symbolic; experiment below uses independent fixed-fiber parity and connectivity instead.

## D. Affine-cover exponential barrier on BOTH polarities (PROVED)
Choose F=AND_{i=1}^k(z_{2i-1} OR z_{2i}) (width-two clauses). Its support S=(F2^2 minus {00})^k has size 3^k. The PARALLEL positive support and SERIES missing support are exactly S. Any affine subspace contained in S projects onto an affine subspace avoiding 00 in every pair; each such projection has dimension at most 1. The concatenated projection is injective, so its dimension <=k and size <=2^k. Every exact union-of-affine-fibers representation of S consequently requires at least ceil((3/2)^k) pieces. Auxiliary affine coordinates projected out do not change this conclusion: a linear projection of an affine set remains affine. Covers may overlap. This refutes a universal polynomial affine-cover representation even with physical treewidth 2, degree <=4, exactly one frustrated edge and independent source pairs. Both polarities are obstructed across the two compilers. The family is algorithmically easy through its product expression, and has constant-width guard-aware decomposition. Symbolic Boolean formulas and DAGs avoid the affine-cover lower bound. No unrestricted runtime implication.
