## 3. DA04-A (PROVED): deficit-parameter exact solver
Define current deficit set D={v: |L(v)|<deg_G(v)} and k=|D|. Pinning and removing u deletes one incident edge and at most one list color at each neighbor, so deg(v)-|L(v)| cannot increase. No previously nondeficient vertex becomes deficient. If u is deficient, each nonrejected child has k'<=k-1. This remains true after arbitrary cascades of singleton closure. Thus branch only on deficient vertices and never branch in a degree-list terminal.

Degree-list terminal: all lists have cardinality >= current degree. For each component, if some list is larger than degree, root a spanning tree at that vertex and color in reverse tree order, leaving at least one unused color at every step. Otherwise each list equals degree; edge exclusions induce a correspondence cover (restricted translation is a partial matching). By Bernshteyn-Kostochka-Pron, Theorem 9 of arXiv:1609.00763v1, a connected simple graph with any block neither clique nor cycle is DP-degree-colorable; hence accept regardless of signs/lists under the hypothesis. If every block is a clique or cycle (GDP-tree), exact block-cut tree DP: vertex message intersects list and child masks; block message gives the allowable colors of its parent vertex by combining child masks with signed edge tests. Clique size <=5 because degree <=4; enumerate <=4^5 words. For cycles, fix parent color, traverse remaining path with a 4-bit reachable-state mask, and enforce closing signed edge. Each subtree participates once, conditioned on its articulation color; no independent witness mixing. Isolated vertices handled by slack or singleton closure. Thus all connected components decided exactly in polynomial time. No use of complete semantic closure as an oracle.

Witness: at an accepted degree-list state try each possible color of a chosen vertex as an actual pin followed by closure and the SAME degree-list decision algorithm on the induced residual. In every nonempty child the degree-list inequality persists by the pin argument. At least one child is feasible; select one and iterate, at most 4n trials. This is polynomial self-reduction, not a call to unrestricted SAT. Reinsert original fixed vertices and verify original signs and lists. Internal degree-list decision invocations are not treated as additional unexplored search branches. A GDP tree is not automatically UNSAT; exact DP is required. Balanced K5 rejects, all tested one-edge deletion and one-edge sign perturbations accept. A simple signed graph with all lists full and max degree <=4 is therefore an exact polynomial terminal, regardless of raw tensor width. This is a direct application of known DP-degree-colorability with our deficit and projection adapter, not a new Brooks theorem.

Aggregate frontier credit: mu(k)=(4^(k+1)-1)/3, an integer of O(k) bits. Each deficient branch has at most four children with k_i<=k-1, so sum mu(k_i)<=4mu(k-1)=mu(k)-1. Each terminal consumes one job; unary closure/discovery is charged to that job (it need not strictly drop k). No cache needed. All processed branch/terminal nodes <=mu(k_initial). For fixed k<=C log N (any fixed C), credit magnitude and total time polynomial. For arbitrary k this is FPT, not universal. T<=4^k poly(N), S<=poly(N) using depth-first branch copies and polynomial self-reduction workspace; CM<=(T+1)S in one bounded-bit cell model. Conservative implementation-level polynomial per node O(N^5) time/O(N^3) space suffices with elementary scans, bounded block enumeration, sorting/indices, recursion and full source witness verification. ## 4. DA04-B (PROVED): universal nonlinear projection across large interfaces
Let C be a connected induced region containing only vertices with |L(v)|>=deg_G(v) in the FULL graph, including edges to exterior. Fix ANY exterior word respecting its own lists; the interior list L_C(v) deletes forbidden colors from exterior incidences, at most one per edge. Thus |L_C(v)|>=deg_{G[C]}(v). If some original vertex has strict slack, or C has a block neither clique nor cycle, the entire region is extendible for EVERY exterior word. The exact existential projection of all its internal and crossing constraints onto the exterior is the tautology. Delete the region and its edges, retain source and lift recipe; exterior constraints remain exact and no new deficit appears. Given any accepted exterior solution, construct L_C and use DA04-A degree-list witness routine to lift, checking all edges. Sequential deletions reconstruct in reverse. Must not erase any protected interior observation or hidden external coupling: either explicitly retain in the interface or decline. Discovery by scanning connected components of the induced graph of nondeficient vertices, plus block/slack tests; polynomial, no boundary enumeration.

For GDP components exact projection is not necessarily tautological, but can be represented as a pointwise polynomial-size Boolean mask circuit in exterior color bits: compute L_C from ALL boundary incidences, evaluate the block-cut masks as above for each articulation color. This exact circuit does not imply polynomial global coverage/identity evaluation. For a private cycle with two spokes per vertex this recovers the feasibility problem of BW03; their six affine guards can be more compact. Full symbolic GDP circuit synthesis and actual deletion/reverse recipe orchestration were not executed here; pointwise terminal and arbitrary-boundary witnesses were executed.

High-interface control: take a circular ladder on 2h vertices (h>=3), each internal degree 3, attach one distinct exterior port to each vertex; connect ports in a cycle. The interior is one non-GDP block and each full interior degree is 4, so every exterior word extends, regardless of signs. There are 4^(2h) possible port words. The tensor observation cut between all interior/crossing edges and all exterior-cycle edges has rank 2(2h-1): the connected interior/crossing graph spans all even vertex vectors on the union, the connected exterior graph spans the even subspace on ports; their intersection is exactly that subspace, duplicated in two bits. This is a specified cut, NOT the optimum factor-tree width. Thus high cut rank and raw boundary capacity do not prevent exact tautological projection. This directly bypasses the linear-guard approach of DA03 without contradicting it. Larger non-GDP components can have large internal treewidth; no bound on treewidth is needed by the theorem.

## 5. DA04-C (PROVED): critical-core obstruction to the new parameter
For an edge/vertex-minimal non-four-colorable simple unsigned graph H, minimum degree >=4. More generally a vertex-minimal uncolorable signed simple graph with full lists also has min degree >=4. Let L be vertices of degree exactly 4, B the vertices of degree >=5. For every component of H[L], if it is non-GDP then DA04-B extends every coloring of its complement, which is colorable by criticality, contradiction. Therefore H[L] is a GDP forest (components of clique/cycle blocks). This is the standard critical low-vertex structure as a consequence of DP-degree-colorability; no claim of novelty for the classical theorem. For girth g>=4, only bridge and cycle blocks remain, each cycle has length at least g. With l=|L| and c=number of its components, e(L)<= (1+1/(g-1))(l-c), because each cycle consumes at least g-1 new vertices and adds one excess edge over a tree. Thus cut(L,B)=4l-2e(L)>=(2-2/(g-1))l. If max degree <=Delta and a=2-2/(g-1), then Delta|B|>=a|L|, hence |B|>=a n/(Delta+a). Holds also if L is empty. For DO04's bounded-degree high-girth critical family, initial deficit k=|B| is linear. Every single-edge SAT neighbor has at least |B|-2 initial deficits with full lists (only endpoints can lose degree>4). The bound is on this parameter, not the actual number of visited branches. Nonlinear transformations or newly certified deductions can still permit cheap solving. In particular high initial k does not entail expensive computation (e.g. arbitrary bipartite graph).
