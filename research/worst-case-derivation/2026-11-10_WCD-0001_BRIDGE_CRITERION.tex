\section{Setup and the two halves}
$\Wb_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$.
Take $R$ a Cook--Reckhow system (verifier poly-time). Charging nothing for finding
$\pi$ is exactly the $\exists$ of the definition, so $\Wb_R$ is the optimal-proof-size
function; the program has two independent halves:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(LB)] $\Wb_R(N)\ne N^{O(1)}$ for one fixed natural $R$;
\item[(BR)] $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\Wb_R(N)\le N^{O(1)}$.
\end{enumerate}
(LB)+(BR) $\Rightarrow$ P$\ne$NP. (LB) alone does \emph{not}: ``EF not polybounded''
is consistent with NP$=$coNP.

\section{Theorem 1 (bridge criterion)}
Let $A$ be a polynomial-time decider for SAT and $\mathrm{Snd}_A(n)$ the propositional
tautology in variables $x\in\{0,1\}^{n}$ (CNF encoding) and assignment $a$:
``$A(x)=0\to\neg\mathrm{Sat}(x,a)$''. If $R$ is closed under substitution, can
evaluate polynomial-size circuits (EF; Frege only for formula-size/NC$^1$ pieces) and
$\mathrm{Snd}_A(n)$ has $R$-proofs of size $s(n)$, then
$\Wb_R(N)\le s(N)+\mathrm{poly}(N)$.

\emph{Proof.} For $F\in$ UNSAT, $A(F)=0$. Substitute $x:=F$ into $\mathrm{Snd}_A$.
The run of $A$ on the constant input $F$ is a poly-size straight-line computation;
EF derives each step by local extension-variable axioms, concluding $A(F)=0$.
Modus ponens gives $\neg\mathrm{Sat}(F,a)$ with $a$ free, which is $\bot$ after
unfolding the clauses of $F$. \qed

\textbf{Consequence.} The weakest \emph{provable} sufficient form of (BR) for
$R=$EF is ``P$=$NP implies a poly-time $A$ whose soundness EF proves in polynomial size'',
the propositional shadow of PV$\vdash$``$A$ solves SAT'' (Cook's translation). It is
conjectured that no poly-time $A$ is PV-provably a SAT solver; the converse
direction is not needed, since (BR) only has to hold, not be provable.

\section{Theorem 2 (dichotomy: strong enough vs.\ lower-boundable)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item (KNOWN, Cook--Reckhow) If NP$=$coNP some $R$ has $\Wb_R\le$ poly, and then
(LB) fails for that $R$; the ``trivial'' $R_A$ (empty proof, verifier runs $A$)
satisfies (BR) with equality: $\Wb_{R_A}$ superpoly $\iff$ SAT$\notin P$.
This $R_A$ is a \emph{reformulation} of P$\ne$NP, not progress (cf.\ ledger
rule: equivalent statements are not partial results).
\item For natural $R$ (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege) (LB) is
known but (BR) is false-in-spirit: SAT$\in P$ does not make $R$ strong
(resolution is exponential on PHP, Tseitin, random 3-CNF regardless).
\item For $R\supseteq$ EF, (BR) is plausible but (LB) is the open Cook--Reckhow
conjecture, with no hard family known: PHP has poly Frege proofs (Buss), Tseitin
formulas have poly EF proofs (Gaussian elimination under extension variables);
random near-threshold 3-CNF is the only widely believed candidate, EF-hardness open.
\end{enumerate}
So the sufficient bridge and the lower bound sit at opposite ends of one monotone
scale; any proof must land on a system strictly between bounded-depth Frege and a
``trivial'' $R_A$, and such a system has no known lower-bound technique (no feasible
interpolation beyond weak systems; natural-proofs-type barriers for circuit-based
techniques).

\section{Consequences for the five roles}
\begin{description}
\item[Lower-Bound Lead] Target (BR) in the form of Theorem~1 and ask for the weakest
$R$ with poly proofs of $\mathrm{Snd}_A$ for \emph{some} $A$; list which of
resolution+counting, PC/IPS, $\mathrm{AC}^0[2]$-Frege can p-simulate it.
\item[Reduction Builder] Fix the combined system as Frege$+$extension$+$algebraic
(IPS-style) rules; certify minimal DAG sizes on all $n\le 6$ UNSAT formulas by
exhaustive search to calibrate against Theorem~1's overhead.
\item[Hypergraph/Certificates] Hard families must beat EF, not resolution; test random
3-CNF at ratio $\approx4.3$ against Gaussian/IPS/Nullstellensatz degree and
shared-DAG compression; Tseitin and PHP are \emph{excluded} as EF-easy.
\item[Time--Space Auditor] The free-choice minimum is a size measure; if space is
charged, a $\max$ over states (peak memory) is $\le$ size, so superpoly space implies
superpoly size, but the converse transfer needs a pebbling-style theorem (OPEN).
\item[Red Team] Attack (BR): find an oracle $O$ with P$^O=$NP$^O$ yet EF$^O$ not
polybounded (a relativized separation of the bridge); attack (LB): any proposed
hard family with a poly EF proof via counting/Gaussian elimination.
\end{description}

\section{Literature check (this run)}
Pich--Santhanam (STOC'21) prove PV cannot prove \emph{strong} co-nondeterministic
lower bounds for NP; Li--Oliveira extend to APC$_1$/S$^1_2$. These do not rule out
(LB) for EF but indicate that a bounded-arithmetic-formalizable proof of a strong
form is unavailable. A 2025 result shows proof-search for EF (PAP) is NP-complete,
consistent with there being no cheap automation route. Cook's translation links
PV-provable statements to poly EF proofs, giving the Theorem~1 route. Prior
repository coverage: PROOF-SYSTEM-FALLBACK163 (Cook--Reckhow boundary, empty proofs).
