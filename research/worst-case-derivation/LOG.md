# Living log: worst-case derivation complexity W_R(N)

P versus NP remains OPEN.

## 2026-11-06 WCDERIV-0001
- W_R(N) with free optimal derivation = Cook-Reckhow proof-size function. Fixed-R lower bound alone does not give P != NP; the bridge SAT in P => W_R poly is load-bearing.
- Bridge is free for *some* R (Cook-Reckhow) but for a named R it is only known for optimal R. For R with an unconditional superpoly lower bound (resolution etc.) the bridge is equivalent to P != NP, i.e. vacuous.
- Useful R must be Frege/EF or stronger, where no lower bound is known. Pich-Santhanam (FOCS 2019) give conditional evidence that this is hard.
