\section*{Ranking and exact missing implications}
\noindent\textbf{Primary I: MCSP hardness magnification (highest priority).}
Published Oliveira--Pich--Santhanam Gap-MCSP magnification and McKay--Murray--Williams streaming magnification give externally proved separation endpoints. Cheraghchi--Hirahara--Myrisiotis--Yoshida (CHMY) provide unconditional strong one-tape MCSP lower bounds only at larger size parameters, in a randomized one-way-read-only-input plus one-work-tape model. The project's COFINAL-SLOPE-ABSORB1 is a conditional exponent argument: a sequence $\beta_j\downarrow0$ with lower-margin $g(\beta_j)/\beta_j\to\infty$ suffices only when hypotheses and model are aligned. A candidate transport with $\beta'\le\lambda\beta$ and $g'\ge\rho g$, $\rho>\lambda$, remains \textbf{OPEN}. A correct map must preserve all YES and NO promises, parameter/threshold, random error, target label uniformity, and physical one-tape/streaming implementation. The project has RETIRED fixed-threshold Partial-MCSP sampling, source-independent sample shortcuts, fixed-hash tricks, uncharged oracle/tape fusion, and support-count-only advantage iteration. SIGNED-FIBER22's total-variation cancellation identity is exact but has no CHMY-specific low-complexity quotient established.

\noindent\textbf{Primary II: ARC/FLMV charged resource contraction (second priority).}
The conditional map
\[
t_{j+1}\le\max\{11/10,3/5+t_j/2\},\qquad
s_{j+1}\le\max\{1/2,s_j/2\}
\]
has fixed point $(6/5,1/2)$, within the published matched-random-access SAT time-space excluded region (the sufficient FLMV boundary at $c=6/5$ is $23/45>1/2$). The crucial \textbf{OPEN} arrow is: every arbitrary correct polynomial SAT decider $A$ under $P=NP$ yields a uniform, recursively eligible, fully charged replacement algorithm meeting the quantitative map. Include descriptor acquisition, source reads, output serialization, child addresses, local combination, cache, replay, all ancestor/live storage, machine simulation, off-image correctness, and constants. A source-only recursively composable splitter meeting the numerical contract would itself create an unconditional forbidden algorithm and cannot be simply assumed. The old WRAPPER timing/hash race and oracle-status-only requests do not supply this arrow.

\noindent\textbf{Secondary theorem inventory, not a direct separation.}
Exact-(2,2) compilers, PC/PCR all-field graph-coloring transfer, ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5's candidate proof-system size-depth lifting, the signed NAE/Fano linear compiler, local pin, FTNC port ledgers, and corrected REVFIX anchored/circular degree transport produce restricted proof-system or semantic results. There is no valid general inference that every polynomial SAT decider yields a short PC/CP/Resolution proof. The FTNC hidden physical matching-compatibility rule is still OPEN. Fano cuts, treewidth, proof degree, tensor rank, wire/switch census, and many fold histories are not arbitrary-machine time lower bounds. Keep these as adversarial controls or separate mathematical theorem work, not as the primary separation claim.

\noindent\textbf{Deferred/retired as primary routes.}
UWI/free-hindsight is an exact equivalence requiring a same-input universal obstruction, not evidence it exists. PFCERT, bounded-output source invariants, extractor and HEC/CRS-PPC bridges lack all-algorithm, polynomially checkable certificate or resource transfer. HASH/TIMER WRAPPER is vulnerable to padding/direct-decider/syntactic exposure; short hash equality is not semantic equality. Alternating SAT/Circuit-SAT cyclic outputs admit global solving; uncharged UNSAT-answer gates would place coNP in NP. Affine Tseitin is a decisive counterexample to DNNF/Resolution/static-size to dynamic-time inference. Circuit gates/MOS/nets/fanout require fixed physical layouts and still do not quantify over arbitrary SAT algorithms. Liu-chain faster deterministic 3-SAT is potentially separate publishable algorithmic work but is not a P-versus-NP proof.
