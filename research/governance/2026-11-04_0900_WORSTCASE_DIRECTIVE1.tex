\section*{Shared worst-case derivation-cost directive (WORSTCASE-1)}
\textbf{Additive.} Changes no schedules, agent count or role ownership of other allocations. P versus NP remains OPEN.

\paragraph{Common target.} With $R$ a fixed inference system whose single steps are checkable in polynomial time,
\[
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\,|\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi).
\]
Strategy discovery is not charged. Priority bridge: $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)$.

\paragraph{Roles.} (1) General lower-bound lead: formalise the cost and the weakest sufficient bridge. (2) SAT-circuit reduction builder: fixed combined inference system with certified shortest DAGs. (3) Hypergraph and proof certificates: hard UNSAT families surviving alternative reasoning. (4) Time-space auditor: step costs, proof encodings, peak memory. (5) Red team: counterexamples, shorter derivations, failed transfers.

\paragraph{Rules.} Living log: \texttt{research/worstcase/LOG.md} (append-only entries, newest last). Every run checks current literature for clues to the specific open arrow and records it. Statuses per AGENTS.md. Cost must charge every step's verification, or the quantity trivialises (see WORSTCASE-0001, Prop.~2).
