\paragraph{The exact target.}
Let $\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ be CNF formulas with unique proper ternary OR-clauses, each containing three distinct variables and all positive or all negative literals; every active variable appears exactly twice positively and twice negatively. For each clause $C$, $\bar C$ means the all-sign-reversed \emph{OR clause}. The target $\mathcal C_\star$ is $\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ excluding only \emph{fully} complementary-clause-paired formulas $F$ with $\forall C\in F,\bar C\in F$. \emph{Partial complementary pairs remain legal.} The language $L_\star$ consists of all satisfying properly encoded $F\in\mathcal C_\star$; malformed strings are NO. With $k$ active variables, $3m=4k$ and full ordinary indexed encoding size $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$. The full M22 class is NP-complete by D\"ocker; the target remains NP-complete by replacing fully paired SAT inputs with a fixed nonpaired SAT fixture, and identity otherwise. These are NOT superpolynomial lower bounds.
