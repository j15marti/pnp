\section{ARC-0005: bounded capacity and row-symmetry preprocessing}
The original double wrapper's exact semantics, small-leaf cutoff and
recursive termination remain established at their previous scopes.
ARC-0005 adds a partial preprocessor at every recursive call, rather than
replacing those interfaces. It discovers a signed literal matrix from
variable-disjoint unique-literal clauses and complementary binary conflicts.
A verified capacity contradiction may return an exact zero-child VALUE.
Otherwise it verifies a full row action and applies the known orbitopal
fixing rule of Anders, Codel and Heule (2026), Definition 2 and Theorem 3.1:
\url{https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16855v1}.
No graph-isomorphism or SAT oracle is assumed.

For the verified action, a clause's signature retains outside literals and
its multiset of signed row profiles. If profile $\tau$ has multiplicity
$m_\tau$ and $k=\sum_\tau m_\tau$, its orbit has cardinality
\[
 \frac{r!}{(r-k)!\prod_\tau m_\tau!}.
\]
A duplicate-free clause set is invariant exactly when every observed
signature occurs that many times. The executable uses exact comparison-tree
counts and capped integer arithmetic, not a hash-only merge. The proof and
its independent finite checks are in the ARC-0005 report; final independent
RED promotion remains pending.

Before allocation, variable arrays and pair counters are capped by
$B=\lfloor M^{2/3}\rfloor$ cells. Retained profile payload has the same cap;
propagation is limited to eight source scans, and a charged pre-emission pass
requires ordinary child length at most $B$. Every cap exit returns FAIL.
The report gives a \textsc{Candidate} comparison/array-model proof of
$\widetilde O(M)$ local time and $\widetilde O(M^{2/3})$ live bits, including
FAIL outcomes, on its declared canonical indexed-CNF domain. This is not a
unit-coefficient Python heap bound or completed endpoint-model simulation.
It is also not a bound on the adapter's unchanged fallback and caches.

The final tests include 586 complete adapter answers checked by exhaustive
search, 900 orbit checks, 68 capacity cases through PHP(25,24), and 110
hostile checks. On PHP(7,6) the original inner builder has 197 DAG nodes and
1,956,661 incremental Python peak bytes; the bounded capacity member returns
UNSAT with zero children and 12,384 peak bytes. PHP(8,7) changes from 654
nodes and 7,600,902 peak bytes to zero children and 15,296 peak bytes.
These compare the unchanged original double-wrapper source, not the later
hybrid builder. Timings and allocations are finite evidence only.

The output cap deliberately eliminates many uncapped successes: the small
mode battery has 9 VALUE and 1,163 FAIL outcomes across 1,172 executions.
A separate easy-factor product produces a checked 114-bit ordinary child
from 2,202 bits, preserving rather than freely deciding its arbitrary core.
The member therefore strengthens the existing partial-portfolio construction
but does not establish all-CNF coverage, a general shrinker, or an ARC point.
Details and raw-output provenance are in
\url{https://github.com/j15marti/pnp/blob/main/research/agents/lead/2026-10-08_045552_LEAD_ARC-0005.tex}.
The joint general-SAT WRAPPER implication and P versus NP remain
\textsc{Open}.
