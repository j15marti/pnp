\subsection{A bounded partial symmetry/capacity member}
The ARC-0005 executable adds a source-discovered matrix-capacity and verified
row-symmetry member to the existing recursive WRAPPER. Its local arrays,
profile payload and ordinary output have input-size-dependent
$\lfloor M^{2/3}\rfloor$ caps; source-scanning propagation has eight passes.
Exhausting a cap returns FAIL, not UNSAT. The source-level comparison/array
accounting gives a candidate $\widetilde O(M)$-time,
$\widetilde O(M^{2/3})$-space partial interface, including failed attempts,
on the declared canonical read-only indexed-CNF domain. Independent RED
review and concrete endpoint-model transport remain pending.

This supplies a concrete partial member, not general achievable-region
closure: no all-CNF success guarantee has been proved, and the recursive
adapter's unchanged fallback retains its original costs. On standard dense
pigeonhole inputs the checked capacity certificate returns an exact VALUE
without any recursive children; the bounded orbit-only path also eliminates
the earlier small PHP DAG controls. Across 586 small formulas the complete
adapter agrees with exhaustive answers. The partial-member mode battery has
1,163 FAIL outcomes among 1,172 executions, so coverage is not inferred.
An additional easy-factor product produces a genuine ordinary successor of
114 bits from a 2,202-bit source, but does not compress the arbitrary core
before that factor was supplied.
