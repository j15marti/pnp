## 2. Exact marked-circuit adapter (all P3DM, even nonplanar 3DM)
Let A be 3n by m incidence columns over F2, t=all ones. Every binary solution s of As=t has a positive odd INTEGER occupancy at each element. In any one color, sum of these occupancies is |s|, hence |s|>=n. Equality iff each occupancy is exactly one in every color, since all colors have n elements and the same |s|. Thus exact cover iff the minimum Hamming-weight solution of As=t has weight n. If inconsistent, reject. No parity-only acceptance. With nonnegative integer weights w define C(A,t;w)=min_{I: XOR_{i in I} a_i=t} sum_{i in I} w_i, infinity if impossible. All initial weights 1; guards consisting of affine equations append rows to A,t and retain same argument. Arbitrary nonaffine guards are NOT free. Forced source selected triples remove their covered elements and competitors; XOR their columns into target, subtract their count from threshold, store original IDs. Zero-pins delete columns. Can retain original row coordinates including covered rows (now zero). Before each source-level reduction, after changes and at stagnation run exact-one empty/singleton/competitor propagation, three pair-projection matching tests including indexed opposite pins, and affine consistency/opposite-pin checks. Each failed relaxation is a valid rejection under its pin, not a complete solver. Saturate O(m) strict pins, polynomial work; audit implements these three checks. After abstract matroid contractions no longer apply source occupancy propagation to virtual columns as if they were triples; use rank consistency and exact objective only; retain original guard scope.
For t nonzero, augment A by marked element e with column t. Every feasible subset plus e is a binary cycle (zero-sum columns); remove any nonempty zero-sum subset not containing e. With nonnegative weights this never increases cost and reaches a circuit containing e (minimal dependent set). Thus C equals min cost of a circuit through e excluding e's cost. If t=0 empty is optimal; if t outside span A reject by Gaussian elimination. This is a marked syndrome minimum, not an assertion that an arbitrary minimum circuit contains e.

## 3. Regular augmented-matroid terminal
Theorem A. For any explicitly represented binary A,t and nonnegative integer weights, if the column matroid of [A|t] is regular, C and an actual optimizing subset are obtainable in uniform deterministic polynomial bit time.
Proof. Row-reduce over F2 to full-row-rank standard form [I|D], keeping column identities and position of e. Walter–Truemper arXiv:1202.4061v2 p2 (screenshot inspected) explicitly states that regularity of M(D) means D admits TU signing; their polynomial recognition and signing algorithm supplies the signed standard matrix U=[I|D_signed]. Row operations preserve binary dependencies and U mod2 is the standard form. Let U_A omit marked e and u_e its column. Solve the bounded rational linear program
min sum_i w_i(x_i^+ + x_i^-), U_A(x^+-x^-)=u_e, 0<=x_i^+,x_i^-<=1.
Matrix [U_A|-U_A] is TU (duplicate/sign columns), adding identity bounds and negated rows preserves TU. Integral RHS implies all vertices integral. Exact deterministic rational LP returns optimum; select a vertex by polynomial lexicographic tie breaking (integer vertex objectives; powers of two with O(m) additional bits) or successive optimization on faces. Any integral feasible vector gives a binary subset via (x^+-x^-) mod2; its cost <= LP objective. Conversely take a minimum-cost binary circuit through e as above, delete e to leave an independent set and extend to a column basis of U. The expression of u_e in this basis has zero outside the circuit minus e, and coefficients in {0,±1} by Cramer's rule (basis det ±1 and replacement minors 0,±1). Its signed support is exactly that circuit minus e, since dependence is minimal. Thus it is feasible for LP with identical cost. Both optimum inequalities give equality, and the modulo-two lift is exact. Handles zero weights too. No real-number oracle or randomized isolation. If ordinary incidence A is regular but [A|t] is not, this theorem is not applicable; adding a syndrome is not a safe regular-matroid extension. For an affine guard, test the newly augmented representation, do not assume regularity survives.
Independent signing implementation for small audit: use support bipartite graph of D and a spanning forest; gauge forest signs positive. Each chordless cycle of length 2k forces sign XOR k mod2 because its square submatrix has exactly two determinant terms and even determinant must be zero for TU. Split each fundamental cycle along chords recursively, add RHS parities, set the non-tree sign. If a TU signing exists these forced fundamental-cycle signs are its forest-gauged signing; then enumerate all square minors exactly to check (AUDIT ONLY). Production uses polynomial TU recognition/signing; no enumerative minors or LP bases in claimed algorithm.

## 4. Exact weighted seam replacement of arbitrary rank
Theorem B. Partition the nonmarked columns into L,R. Let W=span(A_L) intersect span(A_R,t), k=dim W. For every q in W let f(q)=C(A_L,q;w_L). These values are finite, nonnegative, f(0)=0; store one actual minimizing subset S_q for each. Replace all of L by one virtual column q of cost f(q) for EACH nonzero q in W. Keep R, original target t, and weights. Then C is EXACTLY unchanged, even if multiple virtual columns are selected or their witnesses overlap.
Proof. Any actual feasible subset splits into left and right; its left XOR q is in W, so one virtual q (or none if q=0) replaces left with no greater cost. Conversely any feasible contracted word selects some virtual directions Q. Substitute symmetric difference of the actual left witness subsets S_q, not union and not artificial affine-hull points. Its syndrome is XOR Q and nonnegative additive weight is at most sum_{q in Q}f(q), since duplicates cancel. Together with unchanged right subset this gives feasible original word of no greater cost. The two inequalities prove equality, and at an optimal contracted word the expanded cost is necessarily optimal. This proof also establishes subadditivity f(q+q')<=f(q)+f(q') but does not assume linearity or independent basis costs. Append affine guard rows BEFORE the split, so exact XOR lift preserves them. Nonlinear guards not covered. This terminal is a cost-preserving reduction, not equality of all feasible matching supports. In P3DM a final minimum equal to n implies exact integer occupancy; intermediate virtual words are not source witnesses.
Rank one: only one virtual column, one local optimization. Rank two: three directions a,b,a+b, all with actual independent weights; three local optimizations. Example L=(a,b,a+b), weights (2,2,1) gives costs (0,2,2,1), so replacing by independently weighted basis costs is false (would charge 4 for a+b). Unlike a generic four-state relation, all three nonzero states can be expressed as weighted columns with safe simultaneous use; XOR lift removes overlaps.

## 5. Polynomial discovery and aggregate factoring for rank-one seams
Apply to M=[A|t] with marked e. For partition S,E-S define connectivity lambda(S)=rank(S)+rank(E-S)-rank(E)=dim span intersection. It is symmetric submodular (rank and complementary rank each submodular). Deterministic submodular minimization (Iwata–Fleischer–Fujishige, JACM 2001, DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/502090.502096, arXiv:math/0004089; exact integer value-oracle theorem) on remaining free elements after pinning membership is polynomial; oracle is two Gaussian ranks. For nontrivial rank-zero separation pin one element each side and minimize; enumerate O(m^2) ordered pairs. If one exists, discard the side not containing e, since empty optimal there, and recurse. On a connected matroid, detect any proper rank-one seam with >=2 elements each side by pinning two on each side and minimizing, enumerating O(m^4) tuples. If no rank-one seam, stop. No guessing a partition or semantic oracle. Choose deterministic ordering of pinned tuples and returned minimizer; intersection vector obtained by linear elimination, nonzero unique. Matroid separations correspond to standard binary 1-/2-sums but proof here is direct linear algebra and objective transfer.
At rank-one split let e be in R part. Recursively solve L with q as its marked target; replace L by virtual q with that cost; solve right with original marked target. There are precisely two recursive calls on smaller augmented representations, one per side, NOT two calls to the right per seam state. Each child has |side|+1>=3 columns; potential |E|-2 is additive, so number of leaves and internal calls O(m), after discarding rank-zero components. Thus aggregate polynomial if every terminal leaf is either regular or of size <=c log N (fixed c, enumerate all actual binary subsets on this leaf). No need global regularity; may have arbitrarily many nonregular small leaves. If a nonregular leaf exceeds cap, return UNKNOWN, not NO. No claim that every P3DM admits this cap. A known regular whole instance may be admitted before splitting. Can run this solver on each q of a rank-two table.
Rank-two extension: once an acquired side L with |L|>=4 has all three local optimizations admitted by the preceding rank-one/regular/bounded-size solver, replace it by three virtual columns. Size strictly decreases. To acquire candidate rank-two seams, after handling rank-zero/rank-one cuts, enumerate fixed membership of four on each side (including e on the retained side or force separately), minimize lambda. Each qualifying minimizer has lambda<=2; on a 3-connected matroid it is exactly 2. Try these polynomially many candidates, test table admission with fixed cap and discard failed tries. A found productive seam contracts; repeat. This is a polynomial exact reduction pipeline on admitted inputs, with O(m) successful rank-two reductions, not universal recognition of every productive seam (a minimizer may choose a different side). Do NOT recursively call unrestricted rank-two solver three times on each left: that would need an additional recurrence argument. Rank-two table calls are to the independently polynomial-capped rank-one solver only.
Store virtual-column provenance as a length-m original-ID bitset; XOR already stored bitsets to build each new lift, so no exponential expansion of nested witness DAGs. Original costs nonnegative, maximum virtual cost <= sum current costs; rank-two step may multiply total cost by <=3, O(m) steps -> polynomial bit length; rank-one disjoint split weights bounded by analogous aggregate sums. Intermediate stored cost may overestimate its source lift cost; lift always <= cost, and final optimality ensures equality. All source variables, guard rows, ranks, candidate scans (including unsuccessful SFM and admissions), bounded local enumeration, TU signing, rational LP coefficients/bit costs, witness bitsets, canonicalization and reconstruction are charged. Polynomial peak storage under serial processing and fixed c; cumulative memory <=(T+1)S for same deterministic run. No actual bit-machine benchmark or optimized exponent claimed.

## 6. Obstruction to global regularity, genuine planar source
All eight binary triples on X,Y,Z each of size 2 are the eight triangular faces of a properly 3-colored octahedron. Draw triple vertices in faces and radial incidences: planar. Every triple extends to a perfect cover with its bitwise complement. Thus no actual indexed bit is forced; source singleton/all-three matching and affine pin closure cannot remove anything. Let a_000 be contracted in the binary column matroid. The other seven columns reduce to seven distinct nonzero vectors of a 3-dimensional space (the triples' columns are an affine 3-cube; differences span the three independent color-pair vectors). Hence the contraction is the Fano matroid F7, not regular. This already refutes universal regularity of A and [A|t], even after sound bit-forcing. This is general P3DM with degree four, NOT a counterexample to a restriction to degree 2/3. It is a small nonregular leaf, so does not refute the factored solver. All eight triples have exact complement witnesses, so all-solution-preserving bit pinning cannot remove them. Other terminals may solve it trivially.
Additional computed target-extension obstruction: n=3, T={000,001,011,101,111,012,022,210,212,220}; regular A by complete TU audit but nonregular [A|t], min weight 3. This is a subinstance of the planar octahedron face-sum core in P3CPS03. Its obstruction is to raw target augmentation, not post-closure regularity. Parity-consistent UNSAT example n=2 T={000,001,010,100}: all four columns XOR to all ones, min weight4>n=2, regular marked representation. Demonstrates necessity of threshold. Closure also rejects this easy NO; no claim of an independently hard NO solved.
