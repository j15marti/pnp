## Theorem A: complete obstruction to separate 2x2 edge gauges on the original incidence network
Over C let E=e0^3+e1^3 be each triple tensor, and W_d=sum over positions of the tensor with e1 in that position and e0 elsewhere be the exact-one tensor at a degree-d element. The sum over contracted binary edges equals #P3DM, preserving actual triple bits, no separate pairwise witness. Permit arbitrary invertible 2x2 matrices G_e on element legs and G_e^{-T} on triple legs; contraction invariant. Claim: assuming no isolated vertices, all transformed local tensors can have definite Hamming parity iff all elements have degree two. Necessity holds even if all displayed nonzero tensor entries are replaced by arbitrary nonzero complex weights. This is a restricted representational obstruction, not a complexity lower bound.
Proof: let Z=diag(1,-1). Parity of transformed tensor means original is an eigenvector with eigenvalue +/-1 of the tensor product of A_e=G_e^{-1} Z G_e at element legs, and B_e=A_e^T at triple legs (involutions, trace zero, eigenvalues +/-1). At E, flatten along the third leg. Image on first two legs spans e0⊗e0 and e1⊗e1. Its only rank-one lines are those two: diagonal 2x2 matrices rank one iff exactly one diagonal entry nonzero. B1⊗B2 must preserve that span and its two rank-one lines, hence B1,B2 are both diagonal or both antidiagonal, with consistent permutation. Equality of the two nonzero tensor coefficients after transformation forces B3 to follow the same permutation. If all diagonal, each trace-zero involution has opposite diagonal entries, so the two supported words acquire opposite eigenvalues (odd arity 3), impossible. Thus all B_e, hence all A_e, must be antidiagonal.
At W_d, d>=3, contract leg e by covector (alpha,beta). The remaining tensor is alpha times a weighted W_{d-1} plus beta times a nonzero multiple of e0^{⊗(d-1)}. For alpha!=0, flatten one of the remaining legs vs the rest: the row with index 1 is a nonzero multiple of the all-zero tensor, the row with index 0 has a nonzero weight-one component, hence rank two. For alpha=0 and beta!=0 it is decomposable. This unique rank-one contraction line span(e1*) must be preserved by right multiplication by A_e, since all other leg transformations are invertible and the tensor is an eigenvector. Thus A_e has lower-left entry zero, impossible for invertible antidiagonal. Degree one W_1=e1 likewise cannot be an eigenvector of an antidiagonal. Degree zero reject. This proves necessity. For unweighted all degree-two, H=[[1,1],[1,-1]] on all element legs and H^{-T}=H/2 on triple legs transforms W_2 to 2(e00-e11) and E to a scalar times the sum of all four even words. Both signatures satisfy matchgate identities (arity <=3); explicitly feasible by weighted planar gadgets. More directly, each degree-two element imposes complementary triple bits. Test bipartiteness of the graph of triple variables and lift the two-coloring; one choice per component; count 2^c if consistent. O(N) graph ops, polynomial bit costs. Sufficiency is asserted only unweighted; necessity allows arbitrary nonzero reweighting.
Falsifiers excluded: edge-specific rather than uniform basis, complex cancellations in weighted W, parity sign at a triple, support reweighting. Does not rule out blocking, larger bond dimensions, sums of Pfaffians, matchings after nonlocal transformations, or other decision algorithms. Recognition of raw parity possibility by scanning degrees; no attempted exponential basis search.

## Theorem B: color-closed small interfaces yield matching composition
For any set S of incidence vertices, give a crossing edge its element color c and sign + if triple inside S, element outside, and - otherwise. Let b_c be signed sum of chosen crossing bits of color c, and a_c the number of elements of color c inside S. Let k count selected triple vertices inside S. Summing local exact-one and equality equations gives b_c=k-a_c, for EACH color. If a color is absent from the boundary, k=a_c for that color, hence every signed color sum and the parity of the entire boundary are fixed. This is true for arbitrary degree and disconnected S, without assuming boundary assignments extend. Under an acquired exact support table, any such region with <=3 crossing edges is an even or odd parity relation and is realizable by a constant-size ordinary matching gadget. For even 3-port mask use ES03 construction: only 000 -> private leaves; otherwise add one internal vertex joined to each port complementary to an allowed pair, and add a single edge between ports of one allowed pair iff 000 allowed. Empty mask rejects. Lower arity pad with permanently zero dummy ports, or directly handle unary/binary masks. For odd parity twist one port using a fresh leaf as new port, i.e. toggle that port's removal bit; its old port is forced matched to the leaf when present. Gadget can be drawn with up to three ports on boundary in any cyclic order; only ordinary matching feasibility is needed, not a common Pfaffian signing for counts.
If the incidence vertices are partitioned into such regions with exact tables and one stored internal witness per allowed word, join ports across ORIGINAL crossing edges, and find ordinary perfect matching. If a wire is selected, both incident ports are covered externally, otherwise both remain for internal matchings. Exact equivalence and witness lift follow by restricting the global matching to each gadget, reading the word, retrieving its stored witness, and gluing the identical original wire bits. Conversely any P3DM solution selects the corresponding wire edges and local internal matchings. This handles arbitrarily many admitted regions without multiplicative branching. If region size <= c log N in number of triple vertices for fixed c, obtain tables by enumerating internal triple assignments and <=8 boundary words and checking all element/equality equations; otherwise table acquisition separately charged, not free. Also applies to any exact table from another certified terminal with provenance. Full partition existence and discovery remain OPEN. If a guard pins bits, use exact tables under that guard only, never export branch-specific facts unconditionally. False claim that all 3-port cuts are parity-fixed: single triple has all colors and support {000,111}. No count-preservation from the support gadgets.
