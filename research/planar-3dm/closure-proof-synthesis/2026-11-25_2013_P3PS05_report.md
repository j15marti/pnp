## 1. Four-terminal disk switching (self-contained)
Let a finite graph with real edge couplings J be embedded in a closed disk, four distinct boundary vertices 1,2,3,4 in cyclic order, all private vertices and non-boundary edges on the disk side. Boundary rim edges allowed. E(s)=sum_e J_e s_u s_v + C, no fields. f(s1,s2,s3,s4) is the exact minimum over private spins, invariant under simultaneous global flip. Write its unique Walsh expansion f=C0+sum_(i<j) J_ij s_i s_j+q s1s2s3s4. Denote A=J13, B=J24 and t=(+,-,+,-). For each boundary word s, consider minimum pair energy f(s)+f(s*t). It equals 2(C0 + A*s1*s3+B*s2*s4+q*s1*s2*s3*s4), because adjacent pair products cancel. Let P+ and P- be the minimum pair energies over product +1 and -1, respectively.
Take actual minimizing extensions u,v for any pair (s,s*t). Color each vertex by ratio u_i*v_i; boundary ratios alternate. If both same-ratio pairs of opposite boundary vertices were connected within their respective color classes, their paths would be vertex-disjoint and connect alternating boundary endpoints, impossible in a disk (Jordan separation). Hence one of the two ratio classes has its boundary vertices in distinct components. Globally flip v if needed so that this class is the disagreement class. This changes no energy and changes the second boundary word only by overall sign. Swap u,v on the entire disagreement component containing exactly one of the four boundary vertices. For an edge inside or outside that component the sum of two energies is unchanged. Across it, the other endpoint has agreement (otherwise it belongs to the same disagreement component), so again the pair energy sum is unchanged. The new pair still has alternating ratios up to overall flip; the four-spin product of each word changes sign. Thus P-<=P+ and, starting in opposite category, P+<=P-. Exact equality.
By evaluating the two choices of opposite pair products in each category:
P+=2(C0+q-|A+B|), P-=2(C0-q-|A-B|).
Therefore
  2q = |A+B|-|A-B|, equivalently q=sign(AB) min(|A|,|B|),
with q=0 if either diagonal is zero. This is an exact topological constraint, not a probabilistic identity or an assumption of ferromagnetism. It uses cofacial cyclic order; planarity of the abstract graph alone does NOT suffice. Counterexample: the two disjoint crossing diagonals in the prescribed disk order give A=B=1,q=0.

## 2. Complete constructive signed star-rim realization
Every globally flip-invariant function on four spins satisfying that identity is exactly the conditional minimum of one central spin plus a constant and four cyclic rim edges. Let h=min(|A|,|B|), a=|A|+h, b=|B|+h. Begin with star coupling strengths (a,b,a,b). Its minimized energy is g=-|a(s1+s3)+b(s2+s4)|. Direct averaging of eight relative boundary words shows opposite coefficients (-a+h,-b+h)=(-|A|,-|B|) and quartic h: if a>=b, J13=-a+b/2, J24=-b/2,q=b/2, and symmetrically otherwise. Here min(a,b)=2h. Gauge-flip the third boundary spin if A>0 and fourth if B>0 (zero cases arbitrary). This sets opposite coefficients exactly A,B, and the quartic is sign(AB)h, also correct if h=0. Use these signs on actual star edges, not on shared exterior variables. Set rim coupling differences J_12-g_12, J_23-g_23, J_34-g_34, J_14-g_14 and constant C0-g0. All eight table values now match by unique even Walsh expansion. This proves both necessity and sufficiency for the class of finite real conditional minimum tables from signed zero-field disks. No negative counting weights or cancellation of physical probabilities involved.
If q=0, at most one diagonal nonzero: can remove the center entirely, retain the four rim edges and at most one diagonal (drawn inside disk). If q nonzero, one center suffices irrespective of sign or magnitude, so prior PS04 quartic cancellation is unnecessary for local compression into a planar network with an auxiliary variable.
