## A. Exact polynomial recognition of ownerwise coordinateization
On an affine chart s=a+Dz, every at-most-one clause is a product of two affine false-literal forms set to zero. Normalize constant/unary products by Gaussian deduction, including cancellation of quadratic parts as in AB03. A remaining nonlinear owner has independent homogeneous rows u,v and dual plane P=span(u,v); it forbids exactly one functional on P. Its three nonzero directions are u,v,u+v. Deduplicate planes geometrically while retaining EVERY owner/sign. A global independent coordinate set can make every individual owner an ordinary binary clause exactly when it contains two nonzero directions of every plane (extend to a full basis). Necessity also for representing each single owner as a conjunction of ordinary binary clauses in those coordinates: every nontrivial clause falsification flat must be inside the owner's codimension-two forbidden flat, hence equal (units cannot be contained); their homogeneous planes coincide. This is OWNERWISE recognition, not recognition of every possible semantic reformulation of the conjunction.

Let F be all nonzero directions belonging to at least two DISTINCT planes. Every coordinateizing basis must contain F: two distinct coordinate planes sharing a line share a coordinate. Begin with F and add arbitrary nonzero directions of each plane until it contains at least two selected directions. Call the resulting set D. If a basis exists, no plane contains three forced directions, and each added direction is private to that plane; the number selected is |F|+sum_i(2-|F intersect P_i|), the same as the number of coordinates of any such basis lying in union of planes. D spans U=sum P_i. Thus a basis exists iff D is independent (rank=|D|); if independent, extend by Gaussian elimination to full dual space. Conversely if dependent, no ownerwise basis exists by the cardinality argument (three forced directions already imply dependence). All membership, deduplication, rank and basis expansion polynomial. With basis observations, each owner becomes one ordinary 2CNF clause by evaluating its forbidden two-bit pattern, with affine constants retained. SCC 2SAT gives both labels and a Gaussian source lift. No need for low quadratic rank or few live planes.

## B. Excess coordinate cover and exact deterministic target acquisition
Let G have one vertex for every distinct nonzero direction in the union of planes and a triangle on the three directions of each plane. A coordinate cover D chooses at least two directions per plane iff D is a vertex cover of G. Every cover spans U, rank r. Therefore the minimum possible excess is tau(G)-r; no promise of polynomial optimum discovery. An explicit acquired cover D of K directions gives exactly e=K-r independent homogeneous relations among their values, acquired by Gaussian elimination with provenance. Each owner becomes an ordinary binary clause using any two selected directions in its plane; its other direction is their XOR. Extra directions may be dependent, so impose ALL e relations. Conversely every cover-value word obeying the relations extends to a functional on the full chart, obtained by Gaussian elimination, and satisfies every original owner iff the binary clauses hold. If unconstrained directions are relevant for targets, append a basis complement (does not increase e). The cover graph is NOT assumed planar even though the source incidence is planar; equal coefficient directions may arise nonlocally. Discovery: first run exact zero-excess test; if it fails, try four deterministic maximal-independent-set orders on G (natural, reverse, degree ascending, degree descending) and use best complement. No optimality claim. Charge all four.

Exact terminal for fixed e, historical AB02 adapted: find a 2CNF model a (if none reject). Translate by a, so zero satisfies; every nontrivial clause is an implication or forbids a simultaneous pair of active bits (negative units are self-pairs). For each set S of at most e active variables, take its directed implication closure C(S); if it contains no forbidden pair, it is an actual 2CNF model after translating back. For a degree-h polynomial p in translated bits, monomial for support S is true on a model precisely when C(S) is contained in that model's support. If C(S) contains a forbidden pair, monomial identically zero; otherwise group monomials by distinct closures. Ordered by size, evaluations on these feasible closures give triangular containment matrix with diagonal ones. Hence closures of at most h active bits suffice to detect every nonzero degree-h polynomial on feasible 2CNF words. For e exact parity equations the product of their truth indicators has degree <=e, is one exactly on simultaneous parity success. Enumerating closures of size <=e detects whether it is ever one.
