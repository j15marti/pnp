## Theorem A: exact minimum-weight parity equivalence and cost compression
Let A be the original 3n-by-m GF(2) incidence matrix. Every word As=1 has odd positive occupancy at every element. For each of three colors the sum of integer occupancies equals |s|. Consequently |s|>=n and equality holds iff every occupancy equals 1. Thus source decision under any actual affine guards and sound deductions equals minimum Hamming weight n in the corresponding affine chart; if chart inconsistent reject. Extra affine deductions must retain every guarded source witness, not every parity word. Any parity word of weight n in chart is an actual source witness. No pairwise exclusion needs to be imposed separately in the optimization.

Gaussian parameterization s_i=a_i+ell_i(z). Group all nonzero homogeneous rows ell by exact equality. Let p_ell count offset 0, q_ell count offset 1. Its integer weight is q+(p-q)*ell(z). Constant rows contribute their offsets; balanced groups contribute a constant p and are discarded from the objective, not from witness reconstruction. For unbalanced groups use y_j=ell_j(z)+[p<q] mod2, w_j=|p-q|, C=sum constant offsets + sum min(p,q). Then |s|=C+sum w_j y_j. Form L with distinct retained rows; find basis of left kernel of L by Gaussian elimination, H L=0 and H has full row rank q-rank(L), where q=#retained forms. Exactly realizable y are Hy=H d (d_j=[p<q]), since ker H=im L. For any feasible y solve Lz=y+d and reconstruct all original bits; dropped balanced directions unrestricted as far as cost but retained in the chart. Handles arbitrary affine guards and duplicate/complementary forms. This is exact all-value objective projection, not a fractional or affine feasibility substitution for occupancy.

## Obstruction
Take n=2 and all eight tuples (cube bits). Its incidence graph is planar: element vertices are six octahedron vertices (opposite pairs form colors); triple vertices are eight triangular faces, connected to their corners. A's column matroid is rank4 affine cube AG(3,2): invertible row coordinate map columns to (1,x,y,z). Contract 000, remaining seven columns become the seven distinct nonzero vectors in GF(2)^3, F7, a nongraphic minor (a simple graph of rank3 has at most six edges). Thus planar incidence does not imply graphic A. For parity chart all eight selection bits have distinct, nonzero homogeneous forms: any constant or equality would mean a unit or pair vector in row span A, whose nonzero weights are 4 or 8. Hence no cost cancellation; H in Theorem A has row space A, so the compressed check matroid is also nongraphic on raw chart. All opposite complementary tuple pairs are exact covers. No universal graphical coverage; not a hardness or lower-bound result. Disjoint union gives arbitrarily many nongraphic blocks, but each can be solved by fixed local enumeration. This motivates blockwise rather than global admission. Does not show all stronger closure preserves this obstruction.
