## 3. Theorem A: paid actual cycle basis and embedding-independent sign acquisition on the planar promise

Let G be an indexed bipartite graph with a baseline perfect matching M0 (otherwise reject). Contract its matching edges, orient each nonbaseline indexed edge from its left vertex u to the left vertex v whose baseline edge has its right endpoint. Parallel alternative to a baseline edge is a directed loop. Every directed simple cycle of k arcs gives a genuine M0-alternating flip, changing k matched pairs; its full indexed difference vector c is the XOR of the k nonbaseline edges and k baseline edges. Every matching symmetric difference with M0 decomposes into such disjoint alternating cycles.

Compute SCCs or mutual directed reachability. In each SCC choose root r and paths P_v from r to v and Q_v from v to r. For every vertex take the closed walk P_v Q_v, and for each internal arc a:u->v take P_u a Q_v. Decompose these walks by repeated-vertex splitting into directed simple cycles. There are O(n+m) walks of length O(n), all acquired by ordinary graph searches. No cycles use arcs crossing SCCs.

**Span proof.** Suppose a binary arc functional vanishes on all collected simple cycles. It vanishes on the sampled closed walks. Writing phi(v) for its value on P_v, the first walks imply value(Q_v)=phi(v). The second imply value(a)=phi(u)+phi(v). This telescopes to zero on every directed cycle. Equality of annihilators proves the sampled arc cycle vectors span all directed cycle vectors. The mapping from arc vectors to full matching differences is linear (add the baseline edge for each selected outgoing arc at its tail); thus the resulting actual flip vectors span all M+M0. Retain a linearly independent subset of actual flip vectors, not artificial reduced rows as purported physical flips. The exact variation space of tags W is the image under the label map L of this span. For any matching M, its tag lies in a+W with a=L(M0); conversely W is the span of actual tag differences, not their attainable support.

Now assume G is planar. Kuperberg, *An exploration of the permanent-determinant method*, arXiv:math/9810091v2, Theorems 3 and 5, printed pp.3-4 (page screenshots checked), https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9810091, states the sign coherence of flat bipartite plane matrices and existence of a Pfaffian orientation on planar graphs. Each matched component has even vertex count; parallel edges may inherit a common sign from the simple planar projection, as their selection does not change permutation parity. Hence some indexed edge-sign vector s* exists for which all determinant matching monomials have the same sign.

For each retained actual cycle of k arcs impose c^T s = k-1 mod2. Necessity follows because its flip permutes k matched right endpoints cyclically and therefore changes permutation sign by (-1)^(k-1), including k=1 loops. The equations are consistent by existence of s*. Any Gaussian solution s differs from s* by a vector annihilating the full matching-difference space. Therefore it is also coherent on EVERY matching. Fix the common determinant sign from M0; do not take absolute values of independently character-weighted determinants. This is a polynomial sign-construction on authenticated planar input, not a general test for Pfaffian graphs from arbitrary basis equations. It avoids a separate rotation-dependent signing implementation and works with parallel indexed edges. No claim that the new construction is faster asymptotically than existing planar signing algorithms.

More general Pfaffian recognition is a distinct problem: Robertson, Seymour and Thomas, *Permanents, Pfaffian orientations, and even directed circuits*, Annals 150 (1999) 929-975, https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1999/150-3/p05, establishes a structural characterization and polynomial algorithms; we neither need nor implement its full recognition procedure.

## 4. Theorem B: exact conditional chart and two-sided quotient test

Let G have tags in F2^d, reference offset a, exact variation space W. An independent switch chart consists of vertex-disjoint blocks, each offering two actual internal perfect matching words (e.g. two parallel indexed edges or the two alternating patterns on a simple even cycle). Restrict G to F by allowing only the edges of the two patterns at those block vertices, leaving the external induced remainder unchanged. For the parallel/single-cycle constructions every F matching uses exactly one of the two patterns, and the other can replace it independently. Verify F has a perfect matching by an ordinary matching call; otherwise discard this chart. More general blocks must prove the same exact pattern property, or retain an explicit restricted baseline choice and alternate lift. Let h_i be XOR tag difference of the two patterns, H=span(h_i). H<=W since these are real flips in an actual F matching. Select independent h_i and retain actual source block patterns for reconstruction.

The support S_F of F satisfies S_F+H=S_F: starting with any actual F matching, flip the appropriate independent blocks. Crucially H need not be a period of the full support S_G. Let pi:V->V/H. For every target t:

* t outside a+W, or pi(t) not in pi(S_G): exact NO.
* pi(t) in pi(S_F): exact YES, because an actual F quotient witness has difference from t in H and independently changing its block patterns realizes t.
* pi(t) in pi(S_G) but not in pi(S_F): UNKNOWN, not NO. The imposed chart may miss a feasible target.

This is a two-sided certified terminal with an explicit unknown outcome. It does not require each unrestricted matching to have a switch, as in CC03/MF01 universal switch certificates. It can exploit one target-specific full-rank chart. If H=W and F is feasible, the whole support equals a+W, so a single anchor certifies the entire affine space. If pi(S_F)=pi(S_G), then H really is a period of S_G, and the chart gives actual lifts for every quotient signature. A polynomial collection of charts containing the same H (each certifies all H shifts) also suffices if their projected supports jointly cover pi(S_G). One may use a common subspace of several chart spans. Coverage of one target quotient requires only one chart, not all fibers.

**Acquisition.** Enumerate parallel indexed edge pairs, greedily choose independent directions on endpoint-disjoint bundles, then verify restricted matching feasibility. Failed candidate sets are discarded or retried under a polynomial attempt cap; no optimal chart discovery claim. Alternative: use actual alternating cycles from the polynomial rooted-walk list around M0, greedily select vertex-disjoint cycles with independent tag differences; M0 then extends the forced patterns automatically. This latter extension is theorem-level; audit implements parallel blocks. Enumerating all cycles up to any fixed length is another polynomial candidate list, but no unbounded cycle enumeration is needed. Other agents may supply exact local two-pattern blocks with certified provenance. All guards must remain encoded as tags or actual restrictions, not assumed compatible.

**Exact quotient acquisition and lift.** Let r=dim W and h=dim H, w=r-h. Gaussian elimination chooses a linear map rho:V->F2^w inducing an isomorphism on W/H (its values away from W can be extended linearly). After checking t in a+W, rho(L(M))=rho(t) iff pi(L(M))=pi(t). For any edge subgraph B of G and character c in F2^w, build n x n matrix with (x,y) entry sum over live indexed edges of (-1)^(s_e+c^T rho(l_e)). With fixed global sign sigma from M0,
N_B(t) = 2^(-w) sum_c (-1)^(c^T rho(t)+sigma) det(matrix(B,c)).
Here sigma is a bit; exact signed cancellation yields a nonnegative integer count. Keeping the original coherent signs valid under deletions is essential. Compute N_G and N_F; zero/nonzero gives the three cases. For w=0 ordinary perfect matching suffices. When N_F>0, delete edges one at a time whenever the same target count stays positive. At most m additional count queries produce one actual quotient-target matching. Solve its difference from t in the stored h_i basis and flip actual disjoint blocks, even if those alternative edges were temporarily deleted during self-reduction. They were present in the original chart F. Verify the final original indexed source word. The same signed determinant table (all characters, inverse Walsh transform) gives complete quotient support at 2^w poly(N) work, to compare all fibers if needed.

For input length N (including all tags), all parsing, closure, SCC/path discovery, basis/sign solving and polynomial chart attempts are polynomial bit work. Matrices have entries of magnitude <=m. Hadamard bounds determinant/minor bits by O(n log(n+m)); fraction-free elimination and character sums require only polynomial bit lengths (sum adds w bits), no real arithmetic or randomization. Single-target decision + lift costs 2^w poly(N) and polynomial peak space by streaming the characters; full quotient tables cost 2^w poly(N) space. Polynomial list of charts adds their costs, with common quotient when checking full cover. A fixed cap w<=c log_2 N yields a uniformly polynomial time partial solver, including failed acquisition and reconstruction. Source planarity verification costs polynomial; no free planarity or matching oracle. Cumulative memory on the same sequential execution <=(T+1)S. For a general input there is no proved logarithmic-w successful chart, no assumption that a positive unrestricted count implies an F witness, and no P=NP claim.

## 5. Theorem C: genuine connected cubic source with zero global period in XY, solved in XZ

Use P3SW03's circular ladder family, for even n>=6:
T={(i,i,i),(i,i+1,i),(i+1,i,i): i in Z_n}.
XY projection is a circular ladder: two n-cycles and n radial rungs; bipartition alternates around each cycle, opposite colors at a column. Put label z_i in square face i, incident to its two circumferential edge subdivisions and rung i. This constructs an incidence-planar connected P3DM instance, with n elements per color, exactly 3n distinct triples and every element degree 3. The three collections of triples of the respective displayed forms are each actual source matchings. Thus every triple occurs in one source witness and is omitted from another; no sound unconditional bit deletion or fixing is possible, even with a complete semantic bit test. This does not preclude acquiring other relational constraints.

In the XY lane, when at least one rung is used, each row segment between used rungs is a path, which has a perfect matching iff its length is even, and its matching is then unique. Both rows therefore use the same horizontal pair positions (dominoes). A horizontal pair has both edges labeled i, so cancels in binary tag sum; rung i contributes e_i. When no rung is used, horizontal perfect matchings on the two even cycles can use the same alternation (giving all-zero tag) or opposite alternations (giving all-one tag). Consequently the support S_XY is 1^n+F_n where F_n is the set of subsets of the n-cycle tileable by adjacent dominoes: empty/full sets included; each proper nonempty run must have even size. Every such tiling is realizable by the horizontal-pair and rung pattern. Selecting arbitrary subsets of a fixed collection of n/2 disjoint dominoes gives >=2^(n/2) distinct signatures. All tag differences have even parity; single-domino toggles e_i+e_(i+1) span the full even-parity hyperplane, so affine variation rank is exactly n-1.

**No nonzero period.** If P is a period of F_n, then P belongs to F_n because empty belongs. If P is proper and nonempty, a run of length >=4 cannot occur: toggling the adjacent pair in positions 2 and 3 of that run leaves its first vertex isolated, contradicting tileability. Therefore all runs would have length 2. Toggling the adjacent pair consisting of the last vertex of one such run and the next (absent) vertex leaves its first vertex isolated, again a contradiction. If P is the whole cycle, take tileable Q={0,1,3,4}. Its complement has the singleton run {2}, not tileable for n>=6. Hence P cannot be full either. Per(S_XY)={0}. This excludes ALL exact period absorption in that fixed lane, not just periods with bounded coalition size, no matter how discovered. It is a genuine planar cubic family surviving any source-level unconditional bit fixing, rather than only an abstract signature-table cycle. It does not exclude alternate lanes, nonperiod chart terminals, source contractions or arbitrary algorithms. It is an easy SAT family, not an unrestricted lower bound.

**Complementary lane.** In XZ, each diagonal pair (i,i) has two indexed edges with omitted-Y labels e_i and e_(i+1), and each shifted pair (i+1,i) has one edge. The diagonal bundles are vertex-disjoint, and forcing one indexed edge per bundle gives an actual perfect matching. Their n directions e_i+e_(i+1) span the even-parity hyperplane W (rank n-1). A greedy independent subset can force n-1 bundles, leaving the last possible pair still extendible. All perfect matching tags have even parity by fixed even cardinality; the independent chart attains all of that parity hyperplane. Thus h=r=n-1, w=0. The same source that defeats exact semantic saturation in XY is fully solved by a discovered independent XZ chart, without assuming a target-supplied matching. The ordinary rung witness alone shows this family's source SAT; the stronger statement is exact full XZ support and its polynomial acquisition. This proves the complementary grouping idea for a nontrivial infinite family, not universal lane coverage.

**Guarded firewall.** Append one guard bit to the tag of the shifted triple (1,0,0), require it selected, on n=6. The shift-only matching is a source witness satisfying the guard. In XZ the chosen diagonal chart spans h=5 of r=6 directions; w=1. The unrestricted quotient is attainable but that chart's quotient is absent. Exact terminal returns UNKNOWN, correctly not NO. Hence logarithmically small residual rank for a chart by itself does not prove universal completeness. Other charts or lanes may solve this fiber. Under such a guard, rerun source closure for production; this particular test evaluates conditional tagged matching without source-pin propagation to isolate the interface distinction.
