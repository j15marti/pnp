## 2. Exact DBM recognition theorem
Let C be one nonempty positive region over F variables, B finite integer span bound, S={1,...,k} its actual boundary. Define U_ij=max_{h satisfies C}(h_j-h_i) for i,j in S. All extrema are attained because normalized feasible words are finite. D(U)={z integer: z_j-z_i<=U_ij for all i,j}. Necessarily projection R is contained in D(U). U_ii=0 and U_ij<=U_ia+U_aj for every a by triangle inequality of actual differences.

Acquire all U in k local solver calls: normalize h_i=0 and start other heights at B. Descend violating lhs to rhs for difference or max constraints. Every feasible normalized vector is below the maintained vector. Reject on forced descent below -B or below 0 at fixed root; otherwise terminate after at most 2BF strict updates. The stable vector is the greatest actual feasible normalized word (not merely coordinatewise independent maxima), giving the whole row U_i. If infeasible, reject C. Each solve polynomial in B and region encoding; B=m is derived from explicit source, not exponential in encoded input.

Define anchor lower corner L^(i)_j=-U_ji. It lies in D(U): L^(i)_b-L^(i)_a=U_ai-U_bi<=U_ab by triangle inequality. In particular L^(i)_i=0. In k further pure solves pin each full boundary to L^(i), using difference equalities; obtain complete actual internal witness W^(i), or failure. If any fails, its corner lies in the pairwise difference hull but has no extension. Therefore the exact relation is not DBM representable (any exact DBM would equal its tightest hull). Conversely if all k pin queries succeed, for every z in D(U), take
  W(z)=componentwise max over i [ W^(i)+z_i ].
Each shifted word satisfies C; closure under componentwise max gives an actual feasible word. On boundary j, W(z)_j=max_i(z_i-U_ji)=z_j, since the inequalities imply z_i-U_ji<=z_j and equality occurs at i=j. Thus R=D(U) exactly. The lift uses only actual stored words, not an affine/convex relaxation.

Negative region: negate every height and reverse the difference inequalities; equivalently acquire U with k least normalized words, using column j to get U_ij, test upper corners H^(i)_j=U_ij, reconstruct min_i(W^(i)+z_i). Exact same proof by negation. k=0 one feasibility call, k=1 one normalized witness suffices; k=2 every nonempty projected relation passes via SW03 interval interpolation. For k>=3 this is a genuine recognition condition. It is both necessary and sufficient for DBM export from this given pure region and boundary, with <=2k local solves and k actual witness words. It does not decide arbitrary mixed-polarity region feasibility.

## 4. Counterexamples and limits
Abstract positive relation h0<=max(h1,h2), span<=1, normalized h0=0: (h1,h2)=(-1,0),(0,-1) feasible; (-1,-1) lies in all pairwise difference bounds but not relation. More strongly, odd cycle on k binary variables with common root h0=0 and 0<=h_i<=1. Positive group requires h_i OR h_j on each adjacent pair (0<=max(h_i-1,h_j-1)); negative group requires NOT both (0>=min(h_i,h_j)). Each group has the full binary-box difference hull (all ones/one-zero vs all zeros/one-one); propagating difference bounds stalls; mixed system requires alternating bits, UNSAT for odd k, SAT for even k. This is an abstract mixed potential obstruction, not a claim of an incidence-planar reduction. Ordinary 2-SAT handles it.

Genuine P3DM source counterfamily, even n>=2: X_i,Y_i paired, A_i=(i,i,i), B_i=(i,i,(i+1) mod n). The incidence graph is a necklace of 4-cycles (X_i,Y_i and A_i,B_i) with Z_i linking A_i to B_{i-1}; embed all Z in the common outer face of the projected digons. Choose alternating ordering of parallel A/B edges so A_i sign relative to outer alternates. Exactly two source matchings (all A or all B): label z_i gives x_i<=x_{i-1} for bit x_i indicating A_i, hence all bits equal. Every original tuple occurs in an actual source solution, so neither singleton nor all-three support closure (nor even complete unary support) can remove any. All A is reference. Normalized private digon heights of all A are zero; all B gives +1 at even i and -1 at odd i. Positive atoms on disjoint adjacent pairs reject the coordinatewise minimum of these words (both edges of one positive label selected); negative atoms reject their maximum. Thus neither whole positive nor whole negative projection on all faces is DBM (difference inequalities always max- and min-closed). Infinite family of fixed-chart failure, despite trivial SAT and degree two, no prime/alternate-embedding/lane barrier. In n=2 source T={000,001,111,110}, exact failed normalized corners were [0,0,-1] and [0,1,0]. Do not replace all wide interfaces by pairwise intervals. Next: seek signed height gauges, embedding changes, alternate lane choice and joint constraint regrouping; compare P3MF02 threshold DAG and P3AB01/FCG04 exact guarded interfaces. Need a universal paid progress theorem, not more isolated terminals.
