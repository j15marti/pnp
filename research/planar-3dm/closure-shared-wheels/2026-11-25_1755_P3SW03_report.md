## 2. Exact compiler (universal, polynomial)
Let m=|T|, with equal cardinalities n. Parse explicitly and verify incidence planarity (ordinary polynomial planarity algorithm with rotation system). For each disconnected incidence component, compare its counts of the three element colors; unequal counts reject; otherwise solve independently. Hence assume connected incidence. Initially, after deductions, and at stagnation apply singleton exact-one closure and all three pair-projection allowed-edge pruning. A pair projection is a multigraph retaining triple IDs, not just a simple graph. A projected perfect matching is necessary, not sufficient. Supported edges acquired by alternating-cycle SCCs, or slower forced-edge matching calls. At most m deletions and n selections; keep deleted IDs and certified pins.

Choose XY projection: delete Z vertices/spokes from a certified embedding, suppress each degree-two triple vertex to its own edge, orient all edges X->Y. Keep parallel edges and actual IDs. The old z and its incident spokes are in a single face of the resulting plane multigraph (which may be disconnected); every edge labeled z borders this face f(z). Acquire global face IDs by tracing original incidence face darts then merging across removed spokes, preserving nesting of disconnected projected components, not by independently guessing their outer faces. Suppression preserves face sides. One embedding suffices for this exact compiler. When deleting triples by closure retain the original embedding and enforce deleted edge bit 0; there is no need to recompute faces. A pin 1 is represented by equal lower and upper edge bounds 1. Source authenticates planarity, not just projected planarity.

Find a reference XY perfect matching M0 respecting current necessary deletions/pins; otherwise reject. Let s0_e be its 0/1 incidence vector. For any other XY matching s, the difference s-s0 is an integral circulation in oriented XY. On a plane graph every such circulation is a signed difference of face potentials: fix outer height 0, write s_e=s0_e+h_L(e)-h_R(e). Proof: integrate circulation across a dual spanning tree; the signed sum around each closed dual walk is the net primal divergence inside it, zero. Equivalently face-boundary vectors generate the integral cycle lattice. Bridges have identical faces and zero circulation. The dual (including global outer/nesting faces) is connected even if XY disconnected. Conversely any potential creates a circulation by telescoping around each primal vertex. Bounds 0<=s_e<=1 (or pinned equalities) then make exactly an XY matching: divergence at each X is 1, each Y -1, and all edge values binary. Thus integral potentials and matching edge words correspond bijectively after normalization. Each adjacent-face height difference has magnitude <=1, so every pair of face heights differs by <=m using a dual path. This is a derived universal bound, not a guessed restriction. O(m) faces and O(log(m+1)) bits per height.

For a label z, exact one is equivalent to at most one, globally, since an XY matching has n edges and there are n labels. For each pair of distinct active edges i,j of z, impose s_i+s_j<=1. If they share an X or Y endpoint it is redundant. If either is a bridge it has a constant bit; bit 0 imposes no condition, bit 1 forces the other to 0. Otherwise both edges border f=f(z), with other faces a,b. Write s_i=s0_i+eps_i(h_f-h_a), s_j=s0_j+eps_j(h_f-h_b), eps_i,eps_j in {+1,-1}. The exact pair forms:
* (+,-): h_b-h_a<=1-s0_i-s0_j (swap indices for (-,+)).
* (+,+): h_f<=max(h_a-s0_i,h_b-s0_j), because two binary indicators are not both 1 iff at least one is 0.
* (-,-): h_f>=min(h_a+s0_i,h_b+s0_j).
If a=b and signs equal, the pair reduces to a single difference inequality by dividing the sum bound by 2 and taking integer floor (negative-sign case reverse sides). Every original edge bound is a difference inequality. These relations are exactly equivalent to P3DM, not an affine relaxation. The two same-sign types are called positive and negative polarity. The edge orientation sign convention may globally reverse both. At most O(m^2) pair constraints, or O(m) in degree-2/3 restriction. All retained constants have O(log m) bits. P3AGE03 color-closed matching masks and FG03 actual syndrome groups remain alternative exact terminals; this construction avoids acquiring a full matching-syndrome table.

## 3. Uniform one-polarity terminal
Difference constraints h_v<=h_a+c and positive atoms h_v<=max(h_a+c,h_b+d) are closed under coordinatewise maximum and translation. Initialize h_v=m, except h_outer=0. Repeatedly lower any violating lhs to its current rhs; if a value would fall below -m or the fixed root below 0, reject. Inductively every normalized feasible solution stays <= the current vector, since each RHS is monotone; hence rejection is sound. Each successful integer update decreases the sum of heights by at least one; at most 2mF updates, where F=#faces. On a stable vector every atom holds, hence s is an actual source matching if no negative atoms remain. Negative-only case negate every h and reverse difference constraints. Scan all O(m^2) constraints per sweep: O(m F (m^2+1)) arithmetic operations and O(m^2+F) words, plus polynomial preprocessing, closure and verification. All intermediate integers have O(log(m+1)) bits. This is deterministic and polynomial, not a general mean-payoff-games theorem.

For mixed polarity, the one-sided relaxation can already give exact NO if infeasible or exact YES if its actual lifted XY witness passes all label constraints. Otherwise UNKNOWN. Let k be the number of labels owning genuine atoms of the minority sign (after redundant-pair simplifications, with the embedding fixed). Branch on one chosen triple per such label, remove all competing triples sharing any coordinate, and run certified closure after each branch. No need to guess arbitrary height vectors. Fixing all these labels removes all their pair constraints, so the other polarity is enough. All choices are exhaustive, both labels preserved, actual lift. Cost <=poly(N)*product over chosen labels of their original degrees (including internal failed branches via a polynomial factor); with bounded degree 3, <=3^k poly(N). Fixed k, or logarithmic k on bounded-degree instances, is polynomial. A cap returning UNKNOWN is not NO. At most polynomially many reference matchings recomputed per branch. Our audit dynamically chooses the smaller side; for a simple fixed-product bound freeze the chosen polarity at the root. For dynamic choice, k decreases after every branch; use m^k bound, or 3^k on degree-3 inputs.

## 4. Main new grouping theorem: exact two-face interval composition
Consider a tree decomposition of the face-constraint primal graph (make each atom's scope a clique), each atom assigned to a bag containing its full scope. Suppose every tree adhesion has size at most 2, and each bag's *assigned* nondifference atoms are all positive or all negative (or none). Difference constraints may occur everywhere. There may be arbitrarily many bags of either polarity and arbitrarily large bags. Then P3DM is deterministically polynomially decidable with actual source witness lift, provided the decomposition is polynomially acquired/represented.

Proof. All atoms are translation invariant. Root each bag at one parent interface face (arbitrary face for root/empty adhesion). Add local relative bounds |h_v-h_root|<=m for every vertex in the bag. These are globally necessary from the dual bound and are difference constraints. They do not remove any true global solution. In a given subtree, assume exact child summaries have been acquired. An empty interface reports feasible/infeasible; a one-face interface also reports feasible/infeasible because translating the entire subtree can realize any prescribed common height. For a two-face interface (u,v), the exact summary is an interval of integer differences d=h_v-h_u, not a full boundary configuration list. Proof by induction: child interval constraints l<=h_b-h_a<=r are two difference inequalities; thus each bag plus these summaries is translation invariant and max-closed or min-closed, according to its polarity. If it admits d1<d2, normalize both actual feasible bag words L,H by setting h_u=0. For any integer d in [d1,d2], in a max bag take componentwise max(L,H+d-d2); the u coordinate is 0 and v coordinate is d. In a min bag take min(H,L+d-d1); same conclusion. All relative bounds, pins and child interval constraints survive, since they are difference constraints, closed under both max/min and translation. This gives every intermediate difference with an actual word. Since children can translate to the required common heights and independently lift their prescribed differences, the summaries are exact for the entire subtree. No assumption that the child's solution set shares the parent's polarity. This proves the induction.

Acquisition: test each d=-m,...,m with the one-polarity algorithm and d fixed as two difference inequalities, each test polynomial. An empty result rejects; otherwise store only the extreme values and their actual bag words. For zero/one-face boundary keep one witness. At the root acquire one word. In reverse traversal interpolate the appropriate child endpoint words and translate by the actual shared height; running intersection guarantees consistency, and every original constraint is verified on reconstruction. Each bag has only two endpoint vectors, not O(m) retained tables and not exponential compositions. All translations during reconstruction have polynomial bit cost (even along a chain of polynomial length); after assembly original dual constraints imply global normalized differences <=m. Total work is additive sum of polynomial local work times O(m) tests per bag. Charge all input/discovery, copies, atom ownership, scans, failed d tests, endpoint storage, translations and final edge-bit verification; no branch aggregation across interfaces.

Uniform decomposition acquisition: build primal graph explicitly, take connected components/articulation blocks first. Within each 2-connected torso, enumerate vertex pairs whose removal disconnects it. Split along a found pair into two nonempty sides with the pair duplicated and completed by an artificial separator edge in both torsos. Child torsos remain 2-connected; paths using the other side can be rerouted across the artificial edge, or when one separator vertex is deleted all components still attach to the other. Original atom scopes are cliques, so each fits on one side, assigning a scope lying entirely in the separator once. Retain each previous adhesion as a clique edge and attach its neighbor to a descendant containing it; recursive joins form a running-intersection tree. Each 2-cut split preserves sum of (torso size - 2), both children have >=3 vertices, so O(F) such bags inside each original block; articulation blocks have total O(F) vertex incidences. Brute graph searches per split are polynomial; standard SPQR is an optional faster implementation, not required. Artificial torso edges guide acquisition only, never add original logical constraints. If every final bag has one polarity, apply the theorem. If a bag has both, its local closure or another exact terminal may decide; no guarantee. Generic decomposition acquisition proved at algorithm level, not executed in this audit (supplied-chain DP executed). A more general mixed-bag exact interval interface cannot be assumed: a mixed bag may export a noninterval set. Need prove interval or store actual support under a charged acquisition.

## 5. Source-authenticated obstruction to automatic coverage in a fixed lane
For even n>=4 take a circular ladder projection C_n square ring with two concentric cycles, n radial rungs. Bipartition by alternating column parity, opposite on inner/outer row. There are n vertices each color X,Y; each square face z_j gets one label for its two circumferential edges and one radial rung (the rung at column j). Exactly 3n edges/unique triples. Embed a label vertex in its square, place triple vertices on the edges and draw noncrossing spokes within that face. This is genuine P3DM, each element degree 3. All rungs give one exact source matching. Each XY edge is in a projection perfect matching (rungs, or alternating cycle edges); each XZ and YZ projection is an even cycle with each adjacent coordinate pair represented by actual triple IDs, so every edge is supported by a pairwise perfect matching. No singleton or pair support deletion. In each square the two circumferential edges have the same sign, opposite signs on alternating squares, and distinct other faces (inner and outer). The genuine same-sign pair has disjoint X,Y endpoints, yielding n/2 positive and n/2 negative atoms. The face-primal graph contains the cycle of square faces (rungs) plus two apex faces adjacent to every square (circumferential edges), hence is at least 3-connected for n>=4: removing <=2 vertices leaves it connected. Therefore its natural XY chart is a growing mixed-polarity prime with no one/two-face cut, even under singleton+all-three allowed-support closure. This refutes universal coverage by this fixed chart decomposition criterion. It is an easy SAT family, not a lower bound; our one-sided relaxation itself found source witnesses on all tested sizes. No assertion that all three lanes/all embeddings fail, or that stronger guards stall. For degree-3 labels, at most one genuine polarity per label, but not a constant bound on the number of labels of each polarity.
