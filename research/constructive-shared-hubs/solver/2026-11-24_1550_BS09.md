## 2. Model and exact quotient (BS09-A, PROVED)
Explicit affine chart G in F2^n, private bipartite resource region R. Owner i requires one distinct resource iff its affine failure event B_i holds. Resources may idle; adjacency N_i explicit; no external uses of the private matching or hidden tags/budgets. Outside factors observe registered affine rows W_out; their support need not be affine. Preserve actual owner IDs, adjacency, G and all outside observations.
Partition owner IDs into bundles C such that (i) N_i=N_C for every member and (ii) G intersect B_i intersect B_j is empty for every distinct i,j in C. Both are polynomially verifiable (neighborhood comparisons and Gaussian consistency). The virtual owner C has demand D_C=OR_{i in C} 1_{B_i}. Demand is Boolean on G; it need not be affine. For every z in G the demanded original owners and demanded bundles are in bijection, via the unique active member of each demanded bundle. They have precisely the same neighbor sets. Thus original matching feasibility iff bundle matching feasibility, pointwise, with exact two-way matching lift and identical selector compatibility. This does not preserve arbitrary private matching counts or quotas. If neighborhoods merely intersect, assigning the intersection is a sufficient condition only, not an exact quotient or sound UNSAT test.
A deterministic paid discovery algorithm can greedily pair equal-neighborhood owners with inconsistent simultaneous failures; all pair tests charged. Larger groups with pairwise conflicts are also sound and can be greedily acquired, but this implementation pairs only. No optimization over partitions is assumed.
Forced-information closure: disjointness gives |G intersect demand_C|=sum_i |G intersect B_i|. Equality to |G| means virtual owner always demanded. If the forced virtual owners have no saturating matching, reject G. This is stronger than testing forced individual owners and is necessary, not a heuristic. The original guard is retained for every conditional deduction. No global semantic closure claimed.

## 3. Exact universal fiber deletion (BS09-B, PROVED)
Let W span G rows plus every registered observation of all other live regions and factors (including all protected tags expressible as observations). Fix a nonempty exterior fiber Q inside G. For each B_i inconsistent with G take p_i=0; otherwise let p_i=2^(-[rank(W+row(B_i))-rank(W)]). Gaussian fiber counting gives Pr_Q(B_i) in {0,p_i}. The virtual demand probability is the sum of its mutually exclusive member probabilities, hence at most w_C=min(1,sum_{i in C} p_i).
Find a maximum-weight matchable subset M of virtual owners by transversal matroid greedy with augmenting paths (or equivalent unit-demand integral max-cost flow). This minimizes rho=sum_{C not in M} w_C for this fixed partition. If rho<1 then in EVERY nonempty exterior fiber Q there exists z satisfying the region. Proof: the probability of any uncovered virtual demand is at most rho; choose a point without such demand. Each matched bundle reserves one distinct resource which serves its unique active member if any. No independence assumption.
Deterministic reconstruction: since rho<1 each unmatched w_C<1, so the min(1,...) clipping is inactive for all unmatched groups. Thus C(Q)=|Q|-sum_{unmatched bundles C} sum_{i in C}|Q intersect B_i| is strictly positive, additive under a disjoint coordinate split, and computed by Gaussian elimination using O(n+log m)-bit integers. Select a positive-credit child until a word, then obtain actual matching and verify. Preserve W and G in each saved lift, reverse order of deletions as BS08. All other factors keep their actual observation values. Region deletion preserves existence, not model counts. Matched owners and bundle IDs are not exported as unconditional pins.
Try original singleton certificate first, then bundle certificate; failed certificates yield UNKNOWN, never UNSAT. Equality-neighborhood test is required for forced closure; no merge solely from summaries. For resource capacities, explicit unit slot replication after clipping to number of owners is a polynomial symbolic adapter; executed graph uses unit slots only.

## 4. Strict improvement and limits
For d>=2, take d selector bits x_i, 2d owners with failure events x_i=0 and x_i=1 for each i, and d private resources adjacent to every owner. W contains all d coordinate observations (one outside bad event fixing all bits is enough). Every selector has exactly d active owners. Static BS08 has p_i=1 and uncovered credit d, so fails; no private selector freedom. BS09 finds d complementary pairs, all forced with common complete adjacency, and matches all d virtual owners, rho=0. Deleting ONE resource makes every selector infeasible, which BS09 forced-bundle closure rejects at root; the original individual forced set is empty. This is a matching-resource deletion neighbor, not the DO01 single-owner relaxation pattern. Both labels, d=2,4,8,16,32,64, executed.
Nonaffine example on x,y,z: B_1=(x=0,y=0), B_2=(x=1,z=0), both adjacent to one resource. They are disjoint, their four-point union is not affine (XOR of four points nonzero). With W full, singleton credit=1 fails; bundle cert gives zero and every exterior word lifts unchanged. Eight executed words.
Falsifier to universal bundle progress (symbolic): three owners with B_1=(x=0), B_2=(y=0), B_3=(x+y=1), two resources adjacent to all, W full. Each pair of failure events intersects G, so no nontrivial mutually exclusive group exists. At every word demand count is 0 or 2, hence always feasible. Each p_i=1, rho=1, and no forced owner. This refutes completeness of the new test, even allowing arbitrary group size; capacity-two correlated bundles or a different terminal solve it. No lower bound on runtime. A strict threshold rho<1 is essential: at equality the credit need not be positive.
