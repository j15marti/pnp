## 2. Private-direction fusion (PROVED)
Let U_i be the rowspace of A_i. Find nonzero v in the common kernel of every owner except a set J of at most two, with A_i v nonzero for every i in J. Use a coordinate j with v_j=1 and unique chart x=z+t v, z_j=0, t in GF(2). Other owners are invariant along v. In every affected packet choose a row (a_i,b_i) with a_i v=1 and clear t from all its other rows by row addition. The remaining r_i-1 equations, denoted C_i(z), decide activity; if active, exactly the bit t=b_i+a_i z is forbidden. If one packet is affected, every surviving z lifts, so delete it. If two are affected, reject z exactly when both active and their banned t values differ:
C_1(z) AND C_2(z) AND (a_1+a_2)z=b_1+b_2+1.
This is ONE forbidden affine packet (possibly empty or contradictory). Both directions: if it holds both bits are forbidden; otherwise at least one bit satisfies both, and every other constraint is invariant. Lift by checking the two actual assignments. Projection keeps the actual quotient coordinates, not separate marginal observations. On a two-owner fiber this is BA14's beta=1 specialized to the rejection side, avoiding a union of acceptance pieces. Coordinate case recovers ordinary two-clause resolution or pure literal elimination; dense directions are allowed. Arithmetic support of rejection is the product of the three affine indicators, not independent signed channel tests. The proof does not require any counting or cancellation oracle.

## 3. Paid discovery and aggregate rank mass
For a candidate pair, compute K=intersection of kernels of all other rows by elimination. Test whether A_i|K and A_j|K are nonzero. If both, choose basis witnesses u,w respectively. If u hits j or w hits i use that witness; otherwise u+w hits both. For singleton use a single basis witness. Enumerate O(m^2) candidate sets and charge failed tests. Each successful fusion replaces ranks r_i+r_j by at most r_i+r_j-1, and each singleton removes positive rank. Rank-one closure removes a packet and substitution cannot increase remaining ranks. Hence rank mass M=sum_i rank(A_i) decreases by at least one per unary transformation; chart observation rank also decreases when a private direction is eliminated (unobserved free coordinates may be retained as zero placeholders). Number of packets never increases. With original encoded length N and n renumbered occurring variables, all intermediate dense rows, snapshots, provenance and Gaussian work have polynomial size in N; there are at most initial M unary steps, so one no-branch run is polynomial even without memoization. A conservative dense implementation is O((N+1)^8) bit operations for these steps including candidate scans, reconstruction and simple matching/low-rank count; not an optimized exponent. A branch on any nonzero observed form sets its actual bit, reduces total row rank by at least one, and is exact by the exhaustive disjunction. It may double the work. No universal child-sum contraction proved. Within the domain where closure/fusion/terminal dispatch completes without fallback, use mu=1+M: unary child credit drops by >=1, terminal paid separately.
