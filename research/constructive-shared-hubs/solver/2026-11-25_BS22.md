## Universal dependency and theorem
Use the existing direct 3-CNF-to-forbidden-affine-packet compiler. After paid guard substitution and rank-zero/rank-one closure, each live owner has an observation rowspace U_i in the dual of the free coordinate space. Let V=sum U_i, r=dim V. Take a basis B of V from actual owner rows, express every owner row in B, and union all basis indices appearing in any row of the same owner. The connected components give the unique finest partition of owners with direct-sum observation spans (ignore zero-rank already decided owners). Proof: each component uses only its disjoint subset of B, and every basis vector occurs in a component; any direct-sum owner partition partitions B accordingly since B consists of actual owner rows. Every row has its unique expansion in its own block, so the union rule never crosses any such partition. Conversely all discovered blocks are direct sums. Each legal assignment of linear functionals on these blocks glues to a functional on V and extends to the free source dual; solve the resulting equations with the actual guard. Decision is AND; witnesses are independently verified and lifted. This is structural, not semantic equality, and does not enumerate all owner subsets or truth tables. The underlying fundamental-circuit matroid decomposition is standard; the owner must-link and guarded aggregate use are the point here.

A consistent guard of rank k in V decreases observation rank by at least k. For each attainable guard value, run certified closure, factor and return an OR of the AND blocks, skipping proven contradictory values, with constant no-owner sides decided in the parent. All positive-rank children have ranks r_bj, sum_j r_bj <= r-k. Use occurrence credit r^c, c>=2 fixed. Admit a split only if sum_bj r_bj^c <= r^c-1. A sufficient condition is max r_bj <= alpha*r for fixed alpha<1 and 2^k alpha^(c-1)<=1, since sum_bj r_bj^c <= 2^k(alpha*r)^(c-1)(r-k)<r^c. For unguarded nontrivial factors, sum r_j^c<r^c. Fixed k can be searched on a polynomial list of independent guard candidates, not all possible affine directions. BS22 implementation searches single actual observation rows, c=2, and leaves of rank <=6. All unsuccessful guard trials are paid; no reserve resets or branch sharing assumed. Original-N polynomial bit cost per occurrence: at most original owner count, n-bit masks, at most n independent necessary rank-one additions with fresh scans, polynomial Gaussian basis, row membership, union-find, source lifts, and fixed leaf cap; at most n² occurrences by telescoping. The implementation can return OPEN; a universal guarantee of a nondeclining move remains unproved. Peak serial space polynomial and same-execution CM <= (T+1)S; no physical measurement. Witness gluing does not combine guard-incompatible facts. This is a concrete candidate for thm:aggregate but not its universal premise.

## All-sign counterexample to this exact dispatch
For a complete-pair signed Steiner NAE source, total observation rank R=n-1 and every owner partition has one side spanning R: the side graphs partition K_n, and the complement of a disconnected graph is connected. Thus root factors never split. For a candidate actual pair observation guard, precisely one owner is affected (each pair belongs to one owner). On either bit value that owner is either tautological or forces one additional independent pair equation inside its triangle. The resulting guard rank g is 1 or 2; all three nonzero vectors of that triangle's rank-two space are its own pair rows, disjoint from every other owner plane. Thus all other packets remain full-rank, local closure stops, neither side rejects, and exactly the affected owner is removed. For n>=7, remaining graph K_n minus three triangle edges is connected, hence remaining rank r=R-g. For ANY partition of remaining owners, add the three missing edges to one side; one of the resulting complementary graphs is connected. The original side therefore has rank >= R-3, and after guard restriction rank >= r-3. In a direct-sum partition, for every bipartition of components one side consequently has rank <=3. For r>9 there must be a component of rank >=r-3: if none exceeds 3, accumulate components until mass first exceeds 3 (at most 6), leaving >3; otherwise the complement of a component >3 must be <=3. Hence each of the two one-bit sides has a component of rank >= n-6. For n>=31, 2(n-6)^2>(n-1)^2. Every allowed split fails the squared-credit gate, regardless of signing. This is an exact counterexample to the specified actual-row, rank-one-closure dispatch on both labels, not to arbitrary affine guards, stronger deductions, high-exponent credit, spectral/direct terminals or arbitrary SAT. GD29's complete-pair cut result supplies a related but different all-sign table obstruction. BS21/DA22 already bypass examples. The proof is asymptotic, not extrapolated from small tests.
