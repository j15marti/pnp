## 2. Source and closure
Common z in GF(2)^n under affine guard G. Explicit private two-spoke rims, each with affine forbidden colors a_i(z),b_i(z) in GF(2)^2 and edge x_i+x_(i+1)!=s_i. Each rim projects to at most three bad affine events (BW07/08): common difference d!=0, a_i+a_(i+1)+s_i in span(d) at every site, and XOR s_i=(1+(L mod 2))d. For fixed d these are affine equations. Retain original rims for 4-color cycle DP. Additional protected hub nonzero pins translate to affine equations and zero pins to bad-event factors per BW08; that extended adapter is symbolic, not executed. Arbitrary 3-CNF maps to independent clause rims via a_0=0,a_j=2*(literal truth), b_i=a_i XOR 1, padded to odd length at least 3. Precisely when all literals false the rim rejects; otherwise it lifts. The checker also uses a second clause adapter: two owners, one resource adjacent to both, one owner always needs a resource, the other's need event is the conjunction of false literals. This projection equals the clause.
Each matching region has owners E, independent private resources, one unit per owner, each resource idle or serves one adjacent owner; an owner has exterior support iff it is NOT in its affine failure event B_v. B_v is the conjunction of negations of the supplied affine equality support alternatives. Empty conjunction means always needs private resource. Inconsistent conjunction means never needs one. Given z, a matching must saturate all failed owners. No hidden coupling between private regions; all common dependencies represented by the shared chart and retained factors. Explicit row identities retained. An integral flow with a unit edge at each owner is equivalent. For resource capacities, clip each at |E| and replicate its slots; polynomial even with binary encoded capacities in this unit-demand domain (symbolic extension).
Certified closure: Gaussian consistency; rank-zero bad event rejects, inconsistent bad event ignored; relative rank-one bad event forces complementary equation. Restart after each pin. For matching regions, owners whose failures are entailed by G must be matchable; otherwise reject. Reclose after each elimination/compilation and before branching. This is a scoped necessary deduction repertoire, not complete semantic or all peer closure. Branch/lift prefixes retain their guards.

## 3. BS08-A weighted Hall-fiber certificate (PROVED)
Fix one matching region R. Let W be the row span of G and every observation used by other remaining regions and factors. Including all exterior rows conservatively is polynomial and requires no minimum-interface oracle. Each nonempty external fiber fixes a consistent RHS for all W rows and G, and its direction is ker W. For owner v set p_v=0 if G intersect B_v is empty; otherwise
  r_v=rank(W+row(B_v))-rank(W), p_v=2^(-r_v).
Uniform conditional probability of B_v in any nonempty external fiber is either zero or p_v, since imposing B_v adds precisely r_v independent equations if consistent. No independence hypothesis.
A subset M of owners is matchable iff independent in the transversal matroid. Choose a maximum-weight independent subset for weights p_v (greedy by decreasing nonnegative weight with an augmenting-path independence oracle, or max-cost matching). Store an actual matching saturating it. Define rho=min_M sum_{v outside M}p_v.
If rho<1, for EVERY nonempty exterior fiber there exists a z in that fiber for which all owners outside M receive exterior support. Proof: union bound over failures of these owners gives probability at most rho<1. Owners in M may fail, but the fixed matching already serves them. Unneeded serving rows can idle. This is a sound universal projection of the region to true on the exterior fiber. It is not a claim about every z, not preservation of original model counts, and not a forced pin. Uniform weights are conservative; fiber-specific exact Gaussian probabilities can sometimes certify more.
Weights share denominator 2^n (O(n) bits). The greedy optimum follows from matroid basis exchange; alternatively use integral max-cost bipartite flow with weight on owner arcs. No enumeration of Hall hyperplanes, chart words or guard assignments is necessary. DR08 is the no-resources special case. Unlike BS07H's deficiency terminal, no bound on d, |W| or global dependency rank is needed when rho<1.

## 4. Deterministic reconstruction, repeated deletion and potential (PROVED)
For a requested nonempty fiber Q, keep integer credit C(Q)=|Q|-sum_{v outside M}|Q intersect B_v|>0. All counts by Gaussian elimination, O(n)-bit integers. For each successive free coordinate, C(Q)=C(Q_0)+C(Q_1), so choose a child with positive credit. At a singleton, positive credit implies no uncovered owner fails. Then compute actual matching/flow and verify. The checker also computes real rim paths and matching maps during verification; its persisted output is aggregate results, not all row IDs.
Delete a certified region only after closure and store G,W, original R and matching basis. The forward direction is trivial by removing a conjunct. For the reverse direction extend a reduced model inside its saved outside fiber, retaining every observation of the other active factors at the time of deletion. Restore in reverse order: later-deleted regions have already been reconstructed and their observations are included in the earlier saved W. Protected G is retained. A later deduction derived from the remaining factors remains logically satisfied; no new unrelated external observation may be silently introduced. Every deletion preserves satisfiability, not counts.
A polynomial scan over all remaining regions finds this specific certificate if one exists, using only rank and matching. Repeat after each deletion and closure. Potential mu=1+#active matching regions decreases by one on each deletion; at most original count many passes, with polynomial charge for failed scans and retained reverse maps. This is unary exact progress, not universal weighted child contraction.
At stagnation, compile any remaining region with d<=2 via BS07H hyperplanes: dual rank r*(S)=|S|-r(E)+r(E\\S); each hyperplane is closure of an independent size d-1 seed, each rejection event combines B_v for v outside it. d=0 no bad events. Count exactly by signed sum of fiber sizes. A fixed polynomial key cap aborts to UNKNOWN, then complete coordinate search with closure at each node. Positive count reconstructs via guarded conditional counts; if cap interrupts reconstruction fall back to search. Counts are only residual-chart counts after any existential deletion. Separate count audit on original event products does not delete regions. Finite complete recursion fixes an independent coordinate per split and verifies fixed words. ## 5. Scaling and sharp falsifiers (PROVED)
Let d resources be adjacent to all 2d owners. The first d owners have no exterior support (p=1). The remaining d have failure events [x_i=0 AND h_1=...=h_r=0], with r=ceil(log2 d)+1 private shared bits. Other factors observe all x_i and no h_j, even when x_i are not pinned. Greedy covers exactly the first d owners; rho=d/2^r<=1/2. The entire region deletes irrespective of exterior rank d and matching deficiency d. Raw dual matroid is uniform rank d and has binom(2d,d-1) hyperplanes, but this is an enumeration capacity, not a lower bound on distinct events or any solver. Reconstruction works for every exterior word, without changing it. This family is deliberately easy, not a DO01 hard core.
Strict inequality cannot be weakened generally: two owners without resources, failure events x=0 and x=1, rho=1, infeasible; identical x=0 failures have rho=1 and feasible. Even optimal static matching credit is not necessary: d owners fail at x=0, d at x=1, d universal resources. Every word leaves exactly d failed owners and admits a matching, but every static basis leaves d owners at weight 1/2, so rho=d/2>=1 for d>=2. Adaptive matching could solve this without choosing a fixed basis. This defeats the proposed universal static-certificate completeness, not P=NP. At the opposite end, deficiency-one clause gadgets with no private selector freedom on each clause in an arbitrary synchronized registry encode arbitrary 3-SAT (BS07H/BW08); no theorem that some region must pass this scan. Need adaptive matroid/budget compatibility or an independent terminal/aggregate progress.
