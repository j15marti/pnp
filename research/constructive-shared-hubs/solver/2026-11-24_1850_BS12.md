## A. Source and closure (PROVED)

Reduce each packet modulo G; inconsistent rejection drops, universal rejection contradicts, rank-one rejection forces its opposite equation. Same-rowspace packets with distinct syndromes merge: their union is the affine preimage of the two-element affine line in syndrome space, rank one lower. Identical packets deduplicate. ## B. Guard-uniform overlap certificate (PROVED)
Fix deleted owners D and a protected rowspace R on the original chart (including guard rows). For each actual exterior linear functional z on R, the fiber C_z is the affine set agreeing with z. For owner i let r_i=dim((R+U_i)/R), U_i=row M_i, p_i=2^(-r_i). Let E_i be the exact projected affine system on R: all linear combinations of packet equations whose homogeneous rows lie in R, with their actual right sides. Eliminating quotient rows while carrying original augmented rows computes E_i and r_i. Then A_i intersects C_z iff z satisfies E_i, in which case conditional mass is p_i. This follows by extending the linear functional to R+U_i; inconsistent augmented rows correctly reject.
For pair i,j compute E_ij analogously for the concatenated equations. Admit a certified pair only if E_i AND E_j is consistent and implies E_ij (Gaussian implication). This is equivalent to equality of the pair projection with the intersection of the nonempty individual projections. When both packets are active, their conditional intersection mass is exactly q_ij=2^(-dim((R+U_i+U_j)/R)); otherwise it is zero. All discovery is polynomial in explicit row sizes. No exterior enumeration.
Let T be any forest on owner IDs using certified pairs. For each word x the integer
  H_T(x)=sum_i 1_Ai(x) - sum_ij in T 1_Ai(x)1_Aj(x)
equals the number of components of the induced forest of violated owners (including isolated vertices). Hence H_T>=0 and H_T=0 iff all owners satisfied. For actual activity a_i(z)=1 iff E_i holds, the conditional expectation is exactly
  E[H_T|z]=sum_i p_i a_i - sum_ij in T q_ij a_i a_j.
A sufficient uniform certificate is
  B_T=max_{a in {0,1}^|D|} [sum_i p_i a_i - sum_ij in T q_ij a_i a_j] < 1.
Actual activity patterns are a subset, so maximizing over all patterns is conservative. Compute maximum by tree DP: D_i(0)=sum_child max(D_j(0),D_j(1)); D_i(1)=p_i+sum_child max(D_j(0),D_j(1)-q_ij). Sum root maxima. Use integers scaled by 2^n, O(n+log m) bits. Greedy maximum-weight spanning forest and degree-two capped forest are two efficiently discovered candidates; select smaller certified bound. No claim to minimize B_T over all forests. Empty forest recovers BA12. Disjoint pair matching is a special case.
When B_T<1 every compatible exterior fiber admits a model. Deterministic lift: fix z, and successively choose the half of the current affine fiber with lower conditional expectation (free coordinate gives equal halves). Exact numerator is sum of Gaussian intersection cardinalities minus the certified pair cardinalities on that actual fiber, so at most O(n m) Gaussian jobs for a forest. At singleton H_T is a nonnegative integer below one, hence zero. Pair projection need only be certified for the original exterior; during reconstruction recompute actual intersections, never reuse stale moments. Retain guard and all actual retained-owner observations, so replacement is existentially exact in BOTH directions. This is not original model-count preservation.
All-Ai fixed-fiber union bounds with pairwise tree corrections are classical (Hunter 1976); the contribution here is guarded uniform projection certification + worst-activity tree DP + exact deterministic source lift/controller. No novelty priority claim.

## C. Two firewalls and strict gain (PROVED, tested)
1. A={x=0}, B={x+z=0}, protected z. Both are individually active for every z but intersection occurs only for z=0. q=1/2 cannot be subtracted uniformly: z=1 covers the fiber. E_A,E_B are empty and E_AB forces z=0, so the pair is rejected.
2. Stronger: two private bits u,v and protected z. Center A0={z=0,u=v=0}. Four leaves A_ab={u=a*z,v=b*z}, a,b in GF2. Each relative p=1/4, all four center-leaf q=1/4 certified. All-active subtraction on the star gives 5/4-4/4=1/4, falsely certifying every exterior. At z=0 three of four private words are valid; at z=1 all four leaves partition the private cube, no extension. Worst-activity DP returns exactly 1. Thus even valid pair certificates do not authorize using a single all-active weight.
3. Strict extension of raw BA12: five distinct codimension-two linear packets on three private coordinates, whose kernels are five distinct one-dimensional lines, share only zero in each pair. Each p=1/4, q=1/8. Raw density=5/4 and all-owner independent representative rank <=3<5. A five-node path yields B_T=3/4 (degree at most 2 means activating another vertex never decreases objective). Two of eight private words satisfy. Replace private coordinates by private bits XOR arbitrary linear exterior functions; all five projections remain universal and pair moments unchanged, so certificate and reconstruction work with arbitrarily large protected rank. This beats raw density and full independent-representative saturation, not every BA12 hybrid or BS11 solver. On Fano-like 7 rank-two owners with pair mass 1/16, this forest method cannot give bound<1 from the all-active pattern: 7/4-6/16=11/8. Not a universal progress result.
