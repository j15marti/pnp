\section{Repeated projection, affine-cover blowup and a quadratic escape (BS17)}\label{sec:bs17}
The post-projection part of Obligation~\ref{obl:progress} is not implied by S23's span credit. We distinguish local exact elimination, the availability of another paid move, and the output cost of complete compatibility acquisition.

\begin{lemma}[Paid repeated coordinate projection]\label{lem:bs17-bucket}
For explicit Boolean table owners, let $x$ be an unpinned coordinate, $B$ the nonempty bucket of owners involving it, and $W$ the union of their other coordinates. If $|W|\le C\log_2(N+2)$ for fixed $C$, compute the table
\[
P(w)=\bigvee_{b\in\{0,1\}}\bigwedge_{f\in B}f(w,b).
\]
Replace $B$ by $P$, with one recorded extension bit for each accepted word. This is an exact single-child decision transformation, with strict credit for $\mu=1+\#\{\text{remaining coordinates}\}$. Under the same cap on every subsequent selected bucket, and polynomial-size input tables, the sequence including discovery, closure and reconstruction is polynomial.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The other owners do not use $x$, so existential quantification distributes over their conjunction with the bucket. The stored extension witnesses prove the reverse direction; replay in reverse elimination order and verify every original owner and guard. This remains valid under actual coordinate pins by absorbing them first; additional arbitrary affine guards must be included among owners or absorbed with a separate adapter, never dropped. Enumerate at most $(N+2)^C$ boundary words and two values per word. At most one table replaces a nonempty bucket, so owner count does not increase; the number of coordinates decreases strictly, even for a full projected table which is discarded. At most $n$ accepted elimination steps and $n$ scans of candidate coordinates per step suffice under minimum-boundary discovery. Local table-hull equations can be acquired by Gaussian elimination on registered accepted rows; a coordinate-only implementation uses just forced-coordinate closure and is incomplete. Reclose after deductions and before fallback. Table entries, extension maps, failed discovery and reconstruction have polynomial total size and bit costs; the same serial execution has $CM\le(T+1)S$. This is classical bounded-width bucket elimination; its cap is a precondition, not a theorem that the cap always holds. An incomplete capped fallback cannot count as a decided leaf.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact projection can force exponentially many affine packets]\label{lem:bs17-cover}
For $k\ge1$, let $q(x,y)=\sum_{i=1}^k x_i y_i$ over $\mathbb F_2$ with $2k$ exterior bits. There is an explicit $O(k\log(k+2))$-bit 3-CNF with private auxiliaries whose exact existential projection is $q=b$, for either bit $b$. Any equivalent conjunction of affine-packet avoidance predicates on only the exterior bits requires at least $2^{k-1}$ packets for $b=0$ and $2^{k-1}+1$ for $b=1$, even after an arbitrary invertible affine reparameterization.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Introduce private $u_i=x_iy_i$ with the three usual AND clauses, and private running XOR gates computing $\bigoplus_i u_i$ using four clauses per binary XOR; pin the output to $b$. Gate truth tables establish both directions and a unique gate extension. Every forbidden affine packet of any exact exterior-only avoidance representation is contained in the opposite level $q=1-b$. Write a nonempty such packet as $a+L$. For all $v,w\in L$ its second difference is
\[
q(a)+q(a+v)+q(a+w)+q(a+v+w)
=B(v,w)=v_x\cdot w_y+v_y\cdot w_x=0 .
\]
The bilinear form is nondegenerate on $\mathbb F_2^{2k}$ (test against each coordinate basis vector). Thus $L\subseteq L^\perp$, and $\dim L\le k$. Each forbidden packet covers at most $2^k$ exterior words. Counting $y$ for each $x$ gives level sizes $2^{2k-1}+2^{k-1}$ for zero and $2^{2k-1}-2^{k-1}$ for one. Covering the opposite level yields the claimed ceiling bounds. Invertible affine maps preserve affine packets and their cardinalities. Consequently neither flat explicit tables nor exterior-only packet expansion can be an always-polynomial output format for exact projection. The source remains compact and trivially decidable, so this is not a lower bound on SAT, on factored representations with auxiliaries, or on decision-only algorithms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Quadratic terminal under actual affine guards]\label{lem:bs17-quadratic}
One explicit Boolean quadratic equation $q(z)=b$ together with arbitrary explicit affine equations has a deterministic polynomial decision and witness algorithm. After Gaussian consistency and parametrization $z=a+\sum_{i=1}^d t_iv_i$, substitute to obtain a multilinear degree-at-most-two polynomial $p(t)=q(z)+b$. It is zero at some word iff it is zero at one of $0,e_i,e_i+e_j$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If none is a root, all these evaluations are one. The constant coefficient is then one, all linear coefficients vanish by the evaluations at $e_i$, and all quadratic coefficients vanish by those at $e_i+e_j$. Hence $p$ is identically one. A root found in these $1+d+\binom d2$ evaluations lifts by the actual Gaussian map and is verified. An explicit small truth table can be converted to algebraic normal form by its Boolean Möbius transform in $O(w2^w)$ bit operations, accepting the quadratic adapter only if all terms of degree greater than two vanish (for the indicator of rejection). Multiple simultaneous quadratic equations are not covered; even AND gate equations plus linear clauses can encode Boolean circuits. This is an escape from the preceding output-format barrier, not a universal quadratic solver.
\end{proof}
