\begin{lemma}[Paid parametric implication consumer]\label{lem:bs20-param}
Consider an explicit affine-packet chart on private bits $z$ and exterior bits $y$, with actual guard already absorbed or involving only $y$. Suppose each packet observes at most two private coordinate bits, with independent columns for those bits. Gaussian elimination gives its forbidden event as $E_i y=h_i$ and $z_j=f_{ij}(y)$ for the observed private coordinates, where $f_{ij}$ is affine. For each private coordinate choose one incident affine function $g_j$, and set $u_j=z_j+g_j(y)$. Let $r$ be the rank of the exterior linear forms in all $E_i$ and $f_{ij}+g_j$ (include remaining exterior guard forms, or restrict to their consistent affine domain first). The complete chart is decidable and a witness is recoverable in $2^r\operatorname{poly}(N)$ bit work and polynomial serial space for polynomial-sized explicit charts and supplied partition. The exact feasible exterior projection has a polynomial-size Boolean circuit, even for large $r$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On each attainable observation word, each owner is inactive or forbids one assignment of zero, one or two private bits. A zero-bit active owner rejects; others are unary/binary clauses. The actual private implication graph decides by mutually reachable complementary literals. For the compact circuit, encode each edge's presence as the conjunction of the owner's activation equations and normalized sign tests (OR over alternate owners), then compute reachability by the usual Boolean Floyd--Warshall recurrence and reject when complementary literals are mutually reachable or a zero-bit active owner exists. Its size is polynomial, and every edge predicate has its actual affine coefficients. Enumerate only a basis of the observation forms, lifting by Gaussian elimination to the actual $y$; each resulting 2-SAT witness lifts by $z_j=u_j+g_j(y)$ and the original source map. Charge rank computation, graph construction, reachability, failed jobs, basis and guard tests, consumer access and source verification. Polynomial-sized circuit alone does not imply inexpensive existential decision over $y$. Arbitrary guards coupling private bits are not covered by this statement.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Exact coordinate gauge rank and discovery]\label{lem:bs20-gauge}
For an unpinned signed NAE registry on distinct variables per owner, take exterior coordinates $Y$ meeting every owner so each owner has at most two private variables. Assume no additional predicates and remove unused variables. If the primal graph has $c$ connected components, the observation rank of the normalization in Lemma~\ref{lem:bs20-param} is exactly $|Y|-c$. For each owner choose an exterior anchor and use its signed value as the forbidden common value. For each private variable choose as gauge the value of one incident exterior anchor. All activation and normalized sign forms are differences of exterior coordinates. On a connected component an exterior set of size at most $h$ can be discovered or ruled out in $3^h\operatorname{poly}(N)$ bit work.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The difference graph on exterior coordinates has edges joining each owner's anchor to its other exterior coordinates, and joining a private variable's reference anchor to the anchors of all owners containing that variable. Every primal connected component maps onto exactly one connected exterior difference graph: each owner and each incidence through a shared private coordinate lies in one such component. Conversely every difference edge stays within its primal component. The binary edge difference rowspace of a connected graph on $t$ vertices has rank $t-1$; signs affect only constants. Fix one actual exterior coordinate to zero per primal component by independent global complement symmetry; this loses no SAT witness. For discovery, starting with empty $Y$, any owner with more than two private coordinates requires choosing at least one of any three of those coordinates. An owner of size at most two with no exterior also requires at least one of its coordinates. Branch on those at most three choices, stopping at budget $h$. Check all scopes at each node; the branching exhausts every eligible set of size at most $h$. Thus a logarithmic cap per independent component gives a uniform polynomial terminal including discovery. On any connected $d$-regular ternary registry, $|Y|\ge m/d=n/3$ by incidence counting, so every such coordinate partition has $r\ge n/3-1$. This falsifies a universal small raw gauge-rank shortcut, not general affine backdoors, nonlinear changes, or a runtime lower bound.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Conflict-path affine packet and exact coverage gate]\label{lem:bs20-learn}
For a failed exterior job in Lemma~\ref{lem:bs20-param}, retain two directed paths between a complementary literal pair, or the active zero-bit owner's equations. Collect the actual activation equations and normalized sign values of the owners supplying the path edges. Their conjunction is a consistent affine packet containing that job's exterior word and excluding no feasible exterior word. Such a packet is constructible and verifiable in polynomial time. A polynomial capped affine-flat complement consumer (BA20), with actual guard/version keys, may use these deductions to select an uncovered exterior word or certify that all words are excluded; the number of acquired certificates and complement pieces must both be separately capped and charged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On every word of the packet the same owner activations and private forbidden signs hold, so the same two paths or zero-bit contradiction hold. No global forced-information claim about private coordinates follows. The packet can be installed as an unconditional exterior exclusion within the chart guard. Reclose after each accepted deduction; rank-one forbidden packets give necessary opposite equations. The complement of an affine packet in a current affine flat is an explicit disjoint union of at most the number of independent new equations, by first violated equation, so exact subtraction and reconstruction are polynomial per bounded piece. A supplied piece cap and iteration cap give a polynomial decline-capable procedure only; neither complete coverage nor continued progress follows. Circuit reachability, exact packet verification and reconstruction do not require a semantic-equivalence oracle.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Opposite quadratic-chain obstruction to separate conflict coverage]\label{lem:bs20-chain}
For every $k\ge1$, an explicit $O(k)$-owner affine chart has two disjoint private implication components with common exterior $(x,y)\in\mathbb F_2^{2k}$. Each component's fixed-exterior feasibility is 2-SAT. Their conjunction is UNSAT, but every certificate consisting only of an explicit cover by affine packets each certified by one original component needs at least $2^k+1$ packets. A one-endpoint change gives a SAT neighbor. Joint quadratic cancellation decides this constructed family in polynomial time.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In each component impose private endpoints $z_0=0,z_k=b$ and transitions $z_i+z_{i-1}=x_i y_i$. For each of the four exterior input pairs, activate the two binary forbidden assignments of consecutive private states that violate the transition. These are explicit affine packets of rank at most four; the endpoint packets are unary. The conditional implication graph is a chain of parity equalities or disequalities with endpoint pins. Thus the feasible exterior set is precisely $q(x,y)=b$ where $q=\sum_i x_i y_i$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Choose $b=0,1$ in the two components. For any exterior word exactly one component fails. A packet certified by only one component must lie in its failing quadratic level. By the BS17 polarization argument any affine flat contained in either level has dimension at most $k$: its direction is totally isotropic for the nondegenerate symplectic polar form. The levels have sizes $2^{2k-1}\pm2^{k-1}$, so their separate covers need at least $2^{k-1}+1$ and $2^{k-1}$ packets. This includes adaptive implication-path certificates, but not arbitrary cross-component certificate combinations. Changing one endpoint to make both target bits zero admits the all-zero witness. In contrast, sum the transition identities in each component over $\mathbb F_2$, cancel the internal state bits and then subtract the two common quadratic expressions to obtain $0=1$. All identities follow by checking each constant-size local relation; in the supplied chain registry this is linear many polynomial-size operations. This is a constructive nonresolution escape, not an unrestricted lower bound. General acquisition of cross-component quadratic cancellation or another polynomial joint consumer remains OPEN.
\end{proof}
