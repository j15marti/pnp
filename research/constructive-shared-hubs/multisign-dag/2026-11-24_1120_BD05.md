## 2. Character quotient theorem (PROVED)
For every character chi in GF(2)^q define sparse signed function f_i^chi(t)=sum of w*(-1)^(chi dot g) over rows at t. Let F_chi=f_1^chi * ... * f_m^chi, convolution on V. Exactly
Z(b,h)=2^(-q) sum_chi (-1)^(chi dot h) F_chi(b).
This follows by character orthogonality on the joint guard, so no independent guard marginal replacement occurs. If one f_i^chi is identically zero the entire channel is zero, not necessarily the source decision.

For any nonzero f_i define signed period pairs (v,e) with f_i(t+v)=(-1)^e f_i(t) for all t. The period directions form a subspace S_i and e_i is a unique linear functional on it, by composition and nonzero support. Discover ALL pairs by choosing a nonzero anchor a: v=a+x with x in the nonzero support and f_i(x)=+/-f_i(a); check translated membership and signed equality at every nonzero entry. Since translation is bijective, this suffices including zeros outside the support. Quadratic candidate-entry work in explicit support, with charged integer and tag bit work.

Convolution inherits every attributed signed period: translate the corresponding summation variable. Let H=sum_i S_i. Gaussian elimination on signed generators either detects a relation with XOR direction zero and XOR sign one, or supplies a consistent linear functional ell:H->GF(2). In the first case F_chi=-F_chi, hence F_chi=0 over integers. In the second extend ell linearly to lambda:V->GF(2) (echelon reduction provides deterministic extension). Let ftilde_i(t)=(-1)^lambda(t) f_i(t). Then their convolution Ftilde is invariant under H, and Ftilde(b)=(-1)^lambda(b) F_chi(b).
For the quotient map pi:V->V/H define Q_i(u)=sum_{pi(t)=u} ftilde_i(t). Projection commutes with convolution by grouping all tuples. Invariance gives
   (Q_1*...*Q_m)(pi(b))=|H| Ftilde(b).
Therefore the exact channel count is
   F_chi(b)=(-1)^lambda(b) (Q_1*...*Q_m)(pi(b))/2^dim H.
All divisions are exact. Use signed addition/multiplication, not OR/min or absolute value. Each chi has its own period basis, twist, quotient and target. Integer signs from GD11 or weights from BW05 can be handled only after source semantics have been verified; no independent physical integration executed.
