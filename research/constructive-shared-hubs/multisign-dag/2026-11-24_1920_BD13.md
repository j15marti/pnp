## B. Witness-span theorem (PROVED)
For key lambda on W_S define X_S(lambda)={f_S(x): actual words on V_S restricting to lambda}. Store a basis of span_Q X_S(lambda) selected FROM X_S(lambda), together with one actual observation word per basis vector. Zero vectors need not be stored. At leaf construct the basis by Section C. At a join enumerate compatible child-key pairs and all pairs of retained actual basis vectors a,b; calculate B_S(a,b), glue their actual words, and retain the vector only if independent of the current parent basis. This retains actual words and actual vector evaluations, never fictional signed combinations as witnesses. Every genuine output is bilinear combination of child basis outputs because child spans are exact and each compatible pair of words glues. Conversely every retained output is genuine. Induction proves exact span at every node. At root W=0, there is a nonzero readout iff some retained basis vector has nonzero readout. Its stored word extends to the ambient chart; reconstruct and verify original source. No global exact count is required.

Consumer-completeness: for a fixed compatible exterior word, every multilinear tree context above S induces a linear map in its single occurrence of f_S. Annihilation of the basis is equivalent to annihilation of all actual vectors in X_S(lambda). Therefore this summary is sufficient for existential nonzero support in every such context. It is not equality of residual supports under arbitrary singleton guards (DR13). Equal spans {1} and {0,1} cannot distinguish zero-existence. Multiple uses of the same subtree in a nonlinear context require an authenticated joint tensor/feature map or explicit occurrence copies with actual correlations retained; no arbitrary DAG unfolding claim. E.g. for shared x, separately positive x and 1-x never have jointly positive product; independent use of their scalar spans at an empty key would be false. Their actual W contains x and the theorem rejects both incompatible choices. Signs are retained throughout the bilinear map.

## C. Acquire actual leaf basis without local word enumeration (PROVED and executed)
For any affine fiber C consistent with a leaf key, form Q_C=sum_{x in C} f_i(x) f_i(x)^T (sum over ambient words, or local words times a common positive multiplicity). Each entry expands into pairwise signed packet intersections; Gaussian elimination supplies exact powers of two or zero. This costs O(L_i^2) affine counts for L_i total signed terms. Because Q_C is PSD, kernel Q_C equals the vectors orthogonal to every attained f_i(x); hence range Q_C=span X_C over Q. Given a retained actual basis with span H, rank([H,Q_C])>dim H iff there exists an actual word whose vector is outside H. Repeatedly restrict one chart coordinate to 0 if that test succeeds on the restricted fiber, otherwise to 1. The union of the two halves preserves the existence invariant, so after <=d tests a genuine word outside H is obtained. Repeat at most D_i times; empty/zero-only fiber gives empty basis. Each probe has its actual guard; these witness-selection restrictions are not unconditional consequences. Use exact Gaussian/linear algebra, no sampling, no full global count or semantic equivalence oracle. High local rank alone is not a bottleneck.

A scalar leaf with f=sum_{j=0}^{d-1} 2^j x_j has 2^d attained values but span dimension one. Its nonzero support can be contracted with other stages by a product, without GD19 finite-alphabet recognition. For mixed stage predicates, use GD19 only for zero tests where its fixed-degree certificate is paid; nonzero tests can remain signed arithmetic factors (product nonzero iff every factor nonzero). In contrast, a scalar f equal to number of violated CNF clauses has range in {0,...,m}, and deciding whether zero occurs is arbitrary SAT even though scalar span rank is at most one. This falsifies universal zero-existence inference from span; no unrestricted hardness conclusion.
