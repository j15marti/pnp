## B. Character lift theorem (PROVED)

The constant leaf must retain observation metadata even though its original packet function is constant. This covers unobserved coordinates and yields total observation space GF(2)^d.
Find, by linear solving, a decomposition a=sum_i a_i with each a_i in V_i. Let chi_i(x)=(-1)^(a_i·x). Replace leaf by g_i(x)=(f_i(x),chi_i(x) f_i(x)).   B'_S((u,u'),(v,v'))=(B_S(u,v),B_S(u',v')).
