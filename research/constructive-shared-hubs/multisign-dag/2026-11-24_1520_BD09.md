## A. Exact signed packet convolution (PROVED)

Each authenticated local factor f_i is an explicitly encoded signed integer sum of affine indicators, nonnegative pointwise for decision. Run certified closure before computation, restart after every forced deduction, and at stagnation before any choice: impossible/duplicate bad events, universal rejection, relative codimension-one bad event forces its complement. For explicit nonnegative local factors one can also certify an affine pin via exact local signed fiber mass zero; this is polynomial in explicit term list, not global semantic closure. Conditional deductions stay under their guard.

Use normalized Walsh coefficients fhat(w)=2^(-d) sum_z f(z)(-1)^(w dot z). A term 1_{Az=b} with rank r and solution a transforms to 2^(-r) 1_{row A}(w)(-1)^(a dot w). A packet is c 1_{a+U}(w)(-1)^(p dot w), rational dyadic c, explicit affine coset and phase p. Canonicalize U in RREF, reduce a modulo U, replace p by the canonical representative of its restriction to U (pivot-supported vector), absorb the constant phase difference at a into c. Thus syntactically identical normalized coset/phase keys can add signed weights and drop zero. Distinct keys may still represent dependent functions; this is not complete semantic minimization.

Given two packets A=(a+U,p,c), B=(b+V,q,e), convolution at w sums over x+y=w. It is zero unless w in a+b+U+V. The fiber is a translate of {(k,k): k in K=U intersect V}. If (p+q) is nontrivial on K, every such fiber cancels exactly. Otherwise find h with h|U=p|U and h|V=q|V by linear elimination. For w in output coset the convolution is
    c e 2^dim K (-1)^(p dot a + q dot b + h dot (a+b)) (-1)^(h dot w).
Proof: substitute x=a+u,y=b+v; the phase depends only on u+v iff the kernel is annihilated. The output is ONE phased affine packet, regardless of the number of frequency words or fiber size. Restrict to any affine output set by Gaussian intersection and recanonicalize. Every operation polynomial in encoded dimensions and coefficient bit lengths; no raw coset expansion.
