## Exact consumer (proved)
For n bits, m signed NAE3 owners, choose L=2^(n+1). For each owner, give each of its three unordered literal pairs weight L when its two signed literal bits differ, and 1 otherwise. The product is L^2 for a satisfying owner and 1 for an unsatisfied owner. Merge parallel pair factors by multiplying their two values. Retain any remaining exact Boolean guards/wide consumers H. The weighted sum Z over all actual assignments has nonnegative base-L digits at even exponents; each digit is at most 2^n, so no carry. The coefficient/digit at 2m counts satisfying assignments including H. No model-count promise on source with nonunique lift.

Choose an acquired planar pair subgraph P. All nonplanar factors and H form a correction C(Rx), where R has independent rows spanning every removed pair observation and all supplied linear observation forms through which H factors. H can be an arbitrary polynomial-size Boolean circuit on those coordinates (including a wide NAE via all relative differences). This is an authenticated syntactic factorization, not a semantic equivalence query. Gaussian elimination supplies coordinates. With r=rank(R), compute all 2^r values of C, then unnormalized integer Walsh coefficients c_lambda. Exact identity:
2^r Z = sum_lambda c_lambda sum_x (-1)^(lambda R x) prod_{e in P} w_e(x_u xor x_v).
This coalesces all sign modes with the same actual vertex syndrome, rather than paying 2^(number of deleted factors). It retains all guard correlations, including dense affine ones. Exact evaluation is 2^r poly(N); rank threshold c log2(N+2) for fixed c and explicit uniform original-N polynomial-size descriptions. Greedy edge insertion and planarity checks give one deterministic acquisition rule; accept only after actual rank check, not optimization of the modulator for free.

For a mode with vertex syndrome T, write A_e=w_e(0)+w_e(1), B_e=w_e(0)-w_e(1). High-temperature expansion gives 2^(n-|E(P)|) times sum over edge subsets S with odd-degree boundary T of prod_{e notin S} A_e prod_{e in S} B_e. Factors B_e=0 require e absent; multiply their A_e and remove them. If T has odd cardinality in a remaining connected component, sum is zero. Otherwise acquire a T-join J by pairing marked vertices and XORing paths. S=J xor C' for even subgraphs C', so swap A_e and B_e on J and evaluate an even-subgraph weighted polynomial. Nonzero A and B after zero filtering/swaps. This handles arbitrary sign cancellation; absolute Pfaffian would be wrong.

Explicit Fisher-type matching: split any degree d>3 planar vertex into a planar tree of d-2 ternary parity nodes with auxiliary edges of weights (1,1); cyclic port order preserved, auxiliary parity choices unique for any even assignment of external edges. Replace each degree 1,2,3 node by a clique of its incident ports. A perfect matching selects an external link exactly when that edge is absent in the even subset. Unmatched local ports are even in number, and have a unique internal matching (0 or2). Thus bijection; weight external link A_e/B_e, internal links 1, multiply by prod B_e. Kasteleyn orientation is acquired by leaf-to-root dual-face tree; all perfect-matching terms have identical permutation/orientation sign independent of weight signs. Fix that sign by the explicit all-external perfect matching, not abs(Pfaffian). Exact rational Pfaffian elimination; polynomial bit length using minor bounds/fraction-free variants. Count every matrix entry and mode; signed numerator magnitude is bounded by polynomial-bit powers of L and 2^n,r, graph dimension polynomial. References: Gobron arXiv:1312.7289, Kasteleyn weighted matching theorem (see Kenyon, The geometry of dimer models, https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/wbln.3.pdf); Barahona 1982 planar zero-field vs fields distinction (J Phys A 15 3241). No external theorem proving universal tractability.

## Universal candidate falsified
For linear 4-regular 3-uniform NAE3, pair graph simple 8-regular, |E|=4n. Every planar subgraph omits at least n+6 edges (Euler bound for n>=3). Let D be omitted graph, r_D=rank of its binary incidence observation rows. A component with s>=2 vertices contributes s-1 rank, so number of nonisolated vertices <=2r_D; max degree 8 gives |E(D)|<=8r_D. Thus r>=r_D>=(n+6)/8 for every choice of raw planar core, invariant under invertible change of coordinates for that fixed selection. Infinite connected SAT controls: for odd q>=7 use parts x_i,y_i,z_i in Z_q and triples (x_i,y_(i+p),z_(i+2p)), p=0,1,2,3. Linearity and degree4 by modular differences, connected by shifts, explicit part-color witness. This falsifies universal raw logarithmic correction rank even with optimal planar selection; not a hardness result, not a simultaneous portfolio obstruction. Nonlocal normalization can change the graph and escape.
