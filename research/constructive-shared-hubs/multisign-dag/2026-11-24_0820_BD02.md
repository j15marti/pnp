## 2. Exact four-port Walsh algebra (PROVED)
For any finite explicit factor f on at most four DISTINCT Boolean ports, with nonnegative integer costs and complement symmetry f(x)=f(1-x), use the common scale 16. Write chi_S(x)=(-1)^(XOR_{i in S} x_i). Its integer coefficient is J_S=(16/2^|scope|) sum_x f(x)chi_S(x). Odd |S| vanish by complement symmetry. Thus 16 f is exactly constant + pair characters + at most one four-port character. Sum coefficients on identical original variable subsets, including constants. Cancel zeros only after exact addition, keeping source factors and witness-lift records. This extends BD01 without treating nonaffine four-port messages as pairwise; a sole even parity violation has a nonzero quartic coefficient, so that old extension is false. No cancellation of feasibility sets or signed counts is inferred from unrelated terms.

Example: f(x)=min(number of ones, number of zeros), the exact cost of a private central spin connected to four ports by unit equality penalties. Its scaled quartic coefficient is +4. Flip the sign of one spoke: f'(x)=f(x XOR 0001), coefficient -4. The sum cancels the quartic exactly (and pair terms incident to the flipped port). Minimum f+f'=1, even though both individual minima are zero. Same-sign copies have nonzero quartic and minimum zero. Independent private centers are genuinely independent conditional on the complete ordered four-bit boundary; synchronized source copies must instead share their variables/constraints. Cancellation is pointwise equality of aggregate energy, not cancellation of logical constraints.

## 3. Planar-eligible guard hitting, factor-three rank approximation (PROVED)
Let a supplied polynomial-size simple planar host G on the patch variables contain every nonzero aggregate pair term and every edge used for relative boundary pins. Planarity is testable. For a surviving four-set S, define E_S to contain each pair e subset S such that both e and S minus e are edges of G. Reject this terminal (UNKNOWN) if E_S is empty. Treat e as the GF2 row with ones at its endpoints.

Define b* = min dim K over linear subspaces K containing at least one member of each E_S. This is an optimization definition, not an oracle used by the algorithm. K may contain other directions. Initialize H=0; if a common eligible edge exists, take it (rank one, optimal for nonempty Q). Otherwise scan uncovered S with E_S disjoint from H and append an independent basis of span(E_S) to H. Every step adds at most three dimensions since all vectors on four coordinates have even parity. For any optimal K*, a member of E_S intersect K* is outside old H but inside new H. Thus dim(H intersect K*) increases by at least one per step. At most b* steps, and r=dim H <=3 b*. All generator rows are actual host edges. Membership, basis coefficients and discovery by scanning six candidates per S use polynomial bit work. No search through guard assignments is needed to find H. This is not a general higher-rank isotropic approximation.

For each S choose e in E_S intersect H and represent it as a sum of basis edges. Enumerate all 2^r assignments g to those independent observations; compute each chi_e(g) with its GF2 coefficients. On guard g:
  J_S chi_S = J_S chi_e(g) chi_(S minus e).
Thus every quartic is replaced by a signed pair character on a host edge. Correlated selected pairs are evaluated via the SAME g, never independent bits. Every x belongs to exactly one g. Combine boundary parity pins with basis pins and reject inconsistent systems by Gaussian elimination. On a consistent guard, minimize the resulting pair energy with the pins enforced. Either add a penalty M>2 sum|J_pair| for every violated pin (all such edges in host), or contract pinned host edges via signed union-find, updating coefficients and offsets, which preserves planarity. A feasible pin pattern exists; the penalty dominates the entire original pair energy range, so a minimum satisfies the pins. All coefficients exact integers with polynomial bit lengths. The two enforcement variants are symbolic, not executed in the reference code.

Use BD01's planar dual odd-face T-join and minimum-weight perfect matching. Convert J chi_e to -|J| + 2|J| times mismatch with preferred parity, add constant. Dual odd faces encode exact cycle parity; pair defects by shortest-path metric closure and blossom, XOR paths, integrate original spins and verify. This gives deterministic polynomial bit time per consistent guard (known planar Ising terminal). A single patch exact minimum and witness therefore cost <=2^r poly(N) <=8^b* poly(N). Store an actual guarded minimizer; no counting claim. If b*=O(log N), this is a polynomial terminal with polynomial guard discovery. The bound includes the host, source input and all failed checks. If no host is found, return UNKNOWN; do not assume a universal planar host.

## 4. Four-port patch DAG and charged resources (PROVED conditional)

Verify source scope ownership, tree, intersections, ordered port IDs, and complement symmetry. For each bag acquire children bottom-up. Store m_B(b)=minimum original subtree cost for every relative boundary word b; there are <=8 cells. Each cell has an actual local minimizer and child backlinks. With full boundary assignments, distinct child interiors are independent by running intersection; hence child minima add EXACTLY, even if multiple children have identical boundary sets. Absolute orientation retained by selecting actual parent bits and complementing an entire child witness as needed. Never drop a cross-boundary guard, owner budget or hidden correlation; the theorem assumes every such constraint compiled into exact local factors or separators. Absolute pins that break symmetry are not covered.

Treat each child message as an arbitrary complement-invariant <=4-port cost, aggregate Fourier coefficients BEFORE constructing/checking the local host. Host may be supplied per bag; it must contain nonzero pairs, required anchor fan edges for boundary pins, and one eligible partition for every surviving four-set. It need not contain the clique of a canceled interaction. No universal host/manifest discovery theorem. For every relative boundary word, minimize by Section 3 with all anchor pins; retain actual witness. At root min=0 iff original SAT, with reconstruction by running intersection. All messages are ordinary unscaled integer costs; divide exact scaled minimum by 16 before passing upward. W=sum source maximum costs bounds every actual subtree minimum; disjoint subtree maxima add, preventing recursive coefficient growth. Binary bit lengths O(log(W+1)+log N), and source validation/lifting polynomial. This extends BD01 to genuine four-port messages, not arbitrary signed OR or arbitrary E4/B4 physical compilation.
