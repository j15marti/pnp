## BD07-A: Exact signed suffix marginal (PROVED)
Input: n explicitly represented Boolean coordinates (or renumbered occurring coordinates), affine guard G, ordered list of explicit affine rejection events E_0,...,E_(m-1). Legal x iff x in G and outside every E_i. N includes all coefficients, RHSs and any supplied authenticated source/lift maps. Counts are distinct full chart words, not physical lift counts. General CNF compiles by one bad affine conjunction of false literals per clause, with polynomial construction; repeated literals, tautologies and empty clauses are treated by Gaussian consistency. BW07's private rim adapter and BA06's explicit dual events can feed this interface if their full acquisition and verification are charged. DA07 disjunctions of collision equalities are not silently converted to single affine bad events; integer owners from S06 remain separate.
Closure before evaluation: Gaussian inconsistency of G => UNSAT; relative empty bad event => drop; G contained in event => UNSAT; relative codimension-one event => impose complementary equation; duplicate relative events => retain one. Restart after every deduction/deletion, repeat at stagnation. Each strict change reduces dimension or event count, polynomial. Only this certified repertoire is implemented, not full semantic closure. A conditional deduction retains its guard.

Let V=GF(2)^n be the coefficient space and U_i=span of all coefficient rows of E_i,...,E_(m-1); U_m=0. For a nonempty affine equation system K, let H_i(K) denote its equation consequences with coefficient vectors in U_i, represented by canonical augmented RREF. This is a consistent affine relation on U_i^*, the space of linear functionals on U_i. For any functional eta satisfying H_i(K), the number of K words inducing eta is the same, by the affine-fiber theorem. For all others it is zero. In particular |K| is divisible by 2^(dim U_i-rank H_i(K)).
Consider signed inclusion-exclusion terms K=G intersect all events selected from the first i, coefficient (-1)^number selected; inconsistent terms contribute zero. Maintain table M_i[H] equal to the signed SUM of |K| over terms with H_i(K)=H. The key includes all RHSs, not just coefficient span or rank. Index i and authenticated source/order/guard are also part of its scope. Initially one key H_0(G) with mass |G|.
For each nonzero mass w at key H in layer i, create at most two attempts:
- skip E_i: add +w to key project(H,U_(i+1));
- include E_i: form J=H union equations E_i, drop if inconsistent; otherwise delta=rank J-rank H, add -w/2^delta to key project(J,U_(i+1)).
Here projection means restricting the equation consequences to the next coefficient space, not deleting coordinate names or forgetting RHSs. Because E_i observes only U_i, each K in the class has the same relative fraction 2^-delta of extensions satisfying E_i and the new projected key is identical for every such K. Uniform fibers prove both facts. Each division is exact integer arithmetic even with negative w; linearity allows merging and deleting zero total masses. Induction proves the invariant. At final layer the sole possible key is the empty relation; its mass equals Z=|G outside union E_i| by inclusion-exclusion. Negative intermediate weights do not represent actual witnesses.
Implementation of project: reduce each homogeneous coefficient row modulo a basis for U, eliminate the remainders while carrying the full augmented row along, and collect rows whose remainder is zero. Canonical RREF of these rows spans exactly the intersection of the coefficient space with U and preserves RHS. Polynomial Gaussian operations, no enumeration of U^*. Two equal ranks or equal coefficient spaces alone are NOT sufficient keys; opposite RHS on the same coefficient can be distinguished by a later event. Equality of full projected keys is a congruence only for queries supported on the declared suffix, not arbitrary future consumers.

## BD07-B: Reverse witness lifting without conditional recompilation (PROVED)
Retain all marginal tables and nested U_i. For any consistent affine query P whose coefficients lie in U_i, define C_i(P)=sum over H compatible with P of M_i[H] / 2^(rank(H+P)-rank H). By uniform fibers and inclusion-exclusion, this is EXACTLY the number of words satisfying G, avoiding the first i events, and satisfying P. This includes queries combining several equations simultaneously; it is not a marginal independence approximation. Each term division is integral.
If Z>0, start at layer m with empty functional on U_m. Traverse layers backwards. Invariant: fixed functional P on U_(i+1) has positive count of words avoiding first i+1 events, and all later events are already avoided (their observations lie in U_(i+1)). Extend P to a complete functional on U_i one independent direction at a time. For a tentative partial extension Q, compute
  C_i(Q) - C_i(Q+E_i).
This is the exact number of words in the fiber avoiding first i+1 events; later events remain fixed. Choose an extension with positive difference; the two complementary choices partition the positive count. At completion all E_i are avoided and the invariant moves backwards. There are at most n total independent extension choices, plus O(m) invariant checks. Final P on U_0 has a consistent lift with G by Gaussian elimination; every event is determined by P. Verify the original guard and every original event, then any authenticated physical lift. Code re-runs the certified source closure under each retained reverse pin and accumulates its proved guard consequences separately; prefix counts use the original saved tables and do not incorrectly apply suffix-derived facts to the prefix semantics. There is no new exponential search or rebuilding of the marginal DAG during reconstruction.
This is the key improvement over arbitrary guard resetting in BD06/DR07. Queries are restricted to already represented observation space at the corresponding saved layer. No claim that arbitrary new external guards can be answered cheaply, or that a zero signed channel supplies a witness. For a fresh guard outside that scope reacquire or decline; do not reuse the projected table.

## BD07-C: charged bound and potential (PROVED)
Let W_i be the number of nonzero canonical keys AFTER each layer and A=sum_i W_i. At most 2 W_i attempted transitions including incompatible branches; transient next table has at most 2 W_i keys. Each Gaussian projection, merge, rank test, exact division and serialization costs poly(N) bit time. Absolute mass <=2^(n+m) (sum of at most 2^m inclusion-exclusion term sizes), hence O(n+m) bits. Deterministic sorted-map or comparison dictionary supplies polynomial key operations (even a linear scan remains polynomial in A, but balanced tree gives log A=O(poly(N)) as key length polynomial). Saved tables use O(A poly(N)) space; suffix spans, source authentication and reconstruction workspace add poly(N). Reverse probes scan at most O((n+m) A) terms, plus polynomial closure/verification, giving T<=poly(N)(1+A) with deterministic dictionary, S<=poly(N)(1+A), CM<=(T+1)S on same serial realization. Count-only rolling tables use O(max W_i poly(N)) space. Source compiler and actual original lift cost must be added if not polynomial. No peak byte/CM measurements were made.
A fixed polynomial cap can be enforced on attempted edge and stored key counts; cap failure returns UNKNOWN, not UNSAT. A<=N^c for fixed c gives a uniform polynomial terminal in the given order, with exact witnesses. A is discovered through charged evaluation, not assumed cheap to predict. After schedule acquisition, reserved jobs for each transition and each reverse query give a strict additive arithmetic-job credit decrement; not a universal SAT branching potential and not a proof of polynomial initial credit on all sources.

## BD07-D: separation from existing rank criteria and order obstruction (PROVED)

With h=m growing, this terminal is polynomial even though both raw criteria are large. After all A, there are 2^m different keys x_i=0 for arbitrary subsets, with nonzero mass (each subset has exactly one contributing term). No root closure removes events or fixes a bit.
