## Exact proof
See [shared manuscript, sec:bd15, lem:bd15-basis and lem:bd15-rank, lines 253 onward](https://github.com/j15marti/pnp/blob/4c0f9e995b7f00061490243269cd4b44435579b8/research/final-proof/main.tex#L253), blob 23350458310d2f1e79286eba853f07e14b83da93.

1. For distinct rank-two observation planes U, D is the union of their pairwise nonzero intersections. Every common independent coordinate family must include D, because intersections of coordinate subspaces are spanned by common basis vectors. If any plane contains >2 mandatory forms, decline. Otherwise add nonshared forms to complete each plane's two-element basis. Any nonshared form occurs in only one plane, so choices do not interfere. This union spans the total row space; it is independent iff its cardinality |D|+sum_U(2-|D intersect U|) equals the total row rank. This is an exact polynomial-time decision for whether the current explicit planes can simultaneously be coordinate planes in ANY invertible linear change of basis. Multiple signed packets on a plane retain their actual RHS; only geometry is deduplicated. Upon success, their avoidance constraints are ordinary 2-CNF in actual independent observations, solved by SCC/reachability, lifted by Gaussian elimination, source-verified. Arbitrary affine guards are first actually substituted and closure repeated; eligibility rechecked, not assumed preserved. Local same-plane support is exactly four values, and rank-one exclusions are necessary affine rows. No uncharged semantic closure.
2. I21 reverse compilation preserves the rank-two DO01 registry. In a connected linear 3-uniform hypergraph, different planes have disjoint nonzero forms; total rank is n-1. Every vertex in a critical core has degree >=2, so 2m>n-1. No such common basis exists, including Fano (14 vs 6) and sign neighbors. This falsifies universal affine coordinate-basis normalization; no claim about arbitrary nonlocal transformations.
3. Grid G of side L even, shared actual r, NAE(x_u,x_v,r) on each edge. In coordinates y_v=x_v XOR r it is ordinary monotone 2-CNF. All-one and every single-zero word show full affine hull; r is free. Thus even complete unconditional affine closure cannot change the hull. Fix r=0 for analysis. Every balanced variable cut has >=L/4 crossing edges by mixed-row or full-opposite-row argument. Max degree 4 gives induced matching k>=L/160 by greedy removal of <=40 edges per choice. Set all other variables to 1. For any scalar amplitude with exactly the satisfying support (not merely the characteristic function), the submatrix indexed by left one set A and right zero set B has nonzero entries precisely B subset A. Order by size: triangular with nonzero diagonal, rank 2^k over any field. Every ordered read-once linear transfer, and every binary variable-leaf tensor tree with such linear cut factorization, must have dimension >=2^k at a balanced cut. Arbitrary signed cancellation does not avoid this rank requirement. No lower bound on arbitrary decision DAG, arbitrary guard rewrites, other tensor decompositions, or unrestricted runtime; the grid is trivially SAT and handled by rule 1. This invalidates requiring universal polynomial full-support linear transfer as a necessary property of a polynomial decision solver.
