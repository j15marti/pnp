## Exact cancellation
Use zero-field rational pairwise energy H(s)=C-sum J_uv s_u s_v, s_i in {-1,1}, authenticated guard absorbed first. Two actual degree-four vertices u,v with identical ordered distinct neighbors B cannot be adjacent. Their private energies minimize independently to f_u(s_B)+f_v(s_B), where f_z=-abs(sum J_zi s_i). Each function is complement-symmetric; its Walsh spectrum has only constant, six pair terms, and one four-spin term q_z. Compute exact numerators by 16-word transform. If q_u+q_v=0, their sum is pairwise on the original B, and both centers can be eliminated jointly without any new variables. All other terms are unchanged. Reverse minimizing lift chooses sign of the original weighted sum, either sign on ties. A supplied threshold is preserved in both directions, even for non-minimal feasible words because an optimum suffices for decision. Never export these chosen minimizing bits as forced. Any guard involving eliminated centers must first be absorbed or separately retained and exactly included in the conditional minimization; no free removal of guards.

Discovery groups by canonical actual neighbor list and opposite nonzero quartic numerator. The code scans degree-four candidates, computes 16x16 transforms, and finds opposite values in a dictionary; deterministic sorting also suffices. The transformation reduces active vertices by two, keeps at most n(n-1)/2 edges, and exact scaling by 16 per round increases bit lengths by O(n+input bits) (each round is linear sums with constant or polynomial factor; O(n log n) is a safe bound). Store actual weighted incidences and scale for reverse witness; no exponential expansion or branch. At most n/2 pair eliminations, then balanced-sign graph exact terminal or decline. Paid guard/pair-packet closure first and after any imported deduction; balanced check after each projection. No semantic closure oracle and no claim of universal closure implementation. ## Connected family with actual complementary gain
Take a positive clique on 4b distinct boundary vertices, divided into b four-sets. Attach two private degree-four centers to each set, all couplings +1 except one edge at the second center is -1. Each block has a vertex-disjoint frustrated 4-cycle, so any balanced deletion set has size >=b=n/6. No separator of size <=3 disconnects the graph (for b>=1 the remaining clique is connected and each surviving center has a remaining neighbor). Each center individually has nonzero quartic coefficient (+/-1/2) and four equal magnitudes, so ordinary single-vertex pairwise minimization and dominance fail there. Put S=s1+s2+s3 and t=s4. Minimum mismatch is exactly b, attained by all boundary spins equal with minimizing centers. Threshold b SAT and b-1 UNSAT. ## Anchored cancellation search
For binary-encoded positive integers T,a1,...,am, construct m+1 independent degree-four stars on the same four neighbors. The distinguished anchor has all couplings T; each remaining star has three +ai and one -ai. The quartic coefficient of -a|s1+s2+s3+s4| is +a/2 (direct Walsh sum), and flipping one sign negates this coefficient. A subset including the anchor cancels its quartic iff the selected ai sum to T. Thus specified-anchor cancellation-subset acquisition is NP-complete by SUBSET SUM, even with this fixed common four-port scope and simple graph. Membership follows from summing rational coefficients. The whole group sum and opposite-pair scan are easy, but complete anchored subset acquisition must not be assumed polynomial for arbitrary binary weights. This is weak NP-completeness, not a claim of hardness under unit weights or a SAT lower bound; pseudo-polynomial numeric DP can be used under an independently bounded integer total. Optional normalization need not solve this auxiliary query.
