# CA01: Can the recovered constructive proof spine close P = NP?

**24 November 2026, 10:27 PST draft, with publication-time update. Evidence cutoff: reports through 10:30 PST.**

**Verdict: no complete proof is established by the recovered material.** Even provisionally granting the restricted peer lemmas, their combined hypotheses do not cover every source instance with a uniform polynomial bound. The remaining obligation is source-authenticated, guard-preserving, polynomially paid progress or terminal coverage for every reachable residual. This is a mathematical gap, not simply missing presentation or software integration.

This audit also derives a useful restricted bridge: M10's count-dominance certificate supports an arbitrary supplied joint q-bit affine probe when M > 2^q B, including the two-bit demands needed by DP04 when M > 4B. It supplies an infinite family showing why single-parity completeness alone cannot replace that joint guarantee. The complete M10 thin-complement terminal can decide that counterfamily; it is not a universal portfolio obstruction. The concurrently published MS10 has a broader uniform-fiber probability-margin principle; this audit claims no priority for that shared argument. Section 10 integrates six additional reports through 10:30 PST.

## 3. Exact obstruction to simply joining tractable modules

### CA01-A: 2-SAT plus disjoint parity equations already encodes arbitrary 3-SAT

For each clause C_i = (l_i1 OR l_i2 OR l_i3), introduce selectors y_i1,y_i2,y_i3.

Put in a 2-CNF B:

- The three pairwise at-most-one clauses NOT y_ij OR NOT y_ik.
- The three implications NOT y_ij OR l_ij.

Put in an affine system A over GF(2):

  y_i1 + y_i2 + y_i3 = 1.

The 2-CNF B is always satisfiable by setting every selector to zero. A is always satisfiable and its equations use disjoint selector triples. In their conjunction, at-most-one and odd parity force exactly one selector per clause, whose implication certifies a true literal.

Conversely, a satisfying source assignment selects any true literal in each clause. Thus F is satisfiable if and only if A AND B is satisfiable. There are n+3m variables, 6m binary clauses and m parity equations; construction and both witness maps are polynomial.

This construction, recovered in PR #325 and checked independently here, refutes an automatic inference from two tractable components to a tractable common witness. It does not prove their conjunction is outside P. A universal polynomial gluing theorem for this domain would itself decide arbitrary 3-SAT [14].

### CA01-B: easy regions and constant crossing size do not imply easy augmented regions

Use Q = GF(2)^2 colors. For each original edge uv of a graph H, create a private leaf w. Three crossings u-w forbid differences 1,2,3, forcing w=u. The fourth crossing v-w forbids difference 0, requiring v!=w.

The exact leaf projection is u!=v. An edgeless center containing all original vertices, with one singleton leaf per edge, is a region tree with four crossings per adjacency. Every unaugmented region is trivial. After projection the central region is arbitrary four-coloring of H.

This is DP04's precise augmentation warning. It does not refute DP04, whose hypotheses already require a paid oracle for augmented local jobs. It refutes replacing that hypothesis by tractability of the base regions. The example is in the stated signed graph/multiedge language; no strict physical tetravalence transfer is asserted [6].

### CA01-C: Gaussian consistency plus one reserve budget is source-complete

For each 3-SAT clause, create seven selectors, indexed by its seven satisfying local truth patterns. Require odd selector parity in each block. Equate the affine literal observations across occurrences, with the appropriate sign offsets.

Impose one total Hamming-weight bound at most m. Every odd-parity block has at least one selected bit, so this bound forces exactly one selected row per clause. Synchronized observations then give a satisfying global source assignment; a source model selects its actual local pattern and gives a reverse witness.

If m is odd, append one forced selector equal to 1 and increase the cap to m+1. The permitted total weight is now even. The source problem is therefore equivalent to

  A y = t,   wt(y) <= K,   wt(y) even.

There are O(m) selectors and equations; dense binary encoding is O(m^2). Unused source variables can be filled arbitrarily.

This even-cardinality bound is precisely the empty-boundary-graph reserve model with K mandatory reserve vertices: all selected boundary vertices must match distinct reserve vertices, and leftover reserves match internally. The reserve clique and boundary joins have polynomial size. Thus an arbitrary affine-plus-reserve compatibility solver would decide arbitrary 3-SAT.

This is a direct construction, not an inference from an experimental failure. It complements M10's independent syndrome-decoding reduction and the classical general decoding result [10,17]. It is not a P != NP argument.

## 4. Restricted constructive bridge: joint probes from count margins

### CA01-D: paid q-bit probe theorem for an authenticated M10 bundle

Consider the exact M10 parity-class domain. For class variables p in GF(2)^k, let

  A = {p : Gp=t, 1.p=H mod 2}

be a nonempty affine space of size M=2^nu. Each class has width w_i, total W, and omega_i=w_i mod 2. Exact feasibility requires

  wt(p) <= H,   wt(p XOR omega) <= W-H.

Let B be an efficiently computable upper bound on the number of parity-compatible ambient words violating at least one inequality. M10 uses the sum of two parity-filtered binomial tails; overlaps may be counted twice.

For any *supplied, authenticated* affine observation Lp=b with at most q rows, assume

  M > 2^q B.

Then:

1. If the augmented linear equations are inconsistent, the probe is infeasible.
2. Otherwise the affine fiber has size M/2^r for some r<=q, hence strictly more than B elements.
3. At most B of those elements can violate capacity, so the fiber contains an actual feasible word.

The test is exact under this sufficient margin. No listing of the 2^q possible targets is required for a supplied query.

For constructive recovery, apply M10's prefix procedure to the augmented linear system. At any prefix, count its affine completions by Gaussian elimination and bound bad ambient completions by the two binomial tails. Both quantities split additively between next-bit choices. A positive difference at the parent implies a positive difference at some child. Continue for k bits, then use M10's pair-fill and original-arc fill. This uses polynomially many Gaussian/count operations on polynomial-length integers, and preserves the full guard.

Consequently M>4B supplies an exact two-bit decision-and-witness oracle on this authenticated class domain. The stronger M>2^(q+1)B also ensures the feasible part of each consistent q-bit fiber has its full affine hull: a proper affine subspace contains at most half of that fiber.

**Limits.** These are observations on the existing class variables. An original-arc probe that splits classes requires reaggregation and recertification. DP04 must supply a proved pullback of its actual relative-color probes and incoming summaries into this domain. Multiple summaries may introduce more constraints or change the capacity problem; a margin for two rows is not an unlimited augmentation certificate. For fixed thin-complement parameter D, M10 already gives an exact alternative for arbitrary class-level affine rows even when this margin fails.

### CA01-E: an infinite family separates single-parity and joint completeness

Let r>=4 be even. Use k=2r distinct tag classes, each of width 2, and net H=2r-2. Choose the tag equations so that the first r parity bits are all equal to a and the last r all equal to b.

An explicit full-rank parity-check matrix uses rows p_0+p_j=0 for 1<=j<r and p_r+p_(r+j)=0 for 1<=j<r. Its 2r columns are distinct: within each block they are the all-ones column and r-1 unit columns in disjoint coordinate sets. Thus this is a legitimate class partition, without hidden duplicate-tag merging.

The affine domain consists of four words, corresponding to (a,b)=00,10,01,11. Since r is even, the total-parity equation is automatic. Capacity admits exactly 00,10,01: their weights are 0,r,r, whereas 11 has weight 2r>H. The upper-capacity inequality is inactive.

Here M=4 and the ambient parity-filtered forbidden set has size B=1. Every scalar affine probe has exactly the same support on the three feasible words as on the four-word affine hull. Nevertheless the joint target (a,b)=11 is absent.

Thus M>2B and complete single-parity information do not ensure arbitrary two-bit support. The strict M>4B sufficient condition cannot be weakened to M>=4B in general. This is an infinite family proof, with r=4 checked independently.

**This is not an obstruction to the full M10 terminal.** Its complement parameter is fixed, so the exact thin-complement method can reject the missing joint target. The result identifies why affine-hull-only handoffs are unsound and why explicit joint support or a stronger margin is needed.

## 5. What forced information, factorization and topology do not provide for free

For the always-satisfiable formula G(F,z)=AND_C(z OR C), z=1 always works. The bit z is forced to 1 exactly when F is unsatisfiable. Hence acquiring all semantic forced literals on arbitrary formulas already decides the source UNSAT question. Sound unit, affine, matching, flow and typed-gadget closure should be applied whenever eligible, before work, after deductions and at stagnation; its actual discovery and verification costs must remain in the algorithm. Conditional facts retain their guards. The wrapper argument is about unrestricted semantic closure, not a criticism of certified polynomial closure [7].

Likewise an affine span is not a support relation. With eight boundary ports and two universal reserves, the empty boundary graph supports 29 even subsets of size at most two, while its affine hull is the entire 128-word even subspace. Missing weight-four words remain absent. Even many pinned conditional hulls can remain full without the global relation becoming affine.

Prime/modular consistency also need not recover Boolean feasibility. PR #326 gives the exact-one triples

  (0,5,6), (1,2,5), (0,1,2), (2,3,6), (3,4,5), (4,5,6).

The integer vector (0,2,-1,1,0,0,1) satisfies every row sum equal to one, so every modulus is consistent; the all-1/3 vector is fractionally feasible. Yet no Boolean solution exists. Subtracting the second and third rows gives x_5=x_0; subtracting the fifth and sixth gives x_3=x_6. The first row gives 2x_0+x_3=1, forcing x_0=0,x_3=x_6=1; the fourth row is then impossible. A displayed 6x6 minor has determinant one. All six single-row deletion neighbors are SAT [15].

This counterexample is solved by simple additional Boolean reasoning. It excludes the sufficiency of the stated algebraic relaxations, not all cooperative closure.

The older recovery audit also distinguishes topological representation size from necessary SAT computation. Its equality-product control has an exponentially large topological invariant and an immediate witness. Current DO04 raw-width barriers are more carefully scoped, and DA04 now gives nonlinear deletion examples even with high raw interface rank. No topological, spectral or prime-factor count alone supplies a universal runtime theorem.

## 6. The conditional theorem that would finish the constructive route

Fix a single uniform deterministic algorithm, input bit length N, an exact polynomial source compiler, and all reconstruction maps. Suppose every reachable source-authenticated residual R is handled by either:

- A paid exact terminal; or
- An exact guarded AND/OR reduction to children R_i, with the correct aggregation rule and preserved witness maps.

Assume a nonnegative credit C(R)>=1 with root C(R_0)<=N^a and

  sum_i C(R_i) <= C(R)-1,

and that each processed job costs at most N^b bit operations for fixed a,b. The bound must include unsuccessful discovery, source acquisition, verification, all eligible closure, canonical keys, all edge attempts, representation changes, storage management and reconstruction. If extra phases do not decrease C, their total work must be charged into the job or another globally bounded account.

Induction gives at most C(R_0) processed tree jobs. Total work is O(N^(a+b)) plus the already polynomial source/lift work. On a standard bit-machine accounting, peak stored bits S<=O(N+T) and cumulative memory CM<= (T+1)S, so both are polynomial for the same execution. More refined space bounds are welcome but not needed to prove P=NP.

The user's proposed condition sum_i mu(R_i)^c <= mu(R)^c-1 is a special case with C=mu^c, fixed c, polynomial root magnitude and paid evaluation. A DAG version may share jobs, but must charge all key construction, incoming attempts, guard-dependent uses and updates. A small circuit representing an exponential search is not this ledger.

DP01 proves useful contraction identities for count boxes. With n independent binary types, however, P=product_j(m_j+1)=2^n and credit 2P-1 has only n+1 bits while its *magnitude* is exponential. DA04 similarly has exact credit (4^(k+1)-1)/3, polynomial only when the applicable k is logarithmic or bounded. A polynomial-size integer encoding is not a polynomial execution bound [3,11].

For naive binary size-decrement branching, 2(n-1)^c>n^c for sufficiently large n and every fixed c. Thus raw size^c cannot automatically pay both branches. This does not rule out stronger potentials, better branch rules, contractions, exact terminals or sharing.

DS02 importantly weakens what must be acquired: an algorithm need not enumerate all source rows or find an optimal decisive subset. A polynomial task manifest, a suitably cheap decisive subset, and a correct paid outer evaluator can suffice under fair scheduling. None is yet universally bounded.

If "some paid normalization into some polynomial terminal" is left unconstrained, the asserted existence is essentially another formulation of P=NP: a polynomial SAT algorithm could be used directly as that terminal. The work needed is a concrete structural theorem establishing these premises from source syntax, without calling the desired result as an oracle.

## 7. Correct diagonal quantifiers

For one fixed total correct portfolio A with no uniform polynomial runtime bound, for every j there are arbitrarily long inputs whose runtime exceeds N^j, after absorbing fixed constants. Otherwise finitely many exceptional lengths could be absorbed into a polynomial bound. Choose an increasing-length sequence F_j.

Under a genuinely fair, paid simulation, a sufficiently late F_j must avoid every fixed registered channel with a polynomial accessible successful path. This is a simultaneous statement about the same F_j, not unrelated hard examples for each channel.

It is still only a statement about A and its actual channels. A slow exact portfolio can exist even if P=NP. High values of an upper-bound parameter do not prove that A actually runs slowly. To get a contradiction, one must prove that every possible F_j admits a paid successful transformation or terminal in this specific system.

DO01/DO04 establish simultaneous failures of named raw criteria, with valuable SAT neighbors. DA04 adds another parameter those raw critical families can keep large. BW04 excludes a particular private fanout normalization. None proves resistance to all geometry-changing, nonlinear or future exact methods. No P!=NP or oracle separation is established here [8,11,12].

## 9. Specific peer feedback and next theorem

GD10: retain the repaired affine-identity adapter and actual parity witnesses. Do not promote it to arbitrary joint support. A synthetic span basis is not a list of realizable rows.

M10 / DP04: register CA01-D where relative-color probes have a proved affine class pullback. Fixed thin-complement terminals already accept joint class constraints. The count variant needs M>4B for two-bit feasibility and M>8B for full hull after such probes. Incoming summary accumulation and original-arc class splitting remain paid recertification steps.

DA04 / BW04: the new nonlinear degree-list terminal is a meaningful escape from raw-width barriers. Full-domain synchronized spare-port fanout cannot universally remove its deficit parameter by private sharding. Direct E4/B4, overlapping hubs, different constraints and non-affine encodings need their own exact transfer proofs.

DS02 / DP03: keep selective acquisition and monotone propagation as orchestration theorems. Supply the actual polynomial task universe, exact outer evaluator, source certificates and full phase/key budget before invoking a universal runtime bound.

DO04 / BH03 / BA03: continue testing both critical roots and SAT deletion neighbors. Raw core rank, high deficit, or absence of a switch certificate is an eligibility failure, not an unrestricted time lower bound.

**Next decisive target:** prove a source-checkable dichotomy for every post-closure residual: either an authenticated tractable augmented domain (possibly the new count-margin or degree-list domain), or an exact transformation with polynomial-root aggregate credit. Require this after all accumulated protected observations, not just on the initial factors. The restricted q-bit bridge advances one interface; it does not establish that dichotomy.

## 10. Publication-time update through 10:30 PST

A final issue refresh acquired six additional reports. Their relevant theorem and open-obligation sections were read, but their experiments were not rerun. They extend the positive portfolio and sharpen the missing premise; they do not establish universal closure.

**DR04, four-edge source equivalence.** For arbitrary proper 3-CNF F, an explicit always-SAT signed graph region has four ports y_a=r+a*z indexed by GF(4). Its attainable selectors are every z when F is SAT and precisely the three nonzero z when F is UNSAT. Polynomially supplied nonzero witnesses already determine the same complete joint affine boundary hull and all scalar affine supports for both labels. A parent with one vertex p and four exclusions p!=y_a extends exactly when z=0: for nonzero z the four port colors exhaust GF(4), whereas for zero they coincide. Thus the join is SAT exactly when F is SAT. This strengthens the earlier star example: TWO known-SAT regions and complete boundary affine information suffice to retain the original 3-SAT decision problem. DP04's aggregate credit is constant here, but the one genuinely unresolved acquisition job still needs a paid solver. DR04 expressly makes no hardness transfer to GD10's matching domain or strict physical E4/B4 [19].

**I10, overlapping physical observations.** A disjoint optional-edge matching source can have zero vertical/nonphysical defect rank while paired tag masks encode arbitrary 3-coloring. The missing condition is compatible owner injection or another valid overlap treatment. I10 supplies a deterministic terminal for residual rank at most one and a one-sided randomized Pfaffian construction for larger rank. A probabilistic zero is not an exact UNSAT certificate and cannot fill this deterministic proof spine [20].

**BD04, actual state and edge accounting.** For supplied ordered disjoint blocks with affine images U_i, prefix image P_i, suffix image S_i and total rank R, the affine-extendable cut has 2^w_i states with w_i=dim(P_i)+dim(S_i)-R. Transitions have exponent tau_i=dim(P_(i-1))+dim(U_i)+dim(S_i)-R; distinct demanded local tags and their acquisition have separate exponents. This is more informative than total circuit size or total rank. Favorable order and paid local costs are still hypotheses; a low-width trellis with an expensive local acquisition does not close the algorithm [21].

**DS04, an exact guarded rejection-tree terminal.** For explicit affine rejection events E_i in affine guard G, let c_i and c_ij be their Gaussian-computable intersection counts. For a tree T, U_T=sum c_i-sum_(ij in T)c_ij bounds rejected words. It is exact precisely when every pair intersection implies each event on the intervening tree path. The path implications are polynomial Gaussian checks, and if any exact tree exists then every maximum-weight intersection tree is exact. Exactness persists under every affine subguard, providing actual joint probes and witnesses. If not exact, strict positive count credit may still certify SAT; otherwise return UNKNOWN. Raw DO04 triangle-free families fail the stated terminal, so the proof does not supply universal event-tree coverage [22].

**MS10, uniform recourse rather than one-fiber feasibility.** Protect all outside event maps. Bound bad-event mass uniformly in every protected fiber, subtract a pair intersection only when its projection covers every protected target, and choose a maximum-weight correction tree. A bound U<1 gives a uniform actual repair and exact existential deletion. U<2^-d supplies every consistent relative-rank-d affine probe. Positive signed count credit gives a deterministic lift. This is a broader companion to CA01-D's M10 specialization. Its polynomial guarantee needs explicit paid event acquisition and a supplied polynomial candidate-group list; residual progress after stagnation remains open [23].

**BA04, a canonical restricted quotient closure.** With explicit factor tables and protected keys, intersect each factor's actual translation stabilizers over every key fiber. Quotient by their sum and repair with attributed real periods. On fixed tables, repeated projection reaches an order-independent least fixed point in at most D strict dimension increases. This is a useful normalization theorem, but remaining factors can be aperiodic, source tables may be expensive to acquire, and target-conditioned pruning is not covered by that confluence claim. Remaining DAG work is separately charged [24].

These updates suggest the most coherent constructive pipeline: authentic source compilation, certified closure, real-period quotient and uniform event-group repair, recomputed trellis interfaces, then an exact eligible terminal with all local acquisition paid. The missing theorem is that this process always reaches a paid terminal or admits an aggregate-credit contraction after stagnation. No currently reviewed source proves it.
