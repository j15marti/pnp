## 1. Domain and rank budget (PROVED)
Over GF(2), take an explicit common exterior word z in GF(2)^d and private word p in GF(2)^n. All region constraints are the complements of affine packets P_i given by A_i p = b_i + B_i z. Fixed exterior guards are affine equations and can be carried in z. A supplied consistent affine chart on (z,p) can first be parameterized by Gaussian elimination: parameterize its image on z, then choose an affine lift and a basis of the kernel of projection to z. Substitute into every packet; retain the reconstruction map. The theorem is on these independent coordinates, with no omitted private quota or external consumer. For several regions, shared private dependencies must be promoted to exterior or jointly parameterized; do not treat correlated private variables as independent. For a supplied exterior split, the private-coordinate/packet incidence components are polynomially discoverable; this is not a theorem of universally discoverable good splits.

Write r_i=rank(A_i), c_i=2^(n-r_i), D=2^n. Every fiber P_i(z) is empty or an affine coset of size c_i, since the coefficient matrix is independent of z. Define B=sum_i c_i. Recognize B<=D using exact O(n+log m)-bit integers (m packets). Globally inconsistent packets may be dropped first. The implementation conservatively counts even globally empty packets. Rank-zero packets allowed. The budget is an upper bound on SUM of fiber volumes, not a union count.

For fixed z, let A(z)=sum_i |P_i(z)| and U(z)=union_i P_i(z). The acceptance condition is U(z) != GF(2)^n.
If B<D, every exterior word has a lift.
If B=D, U(z) is the full space iff ALL packets are active (nonempty) and their fibers are pairwise disjoint. Proof: |U|<=A<=D. If |U|=D then both inequalities are equalities, so all positive capacity packets active and no overlap; conversely disjointness and activity give |U|=D. This also holds for n=0 and empty exterior dimension.
Therefore under B=D the exact projected support is
R(z) = OR_i [P_i(z) empty] OR OR_{i<j}[P_i(z) intersect P_j(z) nonempty].
Both directions follow from the cardinality inequalities, not from approximate hulls.

## 2. Exact syndrome quotient and reusable packet list (PROVED)

## 3. Deterministic private witness without a search tree (PROVED)
For an accepted z define X(p)=number of forbidden packets containing p. Under a uniform p, E[X]<=1. Compute exact conditional sum M(Q)=sum_i |P_i(z) intersect Q| for a prefix affine chart Q by Gaussian ranks; divide by |Q|. If E[X]<1, choose each next free coordinate so the conditional mean remains <1 (one of two halves always does). At the last singleton X is a nonnegative integer <1, hence zero.
At equality, there is a pair intersection by the theorem. Obtain an actual collision word p* by Gaussian solve; X(p*)>=2. Follow its coordinate prefixes until the first conditional mean >1. This exists by the final singleton; its predecessor mean <=1, so the opposite half has mean strictly <1. Switch to that half and finish as above. At most n prefix probes to find escape and n further probes, each costing m rank calculations (plus at most binom(m,2) intersection tests). No exponential tree, no randomization, no hidden model counting; only counts of individual affine intersections. Final witness checks ALL original packets and lifts the original chart. Negative fibers are certified by all-active pairwise disjointness or local contradiction.
Mandatory certified local closure before projection, after each deduction and at stagnation: intersect each forbidden packet with current affine chart. Empty intersection deletes no model; equal chart rejects; relative codim1 complement is one necessary affine equation (choose an independent packet row and reverse its RHS); higher codim gives no single-owner affine deduction (complement has full relative affine hull). Insert one new row and restart. Under fixed z do this again with z guard retained. One may keep sound rows as redundant metadata while computing the tight budget on the original parameterized chart, so budget is not invalidated by narrowing the chart. The checker does this and verifies witnesses satisfy all acquired rows. Witness prefix selections are not exported as unconditional deductions, and are not search branches.
