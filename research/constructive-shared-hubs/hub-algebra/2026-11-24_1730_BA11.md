## A. Exact semantic observation space (BA07, BD08; reused and executed)
For a normalized consistent affine chart x in GF(2)^d, explicit integer signed sum f=sum_t c_t 1_{A_t x=b_t}, define F(w)=sum_x f(x)(-1)^(w dot x). Discard inconsistent terms, let a_t be a solution and K_t homogeneous kernel. The Fourier contribution is c_t 2^dim(K_t) (-1)^(w dot a_t) 1_{w in K_t^perp}. For affine frequency query Q, E(Q)=sum_{w in Q}F(w)^2; compute by L^2 signed pair character sums over Q intersect K_t^perp intersect K_s^perp. For intersection a+K, character sum is zero unless a_t+a_s annihilates K, else (-1)^((a_t+a_s) dot a) 2^dim K. Hence E is exact, nonnegative, and positive precisely when some frequency in Q is nonzero.

Start V=0, query w dot h=1 for a basis h of V^perp. If all zero, finish; otherwise self-reduce a positive query coordinatewise to a nonzero frequency outside V, insert it and repeat. O(d^2) energy queries, each O(L^2) Gaussian jobs; all integer bit lengths polynomial. Fourier inversion shows f invariant under translation by h iff h annihilates its Fourier support. Thus V is the unique smallest rowspace preserving VALUES, including for nonaffine Boolean f. For Boolean f this is also the support observation space. For nonnegative multiplicities it need not be; BA07 already proved coNP-complete support-period recognition for positive affine sums via sum_C [C false]+t. Do not confuse the two. BD11's V_i can be replaced with this exact semantic space, preserving its lift proof; rejection-hull candidate U_i lies in V_i modulo the guard. Recompute after a new actual guard if needed. Keep registered S06 quotas, tags, occupancy and any external observations separate.

Given basis V, choose Gaussian linear section x(s) of the observation map. Restrict every affine term to x(s). Because f is constant on fibers this gives the same exact function in syndrome coordinates, with no increase in term count and integer coefficients. Source authentication is not implied by Booleanity; for an integer list verify Booleanity via sum(f^2-f)=0 as in BD11, or retain certified nonnegative weight semantics.

## B. Pair projection (BA07 specialization and executable upgrade)
Let f,g depend on their semantic rowspaces V,U on the same d-bit chart. W=V intersect U, k=dim W. This is the unique least linear equality test on two independently proposed full syndromes: for arbitrary x,y, if W values agree, the functionals on V and U combine consistently on V+U, so Gaussian lifting yields a common word. If another tested rowspace T omits some w in W, choose h in T^perp with w(h)=1; x and x+h agree on T but cannot be lifted jointly. Minimality is for all proposed syndrome values, not restricted actual accepting supports.

Project each term to W coordinates; if A=a+K, image is Wa+WK, and every nonempty fiber has 2^(dim K-rank(WK)) points. The signed projected counts C_f,C_g are explicit lists of at most the respective original lengths. For each shared syndrome eta, the exact joint count on the d-bit ambient chart is
    C_fg(eta)=C_f(eta) C_g(eta) / 2^(d-k).
Proof: for fixed eta, independent V-private and U-private functionals lift with constant multiplicity 2^(d-dim(V+U)); each C_f has multiplicity 2^(d-dim V), similarly g. The same count holds for any nonnegative weights, with weighted semantics only. Sum over eta using pair Gaussian intersections instead of enumerating 2^k words. A direct pair product on the original chart also has polynomial cost; the value of this formulation is an exact separator and interface-preserving projection, not an asymptotic speedup for two flat factors. For a protected observation space W0, augment V and U by W0, perform the same construction, then project the product to W0 and divide by the known power of two. This gives an exact conditional count representation (can retain a scaled integer list). External output and independent physical lifts remain charged. This W0 variant is symbolic, not executed.

Witness reconstruction: find a common syndrome with positive product by conditional signed counts, then choose actual local accepting words on that fiber, glue their V and U observations by Gaussian elimination and validate original source. Do not choose a single signed term as a Boolean witness.

## C. New theorem: high-rank Cartesian packet-label tree terminal (PROVED)
For every finite explicit family of authenticated nonnegative signed affine factors f_i on a common affine chart, suppose the following conditions hold, all polynomially verifiable once the lists and source are acquired.
1. Compute exact semantic V_i as in A (or certified conservative sufficient spaces). Construct the graph with edge ij for each nonzero W_ij=V_i intersect V_j. Require a forest; disconnected components can be multiplied, or joined by empty interfaces. For each i choose a complement P_i to the sum of incident W_e in V_i. Require the SINGLE registry consisting of one copy of each W_e and each P_i to be a direct sum, verified by one global rank computation. This simultaneously detects higher-order dependencies even when pairwise overlaps look harmless. Merely having a tree of pairwise intersections is insufficient: a central space span(x,y) and three leaves span(x), span(y), span(x+y) fail the direct-sum check.
2. Use the independent edge and private syndrome coordinates as the common chart; unused ambient bits contribute multiplicity 2^(d-rank(sum V_i)). For each i restrict its original signed list via a linear section of V_i. For each nonempty affine term A in the local edge/private coordinates, project away P_i to all incident edge coordinates, obtaining J. Project J to each incident edge e, obtaining A_ite. Require
    dim J = sum_{e incident i} dim A_ite.
Since J is contained in the product of these marginals, and each is affine, equality of dimensions is necessary and sufficient for J=product_e A_ite. This checks the actual joint relation; no independence guess. Empty term contributes zero. Multiply its signed coefficient by the uniform private-fiber cardinality. Let this be a_it. A leaf with one incident interface always meets this Cartesian term test.
3. No unregistered private coupling, protected tag, capacity or external guard. Initial arbitrary affine guard is parameterized first; a new arbitrary guard can change the registry and requires recertification. Edge-local affine restrictions may be inserted directly. All existing source constraints are retained as factors.

After these paid tests each local private-fiber count is
    g_i(y_inc)=sum_t a_it product_{e incident i} 1_{A_ite}(y_e).
The count of the original chart is
    Z=2^(d-rank(sum V_i)) sum_{t_1,...,t_m} product_i a_i,t_i product_{e=ij} K_e(t_i,t_j),
where K_e(t,s)=|A_ite intersect A_jse|. K_e is acquired by Gaussian elimination in the actual edge coordinates, optionally intersecting an edge-local guard. Derivation: expand the finite product of signed sums, exchange the sums, then use independence of the ONE copy of each edge coordinate block. The formula is exact despite signed cancellation and nonaffine fibers; under Boolean factors it counts original selector words, not physical choices discarded by decision-only adapters. For weighted factors it counts the authenticated weighted product.

Root the tree. For label t at node i use
    M_i(t)=a_it product_{j child of i} (sum_s K_ij(t,s) M_j(s)).
Then Z is the unused-chart multiplicity times sum_t M_root(t). No tuple of all labels or all edge assignments is materialized. Actual matrix entries <=sum_edges L_i L_j. High-degree nodes cause multiplication of child messages, not a Cartesian expansion across their packet lists. Forest components independent by the same rank test. Global nonnegativity follows from the source, not from positivity of individual messages. Intermediate signed numbers may be negative. In particular a nonzero message/label is NOT a witness.

This new terminal exploits bounded packet interaction on a tree rather than bounded syndrome rank. It handles arbitrarily large edge dimensions, arbitrary tree degree, and explicit polynomial lists. It is not necessary that the original regional function be affine or coaffine. It differs from BD09's phased coset contraction: no convolution of child packet lists is required after the Cartesian certificate; labels are fixed original local term IDs and edges carry a finite numeric matrix. It differs from BA07, which enumerates all assignments of the joint overlap. It is a restricted weighted tree contraction theorem with a polynomial acquisition test, not a priority claim.

## D. Controls and limitations
Let P_r(x)=[first two bits all zero] OR [remaining r-2 bits all zero], r>=4. It has a three-term affine list by inclusion-exclusion, and |P_r|=2^(r-2)+3. Both polarities affinely span the whole r-bit block: P contains both coordinate subspaces; complement is the product of the nonzero words of two groups of dimension >=2, each affinely spans its group. P has no nonzero translation period: if h has a nonzero component in one group choose that group zero, the other nonzero both before and after (possible in dimension>=2). Then the truth value changes. Its two polarity cardinalities differ, so no translation complements P. Hence on a path the relation P_r(left)=P_r(right) depends on the full 2r-dimensional semantic observation space. It also has an explicit 16-term list 1-P_l-P_r+2P_lP_r; each term is Cartesian across its two edge blocks. Endpoints P/P give (2^(r-2)+3)^m accepted words for m edges; endpoints P/not P give zero. Root local affine hulls of endpoints are full; middle relation has full hull since it contains the product of the full-hull P supports. Thus even the full local affine hull closure is stagnant, although global closure/terminal can decide. Total joint overlap is mr. This is a real high-rank opposite-label family, but not a hard family: the tree contraction is the cheap escape. Star with leaves P_r and center excluding only the all-zero concatenated word yields (2^(r-2)+3)^m-1 with unbounded degree, two center terms. Center terms are Cartesian.

Hostile check: a term [x=y] on two independent one-bit edge blocks has full single-edge projections, but their product is the full square, not the diagonal. Reject the packet-product promise; UNKNOWN, not UNSAT. In a general registry the rank test also rejects hidden linear dependence among edge blocks. There is no universal guarantee of a forest, a direct-sum registry, Cartesian projected packets or polynomial explicit packet acquisition. BW11 proves arbitrary 3SAT survives joint gluing of locally simple wheel projections. The current theorem is a complementary restricted terminal, not a way to evade that result. BD11 peeling can reduce the registry first; do not infer counts from its decision-only lift. DO11 Hall-deficiency conservation unaffected.
