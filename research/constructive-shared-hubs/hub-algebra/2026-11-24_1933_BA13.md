## A. Exact graphic rank-one private projection (PROVED)
Work in a consistent affine chart with protected exterior coordinates z and private p. Initial affine equations can be row-reduced while retaining actual exterior equations and private lift; for this theorem the chart and graphic basis must be supplied or recognized directly, not discovered by an uncharged change of basis. A forbidden packet is a system of linear equations in (z,p). If its private rowspace has rank zero it is an exterior-only forbidden affine packet. If rank one, row operations produce exterior-only equations defining activation g_i(z), together with a single private equation d_i p = b_i(z). The complement of the packet is exactly NOT g_i(z) OR d_i p = 1+b_i(z). If g_i is inconsistent drop the packet; if g_i is identically true, the escape equation is unconditional. Rank-zero contradictory or universal packets handled directly. Restrict to rank-one d_i of the form e_u+e_v or e_u (the latter uses a reference vertex whose bit is zero), and any required signed graphic equalities. All other packets cause this terminal to decline or remain outside the chosen private region, with their actual consumers protected. No remaining private quotas, multiplicities or other hidden constraints.

For a fixed z, each activated packet is exactly a tagged parity edge. Add an unconditional edge for every required private graphic equality, with reference pins represented relative to vertex 0. Solve unconditional equations first by Gaussian/weighted union-find closure; archive each necessary row under its actual guard; restart after strict deductions. Contradictory unconditional equations reject; contracting them preserves signs on remaining edges, including loops and parallels. After evaluating a particular exterior guard, all newly active equations are required in THAT fiber only; propagate and reclose until stagnation. No branching is needed for the private graph. A spanning forest assigns private bits, verifies every edge, and reconstructs the original chart. In a component containing reference 0 choose its bit zero; other component gauges arbitrary. A contradiction is an odd-XOR active cycle, with a verifiable path certificate.

Uniform symbolic version: form graph on (u,b), b in GF2. Edge i contributes an undirected link (u,b) to (v,b+s) under Boolean predicate g_i(z) AND [1+b_i(z)=s], for s=0,1. Proof: a path from (u,0) to (u,1) telescopes to an odd closed walk; conversely any parity inconsistency yields such a walk using a spanning forest. If no such walk exists, the component labels give a consistent potential. ## B. Flat affine-packet projection can require exponential size (PROVED)
For every k>=1, take private p_0,...,p_k and exterior pairs (a_i,b_i), i=1..k. Impose p_0+p_k=1. For each i impose the two implications
 a_i=1 -> p_(i-1)=p_i,
 b_i=1 -> p_(i-1)=p_i.
These are rank-one private packets (forbidden conjunction of guard bit one and private difference one), or two 3-CNF clauses per implication. Private graph has at most degree four (parallel wires counted, endpoints degree three). Source size O(k) rows, O(k log k) standard indexed bits. No strict physical transfer is asserted. Required endpoint equation is included in unconditional closure. A compatible private assignment exists iff some i has a_i=b_i=0. If every layer active, equality propagates across the path contradicting the endpoint inequality. Otherwise choose a cut at an inactive layer, set the private potential constant on each side with different values; this is a verified lift. Thus the exact exterior REJECTION set is Q_k=(GF2^2 minus {00})^k, of size 3^k. Both accepted and rejected exterior words occur.

Suppose the exact projection is represented on the SAME exterior chart by a conjunction of complements of affine packets, with no new auxiliaries and no signed cancellation. Its rejection support is the union of their affine flats. Each nonempty flat L must lie wholly inside Q_k. For each coordinate pair, the affine image of L lies inside the three nonzero words, so has affine dimension <=1. The joint coordinate projection is injective, therefore dim L <= k and |L| <= 2^k. Consequently at least ceil((3/2)^k) packets are required, even permitting arbitrary overlaps and arbitrary dense affine equations. The same lower bound applies to nonnegative sums of packet indicators with exact rejection support; not to signed sums, circuits, new auxiliary variables, or unrestricted SAT algorithms. This is exponential in k, superpolynomial in source bit length. It refutes universal polynomial flattening under the stated representation restriction, not P=NP. This family is EASY: direct support circuit OR_i [a_i=b_i=0] has O(k) gates. In fact BD13 can use the scalar nonnegative leaf f=sum_i [a_i=b_i=0], nonzero iff an exterior word extends, with only k explicit terms and no new guard enumeration. Signed zero indicators or arithmetic products are different representations. If further exterior constraints exist, joint acquisition/width must be paid again.
