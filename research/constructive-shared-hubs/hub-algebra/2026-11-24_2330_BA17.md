## 1. Exact simultaneous phase criterion and aggregate decomposition
Let U_i=<a_i,b_i> be rank-two observation planes over F2, W=sum U_i. A simultaneous phase is a linear J:W->W with J²+J+I=0 and J(U_i)=U_i. On each plane J must be one of the two 3-cycles of nonzero rows: rho_i(a_i)=b_i, rho_i(b_i)=a_i+b_i, or rho_i+I. Write J|U_i=rho_i+eps_i I. Compute a basis B from the actual labeled rows, express all rows in B, and impose equality between their proposed image and the same combination of the proposed images of B. This is an explicit linear system in m bits eps_i, with O(m dim ambient) scalar equations. Consistency is sufficient: extend from B, then the relation J²+J+I vanishes on every owner plane and hence W. Necessity immediate. This is deterministic Gaussian acquisition, not guessed semantic equivalence.
Build dependency graph on owners: join the owner of each row to every owner of a basis row with nonzero coefficient in its expansion. If two solutions differ, D=J+J' acts by lambda_i I on U_i. Unique coordinates in B show lambda_i=lambda_j along each dependency edge. Conversely any componentwise constant labels define such a D. Thus a consistent chart has exactly 2^c phase choices for c graph components, and W is the direct sum of the component observation spaces (each is spanned by its own basis rows). This decomposition also holds when phase equations are inconsistent. It is independent of the choice of basis as a partition into finest direct-sum owner blocks: any direct decomposition forces all fundamental dependencies to remain inside blocks. Independent local models glue by Gaussian solution of the combined observed basis values, including the affine lift after actual guard absorption. This is an exact conjunctive reduction; no independent marginal approximation. Positive rank masses M_j sum to M, so with mu=1+M, c_exponent=2, sum_j(1+M_j)^2 <= (1+M)^2-1 for at least two nonempty blocks. Per-node discovery and lifts polynomial; universal decomposition false.

## 2. Complete characterization on linear ternary NAE
For distinct vertices u,v,w an NAE owner has U=<e_u+e_v,e_u+e_w>; signs affect its forbidden value, not the plane. A linear triple hypergraph admits a simultaneous phase iff its bipartite variable-owner incidence graph is a forest. If acyclic, each tree can be peeled by leaf owners (root at a variable, take a farthest owner with two private variable leaves), proving the owner planes form a direct sum. Any local phase orientations extend. Each owner can take any of its three permitted words; Gaussian lifting gives an exact SAT terminal, already covered by DS15 rank-Hall.
If cyclic, take a shortest incidence cycle with k owner vertices. Linearity implies k>=3. Each of its triples contains two consecutive cycle variables and a third outside the cycle; otherwise a chord shortens the cycle. The third vertices are pairwise distinct: adjacent owners sharing one would violate linearity, and nonadjacent sharing one gives a shorter cycle via the shorter arc. The k pair rows along the cycle sum to zero. Each phase sends the cycle pair to one of the two other pairs, involving its private third vertex. Their sum has every such third coordinate equal to one and is nonzero, contradicting linearity. Therefore even one cycle blocks simultaneous phase compatibility, independent of signs or actual SAT label. No source constraint has been deduced merely from this diagnostic.

## 3. Guard rank needed to repair phases on original planes
Let H be the span of all absorbed affine guard and necessary-equation rows, rank h. For each vertex-disjoint loose cycle, either some owner plane drops rank in the quotient by H, giving a nonzero vector of H supported on that cycle's vertices, or all remain rank two. If their images have compatible phases, apply J to their zero cycle sum: the sum of the corresponding original rotated rows belongs to H and has all third-vertex coordinates nonzero. These nonzero vectors from vertex-disjoint cycles are independent. Thus h >= packed cycle count even if rank-dropped owners are removed, but NOT if arbitrary nonlocal rewrites delete intact planes. For genuine degree-four positive four-perfect-partition charts, greedily find shortest incidence cycles and remove all vertices of their owners and all incident owners. Until at least n/12 variable vertices have been removed, at least n of the original 4n/3 owners remain, since each removed vertex hits at most four. The residual incidence graph has average degree at least 3 (3m' edges, at most n+m' vertices), at most 7n/3 vertices. Prune degree <=1 vertices preserving average >=3. The irregular Moore bound (Alon-Hoory-Linial, DOI 10.1007/S003730200002) gives shortest cycle length O(log n). Each greedy step deletes twice its Berge length in variable vertices, O(log n). Thus h=Omega(n/log n) for any such direct guard linearization retaining intact planes. Shortest cycles and their supports are polynomially discoverable. This does not lower-bound arbitrary decision or transformed representations. Single pins tested separately; sign/guard provenance retained.

## 4. Even successful F4 synchronization leaves a universal decision problem
Graph 4-coloring reduces to homogeneous rank-two packets forbidding X_u+X_v=0 for X_v in F4. Multiplication by omega induces a known simultaneous J on the binary observation rows (choose the same local phase convention everywhere). Each simple-graph edge has distinct rank-two plane and its quadratic violation has a cross monomial a_u b_v unique to that edge, so singleton/same-plane/polar closure stagnates. 4-coloring is NP-complete by adding a universal vertex to 3-coloring. DS15 exact rank-Hall core extraction preserves the known J and this closure property, since the remaining owner subspaces are J-invariant and the retained rank is computed on those same rows. Nonempty returned cores satisfy the strict deficiency condition. For empty SAT cores use a fixed strict SAT chart: K5 with one edge forbidding difference 1 instead of 0; endpoints share a color, the other three use the three other colors. Fixed strict NO is K5 itself. For K5 all ten edges have rank eight; proper connected subgraphs on <=4 vertices have deficiency <=0, proper connected spanning subsets <=1, and disconnected proper subsets <=0. Thus full deficiency two is strictly maximal, independently of signs. This is a uniform many-one reduction to phase-compatible rank-two strict cores with stagnant local/polar closure. The known phase is not a polynomial solver. This uses unrestricted physical degree and does not contradict BW15; no claim of hardness for the native degree-feasible exclusion subclass.

## 5. Cooperative nonlinear escape, not just a phase obstruction
Use DR17 mask rules R(i;j,k): z_i in {z_j,z_k}, and actual mask pins z_d=0 and pinned coordinates zero. Any feasible flip set S is closed under Horn rule {j,k}->i and satisfies i->j OR k. A directed minority cycle gives a candidate seed C (not necessarily a feasible mask). Its least Horn closure H(C) is included in every legal mask containing C, by induction on the Horn additions. If it reaches a forbidden coordinate then this entire conditional seed is impossible, not the source. If it also satisfies the disjunctive rules it is an exact common flip, verified on both actual side words. Enumerate each directed arc and one BFS shortest return path avoiding forbidden vertices, run closure, deduplicate verified masks; O(m) candidates and O(poly(N)) work. Attempt immediate common word, then at most eight candidates with capped M23 plateau search (n² states each). This integrates nonlinear closure, graph geometry and matching-origin actual side guards; no promise that one of the selected cycles seeds every useful mask, no target-free guarantee. The exact Horn-closure condition is sound but incomplete; no universal repair claim. Initial local source closure on unguarded/one-pin rank-two charts is stagnant as verified in primary checker; conditional Horn closure paid on every candidate to fixed point. A successful verified common word terminates the source, no need to acquire further semantic equations. These are speculative moves and no conditional equation escapes its guard.
