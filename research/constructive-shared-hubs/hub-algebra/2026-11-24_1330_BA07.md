## 1. Minimum exact linear value quotient (PROVED)
Let d explicitly represented Boolean coordinates z parameterize a consistent affine guard by Gaussian elimination. Input f(z)=sum_{i=1}^L c_i 1_{A_i z=b_i}, with explicit affine systems and binary integer weights c_i (rational weights also by clearing denominators). Inconsistent terms vanish. For composition assume f nonnegative pointwise, authenticated by source construction, not by an uncharged generic positivity check. Period computation itself works for arbitrary signed f. Define unnormalized Walsh transform F(w)=sum_z f(z)(-1)^{w dot z}. For term i choose particular solution a_i, coefficient row space V_i, rank r_i. Its transform is c_i 2^(d-r_i) (-1)^{w dot a_i} 1_{w in V_i}, by character orthogonality on the homogeneous kernel. Full Fourier support may be exponential.

Let W=span{w:F(w)!=0}. Fourier inversion implies f(z+h)=f(z) for every z iff h is orthogonal to W: each Fourier coefficient is multiplied by (-1)^{w dot h}, and independent characters cannot cancel across different frequencies. Thus W is the unique smallest coefficient space of any linear observation quotient preserving every value of f. For any affine map preserving f, its kernel is contained in W-perp; its row space contains W. Conversely observations on a basis of W suffice. For Boolean f this also preserves exact feasibility. For nonnegative count-valued f the minimal support-only quotient can differ.

## 2. Polynomial deterministic spectral discovery without Fourier enumeration (PROVED)
For arbitrary affine frequency query H, compute E(H)=sum_{w in H} F(w)^2. Expand the square over at most L^2 ordered term pairs. For i,j intersect H with V_i and V_j by Gaussian elimination, obtaining an empty set or a+K. The sign sum over it is zero if a_i+a_j is not orthogonal to K; otherwise it is (-1)^{a dot (a_i+a_j)} 2^dim K. Multiply by c_i c_j 2^(2d-r_i-r_j), sum exactly. All cancellations are accounted for before comparison with zero. E(H)>0 iff H contains some nonzero Fourier coefficient (including frequency zero if permitted). Coefficients and intermediate sums have O(d+log L+B) bits for B=max coefficient bit length. No positivity of f required.

Starting W0=0, take basis of W0-perp. Test each frequency constraint w dot v=1; if all energies zero then support is contained in W0. Otherwise fix that constraint and greedily pin each frequency bit to zero if the remaining energy is positive, else to one. The resulting actual frequency has nonzero transform and is outside W0. Insert into basis, repeat. At most d independent additions, O(d^2+d) energy calls (constant factors include each orthogonal probe and bit). Pair intersection and energy are polynomial in d,L,B. No black-box sampling or semantic SAT oracle. This computes the minimal value separator even when all Fourier coefficients are not listed. Any nonzero frequency returned can be certified by the signed sum formula. The all-zero and constant cases both have W=0 and are distinguished by mass/value.

If desired, materialize an explicit signed sum on quotient coordinates: average every affine term over a fiber of the map with row space W. Each term becomes its projected affine indicator times 2^{-s}, where s is the rank of its equations modulo W; Gaussian elimination obtains the projected constraints. Because f itself is constant on fibers, the resulting sum is exact. No term count increases. Keep rational dyadic weights and clear denominators when needed. This optional rewrite is symbolic, not executed.

## 3. Exact joint overlap separator (PROVED)
For k independently realized regional factors f_i on a common normalized chart, compute their respective minimum observation spaces W_i. Independence here means at fixed z there is no unrepresented private coupling; a Boolean projected factor records existence of its own private lift. Count-valued factors may encode multiplicity only when actual private choices are independent. Define I_i = W_i intersect sum_{j != i} W_j and U=sum_i I_i, r=dim U. This is polynomial Gaussian discovery, not a supplied variable partition.

The W_i modulo U are jointly linearly independent: if sum_i w_i lies in U, write it as sum_i u_i, u_i in I_i. Then w_i+u_i is the sum of the other w_j+u_j, hence lies in I_i, so w_i lies in U for every i. Thus on each complete assignment eta of the observations U, the restrictions of the uniform chart to the remaining private observations of each W_i are independent. Unobserved coordinates contribute only uniform fiber size.

## 5. Sharp barrier: minimal support quotient of count-valued sums (PROVED)
Given arbitrary 3CNF F(x), let g(x)=sum_C [C false], and f(x,t)=g(x)+t (t Boolean). This is a nonnegative explicit sum of affine indicators, polynomial input size, no signed cancellation needed. The direction e_t is a period of the support predicate [f>0] iff F is UNSAT: if F has a model x then f(x,0)=0 and f(x,1)=1; if F is UNSAT every x has g>=1 and both t values have positive weight. Non-invariance has an NP witness (an explicit word with differing support), so support-period membership is coNP-complete on this promised domain. Thus a universally polynomial minimum support separator for count-valued sums would imply P=NP. The value period remains easy to discover and always excludes e_t in this construction. No claim of P!=NP; no contradiction of DR07.

Also high value rank is not necessary for decision complexity: one point indicator on a full cube has full Fourier span, but trivial SAT and Gaussian count. Even two identical point indicators retain full joint rank, while an exact pair product is cheap. This excludes a universal low-rank assumption without excluding alternative terminals.
