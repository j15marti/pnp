## 3. BA08-A: exact factored squared spectral energy (PROVED)
For all explicit factor products above, choose a linear complement C to D in the direct sum. Find pivot coordinate positions of a row echelon basis of D and require those coordinates to be zero. D projects bijectively onto its delta pivot bits, so C is a complement and the restriction S:C -> image S is bijective. In particular C is a Cartesian product of local coordinate subspaces; arbitrary prefix pinning of its other coordinate bits stays a Cartesian product Q=prod Q_i. For u in C let
  K(u)=sum_{a in D} prod_i L_i(u_i+a_i).
By Fourier inversion, F(Su)=2^(d-M) K(u), where F is the unnormalized global Walsh transform, and F is zero outside image S. This scaling may be rational in intermediate expressions, but all K are integers and nonzero tests unchanged.

For any product of local affine frequency queries Q_i (restricted to C) and global direction h, define
  E(Q,h)=sum_{u in Q} K(u)^2 (-1)^(h dot Su).
Expanding ONLY the two dependency sums yields
  E(Q,h)=sum_{a,b in D} prod_i [sum_{u_i in Q_i} L_i(u_i+a_i)L_i(u_i+b_i)(-1)^(h dot S_i u_i)].
No global Fourier support enumeration. Each bracket is computed by at most L_i_count^2 term pairs. For a term pair, intersect Q_i with the two shifted rowspaces (v+a_i in V_A, v+b_i in V_B). This is a Gaussian affine system. Its contribution is a constant signed power of two times a character sum on an affine fiber x+K0. The latter is zero unless the combined phase is orthogonal to K0, otherwise sign(x) times 2^dim K0. Includes all shifts, RHSs and exact cancellation. Products and sum have polynomial bit length: at most O(M+d+delta+sum_i(max coefficient bits + log term count)) bits up to constant factors. No approximate arithmetic, sampling, or guessed spectral support.

For a parity test h dot Su=1, restricted nonnegative energy is exactly
  E_1(Q,h)=(E(Q,0)-E(Q,h))/2.
This retains factorization despite a GLOBAL parity test, because each local character separates multiplicatively. Multiple arbitrary global parity conditions are not asserted cheap.

## 4. BA08-B: polynomial-query global minimum interface (PROVED)
Maintain an independent frequency basis B initially empty. For each h in a basis of B-perp evaluate E_1(C,h). If all vanish, every nonzero global frequency lies in B (otherwise one orthogonal-basis test detects it). Else choose positive h, then fix the free coordinate bits of u in C successively, keeping a positive E_1 on one prefix. Positivity partitions exactly by the next bit; at full prefix K(u) nonzero and h dot Su=1. Append Su, independent of B. Repeat at most d times. Uses O(d(d+M)) energy calls including failed tests. By BA07 Fourier independence, span B is the unique minimal exact VALUE observation space of f. Its perpendicular is exactly the translation period space. Empty B may mean constant nonzero or zero, distinguished by coefficient at zero / total norm.

## 6. BA08-D: unrestricted period acquisition is coNP-complete even with guaranteed SAT (PROVED)
For arbitrary 3CNF F(x), introduce selector bits u,t. Define Boolean f(x,u,t)=(not u OR t) AND product over clauses C of (not u OR C). This is product of 1-minus-single-affine-bad-event factors of rank at most four. Always has satisfying assignments at u=0. At u=1, f=t F(x). Therefore translation e_t is a period iff F is UNSAT. Checking failure of period has an NP certificate (two evaluations at x and x+e_t). Thus period decision on this promised-SAT input class is coNP-complete, and a universal polynomial minimum value-interface discovery algorithm implies P=NP. In this Boolean example support and value are the same. This rules out assuming cheap global cancellation or low output rank without a parameter or proof. It is not a separation, nor a lower bound on arbitrary SAT algorithms.
