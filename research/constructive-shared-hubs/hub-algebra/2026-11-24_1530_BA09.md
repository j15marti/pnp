## 2. BA09-A: exact private-fiber projection and Booleanization (PROVED)
For every authenticated local relation R(y,z), after Gaussian parameterization of any protected affine chart, z in GF(2)^d is the complete external word (all cross-region observations retained), y in GF(2)^m private. Assume R is supplied or acquired at charged cost as a flat signed integer sum of L affine indicators, pointwise Boolean by a source certificate. More generally R>=0 suffices for decision and positive weighted lift, not physical counting. All private variables of different regions must be disjoint conditional on the shared chart. A nonaffine external constraint, cost, quota, synchronization or tag cannot be erased; retain it as a factor or put its actual exported values in z. In particular, neither S06 flow nor DP01 count-box domains are automatically flat affine lists. Arbitrary source-to-flat compilation is not assumed polynomial.

Define f(z)=sum_y R(y,z). For a term c*[Ay+Bz=b], let r=rank A and let J span the left kernel of A. Its projected term is c*2^(m-r)*[JBz=Jb]. The term is zero off that affine event, by exact Gaussian solvability, and counts all 2^(m-r) lifts on it. Project each term, merge identical RREF keys and cancel zero coefficients. This works despite signed terms and overlaps; f is pointwise nonnegative because of the source contract. It retains at most L affine keys. It is not a Boolean relation in general.

Fix integer b>=1 independent of input length. Let M_j=sum_z f(z)^j, M_0=2^d. Compute j=1..2b+1 via flat signed products: intersection of affine events by Gaussian elimination and identical-key merging, exact count 2^(d-rank) for each consistent term. Define H_ij=M_(i+j+1), 0<=i,j<=b. Solve over rationals Hc=0, c_0=1. For q(X)=sum c_i X^i,
  c^T H c = sum_z f(z) q(f(z))^2.
Since f>=0, this is zero iff q vanishes at every positive attained f. Thus the system has a solution iff there are at most b distinct positive f values (including empty support). Necessity is the polynomial root bound; sufficiency q(X)=prod_(a>0 attained)(1-X/a), padded with zeros. No promise or externally supplied multiplicity list is needed for soundness. Failure returns UNKNOWN for this terminal, not UNSAT.
  g(z)=1-q(f(z)) = 1_[exists y R(y,z)>0].
In particular g has a signed affine list of at most sum_(j=1)^b L^j terms before cancellation; coefficients are exact rationals, with a common positive denominator. Zero support and full support can be certified by exact sum g, without checking every z. This is a pointwise identity, NOT a comparison of marginal moments of different regions (DR09). Private source witness reconstruction later uses R itself, not an arbitrary signed term.

## 3. BA09-B: discover the minimum linear support interface (PROVED)

is obtained by ordered term pairs: intersect Q with the two rowspaces, multiply signed amplitudes, and sum the character (-1)^((a_i+a_j) dot w) on that affine intersection. A character sum on x+K is zero unless its phase annihilates K, in which case it is (-1)^(phase dot x)2^dim K. All steps Gaussian. No per-frequency enumeration.
Start B=0. For each h in a basis of B^perp test E({w:h dot w=1}). If every test is zero, actual spectral support lies in B. Otherwise find a nonzero frequency outside B by successively restricting its coordinate bits while maintaining positive energy. Add that frequency to B. At most d iterations; O(d^2+d) queries including failed probes. The final B is span of actual Walsh support (not raw term scopes). By Fourier inversion translation periods are precisely B^perp, so B is the unique minimal linear observation rowspace through which g factors. Any linear quotient g(z)=h(Lz) has rowspace L containing B. ## 5. Separation of count rank and support rank (PROVED)
Let z=(u,w), u in GF(2)^2, w in GF(2)^(d-2), m=d+1 private bits including t and d free bits. R= [u!=0] * ([t=0]+[t=1][w=0]). It is a genuine Boolean nonaffine relation, four signed affine terms. Then f=2^d*[u!=0]*(1+[w=0]), two distinct positive multiplicities 2^d,2^(d+1). Its support is g=[u!=0]. For d>=3 support invariance forces any translation's u component zero, and count invariance then forces w component zero by evaluating at w=0 and nonzero u. Thus count rank d, support rank exactly 2. The projected selector count is 3*2^(d-2). This is a source-level lift with actual private freedom, not just rescaling an arbitrary numerical function.
Stronger symbolic composition control: k such regions with independent two-bit u_i, one shared r-bit w and disjoint private bits. Before Booleanization the sum of observation ranks is k(r+2), joint rank r+2k, nullity (k-1)r. Afterwards support observation ranks are 2 each and jointly independent, nullity zero. Exact projected count 3^k*2^r. This is a high-dependency cooperative escape, not a claim about DO09's unchanged raw critical NAE core. Not executed as multi-region benchmark.

Hostile control (PROVED): f(z)=1+sum_i 2^i z_i takes 2^d distinct positive values while its support is constant 1. It is the true private count of an explicit disjoint affine union: allow y=0, or highest set bit of private d-bit word y is i and z_i=1. Each class contributes 2^i, classes disjoint. No bounded degree annihilator is possible although decision and projection are easy by another route. This falsifies universal bounded-positive-multiplicity acquisition, not P=NP. Full-support rank may be zero even on a missed terminal. BA08 and BW09 already show general period/coverage acquisition is coNP-complete in their factored domains, so no claim of universal support quotient discovery.
