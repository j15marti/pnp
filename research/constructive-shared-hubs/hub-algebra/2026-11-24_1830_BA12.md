## A. Uniform fiber-density certificate (PROVED)
Input: explicit forbidden affine packets A_i={x:M_i x=b_i} over GF(2)^d, required consistent affine guard G, protected observation rows P (all actual exterior consumers). Ordinary CNF is the complement of one affine packet per clause. Native four-color signed edge exclusion is one forbidden rank-two packet on the actual two-bit vertex chart; no strict E4/B4 compiler is assumed. Run certified closure: empty forbidden packets drop; universal forbidden packets contradict; relative rank-one nonempty packets force the opposite of an independent row, with restart. This is owner-local, not full semantic closure.

Choose retained owners K, and let R=span(rows(G),P,rows(M_i):i in K). For deleted owner i define r_i=dim(R+row M_i)-dim R. For every consistent assignment to R extending G and satisfying K, its fiber is an affine coset of R-perp. Each forbidden packet has conditional probability either zero or exactly 2^(-r_i), by Gaussian rank-nullity. Therefore if D=sum_{i outside K} 2^(-r_i)<1, every such fiber has a simultaneous extension satisfying all deleted owners: the expected integer number of violated owners is <1. No independence or product of marginals assumed. Conversely every original model satisfies K. Thus projection onto (P, observations of K) is EXACT. A replacement retains only K and G, with the original observation map and a lift record; if K empty the exterior support is universal relative to G. Not count preserving. Strict inequality necessary for this certificate: two opposite singleton forbidden sets on one bit cover the chart at D=1 (rank-one closure detects contradiction).

Deterministic reconstruction: fix actual compatible values of R. At each free coordinate split current affine fiber into its nonempty halves (equal size if both nonempty). Compute exact expected bad-owner counts via sum of Gaussian intersection cardinalities / fiber cardinality. Choose a half with minimum expectation. It remains <1. At a point the count is a nonnegative integer and hence zero. Verify all original source owners, including those removed by initial closure. O(d*m) Gaussian queries, no enumeration of P. Conditional lift pins are not unconditional forced deductions.

## B. Paid discovery and small-core parameter (PROVED)
Start R=span(G,P), K empty. Recompute ranks and D. If D>=1, choose a remaining owner of minimum relative rank (fixed source order ties), transfer it to K, enlarge R by its rows, repeat. At most m transfers, O(m^2) rank calculations (rank once per round also charged). Stop at D<1, even if K=all. This is a deterministic sufficient certificate, not optimal core discovery. At fixed exterior valuation, rho=dim R-dim span(G,P) is the additional core rank. Enumerating 2^rho core observation words with exact linear checks, then lifting one feasible word, decides the fiber in 2^rho poly(N); rho=O(log N) sufficient. To replace a region in the cooperative solver, one need not enumerate exterior valuations at all: retain explicit K predicates on the chart, discard the other owners, and store their conditional lift. This is nonaffine support projection, not a claim that a high-rank retained core can always be solved.

## C. Hybrid independent-row / density certificate (PROVED)
After fixing R, choose one indexed row v_i of M_i for each selected deleted owner i in E, with all v_i jointly independent modulo R. Assign v_i the opposite of its forbidden RHS. These are constructive escape choices, NOT necessary equations. Any valuation of R extends to these choices by independence. Put R'=R+span(v_i:i in E). If sum_{i outside K union E} 2^(-dim((R'+row M_i)/R'))<1, the previous conditional-expectation proof lifts every retained fiber through the escape choices. This strictly combines the full independent-representative and raw density sufficient tests: E empty recovers density; no remaining owners recovers Rado-style extension. A simple polynomial partial discovery tries relative rank-one/two owners in fixed order, choosing an independent row and recomputing the bound; if no such row or no sufficient certificate, decline. Optimal discovery not claimed. Independence certificate prevents accidentally imposing a coupling on protected P. If choosing a row worsens other ranks, recomputation pays the actual cost.

Concrete separation: four rank-two blockers on disjoint bit pairs plus 2r distinct forbidden points on another r-bit block, r>=5. Initial sum is 1+2r/2^r>1; full Rado saturation fails because 2r>r. Escape one or two of the four rank-two blockers leaves residual sum <1, so the hybrid solves the complete system without retaining a core. This is a restricted algorithmic improvement, not hardness of other solvers.

Extension (symbolic only): for an authenticated Boolean rejection f_i=sum_t c_it 1_Ait, upper-bound its fiber mean by sum over positive coefficients c_it*2^(-r_it), ignoring negative terms safely. When the bound is <1 use exact signed Gaussian intersection sums in conditional expectation. Do not interpret signed labels as actual witnesses or assume Booleanity/source equivalence for free. The independent-row escape part requires a single forbidden conjunction or a separately certified sufficient escape condition.
