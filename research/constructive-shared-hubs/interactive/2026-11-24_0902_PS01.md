## 2. Consolidated earlier results: typed tetrahedra and Point #7

## 3. Exact NAE cycle-syndrome source compiler (PROVED)
For signed ternary NAE with distinct variables per clause, let G be the bipartite variable/clause incidence graph with n variables, m clauses, c components, 3m edges. Use one bit per occurrence, signs s_e. Literal bits y=s+x at occurrences. The three local admissible literal patterns modulo simultaneous clause complement are represented by a single selected minority occurrence. A vector of minority choices z (one per clause) can be made consistent by variable and clause flips iff z+s is in the cut space of G. Let C be a full row-rank fundamental cycle basis matrix. Orthogonality of cycle and cut spaces gives C z=C s. Conversely reconstruct variable and clause flips by integrating z+s on spanning trees, checking all non-tree edges. All assignments satisfy exact original NAE.
Thus local factor at clause j is {C_e: e incident to j}; its three columns XOR to zero. If its plane V_j has rank 2, factor is V_j minus zero. If rank <=1, the factor is all V_j, since at least one column zero and the other two equal. Syndrome dimension r=2m-n+c; also obtained by quotienting occurrence-equality syndrome of rank 3m-n_active by m-c_active local complement freedoms. Full cycle-basis acquisition is polynomial.

General 3SAT reduction: introduce one shared reference bit z; each original clause a OR b OR c becomes NAE4(a,b,c,z). Set z=0 for original models, use global complement symmetry to normalize any NAE model to z=0. Split NAE4 into NAE(a,b,t) AND NAE(not t,c,z) with private t: if a=b, the first forces t opposite, and the second excludes precisely c=z=a; if a!=b, choose t to satisfy the second. Each NAE3 is B4 with a private fourth port, and equality of occurrence copies can be implemented by signed E4 chains with unused ports existentially private. This gives a logical reduced-port compilation; exact physical four-color distributed-wheel routing/geometry not asserted.

## 4. PS01 punctured sumset theorem (PROVED)
Let V,W be nonzero binary subspaces and P(V)=V minus {0}. For independent factors with additive syndrome constraints, d=dim(V intersect W).
- d=0: direct sum, retain factored product.
- d=1: P(V)+P(W)=(V+W) minus {h}, where h is the unique nonzero intersection vector. Equivalently h+P(V+W).
- d>=2: P(V)+P(W)=V+W.
Proof: for s in V+W, all decompositions s=v+w form an affine fiber of size 2^d; at most two candidates excluded, v=0 and w=0. For d=1 both excluded iff s=h (for s=0 they coincide); for d>=2 at least two candidates survive. Offsets add. This is a strict symbolic compression beyond explicitly enumerated BA02 pair tables.
If projecting P(V) by pi with V intersect ker pi nonzero, every image fiber has >=2 elements, so pi(P(V))=pi(V). Otherwise the projection remains punctured. A full affine sumset is an absorbable freedom and can be quotiented out using section 2. Exact finite-termination algorithm: repeatedly absorb full spaces and project every remaining factor; absorb each factor whose dimension drops; fuse any intersecting pair, shifting target by h for d=1 or absorbing their span for d>=2. Recompute after each change until pairwise-disjoint punctured spaces remain. Test all pairs deterministically. Each accepted action removes >=1 active factor; initial count <=m. Bit-vector bases have O(r^2) bits; total source factor dimensions <=2m and never increase, even through fusion; polynomial Gaussian discovery including failed scans. No branch or full table enumeration in closure. Charge all preprocessing, basis transformations, snapshots, failed checks and reverse lift. A conservative poly(N) time/space ledger suffices; e.g. O(N^6) time and O(N^3) bits of retained state/snapshots on same sequential realization, CM<=T*S, with simple bit-level elimination and explicit input size N. Residual DP may still be exponential. Witness lift symbolically: reverse full-space absorption by linear preimage solve, choose a nonzero preimage where a puncture disappeared, and reverse pair fusion by solving v+w=s and avoiding at most two excluded choices. A polynomial lift follows for decisive closure; implemented code checks decisions, not full reverse witness reconstruction. Do not use projected tag equality to retire source rows or infer counts.

## 5. New infinite-family terminal: every signed NAE3 with variable occurrence <=2 (PROVED)
All clauses have distinct variables. Degree-one variable incidence is a bridge, with zero cycle column; its clause plane has rank <=1 and is initially free. Each degree-two variable has two identical columns (their sum is a cut vector). At closure fixed point, suppose a punctured group of k original clauses remains. Every constituent original rank-two plane still embeds injectively into the current group and quotient: the group was created only by d=1 fusions, and any subsequent rank drop would have absorbed it. Its dimension is k+1 by induction (two rank-2 factors join with exactly one shared dimension). None of its three columns per clause is zero. Each degree-two variable's other occurrence must belong to the same group: if its other clause was absorbed the column is zero in quotient; if in another active group the two groups intersect nontrivially. Degree-one variables cannot occur in such a group.
Consequently pair the 3k ports into 3k/2 internal variables, viewed as edges of a loopless cubic multigraph on these k clauses. If it has c' components, the k clause relations (sum of three incident columns zero) have rank k-c' on the edge-label space (ordinary binary incidence matrix). Hence group dimension <=3k/2-(k-c')=k/2+c'<=k, since each component has >=2 vertices. Contradiction to dimension k+1. Thus no punctured group remains, and since initial factors' spans generate the syndrome space, all syndromes are absorbed. Every signed NAE3 instance of this degree-two class is SAT and a polynomial lift exists. This is an exact algebraic terminal, independent of rim discovery, girth and treewidth; not a claim of first known tractability for this class.

Stronger density consequence: in a connected source incidence component containing high-degree variables, any remaining punctured group of k clauses needs at least k+2 occurrences h of variables of degree >=3. Degree-two variables must be paired internally as above. There are (3k-h)/2 such pairs and h high occurrences. Their formal column labels have k independent clause relations, because every component of the internal degree-two graph must meet a high port (otherwise a closed source component). Group rank <=(k+h)/2, whereas rank=k+1. Hence h>=k+2. Genuine residual defects require high-multiplicity synchronization; no universal cap on h follows.

## 6. Falsifier: infinite stagnant source family (PROVED)
For even n>=4 take clauses NAE(x_i,x_(i+1),x_(i+2)) modulo n. Alternating assignment satisfies. Incidence graph has a Hamiltonian cycle alternating variable and clause via shifts 0 and 1, plus the shift-2 perfect matching. No bridge. Any two-edge cut would split the Hamiltonian cycle into contiguous intervals with no crossing third-matching edge. Choose smaller interval; it has length <=n and even length if closed under the matching. Its complement has length >=n>=4. If the interval begins at variable v_i, its shift-2 matched clause is three positions earlier on the cycle; if it begins at clause c_i, the next variable v_(i+1) has its shift-2 partner three positions earlier. In either case the matching crosses the cut, contradiction. Hence 3-edge-connected. In a binary cycle basis two different nonzero incidence columns can be equal only when their two-edge set is a cut. Every clause has rank-two plane containing precisely its three nonzero columns. Distinct clause planes are disjoint. No raw PS01 pair fusion or projection absorption is available. Rank n+1 grows linearly. Fano UNSAT similarly stagnates in the executed control, all seven single-clause deletion neighbors SAT. This only falsifies universal coverage by these rules, not a lower bound on SAT or on the entire cooperative solver. DO03 proves stronger related raw barriers for ordinary 4-coloring. Guarded feedback, non-pair groupings, alternate algebra and other terminals remain legal.
