## 1. Exact nonaffine paired-owner compilation (PROVED)
Explicit signed ternary factors on distinct Boolean variables, spins s_v in {-1,+1}, occurrence signs a_ev in {-1,+1}. A mask forbids both constant literal words (full NAE), exactly one (single relaxed owner), or none. Input may contain protected unary pins. No other protected tag, affine guard, integer quota or physical constraint is discarded: such inputs need an authenticated extra interface or are outside the implementation.
Run local support closure on all actual factor rows before compilation, restart after every forced pin, and reach stagnation before any further choice. In the checker this is exact local ternary support/forced-coordinate propagation only, not semantic closure. An empty local support proves UNSAT. Retain all external pins; DP state extensions are conditional, not global deductions.

Let M be number of full NAE factors, and J_uv=sum over full factors containing u,v of a_eu a_ev. For each spin assignment the count of violated FULL factors B(s) satisfies
  4 B(s) = M + sum_{u<v} J_uv s_u s_v.
Each full factor contributes 3 when monochromatic, -1 otherwise; adding one yields 4 or 0, a nonnegative local loss. Signed coefficients may cancel across factors, but the identity is pointwise and total loss zero iff every full factor legal. Keep all single-mask factors as actual hard local relations, NOT as full NAE. This is exact for DO01 owner budgets 2/2 and 2/3 or 3/2, using original row IDs and spin-to-bit conversion. Under BW08/BH01 authenticated hub projection, the source assignment determines original relative hub state (x_u XOR x_v,x_u XOR x_w); verify three-state membership or the appropriate surviving owner. Physical private lifts require their recorded source maps and are not executed. In particular arbitrary protected BW08 zero pins do not become independent.
The terminal returns a projected count of satisfying source spin assignments, not number of physical wheel colorings.

## 2. Dense signed background and moment DAG (PROVED)
Choose any integer lambda. Write J_uv=lambda+R_uv, so
  4 B(s)=M+lambda ((sum_v s_v)^2-n)/2+sum_{u<v} R_uv s_u s_v.
Build correction graph on all vertices: edge for every nonzero R_uv; clique each hard single-mask relation (or other authenticated bounded-arity hard guard). Fix a vertex order. At prefix i frontier F_i contains assigned vertices with a future neighbor; let w=max |F_i|. A key consists of exact frontier spin word AND integer moment q_i=sum of assigned spins. Every residual interaction is evaluated when its later endpoint enters, every hard relation when its last endpoint enters. No omitted earlier bit can affect a future transition except through q_i. For each key store minimum partial signed correction energy E, number of histories attaining that minimum, and one actual history/backpointer. On equal keys retain smaller E, add multiplicities only on ties. This is sound even with negative residual weights: for any legal continuation both histories have identical future correction and final background, so lower E dominates. At final i=n compare M+E+lambda(q^2-n)/2 across all moment keys. The result is nonnegative, divisible by four, because each retained history is an actual assignment. SAT iff at least one final value zero; all zero-energy histories are counted because a discarded higher-energy history cannot be zero if a strictly lower energy legal history with same final key exists. Reconstruct and verify the original factors and pins, not just the energy. If no final state, UNSAT by hard constraints. This is a min-plus DAG, not an inclusion-exclusion count identity for arbitrary signed factors.

The key universe at prefix i is at most (i+1)2^|F_i| (q ranges -i,-i+2,...,i). Each key has at most two attempted extensions. For an acquired order the aggregate key and transition credit
  A=1+sum_i (i+1)2^|F_i|
bounds charged edge work up to poly(N) including each state comparison, integer count bit lengths O(n), signed weight arithmetic, saved history, source reconstruction, verification, failed transitions, and all discovery. T,S <= poly(N)2^w, CM <= (T+1)S on the same serial execution; no exact measured bytes/CM. Reserving per-layer/edge credit gives strictly decreasing arithmetic-job credit, not arbitrary SAT-child contraction. The implementation tries lambda=0 and all observed off-diagonal J values (polynomial many), both natural and greedy immediate-frontier orders, chooses least measured w, charges all candidates. A lambda absent among weights produces the complete correction graph, so cannot improve width over lambda=0. Plan discovery is polynomial, but the greedy order is not asserted optimal. Return UNKNOWN on width cap, never interpret it as UNSAT. With w=O(log N) the terminal is polynomial. Counts are exact integers; checker uses BigInt for counts, small safe integer energy on executed controls. The general theorem uses polynomial bit arithmetic. No cap UNKNOWN encountered in this suite.

## 3. General moment + local separator adapter (PROVED symbolic)
Suppose an explicitly acquired symmetric integer background matrix A (including any diagonal) of rank r and polynomial-bounded entries has off-diagonal J_uv-R_uv. Select r independent rows C=A[I,:] and an invertible principal submatrix A[I,I] (symmetric Gaussian elimination permits 1x1 or 2x2 pivots and such a principal basis). Then A=C^T A[I,I]^{-1} C; this follows because the selected columns span A and the selected-row restrictions determine their expansion. Maintain vector q=C s_prefix instead of scalar q. Final contribution is (q^T A[I,I]^{-1}q - trace A)/2. For |A_uv|<=B, each moment coordinate ranges [-nB,nB], so at most (2nB+1)^r possible moment keys. This yields T,S<=poly(N)(2nB+1)^r 2^w for acquired A and order, polynomial for fixed r, B=poly(N), w=O(log N). Neither discovery of a good A nor logarithmic r suffices unconditionally (bit-size alone does not bound numeric B). The exact rational inverse and bit lengths are charged. Not implemented. For DS08 supplied exact syndrome separator trees, local moment contributions add over disjoint source variables; retain the joint syndrome/boundary key PLUS moment vector and minimum correction cost. Add only disjoint assigned contributions, charge every attempted pair, and carry the original guards/lifts. This adapter is symbolic and requires an authenticated local energy/fiber table with its costs paid; no generic S06 owner or arbitrary affine pin is assumed free.

## 4. Fano and full Steiner systems (PROVED)
For a monotone Steiner triple system each pair occurs exactly once. Thus J_uv=1, lambda=1, R=0, w=0. With S=sum s_v and n vertices, M=n(n-1)/6,
  B(s)=[n(n-4)+3 S^2]/24.
For n=7, min B=1 since S is odd. This yields an exact nonaffine contradiction from one integer moment, without enumerating any hub or using PSD. A single relaxed owner can only admit a coloring with exactly one monochromatic triple, necessarily the designated one, in the permitted orientation. Then S=+/-1, so that triple's color occurs 3 or 4 times globally. There are 1+4=5 assignments per owner (none or one of the other four vertices has that color); all satisfy remaining factors because B=1. If n>7 for the executed systems n=15,31,63, min B>1 and no one-owner relaxation can suffice. For the relaxed terminal remove that triple's three pair terms and keep its actual one-sided clause; correction graph is one triangle, w<=2, regardless of n. This exact count and witness mechanism generalizes BH01's PSD certificate, which did not by itself characterize the one-sided support. Complete pair balance is not a high-girth property.

## 5. High-girth hostile theorem (PROVED)
For monotone linear 3-uniform H with Berge girth >4, the pair graph has no simple 4-cycle. Each cycle pair belongs to a unique hyperedge. Opposite pairs cannot be in the same triple; if all four hyperedges distinct this gives a Berge 4-cycle. If consecutive pairs share an edge, compress those two to a single adjacency between their outer endpoints. A second consecutive-pair compression would give two hyperedges sharing both remaining endpoints, violating linearity; otherwise the three distinct hyperedges form a Berge 3-cycle. Thus no 4-cycle. In particular any two vertices have at most one common neighbor.
For lambda=1, the correction graph is the complement of the pair graph. At the prefix consisting of n-2 vertices, with two future vertices, any already assigned vertex absent from the correction frontier is adjacent in the original pair graph to both future vertices. At most one such vertex exists. Therefore EVERY order has correction width >=n-3. For any nonzero lambda other than 1, every pair has nonzero correction (original weights 0 or 1), giving width n-1. More generally any proposed everywhere-nonzero background off-diagonal creates a correction edge for every original nonedge, so the n-3 bound still holds. This excludes the universal dense-baseline escape specifically on DO01 high-girth monotone inputs. It does not exclude sparse/low-rank backgrounds with zeros, local transformations, other representations, PSD, nonlinear algorithms, or lambda=0 low-width cases. No unrestricted lower bound. For the high-girth critical family, our new dense moment terminal has not solved the asymptotic obstruction.
