## B. Adaptive fields (PROVED)
Fix an explicit vertex order; P_i prefix, Q_i suffix. Store f_i(v)=sum_{u in P_i}J_uv s_u for each v in Q_i. Store a bit for each crossing hard OR indicating whether a true literal has appeared; unopened bits constant, closed bits must be true. Also store minimum prefix pair energy E_i=sum_{u<v in P_i}J_uv s_u s_v, count of histories attaining that minimum, and one actual prefix witness. All pins retained. For every possible continuation t the complete pair energy is
 E_i+ sum_{v in Q_i} f_i(v)t_v + sum_{v<w in Q_i}J_vw t_v t_w.
Thus two histories with identical field and OR status have identical continuation domains and suffix contributions. Keep the smaller prefix energy, sum multiplicities on equal minimum only. Extend with s at next vertex v by E'=E+s f_i(v); f'(w)=f_i(w)+s J_vw, update actual OR status and reject a closed false OR. The state key does not rely on a semantic oracle. Induction proves exact minimum over all hard-legal completions, with actual witnesses. At the final key, SAT iff M+E=0 (all full owners legal); no key means hard-mask UNSAT. If zero, all full legal histories are minimum, so tie multiplicity is exact source count. No count claim for native physical colorings. Min-plus dominance cannot lose a zero witness because any lower-energy history with same legal suffix would violate nonnegativity of total loss. The checker stores full field vectors and full prefix witness, not a compressed rank certificate.

## D. Separation from one fixed global background on same order (PROVED)
Take k complete tripartite NAE modules, each with A_i,B_i,C_i of d variables, one NAE for every triple with one variable per part. Share one actual vertex h of C_i across all modules (no other shared variables). Attach a Fano core on h and six additional vertices. Root UNSAT; relax one Fano budget gives SAT. Natural order h, other Fano vertices, then each A_i,B_i,C_i minus h. Every pair across different parts of one module has weight d, no other intermodule pair except h. Within active module processed non-h rows have at most three distinct suffix patterns; h contributes one additional row; completed module rows zero. Hence r<=4 in module cuts, and <=7 in the initial fixed Fano portion, h_i<=1 (one OR). No dependence of this rank bound on k or d.
Compare BH08 fixed background A plus correction graph. At cut just after A_i, if correction frontier w<d then some u_i in A_i has no correction edges to any future vertex. For any chosen v_i in B_i, A[u_i,v_i]=d; for i<j, A[u_i,v_j]=0. Thus k-by-k submatrix on selected rows/columns is lower triangular with nonzero diagonal, rank(A)>=k. Therefore on this SAME order either w>=d or rank(A)>=k, while moving fields have constant rank. This is a separation of parameter certificates, not an unrestricted hardness result or a statement about other orders or prior component preprocessing (articulation separation also handles this family).
