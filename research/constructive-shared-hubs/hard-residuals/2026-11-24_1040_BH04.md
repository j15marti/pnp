## 2. The representative-cycle terminal (PROVED; monotone square recognition is established literature)
Input is an explicit conjunction of finite NAE clauses on signed Boolean literals x_v XOR sigma and optional constants. Pins are fixed in the present guard. Designated free/protected interface variables may be excluded from the representative pool. All other constraints must be included or retained as protected roots. No arbitrary joint affine guard assumed. A pairwise signed synchronization guard can first be exactly substituted by parity union-find, with offsets and original-source lift; arbitrary higher-arity guards must be separately handled or the terminal must decline. This substitution extension is symbolic, not executed.

Certified closure first: simplify opposite signed duplicates (tautology), identical duplicates, pinned literals, clauses with both constant values (tautology), empty/constant-monochromatic constraints (reject), unary requirements (force the opposite of the known literal value). Restart after each force, and exhaust at stagnation. Analytic local support tests are polynomial for arbitrary NAE arity; code enumerates only the bounded local clause scopes used in tests. Conditional deductions stay in their pinned guard.

Suppose each remaining clause C has a distinct eligible representative variable r(C) occurring in it (a system of distinct representatives, SDR, found by ordinary bipartite maximum matching). The sign of r in C is sigma_r. For every other variable literal v with sign sigma_v add directed arc r->v with label p=1 XOR sigma_r XOR sigma_v. It means x_r XOR x_v=p. For a pinned/constant literal with value b add arc to a fixed zero root with label 1 XOR sigma_r XOR b. Unrepresented variables, including protected interfaces, have no outgoing arcs and are free or fixed roots. Distinct same-signed duplicates can be suppressed; opposite-signed duplicates were removed as tautologies. The source clause is satisfied iff at least one of the arcs out of its representative has its labeled equality satisfied. This is exact for every source assignment, not an affine relaxation. Every original variable has at most one clause as representative; all source clauses and signs are retained.

Theorem A: the SDR system is SAT iff EVERY sink SCC consisting of representative vertices has a directed SIMPLE cycle whose XOR of arc labels is zero. Sink SCCs that are unrepresented roots need no cycle.

Necessity: in a satisfying assignment each representative can select an outgoing satisfied arc. In a closed representative SCC this finite functional digraph has a directed simple cycle. Summing its actual equalities gives XOR zero.

Sufficiency: choose one zero-parity cycle in each closed representative SCC, give it a consistent binary coloring starting at either value, and assign arbitrary or supplied values to unrepresented roots. Every vertex can reach one of these chosen cycles or roots. Reverse BFS from the assigned vertices chooses for each previously unassigned vertex one arc to an already assigned vertex; set its bit to successor bit XOR arc label. All chosen arcs are satisfied and every representative clause has a satisfied arc. This works for EVERY assignment to all free roots simultaneously, not merely one witnessed assignment. Preserve all protected root values. Verify all source clauses and original pins. Counts are NOT preserved; the procedure constructs one lift per fixed root assignment.

The parity-zero SIMPLE cycle test is deterministically polynomial: subdivide every label-0 arc into two directed arcs using one private vertex; leave label-1 arcs single. Simple cycles correspond bijectively after contraction, and even length is equivalent to parity zero. Deduplicate identical parallel arcs if necessary; labels that differ retain distinct paths. Robertson, Seymour and Thomas, *Permanents, Pfaffian orientations, and even directed circuits*, Ann. Math. 150 (1999), 929–975, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9911268, gives a deterministic polynomial recognizer for even directed circuits; independently McCuaig (2004). Obtain an actual witness by deleting each edge whenever an even circuit still exists (at most linear many recognizer calls); the edge-minimal nonempty remainder is one even circuit. Do not substitute reachability on a parity double cover: an odd directed triangle traversed twice is an even closed walk but contains no even simple cycle. The 1999 result already treats square condensers; the contribution here is the guarded signed/external-root projection, Hall-core integration, owner repair and hostile-family application, not a claim to have discovered the even-cycle theorem.

## 3. Exact maximum Hall-deficit core (PROVED)
Let L be current active clauses, V the eligible unpinned/nonprotected representative variables, and N(J) the neighborhood of a clause set J in this bipartite graph. Find a maximum matching. From unmatched clause vertices take alternating reachability (clause to incident variable, variable to its matched clause), and let J be the reached clauses, W=N(J) the reached variables. All W are matched (otherwise an augmenting path), and their matched clauses belong to J. Every reached matched clause has its matched variable in W. Thus
    |J|-|W| = number of unmatched clauses = d,
    d = max_{X subset L} (|X|-|N(X)|).
The upper bound follows because any matching leaves at least |X|-|N(X)| unmatched clauses. Every exterior clause has a distinct representative outside W. Treat all core variables and protected variables as free/fixed roots. Apply Theorem A to exterior clauses alone. If an exterior closed SCC has no balanced cycle, the exterior conjunction itself is UNSAT independently of any root assignment. Otherwise every assignment to the core variables and protected roots extends through the exterior. Therefore the exact existential projection onto those variables is just the original core conjunction. J=empty is a paid terminal. A proper J is a paid exact unary contraction, with a stored reconstruction DAG of chosen successors and cycles, not a model-count-preserving transformation. If J=all and no other terminal resolves it, decline; arbitrary closure or projected tag additions require recertification. This extraction is not BH03 affine rank or S06 owner-type matching: it is a separate incidence matching followed by a genuine nonaffine signed directed-circuit test.

For a critical monotone NAE root without pins every proper subset is SAT. If d>0 and J proper, the exterior is SAT so all root assignments extend; the core is SAT by criticality, contradiction. Hence J=all. For m=n, the full deficit m-n is zero, so d=0. Therefore ALL connected edge-critical monotone NAE roots with m=n (including BH03 deficiency-one roots) have a perfect incidence matching and are deterministically polynomially decidable by the terminal, at any girth. More generally all SDR inputs, not just critical ones, are covered. This is a recognition/evaluation theorem, not a claim to efficiently recognize criticality by testing all unmatchable instances. For monotone connected critical roots all variables occur.

## 4. Direct all-owner repair and source compilation (PROVED)
DO01 each monotone NAE triple is exactly the two owner bounds 1<=x+y+z<=2; relaxing one bound gives NAE(x,y,z,b) for a fixed bit b, allowing just the formerly forbidden monochrome 1-b. The same holds for signed literal values. Thus the original paired-budget semantics, with that relaxation only, compile to signed representative arcs with a new fixed-root option. No independent clone assignments or owner scalarization. The compilation is linear in the explicit semantic source; private wheel/carrier witness extension is conditional on BW01's authenticated pointwise compiler. The strict physical compiler is not executed.

In a critical m=n root with SDR, at least one bad sink SCC exists. If proper, its own representative clauses involve only its internal variables and are an UNSAT proper subset. Criticality excludes that. Hence the entire representative digraph is strongly connected and has no balanced simple cycle. Conversely a strongly connected representative digraph without balanced simple cycles is UNSAT and each single-owner relaxation or whole-clause deletion is SAT: set the relaxed representative to the bit making its literal opposite the appended constant, then reverse BFS assigning every other representative using signed arc parity. Each other clause has its chosen opposite-literal arc. For a monotone clause the root value is 1-b. This direct all-neighbor lift does not need any cycle recognizer, or even the UNSAT premise, once strong connectivity and the SDR are verified; UNSAT is needed to infer criticality and that each relaxed clause must be monochrome. Reuse one authenticated matching and reverse adjacency for all 2m owner queries. O(m(n+L)) work including output and verification; streamed O(n+L) words and polynomial bit overhead. A SAT-neighbor witness is not an UNSAT-root proof.

## 5. Hostile high-girth control (PROVED)
The complete 3-uniform hypergraph on five vertices, K_5^(3), is edge-critical UNSAT: any 2-coloring has at least three vertices of one color; deleting one triple permits the 3/2 coloring with that triple as the sole monochromatic edge. Each of 20 owner relaxations has exactly one model. It has n=5,m=10,d=5, no representative matching, and local unary closure has no deductions. The entire root is its Hall core. Neighbor m,n unchanged, all clause neighborhoods unchanged, so their Hall deficit also equals five.

New counting lemma for DO02 high-girth stretching: any SAT inclusion-minimal two-terminal equality gadget K (distinct terminal u,v, all variables counted, no external constraints) enforcing u=v has m_K >= n_K-1. Exclude u,v from representative pool and apply Hall-core theorem with these ports as arbitrary free roots. K is satisfiable, so exterior bad SCC cannot exist. If the Hall core is proper, minimality supplies a model of the proper core with u!=v, which would extend through exterior, contradicting equality. Therefore J=all and d>0 (J=empty would allow arbitrary port values). Thus m_K>n_K-2. This proof does not claim small size or efficient construction of K as girth varies.

In DO02 incidence stretching every occurrence adds n_K-1 variables and m_K clauses, preserving or increasing m-n. Stretch K_5^(3) with the fixed high-girth equality gadgets of DO02 (for each fixed girth). Both labels and edge-criticality follow from DO02's local source/deletion lifts, and the resulting roots retain m-n>=5; no root SDR and the entire critical root is its Hall core. Every single-owner relaxation has same variable neighborhoods and no unary deductions, hence positive Hall deficit too. This gives arbitrarily high-girth simultaneous *raw* failures of the SDR terminal on both labels. It does not rule out proper-core extraction on neighbors, other channels, or arbitrary nonlinear rewrites. One may first insert DO02 weak vector equality chains (each link preserves m-n) to also retain its basic SDP obstruction; this combined asymptotic transfer is symbolic, not executed. No unrestricted hardness or universal diagonal hard sequence claimed.
