## 2. Hall-certified guarded regional projection (PROVED)
Domain: finite explicit homogeneous signed Boolean NAE factors on actual shared variables, arities <=3 after normalization (NAE2 allowed), with no hidden absolute pins, quota, tag or other private consumers. A candidate interior C includes EVERY factor touching it, and B is the complete external neighborhood in the primal graph. Protected variables cannot be put in C. All constraints not touching C remain in the outer problem. NAE means the signed literals are not all equal. Global simultaneous complementation preserves every factor.

For b=|B|>=1 choose anchor a in B, define relative word q_j=x_a XOR x_j. For q_j=p, add NAE2(a, x_j) for p=1 or NAE2(a, not x_j) for p=0. The incidence graph of these b-1 guard factors is independent of q. Suppose the augmented graph has a matching saturating all factors. Then *every* one of the 2^(b-1) parity queries can be decided in deterministic polynomial time (per query) by BH10 and the RST even directed simple circuit theorem.

Matching-preserving closure: remove opposite signed literals in a common factor by deleting the tautological factor, coalesce equal duplicates, and reject an empty or single-literal NAE. A nontrivial NAE2 forces one parity. Its matched pivot is one endpoint; no more than one other factor uses the two endpoints in the matching. Substitute the forced relation everywhere, merge endpoints, discard the NAE2 and any newly tautological factors. Keep all surviving matched factors assigned to the same variable or the merged representative. This remains injective and incident. Repeat after each merge, before graph tests and at stagnation. The proof handles any signs; each guard stays local until complete boundary support is acquired. A fresh matching can be recomputed and checked instead of retaining the previous one. A failed initial match is not an UNSAT certificate; try paid closure then matching or another terminal.

For completeness of BH10: match each factor to pivot u, add arcs to every other signed literal v carrying p=1 XOR signs. A valid assignment enables at least one such arc at every matched pivot. Every sink SCC without free vertex then contains a selected directed simple cycle of total parity zero. Conversely choose a consistent simple cycle in each such SCC, set unmatched sinks freely, propagate backward along arcs into assigned vertices. Subdivide label-0 arcs once and leave label-1 arcs unchanged, so even directed simple cycles correspond exactly to parity-zero cycles. RST deterministic polynomial recognition applies, and at most linear many edge deletion decisions extract one cycle witness per SCC (no closed-walk relaxation). Back-substitute all parity merges. This proves both SAT directions on the actual original port word (complement the local witness to its anchor value); no independent-marginal gluing.

For b=0 or 1, an unconditioned exact query suffices by complement symmetry. For fixed b<=3 this is a polynomial all-fiber acquisition even at unbounded interior size. Store one checked witness per feasible relative word; no arbitrary semantic query oracle. Use fixed local bounded-table backend as an alternative, not a hidden exponential step for large C.

## 3. Three-port closure and exact marginal shortcut (PROVED)
For three ports a,b,c the support S is a subset of F2^2 in coordinates (a XOR b,a XOR c). All possible supports remain in the signed NAE language:
- Empty: contradiction; full: delete regional factors.
- Singleton: two signed NAE2 equations.
- Two points: an affine line over F2, one parity equation on a,b or a,c or b,c.
- Three points: one signed NAE3 forbidding the missing relative word (and its global complement in the original three bits).

This is an exact nonaffine contraction when three words survive. Pair projections alone do NOT in general certify full support. But if the three binary parity marginals are all acquired exactly and any one is proper, their common intersection has at most two relative words. It equals S: if two candidates remain, some parity distinguishes them; the certified marginals guarantee actual witnesses for both. If a marginal is empty S is empty. Extract each witness from a query whose parity uniquely identifies that candidate. The singleton case is immediate. If all three marginals are full, S may have size 3 or 4, so require a joint query or return UNKNOWN. Explicit example: NAE(a,b,c) and the unconstrained triple have all three marginals full but different joint support. A private realization of NAE uses NAE(a,b,x) and NAE2(not x,c), forcing x=c. Thus private projection does not remove this issue.

A useful weaker matching premise for the shortcut: every nonempty subcollection J of regional factors has |V(J)|>=|J|+1. Then adding ANY single two-port guard preserves Hall: for a subset containing the new factor and nonempty J, use the surplus; the guard alone has two vertices. All six parity queries are paid. No guarantee that adding two guard factors preserves Hall. This covers a region obtained by deleting one edge of a deficiency-one critical monotone NAE core with the strict proper Hall surplus proved in BH10, and any subcollection of that region. In H-e, every assignment makes all three removed-edge ports equal by critical UNSAT; the three exact pair supports therefore recover the complete joint equality relation without requiring the stronger two-guard matching. This holds at any girth, for the stated density/surplus domain. It does NOT extend to every DO01 high-girth core or every K in DO11, since m may exceed n and strict surplus is not universal.

## 5. Recognition firewall (PROVED)
Even if every pair marginal is known full, unrestricted recognition of full three-port support is NP-complete. Given arbitrary 3CNF F(z), construct a complement-invariant NAE gadget with projected support
  S(q1,q2) = q1 OR q2 OR [exists z F(z)]
for q1=a XOR b, q2=a XOR c. In the gauge a=0 use CNF clauses (q1 OR q2 OR C_j). Convert bounded length clauses to 3CNF with fresh private chain auxiliaries, preserving existential support on original q,z. Homogenize every literal using a as reference: a clause is NAE of its signed literals plus the reference literal that rejects exactly the false word in gauge a=0. NAE4 splits into NAE3(A,B,t) AND NAE3(not t,C,D) with private t, by checking the two equal-pair cases (incompatible iff all four literals equal). Thus polynomial exact construction, complementary gauge lift. If F SAT then S=F2^2; if UNSAT then S excludes 00. All three pair marginals full in both cases. Full-support recognition is in NP since four relative words need only four witnesses. This is an NP-completeness reduction for arbitrary regional factors, not a claim of connected region or Hall-covered hardness, not P!=NP. It falsifies the inference that exact pair marginals are sufficient or that low boundary rank alone makes acquisition inexpensive.

Raw terminal coverage is not universal: complete triple system on n>=5 vertices is UNSAT by pigeonhole and has no proper <=3 separator when n sufficiently large, and m>n. Complete tripartite k,k,k triple system is SAT by giving first and second parts opposite colors, has m=k^3>3k for k>=2 and primal connectivity 2k. Both eventually exceed any fixed small truth cap. These analytic examples are easy by other terminals; not unrestricted hardness. DO01 high-girth dense case remains the real target.
