## A. Exact factored star projection (PROVED)
For every explicit monotone NAE-3SAT hypergraph, optionally with individual paired-owner relaxations (mask per triple specifying which monochromatic colors are forbidden), choose a private vertex v, whose entire incidence bucket is included. No external quota, observation, tag or guard involving v is discarded; otherwise v is protected and ineligible. Denote its d incident triples by (v,a_i,b_i). Define A_i=[a_i=b_i=0] if mask forbids 0 else 0; B_i=[a_i=b_i=1] if mask forbids 1 else 0. These are affine indicators (or zero). For fixed boundary z, U=product_i(1-A_i), V=product_i(1-B_i) indicate legal choices v=0,1 respectively. The exact count and existential compatibility are
    f=U+V,  g=U+V-UV.
Thus g is Boolean, can be acquired as O(d) factored arithmetic nodes with coefficients +,+,-; local positive fiber multiplicities are only 1 and 2, so BA09's degree-two annihilator applies (indeed g=(3f-f^2)/2). A direct O(d^2)-size equivalent conjunction is product_{i,j}(1-A_i B_j). Each conflict A_i B_j is an affine intersection, possibly inconsistent; retain original IDs. Reconstruct v by testing U, then V. Both SAT directions hold pointwise under every fixed exterior guard, including overlapping exterior variables; no independence of boundary observations assumed. Counts of original assignments require f rather than g. For an independent vertex set S in the primal graph (no triple has two chosen vertices), all private buckets are disjoint as factors, and their centers can be reconstructed independently given the SAME exterior word. This is an exact source-to-affine-factored-DAG reduction, not a claim that a general physical network compiles for free. ## B. Exact support rank after Booleanization (PROVED)
For a full-mask star with d>=2 disjoint leaf pairs, write the unnormalized Walsh transform as sum_z g(z)(-1)^(w dot z). At frequency corresponding to two variables in the same pair:
    ghat_raw=2^d-2*3^(d-1) !=0.
For two variables in different pairs:
    ghat_raw=2*3^(d-2) !=0.
Proof: U and V have local three-point allowed supports. At a pair frequency each local transform is -1 and at zero is 3; at a singleton frequency the transforms of U and V are opposite unit signs, so the cross-pair products agree. UV is product of two-point mixed supports; its within-pair frequency is -2 times 2^(d-1), and its cross-pair coefficient vanishes. Subtracting gives the identities. For f the corresponding coefficients are -2*3^(d-1) and 2*3^(d-2), also nonzero. Complement symmetry forces all odd-weight frequencies to vanish, so the exact spectral span of BOTH functions is the full even-weight space on 2d boundary bits, of dimension 2d-1. All pair differences occur, not merely a basis. The minimum linear support quotient is therefore exactly the minimum value quotient; Booleanizing cannot remove a single relative direction in this star. It removes the central variable and preserves the global complement gauge, but not the boundary correlations. There are 2*3^d-2^d legal boundary words; both zero and each unit vector are legal, so the full affine hull gives no nontrivial local affine deduction. An uneliminated full NAE triple similarly has the even-weight observation space of dimension 2 (all 3 pair coefficients nonzero). These claims are for the unguarded source; arbitrary guards or further nonlinear transformations can change the rank.

## C. Persistence on DO09 after EVERY independent-star projection (PROVED conditional on DO09 family)
Let H be a linear 3-uniform critical monotone NAE hypergraph with maximum degree D, primal graph G of treewidth t. Choose ANY independent set S of its vertices; no efficiency of choosing an optimal set is required. Project every chosen center exactly as A. Define G' on surviving variables by retaining unhit triple cliques and making each star's boundary a clique. Every such clique is an exact support observation space by B; no syntactic overestimate. Each original neighbor of v in S survives and is contained in its boundary clique. Any tree decomposition of G' has a bag containing that clique (Helly property for the vertex subtrees); attach a bag N_G(v) union {v} for each removed center. It is a valid decomposition of G, since S independent. Thus t<=max(tw(G'),2D). In particular when t>2D, tw(G')>=t.

For every factor-leaf subcubic tree of this projected registry, let w be its maximum actual overlap rank dim(sum_left W_i intersect sum_right W_i). Assign each edge of G' to ONE factor whose scope contains it. Refine factor leaves into edge-leaf trees and prune unused leaves. For cuts between factor blocks the edge-vector overlap is at most w (edge vectors lie in their owner's true observation space). For cuts inside one refined leaf gadget, one side contains only edges of that factor, so overlap is at most max_i dim W_i <= max(2,2D-1). For a nontrivial 2-connected block B of maximum treewidth in G', restrict the edge tree to B. Graphic matroid rank is the rank of the binary pair-incidence vectors, so its cut connectivity (without +1 convention) is exactly this intersection dimension. Mazoit's branchwidth equality for bridgeless graphs and the standard graph branchwidth/treewidth inequality give
    max(w,2D-1) >= (2/3)(tw(G')+1)-1
when tw(G')>=2. (D>=2.) Therefore for the bounded-D, linear-treewidth DO09 family, w=Omega(n) for EVERY independent-star set and every factor tree, using true local support spaces, even though each projected fiber has only two positive multiplicities and the projection is paid polynomial. By DO08-B, componentwise maximum dual dependency nullity and global shared overlap rank are at least w as well. Same parameter obstruction holds for either single-owner relaxation: at most one projected factor differs, so omit it from the rank lower-bound subgraph, deleting at most one clique's edges; adding its at most 2D vertices to every bag restores a decomposition of G'. Thus the treewidth loss is at most 2D, and unaffected support spaces still certify the lower bound. This does NOT classify modified factor rank or claim its omission preserves decision.
