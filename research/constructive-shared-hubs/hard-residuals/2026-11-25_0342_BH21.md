\begin{lemma}[Every-direction magnitude obstruction]\label{lem:bh21-spectrum}
Let a field-free quadratic spin energy be $E=C+\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}s_is_j$ with $s_i\in\{-1,1\}$ and every $J_{ij}\ne0$. Modulo the global complement kernel, every nonzero affine flip direction splits the coordinates into two nonempty sets $A,B$, with $|B|\ge |A|$ after interchange. In the section of that direction, the number of distinct magnitudes of $E(x)-E(x+q)$ is at least
\[
\left\lceil\frac{\lceil |B|/2\rceil+1}{2}\right\rceil.
\]
Thus no fixed BA21 magnitude cap is universally eligible on these raw full-support charts, even with arbitrary invertible affine reparameterization.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The difference is twice the crossing bilinear sum $\sum_{j\in B}s_j\sum_{i\in A}J_{ij}s_i$; its absolute value is constant on each flip orbit. For uniform independent $s_i$ in $A$, each inner sum is zero with probability at most $1/2$ by conditioning on all but one nonzero term. Hence some fixed $A$ word leaves $r\ge\lceil |B|/2\rceil$ nonzero coefficients. Start the remaining $s_j$ opposite to their respective coefficient signs, then reverse those $r$ spins successively. The sum strictly increases at each step, giving $r+1$ distinct real values. Absolute value identifies at most two of them. The count is invariant under a change of basis since a direction still corresponds to an actual flip and a section contains one point per orbit. This is not a claim about charts after arbitrary guards or nonlinear transformations.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Baseline-subtracted component count consumer]\label{lem:bh21-count}
For an explicit integer quadratic spin energy $E=C+\sum_i h_i s_i+\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}s_is_j$, try a baseline $\beta$ from zero and the distinct pair coefficients (including implicit zeros). Form the residual graph with edges $J_{ij}\ne\beta$. Let its connected components be $G_\ell$. For each, acquire the exact minimum local residual energy $D_\ell(t)$ subject to $\sum_{i\in G_\ell}s_i=t$, with one attaining word. This acquisition is polynomial if every component either has constant residual pair coefficient on all distinct pairs or has size at most $C_0\log_2(N+2)$ for a fixed $C_0$, where $N$ is original encoded input length. Then the global minimum and a genuine minimizing word are deterministically computable in polynomial time, irrespective of the number of components or the product of their count ranges.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Subtract the uniform baseline, using the identity
\[
E=C+\frac{\beta}{2}\bigl((\sum_i s_i)^2-n\bigr)+\sum_\ell\bigl[\sum_{i\in G_\ell}h_i s_i+\sum_{i<j\in G_\ell}(J_{ij}-\beta)s_is_j\bigr].
\]
For uniform residual coefficient $a_\ell$, the pair term is $a_\ell(t^2-|G_\ell|)/2$. For fixed number of negative spins, sorting local fields gives the minimizing linear term (select the largest fields for negative spins). For capped small components enumerate their words and retain the minimum for each count. Pins can be substituted before recognition, adding fields and constants. For the union of the first $j$ components, store $M_j(T)=\min_t[M_{j-1}(T-t)+D_j(t)]$, with $M_0(0)=0$ and infeasible entries infinite. There are at most $2n+1$ counts at any stage. Minimize $C+\beta(T^2-n)/2+M_k(T)$, and follow attaining pointers and local words. Counts only compress the baseline coupling; local residual terms are never discarded. Discovery tries at most $O(n^2)$ baselines, builds and checks components by matrix scans, and declines before any oversized nonuniform local enumeration. For each admitted trial there are $O(n^3)$ DP transitions and at most $n(N+2)^{C_0}$ enumerated words, each with polynomial verification. Integer bit lengths are polynomial by the original coefficient bounds and sums. Store polynomial tables and reverse logs; all failed trials, authenticated coefficient compilation, closure, accesses and final source verification are charged. This is a paid terminal, not a universal contraction claim. For a supplied verified bounded-rank integer baseline feature map, the same proof works with vector counts when the number of coordinates is fixed and their summed absolute ranges are polynomial in original $N$; discovery of that more general map is not asserted.
\end{proof}
