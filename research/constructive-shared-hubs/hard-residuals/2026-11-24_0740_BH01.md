## 1. Exact paired-wheel syndrome compiler and combinatorial cut rank (PROVED)
For clause e=(a,b,c), introduce relative state d=(x_a XOR x_b,x_a XOR x_c). With occurrence signs s, precisely one of its four possible d is forbidden, namely (s_a XOR s_b,s_a XOR s_c). Replace the triple by a private indexed two-edge star a-b,a-c in multigraph G; retain all original factor and port identities, including parallel edges from different clauses. Let c be component count including isolates, n variables, m factors. A spanning forest gives a fundamental cycle matrix C over GF(2) of rank r=2m-n+c. A complete tuple of local relative labels integrates to Boolean variables iff C d=0; assign one arbitrary root bit per component, propagate tree differences and verify closing edges. Each factor contributes one of three syndrome vectors in its column span; XOR all to zero. This is pointwise exact. A valid lift selects original RT05 variable rows and extends BW01 private rims using its constructive OR witness proof; physical graph lifting not executed here. Additional external guards must be compiled exactly or kept separately; complement symmetry is not assumed under arbitrary unary pins.

For any factor subset Q let R=E\Q, c_Q,c_R be component counts of the corresponding spanning star subgraphs (all n vertices). Let V_Q be span of the two cycle-matrix columns per factor in Q. The kernel of C restricted to these columns consists exactly of graph coboundaries supported on Q. A generating vertex labeling must be constant on each R-component and is unique modulo a global flip on each G-component. Hence
  rank V_Q = 2|Q| - c_R + c.
Therefore
  w(Q)=dim(V_Q intersection V_R)=n+c-c_Q-c_R.
Equivalently, build a bipartite multigraph with Q-components and R-components as vertices, one edge per original variable linking its two component IDs. Its component count is c; its cycle rank is w(Q). Shared carriers need not individually cost a bit; only overlap-component cycles do. This rank is deterministically computable by union-find without constructing the syndrome matrix. Exact cut computation does not discover a globally optimal order for free.

With baseline local rows and remaining affine difference spans, every viable prefix syndrome belongs to intersection of two translated spaces of dimension at most w(Q). At most 2^w such states. BA01 suffix-affine pruning before every insertion is sound; merge only identical full syndrome at same ordered factor layer under fixed input/guard, retain one actual row history. At final zero syndrome integrate labels and verify the source. Greedy deterministic order repeatedly chooses next factor minimizing immediate component cut rank, charging every unsuccessful candidate and connectivity scan; any supplied or acquired ordering is sound, but not guaranteed small width. Bound T,S <= 2^w poly(N) including full saved row histories, source compilation, all Gaussian operations, key equality, verification, ordering and reconstruction; CM<=TS. This is a conditional exact decision terminal, not a counting algorithm.

## 2. No emergent two-hub period in any edge-critical NAE core (PROVED)
For an inclusion-minimal UNSAT complement-symmetric NAE formula, remove isolates. Deleting any factor leaves a satisfiable formula, and its three literal carriers must be all equal in every remaining assignment. If the deleted edge meets more than one remaining connected component, flipping one such component changes a nonempty proper subset of the three literal values, satisfying the deleted NAE while preserving every other factor. Contradiction. Therefore the endpoints remain connected and removing that edge leaves the original connected component connected. Consequently each individual V_e has rank 2, and its legal syndrome set A_e contains exactly three of four plane elements.

For any pair e,f, let s=dim(V_e intersection V_f).
* s=0: the sumset has 9 distinct elements, hence no nonzero binary translation period.
* s=1: split each plane by the common 1D direction into two cosets. The legal three-state set has fiber sizes 2 and 1. The pair sumset has four disjoint quotient fibers of sizes 2,2,2,1, total 7. Again odd cardinality forbids any nonzero translation period.
* s=2: the two planes coincide and A_e+A_f is the whole plane (3+3>4). This is impossible in a critical UNSAT core: solve the proper subformula omitting both factors, extend its variable assignment arbitrarily to all n variables, then its syndrome can be corrected by some legal pair of states because the missing pair's unconstrained syndrome lies in that same plane. That would satisfy the whole formula.

Thus no root pair merger of fully fused, independent three-state NAE hubs can create a period on any edge-critical NAE core. This does NOT contradict BA01's explicit owner-row representation or synthetic independent coincident-plane controls; representation and guard provenance are crucial. Simply duplicating a wheel does not introduce a new independent legal selector. Further, for any Q whose complement retains all G connectivity, the restricted cycle map is injective and its exact sumset has 3^|Q| rows, odd, hence no period. If Q is internally acyclic, every legal Q tuple integrates on Q and extends to unconstrained edge labels on R, so no suffix-affine row support test can delete it. For DO01 Berge girth >g, every Q of at most g clauses is internally a forest (after star expansion); hence small complement-connected mergers cannot provide this route to progress. Even without connectivity, for a single original factor e, V_rest has rank r by c_{e}=n-2; root affine row closure never deletes any original NAE row. These are restricted barriers, not solver lower bounds. Larger mergers, disconnections, alternate owner encodings and guarded rewrites remain available.

## 3. Exact single-owner neighbor transfer (PROVED)
Delete the entire NAE pair at e, solve all other NAE constraints, then globally complement the resulting assignment if needed to satisfy the one remaining monotone or signed OR at e. One of the two orientations always satisfies that lone OR. Conversely any one-owner relaxed solution satisfies the deleted-edge subformula. This exact equivalence requires no asymmetric extra guard and holds even if original not critical. If original is known UNSAT, the removed triple must be monochromatic, with the surviving owner determining its orientation. Keep original owner IDs and verify source cost bounds. This saves introducing asymmetric new local states in the tested DO01 one-owner controls.

## 4. Nonlocal weighted spectral certificate and small-kernel terminal (PROVED)
Let B be signed incidence (one row b_e with three entries +/-1), D=diag of vertex occurrence counts, z_v=(-1)^x_v. Define integer symmetric M=3 B^T B-D. For EVERY Boolean assignment:
  z^T M z=24 times (# violated NAE constraints).
Indeed the signed sum in a triple has absolute value 1 when mixed, 3 when monochromatic; 3*(sum)^2-3 equals 0 or 24. More generally take any strictly positive rational edge weights q and M=3 B^T diag(q) B-diag(sum incident q); the right side is 24 times total violated weight. Thus if M is positive definite, UNSAT is certified. If M is PSD with nullity k, all satisfying spins lie in ker M. Rational elimination identifies k free coordinates; enumerate their +/-1 assignments, solve the dependent coordinates exactly, require each to be +/-1, verify every source constraint. T<=2^k poly(N), streamed polynomial S, CM<=TS. This also applies to guarded relative equalities z_v=+/-z_u by verified signed-copy substitution z=T y and matrix congruence T^T M T; arbitrary guards are not silently dropped. PSD/PD and kernel can be discovered deterministically in polynomial bit time by exact rational symmetric elimination (zero diagonal in PSD must have zero corresponding row; otherwise fail; positive pivot Schur complements; bit growth bounded by rational minors). Exact rational kernel implementation and arbitrary weighting/guarded congruence were not executed. Non-PSD is UNKNOWN, not SAT or UNSAT.
Practical exact certificate: propose an integer lower triangular C approximately K times chol(M-epsilon I), then form integer R=K^2 M-C C^T. Verify every R_ii-sum_{j!=i}|R_ij| >= delta>0. This proves R>=delta I and M>=(delta/K^2)I via quadratic diagonal dominance. Floating point proposal is never authority. Test backend proves all integer products/partial sums stay exactly representable by the stated safe-integer bound. General verifier uses arbitrary precision. Certificate discovery via exact rational elimination is independently polynomial; no claim floating proposal always succeeds. Consequence: every coloring violates at least ceil(n*delta/(24 K^2)) constraints. A critical UNSAT core has a coloring violating exactly one constraint, hence any spectral lower bound M>=alpha I must have alpha<=24/n. Robust precursor certificate need not survive critical extraction.

## 5. An infinite high-girth spectral-easy precursor family (PROVED using matrix Bernstein; not experimentally generated by this distribution)
Strengthen the DO01 probabilistic construction BEFORE critical-core extraction. Fix g>=2. Include each triple independently with probability p=n^(-2+epsilon), epsilon=1/(4g). Write M=sum of included local M_e=3 b_e b_e^T-D_e (monotone signs). Each local symmetric M_e has eigenvalues 8,-1,-1 on its triple and zero off it, so norm<=8 and M_e^2 <=64 D_e in PSD order. Set d=p binom(n-1,2). The mean M has diagonal 2d and off-diagonal 3p(n-2), hence smallest eigenvalue 2d-3p(n-2). For independent centered summands X_e=(I_e-p)M_e, norm<=8 and variance norm ||sum E X_e^2||<=64d. Apply Tropp matrix Bernstein to X and -X with t=d/2:
  Pr(||M-E M||>=d/2) <=2n exp[-(d/2)^2/(2(64d+8(d/2)/3))]=o(1),
since d=Theta(n^epsilon) dominates log n. Thus with high probability M>=d I for all sufficiently large n.

Any two distinct Berge cycles of length <=g whose full hyperedge vertex supports overlap have a connected union containing two independent incidence cycles. If it uses e<=2g hyperedges and v vertices, its full incidence graph has cycle rank 2e-v+1>=2; so v<=2e-1. There are only finitely many such patterns for fixed g, and their expected number is O_g(sum_{e<=2g} n^(2e-1)p^e)=O_g(n^(-1/2))=o(1). This includes cycles sharing an edge or an off-cycle endpoint. Hence with high probability all short cycles have disjoint full supports. Delete one hyperedge from each short cycle. The deleted local matrices have disjoint vertex supports, so their sum has operator norm at most 8. The remaining hypergraph has Berge girth >g and M_remaining >=(d-8)I, positive definite for large n. It has Omega(n^(1+epsilon)) unavoidable monochromatic edges by the identity. This proves arbitrarily high-girth monotone UNSAT families with an explicit, deterministic polynomial spectral recognition/certificate terminal, despite large raw local parameters. Random construction and alteration are existential here, not a deterministic generator for every instance. Crucial: edge-minimal critical subcores chosen in DO01 lose this robustness; no assertion of spectral coverage of them. Also isolated vertices can be discarded if needed.
