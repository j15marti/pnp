## PROVED: exact Helly binary fill
On any finite explicitly acquired binary owner-selector registry, preserve actual IDs and all current guard constraints (absorbed or represented exactly in the chart). Let D be a nonempty owner domain and S(r) the subset of D compatible with each other live row r. Reclose arc consistency first. For every allowed pair r,s from distinct exterior owners delete it if S(r) intersects S(s) trivially. These pair deletions are necessary. If the family of nonempty S(r) is Helly (every pairwise-intersecting subfamily has common point), then the resulting exterior binary chart is EXACTLY the existential projection eliminating this owner. For any surviving transversal, all chosen supports pairwise intersect after fill; Helly gives a common owner row. Conversely every original transversal survives. Store actual supports and domain to lift in reverse order. Zero or one exterior owner is immediate by nonempty domain and AC. This is projection, not necessity of the removed coordinates. At a PC fixed point all fill pairs already absent; unlike DS18 tree-convexity, no common tree is required.

Recognition: for every triple x,y,z of distinct members of D, intersect all supports containing at least two of the triple. Require nonempty intersection (empty collection intersection D). Necessity holds since all such sets meet pairwise. Sufficiency: if minimal pairwise-intersecting bad subfamily E1,...,Et exists, t>=3. For i=1,2,3 choose xi in the intersection omitting Ei; xi is outside Ei, so the three are distinct. Every set in the subfamily contains at least two xi, contradiction. This is the established Berge–Duchet Helly criterion, not a new general Helly theorem. A four-cycle of two-element supports {0,1},{1,2},{2,3},{3,0}, plus full supports, is Helly but cannot be tree-convex (all four edges would be required). Any owner of at most two rows is eligible after AC.
