## Exact repair identity
Given signed ternary NAE registry and supplied assignment w satisfying every owner except e*, set z=x XOR w. On every good owner there is a unique minority literal a relative to w; with other vertices b,c, the constraint in flip variables is R(a;b,c): z_a in {z_b,z_c}, i.e. NAE(not z_a,z_b,z_c). The bad owner becomes positive NAE. Equivalently, both flip classes must be closed under the implication {b,c}->a for every good owner, and the bad triple must meet both classes. This is an exact signed nonlinear formulation, not independent affine hulls. Necessary closures of two differently pinned classes may remain disjoint without any valid extension: Fano controls below. Apply certified closure at root, after each actual deduction and at stagnation; pins are conditional. Complete semantic closure is not assumed.

## Exact NP-complete repair promise
Input: signed NAE on distinct triples, linear hypergraph, maximum occurrence four, a word w violating exactly one owner, and that owner ID. Decide whether the restored registry is SAT. Equivalently w is supplied with its single-budget neighbor (one of two monochromatic literal words allowed on e*). NP membership: polynomial check of promises and witness. For hardness, start with arbitrary signed NAE F on n variables and m triples from I21 (repeated positions permitted at this intermediate stage). Introduce X_i, Y_i and three selectors s_0,s_1,s_2. For each i and j impose R(s_j;X_i,Y_i). The only designated bad owner is NAE(s_0,s_1,s_2). All these R constraints accept the all-zero word. On any solution, the selectors contain both values; hence each X_i != Y_i. For each source literal use X_i for positive, Y_i for negative; if a,b,c are the three resulting literal variables, impose R(a_bar;b,c) with a_bar the opposite representative of a. This is exactly the source NAE once complement synchronization holds. All zero still satisfies these new R constraints. Conversely every source model lifts using Y_i=1-X_i and any nonconstant selector word. Every satisfying base word decodes by taking X_i. Thus there are exactly six base lifts per source model, even with repeated source positions. All source and selector occurrences and signs are retained.

### Linear bounded-degree synchronization without using a SAT oracle
The Fano plane has seven triples with every pair in exactly one triple. If every line is NAE, exactly seven same-color pairs occur, but any 2-color partition of seven points has at least nine; contradiction. Remove line 012: every model has endpoints equal, and the explicit word 0001111 is legal (every remaining line intersects the deleted line in exactly one vertex). XOR-switch by that word to make all zero a known legal gadget word, preserving endpoint equality. All one also legal by complement. No hidden pins. Use fresh occurrence leaves for every position of every base clause (even repeated positions), preserving literal signs. For each original base variable v with t occurrences, connect a chain of fresh backbone vertices v_0=v,...,v_t. For j=1,...,t attach a separate switched Fano-minus-line gadget to terminals (v_{j-1},v_j,leaf_j), with four fresh private vertices. Every model has all those terminals equal, so all occurrences recover v. Conversely extend any base word by setting every gadget vertex to that bit, valid by the two known gadget words. Distinct gadgets intersect in at most one backbone vertex; external clauses are pairwise disjoint and meet each gadget in at most one occurrence leaf. Thus the output is linear, all clauses have distinct vertices, backbone degree at most 4, leaf degree 3, private degree 3. A word of all zero violates exactly the designated external clause, whose original single-budget neighbor is given for free by construction. Size exactly B+18M variables and 19M clauses for base variable count B=2n+3 and clause count M=3n+m+1 (counting any unused base variable). The reduction is deterministic linear in objects and polynomial in encoded bits. Output witnesses decode in polynomial time and can be verified against the original source. Girth version: for each fixed g>=2 replace the Fano gadget by an inclusion-minimal non-2-colorable monotone 3-uniform hypergraph of Berge girth >g with one edge deleted, from DO01. The deleted terminals must all equal; take a known witness with them zero (complement if necessary), switch signs. The gadget has no short cycle and any path between distinct terminals has at least g hyperedges, or restoring the missing edge would give a cycle of length <=g. Any cycle crossing gadgets must traverse at least one gadget between distinct terminals because external clauses are disjoint and the synchronization chains are trees of gadgets. Consequently the constructed network has girth >g (a cycle traversing a gadget and any outside edge has length >=g+1). For fixed g the gadget/witness are finite constants, obtainable by exhaustive enumeration before the fixed reduction is defined, with a constant degree bound depending on g; no uniform-in-g size or degree-four high-girth claim. This is NP-completeness for every fixed girth threshold, not an efficient generator of the growing-girth DO09/MS21 critical sequence.

## Implication for U-PROGRESS
A solver guaranteed polynomial whenever one violated owner and a supplied budget-neighbor witness exist would already solve all 3-CNF by this adapter. The easy anchor does not discharge the universal resource obligation. A known witness of the relaxed problem is not an oracle for the restored support. The closure formulation identifies the exact remaining nonaffine cut problem; union of two separately closed seed sets is not guaranteed to extend. Fano itself remains solvable by paid PSD rejection (MS21), so these tests are not simultaneous portfolio hardness. Signed synchronization creates many short cycles for the degree-four version; alternate exact simplification, rank-Hall or algebraic closure may exploit it. No claim of minimal UNSAT for compiler outputs. If P=NP then the repair problem is polynomial; if a fixed repair algorithm were shown nonpolynomial it would not by itself imply P != NP. To exploit this new normal form construct a complete polynomial algorithm for the two-color closed-set cut (or another nonlocal transformation) with a fixed aggregate reserve. The reduction is a sharpened equivalent target, not a proof of closure.
