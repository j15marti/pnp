## FALSIFIED: bounded arity or polynomial explicit exterior CNF, even for native incidence trees
Take three disjoint full binary trees of height h, variable at each node, positive NAE(parent,child1,child2) at each internal node; join top variables in central NAE. Incidence graph is a tree, variable degree <=2. L=3*2^h leaves, n=2L-3 variables, m=L-2 owners. Two independent exterior Boolean flags a_i,b_i per leaf, either pins it to zero. If all leaves pinned, internal values alternate by level, three roots equal and central NAE rejects. If some leaf unpinned, keep all other leaves zero, flip that leaf and each ancestor on its path: each changed gate has unequal children and root triple becomes nonconstant. Thus exact rejection is AND_i(a_i OR b_i). There are 2^L minimal rejected flag words. Every exterior-only CNF expressing acceptance needs >=2^L clauses: at each minimal rejected word some clause is false; flipping any selected flag to zero makes the word accepted, so that clause contains every selected negative literal and cannot reject another minimal rejected word. Width >=L, even allowing arbitrary positive literals. Factored circuit linear. This strengthens DR19's Horn example in native NAE, not a novel general tree CSP algorithm, and not unrestricted hardness. Exterior flags are explicit interface data, not a raw high-girth critical source.
