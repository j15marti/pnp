## 2. The conditional extension lemma (PROVED)

Let S be any set of Boolean suffix words, including possibly an empty set, and phi_D(y) be the vector of all squarefree GF(2) monomials of degree <= D, including 1. Let R be a subset of ACTUAL words of S with span phi_D(R)=span phi_D(S). Let L be an affine Boolean function of y; define a new allowed head bit u by u=0 always legal, u=1 legal precisely when L(y)=1. Let S' consist of these legal extensions and R' be the corresponding extensions of R.

Then span phi_D(R')=span phi_D(S').

Proof: Let P(u,y) be any polynomial of degree <=D that vanishes on R'. Write P=P0(y)+u Q(y), where deg P0<=D, deg Q<=D-1 (Q=0 when D=0). Because zero extension of every representative is present, P0 vanishes on R, hence on S. For representatives with L=1, Q must vanish; equivalently LQ vanishes on all R. After squarefree reduction deg(LQ)<=D. Therefore LQ vanishes on all S. For all legal u=1 extensions Q vanishes, and hence P vanishes on all S'. Every linear functional on phi_D is such a P. Equality of annihilators implies equality of spans; reverse inclusion also follows from R' subset S'. This proves exactness with conditional gates, not merely fixed-coordinate linear extension. All retained words are actual legal assignments; row XORs are only tests of dependence, never synthetic witnesses.

Consequently for d triangular head/free bits u_i, constraints u_i=0 OR L_i(u_(i+1)..u_d)=1, unrestricted bits encoded with L=1, scan backwards. Start empty suffix word, extend each representative by zero and by one iff its own affine gate passes, reduce by Gaussian elimination on feature evaluations and keep actual admitted words. At most M_D(d)=sum_{j=0}^{min(D,d)} binom(d,j) representatives and at most 2d M_D(d) attempted extension edges. No state indexed by every joint suffix guard word. For any extra predicate P given by a paid polynomial-size evaluator and certified GF2 degree <=D, existence of P=1 on base iff it is 1 on a retained word. For conjunction of exceptional ORs of l_j affine tests the product of their Boolean satisfaction indicators has degree <=sum_j l_j. Evaluate directly without expanding it. More generally any supplied authenticated degree bound is valid. This is exact existential decision, not exact integer counting.

For complete affine feedback in the eligible domain, use a degree-(D+1) basis, filter by P=1. For any affine test A, P*A has degree <=D+1, so if no filtered word violates an affine identity then no original legal word violates it. Thus filtered representatives give the complete affine hull of all feasible selectors. The same reasoning handles fixed-degree protected features by raising degree accordingly. This feedback theorem is symbolic; the code checks base feature spans and specific protected queries, not the entire post-filter affine-hull routine. Conditional queries retain their predicate. Arbitrary new affine restrictions may invalidate triangular discovery, so reauthenticate rather than erase them.

## 3. Paid coordinate discovery (PROVED)

Input: explicit conjunction of ORs of affine tests a*x=b in an authenticated Boolean chart. Certified closure first: substitute all forced affine equations, discard constant-true clauses, remove constant-false or duplicate tests, recognize opposite tests on one mask as a tautology; empty rejects, a unit forces its affine equation, restart. This is not complete semantic closure. The main checker implements these exact rules. Extra source constraints or guards must be included as factors, retained exceptions or authenticated in the chart.

For a remaining two-test factor i, let V_-i span every coefficient mask in the other remaining base factors. A private-pivot step is available iff one coefficient a_i is not in V_-i. Gaussian elimination obtains v_i with a_i*v_i=1, all other remaining masks annihilating v_i. Delete i from the base discovery list (not from source semantics), record its two tests and v_i. Eligibility is monotone under deleting other factors; any available factor can be peeled first without destroying a possible full order. This greedy recognizer is complete for this specified private-pivot class. It is not an optimal general chart recognizer.

Let the discovered order be i=1..p. Rows a_j evaluated on v_i are zero for j>i and one for j=i, so the v_i and selected a_i are independently ranked p. Complete the selected a_i to an observation basis by adding a basis of the annihilator of span(v_i); invert this basis by Gaussian elimination. Define u_i=a_i*x+b_i, i<=p, so u_i=0 satisfies the first test. Every mask of factor i annihilates v_j for j<i; therefore its expression in the new observation basis has no coordinates before i (induct through the triangular matrix). The second test on u_i=1 is an affine test only of suffix bits. Remaining observation coordinates free. This constructs exactly the triangular system of section 2. Store the affine inverse and every original factor ID, not just a chosen branch. Both SAT directions follow from invertibility and pointwise OR evaluation.

For fixed exception cap e, enumerate all <=e factor subsets; every remaining factor must be a two-test base factor with full peel. Other factors are exceptions. For bounded exception width l and fixed e, D<=el is fixed, so this is a deterministic polynomial terminal with original row verification. No assumption that minimal e is small for arbitrary input. Source DO01 full NAE(x_u,x_v,x_w) is OR[x_u+x_v=1, x_u+x_w=1]. Signed literals merely change RHS. One owner relaxed gives the actual three-test OR[x_v=b for v in edge], with b=1 or 0, respectively. This adapter preserves both labels and actual source bits. No arbitrary budget elimination or physical source acquisition asserted.

## 4. A high-interface, exponentially many model family (PROVED and executed)

Let h be even, d=2h, y free and u_i=1 only if L_i(y)=sum_{j!=i} y_j=1. The h-by-h matrix I+J (J all ones) is invertible over GF2 since J^2=hJ=0. Hence all 2^h joint affine guard patterns occur. In coordinates l_i=L_i(y) the base is a product of h three-state relations (u_i,l_i) in {(0,0),(0,1),(1,1)}, with exactly 3^h actual legal words. At the raw head/suffix interface the observation rank is h. Nevertheless a degree-two joint target, e.g. u_0=1 AND L_0=b, is answered with <=1+2h+binom(2h,2) actual representatives. In fact the degree-two span dimension is exactly this quantity minus h: on each pair the restricted functions 1,u,l are independent, ul=u; product function basis removes precisely the h within-pair monomials at degree two. An invertible affine chart preserves the degree filtration.

Executed dense family h=4,8,12,16: actual base model count formula 81,6561,531441,43046721; retained reps 33,129,289,513; capacities 37,137,301,529. h=4 count independently enumerated, all ranks checked computationally, opposite-label protected queries and witness verified at every scale. Count at larger scales is theorem, not full enumeration. This shows why raw interface rank alone is not a compulsory cost. It is not a universal low-degree representation theorem.

Hostile gate: S={00,10,01,11}, R={00,10,01} spans all affine features. If u=1 requires y1*y2=1, no retained word allows u=1 though actual word 11 does. Thus replacing an affine activation by arbitrary quadratic activation while preserving degree one is false. Cyclic dependencies are also not covered by this recurrence; no free SCC closure.

## 5. New critical obstruction and constructive repair (PROVED)

Every unguarded conjunction of proper signed NAE triples that is fully private-pivot peelable has FULL AFFINE HULL on its original variables. Indeed each NAE factor has two independent coefficient masks. In its triangular chart the second test cannot have only the head coefficient, as then it would be proportional to the first. Thus every head gate is a nonconstant affine predicate of suffix bits. Starting with unrestricted suffix, induction gives full affine hull: zero extensions span the full suffix affine space; some actual suffix witness has gate=1, giving an independent head direction. Invertible chart preserves full affine hull. Therefore any satisfiable proper NAE equality gadget forcing distinct ports to agree is NOT fully private-pivot peelable.

Peelability is hereditary under taking a subfamily of factors: retain the original elimination order and each private mask is still private relative to fewer factors. Consequently a base containing ALL factors of any one equality gadget cannot peel. If there are t factor-disjoint such gadgets, every deletion-to-peelable set has size >=t. Relaxing one owner can affect at most one gadget, so >=t-1 for every neighbor. This lower bound concerns RAW proper NAE representation; a successful equality quotient removes it. It is not a lower bound on general SAT algorithms or on the optimal transformed source.

DO01/DO02 transfer for every fixed g: choose edge-critical monotone NAE H_g with Berge girth >g, remove an edge {a,b,p}. The remainder has models and forces a=b=p, else it colors H_g. Minimize its factors for enforcing a=b; it stays SAT and every internal factor deletion admits unequal ports in both orientations by complement symmetry. No Berge path between a and b can have length <g, as adding the removed edge closes a shorter Berge cycle. The gadget retains girth >g. For each occurrence in any monotone source H introduce a private occurrence port, connect it to its original variable through a copy of this gadget (private interiors), put the original clause on occurrence ports. Both SAT directions and lifts exact by the boundary relation. Any external Berge cycle must traverse at least one gadget between its two distinct terminals (length >=g) and an additional edge; internal cycles already have girth >g. If H is edge-critical, every expanded clause deletion is SAT: deleting a terminal clause lifts a deletion witness; deleting an internal edge allows switching just that occurrence in a witness with its source clause monochrome, satisfying that clause. Both single-owner neighbors follow by complement. All 3m gadgets are factor-disjoint. For each fixed g, gadget size is finite constant, so the raw exception count is linear in the number of expanded clauses, superlogarithmic in encoded length as source size grows. This is an existential high-girth family, not an efficient uniform construction in variable g. Fixed-size motif enumeration can be charged as N^{O(s)}2^{O(s)} for gadget size s, verifying actual boundary table, exterior usage and reverse witnesses. No such bound uniform in g is asserted.

Stronger explicit infinite fixed-girth control: Fano-minus-line has 7 vertices, 6 clauses, and minimal two-port equality (verified table). Replacing each occurrence of a connected critical source by this gadget adds 6 variables and 6 clauses per occurrence. Repeatedly stretch Fano itself. Every expanded source is connected, linear, edge-critical, m=n, so BH03 deficiency m-n+1 stays ONE. Yet each fresh expansion has t=3m_old=3m_new/19 disjoint equality regions, hence every raw private-pivot deletion set has size >=3m_new/19 (neighbors >=that-1). This is a sharp separation from merely small Hall deficiency, not a claim against BH04/other terminals. Actual finite Fano gadget has Berge girth 3, not arbitrary growing girth. Raw critical root also has no first peel: deleting any edge leaves its three endpoints connected, or complementing disconnected components of a deletion witness would color the root. For NAE the coefficient span is graph cut-observation span of the other triples, so neither test is private.

Constructive escape: authenticate a bounded-size private equality gadget by exact boundary table, remove its private factors, impose actual equality, retain a reverse witness table. Reapply certified unconditional closure after each deduction. This is the same safe interface as the distributed-wheel private diamond quotient, not an assumption that arbitrary equalities can be detected. For the finite Fano motif, a degree-three interior vertex has a seven-vertex incident neighborhood. Scan candidates, require six full NAE edges on it, three local degree-two boundary vertices, no outside edges at the four interiors; enumerate all 128 local assignments and verify exact all-equal boundary support and lifts. Discovery uses only raw geometry, not the generator manifest. Cache fixed-source motif geometry; recheck every motif's actual guard/factor masks, active IDs and private interior against each residual before projection. Use signed union-find closure for degenerate NAE and owner units, restart after every strict equation; one motif projection is a certified atomic deduction. Never erase a protected interior: in general check every outside guard as well; current tests have only explicit NAE/owner constraints. Exhaustively solve the remaining small residual in the experiments and reconstruct all original bits. The final exhaustive step is not claimed polynomial for arbitrary source.

## 7. Same-realization resource proof and gates

Let N encoded length of authenticated explicit source, d<=poly(N) chart dimension, M=M_D(d). Source acquisition, affine substitution, private pivot recognition, reconstruction, guard checks polynomial in N per tested exception subset. Fixed e search attempts <=sum_{j<=e} binom(m,j); no free discovery. For one eligible chart each feature candidate costs at most M monomial evaluations (each poly(N)) and at most M XORs of M-bit rows. Thus a safe bit-time bound T <= N^{O(e+1)} + poly(N)*(1+M^3) including 2dM attempted edges, canonical monomial/pivot ordering, Gaussian admission and final predicate/witness verification; S <= poly(N)*(1+M^2) for serial candidate processing and two layers, original chart, actual words and inverse. CM <= (T+1)S for the same serial realization. Fixed e, fixed max width, or independently certified fixed D and paid chart discovery gives polynomial resources. Supplied extra query circuit must itself be polynomial evaluation cost; degree claim authenticated, not guessed. Layer job credit (d+1)(2M+1) pays each attempted transition and layer advance; each job has separately bounded polynomial-in-(N,M) cost. No unrestricted branch credit: exception discovery can be expensive and growing D can make M exponential. No counting credit from linear dependence. Actual representative identities and all query predicates retained; no guard-blind cache or overlap summation.

Motif closure: each certified projection strictly decreases number of active factors; retain exact original IDs and a constant-size witness archive per Fano motif. Revalidation/discovery/failed motif scans/DSU/closure polynomial for fixed motif size; residual computation and source compiler separately charged. A universal algorithm cannot assume bounded final residual after all projections. Test cap was 16 residual roots and did not fire. No memory bytes/CM measurement, full physical E4/B4 transfer, full cooperative portfolio, general high-girth acquisition, full affine feedback execution, or unrestricted lower bound. DISJ/both-label gate: exact existence/actual witnesses only, not additive overlap counts. Relativization/algebrization/Natural Proofs: scoped algebraic algorithm, no claimed nonrelativizing contradiction or unrestricted separation. Deterministic configuration/DTISP: bounds from the same serial execution, not independent resource optima. Critical stretch is an easy family after legitimate motif contraction, not diagonal-hard sequence evidence.
