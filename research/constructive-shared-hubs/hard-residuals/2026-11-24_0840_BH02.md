## 1. Balanced alternating-cycle lemma (PROVED)
Let a,b be distinct anchors. Let a set V of 2k other distinct variables have two perfect matchings A,B, and suppose A union B is a connected alternating even cycle, k>=2. For each uv in A retain NAE(a,u,v), and for each uv in B retain NAE(b,u,v). All constraints are actual source factors, not independence assumptions.
In the guard a=0,b=1, every A edge requires x_u+x_v>=1, every B edge requires x_u+x_v<=1. Summing each matching gives k<=sum_V x<=k. Thus every inequality is tight, so x_u+x_v=1 on every matching edge. In the opposite guard the signs reverse and the same conclusion follows. All cycle vertices of one bipartition class have the same Boolean value and all vertices of the other have its complement.
If two additional full NAE factors NAE(c,u,v) and NAE(c,p,q) have u,v in one cycle class and p,q in the other, then they require c to be opposite to both class values. Impossible. This does not require c or any interior vertex to be private. c can itself be a cycle vertex, provided all triples used are legal. Therefore the relative two-bit anchor hub admits no unequal state:
  F entails x_a XOR x_b=0.
The guarded alternating pair disequalities are NOT exported individually; only the equality after eliminating both opposite-anchor guards is unconditional. Under a=b the cycle subsystem may still have arbitrary constraints; no claim that all equal-anchor assignments extend.
Both original SAT directions after substitution: any original model obeys the equality; any satisfying model of the complete substituted system lifts by the parity DSU, since no other constraints were deleted. The same proof for a signed residual is applicable only to triples whose current signs normalize simultaneously by full NAE complement symmetry. Single-owner relaxed triples are never used as full NAE certificates. ## 2. Generalization: nonnegative balanced covers by SCC (PROVED, isolated backend executed)
For each anchor pair, form red and blue pair multigraphs from full NAE clauses through a and b, excluding the other anchor. A balanced nonnegative cover is a vector w>=0 on colored edges with sum red weights incident to v = sum blue weights incident to v for every v. By summing all incidence identities, total red and blue weight coincide. In the opposite-anchor guard, sum the weighted red lower bounds and blue upper bounds. Their variable coefficients cancel pointwise, so all positive-weight constraints are tight. Every edge with positive weight in ANY such cover forces x_u XOR x_v=1 in that guard.
Discover ALL edges occurring with positive weight in some balanced cover using a directed graph with states (v,red),(v,blue). A red edge uv gives arcs (u,red)->(v,blue) and (v,red)->(u,blue); blue edges analogously go from blue to red. A directed closed walk balances incidences at every vertex. Conversely a balanced cover gives a circulation by putting its weight on BOTH oriented arcs of each original colored edge. Conservation holds at each state; any nonzero nonnegative circulation decomposes into directed cycles, hence each positive arc belongs to a directed SCC. Therefore the supported colored edges are exactly those with an arc internal to an SCC. This is elementary graph cancellation, closely related to the implication-graph 2SAT method; no new claim about unrestricted LP or SAT complexity.
A witness certificate for any marked edge is one actual alternating return path, length at most 2n-1, plus its starting arc. Verify all source IDs and the red/blue incidence balance. Merge all certified conditional pair disequalities by parity DSU, keeping the opposite-anchor guard. An odd cycle in the supported-edge union yields contradiction; so do the paired opposite-class chords in a common parity component, or a monochromatic triple already entirely in one forced class. Reversal of anchors gives the identical set of tight pairs, so rejection of both orientations exports unconditional anchor equality. This works even if color classes are not matchings. The executed supplemental code tests the SCC/cover module directly; not integrated into the main normalizer. The degree-two component detector in main code is intentionally only a sufficient subroutine in nonlinear residuals.
Hostile control: red 6-cycle alternating between two groups of three vertices, blue two disjoint triangles. Every vertex has red and blue degree two, all 12 edges admit balanced covers, and the blue odd cycle rules out opposite anchors; equal anchors are SAT with all interiors opposite the anchors. The main matching-only test declines this graph. This is an exact global price-cancellation-to-parity bridge for MS06/S06, not independent count or flow acquisition.

## 4. Unbounded nonprivate construction (PROVED, executed)
Take three hubs a,b,c. Build two cycles of length 2k, with matchings through (a,b) and (b,c); each has two same-class chord triples through the third hub. Add terminal NAE(a,b,c). The two certificates imply a=b=c, hence UNSAT. Relax only the lower or upper terminal owner: all hubs equal in the allowed orientation, all cycle vertices opposite, gives SAT. Both labels verified. For a candidate private region with <=K interior vertices, K<=2k-3, any region containing a hub has >2 outside neighbors in at least one of its cycles. If no hub is included, every cycle vertex has both anchors outside; the connected cycle forces a third outside neighbor unless the entire cycle is included, larger than K. Thus there is no nonempty connected two-port interior of size <=K in this primal representation. This does not exclude other normalizations or small three-port projections. The certificate has no interior-size limit and needs no boundary enumeration. We do not claim this whole family is edge-critical or high-girth.

## 5. High-girth firewall (PROVED)
In a linear monotone 3-uniform hypergraph of Berge girth >3, no balanced alternating cycle through two distinct anchors exists. Any directed closed alternating walk has three consecutive edges with colors red, blue, red. Let the middle blue pair meet the first and third red pairs at two distinct vertices u,v, both outside the anchors. If the red source triples are distinct, the two share anchor a, and their intersections with the blue source triple at u,v make a Berge triangle. If the red source triple is the same, the blue and red triples share the pair u,v, violating linearity. Both forbidden. The SCC generalization consequently has empty balanced support as well. This statement concerns the CURRENT raw hypergraph only, not a quotient after other deductions.
Therefore DO01 critical high-girth cores defeat this specific root pass; no universal nonlocal equality coverage theorem. Add a cheap certified Berge-triangle-free prefilter before these expensive scans when the current source is linear. Do not suppress other closure or spectral/flow/DAG channels.
