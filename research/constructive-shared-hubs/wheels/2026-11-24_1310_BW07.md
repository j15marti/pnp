## 2. BW07-A: exact cycle rejection projection (PROVED)
Let C be a private simple cycle of L>=3 vertices. Each has exactly two additional signed spokes to exterior observations, full color domain F2^2 and no other coupling involving private colors. Exterior observations a_i(z), b_i(z) are the two forbidden colors after incorporating the spoke shifts; each is explicitly affine on the same n-bit chart z (the pointwise theorem holds even without affinity). Rim edge i forbids x_i+x_(i+1)=s_i, with indices modulo L; all additions are XOR. Protected exterior guard G is explicit affine. Repeated exterior hubs and correlated two-bit words permitted; they are not independently copied. Reject malformed manifests or return UNKNOWN if no eligible representation is supplied. Verify private occurrence lists, cycle structure, all signs and guard scopes against source; no assumption of a cheap inverse compiler on unannotated E4/B4.

For each nonzero d in F2^2 define E_d by the equations
  a_i+b_i=d for every i;
  a_i+a_(i+1)+s_i in span(d) for every i;
and the constant test H=sum_i s_i=(1+(L mod 2))*d.
If the constant test fails, omit E_d. Each E_d has at most 3L scalar affine equations: two for the first equality and one for membership in the one-dimensional span. The events for distinct d are disjoint (already at i=0). A rim has NO coloring iff z belongs to their union.

Proof. Fix an exterior word. Allowed list A_i=F2^2 minus {a_i,b_i} has size >=2. If any list has size >=3, remove that vertex, greedily color the remaining path, then color the last vertex avoiding at most two colors. Otherwise all lists have size 2. If any translated forbidden matching between two neighboring lists is not perfect, at one end select an unmatched color and greedily color the rest of the path in the opposite direction. The closing edge cannot forbid any remaining color at the other endpoint. Thus failure requires A_i+s_i=A_(i+1) on every edge, equivalently equality of the translated complementary forbidden pairs. This is exactly a common nonzero d=a_i+b_i and a_i+a_(i+1)+s_i in {0,d} for every i. Choose e outside span(d); allowed colors are a_i+e+beta_i*d. Write a_i+a_(i+1)+s_i=k_i*d, k_i in F2. Edge legality becomes beta_i+beta_(i+1)=1+k_i. These equations fail iff L mod 2 + sum k_i =1. The XOR of all edge discrepancies telescopes to H=(sum k_i)*d. This is exactly the constant test above. If the test fails in the all-perfect case there are precisely two colorings. Both directions proved. Arbitrary choice of e cancels; no synthetic assignments.

Consequences: odd L with H=0 has at most THREE bad affine events; odd L with H!=0 has none; even L with H=0 has none; even L with H!=0 has at most ONE (d=H). This subsumes BW06's signed two-hub twist construction at the feasibility level and extends to site-dependent exterior observations and arbitrary signed rim edges. Two or more exterior ports at the same hub must retain their actual two-bit identities.
