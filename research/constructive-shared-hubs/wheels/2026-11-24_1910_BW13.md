## 2. Exact star projection (PROVED)
Color x_v=l_v+2h_v with bits l,h. A private center y has FOUR edges, two to pivot a and one to each endpoint b,c, all distinct terminals. Each edge forbids y=x_v XOR constant. Let the four forbidden values be
  x_a XOR s_a, x_a XOR s_a XOR 2, x_b XOR s_b XOR 1, x_c XOR s_c XOR 3 XOR (2 eta),
where s_a,s_b,s_c,eta are fixed bits. Define active(e,l) iff l_a XOR s_a = l_b XOR s_b = l_c XOR s_c. If inactive, the exclusions occupy at most three colors, and a center exists for every high word. If active, pivot occupies both colors in its low coset, and b,c lie in the complementary coset; they are distinct iff h_b XOR h_c=eta. Thus center exists exactly iff
  NOT active OR (h_b XOR h_c=1 XOR eta).
No constraint on h_a; no affine sign oracle. Port degrees (2,1,1), center degree4. All 64 terminal words, 8 literal sign triples and 2 eta signs checked; local support affine hull is full (six bits). Both SAT directions follow by choosing the least unused center color or observing four distinct forbidden colors.

All high bits extend iff every active cycle has tag XOR zero, including length-two cycles of parallel edges.
