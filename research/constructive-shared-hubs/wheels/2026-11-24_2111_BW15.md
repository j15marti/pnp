## Theorem 1 (known theorem plus exact algorithmic corollary)
For a loopless explicit multigraph of maximum multiplicity-counted degree 4, each edge uv carries one exclusion x_u XOR x_v != s_e, x in F2^2. Full color pins may be supplied on any vertices. Additionally permit explicit nonempty lists on others if after forced full-color pin deletion and closure every remaining list has size >= remaining degree. Empty lists reject; violation of the promise declines, not UNSAT. This domain has a uniform deterministic polynomial SAT decision and witness algorithm.

Pin deletion: verify a pinned edge when both ends fixed, delete fixed vertex, remove each of its forbidden neighbor colors from actual lists, repeat singleton propagation after each deduction, then check capacity at stagnation. Each incident edge removes at most one color, so deleting any fixed vertex preserves degree-feasibility at previously feasible unpinned vertices. Low-only pins of size 2 at degree 4 are NOT automatically eligible. Arbitrary affine rows, E4/B4 higher-arity equality/balance, quotas and protected tags are outside this theorem.

For each remaining connected component form the DP-cover with one clique of actual list colors per vertex and one forbidden matching for each physical edge (restrict to lists, union duplicates). The external theorem of Bernshteyn, Kostochka and Pron, On DP-coloring of graphs and multigraphs, Siberian Math J 58 (2017), Theorem 9, https://doi.org/10.1134/S0037446617010049, manuscript https://kostochk.web.illinois.edu/math581/Pron.pdf p.5, says precisely that connected multigraphs not DP-degree-colorable are those all of whose blocks are uniformly multiplied cliques or cycles. Its hypotheses hold: loopless graph, edges counted with multiplicity, each cross-edge conflict union is a union of matchings, list size at least degree. If a component contains a nonbrick block it is SAT. This is a decision, not a witness oracle.

If all blocks are bricks, compute exact block-cut messages, indexed by the actual articulation color. A uniformly multiplied clique contains at most five vertices by degree 4, so exhaust <=4^5 words. A uniformly multiplied cycle is processed by a path DP for each of four fixed initial colors with a closing-edge check; each transition checks all parallel signed exclusions. Combine child masks by intersection, and check nonempty root. Block incidence tree gluing preserves every actual color, not just a low marginal, and supplies both SAT directions. Isolated vertices check their actual nonempty lists.

Recover witnesses by self-reduction: try each of at most four colors at each not yet fixed vertex, ask the same decision algorithm with that full-color pin; retain a successful pin as a witness choice, not a necessary source deduction. All residual calls remain degree-feasible after pin deletion because every removed edge costs at most one list color. Reclose at every pin and at stagnation. At most 4n+1 paid decisions, including failed pins, no recursion tree of independent branches. Verify the complete source witness. This is not BD14's dense affine probe: arbitrary parity guards may fall outside the domain. The closure used is certified singleton deletion and actual unary list restrictions, not free global semantic hull.

## Theorem 2 (strict native star compiler obstruction)
Every unpinned BW13/BW14 pure native star network with distinct private centers, pivot and two distinct endpoints, physical degree <=4, no extra predicates, is SAT. No cactus or edge-disjoint-cycle premise is needed.
Proof: if a connected component has a vertex of degree <4, greedy color in reverse spanning-tree order rooted at that slack vertex. Each nonroot has at least one uncolored incident edge, root has at most three total, so a color remains. Otherwise the component is 4-regular and bipartite between stars and terminals. A star has three distinct neighbors with edge multiplicities 2,1,1. Suppose every block is a uniformly multiplied clique or cycle. Take a leaf block, or sole block. A clique in a bipartite graph is at most a pair; no pair has four parallel edges, so its noncut vertex cannot have degree four. A cycle has even length >=4 and at least two centers, one noncut. That center would have exactly two distinct neighbors in the entire graph, contradicting three. Thus a nonbrick block exists and Theorem 1 decides SAT and reconstructs. No assumption on endpoint cycle overlap. A single full-color pin in each component is also always extendible by global XOR translation of a solution, which preserves exclusions.
Consequence: an exact both-label compiler of arbitrary 3-CNF to THIS unpinned native star class is mathematically impossible, even with unbounded gadget size, because every image is SAT. The original DO01 Fano UNSAT registry is an explicit falsifier, with degree-four one-star encoding SAT and exact doubled encoding exceeding degree4 (degree8). More general pure-exclusion degree4 plus arbitrary full pins is polynomial by Theorem 1, so a uniform polynomial exact compiler to it would itself suffice to establish P=NP, not follow from local gadget equivalence. No proof that such a compiler exists. Low-only pins at excess degree, higher-arity balance and guard relations must remain explicit. This closes the loophole in BW14's proposed overlapping-cycle splitting direction on the pure unpinned subclass; do not try to derive universal coverage by inventing free incidence.
