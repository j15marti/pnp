## Uniform planarization
Given any strict signed E4/B4 network, or pre-wire 4-valent multigraph, place vertices in disjoint disks along a horizontal line, four distinct port columns per vertex; route each wire from port column vertically up to its own unique integer height, horizontally to the other column, then vertically down. Inside a disk four arcs to its vertex can be nonintersecting. Distinct heights and columns avoid triple crossings, and at most 2*choose(W,2) crossings occur for W original wires. Replace each crossing by the disk gadget, with crossed boundary pairs corresponding to the two original wires. Preserve actual original wire sign on one segment, compensating gadget endpoint offsets; other segments transport the raw bit unchanged. Ordered crossings can be computed by pair tests and sorting. Every satisfying source lift extends using the constant table; every satisfying planar lift transports exactly the original wire signs and decodes to a source solution.

For simplicity even with loops or parallel segments use a planar 2-terminal equality gadget on the octahedral graph minus edge 0-2 (opposite pairs 0/1,2/3,4/5). Endpoints are cofacial in the merged face. At every vertex B4; summing degrees gives equal exterior bits by parity. Hamiltonian cycle 0,4,2,1,5,3,0 witnesses exterior 00 and its complement witnesses 11. Replace each segment with a private copy, assign its sign at the entrance. This preserves existence, not necessarily count. It yields a simple closed degree-four graph; all original port incidences are retained and each new vertex has degree four. Total vertices and wires O(V+W^2), integer coordinates and labels polynomial in original N; construction, output, checks, witness lifting and reverse reading polynomial. No general native SAT decision is supplied. I21/BW23 source adapter + planarization gives NP-hardness; an explicit port assignment is a polynomial certificate, hence NP-complete. This includes known embedding and no pins. Universal planar native terminal would itself imply P=NP; not established. A planarity-only progress claim does not discharge U-PROGRESS.
