## A. Four-state holonomy meet (PROVED)
For every explicit private simple rim of length L>=3, two forbidden exterior colors a_i(z), b_i(z) per site, signed edge x_i+x_(i+1)!=s_i, all over F2^2, and actual common affine chart G, define
 d_i=a_i+b_i; k_i=a_i+a_(i+1)+s_i;
 t_i=d_i if d_i!=0 and k_i in {0,d_i}, otherwise t_i=0.
Indices modulo L. Define p meet q = p if p=q, else 0. This is associative, commutative, idempotent with 0 absorbing: the meet of a nonempty sequence is nonzero precisely when every entry equals the same nonzero d. For any binary tree on nonempty disjoint leaf sets let each internal hub store the meet of its children. Let t be root and H=XOR_i s_i. BW07 gives exact rejection iff t!=0 and H=(1+(L mod 2))*t. Indeed BW07's common nonzero forbidden difference and edge span conditions are exactly t=d; its holonomy test is the last equation. No independence assumption on any of the original exterior words; retain every incidence and guard. Every selector word has exactly one full hub assignment (including words rejected at the root). A balanced tree has 2L-1 state hubs and ceil(log2 L) depth. Any prescribed binary split works. These hubs are holonomy summaries, not private rim color assignments.

Sharp within deterministic compositional summaries: for odd unsigned rims with all a_i=0, each nonempty segment can realize any state independently by setting b_i=d for all its sites (d=0 gives state zero). With two nonempty segments, rejection is exactly p=q!=0. The four rows of this binary relation are distinct: choose the other segment's nonzero state d to distinguish d from any other p, including 0. Therefore any deterministic summary of one segment that must work against all other segments needs at least four values (two bits), even if the common boundary a is supplied. This is a local summary lower bound, not a runtime or nondeterministic representation lower bound.

## B. Exact bounded odd-wheel compiler (PROVED)
A conjunction E of r<=5 scalar affine equations can be forbidden by a single private unsigned odd rim: take an odd length >=max(3,r+1), at most 7. Put a_0=0, a_j=2*f_j for the r equation-failure bits f_j, pad remaining a's with 0, put b_j=a_j+1 at every site. The BW07 criterion says it fails exactly when all f_j=0. The direct argument is that when all are zero the identical two-color lists cannot alternate around an odd rim; otherwise at least one adjacent pair of lists differs, so a greedy path starting with an unmatched color closes. Every actual port is an affine observation of the same shared chart, including new two-bit hub variables. No new independent copies of original literals or hub bits.

For a leaf, t_i=d iff E_d where E_d consists of three scalar equations (two for a_i+b_i=d, one for k_i in span(d)). For each d!=0 forbid (t_i=d AND NOT row_j) for each of the three rows, and (E_d AND t_i[j]!=d[j]) for each of two output bits. All these are affine conjunctions; 5 per d, 15 total, at most four rows. Enforcing all three iff conditions also forces t_i=0 when no E_d holds. For a merge, t=d iff p=q=d, now four defining rows: 6 forbidden events per d, 18 total, max five rows. For root forbid t=d for each nonzero d passing H test (at most 3). Compile every forbidden event to its private odd rim as above. Total at most 15L+18(L-1)+3=33L-15 rims, length at most seven; private interiors independent at fixed chart, including fixed summary hub words. Actual summary words use one common pair of bits at all consumers; physical port copies can be explicit affine synchronization constraints, not free equality assumptions. This is O(L) new wheels and hubs, O(L) graph vertices/edges for fixed gadget sizes (indexed encoding and dense chart rows polynomial in total encoded input). Both directions: any original extension supplies legal exterior z, evaluate all hubs uniquely, then each new rim has an actual lift. Any expanded model satisfies every iff and root test, hence has original feasible z, and four-color cycle DP gives actual original rim coloring. Projection count on exterior chart or exterior plus unique summary words is preserved; multiplicity of private replacement rims need not equal original private rim count.

For arbitrary explicit signed two-bit exclusion graphs, BW06 per-edge two-hub twisted odd rims are an exact first step (one twist suffices for decision, all three for BW06's original balanced multiplicity). Apply B to their disjoint interiors; keep original shared vertex bits, signs, outside predicates, and isolates. A fixed length yields polynomial construction and source reconstruction. This is a forward adapter for signed-edge geometry with explicit affine synchronization, not recognition/inversion of arbitrary unannotated strict E4/B4 source. If required, guards themselves can be forward-encoded using Boolean XOR-chain constraints and CNF gates with standard constant-size clauses, then BW06's relative-anchor proper-3CNF compiler (short clauses can be padded using fresh bits and both signs for satisfiability). ## C. Arbitrary summary pins have exact affine-event transport (PROVED)
For node J and d!=0, define E_(J,d) as the conjunction of the three leaf equations for each i in J. By A, t_J=d iff E_(J,d); t_J=0 iff no E_(J,d) holds. Thus every protected nonzero hub pin can be appended as affine equations to the original chart; every protected zero pin contributes at most three disjoint rejection events. Root contributes its at most three disjoint bad events. This eliminates *all* newly created hubs even under arbitrary lists of exact hub-value pins, without enumeration of hub assignments. For a balanced tree, summing explicit leaf-row occurrences over all pinned nodes is O(L log L), before any dense coordinate representation. Unbalanced trees are O(L^2) and still polynomial. Shared exterior observations and every pin retain the original guard ID; conditional projections cannot be exported to the unpinned root. Pins on private rim colors or arbitrary affine combinations of summary bits are not included.

If a nonzero pin is above another pin, every descendant must have the same nonzero value; detect inconsistent pins, remove entailed ones. If a zero pin has a zero-pinned descendant, the ancestor is redundant (0 absorbing). Retain the inclusion-minimal zero pins, which are an antichain. Any zero pin implies zero at the root so the root bad events can then be omitted. Let k be the number of retained zero pins. Intersect all nonzero equations into G. For each zero pin expand (1-sum_d 1_E_(J,d)); root has the same form with only holonomy-eligible d. Expanding gives at most 4^(k+1) signed intersections; each Gaussian cardinality exact. This is a deterministic count/decision terminal in T<=4^(k+1) poly(N), S<=poly(N) using serial summation and exact integers, including construction, verification, closure and at most 2n conditional counts for original selector reconstruction (n free exterior bits; these extra pins are affine, not new zero-summary guards). For one original rim with k=O(log N), polynomial; arbitrarily many nonzero pins do not increase the exponential parameter. For many disjoint rims, charge all independent root conditions or use the existing BD07/DP08/BA07 domain-specific terminals. No polynomial cost from just a small local interface.

Closure repertoire: at the original/transported affine-event layer Gaussian inconsistency, empty intersection deletion, full cover rejection and relative codim-one complement pin; restart after every strict deduction and at stagnation before branching; each step decreases event count or chart dimension. Above/below pin propagation is certified on actual tree IDs. At fixed exterior word, signed-edge arc support is rerun before DP; in the checker the guarded CNF block adapter uses unit closure before each branch and restarts after every pin. ## D. Protected-pin firewall (PROVED)
Even a SINGLE always-feasible original odd rim can encode arbitrary 3SAT through zero pins on disjoint summary subtrees. Given m>=1 clauses, make L=4m+1, all s=0. For clause j use four consecutive sites a=(0,2*l_1,2*l_2,2*l_3), where l are literal truth bits on the common original selector chart. The next site starts at 0. Put b=a+1 on these sites. In each four-leaf block, t_J=1 iff every consecutive high-bit difference vanishes, iff all three literals are false; otherwise t_J=0. At the final extra site a=b=0, so its state is 0 and root always 0. Choose a binary tree grouping the four sites of each clause first, then balance the m block roots and the final site. The entire unpinned rim is extendable for every original selector assignment. Protect each clause-block hub with t_J=0. Joint feasibility is now exactly the source CNF. Both directions follow from A and original DP; construction polynomial, NP membership immediate. m=0 can be handled by a three-site trivial rim. The local tree of summary gates and all its hubs has constant per-edge interface, but the shared external affine chart carries the original source correlations. This refutes universal polynomial guarded composition based solely on the size of a two-bit summary or number of colors; such a solver would imply P=NP. It does NOT refute P=NP, prove that the unpinned roots are hard, or exclude nonlinear reorganization. In particular S06/DP01's independent row/guard assumptions do not follow. This is a concrete instance of DR07's guard acquisition warning.
