## B. Doubled rim: exact affine directional projection (PROVED)
Let a cycle of length l>=3 have two signed edges at each consecutive pair, with affine signs s_i,t_i, and d_i=s_i+t_i. Write J(u,v)=(v,u+v), the order-three permutation on nonzero colors. If some d_i=0 the duplicate creates slack. Otherwise the allowed color differences on edge i are precisely the affine line
       A_i=s_i+J(d_i)+span(d_i).
(The complement formula is DA11 B.) A cycle coloring exists iff one can choose a_i in A_i with XOR_i a_i=0: necessity by telescoping vertex colors, sufficiency by starting at any color and cumulatively XORing differences. Hence, with R=XOR_i (s_i+J(d_i)), support holds iff R lies in span{d_i}. Over F2^2 two distinct nonzero directions span the whole color space.
Exact escape union on the entire chart:
       OR_i [d_i=0]
       OR_{i>0} [d_i != d_0]
       OR [R=0] OR [R=d_0].
Interpret each vector inequality as the OR of the three equations to its nonzero values. Thus <=4l-1 raw affine terms, each <=2 scalar rows, including all degeneracies. This is independent of the number of signed track selections or of the exterior dimension. If all directions equal d, the rim rejects precisely when R is outside {0,d}. For each fixed nonzero d, choose a nonzero linear functional with kernel span(d); the two exclusions on a doubled edge become an exact single-bit equality on the color difference, but the direction itself is guarded. Changing direction can remove the global obstruction; replacing variable directions by a single unconditional parity pin is unsound.
Fixed-word lift by 4-state XOR-sum DP over the differences, storing one predecessor per layer and state, O(l) word work/space plus sign evaluation, then telescope and verify original two exclusions per edge. No enumeration of 2^l difference choices necessary. Even/odd rims handled uniformly.
