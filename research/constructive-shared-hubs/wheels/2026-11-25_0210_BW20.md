For any two feasible boundary words at Hamming distance d, choose matching lifts. Symmetric difference on the common ambient graph is a disjoint union of alternating cycles and paths; exactly d endpoints at changed leaves, hence d even and d/2 paths. Switching each path preserves matching feasibility for the resulting boundary word. The k+r tag-difference matrix on these paths has kernel dimension at least max(0,d/2-k-r). Independent switches have distinct boundary words. Thus |R| >= 2^max(0,d/2-k-r). For equality on d even ports (two words, distance d), k+r >= d/2-1. A single pure B4 graph cannot realize equality on four or more open ports even with unlimited size, nor can signed duplications bypass this bound. This is a gadget bound, not an unrestricted complexity lower bound. Guard rows need actual port maps, not free semantic identities.
