## 2. The signed two-spoke cycle theorem (PROVED)
Let Q=F2^2, x in F2^n encode all shared observations, and let z_0,...,z_(k-1) be private rim colors for k>=3. Each rim edge forbids z_(i+1)=z_i+s_i, with known s_i in Q (indices cyclic). Exactly two spoke exclusions at each rim vertex forbid z_i=A_i(x), z_i=B_i(x), with arbitrary given affine maps A_i,B_i:F2^n->Q. Identical hub IDs remain identical. In a literal signed-color graph, each map is one hub color plus a constant. General affine maps are a typed extension, not a claim of free physical realization. No unaccounted incident factor may use a private rim variable. If costs, tags, fixed rim observations, or other couplings exist they must be retained explicitly; the following unconditional elimination is for pure feasibility with private interiors.

For fixed x, list L_i=Q minus {A_i(x),B_i(x)} has size 2 or 3. Write P_0=0, P_i=sum_(j<i)s_j, H=sum_i s_i. If any list has size>=3, choose that vertex last and greedily color along the remaining path, then avoid its two forbidden neighbor colors. If all lists have size two but some edge has L_(i+1) != L_i+s_i, choose at endpoint i a color whose translated value is absent at endpoint i+1. Traverse the other way around the cycle, greedily choosing colors. The closing edge is automatically satisfied. In both cases a coloring exists.

Otherwise all lists are translates of L_0 around the cycle, including the wrap edge. Let L_0={u,u+d}, d!=0. Each edge forces z_(i+1)=z_i+s_i+d, because one of the two list entries is excluded. A consistent cyclic coloring exists iff H=(k mod 2)d. The wrap-list equality itself requires H in {0,d}. Thus the complete failure condition is: all lists are translated copies of one two-list, H in {0,d}, and H!=(k mod 2)d.

Equivalently enumerate the six unordered excluded pairs D={p,p+d} in Q. (Their complements have the same difference d.) Retain only candidates with H in {0,d} and H!=(k mod 2)d. For each candidate define an affine system G_D(x):
    A_i(x)+B_i(x)=d
    ell_d(A_i(x))=ell_d(p+P_i)
for every i, where ell_d is the unique nonzero binary linear form on Q annihilating d. First equation is two bits; second is one. These equations say exactly that the unordered excluded pair at i is D+P_i. Each G_D is an affine subspace or empty; reduce by GF2 elimination, discard inconsistent guards. For every x, the rim is extendible iff x avoids the union of these at most six affine subspaces. If k odd and H=0, at most six; if k odd and H!=0, none; if k even and H!=0, at most two; if k even and H=0, none. This is an exact symbolic projection with <=18k raw binary equations, no enumeration of hub assignments or private rim assignments.

The proof is both directions and explicitly handles signed holonomy. A nonzero rim twist is NOT removable by a gauge around a cycle unless the full holonomy condition is retained. For witness construction one may use the greedy proof or a four-color path DP conditioned on at most four first colors. DP stores predecessor per vertex/color and tests closing edge, O(k) bounded-color transitions and words. Recheck every original spoke and rim edge. Segment splitting is exact by Boolean 4x4 transfer relations conditional on the SAME shared observation word, using associative relational composition and an exact closing relation; segment boundaries or copied rim variables must be synchronized, not independently existentially chosen. No enumeration of all shared words is implied by this composition.

## 3. New two-tip gadget: all four relative-state masks (PROVED)
For a fixed nonzero t in Q, desired forbidden difference d0, two shared tips a,b in Q, take any odd private rim length k>=3, all rim shifts zero. At every rim vertex except i=1 exclude {a,b+d0+t}; at i=1 exclude {a+t,b+d0}. These are two signed spoke incidences per vertex; with distinct tips every rim vertex has degree four. If a+b=d0, excluded pairs coincide everywhere and have two distinct entries, so odd rim is impossible. Conversely if a+b=d0+t, all excluded pairs are singleton, hence extendible. For either remaining difference, two excluded pairs are distinct translates by t; hence extendible by the cycle theorem. Therefore
    exists rim(z): W_(d0)(a,b,z) iff a+b != d0.
This is a signed inequality gadget, not the earlier fixed-center OR. An ordinary odd double wheel with the same two spokes at every vertex instead enforces equality of the tips (or shifted equality after changing a spoke label globally). The distinguished translated vertex is what reverses that relation to inequality.

Any relative-state mask M subset Q is the conjunction of at most four independently private wheels W_d for d not in M, on the SAME two tips. All 16 masks, including empty/full, are obtained pointwise; sign shifts are retained. Copies for independent constraints have private rims and may reuse/synchronize tips through equality wheels. Projection and lift commute with conjunction exactly when private interiors disjoint and every boundary assignment is shared. Opposite pin constraints must not be erased by a local palette relabeling.

## 4. Source transfer and a sharp global limitation (PROVED, composition symbolic)
Apply BW01's already established pointwise ordinary four-color compiler to any explicitly encoded signed E4/B4 network in its source domain. Replace each ordinary graph edge uv by one length-three W_0(u,v). For each fixed assignment of the original graph colors, local rims have extensions iff that assignment was a proper coloring. This includes palette edges; coordinate colors for signed shifts may be chosen globally and the projected ordinary edge relation does not depend on the coordinate naming. Both SAT directions follow by composing BW01's lift and the new local lift; all source IDs kept. For a graph with v vertices and e edges this adds 3e private vertices and 9e signed edges (3 rim +6 spokes per wheel), total v+3e vertices, with linear incidence count and O(log(v+e)) bit IDs. Each private rim vertex has degree exactly four; shared tips may have arbitrarily large degree, the result is not a strict E4/B4 network or a globally 4-regular graph, and not claimed planar. Typed signed graph edges forbid one XOR difference, not ordinary graph edges in the gadget interior. Full end-to-end BW01 compiler replay not executed here.

The resulting local guards for W_0, after projection simplification, reject exactly the affine codimension-two system a+b=0. Thus global SAT for a collection of rank-two rejection guards, or constant-size two-tip wheels with exact cheap acquisition, includes arbitrary ordinary four-colorability. Standard graph-colorability NP-completeness transfers via linear construction (and NP membership by explicit graph witness). A uniform polynomial solver for all these rank-two guard systems would establish P=NP. This is not a proof that such a solver cannot exist. In particular the DO03 high-girth graph family may be mapped to the wheel-only form; no claim that its raw girth/width obstructions transfer through the new encoding. The graph-level residual after projection is exactly the original graph. Splitting all source constraints into private degree-four rims and low-rank interfaces alone is not a universal progress argument.

## 5. Certified affine rejection closure and exact union cancellation (PROVED)
For a fixed authenticated branch K of affine equations, reduce each bad guard relative to K using Gaussian elimination. An inconsistent guard is impossible; rank-zero consistent guard means UNSAT in K; a consistent rank-one guard requires its sole remaining equation to be false, so append the opposite equation and restart closure. Root deductions are unconditional, all deductions under K retain K. Do not confuse the local bad guard with an already established global contradiction. Test closure initially, after deductions and at stagnation before any branch.

Parallel-coset cancellation: for two canonical nonempty guards with equal row-space basis A, RHS b and c, if b=c they are duplicates. If b!=c then their union is the affine system A x in b+span(b+c). Choose one row index where b+c=1, drop that equation, and XOR it into each remaining row with corresponding difference one. This exact Boolean OR merge reduces guard count and codimension by one. Containment deletion is also safe (symbolic). Pair-parallel merge is not complete coverage: in F2^2 the three nonparallel lines x1=0, x2=0, x1+x2=0 cover all four words; no two are parallel. Rank-one forcing solves this particular example, so it is a barrier only to the merge rule alone. More general guard avoidance is not automatically 2SAT: rank-two constraints may use arbitrary correlated observations.

Conditional terminal: after closure, suppose a supplied common independent observation basis on the free affine coordinates makes each surviving rank<=2 guard a system fixing at most two basis bits (check by linear algebra, reduce RHS). Its complement is one ordinary clause of width <=2. Solve 2SAT by SCC and reconstruct full free coordinates by linear algebra, then rim lifts. This is exact and polynomial including verification of the basis, not a proof that such a basis can always be found or exists. Basis discovery and SCC backend were not executed. DO03/DA03: raw rank-two guards need not satisfy this alignment. S06 matching/count hypotheses likewise are not implied by wheel shape. Any conditional deduction on an owner/cost-bearing region requires preservation of that interface; no budget/cost projection theorem claimed.
