## Theorem A: affine proof transport, not merely equisatisfiability
Use the Boolean ring over F2, squarefree polynomials, XOR and multiply by one variable with Boolean reduction, as DA16. A signed E4/B4 graph has separate port bits p. Let L consist of all signed wire rows, E4 equality rows and B4 even-parity rows; include the actual affine guard if any. Each B4 also has the quadratic axiom Q=(1+p0+p1)(1+p0+p2)=0. Under even parity, this exactly excludes both constant words and leaves precisely balance two.

Gaussian closure either refutes L at degree one or gives an exact chart p=Ax+b with x chosen from free actual port coordinates. Include reduced quadratics q_i=Q_i(Ax+b) as explicit chart axioms, even if singleton or constant before subsequent certified closure. Both-label equivalence and witness lifting are pointwise, preserving all guarded equations and source maps.

Given a native BPC refutation of degree d, substitute p=Ax+b into every proof line. Every linear axiom maps to zero, Q_i to q_i. XOR commutes. A multiplication by p_j becomes multiplication by its affine form: form all variable products of the substituted premise separately then XOR, with the possible constant term. Intermediate degree <=d+1 even when high-degree terms cancel in the resulting line. Thus native degree d implies chart degree <=max(2,d+1); for d>=2, <=d+1. No hidden assumption about satisfying assignments is used.

Conversely derive each native equation p_j+(Ax+b)_j=0 by recorded XOR of L. Lift a chart axiom q_i to the original ring using free actual port bits. Write Q_i=UV, q_i=U'V' where U,V,U',V' are affine. Then Q_i+q_i=(U+U')V+U'(V+V'). Both affine differences are in the span of L. Derive their products by XOR and individual variable multiplications of degree <=2. XOR with Q_i to derive q_i. Every subsequent chart BPC multiplication is by an actual free port, so a chart degree d proof gives a native degree <=max(2,d) proof. The same argument works with signed representative ports (multiply by p_j+s; adding the premise cannot cancel a new degree d+1 term, so the multiplication step stays within d). This is a constructive proof lift, not an assertion that equal affine hulls imply semantic equivalence.
