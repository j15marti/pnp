\section*{ODDFIELD1: odd-field product rank does not control Hadamard exterior rank}
\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT finite algebra plus COMPUTATIONAL comparison; P versus NP remains OPEN.}
\paragraph{Discriminator.} For Boolean-grid interpolation polynomial \(f\) over \(\mathbb F_p\) and primitive root \(g\), compare ranks of shifted functions and shifted products with binary Hadamard exterior rank \(d\).
\paragraph{Hostile separation.} For COVERBREAK1 word 0000110 at \((p,h,m)=(13,7,5)\), odd-field shifted-product rank is
\[
\boxed{10=\binom52},
\]
while verified binary exterior rank is \(\boxed{d=5}\). Thus pre-label product-rank deficiency is not necessary for low binary exterior rank.
\paragraph{Exhaustive comparison.} Over all 126 nonconstant length-7 Boolean words at \((13,7,5)\), counts for (odd-field product rank,binary d) are
\[
(10,10):94,\ (9,10):12,\ (10,5):6,\ (9,5):6,\ (8,10):4,\ (10,9):3,\ (10,8):1.
\]
At \((17,7,5)\), all 126 words have binary \(d=10\), while odd-field product rank ranges from 7 to 10. Neither rank is a monotone proxy for the other.
\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT value-diversity lower bound.} In the univariate multiplicative-orbit model, the nonzero orbit visits every \(x\in\mathbb F_p^*\). If nonconstant \(f\) has degree \(D\le h-1\), then each \(f-c\) has at most \(D\) roots. Hence
\[
\boxed{|f(\mathbb F_p^*)|\ge\left\lceil\frac{p-1}{D}\right\rceil\ge\left\lceil\frac{p-1}{h-1}\right\rceil.}
\]
For hostile 0000110 at \(p=13\), the orbit-value set is exactly \(\{0,1,7\}\), size 3. The same grid word at \(p=17\) has ten distinct orbit values and binary \(d=10\). Thus actual vMS-scale \(p/h\) forces enormous value diversity for every nonconstant leaf.
\paragraph{Guard.} Large value diversity alone does not prove \(\dim U=\omega(m)\). Distinct binary labels may have dependencies, and exterior growth is a synchronous sliding-window property. A theorem must use both diversity and multiplicative-orbit ordering.
\paragraph{Falsified route.} The implication low binary \(d\) implies low \(\mathbb F_p\) shifted-product rank is FALSIFIED by the finite control. Thus an \(f/q\)-only Schur-product rank argument cannot be the missing invariant.
