\section*{COVERBAT1: exact finite slice-cover battery}
\textbf{STATUS: COMPUTATIONAL / OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. This experiment tests the current single team target on the declared source-sanctioned SU05/Hadamard axis-parallel Boolean-grid LDE control realization. It does not recover the author-unique vMS binary sampler and proves no asymptotic theorem.

\paragraph{Method.}
For each nonconstant Boolean grid word in
\[
(p,h,m)\in\{(17,4,4),(29,5,5),(37,6,6),(43,7,7)\},
\]
construct the cyclic legal slice spaces \(S_s=\operatorname{im}X_s\). Compute
\(R=\dim\sum_sS_s\), synchronous exterior dimension
\(d=\dim\sum_s\wedge^2S_s\), greedy maximum-innovation cover, and the true minimum
\[
\tau_L=\min\{|Q|:\sum_{s\in Q}S_s=W_L\}
\]
by exhaustive subset search up to the greedy cover size.

\paragraph{Results.}
All 232 nonconstant words had
\[
\boxed{R=m,\qquad d=\binom m2.}
\]
The exact cover distribution was:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
(p,h,m)&\tau=1&\tau=2&\text{innovation profiles}\\\hline
(17,4,4)&14&0&(4)\\
(29,5,5)&29&1&(5)\text{ or }(4,1)\\
(37,6,6)&60&2&(6)\text{ or }(5,1)\\
(43,7,7)&0&126&(6,1)
\end{array}
\]
Greedy cover equalled the exact minimum in every tested instance. Examples of the \(\tau=2\) controls are grid words \(11011\) at \((29,5,5)\), \(001100\) at \((37,6,6)\), and \(0000001\) at \((43,7,7)\).

\paragraph{Certificates.}
DIMSUM2 gives
\[
\rho(H)\ge1+R+d=1+m+\binom m2=M,
\]
hence the quadratic-feature rank is maximal in every tested nonconstant instance. Therefore \(K_2(H)=0\) in these finite controls. SLICECOVER1 independently gives superlinear lower bounds; for \(\tau=1\) it also reaches \(M\). For \(\tau=2\) it is weaker than DIMSUM2 but still demonstrates the small-cover behavior sought by the experiment.

\paragraph{Hostile-regime verdict.}
The hostile survivor
\[
R=\Theta(m),\quad\tau_L=\Theta(m),\quad d=O(m)
\]
was \textbf{not observed}. The tested controls instead sit at the opposite extreme:
\(R=m\), \(\tau_L\le2\), \(d=\binom m2\). This is evidence against an \(O(m)\) product-span contraction for this finite declared realization, not a theorem about the vMS asymptotic family.
