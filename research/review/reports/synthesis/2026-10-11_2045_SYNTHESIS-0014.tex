\section{The exact resource theorem}

Let $C$ be any polynomial-time recognizable NP-hard family of encoded $3$-CNFs.  A \emph{log-succinct decision resource scheme} is a deterministic uniform polynomial-time predicate $B(F,z)$ and a constant $a$ such that, for every $F\in C$ of encoded length $N$,
\[
F\in\SAT\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists z\in\{0,1\}^{\le a\log_2N+a}:B(F,z)=1.
\tag{R}
\]
The string $z$ may encode a restriction, compatibility certificate, program, circuit, seed, transcript, or any other object; the theorem charges its complete serialization and the fixed predicate that uses it.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{LOG-SUCCINCT-RESOURCE-EQUIV1}]
For every such NP-hard family $C$, $\SAT\in\mathsf P$ if and only if a log-succinct decision resource scheme for $C$ exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\SAT\in\mathsf P$, let $B(F,z)$ ignore $z$ and run the fixed polynomial SAT decider; take $z=\epsilon$.  Conversely, enumerate all strings of length at most $a\log_2N+a$, evaluate $B(F,z)$, and accept iff one evaluation accepts.  There are at most
\[
2^{a\log_2N+a+1}=2^{a+1}N^a
\]
candidates.  Thus restricted SAT on $C$ is in polynomial time, and the uniform many-one reduction from SAT to $C$ yields $\SAT\in\mathsf P$.
\end{proof}

If $B$ runs in $N^{b+o(1)}$ time, the enumerating decider runs in $N^{a+b+o(1)}$ time, plus encoding and verification.  A polynomial-time decoder that maps $(F,z)$ to a satisfying assignment is a special case by composing the decoder with witness verification.  MODELBRIDGE-0018's theorem is recovered by taking $z$ to be the source-relative program and $B$ to boundedly simulate it and verify its output.

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{LOG-SUCCINCT-RESOURCE-AS-INTERMEDIATE1}]
The absence of all log-succinct, polynomially testable resources on an NP-hard clean core is separation-strength.  It cannot be used as a previously weaker intermediate lemma without independently proving the equivalent lower bound.
\end{corollary}

The corollary is \RETIRED{} only as an \emph{intermediate route}.  It does not say a useful source pass cannot exist; it says that ``no short resource exists'' merely restates the separation once the resource is uniformly testable.

\section{The size/access/SAME\_REALIZATION trilemma}

Every proposed universal resource now falls into one of three declared cases.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Case \Alph*.}]
\item $|z|=O(\log N)$ and $B$ is polynomial: exhaustive access is polynomial, so existence is equivalent to $\SAT\in\mathsf P$.  No resource lower bound has been obtained.
\item $|z|=\omega(\log N)$ or the test/decoder is superpolynomial: enumeration is no longer a polynomial construction.  But representation size alone does not lower-bound the one-bit SAT decision; LB-0017 and TESTS-0013 give the explicit/implicit witness controls.
\item The object is implementation-internal, untestable externally, or defined from a preferred trace: wrapper-equivalent programs can change or omit it.  A new arbitrary-decider SAME\_REALIZATION transfer is required.
\end{enumerate}

This trilemma falsifies the candidate that ``succinct compatibility'' by itself supplies a universal extraction bottleneck.  It does not falsify an externally fixed source pass with a separately proved recursive endpoint.

\section{Counterfactual $P=NP$ and the surviving proof spine}

Assume $\SAT\in\mathsf P$ via an arbitrary uniform deterministic decider with exponent $K$.  Standard self-reduction produces a witness in polynomial time with exponent depending on $K$; the empty-resource scheme above also exists.  Neither fact contradicts an unconditional theorem.  A contradiction would require one ordinary realization that, after charging acquisition, all resource probes, failed routes, replay, output, and peak workspace, falls inside a model-matched forbidden class such as the applicable FLMV time--space region.

The shortest surviving spine is therefore
\[
\SAT\in\mathsf P
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{externally fixed, predecision, hereditary source pass}}
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{one charged forbidden realization}}
\Longrightarrow\bot.
\]
Both boxed implications remain \OPEN.  The first may not be justified by log-succinct resource nonexistence, semantic witness volume, compatibility rank, or an internal trace requirement.

\section{Barrier, cross-domain, and post-quantum audit}

SAT self-reducibility and enumeration of $O(\log N)$ nondeterministic bits are \textbf{direct theorem transfers}.  Search-versus-decision separations for general NP relations delimit the claim: this report uses SAT's special self-reducibility only for the witness corollary, while the predicate theorem needs no search-to-decision premise.

VLSI routing/storage and distributed communication lower bounds charge explicit materialized signals, messages, or local wires.  They are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to an unrestricted one-bit RAM/Turing computation without a simulation theorem.  Control-theoretic observability is an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}: an observable state is defined relative to an observation model, which has not been forced on arbitrary SAT executions.

Schur squares, quadratic relation ranks, Hilbert functions, and syzygies are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} here because no code or graded algebra is forced by (R).  Collision entropy is exact for a declared map $z\mapsto B(F,z)$, but an $O(\log N)$ domain is already exhaustible; it adds no lower bound.  LWE-style worst-case/average-case architecture and special-soundness extraction remain \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} until a construction-specific black-box reduction and forbidden witness theorem are supplied.  Cryptographic binding and random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible.

The theorem relativizes if the same oracle is supplied to $B$ and to the resulting enumeration, so it does not cross Baker--Gill--Solovay.  Its proof is equally compatible with low-degree/algebraic oracle access and provides no nonalgebrizing hinge.  Natural Proofs is not directly triggered because no constructive large circuit property is established.  Uniformity is essential: per-input nonuniform strings or decoders are not a uniform resource scheme.
