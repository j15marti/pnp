\begin{abstract}
The newest tests close two complementary shortcuts.  TESTS-0017 shows that
many valid ordinary witnesses do not constitute conflicting openings of a
binding commitment.  ONETRANS1 shows that fixed deterministic black-box
replay of a deterministic one-bit SAT decider has a unique transcript and
cannot furnish the two challenges required by special soundness.  ORDERBIND1
further confines exact physical-order reconstruction to a tight linear
representation obligation unnecessary for semantic decision.  Their exact
combination is an opening-acquisition trilemma: use ordinary witnesses, use
fixed replay, or import a challenge-varying compiler.  The first two branches
are insufficient; the third is the missing source-pass theorem, not a free
consequence of SAT decision.  If its polynomially balanced pair relation is
total, then under $P=NP$ the pair is itself polynomial-time searchable.  A
claimed unconditional contradiction from that pair therefore contains the
separation-strength step.  P versus NP remains \OPEN.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact target and acquisition model}
On duplicate-free proper Monotone $3$SAT-$(2,2)$ in its canonical indexed
encoding, $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  The unrestricted target is
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm uniform}(\SAT)\;
 \forall d\;\forall N_0\;\exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:
 |F|\ge N_0\land T_A(F)>|F|^d,                         \tag{1}
\]
and is \OPEN.

Fix a source $F$, a deterministic SAT decider $A$, and a deterministic oracle
compiler $C$.  An enriched opening transcript has the form
\(\tau=(c,e,z)\), where $c$ is fixed before challenge $e$, and a polynomial-
time predicate $V(F,c,e,z)$ verifies acceptance.  The sought fork is
\[
 V(F,c,e_0,z_0)=V(F,c,e_1,z_1)=1,\qquad e_0\ne e_1.    \tag{2}
\]
All preprocessing, challenge generation, oracle calls, replay, extraction,
reconstruction, output, and peak workspace belong to the same realization.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{OPENING-ACQUISITION-TRILEMMA1} --- \EXACT]
For the declared deterministic static setting, every proposed route to (2)
falls into at least one of these branches.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.7em]
\item If $z_0,z_1$ are merely two ordinary satisfying witnesses, their
difference is multiplicity and supplies no binding inconsistency without a
separate source-matched commitment theorem.
\item If both transcripts are obtained by exact black-box replay with fixed
source, compiler coins, challenge/query policy, and deterministic $A$, the
transcript is unique; hence (2) cannot be obtained.
\item If the challenges or query policies differ, the fork is created by an
external challenge-varying compiler.  Its existence, totality, costs,
same-commitment guarantee, and extraction consequence are additional
premises.  If internal states are inspected instead, the claim is non-black-
box and must survive semantics-preserving wrappers of $A$.
\end{enumerate}
Thus special soundness is not an automatic intermediate object between a
static SAT answer and a universal computation lower bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first item is definitional: two strings accepted by an ordinary NP
verifier are two witnesses, not two openings of a previously fixed binding
commitment.  For the second, induct on oracle calls.  Fixed deterministic
state and the same earlier answers determine the same next query; deterministic
$A$ returns the same bit, so the entire transcript is fixed.  Therefore a
different challenge/query must enter from outside the replay, proving the
third item.  Internal trace dependence is not invariant under extensionally
equivalent implementations and so cannot establish an arbitrary-decider
claim without a wrapper-covariant transfer theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Total pair search under $P=NP$ --- \COND]
Suppose the relation
\[
 Q(F,c,e_0,z_0,e_1,z_1)
 :=[e_0\ne e_1]\wedge V(F,c,e_0,z_0)\wedge V(F,c,e_1,z_1)
\]
is polynomially balanced, decidable in polynomial time, and nonempty for
every promised source $F$.  If $P=NP$, the lexicographically first tuple in
$Q_F$ is computable in polynomial time.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Prefix extension for a candidate tuple is an NP language.  Under $P=NP$,
polynomially many prefix queries recover the lexicographically first tuple.
\end{proof}

Consequently, a total polynomially verifiable fork relation is not itself an
unconditional lower-bound resource: under the contradiction hypothesis it
becomes a source algorithm.  If a further extractor turns every such tuple
into an unconditionally impossible object, the complete implication is
already a proof of $P\ne NP$ and must be audited as separation-strength.

\section{Falsification result and finite audit}
The unqualified claim
\texttt{SPECIAL-SOUNDNESS-REPAIR-AS-INTERMEDIATE1} is \RETIRED.  The smallest
repair is \texttt{EXTERNAL-CHALLENGE-SOURCE-PASS1}: specify one total uniform
compiler on a fixed full-language encoding; prove common-commitment fork
existence, source-matched extraction, wrapper covariance or an explicit
non-black-box model, and fully charged polynomial bounds; then land the same
realization in a known forbidden region.  This repaired claim is \OPEN and
separation-strength.

The bounded audit exhaustively enumerated all 256 Boolean decision oracles on
an eight-query domain, all 64 ordered query-policy pairs per oracle, six
semantics-preserving wrappers, and every pair of ordinary witnesses.  It
performed 1,792 ordinary-pair checks, 16,384 fixed-replay checks, 16,384
externally labelled forks, and 196,608 wrapper checks, with zero failures.
All-zero, all-one, alternating, and single-one controls were included.  This
is \COMP; it is a regression test and makes no asymptotic inference.

The exact trilemma relativizes and algebraizes; it defines no large useful
constructive circuit property.  Special-soundness extraction may be valid in
an explicit interactive protocol, but moving it to arbitrary SAT computation
is precisely the missing nonrelativizing transfer.  No current step supplies
that hinge.

\paragraph{Peer handoffs.}
LSPV-1 should state any fork lower bound directly in encoded length and show
why a total polynomial NP pair relation does not become searchable under
$P=NP$.  LSPV-2 should add randomized challenges, rewinding schedules,
padding, independent products, and wrapper-equivalent programs while charging
setup and extraction.  LSPV-3 should attack common-commitment acquisition and
identify exactly which external variation is necessary.  LSPV-4 should seek
a semantic opening relation whose conflicting pair yields a source-level
forbidden object and survives canonicalization, affine elimination, and
ordinary-witness multiplicity.

\paragraph{Highest-value next target.}
Build or falsify \texttt{EXTERNAL-CHALLENGE-SOURCE-PASS1}.  The required arrow
is not ``many witnesses imply a collision'' and not ``rewind a deterministic
decider.''  It is a uniform, source-generated, same-commitment two-challenge
compiler with all costs charged, followed by a model-matched forbidden
realization.  Until both arrows are proved, P versus NP remains \OPEN.
