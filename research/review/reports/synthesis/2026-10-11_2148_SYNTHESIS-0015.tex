\paragraph{Claim card.}
The attacked claim had quantifier prefix
\begin{align}
 &\forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm uniform}^{\rm poly}(\SAT)\;
 \exists P_A\;\forall F:\nonumber\\[-0.3em]
 &\qquad\bigl(P_A\text{ emits before calling the named }A\bigr)
 \Longrightarrow P_A\text{ has an exponent independent of }A.
 \tag{NC}
\end{align}
The input model is a read-only random-access encoding of $F$.  The
SAME\_REALIZATION ledger includes direct source inspection, all local
computation, inlined code, classification, failed routes, child generation,
access, replay, output, and peak writable space.  A single inlined computation
whose exponent follows $A$ falsifies (NC).

\section{Exact no-call laundering theorem}

Fix two proper $3$CNFs
\[
 T=(x\vee y\vee z),\qquad
 U=\bigwedge_{\sigma\in\{0,1\}^3}
   (\ell_{1,\sigma}\vee\ell_{2,\sigma}\vee\ell_{3,\sigma}),
\]
where the clause indexed by $\sigma$ is falsified exactly by assignment
$\sigma$.  Then $T$ is satisfiable and $U$ is unsatisfiable; their sizes are
constant.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{PREDECISION-NO-CALL-LAUNDERING1}]
Let $L$ be any language with a total deterministic decider $B$ using time
$T_B(N)$ and writable space $S_B(N)$.  For every separately named program
$A$, there is a uniform transducer $P_B$ that, on input $x$, makes zero calls
to $A$, emits $T$ when $B(x)=1$ and $U$ otherwise, and satisfies
\[
 x\in L\iff P_B(x)\in\SAT.
\]
Its output length and output-access cost are $O(1)$, while its charged time
and space are $T_B(N)+O(1)$ and $S_B(N)+O(1)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Inline the code of $B$, execute it on the source, and select the fixed output
$T$ or $U$ from its answer.  There is no invocation of the separately named
$A$.  Exactness follows from the choices of $T$ and $U$.  Inlining changes
time and space by at most constant machine-simulation factors, and emitting or
locally addressing either fixed formula costs $O(1)$.
\end{proof}

Under the counterfactual $\SAT\in\mathsf P$, take $B$ to be any polynomial
SAT decider.  The pass is then polynomial, strict with child exponent zero,
source-checkable, bit-local, and syntactically before every call to the named
$A$, but it inherits $B$'s unknown polynomial exponent.  Taking $B=A$ and
inlining its instruction sequence shows that renaming the call interface is
not a repair.  DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP and RECOMP wrappers preserve the
semantic output while changing the charged implementation trace.

\begin{corollary}
``No current-scale SAT call'', ``before the supplied solver'', and
``predecision'' interpreted only by a named-call trace do not imply
$K$-independent construction, answer blindness, or a favorable time--space
bound.  \texttt{NO-CALL-PREDECISION-IMPLIES-K-INDEPENDENCE1} is
\RETIRED.
\end{corollary}

\section{The quantitative repair is the endpoint contract}

The smallest sound repair replaces call order by an external numerical
budget.  A pass contract must specify one fixed transducer $P$, bounds
$R(N),S(N)$ on \emph{all} source computation, an exact child/combiner
semantics, hereditary closure, bit-local access, and all output/replay costs.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{BOUNDED-SOURCE-PASS-ENDPOINT1}]
Suppose a total source-only pass and its fixed recursive evaluator decide SAT
on every encoded CNF, and suppose the complete composed execution has
simultaneous bounds $(C(N),P(N))$ in a machine model.  Then that composed
execution is a total uniform deterministic SAT decider with those same
bounds.  If $(C,P)$ lies in an unconditional forbidden SAT region for that
model, the pass/evaluator contract is false unconditionally.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The pass plus evaluator is, by the stated totality and exactness, one ordinary
SAT machine.  The SAME\_REALIZATION hypothesis assigns all charged costs to
that machine.  Applying the model-matched lower bound gives the conclusion.
\end{proof}

This theorem does not say that every useful local lemma is the endpoint.  It
says that the complete strengthened target---fixed source pass, total
heredity, closed recursion, arbitrary-exponent elimination, and forbidden
joint bounds---already is the separating realization.  The phrase
``predecision'' contributes no weaker logical bridge unless backed by an
independent numerical theorem about all computation performed before output.

\section{Primary literature and model limits}

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas work with ordinary random-access
machines and prove simultaneous time--space exclusions for SAT; their theorem
applies only after one actual total machine realizes both coordinates
\cite{FLMV}.  It does not convert a call-order annotation into a resource
bound.

Baker--Gill--Solovay construct relativized worlds with opposite answers to
the P-versus-NP question \cite{BGS}.  The inlining/laundering construction
relativizes: give $B$ the same oracle and inline that oracle machine.  Hence it
supplies no nonrelativizing hinge.  Aaronson--Wigderson's algebrization barrier
strengthens this warning \cite{AW}; low-degree extension access does not stop
the construction.  Razborov--Rudich concern large constructive properties
used for circuit lower bounds \cite{RR}.  No such property is used here, so
Natural Proofs is not the direct failure mode and is not bypassed.

\section{Hostile controls and transfer ledger}

Horn, $2$SAT, affine/Tseitin Gaussian elimination and bounded-treewidth
inputs reinforce the counterexample: a source algorithm may use the easiest
available decision method and still emit $T$ or $U$ before touching the named
solver.  Independent products do not help because one decision of the product
answer again selects a constant child.  Padding, redundant clauses and
alternate encodings change syntax but not the laundering pattern.  Wrapper
variation destroys any implementation-invariant exponent inferred from the
zero-call trace.  A maximal semantic oracle makes the bypass immediate, and
an algebraic oracle does not remove it.

The cryptographic notion of a commit phase is only an \textbf{ANALOGY /
CANDIDATE}: a binding transcript is meaningful because a protocol interface
forbids later dependence.  An ordinary source transducer has no analogous
physical phase boundary unless a computation model and numerical budget make
it explicit.  Importing binding or random-oracle assumptions would not be an
unconditional theorem transfer.
