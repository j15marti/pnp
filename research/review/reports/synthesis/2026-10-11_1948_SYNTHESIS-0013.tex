\begin{abstract}
RT-0017 generalizes the recognizable-island barrier from constant-answer to
mixed-answer clean-core families.  This synthesis sharpens the counterexample:
the YES and NO instances can be paired at exactly the same numbers of variables,
positive clauses, negative clauses, total clauses, and canonical encoded bits.
The numerical coincidence is exact.  A paired $K_{3,3}$ block has $(9,12)$
variables/clauses, while D\"ocker's fixed unsatisfiable ordinary Monotone
3-SAT-$(2,2)$ component has $(198,264)=22(9,12)$.  Thus $P_{\ell+22}$ is SAT
and $P_\ell\sqcup U$ is UNSAT at the same layer for every $\ell\ge1$, yet one
uniform syntax classifier decides their union in polynomial time.  Therefore
cofinal same-layer answer balance cannot repair pointwise compatibility
extraction, state exposure, memory, communication, or time necessity.  The
only surviving quantifier remains worst-case existential over inputs.  A
117,408-check audit passed.  No arbitrary-computation lower bound follows;
P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Primary-literature and transfer preflight}

The major obstacles were (i) whether answer diversity repairs the
recognizable-island counterexample and (ii) whether a fixed structural family
can be promoted to an arbitrary-decider lower bound.  D\"ocker's primary
paper proves that ordinary Monotone 3-SAT-$(2,2)$ has unique proper clauses,
exact signed degree $(2,2)$, a fixed UNSAT instance with 198 variables and 264
clauses, and an always-satisfiable complementary-clause subclass
\cite{docker}.  This transfers directly to the declared clean core.  Lynch's
complexity-core theorem says that recursive languages outside P have infinite
polynomial complexity cores \cite{lynch}; using it for SAT would already
require SAT outside P, so it cannot supply the missing implication.

VLSI routing, observability, distributed cuts, proof search, Schur-square
growth and branching-program size do not transfer to arbitrary SAT computation
without a model compiler.  The exact object correspondence here is only
formula-to-formula and wrapper-to-machine.  All other cited domains remain
useful structural analogies requiring a new transfer theorem.

\section{Clean core, encoding and exact quantifiers}

Let $\Htwo$ be duplicate-free proper Monotone 3-SAT-$(2,2)$.  In the declared
canonical fixed-width encoding, each literal has a sign bit and a
$w=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$-bit variable index; the header and clause
separators are deterministic functions of $(n,m)$.  Consequently formulas
with the same $(n,m)$ have the same encoded length $N=\Theta(m\log n)$.

The now-retired pointwise target was of the form
\[
 \exists b\ \forall A\ \exists N_0\ \forall F\in\Htwo:
 N_F\ge N_0\Rightarrow\operatorname{cost}_A(F)\ge b(N_F),
 \tag{PW}
\]
or replaced the inequality by mandatory exposure of a prescribed object.
The surviving target is instead
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm uniform}(\mathrm{SAT})\;
 \forall d\;\forall N_0\;\exists F\in\Htwo:
 N_F\ge N_0\land T_A(F)>N_F^d.
 \tag{WC}
\]
Equation (WC) is precisely separation-strength.  It remains \OPEN{}.

\section{Exact same-layer construction}

Let $P_k$ be the disjoint union of $k$ canonically numbered $K_{3,3}$ edge
blocks.  For every graph vertex, include its three incident edge variables as
a positive clause and include the complementary negative clause.  Each edge
variable belongs to its two endpoint neighborhoods, so it appears twice with
each sign.  The six unsigned neighborhoods are distinct, and the diagonal
perfect matching sets exactly one variable in every neighborhood to true.
Hence $P_k$ is proper, duplicate-free, exact-$(2,2)$ and satisfiable, with
\[
 (n,m,m_+,m_-)(P_k)=(9k,12k,6k,6k).
 \tag{1}
\]

Let $U$ be D\"ocker's fixed UNSAT member of $\Htwo$.  Proposition 1 gives
\[
 (n,m,m_+,m_-)(U)=(198,264,132,132)=22(9,12,6,6).
 \tag{2}
\]
The positive/negative counts in (2) also follow from exact signed degree:
$2n=3m_+=3m_-$.  For every $\ell\ge1$, define
\[
 Y_\ell=P_{\ell+22},\qquad Z_\ell=P_\ell\sqcup U.
 \tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{LAYER-BALANCED-H22-ISLAND1}]
For every $\ell\ge1$, $Y_\ell,Z_\ell\in\Htwo$; $Y_\ell$ is SAT and
$Z_\ell$ is UNSAT; and
\[
 (n,m,m_+,m_-,N)(Y_\ell)=(n,m,m_+,m_-,N)(Z_\ell).
 \tag{4}
\]
Moreover $S=\{Y_\ell,Z_\ell:\ell\ge1\}$ is uniformly recognizable and SAT
restricted to $S$ has a uniform polynomial-time classifier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The structural and SAT claims for $P_k$ follow from the construction above.
The claims for $U$ are \KNOWN{} from D\"ocker.  Disjoint union with fresh
variables preserves properness, clause uniqueness, exact signed occurrence
and UNSAT when one component is UNSAT.  Substituting (1)--(2) into (3) gives
equality of all four integer parameters.  The encoding rule then gives equal
$N$.  Canonical numbering lets one parser check either template and return 1
for $Y_\ell$, 0 for $Z_\ell$; it may reject every other string.
\end{proof}

Combining the theorem with RT-0017's recognize-first wrapper yields:

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{COFINAL-ANSWER-BALANCE-REPAIR1}]
Requiring infinitely many exact input layers containing both SAT and UNSAT
instances does not repair (PW).  This repair is \RETIRED{}.
\end{corollary}

The result is stronger than merely having both answers somewhere.  It survives
matching the standard size parameters exactly.  It does not say all formulas
on those layers are easy and therefore does not touch (WC).

\section{SAME\_REALIZATION and hostile controls}

On $S$, one concrete execution parses the source, recognizes the template and
returns its one-bit answer.  The fallback SAT solver is not reached.  No
compatibility matrix, branch vector, residual DAG, proof object, communication
transcript or assignment reconstruction is required.  All recognition work is
charged; at minimum, relevant source bits must be read.

Renaming, clause reordering, undeclared padding, redundant clauses and alternate
encodings may cause recognition to fail; the wrapper then invokes its exact
fallback.  That representation sensitivity does not save a universal
pointwise claim, because the canonical formulas are still quantified over.
Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin and bounded-treewidth families give independent
mixed-answer controls with different polynomial classifiers.  In products, a
recognized fixed UNSAT component decides the conjunction.  DELAY, COPY,
INTERP and RECOMP wrappers preserve the bit while changing trace resources.

The result relativizes and algebrizes: the recognizer/classifier ignores any
oracle on $S$.  It therefore supplies no escape from Baker--Gill--Solovay or
Aaronson--Wigderson \cite{bgs,aw}.  Natural Proofs is not directly triggered
by this wrapper theorem, but remains an audit for any future constructive large
circuit property \cite{rr}.  Nonuniform advice only makes easy-island bypasses
easier.  Arbitrary polynomial exponents remain unbounded.
