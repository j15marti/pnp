\paragraph{Executive status.}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  The newest peer results jointly remove
two tempting repairs of the universal-core route: raw-cell causality can be
destroyed by replication, and decoded causality can be inserted as optional
instrumentation.  This synthesis tests the next conjunction: perhaps decoded
causal use together with a strict ordinary-CNF successor is enough to remove
the unknown exponent of a hypothetical polynomial SAT decider.

It is not enough.  \textsf{DECIDE-GUARD-CONTRACT1} below gives, for every
total deterministic decider $A$ and every polynomial-time source functional
$q$, a same-realization wrapper in which $q$ is decoder-causally
indispensable and the emitted equivalent successor has constant encoded
length, hence exponent $\lambda=0$.  The wrapper first runs $A$.  Its fully
charged local-work exponent is therefore at least the unknown exponent $K$,
so the recurrence remains at least $K$.

The theorem is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in the declared checkpoint and encoding
model.  It \textbf{FALSIFIES/RETIRES}
\textsf{CAUSAL-STRICT-SUCCESSOR-IMPLIES-K-ELIMINATION1}.  It is not an
unrestricted SAT lower bound and not a P-versus-NP proof.  The surviving
target must force a strict successor \emph{before} paying current-scale
decision cost, or otherwise prove a constructor bound independent of $K$;
that arbitrary-algorithm transfer remains \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Clean core, encoding, and quantified target}
Retain the canonical clean core $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ from SYNTHESIS-0001:
monotone proper 3-CNFs in which each variable occurs exactly twice positively
and twice negatively, with canonical variable names and lexicographic clause
order.  The indexed self-delimiting encoding gamma-codes counts and widths and
writes each literal as one sign bit plus a fixed-width index.  Let
$N=|\operatorname{enc}(F)|$.

The desired contradiction schema remains
\[
\forall A\,\forall K\quad
 [\operatorname{CorrectSAT}(A)\wedge T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}]
 \Longrightarrow \exists B_A\quad
 [T_{B_A}(N)\le N^{c+o(1)},\ S_{B_A}(N)\le N^{\rho+o(1)}],       \tag{1}
\]
where $(c,\rho)$ lies in one cited unconditional SAT forbidden region in the
same random-access machine and encoding model, and $c,\rho$ do not retain the
arbitrary $K$.  SAME\_REALIZATION requires acquisition, failed routes, source
reads, decoding, child output, access, replay, recursion, final output, and
peak writable storage to be charged to this one $B_A$.

For a recursive service with local work $N^{r+o(1)}$, at most
$N^{\eta+o(1)}$ child calls, and child length $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, the exact
time ledger is
\[
C_{t+1}=\max\{r,\eta+\lambda C_t\},\qquad C_0=K.             \tag{2}
\]
Strict $\lambda<1$ controls only the child term.  It does not erase $K$ if
the constructor term $r$ already contains $K$.

\section{The tested candidate and exact countermodel}
Fix two constant proper 3-CNFs over $z_1,z_2,z_3$.  Let
\[
T=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{8}(z_1\vee z_2\vee z_3)
\]
and let $U$ contain all eight clauses obtained by independently choosing the
sign of each $z_i$.  Then $T$ is satisfiable, $U$ is unsatisfiable, both have
eight proper width-three clauses, and they have the same encoded length under
the declared format.

\begin{theorem}[DECIDE-GUARD-CONTRACT1]
Let $A$ be any total uniform deterministic decider for a language $L$.  Let
$q(x)$ be any total polynomial-time, polynomial-output source functional, and
let $e,d$ be polynomial-time maps with $d(e(y))=y$.  There is a total uniform
deterministic same-realization machine $W_{A,q,e}$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item on every legal execution it emits exactly one ordinary proper 3-CNF
$H_x\in\{T,U\}$ satisfying
$\operatorname{SAT}(H_x)=A(x)=\mathbf 1[x\in L]$;
\item $|\operatorname{enc}(H_x)|=O(1)$, so the successor exponent is
$\lambda=0$ and the successor family is trivially hereditary;
\item the checkpoint buffer $e(q(x))$ is decoder-relative causally
indispensable for the satisfiability of the emitted successor; but
\item
\[
T_W(N)=T_A(N)+2T_q(N)+T_e(N)+T_d(N)+O(|q(x)|),              \tag{3}
\]
with the corresponding charged peak-space bound.  If
$T_A(N)=N^{K+o(1)}$, equation (2) has $r\ge K$ and therefore every resulting
bound satisfies $C_{t+1}\ge K$ despite $\lambda=0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Compute $y=q(x)$ and store $c=e(y)$.  Run $A(x)$ and store its bit $b$.
At the declared checkpoint recompute $y'=q(x)$ and decode $z=d(c)$.  If
$z=y'$, emit $T$ when $b=1$ and $U$ when $b=0$.  Otherwise emit the opposite
constant formula.  On legal executions $z=y'=q(x)$, so the unique child has
the same decision bit as $x$.  Replacing $c$ by $e(y^*)$ for any
$y^*\ne q(x)$ flips the satisfiability of the emitted child, establishing
decoded causal indispensability.

Only one constant-size child is emitted and both possible children have the
same encoded length.  The explicit time and space accounting is (3).  With
$\eta=0$ and $\lambda=0$, (2) gives
$C_{t+1}=\max\{r,0\}\ge K$.  Recursing on the constant child does not refund
the work already spent deciding the parent.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Construction dominance]
For any exact successor compiler $C$ and child decider $D$, total time is
at least the charged time of $C$ and at most that time plus the work of $D$
on the emitted representation.  Hence a size exponent $\lambda<1$ can remove
$K$ only from the child term.  A $K$-independent endpoint requires a
$K$-independent constructor bound in the same realization.
\end{corollary}

This theorem is stronger than the output-bit objection but deliberately not a
hardness theorem: it simultaneously supplies a rich optional causal buffer,
the strongest possible size contraction, exact semantic preservation, and
heredity.  The implication still fails because the chronological and resource
location of the work matters.

\section{P=NP consequence without a hidden separation premise}
Assume $\Pclass=\NPclass$ and choose the hypothesized SAT decider $A$ with
some fixed but arbitrary exponent $K$.  The theorem then yields a polynomial
compiler from every formula, including every member of $\mathcal H_{2,2}$,
to one of two constant formulas.  This is perfectly consistent with
$\Pclass=\NPclass$: the compiler has already decided the source.  It gives no
unconditional contradiction, and its exponent remains $K$.

This also explains why generic semantic ``compression'' is too weak.  Under
$\Pclass=\NPclass$, decide-then-emit is a polynomial constant-size
compression for SAT.  Fortnow--Santhanam, Drucker, and Dell--van Melkebeek
prove strong instance-compression or sparsification consequences only under
explicit complexity-collapse conditions.  Those conditions cannot be used as
the endpoint here: $\Pclass=\NPclass$ itself implies
$\NPclass=\operatorname{coNP}\subseteq\Pclass/\mathrm{poly}$ and collapses
the polynomial hierarchy.  Invoking ``unless PH collapses'' while assuming
$\Pclass=\NPclass$ would smuggle the desired separation into a premise.

ledger, not an asymptotic SAT algorithm or lower bound.

\section{Literature and barrier audit}
Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas prove unconditional SAT time--space
lower bounds in a declared random-access model.  DECIDE-GUARD-CONTRACT1 does
not enter their forbidden region because its constructor inherits $A$'s
arbitrary time and space.  Fixed-DAG pebbling, branching-program, circuit,
VLSI, communication, and memory-hierarchy lower bounds remain restricted
structural results until a cost-preserving compiler puts every SAT decider in
their model.

Dell--van Melkebeek show that an $O(n^{d-\epsilon})$ communication protocol
for $n$-variable $d$-CNF SAT implies
$\operatorname{coNP}\subseteq\NPclass/\mathrm{poly}$ and hence a PH collapse.
Fortnow--Santhanam and Drucker obtain related OR/AND-compression barriers.
These are directly relevant warnings about output size, but not unconditional
lower bounds against the $P=NP$ counterfactual.  Their conclusions are already
compatible with that counterfactual.

The countermodel relativizes: with a common oracle $O$, run $A^O$ and $q^O$
before emitting $T$ or $U$.  Baker--Gill--Solovay therefore classifies it as
a route obstruction, not a separating hinge.  It also algebrizes in the only
relevant sense: algebraic or semantic oracle access can be forwarded through
the wrapper, and affine/Tseitin controls remain easy by Gaussian elimination.
Aaronson--Wigderson thus supplies no promotion.  No constructive large useful
circuit property is asserted, so Natural Proofs is only a future audit trigger,
not a theorem used here.

\section{Duplication, contradiction, and smallest repair}
RT-0005 already proves optional causal instrumentation.  Main4/main5 already
warn against zero-child endpoint restatements and scale laundering.
SYNTHESIS-0002 does not rename those facts.  Its new exact combination is a
single quantified countermodel showing that optional decoded causality,
ordinary-CNF output, exact equivalence, heredity, and even $\lambda=0$ can all
hold simultaneously while the charged recurrence retains $K$.

The smallest surviving repair is:
\begin{definition}[PREDECISION-CONTRACT1, OPEN]
For every clocked total uniform SAT decider $A$ with arbitrary polynomial
exponent $K$, construct a same-realization uniform machine $B_A$ that, on
every canonical input, either decides locally inside a fixed
$N^{r+o(1)}$ time and $N^{\sigma+o(1)}$ space bound independent of $K$, or
produces locally addressable ordinary-CNF successors before any current-scale
use of $A$, with one fixed $\lambda<1$, charged aggregate exponent $\eta$,
hereditary closure, and a closed $(c,\rho)$ entering a model-matched
unconditional SAT forbidden region.
\end{definition}

The phrase ``before use of $A$'' is only a syntactic guard and is not invariant
under inlining, DIRECT/RECOMP, or semantic-oracle replacement.  The robust
content is the proved $K$-independent same-realization resource bound.  Obtaining
that bound from an arbitrary one-bit decider is precisely the missing transfer;
assuming it is cheap because the child is small is circular.

\begin{description}
\item[ADOPTED.] UCORE-CONTRACT1's exact recurrence; SAME\_REALIZATION;
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$; RT-0005 guard insertion; LB-0006 representation guard;
MODELBRIDGE-0004's linear restriction floor; TESTS-0001's no-sharing control.
\item[REJECTED.] Output size, decoded causal use, heredity, or exact semantic
equivalence as a substitute for charged construction cost.
\item[FALSIFIED/RETIRED.]
\textsf{CAUSAL-STRICT-SUCCESSOR-IMPLIES-K-ELIMINATION1}, including every
decide-then-emit realization of a strict successor.
\item[OPEN.] PREDECISION-CONTRACT1; an implementation-covariant service for
DIRECT/RECOMP solvers; a $K$-independent all-input constructor; simultaneous
space; physical child access; and the exact FLMV endpoint compiler.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Peer handoffs.}
MODEL\_BRIDGE should require the acquisition clock to end before any charged
current-scale decision work and should report whether that condition survives
inlining.  TESTS should wrap proposed services in decide-then-emit and
recompute variants and measure constructor versus child cost separately.
REDTEAM should reject any $\lambda<1$ claim whose local exponent $r$ inherits
$K$ or whose ``predecision'' condition is merely source-code syntax.
PROOF\_MAP should require the forced representation to exist before the answer
is known and to preserve FLMV machine access.  COST\_CERTIFICATES should put
constructor, reset, child serialization, accessor, recursion, and final output
in one peak-time/space ledger.

\paragraph{Single highest-value target.}
Prove or falsify PREDECISION-CONTRACT1 on the canonical
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ encoding.  The exact missing arrow is not ``small child''
but
\[
\text{arbitrary one-bit SAT execution}
\Longrightarrow
\text{$K$-independent, fully charged strict-successor construction}.       \tag{4}
\]
No current report proves (4).  Until it and the model-matched endpoint are
established, P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.
