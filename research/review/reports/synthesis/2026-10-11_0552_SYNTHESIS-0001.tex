\paragraph{Executive status.}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  This synthesis reconciles the newest
universal-core tests, red-team audit, model bridge, proof-map audit, and
cost-certificate controls with the actual current \texttt{main4.tex} and
\texttt{main5.tex}.  The positive universal fact is external witness
extraction by pinning.  The negative facts are now cumulative: membership
answers do not bind an implementation; compatibility rank does not imply
computation cost; minimal DAG size is representation-specific; raw or decoded
state exposure is not universal; and separately written nested pinning volume
can collapse under batching.

This run tests the remaining candidate that pinning itself supplies the
strict recursive contraction needed to eliminate an arbitrary polynomial SAT
exponent.  It does not.  Every explicit pinned query retains the source, so
its query-scale exponent is $\lambda=1$.  The induced recurrence never removes
the unknown exponent $K$.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} accounting and
\textbf{FALSIFIES/RETIRES} \textsf{PINNING-CONTRACTION1}.  It is not an
unrestricted SAT lower bound.

\section{Explicit clean core and encoded length}
\begin{definition}[Canonical clean core $\mathcal H_{2,2}$]
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ is the family of canonical monotone 3-CNFs in which every
clause has three distinct literals and is entirely positive or entirely
negative, every variable occurs exactly twice positively and exactly twice
negatively, variables are named $1,\ldots,n$, and clauses and literals use a
fixed lexicographic order.  The encoding gamma-codes the variable and clause
counts and clause widths, and writes every literal as one sign bit plus a
fixed-width variable index.  Its encoded bit length is denoted $N$.
\end{definition}

The unconstrained ordering of Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) is NP-complete
\cite{doecker}; canonical sorting is a linearithmic representation
normalization and does not change satisfiability.  This makes
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ a legitimate hard hostile family.  It does not itself give
a fine-grained reduction into the precise random-access endpoint model; that
encoding/compiler transfer remains an explicit open arrow.

\section{The candidate and its exact failure}
Assume $\Pclass=\NPclass$.  Fix one clocked uniform deterministic SAT decider
$A$ and a constant $K$ such that for every explicit $M$-bit CNF,
\[
A(F)=\operatorname{SAT}(F),\qquad T_A(M)\le M^{K+o(1)}.
\tag{1}
\]
A source-first recursive service makes at most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ child calls of
length $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ after local work $N^{r+o(1)}$.  Its exponent
accounting is
\[
C_0=K,\qquad C_{t+1}=\max\{r,\eta+\lambda C_t\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The proposed \textsf{PINNING-CONTRACTION1} identifies the children with the
nested pinning instances of EDGE-EXTRACT1 and hopes that recursion removes
$K$.

\begin{theorem}[PINNING-NOCONTRACT1]
For standard explicit pinning, $\lambda=1$.  Consequently every finite-depth
recurrence (2) satisfies $C_t\ge K$.  If $\eta>0$ and the local term does not
dominate, then $C_t=K+t\eta$.  Thus pinning cannot eliminate the unknown
polynomial exponent, regardless of its query count or separately
materialized transcript volume.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every pinned query contains the entire source formula plus prefix clauses.
Hence its length $L(N)$ obeys $L(N)\ge N$ and
$L(N)=N^{1+o(1)}$ for the canonical encodings used by EDGE-EXTRACT1, so
$\lambda=1$.  Substituting in (2) gives
$C_{t+1}=\max\{r,\eta+C_t\}\ge C_t\ge K$.  Induction proves the first
claim; equality in the stated regime proves the second.
\end{proof}

For comparison, $\lambda<1$ yields the fixed-point accounting
\[
C_*=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-\lambda}\right\},
\tag{3}
\]
after a fixed depth chosen from the fixed constant $K$.  Equation (3) is the
conditional contraction already preserved in main4/main5.  It is useful only
after constructing a genuine strict-shrink child service with hereditary
closure, access, replay, output, and simultaneous space fully charged.
Pinning supplies none of that shrinkage.

\section{Reproducible accounting test}
The exact-rational audit evaluated (2) for
$K\in\{1,\ldots,12\}$, four values of $\eta$, three local-work exponents, and
depth eight.  It checked 1,296 $\lambda=1$ recurrence values and 144
$\lambda=1/2$ hostile controls.  Every $\lambda=1$ value retained at least
$K$; the $\lambda=1/2$ controls converged toward (3).

The same program constructed the exact indexed lengths of the canonical
proper-three-CNF pin family for $n=1,2,\ldots,256$, with both unit and
proper-three-CNF prefix encodings.  It passed 3,156 length and volume checks.
At $n=256$, the source used 38,952 bits; the 769 proper-three-CNF pinning
queries used 81,742,896 bits in total, while the declared batch source-touch
plus transcript/witness output ledger used 4,608 units.  These finite values
verify the implementation and formulas, not an asymptotic SAT claim.

An initial unchunked request through $n=2048$ hit the 30-second execution
limit before producing output.  The verified run used the independent
$n\le256$ chunk and completed in 0.45 seconds.  The symbolic theorem does not
depend on either limit.

\section{Exact universal target after synthesis}
The surviving target must produce a contraction, not merely an externally
extractable object.

\begin{definition}[UCORE-CONTRACT1, OPEN]
For every clocked total uniform SAT decider $A$ satisfying (1), construct one
uniform source-first machine $B_A$ and constants
$r,\eta,\lambda,\sigma$ with $\lambda<1$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item for every canonical input, including every
$F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$, the construction either decides $F$ locally or emits
a self-contained finite family of ordinary CNF children whose disjunction is
exactly $\operatorname{SAT}(F)$;
\item acquisition, failed routes, source scans, child construction and
writing, indexing, local access, decoding, replay, witness lifting, output,
recursion stack, and peak writable storage are included in the same
realization;
\item every unresolved child is in the hereditary domain of the same
construction, with total call exponent $\eta$, child-length exponent
$\lambda$, local time exponent $r$, and simultaneous space exponent
$\sigma$;
\item the closed recurrence (3) and its space ledger enter a cited
unconditional SAT time--space forbidden region in the same machine and
encoding model; and
\item the theorem survives DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, direct decision,
affine elimination, Horn/2-SAT, bounded treewidth, compact circuits and
branching programs, source rereading, memoization, vectorization, semantic
oracles, and algebraic oracles.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The exact contradiction schema is
\[
\begin{split}
\forall A\,\forall K\quad
&[\operatorname{CorrectSAT}(A)\wedge T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}]\\
&\Longrightarrow \exists B_A\quad
[\operatorname{CorrectSAT}(B_A)\wedge
T_{B_A}(N)\le N^{C_*+o(1)}\wedge
S_{B_A}(N)\le N^{\rho+o(1)}],
\end{split}
\tag{4}
\]
with $(C_*,\rho)$ inside the same declared unconditional forbidden region.
Because $K$ is a fixed constant for fixed $A$, a recursion depth depending on
$K$ is still uniform.  The load-bearing missing implication is the
source-visible, all-input, strict-shrink construction in items 1--3, plus its
endpoint compiler.  \textsf{CORE-ACQUIRE1}, \textsf{DAG-ACQUIRE1}, and
\textsf{QUERY-NOSHARE1} are useful only if they discharge these obligations;
acquisition without $\lambda<1$ is not enough.

\section{Literature and barrier audit}
Kawachi--Rossman--Watanabe prove witness-finding query lower bounds in a
hidden-set oracle model \cite{krw}; they do not prove additive time for
source-sharing SAT computations.  Besselman--G\"o\"os--Guo--Maystre--Yuan
prove direct sums for randomized parity decision trees under discrepancy or
product-distribution hypotheses \cite{bggmy}; this is not an unrestricted SAT
direct-sum theorem.  Ren--Williams analyze bounded-round adaptive NP queries
inside a specific circuit-lower-bound construction \cite{rw}; the result does
not supply UCORE-CONTRACT1.  Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas provide a
genuine SAT time--space endpoint for their random-access model \cite{flmv},
but do not force a compatibility object, DAG, or pinning transcript inside an
arbitrary decider.

PINNING-NOCONTRACT1 and the black-box extraction/separation arguments
relativize.  They identify a dead route and cannot be the nonrelativizing
hinge required after Baker--Gill--Solovay.  Affine/Tseitin controls remain a
mandatory algebrization test because Gaussian elimination can collapse the
putative state.  Natural Proofs becomes relevant only if UCORE-CONTRACT1 is
recast as a large constructive useful circuit property; no such property is
claimed here.

The repository's post-quantum prompts do not repair the transfer.  Schur
squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, and syzygies are
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} without an exact object map.  Collision entropy is
exact for a fixed proof-to-node map but does not establish strict child
shrinkage or no-sharing.  LWE-style external extraction and special soundness
remain \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; cryptographic assumptions cannot support an
unconditional P-versus-NP conclusion.

\section{Duplication, contradiction, and adoption audit}
PINNING-NOCONTRACT1 is an explicit application of the earlier KFIX/recursive
closure accounting, not a new general recurrence theorem.  Its new role is
to close the apparent gap left by EDGE-EXTRACT1: the only universal positive
bridge presently available has exactly the noncontracting scale
$\lambda=1$.  This is consistent with main4/main5, which already require
strict shrinkage, and with TESTS-0001, which separately invalidates additive
query-volume charging.

\begin{description}
\item[ADOPTED.] EDGE-EXTRACT1 as external witness extraction;
PINNING-BATCH-SEPARATION1; RT-0004 exposure separation; the OBDD-order and
constant-decision-DAG controls; CC-0005 rank/easy-family controls; conditional
strict-shrink recurrence (3); SAME\allowbreak\_REALIZATION.
\item[REJECTED.] Query count, summed pinning volume, compatibility rank,
minimal DAG size, raw state exposure, or decoded state exposure as stand-alone
universal SAT lower bounds.
\item[FALSIFIED/RETIRED.] PINNING-CONTRACTION1 and every promotion that treats
source-retaining pinning calls as $\lambda<1$ descendants or charges their
separate work to the original one-bit execution without a proved service.
\item[OPEN.] UCORE-CONTRACT1; strict source-visible shrinkage on the hard
core; hereditary closure; model-matched endpoint transfer; arbitrary-decider
acquisition when needed; nonrelativization and nonalgebrization.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Peer handoffs.}
MODEL\_BRIDGE should stop at EDGE-EXTRACT1 unless a richer service produces
$\lambda<1$.  TESTS should attack proposed strict-shrink services with batch,
reread, affine, and compact-representation controls.  REDTEAM should reject
any recurrence whose child retains the whole source or whose direct-sum
premise is imported across models.  PROOF\_MAP should use DAG lower bounds
only after the same-execution compiler also proves strict source-scale
contraction.  COST\_CERTIFICATES should treat witness acquisition as useful
only when its verifier/child representation closes (4) with all reset and
access costs.

\paragraph{Single highest-value target.}
Construct or falsify one explicit $\lambda<1$ ordinary-CNF successor on
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ that is source-first, locally addressable, hereditary, and
fully charged.  Pinning, compatibility extraction, and minimal DAGs do not
supply it.  Until that construction and its model-matched endpoint transfer
are proved, P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.
