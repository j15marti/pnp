\begin{abstract}
The latest four specialist reports appeared to disagree about whether a
local clean-core reduction needs inverse incidence.  They do not.  This
report proves the ownership-sensitive synthesis.  A parent-owned compiler
for a bounded-fanout circuit can stream each gate's constant clause template
using only gate type and child labels; no parent port is needed.  By contrast,
if a target-owned normalizer must certify a target's complete parent set or
bounded fanout from a child-only oracle, it needs one query for every
potential parent in the worst case.  Thus reverse incidence is neither
generically available nor generically necessary.  The unqualified claim
that a parent port is the missing Local-Cook ingredient is retired.

The correct concrete target is now a direct on-demand encoder from the
Jahanjou--Miles--Viola source into the exact monotone $(2,2)$ clean core, or a
composition of forward-only stages with the same bit-local resource bound.
Even proving that target would only close a model-matched reduction leg; it
would not force an arbitrary SAT decider to expose a compatibility object or
remove its unknown polynomial exponent.  The arbitrary-decider bootstrap,
nonrelativizing hinge, and P versus NP remain open.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Clean core and input length.}
Let $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ be the duplicate-free proper Monotone
3-SAT-$(2,2)$ family: every clause has three distinct literals of one sign,
and every variable appears exactly twice with each sign.  This language is
NP-complete \cite{doecker}.  With $n$ variables and $m$ clauses, $3m=4n$;
under canonical indexed binary encoding,
\[
 N_F=|\operatorname{enc}(F)|=\Theta(n\log n).
\]
The separation-strength target remains
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm unif}(3\mathrm{SAT})\ 
 \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:
 N_F\ge N_0\ \land\ T_A(\operatorname{enc}F)>N_F^d.
\tag{UCORE-TIME1}
\]
It is \OPEN, and none of the interface theorems below implies it.

\section{Exact port dichotomy}

Consider a circuit with $s$ gates from a fixed finite gate basis.  Each gate
has constant fanin, and a forward accessor returns its type and child labels.
A fixed Tseitin-style template emits $O(1)$ variables and clauses per gate.
Let $\beta$ bound the template's internal occurrences of a gate variable and
$\alpha$ its occurrences per incident outgoing wire.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{LOCAL-REDUCTION-PORT-DICHOTOMY1}]
The following statements hold in the same explicit indexed-label model.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If source fanout is at most $\Delta=O(1)$, a parent-owned emitter
streams a proper 3-CNF of size $O(s)$ using $O(1)$ forward child queries per
gate and zero parent queries.  Every gate variable has occurrence at most
$\beta+\alpha\Delta=O(1)$.  Exact witness extension and projection hold.
\item In a child-only two-layer acyclic oracle with one target $u$ and $s$
possible later parents, every deterministic exact procedure that decides
fanout$(u)=0$, outputs the complete parent set, or certifies
fanout$(u)\le\Delta<s$ has worst-case query complexity exactly $s$.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently a reverse port is unnecessary for item 1 but cannot be inferred
from forward locality for item 2.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For item 1, enumerate parents, not targets.  At a gate $p$, query its constant
list of children and emit the fixed local gate and wire clauses owned by
$p$.  Every edge is emitted once.  Gate-local clauses contribute at most
$\beta$ occurrences to a gate variable, and at most $\alpha$ occurrences
arrive from each of its at most $\Delta$ parents.  Fresh Tseitin auxiliaries
extend any circuit evaluation to a satisfying assignment, while restriction
to the original gate variables projects every satisfying encoding to a
consistent evaluation.  This is the verified TESTS-0009 construction.

For item 2, answer every queried potential parent with a private dummy child.
If the procedure stops before querying all positions, change one unqueried
child to $u$.  The transcript is unchanged but both zero-fanout and complete
enumeration change.  For the $\Delta$ predicate, answer $u$ on the first
$\Delta$ queried positions and dummy thereafter; an unqueried position has
completions of weights $\Delta$ and $\Delta+1$.  Scanning all positions is a
matching upper bound.  This is RT-0014's exact adversary.
\end{proof}

The theorem resolves the apparent conflict among SYNTHESIS-0009,
MODELBRIDGE-0014, LB-0014, TESTS-0009 and RT-0014.  A supplied parent port is
a sufficient interface.  It is necessary only for constructions whose local
output contract is target-owned and requires complete reverse incidence.
Parent-owned emission bypasses that contract.  Optional annotations or
materialized inverse indices cannot be silently charged to arbitrary
algorithms.

\section{The corrected Local-Cook target}

Jahanjou--Miles--Viola prove a quasilinear-size 3-SAT reduction for which an
explicit circuit maps a clause index to the indexed clause and, in the
strongest regime, every output bit depends on only constantly many index
bits \cite{jmv}.  That is a powerful forward output interface.  Its theorem
statement does not supply target-owned reverse incidence, but the dichotomy
shows that such incidence should not be demanded unless the next normalizer
actually needs it.

The smallest useful target is therefore direct:
\begin{quote}
\texttt{JMV-TO-H22-STREAM1}.  There is one uniform transducer which, given a
source $x$, an output-bit address $(j,b)$, and the canonical output length,
returns bit $b$ of clause $j$ of a satisfiability-equivalent formula
$F_x\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$ in $\log^{O(1)}T$ time and $O(\log T)$ writable
space; $|F_x|=T\log^{O(1)}T$, and all preprocessing, address translation,
padding, uniqueness and witness maps are included.
\end{quote}

This target may be proved by composing JMV's clause generator with a
forward-only proper/bounded-occurrence stage and a new exact Monotone
$(2,2)$ normalizer.  Alternatively it may be proved by a direct output-bit
construction.  Döcker's NP-completeness reductions establish polynomial
many-one hardness of the exact clean core, but their published statements do
not establish this bit-local, quasilinear, simultaneous-space contract
\cite{doecker}.  Ordinary NP-completeness is insufficient: SYNTHESIS-0007's
exact FLMV budget permits only essentially linear overhead.

The concrete missing subclaims are:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \texttt{JMV-FORWARD-LABEL1}: unfold the actual JMV output labels at the
granularity consumed by parent-owned emission;
\item \texttt{MONOTONE22-LOCAL-NORMALIZER1}: exact proper bounded-occurrence
3-CNF to duplicate-free Monotone 3-SAT-$(2,2)$ with bit-local
quasilinear/log-space access;
\item \texttt{COMPOSITION-ADDRESS1}: prove that address translation and
witness extension/projection preserve those bounds without an offline index.
\end{enumerate}
All three are \OPEN.  Reverse fanout is not promoted to a fourth obligation.

\section{SAME\_REALIZATION and P-versus-NP relevance}

Suppose \texttt{JMV-TO-H22-STREAM1} is proved.  It would allow one machine to
answer arbitrary target-input bit requests on demand while charging source
reads, address translation, workspace and output length.  This closes a
model-matched reduction leg.  It still does not perform either of the two
separation-bearing tasks:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item force every uniform polynomial-time SAT decider to expose or compute a
particular compatibility, proof, memory or communication object; or
\item transform an arbitrary $N^{K+o(1)}$ SAT decider into one realization
with the FLMV-forbidden $N^{6/5+o(1)}$ time and $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ space bounds,
independently of $K$.
\end{enumerate}
The first requires an external solver-to-resource compiler with a lower
bound in the same model.  The second remains
\texttt{A-RELATIVE-FRACTIONAL-BOOTSTRAP1}.  A local reduction cannot erase
an arbitrary oracle solver exponent: composing with the solver inherits its
cost.  Under $P=NP$ one obtains some fixed polynomial decider, but no stated
premise bounds its exponent or space, so no unconditional contradiction
follows.

\section{Barrier-transfer and post-quantum audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{DIRECT THEOREM TRANSFER.}
JMV clause-bit locality transfers to exactly its declared indexed-output
function.  TESTS-0009 parent-owned emission and RT-0014's OR adversary
transfer to their declared forward-child interfaces.  No stronger interface
is imported.
\item[] \textbf{USEFUL ANALOGY REQUIRING A NEW TRANSFER THEOREM.}
VLSI fanout/routing, reverse-adjacency data structures, distributed
in-neighbor discovery, control observability, and LWE-style external
extraction motivate interface accounting.  They do not compile an arbitrary
SAT computation to that interface.
\item[] \textbf{MISLEADING / FAILED TRANSFER.}
Proof-system size, branching-program width, pebbling, semantic compatibility
rank, Schur squares, quadratic-relation rank, Hilbert functions, syzygies,
collision entropy, special soundness, and cryptographic binding do not
lower-bound unrestricted one-bit SAT computation without an exact
same-realization map.
\end{description}

The port dichotomy relativizes and algebrizes.  It therefore cannot be the
decisive hinge in view of relativization and algebrization barriers
\cite{bgs,aw}.  Natural Proofs is not directly triggered because no large
constructive property against general circuits is established.  PQ-derived
ideas are classified as ANALOGY/CANDIDATE for external extraction and FAILED
TRANSFER for the port theorem itself; no hardness or random-oracle assumption
is used.
