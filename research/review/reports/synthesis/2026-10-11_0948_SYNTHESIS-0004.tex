\begin{abstract}
TESTS-0003 proved that exact Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ balance does not block
a nonempty decision pin, but its lift is a disjoint union.  This synthesis
attacks the smallest natural repair: replace one positive clause in each of
two incidence components by one connected, occurrence-balanced monotone
gadget whose existential projection is exactly the conjunction of the two
removed clauses.  Degree arithmetic forces the number $k$ of internal
variables to satisfy $k\equiv0\pmod3$.  The cases $k=0$ and $k=3$ are
excluded exactly.  An exhaustive, reproducible $k=6$ audit enumerated
$1{,}417{,}800$ positive hypergraphs and the two isomorphism types of
negative hypergraphs.  It found 150 semantic simulators, but every one
contains the two original boundary clauses and is disconnected.  This is a
finite \COMP obstruction, not an asymptotic theorem; $k\ge9$ and the global
connected lift remain \OPEN.  D\"ocker's primary construction independently
shows that balance does not obstruct single-clause simulation.  No
arbitrary-algorithm lower bound follows.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Clean core and separation-strength target}

Let $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ be the canonically encoded class of duplicate-free
monotone CNFs with three distinct variables per clause and exactly two
positive and two negative occurrences of each variable.  If $F$ has $n$
variables and $m$ clauses, then $3m=4n$ and its full identifier encoding has
length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  D\"ocker proves that satisfiability on this
class is NP-complete.

The project endpoint remains
\[
\tag{UCORE-TIME1}
\forall A\ \forall d\in\mathbb N\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:
\quad |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0\quad\wedge\quad
T_A(\operatorname{enc}(F))>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,
\]
where $A$ ranges over total uniform deterministic exact deciders.  This is
separation-strength and remains \OPEN; it is never used as a premise below.

\section{Exact connector model}

\begin{definition}[Two-clause balanced connector]
Let $B_1=\{b_0,b_1,b_2\}$ and $B_2=\{b_3,b_4,b_5\}$ be boundary variables,
and let $I=\{u_1,\ldots,u_k\}$ be fresh internal variables.  A connector
$J(B_1,B_2;I)$ is a proper, duplicate-free monotone $3$-CNF satisfying:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item each boundary variable occurs once positively and never negatively;
\item each internal variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice
negatively;
\item for every boundary assignment $\beta$,
\[
 \exists\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{I}\ J(\beta,\alpha)=1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Bigl(\bigvee_{i=0}^{2}b_i\Bigr)\wedge
 \Bigl(\bigvee_{i=3}^{5}b_i\Bigr)=1;
\tag{1}
\]
\item the variable--clause incidence graph of $J$ is connected.
\end{enumerate}
Replacing two positive source clauses by such a $J$ preserves every source
variable's $(2,2)$ degree, preserves satisfiability under projection, and
joins the two incidence components.
\end{definition}

This is a deliberately local model.  It does not quantify over all possible
connected lifts, preprocessing algorithms, encodings, or SAT deciders.

\begin{proposition}[Exact arithmetic floor]
Any two-clause balanced connector has $k\equiv0\pmod3$.  No such connected
connector exists for $k=0$ or $k=3$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The positive occurrence total is $6+2k$ and the negative total is $2k$;
both must be divisible by three, so $k\equiv0\pmod3$.  At $k=0$, (1) is a
positive $3$-CNF in which every boundary variable occurs once.  Its two
prime clauses must be the two displayed boundary triples, up to order, so
the incidence graph is disconnected.  At $k=3$, the six negative internal
occurrences require two distinct proper negative triples on three variables,
but only one such triple exists.  Clause uniqueness makes the model
impossible.
\end{proof}

\section{Primary literature and transfer audit}

D\"ocker's primary paper does more than establish NP-completeness.  It
constructs a monotone enforcer $S(v_1,v_2,v_3)$ with 99 fresh variables and
133 clauses.  Every internal variable occurs twice in each sign, and the
existential projection on the three boundary variables is exactly
$v_1\vee v_2\vee v_3$.  Therefore the broad claim that exact $(2,2)$ balance
prevents clause simulation is \RETIRED.  The paper does not state the
two-clause connected bridge (1), does not provide an all-decider compiler,
and does not prove a SAT time lower bound.

Paul--Tarjan pebbling and later branching-program results give genuine
time--space lower bounds for computations that evaluate a declared DAG or
belong to a declared branching-program model.  FLMV gives genuine
simultaneous time--space exclusions for SAT in its random-access model.
None says that an arbitrary SAT decider must first expose this connector, a
minimal residual DAG, a separator table, or a chosen pebbling instance.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.5cm,style=nextline]
\item[THEOREM TRANSFER.]
Occurrence counting transfers exactly to the clause hypergraph.  If a gadget
satisfies Definition 1, clause replacement preserves degree, satisfiability,
and connects the two incident components.
\item[ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.]
Pebbling, VLSI routing, communication cuts, and memory hierarchy motivate a
joint acquisition--storage--recomputation measure.  A new compiler must map
every static SAT implementation into that interface with all costs charged.
\item[FAILED TRANSFER.]
The absence of a small connector, or a lower bound for evaluating one fixed
DAG, is not a lower bound for the original one-bit SAT execution.
\end{description}

\section{Reduction-versus-solving and minimal-DAG audit}

For a declared representation class $\mathcal R$, the frontier
\[
 \Phi_{F,\mathcal R}(S)=
 \min_{R\in\mathcal R}
 \left[A(F,R)+P_{G_R}(S-S_R)\right]                         \tag{2}
\]
is a legitimate model-specific quantity: $A$ charges construction, $S_R$
retained representation, and $P_{G_R}$ evaluation or pebbling under the
remaining space.  Equation (2) can prove a reduction-versus-solve tradeoff
inside $\mathcal R$.

It does not itself imply UCORE-TIME1.  For one fixed formula and one decision
bit, an answer-dependent minimal DAG is a constant.  If source-plus-program
representations are admitted, the source is a linear-size compact DAG/service
description but query time retains the unknown SAT exponent $K$.  If they are
excluded, the missing implication is
\[
\tag{DAG-COMPILER1}
\forall A\ \exists C_A\ \forall F:\quad
C_A(F)\in\mathcal R
\quad\text{before current-scale decision work, with a $K$-independent
same-realization cost.}
\]
This is \OPEN.  It is the exact transfer gate already exposed by
MODELBRIDGE-0007 and RT-0006, not a consequence of graph minimality.

\section{Hostile controls and barrier audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.3cm,style=nextline]
\item[Horn, 2-SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded treewidth.]
Their semantic algorithms bypass a generic connector or DAG representation.
Occurrence balance and connectivity can coexist with propagation, SCC,
Gaussian elimination, or dynamic programming and therefore are not runtime
resources by themselves.
\item[Products, padding, and redundant clauses.]
Disjoint products directly falsified the previous balance repair.  Padding
and duplicate clauses may preserve semantics while changing connectivity,
degrees, or the uniqueness promise.  All must be charged as representation
changes, not computational progress.
\item[Wrappers and one-bit output.]
DIRECT may return only $A(F)$; RECOMP retains the source and runs an evaluator;
COPY, DEAD, DELAY, and INTERP change the ledger but cannot force a connector
or all service coordinates.  The requested output remains one bit.
\item[Compact representations and nonuniformity.]
Circuits, branching programs, source programs, advice, or semantic oracles
can encode behavior without exposing the searched hypergraph.  Excluding
them requires an explicit uniform representation theorem.
\end{description}

The degree argument, D\"ocker clause simulation, and finite search all
relativize.  Algebraic-oracle access only expands possible bypasses; no
nonalgebrizing hinge is present.  Baker--Gill--Solovay and
Aaronson--Wigderson therefore remain active barriers.  No large constructive
circuit property is used, so Natural Proofs is not directly invoked; any
future promotion through a circuit property must audit it.

Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies,
graded Betti numbers, and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for
the present local connector question: the one-bit execution need not expose
those objects.  LWE-style external black-box extraction and
special-soundness-style collision extraction remain
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE} pending DAG-COMPILER1.  Hash or random-oracle
assumptions are inadmissible in an unconditional separation.

\section{$\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ audit}

Assume only in this section that $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$, and let a uniform
SAT decider run in $N^{K+o(1)}$ time for an unknown constant $K$.  It can
decide the clean core and every explicitly constructed connector instance in
polynomial time.  A source-first gadget transducer adds only polynomial
overhead, while a source-backed DAG evaluator retains exponent $K$.  The
finite $k\le6$ obstruction gives neither a superpolynomial acquisition cost
nor a model-matched simultaneous time--space bound.  Hence no unconditional
contradiction follows, and no premise has smuggled in $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$.
