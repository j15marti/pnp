\section{Primary literature and obstacle preflight}

The two obstacles are presentation invariance and model-matched landing.
Hartmanis--Stearns and Blum formalize complexity as a measure of computations
or indexed programs, not as a source-semantics object~\cite{HS65,Blum67}.
Blum's speed-up phenomenon further warns that arbitrary computable functions
need not possess a canonical optimal program; it does not by itself decide
SAT.  FLMV proves simultaneous SAT time--space exclusions only for the stated
ordinary random-access realization~\cite{FLMV05}.  Baker--Gill--Solovay and
Aaronson--Wigderson remain barrier checks because all three guards below
relativize and algebrize~\cite{BGS75,AW09}.

No primary theorem supplies the missing all-decider wrapper quotient or turns
a clock inequality into a compatibility-exposure obligation.

\section{Exact synthesis theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{CLOCK-PRESENTATION-LANDING-TRILEMMA1}]
Fix rational $0<\alpha<1$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=1.7em]
\item For every $(A,t,s)\in\ExactSAT$ and integer
$q\ge\lceil1/\alpha\rceil$, the looser clock
$t_q(n)=\max\{2,t(n)\}^q$ is valid for the identical machine and
\[
                    T_A(x)\le t_q(|x|)^\alpha.                \tag{2}
\]
Thus an existential favorable supplied-clock law has no execution content.
\item For every total $A$ and fixed $q\ge2$, a uniform wrapper $D_q(A)$
returns $A(x)$ after at least $T_A(x)^q$ real dummy transitions.  Hence
\[
 \tau_{D_q(A)}(n)\ge\tau_A(n)^q,
 \qquad \tau_A(n)\le\tau_{D_q(A)}(n)^\alpha
 \quad(q\alpha\ge1),                                       \tag{3}
\]
while a fixed parser of the declared wrapper syntax returns the embedded
$A$.  Exact clocks therefore do not make strict power comparison invariant
under equivalent implementations.
\item Suppose $L(z,y)$ is false for every typed $z,y$.  For every total
transformation $\mathcal T$,
\[
 \forall z\in\PolySAT\ L(z,\mathcal T(z))
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \PolySAT=\varnothing.         \tag{4}
\]
For SAT, the right side is equivalent to $P\ne NP$.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently \texttt{CLOCK-POWER-NORMALIZATION-AS-INTERMEDIATE1} is
\RETIRED.  A viable claim must avoid (2), survive (3), and prove the
specialization in (4) as its explicit separation-bearing arrow.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Part (i) is CLKSLACK1: validity gives $T_A(x)\le t(n)$ and
$q\alpha\ge1$ gives the displayed power inequality.  Part (ii) is
TIGHTDELAY1: run $A$, count its $L$ transitions, execute an $L^q$ dummy loop,
and return the stored bit.  Taking the maximizing input at length $n$ proves
(3); the wrapper tag exposes the original code.  For (iii), if $\PolySAT$ is
nonempty, any element would contradict the unconditional falsity of $L$; if
it is empty, the universal statement is vacuous.  Cook's theorem supplies
$\PolySAT\ne\varnothing\Leftrightarrow\SAT\in P$.  The conclusion merely
combines these exhaustive guards and makes no claim about all possible
resource relations.
\end{proof}

\section{What the theorem does and does not close}

The theorem closes a specific candidate family, not universal lower-bound
research.  Fixed-program accounting remains meaningful: the delayed program
really pays its delay, and FLMV still applies to any actual machine satisfying
its hypotheses.  What fails is promotion from a presentation-relative power
ratio to solver-independent progress.

The surviving schema is
\begin{align*}
\textsf{CrossRegime}:\quad&
 \exists\mathcal T\ \forall z\in\ExactSAT\ R(z,\mathcal T(z)),\tag{5}\\
\textsf{Specialize}:\quad&
 \forall z\in\PolySAT\,[R(z,\mathcal T(z))Rightarrow
 L(z,\mathcal T(z))],                                      \tag{6}
\end{align*}
where $R$ is stated on actual executions in encoded length, has quantitative
content on a fixed exhaustive decider, is invariant under loose-clock and
actual equivalent-wrapper presentations, and does not contain $L$ by
definition.  Both arrows are \OPEN.

\section{Hostile controls, barriers, and post-quantum ledger}

Horn, $2$SAT, affine/Tseitin Gaussian elimination and bounded-treewidth
dynamic programming show that semantic structure may be an algorithmic
shortcut.  Independent products, padding, redundant clauses and alternate
encodings change costs but not the guard.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP make
presentation variance explicit.  A maximal semantic oracle erases the
putative acquisition burden; algebraic-oracle access does not repair it.
One-bit output forces neither compatibility materialization nor communication.

The theorem relativizes and algebrizes.  No constructive large circuit
property is supplied, so Natural Proofs is not directly triggered or bypassed.
Uniform fixed-$q$ wrappers use no advice; nonuniformity only enlarges the
hostile implementation class.

\textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER:} FLMV transfers only after one ordinary machine has
the required simultaneous resources.  Standard step counts transfer in the
declared machine model.\\
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE:} robust compilation and LWE-style external

unconditional worst-case all-solver bridge is missing.\\
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFER:} VLSI clocking, control observability, communication

squares, quadratic relation rank, Hilbert functions and syzygies lack a
forced code object.  Collision entropy measures wrapper fibers but not
runtime.  Special soundness lacks conflicting openings.  Cryptographic
binding and random oracles are inadmissible.
