\begin{abstract}
The newest peer chain asks for an explicit source-defined, hereditary,
strictly fractional SAT successor with a fixed complete ledger.  This report
closes an ambiguity in that handoff.  A total source-only successor satisfying
that contract on all descendants is already a standalone recursive SAT
algorithm; it is not an intermediate object whose existence follows merely
from an arbitrary polynomial SAT decider.  If local charged work is
$N^{r+o(1)}$, maximum child length is $N^{h+o(1)}$ with $h<1$, and aggregate
child volume is $N^{v+o(1)}$, depth-first evaluation has
\[
 T(N)=N^{C+o(1)},\qquad
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{v-h}{1-h}\right\},
\]
and conservative peak writable space $N^{P+o(1)}$ with
$P=\max\{p,h\}$ when the declared $N^{p+o(1)}$ ledger includes retained
state and local access.  Conversely, any endpoint decider gives a terminal
source compiler by decide-then-emit.  Thus a source-only contract whose
closed pair $(C,P)$ lies in the FLMV forbidden region is itself the forbidden
algorithm.  Under $P=NP$, decide-then-emit only gives $r=K$ for an unknown
polynomial exponent $K$.  The genuinely missing implication is therefore an
$A$-relative, strictly-smaller-instance bootstrap that converts every assumed
polynomial SAT decider into the favorable closed realization without a
current-scale call or hidden fallback.  A 59,404-check exact-arithmetic audit
passed.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim card, clean core, and target}

Let $\Htwo$ be duplicate-free proper Monotone $3$SAT-$(2,2)$ under the
canonical indexed encoding: every clause contains three distinct variables
with one sign, and every variable occurs twice positively and twice
negatively.  Thus $m=4n/3$ and complete encoded bit length is
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  D\"ocker proves this restriction NP-complete
\cite{doecker}; that source theorem is not a time lower bound.

The all-algorithm target remains
\[
 \forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\Htwo:\quad
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0
 \ \land\ T_A(F)>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,              \tag{1}
\]
where $A$ ranges over total correct uniform deterministic random-access SAT
deciders and all source reads, construction, access, replay, reconstruction,
output, retained state, and transient workspace are charged.  Statement (1)
is \OPEN and is not assumed.

The claim tested here is the current handoff
\[
 \text{``source-only, hereditary, fixed-ledger fractional successor''}
 \quad\text{as an intermediate consequence of }\SAT\in\mathsf P. \tag{2}
\]
Its exact quantifier issue is whether the successor is a total uniform
source transform independent of an assumed program $A$, or an $A$-relative
procedure whose calls and fallback paths are part of the realization.  The
weakest useful result is to settle the first reading and state the remaining
second reading without importing $P\ne NP$ into a premise.

\section{Fully charged hereditary source contract}

\begin{definition}[Source-only recursive successor]
For every ordinary encoded CNF $F$ of length $N>N_0$, a single total uniform
machine $S$ produces or locally exposes children $G_1,\ldots,G_q$ and a
deterministic combiner $B_F$ such that
\[
 \SAT(F)=B_F(\SAT(G_1),\ldots,\SAT(G_q)).                 \tag{3}
\]
For one fixed tuple $(r,p,h,v)$ and one uniform $o(1)$ envelope:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item local work, including failed routes, discovery, child access or
serialization, combiner work, reconstruction, and output, is
$N^{r+o(1)}$;
\item local writable space, including retained state needed while a child is
evaluated, is $N^{p+o(1)}$;
\item $1\le |G_i|\le N^{h+o(1)}$ for every unresolved child, where $h<1$;
\item $\sum_i |G_i|\le N^{v+o(1)}$; and
\item the same contract holds for every unresolved descendant.  Inputs of
length at most $N_0$ have a fixed exact base evaluator.
\end{enumerate}
Compressed children count only with a charged decoder/local accessor in the
same realization.  A materialized child buffer is writable space.  Read-only
source input follows the FLMV random-access convention.
\end{definition}

This definition permits a one-bit final answer and arbitrary Boolean
combiners.  It does not demand materialization of the semantic compatibility
table $H_F(\alpha)=[\alpha\models F]$ or any particular solver state.

\section{Recursive endpoint theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{SOURCE-ONLY-RECURSIVE-ENDPOINT-EQUIV1}, forward direction]
Assume the source-only recursive successor above.  Then SAT has one uniform
deterministic depth-first decider with
\[
 T(N)=N^{C+o(1)},\qquad
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{v-h}{1-h}\right\},               \tag{4}
\]
and conservative peak writable space
\[
 S(N)=N^{P+o(1)},\qquad P=\max\{p,h\}.                    \tag{5}
\]
Every resource in (4)--(5) belongs to this same recursive realization.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Recursively evaluate the children, depth first, and apply $B_F$.  Strict
power shrink gives path length $O(\log\log N)$ before the fixed base case.
For any $C\ge1$ and child lengths $n_i$,
\[
 \sum_i n_i^C
 \le (\max_i n_i)^{C-1}\sum_i n_i
 \le N^{h(C-1)+v+o(1)}.                                  \tag{6}
\]
The choice (4) makes $h(C-1)+v\le C$ and also pays the input read and local
work.  Induction with an arbitrarily small fixed slack absorbs the uniform
$o(1)$ terms, then yields $N^{C+o(1)}$.

For space, evaluate one child at a time.  At recursion depth $j$, the current
input scale is at most $N^{h^j+o(1)}$.  Local/retained state and a conservative
materialized next-child allowance are bounded by exponents $p h^j$ and
$h^{j+1}$, respectively.  The $O(\log\log N)$-term stack is dominated by
$N^{\max\{p,h\}+o(1)}$.  This pays suspended ancestors and transient child
state in one execution; it is not a coordinatewise infimum over different
machines.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Count/max-child form]
If instead $q\le N^{\eta+o(1)}$ and every child has length at most
$N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, $\lambda<1$, the exponent recurrence is
\[
 C_{t+1}=\max\{r,\eta+\lambda C_t\},
 \qquad C_*=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-\lambda}\right\}. \tag{7}
\]
Including source reading gives $\max\{1,C_*\}$.  A current-scale call adds a
$K$ term and forces $C\ge K$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Converse endpoint]
Let $L$ have fixed encodable yes- and no-instances.  If $L$ has a uniform
decider in a declared deterministic resource class $\mathcal R$ closed under
constant postprocessing, it has a source-only terminal successor in
$\mathcal R$: run the decider and emit the fixed yes-instance or no-instance.
For SAT the children may be fixed proper three-CNFs, $h=v=0$.  Conversely,
reading which endpoint was emitted decides $L$ in the same class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is the endpoint construction and readback of TESTS-0006.  All decision
work is constructor work, so no exponent disappears.
\end{proof}

The forward direction rederives the corpus's FORMAL-SPINE1 aggregate closure
in the present review notation; the converse is TESTS-0006.  The synthesis
advance is their exact consequence for (2), not a novelty claim for either
component theorem.

\section{Why the source-only target is not intermediate}

FLMV Corollary 5.8 gives, in its deterministic random-access model,
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
 \operatorname{DTISP}\!\left[n^c,o\!\left(n^{d(c)}\right)\right],
 \qquad d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right),\ c>1, \tag{8}
\]
and their locally computable SAT reduction preserves the required access
model \cite{flmv}.  At $c=6/5$, $d(c)=23/45>1/2$.  Therefore a contract whose
same-machine closure has time $N^{6/5+o(1)}$ and space $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ would
already be the forbidden endpoint algorithm, after the stated local encoding
transfer.  It is not a resource every arbitrary SAT implementation is known
to expose.

If $P=NP$, choose a polynomial SAT decider with some exponent $K$.  The
terminal converse gives $h=v=0$ but local exponent $r=K$, so (4) returns
$C\ge K$.  No contradiction follows.  To obtain a separation, one must prove
the exact still-open implication
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \forall A\,[A\text{ is a total uniform polynomial SAT decider}]\quad
 \Longrightarrow\quad &\text{one charged recursive realization using only}\\[-1mm]
 &\text{strictly smaller $A$-relative calls, with fixed favorable }(C,P),
 \end{aligned}                                             \tag{9}
\]
with no current-scale decision, hidden fallback, uncharged code analysis, or
different-machine resource coordinates.  Equation (9) is
\texttt{A-RELATIVE-FRACTIONAL-BOOTSTRAP1} and is \OPEN.  Proving it into the
FLMV region would prove $P\ne NP$; it must not be assumed under another name.

\section{Hostile controls and SAME\_REALIZATION audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{Terminal decide-then-emit.}
It has $h=v=0$ but $r=K$; contraction does not erase construction cost.
\item[] \textbf{Full Shannon restriction.}
Polynomially many bounded-occurrence restrictions remove only $O(\log N)$
source clauses, so their effective child exponent tends to one.  Enumerating
a linear restriction set makes branch volume exponential.  SYNTHESIS-0005 is
absent from GitHub; this statement is independently supported by the explicit
restriction floor in MODELBRIDGE-0004 and the bounded control below, not by a
reconstructed missing report.
\item[] \textbf{Horn, 2-SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded treewidth.}
These admit semantic shortcuts or specialized algorithms.  The theorem maps
whatever actual source contract exists to its endpoint cost; it does not
infer hardness from semantic states, treewidth, parity, or clause geometry.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products.}
Equation (6) charges all children through aggregate volume and does not assume
that independent factors require simultaneous storage.
\item[] \textbf{Padding and encodings.}
All powers use complete physical encoded length.  Padding changes the exponent
conversion and must be undone explicitly; compressed or auxiliary-variable
children carry decoder and reconstruction costs.
\item[] \textbf{DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP.}
A source-only algorithm is independent of the supplied implementation and
therefore proves no internal state obligation for these wrappers.  An
$A$-relative version must charge transport, delays, interpretation, and
recomputation in (9).
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output.}
The recursive evaluator returns only the SAT bit.  No compatibility table,
witness list, proof, communication transcript, branching program, or circuit
representation is presumed.
\end{description}

\section{Literature, barrier, and transfer audit}

FLMV is an exact endpoint/model transfer only after one actual uniform
all-length machine has both simultaneous resource bounds.  Its theorem does
not manufacture the successor in (9) \cite{flmv}.  Harsha, Mitropolsky, and
Rosen formalize downward self-reducibility for TFNP and prove consequences in
that search setting \cite{hmr}; their results are useful model discipline but
do not transfer to arbitrary decision-SAT programs.  Chavrimootoo et al. give
a directly relevant warning: assuming that arbitrary algorithms have a
particular self-reduction structure invalidates a purported SAT lower-bound
argument \cite{chkln}.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Only the elementary recursive closure and the declared FLMV endpoint/local
reduction transfer exactly to this realization.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Downward self-reduction, local decoding, pebbling, recursive separators, and
black-box reductions suggest possible designs for (9).  Each still needs the
all-input uniform compiler and charged physical access.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Communication, VLSI, streaming, proof-system, branching-program, Schur-square,
quadratic-relation, Hilbert-function, collision-entropy, and cryptographic
binding bounds constrain declared interfaces.  They do not force a one-bit
SAT decider to instantiate those interfaces.  No post-quantum technique gives
an exact transfer here.
\end{description}

The recursive accounting proof relativizes: give both the successor and
evaluator the same oracle and repeat it.  Algebraic-oracle access does not
invalidate the closure.  Thus the proof does not cross the Baker--Gill--Solovay
or Aaronson--Wigderson barriers \cite{bgs,aw}; the missing implication (9) must
supply any required nonrelativizing and nonalgebrizing hinge.  No large
constructive property of circuits is used, so Natural Proofs is not the
present hinge.  Nonuniform advice would create an additional compiler and
uniformity obligation, not repair the theorem.
