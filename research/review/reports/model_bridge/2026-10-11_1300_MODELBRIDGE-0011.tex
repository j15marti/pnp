\section*{Run result}

\textbf{New result (\EXACT): EXTENSIONAL-DOWNWARD-ORACLE-ELIMINATION1.}
A deterministic transducer that decides a language using only adaptive truth-bit
queries to the same language on strictly fractionally smaller encodings can be
converted by well-founded recursion into an ordinary decider.  If it makes at
most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ queries of length at most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, where
$\lambda<1$, and spends $N^{r+o(1)}$ charged local time, the resulting time
exponent is
\[
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{\eta}{1-\lambda}\right\}.
\]
Adaptivity and the unknown exponent $K$ of a supplied SAT implementation both
disappear.  Consequently an ``$A$-relative fractional bootstrap'' is not a
separate intermediate merely because it is allowed black-box access to an
assumed polynomial SAT solver $A$.  Once the strict downward bounds have been
proved, the supplied solver can be eliminated.

\textbf{Retired target (\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}):}
\texttt{BLACKBOX-A-RELATIVE-IS-INTERMEDIATE1}.  Pure answer-bit access to $A$
cannot distinguish DIRECT/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP implementations because all
exact SAT deciders compute the same Boolean function.  A surviving compiler may
inspect code, timing, or traces, but then it is intensional, must pass wrapper
covariance/SAME\_REALIZATION, and inherits the static-analysis and normalizer
obstructions already isolated by MODELBRIDGE-0010.

\textbf{Highest-value open arrow (\OPEN):}
\texttt{INTENSIONAL-COVARIANT-DOWNWARD-COMPILER1}.  From every assumed uniform
polynomial-time SAT decider, derive one all-input strictly downward contract
with fixed favorable parameters and no current-scale or fallback use of its
unknown exponent, while proving transport under equivalent wrappers and
charging construction, access, serialization, suspended state, replay, and
output.  This arrow is separation-strength; it is not bookkeeping.

\section{Primary-literature and barrier preflight}

The two blocking obstacles are (i) extracting a strictly downward, favorable
contract from an arbitrary polynomial SAT decider without assuming its
internal structure, and (ii) matching that contract to an unconditional
lower-bound endpoint without crossing models.  The literature check used
primary sources before promotion:

\begin{itemize}
\item Baker--Gill--Solovay prove opposite relativized outcomes for the
$P$ versus $NP$ question.  The present elimination lemma is a simulation fact;
it supplies no nonrelativizing hinge \cite{BGS75}.
\item Razborov--Rudich delimit broad constructive/useful circuit-property
arguments under pseudorandomness assumptions, and Aaronson--Wigderson extend

does not claim immunity: it builds no circuit property and no nonalgebrizing
step.
\item Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas give the model-matched SAT
time--space endpoint used by the project; their theorem does not manufacture
the source-to-contract compiler \cite{FLMV05}.
\item Chavrimootoo et al. identify the specific error of assuming that an
arbitrary SAT algorithm has a downward-self-reducible internal structure
\cite{CHKKN23}.  EXTENSIONAL-DOWNWARD-ORACLE-ELIMINATION1 is conditional on an
explicit transducer contract and therefore does not make that assumption.
\end{itemize}

\section{Encoding and semantic compatibility}

Use the canonical exact 3-CNF encoding fixed in the model-bridge spine.  For a
formula $F$ split across variables $X,Y$, define
\[
 H(F)_{u,v}=1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F(u,v)=1,
 \qquad
 \SAT(F)=\bigvee_{u,v}H(F)_{u,v}.
\]
This is a semantic object, not a mandatory state of a decider.  On the explicit
three-variable family containing any subset of the eight full width-three
clauses, the full semantic row has eight bits while satisfiability is simply
the test that not all eight clauses are present.  Thus even the cleanest finite
compatibility object need not be materialized to produce the one-bit answer.

The clean-core target remains an explicit infinite family of canonical proper
3-CNFs.  The theorem below is stronger in one direction: it applies to every
encoded input of an arbitrary language and hence to any such clean-core family
once an all-input downward transducer is actually supplied.  It is weaker in
the load-bearing direction: it does not construct that transducer.

\section{Exact theorem}

\begin{definition}[Extensional fractional downward transducer]
Let $L\subseteq\{0,1\}^*$.  A single uniform deterministic oracle transducer
$M^L$ has parameters $0\le\lambda<1$ and $\eta,r,p\ge0$ when, uniformly for all
inputs $x$ of encoded length $N$ above a fixed threshold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M^L(x)=1$ iff $x\in L$;
\item it makes at most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ adaptive oracle queries, each of encoded
length at most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$;
\item excluding the recursive cost of answering those queries, all executed
work---including query construction/access, serialization, resumption,
combining, and output---is at most $N^{r+o(1)}$;
\item its retained/local workspace is at most $N^{p+o(1)}$, with all suspended
frames charged; explicit active query buffers are separately charged by their
encoded length;
\item the transducer observes each oracle invocation only through its query
string and returned membership bit.  It does not observe the oracle program,
trace, internal state, or response time.
\end{enumerate}
The $o(1)$ envelopes are common and uniform over all nodes reached at a given
size.  Queries strictly decrease above the finite base threshold.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[EXTENSIONAL-DOWNWARD-ORACLE-ELIMINATION1]
If such an $M^L$ exists, then $L$ has one ordinary uniform deterministic
decider $D$ with
\[
 T_D(N)\le N^{C+o(1)},
 \qquad
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{\eta}{1-\lambda}\right\},
\]
and conservative explicit-buffer workspace
\[
 S_D(N)\le N^{P+o(1)},
 \qquad P=\max\{p,\lambda\}.
\]
The conclusion allows fully adaptive query selection.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Define $D(x)$ by well-founded recursion on encoded length.  Below the fixed
threshold, use a finite hard-coded table.  Above it, simulate $M(x)$.  Whenever
$M$ queries $y$, recursively compute $D(y)$ and return that bit.  Every query is
strictly smaller, so the recursion terminates.  Induction on length gives
$D(y)=L(y)$ for every query, hence the simulation sees exactly the oracle bits
seen by $M^L$.  Its adaptive future queries and final bit are therefore
identical, and $D(x)=L(x)$.

Suppressing uniform subpolynomial envelopes, the realized-path computation
obeys
\[
 T(N)\le N^r+N^\eta T(N^\lambda).
\]
For any fixed $\delta>0$, the usual induction with a sufficiently small common
error envelope bounds every child term by
$N^{\eta+\lambda(C+\delta)+o(1)}$.  The definition of $C$ gives
$\eta+\lambda C\le C$ and $r\le C$; choosing the envelope smaller than
$(1-\lambda)\delta$ yields $T(N)\le N^{C+\delta}$ above a larger threshold.
Finite exceptions change only the multiplicative constant.  Since this holds
for every $\delta>0$, the stated $N^{C+o(1)}$ bound follows.  Adaptivity changes
which realized queries occur, not their number or maximum length.

For depth-first evaluation, the root retains at most $N^{p+o(1)}$ local state
and an explicit query buffer of at most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ bits.  Descendant
root sizes are $N^{\lambda+o(1)},N^{\lambda^2+o(1)},\ldots$; summing charged
frames and buffers is dominated by $N^{\max\{p,\lambda\}+o(1)}$.  Streaming
access can improve this conservative buffer term only after its accessor costs
are proved.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact quantifier consequence.}
Suppose $P=NP$ and fix any exact polynomial-time SAT decider $A$, with unknown
exponent $K$.  If a claimed transformation produces an all-input transducer
$M_A$ satisfying the definition with fixed favorable $(\lambda,\eta,r,p)$ and
uses $A$ only through SAT answer bits on its smaller queries, the theorem
replaces every call to $A$ by recursive calls to $M_A$.  The exponent $K$
vanishes.  If the resulting $(C,P)$ lies in the forbidden FLMV region, this is
already the model-matched contradiction.  Therefore obtaining the contract is
the separation-strength arrow; labeling it ``$A$-relative'' does not weaken it.

\section{Wrapper, fallback, and hostile-control audit}

For a fixed input, DIRECT, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP implementations all
return the same exact membership bit on every query.  Induction on the adaptive
query number then gives the same transcript to an extensional transducer.
DEAD code is unobserved.  A timing-sensitive or trace-sensitive construction
escapes this induction only by leaving the extensional oracle model, at which
point SAME\_REALIZATION and wrapper covariance must be proved explicitly.

The strictness guards are sharp for this method:
\begin{itemize}
\item If $\lambda=1$, the recurrence becomes
$T(N)\le N^r+N^\eta T(N)$ and supplies no elimination bound.
\item A current-scale direct call to $A$ costs $N^{K+o(1)}$ and retains $K$.
\item An exact fallback to $A$ on an unresolved domain has exponent
$\max\{r,K\}$ absent an independent residual-domain theorem.
\item Padding, redundant clauses, and alternative encodings cannot be called
``smaller'' without proving the encoded-length inequality in the declared
format.
\item Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin Gaussian elimination, and bounded-treewidth
families remain polynomial controls with compact semantic summaries.  The
theorem imposes no compatibility extraction or state-exposure obligation on
them or on general inputs.
\end{itemize}

\section{PQ-technique ledger}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lossiness/collision entropy: ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new

fibers; that lossiness is an algorithmic shortcut, not a forced memory cost.
\item \textbf{Schur squares, quadratic relation spaces, Hilbert functions and
syzygies: FAILED TRANSFER here.}  They are invariants of chosen algebraic
representations.  No theorem forces an arbitrary truth-bit decider to expose
those representations.
\item \textbf{Worst-case/average-case external extraction: ANALOGY/CANDIDATE.}
Black-box behavior can recover SAT witnesses by self-reduction, but it does not
extract the favorable strict-downward resource contract proved necessary here.
\item \textbf{Collision extraction/special soundness and hash/random-oracle
methods: FAILED TRANSFER for an unconditional separation.}  Cryptographic
binding or random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible, and no construction-
specific conflicting-opening theorem is present.
\end{itemize}
In every case, hidden algebraic structure may help the solver; it is not
automatically a hardness source.
