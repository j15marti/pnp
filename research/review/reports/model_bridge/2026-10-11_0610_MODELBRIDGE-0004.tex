\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  This run attacks the most literal repair
of the retired pinning-contraction route: perhaps a source-first service can
assign only $o(n)$ variables of the canonical Monotone
$3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ clean core and emit a strict-power smaller ordinary CNF.
It cannot do so by standard syntactic restriction.  If $q$ variables are
assigned, at most $4q$ clauses are touched, while the core has $4n/3$
clauses.  Therefore at least $4n/3-4q$ unique proper three-clauses remain
verbatim.

In the declared self-delimiting indexed encoding, any residual of bit length
at most $N^\lambda$, for fixed $\lambda<1$, requires
\[
 q\ge \frac n3-\frac{N^\lambda}{44}
   =\frac n3-o(n).
\]
The symbolic statement is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for ordinary syntactic
restriction, optional deletion of exact duplicate clauses, clause/literal
reordering, and injective or dense variable renaming.  Promotion to the
living synthesis is left to independent red-team review.  The statement
does \emph{not} cover subsumption, resolution, affine elimination, a compact
circuit/branching-program representation, or a semantic local decision.
Consequently it falsifies \textsf{SUBLINEAR-RESTRICTION-CONTRACT1}, not the
full \textsf{UCORE-CONTRACT1} target and not arbitrary polynomial-time SAT.

The finite audit passed 20,412 exhaustive partial assignments, 408 seeded
large-core cells, 108,156 exact regression checks, and 2,856
renaming/order/complement wrapper checks.  These measurements are
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}; the asymptotic result rests on the proof below.

\section{Explicit clean core, compatibility object, and encoding}
\begin{definition}[Canonical clean core]
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ contains canonical monotone $3$-CNFs in which every
clause has three distinct variables, is all positive or all negative, is
unique, and every variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice
negatively.  Variables are $1,\ldots,n$ and clauses/literals are sorted.
Thus every variable has total occurrence degree four and
\[
 m=\frac{4n}{3}.
 \tag{1}
\]
D\"ocker proves that the unrestricted ordering of this family is
NP-complete~\cite{doecker}.  Sorting changes representation only.
\end{definition}

For a split $z=(x,y)$ define the semantic compatibility table
\[
 H(F)_{u,v}=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F(u,v)=1.
 \tag{2}
\]
Restriction selects a subcube of the row/column domain of $H(F)$, but (2) is
not an internal state obligation.  MODELBRIDGE-0003's external witness
extractor reveals one accepting coordinate using new pinned calls; it does
not force $H(F)$, a row, rank, DAG, or residual inside the original decider.

For a CNF with $v$ named variables and $s$ clauses, encode each nonnegative
count $a$ as Elias-gamma$(a+1)$, each clause width $d$ as
gamma$(d+1)$, and each literal by one sign bit and a fixed-width index of
width $w=\max\{1,\lceil\log_2(v+1)\rceil\}$.  On
$F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$,
\[
 N=|\Enc(F)|
 =\ell_\gamma(n+1)+\ell_\gamma(4n/3+1)
   +\frac{4n}{3}\bigl(5+3(1+w)\bigr)
 =\Theta(n\log n).
 \tag{3}
\]
The child may keep original IDs or densely renumber surviving variables.
The proof uses only a constant number of bits per surviving proper clause
and therefore survives either convention.

\section{Exact restriction obstruction}
Let $\alpha$ assign a set $Q$ of $q$ variables.  Standard restriction
deletes a satisfied clause and deletes a falsified literal from a remaining
clause.  A clause is \emph{untouched} when it contains no variable in $Q$.

\begin{theorem}[RESTRICTION-LINEAR-FLOOR1]
For every $F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$ and every partial assignment $\alpha$ on
$q$ variables, at least
\[
 U(F,\alpha)\ge \frac{4n}{3}-4q                           \tag{4}
\]
unique proper three-clauses remain verbatim in
$F\upharpoonright\alpha$.  This remains true after clause/literal
reordering, injective or dense variable renaming, and deletion of exact
duplicate clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each assigned variable occurs in exactly four clauses.  Hence the union of
clauses incident to $Q$ has size at most $4q$.  Every clause outside that
union contains no assigned variable, so restriction neither satisfies it nor
deletes a literal; it remains verbatim.  The original clauses are unique.
Distinct untouched width-three clauses stay distinct under an injective
renaming.  A touched surviving clause has width at most two, so it cannot
duplicate an untouched clause.  Thus optional exact deduplication does not
decrease their count.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[FIXED-POWER-RESTRICTION-BARRIER1]
Under the declared encoding,
\[
 |\Enc(F\upharpoonright\alpha)|
 \ge 11\max\{0,4n/3-4q\}.                                 \tag{5}
\]
Consequently, if for a fixed $\lambda<1$ the emitted explicit residual has
length at most $N^\lambda$, then
\[
 q\ge \frac n3-\frac{N^\lambda}{44}
   =\frac n3-o(n).                                         \tag{6}
\]
In particular every $q=o(n)$ ordinary restriction has residual length
$\Omega(n)=N^{1-o(1)}$, so its scale exponent is one, not a fixed
$\lambda<1$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For each untouched width-three clause, gamma$(3+1)$ uses five bits and its
three literals use at least two bits each (one sign and one index bit), for
at least eleven bits.  This proves (5).  Rearranging (5) under the
$N^\lambda$ upper bound gives (6).  Equation (3) and $\lambda<1$ imply
$(n\log n)^\lambda=o(n)$.
\end{proof}

For a satisfiable parent, any exact disjunctive restriction cover has at
least one satisfiable, hence non-false, child.  If every emitted child fixes
$o(n)$ variables, (5) prevents fixed-power explicit shrink on that child.
A branch that already contains an empty clause may terminate FALSE locally.
Satisfying all clauses and terminating TRUE requires touching at least $m$
clauses and hence at least $n/3$ variables by the same count.  No direct-sum
conclusion follows: a semantic service could prune branches, find a witness,
or decide the parent without emitting a cover.

\section{Why this does not prove a computation lower bound}
The theorem is a representation lower bound on one transformation.  It is
also true, with the appropriate maximum occurrence $\Delta$, that any
bounded-occurrence easy CNF leaves at least $m-\Delta q$ untouched clauses.
The audit instantiated:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Control & Polynomial shortcut & Maximum occurrence\\
\midrule
Horn implication cycle & forward/SCC propagation & $2$\\
Equality $2$-SAT & implication graph & $4$\\
Affine parity path in $2$-CNF & path propagation/Gaussian elimination & $4$\\
Independent exact-one triples & constant-width component DP & $3$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All exhibit the same linear residual-size phenomenon under
$q=\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$ restrictions.  Thus (4) cannot be promoted from
syntax to runtime.  Compact circuits, branching programs, affine systems,
or bounded-treewidth tables may represent the same semantics without
writing the residual CNF.  Padding can inflate the parent length and
manufacture a false apparent exponent; (6) deliberately measures the
canonical unpadded core.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP wrappers change
execution traces but not the incidence proof.

Assume $\Pclass=\NPclass$ and let $A$ be any fixed uniform polynomial SAT
decider with exponent $K$.  Equation (6) only says that a particular
restriction compiler must fix linearly many variables to obtain strict
explicit shrink.  Under the assumption, $A$ may decide locally, a standard
self-reduction may find a witness in polynomial time, and an alternative
representation may avoid the residual.  There is no unconditional
contradiction and no premise equivalent to $\Pclass\ne\NPclass$ has been
inserted.

\section{Primary literature and transfer audit}
The Sparsification Lemma of Impagliazzo--Paturi--Zane expresses a $k$-CNF as
an OR of at most $2^{\epsilon n}$ bounded-occurrence $k$-CNFs with $O(n)$
clauses~\cite{ipz}.  This is a \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} in its exact
fine-grained setting.  It does not produce polynomially many
$N^\lambda$-bit children for fixed $\lambda<1$; the present clean core is
already sparse, with exactly $4n/3$ clauses.  Treating \emph{sparse} as
\emph{strict-power smaller} is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas give genuine SAT time--space forbidden
regions for deterministic random-access Turing machines~\cite{flmv}.
Equations (4)--(6) do not place an arbitrary decider in that region.  The
missing theorem is still a total hereditary source-first service with
physical child access, replay, output, and peak space charged in the FLMV
model.

The incidence proof has an exact hypergraph correspondence: assigned
variables are deleted vertices and untouched clauses are hyperedges disjoint
from the deleted set.  The degree-union bound transfers exactly at the
\emph{representation} level.  VLSI locality, memory hierarchy,
communication cuts, pebbling, and proof-system width remain
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} or \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} to unrestricted SAT
without a compiler that forces the same interface.

The proof relativizes and survives a semantic SAT oracle as a statement
about explicit syntax; it is not the nonrelativizing hinge required after
Baker--Gill--Solovay.  Affine controls show that algebraic access can bypass
the representation, so no nonalgebrizing hinge is supplied.  Natural Proofs
is not triggered because no large constructive useful circuit property is
asserted.

Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies, and
collision entropy have no object correspondence to the touched-clause count
and are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} here.  LWE-style black-box extraction and
special-soundness binding remain \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; external
witness extraction does not create strict shrink.  Cryptographic hardness
and random-oracle assumptions are not admissible.
