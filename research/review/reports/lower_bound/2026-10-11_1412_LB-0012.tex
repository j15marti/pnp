\begin{abstract}
This run constructs an explicit infinite family inside proper unique
Monotone 3-Sat-(2,2) whose semantic compatibility matrices have rank
$2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$, where $N$ is the indexed input length, while every
member has a fixed matching witness and is recognizable/decidable in linear
time in canonical encoding.  The base $K_{3,3}$ block has nine variables,
twelve clauses, and a $32\times16$ compatibility matrix of full column rank
16 over both $\mathbb F_2$ and $\mathbb R$.  Tensoring $k$ independent blocks
gives rank $16^k=2^{4k}$.  Thus exponential compatibility rank, even inside
the project's NP-complete clean-core syntax, does not force extraction,
materialization, memory, time, or communication in a one-bit SAT execution.
The unrestricted worst-case lower bound remains OPEN; P versus NP remains
open.
\end{abstract}

The explicit clean core is
\(
\Htwo
\), the canonical indexed proper unique Monotone 3-Sat-(2,2) language:
every clause has three distinct variables, every clause is all-positive or
all-negative, clauses are unique, and every variable occurs exactly twice

\[
 \tag{COMPAT-RANK-FORCES-WORK1}

\section{Primary-literature and barrier preflight}

The two obstacles are (i) distinguishing semantic compatibility complexity
from the resources of a one-bit formula decider, and (ii) transferring any
restricted communication or representation lower bound to arbitrary uniform
SAT computation.  Primary sources were checked first.

D\"ocker's paper supplies both the exact clean-core definition and the fact
that paired clauses $\{C,\overline C\}$ form an always-satisfiable special
case at occurrence two \cite{doecker}.  Mehlhorn--Schmidt's rank method gives
the standard communication lower bound
\(D(f)\ge\log_2\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb R}M_f\) for the separate task of
evaluating a two-party Boolean matrix \cite{mehlhornschmidt}.  FLMV supplies
simultaneous SAT time--space lower bounds on its declared random-access model,
not a theorem identifying semantic matrix rank with arbitrary SAT work
\cite{flmv}.  Relativization and algebrization remain explicit barriers
\cite{bgs,aw}; no circuit property is constructed, so Natural Proofs is not
the active hinge.

\paragraph{Transfer classification.}
Rank multiplicativity under Kronecker product and the communication log-rank
lower bound are \textbf{directly transferable} to the declared compatibility
evaluation problem.  VLSI routing, tensor-network contraction, separator
communication and branching-program width are \textbf{useful analogies
requiring a new transfer theorem}.  Treating any of them as a bound on a
static one-bit SAT execution is a \textbf{misleading analogy / failed
transfer}.

\section{The paired $K_{3,3}$ block}

\begin{definition}[Base formula]
Let $G=K_{3,3}$ with bipartition
$U=\{u_0,u_1,u_2\}$ and $W=\{w_0,w_1,w_2\}$.  For every edge
$e=(u_i,w_j)$ introduce a variable $x_{ij}$.  At each of the six vertices
$v$, include the pair
\[
 C_v^+=\bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e,
 \qquad
 C_v^-=\bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e.
\]
Call the resulting 12-clause formula $B$.
\end{definition}

Every variable is incident to its two endpoints and therefore occurs twice
positively and twice negatively.  Each clause has three distinct variables,
is monotone, and is unique.  Thus $B\in\Htwo$.  The diagonal perfect matching
$\{(u_i,w_i):0\le i<3\}$ gives a satisfying assignment by setting matching
edges to one and the other edges to zero: every vertex then sees both values.

Index the nine edges row-major from zero to eight.  Split the variables as
\[
 X=\{2,4,6,7,8\},\qquad Y=\{0,1,3,5\},
\]
and define the semantic compatibility matrix
\[
 H_B(a,b)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B(a,b)=1,
 \qquad a\in\{0,1\}^{5},\ b\in\{0,1\}^{4}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{H22-COMPAT-RANK-GUARD1}]
Let $F_k$ be the conjunction of $k$ variable-disjoint canonically ordered
copies of $B$, with the preceding split applied blockwise.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F_k\in\Htwo$ has $9k$ variables and $12k$ clauses;
\item $F_k$ has a fixed, uniformly constructible matching witness;
\item over both $\mathbb F_2$ and $\mathbb R$,
\[
 \operatorname{rank}H_{F_k}=16^k=2^{4k};
\]
\item under the canonical indexed encoding,
\[
 N_k=|\operatorname{enc}(F_k)|=\Theta(k\log k),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}H_{F_k}=2^{\Theta(N_k/\log N_k)};
\]
\item one uniform total exact 3-SAT decider recognizes canonical $F_k$ and
returns SAT in $O(N_k)$ time and $O(\log N_k)$ writable workspace, using any
exact fallback off the family.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The clean-core incidence and witness claims follow blockwise from the base
construction.  Direct elimination over $\mathbb F_2$ gives
\(
\operatorname{rank}H_B=16
\), the full column count.  Equivalently, a selected $16\times16$ minor has
odd determinant.  The same minor is therefore nonsingular over $\mathbb R$,
so the real rank is also 16.

Because the blocks use disjoint variables and are conjoined, compatibility
factors exactly:
\[
 H_{F_k}=H_B^{\otimes k}.
\]
Matrix rank is multiplicative under Kronecker product over either field,
giving $16^k$.  The input has $\Theta(k)$ fixed-width clause records, each
using $\Theta(\log k)$ bits for variable identifiers, so
$N_k=\Theta(k\log k)$.

For the algorithmic claim, scan the canonical header and clause records and
compare them on demand with the fixed $k$-block template.  Indices and the
block counter use $O(\log N_k)$ work bits.  If every record matches, return
one.  Otherwise invoke any fixed total exact fallback, for example brute
force.  This is a uniform total exact decider and is linear-time on the
displayed family.  Under a hypothetical polynomial-time SAT decider $A$, use
$A$ as fallback; the resulting solver remains polynomial on all inputs and
still runs linearly on every $F_k$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Rank is not one-bit SAT work]
Semantic compatibility rank, even at scale
$2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$ inside the NP-complete clean-core syntax, does not
force its materialization, extraction, memory footprint, communication
transcript, or comparable running time in every exact execution.
\end{corollary}

The corollary is pointwise.  It does not show that the full clean core is
easy, nor does it refute the worst-case quantifiers of UCORE-TIME1.

\section{Communication and SAME\_REALIZATION audit}

The log-rank method correctly proves that two parties separately holding
$a\in\{0,1\}^{5k}$ and $b\in\{0,1\}^{4k}$ require at least $4k$ deterministic
communication bits to evaluate $H_{F_k}(a,b)$.  A SAT decider instead receives
the formula $F_k$ and must output
\[
 \bigvee_{a,b}H_{F_k}(a,b).
\]
That answer is one for every $k$ and has a fixed matching certificate.  The
assignment pair is not the actual split input to the decider.  Charging the
two-party protocol to the SAT execution would change the input, output and
realization.  SAME\_REALIZATION therefore blocks this transfer exactly.

Assuming $P=NP$ does not rescue it.  For any polynomial SAT decider, the
recognize-first wrapper above is a polynomial SAT decider that avoids the
compatibility task on $F_k$.  This supplies no unconditional contradiction;
it shows why a universal structural premise cannot be smuggled into the
contradiction argument.

\section{Hostile controls, encodings, and barriers}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.9cm,style=nextline]
\item[Affine/Horn/2SAT.] Gaussian elimination, implication propagation and
SCC algorithms are established semantic shortcuts; they remain controls
against promoting large compatibility objects to arbitrary work.  The new
family adds a matching shortcut inside $\Htwo$ rather than replacing those
controls.
\item[Bounded treewidth.] Independent products can have small component
treewidth even while tensor rank multiplies.  Conversely LB-0008 already
exhibits high clean-core treewidth with an easy matching route.  Neither
direction forces computation.
\item[Alternative encodings.] Variable renaming, clause permutation and
global complementation only permute matrix rows/columns and preserve rank.
The canonical recognizer is linear; decoded encodings require their decoder
to be charged.  Padding and redundant clauses cannot be used to change the
encoded exponent silently.
\item[Wrappers.] DIRECT, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, RECOMP and DEAD wrappers preserve
the decision.  RECOMP may regenerate the fixed matching instead of storing
it.  None is forced to expose $H_{F_k}$.
\item[One-bit output.] The required answer is a single SAT bit.  Requiring a
matrix, table, witness list or protocol transcript defines another problem.
\end{description}

The recognizer and counterroute relativize, and algebraic oracle access can
only make shortcuts stronger.  Thus this result identifies rather than
crosses the Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson barriers.  It proves
no large constructive circuit property and invokes no cryptographic
assumption.

\paragraph{Post-quantum technique ledger.}
The tensor/Kronecker identity is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER} for
the compatibility matrices.  Schur squares, quadratic relation spaces,
Hilbert functions, syzygies and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED
TRANSFER} for the present all-algorithm claim because no code or proof-node
map is part of the one-bit execution.  LWE-style external extraction is an
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; it still needs an unconditional worst-case,
same-realization compiler.  Hash and random-oracle binding are inadmissible.
