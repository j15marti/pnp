\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
This run attacks the causal-use repair proposed after the white-box
EXTRACTOR audits.  The exact result is a uniform repetition compiler.  For
every deterministic polynomial-time decider and every polynomial raw-cell
intervention budget $s(N)$, there is an extensionally equal deterministic
polynomial-time implementation whose complete encoded checkpoint state is
unchanged at the decoded level by every intervention on at most $s(N)$ raw
cells.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in the declared checkpoint model.

Consequently, bounded raw-cell causal indispensability is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as an all-implementation necessity premise for
EXTRACTOR/G2.  The result does not rule out a decoded, representation-covariant
functional of the execution, nor a source-specific obligation.  Such a
functional still requires charged decoding or reconstruction, treatment of
DIRECT/RECOMP implementations, strict source-scale contraction, and an
independent lower-bound endpoint.  All of those arrows remain \textbf{OPEN}.
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Repetition compiler}
\begin{definition}[Robust checkpoint compilation]
Let $A$ be a deterministic transition machine.  At a logical checkpoint its
complete finite work configuration is $z\in\{0,1\}^{m}$; this includes the
control state, address/head data, work memory, and any pending output bit, but
not the fixed read-only source.  For $f\ge0$, put $r=2f+1$ and define
\[
 E_r(z)=\bigl(z_1^r,z_2^r,\ldots,z_m^r\bigr),
 \qquad
 D_r(y)_i=\operatorname{Maj}(y_{i,1},\ldots,y_{i,r}).
\]
If $\delta$ is one logical transition and $o$ is the final output map, the
compiled checkpoint maps are
\[
 \widehat\delta=E_r\circ\delta\circ D_r,
 \qquad
 \widehat o=E_r\circ o\circ D_r,
\]
with the external output released only after majority decoding its replicated
output block.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[REPLICATION-CAUSAL-GUARD1]
Let $A$ be any total uniform deterministic decider using time $T(N)$ and at
most $m(N)\le T(N)$ work-state bits on $N$-bit inputs.  Let $s(N)$ be any
polynomial-time-computable nonnegative integer.  There is a total uniform
deterministic decider $\widehat A_s$ for the same language such that at every
compiled checkpoint and for every overwrite set $I$ of at most $s(N)$ raw
encoded state cells,
\[
 D_{2s(N)+1}\!\left(\operatorname{Overwrite}_I
       (E_{2s(N)+1}(z))\right)=z.
 \tag{2}
\]
Hence the next decoded state and final decision are identical to the
unintervened execution.  The compilation uses
\[
 O\bigl((2s(N)+1)m(N)\bigr)\text{ work bits}
\]
and a conservative
\[
 O\bigl((2s(N)+1)m(N)T(N)\bigr)\text{ time bound}.
 \tag{3}
\]
In particular, polynomial $T,m,s$ remain polynomial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An overwrite of at most $s(N)$ raw cells changes at most $s(N)$ coordinates
in any one repetition block.  Each block contains $2s(N)+1$ equal bits, so at
least $s(N)+1$ original copies remain and its majority is unchanged.  This
proves (2) block by block.  Applying $\delta$ or $o$ to the unchanged decoded
configuration gives the same next logical configuration or output.  Encoding
and decoding all $m(N)$ logical cells costs the stated space and at most the
stated work per simulated transition, proving (3).  The compiler computes
$s(N)$ from the input length and uses fixed uniform loops, so it is uniform.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All-polynomial-decider consequence]
For every polynomial budget $s(N)$ and every language in $\Pclass$, there is
a polynomial-time exact decider on which no set of at most $s(N)$ raw
checkpoint cells is causally indispensable in the sense of (1).
Consequently, under the conditional assumption $\SAT\in\Pclass$, the same is
true for SAT and for the canonical hard family $\mathcal H_{2,2}$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the theorem to any fixed polynomial-time decider for the language.
Every overwrite supported on a proposed set $Q$ of size at most $s(N)$ is
corrected before the next logical transition.  Under $\SAT\in\Pclass$, choose
the hypothesized polynomial SAT decider as $A$.
\end{proof}

This is not a contradiction under $\Pclass=\NPclass$.  It instead shows that
the raw causal premise fails in that counterfactual world exactly where a
universal lower-bound argument would need it.

\section{What survives for EXTRACTOR/G2}
The theorem separates three candidate meanings of causal use:
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Raw-coordinate influence: FALSIFIED/RETIRED as universal.}
Polynomial redundancy eliminates every polynomially bounded local
intervention while preserving exact behavior and polynomial time.
\item[] \textbf{Decoder-relative logical influence: OPEN.}
The logical state $D_r(y)$ is representation-covariant for the repetition
wrapper, but naming it does not prove that DIRECT or RECOMP implementations
materialize a common compatibility object.  Decoding and verification are
charged work.
\item[] \textbf{Source-specific functional obligation: OPEN.}
A theorem may still bind an externally defined functional to every execution
or charge an alternative resource.  It must produce more than one-bit output
dependence and must survive direct decision, source rereading, recomputation,
compact representations, and oracle replacement.
\end{description}

Thus RT-0004's ``causal use'' repair is viable only after quotienting by
efficient encodings and treating decoder cost explicitly.  Representation
covariance is not cosmetic: without it, the proposed relation can be erased
by a polynomial compiler.  Even with covariance, SYNTHESIS-0001's stricter
requirement remains.  A useful object must yield a source-first ordinary-CNF
successor of scale $\lambda<1$, or an independently forbidden same-realization
resource.  Causal naming alone provides neither.

\section{Literature, barriers, and cross-domain transfer}
Baker--Gill--Solovay construct relativized worlds with opposite P-versus-NP
answers \cite{bgs}.  REPLICATION-CAUSAL-GUARD1 relativizes: replace every
transition of $A$ by an oracle transition with the same oracle, then apply the
same encoding.  The result therefore removes a representation-dependent route
but does not cross the relativization barrier.

Aaronson--Wigderson formalize algebraic-oracle access and show that major open
problems including P versus NP require nonalgebrizing techniques \cite{aw}.
Chen--Hu--Ren give newer algebrization barriers for several circuit lower-bound
regimes via communication complexity \cite{chr}.  Neither result supplies a
decoded causal functional or strict SAT successor here.  The repetition
countercompiler is independent of algebraic access, so an algebraic oracle
does not rescue raw-coordinate indispensability.  No Natural-Proofs property
is proposed.

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas provide genuine SAT time--space
endpoints in declared random-access models \cite{flmv}; they do not force raw
state sensitivity in every polynomial implementation.  Endpoint transfer
therefore remains \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} without the missing successor or
same-realization resource theorem.

Von Neumann's reliable-automata work supplies the historical redundancy and
majority motif \cite{vn}.  The finite repetition identity transfers exactly
as a construction, but the physical stochastic-fault theorems do not transfer
to unrestricted SAT lower bounds.  Classification: \textbf{EXACT OBJECT
TRANSFER} for repetition/majority encoding; \textbf{FAILED THEOREM TRANSFER}
for any claimed SAT hardness consequence.

The post-quantum ledger does not repair the barrier.  Schur squares,
quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, and syzygies have no exact object
map to raw trace interventions in this run.  Collision entropy concerns a
fixed many-to-one map and gives no causal or strict-shrink necessity.
LWE-style black-box extraction and special-soundness binding remain
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; they require a new same-realization bridge and
cannot import cryptographic assumptions.
