\section{Exact selected implication and quantifiers}
The selected question was whether the newly recovered source-probe guard
can promote a strict semantic successor into an obstruction applicable to
every polynomial-time SAT algorithm. The model is a deterministic
random-access clause-slot machine on a canonical CNF encoding of bit length
$N$. One same-realization constructor and its consumer must charge every
source probe, output bit, retained source pointer, deferred query, cache,
replay, and workspace bit. The minimum useful theorem would have to force
either superpolynomial time, or a named forbidden simultaneous
time--space point, for every exact all-input SAT implementation. A mere
$\Omega(N/\log N)$ probe floor is not enough.

\section{Linear-sharpness theorem}
For each $r\ge1$, force variables $x_1,\ldots,x_r,y,z$ to zero with
proper width-three gadgets and append switch clauses
\[
 C_i^0=(\neg x_i\vee y\vee z).
\]
Let $F_0$ contain all $C_i^0$, and let $F_j$ change only slot $j$ to
$C_j^1=(x_j\vee y\vee z)$. Then $F_0$ is satisfiable and every $F_j$
is unsatisfiable.

\begin{theorem}[Probe-floor tightness and barrier classification]
In the clause-slot model:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every deterministic self-contained exact semantic transporter must
query all $r$ switch slots on $F_0$;
\item a direct scan of those slots decides this family, emits one Boolean
value, and uses $O(r)$ time and $O(\log r)$ work bits;
\item both statements remain true relative to an arbitrary common oracle
$O$ when constructor and evaluator receive the same $O$.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently the probe argument proves no exponent larger than one and
does not furnish the nonrelativizing hinge required for P versus NP.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If slot $j$ is not queried on $F_0$, the deterministic transcript and
self-contained output are identical on $F_0$ and $F_j$, contradicting
their opposite SAT values. Conversely, scan the switch signs and output
TRUE exactly when every sign is negative. The forcing gadgets make this
criterion exact. A counter and current sign use $O(\log r)$ work bits.
The indistinguishability and scan arguments never inspect or restrict
oracle answers, so attaching the same oracle to all machines preserves
them verbatim.
\end{proof}

Under the hypothetical assumption $\SAT\in\mathsf P$, the still more
general decide-then-emit constructor runs the assumed decider and emits a
fixed YES or NO target. It gives constant output length, but retains the
unknown decider's time and space. Hence strict semantic size contraction
does not imply a favorable charged successor.

\section{Hostile controls and failed promotions}
The theorem survives padding only after measuring the active encoding;
padding can weaken a probe ratio. A lazy child carrying a source pointer
is outside the self-contained clause, but the combined constructor and
consumer must eventually pay the same switch probes. COPY, RECOMP and
memoization move or duplicate the charge rather than remove it.
INTERP and an oracle can still compute the semantic bit by another route.
Easy-family controls are decisive: the switch family itself is solvable by
the linear scanner. Root-level access says nothing about descendant
closure when descendants lie in a promise domain. No finite test is used
to infer asymptotics.
