\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
This run proves three exact audit lemmas.  The PFCERT global list premise
is a bound on distinct outputs of an NP multivalued function, not on its
accepting paths.  Per-witness endpoint bounds combine only by a tight
union bound.  Per-program fiber sparsity need not make the joint graph a
sparse language.  These results retire proposed automatic transfers
through $\mathsf{UP}$, $\mathsf{FewP}$, and Mahaney's theorem; they do not
construct the endpoint list cover or a nonrelativizing hinge.
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  The proofs are human-readable LaTeX,
not proof-assistant verification.

\section{Selected implication and exact quantifiers}
The selected missing implication is: for one fixed deterministic
polynomial-time verifier $V$ and polynomial $q$, prove for every length
$n$ and every permitted program $p$ that
\[
 \left|\{x\in\{0,1\}^n:\exists\pi,\ |\pi|\le q(n),
                  V(1^n,p,x,\pi)=1\}\right|\le L(n),
\]
while accepting every required true endpoint.  The cardinality is taken
\emph{after} the existential union over all witnesses.  The same verifier,
encoding, witness polynomial, and ordinary machine must work uniformly
on all inputs.  Certificate discovery is not needed for membership in
$\NP$, but encoding, source access, verification, and every output bit
remain charged wherever an algorithm actually constructs the cover.

\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
Mahaney's theorem proves that a sparse NP-hard language under
polynomial-time many-one reductions collapses P and NP
\cite{mahaney1982}; it does not turn fiberwise list bounds into global
sparsity.  Hemaspaandra--Naik--Ogihara--Selman show that generic unique
solution computation has polynomial-hierarchy collapse consequences
\cite{hnos1996}; unique output is not a cost-free verifier normal form.
Hirahara--Kabanets--Lu--Oliveira prove exact search-to-decision results in
specified time-bounded Kolmogorov regimes \cite{hklo2024}; their theorem
does not construct this all-input list cover.  Ren--Wang--Zhong's current
range-avoidance result is conditional on weak pseudorandom generators
\cite{rwz2026}; cryptographic assumptions are inadmissible for an
unconditional separation.

The multivalued-function correspondence is a directly transferable
definition.  Using sparse-complete-set or generic range-avoidance
theorems is a failed transfer without a new global-density/reduction or
unconditional generator theorem.  Canonical proof selection remains a
candidate requiring a charged construction-specific theorem.

\section{Exact witness-union and padding lemmas}
For a fixed $V$, let
\[
 f_V(1^n,p)=\{x:\exists\pi\ V(1^n,p,x,\pi)=1\}.
\]
An NP transducer guesses $(x,\pi)$, verifies, and outputs $x$; global
list soundness bounds the number of distinct values of $f_V$.

\begin{lemma}[Witness-union gate]
For $R\subseteq X\times\Pi$, if every fiber
$R_\pi=\{x:(x,\pi)\in R\}$ has size at most $b$, then
\[
 \left|\bigcup_{\pi\in\Pi}R_\pi\right|
 \le\min\{|X|,b|\Pi|\}.
\]
The bound is tight when $X$ contains $b|\Pi|$ elements.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the union bound.  Pairwise disjoint fibers of size $b$ attain it.
\end{proof}
For witnesses of length at most $q$, the automatic bound is only
$b(2^{q+1}-1)$.  Two singleton fibers with different outputs are the
smallest counterexample to the inference ``single-valued per witness
implies single-valued after existential projection.''

\begin{lemma}[Witness-padding control]
Appending $d$ ignored witness bits preserves the endpoint image exactly
and multiplies each accepting-witness count by $2^d$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The verifier ignores the suffix.  Every old accepting witness has all
$2^d$ extensions, while the accepted endpoints are unchanged.
\end{proof}
Thus output multiplicity and accepting-path multiplicity are formally
different.  Lexicographically least accepting witnesses do not repair the
gap for free: minimality has a coNP universal condition.  Under $\PP=\NP$
it is decidable in polynomial time, but with an uncontrolled exponent,
so it is not the needed charged construction.

\section{Fiber sparsity countermodel}
\begin{lemma}
A polynomial-time relation can have one endpoint per program but
exponentially many accepted program--endpoint pairs at infinitely many
total lengths.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every $(n-1)$-bit $p$, accept only $x=0p$.  Each fiber has size one,
but there are $2^{n-1}$ accepted pairs of total length $2n-1$, up to fixed
encoding overhead.
\end{proof}
Mahaney's globally sparse-language premise therefore does not follow.
The construction relativizes, as do the union and padding lemmas.  They
are obstruction audits, not the missing nonrelativizing mechanism.

\section{Bounded reproducible test and hostile controls}
The command
\begin{verbatim}
timeout 30s python3 experiments/review/lower_bound_witness_union_gate.py
  --max-n 14
  --output results/raw/review/2026-10-10_lower_bound_witness_union_gate.json
\end{verbatim}
used a 25-second cooperative alarm and finished in 0.309 seconds.  It
exhausted all 65,536 relations on four outputs by four witnesses, found
564 relations with at most one output per witness but a union larger than
one, checked 928 tight union cases and 144 padding cases, and reported no
failures.  The smallest recorded counterexample has two singleton fibers
with distinct outputs.  These checks are \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL} harness
validation only.

Padding, independent-product and easy-family controls agree with the
proofs.  An endpoint image can remain fixed while path counts explode;
independent witnesses can make the union bound tight; and the singleton
graph countermodel is decidable in linear time.  Oracle replacement
preserves every audit lemma.  Finite success cannot establish an
asymptotic cover.
