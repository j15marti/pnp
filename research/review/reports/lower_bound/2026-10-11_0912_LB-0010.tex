\begin{abstract}
MODELBRIDGE-0007 shows that a residual service containing the empty query
already contains the original SAT decision problem.  This report tests the
obvious repair: forbid the empty query and require a genuine pin.  The repair
fails generically.  A two-clause, proper, monotone, source-connected gadget
makes either value of one fresh pinned variable equal the original SAT bit,
with linear encoded-size overhead.  Conversely, a single pinned coordinate
compiles in linear time to proper 3-CNF.  Thus the unrestricted one-coordinate
nonempty-pinning problem is linearly interreducible with its source SAT
language.  A lower bound against all polynomial algorithms for that service
is already separation-strength, not an easier EXTRACTOR bridge.  A bounded
audit passed 7,296 semantic, encoding, wrapper, recomputation, clean-core, and
hostile-family checks.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Clean source family and exact target}

Let $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ denote the canonical proper Monotone
$3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ language: clauses are unique monotone triples of distinct
variables and every variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice
negatively.  D\"ocker proves this language NP-complete.  With $n$ variables,
$3m=4n$, and the canonical identifier encoding has length
\[
 N=\Theta(n\log n).                                          \tag{2}
\]

For $F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$ choose its least variable $x$ and fresh variables
$z,a,b,c$.  Define the pin lift
\[
 G_x(F)=F\wedge(z\vee a\vee b)
             \wedge(\neg z\vee\neg x\vee\neg c).            \tag{3}
\]
Both added clauses are proper, monotone, unique, and the second meets the
source at $x$.  Thus the gadget is not a syntactically disconnected unused
variable.  Let
\[
 \mathcal H_{\rm pin}=\{G_x(F):F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}\}.        \tag{4}
\]
The lift has $n+4$ variables, $m+2$ clauses, and encoded length $N'=\Theta(N)$.
It does not preserve the exact $(2,2)$ occurrence promise, which is recorded
as the separate \texttt{BALANCED-PIN-LIFT1} target.

The separation-strength target remains
\[
\tag{UCORE-TIME1}
\forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0
 \ \wedge\ T_A(\operatorname{enc}(F))>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,
\]
where $A$ ranges over total uniform deterministic exact deciders.  It is
\OPEN.  No premise below assumes it.

\section{Nonempty-pin equivalence theorem}

Let $\operatorname{PinSAT}_{\mathcal H_{\rm pin}}$ receive
$G_x(F)$ and the one-variable partial assignment $z=v$, where
$v\in\{0,1\}$.  Its required output is
\[
 \mathbf 1[G_x(F)\!\upharpoonright_{z=v}\text{ is satisfiable}]. \tag{5}
\]

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{NONEMPTY-PIN-EQUIVALENCE1}]
For either fixed $v\in\{0,1\}$, the source problem
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$-SAT reduces uniformly to (5) with linear construction and
encoded-size overhead.  Conversely, one requested pin of any explicit
proper 3-CNF compiles uniformly in linear time and size to an equisatisfiable
proper 3-CNF.  More precisely:
\[
 F\in\mathsf{SAT}iff
 G_x(F)\!\upharpoonright_{z=v}\in\mathsf{SAT};                \tag{6}
\]
and a time-$T_E$, space-$S_E$ evaluator $E$ for (5) yields a source decider
with conservative explicit-buffer bounds
\[
 T_B(N)\le O(N)+T_E(cN),\qquad S_B(N)\le O(N)+S_E(cN)         \tag{7}
\]
for a fixed encoding constant $c$.  A time-$T_A$, space-$S_A$ general
3-SAT decider yields a coordinate evaluator with the analogous bounds
\[
 T_Q(N)\le O(N)+T_A(c'N),\qquad S_Q(N)\le O(N)+S_A(c'N).      \tag{8}
\]
Any stronger virtual-input or streaming space bound must declare and charge
its accessor separately.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $z=0$, the first added clause becomes $(a\vee b)$ and the second is
satisfied.  Every satisfying assignment of $F$ extends by taking $a=1$, and
restriction cannot repair an unsatisfied clause of $F$.  If $z=1$, the first
added clause is satisfied and the second becomes $(\neg x\vee\neg c)$.
Every assignment of $F$ extends by taking $c=0$.  This proves (6) for both
nonempty pins.  The lift is emitted by one scan, and increasing the variable
identifier width after adding four variables changes the explicit encoding
by only a constant factor, proving the first reduction and (7).

For the converse, scan the clauses, delete satisfied clauses, and delete the
assigned literal from the others.  A residual 2-clause $(p\vee q)$ becomes
$(p\vee q\vee y)\wedge(p\vee q\vee\neg y)$ with fresh $y$.  A residual unit
$(p)$ becomes the four clauses $(p\vee\sigma y\vee\tau w)$ over
$\sigma,\tau\in\{+,-\}$.  An empty residual clause becomes the eight clauses
containing every sign pattern on three fresh variables.  Tautologies are
deleted and duplicate literals are normalized.  The result is proper 3-CNF,
is equisatisfiable, and has linear length.  Materializing it proves (8).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No generic nonempty-query repair]
An unrestricted superpolynomial lower bound for the one-coordinate service
(5) already gives a superpolynomial lower bound for the NP-complete source
language.  Therefore nonemptiness, pin cardinality one, source connectivity,
and proper monotone syntax do not provide an easier intermediate lower-bound
arrow.  A data-structure theorem becomes distinct only after it restricts
representation, preprocessing, source access, query access, or amortization,
and then a separate all-decider compiler is required.
\end{corollary}

This does not prove that every restricted compiler is impossible.  It proves
that deleting the empty query alone does not evade the reversal in
MODELBRIDGE-0007.

\section{Consequences under $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$}

Assume only in this section that $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ and fix a uniform
exact 3-SAT decider running in time $N^{K+o(1)}$.  Equation (8) evaluates the
nonempty coordinate in $N^{K+o(1)}$ time after linear compilation.  The
unknown exponent $K$ remains.  Conversely, any favorable evaluator for (5)
is already an algorithm for the NP-complete source problem by (7).  Calling
that evaluator ``predecision extraction'' does not make its cost independent
of SAT.

If a proposed preprocessing/query tuple has empty-or-nonempty coordinate time
$N^{q+o(1)}$ after preprocessing $N^{p+o(1)}$, its sequential source decider
has exponent $\max\{p,q\}$, with peak space given by the charged maximum plus
any retained object and buffer.  A contradiction arises only if these same
resources enter a genuine unconditional endpoint such as the precise FLMV
random-access time--space region.  The present equivalence supplies no such
endpoint.

\section{Hostile controls and model audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{Affine, Horn, and 2-SAT.}
The lift can syntactically leave an easy class, but a recognizer can delete
the slack gadget and apply Gaussian elimination, propagation, or implication
graphs to the source.  Added syntax does not destroy a semantic shortcut.
\item[] \textbf{Alternative encodings.}
Variable renaming, complementation with the pin bit flipped, clause order,
COPY, DEAD tautologies, serialization/INTERP, and proper-3-CNF residual
conversion preserve the equality after charged decoding.
\item[] \textbf{DIRECT and RECOMP.}
A direct source decider may return one bit.  A recomputing evaluator rebuilds
the residual on demand and stores no service table.  The theorem charges the
actual chosen route and infers no internal materialization.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products.}
The pin gadget preserves conjunction of independent source components; it
does not force their tables or messages to be joined.
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output.}
Both sides request one bit.  No explicit-vector output lower bound is used.
\item[] \textbf{Balance limitation.}
The fresh slack variables have low occurrence and (3) is not in
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$.  Excluding such slack by an exact balanced promise is a
real restriction, not a refutation of the theorem.  It becomes the next
construction target and still needs a model-matched lower bound.
\end{description}

\section{Literature and cross-domain transfer audit}

D\"ocker's theorem supplies the exact NP-complete clean source and does not
supply a time lower bound.  FLMV supplies unconditional simultaneous
time--space exclusions only in its stated random-access model.  The
Chakraborty--Kamma--Larsen cell-probe tradeoff fixes a read-only matrix,
retained side bits, and charged probes; it does not force that layout on a
one-shot SAT decider.  Cadoli--Donini--Liberatore--Schaerf likewise formalize
a declared fixed/variable compilation split rather than extracting one from
every implementation.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Ordinary reduction composition transfers the one-bit source problem to the
nonempty pin coordinate exactly as in (6)--(8).
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Cell-probe, knowledge-compilation, VLSI locality, and memory-hierarchy bounds
suggest restricted service tuples after the exact compiler is fixed.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Physical connectivity of the two-clause gadget, many possible pins, or a
model-specific data-structure lower bound does not become unrestricted SAT
time without the same-execution representation compiler.
\end{description}

The current Chen--Hu--Ren work strengthens known algebrization barriers for
circuit lower bounds.  It provides no missing nonalgebrizing SAT interface;
instead it reinforces the requirement that the final load-bearing line be
identified explicitly.

\section{Barrier and post-quantum audit}

The pin-lift reductions relativize: replace the source/evaluator decider by
its oracle version and the same syntactic maps work.  They also algebrize,
because algebraic-oracle access only strengthens the evaluator.  Thus the
theorem is a guard, not a final hinge.  No large constructive circuit property
is used, so Natural Proofs is not directly invoked.

Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies, graded
Betti numbers, and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} at this
barrier: the coordinate evaluator need not instantiate their objects.
LWE-style black-box reduction architecture and special-soundness-style
collision extraction remain \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE}; they need an
unconditional model-preserving bridge.  Hash or random-oracle binding is
inadmissible.  The same structured gadget is algorithmically recognizable,
so its hidden structure is an easy bypass, not a hardness source.
