\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
This run attacks \textsf{SERVICE-UCORE1} and the global-binding repair left
after decoded causality and fixed-arity compatibility failed.  It proves an
exact information theorem.  On the canonical proper-three-CNF pin family
$\{P_n(a):a\in\{0,1\}^n\}$, every source-free summary from which one fixed
resolver recovers a projected satisfying assignment must take at least
$2^n$ distinct values; a fixed-length binary summary therefore has length at
least $n$.  The bound is tight: the $n$ witness bits themselves suffice and,
on this family, are recoverable by a uniform source scan in $O(N)$ time and
$O(\log N)$ writable space for $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

\textsf{BINDING-RANGE-TIGHT1} is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in the declared
source-free resolver model.  It \textbf{FALSIFIES/RETIRES} any generic
inference from global witness binding or its range size to superlinear, let
alone superpolynomial, SAT decision cost.  For arbitrary formulas, a total
binding summary of $n+1$ bits exists semantically and can be extracted from
any exact SAT decider by ordinary self-reduction, but the charged construction
uses at most $n+1$ current-scale calls and retains the decider's unknown
exponent $K$.  It also need not occur in the original one-bit execution.

The result does not falsify every source-specific service on the NP-complete
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ clean core.  A $K$-independent same-realization constructor,
a lower bound for its exact service, strict ordinary-CNF contraction, a
matched endpoint, nonrelativization and nonalgebrization remain
\textbf{OPEN}.  P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Source-free binding model}
\begin{definition}[Projected witness-binding summary]
Fix $n$ distinguished semantic variables.  A pair $(J,R)$ is a source-free
projected witness-binding scheme on a satisfiable formula family $\mathcal F$
when $J(F)$ is a binary summary and the resolver $R$ receives only $J(F)$,
not $F$, and returns an $n$-bit assignment lying in the projection of
$\operatorname{Sat}(F)$ onto the distinguished variables.
\end{definition}

Source-free recovery is stronger than merely retaining the source alongside
the summary.  It isolates exactly the global-gluing role proposed after
pairwise projections failed.  It says nothing yet about acquisition,
verification, internal exposure, decision time, or strict contraction.

For $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$, let $z_i$ be semantic variables and let $p_i,q_i$
be fresh auxiliaries.  Put $\ell_i=z_i$ if $a_i=1$ and
$\ell_i=\neg z_i$ otherwise, and define
\[
P_n(a)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^n\ \bigwedge_{(s,t)\in\{0,1\}^2}
 (\ell_i\vee p_i^{(s)}\vee q_i^{(t)}),                                  \tag{3}
\]
where $p^{(0)}=p,p^{(1)}=\neg p$, and similarly for $q$.
Each block is equivalent to $\ell_i$, so the projected solution set is the
singleton $\{a\}$.  There are $3n$ variables and $4n$ proper width-three
clauses.  Under the current indexed self-delimiting encoding,
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

\begin{theorem}[BINDING-RANGE-TIGHT1]
For every $n\ge1$ and every source-free projected witness-binding scheme
$(J,R)$ on $\mathcal P_n=\{P_n(a):a\in\{0,1\}^n\}$,
\[
 |\{J(P_n(a)):a\in\{0,1\}^n\}|\ge2^n.                                  \tag{4}
\]
If every summary has fixed binary length $b(n)$, then $b(n)\ge n$.
Both bounds are tight.  The tight summary is $J(P_n(a))=a$, and it is
computable from the canonical source in $O(N)$ time using $O(\log N)$
writable space and a write-only output stream.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Because the projected solution set of $P_n(a)$ is $\{a\}$, correctness gives
$R(J(P_n(a)))=a$.  If $a\ne a'$ but the summaries were equal, the fixed
resolver would have to return both $a$ and $a'$ on the same input, a
contradiction.  Thus $a\mapsto J(P_n(a))$ is injective, proving (4), and
$2^{b(n)}\ge2^n$ gives $b(n)\ge n$.

For tightness, set $J(P_n(a))=a$ and let $R$ be the identity.  A uniform
transducer scans the $n$ canonical four-clause blocks, validates their
literal pattern, and outputs the sign of the common semantic literal in each
block.  Counters and literal indices use $O(\log N)$ work bits; source
validation and $n$ output bits take $O(N)$ time.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Binding information is not a hardness measure]
There is no valid generic implication from the range lower bound (4), or
from exact global witness recovery, to superlinear time, large writable
space, communication, or an unrestricted SAT lower bound.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The stated transducer attains the optimal binding range using linear source
time and logarithmic writable space.  Moreover a decider for this family can
validate each block, discard its sign immediately, and output SAT without
retaining the $n$-bit summary.  Optional instrumentation may store it, while
a RECOMP implementation may recover requested bits by rescanning.  Hence
range, materialization and decision cost are distinct.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact positive boundary for arbitrary formulas}
For a formula $F$ on $n$ distinguished variables, define the total semantic
summary
\[
B(F)=
\begin{cases}
0\,0^n,&F\text{ is unsatisfiable},\\
1\,\operatorname{LexFirst}(\operatorname{Sat}(F)),&F\text{ is satisfiable}.
\end{cases}                                                               \tag{5}
\]
The lexicographic witness is taken after existentially projecting any
auxiliaries.  Equation (5) uses $n+1$ bits, identifies SAT status, and
distinguishes every two sources with disjoint projected solution sets.
Thus global semantic binding itself has a linear-size universal solution.

Given a total exact SAT decider $A$, standard prefix pinning computes (5)
with at most $n+1$ calls.  If each query has encoded length $L=\Theta(N)$ on
a sparse canonical family and $T_A(q)=q^{K+o(1)}$, the separately executed
ledger is
\[
T_B(N)=O\bigl(n(T_A(L)+L)\bigr)=N^{K+1+o(1)}.                             \tag{6}
\]
Batch sharing can improve (6) on structured families, as TESTS-0001 proves,
so (6) is an upper bound rather than a lower bound.  Either way, the generic
construction does not provide a $K$-independent exponent and does not show
that $A(F)$ internally exposes $B(F)$.

Under $\Pclass=\NPclass$, equation (5) is polynomial-time computable by this
self-reduction.  That consequence is consistent with the hypothesis.  A
hypothetical polynomial SAT solver can also recognize the pin family, return
SAT there without retaining $a$, and invoke its ordinary polynomial fallback
elsewhere.  Therefore global witness binding is neither contradictory under
$\Pclass=\NPclass$ nor an all-implementation internal necessity.

\section{Literature, barrier, and cross-domain audit}
Ordinary SAT self-reducibility supplies the external witness construction;
the current corpus already formalizes its exact pinning and encoding ledger.
Kawachi--Rossman--Watanabe and Bj\"orklund--Kaski--Kowalik study witness
finding in declared oracle interfaces.  Their query bounds transfer only
after an exact compiler to those interfaces; they do not make the calls work
performed by one arbitrary SAT execution.  Fortnow--Santhanam and Drucker
prove conditional barriers for multi-instance compression.  Those theorems
do not prohibit the one-instance summary (5), and their hierarchy-collapse
conclusions cannot contradict a proof branch that has already assumed
$\Pclass=\NPclass$.

The counting step $b\ge\log_2|\operatorname{im}J|$ is an exact
information-theoretic theorem transfer after the resolver model is fixed.
Its promotion to computational hardness is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}, as
the linear pin-family transducer demonstrates.  Parity cosets are an exact
coding-theoretic object transfer, but their global binding is exploitable by
Gaussian elimination.  VLSI locality, communication cuts, pebbling and
memory-hierarchy bounds remain \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to unrestricted
random-access SAT without a cost-preserving arbitrary-solver compiler.

The range theorem and external extractor relativize.  A common semantic
oracle can return the SAT and pinning bits, so Baker--Gill--Solovay classifies
them as route diagnostics rather than a separating hinge.  Algebraic access
only strengthens the affine hostile control; Aaronson--Wigderson supplies no
promotion.  No constructive large useful circuit property is asserted.

The post-quantum ledger gives the same conclusion.  Parity-code puncturing is
an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}; Schur powers, quadratic-relation
ranks, Hilbert functions and syzygies are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} because
no same-execution time bridge is present.  Collision entropy counts a fixed
map but cannot turn its image size into acquisition cost.  Special-soundness
binding remains an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; cryptographic or random-oracle
binding is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for an unconditional separation.
