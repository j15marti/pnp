\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
This run attacks the decoder-relative causal repair left open by RT-0004 and
LB-0006.  It proves an exact trilemma.  First, if the candidate functional may
be one bit, every nonconstant decider has the final output bit as a trivial
causal functional.  Second, every polynomial-time source functional can be
made decoder-relative causally indispensable by a uniform polynomial-time
guard compiler, without changing the accepted language.  Third, RT-0004's
DIRECT and RECOMP equality deciders omit the designated rich mismatch vector
that MATERIALIZE and the new guard use.  Thus causal use by itself is neither
a lower-bound resource nor an extensional invariant of the decision problem.

\textsf{CAUSAL-GUARD-INSERT1} below is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in the declared
checkpoint-intervention model.  The claim that decoded causal use alone
forces acquisition, time, space, communication, or strict source contraction
for every exact SAT solver is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.  This does not rule
out every source-specific functional on the NP-complete clean core.  A useful
repair must exclude the output bit and optional guards, survive DIRECT/RECOMP,
bind cost to the same execution, and yield a strict $N^\lambda$ successor for
one fixed $\lambda<1$ or another independent endpoint.  Those arrows and P
versus NP remain \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Exact decoded checkpoint model}
\begin{definition}[Decoder-relative intervention]
Let $q(x)\in\{0,1\}^{m(|x|)}$ be a total functional.  An implementation has a
declared checkpoint buffer $c=e(q(x))$, where $e$ is injective and has a
decoder $d$ satisfying $d(e(y))=y$.  A decoded intervention replaces $c$ by
$e(y)$ for some $y\ne q(x)$, keeps the read-only source fixed, and resumes the
same transition program.  The buffer is decoder-relative causally
indispensable when such an intervention changes the final output.
\end{definition}

This definition is deliberately stronger than raw-coordinate occurrence and
is invariant under the particular invertible encodings named in the model.
It is still implementation-relative: an execution with no such buffer offers
no checkpoint on which the intervention is defined.

\section{Causal-guard insertion theorem}
\begin{theorem}[CAUSAL-GUARD-INSERT1]
Let $A$ be a total deterministic decider for a language $L$, and let $q$ be a
total deterministic source functional.  Suppose $A$, $q$, $e$, and $d$ run in
time polynomial in $N=|x|$, $|q(x)|$ is polynomially bounded, and
$d(e(y))=y$.  There is a total uniform deterministic decider
$G_{A,q,e}$ for $L$ with polynomial time and space such that the declared
buffer $e(q(x))$ is decoder-relative causally indispensable on every input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On source $x$, compute $y=q(x)$, store $c=e(y)$, compute $b=A(x)$, and pause
at the declared checkpoint.  Then compute $y'=q(x)$ again, decode
$z=d(c)$, and output
\[
 G_{A,q,e}(x,c)=
 \begin{cases}
 b,&z=y',\\
 1-b,&z\ne y'.
 \end{cases}                                               \tag{2}
\]
On an ordinary execution $c=e(q(x))$, so $z=y'=q(x)$ and the output is
$A(x)$.  Under any decoded intervention $c\leftarrow e(y^*)$ with
$y^*\ne q(x)$, equation (2) outputs $1-A(x)$.  Hence the buffer is causally
indispensable.  The construction runs $A$ once and $q$ twice and performs
one encoding, decoding and comparison.  Polynomial bounds and uniformity are
preserved.
\end{proof}

The theorem is not a SAT lower bound.  It is a compiler for adding a
counterfactual dependency that is a semantic no-op on every legal execution.
Its cost is
\[
 T_G(N)=T_A(N)+2T_q(N)+T_e(N)+T_d(N)+O(m(N)),              \tag{3}
\]
with peak writable space bounded by the corresponding sum (or a smaller
reused-workspace implementation).  Equation (3) preserves ``polynomial'' but
does not remove an arbitrary exponent $K$.

\begin{corollary}[One-bit triviality]
If interventions are allowed immediately before emission, the final output
bit of every nonconstant one-bit decider is a decoded causal functional of
length one.  Therefore an existential statement of causal use without an
independent richness and cost condition cannot imply a superconstant resource.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[P=NP-compatible consequence]
Assume $\Pclass=\NPclass$ and choose the hypothesized polynomial SAT decider
$A$.  For every $q\in\mathsf{FP}$ and every polynomial invertible encoding,
$G_{A,q,e}$ is still a polynomial exact SAT decider.  Thus optional decoded
causal use creates no contradiction under the hypothesis.
\end{corollary}

\section{What is actually falsified}
RT-0004 supplies the omission side on a proper-three-CNF equality family.  A
DIRECT solver streams comparisons into one OR bit, while RECOMP regenerates
prefix information from the source; neither retains the length-$n$ mismatch
vector $q=u\oplus v$.  MATERIALIZE retains $q$ but need not consume it.
Applying CAUSAL-GUARD-INSERT1 makes the same $q$ indispensable.  All versions
have identical unmodified one-bit semantics.

Consequently the following generic implications fail:
\[
 \text{decoded causal use in one exact implementation}
 \Longrightarrow \text{semantic necessity},              \tag{4}
\]
\[
 \text{decoded causal use}
 \Longrightarrow \text{large time, space, or communication},              \tag{5}
\]
and
\[
 \exists Q\ \mathsf{Dcause}(Q;\tau_A(F))
 \Longrightarrow \text{nontrivial lower-bound object}.    \tag{6}
\]
Equation (4) is refuted by optional guard insertion, (5) by its polynomial
overhead and easy controls, and (6) by the output bit.  The theorem does not
prove that every possible $q$ can be omitted on the NP-complete
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ core.  A source-specific fixed-functional theorem with an
implementation-independent interface remains \textbf{OPEN}; it must add a
cost-preserving compiler for executions that never expose the object.

\section{Literature, barrier, and transfer audit}
Halpern and Pearl formalize counterfactual interventions through structural
models~\cite{hp}.  That semantics transfers exactly only after the program
variables, intervention point, and structural equations are fixed.  It does
not make causal attribution representation- or implementation-independent.
Classification: \textbf{THEOREM/OBJECT TRANSFER} for the declared
counterfactual model; \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} from causal attribution to an
unrestricted computation lower bound.  Program-dependence and database
provenance methods have the same missing bridge: they explain one chosen
implementation or query plan, not every extensionally equivalent SAT solver.

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas prove genuine SAT time--space lower
bounds in a declared random-access model~\cite{flmv}.  CAUSAL-GUARD-INSERT1
does not place $G$ inside any forbidden region and supplies no strict
$N^\lambda$ successor.  Importing fixed-DAG pebbling, VLSI locality,
communication cuts, or memory-hierarchy bounds remains a \textbf{FAILED
TRANSFER} without a cost-preserving arbitrary-solver compiler.

Baker--Gill--Solovay exhibit oracle worlds with opposite P-versus-NP
answers~\cite{bgs}.  The guard theorem relativizes with one fixed common
oracle: run $A^O$ and $q^O$ and retain the same comparison.  Aaronson--Wigderson
show that P versus NP requires nonalgebrizing techniques~\cite{aw}; algebraic
oracle access does not invalidate the optional guard or make it a hardness
resource.  No Natural-Proofs property is asserted.  A future broad
constructive, large and useful circuit property would require a separate
Razborov--Rudich audit~\cite{rr}.

The post-quantum ledger supplies no repair.  Schur squares, quadratic
relations, Hilbert functions, syzygies and collision entropy have no exact
object map to optional decoded causal guards and are \textbf{FAILED
TRANSFERS} here.  LWE-style behavioral extraction and special-soundness
binding remain \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}: they need a new theorem binding a
rich interface to every same execution without importing cryptographic
hardness or random-oracle assumptions.

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}
LB-0006's phrase ``decoder-relative logical influence: OPEN'' is correct as a
broad research area, but any narrower claim that influence itself certifies a
lower-bound resource is now overstated.  The surviving target must specify a
nontrivial interface, a minimum information/service content beyond the output
bit, and an independent lower bound preserved by a charged compiler.  A
counterfactual intervention on an illegal state is evidence about the chosen
program model, not automatically work performed on legal executions.
