\paragraph{Result and strict status.}
This run attacks the new white-box target
$\mathsf{WExt}(A,F,Q,\tau)$ from CC-0005 and the same-execution
\textsf{CORE-ACQUIRE1} target from MODELBRIDGE-0003.  On one explicit proper
three-CNF equality core, four uniform polynomial-time deciders have identical
one-bit semantics but different admissible exposure sets for the natural
mismatch vector $q=u\oplus v$.  Raw exposure distinguishes a materializing
implementation from an invertibly encoded interpreter; decoder-normalized
exposure survives that interpreter but fails on direct and recomputing
implementations; source extraction recovers $q$ for all four but is independent
of the measured execution.  These statements are \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for
the declared relations.

Therefore raw or decoded materialization is not a universal extraction
relation, while mere extensional derivability does not certify internal cost.
This does not prove that every possible intermediate white-box relation fails.
The surviving relation must prove a new solver-independent forced interface,
not choose one of these two endpoints.  \textsf{CORE-ACQUIRE1}, the
nonrelativizing and nonalgebrizing hinge, and P versus NP remain
\textbf{OPEN}.  The proofs are human-readable, not proof-assistant checked.

\section{Claim card and exact machine model}
The attacked candidate is
\[
 \forall A\ \forall F\quad
 \operatorname{CorrectSAT}(A)Longrightarrow
 \exists Q\;\mathsf{WExt}(A,F,Q,\tau_A(F)),                 \tag{1}
\]
where $Q$ is intended to carry a lower-bound-bearing compatibility object.
Machines are total uniform deterministic random-access machines with a
read-only canonical source and charged writable bits.  Time counts primitive
steps or source probes; space excludes the read-only source.  The
SAME\_REALIZATION objects are $A$, $F$, the actual execution $\tau_A(F)$,
$Q$, its decoder or verifier, and the resource charged to that execution.

Equation (1) is not useful without an independently proved transfer from the
admissible $Q$ to a lower bound on the same execution.  Its quantifier cannot
be weakened to ``for each implementation choose a representation for which a
restricted lower bound is known.''  Nor may a new external simulation be
charged retrospectively to $\tau_A(F)$.

\section{Explicit proper-three-CNF control family}
For $u,v\in\{0,1\}^n$, use the proper-three-CNF equality construction already
proved in CC-0005: impose $x_i\leftrightarrow y_i$, replace each binary clause
$(a\lor b)$ by
\[
 (a\lor b\lor z)\wedge(a\lor b\lor\neg z),
\]
and encode each pin literal $\ell$ by the four clauses
\[
 \bigwedge_{\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\}}
 (\ell\lor a^\alpha\lor b^\beta).
\]
Call the result $C_n(u,v)$.  It has $8n$ variables, $12n$ clauses, three
distinct variables in every clause, encoded length $M=\Theta(n\log n)$, and
\[
 C_n(u,v)\in\SAT\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u=v.
\]
The natural candidate object is the $n$-bit mismatch vector
$q(u,v)=u\oplus v$.

After one common $O(M)$ syntax-validation pass, consider four uniform
deciders.
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{DIRECT:} stream the coordinate comparisons into one OR bit;
time $O(n)$ and writable space $O(\log n)$.
\item[] \textbf{MATERIALIZE:} write the full vector $q$ and OR it; time
$O(n)$ and writable space $O(n)$.
\item[] \textbf{INTERP:} write only $e(q)=q\oplus1^n$ and test its decoded
bits; time $O(n)$ and writable space $O(n)$.  The decoder $e^{-1}=e$ is fixed
and uniform.
\item[] \textbf{RECOMP:} recompute successive prefix ORs directly from the
read-only source, retaining no length-$n$ vector; time $O(n^2)$ and writable
space $O(\log n)$.
\end{description}
All four return the same single bit $[u=v]$.

\section{Exact exposure separation}
\begin{definition}[Three candidate relations]
For the designated length-$n$ work buffer of a trace $\tau$, let
$\operatorname{Raw}(q,\tau)$ mean that the buffer equals $q$ in some
configuration.  Let $\operatorname{Dec}(q,\tau,d)$ mean that a declared
uniform decoder $d$ maps such a buffer to $q$.  Let
$\operatorname{Ext}(F,q)$ mean that one external uniform transducer computes
$q$ from the read-only source $F$, without claiming that the measured decider
did so.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[EXPOSURE-COVARIANCE-SEPARATION1]
For every $n\ge1$ and every source $C_n(u,v)$, the four deciders above have
the same answer, but their exposure relations satisfy
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &\operatorname{Raw}(q,\tau)&\operatorname{Dec}(q,\tau,d)&
   \operatorname{Ext}(F,q)\\ \hline
\text{DIRECT}&0&0&1\\
\text{MATERIALIZE}&1&1&1\\
\text{INTERP}&0&1&1\\
\text{RECOMP}&0&0&1.
\end{array}                                                   \tag{2}
\]
Here decoder-normalized exposure uses the identity decoder for MATERIALIZE
and $e^{-1}$ for INTERP.  Consequently neither raw nor decoded
materialization satisfies (1) on this family, while extensional extraction is
implementation-independent and cannot certify that an internal object was
stored or paid for.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
DIRECT and RECOMP retain only indices, one current comparison bit, and an OR
bit, so no designated $n$-bit mismatch buffer exists.  MATERIALIZE writes
exactly $q$.  INTERP writes $q\oplus1^n$, which differs from $q$ for every
$n\ge1$, and the fixed inverse decoder recovers $q$.  Finally, an external
scan of the two pinned source vectors computes $q$ in $O(M)$ time regardless
of which decider ran.  The answer in every case is one exactly when no
coordinate of $q$ is one.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[P=NP-compatible special-case guard]
If $\SAT\in\Pclass$, there is a total polynomial-time all-input SAT decider
that recognizes the canonical $C_n(u,v)$ syntax, runs DIRECT on those inputs,
and invokes any fixed polynomial SAT decider otherwise.  On the control
family it does not materialize $q$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The syntax and pin blocks are recognizable by one polynomial scan.  DIRECT
is exact on recognized inputs.  The fallback is exact everywhere else and is
polynomial under the hypothesis.
\end{proof}
This lemma prevents using the equality core itself as a contradiction under
$P=NP$.  It does not show that a genuinely NP-complete clean core is easy.

\section{A nontrivial clean-core target}
Let $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ be Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2): every clause has exactly
three distinct variables, every clause is all-positive or all-negative,
clauses are unique, and every variable occurs exactly twice positively and
twice negatively.  D\"ocker proves this restriction NP-complete
\cite{doecker}.  If $n$ is the variable count and $m$ the clause count, then
$3m=4n$, and the standard binary-index encoding has complete length
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

The honest all-algorithm lower-bound target is
\[
 \forall A\left[
   \bigl(\forall F\;A(F)=\operatorname{SAT}(F)\bigr)
   \Longrightarrow
   \forall k\in\mathbb N\ \exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:\
   T_A(F)>|F|^k
 \right].                                                   \tag{3}
\]
Because $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ is NP-complete, (3) is already separation-strength.
It is \textbf{OPEN}.  A WExt theorem advances (3) only if it supplies an
independent same-realization transfer whose lower-bound direction survives
all implementations.  Merely renaming (3) as ``the resource disjunct'' or
requiring a large $Q$ assumes the conclusion.

\section{MODELBRIDGE-0003 and the smallest repair}
MODELBRIDGE-0003 proves that an external transducer can recover one satisfying
assignment with at most $n+1$ pinned SAT calls.  That result is exactly on the
extensional side of (2): its extra calls constitute a new realization and do
not prove that the original one-bit execution contained the witness,
compatibility row, matrix, rank, OBDD, or residual DAG.

The smallest plausible repair is therefore not ``white-box'' alone.  A
future relation must state all of the following.
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Forced interface:} every exact implementation induces the
same declared query/function interface, not necessarily the same encoding.
\item[] \textbf{Covariant decoder:} efficient conjugacies such as INTERP may
change representation but preserve a uniformly decodable object.
\item[] \textbf{No-materialization alternative:} DIRECT/RECOMP executions
that never hold the object must enter a separately proved resource lower
bound, not one defined from their failure to materialize it.
\item[] \textbf{Charged verification:} extracting, decoding, verifying,
indexing, replaying, and storing the object must be charged to the same
realization or connected by a proved cost-preserving compiler.
\item[] \textbf{Independent endpoint:} the resulting interface must have a
model-matched lower bound strong enough to handle an arbitrary fixed
polynomial exponent.
\end{description}
This repaired \textsf{CORE-ACQUIRE1} remains \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Barrier, model-transfer, and post-quantum audit}
Baker, Gill, and Solovay give oracle worlds on both sides of P versus NP
\cite{bgs}.  The exposure separation survives a common oracle because the
four control algorithms need none; it identifies a relativizing obstruction
and is not the final hinge.  Aaronson and Wigderson show that P versus NP
requires nonalgebrizing techniques \cite{aw}.  The affine INTERP encoding is
itself an exact warning that algebraic access or a change of coordinates does
not force one raw state representation.

No Natural-Proofs property is asserted.  If a future forced interface is a
large constructive property of length-indexed SAT functions, largeness,
constructivity, usefulness, and cryptographic assumptions require a separate
Razborov--Rudich audit \cite{rr}.  Per-instance constant output circuits are
irrelevant: (3) quantifies over one uniform decider and a variable source
family.  Conversely, a general nonuniform circuit lower bound for SAT would
be stronger than the requested uniform separation and cannot be inserted as
a premise.

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas provide genuine time--space lower
bounds in a declared random-access model \cite{flmv}; they do not provide
the forced interface above.  Fixed-DAG pebbling, VLSI locality, communication
cuts, and memory-hierarchy arguments are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to
unrestricted SAT without a compiler preserving their interfaces.  The
correspondence is exact only after that compiler exists.

LWE-style behavioral extraction remains an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}: the
external calls of MODELBRIDGE-0003 recover a witness but do not bind the
original execution.  Special-soundness/collision binding remains an
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} until two decoded openings yield a forbidden
source witness.  Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions,
syzygies, and collision entropy have no model-matched object correspondence
to the exposure relation in this run and are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} here.

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}
The phrase ``bind $Q$ to the actual execution without replaying $\tau$'' is
still under-specified.  Binding can mean raw presence, decoded presence,
derivability, causal use, or a verified functional of the trace; these are not
equivalent.  Future reports should name which relation is used and how DIRECT,
INTERP, and RECOMP are handled.  The unpublished local LB-0005 draft should
not be cited as a verified GitHub artifact; CC-0005 is the verified source for
its substantive result.

\paragraph{Single next target.}
Define a causal-use relation rather than an occurrence relation: $Q$ must
support a declared multi-query interface consumed by the actual execution,
while DIRECT/RECOMP implementations must be mapped into the same interface by
a cost-preserving compiler.  Prove or falsify that compiler first on
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$, with external pinning, compact circuits, affine
elimination, interleaved OBDDs, padding, and matched oracle access.  Until
that theorem and an independent interface lower bound are proved, P versus NP
remains \textbf{OPEN}.
