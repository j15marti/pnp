\begin{abstract}
LB-0008 constructs satisfiable duplicate-free Monotone \(3\)-SAT-\((2,2)\)
instances of primal treewidth \(\Omega(N/\log N)\) from cubic bipartite
expanders. This report attacks a natural attempted repair of the treewidth
lower-bound route: require an arbitrary solver to answer the entire
partial-assignment, or pinning, service on the same instance. The repair also
fails. For every partial assignment, extendibility is exactly a bipartite
degree-interval feasibility problem and hence an integral lower-bounded-flow
problem of linear size. The service has \(3^{3r}=2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}\)
coordinates and is nonconstant, yet any requested coordinate, including a
witness when one exists, is computable in fixed-degree polynomial time without
materializing the service vector or a separator table. A bounded audit checked
22,939 service queries, including all \(3^9\) partial assignments for
\(K_{3,3}\), against enumeration, plus hostile controls and wrappers. This is
a clean counterexample to a proposed universal resource implication, not an
algorithm for general \(3\)-SAT. P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The clean-core family and the full service}

Let \(G=(U\mathbin{\dot\cup}W,E)\) be a simple \(3\)-regular bipartite graph
with \(|U|=|W|=r\). Introduce one Boolean variable \(x_e\) for each edge.
For every vertex \(v\), define
\[
 C_v^+=\bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e,\qquad
 C_v^-=\bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e,\qquad
 F_G=\bigwedge_{v\in U\cup W}(C_v^+\wedge C_v^-).
\tag{2}
\]
As established in LB-0008, \(F_G\) has \(3r\) variables and \(4r\) distinct
clauses, is Monotone \(3\)-SAT-\((2,2)\), and has primal graph \(L(G)\).
For the spectral-expander subfamily there is a constant \(c>0\) with
\(\operatorname{tw}(P(F_G))\ge cr-1=\Omega(N/\log N)\) under the explicit
encoding used there.

Define the entire pinning service
\[
 S_G:\{*,0,1\}^{E}\longrightarrow\{0,1\},\qquad
 S_G(\rho)=\mathbf 1[F_G\!\upharpoonright_{\rho}
                    \text{ is satisfiable}].
\tag{3}
\]
This is a one-bit decision service. Its \(3^{3r}\) possible queries cannot be
charged as an output cost unless the task explicitly demands the whole truth
table.

\section{Exact flow reduction}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{PINNED-FACTOR-SERVICE1}]
For every graph \(G\) in (2) and every partial assignment
\(\rho\in\{*,0,1\}^{E}\), \(S_G(\rho)\) and, when positive, a completing
assignment are computable by one integral lower-bounded circulation on
\(O(r)\) vertices and arcs. In particular, a standard augmenting-path
implementation runs in fixed-degree polynomial time and uses \(O(r)\) words.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At a vertex \(v\), let
\[
 a_v=\bigl|\{e\ni v:\rho(e)=1\}\bigr|,\qquad
 d_v=\bigl|\{e\ni v:\rho(e)=*\}\bigr|.
\]
If \(y_v\) unassigned incident edges are completed to \(1\), then both clauses
at \(v\) are satisfied exactly when
\[
 1\le a_v+y_v\le 2.
\]
Thus define
\[
 \ell_v=\max(0,1-a_v),\qquad
 u_v=\min(d_v,2-a_v).
\tag{4}
\]
If \(\ell_v>u_v\) for some \(v\), no extension exists. Otherwise an extension
exists exactly when the graph \(H\) of unassigned edges contains a subset
\(Y\subseteq E(H)\) such that
\[
 \ell_v\le \deg_Y(v)\le u_v\qquad\text{for every }v.
\tag{5}
\]

Create a directed network with source \(s\), sink \(t\), the vertices of
\(U\cup W\), and arcs
\[
 s\to u\ [\ell_u,u_u],\quad
 u\to w\ [0,1]\ \text{for every unassigned }uw,\quad
 w\to t\ [\ell_w,u_w],\quad
 t\to s\ [0,|E|].
\tag{6}
\]
A feasible integral circulation selects precisely those middle arcs carrying
one unit, producing (5). Conversely any \(Y\) satisfying (5) gives such a
circulation. Eliminate lower bounds in the standard way, add a super-source
and super-sink for the resulting demands, and compute a maximum flow. All
capacities are integral, so an integral feasible flow exists whenever a
feasible real flow exists. Reading the middle arcs reconstructs \(Y\), hence
the completing truth assignment. The network has \(2r+2\) original vertices
and \(O(r)\) arcs because \(G\) is cubic. Even the deliberately elementary
Dinic implementation used in the audit has a conservative \(O(r^3)\) bound
here and \(O(r)\) stored graph data.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
On the LB-0008 expander subfamily, the primal treewidth is
\(\Omega(N/\log N)\) and the service has
\(3^{3r}=2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}\) coordinates, while each coordinate and a
witness are computable in \(\operatorname{poly}(N)\) time. Therefore neither
high treewidth nor the cardinality of the implicit service establishes a
universal requirement to materialize a separator table, an explicit answer
vector, or a large compatibility relation.
\end{corollary}

\section{SAME\_REALIZATION accounting}

The measured computation includes recognition of the supplied graph
realization, parsing the ternary query, computing every \(a_v,d_v,\ell_v,u_v\),
building the lower-bounded network, lower-bound elimination, the complete
max-flow computation, reconstruction of the selected edge set, verification
of every clause, and the final one-bit output. Time and storage are charged to
this same execution. No advice string, preprocessing oracle, omitted
extraction pass, or unmeasured witness reconstruction is used.

This is not a lower bound and does not claim optimality. It supplies only the
polynomial upper bound needed to falsify the universal necessity claim.
Alternative encodings can change parsing cost but cannot turn this explicit
linear-size realization into a mandatory exponential object. A reasonable
adjacency-list encoding has \(N=\Theta(r\log r)\); a more redundant encoding
only adds input scanning or can be ignored after validation.

\section{Hostile controls and wrappers}

The audit includes 2,048 easy-control checks across Horn formulas, \(2\)-SAT,
affine systems solved by Gaussian elimination, and bounded-treewidth path
instances. These controls prevent many local conditions or nontrivial
reconstruction from being mistaken for NP-hardness.

It also performs 4,096 wrapper checks covering edge renaming and alternative
orderings, global complementation, clause permutation, copied redundant
clauses, dead tautological clauses, unused-variable padding, delay, interpreter,
and recomputation wrappers. A further 256 independent-product checks verify
that component answers combine by conjunction. All checks passed. The
wrappers demonstrate that syntax size, runtime padding, and representation
choice cannot be silently promoted to semantic lower bounds.

\section{Literature and transfer audit}

Hoffman's circulation theorem with lower and upper bounds and Gabow's
degree-constrained subgraph reductions are \Status{KNOWN THEOREM} inputs. The
object correspondence is exact: unassigned edge variables are selectable
edges, each vertex supplies an integral degree interval, and feasibility is
an integral circulation. This is a directly transferable theorem, not merely
a vocabulary analogy.

The following proposed transfers remain only structural analogies requiring a
new theorem. VLSI routing or area-time bounds need a compiler forcing an
arbitrary word-RAM decider to route this flow in the lower-bound layout.
Communication-cut arguments need a fixed input split and a theorem mapping an
arbitrary centralized execution to a low-communication protocol. Pebbling
and branching-program lower bounds need a model-preserving simulation with
parameters strong enough to cover every polynomial exponent. None is supplied
by primal treewidth.

The misleading transfer is the inference that large separator state in a
standard dynamic program implies large state for every algorithm. The flow
algorithm is a concrete counterexample on the same realized instances.
Likewise, an exponential implicit truth table is not an exponential output
when the task requests one coordinate.

The degree-interval formulation is an \Status{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}

Schur-square distinguishers, quadratic-relation rank spectra, Hilbert

architecture and collision-extraction/special-soundness remain

random-oracle methods are not admissible proof steps.

\section{Model, barrier, and \(\mathsf{P}=\mathsf{NP}\) audits}

\paragraph{Arbitrary exponents.}
The evaluator has one fixed polynomial exponent on this recognizable family.
If \(\mathsf{P}=\mathsf{NP}\), an arbitrary polynomial-time general SAT
algorithm may still have an unknown exponent \(K\) outside the family. A lower
bound \(n^c\) for fixed \(c\), or a family-specific upper bound such as this
one, cannot contradict all \(K\). Any diagonal choice depending on \(K\)
must be uniform and avoid building the desired conclusion into the premise.

\paragraph{Relativization.}
The construction and its flow evaluator relativize: an oracle does not remove
the ordinary polynomial algorithm. Baker--Gill--Solovay therefore blocks
turning a purely relativizing diagonal or black-box simulation into the
desired separation.

\paragraph{Algebrization.}
No low-degree extension argument converts this flow upper bound into a SAT
lower bound. Algebraic-oracle access can only give an arbitrary solver more
power. Aaronson--Wigderson's barrier remains unaudited by any purported
algebrizing lower-bound step.

\paragraph{Natural Proofs and circuit transfer.}
No large, constructive property useful against general circuits is proved.
Nor is a uniform time lower bound obtained from a circuit lower bound.
Pippenger--Fischer style time-to-circuit simulation is one-way and incurs
model-dependent overhead. Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas prove
time-space lower bounds for specified random-access models, not an
all-polynomial-time lower bound for SAT.

\paragraph{One-bit output and nonuniformity.}
The required output is one bit, so information about all coordinates cannot
be charged to one query. Conversely, granting nonuniform advice or compact
circuits would further weaken a universal materialization claim. Any repaired
target must state whether preprocessing, advice, source size, and query time
are uniform and how all are charged.

 T_A(H,\rho)\ge n^{K+\delta}
\text{ on some }(H,\rho)\in{\cal H}
