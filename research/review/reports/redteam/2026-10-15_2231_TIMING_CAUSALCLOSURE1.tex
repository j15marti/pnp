\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\begin{theorem}[Timing-invariance trilemma]
For any two alleged exact SAT outputs a,a' concerning formulae F,F', either (i) F=F' and a=a', or (ii) F differs from F', making the semantic input different, or (iii) F=F' and a differs from a', so at least one output is incorrect. Different time tags alone cannot cause case (iii) for two correct deciders.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Every fixed explicit formula has one Boolean SAT value.\end{proof}
Fix a polynomial-time SAT decider A with time bound C_A(m+2)^k. A bounded causal controller has at most R=(N+2)^a rounds, each with generated formula length M=(N+2)^b and generation cost G=(N+2)^c, retained history H(N) polynomial, and consumes E=(N+2)^d external bits disclosed only after earlier completed events. The controller can hash the full current source-machine state after every primitive transition, with hash time O((L+2)^e) on L explicit bits, e fixed. Set U=C_A(M+2)^k and Q=U+M+H+|A|+2.
\begin{theorem}[Single-machine observer-inclusive closure]
One uniform source-matched machine B_A generates a valid causal transcript with active time
\[
O_A\left(R(G+U+UQ^e+M)+E+|\mathrm{transcript}|\right)
\]
and retained space
\[
O_A(H+U+M+E+|\mathrm{transcript}|).
\]
Both are polynomial for the declared caps. No near-linear time or subpolynomial space is claimed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Simulate the fixed source A step by step, retaining its explicit configuration of length at most U+M+|A| and only actually disclosed external bits. Scan/hash the configuration and history at each of at most U steps for cost at most UQ^e. Charge each generated formula, history operation, external sample and written transcript, then sum across R rounds. The machine itself performs all these operations. Physical delays are unbounded unless separately constrained; simulation need not reproduce a different machine's historical physical timestamps.\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Digest-only future index limitation]
For any h<m and any fixed H mapping m-bit states to h-bit strings, two different states share a digest and differ on some bit j. A future continuation requesting bit j cannot be answered exactly from digest and j alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}By pigeonhole there are colliding states. The later coordinate request distinguishes them. Full source replay or retaining the state can repair this, with charged cost. This is the project's previously known hash-quotient limitation applied to a timed interface.\end{proof}
Under P=NP the ordinary SAT projection can be obtained by running A(F) directly, without producing every tag. The source-observing argument relativizes to oracle worlds with P^O=NP^O; no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing SAT contradiction is established. Self-reference still needs a same-formula fully charged compiler and complete absolute clock for A on its own formula, as SELFBOUND1 and LEAD-0026 already require.
\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
Four independent batches examined all 256 proper three-variable, distinct-clause subset formulae under 0, 25, 100 or 225 source no-ops and four-step causal transcript generation. Across 4,096 complete executions, 37,888 assertions and 3,072 comparisons on unchanged initial CNFs, there were zero changes to the original SAT answer, 1,501 changes to the successor formula and its SAT value, and 1,548 invalid substitutions of a successor answer for the original answer. A deterministic SHA-256-indexed test tape supplies reproducible samples; no cryptographic or random-oracle assumption is made. Four independent state-fiber batches found digest-equal, future-index-distinguishable witnesses at parameters (m,h)=(10,4),(12,6),(14,8),(16,10), producing 24 more checks. Total 37,912 assertions, zero discrepancies. All eight scripts ran in under 30 seconds each.
