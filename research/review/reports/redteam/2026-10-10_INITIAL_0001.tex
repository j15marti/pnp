\section*{A concrete finite-field correction}
MAIN58501 correctly uses evaluation surjectivity, but its statement that
there can be no nonzero formal field-polynomial relation is too broad:
$X_1^q-X_1$ is a nonzero polynomial vanishing throughout $\mathbb F_q^m$.
The conclusion must concern nonzero polynomial functions, or representatives
of individual degree below $q$.

\begin{lemma}[Repaired interpolation statement]
For distinct points $P_1,\ldots,P_m\in\mathbb F_q^d$ and arbitrary
$a_1,\ldots,a_m\in\mathbb F_q$, there is a polynomial of total degree
at most $m-1$ taking value $a_i$ at each $P_i$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each ordered pair $i\ne j$, choose a coordinate $k(i,j)$ in which
$P_i$ and $P_j$ differ. Define
\[
 L_i(X)=\prod_{j\ne i}
 \frac{X_{k(i,j)}-(P_j)_{k(i,j)}}
 {(P_i)_{k(i,j)}-(P_j)_{k(i,j)}}.
\]
All denominators are nonzero, $L_i(P_i)=1$, and $L_i(P_j)=0$ for $j\ne i$.
Then $\sum_i a_iL_i$ has the asserted degree and values.
\end{proof}
No common separating linear functional or extra field-size assumption is
needed. The source's shared-functional proof needs an explicit condition,
for example $q>\binom m2$, if retained. The repaired surjectivity conclusion
still rules out a nonzero Boolean multilinear function vanishing on the
full Boolean cube; that downstream conclusion survives.

\section*{Reproduced attachment failures}
The six- and eight-character functions are 64-bit FNV-1a reduced modulo
$62^6$ and $62^8$, respectively. Their range bounds are
$56{,}800{,}235{,}584$ and $218{,}340{,}105{,}584{,}896$.
COST\_CERTIFICATES found a collision, and this reviewer independently
executed the actual extracted six-character function on both inputs:
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
pnp:4af52079e1a2cf84d4461513ff3e9756\\
pnp:b7bf65d9c5ec956423bc6173a00eb836
\end{quote}
Both return \texttt{kUWA9k}. This refutes injectivity, not SAT hardness.
A hypothetical answer-preserving digest could merge same-answer inputs;
collisions alone do not prove mixed SAT/UNSAT fibers.

For cleaned seed length $s>0$, the generator selects index $s+7i$ at step
$i=0,\ldots,6$. It never wraps because $8i<50$; the previous $i$ removals
make it select original random position $8i$. Thus every nonempty cleaned
seed is completely ignored for a fixed random tape. This algebraic defect
was independently checked. Original attachments were not edited.
The computational evidence and source digests are in
\texttt{attachment\_audit\_results.json} from COST\_CERTIFICATES.

\section*{Barrier and corpus reconciliation}
Baker--Gill--Solovay supplies equality and separation oracles \cite{bgs}.
The new matched-oracle immunity theorem therefore locates a failure needed
by any universally relativizing verifier construction. It does not rule out
the ordinary verifier at the empty oracle.
Algebrization concerns extended oracle access rather than notation;
Boolean-only syntax is no certificate of avoidance \cite{aw}.
Natural Proofs is conditional on its hardness assumption and formal
constructivity/largeness conditions, not a universal ban on invariants
\cite{rr}. Proof-system lower bounds require their own arbitrary-algorithm
bridge; the relevant short-proof characterization concerns NP versus coNP
\cite{cr}.
