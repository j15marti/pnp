\begin{abstract}
TESTS-0007 left ``nonpaired'' syntax as the smallest proposed repair after
connectedness failed to convert semantic compatibility rank into work forced
on every SAT decider.  This report falsifies that repair.  Starting from the
connected cubic-bipartite 2-switch chain, exchange two internal variables
between two negative clauses in every block, while leaving all positive
clauses fixed.  The operation preserves proper unique Monotone
3-Sat-$(2,2)$, connectedness, and a perfect-matching satisfying assignment,
but the positive and negative clause-triple sets are no longer equal.  After
fixing the cross-edge variables to zero, the compatibility matrix contains a
Kronecker-product submatrix with end ranks $13,13$ and interior rank $7$.
Thus its rank is at least
$13^2 7^{k-2}=2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$ over both $\mathbb F_2$ and $\mathbb R$,
yet a canonical recognizer returns SAT in linear input time on the family.
The bounded audit passed 66,576 checks.  The missing arbitrary-algorithm
transfer is unchanged; P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Construction}

For $k\ge2$, start with TESTS-0007's graph $G_k$: $k$ disjoint copies of
$K_{3,3}$ are joined in a chain by degree-preserving bipartite 2-switches.
For adjacent blocks $i,i+1$, remove edges $(0,0)$ in block $i$ and $(1,1)$
in block $i+1$, then insert the two crossed edges.  The resulting graph is
simple, connected, cubic and bipartite, with $6k$ vertices and $9k$ edges.

Let $x_e$ be the variable associated with edge $e$.  Initially place, for
each graph vertex $v$, the positive and negative clauses
\[
 P_v=\bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e,
 \qquad
 Q_v=\bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e.
\]
Within every block, let $a=x_{(0,2)}$ and $b=x_{(2,0)}$.  Modify only the
two negative clauses indexed by left vertices $0$ and $2$: exchange the
occurrence of $a$ in $Q_{L_0}$ with the occurrence of $b$ in $Q_{L_2}$.
Call the resulting formula $F_k^{\rm np}$.

This is an elementary two-incidence switch.  Every affected clause still
contains three distinct variables.  Each of $a,b$ still occurs in two
negative clauses, and every other occurrence is untouched; hence every
variable occurs exactly twice with each sign.  The modified negative triples
are unique and the negative-triple set differs from the positive-triple set,
so the family is genuinely nonpaired.  Positive clauses alone retain the
connected incidence structure.  Therefore
\[
 F_k^{\rm np}\in\Htwo,qquad
 n_k=9k,qquad m_k=12k,qquad
 N_k=\Theta(k\log k).
\]

\section{Exact rank and shortcut theorem}

Put every cross-edge variable in $X$.  After those variables are fixed to
zero, choose block-local splits as follows.  For the left end use
\[
 X_L=\{(0,2),(1,2),(2,0),(2,1)\};
\]
for every interior block use
\[
 X_I=\{(1,2),(2,1),(2,2)\};
\]
and for the right end use
\[
 X_R=\{(1,2),(2,0),(2,1),(2,2)\}.
\]
All other surviving internal variables are placed in $Y$.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{NONPAIRED-H22-COMPAT-RANK-GUARD1}]
For every $k\ge2$, $F_k^{\rm np}$ is a connected nonpaired member of
$\Htwo$ and
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}H_{F_k^{\rm np};X,Y}
 \ge 13^2 7^{k-2}.
\]
The same lower bound holds over $\mathbb R$.  Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{rank}H_{F_k^{\rm np};X,Y}
 =2^{\Theta(N_k/\log N_k)}.
\]
Every $F_k^{\rm np}$ is satisfiable.  One uniform total exact 3-SAT decider
recognizes and accepts this family in $O(N_k)$ time and $O(\log N_k)$
writable workspace, with any total exact fallback on other inputs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix all $2(k-1)$ cross-edge variables to zero.  Every remaining variable is
internal to one block, while every clause is also block-local.  Hence the
restricted compatibility matrix is the Kronecker product of two end matrices
and $k-2$ copies of one interior matrix.  Exact $\mathbb F_2$ elimination on
the displayed splits gives ranks $13,7,13$.  Matrix rank is multiplicative
under Kronecker product and monotone under taking submatrices, proving the
bound.  Each nonzero binary minor has an odd integer determinant and is
therefore nonsingular over $\mathbb R$ too.  Since rank is at most $2^{9k}$,
the encoded-length asymptotic follows.

Every cubic bipartite graph has a perfect matching by Hall's theorem.  Set
matching-edge variables to one and all others to zero.  Each positive vertex
clause contains exactly one one.  Before the negative switch, each negative
clause contains exactly two true negative literals.  In a switched clause,
the number of underlying one-valued variables changes from one by at most
one, and thus is at most two; the negative clause remains true.  Therefore
the same matching assignment satisfies $F_k^{\rm np}$.

A scanner can compare the source against the canonical switched-chain
template using logarithmic counters.  On a match it emits one; otherwise it
calls a fixed total exact fallback.  This is one uniform exact decider with
linear on-family time.  If SAT lies in P, using a polynomial fallback gives
a polynomial decider on every input without changing the shortcut.
\end{proof}

\section{SAME\_REALIZATION and hostile-control audit}

The matrix task receives an assignment pair $(a,b)$ and computes
$F_k^{\rm np}(a,b)$.  The SAT execution receives the formula and computes
$\bigvee_{a,b}F_k^{\rm np}(a,b)$.  These are different inputs and outputs.
The rank theorem transfers exactly to deterministic communication for the
first task, but not to central decision time, memory, extraction, or state
exposure for the second.  A one-bit output is especially hostile: every
family member has the same answer.

The negative-clause switch also shows why syntactic ``nonpairedness'' is not
representation-covariant.  It is a constant local edit preserving degree,
connectedness, encoded scale and the same matching witness.  Clause order,
variable renaming, complementation with corresponding sign changes, padding,
duplicates outside the strict core, or auxiliary encodings do not create an
all-algorithm obligation.

DIRECT returns after template recognition.  MATERIALIZE may build the
matrix; INTERP may encode it differently; RECOMP may recover requested
entries from the source; DEAD/COPY/DELAY may add or omit arbitrary traces.
Extensionally equivalent wrappers therefore continue to separate semantic
structure from required execution state.  Horn, dual-Horn, 2SAT,
affine/Tseitin Gaussian elimination, bounded-treewidth dynamic programming,
independent products, padding and alternate encodings remain mandatory easy
controls.  They are inherited from the verified regression suite and were
retained as explicit current-run control classes; no runtime conclusion is
drawn from their semantic ranks.

\section{Literature and barrier-transfer audit}

D\"ocker proves the ambient proper unique Monotone 3-Sat-$(2,2)$ language
NP-complete and identifies the paired satisfiable special case
\cite{doecker}.  The present construction is outside that paired subclass;
its satisfiability is proved directly.  Degree-preserving switches are a
standard exact operation in fixed-degree graph/hypergraph realization and
sampling \cite{hubai}; that literature transfers only the occurrence ledger,
not SAT hardness.  The log-rank theorem transfers exactly to the declared
two-party assignment-split function \cite{mehlhorn}.  Its solver-to-protocol
transfer is missing.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Two-incidence switching preserves the declared occurrence counts; tensor
rank multiplies; Hall's theorem supplies a perfect matching; log-rank applies
to the assignment-split communication matrix.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
VLSI routing congestion, distributed cuts, control-theoretic observability,
and LWE-style external black-box extraction suggest ways to expose a jointly
used resource.  None provides a compiler from an arbitrary static SAT
decider to that restricted interface.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Graph connectedness, paired/nonpaired syntax, semantic rank, Schur-square or
quadratic-relation language, Hilbert functions, syzygies, collision entropy,
and physical routing do not lower-bound the formula-input one-bit execution
without an exact object map and same-model compiler.  Cryptographic binding
and random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible.
\end{description}

The counterexample relativizes: oracle access can be forwarded to the
off-family fallback.  Low-degree/algebraic access can be forwarded likewise.
Thus it proves no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing hinge.  Recent
algebrization-barrier work reinforces that crossing relativization alone
would still be insufficient \cite{chen}.  Natural Proofs is not the immediate
hinge because no general-circuit lower bound or constructive largeness
property is claimed.  Nonuniformity also does not help the positive route:
the displayed uniform recognizer already defeats the pointwise necessity.
