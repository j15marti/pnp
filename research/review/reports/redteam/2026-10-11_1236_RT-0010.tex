\begin{abstract}
TESTS-0006 leaves a ``truth-independent, non-Shannon structural successor''
as the proposed repair of decide-then-emit.  This report separates three
meanings of truth independence.  Fixed program text is automatic for every
uniform algorithm.  A fixed operation or address schedule is also too weak:
Pippenger--Fischer oblivious simulation followed by a branch-free multiplexer
first decides the source and then emits one of two constant proper 3-CNFs in
$O(T\log T)$ time.  It retains the unknown solver exponent $K$.  At the other
extreme, semantic noninterference of the complete terminal view is too strong:
if a yes source and a no source have the same closed view, deterministic
reconstruction gives the same answer.  Therefore the phrase
``truth-independent'' supplies no intermediate resource theorem.  A viable
repair must positively specify one source transform and prove its fixed
$K$-independent full ledger; it cannot rely on syntactic absence of a decision
branch.  A bounded hostile audit passed $11{,}445$ checks.  P versus NP
remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim card, clean core, and quantifiers}

The clean core remains duplicate-free Monotone $3$SAT-$(2,2)$, denoted
$\Htwo$, under the canonical indexed encoding.  Every variable occurs twice
positively and twice negatively, $m=4n/3$, and encoded bit length is
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  Its NP-completeness is a source fact, not a time lower
bound \cite{doecker}.

The all-algorithm target is still
\[
 \forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\Htwo:\quad
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0
 \ \land\ T_A(F)>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,             \tag{1}
\]
where $A$ is a total correct uniform deterministic random-access SAT decider
and all source access, construction, output, reconstruction, and workspace are
charged.  Statement (1) is \OPEN.

The claim attacked in this run has prefix
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \forall D\,[D\text{ decides }L\text{ in time }T(N)]\quad
 \exists C_D\,[&C_D\text{ has a truth-independent schedule}\notag\\
                &\text{and emits a constant exact successor}].
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{2}
\end{equation}
The SAME\_REALIZATION contains $D$ or its simulation, the decision bit,
terminal-child construction, emitted bytes, reconstruction, final output,
and peak storage.  The weakest material advance is to show that (2) retains
$T$, so schedule language does not produce a $K$-independent constructor.

\section{Three definitions of truth independence}

\begin{definition}[Code independence]
One fixed finite program is used on every input of a given length.  This is
already part of uniform deterministic computation and excludes nothing.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Schedule obliviousness]
In the sequential-tape model of Pippenger--Fischer, head positions at each
time are functions of time and input length, not of the input contents.  Tape
symbols and the final output may still depend on the input \cite{pf}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Closed-view noninterference]
A terminal compiler has complete view $V_C(x)$ consisting of every emitted
child bit, accessor, combiner, and reconstruction datum available after
acquisition.  It is semantically noninterfering across a pair $x,x'$ when
$V_C(x)=V_C(x')$.  No later source read or SAT channel is permitted in this
terminal definition.
\end{definition}

The first definition is vacuous.  The next two yield the promised dichotomy.

\section{Exact branch-free countermodel}

Fix equal-length proper width-three CNFs $Y$ and $U$.  Formula $Y$ repeats the
clause $(z_1\vee z_2\vee z_3)$ eight times.  Formula $U$ contains all eight
sign patterns on $z_1,z_2,z_3$.  Hence $Y$ is satisfiable and $U$ is
unsatisfiable.  Clause repetition is irrelevant to semantics and both fixed
encodings can be padded to one constant length.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{OBLIVIOUS-DECIDE-EMIT1}]
Let $D$ decide a language $L$ on the one-dimensional-tape model in time
$T(N)\ge N$.  There is a uniform schedule-oblivious terminal compiler $C_D$
that emits $Y$ exactly on $x\in L$ and emits $U$ otherwise in
\[
 O(T(N)\log T(N))+O(1)                                  \tag{3}
\]
time.  The emission scan is branch-free.  Consequently, if
$T(N)=N^{K+o(1)}$, then (3) is $N^{K+o(1)}$ and retains $K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Pippenger and Fischer prove that a machine with one-dimensional tapes can be
simulated online by an oblivious machine with two one-dimensional tapes in
$O(T\log T)$ time, where head positions are input-independent \cite{pf}.
Run that simulation and let $b\in\{0,1\}$ be its answer.  Write the fixed
equal-length encodings $y,u$ coordinatewise using
\[
 c_i=u_i\oplus\bigl(b\wedge(u_i\oplus y_i)\bigr).        \tag{4}
\]
The same Boolean operations and output positions are visited for both values
of $b$.  Equation (4) writes $Y$ when $b=1$ and $U$ when $b=0$.  The fixed
scan adds constant work because $Y,U$ have constant length.  Multiplication
by a logarithm changes only the $o(1)$ term of a polynomial exponent.
\end{proof}

The theorem is deliberately model-scoped.  It does not assert a
space-preserving FLMV random-access simulation.  It suffices to refute an
unqualified claim that input-independent schedules exclude deciding first.
In an instruction-oblivious RAM reading, ordinary predication already gives
the same conclusion; address-obliviousness requires the declared simulation
theorem and its overhead.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{TERMINAL-NONINTERFERENCE1}]
Let $C$ be a closed terminal compiler for a nonconstant language $L$, and let
$R$ be a deterministic reconstruction function of the complete view.  If
there are $x\in L$ and $x'\notin L$ with $V_C(x)=V_C(x')$, then $R$ is
incorrect on at least one of $x,x'$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Determinism gives
$R(V_C(x))=R(V_C(x'))$, whereas exactness requires the two values to be one
and zero.  This is impossible.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Truth-independence dichotomy]
If truth independence constrains program text or schedule but permits
data-dependent values, \texttt{OBLIVIOUS-DECIDE-EMIT1} passes it and inherits
$K$.  If it requires noninterference of the complete closed view,
\texttt{TERMINAL-NONINTERFERENCE1} makes exact terminal reconstruction
impossible.  Therefore truth independence alone supplies no intermediate
lower-bound bridge.
\end{corollary}

For nonterminal identities, a later child-SAT channel may carry the missing
bit.  Its construction, query length, count, evaluation, and recursion then
belong in the same ledger.  Calling the earlier phase noninterfering does not
charge that later channel or remove $K$.

\section{P equals NP and arbitrary-exponent audit}

Assume $\SAT\in\mathsf P$ and choose a fixed decider $D$ with arbitrary
polynomial exponent $K$.  The theorem produces a uniform oblivious,
branch-free, non-Shannon, source-only, one-child successor of scale
$\lambda=0$ on every source, including $\Htwo$.  Its construction time is
$N^{K+o(1)}$.  This is fully compatible with $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ and
does not enter a fixed unconditional forbidden region.  No premise assumes
$\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$.

Inlining, predication, arithmetic selection, lookup tables, and circuit
unrolling also show why a syntactic ban on a named SAT call or an explicit
\texttt{if} instruction is not representation-covariant.  A total analyzer
that instead recognizes a nontrivial semantic program property encounters
the same undecidability boundary already isolated by MODELBRIDGE-0010 and
Rice's theorem \cite{rice}.  This run does not relabel that prior theorem as
new.

\section{Literature, barriers, and cross-domain transfer}

Pippenger--Fischer is a direct warning that oblivious schedule is not
semantic ignorance: arbitrary sequential-tape computation has an oblivious
online simulation with logarithmic time overhead \cite{pf}.  Goguen--Meseguer
noninterference formalizes an output-invariance condition, not a runtime lower
bound \cite{gm}.  The terminal theorem above uses only its elementary
functional core.  FLMV applies only after one total evaluator inhabits its
specified random-access model with simultaneous charged resources
\cite{flmv}; equation (3) supplies no such favorable landing.

The endpoint composition relativizes: for one fixed oracle $O$, run $D^O$
and multiplex its answer.  We do not claim that Pippenger--Fischer preserves
a unit-cost random-access oracle interface or makes oracle-query addresses
oblivious; that would require a separate model conversion.  A schedule
definition that merely refuses oracle operations does not thereby become a
nonrelativizing hinge.  Algebraic-oracle access can likewise forward the
oracle-produced decision bit.  Thus the composition identifies, not crosses,
the Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson barriers \cite{bgs,aw}.  No
broad constructive property of general circuits is used;
Natural Proofs remains a future trigger rather than a theorem establishing or
refuting this composition \cite{rr}.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Pippenger--Fischer transfers exactly in its sequential-tape model and proves
schedule obliviousness with $O(T\log T)$ time.  FLMV transfers only after a
separate exact model conversion with all space and access costs.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Security noninterference, constant-time programming, ORAM, circuit
predication, and control-theoretic unobservability clarify possible
definitions.  None supplies a universal SAT lower bound without a
model-preserving compiler.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
VLSI locality, branching-program/OBDD size, pebbling, fixed memory access,
and absence of explicit control branches do not lower-bound arbitrary
uniform SAT computation.
\end{description}

For the post-quantum ledger, the map from bounded sources to terminal views
has image exactly $\{Y,U\}$.  Its collision count is
\[
 \operatorname{Coll}(C)=|L\cap A|^2+|A\setminus L|^2,
 \qquad |\operatorname{im}C|\ge |A|^2/\operatorname{Coll}(C). \tag{5}
\]
Equation (5) is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER} of the elementary
collision-entropy identity, but it yields no acquisition lower bound; the
algorithm deliberately exploits the two-class quotient.  Schur squares,
quadratic relations, Hilbert functions, syzygies, and special-soundness
binding are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for this terminal target absent a new
source-structural theorem.  LWE-style black-box extraction remains
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE}; cryptographic hardness and random-oracle
assumptions are inadmissible.

\section{Smallest repair and handoff}

Replace the negative slogan by
\texttt{FIXED-LEDGER-STRUCTURAL-SUCCESSOR1}: exhibit one concrete uniform
source transform $S$ and prove, without inspecting arbitrary solver code,
\[
 \SAT(F)=\mathcal B_F(\SAT(G_1),\ldots,\SAT(G_q)),        \tag{6}
\]
where $S$ constructs the combiner and locally addressable ordinary-CNF
children in fixed $N^{r+o(1)}$ time and $N^{\sigma+o(1)}$ space independent
of the unknown solver exponent $K$; every child has length
$N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ for one $\lambda<1$; $q\le N^{\eta+o(1)}$; the contract is
hereditary; and acquisition, failed routes, access, serialization, replay,
reconstruction, ancestor state, final output, and peak workspace close in one
model-matched forbidden region.  The proof must establish the positive bound,
not ask an undecidable syntactic or semantic checker to certify that $S$
``did not decide SAT.''

Then attack the actual transform with Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin,
bounded-treewidth, independent products, padding, alternate encodings,
DIRECT, INTERP, RECOMP, semantic oracles, and algebraic oracles.  Equation
(6), its hereditary fixed ledger, the FLMV model conversion, a
nonrelativizing and nonalgebrizing hinge, UCORE-TIME1, and P versus NP remain
\OPEN.
