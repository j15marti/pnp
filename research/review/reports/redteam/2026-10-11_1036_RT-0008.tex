\begin{abstract}
Independent review accepts TESTS-0004's connected two-pin theorem after
making its evaluator-correctness premise explicit.  The proposed next repair,
however, asks only for a service not recoverable from a constant number of
ordinary SAT restriction decisions.  That condition is too weak.  If a
uniform controller performs local work $N^{c+o(1)}$, makes at most
$N^{b+o(1)}$ adaptive SAT calls of length at most $N^{a+o(1)}$, and a fixed
SAT decider runs in time $M^{K+o(1)}$, direct substitution computes the full
service in
\[
 N^{\max\{c,b+aK\}+o(1)}.
\]
This is the direct-route theorem already present in the verified corpus,
applied here to the new service target.  Parity supplies a sharp hostile
control: its exact black-box decision-query complexity is $q$, but it has a
width-two ordered branching program of size $2q-1$ and polynomial sequential
evaluation.  Thus superconstant SAT-query complexity, even for a nonmonotone
one-bit service, does not remove the arbitrary polynomial exponent $K$ and
does not yield a universal lower bound.  A bounded audit passed $243{,}622$
checks.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim card and exact quantifiers}

\paragraph{Target before review.}
TESTS-0004 proposed a fixed source-dependent output not computable from
$O(1)$ ordinary restriction-decision calls, followed by a $K$-independent
compiler from every polynomial SAT decider into that service.

\paragraph{Attacked implication.}
The falsification target is the premise that the first condition supplies a
meaningful computational resource:
\[
 Q_{\SAT}(S_N)=\omega(1)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \text{$S_N$ forces superpolynomial work in arbitrary computation}.       \tag{1}
\]
Here $Q_{\SAT}$ is a declared black-box decision-query measure.  No such
implication follows.

\paragraph{Model and SAME\_REALIZATION.}
Fix a uniform deterministic random-access controller $G$ on an explicit
source $x$ of encoded length $N$.  Its local work includes source reads,
query construction and writing, adaptivity, aggregation, output, and all
wrapper overhead.  Every oracle query is an explicitly encoded SAT instance.
The substituting decider $A$ is one fixed total uniform deterministic exact
SAT decider.  SAME\_REALIZATION consists of $x,G,A$, every query and answer,
all live buffers, the final service bit, and peak workspace.  No solver trace,
compatibility table, or service vector is assumed to exist.

\section{Independent review of TESTS-0004}

Let $F\in\Htwo$ and let $F_1,\ldots,F_t$ be its incidence components.  Every
occurrence of a variable lies in one component, so each $F_i$ inherits exact
two-positive/two-negative occurrence balance, proper unique monotone clauses,
and connected incidence.  For arbitrary $z_i\in V(F_i)$,
\[
 \SAT(F)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^t
 \left(\SAT(F_i\upharpoonright_{z_i=0})\vee
       \SAT(F_i\upharpoonright_{z_i=1})\right).            \tag{2}
\]
For a correct service evaluator $E$ with $T_E(L)\le CL^K$, $K\ge1$,
\[
 T(N)\le O(N)+2C\sum_iL_i^K
 \le O(N)+2C\left(\sum_iL_i\right)^K
 =O(N^K).                                                  \tag{3}
\]
The aggregate component encoding length is $O(N)$ when headers and identifiers
are charged.  A sequential implementation has conservative space
$O(N)+\max_iS_E(L_i)$.  The reverse pin-to-proper-3CNF compiler is inherited
from LB-0010.

The displayed quantifier prefix in TESTS-0004 wrote ``for all $A$'' while
its prose required $A$ to be correct.  The exact repaired prefix is
\[
 \forall F\in\Htwo\ \forall(F_1,\ldots,F_t)\ \forall(z_1,\ldots,z_t)\
 \forall E\,
 \left[
   \forall(G,z,v)\in\mathcal D\quad
   E(G,z,v)=\mathbf1[G\upharpoonright_{z=v}\in\SAT]
 \right]
 \Longrightarrow (2),                                    \tag{4}
\]
where $\mathcal D$ is the legal connected-service domain.  This is a premise
clarification, not a mathematical weakening.  The theorem and its retirement
of connectivity as a service repair are independently accepted as \EXACT.

\section{Polynomial-query closure}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{POLYQUERY-SERVICE-CLOSURE1}]
Suppose $G$ on length-$N$ sources performs at most $N^{c+o(1)}$ charged local
work and makes at most $N^{b+o(1)}$ adaptive SAT queries.  Every query has
encoded length at most $N^{a+o(1)}$.  Fix a total deterministic exact SAT
decider $A$ satisfying
\[
 T_A(M)\le M^{K+o(1)}
\]
for one constant $K\ge1$.  Replacing every oracle call of $G$ by $A$ yields
a uniform deterministic service evaluator with
\[
 \boxed{T_{G[A]}(N)\le N^{\max\{c,b+aK\}+o(1)}}.           \tag{5}
\]
This holds for adaptive queries and for an arbitrary polynomial-time final
Boolean aggregation of their answers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Execute $G$ exactly.  Whenever it emits a query $y$, run the same fixed $A$
on $y$ and return the resulting bit to $G$.  Adaptivity changes later query
contents but not the number or maximum length of calls.  The sum of all query
times is at most
\[
 N^{b+o(1)}\left(N^{a+o(1)}\right)^{K+o(1)}
 =N^{b+aK+o(1)}.
\]
Taking the maximum with charged local work proves (5).  Query construction,
writing, aggregation, and output are already included in $c$.  Sequential
simulation requires only the controller state, one live query buffer, and the
largest live workspace of $A$; no answer vector need be materialized.
\end{proof}

The theorem is not new to the corpus.  ENVELOPE1 and root \texttt{main.tex}
already record the direct-route exponent $\max\{c,b+aK\}$ in equivalent
notation.  The new contribution is its application to the exact handoff from
TESTS-0004 and the black-box parity countercontrol below.

\begin{corollary}[No superconstant-query repair]
Assume $P=NP$ and fix any polynomial SAT decider $A$.  Every service meeting
the hypotheses of the theorem is in P, even when its output depends on
polynomially many answer bits and is nonmonotone.  Therefore proving only
that the service needs more than $O(1)$ SAT-oracle decisions cannot yield a
contradiction from $P=NP$.
\end{corollary}

The exponent $K$ remains in (5).  Replacing $O(1)$ by $N^b$ adds $b$; it
does not create a $K$-independent computation.

\section{Sharp parity countercontrol}

For $q\ge1$, define the abstract truth-table service
\[
 \operatorname{PARITYSAT}_q(F_1,\ldots,F_q)
 =\bigoplus_{i=1}^q\mathbf1[F_i\in\SAT].                  \tag{6}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Query lower bound and compact representation]
In the deterministic black-box model exposing only the $q$ membership bits,
computing (6) requires exactly $q$ queries in the worst case.  Nevertheless,
the answer function has a width-two ordered binary decision diagram with
$2q-1$ nonterminal nodes, and sequential use of any SAT decider computes it
with $q$ calls.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If one bit is unqueried, two completions differing only in that bit have
opposite parity, so no deterministic algorithm can stop correctly.  Querying
all bits is sufficient.  The branching program stores only the running
parity; after the root there are two residual states at every remaining
level, giving $1+2(q-1)=2q-1$ nonterminal nodes.  Sequential evaluation is
immediate.
\end{proof}

This is a nonmonotone service with the strongest possible deterministic
query dependence on its $q$ input bits.  It still supplies no ordinary time
lower bound.  On an explicit hostile family, encode bit one by one satisfiable
proper ternary clause and bit zero by all eight clauses on the same three
variables.  The source syntax itself reveals every bit in linear time.  Thus
an exact oracle-query lower bound can coexist with a direct syntactic shortcut,
compact branching program, and trivial one-bit computation.

The primary bounded-query literature treats exactly this distinction.
K\"obler, Sch\"oning, and Wagner define truth-table reductions by a polynomially
computed Boolean circuit over oracle answers and locate the resulting
hierarchies between NP and $P^{NP}$.  Their parity-of-SAT tuples give complete
problems at fixed difference-hierarchy levels.  Beigel distinguishes bounded
parallel and serial NP queries and proves conditional separations among those
query classes.  These are exact query-model classifications, not unrestricted
runtime lower bounds.

\section{Wrapper, barrier, and transfer audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{Easy algorithms.}
Horn propagation, 2-SAT SCCs, affine/Tseitin Gaussian elimination, and
bounded-treewidth dynamic programming can evaluate the relevant residuals
without constructing any compatibility object.  Their existence defeats any
claim that service query count alone measures hardness.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products and compact representations.}
Parity depends on every independent coordinate but retains a width-two OBDD.
Semantic dependence is therefore distinct from representation size and time.
\item[] \textbf{Padding and encodings.}
Clause order, duplicate clauses, tautological padding, renaming, and global
complementation preserve the service bit.  Charged parsing prevents padding
from manufacturing hardness.
\item[] \textbf{DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP.}
DEAD materialization may be skipped; COPY shares one answer; DELAY inflates
a trace only; INTERP decodes an alternate representation; RECOMP is exactly
the sequential substitution proof.  None forces same-execution state.
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output.}
The final parity, pin, or SAT answer is one bit.  An explicit answer vector is
a different interface and its output length cannot be charged for free.
\end{description}

The closure proof relativizes: replace $A$ by $A^O$ and every call by the
same oracle-relative computation.  Algebraic-extension access can only make
the evaluator stronger.  Thus the result exposes, rather than crosses, the
Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson barriers.  No large constructive
property against general circuits is proved, so Natural Proofs is not the
immediate obstruction; it must be audited if a later repair asserts such a
circuit lower bound.  Nonuniform advice can hardwire more source-specific
information, so it cannot rescue a universal necessity claim.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Bounded truth-table and bounded adaptive-query hierarchies transfer exactly
as classifications of oracle-query interfaces.  FLMV transfers only after
one same machine satisfies its simultaneous time-space premises.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Communication, cell-probe, pebbling, VLSI routing, and memory-hierarchy lower
bounds may constrain a fixed batch service.  The missing theorem maps every
SAT decider into that exact interface with all source rereads and recomputation
charged.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Parity decision-tree complexity, physical fan-out, explicit-vector length,
and restricted branching-program lower bounds do not imply unrestricted SAT
runtime without the model-preserving compiler.
\end{description}

For the post-quantum ledger, the lossiness calculation is exact and negative:
parity maps $2^q$ answer vectors into two fibers of size $2^{q-1}$, so collision
entropy is large while evaluation remains linear.  Schur squares, quadratic
relation ranks, Hilbert functions, syzygies, and cryptographic binding do not
block this quotient and are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for this target.
LWE-style external extraction and special-soundness collision extraction are
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE}; they still require a source-matched unconditional
bridge.  Random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible.

\texttt{FRACTIONAL-PREDECISION-COMPILER1}:
