\begin{abstract}
This report independently attacks MODELBRIDGE-0011.  Its adaptive
strictly-downward oracle-elimination theorem survives within the declared
extensional, uniform, explicit-query interface.  If child exponent
$\lambda<1$, query exponent $\eta$, local-time exponent $r$, and space
exponent $p$ are fixed, elimination yields
\[
 C=\max\{1,r,\eta/(1-\lambda)\},\qquad P=\max\{p,\lambda\}.
\]
Composing with the FLMV random-access SAT lower bound excludes every contract
with $C<\varphi$ and $P<d(C)$, where
$d(c)=\frac12((c+2)/c^2-c)$.  The proposed compiler from every polynomial SAT
decider into this forbidden class is therefore not an intermediate:
\[
 \exists K\ \forall a\,
 [a\in\PolySAT\Rightarrow\mathsf{Good}(K(a))]
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \SAT\notin P.
\]
If a polynomial solver exists, its image contradicts the endpoint; if none
exists, the specification is vacuous.  The all-solver compiler is thus
separation-strength by its exact quantifier, not merely difficult
bookkeeping.  An exact-rational audit checked 64,980 parameter tuples and
389,880 recurrence/space inequalities with zero failures.  P versus NP
remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact claim and accepted interface}

MODELBRIDGE-0011 fixes one uniform deterministic transducer correct on every
encoded input, with a common uniform $o(1)$ envelope.  Each invocation makes
at most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ adaptive membership-bit queries, each of explicit
encoded length at most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, where $\lambda<1$.  All local
query construction, access, serialization, resumption, combination, output,
retained frames, and buffers are charged.  Queries strictly decrease above a
finite threshold.  The transducer cannot observe oracle code, timing, traces,
or state.

Under exactly these quantifiers, well-founded substitution is sound.  Its
recurrence and conservative depth-first space are
\[
 T(N)\le N^{r+o(1)}+N^{\eta+o(1)}
       T(N^{\lambda+o(1)}),\qquad
 S(N)\le N^{\max\{p,\lambda\}+o(1)},                     \tag{1}
\]
and hence
\[
 T(N)\le N^{C+o(1)},\quad
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{\eta}{1-\lambda}\right\},\qquad
 P=\max\{p,\lambda\}.                                    \tag{2}
\]
Adaptivity changes which queries occur, not their maximum number or size.
The common envelope is essential: node-dependent nonuniform bounds would not
justify the induction.

The accepted theorem excludes several apparent extensions.  Succinct query
descriptors are not explicit decoded query lengths without a charged local
accessor theorem.  A current-scale query ($\lambda=1$), a same-scale fallback,
or a hidden invocation of the supplied solver retains its unknown exponent.
Polynomial padding changes the encoded realization.  Conversion from an
explicit CNF to canonical proper 3-CNF may be absorbed only when the reduction
has near-linear size, locally computable bits, and charged setup/access.

\section{The exact forbidden frontier}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{CONTRACT-FLMV-FRONTIER1}]
In the uniform deterministic random-access model matched to FLMV, no
all-input extensional contract satisfying (1)--(2) exists when
\[
 C<\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2},\qquad
 P<d(C),\qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).             \tag{3}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
MODELBRIDGE-0011 gives an ordinary SAT decider in time $N^{C+o(1)}$ and
space $N^{P+o(1)}$ on the same realization.  The FLMV curve is continuous
and strictly decreasing because
\[
 d'(c)=\frac12(-c^{-2}-4c^{-3}-1)<0.
\]
The strict inequalities in (3) permit a rational $c$ with
$C<c<\varphi$ and $P<d(c)$.  For sufficiently large $N$, the eliminated
decider uses at most $N^c$ time and $N^{d(c)}$ space, contradicting the
model-matched FLMV theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Concrete rational box]
No contract satisfies
\[
 \lambda\le\tfrac12,\quad p\le\tfrac12,\quad r\le\tfrac65,\quad
 \eta\le\tfrac65(1-\lambda).                             \tag{4}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Then $C\le6/5$ and $P\le1/2$.  At $c=1201/1000$,
\[
 d(c)=\frac{1468676399}{2884802000}
 =\frac12+\frac{26275399}{2884802000}>\frac12,
\]
while $6/5<c<\varphi$.
\end{proof}

The boundary is strict.  Equality $P=d(C)$ is not certified because any
$c>C$ lowers $d(c)$.  This report does not import an obsolete universal
$2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint.

\section{Quantifier red team: the compiler is the separation}

Let $\PolySAT$ be the codes of total correct uniform polynomial-time SAT
deciders.  Let $\mathsf{Good}(m)$ mean that $m$ codes a contract satisfying
(3), including the SAME\_REALIZATION and explicit-query conditions.
The preceding theorem proves
\[
 \forall m\quad\neg\mathsf{Good}(m).                     \tag{5}
\]

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{FORBIDDEN-COMPILER-VACUITY1}]
The semantic compiler specification
\[
 \exists\hbox{ total computable }K\ \forall a\quad
 [a\in\PolySAT\Longrightarrow\mathsf{Good}(K(a))]        \tag{6}
\]
is logically equivalent to $\SAT\notin P$.  The same holds for
$\forall a\in\PolySAT\,\exists m\,\mathsf{Good}(m)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If (6) and $\SAT\in P$, choose $a\in\PolySAT$.  Then
$\mathsf{Good}(K(a))$, contradicting (5).  Conversely, if $\SAT\notin P$,
then $\PolySAT=\varnothing$; any constant total computable $K$ satisfies (6)
because its antecedent is always false.  The second formula has the same
empty-domain analysis.
\end{proof}

This is not a proof of (6) or $P\ne NP$.  It is a proof that the bare
all-solver semantic statement is not a weaker missing arrow.  White-box,
trace-sensitive, or code-sensitive variants avoid the extensional induction
only by creating new obligations: total static analysis, covariance under
DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, arbitrary exponents, and a nonvacuous premise
that is not already equivalent to separation.

\section{Barrier and primary-literature audit}

FLMV is a \textbf{DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE KNOWN THEOREM} only after the machine,
encoding, time, and space match.  Baker--Gill--Solovay show that relativizing
methods cannot settle the unrelativized question.  Aaronson--Wigderson extend
the barrier to algebrizing methods, and Chen--Hu--Ren show that several circuit
lower-bound approaches already beyond relativization still algebrize.
Razborov--Rudich applies only if a future route becomes a large constructive
useful circuit property; no such property is used here.

Chavrimootoo--Clingerman--Luu--Subramanian identify the precise error of
assuming an arbitrary SAT solver itself has a downward-self-reducible
structure.  The accepted elimination theorem is conditional on an explicitly
supplied transducer; (6), not the recurrence, is the unproved separation-
strength step.

VLSI area--time, pebbling, proof space, communication cuts, observability, and
branching-program width are \textbf{ANALOGIES REQUIRING A NEW TRANSFER}.
Importing their restricted interfaces to an arbitrary RAM decider is a
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.  Nonuniform circuit advice cannot replace the
uniform compiler, and a one-bit decision interface need not expose any
compatibility object.

\section{Post-quantum ledger}

Schur-square distinguishers, quadratic relation ranks, Hilbert functions,
syzygies, and Betti numbers are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; hidden algebraic
structure may make instances easier and no theorem forces arbitrary solvers
to expose it.  Collision entropy retains
$|\operatorname{im}L|\ge|A|^2/\operatorname{Coll}(L)$ but lacks a charged
execution map.  LWE-style external extraction is a candidate architecture;
(6) shows that mapping into this already-forbidden target is
separation-equivalent.  Special-soundness can help only through a
construction-specific forbidden witness.  Cryptographic binding and random
oracles are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for an unconditional proof.
