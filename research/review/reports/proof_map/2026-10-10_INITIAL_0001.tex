\section*{Direct composition: the shortest current conditional route}
\begin{theorem}[Recovered finite composition]\label{thm:single}
Suppose one total uniform constructor $W$ maps every sufficiently large
ordinary CNF to an equivalent ordinary CNF of length $m^{h+o(1)}$,
$0<h<1$, in time $m^{r+o(1)}$ and writable space $m^{p+o(1)}$.
Assume common uniform error bounds, closure of the output domain, and all
construction/physical-access costs charged. Fix a constant threshold $N_0$
above all exceptional sizes. For every fixed integer $d\ge1$, apply $W$
at most $d$ times, checking the current length before each call. If it is
below $N_0$, stop and run $A$ immediately; otherwise run $A$ after the
$d$th transformation. This gives
\[
 t_d\le\max\{r,Kh^d\},\qquad
 s_d\le\max\{p,h,Lh^d\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $d$, on any branch that has not stopped early, after $i$ steps
the encoded size is at most $N^{h^i+o(1)}$.
The finite sum of construction times is bounded by its largest exponent,
and the seed costs $N^{Kh^d+o(1)}$. During successive constructions charge
the old writable child, new child, and the constructor's work simultaneously;
their maximum exponents are bounded by $h$ and $p$. The original input is
read-only. The seed workspace contributes $Lh^d$. Discard an obsolete
derived parent once its child is complete. Every early-stop seed invocation
has bounded input length and therefore costs a fixed finite amount of time
and space because $A$ is total. These constants do not change the asymptotic
bounds. Exactness follows by induction, and fixed composition with this
finite-base rule preserves totality and uniformity.
\end{proof}

For $r=1$ and $s=\max\{p,h\}<1$, choose a fixed $d$ with $Kh^d<1$
and $Lh^d<s$. Then the one resulting algorithm has time $N^{1+o(1)}$
and space $N^{s+o(1)}$, contradicting \cite[Corollary 3.3]{fortnow}.
The concrete source target has $h=p=2/3$. Neither $W$ nor its cost theorem
has been constructed for all inputs.

The balanced-branch contract is distinct. Its recurrence is
\[
 T(N)\le N^{1+o(1)}+N^{1-h+o(1)}T(N^{h+o(1)}).
\]
Finite unfolding from exponent $K$ gives $1+h^d(K-1)$, which need not equal
$1$ for finite $d$. FORMAL-SPINE1 correctly proves a single fully recursive
algorithm with the near-linear bound under the full closed contract.
Do not borrow the single-child finite-depth conclusion without the
single-child hypothesis.
