\title{Certified Global Merge: Exact Semantics and a Contraction Obstruction}
\date{2026-10-10}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status.} P versus NP remains OPEN. Canonical duplicate/tautology removal, subsumption, unit propagation, and connected-component factoring are exact source-first transformations in their stated models. No all-CNF fixed-power shrinkage is established.
\paragraph{Construction.} Normalize clauses as sets, remove tautologies and duplicates, remove a clause when a strict subset clause exists, propagate unit literals until fixed point or contradiction, and factor the residual clause-variable incidence graph into connected components. A CNF is satisfiable iff all its variable-disjoint components are satisfiable, with an empty clause handled as UNSAT. For a chosen variable, exact Shannon expansion combines both branches by OR. Each operation has a source-checkable local justification and requires no SAT decision call.
\paragraph{Falsification.} Connected irredundant positive 3-CNF chains
$F_q=\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-1}(x_{2i+1}\vee x_{2i+2}\vee x_{2i+3})$
have no tautologies, duplicate clauses, subsumed clauses, unit clauses, or disconnected components. This entire specified preprocessing portfolio returns $F_q$ unchanged. Hence it does not guarantee child size $N^h$ for fixed $h<1$ on all inputs. This is a restricted preprocessing counterexample, not a SAT lower bound; the family is easy by all-TRUE assignment.
\paragraph{Computational check.} Seed 10102026. 210 random CNFs of 3--8 variables plus 37 independent-product/connected-chain structural controls, 247 cases total. Brute-force SAT checked normalization, unit propagation, component conjunction, and Shannon OR. All assertions passed after correcting the harness to treat an empty clause as UNSAT. Runtime approximately 0.06 seconds. Raw results and code are separate. The harness itself is not proof of asymptotic properties.
\paragraph{Open arrow.} Obtain an exact, uniform all-input source-first merger with charged discovery and a fixed-power bound on every generated child, including UNSAT and fallback, without parent-scale semantic calls. Random route order cannot establish this. Under hypothetical SAT in P, decide-then-emit gives small output but inherits the unknown polynomial exponent.
