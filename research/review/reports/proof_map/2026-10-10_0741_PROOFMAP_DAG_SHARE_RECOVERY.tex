\section*{DAG Sharing and Residual Quotient: historical recovery and bounded test}
\textbf{Status:} P versus NP remains OPEN. The experiment is COMPUTATIONAL; no unrestricted lower bound is claimed.
\paragraph{Historical recovery.} September 16 provenance-DAG experiments reported 10,224 histories collapsing to 79 semantic residuals, and 43,296 child evaluations collapsing to 2,716 under memoization. September 27 NONSHARE/PACK proposals explicitly demanded future-equivalence and anti-sharing proofs. October 2 universal-DAG enumeration reported 10,518,300 four-variable eight-clause formulas collapsing to 29,417 semantic masks. These are historical reports, not independently rerun here. The October 10 implicit BVE construction already established exact lazy resolvent generation. This run does not rename those discoveries.
\paragraph{Literature.} Darwiche, Compiling Knowledge into Decomposable Negation Normal Form (IJCAI 1999), proves tractable DNNF compilation on bounded-treewidth CNFs and tractable reasoning after compilation; see https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/99-1/Papers/042.pdf. Darwiche, A Compiler for Deterministic, Decomposable Negation Normal Form (AAAI 2002), studies explicit d-DNNF compilation, https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/2002/AAAI02-094.pdf. Atserias, Fichte, Thurley (JAIR 2011) relate clause-learning with restarts to bounded-width resolution, https://doi.org/10.1613/JAIR.3152. These are representation- or proof-system-specific results, not unrestricted SAT machine lower bounds.
\paragraph{Model.} A residual-DAG memoizes exact simplified CNF states after partial assignments. Its node count measures reusable states, not runtime of arbitrary SAT algorithms. An implicit BVE node retains original source references and indexed resolvent generation; shared source access, index computation, all generated queries, cache, and replay must be charged.
\paragraph{Test.} Seed 20261010. 175 CNFs on 3--9 variables, including random 3-CNFs, forced literals, contradictory units and tautological controls. Brute-force satisfiability agreed with recursive memoized residual evaluation in all 175 cases. The largest observed recursive call count was 43. Runtime 0.0296 seconds. This validates the toy evaluator, not a minimum DAG, an asymptotic bound, or a general-SAT strict successor.
\paragraph{Falsification.} Repeated syntactic histories can share a semantic residual; exponential history multiplicity therefore cannot be charged as distinct mandatory computation. Conversely, exact semantic quotient construction can hide SAT solving, and a read-only source can be reread. Random scheduling cannot force retention of otherwise bypassable DAG states.
\paragraph{Next gap.} Prove or refute a source-checkable, efficiently acquired, future-equivalent quotient with fully charged random access and no original-scale SAT calls that achieves fixed-power child shrinkage for every CNF, including UNSAT. This is OPEN.
