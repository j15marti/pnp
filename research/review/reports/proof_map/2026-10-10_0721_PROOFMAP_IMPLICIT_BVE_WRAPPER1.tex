\section*{IMPLICIT-BVE-WRAPPER1: source-first lazy resolution audit}
\textbf{Status:} P versus NP remains OPEN. Exact Davis--Putnam elimination is a known transformation; the implicit generator is an exact representation, not an all-CNF shrinkage theorem.
\paragraph{Construction.} Partition clauses into positive pivot occurrences $P_x$, negative occurrences $N_x$, and unaffected $R_x$. Store these sources and generate indexed resolvents from $P_x\times N_x$ on demand, discarding tautologies. Satisfiability of the projected CNF equals the original. Lazy generation saves explicit cross-product storage, but charges all source reads, index decoding, query multiplicity, and requested output bits.
\paragraph{Counterexample.} For $p$ clauses $(x\vee a_i)$ and $p$ clauses $(\neg x\vee\neg b_j)$ on disjoint auxiliary variables, $2p$ clauses generate $p^2$ distinct resolvents. A consumer requesting all outputs pays quadratic output work. If the pivot is absent, the residual remains parent-scale. Therefore this rule cannot alone establish fixed-power ordinary-CNF contraction.
\paragraph{Computational audit.} Seed 20261010, 180 random 3-CNFs with 3--8 variables, seven cross-product controls $p=2,3,4,5,8,16,32$, and a no-pivot control. 1566 assertions passed, including brute-force SAT equivalence and indexed resolvent reconstruction. Runtime 0.014 seconds. Code and raw results were produced locally; they are not claimed published in GitHub. A more detailed local LaTeX report compiled successfully.
\paragraph{Prior art.} Een--Biere, Effective Preprocessing in SAT through Variable and Clause Elimination, SAT 2005. Project ARC-STRENGTHEN5 previously examined iterated BVE. STREAMCOMP1 and ORBITBATCH1 already supply conditional implicit-input and shared-setup accounting.
\paragraph{Open implication.} Construct one uniform all-input source-first strict successor, with fully charged discovery, source access, generated queries, replay, output and workspace, and no current-scale semantic call. Random scheduling and implicit cross products do not establish it.
