\section{Selected implication and model}
The selected implication is the quantitative certificate-acquisition arrow.
Let $N$ be complete encoded bit length, let read-only inputs be random-access,
let $V$ be one fixed deterministic verifier, and let candidates have length
at most
\[
 w(N)=\kappa\log_2N+o(\log N).
\]
The same implementation must enumerate, address, verify, retain output and
share workspace; verifier time $q(N)$ already charges its input probes.
The weakest useful theorem is an exact exponent for the implementation
actually stated, not a lower bound on every acquirer.

\section{Acquisition correction}
\begin{theorem}[Correct exponent of the displayed CC-0002 bound]
Assume $|p|=O(N)$,
$q(N)=N^{v+o(1)}$, and
$w(N)=\kappa\log_2N+o(\log N)$.  The bound displayed in CC-0002,
\[
 (2^{w+1}-1)(q+N+|p|+w+1),
\]
has exponent $\kappa+\max\{v,1\}$.  It implies $v+\kappa$ only when
$v\ge1$ or when the non-verifier terms are separately dominated by $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first factor is $N^{\kappa+o(1)}$.  The second is
$N^{\max\{v,1\}+o(1)}$ because it contains $N$, has $|p|=O(N)$, and
$w=O(\log N)$.  Multiplication adds exponents.  If a random-access
implementation instead shares read-only $(p,x)$ across trials, it may avoid
the repeated $N+|p|$ term, but that is a distinct implementation theorem
whose pointer initialization, verifier probes, candidate counter, and
writable-space conventions must be stated and proved.
\end{proof}
The correction does not weaken the existence/acquisition distinction:
existential PFCERT membership still requires no acquirer, whereas an
executable wrapper must charge the one it actually uses.

\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
Hirahara's Theorem 3.9 gives P-immunity for superpolynomial-time bounded
Kolmogorov-random strings, supporting the conditional endpoint but not the
verifier construction \cite{hirahara}.  Hirahara--Kabanets--Lu--Oliveira
explicitly treat exact search-to-decision as longstanding open and prove
only specified regimes \cite{hklo}; therefore certificate existence cannot
be promoted to discovery.  Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas define a
random-access model with read-only input excluded from workspace and prove
locally computable reduction-bit access in Theorem 3.1 \cite{flmv}.  This is
an exact warning that input sharing and local access require their own model,
not permission to erase access cost.  Carter--Wegman hashing is a failed
transfer to exact semantics: probabilistic collision bounds do not imply
fiber monochromaticity \cite{cw}.

Easy families, padding, source carry, direct-solve/current-scale calls,

unchanged: enumeration is one implementation and cannot prove acquisition
cost for all algorithms.  The correction relativizes and algebrizes.
