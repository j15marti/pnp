\title{EFFECTIVE-WIDTH1: Structural, Semantic and Acquisition Frontiers}
\date{2026-10-10}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status.} P versus NP remains OPEN. This report defines a stronger restricted-model frontier and tests its structural-versus-semantic distinction. No unrestricted SAT lower bound is claimed.
\paragraph{Definitions.} For a CNF F with n variables let w(F) be minimum elimination width of its primal graph. Let s_{\rm OBDD}(F) be the minimum nonterminal node count of a reduced ordered BDD over all variable orders. These are different invariants: graph width concerns a chosen structural elimination interface; OBDD size measures a semantic representation class. For a fixed compiler family C, define a charged frontier
\[
\mathcal E_C(F)=\operatorname{ParetoMin}\{(T_C(F),S_C(F),L_C(F))\},
\]
where T includes construction, source reads, normalization, search, and all queries, S includes peak live encoded workspace, and L is the resulting representation's encoded length. A small s_{\rm OBDD} is not evidence that T_C is small.
\paragraph{Exact controls.} A CNF expressing full parity of n variables has primal width n-1, yet a reduced OBDD for parity has O(n) nodes. A tautological dense CNF may have maximal primal width and constant-TRUE semantics with zero OBDD nodes. An explicitly contradictory CNF similarly has zero OBDD nodes. These facts refute any universal lower bound based solely on primal width. The XOR CNF here is exponential in n, so its encoded length must be charged; it is a representation control, not a hard SAT family.
\paragraph{Finite experiment.} Seed 20261010, 64 CNFs on 3 through 6 variables: AND, XOR, UNSAT, dense tautological and 12 random 3-CNFs per size. Exact enumeration of all variable orders computed primal width and minimum OBDD size. Forty-two cases had width at least n-1 and at most 2n OBDD nodes. Runtime 0.7842 seconds. This is COMPUTATIONAL and does not estimate minimum compiler time.
\paragraph{Prior research and literature.} Earlier MAX-COMPRESSION-FRONTIER1 optimized OBDD order; the joint-frontier experiments considered charged certified transformations. This run tests their proposed synthesis. Darwiche and Marquis, A Knowledge Compilation Map (JAIR 2002), distinguish succinctness from supported operations. Standard treewidth dynamic programming supplies an upper bound for a supplied decomposition, not a lower bound on arbitrary SAT machines.
\paragraph{Next theorem.} Establish a source-checkable compiler with joint T,S,L guarantees for all ordinary CNFs, or a model-matched lower bound robust to XOR, Horn, source rereading, direct SAT decision and semantic-oracle bypass. This remains OPEN.
