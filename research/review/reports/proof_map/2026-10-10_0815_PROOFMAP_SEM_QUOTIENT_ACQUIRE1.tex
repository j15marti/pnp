\section*{SEM-QUOTIENT-ACQUIRE1: acquisition-aware semantic quotient}
\textbf{Status:} P versus NP remains OPEN. Finite results are COMPUTATIONAL, not a lower bound on arbitrary SAT algorithms.
\paragraph{Prior work.} The September provenance DAG and NONSHARE/PACK experiments, October universal-DAG, STREAMCOMP1, ORBITBATCH1 and implicit BVE already identify history sharing, semantic quotient, and charged access. The present test distinguishes syntactic residual canonicalization from semantic quotient acquisition.
\paragraph{Exact definition.} For a fixed $n$-variable CNF $F$ and prefix assignment $\rho$ of length $d$, the suffix truth table $\sigma(F,\rho)$ has $2^{n-d}$ bits. Two such residuals are functionally equivalent iff their tables agree. Syntactically identical canonical residuals have equal tables. Computing all $2^d$ tables by exhaustive evaluation requires $2^n$ assignment evaluations plus formula-evaluation and storage charges. This is a constructive upper bound, not a lower bound on equivalence testing.
\paragraph{Experiment.} Seed 20261010. For $n=3,\ldots,8$, 14 random 3-CNFs each: 84 cases. In 48 cases the semantic residual count was strictly smaller than the canonical syntactic residual count. All 84 inequalities were verified; a tautology control passed. Runtime 0.0713 seconds. Python and raw JSON were generated locally and are not claimed published here.
\paragraph{Hostile controls.} Source rereading, a semantic SAT oracle, cheap equivalence certificates, and solver-based constant-output compression bypass raw history counting. Truth-table acquisition does not force arbitrary deciders to enumerate. No fixed-power ordinary-CNF child-size guarantee is established.
\paragraph{Next target.} Construct or falsify a source-checkable semantic quotient with fully charged discovery, indexed access, replay, and workspace on all CNFs, including UNSAT, without parent-scale SAT calls. This remains OPEN.
