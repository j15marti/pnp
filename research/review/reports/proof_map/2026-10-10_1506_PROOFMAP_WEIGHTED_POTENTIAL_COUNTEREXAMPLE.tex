\title{WEIGHTED-POTENTIAL-FAIL1: An Infinite Family with Negative Branch Gain}
\date{2026-10-10}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status.} PROVED/EXACT counterexample to monotone descent of the proposed weighted measure for the declared unit-propagating branching heuristic. P versus NP remains OPEN. This does not exclude a different potential or the known sub-$2^n$ 3-SAT algorithms.
\paragraph{Family.} Let $F_n$ contain every positive 3-clause on distinct variables from $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. It has $\binom n3$ clauses, no units, and all variables are symmetric. Define $\mu(F)=|\operatorname{LiveVars}(F)|+\sum_{C\in F}1/(|C|+1)$.
\paragraph{Exact calculation.} On the false branch $x_1=0$, exactly $\binom{n-1}{2}$ 3-clauses shorten to 2-clauses, each increasing clause weight by $1/3-1/4=1/12$. The live-variable term decreases by one. Hence
\[
\Delta_0=\mu(F_n)-\mu(F_n|_{x_1=0})
=1-\frac{\binom{n-1}{2}}{12}.
\]
On the true branch, all clauses containing $x_1$ disappear, giving
\[
\Delta_1=1+\frac{\binom{n-1}{2}}4.
\]
For every $n\ge7$, $\Delta_0<0$. Because the formula is symmetric, the weighted maximin pivot cannot avoid this negative branch gain. Therefore no universal positive per-branch decrease, nor the proposed naive measure-and-conquer induction, follows from this potential. The family is trivially SAT by all-TRUE assignment; adding a pure-literal rule defeats this example, not the logical defect of the unqualified potential.
\paragraph{Bounded verification.} Exact rational calculations for $n=5,6,7,8,10$ yielded false-branch gains $1/2,1/6,-1/4,-3/4,-2$ and true-branch gains $5/2,7/2,19/4,25/4,10$. These are arithmetic checks of the displayed identities, not evidence of hard SAT.
\paragraph{Research implication.} Any improved measure-and-conquer analysis must either impose and prove preprocessing invariants excluding this family, choose a potential with provable decreases under all reachable transformations, or use a multistep amortized recurrence with rigorously charged costs. A better base for this particular weighted heuristic is not established. The all-input SAT WRAPPER bridge remains OPEN.
