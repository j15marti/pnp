\section*{COMBINED-CERT-GRAPH1: finite certified reduction cost dual}
\textbf{Status:} P versus NP OPEN. Finite graph optimality PROVED/EXACT in the declared model; tests COMPUTATIONAL.
\paragraph{Model.} States are normalized explicit CNFs. Edges are exact unit propagation, clause subsumption, and Davis--Putnam elimination of one variable. Costs are positive artificial accounting weights: unit $1+$ clauses scanned, subsumption $1+m^2$, elimination $1+m+|P_x||N_x|$. A shortest-path distance $d$ from the source obeys $d(v)-d(u)\le c(u,v)$ for every reached edge. Therefore every path costs at least its dual distance, with equality for shortest paths. This is a model-specific exact optimization, not physical RAM optimality.
\paragraph{Critical correction.} Eliminating all variables by Davis--Putnam resolution always reaches a constant TRUE or FALSE representation. Therefore the minimum clause count alone is degenerate: it ignores potentially exponential intermediate clause generation. A useful frontier must constrain total acquisition cost and peak encoded length, not only terminal compression.
\paragraph{Test.} Seed 20261010. Forty-eight random 3-CNFs, 3--6 variables, exhaustive SAT checks on every generated edge. Dijkstra distances, positive costs and all shortest-path dual inequalities verified; zero dual violations. All 48 had some path reducing clause count. Runtime 0.062 seconds. The test does not show that these paths satisfy any favorable asymptotic bound.
\paragraph{Prior work.} The project previously developed RUP-IMPLICATION-SELECT1, STREAMCOMP1, ORBITBATCH1, and MAX-COMP-DUAL1. This run extends the finite dual to three additional exact source-first operations, not to semantic-oracle or unrestricted computation. Een--Biere (SAT 2005) supplies standard bounded variable elimination. Wetzler--Heule--Hunt (SAT 2014) supplies RUP proof-checking. These mechanisms do not force cheap discovery or universal contraction.
\paragraph{Open target.} Construct an all-input, uniform, hereditary source-first transformation with strict encoded child shrink and fully charged peak time/space, or prove a genuinely general lower bound. Direct SAT decision and constant output bypass restricted transformation graphs.
