\section*{SEM-CERT-UNSAT1: connected UNSAT semantic quotient audit}
\textbf{Status:} P versus NP remains OPEN. This is a COMPUTATIONAL falsification test of an incomplete local certificate, not a general lower bound.
\paragraph{Prior work.} WRP-0003 already proves a RUP-certified implication selector for explicit OR frontiers, with acquisition and checking costs; SEM-QUOTIENT-ACQUIRE1 compares canonical residuals with exact truth tables. This run tests the known RUP incompleteness against connected UNSAT residuals, without assuming equivalence is freely discoverable.
\paragraph{Construction.} Two independently variable-renamed copies of pigeonhole CNF PHP$(h+1,h)$ are semantically equivalent (both UNSAT), connected, and have distinct clause representations. RUP$(A,B)$ requires unit propagation on $A$ with each clause of $B$ falsified. A source-checkable Davis--Putnam resolution elimination can instead derive an empty clause, but generation and checking must be charged.
\paragraph{Test.} Seed 20261010; 50 cases: two connected PHP pairs and 48 randomized implication controls. PHP$(3,2)$: RUP succeeds in both directions; elimination generates 14 non-tautological resolvents from 16 pairs. PHP$(4,3)$: RUP fails in both directions despite exact semantic equivalence; elimination generates 357 resolvents from 458 pairs and reaches peak 59 clauses. All assertions passed, elapsed 0.0303 seconds. The local Python test, raw JSON and compiled PDF are retained outside GitHub in this run.
\paragraph{Literature.} Wetzler, Heule and Hunt, DRAT-trim (SAT 2014), supplies the standard RUP checking interface. Haken, The Intractability of Resolution (TCS 1985), proves exponential resolution lower bounds for growing pigeonhole formulas; this does not extend to unrestricted SAT machine time.
\paragraph{Missing implication.} No all-input, source-first, polynomially discoverable equivalence certificate with favorable recursive shrinkage and fully charged source access, proof search, replay and workspace is known. Semantic equality of UNSAT residuals is insufficient to produce such a certificate cheaply. Randomized wrapper routing does not repair this. Status OPEN.
