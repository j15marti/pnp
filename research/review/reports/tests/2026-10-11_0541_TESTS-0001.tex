\paragraph{Result and status.}
This run falsifies a proposed additive-cost promotion of the universal
external pinning extractor.  There is an explicit proper-three-CNF family
$\mathcal P=\{P_n(a):a\in\{0,1\}^n\}$ whose standard bit-by-bit
self-reduction has $3n+1$ nested SAT queries and separately materialized
encoded volume $\Theta(n^2\log n)$, although one uniform syntax-directed
transducer emits the exact greedy answer transcript and a satisfying witness
in $O(N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ writable space, where the source length is
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in the declared encoding
and machine model.

The bounded regression checked 6,152 extractor configurations, 92,648 encoded
queries, 86,496 semantic agreements, and 345,984 wrapper conditions across
proper-three-CNF pins, Horn equality, affine/Tseitin paths, and treewidth-two
independent exact-one products.  It also ran finite Monotone
3-SAT-(2,2) truth-table controls.  These measurements are
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}; they do not extrapolate a polynomial batch algorithm
for the NP-complete core.  A model-matched direct-sum or anti-sharing theorem
for arbitrary SAT deciders remains \textbf{OPEN}.  P versus NP remains
\textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Predecessor and exact claim attacked}
The newest verified peer artifacts were CC-0005, PROOFMAP-0004,
MODELBRIDGE-0003, and RT-0004.  MODELBRIDGE-0003 proves the positive external
boundary: for any total uniform exact SAT decider $A$, standard pinning
recovers one witness using at most $d+1$ calls on a $d$-variable satisfiable
formula.  RT-0004 then proves that external derivability is not internal
exposure.  Their GitHub reports are substantive and sentinelled, so no write
recovery was required.

The tempting next claim is
\[
\begin{split}
\forall A\,\forall F\quad
&\bigl[A\text{ is a total uniform exact SAT decider}\bigr]\\
&\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Cost}\bigl(A(F),\text{or a forced interface used by }A(F)\bigr)
\ge
\sum_{i=0}^{d}\operatorname{Cost}(A(F_i)),
\end{split}
\tag{1}
\]
where $F_0,F_1,\ldots,F_d$ are the nested pinned instances generated by the
external self-reduction.  Even a weaker promotion that charges the total
written query volume $\sum_i|F_i|$ to the original one-bit execution $A(F)$
has the same quantifier defect: the queries are work done by an external
extractor, and related queries may share their source and semantic work.

Equation (1) is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as a generic inference.  It could
be repaired only by stating a concrete multi-query service and proving a
model-matched no-sharing/direct-sum theorem for that service.

\section{Canonical proper-three-CNF counterfamily}
For $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in\{0,1\}^n$, use semantic variables $z_i$ and fresh
auxiliaries $p_i,q_i$.  Let $\ell_i=z_i$ when $a_i=1$ and
$\ell_i=\neg z_i$ when $a_i=0$, and set
\[
P_n(a)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{n}
\bigwedge_{(s,t)\in\{0,1\}^2}
(\ell_i\vee p_i^{(s)}\vee q_i^{(t)}),
\tag{2}
\]
where $p^{(0)}=p$, $p^{(1)}=\neg p$, and similarly for $q$.
For each $i$, conjunction of the four clauses is logically equivalent to
$\ell_i$.  Hence $z_i=a_i$ is forced while $p_i,q_i$ are free.  Formula (2)
has $d=3n$ variables and $4n$ proper width-three clauses.

The declared self-delimiting indexed encoding writes gamma-coded variable and
clause counts, a gamma-coded width for each clause, and for each literal one
sign bit plus a fixed-width variable index.  Thus
\[
N=|P_n(a)|=\Theta(n\log n).
\tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[PINNING-BATCH-SEPARATION1]
In the declared encoding, the standard greedy self-reduction on $P_n(a)$ has
$3n+1$ queries.  Whether prefixes are represented by unit clauses or by the
same four-clause proper-three-CNF pin gadget, separately writing all queries
uses $\Theta(n^2\log n)$ bits.  Nevertheless the exact answer transcript and
the lexicographically greedy witness are produced from the canonical source
in $O(N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ writable space.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The extractor first asks whether $P_n(a)$ is satisfiable.  At variable $j$ it
asks whether the current prefix extended by zero is satisfiable, and chooses
zero exactly when the answer is yes.  There are $d=3n$ such steps.  Every
query contains the base source, so total encoded volume is at least
$(3n+1)N=\Omega(n^2\log n)$.  A unit prefix adds $O(j\log n)$ bits; a proper
three-CNF prefix adds $4j$ clauses and $2j$ auxiliaries, also
$O(j\log n)$ bits.  Summing over $j\le3n$ gives the matching
$O(n^2\log n)$ upper bound.

For batching, scan the canonical four-clause blocks and recover each $a_i$
from the common semantic literal.  The zero-trial answer for $z_i$ is yes iff
$a_i=0$; its chosen witness bit is $a_i$.  Every $p_i,q_i$ is free, so each
remaining zero trial is yes and the chosen bit is zero.  A second scan or two
write-only output streams emits the transcript and witness in order.  Block
validation, counters, and indices require $O(\log N)$ work bits; two scans and
$O(n)$ output take $O(N)$ time.  No SAT oracle call is required on this
family.
\end{proof}

The theorem separates three quantities that must not be conflated:
\[
\text{separate materialized-query volume}
\ne
\text{minimum batch work}
\ne
\text{cost of the original one-bit run }A(F).
\tag{4}
\]

\section{Smallest repair: QUERY-NOSHARE1}
A surviving theorem must quantify over a declared service, not over an
external proof script:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\forall A\ \forall F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}\ \forall\tau
\quad \operatorname{CorrectSAT}(A,F,\tau)\\
\Longrightarrow
\bigl[\operatorname{BatchRep}(\tau,B)\wedge
\operatorname{Charge}(B,\operatorname{access},\operatorname{output})\bigr]
\ \lor\ 
\operatorname{DirectSumCost}(\tau)\ge L(N).
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]
Here $B$ ranges over all allowed shared representations, and acquisition,
validation, access, decoding, output, storage, and recomputation are charged.
The theorem must survive rereading, memoization, vectorization, compact
circuits or branching programs, affine elimination, semantic shortcuts,
INTERP, RECOMP, padding, and a matched semantic or algebraic oracle.  It also
needs an independent lower-bound endpoint; defining
$\operatorname{DirectSumCost}$ as optimal remaining SAT work would be
circular.  QUERY-NOSHARE1 is \textbf{OPEN}.

If $\Pclass=\NPclass$, a total polynomial SAT decider can recognize (2), use
the linear batch transducer there, and fall back to any polynomial SAT
algorithm elsewhere.  Hence this counterfamily creates no contradiction
under $\Pclass=\NPclass$ and does not smuggle $\Pclass\ne\NPclass$ into a
premise.

\section{Primary literature and barrier-transfer audit}
Kawachi--Rossman--Watanabe study witness finding for a hidden set through
queries ``is $Q\cap W$ nonempty?'' and prove $\Omega(n^2)$ for their
nonadaptive error-free oracle model \cite{krw}.  That is a
\textbf{DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE THEOREM} only after an exact compiler to that
oracle/query model; it is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} to unrestricted SAT
running time by itself.  Bj\"orklund--Kaski--Kowalik obtain $O(k\log n)$
queries for a set-inclusion decision oracle, demonstrating again that query
counts depend on the interface \cite{bkk}.

Besselman--G\"o\"os--Guo--Maystre--Yuan prove direct-sum results for randomized
parity decision trees under discrepancy or product-distribution hypotheses
\cite{bggmy}.  The theorem is exact in that model, but importing it to
arbitrary uniform SAT algorithms without a locality-preserving compiler is a
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.  Ren--Williams analyze bounded-round adaptive NP
queries inside a specific circuit-lower-bound proof \cite{rw}; this is a
\textbf{USEFUL STRUCTURAL ANALOGY} rather than unrestricted SAT no-sharing.
VLSI routing, memory hierarchy, online communication, and chronogram lower
bounds likewise require an exact layout, probe, or online-arrival map not
present here.

The negative separation relativizes and survives a matched semantic oracle;
it identifies rather than crosses the Baker--Gill--Solovay barrier.  Affine
controls show why algebrization is dangerous: Gaussian elimination batches
the entire transcript.  Natural Proofs is not yet triggered because (5) is
not a constructive large circuit property; any future promotion to circuit
lower bounds must re-audit usefulness, largeness, and constructivity.

The post-quantum ledger supplies no missing bridge.  Schur squares,
quadratic-relation ranks, Hilbert functions, and syzygies are
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to this query-sharing question absent an object
correspondence.  Collision entropy is exact for a fixed map but does not force
independent computation of correlated queries.  LWE-style external
extraction and special soundness are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} only;
cryptographic hardness or random-oracle binding is inadmissible in an
unconditional P-versus-NP proof.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
The corpus already says that query length is not generically additive under
sharing, and MAIN explicitly keeps representation-invariant no-sharing OPEN.
TESTS-0001 is not a new discovery of that slogan.  What is new is the exact
nested-pinning counterfamily in the current universal-core interface: it
matches MODELBRIDGE-0003's witness extractor, uses canonical proper-three-CNF,
proves a quadratic written-transcript versus linear batch separation, and
tests the required easy-family/wrapper controls.  It is consistent with
QLEN1, which is local explicit-query-writing accounting, not a theorem that
an arbitrary solver or batch service must write each query independently.

\section{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN}
Root main correctly lists representation-invariant no-sharing as OPEN.  The
new constraint is that any future CORE-ACQUIRE1 or causal multi-query repair
must explicitly defeat PINNING-BATCH-SEPARATION1.  Summing the lengths or
running times of externally generated nested pinning calls is not admissible
unless QUERY-NOSHARE1 is first proved for the declared hard family and model.
Root main was not edited because this review role has no LSPV ownership region.

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}
Any statement that MODELBRIDGE-0003 already makes $n+1$ SAT-call costs
``unavoidable'' is overstated.  It proves an external upper-bound extractor,
not a lower bound, and the present family shows exact sharing can collapse its
separately written transcript.  Likewise, ``sequentially adaptive'' is not by
itself an anti-batching condition when later queries are deterministic nested
functions of a source whose structure is reusable.

\paragraph{Single next target.}
Define the smallest legal batch representation for the hard clean core
$\mathcal H_{2,2}$ and attempt to prove or falsify QUERY-NOSHARE1 against
static reread, circuit/branching-program transcripts, affine preprocessing,
and semantic-oracle access.  The missing universal implication is precisely
the compiler from arbitrary SAT executions to that model with cost and
independence preserved.  Until it is proved, P versus NP remains
\textbf{OPEN}.
