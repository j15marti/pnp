\begin{abstract}
The newest surviving repair asks for a source-first, wrapper-covariant service
produced before the final decision, with only fractional SAT channels.  This
report isolates a necessary correction to the word ``before.''  Given any
exact deterministic 3SAT decider $A$, a uniform wrapper may first simulate
$A(F)$ to completion while suppressing its external output, then compute the
two-pin service
\[
 Q_{A,z}(F)=\bigl(A(\operatorname{Pin}_3(F,z,0)),
                  A(\operatorname{Pin}_3(F,z,1))\bigr),
\]
and only then release the already known source answer.  The service is exact,
renaming-covariant, and complement-covariant, and it appears before external
output.  It is nevertheless computed after the source decision and costs two
additional current-scale calls.  If $A$ has exponent $K$, the wrapper retains
$K$.  Thus pre-output chronology, even with a semantic covariant service, is
not pre-decision acquisition and cannot be charged to the original execution.
A bounded audit passed 3,559 checks with explicit phase counters.  The result
does not refute a future externally fixed fractional checkpoint contract.
P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim card and clean-core target}

The input is canonical indexed 3-CNF of encoded length $N$.  Let $\Htwo$ be
duplicate-free Monotone 3SAT-$(2,2)$: every clause contains three distinct
variables with one sign, and every variable occurs exactly twice positively
and twice negatively.  Hence $m=4n/3$ and $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.  The exact
universal target remains
\[
 \forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\Htwo:\quad
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0\quad\land\quad
 T_A(F)>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,                       \tag{1}
\]
where $A$ ranges over total correct uniform deterministic deciders.  This is
\OPEN and is never used as a premise.

The attacked implication is
\[
 \left[\text{a covariant service occurs before external output}\right]
 \Longrightarrow
 \left[\text{the service was acquired before the source decision}\right]. \tag{2}
\]
The SAME\_REALIZATION objects are the source encoding, the fixed decider $A$,
the wrapper transition trace, both compiled pin queries, their answer bits,
the suppressed source bit, and the final output event.  Query construction,
writing, simulation, decoding, and output are charged.

\section{Exact pin service and compiler}

For a formula $F$, variable $z$, and $v\in\{0,1\}$, first simplify
$F\upharpoonright_{z=v}$.  Compile residual clauses back to proper 3-CNF by
the standard constant-size local rules: a binary clause $(a\vee b)$ becomes
\[
 (a\vee b\vee u)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg u),
\]
a unit clause is expanded with two fresh variables into its four ternary
extensions, and the empty clause becomes a fixed eight-clause unsatisfiable
ternary formula.  Denote the result by $\operatorname{Pin}_3(F,z,v)$.  There
is a constant $c$ such that its encoded length is at most $cN+O(1)$, and the
compiler uses $O(N)$ time and output bits.

For a correct exact decider $A$, define
\[
 Q_{A,z}(F)=
 \left(A(\operatorname{Pin}_3(F,z,0)),
       A(\operatorname{Pin}_3(F,z,1))\right).             \tag{3}
\]
Shannon restriction gives
\[
 A(F)=Q_{A,z}(F)_0\vee Q_{A,z}(F)_1.                     \tag{4}
\]
If $\pi$ is a variable renaming, then
\[
 Q_{A,\pi(z)}(\pi F)=Q_{A,z}(F).                         \tag{5}
\]
If $\overline F$ globally complements every literal, then
\[
 Q_{A,z}(\overline F)=
 \left(Q_{A,z}(F)_1,Q_{A,z}(F)_0\right).                 \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) are exact covariance identities, not claims that $A$
materializes the pair.

\section{Pre-output delay theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{PREOUTPUT-SERVICE-GUARD1}]
Let $A$ be any total correct uniform deterministic 3SAT decider.  There is a
total correct uniform wrapper $W_{A,z}$ which, on $F$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item simulates $A(F)$ to completion and retains the answer $b$ without
releasing it externally;
\item computes the exact covariant service $Q_{A,z}(F)$ from (3);
\item releases the already retained bit $b$.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the service exists in the trace before external output but after the
source answer is fixed.  If
\[
 T_A(M)\le M^{K+o(1)},
\]
then
\[
 T_{W_{A,z}}(N)
 \le T_A(N)+2T_A(cN+O(1))+O(N)
 =N^{K+o(1)}.                                             \tag{7}
\]
Sequential execution needs one live compiled query plus the maximum live
workspace of $A$; it does not require both query traces simultaneously.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Universal simulation supplies step (i), with the simulated output event
intercepted and its one-bit payload retained.  The local compiler and two
further simulations of the same fixed $A$ supply step (ii).  Correctness of
$A$ and (4) show that the service is exact and agrees with the retained bit.
Step (iii) outputs that retained bit.  The sum in (7) charges compilation,
query writing, both simulations, and output.  Constant input dilation does
not change exponent $K$.  The schedule explicitly orders the source decision
before service acquisition, so the service is not a causal predecessor of
that decision.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Movable external boundary]
For any polynomial-time source functional $q(F)$, the same construction can
run $A(F)$ first, compute $q(F)$ second, and output last.  Therefore the
predicate ``computed before external output'' is not a wrapper-invariant
certificate of computational necessity.
\end{corollary}

The theorem is compatible with $P=NP$.  If $A$ is polynomial, the wrapper is
polynomial.  It derives no contradiction.  It also explains why the unknown
solver exponent remains: the two semantic channels in (7) have scale one,
not strictly fractional scale.

\section{Three attempted decision boundaries}

There are three natural readings of ``pre-decision,'' and none is sufficient
without further structure.

\paragraph{Semantic determination.}
For a fixed read-only input and deterministic transition function, every
reachable configuration has one unique continuation and one unique eventual
output.  In that extensional sense the answer is already determined at the
initial configuration, making the proposed boundary empty.

\paragraph{Raw answer register.}
The first occurrence of a literal raw answer bit is representation-relative.
INTERP may store $1-b$ and decode only at output, or store any efficient
invertible encoding.  Decoder-relative knowledge can remain present while raw
exposure disappears.

\paragraph{External output event.}
Theorem 1 moves this event after arbitrary polynomial instrumentation.  A
useful replacement must therefore fix an external source-defined checkpoint
and prove that no current-scale decision or equivalent semantic shortcut has
already occurred before that checkpoint.

\section{Literature, transfer, and barrier audit}

Anderson and Dawar prove a precise correspondence between uniform symmetric
majority circuits and fixed-point logic with counting.  This is a genuine
lower-bound route only after restricting computation to that symmetric
circuit model.  It does not make covariance a lower bound for arbitrary
Turing/RAM SAT deciders.  Huang, Li, and Zhong likewise obtain range-avoidance
and circuit-lower-bound equivalences with explicit circuit-class and
$\mathrm{FP}^{\mathrm{NP}}$ hypotheses.  Li and Tell's 7 October 2026
plasticity results are conditional on range-avoidance hardness.  None supplies
the source-defined checkpoint compiler required here.

The wrapper theorem relativizes verbatim: replace every call to $A$ by the
same $A^O$.  Algebraic oracle access can only make service acquisition easier.
Chen, Hu, and Ren's recent algebrization results reinforce that merely moving
beyond a simple relativizing presentation is insufficient.  No broad
constructive circuit property is asserted, so Natural Proofs is not the
immediate obstruction; it must be audited if a future repair targets general
circuits.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
The symmetric-circuit/logical correspondence transfers exactly inside its
declared model.  The two-pin identity and wrapper simulation transfer exactly
to this report's machine model.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Online communication, chronograms, control-theoretic observability cuts, and
VLSI latch boundaries suggest externally fixed checkpoints.  The missing
theorem compiles every static SAT decider to one such checkpoint while
charging preprocessing, rereads, recomputation, and alternate encodings.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Physical ``before/after,'' symmetric-circuit support, explicit service output,
and solver-local output timing do not constrain arbitrary static computation
without that compiler.
\end{description}

For the post-quantum ledger, the two-pin map is a two-bit lossy service whose
collision structure does not imply acquisition cost.  Schur squares,
quadratic-relation ranks, Hilbert functions, and syzygies do not repair the
movable output boundary and are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for this target.
LWE-style external extraction and special-soundness collision extraction are
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE}; they still need an unconditional source-matched
checkpoint theorem.  Cryptographic and random-oracle assumptions are
inadmissible.

\section{Smallest repair and handoff}

Retain \texttt{FRACTIONAL-PREDECISION-COMPILER1}, but add an exact boundary
clause:
\begin{quote}
The checkpoint is fixed by the source/interface rather than by the solver's
output event.  Before it, the compiled realization performs no current-scale
SAT decision, no computation from which the source answer is already
available under the declared decoder, and no semantic shortcut equivalent to
that decision.  Every surviving SAT channel is strictly fractional and the
contract is hereditary on all unresolved descendants.
\end{quote}
The next bounded test should try to define such a source-fixed checkpoint on
the clean core and immediately attack it with preprocessing, direct semantic
shortcuts, INTERP, and RECOMP.  The compiler, checkpoint non-bypass theorem,
matched lower bound, nonrelativizing hinge, nonalgebrizing hinge, UCORE-TIME1,
and P versus NP remain \OPEN.
