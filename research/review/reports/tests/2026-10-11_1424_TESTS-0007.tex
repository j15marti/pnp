\begin{abstract}
LB-0012 exhibited exponential semantic compatibility rank inside proper
unique Monotone 3-Sat-$(2,2)$ using disconnected $K_{3,3}$ products.  This
run tests the proposed repair ``require the clean core to be connected.''
It fails.  Join $k$ copies of $K_{3,3}$ in a chain by bipartite
degree-preserving 2-switches and form the paired positive/negative incidence
formula.  The formula is connected and remains in the exact clean-core
syntax.  Fixing the $2(k-1)$ connector variables to zero leaves a
block-factorized submatrix whose two end ranks are $13$ and whose interior
rank is $8$ over $\mathbb F_2$.  Therefore the full compatibility matrix has
rank at least $13^2 8^{k-2}=2^{\Theta(k)}=
2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$ over both $\mathbb F_2$ and $\mathbb R$.  Nevertheless
every member is satisfiable by a perfect matching of its cubic bipartite
incidence graph, and a canonical recognizer decides the family in linear
input time.  The runner exhaustively verified the local matrices and 11
connected chains in 1.36 seconds.  No arbitrary-algorithm lower bound
follows; P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Connected family and exact rank lower bound}

For block $i\in\{0,\ldots,k-1\}$ let
\(L_i=\{\ell_{i,0},\ell_{i,1},\ell_{i,2}\}\) and
\(R_i=\{r_{i,0},r_{i,1},r_{i,2}\}\).  Start with all nine edges of
$K_{3,3}$ inside every block.  For each $i<k-1$, delete
\[
 (\ell_{i,0},r_{i,0})\quad\text{and}\quad
 (\ell_{i+1,1},r_{i+1,1})
\]
and insert the cross edges
\[
 (\ell_{i,0},r_{i+1,1})\quad\text{and}\quad
 (\ell_{i+1,1},r_{i,0}).
\]
Call the resulting graph $G_k$.  It is simple, connected, cubic and
bipartite, with $6k$ vertices and $9k$ edges.

Associate a Boolean variable $x_e$ with each edge.  For every vertex $v$,
include both clauses
\[
 \bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e,
 \qquad
 \bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e.
\]
Call the resulting formula $F_k$.  Every variable occurs twice positively
and twice negatively, every clause contains three distinct variables, and
distinct vertices have distinct incident-edge triples.  Thus $F_k\in\Htwo$
has $9k$ variables and $12k$ proper unique clauses.  Its incidence graph is
connected.

Put every cross-edge variable in $X$.  Among the internal edges of an end
block, put
\[
 (0,2),(1,2),(2,0),(2,1)
\]
in $X$ and the other four surviving internal edges in $Y$.  In every
interior block put
\[
 (1,2),(2,1),(2,2)
\]
in $X$ and its other four surviving internal edges in $Y$.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{CONNECTED-H22-COMPAT-RANK-GUARD1}]
For every $k\ge2$ the preceding connected $F_k\in\Htwo$ satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}H_{F_k;X,Y}
 \ge 13^2 8^{k-2}.
\]
The same lower bound holds over $\mathbb R$.  In the canonical indexed
encoding,
\[
 N_k=\Theta(k\log k),\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}H_{F_k;X,Y}=2^{\Theta(N_k/\log N_k)}.
\]
Every $F_k$ is satisfiable, and one uniform total exact 3-SAT decider can
recognize and accept this family in $O(N_k)$ time and $O(\log N_k)$ writable
space while using any total exact fallback off the family.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Restrict all $2(k-1)$ cross-edge variables to zero.  No remaining variable
then belongs to two different blocks, so the restricted truth matrix is the
Kronecker product of two end matrices and $k-2$ identical interior matrices.
Exact elimination over $\mathbb F_2$ gives end ranks $13,13$ and interior
rank $8$ for the displayed local splits.  Therefore this submatrix has rank
$13^2 8^{k-2}$.  A submatrix cannot have greater rank than its parent.
Moreover each nonzero $r\times r$ minor over $\mathbb F_2$ has an odd
integer determinant, hence is nonsingular over $\mathbb R$ as well.

There are $\Theta(k)$ fixed-width indexed records and each identifier uses
$\Theta(\log k)$ bits, so $N_k=\Theta(k\log k)$.  The lower bound is
$2^{3k+O(1)}$.  The full rank is at most $2^{9k}$, which supplies the matching
upper asymptotic exponent.

Finally, every cubic bipartite graph has a perfect matching: for
$S\subseteq L$, its $3|S|$ incident edges enter $N(S)$, whose vertices have
total degree at most $3|N(S)|$, so $|N(S)|\ge|S|$ and Hall's theorem applies.
Assign one to matching edges and zero to all other edges.  Each vertex sees
one one and two zeros, satisfying both paired clauses.  A canonical scanner
checks the fixed chain template and then returns SAT.  It needs only record,
block and identifier counters.  On a mismatch it invokes a fixed total exact
fallback.  The displayed family therefore has large compatibility rank but
does not make the one-bit execution evaluate, extract, communicate, or store
the compatibility matrix.
\end{proof}

The communication rank method still correctly gives a lower bound of

interface boundary; it does not become a central SAT time or memory lower

\section{Literature, barriers, and cross-domain transfer}

D\"ocker's primary paper supplies both the NP-complete ambient clean core
and the satisfiable paired special case \cite{doecker}.  Hall's theorem
supplies the matching existence proof.  Matrix rank is an
\textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER} to assignment-split deterministic
communication, but not to formula-input SAT execution \cite{mehlhorn}.
FLMV supplies unconditional SAT time--space lower bounds only after one
constructs a same-model evaluator inside its forbidden region
\cite{flmv}; this report constructs no such compiler.

The electronics/VLSI language of routing congestion and the distributed
language of cut communication are \textbf{USEFUL STRUCTURAL ANALOGIES} for
the cross edges, but require a new theorem that forces an unrestricted RAM
decider to realize that routing.  The control-theory term ``observability''
is likewise only an analogy: the one-bit output does not request the hidden
assignment state.  Tensor-product rank is exact algebra.  Schur squares,
quadratic relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies and collision entropy
are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} for the present claim because no codeword,
graded ideal or proof-to-node map occurs in the actual SAT execution.
LWE-style external extraction remains an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} needing
an unconditional worst-case, same-realization black-box theorem.

The counterexample and recognize-first wrapper relativize: an oracle can be
forwarded only to the off-family fallback.  Algebraic-oracle access can be
forwarded as well.  Thus this negative result does not cross the
relativization or algebrization barriers \cite{bgs,aw}.  Natural Proofs is
not the immediate hinge; no general-circuit lower bound is proved.
