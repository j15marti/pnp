\begin{abstract}
LB-0010 left open whether exact Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ balance blocks its
nonempty-pin decision reduction.  It does not.  This report gives a minimum
six-variable, eight-clause balanced component $H$ with a designated variable
$z$ such that $H\upharpoonright_{z=0}$ and
$H\upharpoonright_{z=1}$ are both satisfiable.  For every proper, unique,
monotone $(2,2)$ source $F$, the disjoint union $G(F)=F\wedge H$ stays in the
same class and either genuine pin returns the SAT bit of $F$, with linear
construction and encoded-size overhead.  Thus \texttt{BALANCED-PIN-LIFT1},
as literally stated, is proved, while balance alone is retired as a repair.
The price is explicit: $G(F)$ is disconnected.  A source-connected balanced
lift remains open.  A bounded audit passed 25,262 semantic, balance,
representation, wrapper, product, padding, and easy-family checks.  P versus
NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Clean-core family and separation-strength target}

Let $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ consist of CNFs whose clauses are unique monotone
triples of distinct variables and in which every variable occurs exactly
twice positively and twice negatively.  D\"ocker proves that its
satisfiability problem is NP-complete.  With $n$ variables and $m$ clauses,
$3m=4n$; in the canonical identifier encoding,
\[
 N=|\operatorname{enc}(F)|=\Theta(n\log n).                  \tag{2}
\]
The desired unrestricted lower bound remains
\[
\tag{UCORE-TIME1}
\forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}:
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0
 \ \wedge\ T_A(\operatorname{enc}(F))>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,
\]
where $A$ ranges over total uniform deterministic exact deciders.  This is
\OPEN and is not assumed below.

\section{Minimum balanced pin component}

Use variables $z,a,b,c,d,e$ and the four hyperedges
\[
 E=\{zab,zcd,ace,bde\}.                                     \tag{3}
\]
For every $uvw\in E$, include both $(u\vee v\vee w)$ and
$(\neg u\vee\neg v\vee\neg w)$; call the resulting eight-clause formula
$H$.  Each variable belongs to exactly two edges, so it occurs twice in
each sign.  The four edges are distinct and connected, hence $H$ is a
proper, unique member of $\mathcal H_{2,2}$ with connected incidence graph.
The assignment
\[
 (z,a,b,c,d,e)=(0,0,1,1,1,0)                               \tag{4}
\]
satisfies $H$, and its complement also satisfies $H$.  Therefore both
$H\upharpoonright_{z=0}$ and $H\upharpoonright_{z=1}$ are satisfiable.

This component is minimum by variable count.  Indeed the positive occurrence
count gives $3m_+=2n$, so $3$ divides $n$.  At $n=3$ there is only one
positive triple, while every variable would require two distinct positive
clauses.  Thus $n\ge6$, and (3) attains the bound.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{BALANCED-PIN-LIFT1}]
For fresh copies of the variables of $H$, define
\[
 G(F)=F\wedge H.                                            \tag{5}
\]
For every $F\in\mathcal H_{2,2}$ and either $v\in\{0,1\}$,
\[
 G(F)\in\mathcal H_{2,2},\qquad
 G(F)\upharpoonright_{z=v}\in\SAT
 \iff F\in\SAT.                                            \tag{6}
\]
The map is uniform, linear-time, and has encoded length
$|\operatorname{enc}(G(F))|=\Theta(N)$.  An evaluator with time $T_E$ and
space $S_E$ for the declared pinned coordinate yields a source decider with
conservative explicit-buffer bounds
\[
 T_B(N)\le O(N)+T_E(cN),\qquad
 S_B(N)\le O(N)+S_E(cN)                                    \tag{7}
\]
for a fixed encoding constant $c$.  Conversely any general SAT decider
evaluates the coordinate after a linear residual/proper-3-CNF compilation,
as in LB-0010.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Freshness makes the variable sets disjoint.  Formula $F$ and the fixed
component $H$ separately satisfy the exact positive and negative occurrence
conditions, so their union does too; no clauses collide.  For either pin,
\[
 \SAT(G(F)\upharpoonright_{z=v})
 =\SAT(F)\wedge\SAT(H\upharpoonright_{z=v})=\SAT(F),         \tag{8}
\]
where the final equality uses (4) or its complement.  A transducer copies
$F$, renames six fixed variables, and appends eight fixed clauses.  Rewriting
the canonical header and identifiers costs linear time and changes encoded
length by at most a constant factor, proving (7).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Balance is not the missing resource]
A lower bound for this unrestricted balanced one-pin service is already a
lower bound for the NP-complete source language.  Therefore exact balance
alone does not isolate a cheaper acquisition, extraction, state, memory,
communication, or time obligation.  Any repair must add a material promise,
such as global incidence connectivity or an independently justified
representation restriction, and must still prove an all-decider compiler.
\end{corollary}

The fixed component itself is connected, but (5) has at least two incidence
components.  This is the precise reason \texttt{CONNECTED-BALANCED-PIN-LIFT1}
is not claimed.

\section{Consequences under $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$}

Assume only here that $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ and let a uniform exact SAT
decider run in $N^{K+o(1)}$ time.  Construction (5) and the residual compiler
evaluate either pin in $N^{K+o(1)}$ time.  The unknown exponent $K$ remains.
Conversely, a favorable pin evaluator is already a source decider by (7).
Calling the fixed component a predecision resource neither removes $K$ nor
forces its materialization inside the original one-bit execution.  No
unconditional contradiction follows.

\section{Hostile controls and model audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{2-SAT, Horn, affine/Tseitin, bounded treewidth.}
The fixed component can be recognized and discarded before the appropriate
SCC, propagation, Gaussian-elimination, or path-DP solver runs on an easy
source.  The experiment separately charges recognition and the specialized
source computation.  Appending balanced syntax does not erase a semantic
shortcut.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products and padding.}
Further disjoint sources or further copies of $H$ preserve exact balance.
The one-bit answer is the conjunction of source answers; no cross-product
table is forced.
\item[] \textbf{Redundant clauses and encodings.}
Variable permutations, global complementation, reverse clause order, and
DIMACS serialization preserve the result.  A duplicate clause preserves
semantics but deliberately exits the unique-clause promise, so it is a
representation control rather than another clean-core member.
\item[] \textbf{Wrappers.}
DEAD tautologies, repeated COPY evaluation, DELAY, serialization/INTERP, and
RECOMP preserve both pin answers after their actual operations are charged.
No wrapper forces a retained service table.
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output.}
The service returns one bit.  Neither the $2^{|V|}$ assignment space nor all
possible pins is charged as explicit output.
\item[] \textbf{Connectivity.}
The construction exploits class closure under disjoint union.  Any theorem
requiring a connected or highly connected full incidence graph must state
that promise explicitly and re-establish NP-hardness, pin closure, and the
arbitrary-algorithm bridge.
\end{description}

\section{Literature and transfer audit}

D\"ocker supplies the exact NP-complete source definition and notes that the
paired positive/negative special case is satisfiable; the six-variable
component above is a direct instance.  His theorem does not supply a runtime
lower bound.  FLMV supplies simultaneous time--space exclusions only in its
declared random-access model and does not turn disjoint syntactic padding
into forced work.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Degree counting and disjoint union transfer exactly from the 3-uniform
hypergraph to clause occurrences.  Complement symmetry transfers exactly
from NAE-style paired clauses to both pin values.  Ordinary reduction
composition then gives (6)--(7).
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Connected-network, VLSI, cell-probe, memory-hierarchy, and communication-cut
bounds suggest strengthening the input promise and service model.  They need
a theorem mapping every static SAT implementation into that model.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Internal connectivity of $H$, exact degree balance, many possible pins, or a
restricted data-structure lower bound does not force the source computation
to acquire a compatibility object.
\end{description}

The construction relativizes: replace the source evaluator by an oracle
evaluator and (5)--(8) are unchanged.  It also survives low-degree/algebraic
oracle access, which only strengthens the evaluator.  Baker--Gill--Solovay
and Aaronson--Wigderson therefore remain active barrier warnings; the newer
Chen--Hu--Ren algebrization barriers supply no missing all-decider bridge.
No broad constructive circuit property is used, so Natural Proofs is not
directly invoked.

Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies,
graded Betti numbers, and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for
this barrier: neither side of (8) must instantiate them.  LWE-style
black-box extraction and special-soundness-style collision extraction remain
\textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE} pending an unconditional same-realization
compiler.  Hash/random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible.  Here the hidden
balanced structure is directly exploitable by recognition and removal.
