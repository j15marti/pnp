\begin{abstract}
TESTS-0003 proved a balanced nonempty-pin lift by disjointly appending a
fixed component, leaving global connectivity open.  This report shows that a
connected one-query lift is unnecessary for the proposed lower-bound route.
For every formula $F$ and every variable $z$,
\[
 \SAT(F)=\SAT(F\upharpoonright_{z=0})\vee
          \SAT(F\upharpoonright_{z=1}).
\]
Moreover, every exact $(2,2)$ formula decomposes into incidence-connected
exact $(2,2)$ components, and the sum of the $K$th powers of their encoded
lengths is at most the $K$th power of total length.  Hence a polynomial-time
evaluator for both genuine one-variable pins on connected clean-core inputs
gives a polynomial-time decider for arbitrary Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ with
no increase in the fixed polynomial exponent $K\ge1$.  Conversely, one pin
compiles linearly to ordinary proper 3-CNF.  Thus the connected two-valued
pin service is already separation-strength, not an easier EXTRACTOR/G2
resource.  A one-query connected balanced lift remains open as a construction
question but is retired as a necessary repair to this service route.  A
bounded audit passed $4{,}746$ checks.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Clean core and exact lower-bound target}

Let $\Htwo$ be the formulas whose clauses are unique monotone triples of
distinct variables and whose variables each occur exactly twice positively
and twice negatively.  D\"ocker proves $\Htwo$-SAT NP-complete.  With $n$
variables and $m$ clauses, $3m=4n$, and the canonical identifier encoding has
length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

The separation target remains
\[
\tag{UCORE-TIME1}
\forall A\ \forall d\ \forall N_0\ \exists F\in\Htwo:\quad
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\ge N_0\ \wedge\
 T_A(\operatorname{enc}(F))>|\operatorname{enc}(F)|^d,
\]
for total uniform deterministic exact deciders $A$.  It is \OPEN and is not
used as a premise.

\begin{definition}[Connected two-valued pin service]
The input is $(F,z,v)$, where $F\in\Htwo$ has connected clause-variable
incidence graph, $z\in V(F)$, and $v\in\{0,1\}$.  The required one-bit output
is $\mathbf1[F\upharpoonright_{z=v}\in\SAT]$.
\end{definition}

Every query is genuinely nonempty.  The service promises both possible
query values, not an explicit vector and not a retained data structure.

\section{Two-pin and component theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{CONNECTED-TWOPIN-EQUIVALENCE1}]
For every Boolean formula $F$ and every variable $z\in V(F)$,
\[
 \mathbf1[F\in\SAT]
 =\mathbf1[F\upharpoonright_{z=0}\in\SAT]\vee
  \mathbf1[F\upharpoonright_{z=1}\in\SAT].                \tag{2}
\]
If $F\in\Htwo$ and $F_1,\ldots,F_c$ are its incidence components, then each
$F_i$ is a connected member of $\Htwo$ and, for arbitrary
$z_i\in V(F_i)$,
\[
 \mathbf1[F\in\SAT]
 =\bigwedge_{i=1}^c
   \left(
    \mathbf1[F_i\upharpoonright_{z_i=0}\in\SAT]\vee
    \mathbf1[F_i\upharpoonright_{z_i=1}\in\SAT]
   \right).                                                \tag{3}
\]
The components and pins are found in $O(N)$ time and $O(N)$ explicit-buffer
workspace.

If a clocked evaluator $E$ for the connected pin service has
$T_E(L)\le C L^K$ for fixed $C$ and $K\ge1$, then the resulting source
decider has
\[
 T_B(N)\le O(N)+2C\sum_{i=1}^c L_i^K
          \le O(N)+2C\left(\sum_{i=1}^cL_i\right)^K
          =O(N^K),                                         \tag{4}
\]
where $L_i=|\operatorname{enc}(F_i)|$ and
$\sum_iL_i=O(N)$.  Thus the polynomial exponent $K$ is not increased.
The conservative peak-space bound is
\[
 S_B(N)\le O(N)+\max_i S_E(L_i).                           \tag{5}
\]
Conversely, a requested pin compiles in linear time and length to an
equisatisfiable proper 3-CNF, as in LB-0010.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every satisfying assignment of $F$ gives one of the two values to $z$ and
hence satisfies the corresponding restriction.  Conversely, any satisfying
assignment of either restriction extends with its declared value of $z$ to a
satisfying assignment of $F$.  This proves (2).

Variables shared by two clauses put those clauses in the same incidence
component.  Therefore every occurrence of a variable lies inside one
component, so exact two-positive/two-negative balance is inherited.  Clause
properness, monotonicity, and uniqueness are also inherited.  Disjoint
variable sets make satisfiability of the union exactly the conjunction of
the component answers.  Applying (2) inside each component gives (3).

A linear graph traversal finds all components.  Local canonical renaming and
headers add only linear aggregate encoding overhead.  For $K\ge1$ and
nonnegative $L_i$, $\sum_iL_i^K\le(\sum_iL_i)^K$, which proves (4).
Sequential evaluator calls give (5).  The converse residual compiler is the
linear construction proved in LB-0010.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No connectivity repair for the service route]
If the connected two-valued pin service is in P, then $\Htwo$-SAT is in P.
If $\Htwo$-SAT is not in P, then the service is not in P.  Consequently an
unconditional all-polynomial lower bound for that service is already a
$P\ne NP$ result; it is not an easier intermediate EXTRACTOR theorem.
\end{corollary}

The theorem does not turn two calls into one same-execution state and does
not prove the still-open one-query connected lift.  It instead shows that the
lift is unnecessary for class-level and fixed-exponent service equivalence.

\section{Connected balanced hostile family}

For $k\ge3$, let $G_k=C_k\square K_2$ be the cubic prism graph.  Create one
Boolean variable for every edge of $G_k$.  For every vertex, include the
positive clause on its three incident edge variables and the corresponding
negative clause.  Call the formula $P_k$.

Each edge belongs to two graph vertices, so its variable occurs exactly twice
in each sign.  The incidence graph is connected, clauses are proper and
unique, and $P_k\in\Htwo$.  Set every rung-edge variable to one and every
cycle-edge variable to zero.  Each vertex clause then has two zeros and one
one, so both its positive and negative clause are satisfied.  The complement
assignment is also satisfying.  Hence for every variable $z$,
\[
 \SAT(P_k\upharpoonright_{z=0})=
 \SAT(P_k\upharpoonright_{z=1})=1.                         \tag{6}
\]
Thus connectivity, exact balance, unbounded input size, and both genuine pin
values coexist with a direct constructive shortcut.  D\"ocker's paired-clause
theorem gives the broader satisfiability guard; the prism supplies an explicit
connected unbounded subfamily.

\section{Hostile-control and arbitrary-algorithm audit}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{Affine, Tseitin, Horn, 2-SAT, and bounded treewidth.}
The identity (2) is representation-independent.  The audit runs actual
$\mathbb F_2$ Gaussian elimination on random affine systems and both charge
parities of cycle-Tseitin systems; the other easy families may use
propagation, SCCs, or dynamic programming inside the two calls.  No
compatibility object is forced.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products.}
Equation (3) is the exact product rule.  It aggregates one-bit answers and
never materializes a Cartesian witness or state table.
\item[] \textbf{Alternative encodings, padding, and redundancy.}
Canonical renaming, global complementation with the pin value flipped,
clause ordering, duplicate clauses, tautological padding, and explicit
residual conversion preserve the identity.  Aggregate component length, not
raw identifier magnitude, is charged; padding therefore cannot create a
semantic obligation.
\item[] \textbf{Wrappers.}
DIRECT, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP may expose different traces but
return the same two bits.  DEAD padding is irrelevant after charged parsing.
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output.}
Each call returns one bit and there are exactly two calls per component.
No explicit restriction table, witness, compatibility graph, or quotient is
silently charged.
\item[] \textbf{Maximal semantic and algebraic oracles.}
Supplying either restriction answer for free only shortens the construction.
The theorem cannot establish same-execution exposure.
\end{description}

\section{$P=NP$, barriers, and transfer classification}

Assume only here that $P=NP$ and fix a clocked $\Htwo$-SAT or ordinary SAT
decider of exponent $K$.  It evaluates each pin after linear restriction
compilation; (4) then gives an $O(N^K)$ source decider.  This is fully
consistent with $P=NP$.  No exponent is removed and no contradiction follows.

The two-pin identity and component rule relativize verbatim.  Algebraic
oracle access only strengthens the evaluator.  Hence they identify a failed
intermediate route and do not cross the Baker--Gill--Solovay or
Aaronson--Wigderson barriers.  No broad constructive circuit property is
used, so Natural Proofs is not directly invoked.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE THEOREM.}
D\"ocker's exact definition and NP-completeness theorem justify the source
class; his paired-clause satisfiability result applies directly to the prism
control.  FLMV applies only after a compiler lands one machine inside its
declared simultaneous time-space region.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY / CANDIDATE.}
Connected-network, VLSI routing, communication-cut, cell-probe, knowledge-
compilation, and self-reduction results suggest restricted models, but an
all-decider same-realization compiler is still missing.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Physical or incidence connectivity does not prevent two-pin Shannon
decomposition.  A lower bound for a restricted connected representation is
not an unrestricted SAT lower bound without a model-preserving compiler.
\end{description}

The post-quantum lossiness lesson transfers exactly but negatively: the two
pin bits collapse to one source decision bit by OR.  Schur squares,
quadratic-relation spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies, and Betti numbers do
not obstruct this quotient and are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} here.  LWE-style
black-box extraction and special-soundness collision extraction remain
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; cryptographic or random-oracle binding is
inadmissible in an unconditional proof.
