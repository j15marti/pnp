\begin{abstract}
LB-0016 proves an exponential output-volume lower bound for one declared
algorithm: explicitly restrict a variable block in every possible way and
emit every residual.  This run tests the tempting pointwise promotion to
source-dependent pruned passes.  It fails on an already known paired-incidence
island.  For every cubic bipartite graph $G$, the paired positive/negative
incidence formula $F(G)$ is proper duplicate-free Monotone
$3$SAT-$(2,2)$.  A perfect matching selects one full restriction whose
residual is TRUE.  Recognition, matching, restriction construction and
verification are polynomial and fully charged, while the all-variable
all-branch pass has $2^n$ children.  The symbolic result is a corpus-derived
\EXACT{} corollary; the new contribution is the explicit pruned-pass
realization and cost audit.  It does not refute a worst-case theorem over all
clean cores and does not constrain an arbitrary SAT decider.  P versus NP
remains \OPEN.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact family, encoding and false promotion}

Let $G=(L,R,E)$ be a simple cubic bipartite graph.  Associate one Boolean
variable $x_e$ with each edge and define
\[
 F(G)=\bigwedge_{v\in L\cup R}
 \left[
   \left(\bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e\right)
   \wedge
   \left(\bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e\right)
 \right].
 \tag{1}
\]
Every variable occurs positively at its two endpoints and negatively at its
two endpoints.  Every clause has three distinct literals and is monotone.
When the three-edge neighborhoods are distinct, the clauses are
duplicate-free; the circulant family used below has this property.  Thus
$F(G)\in\Htwo$.  If $n=|E|$ and $N$ is the canonical indexed clause-list
length, then $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

LB-0016 considers the declared pass that chooses $S\subseteq[n]$ and emits
every residual $F\upharpoonright_\alpha$ for
$\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{S}$.  For $S=[n]$ it emits exactly $2^n$ constant
residuals.  Its theorem correctly charges that output volume.

The tempting pointwise promotion tested here was:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \forall F\in\Htwo\quad
 &\text{every exact source-dependent pass that replaces $F$ by strict}\\
 &\text{explicit restriction children must expose
 $2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$ children.}
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{2}
\]
Statement (2) is false.  Its quantifier over every $F$ is essential.  This
run does not falsify a worst-case statement
$\forall P\,\exists F$ about total passes on all of $\Htwo$.

\section{One-branch theorem}

\begin{theorem}[PAIRED-MATCHING-ONEBRANCH1 --- \EXACT]
There is a deterministic promise pass $P_{\rm match}$ such that for every
paired-incidence formula $F(G)$ of (1), where $G$ is cubic bipartite,
$P_{\rm match}$:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item recognizes the paired clauses and recovers $G$ in polynomial time;
\item computes a perfect matching $M$ of $G$ in polynomial time;
\item emits the single full restriction
\[
 \alpha_M(x_e)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e\in M;
 \]
\item produces the empty TRUE residual and verifies the assignment against
the source formula.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently its fanout on this family is one, even though the explicit
all-variable all-branch pass has $2^n=2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$ children.
On inputs outside the recognized promise, the tested pass returns UNKNOWN.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Pair each positive clause with the negative clause having the same set of
three variables.  Regard each pair as a vertex and each variable as an edge
between the two clause-pairs containing it.  Exact $(2,2)$ occurrence gives
two endpoints and the three-literal promise gives degree three.  Bipartite
coloring verifies the declared graph promise.

Every regular bipartite graph has a perfect matching.  Indeed, for
$S\subseteq L$, the $3|S|$ edges incident with $S$ end in $N(S)$, whose
vertices have total degree at most $3|N(S)|$.  Hence
$|N(S)|\ge |S|$, and Hall's theorem applies.  A standard bipartite
matching algorithm finds such an $M$ in polynomial time.

At each vertex exactly one incident matching edge is assigned one and two
nonmatching edges are assigned zero.  The positive clause sees the one and
the negative clause sees the two zeros.  Thus every clause is satisfied and
the full restriction simplifies to TRUE.  Recognition, matching, writing
$n$ assignment bits and source verification are all polynomial.  Only one
restriction is emitted.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Quantifier and SAME\_REALIZATION boundary.}
The theorem is about one explicit source pass on one recognizable promise
family.  Appending an arbitrary exact fallback makes a total exact SAT
decider, but gives no polynomial worst-case bound unless the fallback is
polynomial.  Under the counterfactual $P=NP$, a polynomial fallback exists,
so the recognize-first wrapper is polynomial; this is not a contradiction.
Requiring every arbitrary decider to run this recognizer or expose its
matching would violate SAME\_REALIZATION.

\section{Barrier-transfer and post-quantum audit}

The two load-bearing obstacles are now sharp:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item replace the pointwise claim by a worst-case all-input hereditary
source pass with a complete SAT/UNSAT coverage theorem and no current-scale
SAT fallback;
\item transfer that pass to one ordinary uniform machine in a
model-matched forbidden time--space region, with access, replay, failed
selection, output and writable memory charged.
\end{enumerate}

Primary literature was checked before the candidate was tested.
Döcker proves NP-completeness of the exact clean-core language and identifies
the always-satisfiable paired special case.  Hall's theorem and polynomial
bipartite matching transfer directly to the recovered graph; Hopcroft--Karp
is a standard explicit algorithmic endpoint.  FLMV is directly transferable
only after one ordinary random-access SAT machine satisfies its simultaneous
resource hypotheses.  Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson remain
barrier checks: the matching bypass and recognize-first wrapper relativize
and algebrize, so they cannot be the missing separation hinge.

\section{Counterfactual $P=NP$ audit}

Assume a fixed uniform deterministic SAT decider runs in time $N^K$ for some
unknown constant $K$.  The matching pass already runs in polynomial time on
the paired island and needs no SAT call.  Off the island, falling back to the
hypothetical decider gives time $N^{K+O(1)}$, which is fully consistent with
$P=NP$.  No unconditional theorem proved here forces this total machine into
an FLMV-forbidden region.  Claiming that every nonpaired or unrecognized
formula defeats all polynomial pruning would simply restate the missing
separation.
