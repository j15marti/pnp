## Result

- `SEMCOMP-DISJ1` — **PROVED/EXACT (scoped):** the proper Horn 3-CNF family
  `D_n = AND_i [(not x_i or not y_i or z_i) and (not x_i or not y_i or not z_i)]`
  has semantic compatibility `H_D(a,b)=1` iff `a & b = 0`. Its deterministic
  communication complexity is at least `n` by a `2^n` fooling set, although
  deciding satisfiability of each fixed formula is trivial (and Horn-SAT is
  linear-time). Equality 2-CNF gives the analogous separation.
- `SPLIT-TRACE-SIM1` — **PROVED/EXACT (scoped):** when the split pair `(x,y)`
  is itself the input to a deterministic centralized decider, parties can
  simulate it by communicating the owner-supplied bits read by the execution.
  Hence `Dcc(g) <= R_M+1` (or `w R_M+O(1)` for `w`-bit cells). This is a real
  transfer theorem, but the generic send-one-side protocol caps the resulting
  lower bound at linear input length and gives no central workspace lower
  bound.
- `COMPAT-COMM-IMPLIES-SAT1` — **FALSIFIED/RETIRED:** a hard compatibility
  matrix of a fixed formula does not force the one-bit SAT execution on that
  formula to receive, expose, store, or compute the compatibility arguments.
- `JOINT-SPLIT-RESOURCE1` — **OPEN:** the missing bridge is still a
  K-independent, predecision, same-realization compiler into a model with a
  superpolynomial unconditional endpoint, with all construction, access,
  replay, output, and peak-space costs charged. Ordinary deterministic
  communication complexity cannot provide that endpoint because of its
  linear communication ceiling.
