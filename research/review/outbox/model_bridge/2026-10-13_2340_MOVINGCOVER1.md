## Result and scope

Generation, runner/interpreter initialization, verification, reset, parameter storage and iterator state are charged. If the collection covers every input, the result is one uniform exact decider. This is not advice-based selection and does not assume the successful index is computable without trying the list.

A separate budget-interface assumption permits geometric restart when the winning time is unknown. No cheap universal simulator is silently assumed.
