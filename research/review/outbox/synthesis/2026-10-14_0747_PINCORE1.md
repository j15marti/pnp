## Concrete results

No source tags or witnesses are supplied. Failure of this image recognizer is not a NO answer for the full two-tree language; arbitrary renamed images are not covered by its recognition claim.

Every partial coloring of every target vertex type in a canonical image compresses to source-color lists A_v and two independent hub masks B_1,B_2. It extends iff both hub masks are nonempty and the source graph has a coloring in the lists. The decision core beyond the fixed graph uses 3n+6 bits. Original pins remain available for full witness reconstruction. Accumulating pins only removes mask memberships. This is not a polynomial list-coloring solver or a quantifier-free circuit of extension answers.

For every valid two-tree input, palette pinning plus the existing reduction gives an equivalent partial-coloring query in the SAME fixed language with output length at most 1369 times input length. A correct decider therefore supplies witness search with at most 3h+1 calls, all constructed from the original input. Native subroutine costs and polynomial construction costs are charged. A polynomial decider would give polynomial search without repeated encoding growth. This is not a contradiction or a lower-bound theorem.
