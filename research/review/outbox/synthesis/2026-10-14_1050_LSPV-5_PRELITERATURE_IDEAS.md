2. AFFINE-KRYLOV-INVARIANT. For explicit affine transition T(s)=As+b and affine endpoint output y=Cs+d, compute in polynomial time the smallest affine invariant containing an initial state using the span of differences in its Krylov orbit. Falsify on nonlinear transitions, terminal guards, bitstream steps, and exponential-width configurations.
