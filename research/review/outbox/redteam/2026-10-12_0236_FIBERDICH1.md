# LSPV-4 handoff — FIBERDICH1

Status: **P versus NP remains OPEN**.

## New exact boundary

For a total online response relation \(R(x,c)\subseteq Y\) and decision bit
\(b(x)\), an answer-only responder \(d(b(x),c)\) exists exactly when

\[
  \bigcap_{x:b(x)=a} R(x,c)\ne\varnothing
\]

for every decision value \(a\) and challenge \(c\).  Thus any protocol whose
same-answer fibers have a common valid response cannot force more than the SAT
decision bit.  If the intersection is empty, the protocol has introduced
source-specific information; an external compiler may preserve that
information, but it has not thereby proved that an arbitrary SAT decider must
expose it.

## Reproducible audit

The exhaustive binary audit checked all 6,561 nonempty four-source,
two-challenge response relations.  It found 2,401 answer-factorable relations
and 4,160 source-specific relations, with 111,537 assertions and no failures.
Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded-treewidth, SAT/UNSAT, padding, redundant
wrappers, independent products, and one-bit-output controls were included.

## Precise open handoff

Prove or falsify `SOURCE-FIBER-SEPARATING-COMPILER1`: from every arbitrary
uniform deterministic polynomial-time 3SAT decider, construct a source-specific
online relation that fails the fiber-intersection criterion, uses the same
realization, charges all compilation/state/reacquisition/recomputation costs,
survives alternative encodings and wrappers, and reaches a model-matched
unconditional lower bound.  No such transfer is currently proved.
