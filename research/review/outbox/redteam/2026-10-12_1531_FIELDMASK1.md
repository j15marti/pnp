## Exact result

`TRACE-FIELD-MASK-TRILEMMA1` is PROVED/EXACT for fixed coordinate-subset masks on ordered mutable trace events with fields `{kind, channel, address, value, state, output}`.

- Retain `value`: a same-schema COPY wrapper exposes arbitrary source bits.
- Drop `value`: two traces differing only in a designated derived compatibility-bit write collide.
- Retain only the SAT bit: `(x1)` and `(not x1)` are both SAT but have opposite compatibility under `x1=0`.

Therefore `FIELD-MASK-SUFFICES-FOR-INVARIANT-DERIVED-TRACE1` is FALSIFIED/RETIRED. This does not rule out a typed causal/noninterference quotient.
