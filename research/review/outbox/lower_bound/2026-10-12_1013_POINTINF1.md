## New exact result

For every decidable language \(L\), including SAT unconditionally,

\[
\underline T_L(N)=\inf_{A\in\mathsf{Dec}(L)}\max_{|x|=N}T_A(x)=O(N),
\qquad
\underline S_L(N)=O(1).
\]

For each fixed \(N\), a different total exact machine hardwires the complete
length-\(N\) truth table into exponential finite control and uses a baseline
decider on other lengths. Hence \(\forall N\exists A_N\) does not yield one
\(\exists A\forall N\) uniform polynomial-time decider.
