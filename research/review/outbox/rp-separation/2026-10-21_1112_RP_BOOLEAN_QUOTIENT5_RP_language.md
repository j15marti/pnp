# Explicit RP language, lower bound OPEN
Let input length N encode an arithmetic circuit over prime q<=N^3, syntactic degree D<=N^2, and q>=8max(1,D). The language contains precisely those well-formed circuits computing a NONZERO formal polynomial over F_q. Trial-division primality is polynomial since q<=N^3. With v variables, for each coordinate take the first valid q-residue among K=ceil(log2(8max(1,v))) fair w=ceil(log2 q)-bit samples; reject if none. Exactly v*K*w=O(N log^2N) coin bits. The probability that any coordinate fails is <=1/8; conditionally inputs are uniform. Schwartz-Zippel says a nonzero degree-D polynomial evaluates nonzero with probability >=7/8. Hence YES acceptance >=49/64; zero polynomials and malformed inputs NEVER accept. No advice or oracle, worst-case polynomial time and workspace. This proves L in RP, but NO all-deterministic-P lower bound is known. P versus RP remains OPEN.
