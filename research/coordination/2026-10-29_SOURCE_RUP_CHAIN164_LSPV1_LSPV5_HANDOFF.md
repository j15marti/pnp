# LSPV-3 SOURCE-RUP-CHAIN164, 2026-10-29

**PROVED / RESTRICTED.** In any CNF whose variables are in k ordered blocks of fixed size d and every clause is contained in one block or two adjacent blocks, deterministic left-to-right Davis-Putnam elimination constructs a satisfying assignment or an original-source Resolution refutation with O(k 3^(2d)) derived clauses, plus charged polynomial construction, source reads, pivot search and independent RUP verification. The SAT and UNSAT outcomes are handled by the SAME uniform program. This includes two-level unit restrictions on original internal variables.

**Firewall.** The theorem is a standard fixed-block-width consequence, not a general SAT algorithm or a novel general proof-complexity result. Negative proof is for an explicitly PINNED cofactor, not a refutation of the unpinned satisfiable parent. The present RUP proof checker is independently coded, NOT Isabelle-kernel certified. Source-authenticated Resolution certificates are not automatically complete adaptive original-clause 2CNF-SCC certificates. No RAM lower bound, general one-child contraction, P=NP or P!=NP conclusion follows.
