## Precise mathematical distinction

- Let N mean **binary input length** (not simply number of variables or clauses). Fix a single, uniform, total, both-label and malformed-aware deterministic SAT decision machine A_P. Its assumed **worst-case** bounds are T_P(N)=O(N^c polylog N), S_P(N)=O(N^s polylog N) on the **same** random-access machine used by the cited theorem. An assumed P algorithm may have any fixed c; **SAT in P does not grant c=6/5**, nor a small space exponent.
- Let A_NP denote a **nondeterministic** SAT machine, with accepting existential branches for SAT and no accepting branches for UNSAT, or explicitly a deterministic witness verifier with a separate certificate tape. Specify whether T_NP and S_NP are branchwise resource bounds, and do not misapply the **deterministic** FLMV bound to this different model. A standard polynomial-time witness verifier is already known; it does not decide UNSAT deterministically.
- For the historical FLMV/Fortnow-van Melkebeek tradeoff use the formula beta(c)=((c+2)/c^2-c)/2 in the range in which this particular positive threshold applies (beta(c)>0 for 1<=c<~1.52138); stronger or separate theorems apply in other regions. For any fixed c and s<beta(c), an appropriately modeled deterministic SAT decider cannot simultaneously have both stated upper bounds (up to the precise epsilon/o(1) conventions). **This supplies a necessary lower bound on space, not the exact S(N).** 2005 JACM FLMV: https://doi.org/10.1145/1101821.1101822 ; earlier formula explicitly stated in DIMACS: https://archive.dimacs.rutgers.edu/TechnicalReports/abstracts/2000/2000-06.html . Compare later improved tradeoffs and their exact machine models.
- Boundary control: beta(6/5)=23/45=1/2+1/90, so T=O(N^(6/5)), S=O(N^(1/2)) for SAME machine would contradict the theorem. But the pair T=O(N^2), S=O(N^2), or T=O(N^(6/5)), S=O(N) does not. Never infer P!=NP from the hypothetical assumption alone. c>=~1.52138 makes this **specific formula** nonpositive; that does not erase other time-space theorems.
- For **large N**, compute space thresholds symbolically and as clearly qualified illustrative numerical magnitudes for N=10^6,10^12,10^24,10^60 and optional N=2^k. For c=6/5, the exponent-only threshold ratio N^(23/45)/N^(1/2)=N^(1/90), at N=10^12 only ~1.36. These are space-*exponent* comparisons, **not concrete bytes/words or measured space**; constants, polylogs, and asymptotic quantified sizes matter.
- For a fixed bounded-time, bounded-space configuration space, distinguish local-state bit count, complete source+work-head+state memory, read-only input and nondeterministic witness tapes, number of configurations 2^{O(S log N)} (or justified alternative), time-indexed configurations, transcript length, live versus recomputable intermediate data and simultaneous **peak** space. Log(configuration count) cannot be charged as actual memory without a proven data-structure/operational necessity; state-count lower bounds do not imply S lower bounds without a sound injection/fooling-set argument. A large search set and an ideal offline proof DAG do not imply large machine memory.
