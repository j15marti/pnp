## What was attempted and established

Four falsifiers were fixed before searches: single-block negative certificates, signed exact-(2,2) two-level closure, cheap ordinary-SAT transfer, and universal-K all-child threshold. Search recovered that MINIMAL-UNSAT-PROJECTION20 had already shown a one-clause deletion collision; Role 5 CERTIFICATE-PRODUCT-CEILING109 already proves the AND/OR black-box decision-query result; Role 4 ARC-CIRCUIT-VOLUME-AUDIT124 and ARC-SIZE-SPECTRUM129 established weighted aggregate and histogram ledgers. The new integration must not rebrand those results as new.

An explicit fixed original W20 source from ARC184 is an UNSAT proper signed exact-(2,2) 3CNF, exhaustive model count 0 out of 2^15. Its SAT mate V flips the sign of x1 in source clauses 0 and 7, keeps the same absolute-support schedule and every signed quota, has 216 satisfying assignments and the all-zero witness. These are signed, not clause-monotone. For q disjoint source-renamed W/V modules, n=15q, m=20q, all binary source choices have identical physical encoded length, and SAT iff all q choices are V. Both literal SAT and UNSAT are preserved through grouping two modules into a first-level ordinary signed exact-(2,2) child, then splitting into genuine two 15-name grandchildren. The script serialized and reparsed every child.

The new exact quantifier firewall is that for ANY authenticated conjunction of pairwise variable-disjoint source blocks, a sound and complete UNSAT certificate relative to one fixed deterministic exact all-input guest A is a single block index and A's actual negative answer on that block. Every invalid index, malformed input, failed branch and guest step needs a uniform clock. Complementing each branch gives a co-nondeterministic SAT machine on the restricted PRODUCT language. A deterministic all-block route can require q hidden-label queries on the all-YES promise, but the source templates here are readily recognizable by a charged scan, so this is NOT an unrestricted SAT time lower bound.

For assumed fixed A with polynomial time exponent K>1, max block length N^lambda, fixed all-input authenticating parser N^{1+o(1)}, and virtual guest probe overhead N^u, the promised one-negative-branch has conditional time exponent max(1,u+lambda*K). A deterministic q=N^{1-lambda+o(1)}-block all-child coarse upper certificate with u=0 is 1+lambda*(K-1); this is NOT a time lower bound. Under the original van Melkebeek 2007 Theorem 1.3 the strict c frontier is H(K)=max(1+1/K,(2K-1)/(K(K-1))). The all-child coarse *proof of strict inequality* requires lambda<(H(K)-1)/(K-1), and for K>=2 reduces to lambda<1/(K(K-1)). A one-negative block of lambda=1/K instead has c=1 (when u=0), but only on its promised product language. K=10 gives one-neg c=1, all-child upper certificate c=1.9 versus H(10)=1.1. Neither gives a general ordinary SAT improvement without a separately charged fine-grained local reduction and without the separate deterministic small-space seed. P=NP only provides SOME unknown polynomial K and space exponent, not the near-boundary hypothesis K=901/500 with subpolynomial space.

Reproducer: 9,496 explicit assertions, 92 genuine signed-exact root products (6 SAT, 86 UNSAT), 868 actual first-level children, 1,736 grandchildren, zero discrepancies. q=2,4,8,16,32.
