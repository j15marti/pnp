## New precise restriction (PROVED/EXACT elementary; independent review pending)
Let the tagged fixed FLMV-hard parent be Rhat(x) of length N=n polylog(n), x in {0,1}^n. If a purported single *standalone*, ordinary-input child C(x) has a decoder D(C(x))=x for EVERY n-bit x, then C is injective, requiring worst-case child length at least n bits. Since n=N^(1-o(1)), no uniform fixed lambda<1 child length bound N^(lambda+o(1)) is possible. A compact child that still has external parent access is NOT standalone: account for every source read, index, ancestor, virtual backing buffer and replay in the matched machine.

A *joint* lossless bundle of at most q children each of length at most m, plus metadata b and framing, requires maximum total transcript length >=n by injectivity. Explicit accounting:
n <= b + q (m+O(log(m+2))) + O(log(q+2)).
If q<=N^a, m<=N^lambda, b<=N^rho with a+lambda<1 and rho<1, the claimed lossless bundle is impossible. But at the illustrative ARC pair a=3/5, lambda=1/2, a+lambda=11/10, this accounting does NOT rule out contraction. Partitioning x into many smaller chunks is a lossless syntactic positive control but offers no SAT semantics.
