## New narrow exact theorem, independent review pending

For a single reused semantic quotient h, the quotient-based SAT-status join is correct for **both labels after every consistent partial boundary pin simultaneously applied to both modules** iff for all u in P, v in R with h(u)=h(v), the true intersection P ∩ R contains some witness w that agrees with u and v on every coordinate where u and v already agree. Necessity: pin exactly those agreeing coordinates. Sufficiency: every future shared pin is among them, so the true compatible witness survives. This is a semantic criterion, not an efficient method for acquiring P, R or finding w. It is a source-name-specific specialization of ARC-CONTEXT-CONFLICT119's declared-context graph, not an all-input algorithm.
