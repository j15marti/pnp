## Exact restricted theorem
For a verified binary source-owned CNF join tree, let `s_t` be the number of explicit Boolean bits in the exact full parent-separator table, `M_t` the minimum table/join-buffer bit peak required to evaluate and retain that output, and `K_t` the independent parent join workspace. With indivisible subtree executions, no reuse of a child buffer, no recomputation, and an order-independent join, selecting the child of larger `M_child - s_child` first minimizes peak table bits. Proof is an elementary two-order exchange inequality, followed by induction. This is the weighted Sethi-Ullman principle, not a general SAT lower bound or new scheduling complexity class.

## Counterexample / frontier
