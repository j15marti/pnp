## New exact restricted arrow, independent mathematical peer review pending

Let D(Y,U), R(Y,V) be two **fixed** ordinary CNF modules, with precisely matched original boundary name vector Y, disjoint private names, and projected feasible supports P,Q. Let h be one fixed reusable quotient. For every **common appended ordinary proper-3CNF continuation filter** C(Y,W) on the same Y with fresh private W, exact true join accepts iff P∩Q∩S_C ≠∅ while the coarsened quotient accepts iff h(P∩S_C)∩h(Q∩S_C)≠∅.

**PROVED/EXACT restricted iff**: equality of these two labels for every such C is equivalent to

    for every u in P\Q and every v in Q\P, h(u) != h(v).

Necessity is witnessed by the completely ordinary proper-3CNF selector for S_C={u,v}: fresh selector b, each source coordinate i gets (b OR L_i(u)) AND (NOT b OR L_i(v)), each binary clause replaced by two proper ternary clauses with fresh auxiliary. Exactly 4k ternary clauses and 2k+1 fresh private names, fully charged identifiers/headers/body. Sufficiency is elementary: a same-label surviving quotient pair must have at least one member already in P∩Q, because every cross-exclusive collision is forbidden. This result has different quantifiers from previous **ARC-PIN-HEREDITY120** (all consistent coordinate pins only) and **ARC-FIBER-HEREDITY117** (fixed prefix against arbitrary *opposing* suffix), and does **not** imply that finding h is cheap.
