# ARC235 role-safe handoff, 2026-10-29 17:15 PDT

**Formal proof lead, LSPV-1.** The missing lemma was whether paid polynomial discovery of at-most-two-variable primal separators extends the earlier block-cut mixed 2CNF+XOR solver to a class with unbounded primal width and global affine rank. A deterministic exhaustive zero/one/two-cut procedure constructs a source-owner-verified join tree. On every leaf where genuine affine row rank is at most one, an exact parent/child message has arity at most two and therefore a 2CNF encoding using at most four exclusion clauses. Original source-tagged arity-zero/one/two affine equations are normalized to exact 2CNF. This gives a uniform, polynomial time, deterministic restricted SAT/UNSAT solver with explicit model reconstruction and exact replayable NO tables. The result is a restricted constructive theorem, not a general SAT algorithm, and the composition is a specialization of prior project work, not a novel replacement for ARC232.

**Independent computational controls.** Exhaustive one-parity audit: 3,584 cases, 1,482 SAT, 2,102 UNSAT, zero failures. **Mathematical frontier.** With connected strict signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF static structural assumptions, n<=3b+12bq is necessary but not a runtime lower bound. Its illustrative T=2^(b+q) and S=2^b exponent proxies have b*=sqrt(n/12), first equality at (n/3,0), and, at n=1200, integer min 20 at b=9/10/11. No decomposition realizability is implied. In the different typed clique-chain class, N=Theta(q^3 log q), a deliberately conservative RAM implementation satisfies T=O(q^8 polylog q), S=O(q^4 polylog q), hence upper exponents <=8/3 and <=4/3. These resource pairs and Python measurements are NOT fixed one- or two-tape measurements.
