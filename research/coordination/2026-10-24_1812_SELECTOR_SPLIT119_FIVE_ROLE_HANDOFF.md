## Exact recovered/constructed link

For an arbitrary proper 3-CNF F with n original variables and m clauses, create occurrence-selector bits y_(i,j), one independent parity-one equation on each clause triple, three at-most-one binary clauses per clause, and one binary implication (y_(i,j) => original signed literal) per occurrence. The affine system A has m independent rows. The 2CNF system B has 6m binary clauses and 3m+n source/selector bits. Both A and B are individually SAT for **every** F (A choose first selector per clause; B all-zero selectors). Their joint SAT condition is exactly original F's SAT condition. A single selected true literal is a witness; original clauses can have many true literals. This is an explicit linear-record reduction to the known affine-constrained 2SAT setting of Arvind and Guruswami, not a novel NP-completeness claim.

Splitting into the 7m individual atomic constraints gives children of encoded size O(log(n+m)), each SAT independently. Therefore no assembler that receives **only their SAT bits** (and fixed input-independent metadata) can distinguish an opposite-label parent pair. A general source-aware assembler may still decide the original source by any means; such decision, source rereading, gluing, alternate representations and all costs must be charged. This is a semantic obstruction to one invalid MULTICHANNEL/ARC promotion, **not** an all-decider lower bound.
