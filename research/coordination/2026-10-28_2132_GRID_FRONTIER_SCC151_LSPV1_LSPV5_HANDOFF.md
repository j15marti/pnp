The certificate has O(log N) encoded bits for N=Theta(K log K), but its deterministic discovery does NOT have logarithmic source-read cost. This *falsifies as a general inference* that all-order large syntactic frontiers or large global hitting backdoors force costly adaptive UNSAT certificate acquisition.
