## Scoped theorem: fully charged persistent guard saturation
Fix a polynomially acquired CNF chart F on r original-coordinate-mapped Boolean variables, with L total literal occurrences, fixed width w, and guard dictionary G all consistent literal conjunctions of width <=w (including empty), Q=sum_{i<=w}2^i binom(r,i). Normalize tautologies and duplicates. For each guard, retain its unit-propagation fixed point or a recorded conflict, with chronological reasons. For each globally admitted new verified clause (at most K<=Q for width w), insert it into all live contexts. A clause satisfied or with >=2 unassigned literals at insertion cannot create a new unit/conflict immediately. Otherwise enqueue unit or mark conflict and propagate through indexed incident clauses. A live context extends its previous closure, never retracts an assigned literal, until contradiction. Induction on UP derivation lengths establishes equality to fresh UP closure whenever consistent, and same conflict status otherwise. Soundness: each propagation reason is a current source or verified learned clause under exact guard; each conflicting guard yields its negated disjunction by RUP. Fairly insert every newly refuted guard's negation once; deterministic lex order queue, fixed chart, no deletions or guard creation. Monotonicity means each conflict remains a conflict. Once no new insertions and all queues empty, no width-w RUP candidate remains unadmitted: it is exactly the least bounded-width RUP fixed point, independent of processing order. Sound on YES; on NO may stall. 
Analytical implementation (not the full-scan measured runner): per guard, at insertion evaluate each clause once, maintain satisfied/unassigned counts and XOR of unassigned literal IDs (valid because distinct literals), index each literal occurrence, update it at most once on assignment of its variable. Every variable assigned once per context. Hence O(Q*(L+Kw+r)) word events for propagation and insertion; per-context O(L+K+r) counters/reasons plus guard table, total O(Q*(L+K+r)) words plus shared proof/candidate metadata. Assignments/IDs/counters O(log(L+Q+r)) bits in explicit chart. Deterministic array indexing with polynomial bit-model simulation, or explicit indexed tape implementation with polynomial overhead, keeps fixed polynomial original-N cost provided chart/map acquisition and any external proofs are paid polynomial. Conflict reason extraction at most polynomial per context; output explicit RUP proof chronological, independently verify (polynomial in total output and source length). A fixed guard cannot reset its credit: preallocate one insertion plus one assignment-update per possible occurrence in each context, one assignment per variable and one retirement per guard; charge all events by integer decrease, plus fixed per-event checking and bounded external admission allowances. No credits for rollback, arbitrary clause deletion, chart reset or newly unbounded guard dictionary. This is a scoped accounting theorem (known BCP principles); not new proof strength and not an all-NO deadline. Measured delta runner rescans on activation, so stronger event bound not a measured performance claim. Early closure and partner CEC/TETRA progressive restoration are complementary instantiations. Falsifiers: malformed/incorrect guard maps, unsupported learned clause, clause removal or guard weakening (monotonicity fails), width growth (Q no longer polynomial). First OPEN arrow: universal polynomially acquired nonredundant obstruction or complementary terminal at a fully saturated fixed chart. Arbitrary branching requires separate credit. Dovetail with other spines, do not let this polynomial but expensive lane starve early terminals.
