## Exact result

Let `V` be the set of *distinct nonzero* vectors `v_i` for `i in Z`. Then these are equivalent for consistent `A`: (1) `V` is linearly independent; (2) the projection of `A` onto `Z` is definable by unary/binary parity constraints; (3) that affine projection is majority-closed. The proof uses surjective affine reparameterization in one direction, and a minimal dependency of size at least three producing a non-majority-closed parity hyperplane in the other.
