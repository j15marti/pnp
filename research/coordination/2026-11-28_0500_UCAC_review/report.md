## Constructive bridge: remove the decomposition-as-advice assumption within a narrower class
For exact disjoint explicit response dictionaries S_i in F2^r, shift a chosen anchor from each into the target and obtain centered local direction spans U_i. Optionally quotient by H=sum_i H_i, where H_i consists of translation periods of the entire centered dictionary of block i. Discover H_i by checking each candidate h in S_i (0 in S_i) for S_i+h=S_i; at most |S_i|^2 membership checks per block, with exact sorted vectors or deterministic search, then GE. If quotient options sum to pi(b), the discrepancy in H is decomposed by tagged GE as sum h_i, h_i in H_i; add h_i to the chosen original representative in each block. Exact witness lift, no semantic oracle. This is prior peer period machinery, not a novelty claim.

On the quotient spans define f(P)=rank(sum_{i in P} pi(U_i))+rank(sum_{i not in P} pi(U_i))-rank(sum_i pi(U_i)). This is a normalized nonnegative integer symmetric submodular connectivity function: rank of a union of assigned local generator sets is submodular (intersection of their spans contains the span of their common blocks); complement preserves submodularity. A direct GF2 rank oracle costs polynomial in explicit chart length L, e.g. O(L^3) bit operations conservatively; no assigned decomposition needed for this oracle.

Korhonen and Oum, "Branch-width of connectivity functions is fixed-parameter tractable", arXiv:2601.04756 (2026-01-08), state an algorithm finding a branch-decomposition of width at most k or rejecting in 2^{O(k^2)} gamma m^6 log m time, gamma cost of oracle, m blocks. Conditional on this published preprint theorem as stated (not independently implemented or kernel-audited here), for polynomially acquired exact dictionaries of length L<=N^a and k<=floor(c sqrt(log2 N)) with fixed c, paid acquisition is polynomial in original N, including rank oracle. Apply exact target-conditioned tree DP: at node P retain actual partial sums x with b+x in U_complement. At most 2^{f(P)} such values; combine children by checking sum modulo outside span. Every actual global witness survives; conversely every root row is composed of valid local options. Backpointers give lift; cost O(m 4^k poly(L)), storage O(m 2^k poly(L)). Reconstruct quotient shifts and native witness via certified source map. Thus a **uniform recognition-and-decision module** for the square-root-log branchwidth promise, with polynomial discovery and both labels, contingent on paid chart construction and external theorem audit. High width or clock exhaustion is UNKNOWN, not NO. Mere O(log N) branchwidth is insufficient for this particular acquisition bound: 2^{O(log^2 N)} is quasi-polynomial. A certified provided logarithmic-width tree is still sufficient for the DP itself. Earlier Hlineny/Oum partitioned-matroid FPT work (doi:10.1137/070685920) also does not imply a polynomial clock at logarithmic k without explicit parameter dependence. Kashyap doi:10.1137/070691152 establishes general trellis ordering NP-hard; do not demand optimal order for free. No inference of universal low width or P=NP.
