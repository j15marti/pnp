## Exact typed transformation

For a paid two-input gate `z=f(a,b)`, a gauge `delta` fixing every original input replaces the four-bit table by

$$
f^{\delta}(s,t)=f(s\oplus\delta(a),t\oplus\delta(b))\oplus\delta(z).
$$

Every positional fixed-DEF facet reference `j=2u+v` must change to

$$
\boxed{j^\delta=2(u\oplus\delta(a))+(v\oplus\delta(b)).}
$$

Every RES signed clause flips occurrences of complemented auxiliary variables; RES remains the same **oriented** pivot rule. A GF(2) row `mask M, rhs r` transforms its RHS to `r XOR XOR_{x in M} delta(x)`. Currently exported AND3/LOCAL supports in CANON2 are original-only, hence their factor tables and all legal annotations remain unchanged. External source bytes, source IDs/hash, paid node IDs, parent DAG topology and terminal contradiction are identical. The transformation is an involution.

**Proved theorem:** Every accepted CANON2 proof with `q` paid unit nodes and `E` serialized bits has a source-identical accepted transformed proof with precisely `q` nodes and `O(E)` output bits. Transforming an explicitly supplied certificate has polynomial bit time and buffered `O(N+E)` peak space, with all external source reads, writes and verification charged separately. This is **proof-to-proof normalization**, not a B3 size bound.
