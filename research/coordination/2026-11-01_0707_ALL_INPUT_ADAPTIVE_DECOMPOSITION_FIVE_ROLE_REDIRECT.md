## Formal all-input challenge: what must actually be proved

Let `L_D` be **all valid** duplicate-free proper Döcker Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) originals, with every original clause three distinct same-sign literals, each variable occurring exactly twice positively and twice negatively, and malformed binary inputs rejected. For each source F in L_D, build a coherent **source-indexed** residual state R=(F,p,certificates,environment,ledger), where p assigns original variables, retained provenance/assumptions are typed, and all costs are observable.

Seek an effective, **uniform**, algorithmically computable state-dependent parameterized selector `D` which, on every legal state reachable from **every** legal source, chooses among exact certified actions: signed-spill/defect bounds, edge-disjoint odd-cycle `delta=2f-u-eta-nu=0` affine union with all cycle edge choices, authenticated fractional odd-cycle/K4 cuts, matroid-component factoring, Horn/2SAT/XOR closures, exact AND independent-components / OR assignment branches, typed certificate DAG/reuse, or an exact fallback. Each action must either:

1. Return SAT with a fully checked original-clause witness, or UNSAT with a complete original-source authenticated UNSAT proof (including exhausted leaf cover); or
2. Construct a finite, exactly sound/exhaustive family of **properly encoded canonical target-valid** recursive children, or provide a separately proved sound low-overhead simulation of noncanonical children; or
3. Record UNRESOLVED, with the failed work charged, and proceed to an exact route.

**Decisive requirement:** prove a nontrivial ***all-input quantitative bound***. Merely saying “one policy works” existentially, a fixed collection of tractable cases, or completeness of exponential DPLL is NOT a theorem about polynomial complexity. Specify the total-work recurrence, number of children, sizes of descendants, selection/acquisition time, space retained across branches, proof output and peak live memory; solve it for every legal source, not only favorable states. Any potential/measure must be justified under verified `delta:0 -> 1` increases, changing cycle selection, semantic DAG merges and policy restarts.

Keep logically separate the two possible proof routes:

- **Constructive P=NP route:** prove fully uniform polynomial *total* time and space for the entire target language, with both SAT and UNSAT certified correctly, including malformed input rejection, all discovery and failed decisions. Since the target is NP-complete, an actual polynomial-time decider would imply P=NP.
- **ARC/P!=NP contradiction route:** for **arbitrary** correct polynomial target decider A with unknown exponents (K,L), prove an all-input power-short hereditary transformation `A -> Phi(A)` with paid source/index/guest/output/ancestor costs and a strict uniform contraction in both required resources. **Separately** prove a numerically exact, local-bit, same-machine SAT-to-Döcker reduction or direct target lower bound that places the contracted decider inside the actual FLMV forbidden region. NP-completeness alone gives NO quantitative transfer. A complete proof of the stated contraction and reduction requirements would need separate verification before any separation conclusion.

Unmeasured Python word-time or a `poly(N)` workspace for GE/matroid operations cannot stand in for FLMV `N^(6/5)` time and `N^(1/2)` space. Source input grammar, queries, index tape and jumps, output tape, matrix/proof memory, policy switching, failed options, caches, guest DELAY/COPY/RECOMP, and *simultaneous* peak space must be included.
