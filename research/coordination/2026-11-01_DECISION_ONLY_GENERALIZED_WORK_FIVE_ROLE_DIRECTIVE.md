## Core change: do not require value recovery

The older CUBEKEY306 proved a genuine but **restricted representation tariff**: among 84^t distinct syntactic coherent pinned-variable states, a self-contained *injective lossless key* requires at least ceil(t log2(84)) bits to recover the exact pin names/values. This does **NOT** imply the SAT-decision computation must store such a key or perform 84^t steps. A Boolean decider may discard the pins, use an exact smaller semantic state, or directly decide the answer.

Study instead **actual generalized work**, with an explicitly declared deterministic indexed read-only-input model:
```
W_D(F) = W_grammar + W_source/index/head + W_discover
       + W_select/switch/failed + W_deduce/GE/matroid/proof
       + W_cache/replay + W_child/output/access + W_guest.
S_peak(F) = max_t combined simultaneously live writable/index/guest/ancestor/
            cache/matrix/proof/output-tape memory.
```
A completed decision has a one-bit output, but **obtaining** it is not free. No oracle may provide the correct semantic quotient, best policy, minimal boundary relation, exact SAT label or instance-specific compressor without charging discovery/computation.

The user requests either a constructive **polynomial total work** bound on EVERY valid duplicate-free proper Döcker Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) input, SAT and UNSAT (malformed strings reject), or a genuinely **generalized lower bound** for the all-input arbitrary-decider ARC route, plus an independently valid model-matched numeric FLMV transfer. The previously required all-input typed soundness, residual authentication, exact fallback, canonical-successor or charged re-encoder, unknown-K/DELAY/COPY/RECOMP tests remain active. This does NOT magically yield a superpolynomial lower bound.

### Known decision-only adversary (not new SAT hardness)

For any input F and opposite-label allowed G, let D(F,G) be the physical input-bit differences. On F an exact deterministic adaptive bit-query decider must inspect a set hitting each D(F,G), or else runs on F and G are identical. The minimum hitting-set cardinality is a sound **query lower bound**, with NO state-recovery assumption.

The same-original connected target G_t with coherent A and B pin configurations has WA=[[1,1],[0,0]] and WB=0. All A has precisely one extension; any one B block has none. These t opposite-label inputs differ in pairwise disjoint two-bit pin-value positions, giving an exact necessary t pin queries and a matching O(t) promise decision algorithm. Hence exponentially many syntactic states produce only **linear necessary decision work** in this control. Since an N-bit input can always be read in N queries, bit-probe complexity *alone* cannot prove superpolynomial running time. Do not misinterpret a promise problem over pinned residuals as the original-language all-input lower bound.

### Distinguish three equivalences

1. **Current-instance decision equivalence:** same current Boolean label. This may have only two classes and gives no free method for finding the class.
2. **Compositional continuation equivalence:** same responses under every permitted future boundary context/plug-in, with original-clause provenance. Exact boundary relations or verified transfer matrices can suffice on paid bounded-interface families.
3. **Lossless syntactic equivalence:** same original pinned names/values, useful for replay but unnecessary for decision-only complexity.

Any semantic memoization claim must declare which is needed and the cost of acquiring/checking the representation. A bounded-width transfer matrix that collapses many states is a legitimate hostile test, not evidence against decision-only algorithms.
