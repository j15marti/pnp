## Verified new restricted result

The fixed read-only source supplies original m data bits and pre-materialized triple-parity bits, each addressed through a one-bit indexed probe. Each probe is a measurement row of weight 1 or 3; all source bits, including original bits, are retained and permitted. Target is one publicly known parity row t, not its answer. Every terminating deterministic exact adaptive execution must query rows whose GF(2) span contains t. Otherwise input theta and theta+h on an orthogonal kernel vector are indistinguishable but have opposite targets. Hence optimal worst-case probe depth is the shortest XOR representation of t using the allowed probe menu, although finding that representation can be computationally hard.

For target all ones over 3q variables, the optimal policy uses at most q probes if and only if the triple family has an exact cover: each of q weight-at-most-three observed rows must be a disjoint triple covering all 3q positions. This is NP-complete policy discovery by classical X3C/coding incidence reduction, not NP-hardness of evaluating a fixed policy, not a novel incidence reduction, and not an all-algorithm SAT bound.

The independently defined uncovered-target-support potential Gamma drops at most 3 per charged primitive probe and necessarily reaches zero for every exact decision, giving total preparation-plus-continuation source probes at least ceil(weight(t)/3). Its zero value alone is not sufficient for a correct answer. The independently defined target-uncertainty Phi is the indicator that t is absent from the observed row span, also uniform-prior conditional entropy, but is not optimal running time.

For disjoint triple modules, k_j target-one coordinates in block j give the exact adaptive optimum sum_j min(k_j,4-k_j). A polynomial metadata recognizer and deterministic uniform policy attain it. Synergy: two disjoint triple shares of a six-bit target have value gains (0,0,0,1); duplicates have zero marginal; net acquisition benefits can be negative. Score evaluation, authentication and strategy selection are separate charged costs.
