## Cooperative proof architecture

The constructive chain is: authenticated NP-complete input -> exact residual/deduction semantics -> polynomially acquired terminal or productive step -> fully charged aggregate bound -> correct decision on every input. C01 owns the coverage and solver integration; C02 owns the global cost argument; C03-C08 supply distinct structural and semantic modules. Existence of a repair, short path, useful projection, small representation or decomposition is separate from its discovery cost.

The lower-bound chain is: source-sensitive decision family -> independently justified invariant or forbidden representation -> faithful all-operation model transfer -> exclusion of every fixed polynomial cap. L01 owns arithmetic-to-Boolean transfer; L02 owns the restricted geometric/interface theorem; L03 owns arbitrary-decider coverage; L04 owns resource/diagonalization transfer; L05 owns actual source families and reductions. A restricted result can be valuable without being promoted to an unrestricted consequence.

X01 independently audits both chains and maintains the common claim/dependency and source ledgers under its own namespace. It records the first open implication in each chain and assigns repair handoffs without changing other agents' saved settings. A result is not independently audited merely because another agent repeats it.

At an obstruction, use at least two substantively different approaches, for example exact construction versus minimal-counterexample contradiction, matching/flow versus affine analysis, geometric decomposition versus an implicit semantic operation, or lower-bound invariant versus an explicit counter-algorithm. Compare on the identical source instance and guard. Share a certificate or corrected lemma rather than an unsupported conclusion. If direct live-agent communication is unavailable, use readback-verified owned handoffs and the established coordination issue.

For parallel track coverage, require one common fixed polynomial cap, polynomial acquisition of the candidate track set, sound completion certificates and a fully charged schedule. Per-instance exponents, free creation of exponentially many tracks, and uncharged certificate discovery do not give a polynomial algorithm. Logical disjunction of track successes is not their ordinary arithmetic sum without an exact overlap rule.

A contradiction proof must identify the assumed statement, every derived premise, the explicit incompatible pair, and the scope of each simulation or theorem used. Failure of a proposed solver is not a proof of P!=NP; failure of a lower-bound attempt is not a proof of P=NP. Keep lower bounds for arithmetic circuits, Boolean circuits, proof systems, compilations and uniform machines distinct.

## Completion criteria for the overall project

For P=NP, establish one correct uniform polynomial-time decider for a genuinely NP-complete decision language with its whole construction and worst-case bit cost accounted for. A sufficient SAT-side algorithm must have a proved uniform polynomial bound and a sound way to decide NO within that bound.

For P!=NP, establish a complete unrestricted implication excluding all fixed polynomial-time caps with exact quantifiers, authentic inputs and valid model transfers. A fixed-exponent bound or restricted representation barrier is insufficient.

Until one complete chain passes independent audit, both outcomes remain open. This coordination document claims no mathematical resolution.
