## Result and exact logical boundary

P_H's default is ordinary Resolution plus a mandatory charged FINAL terminal, and its special one-node terminal checks an arbitrarily long explicit universal-TM halting transcript only for a syntactically contradictory family F_e. Then W_PH(F_e)=1 iff U(e) halts, otherwise 2. The shortest proof exists but optimizing node count would decide halting. All source and transcript bit reads and validation may be enormous even though special logical cost is one.

**Positive complement:** For any fixed sound complete effective refutation system R admitting a total computable bit-cap b(N,q) such that every cost-<=q proof can be replaced by an equivalent full proof of <=b(N,q) bits, exact budget feasibility, W_R(F) on UNSAT, and Wbar_R(N) are **computable** by finite enumeration. If b and the verifier are polynomial, unary-budget proof feasibility is NP (existing LSPV-1/364), but neither polynomial existence nor B3 follows.
