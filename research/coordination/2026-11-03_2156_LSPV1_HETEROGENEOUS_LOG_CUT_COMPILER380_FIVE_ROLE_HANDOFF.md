**New scoped lemma:** For any signed conservative AND2 acyclic graph with B previously authenticated boundary wires, r complete public output references and syntactic fan-in frontier S_j for output j of size <= b, an all-assignments joint-equivalent replacement bank uses <= sum_j |S_j|*2^|S_j| <= r*b*2^b paid conservative gates, plus at most r paid aliases if the grammar requires them. The sets S_j can differ and their union can be arbitrarily large. The proof constructs exact per-output DNF using paid signed-AND2 and De Morgan OR; the entire output vector is preserved simultaneously under any correlation between boundary wires. This is valid only for downstream extensional use and consistent legal aliasing, never hidden source-semantic inference. Under b<=c log2(L+2), where L is the ENTIRE serialized original circuit (not CNF length N or q paid nodes), the bank and an explicit uniform compiler are polynomial in L; one safe all-phase bound is T=O((r*2^b+1)(L+Q+2)^3), S=O(L+Q+2) with Q=O(r*b*2^b*log(L+r*b*2^b+2)). No polynomial-in-(N+q) conclusion if opaque payload length L is exponential.

**Counterexample to raw-DAG invariance:** Paid chain z1=u AND u, z_{i+1}=z_i AND u of arbitrary t private gates still outputs u, requiring zero gates after extensional alias normalization. Root-only quotient is invalid when an exposed second wire is v versus NOT v. The logarithmic heterogeneous-cut lemma is a strict extension of LSPV-1/379's fixed common b, not a better implementation of LSPV-2/379's 783-gate universal three-wire bank.
