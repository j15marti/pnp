## Actual counterexample and reproducibility

Pad with independent positive ternary clauses to ordinary encoded length N^(1/2+o(1)). Applying the same procedure a second time gives a real ordinary grandchild of length N^(1/4+o(1)). Scoped width-one stream is trivial after A(F). This does **not** bypass the first parent-scale solver call.
