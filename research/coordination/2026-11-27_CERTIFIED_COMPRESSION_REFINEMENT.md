## Soundness and useful hybrid interfaces
* Lossless: keep exact source semantics, decoder/lift, or independently checkable equivalence. A dictionary hit or short hash alone is only a candidate. Preserve occurrence-specific mappings and all joint dependencies; count reuse only where guards and interface maps are certified. Do not silently unfold a succinct exponential object.
* Lossy: a checked outer relaxation R subset of A can refute R when A is empty or provably infeasible. A relaxed witness is only a candidate; validate against the original source. For a carrier A and verified forbidden guards G_i disjoint from original solution support, A minus union G_i is a sound outer approximation; it is exact only with a completeness proof. Full carrier coverage certifies UNSAT; use exact GF(2) rank/intersection counts for small affine guard families (five guards: at most 31 intersection counts); charge acquisition and do not assume coverage for large/factored families.
* Energy compression: if E is the exact nonnegative violation count and E=E_core+R, certify a uniform rational error bound |R|<=epsilon or a one-sided lower bound on R; any checked lower bound L on min E_core with L-epsilon>0 certifies UNSAT. An L2 or average approximation is not a uniform bound. For explicit sparse Fourier coefficients, sum of absolute discarded coefficients is a valid conservative bound; exact signs, arithmetic and minimization are charged. For SAT, verify a lifted exact witness. Compression scores, low entropy, mod-3 collisions and predictions are not certificates.
* Predictive compression: predict forced bits/merges only as search ordering; discharge each proposed pin or relation with an exact checker under the current guard, then record the correction/delta. Never discard alternatives without proof.
* Hybrid: transfer a verified exact response, exclusion, guard, energy lower bound or affine equality to another track and reapply closure. Compare standalone exact, lossless-only, lossy/refinement and hybrid work under matched original inputs, seeds and total caps; preserve independent progress. Retain prior mandatory signed table and mod-3 experiments. Regress both SAT and UNSAT controls; include pairs with the same compressed representation but different feasibility and growing hostile families.
* Potential: attempt exact state/dictionary/correction credits, including the scoped bounded-degree affine quotient potential where justified. A refinement must decrease a specified numeric polynomial global credit, or be paid from an explicit bounded reserve; do not count exponentially many eliminated assignments as polynomial credit or replenish without charge. Guard splits, recompression, new dictionary entries, verification, failed lookups, communication, decompression, all lanes, arithmetic precision and witness reconstruction must be included in one uniform original-N resource ledger. Low serialized size or per-instance compression ratio cannot replace a proof of worst-case acquisition, verification and aggregate work.
