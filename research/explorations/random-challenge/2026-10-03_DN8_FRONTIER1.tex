Let \(D(n,8)\) denote simple genuine 3-CNFs over an ambient set of \(n\) variables with exactly eight distinct clauses, encoded by one presence bit for each of the
\[
M(n)=8\binom n3
\]
possible signed three-variable clauses.

Every genuine 3-clause falsifies exactly \(2^{n-3}\) assignments. Hence seven such clauses falsify at most \(7\cdot2^{n-3}<2^n\) assignments, so no seven-clause genuine 3-CNF is UNSAT. Every UNSAT member of \(D(n,8)\) is therefore minimally unsatisfiable. Tarsi's lemma then implies that its actual variable support has size at most seven.

Exhaustive exact-cover enumeration yields the full-support labeled core counts
\[
u_3=1,\qquad u_4=268,\qquad u_5=3030,\qquad u_6=7800,\qquad u_7=5040.
\]
Consequently, for every \(n\ge3\),
\[
\boxed{
U(n,8)=
\binom n3+
268\binom n4+
3030\binom n5+
7800\binom n6+
5040\binom n7
}.
\]
Direct hostile-control enumerations at \(n=8\) and \(n=9\) give respectively
\[
447{,}216\quad\text{and}\quad1{,}252{,}272
\]
UNSAT formulas, exactly matching this formula, with no support-eight or support-nine core.

For \(n=4,5,6,7\), exhaustive enumeration also verifies the singleton-intersection property: for every clause input \(i\), the intersection of all UNSAT exact covers containing \(i\) is exactly \(\{i\}\). Thus every unordered pair \(\{i,j\}\) has a legal opposite-output swap witness. A clause-query DAG with at most \(M(n)-2\) internal nodes can mention at most \(M(n)-2\) distinct clause inputs, leaving at least two globally unqueried. Their opposite-output witness pair is indistinguishable, so
\[
\boxed{B(n,8)\ge8\binom n3-1}
\]

For any \(n\ge7\), a pair of 3-clauses uses at most six variables; signed permutation into the seven-variable certificate followed by embedding back into the ambient variable set extends the same result to every \(n\ge7\). Hence the displayed lower bound holds for every \(n\ge4\).

Finally, because eight clauses exactly saturate the union bound, UNSAT is equivalent to pairwise disjointness of the eight falsifying codimension-three subcubes. SAT is therefore exactly the existence of a selected conflicting clause pair. A fixed clause has conflict degree
\[
d(n)=8\binom{n-3}{3}+12\binom{n-3}{2}+6(n-3),
\]
so the conflict graph has
\[
E(n)=4\binom n3 d(n)
\]
edges and a naive two-level pair-conflict circuit of size \(2E(n)-1=O(n^6)\). Thus the fixed-eight-clause family has a polynomial-size universal circuit in this presence-bit encoding. It is a finite structural laboratory and cannot by itself establish unrestricted 3-SAT hardness.
