\paragraph{Density-range pair-witness theorem.}
Assume the certified all-pair \(D(n,8)\) witness property. For a pair \(i,j\), let \(B\cup\{i\}\) be UNSAT and \(B\cup\{j\}\) SAT, with \(|B|=7\). Fix an assignment \(a\) satisfying the SAT member. Then \(a\) satisfies every clause in \(B\cup\{j\}\) and falsifies \(i\). Exactly \(7C_n\) of all \(M_n\) genuine clauses are satisfied by \(a\). Hence for every
\[
8\le m\le 7C_n
\]
we may add the same \(m-8\) further clauses satisfied by \(a\) to both formulas. The UNSAT member stays UNSAT and the SAT member remains satisfied by \(a\). Therefore every input pair retains an opposite-output witness throughout this entire density interval.

For \(n=4,5,6,7\) the base all-pair property is exhaustive; for larger \(n\), any two clauses use at most six variables and can be embedded into the certified seven-variable case. Consequently, for every \(n\ge4\) and \(8\le m\le7C_n\),
\[
B(n,m)\ge M_n-1=8\binom n3-1
\]
for the global clause-query DAG model. For the declared connected fan-in-two AND/OR plus unary-NOT circuit basis,
\[
G(n,m)\ge M_n-2.
\]

The endpoints are exact constant regimes:
if \(m<8\), the falsified-assignment union bound implies SAT; if
\[
m\ge7C_n+1,
\]
every formula is UNSAT because a fixed assignment satisfies exactly \(7C_n\) of the \(M_n\) possible clauses.

Thus the pair-witness lower bound occupies the entire nonconstant fixed-\(n\) density interval. This remains a model-specific polynomial lower bound, not an unrestricted SAT-time lower bound. The generic assignment-coverage circuit supplies
