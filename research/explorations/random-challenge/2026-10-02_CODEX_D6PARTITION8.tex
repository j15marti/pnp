\section*{Target, encoding, and quantifiers}
Let $M_n=8\binom n3$. Inputs are $M_n$ clause-presence bits with exactly eight
ones, representing simple genuine 3-CNF on the available $n$-variable universe.
Unused variables are permitted, matching the prior binomial counts. Clause IDs
enumerate triples lexicographically and then their falsifying patterns $0..7$.
For each published classifier $C_n$ we establish
\[
\forall b\in\{0,1\}^{M_n},\quad |b|=8
\Longrightarrow C_n(b)=\mathrm{SAT}(F_b).
\]
Exported query DAGs instead label terminal one UNSAT and terminal zero SAT.
Every certificate uses this same realization and input ordering.

\section*{Partition lemma and complete enumeration}
Each genuine 3-clause excludes $2^{n-3}$ assignments. Eight exclusions have
total capacity $2^n$, so they cover the cube if and only if they are pairwise
disjoint. Therefore UNSAT inputs are exactly the partitions into eight
codimension-three subcubes.

The bitset exact-cover recursion chooses one uncovered assignment and branches
over every nonintersecting subcube containing it. Any final partition has
exactly one member covering this chosen point. Induction gives both completeness
and uniqueness of enumeration. This is the Algorithm X branching principle
\cite{knuth}, without the dancing-links implementation.

\section*{Classifiers and whole-promise verification}
Six starting orders followed by one adjacent-swap pass give
\[
159\le B(6,8)\le50,460,\qquad 79\le B(5,8)\le13,147.
\]
The searches evaluated 165 and 85 orders respectively. No adaptive-order or
global optimum is claimed. The $n=6$ sign-round-robin baseline has 158,846
nodes, while grouping by variable triple has 51,094; the ordering lesson from
$n=4$ does not transfer unchanged.

The verifier independently checks all UNSAT rows against literal semantics,
their distinctness, and equality with the independent complete partition count.
It checks orderedness and acyclicity of the DAG, evaluates every UNSAT row,
then counts its accepting weight-eight assignments exactly. Skipped input
positions contribute binomial coefficients. The acceptance counts are exactly
30,020 and 4,380, so accepting every actual UNSAT row leaves no room for a
false positive. This verifies the whole promise without enumerating trillions
of SAT inputs.

For each pair of clause positions, a stored witness replaces one clause of
an UNSAT partition by the other, producing SAT. The CSV records the partition
index and a satisfying assignment of the exchanged formula. All 12,720 pairs
at $n=6$ and 3,160 at $n=5$ are checked. A classifier omitting any two input
coordinates entirely cannot distinguish that pair, proving input support at
least $M_n-1$ and the stated query-node lower bounds. This is a global support
argument, not a per-execution depth bound.

\section*{Shared arbitrary-Boolean circuits}
For every assignment $a$, OR the clause bits whose clauses $a$ falsifies;
AND all coverage bits and negate. Hash-consing identical pure-OR subexpressions
gives a 720-gate circuit for $n=6$ and a 256-gate circuit for $n=5$.
The basis is binary AND/OR, unary NOT, free fan-out, and uncharged input wires.
At $n=6$ there are 656 OR gates, 63 AND gates and one NOT, depth 12.

The circuit verifier reconstructs each vertex's exact OR support and the final
AND/NOT structure. This is a structural equivalence check for every clause set,
not random testing. The circuits work at every clause count. Support of at
least $M_n-1$ requires at least $M_n-2$ binary gates in a connected output cone,
so
\[
158\le G(6,8)\le720,\qquad 78\le G(5,8)\le256.
\]
The general unshared construction costs $2^n\binom n3$ gates and remains
exponential in $n$. No unrestricted SAT lower bound follows from the DAG sizes.

\section*{Sign imbalance, heterogeneity, and 2P2N}
All equal-volume UNSAT partitions satisfy $I=\sum_i|d_i^+-d_i^-|=0$.
Indeed, if $a$ clauses fix a coordinate to zero in their falsifying cubes and
$b$ fix it to one, the fraction of the partition with that coordinate zero is
\[
\frac a8+\frac{8-a-b}{16}=\frac12+\frac{a-b}{16}.
\]
Uniform coverage forces $a=b$. This is necessary, not sufficient, for UNSAT.

The exhaustive $n=6$ UNSAT histogram has
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
V&24&32&40&48&56&64&72&96\\\hline
\#&4560&7380&8640&3840&4320&540&720&20.
\end{array}
\]
Here $V=\sum_i(d_i-4)^2$, and all counts include unused-variable instances.
There is no UNSAT row with $V=0$. Thus every 2P2N formula in this particular
$D(6,8)$ family is SAT. This does not assert tractability or satisfiability of
larger 2P2N families. Only the UNSAT population's $I,V$ histogram was computed;
the full SAT histogram, semantic-function count and isomorphism quotient remain
uncomputed. All labeled UNSAT clause sets are retained.
