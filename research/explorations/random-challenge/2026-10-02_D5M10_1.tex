\section*{D5M10-1: ten-clause five-variable universal-classifier control}

\textbf{STATUS: EXACT counting + CONSTRUCTIVE UPPER BOUNDS. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

There are
\[
8\binom53=80
\]
possible genuine three-literal clauses on five variables and
\[
\binom{80}{10}=1{,}646{,}492{,}110{,}120
\]
distinct simple ten-clause formulas.

Exact symbolic dynamic programming over covered assignments gives
\[
\boxed{53{,}953{,}360}
\]
UNSAT formulas and
\[
1{,}646{,}438{,}156{,}760
\]
SAT formulas. Reversing the sign-pattern and variable-triple order in an
independently compiled count-only recurrence reproduced the same UNSAT count.

A reduced ordered clause-query DAG using sign-pattern round-robin ordering across
the ten variable triples has
\[
\boxed{1{,}435{,}660}
\]
decision nodes, peak width 62,377 at level 42, and 8,021,018 visited residual
states. This is exact for that declared ordering but is only an upper bound on
the globally minimum clause-query DAG.

For comparison, the same ordering at D(5,9) gave 195,468 nodes. Thus adding one
clause increases this particular ordered representation by a factor of about
7.345 while leaving the direct coverage circuit unchanged.

The coverage circuit uses 32 OR-of-10 indicators followed by an AND of the
32 assignment-coverage bits:
\[
32(10-1)+(32-1)=319
\]
binary gates for UNSAT, or 320 gates including one output NOT for SAT. Hence
\[
G(5,10)\le320
\]
for the declared basis.

A grouped-by-variable-triple ordering was also attempted as a hostile ordering
control. It exceeded approximately 2.58 GB resident memory and was terminated
before producing a DAG size, reinforcing that ordered-DAG size is highly
ordering-sensitive. No lower bound follows from that failed run.

No minimum circuit or minimum adaptive DAG is claimed.
