\section*{UNIVERSALDAG2: First exact reduced universal decision-DAG baseline}
\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT FOR FIXED ORDER; GLOBAL HYPERGRAPH MINIMUM OPEN.}

The all-formulas n=4,m=8 universe contains 10,518,300 simple formulas and 29,417 semantic falsified-assignment masks. We now impose one fixed lexicographic order on the 32 possible clauses and represent each formula by its clause-incidence vector, restricted to Hamming weight eight.

At level i the sufficient dynamic state is
\[
(i,k,M),
\]
where k clauses have been selected and M is the union of assignments falsified by those selected clauses. Bottom-up reduction identifies two states whenever their exclude/include continuation IDs are identical, and suppresses a test when both continuations agree. This is the standard behavioral reduction for the declared ordered read-once decision DAG.

Exact results:
\[
D_{\rm ord}(3,8)=8,
\qquad
D_{\rm ord}(4,8)=2107
\]
nonterminal decision nodes for the chosen fixed order. Thus
\[
\log_2 2107\approx11.0396.
\]

This is substantially below both the 10,518,300 syntactic formula count and the 29,417 semantic-mask count, showing that continuation sharing compresses even the semantic-state inventory.

The result must not be strengthened. It is not the minimum over the 32! clause orders, not an FBDD minimum, not a minimum DPLL DAG, and not the desired unrestricted hypergraph-DAG minimum. It is an exact finite upper baseline.
