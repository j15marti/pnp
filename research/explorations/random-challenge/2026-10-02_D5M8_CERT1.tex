independent verifier checks validity, distinctness, exact Hamming weight, both

c57184facc7633e50445ac6e6d5d4356e349110f20f6d4b8df9462474dd76ee6.

If a global clause-query DAG had at most 78 internal nodes, at most 78 distinct
input bits could label its nodes. Hence at least two bits \(i,j\) would never
be queried. The certified opposite-output pair for \(\{i,j\}\) differs only
on those unqueried bits, so both inputs would traverse the same computation and
receive the same output, a contradiction. Therefore
\[
\boxed{B(5,8)\ge79}.
\]
Combining with the explicit ordered construction gives
\[
\boxed{79\le B(5,8)\le15{,}954}.
\]
