\section*{DN9-CLOSE1: exact closure of the nine-clause frontier}

\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT for the finite combinatorial identities below. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

Let \(v_k\) denote the number of labeled full-support minimally UNSAT genuine
three-CNF formulas with nine clauses on exactly \(k\) variables. Direct enumeration and
finite inversion already gave
\[
v_4=9{,}408,\qquad
v_5=324{,}000,\qquad
v_6=1{,}913{,}280,\qquad
v_7=3{,}430{,}560.
\]

The unresolved case is \(v_8\). A hypothetical full-support minimally UNSAT
\(D(8,9)\) formula has deficiency one and hence belongs to \(\mathrm{MU}(1)\).
By the classical strict-resolution-tree characterization of \(\mathrm{MU}(1)\),
the formula is exactly the set of leaf clauses of a strict resolution tree. Nine leaves
give eight internal resolution nodes. Since the formula has support eight, every one of
its eight variables must be resolved somewhere, so the eight internal nodes use the
eight variables exactly once. Without weakening, a leaf contains exactly one literal for
each resolution node on its root-to-leaf path, hence its clause length equals its leaf
depth. Genuine three-CNF therefore requires every leaf to have depth three. But a full
binary tree whose leaves all have depth three has exactly eight leaves. Thus
\[
\boxed{v_8=0}.
\]

The accompanying verifier independently enumerates full-binary-tree leaf-depth profiles:
the all-depth-three profile exists for eight leaves and not for nine leaves.

There is also at most one eight-clause UNSAT core inside any nine-clause formula.
Every UNSAT eight-clause formula is an exact partition of the assignment cube into eight
equal falsifying subcubes. Two distinct eight-clause cores inside the same nine-clause
formula would share seven clauses; the uncovered complement of those seven subcubes is
unique, forcing the two differing eighth clauses to have the same falsifying subcube and
therefore to be the same genuine clause.

Using the previously established eight-clause full-support counts
\[
u_3=1,\quad u_4=268,\quad u_5=3030,\quad u_6=7800,\quad u_7=5040,
\]
write
\[
U_8(n)=\binom n3+268\binom n4+3030\binom n5+
7800\binom n6+5040\binom n7.
\]
The exact minimally UNSAT nine-clause count is
\[
M_9(n)=9408\binom n4+324000\binom n5+
1913280\binom n6+3430560\binom n7.
\]
Every nonminimal UNSAT nine-clause formula consists of its unique eight-clause UNSAT core
plus one additional distinct clause. Since there are \(8\binom n3\) possible genuine
clauses,
\[
\boxed{
U_9(n)=\left(8\binom n3-8\right)U_8(n)+M_9(n)
}\qquad(n\ge4).
\]

This reproduces the independent direct counts
\[
15{,}936,\ 686{,}400,\ 8{,}561{,}440,\ 60{,}047{,}120
\]
for \(n=4,5,6,7\), respectively, and yields the new exact value
\[
\boxed{U_9(8)=296{,}593{,}920}.
\]

For \(D(8,9)\), the previously proved density-range pair-witness result gives
\[
B(8,9)\ge447
\]
in the global clause-query DAG model and
\[
G(8,9)\ge446
\]
for the declared connected fan-in-two AND/OR plus unary-NOT circuit basis. The generic
assignment-coverage construction gives \(G(8,9)\le14336\). These remain finite
model-specific controls, not unrestricted SAT-time lower bounds.
