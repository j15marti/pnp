\section*{EVACR1: Evolving authenticated replay and future-branch selection}
\textbf{STATUS: EXACT compositional collapse + COMPUTATIONAL hostile control + CANDIDATE nonconfluent repair. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

\section*{Question}
Can post-commitment challenges evolve the SAME\_REALIZATION source so that each new authenticated level forces another polynomial multiplicative cost,
\[
T(n,d)\ge n^cT(n,d-1),
\]
and hence \(T(n,d(n))\ge n^{cd(n)}\) for unbounded \(d(n)\)?

\section*{Construction and test}
A committed source word is progressively restricted. A challenge fixes a still-live source coordinate; the next challenge acts on the restricted descendant. The explicit fixed-order binary tree has \(2^{h+1}-1\) nodes through depth \(h\). The committed experiment is
\texttt{experiments/acr/evolving\_source\_replay.py}; raw control output is
\texttt{results/raw/acr/2026-10-02\_LSPV5\_EVACR1.json}. It tests \(h=4\) through \(10\), with permutation-rich histories through depth \(7\).

\section*{PROVED/EXACT collapse for compositional restrictions}
Let \(R_r\) be a restriction operator and suppose legal evolution satisfies
\[
R_{r_2}(R_{r_1}(X))=R_{r_1\circ r_2}(X)
\]
with a polynomial-size canonical representation of the composed restriction. Then every challenge history \(H=(r_1,\ldots,r_d)\) has a succinct quotient \(q(H)\) such that
\[
X_H=R_{q(H)}(X).
\]
If \(q(H)\) is polynomially updatable and \(R_q(X)\) polynomially computable from static \(X\), explicit descendant-tree size does not imply
\[
T(n,d)\ge n^cT(n,d-1).
\]
The machine stores or rereads \(X\) and \(q(H)\) and computes only the realized descendant. Costs compose rather than multiply. Ordinary partial assignments have exactly this property.

\section*{Interpretation}
NP's accepting branch need not explore rejected branches. Adaptive future challenges remove advance knowledge of which branch will be requested, but that is insufficient when one succinct strategy generator maps challenge history to the correct descendant. The generator supplies all future responses without materializing the future tree.

\section*{Necessary repair: nonconfluent authenticated evolution}
A candidate multiplicative construction must defeat the quotient from histories to a compact current state, or prove evaluation of that quotient expensive. Seek histories \(H\ne H'\) with the same ordinary semantic current state
\[
\pi(X_H)=\pi(X_{H'})
\]
but source-legally different future continuation sets
\[
\mathcal C(X_H)\ne\mathcal C(X_{H'}).
\]
This is an evolving Lossy Quotient Provenance condition. Mere nonconfluence is still insufficient: the provenance state may itself have a polynomial-size canonical representation.

\section*{Next theorem target}
Define an exact source-legal transition system on the SU/vMS realization and seek unbounded depth \(d(n)\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item semantic descendants repeatedly collide while source-legal continuation sets differ;
\item no polynomial-size canonical provenance quotient restores confluence;
\item the next unpredictable challenge requires independent retained or reacquired information not reusable from previous levels;
\item a direct-sum/product theorem proves multiplicative rather than additive cost;
\item the recurrence survives static reread, recomputation, succinct strategy generators, semantic/algebraic oracles, INTERP and RECOMP.
\end{enumerate}
Only item 4 justifies exponent accumulation.

\section*{Strict conclusion}
\textbf{FALSIFIED for ordinary restrictions:} evolving post-commitment restriction alone forces multiplicative replay cost.

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT:} polynomially compositional restrictions admit a succinct history quotient and do not justify tree multiplication.

\textbf{CANDIDATE:} nonconfluent source-bound evolution / evolving LQP.

\textbf{OPEN:} source-matched nonconfluent family with no succinct provenance quotient; direct-product recurrence; arbitrary-SAT compiler; nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing hinge; final contradiction.

P versus NP remains OPEN.
