\section*{FORMULA-CONTINUATION4: exact decision-continuation quotient over formula streams}

\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT for the declared finite quotient calculations; COMPUTATIONAL for the tested $D(4)$ order search. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

\paragraph{Decision quotient.}
Fix a finite constraint universe $U$ and an order $\pi$ of its presence bits.  At cut $k$,
let $P_k$ be the processed constraints and $S_k=U\setminus P_k$.  A prefix history
$H\subseteq P_k$ induces
\[
r_H(K)=\operatorname{SAT}(H\cup K),\qquad K\subseteq S_k.
\]
Define
\[
H_1\equiv_k H_2
\iff
\forall K\subseteq S_k,quad
r_{H_1}(K)=r_{H_2}(K).
\]
Let $W_\pi(k)$ be the number of equivalence classes.  For the declared clause-presence
encoding and fixed order, this is exactly the residual-subfunction count, hence the minimal
deterministic ordered state count at that cut.  It is a streaming/ordered-branching resource,
not an unrestricted RAM lower bound.

\paragraph{Canonical residual representation.}
For a conjunction of constraints, let $C(H)$ be the assignments already falsified by at
least one selected prefix constraint.  The suffix makes the formula UNSAT exactly when it
covers every assignment outside $C(H)$.  Hence the residual UNSAT predicate is the positive
monotone CNF
\[
\bigwedge_{a\notin C(H)}
\bigvee_{c\in S_k:\ c\text{ falsifies }a} z_c.
\]
Deleting duplicate and subsumed positive clauses gives an exact canonical residual signature.

\paragraph{Exact tractable controls.}
For the complete 3-variable 2-SAT universe of 12 non-tautological binary clauses,
dynamic programming over all clause orders gives
\[
\boxed{\min_\pi\max_k W_\pi(k)=17}.
\]
One optimal profile is
\[
1,2,4,8,16,17,17,17,17,10,5,3,2.
\]
The implication-graph transitive-closure state, with already-UNSAT states collapsed, is
sufficient at every cut: zero canonical-state collisions map to distinct residual functions.
It is nevertheless over-refined; at cuts with $W=17$ the canonical-state counts include
$25,40,62,97$.

For the 12-constraint Horn graph universe consisting of facts, binary implications, and
negative goals,
\[
\boxed{\min_\pi\max_k W_\pi(k)=9},
\]
with profile
\[
1,2,4,8,9,9,8,9,9,8,5,3,2.
\]
The forward-closure/transitive-implication state is sufficient with zero violations, but at
cuts with $W=9$ it can have $16,32,112,168$ distinct canonical states.

For the complete 3-variable affine universe of all 14 nonzero equations
$a\cdot x=b$,
\[
\boxed{\min_\pi\max_k W_\pi(k)=29},
\]
with profile
\[
1,2,4,7,13,20,22,24,24,29,29,9,6,3,2.
\]
Reduced augmented row space is sufficient with zero violations; the two peak cuts have 36
and 42 Gaussian states respectively.

These controls show that the formula-continuation quotient is closer to a minimal sufficient
decision state than the standard solver representations, which generally retain information
that the particular future universe can no longer distinguish.

\paragraph{Bounded-width chart.}
For the 16-clause local universe containing all signed clauses on $(x_0,x_1,x_2)$ followed
by all signed clauses on $(x_1,x_2,x_3)$, the natural local order has profile
\[
1,2,2,4,4,8,8,16,16,17,17,17,17,9,5,3,2.
\]
At the central block boundary, $W=16$.  The standard separator statistic---which assignments
to $(x_1,x_2)$ remain extendible through the left block---also has exactly 16 states and zero
sufficiency violations.  Thus at that cut the path-DP chart realizes the exact quotient.

\paragraph{Full four-variable 3-SAT universe.}
For all 32 genuine signed 3-clauses on four variables, eight declared clause orders were
tested exactly.  The triple-major order peaks at 10,000 residual classes.  The sign-major
order
\[
0,8,16,24,1,9,17,25,\ldots,7,15,23,31
\]
peaks at only
\[
\boxed{257}.
\]
Its exact profile is
\[
1,2,4,8,16,24,28,44,65,65,97,113,129,193,225,241,
257,257,257,193,129,129,129,97,65,65,41,25,17,10,6,3,2.
\]
Five seeded random orders peak at $2766,4392,4656,2983,2503$.  Therefore the $257$ value
is only a tested upper bound on the minimum over all $32!$ orders.  The collapse from
10,000 to 257 is itself a strong representation-order hostile control.

\paragraph{Wrapper controls.}
For complete 3-variable 2-SAT, adding a semantically DEAD tautology bit or a duplicate
clause bit leaves the exact optimized peak unchanged:
\[
17\to17.
\]
Output complementation also preserves $W_\pi(k)$ because complementing residual Boolean
functions is a bijection.

\paragraph{Literature/model interpretation.}
The quotient is an ordered-stream resource.  This limitation is not merely philosophical:
Chen--Lin--Tsai prove that one-pass streaming 2-SAT requires $\Omega(n^2)$ bits even though
offline 2-SAT is linear-time.  Thus large formula-continuation state can coexist with a
problem in P.  Aspvall--Plass--Tarjan provide the classical SCC algorithm for 2-SAT, while
Dowling--Gallier give linear-time Horn-SAT by graph/data-flow propagation.

Consequently,
\[
\boxed{
\text{large formula-continuation quotient}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{large unrestricted SAT runtime}.
}
\]
The quotient remains useful as a calibrated minimum state measure under declared ordered
access.

\paragraph{Next hostile extension.}
Remove fixed order.  Construct the adaptive clause-query/FBDD analogue of the formula-presence
decision problem, then allow read-twice/RECOMP.  Compare the resulting state counts to
projective-dimension or other general branching-program invariants.  A collapse under
adaptivity is a successful falsification; survival would identify a stronger state resource.
