\section*{D7M8-CONFLICT2: exact conflict factorization and corrected enumeration}

\textbf{STATUS: COMPUTATIONAL / PROVED-EXACT finite identities. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

\paragraph{Correction.}
A prior chat-level count of 17,280 UNSAT formulas for the seven-variable,
eight-clause family was erroneous. Re-running the exact-cover recurrence on the full
regression sequence gives
\[
1,\ 272,\ 4380,\ 30020,\ \boxed{132685}
\]
for ambient variable counts $n=3,4,5,6,7$, respectively. The $n=4$ and $n=6$
values reproduce earlier controls. Among the 132,685 labeled $D(7,8)$ exact covers,
only
\[
\boxed{5040}
\]
use all seven variables. The remainder are embeddings of lower-support partitions.

\paragraph{Conflict graph.}
There are $8\binom73=280$ possible clause inputs. Two clause-falsifying
codimension-three subcubes intersect exactly when their partial assignments agree on
all shared constrained variables. Exhaustive checking of all $\binom{280}{2}=39060$
clause pairs gives zero mismatches with this factorization. The conflict graph is
128-regular with
\[
\boxed{17920}
\]
edges, partitioned as
\[
4480+10080+3360
\]
according as the two variable triples share zero, one, or two variables.

Under the exact-weight-eight promise, each selected clause covers 16 of the 128
assignments. Consequently
\[
\mathrm{UNSAT}
\iff
\text{the eight selected subcubes are pairwise disjoint},
\]
and hence SAT is exactly existence of a selected conflict edge.

\paragraph{Factored circuit.}
The direct assignment-coverage circuit used 4480 gates for SAT. The conflict
representation admits substantial sharing.

For each of the 35 variable triples construct all twelve OR-of-two pair-pattern
signals, six OR-of-four one-variable-sign signals from those pair signals, and one
OR-of-eight occupancy signal. This costs $35\cdot19=665$ gates.

For disjoint variable triples, an explicit biclique-cover certificate for $KG(7,3)$
computes the conflict block in 94 gates.

For triples sharing exactly one variable, fixing the shared variable and its sign
reduces the local conflict relation to $KG(6,2)$. An explicit verified weighted
biclique-cover certificate computes one such local block in 42 gates. There are
14 variable/sign blocks and 13 gates to OR their outputs, for 601 gates total.

For triples sharing exactly two variables, fixing the shared pair and its two-bit
pattern leaves five candidate triples. Conflict is threshold at least two among
those five signals. A 10-gate threshold circuit is used for each of the
$21\cdot4=84$ blocks, followed by 83 OR gates, for 923 gates.

Two final OR gates combine the three intersection categories. Therefore
\[
\boxed{G(7,8)\le 665+94+601+923+2=2285}
\]
for the declared fan-in-two AND/OR plus unary-NOT basis and exact-weight-eight
input promise. This is a constructive upper bound, not a minimum.

\paragraph{Literature control.}
Filmus et al., \emph{Irreducible Subcube Partitions}, EJC 30(3) (2023), Theorem 60,
prove that an irreducible homogeneous codimension-three subcube partition exists only
at length four and is unique there up to coordinate permutation and flipping. Thus a
tight seven-coordinate homogeneous codimension-three partition is reducible in their
sense. This supports the observed recursive structure but does not itself imply the
2285-gate bound.

the same finite SAT predicate can collapse substantially after a different exact
factorization. No unrestricted lower bound or asymptotic hardness claim follows.
