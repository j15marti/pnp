\section*{Target}
Test whether commit--challenge--authenticated-replay can bridge the causal HEC/CRS-PPC/CCSPC branch to the structural SU/vMS/Hadamard branch under the counterfactual $P=NP$.

\section*{Literature audit}
Valiant's incrementally verifiable computation shows that composable authenticated/verified continuation can itself be efficient; therefore replay authentication is not intrinsically a lower bound. Blum--Evans--Gemmell--Kannan--Naor and later memory-checking/delegation work formalize online authenticated memory, but security/verification does not imply unrestricted SAT-time hardness. Mahmoody--Moran--Vadhan proofs of sequential work require collision-resistant hashing plus an inherently-sequential-hash assumption; this is not admissible as an unconditional hinge. Pebbling and sustained-space results give genuine dependency-model lower bounds, but known transfers remain model-specific. Alman--Wang--Yu show that online communication can prevent batching; pointer chasing likewise gives round/communication lower bounds. Chandra--Kozen--Stockmeyer prove $AP=PSPACE$, locating polynomial alternation outside PH, but this does not force a SAT decider to materialize an explicit strategy.

\section*{ACR model}
An ACR execution has committed realizations $R_i$, commitments $C_i$, later challenges $r_i$, and authenticated openings $o_i$:
\[
R_i\to C_i\to r_i\to o_i\to R_{i+1}.
\]
The desired SAME\_REALIZATION condition is that $o_i$ must be a legal opening/restriction of the already committed $R_i$, not a fresh equivalent witness selected after seeing $r_i$.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: realized-path closure}
If a complete realized ACR transcript has polynomial length and its validity relation is polynomially balanced and lies in fixed PH, then PH-PROV-CLOSE1 applies. Under $P=NP$ a valid realized transcript is selectable in FP. If the transcript is the deterministic polynomial execution trace of a solver plus a polynomial authenticator, TRACECLOSE1 applies directly. Thus a fixed number of replay-authentication layers, or polynomially many deterministic replay steps whose realized transcript is polynomially checkable, do not by themselves escape polynomial closure.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: semantic continuation congruence}
If the next-step rule depends only on the current extensional Boolean state, then semantically equivalent histories have identical legal semantic continuations. Provenance helps only if legality is independently defined on the richer source realization.

\section*{COMPUTATIONAL hostile control ACRTREE1}

\section*{Negative result: naive ACR does not fill the gap}
Three generic routes fail:
\begin{enumerate}
\item deterministic authenticated replay is P-closed by TRACECLOSE1;
\item fixed-PH replacement/authentication is closed under $P=NP$ by PH-PROV-CLOSE1;
\item explicit strategy-tree explosion is defeated by succinct strategy representations, as ACRTREE1 illustrates.
\end{enumerate}
Cryptographic sequential-work assumptions, random oracles, collision resistance, and authenticated-memory security are FAILED TRANSFERS for an unconditional P-vs-NP proof.

\section*{Surviving candidate: Source-Bound Adaptive Replay}
The strongest surviving formulation is not authentication itself. Fix an exact source-typed realization $R$ before adaptive challenges. For challenges $r_1,\ldots,r_T$, require openings $o_i$ satisfying a source-specific relation
\[
\operatorname{Open}(R,r_{\le i},o_i)=1
\]
and SAME\_REALIZATION consistency across rounds. The target theorem must show that every correct continuation mechanism either
\[
\text{retains }L(R)\text{ information}
\quad\text{or pays charged reacquisition/recomputation }C(R),
\]
where the lower bound survives static reread, succinct representations, memoization, pointer jumping, INTERP, RECOMP, semantic-oracle replacement, and algebraic-oracle replacement.

The promising source match is to choose challenges as legal SU/vMS/Hadamard restrictions/slices after commitment and use the characteristic-two operator $\Phi$, MATRIXCERT1, EXCESS/TURANEX1, and binary-dual/odd-minor diagnostics to measure what same-realization information different challenges jointly expose. This is CANDIDATE only.

\section*{Alternation boundary}
Polynomially many genuine adaptive universal/existential rounds can express an alternating-polynomial-time strategy condition and hence enter PSPACE by $AP=PSPACE$. This escapes PH-PROV-CLOSE1 syntactically, but two gaps remain: a winning strategy can be succinct, and no theorem forces an arbitrary static SAT decider to solve that strategy problem. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\text{polynomial alternation}\not\Rightarrow\text{SAT lower bound}.}
\]

\section*{Barrier audit}
The generic ACR model relativizes: an oracle can be supplied to both replay generator and verifier. Algebraic authentication similarly does not by itself evade algebrization. A useful hinge must identify a source-specific continuation relation that cannot be replaced by semantic/oracle answers and prove a model-preserving compiler from every hypothetical static SAT decider.
