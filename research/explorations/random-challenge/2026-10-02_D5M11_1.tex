\section*{D5M11-1: eleven-clause five-variable universal-classifier control}

\textbf{STATUS: EXACT counting + CONSTRUCTIVE UPPER BOUNDS. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

For five variables there are 80 possible genuine three-literal clauses. At
eleven distinct clauses the promised family has
\[
\binom{80}{11}=10{,}472{,}222{,}825{,}920
\]
formulas.

Exact symbolic counting gives
\[
\boxed{2{,}071{,}359{,}840}
\]
UNSAT formulas and
\[
10{,}470{,}151{,}466{,}080
\]
SAT formulas. A separately compiled reverse-order recurrence reproduced the
UNSAT count exactly.

Under the same sign-pattern round-robin query order used for D(5,9) and
D(5,10), the reduced ordered clause-query DAG has
\[
\boxed{4{,}418{,}372}
\]
nodes, peak width 192,655 at level 42, and 22,481,988 visited residual states.

The identical-order sequence is therefore
\[
195{,}468\to1{,}435{,}660\to4{,}418{,}372
\]
for m=9,10,11. The multiplicative growth decreases from approximately 7.345 to
3.077, making the location of the eventual fixed-n density peak an empirical
target rather than a monotone-growth assumption.

The direct coverage circuit remains unchanged:
\[
32(10-1)+(32-1)+1=320
\]
AND/OR/NOT gates for SAT. Thus
\[
G(5,11)\le320.
\]

The ordered-DAG growth therefore remains representation-specific and cannot be
used as an unrestricted circuit lower bound. No global DAG or circuit minimum
is claimed.
