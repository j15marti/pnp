\section*{D5M9-1: first nine-clause five-variable universal-classifier control}

\textbf{STATUS: EXACT counting + CONSTRUCTIVE UPPER BOUNDS. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

There are
\[
8\binom53=80
\]
possible genuine three-literal clauses on five variables and
\[
\binom{80}{9}=231{,}900{,}297{,}200
\]
distinct simple nine-clause formulas.

Exact symbolic dynamic programming over covered assignments gives
\[
\boxed{686{,}400}
\]
UNSAT formulas and
\[
231{,}899{,}610{,}800
\]
SAT formulas.

A reduced ordered clause-query DAG using sign-pattern round-robin ordering across
the ten variable triples has
\[
\boxed{195{,}468}
\]
decision nodes, peak width 8,793 at level 42, and 1,686,747 visited residual
states. This is exact for that declared ordering but is only an upper bound on
the globally minimum clause-query DAG.

A much smaller unrestricted Boolean-circuit construction follows directly from
the coverage representation. Each assignment is falsified by exactly one sign
pattern on each of the ten variable triples, so ten input clause bits can cover
a fixed assignment. Build 32 OR-of-10 coverage indicators and AND them:
\[
32(10-1)+(32-1)=319
\]
binary gates for UNSAT, or 320 gates including an output NOT for SAT. Therefore
\[
G(5,9)\le320
\]
for the declared two-input AND/OR and unary-NOT basis.

This case is useful because nine clauses break the eight-clause exact-partition
condition: overlap among clause-falsifying four-vertex subcubes is now possible.
The large gap between the clause-query DAG and the direct coverage circuit again
shows that query-DAG growth is not unrestricted circuit growth.

No minimum circuit or minimum adaptive DAG is claimed.
