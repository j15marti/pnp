\section*{HEC-SAT proved core: state/result separation under future random challenges}
\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT IN THE DECLARED ONLINE NO-REREAD MODEL; STATIC-SAT TRANSFER OPEN.} P versus NP remains open.

\section*{Model}
Let independent epoch payloads be
\[
X_e\in\{0,1\}^{K_e},
\qquad K=\sum_{e=1}^E K_e.
\]
After all currently relevant payload bits have passed, but before a future challenge is revealed, a deterministic machine is reduced to an internal state \(S\) of at most \(s\) bits. The old payload is unavailable for rereading. A free semantic side channel
\[
Z=Z(X_1,\ldots,X_E)
\]
takes at most \(2^r\) values. Later the challenge may request any coordinate \((e,j)\), and the machine must output \(X_{e,j}\) exactly.

\section*{Theorem 1: side-information state lower bound}
Let \(F_z=Z^{-1}(z)\) be a side-information fiber. Exact future coordinate recovery implies that, for every fixed \(z\), the state map is injective on \(F_z\). Therefore
\[
s\ge \log_2 \max_z |F_z|.
\]
Since \(Z\) has at most \(2^r\) values and the payload domain has size \(2^K\),
\[
\boxed{s\ge K-r}
\]
for the worst side-information fiber.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Fix \(z\). If two distinct payloads \(x,x'\in F_z\) produced the same state, they differ in some coordinate \((e,j)\). Issuing that future challenge forces different correct outputs from the same pair \((z,S)\), contradiction. Thus all payloads in \(F_z\) require distinct states. Pigeonhole gives \(max_z|F_z|\ge 2^{K-r}\).

\section*{Corollary 1: free SAT decision result}
If the free semantic channel is only the current SAT yes/no answer, then \(r=1\) and some semantic fiber requires
\[
\boxed{s\ge K-1.}
\]
Thus giving away the current decision result does not identify the deterministic cut state with the semantic result.

\section*{Corollary 2: explicit witness side channel}
If an explicitly transmitted witness/result string has length at most \(w\) bits, the same counting argument yields a worst-fiber bound
\[
\boxed{s\ge K-w}
\]
provided future coordinate challenges remain valid within that fiber. If no witness is transmitted to the deterministic machine, witness existence alone changes nothing in the state bound.

\section*{Corollary 3: multi-epoch direct state accumulation}
For independent payloads across epochs, concatenate them into one \(K\)-bit vector. If every payload coordinate remains a possible future query, then with an \(r\)-bit semantic side channel
\[
\boxed{s\ge \sum_{e=1}^E K_e-r.}
\]
This is an exact direct-sum statement for state capacity in the declared no-reread model.

\section*{Theorem 2: adaptive masking preserves perfect randomness}
Let \(R_t\) be fresh independent uniform bits. Let \(b_t\) be any bit determined by prior history and by a completed heterogeneous gate computation, but independent of the fresh \(R_t\). Define
\[
Y_t=R_t\oplus b_t.
\]
Then conditioned on the entire past, \(Y_t\) is uniform. By induction, the emitted \((Y_t)\) sequence is independent uniform even though gate answers change the realized digits. Gate computations therefore control timing/routing without manufacturing entropy.

\section*{Theorem 3: unresolved selector amplification}
Suppose \(u\) future selector bits can still select any one of \(2^u\) independent payload coordinates after the cut. Taking \(K=2^u\) in Theorem 1 gives
\[
\boxed{s\ge 2^u-r.}
\]
This is exponential in unresolved selector length, but only because the model contains \(2^u\) independent payload bits. It is not an exponential lower bound relative to a compressed static input.

\section*{Known quantum extension}
Nayak's random-access-code theorem gives the exact post-quantum analogue for a uniformly random \(K\)-bit payload encoded into \(m\) qubits: if every requested bit is recoverable with success probability \(p\), then
\[
\boxed{m\ge (1-H_2(p))K.}
\]
This is a theorem transfer at the random-access state interface only; it is not a theorem about unrestricted SAT.

\section*{Heterogeneous gate calibration}
Different gate jobs may be used to create multiple causal timescales. For rigorous unconditional timing tiers, the deterministic time hierarchy theorem supplies artificial languages at separated time scales: for suitable time-constructible \(f,g\) with \(f\log f=o(g)\),
\[
DTIME(f)\subsetneq DTIME(g).
\]
Natural logspace or other easy problems should be retained as hostile controls. Complexity of a gate does not add entropy; all generator work must be charged.

\section*{Literature-aligned state/result separation}
Husfeldt and Rauhe prove dynamic partial-sum lower bounds in the cell-probe model even with nondeterministic queries and even when the model is supplied the right answer as an oracle. This is a direct methodological precedent for separating semantic answer from maintained information. Alman--Wang--Yu's online communication framework prevents batching by forcing answers before future query pieces are revealed. Pointer-chasing lower bounds provide a model for sequentially dependent future relevance.

\section*{What is now proved and what remains open}
\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT in HEC.}
(i) side-information state lower bound; (ii) free-result corollaries; (iii) exact multi-epoch direct state accumulation; (iv) adaptive XOR masking preserves a perfect random stream; (v) unresolved-selector amplification subject to explicit entropy accounting.

\paragraph{KNOWN THEOREM TRANSFERS.}
Nayak random-access-code lower bound; deterministic time hierarchy; dynamic/cell-probe and online-communication precedents in their own models.

\paragraph{OPEN / load-bearing.}
Construct a polynomial-size static SAT family and a model-preserving compiler such that every arbitrary polynomial-time static SAT decider yields an HEC/chronogram solver with controlled cut state and post-challenge work. Without this Static-to-HEC theorem, the proved state bounds remain restricted-model results.

\paragraph{Entropy guard.}
Injecting \(K\) independent random bits can justify at most \(K\) bits of pure information burden. Making \(K\) exponential by literally streaming exponentially many fresh bits does not prove static SAT hardness. Replacing them by a short pseudorandom seed collapses the unconditional information content to the seed length unless an additional computational-hardness assumption is introduced.
