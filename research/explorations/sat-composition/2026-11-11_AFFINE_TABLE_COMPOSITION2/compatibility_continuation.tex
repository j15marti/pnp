\section*{Status and accomplished step}
\textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN.} The earlier AFFINE-TABLE-COMPOSITION1
prototype had exact affine/table operations but a polynomial 2-SAT terminal
requiring independent coordinate forms. This continuation implements exact
compatibility of dependent coordinates by closure polynomials, adds a Horn
backend and a polynomial-workspace seed backend, and connects them to the
existing interfaces and a general CNF selector encoding.

The fixed-codimension tractability regime is \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM}, already
partly recorded in project ARC225--229 and established in the literature
\cite{arvind2021,brakensiek2019,mastrolilli2019}.
The complete constructive proofs below are \textbf{PROVED/EXACT}

\section{Input, target, and exact bridge}
The target is to decide \(H(x)\land Ax=b\), where \(H\) is an explicitly
given Horn CNF or 2-CNF, and \(A\) is an explicit binary matrix.
Let \(n\) be the number of dense variable slots and \(N\) the explicit input
length, including that variable universe. Computation uses exact bit vectors
and ordinary deterministic polynomial-cost graph, closure, and Gaussian
operations. A source may contain positive facts, negative constraints, and
redundant or inconsistent affine rows. Every returned model is checked
against the original source. All costs refer to this same execution.

For the preceding interface language,
\[
 R_i(x)=[A_i x=b_i]\land f_i(L_i x),
\]
combine the affine cores and reduce every coordinate form modulo them,
retaining all constant shifts. A table on at most two coordinates is
bijunctive. A larger \emph{already explicit} table can also be recognized
as bijunctive by collecting every implied unary and binary clause and
checking that their conjunction agrees with every table entry.
This is polynomial in the explicit table size, not a free CNF
compilation operation.

After substituting reduced forms, retain every coordinate occurring in
a unary \emph{or} binary clause. Constants and repeated forms are handled
exactly. This explicitly preserves the ARC228 repair of the earlier
binary-only observation error. Let the remaining distinct forms be rows
\(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_q\), named \(y_1,\ldots,y_q\).
Their exact image is
\[
 \{y:D y=0\},
 \qquad
 d=\operatorname{rank}D
   =q-\operatorname{rank}\{\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_q\}.
\]
A tagged Gaussian reduction of the \(\ell_i\) computes a full basis of
dependencies \(D\) in polynomial time. The residual tables become a
2-CNF \(H(y)\). Hence the new backends decide the exact projected problem
\(H(y)\land Dy=0\). A satisfying \(y\) lifts by Gaussian solving to the
original variables, and every original factor is rechecked.
The previous independent-coordinate terminal is the special case \(d=0\).
Unsupported non-bijunctive explicit tables return \texttt{UNRESOLVED}.

\section{The exact closed-set function basis}
A Horn clause has at most one positive literal. It can be viewed as a
rule whose antecedent variables imply a positive head, or imply
contradiction when no positive head occurs.
Let \(\mathcal R\) be the family of supports of satisfying assignments
of a nonempty Horn formula \(H\).
For \(S\subseteq[n]\), let
\[
 \cl_H(S)=
 \bigcap\{T\in\mathcal R:S\subseteq T\},
\]
with result \(\bot\) if no such model exists.

\begin{lemma}[Least-model closure]
If \(\cl_H(S)\ne\bot\), it is itself an \(H\)-model and is the least model
containing \(S\). It is computable by Horn forward chaining in polynomial
time. For every \(T\in\mathcal R\),
\[
 S\subseteq T
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \cl_H(S)\ne\bot\ \hbox{and}\ \cl_H(S)\subseteq T.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Horn models are closed under intersection: if every variable in a rule
antecedent is true in an intersection, that antecedent is true in every
intersected model, so its head is also true there, or the negative rule
would already be violated. Forward chaining from the asserted variables
and positive facts adds only variables forced in every extending model.
If a negative rule fires there is no extension. Otherwise the fixed
point satisfies every rule and is contained in every extending model.
An antecedent-counter implementation processes each rule incidence at
most once per closure call.
\end{proof}

Write \(x_S=\prod_{i\in S}x_i\). On \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 x_S =
 \begin{cases}
 0,&\cl_H(S)=\bot,\\
 x_{\cl_H(S)},&\cl_H(S)\ne\bot.
 \end{cases}
\]
In particular the canonical key of the constant function is
\(\cl_H(\varnothing)\), which can be nonempty when \(H\) has positive facts.

\begin{lemma}[Independent canonical keys]
The functions
\[
 f_K(T)=\mathbf 1[K\subseteq T],
 \qquad K,T\in\mathcal R,
\]
form a basis of all functions \(\mathcal R\to\F\).
Their multiplication is
\[
 f_K f_L =
 \begin{cases}
 0,&\cl_H(K\cup L)=\bot,\\
 f_{\cl_H(K\cup L)},&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Order \(\mathcal R\) by nondecreasing cardinality. Its evaluation matrix
has entry \(\mathbf 1[K\subseteq T]\) in row \(T\), column \(K\).
It is triangular with diagonal entries one, and hence invertible over
\(\F\). The product identity is the least-model closure identity applied
to \(K\cup L\).
\end{proof}

Thus distinct consistent closed keys cannot secretly cancel as functions.
For implication-only Horn systems a union of closed sets is already
implication-closed, and one only checks negative conflicts.
For general Horn rules, the full closure of the union is essential:
a multi-antecedent rule may fire only after the union.

\section{Exact parity compatibility and a direct witness}
Gaussian elimination either detects an inconsistent affine system or
retains \(d\) independent equations \(a_jx=b_j\).
Define the following Boolean satisfaction indicator over \(\F\):
\[
 P(x)=\prod_{j=1}^{d}\bigl(1+b_j+a_jx\bigr).
\]
Each factor is one exactly when its equation holds.
The implementation expands this product in the canonical closed-set
basis, dropping inconsistent closures and adding coefficients modulo two.

\begin{theorem}[Closure-polynomial compatibility]
The conjunction \(H(x)\land Ax=b\) is satisfiable exactly when its
canonical closure-polynomial dictionary is nonempty.
If it is nonempty, a key of minimum cardinality gives a satisfying
assignment directly.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every \(H\)-model, \(P\) is exactly the indicator of \(Ax=b\).
Every closure replacement and coefficient cancellation preserves this
function. By independence of the canonical basis, an empty dictionary
is equivalent to \(P\) vanishing on every \(H\)-model.

Conversely choose a minimum-cardinality surviving key \(K\).
At the assignment with support \(K\), a surviving term \(f_L\) contributes
one only when \(L\subseteq K\). No strictly smaller surviving key exists,
so precisely \(f_K\) contributes one. Hence \(P(K)=1\).
By construction \(K\) itself is an \(H\)-model. This gives a common model,
which the implementation verifies against all source clauses and rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[2-CNF backend]
The same construction handles an arbitrary 2-CNF \(C\) together with
affine equations.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Find a model \(a\) of \(C\) using ordinary 2-SAT, or conclude UNSAT.
Substitute \(x=a\oplus y\). The translated formula is satisfied by zero.
A nontrivial translated clause has a negative literal; because its width
is at most two, it is a negative unit, an implication, or a negative
pairwise constraint, and is Horn. The affine right-hand side becomes
\(b+Aa\). Apply the theorem and undo the substitution.
\end{proof}

This operation retains the exact interaction of the two components.
It does not replace either component by only its affine hull.

\section{Complete costs and a polynomial-workspace alternative}
Let
\[
 D(n,j)=\sum_{i=0}^{\min(n,j)}\binom ni.
\]
Let \(B_j\) be the number of surviving closed keys after multiplying
the first \(j\) affine indicators.

\begin{theorem}[Generator degree and charged work]
For every stage \(j\), including a temporary output prefix,
the number of distinct keys is at most \(D(n,j)\).
If \(F_j\) is the number of terms in the canonicalized \(j\)-th affine
factor, then \(F_j\le n+1\), and the uncapped implementation generates
\(B_{j-1}F_j\) candidate products at that stage.
Its time is bounded by
\[
 \operatorname{poly}(N)
 \left(1+\sum_{j=1}^{d}(n+1)B_{j-1}\right)
 \le \operatorname{poly}(N)D(n,d).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Before closure, the product of \(j\) linear factors has degree at most
\(j\). Boolean multilinearization makes every monomial \(x_S\) have
\(|S|\le j\). Every canonical key is therefore \(\cl_H(S)\) for such
a seed \(S\), even when the closure itself is large. This applies also
to any prefix of the raw products. There are \(D(n,j)\) possible seeds.

Each affine factor has at most \(n+1\) raw terms, and merging cannot
increase this number. The program visits every pair of current and
factor terms, performs a polynomial-cost closure and consistency check,
and inserts or cancels the resulting key. Canceled products still cost
work. Initial graph and Gaussian work, factor construction, sorting,
source checks, and \(n\)-bit arithmetic add polynomial overhead.
Since \(d\le n\), summing stages yields the stated bound.
\end{proof}

If \(B=\max_j B_j\), a temporary product can contain up to
\((n+1)B\) keys; completed-stage sizes alone do not account for this
temporary work. The optional term cap is checked at each attempted
insertion, before a future cancellation. Cap exhaustion returns
\texttt{UNRESOLVED}. Reported counters count selected operations and
active polynomial sets, not all Python object memory or native tape
instructions.

For fixed \(d\), the bound is polynomial. For \(d=O(\log N)\), this
bound generally gives only \(2^{O(\log^2 N)}\), not polynomial time.
Affine row order, row basis, and the chosen 2-SAT base model can change
actual intermediate sizes; the measured \(B\) is an execution property.

\begin{corollary}[Small generating seed and streaming space]
If \(H\land Ax=b\) is satisfiable, there exists \(S\subseteq[n]\),
\(|S|\le d\), such that \(\cl_H(S)\) is a common model.
Enumerating these seeds one at a time decides the conjunction in
\[
 \operatorname{poly}(N)D(n,d)
\]
time and polynomial workspace, even when \(d\) grows.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The minimum surviving key in the compatibility theorem is the closure
of a seed of size at most \(d\), by the degree argument.
For an alternative algorithm, stream every seed of size at most \(d\),
compute its least Horn model, and check the affine equations.
A found model is verified. If all seeds fail, the preceding existence
statement proves UNSAT. Store only the source, row basis, current seed,
its closure, and iterator state. Discard unsuccessful closures rather
than caching all visited sets. Each of these objects has polynomial
size. The 2-CNF translation and witness lifting also use polynomial space.
\end{proof}

The implementation supplies both strategies. The seed cap bounds
attempted seeds and returns \texttt{UNRESOLVED} on exhaustion.
Polynomial workspace does not make the potentially exponential seed
search polynomial.

\section{A precise limit of separate abstract summaries}
For a 2-CNF \(H\), let \(\alpha_{\rm aff}(H)=\aff(\operatorname{Mod}H)\).
For affine \(A\), let \(\alpha_2(A)\) be the conjunction of all unary
and binary clauses implied by \(A\). Consider the repeated exact exchange
\[
 A\leftarrow A\cap\alpha_{\rm aff}(H),
 \qquad
 H\leftarrow H\land\alpha_2(A).
\]
These operations are sound, but a consistent fixed point need not
contain a common model.

\begin{lemma}[Affine consequences of 2-CNF]
The affine hull of a satisfiable 2-CNF is defined by forced literals and
variable equalities or complemented equalities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Its model set is majority-closed: if the majority of three models
falsifies a binary clause, one of the three falsifies both literals.
Translate by a model \(a\), obtaining a majority-closed set \(S\) containing
zero. Then \(S\) is closed under coordinatewise AND because
\(\operatorname{maj}(u,v,0)=u\wedge v\).
Its linear span is also AND-closed by bilinearity of coordinatewise
multiplication over \(\F\).
An AND-closed linear subspace consists of vectors constant on equivalence
blocks and zero on inactive coordinates. To see this, construct the
union of supports using \(u\vee v=u+v+(u\wedge v)\), then multiply vectors
and their complements relative to this active support to isolate each
class of coordinates with identical evaluation maps. The resulting
block indicators span exactly that subspace.
Translate back by \(a\).
\end{proof}

Polynomially many ordinary 2-SAT entailment tests find these constraints.
For an affine relation, all binary consequences are likewise computed by
Gaussian consistency checks of the four possible assignments to each
coordinate pair.

\begin{lemma}[One equation is special]
For any nonempty relation \(R\) and affine hyperplane \(E\) over \(\F\),
\[
 R\cap E\ne\varnothing
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \aff(R)\cap E\ne\varnothing.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If a linear functional never has the required Boolean value on \(R\),
it has the opposite constant value on all of \(R\), hence on its affine
hull. The other direction follows by inclusion.
\end{proof}

It is unsound to iterate this using the intersection of the \emph{old}
hull in place of the hull of the exact residual. For \(H=(x\vee y)\),
adding \(x\oplus y=0\) leaves the actual model \(11\), whereas intersecting
the old full hull leaves \(00\) as well. The subsequent condition \(x=0\)
distinguishes them.

\begin{proposition}[Smallest rank-two fixed-point failure]
On five variables let
\[
 H=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{4}(\neg x_{i+1}\vee x_i),
\]
and impose
\[
 x_2+x_3+x_5=1,\qquad x_1+x_4+x_5=0.
\]
Then \(H\land A\) is UNSAT, but the separate-summary exchange is already
at a consistent fixed point. Five variables are minimal for this
phenomenon at affine codimension two.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The models of \(H\), in variable order, are
\(00000,10000,11000,11100,11110,11111\).
Successive differences are the five unit vectors, so their affine hull
is full. The two left-hand sides take respectively the value pairs
\[
 (0,0),(0,1),(1,1),(0,1),(0,0),(1,1),
\]
never the required \((1,0)\).
The three nonzero homogeneous row-space vectors have weights \(3,3,4\);
there is no nontrivial unary or binary affine consequence. Thus neither
direction of exchange refines the other component.

For minimality, if a rank-two affine system has a nonzero consequence
of support at most two, the full binary abstraction adds it to \(H\).
Only one further independent affine condition remains, and the previous
lemma makes hull compatibility exact. Otherwise all three nonzero
dual rows have weight at least three. Every nonzero column contributes
two to their total weight, so \(2n\ge9\), forcing \(n\ge5\).
\end{proof}

For a larger projection scope \(k\), take two blocks of \(k+1\) variables,
all cross-block negative pairwise clauses in \(H\), and odd parity in
each block. The conjunction is UNSAT, the Horn hull is full because
zero and all unit vectors are models, and every at-most-\(k\)-coordinate
projection of \(A\) is full. This refutes that bounded-coordinate
exchange. These simple examples are solved by failed-literal probing,
so a stronger control is needed for that extension.

\section{A verified obstruction to every singleton probe}
Construct the Mycielski graph of a five-cycle. Its original vertices
are \(v_0,\ldots,v_4\). Add \(u_0,\ldots,u_4\), joining \(u_i\) to
the two cycle neighbors of \(v_i\), and an apex \(w\) joined to every
\(u_i\). There are eleven vertices and twenty edges, with no triangle.

For each vertex introduce three color bits. The Horn 2-CNF \(H\)
contains within-vertex pairwise exclusions and same-color edge
exclusions. It has ninety-three binary clauses.
The affine part \(A\) imposes odd parity on each of the eleven
variable-disjoint triples. There are thirty-three Boolean variables.
Together the constraints require exactly one color per vertex and a
proper three-coloring.

\begin{proposition}[UNSAT with consistent singleton fixed points]
This instance is UNSAT, but the full iterated affine-hull/binary exchange
is consistent after either value is pinned to any one of its thirty-three
bits. Thus every one of its sixty-six singleton probes survives.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Suppose the graph had a three-coloring. Give the apex color \(3\);
all \(u_i\) then have colors \(1\) or \(2\).
Recolor each original cycle vertex \(v_i\) of color \(3\) with the color
of \(u_i\). Adjacent cycle vertices could not both have been color \(3\).
The edge from \(u_i\) to each cycle neighbor guarantees that a recolored
vertex differs from those neighbors. This yields a two-coloring of the
odd cycle, a contradiction.

The following fixed-point argument holds for every triangle-free graph
under this encoding. Pinning \(x_{v,c}=0\) generates an affine relation
\(E\) consisting of that zero and a disequality between the two other
color bits at \(v\). The two available local colors give the local
affine direction. For every external coordinate, choose a compatible
available color at \(v\), set that external bit to one with all other
external bits zero, then toggle it off. These are \(H\land E\)-models
and yield every external unit direction. Hence
\(\aff(H\land E)=E\).
The disjoint parity blocks in \(A\land E\) give no further binary
consequences, so the exchange is at a consistent fixed point.

Pinning \(x_{v,c}=1\) fixes the full color triple at \(v\), excludes color
\(c\) at every neighbor, and gives each neighbor a disequality between
its other two colors. Let these restrictions be \(E\).
Triangle-freeness makes the neighbors independent. All combinations
of their two available colors, with the rest uncolored, are valid
\(H\land E\)-models, spanning all local freedoms.
For a color coordinate outside the closed neighborhood of \(v\), turn it
on and choose each adjacent neighbor's available color to differ from
it. Such choices are independent. Toggling the external bit off yields
its unit direction. These directions show
\(\aff(H\land E)=E\), while the remaining independent parity triples
again give no new binary consequence. The fixed point is consistent.
Thus none of the singleton branches yields a failed literal.
\end{proof}

The independent verifier computed all sixty-six fixed points, each after
two rounds. Its separate graph-coloring search visited 154 nodes and
found no three-coloring. It also checked its 2-CNF affine-hull routine
against direct affine spans for all 166 three-variable 2-CNF relations.
The complete mixed instance and a 137-clause ordinary CNF encoding are
included. The new exact closure-polynomial engine refutes the mixed
instance with 1,416 candidate products and a peak of 120 polynomial
terms. The old independent-coordinate terminal returns unresolved.

This is a limitation of the specified summaries and singleton
conditioning. It does not limit arbitrary abstract interpretation,
stronger lookahead, or exact SAT algorithms.

\section{Why exact symbolic composition still needs a controller}
Use \(m\) selector triples \((p_i,q_i,r_i)\), pairwise exclusions within
each triple, and implications from every selector to one common anchor
\(a\). Add odd parity in each triple. This is a connected easy SAT family:
choose every \(p_i\) and the anchor true. Bags
\(\{a,p_i,q_i,r_i\}\) give primal treewidth at most three.

\begin{proposition}[Exponential flat expansion on an easy family]
For the Horn backend, the canonical product after \(j\) odd-parity
factors has exactly \(3^j\) surviving keys.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The \(i\)-th parity indicator is \(p_i+q_i+r_i\) over \(\F\).
Every product selecting one variable per processed triple is consistent.
Its Horn closure adds the common anchor and no other selector.
Different selections give different closed supports, all with coefficient
one. No cancellation occurs. There are \(3^j\) such selections.
\end{proof}

A factored product or an anchor separator avoids this flat expansion.
The proposition therefore refutes a universal polynomial bound for this
particular expansion path, not for SAT decision.
The code reproduces \(3^m\) terms through \(m=7\), and the independent
closed-support checker through \(m=9\).

For arbitrary CNF, the earlier selector reduction is now executable.
For each clause \((\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_k)\), introduce \(k\) selectors,
odd parity, all pairwise exclusions, and implications from each selector
to its literal. Odd parity plus at-most-one means exactly one selection,
so the exact projected relation is the original clause.
The binary part is Horn even with negative original literals.
An empty clause produces an inconsistent zero-row parity.
The DIMACS frontend densely relabels occurring identifiers before
allocating masks. The resulting solver is exact when uncapped, but its
affine rank and symbolic or seed cost can grow with the input.

\section{Barrier audit and next target}
All algorithms here operate on explicit Horn or 2-CNF sources and
explicit affine rows. Replacing closure by a semantic oracle on arbitrary
CNF would remove a paid polynomial premise and is not permitted.
No relativization or algebrization barrier is claimed to be bypassed.
The finite-poset function basis is an exact algebraic transfer; no
cryptographic or physical assumption enters the proofs.

The negative examples concern fixed summary languages or eager flat
representations. They do not authenticate every historical mixed rule,
force arbitrary algorithms to build these objects, or imply an
unrestricted proof-size or machine-time lower bound. No Natural Proofs
property separating general circuits is asserted.
The polynomial-workspace backend is a concrete algorithmic guarantee;
its potentially exponential time gives no simultaneous FLMV
contradiction.

The next target is a uniformly charged \emph{factored} compatibility
controller: preserve exact factor scopes, choose multiplication,
conditioning, and elimination operations, and bound the actual total
intermediate representation and acquisition cost. It should exploit
the anchor-separated easy family while retaining enough joint
information to refute the singleton-resistant graph example.
Prior bounded-separator theorems are reusable components.
A proof that every SAT input admits, and permits polynomial discovery
of, such a polynomial-total-cost execution remains \textbf{OPEN}.
