\section*{BILINREC1: low exterior dimension forces a large bilinear recurrence module}
\textbf{STATUS: PROVED/EXACT consequence of DIAGCONV1; source exclusion OPEN. P versus NP remains OPEN.}
\paragraph{Literature audit.} Periodic binary linear complexity is governed by shortest LFSR recurrences and gcds with \(z^N-1\). Cyclic/constacyclic Schur-product literature shows product-space degeneracy need not be reflected by ordinary linear complexity. No reviewed theorem directly covers \(b_j(t)=\operatorname{bit}_j(f(g^t))\).
\paragraph{Exact source object.} Define \(h_{rt,\delta}(s)=b_r(s)b_t(s+\delta)+b_t(s)b_r(s+\delta)\). DIAGCONV1 gives a block-circulant operator \(\Phi\) from the triangular supported diagonal streams to the correlation streams, with supported kernel exactly \(U^\perp\).
\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT dimension consequence.} The supported domain has dimension
\[
M_2=\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}(m-\delta)=\binom m2.
\]
If \(d=\dim U=\operatorname{rank}\Phi=O(m)\), then
\[
\boxed{\dim\ker\Phi=\binom m2-O(m)=\Theta(m^2).}
\]
Thus a low-exterior line admits quadratically many independent triangularly supported identities
\[
\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}q_\delta*h_{rt,\delta}=0
\quad\forall r<t.
\]
\paragraph{Non-implication.} Low \(d\) does not generally imply low linear complexity of any individual \(b_j\). A proof must attack the bilinear correlation module itself.
\paragraph{Candidate source-exclusion theorem.} For nonconstant Boolean-grid interpolation \(f\), primitive \(g\), standard binary labels, and vMS-scale \(h\gg m,\ p\gg h\), prove \(\operatorname{rank}\Phi=\omega(m)\), equivalently rule out a quadratic-dimensional supported bilinear recurrence module.
\paragraph{Potential interfaces.} Cyclic/constacyclic Schur-product support growth; Fourier/Hasse-jet ranks of \(h_{rt,\delta}\); shortening of the supported kernel; additive-combinatorial growth of spectral supports. Each requires a new source-matched transfer theorem.
