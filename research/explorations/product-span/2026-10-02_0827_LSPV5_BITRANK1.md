## Correction 1: complete versus short orbit
The objection that known exponential-sum bounds require an orbit segment of length p^epsilon matters only if the t-sum is truncated to a short consecutive orbit. BILINREC1 uses block-circulant correlation streams over the full declared orbit; the triangular restriction is on lag-coefficient support q_delta. Thus complete-orbit character sums are not excluded merely because m << p^epsilon. Any later orbit truncation must reopen the Konyagin-Shparlinski threshold audit.

## Correction 2: actual missing field transfer
DIGCORR1 + SPARSEEX1 remove the simple phase-degeneracy obstruction for legal small lags in the stated parameter regime. Complex Fourier expansion of standard bit predicates can therefore potentially combine with complete exponential-sum cancellation.

But Phi is a binary operator. High real/complex Gram rank or approximate orthogonality of signed lifts does **not** imply high rank over F_2. A characteristic-zero nonzero determinant can be even and vanish modulo 2. Therefore the earlier proposed “almost orthogonal signed lifts -> Gram rank -> binary rank” bridge is **INSUFFICIENT AS STATED**.

## Exact parity reformulation
For a binary matrix M, rank_F2(M) <= r iff all (r+1)-minors vanish modulo 2. Hence an analytic route must eventually certify an **odd minor**, or another invariant directly lower-bounding binary rank. Complex cancellation alone does neither.

## Candidate A: odd-minor extraction
Find a legal square submatrix M_S whose integer determinant is odd. Analytic estimates may help locate a well-conditioned submatrix, but real diagonal dominance alone is insufficient. The parity pattern must be controlled exactly. OPEN.

## Candidate B: correlation-to-linear-complexity
The pseudorandom-sequence literature gives characteristic-two links between small higher-order correlation and large binary linear complexity; conversely low linear complexity forces a large correlation peak. This is better typed than complex Gram rank.

However BILINREC1's Theta(m^2) kernel is a **joint bilinear recurrence module across many h_{rt,delta} streams**, not an ordinary recurrence of one bit stream. No reviewed theorem supplies that transfer. A multi-stream extension is required.

## Candidate C: binary dual-code correlation theorem
View rows of supported Phi as a binary code. A promising exact target is:

> Every nonzero triangularly supported annihilator q forces a full/half correlation peak in at least one legal bit-pair stream, while source-specific exponential-sum/correlation bounds forbid such peaks.

This stays in characteristic two and avoids the invalid real-to-binary rank transfer. It is OPEN.
