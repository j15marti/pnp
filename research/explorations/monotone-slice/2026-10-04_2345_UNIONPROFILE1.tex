\section*{UNION-PROFILE-BONFERRONI}
Let
\[
H(z)=\bigvee_{a=1}^{m}\bigwedge_{i\in A_a}z_i
\]
be a monotone DNF. Let $\mu_+$ and $U_{M,t}$ match all squarefree coordinate moments needed by unions of at most $R$ terms, where $R$ is even. Define
\[
X(z)=\sum_{a=1}^m {\bf 1}[A_a\subseteq z].
\]
Bonferroni gives the exact two-distribution bound
\[
\left|\Pr_{\mu_+}[H=1]-\Pr_{U_{M,t}}[H=1]\right|
\leq
\mathbb E_{U_{M,t}}\binom XR.
\]
For the exact slice,
\[
\mathbb E_{U_{M,t}}\binom XR
=
\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq[m]\\ |I|=R}}
\frac{(t)_{\left|\cup_{a\in I}A_a\right|}}
     {(M)_{\left|\cup_{a\in I}A_a\right|}}.
\]
Hence, if every $R$-term subfamily satisfies
\[
\left|\bigcup_{a\in I}A_a\right|\geq L_R,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{
\left|\Pr_{\mu_+}[H=1]-\Pr_{U_{M,t}}[H=1]\right|
\leq
\binom mR\frac{(t)_{L_R}}{(M)_{L_R}}
}.
\]
\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT.}

\paragraph{Proof.}
The first inequality is the even-order Bonferroni remainder after equality of the relevant factorial moments. On the exact slice, the probability that a fixed $s$-coordinate set is contained is $(t)_s/(M)_s$. Summing over $R$-subsets of DNF terms yields the displayed identity. Since $(t)_s/(M)_s$ is nonincreasing in $s$, the union lower bound gives the claimed estimate. \qed

\section*{Pairwise-overlap corollary}
If every term has width $w$ and
\[
|A_a\cap A_b|\leq\lambda\qquad(a\neq b),

\[
\left|\bigcup_{a\in I}A_a\right|
\geq Rw-\lambda\binom R2.
\]
Thus, provided the two distributions match the required moments through degree $Rw$,
\[
\boxed{
\left|\Pr_{\mu_+}[H=1]-\Pr_{U_{M,t}}[H=1]\right|
\leq
\binom mR
\frac{(t)_{Rw-\lambda\binom R2}}
     {(M)_{Rw-\lambda\binom R2}}

\]
