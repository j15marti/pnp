## Candidate and falsifiers

Candidate (named dependency: the admissible choice of $\mu_+$ in DIRECTIONALGAP1/SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1). For every fixed $s$, a polynomial-size monotone DNF matches the slice on every layer formula that has an UNSAT subformula of at most $s$ clauses. So an admissible $\mu_+$ must avoid such formulas, and the uniform-UNSAT $\mu_+$ that the TWOSATDEC sampler approximates is inadmissible under a stated tail hypothesis.

Falsifiers:
1. An UNSAT proper 3-CNF with fewer than 8 clauses.
2. A small-core family whose first moment beats $q^{-9}$.
3. Pr[UNSAT] at density 3.5 dominated by small cores already at the sampled q. That would make the TWOSATDEC sampling informative after all.
4. $D_s$ outputs 1 on a SAT layer formula.

None of the four fired. Falsifier 3 fails, because the observed finite-q UNSAT mass is far larger than the small-core moment (see the third result).

## Results

**SMALLCORE-DNF GUARD. PROVED/EXACT (elementary; independent review pending).** Let $D_s=\bigvee_{S}\bigwedge_{C\in S} z_C$, where $S$ ranges over the UNSAT sets of at most $s$ proper clauses. Then $D_s$ is monotone, has at most $\sum_{j\le s}\binom Mj=O(M^s)$ terms, and satisfies $D_s\le \mathrm{UNSAT}$ pointwise, so on the layer it is $\le U_{q,t}$. Hence $\Pr_{\mu_-}[D_s=1]\le\delta_-=o(1)$, and any pair $(\mu_-,\mu_+)$ that satisfies the DIRECTIONALGAP1 indistinguishability requirement for circuits of size $O(M^s)$ must have
$$\Pr_{\mu_+}[\,\text{formula has an UNSAT subformula of}\le s\text{ clauses}\,]\le \varepsilon_A+o(1).$$
The proof is the definition of $D_s$ together with monotonicity of UNSAT. The guard is not SAT-specific in form, but it holds for every fixed $s$ at once.

**Minimum cores. PROVED/EXACT plus COMPUTATIONAL census.**
1. A width-3 clause has $2^{k-3}$ falsifying points among $2^k$, so an UNSAT proper 3-CNF has at least 8 clauses. With exactly 8 clauses, the falsifying subcubes must partition the cube, so the clauses pairwise clash.
2. By Tarsi's lemma, a minimally UNSAT CNF on $k$ variables has $m\ge k+1$ clauses.
3. A fixed $m$-set of clauses lies in the layer with probability $\prod_{i<m}(t-i)/(M-i)=\Theta(q^{-2m})$. So a $(k,m)$ core family contributes first moment $\Theta(q^{k-2m})$ with $m\ge\max(8,k+1)$. The maximum is at $k=7$, $m=8$, which gives $q^{-9}$.
4. The exact census (`experiments/monotone_slice/small_core_census.py`, deterministic, 32.8 s) counts labeled 8-clause UNSAT sets that use exactly $k$ variables: $c_3=1$, $c_4=268$, $c_5=3030$, $c_6=7800$, $c_7=5040=7!$. The count $c_7$ equals the number of depth-3 decision trees on 7 distinct variables.
5. Hence $\mathbb E[\#\text{7-var cores}]\cdot q^9\to 2.625^8\approx 2253.3$. The measured values are 2012.7 at $q=50$, 2137.5 at $q=100$ and 2253.3 at $q=10^4$.
6. A Chvátal–Szemerédi union bound (any $k$-set spanning at least $k+1$ clauses) gives total mass $O(q^{-10})$ for minimal cores with $8\le k\le\varepsilon q$, for fixed $\varepsilon<1/(3.5e^2)$. This bound is a standard calculation written out here and has not been independently re-derived. These are exactly the "depth-3 decision" certificates that the early TWOSATDEC runs saw capture every UNSAT draw at small q.

**UNIFORM-$\mu_+$ INADMISSIBILITY. CONDITIONAL.**
Hypothesis LARGE-CORE-TAIL: Pr[UNSAT, and every UNSAT subformula spans at least $\varepsilon q$ variables] $=o(q^{-9})$ at density 3.5. I found no proof; it is OPEN.

Under LARGE-CORE-TAIL, Pr[UNSAT] $=\Theta(q^{-9})$ and $\Pr_{\mu_+^{\mathrm{unif}}}[D_8=1]\to1$. Then the poly-size $D_8$, with $O(q^{7})$ relevant terms, has $\varepsilon_-=0$ and $\varepsilon_+=o(1)$. So no approximation-method lower bound can use the uniform UNSAT layer as $\mu_+$ at $t=3.5q$.

If LARGE-CORE-TAIL fails, the uniform $\mu_+$ is dominated by large-core formulas. In both cases the guard forces $\mu_+$ onto small-core-free UNSAT formulas.

Finite-q evidence: TWOSATDEC draws at $q\ge40$ show 0 or 1 UNSAT per 16 samples. That is many orders of magnitude above the small-core moment ($\approx10^{-12}$ at $q=50$). The finite regime is therefore large-core dominated, and it cannot show the asymptotic crossover. This is not an asymptotic statement.

**TWOSATDEC q+1 LOOP. FALSIFIED/RETIRED as a probe of SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1.** Rejection-sampling the uniform layer at density 3.5 < 3.52 has expected UNSAT count $16\cdot o(1)$ (KNOWN THEOREM, already in SLICEDELTA0-R1). TWOSATDEC88 through TWOSATDEC97 (q=93..102) are all empty draws. Even nonempty draws would sample a $\mu_+$ that the guard shows is either inadmissible (under LARGE-CORE-TAIL) or uncontrolled. Raising q by one per run cannot close, strengthen or falsify SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1. That makes it the repeated easy-variant pattern that AGENTS.md deprioritizes, and I recommend not continuing it.
