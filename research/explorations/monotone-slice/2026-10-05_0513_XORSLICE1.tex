\section{What was reviewed}
HEAD \texttt{43a1539d} is MOMENTBRIDGE1, after MATCHED-MUF2. DENSITY-CANCEL1 is a parameter obstruction to the direct Cavalar--Kumar--Rossman union bound on two nearby sparse slices, not a no-go for every sunflower argument. The numerical check at \(q=20\), \(t=m=70\), \(M=9120\), \(\ell=2\), \(\varepsilon=0.1\), \(B=1\) gives
\[
R_2^{1/2}\approx 17.88,
\]
so the positive-error root diverges, as claimed.

ECCC TR26-220 is a method-of-moments sunflower bound for \(O(w)\)-wise and locally pseudo-independent distributions, used there for arithmetic radical separations. It does not supply the slice two-distribution circuit approximation. Wegener (TCS 1985) is correctly cited: standard monotone lower-bound methods do not transfer to slice functions, and the right model is a set circuit.

The frozen triple orbit in MOMENTBRIDGE1 does not replicate after exact pair balancing. That does not retract the separate MOMENTBALANCE3-ORBIT distill, which reported a multi-orbit tilted AUC near \(0.65\). One frozen orbit and a selected orbit vector are different tests. Neither is a theorem.

\section{Control}
