\section{Target and literature status}
Let
\[
M=8\binom q3,\qquad t=\lfloor3.5q\rfloor,
\]
and let \(S_{q,t}\) be the Valiant slice extension of clause-presence
\(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,q}\). Hajiaghayi--Sorkin prove random proper 3-SAT is
satisfiable whp below density \(3.52\). Because \(t=O(q)\) and
\(M=\Theta(q^3)\), duplicate clauses in the with-replacement model occur with
probability \(O(t^2/M)=O(1/q)\), so the result transfers to the uniform
distinct-clause exact layer.

Scott--Sorkin characterize rare UNSAT below the pure-literal threshold by
small minimal UNSAT subformulas, but explicitly leave the interval between
pure-literal and satisfiability thresholds unresolved. Their Question 1 asks
whether linear-size MUFs below threshold are superpolynomially rare. This is
almost exactly the structural fork needed here.

Caragiannis--Gravin--Jiang (ESA 2025) study \(k\)-wise independent random
3-SAT. Their fixed-\(k\) general satisfiability guarantee is at
\(m=O(n^{1-1/k})\), not our linear \(m=\Theta(n)\) regime. Ahmadi et al.
(COLT 2026) obtain statistical-query hardness by quiet planting satisfiable
\(k\)-SAT while controlling low-order marginals; this is an analogy, not a
theorem transfer.

ECCC TR25-124 on switching-lemma lower bounds for random satisfiable 3-CNF
was withdrawn by its author on 19 August 2025 after a flaw in the main
argument was recognized. It is retired as literature support.

\section{Exact first-order symmetry}
Let
\[
G=S_q\ltimes(\mathbb Z_2)^q
\]
act by variable permutations and independent variable complementation. This
action is transitive on all proper signed 3-clauses and preserves UNSAT and
exact weight.

\begin{theorem}[SYM1]
If \(A\) is uniform on any nonempty \(G\)-invariant family
\(\mathcal F\subseteq\binom{\mathcal C_q}{t}\), then every clause coordinate
has marginal
\[
\Pr[C\in A]=\frac tM.
\]
In particular this holds for uniform exact-layer UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All coordinate marginals are equal by transitivity, and their sum is
\(\mathbb E|A|=t\).
\end{proof}

Hence, under conditioned UNSAT,
\[
\mathbb E d_i=\frac{3t}{q},\qquad
\mathbb E d_i^+=\mathbb E d_i^-=\frac{3t}{2q},
\]
and for a variable pair \(i\ne j\),
\[
\mathbb E d_{ij}=\frac{6t}{q(q-1)}.
\]
Thus first-order statistics are exactly camouflaged.

\section{Pair-orbit algebra}
For distinct clauses \(C,D\), let \(s\) be the number of shared variables and
\(h\) the number of shared variables with opposite signs. These \((s,h)\)
classify the pair orbits under \(G\). For fixed \(C\), the number of \(D\) in
orbit \((s,h)\) is
\[
n_{s,h}
=
\binom3s\binom{q-3}{3-s}2^{3-s}\binom sh,
\]
excluding \((3,0)\). Equivalently,
\[
n_{0,0}=8\binom{q-3}{3},\qquad
n_{1,0}=n_{1,1}=12\binom{q-3}{2},
\]
\[
n_{2,h}=6(q-3)\binom2h,\qquad
n_{3,h}=\binom3h\quad(h\ge1).
\]

For any \(G\)-invariant exact-layer distribution, let \(\theta_{s,h}\) be the
pair-inclusion probability for orbit \((s,h)\). Then
\[
\frac M2\sum_{s,h}n_{s,h}\theta_{s,h}=\binom t2.
\tag{PAIR-SUM}
\]
For the uniform layer,
\[
\theta_0=\frac{t(t-1)}{M(M-1)}
\]
for every orbit.

\section{Degree-two monotone hostile statistic}
Define
\[
T(A)=\sum_{i=1}^q d_i^+(A)d_i^-(A).
\]
Each pair in orbit \((s,h)\) contributes \(h\), so
\[
\mathbb E T
=
\frac M2\sum_{s,h}n_{s,h}h\theta_{s,h}.
\]
Writing
\[
L=4\binom{q-1}{2},
\]
the uniform exact-layer expectation is
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_0T
=
qL^2\frac{t(t-1)}{M(M-1)}.
}
\tag{T0}
\]

For every threshold \(K\), the predicate \(T\ge K\) has polynomial-size
monotone circuits: AND all opposite-sign incident clause pairs and threshold
the polynomial number of resulting bits with a monotone sorting network.

More generally, for any fixed \(r\), any threshold on the count of a fixed
\(r\)-clause pattern family has polynomial-size monotone circuits. There are
at most \(M^r\) patterns, each is a monotone AND, and a monotone sorting
network performs the threshold. Call this \textbf{LOCALPATTERN-GUARD}.

Thus any proposed positive/negative distribution separated with constant
advantage by a fixed-order pattern-count threshold is already defeated by a
polynomial-size monotone circuit. Low-order camouflage is necessary, though
not sufficient.

\section{INTERMEDIATE-PAIR1 experiment}
The exact DPLL implementation in
\texttt{experiments/monotone\_slice/intermediate\_pair\_signal.py} was checked
against brute-force truth tables on 500 random formulas with
\(q=4,5,6,7,8\).

For each \(q\in\{20,24,28,32\)\), 5000 uniformly sampled distinct-clause
formulas at \(t=3.5q\) were tested with seed \(5252+q\). The classifier was
fixed before this rerun:
\[
T(A)\ge \mathbb E_0T+0.5q.
\]
No per-\(q\) threshold was fitted.

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c}

This is finite \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL} evidence only. It does not establish an

is vulnerable at these sizes to a degree-two monotone statistic.

\section{Deletion-minimal core check}
For every sampled UNSAT formula, one deterministic clause-deletion pass
extracted a deletion-minimal UNSAT subformula:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|c}
q&U&\overline m_c&m_{min}&m_{max}&\overline n_c&n_{min}\\
\hline
20&114&34.18&15&44&18.48&10\\
24&78&42.51&26&55&22.29&17\\
28&56&50.04&35&60&25.89&21\\
32&49&60.43&44&73&30.00&26
\end{array}
\]
The maximum core-variable counts were \(20,24,28,32\).

This does not prove absence of a smaller MUF: deletion order can select a
different minimal core. Therefore it does not answer Scott--Sorkin's open
question.

\section{Next targets}
\paragraph{PAIR-CAMOUFLAGE1.}
Move from scalar \(T\) to the full pair-orbit vector. Prove or falsify the
existence of a large exact-layer UNSAT subfamily whose pair-orbit counts lie
inside uniform-layer typical windows. A first filtered family is
\[
\mathcal Y_{q,\eta}
=
\{A:|A|=t,\ A\text{ UNSAT},
\ |T(A)-\mathbb E_0T|\le\eta q\}.
\]
It remains \(G\)-invariant and therefore retains SYM1 whenever nonempty.

\paragraph{INTERMEDIATE-MUF1.}
On the pair-camouflaged family, search for smaller MUFs using multiple
deletion orders and exact branch-and-bound at smaller \(q\). The asymptotic
question is adjacent to Scott--Sorkin Question 1 and remains open.

\paragraph{SAT-SUNFLOWER2.}
Only after pair camouflage and large-core structure survive should a
3CNF-specific sunflower/approximation lemma be attempted.

\paragraph{HIGHER-ORDER-GUARD.}
Enumerate all signed-overlap pattern counts at order two, then order three.
Any constant-advantage threshold gap triggers LOCALPATTERN-GUARD.

\paragraph{Switching and lifting.}
The withdrawn ECCC report supplies no switching theorem. A switching argument
must be rebuilt independently. Block-local exact-weight lifting remains
retired by SLICELIFT2.
