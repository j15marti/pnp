## Test

One chunk, under the 30-second rule. Exact binomial ratios on the threshold guard, plus the same 400-sample XOR sweep as DIRERR1 (seed 261222).

DIRERR1 set `eta_minus_closure = C(M-L, t-(w-1)) / C(M-(w-1), t-(w-1))`. That is the conditional probability that no other block coordinate is present given the core, not the joint event. The approximation method charges the joint.

At `(q,L,w)=(10,12,4)`, `t=floor(3.5 q)`, `M=960`:

- `p_w = 5.655760e-04`
- `joint = 3.274199e-05`
- `joint / p_w = 5.789141e-02`
- `cond = 7.353592e-01` (the DIRERR1 figure)
- `cond / p_w = 1.300195e+03` (mixed ratio, not an absolute overcharge)
- full pure layer `Pr[block weight = w-1] / p_w = 1.273611e+01`

So one closed core does not overcharge `mu-` relative to the layer mass. Closing every core does: the pure layer is about 12.7 times `p_w`.

On XOR-SLICE-EASY the both-parity witness, a monotone OR of 35 pairwise ANDs, hits 144/292 unsat and 0/108 sat. One-sided miss `eta_plus = 0.506849`, sample `eta_minus = 0`. Local parity collision does not cover the GE-easy slice.
