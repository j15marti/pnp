\section{Target}

Let
\[
M=8\binom q3,\qquad t=6q,
\]
and let \(S_{q,t}\) be the Valiant slice extension of proper-clause
\(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,q}\). SLICEPAD1 gives a candidate proof that exact-\(t\)
SAT is NP-complete and exact-\(t\) UNSAT is coNP-complete. SLICEDEPTH2 gives
\(\exp(\Omega(q\log q))\) lower bounds for monotone depth two. The remaining target is
\[
\boxed{C_{\rm mon}(S_{q,6q})=M^{\omega(1)}}.
\]

\section{Literature map}

\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{Valiant (1986).} For slice functions, monotone circuit complexity is within a constant factor of unrestricted circuit complexity plus \(O(M\log^2 M)\).

\item[] \textbf{Garg--G\"o\"os--Kamath--Sokolov (2020).} Monotone circuit size is rectangle-DAG complexity of monotone Karchmer--Wigderson search. Their lifting theorem converts Resolution/conjunction-DAG width into rectangle-DAG size after composition with a polynomial-size index gadget.

\item[] \textbf{G\"o\"os--Kamath--Robere--Sokolov (2019).} Their Lemma 7 maps a lifted CSP search problem to \(KW^+(C\hbox{-}Sat)\). Alice gets a renamed sparse unsatisfiable source CSP; Bob includes all target constraints satisfied by one assignment.

\item[] \textbf{Lovett--Meka--Mertz--Pitassi--Zhang (2020).} DAG-like deterministic lifting can be proved via sunflower methods, but the known gadgets are nonconstant size. Work in 2024 still calls constant-size-gadget lifting a major open problem.

\item[] \textbf{Cavalar--Kumar--Rossman (2022).} Robust-sunflower approximation gives strong monotone lower bounds. Their spread theorem requires \(r=B\log(\ell/\epsilon)/p\). They explicitly identify specialized sunflowers and non-product distributions as potential ways forward.

\item[] \textbf{Matching-specific advances (2025--2026).} Cavalar et al. and Rao obtain substantially stronger Perfect Matching lower bounds using new matching-specific sunflower/spread-matching lemmas. This is direct precedent for a SAT-specific sunflower lemma.

\item[] \textbf{Cavalar--Fabris--Mukhopadhyay--Srinivasan--Yehudayoff (2026).} Their September 2026 derandomized sunflower theorem works for \(O(w)\)-wise independent and a broader pseudo-independent class. The natural SAT-supported negative distribution below fails their declared pseudo-independence condition already on one variable triple.

\item[] \textbf{Carenini (2026).} Ranked spreadness removes width dependence from some random-containment estimates. A separate 2026 result proves strong \(k\)-DNF Resolution lower bounds for random 3-CNF up to \(k=O(\sqrt{\log n})\). These are structural tools, not unrestricted slice-circuit lower bounds.

\item[] \textbf{Beimel--Nir (2026).} Multislices support strong formula-model lower bounds, but currently do not supply the needed unrestricted monotone circuit lower bound.
\end{itemize}

\section{Hostile control: full 3XOR hard, every 3XOR slice easy}

Let \(X_N\) be the monotone incidence function that outputs one iff a set of 3XOR constraints is inconsistent. The full-cube function has an exponential monotone lower bound through the GGKS/GKRS framework. For threshold \(t\), define
\[
X_{N,t}(z)=
\begin{cases}
0,&|z|<t,\\
X_N(z),&|z|=t,\\
1,&|z|>t.
\end{cases}
\]

\begin{proposition}[XOR-SLICE-EASY]
For every \(t\), \(X_{N,t}\) has polynomial-size monotone circuits.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Compute \(|z|\), compare it with \(t\), and on equality solve the represented linear system over \(\mathbb F_2\) by Gaussian elimination. This gives polynomial-size unrestricted circuits. Valiant's slice theorem converts them to polynomial-size monotone circuits.
\end{proof}

Thus generic full-CSP monotone hardness, Resolution width, or incidence expansion cannot by itself imply hard exact slices. 3XOR is a mandatory hostile control.

\section{Why the existing lifting reduction misses one slice}

In GKRS Lemma 7, Alice has only the source constraints after renaming. Bob includes every target constraint satisfied by his assignment. For 3XOR Bob has density \(1/2\) in the target constraint universe; for proper 3CNF the analogous object has density \(7/8\). Thus the natural reduction has
\[
|A_{\rm Alice}|=O(Q),\qquad |B_{\rm Bob}|=\Theta(M),
\]
whereas an exact-layer KW pair requires both weights equal to \(t\).

\begin{lemma}[Output-inert padding cannot repair the gap]
Suppose fresh padding coordinates are \(D_A,D_B\), and an Alice-only fresh coordinate would not decode to a source-search answer. Correctness for every KW output requires \(D_A\subseteq D_B\), and hence
\[
|A\cup D_A|-|B\cup D_B|\le |A|-|B|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Any coordinate present for Alice and absent for Bob is a legal monotone-KW answer. Therefore every nondecodable fresh coordinate present for Alice must also be present for Bob. The cardinality inequality follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Proper 3-clause bundle rigidity]
For proper width-three clauses \(C,D\),
\[
C\models D\quad\Longrightarrow\quad C=D.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The falsifying sets of \(C\) and \(D\) are subcubes of equal size \(2^{Q-3}\). The implication gives inclusion of one falsifying set in the other, hence equality, which fixes the same literals.
\end{proof}

So cosmetic/common padding cannot exact-weight the standard lifting, and the simplest clause-replication gadget is also blocked.

\section{Exact spreadness}

Let
\[
\mathcal U=\{A\in\tbinom{[M]}t:A\text{ is UNSAT}\},\qquad
\delta_q=\Pr[A\text{ is SAT}]\le\rho^q,
\]
where \(\rho=2(7/8)^6<1\).

\begin{proposition}[SPREAD1]
For \(|S|=s\ge1\),
\[
\frac{|\{A\in\mathcal U:S\subseteq A\}|}{|\mathcal U|}
\le
\frac{(t)_s}{(M)_s}\frac1{1-\delta_q}.
\]
For all sufficiently large \(q\),
\[
\frac{|\{A\in\mathcal U:S\subseteq A\}|}{|\mathcal U|}
\le
\left(\frac{2t}{M}\right)^s.
\]
Hence \(\mathcal U\) is \(r_0\)-spread for \(r_0=M/(2t)=\Theta(q^2)\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At most \(\binom{M-s}{t-s}\) exact-\(t\) sets contain \(S\), while
\(|\mathcal U|\ge(1-\delta_q)\binom Mt\). Divide and simplify.
\end{proof}

No nonempty \(t\)-uniform family can be \(r\)-spread for \(r>M/t\), by averaging singleton incidences.

\section{Generic robust-sunflower barrier}

The CKR theorem requires
\[
r=B\frac{\log(t/\epsilon)}p.
\]
For a natural below-threshold product negative distribution let
\[
p=c\frac tM,\qquad 0<c<1.
\]
Then
\[
r=\frac Bc\frac Mt\log(t/\epsilon)>\frac Mt
\]
for sufficiently large \(t\), which exceeds the maximum possible spread of any nonempty \(t\)-uniform family.

Thus the off-the-shelf robust-sunflower theorem cannot directly start from the full exact-\(t\) minterm family against a product distribution a constant factor below the slice. This is a logarithmic parameter barrier, not merely weak spreadness.

\section{Natural SAT-supported negative distribution}

Define \(\mathcal D^-_{q,t}\) by choosing a uniform assignment \(\beta\), then a uniform \(t\)-subset of the \(7M/8\) clauses satisfied by \(\beta\). Every sample is exact-\(t\) and SAT, and each coordinate has marginal \(t/M\).

Fix one variable triple and its eight signed proper clauses. No assignment satisfies all eight, so
\[
\Pr_{B\sim\mathcal D^-}[\text{all eight clauses occur}]=0.
\]
The 2026 pseudo-independence definition requires every marginal on at most \(k\) coordinates to be a convex mixture of product distributions whose positive marginals stay within a positive multiplicative window around the common marginal.

\begin{proposition}[PSEUDOIND-FAIL]
For \(k\ge8\), positive marginal, and \(0<\epsilon<1\), \(\mathcal D^-_{q,t}\) is not \((k,\epsilon)\)-pseudo-independent in the sense of Cavalar et al. 2026.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On the eight clauses of one triple, every product component with strictly positive bit marginal assigns positive probability to the all-one pattern, and so does every convex mixture. The SAT-supported distribution assigns it probability zero.
\end{proof}

Therefore the newest derandomized-sunflower theorem is not a black-box solution for the most natural SAT-supported exact-layer distribution.

\section{A hard-core family with no small UNSAT core}

Tarsi's theorem states that a minimally unsatisfiable CNF with \(r\) clauses uses at most \(r-1\) variables. Fix constant \(L\). For uniform \(A\in\binom{\mathcal C_q}{6q}\),
\[
\Pr[\exists\text{ UNSAT subformula with at most }L\text{ clauses}]
\le
\sum_{r=1}^{L}\sum_{v=0}^{r-1}
\binom qv
\binom{8\binom v3}{r}
\frac{(t)_r}{(M)_r}
=o(1).
\]
For fixed \(r\), each nonzero summand is at most \(O(q^{r-1})O(q^{-2r})=O(q^{-r-1})\).

Since a random exact-\(6q\) formula is UNSAT with probability \(1-\rho^q\), the same \(1-o(1)\) conclusion holds conditioned on UNSAT.

\begin{proposition}[SMALLCORE1]
For every fixed \(L\), a \(1-o(1)\) fraction of exact-\(6q\) UNSAT minterms contain no UNSAT subformula of at most \(L\) clauses.
\end{proposition}

Define the resulting hard-core family
\[
\mathcal U^{(L)}_{q,6q}
=
\{A\in\mathcal U_{q,6q}:\text{every subformula of size }\le L\text{ is SAT}\}.
\]

\section{What changed}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Generic full-CSP-to-slice transfers are retired. 3XOR is a decisive counterexample.
\item Direct GGKS/GKRS exact-weight lifting is downgraded: it has a weight-orientation mismatch, and output-inert padding cannot fix it.
\item Off-the-shelf robust sunflowers on the full \(t\)-minterm family are retired for natural below-threshold product negatives because of the unavoidable \(\log t\) spread gap.
\item A SAT-specific sunflower route is promoted. Matching-specific sunflower lemmas in 2025--2026 show that function-specific combinatorics can materially outperform generic robust sunflower bounds.
\item Ranked spreadness, Johnson/Hamming analysis, and random-3CNF proof complexity remain auxiliary tools until they produce a gate-level or rectangle-DAG theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Ranked next steps}

\subsection*{Priority 1: SAT-SUNFLOWER1}
For slowly growing \(L=L(q)\), prove a SAT-specific robust-sunflower statement for a large spread subfamily
\[
\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal U^{(L)}_{q,6q}
\]
relative to \(\mathcal D^-_{q,6q}\). The desired core must be proper and conditioning on it should make a petal occur with high probability. The theorem must fail on the XOR-SLICE-EASY control for an explicit OR-clause-specific reason.

\subsection*{Priority 2: grow SMALLCORE1}
Find the largest \(L(q)\) for which exact-\(6q\) random 3-CNFs have no UNSAT core of size \(\le L(q)\) with high probability. Use Carenini's 2026 random-3CNF proof-complexity methods as a possible source of sharper structure beyond the crude Tarsi count.

\subsection*{Priority 3: SLICELIFT1}
Define a local exact-weight KW gadget class and search for a solution-carrying balancing gadget satisfying:
\begin{enumerate}
\item exact equality of Alice/Bob target weights;
\item every Alice-only added coordinate decodes to a source answer;
\item proper width-three CNF syntax;
\item polynomial target dimension;
\item explicit survival of the XOR hostile control.
\end{enumerate}
If impossible, prove an impossibility theorem for that gadget class and retire the route.

\subsection*{Priority 4: restricted-model extension}
Use Krieger-style prime-implicant incompressibility, the 2026 multislice formula work, or ranked spreadness to extend SLICEDEPTH2. This is lower reward because it does not close DAG sharing.

\section{Barrier ledger}

The principal current barrier is model transfer to unrestricted monotone DAGs. Relativization and algebrization are not resolved. Natural Proofs must be audited if a constructive large circuit property emerges. No unrestricted lower bound has been proved.

\section{Handoff}

The highest-reward next theorem is SAT-SUNFLOWER1 on \(\mathcal U^{(L)}_{q,6q}\) versus \(\mathcal D^-_{q,6q}\). In parallel, run one explicit SLICELIFT1 balancing-gadget construction and then attempt a local-gadget impossibility theorem if it fails.
