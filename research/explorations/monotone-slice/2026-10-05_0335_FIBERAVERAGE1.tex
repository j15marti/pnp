\section*{FIBER-AVERAGE}
Let $\mu$ be supported on $\Omega_{M,t}=\{X\subseteq[M]:|X|=t\}$ and assume for every $k$-set $T$,
\[
\Pr_\mu[T\subseteq X]=p_k:=\frac{(t)_k}{(M)_k}.
\]
For every fixed such $T$ and $k\le r\le t$,
\[
\boxed{
\frac{1}{\binom{M-k}{r-k}}
\sum_{\substack{S\supseteq T\\|S|=r}}
\Pr_\mu[S\subseteq X]
=
\frac{(t)_r}{(M)_r}=:p_r.
}
\]
\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT.}

For each realization containing $T$, exactly $\binom{t-k}{r-k}$ of the $r$-supersets of $T$ are contained in $X$; otherwise none are. Thus the numerator sum is $\binom{t-k}{r-k}p_k$. Division by $\binom{M-k}{r-k}$ gives $p_r$.

For $r=k+1$, writing $a_j=\Pr_\mu[T\cup\{j\}\subseteq X]$,
\[
\sum_{j\notin T}a_j=(t-k)p_k,
\qquad
\frac1{M-k}\sum_{j\notin T}a_j=p_{k+1}.
\]
Since $0\le a_j\le p_k$, the finite-variable Bhatia--Davis inequality gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{j\notin T}(a_j)
\le p_{k+1}(p_k-p_{k+1}),
\]
hence
\[
\frac{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(a_j)}}{p_{k+1}}
\le \sqrt{\frac{M-t}{t-k}}.
\]
This ceiling is permissive in the sparse regime and is not a pseudorandomness theorem.
