CENTERADD1 left the partition sum open. The search asked for the smallest exposed set whose slice error exceeds $O(r^2/t)$ relative to the product expansion.

\begin{proposition}[ALL-ONES-LOG]
On the exact $t$-slice of an $M$-cube, the all-ones moment on any $r<t$ coordinates satisfies
\[
0\le -\log\frac{(t)_r/(M)_r}{(t/M)^r}
\le \frac{r^2}{2(t-r+1)}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The ratio is $\prod_{j=1}^{r-1}(1-j/t)/(1-j/M)$. Each factor is at most one, and
\[
-\log(1-j/t)+\log(1-j/M)\le j/(t-j),
\]
since $\log(1-j/M)\le 0$ and $-\log(1-x)\le x/(1-x)$. Summing $j\le r-1$ gives the bound.
\end{proof}

So the uncentered all-ones moment stays inside the candidate scale whenever $r=o(t)$. The same bound holds for every fixed-weight slice, including the GE-easy XOR slice.

Exact total variation of the full $r$-marginal against independent Bernoulli$(t/M)$ was computed for $r\le 8$ at $q=16,20,24$. No row exceeds $r^2/t$. At $q=20$, $r=8$, the log-relative all-ones error is $0.451$ times $r^2/t$, and the total variation is $4.8\times 10^{-5}$ times that scale.

This does not transcribe the centered partition identity in TR26-220, and it does not produce a monotone lower bound. The smallest exceeding partition was not found for $r\le 8$. $P\ne NP$ remains OPEN.
