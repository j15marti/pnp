## Model

Exact layer `L(q,t)`: a uniform `t`-subset of the `M=8*binom(q,3)` proper 3-clauses on `q` variables (same generator as TWOSATDEC; replay below). Depth-r certificate: a set `S` of `r` variables such that every assignment `rho` to `S`, followed by unit propagation, yields an empty clause or an unsatisfiable width-at-most-2 skeleton (surviving width-3 clauses ignored). `d(F)` is the least such `r`.

## Results

KNOWN THEOREM (SAT side; Kaporis-Kirousis-Lalas 2006, Hajiaghayi-Sorkin 2003). Random 3-CNF with density below 3.52 is satisfiable w.h.p. Since 3.5 < 3.52, `Pr[L(q, floor(3.5q)) is UNSAT] -> 0`. So the empty draws at q = 40...98 are predicted outcomes, and as q grows the ladder's residual stays undefined with probability tending to 1. It cannot inform SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1. Transfer to the exact layer: with `t=Theta(q)` and `M=Theta(q^3)`, a with-replacement draw has a repeated clause with probability `O(t^2/M)=O(1/q)`. Conditioned on no repeats, the draw is uniform on t-subsets, so the w.h.p. statements carry over.

PROVED/EXACT (elementary; self-checked only, independent review pending). Depth-to-width lemma: if `d(F) <= r` then `F` has a resolution refutation of width at most `r+3`.
Proof. Fix `rho` on `S`. Each unit-propagation step resolves a derived unit against a clause of width at most 3, so every resolvent has width at most 2. An unsatisfiable 2-CNF has a resolution refutation using only clauses of width at most 2 (Krom; resolving two 2-clauses gives width at most 2). So `F|rho` has a refutation of width at most 3. Add back the literals of `S` that `rho` falsifies. Each `F|rho` derivation then becomes a derivation from `F`, of width at most `3+r`, of the clause `C_rho` falsified exactly by `rho`. Resolving the `2^r` clauses `C_rho` along the complete decision tree on `S` uses clauses of width at most `r` and ends in the empty clause. QED.

KNOWN THEOREM (Ben-Sasson-Wigderson 2001, JACM 48(2), via Chvatal-Szemeredi 1988 expansion). For a constant `Delta`, a random 3-CNF with `n` variables and `Delta n` clauses w.h.p. has resolution width at least `c n / Delta^{1+eps}`.

PROVED (as a corollary of the two statements above). For every constant `Delta > 4.4898`, the density at which random 3-CNF is UNSAT w.h.p. (Diaz-Kirousis-Mitsche-Perez-Gimenez 2009), the exact layer `L(q, Delta q)` w.h.p. is UNSAT and has `d(F) >= c_Delta q - 3`. Bounded decision depth is therefore FALSIFIED wherever UNSAT is typical. The depth-r procedure also refutes in time at least `2^{d(F)}`, so the procedure itself has a `2^{Omega(q)}` lower bound on these instances. This is a restricted-algorithm (width-(r+3) resolution) bound, not a bound on general algorithms or circuits.

Scope gap (OPEN, low value). At density 3.5, `d(F)` conditioned on the rare event UNSAT is not covered. BSW expansion failure has only polynomially small probability, which may exceed `Pr[UNSAT]`. Sampling that conditional law does not bear on worst-case or typical-case SAT hardness.
