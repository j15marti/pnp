## Test

Each core covers 9 of the 495 width-4 sets. A full cover needs at least `C(12,4)/9=55` cores. Greedy used 94.

- half layer, k=30: `917/528 = 1.736742e+00`
- full greedy cover, k=94: `43099/7920 = 5.441793e+00`

So the negative charge crosses the layer mass while about two thirds of the layer is still uncovered. Covering the layer and keeping `eta_minus < p_w` are incompatible for this greedy closure. An optimal 55-core cover was not enumerated.
