## Results

**Lemma 1 (PROVED, elementary; not independently reviewed).** If S with |S|=r certifies F, then F has a resolution refutation of width at most r+3.

*Proof.* Fix rho in {0,1}^S. Unit propagation on F|rho is a sequence of resolution steps, each between a derived unit clause and a clause of width at most 3. A contradiction in the 2-CNF skeleton (x =>* not-x =>* x in the implication graph) resolves into units x and not-x using only clauses of width at most 2. So F|rho has a refutation pi_rho of width at most 3. Replace each restricted axiom D|rho by D. Each line C of pi_rho becomes C OR (a subclause of neg-rho), and every resolution step stays valid because no resolved variable is in S. This derives a subclause of neg-rho from F with width at most 3+r. Combining the 2^r derivations along the complete decision tree on S resolves only S variables, with width at most r. QED. Size is at most 2^r * poly(q).

**Lemma 2 (PROVED, first moment).** For t>=Delta q with Delta > ln 2 / ln(8/7) = 5.1909..., the exact-layer F is UNSAT with probability 1 - o(1). *Proof.* For any fixed assignment, exactly M/8 proper clauses falsify it. P[it satisfies all t sampled clauses] = binom(7M/8,t)/binom(M,t) <= (7/8)^t. A union bound gives P[SAT] <= 2^q (7/8)^{Delta q} -> 0. QED.

**Theorem 3 (CONDITIONAL on the cited KNOWN THEOREMs; not independently reviewed).**
(a) For Delta=3.5 the exact layer is SAT with probability 1-o(1). KNOWN THEOREM: the 3-SAT threshold is at least 3.52 (Kaporis, Kirousis, Lalas, Random Struct. Alg. 28(4), 2006; Hajiaghayi, Sorkin 2003).
(b) For every constant Delta>5.19, with probability 1-o(1) F is UNSAT and every certifying set has r >= c_Delta q - 3, where c_Delta = Omega(Delta^{-1-eps}). KNOWN THEOREM: Ben-Sasson and Wigderson, JACM 48(2), 2001, the random k-CNF width bound w(F |- empty) = Omega(n/Delta^{1/(k-2)+eps}), here k=3. Combine with Lemmas 1 and 2.

Model transfer. Both cited theorems are stated for clauses drawn independently (with replacement), possibly with improper clauses. In that model the event "all clauses proper and distinct" has probability bounded below by a constant: the expected number of repeated clauses is O(t^2/M)=O(1/q), and the number of improper clauses is asymptotically Poisson with constant mean. Conditioned on that event, the model is exactly the exact layer. So any event with probability 1-o(1) still has probability 1-o(1) after conditioning. Caveat: the margin 3.5 < 3.52 is thin, so (a) is a statement about the limit, not about q around 100.

**Consequence.** The free-2-SAT local certificate cannot be bounded depth. On the protocol's density 3.5 it has nothing to certify (a). At densities where UNSAT is guaranteed it needs linear depth (b). The certifier family therefore costs 2^{Omega(q)} at Delta>5.19. That is a lower bound for this specific algorithm/proof family only, a known consequence of resolution width. SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1 in its bounded-depth free-2-SAT form is FALSIFIED asymptotically. The TWOSATDEC q-increment protocol at Delta=3.5 is RETIRED as an evidence source. More empty draws are predicted by (a) and carry no information.
