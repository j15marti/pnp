\section*{Directional gap accounting}
Let $f$ be the monotone UNSAT predicate on the exact clause slice. Let
$\mu_+$ be supported on UNSAT formulas and let $\mu_-$ be any comparison
distribution. Define $\delta_-:=\Pr_{\mu_-}[f=1]$.

For any Boolean approximator $A$, assume
\[
\Pr_{\mu_+}[f=1,A=0]\le\eta_+,
\qquad
\Pr_{\mu_-}[f=0,A=1]\le\eta_-.
\]
Then
\[
\Pr_{\mu_+}[A=1]\ge1-\eta_+,
\qquad
\Pr_{\mu_-}[A=1]\le\delta_-+\eta_-,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{
\Pr_{\mu_+}[A=1]-\Pr_{\mu_-}[A=1]
\ge1-\delta_--\eta_+-\eta_-.
}
\]
Thus if an independent moment argument proves
\[
\left|\Pr_{\mu_+}[A=1]-\Pr_{\mu_-}[A=1]\right|\le\varepsilon_A,
\]
the four hypotheses are incompatible whenever
\[
\boxed{\delta_-+\eta_++\eta_-+\varepsilon_A<1.}
\]

The proof is direct event decomposition. No runtime lower bound follows
without a theorem producing such an approximator for every relevant
