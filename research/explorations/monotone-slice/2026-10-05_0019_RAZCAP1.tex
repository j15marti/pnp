\section{Literature cap}
Razborov (1989), as recorded in the approximation-method limitation literature, showed that the pure approximation method cannot prove a general-circuit lower bound better than $O(nn_0)$ for any Boolean function, where $n_0$ is the number of essential variables. The probabilistic-counting form used for monotone and constant-depth bounds is capped by $24(n_0+1)$.

\begin{proposition}[RAZBOROV-SLICE-CAP]
On the proper signed 3-clause universe, $n_0\le M=8\binom q3$. A probabilistic-counting approximation argument of the cited form therefore cannot prove a set-circuit lower bound larger than $24(M+1)$.
\end{proposition}

The cap is $107{,}544$ at $q=16$, $218{,}904$ at $q=20$, $388{,}632$ at $q=24$, and $952{,}344$ at $q=32$. This caps the method. It does not cap SAT, and it applies equally to the GE-easy XOR slice, whose Valiant lift is polynomial.

\section{Product span}
\begin{proposition}[PAIR-SPAN]
Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ agree on every squarefree moment through order $k$. Every real linear combination of squarefree monomials of degree at most $k$ has the same expectation under $\mu$ and $\nu$. In particular, if an order-three fractional design exists, the product span of width-$\le 3$ monomials does not separate its support from the uniform slice.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Expectation is linear, and each monomial of degree at most $k$ is a matched moment.
\end{proof}

The H2202 distill claims that design at $(q,t)=(20,70)$ by an 84-formula rational certificate. This note does not re-audit that certificate. The corollary is conditional on it. Unions are not linear combinations: SLICEAPPROX1 already bounds that remainder, and it is also generic to the XOR slice.

\section{Next theorem}
The missing step is a compiler that sends SAT, and not the XOR slice, to a model whose per-gate approximation error survives Proposition RAZBOROV-SLICE-CAP. Moment matching and product span do not supply it. $P\ne NP$ remains OPEN.
