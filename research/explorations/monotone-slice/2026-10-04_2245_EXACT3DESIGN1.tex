\section{Statement}

Let \(\mathcal C_q\) be the universe of proper signed 3-clauses on \(q\)
variables and let
\[
M_q=8\binom q3.
\]
For \(t\)-subsets of clauses, write \(U_{q,t}\) for the uniform exact slice.

\begin{theorem}[Q16-ORDER3-DESIGN]
There exists a probability distribution \(\mu_{16}\) supported entirely on
UNSAT formulas \(F\subseteq\mathcal C_{16}\) with
\[
|F|=56
\]
such that for every set \(S\subseteq\mathcal C_{16}\) of distinct clauses
with \(|S|\le3\),
\[
\Pr_{F\sim\mu_{16}}[S\subseteq F]
=
\Pr_{F\sim U_{16,56}}[S\subseteq F]
=
\frac{(56)_{|S|}}{(4480)_{|S|}}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Q18-ORDER3-DESIGN]
There exists a probability distribution \(\mu_{18}\) supported entirely on
UNSAT formulas \(F\subseteq\mathcal C_{18}\) with
\[
|F|=63
\]
such that for every set \(S\subseteq\mathcal C_{18}\) of distinct clauses
with \(|S|\le3\),
\[
\Pr_{F\sim\mu_{18}}[S\subseteq F]
=
\Pr_{F\sim U_{18,63}}[S\subseteq F]
=

\end{theorem}

\section{Certificate construction}

For each \(q\), an exact canonical orbit census is taken over all unordered
three-clause subsets of each sampled UNSAT formula.  The exact target orbit
vector of the uniform slice is generated independently using the
FIXED-CLAUSE-TARGET identity from FRACTIONALDESIGN3-EXACT1.

Dual-guided column generation enlarges the UNSAT orbit hull until the
floating-point LP reaches zero residual.  A basic feasible solution uses
exactly 84 UNSAT formulas.

The 84-by-84 system is then solved over the integers/rationals using a
fraction-free domain-matrix solve.  In both cases:

\begin{itemize}
\item every rational weight is strictly positive;
\item the weights sum exactly to one;
\item all 84 canonical triple-orbit equations have integer residual exactly
zero.
\end{itemize}

This exact rational step, rather than the preceding floating-point LP, is the
certificate underlying the theorem.

\section{Independent UNSAT verification}

The 84 support formulas in each certificate were checked independently by an
exhaustive bit-parallel truth table.

For \(q=16\), all
\[
2^{16}=65{,}536
\]
assignments were tested against every support formula.

For \(q=18\), all
\[
2^{18}=262{,}144
\]
assignments were tested against every support formula.

Every support formula had zero satisfying assignments.

Thus the finite theorem does not rely only on the DPLL implementation used
during sampling and search.

\section{Why orbit matching gives full three-wise matching}

Let
\[
G=S_q\ltimes(\mathbb Z_2)^q
\]
act on the clause universe by variable permutations and independent variable
complementations.

Start from the exact rational distribution on the 84 certificate formulas and
symmetrize it under \(G\).  UNSAT and exact weight are invariant under this
action.

The canonical three-clause orbit equations imply that for every orbit
\(\mathcal O\) of distinct clause triples, the expected number of selected
triples in \(\mathcal O\) equals the uniform-slice expectation.

After symmetrization, every particular triple within an orbit has the same
inclusion probability.  Therefore every distinct three-clause set \(T\)
satisfies
\[
\Pr[T\subseteq F]
=
\frac{(t)_3}{(M)_3}.
\tag{1}
\]

For a fixed pair \(A\) of clauses,
\[
\sum_{C\notin A}
\Pr[A\cup\{C\}\subseteq F]
=
(t-2)\Pr[A\subseteq F].
\]
Using (1) on the left yields
\[
\Pr[A\subseteq F]
=
\frac{(t)_2}{(M)_2}.
\]
Summing once more over partners gives the one-clause marginal
\[
\Pr[C\in F]=\frac tM.
\]
Hence the symmetrized certificate matches every squarefree clause moment
through degree three, not merely the averaged orbit counts.

\section{Connection to known local-refutation theory}

In random-CSP proof complexity, \(t\)-wise uniformity is a central hardness
parameter for low-degree Sum-of-Squares refutation.  Kothari--Mori--
O'Donnell--Witmer prove SoS lower bounds when the predicate supports
\(t\)-wise-uniform satisfying distributions, and 2026 work of
Chan--d'Orsi--Xu extends the local-independence viewpoint to more general
random CSP models.

The present object is different: the distribution is over globally UNSAT
3-CNF instances and matches low-order \emph{clause-incidence} moments of the
uniform exact slice.  No SoS theorem is being transferred here.  The
literature only confirms that growing local-design order is a natural
refutation-hardness coordinate.

\section{What this proves and does not prove}

The exact finite result proves that global UNSAT can coexist with perfect
three-wise camouflage in the clause-incidence coordinates at two distinct
sizes.

It does not prove:
\begin{itemize}
\item existence for all \(q\);
\item order-four or growing-order designs;
\item pseudo-independence with the parameters required by TR26-220 at growing
width;
\item indistinguishability by general monotone circuits;
\item a superpolynomial monotone slice lower bound;
\item \(P\ne NP\).
\end{itemize}

\(\binom{M-1}{3}\) triples around a fixed clause.

At the same time, retain XOR-SLICE-EASY as a mandatory hostile control:
growing local designs alone cannot imply a general monotone lower bound.
