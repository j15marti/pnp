\section{Abstract local gadget}
Let \(V\) be source constraint variants with \(|V|\ge3\). Let \(F\) be Bob states and let
\[
b:F\to V
\]
be surjective, where \(b(f)\) is the unique variant violated by state \(f\).
A local set gadget has coordinate universe \(U\), Alice sets \(A_v\subseteq U\), and Bob sets \(B_f\subseteq U\).

Assume local soundness and completeness:
\[
v\ne b(f)\Longrightarrow A_v\subseteq B_f,
\tag{LS}
\]
\[
A_{b(f)}\setminus B_f\ne\varnothing.
\tag{LC}
\]
Assume exact block weights
\[
|A_v|=a\quad\forall v,\qquad |B_f|=b_0\quad\forall f.
\]

\begin{theorem}[LOCAL-WEIGHT-GAP]
Every locally sound and complete gadget with \(|V|\ge3\) and surjective \(b\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{a<b_0}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(f\). Choose \(v\ne b(f)\). By (LS), \(A_v\subseteq B_f\), so \(a\le b_0\).

Assume \(a=b_0\). Every inclusion in (LS) is then equality:
\[
v\ne b(f)\Longrightarrow A_v=B_f.
\tag{1}
\]
Choose distinct \(u,v,w\in V\). By surjectivity choose \(f_u,f_v\) with \(b(f_u)=u\) and \(b(f_v)=v\). Equation (1) gives
\[
A_v=A_w=B_{f_u},\qquad A_u=A_w=B_{f_v},
\]
so \(A_u=A_v=A_w\). Repeating with every other variant shows all \(A_v\) equal one common set \(A\).

For arbitrary \(f\), choose \(v\ne b(f)\). Then (1) gives \(B_f=A_v=A\). But \(A_{b(f)}=A\), contradicting (LC). Hence \(a<b_0\).
\end{proof}

\section{Exact-weight consequence}
Consider a block-separable reduction with independent source blocks and disjoint local coordinate universes. Exact total weight for every independent Alice choice forces a constant Alice weight \(a_i\) in each block; exact total weight for every independent Bob choice forces a constant Bob weight \(b_i\). LOCAL-WEIGHT-GAP gives
\[
a_i<b_i
\]
for every nontrivial block, so
\[
\sum_i a_i<\sum_i b_i.
\]
Thus such a reduction cannot put Alice and Bob on the same exact Hamming layer.

Proper 3CNF has eight signed variants on each variable triple, so the hypothesis \(|V|\ge3\) holds. The same obstruction applies to the usual local 3XOR alphabet.

\section{Scope}
The theorem does not rule out cross-block compensation, globally coupled encodings, or nonlocal decoding rules. It rules out the natural block-separable exact-weight repair of the GGKS/GKRS local lifting architecture.

\section{Research consequence}
Cosmetic local padding and local constant-weight code replacements should be retired. A surviving lifting construction must use genuinely nonlocal balancing while ensuring every Alice-only coordinate decodes to a violated source constraint.

The primary effort should therefore move to SLICEDENSITY1, especially \(t=q\), where the exact layer is computationally hard but the uniform layer is SAT with high probability.

\section{Status}
LOCAL-WEIGHT-GAP has a complete elementary proof at the declared abstraction, but promotion is withheld pending independent review of whether the abstraction covers every intended local-lifting proposal. No unrestricted monotone lower bound follows. \(P\ne NP\) remains OPEN.
