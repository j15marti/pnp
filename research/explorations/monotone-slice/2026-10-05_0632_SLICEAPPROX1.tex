{\Large SLICEAPPROX1: moment matching fixes a DNF only up to the next inclusion}\\[4pt]

Razborov's approximation method replaces each gate by a small-width DNF and takes a union bound on the error. Wegener's theorem is that a slice function is a set circuit, and the standard monotone lower-bound methods do not transfer. TR26-220 and Cavalar--Kumar--Rossman supply sunflower control for DNF approximators, not a slice SAT theorem.

\begin{theorem}[SLICE-DNF-GAP]
Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be laws on the same exact $t$-slice that agree on every squarefree moment through order $k$. Let $F$ be a monotone DNF of width $w\le k$ with term set $\mathcal T$. Then the first Bonferroni sum of $F$ agrees under $\mu$ and $\nu$, and
\[
\bigl|\mathbb E_\mu F-\mathbb E_\nu F\bigr|
\le
\sum_{\substack{\{T,T'\}\\|T\cup T'|>k}}
\Pr[T\cup T'\subseteq x].
\]
If $k\ge 2w-1$, every intersecting term pair is matched, and the sum is at most
\[
\binom{|\mathcal T|}{2}\frac{(t)_{2w}}{(M)_{2w}}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each term is a squarefree monomial of degree $w\le k$, so the first Bonferroni sum agrees. The truncation error of the union is at most the sum of pairwise term intersections. A pairwise intersection on at most $k$ coordinates is a matched moment and cancels in the difference. Two distinct width-$w$ terms that intersect use at most $2w-1$ coordinates, so they cancel once $k\ge 2w-1$. A disjoint pair uses $2w$ coordinates, whose exact-slice probability is $(t)_{2w}/(M)_{2w}$.
\end{proof}

The disjoint-pair scale at density $3.5$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
q&w&|\mathcal T|&\text{scale}\\
\hline
20&2&1000&1.59\times 10^{-3}\\
20&3&1000&8.21\times 10^{-8}\\
24&2&1000&3.37\times 10^{-4}\\
24&3&1000&8.12\times 10^{-9}
\end{array}
\]
The width-3 row requires matching through order $5$, not order $3$. The width-2 row is the order-3 case already in ORDER4GAP1.

This is the approximation-method statement that moment matching can support. It does not make the approximator err on one distribution and not the other, and it applies unchanged to the GE-easy XOR slice. A gate-by-gate union bound still needs a separate per-gate error that survives that control. $P\ne NP$ remains OPEN.
