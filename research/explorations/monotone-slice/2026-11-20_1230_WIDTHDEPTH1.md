## Candidate and falsifiers

Candidate (the bounded-depth branch of SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1): for some fixed `r`, or `r=o(n)`, "free exact 2-SAT after unit propagation under every assignment of an `r`-variable set" certifies a non-vanishing fraction of UNSAT random 3-CNF formulas at constant density.

Falsifiers considered: (F1) the certifier is simulated by bounded-width resolution, and random 3-CNF needs linear width; (F2) the without-replacement proper-clause generator differs from the Ben-Sasson–Wigderson model on a set of non-vanishing probability; (F3) conditioning on UNSAT below the threshold puts the mass on atypical, non-expanding formulas. F1 holds. F2 fails, so the transfer goes through. F3 is open, and it limits the result to densities above the threshold.

## Lemma (PROVED/EXACT, standard; not independently reviewed)

Let `F` be a 3-CNF and `S` a set of `r` variables. Suppose that for every assignment `rho` to `S`, unit propagation on `F|rho` either produces the empty clause or leaves a residual whose width-at-most-2 clauses form an unsatisfiable 2-CNF. Then `F` has a resolution refutation of width at most `r+3`. The same holds for an adaptive decision tree of depth `r`.

Proof. Fix a leaf `rho`. Each unit-propagation step resolves a unit clause against a clause of `F|rho`, which has width at most 3, and produces a clause of width at most 2. An unsatisfiable 2-CNF has a resolution refutation in which every derived clause has width at most 2, because 2-SAT is decided by width-2 resolution (the implication-graph closure). So `F|rho` has a refutation of width at most 3. Ben-Sasson–Wigderson (JACM 2001, Lemma 3.2) gives `w(F) <= max(w(F|x=0), w(F|x=1)) + 1`. Applying it along the `r` levels of the decision tree (or of the fixed-set tree) gives `w(F ⊢ ⊥) <= r + 3`. ∎

## Consequence (KNOWN THEOREM applied; scope stated)

Let `F` be a random 3-CNF with `n` variables and `m = Δn` clauses, with `Δ` a constant above 4.4898. Above that density `F` is UNSAT w.h.p. (Díaz–Kirousis–Mitsche–Pérez-Giménez 2009). For every `ε>0`, w.h.p. every resolution refutation of `F` has width `Ω(n/Δ^{1+ε})` (Ben-Sasson–Wigderson 2001, Cor. for k=3; size version in Chvátal–Szemerédi 1988).

Transfer to the repository generator. The generator samples `t` distinct proper clauses uniformly. With-replacement sampling has no repeated clause with probability `1 - O(m^2/(8 binom(n,3))) -> 1`, and conditioned on that event the two models coincide. So the w.h.p. statement transfers.

Hence any certifier of the TWOSATDEC form with `r <= c·n/Δ^{1+ε} - 3` certifies a `o(1)` fraction of the formulas, and its residual among UNSAT formulas tends to 1. Fixed `r`, and every `r=o(n)`, fails asymptotically. The bounded-depth branch of SAT-LOCAL-ERROR1 is therefore FALSIFIED at densities `Δ > 4.4898`.
