\section{Question}
PAIRMATCH3-REVIEW asked whether the residual at \(t=\mathrm{round}(3.5q)\), after pair matching, is a small excess core or a delocalized incompatibility. Exact \(2\)-SAT, exact Gaussian elimination, and depth-\(1\) triple attention are treated as free. This pilot measures the core directly.

\section{Method}
Distinct proper \(3\)-clauses, \(t=\mathrm{round}(3.5q)\), seed \(80420\), independent of PAIRMATCH3. SAT is the assignment-cube mask, checked against brute force on \(30\) formulas at \(q=6\). The full \(8\)-clause triple is recovered as an \(8\)-clause MUS.

Pair matching uses the six PAIRMATCH3 coordinates, standardized on SAT, caliper \(d_{\mathrm{pair}}\le 0.75\). Deletion produces a certified MUS: every one-clause deletion is SAT. Branch-and-bound then tries to certify minimum cardinality, with a \(40000\)-node cap. Excess is the Scott--Sorkin coordinate \(e=2m-n_{\mathrm{core}}\). The free-resource checks are the parity shadow of each clause, eliminated over \(\mathrm{GF}(2)\), and the coefficient of variation of LP slack \(\min(x_i,1-x_i)\).

\section{Results}

No kept pair received a certified cardinality minimum. A separate \(40\)-start deletion pass still had \(m_{\min}\in\{13,\ldots,18\}\) at \(q=10\) and \(\{14,\ldots,23\}\) at \(q=12\). Shared core clauses with the matched SAT twin were \(0\), \(2\), and \(1\). Variable overlap is \(1\): both formulas use essentially every variable, so it is not a localization statistic.

The parity shadow is inconsistent for every sampled SAT formula and every sampled UNSAT formula at these three sizes (\(187/187\), \(190/190\), \(194/194\) SAT). Gaussian elimination does not separate the populations. The LP relaxation was feasible on every kept pair. Slack-variation medians were \(0.56/0.55\), \(0.91/0.85\), and \(0.84/0.73\) for UNSAT/SAT. That is not a separator at this sample size.

\section{Reading}
These are not the unique minimum-excess MUFs of the pure-literal regime. The certified objects are minimal cores whose clause count is a large fraction of \(q\), whose triples are mostly absent from the pair-matched twin, and which survive the two free global checks that were actually run. Cardinality-minimum remains uncertified, kept counts are single digits, and \(q\le 14\). So this is an upper bound on core size, not a linear-core theorem, and not a monotone lower bound.

The resource that still has to scale is certifying this core. Pair charts, the parity shadow, and LP slack do not name it. Depth-\(1\) triple attention on the bulk formula was already quiet. The core's own triples are a sparse symmetric difference against the twin, which is a search over which triples synchronize, not another low-order marginal.
