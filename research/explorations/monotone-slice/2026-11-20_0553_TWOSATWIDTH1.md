## Candidate and falsifiers

CANDIDATE (now PROVED, conditional on the cited KNOWN THEOREMS): let F be an unsatisfiable 3-CNF with a set S of variables, |S| = r. Suppose every assignment of S, after unit propagation, yields either an empty clause or an unsatisfiable width-at-most-2 skeleton. This is exactly the TWOSATDEC certificate. Then the resolution width of refuting F is at most r + 3. Equivalently, r_min(F) >= w(F |- empty) - 3.

Falsifiers considered:
1. The TWOSATDEC certificate counts something resolution cannot simulate in small width. Checked: the certificate uses only unit propagation and a 2-SAT SCC test, and width-3 clauses are explicitly not charged.
2. The sampling model does not match the random-CNF model in the cited width theorem. Handled by contiguity, below.
3. The density 3.5 is in the regime where the width theorem applies. It is not, and this is the main diagnosis below.
4. Finite data contradict the bound. They cannot, because the bound is asymptotic with a small constant. The finite runs here are a consistency check only.

## Diagnosis of the TWOSATDEC protocol (KNOWN THEOREM)

Random 3-SAT at clause density below 3.52 is satisfiable with probability tending to 1 (Kaporis, Kirousis and Lalas, Random Structures and Algorithms 2006; Hajiaghayi and Sorkin 2003). The TWOSATDEC sampler uses density 3.5 < 3.52, so P[UNSAT] -> 0 as q grows. The empty draws in TWOSATDEC57 through TWOSATDEC94 are therefore what the theorem predicts. They carry no information about decision depth, and adding more q values cannot change that. Any UNSAT draws that do occur at this density come from a vanishing-probability event. A CANDIDATE explanation, not proved here, is that small unsatisfiable cores dominate them, and small cores give small r. If so, the earlier depth-4 and depth-5 survivors at q = 26 to 42 come from that rare-event regime and do not show the typical behavior.

## Lemma (PROVED/EXACT; elementary; not independently reviewed)

Let F be a CNF whose clauses have width at most 3. Let S be a variable set with |S| = r such that, for every rho in {0,1}^S, unit propagation on F|rho either derives the empty clause or leaves a set of width-at-most-2 clauses that is unsatisfiable. Then F has a resolution refutation of width at most r + 3.

Proof. Fix rho.
1. Each unit-propagation step resolves a unit clause against a clause of width at most 3 and produces a clause of width at most 2. The propagated restriction of every original clause is derivable from F|rho in width at most 3.
2. An unsatisfiable 2-CNF has a resolution refutation of width at most 2, because resolving two width-2 clauses gives width at most 2. If the empty clause appears during propagation, step 1 already gives it.
3. So F|rho has a refutation in width at most 3.
4. Standard restriction lifting (Ben-Sasson and Wigderson 2001, the restriction lemma): add back to every line the literals of rho that the restriction removed. This turns the refutation into a derivation from F of the clause C_rho falsified by rho, in width at most 3 + r.
5. The 2^r clauses C_rho are the leaves of a complete decision tree on S. Resolving them up the tree derives the empty clause in width at most r.

So the total width is at most r + 3. QED.

## Consequence (CONDITIONAL on KNOWN THEOREMS; scope stated)

Ben-Sasson and Wigderson (JACM 48(2) 2001) prove the following for random 3-CNF with Delta*n clauses and constant Delta: with probability tending to 1, every resolution refutation has width Omega(n / Delta^{1+eps}). Above density 4.4898 random 3-CNF is UNSAT with probability tending to 1 (Diaz, Kirousis, Mitsche and Perez-Gimenez, TCS 2009).

The TWOSATDEC generator samples t distinct proper clauses uniformly. That is the with-replacement model conditioned on no repeated clause. For t = Theta(q) the expected number of repeats is O(1/q), so every with-high-probability event transfers between the two models.

Therefore: for every constant Delta > 4.4898, with probability tending to 1, the exact-layer formula is UNSAT and r_min(F) >= c_Delta * q - 3 for some c_Delta > 0.

On the typical-UNSAT layer, free exact 2-SAT decision depth is unbounded and in fact linear. This retires the "bounded depth on the exact layer" hypothesis that the TWOSATDEC notes kept open, in the regime where UNSAT is typical. At density 3.5 the question is about a conditioned rare event, and this note leaves that case OPEN.

This is a proof-system statement about one certificate class, which is a restriction of tree-like resolution with 2-SAT leaves. It gives no lower bound for arbitrary algorithms, circuits or monotone circuits. Resolution lower bounds for random 3-CNF have been KNOWN since Chvatal and Szemeredi (1988). The new content here is only the exact transfer to the TWOSATDEC certificate semantics.

Source disclosure: the cited theorem statements were recalled, not re-fetched, in this run. In particular, the exact BSW corollary number and the epsilon form of the density exponent should be checked against the primary PDF before anyone promotes this result.
