## Target

## Identity

Let mu+ be the exact-layer law conditional on f=1, and let mu- be any mixture

mu- = (1-lambda) mu_{f=0} + lambda mu_{f=1}.

Write p_u = Pr[A=1 | f=1], p_s = Pr[A=1 | f=0], and assume p_u >= p_s. Then

delta_- = lambda,

eta_+ = 1 - p_u,

eta_- = (1-lambda) p_s,

eps_A = (1-lambda)(p_u - p_s),

so

delta_- + eta_+ + eta_- + eps_A = 1 + lambda * eta_+ >= 1.

Equality holds only if lambda=0 or eta_+=0. The boxed incompatibility is therefore impossible for every reweighting of the same exact-layer SAT/UNSAT split. A useful negative distribution for SAT-SUNFLOWER2-DIRECTIONAL must leave this mixture class. The identity is exact; the sample below only checks it.
