\title{Worst-case derivation cost: what the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ can and cannot be}
\author{CLAUDE, scheduled run 2026-11-07}\maketitle
\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} No lower bound is claimed.

\section*{Setup}
$R$ is a Cook--Reckhow system: a sound, complete refutation system with polynomial-time verifier $V_R(F,\pi)$; $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)=|\pi|$ (DAG encoding of a derivation over the signed 3-uniform hypergraph $H_F$: vertices are variables, a hyperedge with sign vector is a clause). $\overline W_R(N)=\max_F\min_\pi|\pi|$. Free knowledge of the best $\pi$ is exactly the min.

\section*{Results (PROVED/EXACT unless stated)}
\begin{prop}[Nondeterminism] $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is p-bounded; then UNSAT$\in$NP, so NP$=$coNP.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[Bridge dichotomy] Let Bridge$(R)$ denote $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. Since $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\iff P=NP$, Bridge$(R)\iff(P\ne NP)\ \vee\ (R\text{ p-bounded})$.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[Circularity] If $R$ is unconditionally known not p-bounded (Resolution: Haken; Cutting Planes; Polynomial Calculus; bounded-depth Frege), then Bridge$(R)\iff P\neq NP$. Hence for such $R$ the bridge is not a weaker sufficient lemma, and a lower bound plus bridge is just $P\ne NP$ restated.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[Non-circular zone, KNOWN THEOREM via Cook's translation, citation to be rechecked] If a polynomial-time $A$ decides SAT and PV proves $\forall F\,(A(F)=0\to F\in\mathrm{UNSAT})$, then EF has polynomial-size refutations of all UNSAT $F$.\end{prop}
So a non-circular bridge exists only for $R\supseteq$ Frege/EF and requires: $P=NP\Rightarrow\exists A$ with feasibly provable UNSAT-soundness (a reflection gap; OPEN). Free semantic answer rules are unsound and remain RETIRED.

\section*{Consequence for the five roles}
The program splits into (i) bridge for EF-strength $R$ (provability/reflection gap) and (ii) superpolynomial $\overline W_{EF}$ (no unconditional Frege/EF lower bound is known). Hard families must survive all of EF, so resolution/PHP/random-3CNF width arguments give controls, not a proof. PQ ledger: Schur-square/Hilbert-function invariants are ANALOGY/CANDIDATE only here.
