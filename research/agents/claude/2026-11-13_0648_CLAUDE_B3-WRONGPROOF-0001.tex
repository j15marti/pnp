\section*{Shared objective and the arrow audited}
The scheduled directive fixes
\[
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi),
\qquad
\mathrm{B3}_R:\ \mathrm{SAT}\in P\ \Longrightarrow\ \overline W_R(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N).
\]
Free choice of the optimal derivation is granted (the minimum is not charged for search). Together with an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound on $\overline W_R$, $\mathrm{B3}_R$ would give $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. \section*{Literature preflight (barrier-transfer)}
Main obstacles: (O1) SAT$\in P$ supplies an algorithm, not a \emph{proof} of its correctness inside a weak theory; (O2) no superpolynomial EF lower bound is known. Primary source read this run (full text extracted locally from ECCC):
N.~Fleming, S.~Grosser, T.~Pitassi, R.~Robere, \emph{Provable Reductions in TFNP}, ECCC TR26-109 (June 26, 2026), \url{https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/109/}. Relevant statements, quoted in substance:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Def.~3.7 / Prop.~3.8.} $R\in\mathrm{TFNP}$ is \emph{EF-provably in FP} if for every $n$ there is a poly-size circuit $A$ and a poly-size EF proof that $V_R(x,A(x))$ holds. If so, $\langle EF,R\rangle\equiv_p EF$.
\item \textbf{Thm.~5.2.} If $P\ge_p EF$ has poly-size $P$-proofs of its reflection principle $\mathrm{Refl}_P$, then $P\equiv_p\langle EF,\mathrm{WrongProof}_P\rangle$, where $\mathrm{WrongProof}_P$ = given $(F,x,\Pi)$, output a clause of $F$ falsified by $x$ or an incorrect step of the claimed $P$-refutation $\Pi$. Always $\mathrm{WrongProof}_P\in\mathrm{FP}$.
\item \textbf{Consequence stated by the authors.} $G_1\equiv_p\langle EF,\mathrm{WrongProof}_{G_1}\rangle$; so if $\mathrm{WrongProof}_{G_1}$ were EF-provably in FP, EF would p-simulate $G_1$.
\end{itemize}
Also consulted (via search, secondary): Cook--Reckhow 1979; Krajíček, \emph{A note on SAT algorithms and proof complexity}, IPL 112 (2012) (soundness-provability condition). Transfer classification: FGPR Thm.~5.2/Prop.~3.8 are \textbf{directly transferable theorems} for $R=EF$; for $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ they transfer only through the declared ER2 subgrammar (lower direction) and are \textbf{not} transferred to the full mixed native grammar (open arrow C3 in the corpus).

\section*{Results}
\begin{definition}
For a total correct polynomial-time SAT decider $A$ (exists iff SAT$\in P$), let $P_A$ be the proof system whose refutations of a CNF $F$ are either EF-refutations or the one-symbol certificate $\star$, accepted iff $A(F)=\textsc{unsat}$. Let $R^\ast_A:=\mathrm{WrongProof}_{P_A}\in\mathrm{FP}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact reformulation of $\mathrm{B3}_{EF}$]\label{thm:reform}
\[
\mathrm{B3}_{EF}\iff\Bigl[\mathrm{SAT}\in P\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists A\ \text{(correct poly-time decider)}:\ EF\ge_p\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle\Bigr].
\]
A sufficient condition for the right side is: SAT$\in P$ implies that for some such $A$, $R^\ast_A$ is EF-provably in FP (Def.~3.7).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume SAT$\in P$ and fix $A$. (i) $P_A\ge_p EF$ by construction, and $P_A$ is p-bounded: every UNSAT CNF has the constant-size refutation $\star$. In particular each (Tseitin-encoded negation of a) reflection formula $\mathrm{Refl}_{P_A}$, being a tautology of polynomial size, has a constant-size $P_A$-proof. So Thm.~5.2 gives $P_A\equiv_p\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle$, hence $\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle$ is p-bounded.
($\Leftarrow$) If $EF\ge_p\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle$ then EF is p-bounded, i.e.\ $\overline W_{EF}(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N)$ (line count and bit size are polynomially related for EF).
($\Rightarrow$) If EF is p-bounded and SAT$\in P$, then EF-refutations of polynomial size can be \emph{found} in polynomial time by search-to-decision self-reduction on the NP relation ``$\Pi$ is an EF refutation of $F$ of size $\le p(|F|)$''. Hence a polynomial-time map sends any $\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle$-refutation of $F$ to an EF-refutation of $F$, i.e.\ $EF\ge_p\langle EF,R^\ast_A\rangle$. The sufficient condition is Prop.~3.8.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[convention check]\label{rem:conv}
Thm.~5.2 is stated for FGPR's precise encoding of $\mathrm{Refl}_P$ and of ``$P$ proves a formula''. The step ``$P_A$ has poly-size proofs of $\mathrm{Refl}_{P_A}$'' uses only that $P_A$ refutes \emph{every} UNSAT CNF of the appropriate (Tseitin) form in constant size; any polynomial overhead of FGPR's convention is absorbed. This should be independently checked against FGPR Def.~2.4 by a peer before any LSPV adoption.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Membership-only barrier]\label{thm:barrier}
Call a purported proof of $\mathrm{B3}_{EF}$ \emph{membership-only} if the only property of $R^\ast_A$ it uses is that $R^\ast_A$ is a total search problem in FP, i.e.\ if it is an instance of the schema
\[
\mathcal M:\qquad \forall R\in\mathrm{TFNP}\cap\mathrm{FP}:\quad EF\ge_p\langle EF,R\rangle .
\]
Then $\mathcal M\Rightarrow G_1\le_p EF$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$G_1\ge_p EF$ and $G_1$ has poly-size proofs of its own reflection principle (KNOWN; used in FGPR \S1.1). By Thm.~5.2, $G_1\equiv_p\langle EF,\mathrm{WrongProof}_{G_1}\rangle$ with $\mathrm{WrongProof}_{G_1}\in\mathrm{FP}$. Apply $\mathcal M$.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Interpretation.} $G_1\le_p EF$ is \textbf{OPEN} and believed false (FGPR: it relates to $\Pi^b_1$-conservativity of $T^1_2$ over $S^1_2$). So unless that is true, \emph{any} proof of $\mathrm{B3}_{EF}$ must exploit something about $A$ beyond ``$A$ is a correct polynomial-time algorithm'' --- for instance, that $A$'s correctness has short EF proofs (Prop.~3.8), or SAT-specific structure. This is the precise formal location of obstacle (O1), and it explains why the corpus' universal-reflection families $H_{A,N}$ (LSPV-1/353, /357, /361) have stalled: short ER2/EF proofs of $H_{A,N}$ are \emph{exactly} the statement that $R^\ast_A$-reductions are EF-simulable, and the mere existence of $A$ does not supply them.

\noindent\textbf{Effect on the user's ``free optimal sequence'' strategy.} Granting free knowledge of the optimal derivation is legitimate for the lower-bound half and is preserved here. It does not help the bridge: Theorem~\ref{thm:reform} shows the bridge is about \emph{existence} of short EF proofs, already a free-choice quantity, and Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier} shows that existence cannot be inferred from the algorithm's existence alone without new provability input.
