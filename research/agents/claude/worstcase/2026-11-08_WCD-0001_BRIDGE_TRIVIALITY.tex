\section{Setting}
$R$ is a sound Cook--Reckhow system, $\mathrm{cost}_R$ proof size (free strategy). $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$: $\SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. $\mathrm{LB}(R)$: $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial.

\begin{theorem}[Triviality dichotomy]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
(a) If $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ is a theorem, then $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\iff \SAT\notin P$.
(b) $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\wedge\mathrm{LB}(R)\Rightarrow \SAT\notin P$ for every $R$; but if $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ is already known, $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ is exactly as hard as the goal.
\end{theorem}
\emph{Proof.} (a) $\mathrm{Bridge}$ is $A\Rightarrow B$ with $B$ false, i.e. $\neg A$. (b) contrapositive. $\square$

Hence for $R=$ Resolution (Haken), bounded-depth Frege, etc., the bridge is not a ``weaker'' target. A useful bridge needs an $R$ where \emph{both} halves are open, and must be proved by simulation, not by the lower bound.

\begin{proposition}[Provability barrier]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
Let $R_T$ accept $(F,\sigma,t)$ where $\sigma$ is a $T$-proof of $\mathrm{Cor}(M)$ and $t$ the accepting trace of $M$ on $F$. If $T\vdash\mathrm{Cor}(M)$ for a polynomial $M$, proofs have size $O(1)+\mathrm{poly}(N)$, so $\overline W_{R_T}\le\mathrm{poly}$. Conversely $\mathrm{Bridge}(R_T)$ needs: $\SAT\in P$ via $M\Rightarrow T\vdash\mathrm{Cor}(M)$ (up to poly-size proofs). This is a reflection principle, not derivable from $\SAT\in P$ alone ($\Pi_1$ truths are not all $T$-provable, G\"odel).
\end{proposition}
So $\mathrm{LB}(R_T)$ would only show that $T$ cannot prove any polynomial decider correct---an independence-type statement strictly weaker than $P\ne NP$ in general. \status{PROVED/EXACT} for the equivalence above; the claim that it is strictly weaker is \status{CANDIDATE}.

\begin{proposition}[Nondeterminism]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
$\overline W_R$ is a nondeterministic (proof-size) measure. $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ for one $R$ gives nothing about $\SAT\notin P$; Cook--Reckhow: superpolynomial bounds for all $R$ is $NP\ne coNP$, strictly stronger than $P\neq NP$ unless the bridge supplies the link.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Portfolios and switching]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
In a system whose proofs are shared DAGs, union of derivations $\pi_1,\dots,\pi_k$ costs $\le\sum|\pi_i|$, so adaptive strategy switching adds no more than additive overhead and the min over strategies is the best single one. For a combined system $R_1\oplus R_2$ (e.g. clauses plus polynomials) translation rules are required; lower bounds for $R_1,R_2$ do not transfer \status{CANDIDATE: needs per-system audit}.
\end{proposition}

\section{Productive restriction}
Replace the bridge by $\mathrm{Bridge}_{\mathcal C}(R)$: every algorithm in class $\mathcal C$ deciding UNSAT yields poly-size $R$-derivations. \status{KNOWN THEOREM}: DPLL$\to$tree-like Res; CDCL with restarts $\to$ Res polynomially simulates Res (Atserias--Fichte--Thurley; Pipatsrisawat--Darwiche); Gr\"obner $\to$ PC. The open task: a fixed $R$ and class $\mathcal C$ containing all polynomial algorithms with $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ open. Optimal proof systems are open (Kraj\'i\v{c}ek--Pudl\'ak: $NEE{=}coNEE$ suffices; $P{=}NP$ gives one non-uniformly) \status{KNOWN}; no uniform fixed $R$ with $P{=}NP\Rightarrow R$ $p$-bounded is known to be explicit and sound.
