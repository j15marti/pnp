\title{Worst-case derivation complexity $\overline W_R(N)$: audit of the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$}
\date{2026-11-06}\begin{document}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} No lower bound and no transfer theorem is claimed.

\section*{Setup}
$R$ is a fixed sound derivation system (e.g.\ a multisigned DAG-hypergraph rewriting system), $\mathrm{cost}_R$ is DAG size with sharing, and
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$.
Write $\mathrm{LB}(R)$: $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial. $\mathrm{Br}(R)$: $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.
Free choice of the optimal $\pi$ is harmless: it only lowers $\overline W_R$, so a lower bound survives (PROVED/EXACT, trivial).

\section*{Results of this run}
\begin{lemma}[Composition, PROVED/EXACT]
$\mathrm{Br}(R)\wedge\mathrm{LB}(R)\Rightarrow \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{lemma}[Circularity for known-hard $R$, PROVED/EXACT]
If $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ is already a theorem (resolution/Haken, bounded-depth Frege, cutting planes on known families, \dots) then $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ is logically equivalent to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$. Hence resolution, algebraic proof systems with known bounds, etc.\ cannot supply a non-circular bridge; a bridge is meaningful only for $R$ whose $\mathrm{LB}$ is open (Frege, Extended Frege, or stronger).
\end{lemma}
\begin{lemma}[Single-system lower bound does not give $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$, KNOWN THEOREM (Cook--Reckhow)]
$\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$ needs superpolynomial bounds for \emph{all} proof systems; one $R$ gives nothing alone. So $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ is the load-bearing arrow, not a convenience.
\end{lemma}
\begin{lemma}[What a bridge must contain, CONDITIONAL]
Given a correct polynomial-time decider $A$, a derivation of $F$ in $R$ of size $\mathrm{poly}$ must certify, inside $R$, that $A(F)=\mathrm{reject}$ implies $F$ unsatisfiable. If $R\supseteq$ Extended Frege and the correctness of $A$ is provable in $\mathrm{PV}$ (Cook's translation), such polynomial-size derivations exist (KNOWN THEOREM, standard). Without provable correctness no simulation is known: $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow$ ``EF is polynomially bounded'' is not established in the literature I found. It would follow from the existence of an optimal proof system, itself open (known under $\mathrm{NE}=\mathrm{coNE}$).
\end{lemma}
\begin{lemma}[Universal-search obstruction, FALSIFIED/RETIRED route]
Defining $R$ via the (unknown) decider $A$, or via Levin-style enumeration of pairs (machine, correctness proof), either makes $R$ unsound or reduces $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ to the provability hypothesis above. Non-uniform advice does not help: it is the same certification obligation.
\end{lemma}

\section*{Consequence for the five-agent program}
The weakest sufficient transfer theorem is not a time/size accounting lemma; it is a \emph{reflection-simulation} theorem: ``every polynomial-time SAT decider that exists can be certified in $R$ at polynomial cost.'' Candidate directions (all OPEN): (i) prove optimality of $R$ among polynomially-bounded-if-$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ systems; (ii) show $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ forces correctness of some decider provable in $R$-style arithmetic (cf.\ Pich--Santhanam, arXiv:2312.08163, who obtain $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ from EF lower bounds \emph{plus} bounded-arithmetic hypotheses, and stress that concrete systems differ sharply from existential statements); (iii) relativized separation attempt: oracle with $\mathrm P^O=\mathrm{NP}^O$ and $R^O$ not polynomially bounded would refute any relativizing proof of $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ (not carried out).
Hard-family tests: $\mathrm{LB}$ for the multisigned DAG system on PHP, Tseitin, random 3-CNF should be checked against EF's known polynomial PHP proofs; any $R$ that polynomially simulates EF inherits them, so hard families must come from outside the PHP/counting regime.

\section*{Literature check}
arXiv:2312.08163 (Pich--Santhanam); arXiv:2506.16956 (notes $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ implies automatability of every proof system, a statement about search, not about $\mathrm{Br}(R)$); Krajíček on PV/EF.

\section*{Next target}
Decide whether $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ can hold for any sound $R$ without an optimal-proof-system hypothesis (relativization audit, item iii).
