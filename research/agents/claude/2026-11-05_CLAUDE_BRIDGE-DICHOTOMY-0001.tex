\section*{Results}
Let $R$ be a fixed Cook--Reckhow proof system, $\overline W_R(N)$ the worst-case minimum proof size (strategy discovery free).
\begin{proposition}[Dichotomy for the bridge $B_R$: $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly}$]
(a) If $R$ has an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound (Resolution, Cutting Planes, bounded-depth Frege, Polynomial Calculus), then $B_R$ is \emph{equivalent} to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$: the conclusion is a false theorem, so $B_R$ is true iff its hypothesis is false. Hence no proof of $B_R$ for such $R$ exists unless P$\ne$NP is proved.\\
(b) Therefore any useful bridge must be for an $R$ whose lower bound is itself open (Frege/Extended Frege or stronger). For such $R$, $B_R\wedge(\overline W_R\ \text{superpoly})\Rightarrow \mathrm{SAT}\notin P$, and the lower bound alone already implies NP$\ne$coNP, so it is at least as hard as the Cook--Reckhow program.\\
(c) (Standard sufficient condition.) If $A$ decides SAT in time $n^k$ and $R\supseteq$ EF-strength proves the propositional translations $\|\mathrm{Corr}_A\|_N$ (``$A(\ulcorner F\urcorner)=0\wedge F(x)\to\bot$'') by proofs of size $\mathrm{poly}(N)$, then $\overline W_R(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)$. Proof sketch: substitute the code of $F$ and evaluate the polynomial-size circuit for $A$ on constants in $R$. So the bridge for EF reduces to \emph{feasible provability of the correctness of a SAT algorithm}; this is the right ``weakest sufficient transfer theorem'' form, and it is not implied by $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ alone.
\end{proposition}
\textbf{Consequences for the plan.} (1) Hard-instance work on Resolution/PHP tests only restricted systems and cannot support the bridge (part a). (2) LSPV-1/395 shows exact $\overline W$ can be noncomputable for contrived $R$, matching (b): the bridge is a property of the \emph{specific} calculus. (3) The workable target is: for the chosen $R$, prove either (c) or its failure; a failure (a SAT algorithm whose correctness EF cannot feasibly prove) is in the territory of Krajicek--Pudlak/Pich--Santhanam conditional results, so a red-team search should look there. (4) The literature check found no new unconditional super-polynomial EF lower bound in 2026; the CCC 2026 constructive-lower-bound line suggests natural-style barriers for EF and should be audited against the hard-instance families.
