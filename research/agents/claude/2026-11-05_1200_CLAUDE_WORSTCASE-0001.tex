\noindent\textbf{Status.} P versus NP remains OPEN. This report proves nothing about the
lower bound; it audits the proposed \emph{bridge} and records a structural obstruction.
Not an LSPV run; no schedules, allocations or root \texttt{main.tex} regions changed.

\section{Setup}
Fix a sound, complete refutation system $R$ (Cook--Reckhow: polynomial-time checkable proofs of UNSAT),
with cost $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\ge|\pi|$ (size; time--space overheads only increase it). Put
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)$.
The inner minimum is over \emph{all} derivations, so no search cost is charged: this is exactly
shortest-refutation size (for size cost), i.e.\ ordinary proof complexity. Write
\emph{Bridge}$_R$: $\SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\section{Results}
\begin{proposition}[Bridge triviality]\label{p:triv}\status{PROVED/EXACT}
$\text{Bridge}_R\iff(\mathrm{P}\neq\mathrm{NP})\ \lor\ (\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly})$.
In particular if $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial (e.g.\ resolution, DAG-like or tree-like,
via Haken/Urquhart/Ben-Sasson--Wigderson families; Nullstellensatz/polynomial calculus degree bounds), then
$\text{Bridge}_R\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} Material implication, since $\SAT\in P\iff P=NP$. \end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{c:circ}\status{PROVED/EXACT}
For any $R$ with a known superpolynomial lower bound, a proof of the bridge is a proof of $P\neq NP$.
For any $R$ whose lower bound is open (Frege, Extended Frege), the bridge is equivalent to
$P\ne NP\lor$ ``$R$ is polynomially bounded''. A bridge proved \emph{without} using the structure of
$R$ cannot exist unless it proves $P\neq NP$ or $R$ is bounded.
\end{corollary}
So the ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is not weak: its strength equals that of the target. The
content must come from a \emph{simulation} theorem that turns an algorithm into short $R$-derivations.

\begin{theorem}[Provable-correctness bridge; Cook 1975, Krajíček--Pudlák 1989]\status{KNOWN THEOREM}
If $M$ runs in polynomial time and the theory $PV$ (equivalently the EF-translation) proves
$\forall F,\tau\,(M(F)=0\to\neg\mathrm{Sat}(F,\tau))$, then Extended Frege has polynomial-size refutations
of every UNSAT $F$: evaluate the trace of $M$ on $F$ (poly size) and instantiate the
translated correctness statement.
\end{theorem}
Hence: $\text{EF superpolynomial}\Rightarrow$ no $PV$-provably-correct polynomial SAT algorithm.
This is a \emph{restricted} separation (provably correct algorithms only); the step to arbitrary $M$
fails because correctness of an arbitrary $M$ need not be provable in EF/PV. Status of the full bridge
for EF: OPEN (it is $P\ne NP\lor$ EF bounded).

\begin{proposition}[No diagonal system]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
Taking $R_\infty=$ ``traces of any polynomial $M$'' is unsound: soundness requires correctness of $M$,
which is undecidable to certify in polynomial time (Levin-style optimal systems would give an
optimal proof system, whose existence is open and implies no lower bound by itself).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Free strategy choice and heavier costs]\status{PROVED/EXACT}
$\overline W_R(N)\ge$ shortest-refutation size, so size lower bounds transfer to any cost measure
dominating size, while the bridge for the heavier measure is strictly stronger. Taking min over all
$R$ gives $\min_R\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\iff NP=coNP$ (Cook--Reckhow). Thus
``$\overline W_R$ superpolynomial for \emph{every} sound $R$'' is $NP\neq coNP$, a consequence of $P\ne NP$'s
failure but not equal to it; the fixed-$R$ route needs the bridge of Prop.~\ref{p:triv}.
\end{proposition}

\section{Red-team verdict on the hard families}
Hypergraph/Tseitin/pigeonhole families that survive resolution and algebraic reasoning only yield
lower bounds for those systems; by Prop.~\ref{p:triv} they cannot feed the bridge without already
proving $P\ne NP$. Shared DAGs, circuit compression and adaptive switching correspond to
stronger systems (e.g.\ EF, or Frege with extension); no unconditional superpolynomial lower bound
is known for them. Status: \status{OPEN}.

\section{Recommended next targets}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove or refute: $P=NP\Rightarrow$ EF polynomially bounded (equivalently, Bridge$_{EF}$);
 identify what extra uniformity assumption (e.g.\ PV-provable correctness, or $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ with an
 EF-provable witness) is minimal.
\item Charge-aware variant: restrict to $T$-provable algorithms and ask which $T$ gives
 lower bounds unconditionally (bounded-arithmetic independence results, Pich--Santhanam style).
\item Audit the multi-sign DAG hypergraph system: place it in the Cook--Reckhow simulation order
 (does it $p$-simulate resolution? is it $p$-simulated by EF?). Any placement below EF yields
 no bridge (Cor.~\ref{c:circ}); above EF is open.
\end{enumerate}
