\section{Target as assigned}
For a fixed sound and complete refutation system $R$ (the multisign DAG-hypergraph
reduction calculus, or any fixed alternative) and a cost function $\operatorname{cost}_R$
polynomially related to proof bit length, define
\[
\Wbar_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\in\UNSAT\\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N}}\
\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi).
\]
The minimal reduced logical state of an unsatisfiable formula is $\bot$ (the empty signed
hyperedge); $\Wbar_R$ is the worst-case cost of reaching it with the best sequence granted
for free. The program asks for
\begin{itemize}
\item[(LB)] $\Wbar_R(N)$ is superpolynomial, and
\item[(BR)] $\SAT\in\mathrm P\implies \Wbar_R(N)\le\poly(N)$.
\end{itemize}
(LB)$\wedge$(BR) $\Rightarrow\SAT\notin\mathrm P$ by contraposition. The free choice of
$\pi$ is a legitimate lower-bound relaxation: any executed sequence costs at least the
minimum, and discovery costs only add. Note that $\min_\pi$ is a nondeterministic
(coNP-side) quantity; for a single optimal $R$, (LB) would give $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$,
which is stronger than $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.

\section{Result 1: weak systems make the bridge equal to the whole problem}

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT]\label{thm:weak}
Let $R$ be any fixed system for which (LB) is an unconditional theorem. Then (BR) for $R$
is logically equivalent to $\SAT\notin\mathrm P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
($\Leftarrow$) If $\SAT\notin\mathrm P$, (BR) holds vacuously.
($\Rightarrow$) (BR) and (LB) give $\SAT\notin\mathrm P$ by contraposition.
\end{proof}

\noindent (LB) is a KNOWN THEOREM for: tree-like and general resolution (Haken 1985; for
Tseitin, Urquhart 1987, Ben-Sasson--Wigderson 2001); cutting planes (Pudl\'ak 1997);
Polynomial Calculus (Razborov 1998; Ben-Sasson--Impagliazzo 1999); bounded-depth Frege
(Ajtai 1988, Beame--Impagliazzo--Pitassi, Krají\v{c}ek--Pudl\'ak--Woods, H\aa stad 2025
improvements); $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege for specific DNF families (Lu--Santhanam--Tzameret,
ECCC 2025/134). Hence:

\begin{corollary}
If the multisign DAG-hypergraph calculus is p-simulated by any of the systems above (in
particular, if its only rules are signed hyperedge merging/resolution, unit propagation,
subsumption and deletion, without extension variables), then priority (LB) is already
achieved by the literature and the remaining (BR) \emph{is} the P versus NP problem with no
reduction in difficulty. Such a calculus cannot serve as a ``weakest sufficient bridge''.
\end{corollary}

\noindent This complements the corpus result LSPV-1/334 (FREE-CHOICE-TRANSFER-BARRIER334),
which falsifies the stronger pointwise simulation TR(Resolution) unconditionally via
Tseitin formulas plus a Gaussian-elimination decider. Theorem~\ref{thm:weak} concerns the
weaker conditional bridge (BR) and shows it cannot be falsified or proved for such $R$
without settling P versus NP.

\section{Result 2: a fixed system whose bridge reduces to a provability residual}

Fix a consistent, recursively axiomatized theory $T\supseteq$ Robinson's $Q$ with
polynomial-time proof checking (e.g.\ PA or ZFC). For a deterministic machine $A$ and
$k\ge1$ let
\[
\mathrm{Snd}_{A,k}:\quad \forall F\,\forall x\ \bigl[A\text{ halts on }F\text{ within }
|F|^k+k\text{ steps with output }\UNSAT\ \rightarrow\ x\not\models F\bigr],
\]
a $\Pi_1$ sentence (all inner quantifiers bounded).

\begin{definition}
$R_T$ accepts, as a refutation of $F$, either (a) an Extended Frege refutation of $F$, or (b)
a tuple $w=(A,k,p,1^{|F|^k+k})$ where $p$ is a $T$-proof of $\mathrm{Snd}_{A,k}$ and $A$,
simulated on $F$ for $|F|^k+k$ steps, outputs $\UNSAT$. Cost $=|w|$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT]\label{thm:RT}
(i) $R_T$ is a Cook--Reckhow proof system: polynomial-time checkable, sound and complete.
(ii) If some deterministic $A$ decides SAT in time $|F|^k+k$ and $T\vdash\mathrm{Snd}_{A,k}$,
then $\Wbar_{R_T}(N)\le C_{A,T}+O(N^k)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
(i) Checking (a) is the EF checker. For (b), verify $p$ against the fixed formula
$\mathrm{Snd}_{A,k}$ (polynomial in $|p|+|A|$) and simulate $A$ for $|F|^k+k$ steps
(polynomial in the padding length). Soundness of (a) is EF soundness. For (b): a false
$\Pi_1$ sentence has a true $\Sigma_1$ negation, which $Q$ proves; so consistent
$T\supseteq Q$ proves no false $\Pi_1$ sentence. Thus $\mathrm{Snd}_{A,k}$ is true and the
accepted $F$ is unsatisfiable. Completeness is inherited from EF.
(ii) For every unsatisfiable $F$ the tuple $(A,k,p,1^{|F|^k+k})$ is accepted, and its length
is $|A|+|p|+O(\log k)+|F|^k+k$, where $|A|,|p|,k$ are constants independent of $F$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[exact decomposition of the bridge for $R_T$]\label{cor:decomp}
Let $\mathrm{PROV}_T$ be: ``if $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ then some polynomial-time SAT decider is
$T$-provably UNSAT-sound for some explicit clock''. Then
\[
\mathrm{PROV}_T\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{(BR)}\text{ for }R_T,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
\mathrm{(LB)}\text{ for }R_T\ \wedge\ \mathrm{PROV}_T\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}.
\]
Unconditionally, (LB) for $R_T$ implies that no polynomial-time SAT algorithm is
$T$-provably UNSAT-sound.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[relation to the corpus; what is new]
(1) Codex 2026-11-11 TRANSFER\_RECOVERY uses an $A$-\emph{dependent} system $Q_A$; $R_T$ is
fixed before any algorithm is chosen and covers every $T$-provably sound decider at once.
(2) LSPV-1 2026-11-13 PA\_CORRECTNESS\_CERTIFICATION\_TRAP shows by G\"odel~II that some
correct decider $A_{T,D}$ is not $T$-provably correct, so a certificate-only transfer
cannot cover \emph{all} deciders. That does not refute $\mathrm{PROV}_T$: (BR) needs
\emph{one} provably sound polynomial decider, not all of them. The residual is therefore
exactly $\mathrm{PROV}_T$, and only UNSAT-side soundness is required. The runtime bound
need not be $T$-provable because the clock is paid in the proof.
(3) $R_T$ is a version of the standard ``proof system of a theory'' construction
(Krají\v{c}ek--Pudl\'ak 1989). The corollary is written out here for the $\Wbar_R$ target.
\end{remark}

\section{The dichotomy}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{3.2cm}|p{5.3cm}|p{5.3cm}}

Weak $R$ (Res, CP, PC, AC$^0$-Frege, rule-only hypergraph merging) & KNOWN THEOREM & $\equiv$ P$\ne$NP (Thm.~\ref{thm:weak}); no progress possible without solving the problem\\\hline
$R_T$ (EF + $T$-reflection) & OPEN; at least as hard as EF lower bounds. Best known unrestricted Frege bounds are only quadratic (as noted in Jeřábek 2025). & Reduces to $\mathrm{PROV}_T$ (Cor.~\ref{cor:decomp}); OPEN\\\hline
Optimal $R^\ast$ (if it exists) & $\equiv$ NP$\ne$coNP & PROVED if $R^\ast$ exists: NP$=$coNP $\Rightarrow$ some p-bounded system $\Rightarrow R^\ast$ p-bounded (Cook--Reckhow 1979); existence OPEN (follows from NE$=$coNE, Krajíček--Pudlák 1989)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent\emph{Consequence for the program.} For a multisign DAG hypergraph calculus to be a
non-vacuous bridge target it must include \emph{extension} (fresh nodes defined as signed
gates of old nodes, i.e.\ shared DAG definitions), so that it reaches at least EF strength.
At that point (LB) is beyond every currently known proof-complexity technique. The program
moves the difficulty between the two arrows but does not remove it. The useful work is to
find the weakest $R$ for which (BR) is provable without solving P versus NP.
Theorem~\ref{thm:weak} shows such an $R$ must lie strictly above every system with a known
lower bound.

\section*{Barrier-transfer and post-quantum ledger}
Proof complexity and logic: feasible interpolation and reflection principles are a
\emph{directly transferable theorem} domain (Cook--Reckhow; Krajíček--Pudlák). Optimization
duality (LP/cutting planes): a \emph{failed transfer} for (BR), because CP has known lower
bounds and Theorem~\ref{thm:weak} applies. PQ item 5 (worst-to-average reductions):
ANALOGY/CANDIDATE only, since $\mathrm{PROV}_T$ is a uniform provability statement, not an
average-case one. Relativization: Theorems~\ref{thm:weak} and~\ref{thm:RT} relativize, so
they are structural bookkeeping, not separation techniques.
