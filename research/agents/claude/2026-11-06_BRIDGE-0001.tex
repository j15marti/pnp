\noindent\textbf{Status.} Propositions 1--3 are KNOWN THEOREM combinations (short proofs below), not new deep results. They triage the directive's ``weakest sufficient bridge''. \textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN}; no lower bound or bridge for the intended fixed $R$ is claimed.

\section*{Setup}
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$, with $\mathrm{cost}_R\ge$ proof size (any cost model that charges at least one unit per line). Granting the optimal $\pi$ for free is exactly the nondeterministic guess, so ``$\overline W_R$ polynomial'' is Cook--Reckhow polynomial-boundedness of $R$ (for size-like costs). Write $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ for $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathsf P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\begin{prop}[weak $R$: the bridge \emph{is} the goal]
If $\overline W_R$ is superpolynomial unconditionally (Resolution: Haken; Cutting Planes; bounded-depth Frege; $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$ for bit-PHP, Braun), then $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\iff\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$.
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} The conclusion is false, so the implication is equivalent to the negation of its hypothesis. \qed

\begin{prop}[the bridge holds for \emph{some} $R$ under the hypothesis]
If $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ then $\mathsf E=\mathsf{NE}$ (padding: for $L\in\mathsf{NE}$, $\{x01^{2^{|x|}}\}\in\mathsf{NP}=\mathsf P$), hence a $p$-optimal proof system $R^*$ exists (Kraj\'i\v cek--Pudl\'ak 1989). As $\mathsf{NP}=\mathsf{coNP}$ some system is polynomially bounded, so $R^*$ is, and $\mathrm{Bridge}(R^*)$ holds. \qed

\noindent The system $R^*$ is obtained from the unknown machine, so it is existential and cannot be a fixed, independently specified calculus. Whether Bridge holds for a \emph{natural} strong system (Extended Frege) is not settled by this argument; a literature search found no proof, so it stays OPEN here.

\begin{prop}[optimal $R$: the lower bound is already $\mathsf{NP}\ne\mathsf{coNP}$]
If $R$ is $p$-optimal, $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial $\iff\mathsf{NP}\ne\mathsf{coNP}$ (Cook--Reckhow), which implies $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$; Bridge is then automatic but buys nothing.
\end{prop}

\section*{Consequence (trichotomy)}
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lll}
class of $R$ & lower bound & Bridge\\\hline
weak (known lower bounds) & known & $\equiv\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$ (Prop.~1)\\
$p$-optimal & $\equiv \mathsf{NP}\ne\mathsf{coNP}$ (Prop.~3) & automatic\\
intermediate (EF, $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$) & OPEN & OPEN; extra hypotheses needed
\end{tabular}\end{center}
So the programme is only non-vacuous for intermediate $R$ where \emph{both} the lower bound and the bridge are open; the two must be proved together, and proving Bridge for such $R$ needs a feasible-correctness step: $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ must yield \emph{short $R$-proofs that a specific polynomial decider is correct}. This is the reflection/B3 obligation already in the corpus. Pich--Santhanam (arXiv:2312.08163, \emph{Towards $\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$ from Extended Frege lower bounds}) show the same shape: an EF lower bound gives $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$ only under additional bounded-arithmetic hypotheses (feasible anticheckers, witnessing $\mathsf{NP}\not\subseteq\mathsf P/\mathrm{poly}$, or hardness-plus-learning conditions); I read only the abstract, so exact hypotheses must be checked in the full paper.
