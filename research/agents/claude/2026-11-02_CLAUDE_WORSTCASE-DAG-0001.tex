\title{Worst-case minimal derivation cost $\overline W_R(N)$: audit of the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$}

\section*{1. Definitions}
$R$ a Cook--Reckhow system (poly-time verifier). $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{|F|\le N,\,F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$. Free choice of the best $\pi$ is exactly nondeterminism; the min is the proof-complexity measure, so ``not charging for search'' loses nothing and gains nothing over Cook--Reckhow.

\section*{2. Results (PROVED/EXACT, elementary)}
\textbf{Prop A (reformulation).} $\overline W_R$ polynomial for \emph{some} Cook--Reckhow $R$ $\iff$ $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ (Cook--Reckhow 1979). Hence ``superpolynomial for all $R$'' is $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$, which implies $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$; it is a reformulation, not progress (ledger rule: equivalence $\Rightarrow$ reformulation).

\textbf{Prop B (bridge is $A$-dependent).} If $A$ decides SAT in poly time, $R_A:=\{(F,\epsilon): A(F)=\text{UNSAT}\}$ is a Cook--Reckhow system with $\overline W_{R_A}\le\mathrm{poly}$. So $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\exists R\ \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ is trivial, but $R_A$ is not fixed in advance and says nothing about a given natural $R$.

\textbf{Prop C (fixed $R$ needs uniform soundness).} For fixed $R$ (e.g.\ EF), deriving $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ from $A\in\mathrm P$ is the statement that $R$ has poly-size proofs of $\mathrm{Sound}(A)$ instances. Known sufficient condition (Cook, Krajicek--Pudlak): $PV\vdash\mathrm{Sound}(A)\Rightarrow$ EF poly-bounded. Without provability of correctness no implication is known; the bridge for EF is then equivalent in difficulty to open EF questions (and EF lower bounds are themselves open). For weak $R$ (resolution, PC, CP) the bridge is \emph{false relative to known lower bounds unless P$\ne$NP-type consequences}: exponential resolution bounds (Haken; Urquhart, Ben-Sasson--Wigderson, Chvatal--Szemeredi) hold unconditionally, so ``SAT$\in$P $\Rightarrow\overline W_{\mathrm{Res}}\le$poly'' would give P$\ne$NP; the bridge cannot be true for $R$ with known lower bounds without proving P$\ne$NP.

\textbf{Prop D (weakest sufficient bridge, sharpened).} The bridge is only meaningful for $R$ strong enough to certify poly-time computation with uniform soundness (EF/PV-level), and then lower bounds for $\overline W_R$ are as hard as EF lower bounds. Contrapositive target: EF superpolynomial $\Rightarrow$ P$\ne$NP is \emph{not} known (EF lower bound gives only that EF is not p-bounded, not NP$\ne$coNP).

\section*{3. Consequences for the five roles}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lead: replace the bridge by ``$\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\wedge PV\vdash\mathrm{Sound}(A)\Rightarrow$ EF poly'' (KNOWN) and note P$\ne$NP then needs unprovability or an EF bound.
\item Builder: ``fixed combined inference system'' must be a Cook--Reckhow system; resolution+algebraic+circuit compression is at most Frege/EF, so hard-instance families for it are Frege-hard (open).
\item Certificates: families (Tseitin on expanders, random 3-CNF, PHP) are exponential for Res/PC/CP but have poly Frege/EF proofs for Tseitin and PHP; they cannot survive strong systems.
\item Auditor: cost$\ge$ size; a time-space cost must be a Cook--Reckhow-verifiable quantity.
\item Red team: any claimed $\overline W_R$ bound for EF-strength $R$ implies breakthrough; check it for hidden $A$-dependence.
\end{itemize}
