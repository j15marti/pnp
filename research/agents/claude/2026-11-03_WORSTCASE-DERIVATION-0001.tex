\section*{Worst-case derivation cost $\overline W_R(N)$: status of the bridge}
Status: P versus NP remains OPEN. Nothing below proves a lower bound.

Setup. $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in UNSAT,|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$.
Because the inner min is over a freely chosen $\pi$, the quantity is a nondeterministic (proof-size) measure.

\begin{prop}[Bridge is equivalent to P$\neq$NP for any $R$ with a known superpolynomial bound; PROVED/EXACT, elementary]
Let $R$ satisfy $\overline W_R(N)\ge N^{\omega(1)}$ (e.g.\ resolution: Haken 1985; also cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege). Then
$(\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly})$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$.
\end{prop}
\begin{proof} The conclusion is false, so the implication is equivalent to the negation of the hypothesis. \end{proof}
Consequence: the ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is not weaker than the goal for any system with known lower bounds. For such $R$ proving it is proving P$\ne$NP.

\begin{prop}[Trivial-cost systems; PROVED/EXACT]
If $\mathrm{cost}_R$ charges the full computation of a deterministic algorithm (derivation = accepting trace), the bridge holds trivially and $\overline W_R$ is the deterministic time of UNSAT, so a superpolynomial bound is exactly $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$: a reformulation, not progress.
\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[Cook--Reckhow regime; KNOWN THEOREM + observation]
If $R$ has poly-time checkable derivations, $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial only shows $R$ is not $p$-bounded. $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$ requires this for every $R$. SAT$\in$P gives automatability of every $R$ (finding a shortest proof in time poly in its size) but not $p$-boundedness: a bridge for fixed $R$ needs $R$ to certify the soundness of the poly-time algorithm, a bounded-arithmetic provability statement (CANDIDATE obstruction, not proved here).
\end{prop}

\textbf{Implication for the five roles.} A useful $R$ must lie strictly between: strong enough that SAT$\in$P plausibly yields short $R$-derivations (needs a provable simulation of the algorithm inside $R$, uniformly), yet with a lower bound technique not already equivalent to the goal. This is the same shape as the open problem of Frege/EF lower bounds. Open arrow: any uniform simulation theorem of the form ``$A$ decides SAT in time $T$ $\Rightarrow$ $R$ derives $\bot$ for UNSAT $F$ in $\mathrm{poly}(T)$'' with $R$ having a plausible lower-bound route.

Literature check (2026-11-03): Atserias--M\"uller (resolution automatability is NP-hard; P=NP makes every system automatable), Papamakarios (depth-$d$ Frege NP-hard to automate), Cook--Reckhow, and 2025 work (arXiv 2506.16956) noting self-lower-bound provability is open outside resolution. Classification: ANALOGY/CANDIDATE; no transfer theorem.

Adopted: Cook--Reckhow framing. Retired: bridge for any $R$ with known unconditional lower bounds. Not claimed: any lower bound.
