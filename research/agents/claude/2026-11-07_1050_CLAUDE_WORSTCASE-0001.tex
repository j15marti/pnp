\title{Worst-case derivation cost: what the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ can and cannot give}
\author{CLAUDE WORSTCASE-0001} \date{2026-11-07}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status.} P versus NP remains OPEN. Nothing below proves a lower bound; it classifies the proposed bridge.

\section*{Setup}
$R$ is a Cook--Reckhow system (poly-time verifiable derivations, sound, complete for UNSAT), $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)\ge|\pi|$.
$\W_R(N)=\max_{|F|\le N}\min_\pi \mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$. Write $LB(R)$: $\W_R$ superpolynomial; $Br(R)$: $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \W_R\le \mathrm{poly}$.

\section*{Results (PROVED/EXACT unless marked)}
\begin{thm}[T1] $LB(R)\wedge Br(R)\Rightarrow P\ne NP$. $LB(R)$ alone only says $R$ is not polybounded.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} If $P=NP$ then $Br(R)$ gives $\W_R$ polynomial, contradicting $LB(R)$.\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[T2: circularity] If $\W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial (resolution, cutting planes, Nullstellensatz/PC, bounded-depth Frege), then $Br(R)\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin P$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} The consequent is false, so the implication is equivalent to the falsity of its antecedent.\end{proof}
So a bridge into any system with a known lower bound is a restatement of the goal (``equivalent to $P\ne NP$'' in the ledger taxonomy).

\begin{thm}[T3: tradeoff] If $R\le_p R'$ ($R'$ p-simulates $R$) then $LB(R')\Rightarrow LB(R)$ and $Br(R)\Rightarrow Br(R')$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} Proofs in $R'$ are at most polynomially longer than the shortest in $R$.\end{proof}
Weak $R$: lower bound easy, bridge circular (T2). Strong $R$ (Frege, EF): bridge plausible, lower bound open; best known size bounds for (E)Frege are only linear/quadratic (KNOWN, literature check below).

\begin{thm}[T4: optimal systems] If some $R$ is optimal (every system polynomially simulated by $R$, p-optimality not needed), then $Br(R)$ holds, but $LB(R)\iff NP\ne coNP$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} $P=NP\Rightarrow NP=coNP\Rightarrow$ a polybounded system $R_0$ exists (Cook--Reckhow) $\Rightarrow$ $R$ simulates $R_0$. Conversely $\W_R$ poly iff $R$ polybounded iff $NP=coNP$.\end{proof}
Thus the weakest bridge reachable through optimality targets $NP\ne coNP$, a statement at least as strong as $P\ne NP$. Existence of optimal systems is OPEN (Krajíček--Pudlák: follows from $NE=coNE$).

\begin{thm}[T5: theory systems, CONDITIONAL] Let $R_T(F,p)$ accept iff $p$ is a $T$-proof of ``$F$ is unsatisfiable'', $T\supseteq$ a standard arithmetic. If $T$ proves correctness and time bound $n^k$ of some algorithm $A$ for SAT, then $\W_{R_T}\le O(N^{k}\mathrm{polylog})$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof} Constant-size proof of correctness of $A$, plus a $T$-proof (size $\tilde O(N^k)$) of the true bounded statement $A(F)=\text{reject}$.\end{proof}
So $Br(R_T)$ holds for any $T$ that can certify the algorithm; it can fail only if a polynomial algorithm exists whose correctness $T$ cannot prove (independence). This is the exact gap: \emph{free strategy discovery} removes the cost of finding $A$ but not the cost of \emph{verifying} that the derivation is sound.

\begin{thm}[T6: free strategy] Because the minimiser $\pi$ is free, $\W_R$ is a nondeterministic measure: $\W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ means $\mathrm{UNSAT}\in NP$ (via $R$). Free strategy search therefore cannot make the lower bound easier than $NP\ne coNP$-type statements for sufficiently strong $R$.\end{thm}

\section*{Consequences for the five roles}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The multisign DAG hypergraph system must be formalised as a Cook--Reckhow $R_H$ and located in the simulation order. If $R_H\le_p$ resolution/PC/CP, then its bridge is circular (T2). It is only useful if it is incomparable-with or above bounded-depth Frege.
\item The only non-circular route: a system $R$ with $LB(R)$ open and $Br(R)$ derivable from a hypothesis weaker than ``P=NP is provable in $T$''. Candidates to examine: AC$^0[p]$-Frege (lower bound a known open frontier), and fixed-depth Frege with parity gates.
\item Red team: find an $R_H$ where $Br$ follows from $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ alone (would need a uniform feasible reflection principle inside $R_H$), or a counterexample to T4's use of optimality.
\item Resource auditor: costs of $R_H$ must be charged per Cook--Reckhow verification time; peak memory of a DAG derivation is a separate (space) measure, not controlled by size.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Literature check (2026-11-07)}
No super-polynomial Extended Frege or Frege lower bound found; best known EF size bound is linear and Frege quadratic (surveys by Krajíček, Segerlind). Optimal proof systems: existence open. Håstad 2025 (TheoretiCS) treats small-depth Frege for PHP, a restricted-system result consistent with T3. Relevant sources: arXiv 1607.00443 (algebraic proof complexity survey); Segerlind, ``The complexity of propositional proofs''. I did not verify a claimed ITCS 2026 AC$^0[p]$-Frege paper (arXiv 2509.16824).
