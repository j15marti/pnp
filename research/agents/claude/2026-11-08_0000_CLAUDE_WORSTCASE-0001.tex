\section*{Setup}
Let $R$ be a refutation system with derivations checkable in time poly in $|\pi|$ (Cook--Reckhow), sound and complete for UNSAT, $\overline W_R(N)=\max_F\min_\pi \mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$.

\begin{prop}[Free optimal sequence $=$ nondeterminism; KNOWN/PROVED]
$\overline W_R(N)\le \mathrm{poly}(N)$ iff $R$ is polynomially bounded, which implies $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$.
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Cost is polynomially related to $|\pi|$, so a cheap minimizer is a short certificate checkable in poly time; UNSAT is then in NP. Conversely poly-bounded gives the bound. $\square$

\textbf{Consequence.} A superpolynomial $\overline W_R$ for any such $R$ proves $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$ ($\Rightarrow \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$), i.e.\ it is a proof-complexity lower bound for $R$. This is strictly stronger than $\mathrm P\ne \mathrm{NP}$ unless a bridge makes the converse available. Not charging for strategy search is harmless but also buys nothing: it is already what the min does.

\begin{prop}[Optimal $R$: bridge true, no leverage; PROVED]
If $R$ p-simulates every Cook--Reckhow system, then $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$; moreover $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial $\iff \mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$.
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$ gives $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ (decider $+$ empty proof is a poly-bounded system $R'$); $R$ simulates $R'$. The equivalence is Prop.\,1 plus simulation. $\square$

\begin{prop}[Fixed natural $R$: exact bridge; CONDITIONAL]
For $R=$ Frege/EF the bridge is precisely ``$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R$ poly-bounded''. It holds if for some poly-time SAT algorithm $A$ the system $R$ has polynomial-size proofs of the reflection statement ``$A(F)$ rejects $\Rightarrow F$ unsatisfiable'' (T-REFL; for EF, e.g.\ if PV proves correctness of $A$).
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Given UNSAT $F$, $A(F)$ rejection has a poly-size EF computation trace; combine with reflection. $\square$
T-REFL is the weakest sufficient transfer I can state; it is OPEN, and is a statement about provability of an algorithm's correctness, not about its running time. A cost model with a ``run machine $M$'' rule is unsound unless $M$'s correctness is certified (FAILED shortcut: soundness gap, not search cost, is the obstruction).

\section*{Hard-instance red team (hypergraph/proof certificates)}
Pigeonhole, Tseitin (over $\mathbb F_2$), ordering principle and clique-coloring have polynomial Frege/EF or PC proofs, so they fail the ``survives alternative reasoning'' test. Random $k$-CNF has exponential resolution/PC/bounded-depth bounds but no superpolynomial Frege/EF bound is known; best EF size bound is essentially linear. Shared DAGs: DAG-like resolution is already the model for PHP/Tseitin separations only for resolution. Signed-hypergraph instances are therefore \textbf{CANDIDATE} only; each must be checked against EF simulation of the relevant counting argument.

\section*{Time--space audit}
Peak memory of a DAG derivation is the width of a pebbling of the DAG; time--space tradeoffs for resolution are known but are not transferable to EF. Min shared-DAG derivation is a min-cost Steiner hyperpath problem; we do not charge its search.
