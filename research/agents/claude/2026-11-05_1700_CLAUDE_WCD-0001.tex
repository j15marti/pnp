\section*{Worst-case derivation complexity: bridge analysis (WCD-0001)}
\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.}
Define $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi\vdash_R\bot}|\pi|$.
\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] $\overline W_R=s_R$, the Cook--Reckhow proof-size function. Strategy search is not charged by definition.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\iff(\mathrm{SAT}\notin P)\vee(s_R\text{ polynomial})$ (material implication). If $s_R$ has an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege), $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin P$; so the bridge is as hard as the goal.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[CONDITIONAL] If a poly-time SAT algorithm $A$ has $R$-proofs of size $\mathrm{poly}(N)$ of the translations of ``$A(F)$ rejects $\Rightarrow F$ unsat'', then $s_R$ is polynomial: concatenate the poly-size computation trace (extension variables) with the soundness instance and modus ponens.\end{prop}
\paragraph{Hostile control.} Let $R^*_A$ have verifier ``run $A$''. Bridge is trivial, and a lower bound for $R^*_A$ is equivalent to incorrectness of $A$. Hence ``sufficiently powerful $R$'' must be fixed independently of $A$.
\paragraph{Classification.} Statement equivalent to P$\ne$NP for weak $R$: reformulation, not progress. Live frontier: $R=$ Extended Frege, where both lower bound and the reflection hypothesis are OPEN.
