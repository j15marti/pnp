\title{Worst-case derivation cost $\overline W_R(N)$: classification of the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$}
\date{2026-11-05}\begin{document}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} Nothing below proves a lower bound.

\section*{Setup}
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$; search for $\pi$ is free (agreed: this only strengthens a lower bound on executing the best $\pi$). $R$ is a \emph{fixed} system, defined independently of any SAT algorithm. Instances are signed hypergraphs (clauses = signed hyperedges); $\pi$ is a derivation DAG.

\section*{Obligations}
(A) Lower bound: $\overline W_R(N)\ne N^{O(1)}$. \quad (B) Bridge: $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\paragraph{Proposition 1 (A and B jointly give P$\ne$NP; PROVED/EXACT, trivial).} If $R$ satisfies (A) and (B) then $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. Also (A) alone, with cost $\ge$ proof size and $R$ a Cook--Reckhow system, gives NP$\ne$coNP$\Rightarrow$P$\ne$NP is \emph{not} implied; it gives NP$\ne$coNP only if $R$ is polynomially bounded-complete, i.e.\ never for a single weak $R$.

\paragraph{Proposition 2 (Dichotomy; PROVED/EXACT given cited lower bounds).} Let $R$ have an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound (Resolution: Haken 1985, Urquhart 1987, Ben-Sasson--Wigderson; Cutting Planes: Pudl\'ak; bounded-depth Frege: Ajtai, Kraj\'i\v cek--Pudl\'ak--Woods, Pitassi--Beame--Impagliazzo). Then (B) for $R$ is \emph{equivalent} to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. 
\emph{Proof.} ($\Leftarrow$) vacuous: $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ is false so the implication holds. ($\Rightarrow$) (A) holds, so (B) forces $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. $\square$
Consequence: for every system whose lower bounds are known, the bridge is a restatement of P$\ne$NP; it cannot be proved without proving it. Conversely a bridge for a system with unknown lower bounds can be genuine progress but then (A) implies NP$\ne$coNP-type strength.

\paragraph{Proposition 3 (Tautological systems; PROVED/EXACT).} Define $R_{\rm univ}$: a derivation of $F$ is a pair $(i,t)$ with $M_i$ halting in $t$ steps rejecting $F$ and a certificate that $M_i$ is a correct SAT decider on all inputs of length $|F|$ is \emph{not} required (soundness unverifiable); with cost $=\min t$ over \emph{sound} $i$, (B) holds and (A) is literally $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. So any bridge-valid system obtained by hard-wiring computation is a reformulation, not progress (cf.\ world ledger rule: equivalent-to-P$\ne$NP statements are reformulations).

\paragraph{Proposition 4 (Provable-correctness bridge; KNOWN THEOREM, standard Cook/Buss translation, verify exact statement).} If $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ via $M$ and PV (equivalently $S^1_2$-style) proves $M$ correct, then Extended Frege has polynomial-size refutations of all UNSAT formulas, so $\overline W_{EF}\le\mathrm{poly}$. The unconditional bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_{EF}\le\mathrm{poly}$ needs the provability hypothesis removed; that is precisely the open question of EF being polynomially bounded under P=NP. Literature search (this run) found no source proving the unconditional version; related conditional work: arXiv 2312.08163 (EF lower-bound unprovability $\Rightarrow$ P$\neq$NP type statements).

\section*{Weakest sufficient transfer theorem (CANDIDATE)}
\textbf{T-EF:} P=NP $\Rightarrow$ EF is polynomially bounded. Equivalent to nothing known to be false; it is implied by ``P=NP is provable in $S^1_2$''. Via Prop.~2 it is the only natural strength level where (B) is non-vacuous and not already P$\ne$NP; (A) at that level is NP$\ne$coNP, strictly harder than the goal. Hence: \emph{the proposed program, with $R$ fixed and natural, reduces to either a known-lower-bound system (bridge $\equiv$ P$\ne$NP) or EF-strength (lower bound $\Rightarrow$ NP$\ne$coNP, open since 1979).}
