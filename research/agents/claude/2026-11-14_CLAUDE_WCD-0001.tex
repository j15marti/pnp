\section{Results}
Write $R\le_p R'$ if $R'$ p-simulates $R$ on CNF refutations with the source
formula preserved: there is a polynomial $q$ with
$W_{R'}(F)\le q(|\langle F\rangle|+W_R(F))$ for all UNSAT $F$.

\begin{lemma}[monotone sandwich]\label{l:mono}
If $R\le_p R'$ then (a) $\overline W_{R'}(N)\le q(N+\overline W_R(N))$;
(b) a proved superpolynomial lower bound for $\overline W_{R'}$ yields one for
$\overline W_R$; (c) $\mathrm{B3}_R\Rightarrow\mathrm{B3}_{R'}$.
Hence the class $\mathsf{LB}$ of systems with proved superpolynomial
$\overline W$ is downward closed and the class $\mathsf{BR}$ of systems with
proved B3 is upward closed under $\le_p$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
(a) take maxima of the pointwise inequality over $|\langle F\rangle|\le N$, using
monotonicity of $q$. (b) contrapositive of (a). (c) under SAT$\in$P,
$\overline W_R\le p$ gives $\overline W_{R'}\le q(N+p(N))$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[the route closes only on the intersection]\label{l:inter}
The proof scheme ``superpolynomial $\overline W_R$ plus $\mathrm{B3}_R$'' yields
$\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$ iff one fixed $R$ lies in
$\mathsf{LB}\cap\mathsf{BR}$. Moreover, for every $R$, $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P
\Rightarrow \mathrm{B3}_R$ (vacuous antecedent), and for every
$R\in\mathsf{LB}$, $\mathrm{B3}_R\Leftrightarrow\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first claim is modus ponens/tollens. The second is propositional. The third:
if $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial the consequent of
$\mathrm{B3}_R$ is false, so $\mathrm{B3}_R\equiv\neg(\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P)$.
(The third clause duplicates LSPV-1/339, LSPV-1/398 and LSPV-3/420; restated, not new.)
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[red-team classification constraint]
No finite or explicit computation can \emph{refute} $\mathrm{B3}_R$ for any $R$
unless it proves $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$. Red-team outcomes against B3 are
therefore necessarily GAP findings (unsupported step), or relativized/theory-relative
countermodels; they must never be labelled FALSIFIED. This sharpens the
directive's ``CONDITIONAL-BRIDGE GAP/FAILURE'' class: only GAP is reachable.
\end{corollary}

\begin{lemma}[current occupancy, KNOWN THEOREMS]\label{l:occ}
$\mathsf{LB}$ contains Resolution (Haken), bounded-depth Frege, Cutting Planes,
Polynomial Calculus, tree-like/regular/bounded-depth Res$(\oplus)$
(Efremenko--Garl\'ik--Itsykson STOC~2024; Bhattacharya--Chattopadhyay
arXiv:2507.23008; Efremenko--Itsykson ECCC TR25-188). General dag-like Res$(\oplus)$
is \emph{not} in $\mathsf{LB}$ as of this search. $\mathsf{BR}$ contains no
system for which the membership proof avoids proving $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$:
the only known positive mechanism is Cook's PV$\to$EF translation, which gives
$[\exists A\ \mathrm{PV}\vdash\mathrm{Corr}_A,\ A\in\mathrm P]\Rightarrow
\overline W_{\mathrm{EF}}\le\mathrm{poly}$, a strictly stronger antecedent than
SAT$\in$P (cf. LSPV-1/357).
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Consequence for the five assignments.}
By Lemma~\ref{l:mono}, any candidate $R$ must be at least as strong as every
system for which a B3 proof is plausible (the corpus shows CANON2R$\equiv_p$EF,
REFEREE365) and at most as strong as a system with a provable lower bound.
Since $\mathsf{LB}$ currently stops strictly below dag-like Res$(\oplus)$ while
the only B3 mechanism needs at least EF, the gap is the entire interval
Res$(\oplus)\le_p R\le_p$EF \emph{plus} the reflection gap
SAT$\in$P vs.\ PV$\vdash$Corr$_A$. The highest-value single target this
implies: a B3-type bridge for any system strictly weaker than EF, or
equivalently a feasible-reflection statement derivable from bare SAT$\in$P.
Neither is known; both are OPEN.
