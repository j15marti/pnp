\section{Setting}
$R$ is a fixed Cook--Reckhow system (polynomial-time proof checker). $W_R(F)$ is the minimum cost of an $R$-refutation of UNSAT $F$, with free choice of the optimal derivation; $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{|F|\le N}W_R(F)$. Bridge: $B(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathsf P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. Lower bound: $L(R):\ \overline W_R$ superpolynomial.

\begin{theorem}[Joint theorem is exactly $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$]
$B(R)\wedge L(R)\Rightarrow \mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$. Moreover, if $L(R)$ is already a theorem (e.g. $R$ = Resolution, bounded-depth Frege, Cutting Planes), then $B(R)\Leftrightarrow \mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} $L(R)$ negates the conclusion of $B(R)$, so $B(R)$ gives $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathsf P$. If $L(R)$ holds outright, $B(R)$ is $(\mathrm{SAT}\in \mathsf P)\to\bot$. \end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Consequence.} For every system whose lower bounds are known, the bridge cannot be obtained by a simulation or transfer argument; proving it proves $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$. This explains the earlier falsification for ordinary Resolution (LSPV-1 334). The bridge is only a useful \emph{reduction of the problem} for systems $R$ with $L(R)$ unproved, i.e.\ Extended Frege and stronger.

\begin{theorem}[Bridge $\Leftarrow$ feasible correctness]
Let $\mathrm{ER}$ be Extended Resolution. If some polynomial-time $A$ decides SAT and $\mathrm{ER}$ (equivalently EF) proves, in polynomially bounded size, the reflection family ``$A(s)=\mathrm{UNSAT}\to\neg\exists y\,F_s(y)$'' uniformly in $|s|$ (i.e.\ PV proves $A$ correct, Cook 1975), then $B(\mathrm{ER})$ holds for that $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} Standard: instantiate the reflection proof at $s=\mathrm{enc}(F)$ and add a polynomial-size computation trace of $A$ on $F$. This is the content of LSPV-1 337/353/354 specialized to ER (Rmix341 compiler). \end{proof}
\noindent So $B(\mathrm{EF})$ is implied by ``$\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ with feasibly provable correctness''. The reverse (bridge from bare $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$) is, as far as I know, open; it is the real content the bridge must carry.

\begin{theorem}[Cheapest sufficient form]
Let $T_A$ be the theory PV$+\mathrm{Corr}(A)$. $B(\mathrm{EF})$ for all $A$ follows from: for every polynomial-time $A$ correct on SAT, EF has polynomial-size proofs of $\mathrm{Corr}_A(n)$. By the 353 family $H_{A,N}$, this is equivalent (under $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$) to polynomial EF-refutations of the \emph{single} explicit UNSAT family $H_{A,N}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} $\Leftarrow$ Theorem 2. $\Rightarrow$: $H_{A,N}$ is UNSAT, so $B$ applied to it gives polynomial size. \end{proof}

\section{Literature clue for this run}
Pich--Santhanam (ECCC TR23-199) derive $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$ from an EF \emph{unprovability} pattern (circuit lower bounds for $f_n$ hard to approximate but with no short EF proof), rather than from an EF lower bound for UNSAT instances. Structurally this is the same obstruction as Theorem 1: lower bounds for $R$ transfer only if their statement is itself inside the object being bounded. \textbf{Classification: USEFUL ANALOGY requiring a new transfer theorem.} It suggests replacing target $L(R)$ by ``$\mathrm{Corr}_A(n)$ lacks short EF proofs for every candidate $A$'', which is not weaker (by Theorem 3 it is $L$ for $H_{A,N}$) but is a \emph{single} family per $A$ rather than a worst case over all $F$. A 2026 result (Proof Analysis Problem, arXiv 2506.16956) shows proof analysis for EF is NP-complete; it gives no size lower bound and does not touch the bridge. Search found no 2026 EF lower bound. Cook--Reckhow: polynomially bounded system exists iff $\mathsf{NP}=\mathsf{coNP}$; optimal proof systems remain open.
