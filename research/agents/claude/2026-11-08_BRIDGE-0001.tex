\section*{Results (all elementary)}
Let $R$ be a fixed Cook--Reckhow system (poly-time proof checker) for UNSAT, $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\ge|\pi|$ up to polynomial factors.

\begin{proposition}[PROVED/EXACT: no free lunch for known-lower-bound systems]
If $\overline W_R(N)$ is superpolynomial unconditionally (e.g.\ $R$=resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege), then the bridge for $R$ is logically equivalent to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$. Hence the bridge cannot be proved for such $R$ without proving $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$. 
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} The premise of the implication is $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$, the conclusion is false; so the implication is equivalent to $\neg(\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P)$. \end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[PROVED/EXACT: necessary consequence]
For a Cook--Reckhow $R$, $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ implies $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. So the bridge for such $R$ asserts $\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow$ ``$R$ is polynomially bounded''; for $R$ with no known lower bound this is a statement about $R$ alone that is at least as strong as the open question ``is $R$ complete for the poly-bounded class when $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$''.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[CANDIDATE/OPEN: the real content of the bridge]
Given a poly-time decider $A$ for SAT, an $R$-proof of an unsatisfiable $F$ of size $\mathrm{poly}(|F|)$ would follow if $R$ had short proofs of the uniform soundness statement ``$A(x)=0\Rightarrow x\in\mathrm{UNSAT}$''. The bridge for a \emph{fixed} $R$ therefore requires that every correct poly-time $A$ has such feasible $R$-provable correctness, with proof length depending only polynomially on $A$'s run-time exponent. This is the feasible-provability/optimal-proof-system issue (Kraj\'i\v cek--Pudl\'ak; the existence of p-optimal systems is open). Choosing $R$ as ``transcripts of arbitrary machines $A$'' is not a fixed sound checker, since soundness of $A$ is not locally verifiable.
\end{proposition}

\section*{Consequence for the research plan}
\begin{itemize}
\item The weakest sufficient bridge should be stated for a system $R$ \emph{strong enough to prove correctness of its own deciders} (Frege/extended Frege/EF$+$reflection scale). Then the missing arrow splits into (i) $\overline W_{EF}$ superpolynomial (open, equivalent to EF not polynomially bounded, i.e.\ $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$-type hardness for EF) and (ii) the provability-of-correctness arrow above.
\item For weak $R$, the worst-case lower bounds are known but the bridge is equivalent to $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ (Prop.~1), so no transfer is gained. This explains why the Rmix341-style fixed combined systems in the corpus can have proved $\overline W_R$ values without implying anything about SAT.
\item Strategy discovery being free is harmless (as stated in the task) but does not address Prop.~3.
\end{itemize}
