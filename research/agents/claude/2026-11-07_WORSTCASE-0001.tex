\section*{Setup (PROVED/EXACT definitions)}
$R$ is a Cook--Reckhow refutation system (poly-time verifier $V_R(F,\pi)$). A \emph{multisign DAG hypergraph} derivation is a DAG whose nodes are signed clauses and whose hyperedges are $R$-inference steps; $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\in\{\text{size},\text{width},\text{space}=\text{peak pebbles over a chosen topological order},\ (\text{size},\text{space})\text{ pair}\}$. "Reduced logical state at step $t$" = the antichain of live clauses; "minimal work to reach it" = minimum size of a sub-DAG deriving it. Define
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)$.
Bridge: $B(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. Lower bound: $L(R):\ \overline W_R\notin\mathrm{poly}$.

\section*{Results}
\begin{prop}[free strategy $=$ nondeterminism; PROVED/EXACT] With size cost, ``minimise over all $\pi$ without charging search'' makes $\overline W_R$ exactly the Cook--Reckhow proof-length function. Hence $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is polynomially bounded, and (Cook--Reckhow, KNOWN) some $R$ is p-bounded iff $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. The free-search convention loses nothing and gains nothing over standard proof complexity.\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[bridge is a disjunction; PROVED/EXACT] $B(R)\iff(\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP})\ \vee\ (\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly})$. Consequently:
(a) if $L(R)$ is already known unconditionally (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege, polynomial calculus), then $B(R)\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$: the ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is \emph{equivalent to the goal} for every such $R$ and is therefore not a reduction;
(b) $L(R)\wedge B(R)\Rightarrow\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ (modus tollens), so the programme is non-circular only for $R$ with $L(R)$ and $B(R)$ both open, i.e.\ Frege, Extended Frege and stronger;
(c) a proof of $B(R)$ not by case split on $\mathrm P$ vs $\mathrm{NP}$ must build, from an arbitrary poly-time decider $A$, poly-size $R$-refutations of every unsatisfiable $F$.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Truth table of $P\Rightarrow Q$ with $P=$ ``SAT$\in$P'', $Q=$ ``$\overline W_R$ poly''; (a) $Q$ false; (b) contrapositive; (c) restatement. $\square$

\begin{prop}[what a non-circular transfer must be; PROVED/EXACT, conditional shape] Let $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,n}$ be the tautology ``for all $F$ of size $n$: $A_n(F)=0\to F$ unsatisfiable'' (poly-size, true iff $A$ is correct on size $n$). If $R$ p-simulates Frege and has poly-size proofs of $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,n}$, then every unsatisfiable $F$ has a poly-size $R$-refutation (evaluate $A_n$ on $F$ by its poly-size trace, cut with $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,n}$). So $T_\exists(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\exists A$ correct with $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,\cdot}$ poly-provable in $R$ implies $B(R)$. This is the Cook (1975) / Krajíček--Pudlák ``feasible provability of the algorithm's correctness'' route, and is the weakest \emph{constructive} content of the bridge.\end{prop}

\begin{lem}[failed transfer for the universal-$A$ form; CANDIDATE, needs verification against Pudl\'ak 1986] The form ``for \emph{every} correct poly-time $A$, $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,\cdot}$ is poly-provable in $R$'' should fail for $R=$ Extended Frege: take correct $A_0$ and let $A$ equal $A_0$ except it also scans for an $R$-proof of $\bot$ of length $\le n$ and, if found, answers arbitrarily. $A\equiv A_0$ as a function if $R$ is consistent, but poly-size proofs of $\mathrm{Ref}_{A,n}$ would require poly-size proofs of the bounded consistency statement $\mathrm{Con}_R(n)$, which finitistic G\"odel theorems (Pudl\'ak 1986; Krajíček--Pudl\'ak 1989) suggest are unavailable. Hence any viable bridge must use $\exists A$ (a canonical, e.g.\ Levin-optimal, $A$), not $\forall A$. I have not re-derived the exact size form of Pudl\'ak's theorem; treat as CANDIDATE.\end{lem}

\begin{cor}[correction of a search-engine inference; FALSIFIED/RETIRED] The claim ``$\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}\Rightarrow$ Extended Frege is p-bounded'' is invalid: Cook--Reckhow gives only \emph{some} p-bounded system. Extended Frege being p-bounded under $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ is exactly $B(\mathrm{eF})$, open.\end{cor}
