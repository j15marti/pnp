\title{Worst-case derivation cost $\overline W_R$: formalization, bridge audit, and small exact data}
\author{Claude (WORSTCASE-0001)}\date{2026-11-05}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} Nothing below is a lower bound for SAT.

\section{Definitions}
A \emph{derivation system} $R$ is a Cook--Reckhow proof system: a polynomial-time verifier $V_R(F,\pi)$ that accepts only unsatisfiable $F$ and, for every unsatisfiable $F$, accepts some $\pi$. Multisigned DAG-hypergraph derivations are one instance (nodes are derived signed clauses, hyperedges inference steps). $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)=|\pi|$. Define
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:V_R(F,\pi)}|\pi|$. Since the $\min$ is free, $\overline W_R$ is the NP-style certificate size of $R$.
Bridge: $B(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\section{Results}
\begin{prop}[Cook--Reckhow, KNOWN THEOREM]
$\exists R:\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. Hence ``$\forall R$ superpolynomial'' iff $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$, which implies $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$; a lower bound for one fixed $R$ implies nothing by itself.
\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[Bridge dichotomy, PROVED/EXACT]
If $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally known to be superpolynomial (resolution, bounded-depth Frege, polynomial calculus, ...), then $B(R)\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$.
\end{prop}
\begin{proof}
$B(R)$ is $X\Rightarrow Y$ with $Y$ ($\overline W_R\le$ poly) false; so $B(R)\equiv\neg X$. \qedhere
\end{proof}
So for every $R$ whose lower bound can be proved today, the bridge is a reformulation of the conclusion. For $R$ whose lower bound is open (Frege, Extended Frege), $B(R)$ reads $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R$ polynomially bounded; no proof or counter-model is known, and the lower bound and the bridge are independent open items.

\begin{prop}[Reflective bridge, CONDITIONAL]
Let $T$ be a sound theory with polynomial-time axioms and $R_T$ its proof system. If $T\vdash$ ``$A$ decides SAT in polynomial time'' for an explicit $A$, then $\overline W_{R_T}\le\mathrm{poly}$: the proof of $\neg F$ is the constant-size correctness proof plus the length-$\mathrm{poly}(|F|)$ evaluation of $A(F)$.
\end{prop}
This needs the correctness proof inside $T$; it cannot be assumed unconditionally (it is the Krajicek--Pudlak consistency-prover phenomenon).

\begin{prop}[Free strategy removes search, PROVED/EXACT]
$\overline W_R$ is invariant under any cost for finding $\pi$; it therefore cannot distinguish $R$ that are automatable from those that are not. Any route from a derivation lower bound to $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$ must pass through the opposite direction (automatability: search time $\le\mathrm{poly}(\overline W_R)$), not the stated bridge.
\end{prop}

\section{Hardness of the free quantity and literature}
Searched; secondary summaries only, primary texts not verified. Iwama and successors: deciding whether a refutation with $\le k$ resolution steps exists is NP-complete, with no constant-factor approximation unless $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$, so the freely granted minimum is itself hard to compute. Papamakarios (CCC 2024): depth-$d$ Frege is not automatable unless $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ (automatability direction). Krajicek--Pudlak: an optimal proof system exists iff a fast consistency prover exists; non-existence implies $\mathrm{NE}\ne\mathrm{coNE}$; existence open. Davis--Robere, ECCC 2026/055 (abstract only): Res(log) proves bounded-depth Frege lower bounds. Classification: Cook--Reckhow, Krajicek--Pudlak: directly transferable; automatability results: useful analogy requiring a new transfer theorem; none transfers the bridge.
