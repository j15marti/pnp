\section*{Setting}
$R$ is a Cook--Reckhow system closed under substitution and able to evaluate poly-size circuits with poly-size derivations (true for Extended Frege). $\overline W_R(N)$ as in the directive; the optimal derivation is free.

\begin{proposition}[Single-algorithm equivalence; PROVED/EXACT]
Let $A$ be a correct poly-time SAT decider and $H_{A,\ell}$ the reflection CNF expressing $\mathrm{Valid}(s)\wedge A(s)=\mathrm{UNSAT}\wedge y\models F_s$. Then $R$ is p-bounded iff $H_{A,\ell}$ has $\mathrm{poly}(\ell)$-size $R$-refutations.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
($\Leftarrow$) Substitute $s=\mathrm{enc}(F)$, evaluate $A$ and the parser by a poly circuit derivation, and obtain a refutation of $F$. ($\Rightarrow$) $H_{A,\ell}$ is UNSAT because $A$ is correct, and has size $\ell^{O(1)}$. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Why the free-optimum measure is an NP/coNP statement; KNOWN + PROVED]
(i) $\overline W_R$ polynomial iff $R$ is p-bounded, which implies $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ (Cook--Reckhow). (ii) If an optimal system $R^*$ exists and $\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}$, then $R^*$ is p-bounded: P$=$NP gives a p-bounded system, which $R^*$ simulates. So for $R^*$ the bridge holds, and ``$\overline W_{R^*}$ superpolynomial'' is equivalent to $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$, a statement at least as strong as $\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\end{proposition}
Consequence: the program as framed targets $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$-type bounds for $R$ unless the cost model charges time. Lower bounds for explicit $R$ transfer to P$\ne$NP only through the bridge, which is the open step.

\begin{proposition}[Quantifier obstruction; PROVED/EXACT]
For incorrect $A$, $H_{A,\ell}$ is satisfiable. Hence no family of hard UNSAT instances can be built as $H_{A,\ell}$ uniformly over all poly-time $A$: a lower bound must either quantify over correct $A$ only (a hypothesis) or use instances independent of $A$.
\end{proposition}
