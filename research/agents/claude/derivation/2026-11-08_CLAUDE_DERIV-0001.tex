\title{CLAUDE DERIV-0001\\[2pt]\large Worst-case minimum derivation cost $\overline W_R(N)$:\\ what the bridge $\SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ can and cannot do}
\author{CLAUDE (scheduled run)}\date{2026-11-08}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status.} P versus NP remains OPEN. Nothing below proves a lower bound.

\section{Setup}
A \emph{multisign DAG hypergraph} state: vertices = variables, hyperedges = clauses with a sign vector in $\{+,-\}^{e}$; a reduction replaces/adds hyperedges by a sound rule; a derivation $\pi$ is a DAG of states ending at the contradiction (empty hyperedge). $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$ is the number of derived hyperedges (size) or its encoding length. $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_\pi \mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$. The minimum over $\pi$ is free (no search charge): this is nondeterminism.

\section{Results}
\begin{proposition}[Cook--Reckhow transfer; \status{KNOWN THEOREM}, restated]\label{p1}
If $R$ has polynomial-time checkable derivations and $\overline W_R(N)$ is superpolynomial, then NP $\neq$ coNP, hence P $\neq$ NP.
\end{proposition}
\noindent So for any honest inference system the \emph{lower bound alone} already is the stronger statement NP$\ne$coNP; it is at least as hard as the open problem for Frege/Extended Frege (best known EF size bound is linear).

\begin{proposition}[Bridge triviality for systems with known bounds; \status{PROVED/EXACT}]\label{p2}
Let $R$ satisfy an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound (e.g.\ resolution: Haken; Chv\'atal--Szemer\'edi on random 3-CNF). Then ``$\SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$'' is logically equivalent to $\SAT\notin P$.
\end{proposition}
\emph{Proof.} The consequent is false, so the implication is equivalent to the negation of its hypothesis. $\square$

\begin{corollary}[\status{PROVED/EXACT}]
The bridge is informative only for $R$ whose worst-case cost is unknown, and then (Prop.~\ref{p1}) the lower bound alone already suffices for P$\ne$NP; the bridge is redundant in the role of a proof route. Bridge plus lower bound is not weaker than lower bound.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[What the bridge needs; \status{CONDITIONAL}]
A fixed $R$ satisfying the bridge for all poly-time deciders must p-simulate (up to the hypothesis) every Cook--Reckhow system's proofs of the form ``decider $M$ halts rejecting'', i.e.\ it behaves like a p-optimal system. Existence of p-optimal systems is open; known sufficient conditions are collapses of exponential-time classes (Kraj\'i\v{c}ek--Pud\l\'ak).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Pointwise vs.\ worst case; \status{PROVED/EXACT}]
Using $\max_F\min_\pi$ is correct for a lower bound on any algorithm that outputs a derivation. But an algorithm for SAT need not output one; the bridge must therefore be proved for a system that can \emph{encode a computation trace} (Extended Frege / Frege with reflection), where lower bounds are unavailable. Weak systems (resolution, polynomial calculus, cutting planes) have bounds but fail the bridge unconditionally (Prop.~\ref{p2}).
\end{proposition}

\section{Consequence for the five-agent plan}
Dichotomy: (a) $R$ weak $\Rightarrow$ lower bounds exist, no bridge; (b) $R$ strong $\Rightarrow$ bridge plausible, lower bound $\equiv$ NP$\neq$coNP-hard. The unexplored middle is a \emph{non-Cook--Reckhow cost}, e.g.\ charging derivations time \emph{and} space (peak simultaneous hyperedges, as in resolution width/space), where the cost can be superpolynomial while derivation checking stays easy only if the measure is not size. Suggested next targets: (i) space-cost $\overline W^{sp}_R$ for Frege-type $R$ (known: Ben-Sasson--Nordstr\"om type time-space tradeoffs), (ii) a transfer from space lower bounds to SAT time-space, which would meet the ARC/FLMV spine.

\section{Literature check}
Cook--Reckhow: polynomially bounded system exists iff NP=coNP. Kraj\'i\v{c}ek--Pud\l\'ak: p-optimal systems exist under exponential-time collapses. Extended Frege: only linear size lower bounds known (survey: Kraj\'i\v{c}ek). Proof Analysis Problem (arXiv 2506.16956): analyzing EF proofs is NP-complete (meta-complexity, no new bound). No clue found closing the bridge. Status: failed-transfer for weak $R$; analogy only for space measures.
