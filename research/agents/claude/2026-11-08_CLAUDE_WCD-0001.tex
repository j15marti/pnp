\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} No lower bound and no transfer theorem is claimed.

Let $R$ be a proof system with polynomial-time checkable derivations and $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$, with size-type cost. Say $R$ is \emph{$p$-bounded} if $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. Bridge: $B(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow R$ $p$-bounded.

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] $B(R)\iff(\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}\ \lor\ R\ p\text{-bounded})$. Hence if $\overline W_R$ is superpolynomial, $B(R)\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Material implication, and $\neg(R\ p\text{-bounded})$. \qed

So the bridge is not a weaker sufficient condition than the target; it is equivalent to it given the lower bound. Its content must come from an independent proof of $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R$ $p$-bounded.

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] If $R$ is $p$-bounded then $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ (Cook--Reckhow). Thus free-strategy $\overline W_R$ measures nondeterministic certificate size; a superpolynomial bound for one $R$ separates P from NP only through $B(R)$.\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[KNOWN THEOREM, Cook 1975; Krajíček] If $A$ is a polynomial-time SAT algorithm and PV proves $\forall F\,(A(F)=0\to F\notin\mathrm{SAT})$, then Extended Frege has polynomial-size proofs of all unsatisfiable $F$. So $B(\mathrm{EF})$ holds when $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ is witnessed by a PV-provably correct algorithm.\end{prop}

\textbf{Obstacle.} Correct but unprovable $A$: no known short EF proofs, so $B(\mathrm{EF})$ is OPEN. Per-instance minimisation over algorithms collapses (answer hard-wired), so free knowledge is legitimate only for sound locally-verifiable steps.

\textbf{Candidate next theorem.} Find a hypothesis $H$ (e.g.\ uniform reflection) with $H\wedge\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{EF}$ $p$-bounded and test whether $\neg H$ is a plausible route; literature: Pich--Santhanam TR23-199 (barrier), Alekseev ToC 2026 (algebraic EF-strength lower bound), Khaniki--Pich--Sokolov CCC 2026.
