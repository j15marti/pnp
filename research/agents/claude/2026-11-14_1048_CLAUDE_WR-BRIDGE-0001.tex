\section{Object and quantifiers}
Fix a Cook--Reckhow proof system $R$ for UNSAT CNFs (polynomial-time checker,
sound, complete). For $F$ UNSAT let
$w_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)$ where
$\operatorname{cost}_R$ is encoded proof length (shared DAG nodes counted once),
and
\[
 \WR(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N} w_R(F).
\]
The minimum is over \emph{all} valid derivations; discovery of the optimal
derivation is not charged (the directive's convention).

\emph{Multisign DAG hypergraph calculus.} Represent a 3-CNF as a hypergraph
whose hyperedges are signed variable sets (clauses). A ``reduction step'' that
merges two signed hyperedges on a clashing variable is a Resolution step; a
DAG of such steps with reuse is DAG-like Resolution, and the minimal reduced
logical state for UNSAT inputs is the empty hyperedge $\bot$. If the calculus
also allows introducing a fresh node $z\leftrightarrow\varphi(\cdot)$ for
already-present nodes (shared definitions), it is Extended Resolution, which
is p-equivalent to Extended Frege (EF) [Cook--Reckhow 1979]. This
classification decides which of the theorems below applies.

\section{Results}

\begin{proposition}[Execution lower bound; PROVED/EXACT]\label{p:exec}
Let $A$ be any algorithm such that on every UNSAT input $F$ its run can be
converted, in time $\poly(|F|+\operatorname{time}_A(F))$, into an
$R$-refutation of $F$ (e.g.\ $A$ outputs one). Then
$\operatorname{time}_A(F)\ge w_R(F)^{1/c}$ for a constant $c$ depending only
on the conversion, hence $T_A(N)\ge\WR(N)^{\Omega(1)}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The converter's output length is at most its running time, which is
$\le(|F|+\operatorname{time}_A(F))^{c}$, and it is an $R$-refutation, so
$w_R(F)\le(|F|+\operatorname{time}_A(F))^{c}$. Since $w_R(F)\ge$ the number of
input clauses used, $|F|$ is absorbed for the hard instances.
\end{proof}
This formalizes the directive's point that free knowledge of the optimal
sequence only helps the minimizer. Its scope is exactly the class of
$R$-\emph{transcript-producing} algorithms. Example (KNOWN THEOREM):
CDCL with restarts produces Resolution refutations and polynomially
simulates Resolution [Pipatsrisawat--Darwiche 2011; Atserias--Fichte--Thurley
2011], so Haken's bound gives CDCL exponential time on $\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n$.

\begin{proposition}[Bridge vacuity / equivalence; PROVED/EXACT]\label{p:vac}
Let $\mathrm{B}_R$ denote $\SAT\in\Pc\Rightarrow\WR(N)\le\poly(N)$ and
$\mathrm{LB}_R$ denote ``$\WR$ is superpolynomial''.
(a) $\Pc\ne\NP\Rightarrow\mathrm B_R$ for every $R$.
(b) $\mathrm B_R\wedge\mathrm{LB}_R\Rightarrow\Pc\ne\NP$.
(c) Hence, for every $R$ for which $\mathrm{LB}_R$ is a theorem
(tree-like and DAG Resolution, Cutting Planes, bounded-depth Frege,
Polynomial Calculus, Res(lin)), $\mathrm B_R\Leftrightarrow\Pc\ne\NP$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
(a) vacuous antecedent; (b) contraposition; (c) combine.
\end{proof}
\emph{Duplication note:} the same observation appears for quasipolynomial
bounds in Codex 2026-11-11 SAT-POLYNOMIAL-ASSUMPTION-AUDIT (``once
$\neg\mathrm{QBound}(R)$ is proved, (12) is logically equivalent to the
desired separation''). Adopted, stated here for the polynomial directive.
Consequence: choosing a weak $R$ with known lower bounds (the signed-hyperedge
merge calculus without extension) does not shrink the problem; the entire
difficulty moves into $\mathrm B_R$.

\begin{proposition}[Family-local bridge is false; PROVED/EXACT from KNOWN THEOREM]\label{p:local}
The pointwise strengthening ``if a polynomial-time algorithm correctly
rejects every member of an UNSAT family $\mathcal F$ then $w_R\le\poly$ on
$\mathcal F$'' is FALSE for $R=$ Resolution.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The family $\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n$ in its standard encoding is recognized
syntactically and rejected in linear time, while
$w_{\mathrm{Res}}(\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n)=2^{\Omega(n)}$ [Haken 1985].
\end{proof}
So every proof of $\mathrm B_R$ must use the \emph{global} hypothesis that one
algorithm is correct on all formulas (including formulas expressing its own
correctness); per-family speed is irrelevant. Status of the pointwise
simulation route: FALSIFIED/RETIRED for weak $R$.

\begin{theorem}[Self-certification form of the EF bridge; PROVED/EXACT modulo
standard translations]\label{t:self}
For $n$ let $\mathrm{Corr}_{A,n}$ be the propositional translation (with
extension variables for the computation of $A$ on length-$n$ inputs) of
``$A(\langle F\rangle)=0\Rightarrow F$ is falsified by every assignment''.
Then
\[
 \mathrm B_{EF}\iff\Bigl(\SAT\in\Pc\Rightarrow\exists A\text{ poly-time, correct
 for }\SAT,\ \text{with }\mathrm{Corr}_{A,n}\text{ having }\poly(n)\text{-size EF proofs}\Bigr).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
($\Leftarrow$) Given UNSAT $F$, $A(\langle F\rangle)=0$; the concrete
evaluation of the polynomial-size circuit for $A$ on the fixed input
$\langle F\rangle$ has a polynomial-size EF proof (gate-by-gate evaluation).
Substituting $\langle F\rangle$ into the EF proof of $\mathrm{Corr}_{A,n}$ and
applying modus ponens yields a polynomial-size EF refutation of $F$
[Cook 1975; Krajíček--Pudlák 1989]. Hence $W_{EF}(N)\le\poly(N)$.
($\Rightarrow$) If $\SAT\in\Pc$ via $A$ and $\mathrm B_{EF}$ holds, EF is
polynomially bounded on UNSAT CNFs, hence (by the standard Tseitin
transformation of the $\mathrm{Corr}_{A,n}$ tautology to an UNSAT CNF of
polynomial size, and EF's p-equivalence of tautology proving and CNF
refutation) $\mathrm{Corr}_{A,n}$ has polynomial EF proofs; $A$ is correct, so
these are tautologies.
\end{proof}
In particular, by Cook's PV--EF correspondence, $\mathrm B_{EF}$ holds for
every $A$ whose correctness is provable in $\mathrm{PV}$ (or $S^1_2$)
(KNOWN THEOREM), so $\mathrm{LB}_{EF}$ alone already rules out every
$\mathrm{PV}$-certified polynomial SAT algorithm. The remaining gap is exactly
correct algorithms whose correctness tautologies lack short EF proofs.
This is the frame of Pich--Santhanam (arXiv:2312.08163): EF lower bounds plus
$S^1_2$-provable hypotheses (anticheckers, learning from $\neg$OWF, etc.)
imply $\Pc\ne\NP$. Status of $\mathrm B_{EF}$ unconditionally: OPEN;
CONDITIONAL on those hypotheses.

[Krajíček--Pudlák 1998]).
