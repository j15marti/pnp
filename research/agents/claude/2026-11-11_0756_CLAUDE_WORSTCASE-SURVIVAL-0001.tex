\section{Target}
The shared directive asks for
\[
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi),
\qquad
\text{B3: } \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Longrightarrow \overline W_R(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N).
\]
The inner minimum lets the best derivation be chosen for free, as the directive requires. So $\overline W_R$ is a \emph{nondeterministic} (proof-length) measure: $\overline W_R(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N)$ says exactly that $R$ is polynomially bounded on UNSAT CNFs (up to the node-cost versus bit-length normalization already flagged as OPEN in REFEREE357).

\section{Bridge logic (KNOWN THEOREM; recorded for reconciliation, no novelty claimed)}
Write $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ for ``$\overline W_R$ is superpolynomial''. Write $R\le_p R'$ when $R'$ p-simulates $R$ with charged cost.
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:lattice}
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $R\le_p R'$, then $\mathrm{B3}(R)\Rightarrow\mathrm{B3}(R')$ and $\mathrm{LB}(R')\Rightarrow\mathrm{LB}(R)$.
\item $\mathrm{B3}(R)\wedge\mathrm{LB}(R)\Rightarrow\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$, for every $R$.
\item $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P\Rightarrow\mathrm{B3}(R)$ for every $R$ (vacuously). Hence $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P\iff\exists R\,[\mathrm{B3}(R)\wedge\mathrm{LB}(R)]$, with witness $R=$ Resolution (Haken).
\item If $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ is already a theorem, then $\mathrm{B3}(R)\iff\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
(1) Simulation transports polynomial upper bounds up the order and lower bounds down. (2) If SAT were in P, B3 would make $\overline W_R$ polynomial, contradicting LB. (3) The forward direction takes Resolution, which has a proved LB. The converse is (2). (4) follows from (2) and (3).
\end{proof}
\noindent Item 4 for Resolution is already in the corpus (LSPV-1/398, main.tex line~4293). The new use of the proposition here is \emph{diagnostic}. The ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is weak only for systems whose lower bound is not yet known. For every system with a proved superpolynomial lower bound (Resolution, cutting planes, polynomial calculus, bounded-depth Frege, bounded-depth Res($\oplus$)), proving B3 \emph{is} proving P$\ne$NP. Moving up in $\le_p$ makes B3 easier and LB harder. Size-optimality (CLOCKED336, REFEREE357) is the top of this order, where B3 holds outright and LB is equivalent to NP$\ne$coNP. No choice of $R$ makes both conjuncts easier at once. This is a bookkeeping constraint on the allocation, not a barrier theorem in the Baker--Gill--Solovay or Razborov--Rudich sense.

\begin{observation}[CONDITIONAL retirement of dense random 3-CNF]
M\"uller and Tzameret (arXiv:1101.3970) give polynomial-size TC$^0$-Frege refutations of random 3-CNFs with $m\ge c n^{1.4}$ clauses, with high probability. They formalize the Feige--Kim--Ofek witnesses. Frege p-simulates TC$^0$-Frege, and Extended Resolution p-simulates Extended Frege and hence Frege. So if the RES+VARDEF fragment of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ is full ER, and polynomial ER length gives polynomial charged node cost (corpus LSPV-1/354 copies RES+VARDEF proofs unchanged), then dense random 3-CNF cannot witness superpolynomial $\overline W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}$.
\end{observation}
\noindent Consequence: a superpolynomial $\overline W$ for any calculus containing ER, witnessed by \emph{any} explicit or random family, would be a superpolynomial Extended-Frege lower bound. That is a long-standing open problem with no known technique. The surviving candidate, constant-density random 3-CNF, has no known Frege upper or lower bound.

\section{Finite-size width screen (COMPUTATIONAL)}
\texttt{research/agents/claude/worst-case/survival\_width.py} computes, for each instance, the exact minimum Resolution refutation width $w(F\vdash\bot)$ by width-bounded saturation at $k=3,4,\dots$. An independent replay function re-checks each extracted refutation line by line: axiom membership, a single clashing pivot, the exact resolvent, and width at most $k$. It also counts locally implied XOR constraints and tests whether GF(2) elimination on them refutes. Brute-force UNSAT is checked for $n\le16$. Above that, the replayed refutation is itself the UNSAT certificate. Raw output is in \texttt{survival\_width\_results.jsonl}. Each call runs in under about 30\,s; the batch is chunked one instance per call.

\paragraph{Findings.}
(i) Random 3-CNF at densities 5 and 8 has minimum width exactly 3, equal to the axiom width, for every tested UNSAT instance up to $n=40$. Satisfiable draws at density 5 were filtered out. Ben-Sasson--Wigderson width $\varepsilon n$ only appears beyond this range, so exact small-$n$ minima \emph{cannot} show random-3-CNF hardness, and finite exact $W_R$ tables for $n\le40$ should not be read as evidence about $\overline W_R$ growth.
(ii) Tseitin width already grows (4 at $n=9$, 5 at $n=12$), but GF(2) elimination on the local XORs refutes every Tseitin instance. This reproduces the known retirement.
(iii) Random 3-CNF has zero locally implied XORs. The GF(2) rule gets no direct purchase without paid extension variables.
(iv) Control: a satisfiable random 3-CNF ($n=12$, density 3) admits no width-$\le4$ refutation, as soundness requires. PHP$^{h+1}_h$ for $h=2,3$ has width $h$, its axiom width. (v) These are tiny instances. Width is only a Resolution proxy, and none of this bears on ER or $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ minima.
