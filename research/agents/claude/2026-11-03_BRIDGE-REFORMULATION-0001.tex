\title{The conditional bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ is a reformulation}
\author{Claude (Red Team / Auditor role)}\date{3 November 2026}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status.} PROVED/EXACT (elementary). P versus NP remains OPEN; no lower bound is claimed.

Let $R$ be a fixed Cook--Reckhow proof system, $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ the statement $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)$, and $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ the statement ``$\overline W_R$ is superpolynomial''.
\begin{proposition}\label{p1}
$\mathrm{LB}(R)\wedge\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\Rightarrow \mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$. Conversely, if $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$ then $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ holds vacuously for every $R$. Hence, given $\mathrm{LB}(R)$, $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$ (as truth values).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} Contraposition, and the vacuous case. \end{proof}
So for any $R$ with an unconditional lower bound (Resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege), proving $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ is exactly as hard as $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ and is a reformulation, not progress. For $R$ strong enough that $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ is plausible (Extended Frege), $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ is the long-open Frege/EF lower-bound problem.
\begin{proposition}\label{p2}
If $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$ via a machine $A$, the system $R_A$ whose proof of $\bot$ from $F$ is the empty string, verified by running $A(F)$, is a poly-time-verifiable system with $O(1)$-size proofs. Thus $\mathrm{Bridge}(R)$ holds whenever $R$ simulates every poly-time-verifiable system on UNSAT (an optimal system), and the weakest hypothesis on $R$ obtainable this way is simulation of $R_A$ for the unknown $A$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} $A$ accepts exactly satisfiable $F$, so rejection certifies UNSAT; verification is polynomial. Simulation of $R_A$ gives polynomial size. \end{proof}
\textbf{Consequence for the programme.} A fixed $R$ cannot be tailored to the unknown $A$; a non-vacuous bridge needs either optimality of $R$ (open; Krajíček--Pudlák 1989, Messner, Egidy 2026 arXiv:2602.02294) or provability of $A$'s soundness in $R$ (the reflection route already in the corpus, row ``universal reflection 353''). The sound program is therefore: pick $R$ with $\mathrm{LB}(R)$ unconditional \emph{and} charge the bridge to a stated, separately falsifiable hypothesis (CONDITIONAL), never to $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$ alone.

\textbf{Literature check.} Cook--Reckhow: $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ iff a polynomially bounded system exists (so $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ gives \emph{some} such system, namely $R_A$, not a prescribed one). Optimal-system existence remains open (arXiv:1609.04218 survey; arXiv:2304.14702; arXiv:2602.02294). No 2026 EF lower bound found.
