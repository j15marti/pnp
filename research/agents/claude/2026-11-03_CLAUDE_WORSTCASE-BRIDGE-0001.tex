\section*{Worst-case derivation cost $\overline W_R$: the bridge $SAT\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly}$}
\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} Nothing below is a lower bound.

\paragraph{CORPUS REVIEWED.} Repo tree; AGENTS.md; LSPV-1 reports 336--356 (several already use \texttt{WORSTCASE}/min-cost language: 348, 350, 352); LSPV-4 341; Claude world-ledger and TPORT notes. Not all re-read in full; this note is a bridge-logic audit, not a corpus rewrite. No MAIN edit made (ownership regions belong to LSPV roles).

\paragraph{Dichotomy for the bridge (PROVED/EXACT, elementary).}
Let $\operatorname{cost}_R$ be the derivation cost.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{R is a Cook--Reckhow proof system, cost = proof size.} A bound $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ means $R$ is polynomially bounded, which holds iff it witnesses $NP=coNP$ (KNOWN THEOREM, Cook--Reckhow 1979). Then a superpolynomial lower bound proves $NP\neq coNP$, strictly stronger than $P\neq NP$. The bridge is about whether $P=NP$ forces $R$ to be bounded: $P=NP\Rightarrow NP=coNP$ gives \emph{some} bounded system, but not that a given explicit $R$ (Frege, EF) is bounded.
\item \emph{Existence is vacuous.} By cases on $P=NP$, a fixed $R$ with ``$SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le$ poly'' exists classically. A lower bound for it is equivalent to $P\ne NP$, so the bridge gives nothing for an explicitly constructed $R$. (PROVED/EXACT, non-constructive.)
\item \emph{R = machine-trace model, cost = running time, strategy chosen freely.} Bridge is trivial, but then the free min is nondeterministic, so the model is coNP-like unless derivation steps are locally sound. Soundness of an arbitrary claimed decider is not locally checkable; this is the same obstruction as in 1.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Consequence (PROVED/EXACT):} the weakest sufficient bridge for an explicit $R$ is equivalent to a \emph{provable-soundness/reflection} statement: $R$ (or its simulation) must prove, in size poly in the instance, that a fixed poly-time SAT decider is correct. Without that, bridge for explicit $R$ is itself OPEN. Ledger class: reformulation, not progress.

\paragraph{Literature clues (checked this run).}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cook--Reckhow: bounded system $\iff NP=coNP$. KNOWN THEOREM.
\item Krajíček--Pudlák 1998: EF not weakly automatable unless RSA insecure against P/poly. KNOWN (conditional). Relevance: free-strategy assumption cannot be replaced by efficient search for EF-type systems under crypto assumptions; harmless for lower bounds, fatal for upper-bound constructions.
\item Pich--Santhanam (2023): lower bounds for EF plus axioms imply circuit lower bounds for SAT. KNOWN THEOREM; closest published form of a ``derivation lower bound $\Rightarrow$ SAT hardness'' bridge, but requires a specific tautology-witnessing hypothesis and is not an unconditional bridge.
\item Provability of $P=NP$ in PV connects to collapse of bounded arithmetic; relevant to whether the reflection above can be proved.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Correction (STALE/OVERSTATED claim caught):} an unchecked search summary suggested ``$P=NP$ makes EF polynomially bounded.'' That does not follow. $P=NP$ gives \emph{automatability} (short proofs, when they exist, can be found in time poly in their size), not existence of short proofs of every tautology. Retired inference.

\paragraph{Hard-instance survey (no new result).} Known: resolution lower bounds for pigeonhole/Tseitin on expanders; algebraic (PC/NS) and cutting-planes bounds; none extends to Frege/EF. ``Survives circuit compression / shared DAGs'' is exactly the open Frege/EF regime; shared-DAG sharing is what EF already adds to resolution.
