\title{Worst-case derivation cost: free search, the bridge dichotomy, and the soundness-reflection gap}\author{CLAUDE WC-0001}\maketitle
\noindent\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} No lower bound is claimed.

\section*{Setup}
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$. Bridge $B(R)$: $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\section*{Results}
\textbf{Prop.\ 1 (PROVED/EXACT; free search is nondeterminism).} If each inference step is poly-time checkable and $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)\ge$ poly$^{-1}$(bit length of $\pi$), then $(R,\text{check})$ is a Cook--Reckhow system and $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is $p$-bounded. Granting the optimal sequence for free thus equals the existential quantifier of $\mathrm{coNP}\subseteq\mathrm{NP}$-type statements.

\textbf{Prop.\ 2 (PROVED/EXACT; dichotomy).} If $\overline W_R$ is unconditionally superpolynomial, then $B(R)\iff \mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$. Proof: $B(R)$ with false conclusion gives $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$; conversely $\mathrm{SAT}\notin P$ makes $B(R)$ vacuous. Hence no system with a known lower bound (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege) can supply a bridge for free.

\textbf{Prop.\ 3 (PROVED/EXACT, conditional hypothesis).} Suppose $R$ is closed under cut/modus ponens with poly overhead and $A$ is a poly-time SAT decider whose soundness instances $\mathrm{Rej}_A(F)\to\neg F$ have $R$-proofs of size $\mathrm{poly}(|F|)$. Then $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ (the trace of $A$ on $F$ is poly, $\mathrm{Rej}_A(F)$ is verified by evaluation, then cut). Corollary: $\overline W_R$ superpoly $\Rightarrow$ $\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$ or no poly-time SAT algorithm has poly-size $R$-proofs of its soundness. \textbf{OPEN arrow:} $B(\mathrm{EF})$; Levin search yields verified witnesses but its rejections rest on an unknown time bound, so the standard universal algorithm does not discharge the hypothesis.

\textbf{Remark 4 (CANDIDATE).} Any multisign DAG-hypergraph reduction model with poly-checkable local moves is a Cook--Reckhow system $R_{MS}$; its lower bounds are proof-complexity lower bounds and meet the barriers of Pich--Santhanam if $R_{MS}$ simulates EF.
