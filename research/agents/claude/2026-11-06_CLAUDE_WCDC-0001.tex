\section{Formalization}
A \emph{derivation system} $R$ is a sound and complete refutation system for CNF with a polynomial-time verifier for derivation DAGs (this is what ``multisign DAG hypergraph derivation'' must be shown to be; otherwise $\overline W_R$ is not a proof-size measure). Let $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)=|\pi|$ (DAG size; encoding and time--space overheads are polynomial and do not move the superpolynomial threshold). Then
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}|\pi|$.
Charging nothing for finding $\pi$ is exactly the Cook--Reckhow convention.

\section{Results}
\begin{theorem}[Cook--Reckhow, \status{KNOWN THEOREM}]
$R$ is polynomially bounded ($\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$) iff the verifier makes $\mathrm{UNSAT}\in\mathrm{NP}$. Hence ``$\overline W_R$ superpolynomial for every $R$'' $\iff$ $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$, which implies $P\ne NP$ and is strictly the stronger target. A fixed $R$ gives only a subgoal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Bridge is circular where bounds are known; \status{PROVED/EXACT}]
Write $\mathrm{Br}(R)$ for $\SAT\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$. If $\overline W_R$ is known superpolynomial (e.g.\ resolution, Haken; bounded-depth Frege), then $\mathrm{Br}(R)\iff \SAT\notin P$. So the bridge is \emph{not} weaker than $P\ne NP$ for such $R$. For $R$ with open status, $\mathrm{Br}(R)\iff(P=NP\Rightarrow R\text{ is p-bounded})$, which holds if $R$ is an optimal proof system (P=NP gives NP=coNP, hence some p-bounded system, which $R$ would p-simulate). Existence of an optimal system is open and widely doubted.
\end{proposition}
\emph{Proof.} Truth-table of an implication with true consequent / equivalence of antecedent; second part is the definition plus Cook--Reckhow. \qed

\begin{proposition}[Provable-correctness bridge, \status{KNOWN THEOREM}, Cook 1975; Krajíček--Pudlák]
If $A$ is a polynomial-time SAT decider and $PV\vdash$ ``$A(F)=0\Rightarrow\forall a\,\neg F(a)$'', then Extended Frege has polynomial-size refutations of every UNSAT $F$.
\end{proposition}
\emph{Sketch.} Translate the PV theorem to propositional tautologies of polynomial EF-proof size; substitute the concrete $F$; the computation $A(F)=0$ is a polynomial-size evaluation, then conclude. \qed

\begin{corollary}[\status{PROVED/EXACT} from the two facts above]
A superpolynomial EF lower bound proves only: $\SAT\notin P$ \emph{or} no polynomial SAT algorithm is PV-provably correct. It does not by itself give $P\ne NP$. The missing arrow is exactly provability of soundness of the algorithm in a theory EF can simulate; the transcript of $A$ on $F$ is not a proof of unsatisfiability. Pich--Santhanam (2023) obtain $P\ne NP$ from non-p-boundedness of EF only under additional assumptions \status{CONDITIONAL}.
\end{corollary}

\section{Red-team verdict and next target}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The ``free best sequence'' convention is legitimate but makes the target a proof-complexity theorem equivalent in difficulty to a Frege/EF lower bound (open for 40+ years). No route in the corpus lowers this difficulty.
\item The weakest sufficient bridge is not a pure complexity statement: it needs either optimality of $R$ or provable correctness of every polynomial SAT algorithm. Proposed target \textbf{T-PROV}: find a theory $T\supseteq PV$ and show that if $\SAT\in P$ then \emph{some} algorithm is $T$-provably correct (false-friend: Levin's universal search is provably correct only for its \emph{verifier}, not for its halting claims). \status{OPEN}.
\item Any ``multisign DAG hypergraph'' reduced-state normal form must be shown to be (a) sound, (b) p-verifiable, (c) either p-simulated by EF or stronger; otherwise a lower bound on it does not enter the chain above.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Literature check (2026-11-06).} Search found no result giving EF lower bounds $\Rightarrow$ $P\ne NP$ unconditionally; Pich--Santhanam conditional implications; recent work showing the Proof Analysis Problem for EF is NP-complete (so under $P\ne NP$ automatability of EF fails; this is consistent with, not a step toward, the bridge). Classification: Cook--Krajíček bridge = directly transferable theorem; PQ-style hidden-structure ideas = no transfer identified.
