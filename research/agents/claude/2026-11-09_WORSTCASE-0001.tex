\section{Setting}
Let $R$ be a Cook--Reckhow proof system (poly-time verifier for UNSAT; the multisign DAG hypergraph derivations are a particular such system if derivation validity is poly-time checkable). Let $\mathrm{cost}_R$ be size (or a time--space measure) and
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$.

\section{Results}
\begin{proposition}[free strategy = proof complexity] \status{PROVED/EXACT}
Granting the best derivation for free makes $\min_\pi\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$ exactly the shortest-proof measure. So $\overline W_R$ is the proof-complexity function of $R$; nothing about search is charged, and $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is $p$-bounded. Hence $\overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly}\Rightarrow \mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ (Cook--Reckhow).
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[bridge holds exactly for optimal systems] \status{KNOWN THEOREM, rederived}
If $R$ is optimal (polynomially simulates every proof system), then $\SAT\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$, and moreover $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\iff \mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$.
\end{theorem}
\emph{Proof.} $P=NP$ gives $NP=coNP$, so a $p$-bounded system $S$ exists; $R$ simulates $S$. Converse by the Proposition. $\square$

\begin{corollary}[the programme overshoots] \status{PROVED/EXACT}
For optimal $R$ a superpolynomial lower bound on $\overline W_R$ is \emph{equivalent} to $NP\ne coNP$, which is at least as strong as $P\neq NP$ and not known equivalent. The existence of an optimal $R$ is OPEN (Krajicek--Pudlak: implied by $NE=coNE$; equivalent to fast consistency provers). So the bridge, for a fixed $R$, is either (a) a theorem requiring an open existence result, or (b) for a non-optimal $R$, see below.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[no content for systems with known lower bounds] \status{PROVED/EXACT}
If $R$ has an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound (resolution, bounded-depth Frege, polynomial calculus, cutting planes), the conclusion of the bridge is false, so the bridge is equivalent to $\SAT\notin P$. It then gives no route to $P\ne NP$; it is merely a restatement. The bridge is informative only for $R$ whose lower bounds are unknown (Frege, Extended Frege, and the proposed combined system if it p-simulates EF).
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[weakest sufficient hypothesis found] \status{CONDITIONAL / KNOWN (Cook 1975 translation)}
If $\SAT$ has a poly-time algorithm $A$ whose correctness is provable in $PV$ (equivalently, a uniform poly-size EF/PV-proof family of ``$A$ accepts $\Rightarrow$ assignment valid'' for the reflection form), then Extended Frege is $p$-bounded. Plain $\SAT\in P$ is not known to suffice: the trace of $A$ on $F$ is a poly-size object, but verifying that a rejecting trace implies unsatisfiability needs $A$'s soundness inside $R$ (a reflection principle); the system $R_A$ ``accepting traces of $A$'' is $p$-bounded but depends on $A$ and is sound only if $A$ is correct. This is why the stated bridge cannot be proved for a \emph{fixed} $R$ by this method without a soundness hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

\section{Consequences for the five roles}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lead: restate the target as a bridge to $R=$EF with the PV-correctness hypothesis (CONDITIONAL), or accept that the programme proves $NP\ne coNP$-strength statements.
\item Reduction builder: certify the combined system p-simulates EF; otherwise by the Proposition it inherits no information for $P$ vs $NP$ when its lower bounds are already known.
\item Hard-instance family: candidates must defeat EF. Known degree/width lower bounds (Ben-Sasson--Wigderson, Grigoriev, Atserias--Dalmau) only kill weak fragments; they do not survive circuit compression or shared-DAG extension variables.
\item Auditor: time--space cost refines but does not remove the above: space-bounded variants are $\le$ size, so size lower bounds are needed first.
\item Red team: Pich--Santhanam type results indicate strong-system lower bounds imply circuit lower bounds, so natural-proof-style barriers apply; any argument must also fail on relativized worlds where $NP\ne coNP$ is not forced either way.
\end{itemize}
