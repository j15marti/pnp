\section{Definitions}
For a rule system $R$ with locally checkable steps, $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$,
with cost the bit-length of the derivation DAG. Bridge: $B(R):\ \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] $B(R)\iff(\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP})\lor(\overline W_R\in\mathrm{poly})$. Hence if $\overline W_R$ is known
superpolynomial (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege on suitable families) $B(R)\iff \mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$:
the bridge is then a restatement and cannot be proved without proving $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.\end{prop}
\begin{proof} Material implication; $\overline W_R$ superpolynomial makes the second disjunct false.\end{proof}

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT, free strategy] If derivation steps are poly-time checkable, $R$ is a Cook--Reckhow system and
$\overline W_R$ is its proof-size function; granting the optimal $\pi$ is already the standard definition. A superpolynomial
$\overline W_R$ for one $R$ does not imply $\mathrm{NP}\neq\mathrm{coNP}$ (all systems needed).\end{prop}

\section{Where the bridge is non-vacuous}
Only for $R$ with no known superpolynomial lower bound (Frege, Extended Frege and stronger). KNOWN: $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$
iff some system is p-bounded (Cook--Reckhow); existence of an optimal system is open (Krajicek--Pudlak). If $R$ were optimal,
$B(R)$ would follow, since $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. So a proof of $B(R)$ for fixed $R$ without
optimality must use the specific structure of $R$.

\begin{prop}[CANDIDATE diagnosis] Given a decider $A$ for SAT in time $p$, a derivation of $\bot$ from UNSAT $F$ must certify
``$A(F)=0\Rightarrow F$ unsat''. Levin search only certifies the accepting side (assignments are checkable). The rejecting side is a
$\Pi_1$-type soundness statement; $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ alone does not make it provable in $R$. Under provable correctness in $\mathrm{PV}$,
Extended Frege p-simulation gives $B(\mathrm{EF})$ (standard Cook translation; KNOWN, to be re-verified citation-wise).\end{prop}
