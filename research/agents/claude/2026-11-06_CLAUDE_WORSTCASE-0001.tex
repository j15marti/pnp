\paragraph{Status.} P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}. Nothing below proves a lower bound or the bridge.
Let $R$ be a Cook--Reckhow proof system (polytime-verifiable $\pi$), $\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)=|\pi|$,
and $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}|\pi|$.
Bridge: $B(R)\;:=\;(\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly})$.

\begin{thm}[PROVED/EXACT: free optimal sequence $=$ nondeterminism]
$\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is polynomially bounded. Hence a superpolynomial bound on $\overline W_R$ is an
NP-vs-coNP-type statement about $R$; granting the optimal sequence for free removes exactly the search cost, and
nothing else. (Immediate from definitions.)
\end{thm}

\begin{thm}[PROVED/EXACT: bridge is vacuous for provably hard $R$]
If $\overline W_R$ is \emph{known} superpolynomial (resolution, cutting planes, polynomial calculus, bounded-depth Frege),
then $B(R)\iff \mathrm{SAT}\notin P$. So proving $B(R)$ for any such $R$ \emph{is} proving $P\neq NP$ (it is not a weaker step).
\end{thm}

\begin{thm}[PROVED/EXACT: sufficient form of the bridge]
For a correct polytime SAT decider $A$ let $Q_A$ be: a proof of unsatisfiable $F$ is the accepting-$0$ trace of $A$ on $F$.
$Q_A$ is a Cook--Reckhow system exactly when $A$ is correct. If $R$ p-simulates $Q_A$ for every correct $A$, then $B(R)$;
conversely $B(R)$ and $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ give $R$ p-simulating every $Q_A$ (R is p-bounded). The simulation is the tautology
family $\mathrm{Sound}_{A,n}$: ``trace of $A$ on $F$ outputs 0 $\Rightarrow$ no assignment satisfies $F$''.
\end{thm}

\begin{cor}[KNOWN THEOREM-based, CONDITIONAL]
If $\mathrm{PV}\vdash$ ``$A$ decides SAT'', then by the standard PV$\to$EF translation (Cook 1975; Krajíček--Pudlák; Krajíček's book) EF has
polynomial-size proofs of $\mathrm{Sound}_{A,n}$, so $\overline W_{EF}\le\mathrm{poly}$. Contrapositive: a superpolynomial EF
bound would show \emph{no PV-provably-correct polytime SAT algorithm exists} --- strictly weaker than $P\ne NP$. The
literature (Krajíček, PV and P vs NP) frames it as consistency of $P\neq NP\neq coNP$ with PV, matching this.
The step ``drop provability'' is the open load-bearing arrow; Levin-style universal search does not supply it.
\end{cor}

\paragraph{Weakest sufficient bridge (CANDIDATE target).}
$U$: \emph{for every correct polytime $A$, $R$ has $\mathrm{poly}$-size proofs of $\mathrm{Sound}_{A,n}$ for a fixed $R$ that
also admits a superpolynomial lower bound.} Dilemma: weak $R$ ⇒ lower bounds exist but $U$ is equivalent to $P\ne NP$
(Thm 2); strong $R$ (EF, circuit Frege) ⇒ $U$ is plausible only under provable correctness, and the lower bound is beyond current
techniques (feasible interpolation fails for EF/TC$^0$-Frege under factoring hardness, Bonet--Pitassi--Raz).

\paragraph{Red team / hard-family status.} No candidate hard UNSAT family surviving EF is known; natural candidates
(Nisan--Wigderson/PRG tautologies, Krajíček) are assumption-dependent. Time--space audit: the ``peak memory'' of $\pi$
(clause space) is not the memory of $A$; $Q_A$ must be charged $(T_A,S_A)$ of the decider, which is polynomial, so the
time-space dimension adds no obstruction to Thm 3 but also no lower-bound leverage.

\paragraph{Literature check (this run).} Krajíček: PV proves $P=NP$ iff PV defines a polytime algorithm outputting a
satisfying assignment or an EF refutation; EF lower bound $\Rightarrow$ consistency of $P\neq NP\neq coNP$ with PV.
Pich--Santhanam 2023: EF-with-axioms lower bound $\Rightarrow$ SAT needs superpolynomial circuits (if witnessing formulas are tautologies).
Atserias--M\"uller / Proof Analysis Problem (2025, rev. 2026): PAP for EF is NP-complete; $P=NP$ implies automatability of every
proof system (automatability, not boundedness). Krajíček June 2026 (arXiv 2606.13367): EF equivalence proofs and circuit rewriting chains.
Optimal-proof-system question remains open (KP89). Classification: Cor.~is a directly transferable theorem; Pich--Santhanam is an
analogy needing a new bridge (it yields circuit, not uniform, lower bounds); automatability results are a misleading analogy for boundedness.
Not independently re-verified beyond abstracts.

a non-PV-provable correct $A$ can be forced by relativization/oracle constructions.
