\section{Setting}
$\mathcal R$ is a reduction system whose rule applications are checkable in time polynomial in the size of the step (otherwise the jump $F\to\bot$ is free; cf.\ task item 4). Then $W_{\mathcal R}$ is, up to a polynomial, the minimum proof size of a Cook--Reckhow system $V_{\mathcal R}$ (the verifier replays and checks the DAG). Write $\mathrm{pb}(\mathcal R)$ for ``$W_{\mathcal R}(F)\le \mathrm{poly}(|F|)$ on all UNSAT $F$'', and $\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\mathcal R)$ for the transfer theorem restricted to polynomial-time correct deciders: every correct $A$ with $T_A\le \mathrm{poly}$ has $W_{\mathcal R}(F)\le p_A(N+T_A(F))$.

\section{Results}
\begin{proposition}[Transfer is not independent of $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$]
$\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\mathcal R)\iff(\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{pb}(\mathcal R))$. Hence
\[
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{poly}}(\mathcal R)\wedge\neg\mathrm{pb}(\mathcal R)\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}\wedge\neg\mathrm{pb}(\mathcal R).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
($\Rightarrow$) If $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ a correct poly-time $A$ exists and the transfer gives $W_{\mathcal R}\le p_A(N+\mathrm{poly}(N))$. ($\Leftarrow$) If $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ no correct poly-time $A$ exists, so the statement is vacuous; if $\mathrm{pb}(\mathcal R)$ holds the conclusion is immediate. The second line follows by conjoining $\neg\mathrm{pb}$.
\end{proof}
\emph{Consequence.} Any unconditional proof of the polynomial-time transfer for a system with a superpolynomial lower bound already proves $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$. The proposed programme (transfer $+$ lower bound) therefore cannot be a decomposition into two independently easier lemmas via poly-time deciders alone; only the \emph{non-vacuous} transfer, covering superpolynomial-time deciders with translation overhead controlled by $T_A$, carries independent content. That is exactly the user's item~3 ``covering every algorithm''.

\begin{proposition}[Closure under automatizable systems]
Let $Q$ be a Cook--Reckhow system that is automatizable: there is a correct SAT decider $A_Q$ with $T_{A_Q}(F)\le q(N+S_Q(F))$, $S_Q(F)$ the minimum $Q$-proof size. If the transfer holds for $A_Q$, then $S_{\mathcal R}(F):=W_{\mathcal R}(F)\le p(N+q(N+S_Q(F)))$, i.e.\ $\mathcal R$ polynomially simulates $Q$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} Instantiate the transfer at $A=A_Q$. \end{proof}
So a transfer-closed $\mathcal R$ must polynomially simulate every automatizable system: Nullstellensatz/polynomial calculus at degree $d$ ($n^{O(d)}$ time), Sherali--Adams, bounded-degree sum-of-squares, bounded-width Resolution ($n^{O(w)}$), and the XOR/Gaussian-elimination escape (Tseitin). This is the exact version of the failure found earlier in the corpus for $\mathcal R=$ ordinary Resolution (LSPV-1/334). Any lower bound must hold for a system at least this strong, and by the known inclusions of such systems in Frege-type systems with extension, the natural target is Extended Frege (or stronger), where no superpolynomial lower bound is known.

\begin{proposition}[Per-algorithm transfer needs a feasible soundness proof]
If $\mathcal R$ has a translator from traces of $A$ to $\mathcal R$-proofs with $|\pi|\le p_A(N+T_A)$ \emph{and} the translator is itself uniformly verifiable, then a short proof of $\mathrm{Sound}(A)$ in the theory $T_{\mathcal R}$ associated with $\mathcal R$ (Krajíček--Pudlák) is obtained by composing reflection with the translator's correctness; conversely, a uniform translator requires $T_{\mathcal R}\vdash \mathrm{Sound}(A)$ for the algorithm in question.
\end{proposition}
\emph{Status: CANDIDATE.} I state this as the expected form of the bridge (a correct $A$ whose soundness is unprovable in $T_{\mathcal R}$ would break uniform translation); I have not proved the converse direction and do not claim it. It follows the correspondence of Krajíček and Pudlák (J.\ Symbolic Logic 54 (1989) 1063--1079) between propositional proof systems and first-order theories, which would need to be matched exactly to the machine model.

Classification: Cook--Reckhow $=$ directly transferable framework; automatizability closure $=$ exact (Prop.\,2); $PV_1$ formalization $=$ analogy needing a new transfer theorem. No result found giving a general translation from arbitrary SAT algorithms to short proofs, as expected: it would imply $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ \emph{from a closed system}, i.e.\ a strong conditional.
