\title{Worst-case derivation complexity $\overline W_R(N)$: what the free-strategy convention does and does not buy}
\date{2026-11-05}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status.} P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}. Nothing below is a lower bound on $\overline W_R$ for any strong $R$.
\section*{Setup}
$\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$, with the argmin chosen freely (search uncharged).
Derivations are DAGs (hyperedges = rule applications with several premises); $\mathrm{cost}$ = total bit size of nodes (time) or peak live-node size (space).
\section*{Results}
\begin{prop}[free-semantic collapse; PROVED/EXACT]\label{p1}
If $R$ has a rule ``$\varphi$ follows semantically from $\Gamma$'' (or any rule whose validity is not checkable in time polynomial in its cost), then $\overline W_R(N)=O(N)$ with the uncharged argmin. Hence $R$ must have poly-time checkable rules. (Matches the retired status of ``free semantic SAT answers''.)
\end{prop}
\begin{thm}[ceiling of the free-strategy convention; PROVED/EXACT]\label{t1}
Let $R$ be a Cook--Reckhow system (derivations checkable in time poly in $|F|+|\pi|$, sound, complete). Then $\overline W_R\le \mathrm{poly}$ iff $R$ is polynomially bounded, which implies $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$.
\end{thm}
\emph{Proof.} $\min_\pi\mathrm{cost}(\pi)$ is exactly the shortest-proof length $\ell_R(F)$ (up to the polynomial checking time); this is Cook--Reckhow. A poly bound on $\max_F\ell_R(F)$ puts UNSAT in NP. \qed

\noindent\textbf{Consequence.} Because the argmin is free, $\overline W_R$ is a \emph{nondeterministic} measure. Superpolynomial lower bounds for every such $R$ would give only $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$ (implying $\mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$, but as the harder statement); a lower bound for one fixed $R$ is weaker still. The bridge $B(R)$: ``SAT$\in$P $\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$'' is therefore the entire content connecting a proof-complexity bound to $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\begin{thm}[characterization of the bridge; PROVED/EXACT for (a),(b); (c) KNOWN, recalled from Krajíček--Pudlák/Cook, citation not re-verified]\label{t2}
Assume SAT$\in$P via machine $M$. Let $S_M$ be the system whose derivation of $F$ is empty and which accepts iff $M(F)$ rejects. Then:
(a) $S_M$ is a poly-time checkable, polynomially bounded proof system.
(b) $B(R)$ holds whenever $R$ p-simulates (even just simulates) every Cook--Reckhow system, i.e.\ $R$ is optimal. Existence of optimal systems is open (follows from NE$=$coNE).
(c) For $R=$ Extended Frege, $B(R)$ holds if the correctness of $M$ is provable in PV/$S^1_2$; unconditional $B(\mathrm{EF})$ is OPEN.
\end{thm}
\emph{Proof of (a),(b).} (a) is immediate; (b): $R$ simulates $S_M$, so $\ell_R(F)\le\mathrm{poly}(\ell_{S_M}(F))=\mathrm{poly}(|F|)$. \qed

\noindent So the ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is a statement about $R$ simulating the unknown machine $M$ whose existence is the hypothesis; no fixed $R$ is known to satisfy it unconditionally. This identifies the bridge as the optimal-proof-system / EF-automatization-type problem, not a routine lemma. Candidate attack: show $\overline{W}_R$-bounded for $R=\mathrm{EF}$ conditional on SAT$\in$P \emph{without} provability of $M$'s correctness (OPEN; related to Pich--Santhanam 2023).
\begin{prop}[adaptive switching gives nothing; PROVED/EXACT]\label{p2}
For systems $R_1,\dots,R_k$ the tagged union $R$ has $\ell_R(F)\le\min_i\ell_{R_i}(F)+O(\log k)$ and $\ell_R\ge\min_i\ell_{R_i}$. Hence a hard family for ``adaptive'' reasoning must be hard for each $R_i$ simultaneously; this is no harder than hardness for a single stronger system.
\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[sharing/compression; KNOWN]\label{p3}
Tree-like Frege p-simulates DAG-like Frege (Krajíček); tree-like resolution is exponentially weaker than DAG-like resolution. DAG sharing matters for resolution/CP, not for Frege-strength $R$. Circuit compression of lines corresponds to Frege $\to$ Extended Frege.
\end{prop}
\section*{Hard families: where they stand (KNOWN)}
Exponential DAG-resolution bounds: Haken (pigeonhole), Urquhart (Tseitin), Ben-Sasson--Wigderson (random $k$-CNF, width--size). Polynomial calculus / Nullstellensatz: Tseitin and random formulas (Alekhnovich--Razborov; Buss et al.). Cutting planes: Pudl\'ak, Hrube\v s--Pudl\'ak. Frege and Extended Frege: no superpolynomial lower bounds known (best linear). Web check 2026-11-05: no new full Frege/EF lower bound found; Alekseev (ToC 22, 2026) is a lower bound for a system simulating EF but for a restricted measure (refutation-function size), and arXiv 2604.28172 is tree-like semantic Frege with bounded line size. Neither moves $\overline W_{\mathrm{EF}}$.
\section*{Time--space audit}
Space = peak live lines. Resolution time--space tradeoffs (Ben-Sasson--Nordstr\"om) are known; Theorem~\ref{t1} is unaffected by the space measure. Encoding overhead: each DAG node costs $O(\log|\text{DAG}|)$ pointer bits; negligible for the polynomial/superpolynomial dichotomy.
\section*{Red-team verdict}
The route as posed cannot reach P$\neq$NP without (i) an unconditional lower bound for a system strong enough to make $B(R)$ true and (ii) $B(R)$ itself. Both are open and (ii) is not weaker than known open problems. Next targets: T-A, find the weakest natural $R$ (above resolution/CP) for which $B(R)$ is provable; T-B, test $B(R)$ for $R$ = $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$/PC against SAT$\in$P hypotheses on restricted $M$ (e.g.\ $M$ itself a resolution-style algorithm, where $B$ is true by definition).
