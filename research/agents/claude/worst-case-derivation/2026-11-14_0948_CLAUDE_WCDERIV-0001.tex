\section*{Shared target}
For a fixed sound and complete refutation system $R$ (multisign DAG
hypergraph reductions are one instance) let
\[
\W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\\|\langle F\rangle|\le N}}
\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi).
\]
Bridge$_R$: $\SAT\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\W_R(N)\le\poly(N)$.
LB$_R$: $\W_R(N)$ is superpolynomial. Then Bridge$_R\wedge$LB$_R\Rightarrow
\SAT\notin\mathrm P$ (PROVED/EXACT, by modus tollens).

\section{Free choice of the sequence is nondeterminism}
Choosing the optimal derivation for free (not charging the search) makes
$\min_\pi$ a nondeterministic certificate length. Hence
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} (Cook--Reckhow 1979): $\W_R\le\poly$ for
\emph{some} Cook--Reckhow system $R$ iff NP $=$ coNP. A superpolynomial
lower bound for every $R$ is therefore the NP $\ne$ coNP statement, which
is stronger than P $\ne$ NP. This confirms the user's remark that strategy
discovery need not be charged: the min is a legitimate lower-bound
quantity, but it is a coNP-proof-length quantity, so every lower bound on
it must be a proof-complexity lower bound.

\section{Bridge-collapse dichotomy}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT]\label{thm:collapse}
Fix $R$. If LB$_R$ holds, then Bridge$_R \iff \SAT\notin\mathrm P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If LB$_R$ holds, the consequent of Bridge$_R$ is false, so Bridge$_R$ is
equivalent to the negation of its antecedent.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[PROVED/EXACT given cited KNOWN THEOREMS]
For tree-like and DAG resolution, cutting planes, polynomial calculus,
bounded-depth Frege, and regular Res($\oplus$), unconditional
superpolynomial lower bounds are known (Table~\ref{tab:surv}; Efremenko--Garl\'ik--Itsykson
for regular Res($\oplus$)). For each of these systems, proving Bridge$_R$ is
\emph{exactly} as hard as proving P $\ne$ NP. The bridge is ``weaker''
than the target only for systems without a known superpolynomial lower bound
(currently DAG-like Res($\oplus$), AC$^0[p]$-Frege, Frege, Extended Frege
and stronger systems). For those, LB$_R$ is itself a long-standing open
problem.
\end{corollary}
\emph{Consequence for the five-role plan.} Builder and Lead must pick $R$
at least as strong as DAG Res($\oplus$). The Lower-Bound Lead's ``weakest
sufficient bridge'' cannot be proved separately for any system where the
Hypergraph role can already prove LB$_R$, unless it proves P $\ne$ NP
itself.

\section{What a bridge proof must use}
\begin{proposition}[PROVED/EXACT]\label{prop:global}
No bridge argument may use only ``the hard family is decidable in polynomial
time''. The family $\{\neg\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n\}$ (all UNSAT, so decided by a
constant-time algorithm on the promise) has exponential
resolution complexity (Haken 1985). Tseitin formulas on bounded-degree
expanders are decided in polynomial time without any promise (Gaussian
elimination over $\mathbb F_2$) and have exponential resolution complexity
(Urquhart 1987; Ben-Sasson--Wigderson 2001).
\end{proposition}
So a valid bridge must use that the algorithm is correct on \emph{all} CNFs.
The corpus already contains the strongest version of this mechanism: the
solver-clock system $Q_A$ and reflection formulas
(\texttt{research/agents/codex/2026-11-11\_1331\_TRANSFER\_RECOVERY\_AND\_RATE\_AMPLIFICATION.tex},
LSPV-1/336, /361) and the Pich--Santhanam witnessing transfer. In that
language, Bridge$_R$ for polynomial cost is equivalent to: whenever
$\SAT\in\mathrm P$, $R$ polynomially simulates $Q_A$ on UNSAT inputs for
some correct polynomial-time $A$ (PROVED/EXACT, since $Q_A$ is then
p-bounded). For $R=$ EF, Cook's reflection characterization reduces this to
short EF proofs of the soundness of $Q_A$; OPEN. The literature check of
this run found no theorem that P $=$ NP implies EF is polynomially bounded;
P $=$ NP is known to imply automatability of every system, a different
property.
