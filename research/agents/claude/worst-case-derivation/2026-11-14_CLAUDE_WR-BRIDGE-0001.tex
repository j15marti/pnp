\section{Target}
For a fixed sound and complete refutation system $R$ with polynomial-time proof checking,
\[
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi),
\]
where $\operatorname{cost}_R$ is any measure polynomially related to $|\pi|$ (size of the derivation DAG,
counting shared nodes once). The derivation $\pi$ is chosen freely (no search cost); this is legitimate because
$R$ is fixed and the minimum is over \emph{derivations}, not over algorithms per length (the per-length
lookup-table objection of the TMR directive does not apply). Bridge target:
\[
(\mathrm{B}_R)\qquad \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\ \Longrightarrow\ \overline W_R(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N).
\]
Lower-bound target: $(\mathrm L_R)$ $\overline W_R(N)\ne N^{O(1)}$. Trivially $\mathrm B_R\wedge\mathrm L_R\Rightarrow \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.

\section{Results of this run}
\begin{proposition}[Weak-system collapse, PROVED/EXACT]
If $R$ is a system for which $\mathrm L_R$ is a known theorem (resolution, tree-like resolution, cutting planes,
polynomial calculus, bounded-depth Frege, $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege on suitable families), then
$\mathrm B_R\iff\mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}($\Rightarrow$) $\mathrm B_R$ and the known $\mathrm L_R$ give $\mathrm{SAT}\notin\mathrm P$.
($\Leftarrow$) If $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$ the hypothesis of $\mathrm B_R$ is false, so $\mathrm B_R$ holds vacuously.\end{proof}
So for every system with a known superpolynomial lower bound the ``weakest sufficient bridge'' is a
\emph{reformulation} of P$\ne$NP (World-Ledger category: equivalent), not a weaker step. It must not be
recorded as progress.

\begin{proposition}[Exact reduction of $\mathrm B_R$ to reflection, PROVED/EXACT modulo cited KNOWN THEOREMS]
For a polynomial-time machine $A$ define the proof system $P_A$: the empty string is a $P_A$-refutation of
$F$ iff $A(F)=\mathrm{UNSAT}$. If $A$ decides SAT, $P_A$ is sound and polynomially bounded. Hence
$\mathrm B_R$ holds iff for every correct polynomial-time SAT decider $A$ (if one exists), $R$ polynomially
simulates $P_A$ on 3-CNF refutations. For $R$ extending (circuit/extended) Frege, $R$ p-simulates $P_A$ iff $R$ has
polynomial-size proofs of the propositional reflection/soundness formulas of $P_A$, i.e.\ of
$\|A(F)=\mathrm{UNSAT}\rightarrow \neg\mathrm{Sat}(F,x)\|_n$ (KNOWN THEOREM: Krajíček--Pudlák 1989/1990;
Pudlák 2020, Cor.~3.2 and Thm.~4.3 for extensions of CF).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Provably-correct bridge for EF, KNOWN THEOREM transfer]
If the soundness of $A$ (``$A(F)=\mathrm{UNSAT}\Rightarrow F$ unsatisfiable'') is provable in Cook's theory PV
(equivalently, for this purpose, $S^1_2$), then Extended Frege has polynomial-size refutations of all UNSAT
CNFs: $\overline W_{\mathrm{EF}}(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N)$. (Cook 1975; Krajíček--Pudlák 1990; Pudlák 2020,
Thm.~4.1: EF is the strongest system whose soundness is provable in $S^1_2$.)
\end{proposition}
\noindent\textbf{Consequence (the tension).} With $R=\mathrm{EF}$:
an EF lower bound $\mathrm L_{\mathrm{EF}}$ (OPEN, a major open problem) would unconditionally yield only
``no polynomial-time SAT algorithm has PV-provable soundness.'' The residual gap to P$\ne$NP is exactly the case of a
correct polynomial-time decider whose soundness is \emph{not} PV-provable. Thus
\[
\mathrm B_{\mathrm{EF}}\ \Longleftarrow\ \text{(every poly-time SAT decider, if any, has PV-provable soundness)}\qquad\text{[OPEN]}.
\]
Weak systems: bridge = full P$\ne$NP. Strong systems (EF, Frege): bridge reduces to a provability/reflection
statement, but the lower bound is itself open. No fixed $R$ currently known has both halves tractable.
This is the load-bearing barrier for the directive; status \textbf{OPEN}.
