\section{Target and fixed system}
The shared target is
\[\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\,|\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi).\]
Each distinct derived node of a shared DAG costs one unit. The optimal derivation is chosen freely. Depth, proof search and space are not charged.

$R^-=\{\mathrm{RES},\mathrm{AND3},\mathrm{XOR},\mathrm{LOCAL\text{-}CONVERT}\}$ is the definition-free fragment of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. Its rules are exactly as written in \texttt{research/agents/lspv-2/2026-11-02\_1216\_LSPV-2\_MIXED\_TSEITIN\_MIN\_DAG341.tex}, without DEF, as declared in WCDC-001 (branch \texttt{ccr-1695bd63-0nas44}). The local supports are at most 3, as in that text.

$\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$ is the system defined in TR26-018, \S1, quoted from the primary text read this run:
\begin{itemize}
\item a line is a linear clause, that is, a disjunction of $\mathbb F_2$-linear equations;
\item the resolution rule derives $C\vee D$ from $C\vee(f=0)$ and $D\vee(f=1)$, for any linear form $f$;
\item semantic weakening derives $D$ from $C$ whenever $C\models D$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Results}
\begin{lemma}[linear simulation]\label{lem:sim}
Let $\pi$ be an $R^-$ refutation of a CNF $F$ with $W$ derived nodes. Then $F$ has a $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$ refutation with at most $|F|+K W$ lines, where $K\le 120$. The per-rule costs are: RES 1, XOR 2, AND3 at most 23, LOCAL-CONVERT at most 120.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Represent each $R^-$ object by Res($\oplus$) lines:
\begin{itemize}
\item a clause by itself;
\item an equation $f=b$ by the one-equation line $(f=b)$;
\item a factor on $V$ with $|V|\le3$ by the full-width maxterm CNF of its truth table, which has at most 8 lines.
\end{itemize}
Translate each rule as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{RES.} This is the Res($\oplus$) resolution rule with $f=x$, so it takes 1 line.
\item \emph{XOR.} The premises are $(f=a)$ and $(g=b)$. Weaken $(g=b)$ to $(f=a{+}1)\vee(f{+}g=a{+}b)$; this is sound because $g=b$ together with $f=a$ gives $f+g=a+b$. Then resolve this line with $(f=a)$ on $f$. The result is $(f{+}g=a{+}b)$ in 2 lines. A row of $0=1$ is the empty line.
\item \emph{AND3.} The maxterms of $f_1\wedge f_2$ are exactly the union of the maxterms of $f_1$ and of $f_2$ over $V$. Each one is a weakening of a premise line, which gives at most 8 lines. An optional GF(2) row annotation is a further line implied by those maxterms.
\item \emph{LOCAL-CONVERT.} The conclusion is a clause, a row, or a condition on at most 3 variables, given by at most 8 maxterms. It is implied by the premise's maxterms.
\end{itemize}
A line $T$ implied by a maxterm CNF $S$ on $\le3$ variables is derived by a decision tree. The tree branches on the variables outside $T$. At each leaf $\rho$, the line $T\vee\neg\rho$ is a weakening of a premise, or a tautology weakened from any premise. Resolving upward yields $T$.

The script \texttt{resxor/rminus\_resxor\_transfer.py} builds every such derivation for all 256 Boolean functions on 3 variables. That covers all 2113 implied clauses and rows, and each step is checked by the Res($\oplus$) rule checker. The maximum is 7 lines for an implied clause and 15 for an implied row. The XOR translation is checked on all 1032 instances with $n\le4$; the general proof is the two-line argument above. Axioms of $F$ are added once. The root is the empty line.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[top-down size]\label{lem:td}
A bottom-up Res($\oplus$) refutation with $s$ lines yields a parity decision DAG, the top-down model of TR26-018 \S1.5, with at most $s$ nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Label the node of line $C$ by the linear system $\neg C$, and contract weakening chains. Semantic implication composes, so the result is still valid.
\begin{itemize}
\item A resolution step on $f$ becomes a query of $f$. The children are valid because $\neg C\wedge\neg D\wedge(f=\alpha)$ implies the negation of the matching premise.
\item A line obtained by weakening an axiom $A$ becomes a sink, because $\neg D\models\neg A$.
\item The empty line becomes the source, labelled ``true''.
\end{itemize}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[unconditional quadratic bound for unrestricted $R^-$]\label{thm:quad}
Let $G$ range over an explicit family of $d$-regular graphs on $h$ vertices with constant $d$ and $e(G)\ge ch$. Here
\[e(G)=\min_{h/3\le|U|\le2h/3}|\delta(U)|,\]
and $T_G$ is odd-charge Tseitin. Let $F_G=T_G\circ\mathrm{MAJ}_3$, a $2d$-CNF with $2^{d-1}3^d h$ clauses. Then
\[W_{R^-}(F_G)\ \ge\ \frac{e(G)(e(G)+1)/2-|F_G|}{120}=\Omega(h^2).\]
Hence $\overline W_{R^-}(N)\ge\Omega(N^2/\log^2N)$ over CNFs of constant width.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathrm{MAJ}_3$ is 1-stifling, which is checked exhaustively.
\item By Ben-Sasson--Wigderson, every resolution refutation of $T_G$ has width at least $e(G)$.
\item By TR26-018 Thm.~1.6, every Res($\oplus$) refutation of $F_G$ therefore has size at least $e(G)(e(G){+}1)/2$. This bound is quoted from the primary text.
\item Apply Lemmas~\ref{lem:sim} and~\ref{lem:td}.
\end{itemize}
For encoded length: $|F_G|=\Theta(h)$ and $N=\Theta(h\log h)$ bits.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Calibration.} For cubic graphs, $|F_G|=108h$, so the bound is positive only when $h>216/c^2$. With $c\approx0.1$ (random cubic bisection), that means $h>21600$. On the five executed graphs (K4, K33, prism, cube, Petersen) the numeric bound is \emph{vacuous} (0), and the raw JSON records this.

The controls all agree with the construction:
\begin{itemize}
\item the lifted odd K4 formula has 0 models;
\item the lifted even K4 formula has $32768=8\cdot4^6$ models, the SAT control;
\item unlifted odd Tseitin has the linear optimum $W_{R^-}=4h-1$ (LSPV-2/341), so the lift is essential.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Failed transfers.} The depth-capped Res($\oplus$) results do not lower-bound $\overline W$:
\begin{itemize}
\item TR26-018 Thm.~1.4 and Cor.~1.5, depth $o(n^2/\log^4 n)$;
\item Bhattacharya--Chattopadhyay, arXiv:2507.23008, depth $N^{2-\varepsilon}$;
\item Byramji--Impagliazzo, $\exp(\Omega(n^3/D^2))$ at depth $D$.
\end{itemize}
In the supercritical cases the formulas have polynomial-size \emph{resolution} refutations. The free choice of the optimal derivation then makes $\overline W$ polynomial on them. These bounds would matter only if a cost model charged depth or span, and the present directive does not. The classification is FAILED TRANSFER.

\paragraph{What this does not give.}
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] \emph{A 3-CNF family.} Splitting the width-6 clauses adds extension variables, and Res($\oplus$) size preservation under splitting is not proved here. This is the new target RX-T1, which is OPEN.
\item[(ii)] \emph{A superpolynomial bound.} The strongest known unrestricted DAG Res($\oplus$) size bound is quadratic, and superpolynomial DAG Res($\oplus$) is OPEN (TR26-018 \S1). The upper bound for $W_{R^-}(F_G)$ is also unknown. Resolution is exponential on $F_G$ by lifted width, and $R^-$ cannot build the 9-variable lifted parities without DEF. So $W_{R^-}(F_G)\in[\Omega(h^2),2^{O(h)}]$, which is CANDIDATE hard and OPEN.
\item[(iii)] \emph{DEF.} With DEF, $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ contains Extended Resolution (\texttt{c6iury} TIER-ER-0001), and the EF barrier (log item 9) applies.
\end{itemize}
