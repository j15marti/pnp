\section*{Shared objective, exact form}
For a fixed sound, complete, polynomial-time-checkable refutation system $R$
(Cook--Reckhow sense), and explicit binary CNF encodings,
\[
 w_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi),\qquad
 \Wbar_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N} w_R(F).
\]
$\operatorname{LB}(R)$ denotes ``$\Wbar_R$ is not polynomially bounded'' and
$\operatorname{Bridge}(R)$ denotes
$\SAT\in\mathsf P\Rightarrow\Wbar_R\le\operatorname{poly}$.
$\operatorname{Bridge}(R)\wedge\operatorname{LB}(R)\Rightarrow\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$.

The user's principle --- minimising over all valid reduction sequences, with
free knowledge of the best sequence, yields a legitimate lower bound --- is
correct \emph{for executions that are $R$-derivations}. Theorem~1 states
exactly what it proves; Theorem~2 states what it cannot prove alone.

\section{Role 1 (General SAT Lower-Bound Lead): what the free-sequence bound proves}
\begin{theorem}[Trace-faithful transfer; PROVED/EXACT, elementary]
Call a decider $A$ \emph{$R$-trace-faithful with overhead $p$} if there is a map
sending each rejecting run of $A$ on UNSAT $F$ to an $R$-refutation $\pi$ of
$F$ with $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\le p(\operatorname{time}_A(F))$, $p$
increasing. Then $\operatorname{time}_A(F)\ge p^{-1}(w_R(F))$ for every UNSAT
$F$, and hence $T_A(N)\ge p^{-1}(\Wbar_R(N))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$w_R(F)$ is a minimum over all refutations, so it is at most the cost of the
one extracted from $A$'s run. No search cost is charged; that only weakens the
bound, which is exactly the user's clarification.
\end{proof}
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM instantiation.} DPLL runs are tree-like Resolution
refutations (linear overhead). CDCL runs yield general Resolution refutations
(Beame--Kautz--Sabharwal 2004). With unlimited restarts CDCL polynomially
simulates Resolution (Pipatsrisawat--Darwiche 2011; Atserias--Fichte--Thurley
2011). With Haken's 1985 pigeonhole bound, every DPLL/CDCL solver therefore has
exponential worst-case time. This is the full scope of the free-sequence
method: \emph{a lower bound on the class of $R$-trace-faithful algorithms}.
An arbitrary polynomial-time machine is not known to be trace-faithful to any
fixed $R$; that is the transfer gap.

\section{Role 5 (Red Team): the bridge collapses for every system we can lower-bound}
\begin{theorem}[Bridge collapse; PROVED/EXACT, elementary]
If $\operatorname{LB}(R)$ is an unconditional theorem, then
$\operatorname{Bridge}(R)\iff\SAT\notin\mathsf P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
($\Rightarrow$) If $\SAT\in\mathsf P$, the bridge gives
$\Wbar_R\le\operatorname{poly}$, which contradicts $\operatorname{LB}(R)$.
($\Leftarrow$) If $\SAT\notin\mathsf P$, the implication is vacuously true.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Monotonicity; PROVED/EXACT]
If $R'$ polynomially simulates $R$, then
$\operatorname{Bridge}(R)\Rightarrow\operatorname{Bridge}(R')$ and
$\operatorname{LB}(R')\Rightarrow\operatorname{LB}(R)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$w_{R'}(F)\le q(|F|+w_R(F))$ for a polynomial $q$, so $\Wbar_{R'}\le q(N+\Wbar_R)$.
\end{proof}

\textbf{Consequence for the directive.} Its first priority (a superpolynomial
$\Wbar_R$ lower bound for a fixed $R$) and its third priority (the weakest
bridge) pull in opposite directions along the simulation order. For
Resolution, Cutting Planes (Pudlák 1997), bounded-depth Frege
(Ajtai; Krajíček--Pudlák--Woods; Pitassi--Beame--Impagliazzo) and Polynomial
Calculus, $\operatorname{LB}(R)$ is known, so by Theorem~2 the boxed bridge
\emph{is} $\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$ restated, with no weakening. A bridge that
is strictly weaker than the separation can exist only for systems
\emph{beyond the current lower-bound frontier}: $\mathrm{AC}^0[p]$-Frege,
$\mathrm{TC}^0$-Frege, Frege, Extended Frege, and unrestricted
$\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$. For $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$ the fresh literature check
found exponential bounds only under depth restrictions
(Alekseev--Itsykson STOC 2025, depth $\le n\log\log n$; Efremenko--Itsykson
2025; Bhattacharya--Chattopadhyay 2025, depth $O(N^{2-\epsilon})$;
Itsykson--Knop ITCS 2026; Byramji--Impagliazzo 2025). The unrestricted
$\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$ size bound remains open in those sources.
Classification: the directive's two boxed targets are an
\textbf{equivalent reformulation} of $\mathsf P\neq\mathsf{NP}$ for every
currently lower-bounded $R$, in the sense of the world ledger
(\path{research/agents/claude/world-ledger/}).

\section{Role 2 (SAT-Circuit Reduction Builder): the only known bridge mechanism}
Every polynomial-time $A$ induces the Cook--Reckhow system $S_A$: the proof is
empty and the verifier runs $A$. So $\operatorname{Bridge}(R)$ follows from
\emph{``$R$ polynomially simulates $S_A$ for some correct polynomial-time $A$''},
which is equivalent to $R$ having polynomial-size refutations of $A$'s
reflection formulas. This is the corpus statement (29) of
\path{research/agents/codex/2026-11-11_1331_TRANSFER_RECOVERY_AND_RATE_AMPLIFICATION.tex}
(LSPV-1/361), \textbf{adopted, not new}. For $R=\mathrm{EF}$, the classical
Cook (1975) PV--EF correspondence gives a sufficient condition: if A's
soundness on UNSAT inputs is provable in PV, then EF polynomially simulates $S_A$.
The literature search found no unconditional theorem
$\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}\Rightarrow\mathrm{EF}$ polynomially bounded. This run
treats that implication as \textbf{OPEN}. The Pich--Santhanam 2023 transfer
already in the corpus is the sharpest published narrowing.

reduced logical state'' is the input itself. MUS size carries no
superpolynomial content. (ii) The tree-like minimum grows by a factor of about
4 per step for PHP and 2.7 for Tseitin. Fully charged Gaussian elimination
