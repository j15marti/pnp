\section*{WCDC-0001: audit of the worst-case derivation bridge}
\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.} No lower bound and no transfer theorem is claimed.

Let $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT},|F|\le N}\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\mathrm{cost}_R(\pi)$, $R$ a sound system with polynomial-time checkable derivations.

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] Let $R$ have a known unconditional superpolynomial lower bound $\overline W_R(N)\ge N^{\omega(1)}$ (resolution, cutting planes, bounded-depth Frege). Then ``$\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow \overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$'' is equivalent to $\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} The conclusion is false, so the implication holds iff its hypothesis is false. $\square$
Hence the bridge is a weaker sufficient condition only for systems $R$ whose lower bounds are unknown.

\begin{prop}[KNOWN THEOREM, Cook--Reckhow] Some sound $R$ has $\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$ iff $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$. Since the minimum over $\pi$ is free, $\overline W_R$ is a nondeterministic measure; a superpolynomial bound for a fixed $R$ is a proof-complexity statement and gives $\mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$ only together with the bridge.\end{prop}

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT, circularity] Let $A$ be a deterministic decider for UNSAT in time $N^k$ and $R_A$ the system whose derivations are accepting runs of $A$. Then $\overline W_{R_A}\le N^k$, but $R_A$ is sound only if $A$ is correct, and a lower bound for $\overline W_{R_A}$ (over all $A$) is exactly $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.\end{prop}

\textbf{Weakest non-circular hypothesis (B*), CANDIDATE.} $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ implies there is a decider $A$ and a polynomial $p$ such that for every UNSAT $F$ the statement ``$A(F)$ rejects'' has an $R$-derivation of cost $\le p(|F|)$. This is the provable-correctness / optimal-proof-system question; as of the 2026 literature no set outside NP is known to have an optimal proof system (arXiv 2602.02294), and Pich--Santhanam show strong co-nondeterministic lower bounds for NP are not provable in feasible theories under standard assumptions (to be re-verified). Failed transfer: counting gives nothing, since proofs and UNSAT formulas both number $2^{\mathrm{poly}(N)}$.
