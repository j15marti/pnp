## Candidate and falsifiers

Candidate (doc, Sections 2.2 and 4): the failure of IB and the roABP-IPS lower bounds of IIB come from one object, "the multilinear evaluation matrix of an unsatisfiable or #P-hard constraint system across variable bipartitions exhibits full rank 2^Omega(n)", and exact contraction forces "volume-law MPS" bond dimension chi = 2^Omega(n) on hard 3-SAT.

Falsifiers set before computing: (F1) an UNSAT or unique-SAT 3-CNF whose solution-indicator matricization has rank 0 or 1 on every split; (F2) the actual IPS_LIN' object 1/f failing to be full rank on some split; (F3) a primary-source misstatement in a theorem the doc uses as load-bearing.

## Results

**R1, PROVED/EXACT (elementary), independent review OPEN. Falsifies the doc's IB/Section 4 rank identification.** For any CNF phi on n variables and any split (A,B), M_phi[a,b] = phi(a,b) has rank over Q at most #SAT(phi): its nonzero entries are the solutions, so it has at most #SAT nonzero rows. UNSAT gives the zero matrix, a unique solution gives rank 1. The volume-law claim is false on the solution-indicator MPS for exactly the Z=0 versus Z=1 instances the doc itself says matter. Any exponential cost of exact contraction sits in intermediate messages of a chosen network and order. Their size is 2^(frontier) (Markov–Shi: exp(O(tw)) upper bound, order-dependent), and this is a property of the scheme, not an instance-intrinsic rank lower bound. This matches the repo's existing unique-solution rank-one control and the rule against unrestricted inference from static rank.

COMPUTATIONAL confirmation: seed 20261120, n=14, 4 random 3-CNFs at each of alpha = 3.0, 4.26, 5.5, with 3 balanced splits each. All 36 mod-(2^31-1) ranks are at most #SAT (a mod-p rank is a lower bound on the Q rank, and #SAT is an upper bound). All 5 UNSAT draws give rank 0. The largest rank was 12 versus a full rank of 128 (instance with 72 solutions); at alpha=4.26 the ranks were 1, 2, 3, 0.

**R2, KNOWN THEOREM, re-derived and checked. It locates the real full-rank object.** For f = 1 + sum_i 2^(i-1) x_i, which has no Boolean root, the functional multiplier g = 1/f has the evaluation matrix M[a,b] = 1/(x_a + y_b) on EVERY split (A,B), with x_a = 1 + val_A(a) and y_b = val_B(b). Binary weights make the subset sums distinct within each side, so this is a Cauchy matrix with nonzero denominators and is nonsingular: full rank 2^min(|A|,|B|). Exact Fraction elimination confirms full rank for n = 6, 8, 10 over 9 splits. This is the Forbes–Shpilka–Tzameret–Wigderson functional mechanism for roABP-IPS_LIN' (any variable order). It concerns 1/f for a single rootless axiom, not a CNF solution indicator. As the doc itself notes, for a CNF the multipliers g_j can cancel against each other, so R2 does not transfer to 3-CNF refutations.

**R3, source corrections (F3), CONDITIONAL on my reading of the primary sources; recheck before citing.**
- Doc §2.3 / Attempt IC says Bürgisser's VP=VNP over a finite field gives P=NP "unconditionally". The theorem gives the NONUNIFORM collapse NC/poly = P/poly = #P/poly (over C under GRH). That yields NP ⊆ P/poly and a PH collapse via Karp–Lipton, not uniform P=NP. The title implication "NP=coNP and VP=VNP ⇒ P=NP" is not a known theorem.
- Doc §2.3 says PIT ∈ P implies lower bounds for "NEXP ∩ coNEXP or VNP". Kabanets–Impagliazzo give NEXP ⊄ P/poly or Perm ∉ VP (arithmetic).
- The IIC figures ((3+1/86)n affine dispersers; the 2n+O(n/loglog n) sharp magnification threshold; the locality barrier) agree with Find–Golovnev–Hirsch–Kulikov and Chen–Hirahara–Oliveira–Pich–Rajgopal–Santhanam. The "2n−2 probabilistic lower bound for an O(n)-sparse language in P" was NOT checked against its source.
