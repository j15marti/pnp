\section{Target and model}
Fix a Cook--Reckhow refutation system $R$ (sound, complete, polynomial-time
checkable), chosen \emph{before} any instance. For UNSAT $F$ let
$W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)$, with cost the number of
distinct derived DAG nodes (or encoded bit length, stated where used), and
\[
\Wb_R(N)=\max_{F\in\UNSAT,\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N} W_R(F).
\]
This is exactly the governing directive
\texttt{research/coordination/2026-11-02\_1204\_WORST\_CASE\_MINIMUM\_DERIVATION\_FIVE\_ROLE\_REFOCUS.md}
and the user's 2026-11-12 restatement. B3$(R)$ denotes
$\SAT\in\mathsf P\Rightarrow \Wb_R(N)\le\poly(N)$; LB$(R)$ denotes
``$\Wb_R$ is superpolynomial''.

\paragraph{Multisign hypergraph reading.} Encode $F$ as the signed 3-uniform
hypergraph (vertices = variables, hyperedges = clauses with a sign per
incidence); a reduction step is an $R$-inference adding a derived signed
hyperedge (or extension vertex) to a DAG. For UNSAT $F$ and any sound complete
$R$, the \emph{minimal reduced logical state} is the empty hyperedge $\bot$; so
the ``minimal state'' half of the objective carries no information and the
whole content is the ``minimal work'' $W_R(F)$. For SAT $F$ the worst case is
dominated by checking a witness ($O(|\langle F\rangle|)$), so the max over all
inputs equals the UNSAT max up to an additive linear term. (PROVED/EXACT, trivial.)

\section{Lead: what the free-sequence principle does and does not give}

\begin{proposition}[Free-sequence principle; PROVED/EXACT]\label{prop:free}
Let $A$ be any algorithm (even one given the optimal derivation by an oracle)
that on every UNSAT input \emph{writes out} an $R$-refutation. Then
$\mathrm{time}_A(F)\ge L_R(F)\ge W_R(F)$, where $L_R$ is minimum bit length,
so $T^{wc}_A(N)\ge\Wb_R(N)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}A machine writes $O(1)$ bits per step; the output is some
refutation, whose length is at least the minimum. \end{proof}

This confirms the user's clarification: not charging the search for the best
sequence only weakens the lower bound, so the bound is legitimate. It applies,
however, only to algorithms whose computation \emph{is} (or outputs) an
$R$-derivation. An arbitrary SAT decider outputs one bit. Its run is a proof
in its \emph{own} clocked system $Q_A$ (LSPV-1/336), not in $R$. The bridge
exists to close exactly this gap.

\begin{proposition}[Simulation-order sandwich; PROVED/EXACT]\label{prop:sandwich}
Write $R\le R'$ if $R'$ length-simulates $R$
($L_{R'}(F)\le p(L_R(F)+|F|)$). Then
(i) $R\le R'$ and B3$(R)$ imply B3$(R')$; (ii) $R\le R'$ and LB$(R')$ imply LB$(R)$;
(iii) B3$(R)\wedge$LB$(R)\Rightarrow \SAT\notin\mathsf P$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}(i),(ii): compose the simulation polynomial with $\Wb$.
(iii): contraposition. \end{proof}

So B3 is upward closed and LB is downward closed in the simulation order; the
program needs one system in the intersection. Current ledger
(KNOWN THEOREM unless marked):
\begin{itemize}
\item LB proved: tree-like and general resolution (Haken 1985; Urquhart 1987;
Chv\'atal--Szemer\'edi 1988; Ben-Sasson--Wigderson 2001), polynomial calculus
(Razborov 1998; Alekhnovich--Razborov), cutting planes (Pudl\'ak 1997 via
interpolation; random $O(\log n)$-CNF and random $k$-CNF results of
Fleming--Pankratov--Pitassi--Robere and Hrube\v{s}--Pudl\'ak, 2017),
bounded-depth Frege (Ajtai; Pitassi--Beame--Impagliazzo; Kraj\'\i\v{c}ek--Pudl\'ak--Woods),
regular and bounded-depth $\Res(\oplus)$ (Efremenko--Garlik--Itsykson, STOC 2024;
subsequent 2025 depth extensions by Efremenko--Itsykson, Alekseev--Itsykson,
Bhattacharya--Chattopadhyay, Byramji--Impagliazzo).
\item LB OPEN: general $\Res(\oplus)$, $AC^0[p]$-Frege, Frege, $\EF$.
\item B3 has a known \emph{conditional} route only at $\EF$ and above:
if some polynomial-time SAT decider is provably correct in PV (or $S^1_2$),
Cook's translation gives polynomial $\EF$ refutations of all UNSAT
(Cook 1975; Cook--Kraj\'\i\v{c}ek 2007; already in corpus as LSPV-1/357).
Whether $\SAT\in\mathsf P$ implies PV-provable correctness of some decider is OPEN.
\end{itemize}
No system is currently known to lie in both regions.

\begin{proposition}[Vacuity audit of B3; PROVED/EXACT]\label{prop:vacuity}
(a) If LB$(R)$ is a theorem, then B3$(R)$ is logically equivalent to
$\SAT\notin\mathsf P$. (b) $\exists R\,[\mathrm{B3}(R)\wedge\mathrm{LB}(R)]$ is
equivalent to $\SAT\notin\mathsf P$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}(a) B3$(R)\equiv[\SAT\notin\mathsf P]\vee\neg$LB$(R)$ and the second
disjunct is false. (b) ($\Rightarrow$) Prop.~\ref{prop:sandwich}(iii).
($\Leftarrow$) If $\SAT\notin\mathsf P$, take $R=\Res$: B3 holds vacuously and LB
is Haken's theorem. \end{proof}

\noindent\emph{Consequence.} The B3 route is a \emph{reformulation} of
$\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$ (world-ledger category ``equivalent'') unless B3$(R)$ is
proved by an argument that never uses the falsity of its hypothesis --- i.e.\
by a uniform construction turning a polynomial-time decider $A$ into short
$R$-proofs. That is precisely a B1/B2-type translation or a reflection
theorem. Part (a) is recorded in the corpus for systems containing resolution;
part (b) and the sandwich formulation are the consolidation added here.

\section{Red team: falsifications at the correct quantifiers}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{POINTWISE-TRANSFER FAILURE (B1 for $\Res$)}, KNOWN: Tseitin
formulas on expanders need $2^{\Omega(n)}$ resolution (Urquhart; BSW) yet
Gaussian elimination refutes them with $|V|-1$ row additions. Exact small
evidence in \S4. This does not refute B2/B3 (the corpus already notes this).
\item \textbf{FAMILY FAILURE under combination}, KNOWN: PHP is short in
cutting planes (Cook--Coullard--Tur\'an 1987); Tseitin is short with an
$\mathbb F_2$ rule. Any fixed combined $R$ containing CP and parity rules
kills both families. Random 3-CNF at large constant density survives Res, PC
and (for wider clauses) CP; its status in general $\Res(\oplus)$ and in
Res+CP+parity combinations is OPEN.
\item \textbf{Free-sequence overreach}: Prop.~\ref{prop:free} gives
$T^{wc}_A\ge\Wb_R$ only for $R$-producing $A$. Reading it as a bound on all
algorithms silently assumes B1. REJECTED as a bridge.
\item \textbf{Dichotomy pressure}: by Prop.~\ref{prop:sandwich}, raising $R$ to
make B3 plausible (towards $\EF$) moves LB into territory where no
superpolynomial bound is known for any family; lowering $R$ to obtain LB makes
B3 equivalent to the goal (Prop.~\ref{prop:vacuity}a). The candidate
mixed calculus $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ contains RES+VARDEF $=\mathrm{ER}_2$
(LSPV-1/357), so it is $\EF$-strength: B3 is conditionally reachable, LB is
as hard as $\EF$ lower bounds.
\end{itemize}

\section{Resource Auditor}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cost conventions: node count $W_R\le$ bit length $L_R\le W_R\cdot O(n\log n)$
for clause systems; Prop.~\ref{prop:free} needs $L_R$, and the gap is a
polynomial factor, harmless for superpolynomial targets. $N=|\langle F\rangle|
=\Theta(m\log n)$, not $n$; all table rows report $n,m$, and polynomial claims
must be restated in $N$.
\item Space: clause-space lower bounds (Esteban--Tor\'an; Atserias--Dalmau
width $\le$ space $+O(1)$) bound \emph{$R$-producing} algorithms only, the same
limitation as Prop.~\ref{prop:free}. They give no link to the ARC/FLMV
time--space box; the known SAT time--space bounds (Williams; Buss--Williams
barrier, world-ledger L13) are about deciders, not proofs. No transfer claimed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Barrier-transfer and post-quantum checks}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Logic / bounded arithmetic}: directly transferable theorem
(Cook 1975; Cook--Kraj\'\i\v{c}ek 2007): PV-provable $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$
$\Leftrightarrow$ PV-definable algorithm giving a satisfying assignment or an
$\EF$ refutation. Missing theorem: $\SAT\in\mathsf P\Rightarrow$ PV proves
correctness of some decider. Pich--Santhanam (2023) give a converse-direction
link (EF lower bounds towards $\mathsf P\ne\mathsf{NP}$ under hypotheses); OPEN as a bridge.
\item \textbf{Coding theory / PQ ledger item 1--2} (Schur squares, quadratic
relations): exact object transfer for Tseitin: the $\mathbb F_2$ parity structure
is \emph{exploitable by an algorithm}, which is exactly why the family fails in
combined systems. Classification: EXACT OBJECT TRANSFER, negative for hardness.
\item \textbf{Optimization (cutting planes / integrality)}: PHP's integrality gap
is closed by CP rounding; FAILED TRANSFER as a hardness source.
\item \textbf{Electronics/control}: no model-matched transfer identified;
misleading analogies avoided.
\end{itemize}
