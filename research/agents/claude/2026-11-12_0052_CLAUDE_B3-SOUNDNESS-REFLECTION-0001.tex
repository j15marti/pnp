\section*{Shared objective and which bridge is addressed}
For a fixed sound complete calculus $R$ with unit-weight distinct DAG nodes,
\[
\Wbar_R(N)=\max_{F\in\UNSAT,\ |\langle F\rangle|\le N}\ \min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi).
\]
The inner minimum is taken over all derivations, so optimizer search cost is not
charged, as the directive allows. This report addresses only the
\textbf{primary bridge B3}:
\[
\mathrm{B3}(R):\qquad \SAT\in\mathrm P\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists C,k\ \forall N:\ \Wbar_R(N)\le C(N+1)^k .
\]

\section{Definitions}
Fix the ordinary binary CNF encoding of the corpus, in which malformed strings
encode no formula. Let $A$ be a total deterministic polynomial-time decider with
output $1$ for ``satisfiable'' and $0$ for ``unsatisfiable or malformed''. By
Cook--Levin unrolling there is a uniform circuit family $C_n$ of size $\poly(n)$
computing $A$ on length-$n$ inputs. Let $\mathrm{Def}_{C_n}(s,w)$ be the
Tseitin clauses defining the gate variables $w$ from the input bits
$s\in\{0,1\}^n$, and let $\mathrm{Sat}_n(s,t,u)$ be a polynomial-size CNF with
Tseitin auxiliaries $u$ saying that ``$s$ is well-formed and the assignment $t$
satisfies the CNF encoded by $s$''. The \emph{soundness CNF} is
\[
\Sigma_{A,n}(s,t,w,u)\;:=\;\mathrm{Def}_{C_n}(s,w)\ \wedge\ (\mathrm{out}(w)=0)\ \wedge\ \mathrm{Sat}_n(s,t,u).
\]
Its size is $\poly(n)$, and it is unsatisfiable \emph{iff} $A$ makes no false
UNSAT answer on inputs of length $n$. Write $\sigma_{A,n}:=\neg\Sigma_{A,n}$
(a tautology when $A$ is sound on length $n$).
Write $S_{\EF}(\tau)$ for the minimum EF proof size of $\tau$.

\paragraph{Assumption EQ (exact for CANON2R; REFEREE365).}
There are polynomials $p,q$ such that for every UNSAT CNF $G$ of encoded size $N$:
$W_R(G)\le p(N+S_{\EF}(\neg G))$ and $S_{\EF}(\neg G)\le q(N+W_R(G))$.

\section{Main theorem}
\begin{theorem}[B3 reduces to one soundness family]\label{thm:main}
Let $R$ satisfy EQ. The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\mathrm{B3}(R)$.
\item[(ii)] If $\SAT\in\mathrm P$, then \emph{some} correct polynomial-time decider $A$ has
$S_{\EF}(\sigma_{A,n})\le\poly(n)$.
\item[(iii)] If $\SAT\in\mathrm P$, then \emph{every} correct polynomial-time decider $A$
has $S_{\EF}(\sigma_{A,n})\le\poly_A(n)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(iii)$\Rightarrow$(ii).} Under $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ a correct polynomial-time
decider exists.

\emph{(ii)$\Rightarrow$(i).} Assume $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ and fix $A$ from (ii), with
EF proofs $\pi_n$ of $\sigma_{A,n}$ of size $\le r(n)$. Let $F$ be UNSAT with
$n=|\langle F\rangle|\le N$. (a) Substituting the constants $s:=\langle F\rangle$
into $\pi_n$ gives an EF proof of $\sigma_{A,n}[s:=\langle F\rangle]$ of size
$O(r(n)\cdot n)$, since Frege/EF is closed under substitution (KNOWN). (b) With
$s$ fixed, introduce $w$ by exactly the extension definitions
$\mathrm{Def}_{C_n}(\langle F\rangle,w)$. Gate-by-gate evaluation proves every
$w_g$ or $\neg w_g$, and in particular $\mathrm{out}(w)=0$, because $A(F)=0$
since $A$ is correct. This costs $O(|C_n|)$ lines. (c) Frege proves
$\mathrm{Sat}_n(\langle F\rangle,t,u)\leftrightarrow F(t)$ in size $\poly(n)$,
with $u$ handled by its definitions; this is structural unfolding of a
constant-substituted formula. (d) Modus ponens yields $\neg F(t)$. The total EF
size is $\poly(n)$ with a polynomial depending only on $A$. By EQ,
$W_R(F)\le\poly(N)$ uniformly, so $\Wbar_R(N)\le C(N+1)^k$.

\emph{(i)$\Rightarrow$(iii).} Assume $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ and $\mathrm{B3}(R)$, and let
$A$ be any correct polynomial-time decider. Then $\Sigma_{A,n}$ is an UNSAT CNF of
encoded size $M_n=\poly(n)$. Hence $W_R(\Sigma_{A,n})\le C(M_n+1)^k$, and by EQ
$S_{\EF}(\sigma_{A,n})\le q(M_n+C(M_n+1)^k)=\poly_A(n)$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What the theorem says.} At the EF level, B3 is not a statement
about all UNSAT formulas. It is equivalent to the claim that a \emph{single
explicit family}, the soundness CNFs of \emph{one} hypothetical
polynomial-time SAT algorithm, has polynomial EF refutations. Which algorithm
is chosen does not matter, by (ii)$\Leftrightarrow$(iii). This is the exact form,
at the EF level, of the ``single soundness family'' route that
LSPV-1/353 proposed for $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. There, the RT/SC (restriction and
transport) obligations were OPEN; here they are discharged by steps (a)--(c) and
EQ.

CONDITIONAL on node-to-bit normalization of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ into EF.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}

\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[sufficient condition; KNOWN THEOREM transfer]\label{prop:pv}
If $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ is witnessed by a PV-definable decider $A$ whose soundness
$\forall s,t\,(A(s)=0\to\neg\mathrm{Sat}(s,t))$ is provable in Cook's theory PV,
then (ii) holds, and therefore $\mathrm{B3}(R)$ holds for every $R$ satisfying the
first half of EQ, including $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Cook (1975): a PV proof of an open formula $\varphi(x)$ yields polynomial-size EF
proofs of its propositional translations $\|\varphi\|_n$. The translation of
the soundness formula is $\sigma_{A,n}$ up to a polynomially provable
equivalence. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:main} or Corollary~\ref{cor:mix}.
\end{proof}

\section{Firewalls and falsifier tests}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{FW1}] \textbf{(Circularity.)} In (ii), ``EF'' cannot be replaced by
``some proof system''. Under $\SAT\in\mathrm P$ the rejecting-trace system of $A$
is polynomially bounded, so that version is trivially true and fixes no
preselected $R$. This is the Cook--Reckhow existential point already recorded in
REFEREE334.
\item[\textbf{FW2}] \textbf{(The provability gap is exactly the missing step.)}
Define $\mathrm{GAP}$: $\SAT\in\mathrm P$, yet every correct polynomial-time $A$
has EF-superpolynomial $\sigma_{A,n}$. Then $\mathrm{B3}(R)\iff\neg\mathrm{GAP}$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:pv} excludes GAP only when correctness is PV-provable.
Whether a true $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ could be PV-unprovable \emph{in this
propositional-size sense} is OPEN. This is the load-bearing open arrow of B3 at
the EF level.
\item[\textbf{FW3}] \textbf{(B3 is not weaker for systems with known lower bounds.)}
If $R'$ has a proved superpolynomial $\Wbar_{R'}$ (Resolution by Haken; cutting
planes; polynomial calculus; bounded-depth Frege), then
$\mathrm{B3}(R')\iff\SAT\notin\mathrm P$. Contrapositive proof: if
$\SAT\in\mathrm P$, then B3 would give a polynomial bound, contradicting the
lower bound; conversely B3 is vacuous when $\SAT\notin\mathrm P$. Such a B3 is a
\emph{reformulation} of P~$\neq$~NP (World Ledger category), not progress.
B3 is also monotone upward under node-cost p-simulation. So the
lower-bound half and the bridge half of the program cannot both be ``easy'':
B3 is plausible only at or above the current lower-bound frontier. At the EF
level, the lower-bound half is the classical open EF lower-bound problem, which
is implied by NP~$\neq$~coNP.
\item[\textbf{FW4}] \textbf{(Red-team tool: decider invariance.)} A claimed B3
countermodel built from one unusual correct polynomial-time decider whose
soundness family is EF-hard \emph{already refutes} (iii), and hence B3, for
\emph{all} deciders. It cannot coexist with a different decider having short
EF soundness proofs. Any such claim must therefore establish GAP globally.
\end{enumerate}

bound $\Rightarrow\#\mathrm P\not\subseteq\mathrm{FP/poly}$. This is a different

theories}, arXiv:2007.14835: systems from EF upward prove their own reflection
principles efficiently. This is consistent with steps (a)--(d). It does not show

\textbf{Barrier-transfer classification.} Logic/proof-complexity
(bounded-arithmetic witnessing) gives a DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE THEOREM (Cook
1975) for the provable case. Control-theory ``certificate'' analogies, such as
Lyapunov or barrier certificates for a dynamics, correspond to $\sigma_{A,n}$ as
a soundness certificate for the dynamics of $A$. That is a USEFUL ANALOGY only:
the missing theorem is short certificate existence for an arbitrary correct
system, which is exactly FW2. \textbf{PQ ledger:} item 6 (special
soundness/collision extraction) is a FAILED TRANSFER here, because B3 needs
proof \emph{existence}, not extraction from conflicting openings. Items 1--4 are
not relevant to this bridge.
