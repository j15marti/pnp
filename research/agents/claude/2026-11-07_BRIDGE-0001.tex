\section*{Statements}
Let $R$ be a fixed proof system with polynomial-time-checkable DAG derivations and $\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\ge|\pi|$.
\begin{proposition}[Lower-bound direction is stronger than P$\neq$NP]
If $\overline W_R(N)$ is superpolynomial then $R$ is not polynomially bounded, hence (Cook--Reckhow) this is consistent with, and a priori only a step toward, $\mathrm{NP}\neq\mathrm{coNP}$. It implies $\mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$ only through the separate implication $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R$ p-bounded, i.e.\ the bridge.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proposition}[Bridge for generic $R$]
$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow$ ``$R$ is p-bounded'' is equivalent to a reflection-type simulation: $R$ must polynomially simulate the trivial system whose proofs are empty and whose verifier runs the SAT decider. For $R$ = Frege or Extended Frege this is not known (it would follow if EF were optimal, and optimality of EF is open; Krajicek--Pudlak). Hence a bridge for a fixed classical $R$ is not weaker than the open problem it serves.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proposition}[Making the bridge true by construction]
If $R^{+}$ adds a rule ``$M$ accepts $x$ within $t$ steps'' with cost $t$, the bridge holds trivially, but then $\overline W_{R^+}$ superpolynomial is equivalent to SAT having no polynomial-time algorithm, i.e.\ a restatement of P$\neq$NP, not progress (ledger principle: equivalent of P$\neq$NP is a reformulation).
\end{proposition}
\noindent So the project's spectrum is: fixed weak $R$ (bridge unproved, lower bound plausible) versus computation-augmented $R^+$ (bridge trivial, lower bound equals the target). The open design question is an intermediate $R$ with a \emph{proved} simulation of every poly-time decider at polynomial cost yet with an independently provable lower bound. No such system is known; the corpus Rmix341 results (minimum-cost atlas, orbit quotient) are unconditional facts about a fixed finite-rule system and do not supply the bridge.
