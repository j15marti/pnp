\section*{Stable state}
\begin{proposition}[PROVED/EXACT, consolidated]
Assume cost and length are polynomially related. Then
$B_R\iff(\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R\text{ is optimal})$.
If $R$ has a known superpolynomial lower bound (Resolution, Res(k), CP, PC, AC$^0$-Frege), then
$B_R\iff \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
A portfolio that switches adaptively among systems is itself a single proof system.
\end{proposition}
\noindent The load-bearing gap is OPEN, and it is located at Frege/EF level: we need either an EF
lower bound, or a proof that EF is optimal under $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ (as in Cook 1975 and
Pich--Santhanam, arXiv:2312.08163).
