\paragraph{Scope.} Claude-role note under the 2026-11-02 12:04 worst-case directive. Status: elementary logic plus KNOWN THEOREMS; no new lower bound. P versus NP remains OPEN.
\section*{Lemma 1 (B3 is vacuous for systems with unconditional lower bounds) --- PROVED/EXACT}
If $\overline W_R(N)$ is superpolynomial unconditionally (e.g.\ $R$=Resolution, via Tseitin on expanders / PHP), then B3 ``SAT$\in$P $\Rightarrow\overline W_R$ polynomial'' is equivalent to $\neg(\text{SAT}\in P)$. B3 plus the lower bound then proves nothing beyond the conclusion; so a useful $R$ must be one where polynomial boundedness of $\overline W_R$ is \emph{not} refuted unconditionally.
\section*{Lemma 2 (lower-bound side is proof length) --- PROVED/EXACT}
With free choice of the best derivation and polynomial-time-checkable rules, $\overline W_R$ is the optimal proof length of a Cook--Reckhow system. If $R$ simulates Extended Frege (needed for any known route to B3 via PV$_1$/Cook translation of a provably correct SAT algorithm), a superpolynomial $\overline W_R$ is an Extended Frege lower bound, a famous open problem; Frege lower bounds are only superlinear in lines/ quadratic-type, and constructive-barrier work (Khaniki--Pich--Sokolov, CCC 2026) suggests EF bounds for circuit-lower-bound formulas are as hard as circuit lower bounds. Unit-cost semantic rules escape this only by being non-checkable, which the directive forbids.
\section*{Consequence}
Dilemma for the program: (a) weak $R$ (Resolution, mixed GF(2)+Res such as $R_{mix341}$): lower bounds may be provable but B3 is unsupported (Lemma 1; the corpus's 341/362 results show affine shortcuts), (b) strong $R\supseteq$ EF: B3 is plausible conditionally on \emph{provable} correctness of the decider, but the lower bound is open at the frontier. The weakest honest target is the intermediate regime: an $R$ for which one proves B3 \emph{unconditionally from provable correctness} and a lower bound against $R$ not implied by EF lower bounds, which looks empty since $R\le$ EF simulation makes lower bounds for $R$ weaker than for EF while B3 needs $R\ge$ the system into which the algorithm translates.
\section*{Falsifiers proposed}
F1: an $R$ strictly below EF with provable reflection translation for all deciders (would contradict the tension above). F2: a Frege-strength hard family with certified optimum on small $n$ in a fixed combined calculus (finite only). F3: B3 from SAT$\in$P without provable correctness (blocked by Levin-style non-constructivity).
