\section*{Identity (PROVED/EXACT)}
Fix an orbit $o$ of 4-sets of clauses and any copy $S\in o$. By symmetry, $\mathbb E[N_o\,\mathbf 1_{\rm UNSAT}]=|o|\Pr[S\subseteq F]\Pr[{\rm UNSAT}\mid S\subseteq F]$. So $\mathbb E[N_o\mid{\rm UNSAT}]=e_o\rho_o$, where $e_o=\binom{t}{4}|o|/\binom{M}{4}$ and $\rho_o=\Pr[{\rm UNSAT}\mid S\subseteq F]/\Pr[{\rm UNSAT}]$. Conditioning on $S\subseteq F$ is exact planting: put $S$ in, then add a uniform $(t-4)$-subset of the other $M-4$ clauses. For a pool of $n$ independent UNSAT draws, $\Pr[\text{pool hits }o]\le n\,e_o\rho_o$.
