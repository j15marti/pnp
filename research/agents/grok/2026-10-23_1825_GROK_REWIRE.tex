\section{5-cut growth step (COMPUTATIONAL)}
\paragraph{Method (own code).} \texttt{rewire5.py} (\texttt{02f8972bc4fc69e0}) first runs the pass-2 4-cut REWIRE with the full 222-class oracle to a fixed point, then alternates a 5-cut pass and the 4-cut pass until size stops dropping (at most 4 rounds). In the 5-cut pass, each node keeps up to 12 cuts, preferring 5-leaf ones. A 5-leaf cut is tried only if its function has full 5-variable support and its maximum fanout-free cone (MFFC) has at least 2 nodes. The library is keyed by the raw 32-bit truth table on sorted leaves (no NPN canonicalization at width~5). On a miss, SAT exact synthesis (Glucose~4, 5\,s per $k$) tries $k=1,2,\dots,\min(\mathrm{MFFC}-1,8)$ in order, so a found circuit is minimum. A circuit is stored only when applying it gains. A proven floor (``no circuit up to the cap'') is stored as a negative entry so it is not recomputed. The cap is 64, and eviction removes the least-used entry from the older half. Control: the $n$-input encoder reproduces 7 sampled four-input sizes (SAT at $k$, UNSAT at $k-1$) with 0 disagreements. All 42 runs preserve the truth table (\textbf{0} mismatches).

\paragraph{Hostile family.} Carry chains $c_{j+1}=g(c_j,x_{4j+1},\dots,x_{4j+4})$ with $m$ blocks. Each block is a \emph{fresh} redundant sum-of-minterms encoding of a fixed 5-input $g$. The three $g$ are random full-support functions whose minimum AIG size is exactly \textbf{4} (EXACT: SAT at 4, UNSAT at 1--3): \texttt{0x100000} (one minterm), \texttt{0xcccc8ccc} and \texttt{0xf050d05}. So $4m$ is an upper bound for every chain.

\paragraph{Random 5-input controls.} There were 24 random AIGs on 5 inputs with 10, 14 or 18 generator steps. In 23 of 24, the final size is proven optimal (EXACT: UNSAT for every smaller $k$). In one case (18 steps, seed 3), the 4-cut fixed point is 8 and the 5-cut step reaches \textbf{6}, which is optimal, so the 5-cut step closes a gap of 2. In the remaining case (10 steps, seed 6), the final size is 4 but the optimum is 3. The output's function has support 3 (\texttt{0xaa00ff55}), and the 3-leaf root cut has class \texttt{0x3cf} of size 3 with an MFFC of 4. The pass-2 4-cut pass should have taken this gain of 1 and did not. This is a suspected defect in \texttt{rewire2.py} (leaf-slot or transform handling for cuts with fewer than 4 leaves). It is OPEN and will be debugged next.
