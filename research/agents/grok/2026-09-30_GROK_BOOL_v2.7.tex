Used this run as FOREIGN or representation-relative:
Total influence Inf(F) = ∑_i Inf_i(F), equivalently the expected sensitivity, equivalently 4 times the sum of |ŝ(S)|^{2} |S| over Fourier coefficients (Kahn–Kalai–Linial; O'Donnell). Influence is an analytic property of the Boolean cube, not a rewrite identity.

3. Why BOOL rather than algebra (restated)
Algebrization replaces a Boolean function by a low-degree or multilinear extension over a field. Complement, absorption, consensus, and unit collapse are equalities of Boolean functions, not of polynomials. Influence, algebraic immunity, and Fourier sparsity are themselves algebraic or analytic summaries of F. They measure a different object from ForcedBranch on a residual after I1–I17.
BOOL proofs must still be written as named rewrites. A SIL event becomes a BOOL obligation only after a charged READ, QUERY, or BRANCH that materializes the influenced coordinate.

6. Candidates introduced and broken this run
C74. Total influence of the BOOL-cleaned residual as a predictor of minimum exact 3-SAT binary steps. Statement: after free BOOL closure, let I(F) = TotInf(F). Claim: Bstep of an optimal exact solver is 2^Θ(I) or otherwise monotone in I. BREAK: Majority and tribes have large total influence and are not 3-SAT hardness cores; they are decided by counting or by a constant-depth threshold circuit, which is FOREIGN[TC0]. XOR of n bits has Inf = n and is decided by FOREIGN[Gaussian] in linear time. A 2-SAT implication chain can be padded with dummy literals so that syntactic Inf of an encoding grows while I15+I16 still collapse the residual to Bstep 0. Satisfiable Horn instances with many relevant variables have large influence and unit-propagation terminals. Status: FALSIFIED as a general predictor. Status as an analytic index: retained as TotInf(F) inside FOREIGN[Fourier].
C75. Algebraic immunity of the residual as a predictor of minimum exact binary steps. Statement: let a(F) = AI(F). Claim: optimal exact 3-SAT branching is exponential in a(F). BREAK: Algebraic immunity is defined for a single Boolean function as a map {0,1}^n → {0,1}. A 3-CNF is a conjunction of local constraints; AI of the characteristic function of the onset can be large while the formula remains 2-SAT or affine. High-AI cryptographic Boolean functions are typically not presented as 3-CNF residuals and are evaluated by table or circuit, not by Shannon SAT search. Low-AI functions (degree-1 annihilators) include all affine functions, which FOREIGN[Gaussian] decides without branching. Adding dummy Tseitin variables can change the ANF degree of a wrapper without changing SAME_REALIZATION SAT cost. Status: FALSIFIED as a solver-invariant predictor. Status as an algebraic-attack index: retained as AI(F) for FOREIGN[Annihilator] only.
C76. Fourier sparsity / spectral ℓ₁-norm of the residual as a predictor of minimum exact binary steps. Statement: let s(F) be Fourier sparsity (or SpecL1(F)). Claim: Bstep = Θ(s) or 2^Θ(log s) for every exact solver. BREAK: Parity has sparsity 1 and Inf = n; it is easy for XOR and hard for naive Shannon order if treated as a CNF of all odd-weight clauses. Address functions and juntas can be sparse and still require BRANCH on the junta variables only; the rest is Smooth. Compiled d-DNNF can answer SAT after MATERIALIZE with remaining BRANCH = 0 while the source function remains Fourier-dense. Padding and renaming do not preserve sparsity of a particular encoding. Status: FALSIFIED as a wrapper-robust runtime predictor. Status as a spectrum index: retained as FSparsity(F) and SpecL1(F) with mandatory ESCAPE[Spectrum].
Internal tautology that survives: after free closure, the Shannon DAG grows iff ForcedBranch remains on the residual and no FOREIGN[K] is invoked. C74–C76 measure cube-analytic or annihilator objects. They coincide with Bstep only inside named machines.
