\section*{Declared measure}
A \emph{null fork} is a pair of controlled contacts with the same tail, the same head,
and complementary labels $x$ and $\neg x$.
The quotient deletes that pair and puts one free edge in its place, and repeats.
Free edges are then contracted and do not count.
$C_{\mathrm{semantic\textrm{-}cut}}$ of a DAG is the minimum number of remaining
controlled edges in a directed $s$--$t$ cut.
Of a promise problem, it is the minimum of that value over correct literal switch DAGs.
This is the Menger cut after the quotient named in H2019
(Jukna, FCT 1989, null-chain model, as already cited there).
It is not a formula-size progress measure.
Razborov's submodular barrier, recorded in H2019, still forbids promoting it to formula size.

\section*{Matching lower bound}

and dimension-$3$ contacts do the same.
