\section*{Why this claim next}
PAIR-CAMOUFLAGE1 measures the full $9$-orbit pair vector $n_{s,h}(F)$ (pairs of clauses sharing $s$ variables, $h$ of them with opposite sign) for SAT and UNSAT formulas at density $3.5$. The camouflage target needs UNSAT formulas whose \emph{whole} pair vector looks typical. H1857 showed the window $|T-\mathbb E_0T|\le q/2$ on the single statistic $T$ is nonempty. The smallest next question: is matching $T$ enough, or do the other orbit coordinates still give UNSAT away?

\section*{Checks}
Scratch script \texttt{experiments/paircam\_audit.py} (not pushed); pair vectors computed by matrix products $|A||A|^\top$ and $AA^\top$ on the signed clause-variable matrix, satisfiability by CaDiCaL~1.5 (PySAT).
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reproduction (COMPUTATIONAL).} Peer generator, seeds $8000+q$, $N=4000$: UNSAT counts $89,72,56,40$ at $q=20,24,28,32$ and every printed SAT$-E_0$, UNSAT$-E_0$, U$-$S entry match the raw file to two decimals. The peer DPLL is not a source of error here. The raw file lists only six orbits at $q=20,24,28$; the three $s=3$ orbits there are below $0.35$ in absolute deviation on both seed families.
\item \textbf{Three exact identities (PROVED/EXACT, checked on all $32000$ formulas).} With $T=\sum_v \mathrm{pos}_v\mathrm{neg}_v$ and $d_v$ the variable degree,
\[ T=\sum_{s,h} h\,n_{s,h},\qquad \sum_v d_v^2=3t+2\sum_{s,h} s\,n_{s,h},\qquad \sum_{s,h}n_{s,h}=\tbinom t2. \]
Each is a double count of ordered occurrence pairs (opposite-sign occurrences of one variable in two clauses; all occurrence pairs of one variable; all clause pairs). So the $9$-vector has $8$ free coordinates, two of which are $T$ and the degree second moment.
\item \textbf{Signal beyond $T$ (COMPUTATIONAL).} Fisher LDA, stratified $5$-fold cross-validation, $20$ repetitions, mean held-out AUC:

The residual column regresses $T$ and $\sum d^2$ out of the $8$ coordinates first; it still separates at AUC $0.62$--$0.73$.
\item \textbf{Inside the $T$ window (COMPUTATIONAL, small samples).} Restricting to $|T-\mathbb E_0T|\le q/2$ with $\mathbb E_0T=qL^2\,t(t-1)/(M(M-1))$: windows of $1446$--$1754$ formulas hold only $11$--$29$ UNSAT, and the full-$8$ LDA gives held-out AUC $0.619,0.751,0.709,0.819$ (peer) and $0.808,0.716,0.645,0.812$ (fresh) at $q=20,24,28,32$. With $11$--$29$ positives these AUCs are noisy, but all eight exceed $0.6$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Reading.} Matching $T$ does not camouflage UNSAT against fixed-order pair statistics at these sizes: the other orbit coordinates still carry held-out signal (the largest mean UNSAT deviations sit in orbits $(1,0)$, $(1,1)$ and $(0,0)$; which residual direction carries the signal was not isolated). A PAIR-CAMOUFLAGE family therefore has to pin the whole $8$-dimensional orbit vector near $\mathbb E_0$, not one coordinate. Whether such a family exists with UNSAT density bounded away from zero, and whether the signal persists as $q$ grows, is \textbf{OPEN}; four sizes up to $q=32$ cannot decide an asymptotic gap. Nothing is falsified, and nothing improves a charged $T$, $S$, or $R$.
