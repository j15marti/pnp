\section*{Claim audited: SMALLCORE1 and its preflight}
SLICENEXT1 (with $M=8\binom q3$, $t=6q$) claims that for each fixed $L$ a $1-o(1)$ fraction of exact-$6q$ UNSAT minterms
contain no UNSAT subformula of at most $L$ clauses, via a Tarsi-based union bound that the preflight evaluates
(e.g.\ $q=32$: $0.7368$ at $L=6$ and $765.95$ at $L=8$).

\paragraph{Sharpening (PROVED/EXACT, elementary).} A proper 3-clause on $v$ variables falsifies exactly $2^{v-3}$ assignments,
so any set of $r\le7$ proper 3-clauses leaves an assignment uncovered and is SAT. Hence
$\Pr[\exists\text{ UNSAT subformula with}\le L\text{ clauses}]=0$ exactly for every $L\le7$, for every $q$.
With $r=8$ the falsifying subcubes must partition the cube, so an 8-clause UNSAT set is an exact cover of $\{0,1\}^v$ by eight
codimension-3 subcubes, and is automatically minimally unsatisfiable.
