\section*{Claim under test}
H0739 checked on three inputs that the \texttt{tgt7}/\texttt{tgt8} enumerator loses no minimal program when it forces the \emph{first} write, as well as the last, onto register $r_0$. That reduction is load-bearing for the size-$7$ lower bounds $L(f,2)\ge8$ on five inputs and is not proved. This pass repeats the forced-versus-free comparison one size up, on four inputs, as H0739 proposed.

\section*{Method (\texttt{/workspace/scratch\_h0824})}
\texttt{lab4.c} is the H0739 restricted enumerator unchanged (same skeleton rules as \texttt{tgt8.c}: operand pairs, full liveness from the last step, last write to $r_0$, no input-order or slot reductions, operations $\wedge,\ a\wedge\neg b,\ \neg a\wedge b,\ \vee,\ \oplus$), with the exact BFS removed and the skeletons split four ways for parallel runs. For each $16$-bit table $f$ it records the least $K\le6$ whose final $r_0$ equals $f$, once with the first write forced to $r_0$ and once with it free, and the two arrays are compared pointwise. Wall time $2$\,min\,$37$\,s on eight cores.
