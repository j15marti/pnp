\section*{2. The claim}
Model, plain and tight encoders as in H1948 to H2045 (two one-bit registers starting at $0$, $n$ read-only inputs, a step is $R_w:=g(a,b)$, $f$ in $R_0$ at the end). Lemma TIGHT-AT-MIN is PROVED/EXACT (H2045), so if plain $L-1$ is UNSAT then tight $L$ UNSAT implies plain $L$ UNSAT.

\textbf{Claim CUBE-COVER (PROVED/EXACT, from the clauses of \texttt{regsat\_tight.py}).} For $L\ge 2$, every program admitted by the tight encoder has a first step $(w,s_a,s_b,g)$ with $w\in\{0,1\}$, $s_a<s_b$ both inputs, and $g\in\{2,4,6,8,14\}$. So the tight instance is UNSAT if and only if each of the $2\binom{n}{2}\cdot 5$ cubes fixing the first step to one of these values is UNSAT.

\emph{Proof.} At step 1 no register has been written, so condition (ii) forbids a register operand; condition (iv) gives $s_a<s_b$; since $1<L$, condition (iii) gives $g(0,0)=0$, i.e.\ $g$ even, and condition (i) removes $0,10,12$ from the even tables, leaving $2,4,6,8,14$. \qed

For $n=5$ this is 100 cubes and for $n=6$ it is 150.
