\section*{Checks}
Scratch script \texttt{experiments/match3.py} (not pushed). Same generator as H1910 (uniform distinct clauses on $q$ variables, $t=3.5q$), CaDiCaL~1.5 via PySAT, fresh seeds $20261927+q$, $N=40000$ per $q$, first half train and second half holdout. \item \textbf{The cubic signal is mostly pair signal (COMPUTATIONAL).} Holdout AUC of train-fitted Fisher LDA, and of $U_3$ after conditioning on the pair vector:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rrrrrrr}
$q$ & $U_3$ alone & pair$_8$ & pair$_8{+}U_3$ & pair$_8{+}U_3{+}\sum d^3$ & $U_3$ in window & $U_3$ stratified on pair$_8$\\\hline

pair$_8$ is the eight free orbit coordinates. ``Stratified'' bins the holdout by quintiles of the pair$_8$ LDA score (cuts from training SAT) and averages the within-bin $U_3$ AUC weighted by $n_{\mathrm{SAT}}n_{\mathrm{UNSAT}}$. Adding $U_3$ (and $\sum_v d_v^3$) to the pair vector moves holdout AUC by at most $0.004$, and once the pair score is held roughly fixed, $U_3$ sits at AUC $0.49$--$0.54$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Reading.} The PAIRCAM2 cubic number is reproduced (in-window $U_3$ AUC $0.64$--$0.70$), but on these samples it is not evidence of an order-three signal beyond pairs: an axis-aligned box on six orbits leaves the separating pair direction (excess $(1,1)$, deficit $(1,0)$) partly unconditioned, and $U_3$ is correlated with that direction. So the finite hierarchy at $q\le32$ is: first order exact camouflage, pair order clear signal, order three no detectable signal beyond pairs under linear tests. This sharpens the peer's recommended next step: matching should be done on the pair \emph{score} (or the full pair vector), not on a coordinate box, before any order-three test is read. Nonlinear order-three tests, the other order-three signed-overlap counts, and any asymptotic statement are \textbf{OPEN}. Nothing is falsified, and nothing improves a charged $T$, $S$, or $R$.
