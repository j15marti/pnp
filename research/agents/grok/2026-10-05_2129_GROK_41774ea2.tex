\section*{2. The claim}
Model, tight encoder and first-step cubes as in H2013 to H2118: two one-bit registers $R_0,R_1$ starting at $0$, $n$ read-only inputs, step $R_w:=g(a,b)$ with $g$ indexed by $p+2q$, operands $s_a<s_b$ (sources $0..n-1$ are inputs, $n+i$ is $R_i$), final step writes $R_0$ and $R_0=f$ at the end. By CUBE-COVER and W-SWAP (both PROVED/EXACT) only the $5\binom n2$ cubes with step 1 $=(0,s_a,s_b,g)$ matter.

\textbf{Lemma SPLIT2 (PROVED/EXACT, from the clauses of \texttt{regsat\_fix.py}).} Let $L\ge 3$ and fix a parent cube $(w_1,s_a,s_b,g_1)$. Every program the tight encoder admits in that cube has step 2 in the list
\[
w_2\in\{0,1\},\quad s_a'<s_b' \text{ from } \{0,\dots,n-1\}\cup\{n+w_1\},\quad g_2\in\{2,4,6,8,14\}.
\]
So the parent cube is UNSAT if and only if all $2\cdot\binom{n+1}{2}\cdot 5$ subcubes are UNSAT ($210$ at $n=6$, $150$ at $n=5$, $100$ at $n=4$).

\emph{Proof.} The operand order $s_a'<s_b'$ is clause (iv). After step 1 only $R_{w_1}$ has been written, and clause (ii) forbids reading an unwritten register, so $n+1-w_1$ is excluded. Since $L\ge3$, step 2 is not the last step, so $g_2$ is normal ($g_2(0,0)=0$, clause (iii)) and non-degenerate (clause (i)); the normal non-degenerate ops are exactly $2,4,6,8,14$, because $10$ and $12$ are projections and $0$ is constant. Nothing else about step 2 is constrained a priori. \qed
