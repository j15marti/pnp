\section*{Claim 1: the SLICE-UNSAT ``Immediate next theorem''}
The plan asks for an explicit $t=t(n)$ and a polynomial transformation from arbitrary 3-CNFs to the exact-$t$ clause
layer preserving satisfiability, with polynomially related universe size $N=8\binom n3$. It is stated there as OPEN.

\paragraph{Lemma (PROVED/EXACT, elementary).} Let $M=8\binom n3$ and add $k$ fresh variables $y_1,\dots,y_k$.
Call a signed 3-clause on the $n+k$ variables a \emph{pad} if it contains at least one positive literal $y_i$. For a
3-CNF $F$ on $x_1,\dots,x_n$ with $m\le M$ clauses and any set $P$ of $M-m$ distinct pads, $F\cup P$ is satisfiable iff
$F$ is. \emph{Proof.} If $a\models F$, extend by all $y_i=1$; every pad is satisfied. If $b\models F\cup P$, its restriction
to the $x$-variables satisfies $F$, since clauses of $F$ mention no $y_i$. The pad capacity is
$\mathrm{cap}(n,k)=8\binom{n+k}{3}-\sum_{j=0}^{3}\binom kj\binom n{3-j}2^{3-j}$, so the lemma applies whenever
$\mathrm{cap}(n,k)\ge M$.

Hence $t(n)=M$ on the $(n+k)$-variable universe works, and with $x=k/n$ the condition is asymptotically
$8(1+x)^3-(2+x)^3\ge 8$, i.e.\ $x\gtrsim0.396$. The universe grows by $N'/N\approx(1+x)^3$.

\paragraph{Consequence for the branch (PROVED/EXACT, elementary; circuit-size accounting).}
The padding map depends on $m=|z|$ only through $p_j=[\,j\le M-m\,]$, computable by a poly$(N)$-size counter. So
$C(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n})\le C(U_{n+k,M})+\mathrm{poly}(N)$. Conversely
$U_{n,t}(z)=[|z|>t]\vee([|z|=t]\wedge\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n}(z))$ gives $C(U_{n,t})\le C(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n})+\mathrm{poly}(N)$
for every $t$. So for unrestricted circuits the slice and the full function are polynomially equivalent, and the
plan's padding prerequisite carries no difficulty. The padding map is \emph{not} a monotone projection (the $p_j$
are anti-monotone in $z$); that matters only for transfers that must stay monotone.

\paragraph{Hostile reading (no label change).} With Valiant's and Berkowitz's slice theorems (KNOWN THEOREM; both
\emph{unread} in this pass, carried at the plan's description), $C_{\rm mon}(U_{n,t})$ is polynomially equivalent to
$C(U_{n,t})$. Combined with the lemma, the target $C_{\rm mon}(U_{n,t})=N^{\omega(1)}$ is then polynomially equivalent to
$\mathrm{NP}\not\subseteq\mathrm{P/poly}$ for 3-UNSAT. That is a restatement, not a reduction in difficulty. The
load-bearing monotone slice lower bound is \textbf{OPEN}. Razborov's limits on the approximation method for general
circuits are relevant here but \emph{unread} in this pass. Relation to our surface: a nonuniform lower bound would make the
SAT-to-charged-model compiler unnecessary (P $\subseteq$ P/poly, KNOWN THEOREM), so the branch is orthogonal to that
compiler and does not discharge it.
