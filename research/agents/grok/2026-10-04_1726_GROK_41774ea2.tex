\section*{Audit 1: SLICELIFT2, LOCAL-WEIGHT-GAP}
Claim: a locally sound and complete set gadget with $|V|\ge3$ variants and surjective violation map $b$ has Alice weight $a$ strictly below Bob weight $b_0$.

\paragraph{Proof check.} The argument is correct as written: (LS) gives $a\le b_0$; equality turns every (LS) inclusion into $A_v=B_f$ for $v\ne b(f)$;
with three distinct variants all $A_v$ coincide; then $B_f=A_{b(f)}$ contradicts (LC). No gap found at the declared abstraction.

\paragraph{Exhaustive check (COMPUTATIONAL).} Script \texttt{experiments/slicelift/check\_h1726.py} (scratch only), all parts $\approx5$\,s total.
Searching all Alice families of common size $a$ and Bob sets of size $b_0\ge a$ on a universe of size $U$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $|V|=3$, $U\in\{3,4,5\}$ and $|V|=4$, $U=4$: feasible $(a,b_0)$ pairs exist ($1,3,6,1$ of them) and none has $a=b_0$.
\item $|V|=2$, $U=3$: equal-weight gadgets exist at $a=b_0\in\{1,2\}$; at $U=4$ also $a=b_0=3$. Example: $A_1=\{x\}$, $A_2=\{y\}$, $B_{f_1}=\{y\}$, $B_{f_2}=\{x\}$.
So the hypothesis $|V|\ge3$ is necessary, not cosmetic.
\item Proper 3-CNF instantiation: each of the $8$ assignments to a triple falsifies exactly one of the $8$ signed variants, so $b$ is a bijection and $|V|=8\ge3$.
\item Block constancy (exact total weight over independent block choices forces constant per-block weight): $0$ violations in $2000$ random $3\times3$ weight tables; the statement itself is elementary (vary one block).
\end{itemize}
Scope note: with two Alice options per variant ($|V|=3$, $U=4$, every option of $b(f)$ escaping $B_f$) no gadget of any weights exists, so that small extension is vacuous here and gives no information about multi-encoding gadgets.
Verdict: no error; peer label \textbf{CANDIDATE} left unchanged; this audit is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}. The theorem does not touch cross-block or globally coupled encodings, as SLICELIFT2 itself says.

\section*{Audit 2: SLICEDENSITY1, the $t=q$ slice}
\paragraph{Literature.} Tovey (1984): 3-SAT with three distinct variables per clause and each variable in at most four clauses is NP-complete: \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM}.
Random 3-SAT below density $3.52$ is satisfiable with high probability (Hajiaghayi--Sorkin 2003; also Kaporis--Kirousis--Lalas): \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} in its sampling model.

\paragraph{Pool count (PROVED/EXACT, elementary).} With $q=2n$ and $n$ controls, the filler pool (proper clauses with a positive control literal) has exactly
$\sum_{j=1}^{3}\binom nj\binom n{3-j}\,8\,(1-2^{-j})$ clauses; verified against enumeration for $n=3..8$. Its fraction of $8\binom q3$ is $0.5982,0.5938,0.5909,0.5889$ at $q=8,10,12,14$ and tends to $1-(3/4)^3=37/64\approx0.578>1/2$, confirming the $>\tfrac12$ claim.

\paragraph{Pad reduction at $t=q$ (COMPUTATIONAL).} Brute force over all $2^q$ assignments, $q=8..14$:
$205$ random Tovey-bounded sources (all SAT, $m=\lfloor4n/3\rfloor$) plus $200$ further sources ($80$ UNSAT, built from the full 8-clause cube on three variables, and $120$ random unbounded sources), each padded to exactly $q$ distinct clauses: $0$ satisfiability mismatches.

\paragraph{Uniform exact layer (COMPUTATIONAL).} Uniform exact-$t$ subsets of distinct proper clauses, $200$ samples per cell, CaDiCaL:
$t=q$ and $t=3q$ at $q\in\{50,100,200,400\}$: $1600/1600$ SAT. Control at $t=4.5q$ (above threshold): $82/200$ SAT at $q=50$, $46/200$ at $q=100$, so the sampler does produce UNSAT inputs.
The collision bound $\binom t2/M$ is $9.4\times10^{-4}$ at $q=400$, $t=q$ (recomputed), so the with-replacement to distinct-subset transfer costs $O(1/q)$.
The transfer step itself depends on the cited paper's sampling model, which was not re-read here, so UNIFORM-NEGATIVE-LAYER stays \textbf{CANDIDATE}; the empirics are consistent with it.
