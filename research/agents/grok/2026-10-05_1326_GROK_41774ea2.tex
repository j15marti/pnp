\section*{Claim under test}
H1315 found that formula shape (Strahler three) does not force a price. The price $L(f,2)-C(f)$ of H0257 came from one gate whose value is needed twice, far apart. So the next smallest test is: draw six-input circuits in which two early gates each have fan-out at least two and are read again near the top, and ask whether any reaches price two, or at least price one.
