\section*{Claim under test}
H0401 showed $L(f,2)\le C(f)+1$ for every $f$ on at most five inputs with $C(f)\le6$. Its next step: does any function on six or seven inputs with $C(f)=6$ have $L(f,2)\ge8$? Model as in H0228--H0401: free read-only inputs, two one-bit registers starting at $0$, each step writes any binary operation of two inputs or registers into one register.

\section*{Method (\texttt{/workspace/scratch\_h0427})}
\emph{Shapes} (\texttt{shapes6all.py}, H0333's \texttt{peb2.py}). Six-gate shapes reaching the output with at least six input slots: 379, by (slots, syntactic price) $(6,0)$:271, $(6,1)$:35, $(6,2)$:4, $(6,\infty)$:8, $(7,0)$:61. No six-gate shape has eight slots, and every seven-slot shape has price 0. So a function with seven essential inputs and $C=6$ has $L(f,2)=6$, and a gap of two needs exactly six essential inputs and a six-slot shape of price at least 2.

\emph{Tables} (\texttt{gen6x.c}). Labellings using all six variables (restricted growth strings), the five operation classes, $f$ and $\bar f$. Certified set $\mathcal C$ (price $\le1$ shapes): 3803098 tables; set $\mathcal B$ (price $\ge2$ or infeasible): 58980, of which 54600 depend on all six variables.

\emph{Correction.} Restricted growth strings fix labellings only up to variable permutation, so $\mathcal C$ is not permutation-closed. The literal difference $\mathcal B\setminus\mathcal C$ gave 640 tables; testing all 720 permutations and output complement against $\mathcal C$ (\texttt{permchk.c}) certifies 128 of them and leaves 512. All 512 come from the eight two-register-infeasible shapes, none from the price-2 shapes. They form 28 permutation-and-complement classes (\texttt{classes6.c}; the canonicalizer gives the expected 40 classes on all 3-input functions).

\emph{Search} (\texttt{r2s6x.c}, H0401's \texttt{r2s6c.c} lifted to 64-bit tables). Breadth-first search over unordered register pairs to depth 4 (163, 21646, 659730, 16593431 new states), full probes to depth 5, depth 6 by inverting the last operation (XOR type, and AND type via literal masks). The inversion probes one output polarity, so each table takes the minimum over itself and its complement, which is valid because $L(f,2)=L(\bar f,2)$. Controls (H0401 values, lifted with $x_5$ unused, complements included): \texttt{0x90ca96cc}, \texttt{0x0e00aa0c}, \texttt{0xf7506350} at $>6$; \texttt{0x02604260}, \texttt{0x41f0f100}, \texttt{0x4cc403c4} at $6$, all matching. Witness check (\texttt{r2wit.c}): \texttt{0x0006000600069990} gets the explicit 6-step program $g_1=\mathrm{NOR}(x_4,x_5)$, $g_2=\mathrm{NOR}(x_2,x_3)$, $g_3=g_1\oplus g_2$, $g_4=\overline{x_1\oplus g_2}$, $g_5=\overline{x_0\oplus g_4}$, $f=\bar g_5\wedge g_3$, a price-0 shape, which is how the correction above was found.

\section*{Results}
Of the 640 literal candidates, 128 have $L(f,2)=6$ and 512 have $L(f,2)>6$. The two splits agree exactly: the 128 are the permutation-certified tables, and none of the 512 is. Each of the 512 has $C\le6$ by construction and $C\ge6$ by NOPRICE-C5, so $C(f)=6$ and $L(f,2)\ge7$.
