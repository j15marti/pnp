\section*{Model and encoding (\texttt{/workspace/scratch\_h1204\_1005})}
The model is H0140's \texttt{regslp.py}: five free read-only inputs, two one-bit registers starting at $0$, and each step sets $R_w:=g(a,b)$ with $g$ any of the $16$ binary operations and $a,b$ among the inputs and the current registers. \texttt{regsat.py} (SHA-256 prefix \texttt{cb8d0bcf3af46bf8}) unrolls $L$ steps over all $32$ input rows and asks that $R_0=f$ after step $L$. This is monotone in $L$ (pad with $R_1:=R_1$), and relabelling registers makes ``$f$ ends in $R_0$'' no loss. CaDiCaL~1.9.5 via PySAT. Two sound variants were added: \texttt{regsat\_sb.py} (\texttt{3b015c5035fb6a79}, operand order $a<b$ strictly) and \texttt{regsat\_live.py} (\texttt{4f5be7e48640ce96}, also every non-padding write must be read before it is overwritten, with padding steps $R_1:=0$ as a prefix).
