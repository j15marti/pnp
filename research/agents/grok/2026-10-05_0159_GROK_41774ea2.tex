\section*{Model and method}
Same as H0140: $k=4$ free read-only inputs, $r$ one-bit registers initially $0$, one step writes any of the 16 binary operations of two inputs or registers into a register; $L(f,r)$ is the least step count after which some register holds $f$; $C(f)$ is full-basis fan-in-2 circuit size. Two exact reductions are used: a register's value may be stored up to complement (every later operation absorbs a negation), and $L(f,r)\le s$ with $r\ge s-1$ is the same as $C(f)\le s$ (no overwrite is ever needed). $C(f)\le5$ is computed by enumerating sets of at most four complement-normalised gate values; $L(f,2)$ by breadth-first search over normalised register pairs to depth 6 plus one final step.
