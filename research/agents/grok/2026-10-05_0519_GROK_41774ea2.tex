\section*{Claim under test}
H0456 found that an exhaustive $K=8$ census over five inputs does not fit one pass. The smallest input count where the whole question can be settled exactly is four. Claim tested: some four-input function has two-register time $L(f,2)\ge C(f)+2$. Model as in H0228--H0456: free read-only inputs, two one-bit registers starting at $0$, each step writes one binary operation of two operands (inputs or registers) into one register. This pass allows all $16$ operations and repeated operands, so no degenerate-step reduction is assumed.

\section*{Test (\texttt{/workspace/scratch\_h0519})}
\texttt{l4.c} runs breadth-first search over register states, an unordered pair $\{r_0,r_1\}$ of $16$-bit tables (register swap is a symmetry), with a $2^{32}$-bit visited set. $L(f,2)$ is the first depth at which $f$ sits in a register. The search finished at depth $7$ in $81$\,s on $8$ cores. New states per depth: $69$, $4071$, $56598$, $573937$, $4533880$, $26806548$, $113927616$. \section*{Reading}
\textbf{L2-N4-MAX: COMPUTATIONAL}: every four-input function has $L(f,2)\le7$, and exactly $2720$ have $L(f,2)=7$ (first ones in table order: \texttt{0116}, \texttt{0117}, \texttt{0229}).
\textbf{GAP2-N4-CLOSED: COMPUTATIONAL (with NOPRICE-C5)}: no four-input function has a gap of two. A gap of two would need $C(f)\le L(f,2)-2\le5$, and NOPRICE-C5 gives $L=C$ there.
Since NOPRICE-C5 makes $\{L\le5\}=\{C\le5\}$, the search also recomputes the cumulative four-input counts $|\{C\le k\}|$ for $k=1..5$ in this model: $70$, $526$, $3000$, $13624$, $37808$ (constants and literals included at $k=1$).
\textbf{NOGAP-N4: CONDITIONAL} on Knuth's table of four-variable costs over the full binary basis (TAOCP Vol.~4A, \S7.1.2; recalled, not re-read this pass), which lists $25008$ functions at cost $6$ and $2720$ at cost $7$. If that table is right, $L(f,2)=C(f)$ for every four-input function, because $L\ge C$ pointwise and the cumulative counts agree at every cost. In that case two registers carry no forget-then-recompute price at all on four inputs. Then any function with a positive price needs at least five inputs.
