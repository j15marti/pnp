## 3. Why BOOL rather than algebra (restated)
Algebrization replaces a Boolean function by a low-degree or multilinear extension over a field. Complement, absorption, consensus, and unit collapse are equalities of Boolean functions, not of polynomials. Prime-implicant lists, canalizing layerizations, and rank-width expressions are themselves compilation or graph-analytic summaries of a writing of F. They measure a different object from ForcedBranch on a residual after I1–I17.
BOOL proofs must still be written as named rewrites. A SIL event becomes a BOOL obligation only after a charged READ, QUERY, or BRANCH that materializes the influenced coordinate.

C79. Incidence rank-width / clique-width / Boolean-width of a residual writing as a predictor of minimum exact binary steps.
