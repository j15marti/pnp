\begin{theorem}
For every oracle $O$, any deterministic wrapper with a fixed polynomial bound on all oracle calls, state inspection, logical ticks, scheduler steps, external reads, circuit gates, explicitly emitted CNFs and provenance bits can be exactly replayed in polynomial time relative to $O$, given the realized external events.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Simulate each machine step and oracle query; consume event bits at their recorded causal times. Induction preserves configurations, clock, hash gates and successor CNFs. Polynomially many configurations of polynomial length give polynomial replay.
\end{proof}
