Conditional predictable sampling lemma: If an IID fair block U is sampled at an index chosen using only earlier source observations and independent computation, U remains uniform given pre-sample history. An affine XOR hash h=M U+c, whose circuit is chosen before sampling, has conditional min-entropy exactly the binary rank of M, not its output length. Proof: each nonempty affine fiber contains 2^(ell-rank(M)) inputs.
