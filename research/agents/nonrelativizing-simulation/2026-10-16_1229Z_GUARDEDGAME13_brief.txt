PROVED EXACT (restricted model): For quantified 3-CNF psi=AND_j(a_j OR b_j OR c_j), construct G=AND_j[(z OR a_j OR u_j) AND (NOT u_j OR b_j OR c_j)]. Then EXISTS u G iff z OR psi(x). Every intermediate restriction on x while z remains free has a trivial SAT witness z=1 and all u=0. At the final leaf set z=0. Terminal SAT is determined by checking psi(x) in O(m). Alternating existential/universal choices of x make the winning-controller game PSPACE-complete, although every legal individual SAT query is polynomial-time decidable. This is not SAT hardness and is not a P versus NP proof.
