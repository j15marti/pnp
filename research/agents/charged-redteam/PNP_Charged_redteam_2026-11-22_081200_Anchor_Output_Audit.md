## The audited entropy statement survives

These conclusions are correct at the declared checkpoint. They do not imply a universal upper bound smaller than k on I(X;W). A convention that records all initially hidden cells may include symbols already inferable from W. This is a valid but redundant code. ## One new lemma: output geometry remains after the entropy vanishes

For every A above and every k, let V_R count distinct physical cells of R read after tau. For each fixed X,I pair the two C executions have the same origin symbol at tau and opposite required final bits. At least one must subsequently write the origin. Starting at p, this requires at least p unit moves and reading every cell of R. Therefore, averaging over C and then X,I,

E[T-tau]>=p/2, and E[V_R]>=|R|/2.

If the origin symbol is not already either valid output bit, both members must return, which only strengthens the bound. No assumption that a cached answer requires another semantic computation enters the proof.

For the specified physical span charge, the returning execution visits post-transition head positions p-1,p-2,...,0 at distinct times. Each configuration's span contains the origin and head. Hence its suffix physical CM is at least p(p-1)/2 in cell units, and its expectation is at least p(p-1)/4. Fixed alphabet-to-bit constants can be restored. This is a same-execution geometric sum, not a product of unrelated maxima. It establishes no corresponding quadratic minimum-description bound.

The new lower bounds are only Omega(L) expected time and Omega(L^2) expected physical CM. They should be combined with the entropy bound by maximum unless disjoint charges are proved. In particular, a physical source-region crossing may be paid output delivery after all relevant information has already been copied. An empty fresh-information certificate is not a zero physical-read assertion.

## Legal paid cache and full correctness

There is one fixed A* with an authenticated fast path on this whole family and exhaustive fallback on all other valid packed instances. Preserve the input on a base track of a fixed finite product alphabet. Before first selector arrival, parse the headers, require m=n=5k+3, and check all 5k data triples against the exact F/G alternatives, saving each X_j on a spare track in header cells 2,...,k+1. These cells are outside R and do not overlap the two output cells. Counters and a bounded number of field buffers occupy additional fixed tracks. Each block verification and each transfer from source to cache is paid. No selector value is read during this phase. There is no preliminary whole-input length scan on this fast path: consume fields as encountered, reject premature end, and defer the final end check until after the selector. The unary n bounds fast-path workspace by physically encountered input; failed header guards switch to the general parser. Normalize temporary header workspace and counters if desired; W includes everything retained regardless.

After tau, authenticate all three selector triples, their fresh names, selected-name range, exact length and end. Access the selected cached coordinate, compute X_I XOR C, and write the terminated origin output. Authentication is syntactic, not a hash-equality test. On a failed candidate, parse the retained original and enumerate its n-bit assignments. Malformed encodings are rejected as malformed; valid nonfamily inputs are not labeled UNKNOWN or guessed UNSAT. Bounds on declared lengths are checked against the actual finite input before allocating claimed storage.

Since the cache in W explicitly contains all of X and the prefix has no other sampled inputs, I(X;W)=H(W)=k on this family. Thus H(D|W,I,C)=0. The conditional record Pi can be empty, E empty, h=0, rho=1 and b=1, with decoder D=cache[I] XOR C. This proves failure of a universal positive fresh-demand claim at this checkpoint with the stipulated outside-R side information. It does not refute Bounds' parameterized inequality, whose right side is then zero. A* still crosses R to deliver output. Subsequent normalization at the origin can leave one decision bit and a constant-size suffix, as in the earlier Accounting control.

## Complete conservative ledger

The executable checker is a semantic implementation; the sole-tape realization below is symbolic and was not executed. Its tariff is deliberately loose, not a proposed improvement to Bounds' sharper O(L^2 log L) family upper bound.

| Phase of A* | Sole-tape accounting |
|---|---|
| Parse and authenticate source/header | Fixed loops over at most L fields, O(log L)-bit arithmetic; all source and address access by paid sweeps |
| Prefix payload caching | At most k transfers, O(L) physical travel per transfer with counters/buffers included conservatively |
| Selector authentication and cache lookup | Exact comparison of the three rows and end, then actual cache access; no free remote lookup |
| Copies, retention, temporary records | O(L) span including original, cache, finite tracks, counters and output |
| Clearing, switching and origin output | O(L^2) safe allowance within the bound below |
| Recognized family, total | T=O(L^4), S=O(L), CM_phys=O(L^5), CM_min=O(L^5) |
| Valid nonfamily fallback | T=O(L^4+2^n L^4), S=O(L), both cumulative upper bounds O(L^5+2^n L^5) |

To justify the conservative polynomial total, source recognition uses at most O(L) records and comparisons on O(L)-bit strings. A fixed multitape implementation with naive scans and counter arithmetic fits O(L^3) bit transitions and O(L) cells. Fixed-track sole-tape sweeps across its O(L) workspace simulate each virtual transition in O(L), giving O(L^4). For the special pre-selector implementation, use the header tracks and shuttle directly to each current source field, never crossing p prematurely; the same loose bound covers these operations. Fallback enumerates assignments one at a time with O(L) bits and scans the retained formula per assignment, with the stated conservative polynomial allowance. Initialization and failed recognition are included once. Minimum-description CM follows separately by replay from the L-bit input and O(log(T+2))-bit transition index, not by equating information and physical storage.

Under T<=K L^a, compare with S=O(L^a), physical CM=O(L^(2a)), minimum-description CM=O(L^(a+1)). The new geometric bounds exclude no a>=1. The conditional entropy numerator is at most one, and becomes zero for the full cache, versus capacity b rho K L^a. Count-map circuit size O(L^a log L) is a separate counting consequence. The internally reviewed permutation control and its matching quadratic/cubic baseline and static ceilings remain unchanged. No unrestricted polynomial algorithm follows from A* because its fallback can be exponential.
