# ORBIT39, 2026-10-22: Robust HIGH-KT mask-orbit obstruction

**Proved restricted counting lemma.** For any fixed family of 2^b M-bit masks mu_s, choose ell=p+b+1<=M and H_u=0^(M-ell)||u. At least 2^ell-2^b(2^(p+1)-1)>=2^b suffixes u satisfy ordinary KT_U(H_u XOR mu_s)>p for every s simultaneously. Reason: at most 2^(p+1)-1 binary programs have description length <=p; each XOR-by-mask map is injective; apply the union bound. This holds for genuine ordinary KT with the end-marker requirement, not a toy or cryptographic surrogate.

**New PH encoding.** For fixed k and M<=p^k, assuming polynomial-in-p indexed mask evaluators, robust prefix extension is an ordinary Sigma_3 predicate: exists remaining suffix, forall seed and candidate program, exists failing index and bounded computation tableau. Queries can be serialized in poly(p) bits, but the solver exponent C is unknown and must be charged.

**Attempted G_241 falsified.** Let G(F,i)=H_robust[i] XOR mask_checksum(F)[i], output full M=N^(6/5+o(1)) bits and threshold p>=log M. Exhaustive H search is uniform/total and bounded-space, but takes 2^O(p+b) M poly(p) time, beyond N^(241/200). Even with a hypothetical speedup, all SAT and UNSAT outputs have HIGH KT, violating SAT->LOW. No all-input ordinary-MKTP reduction is obtained.

**Hostile tests.** A local standalone Python verifier completed 3,060 assertions, zero failures, with periodic, SHA counter, and greedily adversarial mask families, 12 padded easy SAT/UNSAT sources, 98 INDEX sources, 49 minimally UNSAT eight-clause INDEX cores, and 392 successful core-clause deletions. Toy periodic-program tests are not genuine universal KT estimates.

**Resource gap.** For k=241, the exponent margin between M*p and N^(241/200) is exactly 1/48200; a p^(1+delta)-time-per-index decoder fits only for delta<1/240, disregarding subpolynomial factors. A slow specific decoder does not establish HIGH KT.

**First missing arrow.** Need a fully charged SAT-sensitive source transformation that yields LOW ordinary KT for every SAT input and HIGH ordinary KT for every UNSAT input, including padded easy sources and late INDEX continuations. Source-dependent changes among seeds of a robust HARD core do not supply it.

Original source checked: McKay, Murray, Williams, STOC 2019, Theorem 4.2, https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/MCSP-MKTP-stoc19.pdf . Its PH-oracle streaming MKTP algorithm receives an already-existing target and does not produce SAT-to-MKTP targets.
