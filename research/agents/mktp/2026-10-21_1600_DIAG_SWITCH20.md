## A precise all-input contractor that fails semantically
Fix k=241, N=max(|F|,2), a=ceil(log2 N), L=ceil(6a/5), M=2^L, p_k=max(L,ceil(2^(L/241))). Integer power comparison suffices; no floating rounding. Input F is an arbitrary binary SAT encoding, malformed treated NO. Ordinary output is the canonical self-delimiting pair (M,p_k,T), with T length M.

Define D_L[0]=1. For each 1<=i<M put q=i-1, ell=floor(log2(q+1)), v=q-(2^ell-1), and d=ell-bit binary v (empty when ell=0). Simulate fixed indexed universal machine U(d,i) for L-ell steps. If it outputs b in time set D_L[i]=1-b, otherwise D_L[i]=0. Out-of-range index yields the end marker. The attempted next-target-bit generator is G_k(F,i)=D_L[i], independent of source content. It requires one charged source length scan and no rereads, deterministic O(N+M polylog M)=N^(6/5+o(1)) output time, O(log M) work memory, no SAT/PH/random oracle, no advice or side table. Resource bounds pass. Semantic reduction fails.

### Theorem 1 (any fixed U)
KT_U(D_L)>L for M=2^L. Any hypothetical witness |d|+t<=L has |d|<L, and one of the exactly 2^L-1 such binary programs is assigned its unique coordinate i(d)=1+(2^|d|-1)+value(d). At that coordinate D_L is opposite U^d(i) if it halts in L-|d| steps, contradicting all-index correctness. Bit 0 is reserved as a visible marker. The endmarker cannot repair the contradiction.

### Theorem 2 (efficient fixed universal U)
KT_U(D_L)<=poly(L): a fixed efficient indexed program, with L encoded in O(log L) bits, can decode i, boundedly simulate U on its assigned d, and compute the bit in poly(L) time, including end-marker checks. Thus D_L is HIGH at the boundary threshold p=L yet becomes LOW at every positive-power threshold p=M^epsilon for sufficiently large M. In particular p_241 eventually exceeds KT(D_L). This is a concrete counterexample to promoting the logarithmic diagonal into the required polynomial-KT semantic hardness. The upper bound assumes standard efficient universal simulation, not a deliberately exponentially padded interpreter. At p=L, even zero^M is not guaranteed LOW for an arbitrary U, because KT charges both description and time.

The crossing 2^(L/241)>L on the upper branch first occurs at integer L=2754. At all experimentally practical L<=12, p_241=L, so one cannot extrapolate finite high-at-log status.

## Witness-compatibility adversary
For r>=1 define C_r=(x0) AND [AND_{j=1}^r (NOT x_{j-1} OR x_j)] AND (NOT x_r). Expand each unit to four proper 3-clauses with two fresh variables and all auxiliary sign combinations. Expand each implication to two proper 3-clauses with a fresh third variable and both signs. Result Q_r has 2r+8 clauses, 2r+5 variables, one connected incidence component, maximum variable-occurrence degree 6, and is minimally UNSAT even after deleting any single expanded three-clause. Global UNSAT follows from the implication chain. Every deletion is SAT by satisfying all other original clauses and selecting the removed gadget's auxiliary assignment to falsify precisely the removed clause. All proper triples use distinct variables. Hence all strict clause subsets being SAT does not certify global compatibility.
