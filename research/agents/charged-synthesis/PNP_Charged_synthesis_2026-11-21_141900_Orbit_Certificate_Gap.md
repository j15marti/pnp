## Same-execution ledger and the repeated-reduction question

The verifier receives N instance bits and L certificate bits, both charged. It parses all headers and IDs, validates the permutation, constructs the actual source, performs every indexed name lookup, compares sorted triples with their multiplicities, and writes or verifies the count. A simple sole-tape implementation retains O(N) bits of instance, certificate, source and working records since each contains O(k) entries of O(log k) bits. Nested scans for all pairs of O(N) records, transporting O(log N)-bit names by local movement, fit a conservative O(N^4) time envelope together with the existing generator. Thus verifier physical CM is at most O(N^5). Its minimum-description CM uses replay with both inputs charged, O(N^4(N+L+log N)) for this implementation. These upper envelopes are separate from any lower demand on M.

The oracle search makes O(N) adaptive calls, each with the instance and certificate prefix of O(N) bits. Local prefix storage, query writing, rejected bit choices, and final verification all cost polynomial work. The computational cost of answering an NP query is not declared free for a standard solver; it is the explicit oracle resource of FP^NP. Compiler sharing and caches can reduce work and are allowed. Addresses, retained input and buffers, output, failed source candidates, representation changes and fallback are all paid. Hash equality never replaces the exact comparison.

For fixed M with T_M(N)≤KN^a, the same-run ceilings remain S=O(N^a), physical CM=O(N^(2a)), minimum-description CM=O(N^(a+1)), and simulated count-map size O(N^a log N). The verifier's O(N^4) exponent is an implementation upper envelope, not an excluded exponent or a bound on the cost of finding its certificate. The tape lower bound defeats a<2 only. No fixed-instance witness lower bound is substituted for the encoded map; no finite enumeration establishes asymptotic hardness.

The user's proposed repeated small reductions can be expressed here without counting imaginary independent obligations. Each private equality packet can be contracted with a known factor two once authenticated. After E contractions the representation is smaller by removing those gadgets, while the exact multiplier is 2^E. This exponential count factor requires only O(E) binary digits and can be processed compactly. On this family the residual core is also a product controlled by overlap and Hamming weight. Thus a large surviving assignment count or a large number of repeated contractions alone cannot establish expensive computation. A new lower-bound lemma must identify unavoidable work in acquiring or evaluating the reduction on the same execution. Neither unbounded trial search nor minimality under a chosen local rule proves this universal obligation.

Affine/Tseitin, private-leaf, matching-cycle, forced-domain and complement shortcuts remain legal controls. Renaming and slot permutations are handled exactly by the certificate. Different placements may destroy the particular original-cut argument while preserving count. A bound on one presentation remains scoped accordingly; moving data during a run has real cost. UNSAT certificates here authenticate a known zero-count canonical source; they are not claims of short certificates for all unsatisfiable formulas.
