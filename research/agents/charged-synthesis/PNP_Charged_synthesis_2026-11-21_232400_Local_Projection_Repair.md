## Fixed algorithms, quantifiers and falsifiers

Fix algorithm P4 before the input. It authenticates Construction's canonical image by extraction and exact bitwise regeneration, then applies the following procedure to the recovered proper 3-CNF source F. Each clause becomes its exact Boolean relation on at most three variables. At each iteration delete identically true factors, detect any empty relation as UNSAT, and choose the smallest live variable v whose incident factors have scope union W of size at most four. Every factor mentioning v must be included. Enumerate all at most 16 assignments to W, conjoin those factors, existentially project v, and replace its bucket by the resulting relation on W minus v. If no factors remain return SAT. If no variable is eligible return UNKNOWN from this shortcut. On authentication failure or UNKNOWN use a correct exhaustive decision procedure on the retained original input. Never return UNSAT merely because admission fails.

For every valid canonical input on which this elimination finishes, P4 returns the correct decision in O(N^5) time and O(N) occupied span, with physical and minimum-description CM each separately O(N^6). On every other valid input P4 remains correct by fallback, without a polynomial total-time claim. The shortcut's cost before fallback is polynomial on every input. There is no advice, supplied elimination order, guessed decomposition or uncharged parameter search.

For every even k>=4 and every s in {0,1}^k, P4 finishes on the family below. On these same sources a support-group affine filter followed by the pinned Discovery acquisition still spends at least (3^(k-1)-1)/2 search calls. The filter means exactly: group all clauses with the same variable support; accept for Gaussian solving only if each resulting local relation is affine over GF(2). Closure of a finite Boolean relation under ternary XOR is checked on its at most eight rows. This is a precise syntactic sufficient test. No claim is made about every possible hidden-affine recognizer, and a globally UNSAT relation is itself empty and affine.

Four falsifiers are: projecting a factor without joining all other factors sharing v; a family elimination bucket exceeding four variables; a pivot splitting the remaining private supports so that p<k-1; and accepting a purported compiler image without authenticating every original bit. ## Family, both-label semantics and pinned search

This is a complete generator of c=9k proper clauses on d=3k+1 variables. The incidence graph is connected.

A private block permits precisely 110, 011 and 101 on (x,y,z). For z=0, choose x=y=1; for z=1, choose (x,y)=(0,1) or (1,0). Conversely, if the sign parity is zero, choose g,z_1 freely and propagate z_(i+1)=z_i XOR g XOR s_i, then extend each private block as above. Hence SAT iff the sign parity is even. ## Projection proof and full cost ledger

For arbitrary factors, write F=G AND conjunction(bucket(v)), where v does not occur in G. Then existential-v F equals G AND existential-v conjunction(bucket(v)). The truth-table replacement is exactly this identity, so SAT status is preserved at every step. Projection of the empty relation detects UNSAT; projection of a true factor is safe to delete. Induction proves correctness on all admitted formulas. Scope size is an algorithmic check, not an assumed structural promise.

On this family the smallest-live-variable progression starts x_1,y_1,z_1,x_2,y_2,z_2,..., possibly omitting variables that disappear in true factors. Eliminating x_i uses scope {x_i,y_i,z_i} and leaves y_i OR z_i. Eliminating y_i then removes that constraint. Before eliminating z_i, its remaining cyclic neighbors are the next z and the last z_k, together with g. Their union with z_i has size at most four. Joining and projecting the two parity factors composes their parity equations on those remaining names. A canceled g may be retained as a dummy table coordinate; the union bound is unchanged. At the final closure the relation is false for odd sign parity and true for even parity. Thus no bucket exceeds four and no fallback occurs.

Use one writable finite-alphabet tape, local head moves, all input/work counted, head initially at origin and answer plus terminator at origin. For c source clauses and d occurring variables, r is the least odd integer greater than c and at least three.
