\section{Exact proper signed $(2,2)$ source, physical length, and local semantics}
Fix $m\ge1$ and $a,b\in\{0,1\}^m$, and original common source variables $x_1,\ldots,x_m$. The PC22 source $Q$ is the 19-clause proper ternary gadget $W\setminus\{(p\vee q\vee r)\}$ on 15 private names, with the exact theorem
\[\SAT(Q),\qquad Q\models(\neg p\land\neg q\land\neg r).\tag{1}\]
The explicit enumeration yields 244 satisfying assignments, every one with $p=q=r=0$. The transparent seven-clause $J(\ell)$ pads one signed occurrence while all five fresh auxiliaries may be zero, regardless of the truth of $\ell$. Define $s(1)=+1$, $s(0)=-1$ and the side block
\[B_s(x)=Q(p,q,r,U)\land(sx\vee p\vee q)\land(-sx\vee u\vee v)\land(r\vee z\vee w)\land\bigwedge_{y\in\{u,v,z,w\}}J(y)J(\neg y)J(\neg y),\tag{2}\]
where the $J$ occurrences have disjoint private names, and $sx$ denotes the signed literal. By (1), every model of $B_s(x)$ satisfies $sx$; conversely for $sx$ TRUE, assign all $Q$ variables zero, take $u=z=1$ and set every $J$-auxiliary to zero. Hence the full projected relation is exactly $sx$. Every side has 79 private variables, 106 proper distinct ternary clauses, and provides one positive and one negative occurrence of $x$; every private name is signed $(2,2)$.

For a nine-edge $K_{3,3}$ incidence block, use positive and negative clauses on each of its six degree-three vertex stars: 9 names, 12 clauses, all signed $(2,2)$. A checkerboard assignment satisfies both signs on every star. Insert $m$ disjoint such blocks as the physically long bit-independent GAP, between LEFT$(a)$ and RIGHT$(b)$. Then
\[E_m(a,b)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m B_{s(a_i)}^{L}(x_i)\ \land\ \mathrm{GAP}_m\ \land\ \bigwedge_{i=1}^m B_{s(b_i)}^{R}(x_i),\]
\[\boxed{\quad n=168m,\quad q=224m,\quad\SAT(E_m(a,b))\Longleftrightarrow a=b.\quad}\tag{3}\]
All names are source-identified and each signed occurrence count is exactly two. LEFT clauses depend only on $a$, GAP clauses on neither vector, RIGHT clauses only on $b$. Serialize a fixed-width ordinary signed clause encoding: unary prefix for identifier width $w=\lceil\log_2(168m+1)\rceil$, binary counts, then three literal records each containing a sign bit and a $w$-bit positive identifier. The fixed header has $3w+3$ bits and every clause exactly $3(w+1)$ bits. Thus
\[N_m=3w+3+672m(w+1)=\Theta(m\log(m+2)),\tag{4}\]
with identical physical length for all $a,b$ at fixed $m$. The proof applies to this declared encoding, not arbitrary DIMACS parser conventions.

\section{New source-local constant-record refutation and essentiality}
\begin{theorem}[Two-shore local contradiction and proof-size gap]\label{th:local}
For each $i$ with $a_i\ne b_i$, a subset $D_i$ of precisely 40 authentic original clauses is UNSAT: the 19 private $Q$ clauses and the single sign-forcing clause from each shore at index $i$. It admits a deterministic fixed-template 343-record Resolution refutation using 78 authenticated source introductions and 265 checked binary resolution steps, each with at most seven literals. Encoded source IDs, variable IDs, proof-line pointers and pivots require $O(\log N_m)$ bits; the entire explicit certificate has $O(\log N_m)$ bits with constants independent of $m$. Each accepted record is verifiable against the original source string, with no guest SAT calls or free UNSAT labels.

If $a$ and $b$ differ at exactly one index $i$, \emph{both} its LEFT and RIGHT sign-forcing clauses are essential original clauses of $E_m(a,b)$. Therefore every UNSAT original-clause subset, and every sound source-subformula A-SOURCE leaf, must include both sign clauses. This does not hold for arbitrary derived semantic children, and does not impose a RAM time lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By (1), the LEFT $Q$ clauses resolve to $\neg p_L$ and $\neg q_L$; combining with $(s(a_i)x_i\vee p_L\vee q_L)$ derives $s(a_i)x_i$. The same independently derives $s(b_i)x_i$ from RIGHT. For unequal bits they are complementary, and final resolution gives the empty clause. Construct these two Q-unit derivations by Davis--Putnam variable elimination over the other 14 internal names: every generated resolvent is recorded with its two earlier parents and opposite signed pivot. Duplicate/subsumed live clauses may be discarded, but their derivations remain in the explicit transcript. The fully specified deterministic pivot tie-breaking in the reproducer gives 343 total records, whose independently recomputed resolvents check exactly, reaching empty. Since the Q template contains only 15 private variables and 19 source clauses, even a nonoptimized exhaustive Resolution proof would have a finite absolute record bound; the reported 343 count is a checked exact template, not an asymptotic experimental extrapolation.

If the mismatch is unique, remove the LEFT forcing clause. Choose $x_i$ to satisfy RIGHT. All other matching $x_j$ obey both sides, and the remaining LEFT dummy/J/Q constraints extend for either value of $x_i$. The GAP also extends. Thus the clause-deleted formula becomes SAT. Symmetrically deleting the RIGHT forcing clause makes it SAT. Both are essential. Any UNSAT original-source subset must contain each essential clause, for otherwise it would be a subset of a satisfiable clause-deleted formula.\end{proof}

\section{Three explicitly separated machine ledgers}
The supplied certificate and a total deterministic selector are different computations. For an arbitrary SAT input, a sound general Resolution verifier must parse the entire ordinary encoding (rejecting malformed inputs), authenticate original clause IDs, check every record and the final empty clause. On the fixed-width language above this costs $N^{1+o(1)}$ bit time and $O(\log N)$ work in indexed RAM or a suitable fixed-tape streaming implementation; extracting a few selected records on the \emph{valid-canonical-promise} can instead be done in $\operatorname{polylog}N$ indexed time. The promise fast path is not a general verifier without a fully charged format check.

\paragraph{M1, deterministic one shared read/write tape.} $T_1$ counts all finite-state transitions and unchanged/changed writes; $P_1$ includes the original $N$-bit source plus a supplied certificate, $H_1$ counts one-head moves, $C_1$ actual crossings of named physical boundaries, $I_1$ inspected original cells, $L_1$ emitted bits. A local proof must authenticate clauses from both shores. Their forcing clauses lie at distance $\Theta(N)$ on the initial tape, hence even a source-identified verifier seeking both needs $H_1=\Omega(N)$ physical moves on this promise. An explicit scan-and-buffer verification gives $T_1=N^{1+o(1)}$ and $P_1=N+O(\log N)$ for this constant-record proof. Neither $P_1$ nor its crossing count is interchangeable with Mk work-only space.

\paragraph{Mk, fixed finite $k$ sequential work tapes plus ONE sequential read-only input head.} Count $T_k$ transitions, $S_k$ visited work cells (input excluded), $Q_k$ cell probes, $C_k$ input/work-head crossing events and $B_k$ actual carried bits when well-defined. Streaming the source and retaining the constant 40 selected clauses costs $T_k=N^{1+o(1)}$, $S_k=O(\log N)$; $Q_k,C_k=O(kT_k)$ are \emph{subcounters} of time, not an additional term. A source-authenticated proof may be guessed for co-nondeterministic SAT polarity only with uniform cap and all malformed/timeout branches clocked. The physical read-head motion remains charged even if proof length is logarithmic.

\paragraph{RAT, indexed read-only random-access input and indexed work storage.} Two distant sign-bit locations can be probed at polylogarithmic bit cost each, with $O(\log N)$ index bits and no physical head traverse. Checking an already syntactically validated local proof uses only a constant number of named source clauses, so no tape crossing lower bound survives. A total \emph{promise-family} selector probes corresponding $a_i,b_i$ positions for all $i$, outputs this proof on the first mismatch and ``none'' if equal, within $O(m\operatorname{polylog}N)=N^{1+o(1)}$ bit time and $O(\log N)$ workspace. It neither solves arbitrary SAT nor constructs all-UNSAT affordable certificates.

\paragraph{Deterministic acquisition is different.} A total certificate selector correct on all $E_m(a,b)$ decides equality by whether it emits an authenticated refutation. Therefore the previously verified exact22 tape-transport result applies to this selector: on M1 one-tape worst-case time is $\Omega(N^2/\log N)$ on some equality/yes cases; on Mk with one sequential input head the certified product inequality is $T_k(S_k+\log(T_k+N)+1)=\Omega(N^2/\log N)$ (native physical model). This is inherited from the governance construction and its crossing-sequence proof, not obtained merely from our local certificate's two source pointers. A coNTIME verifier may guess a mismatch certificate; a deterministic ARC compiler may not import that guess for free.

The conservative fixed-$k$ simulation into M1 has $T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)))$ and $P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))$ and does not transport a one-tape $N^2$ lower bound directly to Mk or RAT. In indexed FLMV notation the illustrative leaf-summation inequality $\sum m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V$ remains algebraically correct, but our constant-record Resolution proof has no guest-$A$ calls, so $M,V$ are inapplicable. When guest calls exist, all children must be proper encoded ordinary SAT, and acquisition/selection/probe replay and two hereditary levels are separately charged. A $P=NP$ hypothesis provides unknown finite $K$, not the unproved seed $d=1.802$.
