\begin{theorem}[Uniform Hall-to-PHP source extraction and mixed-proof upper bound]
There is one fixed polynomial $P$ such that, for every $n\ge2$ and every $a\in\{0,1\}^{n^2}$ for which $F_{n,a}$ is UNSAT, $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_{n,a})\le P(n)$. More precisely, for any Hall-deficient set of left vertices $S$ with $H=N(S)$ and $h=|H|<|S|$, let $P_0\subseteq S$ consist of any $h+1$ distinct vertices. There is a legal source-preserving RES derivation of a renamed \emph{complete} $\operatorname{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ source with at most
\[
\boxed{2(h+1)(n-h)}
\]
charged RES nodes. Following it with Cook's strict polynomial ER refutation gives
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_{n,a})
       \le 2(h+1)(n-h)+O((h+1)^4)=O(n^4).}
\]
For $h=0$ the extracted row itself is empty and no Cook refutation is needed. The constructions can be chosen uniformly from a maximum bipartite matching and Cook's fixed recursion; proof \emph{discovery and checking} are polynomial here but are not part of $W_R$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Hall's marriage theorem gives $S$ since no perfect matching exists. By definition of $H=N(S)$, for every $i\in S$ and every $j\notin H$, $a_{ij}=1$. Therefore both $(\neg p_{ij})$ and $(p_{ij}\lor\neg x_{ij})$ occur as independently authenticated source clauses. One binary RES node derives $\neg x_{ij}$, and a second RES node eliminates $x_{ij}$ from the original positive row clause. Repeat for all $j\notin H$, separately for each of the $h+1$ chosen rows. The result for each chosen $i$ is exactly the positive row clause $\bigvee_{j\in H}x_{ij}$, at cost $2(n-h)$, with no uncharged weakening and no semantic conversion. For every distinct chosen pair $i,k$ and every $j\in H$, the original signed source already contains $(\neg x_{ij}\lor\neg x_{kj})$. These rows and at-most-one sources, under the bijective renaming $(i,j)\mapsto(i',j')$, are \emph{exactly} the standard unary $\operatorname{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ axioms. Thus replay the constructive polynomial Extended Resolution refutation from Cook 1976. The pre-established strict ER-to-$R_{\rm mix341}$ translation (LSPV-3/374, blob \texttt{ac9e4595}) replaces each fresh binary extension gate by one paid conservative signed DEF with clause views and each RES step by a paid RES node, preserving the DAG and source. Cook's three-input-looking auxiliary expressions factor into a constant number of binary signed gates; the total has polynomial overhead. For $h=0$ the complete derived row is the empty clause. Every source identity and inference is charged exactly as declared; shared nodes are never counted repeatedly. Therefore the displayed upper bound holds for \emph{all} UNSAT masks. A standard augmenting-path matching computation yields either a perfect matching or the Hall witness $S$ by alternating reachability from unmatched left vertices; the latter is verified by checking $|N(S)|<|S|$. 
\end{proof}
