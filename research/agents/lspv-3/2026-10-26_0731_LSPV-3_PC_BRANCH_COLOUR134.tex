\section{Typed source-authenticated hybrid grammar}
Fix a canonical physical ordinary proper signed 3CNF $F=C_1\wedge\cdots\wedge C_m$ of bit length $N$. A complete \emph{unshared explicit binary tree} is constructed using $\mathsf{BRANCH}(x,P_0,P_1)$ on an as-yet unassigned \emph{original} source name with both children checked. For each root-to-leaf restriction $\alpha$, a terminal certifies contradiction from authentic source clause byte offsets under the \emph{same} ancestor assignment, using exactly one of the following admissible formats:
\begin{description}
\item[] $\mathsf{EMPTY}$: a physically authenticated falsified original clause.
\item[] $\mathsf{PATH}$: the paired implication paths of ADAPTIVE-SOURCE-POINTER-STREAM131, each implication justified by an authenticated source residual unit or binary clause, with unrelated surviving source ternaries permitted.
\item[] $\mathsf{PAIR\mbox{-}XOR}$: original-source four-clause parity quartets plus reciprocal equal-original-name slot pairing and odd charge, as audited in SOURCE-PAIRED-AFFINE132, over $\mathbb F_2$.
\item[] $\mathsf{RES}$ or $\mathsf{PCR}$: a supplied, line-by-line syntactically verified Resolution or polynomial-calculus-with-resolution proof of the residual source clauses, in a \emph{single fixed field} $\mathbb F_2$. Each field element, term, line, source offset, multiplication, addition and premise reference is explicitly encoded. Source-derived axioms must be verified as derivations, not claimed semantically for free.
\end{description}
An arbitrary clause-subset refutation may ignore surviving unrelated source clauses, but the premises actually used must match the current source restrictions. Malformed paths, mixed source names, illegitimate cuts, false empty clauses, invalid algebraic lines, timeouts and incomplete branches reject. The larger candidate grammar allowing cutting planes, general semantic lifts, multiple fields in one tree, compressed exponentially long proofs, source-sharing DAGs, or total exact $A$ leaves is \emph{not} claimed covered. $A$ is not called here.

Let $B$ be the number of branch nodes, $L=B+1$ the number of leaves, $E$ the source-edge or proof inference records, and $P$ the complete physical certificate length (including explicit PCR monomial lists, names, offsets, coefficients, headers and field). Every accepted tree is sound because all leaf contradictions follow from original clauses restricted by that same $\alpha$ and both branch values are covered. DPLL on all variables plus authentic EMPTY leaves gives completeness, but only with possibly exponential $P$; no polynomial all-UNSAT coverage is being assumed.

\section{Restriction-gated lifting in characteristic-two PCR}
For every Boolean variable $x$, PCR has formal complementary symbols $x,\bar x$, complement equation $x+\bar x-1=0$, annihilation equation $x\bar x=0$, and Boolean idempotence consequences. The authentic source-clause falsity axiom is the monomial
\[
 p_C=\prod_{\ell\in C}\overline\ell=0,
\tag{1}
\]
where $\overline\ell$ is the formal opposite of the literal $\ell$. For a partial assignment $\alpha$ of length $h$, put
\[
 \delta_\alpha=\prod_{\alpha(x)=1}x\prod_{\alpha(x)=0}\bar x.
\tag{2}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Restriction-gated PCR proof transport]\label{lem:lift}
Let $F$ have source width at most three. If $F|\alpha$ has an explicit PCR refutation over the same fixed field of maximum degree $k$, then $F$ has a PCR refutation of degree at most
\[
 \boxed{\deg_{\PCR}(F)\le h+\max\{3,k\}.}\tag{3}
\]
This theorem concerns degree, not a claimed polynomial bound on the size of the globally lifted proof.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the Boolean quotient, each assigned variable equals its assigned constant when multiplied by $\delta_\alpha$. Hence for every source polynomial $p_C$,
\[\delta_\alpha p_C=\delta_\alpha(p_C|\alpha).\tag{4}\]
This identity has an explicit PCR derivation using $x\bar x=0$, $x+\bar x-1=0$ and idempotence, by eliminating assigned source factors one at a time; its degree is at most $h+3$. Start from each original clause axiom and multiply its factors by the $h$ factors of $\delta_\alpha$. Under (4), the product is the restriction-gated residual source axiom. Restricting a formula cannot introduce non-source clauses, and a source clause satisfied by $\alpha$ yields zero. Apply $\delta_\alpha$ to every line of the local PCR refutation. Linear combinations remain linear combinations and multiplication by one literal remains a valid PCR multiplication. The degree of each lifted line is at most $h+k$ (counting the preceding axiom bridge gives $h+\max\{3,k\}$). The lifted final line is exactly $\delta_\alpha\cdot1=\delta_\alpha$. This derives $\delta_\alpha=0$ from the original source. In particular, no claim about a source-to-child semantic lifting oracle was made: all identities are derivable in PCR from authenticated source polynomial axioms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Branch merge and local-terminal simulation]\label{lem:merge}
At a branch on unassigned original name $x$,
\[
 \delta_{\alpha,x=0}+\delta_{\alpha,x=1}
 =\delta_\alpha(\bar x+x)=\delta_\alpha.\tag{5}
\]
Thus if both child PCR derivations end in their indicators, a parent indicator is derivable without increasing the maximum degree beyond the greater child depth plus one. Every authenticated EMPTY, PATH and PAIR-XOR terminal has an $\mathbb F_2$ PCR refutation of degree at most three and size polynomial in its \emph{explicit} source-edge record count. A supplied ordinary Resolution proof has a polynomial-size PCR simulation, while an explicit PCR leaf is already a PCR proof.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Equation (5) follows by multiplying the standard complement axiom by $\delta_\alpha$. EMPTY is the residual empty-clause polynomial $1=0$. An implication edge $u\to v$ is the residual axiom $u(1-v)=0$ of degree at most two, or follows from a unit axiom. Composing $u\to v$ and $v\to w$ uses the literal degree-three polynomial identity
\[
 u(1-w)=u(1-v)(1-w)+uv(1-w),\tag{6}
\]
so source-authenticated implication paths yield $x=0$ and $1-x=0$ at degree at most three, hence $1=0$. Characteristic-two four-clause parity quartets yield the linear equation by the exact identity
\[
 \sum_{w:\oplus_j w_j\ne b}\prod_j(X_j+1+w_j)
 =X_1+X_2+X_3+b\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_2[X]/\langle X^2-X\rangle.\tag{7}
\]
Adding source-paired equations cancels every identically named literal and yields $0=1$, so PAIR-XOR has degree three and linear-size PCR. Ordinary Resolution $C\vee x,D\vee\neg x\vdash C\vee D$ becomes a constant number of PCR additions and multiplications (by the monomial factors for $C,D$, and $x+\bar x-1$), with polynomially many literal-by-literal multiplications in the \emph{explicit} Resolution proof length. In PCR the complementary literals remain formal symbols, so no exponentially expanded $(1-x)$ product is required. No polynomial simulation claim is made for general cutting planes or an exact SAT-decider call.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Depth versus leaf degree.} Write $D(F)$ for minimum PCR degree. Lemma~\ref{lem:lift} gives, for every terminal at depth $h$ with an actual source-audited PCR simulation of degree $k$,
\[
\boxed{h+\max(3,k)\ge D(F).}\tag{8}
\]
The prior shorthand $h+k$ is legitimate when $k\ge3$; without that qualification it can fail on width-three sources. A tree with many deep branches may still have a short path, so (8) alone does not prove an exponential tree. The next theorem closes that gap by applying the \emph{size-degree theorem to any shallow leaf}.

\section{A proper signed ternary transfer of all-field graph-colouring hardness}
For a graph $G=(V,E)$ with $v=|V|$, use original Boolean names $x_{i,c}$, $i\in V$, $c\in\{1,2,3\}$. The standard colouring CNF contains
\[ (x_{i,1}\vee x_{i,2}\vee x_{i,3}),\quad
 (\neg x_{i,c}\vee\neg x_{i,c'}),\quad
 (\neg x_{i,c}\vee\neg x_{j,c})\ (ij\in E).\tag{9}
\]
For each binary clause $a\vee b$, introduce an individually new named variable $z_{a,b}$ and replace the clause with both proper ternaries
\[(a\vee b\vee z_{a,b}),\quad(a\vee b\vee\neg z_{a,b}).\tag{10}\]
Let $H_G$ be the resulting physically ordinary, proper signed exact-width-three CNF, and $Q_G$ the standard mixed-width colouring CNF. If $G$ has $v$ vertices and $e$ edges, the actual source counts are
\[
 \boxed{n(H_G)=6v+3e,\qquad m(H_G)=7v+6e.}\tag{11}
\]
For bounded-degree graphs $e=\Theta(v)$, its fully encoded canonical decimal-name DIMACS length is $N=\Theta(v\log v)$.

\begin{lemma}[Fresh-zero proof-preserving properization]\label{lem:proper}
For each fixed field, \(H_G\) is SAT iff \(Q_G\) is SAT, and
\[\boxed{D_{\PCR}(H_G)\ge D_{\PCR}(Q_G).}\tag{12}\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two clauses in (10) are equivalent under existential quantification of the fresh $z$ to $a\vee b$. More strongly, the \emph{single uniform restriction} setting every fresh $z=0$ sends the first clause to $a\vee b$, makes the second clause satisfied, and fixes every original colour variable. The clauses of (9) of length three are unchanged. Apply the same substitution to every line of any PCR refutation of $H_G$: initial source axioms become source axioms of $Q_G$ or zero, complement and Boolean axioms stay valid or become zero, and polynomial inference rules remain valid under substitution. Degree cannot rise. Thus every PCR refutation of $H_G$ would give a PCR refutation of $Q_G$ of no greater degree. This proves (12) without asserting that a generic NP reduction preserves proof complexity.
\end{proof}

\section{Exponential hybrid certificate barrier}
Conneryd, de Rezende, Nordstr\"om, Pang and Risse (FOCS 2023; full version 2025) prove that for fixed $d\ge6$, sparse random $d$-regular graphs have, with probability tending to one, $\Omega(v)$ PC refutation degree over \emph{any fixed field} for standard 3-colourability. Their Section~2.2 explicitly states that the bound applies to the standard CNF translation (not merely the linear-polynomial vertex axiom). Their size-degree theorem is also valid for the stronger PCR proof system. For our theorem fix $\mathbb F_2$; on an infinite connected UNSAT subfamily, there is $\delta>0$ with
\[
 D_{\PCR}(H_{G_v})\ge \delta v.\tag{13}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Shallow-leaf/deep-tree dichotomy; falsification of polynomial coverage]\label{th:barrier}
On infinitely many \emph{connected} proper signed exact-width-three UNSAT inputs $H_{G_v}$, every sound, accepted \emph{explicit unshared} $\mathbb F_2$ source-authenticated BRANCH/EMPTY/PATH/PAIR-XOR/RES/PCR hybrid tree has certificate bit length
\[
\boxed{P(H_{G_v})\ge 2^{\Omega(v)}
 =2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}.}\tag{14}
\]
Therefore no fixed polynomial $N^{a+o(1)}$ is an all-UNSAT worst-case certificate length cap for this precisely declared grammar. This includes the proposed hypothetical $a<1.4008217497$ guest-volume target when there are no guest calls. It does \emph{not} cover cutting planes, stronger redundancies, mixed-field leaves, generalized proof DAGs, or $A$ calls.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $D=D_{\PCR}(H_{G_v})\ge\delta v$, and write $n'=n(H_{G_v})=\Theta(v)$. Choose $v$ large so $D/2>3$. If an accepted proof has a leaf at depth $h\le D/2$, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:lift} to a \emph{minimum-degree} PCR refutation of the restricted source, not merely to the offered terminal. It follows that its \emph{minimum} degree $k_{\min}\ge D-h\ge D/2$. The Impagliazzo--Pudl\'ak--Sgall PCR size-degree theorem, in the form used in the cited 2025 graph-colouring paper, yields for \emph{every} PCR refutation of the restricted source
\[
 S_\ell\ge\exp\!\left(\Omega\!\left(\frac{(k_{\min}-3)^2}{n'}\right)\right)
 =\exp(\Omega(v)).\tag{15}
\]
Here every source clause after restriction has width at most three, $n'\le O(v)$, the proof is explicitly encoded, and each term/line consumes at least one certificate bit. EMPTY/PATH/PAIR-XOR/RES all have polynomial-size PCR simulations by Lemma~\ref{lem:merge}, so any small such leaf is also ruled out. Thus a shallow leaf already has $P\ge\exp(\Omega(v))$.

Otherwise every leaf has depth $>D/2$. Because every BRANCH has exactly two children, a full binary tree with minimum leaf depth $>D/2$ has at least $2^{\lfloor D/2\rfloor}$ distinct leaves. Each is explicitly encoded and read, giving $P\ge 2^{\Omega(v)}$. Both cases establish (14). An inference that a low-degree source proof necessarily has low time for an arbitrary decision algorithm is \emph{not} used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Strict failure of the narrow unshared hybrid all-UNSAT coNTIME route]
Every nondeterministic verifier which explicitly guesses/reads this hybrid certificate must charge at least $P$ bit steps on a corresponding accepting branch. Hence no \(N^{c+o(1)}\) length-only uniform clock for any fixed $c$ covers all UNSAT inputs \emph{via this grammar}. The statement does not rule out another stronger co-nondeterministic algorithm or prove a SAT decision-time lower bound. Conditional near-boundary two-algorithm composition cannot be instantiated from this certificate grammar.
\end{corollary}

\section{Charged controls, tests, and exact nonimplications}
An independently implemented, dependency-free Python audit generated six source-canonical graph-colouring instances and serialized/reparsed every proper physical ternary clause. The instances are $K_3$ (SAT), $K_4$ (UNSAT), $K_{3,3}$ (SAT), $K_7$ (UNSAT), and connected six-regular circulants of orders eight and nine (both UNSAT). For each instance, an independent graph 3-colour search agreed with actual DPLL on the proper encoded CNF, and setting every auxiliary of (10) to zero reconstructed \emph{exactly} the original colour-constraint clauses. Over both $\mathbb F_2$ and $\mathbb F_5$, every lifted proper source binary-clause polynomial restricted at $z=0$ equalled its authentic base axiom while the paired opposite clause became zero. A two-level original-name PIN descendant of the $K_3$ source had actual byte lengths $450\to494\to538$ and both-label trace SAT,SAT,UNSAT. A separately read and independently checked authentic signed exact-$(2,2)$ source $W_1$ had 15 variables, 20 proper ternaries, four occurrences per name (two of each polarity), was UNSAT, and had all 20 one-clause deletions SAT. It is a \emph{control}, not a transported graph-colouring lower-bound witness.

Over both fields, 700 seeded randomized degree-three polynomial/assignment identity tests checked $\delta_\alpha p=\delta_\alpha(p|\alpha)$ and 700 two-child merges. The degree-three implication-composition identity and characteristic-two four-clause parity identity also passed. The complete audit checked \textbf{5,817 assertion groups, zero failures} and two independent runs produced the same raw SHA-256 \texttt{b40579712308c6514abbe71902ea2375f98c3b7e1fac059de39dfda82a9e14e2}. These tests establish only finite identities/encodings; no asymptotic random-graph PCR hardness or indexed-machine time is inferred from their execution.

The proof has no guest $A$ calls, so $M,V$ and $\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V$ do not supply an escape or numerical speedup here. On routes with actual $A$ children, physical sizes, per-probe source access, materialization, field coefficients, oracle execution, $B+L+E$, failed policies, nondeterministic proof-guess clock, deterministic discovery, and \emph{peak} simultaneously alive writable/index storage must all be paid. General PCR proof verification may use much more work space than PATH; the earlier AVL $O(h\log N)$ bound is not claimed to handle arbitrary large PCR lines. The $\mathbb F_2$ proof cannot rule out a mixture of terminals from incompatible fields without another common-field simulation. In the original signed exact-$(2,2)$ subclass, source variables have two occurrences of each polarity, whereas (10) has fresh $z$ with one of each. An NP-completeness regularizer does not automatically transport PCR degree hardness.
