\section{Claim audited and exact model}
After eliminating the affine equations $B_Xx=c$, write the remaining system
as $Q(u,v,a+Lz)$.  The precise candidate challenged here was:
\begin{quote}
If three genuinely shared-variable quadratic residual equations have
unbounded projected interface rank and every nonzero linear combination of
their polar matrices is nonsingular, then their satisfiable instances force
unavoidable work for every uniform polynomial-workspace witness finder.
\end{quote}
One explicit infinite family and one uniform polynomial-time finder refute
that universal implication.  The input is explicit: a graph $X$, spanning
forest $T$, charge vector $c$, a residual dimension divisible by six, an
invertible affine coordinate map, and a three-bit right-hand side.  Parsing,
validation, affine inversion, forest reconstruction, verification, and the
full edge-assignment output are charged in encoded bit length.  Invalid
encodings and charge vectors with odd component parity are rejected.

The project endpoint remains much stronger: a common satisfiable input must
defeat the entire eligible short-program portfolio at each UWI budget, or a
fixed NP-complete language must have algorithm-dependent hard inputs against
every total uniform decider and every polynomial exponent.  No such statement
is proved here.

\section{Primary-literature and transfer preflight}
Gupta and Mandal construct large constant-rank subspaces of alternating
bilinear forms in which every nonzero member is nondegenerate under stated
field-theoretic hypotheses \cite{GM}.  Gow gives rank--dimension results for
subspaces of bilinear forms \cite{Gow}.  These are directly relevant to the
\emph{existence and structure} of nonsingular alternating pencils.  Neither
paper states that evaluating, satisfying, or finding one preimage of the
associated quadratic map requires superpolynomial time.  That proposed move
is therefore a failed model transfer, and the present finite base block makes
the failure explicit over $\F$.

The same obstacle appears in coding-theoretic quadratic-relation attacks:
rank spectra and relation spaces are exact algebraic objects, but visible
structure can be an algorithmic weakness.  No current theorem found converts
full rank of all nonzero pencil members, DNNF size, proof size, cutwidth, or a
pebbling lower bound into unrestricted multitape/RAM witness-search time.
Any such transfer must allow compact circuits, Gaussian elimination, full
source access, and polynomial workspace on the same realization.

\section{The explicit base block}
Order the fifteen pairs $0\le i<j<6$ lexicographically.  For a fifteen-bit
mask $m$, define
\[
 q_m(z)=\bigoplus_{(i,j):m_{ij}=1}z_i z_j.
\]
Use the three masks
\[
 m_1=16692,\qquad m_2=3954,\qquad m_3=18969.              \tag{1}
\]
Let $M_i$ be the alternating polar matrix of $q_{m_i}$.

\begin{theorem}[Three-pencil firewall]
For the masks in (1):
\begin{enumerate}
\item every $\lambda\in\F^3\setminus\{0\}$ satisfies
$\operatorname{rank}(\lambda_1M_1+\lambda_2M_2+\lambda_3M_3)=6$;
\item the joint quadratic map
$q=(q_{m_1},q_{m_2},q_{m_3}):\F^6\to\F^3$ is surjective;
\item for outputs $0,1,\ldots,7$ (least significant bit first), the integers
\[
 0,9,5,6,3,48,12,33                                      \tag{2}
\]
encode valid preimages, each of Hamming weight at most two.
\end{enumerate}
For $t\ge1$, the blockwise XOR of $t$ copies has dimension $6t$, every
nonzero polar combination has rank $6t$, and the selectors (2), placed in
the first block with zeros elsewhere, remain valid.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exact $\F_2$ row reduction of the seven nonzero combinations gives ranks
\[
 (6,6,6,6,6,6,6).
\]
Exhaustive evaluation of the sixty-four base inputs gives fiber cardinalities
\[
 (15,7,7,7,7,7,7,7),                                    \tag{3}
\]
and direct substitution verifies (2).  These are finite exact certificates
checked independently by the accompanying dependency-free program.  For
block repetition, each polar combination is block diagonal with $t$ copies
of its nonsingular $6\times6$ base matrix, so ranks add.  Because the joint
quadratic output is the XOR of block outputs, putting a base selector in one
block and zeros in the rest realizes the requested output.
\end{proof}

The finite base assertions are \COMP{} pending independent review of the
listed masks and verifier; the displayed direct-sum consequence is a
\CAND{} symbolic theorem until that review is complete.  This discipline
avoids promoting a computational certificate beyond its verified scope.

\section{Dense affine mixing and Tseitin lifting}
Let $d=6t$ and assume $d/2$ is even.  Define
\[
 A=I+\mathbf 1v^T,
 \qquad v=(1^{d/2},0^{d/2}).                              \tag{4}
\]
Then $v^T\mathbf1=d/2=0$ in $\F_2$, hence $A^2=I$.  Compose (4) with an
arbitrary coordinate permutation $P$ and phase $r$.  Given requested output
$b\in\F^3$, take its fixed selector $w_b$ from (2) and compute
\[
 y=A(P^{-1}(w_b+r)).                                      \tag{5}
\]
The transformed residual evaluates the original block map at $PAy+r=w_b$.
Congruence by the invertible linear part preserves the rank of every polar
combination.  Thus apparently dense dependence does not remove the selector.

For $X=K_{s,s}$ with odd $s$, use the star-of-stars spanning tree.  Its
cotree dimension is
\[
 d=(s-1)^2,
\]
which is divisible by six for $s=6k+1$.  Every cotree assignment extends
uniquely through a consistent Tseitin charge vector: subtract its cotree
incidences and process each tree leaf-to-root.  Equation (5), followed by this
forest lift, is a uniform polynomial-time, polynomial-workspace witness map.
It reads the actual charge and produces the complete edge assignment; it does
not assume a hidden source witness or advice.

\begin{corollary}
There is an explicit family with projected rank $d\to\infty$, three
quadratic equations, and a three-dimensional alternating matrix space whose
seven nonzero members all have rank $d$, yet every right-hand side has a
uniform polynomial-time Tseitin witness.
\end{corollary}

This does not solve arbitrary MQ.  It retires only the inference
\[
 \bigl[\forall\lambda\ne0:\operatorname{rank}M(\lambda)=d\bigr]
 \Longrightarrow \text{unavoidable witness-search work}.               \tag{6}
\]
High projected rank remains an enumeration parameter, not a lower bound.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
FULLRANKQFW1 already retired one full-rank quadratic equation as a hardness
premise.  PENCILFW1 strengthened this to two forms whose three nonzero polar
combinations are nonsingular.  The new contribution is not another one-form
example: it gives three forms, verifies all seven nonzero combinations, and
retains a constant-support selector after dense affine/Tseitin lifting.
Repository search found no earlier report with these masks or this exact
three-dimensional pencil certificate.

There is no contradiction with projected-rank enumeration: the upper bound
$2^\rho\operatorname{poly}(N)$ is generic, while this family uses extra
structure to do better.  There is no contradiction with NP-completeness of
general MQ, exact-(2,2) SAT, or restricted DNNF/PC lower bounds; those concern
larger input classes or different models.
