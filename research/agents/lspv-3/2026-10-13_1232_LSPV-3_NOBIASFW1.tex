\section{Primary literature and exact theorem scope}
Conneryd, de Rezende, Nordstr\"om, Pang, and Risse prove that sparse random
regular and Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi graph-colouring formulas require linear
Polynomial Calculus degree over every field, hence strongly exponential
expanded-monomial proof size \cite{conneryd}.  Their Corollary 6.2 states for
random \(d\)-regular graphs a degree lower bound in the regime
\(6d^3\le\log h\); for \(d\ge6\), the same discussion invokes asymptotic
non-3-colourability.  This is a \emph{directly transferable theorem} through
the verified variable/zero projection, within PC/PCR.

The source paper itself does not claim an arbitrary decision-time lower bound.
The earlier Lauria--Nordstr\"om worst-case construction makes the model
separation especially explicit: its PC-hard graph-colouring instances have
polynomial-size Cutting Planes refutations \cite{lauria}.  This is a direct
counterexample to any representation-independent inference from PC hardness.
Average-case complexity also requires a distributional decision problem, not
merely certificates that are hard in one system.  A worst-case/average-case
bridge for this fixed NP language would be a new, load-bearing theorem.

\section{NO-biased distribution firewall}
Let \(G_h\) be a uniformly sampled simple 6-regular graph on \(h\) vertices in
the source theorem's admissible subsequence.  Let \(K_G=U(P(F_G))\) denote the
transparent properization and exact-((2,2)) compiler from PC22\_ALLFIELD1,
and let \(\mathcal D_h\) be the pushforward distribution of \(K_{G_h}\).

\begin{theorem}[NO-biased pushforward]
Assume the cited non-3-colourability conclusion and the verified exact
satisfiability identity
\[
                 K_G\in\Ltwo\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
                 G\text{ is 3-colourable}.                \tag{2}
\]
Then
\[
          \Pr_{K\sim\mathcal D_h}[K\in\Ltwo]=o(1).         \tag{3}
\]
Consequently the constant algorithm \(R(K)=0\) has distributional error
\(o(1)\) on \(\mathcal D_h\), even though with probability \(1-o(1)\) every
PC/PCR refutation of \(K\) has exponential expanded-monomial size over every
field.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By (2), the event \(K_{G_h}\in\Ltwo\) is exactly the event that \(G_h\) is
3-colourable.  The source non-colourability theorem makes this event have
probability (o(1)), proving (3).  The constant rejector errs precisely on
this event.  The source PC degree theorem and verified proof projection apply
on the complementary high-probability event and are logically compatible
with the trivial decision rule.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Average-case promotion retired]
The implication
\[
 \text{PC/PCR-hard with high probability under }\mathcal D_h
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \text{decision-hard on average under }\mathcal D_h
\]
is \RET{}.  A balanced or otherwise nontrivial decision distribution would
still need an unconditional hardness theorem and a SAME\_REALIZATION bridge.
\end{corollary}

This result does not refute worst-case existence of hard inputs, does not give
an exact solver for \(\Ltwo\), and does not weaken the PC/PCR theorem.  It
pinpoints why a known-NO benchmark family cannot carry the required
all-decider quantifiers.

\section{Barrier, advice, and post-quantum audit}
The firewall relativizes and algebraizes.  It gives no nonrelativizing hinge
and defines no useful constructive circuit property, so it does not evade
Natural Proofs.  The constant rejector uses no advice; recognizing a finite
or padded benchmark is unnecessary because the distribution itself places
vanishing mass on YES.  Any balanced repair must prohibit a trivial tag or
recognizable component that reveals the answer.

Proof verification, proof search, and decision remain distinct.  Any external
extraction run must charge source acquisition, decoding, replay, randomness,
all solver calls, and joint workspace.  The post-quantum Schur-square,
quadratic-relation, Hilbert/syzygy, collision-entropy, and special-soundness
prompts are \textbf{NOT APPLICABLE} to the one-sided distribution defect.
Worst-case/average-case reduction architecture is a useful
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}, but known NP barriers mean that the reduction is
precisely the missing theorem, not a free transfer.
