\section*{Fixed hard starting point and forbidden transfer}
Haken's 1985 theorem uses the \emph{unary} pigeonhole CNFs $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$: $h(h+1)$ variables $p_{ij}$, $h+1$ positive length-$h$ occupancy clauses and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative binary collision clauses, encoded as explicit signed ASCII DIMACS with $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. Its proof-length statement has quantifiers
\[
 \exists c>0\,\exists h_0\,\forall h\ge h_0\,
 \forall\pi\in\mathrm{OrdinaryResolutionRefutations}(P_h):
 \quad |\pi|\ge 2^{ch}.
\]
The quantifier over $\pi$ includes arbitrary shared Resolution DAGs; $|\pi|$ counts inferred lines. It does not quantify over Extended Resolution, $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$, $\Rmix$ or uniform general SAT algorithms. Cook's polynomial Extended Resolution PHP construction is an explicit stronger-system escape. Odd/even bounded-degree Tseitin, with polynomial native GF$(2)$ handling, remains an affine shortcut control. No new hard family was constructed, and neither Haken nor Cook was experimentally reproved.

The role's present question is more local and precise: when a signed parity relation is made conditional on a new guard, can arbitrary \emph{affine-only} auxiliary variables, shared XOR proof DAGs and implicit affine representations preserve it without a genuinely nonlinear interface? This is a representation-equivalence question, not a shortest-proof or unrestricted machine-time claim.

\section*{Exact semantic interface and model}
Fix $n\ge1$, $0\ne a\in\mathbb F_2^n$, $b\in\mathbb F_2$, and a fresh guard $t$. Define
\[
 L_{a,b}(x) = a\cdot x\oplus b,\qquad
 Q_{a,b}(t,x)=[t=0]\lor[a\cdot x=b].
\]
Here $L=1$ is the violation bit. A \emph{pure-affine representation} is any finite conjunction of affine equations over $(t,x,z)\in\mathbb F_2^{n+1+r}$, where any number $r\ge0$ of fresh auxiliary wires are existentially projected away. Equations may be represented lazily by arbitrary shared XOR DAGs; the represented solution relation and all auxiliary defining equations remain affine. This is not the complete $\Rmix$ grammar, which also contains nonlinear conservative gates and source-authenticated local rules.

In the XOR--AND circuit model, XOR, constants, affine wire identities and NOT are free for the purpose of counting \emph{nonlinear AND gates}; each binary AND is charged one unit. This multiplicative-complexity measure is distinct from both the paid-node convention of \Rmix{} and encoded physical work.

\begin{theorem}[Affine-projection impossibility and one nonlinear gate]
For all $n\ge1$, all nonzero $a\in\mathbb F_2^n$ and all $b\in\mathbb F_2$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] (i) $Q_{a,b}$ is not affine. In fact it has exactly $3\cdot2^{n-1}$ satisfying $(t,x)$ pairs and fails the three-point XOR closure identity. Thus \emph{no} pure-affine existential representation, with any number of fresh shared/lazy affine wires, defines $Q_{a,b}$.
\item[] (ii) The violation function $v_{a,b}(t,x)=t\cdot(a\cdot x\oplus b)$ has algebraic normal form degree exactly two. Its unrestricted XOR--AND multiplicative complexity equals \emph{one}, with arbitrary fan-out and sharing permitted.
\item[] (iii) One can encode $Q_{a,b}$ with affine parity computation for $L$, one nonlinear AND wire $w=t\land L$, and the asserted output constraint $w=0$. If only paid signed two-input AND definitions and signed negations are used to construct the parity network, an explicit implementation needs $3(\mathrm{wt}(a)-1)+1$ AND2 gates plus the output constraint. This is an \emph{encoding} statement, not a claim that the output constraint is a free derivation step from arbitrary authenticated original sources.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $t=0$ every one of the $2^n$ data assignments is allowed; at $t=1$ precisely $2^{n-1}$ assignments solve the nonzero linear equation. Their sum is $3\cdot 2^{n-1}$, not a power of two. A nonempty affine subspace over $\mathbb F_2$, and every coordinate projection of an affine subspace, has power-of-two cardinality. This proves (i) even if the affine extension has exponentially many wires.

For an explicit three-point witness, choose an index $i$ with $a_i=1$, some $x_0$ satisfying $a\cdot x_0=b$, and $x_1=x_0\oplus e_i$. The three allowed points $(0,x_0)$, $(0,x_1)$, $(1,x_0)$ have XOR $(1,x_1)$, which is forbidden. Their existence also rules out non-affine-looking \emph{encodings} consisting only of affine constraints.

For (ii), $v=t(\bigoplus_{a_i=1}x_i\oplus b)$ contains a nonzero quadratic monomial $tx_i$ in its unique Boolean algebraic normal form, hence cannot be computed by zero nonlinear gates. One AND gate whose two inputs are the affine wires $t$ and $L$ computes $v$, proving sharpness. For (iii), an XOR of two signed literals $u\oplus v$ is obtained with three conservative signed AND2 gates: $p=u\land\neg v$, $q=\neg u\land v$, $r=\neg p\land\neg q$, then the signed literal $\neg r$ is $u\oplus v$. Fold the $\mathrm{wt}(a)$ selected inputs using $3(\mathrm{wt}(a)-1)$ gates, complement for $b=1$, form one final $w=t\land L$, and require $\neg w$. A direct truth-table check confirms existential equivalence to $Q_{a,b}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Failure of rowwise additive nonlinear cost]
There is no universally valid lower bound of one nonlinear AND per \emph{guarded source row} after conjunction and semantic simplification. For every nonzero $a$, the conjunction
\[
 Q_{a,0}(t,x)\land Q_{a,1}(t,x)\quad\equiv\quad(t=0)
\]
is affine and has zero XOR--AND multiplicative complexity as a characteristic predicate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At $t=0$ both conditions are vacuous. At $t=1$ the two inconsistent affine equations cannot both hold. Thus the relation is exactly the hyperplane $t=0$. An algorithm or strong proof system may share, cancel or semantically combine the two source constraints. A lower bound that adds independent per-row nonlinear interface costs would overcount.
\end{proof}

\section*{Falsification ledger and controls}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{F1.} Unlimited affine auxiliaries, even represented by exponentially large shared XOR DAGs, might existentially encode a single genuinely guarded nonconstant parity condition: \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} by affine projection closure and the three-point counterexample.
\item[] \textbf{F2.} A signed literal substitution or source-variable renaming eliminates that obstruction: \textbf{FALSIFIED} for invertible signed renamings, since the number of models and affine closure are invariant.
\item[] \textbf{F3.} Each guarded affine source row contributes one unavoidable nonlinear gate to the \emph{global} minimized source relation: \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the opposing pair in the lemma. Source provenance alone does not imply additive intrinsic work.
\item[] \textbf{F4.} The degree-two guard interface forces superpolynomial physical work, or a superpolynomial $\Rmix$ derivation cost: \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the displayed $O(\mathrm{wt}(a))$ explicit conservative circuit. The source-to-proof transformation, verification and output costs must still be charged.
\end{enumerate}

The executable tested all $240$ nonzero signed affine rows for $1\le n\le6$ and $b\in\{0,1\}$, all $21{,}336$ $(t,x)$ valuations, $240$ explicit three-point closure counterexamples, $10{,}668$ $t=0$ SAT controls, and $5{,}334$ $t=1$ nonmodel controls. The producer was fetched back from GitHub and reexecuted before raw data publication. These small tests are \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not proof-system minimum certificates. Original-source authentication is separate: an encoded guarded parity row must be recovered from authentic signed clauses or a justified conservative definition. Neither simply recognizing $a,b$, nor asserting the output unit, licenses a free original-CNF consequence. \section*{Machine-work audit and B1/B2/B3}
Let $N$ be the serialized input bit length, $L$ the complete serialized evidence length, and $k=\mathrm{wt}(a)$. For the explicit signed-AND2 construction, the number of generated gates is $3(k-1)+1$, evidence contains $O(k\log(n+k))$ signed-index bits with conventional variable IDs, and reading, building, writing and checking the complete representation requires at least its actual bit-length cost. A straightforward sequential writer and checker uses polynomial bit-time and $O(k\log(n+k))$ serialized output. The intermediate linear parity can be represented as a compact signed-literal DAG; expanding it into a truth table or a full proof tree is unnecessary. An exponentially large alternative representation is not forbidden, but its serialization or actual access cost cannot be concealed. No claim about simultaneously required FLMV small space follows. Charge parser/source reads, all local temporary allocations (the $\rho$ term), DAG access, verification and output in any proposed machine implementation.

For a fixed uniform total correct encoded SAT decider $A$ (correct on malformed, SAT and UNSAT inputs), define $T_A^{wc}(N)=\max_{|x|\le N}T_A(x)$ and the preselected \Rmix{} minimum $W_{\Rmix}(F)$, $\overline W_{\Rmix}(N)=\max_{F\in UNSAT,\ |\mathrm{encode}(F)|\le N}W_{\Rmix}(F)$. The optional pointwise B1 is $\forall A\exists p_A\forall F\in UNSAT,\ W_{\Rmix}(F)\le p_A(|F|+T_A(F))$. The weaker B2 replaces the instance by the respective worst cases for every $N$. The weakest sufficient B3 is $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_{\Rmix}(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)$ for the same fixed calculus. B1 implies B2 implies B3, but none follows from the present theorem; all remain \textbf{OPEN}. Proof search and ideal minimum DAG cost are distinct from scheduled runtime.
