\begin{theorem}[Three-node weakening]
For every nonempty nontautological authenticated clause $D$ and compatible literal $\ell$, the clause $D\vee\ell$ has a derivation with at most one charged signed VARDEF and two exact binary RES nodes, independently of $|D|$. Adding $r$ compatible new literals costs at most $3r$ nodes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $D=u\vee D_0$, choosing any $u\in D$. If $\ell\in D$ use the parent directly. Otherwise define $y\leftrightarrow(u\land\neg\ell)$, obtaining slots $H=(y\vee\neg u\vee\ell)$ and $J=(\neg y\vee u)$ at the single paid definition node. Resolve $D$ with $H$ on $u$ to derive $D_0\vee y\vee\ell$. Resolve this with $J$ on $y$ to obtain $D_0\vee u\vee\ell=D\vee\ell$. Compatibility makes both resolvents nontautological. The third authentic gate slot remains available without extra charge. Iterate with fresh output gates for $r$ literals. Each gate is conservative under all preceding valuations and contains no SAT-label oracle. This is an upper bound, not a certified optimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform support-$k$ explicit-clause compiler]
Fix $k\ge1$, a nonempty list $S$ of nonempty nontautological CNF clauses over $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}$, and a nontautological clause $C$ over $X$ of width $s$. Then
\[
\forall k,S,C\quad
S\models C\ \Longrightarrow\
W_{\mathrm{ER2}}(S\vdash C)\le (3k+1)2^{k-s}-1.
\]
The displayed quantity is the paid cost of an explicitly constructed certificate and need not be the shortest proof. An empty source clause is already a zero-node refutation and is excluded from the clause-output formulation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each of the $2^{k-s}$ assignments $\alpha$ falsifying $C$, scan the actual sources and select one $D_\alpha\in S$ falsified by $\alpha$. Let $B_\alpha$ be the full $k$-literal blocker false under $\alpha$. Since $D_\alpha\subseteq B_\alpha$ as signed literal sets, the weakening lemma derives $B_\alpha$ with at most $3(k-|D_\alpha|)\le3k$ charged nodes. Resolve complementary complete blockers on all variables not in $C$ in a full binary tree: this uses $2^{k-s}-1$ exact pivots and yields $C$. The source-selection scan is constructive and is not a semantic inference rule. Sharing is allowed and can only improve the bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Parameterized consequence and limitations.} For explicit authenticated local CNF implication on $k\le c\log_2(N+2)$ variables the certificate uses polynomially many nodes in encoded input length $N$. When all blockers of an explicit Boolean truth-table factor are individually implied, compiling at most $2^k$ clauses uses $O(k4^k)$ nodes. This does \emph{not} prove a bound on the number of local steps in arbitrary UNSAT derivations, simulate general circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT or global GF(2), or establish B3. It also does not transform node counts into machine running time.
