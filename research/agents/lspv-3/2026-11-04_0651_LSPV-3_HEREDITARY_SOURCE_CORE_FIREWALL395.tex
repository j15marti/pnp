\section*{A complete semantic source-core invariant}
Let $F=(C_1,\ldots,C_m)$ be \emph{any} finite valid UNSAT labelled CNF. For every assignment $a$ to its original variables, define the hyperedge
\[
U_F(a)=\{i\in[m]:C_i(a)=0\}.
\]
All such edges are nonempty. Define the \emph{integral falsified-source transversal}
\[
\kappa(F)=\min\{|S|:S\subseteq[m],\quad
                    \forall a,\;S\cap U_F(a)\ne\varnothing\}.
\]
The minimum is attained since the ground set is finite.

\begin{theorem}[Core-transversal equality and universal provenance floor]
For every valid UNSAT $F$, $\kappa(F)$ equals the minimum cardinality of an UNSAT subformula of $F$. For \emph{every} sound, source-explicit, conservative, at-most-two-parent refutation DAG $\pi$ of $F$, including every legal $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ proof with adaptive rule choice and shared subderivations,
\[
       \operatorname{paid}(\pi)\ \ge\ \kappa(F)-1 .
\]
More precisely, the set $S_\pi$ of distinct original source labels actually used in the final contradiction's ancestry is UNSAT, and
$|S_\pi|\le 1+\operatorname{paid}(\pi)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any $S\subseteq[m]$, the conjunction $\bigwedge_{i\in S}C_i$ has no model exactly when for every assignment $a$ some $i\in S$ is false. This is exactly the transversal condition, proving the equality. Suppose the ancestry's source subset $S_\pi$ were SAT. Take a satisfying assignment to the original variables and extend it to every introduced auxiliary variable according to its fresh conservative gate definition in topological order. Each original source used by $\pi$ and each gate definition would hold. Induction on the sound checked proof rules then makes every later inference true, contradicting a final falsehood. Hence $S_\pi$ is UNSAT and has size $k\ge\kappa(F)$. The undirected graph of the final ancestor cone is connected, with $k$ free source vertices and $q$ paid vertices. It has at least $k+q-1$ edges. Every edge ends at a paid child whose indegree is at most two, so there are at most $2q$ edges. Thus $k+q-1\le 2q$, giving $q\ge k-1\ge\kappa(F)-1$. No search optimality, computational step count, or representation restriction beyond sound ancestry and the fixed paid parent bound enters.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Hereditary conservative-definition stability]
Let $D(x,y)$ be any collection of fresh conservative definitions such that for \emph{every} valuation $a$ of the original variables, a valuation $b$ of the auxiliary variables exists satisfying $D(a,b)$. Then for \emph{every} original source subset $S$,
\[
   \bigwedge_{i\in S}C_i \text{ is SAT }
   \iff (\bigwedge_{i\in S}C_i)\land D \text{ is SAT}.
\]
Consequently $\kappa(F)$ is unchanged by such paid conservative definitions; the provenance floor is stable under every permitted combination of their derived uses. It need not be stable under a transformation preserving satisfiability of the \emph{whole UNSAT} formula only.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Projecting a model of the conjunction with $D$ gives a model of the original clauses. Conversely every original model extends to one of $D$, by its total conservativity, independently of which source subset $S$ is retained.
\end{proof}

\section*{Exact falsifier: adding two satisfiable source clauses defeats hard-subformula inheritance}
For every $h\ge1$, let $P_h$ be the Haken PHP CNF on $n_h=h(h+1)$ variables; take a new variable $z$ and define the \emph{different source input}
\[
       P_h^+ = P_h\ \cup\ \{(z),(\neg z)\}.
\]
This is \textbf{not} a legal LOCAL-CONVERT operation on $P_h$: it replaces the original source instance with a new input. The original hard subformula is still present verbatim.

\begin{theorem}[Noninheritance within the same Resolution grammar]
For every $h\ge1$, every clause of $P_h$ is individually indispensable and
$\kappa(P_h)=m_h$. Nevertheless
\[
        \kappa(P_h^+)=2,\qquad
        W_{\mathrm{Resolution}}(P_h^+)
        =W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(P_h^+)=1.
\]
In contrast, for all sufficiently large $h$,
$W_{\mathrm{Resolution}}(P_h)\ge 2^{ch}-m_h$.
The additional indexed source length is $O(\log(h+2))$ bits relative to the existing source under a conventional indexed layout, apart from changing the header. No lower bound on the mixed-system cost of $P_h$ beyond its source floor is claimed here.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a positive row clause for pigeon $p$ is deleted, set that pigeon's entire row false and place the remaining $h$ pigeons injectively into the $h$ holes. If a collision clause for pigeons $p,q$ in hole $j$ is deleted, place precisely $p,q$ in hole $j$, put the other $h-1$ pigeons injectively into the remaining holes, and set all other placement variables false. Thus deleting any original clause makes $P_h$ SAT. All $m_h$ originals are indispensable, yielding $\kappa(P_h)=m_h$.

In $P_h^+$ the two added sources have a one-inference Resolution refutation on pivot $z$, so both proof systems have $W\le1$. Every source clause is nonempty and no unit source is itself false, so zero paid inference nodes cannot constitute a sound contradiction; hence $W=1$. No singleton source clause is UNSAT, whereas $\{z,\neg z\}$ is, so $\kappa(P_h^+)=2$. Haken supplies the comparison only for the original instance in the ordinary Resolution model. This proves that mere preservation of a certified-hard subformula and global UNSAT semantics does not preserve the hard lower bound.
\end{proof}

This is an \emph{extraction from an available proof}, not an efficient procedure for discovering the proof or for deciding SAT.
