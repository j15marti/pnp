\section{Exact source-paired grammar}
Use canonical ASCII proper signed ternary source clauses with a validated full DIMACS header. An authenticated source clause identifier is its first byte offset in the immutable original input. A PAIR-XOR record consists of three distinct original variable names \(e_{i1},e_{i2},e_{i3}\), a charge bit \(b_i\), four actual clause-start offsets, and three partner slot indices. The four clauses must \emph{exactly} forbid the four 3-bit words \(w\) of parity different from \(b_i\) in the declared variable order. Each slot \((i,j)\) points to a distinct slot \((k,l)\) carrying the \emph{same original name}, and the latter points back to \((i,j)\). The checker requires \(\bigoplus_i b_i=1\). All source offsets, literal polarities and complete original-name aliases are authenticated. There are no assumed SAT labels, no semantic lifting oracle, no omitted matching slots and no accidental fresh shared names.

\begin{theorem}[Exact semantic soundness of a source-paired affine leaf]
For any valid ordinary proper-3CNF \(F\) and proof \(\pi\), acceptance of PAIR-XOR implies \(\operatorname{UNSAT}(F)\). Thus
\[
F\in\mathrm{SAT}\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall\pi:\ V_{\mathrm{PAIR}}(F,\pi)=0.
\]
A verifier with the \(P=O(q\log(N+q))\)-bit certificate on a separate \emph{indexed read-only random-access proof tape}, plus indexed read-only original input, can achieve
\[
T_V=O((N+q)\operatorname{polylog}(N+P)),\qquad
S_{V,\mathrm{peak}}=O(\log(N+P))
\]
counted work and index bits for canonical fixed-width certificate records. This is NOT automatically a coNTIME bound for a \emph{one-way nondeterministically guessed stream}; making a guessed certificate readable, or supplying a streaming matching protocol, incurs an additional model obligation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The four authentic clauses of record \(i\) encode
\(x_{e_{i1}}\oplus x_{e_{i2}}\oplus x_{e_{i3}}=b_i\).
Add every equation over \(\mathbb F_2\). The involutive, source-name-preserving perfect slot matching means every variable occurs an even number of times, so the left side cancels to zero; the right side is one. This contradicts satisfiability. Extra unused clauses or ancestor restrictions cannot restore SAT.
For resource accounting, a deterministic streaming input-parser pass checks every source byte, grammar, clause count and variable header with \(O(\log N)\) workspace. Each constant-sized fixed-width record holds \(O(\log(N+q))\)-bit names, offsets and partner indices. For every record, obtain all four actual source clause lines, verify their forbidden-word patterns, and read/check each partner record using explicitly charged indexed random accesses. Only current indices, byte offsets, a current triple, charge accumulator and parser counters are live. No entire source, proof, graph, or predecessor table is retained in writable space. The separate proof tape is \emph{not} free when translated to a nondeterministic guess model.
\end{proof}

\section{Literal polynomial-calculus derivation and characteristic firewall}
For each excluded triple \(w\) define the source-clause falsity axiom
\[
Q_w(X)=\prod_{j=1}^3(X_j+1+w_j)=0\quad\hbox{over }\mathbb F_2.
\]
The exact degree-three polynomial identity
\[
\sum_{w:\ w_1\oplus w_2\oplus w_3\ne b}Q_w(X)
=X_1+X_2+X_3+b

shows that additions of the four original cubic axioms yield each linear XOR constraint, and additions of the \(q\) linear constraints using checked original-name pairing yield \(1=0\). \section{Scalable connected high-treewidth counterfamily}
For every \(r\ge3\), replace each vertex of the \(r\times r\) toroidal grid by a four-cycle with ports \(N,E,S,W\); connect \(E(i,j)\) to \(W(i,j+1)\), and \(S(i,j)\) to \(N(i+1,j)\), modulo \(r\). The connected cubic graph \(G_r\) has \(4r^2\) vertices and \(6r^2\) edges. Introduce one Boolean variable per physical edge and each vertex's four proper signed clauses encoding the XOR of its three edge variables. Put charge one on a single vertex and zero on every other vertex. The resulting ordinary proper-3CNF \(T_r\) has
\[
n=6r^2,\quad m=16r^2,\quad N=\Theta(r^2\log r).
\]
It is \emph{not} strict signed exact-(2,2): each edge variable appears four times positively and four negatively.

\begin{theorem}[Local module consistency does not imply proof hardness]
Every \(T_r\) is UNSAT. Every proper subfamily of its entire vertex XOR constraints is SAT. Its primal graph has treewidth \(\Omega(r)=\Omega(\sqrt{N/\log N})\). Nevertheless it has a source-paired affine certificate with \(q=4r^2\) records, \(P=O(N)\) bits and the restricted checker bound above; its characteristic-two PC refutation has degree three and \(O(r^2)\) lines.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The incidence rows of a connected graph over \(\mathbb F_2\) have exactly one nontrivial dependence: their full sum. The full sum gives \(0=1\) for odd total charge. Any proper subset of incidence rows is linearly independent and can satisfy arbitrary right-hand sides. Pair each edge's two vertex slots to verify the affine leaf.
Contract each four-port cycle and delete wraparound edges to obtain the \(r\times r\) planar grid as a minor of \(G_r\), hence \(\operatorname{tw}(G_r)=\Omega(r)\). The primal variable graph is the line graph \(L(G_r)\). A width-\(w\) decomposition of \(L(G_r)\) converts to one of \(G_r\) by replacing each edge-node by its two endpoints; incident edge-nodes form a clique and their bag subtrees intersect. Therefore \(\operatorname{tw}(G_r)\le 2w+1\), so the primal width is \(\Omega(r)\). Finally \(q=4r^2\) and all record fields are logarithmic.
\end{proof}
