Let $s$ be any JSON-parsed string or object such that JavaScript's own property lookup yields a primitive number satisfying
\[
   s.\texttt{length} \; \texttt{===}\; k .
\]
Then replacing \texttt{summary} by $s$ preserves the original checker's acceptance, all returned fixture summaries, all paid node counts and the exact assertion count. In particular neither an Array object nor valid summary-member content is required.

\noindent\textbf{Proof.} Inspect the complete original independent verifier. Its sole read of \texttt{data.summary} is the final instruction
\[
\texttt{assert(cases.length===data.summary.length,"summary count");}.

No statement inspects the type or members of \texttt{data.summary}.
