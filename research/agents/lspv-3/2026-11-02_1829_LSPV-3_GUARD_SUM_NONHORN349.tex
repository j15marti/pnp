\section*{Uniform guarded selector sum}
\begin{lemma}
Let $A$ and $B$ be variable-disjoint clause-minimally UNSAT CNFs. For a fresh selector $s$ define
\[
G_s(A,B)=\bigwedge_{C\in A}(s\vee C)\ \wedge\
\bigwedge_{D\in B}(\neg s\vee D).
\]
Then $G_s(A,B)$ is minimally UNSAT and every independent source-occurrence sign flip makes it SAT. For ordinary Resolution,
\[
W_{\rm Res}(G_s(A,B))\le W_{\rm Res}(A)+W_{\rm Res}(B)+1.
\]
For all sound refutation DAGs in the specified binary-premise, charged combined calculus,
\[
W_R(G_s(A,B))\ge |A|+|B|-1.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At $s=0$ the first group reduces to UNSAT $A$, and at $s=1$ the second reduces to UNSAT $B$. On removal of $s\vee C$, choose $s=0$ and an assignment satisfying $A\setminus\{C\}$. It satisfies all other clauses and necessarily falsifies the removed clause. The symmetric construction works for any $\neg s\vee D$. Flipping any one literal of that falsified source makes it true without changing the other sources, so all occurrence signs are individually sensitive, including each selector occurrence.

Lift any Resolution derivation of $A$ by adding $s$ to each line, preserving all its pivots. This derives the unit $(s)$. Similarly lift $B$ to $(\neg s)$ and resolve once more. For the lower bound, each of the $m=|A|+|B|$ sources must appear in the contradiction ancestry of any sound proof. In the pruned DAG with $q$ paid nodes and $E$ explicitly referenced premise edges, the underlying undirected graph is connected, so $E\ge m+q-1$, while binary premise arity gives $E\le 2q$. Hence $q\ge m-1$. Paid zero-premise conservative definitions cannot lower this bound. No proof-search cost is used.
\end{proof}

The $G_r$ are clause-minimally UNSAT, every signed occurrence is sensitive, they are neither renamable Horn nor renamable dual-Horn, and
\[

\[
\boxed{2k+5\le W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(G_r)\le3k+4.}

\begin{proof}
The lemma proves minimal UNSAT and sensitivity. Under any global complementation of variables, one of the four guarded $K$ clauses has both $u,v$ positive, and another has both negative.
