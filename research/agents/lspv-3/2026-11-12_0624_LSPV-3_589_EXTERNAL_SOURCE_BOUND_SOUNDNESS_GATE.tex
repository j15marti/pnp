Let $F=(C_0,\ldots,C_{4h-1})$ be an externally trusted ordered signed proper-3CNF with independently fixed content. The gate receives $F$ \emph{separately} from the candidate packet $P$ and checks occurrence-by-occurrence exact ordered signed literal equality, rather than trusting source data or a hash supplied inside $P$. It checks cubic graph incidence, $b_v\in\{0,1\}$, $\bigoplus_v b_v=1$, the four distinct signed forbidden local patterns per vertex, and all original-source IDs. Original and derived IDs are typed and globally distinct. Only two earlier distinct parents may be used by AND3 or XOR. All native /341 factor truth tables and support coordinates are recomputed, with numeric arrays mandatory. Every advertised affine alias must be identical to the factor function on all support assignments. Row masks must be canonical hexadecimal, in range $[0,2^E)$, with Boolean RHS. XOR outputs must be exactly the masks and RHS obtained by pairwise GF(2) addition. The final row must be $(0,1)$. The full chronological shared DAG and every original source's ancestry are checked. Cost is counted once per paid derived node, not per fanout or proof chain.

\begin{theorem}[589.1, restricted source-retracting soundness]
For every independently supplied original source $F$ and every parsed packet $P$, acceptance by the separate strict gate implies that $F$ is unsatisfiable. This does NOT imply that the gate accepts every packet accepted by the historical /341 checker.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
External exact signed-source equality identifies the checked original factors with the clauses of $F$. Suppose an assignment satisfies $F$; then every original factor evaluates to one. AND3's independently verified table denotes exact conjunction, so every derived factor encountered on the assignment also evaluates to one. Every advertised row has been checked by exhaustive support enumeration to be equivalent to its factor and therefore is true. Each XOR-derived affine row is exactly the sum of two previously true rows, and is thus true. Induction along earlier-parent edges of the entire shared DAG makes the terminal row true. Its authenticated value is $0=1$, an impossibility. Thus no satisfying assignment exists. An independent second argument observes that the complete original signed cubic odd-charge Tseitin source forces a local parity equation at every vertex and their XOR is $0=1$ because every edge appears twice. Neither argument concerns an arbitrary 3-CNF proof system.
\end{proof}
