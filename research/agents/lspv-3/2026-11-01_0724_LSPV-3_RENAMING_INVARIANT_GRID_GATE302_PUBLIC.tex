\section*{Explicit canonical source construction}

\begin{proof}
In each ring choose either alternating two-edge perfect matching; set its edges TRUE and all other graph edges FALSE. Every original negative triple has exactly one TRUE edge and thus two TRUE negative literals. Every original positive triple, even after swapping its external edge, contains exactly one TRUE internal matching edge, hence one TRUE positive literal. All original clauses hold. Complementation exchanges these true-literal counts, yielding a second SAT witness.
For acquisition, take each negative original clause as a graph vertex and each original variable as an edge joining its two negative occurrences. Validate the canonical grammar and that this reconstructed graph is simple cubic. Enumerate graph $4$-cycles by bounded-degree walks, require unique pairwise vertex-disjoint $4$-cycles covering all graph vertices, with precisely two internal incident edges and one external at each vertex. Require every positive source clause to contain two internal edges incident to a common graph vertex and one external edge. Select one alternating internal ring matching per certified cycle, set other edges FALSE, and verify the complete assignment on the original clause list. Every accepted source is SAT by the same argument, irrespective of renaming. With $\Delta=3$, bounded-length cycle enumeration and clause checking use $O(n)$ word operations given indexed incidence; deterministic source parsing/sorting is $O(n\log n)$ word operations and $O(n)$ words, with polynomial bit-model overhead.
Contracting the $r^2$ ring cycles in $H_r$ gives an $r\times r$ toroidal grid and hence an ordinary square grid minor. Thus $\mathrm{tw}(H_r)\ge r-1$. The negative-clause primal subgraph is the line graph $L(H_r)$. A width-$w$ tree decomposition of $L(H_r)$ yields one for $H_r$ of width at most $2w+1$ by replacing each edge-vertex by its two endpoints. The subtrees for edges incident to one $H_r$ vertex intersect, hence their union is connected. Therefore $\mathrm{tw}(L(H_r))\ge(\mathrm{tw}(H_r)-1)/2\ge(r-2)/2$, and positive clauses cannot reduce width.
\end{proof}
