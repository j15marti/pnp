\section*{LSPV-3 good-event coherence test}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open.

\paragraph{Target.} Test whether vMS reconstruction supplies a deterministic, independently checkable predicate C(r,Q,i) such that C and E_b imply not E_{1-b}, allowing deletion of the opposite-output universal condition.

\paragraph{Primary-source result.}
In the proof of vMS Corollary 4.6, the reconstructor explicitly makes three parallel SAT queries: existence of a successful Merlin response, and existence of responses outputting 0 and 1. It declares failure when success is absent or the last two answers are inconsistent. Thus the published construction does not derive pointwise consistency for free.

In the underlying Corollary 4.5 proof, partial single-valuedness occurs only after probabilistic good events: commitment success holds with high probability; conditioned on successful commitments, queried evaluations agree with fixed functions with high probability; and an incorrect output bit is rejected by PCPP soundness with high probability. The resulting statement is resilient soundness: with bounded error, the protocol fails or outputs the correct bit. This is not a deterministic predicate C on every fixed Arthur random string.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT consequence for the proposed shortcut.}
The cited proof does not establish
\[
\forall r,Q,i\quad E_0(r,Q,i)\oplus E_1(r,Q,i)
\]
whenever a response succeeds. Instead it establishes a probability bound over Arthur randomness. Corollary 4.6 then queries SAT precisely to determine success and output consistency for the sampled protocol instance. Therefore the existing good-commit/partial-single-valuedness argument cannot simply be moved before Viola's final existential block as a deterministic coherence certificate.

\paragraph{Quantifier stress test.}
Let G(r) denote the conjunction of the proof's good events. The source supports a statement of the schematic form
\[
\Pr_r[G(r)]\ge 1-\delta,
\]
with correctness/single-valued behavior conditioned on G, not a cheap deterministic proof of G(r) for arbitrary sampled r. Replacing the universal opposite-witness check by an existential certificate for G would require a new theorem giving efficiently verifiable certificates whose soundness itself does not quantify over all conflicting Merlin/evaluation strategies. No such theorem appears in the cited reconstruction.

\paragraph{Status.}
FALSIFIED/RETIRED: using the existing vMS partial-single-valuedness lemma directly as the missing deterministic C.
OPEN: strengthen the protocol with a pointwise binding/canonical-commitment property that is independently certifiable at near-linear cost.
CONDITIONAL: conservative k^4 reconstruction accounting remains unchanged.

\paragraph{Hostile controls and barriers.}
A proposed C must survive alternate Merlin responses, proof padding, DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, and maximal semantic/algebraic oracle replacement. Merely exposing the random coins or commitment transcript is insufficient if verifying that no conflicting successful response exists recreates coNP. The present reconstruction ingredients are compatible with relativizing reasoning and do not provide the missing nonrelativizing hinge.

\paragraph{BOOL/TransportCert.}
TC4 foreign algorithms must be expanded into primitive execution, but this accounting convention does not certify absence of a conflicting response. No TC1--TC5 rule currently proves the required pointwise incompatibility.

\paragraph{Handoffs.}
LSPV-5: keep the conservative k^4 ledger; the published partial-single-valuedness argument does not yield the proposed k^3 absorption.
LSPV-4: attack any strengthened pointwise binding predicate by asking whether verification hides the opposite SAT/coNP query.
LSPV-1: structural RMV coherence helps LSPV-3 only if it yields a pointwise, independently verifiable incompatibility between successful 0 and 1 responses for fixed Q,r,i.

\paragraph{Next target.}
Test whether the commit-and-evaluate protocol can be modified or canonically normalized so that successful commitment is deterministically binding for the exact sampled transcript, with a short certificate verifiable without universal search. Absent such a theorem, retire the multisign/coherence k^3 branch.
