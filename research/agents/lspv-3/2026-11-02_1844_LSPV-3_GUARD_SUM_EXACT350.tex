\section*{Source-once derivation for the permuted grid}
For integer $r\ge2$, put $k=r^2$, $x_0,\ldots,x_k$, $y_1,\ldots,y_k$ and
\[
\pi(i)=r((i-1)\bmod r)+\left\lfloor\frac{i-1}{r}\right\rfloor+1.
\]
Let
\[
\begin{split}
F_r={}&(x_0)\wedge(\neg x_k)\wedge(\neg y_1)
\wedge\!\!\bigwedge_{j=2}^{k}(y_{j-1}\vee\neg y_j)
\wedge\!\!\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k}(\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i\vee y_{\pi(i)}).
\end{split}
\]
By GRID348, $F_r$ is minimally UNSAT, has $m_F=2k+2$ independently indispensable sources, every source sign is sensitive, and the primal graph has an $r$ by $r$ grid minor.

\begin{lemma}[Sharp source floor for the grid]
For all $r\ge2$,
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_r)=W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_r)=2k+1.}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Start with the original unit $(x_0)$. For $i=1,\ldots,k$, resolve the current clause on $x_{i-1}$ with the unique original guarded transition
$(\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i\vee y_{\pi(i)})$. After $k$ steps, the result is
\[
x_k\vee y_{\pi(1)}\vee\cdots\vee y_{\pi(k)}
=x_k\vee y_1\vee\cdots\vee y_k,
\]
because $\pi$ is a permutation. Resolve with source $(\neg x_k)$, obtaining $y_1\vee\cdots\vee y_k$. Now use the original implication clauses \emph{in reverse index order}: for $j=k,k-1,\ldots,2$, resolve on $y_j$ with $(y_{j-1}\vee\neg y_j)$. Since $y_{j-1}$ already occurs in the current disjunction, that resolution removes $y_j$ without adding another variable. Finally resolve $(y_1)$ with source $(\neg y_1)$. Thus the total cost is
\[
k+1+(k-1)+1=2k+1=m_F-1.
\]
Each original clause is used \emph{exactly once as a proof leaf}, and each internal node except the root has one outgoing dependency edge. The connected-source lower bound matches the exhibited tree. The argument remains valid in the richer combined calculus because its lower-bound premise convention is unchanged.
\end{proof}

\section*{Exact guarded non-renamable-Horn minimum}

\[
K=(u\vee v)\wedge(u\vee\neg v)\wedge(\neg u\vee v)\wedge(\neg u\vee\neg v),
\]

\[
G_r=\bigwedge_{C\in F_r}(s\vee C)\ \wedge\

\]
The earlier guarded-sum lemma proves that $G_r$ is minimally UNSAT and every source-occurrence sign is sensitive. The four-pattern $K$ ensures that after any variable complementation at least one guarded clause has two positive and one has two negative original literals, so $G_r$ is neither renamable Horn nor renamable dual-Horn. The primal graph still contains the original $r$-grid minor.

$n=2k+4$, $m=2k+6$, $L=7k+15$, encoded strict ASCII DIMACS length $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$ bits, primal treewidth at least $r$, and
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(G_r)=W_{\mathrm{Res}}(G_r)=m-1=2k+5.}
\]
This is the \emph{global} minimum over all legal DAG derivations, with perfect hindsight choice, temporary growth, algebraic reasoning and charged extensions permitted; it is not a lower bound on arbitrary SAT machines.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding source-once Resolution tree with a guard $s$ appended to every original and derived clause, producing the unit $(s)$ after $2k+1$ steps. Independently resolve the four clauses of $K$ guarded by $\neg s$ in a source-once three-node Resolution tree to derive $(\neg s)$. One last Resolution step gives the empty clause. The total is $(2k+1)+3+1=2k+5$. Every source occurs once and every charged nonroot proof node is used once. Since every one of the $m$ source clauses is indispensable and every fixed-$R$ operation has at most two explicitly named antecedents, the previously proved $m-1$ global lower bound applies, even to alternative non-Resolution proofs. Equality follows.
\end{proof}

\section*{Important distinction: proof-node minimum versus evidence bytes}
The new exact-optimal proof accumulates clauses of width $i+2$ during the forward $x$ chain. Its widest line has $k+2$ literals, and the reverse $y$ elimination also traverses large clauses. Direct summation of \emph{explicitly written derived literal occurrences} in this particular certificate gives exactly
\[
\boxed{\sum_{\text{derived line }D}|D|=k^2+4k+6.}
\]
Hence a flat, literal-by-literal certificate uses $\Theta(k^2\log k)$ bits in this implementation, even though its DAG has only $\Theta(k)$ nodes. By contrast, the valid earlier $3k+4$-node refutation in GUARD-SUM349 retains line width at most three and has $O(k)$ derived literal occurrences and $O(k\log k)$ explicit certificate bits. This demonstrates a \emph{two-witness node-count/evidence-size tradeoff}; it does not prove that every shortest proof requires quadratic explicit evidence, nor exclude compressed references, lazy clauses or recomputation. Physical checking time, retrieval, and all-phase peak memory are separate from logical $W_R$.
