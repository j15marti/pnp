\section*{Fixed calculus and diagnostic subgrammar}
Original Rmix341 remains free source-authenticated signed CNF clauses, unit-paid binary RES, original-support-at-most-three AND3, GF(2) XOR, checked LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative signed two-input DEF; a shared inference counts once. LOCAL-CONVERT full exported-wire payload syntax is not settled. No rule or weight is modified here.
Separately define an explicitly indexed finite acyclic gate DAG over original inputs, optional NOT views, and binary truth-table gates depending essentially on both arguments. Require at EVERY binary gate that the recursively collected syntactic original-input supports of its children are disjoint.

\begin{lemma}[Exact support, constructive independence witnesses]
For every gate \(v\) in this class, if \(S(v)\) is the recursively computed syntactic original-variable cone, then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ess}(f_v)=S(v).}
\]
For every \(i\in S(v)\), a deterministic polynomial-time procedure constructs two complete original-input assignments differing only at \(i\) and producing different outputs at \(v\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct in topological gate order. Inputs and NOT views are nonconstant with exactly their named support. At \(v=g(u,w)\), both children are nonconstant and their supports are disjoint. For a variable \(i\) essential to \(u\), the induction gives a pair of assignments to \(u\)'s inputs differing only at \(i\), which changes \(u\)'s output. Since the two-argument gate truth table genuinely depends on its first argument, some \(b\in\{0,1\}\) satisfies \(g(0,b)\ne g(1,b)\). Since \(w\) is nonconstant, some assignment to its disjoint variables makes \(w=b\). Combining the sibling assignment with both assignments for \(u\) gives an original-input flip witness for \(v\). The case \(i\in S(w)\) is symmetric. No input outside the syntactic cone can influence \(v\). The same recursion realizes desired output values for each child from its two-entry image and constructs the witnesses explicitly. Every step uses a constant four-row gate truth table and polynomial-length assignments.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Polynomial checker.} Topologically check all gate references and two-input nondegeneracy; propagate \(n\)-bit support masks and check their intersections are empty. This uses \(O(Gn)\) elementary bit operations for \(G\) gates and \(n\) named originals, aside from standard logarithmic index handling. At most \(n\) influence pairs of \(n\)-bit assignments give \(O(n^2)\) witness output bits. If the root support has size at most three, the exact truth table is obtained from at most eight circuit evaluations with the other originals pinned arbitrarily. This gives a sound complete validator WITHIN this subclass, not for arbitrary succinct circuits.

\section*{Controls and failure classification}
The reconvergent circuit \(x\oplus x\equiv0\) has a named source cone but empty essential support; \(x\lor(x\land y)\equiv x\) has source cone \(\{x,y\}\) but essential support \(\{x\}\). Both are rejected by the disjointness rule. An output-gate ignoring one child is rejected by the nondegeneracy rule. These do not contradict semantic invariance of essential support, but show the syntactic certificate can change under equivalent representations. The result does not imply that a general circuit can be converted to this subclass efficiently or that any legal Rmix341 refutation must compute this named port.
