\section{Target, archive and falsifiable mechanisms}
Let $\mathcal E$ contain simultaneous exponent pairs for \emph{one fixed uniform total exact SAT decider on every ordinary input} in the Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (FLMV) read-only indexed-input random-access Turing model. The known forbidden region is
\[
 \mathcal B_{\rm FLMV}
 =\{(t,s):\exists c>\max(1,t),\quad
 s<\tfrac12[(c+2)/c^2-c]\}.
\]
A fully charged all-input achievable-pair successor, or a conditional cofinal finite route to this region, remains \OPEN. This work addresses only one proposed supplier of that successor: detaching affine and binary modules, projecting away selector coordinates, and joining the resulting semantic interfaces.

Before testing, we posed three falsifiable mechanisms. (M1) Perhaps forgetting any one selector leaves the exact affine/2-CNF parent relation unchanged after independent module projection. (M2) Perhaps a growing proper-3CNF minimal-UNSAT source forces \emph{every} named selector coordinate to be retained by this specific marginal-join calculus. (M3) Perhaps this syntactic interface requirement transfers to the work of \emph{every} deterministic SAT decider. M1 is false, M2 true, and M3 false as a general inference: an explicit source-aware parity scan bypasses the detached join on our family.

\section{Source-typed affine and binary modules}
For an ordered proper ternary CNF $F(X)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}C_i(X)$, write
$C_i=\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}$ with three distinct source identifiers.
Create \emph{fresh and distinct} selectors
$U_i=\{u_{i1},u_{i2},u_{i3}\}$, $U=\dot\bigcup_iU_i$, and define
\[
 A_F(U)=\bigwedge_i(u_{i1}\oplus u_{i2}\oplus u_{i3}=1),
 \quad
 B_F(X,U)=\bigwedge_i\left[
 \bigwedge_{j<k}(\neg u_{ij}\vee\neg u_{ik})
 \;\wedge\;\bigwedge_{j=1}^3(\neg u_{ij}\vee\ell_{ij})
 \right].
 \tag{1}
\]
$A_F$ is an affine system with $m$ independent rows; $B_F$ is 2-CNF with $6m$ binary clauses.
Both are independently satisfiable on \emph{every} $F$:
one first selector per clause satisfies $A_F$, and all-zero selectors satisfy $B_F$.
The previously proved common-witness identity is
\[
 \exists U\,[A_F(U)\wedge B_F(X,U)]\quad\equiv\quad F(X).
 \tag{2}
\]
Importantly, the two sides must use the \emph{same} value of each shared selector.
Neither the rank of the source Tseitin equations nor signed-hypergraph NAE cycle rank is the $m$-row rank of $A_F$; these are separately defined structural measures.

For any retained selector-coordinate set $S\subseteq U$, define the \emph{detached projection join}
\[
 J_S(X)=\exists z_S\,\left[
  (\exists a_{U\setminus S}\, A_F(z_S,a))\ \wedge\
  (\exists b_{U\setminus S}\, B_F(X,z_S,b))
 \right].
 \tag{3}
\]
The two tuples $a$ and $b$ quantify \emph{independently} on omitted coordinates, while $z_S$ remains a single shared assignment. This is the precise calculus under audit; it is \emph{not} the same as projecting the already-joined relation.

\begin{theorem}[Selector-erasure identity; PROVED/EXACT in calculus (3)]
For \emph{every} proper ternary CNF $F$, every retained coordinate mask $S\subseteq U$ and every complete original assignment $x\in\{0,1\}^{X}$,
\[
 \boxed{
 J_S(x)=\bigwedge_{\{i:U_i\subseteq S\}} C_i(x).
 }
 \tag{4}
\]
Thus independently existentially forgetting \emph{one or more} selectors from a clause exactly erases that clause in the detached join. If $S=U$, the join equals $F$; if $S\ne U$, it equals the original formula with every clause whose selector triple is not fully retained removed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a source assignment $x$. For a clause $i$ whose three selectors lie in $S$, the affine row requires odd Hamming weight, and $B_F$ permits weight at most one and forces the selected literal to be true. Therefore a common retained row exists exactly when $C_i(x)=1$.
For a clause with at least one selector omitted, assign every \emph{retained} selector zero. The affine factor may independently set one omitted selector to one and all its others zero, satisfying parity. The binary factor may independently set \emph{all} omitted selectors zero, satisfying at-most-one and all implications for every $x$. All selector triples are disjoint, so these local choices extend simultaneously across the entire formula. The only remaining conditions are the clauses with all three selectors retained, proving (4).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A sharper projection consequence.}
For both complete modules separately, projection onto \emph{original} variables is total:
\[
 \exists U\,A_F=\top,\qquad \exists U\,B_F(X,U)=\top
 \quad\text{for all }X.
\]
Yet their \emph{joined} projection is $F(X)$ by (2).
Thus even keeping \emph{all source variable names} while independently forgetting their common selector witnesses loses the precise conjunction. This is a quantified witness-coherence error, not a failure of literal-name preservation.

\section{Scalable opposite-label, clause-minimal hostile controls}
Let $G_q$ be the ordinary prism graph on two disjoint $q$-cycles joined by $q$ rungs, $q\ge3$. It is simple, cubic and 2-vertex-connected, with $|V|=2q$ and $|E|=3q$. Give vertex $v$ a charge $b_v\in\{0,1\}$ and an edge Boolean variable $x_e$ for each $e\in E$. At each vertex require the equation
\[
 \bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v.
\]
Encode each degree-three equation by its four proper three-literal clauses forbidding exactly the four local tuples with the wrong parity. Call this proper 3CNF $T_{q,b}$. The counts are
\[
 n=3q,\quad m=8q,\quad
 N=(32q+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(11q+1))\right\rceil.
 \tag{5}
\]
All charge vectors on the same graph have identical unsigned clause supports, original variable/record counts and encoded length. The source SAT label is
\[
 \SAT(T_{q,b})=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \bigoplus_{v\in V}b_v=0.
 \tag{6}
\]
Choose the zero charge for a SAT member and a one-unit charge for a matched UNSAT member. The latter is \emph{clause-minimally UNSAT}, as previously proved in CUBIC-AFFINE-FIREWALL98.

\begin{lemma}[Single-clause deletion lifts; previously known structure, explicit count]
For odd total charge and every one of the $8q$ proper ternary clauses $D$ of $T_{q,b}$, the formula $T_{q,b}\setminus\{D\}$ is SAT, with exactly $2^{q-1}$ satisfying assignments.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A deleted clause $D$ forbade precisely one local triple $t$ of incorrect parity at its vertex $v$. Set the three edges incident to $v$ to $t$. The remaining graph $G_q-v$ is connected. Its incidence equations have full row rank $|V|-2$ after removing their unique row dependence. The residual charge-sum agrees with the parity of the three prescribed edge values because the original total charge is odd. Hence the remaining equations admit exactly
$2^{(|E|-3)-(|V|-2)}=2^{|E|-|V|-1}=2^{q-1}$ edge assignments. Each such assignment violates exactly the removed clause at $v$, satisfies the other three local clauses there, and satisfies all other vertex gadgets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every selector is indispensable \emph{for the detached calculus}; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Apply (1) to the odd-charge $T_{q,b}$, with $m=8q$ clauses and $3m=24q$ selectors. For every \emph{proper} set $S\subsetneq U$,
\[
 \underbrace{\exists X,U\,[A_F\wedge B_F]}_{\text{UNSAT}}=0,
 \qquad
 \underbrace{\exists X\,J_S(X)}_{\text{SAT}}=1.
 \tag{7}
\]
Thus every one of the $24q$ selector coordinates is critical for exact source-blind independent-projection composition on these UNSAT parents. The retained full-interface size is $\Theta(N/\log N)$ bits, not $O(\log N)$, under the declared coordinate-only calculus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Since $S\ne U$, some clause $C_i$ has $U_i\not\subseteq S$. By (4), $J_S$ is the conjunction of a proper subset of original clauses. The minimal-UNSAT lemma says \emph{every} proper subset is satisfiable. In contrast the fully shared join is exactly the original odd-charge UNSAT formula by (2) and (6).
\end{proof}

\section{Adaptive-state, machine and resource ledger}
The actual hostile-control decider first receives an ordinary read-only source, unknown SAT/UNSAT label, representation tag and remaining budget. It can pay to recognize the canonical signed prism grammar and recover $q$, the graph-incidence support and charge bits, then choose a direct parity scan. Recognition uses indexed source reads and grammar checks rather than an oracle for the answer. The charge-sum scan is linear in records with $O(\log N)$ counters and one parity bit; witness reconstruction for even charge can additionally use a spanning tree and paid output generation. When the promised grammar fails, the total exact fallback is an ordinary SAT search with its \emph{actual} potentially exponential costs. The finite policy menu is only a hostile control, not all-algorithm coverage.

Alternatively, a selector policy chooses the independently projected affine/binary module route; then it must charge parameter discovery, verification of the shared selector names, all omitted-coordinate checks, compatibility, table storage or recomputation, and every failed trial. Every attempted erasure $S\ne U$ is rejected as unsound on our UNSAT family by (7). The raw retention lower bound applies \emph{only} to this specified composition type, not to a source-aware circuit, direct solver, or a different exact quotient that encodes correlations more compactly.
