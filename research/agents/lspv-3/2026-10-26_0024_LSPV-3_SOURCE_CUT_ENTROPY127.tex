\section{Exact source-cut contract for derived ordinary SAT children}
A branch partial assignment $\sigma$ assigns only \emph{original}
variable names. Let $I_\ell\subseteq[m]$ be an exact claimed original
source-axiom dependency set at a terminal $\ell$. Define
$F[I_\ell]=\bigwedge_{i\in I_\ell}C_i$. A derived ordinary
SAT child $H_\ell$ is \emph{source-cut sound} for $(I_\ell,\sigma_\ell)$
when the following \emph{model lifting} condition is established:
\[
\boxed{\quad
\forall\alpha\,[\alpha\models F[I_\ell]\land\sigma_\ell
 \Rightarrow\exists\beta\,
  (\beta\models H_\ell\land
  \beta\!\upharpoonright_{\mathrm{shared}}=
  \alpha\!\upharpoonright_{\mathrm{shared}})] .\quad} \tag{SC}
\]
Here $\alpha$ is a complete assignment of original source variables,
and $\beta$ is a complete assignment of all child variables. All
common original names are \emph{literally identical}; declared
fresh private names must remain distinct across sibling modules.
The contract can be discharged by a supplied and audited local
Tseitin/PIN-style witness construction, a formal proof of
extension equivalence, or an explicit checked source-local
derivation. The verifier may \emph{not} trust an arbitrary declaration
of (SC): recognizing its general semantic truth can itself be hard.
Source-axiom provenance must include every source dependency of the
construction. A terminal also carries a checked proof of
$\neg\SAT(H_\ell)$ or an actual executed, charged, exact call to $A$.
The full derived formula is proper ordinary SAT with complete
headers, literal bodies and accurately charged per-request bit access.

\begin{theorem}[Derived-child source-cut soundness]\label{th:sourcecut}
If (SC) is independently established and $H_\ell$ is UNSAT,
then $F[I_\ell]\land\sigma_\ell$ is UNSAT. Consequently replacing an
original-subformula leaf by a source-cut-audited derived-child leaf
preserves SAT-input rejection for a complete binary original-variable
branch tree. If two successive transformations each have (SC), with
compatible common-name embeddings, their composition also has (SC).
For a JOIN of derived modules it is sufficient that all witness
lifts are jointly compatible and their private names are disjoint;
individual separate extensions alone are \emph{not} sufficient.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A satisfying source assignment $\alpha$ would by (SC) extend to a
satisfying $\beta$ of $H_\ell$, contradiction. For two levels, lift
$\alpha$ to a model of the first actual child and then lift that
model to the second, composing the \emph{same} inherited source
names. When joining separately lifted modules with disjoint private
names, their witnesses amalgamate because they agree on every
shared original name. If private names collide, the witnesses need
not amalgamate. Standard structural induction over BRANCH uses
the source-cut implication at each terminal and both branches
of every unassigned original variable; hence an original SAT source
cannot admit an accepted false-UNSAT certificate.
\end{proof}

\section{A witness-weighted entropy and source-support inequality}
For an UNSAT original source set
\[
E(F)=\{i\in[m]:F\setminus\{C_i\}\in\SAT\},
\qquad e=|E(F)|>0.
\]
For each $i\in E(F)$ fix a \emph{private witness} $\alpha_i$
satisfying every clause except $C_i$. Distinct indices have
distinct private witnesses. Let $T$ be a finite complete
binary decision tree branching only on previously unassigned
original names. At leaf $\ell$, a genuine source-cut-audited
UNSAT terminal has provenance $I_\ell$. Write $d_\ell$ for its
branch depth, and $\kappa_\ell=|I_\ell\cap E(F)|$.
For a private witness $\alpha_i$, denote by $\ell(i)$ the
unique terminal reached. Let $n_\ell$ be the number of private
witnesses reaching $\ell$.

\begin{theorem}[Weighted Kraft and entropy conservation]\label{th:entropy}
Every such accepted source-cut-audited tree satisfies
\[
\boxed{\quad
 n_\ell\le\kappa_\ell,\quad
 \sum_{\ell:n_\ell>0}
 \frac{n_\ell}{\kappa_\ell}2^{-d_\ell}\le1.\quad} \tag{WK}
\]
Taking $J$ uniformly on the $e$ essential-clause indices,
and $D=d_{\ell(J)}$, one also has
\[
\boxed{\quad
 \mathbb E D+
 \mathbb E\log_2\kappa_{\ell(J)}
 \ \ge\ \log_2 e. \quad} \tag{ENT}
\]
More precisely,
$\mathbb ED\ge H(L)$ and
$H(L)=\log_2e-\mathbb E\log_2 n_L$, where $L=\ell(J)$.
Thus $h+\log_2K\ge\log_2e$ when $d_\ell\le h$
and $\kappa_\ell\le K$ on occupied terminals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $i\in E(F)$ and follow $\alpha_i$ through the exact
original-name branches to $\ell(i)$. If $i\notin I_{\ell(i)}$,
then $\alpha_i$ satisfies $F[I_{\ell(i)}]\land\sigma_{\ell(i)}$.
Theorem~\ref{th:sourcecut} says that source subset is UNSAT,
contradiction. Therefore $i\in I_{\ell(i)}$.
Grouping all private witnesses by their terminals gives
$n_\ell\le\kappa_\ell$.
Leaves of a binary decision tree are a prefix-free code, so
$\sum_\ell 2^{-d_\ell}\le1$. Weighting each occupied leaf by
$n_\ell/\kappa_\ell\le1$ proves (WK).
For uniform $J$, the conditional distribution of $J$
given $L=\ell$ is uniform on the $n_\ell$ indices reaching
that leaf. Thus $H(J|L)=\mathbb E\log_2 n_L$ exactly,
and $\log_2 e=H(J)=H(L)+H(J|L)$.
The prefix-free source branch codes have expected length
$\mathbb E D\ge H(L)$ by Kraft's inequality. Finally
$\log n_L\le\log\kappa_L$ proves (ENT).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Critical scope.}
The strong-looking theorem concerns \emph{original source-axiom
dependency counts}, not physical child body length, PC degree,
proof checking time, number of bit reads, an arbitrary deterministic
decider's operations, or implicit source access. Its right-hand
side is only $\log_2 e\le\log_2m=O(\log N)$. It cannot supply
a polynomial lower bound on verifier leaf volume. A one-terminal
proof citing all $m$ clauses has $D=0$ and
$\kappa=e$, \emph{saturating} the entropy tradeoff.
If an A-terminal is supplied with an entirely rewritten formula,
the source-cut witness map and complete provenance must be supplied
and checked; otherwise the theorem does not apply. A PC proof
carrying a long source support may still be short. The prior
PARITY-FIREWALL124 degree inequality $h+k$ for branch-PC leaves is
not a new result of this run.
