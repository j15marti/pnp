\section*{Exact model and quantifiers}
Fix one deterministic exact SAT decider \(A\). For every SAT input \(x\) of length \(n\), suppose a deterministic exact procedure \(R_A\) has finitely many charged SAT-call channels \(j=1,\ldots,s\). Channel \(j\) makes at most \(n^{\eta_j+o(1)}\) calls, each on an explicitly constructed SAT instance of length at most \(n^{\theta_j+o(1)}\). Nonrecursive local work is at most \(n^{r+o(1)}\). The same constants and procedure apply at every length. Every call is an exact SAT query. There is no hidden current-scale invocation of \(A\), and construction, normalization, indexing, writing, and query-buffer costs are charged. SAME\_REALIZATION means the recursively replaced calls are exactly those used by the declared source construction.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: multichannel time closure}
\textbf{Theorem MULTICHANNEL1.}
If \(0\le\theta_j<1\) for every channel, recursive replacement of every SAT call by the same procedure gives
\[
\boxed{T(n)\le n^{C+o(1)},\qquad C=\max\left\{r,\max_j\frac{\eta_j}{1-\theta_j}\right\}.}
\]
The exponent is independent of the original exponent \(K\) of \(A\).

\textbf{Proof.}
Use strong induction on input length. One invocation costs \(n^{r+o(1)}\). Channel \(j\) contributes at most
\[
n^{\eta_j+o(1)}T(n^{\theta_j+o(1)})\le n^{\eta_j+\theta_j C+o(1)}.
\]
Since \(C\ge\eta_j/(1-\theta_j)\), we have \(\eta_j+\theta_jC\le C\), and \(r\le C\). A fixed number of channels changes only the subpolynomial factor. All recursive scales are strictly fractional, so recursion terminates. Hence the bound. \(\square\)

RECURCLOSE1 is the one-channel special case.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: scale-sensitive selector charge}
If a construction channel directly invokes \(A\) on \(n^{\eta+o(1)}\) instances of length \(n^{1+o(1)}\), explicit accounting contains \(n^{\eta+K+o(1)}\). Recursive replacement does not decrease scale when \(\theta=1\). Thus MULTICHANNEL1 cannot erase \(K\) from an unresolved current-scale channel.

Conversely, use of \(A\) is not automatically fatal. A SAT-dependent channel with \(\theta<1\) can be recursively absorbed when its multiplicity is charged and its calls have the exact SAT semantics required above. Thus unresolved current-scale use is fatal to this closure, while fractional use need not be. This is accounting for the declared construction, not an unconditional runtime lower bound.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: space closure}
If local workspace is \(n^{\sigma+o(1)}\) and a depth-first implementation keeps at most one explicit query buffer live at a time, put \(\theta_*=\max_j\theta_j\) and \(\rho=\max\{\sigma,\theta_*\}\). Then
\[
\boxed{S(n)\le n^{\rho+o(1)}}.
\]
If multiple query buffers remain simultaneously live, their aggregate size must be charged separately. Combining the exact closure with the established alternation-trading frontier gives the conditional sufficient collision
\[
\boxed{\max\left\{r,\max_j\frac{\eta_j}{1-\theta_j}\right\}+\max\{\sigma,\theta_*\}<2\cos(\pi/7).}
\]
