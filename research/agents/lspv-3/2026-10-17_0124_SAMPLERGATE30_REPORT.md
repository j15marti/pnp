THEOREM 1 (STRONG SAMPLER NECESSITY). Let a uniform polynomial-time generator G_n(r)=(F_r,w_r), with r uniform of poly(n) bits, output a proper source-visible 3-CNF F_r of length N between n and poly(n), and a valid full witness w_r. If every randomized PPT SAT finder A succeeds on F_r with negligible probability, then h_n(r)=F_r is a strong OWF. Proof: An inverter that finds any r' such that h_n(r')=F_r gives a valid SAT witness by regenerating G_n(r'), checking exact F and returning its verified w'. All acquisition and comparison costs are polynomial.

CONVERSE EXISTENCE. A strong OWF f_n yields such a certified hard SAT sampler: sample x, compute y=f_n(x), compile a functional proper-3CNF for f_n(x')=y, output planted x plus gate-value witness. Every satisfying assignment projects to a true preimage, so a SAT finder inverts f. Thus all-large-length, negligible-success randomized certified witness sampling is equivalent in existence to strong OWFs. This does NOT apply to merely infinitely-often Bogdanov-Talwar-Wan quasi-hardness, nor to existential deterministic UWI.

THEOREM 2 (ONE-WAY PUBLIC FORMULA, EASY SAT). For proper3CNF H_y=AND_i(a_i OR b_i OR c_i) encoding f(x)=y, set E(y)=AND_i [(s OR a_i OR q_i) AND (NOT q_i OR b_i OR c_i)], with fresh s and fresh q_i. In canonical encodings y is readable, and distinct targets have distinct E(y). If f is OWF, the public instance producer g(x)=E(f(x)) is OWF: inverting g would invert f by injectivity. Conversely inverting f permits inverting g by decoding y. Yet E(y) has a trivial satisfying assignment for EVERY y: s=1 and all other variables 0. Thus OWF hardness of seed->public-formula does not imply SAT search hardness. With s=0, existentially removing q_i recovers H_y. The minimal repair is all-witness projection: if EVERY witness w of E(y) had polytime L(y,w) satisfying f(L)=y, the easy dummy would give a polytime inverter, incompatible with f OWF.

EXPERIMENT. Nonlinear toy f_n(x)_i=x_i XOR (x_(i+1) AND x_(i+2)) encoded with AND/XOR gates and proper padding: 13n source clauses, 26n OR-shield clauses, 20n+1 variables. n=3..6: 120 targets, 5440 (x,y) pairs, 80 shuffled and variable-renamed formulas. A source-aware attacker identifies the selector by high positive-literal frequency and solves every perturbed formula. 11,484 main assertions and 80 independent truth-table cases passed, 0 discrepancies. Finite toy mapping is NOT cryptographically secure; tests verify gadget soundness and the easy escape only.
