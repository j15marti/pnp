\paragraph{Fixed calculus, quantifiers, and encoding.}
Use exactly the original $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ system of LSPV-2 MIXED-TSEITIN341, including its fixed types, signed pivots, conservative charged source-independent VARDEF gates and additional AND3, GF$(2)$ and LOCAL-CONVERT rules. The present certificates use \emph{only} ordinary signed binary Resolution, with one charged unit-weight node per distinct derived clause and zero for authentic source clauses. Shared nodes are counted once. There is no new inference rule and no semantic oracle. Input is valid encoded CNF (e.g. properly formed DIMACS, including empty-clause and duplicate handling); malformed strings are rejected. Let $v(F)$ denote the number of \emph{occurring} variable IDs, not the potentially enormous numeric DIMACS header bound, and let $N$ be encoded input \emph{bits}. In any valid explicit encoding, $v(F)\le N$.

\begin{theorem}[Frontier-budget refutation upper bound, standard DP reasoning instantiated in fixed $R$]
For every finite UNSAT CNF $F$, and \emph{every} elimination order $\sigma$ of its $v$ occurring variables, let $w_i(\sigma)$ be the number of still-present neighbors of the variable removed at stage $i$ in the usual \emph{filled} primal graph. Then an authenticated Resolution refutation exists with
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le W_{\mathrm{RES}}(F)\le
       \sum_{i=1}^{v}3^{w_i(\sigma)}
       \le v\,3^{w(\sigma)},\quad
       w(\sigma)=\max_i w_i(\sigma).}
\]
If the source already contains the empty clause, the minimum is zero, and the bound still holds. The argument is uniform in formula, order, and input length and makes no claim about a shortest refutation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a stage with elimination variable $x$, partition active clauses into $x$-positive, $x$-negative and $x$-free. For each complementary pair, take the exact signed-pivot resolvent and discard tautologies. Retain each \emph{distinct} non-tautological resulting clause at most once, with one genuine two-parent Resolution node; if an $x$-free copy already exists, reuse it. Remove clauses containing $x$ only from the \emph{working list} (not from historical DAG ancestry), and continue. This is the classical Davis--Putnam existential elimination identity, so the remaining CNF is equisatisfiable. All literals of a new clause are among the remaining filled neighbors of $x$, whose number is $w_i$; there are at most $3^{w_i}$ non-tautological clauses over those variables, including the empty clause (positive, negative or absent for each variable). Thus at most $3^{w_i}$ distinct new inferences are needed at this stage. Since the initial $F$ is UNSAT, the final variable-free CNF contains an empty clause. All inferences are legal in the \emph{same preselected} $R$; pruning unused DAG nodes decreases cost. Summing the stage bounds proves the claim. Neither source deletions nor optimizer discoveries are inference steps. The minimum exists because the Resolution construction gives a finite nonnegative integer cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A necessary, not sufficient, condition for a superpolynomial-hard family]
For $N=|\operatorname{encode}(F)|$ and primal treewidth $\mathrm{tw}(F)$,
\[
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le N\,3^{\mathrm{tw}(F)}.
\]
Consequently, any growing UNSAT sequence with superpolynomial $W_R(F)$ relative to $N$ has unbounded ratio $\mathrm{tw}(F)/\log(N+2)$ along its hard subsequence. A family with $\mathrm{tw}(F)=O(\log N)$ has polynomial minimum mixed proof cost. Conversely, \emph{large treewidth is not sufficient}: previously checked odd cubic Tseitin families have exact linear mixed proof cost.
\end{corollary}
\noindent The corollary is a direct screening criterion for candidate hard hypergraphs, not a new general proof-complexity lower bound. The standard parameterized upper bound $2^{O(w)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ is known; the displayed $3^{w_i}$ ledger is an explicit charged source-DAG implementation of it. For a sequence with no hard subsequence, no assertion of $\mathrm{tw}/\log N\to\infty$ is made.
