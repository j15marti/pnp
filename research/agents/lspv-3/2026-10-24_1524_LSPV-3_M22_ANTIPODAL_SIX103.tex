\section{Mathematical result}
For \(k=6\), let \(V=\{1,\ldots,6\}\), and let \(P,Q\) be four unsigned positive and four unsigned negative triple-support families, respectively. Both have vertex degree two. Put
\[
q=|P\setminus Q|=|Q\setminus P|=4-|P\cap Q|,
\qquad Q^\perp=\{V\setminus U:U\in Q\},\qquad
h=|P\cap Q^\perp|.
\]
The overlap \(h\) uses \emph{disjoint complementary supports across signs}; it is \emph{not} the same-support clause pairing used by \(q\).
\begin{theorem}[Antipodal witness count, PROVED/EXACT]\label{th:main}
Every \(F\in\CM\) on exactly six active variables has precisely
\[
\boxed{12+h(F)}
\]
satisfying assignments of Hamming weight three, where \(h(F)\in\{0,1,2,4\}\). In particular every such \(F\), including every six-variable target formula, is satisfiable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The twenty balanced assignments are indexed by their TRUE triples \(T\subseteq V\). A positive clause with support \(S\in P\) is false on precisely one balanced assignment, \(T=V\setminus S\). A negative clause with support \(U\in Q\) is false on precisely one, \(T=U\). Uniqueness makes each four-element forbidden list distinct internally. The two lists intersect in exactly \(h\) balanced assignments. Inclusion-exclusion leaves \(20-(4+4-h)=12+h\). Also \(Q^\perp\) has degree two at every vertex, because each vertex occurs in \(4-2=2\) complementary triples. If \(P\) and \(Q^\perp\) share three triples, their fourth triple is uniquely forced by subtracting common incidences from the shared degree vector \((2,2,2,2,2,2)\). Then all four coincide, showing \(h\ne3\).
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Full unary feasibility, PROVED/EXACT]
For any source variable \(v\) and either prescribed Boolean value \(b\), the six-variable formula admits a satisfying assignment with \(v=b\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Of the twenty balanced words, exactly ten set \(v=b\). Since at most eight balanced words are rejected overall, at least two satisfying words have the prescribed bit.
\end{proof}
The bound twelve is tight: \(P=\{123,145,246,356\}\) and \(Q=\{123,145,256,346\}\) give \(q=2,h=0\). The prior six-name source 102 with \(P=\{abu,abv,cuw,cvw\}\), \(Q=\{abu,abw,cuv,cvw\}\) has the \emph{same} \(q=2\), but \(h=2\), giving fourteen balanced and twenty-four total models. Hence complementary-pair defect alone does not determine source boundary witness counts.
