\section{BUILD: exact rank and reset theorems}
\begin{theorem}[Certified complete-state descent, elementary and known in ARC212]
Let one source-correct deterministic total controller receive length-N input and recognize a promised class within polynomial time. Every accepted nonterminal complete state includes all pending recursive calls, representations, source pointers, failed attempts, compatibility joins and certificate obligations. Suppose a nonnegative integer rank Phi is at most N^d initially, decreases by at least one after \emph{every} fully charged transition, each transition costs at most N^c, and every complete configuration occupies at most N^s work bits. Terminal SAT/UNSAT decisions are correct without an answer-class bit, and at most N^a final output bits are written separately or included in live workspace. Then time is O(N^{c+d}+N^a+T_recognition) and peak work space is O(N^s) with a separate output channel, or O(N^{max(s,a)}) if buffered.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A strictly decreasing nonnegative integer rank starting below N^d permits at most N^d nonterminal actions. Summing all full transition charges gives the time claim, and a single complete-configuration bound gives the peak space on the \emph{same} execution. Soundness is an explicit hypothesis and cannot be inferred from the rank.
\end{proof}
\begin{proposition}[Local-rank reset counterexample]
For each n there is a uniform O(n)-bit controller with two local ranks, each taking values only 0 and 1 and strictly decreasing whenever its own strategy executes, that takes 2^{n+1} total strategy actions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use n-bit counter j and phase p. A changes (j,0) to (j,1), decreasing r_A=1-p. B changes (j,1) to (j+1,0), decreasing r_B=p, and halts after j=2^n-1. Each resets the other's rank. Exactly 2^n A/B pairs are executed. A positive weighted sum alpha*r_A+beta*r_B must simultaneously satisfy alpha>beta and alpha<beta to decrease on A and B, impossible. Any global strictly decreasing nonnegative rank needs initial VALUE at least 2^{n+1}, although its encoding uses O(n) bits. This does NOT establish SAT hardness.
\end{proof}
In a weighted resource model Phi=alpha*b+beta*q+gamma*w+delta*e+rho*h+sigma*C, the \emph{whole} delta-Phi must be negative. The two abstract updates (-1,+2) and (+1,-2) in (b,q) cannot both decrease for any positive alpha,beta.
