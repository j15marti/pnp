\section{Rank obstruction and exact threshold}
\begin{lemma}[Component-parity fiber count]
Let $G$ be a finite undirected graph with $h$ vertices, $m$ edges and $c$ connected components. For any requested vertex-parity vector $b\in\mathbb F_2^h$, the system $Bx=b$ has exactly $2^{m-h+c}$ edge solutions if the XOR of $b$ over each connected component is zero, and zero solutions otherwise.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The edge-vertex incidence matrix over $\mathbb F_2$ has rank $h-c$. Its image consists exactly of vectors having even total parity in every connected component. Each attainable right-hand side has a kernel coset of dimension $m-h+c$.
\end{proof}
The original /386 checker first requires globally odd charge and validates every signed SOURCE occurrence and every strict paid RES. It also computes, \emph{only when} \texttt{edges.length <= 12}, a synthetic vector
\begin{verbatim}
let evenCharge=[0,...charge.slice(1)];
\end{verbatim}
and demands that its model count be $2^{m-h+1}$. This is the connected-graph count, not the correct count for $c>1$. Even when the synthetic charge is componentwise even, the correct value is $2^{m-h+c}$. For disconnected $G$ with $c\ge2$, the two cannot agree. If the synthetic charge is inconsistent, its model count is zero and likewise cannot agree with the positive target.
