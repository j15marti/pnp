\section*{Theorem: exact signed-neighbor fractional transversal}
Let $F=(C_1,\dots,C_m)$ be any clause-minimally UNSAT CNF with distinct nonempty non-tautological clauses. Let $L=\sum_i|C_i|$, fresh $z$, and
\[
H_z(F)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m[C_i\wedge(C_i\vee z)\wedge(C_i\vee\neg z)].
\]
Every source sign is an independent bit; the source layout, variable IDs and widths are fixed. For the set $Y$ of ALL satisfiable sign-only variants, let $D_y$ be the flipped bit coordinates and define fractional transversal
\[
\tau^*=\min\{\sum_jw_j:0\le w_j\le1,\ \sum_{j\in D_y}w_j\ge1\;\text{for all }y\in Y\}.
\]
\begin{theorem}
For every such $F$, deletion of any individual source clause or inversion of any individual literal polarity leaves UNSAT; removal of all three sources in any one triple block gives SAT. Moreover the sign-only nearest-SAT Hamming distance is exactly $2$, and both integer and fractional hitting numbers are EXACTLY $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each triple block enforces $C_i$. If one member is changed, the other two unchanged members still imply the original $C_i$; thus no one-clause or one-sign change yields SAT, and the nearest distance is at least two. Minimal UNSAT gives for each $i$ an assignment $a_i$ satisfying all original clauses except $C_i$, so all literals of $C_i$ are false at $a_i$. For each occurrence $(i,k)$, flip that sign in both base clause $C_i$ and the positive-guard clause $C_i\vee z$: the resulting entire CNF is SAT under $(a_i,z=0)$. Alternatively flip base and negative guard and take $z=1$. Hence distance two. The $L$ positive-guard flipped pairs are mutually disjoint support sets of size two, one per original occurrence. Every fractional hitting solution has total weight at least $L$. Selecting every one of the $L$ base-sign coordinates hits ALL SAT sign-only neighbors, because retaining those base signs retains the original UNSAT $F$. Thus $\tau^*=\tau=L$. Whole-block deletion is SAT by clause minimality.
\end{proof}
Using the independently known LSPV-1/390 coupling argument, any one fixed uniform total adaptive randomized SAT/UNSAT/malformed decider with error at most $\epsilon<1/2$ on every binary input probes in expectation at least $(1-2\epsilon)L$ distinct polarity bits on this particular explicit UNSAT source. Pair identical random tapes on the source and each SAT neighbor: their verdicts differ in probability by at least $1-2\epsilon$, so the differing bit-set must be probed with that probability. This is an INPUT-ACCESS result in a one-bit probe model, not a writable-space or machine-time separation.
\section*{Theorem: exact full-mixed proof cost}
For any connected simple bridgeless cubic $G=(V,E)$ with $h=|V|$ and an odd parity-charge vector $\chi$, its standard Tseitin CNF $F_{G,\chi}$ is clause-minimally UNSAT, with four original clauses per vertex (LSPV-2/341). In the SAME $R_{\rm mix341}$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(H_z(F_{G,\chi}))=4h-1.}
\]
The guarded source has $12h$ distinct clauses, $44h$ literal sign occurrences, zero individual criticality, nearest SAT sign distance two, fractional hitting number $12h$, and binary-index length $\Theta(h\log(h+2))$ bits.
\begin{proof}
Partition the signed original source labels into $4h$ triple blocks. Removing any block leaves SAT. In any sound refutation with only conservative auxiliary definitions the contradiction must therefore depend on at least one source from each block, hence at least $s\ge4h$ original source leaves. If $q$ paid nodes have at most two incoming edges, their connected ancestry graph has at least $s+q-1$ edges but no more than $2q$ edges. Consequently $q\ge s-1\ge4h-1$, irrespective of shared subproofs, adaptive ordering, circuit switches, legal LOCAL-CONVERT, GF(2) or DEF. For the upper bound all old original clauses are still source clauses. Three legal AND3 conjunctions per vertex recover its exact three-variable parity row from its four original clauses. Then $h-1$ legal binary XORs yield the odd global sum $0=1$. The constructed proof has $3h+h-1=4h-1$ paid nodes. The global minimum is attained, no depth cutoff.
\end{proof}
By the PRIOR published LSPV-2/393 Resolution restriction isometry, on certified expander odd Tseitin families the identical guarded source still requires $2^{\Omega(h)}$ ordinary DAG Resolution nodes, but has only $4h-1$ mixed nodes. The proof-system discrepancy is not unrestricted computational hardness and not B1/B2/B3 falsification.
