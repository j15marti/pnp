\begin{abstract}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT in a restricted, explicitly stated source-block query model; independent mathematical review pending.} For a connected canonical cubic-prism XOR-to-proper-3CNF grammar with $q$ independently source-encoded charges, any deterministic exact terminal SAT selector must inspect every original vertex block on every legal input. A bounded-error randomized selector must inspect all $q$ blocks with probability at least $1-2\epsilon$ under uniform charges. The same source authenticates an exact cut-parity certificate forbidding $2^{k-1}$ partial assignments to the $k$ rung variables. These facts are established parity decision-tree and graph-incidence consequences, not unrestricted SAT hardness. A streaming recognizer produces a constant-length ordinary SAT child for this grammar, but unrecognized inputs require a complete fallback. Sixteen both-label source controls and an independent verifier test the resulting controller and its total selected work. The universal, hereditary, fully charged $A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ ARC compiler remains \textbf{OPEN}; $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textbf{OPEN}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact input grammar and three falsifiers}
Let $k\geq3$, let $H_k$ be the prism with two $k$-cycles and $k$ rungs, $q=2k$ vertices and $n=3k$ distinct edge variables. For every vertex $v$, read the three incident edge names in canonical increasing order. Given charge $b_v\in\{0,1\}$, emit the four \emph{original}, properly signed three-clauses that exclude exactly the four local assignments with XOR unequal to $b_v$. Keep the $q$ authenticated four-clause blocks in vertex order. Denote their conjunction $F_{k,b}$. Each block authenticates the exact equation
\[
 \bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v.
\]
The experiment's declared physical ordinary-CNF bit encoding is a unary self-delimited variable count and clause count, unary self-delimited width for every clause, and one sign plus $\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$ variable-ID bits per literal. Thus $N=\Theta(q\log q)$ on the prism family; the encoding also supports arbitrary-width ordinary CNF, including empty clauses.

\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{F1, source-acquisition falsifier.} Can a uniform exact controller choose the constant SAT/UNSAT child while omitting one original charge block? \textbf{FALSIFIED} in the declared block-access model by the theorem below. Reading a supplied class label or a hidden syndrome is disallowed.
\item[]\textbf{F2, sound cut-pruning falsifier.} Can a rung assignment with wrong source-authenticated top-cycle parity nevertheless extend to an original-source SAT assignment? \textbf{FALSIFIED} algebraically and by exhaustive original-source checks. A correct rung pattern may still fail because of other constraints; the implication is one-way.
\item[]\textbf{F3, actual work falsifier.} Does an entailed learned cut that prunes more DPLL nodes always lower fully selected work? \textbf{FALSIFIED} for the SAT prism controls: cut checking adds work without deleting any nodes. \section{A tight restricted terminal-policy acquisition gate}
\begin{theorem}[Source-block decision necessity; restricted and classical]
On the promise family $\{F_{k,b}:b\in\{0,1\}^{q}\}$, give a selector $q$ addressable original vertex blocks, each query revealing the complete four-clause block. Every deterministic exact terminal decision procedure, even with unbounded local computation and adaptive block addresses, must query every distinct block on \emph{every} legal input. For any randomized terminal procedure having pointwise two-sided error at most $\epsilon<1/2$, under uniform independent charges $b$,
\[
 \Pr[\text{all }q\text{ blocks queried}]\ge1-2\epsilon,
 \qquad \mathbb E[\#\text{distinct block queries}]\ge(1-2\epsilon)q.
\]
The lower bound also forces any deterministic bit-probe selector to touch at least one original input bit in each block. It is a \emph{block/query} bound $\Omega(q)=\Omega(N/\log N)$, not an unconditional time-space lower bound for SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The four original clauses in block $v$ are satisfied exactly by the local assignments obeying the displayed edge parity equation. Summing the $q$ equations cancels every edge variable twice, so satisfiability requires $\bigoplus_v b_v=0$. Because the prism is connected, a spanning-tree solution of the incidence system exists whenever that total charge vanishes; hence this condition is also sufficient. In particular, changing exactly $b_v$ toggles the SAT label while changing \emph{only} the original block $v$. If a deterministic procedure halts without querying $v$, it has the same query transcript and terminal decision on $b$ and $b\oplus e_v$, a contradiction. This applies to every input and every unqueried block.

For the randomized claim, expose uniform $b$ and the random tape. Conditioned on any halting transcript leaving at least one unqueried block, the XOR of the unseen independent charges, and hence the correct SAT label, is uniform. Success on such a transcript is $1/2$; success after observing all blocks is at most one. Therefore average success is at most $1/2+\Pr[\text{all blocks}]/2$. Average success is at least $1-\epsilon$ under the pointwise error assumption, giving the first inequality. On the all-read event the query count is at least $q$, establishing the second. Arbitrary bit probes can only obtain the distinguishing charge of a block by touching that block.\qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Source-authenticated boundary parity; known GF(2) cut identity]
For every vertex subset $S$ of a graph whose source-authenticated vertex equations are as above, every satisfying assignment satisfies
\[
 \bigoplus_{e\in\delta(S)}x_e=\bigoplus_{v\in S}b_v.
\]
For the top $k$ vertices of $H_k$, $\delta(S)$ is precisely the $k$ rung variables. Exactly $2^{k-1}$ rung cubes are therefore forbidden by this certificate, for \emph{either} satisfiability label of the entire source.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
XOR the authenticated original vertex equations indexed by $S$. Each edge internal to $S$ occurs twice and cancels; each boundary edge occurs once. Hence any assignment with the opposite boundary parity contradicts those same original clauses. Recording the row indices $S$, the resulting edge-incidence XOR and the RHS gives a directly checkable source certificate. Its acquisition and checking cost includes reading all four clauses of every chosen vertex block and scanning their signed edge names. The lemma does not make an incorrect unrestricted UNSAT conclusion from half of the boundary assignments.\qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Companion exact constructive bound, previously established.} For this canonical grammar a deterministic streaming recognizer validates the $4q$ signed source clauses, computes the charge XOR, and emits either the ordinary unit-clause SAT source $(z)$ or the ordinary contradictory pair $(z)\wedge(\neg z)$. It uses $O(N)$ bit-time and $O(\log N)$ auxiliary writable bits in the explicitly \emph{streaming} model. The lower bound above shows that sampling fewer than $q$ independent original blocks cannot replace this complete acquisition. The Python implementation materializes the entire source and is \emph{not} an implementation of that space bound. Verification and emission of a full original-source witness, if demanded, must be charged independently.
