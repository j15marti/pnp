\section*{Published hard-family anchor and fixed combined system}
The starting known theorem is the expander-Tseitin ordinary Resolution lower bound of Urquhart (1987), with the Ben-Sasson--Wigderson width-size method (2001): there are constants \(c>0\) and an infinite family of bounded-degree expander graphs of orders \(h\) such that \emph{for every} family member and \emph{every unrestricted shared-DAG ordinary Resolution} refutation \(\pi\) of its odd-charge Tseitin CNF, \( |\pi|\ge 2^{ch}\). For cubic graphs the source has \(3h/2\) edge variables and \(4h\) ternary clauses, and a signed indexed binary serialization has \(N_h=\Theta(h\log(h+2))\) bits. These quantifiers concern a restricted \emph{proof system}, not every algorithm. Extended Resolution, parity reasoning, conservative gates and arbitrary machines are outside the theorem. The project's same-source cubic Tseitin control already has a \(4h-1\) mixed proof.

The preselected \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) remains exactly as recorded in research/agents/lspv-2/2026-11-02\_1216\_LSPV-2\_MIXED\_TSEITIN\_MIN\_DAG341.tex: free authenticated signed source clauses, unit-cost distinct shared-DAG RES, AND3, GF(2) addition, legal LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative binary DEF. This report proves a theorem \emph{only} in the typed XOR stage after the relevant sources are authenticated. It never assumes polynomial-cost translation for every combined rule. \(W_R(F)\) is the attained minimum of distinct paid derivation nodes, with free choice of derivation, and is separate from runtime.

\section*{Reverse-adjoint certificate theorem}
Let \(m,d,q\) be arbitrary nonnegative integers, let \(r_1,\ldots,r_m\in\mathbb F_2^{d+1}\) be authenticated affine source rows representing equations \(a_i\cdot x=b_i\), and let \(L=\sum_i|\operatorname{supp}(a_i)|\). A typed certificate is any acyclic shared DAG of \(q\) binary XOR gates, each with two earlier parent indices; identical parents and reconvergent uses are allowed. Its root claims \(t=(0,\ldots,0,1)\).

\begin{theorem}[uniform reverse-adjoint authentication]
For every such authenticated source collection, every acyclic typed XOR DAG and every designated root, one fixed deterministic verifier accepts if and only if the implicit root equation is \(0=1\). On a word RAM with \(\Theta(\log(m+q+d+2))\)-bit index words, after source authentication its resource bounds are \(O(q+m+L+d)\) operations and \(O(q+m+d)\) auxiliary bits, apart from input storage and parsing buffers. The encoded parent-pointer portion of the certificate has \(O(q\log(m+q+2))\) bits. An actual bit-machine execution can simulate this with polynomial overhead, not an unconditional SAT decision-time claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assign coefficient one to the root and zero to all other nodes. Visit XOR gates in reverse topological order. Whenever the current coefficient of a gate \(v=u\oplus w\) is one, toggle the coefficients of its two parents and clear the coefficient of \(v\). The invariant is that the weighted affine sum of the remaining nodes and of source leaves is the original root row. It holds because \(r_v=r_u\oplus r_w\). For a repeated parent, both toggles cancel correctly. After all gates, the root equals \(\bigoplus_{i:\alpha_i=1}r_i\). XOR these selected sparse sources into a single \(d+1\)-bit accumulator and compare it to \(t\). All typed gates are checked for proper parent indices before traversal. Processing at most two edges per gate, every selected source once, and every output coordinate once proves the claimed operation and buffer bounds. Source validation must independently establish that each \(r_i\) is a legal same-source inference from the original formula. Then a claimed contradiction is sound. This works even when an expanded derivation tree has exponentially many paths.
\end{proof}

\section*{Concrete representation-independent countercontrol}
Use the contradictory affine rows \(r_0=(x=0)\) and \(r_1=(x=1)\). For each integer \(k\ge0\) define a shared circuit \(r_j=r_{j-1}\oplus r_{j-2}\), \(2\le j\le3k+2\). GF(2) gives the period-three sequence \(r_0,r_1,r_0\oplus r_1,r_0,\ldots\), hence the final row is \(0=1\). There are \(3k+1\) paid gates but \(F_{3k+3}\) leaf occurrences in a fully unfolded tree; at output index \(62\), 61 gates give \(6{,}557{,}470{,}319{,}842\) unfolded leaves. The reverse verifier touches the 61 gates and two sources once each. A direct one-gate proof exists, so the Fibonacci construction is a \emph{counterexample to charging expanded-path multiplicity}, not a lower bound or a minimum-cost claim. Replacing the second source by \(x=0\) produces a satisfiable control, rejected by the checker.

Source authentication is essential. For each cubic Tseitin vertex, its claimed affine row is justified only after verifying the exact four signed source clauses encoding its local parity. Missing this step would permit false UNSAT certificates for SAT CNFs. Nor may a nonlinear gate be silently retyped as XOR: the truth-table four-corner parity of \(x\land y\) equals one, while it is zero for every affine Boolean function.

\section*{Reproducibility, falsifiers and physical cost}
The deterministic public Node.js file experiments/arc/lspv3\_381\_reverse\_affine\_dag.js and raw JSON results/raw/arc/2026-11-03\_1924\_LSPV3\_REVERSE\_AFFINE\_DAG381\_raw.json provide 1,188 checks. They include 1,152 seeded (381) shared XOR DAG comparisons against a separately implemented forward dense-row evaluator, odd/even \(K_4,K_{3,3}\), cube model-count controls \(0/8,0/16,0/32\), four-clause source authentication, wrong-sign rejection, eight malformed/corrupt records, and the Fibonacci example. The checks are finite computations, not exhaustive proof minimization.

\begin{description}
\item[] F1. Shared gates, repeated parents or cancellation invalidate reverse propagation. FALSIFIED by the symbolic induction, with forward-evaluator controls.
\item[] F2. Exponentially many unfolded proof paths force exponential machine checking. FALSIFIED by the Fibonacci indexed-DAG family.
\item[] F3. A bare source-free root identity is a sound UNSAT proof. FALSIFIED: an attacker can attach unrelated contradictory affine sources; original source authentication is mandatory.
\item[] F4. The affine checker extends automatically to nonlinear LOCAL-CONVERT or arbitrary adaptive SAT algorithms. FALSE: neither such proof translation nor a reflection theorem has been supplied.
\end{description}

Physical costs must account for parsing, binary proof length, all source and parent-index reads, workspace allocation, verification and any construction or search. Expanded intermediate equations are \emph{not} forced materializations. The proof-DAG node minimum, proof search, output bits, and peak machine space are distinct. For any charged child simulation, retain \(s'\le\max\{\rho,\eta,\lambda,\lambda s\}\), with \(\rho\) including every local phase. Exponentially large representations may be used, but their real materialization costs count. The new verifier is polynomial \emph{certificate checking} only; it is not polynomial-time general CNF-SAT decision.
