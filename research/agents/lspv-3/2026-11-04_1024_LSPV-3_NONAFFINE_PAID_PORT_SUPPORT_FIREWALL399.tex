\section*{All-size support-amplification theorem}
For integer $m\ge1$, write
\[
 I_m(x)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}(x_{2i-1}\wedge x_{2i}),\quad
 S(a,b,y)=(a\vee y)\wedge(b\vee\neg y),\quad
 T_m(a,b,x)=S(a,b,I_m(x)).
\]
\begin{theorem}
The original-variable support of $S$ on independent named inputs $a,b,y$ is exactly three, while the essential semantic original-variable support of $T_m$ on $a,b,x_1,\dots,x_{2m}$ is exactly $2m+2$. The AND3 conjoining the two source clauses of $S$ is a legal one-node factor when $y$ is an original variable of an augmented source. Literal replay of that factor after replacing $y$ by a paid conservative circuit $y=I_m(x)$ fails the at-most-three-original-variable AND3 type test. Any CNF on just $a,b,x$ describing $T_m=1$ has at least $2^m$ distinct clauses and $\Omega(m2^m\log(m+2))$ fixed-width signed-ID bits. In contrast the same relation has a conservative two-input circuit of $2m+2$ paid gates and one output constraint with $O(m\log(m+2))$ encoded bits. This is a failure of \emph{literal rule transport} and of \emph{forced auxiliary erasure}, not a lower bound on arbitrary different mixed derivations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $(y,b)=(0,1)$ to make $S=a$, $(y,a)=(1,1)$ to make $S=b$, and $(a,b)=(0,1)$ to make $S=y$. For $T_m$, the same settings show $a,b$ pivotal with $I_m$ respectively zero and one. To make any $x_j$ pivotal, set $a=0,b=1$, its paired variable to one, and all other pairs to zero. Hence support grows to $2m+2$.
\par Restrict an arbitrary auxiliary-free CNF for $T_m=1$ to $a=0,b=1$; the restricted formula represents $I_m=1$ with no more clauses. For each $\sigma\in\{0,1\}^m$ form a zero-input witness $w_\sigma$ whose $i$th pair is $(0,1)$ or $(1,0)$ according to $\sigma_i$. No clause can be false at two different witnesses, since its falsifying subcube would then contain the hybrid taking $(1,1)$ on a differing pair and retaining zero-product choices elsewhere, an input with $I_m=1$. Hence at least $2^m$ clauses. Every falsifying clause must fix a coordinate in each pair; leaving both free lets toggling the product change parity, so each restricted clause has width at least $m$. The bit bound follows.
\par Define $m$ AND2 gates for products and $m-1$ XOR2 gates for parity, a total of $2m-1$. Then two signed AND gates compute $\neg(a\vee y)$ and $\neg(b\vee\neg y)$, and their complemented signed views feed one final AND gate for $S$. Thus $2m+2$ paid binary gates, plus a final output constraint, suffice. No exponential explicit original-only CNF is required.
\end{proof}
