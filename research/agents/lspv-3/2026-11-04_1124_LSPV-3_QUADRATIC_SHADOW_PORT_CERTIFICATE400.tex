\section*{All-size elementary proof}
\begin{lemma}[Affine freezing, no intermediate degree restriction]
If $f:\mathbb F_2^n\to\mathbb F_2$ is computed by a shared XAG with $k$ AND gates, there exists a nonempty affine flat $H=a+U$ of codimension at most $k$ such that $f|_H$ is affine.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Order all AND gates topologically. Inductively assume outputs of preceding AND gates are constant on a nonempty affine flat. Their descendants through XOR and NOT, and all primary inputs, are affine on this flat, so both inputs to the next AND gate are affine. If both are constant, so is the output. Otherwise select one nonconstant affine input and restrict the flat to its zero set, a nonempty codimension-one affine hyperplane. The AND output becomes zero, while all preceding AND outputs remain constant. At most one dimension is lost per AND. After all $k$ ANDs, every wire and hence the output is affine on the surviving flat. The argument allows shared high-degree nonlinear intermediate wires.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Quadratic-shadow restriction lower bound]
Suppose there is an injective affine map $\rho:\mathbb F_2^d\to\mathbb F_2^n$ such that $g=f\circ\rho$ has algebraic degree at most two and quadratic polarization
$$B_g(s,t)=g(s+t)+g(s)+g(t)+g(0)$$
of rank $2r$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Then every XAG circuit for $f$ has at least $r$ AND gates, even under arbitrary shared nonlinear intermediate gates. In particular,
$$\boxed{\mathrm{MC}_{\mathrm{XAG}}(I_m)=m}.$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Substituting affine inputs according to $\rho$ never increases the number of AND gates. Apply the freezing lemma to $g$, obtaining a flat $a+U$ of codimension at most $k$ on which $g$ is affine. Since $g$ is quadratic, $B_g(s,t)=0$ for all $s,t\in U$. In a basis extending $U$, the polarization matrix has a zero $\dim U$-by-$\dim U$ block. Thus its rank is at most $2(d-\dim U)\le2k$, giving $k\ge r$. For $I_m$, the polarization is a direct sum of $m$ nonsingular $2$-by-$2$ alternating blocks, hence has rank $2m$. The standard circuit uses exactly $m$ AND gates followed by $m-1$ XOR gates, attaining the bound.
\end{proof}
\section*{Application to the prior nonaffine named port}
For $S(a,b,y)=(a\lor y)\land(b\lor\neg y)$ and $T_m=S(a,b,I_m)$, the identity
$$S(a,b,y)=a\oplus((a\oplus b)\wedge y)$$
gives $T_m$ a compact circuit using $m+1$ AND gates and $m+1$ XOR gates, $2m+2$ total binary gates, with $O(m\log(m+2))$ bits in standard fixed-width gate-reference encoding. Restrict $a=0,b=1$ to recover $I_m$ and apply the theorem: \emph{every} XAG implementation of $T_m$ needs at least $m$ AND gates. Its exact nonlinear-gate optimum is not established here. Earlier /399 proved that forcibly eliminating all auxiliary wires instead produces at least $2^m$ clauses in original-only CNF. Thus representation changes alter explicit evidence size exponentially, but a narrower rank-based nonlinear-gate invariant survives arbitrary XAG circuit sharing and intermediate nonlinear reuse.
