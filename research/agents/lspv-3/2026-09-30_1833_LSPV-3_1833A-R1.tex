\textbf{Exact predicate.} Fix SAME_REALIZATION size m, deterministic Q normalized to exactly m, Arthur randomness r, and output index i. Let Phi_s, Phi_0, Phi_1 be the three SAT formulas for existence of a successful response and successful responses outputting 0 or 1. For answer vector a,
\[
\exists Y^+\forall Y^-\;\bigwedge_{j\in\{s,0,1\}}
[(a_j\to\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge(\neg a_j\to\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))].
\]
Writing E_b = exists w [S(w) and B(w)=b], exact nonfailure output b is
\[
E_b\wedge\neg E_{1-b}
\equiv
\exists w_+\forall w_-[
S(w_+)\wedge B(w_+)=b\wedge
\neg(S(w_-)\wedge B(w_-)=1-b)].
\]
Status: PROVED/EXACT as logical expansion of the three SAT answers. This does not assert pointwise uniqueness.

\textbf{Viola audit.} Viola proves BPTime(t) contained in Sigma_3 Time(t polylog t). In the proof's schematic prefix exists sigma, forall u, exists ell, an ell-dependent SAT query substituted with exact YES/NO semantics gives
\[
\exists\sigma\forall u\exists(\ell,a,Y^+)\forall Y^-\;R'.
\]
Moving Y^- before ell changes its dependency. Thus qualitative Sigma_3^NP=Sigma_3 does not by itself certify near-linear quantitative flattening. Direct fourth-block obstruction: PROVED/EXACT. A different dependency-preserving near-linear oracle elimination: OPEN.
