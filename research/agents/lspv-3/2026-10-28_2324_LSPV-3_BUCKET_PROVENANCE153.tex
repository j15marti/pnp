\end{enumerate}

\section{BUILD: exact clause projection and provenance}
For a CNF $C$ and variable $x$, write $C^+$ for clauses $(x\lor A_i)$, $C^-$ for $(\neg x\lor B_j)$ and $C^0$ for clauses not mentioning $x$. Ignore tautological resolvents. The ordinary Davis--Putnam projection is
\[
 \operatorname{DP}_x(C)=C^0\ \wedge\!\bigwedge_{i,j}(A_i\lor B_j).
\]
\begin{lemma}[Exact, not merely satisfiability-preserving, projection]\label{lem:dp}
For each assignment to all variables other than $x$,
\[\exists x\,C\ \equiv\ \operatorname{DP}_x(C).\]
Each non-tautological produced clause is derived by a \emph{single} source-identified resolution from its two explicitly recorded parents.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For any fixed assignment to the remaining names, satisfying $C^0$ and all $(A_i\lor B_j)$ is necessary. Conversely, if no value of $x$ works, some positive clause forces $x=1$ and some negative clause forces $x=0$, making one $A_i\lor B_j$ false, a contradiction. The resolvent is a standard sound resolution step; the authenticated parent IDs and pivot determine its exact literals, so a separate verifier can recompute it without trusting the producer.
\end{proof}

Let $\pi=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ be an explicitly given ordering and let $w$ be the largest number of distinct still-active \emph{other} variables jointly present with $x_i$ in current clauses at the instant $x_i$ is eliminated. This is the elimination neighborhood width, not just raw original clause width. Duplicate derived clauses are merged with recorded identifiers. Keep each eliminated bucket and a DAG of its original-ID or previously derived-ID clauses for reconstruction and verification.

\begin{theorem}[Paid two-sided acquisition with a given width-$w$ order]\label{thm:bucket}
A single deterministic algorithm, with no answer-class advice, takes a properly encoded CNF $F$ and ordering $\pi$, computes its complete successive exact projections, and either produces a genuine satisfying assignment or a source-authenticated resolution DAG ending with the empty clause. The number of distinct newly derived non-tautological clauses is at most $n3^w$, and the number of candidate resolvent pairs is $O(n9^w)$, in addition to the original source and its parsing and index work. With explicit source IDs, stored eliminated buckets and polynomially charged bookkeeping, the producer and its polynomial-time verifier both run in $2^{O(w)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time and use at most $2^{O(w)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ physical space. For $w=O(\log N)$ these are polynomial bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:dp} to every variable in order. A clause generated when $x_i$ is removed mentions only the then-current neighborhood $U_i$, of cardinality at most $w$. Every non-tautological clause over $U_i$ has each variable absent, positive, or negative, hence there are at most $3^w$ distinct such clauses. Each parent of a non-tautological resolvent contains $x_i$ in one polarity and uses no names outside $U_i$; there are at most $3^w$ distinct parents of each sign, yielding at most $9^w$ candidate pairs. Retain the original $m$ clauses plus these derived clauses as a source-ID DAG. Scans of at most $m+n3^w$ active/database clauses, source pointer storage of $O(\log(m+n3^w))$ bits and literal comparisons add only polynomial factors in $N$ and $2^w$.

If the final projection is false, the precise first empty clause is either an original source clause or a derived resolution node. Its full DAG with source parents is a sound UNSAT refutation, and direct checking of each edge requires polynomial time in the emitted DAG size. If the projection is true, reverse through the stored buckets. At each stage, the current assignment to later variables satisfies the exact projection; Lemma~\ref{lem:dp} guarantees that at least one bit choice for the eliminated variable satisfies every clause in its pre-elimination bucket. Testing both bits reconstructs a satisfying assignment, which is finally verified on \emph{the same} original instance. All unsuccessful bit tests, source reads, retained buckets, ordering checks and output bytes are charged.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Two-parent resolvents are RUP/LRAT steps]\label{prop:lrat}
Suppose a resolution step has parents $(x\lor A)$ and $(\neg x\lor B)$ and non-tautological resolvent $R=A\lor B$. Then the ordered positive LRAT hint list containing the two parent source IDs certifies that $R$ is redundant by reverse unit propagation. Therefore the UNSAT DAG produced by Theorem~\ref{thm:bucket}, in topological order and ending with its derived empty clause, can be serialized into an LRAT-style clausal proof with at most two positive hints per new clause.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Negate every literal of $R$. Under those assumptions $A$ and $B$ are false, so the first parent forces $x$, and the second parent becomes false, exhibiting a unit-propagation conflict. The actual IDs and clause literals are parsed and rechecked in the audit. No completeness claim for arbitrary LRAT generators is needed.
\end{proof}

\section{Counterfeit semantic certificate and format limitations}\label{sec:counterfeit}
Let $F=(x\lor y\lor z)$ and eliminate $x$. Its exact projection is $\top$, since $x=1$ works for \emph{every} setting of $y,z$. A malicious certificate can declare that no feasible boundary rows exist. A checker that merely verifies witnesses for each \emph{listed} row accepts this empty table vacuously, yet its negative claim is false. Thus a complete UNSAT summary requires an exclusion/completeness obligation for \emph{every omitted} row. In Theorem~\ref{thm:bucket} this obligation is discharged by authenticated exhaustive resolvent generation, not by assuming small relation size.

The resolution/LRAT DAG is an \emph{augmented} proof portfolio compared with the narrower original-clause 2CNF-SCC adaptive-leaf grammar. No polynomial simulation into a complete strict original-source SCC branching tree is proved. The produced intermediate clauses can have width greater than three and no longer satisfy signed exact-$(2,2)$ occurrence counts. A strict ordinary-3CNF re-encoding requires fresh Tseitin variables; two actual hereditary SAT/UNSAT child levels were explicitly constructed and measured below. Their sizes do not shrink at a fixed power.
