\section{Exact source-side graph and matching certificate}
For each sign $\epsilon$, define $G_\epsilon(F)$ as a loopless cubic multigraph whose vertices are the \emph{indexed original clauses} of sign $\epsilon$. Original variable $x_i$ labels an edge joining the two source clauses of that sign containing it. Every graph has $n$ labelled edges and $2n/3$ vertices. A negative source clause $D=(\neg x_a\vee\neg x_b\vee\neg x_c)$ is \emph{dangerous} for $G_+$ when its three labelled positive edges are pairwise nonincident. Let $q_+(F)$ count these clauses, and $q_-(F)$ be the swapped-sign analogue. Source occurrences are never independent Boolean variables.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform one-third edge marginals, known Edmonds transfer]
In any finite loopless bridgeless cubic multigraph $G$, there is a probability distribution over perfect matchings with marginal $\Pr[e\in M]=1/3$ for each \emph{labelled} edge $e$. Thus removing any at most two labelled edges leaves a perfect matching.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $z_e=1/3$. Each vertex has incident sum $1$. For every odd vertex set $S$, the cut size is odd by $3|S|=2|E(S)|+|\delta(S)|$. A zero odd cut is impossible because every cubic component has an even number of vertices, and a one-edge cut would be a bridge. Thus $|\delta(S)|\ge3$ and $z(\delta(S))\ge1$. These are the degree and odd-cut constraints of Edmonds' perfect matching polytope \cite{edmonds}; therefore $z$ is a convex combination of incidence vectors of perfect matchings. For any $Q\subseteq E$ with $|Q|\le2$, the expected number of edges of $Q$ in a sampled perfect matching is $|Q|/3<1$. One matching contains none of $Q$, hence exists in $G-Q$, where an exact polynomial maximum-matching algorithm can find it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Source-authenticated bridgeless two-dangerous-clause SAT terminal]
For \emph{every valid canonical} $F$, if $G_+$ is bridgeless and $q_+(F)\le2$, a uniform polynomial procedure constructs and original-source verifies a complete SAT witness. The symmetric statement holds for $G_-$ and $q_-(F)\le2$. Neither statement certifies UNSAT on rejection.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose one label $e_D$ from each dangerous negative original clause and delete the set $Q$ of those at most two labels from $G_+$. Obtain a perfect matching $M$ from the lemma. Set original variable $x_i$ TRUE precisely when its positive edge is in $M$. Every positive clause has exactly one selected incident variable. Each nondangerous negative clause has two edges sharing a positive vertex, which cannot both belong to $M$, and therefore has at least one FALSE variable. Each dangerous negative clause has its designated deleted label $e_D\notin M$, and is likewise satisfied. Replay all original signed clauses to authenticate the witness. For the negative-graph policy, swap all original signs, construct a witness as above, then complement all the original assignment bits. Both sides retain full source-level semantics and source indices.
\end{proof}
