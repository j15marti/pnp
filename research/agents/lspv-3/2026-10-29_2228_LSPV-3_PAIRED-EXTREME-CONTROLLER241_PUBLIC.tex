\section{Recognized exact pairing theorem}
For arbitrary proper 2CNF $G=\bigwedge_{j=1}^M D_j$ on core variables,
and private fresh pivots $p_j$, define the proper source 3CNF
\[
F_G=\bigwedge_{j=1}^M
   \big[(D_j\vee p_j)\wedge(D_j\vee\neg p_j)\big].
\]
\begin{theorem}[Paired-pivot equivalence, PROVED/EXACT]
Every paired source clause is assigned exactly once to a verified
positive/negative occurrence pair on its private pivot, and
\[
 (D_j\vee p_j)\wedge(D_j\vee\neg p_j)\equiv D_j.
\]
Consequently $F_G$ is equisatisfiable with $G$, every core witness
extends by arbitrary pivot values, and every UNSAT implication cycle
of $G$ yields an authenticated source refutation. Both paired source
clauses have $SS=SU=US=UU=\text{false}$ for their local all-literal
extremes, despite being simultaneously disjunctively SAT whenever
$D_j$ is true. The falsifying two-core-literal cube for $D_j$ is
impossible, certified by resolution on pivot $p_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $D_j$ is true both parents are true; if it is false the two
parents force $p_j$ and $\neg p_j$ simultaneously. The source
resolvent is exactly $D_j$. The parents share two core occurrences
with the same polarity and pivot with opposite polarity, so every
S/U pair mode conflicts. A deterministic solver verifies occurrence
counts, complementary pivots, identical signed remainders and disjoint
full source-clause coverage, then computes 2SAT SCCs; failed discovery
is charged and falls back to general DPLL. The SCC implication paths
and their source-parent clause identifiers form checkable certificates.
\end{proof}
Example source indices $C_0=(a\vee b\vee p)$,
$C_1=(a\vee b\vee\neg p)$ have local targets
$S_0=(1,1,1)$, $\neg S_0=U_0=(0,0,0)$,
$S_1=(1,1,0)$, $\neg S_1=U_1=(0,0,1)$,
where coordinates are $(a,b,p)$. The legal $(1,0,0)$ yields true-literal
counts $(1,2)$ and its legal complement $(0,1,1)$ yields $(2,1)$.
Resolution produces $(a\vee b)$ and forbids the entire cube $a=b=0$.
\section{Proved specified-policy exponential gaps, not general hardness}
Let $G_M^{U}$ have the four binary clauses
$(a\vee b),(a\vee\neg b),(\neg a\vee b),(\neg a\vee\neg b)$,
and $M-4$ extra copies of $(a\vee b)$, paired with $M$ fresh pivots.
A chronological UP-first DPLL branching on all pivots before $a,b$,
with no learning/memoization, visits \emph{exactly}
$2^{M+2}-1$ recursion nodes. No source unit exists until a core
variable is chosen; after all pivots are assigned a branch on $a$
forces both $b,\neg b$. The complete pivot tree has $2^{M+1}-1$
nodes and the $2^M$ leaves gain two conflict children each.
The source-recognizing paired-pivot/2SAT controller is polynomial on
this class, including failed endpoint/flip paths and pair checks.
A core-first UP-DPLL visits only seven nodes on the same inputs:
the exponential gap is only for the specified pivot-first policy.

Let $G_M^S=(a\vee b)\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg b)$ plus
$M-2$ copies of $(a\vee b)$, similarly paired. Index the pivots
as low Gray bits before $a,b$. For reflected Gray
$g(i)=i\oplus(i\gg1)$, $i<2^M$ keeps $a=b=0$;
the first satisfying candidate is number $2^M+1$.
The first source clause falsified at $0^{M+2}$ starts with $a$,
so a literal-order first-clause repair flips $a$ and finds a legal
SAT witness after one attempted flip, after separately checking both
global endpoints. Fixed Gray and this fixed repair are not bounds
on arbitrary adaptive solvers.
\section{Resource and finite computational records}
The single RAM experiment computes every indexed source occurrence's
$S,U,\neg S,\neg U$, every $SS,SU,US,UU$ pair matrix, checks
$0^n,1^n$, complement and multiple flips, runs a bounded
source-first complete-candidate repair, then source-authenticated
paired 2SAT, XOR3 Gaussian elimination, direct 2SAT, Horn and
fallback UP-DPLL. Every failed candidate and recognizer is charged,
all SAT witnesses checked against original sources, and SCC/XOR
UNSAT paths replayed. The experiment preserves the full formula;
mode tables require $O(m^2\log N)$ bits, and checked complete flips
$O(nm)$ source reads. A conservative deterministic abstract bit-model
upper bound is $O(N^3)$ time and $O(N^2\log N)$ space on recognized
paired input including diagnostics. General fallback may be
exponential. The selected-work counts are an explicitly declared
instrumented operation proxy, not exact machine steps or physical
head crossings; elapsed time and simultaneous \texttt{tracemalloc}
peak bytes are separately included in raw JSON.
