\section{BUILD I: signed incidence gauge, projection, and counting}
A source-indexed proper 3CNF is a sequence $F=(C_1,\ldots,C_m)$ with three \emph{distinct} variables per clause, indexed original clause IDs and ordered literal slots. Write $\operatorname{scope}(F)$ for the sequence of absolute variable IDs at every slot. For $f\in\{0,1\}^n$, let $\gamma_f(F)$ replace literal $\ell$ by $-\ell$ exactly when $f_{|\ell|}=1$. For an assignment $a$, define $(a\oplus f)_v=a_v\oplus f_v$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact source-gauge recognition and semantic commutation]\label{t:gauge}
Let $F,G$ have the same indexed ordered scope, and suppose each declared variable occurs at least once. Then $G=\gamma_f(F)$ for some $f\in\{0,1\}^n$ if and only if, for each variable $v$, the bit $\mathbf 1[\operatorname{sign}_F(e)\ne\operatorname{sign}_G(e)]$ is constant over all occurrences $e$ of $v$. When it exists, $f$ is unique and computable by a single scan of the source records. Moreover
\[
\gamma_f(F)(a)=F(a\oplus f),\qquad
\proj_Y(\gamma_f(F))=\{b\oplus f|_Y:b\in\proj_Y(F)\}.
\]
Thus SAT/UNSAT, total model count, incidence connectivity and properness are preserved. In strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF, both positive and negative quotas are also preserved.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each source literal, equality after gauging requires exactly one equation $f_v=\operatorname{sign}_F(e)\oplus\operatorname{sign}_G(e)$. All equations for the same $v$ are consistent precisely under the condition, and an occurrence fixes $f_v$. A literal is true under the gauged source and assignment $a$ precisely when its original literal is true under $a\oplus f$, proving the formula identity. Existentially quantifying variables outside $Y$ is a bijection under the same XOR change of assignments. Each variable flip exchanges its two positive and two negative original occurrences; it changes neither scope, source index, clause uniqueness, nor incidence connectivity.
\end{proof}

A canonical representative is obtained by choosing the unique gauge that makes the first signed occurrence of every variable positive. This normalization is source-index authenticated and requires no SAT oracle. It is \emph{not} a smaller formula: all original clauses remain.

\begin{proposition}[Quantitative gauge-orbit coverage and its limit]\label{p:count}
For a fixed connected, proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ formula with $n$ active variables, its gauge orbit has \emph{exactly} $2^n$ different indexed signed formulas, all sharing its SAT label and unsigned incidence support. If every original unsigned clause scope is distinct, there are exactly
\[
6^n\quad\text{balanced signings and}\quad 6^n/2^n=3^n
\]
different gauge orbits on that fixed source list. These counting formulas concern distinguishable indexed incidences and do not claim that the orbits have different satisfiability labels.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each variable has four distinguishable occurrence slots and exactly $\binom42=6$ choices of which two are positive, independently of all other variables. Distinct unsigned scopes exclude duplicate signed clauses for every signing. The group $\{0,1\}^n$ acts freely: if any variable's flip bit is one, at least one of its source signs changes, so the signing cannot remain identical. The orbit-stabilizer count gives $2^n$ elements per orbit and hence $3^n$ orbits. If scopes repeat, the free action and $2^n$ orbit size still hold for every \emph{valid} signing, but the total valid-signing count may be smaller.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: authenticated certificate and two-level child transport}
\begin{theorem}[Proof transport, including original source IDs]\label{t:transport}
For a source-indexed $F$ and any $f$, a source-authenticated Resolution derivation $\pi$ from $F$ is transformed by applying $\gamma_f$ to every source/derived clause and pivot literal, leaving source IDs, parent IDs, and derivation DAG unchanged. Every inference remains exact. An empty-clause refutation therefore remains a refutation, with the same number of steps. Likewise, an adaptive branch certificate with ancestor assignments, exhaustive $0/1$ sibling coverage and source-identified 2CNF-SCC contradiction leaves transports by replacing each branch decision $x=b$ with $x=b\oplus f_x$, and replacing every literal in each authenticated implication path by its gauge image. The number of leaves, path edges and maximum depth are unchanged.

For any two distinct variables $x,z$ and branch values $a,b$, original source-identified restrictions satisfy, up to the same source-record ordering,
\[
(\gamma_f F)|_{x=a,z=b}
=\gamma_{f|_{V\setminus\{x,z\}}}\bigl(F|_{x=a\oplus f_x,z=b\oplus f_z}\bigr).
\]
This explicitly covers two ordinary SAT child levels; it makes no claim that these children shrink physically or that a non-equivariant adaptive child constructor commutes with gauges.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A Resolution step derives $R=(A\setminus\{p\})\cup(B\setminus\{\neg p\})$ from $A\ni p$ and $B\ni\neg p$. Gauging literals commutes with set union and deletion, and maps complementary pivots to complementary pivots. The empty clause maps to itself. In a branch residual, a literal is satisfied or falsified under assignment $a$ on $\gamma_f F$ precisely when its original copy is satisfied or falsified under $a\oplus f$. Simplifying a source-identified clause therefore commutes with the map while keeping its original ID. A binary clause $\neg p\vee q$ corresponds to implication $p\rightarrow q$; after gauging, the implication becomes $\gamma_f(p)\rightarrow\gamma_f(q)$. Paths and directed cycles transform to isomorphic paths and cycles. Opposite literals remain opposite. Branch values undergo an independent XOR permutation on $\{0,1\}$, preserving both-child coverage, ancestor consistency, source pointers and contradiction soundness. Applying the one-variable restriction identity successively proves the stated two-level identity.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Acquisition and polarity firewall.} The theorem transforms a \emph{given, validated} proof; it does not produce a first proof $\pi$ for an unknown UNSAT representative. A uniform polynomial-time template generator plus a polynomial-time recognizer would give a polynomial algorithm on the corresponding orbit union, but storing one proof or decision bit per arbitrary unbounded orbit would be hidden nonuniform advice. The SAT side transports a known satisfying assignment $a\mapsto a\oplus f$ and checks original clauses; the UNSAT side transports a source-authenticated proof. They are restrictions of one recognizer, with no free answer-class bit. In a coNTIME companion one may guess a bounded proof and verify it, but polynomial \emph{length-only} bounds must be established for every relevant UNSAT input separately. Timeout or malformed records reject.
