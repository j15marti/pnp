\section{Named ordinary clauses}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be finite, connected, simple and cubic.
Associate one original Boolean variable $x_e$ with each edge,
identical at both endpoints, and charges $b_v\in\{0,1\}$
with $\bigoplus_v b_v=1$.
For a vertex $v$ order its three incident source edge IDs.
For every local word $w\in\{0,1\}^3$ of wrong parity
$\bigoplus_i w_i\ne b_v$, create the proper three-literal clause
$C_{v,w}$ whose literal on $x_e$ is positive when $w_e=0$
and negative when $w_e=1$. The clause is falsified exactly
by the local edge word $w$.
The actual ordinary 3CNF is
\[
 F(G,b)=\bigwedge_{v\in V}\bigwedge_{\oplus w\ne b_v}C_{v,w}.
\]
These are $4|V|$ different source-ID clauses with three
distinct variable identities each. Every original edge variable
appears four times positively and four times negatively.
Clause $C$ is \emph{essential} when the ordinary
source-identified descendant $F(G,b)\setminus\{C\}$ is SAT.

Write $H_1,\ldots,H_{r_v}$ for the connected components of $G-v$.
Let $E(v,H)$ denote incident edges of $v$ going to $H$.
\begin{theorem}[Exact individual clause criticality]
For every $v$ and every wrong-parity local word $w$,
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(F(G,b)\setminus\{C_{v,w}\})
\Longleftrightarrow
\bigwedge_{j=1}^{r_v}
\left[
\bigoplus_{e\in E(v,H_j)}w_e
=\bigoplus_{u\in H_j}b_u
\right]. \tag{1}
\]
Exactly $2^{3-r_v}$ of the four clauses at $v$ are essential.
The total number is therefore
\[
\boxed{K(G,b)=\sum_{v\in V}2^{3-r_v}.} \tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each complete four-clause bundle $B_u$ is equivalent to its
vertex parity equation $\oplus_{e\ni u}x_e=b_u$.
XORing all source equations gives $0=\oplus_v b_v=1$;
hence $F$ is UNSAT. An assignment satisfying $F\setminus\{C_{v,w}\}$
must satisfy all equations at $u\ne v$ and violate the one
at $v$ precisely with the deleted local word $w$.
XORing the equations in a connected component $H_j$ of $G-v$
cancels all internal edge variables and leaves
$\oplus_{e\in E(v,H_j)}w_e=\oplus_{u\in H_j}b_u$.
This establishes necessity.

Conversely suppose every displayed parity condition holds,
and fix the three edges incident to $v$ to $w$.
For each connected $H_j$, the adjusted vertex charges have
even total parity. The vertex-edge incidence matrix of a
connected graph over $\mathbb F_2$ has image precisely
the even-parity column vectors. Thus its internal free edges
can be assigned to satisfy every $u\in H_j$.
Summing the $r_v$ component conditions gives
$\oplus_{e\ni v}w_e=\oplus_{u\ne v}b_u=1\oplus b_v$.
The local word therefore falsifies exactly $C_{v,w}$,
which was removed, and all other clauses are satisfied.
Finally, the $r_v$ component incident-edge sets partition
the three edges at $v$ into disjoint nonempty groups.
The $r_v$ parity conditions are independent and admit
exactly $2^{3-r_v}$ words. All have wrong parity, proving (2).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact minimal-UNSAT criterion]
The odd charged source $F(G,b)$ is clause-minimally UNSAT
if and only if $G-v$ is connected for every $v$, i.e.,
$G$ is 2-vertex-connected.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
When all $r_v=1$ Theorem 1 makes every single clause essential;
deleting any nonempty set of clauses leaves a subset of some
satisfiable one-deletion formula.
If some $r_v>1$, fewer than four clauses there are essential,
so a nonessential clause can be deleted while retaining UNSAT.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Sharp connected counterexample.}
Take two copies of $K_4$; subdivide one edge in each and join
the subdivision vertices by a bridge.
The resulting simple connected cubic graph has ten vertices
and fifteen edges. Exactly the two subdivision vertices are
cut vertices, each with $r_v=2$; all eight others have $r_v=1$.
For every odd charge,
$K(G,b)=8\cdot4+2\cdot2=36$ of $40$ clauses.
Thus block-minimality from the older MAIN83 is insufficient
to infer individual clause necessity on all connected graphs.

\section{Certificate consequence and exact scope}
For any 2-vertex-connected cubic $G$, every
\emph{proper source-identified clause subformula} of
the odd source is SAT. In particular a root-level
source-subformula UNSAT leaf cannot be power-short.
There is an unbounded family of 2-connected cubic prisms
$C_k\square K_2$ with canonical dense-edge encodings
of $N=\Theta(|V|\log|V|)$ bits, and every UNSAT
source-clause subset contains the complete $\Theta(N)$
bit body. This is a \emph{restricted root-leaf}
representation obstruction, not a lower bound on
branching, non-subformula descendants, source rereads,
virtual bit access, resolution, CP, PC or arbitrary $A$.

Indeed there is a direct affine bypass on these very sources.
Over $\mathbb F_2$, encode clause $C$ by its falsifying
polynomial $f_C=\prod_{\ell\in C}(1-\ell)$,
with Boolean literals represented by $x$ or $1-x$.
The four actual clauses of a vertex satisfy the formal identity
\[
\sum_{C\in B_v} f_C=\sum_{e\ni v}x_e+b_v
\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2 .
\]
Sum these degree-three source-identified equations over $v$.
Each source edge variable cancels twice and odd charge yields
$1=0$, an explicitly checkable degree-three PC refutation
with $O(|V|)$ additions.
All original clause IDs, field choices, input reads,
index/writable bits, polynomial manipulations and source
authentication must be charged. Other-field PC degree lower
bounds do not apply to this characteristic-two derivation.
An ordinary affine-terminal verifier can exploit this recognized
structure without using solver $A$ on a parent-scale child.
This is a countercontrol, not all-input certificate coverage.

The same vertex gadget \emph{cannot} be directly
reused as an exact signed-$(2,2)$ source gadget:
each local bundle already consumes two positive and two negative
appearances of each of its three names; overlapping two bundles
creates four positive and four negative appearances per edge.
A separate occurrence-managed SAT-preserving translation is
required. The original 198-variable monotone exact-$(2,2)$
UNSAT source in D\"ocker is a separate construction.
NP-completeness or proof-system hardness cannot silently provide
a time lower bound for arbitrary ordinary SAT deciders.
