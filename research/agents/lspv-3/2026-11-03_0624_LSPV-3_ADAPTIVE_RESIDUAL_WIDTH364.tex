\begin{lemma}[Zero-weakening lifting]
For every CNF $F$, partial assignment $\alpha$ with UNSAT residual $F\upharpoonright\alpha$, and RES refutation with $q_\alpha$ nodes, there is an authentic RES derivation from original sources of some clause $D_\alpha\subseteq B_\alpha$ with at most $q_\alpha$ nodes. $B_\alpha$ is the disjunction of the signed literals falsified by $\alpha$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every surviving restricted source clause comes from a genuine original clause obtained by adjoining only literals falsified by $\alpha$. Lift every source occurrence back to its original, and perform the identical Resolution pivots on variables outside $\operatorname{dom}(\alpha)$. Inductively the lifted result equals the restricted result together with a subset of $B_\alpha$. These added literals do not contain complementary pairs or the pivot, so every step is legal strict RES. The final empty residual result lifts to a subset of $B_\alpha$. No weakening or semantic oracle is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive residual-width proof envelope]
Let $F$ be UNSAT and let $\mathcal T$ be any finite binary adaptive decision tree (no variable repeats on a path) with UNSAT residual at every leaf $\alpha$. Suppose the leaf has a valid RES refutation of $q_\alpha$ nodes. Then
\[
\boxed{W_R(F)\le |I(\mathcal T)|+\sum_{\alpha\in L(\mathcal T)}q_\alpha.}\tag{1}
\]
For filled primal-neighbor counts $w_i(\sigma_\alpha)$ from independently chosen leaf Davis--Putnam elimination orders $\sigma_\alpha$,
\[
\boxed{W_R(F)\le |I(\mathcal T)|+\sum_{\alpha\in L(\mathcal T)}\sum_i3^{w_i(\sigma_\alpha)}.}\tag{2}
\]
If a set of $b$ variables has primal residual treewidth at most $w$ under every assignment, with binary input length $N$, then
\[
\boxed{W_R(F)\le 2^bN3^w+2^b-1.}\tag{3}
\]
Hence $b+w=O(\log N)$ is incompatible with superpolynomial $W_R(F)$, even if the original primal graph has large treewidth.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lift each leaf proof. At an internal branch $x=0,1$, the two derived clauses are subsets of the prefix blocking literals plus respectively $x$ and $\neg x$. If either omits the branch literal, reuse it. Otherwise resolve the two on $x$, costing one node. Induction to the root gives contradiction and (1). At each Davis--Putnam elimination of $x_i$, every new resolvent lies over at most $w_i$ remaining neighbor variables; there are at most $3^{w_i}$ distinct, non-tautological resolvent clauses. Derive each necessary distinct resolvent once using genuine binary RES. UNSAT elimination eventually yields the empty clause, giving (2). A width-$w$ order uses at most $N$ occurring variables and a complete $b$-variable tree has $2^b$ leaves and $2^b-1$ branches, giving (3). Optimizer discovery and machine work are not included in this free-choice upper bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Heterogeneous guarded Horn paths attain the source floor]
For every $n\ge2$, $b\ge0$, and arbitrary permutations $P_\alpha=(p_1,\dots,p_n)$ indexed by selector assignments $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^b$, let
\[
U(P)=\{x_{p_1}\}\cup\{\neg x_{p_i}\vee x_{p_{i+1}}:1\le i<n\}\cup\{\neg x_{p_n}\},
\qquad H_{n,b}=\bigcup_\alpha\{B_\alpha\vee C:C\in U(P_\alpha)\}.
\]
Every one of the $m=2^b(n+1)$ source clauses is indispensable and individually sign-sensitive, each selector residual is a width-one UNSAT Horn chain, and in the fixed complete mixed system,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_{n,b})=2^b(n+1)-1=m-1.}\tag{4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix selector assignment $\alpha$. Only its indexed guarded path is active. To falsify precisely path clause $j\in\{0,\ldots,n\}$ assign $x_{p_i}=1$ iff $i\le j$. This satisfies every other source, proving deletion SAT and each individual signed-occurrence flip SAT. Hence all $m$ clauses are essential. In any sound conservative proof with $q$ paid at-most-two-premise inference nodes and all $m$ sources in contradiction ancestry, the connected underlying DAG has at least $m+q-1$ premise edges but at most $2q$, so $q\ge m-1$ regardless of sharing, extensions, GF(2), adaptivity or temporary growth. Resolve each guarded Horn path in $n$ strict RES steps to $B_\alpha$, then resolve the $2^b$ blocking clauses through a selector tree using $2^b-1$ further steps. The cost $n2^b+2^b-1=m-1$ attains the global bound. Because all $m$ sources appear explicitly, this is not a superpolynomial bound in actual input bits.
\end{proof}
