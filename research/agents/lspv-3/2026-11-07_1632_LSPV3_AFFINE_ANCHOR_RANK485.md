## Theorem 485.1: exact rank-coset affine decomposition (PROVED/RESTRICTED)
Let X range over GF(2)^n. A finite acyclic circuit has globally fresh Boolean outputs, each defined by one two-input Boolean operation. Each nonlinear gate j must possess one designated operand a_j with a syntactically certified **affine-only ancestor cone**, so a_j(X)=c_j XOR <alpha_j,X> globally. Its other operand may be any earlier circuit wire, including outputs of nonlinear gates and reconvergent shared subgraphs. Let r=rank{alpha_j}. Then exactly 2^r nonempty affine cosets of the original input cube partition all inputs so that **every named wire has an affine expression in X on every coset**.

**Proof.** Choose a basis beta_1,...,beta_r for the anchor coefficient span. Fix the r linear-form values. Their independent linear map is onto, hence its 2^r fibers are disjoint, nonempty and exhaustive. Each anchor a_j becomes a fixed bit on each fiber. Process gates topologically. Affine gates preserve affinity. Every binary nonlinear function has ANF f(u,v)=c XOR a*u XOR b*v XOR d*u*v. Fixing the anchor u=e turns the output into c XOR a*e XOR (b XOR d*e)*v, affine because its other operand v is affine by induction. QED. Sharing and reconvergence do not increase the coset count. Choosing anchors optimally is an offline existence relaxation; practical anchor search must be charged.

**Typed obstruction to dropping the hypothesis.** In h0=x0 AND x1, h1=h0 AND x2, branching only on x0 does not linearize h1: on x0=1 its values on x1,x2=00,01,10,11 are 0,0,0,1, which violates the affine four-point XOR identity. Therefore the designated first operand h0 of h1 is not a valid globally affine anchor. This is a concrete counterexample, not an all-mixed lower bound.
