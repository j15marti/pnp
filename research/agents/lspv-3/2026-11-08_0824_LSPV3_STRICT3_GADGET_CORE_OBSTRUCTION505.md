## ITERATE: exact grammar and obstruction theorem

Fix the **distinct exact-support recovered-core abstraction** `R_core505`. SOURCE introduces the actual original indexed clause as a Boolean factor with its exact three-variable unsigned support. AND3 takes two factor parents and produces their exact conjunction, retaining the **union of declared supports** only when that union has at most three variables; each AND3 has fixed unit cost. An exact Boolean factor can be annotated by a GF(2) row only when its complete truth table is **globally equivalent** to the row; binary XOR adds two such rows with unit cost. Source nodes are free, no other rule is present. In particular, RES, LOCAL-CONVERT, DEF, facet export, support-shrinking conversions and branching/discharge are NOT added. This isolates the fragment actually replayed from /341, but does NOT assert that the historical recovered checker accepts arbitrary new input families: its original packet builder is specialized. The complete historical `R_mix341` is more expressive and cannot be identified with this deliberately incomplete core.

Let `T(G,b)` be original odd-charge signed-edge strict 3-CNF on a connected **simple bridgeless cubic** graph with `h` vertices. There are `3h/2` original edge variables and `4h` original signed proper width-three source clauses. Fix ANY nonempty subset `S` of original clause positions, `1<=k=|S|<=4h`. Replace each selected `C=(a OR b OR c)` by three fresh-variable clauses
$$
A=(a\lor b\lor u),\qquad
B=(\neg u\lor c\lor v),\qquad
D=(\neg u\lor c\lor\neg v).
$$
For each selected source use a globally distinct pair `u,v`; call the resulting formula `H_S`, with `m=4h+2k` strict-three source clauses and `n=3h/2+2k` variables.

**Theorem 505.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED).** `H_S` is UNSAT and every original source clause remains essential as established by /503-/504. Nevertheless,
$$
\boxed{\text{No finite }R_{\mathrm{core505}}\text{ refutation of }H_S\text{ exists for every }k\ge1.}
$$
By contrast `T(G,b)` has the existing `4h-1`-node SOURCE/AND3/XOR proof, and in the DIFFERENT declared `R_bridge503`, `H_S` has exact `4h+2k-1` paid minimum. These are three different proof-language outcomes for closely related UNSAT formulas; no claim of unrestricted hardness follows.

**Proof.** Each gadget A factor depends essentially on the three variables `{x,y,u}`. Its B and D factors have the common declared support `{u,z,v}`; their conjunction is exactly `not u OR c` with 6 of 8 satisfying assignments, not an affine row. Any A-to-B/D conjunction would involve the four **essential** variables `{x,y,u,z}`, even after semantics-based elimination of v, and is therefore illegal under AND3. With historical declared-support retention the union contains five variables. Distinct gadgets have distinct u and v, so their source supports cannot merge. An unchanged original three-edge clause has support `{x,y,z}`; its union with the corresponding A support contains four variables and with corresponding B/D support at least five. Clauses at other vertices of a simple cubic graph share at most one old edge and cannot merge with gadget factors. Hence no factor containing a gadget source can participate in a legal AND3 crossing to a different source block; none is zero and none has an exact nontrivial affine row.

Untouched original vertex clauses at a fixed vertex have one common three-edge support; AND3 can conjoin any subset. Exactly four forbidden parity assignments exist. Conjoining `r` distinct original clauses leaves `8-r` models. A nontrivial three-variable affine row has exactly four models, so only `r=4`, when all four original vertex facets are unmodified, yields an affine row, namely its original vertex parity equation. For `1<=r<=3` the counts are 7, 6, 5 and no affine row exists. There are no cross-vertex AND3 merges because distinct vertices have different three-edge supports with union at least five. Thus **all** GF(2) seeds in the proof are drawn from the complete, unexpanded vertices.

Since `S` is nonempty, at least one vertex lacks a complete set of original parity facets. Any proper subset of the vertex-edge incidence rows of a connected graph is linearly independent over GF(2): its only linear dependency is the sum of all h rows. Therefore the surviving affine rows are jointly satisfiable and no XOR combination yields `0=1`. All AND3 factors are satisfiable, and no other refutation rule exists. QED.

This argument continues to allow arbitrary branch-free reconvergent shared DAGs, repeated parent uses, any number of nonessential AND3 nodes, optimal post-hoc rule choice and unlimited depth within this **fixed restricted** grammar. It does not prohibit legal RES reconstructions in the complete historical system. More permissive semantics such as a new source-port converter would require an explicitly separate calculus and charges.
