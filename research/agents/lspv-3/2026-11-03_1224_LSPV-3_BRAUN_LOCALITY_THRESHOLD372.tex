\section*{Starting published lower bound and fixed proof system}
For $n=2^\ell$, $\ell\ge32$, Braun~\cite{Braun} reports that every unrestricted DAG-like Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of the standard bit pigeonhole CNF $B_\ell$ has more than
\[
L_\oplus(B_\ell)>\exp\left(\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}\right)
\]
total lines, \emph{including} initial source lines. Each of $n+1$ pigeons has $\ell$ original Boolean bits; each pigeon pair and each of $n$ holes contributes one signed width-$2\ell$ collision-exclusion clause. Thus $m=n\binom{n+1}{2}$ clauses and ASCII DIMACS size $N=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ bits. This is a theorem claim about a precise restricted proof grammar, not an unconditional lower bound on arbitrary SAT computation. Res$(\oplus)$ admits disjunctions of affine equations, sound semantic one-premise weakening and complementary-affine resolution.

Maintain the preselected unchanged source-authenticated $R_{\rm mix341}$: original CNF sources have zero charge; strict signed RES, paid local AND3, GF$(2)$ unit-row addition, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative two-input VARDEF each cost one distinct shared-DAG node. The total is a nonnegative integer, so soundness/completeness and finite refutations make the minimum attained. Define $W_R(F)$ and $\overline W_R(N)$ as in the authorized November 2 refocus. Ideal selection is free; proof production, encoded bytes, verification and machine work are different.

\section*{Proper fragment and compilation theorem}
For a parameter $b\ge1$, let $R^{\rm loc}_b$ denote only \emph{those legal derivations already available in} $R_{\rm mix341}$ satisfying these extra restrictions. No VARDEF or fresh variable occurs. Ordinary signed RES on any original-variable clauses is allowed. Binary addition of arbitrary-width explicit affine unit equations is allowed. Other paid local inference nodes may take source-authenticated, exact Boolean factors, ordinary clauses and affine equations, but every input and output of each such node must depend only on a common declared set $S$ of at most $b$ \emph{original} variables, and the node's conclusion must follow semantically from its cited antecedents. Boolean factors carry exact $2^{|S|}$-row tables, translated into their complete falsifying-assignment blocker CNFs. An opaque circuit/payload, implicit fresh wire, unexpanded nonlocal interface, or source-wide semantic implication cannot qualify merely because it has three externally named pins. This is an audited subset of the SAME fixed calculus, not a newly tuned full system.

\begin{lemma}[Explicit local Res$(\oplus)$ macro]
Every sound local node of the declared type with $|S|\le b$ admits a syntactic Res$(\oplus)$ compilation from its already translated antecedents using at most $2^{2b+1}$ additional target lines. Its factor outputs may be reused as complete blocker bundles, and a unit affine output may be reused as a genuine affine equation line. The compiler is effective, source-independent, and permits DAG sharing.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If the output is an ordinary clause $C$ on $S$, for each of the at most $2^{|S|-|{\rm vars}(C)|}$ assignments $\alpha$ falsifying $C$, at least one input factor or antecedent line is false. For a Boolean input factor select one of its already translated blockers false at $\alpha$; otherwise select the falsified ordinary or affine antecedent. Its semantic weakening to the \emph{complete assignment blocker} $B_\alpha$ is sound, because $B_\alpha$ is false only on $\alpha$ and so is its chosen predecessor. Resolve the resulting $B_\alpha$ lines on all variables outside $C$, yielding exactly $C$. This uses at most $2^{b+1}-1$ lines; if a predecessor is already $B_\alpha$, reuse it. Tautological output clauses need no materialization. An exact Boolean output factor consists of at most $2^b$ such clauses, giving strictly fewer than $2^{2b+1}$ additional lines. A false factor is handled by $C=\bot$ and the full cube, not an ex-falso answer axiom.

If the output is an affine \emph{unit} $E=(\oplus_{v\in H}v=\epsilon)$ with nonempty $H\subseteq S$, choose $t\in H$. Each assignment $\gamma$ to $S\setminus\{t\}$ has a unique completion $\alpha$ falsifying $E$. Some already translated input line is false on $\alpha$. Semantically weaken it to $E\vee B_\gamma$ (a legitimate linear clause): this new line is false only at $\alpha$ over $S$. Resolving all $|S|-1$ remaining original variables produces the \emph{unit} $E$ in at most $2^{|S|}-1$ charged target lines. Constant outputs are handled by trivial truth or the empty-clause case. All weakenings are justified from authenticated input lines, not from a global SAT oracle. Every choice is computed by enumerating at most $2^b$ local assignments; the search work is separate from logical output size.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform bounded-locality backtranslation]
For every valid UNSAT original CNF $F$ with $m$ source clauses, every $q$-paid-node $R^{\rm loc}_b$ shared-DAG refutation induces a Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of the \emph{same exact source} with at most
\[
\boxed{L_\oplus(F)\le m+(2^{2b+1}+2)q.}
\]
The quantifiers are $\forall F\,\forall b\,\forall\pi\in R^{\rm loc}_b(F)\,\exists\rho$; the target source is unchanged, all local macros are sound and every shared R node is compiled once.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Replay the R DAG topologically, copying original CNF source lines. Native signed Resolution is one Res$(\oplus)$ resolution line. For global binary affine-row addition, obtain $(f+g=\alpha+\beta)\vee(g\ne\beta)$ by one semantic weakening of $(f=\alpha)$, then resolve it with $(g=\beta)$ to obtain the sum in one more line. Compile every other local Boolean factor or affine conclusion with the lemma, retaining full bundles for downstream uses. No source axiom is inserted and no rule is simulated backwards. The displayed bound allows two lines per node plus the worst local macro cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Locality-or-extension barrier, conditional on Braun]
For all $\ell\ge32$ and every $q$-node $R^{\rm loc}_b$ refutation of the same standard $B_\ell$,
\[
 n\binom{n+1}{2}+(2^{2b+1}+2)q>
 \exp\left(\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}\right),\qquad n=2^\ell.
\]
For each fixed $d$, if $q\le n^d$ for all sufficiently large $\ell$, then
\[
\boxed{b\ge\frac{n}{65536(\ln 2)\ell^2}-O_d(\log n)
=\Omega\left(\frac{N^{1/3}}{(\log N)^{8/3}}\right).}
\]
In particular, a polynomial-node full $R_{\rm mix341}$ proof of this original source \emph{must} either invoke a rule excluded from $R^{\rm loc}_b$ (notably conservative definitions or an opaque/nonlocal circuit representation) or contain a local node whose expanded ORIGINAL-variable interface meets the displayed large-support threshold. This is a necessary disjunction, not a full-R lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding theorem to Braun's lower bound, preserving the input $B_\ell$ and counting the $m=\Theta(n^3)$ input lines. If $q\le n^d$, then the displayed exponential lower bound eventually dominates the polynomial $m+2q$, leaving $2^{2b+1}q\ge\frac12\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))$. Taking natural logarithms gives $2b\ln2\ge n/(32768\ell^2)-O_d(\ln n)$. The bit-size expression uses $N=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ and $\ell=\Theta(\log N)$.
\end{proof}

\section*{Falsifiers, mandatory escapes and costs}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: Global unit GF$(2)$ row addition necessarily escapes Res$(\oplus)$. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the displayed two-line macro is valid even for wide affine rows.
\item[] F2: Local sound Boolean factor conversions require an uncharged oracle. \textbf{FALSIFIED within the declared finite-table fragment}: the explicit assignment-blocker construction validates every output; search is not free for a runtime claim.
\item[] F3: The theorem extends unchanged to arbitrary paid VARDEF and compact-circuit LOCAL-CONVERT. \textbf{FALSIFIED as an unrestricted transfer proposal}: polynomial extended-resolution pigeonhole proofs are known~\cite{CR,Buss}; a circuit can have few pins but a large hidden dependency/payload cone. Such operations are deliberately \emph{outside} the fragment.
\item[] F4: An exponential Res$(\oplus)$ lower bound proves a superpolynomial full mixed minimum or the bridge B3. \textbf{FALSE inference}: $R^{\rm loc}_b\subseteq R_{\rm mix341}$ gives $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}\le W_{R^{\rm loc}_b}$, not its reverse. B3 separately needs $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(N)=N^{O(1)}$.
\end{enumerate}
Same-source odd/even Tseitin parity is a required negative control: an expensive Resolution instance may have a linear authenticated AND3/GF$(2)$ refutation in full R. Horn, 2SAT and tiny cores have short local proofs. No lower bound is claimed for those. No finite optimum is certified here; generated proof DAGs certify \emph{upper} macros only. A q-node proof can contain large circuit payloads, so no polynomial machine-time/space claim follows from q alone.
