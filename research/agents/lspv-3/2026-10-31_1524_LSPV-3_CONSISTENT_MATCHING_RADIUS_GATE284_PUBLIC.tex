\section{Exact theorem and proof}
For any CNF source, coherent complete assignment $a$, and its original false-clause IDs $U_F(a)$, set
\[
\rho_F(a)=\min\{|S|:\forall j\in U_F(a),\ S\cap \mathrm{vars}(C_j)\ne\varnothing\}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Admissible consistent repair bound]
Every satisfying $b$ obeys $d_H(a,b)\ge \rho_F(a)$. For any coordinate $i$,
\[
\boxed{|\rho_F(a)-\rho_F(a\oplus e_i)|\le1.}
\]
Thus $\rho_F$ is an admissible and consistent one-bit-move A* heuristic, although its computation may be NP-hard on unrestricted CNF.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any SAT witness must change some variable in every clause false at $a$. If $S$ covers all clauses false at $a$ and $a'=a\oplus e_i$, any clause newly false at $a'$ contains $i$. Thus $S\cup\{i\}$ covers all clauses false at $a'$, giving $\rho(a')\le\rho(a)+1$. Reversing the roles of $a,a'$ proves the claim.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Gallai matching transfer, already known in project]
In strict exact-(2+,2-), any variable belongs to at most two clauses falsified under one anchor. Let $G_a$ have one vertex per false clause and an edge for every source variable occurring in two false clauses. Singleton variable occurrences provide singleton covers. Then
\[
\boxed{\rho_F(a)=|U_F(a)|-\nu(G_a),}
\]
where $\nu$ is maximum matching cardinality. This is the classical mixed edge/singleton cover identity; blossom computation and source graph acquisition are charged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Exact radius certificate]
Fix a reference $a$ and a partial target assignment fixing coordinates through $i$ with $h$ deviations from $a$. Fill the remaining coordinates from $a$ to get $z$. Let $\rho^{>i}(z)$ be the minimum set of free coordinates hitting all currently false source clauses, or infinity if impossible. If $h+\rho^{>i}(z)>R$, no satisfying completion in the Hamming ball $B_R(a)$ exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A completion differs from $z$ only on free coordinates, and must repair every currently false original clause by hitting its support. Its radius cost is therefore at least $h+\rho^{>i}(z)$.
\end{proof}
The radius certificate does not prove global UNSAT. It does not contradict earlier GATE244 UP-dominance for unrestricted ordinary DPLL cubes.
