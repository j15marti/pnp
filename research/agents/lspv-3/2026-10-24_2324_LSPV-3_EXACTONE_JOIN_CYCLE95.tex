\section{Receiving ARC obligation and preregistered falsifiers}
The common five-role proof spine concerns the set \(\mathcal E\) of simultaneous resource pairs \((t,s)\) achieved by \emph{one} fixed, uniform, total, exact SAT decider on every ordinary encoded input in the Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (FLMV) read-only-indexed-input random-access Turing model. Under \(P=NP\) some finite \((t,s)\in\mathcal E\) exists. A fully charged finite cofinal route of jointly achievable pairs into the matched forbidden region would contradict \(P=NP\). A favorable all-input route remains \OPEN. The old expression \(t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)\) is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as an unrestricted forbidden product region.

The leading new question is whether our earlier SPARSE-BOUNDARY-SUPPORT83 and PROJECTION-LIFT-JOIN94 can infer cheap global computation from each module's \emph{small, readily enumerated} nonaffine feasible relation. Three precise alternatives were examined.

\noindent\textbf{M1.} Every local module might have a three-element source-accessible exact boundary and affine projection rank two, yet global consistency might still encode an NP-complete language. \emph{Prediction: yes; prove a uniform proper-3CNF reduction.}

\noindent\textbf{M2.} Even retaining named equality interfaces and enforcing full pairwise arc consistency might fail to determine SAT versus UNSAT on matched connected instances. \emph{Prediction: construct an equal-support opposite-label pair with the same interaction graph and no local domain pruning.}

\noindent\textbf{M3.} The reduction and exact join may furnish a favorable all-input ARC exponent edge. \emph{Prediction: no; NP-completeness and restricted membership of a compiled family do not produce a resource-contraction algorithm or a necessary lower bound on arbitrary solvers.}

\section{Exact local algebra and semantic type}
Write \(E(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)\) for the Boolean relation in which \emph{exactly one} of the three literal values is true. It has the elementary identity
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
E(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)\ \equiv\
(\ell_1\oplus\ell_2\oplus\ell_3=1)
\ \land\ (\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2).}
\label{eq:one}
\end{equation}
The odd-parity solutions are \(001,010,100,111\), and the last binary clause eliminates exactly \(111\). The identity is an \emph{E-mode} SIL contextual equivalence on the same three visible literal coordinates, not a detached satisfiability-status equivalence. For any choice of literal signs, the feasible source-coordinate relation has exactly
\[
r_i=3,\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{affspan}(\Sigma_i)=2,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\text{local affine equation})=1.
\]
These three parameters are distinct. The local nonaffine feasible support has size three, while the affine hull has four points. Nothing here computes a NAE sign-cycle rank; a signed hypergraph invariant is not being conflated with affine equation rank.

\section{Linear source-named compilation and global join}
Let \(H=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m E(x_{i1},x_{i2},x_{i3})\) be a positive 1-in-3-SAT instance with three \emph{distinct} source variables per triple. Let \(n_+\) count source variables occurring at least once. For each occurrence make a fresh local variable \(u_{ij}\), and put the relation \(E(u_{i1},u_{i2},u_{i3})\) inside module \(i\). For each original variable, order its occurrence copies and connect consecutive copies by \emph{source-name-preserving equality}. Write
\[
L=\sum_{x}(\deg_H(x)-1)=3m-n_+
\]
for the number of occurrence-copy equality links. The local variable sets are now pairwise disjoint. All cross-module interactions are ordinary binary equalities \(u=v\); no independent choice of incompatible local witnesses is permitted.

\begin{theorem}[Three-state equality-join normal form; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every such \(H\), the construction above produces an exact finite-domain compatibility instance \(J(H)\), with \(m\) modules, every local domain of cardinality three and affine hull dimension two, and all cross-module constraints being named equalities. It satisfies
\[
\boxed{\SAT(J(H))=\SAT(H).}
\]
The decision problem for this class is NP-complete, by the established NP-completeness of positive 1-in-3-SAT and the linear source-reduction. This conclusion holds despite polynomial, source-accessible enumeration of \emph{every local} domain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every satisfying source assignment \(x\) extends to local copies by setting \(u_{ij}=x_{ij}\). Every local exact-one relation holds and every copy equality holds. Conversely, the equality chains make all copies of each named source variable identical. Any globally consistent selection of one of the three local signatures therefore defines a single source assignment that satisfies every \(E\)-clause of \(H\). The construction requires \(3m\) occurrence records and \(L\) equality links and is uniformly computed in polynomial time; on supplied occurrence lists it has linear record expansion. A global witness is checkable in polynomial time. NP membership and the positive 1-in-3-SAT reduction yield NP-completeness. This is a \emph{hardness of the joint decision language}, not an unconditional lower bound on exact SAT algorithms.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Ordinary proper-3CNF bit encoding.}
Encode each parity-three equation by its four falsifying-assignment ternary clauses. Encode each local NAND binary clause with a fresh padding variable \(z\) as \((a\vee b\vee z)\land(a\vee b\vee\neg z)\). Encode one equality as two binary implications, each padded with its own fresh variable, giving four proper ternaries per equality link. This conversion preserves satisfiability and source-name compatibility while permitting free private padding witnesses.
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
n'=4m+2L,\quad m'=6m+4L,\quad
w=\lceil\log_2(64(n'+m'+1))\rceil,\quad
N'=(4m'+2)w.}
\label{eq:counts}
\end{equation}
Every ternary clause has three distinct variable IDs, contains no complementary pair, and is unique in the constructed source. The total emitted bits are \(O((m+L)\log(m+L))\). Metadata, sign transport, occurrence grouping, equality-link discovery and output serialization are charged. The compiler does not call a SAT oracle and has no unaccounted workspace guarantee in the FLMV model.

\section{Matched connected SAT/UNSAT pair defeating arc consistency}
Use four source variables \(a,b,c,d\) and four modules on unsigned triples
\[
(abc),\quad(abd),\quad(acd),\quad(bcd).
\]
The \emph{UNSAT} member enforces exact-one on all four positive triples. The \emph{SAT} member changes only the fourth triple to \(E(\neg b,c,d)\). All source-variable occurrence patterns, module graphs, local support cardinalities, affine dimensions, compiled unsigned clause supports, variable counts and bit lengths are identical.

\begin{theorem}[Same-support, arc-consistent opposite labels; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Both members compile to connected proper-3CNF instances with
\[
m=4,\quad L=8,\quad n'=32,\quad m'=56,\quad w=13,\quad N'=2938.
\]
The unsigned module interaction graph has the same five edges
\((12),(13),(23),(24),(34)\) in both members. Every module has precisely three feasible boundary signatures and remains unpruned at the complete binary arc-consistency fixed point, yet the first instance is UNSAT and the second SAT. All individual modules and both halves of the fixed two-level partition \((1,2)\mid(3,4)\) are satisfiable for both labels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the unsigned member, summing the four integer exact-one equations gives
\(3(a+b+c+d)=4\), impossible for Boolean \(a,b,c,d\). For the signed member \(a=1,b=c=d=0\) satisfies all four constraints.

The occurrence-equality chains induce pairwise edges with two shared coordinates on \((12)\) (coordinates \(a,b\)), \((23)\) (\(a,d\)), and \((34)\) (\(c,d\)), and one shared coordinate on \((13)\) (\(c\)) and \((24)\) (\(b\)). For either source member, \emph{both} endpoints of each two-coordinate edge project their three-element local domains to precisely \(\{00,01,10\}\). Both endpoints of each one-coordinate edge project to \(\{0,1\}\). Thus every local signature has at least one consistent neighbor signature along every incident edge; enforcing binary arc consistency deletes nothing, regardless of the order of revisions. The local modules are source-name preserving and have all their own witness lifts, but pairwise supports do not supply one \emph{joint} witness.

For the named original-variable boundary \((a,b,c,d)\), the two-level grouped relations are
\[
P_{12}=\{x:E(a,b,c)\land E(a,b,d)\},\quad
P_{34}^{\pm}=\{x:E(a,c,d)\land E(b,c,d)\ \text{or }E(\neg b,c,d)\}.
\]
Each has cardinality three. The intersections satisfy
\[
|P_{12}\cap P_{34}^{-}|=0,\qquad
|P_{12}\cap P_{34}^{+}|=1.
\]
Exact source-name-preserving two-level gluing therefore recovers the labels; the independent SAT bits of all modules and halves do not. Equation (\ref{eq:counts}) gives the stated metadata. Changing a literal sign permutes clause polarities but not their unsigned supports.
\end{proof}

This finite opposite-label pair disproves \emph{a proposed complete decision statistic}, not a superlinear time bound. A global algorithm may read and reason about the source sign immediately.

\section{Parameterized constructive envelope and adaptive solver}
For a \emph{supplied and validated} tree decomposition of the module-interaction graph of width \(w_G\), classical finite-domain dynamic programming decides exact compatibility in
\[
T_{\mathrm{join}}(N)=3^{w_G+1}\operatorname{poly}(N)
\]
bit operations with conservative corresponding polynomially enlarged working storage. A supplied, validated feedback vertex set \(C\) of size \(f\) yields \(3^f\operatorname{poly}(N)\) time by conditioning its three-state signatures and joining the remaining forest. Tree decomposition discovery, certificate validation, incident equality constraints, failed guesses, reconstruction and source rereads are not free. These are upper bounds, not lower bounds, and increasing raw cycle count alone does not preclude a compact recurrence; Role 1 NONAFFINE-WINDOW131 supplies the explicit easy cyclic control.

A uniform adaptive policy may first inspect source syntax, run SCC/Horn and affine tests, attempt relation-domain enumeration, discover a cheap join order, switch to learning or a compact source-aware direct recognizer, and finally invoke a correct unrestricted SAT fallback. On the promised local language these are legitimate algorithms. In the general fallback case the worst-case time need not improve. There is no SAT/UNSAT label oracle, no obligatory materialization of all contexts, and no assumption that a one-bit root decider outputs witnesses or a DNNF.

The BIT-RESOURCE1 accounting vector is
\[
(W,R_{\rm src},R_{\rm work},W_{\rm work},
F_{\rm flips},S_{\rm peak},BT_{\rm cumulative},|\mathrm{program}|).
\]
Here one elementary bit operation is charged to \(W\), while temporal bit flips \(F_{\rm flips}\) are separate. Writes that do not change a value still count as work. The compiler counts all source fields, occurrence indexes, signed clauses, padding bits, link generation and emitted child bytes; the join counts compatibility checks, failed candidates, scheduling, caches, gluing, reconstruction and peak simultaneous writable state. The reported finite DPLL branch counts are \emph{not} bit-machine time-space measurements.
