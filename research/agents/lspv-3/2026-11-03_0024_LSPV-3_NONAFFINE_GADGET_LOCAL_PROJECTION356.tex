\section*{Exact nonaffine family}
For a connected cubic graph $G=(V,E)$, $h=|V|$, $e=3h/2$, choose odd vertex charges $\sigma_v$. Give every edge $a$ a disjoint $k$-bit tuple $y_a$ and a surjective gadget $g:\{0,1\}^k\to\{0,1\}$. At $v$ require
\[
\bigoplus_{a\ni v}g(y_a)=\sigma_v
\]
using the \emph{canonical} CNF with one width-$3k$ blocking clause for each violating local $3k$-bit input assignment. Let $F_{G,g,\sigma}$ be the union of all vertex clauses. Summing the local equations over all vertices yields $0=1$, so the formula is UNSAT. The original bit input count is $ek$; this is general CNF, not an assertion of strict width three.

\begin{theorem}[Local Resolution projection]
Suppose $g$ has a $c(k)$-gate total fan-in-two Boolean circuit and the fixed paid conservative VARDEF rule introduces each gate with all defining clauses attributed to its paid node. Introduce output $z_a=g(y_a)$ for each edge $a$. For each vertex $v$, the four clauses blocking the wrong parity of $(z_{a_1},z_{a_2},z_{a_3})$ follow from the original local source clauses and gate definitions by a source-explicit ordinary binary Resolution DAG with at most $3^{M_v}$ distinct non-tautological clause nodes, where $M_v\le3k+O(3c(k))+3$ counts all local inputs, gate intermediates and outputs. For $c(k)=O(k)$ and $k=O(\log h)$, these are polynomial-$h$ \emph{local} proof costs. No global affine aggregation is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The conjunction $H_v$ of the original local CNF and the defining clauses fixes all gate values uniquely. Surjectivity and edgewise disjoint inputs imply that existentially projecting $H_v$ onto the three output variables yields precisely their parity relation. Eliminate all original input and intermediate gate variables by Davis--Putnam Resolution: every generated resolvent has two explicit antecedents and a named pivot, and memoization permits at most $3^{M_v}$ different non-tautological clauses over the entire local variable universe. The retained clauses after eliminating all hidden variables form a CNF equivalent to the three-bit parity relation. Every forbidden output assignment must falsify a retained clause. No clause of width one or two can be implied by three-bit parity because each such partial assignment extends to a satisfying parity assignment. Thus the falsified clause must be the exact three-literal blocking clause, so all four appear. The construction never invokes AND3, XOR or a global semantic oracle.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Clause indispensability]
If $G-v$ is connected for every $v$, each original canonical clause of $F_{G,g,\sigma}$ is indispensable and each individual occurrence-sign flip makes the formula SAT. In the unchanged binary-premise, positive-node-cost $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$,
\[
\boxed{W_R(F_{G,g,\sigma})\ge m-1}
\]
for the actual number $m$ of original source clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix the excluded local input pattern of any clause at $v$, with its induced three wrong-parity edge outputs. The parity equations on connected $G-v$ are consistent: their one incidence-row dependency demands exactly this wrong parity on the three boundary edges. Extend the resulting output assignment to gadget inputs by surjectivity, retaining the fixed local pattern. The assignment violates exactly the named original clause. A single polarity flip in that clause also makes it true. Hence every sound refutation uses all $m$ sources. Its rooted connected proof ancestry with $q$ charged at-most-binary-premise nodes has at least $m+q-1$ antecedent edges but at most $2q$, giving $q\ge m-1$. Shared DAGs and paid conservative definitions are included.
\end{proof}
