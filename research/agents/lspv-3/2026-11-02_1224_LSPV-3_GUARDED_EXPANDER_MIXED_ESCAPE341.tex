\section{Infinite guarded multi-sign hypergraph family}
Let $G_h=(V,E)$ be cubic on $h$ vertices with $G_h-v$ connected for every vertex and odd charges $\oplus_v b_v=1$. For vertex $v$, let $T_v$ contain the four clauses forbidding the wrong parity of the three incident edge bits. For $t$ disjoint copies, select variables $z_j$ and define
\[
Q_{G_h,t}=(\bigvee_{j=1}^t z_j)\wedge
\bigwedge_{j=1}^t\bigwedge_{v\in V}\bigwedge_{C\in T_v}
(\neg z_j\vee C^{(j)}).
\]
Parameters are $n=t(1+3h/2)$ variables, $m=1+4ht$ clauses, $L=t+16ht$ literal signs and $\Omega(ht)\le N_{\mathrm{bits}}\le O(ht\log(ht+2))$.

\begin{lemma}Every $Q_{G_h,t}$ is clause-minimally UNSAT. Flipping any one original literal sign produces a SAT instance.\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
One active block imposes odd-charge Tseitin equations; summing all vertex equations yields $0=1$. Removing the master selector clause permits all selectors zero. Deleting a guarded clause at vertex $v$ allows the local forbidden edge pattern $a$. Choose that block's selector one and all others zero. The remaining equations on connected $G_h-v$ have total residual charge
$(1\oplus b_v)\oplus(1\oplus b_v)=0$ and therefore a solution, using incidence rank $h-2$. This witness falsifies exactly the deleted clause. Flipping the sign of any of that clause's literals makes it true without disturbing another clause. The master clause has an analogous witness with all selectors zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}
If $G_h$ additionally has constant edge expansion $|\delta(S)|\ge\alpha\min(|S|,h-|S|)$, then every binary connected-cluster \emph{dense} existential join/projection of one active block has an intermediate table of at least $2^{\alpha h/3-1}$ tuples. Yet
\[
\boxed{\lceil4ht/3\rceil\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix},1}}(Q_{G_h,t})\le t(2h+1).}
\]
Hence this family's \emph{minimum} valid mixed inference DAG cost is $\Theta(ht)$, even with free best-order choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For connected proper vertex cluster $S$, existentially project the internal edges. The resulting exact boundary relation is $\oplus_{e\in\delta(S)}x_e=\oplus_{v\in S}b_v$ by parity cancellation and connected incidence rank; its dense table has $2^{|\delta(S)|-1}$ tuples. A rooted binary cluster tree has a proper cluster of size between $h/3$ and $2h/3$, giving the stated table bound.

For a short $R$-proof in each block, extract $h$ guarded XOR rows using four source clauses each, add them in $h-1$ binary row steps, obtain the contradictory row $\neg z_j\vee(0=1)$, infer $\neg z_j$, then resolve all $t$ such unit clauses against the master clause. Total $t[h+(h-1)+1+1]=t(2h+1)$ nodes.

For the lower bound, minimal UNSAT forces all $m$ source clauses into every sound conservative contradiction ancestry. The connected source-plus-inference DAG with $q$ charged nodes has at least $m+q-1$ dependency edges and at most $4q$ due to the explicit four-premise arity cap. Thus $3q\ge m-1=4ht$. Shared proofs and extra auxiliaries do not evade this accounting. Positive integer weights ensure the minimum is attained.
\end{proof}
Friedman's spectral theorem and Wormald's connectivity theorem imply existence of infinitely many cubic expanders that remain connected after deleting a vertex. This is an existence argument, not an efficient generation claim. Pure Resolution exponential Tseitin bounds of Urquhart and Ben-Sasson--Wigderson cannot be imported into this combined calculus.
