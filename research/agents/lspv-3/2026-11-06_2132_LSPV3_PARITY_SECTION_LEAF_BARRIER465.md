## Theorem: an exact exponential constant-leaf requirement
Let \(X=(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\) be independent original Boolean inputs, \(k\ge2\), and introduce \(Z=(z_1,\ldots,z_{k-1})\). Set \(z_1=x_1\oplus x_2\), \(z_j=z_{j-1}\oplus x_{j+1}\) for \(2\le j\le k-1\). Define each XOR2 gate by its four signed **proper width-three** forbidden-assignment clauses, giving \(G_k\) exactly \(4(k-1)\) clauses and \(k-1\) auxiliary bits. Every original assignment extends uniquely to a model of \(G_k\), in \(k-1\) XOR2 steps; this is a conservative definition.

Take any total binary decision tree querying ORIGINAL \(X\) variables, with no repeated query on a path. Assume each leaf carries a FIXED total valuation of ALL \(Z\) such that **every individual clause** of \(G_k\) becomes true after the leaf's original restriction and its fixed \(Z\) values, independently of all remaining original bits. Then **every leaf fixes all \(k\) original bits**, and hence the minimum possible total leaf count is EXACTLY
$$
L_{\mathrm{const}}(G_k)=2^k.
$$
Proof: the gate equations entail \(z_{k-1}=x_1\oplus\cdots\oplus x_k\). If an original input bit remains free on a leaf, its two completions with that bit flipped require opposite values of the fixed \(z_{k-1}\), contradicting universal satisfaction by one constant auxiliary valuation. A complete depth-\(k\) original-variable decision tree with unique gate values attains \(2^k\). QED.

**Application to [P2-B9]:** its correct bound \(S_{Res}(F)\le L(S_{Res}(F\wedge G_k)+1)-1\) may therefore lose a factor \(2^k\) even when the conservative definition is O(k) and the *functional* Skolem circuit is O(k). This **does not** refute that theorem, nor establish short proofs for the augmented input, nor exclude other polynomial proof transports using functional Skolem sections, paid DEF, GF(2), AND3 or native LOCAL-CONVERT. A result for ordinary Resolution with a particular section method is not a full mixed-calculus lower bound.
