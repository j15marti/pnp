\[
 t< c_\star=\frac{241}{200},\qquad

\section{Source-identified ordinary proper-3CNF construction}
Let $x_1,\ldots,x_b$ ($b\ge3$) have \emph{identical original
variable names} $1,\ldots,b$ in both modules; all other names are
private and disjoint. Four proper ternary clauses encode
$\operatorname{XOR}(a,c,z)$, imposing $z=a\oplus c$.
With fresh variables in each use define
\[
 \operatorname{PIN}(\ell)=
  \bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}
  (\ell\vee\epsilon p\vee\delta q),\qquad
 \operatorname{OR2}(u,v)=(u\vee v\vee h)\wedge(u\vee v\vee\neg h).
\]
Existentially eliminating their private names yields $\ell$ and
$u\vee v$, respectively. All clauses have three distinct identifiers.

The module $R_b$ takes a parity chain $z_0,\ldots,z_b$,
pins $z_0=z_b=0$, and imposes
$\operatorname{XOR}(z_{i-1},x_i,z_i)$.
The separate $Q_{b,u}$ takes a chain $w_0,\ldots,w_b$, pins $w_0=0$,
has a selector $q$, and imposes
\[
 \operatorname{OR2}(q,w_b)\quad\wedge\quad
 \bigwedge_{i=1}^b\operatorname{OR2}(\neg q,\ell_i(u)),
 \qquad
 \ell_i(u)=\begin{cases}x_i&u_i=1,\\\neg x_i&u_i=0.\end{cases}
\]
Consequently, as \emph{existential projections onto precisely the
same original names},
\[
 \mathcal R_b(x)=[\bigoplus_i x_i=0],\quad
 \mathcal Q_{b,u}(x)=[\bigoplus_i x_i=1]\vee[x=u],
 \quad F_{b,u}=R_b\wedge Q_{b,u},
\]
and
\[
 \SAT(F_{b,u})=[\bigoplus_i u_i=0].
 \tag{1}
\]
The combined parent has exactly $m=10b+14$ clauses and
$n=4b+10$ variable names. The paired targets $u=0^b$
(YES) and $v=0^{b-1}1$ (NO) have the \emph{same}
entire ordered clause-support array, header, $n,m$ and canonical
encoded length $N=\Theta(b\log b)$; only \emph{two literal-sign bits}
differ. The source-aware decider is permitted to inspect those bits.

\section{New exact restricted semantic visibility theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary missing-name context is invisible]\label{t:missing}
For each $b\ge3$ and each target $u$, let $H(x_S,y)$ be
\emph{any} finite Boolean constraint system, in particular any
ordinary CNF, on a strict subset $S\subsetneq[b]$ of the original
source names and any private variables $y$.
Suppose $y$ is disjoint from all auxiliaries inside $R_b,Q_{b,u}$.
Then, with $\SAT(H)$ computed over its actual own variables,
\[
 \boxed{
 \SAT(R_b\wedge H)=
 \SAT(Q_{b,u}\wedge H)=\SAT(H)
 }
 \qquad\text{for every target }u .
 \tag{2}
\]
There is no restriction on the number, arity, sign pattern,
internal structure or auxiliary witnesses of the clauses in $H$.
A context can be UNSAT: all three values in (2) are then zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $H$ is unsatisfiable neither conjunction has a model. Otherwise
take any satisfying assignment of $H$, which fixes at most the shared
coordinates $x_S$. Choose any $j\in[b]\setminus S$.
Keeping every assigned coordinate fixed, freely set all other
unmentioned $x$ variables except $x_j$, and choose $x_j$ to make
$\oplus_i x_i=0$ for $R_b$, or $\oplus_i x_i=1$
for $Q_{b,u}$. Both desired parities have extensions.
For $Q$ the odd-parity arm alone suffices, regardless of $u$.
The parity-chain, pins and selector have satisfying \emph{private}
extensions on those words; their variables are disjoint from $y$.
Thus the selected model of $H$ glues to the appropriate module.
The converse follows from conjunction. \end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Source-blind detached-query firewall]\label{t:detached}
Consider any deterministic adaptive controller whose \emph{only}
information about the module parameter $u$ consists of exact Boolean
SAT-status answers to separate calls
$\SAT(R_b\wedge H_j)$ and $\SAT(Q_{b,u}\wedge H_j)$.
Every queried $H_j$ may be selected from all earlier answers, may
introduce arbitrarily many independent auxiliary variables, and
may involve arbitrary subsets of original names, \emph{provided
each} $H_j$ omits at least one $x_i$ and no query contains
both modules together. No other source-sign or target bit channel
is allowed. Then all answer transcripts, including a controller's
termination output, are independent of $u$.
In particular the controller cannot decide both
$F_{b,0^b}$ and $F_{b,0^{b-1}1}$ exactly.
This remains true for any finite number of separately pinned
ordinary descendant levels that have not fixed all $b$ names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Theorem~\ref{t:missing}, every legal answer is simply
$\SAT(H_j)$, independent of $u$. Since the controller starts with
the same state and obtains the same answer to each adaptively chosen
query, induction shows that the entire transcript is the same for
both targets, despite the differing parent labels in (1).
Actual ordinary pin descendants with fewer than $b$ distinct pinned
source names instantiate the same proper-subset hypothesis.
\end{proof}
\noindent
\textbf{Exact scope.}
A real all-input ARC compiler can \emph{read source literal signs}
and use arbitrary source-derived policies, mixed children, learning,
affine elimination, Horn/SCC graph routines, DP, caching, branching,
or direct decision. That extra information breaks the source-blind
hypothesis; these theorems are \emph{not} all-decider lower bounds.
A returned UNSAT label from a valid $H$ is also not a free semantic
oracle for the parent. Theorems~\ref{t:missing}--\ref{t:detached}
generalize the older project
\texttt{INTERFACE-ADAPTIVITY-BARRIER73} prefix-only
oracle adversary and the matched ordinary gadget of
\texttt{ARC-SEMANTIC-PREFIX113}. They do \emph{not}
claim a new general decision-tree lower-bound method.

\section{Exact ordinary representation size floor, with its limit}
\begin{theorem}[Full parity projection forces linear source-size bits]
\label{t:fields}
Fix original identifiers $x_i=i$, $1\le i\le b$.
Suppose any ordinary \emph{explicit} CNF $C_b(x,z)$, with
arbitrary private names $z$, has \emph{exactly} the even-parity
existential projection:
\[
 [\exists z\,C_b(x,z)]=[\oplus_{i=1}^b x_i=0]
 \quad\text{for every }x\in\{0,1\}^b .
\]
Then each of the $b$ original names occurs in a literal
of $C_b$. For the fixed-width canonical ordinary literal encoding
used in this report, $|C_b|\ge b\log_2 b$ bits.
Since $|R_b|=\Theta(b\log b)$ for the explicit proper-3CNF
module above, $|C_b|=\Omega(|R_b|)$.
No fixed $\lambda<1$ allows $|C_b|\le |R_b|^\lambda$
for all sufficiently large $b$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If some $x_i$ were absent, changing only $x_i$ would not change the
satisfiability of $C_b$ under a fixed original-word assignment,
because its private witness quantifier has no access to $x_i$.
Yet parity changes under every such flip. Therefore all $b$
names occur. An encoded literal name uses
$h=\operatorname{bitlength}(2\max(n,m)+1)\ge\log_2 b$
bits whenever its header declares at least $b$ original names.
At least $b$ such fields are present, giving the stated bit bound.
The displayed $R_b$ has $4b+8$ proper ternary clauses and
$O(b)$ names, so its own length is $\Theta(b\log b)$.
An $N^\lambda$ child for fixed $\lambda<1$ contradicts
this asymptotic comparison. \end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{This is a representation theorem, not a SAT lower bound.}
It requires the \emph{full original-name boundary relation}, not
mere equisatisfiability, only a SAT/UNSAT parent label, a lossy
summary sufficient for a \emph{specific} join, or several smaller
collectively complete children. A separate name map or an indexed
virtual accessor must instead be modeled and charged;
the theorem does not prohibit a powerful \emph{nonordinary} compressed
representation. Likewise a context $H$ that can change a
separate-module SAT status in this example must mention \emph{all}
$b$ original names (Theorem~\ref{t:missing} contraposition),
and therefore contains $\Omega(b\log b)=\Omega(N)$
ordinary literal fields. \emph{Necessary, not sufficient:}
even a context mentioning every name can remain uninformative.

\section{ARC resource implication, full cost firewall and literature}
For the declared \emph{copy-and-pin} children, lengths are
$\Theta(N)$ at both depths, regardless of constant-factor
reductions from parent to detached module. Thus the solver guest
scale is $\lambda=1$, not an honest $\lambda=1/2$.
A materialized child takes $\Omega(N)$ writable bits in the
eager implementation. A virtual child may avoid that
buffer but keeps $\Theta(N)$ logical input bits, and all
per-address decoding, source rereads, index arithmetic,
retained ancestors, cached data and repeated probes
must be paid. Every elementary source/workspace read,
changed or unchanged write, rule selection, failed route,
header, name-map, validation, reconstruction and same-time
peak storage belongs to the single machine ledger.
A solver-assisted selector making $N^b$ calls to a
$N^\rho$-bit guest needs exponent $b+\rho t$ for the
guest calls before replay/access costs. For genuinely
$N^\lambda$-bit eligible ordinary children the conditional
eager and fully lazy ledgers are
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 t'_{\rm eager}\le\max(r,a+\lambda+g,a+\lambda t),&
 s'_{\rm eager}\le\max(\lambda,\lambda s,\eta,\kappa),\\
 t'_{\rm virtual}\le\max(r,a+\lambda t+g),&
 s'_{\rm virtual}\le\max(\lambda s,\eta,\kappa).
 \end{array}
\]
Neither ledger supplies the required semantic contractor.
The affine/direct source-aware recognizer here simply
reads the source encoding, validates the recognized gadget
grammar, extracts signed target $u$, and computes its parity.
Its work is polynomial, with logarithmic index/workspace
for a suitable grammar-specific streaming implementation.
Any \emph{all-input} recognizer must charge a valid exact SAT
fallback on unrecognized inputs. Thus there is no
unrestricted SAT decision lower bound from this parity
gadget, its hard-looking joins, its output cardinality, or
its ordinary field-size floor.

FLMV \cite{flmv} supplies the
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} model-matched strict
time-space endpoint after its quasi-linear SAT transfer.
Williams \cite{williams} supplies historical
indirect-diagonalization context, not a license to revive
the retired cosine inequality. Local reductions
\cite{jmv} show that individual source bits may be
locally indexable in some uniform reductions; they do
not prove source-size compression or the present missing
A-dependent semantic implication. No nonrelativization
or algebrization conclusion follows from an easy parity
source or its status-oracle black-box obstruction.
A status-only oracle lower bound typically remains
relativizing for that restricted oracle interface.
No Natural Proofs or circuit-lower-bound inference is used.
