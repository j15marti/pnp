\section{Exact bounded-occurrence construction}
Take a proper three-CNF $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$ with $m\ge1$, all three names distinct in each clause, and all considered original names actually used. Make one new occurrence-name $v_{jk}$ for every source literal $\ell_{jk}$, preserving its sign. For every original name $x$ of occurrence count $d_x\ge2$, connect its occurrence-names in a \emph{directed cycle} by implications $v_{x,i}\to v_{x,i+1}$ (indices cyclic). For $d_x=1$ no edge is needed. An implication $p\to q$ is implemented as exactly two physically identifiable proper ternary source clauses
\[
 I(p,q;z)=(\neg p\vee q\vee z)\wedge(\neg p\vee q\vee\neg z),\tag{1}
\]
with a fresh private padding name $z$ for each edge. Existentially eliminating $z$ gives $\neg p\vee q$. Every satisfying assignment of a directed implication cycle is constant on that cycle, so this enforces the intended equality of all copies, \emph{without} one high-degree representative name.

Take one literally shared boundary name $y$ and guard copies $g_1,\ldots,g_m$. Add the implication cycle $y\to g_1\to g_2\to\cdots\to g_m\to y$, implementing each edge by (1). For each $j$ introduce a fresh $t_j$ and the two original source clauses
\[
 (g_j\vee \ell'_{j1}\vee t_j)\quad\wedge\quad
 (\neg t_j\vee\ell'_{j2}\vee\ell'_{j3}),\tag{2}
\]
where $\ell'_{jk}$ is the signed occurrence-name $v_{jk}$. Let $G_F$ consist of (1)--(2) and the occurrence-name cycles. Its only boundary name is $y$.

To construct the \emph{separate} pin module $P$, let
\[
 \mathrm{Pin0}(z;u,v)=\bigwedge_{s,t\in\{+1,-1\}}
 (\neg z\vee s u\vee t v),\tag{3}
\]
using four ternary clauses and a distinct private pair $u,v$ for each invocation. With new disjoint pin names $z,w$ and their private pairs, put
\[
 P(y)=\mathrm{Pin0}(z;u_z,v_z)\wedge
 \mathrm{Pin0}(w;u_w,v_w)\wedge(\neg y\vee z\vee w).\tag{4}
\]
The last clause is the \emph{only} use of $y$ in $P$. Define $H_F=G_F\wedge P$ and enumerate all actual input clauses in one canonical source order, recording their original clause IDs. There are no oracle predicates, opaque SAT labels, hidden dummy clauses, or differently renamed boundary copies.

\begin{lemma}[Exact projection and strict degree ledger]\label{lem:degree}
Let $D=\sum_{x:d_x\ge2}d_x$. Then $H_F$ has exactly
\[
 V=6m+D+8\quad\text{variables},\qquad C=4m+2D+11\quad\text{clauses},\qquad D\le3m.\tag{5}
\]
Every clause has exactly three distinct variable names and is source-unique. The incidence graph of $H_F$ is connected, and every variable occurs in at most five original source clauses. Its true existential boundary supports are
\[
 \exists\mathrm{Var}(G_F)\setminus\{y\}\,G_F\equiv
 \begin{cases}\top,&F\in\SAT,\\ y,&F\in\UNSAT,\end{cases}
 \qquad \exists\mathrm{Var}(P)\setminus\{y\}\,P\equiv\neg y.\tag{6}
\]
Thus both modules are always individually satisfiable with nonempty affine $\mathbb F_2$-supports, and $\SAT(H_F)\iff\SAT(F)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each pair (1) existentially equals its binary implication. Cycles impose equality. The two clauses (2) existentially eliminate $t_j$ to $g_j\vee\ell'_{j1}\vee\ell'_{j2}\vee\ell'_{j3}$. At $y=1$, every guard is 1 and all split clauses are satisfiable, regardless of the original variables. At $y=0$, every guard is 0 and the remaining constraints are exactly $F$ under the occurrence-name equalities. The four clauses (3) are satisfiable precisely at $z=0$; (4) therefore enforces $y=0$. Both true projections in (6) are affine sets $\{1\}$ or $\{0,1\}$ and $\{0\}$, with the same original shared bit.

There are $3m$ original occurrence-names, $m$ guards, $m$ split names, one boundary name, $m+1+D$ implication padding names, and six pin-internal names. This gives $V$; the source has $2m$ split clauses, $2(m+1+D)$ implication clauses and nine pin clauses, giving $C$. Every occurrence-name lies in one split clause and, when $d_x\ge2$, two incoming padded implications (positive twice) and two outgoing padded implications (negative twice), for at most five total appearances. The guard names do the same, plus one positive split appearance. The boundary name lies in one incoming and one outgoing padded implication, plus one negative pin appearance. Each $z,w$ of (4) occurs four times negatively in its Pin0 and once positively in the last clause. The four private $u,v$ occur twice with each sign, the $t_j$ twice total, and every edge pad twice total. No three-name clause repeats because all fresh names or oriented cycle clauses are distinct. Every source name is joined to some guard in the clause--variable incidence graph, and all guards join $y$; (4) attaches the pin module to $y$. The entire graph is connected.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One-bit, five-occurrence affine-projection hardness]\label{th:hard}
Under the \emph{actual} guarantee that both modules are individually SAT, both existential projections are affine and nonempty, the common boundary has size one, the total ordinary proper three-CNF is connected, and every source name occurs at most five times: (i) deciding whether $G_F$ has codimension $0$ is NP-complete; (ii) deciding codimension $1$, or verifying the supplied semantic affine row $y=1$ as entailed by $G_F$, is coNP-complete; and (iii) deciding SAT of $H_F$ is NP-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By (6), codimension zero and $\SAT(H_F)$ are each equivalent to $\SAT(F)$. A satisfying assignment of $G_F$ with $y=0$ witnesses codimension zero, so the promise language is in NP. Equivalently $G_F\models y$ iff $F$ is UNSAT, and a violating full assignment witnesses non-entailment, placing the entailment language in coNP. All reductions produce (5) in deterministic polynomial time and respect every promise. Proper 3SAT is NP-complete. This is language hardness, not an unconditional RAM or tape lower bound.
\end{proof}

\section{An authenticated Resolution proof simulation with a quantitative bound}
\begin{theorem}[Original-ID proof transport]\label{th:proof}
For every Resolution refutation of the original $F$ having $L$ \emph{derived} lines and maximum width $w$, there is a source-authenticated Resolution refutation of the actual $H_F$ with maximum width at most $\max(4,w)$ and with at most
\[
 \boxed{L+4m+D+9+\sum_x d_x^2}\tag{7}
\]
derived lines. All premises are authentic original clause IDs in $H_F$. The transformation runs in time polynomial in the source and input proof lengths and makes no semantic call to $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Resolve the four source clauses of each Pin0 first in pairs on one private coordinate, then resolve those two binary clauses on the other to obtain $\neg z$, respectively $\neg w$: three inferences each. Resolve (4)'s last clause with those two units to derive $\neg y$: two additional lines, eight total. For each of the $m+1+D$ padded implication pairs (1), resolve on its private $z$ to derive the \emph{actual} binary implication $\neg p\vee q$. Starting with $\neg y$, resolve backward through the guard cycle to obtain $\neg g_m,\ldots,\neg g_1$ in $m$ inferences. For each $j$, resolve the two original clauses (2) on $t_j$ to obtain a four-literal clause, and with $\neg g_j$ to derive the original clause $C_j$ on cloned occurrence-names. These steps cost $2m$ lines and width at most four.

For any original variable $x$, its directed implication cycle contains both paths $v_{x,i}\leadsto v_{x,1}$ and $v_{x,1}\leadsto v_{x,i}$ for each clone. Resolve successive authenticated binaries to derive the needed binary implication of path length at most $d_x-1$. Using the appropriate direction, resolve the resulting binary with its cloned-literal clause to replace each positive clone by representative $v_{x,1}$ and each negative clone by $\neg v_{x,1}$. The total new inferences, including the final replacements, are at most $\sum_x d_x^2$. We have now source-derived all exact literal-renamed original input clauses $C_j$ with no semantic substitution step. Replay each of the $L$ original Resolution steps using the representatives and original pivot names. Each replayed line is exactly the renamed original resolvent, and the final line is empty. Counting $8+(m+1+D)+m+2m+\sum d_x^2+L$ gives (7). Every original premise is authenticated by its actual source clause index; parent IDs, pivots and resolvents are checked, not guessed.
\end{proof}

\section{Three resource ledgers, certificate polarity, and ARC firewall}
For fixed-width binary source names, $N_H=\Theta(m\log(m+2))$, since $D\le3m$ and (5) is linear in $m$. In an indexed logarithmic-word RAM or RAT the source compiler uses $O(N_H\operatorname{polylog}N_H)$ charged bit-time and $O(N_H)$ live work bits if fully materializing (5). The proof transcriber has $O((m^2+L)\operatorname{polylog}(m+L))$ schematic inference work in addition to parsing and authenticating its input Resolution proof. Its output length, with $w'=\max(4,w)$, is at most $O((m^2+L)(w'+1)\log(m+L+2))$ bits; source-origin byte offsets, headers, line indexes and pivots contribute to this total. A straightforward indexed proof verifier may retain all lines in that much work space; a streamed log-space proof verifier with matching linear time is \emph{not} claimed. This is proof \emph{transport}, not automatic proof acquisition from an arbitrary $F$.

\noindent\textbf{M1, single shared read/write tape.} The original $N_H$ input footprint counts in total space $P_1\ge N_H$. Every physical head transition, source traversal, boundary crossing, written output symbol and re-scan contributes to $T_1$. For a conservative compiler/verifier implementation via a fixed-$k$ simulation, $T_1=O(T_k(N_H+S_k+\log(T_k+2)))$ and $P_1=O(N_H+S_k+\log(T_k+2))$; no one-tape crossing lower bound is silently transferred to a RAM source.

\noindent\textbf{Mk, fixed finite sequential tapes.} The read-only input is separate and excluded from $S_k$, whereas every visited work cell and simultaneous proof buffer is counted. All read/write head moves, work-cell probes and traffic are included in $T_k$, with output-only cells exempt from $S_k$ only if never reread. Source indexing can require scans; indexed-RAM access is not free on sequential heads. The construction admits a conservative polynomial scan implementation; no near-boundary exponent is inferred by mixing its time with another machine's space.

\noindent\textbf{RAT / log-word RAM.} Random indexed clause access and parent-line addressing cost actual index arithmetic and bit/word probes, not sequential physical boundary crossing. Proof parsing, all source reads, memory traffic and peak buffers are charged, and a generated child is counted as real materialized bytes unless a proved virtual-access compiler exists. Our compiler produces a larger ordinary 3CNF than $F$, never a power-short child; two levelwise ordinary-clause deletions shrink by only $O(\log N)$ encoded bits per level. For fixed guest $A$ running in $N^{K+o(1)}$, $K$ remains unknown under $P=NP$. The child spectrum inequality $\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V$ applies only for $d\ge1$ to \emph{actual} supported child lengths and buys no strict $\lambda<1$ here.

On SAT input $H_F$ no globally sound source-authenticated refuter can accept any proof. On UNSAT input, a source-supported Resolution certificate exists, but bound (7) depends on the size $L$ of an original Resolution proof and says nothing about a uniform small $L$. For nondeterministic $\coNTIME$ certificate guessing, a \emph{uniform input-length-only} cap and a fixed verifier clock are required: invalid/timed-out guesses reject; if such a polynomial cap held for all ordinary UNSAT sources with a polynomial verifier, Cook--Reckhow yields $NP=coNP$~\cite{CR}. Deterministic ARC additionally pays actual acquisition, failure histories and proof selection. No arbitrary $A$ is required to output Resolution lines or the affine row.
