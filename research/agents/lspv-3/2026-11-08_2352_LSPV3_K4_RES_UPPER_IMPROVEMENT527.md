## FAMILY/THEOREM/ENCODING/METHOD/TRANSFER LEDGER

For K4 on vertices 0,1,2,3, enumerate its six edges lexicographically. Each edge has three Boolean one-hot indicators `x[e,a]` for `a=0,1,2`. Add one positive strict width-three at-least-one source clause per edge. For each cubic vertex v, orient incidences by increasing endpoint and impose the mod-three flow sum equal to b_v, where `b_0=1` and the other charges vanish. Each of the 18 forbidden local edge-value triples produces one strict three-negative-literal source clause. Thus the source is exactly 78 clauses, 18 original atoms, 6 ALO + 4 x 18 forbidden, with no padded clauses or auxiliary sources.

The negative total charge certifies UNSAT by summing vertex constraints in F3, provided one-hot semantics is enforced; independently, enumeration of all 3^6 edge values gives 0 satisfying one-hot assignments, while the charge-zero control gives 27. The local source is an instance of the standard modulo-three Tseitin construction, but NOT itself a growing lower-bound family. Its restricted known-hardness control is Pang Theorem 7.2(b) for qualified expanders and Res(PC_r/F2), not the full historical calculus.

Legal method used here: original SOURCE leaves, exact oriented binary RES, global DAG sharing of repeated clauses and fully paid derived nodes. No splitting, extension gate, XOR shortcut or private export is needed.
