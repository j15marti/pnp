\section{New explicit logical-strength theorem}
Set
\[
f(t)=\max\{11/10,3/5+t/2\},\quad
g(s)=\max\{1/2,s/2\}.
\]
Let $\mathsf C$ be the \emph{abstract} existential closure assertion
\[
\forall(t,s)\in\EE\;\exists(t',s')\in\EE:
\quad t'\le f(t),\quad s'\le g(s).
\]
For a fixed unbounded $K\subseteq\mathbb N$, let $\mathsf H_K$ state that each $(k,k)\in\EE$ with $k\in K$ has a finite route through achievable pairs to $B$, each required edge admitting a source-checkable construction or explicit existence theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Logical equivalence of abstract ARC obligations, PROVED/EXACT]
Assuming the established matched FLMV endpoint and ordinary polynomial equivalence of deterministic machine models, each proposition $\mathsf C$ and $\mathsf H_K$ is logically equivalent to $P\ne NP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The achievable region $\EE$ is empty exactly when $P\ne NP$: any finite pair witnesses a polynomial-time all-input SAT decider, while any polynomial-time SAT machine uses at most polynomial work space after a model-compatible polynomial simulation. If $\EE=\varnothing$, both $\mathsf C$ and $\mathsf H_K$ hold vacuously.

If $P=NP$, choose $(t_0,s_0)\in\EE$. Upward closure puts all sufficiently large $(k,k)$ into $\EE$, and unbounded $K$ contains such a $k$. Since $B\cap\EE=\varnothing$, no route of achievable points reaches $B$, so $\mathsf H_K$ is false. If $\mathsf C$ held, repeated finite existential choices would give
\[
t_j\le\frac65+2^{-j}(t_0-\frac65)_+,\qquad
s_j\le\max\{\frac12,2^{-j}s_0\}.
\]
For some \emph{finite} fixed $j$, $t_j<c_*$ and $s_j\le1/2$, placing an achievable pair in $B$, contradiction. Thus $\mathsf C$ is also false if $P=NP$.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Interpretation.} Existential routes are weaker \emph{implementation obligations} than a universal compiler, but the abstract closure sentence itself is separation-strength. It cannot be marked FILLED merely from recurrence arithmetic or the absence of a counterexample. A substantive all-input source-grounded construction or independently proved existence implication remains the load-bearing OPEN work. This theorem is a metamathematical audit, not an algorithm or a $P\ne NP$ proof.

\section{Exact adaptive shared-interface hostile control}
For $k\ge2$ let
\[
R_b=\{x\in\mathbb F_2^k:\oplus_{i=1}^k x_i=b\}.
\]
Let $e\in\mathbb F_2^k$ be a vector of independently addressed signed source fields and put $p=\oplus_i e_i$. The two-module constraint is $x\in R_0\cap R_p$. Each module is individually SAT.

\begin{lemma}[All strict projections agree but gluing fails, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every strict $S\subsetneq[k]$, $\pi_S(R_0)=\pi_S(R_1)=\mathbb F_2^S$, whereas $R_0\cap R_1=\varnothing$. Therefore no algorithm, even with adaptive selection of arbitrarily many strict-coordinate projection relations, can decide the joint SAT label using only these oracle responses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Any assignment to a strict coordinate subset leaves at least one unassigned coordinate, which can be fixed to meet either parity. Thus all strict projections of the two cosets coincide. The cosets are disjoint. Adaptive queries with identical responses induce identical transcripts and cannot distinguish opposite labels.
\end{proof}
The actual source-aware SAT label is $\oplus e_i=0$. Changing any independently addressable $e_i$ reverses it, so all $k$ fields are necessary in the declared field-query model on every input. A direct parity scan decides in $O(k)$ word operations. XOR3 can be encoded by four proper ternaries and the signed source units by standard forced-literal gadgets; ordinary proper 3CNF has $O(k)$ clauses and $O(k\log k)$ explicit bits, including all auxiliaries. Full syntax recognition and fallback on nonmembers must be charged. Thus the restricted projection-oracle impossibility does not transfer to arbitrary SAT deciders, which may read source fields and switch to Gaussian or direct parity methods.

This parity instance is a \emph{duplicate control}, not novel hardness: role-4 DUAL-PARITY-JOINT77 and HIGH-ORDER-SYNDROME78 already study stronger joint-syndrome holes. Canonical SIL specifies contextual equivalence by equality of full existential boundary relations, not by their satisfiability, rank alone, or a collection of small marginals. LQP provenance and small hashes do not provide the missing exact boundary service.
