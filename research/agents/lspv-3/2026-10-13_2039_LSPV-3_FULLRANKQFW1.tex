\section{Claim card, exact quantifiers, and model}
The structured input is
\[
 I=(X,T,c,d,b),
\]
where $X=(V,E)$ is an explicitly encoded finite graph, $T$ is a spanning
forest, $C=E\setminus T$ is listed in an order of even cardinality $2t>0$,
$c\in\F^V$ is explicit, $d\in\F^C$ is a phase vector, and $b\in\F$.  Invalid
encodings are rejected.  Define
\[
 G_I(x)=[B_Xx=c]\ \wedge\
 \left[\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}
 (x_{C_{2i-1}}\oplus d_{2i-1})(x_{C_{2i}}\oplus d_{2i})=b\right].       \tag{1}
\]
Full source access, parsing, forest validation, charge consistency, residual
evaluation, witness reconstruction, verification, output length, and
polynomial workspace are charged in $N=|\operatorname{enc} I|$.

The audited implication was the following universal sufficiency claim:
\begin{quote}
For every infinite encoded family of satisfiable affine-residual systems,
if the projected affine rank tends to infinity and the residual quadratic
polar form has full rank on that projection, then the family has no uniform
polynomial-time witness finder.
\end{quote}
Theorem~1 gives one infinite family and one fixed uniform polynomial-time
finder, so this implication is \RET{}.  It does not assert that every
full-rank quadratic system is easy.

The desired project endpoint would still require, for one fixed NP-complete
language, a worst-case statement quantified over every total uniform
deterministic decider and every polynomial exponent.  Nothing below proves
that endpoint.  The SAME\_REALIZATION tuple is the one encoded $I$, its actual
charge $c$, phase $d$, residual circuit, returned $x$, and the direct
verification of (1).

\section{Primary-literature and barrier-transfer preflight}
De Colnet and Mengel prove DNNF and regular-Resolution lower bounds for
Tseitin formulas in terms of treewidth \cite{dCM}.  This is a directly
transferable theorem only for the named representations and proof system;
their DNNF lower bound does not lower-bound finding one witness.

Schaefer classifies affine Boolean constraint languages as tractable and
shows that the actual permitted relation set, rather than the adjective
``nonlinear,'' matters \cite{Schaefer}.  This is a useful classification
principle, but Theorem~1 is proved directly.  Couvreur--Mora--Tillich study
matrix spaces of quadratic relations and exploit unusually low-rank matrices
to distinguish or recover structured codes \cite{CMT}; Randriambololona uses
graded Betti numbers of shortened dual codes as a distinguisher \cite{Rand}.
These are exact algebraic objects in their code settings.  Importing
``quadratic rank'' or ``syzygy complexity'' as unrestricted witness-search
time is a failed model transfer.  The present construction makes the opposite
algorithmic point: visible algebraic structure can expose a witness.

No primary source found supplies a theorem converting full quadratic polar
rank, DNNF size, proof size, or a projected cycle-space dimension into an
unrestricted multitape/RAM time lower bound.  The missing theorem would need
to constrain every eligible algorithm on the same input while allowing
compact circuits and polynomial workspace.

\section{Exact full-rank quadratic firewall}
Let $C=E\setminus T$ and suppose $|C|=2t$.  Put
\[
 y_j=x_{C_j}\oplus d_j,
 \qquad
 q_t(y)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}y_{2i-1}y_{2i}.                 \tag{2}
\]

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{FULL-RANK-QUADRATIC-FIREWALL1}]
Assume $c$ has even XOR on every connected component of $X$.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item projection of the affine coset $\{x:B_Xx=c\}$ onto the cotree $C$ is
a bijection with $\F^{2t}$;
\item $\rho=\dim\pi_C\ker B_X=2t$;
\item the polar bilinear form
\[
 \beta_q(u,v)=q_t(u+v)+q_t(u)+q_t(v)
\]
has matrix $\operatorname{diag}(J,\ldots,J)$ with
$J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, hence rank
$2t$;
\item (1) has a witness uniformly computable in polynomial bit time and
polynomial workspace for both $b=0$ and $b=1$.
\end{enumerate}
The residual is shared-variable and uses every cotree coordinate.  Its compact
fan-in-two arithmetic circuit has $t$ AND gates and $t-1$ XOR gates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A cycle-space vector supported on the forest $T$ is zero.  Thus cotree
projection is injective on $\ker B_X$.  Both spaces have dimension
$|E|-|V|+\operatorname{cc}(X)=|C|=2t$, so projection is bijective; translating
by one affine solution preserves the statement.  This gives items 1 and 2.

Expanding (2) yields
\[
 \beta_q(u,v)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}
 (u_{2i-1}v_{2i}+u_{2i}v_{2i-1}),
\]
whose matrix is the asserted nonsingular block diagonal matrix.

For $b=0$, choose $y=0$.  For $b=1$, choose
$y_1=y_2=1$ and all remaining coordinates zero.  Set
$x_C=y+d$.  On each tree component, subtract the cotree incidences from $c$
and process the forest leaf-to-root, assigning each parent edge to discharge
the child charge.  Component parity makes the root equation hold.  Forest
discovery/validation, the scan, and the $|E|$-bit output are polynomial in the
explicit encoding.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Unbounded-width hostile control]
Let $X_r=K_{2r+1,2r+1}$ and take the standard spanning tree consisting of all
edges incident to the first left vertex and all remaining left vertices'
edges to the first right vertex.  Then
\[
 |C|=(2r)^2=4r^2,
\]
so both projected rank and polar rank are $4r^2$, yet the same uniform
witness algorithm applies for every consistent charge and phase.  Hence the
firewall is not confined to the bounded-treewidth friendship control.
\end{corollary}

The zero level set is genuinely non-affine: it contains $0,e_1,e_2$ but not
$e_1+e_2$.  The firewall therefore strengthens RANKNONLINFW1, whose residual
was a bounded-weight predicate: polynomial witness search survives even a
maximally nondegenerate quadratic polar form represented by a linear-size
circuit.

\section{What the theorem does and does not retire}
It retires only the inference
\[
 \rho\to\infty\quad+\quad \operatorname{rank}\beta_q=\rho
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\text{unavoidable witness-search work}.       \tag{3}
\]
It does not solve arbitrary systems of quadratic equations, arbitrary 3SAT
embedded on cotree coordinates, or residuals with $h$ uncharged external
variables.  In the latter case the predecessor's honest direct enumeration
bound remains $2^{\rho+h}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ unless extra structure is
proved.  Nor is the size-increasing affine wrapper a shrinker.

For the UWI benchmark, the family admits one constant-description program
that recognizes the declared structured encoding, selects the two-bit
residual witness, and performs the forest lift.  Thus a lower bound based only
on the two ranks cannot defeat the whole eligible short-program portfolio on
these satisfiable inputs.  This is not a general SAT decider.

\section{Hostile controls, wrappers, and barriers}
\paragraph{Controls.}
The theorem allows arbitrary explicit consistent charges, arbitrary phases,
vertex renaming and edge-order shuffling.  The forest may be disconnected if
component parity is checked; disjoint products still lift componentwise.
The finite suite included affine parity, Horn implication chains, and Krom
equivalence chains on free cotree coordinates.  Ordinary Tseitin remains
polynomial by the same forest solver.  The complete-bipartite family prevents
bounded treewidth from being the sole explanation.

\paragraph{Compact representations and wrappers.}
The residual is supplied as a compact $2t-1$ gate arithmetic circuit; expanded
monomial size is not charged as runtime.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP and
source-assisted replay may wrap the decoder and lifter but cannot force the
algorithm to enumerate all $2^{2t}$ projections.  Program length is not
workspace.  No hidden charge, source assignment, nonuniform advice, or free
decomposition discovery is used: $X,T,c,d,b$ are parsed from the input and
validated.

\paragraph{Relativization, algebrization, Natural Proofs.}
The explicit solver continues to work relative to arbitrary extra oracles,
and an algebraic oracle can only expose additional shortcuts.  The firewall
therefore does not provide a nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing hinge.  It
constructs no large useful circuit property, so Natural Proofs is not the
immediate issue.  The defect is prior: (3) has a direct uniform counterexample.

\paragraph{Post-quantum ledger.}
Cotree projection is an exact puncturing/shortening object transfer, and the
polar matrix is exactly a quadratic-form relation object.  Their ranks are
algorithmically computable and exploitable.  The inference from high rank to
hardness is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.  Syzygies/Betti numbers and
low-rank-relation searches remain \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} diagnostics for
a separately specified residual, not lower bounds.  Collision entropy of the
cotree bijection counts fibers but not time.  LWE-style extraction and
special-soundness require new unconditional model-matched bridges.
