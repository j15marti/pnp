\section*{Exact target and quantifiers}
Let $\mathcal E_I$ be the legal environments/completions associated with one externally fixed instance $I$ of a future $W_{\rm exact}$. Let $\mathsf{Obs}(C)$ denote all information exposed to the compiler for environment $C$, including the static instance, declared realized transcript, allowed SAT-oracle answers, charged preprocessing products, and any explicitly granted off-path access. Let $\mathsf{Req}(C,b)\subseteq\mathcal Y$ be the valid response set for legal off-path challenge $b$.

A deterministic universal compiler is a map
\[
E:\mathsf{Obs}(C)\mapsto S_C
\]
such that for every legal $C$ and legal $b$,
\[
S_C(b)\in\mathsf{Req}(C,b).
\]

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: VIEWSEP1}
\textbf{Theorem.} If such a deterministic universal compiler exists, then for every pair $C_0,C_1\in\mathcal E_I$ and every challenge $b$ satisfying
\[
\mathsf{Req}(C_0,b)\cap\mathsf{Req}(C_1,b)=\varnothing,
\]
one must have
\[
\mathsf{Obs}(C_0)\ne\mathsf{Obs}(C_1).
\]
\textbf{Proof.} Suppose instead that $\mathsf{Obs}(C_0)=\mathsf{Obs}(C_1)$. Determinism gives the same strategy $S=E(\mathsf{Obs}(C_0))=E(\mathsf{Obs}(C_1))$. Correctness at $b$ would require $S(b)\in\mathsf{Req}(C_0,b)\cap\mathsf{Req}(C_1,b)$, contradicting disjointness. This is exactly the contrapositive form of verified OFFPATH1. $\square$
