$$
(\neg z\lor x_1),\qquad

(z\lor\neg x_1\lor\neg x_2).
$$

$$C=(\neg x_1\lor\neg x_2\lor x_3\lor\neg t).$$

## Abstraction/cost interpretation

An exact concrete /386 configuration stores independent source data, a gate-owner table, a globally fresh gate-wire namespace, 69 topologically ordered signed-clause records, facet `gate` references, and the distinct paid owner and Resolution occurrence sets. In the restricted /386 semantics, alpha retains exact signed clauses, gate truth functions, source labels, parent indices and paid identities. Gamma consists of those typed /386 configurations faithfully represented by the abstract records. SOURCE and facet projections cost zero, introducing a gate owner costs one once, and a new RES step costs one. An old owner or RES result used at reconvergence is not paid again. Therefore each exhibited concrete transition is included in the corresponding abstract step, the empty-clause goal is preserved, and abstract cost equals concrete paid cost. This is not an overapproximation theorem for **full /341**, for which LOCAL-CONVERT, AND3/XOR interaction, branching/discharge and native wire export remain unknown.
