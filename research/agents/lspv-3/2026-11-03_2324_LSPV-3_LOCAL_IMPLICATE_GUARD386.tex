\section*{Certified hard starting point and exact scope}
Use standard unary $\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ CNF: variables $p_{ij}$ ($h(h+1)$ names), $h+1$ positive length-$h$ pigeon clauses and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative binary collision clauses. Explicit signed ASCII DIMACS size is $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. Haken (1985) proves
\[
\exists c>0\;\exists h_0\;\forall h\ge h_0\;\forall\pi\in
\mathrm{ResolutionRefutations}(\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}):
\quad|\pi|\ge2^{ch}.
\]
Here $\pi$ is an arbitrary ordinary Resolution \emph{shared DAG} and $|\pi|$ counts its inferred lines. This is neither a lower bound for Extended Resolution, $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$, full mixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, nor uniform SAT algorithms. Cook (1976) constructs polynomial Extended Resolution PHP proofs. Odd/even Tseitin with short paid GF(2) derivations is a mandatory escape control. We generated no new alleged hard family.

\section*{Fixed grammar, cost and quantified goal}
The preselected full $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ has free indexed original CNF sources and charged unit-weight RES, exact AND3, binary GF(2) XOR, authenticated LOCAL-CONVERT and paid source-independent two-input conservative VARDEF. This note proves results only for its existing restricted $E=\{\mathrm{RES},\mathrm{signed\ AND2\ VARDEF}\}$ and the assertion-consequence $L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}$ fragment: add already legal factor AND3 and bounded LOCAL-CONVERT nodes only when \emph{all} premises and conclusions are assertion-valued Boolean factors on one common support of at most three named original-or-auxiliary wires, with no implicit newly introduced wire or semantic oracle. Every local output is a logical consequence of the conjunction of its parents. Exact wire \emph{equality} is not substituted for assertion implication. Original/auxiliary types are retained; the translator itself invokes no AND3.

For any task $(F\vdash C)$ let $D_S(F\vdash C)$ be the minimum unit cost over valid source-authenticated acyclic DAGs in $S$ ending in $C$, charging each distinct inference and definition once. Source clauses have cost zero. Positive integer charges and completeness of RES ensure attained minima on UNSAT inputs.

\begin{lemma}[Complete saturation of a bounded assertion interface]
For every integer $k\ge0$, every explicit signed CNF $\Delta$ on a fixed set $U$ of $k$ Boolean wires and every nontautological clause $C$ with $\Delta\models C$, there is a valid ordinary shared Resolution DAG, adding at most $3^k$ distinct charged clauses, whose conclusion is some $D\subseteq C$. Each inferred clause uses only $U$, provided $C$'s variables belong to $U$; unused output variables can be removed first.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There are exactly $3^k$ distinct proper clauses on $U$ including the empty clause, since each wire occurs positively, negatively or not at all. Starting with authenticated $\Delta$, repeatedly add each missing nontautological binary resolvent. At most $3^k$ distinct inferred clauses can be added, so this is an effective, finite, valid, shared-DAG saturation. To show implicate completeness, eliminate by the Davis--Putnam Resolution procedure all variables in $U\setminus\mathrm{var}(C)$. Each projected clause is an initial or derived clause of the saturation, and the projected CNF is equivalent to existentially quantifying those eliminated variables. Because $\Delta\models C$, the assignment falsifying every literal of $C$ cannot satisfy that projection. Thus one projected clause $D$ is false under that assignment; it uses only variables of $C$, so $D\subseteq C$. This also covers the contradictory case $C=\bot$. No weakening, free semantic clause, or implicit source-conjunction rule was used.
\end{proof}
\noindent If $C$ mentions names absent from $U$, delete those literals before choosing a consequence subclause: a consequence with output-only names has a subclause consequence over its antecedent support. The local factor application below uses a declared common support from the outset.

\begin{theorem}[At most 27 paid RES nodes per three-wire assertion step]
For every finite UNSAT CNF $F$, the assertion-consequence calculus above satisfies
\[
\boxed{ D_{E}(F\vdash\bot)\le
27 D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}(F\vdash\bot). }\tag{1}
\]
The same compilation bound holds for a literal target $C$, including $C=\neg t$ from a satisfiable guarded source, whenever a sound source-authenticated derivation of $C$ exists. This is an \emph{upper} simulation: $E$ uses no local rules, so $D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}\le D_E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Traverse any fixed legal input DAG topologically, preserving its authentic free source clauses and replaying paid AND2 definitions and their exact three clause views. Represent each assertion-valued factor $P$ over its at-most-three declared named wires by a finite conjunction $\widehat P$ of previously derived clauses satisfying $\widehat P\models P$. Initially, every original clause is represented exactly. For a paid local inference from one or two factors $P_i$ to a factor $Q$, the previously derived parent clause conjunction $\Delta=\bigwedge_i\widehat P_i$ implies $Q$. On the same at-most-three names, express $Q$ by its at most eight complete falsifying-assignment blockers $B_\alpha$. For \emph{each} blocker, $\Delta\models B_\alpha$. Saturate \emph{once} on the shared at-most-three-wire universe and use the preceding lemma to select an already-derived subclause $D_\alpha\subseteq B_\alpha$ for each blocker. The entire saturation charges at most $3^3=27$ genuine Resolution nodes, regardless of the number of output blockers. The conjunction of the $D_\alpha$ remains a source-entailed assertion and implies $Q$; it need not be equivalent to $Q$. Every original paid RES step between clauses can be replayed with at most one paid pivot: if both strengthened parents retain opposite pivot literals, resolve; if either has lost its pivot, that strengthened parent itself subsumes the target resolvent. It follows inductively that every original node is represented soundly and each costs at most 27 translated nodes. Shared originals and shared translated clauses count once. At a false root the strengthened output contains the empty clause, yielding a genuine refutation. For a target unit, the translation derives that unit or its only strict subclause, empty; if the sources are satisfiable, the latter is excluded by soundness. Thus the same bound holds for guarded entailment. The construction neither consults a SAT answer nor hardcodes instances.
\end{proof}

\section*{Guarded transport and its explicit dependency}
Put $G_t(F)=\bigwedge_{C\in F}(\neg t\lor C)$, fresh $t$. For UNSAT $F$, $G_t(F)$ is SAT at $t=0$ but entails $\neg t$. LSPV-2/381 (blob \texttt{671d56bfb6448ee6867b053169967e4e2bdb01b6}) proves the exact $t$-dependent signed-AND2 guard isometry
\[
D_E(F\vdash\bot)=D_E(G_t(F)\vdash\neg t)
\]
by restricting $t=1$, simplifying paid conservative gates, and transporting Resolution under literal/constant substitutions. Combining that externally authored theorem with (1), and inclusion of $E$ in $L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}$, gives the \emph{conditional on that audited dependency} quantitative two-sided bound
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}(G_t(F)\vdash\neg t)&\le27D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}(F\vdash\bot),\\
D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}(F\vdash\bot)&\le27D_{L_3^{\mathrm{imp}}}(G_t(F)\vdash\neg t).
\end{aligned}}\tag{2}
\]
This extends LSPV-3/385's $t$-free proof lift to \emph{guard-dependent} paid gate and assertion-local steps with polynomial rather than exact cost. It improves LSPV-1/359's published 440-node exact-blocker compiler only in the \emph{assertion-consequence} setting by allowing resolution-subsuming stronger output clauses. It is not a representation-equivalence theorem for exact definitional wires or unrestricted LOCAL-CONVERT. The guarded formula's encoded size is $O(|\mathrm{enc}(F)|)$ with ordinary explicit DIMACS and bounded per-clause guard insertion.

\section*{Four falsifiers and controls}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{F1 (SUBSUMPTION VS EQUALITY):} Replacing an exact named-wire definition by a stronger clause is not conservative. For example, $x$ implies $(x\vee y)$, but they differ at $(x,y)=(0,1)$. \textbf{VALID counterexample} to extending (1) to exact wire identities, not to the assertion-consequence grammar.
\item[] \textbf{F2 (FOURTH INPUT WIRE):} A local premise on four independent wires has as many as $3^4=81$ proper clauses. \textbf{INVALID as a three-wire operation}; no 27-node claim is made for an unrestricted named-wire circuit.
\item[] \textbf{F3 (GUARD-DEPENDENT AND2):} $z=t\land x$ invalidates naive negative-guard erasure at the individual RES step. \textbf{TRUE as a step counterexample}; LSPV-2/381's nontrivial $t=1$ restriction is the required repair.
\item[] \textbf{F4 (UNRESTRICTED COMPLEXITY):} Haken hardness would force superpolynomial mixed-work or SAT decision. \textbf{FALSE}; Cook's ER PHP proofs and GF(2) Tseitin shortcuts are explicit counters. $G_t(F)$ itself is SAT for every $F$.
\end{description}

\section*{Computational control, encoded cost and corpus}

All 60,510 entailed clauses had a subsuming member of the independently saturated closure; 24,576 required output blockers passed, all 256 $t=1$ cofactors passed, five malformed records and 16 corrupted RES records were rejected. Observed maximum 19 \emph{added} inference nodes, at most 27 possible distinct clauses; this is neither an observed global optimum nor an all-$R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ claim. The logical 27-node bound is not an arbitrary-machine running-time bound. For explicit encoded source/proof length $L$, the compiler reads input/source slots, each proof node, gate definitions, parent IDs and factor/circuit payloads; it computes eight truth-table rows, enumerates at most 27 local clauses and up to a fixed number of Resolution checks, writes the expanded evidence and verifies source provenance. Bit-time, peak work space and output length are polynomial in $L+|\mathrm{enc}(F)|$, not necessarily in \emph{unit-weight node count alone} when a legal operation carries a long payload. Exponentially large explicit representations are charged; lazy representations require their own authenticated access model. No trace-to-refutation, FLMV or same-machine transfer is derived.
