\section{Restricted theorem and proof}
For even $t$, $H_t=F9^{\wedge t}$ on disjoint renamed original variables is itself canonical, with $n=9t$. Let $\mathcal P_n$ contain at most $n^d$ predetermined input-independent variable orders, fixed $d$. For sufficiently large even $t$ there exists a relabeling $\sigma$ under which EVERY $\pi\in\mathcal P_n$ has middle-cut GF(2) Boolean matrix rank at least $2^{\lfloor t/2\rfloor}$. Thus each corresponding exact one-way evaluator needs that many cut states, and a reduced OBDD needs at least $2^{\lfloor t/2\rfloor}-2$ nonterminal nodes overall.
\noindent\textbf{Proof.} Under independent fair left/right coloring, a nine-variable block is split with probability $1-2/2^9=255/256$. For split-block count $X\sim\mathrm{Bin}(t,255/256)$, the multiplicative Chernoff bound gives $\Pr(X<t/2)\le\exp(-16129t/130560)$. Conditioning on exactly $n/2$ left colors produces the uniform random relabeling model; the conditioning event has probability at least $1/(n+1)$. A union bound over $n^d$ orders therefore has failure probability at most $n^d(n+1)\exp(-16129t/130560)<1$ for all sufficiently large even $t$. Under a successful relabeling, at least $\lfloor t/2\rfloor$ disjoint F9 blocks are split in each prescribed order. The complete cut matrix is, up to row/column permutation, a Kronecker product of block cut matrices. Each split block has GF(2) rank at least 2, and an unsplit block has rank 1 because F9 is satisfiable. Rank multiplicativity gives the bound. Distinct Boolean cofactor rows are at least the rank. The same rank holds in the reverse direction after transposition. \hfill$\square$

\section{Declared bit-level resource bounds}
For an explicitly materialized, source-indexed representation of input length $N$, one conservative deterministic multi-tape implementation of the F9-component recognizer uses $O(N^4)$ bit steps and $O(N^2)$ simultaneous writable bits: parse and check every signed occurrence and duplicate triple, construct a primal adjacency bit-matrix, traverse components, perform at most $9!$ constant-size permutations per nine-name component, write the witness and verify every original clause. This is a safe coarse upper bound, not a tight measured cost, and no machine-specific FLMV transfer is claimed. The complete adaptive controller additionally pays all failed recognition, attempted policies, guest subcomputations and retained ancestor/index work.
A declared OBDD compiler which must explicitly materialize its reduced full-function DAG pays at least $\Omega(2^{\lfloor t/2\rfloor})$ node-output work on the adversarial relabeling, even if it chooses its best of the precommitted orders. If the full DAG must remain simultaneously writable, peak representation space is likewise at least its node count in bits; a streaming or recomputing evaluator has no such asserted peak-space bound. A one-way full-function evaluator only needs at least $\lfloor t/2\rfloor$ bits of distinguishable-state capacity, not exponential physical memory. The source-aware SAT witness avoids this DAG entirely. The preceding cost statements never equate this OBDD output lower bound with arbitrary SAT-decision time.
