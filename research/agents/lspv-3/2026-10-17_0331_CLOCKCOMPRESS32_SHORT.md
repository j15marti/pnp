# CLOCKCOMPRESS32: charged compiler barrier
17 October 2026, 03:31 Korea time. UNIVERSAL-OBSTRUCTION research.

Pre-search proposals: short-advice replay, bounded portfolio self-reference, exceptional hash fibers, certified witness acquisition. Checked recent project chats, GitHub COLLISIONESCAPE31/CANONICALGATE18, Drive SIM-2 and clock audits, and Cook/Seiferas-Fischer-Meyer/Bogdanov-Talwar-Wan literature.

EXACT THEOREM: For integers 1<=a<b, some nondeterministic n^b-time machine has no exact explicit proper3CNF reifier simultaneously compiling in O(n^a) and outputting O(n^a) bits on every input. Otherwise a nondeterministic near-linear SAT verifier after compilation would put every NTIME(n^b) language into NTIME(n^a polylog n), contradicting the nondeterministic time hierarchy. Both output and compilation costs are essential. This is an ALL-verifier limitation, not a lower bound on a particular SAT portfolio.

EXACT COUNTERCONTROL: DELAY-SAT_k waits n^k steps before verifying that w satisfies source 3CNF F. Identity compilation outputs F in linear time, and preserves every witness. Thus long literal tableaux are not necessary for source-visible formula equivalence, even for NP-complete SAT.

AUDIT: proper3CNF delay gadget with m+4t+8 clauses. 765 instances, 6120 primary projections, 22452 clause checks, 15 growth controls; zero discrepancies. At n=8 delay=4096, delayed encoding 299417 bytes versus original 225 bytes.

HANDOFF: An actual common-input UWI theorem still requires a satisfiable F defeating the whole eligible short-program portfolio, with compiler, source decoding, simulation, branch access, witness acquisition and time/description bounds charged. A generic hierarchy theorem and a syntactic clock-length argument cannot supply that missing step. UWI and P vs NP remain OPEN. No exact-(2,2) transport proven.
