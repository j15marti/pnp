\section{Four preregistered falsifiers and exact quantifiers}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{H1:} A fully source-identified binary BRANCH tree with a total exact $A$ at residual original-source clause-subset leaves can be replaced by a pure original-variable, original-clause conflict tree of size controlled by the \emph{entire leaf spectrum}, even if $A$ supplies no conventional proof. \textbf{PROVED/RESTRICTED}. The expansion is existential for lower-bound transfer; it is emphatically not a fast algorithm for running $A$.
\item[] \textbf{H2:} Adding charged source-clause-authenticated 2CNF implication-cycle (SCC/PATH) and source-EMPTY exits destroys that expansion argument. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: such leaves have linear-size pure decision expansions and the weighted inequality survives.
\item[] \textbf{H3:} On every connected proper signed three-CNF, a polynomial-length all-UNSAT certificate of this restricted grammar exists if every $A$ child has ordinary bit length $N^{1/2}$. \textbf{FALSIFIED} using the original all-field PCR-hard graph-colouring family and the independently verified proper-ternary transfer of PC-BRANCH-COLOUR134.
\item[] \textbf{H4:} H3 rules out arbitrary small \emph{derived} ordinary SAT children, unrestricted CP, algebraic DAG sharing, or the actual fixed $A$-assisted ARC edge. \textbf{NOT PROVED}; the distinction is a required quantifier/type firewall.
\end{description}

Fix a total uniform deterministic exact ordinary-SAT decider $A$ on \emph{all} binary encodings, with whichever simultaneously achieved indexed read-only-input random-access pair $(t,s)$ it actually possesses. A hypothetical $P=NP$ gives some unknown finite $(K,K)$ and does not grant $d=901/500$. Every claimed $A$ leaf in the following syntax must be executed and return \UNSAT; its verdict cannot be supplied as an unauthenticated bit. No assumption about the steps, time, or internal state of $A$ enters the combinatorial lower bound.

\section{Typed certificate and source-level leaf spectrum}
Let $F$ be a proper signed ordinary $3$CNF of physical canonical encoding length $N$, with distinct immutable original source clause identifiers. An \emph{unshared binary BRANCH certificate} is an explicit finite rooted tree. An internal node selects an original variable not previously fixed on its path, and \emph{both} assigned children must be certified with the same accumulated ancestor assignment. Each leaf is one of:
\begin{description}
\item[] $\mathsf{A\mbox{-}SOURCE}(J)$: $J$ is an authenticated subset of original source clause IDs; under the accumulated assignment $\alpha$, the ordinary CNF $G=(\bigwedge_{j\in J} C_j)|_\alpha$ is materialized (or supplied through fully charged exact virtual source access) with correct headers and source names; $A(G)=\UNSAT$. For this theorem, there are \emph{no new existential auxiliary variables or nontrivial derived clauses} in $G$. Canonical renumbering accompanied by a checked bijective alias map is harmless.
\item[] $\mathsf{EMPTY}$: one authenticated original source clause is already falsified by $\alpha$.
\item[] $\mathsf{SCC}$: an authenticated original-source clause subset becomes a 2CNF (possibly with empty/unit clauses) under $\alpha$ and presents two correctly directed implication paths $x\leadsto\neg x$ and $\neg x\leadsto x$, each edge containing its exact source ID and residual literals. Unrelated surviving ternary clauses outside this subset are permitted. Let $e_\ell$ be the combined number of displayed path edges.
\end{description}
The verifier checks every source offset, clause content, path edge, ancestor alias, full child encoding, entire branching coverage, and the actual guest $A$ answer; a malformed, timed-out, or SAT-labeled $A$ exit rejects. Its implementation and worst-history running time are separately charged. In particular, this definition does not inherit a parent-scale SAT status for free.

Write $R_{\rm DT}(F)$ for the minimum number of terminal leaves in any full binary original-variable decision refutation in which each leaf \emph{directly falsifies an authenticated original source clause}. It is finite exactly when $F$ is UNSAT. For each $A$ leaf $\ell$, write $v_\ell$ for the number of distinct unassigned variables actually appearing in its residual $G_\ell$, $q_\ell$ for the number of surviving clauses, and $m_\ell$ for the \emph{entire physically encoded child bit length}, including headers. Since original clauses have width at most three,
\[
                  v_\ell\le 3q_\ell\le 3m_\ell.
\tag{1}
\]
These are actual \emph{surviving} child clauses: many source IDs may be read and then discarded by restriction, but such discarded clauses do not affect $v_\ell$ or the validity of the bound. All reads and preprocessing still cost time.

\begin{theorem}[Exact original-source leaf-spectrum expansion]\label{th:spectrum}
For every accepted tree in the typed grammar above, with $\mathcal A$, $\mathcal S$, and $\mathcal E$ the sets of $A$-SOURCE, SCC and EMPTY leaves, respectively,
\[
\boxed{\quad
R_{\rm DT}(F)\ \le\
\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal A}2^{v_\ell}
+\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal S}(e_\ell+2)
+|\mathcal E|
\ \le\
\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal A}2^{3m_\ell}
+\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal S}(e_\ell+2)+|\mathcal E| .\quad}
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently, if $M=\max_{\ell\in\mathcal A}m_\ell$ (zero if there are no $A$ leaves) and $P\ge1$ is the complete explicit certificate bit length including every leaf, every SCC edge record, branching nodes and source pointer, then
\[
                       R_{\rm DT}(F)\le cP(2^{3M}+1)
\tag{3}
\]
for an absolute grammar-encoding constant $c$. This is a \emph{proof-complexity inequality}, not a running-time claim about $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At an $A$-SOURCE leaf, \UNSAT\ of the actual residual original-source subset $G_\ell$ means that every assignment of its $v_\ell$ active original variables falsifies at least one surviving, authenticated original clause. Replace the leaf by the full decision tree over those variables. It has at most $2^{v_\ell}$ conflict leaves, needs no guessed guest truth bit, and makes no new aliases. Every source-falsifying leaf persists when grafted under the ancestor assignment. For a source-EMPTY leaf use one terminal.

For an SCC leaf, first branch on the named variable $x$ in the two directions. Starting with either assignment, follow the advertised contradiction path whose initial literal is TRUE. An implication edge $a\to b$ is the residual original clause $(\neg a\vee b)$, or the unit $(b)$ when $a=\neg b$. If $b$ is not already fixed, branch on it: the branch with $b$ FALSE immediately falsifies that original clause, and the branch with $b$ TRUE continues to the next edge. Already-fixed literals yield an immediate conflict or require no branch. The final implication forces $\neg x$ against the initial $x$. This produces at most $e_\ell+2$ direct source-conflict leaves, irrespective of unrelated surviving ternaries. The two paths are checked with the \emph{same} original names and accumulated assignment. Graft these expansions at all terminals and count leaves to obtain the first inequality. The second follows from (1). Since an explicit unshared proof stores at least a positive number of bits for each leaf and each SCC edge, both the leaf count and total $\sum e_\ell$ are $O(P)$, proving (3).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Soundness and unrestricted completeness.} For any satisfiable source, follow its satisfying assignment through all fully covered branches. An A-SOURCE descendant is then SAT, so exact $A$ cannot accept it; an EMPTY original clause cannot be falsified; and valid source SCC paths cannot exist. Thus \emph{every} alleged UNSAT certificate on SAT is rejected. For every UNSAT source, exhaustive branching over all original variables and source-EMPTY exits gives an accepted certificate, possibly of exponential length. The existence of an affordable uniformly capped certificate for every UNSAT source is not deduced.

\section{PCR hardness transport and an unconditional restricted no-polynomial-cap result}
The original theorem of Conneryd, de Rezende, Nordstr\"om, Pang and Risse (FOCS 2023; expanded ECCC TR25-032 in 2025) supplies connected bounded-degree graphs $G_n$ whose standard 3-colourability CNFs require exponential-size polynomial-calculus/PCR refutations over every fixed field, including $\mathbb F_2$. This is a published \emph{proof-system size lower bound}, not a SAT-machine running-time lower bound. To obtain proper ternary source clauses, replace each binary source clause $(a\vee b)$ by $(a\vee b\vee z)$ and $(a\vee b\vee\neg z)$ for one new private $z$. A uniform $z=0$ restriction projects every lifted polynomial clause axiom to its original binary axiom or zero, so a PCR proof of the lifted proper CNF would restrict to a no-larger PCR proof of the original. Properization preserves SAT equivalence, is connected, and for 6-regular graph families has $\Theta(n)$ variables and clauses, with physical conventional indexed encoding length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$. This transfer and its own finite controls were separately formalized in LSPV-3 PC-BRANCH-COLOUR134; we inherit and do not silently strengthen it to signed exact-$(2,2)$.

A direct original-clause decision refutation with $R$ leaves yields a \emph{polynomial-size PCR refutation}: for each terminal path assignment $\alpha$, its indicator $\delta_\alpha$ is a literal monomial; a falsified source-clause polynomial divides it, so multiplying the source clause axiom by the remaining literals derives $\delta_\alpha=0$ using at most the path length in literal multiplications. At each internal split, use $x+\bar x=1$ to derive the parent indicator from the two child indicators. This gives $O(\operatorname{poly}(n)R)$ PCR lines and physically polynomially many bit symbols, uniformly over $\mathbb F_2$. The published PCR exponential size lower bound therefore yields some fixed $\gamma>0$ and an infinite connected proper signed source family satisfying
\[
                R_{\rm DT}(F_n)\ge2^{\gamma n-O(\log n)}.
\tag{4}
\]
The constant is existential: no numerical $\gamma$ is claimed from finite experiments.

\begin{corollary}[No universal small-guest polynomial certificate in this grammar]\label{cor:hard}
For the connected $F_n$ above, every accepted explicit unshared BRANCH / source-EMPTY / source-SCC / source-subformula-$A$ certificate with actual ordinary child lengths $m_\ell\le M$ has
\[
\boxed{\quad
P\ge \frac{2^{\gamma n-O(\log n)}}{c(2^{3M}+1)}.
\quad}
\tag{5}
\]
In particular, for every fixed $\lambda<1$, $M\le N^\lambda$ implies
\[
                 P=2^{\Omega(n)}=2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}.
\tag{6}
\]
No uniform polynomial-size all-UNSAT certificate cap, and hence no $N^{1.8018217497}$ time cap for guessing/verifying explicit certificates of this restricted grammar, follows from oracle access to even a hypothetical fast exact $A$ on child lengths $\le N^{1/2}$. This falsifies a restricted coverage hypothesis, not the desired unrestricted coNTIME or ARC proof.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Combine (3) and (4). Since $N=\Theta(n\log n)$, for each $\lambda<1$, $N^\lambda=o(n)$. Divide by $2^{3M}+1=2^{o(n)}$ and absorb polynomial factors. Every explicit bit of the certificate, including failed-branch closures and source IDs, must be guessed/read/checked; the exponential lower bound on such bits defeats every polynomial full-clock bound under this grammar. Nothing in this proof assumes that $A$ runs efficiently or generates its own PCR proof.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact charge ledger, escaped cases, and proof-system firewalls}
Let $V=\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal A}m_\ell$. For a fixed exact $A$ with $T_A(m)\le m^{d+o(1)}$ and $d\ge1$, the familiar \emph{upper} guest-time accounting is
\[
               \sum_{\ell\in\mathcal A}m_\ell^d\le M^{d-1}V,
\qquad
c\le\max\{r,u+b+\lambda(d-1)\}
\tag{7}
\]
\emph{conditionally} if the physical access/materialization and proof-verification budgets truly obey $M\le N^\lambda,V\le N^b$, local verifier exponent $r$, and per-probe overhead $u$. The inequality itself is elementary since $m_\ell^{d-1}\le M^{d-1}$. It says nothing about the existence of low-cost certificates. At $d=901/500,\lambda=1/2,u=0$, the earlier target $b<1.4008217497$ and $r<1.8018217497$ remains conditional on an independently verified published two-algorithm theorem and a jointly realized near-boundary seed. On the present hard family, (6) invalidates the required polynomial $P$/verification bound \emph{for the declared restricted grammar}. Uncharged selection of a helpful source subset is not allowed; deterministic ARC also pays every attempted/discarded policy, original input read, child header, source pointer, virtual probe, and simultaneous ancestor/guest workspace.

The corollary cannot be imported into certificates using arbitrary derived/equisatisfiable children with private existential witnesses or a merely semantic, unproved source-to-child lift: an exact child can be much smaller than the explicit residual original-source subset. Nor does it cover a supplied cutting-planes proof, DRAT, shared proof DAG, arbitrary-characteristic algebraic combination, or an $A$ call directly on the parent. General PCR terminals were handled in 134 under their own explicit simulation and degree rules; no claim that an arbitrary $A$ verdict has short PCR proof is made here. Fleming et al., \emph{On the Power and Limitations of Branch and Cut} (CCC 2021), establish quasipolynomial-size cutting-planes refutations for CNF encodings of inconsistent finite-field linear systems; CP is therefore a genuine separate escape family, not a free extension of this PCR/branch argument. Circuit witness-verifier gate counts and Role-1's canonical conflict-selector wiring do not authenticate the universal SAT/UNSAT projection.

Temporal state flips, total bit-time, hardware switch/gate count, exact serialized program, and physical peak indexed/writable workspace remain separate measurements. Lower bounds (2)--(6) concern \emph{complete explicit certificate bit length}, not the instantaneous amount of working RAM of a streaming verifier. The latter may stay logarithmic on easy instances even when certificates are long. An exponent lower bound on certificate length is not a deterministic SAT decider time--space lower bound.
