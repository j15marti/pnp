\begin{abstract}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, restricted syntactic source grammar; independent formal review pending.}
A canonical connected cubic prism parity 3CNF family admits a deterministic source-validated one-pass decision transducer with $O(N)$ bit-time, $O(\log N)$ extra writable bits and a constant-length \emph{ordinary SAT} child preserving both satisfiable and unsatisfiable labels. It is an explicit positive example of a physically short source-authenticated child, not an all-input arbitrary-algorithm ARC compiler. Arbitrarily renamed parity sources require a more expensive recognizer in the implemented portfolio; all unrecognized formulas fall back to exact UP-first DPLL. The whole controller has no polynomial all-input guarantee. The original clause-indexed $S/U$ extremes, complement flips and pair compatibility remain adversarial controls; $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textbf{OPEN}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Source grammar and exact theorem}
Fix $k\ge3$, $q=2k$. The connected prism graph $G_k$ has top and bottom $k$-cycles joined by $k$ rungs, with $q$ vertices and $n=3k$ edges. Number the cycle-edge variables top $1,\ldots,k$, bottom $k+1,\ldots,2k$, and rung $2k+1,\ldots,3k$. At vertex $v$, the three incident edge labels are a publicly computable canonical triple $e_{v,1},e_{v,2},e_{v,3}$ in increasing order. Let $b\in\{0,1\}^q$ be \emph{source-encoded}, never supplied by an oracle.

Each vertex contributes exactly the four proper 3-clauses $C_{v,a}$ for $a\in\{0,1\}^3$ with $\bigoplus_i a_i\ne b_v$, where the sign of $e_{v,i}$ is positive if $a_i=0$ and negative if $a_i=1$. Consequently $C_{v,a}$ is false on exactly the local assignment $a$. Clauses are stored in $q$ consecutive four-clause blocks with canonical supports, within-block order unrestricted. The complete physical source is $F_{k,b}$, length $N=\Theta(q\log q)$ with binary variable IDs and normal framing, comprising $4q$ proper original clauses. The recognizer checks every original clause and every four-pattern block before computing a syndrome.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted one-pass short-child compilation]
For every $k\ge3$ and every charge vector $b\in\{0,1\}^{2k}$, a single uniform deterministic source-reading transducer, independent of any guest SAT decider, (i) recognizes exactly the stated canonical grammar, (ii) decides $\SAT(F_{k,b})$ if and only if $\bigoplus_v b_v=0$, and (iii) emits a constant-length ordinary CNF child $H(F)$ with $\SAT(H(F))\Longleftrightarrow\SAT(F)$. For a canonical source, $H(F)$ is the one-unit-clause formula $(z)$ for even charge or the contradictory pair $(z)\wedge(\neg z)$ for odd charge. In the declared bit-encoded read-only source/input-and-sequential-output TM realization, it reads $O(N)$ input bits, takes $O(N)$ bit-time and retains $O(\log N)$ writable bits; its output has $O(1)$ bits. The theorem applies \emph{only after exact recognition}, not to arbitrary 3CNF.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each original three-variable block, its four forbidden local patterns are exactly one parity class; checking all four distinct masks, common parity, and the predetermined variable triple proves the conjunction of its original clauses is precisely $\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v$. Summing these $q$ equations over $\mathbb F_2$ cancels each edge variable twice, so consistency requires $\bigoplus_v b_v=0$. Conversely, because $G_k$ is connected, fix a spanning tree, freely assign non-tree edges, then solve each tree edge from leaf to root. The final root equation holds exactly when the total charge is even, proving sufficiency and the same-source SAT/UNSAT equivalence. The output formulas have the indicated decision labels, yielding an exact ordinary-SAT child for both.

For resources, maintain block index $v$, computed three edge identifiers, four 3-bit falsifying masks, an 8-bit seen mask, the common-parity flag, syndrome, framing counters and parser state. All counters and IDs take $O(\log N)$ bits. The expected three IDs are computed by addition and reduction modulo $k$ on $O(\log q)$-bit words. Each original literal is parsed and compared once; these operations cost $O(\log q)$ bit-time per encoded block, included in $O(N)$ since $N=\Theta(q\log q)$. No original source tape or adjacency list is copied. The output is a fixed-length literal string. Malformed input is rejected as \emph{nonmember}, not called UNSAT; the all-input portfolio uses its sound complete fallback.\qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Source-authenticated certificates.} On the odd-charge source the four-clause truth-table checks at each vertex validate the XOR equations; XOR summation gives the explicit contradiction $0=1$. On even charge, a spanning-tree assignment constructs a full original-source SAT witness. The emitted ordinary child itself carries only the \emph{decision}, not a complete standard DRAT/FRAT proof of the original source; certificate validation and witness extraction must be billed separately if demanded by another controller. This distinction is essential to the ARC model.

\section{Hostile controls and complete work accounting}
The implemented Python controller attempts canonical recognition, grouped-affine incidence recognition (with $\Theta(N)$ auxiliary storage), Krom SCC, Horn least-model closure, and exact UP-first DPLL fallback, in that order. Every failed attempt incurs literal scans and a policy call; all recognized outputs are checked independently against complete original-source assignments. The generic detector handles signed variable permutations but stores source group maps and graph adjacency, unlike the theoretical streaming transducer. Python itself loads all original CNF clauses in memory: \emph{none of the reported Python memory usage is a measurement of the claimed native $O(\log N)$ streaming implementation}. Signed-renaming invariance is bought by a different, higher-space acquisition policy.

\section{Transfer, barrier, and next arrow}
Backdoor methods, SAT portfolios and solver switching are established topics \cite{DOS24,Xu08}. FLMV excludes an all-input exact deterministic SAT decider under its simultaneous time-space bounds, not an efficient decider of a syntactically restricted promise-free subset with exhaustive membership validation \cite{FLMV05}. The same-machine correctness of the canonical transducer does not make the entire fallback controller cheap. The restricted certificate cannot be upgraded to an arbitrary-$A$ contractor through padding, a DELAY/COPY/RECOMP guest wrapper, a hidden source oracle, an uncharged variable-renaming canonicalizer or a free semantic solver. On an unrecognized source the complete DPLL branch can take exponential time and linear-or-larger live memory. The earlier AFFINE-SMALLBIAS-DESCENT-GATE2 remains a separate restricted affine-MCSP no-go, not SAT hardness.

\textbf{OPEN weakest link:} find an all-input *uniformly discoverable* source-authenticated action producing at most $N^{3/5+o(1)}$ ordinary $N^{1/2+o(1)}$-bit children with exact both-label coverage and local $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ work, $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ writable space, while paying discovery, invalid policies, source/tape/guest calls, child serialization, retained ancestors and proof checking. The present theorem supplies only an explicitly verified tractable family. $P=NP$ and $P\ne NP$ remain \textbf{OPEN}.
