## Exact generic interface
Let Z be the **explicit** collection of shared assignments z and F(z,x_1,...,x_B)=AND_i F_i(z,x_i), with disjoint private variable sets. Put C_i={z in Z: F_i(z,.) is UNSAT}. Then F is UNSAT over Z iff Z=UNION_i C_i. A sound reusable negative certificate for block i must verify its original-source binding and EVERY z claimed in C_i (or a symbolic theorem implying all of them). A SAT witness needs ONE common z and a private satisfying assignment for EVERY block. An incomplete deterministic negative specialist may output UNSAT(cert) or UNKNOWN; UNKNOWN never establishes SAT.

## Symbolic four-basis theorem
For a path P of L arcs, r backward with respect to terminal traversal, the attainable endpoint differences in the tournament R(a,b) iff (b-a) mod 5 in {1,3} are
S(P)=L+r+{0,2,...,2L} modulo 5.
If L>=4, S(P)=Z5. If L<=3, |S(P)|=L+1; a length-three path excludes exactly r+1 in {1,2,3,4}, never zero.

**Theorem (PROVED/EXACT).** Every multiset of paths between common terminals u,v, internally vertex-disjoint, has a submultiset of at most FOUR whose endpoint-support intersection is identical to the full intersection, whether empty or nonempty. Four is sharp.

**Proof.** Choose an inclusion-minimal family of k support sets with intersection I. For each essential S_i choose d_i in the intersection of all other chosen sets but not S_i. These witness differences are distinct, all outside I. If I is nonempty, k<=5-|I|<=4. If I is empty, k<=5. Were k=5, the five d_i would exhaust Z5, forcing each S_i to contain four distinct d_j and exclude just d_i. Such a size-four support is necessarily a three-edge path support, but no three-edge path excludes difference zero. Contradiction; k<=4. QED.

**Constructive algorithm.** Scan the original block-delimited source path list. For each path compute its five-bit support mask, append to a current basis, and greedily delete any support redundant for its intersection. The basis is inclusion-minimal and by the theorem has size <=4 after every update. The mask and at most five source-path identifiers fit O(log N) writable bits on a PROMISED, correctly segmented and internally disjoint path-bundle encoding; each supplied orientation word and path reference is parsed and charged. For unrestricted source bytes, recognizing and verifying disjointness, path membership, and complete edge coverage requires a separate source-validating algorithm with its OWN time/space ledger. The finite H9 verifier checks its pinned original arc list directly. No all-input O(log N)-space grammar-recognition claim is made. For a negative result, output the <=4 original-source references and their recomputed masks with empty intersection. For SAT, select an allowed endpoint difference, reconstruct each original private path coloring and output/verify all vertex colors. An unordered graph may require extra discovery and memory; this is NOT an arbitrary-CNF algorithm.
