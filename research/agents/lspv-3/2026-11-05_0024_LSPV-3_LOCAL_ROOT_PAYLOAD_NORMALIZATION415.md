The identity f=f0 XOR [x1 AND (f0 XOR f1)] takes five arbitrary binary gates: f0, f1, XOR, AND, XOR. Unary or two-input functions take one appropriate binary gate, and constant circuit conditions require the declared constant syntax. Apply separately to every LOCAL payload but preserve its asserted ROOT and output type. Topological induction: original RES clauses unchanged; AND3 exact local truth tables and GF(2) equation annotations unchanged; XOR operands keep the exact equations; LOCAL parent and conclusion truth functions and implication unchanged. No other node references private gates. Each output clause has at most n signed literals, each row an n-bit mask and each proof reference O(log q) bits. The bound follows.
