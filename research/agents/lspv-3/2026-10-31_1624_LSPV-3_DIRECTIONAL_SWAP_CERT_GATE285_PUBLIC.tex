\section{Exact theorem}
For complete assignment $a$, let $U_+(a)$ and $U_-(a)$ denote respectively its falsified original positive and negative clauses. Let $\rho_\pm(a)$ denote the minimum number of original variables hitting all those clauses. Because each variable occurs twice per polarity, the known edge-cover matching formula gives $\rho_\pm=|U_\pm|-\nu(G_\pm)$ on the respective false-clause graphs. All names in $U_+$ are zero; all names in $U_-$ are one at $a$.
\begin{theorem}[Directional source model-distance and consistent exchange heuristic]
For every valid canonical M22 source $F$, complete assignment $a$, and satisfying assignment $b$, with $\Delta=|b|-|a|$,
\[
d_H(a,b)\ge\rho_+(a)+\rho_-(a)+
\left|\Delta-\left(\rho_+(a)-\rho_-(a)\right)\right|.
\]
In a fixed-weight slice, $h(a)=\max\{\rho_+(a),\rho_-(a)\}$ is an admissible and consistent unit-cost A* heuristic on single 0/1 exchanges: for any one-swap neighbor $a'$,
\[
|\rho_\pm(a)-\rho_\pm(a')|\le1,\qquad |h(a)-h(a')|\le1.
\]
Exhausting a slice is not a global UNSAT proof.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $u$ be zero-to-one changes from $a$ to a model $b$ and $v$ one-to-zero changes. Every originally false positive clause requires one zero-to-one change; negative clauses require one one-to-zero change. Hence $u\ge p=\rho_+$ and $v\ge q=\rho_-$. Write $u=p+s$, $v=q+t$, with $s,t\ge0$ and $s-t=\Delta-(p-q)$. Thus $d_H=u+v=p+q+s+t\ge p+q+|\Delta-(p-q)|$. At fixed weight $u=v$, so at least $\max(p,q)$ swaps are necessary. In a swap, the 0-to-1 operation can only delete originally false positive clauses incident to the bit, reducing their cover by at most one; the 1-to-0 operation can only create newly false positive clauses incident to its bit, increasing cover by at most one. For negative clauses reverse the roles. Thus each of $\rho_+$ and $\rho_-$ changes by at most one, so does their maximum. Admissibility and consistency follow.
\end{proof}
