\section*{Published starting point and model}
Haken's theorem states that there exist absolute constants \(c>0,h_0\) such that for every \(h\ge h_0\), every ordinary \emph{unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution} refutation of standard unary \(\mathrm{PHP}(h+1,h)\) has at least \(2^{ch}\) inference lines. The source has \(h(h+1)\) variables, \((h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}\) clauses, and \(\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))\) bits under explicit signed binary-index clause encoding. This is a proof-\emph{system} lower bound, not a bound on all uniform SAT deciders. Cook's polynomial Extended Resolution constructions show why it cannot be inherited by a system containing conservative extensions. The preselected \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) has free authenticated original sources and unit-paid RES, AND3, binary GF(2) XOR, justified typed LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative two-input DEF, with each paid DAG node of at most two source/parent dependencies. We do not alter its rules or weights.

Write \(E_{\land2}\) for the \emph{fixed strict subcalculus} of \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) with free source clauses, unit-paid signed binary Resolution and unit-paid conservative signed two-input AND definitions \(z\leftrightarrow(\ell_1\land\ell_2)\). One paid definition releases exactly its three defining CNF clauses, namely \((\neg z\vee\ell_1),(\neg z\vee\ell_2),(z\vee\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2)\). Gate inputs may refer to any earlier gate or original variable, including the fresh guard. All DAG sharing counts once. Let \(D_E(P)\) be the least distinct paid-node cost of an \(E_{\land2}\) refutation of \(P\), which is attained because costs are nonnegative integers and Resolution gives completeness.

\section*{Strengthening of the previously certified guarded-unit theorem}
For any finite UNSAT CNF \(F=\bigwedge_i C_i\) and fresh \(t\), put
\[
G_t(F)=\bigwedge_i(\neg t\vee C_i),\qquad H_t(F)=G_t(F)\wedge(t).
\]
Prior LSPV-2/381 proved \(D_E(F\vdash\bot)=D_E(G_t(F)\vdash\neg t)\) even with guard-dependent paid AND2 definitions, by literal/constant substitution and topological cofactor normalization. The result below is a \emph{strict additional cost statement} about a complete refutation of \(H_t(F)\), not a reannouncement of that prior theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Exact authenticated guard-unit surcharge]\label{thm:one}
For every finite UNSAT signed CNF \(F\), including a source empty clause,
\[
\boxed{D_E(H_t(F)\vdash\bot)=D_E(F\vdash\bot)+1.}
\]
All original sources, definitions and shared proof-DAG steps must be authenticated as above. The quantifier is over \emph{every} legal proof DAG of the fixed strict \(E_{\land2}\), including arbitrary guard-dependent conservative definitions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the upper bound, take any optimal proof of \(F\). Use the old LSPV-2/381 lift to obtain a proof of \(\neg t\) from \(G_t(F)\) with the same number \(q=D_E(F)\) of paid nodes, retaining original \(t\)-free conservative definitions. Resolve its root \(\neg t\) with the additional source unit \(t\) in one paid step. Thus \(D_E(H_t(F))\le q+1\).

For the reverse bound, take any \(E_{\land2}\) refutation \(\pi\) of \(H_t(F)\), of cost \(r\). The source unit \(t\) must belong to the contradiction's source ancestry. Otherwise, the used sources are all drawn from \(G_t(F)\), satisfiable by \(t=0\), and every finite conservative gate sequence extends any such assignment; soundness forbids a refutation. In this strict calculus, a source clause can be consumed only by a signed Resolution inference, not by a DEF operation. Hence there is a paid Resolution node \(e\) using the actual unit source \(t\), necessarily on pivot \(t\), with an opposing parent of form \(\neg t\vee B\). Under the restriction \(t=1\), \(e\)'s resolvent \(B\) is exactly the cofactor of the opposing parent; the paid inference \(e\) is therefore redundant.

Apply the existing LSPV-2/381 topological guard-cofactor construction to the entire shared DAG: simplify guard-dependent signed AND2 definitions to constants, aliases or surviving conservative gates, and substitute signed literals/constants in every Resolution line, retaining a subclause when necessary. All original guarded sources become the corresponding sources of \(F\); the unit \(t\) becomes true. A Resolution step maps to at most one strict Resolution step, and a DEF step to at most one surviving paid DEF. In particular \(e\) maps to no paid step, because its other parent already supplies the needed cofactor or a stronger subclause. The final empty clause stays empty. Therefore \(D_E(F)\le r-1\). Take \(r=D_E(H_t(F))\) to obtain the reverse inequality and equality.
\end{proof}

\noindent The proof does not assert a global identity for full \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\): its AND3, GF(2), and LOCAL-CONVERT rule transport has not been authenticated in this generality. In particular, a DEF gate mentioning \(t\) need not obey naive string-level guard deletion, although its cofactor normalization above is sound.

\section*{A separate full-mixed exact family}
For each \(h\ge1\) let
\[
F_h=(x_1)\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{h}(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})\wedge(\neg x_{h+1}).
\]
It has \(m=h+2\) clauses and \(h+1\) original variables. Every source is indispensable: removing the first unit permits all zeros, removing the final unit permits all ones, and removing link \(i\) permits the step assignment with \(x_1,\ldots,x_i=1\) and \(x_{i+1},\ldots,x_{h+1}=0\). The familiar at-most-binary-premise source-ancestry lemma gives \(W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_h)\ge m-1\). A strict \(h+1\)-Resolution proof matches it.

All \(m+1\) sources of \(H_t(F_h)\) are indispensable: deletion of the added \(t\) unit has model \(t=0\), while deletion of a guarded source has a model with \(t=1\) using the corresponding step witness. The identical global source-ancestry lemma gives \(W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_t(F_h))\ge m\); guarding the original Resolution chain and resolving \(\neg t\) with \(t\) yields \(m\) paid nodes. Thus, for every \(h\ge1\), including all adaptive mixed-rule proofs,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_h)=h+1,\qquad
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_t(F_h))=h+2.}
\]
This is a linear calibration, not a proposed hard family. The uniform explicit signed CNF length and explicit proof length are both \(\Theta(h\log(h+2))\) bits.

\section*{Falsifiers, verification, and resource separation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{F1.} A guard-dependent conservative \(z=t\land x\) definition avoids the extra paid node in strict \(E_{\land2}\): refuted by the mandatory genuine use of the \(t\) source and the cofactor elimination of that use.
\item[] \textbf{F2.} Shared DAGs, temporary enlargement, optimal reordering or free choice lower the full-mixed chain below \(h+2\): refuted by indispensable source count and the two-premise ancestry bound.
\item[] \textbf{F3.} The same theorem establishes a superpolynomial arbitrary-machine SAT-time lower bound: false. Both signed Horn families are uniformly decidable in polynomial time and have linear paid proofs.
\item[] \textbf{F4.} The statement extends unchanged to mixed AND3, GF(2) and LOCAL-CONVERT: OPEN; the strict fragment result is not a complete-rule guard simulation.
\end{enumerate}
The deterministic JavaScript producer reconstructs and checks strict source-labelled Resolution DAGs for \(h=2,3,4,6,8,12,24,64\), with 1,291 checks, every source deletion and original signed-literal-flip SAT neighbor, guard sign flips, guard-off controls, 16 gate-cofactor valuations and three corrupt-proof rejections. A separately implemented exhaustive small-input verifier for \(1\le h\le7\) checked UNSAT, all deletion-SAT neighbors and reconstructed proof costs, recording 133 checks and one corrupt-pivot rejection. These tests \emph{do not} independently certify a minimum over arbitrary mixed proofs; the all-size global minimum follows from the proof above.

Logical paid cost \(D_E\) excludes discovery/optimization. With explicit input length \(N\) and serialized proof length \(L\), the inherited cofactor rewriter is a uniform polynomial-bit-time algorithm, conservatively \(O((N+L)^2)\) with \(O((N+L)^2)\) writable-bit peak-space upper bound. This charges decoding, source access, topological gate reads, alias mapping, clause rewrites, output and independent proof checking. Any exponentially long expanded certificate remains expensive in this representation; compact/lazy alternatives require their own validation and scheduling. The chain proof family admits a uniform polynomial-time constructor and polynomial encoded verification; no inference from paid nodes to unrestricted time or FLMV peak space is made.
