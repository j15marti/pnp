\section{A globally sound extension theorem}
A refutation verifier is a polynomial-time predicate $V(F,\pi)$
where $F$ is a proper-3CNF source, with \emph{soundness}
$V(F,\pi)=1\Rightarrow F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}$ and
\emph{completeness}
$F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\Rightarrow \exists\pi\,V(F,\pi)=1$.
The proof length in this relational convention counts $|\pi|$;
in the Cook--Reckhow functional convention the proof string also
encodes $F$, adding $\Theta(N)$ bits.

\begin{theorem}[Sound extension lemma; PROVED/EXACT]
Let $V_0$ be any polynomial-time sound complete UNSAT refutation
verifier, and let $S\subseteq\mathrm{UNSAT}$ be any polynomial-time
recognizable language of valid proper-3CNF sources. Define
\[
V_S(F,\pi)=
\begin{cases}
V_0(F,\rho),&\pi=0\rho,\\
[F\in S],&\pi=1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Then $V_S$ is sound, complete and polynomial-time verifiable for
\emph{all} proper-3CNF inputs. Every $F\in S$ has a one-bit
relational proof. Under the functional convention there is a proof
of length $N+O(\log N)$ for $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each accepted zero-tag proof is sound by $V_0$. An accepted
one-tag proof belongs to $S\subseteq\mathrm{UNSAT}$, hence is sound.
Every UNSAT formula has a $V_0$ proof, which is accepted with
tag zero. Both branches are polynomial in their entire explicit
input lengths; they read and validate $F$ and the supplied
certificate. For $F\in S$, the single tag is enough in the
relational convention. Encoding $(F,1)$ gives the functional
length claim.
\end{proof}

\section{Concrete cubic-Tseitin extension}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a connected simple cubic graph with $g=|V|$,
$e=3g/2$ edges. Name Boolean edge variables $x_e$. At each vertex,
require $\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v$, encoded by its four proper
ternary clauses rejecting the four wrong local assignments.
Denote the resulting formula $T(G,b)$.

Define $S_{\oplus}$ as the language of \emph{canonically grouped}
proper-3CNF records that pass all of these polynomial-time
source checks: there are four clauses in each vertex block;
all four use the same three distinct variable IDs and exactly
exclude the wrong-parity tuples for one recovered $b_v$;
every variable appears in exactly two distinct blocks, defining
a simple connected cubic graph; and
$\bigoplus_v b_v=1$. Reject malformed sources and
failed recognition rather than inferring a promise.

\begin{lemma}[Source recognition and soundness; PROVED/EXACT]
Membership in $S_{\oplus}$ is polynomial-time decidable and
$S_{\oplus}\subseteq\mathrm{UNSAT}$. With $W=\Theta(\log(N+2))$,
deterministic source validation takes $O(N^2)$ word operations
and $O(N)$ retained words using a deliberately conservative
comparison-based implementation.
For a canonical source $n=3g/2$, $m=4g$ and
$N=(2+16g)W$ bits under the stated two-header/four-word record encoding.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every source local block is checked against the eight possible
three-bit assignments; the recovered graph uses the same
edge variable in exactly two vertex equations. Summing those
equations over $\mathbb F_2$ cancels each edge twice and gives
$0=\bigoplus_v b_v=1$, a contradiction. All checks are finite
source-grammar, connectivity and local truth-table operations,
requiring at most polynomial source access and storage.
\end{proof}

Take $V_0$ to be the standard resolution refutation verifier:
axioms must be source clauses, each derived clause must be the
valid nontautological resolvent of two earlier clauses on a
declared pivot, and the last clause must be empty. Resolution
is sound and refutation-complete for arbitrary UNSAT CNF.
The sound extension lemma gives $V_{\oplus}$, a single fixed
globally sound complete proof system with a one-bit proof
on every $F\in S_{\oplus}$.

\begin{theorem}[No polynomial transport to regular resolution;
PROVED/EXACT given the cited known theorem]
There is no polynomial-time translation taking every
$V_{\oplus}$ refutation of every ordinary proper-3CNF source
$F$ to a \emph{regular-resolution} refutation of the same $F$,
with the translator's total time, source reads and output
length bounded polynomially in $N+|\pi|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are connected bounded-degree cubic graph families
$G_g$ with $\operatorname{tw}(G_g)=\Omega(g)$.
Choose odd vertex charge. Then $T(G_g,b)\in S_{\oplus}$,
so the one-bit certificate $\pi=1$ is accepted by
$V_{\oplus}$ after charged polynomial source checking.
Itsykson--Riazanov--Smirnov (SAT 2022) prove that regular
resolution for Tseitin contradictions on connected graphs
requires $2^{\Omega(\operatorname{tw}(G))}$ lines.
Here this is $2^{\Omega(g)}$, while
$N=\Theta(g\log g)$. Writing the required proof already
takes exponentially more than $\operatorname{poly}(N+1)$
operations. Therefore the translator cannot exist.
\end{proof}

This does \emph{not} contradict the regular-resolution theorem.
The additional axiom recognizer is a different,
sound and globally complete proof system. The construction
is not a proof that arbitrary SAT algorithms must output
$V_{\oplus}$ proofs.

\section{Arbitrary deciders and the Cook--Reckhow boundary}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional algorithm-dependent verifier]
If a fixed uniform total deterministic exact proper-3SAT
decider $A$ runs in polynomial worst-case time, then the verifier
\[
V_A(F,\pi)=[\pi=1]\wedge[A(F)=\mathrm{UNSAT}]
\]
is polynomial-time, sound, complete and has one-bit relational
refutations for \emph{every} UNSAT formula.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The verifier reads and runs $A$ on the entire source, so
soundness and completeness follow from $A$'s global exactness.
Its verifier time includes $T_A(F)$ and source reads.
The submitted certificate tag is one bit, not an uncharged
algorithm transcript.
\end{proof}

This is CONDITIONAL on a polynomial-time exact decider,
not a constructed such decider. Cook--Reckhow's known
characterization says the existence of \emph{some}
polynomially bounded propositional proof system is equivalent
to $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$, not known equivalent to
$P=NP$. A bound against every sound complete polynomial
proof system would therefore confront the general
NP-versus-coNP proof-system problem, not transport a
restricted Tseitin theorem for free.
Regular-resolution/DNNF lower bounds do not constrain
arbitrary NAND2 time-bounded decision circuits (TIMEOUT66)
or $V_{\oplus}$.
