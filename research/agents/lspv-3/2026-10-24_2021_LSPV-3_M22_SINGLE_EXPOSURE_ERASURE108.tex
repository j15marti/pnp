\section{Canonical target, previous work and three falsifiable tests}
The valid source class $\CM$ has unique proper three-name clauses, each entirely positive or entirely negative, and every occurring source name appears exactly twice in each sign. Clausewise monotone does not mean all-positive. $\overline C$ reverses literal signs while keeping ordinary OR. $\CS=\CM\setminus\{F:(\forall C\in F)\overline C\in F\}$ excludes \emph{fully} complementary-paired formulas alone; partial pairs, disconnected easy sources and nonminimal sources remain. Malformed inputs are rejected. The decision language $L_{\star}=\{\operatorname{enc}(F):F\in\CS,\ \SAT(F)\}$ is NP-complete by D\"ocker's source theorem together with a polynomial paired-to-fixed-SAT-$Y$ adapter; this does \emph{not} entail deterministic runtime lower bounds \cite{docker}.

Let $V$ be the $k$ active original names, $P$ and $Q$ the sets of unsigned supports of positive and negative clauses, $H=P\cap Q$, $P'=P\setminus H$, $Q'=Q\setminus H$, $q=|P'|=|Q'|$, $D=\bigcup P'=\bigcup Q'$ and $Z=V\setminus D$. The paired supports each enforce $\mathrm{NAE}_3$, not ordinary Boolean negation of a clause. The signed degree quotas give $3m=4k$, $m_+=m_-=2k/3$ and indexed source size $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$.

Three tests were proposed: (H1) delete the second guard in TWO-HALL-CUBE107 and retain a full paired-boundary cube under the sole guard $|S\cap D|=1$ for each $S\in H$; (H2) ask whether this weaker guard guarantees the \emph{whole} source is SAT; and (H3) test whether allowing $|S\cap D|=2$ preserves the full cube. H1 is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}. H2 is \textbf{FALSIFIED} without a separate unmatched-subformula SAT condition. H3 is \textbf{FALSIFIED} by a six-name source, although pinned membership remains polynomial via a paid 2-SAT computation.

\section{The exact one-exposed-core theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Single-exposure existential erasure; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Let $F\in\CM$, with the source sets above, and suppose
\[
(\forall S\in H)\qquad |S\cap D|=1. \tag{E1}
\]
For \emph{every} fixed boundary assignment $a:D\to\{0,1\}$ there is a polynomial-time, original-name-preserving extension $b:Z\to\{0,1\}$ satisfying all paired positive and negative clauses. Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{\quad \operatorname{proj}_{D}\operatorname{SAT}(H_{\rm paired})
             =\{0,1\}^{D}.\quad}
\]
Writing $G$ for the original \emph{unmatched} signed clauses on $D$,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{SAT}(F)\iff\operatorname{SAT}(G).}
\]
Whenever $G$ has a supplied satisfying $a$, the extension of $a$ to a satisfying original-name assignment for $F$ is polynomial-time constructible. No condition $\deg_H(d)=1$ for every $d\in D$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A variable $z\in Z$ occurs in no unmatched clause, so by the exact signed $(2,2)$ quota it occurs in precisely two distinct paired supports. Each paired $S$ consists of one exposed $d_S$ and two distinct internal names $z_S,z'_S$. Fix $a$. If $a(d_S)=0$, the negative paired clause is already true and the positive clause is $z_S\vee z'_S$; if $a(d_S)=1$, the positive paired clause is already true and the negative clause is $\neg z_S\vee\neg z'_S$. Thus one binary clause remains per paired support, and each internal name appears in precisely two remaining clauses.

Form the bipartite graph of paired-support vertices and internal-name vertices. Both sides have degree two. For any subset $J$ of support vertices, $2|J|$ incidence edges enter neighbors of degree at most two, so $|N(J)|\ge |J|$. Hall's theorem supplies an injective assignment $S\mapsto z(S)\in S\cap Z$. Set $z(S)=1-a(d_S)$ and set any unmatched internal names arbitrarily. As no $z(S)$ is reused, each reduced binary clause and therefore both original signed paired clauses are satisfied. This holds for every $a$. The unmatched clauses mention only $D$, proving exact equisatisfiability and certificate lifting. The matching and every operation of its construction are charged; no oracle supplies this extension.
\end{proof}

The incidence identities under (E1) are
\[
|H|=|Z|=\frac{2k}{3}-q,\qquad
|D|=\frac{k}{3}+q,\qquad
3q=\sum_{d\in D}(2-\deg_H(d)).
\]
The second guard of TWO-HALL-CUBE107 sets all $\deg_H(d)=1$, which additionally makes $G$ uniformly SAT by a \emph{separate} Hall matching on unmatched signed clauses. It was necessary for that stronger all-source-SAT conclusion, \emph{not} for full-cube projection or equisatisfiability reduction. If $H=\varnothing$, (E1) is vacuous and $G=F$: no shrink or easier decision follows.

\section{Paid membership when two exposed bits touch a paired clause}
\begin{proposition}[Source-pinned exact 2-SAT membership; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
If instead every paired $S$ has $|S\cap D|\ge1$, then for \emph{each supplied} $a:D\to\{0,1\}$ exact membership $a\in\operatorname{proj}_D\operatorname{SAT}(H_{\rm paired})$ is decidable by a source-constructible 2-SAT instance on $Z$ in polynomial bit work and polynomial writable/index space. This is \emph{not} a free boundary oracle and does not imply that the projected relation itself is always a cube.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $p_S=\sum_{d\in S\cap D}a(d)$ and $I_S=S\cap Z$; $|I_S|\le2$. The paired condition is $1\le p_S+\sum_{z\in I_S}z\le2$. If $p_S=0$, impose $\bigvee_{z\in I_S}z$; if $p_S+|I_S|=3$, impose $\bigvee_{z\in I_S}\neg z$; otherwise impose no clause. An empty imposed clause means UNSAT. Every nonempty imposed clause has at most two literals, so exact 2-SAT SCC decides the boundary assignment. Source parsing, quota validation, clause creation and SCC graph traffic must be charged.
\end{proof}

A connected six-name within-$\CS$ countercontrol has paired supports $\{abz,cdz\}$, unmatched positives $\{abe,cde\}$ and unmatched negatives $\{ace,bde\}$. Its five-name exposed projection has $28$ of $32$ words, excluding boundary $(a,b,c,d,e)$ words $00110,00111,11000,11001$. Eliminating the common original $z$ yields the \emph{compact mixed-sign} predicate
\[
(a\vee b\vee\neg c\vee\neg d)\ \wedge\
(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee c\vee d).
\]
This predicate lies outside $\CM$ as written. Its four omitted boundary words are not mandatory enumeration work; source-backed 2-SAT handles them.
