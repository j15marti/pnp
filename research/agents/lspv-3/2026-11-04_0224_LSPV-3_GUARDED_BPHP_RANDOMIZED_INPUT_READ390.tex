\section*{Published theorem and fixed calculus firewall}
For $n=2^\ell$ holes, the standard bit-PHP CNF $B_\ell$ has $M=n\binom{n+1}{2}$ clauses of width $2\ell$ over $(n+1)\ell$ variables. K. Braun, arXiv:2609.23015 (September 19, 2026), reports
\[
\forall\ell\ge32\ \forall\pi\in \mathrm{Res}(\oplus)\text{-DAGRef}(B_\ell):
|\pi|>\exp(n/(32768\ell^2)).
\]
The model is unrestricted DAG-like Resolution over disjunctions of affine $\mathbb F_2$ equations, with semantic weakening and affine pivot, \emph{without} conservative Extended Resolution gates. The preprint reports Lean 4 formalization but independent specialist review of the mathematics remains pending. The signed original has $\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ explicitly binary-indexed CNF bits. This restricted proof lower bound is not an arbitrary-machine SAT-time bound. The inherited $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ has zero-cost original sources and unit-cost, at-most-binary-premise RES, AND3, GF$(2)$ XOR, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and conservative two-input DEF nodes; its payload grammar remains partly unresolved. \section*{One fixed original source and explicit input encoding}
For each $i<j$ among $n+1$ pigeons and each hole $a\in\{0,1\}^\ell$, let $C_{i,j,a}$ be the $2\ell$-literal disjunction saying that one of pigeons $i,j$ is different from $a$. Introduce fresh guard $t$ and put
\[
H_\ell=\{\neg t\vee C_{i,j,a}:i<j,\ a\in\{0,1\}^\ell\}\cup\{t\}.
\]
This formula is UNSAT; removing the unit $t$ makes it SAT with $t=0$. Prior LSPV-3/389 proves all its source literals pivotal and $M\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_\ell)\le n^{O(1)}$ under the paid DEF source compiler. We adopt that family unchanged.
For physical bit observations only, define fixed self-delimiting CNF encoding $E$ with tag \texttt{1011}, Elias gamma integers $\gamma(V+1),\gamma(m+1)$ for declared variable count $V$ and clause count $m$, and for every $k$-literal clause $\gamma(k+1)$ followed by a polarity bit and a fixed-width $w=\lceil\log_2(V+1)\rceil$ variable ID for each literal. The parser rejects invalid tags, empty/truncated fields, out-of-range IDs, wrong clause count and trailing bits. This \emph{declared binary observation grammar} is not silently substituted for the repository's original DIMACS parser. On $H_\ell$, $V=(n+1)\ell+1$ and $m=M+1$.
The number of independent literal-polarity bit positions is
\[
K_\ell=M(2\ell+1)+1.
\]
Its complete bit length is
\[
\begin{split}
N_\ell={}&4+|\gamma(V+1)|+|\gamma(M+2)|\\
&+M\left(|\gamma(2\ell+2)|+(2\ell+1)(1+w)\right)
+|\gamma(2)|+1+w.
\end{split}
\]
Thus $K_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell)$ and $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$, hence $K_\ell=\Theta(N_\ell/\log N_\ell)$.
\begin{lemma}[All source sign bits are pivotal]
For every $\ell\ge1$, write $x_\ell=E(H_\ell)$. For each of the $K_\ell$ sign-bit coordinates $p$, the one-bit neighbor $x_\ell^{(p)}$ is a valid SAT CNF encoding of exactly the same length, whereas $x_\ell$ is UNSAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the collision clause $(i,j,a)$, assign pigeons $i,j$ to $a$, all other $n-1$ pigeons bijectively to the other $n-1$ holes, and $t=1$. Only the selected guarded clause is false, and every one of its $2\ell+1$ literals is false. Flipping any selected literal's sign makes the whole formula true under this same assignment. Flipping the final unit $t$ instead makes $\neg t$ an axiom; setting $t=0$ satisfies all guarded clauses and the new unit. The unmodified source is UNSAT since $n+1$ pigeons cannot inject into $n$ holes. Each polarity is one independent encoded bit, so length and grammatical validity are unchanged.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Universal bounded-error source acquisition]
Let $A$ be any fixed total uniform adaptive randomized SAT/UNSAT/malformed classifier, with error probability at most $\epsilon<1/2$ on every binary input. All original input-dependent bit accesses, including preprocessing, lazy circuits, GF$(2)$ processing, conservative auxiliaries and shared DAG work, are charged. If one paid input probe reveals at most $b$ original bits, then for every $\ell$
\[
\mathbb E[\#\text{ distinct sign positions read by }A(x_\ell)]
\ge (1-2\epsilon)K_\ell,\qquad
\mathbb E[T_{\mathrm{probe}}(x_\ell)]\ge(1-2\epsilon)K_\ell/b.
\]
For deterministic exact classifiers, every sign bit must be read. In the bit-probe model this gives $\Omega(N_\ell/\log N_\ell)$ input reads on this explicitly encoded UNSAT family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix sign bit $p$ and couple runs on $x_\ell,x_\ell^{(p)}$ using the identical private random tape. Until $p$ is read, both executions see identical answers and take identical steps. Their outputs can differ only on the event $E_p$ that $p$ was queried. Their probabilities of output UNSAT differ by at least $1-2\epsilon$ because their correct labels are opposite, and by at most $\Pr(E_p)$. Thus $\Pr(E_p)\ge1-2\epsilon$. Sum the indicators of $E_p$ across all $K_\ell$ pivotal coordinates; one paid probe can expose at most $b$ coordinates. All adaptivity and intermediate representations are included in the coupled execution, but no hidden full-input hash or semantic answer oracle is licensed.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Same-certificate verifier extension]
Let $V(x,\pi;r)$ be a sound randomized UNSAT-certificate verifier with soundness error at most $\epsilon$ on \emph{every SAT input and every claimed certificate}. For any fixed certificate $\pi_\ell$ that it accepts on $x_\ell$ with probability at least $1-\epsilon$, its verification execution on $(x_\ell,\pi_\ell)$ obeys the identical two lower bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every SAT neighbor $x_\ell^{(p)}$, submit the \emph{same} $\pi_\ell$. Soundness forces acceptance probability at most $\epsilon$. Couple verifier randomness. The identical-input-bit argument then applies with the proof string fixed, irrespective of proof grammar, sharing or encoded length.
\end{proof}
