\section*{PROVED/EXACT: factorization}
For one shared size-normalized SAME\_REALIZATION \(Q\), coordinate \(i\), Arthur coins \(r\), fixed answer vector \(a\), and query family \(J\),
\[
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)=
\exists Y^+\forall Y^-\bigwedge_{j\in J}
[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge
(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))].
\]
Writing separate tuples \(Y_j^+,Y_j^-\), the conjuncts share no witness/challenge variables once \(a,Q,i,r\) are fixed, hence
\[
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)\iff
\bigwedge_{j:a_j=1}\exists Y_j^+\Phi_j(Y_j^+)
\wedge
\bigwedge_{j:a_j=0}\forall Y_j^-\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)\iff
\bigwedge_{j\in J}[a_j=\operatorname{SAT}(\Phi_j)].}
\]
Thus there is no intrinsic \(\exists/\forall\) interaction inside the fixed-\(a\) answer-certification block. Under a fixed exact SAT decider \(A\), YES and NO bits are both supplied by \(a_j=A(\Phi_j)\). If query lengths are \(s_j\) and \(A(s)\le s^{K+o(1)}\),
\[
\boxed{\tau_{\rm ans}\le\tau_{\rm compile}+\tau_{\rm write}
+\sum_{j\in J}s_j^{K+o(1)}.}
\]
For constant \(|J|\) and \(s_j\le S\), this is \(S^{K+o(1)}\), not \(S^{2K+o(1)}\).
