\begin{lemma}[Odd-cycle charge parity]
In every satisfying extension, the number $r_i$ of both-true edges of an odd residual binary cycle is odd, so its additional defect charge $r_i-1$ is even.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

XOR around a closed cycle cancels every endpoint twice. The number $\ell_i-r_i$ of singleton edges is even. Since $\ell_i$ is odd, $r_i$ is odd.
\end{proof}
