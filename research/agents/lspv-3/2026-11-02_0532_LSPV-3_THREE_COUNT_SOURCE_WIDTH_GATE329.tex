\section{Theorem and proof}
Let $F$ be any valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) original, with exactly three distinct literals of one sign per clause, two occurrences of each polarity per original variable, and $m=4n/3$ original clauses. Malformed encodings are rejected. Fix one source-variable set $S$ and coherent partial assignments $p,q$ with $\operatorname{dom}(p)=\operatorname{dom}(q)=S$. Write $H_p(y)=F(p,y)$ and $H_q(y)=F(q,y)$ over the same free original variables $Y=V\setminus S$. Define
\[
 A=\#_Y H_p,\quad B=\#_Y H_q,\quad I=\#_Y(H_p\wedge H_q).
\]
Then the exact Boolean continuation implications satisfy
\[
 \boxed{H_p\Rightarrow H_q\iff A=I,\qquad H_p\equiv H_q\iff A=B=I.}
\]
Indeed, the models of the conjunction are the intersection of the models of the two residuals, all over the same future domain. This is a set identity, not a proposed new #SAT algorithm.

Let $G_F$ be the primal graph of the original clauses. Each residual clause is either removed or a restriction of an original clause; hence the primal graph of $H_p\wedge H_q$ is a subgraph of $G_F[Y]$. For a fixed original-source elimination order $\pi$ whose \emph{verified induced width} is $w(\pi)$, the restriction of $\pi$ to $Y$ has induced width at most $w(\pi)$ on each of $H_p,H_q,H_p\wedge H_q$, since fill-in for a fixed order is monotone under edge deletion. Three exact sum-product variable-elimination passes therefore compute $(A,B,I)$ with
\[
 T_{3c}(N;p,q,\pi)\le \operatorname{poly}(N)\bigl(1+2^{w(\pi)+1}\bigr),\quad
 S_{3c}(N;p,q,\pi)\le \operatorname{poly}(N)\bigl(1+2^{w(\pi)+1}\bigr).
\]
The polynomial covers strict source grammar and duplicate testing, source-clause indices, source/index/head reads, \emph{acquisition} and independent validation of $\pi$, factor construction, table operations on at most $(n+1)$-bit counts, output-bit access, and table-proof checking. The deterministic greedy min-fill acquisition in the reproducer scans every candidate's missing neighbor edges, with at most $O(n^4)$ word operations. Its width is observed, \emph{not assumed optimal}. Thus the \emph{recognized} acquired-width-$O(\log N)$ subclass admits a polynomial-cost semantic implication test. No such width or controller progress is proved on every canonical original. This application of exact model counting and primal width is known methodology [1--3].
