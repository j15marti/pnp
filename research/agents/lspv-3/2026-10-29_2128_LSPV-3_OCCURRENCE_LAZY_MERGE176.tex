\section{User-directed primitives and falsifiable target}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m C_j$ be a proper width-at-most-$k$ CNF with $n$ variables and physical source length $N$. An occurrence $x_{i,j}$ is the indexed occurrence of $x_i$ in source $C_j$, not a fresh independent variable. Its sign $\sigma_{ij}=\pm1$, preferred satisfying value $b_{ij}=(1+\sigma_{ij})/2$, and partial truth status $\{\mathrm{T},\mathrm{F},\bot\}$ are tracked independently of clause index. For proper ternary source, $N_h(a)$ counts clauses with $h$ satisfied literal occurrences, $0\le h\le3$. Complete complementation gives $N_h(\bar a)=N_{3-h}(a)$, $E=N_0+N_3$ invariant and $D=N_3-N_0$ antisymmetric. These are diagnostics, not oracles. Exact source clause equity is $Q_j(F)=|\{a:a\models F\setminus\{C_j\},\,a\not\models C_j\}|$, computed by exhaustive truth tables only on tiny controls. The operational target is an exact source-authenticated UP closure for a specified \emph{partial assignment}, with discovery and storage charged.

An opposite-sign source pair $C_j=(x\lor A)$, $C_h=(\neg x\lor B)$ has non-tautological resolvent $R=A\lor B$ unless $A\cup B$ contains opposite signed literals. Every completion falsifying $R$ violates one retained parent, so the entire corresponding partial cube is certified impossible. There are at most $P=\sum_xd_x^+d_x^-\le(mk)^2/4$ raw pairs and non-tautological resolvents have width at most $2k-2$. The theorem below handles these first-generation resolvents only, not exponential iterated resolution or SAT search.

\section{Exact no-table UP theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Streaming first-generation resolution closure]\label{th:stream}
For every proper width-$k$ source $F$ and partial assignment $p$, let $\mathcal R_1$ be the full multiset of non-tautological one-step resolvents of pairs of retained source clauses. A deterministic indexed-RAM procedure using $O(\log N)$-bit words produces exactly the conflict/nonconflict result, and in a nonconflicting case the entire forced-literal set, of fully saturated UP on $(F\land\bigwedge_{R\in\mathcal R_1}R)|_p$. It outputs checkable source-parent/pivot provenance for its new inferences, materializes no persistent resolvent table, and has the conservative charged upper bound
\[
T=O(N+(n+1)^2mk+(n+1)Pk^2),\qquad
S=O(N+(mk+nk)\log N)\ {\rm bits}.
\]
This is an upper bound on \emph{one UP closure}, not a polynomial general-SAT algorithm or a lower bound on all algorithms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fully saturate UP on the source $F$ under the current partial assignment. If it conflicts, terminate. Otherwise consider the conflict-free fixed point $s$. A non-tautological source resolvent $R=A\lor B$ cannot be false at $s$, since its two parents would force opposite values of the pivot. Suppose $R$ is unit with only $\ell$ unset. If $\ell$ occurs only in $A$, then all of $B$ is false, forcing $\neg x$ (or a conflict) by the second parent; then the first parent forces $\ell$, contradicting source saturation. The symmetric case is identical. Thus any new unit $R$ must have $\ell$ in both parents with the same sign (a \emph{merge}). The pivot must remain unset at $s$. Scan indexed opposite parent pairs on demand, reject tautological pairs, and on finding such a unit merge $R$ record $(j,h,x,\ell)$ plus the false nonpivot literals, assign $\ell$, and resaturate source UP. If all original clauses are already satisfied, every logical consequence is satisfied, so terminate. Repeat until no such unit pair exists or conflict occurs.

Each fired merge sets a previously unset variable, so at most $n$ firings occur. Every move is a valid UP step of the eager system. At nonconflicting termination no source clause or resolvent is unit or false; hence the state is a fixed point of eager UP. Monotonicity and least-fixed-point uniqueness give exactly the eager closure, and reachability of a contradiction agrees. Source indexing costs $O(mk)$ per scan; at most $n+1$ source saturations of $O((n+1)mk)$ scans and $n+1$ candidate scans of $O(Pk^2)$ each give the stated bound. Only source, occurrence lists, partial values and $O(n)$ parent-authenticated proof records persist.
\end{proof}

\section{Distinct-cube grid: proven unbounded score overlap}
\begin{theorem}[Grid family]\label{th:grid}
For integers $r,s\ge1$, define the connected proper width-three source
\[
G_{r,s}=\bigwedge_{i=1}^r(x\lor a\lor u_i)\ \land\
\bigwedge_{j=1}^s(\neg x\lor a\lor v_j).
\]
It is not strict signed exact-$(2,2)$. Its exactly $rs$ opposing-source pairs produce exactly $rs$ \emph{distinct}, non-tautological merge resolvents $R_{ij}=a\lor u_i\lor v_j$. Naively adding their individual uniform forbidden cube weights gives $rs/8$, whereas the exact union density is
\[
\tfrac12(1-2^{-r})(1-2^{-s})<\tfrac12.
\]
Each positive original clause has exact clause equity $Q_i=2^s$, and each negative original clause $Q_j=2^r$. Under partial $u_i=v_j=0$, with $x,a$ unset, original saturated UP forces neither $a$ nor $x$, but one source-certified $R_{ij}$ forces $a=1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Only pivot $x$ has both signs, and every $(i,j)$ gives the different clause shown. A resolvent is false exactly when $a=0$, $u_i=0$ and $v_j=0$. Their union requires $a=0$, some zero $u_i$, some zero $v_j$, proving the density formula. The summed weight $rs/8$ diverges along $r=s$ while the exact union stays below $1/2$, even after deduplicating resolvents. For positive clause $i$, the only assignment satisfying all other source clauses while violating it has $x=a=u_i=0$, $u_h=1$ for $h\ne i$ and all $v_j$ free, giving $Q_i=2^s$; the other side is symmetric. On the specified prefix the remaining source consists of copies of $(x\lor a)$ and $(\neg x\lor a)$ with no units. Each $R_{ij}$ becomes the unit $a$. Finally, a learned-clause forbidden full-assignment cube is contained in the \emph{original} source forbidden full-assignment union, so the latter's saturated coverage potential cannot pay any sound learning step.
\end{proof}
For a declared partial source-UP state $s$, the independently defined count $\Phi_{\rm unset}(s)$ of still-unset variables drops by one on this inference, so $(a=1)\mid_{v_\Phi=1}$. Its separate acquisition, verification and application cost $C_s$ may dominate this useful potential decrease; duplicate and expensive steps may have zero marginal or negative net value. An exact union of full forbidden assignments gives marginal zero relative to the original source. Contextual values and correlated scores are not additive.
