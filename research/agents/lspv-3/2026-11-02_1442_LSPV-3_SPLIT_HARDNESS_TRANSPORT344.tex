\section*{Exact source splitting in ordinary DAG Resolution}
For a clause $C$ of $F$, indispensable when $F\setminus\{C\}$ is satisfiable, introduce a fresh variable $y$ and split only the occurrence of $C$:
\[
S_y(F,C)=\bigl(F\setminus\{C\}\bigr)\ \cup\
\{C\lor y,\ C\lor\neg y\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The source labels for both children are distinct. This operation preserves the Boolean relation on all original variables, and preserves SAT/UNSAT labels.

\begin{theorem}[Exact one-source split law]
For every UNSAT $F$ and indispensable source clause $C$, with all arbitrary shared-DAG Resolution derivations and optimal orders admitted,
\[
W_{\mathrm{Res}}(S_y(F,C))=W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)+1.
\tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The upper bound resolves the two new clauses on $y$ to obtain $C$ and replays an optimal proof of $F$, redirecting all uses of $C$ to the single shared derived node. The lower bound uses two facts. First, each split child is indispensable: choose an assignment satisfying $F\setminus\{C\}$ and falsifying $C$; one setting of $y$ satisfies the other child. Hence any sound refutation uses both child sources in its contradiction ancestry. Second, the standard restriction lemma for Resolution transforms a proof under $y=0$ into a proof of $F$ with no more inference nodes, by replacing clauses satisfied by the restriction with true, restricting clauses containing the forced literal, and replacing any now-invalid pivot derivation by the surviving stronger antecedent. Because one indispensable source $C\lor\neg y$ restricts to true, at least its first use in the contradiction ancestry is removed. The resulting refutation of $F$ has at most one fewer inference than the original split proof. Thus $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le W_{\mathrm{Res}}(S_y(F,C))-1$. Taking minima proves (2), independent of sharing and optimal order.
\end{proof}

\section*{A growing, minimally UNSAT, nonaffine test family}
Use the same source-labelled four-variable minimally UNSAT core $G$ examined by LSPV-2 MIXED-NONAFFINE-GAP342:

\end{split}
\tag{3}
\]
Its clause-deletion satisfiable model counts are $(1,3,3,3,1,1)$. This proof does not require a claimed shortest mixed proof for $G$.

Construct $F_t$ from $G$ by splitting the shallowest guard leaf descended from $C_0$ on fresh $y_i=x_{4+i}$ at step $i$, with creation-order tie breaking. This produces a balanced selector tree and $t+1$ guarded leaves; the other five source clauses remain untouched.

\begin{theorem}[Growing family: exact restricted optimum, global combined interval]
For every $t\ge0$, $F_t$ is clause-minimally UNSAT with
$n_t=4+t$ original variables and $m_t=6+t$ labelled source clauses. Moreover,
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_t)=6+t},\qquad
\boxed{5+t\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_t)\le6+t}.
\tag{4}
\]
For the declared ASCII DIMACS with signed decimal indices, the encoded bit length is $N(t)=\Theta(t\log^2(t+2))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When indispensable $C$ is split, deletion witnesses for untouched clauses survive, while the two new clauses each have a deletion witness obtained by extending the old witness for $C$ with an appropriate $y$ bit. Induction gives minimal UNSAT for all $t$; repeated use of (2) gives the exact Resolution optimum $6+t$. That proof uses only RES and is therefore also a legal combined-system upper certificate. For any combined proof, all $m_t$ source clauses must occur in the ancestry of false, or the remaining satisfiable source subset could be extended across the conservative definitions and would contradict soundness. The undirected connected ancestry has at least $m_t+q-1$ dependency edges and at most $2q$ antecedent edges, so $q\ge m_t-1=5+t$, even with shared DAGs, adaptive choices and source-preserving representation switches. Balanced splits give maximum guard depth $O(\log(t+2))$; there are $\Theta(t\log(t+2))$ signed literal occurrences, each of typical decimal length $\Theta(\log(t+2))$. This proves the bit-length claim.
\end{proof}
