\section{Original clauses and exact four-mode extremes}
For each original proper signed width-three source $C_j$, occurrence $x_{i,j}$ is the actual global Boolean $x_i$ with signed polarity $\sigma_{i,j}=1$ for positive, 0 for negative. The local all-literals-true target $S_j$ has $x_i=\sigma_{i,j}$, while local all-literals-false target $U_j$ has $x_i=1-\sigma_{i,j}$. Invert $S_j$ to obtain $U_j$ and invert $U_j$ to obtain $S_j$. Local modes never introduce independently variable occurrences. For original clauses $j,k$ with $o_{jk}$ opposite-polarity common variables and $s_{jk}$ same-polarity common variables, $SS,UU$ are feasible exactly when $o_{jk}=0$, and $SU,US$ are feasible exactly when $s_{jk}=0$. If both $o,s>0$, none of these four simultaneous \emph{extreme} modes is compatible; the two ordinary disjunctions can still both be satisfied in middle states. Each coherent global $a$ satisfies $t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a)$, $N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a)$ and
\[
t_j(a\oplus H)=t_j(a)+|H\cap L_j^\mathrm{false}(a)|-|H\cap L_j^\mathrm{true}(a)|.
\]
The complete experiment separately records coherent $0^n,1^n$, actual complement assignments, one/multiple flips, each original clause's $t_j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$, and signed indexed targets and inverted targets.

\section{Root paired-matching firewall}
For coherent full $a$, let $H_a$ be the ORIGINAL clauses false at $a$, and $\tau_a$ the minimum number of original variables necessary to hit every variable support of a clause in $H_a$. Every true satisfying assignment is at distance at least $\tau_a$ from $a$, but repairing those originally false clauses is not sufficient for overall SAT.

\begin{theorem}[uniform signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ width-three root guard]
For EVERY proper width-three CNF in which every source variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice negatively, EVERY coherent complete anchor $a\in\{0,1\}^n$ satisfies
\[
\boxed{\tau_a\le 7n/15,\qquad \tau_a+\tau_{\bar a}\le14n/15<n.}
\]
Thus the earlier paired-complement necessary exclusion $\tau_a+\tau_{\bar a}>f$ at a partial cube with $f$ free coordinates can NEVER fire at the empty root ($f=n$), including under adaptive choice of complete complementary anchors. Exact signed22 recognition in indexed RAM takes $O(N)$ time on a parsed source; the theorem permits uniformly skipping the entire root matching selector without losing any rejection from this selector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each source variable has precisely two literals FALSE under the coherent anchor and so appears in at most two of its originally false clauses. Let $q=|H_a|$. Build a loopless multigraph whose vertices are the $q$ original source-clause indices, with one edge labelled by each variable appearing in two false clauses, and singleton coverage for variables appearing in one. Let $E$ be its edge count and $u$ its number of distinct active original variables. Proper width three and occurrence degree at most two imply
\[
u+E=3q,\quad 2E\le3q,\quad u\le n.
\]
The multigraph has maximum degree three. A greedy edge matching chooses $h$ edges while each choice deletes at most $3+3-1=5$ edges (including parallel edges), so $5h\ge E$. Flip each matched edge's source variable, plus one variable hitting each unmatched false clause: this hits every initially false clause using at most $q-h$ variable flips. Therefore
\[
15\tau_a\le 15(q-h)\le15q-3E
\le21q-7E=7u\le7n,
\]
where the middle inequality uses $4E\le6q$. Repeat the same proof at $\bar a$ and add. The bound includes $q=0$. These flips need not preserve clauses initially satisfied, so the proof never grants a free satisfying assignment or SAT answer.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Scope.} Pinning variables may turn three-literal clauses into binary or unit residuals, invalidating this root-width-three argument; paired cuts may remain meaningful within such descendants. The theorem is not a claim about every SAT algorithm, exact total SAT time, or P versus NP.

\section{Necessary adverse controls}

\[

(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_2)(\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3)(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_3)

has $\tau_0=\tau_1=2$, so $4>n=3$ certifies UNSAT, although a standard 2SAT SCC solver is cheaper. The proper width-three complete signed-NAE source on five variables includes $(\bigvee_{i\in T}x_i)$ and $(\bigvee_{i\in T}\neg x_i)$ for every original triple $|T|=3$; its endpoint hitting numbers are both 3, so $6>5$ also certifies UNSAT. A polynomial direct recognizer sees that any five-bit assignment has a monochromatic triple. This source violates exact22 with six occurrences of each sign per variable. Exact general hitting numbers for this high-degree finite control were computed externally by exhaustive search and were \emph{not} used as a polynomial controller subroutine.
For parents $(x_i\vee A)$ and $(\neg x_i\vee B)$, the non-tautological resolvent $R=A\vee B$ forbids the entire partial cube falsifying $R$; the original indexed parent units then conflict. This is ordinary resolution, often already source-UP-detectable, and no new post-UP-pruning claim is made.
