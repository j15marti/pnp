\section{Exact named-boundary semantics}
Let \(P(x,Y,U)\) and \(Q(x,Y,V)\) be ordinary proper ternary CNF modules. The original source name \(x\), and every source name in the ordered tuple \(Y\), occurs consistently in both modules; the private auxiliary-name sets \(U,V\) are disjoint. Define the Boolean existential feasibility relation
\[
 R_P(b,y):=\bigl[\exists u\ P(b,y,u)\bigr]\quad
 (b\in\{0,1\},\ y\in\{0,1\}^{|Y|}).
\]
The correct join, requiring \emph{the same} named boundary assignment, is
\[
 \SAT(P\wedge Q)=
 \bigvee_{y,b}\bigl(R_P(b,y)\wedge R_Q(b,y)\bigr). \tag{1}
\]
Replace the appearances of \(x\) in \(P\), but \emph{not} \(Q\), by a fresh identifier \(x_L\) and leave all remaining shared names unchanged:
\[
 F=P(x,Y,U)\wedge Q(x,Y,V),\quad
 F^\#=P(x_L,Y,U)\wedge Q(x,Y,V). \tag{2}
\]
This is a source-checkable complete ordinary proper-3CNF rewrite. The original \(x\) remains the suffix's original name, \(x_L\) is a private new name, and both modules retain an exact inverse source-name map. Its only semantic effect is to let the left and right \(x\)-witnesses differ.

\begin{theorem}[Universal-suffix erasure iff a cylinder]\label{th:cyl}
For each fixed proper-3CNF prefix \(P(x,Y,U)\), the following are equivalent:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{(i)} & \forall y\in\{0,1\}^{|Y|}:
       R_P(0,y)=R_P(1,y),\\
\mathrm{(ii)} & \forall\text{ proper-3CNF suffixes }Q(x,Y,V),
  \ U\cap V=\varnothing:
  \ \SAT(P\wedge Q)=\SAT(P(x_L,Y,U)\wedge Q).
\end{array} \tag{3}
\]
There is no promise of small \(|Y|\), and (ii) is required for \emph{both}
SAT and UNSAT labels, under every compatible suffix.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed \(y\), the original join's contribution is
\(\exists b[R_P(b,y)\wedge R_Q(b,y)]\).
The renamed join's contribution is
\((\exists a\,R_P(a,y))\wedge(\exists b\,R_Q(b,y))\).
If the two prefix bits are equal, these expressions coincide for every
\(R_Q\). Disjunction over \(y\) proves sufficiency.

Conversely suppose \(R_P(0,y_0)=1\) and \(R_P(1,y_0)=0\).
Take \(Q\) to enforce \(x=1\) and \(Y=y_0\), with private fresh
auxiliaries. Every unit literal \(\ell\) has the proper ternary realization
\[
 \operatorname{PIN}(\ell)=
 \bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}
 (\ell\vee\epsilon p\vee\delta q),
\]
with two new private names; its existential projection is precisely \(\ell\).
Then \(F\) is UNSAT and \(F^\#\) is SAT. The reversed inequality is handled
by pinning \(x=0\). Thus failure of (i) contradicts (ii).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Two actual levels, recertified]\label{lem:depth}
Suppose a first chosen name \(x\) satisfies (3) for the current prefix
and suffix, and a second chosen source name \(y\) satisfies (3) for the
\emph{newly produced} left prefix and its new suffix. Performing the two
renamings serially produces two complete ordinary proper-3CNF descendants.
Their parent SAT status agrees at each level, including on UNSAT parents.
All unrenamed original names retain exact identity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{th:cyl} once to the actual first split and once to
the actual second split; the second premise concerns the \emph{new}
prefix and is not inherited automatically from the old one.
Rename only the designated occurrences using fresh identifiers and
emit a new complete header and clause body at each level. Properness
of each ternary clause survives injective renaming; the two successive
equisatisfiable transformations compose.
\end{proof}

\section{\(\Pi_2^P\)-complete recognition, not an SAT decision lower bound}
\begin{theorem}[CYL-3CNF recognition]\label{th:ph}
Given a proper signed ternary CNF \(P(x,Y,U)\) with designated distinguished
variable \(x\), named boundary list \(Y\), and private names \(U\), deciding
\[
 \forall y\ \bigl[
  (\exists u\,P(0,y,u))\leftrightarrow
  (\exists u\,P(1,y,u))\bigr] \tag{4}
\]
is \(\Pi_2^P\)-complete under deterministic polynomial many-one reductions.
The hardness holds with all clauses proper and exactly three signed
literals on pairwise distinct names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Its complement guesses \(y\) and one branch \(b\) that is feasible while
the other is infeasible. It has the form
\(\exists y,b,u\,\forall u'[
P(b,y,u)\wedge\neg P(1-b,y,u')]\), a \(\Sigma_2^P\)
predicate, giving membership.

For hardness start with the known \(\Pi_2^P\)-complete
\(\forall Y\exists Z\,\varphi(Y,Z)\), where \(\varphi\) is a proper
ternary CNF. For each original clause \((a\vee b\vee c)\), introduce
a fresh helper \(h_C\) and insert exactly
\[
 (\neg x\vee a\vee h_C)\ \wedge\
 (\neg h_C\vee b\vee c). \tag{5}
\]
All names within either clause are distinct. For fixed \(x=0\)
choose \(h_C=0\), satisfying the gadget independently of all original
values. For \(x=1\), eliminating \(h_C\) imposes exactly
\(a\vee b\vee c\). Conjoin all gadgets. Consequently
\[
 R_P(0,y)=1,\qquad
 R_P(1,y)=[\exists Z\,\varphi(y,Z)]. \tag{6}
\]
The cylinder condition is exactly \(\forall Y\exists Z\,\varphi\).
Each original clause becomes two proper clauses and one helper,
hence the many-one reduction is linear in clause-count and polynomial
in complete ordinary encoded bit length.
\end{proof}
The precise source for \(\Pi_2^P\)-completeness of quantified proper
3-SAT is D\"ocker--Dorn--Linz--Semple~\cite{qbf}. This classification
does \emph{not} give a superpolynomial lower bound on a deterministic
ordinary SAT decider, and cannot contradict \(P=NP\): under that assumption,
the polynomial hierarchy collapses. Recognition cost and same-machine
resource exponents still require an independent argument.

\section{An exact but nonfavorable solver-dependent certificate}
Fix \emph{one} \(A\) as in Section~1. A total controller can parse an
ordinary candidate prefix and separator, enumerate the \(2^{|Y|}\) named
boundary assignments, materialize for each \(y\) the two proper-3CNF
pinned versions of \(P\), and invoke this \emph{same} \(A\) on each
complete ordinary child. It may split \(x\) only if all returned pairs
agree, otherwise retain the original unsplit source. Malformed source
encodings are handled by a fixed total rejection convention; absent a
legal split it may decide the unchanged original with \(A\).
Theorem~\ref{th:cyl} makes every permitted split exactly both-label
preserving. Descendants must be \emph{recertified} with fresh queries.

If \(|Y|\le b\log_2N\), every eager child has length
\(N^{\rho+o(1)}\), generator bit overhead \(N^{g+o(1)}\) per child and
\(N^{r+o(1)}\) other charged work, this architecture's honest envelope
is
\[
 T'(N)\le N^{\max\{r,b+\rho+g,b+\rho t\}+o(1)},\qquad
 S'(N)\le N^{\max\{\rho,\rho s,\eta,\kappa\}+o(1)}. \tag{7}
\]
Its \(2^{|Y|+1}\) SAT calls have cost \(N^{b+\rho t+o(1)}\),
\emph{not} one free predicate. Count original source and workspace
reads, changed/unchanged writes, parsing/headers, source-sign
inspection, actual policy selection and retry loops, caches, complete
child buffers or charged virtual probes, indexing/control, certificate
rows, and all simultaneously live ancestors. In this literal-renaming
implementation, \(\rho=1\) and \(b\ge0\); the time envelope includes
\(b+t\) and eager peak workspace includes the \(N\)-bit readable child.
Two levels do not change the power scale. A parent-scale fallback \(A(F)\)
cannot be hidden in a purported improving transition. This is an
\textbf{all-input correct} conditional wrapper, \emph{not} a favorable
ARC edge.

By contrast, if an entirely separate theorem supplied true
\(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) ordinary children with \(\lambda<1\),
then a paid \(N^b\)-invocation selector costs
\(N^{b+\lambda t+o(1)}\) guest time. That observation does not establish
such a compiler. Virtual child ports incur per-request replay, and a
nonuniform/circuit representation does not create a free readable
input tape. Temporal flips, live-bit time, gate/wire/switch hardware,
and encoded program size are distinct resources, none identified
with the FLMV simultaneous bit-time/writable-space pair here.
