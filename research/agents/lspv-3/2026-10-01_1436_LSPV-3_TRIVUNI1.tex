\section*{LSPV-3: trivial-uniformizer baseline and reconstruction dependency audit}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. This report proves no unrestricted SAT lower bound and no necessity of the conservative $k^4$ exponent.

\paragraph{CORPUS REVIEWED.}
I surveyed the current repository corpus and history, including root \texttt{main.tex}, \texttt{AGENTS.md}, \texttt{CODEX.md}, the pnp-research skill, current task specifications, newest and relevant historical MAIN snapshots, materially relevant LSPV-1--5 work, the LSPV-3 BIND1/FIXCOIN1/QREC1/RECDEP1/DEPDEF1/DEPSWAP1 chain, predecessor LSPV-2 HADSQ1/POLAR1, LSPV-4 PCLOSE1/ACQ1, governance, and the HEC/CRS--PPC/CCSPC/RCCN/Search-G branches. Repository search was also run for reconstruction NO certificates, coNP-hard binding, dependency uniformization, retired routes, and duplicate theorem attempts. POLAR1 is substantive and has the required header and sentinel, so predecessor preflight passes.

\paragraph{Two obstacles and primary-literature preflight.}
The two active obstacles are unchanged: downstream SAT descriptions depend on the middle existential reconstruction choices, and every proposed flattening must charge acquisition, normalization, compilation, verification, indexing, census/output, and writing. van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski (2025) supplies the resilient reconstruction architecture; Shaltiel--Umans (2009) supplies the underlying resilient HSG construction; Viola (2005) proves $\mathrm{BPTime}(t)\subseteq\Sigma_3\mathrm{Time}(t\,\mathrm{polylog}\,t)$ and gives an oracle-relative limitation; current DQBF work makes explicit that dependency information is itself a first-class part of quantified semantics. None supplies the missing construction-specific charged uniformizer.

\paragraph{Exact shared-$Q$ reconstruction predicate.}
Fix one acquired, exactly size-normalized SAME\_REALIZATION $Q$, coordinate $i$, and Arthur coins $r$. For the constant query family $J$, with SAT predicates $\Phi_j$, exact answers satisfy
\[
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)=\exists Y^+\,\forall Y^-\bigwedge_{j\in J}\left[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))\right].
\]
Late substitution retains the schematic dependency
\[
\boxed{\exists\sigma\;\forall u\;\exists z\;\forall y\;R(x,Q,\sigma,u,z,y)},\qquad z=(\ell,a,Y^+),\quad y=Y^-.
\]

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: trivial-uniformizer baseline under the assumed SAT decider.}
Let $D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)$ be any model-matched compiler that produces the $j$th downstream SAT instance, and let the assumed exact SAT decider $A$ run in time $S^{k+o(1)}$. Define
\[
U_A(x,Q,r,u,z,j)=1-A(D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)).
\]
Then $U_A$ exactly computes the downstream NO bit for every legal $z$ and $j$. Thus, under the counterfactual assumption supplying $A$, a uniform algorithm for the NO relation already exists. If $D_j$ has output length $L^{b+o(1)}$, direct evaluation through $A$ costs
\[
\boxed{L^{bk+o(1)}}
\]
per constant-family evaluation, up to the separately charged compiler cost. This is not a new collapse; it is precisely the conservative SAT-elimination cost.

Consequently, mere existence, definability, canonicity, or uniform computability of an upstream representation cannot justify deleting a factor of $k$. A useful uniformizer must beat this trivial baseline after all construction and access costs are included. In particular, a candidate $Z=F(x,Q,r,u)$ is quantitatively useful only if
\[
\tau_F+\tau_{\mathrm{repr}}+\tau_{\mathrm{idx}}+\tau_V+\tau_{\mathrm{compile}}+\tau_{\mathrm{write}}< L^{bk-o(1)}
\]
in the same model and at the stage where one recursive SAT-decider invocation would otherwise be paid. The inequality is a target condition, not an established bound.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: materialized truth-table uniformization can be exponentially larger than on-demand evaluation.}
If the legal middle block has $s$ bits and all $2^s$ strings are legal, a literal table storing one NO bit for each $z$ has $2^s$ entries. Therefore replacing on-demand evaluation by a fully materialized table is not automatically a polynomial-size certificate. This counting fact does not rule out a succinct circuit or algebraic representation; it only forbids treating the full response table as free.

\paragraph{Relation to the older coNP-hard binding guard.}
BIND1 already proved a direct coNP-hardness result for designated uniqueness, so this run does not relabel generic coNP hardness as new. The new point is orthogonal: even when the counterfactual SAT decider makes the NO relation uniformly computable, the obvious uniformizer has exactly the charged $bk$ cost one is trying to remove. Therefore a $k^3$ improvement requires a structure-specific compression or evaluator, not merely a complexity-class collapse.

\paragraph{Conservative recurrence and separate charges.}
If one stage has deterministic matrix cost $n^{\alpha+o(1)}$, its model-matched SAT encoding has size $n^{b_q\alpha+o(1)}$, and $A$ costs size$^{k+o(1)}$, then
\[\alpha_{q+1}\le b_qk\alpha_q+o(1).\]
Hence the current conservative accounting remains
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm det}\le\alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1)}.
\]
Status: \textbf{CONDITIONAL}. It is not a lower bound and does not prove $k^4$ necessary. The resource ledger remains
\[
\tau_{\rm total}=\tau_Q+\tau_{\rm norm}+\tau_{\rm census}+\tau_{\rm amp}+\tau_{\rm idx}+\tau_{\rm compile}+\tau_{\rm rec}+\tau_{\rm write}+\tau_{\rm transport}.
\]
TransportCert is accounting only.

\paragraph{PQ and cross-domain classification.}
\textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER:} Schur/componentwise products, puncturing/shortening when correctly typed, quadratic-relation kernels, POLAR1 alternating-form equations, and collision Cauchy--Schwarz on a declared map. These do not remove a reconstruction quantifier.

\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE:} Hilbert functions, syzygies, low-rank quadratic relations, dependency schemes, special-soundness collision extraction, and LWE-style external black-box extraction. A successful transfer must yield a succinct SAME\_REALIZATION evaluator or forbidden witness whose acquisition and verification beat the trivial $A\circ D_j$ baseline.

\textbf{FAILED TRANSFER:} cryptographic collapse-binding or LWE hardness, random oracles, VLSI locality, control barriers, distributed cuts, topology, and proof-system-specific savings as unconditional unrestricted-SAT runtime claims.

\paragraph{SAME\_REALIZATION, TC4, relativization, and algebrization.}
The compiler $D_j$, shared $Q$, Arthur coins, legal middle choices, and any proposed $Z$ must belong to the same realization. TC4 remains OPEN because an arbitrary exact SAT algorithm may compute the same NO relation without materializing the preferred representation. The trivial uniformizer explicitly relativizes with the SAT decider, and POLAR1's algebraic relation spaces remain visible to algebraic strengthening. No nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing hinge is identified here.

\paragraph{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.}
DEPSWAP1 already proved that generic quantifier commutation is invalid; DEPDEF1 already identified dependency uniformization; QREC1/RECDEP1 already established the conservative recurrence and one-$k$ YES/NO accounting; BIND1 already supplied a coNP-hard generic binding guard. This report does not duplicate those claims. The new exact contribution is the trivial-uniformizer baseline $U_A=1-A\circ D_j$, which shows that uniform computability alone gives no quantitative improvement. No contradiction with POLAR1, HADSQ1, ACQ1, PCLOSE1, HEC, CRS--PPC, CCSPC, RCCN, or Search-G was found.

\paragraph{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN.}
Root MAIN already records the exact four-block dependency, conservative $b_0b_1b_2b_3k^4$ ledger, separate charges, and dependency-uniformization target. The warranted refinement is to require every proposed uniformizer to beat the explicit trivial $A\circ D_j$ baseline, rather than merely exist or be uniformly computable.
