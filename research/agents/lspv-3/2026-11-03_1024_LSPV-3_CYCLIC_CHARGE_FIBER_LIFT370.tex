\section*{Exact charged cyclic-fiber test}
Take any connected simple bridgeless cubic graph $G_h$ of $h\ge4$ vertices, where $h$ is even. For each vertex $v$ create $L_v,R_v,P_v$. Redirect two original incident edges to $L_v$ and the third to $R_v$. Add $L_vR_v$, $R_vP_v$, and the global cycle edges $P_vP_{v+1\bmod h}$. Denote the resulting simple cubic graph by $H_h$. Then $|V(H_h)|=3h$ and $|E(H_h)|=9h/2$. Each triple $X_v=\{L_v,R_v,P_v\}$ is a connected fiber; every old edge survives between its corresponding fibers.

\begin{lemma}[Quantitative expansion under bounded connected fibers]
If $G$ has maximum degree $d$ and edge expansion $\epsilon>0$, and $H$ has connected fibers of at most $b$ vertices preserving one cross-fiber edge for every original edge, then
\[
 \boxed{h_E(H)\ge\frac{\epsilon}{b(d+\epsilon+1)}}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix nonempty $S\subset V(H)$ with $|S|\le|V(H)|/2$. Partition old vertices into $A$ (fibers wholly in $S$), $B$ (fibers wholly out), and $M$ (mixed). Put $t=|\partial_HS|$. Connectedness of each mixed fiber gives $t\ge|M|$. Since fibers have size at most $b$, both $|A|,|B|\ge|S|/b-|M|$. Old $G$-edges between $A,B$ cross $S$, and at most $d|M|$ old edges touch $M$. Therefore
\[
 t\ge\epsilon\min\{|A|,|B|\}-d|M|
 \ge\epsilon|S|/b-(\epsilon+d)|M|.
\]
Combine with $|M|\le t$ and rearrange. This concerns graph cuts, not mixed proof cost.
\end{proof}
For the lift $b=d=3$, so $h_E(H_h)\ge\epsilon/[3(4+\epsilon)]$. $H_h$ is bridgeless: lifted old cycles protect old cross-edges, cycles through two old neighbors protect every $L_vR_v$ (bridgeless cubic $G_h$ has no cut vertex), connectedness of the old-edge subsystem plus the $P$ ring protects each $R_vP_v$, and the ring itself protects each $P_vP_{v+1}$.

\begin{theorem}[Transferred restricted hardness, exact collapse in the combined system]
If $(G_h)$ is any such cubic expander family, encode odd-charge Tseitin parity on $H_h$ by four authentic width-three source clauses per vertex, with charge one at $P_0$ and zero elsewhere. Call the formula $F_h$. It has $n_h=9h/2$ variables, $m_h=12h$ clauses and a standard explicit binary bit length $N_h=\Theta(h\log(h+2))$. For some $c'>0$, for all sufficiently large eligible $h$,
\[
 \boxed{W_{\rm Res}(F_h)\ge2^{c'h}}
 \quad\text{while}\quad
 \boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_h)=12h-1}.
\]
Thus the standard Resolution bound is $2^{\Omega(N_h/\log N_h)}$, but the exact all-rule mixed minimum is $\Theta(N_h/\log N_h)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The expansion lemma plus published odd-Tseitin Resolution lower bound prove the first inequality. The parity rows on the 3h vertices XOR to $0=1$ and certify UNSAT. Since $H_h$ is bridgeless cubic, all 12h original clauses are indispensable: deleting one wrong-parity blocker and fixing that incident triple leaves a consistent incidence GF(2) system on connected $H_h-u$. Every sound, conservative, at-most-binary-premise refutation must connect all indispensable source nodes to its contradiction; its ancestry graph with $m_h$ sources and $q$ charged nodes has at least $m_h+q-1$ edges and at most $2q$ parent arcs. Hence $q\ge m_h-1$. Conversely, conjoin the four local source clauses at each vertex with three AND3 nodes, obtaining an exact parity row for 9h nodes total. Combine 3h rows with 3h-1 binary XOR nodes, giving $12h-1$ charged nodes. The minimum is global, even allowing arbitrary adaptive shared DAGs, conservatively paid auxiliaries and representation switching.
\end{proof}
