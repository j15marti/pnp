\section{Fixed calculi, costs and precise challenge}
Retain the fixed, complete $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ of LSPV-2 341: original labelled CNF clauses are cost-free source nodes; each distinct derived node costs one; legal rules are ordinary binary Resolution (RES), constant-three-variable exact truth-table AND (AND3), binary GF$(2)$ row addition, charged constant-support conversion, and conservative two-input Boolean gate definitions (DEF). The charged DEF node authenticates its defining clauses, available for later RES. Auxiliary circuits, adaptive choice, sharing and temporary growth are permitted; no semantic UNSAT oracle is allowed. Proof DAGs and every local rule are explicitly checked. All input encodings are general DIMACS. RES ensures refutational completeness, not short proofs.

Separately, for a restricted lower-bound \emph{calibration}, fix $R_{\oplus}^{\mathrm{syn}}$: RES on complementary affine GF$(2)$ pivots between disjunctions of affine equations, syntactic addition of affine disjuncts, original ordinary CNF sources. Unit derived-node weights; no fresh conservative definitions. This subsystem is sound, effective, complete and polynomially checkable. It is weaker than the unrestricted semantic-weakening DAG Res$(\oplus)$ system of Braun (2026) and \emph{not} a replacement for the full mixed system.

Write $W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\sum_{v\text{ derived in }\pi}1$ and $\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\text{ UNSAT valid},|\mathrm{enc}(F)|\le N}W_R(F)$, empty maximum zero. The minimum ranges over every finite legal shared-DAG derivation with the best valid strategy granted for free. B3 is the separate, still unproved statement SAT$\in$P $\Rightarrow\overline W_R$ polynomial for the \emph{same} fixed $R$.

\section{A growing minimally UNSAT sign-sensitive family}
For each $\ell\ge1$, put $n=2^\ell$, $m=n+1$. The $\ell$ input bits of pigeon $i$ encode its hole $b_i$. For every pair $i<j$ and hole $z\in\{0,1\}^\ell$, introduce the ordinary CNF collision clause
\[
D_{i,j,z}=\bigvee_{t=1}^\ell[b_{it}\ne z_t]\;\vee\;\bigvee_{t=1}^\ell[b_{jt}\ne z_t],
\qquad F_\ell=\bigwedge_{i<j,z}D_{i,j,z}.
\]
The exact parameters are $v=m\ell$, $M=n\binom{m}{2}=\Theta(n^3)$ clauses, $2\ell M$ occurrences, and $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ \emph{ASCII DIMACS bits}. The extra factor $\ell$ over the literal count comes from the number of decimal digits in indexed variable names.

\begin{lemma}[Every BPHP clause indispensable]
For every $\ell\ge1$, $F_\ell$ is UNSAT and deletion of any one of its $M$ clauses makes it SAT. Flipping the sign of any of its $2\ell M$ occurrences also makes it SAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There cannot be an injective map of $n+1$ pigeons into $n$ holes, so every assignment falsifies some collision clause. For any fixed $D_{i,j,z}$, put exactly pigeons $i,j$ in hole $z$ and bijectively assign the remaining $n-1$ pigeons to the other holes. Under this one assignment exactly $D_{i,j,z}$ is false. Therefore deleting it permits SAT. Every literal of that sole false clause is false, and reversing any one sign makes it true without changing any other clause. This also certifies the same-original sign-flip controls.
\end{proof}

\section{Substantive falsifier: a polynomial proof in the fixed mixed calculus}
\begin{theorem}[Conservative extension collapses BPHP in the combined calculus]
For the above \emph{preselected} $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$,
\[
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_\ell)\le n^{O(1)}=N_\ell^{O(1)}.
\]
Thus BPHP is not a superpolynomial hard family for this combined calculus, even though every source clause and occurrence sign is sensitive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each pigeon $i$ and every nonempty bit prefix $s$ of length at most $\ell$, introduce by a charged two-input DEF a fresh $p_{i,s}$ equivalent to the conjunction of those prefix bits. For a length-one prefix this is a bit or its negation, obtainable with a repeated-input binary gate. Longer prefixes use $p_{i,sb}\leftrightarrow p_{i,s}\wedge[b_{i,|s|+1}=b]$. Exactly $(n+1)(2n-2)=O(n^2)$ DEF nodes suffice. These are conservative explicit abbreviations, not decision bits.

The two child definitions entail and allow constant-many RES steps to derive
\[
\neg p_{i,s}\vee p_{i,s0}\vee p_{i,s1}.
\]
The length-one definitions similarly derive $p_{i,0}\vee p_{i,1}$. Starting with the latter, resolve in each internal split to obtain the unary totality clause $\bigvee_{z}p_{i,z}$ in $O(n)$ nodes for each pigeon. The chain of prefix definitions yields $p_{i,z}\Rightarrow[b_{it}=z_t]$ for each $t$ with $O(\ell)$ binary RES steps. Resolve these implications against the original $2\ell$ mismatching literals of $D_{i,j,z}$, deriving the unary collision clause $\neg p_{i,z}\vee\neg p_{j,z}$. Without caching, this costs at most $O(\ell^2)$ nodes per source, or $O(n^3\ell^2)$ overall. Any optional functionality clauses required by a particular unary proof format also follow via contradictory prefix bits in polynomial work.

The resulting derived clauses are exactly the ordinary unary pigeonhole-principle CNF for $n+1$ pigeons and $n$ holes. The classical Cook--Reckhow polynomial-size Extended Resolution refutation family (1979), or polynomial Frege proof family of Buss (1987) translated into Extended Resolution, completes the contradiction. Every Extended Resolution operation is an RES inference or a charged definitional two-input gate, hence is a legal $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ operation with at most polynomial overhead. Appending that classical proof produces the stated polynomial-cost \emph{legal} mixed proof. This is an \emph{upper} bound and makes no claim of an exact optimum, general polynomial proof search, or any B3 transfer.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Implementation caveat.} This argument requires the DEF defining clauses to be available to RES at their charged node, as specified in the original 341 model. If a revised $R$ instead prohibits those clauses, the ER simulation must be separately reproved. We did not emit an entire parameterized Cook--Reckhow proof DAG in this run, so the full-certificate compiler is the specific pending implementation check.

\section{Recent lower-bound literature and quantified separation of models}
Braun's September 19, 2026 arXiv revision 2 (arXiv:2609.23015) claims, with published Lean links, a theorem for \emph{unrestricted} DAG-like Res$(\oplus)$: for $\ell\ge32$, every proof of $F_\ell$ has more than $\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))$ nodes \emph{including sources}, without a depth or regularity restriction. The paper is an original 2026 preprint, not independently peer reviewed by this agent; its Lean source was not executed here.

Conditional on the claimed theorem, the weaker fixed $R_{\oplus}^{\mathrm{syn}}$ has
\[
W_{R_{\oplus}^{\mathrm{syn}}}(F_\ell)>
 \exp(n/(32768\ell^2))-M
 =\exp\!\bigl(\Omega(N_\ell^{1/3}/(\log N_\ell)^{8/3})\bigr)
\]
asymptotically. The source subtraction is mandatory because this project's cost excludes free input clauses. Such a verified result would already satisfy the \emph{worst-case} superpolynomial $\overline W$ objective for that fixed \emph{restricted} calculus. However, the preceding theorem demonstrates why this does \emph{not} transport to the extension-capable combined calculus. It also does not prove B3 for even the restricted system. Neither shortest-proof length nor the existence of one recognized hard family gives a runtime lower bound for every SAT decider.

\item[] \textbf{F2: A parity-resolution lower bound transfers to DEF-augmented mixed reasoning. FALSIFIED.} The legal binary-to-unary extension is a polynomial shortcut.
