\section{PROPOSE: four falsifiable mechanisms and the missing lemma}
The precise role-owned gap is a universally affordable, constructible, reusable, source-authenticated exact semantic summary, with provable verification on \emph{both} SAT and UNSAT sides. The candidate mechanisms were as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{P1.} A constant-length exact boundary table might also admit a universal polynomial-time checker without supplementary certificates. This is refuted conditionally on $P\ne NP$ by a sharper NP/coNP-complete verification test, Theorem~\ref{thm:polarity}.
\item[] \textbf{P2.} A SAT witness plus the trivially satisfiable boundary side might suffice to certify a proposed negative projection. This is falsified directly: sound negative-side coverage requires excluding the other boundary value.
\item[] \textbf{P3.} Authenticated original-source resolvents followed by complete two-branch, 2CNF-SCC leaves always yield a correct negative proof, albeit possibly exponentially long. This is proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:tree} and implemented.
\item[] \textbf{P4.} A fixed polynomial bound on proof \emph{length} could suffice for deterministic construction. This is false as an inference; polynomially bounded proofs yield an $NP=coNP$ consequence, whereas a polynomial-time deterministic \emph{producer} yields $P=NP$ in this reduction.
\end{enumerate}
The sole research targets remain constructive $P=NP$ and separation directions in parallel, with no answer presumed.

\section{BUILD I: a connected proper 3CNF with a one-bit interface}
Let $F(X)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)$ be an arbitrary proper 3CNF with $m\ge1$ and three distinct variable names per clause. We may remove unused names. Introduce one shared variable $y$ and fresh $t_1,\ldots,t_m$ and form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lift}
G_F(y,X,T)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}\left[(\neg y\vee a_i\vee t_i)\wedge(\neg t_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)\right].
\end{equation}
This is a polynomial-time, source-index-preserving mapping to \emph{connected proper} 3CNF. It has $2m$ original clauses and the same three distinct variables in every clause. Note that $G_F$ itself is \emph{always satisfiable}; the hard task is determining its exact existential projection on $y$, not deciding $G_F$.

\begin{lemma}[One-bit projection and authenticated resolvents]\label{lem:projection}
For the construction \eqref{eq:lift},
\[
\exists X,T\ G_F(y,X,T)\equiv
\neg y\vee \exists X\,F(X).
\]
Furthermore, each derived clause $(\neg y\vee a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)$ is the single resolution of original source clauses with authenticated IDs $(2i-1,2i)$ on pivot $t_i$; with the assumption $y=1$ it reduces to the original $F$ clause.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $y=0$, all first clauses are true, and assigning every $t_i=0$ satisfies the second clauses. For $y=1$, existentially eliminate each distinct $t_i$ from $(a_i\vee t_i)\wedge(\neg t_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)$, obtaining $a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i$. The elimination can be performed independently for all $i$. The stated resolution rule is an ordinary sound inference on $t_i$, verified directly from the two original source literal triples; the explicit source-ID map is fixed by the canonical encoding.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact proposed-table verification has opposite hardness polarities]\label{thm:polarity}
On the connected proper-3CNF image $\{G_F\}$, deciding whether the \emph{proposed exact} projected relation is $R_{+}(y)=\top$ is NP-complete, and deciding whether it is $R_{-}(y)=\neg y$ is coNP-complete. Both proposed tables have constant bit length and $y$ is a single boundary bit. More explicitly,
\[
\proj_y(G_F)=R_+\iff F\in\SAT,
\qquad
\proj_y(G_F)=R_-\iff F\in\UNSAT.
\]
Thus a single polynomial-time sound-and-complete verifier accepting these \emph{bare tables} on all promised inputs would imply $P=NP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equivalences are Lemma~\ref{lem:projection}. The map $F\mapsto G_F$ is uniformly polynomial, is connected, preserves proper clause width, and each side belongs to the asserted class: on the positive side a polynomial witness is an $X$ assignment satisfying $F$, on the negative side a polynomial witness for \emph{nonmembership} is an $X$ assignment satisfying $F$. The standard NP-completeness of proper 3SAT supplies hardness, with coNP-completeness by complementation. If a deterministic polynomial checker of the proposed exact relations existed without any further proof object, run it on $(G_F,R_+)$ to decide SAT. The same statement holds for $(G_F,R_-)$ with opposite output.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Crucial soundness distinction.} If a purported certificate for $R_-$ merely exhibits the easy $y=0$ satisfying assignment, it proves only $R_-\subseteq\proj_y(G_F)$, not the missing exclusion $y=1\notin\proj_y(G_F)$. On SAT inputs, this one-sided certificate exists even though $R_-$ is false. For $R_+$ a witness at $y=1$ suffices because $y=0$ has a fixed witness. For $R_-$ an UNSAT refutation of $G_F\wedge y$ is necessary in the accepted certificate grammar. This is verification polarity, not a free side bit.

\section{BUILD II: a full original-source 2CNF-SCC refutation grammar}
\begin{theorem}[Complete charged, source-authenticated negative certificate]\label{thm:tree}
For every unsatisfiable $F$ on $n\ge2$ variables, a deterministic generator can produce an authenticated refutation of $G_F\wedge y$ consisting of the $m$ source-ID resolution derivations from Lemma~\ref{lem:projection}, a complete binary branching tree on any fixed $n-2$ of the original $X$ variables, and in each leaf either an authenticated empty residual original clause or a 2CNF implication-graph contradictory SCC with both directed paths. Its length and total deterministic construction work are at most $2^{n-2}\operatorname{poly}(N)$, where $N$ is the bit length of the original input; full verification is polynomial in $N$ plus the \emph{actual certificate length}. No uncharged witness guessing is needed for this construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Resolve $t_i$ in source clause IDs $2i-1,2i$ and assume $y=1$, giving the $m$ original ternary clauses $F$. Branch both ways on variables $x_1,\ldots,x_{n-2}$. At a leaf, simplify every authenticated clause according to all ancestor values. No residual has width exceeding two. Since $F$ is UNSAT, this residual 2CNF is UNSAT for \emph{every} branch history, not merely for an existentially selected history. If any residual original clause is empty, its source ID and the ancestor assignments give an immediate contradiction. Otherwise the usual implication-graph characterization of 2SAT unsatisfiability yields some variable $v$ with paths $v\leadsto\neg v$ and $\neg v\leadsto v$. Label each graph edge by the exact original source-derived clause that licenses its implication; a unit clause licenses its single contrapositive implication. A verifier checks every source pair and pivot, the two children at every branch node, consistent ancestor assignments, all simplified clauses, and both authenticated paths at each leaf. This is sound by resolution and the 2SAT SCC criterion; the generator finds all leaves by brute-force traversal and polynomial graph search. The complete tree has $2^{n-2}$ leaves and at most $2^{n-1}-1$ nodes; each node and its bounded-length witness uses at most polynomially many bits in $N$. No claim of polynomial size in $N$ follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Precisely separated consequences of universal certificate assumptions]\label{prop:gap}
Fix any polynomially sound, complete, uniformly decidable proof-checking grammar for UNSAT which accepts the above source mapping. If every UNSAT $F$ had some proof of length $\operatorname{poly}(|F|)$, then $NP=coNP$. If one deterministic \emph{polynomial-time} algorithm constructed accepted negative proofs on all UNSAT $F$ and returned correct SAT-side witnesses or labels on all SAT $F$, then $P=NP$. Neither implication has its converse established for the fixed 2CNF-SCC grammar.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A polynomial-length proof verified in polynomial time witnesses the coNP-complete language UNSAT in NP, yielding $coNP\subseteq NP$ and hence equality. A uniform polynomial producer for the entire answer partition decides arbitrary SAT by invoking the reduction $F\mapsto G_F$ and observing its correctly authenticated side. The second conclusion does not follow merely from polynomially bounded certificates, because searching for them is an independent computational task. The fixed-SCC grammar may demand much longer proofs than other proof systems.
\end{proof}

\section{Resource ledgers and precise open gap}
For $N=|F|$ in an unpadded explicit clause encoding, $G_F$ contains $2m$ clauses and has encoded length $O(N+ m\log(m+2))=\operatorname{poly}(N)$. Its construction, validity checking, source-ID authentication, and the output stream are uniform polynomial-time operations. On an indexed word RAM with $O(\log N)$-bit words, reading and preparing the explicit source is charged, not free. On one read/write tape, the initial input occupies $N$ tape cells and every input/head traversal and output write is charged; a direct naive polynomial simulation may cost more than the RAM routine. On two tapes with read-only input and work/output, input cells are not conflated with work cells, and repeat input traversals and work-cell accesses are charged separately. A fixed universal TM simulates each specified algorithm with its own charged polynomial overhead. No crossing-sequence bound is transferred from the one-tape model to two tapes or RAM.

A SAT-side positive witness uses $O(N)$ bits and is verifiable in polynomial time, but its deterministic \emph{discovery} is not granted. A negative source-authenticated proof in the supplied explicit tree grammar takes $2^{n-2}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ generator time, actual output time $\Omega(L)$, and verification time $\operatorname{poly}(N+L)$. A depth-first construction can keep polynomial auxiliary \emph{work} space when a distinct write-only output tape is allowed; the total printed certificate, or any retained copy of it on a writable tape, still occupies $L$ cells and is never omitted from the resource ledger. All rejected candidate paths and malformed-input timeouts are charged. The bare one-bit summary does not imply polynomial generator or verifier cost.

A sufficient constructive target remains a uniform answer-preserving closed-instance one-child shrinker with output bit length $\le N^\lambda$ for a fixed $\lambda<1$, explicit polynomial generation cost, and constant-size bases. This note supplies no such map on unrestricted SAT. It also supplies no universal superpolynomial RAM lower bound. Under the hypothetical $P=NP$, a SAT solver exponent remains an unknown $K$; no specific exponent is assumed.
