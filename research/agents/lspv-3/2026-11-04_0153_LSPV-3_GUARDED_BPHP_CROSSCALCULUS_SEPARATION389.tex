\section*{Fixed source, exact models, and published theorem}
For $\ell\ge1$, let $n=2^\ell$ and encode $n+1$ pigeons by $\ell$ data bits each. For each distinct pigeon pair $i<j$ and binary hole $a\in\{0,1\}^\ell$, let $D_{i,j,a}$ be the $2\ell$-literal clause that prohibits both pigeon words from equaling $a$. The original bit-pigeonhole CNF $B_\ell$ has $v=(n+1)\ell$ Boolean variables and
\[
m=n\binom{n+1}{2}\quad\text{clauses};\qquad
|\operatorname{enc}(B_\ell)|=N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)
\]
bits in a fully explicit signed binary-variable-ID or ASCII-DIMACS encoding. Its literal-occurrence count is only $\Theta(n^3\ell)$. Fix fresh guard $t$:
\[
G_\ell=\bigwedge_{i<j,a}(\neg t\lor D_{i,j,a}),
\qquad H_\ell=G_\ell\land(t).
\]
Thus $G_\ell$ is SAT by $t=0$, while $H_\ell$ is UNSAT.

The unchanged, preselected $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ uses zero-cost authenticated original CNF clauses and unit-cost distinct acyclic derived nodes of fan-in at most two: signed RES, exact local AND3, binary GF(2) XOR, verified LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative two-input DEF with all defining clause views attributed to its one paid node. The latter four operations are \emph{available}, but the upper proof below needs only RES/DEF. No claim is made that the entire LOCAL-CONVERT payload grammar has been independently authenticated.

A separate, extension-free system, unrestricted shared-DAG Res($\oplus$), reasons with disjunctions of affine equations, arbitrary complementary-affine pivots and semantic weakening, but no conservative extension axioms. $S_\oplus$ counts all distinct initial and derived proof lines. Braun's September 19, 2026 arXiv:2609.23015 Theorem 1.1 reports that for all $\ell\ge32$, every such DAG refutation of $B_\ell$ has
\[
S_\oplus(B_\ell)>\exp\left(\frac{2^\ell}{32768\ell^2}\right).
\]
This is a conditional use of an externally unreviewed preprint, not a deterministic machine-time lower bound. Haken's standard unary-PHP ordinary Resolution exponential bound and Cook's polynomial Extended Resolution PHP proofs are complementary established comparison models, not new lower bounds.

\begin{lemma}[Same-source deletion and literal-sign controls]
Every original signed source of $H_\ell$ is indispensable, and reversing the sign of any one of its $(2\ell+1)m+1$ signed literal occurrences yields SAT. Thus $H_\ell$ is clause-minimally UNSAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a chosen guarded clause associated with $(i,j,a)$, send pigeons $i,j$ to the same hole $a$ and biject the remaining $n-1$ pigeons to the other holes. Exactly the unguarded $D_{i,j,a}$ is false. Set $t=1$. Deleting that guarded clause makes every remaining source true. Reversing any one of its $2\ell$ data-literal signs or the guard sign makes that sole false clause true. The remaining source $(t)$ is indispensable and sign-sensitive because setting $t=0$ satisfies all guarded clauses once it is deleted or changed to $(\neg t)$. The pigeonhole principle itself proves UNSAT before these modifications.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Full mixed binary-ancestry source lower bound]
For the unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ and every $\ell\ge1$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_\ell)\ge m.}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every one of the $m+1$ indispensable sources must occur in the contradiction's ancestor DAG, since any proper source subset is SAT and all auxiliary definitions are total and conservative. If there are $q$ paid nodes, its underlying undirected ancestry graph is connected on $(m+1)+q$ vertices, requiring at least $m+q$ dependency edges. Each paid node has at most two incoming dependencies; hence there are at most $2q$ edges, yielding $q\ge m$. All adaptive orderings, shared DAGs, temporary growth and mixed rules remain included. Positive integer weights and RES completeness ensure an attained minimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform polynomial mixed proof, identical signed guarded source]
For all $\ell\ge2$, with $n=2^\ell$,
\[
\boxed{m\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_\ell)
\le O(n^4+n^3\ell^2).}
\]
The satisfiable $G_\ell$ additionally has a paid RES/DEF derivation of its implied unit $\neg t$ of the same polynomial order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
LSPV-3/346 constructs an explicit uniform, signed, source-authenticated RES/DEF refutation of $B_\ell$ with $O(n^4+n^3\ell^2)$ paid nodes. It introduces conservative Boolean prefix gates, derives unary pigeonhole axioms using strict Resolution, then runs an explicit paid Cook-style elimination with bounded nine-variable RES projectors. Full instances for $n=4,8,16$ were independently replayed in that earlier report; they are not reclassified as newly checked in this run.

Lift its shared DAG by adding $\neg t$ to exactly the nodes with original-source ancestry, while leaving all $t$-free gate definitions and definition-only nodes unchanged. Every RES pivot remains signed and legitimate, and shared node IDs are retained. The final contradiction becomes $\neg t$ because total conservative definitions cannot themselves refute. This is the LSPV-3/385 guard-tag transport, now applied without altering the source or the proof system. Finally append the authenticated source $(t)$ and one paid Resolution inference. The cost is the old polynomial plus one. Other legal mixed operations can only lower the global minimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional exponential output-size noncompilability]
Assume Braun's stated Res($\oplus$) theorem and the cofactor-restriction theorem of LSPV-3/388. For every $\ell\ge32$,
\[
\boxed{S_\oplus(H_\ell)>\exp\left(\frac{2^\ell}{32768\ell^2}\right),\qquad
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_\ell)=n^{O(1)}.}
\]
No compiler can produce explicit Res($\oplus$) refutations of these same-source $H_\ell$ from the above polynomially serialized mixed proof certificates with polynomial \emph{output-length} overhead, even if its search is nonuniform or computationally unbounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restrict any Res($\oplus$) refutation of $H_\ell$ to $t=1$. Its original guarded sources become the authentic $B_\ell$ source clauses, and $(t)$ becomes true. Every pivot remains affine; a constant pivot can be bypassed using the permitted semantic weakening, while all other RES and weakening nodes restrict without expansion. Shared proof ancestry is not unfolded. Hence $S_\oplus(B_\ell)\le S_\oplus(H_\ell)$, and Braun's premise supplies the first bound. The RES/DEF construction and fully explicit clause/parent/pivot/gate serializations have length $n^{O(1)}$, as does $N_\ell$. Since $\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))$ eventually exceeds every polynomial in $n$, no polynomial bound on required explicit Res($\oplus$) output length is possible. The theorem concerns required proof syntax and emitted evidence, not runtime of an arbitrary SAT decider or the existence of a compact alternative proof.
\end{proof}
