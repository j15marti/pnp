\section{Paid sparse-boundary composition theorem}
Suppose a fixed uniform source-aware procedure transforms every ordinary SAT input into exact component relations $\Sigma_i$ and cross relations $K_{ij}$ on a validated forest, with
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(F)\iff\exists(\alpha_i)_i\bigwedge_i[\alpha_i\in\Sigma_i]\wedge\bigwedge_{ij}K_{ij}(\alpha_i,\alpha_j).
\]
No relation, enumerator, decomposition or witness lift is free. Let $D(N)$ charge parsing, discovery, validation, all failed attempts, decomposition, signature generation and policy switching. Let $J(N)$ charge the complete exact join, source access, indexes, reconstruction when required and replay. Let $W(N)$ be the peak live workspace of the entire composed algorithm.

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT within the declared contract]
If $D(N)\le N^{a+o(1)}$, $J(N)\le N^{b+o(1)}$, $W(N)\le N^{s+o(1)}$, and $(\max(a,b),s)\in\mathcal B_*$, no all-input exact procedure can satisfy all these premises.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exact forest messages decide whether one globally compatible signature tuple exists. The source equivalence gives the correct root label on SAT and UNSAT inputs. The resulting single machine uses $D+J=N^{\max(a,b)+o(1)}$ time and $W$ space. A fixed witness $(c,\ell)$ supplies strict margins for FLMV's polylogarithmic completeness transfer, contradicting Theorem 5.7. The conclusion excludes the combined universal contract, not cheap joining on already supplied small relations.
\end{proof}

Write $R_i=|\Sigma_i|$, avoiding confusion with syntactic residual $r=n-d$. If there are $N^{\gamma+o(1)}$ components, $R_i\le N^{\alpha+o(1)}$, and each fully charged pair test costs $N^{\chi+o(1)}$, forest joining costs at most $N^{\gamma+2\alpha+\chi+o(1)}$. If a signature uses $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ bits, retaining signature lists and messages costs $N^{\gamma+\alpha+\lambda+o(1)}$. With sequential pair tests,
\[
t=\max\{a,\gamma+2\alpha+\chi\},\qquad
s=\max\{\sigma,\gamma+\alpha+\lambda\},
\]
where $\sigma$ includes every other simultaneously live source, generator, lift, reconstruction and ancestor object. Caching all pair matrices adds $\gamma+2\alpha$ to the space maximum. Retaining the entire source adds $1$. Full witnesses cannot be treated as constant-sized identifiers without a charged accessor.

\section{Streaming, recursion and adaptive policies}

C=\max\{a,\max_j\eta_j/(1-\theta_j)\},\qquad
