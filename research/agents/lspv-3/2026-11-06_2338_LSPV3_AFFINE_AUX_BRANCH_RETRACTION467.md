## Exact restricted theorem

Let E contain finite independently original-source-authenticated affine rows on X. Add g globally fresh acyclic **paid** XOR2 definitions T. Let z be an auxiliary output whose unique expansion is a **nonconstant** affine f(X). Suppose two acyclic, shared, source-authenticated **binary-XOR-only** refutation DAGs are supplied, one from E+T+(z=0), the other from E+T+(z=1), with q distinct paid XOR inferences in their **union**. Then there exists an E-only XOR refutation with at most q+1 paid XOR nodes, counting pre-established original-source conversions only once.

**Proof.** Substitute every gate output by its affine expansion. Each T definition maps to the zero row, and substitution commutes with XOR. Delete zero images, bypass neutral XOR nodes and memoize shared images (LSPV-3/466). The projected contextual assumptions are the complementary *affine equations* f(X)=0 and f(X)=1, not single literals. Apply the assumption-coefficient construction of LSPV-3/449 separately to each branch: its tagged projection derives either unconditional false or the complement of its assumption without increasing paid XOR nodes. If one branch yields unconditional false, that is a full E-refutation. Otherwise the branches yield opposite unconditional equations, which XOR to false with **one** additional paid node. Both AND cases are discharged. This proves the stated bound without extending historical R_mix341 with SPLIT, DISCHARGE or WEAKENING. QED.

This applies to affine gate definitions and XOR derivations **only**, assuming authenticated source-to-affine conversions. The historical fixed accepted-byte DEF export and LOCAL-CONVERT rule syntax remains underdetermined. No result about arbitrary mixed RES, AND3, Boolean DEF, nonlinear contexts or the B3 implication follows. A separate Gaussian-elimination upper-bound control gives at most m-1 XOR additions to refute an inconsistent source system of m affine rows, exclusive of paid source conversions; this is the standard affine shortcut, not a new lower bound.
