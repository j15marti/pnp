\section*{Published hard anchor and fixed grammar}
Let $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ be the standard unary pigeonhole CNF. Its $h(h+1)$ original variables $p_{ij}$ encode pigeons $i\in[h+1]$ and holes $j\in[h]$. Its original clauses are $h+1$ positive rows $\bigvee_{j=1}^h p_{ij}$ and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative pairwise collisions $\neg p_{ij}\lor\neg p_{kj}$. Thus $m_h=h+1+h\binom{h+1}{2}=\Theta(h^3)$ clauses and $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ explicit signed binary-index CNF bits. Haken (1985) proves that there are constants $c>0,h_0$ for which \emph{every} unrestricted ordinary shared-DAG Resolution refutation $\pi$ of $P_h$, for every $h\ge h_0$, contains at least $2^{ch}$ lines. Subtracting polynomially many original lines leaves $2^{\Omega(h)}$ paid derived nodes. This theorem is not about Res($\oplus$), Extended Resolution, $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, or all uniform SAT machines.

Cook (1976) supplies a \emph{uniform constructive} polynomial-size Extended Resolution refutation of these same $P_h$, using fresh acyclic conservative Boolean definitions and Resolution. A standard explicit upper envelope is $O(h^4)$ proof steps. Fix $R_{\mathrm{res}}$ as ordinary binary Resolution on the declared source clauses, one paid node per derived resolvent, with arbitrary shared subderivations and no extension rule. The separately fixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ is unchanged (LSPV-2/341, blob c87acd11): paid RES, AND3 with at most three old-variable support, binary GF(2) XOR, unary constant-support LOCAL-CONVERT, paid conservative two-input DEF, and no uncharged semantic oracle.

\section*{Hereditary source-conservativity}
\begin{lemma}
For every original CNF $F=(C_1,\ldots,C_m)$ on old variables $x$ and every acyclic family $D(x,y)$ of exact gate equations $y_i\leftrightarrow g_i(x,y_{<i})$ over fresh variables, encoded as full constant-width defining CNF, the following holds for every subset $S\subseteq[m]$ and every old assignment $a$:
\[
\bigwedge_{i\in S}C_i(a)=1
\iff
\exists b\;\bigl(D(a,b)\land\bigwedge_{i\in S}C_i(a)\bigr)=1.
\]
In fact $b$ is uniquely determined for every $a$, even when $S$ is empty.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate every fresh gate in its topological definition order. All defining clauses are exact, so every $a$ extends uniquely to a model of $D$. Original clauses use no fresh $y$. Projection therefore preserves the entire model set of \emph{each} original source subset, not just the global SAT label. Each old-source deletion SAT witness and each original signed literal flip that creates SAT remains a SAT witness after conservative extension.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform conservative-source recoding with exponential Resolution cost gap]
There are fixed uniform polynomial-time generators $h\mapsto E_h=P_h\cup D_h$ and $h\mapsto\pi_h$ and a polynomial $p$ such that every original clause of $P_h$ is retained \emph{verbatim} in $E_h$, $D_h$ consists only of acyclic conservative gate definitions with polynomially many explicitly encoded clauses, and
\[
\forall h\;\forall S\subseteq[m_h]:\quad
\operatorname{proj}_{x}\mathrm{Mod}(\{C_i:i\in S\}\cup D_h)
=\mathrm{Mod}(\{C_i:i\in S\}).
\]
Nevertheless, for all $h\ge h_0$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{res}}}(P_h)\ge 2^{ch}-m_h,\qquad
 W_{R_{\mathrm{res}}}(E_h)\le p(h).}
\]
$D_h$ is satisfiable for \emph{every} valuation of the original variables, and does not insert an empty clause, contradictory units, or an instance-specific answer. $E_h$ has polynomial bit length, conservatively $O(h^4\log(h+2))$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the polynomial uniform Cook Extended Resolution proof of $P_h$. Collect its acyclic fresh-variable definitions into $D_h$. Every defining clause is logically conservative and exact, so the lemma applies to all original source subsets independently of which ones the proof uses. Place these definitions as \emph{extra free source clauses in the new input} $E_h$. Replay the ordinary Resolution steps of Cook's proof unchanged: their premises are now either original sources, preloaded definition clauses, or earlier derived resolvents. This is a polynomial ordinary Resolution refutation of $E_h$. Fresh names and defining clauses can be generated uniformly in polynomial time from Cook's explicit construction; each has constant gate arity. Conversely Haken's published exponential bound applies to the original $P_h$ in the same ordinary Resolution proof grammar. No original source is deleted or syntactically altered.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Mandatory exponential reverse-output overhead]
There is no uniform translation that maps every short ordinary Resolution refutation of $E_h$ to an ordinary Resolution refutation of $P_h$ while bounding the output number of nodes by a polynomial of the input CNF bit length and input proof size. This fails even with unlimited computation allowed to select the output.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply such a translation to $\pi_h$. It would output polynomially many Resolution nodes contradicting Haken's $2^{ch}-m_h$ bound. This output-grammar obstruction is not an unrestricted SAT-time lower bound.
\end{proof}
