\section{Constructive polarity-anchor theorem}
Let F9 have positive triples $(156),(238),(135),(469),(247),(789)$ and negative supports $(468),(167),(234),(358),(129),(579)$. For $t\ge2$, $G_t$ has $t$ copies of F9, with every positive $(x_{1,i},x_{5,i},x_{6,i})$ replaced by $(x_{1,i},x_{5,i+1},x_{6,i})$ cyclically. It has $n=9t,m=12t$, is connected, and is a valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) source.
\begin{lemma}
The negative original-source support hypergraph has exactly $t$ connected nine-variable, six-negative-clause components. Every component has five internal positive clauses, exactly one outgoing positive $(2+1)$ cross clause and one incoming singleton. Their directed interaction graph is a single cycle.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All six F9 negative supports are unchanged, internally connected, and no negative clause crosses blocks. The one swapped positive clause gives one outgoing majority pair and one incoming singleton per block. The positive cross clauses connect the complete signed source. 
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}
The nine local names are uniquely recoverable using constant many source-incidence tests.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The incoming singleton is $x_5$; the outgoing pair is $\{x_1,x_6\}$. The outgoing member co-occurring internally with $x_5$ in a positive clause is uniquely $x_1$, and that clause $(135)$ identifies $x_3$. The negative $(167)$ identifies $x_7$. The other negatives on $x_1$ and $x_6$ supply $\{x_2,x_9\}$ and $\{x_4,x_8\}$. The internal positive clause $(469)$ identifies $x_9$ and $x_4$, leaving $x_2,x_8$. Validate every original clause and cross incidence. Up to cyclic rotation there is no labeling ambiguity.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}
On a connected monotone CNF, an independent per-variable sign complementation preserves original-clause monotonicity only if all variables are complemented or none are.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every clause all three complemented/not-complemented indicators must agree, otherwise their literal signs would mix. Source incidence connectivity propagates equality to every original variable.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}
There is a uniform deterministic source-authenticating recognizer which rejects malformed encodings, returns the correct labeled block cycle for every member of the above fully scrambled and grammar-preserving sign orbit, and returns \texttt{UNRESOLVED} for other valid sources without emitting false matches. With sorted adjacency lists, grammar/recognition costs $O(N\log N)$ word operations and $O(N)$ words of retained mapping/source data; native indexed-tape exponents have not been proved.
For any coherent original-variable pins $p$, let $M_i(a,b)$ count the eight private-variable assignments satisfying block $i$'s eleven internal original clauses and its outgoing cross clause, given $x_{5,i}=a$, $x_{5,i+1}=b$, respecting local pins. Then
\[
\boxed{\#SAT(F\mid p)=\operatorname{tr}\prod_{i=1}^t M_i(p).}
\]
Each matrix is acquired with at most $4\cdot2^8=1024$ local truth-table candidates. Replacing positive entries with one, multiplying Boolean matrices, and testing the diagonal is a sound decision-only SAT/UNSAT terminal. A positive trace permits a checked original-source witness; a zero trace with fully replayed local truth tables and source maps is a complete original-source UNSAT certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The negative-support components and outgoing/incoming positive cross incidences are invariant under source reindexing, allowing the two previous lemmas to reconstruct and verify all source triples. If the initial polarity fails, complement all literal signs and retry; the sign lemma exhausts target-valid per-variable sign gauges of connected $G_t$. Fixing the cyclic $x_5$ boundary values makes the eight-variable private domains disjoint. The number of global satisfying completions is the product of their local counts, summed over the two values of each boundary variable; this sum is exactly the matrix trace. All counts are nonnegative, so Boolean support preserves decision. Backtracking a positive boundary path gives a complete witness; replaying every zero-support local table and its product verifies complete UNSAT.
\end{proof}
