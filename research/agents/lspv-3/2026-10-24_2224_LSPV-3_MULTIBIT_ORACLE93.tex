\section{Receiving claim, falsifiers and source versions}
The load-bearing task is an all-input, one-fixed-uniform-machine, same-realization exact-SAT achievable-pair improvement, or a finite cofinal charged route into the established FLMV random-access time--space forbidden region. The full ordinary input is read-only indexed; work and address tapes are charged. The original ARC abstract region and \texttt{main13.tex} agree that restricted semantic joins do not supply this missing machine.

Three proposals were tested. First, can a connected source-coherent ordinary proper-3CNF have \(k\) independent source-dependent boundary answers rather than the one-bit correlated profiles of ROOT-HARD-PROJECTION129 and BOUNDARY-ORACLE-FACTOR128? \emph{Yes in a restricted image.} Second, can that image have \(k=N^{1/2+o(1)}\) fully serialized ordinary \(N^{1/2+o(1)}\)-size children with a correct two-level same-witness join? \emph{Yes for a canonical guarded-OR promise.} Third, does that imply an all-input ARC successor? \emph{No:} the proof transport loses its savings to a quadratic source-image stretch and arbitrary children do not retain the promise.

\section{Source-coherent guarded binary-trie compiler}
For \(k=2^h\ge2\) and arbitrary pairwise-variable-disjoint nonempty proper-3CNFs \(F_0,\ldots,F_{k-1}\), use address bits \(z_0,\ldots,z_{h-1}\). A functional binary trie with \(2k-2\) gate variables enforces, for each prefix \(w\),
\[
p_{wb}\longleftrightarrow p_w\wedge[z_j=b],\quad b\in\{0,1\};
\]
at depth one the parent is the constant true gate. Standard proper-3CNF Tseitin gadgets encode all binary-gate equalities. A two-literal clause \((u\vee v)\) is encoded by
\((u\vee v\vee a)\wedge(u\vee v\vee\neg a)\)
with fresh \(a\). Thus every address has exactly one true leaf \(g_i\).

For each source clause \(C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)\) in \(F_i\), introduce fresh \(a_C\) and emit
\[
(\neg g_i\vee\ell_1\vee a_C)
\wedge(\neg a_C\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3).\tag{1}
\]
Define \(H(F_0,\ldots,F_{k-1})\) to be the decoder and all guarded clauses. Every clause has three distinct literal-variable IDs, all source variables appear and the incidence structure is connected even if the individual \(F_i\) are disconnected: all guarded clauses share their leaf \(g_i\) and all leaves are tied by the trie. Source names inside each core are injectively preserved.

\begin{lemma}[Local exact Boolean elimination]
For all Boolean \(g,\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3\),
\[
\exists a_C[(\neg g\vee\ell_1\vee a_C)\wedge
(\neg a_C\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)]
\equiv \neg g\vee\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If \(g=0\), take \(a_C=0\). If \(g=1\), take
\(a_C=0\) when \(\ell_1=1\); otherwise the first clause forces
\(a_C=1\) and the second requires \(\ell_2\vee\ell_3=1\).
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Independent pinned SAT profile; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every legal \(k\)-tuple of cores and every address \(i\),
\[
\boxed{\SAT(H[z=i])=\SAT(F_i)},\qquad
\boxed{\SAT(H)=\bigvee_{i=0}^{k-1}\SAT(F_i)}.\tag{2}
\]
Across same-size connected proper-3CNF source tuples with identical unsigned supports, the complete pinned-answer vector attains every point of \(\{0,1\}^k\); hence it has \(2^k\) possible values, in contrast with the two-value profile \(C(z)\wedge\SAT(F)\) of BOUNDARY-ORACLE-FACTOR128.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
With \(z=i\), the decoder makes only leaf \(g_i\) true. The lemma reduces its guarded clauses exactly to \(F_i\), while unselected cores extend with every \(a_C=0\). Existential choice of \(z\) gives the root OR. To realize every vector, choose each source core independently from equal-size SAT and UNSAT members of the cubic-prism parity family. The even-charge source has 16 edge assignments; one odd charge gives zero. Each has nine edge variables, 24 proper signed ternaries, the same unsigned supports and fixed-record length. Source tuple choices independently determine the \(k\) pinned labels.
\end{proof}
In the \emph{restricted black-box model} where a controller learns each source SAT status only through individual pinned queries, deterministic exact root-OR query depth is \(k\): on the all-zero vector, leaving even one coordinate unread is indistinguishable from flipping that coordinate to one. This is not an unrestricted SAT lower bound. One ordinary whole-root SAT query, a source-aware recognizer or a different representation can bypass \(k\) separate membership queries. The full pinned profile is a stronger output contract than a one-bit root decision.

\section{Exact child lengths and the restricted favorable recurrence}
For \(k=2^h\), pad each prism source by \(p=k\) fresh-variable clauses \((x_{i,1}\vee u_{ij}\vee v_{ij})\). Each is satisfiability-neutral and connected to the prism source. The decoder has exactly \(2k-2\) gate bits, \(4k-4\) binary-padding bits and \(10k-12\) ternary clauses. Every guarded source clause contributes one auxiliary and two proper ternaries. The complete parent has
\[
\boxed{n=h+39k-6+3k^2,\quad m=58k-12+2k^2.}\tag{3}
\]
Each independently serialized original selected child has \(n_c=9+2k\) variables and \(m_c=24+k\) clauses. Under the fixed-width representation
\[
w(n,m)=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil,\qquad
N=(4m+2)w(n,m),
\]
the parent size is \(\Theta(k^2\log k)\), each child size
\(\Theta(k\log k)=N^{1/2+o(1)}\), and there are
\(k=N^{1/2+o(1)}\) independently readable children.
A source-checkable canonical parser can validate the decoder,
extract the original records for each ordinary child, serialize its
actual header, call any fixed uniform all-input SAT solver sequentially,
OR the statuses and discard the child. Every source read, child bit,
auxiliary address, failed check, retained metadata and live buffer is charged.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional uniform \emph{promise} wrapper and two levels]
If one fixed exact all-input ordinary SAT machine has simultaneous
time/space exponents \((t,s)\), the uniform promised-image selector
above has the simultaneous upper bounds
\[
t'\le\max\{1,\tfrac12+\tfrac t2\},\qquad
s'\le\max\{\tfrac12,\tfrac s2\},\tag{4}
\]
up to \(N^{o(1)}\) overhead in the indexed bit-Turing model.
The grammar is syntactically hereditary for any fixed depth:
\[
\SAT(H(H(F_{11},\ldots),H(F_{21},\ldots),\ldots))
=\bigvee_{i,j}\SAT(F_{ij}).\tag{5}
\]
By balancing bottom cores of size \(\Theta(k)\) clauses, \(k\) middle
branches, and \(k^2\) outer branches, each of the first two semantic
levels has genuine \(1/2+o(1)\)-power smaller ordinary children.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (2) supplies correctness for SAT and UNSAT. Parsing and writing
all \(k\) ordinary children takes \(N^{1+o(1)}\) bit work; running them costs
\(kN^{t/2+o(1)}\). Only one child, one solver, and the validated lookup
table are live, giving the displayed simultaneous resource bounds.
Equation (5) is repeated substitution of (2), with fresh disjoint variable
copies and actual gate clauses. This proves only a \emph{restricted promised
source} transformation, not a point in the full SAT achievable region \(E\).
\end{proof}

\section{Quadratic transport firewall and ARC gap}
To embed arbitrary ordinary \(F\) with \(q\) source clauses, choose a power
of two \(k\in[q,2q)\), put \(F\) in one core, and use \(k-1\) padded
UNSAT prism cores. Pad every core to \(\Theta(q)\) clauses with
satisfiability-neutral fresh variables. Then
\(\SAT(H)=\SAT(F)\), but construction materializes \(\Theta(q^2)\)
proper clauses and has \(N_H=N_F^{2+o(1)}\) bits, whereas ordinary
children retain source-scale length \(N_F^{1+o(1)}\).
The promised wrapper (4), composed with this reduction, gives at best
\[
T_F(N_F)\le N_F^{\max\{2,\,1+t\}+o(1)}.\tag{6}
\]
For \(t\ge1\) this is not an exponent contraction. Recursively creating
fresh padded children does not repair the source stretch, and a general
fallback retains the original worst-case exponent. The construction
is NP-hard as a promised image but cannot invoke FLMV's forbidden
ordinary-SAT region for its \emph{restricted} algorithm. Thus
independent semantic bits, genuine ordinary child shrinkage,
connected proper-3CNF source and fixed-depth hereditary syntax
\emph{still do not imply} an all-input ARC successor.
