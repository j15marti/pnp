\section*{LSPV-3: quantitative reconstruction-collapse audit QREC1}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. No unrestricted SAT lower bound is proved here.

\paragraph{Verified state and predecessor preflight.}
Repository \texttt{AGENTS.md}, \texttt{CODEX.md}, the \texttt{pnp-research} skill, root \texttt{main.tex}, MAIN 0600, newest verified LSPV-1 ENCSTOP1, LSPV-2 FEAT1, LSPV-3 FIXCOIN1, LSPV-4 KMIN1, and relevant GROK/CLAUDE material were reviewed. \paragraph{Two current obstacles.}
(1) The fixed-coin reconstruction still requires exact truth values for SAT queries, including NO answers.  (2) After eager elimination of those reconstruction SAT calls, Viola gives a near-linear $\Sigma_3$ predicate, but no model-matched theorem currently compiles that three-alternation predicate to SAT with a quantitatively cheaper exponent than recursive use of the assumed SAT decider.

\paragraph{Primary literature boundary.}
van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski (Computational Complexity 34:15, 2025) is the source for the resilient reconstruction architecture. Viola (ECCC TR05-137, 2005) proves $\mathrm{BPTime}(t)\subseteq\Sigma_3\mathrm{Time}(t\operatorname{polylog}t)$ and explicitly states that this inclusion relativizes. Stockmeyer (TCS 3, 1976) supplies the polynomial-hierarchy framework. Robson (TCS 82, 1991) gives an $O(T\log T)$ RAM-to-SAT reduction for his RAM model. These results do not supply a free quantitative collapse of a $\Sigma_3$ computation to one SAT instance.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: fixed-$Q$ reconstruction predicate.}
Fix one acquired and exactly size-normalized deterministic predicate $Q$, coordinate $i$, and all Arthur randomness $r$. Let $J$ be the constant set of density-estimation queries together with the success, output-zero, and output-one SAT questions. For query $j$, write $\Phi_j(Y_j;z,Q,i,r)$ and proposed truth bit $a_j$. Then exact answers are expressed by
\[
\operatorname{ExactSAT}(a)=\exists Y^+\ \forall Y^-\!
\bigwedge_{j\in J}\left[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))\right].
\]
All YES witnesses merge into one existential block and all NO conditions into one universal block because $J$ is constant and parallel. Parallelism does not delete the universal block.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: NO answers do not create a second SAT exponent under eager elimination.}
Assume a deterministic SAT decider $A$ runs in time $S^{k+o(1)}$. The same machine decides UNSAT in the same time by complementing its output. Therefore a constant vector of reconstruction SAT truth values, including both YES and NO answers, costs only a constant number of calls to $A$, hence one exponent factor $k$, not separate YES and NO factors. If a reconstruction query compiled from an $L$-time computation has size $L^{b_0+o(1)}$, the eager reconstruction-oracle cost is $L^{b_0k+o(1)}$.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: direct-compilation barrier.}
Let $\mathrm{QSAT}_3$ denote a standard $\Sigma_3^P$-complete quantified Boolean problem. If there were an unconditional uniform polynomial-time many-one map $f$ such that
\[
x\in\mathrm{QSAT}_3\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(x)\in\mathrm{SAT},
\]
then $\Sigma_3^P\subseteq\mathrm{NP}$, and hence $\Sigma_3^P=\mathrm{NP}$. Thus a syntax-only polynomial direct compiler from arbitrary $\Sigma_3$ predicates to SAT is itself a polynomial-hierarchy collapse theorem. No such unconditional theorem may be inserted into the reconstruction ledger.

Under the counterfactual $P=NP$, a polynomial reduction from $\mathrm{QSAT}_3$ to SAT exists at the class level because PH collapses to P. This does \emph{not} provide a free quantitative compiler. A trivial construction can first decide the $\mathrm{QSAT}_3$ instance and then output a fixed satisfiable or unsatisfiable formula; that construction has already paid the cost of the collapse it is supposed to save. Therefore any claimed improvement from $k^4$ to $k^3$ via direct compilation must exhibit an explicit compiler and charge its dependence on $A$.

\paragraph{CONDITIONAL quantitative recurrence.}
Let $\alpha_0$ be the pre-oracle reconstruction exponent and let $b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3$ be the model-specific computation-to-SAT encoding exponents at the reconstruction layer and three subsequent collapse layers. Eager reconstruction-oracle elimination gives
\[
\alpha_{\rm BP}\le \alpha_0 b_0 k+o(1).
\]
Applying Viola then leaves a $\Sigma_3$ computation. The conservative recursive elimination ledger is
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm det}\le \alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1).}
\]
This is \textbf{CONDITIONAL}; it is not a necessity theorem. Robson supports $b_q=1$ only after the actual machine at stage $q$ is matched to his RAM hypotheses. The shorthand $\alpha_0k^4$ remains unjustified until all four model matches are proved.

\paragraph{Late-substitution quantifier audit.}
If SAT answers are represented logically rather than eagerly evaluated, the unresolved dependency has the schematic form
\[
\boxed{\exists\sigma\ \forall u\ \exists(\ell,a,Y^+)\ \forall Y^-\ R.}
\]
The SAT instances inside $Y^-$ can depend on the later Viola selector $\ell$. Commuting $Y^-$ before $\ell$ is therefore not a valid generic quantifier manipulation. Eager evaluation and late substitution describe the same obstruction in different accounting forms.

\paragraph{Separate resource charges.}
The run keeps the ledger
\[
\tau_{\rm total}=\tau_Q+\tau_{\rm norm}+\tau_{\rm amp}+\tau_{\rm idx}+\tau_{\rm compile}+\tau_{\rm rec}+\tau_{\rm census}+\tau_{\rm write}+\tau_{\rm transport}.
\]
Existence of $Q$ is not acquisition; sparsity is not exact size normalization; per-bit reconstruction is not whole-output census/writing. TransportCert is only an accounting/hostile interface and TC4 foreign-algorithm escape remains OPEN.

\paragraph{Collision binding and post-quantum toolkit.}
Schur-square shortening/puncturing and quadratic-relation spaces are \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFERS} for the declared code objects, including LSPV-2 FEAT1's encoder-square ceiling. They do not alter the reconstruction quantifiers. Hilbert functions, syzygies, and graded Betti numbers are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} for finding structure in an explicitly arithmetized fixed-coin acceptance relation. Collision entropy is an exact image-size accounting identity but is not opposite-output binding. Special-soundness-style collision extraction remains \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}: it would become useful only if two conflicting valid vMS openings forced an explicit locally checkable forbidden algebraic witness. Collapse-binding, LWE hardness, and random-oracle security are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} as unconditional proof steps. An LWE-style external black-box reduction architecture remains a \textbf{CANDIDATE} only if an unconditional, model-preserving reduction is actually proved.

\paragraph{Cross-domain barrier-transfer audit.}
Thompson-style VLSI area--time bounds are exact within geometric chip models, but routing/area hypotheses are absent from unrestricted RAM SAT, so this is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}. Control-theoretic barrier certificates exactly turn a local invariant into global safety for a declared dynamical system, but no theorem forces an arbitrary SAT decider to follow that dynamics, so this is also a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}. Coding-theoretic distance and local-test mechanisms are exact for their code/test models but need a construction-specific fixed-coin vMS bridge. Proof-complexity certificates can refute explicit algebraic systems, but certificate existence, acquisition, verification, and unrestricted decision time remain distinct; no proof-system lower bound is promoted to a SAT runtime lower bound.

\paragraph{Relativization, algebrization, SAME\_REALIZATION, and hostile controls.}
Viola's stated simulation relativizes, so it cannot itself be the nonrelativizing hinge. The reconstruction predicate and Schur/Hilbert objects are extensional/algebraic and do not by themselves cross algebrization. The same acquired normalized $Q$ must serve all Arthur strings, coordinates, and SAT questions; replacing it by $Q_{r,i}$ is a quantifier inversion. Maximal semantic oracle, algebraic oracle, DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, padding, renaming, Horn/2-SAT, affine/Tseitin, and bounded-treewidth controls remain active. TC4 remains OPEN.

\paragraph{Strict status ledger.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT:} exact YES/NO predicate; constant eager SAT-answer vector costs one $k$ factor; unconditional polynomial direct compilation $\mathrm{QSAT}_3\le_m^P\mathrm{SAT}$ would imply $\Sigma_3^P=\mathrm{NP}$. 
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} Viola's near-linear $\Sigma_3$ simulation; Stockmeyer's PH framework; Robson's model-specific $O(T\log T)$ RAM-to-SAT reduction. 
\textbf{CONDITIONAL:} $\alpha_{\rm det}\le\alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1)$. 
\textbf{OPEN:} four-stage model matching; a cheaper explicit $A$-dependent compiler for the structured post-Viola predicate; fixed-coin local collision impossibility; $Q$ acquisition/normalization; nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing transfer; TC4.
