\section{Exact counterexample to the fixed-prime implementation}
EQSPECTRAL1 derives for a Fourier mode $z$ of the $3r$-block ring the
$2\times2$ determinant
\[
 d(z)=182+35(z+z^{-1}).                                 \tag{2}
\]
Over $\mathbb C$, $|z|=1$ gives $d(z)\ge112$, so
$A(N_r)+3I$ is nonsingular.  This proof is correct.

The executable instead computes rank modulo $p=1{,}000{,}003$.  Multiplying
(2) by $z$ gives
\[
 35z^2+182z+35=(5z+1)(7z+35).                           \tag{3}
\]
Thus $z_1=-5$ and $z_2=-1/5$ are roots over every field whose characteristic
is not $5$ or $7$.  For the implemented prime,
\[
 p-1=2\cdot3\cdot166667,qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_p(z_1)=\operatorname{ord}_p(z_2)=500001. \tag{4}
\]
The bounded audit verifies (4) by modular exponentiation: for $z_1=999998$,
$z_1^{500001}=1$, while $z_1^{166667}=499501$ and $z_1^3=999878$; the
inverse root has the same order.  Therefore the first ring length $3r$ that
contains these modes is
\[
 r_0=166667,\qquad 3r_0=500001.                         \tag{5}
\]

For $z=z_1$, the Fourier endpoint coefficients $(X,Y)=(5,2)$ satisfy
\[
 16X+(-5+7z)Y=0,qquad
 (-5+7z^{-1})X+16Y=0\pmod p.
\]
For $z=z_2$, $(X,Y)=(2,5)$ does likewise.  Restoring each block's five equal
core coordinates by $q=-(X+Y)/7$ gives two kernel vectors of
$A(N_{r_0})+3I$ over $\mathbb F_p$.  They are independent already at block
zero because
\[
 \det\begin{pmatrix}5&2\\2&5\end{pmatrix}=21\not\equiv0\pmod p.
\]
Hence the program sees modular nullity at least two and executes its branch
\texttt{return 1}.  But every switched $K_7$ block retains a $K_4$, so
$N_{r_0}$ is not 3-colourable and its promise label is $0$.
