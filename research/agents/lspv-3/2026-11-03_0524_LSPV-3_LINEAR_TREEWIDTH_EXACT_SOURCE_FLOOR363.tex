\paragraph{Construction.}

\[

\begin{lemma}[Path selector exact source floor]
For \emph{every} $n\ge2$ and every pair of Hamiltonian paths $H,V$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F(H,V))=2n+1=m-1.}
\]
Moreover $F(H,V)$ is clause-minimally UNSAT, every signed source occurrence is sign-sensitive, and $\{b\}$ is a size-one strong Horn backdoor.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At $b=0$ the first guarded path reduces to the contradictory Horn implication chain $U(H)$ and every second-path clause is satisfied; at $b=1$ the roles reverse. For any source at local position $j\in\{0,\ldots,n\}$ in one chosen active path $P$, assign $b$ to deactivate the other path and assign $x_{p_i}=1$ exactly for $i<j$. For $j=0$, all $x$ are zero and only the positive first unit is false; for $j=n$, all are one and only the final negative unit is false; otherwise precisely the $j$-th implication is false. This is a satisfying witness for source deletion, and flipping any one signed occurrence in that source makes it satisfied with all other sources unchanged. Thus all $m$ sources are indispensable.
Every sound conservative at-most-binary-premise contradiction DAG must contain all indispensable source nodes in sink ancestry. If its ancestry has $q$ paid nodes, its connected underlying graph contains at least $m+q-1$ premise edges, at most $2q$, hence $q\ge m-1$. Conversely resolve along the first path to derive $b$ in $n$ RES steps, similarly derive $\neg b$ from the other path in $n$ RES steps and resolve them to the empty clause in one further step. This authentic $2n+1$-node Resolution tree attains the bound. Both assignments of $b$ leave a Horn chain. The formula before assignment is non-Horn because it contains $b\lor x_{h_1}$, so the backdoor is minimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Linear-primal-treewidth family, exact minimum]
There are constants $n_0,c>0$ and an effective (not claimed polynomial-time) choice of two Hamiltonian paths $(H_n,V_n)$ for each even $n\ge n_0$ such that
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{tw}(\operatorname{primal}F(H_n,V_n))\ge n/120-2,\qquad W_R(F(H_n,V_n))=2n+1.}
\]
Under the exact decimal ASCII-DIMACS writer used in the finite controls,
\[
N(n)=8\!\left(
\big|\texttt{p cnf }\operatorname{dec}(n+1)\texttt{ }\operatorname{dec}(2n+2)\texttt{\textbackslash n}\big|
+2(n+1)d(n+1)+4\sum_{i=1}^{n}d(i)+13n+7
\right)=\Theta(n\log(n+1)),
\]
where $d(i)$ is the number of decimal digits of $i$. Consequently the primal treewidth is $\Omega(N/\log N)$, essentially the largest asymptotic order possible for bounded-width formulas with $O(n)$ clauses, yet the exact mixed proof cost is $O(N)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Kim--Wormald [KW] prove that a uniformly random simple 4-regular graph on $n$ vertices, $n$ even, admits a decomposition into two edge-disjoint Hamiltonian cycles with probability tending to one. Independently, Friedman's random-regular spectral theorem [Fr] gives second adjacency eigenvalue at most $3.8$ with probability tending to one: $2\sqrt3<3.8<4$. Intersect these two high-probability events. By the standard regular-graph Cheeger lower inequality, the resulting 4-regular $G_n$ has $|\partial_G A|\ge (4-3.8)|A|/2=0.1|A|$ for all $|A|\le n/2$. Delete one edge from each Hamiltonian cycle; the remaining edge-disjoint Hamiltonian paths are $H_n,V_n$. Their union $J_n$ has $|\partial_J A|\ge0.1\min(|A|,n-|A|)-2$ for all $A$.
For any tree decomposition of $J_n$ of width $k$, a standard balanced separator $S$ of at most $k+1$ vertices leaves components of size at most $n/2$. If $|S|\ge n/6$ there is already a linear bound. Otherwise, combine components into $A$ of size between $n/3$ and $2n/3$ (select one such component if available, or greedily accumulate components individually smaller than $n/3$). All $J_n$-edges leaving $A$ end in $S$, hence $0.1n/3-2\le|\partial_J A|\le4|S|$. Therefore $k+1\ge n/120-1/2$, implying $k\ge n/120-2$. Each path edge co-occurs in an implication source, so the primal graph contains $J_n$ and has at least its treewidth.
For effective choice one may lexicographically enumerate labelled pairs $(H,V)$ on $[n]$ and select the first satisfying the finite, exhaustive edge-cut condition $|\partial_{H\cup V}A|\ge0.1\min(|A|,n-|A|)-2$; existence for sufficiently large even $n$ has just been established. This algorithm may be exponential and is not a hidden polynomial-time graph generator or semantic decision oracle. The signed source writer has $2(n+1)$ occurrences of guard ID $n+1$, four occurrences of each $1\le i\le n$, $3n+1$ minus signs, $6n+2$ spaces and $2n+2$ \texttt{0\textbackslash n} terminators, giving the displayed exact bit length.
\end{proof}
