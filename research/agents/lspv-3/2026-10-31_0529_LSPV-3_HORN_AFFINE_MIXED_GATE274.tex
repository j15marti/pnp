\section{Exact source-to-mixed reduction}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}(\ell_{j1}\lor\ell_{j2}\lor\ell_{j3})$ be an arbitrary \emph{proper} original 3CNF with $n$ variables, including both SAT and UNSAT inputs. Introduce for each signed source occurrence $(j,k)$ private $y_{jk}$ and $t_{jk}$, using $6m$ fresh variables. Define $b_{jk}=1$ when $\ell_{jk}=x_i$ and $b_{jk}=0$ when $\ell_{jk}=\neg x_i$. Set
\[
 H_j=(\neg y_{j1}\lor\neg y_{j2}\lor\neg y_{j3}),\qquad
 E_{jk}: y_{jk}\oplus x_i=b_{jk}.
\]
For $b=0$ the two binary clauses encoding $E$ are $(\neg x_i\lor y)\land(x_i\lor\neg y)$; for $b=1$ they are $(x_i\lor y)\land(\neg x_i\lor\neg y)$. Replace each binary clause $D$ by \emph{both} $(D\lor t_{jk})$ and $(D\lor\neg t_{jk})$. Thus each $E_{jk}$ is encoded by exactly four proper ternary clauses and exactly equals its declared affine equation, independently of $t_{jk}$. Let $E$ be the conjunction of all $3m$ authenticated four-clause blocks and
\[
 T(F)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}H_j\ \wedge\ E.
\]
The first $m$ proper clauses form a Horn CNF, and the next $12m$ proper clauses define an affine relation, despite not themselves being syntactically Horn. Every $T(F)$ has $n+6m$ variables and $13m$ proper ternary original clauses. The canonical parser checks \emph{every signed source clause}, not merely metadata or a guessed projector; a malformed/noncanonical source must invoke complete fallback.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit NP-complete join with SAT individual factors]
For every proper original 3CNF $F$,
\[
 \exists (y,t)\,T(F)(x,y,t)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ F(x)
 \quad\text{for every complete }x,
 \qquad \#\operatorname{SAT}(T(F))=2^{3m}\#\operatorname{SAT}(F).
\]
Both component formulas $H$ and $E$ are individually satisfiable for \emph{every} $F$, including when $T(F)$ is UNSAT. The decision problem restricted to such canonically mixed proper-3CNF inputs is NP-complete under a uniform polynomial-time many-one reduction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each affine equation fixes $y_{jk}$ uniquely to the falsity indicator of the source literal $\ell_{jk}$. The two signed-$t$ copies of each binary clause conjoin to that binary clause, and $t_{jk}$ remains unrestricted. Under these unique $y$ values, $H_j$ says that not all three original literals are false, which is precisely $C_j(x)$. Therefore each satisfying $x$ extends to one $y$ tuple and all $2^{3m}$ independent $t$ choices, and an unsatisfying $x$ has no extension. $H$ alone has the witness $y_{jk}=0$, and $E$ alone has a witness for any $x$ by assigning its forced $y$ and arbitrary $t$. Membership in NP is immediate. Since proper 3-SAT is NP-complete and $T$ has $13m$ clauses and can be emitted in polynomial time, the image-restricted decision problem is NP-hard; both labels are preserved. This is a source-aware reduction, not a proof of $P\ne NP$.
\end{proof}
