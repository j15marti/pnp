\section{Single target and falsification mechanisms fixed before literature search}
One original ordinary CNF source $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}C_i$, exact original clause IDs and variable names. We study an explicitly \emph{source-subset supported} adaptive UNSAT certificate: a complete decision-branch tree on original variables, with each leaf carrying a set $I_\ell\subseteq[m]$ and a checked sound UNSAT justification of the ordinary restricted formula $\bigwedge_{i\in I_\ell}C_i|_{\sigma_\ell}$. A leaf can use a source-verified empty clause, resolution, cutting planes, polynomial calculus, affine cancellation, or a directly executed exact call to the same fixed uniform total SAT decider $A$. The terminal's provenance must include \emph{all} source axioms used and the complete current branch assignment; free UNSAT labels or private-source renaming are forbidden. Soundness does not require the verifier to use a particular proof system. This scoped grammar deliberately does not include arbitrary equisatisfiable rewritten children or unconstrained boundary JOIN DAGs without a source-exact tree unfolding.

Four testable mechanisms were fixed before the primary-paper check:
\begin{description}
\item[] H1: Clause-essential private witnesses force an exact conservation law $\sum_\ell|I_\ell\cap E(F)|\ge|E(F)|$, even with heterogeneous sound leaf proof systems. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}.
\item[] H2: Any accepted source-supported complete tree of height $h$, with $|I_\ell|\le K$ for each terminal, satisfies $K2^h\ge|E(F)|$. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}.
\item[] H3: Clause-essentiality alone forces near-boundary $N^{1.4008}$ verification volume, or prevents a short algebraic leaf. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: $|E(F)|\le m=O(N)$ and odd Tseitin admits one degree-three GF(2) terminal citing the entire source.
\item[] H4: For odd $K_4$ Tseitin, the lower bound $L\ge16$ on pure source-clause decision-tree leaves is tight. \textbf{FALSIFIED finite}: exact branch-state minimax gives $L=28$ and depth six.
\end{description}

\section{Typed original-source private witness theorem}
For UNSAT $F$ let the essential original clause set be
\[
E(F)=\{i\in[m]:F\setminus\{C_i\}\text{ is SAT}\}.
\]
For every $i\in E(F)$ choose a \emph{private witness} $\alpha_i$ satisfying every original clause except $C_i$. This necessarily falsifies $C_i$, because $F$ is UNSAT. Witnesses for distinct $i$ are distinct. An accepted tree $T$ branches on unassigned \emph{original} variables, with both Boolean branches required, and a leaf $\ell$ at partial assignment $\sigma_\ell$ certifies UNSAT of $F[I_\ell]|_{\sigma_\ell}$; its complete proof-system input and original source-axiom provenance must be checked. All source subsets and branches remain genuine ordinary encoded SAT inputs when handed to $A$, including charged child headers, source reads and guest bit probes.

\begin{theorem}[Private-witness terminal support conservation]\label{th:mass}
For every UNSAT ordinary CNF $F$, every accepted complete typed source-supported tree $T$, and every $i\in E(F)$, follow the unique branch decisions of $\alpha_i$ to its terminal $\ell(i)$. Necessarily $i\in I_{\ell(i)}$. Consequently
\[
\boxed{E(F)\subseteq\bigcup_{\ell\in\mathrm{Leaves}(T)}I_\ell,\qquad
\sum_{\ell\in\mathrm{Leaves}(T)}|I_\ell\cap E(F)|\ge |E(F)|.}
\]
The assertion remains true if individual terminals switch between exact source-supported CP, PC, resolution, affine and fixed-$A$ UNSAT checks; no upper bound on their acquisition or checker time follows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $i\in E(F)$ and its private witness $\alpha_i$. A complete original-variable decision tree has exactly one leaf consistent with $\alpha_i$, denoted $\ell(i)$. Suppose $i\notin I_{\ell(i)}$. Since $\alpha_i$ satisfies every source clause with identifier other than $i$, its restriction to unassigned variables extends $\sigma_{\ell(i)}$ to a satisfying assignment of $F[I_{\ell(i)}]|_{\sigma_{\ell(i)}}$. This contradicts the leaf's independently checked exact UNSAT semantics. Hence $i\in I_{\ell(i)}$. Summing over $i$ gives the support-incidence inequality. The argument uses no assumption about the internal derivation other than soundness from its \emph{complete} listed original source-axiom support.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Branch-depth versus maximum source support]\label{co:depth}
Let $L$ denote the number of leaves, $h$ the maximum decision-branch depth, and $K=\max_\ell|I_\ell|$. When $E(F)\ne\varnothing$,
\[
\boxed{\quad |E(F)|\le \sum_\ell|I_\ell|\le KL\le K2^h,\quad
h\ge\left\lceil\log_2\frac{|E(F)|}{K}\right\rceil
\text{ whenever }K<|E(F)|.\quad}
\]
For source-clause falsification terminals $K=1$, so $L\ge|E(F)|$ and every explicit full binary tree requires at least $2|E(F)|-1$ nodes. These are \emph{combinatorial proof-tree bounds}, not unconditional deterministic SAT time lower bounds. If a terminal source clause list is represented implicitly, support mass is \emph{not} automatically an equal number of writable bits; if actual child clause bodies and proofs are generated, all their bits, probes and peak memory must be charged separately.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{th:mass} proves the first inequality. Every leaf support has at most $K$ indices, and a binary tree of height $h$ has at most $2^h$ leaves. A full binary tree with $L$ leaves has $2L-1$ nodes.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ application.}
For a proper three-distinct-variable signed exact-$(2,2)$ formula with $n$ original names, $m=4n/3$ by counting $4n=3m$ literal occurrences. If this source is minimally UNSAT, then $|E(F)|=m=4n/3$ and the theorem applies without claiming that the \emph{restricted children} retain exact-$(2,2)$ balance. The 15-name, 20-clause $W_1$ source cited in PCRETRACT1 and the prior CLAUSE-CRITICALITY125 audit is an explicit such instance. For terminals of maximum support $K$, $h\ge\log_2(20/K)$ on this source. Replacing one original name by a fresh accidental alias invalidates the witness-to-source index correspondence.

\section{Affinely easy clause-critical controls and exact finite optimality}
For a connected cubic graph $G$ with odd total vertex charge, the ordinary Tseitin 3CNF has four named three-literal clauses per vertex and is UNSAT. The previously proved CLAUSE-CRITICALITY125 theorem gives individual clause essentiality precisely for 2-vertex-connected $G$; we adopt rather than reprove that graph criterion. For odd $K_4$, $|E(F)|=16$, and for odd $K_{3,3}$, $|E(F)|=24$. These sources are \emph{not} strict exact-$(2,2)$: each edge appears four positive and four negative times. A single source-audited GF(2) polynomial-calculus terminal obtains each vertex parity equation by summing its four falsifying-clause polynomials and sums all vertices to $1=0$. It has degree at most three and O$(|V|)$ displayed additions, but explicitly depends on all source clause IDs. Thus a one-terminal affine proof \emph{satisfies} Theorem~\ref{th:mass} with $K=m$, and falsifies any assertion that clause criticality alone imposes many proof terminals.

On the \emph{pure} binary decision-branch plus original-falsified-clause-leaf grammar, let $D(\sigma)$ be the minimum leaves needed to refute an ordinary residual $F|_\sigma$. The exact finite recurrence is
\[
D(\sigma)=
\begin{cases}
1,&\text{some original clause is already falsified},\\
\displaystyle\min_{x\notin\operatorname{dom}\sigma}
[D(\sigma[x=0])+D(\sigma[x=1])],&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
All states with no falsified clause and no unassigned name would contradict UNSAT. Memoized exhaustive enumeration over 697 reachable ternary assignment states on odd $K_4$ yields exactly
\[
\boxed{D(\emptyset)=28,\quad \min\text{ maximum depth among 28-leaf proofs}=6.}
\]
This is a \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL exact finite optimization}, proved for the finite instance by the displayed exhaustive recurrence. It is not asymptotic evidence against alternative proof systems or algorithms.

Actual physically serialized one- and two-clause \emph{deletion} descendants of all three minimally UNSAT controls are SAT at both levels. Their source identities remain fixed; their ordinary headers are regenerated and reparsed, and they need not retain the original exact-$(2,2)$ grammar. Results:

All three roots are UNSAT; each single-deletion child is SAT. The original $W_1$ source labels and essentiality had already been checked in CLAUSE-CRITICALITY125; the new restricted link is the \emph{tree-terminal source-support conservation} and the finite $K_4$ optimal branch count.

A pair of disconnected odd $K_4$ components gives 32 clauses but \emph{no individually essential clause}: deleting any one clause leaves the other component UNSAT. The mass theorem correctly says nothing useful there. Even-charge $K_4$ is SAT, so it cannot accept the odd-charge tree. Replacing one ordinary source clause by its first-literal sign flip makes that control SAT and invalidates the old proof under exact clause-ID checking. Horn UNSAT, 2SAT SAT and same-name versus freshly-aliased proper ternary pin modules are other controls. These reject the extrapolation from $\#\text{clauses}$ to a universal UNSAT hardness measure.

\section{Joint machine ledger and relation to the near-boundary seed}
One fixed deterministic total exact ordinary-SAT decider $A$ costs at most $m^{d+o(1)}$ time on a fully encoded $m$-bit guest. For terminal guest lengths $m_i$, $M=\max m_i$, and $V=\sum_i m_i$,
\[
\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V\qquad(d\ge1).
\]
This arithmetic upper bound was already established in PARITY-FIREWALL124 and checked independently by the red team. The present conservation theorem counts original-clause \emph{support incidences}; it does not equate these with $m_i$ when children use restrictions, compression, virtual source access, or derived constraints. Nor does it lower-bound $T_A(m)$ by its worst-case upper exponent. An actual clocked nondeterministic UNSAT verifier must charge \emph{all} certificate bytes, invalid attempts, original reads, branch and module joins, strategy switching, guest execution and every virtual probe; simultaneous peak writable/index storage includes live ancestors and proof fragments. Temporal flips, circuit hardware and cumulative bit-time remain different measures.

Only if every UNSAT $N$-bit source has an accepted certificate with $M\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, $V\le N^{b+o(1)}$, other charged verification $N^{r+o(1)}$, and per-guest-step access overhead $N^{u+o(1)}$, may one infer
\[
\mathrm{UNSAT}\in\mathrm{NTIME}(N^{c+o(1)}),\qquad
c\le\max\{r,u+b+\lambda(d-1)\}.
\]
The near-boundary $(d,\lambda,u)=(1.802,1/2,0)$ and $b<1.4008217497$ remain \textbf{CONDITIONAL} on a published two-algorithm model audit \emph{and}, centrally, on unproved all-UNSAT certificate coverage. The essential-source mass bound at most $m=O(N)$ cannot by itself contradict this $N^{1.4008}$ allowance. An A call on a rewritten smaller child is not automatically covered by the source-subformula support theorem. No specific near-boundary $(d,c)$ follows from $P=NP$.
