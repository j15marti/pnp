## ITERATE: source-authenticated all-k paid upper theorem

Take any connected simple bridgeless cubic graph G of h vertices, edge variables n=3h/2, odd vertex charges and original signed strict 3-CNF Tseitin formula T with 4h clauses, four local forbidden-parity clauses per vertex. For any positional subset S of k clauses (0<=k<=4h), replace each distinct C=(a OR b OR c) by A=(a OR b OR u), B=(NOT u OR c OR v), D=(NOT u OR c OR NOT v) using its own pair of fresh variables. The resulting H_S has m=4h+2k strict three-literal source clauses, n+2k variables, and binary source length O((h+k)log(h+k)) for standard indexed literal encoding. The prior /504 source-substitution proof (u:=c,v:=0 for each gadget, dropping tautologies via subclause simulation) transfers established expander-family exponential **ordinary Resolution** lower bounds to H_S; this is not hardness for stronger calculi.

**Theorem 508.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED constructive upper, no global minimum claim).** In the separate fixed typed calculus R_CAST507(w) for each fixed integer w>=1, allowing free authenticated SOURCE, unit RES, exact <=3-original-support AND3, unit binary GF(2) XOR, and paid unary CAST from RES clause to its identical factor at fee w, with no additional historical rules, there exists a legal original-source refutation DAG of H_S with exactly

$$
q_{RES}=2k,\quad q_{CAST}=k,\quad q_{AND3}=3h,\quad q_{XOR}=h-1.
$$

The total weighted work of this **particular construction** is

$$
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{constructed}}=4h+2k-1+kw,}
$$

and, using balanced four-leaf vertex AND3 trees and balanced binary XOR combination, weighted critical-path depth is bounded by

$$
\boxed{D\le 4+w+\lceil\log_2 h\rceil\quad(k\ge1),\qquad
D\le2+\lceil\log_2 h\rceil\quad(k=0).}
$$

**Proof.** Each selected triple is source-indexed; RES(B,D;v) produces (NOT u OR c), RES(A,previous;u) produces precisely C, and exactly one CAST pays w to expose C as a legal factor. For each old vertex its four original clauses, either direct SOURCE or paid CAST outputs, are AND3-combined under three-original-variable support, checked on all eight assignments and recognized only when **exactly** the parity equation. Across vertices XOR cancels every edge variable twice and returns 0=1. Each source leaf and each paid node is used in one acyclic connected ancestor DAG; reconvergent reuse is permitted and charged once. Counts are 2k RES + 3h AND3 + h-1 XOR + k CAST. A 4-leaf factor tree has depth at most 2 beyond its deepest input (the gadget input depth is 2+w); balanced XOR adds ceil(log2 h). The /341 historical grammar is not claimed to accept CAST.

The ordinary-Resolution lower bound is independent of this upper bound and stays in its own model. For k=4h, the constructed work is (12+4w)h-1, which is linear for fixed w. A positive conversion fee therefore does **not** restore ordinary Resolution hardness in this stronger separate calculus. The earlier /507 equality covers k=1 only; no equality or minimum number k of CASTs is claimed for general S.
