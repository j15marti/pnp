\section*{Fixed source, cost, and two published starting points}
Fix the already selected source-authenticated, at-most-binary-premise, unit-cost mixed calculus $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ with Resolution, AND3, GF$(2)$, LOCAL-CONVERT and paid conservative binary definitions (ER2), without changing its grammar or weights. For a general CNF $F$, $W_R(F)$ is the minimum sum of weights of distinct derived nodes in a shared refutation DAG. For binary input length $N$, $\overline W_R(N)$ is the maximum over valid UNSAT encodings of at most $N$ bits, with empty maximum zero. Evidence bits, proof search and actual machine work are separate.

Haken (1985) proved $2^{\Omega(n)}$ ordinary Resolution proof size for the standard pigeonhole CNF with $n+1$ pigeons, $n$ holes, $n(n+1)$ variables and ASCII DIMACS length $N(n)=\Theta(n^3\log(n+2))$. Cook--Reckhow (1979) gave polynomial-size Extended Resolution refutations, and the already fixed $R$ contains its paid ER2 fragment. Thus $W_R(\mathrm{PHP}_n)\le n^{O(1)}$ despite the published Resolution lower bound on the \emph{same source}. The backward simulation required to transfer the lower bound is false; LSPV-2 HAKEN-ER2-NONPROJECTION364 already established this, so it is reused, not rediscovered.

Alekseev (Theory of Computing, June 21, 2026) established an exponential \emph{bit-size} lower bound for the rational polynomial-calculus-with-extension-and-square-root calculus on the binary value principle $1+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}2^ix_i=0$, $x_i^2=x_i$. This is not a theorem about arbitrary Boolean CNF encodings or a count of mixed logical nodes. Prior LSPV-3 BVP-BITBLAST-COLLAPSE354 proved that an explicit $5n+2$-source carry CNF has \emph{exact} mixed minimum $2n+1$. A new independently verified three-wire XOR/AND ripple-carry replication here has $3n+1$ variables, $8n+2$ source clauses and a $4n+1$-node Resolution proof. This is an encoding robustness control and \emph{duplicates the previously known BVP collapse principle}; it is not a new family-hardness theorem.

\section*{Substantive scoped lemma: semantic interface normal form}
LSPV-4 LOCAL-CONVERT-CVP363 and LSPV-5 REFEREE315 correctly found that under a possible reading U an individual LOCAL-CONVERT record can contain an arbitrary-length circuit DAG on only three original inputs, despite costing one logical node. Checking an \emph{as supplied} valid $k$-constant chain requires $k$ bit probes, regardless of its charged-node count.

Define a strictly scoped interpretation $U_{\mathrm{opaque}}(d)$ for testing, \emph{not as a change to the actual R}: each circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT conclusion is a Boolean function of at most $d$ exposed independent inputs. Internal gate wires are private and cannot be referenced downstream. All downstream rules see only the Boolean function of the exposed inputs, not its gate syntax. Every other proof record has polynomial encoding length in $N+q$ after consistent alpha-renaming of paid auxiliary variables.

\begin{theorem}[Bounded opaque-interface normalization]
In $U_{\mathrm{opaque}}(d)$ each circuit conclusion has an equivalent representation using no more than $d+d2^{d-1}$ internal NOT, AND2 and OR2 gates, with constants permitted. For $d=3$, at most 14 gates suffice. An existing valid $q$-node DAG can be replaced topologically by a DAG with \emph{the same charged logical nodes and weights} whose circuit payloads have these bounds. If $d=O(\log(N+q+2))$ uniformly for all such nodes, and the stated ordinary-record bounds hold, the resulting certificate has $(N+q+2)^{O(1)}$ encoded bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent a Boolean function using the smaller of its true assignment set and false assignment set, of cardinality $m\le2^{d-1}$. Form one $d$-literal minterm for each chosen assignment; share $d$ input negations, use $(d-1)m$ AND2 gates, $m-1$ OR2 gates and, if the false assignments were chosen, one final NOT. This gives at most $d+dm$ gates, with constant functions needing none. For $d=3$ and $m=4$, choose the true set, yielding $3+8+3=14$ gates; for $m\le3$, the cost with final NOT is at most $3+3m\le12$. Replace each opaque conclusion by its exact truth-table equivalent. Its interface function stays identical on every assignment, so every later extensional semantic inference remains legal, including shared nodes. Renaming identifiers and serializing each bounded circuit gives the claimed bit bound. Constructing the canonical circuit from a supplied long payload may require reading that entire payload; no bound on the normalizer's \emph{machine time} in terms of $q$ is asserted.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Critical caveat.} Downstream access to named internal gates breaks the argument: equivalent output functions need not preserve intermediate named values or syntactic rules. Likewise, unbounded numbers of exposed auxiliary inputs eliminate the polynomial bound. The original $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ LOCAL-CONVERT grammar remains unresolved, so this is \emph{not} an unconditional normal-form theorem for the full mixed calculus. In particular, it does not contradict the LSPV-5 lower bound for validating a particular oversized supplied record.

\section*{Precisely what the lemma would transfer}
Under additionally verified polynomial-bit and polynomial-time effective checking of \emph{all} other fixed R rules, the condition above would turn bounded-node proof existence into an NP predicate with an explicitly polynomial witness in $N+q$. Under hypothetical SAT in P, standard Cook--Levin reductions plus search self-reduction would then construct shortest proofs in time polynomial in $N+W_R(F)$, with an unknown budget found by doubling and malformed/SAT strings handled separately. This is an output-sensitive extension of the earlier LSPV-1 OUTPUT-SENSITIVE-OPT365, not an unconditional proof of B3. It does not show that $W_R(F)$ itself is polynomial in $N$.

The three bridges still are: B1, $\forall A\exists p_A\forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}$, $W_R(F)\le p_A(|\operatorname{enc}F|+T_A(F))$; B2, $\forall A\exists p_A\forall N$, $\overline W_R(N)\le p_A(N+T_A^{wc}(N))$; and primary B3, $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)=N^{O(1)}$ for this \emph{same preselected} $R$. Here $A$ is a fixed total correct general SAT decider on valid SAT, valid UNSAT and malformed strings, and $T_A^{wc}$ ranges over all binary strings up to $N$ bits. Only B1 implies B2 implies B3 is known. Neither lower-bound family result above gives B1, B2, or B3.
