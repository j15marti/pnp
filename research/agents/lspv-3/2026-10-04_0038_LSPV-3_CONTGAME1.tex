\section*{Exact continuation predicate}
Fix polynomial horizon \(r(n)\). Let \(t_i\) be the transcript before response \(w_i\), with polynomial-time local legality and validity predicates \(L_i,V_i\), and polynomial-time terminal acceptance \(A\). Define recursively
\[
W_i(X,t_i,w_i)=V_i(X,t_i,w_i)\wedge
\forall u_{i+1}\bigl(L_{i+1}\Rightarrow\exists w_{i+1}W_{i+1}\bigr),
\]
with the obvious terminal case at round \(r\).

\paragraph{Theorem CONTGAME1.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT classification.} With \(d=r-i\) future challenge rounds, direct unrolling has \(2d+O(1)\) alternating quantifier blocks. Constant \(d\) therefore gives a fixed PH level. Polynomial \(d\) is decidable in PSPACE by alternating polynomial-time evaluation, equivalently by depth-first evaluation storing a polynomial transcript.

\paragraph{Conditional corollary.}
\textbf{CONDITIONAL on \(P=NP\).} If every \(W_i\) is uniformly contained in one fixed level of PH independent of the remaining horizon, PHABSORB1/ROUNDABSORB1 gives a polynomial-time winning-response selector. If the natural continuation definition has polynomially growing alternation, \(P=NP\) alone does not collapse it because only \(PH=P\) follows, not \(PSPACE=P\).

\section*{Arbitrary-SAT guard}
Even PSPACE-style continuation semantics gives no SAT lower bound. The still-missing theorem must force every arbitrary polynomial static SAT decider, on one SAME_REALIZATION, to solve the global continuation predicate rather than RECOMP a current response, preprocess, memoize, use a compact strategy representation, or choose another wrapper. Thus
\[
\boxed{\text{continuation complexity}\ne\text{universal SAT-decider necessity}.}
\]
