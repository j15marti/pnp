\section{Pre-search falsifiers and scope}
The following four falsifiers were fixed before consulting the UTM-CROSS-WRITE1 handoff, prior project originals and communication-complexity literature.
\begin{description}
\item[] H1: An Alice-only SAT-model printer can be promoted to a \emph{sound accepting verifier} while inspecting no Bob-controlled source bits on every SAT source. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: a fully sensitive SAT source forces all $m$ distinct Bob coordinates to be read on each sound accepting path.
\item[] H2: A length-$L$ external read-only proof tape, even if freely rewound, removes the one-sequential-input-head crossing bottleneck at no resource cost. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: its bits add $L$ to the covering information, yielding Theorem~\ref{th:cap}; when $L=m$ there is a linear-time countercontrol with a single forward input pass.
\item[] H3: Every logarithmic UNSAT source-authenticated certificate must pay this \emph{SAT-acceptance} lower bound. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: an overlapping coordinate supplies an $O(\log m)$-bit authenticated twenty-clause UNSAT leaf on every NO source of the declared promise.
\item[] H4: The finite input-head crossing surface transfers unchanged to indexed RAM or implies a near-boundary uniform all-ordinary-SAT/coNTIME seed. \textbf{FALSIFIED as a proposed deduction}: RAM retains only a near-linear source-sensitivity bound here; an all-input certificate-coverage theorem is absent.
\end{description}

\section{Canonical exact signed source and three computational models}
Let $H(u,v)$ be the authenticated $15$-variable $20$-clause $W_1$ proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ gadget from BINARY-WORK-SAT4 and Role-3 INDEXED-LOCATOR-GAP140. Source clause rows, in original order, are:
\begin{verbatim}
-1 3 -14; 2 -8 9; 10 -13 14; 3 7 -13; -4 -9 -11;
5 8 -14; 4 -8 -11; 1 7 13; 1 -7 10; -5 -6 -15;
-2 -6 12; -4 9 -10; -2 5 -12; -3 6 -15; 6 -7 11;
2 -9 11; 4 8 -10; -3 12 15; -5 -12 15; -1 13 14
\end{verbatim}
Use zero-based row/literal coordinates: the $u$-selector flips both signs at $(6,0),(11,0)$ when $u=0$; the $v$-selector flips both signs at $(17,2),(9,2)$ when $v=0$. Both flips preserve every two-positive/two-negative occurrence count. Exhaustive $2^{15}$ assignment counts, inherited and independently retested, are $772,272,272,0$ for $H(0,0),H(0,1),H(1,0),H(1,1)$ respectively. Thus $H(u,v)$ is UNSAT iff $u=v=1$.

For $a,b\in\{0,1\}^m$, let
\[
F_m(a,b)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}H_i(a_i,b_i),\qquad
\SAT(F_m(a,b))\iff\forall i\;\neg(a_i\land b_i).\tag{1}
\]
The $m$ component name sets are disjoint. Canonically serialize all $2m$ Alice-sensitive rows first, the $16m$ completely fixed rows second, and the $2m$ Bob-sensitive rows last, preserving original clause identifiers in a deterministic row-location table. Every literal is a sign bit followed by a fixed-width binary original-name identifier of $w=\lceil\log_2(15m+1)\rceil$ bits. A complete length-stable header identifies $m,w,15m,20m$. Thus $N=60m(w+1)+O(\log m)=\Theta(m\log m)$; the fixed middle region has $G=48m(w+1)=\Theta(N)$ distinct input cuts. Every paired source has precisely the same length, variable/clause counts and name supports.

\emph{Model M1} is an ordinary finite-control deterministic \emph{one shared read/write tape} with initial source occupying $N$ cells, total footprint at least $N$, one head, and all original input changes and output writes charged. The augmented proof-tape theorem below is \emph{not} silently identified with M1.

\emph{Model $M_{k,\pi}$} has ONE two-way sequential read-only source-input head, fixed $k$ sequential work tapes over a fixed $g$-symbol alphabet, and an additional separately tracked, two-way read-only untrusted proof tape of length at most $L$. The latter's storage is \emph{not} counted in the work-cell total $S$ but its $L$ source-independent or source-dependent bits are counted as a distinct proof-length resource; proof-head position and all proof-head moves are charged to the verifier. $R$ counts reversals of the single \emph{source} input head, $H$ its actual single-cell moves, $T$ \emph{all} machine transitions, and $S$ every visited work cell including blanks and clock/policy counters. An auxiliary output tape is write-only, not a hidden readable workspace. This model is deliberately stronger than an ordinary work-tape-only $M_k$ in granting a reusable proof tape.

\emph{Model RAT} has indexed read-only input and bit-addressed work storage. There is no physical input-cut or reversal resource. A source-bit probe and its index-generation cost are both charged; a word returning many signs counts as a word probe with its actual word width.

\section{Source-only candidate output is not checked acceptance}
There exist local satisfying assignments encoded by $w_0=616$ for both $H(0,0)$ and $H(0,1)$, and by $w_1=4742$ for $H(1,0)$ (least significant bit denotes original variable 1). Hence for any SAT $F_m(a,b)$ the source-only concatenation $w_{a_1}\cdots w_{a_m}$ is a valid model, regardless of the unknown $b$ when generated. On UNSAT $F_m(a,b)$ the same printer can output an invalid model, because some $H(1,1)$ has no model. A candidate printer has no reason to decide or soundly accept either polarity. The UTM-CROSS-WRITE1 sound-acceptance theorem cannot be applied to that printer before a fully charged verifier is incorporated.

\begin{lemma}[Bob-side source sensitivity despite any untrusted proof]\label{lem:bob}
Fix the SAT source $F_m(1^m,0^m)$. On \emph{every} accepting run of any deterministic or proof-branch nondeterministic verifier that is sound on all $F_m(a,b)$ (for all proof strings), the run must inspect a Bob-dependent source-sign position from each of the $m$ modules. In a bit-probe indexed model this forces $m$ distinct source-bit probes; with $O(\log N)$-bit packed words containing at most a constant number of module selectors, it forces $\Omega(m)$ word probes, and with general $O(\log N)$-bit contiguous words the safe bound is $\Omega(m/\log N)$ word probes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Hold the \emph{entire} proof string and deterministic verification branch fixed. If the accepting run fails to probe every Bob-controlled literal-sign cell associated with some module $i$, change only $b_i$ from $0$ to $1$, preserving all headers, literal identifiers, exact occurrence counts, canonical structure, other modules and proof bits. The run's observed source symbols and internal transitions do not change, hence it accepts $F_m(1^m,e_i)$. But that formula contains the full $H(1,1)$ contradiction and is UNSAT, violating soundness. The $m$ Bob-dependent regions are disjoint. A read of one sign in a module suffices for the lower-bound accounting, and a general word can intersect at most its word width in distinct regions. \end{proof}

The lemma is input sensitivity, not a superlinear ordinary SAT lower bound. It is also not universal on each SAT source: when $a=0^m$, the printed model $w_0^m$ is valid for all $b$, so Bob inspection need not be forced on an accepting proof for that particular $a$.

\section{Reusable-certificate length, reversal and space theorem}
Fix a single deterministic clocked verifier $V$ on $M_{k,\pi}$ with $q$ finite-control states, work alphabet of size $g\ge2$, and no oracle. \emph{Soundness} means $\forall a,b,\pi$, if $F_m(a,b)$ is UNSAT then $V(F_m(a,b),\pi)$ rejects. \emph{Completeness with jointly capped resources} means
\[
\forall a\in\{0,1\}^m\ \exists\pi_a,\quad
|\pi_a|\le L,\; V(F_m(a,\neg a),\pi_a)=\text{accept},\;
S\le S_0,\;R\le R_0.\tag{2}
\]
The \emph{same accepting run} must meet both $S_0$ and $R_0$. Every malformed proof, timeout or out-of-range parameter is rejected, and there is no per-input hardcoded strategy beyond the physically present proof bits. Set
\[
Q=2q(2S_0+3)^{2k}(g+1)^{S_0}(L+3)\ge2.\tag{3}
\]
The generous factor accounts for control/direction, finite-alphabet work contents, allocation endpoints, all work-head positions, and the current proof-tape head position. The proof \emph{contents} are held fixed separately for each chosen $\pi_a$.

\begin{theorem}[Fooling-set length--crossing tradeoff]\label{th:cap}
Under (1)--(3),
\[
\boxed{2^m\le(2^{L+1}-1)\sum_{j=0}^{R_0+1}Q^j
\le 2^{L+1}(R_0+2)Q^{R_0+1}.}\tag{4}
\]
In particular
\[
m\le L+1+\log_2(R_0+2)+(R_0+1)\log_2Q.\tag{5}
\]
If $J=\lfloor(m-L-3)/\log_2Q\rfloor\ge1$, and in addition (2) gives an accepting proof for each diagonal source with a simultaneous source-head movement ceiling $H_0$, then
\[
\boxed{H_0\ge\tfrac34 GJ,\qquad
(R_0+1)\log_2Q\ge m-L-1-\log_2(R_0+2).}\tag{6}
\]
Thus if $L\le(1-\varepsilon)m$ for constant $\varepsilon>0$ and $S_0=O(\log N)$, the necessary worst-case accepting path has $R_0=\Omega(m/\log N)$ and $H_0=\Omega(Nm/\log N)=\Omega(N^2/\log^2N)$, with one common accepted source and proof for the $H_0$ lower bound. These are a stronger \emph{length-aware model statement}, not a new unrestricted SAT complexity lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each $a$ select one proof satisfying (2). For each cut $c$ in the fixed gap, record the ordered sequence of \emph{complete} configurations immediately as the source head crosses $c$. The source head is fixed at one of the two adjacent positions, while the work tapes, their heads and the proof head have at most $Q$ event labels. If two distinct fooling inputs $F_m(a,\neg a)$ and $F_m(a',\neg a')$ yield both the same selected proof $\pi$ and the same complete crossing sequence at $c$, the standard deterministic input-cut splice works with \emph{identical proof-tape contents} on both halves: it accepts both crossed inputs $F_m(a,\neg a')$ and $F_m(a',\neg a)$. For $a\ne a'$, at least one crossing source contains a component $H(1,1)$ and is UNSAT, contradicting soundness. Therefore $a\mapsto(\pi_a,\mathrm{trace}_c(a))$ is injective at \emph{every} gap cut. Because consecutive crossings of a fixed cut alternate direction, their number is at most $R_0+1$. There are at most $2^{L+1}-1$ binary proofs and at most $\sum_{j=0}^{R_0+1}Q^j$ traces, proving (4) and (5).

For the head-move bound set $J=\lfloor(m-L-3)/\log_2Q\rfloor\ge1$. At a fixed gap cut, fewer than $J$ crossings can occur on at most
\[
(2^{L+1}-1)\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}Q^j
< 2^{L+1}Q^J\le 2^{m-2}
\]
of the $2^m$ accepted diagonal sources. Hence at least $3/4$ of the chosen accepting pairs have at least $J$ crossings of \emph{each fixed cut in expectation over a uniformly chosen $a$}. Summing across the same $G$ cuts gives average gap-head traffic at least $(3/4)GJ$; one \emph{single chosen accepted proof on one source} attains the bound. Any presumed simultaneous $H_0$ ceiling must exceed it. Taking logarithms in (4) proves the second inequality in (6). The asymptotic claims follow from $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$ and $\log_2Q=O(S_0+\log(S_0+2)+\log(L+3))=O(\log N)$ for the stated caps. No M1-to-$M_{k,\pi}$ or $M_{k,\pi}$-to-RAT conversion is performed.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Sharp hostile countercontrol: a linear reusable proof tape.}
Let $\pi=a$ be exactly $m$ untrusted bits. In a single forward source-input pass, verify the canonical rows, compare the Alice selectors with $\pi$ in order, rewind only the independently counted \emph{proof} head, then scan the fixed middle block and check $\pi_i\land b_i=0$ while reading the Bob selectors. This reader accepts iff (1) is SAT on the declared promise, for one correct proof on each SAT input, rejects \emph{every} proof on UNSAT, uses $L=m$ proof bits and $S=O(\log N)$ work cells, $R=0$ source-input reversals, $H=O(N)$ source-input moves, $O(m)$ additional proof-head moves, and $T=O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}\,N)$ fully charged time. It can check malformed fixed-record rows as it streams. This proves that deleting $L$ from (4) is erroneous; an externally reusable certificate is an independent information channel. If the proof is instead copied to writable work cells, those $m$ cells must be included in $S$.

\section{Opposite polarity: constant-source-subformula UNSAT leaves}
On the declared source family,
\[
F_m(a,b)\in\UNSAT\iff\exists i\in[m]\;[a_i=b_i=1].\tag{7}
\]
An index of $\lceil\log_2m\rceil$ bits identifies exactly twenty original source clause IDs forming $H_i(1,1)$. Authenticate the twenty signed ternary clauses, their original names and source record positions. The verifier can either check the inherited complete $32$-leaf source-ID/SCC template or exhaust all $2^{15}$ assignments to this \emph{constant-size actual source subformula}, a finite bound independent of $m$. No invented UNSAT label is trusted. On the canonical fixed-width promise, an indexed bit-access checker pays $O(\log N)$ source-bit positions plus polylogarithmic address generation and $O(\log N)$ index space; on a sequential input head it may need a full $O(N)$ scan; a total arbitrary-encoding parser adds its actual $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}\,N)$ validation. A blanket polylog-time ordinary-SAT verifier is not claimed.

Clocking all invalid indices, malformed proof strings, overlong witnesses and timeout paths to REJECT gives the exact promise quantifiers
\[
\forall F\in\mathcal F_m:\quad
F\in\UNSAT\iff\exists i\in[m]\ V_{\UNSAT}(F,i)=1,
\quad
F\in\SAT\iff\forall i\in[m]\ [1-V_{\UNSAT}(F,i)]=1.\tag{8}
\]
This is a sound bounded nondeterministic-UNSAT (and SAT co-nondeterministic) companion \emph{only on $\mathcal F_m$}. Under standard arbitrary ordinary input encodings, malformed-input and family-membership conventions must be separately specified and charged. The universal all-ordinary-UNSAT certificate-size and verifier-time bounds remain OPEN. Deterministic certificate \emph{acquisition} must locate a collision; on the SAT anchor $F_m(1^m,0^m)$ and an adversarial last-collision UNSAT source it needs $\Omega(m)$ distinct Bob-selector bit probes even on indexed RAM, whereas guessing the index does not. This is the correct SAT/UNSAT asymmetry.
