\section{Literature preflight and exact construction}
The two obstacles were a genuinely balanced YES/NO family and a model-matched bridge
from structural or proof-system hardness to arbitrary computation.  Primary sources
read first were Yan and Wang, \emph{The smallest pair of cospectral cubic graphs with
different chromatic indexes}, arXiv:2112.06112
(\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06112}); Etesami and Haemers, \emph{On NP-hard graph
properties characterized by the spectrum}, arXiv:1912.07061
(\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.07061}); and Abudayah et al., \emph{Incidence matrices
and line graphs of signed graphs}, Linear Algebra Appl. 2022
(\url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2022.05.018}).  The first and third transfer
directly to the graph objects below.  The second is structural context, not a
computation lower bound.

Let $G_3,G_4$ be the connected cubic graphs at lines 3576 and 3138 of the authors'
\texttt{cubic\_graph16.g6}; they have 16 vertices, 24 edges, the same adjacency
spectrum, and edge-chromatic numbers 3 and 4.  Define
\[
K_i=L(G_i),\qquad X_{i,r}=K_i[\overline{K_r}],\quad i\in\{3,4\},\ r\ge1.
\]
For a cubic graph with unsigned incidence matrix $B$,
$BB^{\mathsf T}=A(G)+3I$ and $B^{\mathsf T}B=A(L(G))+2I$; the nonzero spectra agree.
Thus $K_3,K_4$ are adjacency-cospectral.  Vertex-colouring $L(G)$ is edge-colouring
$G$, so their chromatic numbers are 3 and 4.  Finally,
\[
A(X_{i,r})=A(K_i)\otimes J_r,qquad
\operatorname{Spec}X_{i,r}=\{r\mu:\mu\in\operatorname{Spec}K_i\}
\cup\{0\}^{24(r-1)}.
\]
Hence for every $r$, the pair is connected, exact cospectral, $4r$-regular, has
$24r$ vertices and $48r^2$ edges, but has chromatic numbers 3 and 4.

\section{Red-team counterexample to hardness}
For $r\ge2$, the $r$ copies of every base vertex have identical open neighbourhoods.
Sorting those neighbourhood rows recovers the 24-vertex quotient in polynomial time.
On that quotient, the number of induced seven-vertex sets having degree multiset
$(0,2,2,2,2,2,2)$ is 877 on the YES side and 873 on the NO side.  Since the quotient
size is fixed, its fingerprint computation is constant after the $O((24r)^2)$ direct
quotient pass.  One-dimensional Weisfeiler--Leman from the uniform colouring leaves a
single cell on both sides, so the result is not merely the degree test; it is also not
pseudorandomness against general algorithms.

The smallest repair is precise: exact adjacency-spectrum matching is a valid balanced
structural obstruction, but fixed-base blow-up is retired as a hard distribution.  A
replacement needs a growing quotient and must charge quotient discovery,
canonicalization, decoding, and all source access.
