\paragraph{Two obstacles and literature preflight.}
The active obstacles are (i) the downstream NO obligations depend on SAT descriptions generated after the middle existential reconstruction choice, and (ii) even a mathematically well-defined upstream uniformizer is useless unless acquisition, representation, indexing, verification, compilation, and writing beat the explicit baseline $A\circ D_j$. The 2025 vMS paper supplies the targeted AM/HSG/PCPP architecture but no deterministic fixed-coin NO certificate. Viola supplies $\mathrm{BPTime}(t)\subseteq\Sigma_3\mathrm{Time}(t\,\mathrm{polylog}\,t)$, not a dependency-elimination theorem. Current DQBF dependency-scheme work formalizes removable dependencies and proof checking, but does not imply a cheap unrestricted-SAT uniformizer.

\paragraph{Exact reconstruction predicate retained.}
For one shared size-normalized $Q$, fixed coordinate and Arthur randomness, exact answers to the constant reconstruction SAT family have the form
\[
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a)=\exists Y^+\forall Y^-\bigwedge_{j\in J}
\left[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))\right].
\]
Late substitution therefore retains
\[
\boxed{\exists\sigma\;\forall u\;\exists z\;\forall y\;R(x,Q,\sigma,u,z,y)},
\qquad z=(\ell,a,Y^+),\quad y=Y^-.
\]

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: structured NO-certificate guard.}
Let $D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)$ be a model-matched compiler for a downstream SAT instance and define the structured NO language
\[
L_{\rm NO}=\{\langle x,Q,r,u,z,j\rangle: D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\}.
\]
Suppose every member of $L_{\rm NO}$ has a certificate $C$ of length polynomial in the tuple length and a deterministic polynomial-time verifier $V$ satisfying
\[
D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\iff\exists C\;V(x,Q,r,u,z,j,C)=1.
\]
Then $L_{\rm NO}\in NP$ by definition. If, in addition, there is a polynomial-time many-one reduction $f$ from UNSAT to $L_{\rm NO}$, then UNSAT is in NP. Since UNSAT is coNP-complete, coNP is contained in NP; taking complements gives NP contained in coNP, and hence
\[
\boxed{L_{\rm NO}\text{ coNP-hard and polynomial existential NO certificates}\Longrightarrow NP=coNP.}
\]
This implication is exact. What is OPEN is whether the actual vMS reconstruction NO-query family is coNP-hard.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: trivial uniform evaluator and the quantitative guard.}
Under the counterfactual exact SAT decider $A$, define
\[
U_A(x,Q,r,u,z,j)=1-A(D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)).
\]
If $D_j$ outputs length $L^{b+o(1)}$ and $A$ runs in $S^{k+o(1)}$, this evaluates the NO bit in $L^{bk+o(1)}$ time, plus separately charged compiler cost. Thus existence, canonicity, or ordinary computability of an upstream representation does not remove a $k$ factor. Any proposed $Z=F(x,Q,r,u)$ must beat the fully charged $A\circ D_j$ baseline.

\paragraph{Consequence for the $k^4$ ledger.}
The conservative recurrence remains
\[
\alpha_{q+1}\le b_qk\alpha_q+o(1),
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm det}\le\alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1)}.
\]
Status: CONDITIONAL conservative upper-bound accounting, not a lower bound and not a necessity theorem. The NO-certificate guard does not prove that one factor of $k$ is unavoidable under $P=NP$; under that counterfactual NP=coNP qualitatively. The missing quantitative issue is construction cost and exponent.
