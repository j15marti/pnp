\section{Exact projection and restricted oracle lower bound}
Fix a proper CNF $F=G(B,X)\wedge H(B,Y)$ with \emph{all} shared variables in $B$, and $X,Y$ disjoint. Define $P_G=\{u\in\{0,1\}^b:\exists X\,G(u,X)\}$ and $P_H$ analogously.
\begin{lemma}[Both-label exact interface composition]
$\SAT(F)=1$ if and only if $P_G\cap P_H\ne\varnothing$. In the positive case the two local witnesses for the same $u$ glue; in the negative case the complete intersection is empty. The source must contain the true shared variable map, not guessed boundary positions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof} Restrict a global satisfying assignment to $B$, obtaining a common element. Conversely choose a common boundary assignment and join the two private witnesses: their domains are disjoint except for identically assigned $B$. \end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Membership-only acquisition lower bound; exact, restricted]
Let $U=\{0,1\}^b$, $s=2^b$, with $P_G=U$. An algorithm is supplied \emph{only} adaptive individual Boolean queries of the form $\mathbf1[u\in P_H]$ and must distinguish $P_H=\varnothing$ from $P_H=\{v\}$ for every unknown $v\in U$. On the empty instance every deterministic exact algorithm makes $s$ distinct queries in the worst case. For every randomized algorithm with error at most $\epsilon<1/2$ on \emph{each} promised input, its expected number of distinct queries on the empty instance is at least $(1-2\epsilon)s$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} In a deterministic run on the empty oracle, any unqueried $v$ yields identical observations for the singleton oracle $\{v\}$ and produces an incorrect common decision. Thus all $s$ are queried. For a randomized run couple the random tapes on the empty and singleton-$v$ oracles. Unless it queries $v$ on the empty transcript, the outputs coincide. The acceptance-probability difference, at least $1-2\epsilon$, is bounded by the probability of querying $v$ on the empty run. Sum over $v$ to obtain the expected distinct-query count. \end{proof}

This is elementary membership-oracle decision-tree mathematics, consistent with prior project SPARSE-BOUNDARY-SUPPORT83, ARC-MEMBERSHIP-JOIN162, MAIN222, and the earlier MODELBRIDGE-0006 communication firewall. It is \emph{not} a lower bound against a SAT decider supplied the original CNF. A complete materialized signature needs $2^b$ bits; sequential probes can use substantially less space while paying repeated source/guest work. Even $2^b$ may be only linear in an explicitly materialized truth table.

\section{Source-authenticated proper-3CNF escape and certificates}
For a signed literal $\ell$ and fresh variables $p,q$, put
\[
\operatorname{PIN}(\ell;p,q)=
(\ell\vee p\vee q)\wedge(\ell\vee p\vee\neg q)\wedge
(\ell\vee\neg p\vee q)\wedge(\ell\vee\neg p\vee\neg q).
\]
It is existentially equivalent to the unit $\ell$, for both polarities. Three ordinary resolution steps give $\ell$: eliminate $q$ in the first two and second two parent clauses to get $(\ell\vee p)$ and $(\ell\vee\neg p)$, then eliminate $p$. The cube $\ell=0$ is thereby forbidden with indexed original-clause provenance.

Let $B=(x_1,\ldots,x_b)$. Take $G$ to contain, for each $i$, the pair
$(x_i\vee a_i\vee c_i)\wedge(\neg x_i\vee a_i\vee c_i)$ with fresh private $(a_i,c_i)$: setting every $a_i=1$ gives $P_G=U$. For an arbitrary word $v$, let $H_v$ consist of one PIN forcing each $x_i=v_i$ with pairwise fresh auxiliaries. Let $H_\emptyset$ contain $H_v$ and one opposite-sign PIN for $x_1$ with fresh auxiliaries. All clauses are proper ternaries, and all source identities and occurrences are retained. Then
\[
P_{H_v}=\{v\},\quad P_{H_\emptyset}=\varnothing,\quad
\SAT(G\wedge H_v)=1,\quad \SAT(G\wedge H_\emptyset)=0.
\]

\paragraph{Authenticated adaptive decision.}
First check globally coherent $0^n$ and $1^n$ against the original source; on success retain a checked full witness. Otherwise scan all original clauses, grouping by exact unsigned triples and verifying that every group is either a two-clause free-boundary pair or a four-clause PIN, with private variables occurring in exactly one group and outside the target-name set. The grammar is invariant under arbitrary variable permutation, independent variable negation, clause reordering, and within-clause permutation. Recognition costs a complete paid scan and index construction. An accepted source is decided by consistency of its authenticated forced units. SAT produces a verified complete assignment; UNSAT produces an original-indexed seven-step resolution proof from two opposite PINs. A rejected source switches to complete UP-first DPLL, and therefore correctness does not depend on membership in the grammar. The controller is total, exact on \emph{both labels}, but its worst-case running time remains exponential. No source-independent oracle grants its grammar information.

\paragraph{Important quantitative caveat.}
The experiment uses Python dictionaries and sorting, not the FLMV indexed random-access tape machine. A high-level $O(N\log N)$ source scanner with $O(N)$ retained indexing is not an admissible $O(N^{1/2})$-space proof. Under canonical known block order a source stream might require less auxiliary storage, but no such general small-space algorithm is proved for this signed-renaming-invariant parser. No physical power-short ordinary SAT child, blank-total index accessor, hereditary closure or bounded guest-$A$ simulation was built.
