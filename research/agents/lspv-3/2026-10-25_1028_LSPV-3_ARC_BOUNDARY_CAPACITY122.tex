\section{Exact construction and theorem}
Fix $b\ge4$, an ordered vector of ORIGINAL
boundary variables $Y=(y_1,\ldots,y_b)$,
$X=\{0,1\}^b$, $M=2^b$.
For each balanced $S\subseteq X$ with $|S|=M/2$,
define $F_{b,S}(Y,U_S)$ by forbidding each
$u\in X\setminus S$. Set $L_i(u)=y_i$
when $u_i=0$, and $L_i(u)=\neg y_i$
when $u_i=1$. Replace the length-$b$
clause $\bigvee_i L_i(u)$ by the proper
signed-ternary chain on $b-3$ fresh private
source-distinct auxiliary names:
\[
(L_1\vee L_2\vee z_1),\quad
(\neg z_{j-1}\vee L_{j+1}\vee z_j)
 \quad(2\le j\le b-3),\quad
(\neg z_{b-3}\vee L_{b-1}\vee L_b).
\tag{1}
\]
The middle range is empty for $b=4$.
Each clause has three distinct variable identities.
All $u$-gadgets have disjoint private names, so
\[
 \{w:\exists U_S\,F_{b,S}(w,U_S)\}=S.            \tag{2}
\]
There are $(b-2)2^{b-1}$ clauses and at most
$b+(b-3)2^b$ variable names. Complete standard
DIMACS bit length, including signed decimal identifiers,
headers, spacing and zero terminators, obeys
$N(F_{b,S})=O(b^2 2^b)$.

An A-assisted \emph{standalone source-erasing capsule}
$q=E_A(F)$ includes ALL surviving source-dependent
children, join tables, original-name metadata,
indexes, caches, policy, persistent state and glue.
A fixed receiver $D_A(q,C)$ may also call the SAME
fixed $A$, but not reread discarded $F$ nor use
hidden per-input advice. For every compatible
ordinary proper-3CNF continuation $C(Y,V)$
with new disjoint private identifiers, both-label
semantics demand
\[
D_A(E_A(F),C)=
\mathbf1[\operatorname{SAT}(F(Y,U)\wedge C(Y,V))].
\tag{3}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact boundary capsule capacity]
On the balanced family, every encoder/receiver
satisfying (3) must have
\[
\max_{|S|=M/2}|E_A(F_{b,S})|
\ge\log_2 {M\choose M/2}-1
\ge2^b-\log_2(2^b+1)-1.
\tag{4}
\]
Here length counts complete surviving BINARY bits.
No fixed exponent $\lambda<1$ admits a
uniform all-input $O(N^\lambda)$ source-erasing
all-continuations capsule, even if its compiler
can use the arbitrary fixed SAT decider $A$.
On infinitely many sources the capsule size is
$\Omega(N/(\log N)^2)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every $w\in X$, the original-name condition
$Y=w$ has a proper ternary PIN representation:
for each coordinate, force literal $\ell_i$
using the four clauses
\[
(\ell_i\vee a_i\vee c_i),\
(\ell_i\vee a_i\vee\neg c_i),\
(\ell_i\vee\neg a_i\vee c_i),\
(\ell_i\vee\neg a_i\vee\neg c_i)
\]
on two fresh private variables. Denote the
conjunction by $P_w$. Formula (2) gives
$\operatorname{SAT}(F_{b,S}\wedge P_w)
\iff w\in S$. For distinct $S,T$, select
$w\in S\triangle T$. The two sources
have opposite labels against the SAME
continuation $P_w$, so (3) forbids identical
capsules, regardless of how the fixed $A$ is
used. There are $\binom{M}{M/2}$
different supports but at most $2^{L+1}-1$
binary strings of length at most $L$.
This gives the first bound in (4).
Since the central binomial coefficient is
largest among $M+1$ coefficients summing
to $2^M$, it is at least $2^M/(M+1)$,
giving the second. For fixed $\lambda<1$,
$N^\lambda=O(b^{2\lambda}2^{\lambda b})
=o(2^b)$, proving nonuniformity of the
proposed $N^\lambda$ upper bound. On
a witness for (4), $b=O(\log N)$,
yielding $\Omega(N/(\log N)^2)$.
\end{proof}

ALL-CONTINUATIONS COMPLETE, SOURCE-ERASING
standalone interface. Every balanced source
is SAT by construction; arbitrary pinned
membership is readable from its explicit
source clauses using small indexed memory.
Therefore no general SAT decision lower
bound follows. A genuine FLMV solver may
reread the immutable parent, regenerate
child bits, use Horn/SCC/affine/graph-factor/DP,

peak writable bits; a streamed output need
not incur that space but must pay per-bit
generation, replay and source-access time.

and cumulative bit-time are separately counted.
