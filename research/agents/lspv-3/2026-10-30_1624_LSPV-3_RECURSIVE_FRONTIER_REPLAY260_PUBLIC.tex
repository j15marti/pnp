Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i$ be original proper CNF with authenticated signed occurrences. Let
$B_i$ contain the variables shared by prefix $C_1,\ldots,C_i$ and suffix
$C_{i+1},\ldots,C_m$, let $U_i=B_{i-1}\cup{\rm Var}(C_i)$ and $w=\max_i|U_i|$.
For assignment $\beta$ to $B_i$, $L_i(\beta)$ asserts satisfaction of the entire
prefix; $R_i(\beta)$ asserts satisfaction of the entire suffix. Their exact
inductively justified recurrences are
\[
L_i(\beta)=\bigvee_{a|_{B_i}=\beta}
 [L_{i-1}(a|_{B_{i-1}})\land C_i(a)],\quad
R_{i-1}(\alpha)=\bigvee_{a|_{B_{i-1}}=\alpha}
 [C_i(a)\land R_i(a|_{B_i})].
\]
For original local clause pattern $u$, define
\[
H_i(u)=\bigvee_{a|_{{\rm Var}(C_i)}=u}
 [L_{i-1}(a|_{B_{i-1}})\land C_i(a)\land R_i(a|_{B_i})].
\]
Thus $H_i(u)=1$ exactly when $u$ extends to a global satisfying assignment.
\paragraph{Theorem and proof.}
One can stream all $H_i(u)$ with a divide-and-conquer recursion that retains
$O(\log(m+1))$ simultaneous frontier tables. Given global boundary
$(L_a,R_b)$ on interval $(a,b]$, recompute $L_h$ forward from $a$ to midpoint
$h$ and $R_h$ backward from $b$ to $h$; recurse on the two halves.
At a singleton, perform the displayed exact three-factor join.
Induction on the interval length proves all results. Every recursion level
traverses at most $m$ source clauses, giving charged indexed-word work
\[
O\left(N+\log(m+1)\sum_i
 [d_{i-1}+d_i+2^{|U_i|}{\rm poly}(|U_i|+k+\log N)]
 +mk2^k\right)
\]
and simultaneously live writable bits
\[
O(2^w(w+\log N)\log(m+1)+S_B+\log^2(N+2)).
\]
Here $N$ is source bits, $k$ maximum clause width, $d_i$ is \emph{charged}
boundary acquisition time and $S_B$ is iterator space; streaming output bits
are charged, and retaining the output in RAM cancels the streaming-space claim.
Under the recognizable source band grammar $|v-i|\le b$, source verification
acquires $b$ by an input scan; inspecting $O(b)$ adjacent clauses discovers
each $B_i$ in $O(b^2k)$ occurrence work and $O(bk\log N)$ scratch.
Numeric band is not invariant under variable renaming and is not hardness.
