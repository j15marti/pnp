\section{Target, model and three falsifiable mechanisms}
Fix one NP-complete language \(\mathcal L\): properly encoded signed 3CNF with three \emph{distinct} source variables per clause, no repeated signed clause, and exactly two positive and two negative occurrences per original variable. Invalid encodings have label zero. Its monotone restriction is NP-complete by D\"ocker \cite{Docker}. Use \(n\) for original variables, \(m=4n/3\) for ternary clauses, two header words and four words per clause, word width \(w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil\), and encoded bit length \(N=(4m+2)w\). A word read/write costs one declared indexed-word operation; conversion to a bit machine must account for addresses and \(w\) bits. Set \(k\) only for genuinely added ternaries and \(d\) only for eliminated source variables. No syntactic residual \(r=n-d\) is used here.

A \emph{legal sign swap} exchanges a positive and a negative occurrence of the same original variable, preserving the ordered unsigned sockets and each \((2,2)\) quota. For the \emph{repair objective only}, intermediate or final signed clause duplicates are permitted as distinguishable named slots; duplicates have ordinary conjunction semantics. This ambient-socket convention is necessary because swaps need not preserve the input language's no-duplicate restriction. The input to the repair problem itself \emph{must} lie in \(\mathcal L\).

Define the independently specified structural quantity
\[
\Delta(F)=\min\{t:\text{some sequence of }t\text{ legal source-socket sign swaps
 produces a SAT signing of }F\}.
\tag{1}
\]
\(\Delta\) is not running time, witness count, a syntactic residual, or an algorithm-specific state. Finite repair existence follows from the capacity-matching construction of Role 2: every clause can select an occurrence and no variable need supply more than two selected occurrences. For each variable selected in both polarities, swapping an unselected positive with a selected negative coheres its selected witnesses. Thus \(\Delta(F)\le\lfloor m/2\rfloor\).

\noindent\textbf{Mechanism M1, PROVED/EXACT:} disjoint-component sign-repair distance is \emph{exactly additive}. \textbf{Mechanism M2, PROVED/EXACT with imported fixed UNSAT certificate:} minimizing sign repair is NP-complete at threshold one on a polynomial-time recognizable input family whose \emph{ordinary SAT label is always UNSAT}. \textbf{Mechanism M3, FALSIFIED/RETIRED:} NP-hardness or large repair distance forces a universal exponential root-decision cost on that family; a direct prefix recognizer defeats it.

The universal separation target remains
\[
\forall A\ \forall c>0\ \forall N_0\
\exists F\in\mathcal L:\ |F|\ge N_0\land T_A(F)>|F|^c,
\tag{2}
\]
for every total uniform deterministic exact decider \(A\). None of M1--M3 proves (2).

\section{Exact additivity and the fixed opposite-label seed}
For signed formulas on disjoint variable identifiers, write \(F\sqcup H\) for their ordered clause concatenation with the second formula's variables offset. The two copies have disjoint socket sets.
\begin{theorem}[Disjoint repair additivity; PROVED/EXACT]
For every pair of individually quota-balanced signed formulas \(F,H\),
\[
\boxed{\Delta(F\sqcup H)=\Delta(F)+\Delta(H).}\tag{3}
\]
This remains true if either formula is UNSAT and if the ambient repair calculus permits signed clause duplicates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every legal swap acts on just one original variable and hence belongs entirely to exactly one component. If a repaired conjunction is satisfiable, restricting its final assignment to either component proves each component was repaired to SAT. The swaps used in the two components number at least \(\Delta(F)\) and \(\Delta(H)\), proving the lower bound. Conversely take independently optimal sequences and satisfying assignments; concatenate the sequences and assignments. The disjoint union is SAT after exactly \(\Delta(F)+\Delta(H)\) swaps. Intermediate duplicate slots, when present, remain within components and do not affect the proof.
\end{proof}

Let \(U\) denote the 198-variable, 264-clause strictly monotone UNSAT exact-\((2,2)\) source from D\"ocker, reconstructed and certified in LSPV-2's DOECKER-CORE-CERT61. Its source text is

Let \(V\) be obtained from \(U\) by exchanging the signs of \(x_3\) at zero-based locations \((3,1)\) and \((13,0)\). LSPV-4's TWO-SIGN-SWAP64 and Role 2's POLARITY-REPAIR63 provide a directly checkable satisfying assignment of \(V\). Source reconstruction and witness replay were independently repeated in this run. \[
\operatorname{SAT}(U)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{SAT}(V)=1,\qquad
\boxed{\Delta(U)=1,\quad\Delta(V)=0}.
\tag{4}
\]

\section{Hard repair optimization within a trivial-root family}
For an arbitrary valid \(F\in\mathcal L\) and \(p\ge1\), define the source-coherent disjoint sum
\[
G_p(F)=U\sqcup\underbrace{F\sqcup\cdots\sqcup F}_{p\text{ copies, fresh names}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Every original clause retains exactly its original sign, all original variables keep their source identity within the renamed component, and no intercomponent identification is performed. This is an \emph{answer-blind construction} and never calls a SAT solver.

\begin{theorem}[Root-trivial repair-hard island; PROVED/EXACT]
The canonically encoded family
\(\mathcal I_1=\{G_1(F):F\in\mathcal L\}\)
is recognizable in deterministic polynomial time and every member has root SAT label zero. Nevertheless
\[
\boxed{\Delta(G_p(F))=1+p\Delta(F),\qquad
\operatorname{SAT}(G_p(F))=0.}\tag{6}
\]
The language
\[
\boxed{\{G\in\mathcal I_1:\Delta(G)\le1\}}
\tag{7}
\]
is NP-complete. Its promise version on \(\mathcal I_1\) is NP-hard even though ordinary SAT is constantly UNSAT there.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (6) follows by repeated application of (3) and (4); ordinary SAT of the conjunction fails because it contains unchanged \(U\).
For (7), a certificate consists of zero or one swap's two same-variable socket locations and a complete satisfying assignment for the repaired signed formula. Validation of the original grammar, the swap, and the assignment is polynomial. Given any valid input \(F\) from the fixed NP-complete signed exact-\((2,2)\) language, output \(G_1(F)\) by copying the constant prefix \(U\) and renaming \(F\). Then \(\Delta(G_1(F))\le1\iff\Delta(F)=0\iff F\in SAT\). This is a uniform polynomial-time many-one reduction from SAT \emph{to the repair-threshold problem}, not to SAT on the output. The family is polynomial-time recognizable by checking its fixed prefix, its disjoint name ranges, and the suffix grammar. Every recognized member is UNSAT.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Arbitrarily amplified repair gap; PROVED/EXACT]
For any fixed constant \(0<\varepsilon<1\), a polynomial-time \(M^\varepsilon\)-factor feasible-repair approximation on the root-UNSAT family \(\{G_p(F):p=N_F^K,\ F\in\mathcal L\}\), where \(M=|G_p(F)|\) and \(K>\varepsilon/(1-\varepsilon)\) is fixed, would imply \(P=NP\). Thus the claimed approximation is conditionally impossible unless \(P=NP\), even on a polynomial-time recognizable family with constant ordinary SAT answer.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For inputs \(F\) of encoded length \(N_F\), choose integral \(p=\lceil N_F^K\rceil\). For fixed \(K\), \(M=\Theta(pN_F)\) up to constant word-width factors because all records are explicitly copied and \(w\) grows logarithmically. On SAT inputs \(\Delta(G_p(F))=1\), so the feasible output must use at most \(M^\varepsilon\) swaps. On UNSAT inputs the optimum is at least \(1+p\), so every feasible output requires at least \(1+p\) swaps. The condition on \(K\) gives \(M^\varepsilon<1+p\) for all sufficiently large \(N_F\). A polynomial approximation would therefore decide SAT by comparing its output repair count with \(1+p\). Finitely many small input lengths can be handled by fixed tables. This is an NP-hardness gap, not an unconditional superpolynomial machine-time lower bound.
\end{proof}

\section{Cheap exact root decider and failure of an exponential-distance rule}
For an arbitrary total exact decider \(A\) of \(\mathcal L\), define \(A^\dagger\): parse the declared fixed-width header and check whether the first 264 ternary records, with variables 1 through 198, equal the constant source \(U\). If yes, output UNSAT without reading the suffix. Otherwise execute \(A\) with every access charged. This procedure is total, uniform, and exact on the \emph{full} language, including malformed encodings: a valid formula with the prefix contains an UNSAT conjunct; a malformed source is rejected regardless. On \(\mathcal I_1\) and \(G_p(U)\), it reads a constant number of indexed words and performs at most \(\operatorname{polylog}M\) bit operations for address/header decoding. On a sequential bit representation the explicit fixed prefix is \(O(\log M)\) bits under the stated encoding.

Yet
\[
\Delta(\underbrace{U\sqcup\cdots\sqcup U}_{p\text{ copies}})=p,\qquad
M=\Theta(p\log p),\qquad
T_{A^\dagger}=O(\operatorname{polylog}M)\text{ on these sources}.
\tag{8}
\]
For every fixed \(a>0,b\ge0\), the statement
\(T_A(F)\ge2^{a\Delta(F)}/|F|^b\) \emph{on every sufficiently large such source and every decider} is therefore \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}. This does not falsify a different algorithm-dependent measure \(r_A\) or the existential hard-input quantifiers of (2).

In particular, the difficulty of \emph{finding a minimum repair} cannot automatically be charged to an algorithm required to answer only the one-bit SAT label of the unmodified input. The same warning applies to large flow-cut families, backdoors, fixed-witness NAE completions, certificate minimization, hashes, timers, or structurally difficult promises.

\section{Exact accounting, direction and transport}
If the input \(F\) has \(n_F,m_F,N_F=(4m_F+2)w_F\), then \(G_p(F)\) has
\[
n'=198+pn_F,\quad m'=264+pm_F,\quad
w'=\left\lceil\log_2(64(n'+m'+1))\right\rceil,\quad
M=(4m'+2)w'.
\tag{9}
\]
Relative to one copy of \(F\), there are \(k=264+(p-1)m_F\) \emph{added ternary clauses}, \(d=0\) eliminated source variables, zero additional variables not belonging to a renamed component, and zero SAT-oracle calls. The transducer reads every source record, writes every output record, validates renamed supports, and uses \(O(M)\) bit time on a standard tape or \(O(m'+n')\) indexed word operations with address width \(w'\). It can stream the output in \(O(\log M)\) counter space excluding immutable U and source, or materialize it in \(O(M)\) peak bits. SAT witness verification for an alleged repair reads \(O(M)\) bits; discovering the optimum is the NP-hard \emph{separate} task. Every failed swap guess in an exhaustive search is charged.

For a \emph{SAT-preserving} reduction \(R\) of source length \(N\) into a target of length \(g(N)\), with cost \(R(N)\), other overhead \(H\), \(Q\) target calls and model conversion \(\tau\), only
\[
L_{\rm source}\le R(N)+H+Q\tau T_{\rm target}^{\le}(g(N))
\tag{10}
\]
is generally justified, subject to query and worst-case quantifier alignment. The present map \(F\mapsto G_p(F)\) is \emph{not SAT-preserving}: its target SAT label is identically zero. Equation (10) cannot be applied to transfer SAT hardness through it. Instead (5) is a standard correct many-one reduction from SAT to \emph{repair-threshold optimization}. The old 2-SAT chain's \(q\) probes are likewise consumed by explicit \(\Omega(q)\) target construction in the Roles 4--5 ladder; a larger NP-complete target language by itself produces no amplification.

\section{Integrated proof spine and next falsifiable test}
\[
\underbrace{\text{independently defined structural obstruction}}_{\Delta\text{ can be large or NP-hard to optimize}}
\quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
\underbrace{\text{necessary charged SAT work}}_{\textbf{FIRST OPEN ARROW}}
\longrightarrow
\underbrace{\text{every exact uniform decider}}_{\textbf{OPEN}}
\longrightarrow P\ne NP.
\]
A defensible corrected candidate is to fix an explicit hard family with \emph{no polynomially recognizable UNSAT subformula or easy structural normalizer} and prove an all-algorithm lower bound on \emph{actual bounded-run general computation}, with all source reads, promise validation, failed compression, DAG reuse and direct decision included. Absence of recognizable easy patterns is not, by itself, a computable hardness certificate; this remains \textbf{OPEN}. A useful next experiment is to score the actual 198/264 \(U,V\) pair and multiple independently renamed components under matching-cover, affine, SCC and source-prefix algorithms while explicitly deleting the fixed UNSAT prefix to see which claimed obstructions survive on \emph{unknown} root labels. The five research schedules are unchanged.
