\section{Source-sign construction}
Let H(U,X) be a proper 3CNF with universal variables U and existential variables X. Define
\[
 Z(t;p,q)=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}
 (\neg t\vee\epsilon p\vee\delta q).
\]
For each universal variable introduce ordinary selector s_i and fresh a_i,c_i,p_i,q_i,p'_i,q'_i. Write
\[
 F_H(b)=H(S,X)\wedge\bigwedge_i
 [Z(a_i;p_i,q_i)\wedge Z(c_i;p'_i,q'_i)\wedge
 (\ell_i(b_i)\vee a_i\vee c_i)]
\]
where \ell_i(1)=s_i and \ell_i(0)=\neg s_i. The four clauses of Z force t=0. Hence F_H(b) is SAT iff there exists X with H(b,X), and changing any one b_i toggles exactly one literal-sign input bit in the fixed-width proper-3CNF encoding. All completions share length, metadata, clause positions and variable identifiers.
