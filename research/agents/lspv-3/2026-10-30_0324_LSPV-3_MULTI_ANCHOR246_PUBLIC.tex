\section{Signed original-clause semantics}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{m-1}C_j$ be a proper 3CNF on globally shared original variables.
Each signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$ has polarity $\sigma_{i,j}=1$ for positive
and $0$ for negative. Its local extreme all-literals-TRUE target $S_j$
requires $x_i=\sigma_{i,j}$ and its all-literals-FALSE target $U_j$
requires $x_i=1-\sigma_{i,j}$; inversion interchanges them.
Ordinary disjunctive clause SAT means at least one, not three, true literal.
For original clause pair $j,k$, $SS$ and $UU$ are feasible iff no opposite
signed shared variable exists; $SU$ and $US$ are feasible iff no equal
signed shared variable exists. A pair having both kinds of shared occurrence
admits no jointly extreme local states, but the clauses can both be SAT in
middle states. For coherent full $a$, $t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a)$,
$N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a)$. For actual flip set $H$,
\[
t_j(a\oplus H)=t_j(a)+|H\cap L_j^{false}(a)|-|H\cap L_j^{true}(a)|.
\]

\section{Certified multi-anchor composition}
Fix an original-variable partial cube $\rho$, and designate
$V\subseteq[n]\setminus\operatorname{dom}(\rho)$, $f=|V|$.
Other unpinned coordinates outside $V$ remain entirely unrestricted.
For anchors $a^{(1)},\dots,a^{(k)}\in\{0,1\}^V$ and radii $r_i$,
source-authenticated certificates establish that every full model
$b\supseteq\rho$ satisfies
$d_H(b|_V,a^{(i)})\ge r_i$.
Each source witness for nearby $V$-assignments must falsify an
\emph{original} clause supported entirely in
$\operatorname{dom}(\rho)\cup V$; this ensures the witness applies
regardless of outside free coordinates, with no free oracle bit.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-$k$ forbidden cube]
Once the certificates have been acquired and checked at charged cost $A$,
the intersection of all necessary distance inequalities can be tested
by a deterministic capped-distance dynamic program in
\[
O(A+kf\prod_{i=1}^k(r_i+1))
\]
indexed-RAM operations and $O(N+kf+\prod_i(r_i+1))$ words of workspace
plus retained certificate storage. If no assignment on $V$ meets all
inequalities, \emph{every} complete extension of $\rho$ violates $F$.
For constant $k$ with polynomially constructed certificates this test
is polynomial. Arbitrary discovery and increasing $k$ are not free.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with capped vector $(0,\ldots,0)$. For each designated free
coordinate $v$ and choice $z\in\{0,1\}$ replace
$d_i$ by $\min(r_i,d_i+[z\ne a^{(i)}_v])$.
There are at most $\prod_i(r_i+1)$ states per layer, two
transitions per state, each costing $O(k)$, and only two layers stored.
A satisfying full extension supplies a path to the all-threshold vector.
Its non-reachability excludes the entire cube, including other free variables.
\end{proof}

For radius $2$, one directly checkable certificate is a list of $f+1$
original clause IDs, each falsified respectively by the anchor itself
and by each of its $f$ single flips on $V$. The named clauses can involve
only variables in $\operatorname{dom}(\rho)\cup V$. Discovery by full source
scans costs $O((f+1)N)$ per anchor; replay and final verification are charged.

\begin{theorem}[Four-body obstruction not present in any triple]
On three designated free coordinates let anchors be
$111,100,010,001$ with radius $2$ each. Every one-, two-, and
three-anchor subfamily has a coherent satisfying assignment to its
distance conditions, but the fourfold system has none.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The anchors are precisely odd-parity ternary words, and each pair has
distance two. The omitted anchor satisfies all the other three radii.
Any odd-parity word violates its own radius, and any even-parity
word is at distance one from an odd-parity word. Thus the intersection
of all four feasible regions is empty.
\end{proof}
