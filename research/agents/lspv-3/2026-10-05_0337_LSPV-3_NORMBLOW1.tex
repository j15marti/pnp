\section*{Exact parameter calculation}
Fix constant \(\eta>0\) and underlying nondeterministic time \(T\). In the checked Theorem-24 scaling,
\[
L=T\,\operatorname{polylog}T,\quad m=T^\eta,\quad h=m^{100}=T^{100\eta},
\]
\(r\) is the least power of two with \(h^r\ge L\), \(\Delta=hr\), and
\[
p\in[\Delta^{100},2\Delta^{100}].
\]
Let \(N=p^r\). Since \(h^r\ge T\operatorname{polylog}T\),
\[
100\eta r\ge1+o(1).
\]
Minimality of the power-of-two \(r\) implies that \(r/2\) fails the threshold asymptotically, hence
\[
50\eta r\le1+o(1).
\]
For constant \(r\),
\[
p=T^{10000\eta+o(1)}
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{N=T^{\nu+o(1)},\qquad \nu=10000\eta r,}
\]
where
\[
\boxed{100+o(1)\le\nu\le200+o(1).}
\]

\[
