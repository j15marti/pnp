\section{Canonical proper-3CNF and exact recognizer}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be connected, simple and 3-regular. Assign Boolean variable $x_e$ to each edge and charge $c_v\in\{0,1\}$ at each vertex. Define
\[
T(G,c)=\bigwedge_{v\in V}\left(\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=c_v\right).
\tag{1}
\]
The degree-three equation is represented by its four falsifying-assignment clauses of width exactly three, with three different variables. In a fixed record encoding, $n=3|V|/2$, $m=4|V|$ and
\[
N=(16|V|+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\right\rceil
=\Theta(|V|\log(|V|+2)).
\tag{2}
\]
A deterministic recognizer fully reads and sorts signed clauses by unsigned triple; checks each triple has precisely four distinct sign patterns forming one forbidden parity class; constructs one graph vertex per triple and checks that each variable occurs at exactly two different triples. It computes the charge as the complement of the forbidden parity. Invalid clauses, duplicates, inconsistent groups and malformed encodings are rejected as noncertified, and a general exact fallback solver receives the source.

The complete recognition and grouping cost is $O(m\log(m+2))$ word operations on $\Theta(\log N)$-bit addressed words and $O(m)$ live words, or conservatively polynomial bit time and workspace on a standard multitape machine. No family membership oracle or uncharged graph representation is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[Component parity and witness, PROVED/EXACT]
For every accepted source with recovered graph components $C_1,\ldots,C_h$,
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(T(G,c))=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall i\ \bigoplus_{v\in C_i}c_v=0.
\tag{3}
\]
On SAT, a witness can be reconstructed in $O(n+m)$ additional word operations.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Summing the equations in one graph component eliminates each edge variable twice and forces even total charge. Conversely assign non-tree edges zero and solve each spanning-tree edge by leaf elimination. At the final root the equation holds because total charge is even. The construction is exact, deterministic, and the assignment can be replayed on all original clauses.
\end{proof}

Fix any total exact general proper-3SAT decider $D$. Define the uniform exact decider $A_{\oplus}$ that recognizes the above grammar, directly returns its component-parity label when recognized, and otherwise invokes $D$. It dynamically selects an algorithm from information paid for during source inspection. Both labels take $\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time on every recognized source; all other sources are still decided exactly, possibly slowly.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial-cost Resolution extraction fails, PROVED/EXACT from KNOWN THEOREM]
No transformation $X$ and polynomial $p$ can, for \emph{every} total uniform exact decider $A$ and UNSAT proper-3CNF $F$, output an explicit ordinary Resolution refutation within
\[
p(N+T_A(F)+|A|)
\tag{4}
\]
bit operations, even when allowed to instrument or invoke $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $A=A_{\oplus}$. For a family of simple 3-regular expanders $G_v$, use odd charges $c$. Equation (3) makes the direct 3CNF UNSAT, while $A_{\oplus}$ decides it in polynomial time. The known expander Tseitin theorem states that every ordinary Resolution refutation of these direct clauses has $2^{\Omega(v)}$ clauses. Explicitly writing the proposed output therefore requires superpolynomial time in $N=\Theta(v\log v)$, contradicting (4).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial-cost full-DNNF extraction fails, PROVED/EXACT from KNOWN THEOREM]
No transformation can, on every SAT proper-3CNF $F$ and for every total exact $A$, output a complete DNNF representing the satisfying-assignment function of $F$ within time polynomial in $N+T_A(F)+|A|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use even-charge copies of the same bounded-degree expander family. The recognizer and optional leaf-elimination witness are polynomial. Expander treewidth is $\Omega(v)$, and de Colnet--Mengel's lower bound gives $2^{\Omega(\operatorname{tw}(G_v))}$ DNNF size for the satisfiable Tseitin function on $G_v$. Output length contradicts polynomial extraction. Root SAT and one witness remain easy.
\end{proof}

These are formal impossibilities for compulsory representation extraction, not a proof that a general exact SAT decider exists in polynomial time. In particular they do not disprove the required $\forall A\,\exists F_A$ worst-case lower-bound quantifiers: $A_{\oplus}$ may be slow off the recognized family.
