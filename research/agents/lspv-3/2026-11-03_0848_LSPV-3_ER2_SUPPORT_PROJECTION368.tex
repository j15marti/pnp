\section*{Exact restricted projection theorem}
Define $E$ to be only ordinary strict Resolution and paid, acyclic, conservative signed binary AND definitions $y\leftrightarrow(a\land b)$ with $y$ a fresh variable, distinct fanin variables, and $a,b$ literals on originals or strictly older auxiliaries. One paid gate makes available the three clause slots $(\neg y\vee a)$, $(\neg y\vee b)$, $(y\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)$; these are not free clauses. A refutation contains $q$ distinct charged RES nodes, $e$ paid definition nodes and $s$ distinct \emph{original} variables appearing directly as inputs of any definitions. Evidently $s\le 2e$. Sound shared DAGs, reuse, arbitrary order and temporary growth are allowed. No semantic oracle, free weakening, AND3, GF$(2)$ or LOCAL-CONVERT belongs to E.

\begin{theorem}[Support-limited ER2-to-Resolution projection]
For every valid UNSAT original CNF $F$, and every sound finite E-refutation $\pi$ of $F$ with parameters $q,e,s$, there exists an ordinary source-authenticated Resolution refutation $\rho$ of the same $F$ satisfying
\[
\boxed{q_{\mathrm{Res}}(\rho)\le 2^s(q+1)-1\le 4^e(q+1)-1.}
\]
The quantifier prefix is $\forall F\,\forall\pi\,\exists\rho$. The bound counts distinct derived Resolution nodes, not encoded bytes or machine work.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an assignment $\alpha$ to the $s$ original input-support variables of definitions. Evaluate all gates in creation order under $\alpha$. Their three defining slots are all true. Restricting the existing Resolution DAG by the resulting assignment to original support and auxiliary variables, removing satisfied lines and forwarding subsuming parents across assigned pivots, leaves a valid Resolution refutation of $F\restriction\alpha$ of at most $q$ RES steps. This is ordinary stability of Resolution under restrictions.

Lift each such proof back to the \emph{original} source clauses, not weakened clauses. A surviving restricted source $C\restriction\alpha$ corresponds to the original $C$ consisting of its surviving literals and only literals false under $\alpha$. Replaying a surviving pivot with the unrestricted parents keeps this false-literal baggage; if a pivot is fixed, forward the stronger parent. Induction yields a clause $D_\alpha$ using at most $q$ strict Resolution inferences from the original sources with $D_\alpha\subseteq B_\alpha$, where $B_\alpha$ is the clause of all literals false under the assignment $\alpha$ to $S$. No auxiliary literal remains in the lifted proof.

Merge the $2^s$ clauses $D_\alpha$ along a complete binary assignment tree on the variables of $S$. At each pair of siblings differing on $x$, either one clause omits the pivot and can be forwarded, or Resolution on the complementary pivot produces a clause blocking the common prefix. There are at most $2^s-1$ internal Resolution nodes. The root clause is empty. Total bound: $2^s q+(2^s-1)$. Sharing can only reduce the number of charged nodes. Proof search and actual execution are not bounded by this count.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[PHP conservative-extension support lower bound]
There is $c>0$ such that for every sufficiently large $n$ every E-refutation of $P_n$ satisfies
\[
\boxed{2e+\log_2(q+1)\ge cn.}
\]
Hence $q\le n^d$ for a fixed $d$ forces $e=\Omega(n)$. No superpolynomial lower bound on $e+q$ follows.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Haken's exponential proof-size bound also holds for charged derived Resolution nodes, after subtracting the polynomially many source clauses and shrinking the exponent constant if needed. Combining that with the theorem gives $2^{cn}\le4^e(q+1)$ for large $n$. Take binary logarithms. When $q$ is polynomial, its logarithm is $O(\log n)$.
\end{proof}

\section*{Transfer limitations, false leads, and falsifiers}
This projection is exponential in $e$, not a polynomial backwards simulation for general ER2. It does not cover the additional AND3, GF$(2)$ and LOCAL-CONVERT rules in unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. Odd Tseitin has a cheap GF$(2)$ route. Heule--Kiesl--Biere [4] show polynomial propagation-redundancy pigeonhole proofs \emph{without any new variables}, independently falsifying any unrestricted extension-variable hardness predictor. F1, backwards polynomial mixed-to-RES PHP, is FALSIFIED by known ER2 proofs; F2, a conservative gate slot survives its correct evaluated gate assignment, is impossible; F3, polynomial $q$ and $e=o(n)$ for PHP in E, is impossible by the corollary; F4, the same theorem unchanged for all GF$(2)$, PR or opaque LOCAL-CONVERT, is not established, and its unqualified extension-count version is falsified by PR.

The three bridges are distinct. B1 is $\forall A\exists p_A\forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}$, $W_R(F)\le p_A(|\operatorname{enc}F|+T_A(F))$. B2 is $\forall A\exists p_A\forall N$, $\overline W_R(N)\le p_A(N+T_A^{wc}(N))$, where $A$ is one fixed total correct solver on all SAT, UNSAT and malformed binary strings. B3 is $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)=N^{O(1)}$ for this \emph{same preselected} R. B1 implies B2 implies B3; none is proved. No machine-time lower bound comes from the theorem.
