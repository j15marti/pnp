\section*{LSPV-3: reconstruction dependency and quantitative-collapse audit}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. No unrestricted SAT lower bound is proved here.

\paragraph{Preflight and current verified state.}
LSPV-2 FEAT1 is substantive, correctly headed and sentinelled, and readable from GitHub, so predecessor preflight passes. The current verified MAIN retains two reconstruction obligations: one common size-normalized $Q$ in the same realization, and the universal NO-SAT block. LSPV-4 ACQ1 additionally proves that, once an explicit succinct legal-state verifier exists, common-kernel acquisition is an NP search problem under the project counterfactual but must be charged rather than treated as free.

\paragraph{Two obstacles.}
The first obstacle is dependency: after fixing the shared $Q$ and Arthur randomness, exact answers to the reconstruction SAT family still require certifying both YES and NO answers, and the NO certificate is universal over assignments to an instance selected downstream. The second is quantitative model matching: every elimination by the assumed SAT decider $A$ requires an explicit computation-to-SAT compiler and its own size exponent. A qualitative PH collapse does not supply a cheaper compiler.

\paragraph{Primary-literature boundary.}
Viola proves $\mathrm{BPTime}(t)\subseteq\Sigma_3\mathrm{Time}(t\,\mathrm{polylog}\,t)$ and states that this simulation relativizes. van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski supply the resilient reconstruction architecture used by the project. Robson gives an $O(T\log T)$ RAM-to-SAT reduction in his declared RAM model. Bogdanov--Trevisan show strong PH-collapse consequences for generic nonadaptive NP self-correction, so an LWE-style black-box reduction architecture cannot be imported as an unconditional generic NP theorem.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: bundled exact-answer predicate.}
Fix one acquired, exactly size-normalized shared $Q$, output coordinate $i$, and Arthur coins $r$. Let $J$ be the constant family of SAT instances generated by reconstruction, including the success/output consistency tests. For claimed answer vector $a\in\{0,1\}^{J}$, write $\Phi_j(y)$ for the Boolean verifier of satisfiability of instance $j$. Then exact correctness is
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)
=
\exists Y^+\,\forall Y^-
\bigwedge_{j\in J}
\left[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))
\wedge(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))\right].
}
\]
All YES witnesses can be bundled into one existential block and all NO assignments into one universal block because $|J|=O(1)$. This bundling changes constants and tuple lengths, not the alternation depth.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: eager-oracle factorization.}
Assume the fixed exact SAT decider $A$ runs in $S^{k+o(1)}$ time. If each reconstruction query produced from a length-$L$ computation has SAT encoding size $L^{b_0+o(1)}$, all constantly many queries, YES and NO alike, are decided in
\[
L^{b_0k+o(1)}.
\]
Complementing the output of the same exact decider handles UNSAT at the same exponent. Thus, if $\alpha_0$ is the pre-oracle reconstruction exponent,
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm BP}\le \alpha_0 b_0k+o(1).}
\]
The YES and NO sides do not create two separate powers of $k$.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: recursive elimination recurrence.}
Consider a quantified predicate whose current deterministic matrix is decidable in time $n^{\alpha+o(1)}$. Suppose a model-matched compiler maps the corresponding existential search predicate to SAT of size $n^{b\alpha+o(1)}$. One invocation of $A$ therefore gives a deterministic decision procedure in
\[
n^{b\alpha k+o(1)}.
\]
Complementing $A$ handles a universal block after negating the matrix, with the same leading exponent. Hence the conservative recursive rule is
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{q+1}\le b_q k\,\alpha_q+o(1).}
\]
Iterating through the reconstruction-oracle elimination and three post-Viola levels yields
\[
\boxed{
\alpha_{\rm det}
\le
\alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1).
}
\]
This is an upper-bound accounting recurrence under the declared compilers, not a lower bound and not a theorem that $k^4$ is necessary.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: what a genuine exponent improvement must do.}
Bundling polynomially or constantly many same-polarity witnesses does not remove a $k$ factor in the recurrence, because the factor comes from invoking $A$ after compiling a predicate whose deterministic matrix already carries the preceding exponent. Therefore a genuine $k^4\to k^3$ improvement must eliminate one invocation layer, or prove a direct structured compiler whose construction and output size are asymptotically cheaper than that recursive layer. Merely rewriting several SAT questions as one conjunction, complementing NO answers, or citing $P=NP\Rightarrow PH=P$ does not accomplish this.

\paragraph{OPEN: dependency criterion for the only plausible local flattening.}
The late-substitution view remains schematically
\[
\boxed{
\exists\sigma\;\forall u\;\exists(\ell,a,Y^+)\;\forall Y^-\;R.
}
\]
The last universal tuple ranges over assignments to SAT instances whose descriptions depend on the preceding reconstruction choices. A quantifier commutation would require a construction-specific independence or uniformization theorem, for example a single upstream object $Z$ from which the required NO certificates for every legal downstream instance can be checked without reintroducing universal search. No such theorem is currently present in vMS, Viola, or the project reports. Generic canonical or unique binding is not free and cannot be substituted for this missing uniformization.

\paragraph{Shared-$Q$ and cost ledger.}
The quantifier analysis assumes one SAME\_REALIZATION $Q$ is acquired before reconstruction and then reused. The proof must continue to charge separately
\[
\tau_Q+\tau_{\rm norm}+\tau_{\rm census}+\tau_{\rm amp}+\tau_{\rm idx}
+\tau_{\rm compile}+\tau_{\rm rec}+\tau_{\rm write}+\tau_{\rm transport}.
\]
LSPV-4 ACQ1 shows why $\tau_Q$ cannot be silently dropped: even when a violating legal state is NP-searchable, compiling and deciding that search predicate costs a model-matched SAT call. TransportCert remains an accounting/typing interface only.

\paragraph{Collision-extraction and PQ toolkit.}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER.} Schur/componentwise squares, puncturing/shortening as code operations, quadratic-relation kernels, the fixed-encoder slice matrices, and collision Cauchy--Schwarz are exact in their declared objects. LSPV-2 FEAT1's encoder-square ceiling is exact but does not remove a reconstruction quantifier.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE.} Quadratic-relation rank spectra, Hilbert functions, syzygies and graded Betti numbers may reveal a construction-specific algebraic relation after the full fixed-coin acceptance equations are written. Special-soundness supplies the desired logical pattern only if two conflicting valid openings yield an explicit locally checkable forbidden witness in the actual vMS relation.
\item[] \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE.} LWE-style black-box reduction architecture suggests externally extracting a resource from solver behavior rather than forcing internal materialization. Bogdanov--Trevisan prevents treating generic nonadaptive NP self-correction as free; a new construction-specific reduction is required.
\item[] \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER.} Cryptographic collapse-binding, LWE hardness and random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible unconditional steps. VLSI area--time/routing, control barrier/Lyapunov arguments, distributed cuts, optimization duality gaps, topology, and proof-system-specific lower bounds do not imply unrestricted random-access SAT time without a model-preserving all-algorithm bridge.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Barrier audit.}
Viola explicitly states that its near-linear $\Sigma_3$ simulation relativizes. The eager SAT elimination, recursive compiler recurrence, fixed-code Schur algebra, and ACQ1-style NP acquisition also relativize to the relevant decision oracle. None is the required nonrelativizing hinge. The algebraic pieces are equally visible to a sufficiently strong algebraic oracle, so they do not by themselves cross algebrization. SAME\_REALIZATION is preserved only when the same $Q$, encoder, legal-state family, Arthur coins, and downstream SAT instances are used in the charged computation. TC4 remains open because a foreign exact SAT algorithm may bypass every optional representation-specific resource unless an explicit external reduction extracts it from black-box behavior.

\paragraph{Hostile controls.}
DEAD/COPY/DELAY change cost without semantics; INTERP/RECOMP and representation changes test whether the compiler accounting is model-preserving. Affine/Tseitin with Gaussian elimination, Horn, 2-SAT, bounded treewidth, padding, renaming and auxiliary variables remain mandatory controls. None is contradicted by the recurrence because the recurrence is an upper-bound simulation, not a hardness measure.

\paragraph{Strict status.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT:} bundled exact-answer predicate; YES/NO share one SAT exponent; eager-oracle factorization; recursive exponent rule under declared model-matched compilers; same-polarity bundling alone does not remove an invocation layer.
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} Viola's near-linear $\Sigma_3$ simulation and its relativizing character; Robson's RAM-to-SAT reduction in its stated model; Bogdanov--Trevisan's nonadaptive self-correction barrier.
\textbf{CONDITIONAL:} $\alpha_{\rm det}\le\alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1)$, conditional on all four actual compiler/model matches and the surrounding reconstruction setup.
\textbf{CANDIDATE:} construction-specific collision extraction; direct structured post-Viola compiler; LWE-style external extraction with a new NP-specific bridge; Hilbert/syzygy diagnostics after exact arithmetization.
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED:} free canonical/unique binding; counting YES and NO as two independent SAT exponents; qualitative PH collapse as a quantitative compiler; direct cryptographic/physical/proof-system transfer.
\textbf{OPEN:} any model-matched elimination improving on the conservative four-layer recurrence; fixed-coin vMS collision theorem; exact four-stage $b_q$ values; full $Q$ acquisition/normalization; nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing hinge; TC4; final diagonal.

\paragraph{Single next target.}
Extract Viola's actual $\Sigma_3$ verifier into an explicit bounded circuit with named variable blocks and dependency edges, then test one precise question: does the innermost predicate arising after the vMS reconstruction admit a uniform certificate independent of the middle existential choices? If yes, prove the corresponding quantifier commutation and recompute the exponent. If no, exhibit the dependency explicitly and retain the $k^4$ ledger. Do not pursue generic uniqueness in place of this test.
