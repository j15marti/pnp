## ITERATE: exact extended tagged calculus and theorem

Fix ONCE the distinct, effective, sound, complete, unit-paid R_factorLOCAL for signed CNF sources:

1. SOURCE: free original clauses, with a factor view on their exact ORIGINAL variable set.
2. RES: standard oriented binary Resolution at arbitrary width, from CLAUSE-tagged parents; conclusion tagged CLAUSE.
3. AND3: exact conjunction of two FACTOR-tagged parents when the union of their declared original supports has cardinality at most three; conclusion tagged FACTOR. Repeated parents are legal (idempotence), sharing is charged once.
4. FACTOR-LOCAL: a PAID, UNARY, globally implication-checked step from one FACTOR-tagged parent to either (i) any implied CLAUSE on original variables, **of unrestricted width**, remaining CLAUSE-tagged, or (ii) any implied FACTOR on at most THREE original variables, remaining FACTOR-tagged. The support and complete 2^3-entry table are explicit; no semantic oracle is assumed.
5. GF(2): FACTOR rows may be annotated only when their complete table is **exactly equivalent** to the stated affine equality; two previously certified rows may be added by a paid binary XOR. Trivial 0=0 rows are legal, not contradictions.

No RES-to-factor coercion, auxiliary DEF, derived clause-to-LOCAL input, unbounded factor witness, branch/discharge or source-changing step is admitted. This is explicitly NOT historical R_mix341, CANON1, CANON2 or CANON2R.

**Lemma 498.1 (single-clause LOCAL exhaustion).** Let C be a proper signed clause with unsigned support T, |T|=3. Let G be an arbitrary Boolean predicate on an original-variable support S of at most three variables, with C logically implying G **on the entire Boolean cube**. Then either G is tautological or S=T and G is equivalent to C. Proof: if T is not contained in S, every valuation of S extends to a C-model by setting one missing source literal true, so G must be universally true. If T subset S, the support cap forces S=T; C has precisely seven satisfying valuations on T, so G can only be true on those seven and either false or true on the remaining one. For any arbitrary-width CLAUSE D, C implies D iff D is tautological or C is a syntactic subclause of D.

**Lemma 498.2 (factor closure).** If every unsigned source triple occurs at most once, every non-tautological factor produced from SOURCE, AND3 and FACTOR-LOCAL is equivalent to its single ancestral original source clause. Proof by induction on the acyclic shared DAG. Two different nontrivial source supports T and T' cannot be combined by AND3, since |T union T'|>=4; self-pairs/repeated descendants add no new constraint. Tautological factor parents can be erased. Lemma 498.1 handles arbitrary unary factor conversion, even with semantic essential-support shrinking. There is no nontrivial exact GF(2) row: a proper source clause has seven models on its three variables, whereas any consistent nonzero affine equation has four; tautology gives only 0=0. Hence XOR cannot derive false.

**Lemma 498.3 (erasure of unary clause weakening).** A non-tautological CLAUSE emitted from any legal factor is a weakening of one original source clause. In an arbitrary acyclic shared Resolution DAG, replace each such leaf by its source clause. Propagate a subsuming derived clause at every RES vertex: if both pivots remain, perform one correctly oriented RES; if one pivot is missing, retain the corresponding smaller parent clause. Tautological weakening premises can be bypassed by the other premise whenever the resolvent is non-tautological; other tautological nodes can be discarded. This classical subsumption/weakening-elimination induction yields an ordinary Resolution refutation using no more RES inferences than the mixed DAG's entire paid node count, without duplicating shared ancestors.

\boxed{ W_{R_{\mathrm{factorLOCAL}}}(F)=W_{\mathrm{Resolution}}(F). }
