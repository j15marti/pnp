\section*{PROVED/EXACT theorem}
Fix $r\ge3$, $n=3r$, $h=2r$, $m=8r$. Let $G_r$ be the connected bridgeless simple cubic prism graph (two $r$-cycles plus $r$ rungs). Let $T_r$ be its odd-charge Tseitin CNF with one edge variable and four proper three-literal clauses per vertex.

Let $E_r$ contain all eight signed clauses on $x_1,x_2,x_3$, one clause for each polarity vector, then pad with $m-8$ distinct clauses chosen in order from $(x_1\vee x_2\vee x_i)$ for every $4\le i\le n$, then $(x_1\vee\neg x_2\vee x_i)$ and $(\neg x_1\vee x_2\vee x_i)$ for those $i$. There are $3(n-3)=9r-9$ candidate pads, exceeding $m-8=8r-8$.

\begin{theorem}
For every $r\ge3$, both $E_r,T_r$ have exactly $n=3r$ occurring original variables, $m=8r$ distinct proper width-three original clauses, encoded length $\Theta(r\log r)$, and the same Boolean function FALSE. Nevertheless
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(E_r)=7,\quad W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(T_r)=8r-1.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The eight cube clauses of $E_r$ forbid every $(x_1,x_2,x_3)$. Resolve four pairs on $x_3$, two pairs on $x_2$, and one pair on $x_1$, giving the empty clause in seven strict original-source RES steps. Each proper width-three clause excludes exactly $2^{n-3}$ complete assignments. Any at most seven source clauses therefore have a satisfying assignment. In the connected contradiction ancestry with $s$ source leaves and $q$ charged nodes of indegree at most two, $s+q-1\le 2q$, so $q\ge s-1\ge7$. All native rules preserve soundness and conservative definitions preserve models, so no mixed shortcut below seven exists. Deleting any of the eight core clauses and assigning the unique triple falsifying it, plus $x_i=1$ for every $i\ge4$, satisfies all others, proving these eight are indispensable. Every pad clause is dispensable.

The prism is connected and bridgeless: its horizontal edges lie on the two cycles and its rungs lie on squares. LSPV-2/341 proves every $4h=m$ odd-Tseitin clause indispensable, giving $W_R(T_r)\ge m-1$, and supplies a matching $3h$ AND3 plus $h-1$ XOR proof. Thus $W_R(T_r)=m-1=8r-1$. Its even-charge companion has $2^{n-h+1}=2^{r+1}$ models. All syntactic assertions follow from construction.
\end{proof}
