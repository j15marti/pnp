\section*{Fixed system and published restriction}
Fix the \emph{unchanged} $R_{\rm mix341}$: original signed CNF sources free; every derived RES, exact support-at-most-three-original-variable AND3, binary GF(2) XOR, authenticated constant-original-support LOCAL-CONVERT, or conservative two-input DEF node has weight one; every derived DAG node has at most two antecedents; shared nodes count once. $W_R(F)$ is the attained minimum over finite sound refutation DAGs; $\overline W_R(N)$ is the worst case on valid UNSAT sources of at most $N$ encoded bits.
\par Haken (1985): for every sufficiently large $h$, every unrestricted ordinary shared-DAG Resolution proof of unary $PHP_{h+1}^h$ has $2^{\Omega(h)}$ lines. This CNF has $h(h+1)$ variables, $h+1+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses and $\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ signed binary-index bits. Cook (1976) proves polynomial Extended Resolution PHP refutations. Braun (2026, preprint) states that for every $\ell\ge32$ with $n=2^\ell$, every extension-free DAG Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of standard bit PHP has more than $\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))$ nodes. The bit CNF has $(n+1)\ell$ variables, $n\binom{n+1}{2}$ width-$2\ell$ clauses, and $\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ explicit signed binary-ID bits. None of these lower bounds concerns arbitrary correct uniform SAT algorithms or establishes hardness for the paid mixed calculus.
\section*{Nonaffine erasure theorem}
Let $I_m(a_1,b_1,\dots,a_m,b_m)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^m(a_i\wedge b_i)$ and let $C_m$ be a CNF on precisely the $2m$ input variables, with no auxiliaries, whose models are exactly $\{x:I_m(x)=1\}$.
\begin{theorem}
For every $m\ge1$, $C_m$ has at least $2^m$ distinct non-tautological clauses, each of width at least $m$. It therefore has at least $m2^m$ literal occurrences and $\Omega(m2^m\log(m+2))$ bits under fixed-width signed binary IDs. Conversely $I_m=1$ has a unique-extension two-input circuit consisting of $m$ AND and $m-1$ XOR gates and a final output unit, whose explicit defining CNF has exactly $7m-3$ clauses and $O(m\log(m+2))$ bits. For $m\ge2$ the predicate is not affine, nor an existential projection of affine equations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose $2^m$ zero-input witnesses $w_\sigma$, where each pair is $(0,1)$ or $(1,0)$ according to $\sigma\in\{0,1\}^m$. Suppose two different witnesses falsify the same clause. A clause's falsifying set is an axis-aligned subcube containing their coordinate-wise hull. Choose a disagreeing pair and replace its values in the first witness by $(1,1)$, keeping all other pairs from the first witness. This hybrid lies in that subcube and has $I_m=1$, impossible. Thus each clause excludes at most one of the $2^m$ witnesses.
\par A zero-monochromatic subcube cannot leave both variables of any one AND pair free, since toggling that pair's product changes $I_m$. Hence it has at most $m$ free coordinates; a width-$r$ clause falsifies a subcube with $2m-r$ free coordinates, so $r\ge m$. The circuit uses three CNF clauses per AND, four per XOR and one output unit: $3m+4(m-1)+1=7m-3$. Its $2m-1$ conservative two-input gates admit a unique extension and cost one paid DEF node each when used inside $R$. Finally $|\{I_m=1\}|=(4^m-2^m)/2=2^{m-1}(2^m-1)$, not a power of two when $m\ge2$. Every nonempty existential projection of an affine system is affine, with power-of-two cardinality.
\end{proof}
\section*{Positive, scoped replay theorem}
\begin{lemma}
If $D$ is a topologically ordered collection of $g$ conservative two-input gate definitions and the augmented CNF $F\wedge D$ has an ordinary Resolution refutation with $q$ paid Resolution inference nodes, then the \emph{original} $F$ has an $R_{\rm mix341}$ refutation with at most $g+q$ paid nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Introduce the $g$ paid DEF nodes and expose their authenticated defining clauses; replay the $q$ RES nodes with source references to the gate's clause outputs. All pivots and parent edges are unchanged, and RES does not require its variables to be original. Shared DAG nodes remain shared. This is an upper bound, not a reverse lower-bound transfer.
\end{proof}
\noindent This lemma does \emph{not} cover arbitrary R proofs of $F\wedge D$: AND3/LOCAL-CONVERT checks may count the fresh definition outputs as original in the augmented source, but those same outputs can conceal large original-variable support in $F$. Auxiliary erasure into explicit original-only CNF can cost exponentially many output bits. The fixed system itself allows paid compact circuits, so the erasure lower bound does not imply a hard $R$ inference or superpolynomial SAT runtime.
