\section{Source construction and exact semantics}
Let $H=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$ be an ordered proper three-CNF with $m\ge1$, variable IDs $[n]$, distinct identifiers within each clause, and unused IDs removed when connectivity of the full incidence graph is asserted. Introduce one fresh boundary bit $x$ and one fresh private bit $a_i$ per clause:
\[
 G_H(x,\mathbf y,\mathbf a)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m
  (\neg x\vee\ell_{i1}\vee a_i)
  \wedge(\neg a_i\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}).\tag{1}
\]
Every output clause is proper and has three distinct variable IDs. All clause gadgets meet through $x$, and each private $a_i$ joins its two clauses. The non-isolated incidence graph is connected, and all declared variables are active after the stated source renumbering. The output has
\[
 n_G=n+m+1,\qquad M_G=2m.
\]
Use a read-only fixed-record ordinary encoding with word width
\[
 w(v,c)=\lceil\log_2(64(v+c+1))\rceil,\quad
 N_G=(8m+2)w(n+m+1,2m),
\]
two header words, and four words per clause. Source reads, address computation and child serialization are not free.

The exact existential boundary relation is
\[
 Q_H(b)=
 [\exists\,\mathbf y,\mathbf a\;G_H(x=b,\mathbf y,\mathbf a)],
 \quad b\in\{0,1\}.\tag{2}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Singleton-boundary acquisition firewall; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every $H$,
\[
 Q_H(0)=1,\qquad Q_H(1)=\operatorname{SAT}(H),
 \qquad\operatorname{SAT}(G_H)=1.\tag{3}
\]
Every clause-deletion subformula of $G_H$ is SAT and the all-zero assignment to $x,\mathbf a,\mathbf y$ is an explicit witness. Under the generator promise (1), the language
\[
 \{(G_H,x):Q_H(1)=1\}
\]
is NP-complete by a linear-record many-one reduction from ordinary proper 3SAT. The exact support $\Sigma_H=\{b:Q_H(b)=1\}$ is $\{0\}$ or $\{0,1\}$; determining its size or affine-hull dimension therefore decides an NP-complete language, even though the root label is always SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $x=0$ every first conjunct is true and $a_i=0$ satisfies every second conjunct, independently of $\mathbf y$. This single witness satisfies every clause deletion. For $x=1$, each gadget is
$(\ell_{i1}\vee a_i)\wedge(\neg a_i\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$,
whose existential projection on $a_i$ is precisely
$\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}$.
The $a_i$ are private, so all projections compose exactly to $H$.
The uniform mapping writes two source-dependent proper records per original clause, with no SAT decision or advice. Membership is in NP by guessing $\mathbf y,\mathbf a$ at $x=1$. The source-to-target reduction establishes NP-hardness; the source preprocessing that removes unused variable IDs preserves proper 3SAT. The support-size/affine-dimension distinctions encode exactly the same SAT bit.
\end{proof}

\section{Ordinary pinning does not yield a fractional-size child}
For $b\in\{0,1\}$, write $x^1=x$, $x^0=\neg x$, and with fresh $u,v$ use the proper unit gadget
\[
 U_b(x;u,v)=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{\pm1\}}
 (x^b\vee\epsilon u\vee\delta v).
\]
Set $K_H^b=G_H\wedge U_b$. Then
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(K_H^0)=1,\qquad
 \operatorname{SAT}(K_H^1)=\operatorname{SAT}(H).
\]
Both pinned children share $(n',M',N')$:
\[
 n'=n+m+3,\quad M'=2m+4,\quad
 N'=(8m+18)w(n+m+3,2m+4).\tag{4}
\]
Their sign vectors differ only in the unit gadget. As $m\to\infty$, $N'/N_G=1+O(1/m+1/\log(n+m))$, so this particular correct pin is \emph{root scale}, $N'=N_G^{1+o(1)}$, not $N_G^{1/2+o(1)}$. Computing $Q_H(1)$ from an exact solver for $K_H^1$ is a root-scale SAT call. Other reductions are not ruled out, but cannot inherit a favorable ARC exponent from one boundary bit alone.

Any source-oblivious adaptive oracle protocol asking the SAT status of any finite sequence of deletion-only subformulas (including nested deletions) receives only SAT, regardless of $H$.
