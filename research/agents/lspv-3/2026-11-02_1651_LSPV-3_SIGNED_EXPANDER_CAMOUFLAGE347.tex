\section*{Arbitrary-hypergraph construction and exact theorem}
Fix any finite $r$-uniform hypergraph $H=(V,E)$ with $r\ge2$, $t=|E|\ge1$, and distinct variables within each hyperedge. Shared original vertices across edges, signed renamings and an arbitrary edge-order are allowed. For every $e=(v_1,\ldots,v_r)$ introduce a fresh guard $y_e$ and all $2^r$ clauses
\[
 G_{e,b}=(y_e\vee \ell_{v_1,b_1}\vee\cdots\vee\ell_{v_r,b_r}),
 \qquad b\in\{0,1\}^r,\quad
 \ell_{v,0}=x_v,\quad\ell_{v,1}=\neg x_v.
\]
Choose any full rooted binary tree with exactly $t$ leaves $y_e$, one fresh output $z_g$ per internal node $g$, and source implication $D_g=(\neg a_g\vee\neg b_g\vee z_g)$ for its child variables $a_g,b_g$. Let $o$ be its root output, or $y_e$ when $t=1$. Define
\[
 F_H=\bigwedge_{e\in E}\bigwedge_{b\in\{0,1\}^r}G_{e,b}
 \ \wedge\ \bigwedge_{g\text{ internal}}D_g\ \wedge\ (\neg o).
\]
There are exactly $m=(2^r+1)t$ distinct indispensable source clauses, $|V|+2t-1$ variables, and exactly $L=(r+1)2^r t+3(t-1)+1$ signed literal occurrences. In particular for fixed $r$, standard line-DIMACS bit length $N=\Theta(t\log(|V|+t))$ when every vertex is represented and IDs are dense, up to input-encoding formatting constants. No claim is made for extremely sparse/unencoded vertex universes.

\begin{theorem}[All orders, full mixed calculus, exact minimum]
For every such $H$, with any source occurrence signs obtained by a consistent original-variable renaming,
\[
 \boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_H)
       =(2^r+1)t-1=m-1.}
\]
The same formula is clause-minimally UNSAT, and flipping any single signed literal occurrence in any source clause produces a SAT formula. Every guarded $r$-cube block has the simple exact semantic relation $y_e=1$, so the theorem is an explicit \emph{easy} mixed-system family and not a hardness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed $x$ assignment, exactly one of the $2^r$ $r$-literal cubes is false. Hence the conjunction of the guarded block is equivalent to the unit $y_e$. The full formula forces every $y_e=1$, and the $t-1$ implication clauses force the root $o=1$, contradicting $(\neg o)$.

To delete a guarded clause $G_{e,b}$, set $y_e=0$, choose the unique assignment to the $r$ variables in $e$ falsifying that unguarded cube, and set all $y_{e'}=1$ for $e'\ne e$. Every other block is satisfied regardless of shared $x$ variables. Interpret each $z_g$ as the conjunction of its children; the root is zero, and the remaining sources hold. To delete an implication $D_g$, set every guard to one, set its unique output $z_g=0$, and choose every other gate output as the AND of its children in topological order. The root becomes zero. To delete $(\neg o)$, set all guards and gates to one. Thus every source is indispensable, even when the hyperedges share variables. On each deletion witness, the omitted clause itself is false, so flipping \emph{any} of its literal signs makes precisely that witness satisfy the sign-perturbed complete formula.

A sound source-authenticated binary-premise contradiction DAG must use every one of the $m$ sources; otherwise it would refute the satisfiable deletion instance. If $q$ is its number of charged nodes, its connected source-ancestry graph has $m+q$ vertices, at least $m+q-1$ dependency edges and at most $2q$ incoming inference edges, giving $q\ge m-1$. Additional conservative DEF nodes, sharing, temporary growth or XOR rules cannot invalidate this fixed-system source-arity inequality.

For the matching upper bound, resolve the $2^r$ guarded cubes within each block in a full binary tree, pivoting successively on its $r$ original variables. Exactly $2^r-1$ binary RES nodes derive the unit $y_e$. For each AND-tree source $D_g$, resolve with its two previously derived positive child units, using two binary RES nodes to derive positive $z_g$. Finally resolve $o$ against source $(\neg o)$ in one node. The count is
\[
 t(2^r-1)+2(t-1)+1=(2^r+1)t-1=m-1.
\]
Every inference is legal in unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ because it uses only its RES subsystem. Both inequalities apply to the global minimum over all adaptive orders and shared mixed-rule DAGs.
\end{proof}

\section*{Expander, renaming and representation falsifiers}
Let $G_h=(V_h,E_h)$ be a cubic graph with $h$ vertices. For every edge $\{u,v\}$ introduce a private third variable $w_e$ and form one hyperedge $\{x_u,x_v,w_e\}$. There are $t=3h/2$ hyperedges and $r=3$; the resulting $4$-CNF has $9t$ indispensable clauses and exact global mixed minimum $9t-1$. The terminal graph of the original $x_v$'s contains the incidence of $G_h$: for every cut $S\mid V_h\setminus S$, each crossing graph edge contributes eight distinct signed source clauses incident to original variables on both sides. For any cubic $\alpha$-edge expander, a balanced original-terminal cut touches at least $8\alpha h/3$ such clause records when $|S|\in[h/3,h/2]$. The existence of infinite cubic expander families is known; the four finite graphs below are \emph{not} an asymptotic proof of expansion. This exact linear-minimum construction prevents promoting large incidence cuts, separators or many variable-sign conflicts alone into a superlinear mixed-proof lower bound. It does not rule out an invariant using the \emph{semantic content} of separators.

The guarded $3$-cube syntax is not renamable Horn: under any flips of the $x$ variables, its eight source clauses include one whose three unguarded literals are positive, regardless of the guard's sign. Nonetheless its whole block is semantically the easy unit $y_e$. This exposes the precise algebraic/semantic compression escape. High-rank equality, even/odd Tseitin, Horn/2SAT and lazy/compact encodings remain separate adverse controls; no hardness transfer is claimed from any of them.
