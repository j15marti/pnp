\begin{theorem}[Polynomial minimum for a nonaffine Tseitin gadget lift]

In the fixed typed RES+VARDEF fragment,
\[
W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(F_{G,g})\le h\,3^{O(k+c(k))}+O(h^3).
\]
In particular, if $k=O(\log h)$ and $c(k)=O(k)$, the minimum is polynomial. For $g(a,b)=a\wedge b$, the family has $3h$ input variables, $28h+8$ indispensable sources when exactly one vertex has odd charge, and $N=\Theta(h\log h)$ encoded source bits, yet it has a polynomial typed-ER2 refutation. No AND3 support-rule change is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First introduce the fresh shared edge-output circuit wires $z_e=g(y_e)$ with paid definitions. Ordinary source-explicit Davis--Putnam Resolution eliminates the local input/gate variables at each vertex, producing the four canonical parity clauses on the three $z_e$ outputs. This takes at most $3^{M}$ distinct clause nodes per vertex, with $M=3k+O(c(k))+3$. No width-one or width-two output clause is implied by ternary parity, so each forbidden triple is represented by its exact three-literal clause.

Next define a legal XOR of two distinct signed input literals $a,b$ using three positive-output signed-AND definitions $d=a\wedge\neg b$, $e=\neg a\wedge b$, $f=\neg d\wedge\neg e$. The signed literal $\neg f$ computes XOR. Bounded-variable RES on the authenticated gate clauses gives fixed finite macros for a local parity output unit, associativity, commutativity, congruence, pinned-value evaluation and equality-based cancellation $a\oplus(b\oplus s)=s$ for $a=b$. The complete local projection to the output equality or unit yields the precise required clauses. Every macro has at most 15 named variables, so a fixed RES bound $3^{15}$ suffices per invocation.

Use LSPV-2 RT354's previously proved nine paid node construction of a true rail $T$ and false rail $f_0$ from two different originally occurring input variables. For each of the $3h$ vertex-edge incidences make a different fresh gate $q_{v,e}=T\wedge z_e$. By a paid RES node and its defining slots it is equivalent to $z_e$. For each edge the two occurrence wires are syntactically distinct but can be proved equivalent with two RES steps through $z_e$. Thus no XOR gate has duplicate input IDs. The projected ternary parity clauses and equality of inputs pin each vertex's two-XOR-gate expression to its signed charge.

Build a global XOR tree over the vertex outputs and the false rail. The pinned-charge RES macro proves its root equals one since the total charge is odd. Reassociate the tree into a right comb containing the $L=3h$ distinct occurrence leaves and the false rail, with polynomially many constant-size associativity moves. \end{proof}
