## ITERATE: whole-state typed transformer and soundness proof

Consider a DISTINCT, explicitly specified typed fragment `R_fac,RES`. Each original source clause is a free, syntax-preserving view keyed by its parsed DIMACS source identity. A new **paid** globally fresh binary DEF node `z=f(a,b)` (arbitrary total four-bit Boolean truth table, ordered signed input occurrences, repeats allowed) exposes four owner-qualified *positional defining clauses*, all charged to its **single** node. A new **paid** RES node may reference any earlier ordinary clause view or existing owner-qualified facet `(owner,k)` and derives the *exact* oriented signed-pivot resolvent; strict Resolution removes exactly the chosen complementary literal from each parent. Other rules, in particular CASE/DISCHARGE, AND3, LOCAL-CONVERT, GF(2), are NOT asserted here.

A configuration `s=(F,D,V,P,H,\Gamma,E)` records the independently supplied original source, global acyclic definition bank `D`, available signed clause views `V` including their source/owner ancestries and active-context sets `\Gamma`, paid ID set `P`, available wire names `H` and shared DAG edges `E`. Global payment is `|P|`, not sum over used facet references. A **test-only** ASSUME operation introduces a local unit clause labelled with its undischarged assumption; all consequences carry the exact union of contexts. ASSUME and case discharge are NOT native or charged historical R_mix341 operations, and no actual discharge is implemented.

**Theorem 544.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED).** For every parsed original CNF `F` and every finite acyclic `R_fac,RES` derivation accepted by the typed rules above: (i) every charged node is globally unique and all references precede its use; (ii) for every view `\Gamma\vdash C`, `F \wedge D \wedge \Gamma\models C`; (iii) each original valuation uniquely extends to the entire globally fresh conservative DEF bank; (iv) shared references to already paid owners or conclusions cost zero *additional logical nodes* while each newly inserted DEF/RES costs one; (v) an empty-clause final view with empty context implies **UNSAT of the original F**. Thus the identity abstract transformer on these typed whole-DAG configurations includes every legal concrete typed step at **equal cost**, and an empty-context typed goal is source-sound.

**Proof.** Induct on the DAG insertion order. Source identity follows from exact original DIMACS parsing, not a hash. Fresh DEF output gives a uniquely determined value on every preexisting valuation; all four truth-table position clauses hold on that extension, including when signed inputs coincide and positional clauses become duplicated or tautological. A strict RES pivot `p` must occur in the first parent and `not p` in the second. Under the union of inherited contexts, the truth of both premises forces the syntactic resolvent by the Boolean case split on `p`; no uncharged case inference is created. Each conclusion's sources and owners are unions of actual antecedent provenance, and its context is the union of required assumptions, so cross-branch leakage is prevented. Acyclic global naming and the paid set give exact once-per-new-node work, independent of reconvergence. If an empty clause with empty context were derivable from a satisfiable original, the uniquely extended original model would satisfy all paid gate facets and all sound RES consequences, a contradiction. The identity mapping provides equal-cost typed-step inclusion and goal preservation. QED.
