\section{Projected boundary theorem with cyclic interactions}
Let $F=E\land B$, where $E$ is an explicitly encoded system of GF$(2)$ affine equations and $B$ an arbitrary 2-CNF. Partition the source variables into affine-incidence components $C_i$, making every $E$-free variable a singleton. After paid consistency checks and Gaussian elimination, write
\[
x|_{C_i}=a_i+L_i z_i,\quad z_i\in\mathbb F_2^{d_i}.
\]
Let $P_i$ be \emph{every} variable of $C_i$ appearing in $B$, including clauses internal to $C_i$. Define
\[
t_i=\operatorname{rank}(\pi_{P_i}L_i),\qquad D_i=2^{t_i}.
\]
Let $H$ have one node per affine component and an edge $ij$ exactly when a binary clause of $B$ meets both. Give $H$ a supplied or paid-computed variable elimination order $\pi=(v_1,\ldots,v_g)$. Let $S_j$ consist of $v_j$ and its later neighbors in the induced filled graph immediately before eliminating $v_j$. Set
\[
\Lambda_{\pi}=\sum_{j=1}^{g}2^{\sum_{i\in S_j}t_i},\quad
w_\pi=\max_j(|S_j|-1),\quad t=\max_i t_i.
\]
\begin{theorem}[BOUNDARY-TW81, PROVED/EXACT in the declared model]
There is a deterministic uniform, exact SAT decider and SAT-witness reconstruction algorithm for $F$ with
\[
T(F),S_{\rm peak}(F)\le\operatorname{poly}(N)(1+\Lambda_\pi)
\le\operatorname{poly}(N)2^{(w_\pi+1)t}.
\]
The paid order may be heuristic; $w_\pi$ is its \emph{actual induced width}, not an assumed optimal decomposition. In particular fixed measured width and $t=O(\log N)$ give a polynomial restricted algorithm. This is an upper bound, not an all-decider lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compute a basis of the image $\operatorname{im}(\pi_{P_i}L_i)$ and a constructive right inverse on the image. Exactly $D_i$ distinct boundary signatures occur, and each has an explicitly computable full affine source lift; all unused free directions may be zero. Every $B$-clause depends only on one or two signatures. Construct its exact local unary or cross-component binary Boolean factor, charging source parsing and table generation. The resulting finite-domain binary CSP is satisfiable iff $E\land B$ is.

At step $j$, conjoin every factor incident to signature $v_j$ and existentially eliminate that signature. The standard filled-graph scope invariant ensures every factor read has scope within $S_j$. There are at most $\prod_{i\in S_j}D_i$ rows to inspect at that stage. Keep the reduced factor over $S_j\setminus\{v_j\}$ and, for every feasible row, one supporting value of $v_j$. Reverse the backpointers after the last stage to recover all signatures, lift them to one source assignment, and replay every original clause and affine equation. The existential elimination invariant proves both SAT and UNSAT soundness. Source read, Gaussian elimination, graph/order discovery, factor multiplication, failed choices, bookkeeping, reconstruction and verification cost at most a polynomial in explicit $N$ per table row. Live factors, bases and backups have the same aggregate polynomial-times-$1+\Lambda_\pi$ upper bound. Since each $|S_j|\le w_\pi+1$, absorbing $g\le\operatorname{poly}(N)$ yields the stated estimate.
\end{proof}

\section{Matched cycle family and proper-3CNF transport}
For $q\ge3,h\ge2$, component $i$ has $h$ free inputs and $h-1$ functional XOR-prefix bits, with visible full XOR $s_i$. Each XOR3 gate becomes four proper ternary clauses. Put two proper binary clauses enforcing $s_i\oplus s_{i+1}=\eta_i$ on every edge of the cycle, and one unit $s_1=1$. Thus
\[
F_{q,h,\eta}\in\mathrm{SAT}\iff\bigoplus_{i=1}^q\eta_i=0.
\]
SAT instances have $2^{q(h-1)}$ original solutions; UNSAT instances none. Every affine component has full dimension $d_i=h$ but projected rank $t_i=1$. The connected interaction graph is a cycle, with a paid width-two elimination order. Hence the table bound is polynomial even when $h=q\to\infty$. A direct source-recognizing holonomy/Gaussian algorithm is another polynomial bypass.

The source has $q(2h-1)$ variables, $4q(h-1)$ XOR ternaries, $2q$ binary clauses and one unit. Pad each binary clause $(a\lor b)$ using $(a\lor b\lor u)\land(a\lor b\lor\neg u)$ and the unit $\ell$ by four $(\ell\lor\pm u\lor\pm v)$. The proper-3CNF target has exactly
\[
n'=q(2h+1)+2,\qquad m'=4qh+4,
\quad N'=(16qh+18)\lceil\log_2(64(q(6h+1)+7))\rceil
\]
bits in the declared two-word-header/four-word-clause grammar. A single $\eta_i$ flip reverses the SAT label and alters only a private pair of sign records. Accordingly every total exact deterministic addressed-word decider reads at least one word from each of $q$ orientation groups on either canonical label. That is a genuine input-access bound, matched by a polynomial source scan and \emph{not} a superlinear continuation bound. Read grouping, auxiliary variables and padding are all charged.

\paragraph{DUPLICATION/CONTRADICTION AND ROOT-STATE DELTA.}
This derived theorem removes the forest restriction from BOUNDARY-RANK80 at the price of an actual measured elimination-order width; it does not supersede the superior Gaussian/holonomy solver on affine-permutation networks. Role 5 already closed the entire fixed-number-of-parity-check 2-CNF rung algorithmically through a primary-source result. A fixed variable order, a large residual representation, an exponential static table or a growing syntax-level backdoor does not establish unrestricted SAT running-time hardness. The first OPEN implication remains
\[
\text{source-defined obstruction}\quad
\overset{\mathrm{OPEN}}{\Longrightarrow}\quad
\text{necessary costly post-acquisition computation for every adaptive decider}.
\]
The next falsifiable test should use genuine nonlinear coupled signed exact-$(2,2)$ SAT/UNSAT inputs after fully paid Gaussian, SCC, learning and source-recognition alternatives. Quantifier coverage and model-preserving cost transport are not established by this report.
