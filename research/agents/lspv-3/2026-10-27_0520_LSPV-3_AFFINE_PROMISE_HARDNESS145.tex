\section{Connected ordinary proper-3CNF construction}
Let $F(Z)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m
(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$ be any ordinary
proper 3CNF, with $m\ge1$ and $n$ used original variables.
Every source clause has three different variable names.
Use one shared boundary name $y=n+1$ and fresh
$t_j=n+1+j$ and define the \emph{physically explicit}
source-identified module
\begin{equation}
G_F(y,Z,T)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}
 (y\vee\ell_{j1}\vee t_j)\wedge
 (\neg t_j\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3}).
\tag{1}
\end{equation}
Its authentic original clause IDs are $2j-1,2j$.
With further private $u=n+m+2$, $v=n+m+3$, set
\begin{align}
P(y,u,v)={}&(\neg y\vee u\vee v)
\wedge(\neg y\vee u\vee\neg v)\nonumber\\
&\wedge(\neg y\vee\neg u\vee v)
\wedge(\neg y\vee\neg u\vee\neg v).\tag{2}
\end{align}
The four $P$ clauses have IDs $2m+1,\ldots,2m+4$.
Let $H_F=G_F\wedge P$ with genuinely identical
boundary name $y$. All other names are private.
The resulting graph is connected: every clause gadget
shares $y$ directly or has a path through $t_j$; every
original name occurs inside at least one gadget; and
the pin module also shares $y$. Every source clause is
ternary on distinct names, with pairwise distinct
clauses. There are $n+m+3$ names and $2m+4$ clauses.
Canonical binary-identifier output is polynomial-length;
no signed-(2,2) occurrence claim is made for $H_F$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact source projection]
\label{lem:proj}
For every $F$, the true boundary supports of
the two source modules satisfy
\begin{equation}
S_G=\{1\}\cup
\begin{cases}\{0\},&F\in\SAT,\\
\varnothing,&F\in\UNSAT,\end{cases}
\qquad S_P=\{0\}.
\tag{3}
\end{equation}
Both modules are always individually satisfiable.
Both supports are always nonempty affine subsets of
$\Ftwo$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For Boolean $A,B$ the identity
$\exists t[(A\vee t)\wedge(\neg t\vee B)]
\equiv A\vee B$ follows by considering $t=0,1$.
With $A=y\vee\ell_{j1}$ and
$B=\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3}$, existential
elimination of every private $t_j$ transforms (1)
exactly into $\bigwedge_j(y\vee C_j(Z))$.
At $y=1$ choose every $t_j=0$; this satisfies
$G_F$ for arbitrary $Z$. At $y=0$, satisfiability
is exactly that of $F$. For $y=0$ every $P$
clause is satisfied; at $y=1$ its four clauses
exclude all four choices of $(u,v)$.
Consequently (3) holds and the only possible
nonempty supports are $\{1\},\{0,1\}$ and
$\{0\}$, all affine over $\Ftwo$.
No typed source projection was assumed to be valid.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Affine projection acquisition barrier]
\label{th:hard}
On the explicit family $\{G_F:F\text{ proper 3CNF}\}$,
which promises an \emph{actual nonempty affine} one-bit
projection, deciding whether its affine codimension
$r$ equals zero is NP-complete, and deciding $r=1$
is coNP-complete. Semantic checking that the source
entails the claimed affine unit row $y=1$ is
coNP-complete even under that promise.
Moreover $\SAT(H_F)\iff\SAT(F)$, so SAT of
the connected two-module, individually SAT,
nonempty-affine-projection family $\{H_F\}$
is NP-complete, and UNSAT is coNP-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:proj} gives $r=0$ iff $F$ is SAT
and $r=1$ iff $F$ is UNSAT. The map $F\mapsto G_F$
is uniform polynomial-time; rank-zero membership
has the NP witness of a satisfying assignment to
$G_F$ at $y=0$, and its complement gives rank-one
coNP membership. The claimed row $y=1$ is entailed
iff $G_F\wedge\neg y$ is UNSAT, which the
lemma makes equivalent to $F$ UNSAT. It is in coNP
because a violating assignment to $G_F$ with $y=0$
is a succinct NP counterwitness. Finally
$\SAT(H_F)\iff S_G\cap S_P\ne\varnothing$:
the private name sets are disjoint, and the shared
boundary name must receive exactly the \emph{same}
value. By (3), the join is satisfiable iff $F$ is.
Ordinary proper 3SAT is NP-complete. All completeness
statements are \emph{language} reductions, not
machine-time lower bounds.
\end{proof}

\section{Short module support versus authenticated proof}
If $F$ is UNSAT, the two-line semantic sketch
$S_G=\{1\}$ and $S_P=\{0\}$ witnesses incompatibility,
with $O(\log|H_F|)$ source-module pointer bits.
But the first row does not follow syntactically from
the module label. It requires a real source-authenticated
derivation refuting $G_F|_{y=0}$, or a fully charged
fixed deterministic ordinary-SAT decider $A$
on that genuine child. Accepting the row without
a derivation would incorrectly accept \emph{every}
SAT $F$ as an UNSAT input. Restricting raw
certificate length to two rows is not a sound
all-UNSAT grammar.

For a single meaningful guest call, the target child
is of the same power size as the source:
$m_1=|H_F|^{1-o(1)}$ in unpadded canonical
bit encodings, $M=m_1$, $V=m_1$ and
$\sum_i m_i^d=|H_F|^{d+o(1)}$ for $A$ running
in $N^{d+o(1)}$ time. This is \emph{not} a
power-short contraction with any fixed
$\lambda<1$. The generic inequality
$\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V$ is respected,
but its use grants no improved exponent here.
If $P=NP$ is assumed, the available polynomial
SAT exponent is an unknown finite $K$,
not the extra $d=1.802$ seed.

\begin{theorem}[Correct clocked co-nondeterministic implication]
Suppose one fixed polynomial-time sound verifier
with an \emph{a priori polynomial proof-length
bound depending only on input length} accepts
at least one certificate on every UNSAT $H_F$.
Then $NP=coNP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On any proper 3CNF $F$ deterministically
construct $H_F$. A nondeterministic machine
guesses a length-delimited certificate of at most
the declared polynomial length and runs the
deterministic verifier under an explicit polynomial
clock. Every timeout, wrong source ID, malformed
record or absent branch rejects. On SAT $F$ no
branch may accept by soundness; on UNSAT $F$
at least one branch accepts by coverage. Thus
proper-3UNSAT belongs to NP, and coNP
completeness gives $NP=coNP$.
This conditional does not charge deterministic
certificate discovery as though nondeterministic
guessing were deterministic ARC.
\end{proof}

\section{Independent signed exact-(2,2) XOR isolation}
\begin{theorem}[Complete XOR3 quartets are isolated]
\label{th:xor}
Let $Q_b(x,y,z)$ be the four original proper
three-literal CNF clauses representing the parity
relation $x\oplus y\oplus z=b$, forbidding all
four wrong-parity triples. In a strict signed
exact-(2,2) three-CNF $E$ containing all four
source clauses $Q_b$, no clause outside
$Q_b$ can mention $x,y,z$. Hence $Q_b$ is
a separate connected component, with four
satisfying assignments. Any connected UNSAT
strict signed exact-(2,2) source contains no
complete raw XOR3 quartet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Among four forbidden parity triples, each
coordinate takes values zero and one exactly
twice each. Therefore each original variable
occurs twice positively and twice negatively
in the quartet, \emph{exhausting its exact
signed occurrence quota}. An exterior source
clause cannot mention any of the three names.
Thus the quartet factor is variable-disjoint
from the remainder. It is satisfiable,
so its deletion preserves satisfiability.
If the entire source is connected, the only
possible quartet is the whole four-clause
source, which is SAT and not UNSAT.
\end{proof}
A standard cubic-graph Tseitin encoding uses
an edge variable in two distinct vertex parity
quartets and hence gives that name four positive
and four negative source occurrences, violating
strict signed exact-(2,2). This does \emph{not}
exclude derived affine identities, PC cancellation,
or audited occurrence-splitting compilers:
the theorem is about \emph{unmodified complete
original-clause quartets}.
