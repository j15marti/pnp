\section{Build: one-bit coNP-complete negative projection}
\begin{theorem}
Let $F(X)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)$ be a proper three-CNF with no isolated names. Introduce fresh $y,t_1,\dots,t_m$ and form
\[
G_F(y,X,T)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m
[(\neg y\vee a_i\vee t_i)\wedge
(\neg t_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)].
\]
Every original clause of $G_F$ has three distinct names. Its incidence graph is connected. Its exact existential interface relation is
\[
\exists X\,\exists T\,G_F(y,X,T)\equiv
\neg y\vee\exists X\,F(X).
\]
The language of canonical, syntactically recognized $G_F$ sources whose exact boundary relation equals $\neg y$ is coNP-complete, despite having exactly one boundary bit and an $O(1)$-bit exact relation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any formulas $A,B$ not mentioning $t$, the identity
$\exists t\,[(A\vee t)\wedge(\neg t\vee B)]\equiv A\vee B$
follows by testing $t=0,1$. Apply to $A=\neg y\vee a_i$ and $B=b_i\vee c_i$. Since all $t_i$ are fresh, elimination gives
$\exists T\,G_F=\neg y\vee F(X)$; eliminating $X$ gives the claim. All first clauses share $y$, every second clause attaches to its first by $t_i$, and every original variable occurs in the source. Thus the incidence graph is connected. The canonical paired encoding is constructed and recognized in polynomial time. The proposed relation is exactly $\neg y$ if and only if $F$ is UNSAT. Proper 3SAT is NP-complete, so the recognized negative-relation language is coNP-complete.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Consequences.} A deterministic polynomial-time checker for this proposed exact negative bit exists on all these sources iff $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$. Uniform polynomial-length nondeterministic certificates, guessed and verified with sound polarity and explicit clocks, would imply $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$; the converse is an abstract complexity-class equivalence, not a deterministic construction algorithm. SAT membership at $y=1$ has a short direct assignment witness. The gadget is ordinary proper 3CNF, not strict signed exact-(2,2), since $\neg y$ repeats $m$ times. This does not prove an unconditional time lower bound.

\section{Constructive authenticated frontier exception}
\begin{theorem}
For any ordinary source 3CNF and specified variable order $\sigma$ with frontier width $w$, deterministic exact source-identified frontier tables can be built and independently verified in $2^{O(w)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ bit time, deciding both SAT and UNSAT with one uniform program.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
After step $j$, retain precisely the processed variables that occur in some future source clause. Let $R_j$ contain all retained assignments extendible to an assignment satisfying every clause whose last variable is processed by step $j$. Initially $R_0=\{\emptyset\}$. Extend each state in both binary values of the next variable, check all newly completed original source clauses, existentially forget processed names absent from all future clauses, and deduplicate. Induction proves the invariant. At $j=n$, $R_n=\{\emptyset\}$ exactly for SAT, and $R_n=\emptyset$ for UNSAT. There are at most $2^w$ states and two candidate transitions per state. Recompute frontier sets and source clause IDs from the original parsed input, independently replay every transition, and authenticate all clauses. Parsing, discovery of the given order, transitions, failure paths, trace writing, and verification are charged. The relation-DAG grammar is richer than the earlier residual original-clause 2CNF-SCC adaptive-tree grammar, and a polynomial translation is not claimed.
\end{proof}
