\begin{abstract}
\textbf{KNOWN RULE and exact restricted implementation proof.} A conflicting
unit-propagation (UP) assumption core yields a reverse-unit-propagation
(RUP) clause valid in every later branch, even after backtracking. One-pass
assumption deletion finds an inclusion-minimal UP-conflicting core, with each
failed deletion and source replay charged. This is ordinary proof-carrying
clause learning, not a novel SAT theorem. \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL.} On a connected
but trivially 2SAT-recognizable repeated-context UNSAT family, minimized cores
reduce a specified DPLL tree from $2^{k+2}-1$ to $2k+3$ nodes. At $k=9$,
selected work falls from $622{,}845$ to $28{,}107$. However, W1 strict22 UNSAT
and SWAP6 strict22 SAT consume more work after learning despite fewer nodes.
A charged recognizer selects exact 2SAT/Horn/dual-Horn/GF(2) or complete DPLL;
original-clause $S/U$ extremes remain adversarial controls. The full
$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ ARC/FLMV compiler remains \OPEN, as do $P=NP$ and $P\ne NP$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, primary sources and four falsifiers}
The verified governance \texttt{UNIVERSAL-PARAMETERIZED-FLMV-REDIRECT1}
and \texttt{CONTRACT1} require one integrated, source-authenticated adaptive
residual-SAT controller and a fully charged hereditary arbitrary-decider
contractor. The predecessor \texttt{MONOTONE-PROBE-RESUME254} proved safe replay
only when a fixed CNF's assumptions grow monotonically, while
\texttt{ARC-BRANCH-MASS-GATE261} audited source-cofactor search and physical
child length. A correctly learned \emph{global clause} addresses precisely the
cross-backtracking gap, without suggesting that every solver must learn it.
Existing clause learning, first-UIP analysis, incremental SAT and RUP checking
are primary prior art \cite{een2003,wetzler2014}; our finite extraction is
not claimed new.

\paragraph{F1, stale cache.} Can a prior nonconflicting UP answer be reused after
incompatible branch switching merely because its assigned bits do not disagree
with the new base? \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the source-specific stale example below
becomes conflicting without a directly opposed cached bit.
\paragraph{F2, learned cores universally save work.} Does a shorter proof or
smaller DPLL tree imply less \emph{total} work? \textbf{FALSIFIED} on both W1 and
SWAP6, even though the learned cores are RUP checked.
\paragraph{F3, bounded core minimization is enough.} Does limiting assumption
removal to four tests retain the full benefit? \textbf{FALSIFIED} on the
repeated-context family: $255$ rather than $21$ nodes at $k=9$, with worse work
than plain DPLL.
\paragraph{F4, local branching costs characterize SAT hardness.} The same
family is ordinary 2SAT and is decided by linear-time implication SCC; a
strict22 signed-orbit example has an explicit witness. These are adversarial
controls against promoting a DPLL-specific exponential tree to all-algorithm
hardness.

\section{Exact proof: RUP assumptions are safe across arbitrary branches}
Write $F$ for the immutable, indexed original CNF. Let $C_1,\ldots,C_t$ be
previously validated learned clauses and $D_t=F\land C_1\land\cdots\land C_t$.
A consistent decision cube is a set $\rho$ of distinct signed variable
assignments. Define $\mathrm{UP}(D_t,\rho)=\bot$ when ordinary unit propagation
under those assumptions reaches a false clause.

\begin{theorem}[Global RUP core reuse and inclusion-minimality]
Suppose $\mathrm{UP}(D_t,\rho)=\bot$ and every $C_j$ is RUP relative to
$F,C_1,\ldots,C_{j-1}$. Remove each literal of $\rho$ once, retaining a removal
exactly when UP remains contradictory. Let $D\subseteq\rho$ be the resulting
subset, and let
\[
 L_D=\bigvee_{\ell\in D}\neg\ell.
\]
Then $L_D$ is non-tautological, $F\models L_D$, and it remains valid under
\emph{every} later partial assignment, regardless of backtracking or branch
order. The deletion pass produces an inclusion-minimal $D$ with respect to
UP-conflict, not necessarily a minimum-cardinality semantic unsatisfiable core.
The sequential list of RUP clauses and a complete DPLL refutation tree form a
checkable UNSAT proof. For $D=\varnothing$, $L_D$ is the empty clause and the
RUP sequence itself refutes $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Deleting a decision literal retains the UP contradiction only when the
remainder really unit-refutes $D_t$. By induction on the learned-clause
sequence, $F\models D_t$, so $F\land D$ is unsatisfiable and hence
$F\models L_D$. More directly, UP on $D_t\land\neg L_D$ is precisely
UP on $D_t\land D$ and yields a contradiction; this is the RUP condition.
The decisions in $D$ are consistent, so $L_D$ contains no complementary
literal pair. For each retained $\ell$, removing it did not cause UP conflict
\emph{at the moment it was tested}. Subsequent deletions only weaken the
assumption set; since UP conflict is monotone under added assumptions, it
cannot reappear after further deletions. Thus $D$ is inclusion-minimal for
UP conflict. Finally, $F\models L_D$ has no dependence on the present branch:
placing $L_D$ in the global proof database is correct after backtracking.
Sequential RUP checking proves all added clauses are consequences of $F$;
verification of every contradictory leaf of a complete binary search tree
under those clauses, with both children present at each NO node, gives an
inductive UNSAT proof for the unchanged source. A SAT leaf instead requires a
fully checked original-source witness.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Acquisition accounting.} Let $\ell_t$ be the total literal count
in $D_t$, $n$ the number of occurring variables and $d=|\rho|$. Our deliberately
simple repeated-scan UP makes at most $n+1$ passes and at most $O(n\ell_t)$
indexed-word literal operations per test. Core deletion and a final RUP
recheck use at most $d+2$ complete tests, so one conflict costs
$O((d+2)n\ell_t)$ charged indexed-word operations, beyond branch navigation,
source indexing and output. This is \emph{per encountered conflict}; the
number of conflicts, accumulated learned clauses, complete proof tree and
simultaneous writable storage need not be polynomial. The implementation
retains source clauses, learned clauses, trace records and the explicit test
proof tree. Work-tape index and random-access head costs of FLMV are not
simulated by the Python word-RAM ledger.

\paragraph{Why a prior nonconflict answer is not enough.} The small source
$(\neg p\vee u)\land(\neg u\vee v\vee r)\land
(\neg u\vee\neg v\vee r)$ has nonconflicting UP closure $p=u=1$ under $p=1$.
Adding $r=0$ does not oppose any old value, but it creates a contradiction.
The earlier strict22 SWAP6 probe $x_5=0$ under $x_2=x_8=0$, followed by
$x_{10}=0$, gives a similar source-authenticated example. A globally RUP
learned clause is safe; a previously cached ``nonconflict'' bit is not.

\section{A fully explicit SAT/UNSAT family and its restricted tree theorem}
For $k\ge1$, use distinct $y_i,z_i,x,u,v$ with variable order
$y_1,\ldots,y_k,z_1,\ldots,z_k,x,u,v$. Set
\[
 F_k^- = \bigwedge_{i=1}^k[(y_i\vee z_i)\land(\neg y_i\vee z_i)]
 \land(x\vee u)\land(x\vee\neg u)\land
 (\neg x\vee v)\land(\neg x\vee\neg v).
\]
Let $F_k^+$ delete the last clause. The first two $x$-clauses imply $x$;
the last two imply $\neg x$, so $F_k^-$ is UNSAT. The neighbor $F_k^+$ is SAT,
for example at $x=1,z_i=1$ for every $i$, with all other variables assigned
one. Both are ordinary 2CNF and recognized by an exact implication-SCC solver.

\begin{proposition}[The declared first-variable DPLL and minimal-core traces]
For the specified first-unassigned-variable, zero-first UP-DPLL without
learning, the complete $F_k^-$ decision tree has $2^{k+2}-1$ visited nodes.
With global sequential-RUP learning and full single-pass assumption deletion,
the same implementation visits $2k+3$ nodes and learns two clauses, namely
$(x)$ and the empty clause. Its clause-acquisition work is polynomial in $k$
in this declared implementation; the baseline's repeated branching is
exponential in $k$. This is a comparison between two specified procedures on an
easy 2CNF family, not a lower bound for arbitrary SAT algorithms.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choosing $y_i$ forces $z_i=1$ but leaves both $y_i$ branches, so without
learning the algorithm explores the full binary decision tree on $k$ variables
and then both $x$ values at each leaf. There are $k+1$ binary decision levels
and $2^{k+2}-1$ nodes. On the first explored path, $x=0$ creates a UP conflict
because $(x\vee u),(x\vee\neg u)$ force both values of $u$.
Removing all $y_i$ assumptions preserves this conflict, yielding the globally
valid unit $(x)$. The next branch has $x=1$ forced, while the last two clauses
force both values of $v$. With the learned $(x)$ present, dropping every
remaining $y_i$ assumption still unit-refutes the source and derives the
empty clause. Exactly the initial chain of $k$ decisions plus its first $x$
branch and subsequent refutation gives $2k+3$ counted nodes under the declared
control flow. The independent checker replays both RUP additions and the
complete tree. A direct 2SAT SCC method is faster still.
\end{proof}

\section{Finite results, both labels and full-cost controls}
The executable producer evaluated 60 formulas: 50 SAT, 10 UNSAT. They include
$F_k^\pm$ for $k=3,5,7,9$; connected W1 strict22 UNSAT and SWAP6 strict22 SAT
(54 models); signed renamings; parity/Tseitin SAT and UNSAT; canonical easy
signed22 orbit, 12 newly generated connected strict22 controls; Horn and
 dual-Horn SAT/UNSAT; a binary contradiction; and 28 seeded random proper
ternary formulas. Each source is passed to four complete solver policies:
no learning, unminimized RUP core, four-deletion capped core, and fully
minimized UP core. An adaptive terminal controller charges both original
endpoint trials, Horn/dual-Horn/2SAT/GF(2) recognition, signed22 recognition,

\section{Independent checker, original-clause controls and status}
An independent verifier with its own UP implementation, requiring no import
of the producer, replays every RUP addition against the original source plus
preceding learned clauses, checks all contradictory leaves of the complete
binary proof tree, verifies every SAT witness on the original source, and
compares 55 of the 60 original formulas with exhaustive truth tables (the
$21$-variable signed22 orbit is checked by its witness instead).
Across the three restartable checker chunks it validated 1,952 RUP clause
additions, 3,574 contradictory leaves and 7,829 tree nodes, and performed
3,272 independently enumerated original-clause pair-extreme mode checks.
For source clauses with three distinct variables it checked all per-occurrence
$S_j$ (all literals true) and $U_j$ (all literals false) values, both
inversions, every original-index SS/SU/US/UU mode, coherent $0^n$ and
$1^n$ clause counts, complementary status identities, and intermediate
flip trails. A locally infeasible extreme is never treated as a false
ordinary disjunction; derived pruning uses actual RUP clauses only.
