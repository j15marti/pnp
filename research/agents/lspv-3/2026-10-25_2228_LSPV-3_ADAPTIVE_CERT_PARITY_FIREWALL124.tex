\section{Exact grammar, all-label soundness, all-UNSAT completeness}
A proof is a finite typed tree attached to an exact source input $F$ and
current source-variable partial assignment $\sigma$. The verifier
reconstructs or virtually reads $F|_\sigma$ with exact source IDs,
complete child headers and bodies, and charged bit access.
\begin{description}
\item[] BRANCH$(x,\pi_0,\pi_1)$ verifies both children with
$\sigma[x=0]$ and $\sigma[x=1]$, requiring $x$ unassigned.
\item[] SOURCE-LEAF$(i)$ checks that original source clause ID $i$
is present and all of its literals are falsified by $\sigma$.
\item[] RES/PC/CP$(\pi)$ independently replays every source-identified
resolution, polynomial calculus, or cutting-planes line and checks
the explicit empty clause/equation $1=0$. No claimed rule or field
change can be trusted without checking. Proof length, inference
arithmetic, coefficients, parsing and indices are charged.
\item[] AFFINE$(\mathcal B)$ checks each claimed parity triple is
backed by \emph{all four} actual original proper-3CNF clauses of
the matching XOR relation, with disjoint clause IDs, and verifies
that the GF(2) linear combination cancels all source-variable
coefficients and leaves constant one.
\item[] JOIN$(Y,G,H,\{\pi_w\})$ checks an exact clause partition
$F=G(Y,U)\wedge H(Y,V)$ with $U\cap V=\varnothing$, the same
original identifiers for common variables $Y$, and for every
$w\in\{0,1\}^{|Y|}$ an accepted refutation of at least one
of $G|_{Y=w}$ and $H|_{Y=w}$. No independent incompatible witness
assignments are joined.
\item[] A-CALL accepts only after running the \emph{same fixed total
exact uniform ordinary-SAT decider} $A$ on the actual encoded
$F|_\sigma$, including charged reconstruction, read-index work,
guest steps and timeout handling. No supplied UNSAT output bit
is a trusted certificate. The soundness of this optional rule
assumes the exactness of $A$.
\end{description}

\begin{theorem}[Soundness and unbounded coverage]
For every ordinary $F$, if all rule checkers above are sound and the
optional $A$ is a fixed exact total SAT decider,
\[
F\in\mathrm{SAT}\Rightarrow(\forall\pi)\ V(F,\pi)=0,
\qquad
F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\Rightarrow(\exists\pi)\ V(F,\pi)=1.
\]
The same implication holds when $F$ lies in the strict signed
exact-$(2,2)$ subclass. There is no claimed polynomial bound
on $|\pi|$, leaf size, search depth or verifier runtime.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the proof tree. A source leaf falsifies a real source
clause; sound checked algebraic/proof terminals derive contradiction;
the $A$ terminal gets an actual exact UNSAT result; and both children
of BRANCH eliminate every extension of their common restriction.
For JOIN, any global satisfying assignment gives a single common
word on the original $Y$ identifiers and thus satisfies both
modules, contradicting the certified UNSAT side for that word.
This establishes rejection of \emph{every} certificate on SAT inputs.
Conversely, enumerate all original Boolean variables by complete
BRANCH splitting. Every full assignment of an UNSAT formula falsifies
some original source clause, so place its exact clause ID at that leaf.
The tree verifies, potentially with $2^n$ leaves.
\end{proof}

\section{Polynomial calculus degree audit}
Over a fixed field, encode each clause $C$ by its falsifying
polynomial $f_C=\prod_{\ell\in C}(1-\ell)$, positive literals
evaluating as $x$, negative as $1-x$, and include Boolean
axioms $x^2-x$. The degree of a PC derivation is the largest
degree among all displayed lines and source axioms.
\begin{theorem}[Full-tree branch plus leaf PC degree]
If every leaf $\sigma$ of a complete decision tree has a PC
refutation of the \emph{actual} $F|_\sigma$ over the same
field of degree at most $k_\sigma$, then $F$ has a PC
refutation of degree at most
\[
\max_{\sigma}(|\sigma|+k_\sigma)\le h+k,
\]
where $h$ is maximum branch depth and
$k=\max_\sigma k_\sigma$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $g_\sigma=\prod_{x\in\operatorname{dom}\sigma}
(x\text{ if }\sigma(x)=1,\text{ else }1-x)$.
For every actually used restricted source clause axiom
$f_{C|_\sigma}$, all source literals assigned false
supply their corresponding factors already in $f_C$.
Multiply the original source clause equation only by
guard factors not already present, deriving
$g_\sigma f_{C|_\sigma}=0$ within degree
$|\sigma|+\deg f_{C|_\sigma}$.
Satisfied clauses do not occur among restricted axioms.
Multiply unassigned Boolean axioms by $g_\sigma$.
Inductively lift every PC addition and multiplication-by-variable
line $p=0$ to $g_\sigma p=0$, with maximal degree
$|\sigma|+k_\sigma$. The last leaf line $1=0$ yields
$g_\sigma=0$. At siblings, linear addition gives
$g_{\tau[x=0]}+g_{\tau[x=1]}
=g_\tau((1-x)+x)=g_\tau$.
Merge bottom-up to derive $1=0$ within the asserted degree.
The proof may be exponentially long; no proof-search time
or length bound follows.
\end{proof}
This is a restricted algebraic \emph{upper} relation, not a
transfer of a PC degree lower bound into an arbitrary-machine
decision-time lower bound. Field characteristics and expansion
formats cannot be silently changed.

\section{Strict signed $(2,2)$ parity saturation and affine controls}
For three distinct source variables $(x,y,z)$ and $b\in\{0,1\}$,
let $B_b$ be the four proper ternary clauses excluding the
four assignments of wrong parity. Each source name occurs exactly
twice positively and twice negatively \emph{within this one
four-clause bundle}. Over $\mathbb F_2$, direct expansion gives
\[
\boxed{\sum_{C\in B_b} f_C=x+y+z+b.}
\]
Thus the source-checked bundle derives one linear equation
using only degree-three PC linear combinations.
\begin{theorem}[Occurrence saturation]
A strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ proper 3CNF which is a disjoint
union of complete standard four-clause parity bundles
cannot contain two distinct bundles sharing an original
variable name. Hence every such formula is SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A single complete bundle exhausts both positive and negative
occurrence quotas of each of its three names. Reuse in a
second distinct bundle would contribute another two of each,
giving at least four positive and four negative occurrences.
The exact signed grammar forbids this. Therefore bundle
variable sets are disjoint, and independently satisfying
the nonempty parity relation of each factor satisfies
the entire conjunction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary odd-Tseitin GF(2) terminal]
Let $G$ be a connected cubic graph, with one edge variable
and one parity charge $b_v$ per vertex. Encode each vertex
parity equation as its four source-audited proper 3CNF
clauses. When $\sum_vb_v=1\pmod2$, the ordinary formula
has an explicit degree-three PC refutation over
$\mathbb F_2$ with $O(|V|)$ linear combination lines.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Add the four clause equations for each vertex, obtaining
$\sum_{e\ni v}x_e+b_v=0$. Sum over all vertices.
Every edge is counted twice, canceling in $\mathbb F_2$.
The odd total charge leaves $1=0$.
\end{proof}
Each edge belongs to two vertex bundles, so its actual
3CNF source occurrence is \emph{four positive and four
negative}, rather than the strict $(2,2)$ quota.
This disproves the naive direct gadget transport only.
Auxiliary-variable occurrence-managed translations and
arbitrary signed $(2,2)$ formulas are NOT excluded.
Recognized ordinary Tseitin may also be decided by a
linear parity scan, so its large cores and local expansion
are not general decision-time obstructions. Across other
characteristics, PC degree behavior can differ sharply [2].

\section{Charged leaf-volume budget}

$m^{d+o(1)}$, $d\ge1$, define leaf lengths

For every $i$, $m_i^d=m_i m_i^{d-1}\le m_i M^{d-1}$,
so unconditionally
\[
\boxed{\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V.}
\]

\[
\boxed{c\le\max(r,u+b+\lambda(d-1)).}
