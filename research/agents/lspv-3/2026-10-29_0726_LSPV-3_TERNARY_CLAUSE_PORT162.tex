\section{PROPOSE: missing arrow and falsifiable mechanisms}
The missing bridge in the project is a polynomially constructible, semantically authenticated multi-port grammar for arbitrary strict exact-$(2,2)$ formulas. The written pre-search record (included verbatim in the source archive) proposed \textbf{P1}, extension of the known saturated gadget to an XOR3 relation; \textbf{P2}, potential sufficiency of pairwise-complete summaries (to be falsified by a genuine composable parity inconsistency); \textbf{P3}, polynomial authenticated summary generation on bounded-treewidth module graphs; and \textbf{P4}, possible affine simplification of the known parity-ring class. P1 has \emph{not} been settled: the BUILD found a simpler but genuinely non-bijunctive three-port \emph{clause}, not an XOR3 relation. P2 has a classical abstract parity counterexample, but no new strict-occurrence composability proof. P3 is restricted standard dynamic programming, not universal acquisition. P4 remains an established easy-ring control. The new, narrower finding is a sharp semantic expressiveness threshold at three retained ports, and is reported separately rather than recasting a failed search as the originally proposed result.

\section{BUILD I: an exact signed $(2,2)$ source with a non-bijunctive three-port clause}
Let $G$ be the fixed 15-variable, 20-clause connected strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ proper source explicitly reproduced in the accompanying executable script, agreeing with LSPV-2 PORT-WIRES221. Let $R(a,b,c)$ be the existential relation of $G$ on $(a,b,c)=(x_1,x_2,x_{13})$.
\begin{lemma}[Finite source cofactor table]\label{lem:source}
The source has 272 satisfying assignments and
\[
\boxed{R(a,b,c)\equiv \neg a\vee\neg b\vee c.}
\]
There are exactly 60 unordered three-variable projections of $G$ which are not majority-closed, among all $\binom{15}{3}=455$ projections. Each such relation excludes exactly one of eight assignments. Their respective counts according to excluded integer masks $0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7$ are $13,9,2,5,10,13,2,6$, where bit 0 denotes the first selected variable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The script checks all $2^{15}=32768$ assignments against all 20 explicitly listed source clauses and records complete port fibers, not samples. For $(x_1,x_2,x_{13})$, tuple masks $0,1,2,4,5,6,7$ have respectively $61,16,18,81,68,18,10$ extensions; mask $3$ has none. Every one of the 455 triple relations is checked against all ternary majority operations. The finite assertion is executable and independently reproducible, but has not been translated to a kernel proof. The model counts add to 272.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp semantic two-port/three-port threshold]\label{thm:threshold}
Every Boolean relation on at most two named variables can be represented exactly by a 2CNF on those variables. Conversely, $R(a,b,c)$ above cannot be represented as a 2CNF, \emph{even with an arbitrary number of existentially quantified auxiliary variables}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $d\le2$, for every excluded tuple $t\in\{0,1\}^d$ include the clause of width $d$ false exactly on $t$. Their conjunction defines the relation. For the second claim, satisfying assignments of a 2CNF are closed under componentwise ternary majority: every binary clause true on each of three assignments remains true on their majority. Existential quantification preserves majority closure: take any three extendible port assignments, choose witnesses, take the majority of the three \emph{complete} satisfying assignments, and then project. But $(a,b,c)=(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(1,1,1)$ belong to $R$, while their majority $(1,1,0)$ does not. Hence no such existential 2CNF exists.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: a connected all-size degree-saturated source network retaining the ternary clause}
For $k\ge2$ take $k$ disjoint copies $G_i$ of $G$. Denote the occurrence of $\neg x_4^{(i)}$ in clause index 2 by $C_2^{(i)}$, and the other occurrence in index 3 by $C_3^{(i)}$ (zero-based). For each $i<k-1$, exchange the entire negative literal token $\neg x_4^{(i)}$ in $C_3^{(i)}$ with $\neg x_4^{(i+1)}$ in $C_2^{(i+1)}$. Call the resulting formula $E_k$.
\begin{theorem}[Unbounded connected exact-$(2,2)$ ternary-clause interfaces]\label{thm:chain}
For every $k\ge2$, $E_k$ is connected, proper, strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ with $n=15k$ variables and $m=20k$ clauses, and its exact relation on the three named root ports is
\[
\boxed{\exists\text{ all variables except }(x_1^{(0)},x_2^{(0)},x_{13}^{(0)})\; E_k
\equiv\neg x_1^{(0)}\vee\neg x_2^{(0)}\vee x_{13}^{(0)}.}
\]
Flipping the signs of all four occurrences of any subset of the three root variables obtains all eight possible signed ternary-clause relations on the same connected unsigned scopes, preserving the strict $(2,2)$ occurrence counts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All swaps exchange negative tokens and are on distinct slots. The private clause feet remain local and all variable sign degrees are preserved. The two changed clauses on each edge connect the components, preserving proper ternary clauses and distinct clause identities. The fixed source's exact two-negative-slot puncture table says: if $p_i=x_4^{(i)}=0$, each exposed foot disjunction is $0$, even when both negative slots have been punctured. Thus neighboring unequal port bits make one swapped clause false. Consequently every satisfying assignment has $p_0=p_1=\cdots=p_{k-1}$. If all ports equal, every swapped literal evaluates exactly as its original, so each source module satisfies its original $G_i$. Conversely, for every satisfying root assignment choose its original $G_0$ witness and choose a $G_i$ witness at the same value of $p_i$, which exists for both values. Then every switched clause is satisfied. The root's exact existential relation is therefore $R$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:source}. Flipping a variable swaps its two positive with its two negative literal occurrences, preserving the $(2,2)$ counts and translating the relation by a Boolean coordinate flip.
\end{proof}

\section{Certificates, independent controls, full accounting and limitations}
The provided Python build independently checks properness, signed degrees, distinct clauses, and connectedness, then evaluates all eight root port cofactors on the full original 30-, 45-, 60-, and 75-variable formulas using SciPy HiGHS MILP. Every chain has exactly the seven predicted feasible port tuples, and for the infeasible tuple $(1,1,0)$ the independent solver returns UNSAT. The two-copy negative fiber also has an original-clause-plus-assumption Resolution refutation: 18 derived resolvents, authenticated by a separately written replay checker against 11 source or assumption records, ending with the empty clause. The checker rejects two deliberately corrupted proof records. This is a local computationally verified certificate, not an Isabelle/LRUP kernel result, and not a universal polynomial UNSAT certificate claim.

For the \emph{recognized and labelled chain grammar}, a deterministic compiler checks the complete input and signed occurrence pattern; recognizes the root triple; verifies the constant source and splice templates; and emits the exact three-port clause (or its flipped counterpart). The full instance has canonical input length $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$. A conservative read-only input plus one-work-tape accounting gives $T_{2\mathrm{tape}}(N)=O(N^4)$ and peak work $S_{2\mathrm{tape}}(N)=O(N^2)$, including unsuccessful parsing paths, all clause checks, output, and full source authentication. Standard step-by-step one-tape simulation gives $T_{1\mathrm{tape}}(N)=O(N^8)$ and total footprint at most $O(N^4)$; a fixed universal machine preserves polynomiality with its charged interpreter overhead. These are loose \emph{upper} bounds on a restricted deterministic recognizer, not time/space lower bounds, and do not invoke RAM transport arguments. Any fixed-structure local source-profile table can be recomputed by exhaustive $2^{15}$ enumeration rather than hidden advice.

The compiler can produce a satisfying assignment for every \emph{unpinned} $E_k$; with an externally specified root tuple, it returns SAT with a full source witness for the seven allowed assignments and UNSAT with an exact relational negative certificate for the excluded assignment. The UNSAT certificate for arbitrary $k$ consists of $O(k)$ authenticated local source-profile constraints and a short conflict at the root; it has not been translated into the project's narrower original-clause 2CNF-SCC adaptive-tree grammar. Port-cofactor restrictions add assumptions externally and therefore do not preserve signed exact-(2,2) in the \emph{restricted formula} itself. The underlying $E_k$ does preserve it.

\paragraph{Why this does not establish general SAT hardness or contraction.}
A network of independently composable exact two-port relations is 2SAT regardless of graph shape. Three-port relations can escape 2SAT, but the existence of an individually realizable ternary-clause interface does \emph{not} prove that arbitrary copies can share variables or fan out under the saturated signed occurrence budget without changing their joint source semantics. In particular, the 81-template 2SAT theorem for swaps of the \emph{single} designated $x_4$ port remains valid and does not contradict Theorem~\ref{thm:chain}. The chain's unpinned formula is always SAT, with easily discoverable witnesses. Its three-port output has constant size only because the exact restricted grammar is polynomially recognizable and already easy. The missing theorem is a polynomial, source-cofactor-preserving compiler for arbitrary interacting multi-port constraints, or an algorithm-independent lower bound surviving non-2CNF, affine, Horn, BDD, polynomial-calculus, cutting-planes, and RAM strategies. General one-child $N^\lambda$ SAT contraction, universal UNSAT coverage, and $P$ versus $NP$ remain OPEN.
