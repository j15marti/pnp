\section*{Exact family and declared calculus}
The fixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ counts every derived inference as one, authentic source clauses as free, and allows sound binary Resolution, AND3, GF$(2)$, charged CONVERT and source-independent conservative VARDEF. Only standard non-tautological binary Resolution is used for the present upper bound, with no oracle or new proof rule. The globally shortest shared-DAG derivation is allowed free hindsight selection.

For $n\ge4$, set $E_i=(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})$ with $x_{n+1}=x_1$, $P=\bigvee_{i=1}^n x_i$, $M=\bigvee_{i=1}^n\neg x_i$, and $F_n=(\bigwedge_iE_i)\wedge P\wedge M$. These implication-cycle sources at $n=5,6$ coincide with the repository's authentic Peitl--Szeider examples up to ordering. They are \emph{not} claimed as a newly invented family.

Introduce a single common fresh $g$ and replace every $E_i=(a\vee b)$ by $(a\vee b\vee g)$ and $(a\vee b\vee\neg g)$. Independently replace each length-$n$ clause $(\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_n)$ by the chain
\[
(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee y_1),\
(\neg y_1\vee\ell_3\vee y_2),\ldots,\
(\neg y_{n-3}\vee\ell_{n-1}\vee\ell_n).
\]
Let $T_n$ be this proper duplicate-free strict-3CNF. Its statistics are $v=3n-5$ variables, $m=4n-4$ clauses, $L=12n-12$ source literal signs, and conventional explicit encoded length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$.

\begin{theorem}
For every $n\ge4$, $T_n$ is minimally UNSAT, every source sign is sensitive, and the minimum strong unit-propagation refutation backdoor has size \emph{exactly two}. In the unchanged fixed mixed system,
\[
\boxed{4n-5\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(T_n)\le 5n-7.}
\]
The upper bound is a genuine source-indexed ordinary Resolution certificate; the exact mixed minimum is not asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The guarded pair is equivalent to $E_i$. Existentially quantifying the private chain variables is equivalent to its long clause, so $T_n$ projects to $F_n$. The implications force all $x_i$ equal; $P$ and $M$ prohibit either value, proving UNSAT.

To delete one guarded clause of the $i$th pair, assign $x_i=1,x_{i+1}=0$, every other original $x_j=1$, and set $g$ so the \emph{surviving} guard is true. This falsifies only the deleted original implication. The two wide clauses are true and their chains admit satisfying auxiliary extensions. To delete a clause in the positive chain, set every $x_i=0$; then this chain is $y_1,(\neg y_1\vee y_2),\ldots,\neg y_{n-3}$. Deleting any position makes it SAT by choosing ones before its gap and zeros after; the negative chain is already satisfiable. For negative-chain deletion, take all $x_i=1$ symmetrically. Thus each clause is indispensable. A deletion witness falsifies the missing clause; flipping the sign of any one of its literals satisfies that clause and leaves all others satisfied.

For any $j$, fix $\{x_j,g\}$. The guard assignment activates one unguarded binary $E_i$ per pair. If $x_j=1$, forward unit propagation sets every $x_i=1$; if $x_j=0$, reverse propagation sets every $x_i=0$. The falsified long-clause chain then unit-propagates to contradiction. No \emph{one}-variable assignment yields an initial unit or empty clause because every source has three different variables, so its unit-propagation closure cannot contradict. The exact minimum backdoor cardinality is two.

For a Resolution certificate, reconstruct each $E_i$ from its two guarded clauses in $n$ inferences, and reconstruct $P,M$ from their two chains in $2(n-3)$ inferences. From $P$, resolve successively with $E_2,\ldots,E_n$ to derive $(x_1)$ using $n-1$ inferences. From $M$, resolve with $E_{n-1},\ldots,E_1$ to derive $(\neg x_1)$ using another $n-1$, then resolve the two units once. Every step is syntactically legal and non-tautological, giving $5n-7$ charged nodes. Because all $4n-4$ source clauses are indispensable and every rule in this preselected version has at most two explicit premises, the source-ancestry floor is $m-1=4n-5$, even allowing sharing, algebra, temporary growth, and paid auxiliary definitions.
\end{proof}

\section*{Heterogeneous proof-frontier lemma}
A standard DPLL-to-Resolution deduction, re-established for the current fixed system: for any UNSAT CNF and complete binary adaptive branching tree, where each leaf restricted source formula has a pure Resolution refutation with $q_\ell$ derived nodes,
\[
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le \sum_{\ell}q_\ell + |\text{branch nodes}|.
\]
Lift each restricted source clause to its unreduced source by retaining literals falsified by that leaf assignment, and replay its Resolution steps. Consistent false guards cannot introduce a tautology. Every leaf ends at a subclause of its path-assignment negation. At internal nodes resolve opposite decision literals when both occur; otherwise keep the child clause omitting the decision variable. Root contradiction follows. This proof compiler is restricted to pure Resolution leaves. An analogous uniformly charged lifting of mixed GF$(2)$/AND3/VARDEF proof lines is \textbf{OPEN}; no hidden mixed deduction theorem is assumed.
