\section{Connected ring and exact local support}
For every $k\ge2$ take disjoint renamed $W_i$, $i\bmod k$.
Keep their other nineteen clauses $Q_i$ and replace their first clauses by
\[
 C'_{0,i}=(-x_{1,i-1}\lor x_{3,i}\lor -x_{14,i}). \tag{2}
\]
Let $R_k=\bigwedge_{i<k}(Q_i\land C'_{0,i})$.
\begin{theorem}[Connected rings with constant source-local proofs]
Every $R_k$ is connected proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF
with $n=15k$, $m=20k$, physical padded-DIMACS length
$N=\Theta(k\log k)$, and minimum global strong-2CNF
clause-hitting backdoor exactly $5k$. Yet for each $i$ the
39 genuine original clauses
\[
 U_i=Q_i\land Q_{i+1}\land C'_{0,i+1} \tag{3}
\]
are already UNSAT. The family therefore has constant-record,
$O(\log N)$-bit source-authenticated Resolution refutations,
with source-checked deterministic acquisition in $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}\,N)$
time and $O(\log N)$ work on this family alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $x_{1,i}$ loses one negative occurrence and gains precisely one
from the successor, so every signed count stays two and two.
Foreign source names make the swapped clauses proper.
The original punctured $Q_i$ has connected incidence (a fixed finite
property checked exhaustively for the $15$ names), and each bridge
connects copies around the cycle.
Since each name appears in four distinct clauses, covering the $20k$
clauses requires at least $5k$ variables. The union of the $k$
translated five-name backdoors meets all clauses, including the
bridge through $x_{14,i}$, giving equality.
By (1), $Q_i$ implies $x_{1,i}$, and $Q_{i+1}$ implies
$\neg x_{3,i+1}\land x_{14,i+1}$.
Together they falsify $C'_{0,i+1}$, proving UNSAT of (3).
Resolution completeness yields a finite refutation of this
\emph{fixed} $30$-name source subset. Injectively renaming
the two source blocks gives the same finite proof for all $k$.
The supplied reproducer generates and independently syntactically
checks a particularly small proof: 39 authenticated premises plus
161 ordinary Resolution lines, ending with the empty clause.
Its constant number of records and original source byte/index references
cost $O(\log N)$ total bits asymptotically; a verifier keeps a fixed
number of width-$\le30$ clauses and source indices in $O(\log N)$
work bits, and reads/parses the entire source in one forward scan.
The deterministic family-specific certificate locator selects the first
two blocks, scans to authenticate their source bytes and emits the
fixed renamed proof. This does not refute arbitrary UNSAT inputs.
\end{proof}
A wrong-name hostile control replaces the borrowed $-x_{1,i}$ in (2)
by a fresh unconstrained source name; $Q_i\land Q_{i+1}\land C'$ becomes
SAT. Same-source aliases, not signed incidence counts alone,
make the above semantic join sound.
\section{Separated equality and the deterministic search gap}
In the original 15-name $W_1$, the A slots
$((6,0),(11,0))$ and B slots $((9,2),(17,2))$ (zero-based)
have opposite-sign pairs. Flip both A signs iff $u=0$,
both B signs iff $v=0$, obtaining $H(u,v)$.
Exhaustive model counts are
\[
 (\#H(0,0),\#H(0,1),\#H(1,0),\#H(1,1))
 =(772,272,272,0). \tag{4}
\]
For $a,b\in\{0,1\}^m$, construct variable-disjoint
\[
 E_m(a,b)=\bigwedge_{i<m}
 [H_i^U(a_i,1-b_i)\land H_i^V(1-a_i,b_i)].
 \tag{5}
\]
It is proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ with $30m$ names and $40m$
clauses, and $\SAT(E_m(a,b))\iff a=b$.
Order all Alice-dependent original clauses, all unchanged clauses,
then all Bob-dependent original clauses. Legal padded decimal DIMACS
fields guarantee a fixed byte position for the Alice/middle cut
independent of $(a,b)$; $N=\Theta(m\log m)$.
\begin{theorem}[One-way acquisition versus nondeterministic proof checking]
For any fixed finite-alphabet deterministic machine with one
strictly forward-only read-only input head and fixed $k$ sequential
work tapes, a total exact decider on all $E_m(a,b)$ must use
$\Omega(m)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ visited work cells on some input.
Nevertheless a nondeterministic choice of $i<m$ and orientation
$\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$, at most
$\lceil\log_2m\rceil+1$ bits on the root promise, permits a
source-authenticated UNSAT verifier with a single forward input
scan, $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}\,N)$ bit transitions,
and $O(\log N)$ work. Every SAT formula, even outside the
promise family, is rejected for every certificate; every UNSAT
$E_m(a,b)$ has an accepting certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Immediately after the fixed Alice block, denote the complete
work/control configuration following prefix $a$ by $q_a$.
If $q_a=q_{a'}$ for different $a,a'$, append the SAME
fixed middle and Bob suffix $b=a$. Identical machine
continuations would have to distinguish SAT $E_m(a,a)$
from UNSAT $E_m(a',a)$, impossible.
Thus the $2^m$ values of $a$ have distinct configurations.
For fixed $k$ and finite alphabets, $S$ visited work cells,
contents and head positions yield at most
$2^{O(S)}(S+1)^{O(k)}$ configurations. Hence $S=\Omega(m)$.
This is a one-way deterministic equality argument, not
a claim about unrestricted multipass or random-access SAT.
When $(a_i,b_i)=(1,0)$, the $U$ module is an actual $W_1$;
when $(0,1)$, the $V$ module is. With $j=2i+\epsilon$,
the twenty exact clause ordinals are $2j+[0,1]$,
$4m+16j+[0,15]$, and $36m+2j+[0,1]$, all verified
against actual signed source lines, offsets and original names.
A verified $W_1$ subcollection is UNSAT irrespective of other
clauses, so every SAT source rejects. A mismatch supplies a
successful guess on every UNSAT equality source. The 20-clause
terminal can be expanded into the fixed 32-leaf, source-audited
five-decision-level SCC proof, rather than given a free UNSAT bit.
All malformed/timeout branches are rejected within a uniform clock.
\end{proof}
