\paragraph{Two obstacles and literature preflight.}
The two active obstacles are (i) downstream SAT instances depend on middle existential choices, so their NO obligations cannot be moved upstream without a dependency-preserving theorem; and (ii) every proposed flattening must include acquisition, compilation, verification, indexing, and output costs. Viola's near-linear probabilistic-to-alternating simulation remains the relevant direct theorem. Recent targeted-generator work of Ball--Chen--Tell (CCC 2025) shows that essentially overhead-free derandomization can be engineered under explicit hardness and one-way-function assumptions, but those assumptions are inadmissible as unconditional steps here. Its useful lesson is architectural only: low-overhead simulation requires a dedicated construction, not qualitative class collapse.

\paragraph{Exact reconstruction predicate.}
For one acquired, exactly size-normalized common $Q$, fixed coordinate $i$, and Arthur randomness $r$, let $J$ be the constant family of reconstruction SAT queries. For answer vector $a$, exact truth is
\[
\operatorname{ExactAns}(a;Q,i,r)
=
\exists Y^+\ \forall Y^-\
\bigwedge_{j\in J}
\left[(a_j\Rightarrow\Phi_j(Y_j^+))\wedge
(\neg a_j\Rightarrow\neg\Phi_j(Y_j^-))\right].
\]
YES and NO do not incur separate SAT exponents: one exact deterministic SAT decider decides both SAT and UNSAT by complementing its output. The constant number of queries also does not create an additional exponent.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: dependency-swap obstruction.}
Consider any attempted generic transformation of a prefix
\[\forall u\ \exists z\ \forall y\ R(u,z,y)\]
into one in which the final universal choice is made before the middle existential choice,
\[\forall u\ \forall y\ \exists z\ R(u,z,y).\]
These prefixes are not equivalent even for a constant-size Boolean predicate. Suppress $u$, let $z,y\in\{0,1\}$, and set $R(z,y)$ to the equality predicate $[z=y]$. Then
\[\exists z\ \forall y\ [z=y]\]
is false, whereas
\[\forall y\ \exists z\ [z=y]\]
is true. Therefore no model-independent quantifier commutation can remove the reconstruction dependency. Any improvement must exploit a specific structural property of the actual vMS/Viola predicate.

This elementary counterexample is not a lower bound. Its role is to close a logical loophole: dependency removal cannot follow from bundling, uniqueness, canonicity, or qualitative $P=NP$ alone.

\paragraph{Dependency-uniformization target.}
After late substitution the project still has the schematic dependency shape
\[
\boxed{\exists\sigma\ \forall u\ \exists z\ \forall y\ R(x,Q,\sigma,u,z,y)},
\qquad z=(\ell,a,Y^+),\quad y=Y^-.
\]
A valid flattening needs a construction-specific upstream object $Z=F(Q,r,u)$, independent of the later legal $z$, such that a charged verifier using $Z$ certifies the required downstream NO facts for every legal $z$. Existence or uniqueness of $Z$ is insufficient: acquisition and representation must also be charged.

Thus the current reconstruction accounting remains
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm det}\le \alpha_0(b_0b_1b_2b_3)k^4+o(1)}.
