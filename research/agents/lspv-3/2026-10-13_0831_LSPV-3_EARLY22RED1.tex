\section{Exact objects and quantifiers}

Let $F$ be a proper exact-$(4,4)$ 3-CNF on $n$ variables.  Thus each source
variable occurs four times positively and four times negatively, and
$m=8n/3$.  LBO3SAT-0031 constructs in polynomial time a proper
exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF $P(F)$ with
\[
  v(P(F))=24n,\qquad m(P(F))=32n=\frac43v(P(F)).
\]
Each occurrence is replaced by one of four clones.  The four clones of a
source variable are connected by a directed implication cycle.  Every three
binary implications are padded by one copy of
$Q=W_1\setminus\{(x_1\vee x_7\vee x_{13})\}$, whose three deleted-clause
ports are forced false in all 244 models of $Q$.

The exact substitution $\sigma$ identifies all four clones with their source
variable and maps all gadget variables to zero.  It sends the source clauses
back to $F$, each padded implication to a tautology, and every internal
$Q$-clause to true.  Hence
\[
       F\in\SAT \iff P(F)\in\SAT .
\]
Here historical $N$ counts variables.  For any explicit ordinary clause-list
encoding, the bit length is instead
\[
 |\langle P(F)\rangle|=Theta(m(P(F))\log v(P(F)))=Theta(n\log n),
\]
up to the declared literal and delimiter convention.  No lower bound stated
only as a function of variable count may silently be relabelled as a lower
bound in encoded bits.

LBO3SAT-0032 adds a structured endpoint-local pairing for Tseitin sources.
At a degree-three vertex $u$, the two endpoint clones $a_u,b_u$ for an edge
$e$ are selected according to the falsifying value of a designated other
incident edge $y$.  For $e=uv$ the implication cycle is
\[
        a_u\to b_u\to a_v\to b_v\to a_u.
\]
The preservation of minimal unsatisfiability uses the stated graph
hypotheses (in particular, the structured connected/bridgeless or
2-vertex-connected conditions in the corresponding proof).  It is not a
theorem about all proper exact-$(4,4)$ sources.  For reproducibility, this run
fixes the missing numerical convention: order vertices and edges, sort the
three incident edges at every vertex, and for occurrence edge $e_i$ choose
$y=e_{i+1\bmod 3}$.  This convention is not mathematically load-bearing but
removes an implementation ambiguity in the K4 fingerprint.

PROOF-0034 proves a model-matched Resolution projection.  For each
non-tautological substituted line, maintain a Resolution-derivable subclause;
the induction handles constant pivots, identified pivots, tautological
parents, and disappeared pivots without increasing the number of proof
lines.  The conclusion is a statement about \emph{Resolution refutations}.
It neither compiles arbitrary algorithms into Resolution nor proves that an
arbitrary SAT decider materializes the substituted proof.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item the minimality theorem needs the structured pairing and graph

\section{Separate monotone and affine controls}

LBO3SAT-0007/0008 is a different representation.  A monotone cubic-pair
formula is satisfiable exactly when a chosen edge set covers every positive
clause vertex and its complement covers every negative clause vertex.  The
bounded $n=6$ enumeration above confirms that map and the historical
fingerprint.  It is not the clone-and-forced-port exact-$(2,2)$ compiler and
must not inherit the latter's minimality or Resolution statements.

The two continuation companions must also be read together.  ``21 P C'' left
arbitrary target restrictions open.  ``21 P C A'' supersedes that entry for
the \emph{source-clause-punctured} construction: target satisfiability under
an arbitrary partial assignment is equivalent to local $Q$ compatibility,
clone consistency, and consistency of the punctured affine source system.
That makes hidden algebraic structure an algorithmic shortcut, not a hardness
source.  The limitation remains attached: the theorem has not been shown for
padded-implication or internal-gadget punctures.

\section{Zero-boundary and transfer audit}

The K4 zero-boundary fingerprint was reproduced, but the subsidiary statement
is only as strong as its premises: singleton support-parity blocks rely on the
simple source and the chosen proper compiler instance.  Rank 140 and a
weight-96 kernel vector are finite linear-algebra facts.  They neither imply a
large Resolution refutation nor charge arbitrary computation.  Accordingly I
found no defect that retires LBO3SAT-0031/0032 or PROOF-0034; the defect is the
attempted promotion of their subsidiary fingerprints beyond the represented
model.

Transfer classification under the repository protocol is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER:} substitution projection of
  this target formula into the source formula inside Resolution;
  \item \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge:} treating proof width,
  VLSI routing, communication cuts, observability, branching-program width,
  or collision entropy as a cost of every uniform SAT decider;
  \item \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER:} inferring runtime from 192 singleton
  support-parity blocks, rank 140, a 52-dimensional kernel, 244 gadget models,
  or a hard Resolution family alone.
\end{itemize}

The post-quantum ledger does not change this conclusion.  Schur squares,
quadratic relation spaces, syzygies, and collision distributions are legitimate
invariants of an explicitly chosen code or proof-to-node map.  Here the affine
continuation shows that algebraic structure can help the algorithm.  No
cryptographic binding, random oracle, or worst-case/average-case assumption is
admissible as an unconditional bridge.

\section{Universal-decider and barrier audit}

Let $A$ range over all total uniform deterministic machines deciding the fixed
explicit encoding of exact-$(2,2)$ SAT.  The desired theorem would have to
state, for every such $A$, a lower bound in encoded input length
$N=|\langle F\rangle|$ on the same computation of $A(\langle F\rangle)$.
Neither the compiler nor the projection identifies an internal object that
every $A$ must expose.  A one-bit decider may use Gaussian elimination on an
affine island, dynamic programming on bounded treewidth, implication-graph or
Horn propagation on recognized islands, compact circuit or branching-program
representations, padding-insensitive parsing, or an equivalent wrapper around
another solver.  These controls do not solve the NP-complete full domain, but
they refute universal internal-resource claims that quantify over inputs
without excluding such islands.

Assuming exact-$(2,2)$ SAT is in P yields a polynomial algorithm with some
unknown exponent $k$.  Composing it with LBO3SAT-0031 remains polynomial and
preserves an unknown polynomial exponent.  No cited unconditional theorem
for Turing machines or RAMs forbids that consequence.  The argument
relativizes at the reduction layer and uses low-degree substitutions; it does
not by itself cross relativization or algebrization.  Natural Proofs is not
directly triggered because no large constructive circuit property has been
proved, but it is an additional warning against an unqualified circuit
promotion.  Nonuniform circuits, uniform algorithms, Resolution proofs, and
communication/VLSI implementations remain different models.
