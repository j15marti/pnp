\section{Proper ternary construction}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a simple connected cubic graph with $v=|V|$ even and $e=3v/2$. Put one Boolean $x_f$ on every edge $f$. For each vertex $u$ impose $\bigoplus_{f\ni u}x_f=b_u$. The four forbidden truth tuples form four proper three-literal clauses on the three incident edge variables. Their conjunction is $T(G,b)$ with $n=e$ variables and $m=4v$ clauses. In the fixed signed-record encoding,
\[
N=(16v+2)h,\qquad h=\left\lceil\log_2(64(e+4v+1))\right\rceil=\Theta(\log v).
\]
Every $x_f$ occurs four times positively and four times negatively across its two incident vertex gadgets. Charge changes alter literal polarity, not unsigned supports, lengths or occurrence degrees.

An original variable is live at a clause-order cut if it appears before and after the cut. This is a syntactic frontier for the unchanged, source-name-preserving list, not an invariant of arbitrary semantic rewrites. Define
\[
B(G)=\min_{U\subseteq V,\ |U|=v/2}|\delta_G(U)|.
\]

\begin{theorem}[All-order original source width; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every charge $b$ and every permutation of the $4v$ unchanged clauses of $T(G,b)$, some cut has at least $B(G)$ live original variables. If for every vertex set $U$, $|\delta_G(U)|\ge h_0\min\{|U|,v-|U|\}$ with constant $h_0>0$, then
\[
w\ge h_0v/2=\Omega(N/\log N).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Mark a graph vertex touched on the first appearance of any of its four clauses. Every such clause mentions all three incident edge variables. Stop immediately when the $v/2$-th distinct vertex has been touched, and call the touched vertex set $U$. Every crossing edge $f=\{u,z\}$ with $u\in U$, $z\notin U$ has its variable $x_f$ appearing in the already processed clause at $u$, but still appearing in the unprocessed clauses of $z$. Thus every crossing edge yields a distinct live variable. Hence $w\ge|\delta_G(U)|\ge B(G)$. Expansion gives $w\ge h_0v/2$ and the encoding gives $v=\Theta(N/\log N)$.
\end{proof}
