\section{Exact theorem and proof, with provenance}
Fix one original CNF $F$, a finite, public roster $P_1,\ldots,P_r$ of deterministic partial procedures, and a separate sound finite verifier for every SAT witness or source-authenticated UNSAT certificate. A partial procedure may return \texttt{UNRESOLVED}; it may never announce an unverified NO from a bounded search. Each run begins from the same immutable original source and its entire execution and verification uses $t_i$ measured abstract steps. At least one procedure is complete on all ordinary CNF inputs. The controller executes every unfinished procedure from scratch at budgets $1,2,4,\ldots$ and deletes its state after each timeout, rejection or nonterminal return.

\begin{theorem}[Classical finite-portfolio restart lemma, KNOWN]\label{thm:race}
If some $P_i$ produces and verifies a valid answer in $t_i\ge1$ steps, and $t_* =\min\{t_i:\text{$P_i$ terminates with checked SAT/UNSAT}\}$, then the controller returns the correct SAT/UNSAT answer and uses fewer than
\[
 r\bigl(4t_*+\lceil\log_2 t_*\rceil+1\bigr)
\]
charged abstract steps, provided the one-operation budget overrun convention and policy initialization are charged as one step each. The result holds for source SAT and source UNSAT without knowing either label in advance. It does \emph{not} bound workspace by the workspace of the fastest completed policy.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $J=\lceil\log_2 t_*\rceil$. The winning program finishes no later than its trial of budget $2^J$. Each earlier trial uses at most $2^j+1$ metered steps (the final failed overflow attempt is counted). Thus even charging all $r$ programs in all rounds, total work is at most $r\sum_{j=0}^{J}(2^j+1)<r(4t_*+J+1)$. Every accepted result undergoes the original-source verifier; source-inconsistent or incomplete certificates cannot terminate the controller. The complete DPLL member guarantees eventual termination on both labels. The proof addresses a fixed abstract step clock, not a constant-overhead native FLMV simulator, and it reproduces the previously established result \cite{lspv255,lspv1262}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Failure of winner-space competition, RESTRICTED EXACT]\label{lem:space}
For an infinite pair of easily authenticated proper-3CNF SAT and UNSAT source families of length $N$, there are a partial exact $P_{\rm easy}$ with $O(N)$ full charged time and $O(\log N)$ writable bits on both labels and a silent partial $P_{\rm waste}$, such that the unspaced-capped restarted controller executing $P_{\rm waste}$ before $P_{\rm easy}$ reaches $\Omega(N)$ simultaneous writable bits before accepting the $O(\log N)$-space decision. The controller still runs in $O(N)$ abstract steps for fixed roster size.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use disjoint positive proper triples on $3k$ padding variables. The SAT member contains in addition a fixed positive source triple; the UNSAT member instead contains all eight sign patterns on that fixed triple. Each pair has source length $N=\Theta(k\log k)$ with dense, fixed-width indexed identifiers. A fixed scanner $P_{\rm easy}$ authenticates the source grammar in $O(N)$ time using $O(\log N)$ counters. In the SAT case it verifies the all-TRUE assignment by direct source evaluation; the witness is streamed rather than stored. In the UNSAT case, the eight source clauses forbid all eight assignments to the fixed triple, so a fixed constant-size tree refutation validates NO without examining any assignment to the padding variables. Hence both label decisions are sound and space logarithmic. The silent policy $P_{\rm waste}$ writes one new work-tape cell per step and produces no certificate. Arrange its trial immediately before the fast policy in each geometric stage. At the stage with budget $2^J=\Theta(N)$, the waste policy writes $\Theta(N)$ simultaneous bits before it is aborted. The following easy policy then certifies the answer. Thus peak workspace is $\Omega(N)$ regardless of the small completed winner's space. Geometric total steps remain $O(N)$ for two policies. Capping the losing policy's tape at $B$ enforces $O(B)$ candidate space, but then a winner needing more than $B$ bits is not guaranteed to finish; a subsequent uncapped fallback restores correctness without a $B$-space bound. This is an architectural counterexample, not SAT decision-space hardness.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precisely charged selector state.} A realistic residual state is $z=(F,\rho,\Pi,\mathcal{D},\mathcal{C},\text{source read head},\text{policy history},\text{proof trace})$, with measured $\kappa(z)$ containing residual size, signed occurrences, certified Horn/Krom/GF$(2)$ class, current assignments, failed trials, source probes, strategy switching, verification, live retained bits and guest/child dimensions. The Python host materializes the original clauses and counts selected semantic events, unsuccessful restarts, and proof checks; its \texttt{tracemalloc} peak is an empirical host-heap diagnostic, not a native TM space bound. \section{ARC/FLMV connection, representation and source safeguards}
The fixed six-policy portfolio shows that searching a \emph{fixed, public} policy menu need not invoke an oracle for its fastest member: dyadic metering provides an explicit, highly lossy uniform alternative. For $r=O(1)$ its \emph{abstract time} overhead has no polynomial exponent tax. Yet an unregulated losing policy can destroy the \emph{simultaneous space} exponent even on easy both-label sources. Therefore any attempted recurrence
\[
 K'\le\max\left\{\frac{11}{10},\frac35+\frac K2\right\},\qquad
 L'\le\max\left\{\frac12,\frac L2\right\}
\]
must prove both (i) an enforceable same-machine work-tape cap, with costs of policy generation, source/index access, failed runs and reset, and (ii) complete coverage by successful small-space policies with physically accessible ordinary SAT children of length $N^{1/2+o(1)}$. The actual FLMV test point is $c=241/200$ and $d_*(c)=11642479/23232400$; its strict space margin above $1/2$ is only $26279/23232400$. The known conditional exponent recurrence is not implemented here. No source-only universal child splitter or full-parent call to an arbitrary $A$ may be silently assumed; DELAY/COPY wrappers remain adversarial controls. The 2026 affine-MCSP threshold theorem is a restricted semantic no-go only. A generic proof-size or interface-width lower bound also does not follow from the counting above.

\paragraph{Relativization, algebrization and Natural Proofs audit.} The decision-trace bound is model-relative and source-recognizable, so no theorem over unrestricted SAT machines is asserted. It is compatible with powerful oracle machines and does not rely on a constructive large circuit property or a forbidden relativizing proof of $P\ne NP$. The source grammar itself enables short certificates, making the construction an \emph{easy-family hostile control}. The family has no claim to a random-hard-instance distribution.
