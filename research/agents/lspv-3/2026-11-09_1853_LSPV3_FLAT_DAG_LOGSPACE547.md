## Theorem 547.1, proof, limits
Let the independently supplied original CNF F have N bits and a separately supplied **read-only** proof certificate P have B bits. P is an explicit finite acyclic DAG with q<=B topologically numbered records, all parent indices less than the child index. Each record states signed conclusion, assumptions, provenance, source/owner identity and all rule premises. Suppose the complete original-source-authenticated legality predicate of **every rule** is decidable in deterministic O(log(N+B)) *writable* bits and polynomial time with indexed read-only access to F and P on the same random-access work-tape machine. All case/AND obligations must be checked, and the root must be false with empty context. Then there is a deterministic verifier of the **PAIR** (F,P) with

$$
S_{\rm verify}(N,B)=O(\log(N+B)),\qquad
T_{\rm verify}(N,B)=(N+B)^{O(1)}.
$$

**Proof:** Enumerate every record i in topological order. Reuse one O(log(N+B))-bit scratch region to invoke its logspace local checker, rescanning source, certificate and earlier parent records as required. Store only i, read-only offsets, rule tag, counters and the fixed local scratch. Reject invalid signed contexts, source references, illegal or missing branches, duplicate/noncanonical IDs or future parent references. After all records verify, check the final goal node. Induction over record positions proves all nodes source-sound under their recorded contexts; this mathematical induction is not a runtime recursion. q<=B local checks are polynomial in N+B. Since work storage is dense, the **native high-water** is O(log(N+B)), not just a live-cell count. A globally unique paid inference row costs exactly the fixed calculus weight; shared references/facets do not re-charge it. QED.

The conclusion is conditional on a full fixed local-checkable grammar; the historical R_mix341 accepted-byte grammar for LOCAL-CONVERT, DEF facets, private/exported interfaces and actual branching is unauthenticated [P2-D1]. This result improves supplied-proof verification over [P2-L3-546], NOT the size of any proof and NOT a deterministic decider on F alone. A certificate of exponential length B can have log B=Theta(v) in the number v of source variables. Neither acquisition nor serialization of P from F is free.
