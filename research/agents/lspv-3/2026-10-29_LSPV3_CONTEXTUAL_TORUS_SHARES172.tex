\section{PROPOSE: missing lemma and four falsifiable mechanisms}
Prior CONTEXT-VALUE-AFFINE171 proved that a datum with zero initial decision-information gain can participate in a fully revealing pair, but its concrete decision instance was affine. The missing project-specific lemma is a \emph{physical connected strict signed exact-$(2,2)$} 3CNF SAT/UNSAT promise with growing treewidth in which the same phenomenon survives, with a source-recognizing one-program controller and independent certification costs.
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{M1, XOR masking of signed source occurrences.} An existing two-answer strict22 source pair differing in two balanced signs can be uniformly masked by a whole-variable complementation, producing two raw sign observations with zero individual, but one bit of joint, decision value. \textbf{PROVED/RESTRICTED} below.
\item[] \textbf{M2, structural width forces expensive template decision.} Even if the primal graph has treewidth $\Omega(\sqrt n)$, the canonical promise requires superpolynomial deterministic time. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by an explicit source-authenticated polynomial controller.
\item[] \textbf{M3, an immediate positive marginal-value rule always discovers both sign shares.} Both initial entropy gains are zero, so a policy refusing zero-gain probes fails. \textbf{FALSIFIED} for that stated policy; grouping and lookahead recover the pair.
\item[] \textbf{M4, potential decrease yields general polynomial SAT.} A global polynomial-value countdown works on our certified bounded-work promise, but proving its bound for arbitrary SAT is the unresolved step. \textbf{OPEN}; polynomial-bit-length alone is insufficient.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD II: contextual value, an adaptive controller, and paid certification}
Use the requested notation $x\mid_v$ and $(x=1)\mid_v$ to label a datum's contextual value, not probability conditioning. Specifically, define $v_\Phi(I\mid s)=\Phi(s)-\Phi(s\text{ after certified use of }I)$, and charge acquisition and checking cost $C_s(I)$ separately. If $\Phi$ is \emph{target conditional entropy}, each individual raw share initially has value zero; after one share the complementary share has value one. A duplicate has value zero. Such gains are not additive across correlated observations.

Negative values are also possible for a \emph{different declared} obligation-work potential: if a speculative result leaves one required check and new certified evidence invalidates five units of cached proof, leaving six tasks, then $v_\Phi=1-6=-5$. This is a specified control state, not an observed decrease of Shannon entropy. The global controller must fund both the five lost steps and subsequent reconstruction and cannot use the negative value as a free time saving.

The uniform deterministic controller $\mathcal A$, implemented in the included Python, carries out the following explicitly charged phases:

\item[] Read $a,b$ and compute $(t,r)=(a\oplus b,a)$. Generate $H_{L,t,r}$ from scratch and compare every clause with the input. An invalid template returns \texttt{UNSUPPORTED}, not UNSAT.
\item[] Independently validate all four signed occurrences of each variable, proper ternary clause scopes, duplicate freedom and full connectedness. The canonical promise grammar is a decidable source restriction.
\item[] On SAT, compute the explicit torus assignment, find a source $V$ witness by an exhaustive \emph{fixed 15-bit} scan, choose compatible fixed-guard witnesses from the enumerated table, then verify every original clause.

\begin{theorem}[Charged polynomial controller and a global numerical rank]\label{th:cost}
Let $N$ be the actual physical encoded bit length of $H_{L,t,r}$, under canonical explicit binary or DIMACS encoding, $N=\Theta(n\log n)$. The above one-program canonical-promise controller has polynomial total construction, certification, and reconstruction cost. On an indexed word-RAM with $O(\log N)$-bit words, a conservative bound is
\[
T_{\rm RAM}(N)=O(N^2+C_0),\qquad S_{\rm RAM}(N)=O(N+C_0)\text{ bits},
\]
where $C_0=O(2^{15})$ times a fixed source-size factor pays for all fixed 15-bit enumeration, including failed assignments and guard checks. Two-tape, one-tape, and a fixed efficiently universal machine admit polynomial simulations with \emph{separately charged} head movements, crossings, symbol changes, input footprint and output writes. These simulation bounds are not RAM/tape lower bounds.

There is a complete-state integer potential of \emph{polynomial numerical value} that decreases at every primitive computational step of this verified controller, including an unsuccessful strategy and both certification branches.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The torus has $4L^2/3$ clauses and the connector has 60 more. Each complete source regeneration, source comparison, graph-check scan, and witness verification costs polynomial in $N$; all finite source/guard loops have no dependence on $L$. The implementation retains $O(N)$ encoded input, graph/work vectors, and the $O(N)$ witness. A conservative $O(N^2+C_0)$ word-operation bound admits standard deterministic maps and incidental decoding overhead.

For the rank, partition the fixed control flow into at most eight authenticated phases. Assign each phase a uniform worst-case primitive-step allowance $B(N)=c(N^d+C_0)$, justified by the preceding explicit loops, for some fixed $c,d$. In a phase, let $u$ denote unused phase allowances and $r$ the current unused primitive-operation credit. Define
\[
\Psi(s)=u(B(N)+1)+r,
\qquad 0\le r\le B(N).
\]
Every primitive operation reduces $r$; switching phases discards remaining credit and reduces $u$. Thus $\Psi$ strictly decreases until certified SAT/UNSAT or safe rejection, has polynomial numerical initial value, and accounts for all failed paths that actually occur. The argument certifies \emph{an independently proven polynomial algorithm}; it is not a method to conjure a polynomial budget for arbitrary SAT from a short potential description. Streamed source access and work-tape movement are charged when simulating on the relevant machine, rather than transferring crossing-sequence bounds to RAM.
\end{proof}
