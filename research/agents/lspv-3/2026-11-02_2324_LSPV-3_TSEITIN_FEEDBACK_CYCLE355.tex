\section*{Model and exact quantifiers}
Fix \emph{unchanged} $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$: original signed CNF sources at zero cost; unit-paid derived nodes each with at most two explicit premises; valid binary Resolution, local Boolean AND3 of essential support at most three, locally verified affine row aliases, binary GF$(2)$ addition, charged local conversions and charged source-independent conservative gate definitions. All valid adaptive shared-DAG derivations are admissible and the optimum is selected without discovery cost. $W_R(F)$ is the attained minimum number of distinct paid nodes, and $\overline W_R(N)$ is the maximum for UNSAT formulas of encoded length at most $N$ bits. Neither a globally semantic oracle nor a new proof rule is added.

For an UNSAT formula $F$, a \emph{strong UP-refutation backdoor} $B$ is a set of original variables such that for \emph{every} assignment $\alpha:B\to\{0,1\}$, ordinary iterative unit propagation on $F|_\alpha$ reaches a contradiction. Let $b_{\rm UP}(F)$ be its minimum cardinality.

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite connected loopless cubic graph, $h=|V|$ and $e=|E|=3h/2$. Give each vertex a charge $\sigma_v\in\mathbb F_2$ of odd total sum. Encode each parity equation $\bigoplus_{a\ni v}x_a=\sigma_v$ as the four forbidden-valuation width-three clauses. The resulting proper, duplicate-free strict-3CNF $F(G,\sigma)$ has $e$ edge variables and $m=4h$ source clauses.

\begin{theorem}[Exact feedback-edge UP backdoor]
For every connected cubic $G$ and odd charge $\sigma$,
\[
\boxed{b_{\rm UP}(F(G,\sigma))=e-h+1.}
\]
In fact $B\subseteq E$ is a strong UP-refutation backdoor if and only if $(V,E\setminus B)$ is a forest.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an arbitrary assignment to $B$. The residual canonical parity clauses at a vertex with one remaining edge force it by UP; a vertex with no remaining edges either is satisfied or gives an empty clause; a vertex with at least two remaining edges has no unit or empty clause. Hence UP is exactly leaf peeling in the residual edge graph, detecting an inconsistent fully peeled component.

If $G-B$ is a forest, UP completely peels every tree. The XOR of all residual vertex charges equals the original odd charge because every assigned edge contributes at both ends. Thus at least one tree component is inconsistent, and UP refutes under every assignment to $B$.

Conversely suppose $G-B$ contains a cyclic component. Contract the connected components of $G-B$ to quotient vertices. Edges $B$ between them make a connected quotient multigraph (discard quotient loops for parity). Choose values of $B$ so that every \emph{tree} component has even residual charge. These linear equations have a solution: their rows are a proper subset of the vertex rows of the incidence matrix of a connected quotient graph, and any proper subset of those rows is linearly independent over $\mathbb F_2$. UP peels those trees consistently, while every cyclic component retains a nonempty 2-core, so UP cannot detect inconsistency there. Thus this assignment to $B$ does not refute. A minimum feedback-edge set is the complement of a spanning tree and has $e-h+1$ edges.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Indispensability and exact mixed optimum]
If $G$ is also 2-vertex-connected, $F(G,\sigma)$ is clause-minimally UNSAT and every individual signed occurrence is SAT-repairable. In unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$,
\[
\boxed{W_R(F(G,\sigma))=4h-1,\qquad b_{\rm UP}(F(G,\sigma))=h/2+1.}
\]
For the prism graphs $G_r=C_r\square K_2$, $r\ge3$, one has $h=2r$, $e=3r$, $m=8r$, $W_R=8r-1$ and $b_{\rm UP}=r+1$, with strict DIMACS source length $N=\Theta(r\log r)$. The growing UP-backdoor size is $\Theta(N/\log N)$ despite the linear mixed proof minimum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Odd total charge makes the formula UNSAT. For any particular forbidden-pattern clause at vertex $v$, fix its three incident edges to that wrong-parity pattern. Since $G-v$ is connected, all remaining vertex parity equations are simultaneously soluble: the XOR of their right sides, including contributions of the fixed boundary edges, equals zero precisely because the pattern at $v$ has parity $\sigma_v\oplus1$. This full assignment violates exactly the chosen source clause. Deleting it makes SAT, and flipping any one sign of that source also makes SAT.

Every sound refutation must have all $m$ indispensable source clauses in its contradiction ancestry. If that ancestry has $q$ distinct paid nodes each with at most two named premises, connectedness gives $m+q-1\le 2q$, so $q\ge m-1$, independently of shared nodes, adaptive choices, temporary growth, local algebraic rules and conservative auxiliaries.

For equality, conjoin each vertex's four source clauses by three allowed local AND3 inference nodes, obtaining exactly its three-variable parity truth table and a locally verified affine row alias. Add the $h$ affine rows by $h-1$ binary GF$(2)$ nodes. All edge variables cancel and the odd total charge yields $0=1$. The certificate has $3h+h-1=4h-1$ paid nodes. This uses the previously fixed free locally \emph{verified} alias from a support-three Boolean factor to its equal affine row, not a newly allowed free global conversion. If that alias is charged in a different calculus, the upper cost must be amended.
\end{proof}
