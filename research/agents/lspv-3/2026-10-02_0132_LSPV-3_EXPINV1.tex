\section*{PROVED/EXACT: exponent-normalization lemma}
Suppose a fixed deterministic exact SAT decider \(A\) satisfies
\[
T_A(n)=O(n^{\kappa_0})
\]
for some finite \(\kappa_0\). Then for every constant \(K\ge\kappa_0\),
\[
T_A(n)=O(n^K).
\]
Thus a declaration that \(A\) is an \(n^\kappa\)-time SAT decider does not canonically determine \(\kappa\): every valid exponent can be inflated.

Consequently, a crossover comparison such as
\[
L_V(\kappa)=C c\kappa^4,\qquad L_S(\kappa)=C\kappa^2(\kappa+1)
\]
is exact algebra at the declared exponent, but the threshold
\[
\kappa_*(c)=\frac{1+\sqrt{1+4c}}{2c}
\]
is not an intrinsic property of the underlying algorithm unless the exponent is normalized.

\paragraph{Guard against a false rescue.}
Exponent inflation cannot make a contradiction easier. Replacing a valid \(\kappa_0\) by \(K>\kappa_0\) weakens the runtime upper bound. It may change which symbolic ledger is numerically smaller, but does not improve the best certified runtime for the fixed algorithm. XOVER2's phase diagram is retained as algebra, while interpretation of an unknown SAT exponent as intrinsically above or below its crossover is rejected without normalization.

\section*{Canonical parameterization}
Define
\[
\kappa_*(A):=\inf\{k\ge0:T_A(n)=O(n^{k+o(1)})\}.
\]
This is invariant under restating the same upper bound with a larger exponent. The infimum need not be attained by a simple \(O(n^{\kappa_*(A)})\) bound, and a proof under \(P=NP\) is not entitled to know or compute it. The safe protocol is
\[
\boxed{\text{state ledgers for every certified exponent }K\text{ of fixed }A,\text{ then take only justified infima.}}
\]
If \(T_{\rm rec}(n)\le n^{F(K)+o(1)}\) for every certified \(K>\kappa_*(A)\), with monotone \(F\), the strongest exponent from this family is the right-limit \(\inf_{K>\kappa_*(A)}F(K)\). For continuous polynomial ledgers this equals \(F(\kappa_*(A))\) only as an infimum statement.
