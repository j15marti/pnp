\section*{Proved lemma}
\begin{theorem}[Every occurrence is a same-length opposite-label neighbor]
Let $G=(V,E)$ be connected, simple and bridgeless cubic; let $\chi\in\{0,1\}^V$ have odd total charge. Reversing any ONE literal occurrence polarity in the signed source Tseitin CNF $F_{G,\chi}$ makes it satisfiable, without changing $E$-encoded length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A clause $C_{v,a}$ at vertex $v$ excludes exactly the local edge assignment $a$ with parity $1\oplus\chi_v$. For connected cubic graphs, a cut vertex would distribute three incident edges among at least two components, leaving a component connected to $v$ by one bridge. Hence bridgeless implies $G-v$ connected. Fix the three incident edges to $a$; omit only the equation at $v$ and solve the $h-1$ remaining incidence rows. Since $G-v$ is connected, their only dependence is XOR of all rows, whose RHS is
$\bigoplus_{u\ne v}\chi_u\oplus\bigoplus a=(1\oplus\chi_v)\oplus(1\oplus\chi_v)=0$.
Thus the system has a solution falsifying just $C_{v,a}$ among all source clauses. Reversing any of its three literals makes that clause true on this same solution. The changed sign occupies exactly one bit.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[End-to-end source-observation conservation]
For EVERY deterministic total correct all-input SAT decider $A$ (SAT/UNSAT/malformed) running on the explicit encoding $E$, and EVERY $x=E(F_{G,\chi})$ as above, the executed computation reads all $12h$ source-sign positions. This remains true through arbitrary finite deterministic adaptive compilers, shared DAGs, conservative auxiliary-variable processors, lazy representations, circuit simplifiers and solver stages, provided the full pipeline charges every original-bit acquisition. With word probes exposing at most $b$ original bits per paid step, at least $\lceil12h/b\rceil$ such steps are needed. In the restricted fixed-incidence, sign-only input promise, deterministic certificate complexity at this UNSAT input is EXACTLY $12h$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every original sign position $p$, the previous lemma gives same-length SAT $x^{(p)}$. If the complete deterministic execution never reads $p$ on $x$, its observation transcript, derived machine states, intermediate encodings, and output are identical on $x$ and $x^{(p)}$ by induction over individual charged steps. This contradicts opposite decision labels. Lazy aliases merely postpone the read. The $12h$ coordinates are distinct. In the sign-only promise, all sign bits determine the input, giving the matching certificate upper bound.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Ceiling]
Every $N$-bit input has certificate complexity at most $N$ by reading all its bits. Therefore the robust observation bound is only $12h=\Theta(N/\log N)$ on this family and cannot imply superpolynomial all-algorithm SAT time, mixed-system proof length or B1/B2/B3.
\end{theorem}
