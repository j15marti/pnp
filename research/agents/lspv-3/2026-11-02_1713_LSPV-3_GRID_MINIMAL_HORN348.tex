\section*{Explicit permuted-chain family}
Fix an integer $r\ge2$ and write $k=r^2$. Introduce $x_0,\dots,x_k$ and $y_1,\dots,y_k$. Define the permutation
\[
\pi(i)=r((i-1)\bmod r)+\left\lfloor\frac{i-1}{r}\right\rfloor+1\quad (1\le i\le k).
\]
It maps row-major cell order to column-major order on an $r$ by $r$ grid. Consider the width-at-most-three CNF
\[
\begin{split}
F_r={}&(x_0)\wedge(\neg x_k)\wedge(\neg y_1)
\wedge\bigwedge_{j=2}^{k}(y_{j-1}\vee\neg y_j)\\
&{}\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k}(\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i\vee y_{\pi(i)}).
\end{split}
\tag{1}
\]
Every source record is distinct and non-tautological. There are $n=2k+1$ variables, $m=2k+2$ source clauses and $L=5k+1$ signed literal occurrences. With one clause per line in strict ASCII DIMACS and dense identifiers, $N(r)=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$ bits.

\begin{theorem}[Constructive large-width easy minimum upper bound]
For every $r\ge2$, the formula $F_r$ is clause-minimally UNSAT and all $L$ individual source-occurrence sign flips make it SAT. Its primal graph has an $r\times r$ grid minor and hence treewidth at least $r$. Nonetheless,
\[
\boxed{2r^2+1=m-1\ \le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_r)\ \le 3r^2.}
\tag{2}
\]
An exact combined-system optimum is not claimed. The upper certificate is a deterministic, uniform, legal resolution DAG of $3r^2$ distinct inference nodes. The family is renamable Horn.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The unit $\neg y_1$ and the chain $y_{j-1}\vee\neg y_j$ force all $y_j=0$. The remaining ternaries and $x_0=1$ force $x_1=\cdots=x_k=1$, contradicting $\neg x_k$.

To demonstrate \emph{every} source's indispensability, give a satisfying assignment after deletion of precisely that source. Removing $x_0$ permits all $x,y=0$. Removing $\neg x_k$ permits every $x=1$, every $y=0$. Removing $\neg y_1$ permits every $y=1$, $x_0=1$ and all later $x=0$. Removing the $j$th $y$-chain implication, for $j\ge2$, permits $y_p=0$ for $p<j$ and $y_p=1$ for $p\ge j$; choose $i=\pi^{-1}(j)$ and set $x_0,\dots,x_{i-1}=1$ and $x_i,\dots,x_k=0$. Removing the $i$th guarded $x$-transition permits all $y=0$ and the same $x$ prefix-one/suffix-zero assignment at $i$. In every case the omitted source itself is false under the displayed assignment, while all retained sources are true. Changing the sign of any one literal in that omitted clause makes that literal true, proving all $L$ individual sign-flip SAT controls.

Any sound conservative binary-premise refutation must depend on all $m$ sources: otherwise the relevant deleted-source satisfying assignment, extended uniquely through any conservative definitions, would satisfy every used premise. The undirected ancestry DAG has $m+q$ vertices and at least $m+q-1$ edges but at most $2q$ inference edges. Thus $q\ge m-1$ for every legal adaptive DAG, even using sharing, XOR, temporaries and DEF.

For the upper bound, resolve the initial source $\neg y_1$ successively along the $k-1$ binary $y$ implications, deriving $\neg y_j$ for each $j$. Resolve each source $\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i\vee y_{\pi(i)}$ with the appropriate derived $\neg y_{\pi(i)}$ to get $\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i$ at $k$ further nodes. Then resolve from source $x_0$ through the $k$ binary transitions to obtain $x_k$, and resolve this with source $\neg x_k$ once more. The exact node count is $(k-1)+k+k+1=3k$, with every pivot and source identifier explicit.

For each grid cell indexed by $i$, contract the primal-graph edge $x_i y_{\pi(i)}$ (present in its guarded ternary) and delete vertex $x_0$. The $x_i x_{i+1}$ edges give all row-adjacent pairs, and the $y_j y_{j+1}$ edges give all column-adjacent pairs after contraction; discard any extra boundary edges. This exhibits the complete $r\times r$ grid as a minor, whence primal treewidth is at least $r$ by minor monotonicity and the known treewidth of a square grid.

Finally rename every $y_j$ by $z_j=\neg y_j$. Each ternary becomes $\neg x_{i-1}\vee x_i\vee\neg z_{\pi(i)}$, and each $y$ implication becomes $\neg z_{j-1}\vee z_j$. All clauses now have at most one positive literal, so the family is renamable Horn.
\end{proof}
