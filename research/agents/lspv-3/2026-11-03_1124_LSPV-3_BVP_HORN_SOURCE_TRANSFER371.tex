\section*{Substantive output/source-transfer obstruction}
\begin{theorem}
Let $\pi_n$ be the explicit $(2n+1)$-node $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ refutation of $B_n$. Any mapping $\tau$, even with free discovery time and nonuniform advice, that outputs a VALID Ext-PC-sqrt over $\mathbb Q$ refutation $\tau(B_n,\pi_n)$ of the ORIGINAL algebraic source $\Gamma_n$ must, for some $c>0$ and all sufficiently large $n$, have
\[
|\tau(B_n,\pi_n)|_{\rm bits}\ge c2^n=
2^{\Omega(N_n/\log(N_n+2))}.
\]
No polynomial-output-bit-overhead translation exists on this family. A sequential bit-output implementation necessarily spends $\Omega(2^n)$ operations emitting its output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The indexed Resolution proof has $O(n)$ constant-width lines with $O(\log(n+1))$-bit parent and pivot references, so the source plus proof is $O(n\log(n+1))$ bits. The translated proof is by hypothesis a valid refutation of the precise $\Gamma_n$ in Alekseev's grammar, and thus must have $\Omega(2^n)$ bits by his Theorem 3.5. Substitute $N_n=\Theta(n\log(n+1))$. The output-time statement simply counts emitted bits, not SAT decision time.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Source-axiom compilation corollary.} One cannot simultaneously (i) derive, from $\Gamma_n$ with legal conservative algebraic extensions, all correctly translated $B_n$ source clauses in polynomially many encoded bits, and (ii) replay the known $O(n)$-node Resolution proof in the same algebraic system with only polynomial-bit overhead. Concatenation would contradict Theorem 3.5. This is a conditional no-go for the PROOF compiler/source-axiom premise, not an obstruction to ordinary Boolean circuit evaluation.
