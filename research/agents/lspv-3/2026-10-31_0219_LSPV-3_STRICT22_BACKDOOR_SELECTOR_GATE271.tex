\section{Exact static strong-backdoor characterization}
Let $F$ be a proper CNF with no repeated/complementary variable in a clause. For a subset $B$ of its variables and any assignment $\tau:B\to\{0,1\}$ let $F[\tau]$ be the source CNF simplified by deleting satisfied clauses and falsified literals. A \emph{strong $\mathcal C$-backdoor} $B$ requires $F[\tau]\in\mathcal C$ for \emph{every} $\tau$, where $\mathcal C$ is syntactic Krom (width $\le2$), Horn (at most one positive literal), or dual-Horn (at most one negative literal). The definition does not include recursive unit-propagation closure, component splitting, source-aware isomorphism, or the right to stop once a SAT witness is found. These are different parameter regimes [2].

\begin{theorem}[Exact clausewise criterion, classical strong-backdoor semantics]
For every proper source $F$ and $B\subseteq\operatorname{var}(F)$, $B$ is a strong Krom backdoor if and only if
\[
 \forall C\in F:\quad |B\cap\operatorname{var}(C)|\ge\max\{0,|C|-2\}.
\]
It is a strong Horn backdoor if and only if
\[
 \forall C\in F:\quad |B\cap\operatorname{pos}(C)|\ge\max\{0,|\operatorname{pos}(C)|-1\}.
\]
For dual-Horn replace $\operatorname{pos}$ by $\operatorname{neg}$. The equivalences hold for BOTH SAT and UNSAT original formulas.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $C$ and $B$. To maximize the number of literals of $C$ remaining after an assignment to $B$, set every occurrence belonging to $B$ to FALSE as a literal. This is one coherent assignment because $C$ is proper and has distinct variables. The simplified clause has exactly $|C|-|B\cap\operatorname{var}(C)|$ remaining literals. It is Krom for every assignment precisely when this maximum is at most two. Similarly the maximum surviving positive literals is $|\operatorname{pos}(C)|-|B\cap\operatorname{pos}(C)|$; if any selected literal satisfies the clause it disappears, while falsifying all selected clause literals makes this maximum achievable. Thus the Horn inequality is necessary and sufficient. The negative-literal proof is identical. The conditions are source-clause-local, so conjunction over all original clauses proves the claim regardless of the formula's SAT/UNSAT label.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Universal strict22 lower bound]
If $F$ is a proper strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$-3CNF with $n$ occurring variables, then $m=4n/3$ and
\[
 \beta_{\rm Krom}(F),\ \beta_{\rm Horn}(F),\ \beta_{\rm dualHorn}(F)
 \ \ge\ \lceil n/3\rceil.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For Krom the clausewise criterion requires at least one selected variable in every original clause. Each variable appears in exactly four clauses; consequently $4|B|\ge m=4n/3$. For Horn let $p_C$ be the number of positive literals in source clause $C$. The criterion yields $\sum_C\max\{0,p_C-1\}\le \sum_C|B\cap\operatorname{pos}(C)|=2|B|$. But $\sum_C\max\{0,p_C-1\}\ge\sum_C(p_C-1)=2n-4n/3=2n/3$, hence $|B|\ge n/3$. Replace positive by negative for dual-Horn.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact infinite easy signed22 adversarial control}
For every odd $k\ge7$ use variables $A_i,B_i,C_i$ for $i\in\mathbb Z_k$. For every $i$ and $s\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ include
\[
 (\epsilon_s A_i\lor\epsilon_s B_{i+s}\lor\epsilon_s C_{i+2s}),\qquad
 \epsilon_0=\epsilon_1=+,\quad \epsilon_2=\epsilon_3=-.
\]
Call this source $F_k$. It has $n=3k$, $m=4k$, is proper and connected, and every variable has two positive and two negative occurrences. Its source-known witness $A_i=1$, $B_i=C_i=0$ satisfies all clauses. This family and its easy signed-isomorphism orbit were already constructed in the project's ARC-LINEAR22-SATURATION1: we do \emph{not} claim invention of the family.

\begin{theorem}[Exact large backdoors on a simple SAT family]
For all odd $k\ge7$,
\[
 \boxed{\beta_{\rm Krom}(F_k)=k,\quad
 \beta_{\rm Horn}(F_k)=\beta_{\rm dualHorn}(F_k)=2k.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding corollary gives $\beta_{\rm Krom}\ge k$. Selecting all $k$ variables $A_i$ hits each of the four clauses indexed by $i$ and is a strong Krom backdoor, proving equality. There are exactly $2k$ all-positive source clauses. Every one needs at least two of its three variables selected for a strong Horn backdoor. Each variable appears in precisely two of these positive clauses, so $2|B|\ge4k$. Selecting all $A_i$ and all $B_i$ supplies two selected positive variables in every positive clause and gives a strong Horn backdoor of size $2k$. The same count on the $2k$ all-negative clauses proves the dual-Horn equality, with the same chosen variable types.
\end{proof}

A source-ordered grammar validator can check the canonical $F_k$ and emit the authenticated witness with $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ auxiliary space under a declared indexed input grammar, or emit one constant-size SAT child; outside the grammar it must fall back. The earlier repository's colored bounded-degree graph model plus Luks's polynomial-time bounded-degree graph-isomorphism theorem [3] gives a \emph{polynomial-time} signed-isomorphism-orbit escape, but no small FLMV writable-space bound is inferred for that general recognizer. Thus large \emph{static strong-backdoor size} is not an arbitrary-algorithm lower bound or a statement about adaptive backdoor depth.

\section{ARC multiplicity and explicit-diagnostic-space gates}
An architecture that first chooses a fixed strong Krom backdoor $B$ then emits one ordinary cofactor for \emph{every} $\tau\in\{0,1\}^{B}$ without merging or certified pruning emits at least $2^{n/3}$ children on a strict22 source, and $2^{2k}$ on $F_k$ for a Horn/dual-Horn backdoor. With $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ under a standard indexed clause-list encoding, this is superpolynomial in $N$ and violates the proposed ARC multiplicity $N^{3/5+o(1)}$. It is a conditional architecture-specific obstacle, not a lower bound against adaptive partial branching, early witnesses, algebraic solvers, or cached certificates.

For $m$ original clauses a \emph{literal explicit} retained four-bit compatibility record for each unordered clause pair consumes exactly $4\binom m2=2m(m-1)$ bits, or $\Theta(N^2/\log^2N)$ on strict22. This is incompatible with an $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ live workspace if all records are simultaneously retained. The matrix can instead be streamed, compressed, recomputed or relegated to an offline verification artifact; none of these escape methods is free in a native machine proof. A two-bit true-literal-count vector separately costs $2m$ bits. The current raw experiment deliberately retains full diagnostic arrays and therefore is \emph{not} an ARC space realization.

The actual FLMV exclusion is a simultaneous time-space statement for the same fixed uniform deterministic ordinary-SAT machine [1]. The unconstructed proposed recurrence remains
