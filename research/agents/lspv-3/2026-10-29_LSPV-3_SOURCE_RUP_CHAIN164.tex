\begin{theorem}[Linear source-Resolution proofs for a bounded-block chain]\label{thm:main}
Fix $d\ge1$. Consider any explicit CNF $F$ with variables partitioned into $k$ blocks, each of at most $d$ variables, such that every clause involves variables from at most two \emph{consecutive} blocks. Additional unit restrictions on arbitrary variables are permitted. There is a single deterministic uniform algorithm that decides both SAT and UNSAT on this class. On SAT it constructs an actual model; on UNSAT it constructs an original-clause-ID-authenticated Resolution refutation (hence a two-parent RUP/LRAT-style addition trace) of at most $O(k3^{2d})$ derived clauses. Its acquisition and verification times and peak space are polynomial in the complete explicitly encoded input size. No boundary oracle or SAT/UNSAT side bit is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Process blocks from left to right. Within the current block choose a variable $v$ by any deterministic effectively computable rule, such as the charged minimization of $|\mathrm{Occ}(v)|\,|\mathrm{Occ}(\neg v)|$. For every active clause $A\lor v$ and $B\lor\neg v$ generate the non-tautological resolvent $A\lor B$, with parent IDs and pivot $v$ recorded. Retain each distinct resolvent once in the active database, but keep every prior source and derived clause in the permanent verification log. Delete active clauses containing $v$ and repeat. An empty resolvent yields an authenticated UNSAT certificate. Otherwise elimination of all variables leaves an empty active formula and therefore SAT. Reverse the elimination order. For each removed variable, the saved discarded bucket must admit a choice satisfying all its clauses given assignments to later-eliminated variables, or a falsified resolvent would contradict the retained projected formula. Thus model reconstruction succeeds and every original clause is rechecked.

The locality invariant is crucial. At the beginning of block $i$, every active clause involving a variable of this block refers only to blocks $i$ and $i+1$. This holds for $i=1$ by assumption. Eliminating all block-$i$ variables creates clauses only on block $i+1$; clauses initially connecting $i+1$ to $i+2$ are untouched. The induction follows. During any block, every newly derived clause uses at most $2d$ names. There are at most $3^{2d}$ distinct non-tautological clauses on those names (each variable occurs positively, negatively, or not at all), including the empty clause. At most $15$ eliminations per block for the explicit $d=15$ chain, and in general at most $d$; hence at most $O(kd3^{2d})$ derived lines. Parent and source IDs require $O(\log(N+1))$ bits each. A two-parent resolvent is a RUP addition: under the negation of the conclusion, its positive and negative parents unit-propagate opposite values of the pivot. An independent checker can validate that fact from the original indexed clauses and all prior additions. Since $d$ is fixed, charging repeated scans, adaptive order selection, candidate pairs including tautologies, source reads, proof writes, model reconstruction and peak storage is polynomial.\qedhere
\end{proof}
