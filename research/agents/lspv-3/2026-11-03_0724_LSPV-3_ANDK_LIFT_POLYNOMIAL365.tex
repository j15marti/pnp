\paragraph{Input construction.}
Let $G$ be a 2-vertex-connected simple cubic graph, with even $h\ge4$ vertices and $3h/2$ edges. Fix vertex charges $s_v$ with precisely one charge one and all others zero. Each edge $e$ has $k\ge2$ different original input bits $z_{e,1},\ldots,z_{e,k}$ and conceptual output $g_e=\bigwedge_{j=1}^k z_{e,j}$. At each vertex $v$ with incident edges $e_1,e_2,e_3$, for every output tuple $a\in\{0,1\}^3$ with $\bigoplus_i a_i\ne s_v$, and every index choice $\mathbf j=(j_i:a_i=0)$, place the authenticated source clause
\[
S_{v,a,\mathbf j}=
\bigvee_{i:a_i=0}z_{e_i,j_i}
\ \vee\
\bigvee_{i:a_i=1}\bigvee_{t=1}^k \neg z_{e_i,t}.
\tag{1}
\]
The conjunction of all these clauses is exactly the parity equation on the three conceptual edge outputs. There are $n=3hk/2$ original variables and
\[
m(h,k)=k^3+3k+(h-1)(3k^2+1)
\tag{2}
\]
different original source clauses. The full DIMACS signed-literal input length is $N=O(hk^4\log(hk+2))$ bits and $N=\Omega(m)$, not the variable count $n$.

\begin{lemma}[Polynomial source-to-output extraction]
Inside the unchanged signed-$ER_2$ fragment, each edge needs exactly $k-1$ new paid definitions and at most $(k-2)(k+3)/2$ additional shared strict-Resolution nodes to derive
\[
(\neg g_e\vee z_{e,j})\quad(j=1,\ldots,k),
\qquad (g_e\vee\neg z_{e,1}\vee\cdots\vee\neg z_{e,k}).
\]
Here $g_e$ denotes the freshly defined final output wire. For each forbidden vertex tuple $a$ having $p$ zero entries, its $k^p$ original signed source clauses derive the canonical three-output blocker
\[
B_{v,a}=\bigvee_{i:a_i=0}g_{e_i}\ \vee\ \bigvee_{i:a_i=1}\neg g_{e_i}
\]
in at most $(3-p)k^{p+1}+\sum_{r=1}^{p}k^r$ further strict-Resolution nodes. Thus four blockers at a charge-one vertex cost at most $k^3+7k^2+4k$, and four at a charge-zero vertex cost at most $3k^3+3k^2+6k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Define the left-deep, positive signed-AND chain $t_2=z_1\wedge z_2$, $t_j=t_{j-1}\wedge z_j$ for $j\ge3$. Resolve its authenticated gate slots along the chain to obtain each $(\neg t_k\vee z_j)$ and one $(t_k\vee\bigvee_j\neg z_j)$; summing the reused intermediate pivots gives $(k-2)(k+3)/2$. For each clause (1), resolve its $(3-p)k$ negative input literals from output-one groups against the output-to-input implications, retaining the selected positive literals in the zero groups. After all $k^p$ indexed clauses are converted, eliminate the zero-group indices one group at a time. Each group uses the authenticated wide AND clause and $k$ genuine signed pivots per fixed tuple of remaining indices. The elimination counts are $k^p,k^{p-1},\ldots,k$, yielding the stated bound. Every required derived blocker is an actual clause, not an assumed semantic factor or an unauthenticated oracle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Minimally UNSAT and full-mixed floor]
For every such $G,k$, the original source $F_{G,k}$ is UNSAT; deleting any one source clause makes it SAT; flipping the polarity of any one literal occurrence makes it SAT. Consequently, for the unchanged combined calculus with only at-most-two-premise paid inference nodes,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_{G,k})\ge m(h,k)-1.}
\tag{3}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every source-row equation enforces $\bigoplus_{e\ni v}g_e=s_v$, while summing over all graph vertices gives the contradiction $0=1$. For any one source $S_{v,a,\mathbf j}$, pin the three incident outputs to the forbidden tuple $a$, and satisfy all other vertex parity equations by assigning the remaining edge outputs. Such an assignment exists because $G-v$ is connected and the residual vertex syndromes sum to zero: $\bigoplus_{u\ne v}s_u=\bigoplus_i a_i$. On every output-one edge set all $k$ inputs to one. On every output-zero edge set precisely one input to zero, choosing the indexed position specified by $\mathbf j$ for zero edges incident to $v$. Exactly the targeted clause is falsified and all other sources are true. The same assignment repairs each separate one-literal polarity reversal. Hence every source is indispensable in every sound contradiction ancestry; connectedness and at-most-two paid premises give the lower bound $m-1$, regardless of sharing, extensions, algebraic reasoning, temporary growth or adaptive order. This is only the linear source-floor calibration, not a superpolynomial separation.
\end{proof}
