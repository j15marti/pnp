\section{Original source-indexed extreme modes}
For original signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$ with negative sign bit $\nu_{ij}$, local $S_j$ means that all three literals of original $C_j$ are TRUE and requires $a_i=1\oplus\nu_{ij}$; local $U_j$ means all three FALSE and requires $a_i=\nu_{ij}$. Complementing these demands swaps $S_j$ and $U_j$. The shared original $x_i$ is never an independent occurrence variable. For distinct source clauses $C_j,C_h$, SS and UU are compatible exactly when no shared variable has opposite sign; SU and US are compatible exactly when no shared variable has the same sign. \section{Certified exclusion of two coherent endpoint neighborhoods}
\begin{theorem}[Disjoint falsified-clause packing]
Let $F$ be any CNF and $a\in\{0,1\}^n$ a full coherent source assignment. If $q$ original clauses indexed $j_1,\ldots,j_q$ are falsified at $a$ and have pairwise disjoint original-variable supports, every satisfying $b$ satisfies $d_H(a,b)\ge q$. The source indices, occurrence signs, falsity and disjoint supports give a polynomially verifiable certificate excluding every full assignment in $B_{q-1}(a)$. If a packing of size $q'$ is similarly found at $\bar a$ and $q+q'-2<n$, both excluded endpoint balls are disjoint and their combined invalid full-assignment count is
\[
 \sum_{h=0}^{q-1}\binom nh+\sum_{h=0}^{q'-1}\binom nh.
\]
The result does not assert that these are ordinary unqualified DPLL partial cubes or that $F$ is UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each original clause false at $a$ requires at least one changed source variable to become true at $b$. Disjoint supports force at least $q$ different flips. Ball cardinalities and disjointness follow from elementary Hamming distances, with $d_H(a,\bar a)=n$.
\end{proof}
The theorem holds under simultaneous polarity renaming of source formula and reference assignment. The implemented greedy source-disjoint packing is a sound lower bound on necessary flips; no efficient maximum-packing oracle is assumed.
\section{Connected proper width-three SAT/UNSAT extreme control}
For $k\ge2$, use variables $a_i,b_i,c_i$ and the connected proper 3CNF
\[
F_k=\bigwedge_{i=1}^k[(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)\wedge(\neg a_i\vee\neg b_i\vee\neg c_i)]
\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k-1}(a_i\vee a_{i+1}\vee b_{i+1}).
\]
All $k$ positive ternary NAE members are disjoint and false at $0^{3k}$; all $k$ negative members are disjoint and false at $1^{3k}$. Thus both balls of radius $k-1$ contain no satisfying assignment. Yet $F_k$ is SAT: set $a_i=1,b_i=c_i=0$ for every block; the full complement is also a model, because bridge literals $b_{i+1}$ become true. Both distance bounds are sharp. For each positive/negative triple pair, modes SS and UU are infeasible while SU and US are feasible; in the satisfying middle states BOTH clauses are ordinarily SAT without being all-literals TRUE.
Define $G_k$ by appending the six missing ternary sign patterns on $(a_1,b_1,c_1)$. The resulting eight clauses cover all assignments to these three original variables: each assignment falsifies a source-indexed clause. This gives a complete eight-leaf UNSAT certificate, while the two endpoint packings remain. Both sources are connected, but \emph{neither is strict signed exact-(2,2)}.
There are exactly $2\sum_{h<k}\binom{3k}h=2^{3kH_2(1/3)-O(\log k)}$ invalid complete assignments in the two disjoint excluded balls, where $H_2(1/3)\approx0.918296$. Scanning all candidates through these radii is exponential and never finds a witness; recognizing the $2k$ source clauses yields polynomially short exclusion proofs. This is only a restricted comparison between specified procedures.
A genuine original-variable impossible partial cube comes from resolving indexed parents $(\neg a_1\vee\neg b_1\vee\neg c_1)$ and $(a_1\vee a_2\vee b_2)$ on $a_1$: the non-tautological resolvent $(\neg b_1\vee\neg c_1\vee a_2\vee b_2)$ prohibits $b_1=c_1=1,a_2=b_2=0$. This proof is not merely an extreme-mode incompatibility.
