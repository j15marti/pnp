## ITERATE: Exact quantitative restricted theorem

**Declared separate calculus R_CAST507(w).** Fix w a positive integer independently of formulas. SOURCE leaves are authenticated original CNF clauses, free. RES combines two SOURCE/RES clause records on a complementary pivot (cost 1). AND3 combines two SOURCE/AND3/CAST factor records only if the union of their ORIGINAL-variable supports is <=3, with exact local truth-table check (cost 1). An exact affine factor may carry a GF(2) row. XOR combines two existing affine rows (cost 1). A unary CAST from a previously RES-derived clause with <=3 original variables to its IDENTICAL local Boolean factor is charged w. This is sound, complete (via unrestricted ordinary Resolution), fixed, effective and source-explicit. There are NO paid or unpaid DEF facets, LOCAL-CONVERT, private/exported wire tricks, branch/discharge, weakening, or other cross-type transitions. It is NOT the complete historical R_mix341.

Let S_Res(H_h) denote the minimum number of ordinary Resolution inferences deriving the empty clause from the EXACT original gadget source. Then:

$$
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{CAST507}}(w)}(H_h)
=\min\{S_{\mathrm{Res}}(H_h),\,4h+1+w\}.}
$$

**Proof:** (1) All m=4h+2 original source clauses are individually indispensable: deletion of each is SAT, by the signed Tseitin and strict-3 gadget witnesses [P2-L3-503/504]. Hence every sound refutation's source ancestry contains all m. Let b be the count of binary inference nodes and u the count of unary CAST nodes in the pruned root ancestry. Connectedness gives at least m+b+u-1 premise edges; binary/unary arity permits at most 2b+u. Therefore b>=m-1=4h+1, regardless of reconvergence, shared lemmas, or proof-search ordering. Thus any CAST-bearing proof costs >=4h+1+w. (2) Without a CAST, clause RES objects and source-bound factor AND3/GF(2) objects cannot interact. The exact-support no-refutation result [P2-L3-505] eliminates a factor-only contradiction on H_h, so a no-CAST refutation must use an ordinary RES contradiction, costing >=S_Res(H_h). (3) Both bounds are achievable: an optimal ordinary RES proof, or 2 RES steps recovering original C, a single CAST C, 3h AND3 for the old vertex parity equations, and h-1 XOR additions reaching 0=1. Binary inference count is 2+3h+h-1=4h+1, CAST count 1. The paths use all original source clauses. This proves equality over all valid shared DAGs in this declared calculus, not just over exhibited certificates.

For the published expanding family S_Res(H_h)>=2^{Omega(h)}, the constant-fee construction is eventually optimal: W=4h+1+w for every fixed w>=1 and sufficiently large h. Merely charging a fixed positive amount for RES-to-factor conversion does not preserve an exponential ordinary-Resolution lower bound. This result is about model dependence of shortest legal proof DAG work; it is not a historical R_mix341 theorem, an all-algorithm runtime lower bound, or a proof of B3.
