## Exact support theorem

$$
R_p(b_1,\ldots,b_d,y)\ \Longleftrightarrow\
y=\bigwedge_{j=1}^d[b_j=p_j].
$$

Every one of d original coordinates is essential: set b=p and y=1 to satisfy R_p, then toggle any one bit while retaining the others and y=1. R_p becomes false. Therefore for d>=4 there is no exact equal relation supported on <=3 original coordinates.
