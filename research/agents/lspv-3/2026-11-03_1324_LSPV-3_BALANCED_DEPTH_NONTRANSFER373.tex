\section*{Established theorem, model, and signed encoding}
Urquhart (1987) and Ben-Sasson--Wigderson (2001) imply an infinite family of connected, simple, bridgeless cubic expanding graphs \(G_h\), with \(h\) vertices and odd charges \(\chi\), for which EVERY unrestricted DAG Resolution refutation of their ordinary signed Tseitin 3CNFs requires at least \(\exp(c h)\) lines for some \(c>0\). This does not apply automatically to richer proof systems. Each vertex has four local 3-clauses, exactly the four assignments with wrong parity, so \(m=4h\), \(n=3h/2\) edge variables, and bit-encoded ASCII DIMACS length \(N_h=\Theta(h\log h)\). Every source clause is indispensable because deleting it allows its unique forbidden local assignment, and the other vertex equations are consistent on connected \(G_h-v\). The corresponding even-charge formula is SAT with \(2^{n-h+1}\) models.

Fix the PREEXISTING \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) with zero-cost original sources, and unit-cost at-most-binary-premise strict signed RES, exact 3-original-variable AND3 factors, GF(2) binary row XOR, justified bounded LOCAL-CONVERT, and conservative paid acyclic two-input DEF. Each distinct shared-DAG node costs one. The binary-premise source-ancestry bound applies to all legal adaptive derivations, auxiliary definitions, representation switches, sharing and temporary growth. Completeness of RES and integer weights ensure an attained minimum \(W_R(F)\).

Define \(D_R(F)\) as the minimum, among all legal refutation DAGs, of the maximum number of paid derived nodes on a source-to-false path, source depth zero. This is LOGICAL DAG DEPTH, not machine time or peak space.

\begin{theorem}[Exact joint minimum]
For every connected simple bridgeless cubic \(G\) on \(h\) vertices with odd charges,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_{G,\chi})=4h-1,\qquad
D_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_{G,\chi})=\lceil\log_2(4h)\rceil.}
\]
One and the SAME valid proof attains both minima.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All \(m=4h\) sources are essential and every charged node has at most two parents, so the connected ancestor-DAG edge count forces at least \(m-1\) charged nodes (LSPV-2 341). At depth \(d\) the unfolded binary predecessor tree has at most \(2^d\) source leaves; hence \(d\ge\lceil\log_2 m\rceil\), even with DAG sharing. At each vertex pair its four source clauses into two exact AND3 factors at depth one, then conjoin at depth two. The eight-row result is its parity equation, with explicit truth-table verification, costing \(3h\) nodes. Combine these \(h\) rows by a BALANCED pairwise GF(2) XOR tree. Each edge variable cancels twice and odd total charge yields \(0=1\); there are \(h-1\) further nodes, with additional depth \(\lceil\log_2 h\rceil\). The resulting cost \(4h-1\) and depth \(2+\lceil\log_2 h\rceil=\lceil\log_2(4h)\rceil\) meet both lower bounds. No semantic oracle or instance-specific axiom was used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Size-and-depth source-exact nontransfer]
For an expanding infinite subfamily \(F_h\), the unrestricted DAG Resolution proof size is \(\exp(\Omega(h))\), while the full fixed \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) has proofs of size \(4h-1\) and depth \(\lceil\log_2(4h)\rceil\). Further, EVERY ordinary Resolution refutation has depth \(\Omega(h)\). Thus a source-preserving converter applicable to all mixed proofs cannot output ordinary Resolution proofs with size bounded by ANY fixed polynomial in mixed cost \(q\), mixed depth \(D\) and input bits \(N_h\); nor can it have output depth bounded by a fixed polynomial in \(D\) and \(\log N_h\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first statements combine the fixed-calculus construction with published ordinary Resolution lower bounds. For any binary-premise rooted DAG with depth \(d\), its connected ancestry has at most \(2^{d+1}-1\) total nodes. Resolution size at least \(\exp(ch)\) therefore forces \(d=\Omega(h)\). Since \(q=\Theta(h)\), \(D=O(\log h)\), and \(N_h=\Theta(h\log h)\), a fixed polynomial in \((q,D,N_h)\) is polynomial in \(h\), contradicting exponential ordinary Resolution size; a fixed polynomial in \((D,\log N_h)\) is polylogarithmic in \(h\), contradicting the ordinary Resolution depth floor.
\end{proof}
