\section*{Strict charged-source transport}
Fix any encoded UNSAT CNF $F(X)$, and any list $D$ of $k$ acyclic, conservative, signed-AND2 gates with output IDs $Y$ disjoint from $X$, inputs in $X$ or earlier $Y$. Write $\Gamma(D)$ for the union of the three signed defining clauses per gate, and $G=F\land\Gamma(D)$ as a new ordinary encoded CNF. Let $W_E(H)$ be the attained minimum number of distinct charged E inference nodes in a shared DAG refutation of $H$ (original H clauses are zero cost). Resolution completeness and positive integer weights ensure attainment.

\begin{lemma}[exact E source-extension sandwich]
For every such $F,D$,
\[
\boxed{W_E(G)\le W_E(F)\le W_E(G)+k.}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the first inequality, replay an E proof of F using its original F leaves inside G, with proof-local freshly defined variables consistently alpha-renamed away from Y. For the second, take an arbitrary E proof of G of q charged nodes. On F, buy each gate of D once in topological order, paying k nodes. Every originally free $\Gamma(D)$ leaf of the G proof is now an authenticated view of the paid matching gate. Preserve original F source leaves; rename proof-local fresh definitions away from X and Y; replay each signed RES or DEF inference in its original topological order. Every derived clause, parent reference and signed pivot is identical up to renaming, and the output is still false. The E fragment has no original-variable support guard for RES or DEF. Every shared inference node is replayed once, so the new cost is no greater than q+k. Minimize over q. No semantic oracle or unrestricted free definition is invoked.
\end{proof}

\section*{Constructive non-tautological guard repair}
Take original variables $X=\{a,b,c,d,e,f\}$ and pay $u=a\land b$, $v=u\land c$, $y=v\land d$, three sequential fresh conservative definitions. Consider two \emph{already authenticated local premise clauses}
\[
P=(y\lor e\lor f),\qquad Q=(\neg y\lor e\lor f).
\]
A fresh source parse using y as original has lexical support $\{y,e,f\}$, of size three. The fully typed base support after paid definitions is $\{a,b,c,d,e,f\}$, of size six. Hence verbatim replay of an AND3 step guarded by at most three \emph{original} variables can fail. Nonetheless \emph{one strict RES inference on pivot y} gives the non-tautological $e\lor f$, bypassing that support guard. This repairs that local factor, not an entire arbitrary full-R proof; $P,Q$ are not asserted to be free F sources. Dropping Q invalidates the consequence: $y=1,e=f=0$ satisfies P but falsifies $e\lor f$. The three gates are conservative and their nine defining clause views are paid; no magical unit-cost inference is introduced.
