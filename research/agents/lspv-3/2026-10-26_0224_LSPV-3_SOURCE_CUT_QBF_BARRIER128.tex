\section{Load-bearing target and four falsifiable mechanisms}
Fix an ordinary signed proper-3CNF source $F$ with named original variables and clauses and a proposed derived ordinary SAT child $H$. The previous SOURCE-CUT-ENTROPY127 formalized the semantic model-lifting contract (SC), but explicitly did not establish an affordable checker for it. The present target is whether generic checks for that contract may be inserted as a free leaf-verification rule in an adaptive UNSAT grammar. All source/child bits, identities, headers, control, policy discovery, retries, proof parsing, guest input probes, writes and simultaneously live workspace remain charged. Fix one uniform deterministic total exact ordinary-SAT decider $A$ for all conditional leaf calls.

Four falsifiable hypotheses were fixed before the primary-literature search:
\begin{description}
\item[] H1. Generic verification of (SC) remains difficult even when the original source is a satisfiable, disjoint union of constant-size exact-signed-$(2,2)$ proper-3CNF gadgets with every universal boundary word feasible. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT: $\Pi_2^P$-complete.}
\item[] H2. Retaining every original source variable name in an actual proper-3CNF child and adding a second genuine descendant eliminates this hardness. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: neutral three-literal padding preserves the quantified obstruction at two real levels.
\item[] H3. A fixed contradiction child gives a cheaper universal certificate even when its claimed lift has no supplied derivation. \textbf{FALSIFIED as an inference}: authorizing its (SC) map is coNP-complete on arbitrary exact-$(2,2)$ sources; a trusted unverified map would accept SAT inputs.
\item[] H4. Pairwise boundary satisfiability suffices to compose cyclic adaptive modules. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: three compatible pairs of proper-3CNF affine/equality modules have an UNSAT global join.
\end{description}

\section{Precisely typed source-cut decision predicate}
Let $X=\mathrm{Var}(F)$, $Z=\mathrm{Var}(H)$ and $B=X\cap Z$. Names in $B$ must denote literally the same Boolean variables; all other names must be fresh as specified. For an original-variable partial assignment $\sigma$, write
\[
\Lift(F,H,B,\sigma)\Longleftrightarrow
\forall\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{X}
\left[\alpha\models F\land\sigma\Rightarrow
\exists\beta\in\{0,1\}^{Z}
\big(\beta\models H\land \beta|_B=\alpha|_B\big)\right].\tag{SC}
\]
A checked true (SC) together with $H\in\UNSAT$ implies $F\land\sigma\in\UNSAT$; a declaration of (SC) without independently verified proof is not a certificate. A child may be an ordinary rewritten formula, not necessarily a literal source subformula. Exact ordinary encoding means three distinct names per clause, correct clause/variable header, consistent original aliases, and separately counted source access, materialization or virtual probes.

\begin{theorem}[Quantified source-cut barrier]\label{th:pi2}
The decision language consisting of properly encoded pairs $(F,H)$ satisfying $\Lift(F,H,X,\varnothing)$ is $\Pi_2^P$-complete under polynomial-time many-one reductions. Hardness persists under the simultaneous restrictions that $F$ is satisfiable, every variable of $F$ occurs exactly twice positively and twice negatively in distinct proper signed three-clauses, $F$ is a disjoint union of four-clause three-variable gadgets, and every original source name occurs literally in $H$. Both $F$ and $H$ are ordinary proper signed three-CNF. The same hardness persists when a second actual child $H^{+}$ is required, with a universally valid audited lift $H\to H^{+}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Membership follows directly from the quantifier prefix: failure of (SC) is an existential assignment satisfying $F$ with a universally quantified obstruction to every matching model of $H$. Evaluation of each polynomial-size Boolean matrix is deterministic polynomial time, so the complement lies in $\Sigma_2^P$.

For hardness start with an arbitrary $\Pi_2^P$-complete quantified strict proper-3CNF instance
\[
Q(\Psi)=\forall y_1\cdots\forall y_q\;\exists z_1\cdots\exists z_e\;\Psi(Y,Z).
\]
The source problem remains $\Pi_2^P$-complete even under signed exact-$(2,2)$ occurrence restrictions on the quantified matrix by D\"ocker--Dorn--Linz--Semple \cite{DDSL}; only strict proper 3CNF is necessary here.
For each universal variable $y_i$, introduce original source names $a_i,b_i$ and the four proper clauses
\[
G_i=(y_i\vee a_i\vee b_i)\wedge(y_i\vee\neg a_i\vee\neg b_i)
\wedge(\neg y_i\vee a_i\vee\neg b_i)
\wedge(\neg y_i\vee\neg a_i\vee b_i).
\]
Each of $y_i,a_i,b_i$ occurs exactly twice with each sign. For $y_i=0$, the two active clauses enforce $a_i\ne b_i$, while for $y_i=1$ they enforce $a_i=b_i$. Hence $\exists a_i,b_i\,G_i$ is TRUE for each of the two $y_i$ values. Let $F_Q=\bigwedge_{i=1}^qG_i$; it is an explicitly satisfiable, linearly recognizable, disconnected exact-$(2,2)$ source of $3q$ names and $4q$ clauses, with full projected relation $\{0,1\}^q$ on $Y$.

To enforce the strong source-identity condition, for every original source name $x\in\mathrm{Var}(F_Q)$ introduce fresh child-private $p_x,r_x$ and add
\[
T(x)=(x\vee p_x\vee r_x)\wedge(\neg x\vee p_x\vee r_x).
\]
Every clause is proper ternary, every original name now occurs in the child, and for both values of $x$ the choice $p_x=1$ satisfies $T(x)$. Set
\[
H_Q=\Psi(Y,Z)\land\bigwedge_{x\in\mathrm{Var}(F_Q)}T(x).
\]
All names outside the common original $3q$ names are fresh, and $\Psi$ does not depend on the original gadget-private names $a_i,b_i$. Consequently
\[
\Lift(F_Q,H_Q,\mathrm{Var}(F_Q),\varnothing)
\iff\forall Y\exists Z\,\Psi(Y,Z),
\]
since every $Y$ has a source witness and every source model extends through all neutral pads precisely when its $Y$ has a $\Psi$-witness. The construction is polynomial, with $|\mathrm{Var}(H_Q)|=9q+e$, $|\mathrm{Clauses}(H_Q)|=|\mathrm{Clauses}(\Psi)|+6q$.

For a second genuine encoded level, introduce three fresh private variables $u,v,w$ and form $H_Q^{+}=H_Q\land(u\vee v\vee w)$. Every model of $H_Q$ extends by $u=1$ to a model of $H_Q^{+}$, so $H_Q\to H_Q^{+}$ has an explicit constant-size witness lift that preserves all inherited original names. By composition the root-to-second-level (SC) truth remains $Q(\Psi)$. Serialize with revised ordinary headers; $H_Q^+$ has three more variables and one more clause. Neither level is represented by a free SAT/UNSAT label. This establishes both hardness claims.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fixed false-child authorization is coNP-complete]\label{co:fixed}
Let $U(u,v,w)$ be all eight proper signed clauses on three fresh private variables, one clause falsified by each assignment; $U\in\UNSAT$. For any proper source $F$, let $H_U(F)=U\land\bigwedge_{x\in\mathrm{Var}(F)}T(x)$ using fresh pads. Then every original variable name survives and
\[
\boxed{\Lift(F,H_U(F),\mathrm{Var}(F),\varnothing)\iff F\in\UNSAT.}
\]
Checking this validity promise is coNP-complete already when $F$ ranges over proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ three-CNF formulas, by the established NP-completeness of that source class \cite{Docker2019}. This is a \emph{complexity classification of a proposed semantic checker}, not a new lower bound on the running time of an arbitrary SAT algorithm.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
There is no satisfying $\beta$ of $H_U(F)$ for any shared boundary, so the universal implication in (SC) holds exactly when there is no satisfying $\alpha$ of $F$. The exact-$(2,2)$ UNSAT source language is coNP-complete under complement of the published SAT completeness theorem; the construction retains all source names and has polynomial size.\end{proof}

\section{Two-level and cyclic compatibility hostile controls}
A finite $q=1$ control uses $\Psi(Y,Z)=y_1$ encoded by the four strict proper ternary clauses $(y_1\vee\pm z_1\vee\pm z_2)$. The source, first child and second child are all individually SAT, but $y_1=0$ is a feasible source assignment that has no matching child model, so root-to-child (SC) is FALSE. Their complete DIMACS byte lengths are $48\to103\to114$. A true quantified control uses a single proper clause, and the eight sign patterns over the same three names yield an outright UNSAT matrix. Thus SAT labels of $F,H,H^+$ do not determine (SC).

For cyclic joins use three original names $x,y,z$ and the three separate relations $R_{xy}:x=y$, $R_{yz}:y=z$, and $R_{xz}:x\ne z$. Encode each binary clause $(\ell_1\vee\ell_2)$ by
$ (\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee t)\land(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\neg t)$,
with one fresh private $t$ for that source clause. Each relation is an ordinary proper signed three-CNF. Each factor and each pairwise factor-join is SAT, and every shared single-variable projection contains both truth values. The three-factor join is UNSAT; its first two-factor descendant is SAT and its full second-level descendant is UNSAT. This is an exact cycle-consistency obstruction, not a SAT time lower bound. A join tree with full tuple agreement and the running-intersection property avoids this error; simply checking each edge relation independently does not.

Odd Tseitin on cubic $K_4$ (6 edge names, 16 clauses) and Petersen (15 edge names, 40 clauses) remain UNSAT under independent exhaustive finite checks, while XOR summation over $\mathbb F_2$ yields the short global affine contradiction. They prevent inference of general PC hardness from parity geometry. The project all-field graph-colouring PC/PCR construction is a separate restricted proof-system claim, not a mandatory operation of arbitrary deterministic $A$ \cite{PC22}. Horn UNSAT, 2SAT UNSAT, fixed source-name pads, the exact 15-variable 20-clause minimally UNSAT signed-$(2,2)$ witness $W_1$, and balanced-sign mutations were additional checks. All field identities and private-variable namespaces must be explicitly typed before an affine terminal or boundary merge is accepted.

\section{Adaptive certificate grammar, full quantifiers, and charged bounds}
A \emph{sound typed certificate} for input $F$ may use complete binary branching on original variables, authenticated original-clause contradiction, a checked resolution/CP/PC transcript, checked affine cancellation, a valid joint boundary join with actual common assignments, or a derived ordinary child $H$ accompanied by an independently verified source-cut (SC) witness and a genuine completed exact call to the ONE fixed decider $A(H)$. Every derivation lists original source dependencies and fresh child names. Any invalid proof, claimed semantic lift without a checkable derivation, malformed child, incorrect SAT call, or timed-out branch is rejected. Checking a supplied local explicit model-extension map can be inexpensive; Theorem~\ref{th:pi2} concerns deciding the \emph{arbitrary semantic truth} of the map, not validating a restricted syntactic construction.

\paragraph{Soundness.} Structural induction over a complete branch tree shows that an accepted certificate implies UNSAT of every associated original-source partial assignment and hence $F\in\UNSAT$. In particular, for every $F\in\SAT$, every alleged certificate is rejected, provided each terminal/join rule is really checked. Theorem~\ref{th:pi2} falsifies treating unrestricted (SC) assertions as cheap trusted axioms.

\paragraph{Completeness without a budget.} For every $F\in\UNSAT$, branch on all original variables and at each fully assigned leaf exhibit an original clause falsified by that assignment. This is a finite accepted certificate using real clause IDs, but may have $2^{\Theta(n)}$ leaves and verification length. It does not prove the missing polynomial-volume all-UNSAT coverage or any near-boundary resource bound. A coNTIME claim requires BOTH soundness and a bounded accepting path on every UNSAT input; clock invalid/timeout guesses to reject. Nondeterministic witness guesses do not provide free SAT/UNSAT labels or free source bits.

Let the genuine ordinary child input lengths at verifier leaves be $m_i$, $M=\max_i m_i$, $V=\sum_i m_i$, and assume $d\ge1$ for the same fixed $A$ with guest time $m_i^{d+o(1)}$. Pointwise $m_i^{d}\le m_i M^{d-1}$ gives the sharp elementary volume inequality
\[
\boxed{\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V.}\tag{VOL}
\]
If as an \emph{additional not-yet-established premise} every UNSAT $N$-bit source admits one accepting, fully clocked certificate with $M\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, $V\le N^{b+o(1)}$, all other bit work $N^{r+o(1)}$, and worst-case per-guest-probe overhead $N^{u+o(1)}$, then one may bound verifier time by
\[
c\le\max\{r,u+b+\lambda(d-1)\}+o(1),
\]
after separately paying $V$ materialization, headers, ancestor frames, source rereads, branch/strategy selection, switching, failed attempts, caches, bit indexing and the actual number of guest reads. Peak space is the maximum \emph{simultaneously live} index/writable space, not $V$, and requires its own inequality. For $d=901/500$, $\lambda=1/2$, $u=0$, the conditional two-algorithm frontier is
\[
C(d)=\frac{2d-1}{d(d-1)}=\frac{651000}{361301}
\approx1.8018217497322,\qquad
b<C(d)-\frac{d-1}{2}=\frac{506118299}{361301000}\approx1.4008217497322,
\]
with $r<C(d)$. This is only a CONDITIONAL sufficient resource budget subject to the precise published two-algorithm theorem and same-machine model audit \cite{VM}; $P=NP$ does not supply a $d=1.802$ seed, universal bounded certificates, or a restricted-map verifier. The $d$-parameter space bound must be jointly achieved by that same $A$; different solvers' time/space cannot be combined.
