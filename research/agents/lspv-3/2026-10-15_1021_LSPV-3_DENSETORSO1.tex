\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}

Define G_k^+ by adding a single edge joining two vertices of A. Arbitrary source relabeling is permitted; all operations are performed on the explicit source representation.

\begin{theorem}[High-width SAT family]
For every k>=2,
\[
|V(G_k)|=3k,\quad |E(G_k)|=3k^2,\quad
\delta(G_k)=\kappa(G_k)=\mathrm{tw}(G_k)=2k,\quad
\#\mathrm{3Col}(G_k)=6.
\]
No degree-two series reduction or parallel reduction exists. All 3k vertices and all 3k^2 nonpermutation inequality relations survive unpinned Boolean-interior projection. A fixed deterministic source program identifies arbitrary relabelings of this family and emits a verified coloring and the exact count in polynomial time and space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every vertex has 2k neighbors. Deleting fewer than 2k vertices leaves at least two nonempty parts, whose complete cross edges connect all remaining vertices. Deleting two entire parts leaves k>=2 isolated vertices, giving connectivity 2k. Standard connectivity <= treewidth proves the lower bound. For the upper bound use the path of bags A union B union {c}, one for each c in C; width 2k and valid running intersection. The three nonempty mutually complete parts must use three disjoint nonempty color sets, forcing exactly one color per part, with six permutations. Minimum degree >=4 and no parallel input edges defeat the DUALSEP reduction. Recognize the class by complement connected components, require exactly three components of equal size and verify within-component nonedges and all cross edges. Assign the components colors 0,1,2 and explicitly verify every source edge. All loops, source reads and output are polynomially bounded; outside-grammar inputs receive OUTSIDE, not a SAT claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
