## Theorem 468.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED)
No uniform source-preserving proof-to-proof translator can map every such declared-semantic mixed F_h refutation into an ordinary sparse-PC_K refutation of the *same original* F_h, char(K)!=2, with explicit output bits polynomial in source bits plus input proof bits. **Proof:** apply such a translator to the exhibited O(h^2 log h)-bit mixed proof. It would output a polynomial-bit PC_K proof; the published linear PC-degree + size-degree theorem requires exponentially many monomial occurrences. Contradiction. QED.

This is an **impossibility for that specific simulation direction**, not a superpolynomial lower bound for R_mix341. The existence of short GF(2) proofs is a decisive shortcut and cannot be excluded. A hypothetical target with extension circuits or a parity-native rule is outside the theorem. No step reasons from a hard restricted system to all-algorithm hardness.
