\section*{Fixed calculus and exact theorem}
Use general encoded CNF-SAT and the SAME previously fixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$: original clauses are authenticated free source leaves; every charged derived node costs one and has at most two named premises; permitted operations include sound binary Resolution, checked local AND3, GF$(2)$ addition, paid representation changes and source-independent charged conservative gate definitions. Shared proof DAG nodes count once, and best adaptive inference choice is free. Cost is distinct from proof encoding, search, verification and machine runtime.

For any clause-minimally UNSAT $F$ with $m$ source clauses, define $e_R(F)=W_R(F)-(m-1)$. A fresh split replaces a currently present clause $C$ by $(C\vee y)$ and $(C\vee\neg y)$, with $y$ new, disjoint from all input variables and from the original proof's auxiliary identifiers. Subsequent splits may choose any original or descendant clause.
\begin{theorem}[Heterogeneous splitting excess cap]
For every clause-minimally UNSAT $F$, every integer $t\ge0$, and every adaptive sequence of $t$ fresh splits $S_t(F)$,
\[
\boxed{S_t(F)(x,y)\equiv F(x),\quad |S_t(F)|_{\mathrm{clauses}}=m+t,\quad
m+t-1\le W_R(S_t(F))\le W_R(F)+t.}
\]
Every new source is indispensable and individually sign-sensitive. In particular,
\[
\boxed{0\le e_R(S_t(F))\le e_R(F).}
\]
The lower bound covers every legal mixed derivation, including shared DAGs, algebraic steps, conservative definitions and temporary growth; the upper translation does not require a mixed restriction compiler.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The identity $(C\vee y)\wedge(C\vee\neg y)\equiv C$ holds pointwise. If an untouched clause $D$ is deleted, an old deletion witness satisfies both new children since $C$ was true. If child $C\vee y$ is deleted, extend an old assignment falsifying only $C$ by $y=0$, and use $y=1$ for the other child. Induction gives minimal UNSAT. Under each deletion witness every literal of the omitted source is false, so every individual sign flip produces a SAT neighbor. Any valid refutation of $r=m+t$ indispensable source leaves with $q$ paid at-most-binary-premise nodes must contain all sources in its connected ancestry: $r+q-1\le E\le 2q$, hence $q\ge r-1$. For the upper direction, reverse the split history: one legal Resolution inference on each fresh pivot reconstructs each replaced parent, at exactly $t$ paid nodes. Graft the original optimal shared mixed refutation with its rule types and charged conservative definitions unchanged, renaming its auxiliaries. This gives $W_R(F)+t$ nodes. Subtract the source floor to obtain the excess inequality.
\end{proof}
