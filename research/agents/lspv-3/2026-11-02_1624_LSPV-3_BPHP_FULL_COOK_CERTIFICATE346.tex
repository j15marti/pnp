\section*{Original signed BPHP and essential sources}
For $h=2^\ell\ge4$ holes, encode $h+1$ pigeons by $\ell$ original bits each. For each unordered pigeon pair $i<j$ and hole $a$ put one width-$2\ell$ signed clause $D_{i,j,a}$, falsified exactly when both pigeon words equal $a$. Then
\[
v=(h+1)\ell,\quad m=h\binom{h+1}{2},\quad
N(h)=\Theta(h^3\ell^2)\mbox{ ASCII DIMACS bits}.
\]
Every original clause is essential: put the two named pigeons into its named hole and bijectively map the other $h-1$ pigeons to the remaining holes. The designated clause alone fails, so its deletion makes SAT. Flipping the sign of any one of its original $2\ell$ occurrences also makes SAT under the same assignment. Hence the source-ancestry graph, for any sound conservative at-most-two-parent inference DAG, proves $W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_h)\ge m-1$.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform polynomial mixed-calculus BPHP proofs]
For every $h=2^\ell\ge4$ a fixed deterministic compiler constructs a legal, fully source-authenticated RES/DEF refutation DAG satisfying
\[
\boxed{m-1\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_h)\le O(h^4+h^3\ell^2).}
\]
In particular, this signed, minimally UNSAT family is \emph{not} a superpolynomial minimum-hard family for the unchanged combined proof calculus. The upper is a constructive bound and not a claim of exact global optimum, of B3, or of unrestricted SAT decision-time hardness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each pigeon define a charged two-input AND gate $u_{i,s}$ for every matching binary prefix $s$ of length at least two. Length-one prefixes are already present positive/negative input-bit literals. There are $(h+1)(2h-4)=O(h^2)$ such DEF gates. Their authenticated defining clauses yield the prefix splitting implications $\neg u_{i,s}\vee u_{i,s0}\vee u_{i,s1}$ by one RES pivot per prefix. Initial positive/negative first-bit branches resolve on their actual source input bit, without inserting any free tautology. Subsequent resolution yields each unary totality $\bigvee_{a<h}u_{i,a}$ in $O(h)$ nodes. Derived prefix implications $u_{i,a}\Rightarrow[x_{i,b}=a_b]$ are resolved against each original $2\ell$-literal collision clause to obtain $\neg u_{i,a}\vee\neg u_{j,a}$; even without caching this takes at most $O(\ell^2)$ steps per original. We have therefore derived the ordinary unary PHP with $h+1$ pigeons and $h$ holes at cost $O(h^3\ell^2)$.

Now apply a uniformly effective Cook-style elimination of the last pigeon and last hole. In unary PHP with $k+1$ pigeons and $k$ holes, for $i<k$, $j<k-1$, introduce two paid conservative gates
\[
t_{i,j}=p_{i,k-1}\land p_{k,j},\qquad
q_{i,j}=p_{i,j}\lor t_{i,j}.
\]
Old pigeon totality, old collision with pigeon $k$ at the last hole, and gate slots derive every new totality $\bigvee_{j<k-1}q_{i,j}$ in $O(k)$ RES steps per $i$. Every new pair collision $\neg q_{i,j}\vee\neg q_{i',j}$ follows by the next nine-variable constant-domain RES projector. There are $O(k^3)$ such collisions and $O(k^2)$ new DEF nodes at a stage. Its number of RES nodes is $O(k^3)$ with a uniform fixed local derivation. Summing stages $k=2,\ldots,h$ costs $O(h^4)$, ending in a two-resolution contradiction for PHP(2,1). All nodes and pivots are derived effectively without a generalized SAT oracle. \end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Nine-variable collision projector]
For base variables $a,b,c,d,e$ introduce exactly four charged gate definitions
$t=b\land c$, $q=a\lor t$, $t'=e\land c$, $q'=d\lor t'$. Add old collisions
\[
(\neg a\vee\neg d)\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg c)
\wedge(\neg d\vee\neg c)\wedge(\neg b\vee\neg e).
\]
The existential projection of all these clauses onto $q,q'$ is exactly $\neg q\vee\neg q'$. A fixed complete Davis--Putnam elimination of the other seven variables can derive that clause using at most $3^9$ distinct non-tautological binary-resolution nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $q=q'=1$, either $a=1$ or $b=c=1$ and either $d=1$ or $e=c=1$. The four pair combinations respectively violate one of the four old collisions. The other boundary patterns are realized by setting all five base variables zero, only $a=1$, and only $d=1$. Exact elimination leaves a two-variable relation false only at $(1,1)$, forcing the clause $\neg q\vee\neg q'$ without units or the empty clause. Complete Davis--Putnam RES derives it, and there are at most $3^9$ distinct non-tautological clauses over nine variables. \end{proof}
