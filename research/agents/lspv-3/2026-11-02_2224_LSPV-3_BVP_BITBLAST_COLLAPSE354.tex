\[
\begin{array}{lll}
 A_i=(x_i\lor\neg c_i),&
 E_i=(\neg x_i\lor c_i),&
 D_i=(\neg c_{i+1}\lor x_i),\\
 J_i=(\neg c_{i+1}\lor c_i),&

\end{array}

The pair $A_i,E_i$ enforces the zero sum bit $x_i\oplus c_i=0$; $D_i,J_i,K_i$ encode $c_{i+1}=x_i\wedge c_i$. The unpinned full-adder circuit has a unique output/carry extension for every $x$. Hence this is a faithful, polynomial-size circuit-to-CNF encoding of the stated impossible integer equality, not an instance-tailored unsatisfiable axiom. \section*{Exact global minimum}
\begin{lemma}[Indispensable source clauses]\label{lem:essential}
Every correct sound proof of $B_n$ must cite $2n+2$ distinct source clauses:
$c_1$, $\neg c_{n+1}$, and $A_i,K_i$ for all $i$. Each is individually essential even when ALL the other $5n+1$ source clauses are present.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Deleting $c_1$ permits all $x_i=c_i=0$; deleting $\neg c_{n+1}$ permits all $x_i=c_i=1$. For each $i$, construct a carry discontinuity:
$c_j=1$ for $j\le i$ and $c_j=0$ for $j>i$; set $x_j=1$ for $j<i$ and $x_j=0$ for $j>i$. If $x_i=0$, the assignment falsifies ONLY $A_i$; if $x_i=1$, it falsifies ONLY $K_i$. For all $j\ne i$, zero-output sum and carry equations hold. At the discontinuity, all three remaining stage clauses $E_i,D_i,J_i$ hold, and exactly the designated one of $A_i,K_i$ fails. These are full deletion witnesses and, in particular, individual-sign-flip SAT controls for all essential clauses. Omitting any such source from a conservative refutation would leave its used sources simultaneously satisfiable, contradicting soundness.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Exact BVP carry-CNF mixed minimum]\label{thm:exact}
For every integer $n\ge1$, in the SAME fixed $R_{\rm mix341}$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(B_n)=2n+1=\Theta(n)=O(N(n)).}
\]
The optimum is taken over EVERY valid shared DAG, adaptive order, local algebraic rule, and charged conservative gate definition, with free selection of the best derivation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma \ref{lem:essential}, $r=2n+2$ indispensable source nodes all lie in the contradiction ancestry. For $q$ unit-cost derived nodes of at most two premises, the connected ancestry graph has at least $r+q-1$ dependency edges and at most $2q$, so $q\ge r-1=2n+1$ regardless of DAG sharing and rules.

For an explicit upper proof, resolve $A_i$ and $K_i$ on $x_i$ to obtain $(\neg c_i\lor c_{i+1})$. Starting from $c_1$, resolve this implication with the previously derived unit $c_i$ to obtain $c_{i+1}$. Exactly two RES nodes are used for each $i$, and a final RES of $c_{n+1}$ with source $\neg c_{n+1}$ yields the empty clause. This uses $2n+1$ legal inference nodes and no exotic rules, establishing attainment.
\end{proof}
