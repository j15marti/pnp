\section{Exact source-level theorem}
Let $F$ have $n$ active variables, $m=4n/3$ indexed proper width-three clauses, and two positive plus two negative occurrences per variable, in four different clauses. Deleting satisfied clauses and removing falsified literals defines raw $F|_\rho$, without unit propagation. Variables $x_{i,j}$ denote signed \emph{occurrences}, not independent variables. For $u\ne v$, write $q=q_{uv}$ for their number of common original clauses. For settings $u=b,v=c$, write $a_{bc}$ for common clauses where both literals are true and $b_{bc}$ for common clauses where both literals are false.
\begin{theorem}[Two-level cofactor spectrum]
For every original variable $v$ and either truth value, the residual has exactly $m-2$ original clauses: two binary, $m-4$ ternary, no units or empty clauses, and $3m-8$ literal occurrences. Every other variable retains at least two occurrences.
For every pair $u,v$ and each one of the four assignments:
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_{bc}&=4-a_{bc},&
M_{bc}&=m-4+a_{bc},\\
N_{1,bc}&=b_{bc},&
N_{2,bc}&=4-q+a_{bc}-b_{bc},\\
N_{3,bc}&=m-8+q,&
L_{bc}&=3m-16+q+2a_{bc}-b_{bc}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The sums over four settings are
$\sum M_{bc}=4(m-4)+q$,
$\sum N_{1,bc}=q$,
$\sum L_{bc}=12m-64+5q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A chosen variable has two distinct true-occurrence clauses, which are deleted, and two distinct false-occurrence clauses, which become binary. After two assignments, exactly four original true occurrences have been set, with $a_{bc}$ duplicate satisfactions of shared clauses; hence exactly $4-a_{bc}$ original clauses are deleted. Exactly $8-q$ clauses contain at least one assigned variable, so $m-8+q$ untouched clauses remain ternary. A surviving shared clause is unary exactly when both assigned literals are false, producing $b_{bc}$ units. Subtract units and ternaries from $m-4+a_{bc}$ to find the binary count, then sum literal widths. Each shared clause contributes to $a_{bc}$ once and to $b_{bc}$ once among the four settings, proving the sum formulas. These identities are independent of the source SAT label.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Guaranteed paid zero-overlap pair]

A source scan holding at most eight variable identifiers discovers $v\ne u$ with $q_{uv}=0$ in $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ native bit operations and $O(\log N)$ work bits, including candidate checks, if the dense indexed encoding has $N$ physical bits. For this pair all four raw two-step children have $m-4$ clauses, four binaries, $m-8$ ternaries, zero units and $3m-16$ literals.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Each of $u$'s four clauses has only two other names, leaving at least one nonneighbor among $n-1\ge9$ candidates. Scan every original literal and remember at most eight $O(\log N)$-bit names. The theorem with $q=a=b=0$ gives the residual signatures.
\end{proof}
