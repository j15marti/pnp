\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
For each $k\ge1$, let $D_k$ have spine vertices $u_0,\ldots,u_k$, branches $a_i,b_i$, and exactly the edges $u_0u_k$, $u_{i-1}a_i$, $a_iu_i$, $u_{i-1}b_i$, $b_iu_i$ for $1\le i\le k$. Then $n=3k+1$, $m=4k+1$. Every cycle without the closing edge is a local 4-cycle. Every cycle using that edge chooses one branch at each level, so there are exactly $2^k$ induced odd cycles. All $k+1$ spine vertices form a vertex cover, and the edges $u_{i-1}a_i$ and $u_kb_k$ give a $(k+1)$-edge matching; hence $\tau(D_k)=k+1$. The cycle surplus is $m-n+1=k+1$.

Let $z_v=1$ denote inclusion of vertex $v$ in the singleton third-color class. A full prescribed fiber lifts to a proper 3-coloring iff its selected set is independent and the remaining graph is bipartite. Put $\mathscr W_k=\prod_{i=1}^k\{a_i,b_i\}$ and
\[
 I_k=\bigwedge_{xy\in E(D_k)}(\neg z_x\vee\neg z_y),\quad
 O_k=\bigwedge_{w\in\mathscr W_k}
 \left(\bigvee_{i=0}^kz_{u_i}\vee\bigvee_{i=1}^kz_{w_i}\right).
\]
\begin{theorem}
The exact fiber predicate $G_k=I_k\land O_k$ has a circuit of $O(k)$ gates, yet EVERY equivalent CNF over only the original $z$ bits, with arbitrary clause signs and widths but no auxiliary variables, has at least $2^k$ clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The odd-cycle factor satisfies
\[
O_k=\left(\bigvee_{i=0}^kz_{u_i}\right)\vee
\left(\bigvee_{i=1}^k(z_{a_i}\wedge z_{b_i})\right).
\]
If a spine vertex is selected all odd cycles are hit; otherwise every branch route is hit iff at least one diamond has both inner vertices selected. For each route $w$, define bad input $x^w$ selecting exactly the opposite internal branch at every level and zero spine vertices. It is independent, but leaves route $w$ unhit. Any two different $x^w,x^{w'}$ have a bitwise OR that is independent and selects both branches in a differing diamond; their OR is therefore good. Falsification of any Boolean clause is closed under bitwise OR. Thus no clause can reject two different $x^w$. There are $2^k$ of them, proving the claimed lower bound. An $O(k)$ circuit follows from the displayed factorization, with only $O(k)$ independence edge clauses. Existentially extended Tseitin CNF also has $O(k)$ size.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}
There is a uniform deterministic algorithm that outputs a verified 3-coloring of every relabeling of $D_k$ in time $O(m(n+m))$ and space $O(n+m)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate edges $e=uv$; test whether $G-e$ is bipartite. On success use its two colors; if the endpoints of $e$ share one, recolor $u$ to a third color, then verify the original graph. For $D_k$ the closing edge is a successful trial. On other inputs without a successful trial the algorithm reports OUTSIDE, not a false NO answer. This yields one fixed polynomial partial witness program for the standard one-hot 3-coloring CNF, hence polynomial UWI $H(F(D_k))$. Direct counting by the diamond transfer matrix $3I+J$ gives $\#3COL(D_k)=2(6^k-3^k)$.
\end{proof}
