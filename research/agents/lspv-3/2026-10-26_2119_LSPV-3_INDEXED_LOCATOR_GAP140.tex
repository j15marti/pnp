\section{Signed regular equality modules, including every mismatch}
Let $W_1$ be the twenty-clause minimally UNSAT original proper signed 3CNF on fifteen names authenticated in BACKDOOR-SCC-CERT130. Its exact clause list (each semicolon separates one original ternary clause) is
\begin{verbatim}
-1 3 -14; 2 -8 9; 10 -13 14; 3 7 -13; -4 -9 -11;
5 8 -14; 4 -8 -11; 1 7 13; 1 -7 10; -5 -6 -15;
-2 -6 12; -4 9 -10; -2 5 -12; -3 6 -15; 6 -7 11;
2 -9 11; 4 8 -10; -3 12 15; -5 -12 15; -1 13 14
\end{verbatim}
Two opposite-signed occurrences of name $4$ lie in source zero-based rows $6,11$ at literal position zero; two of name $15$ lie in rows $9,17$ at position two. Flip both of the former signs if $u=0$, both of the latter if $v=0$, denoting the resulting module by $H(u,v)$. Every version remains proper exact signed-$(2,2)$ with identical clause variable supports. Direct exhaustive finite checking, reproduced independently, gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrr}
(u,v)&\#\mathrm{models}&\#(x_1=0)&\#(x_1=1)\\\hline
(0,0)&772&386&386\\
(0,1)&272&178&94\\
(1,0)&272&94&178\\
(1,1)&0&0&0
\end{array} \tag{1}
\]
Explicit $x_1=0$ positive-variable sets for the three satisfiable cases are respectively $\{4,6,7,10\}$, $\{4,6,7,10\}$, and $\{2,3,8,10,13\}$. The fixed $W_1$ UNSAT base fact also has the independently checkable five-variable decision/SCC proof described below; it is not an assumed free SAT label.

For $a,b\in\{0,1\}^m$, take two variable-disjoint translated modules per position:
\[
D_m(a,b)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}\bigl(H_i^U(a_i,1-b_i)\land H_i^V(1-a_i,b_i)\bigr).
\tag{2}
\]
Its $30m$ variables, $40m$ clauses and $120m$ literal occurrences satisfy exact two-positive/two-negative source occurrence quotas. A module is $W_1=H(1,1)$ iff the corresponding bit pair differs: $U_i$ for $(a_i,b_i)=(1,0)$, and $V_i$ for $(0,1)$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\SAT(D_m(a,b))\iff a=b.} \tag{3}
\]
Every UNSAT promise input, with one mismatch or $m$ mismatches, has a constant-clause source-local contradiction. This is \emph{complete coverage on the entire declared equality promise}, not on arbitrary ordinary SAT.

\section{A genuinely coupled connected control, not an exact-$(2,2)$ claim}
Write $x_j$ for original name $1$ in the $j$th of the $2m$ modules. Between each adjacent pair add the proper ternary equality compiler with new private $z_j,w_j$,
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{EQ}(x_j,x_{j+1})={}&
(\neg x_j\vee x_{j+1}\vee z_j)
\land(\neg x_j\vee x_{j+1}\vee\neg z_j)\\
&\land(x_j\vee\neg x_{j+1}\vee w_j)
\land(x_j\vee\neg x_{j+1}\vee\neg w_j).
\end{split}\tag{4}
\]
Existentially eliminating $z_j,w_j$ proves exactly $x_j=x_{j+1}$, not a tautological or always-satisfiable bridge under each boundary assignment. Let $G_m$ be (2) conjoined with all $2m-1$ bridges. Then
\[
n(G_m)=34m-2,\qquad c(G_m)=48m-4.\tag{5}
\]
Each module incidence graph is connected and the bridges connect all of them. By (1), every good local module has a satisfying witness with anchor $x_j=0$, so $a=b$ gives one globally consistent satisfying assignment. Any mismatch leaves an \emph{unchanged} source subcollection isomorphic to the original UNSAT $W_1$; extra equality clauses cannot restore its satisfiability. Hence
\[
\boxed{\SAT(G_m(a,b))\iff a=b}\quad\text{and}\quad
\operatorname{Incidence}(G_m)\ \text{is connected}.\tag{6}
\]
The coupling genuinely removes assignments: on equal inputs, adjacent good modules also each individually admit an anchor-$1$ witness, and an assignment with anchors $(0,1)$ satisfies their product but violates (4). These are nontrivial cross-module constraints. \textbf{Do not label $G_m$ exact signed-$(2,2)$}: the bridge changes the source anchor occurrence quotas. The separate signed-$(2,2)$ connected $R_k$ result of SOURCE-LOCAL-STREAM139 already refutes the weaker connectivity-to-large-proof inference inside the regular grammar.

\section{Exact certificate coverage, both labels, and proper descendants}
For each actual source $W_1$ module, branch on the five original names

Its terminal contains two implication paths $z\to\neg z\to z$ with every source edge and ancestor assignment checked. A finite source-independent fixed template uses exactly 31 decision nodes, 32 leaves and 600 implication-edge records. All original source names are shifted injectively, all source record ordinals are authenticated, and both children of every decision are included. Each pointer, literal name and offset uses $O(\log N)$ bits; thus the entire certificate occupies $O(\log N)$ bits because all counts are constant. The machine does not receive a free UNSAT bit.

Soundness holds on every \emph{well-formed ordinary SAT source}: if the source had a satisfying assignment, it would follow exactly one full branch and satisfy every cited original clause, contradicting the authenticated empty residual clause or SCC paths at that leaf. Completeness is established for every UNSAT source in (2) or (6), by selecting any mismatching module, and is \textbf{OPEN} for general ordinary UNSAT sources with a bound this small.

A verifier using a prevalidated fixed-width clause-record layout can check 20 named rows and the fixed finite proof with $O(\log N)$ bit input probes, $O(\log N)$ writable/index bits, and polylogarithmic bit work on an indexed random-access machine. If the verifier must authenticate the \emph{entire arbitrary source grammar and all unexamined records}, it scans/parses the whole input in $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}\,N)$ time; that separate obligation is never hidden. The proof still has logarithmic \emph{length}. Under an explicit uniform cap, malformed guesses and timeout paths reject; its nondeterministic existential polarity recognizes UNSAT on the promise and its branchwise complement recognizes SAT co-nondeterministically on that promise. Neither is an all-ordinary SAT coNTIME theorem.

For two genuine ordinary source-named descendant levels, set $F_0=G_m$, $F_1=F_0\land\mathsf{PIN}(\neg x_1)$ and $F_2=F_1\land\mathsf{PIN}(\neg x_2)$, where each pin has four proper ternary clauses and two fresh private names. \section{New deterministic RAM source-probe lower bound}
Use the canonical fixed-width binary clause-list encoding with one complete header and a record of three signed $w$-bit consecutive-name identifiers per clause, $w=\Theta(\log m)$. Order all Alice-controlled rows, all fixed rows, then all Bob-controlled rows, with internal module order fixed. This is an explicit ordinary signed-3CNF encoding with $N=\Theta(m\log m)$ \emph{bits}; the fixed-width grammar and full parser are charged. The source-sensitive fields for one index $i$ are at most four Alice signs and four Bob signs, while all clause identifiers, headers, equality bridges, and remaining rows are independent of $a,b$.

\begin{theorem}[Tight indexed source-query acquisition lower bound]\label{th:probe}
Fix either the balanced exact signed-$(2,2)$ family $D_m$ or the genuinely connected ordinary proper family $G_m$. Consider any ONE deterministic exact physical algorithm that, on every valid encoded promise input, correctly distinguishes SAT from UNSAT and on UNSAT emits an accepted source-authenticated certificate; the algorithm may be adaptive, may use arbitrary computations and memory, and may invoke a fixed total exact SAT decider $A$ \emph{only with all guest source-bit accesses charged}. On the SAT baseline $(a,b)=(0^m,0^m)$ it must make at least $m$ individual source-bit probes into mutually disjoint coordinate-sensitive positions. On some singleton-defect UNSAT input $(a,b)=(e_i,0^m)$ it must make at least $m$ such probes before safely accepting, in the bit-probe model. If each indexed read returns a contiguous word of at most $c\log N$ bits for fixed $c$, it still needs $\Omega(m)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ indexed reads in the worst case, with constants depending on $c$. These lower bounds concern input-query \emph{time}, not visited workspace or physical head crossings.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $X_0$ be the fully encoded equal baseline and $X_i$ the singleton-defect input with $a_i=1$ and all other $a,b$ bits zero. From the fixed layout, $X_i$ differs from $X_0$ in exactly the four A-controlled sign characters in the two copies associated with coordinate $i$. Write $P_i$ for those four sign fields. The $P_i$ are pairwise disjoint and all source bits outside $\cup_iP_i$ are identical between baseline and every defect.

Run the alleged total deterministic algorithm on $X_0$. For each $i$, it must read at least one bit in $P_i$. Otherwise, induction on deterministic configurations (including all computed guest calls and generated virtual bits) makes its complete run on $X_i$ identical to its run on $X_0$, yet one is UNSAT and the other SAT, contradicting correctness. Thus its baseline transcript touches all $m$ mutually disjoint $P_i$. Choose the coordinate $i^*$ whose \emph{first} touched sign field comes last in that transcript. The $X_{i^*}$ transcript agrees up to that first hit; since a sound constructor cannot accept UNSAT while the observed transcript is still identical to SAT $X_0$, its accepting run on this singleton-defect UNSAT input has already needed $m$ distinct first-coordinate hits (counting its last one). This proves both-label lower bounds on one fixed algorithm.

For the $O(\log N)$-bit contiguous-word model, the four sign fields of each $P_i$ occupy four successive complete records of length $3(w+1)+1=\Theta(\log m)$, and the coordinate groups are consecutive but disjoint. A contiguous word of at most $c\log N$ bits intersects only $O_c(1)$ groups, since each group spans $\Theta(\log N)$ bits. Repeating the transcript argument with these bounded group hits gives $\Omega_c(m)$ word reads, including a worst-case singleton-defect UNSAT source. Input indexing and arithmetic cannot reduce the number of \emph{distinct source words} which must be observed; they may add charged time. An unlimited-width unit-cost word model is explicitly outside the theorem.
\end{proof}

A matching upper construction reads an Alice sign and a Bob sign at the computed source offsets for each coordinate, detects a mismatch, then emits the fixed $W_1$ source-ID proof. It takes $O(m\,\mathrm{polylog}N)$ bit time with $O(\log N)$ writable/index bits in an indexed bit-RAM/RAT, plus full-source syntax checking if needed. This proves the input-source probe order is optimal up to polylogarithmic factors for \emph{this easy equality promise}. It does not exclude a polynomial-time ordinary SAT decider. In particular $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$, so the result provides no $N^{1+\epsilon}$ lower bound and does not promote bounded certificate size to deterministic acquisition time.

\section{Distinct model ledgers and conditional ARC arithmetic}
\begin{description}
\item[] $M_1$ (single writable input tape): $N\le P_1$, each inspected cell and tape-head move is charged in $T_1$. A source-pinned proof that is verified by a sequential parser need not use more than $\mathrm{polylog}N$ auxiliary bits \emph{beyond} the initial $N$-cell footprint. The prior tape crossing result applies natively to the separated equality promise, not because of the new indexed-query theorem. Never replace $P_1$ by auxiliary work space.
\item[] $M_k$ (one sequential read-only input head and fixed work tapes): visited work cells $S_k$ exclude the input; actual source reads and physical input-head motion $H_k$, reversals $R_k$, and crossing transcripts are charged separately. For a \emph{total deterministic equality comparator}, the same complete-state fooling-set argument as HEADMOVE-1 gives $H_k(S_k+1)=\Omega(N^2/\log N)$ and $(R_k+1)(S_k+1)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ on a single SAT input selected by the crossing count. To check SAME-RUN: for every fixed cut inside the $\Theta(N)$-character unchanged middle block, each SAT source $(a,a)$ has a complete ordered crossing transcript. Two distinct $a,a\prime$ cannot share that transcript: deterministic cut splicing would accept the mixed, UNSAT source $(a,a\prime)$. Each crossing record contains control, direction, full visited work contents and work-head positions and costs $O_k(S_k+1)$ bits. The $2^m$ injective transcripts force mean crossings $\Omega(m/(S_k+1))$ at each middle cut; summing over all those cuts and averaging over SAT $a$ selects ONE SAT run with total head moves $\Omega(Nm/(S_k+1))$. On that same run some cut has $\Omega(m/(S_k+1))$ crossings; their directions alternate, forcing $R_k+1=\Omega(m/(S_k+1))$. This is a separate \emph{sequential-input-head} proof and is not supplied by RAM sensitivity. On one-forward-head acquisition the previously proved $S_k=\Omega(N/\log N)$ applies. A guessed mismatch can instead be streamed and checked in $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ work, but this is not a deterministic finder.
\item[] RAT / indexed bounded-word RAM: no physical transport cost is imported. Total deterministic certificate acquisition on the family has source reads $\Omega(N/\log N)$ even with direct jumps, and $O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}N)$ achievable bit time. A supplied correct certificate has $O(\log N)$ bits and local $O(\mathrm{polylog}N)$ indexed verification after promise-layout validation, but this does not pay deterministic discovery or full arbitrary-input syntax validation.
\item[] Bounded-jump one-input-head $M_J$: when an offset jump of size at most $J=N^{j+o(1)}$ costs one charged operation, the prior same-family transport simulation yields conditional/native $t+s+j\ge2$ under its exact one-head, fixed-work-tape hypotheses. It is not a RAM theorem and not inferred from the new source sensitivity alone.
\end{description}

For the leaf portfolio in this particular family no call to $A$ is required: a fixed authenticated 32-leaf $W_1$ contradiction suffices, so the guest spectrum $\{m_i\}$ is empty and $M=V=0$. For a different portfolio with $m_i\ge1$ and one total exact guest $A$ of unknown exponent $d\ge1$, the elementary inequality
\[
\sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V,\qquad M=\max_i m_i,\quad V=\sum_i m_i \tag{7}
\]
is proved termwise by $m_i^d=m_i m_i^{d-1}\le m_i M^{d-1}$. This is only the \emph{guest execution} part of the full ledger; source access, materialization, policy discovery and retries, $B+L+E$, indexes, ancestors, intermediate memory and both encoded child levels are additionally charged. The budget $c\le\max\{r,u+b+\lambda(d-1)\}$ for $M\le N^\lambda,V\le N^b$ is \textbf{CONDITIONAL} on those resources being proved on every UNSAT ordinary source with uniformly bounded certificate length and all invalid paths clocked. Under $P=NP$, the starting exponent is unknown $K$, not $1.802$. Nothing here satisfies an all-input ARC-EDGE-90 machine-producing implication.

\section{Audit, limitation, and status}
A dependency-free Python reproducer runs 302,646 explicit assertion groups without failure: four exact local model counts; 32 independent five-name authenticated SCC leaves with 600 edge references; 168 fully serialized standard-DIMACS and fixed-width binary two-level sources for $m=1,2,3$ across both graph grammars and all $a,b$; 140 actually authenticated UNSAT certificates; 28 SAT model constructions with all anchor identities checked; 84 connected incidence controls; and five independent 4-sign singleton-defect sensitivity layouts for $m=2,4,8,16,32$. Invalid source pointers, wrong literal signs, incomplete branch trees, and SAT-source certificate attempts are tested. The canonical fixed-width clause rows are reparsed and the headers compared; complete time, head itinerary and peak RAM usage are \emph{not} empirically measured. The finite tests support the exact elementary proof, not a general SAT lower bound.

This theorem is a logically narrow \emph{information sensitivity\,/\,deterministic witness-acquisition} separation: full-promise logarithmic nondeterministic proof length coexists with worst-case nearly linear source interrogation on a direct-access machine. It strengthens the native-RAM side of SOURCE-LOCAL-STREAM139 without claiming its one-pass work-space bound on a RAM. Connectedness and genuine global equality joins still permit local refutation. It neither imports Resolution/Tseitin exponential lower bounds into affine/PC/CP terminals nor assumes arbitrary $A$ outputs small source-ID proofs. Duplicated or derived source children without an authenticated lift remain disallowed. All future universal-coverage statements require a new theorem outside this equality promise.
