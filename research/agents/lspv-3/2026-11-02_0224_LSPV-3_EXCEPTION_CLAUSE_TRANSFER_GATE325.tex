\section{Exact theorem and proof}
Let $G_t$ be the authenticated $9t$-variable cyclic F9-splice source with $12t$ clauses and two cyclic boundary bits per block. Let $F$ be another \emph{grammar-validated canonical original} on the same numbered variables and with the same clause count. Normalize signed clauses as unordered triples. Set $A=F\setminus G_t$, $D=G_t\setminus F$, $H=G_t\setminus D$, and $k=|A|=|D|$. For coherent pins $p$ and any $J\subseteq A$, let $q_J$ force all literals in every clause of $J$ false, and define $Z_J=0$ on a pin conflict. Otherwise let
\[
  Z_J(p)=\operatorname{tr}(W_1^{p\cup q_J}\cdots W_t^{p\cup q_J}),
\]
where each $2\times2$ integer matrix is acquired by enumerating at most $4\cdot2^8$ original-variable assignments to its two boundary bits and eight private variables, checking all surviving $H$ source clauses.
\begin{theorem}[Paid signed-clause exception correction]
For every such canonical $F$ and coherent $p$,
\[
 \boxed{\#\mathrm{SAT}(F\mid p)=\sum_{J\subseteq A}(-1)^{|J|}Z_J(p).}
\]
A uniform source-authenticated decision-only algorithm runs in
$2^{k}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ bit time, where $N$ is the encoded source length. An explicit source-verifiable model-count certificate, including a complete UNSAT certificate when the sum is zero, is obtainable in the same asymptotic time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each complete original assignment satisfying $H$ and $p$, multiply its indicator by $\prod_{C\in A}(1-\mathbf 1[C\text{ false}])$. Expand the product into the $2^{k}$ signed inclusion-exclusion terms. The event that every $C\in J$ is false is exactly the coherent original-variable pin condition $q_J$, or is impossible on a pin contradiction. Every clause of $H$ belongs to one cyclic source block, so summation over the cyclic boundary values and independent eight-variable private assignments gives the matrix trace exactly. Summing the terms yields the exact cardinality, with no SAT oracle. Each term's source-evaluated tables and matrix products can be checked independently; after validating the finite identity, zero certifies complete UNSAT.
\end{proof}
