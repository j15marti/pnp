\section*{Published anchor, encoding, and fixed model}
Urquhart (1987) and Ben-Sasson--Wigderson (2001) establish exponential ordinary unrestricted DAG-Resolution lower bounds on suitable odd-charge cubic expander-Tseitin 3-CNF families: there exist fixed \(c>0,h_0\) such that for all \(h\ge h_0\) in the family, every ordinary Resolution refutation has at least \(2^{ch}\) lines. Vertices number \(h\), edges/Boolean variables \(3h/2\); each vertex parity equation is encoded by four width-three source clauses; explicit indexed CNF length is \(N_h=\Theta(h\log(h+2))\). These are Resolution proof-size lower bounds, not lower bounds for arbitrary SAT deciders. The previously fixed \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) includes free authenticated source clauses and unit paid nodes for each distinct shared-DAG binary Resolution, signed AND3, GF(2) addition, conservative two-input DEF, or legal LOCAL-CONVERT. The prior source-essentiality and GF(2) shortcut provide exact full-mixed cost \(4h-1\) for these odd cubic sources. We do not change the system or infer time from this node cost.
We study the separate pure-XOR stage of a proof after authenticating \(h\) vertex parity rows, each \(\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=\chi(v)\) with odd total charge. Each paid XOR node adds two prior rows and costs one; all adaptive orders and DAG references are permitted. Expanded intermediate rows are NOT necessarily printed or materialized.
\section*{New aggregate-width theorem}
For connected cubic \(G=(V,E)\), \(h=|V|\), let \(\beta(G)=\min_{\emptyset\ne S\subsetneq V} |\delta_G(S)|/\min(|S|,h-|S|)\). Let \(M(\pi)\) be the sum of edge-variable Hamming widths over the \(h-1\) derived rows of a pure-XOR refutation \(\pi\).
\begin{theorem}
For every such \(G\) of order \(h\ge12\) with odd charge, and every legal \(h-1\)-node binary-XOR refutation \(\pi\), even allowing shared-DAG syntax and free best-order selection,
\[
\boxed{M(\pi)\ \ge\ \frac{\beta(G)h}{2}\log_2(h/6)-3h.}
\]
Thus if \(\beta(G)\ge\beta_0>0\) on an unbounded cubic-expander family, every minimum-node pure-XOR stage has \(\Omega(h\log h)\) total \emph{expanded} row-variable occurrences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Append the charge coordinate to each vertex incidence mask. A linear dependency among these augmented vectors has even edge incidence across every edge, forcing its selected vertices to be either none or all by connectedness. The odd total charge excludes all, so the augmented vectors are independent. To derive \((0,1)\), every one of the \(h\) sources must be an ancestor. There are \(h\) source nodes and \(h-1\) binary gates, hence \(2h-1\) nodes and \(2h-2\) parent edges in the connected ancestor graph. It is a tree (even as a multigraph); in particular, a minimum-node derivation cannot share a computed subnode. Every tree edge cuts off some subset \(S\) of source leaves and carries exactly the row \(\bigoplus_{v\in S}r_v\), whose variable mask is \(\delta_G(S)\).

Take a centroid of this maximum-degree-three tree weighted by its \(h\) leaves. Every away-side component has at most \(h/2\) leaves. The sum over all tree edges of the smaller-side leaf count is the sum of distances from the centroid to all \(h\) source leaves. At most \(3(2^r-1)\) tree vertices are within positive distance \(r\). Set \(r=\lfloor\log_2(h/6)\rfloor\). At least \(h/2\) leaves have distance \(>r\); their distance sum is at least \((h/2)\log_2(h/6)\). Graph expansion then forces at least \(\beta(G)h\log_2(h/6)/2\) aggregate edge occurrences across all tree edges. The \(h\) original source-leaf edges contribute exactly \(3h\) occurrences and are free, so subtract these to obtain the claimed derived-row bound.
\end{proof}
\section*{Countercontrol: representation independence fails}
The same tree can be serialized using \(h-1\) pairs of parent indices, occupying only \(O(h\log h)\) bits. A correct typed checker parses all authentic \(4h\) parity clauses, verifies parent indices and a tree using each source once, and checks the odd global charge. It need not expand any intermediate mask, and uses polynomial bit time and space with no semantic oracle or per-instance answer. If instead the certificate convention explicitly lists every intermediate row as fixed-width indexed edge IDs, the theorem forces \(\Omega(h(\log h)^2)\) output bits; using dense edge masks for every node costs \(\Theta(h^2)\) bits. These are costs of \emph{particular certificate formats}, not unrestricted SAT runtime. The previous one-wide-row lemma LSPV-3/375 and full-mixed \(4h-1\) shortcut are mandatory countercontrols. No lower bound covers longer XOR DAGs or every inference in full mixed \(R\).
\section*{Exact finite diagnostic and falsifiers}
The exhaustive recurrence for minimum derived width on a source subset \(S\) is \(f(\{v\})=0\) and
\[
f(S)=|\delta_G(S)|+\min_{\emptyset\ne A\subsetneq S}\bigl(f(A)+f(S\setminus A)\bigr).
\]
In a deterministic producer the exact optima were \(7,15,23,31\) for \(K_4,K_{3,3}\), the cube, and pentagonal prism (orders \(4,6,8,10\)); charged XOR nodes \(3,5,7,9\). Each source graph is cubic; odd-charge satisfying model counts were \(0,0,0,0\), and even-charge controls \(8,16,32,64\). The independently written JS verifier recomputed each exhaustive optimum, replayed signed XOR DAGs, enumerated edge assignments against all local four-clause parity constraints, passed \(299{,}461\) assertions, rejected six corrupted and four malformed inputs. Small instances do not validate the \(h\ge12\) inequality by extrapolation.
\begin{description}
\item[] F1: A shortest \(h-1\) XOR DAG can share a subrow while retaining all \(h\) independent sources. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the connected edge-count tree argument.
\item[] F2: Maximum row width cannot be strengthened to aggregate width. \textbf{FALSIFIED} within the minimum-node stage by the centroid bound.
\item[] F3: Aggregate expanded width is unavoidable physical bit traffic for every representation. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by lazy parent-pointer proof checking.
\item[] F4: The result yields superpolynomial mixed proof cost or unrestricted SAT time. \textbf{FALSE} for this family; the fixed mixed calculus has a linear certified proof.
\end{description}
