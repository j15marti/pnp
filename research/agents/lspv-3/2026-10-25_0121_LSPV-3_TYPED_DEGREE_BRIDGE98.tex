\section{Typed exact-one/equality source and a polynomial degree-two algorithm}
An instance $I$ comprises signed three-distinct-coordinate exact-one constraints
$E(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)$ and two-coordinate equality records $u=v$.
A typed occurrence of $v$ is one appearance in an $E$ or one endpoint
of an equality \emph{record}; the typed degree $d_I(v)$ is their number.
In particular, this is \emph{not} the literal-occurrence count after
replacing equality by two implications in ordinary CNF. Every local
$E$ has three feasible visible signatures and affine-hull dimension two.

\begin{theorem}[Degree-two typed solvability; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Every exact-one/equality source with $\max_v d_I(v)\le2$ can be decided
uniformly in deterministic polynomial time, with optional witness
reconstruction, irrespective of cycles in its constraint graph.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the graph whose vertices are source-variable coordinates and whose
edges are equality records. Its maximum degree is at most two.
Every coordinate in one $E$ has at most one incident equality edge;
a coordinate in two $E$ constraints is isolated in the equality graph.
Therefore every equality component is a path, isolated point, or cycle.
Each component has at most two \emph{positions} across all exact-one
constraints: the endpoints of a path, two $E$ occurrences on an isolated
coordinate, or zero positions on an equality-only cycle.
Contract each component to one Boolean variable. Its truth value extends
uniquely to the component coordinates, while an equality-only cycle can
be set arbitrarily. Each contracted variable occurs in at most two
exact-one positions.

If contraction identifies two or three positions of one $E$, inspect
its fixed eight-row truth table. For instance $E(x,x,y)$ means
$x=0,y=1$, whereas $E(x,\neg x,y)$ means $y=0$.
All such repetitions reduce to unary pins, a tautology or a contradiction.
Propagate pins and retain only exact-one constraints with three distinct
source variables, each with at most two occurrences. The signed
degree-two interval-cardinality graph-factor algorithm of
INTERVAL-FACTOR134 decides this residual in polynomial time. Validation,
union-find, repeated-coordinate simplification, factor construction,
matching/factor search and witness reconstruction are charged. No SAT
label or semantic boundary table is supplied.
\end{proof}

\section{A connected maximum-degree-three equality-only NP-complete image}
Take a positive 1-in-3-SAT instance $H$ with $m\ge1$ triples,
$n_+$ occurring variables and $c$ incidence-connected components.
Make a distinct occurrence copy for each of the $3m$ literal
occurrences, one disjoint local $E$ module per triple, and an equality
path among the copies of each original named variable.
The existing EXACTONE-JOIN-CYCLE95 theorem proves the resulting exact
source-name gluing semantics. Each occurrence coordinate is in exactly
one $E$ and at most two equality records.

For every pair of successive disconnected components choose one distinct,
previously unused equality-path endpoint $u$ in the first and $v$ in the
second. Each component contains at least three original source variables,
and each original variable has an eligible path endpoint, so two distinct
eligible copies are available in any intermediate component.
Introduce six fresh variables $u',z_1,a,v',z_2,b$ and add
\[
 E(u',z_1,a),\qquad E(v',z_2,b),\qquad
 u'=u,\quad z_1=z_2,\quad v'=v .
\]
This two-module three-equality bridge is \emph{neutral}: for every
assignment to $u,v$, extend with
$u'=u,v'=v,z_1=z_2=0,a=1-u,b=1-v$.
It connects the incidence components but imposes no additional relation
on any original variable. Only the two chosen original endpoint
copies acquire one new equality edge, and their typed degrees stay
at most three. All new variables have degree at most two.

\begin{theorem}[Connected three-signature degree-three hardness; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
SAT is NP-complete for connected typed instances of exact-one modules
and binary equality-only intermodule constraints, even with
$\max_v d_I(v)\le3$, every coordinate in exactly one $E$ module,
and all local feasible domains of cardinality three.
Writing $M$ for the total number of local modules and $L$ for equality
records, the reduction has exactly
\[
 M=m+2(c-1),\qquad
 L=(3m-n_+)+3(c-1),\qquad 3M\ \text{typed variables}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equality paths identify copies of the same original variable;
each bridge is neutral by the explicit extension above. Hence the
constructed connected instance is SAT exactly when the source $H$
is SAT. The source language is in NP, and positive 1-in-3-SAT is
NP-complete by Schaefer, with a stronger cubic version by
Moore--Robson. The bridge connects all original components in a path,
preserving the maximum typed degree three and one-$E$-per-variable
promise. All construction work and output records are polynomial,
indeed linear in the source occurrence-record count.
\end{proof}

\section{Ordinary proper-3CNF bits and simultaneous-work firewall}
Use the four proper ternary exclusion clauses for parity
$\ell_1\oplus\ell_2\oplus\ell_3=1$ and replace the binary
$(\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2)$ by
\[
(\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2\vee z)
\land(\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2\vee\neg z)
\]
with one fresh $z$. Each $E$ becomes six proper clauses and one
auxiliary. Equality $u=v$ comprises two binary implications, each
padded by a separate fresh auxiliary, giving four proper ternaries.
The source-named, connected ordinary proper-3CNF therefore has
\[
 \boxed{n'=4M+2L,\quad m'=6M+4L,\quad
 N'=(4m'+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(n'+m'+1))\right\rceil.}
\]
The explicit output uses $O((m+L)\log(m+L))$ bits and preserves exactly
the original SAT label and witness-lift semantics. This compilation
does not yield $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ ordinary children with $\lambda<1$
from arbitrary SAT inputs.

For a matched opposite-label control, use source triples
$(abc),(abd),(acd),(bcd)$. All positive exact-one is UNSAT by
$3(a+b+c+d)=4$; negating only $b$ in the last triple makes it SAT at
$(a,b,c,d)=(1,0,0,0)$. Append a disjoint positive triple $(efg)$ and
apply the neutral bridge. Both resulting connected proper-3CNFs have
$m=5,n_+=7,c=2$, $M=7,L=11,n'=50,m'=86$, and fixed-record
$N'=4844$ bits, with identical unsigned supports and opposite labels.

For a declared typed source instance, one adaptive procedure can
discover $k(I)=|\{v:d_I(v)\ge3\}|$, branch on those $k$ Boolean variables,
simplify all affected $E$ and equality records, and solve the remaining
degree-two instance. This gives a restricted
$2^{k(I)}\operatorname{poly}(|I|)$-time, polynomial-peak-space
\emph{upper bound}. The parameter changes under alternative
occurrence-copy encodings; it is not a solver-independent work lower
bound. A different exact solver can exploit source syntax, affine,
Horn, SCC, direct decision, caching, recomputation, or an alternative
representation, and need not materialize a boundary join.

The calculation makes the receiving gap explicit:
\[
 (t,s)\in\mathcal E\quad
 \overset{\mathrm{OPEN}}{\Longrightarrow}\quad
 \text{a new jointly achievable favorable pair in }\mathcal E .
\]
A typed NP-complete global join and short local exact-one modules do
not supply this all-input ARC contraction. The construction carries
source-scale ordinary encoding, and a full-length exact fallback can
consume every hoped-for improvement. A one-bit root decider need not
output all witnesses, proofs, states or DNNFs. Complete accounting
must separate primitive bit work, source/index and work reads, unchanged
writes, true complete-state flips, live writable bits, cumulative
bit-time, source/child serialization, name aliases, buffers, failed
