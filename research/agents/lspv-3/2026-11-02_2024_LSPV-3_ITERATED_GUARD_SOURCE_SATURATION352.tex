\section*{Fixed system and definition}
Source clauses cost zero. Every derived node costs one and has at most two proof-premise dependencies. The fixed rules include ordinary binary Resolution, bounded local exact AND3, binary GF(2) row addition, paid local conversions, and charged conservative fresh-variable gate definitions. Sharing, temporary growth, adaptive rule choices and optimal after-the-fact selection are permitted. No uncharged multi-source inference or answer oracle is introduced.
Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i(x)$ be clause-minimally UNSAT, and $z_1,\ldots,z_t$ fresh. For $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^t$, put $L_\epsilon=\bigvee_j (z_j\text{ if }\epsilon_j=0,\ \neg z_j\text{ otherwise})$ and
\[
G_t(F)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m\bigwedge_{\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^t}(C_i\vee L_\epsilon).
\]
For every full assignment $(x,z)$ exactly one copy of each original $C_i$ has all guard literals false. Hence $G_t(F)(x,z)\equiv F(x)$ pointwise.
\begin{theorem}[source-floor guard saturation]
If $F$ has a legal $m-1$-node source-once refutation in the fixed mixed calculus, then for every $t\ge0$
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(G_t(F))=m\,2^t-1.}
\]
This is the globally attained minimum over all legal DAGs, including arbitrary shared subproofs, algebraic reasoning, auxiliary gates, representation changes and adaptive inference orders.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a guarded source $D_{i,\epsilon}=C_i\vee L_\epsilon$. By minimal UNSAT, there is an assignment $a_i$ satisfying every original clause except $C_i$. With $z=\epsilon$, precisely $D_{i,\epsilon}$ is false among all the guarded clauses. Deleting it therefore makes $G_t(F)$ SAT, so all $m2^t$ named source clauses are indispensable. For a sound conservative proof with $r=m2^t$ indispensable sources and $q$ paid nodes of arity at most two, connectivity of its dependency ancestry yields $r+q-1\le E\le2q$, so $q\ge r-1$. Sharing and charged source-independent definitions cannot avoid this bound.
For the upper bound, in each guarded pair differing only in the sign of $z_t$, perform one ordinary Resolution inference. This removes $z_t$ and leaves all other literals. Repeat for $z_{t-1},\ldots,z_1$, at a total cost $m(2^t-1)$, obtaining precisely the original $m$ source clauses as derived lines. Connect these lines to the existing legal $m-1$-node mixed proof of $F$. The complete source-once proof has $m2^t-1$ nodes, matching the lower bound.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[individual sign sensitivity]
Every signed literal occurrence of $G_t(F)$ is sensitive. The same $(a_i,\epsilon)$ above falsifies every literal of exactly one guarded source $D_{i,\epsilon}$, and satisfies every other source. Flipping any one literal sign of that source gives a SAT instance. This includes guards and original-clause occurrences.
\end{theorem}
