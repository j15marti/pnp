\section{An exact source-state parameter and its consequences}
Let $F$ be a proper strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ 3CNF with original clauses $C_j$, $n$ variables, and $m=4n/3$ clauses. An original signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$ is the ordinary variable $x_i$ in its source clause, not a new Boolean variable. Fix a coherent partial original-variable assignment $\rho$ of $t$ variables. Define
\[
 s_j(\rho)=\#\{\ell\in C_j:\operatorname{var}(\ell)\in\operatorname{dom}\rho,\ \rho\models\ell\},\quad
 D(\rho)=\#\{j:s_j(\rho)>0\},\quad
 \Omega(\rho)=\sum_j(s_j(\rho)-1)_+.
\]
The number $s_j$ counts TRUE \emph{already assigned} literals, not truth of an unassigned clause. $D$ is the number of original clauses safely deleted upon restriction; $\Omega$ is the exact overlap cost when two or three assigned TRUE occurrences belong to the same original clause.

\begin{theorem}[Exact balanced-sign deletion ledger]
For every proper strict22 $F$ and coherent partial assignment $\rho$,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_j s_j(\rho)=2t,\qquad D(\rho)=2t-\Omega(\rho).}
\tag{1}
\]
If $\rho$ is extended by $x=b$, let $h_x(b;\rho)$ count the already deleted clauses among the \emph{two} original clauses whose signed $x$ occurrence becomes TRUE under $x=b$. Then
\[
 \boxed{D(\rho\cup\{x=b\})-D(\rho)=2-h_x(b;\rho),\quad
 \Omega(\rho\cup\{x=b\})-\Omega(\rho)=h_x(b;\rho).}
\tag{2}
\]
Here $h_x\in\{0,1,2\}$. Both deltas are source-authenticated and maintainable with the indexed original occurrence list; constructing the index and choosing an action are not free.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original variable has exactly two occurrences of each sign. Assigning its value TRUE or FALSE makes exactly two of its original occurrences TRUE, and all assigned variables are distinct. Therefore the total number of TRUE assigned source occurrences is $2t=\sum_j s_j$. For each integer $s\ge0$, $s=\mathbf1_{s>0}+(s-1)_+$; summing over original clauses proves (1). The next variable contributes exactly two new TRUE occurrences, in two distinct clauses because the source is proper. Each previously deleted clause absorbs one new occurrence without increasing $D$; each new clause increases $D$ by one. Hence (2).\end{proof}

For a fixed source-variable-ID encoding with signed literal records of $b=1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$ bits, a two-bit clause-arity field and source-universe header $H=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil+\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil$, write
\[
 N=H+m(2+3b),\qquad N_\rho=H+\sum_{j:s_j(\rho)=0}(2+b\,|C_j\!\restriction\!\rho|).
\tag{3}
\]
Here $C_j\restriction\rho$ deletes FALSE assigned literals; a falsified original clause makes $\rho$ a source-certified NO cube and is \emph{not} a guest leaf. On a nonconflicting leaf, every surviving clause has at least one literal, so
\[
 N_\rho\ge H+(2+b)(m-2t+\Omega(\rho)).
\tag{4}
\]
\begin{corollary}[Source-NO mass required by a short direct-cofactor guest]
Set $L=\lfloor N^\lambda\rfloor$ and $h=\max\{0,\lfloor(L-H)/(2+b)\rfloor\}$, assuming $L\ge H$. Every nonconflicting emitted guest child of at most $L$ bits must fix at least
\[
 \boxed{r=\max\{0,\lceil(m-h)/2\rceil\}}
\tag{5}
\]
original variables. If $g$ explicit logical guest leaves and $E$ is the union of disjoint, source-certified NO prefix leaves in a complete adaptive binary cofactor tree, then
\[
 \boxed{\mu(E)+\sum_{i=1}^g2^{-|\rho_i|}=1,\quad \mu(E)\ge1-g2^{-r}.}
\tag{6}
\]
If there are no NO cubes, $g\ge2^r$. For strict22 $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ and $0<\lambda<1$, $r\ge 2n/3-o(n)$. This is an architecture-specific necessary condition, not an unconditional decision-time or arbitrary guest-query lower bound; shared DAG replies, globally recognized models and semantic compilers are outside its explicit unfolded-tree premise.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By (4), $N_\rho\le L$ implies $m-2t+\Omega\le h$ and hence $t\ge r$. The prefix cylinders of the source-NO leaves and short guest leaves partition the Boolean assignment cube. Their normalized cardinalities sum to one, and each guest cylinder weighs at most $2^{-r}$. The asymptotic uses $m=4n/3$, $h=O(N^\lambda/\log n)=o(n)$. This is LSPV-1's known prefix-mass argument instantiated with the improved LSPV-4 signed22 count and exact residual record minimum; no new machine-independent Kraft theorem is claimed.\end{proof}

\section{Robust algebraic controls, acquisition and machine audit}
A separately archived existing LSPV-3 detector implementation was rerun on three SAT/UNSAT pairs. For Horn SAT/UNSAT, its recognizer took 36/32 selected events; Krom SAT/UNSAT, 46/49; XOR SAT/UNSAT, 58/59. The matched independent UP-first DPLL took 22/13, 43/37 and 71/210 events respectively. Each SAT witness was verified on the original clauses and every UNSAT label compared with exhaustive small truth tables. This confirms no blanket preference for preprocessing: XOR contradiction favors algebraic recognition, whereas tiny Horn/Krom controls favor UP. No external industrial CDCL was available; do not label this a production CDCL benchmark.

Constructing a signed occurrence index costs $\Omega(N)$ source access; an indexed $s_j,D,\Omega$ update costs $O(\deg(x))=O(4)$ local clause examinations per committed variable, but scoring or trying \emph{all} candidate variables and both signs adds discovery/selection work. No optimal policy oracle or rank/witness-count invariant is assumed. The producer's full-scan accounting charges original-source bits read at every tree node, clause access, source conflicts, DPLL guests and source witness checking; peak Python allocated bytes and maximum pending cubes are recorded. It does not implement the FLMV indexed-input TM or a correct arbitrary-guest input-head simulation, so the simultaneous $(K',L')$ recurrence remains CONDITIONAL and unachieved. A parent-size $A(F)$ call, materialized $N$-bit retained index or free sorted guest transcript would independently fail that contract.
