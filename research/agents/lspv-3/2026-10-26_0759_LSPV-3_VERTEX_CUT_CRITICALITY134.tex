\section{Exact cubic vertex-cut essentiality theorem}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a connected simple undirected \emph{cubic} graph, and $\chi:V\to\mathbb F_2$ have odd total charge $\sum_v\chi(v)=1$.  Every edge $e$ is an original Boolean name $x_e$.  For every vertex $v$ and every three-bit word $a$ of parity $1+\chi(v)$, insert exactly one proper signed 3CNF clause $C_{v,a}$ falsified by precisely $a$ on the three edges incident to $v$.  The resulting ordinary CNF is $T(G,\chi)$, with exactly $|E|$ active variables and $4|V|$ \emph{distinct original clause identifiers}.  Summing vertex XOR equations proves $T(G,\chi)$ UNSAT.

Call $C$ \emph{essential} if $T(G,\chi)\setminus\{C\}$ is satisfiable.  For each $v$ write $G-v=H_1\dot\cup\cdots\dot\cup H_{c_v}$, and let $\delta(v,H_j)$ denote original edges from $v$ into component $H_j$.
\begin{theorem}[Exact vertex-cut spectrum]\label{th:cut}
For every $v$ and forbidden word $a$, the following statements are equivalent:
\[
\begin{split}
&C_{v,a}\text{ is essential};\\[-2mm]
&\text{there is a full original-edge assignment satisfying all vertex equations except }v\\[-2mm]
&\text{and taking value }a\text{ at the three original edges of }v;\\[-2mm]
&\bigoplus_{u\in H_j}\chi(u)=\bigoplus_{e\in\delta(v,H_j)}a_e\quad(1\le j\le c_v).
\end{split}\tag{1}
\]
Exactly $2^{3-c_v}$ of the four original clauses at $v$ are essential.  Thus
\[
\boxed{|E_{\mathrm{source}}(T(G,\chi))|=\sum_{v\in V}2^{3-c_v}.}\tag{2}
\]
In particular, \emph{every} original clause is essential, equivalently $T(G,\chi)$ is minimally UNSAT, if and only if $G-v$ is connected for every $v$.  That is, $G$ is two-vertex-connected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Since the original formula is UNSAT, a satisfying assignment after deleting $C_{v,a}$ must falsify that clause.  It therefore fixes all three incident edge names to $a$ and satisfies all remaining parity constraints; conversely, such an edge assignment satisfies every clause other than $C_{v,a}$.  After fixing the incident edges and deleting vertex $v$, each $H_j$ is connected.  Its remaining parity linear system over $\mathbb F_2$ is solvable if and only if the XOR of its right-hand sides is zero, because the edge--vertex incidence matrix of a connected graph has rank $|H_j|-1$.  The right-hand-side parity is exactly the difference between the two sides of (1).

Every component of $G-v$ has at least one edge to $v$, because $G$ is connected.  The $c_v$ linear constraints in (1) involve disjoint, nonempty subsets of the three incident edge bits, and are independent.  Consequently exactly $2^{3-c_v}$ words $a$ satisfy them.  XORing these $c_v$ constraints yields
$\bigoplus_{e\ni v}a_e=\bigoplus_{u\ne v}\chi(u)=1+\chi(v)$.
Therefore \emph{all} these words are among the four forbidden local words.  Summing over vertices proves (2).  Since $c_v\ge1$, four essential clauses occur at $v$ precisely for $c_v=1$, completing the iff claim.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Sharp hostile cut.} Subdivide one edge in each of two $K_4$ graphs and join the subdividers by a single bridge.  The result is simple, connected and cubic with ten vertices.  The two bridge-end subdividers satisfy $c_v=2$, while the other eight vertices have $c_v=1$.  Hence exactly $8(4)+2(2)=36$ of its 40 clauses are essential.  A disconnected-source or bare connectedness heuristic would miss the four redundant clauses.  The reproducer separately checks (1) by actual GF$(2)$ row elimination after pinning each candidate forbidden word, not just by evaluating the symbolic component formula.

\section{A source-critical high-treewidth easy-certificate family}
For $r\ge3$, let $G_r$ have vertices $(i,j,b)$, with $i,j\in\mathbb Z/r\mathbb Z$ and $b\in\{0,1\}$.  Vertex $(i,j,0)$ is adjacent to
\[
(i,j,1),\quad(i-1,j,1),\quad(i,j-1,1).
\tag{3}
\]
This is a simple connected cubic honeycomb torus with $q=2r^2$ graph vertices and $3r^2$ Boolean edge names.  Give vertex $(0,0,0)$ charge 1 and all other vertices charge 0.  Its CNF has $8r^2=4q$ proper, unique signed ternary clauses, and physical decimal DIMACS length $N_r=\Theta(r^2\log r)$.

\begin{lemma}[Source-critical high-treewidth family]\label{lem:honey}
Every original one of the $8r^2$ clauses of the odd $G_r$ Tseitin formula is essential.  The graph has $\operatorname{tw}(G_r)=\Omega(r)$, while every original edge variable occurs exactly four times positively and four times negatively.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The edges from $(i,j,0)$ to $(i,j,1)$ are a perfect matching.  Contracting them produces the square toroidal grid on $r^2$ cells, with vertical and horizontal wraparound edges.  Remove any single endpoint $(i,j,b)$ in the original cubic graph, contract all other matching edges, and observe that the toroidal square grid with cell $(i,j)$ removed remains connected for $r\ge3$.  The one unmatched endpoint in that cell has two surviving neighbors in the remaining connected graph.  Thus $G_r-v$ stays connected for every $v$.  Theorem~\ref{th:cut} gives all-clause minimality.  The contraction also exhibits an ordinary $r\times r$ grid minor, so the standard grid-treewidth bound implies $\operatorname{tw}(G_r)=\Omega(r)$.  Each original edge occurs in exactly two parity-vertex quartets, with two positive and two negative occurrences in each quartet, yielding total $(4,4)$.  Thus the statement does \emph{not} transfer to the strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ class.
\end{proof}

This is stronger than the earlier assertion that no \emph{proper collection of complete parity-vertex modules} is contradictory.  Here deletion of any \emph{single original proper ternary clause} destroys UNSAT, so the ordinary source contains no smaller UNSAT original-clause subset.  Yet its proof system can be extremely short.

\section{Exact degree-three polynomial-calculus proof and encoded length}
Encode an actual clause $C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$ by the characteristic-two vanishing polynomial $p_C=\prod_i(1-\ell_i)$, identifying a positive literal with $x$ and a negative literal with $1+x$.  Equivalently, when $C_{v,a}$ forbids edge word $a$, its polynomial is $\prod_{e\ni v}(x_e\text{ if }a_e=1\text{ else }1+x_e)$.  It has degree at most 3, with all three edge names distinct.
\begin{lemma}[Locally authenticated degree-three PC refutation]\label{lem:pc}
For every odd-charge $T(G,\chi)$ on a cubic graph, there is a characteristic-two polynomial-calculus refutation using only authenticated original clause polynomials and additions, of maximum degree 3, with $8|V|-1$ lines when each original clause is an axiom line.  The local identity is
\[
\boxed{\sum_{a:\oplus a=1+\chi(v)}p_{C_{v,a}}
=\sum_{e\ni v}x_e+\chi(v).}\tag{4}
\]
Summing the linear equations over all $v$ produces the constant polynomial 1.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The four polynomials on the left are characteristic functions of the disjoint forbidden words on the three-dimensional Boolean cube.  Their sum is the Boolean function $\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e+\chi(v)$.  Both sides are multilinear polynomials of degree at most 3, so equality at every Boolean point implies the polynomial identity (4) over $\mathbb F_2$.  Starting from four authentic clause axioms, three PC additions derive the linear local equation.  Across all $|V|$ vertices, another $|V|-1$ additions suffice; every edge variable appears twice and cancels, while the odd charges sum to 1.  The total is $4|V|+3|V|+(|V|-1)=8|V|-1$ lines.  Degree never exceeds 3.  No unknown SAT status, whole-parent oracle call or assignment-gluing approximation is inserted.
\end{proof}

For $r=2^p\ge4$, create the PC additions in a dyadic four-way recursion on square \emph{cells}, putting the two vertex equations of each cell together first.  A rectangle's sum is the XOR of names on the edge boundary of that rectangle, plus its parity of charges.  A square of side $z$ has $O(z)$ boundary edge occurrences.  Over all four-way subdivision regions, the sum of these boundary sizes satisfies
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{p}O(4^j\,r/2^j)=O(r^2)=O(q).
\tag{5}
\]
The three intermediate additions within each quadtree node also correspond to rectangles of comparable perimeter.  Constant-size axiom lines and local additions contribute $O(q)$ monomials as well.  All PC lines together therefore have $O(q)$ explicit monomials and $O(q)$ line identifiers.  With $O(\log q)$ bits per original-name/line/source-offset reference, the \emph{fully explicit, not merely circuit-compressed} proof uses
\[
\boxed{P_{\rm PC}=O(q\log q)=O(N_r)\text{ bits},\quad
\max\deg=3,\quad\#\mathrm{lines}=8q-1.}\tag{6}
\]
A streamed depth-first checker retaining one pending boundary polynomial at each dyadic level uses $O(r\log q)$ simultaneous writable/index bits and $O(q\operatorname{polylog}q)$ total bit work on this structured proof.  Actual unstructured PC proofs do not inherit this DFS bound.  This is a theoretical pseudocode-machine bound for the declared canonical tree format, not a Python peak-RAM claim.

\section{Direct one-way, source-offset authenticated certificate macro}
The conventional read-only, random-access \emph{certificate tape} and a one-way nondeterministic guess stream are distinct models \cite{vanmelkebeek}.  The earlier Role-3 PAIR-XOR construction used a separately indexed proof tape.  For the \emph{canonical} geometry (3), that extra proof-tape access can be eliminated:
\[
e(i,j,t)=3(ir+j)+t+1,\quad t\in\{0,1,2\}.
\tag{7}
\]
The three names at $(i,j,0)$ are $e(i,j,0),e(i,j,1),e(i,j,2)$; at $(i,j,1)$ they are $e(i,j,0),e(i+1,j,1),e(i,j+1,2)$, with modular coordinates.  Thus the complete cross-vertex same-original-name pairing is checked by \emph{deterministic log-index arithmetic}, not by a guessed global involution table.

A certificate first names $r$ and then, in fixed vertex order, supplies one charge bit and four original-source \emph{byte offsets} for each parity quartet.  The verifier scans the complete ordinary DIMACS source once to validate syntax and its declared header, imposes $r^2\le N$ before accepting any certificate, and on receiving each record checks that the offsets are starts of four actual source clauses with precisely the four forbidden words on the direct names in (7).  Offsets are source-authenticated; no private names may be glued unless the integers actually agree.  The verifier XORs the charge bits and accepts after \emph{exactly} $2r^2$ quartet records only if the final parity is odd.  Unrelated extra source clauses are harmless because a verified inconsistent subset already forces the whole source UNSAT.

\begin{theorem}[One-way guarded source-authenticated terminal]\label{th:stream}
For every ordinary SAT input $F$ and every one-way guessed record sequence $\pi$ respecting the uniform length clock, acceptance by the preceding canonical checker implies $F\in\UNSAT$.  On every accepted \emph{canonical odd-honeycomb} source of length $N=N_r$, there exists a record stream of $P=O(N)$ bits accepted in $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ bit work and $O(\log N)$ simultaneous work/index bits.  An all-input clock of $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ work rejects all malformed, overlong and timeout histories.  This establishes a sound restricted co-nondeterministic \emph{certificate terminal}, not a coNTIME algorithm for all UNSAT formulas.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every accepted record authenticates all four original proper clauses encoding one of the stated XOR equations.  By (7), each original edge name occurs in the claimed equations exactly twice; odd charge then gives $1=0$.  Any satisfying assignment of $F$ would satisfy the authenticated subset, a contradiction.  The positive direction uses the actual original offsets and charges from the constructed family.  There are $2r^2=O(N/\log N)$ quartet records, each storing four $O(\log N)$-bit offsets and one bit of charge.  Counters, modular coordinate arithmetic, source line parsing, an offset index and parity accumulator take $O(\log N)$ bits at once.  A source scan plus constant-many charged reads of $O(\log N)$-bit names per record totals $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ elementary bit operations.  Before reading a record the verifier checks a uniform $N$-dependent cap; invalid offsets, aliases, wrong signs, late records or missing records reject.  There is no random access to previously guessed certificate bits.  This proof does not assert a low-space machine for recognizing an arbitrary renamed/unsorted isomorphic graph, or for all-UNSAT proof search.
\end{proof}

The grammar may also offer SCC, Horn, resolution, CP/PC and fixed-$A$ leaves, but no argument establishes a uniform $N^a$-bit accepted proof for every such source.
