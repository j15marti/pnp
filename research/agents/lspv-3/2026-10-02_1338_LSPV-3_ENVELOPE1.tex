\section*{Theorem ENVELOPE1}
Fix one deterministic exact SAT decider $A$ with certified runtime $T_A(s)\le s^{K+o(1)}$. Suppose three correct implementations of the same reconstruction obligation are available in the same declared machine model:
(D) a direct implementation with nonquery time $N^{a+o(1)}$, at most $N^{q+o(1)}$ calls to $A$, and query length at most $N^{b+o(1)}$;
(R) a RAMCOMP1 extension-search implementation with selector length $N^{\ell+o(1)}$ and inner model-matched compilation exponent $c_0$, giving $N^{\ell+c_0K^2+o(1)}$;
(F) an independently acquired SAME\_REALIZATION evaluator $F$ with acquisition/evaluation cost $N^{f+o(1)}$ and a model-matched counterexample SAT compilation of length $N^{c_F+o(1)}$.
Then the deterministic reconstruction time is bounded by the cheapest correct route:
\[
\boxed{T_{\rm rec}(N)\le N^{\min\{\max(a,q+bK),\;\ell+c_0K^2,\;\max(f,c_FK)\}+o(1)}}.
\]
All common preprocessing and postprocessing charges remain outside this minimum and must be charged separately.
\paragraph{Proof.}
For route D, sequentially execute the outer computation and substitute the same fixed $A$ at each query. The total query time is at most $N^{q+bK+o(1)}$, so total time is $N^{\max(a,q+bK)+o(1)}$. Route R is RAMCOMP1 plus $N^{\ell+o(1)}$ prefix decisions, hence $N^{\ell+c_0K^2+o(1)}$. Route F first acquires/evaluates the supplied strategy and then decides the compiled counterexample formula once with the same $A$, costing $N^{\max(f,c_FK)+o(1)}$. A deterministic machine may choose any one already proved-correct implementation, so its runtime is at most the minimum of the three upper bounds. No step composes mutually alternative routes. \(\square\)
