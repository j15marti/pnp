## ITERATE: Theorem 483.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED, exact paid-gate input census)

Take **any** acyclic list of $d$ globally fresh, unit-paid two-input conservative Boolean definitions. Each gate input is an original variable (possibly signed) or an earlier paid gate output; no free opaque wire. Ignore signs and directions when forming the **undirected simple** dependency graph $H$ on its $d$ gate-output vertices and the $s$ distinct original variables directly consumed by at least one gate. Add one undirected edge for each DISTINCT (gate output, input) dependency. Let $e=|E(H)|$, $c$ be the number of connected components of $H$ and $r=e-(s+d)+c$ its cyclomatic rank. Let $\delta=2d-e\ge0$, the deficit from repeated identical gate operands. For $d=0$, set all these quantities to zero. Then

$$
\boxed{s=d+c-r-\delta.}
$$

**Proof.** By construction, every gate has exactly two input incidences and its own new output name, whence $e\le2d$ and $\delta=2d-e$. The graph has $v=s+d$ vertices. For any finite undirected graph, its cycle-space dimension is $r=e-v+c\ge0$. Substitute $v=s+d$ and $e=2d-\delta$ and rearrange. This identity is unaffected by branch reconvergence or repeated uses of earlier derived gates. It is sharp: with $d$ gates arranged as a forest of $c$ binary gate trees with fresh original leaves, $r=\delta=0$ and $s=d+c$. With one connected component $s\le d+1$; with $d$ independent gates $s=2d$; repeated inputs or reconvergence strictly reduce $s$ by the exact indicated amounts. This counts **original coordinates used by DEF**, not all original variables occurring in a refutation.
