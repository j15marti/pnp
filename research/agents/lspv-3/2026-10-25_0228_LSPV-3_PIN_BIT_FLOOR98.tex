\section{Exact source encoding and pin semantics}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a connected cubic graph with even $v=|V|$, $e=3v/2$. Put one variable $x_f$ on each edge and impose $\oplus_{f\ni u}x_f=b_u$ at every vertex. Each 3-XOR equation is four proper ternary clauses; thus $T(G,b)$ has $n=e$ variables, $m=4v$ clauses and, under the fixed-width ordinary-record encoding,
\[
N=(16v+2)h_v,\qquad
h_v=\left\lceil\log_2(64(e+4v+1))\right\rceil
=\Theta(\log v).
\]
For any assigned original boundary variable $x$, the \emph{specified eager compiler} uses two distinct fresh auxiliaries $a_x,c_x$ and four proper clauses
\[
(\ell_x\vee a_x\vee c_x),\quad
(\ell_x\vee a_x\vee\neg c_x),\quad
(\ell_x\vee\neg a_x\vee c_x),\quad
(\ell_x\vee\neg a_x\vee\neg c_x),
\]
where $\ell_x=x$ for pin value 1 and $\ell_x=\neg x$ for value 0. The gadget enforces exactly $\ell_x=1$. Pins generated on both sides of a block are counted separately, even where they assign the same original variable; the receiving theorem is expressly about this \emph{fixed} compiler, not every conceivable encoding.

For a child with $m_c$ clauses and $n_c$ declared variables, the independently serializable ordinary-CNF bit length is
\[
N_c=(4m_c+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(n_c+m_c+1))\right\rceil.
\]
This must be distinguished from the \emph{tagged scoped-stream} accounting $(2+4n_c+4m_c)h_c$ sometimes used in the project's instrumented compiler. Neither is a free indexed virtual-source handle.

\begin{theorem}[PIN-CHILD-SERIALIZATION-FLOOR98, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Fix a family of connected cubic graphs with uniform edge expansion
$|\delta(U)|\ge h_0\min(|U|,v-|U|)$, $h_0>0$ independent of $v$.
For every charge vector $b$, every permutation of the \emph{unchanged, original-name} $4v$ clauses, and every consecutive clause-block partition with maximum block size $B(v)=o(v)$, some internal block boundary has at least
\[
w_v\ge h_0v/2-3B(v)=\Omega(v)
\]
live original edge-variable names. Any eager pinned child adjoining that cut which separately enforces each cut variable by the fixed four-clause/two-auxiliary pin gadget satisfies
\[
m_c\ge4w_v,\quad n_c\ge2w_v,\quad
N_c\ge(16w_v+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(6w_v+1))\right\rceil.
\]
Consequently $N_c=\Omega(v\log v)=\Omega(N)$, and
\[
\liminf_{v\to\infty}N_c/N\ge h_0/2
\]
for the displayed encoding, with the understanding that the actual ratio may be larger. In particular \emph{this explicit source-preserving eager pin compiler} cannot promise all ordinary children of length $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ for any fixed $\lambda<1$ across this source family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
EXPANDER-STREAM-ORDER97 proved the existence, for every clause order, of a cut with at least $h_0v/2$ edge variables appearing on both sides. Crossing one ternary clause changes the live set by at most three elements; moving to an adjacent block boundary loses at most $3B$ live names. Every pinned child adjoining that block boundary explicitly pins each of its $w_v$ original variables, using four new ternary clauses and two fresh local auxiliary variables per pin, irrespective of the selected truth values. The ordinary child therefore has at least $4w_v$ clauses and $2w_v$ local variables and must serialize the full input header and each clause record at word width $h_c\ge\lceil\log_2(64(6w_v+1))\rceil$. This proves the finite bound. Since $B=o(v)$, $w_v\ge(h_0/2-o(1))v$, so $h_c/h_v\to1$ from below and the displayed asymptotic ratio follows. Finally $N^{\lambda+o(1)}=o(N)$ for $\lambda<1$, contradicting the derived floor within this compiler contract.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reachability qualification.}
For even zero charge $b\equiv0$, setting every original edge bit to 0 satisfies the entire source and every consistent zero-pinned block. A \emph{complete eager reachable-frontier enumeration} therefore cannot dismiss the large-cut block as unreachable. For odd total charge the root is UNSAT and a selector might terminate earlier, so the formal serialization claim is conditional on generating a child at that boundary, not on a forced execution by every UNSAT solver.

\section{Exactly what this does not prove}
The expensive pinned child is a \emph{representation choice}. A paid source recognizer can validate the four-clause parity gadget at each vertex, recover $G,b$, and decide both labels from
\[
\S​​AT(T(G,b))\iff\bigoplus_{u\in V}b_u=0.
\]
The SAT relation has $2^{e-v+1}$ witnesses when its charge XOR is even; an odd charge gives none. A fixed program can output a constant-size proper-3CNF YES or NO instance \emph{on recognized sources} after parsing and validation. On unrecognized inputs, a correct general fallback is still needed and can inherit the original exponent. No universal favorable ARC edge is thereby produced.

The expansion lemma is a \emph{syntactic} lower bound for unchanged signed clause records with original variable names. It is neither invariant under Gaussian elimination, SAT-equivalent compilation, caching, recomputation, nor a lower bound on every adaptive algorithm. It forbids a restricted power-shrinking PIN92 implementation, not an existential all-input achievable-pair replacement.
