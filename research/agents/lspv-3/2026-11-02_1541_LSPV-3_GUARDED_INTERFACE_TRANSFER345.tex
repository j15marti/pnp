\section*{The fixed systems, not an upgraded oracle}
Fix ordinary Resolution $R_{\mathrm{Res}}$ with source CNF clauses free and one unit-weight derived node per binary resolvent, shared once in arbitrary DAGs. Separately retain the preselected $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ (LSPV-2, 12:16 PST): ordinary Resolution; exact two-premise Boolean AND3 factors of support at most three original variables; locally verified affine aliases; binary GF(2) row addition; charged local conversions; and paid conservative two-input gate definitions. Rule versions cannot be silently united. No global SAT/equivalence oracle is allowed.

For a fresh $z$ and any finite CNF $F$, put
\[
 H_F(z)=\bigwedge_{C\in F}(\neg z\vee C),\qquad P_F=H_F(z)\wedge z.
\]
The projected boundary of satisfiable $H_F$ is $\{0\}$ when $F$ is UNSAT and $\{0,1\}$ when $F$ is SAT. We refute pinned $P_F$, not satisfiable $H_F$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact unrestricted-DAG Resolution guard-transfer minimum]
For every finite UNSAT CNF $F$, allowing arbitrary sharing, adaptive order and free post-hoc optimal selection,
\[
 \boxed{D_{\mathrm{Res}}(H_F\vdash\neg z)=W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F),\qquad
 W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_F)=W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)+1.}
\]
$D$ counts the minimum derived binary Resolution nodes needed to reach the indicated conclusion; source axioms have zero weight.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lift each base source $C$ to $\neg z\vee C$ and lift every resolution inference on an original pivot. A $q$-node refutation of $F$ yields a $q$-node derivation of $\neg z$. Conversely, every source of $H_F$ contains $\neg z$ and no source contains positive $z$. Every derived clause therefore retains $\neg z$ and no pivot on $z$ is possible. Removing the common $\neg z$ from all lines and sources maps any derivation of $\neg z$ to a refutation of $F$ with identical derived-node count.

Add unit source $z$ and pivot it against $\neg z$ to obtain the second upper bound. For the lower bound, restrict any refutation of $P_F$ by $z=1$. Guarded sources restrict to $F$; source $z$ becomes true. $H_F$ alone is satisfiable by setting $z=0$, so the original refutation depends on source $z$. A dependency path from that positive-$z$ source to the empty clause must contain a pivot on $z$. Under restriction that pivot becomes its surviving other parent and is eliminated. Standard Resolution restriction only removes or preserves inferences, never adds them. The restricted proof refutes $F$ with at most $q-1$ derived nodes. Consequently $q\ge W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)+1$, regardless of DAG reconvergence. If $F$ already has an empty clause, both equalities hold with $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)=0$.
\end{proof}
