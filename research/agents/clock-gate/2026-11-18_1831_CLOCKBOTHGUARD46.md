## Four falsifiers and actual source model

Input: genuine ordinary CNF F(z,x_1,...,x_B) = AND_b F_b(z,x_b), where the sets x_b are private and pairwise disjoint once common z is fixed. Proposed layout R<=n^.195 assignment rows, B<=n^.65 blocks of size L<=n^.35. Falsifiers: incomplete row coverage; overlapping private variables; invalid/time-exceeded/space-exceeded proofs asserting the wrong label; fastest correct nondeterministic path masquerading as uniform bounded-time computation.

**Theorem 46A (PROVED/EXACT classical factorization).** F is SAT iff exists z, for every b exists x_b satisfying F_b(z,x_b). Equivalently, F is UNSAT iff for every z exists b such that F_b(z,x_b) is UNSAT. Proof: fixing z makes all private witnesses concatenable; negate the existential SAT condition.

**Sound source-authentic specialists.** SAT certificate: verify full original-source assignment against every clause. UNSAT certificate: for *every* z, give a named original block and an individually source-authenticated restricted resolution refutation, checking every original premise, parent, pivot, resolvent and empty root. Valid proof certifies only its own label. Bad record, unverified negative answer, resource timeout or space breach yields UNKNOWN/REJECT-CERTIFICATE, **never the opposite language answer**. Without a polynomial bound on complete UNSAT covers this is a certificate verifier, not an all-CNF polynomial decider. All-UNSAT polynomial proof coverage would imply NP=coNP, not established. An explicit universal row list requires R>=2^|z|, hence |z|<=.195 log_2 n if R<=n^.195. Uncharged row compression is not allowed.

**Small counterexamples.** (z OR x) AND (z OR NOT x) is SAT globally but its lone row z=0 is UNSAT. Blocks (x) and (NOT x) are each satisfiable but overlapping variables make the conjunction UNSAT. A complete two-row UNSAT source uses F1=(z OR x) AND (z OR NOT x), F2=(NOT z OR y) AND (NOT z OR NOT y). At z=0 the first block refutes, and at z=1 the second does, each by a three-node resolution DAG. Each unconditioned original block remains satisfiable.

**Theorem 46B (PROVED/EXACT conditional on uniform complete records).** Suppose two total, uniform, sound verifiers, SAT and UNSAT, have an accepted correct-polarity record of <=k(N) bits on *every* source, and every record including invalid ones is checked with all child/source/replay/materialization costs in time <=T_V(N) and peak writable space <=S_V(N). Exhaustive sequential enumeration of both polarities and all strings of length <=k gives a deterministic decider, using time O(2^(k+1)(T_V+k+N_setup))+T_init and space <=S_V+O(k+log N)+S_init if records are generated on the fly and workspace recycled. An externally held record/table/strategy must pay actual access and physical storage. If k=alpha log_2 N, the enumeration incurs a factor N^alpha. Free *verified* polarity means two charged verifier runs. Free *unverified* answer advice is not a uniform, sound decision procedure.

A guessed successful SAT witness is nondeterministic SAT, already in NP. A guessed valid UNSAT certificate is nondeterministic UNSAT (equivalently a co-nondeterministic SAT machine after flipping *every* branch answer, including malformed records and timeouts). The worst **all-branch** time/space is charged, not the cheapest accepting path. This branch-flip theorem is already project LSPV-5 POLARITY-CLOCK160, not new here. A valid alternating adaptive strategy needs fully bounded entire universal branches and a genuine verifiable strategy description, not one convenient path.
