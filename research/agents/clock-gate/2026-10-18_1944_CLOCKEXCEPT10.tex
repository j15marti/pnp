\section*{Exact change-of-measure and rejection-sampling barriers}
Fix a truth table f, finite seed space R, baseline distribution Q, and cheap-image label set
I_{t,r}=\{H_r(g):\mathrm{CC}(g)\le t\}.
Let E_f=\{r:H_r(f)\notin I_{t,r}\} and suppose Q(E_f)\le\delta<1. An adaptive distribution P_f may depend on f, but remains supported within Q.

\begin{theorem}[EXCEPTIONAL-SEED-KL10]
If P_f(E_f)\ge1-\eta, then, in binary logarithms,
\[
D_{\rm KL}(P_f\|Q)\ge d_2(P_f(E_f)\|Q(E_f))
\ge(1-\eta)\log_2(1/\delta)-1.
\]
Also \(\mathrm{ess\,sup}_r P_f(r)/Q(r)\ge (1-\eta)/\delta\).
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof: Data processing (log-sum inequality) for the partition (E_f,R\setminus E_f) yields binary relative entropy. Its expansion is \(q\log_2(1/p)+(1-q)\log_2(1/(1-p))-h_2(q)\); retain the nonnegative middle term and use \(h_2\le1\). The likelihood-ratio inequality follows by averaging the ratio over E_f. If Q(E_f)=0, no absolutely continuous selector can succeed.

\begin{theorem}[INDEPENDENT-TRIAL-EXCEPTION-FLOOR10]
If an algorithm draws at most T independent samples from Q and outputs one sampled seed, even allowing a free oracle to recognize E_f, its chance of producing a seed in E_f is at most T\delta. Hence success 1-\eta requires T\ge(1-\eta)/\delta.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof: Union bound over all baseline draws. Adaptive stopping cannot increase the event that an exceptional candidate was drawn.

\noindent Conditional corollary: in the explicit small-bias affine family of CLOCKSEED9, at width b=Theta(t), t=N^tau, and b\le(1-tau-zeta)t for fixed zeta>0, the baseline chance that any fixed f avoids the weight-v small-circuit section is 2^{-Omega(t)}, with v=floor((t-n+1)/n). The true image-NO event is a subset of section avoidance. A polynomially bounded number of independent baseline-seed tests therefore cannot rescue NO soundness. A direct source-conditioned exceptional-seed algorithm is NOT ruled out, even if its seed distribution has large relative entropy from Q.

\section*{General nonlinear transcript sparse-section theorem}
Let N=2^n. Fix all public coins omega of a deterministic source-bit-oracle transducer Phi_omega. It may compute arbitrary nonlinear functions, query bits adaptively, retain state across outputs, choose source-dependent seeds and generate provenance. It obtains source information exclusively by individually charged bit queries. Let S_{f,omega} be all distinct source addresses queried in the run on f and put q=|S_{f,omega}|.

\begin{theorem}[NONLINEAR-TRANSCRIPT-SECTION10]
For every f,omega, there exists a truth table g such that
\[
\Phi_\omega(g)=\Phi_\omega(f),\qquad
\mathrm{CC}(g)\le n(1+\lfloor q/2\rfloor).
\]
Consequently, for I_{t,omega}=\{\Phi_\omega(g):\mathrm{CC}(g)\le t\}, all source outputs with q satisfying t\ge n(1+\lfloor q/2\rfloor) are image-YES, even for nonlinear source-adaptive seed selectors. A NO image requires violating that inequality.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof: Record all queried addresses and answers during the run on f. Fill every unqueried source position with the majority value among queried bits, keeping the observed values. The new table g differs from a constant at at most floor(q/2) positions. Identical responses inductively force the same entire adaptive computation, including any seed selector and output. Shared input negations and a point-DNF of the exceptional positions, or its complement, require at most n(1+floor(q/2)) fan-in-two gates. This is input-specific and handles sequential nonlinear oracle computations without polynomial-degree hypotheses.

\noindent Corollary: if a b-output-bit quotient uses at most q_0 source probes per output including seed/provenance generation, nontrivial image-NO labels require t<n(1+floor(b q_0/2)). For b=N^{a+o(1)}, q_0=N^{d+o(1)}, t=N^{tau+o(1)}, this requires a+d\ge tau-o(1). Full one-way source sweeps evade this short-query premise.

\section*{Hostile controls and exact tests}
Rarity is not runtime or mutual information: E_f=\{r=\mathrm{prefix}_r(f)\} has baseline rarity 2^{-r}, yet a selector reads r bits and outputs that rare seed. A fixed rare seed for all f also gives no source mutual information. A source-conditioned seed recomputed on a sparse completion can differ if its own source queries were not included; the theorem charges those queries explicitly. Uncharged SAT decisions, free source-as-seed inputs, 2CNF/Horn/affine escapes, parity, hashing promises, randomization error and one-work-tape fusion were not promoted.

Exact enumeration of binary matrices: (N,b,v,f)=(6,2,2,7), (8,1,3,0), (8,2,3,0) yields exceptional counts 189/4096, 1/256 and 765/65536, consistent with 2^b/binom(N,v). In 1000 seeded nonlinear three-probe-per-bit transducer trials (N=32,b=5), zero and majority sparse completions preserved every output; the largest transcript covered 15 bits and mean minority exceptions were 4.934. These are COMPUTATIONAL finite controls only. Full report and source/raw data were preserved as conversation artifacts, subject to GitHub publication.
