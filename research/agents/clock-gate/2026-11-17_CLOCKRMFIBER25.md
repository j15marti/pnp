## Exact theorem: every attained fiber of a low-degree map is large
Let f in F_2^N be the genuine Boolean truth table on n index bits. Let H(f)=(P_1(f),...,P_m(f)) in F_2^m, where the P_i are the *entire* deterministic source-to-output functions in unique multilinear algebraic normal form (ANF), including all source-dependent selectors and coefficients, with degrees d_i>=0. Set D=sum_i d_i, and Y_t={f:CC(f)<=t} for standard fan-in-two Boolean circuit complexity.

For any attainable y in im(H), define
Q_y(f)=product_i (1+P_i(f)+y_i) over F_2. This equals 1 precisely when H(f)=y. Its multilinear degree e_y is <=min(N,D). It is nonzero because y is attained. The binary Reed-Muller minimum-distance fact gives

    |H^{-1}(y)| >= 2^(N-e_y) >= 2^max(0,N-D).

**Elementary proof of the imported fact:** a nonzero multilinear polynomial Q of degree e=0 is the constant one. For e>=1, choose a variable x_i in a degree-e monomial, and write Q=x_i A+B, with A a nonzero polynomial of degree e-1 on N-1 variables. Whenever A=1, Q(0,.) and Q(1,.) differ, contributing at least one 1. Induction gives at least 2^((N-1)-(e-1)) such settings. Hence Q has >=2^(N-e) ones. Applying to the fiber indicator proves the bound. This is equivalent to the binary special case of Warning's second theorem, NOT a newly discovered coding theorem.

## Exact corollary: necessary near-maximal total source ANF degree
For a fixed finite fan-in-two basis with b possible gate functions, circuit counting gives

    |Y_t| <= U(n,t)=(t+1)(n+t+2)[b(n+t+2)^2]^t.

If N-D>log_2 U(n,t), every attained fiber has more than |Y_t| members, so contains a source with CC>t. In particular y=H(0) is simultaneously generated by the source YES constant function and a source NO function. Therefore **universal NO exclusion fails**, irrespective of the image recognition procedure or circuit witness budget. Contrapositively any exact-promise-pure polynomial-output map must have

    D >= N-log_2 U(n,t).

If d_i<=d, necessarily m*d >= N-O(t log(n+t)). For t=N^beta with fixed beta<1, any exact-pure map requires D >= (1-o(1))N. A contracting cascade with m_j<=N^(a lambda^j+o(1)) and d_j<=N^(eta+o(1)), eta<1, eventually violates NO exclusion at a *fixed clock-independent stage* j satisfying a lambda^j+eta<1. The stage is chosen without knowing a PH-runtime exponent. This is a **restricted algebraic necessity**, not a general circuit lower bound or a P-versus-NP proof.

## Why this is stronger than the earlier polynomial sparse-section guard
THRES-POLYFIBER10, MAGRED-SEEDPORTFOLIO22 and CLOCKMIXEDCORE17 use Chevalley-Warning to construct a low-support representative with point-DNF size O(nD) when D is small compared with the YES-circuit threshold. The new argument needs only D<N-O(t log(n+t)), and can apply even when D is far larger than t, because it creates an enormous fiber and uses counting to find a NO member rather than constructing a YES representative. Repository searches for Ax-Katz, Reed-Muller fiber and Warning's second theorem found related sparse-section results, not an identical explicit CLOCKASYMTRANSFER24 total-degree condition. No originality or priority claim for classical ingredients.

## Costed example and falsifiers
The map H(f)=XOR_{r=0}^{N/2-1}(f_{2r} AND f_{2r+1}) has raw-source ANF degree 2. For n>=1, a deterministic **headerless one-way-input, one-work-tape** scanner reads the N actual source bits in order, retaining a pending bit and an accumulator, and writes one output bit at EOF. It costs O(N) time and O(1) writable workspace, but its output fibers mix low- and high-circuit-complexity truth tables, proving it cannot be the requisite pure quotient. Conversely OR of all N source bits has ANF degree N and singleton zero fiber. Its high degree does NOT establish purity. The general algebraic theorem does not assert that arbitrary dense ANFs are computable in near-linear time, nor that their supplied-circuit equality is jointly PH-low.
