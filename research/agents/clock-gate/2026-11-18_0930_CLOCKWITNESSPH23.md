## Definitions and theorem 23.1: PH-WITNESS-SEARCH
Let n be an address width, N=2^n, and H_n:{0,1}^N -> Y_n a fixed uniform semantic quotient with legal target labels of length at most n^a for one constant a. Fix a polynomial-time-decodable padded n-input circuit encoding with descriptions C of length exactly L(n)<=n^b for one constant b. Define the JOINT relation
E(n,y,C) := [C is a legal padded n-input circuit AND H_n(TT(C))=y].
Assume E belongs to one fixed Sigma_j^P or Pi_j^P level, uniformly in n,y,C. Assume every attainable y has at least one such C satisfying E. These hypotheses are JOINT and stage-independent, not one unrelated verifier per later clock c.

Then an FP^Sigma_{j+1}^P algorithm G, on (1^n,y,i), returns the i-th bit of ONE globally consistent circuit truth table g_y=TT(C_y), with H_n(g_y)=y. It uses at most L+1 extension queries and polynomial local decoding/evaluation work; its chosen C_y depends only on n,y, not i.

Proof. For a circuit description prefix p of length <=L, define EXT(n,y,p) iff exists suffix s of the remaining description length with E(n,y,ps). If E is Sigma_j, this lies in Sigma_j (at worst Sigma_{j+1}); if Pi_j, this lies in Sigma_{j+1}. Query EXT(empty), then for each of L description bits query EXT(p0), appending 0 on YES, otherwise 1. The invariant EXT(p) holds, and after L steps the chosen circuit satisfies E. Evaluating it at i is polynomial in n+L. Every invocation uses the SAME lex-first C_y. QED.

Under P=NP, every fixed PH level collapses to P. Hence G is uniformly polynomial-time in n+|y| with one finite exponent independent of later decoder clock c. This is a conditional complexity-class statement, not a free SAT oracle in the one-tape compiler.

## Corollary 23.2: HARD-FORCED-BIT VERIFIER BARRIER
Suppose a PSPACE-complete predicate V(x) reduces via polynomial-time x->(1^n,y_x,i_x) to a FORCED source bit: for EVERY f in H_n^{-1}(y_x), f[i_x]=V(x). Also suppose every such y_x admits one polynomial-length circuit witness, and the joint supplied-witness E is in fixed PH. Then PSPACE=PH.

Proof: Use G to compute the forced bit in P^Sigma_{j+1}^P, which lies in PH. Thus PSPACE subseteq PH, while PH subseteq PSPACE is known. This is CONDITIONAL: no collapse is claimed established. If also P=NP, these joint premises imply P=PSPACE.

Apply to CLOCKMASKEDINVERSE22: H(f)=(D,a XOR TQBF(D), adjacency(h)), y=(D,b,q). Every legal target has a small point-DNF preimage; image membership is P; nevertheless the mandatory source bit is a=b XOR TQBF(D). In that example the supplied circuit equality predicate is PSPACE-complete, not PH-low (unless PSPACE=PH). This explains exactly why EASY IMAGE MEMBERSHIP cannot be substituted for the JOINT PH verifier in the proposed P=NP proof chain.

## Corollary 23.3: CHEAP SECTION / PROMISE COLLISION
If each attainable image y has a source circuit preimage with CC<=s(n), then H(Y_s)=im(H), where Y_s={f:CC(f)<=s}. Any nonempty source NO class disjoint from Y_s necessarily maps entirely to target labels also attained by YES sources. Thus exact two-sided MCSP promise preservation through image membership alone fails. This conclusion is unconditional and does NOT depend on extracting the small source efficiently. Additional authenticated target data not factored through H requires a separate analysis.

## Constructive PH-low ordered-feature control
For N>=4 let H(f)=(f[0], OR_i f[i], OR_{i<N-1}(f[i] AND f[i+1])). Its complete image is exactly five labels 000,010,011,110,111, represented respectively by supports empty, {1}, {1,2}, {0}, {0,1}. Every image has <=2 support bits and an O(n)-gate point-DNF circuit. Supplied-circuit equality is in P^NP: f[0] is direct evaluation, OR is SAT(C), adjacency is SAT(C(i) AND C(i+1)). This is an actual positive control for the conditional extraction lemma and a negative control for MCSP promise purity.
