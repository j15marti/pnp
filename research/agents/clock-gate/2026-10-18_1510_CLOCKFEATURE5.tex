\section{MODEL}
Fix $x\in\{0,1\}^{M}$ and one decoder description $d$. On each coordinate $i$, $d$ prepares one deterministic adaptive tree probing at most two seed bits, without carrying mutable state to another coordinate. Set
\[
C_{d,x}(z)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{M}[U_d(i;z)=x_i].
\]
Partition seed $z=(w,y,a)$ into witness bits $w\in\{0,1\}^n$, claimed feature bits $y\in\{0,1\}^q$, and arbitrary auxiliary bits $a$. The exact consistency condition is
\[
\forall w\,\forall y:\quad (\exists a\,C_{d,x}(w,y,a))\Longleftrightarrow y=h(w).
\tag{1}
\]
Here $d,x$ are fixed before $w,y,a$. This is strictly stronger than the decision-only ULR2 membership condition $\exists d,z\;G_d(z)=x$.

\begin{lemma}[TWO-PROBE-PP-CLOSURE5]
For fixed $d,x$, $C_{d,x}$ is a 2CNF. Its projection under arbitrary auxiliary existential seed bits is also 2CNF.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first probe selects between two branches, each of which tests at most one more seed bit. Equality with $x_i$ is therefore a conjunction of at most two clauses, each with at most two literals. Conjunction over $i$ is a 2CNF. Eliminating a seed variable by resolution produces resolvents of width at most two. Equivalently, majority preservation is closed under existential projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FUNCTIONAL-BIJUNCTIVE-GRAPH5, PROVED/EXACT]
If the full graph of a total Boolean map $h:\{0,1\}^{n}\to\{0,1\}^{q}$ is 2CNF-definable, allowing arbitrarily many existential auxiliary variables, then each $h_j$ is a constant, $w_k$, or $\neg w_k$. Conversely all such maps have 2CNF-definable graphs. Consequently (1) cannot hold if any feature coordinate is a genuinely nonlinear Boolean function of unrestricted witness bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the 2CNF graph to $(w,y_j)$, which gives the graph of a total single-output Boolean function $f(w)$. Any input-only clause must be a tautology because every $w$ has a permitted output. Unless $f$ is constant, there can be no unit clause forcing $y$. The remaining nontrivial clauses have forms $(y\vee\ell)$ or $(\neg y\vee\ell)$ for input literals $\ell$. Clauses of the first form prohibit $y=0$ on their coordinate halfcubes $\{\ell(w)=0\}$; second-form clauses prohibit $y=1$ on their halfcubes. Totality and uniqueness say the unions of these two families are nonempty, disjoint, and cover the entire Boolean cube. A coordinate halfcube from one family is disjoint from one in the other only if they use the same input coordinate with opposite fixed bit values. Therefore all halfcubes of each family coincide with one halfcube, and $f$ is a signed coordinate. Constants and signed-coordinate graphs are encoded by unit clauses or two binary implications.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[NONLINEAR-FEATURE-AS-SEED5, PROVED/EXACT]
Suppose $B(D,i)=T(i,h(D))$ for $i\in[M]$, where $h(D)$ is any $q(M)$-bit nonlinear feature extractor, $q(M)$ is source-independent and uniformly computable, and $T$ is computed by the SAME fixed uniform adaptive two-probe program $u$ on feature bits. If $q(M)\le S(M)$, the entire program/parameter metadata fits the $D_{\max}(M)=\lfloor\alpha\log_2M\rfloor$-bit description budget, and its coordinate preparation fits $(\log M)^{c_0}$ time/space for one fixed $c_0$, then
\[
\forall c\ge c_0\;\forall D:\quad B(D,\cdot)\in\mathrm{ULR2}[c].
\tag{2}
\]
This holds without knowing how to compute or invert $h(D)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose existential seed $z=h(D)$ padded with zeros and choose decoder $u$, identically for every source. On every coordinate it outputs $T(i,z)=B(D,i)$. The compiler does not evaluate or invert the hash. No YES/NO source semantics survive.
\end{proof}

\section{CONSTRUCTION, FALSIFICATION, AND CONTROL}
A concrete fixed clock-independent PSPACE (in fact P) map takes a five-bit $D$, a seven-bit nonlinear feature vector
\[
h(D)=(D_0\oplus D_1\oplus D_2,\ D_0D_1,\ D_2\lor D_3,\
\operatorname{maj}(D_0,D_1,D_4),\ D_4,\ D_1\oplus D_3D_4,\ \neg D_0\land D_2)
\]
and produces $M=97$ output bits $B(D,i)=T(i,h(D))$. The decoder probes first at $(7i+3)\bmod7$ and adaptively probes second at $(5i+1)\bmod7$ or $(3i+2)\bmod7$, XORing the two replies and the parity of $i$. All sources, regardless of SAT/UNSAT labels, produce members of this two-probe range. It is a negative example, not the required gate.

A hypothetical exact consistency check for $y=\operatorname{XOR}_3(w)$, $y=w_1\land w_2$, or a nontrivial hash is impossible by the functional-graph theorem, even with auxiliary seed bits, conditional shields and arbitrary clock time. This does not rule out nonlinear *external* witness recovery, restricted-domain encodings, decision-only constructions, stochastic promises, or target classes of higher locality. Such escapes require their own resource and soundness proofs; no SHA/collision security premise is invoked.
