\section{Exact construction}
Let all \(4t\) variables \(a_i,b_i,c_j,d_j\) be distinct, \(t\ge1\).
Put \(A_i=a_i\vee b_i\), \(B_j=c_j\vee d_j\), and
\[
P_t=\left(\bigwedge_i A_i\right)\vee\left(\bigwedge_j B_j\right),\qquad
F_t(z)=\bigwedge_i(z\vee A_i)\wedge\bigwedge_j(\neg z\vee B_j).
\]
Then \(\exists z\,F_t(z)\equiv P_t\), whereas exact auxiliary-free
projection is \(\bigwedge_{i,j}(A_i\vee B_j)\), with \(t^2\) width-four
clauses. The \(t^2\) optimal auxiliary-free clause count was previously
proved by LSPV-1 ARC207; it is not a new claim of this report.

\begin{theorem}[No short implicate and tight auxiliary count]
No non-tautological signed clause of width at most three is an implicate
of \(P_t\). Every 3CNF whose existential projection onto the \(4t\)
original names is \(P_t\) therefore needs at least one auxiliary
variable; one auxiliary suffices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any non-tautological clause \(D\) of width at most three, set to zero
all variables appearing positively in \(D\) and set all other variables
to one. The assignment falsifies \(D\) and has at most three zeros.
Falsifying \(P_t\) requires a full \(A_i\) pair and a disjoint full
\(B_j\) pair to be zero, requiring at least four zeros. Consequently
\(P_t\) is true on the assignment falsifying \(D\), so \(D\) is no
implicate. A no-auxiliary 3CNF representing \(P_t\) would have no
non-tautological clause, hence be a tautology, contradiction.
With a fresh \(w\), the proper 3CNF
\[
Q_t(w)=\bigwedge_i(w\vee A_i)\wedge\bigwedge_j(\neg w\vee B_j)
\]
satisfies \(\exists w\,Q_t(w)=P_t\), attaining one auxiliary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Byte-identical one-child fixed point]
Define \(C\) on the canonical \(F_t\) family by forgetting \(z\),
taking the compact factorization \(P_t\), and rebuilding \(Q_t(w)\)
with \(w\) assigned the old pivot's numeric identifier and the original
clause order. Then \(C(F_t)=F_t\) as a physical encoded string and
\(C^r(F_t)=F_t\) for every \(r\ge1\). The map is polynomial-time and
answer-preserving but provides no strict encoded-length descent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every output clause, polarity, variable identifier, header field and
record order is identical after the designated selector renaming.
Induction yields identity under arbitrary repetition.
\end{proof}

\section{Compact circuit versus physical ordinary SAT}
A direct shared circuit for \(P_t\) uses \(2t\) initial OR gates,
\(2t-2\) AND gates, and one final OR, totaling \(G=4t-1\) gates
and \(2G=8t-2\) gate-input references. The fixed three-clause
bi-implication Tseitin compiler with one pinned root has
\[
m_T=12t-2,\qquad L_T=28t-6,\qquad v_T=8t-1.
\]
The original formula has \(2t\) clauses, \(6t\) literal occurrences,
and \(4t+1\) variables. Hence both original and Tseitin child have
\(\Theta(t\log t)\) physical indexed length. The latter is not
\(N^\lambda\) for any fixed \(\lambda<1\); in fact the given
Tseitin construction has larger length at every tested \(t\).
Using one private auxiliary to split each four-literal resolvent
into two proper width-three clauses instead yields \(2t^2\) clauses
and \(t^2\) new variables.

\begin{proposition}[Dominant unrelated source]
Let \(R\) be an arbitrary disjoint ordinary 3CNF and
\(F_R=R\wedge F_1\). Since \(F_1\) is satisfiable,
\(\mathrm{SAT}(F_R)=\mathrm{SAT}(R)\); the input family is NP-hard.
Any local successor that literally retains all \(R\) records
has \(N_{\rm child}\ge N_R\) while
\(N_{\rm parent}=N_R+O(\log N_R)\). Such a successor
cannot guarantee \(N_{\rm child}\le N_{\rm parent}^\lambda\)
for any fixed \(0<\lambda<1\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The append gadget has two constant-count clauses and five fresh variable
identifiers. The record-copy hypothesis lower-bounds child length.
Then \(N_R>(N_R+O(\log N_R))^\lambda\) eventually. No claim is
made against a global semantic solver that simplifies \(R\).
\end{proof}
