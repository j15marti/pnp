\textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN.}
This report proves a restricted exact preprocessing theorem for a class of 2CNF plus k genuine ternary clauses.
For each vertex v use Boolean variables $x_{v,0},x_{v,1},x_{v,2}$ and exactly-one constraints consisting of one positive ternary and three negative binary clauses. A permutation edge $u\to v$ labeled $\pi\in S_3$ contributes the six binary implications enforcing $c_v=\pi(c_u)$. An inequality edge forbidding a perfect matching $\rho\in S_3$ contributes the three clauses $(\neg x_{u,a}\vee\neg x_{v,\rho(a)})$, $a=0,1,2$.
The signed-clause normalizer identifies disjoint positive ternaries, recovers groups, checks every binary edge pattern, and regenerates the exact complete clause multiset. An unsuccessful recognition attempt uses the general 2CNF-plus-k fallback and does not presume the restricted structure.

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted theorem.}
Build a spanning forest of permutation edges, and for each vertex v obtain its transport permutation $p_v$ from its component root. Nontree permutation edges and internal inequality edges restrict the root domain $D_j\subseteq\{0,1,2\}$. A cross-component inequality edge contributes the exact compatibility relation
\[
Q_{ij}=\{(a,b)\in D_i\times D_j:p_v(b)\ne\rho(p_u(a))\}.
\]
Original satisfying Boolean assignments and satisfying assignments of this root-color CSP are in bijection. Proof: each one-hot triple specifies a unique color; the permutation edges determine every group color from its component root; remaining edge clauses are exactly the listed unary restrictions and binary relations, without omitted shared source variables.
If the quotient graph is a forest, its exact bottom-up recurrence is
\[
M_j(a)=[a\in D_j]\wedge\bigwedge_{\ell\text{ child of }j}\bigvee_{b=0}^2
\bigl([(a,b)\in Q_{j\ell}]\wedge M_\ell(b)\bigr).
\]
The formula is SAT iff each quotient-tree root has a nonempty feasible state. This recurrence gives checkable SAT witnesses or UNSAT message tables. Exact clause sorting, forest discovery, transport and verification use $O((n+m)\log(n+m+2))$ deterministic word operations and $O(n+m)$ peak words, counting all source reads and replay.

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT joined hostile pair.}
Take two t-vertex identity-path cycles. Close the first with transposition (01), fixing color 2. Close the second with transposition (01), fixing color 2, for the UNSAT input; close it with (02), fixing color 1, for the SAT input. Connect the first vertices by one identity inequality edge. Every local cycle is individually uniquely SAT. Their source-shared join is respectively UNSAT or uniquely SAT. Both have $n=6t$ Boolean variables, $k=2t$ ternaries, $m=20t+3$ clauses and quotient size $J=2$. The minimum strong syntactic 2CNF backdoor is $k$ due to disjoint positive ternaries. The charged forest algorithm nonetheless decides both labels in polynomial time.

\textbf{KNOWN NP-complete endpoint.}
Without permutation edges and with identity inequality edges this formula is exactly a three-coloring encoding of any source graph G, using $n=3|V(G)|$, $k=|V(G)|$, and $m=4|V(G)|+3|E(G)|$. Satisfying assignments biject with proper colorings. This is a polynomial reduction from the known NP-complete 3-coloring problem, not a SAT time lower bound.

\textbf{Complete general fallback.}
Selecting one variable per not-yet-covered ternary clause gives a set S with $|S|\le k$. All $2^{|S|}$ assignments leave 2CNF, solved by SCC. Each UNSAT child has contradictory implication paths or an empty clause, with unsuccessful branches and reconstruction charged. Overall $O(2^k(n+m))$ deterministic word work is an upper bound. Three globally forced-zero padding variables convert shorter clauses into proper 3CNF while preserving one unique extension for each satisfying original assignment.

\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL.}
The deterministic stdlib audit with seed 202610231512 tested 187 inputs with 681 checks and zero failures. For the matched $t=70$ pair: $n=420$, $k=140$, $m=1403$, $J=2$, and both labels were decided by forest DP. Brute-force independent controls, source renaming, deleted clauses, padded 2SAT, Horn, XOR, cycle and graph-coloring inputs were checked. This does not prove asymptotic hardness.

\textbf{Resource ledger.}
Under a fixed four-word clause encoding with $w=\lceil\log_2(16(n+m+1))\rceil$, $N=4w(m+1)$ bits. The discovery, normalization, verification and continuation costs are separately bounded by $O((n+m)\log(n+m))$, $O(n+m)$, $O((n+m)\log(n+m))$ and $O(n+m)$ for the recognized forest class. This is an accounting identity and upper-bound theorem. General compact certificates, source acquisition, alternative algorithms and decide-first normalization are not ruled out.
