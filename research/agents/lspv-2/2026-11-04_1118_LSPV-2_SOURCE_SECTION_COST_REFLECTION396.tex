\section*{Exact model and new lemma}
Ordinary Resolution is binary, shared DAG, one paid inference per resolvent; source clauses are free. Let $F(X)$ be UNSAT, $D(X,Y)$ any CNF bank, and $a$ a total assignment to fresh $Y$. Denote by $G_a$ the remaining clauses of $D$ under $Y=a$ after dropping satisfied clauses. Define $q_a(F,D)$ as the minimum paid shared-DAG Resolution nodes needed to derive from $F$ some clause $H_G\subseteq G$ for each $G\in G_a$.
\begin{theorem}
For all such $F,D,a$,
\[
0\le W_{\rm Res}(F)-W_{\rm Res}(F\land D)\le q_a(F,D).
\]
In particular if every residual $G$ is subsumed by an original source clause, $q_a=0$ and the Resolution minima agree.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The source refutation remains available after bank addition. Restrict a shortest extended refutation by $Y=a$; closure under restrictions yields an $F\land G_a$ refutation with no additional nodes. Prepend a shared $q_a$-node derivation of source-derived subclauses $H_G$. Replaying the DAG under these stronger source clauses is no harder: a Resolution pivot is either retained and resolved once or loses a pivot in one strengthened parent, which then subsumes its intended resolvent and bypasses the node. Sharing is preserved and the empty clause survives.
\end{proof}
\section*{ITERATE I: nonneutral conservative AND2 bank}
Let $P_h$ be explicit unary PHP with $h+1$ pigeons and $h$ holes: $h(h+1)$ variables, $(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses. Add a fresh old-source unit $(\neg u)$. For every PHP variable $x_i$, define fresh $y_i=u\land x_i$ by three conservative AND2 facets
\[
(\neg y_i\lor u),\quad(\neg y_i\lor x_i),\quad(y_i\lor\neg u\lor\neg x_i).
\]
This bank has no universally neutral fixed assignment to all outputs: with $u=0$ every $y_i=0$, whereas with $u=1$ and all $x_i=1$, every $y_i=1$. Every old assignment nevertheless has a unique gate completion. The constant $y_i=0$ section leaves only $(\neg u\lor\neg x_i)$, all subsumed by the old source unit $(\neg u)$. Hence $q_a=0$. Restricting $u=0$ further gives
\[
\boxed{W_{\rm Res}(P_h\land\neg u\land D_h)
=W_{\rm Res}(P_h\land\neg u)=W_{\rm Res}(P_h).}
\]
The bank has $h(h+1)$ auxiliary outputs and $3h(h+1)$ clauses, adding $O(h^2\log h)$ bits; the full encoded input remains $\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. Haken's exponential ordinary Resolution lower bound persists through this nonneutral conservative bank. This says nothing about mixed $R_{\rm mix341}$ or arbitrary SAT algorithms.
\section*{ITERATE II: Cook-section backtranslation obstruction}
Haken (1985) establishes $W_{\rm Res}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}$ for all large $h$, against every unrestricted ordinary shared-DAG Resolution refutation of the explicit unary source. Cook (1976) provides polynomial Extended Resolution refutations. Collect its polynomial-size complete acyclic conservative gate definitions as free clauses in bank $E_h$. Ordinary binary Resolution can refute the extended source in at most $h^d$ inferences. Applying the new lemma to EVERY constant auxiliary assignment $a$ gives
\[
\boxed{q_a(P_h,E_h)\ge2^{ch}-h^d=2^{\Omega(h)}.}
\]
Let $M_h=\operatorname{poly}(h)$ be the number of surviving bank clauses. Concatenating separate strongest-subclause derivations gives $q_a\le\sum_{G\in G_a} t_G$, so some residual $G$ has individual minimum derivation cost $t_G\ge(2^{ch}-h^d)/M_h=2^{\Omega(h)}$. Standard fan-in-two extension facets contain at most three literals, at least one an auxiliary gate, so full gate assignment leaves at most two old literals. Choosing $a$ as outputs evaluated on the all-zero old input ensures all restricted gate clauses can be satisfied by that input; thus a hard residual can be taken nonempty. This rules out cheap constant-section erasure in strict Resolution, NOT alternative compact translations, arbitrary mixed proofs, or machine decision shortcuts.
