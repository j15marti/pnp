Thus the unsigned literal degree prefix preceding record index $r$ and field $f$ is exactly
\[
 D(r,f)=15wr+5\min(r,L)+8\max(0,r-L)+15\min(f,w).
\]
Every comparator gate, equality guard and unit/binary padding block has a source-independent fixed clause template. Their full-block unsigned degree sums are arithmetic. The only output-wire external degree comes from one next-stage comparator or zero, one, or two final adjacent-equality blocks, determined by the fixed bitonic pairing. Hence the degree prefix $D(x)=\sum_{y<x}\deg_{E(F)}(y)$ is computable for all intermediate IDs in polylogarithmic time and space and satisfies $D(V+1)=3M$.
For each intermediate variable $x$, source-indexed reverse incidence is obtained by visiting its source/pin/producer comparator block, at most one next-stage comparator, and at most two final key-adjacency blocks.
