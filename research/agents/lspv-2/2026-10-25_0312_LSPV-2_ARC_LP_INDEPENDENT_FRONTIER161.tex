\section{Exact theorem: an extensive pinned LP blind spot}
For a proper signed 3CNF \(F\), every clause has exactly three literals
with three distinct source-variable identities, and \(P_F\) denotes the
\emph{basic real clause relaxation}
\[
P_F=\left\{x\in[0,1]^{V(F)}:
 \sum_{\ell\in C} \ell(x)\ge1\quad\text{for every }C\in F\right\},
\quad
\ell(x)=\begin{cases}x_v&\ell=v,\\1-x_v&\ell=\neg v.\end{cases}
\]
This is not a Sherali--Adams lift or an exact Boolean model set. Let \(G_F\)
be the primal co-occurrence graph: distinct original variable names are
adjacent exactly when they occur together in at least one clause.

\begin{theorem}[Exact all-assignment independent-pin feasibility]
Let \(I\) be \emph{any} independent set of \(G_F\), and let
\(\alpha:I\to\{0,1\}\) be \emph{any} complete or partial assignment.
Then the explicitly defined fractional point
\[
x_v=\begin{cases}
\alpha(v)&v\in\operatorname{dom}\alpha,\\
\tfrac12&v\notin\operatorname{dom}\alpha
\end{cases}
\]
lies in \(P_F\). Consequently every pinned basic LP
\(P_F\cap\{x_i=\alpha_i:i\in J\}\), for \(J\subseteq I\), is feasible.
No sound rational Farkas infeasibility certificate, hence no
Farkas-certified subcube nogood, can use \emph{only} pins from \(I\).
This holds equally for SAT and UNSAT \(F\), and for an arbitrary
adaptive order of pins inside \(I\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every source clause contains at most one variable of \(I\). If its one
pinned literal is false, the other two unpinned literals each evaluate to
\(1/2\), so their sum is exactly one. If that pinned literal is true,
the clause inequality is satisfied. If no literal is pinned, the sum
is \(3/2\). All box rows hold. This constructs a feasible rational point
for every pin assignment. A valid Farkas contradiction would be
incompatible with the displayed feasible point.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Co-clausal support necessity]
Suppose an exactly rational-verified Farkas certificate under pinned
proper-3CNF source rows yields the clause
\(\bigvee_{i\in J}(x_i\ne\alpha_i)\), with \(J\) the positive-multiplier
root-pin support as in RELAX-DUAL-CUT159. Then \(J\) contains at least two
variables which co-occur in a source clause.
In particular \(J\) cannot have size one. This is a necessary
certificate support condition, not a sufficient criterion for infeasibility.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Otherwise \(J\) would be primal-independent, contradicting the theorem.
\end{proof}
Note that a clause learned at a second recursive level may use one
\emph{new} pin together with a previously retained ancestral pin.
A purported one-pin \emph{root} cut must count \emph{all} inherited pins
and cannot evade this support condition.

\begin{corollary}[Linear boundary on the canonical M22 family]
For each valid clausewise-monotone exact-\((2,2)\) three-CNF,
\(3m=4k\), each source variable occurs in exactly four clauses,
and \(\Delta(G_F)\le8\). Greedy independent-set selection therefore
gives \(|I|\ge\lceil k/9\rceil\). Even for an UNSAT \(F\in C_\star\),
every one of the \(2^{|I|}\) assignments to this linear-size boundary has
a feasible, explicitly verifiable basic LP. Furthermore at least
\(m-4|I|\) original ternary clauses are untouched by \emph{all} pins in \(I\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Each of four clauses supplies at most two distinct co-occurrence
neighbors, yielding degree at most eight. Greedy selection deletes
at most nine candidates for each selected vertex. Each chosen
variable lies in exactly four source clauses; independent variables
lie in disjoint clause-incident sets, and no untouched clause is changed
by a raw restriction.
\end{proof}
For the original 198-name, 264-clause connected strict-monotone UNSAT
source retained from D\"ocker, the deterministic greedy set has 42
variables and leaves 96 completely untouched source clauses. Thus
there are \(2^{42}\) conditionings on which the basic LP remains
feasible although the original formula is UNSAT. The UNSAT label is
imported from the already checked published construction, not
an independent \(2^{198}\)-assignment computation in this run.
The result does \emph{not} imply that any exact decider explores these
conditionings: a decider can recognize a global contradiction or
use affine, Horn, SCC, resolution, caching, source rereads or an entirely
different representation.

\section{A genuine all-input, assumption-dependent two-level transformation}
Fix ANY total, uniform, exact ordinary-SAT decider \(A\), with joint
resource pair \((t,s)\in E\). Define a fixed uniform machine
\(B_A\) on every binary input, including malformed ordinary encodings:
delegate malformed/unsupported instances to \(A\) according to the
same total convention. On a valid proper-3CNF with an independent pair
of source variables \(x,y\), compute the graph and \(x,y\)
with fully charged source parsing, discovery and control.
At level one generate the ordinary residual \(F|_{x=b}\) for \(b=0,1\);
at level two generate the ordinary residual
\(F|_{x=b,y=c}\) for \(c=0,1\).
Each child is a \emph{real} independently serialized ordinary-CNF,
with unchanged original variable names, signs and headers corrected,
and with each source occurrence either copied, removed by a true
clause or deleted as a false literal. The guest \(A\) actually
decides the four level-two children; join their four answer bits
with OR. A zero-clause formula is SAT and a formula containing an
empty clause is UNSAT, following the ordinary total encoding convention.
The wrapper may attempt a certified LP prune, but on these selected
independent pins none exists by the theorem, so a rule that knows
the selected incidence graph may skip that failed avenue.
No witness or full model table is demanded of \(A\).

\begin{theorem}[Two real levels, exact but not favorable]
For every \(A\) described above, \(B_A\) is uniform, total and
correct on all ordinary SAT inputs; both SAT and UNSAT parents are
preserved. Under the straightforward eager explicitly-serialized
implementation, for original bit length \(N\), each of at most four
\(A\) queries is a genuine ordinary CNF child with
\(L_j=O(N+\log N)\) bits. Charging parsing, the two actual
restriction levels, headers/bodies, unchanged and changed bit writes,
source/work traffic, indexing and finite control yields
\[
T_{B_A}(N)=O(N\,\operatorname{polylog}N)+
  \sum_{j=1}^{B\le4}T_A(L_j),\qquad
S_{B_A}(N)=O(N)+\max_{j\le B}S_A(L_j).
\]
Consequently its conservative \emph{joint upper ledger} is
\[
\boxed{\quad t_{B_A}\le\max\{1,t\},\qquad
s_{B_A}\le\max\{1,s\}.\quad}
\]
For bounded-degree M22 sources and any \(d=o(k)\) raw pinned
original variables, at least \(m-4d=\Theta(k)\) ternaries
survive unchanged, giving \(L=\Omega(k\log k)=\Omega(N)\)
under dense indexed encoding. In particular these independent
two-level descendants do not attain \(L\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\)
for any fixed \(\lambda<1\) on that growing family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Shannon identity
\(\SAT(F)=\bigvee_{b,c\in\{0,1\}}\SAT(F|_{x=b,y=c})\)
is exact for any distinct original source variables,
and total \(A\) decides every ordinary residual, even after
the children exit \(C_\star\). Each eager child can be produced
by at most two sequential source scans and ordinary fixed-width
record/header rewrites; at fixed depth, all work costs
\(N\operatorname{polylog}N\) in the FLMV bit model. Depth-first
operation holds only one actual guest and its ancestors at a time:
the source parse, stage buffers, headers, index tapes, caches and
control all fit \(O(N)\) simultaneously writable bits, plus the
currently invoked guest's \(S_A(L_j)\). At most four
guest calls occur. The clause-count and dense-encoding lower
bound is source retention, not a lower bound for arbitrary
semantic compilers. No claim is made that \emph{all} algorithms
must materialize these children or use this decision tree.
\end{proof}

The result supplies a \emph{genuine all-input} transformation under
the existence of \(A\) but \emph{does not improve} its achievable
time-space exponents. In particular the eager workspace exponent
at least one in the displayed upper ledger prevents it from
establishing the strict FLMV space endpoint. Replacing eager output
with a source-backed lazy accessor requires a fresh, fully charged
per-probe time/space proof, not a free child input tape.

\section{Quantified cover condition and ARC resource implications}
Suppose a set \(B\) of \(b\) named Boolean boundary variables is
covered entirely by \(h\) source-valid Farkas nogoods with supports
\(J_1,\dots,J_h\subseteq B\). Each cut forbids precisely a
fraction \(2^{-|J_i|}\) of the boundary cube. Hence the elementary
union bound gives the \emph{necessary} condition
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{h}2^{-|J_i|}\ge1.
\]
This says nothing about an arbitrary SAT machine's required work,
but specifies what a \emph{dual-certificate-only} controller must
obtain before claiming complete UNSAT coverage. On a primal-independent
boundary the available Farkas-cut family is empty, so this route
cannot discharge even one of its \(2^b\) assignments through
the declared basic LP. For other boundaries, finding a short
certificate, verifying it, selecting it over competing channels,
checking cover and caching/replaying it all remain charged.

For the proposed ARC eager children with \(B\le N^{a+o(1)}\),
\(L\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), total non-guest cost exponent \(r\),
per-output-bit overhead exponent \(g\), generator workspace \(\eta\)
and retained ancestor exponent \(\kappa\), retain only the
CONDITIONAL ledger
\[
t'\le\max\{r,a+\lambda+g,a+\lambda t\},\qquad
s'\le\max\{\lambda,\lambda s,\eta,\kappa\}.
\]
The independent-pin policy has \(\lambda=1\) on growing M22
sources (and \(B=4\)), so it cannot realize the wished-for half-scale
\(t'\le\max(11/10,3/5+t/2)\) route. A whole-parent \(A(F)\)
fallback retains the parent time exponent. Even in a \(P=NP\)
world, the availability of polynomial \(A\) alone does not
pay for discovering a semantic \(N^\lambda\) representation.
A fixed hard image may replace an all-input final promise only
after FLMV-local re-entry and hereditary child eligibility are charged.
