\section{BUILD I: an explicit affine four-layer hypergraph}
Let $p\ge5$ be odd and let $n=3p$. Variables are $A_i,B_i,C_i$ indexed in $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$. For $s\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ and $i\in\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$, put the proper three-variable clause
\[
 D_{i,s}=\bigl(A_i^{\alpha_s}\vee B_{i+s}^{\beta_s}\vee C_{i+2s}^{\gamma_s}\bigr),
 \qquad v^{+}=v,\quad v^{-}=\neg v,
\]
with sign columns
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}s&0&1&2&3\\\hline
\alpha_s&-&-&+&+\\
\beta_s&+&+&-&-\\
\gamma_s&-&+&-&+
\end{array}.
\]
Define $F_p=\bigwedge_{s=0}^3\bigwedge_{i=0}^{p-1}D_{i,s}$.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform strict exact-$(2,2)$ affine no-short-resolvent control]
For every odd $p\ge5$, $F_p$ is connected, has $n=3p$ variables, $m=4p$ distinct proper ternary clauses, every variable occurs twice positively and twice negatively, and any two clauses share at most one variable. The assignment $0^n$ satisfies $F_p$. For \emph{every} pivot $x$, the complete Davis--Putnam resolution step on $x$ removes four ternary clauses and creates exactly four \emph{distinct non-tautological width-four} resolvents. None is subsumed by a surviving source clause, so ordinary width-three-only resolution cannot eliminate any first pivot.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $s$. The clauses $D_{i,s}$ form a partition of each of the $A,B,C$ groups, so each variable appears exactly once in every layer. Each of the three sign rows has two pluses and two minuses. Every layer has a negative literal (at $s=0$ both $A,C$; $s=1$ the $A$; $s=2$ both $B,C$; $s=3$ the $B$), hence $0^n$ satisfies every clause. The layers $s=0,1$ connect $A_i$ to $B_i$ and $B_{i+1}$, which connects $A_i$ to $A_{i+1}$; all $A$ and then $B,C$ variables lie in one incidence component.

Suppose distinct clauses $D_{i,s}$ and $D_{j,t}$ share two variables. If they share $A,B$, equations $i=j$ and $i+s=j+t$ imply $s=t$, hence identical clauses. The same holds for $A,C$ since multiplication by $2$ is injective modulo odd $p$ and $0\le s,t\le3<p$. If they share $B,C$, subtract $i+s=j+t$ from $i+2s=j+2t$ to obtain $s=t$ and then $i=j$. Thus they share at most one variable. In particular, the eight variables outside pivot $x$ in its four incident clauses are pairwise distinct (a repeated outside variable would create an intersection of size two with pivot $x$). Each pair of an $x$-positive and an $x$-negative clause therefore resolves to a genuine four-literal clause; all four are distinct, with no tautology or duplicate literal. Every original clause uses one variable from each of the groups $A,B,C$, whereas a resolvent on $x$ uses only the other two groups. Thus no surviving three-literal source clause subsumes it.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: exact projection, source-free factoring and physical reentry}
Write the four source clauses through $x$ as
\[
 (x\vee P_0)\wedge(x\vee P_1)\wedge
 (\neg x\vee M_0)\wedge(\neg x\vee M_1),
\]
where each $P_i,M_j$ is a disjunction of two literals, and the four supports are pairwise disjoint by the theorem. The elimination output is
\[
 E=(P_0\vee M_0)\wedge(P_0\vee M_1)\wedge
 (P_1\vee M_0)\wedge(P_1\vee M_1),
\quad
 E\equiv(\bigwedge_iP_i)\vee(\bigwedge_jM_j).
\]
The equality is pointwise on the remaining variables, not merely a SAT-status statement. Exact one-selector reentry is
\[
 E\equiv\exists z\,[(z\vee P_0)\wedge(z\vee P_1)\wedge(\neg z\vee M_0)\wedge(\neg z\vee M_1)].
\]
The four resolvents alone determine the four size-two feet up to row/column swaps and reversal: two resolvents that share the same row (or column) have a two-literal intersection; opposite corners are disjoint. Checking at most $4!$ corner assignments recovers a valid rectangle and verifies the complete cross-product. This is \emph{source-free, finite-control local factoring}, not a hidden occurrence table. It uses $O(\log n)$ bits for the eight literal identifiers and a constant-size search.

\begin{proposition}[Two specific reentry routes fail power shrinkage]
On $F_p$, eliminating any pivot and then Tseitin-converting each genuine width-four resolvent to two proper width-three clauses introduces four fresh variables and eight ternary clauses. Relative to the parent, the child has $n+3$ variables and $m+4$ clauses. Alternatively, factoring the four resolvents into a shared selector restores four ternary clauses and $n$ variables with the \emph{same complete signed DIMACS bit length} as the parent, even if the selector orientation changes clause signs. Choosing the original row/column orientation reproduces the exact original clauses, hence an exact fixed point. Neither specified reentry route gives $N_{\rm child}\le N^\lambda$ for fixed $0<\lambda<1$ and all large $N$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For one resolvent $\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3\vee\ell_4$, introduce fresh $y$ and the two clauses $(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee y)$ and $(\neg y\vee\ell_3\vee\ell_4)$. Existential elimination of $y$ recovers that resolvent. There are four such resolvents. Thus four deleted 3-clauses become eight 3-clauses and one deleted variable becomes four auxiliaries. Source-free rectangle factoring replaces the four deleted clauses by four 3-clauses using the same eight other signed literals and a new selector named $x$, twice positive and twice negative. Hence the multiset of literal identifiers and negative-sign counts has the same ASCII length, as does the unpadded header (same $n,m$); sorting clauses cannot change total physical length. The original orientation gives the parent itself. Since the factored child has exactly $N$ bits, $N\le N^\lambda$ fails whenever $N>1$. The Tseitin child has at least as many clauses and eventually more encoded bytes, which the executable checks directly. This is a counterexample \emph{only to these compilation rules}: global semantic simplification could decide the recognizable family and output a fixed YES instance.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Closed adaptive no-contraction orbit for the specified compiler]
Let $F_p^{\mathbf v}$ be obtained from $F_p$ by complementing precisely the variable names with $\mathbf v_x=1$, and let $\mathcal O_p=\{F_p^{\mathbf v}:\mathbf v\in\{0,1\}^{3p}\}$. Consider a transition that selects \emph{any} pivot $x$ (with its selection strategy possibly depending on the entire current state), eliminates it by all non-tautological resolution pairs, recovers a validated four-corner rectangle from those four resolvents alone, and reenters a single selector using the vacated identifier $x$. Then
\[
 F_p^{\mathbf v}\longmapsto F_p^{\mathbf v}\quad\text{or}\quad F_p^{\mathbf v\oplus\mathbf e_x}.
\]
Thus $\mathcal O_p$ is closed under any number of such one-child transitions, and every intermediate ordinary-3CNF output has the \emph{same physical unpadded DIMACS length} $N$ as the input. The formula is SAT at every stage, with known satisfying assignment $\mathbf v$. Hence no number of adaptive rule choices \emph{within this fixed local rewrite family} gives a power-short child; replacing the rewrite by a global SAT recognizer immediately escapes this obstruction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Complementing variable occurrences preserves the unsigned incidence hypergraph and the twice-positive/twice-negative quota. For each pivot, its four incident clauses still have four pairwise-disjoint two-literal feet and their full resolvent set is exactly the four edges of a $K_{2,2}$ between those feet. The four sets are recognized uniquely up to permutations within the two sides and swapping the sides: edges that share a foot have a two-literal intersection, while opposite edges have empty intersection. Consequently every valid rectangular reconstruction uses the same four original two-literal feet, assigning to the reintroduced $x$ either their original polarity partition or the reversed partition. The first choice restores $F_p^{\mathbf v}$; the second flips every occurrence of $x$, giving $F_p^{\mathbf v\oplus\mathbf e_x}$. Both cases have exactly the same three-variable scopes and, as each identifier occurs equally with both signs, exactly the same signed byte length. The induction applies after every adaptive transition. The witness $\mathbf v$ is the original all-zero witness transported by the same variable complements.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Physical and computational bookkeeping.}
Unpadded signed decimal DIMACS has $N=\Theta(p\log(p+2))$ bits because every one of the $4p$ clauses contains a $C$-identifier of size $\Omega(\log p)$, with at most three such identifiers. Clause count $4p$ and literal-wire uses $12p$ are tracked separately; they are not gates in an evaluated Boolean decision circuit. The recognizer checks source order, signs, all identifiers and the header and rejects malformed encodings as outside its promise. A conservative fixed-multitape read-only-input implementation with separately writable output can read and compare the entire source using $O(N^2)$ head steps and $O(\log N)$ auxiliary work cells, storing at most four pivot clauses (constant-many $O(\log N)$-bit identifiers). It writes $\Theta(N)$ output bits for the shown transformations, never treating them as free virtual data. Its one-read/write-tape simulation incurs $T_1=O(T_m(N+S_m+\log(T_m+2)))=O(N^3)$ steps and $P_1=O(N)$ total tape cells, counting source and output. A fixed universal TM incurs an additional polynomial compilation/interpreter overhead; no sharp same-machine time--space lower bound or RAM transfer is claimed. A word-RAM implementation supports direct index arithmetic but must still pay its parsing, source reads and output bytes.

\section{BUILD III: logical classification of the unrestricted constructive target}
\begin{proposition}[Equivalence at the existential level]
Assume the target is existentially quantified over a polynomial-time decidable, syntactically recognized, recursively closed encoded class that contains every proper ordinary 3CNF and contains distinct fixed satisfiable and unsatisfiable base instances. Then the existence of a \emph{uniform deterministic polynomial-time} one-child decision-preserving map to $\le N^\lambda$ physical bits for all sufficiently large $N$, with direct bounded-base decision, is \emph{equivalent} to $P=NP$. In particular the one-child property is sufficient, and is also logically necessary \emph{under the existential choice of class}, via a trivial constant-output map if $P=NP$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Iterate the single child. Above the fixed base threshold, the bit-length recurrence is $N\mapsto\lfloor N^\lambda\rfloor$ and each map has polynomial cost in its current length; after $O(\log\log N)$ levels the base is reached, and the total is polynomial. This decides 3SAT, so $P=NP$. Conversely if $P=NP$, a uniform deterministic polynomial-time decider for ordinary 3SAT exists. On the class of ordinary proper 3CNFs plus designated fixed bases, invoke it, then output the fixed YES or NO base accordingly. That is one child of constant length and hence power-short above a threshold; the closure and correctness requirements are automatic. This converse is a \emph{logical conditional}, not an unconditional construction, and is not available for an arbitrary preselected super-class containing unrelated undecidable languages. If the proposed mapping is additionally forbidden from solving the parent instance, that is a strictly different target requiring a precise restriction.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Consequence.} The full unrestricted contraction is not presently an intermediate solved lemma: its existence is a reformulation of $P=NP$ with an explicit construction certificate. Restricted positive examples and local negative examples remain scientifically useful for identifying compiler mechanisms, but do not decide that equivalence.
