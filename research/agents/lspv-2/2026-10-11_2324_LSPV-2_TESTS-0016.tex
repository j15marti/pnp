\begin{abstract}
\status{PROVED/EXACT} A proper Horn 3CNF with $m$ physical clause records can
have, after one one-bit restriction, a residual containing exactly one
two-literal clause, while a deterministic no-preprocessing clause-probe
accessor needs exactly $m-1$ probes in the worst case to locate that clause.
In the declared indexed encoding the parent length is
$N=\Theta(m\log m)$ and the residual length is $O(\log m)$, so small semantic
or explicit residual size alone does not imply cheap local access.  One
charged scan and a $\lceil\log_2m\rceil$-bit locator restore constant-time
access.  Thus the result separates child existence/size from acquisition;
it is an input-access fact on an easy Horn family, not a SAT hardness result.
The finite audit passed $152{,}740$ checks in $2.167364$ seconds.  The prior
VIRTUAL-CHILD-SPACE1 and STREAMCOMP1 accounting theorems are adopted, not
reproved as new.  P versus NP remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact model and the claim attacked}

The parent is a conventional indexed CNF stream of $m$ constant-arity
clause records.  Variable identifiers occupy $\Theta(\log m)$ bits, so the
encoded length is $N=\Theta(m\log m)$.  One \emph{clause probe} reads one
$\Theta(\log N)$-bit record.  A no-preprocessing accessor receives the parent
and a restriction but no survivor list, rank/select index, or hidden locator.
All probes used to locate the requested residual record count.  A
preprocessing algorithm may instead scan the parent, but its time and stored
index are charged.

The attacked inference is
\begin{equation}
\tag{SCL}
\begin{aligned}
|R(F,\alpha)|&\le N^{\theta+o(1)},\quad\theta<1\\
&\Longrightarrow
\text{ every requested residual bit/record is obtainable in }N^{o(1)}
\text{ time without charged acquisition.}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
This is stronger than VIRTUAL-CHILD-SPACE1, whose premise explicitly
\emph{supplies} the local transducer.  It is also stronger than LB-0020,
which explicitly materializes a child and charges that work.

\section{The one-survivor family}

Fix $m\ge2$ and a hidden location $t\in[m]$.  On variables
$x,a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_m,b_m$, define the proper Horn 3CNF
\begin{equation}
H_{m,t}=
\left(\bigwedge_{i\ne t}(\neg x\vee\neg a_i\vee\neg b_i)\right)
\wedge (x\vee\neg a_t\vee\neg b_t).
\tag{1}
\end{equation}
Every clause has three distinct variables and the formula is Horn.  Under
the restriction $x=0$, every clause with $i\ne t$ is satisfied and deleted,
while the unique survivor is
\begin{equation}
R(H_{m,t},x=0)=(\neg a_t\vee\neg b_t).
\tag{2}
\end{equation}
Thus the residual has one physical clause and encoded length $O(\log m)$ if
original names are retained.  With local renaming its logical clause content
is constant-size, but reconstruction metadata still includes the source
location $t$.

There is an even simpler 2CNF/Horn control: use
$(\neg x\vee\neg a_i)$ for $i\ne t$ and $(x\vee\neg a_t)$ at $t$.
Restriction $x=0$ leaves the single unit clause $\neg a_t$.

\begin{theorem}[ONE-SURVIVOR-LOCALITY-SEPARATION1]
\status{PROVED/EXACT} In the declared no-preprocessing clause-probe model,
locating the unique record of $R(H_{m,t},x=0)$ has deterministic worst-case
query complexity exactly $m-1$.  Nevertheless one preprocessing scan plus a
$\lceil\log_2m\rceil$-bit locator gives constant-time access thereafter.
Consequently (SCL) is \status{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Encode the signs of the $x$-literals as a length-$m$ mask with exactly one
one, at $t$.  An accessor can probe $m-1$ records; if all answers are zero,
the unprobed location is $t$, so $m-1$ probes suffice.  Conversely, let an
adversary answer zero to every probe.  Before $m-1$ distinct locations have
been probed, two unqueried locations remain, and either can be the unique one.
They induce different correct source locations, so a deterministic accessor
cannot yet answer.  A single scan finds $t$ and storing $t$ costs
$\lceil\log_2m\rceil$ bits.  Equation (2) gives a child of $O(\log m)$ bits,
whereas the no-index location cost is $m-1=\Omega(N/\log N)$ clause probes.
\end{proof}

The lower bound is intentionally weak and model-specific.  It is merely a
nearly linear input-location cost, and the scan/index upper bound matches it.
Because (1) is Horn, linear-time Horn algorithms are a positive control, not
an attack on the theorem.

\section{Adopted virtual-child accounting}

The corpus already contains VIRTUAL-CHILD-SPACE1 and the more general
STREAMCOMP1.  For one recursive type with at most
$N^{\eta+o(1)}$ children, logical child length
$N^{\theta+o(1)}$, local work $N^{r+o(1)}$, per-request generator time
$N^{g+o(1)}$, local space $N^{p+o(1)}$, and generator space
$N^{s+o(1)}$, their exact conservative inequalities specialize to
\begin{align}
C&\ge\max\{r,\eta+g+\theta C\},
& C&=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta+g}{1-\theta}\right\},\tag{3}\\
\rho&\ge\max\{p,s,\theta\rho\},
& \rho&=\max\{p,s\}.\tag{4}
\end{align}
Descriptions, preprocessing, retained parent state, nested generators,
caches, repeated reads, output and reconstruction remain charged.  Setting
$g=s=0$ recovers the subpolynomial local-addressability case.  LB-0020's
explicit materialization statement is therefore accepted at its declared
scope; the present result shows why a virtual replacement needs an
independent locator/transducer premise.

\begin{proposition}[FAVORABLE-PASS-LOCAL-ADDRESSABILITY1]
\status{OPEN} A P-versus-NP-relevant pass still needs, for one fixed uniform
deterministic SAT decider and on every recursive successor actually used, a
K-independent hereditary ordinary-CNF pass together with either charged
explicit emission or a source-matched random-access generator satisfying
favorable instances of (3)--(4).  Semantic residual size does not supply
this interface.
\end{proposition}

\section{Hostile controls, wrappers, and barriers}

\paragraph{Easy-family controls.}
Horn forward chaining, implication-graph 2SAT, Gaussian elimination for
affine/Tseitin systems, and bounded-treewidth dynamic programming may bypass
generic residual interfaces.  Independent products may expose component
arithmetic.  None is charged for an object it does not construct.  The
one-bit SAT output needs neither a witness nor the source locator $t$.

\paragraph{Representations.}
An explicit survivor list, sparse dictionary, bitvector plus rank/select
index, or a constructor that passes $t$ as metadata gives cheap access only
after its acquisition and stored bits are charged.  Alternate clause order,
padding and duplicate records change the physical mask and the actual encoded
length $N$; exponents are always measured in that padded length.  Local
renaming can erase the clause names but cannot provide reconstruction for
free.  INTERP and RECOMP are charged by their realized probe/generator work;
DEAD and DELAY cannot prove necessity; COPY changes representation size.

\paragraph{Barrier audit.}
The adversary proof relativizes, survives algebraic oracles only as the same
declared raw-record query statement, and supplies no nonrelativizing or
nonalgebrizing hinge.  Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson therefore
remain active barriers \cite{BGS,AW}.  Natural Proofs is not crossed: no large
constructive property separating general circuits is proposed \cite{RR}.
No solver-to-protocol, proof-system-to-algorithm, or nonuniform-to-uniform
transfer is inferred.
