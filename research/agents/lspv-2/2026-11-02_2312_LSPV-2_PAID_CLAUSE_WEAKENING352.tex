\begin{theorem}[Paid exact clause-weakening compiler]
For all $k,r\ge1$, every nonempty nontautological clause $C=\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_k$ and nontautological disjoint-literal extension $B=b_1\vee\cdots\vee b_r$ with $C\vee B$ nontautological admit an explicit derivation $C\vdash_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}C\vee B$ with exactly
\[
\boxed{k+r-1\ \mathrm{VARDEF},\quad 2k+r-1\ \mathrm{RES},\quad q=3k+2r-2.}
\]
All definitions are fresh conservative binary gates, every antecedent is explicit, and every strictly resolved clause is non-tautological. This is an upper-bound construction, NOT a minimum-cost theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $r=1$, let $w=\neg b_1$ be an existing signed literal. For $r\ge2$ define fresh $w_2\leftrightarrow(\neg b_1\land\neg b_2)$ and $w_j\leftrightarrow(w_{j-1}\land\neg b_j)$ for $3\le j\le r$, then put $w=w_r$. Introduce one fresh gate
\[
q_i\leftrightarrow(\neg\ell_i\land w)
\]
per source literal. Its charged slots contain $(\neg q_i\vee\neg\ell_i)$ and $(q_i\vee\ell_i\vee\neg w)$. Resolve the current $C$ successively with the former slots on pivots $\ell_i$, reaching $\bigvee_{i=1}^k\neg q_i$ in $k$ nodes. Resolve this current clause successively against the latter slots on pivots $\neg q_i$, reaching $C\vee\neg w$ in another $k$ nodes. For $r=1$ this already equals $C\vee B$. For $r\ge2$, successively resolve against the third defining slots $(w_j\vee\neg w_{j-1}\vee b_j)$ for descending $j=r,\ldots,3$, ending with $(w_2\vee b_1\vee b_2)$. These $r-1$ pivots expand $\neg w$ into $B$, producing the requested exact $C\vee B$. At every step the named pivot is complementary, and the final disjunction is nontautological by hypothesis. The gate count is $k+r-1$, the resolution count is $2k+r-1$, and all relations are source-independent conservative extensions.
\end{proof}
