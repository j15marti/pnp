\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let \(F=C_1\wedge\cdots\wedge C_m\) be a width-at-most-three
CNF on \(n\) variables, with \(N\) total input literal occurrences.
For assignment \(a\), let
\[
V_F(a)=\begin{cases}
\min\{j:C_j(a)=0\},&\text{if such a clause exists},\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact source selector; PROVED/EXACT]
A uniform polynomial-time constructor emits a fan-in-two Boolean
circuit of \(O(N+m\log(m+1)+n)\) gates and \(O(\log(m+2))\) depth
that computes \(V_F\). It has \(\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil\) output
bits. This construction works on every CNF, satisfiable or not,
and requires no SAT queries.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each clause form the violation flag
\(e_j(a)=\bigwedge_{\ell\in C_j}\neg\ell(a)\), of constant
depth. The \(j\)-th leaf stores \((e_j,j)\), each binary index
bit masked by \(e_j\). Combine subtree results by
\[
 e=e_L\vee e_R,\qquad
 b_i=(e_L\wedge b_{L,i})\vee(\neg e_L\wedge b_{R,i}).
\]
This selects the first active index in left-to-right order,
or zero if none is active. A balanced tournament of height
\(O(\log(m+2))\), with \(O(\log(m+1))\) gates per internal
node, proves the bound. All gates and indices have uniform
polynomial-bit encodings. An empty clause list yields zero.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Totality gate; PROVED/EXACT]
The property \(\forall a\,V_F(a)\neq 0\) is coNP-complete
even when the circuit is precisely the foregoing canonical
uniformly generated low-depth circuit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every Boolean assignment \(a\),
\[
V_F(a)=0\iff F(a)=1.
\]
Thus totality is identical to UNSAT. A falsifying assignment is
an NP witness for failure; coNP-hardness is inherited from
3-CNF UNSAT under the polynomial constructor. The result also
applies to the valid signed exact-(2,2) subdomain conditional
on its established fixed-language NP-completeness.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Proof transport is additional; PROVED/EXACT]
The canonical selector, its locally verified gates, and its
polynomial-time evaluation do not themselves constitute
an UNSAT proof. Under a globally polynomial correct decider
\(A\), totality can be decided in polynomial time, but the
one-bit result is not an explicit \(\sCP\) derivation.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The same selector exists for SAT formulas, and totality
itself is UNSAT. Semantic-CP lines require genuine Boolean
inequality axioms or sound inferences, not a free invocation
of a decision result. Any external extractor must be shown
to output the actual inequalities with fully charged costs.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Decider-indexed proof system.}
If a globally correct polynomial-time SAT decider \(A\) existed,
accepting a one-symbol auxiliary proof precisely when
\(A(F)=\mathrm{NO}\), plus a tagged ordinary Resolution refutation
for completeness, would define a sound polynomial-time-checkable
Cook--Reckhow-style UNSAT proof system. It is not the sCP system,
and a p-simulation of it by sCP remains OPEN. A polynomial gate
trace of \(A(F)=0\) verifies that single calculation, but deriving
UNSAT from it requires a global correctness/reflection statement
in the target calculus. The constant-NO incorrect program has such
a trace even on SAT formulas, exposing the absent inference without
claiming that incorrect machine is a legitimate global decider.
