\paragraph{Exact input.}
Write the legal periodic label word as
\[
a_t=(b_1(t),\ldots,b_k(t))\in\mathbb F_2^k,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z.
\]
For \(1\le r<t\le k\) and cyclic lag \(\delta\), define the alternating pair sequence
\[
h_{rt,\delta}(s):=
b_r(s)b_t(s+\delta)+b_t(s)b_r(s+\delta)\in\mathbb F_2.
\]
Let \(Q=(Q_{ij})\) be an alternating \(m\times m\) matrix, representing a candidate polar relation. For \(1\le\delta\le m-1\), pad its \(\delta\)-th upper diagonal to a cyclic length-\(N\) sequence
\[
q_\delta(i)=
\begin{cases}
Q_{i,i+\delta},&1\le i\le m-\delta,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]
with the harmless origin/reversal convention fixed once and for all.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: diagonal-convolution normal form.}
WINDOWREC1's same-shift condition
\[
X_s^\top QX_s=0\qquad\text{for every }s
\]
is equivalent to the family of cyclic convolution identities
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}
(q_\delta*h_{rt,\delta})(s)=0
\qquad
\forall s\in\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z,\quad 1\le r<t\le k,
}
\]
up to the fixed reversal convention in the definition of cyclic convolution.

\paragraph{Proof.}
The \((r,t)\)-entry of \(X_s^\top QX_s\) is
\[
\sum_{1\le i<j\le m}Q_{ij}
\bigl(
b_r(s+i)b_t(s+j)+b_t(s+i)b_r(s+j)
\bigr).
\]
Set \(\delta=j-i\). The inner factor becomes
\[
h_{rt,\delta}(s+i).
\]
Grouping terms by \(\delta\) gives
\[
\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}\sum_{i=1}^{m-\delta}
Q_{i,i+\delta}h_{rt,\delta}(s+i),
\]
which is precisely the displayed cyclic-convolution system after reversing one cyclic index. No cross-shift wedge has been introduced: every term comes from one legal shift \(s\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: one block-circulant operator.}
Let
\[
\Phi:\bigoplus_{\delta=1}^{m-1}P_{m-\delta}
\longrightarrow
(R_N)^{\binom{k}{2}}
\]
send the padded diagonal family \((q_\delta)_\delta\) to
\[
\Phi((q_\delta))_{rt}
=
\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}q_\delta*h_{rt,\delta}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{U_L^\perp\cong\ker\Phi.}
\]
Thus the exterior problem is exactly the kernel of a block-circulant convolution operator restricted to triangular support. This is the exterior analogue of GCD1 at the correct object level. Unlike the linear case, it is a multi-input convolution map, so one scalar gcd does not generally solve it.

\paragraph{CONDITIONAL exact Fourier certificate in the semisimple case.}
Assume \(N\) is odd, so \(z^N-1\) is square-free over characteristic two, and extend scalars to a splitting field \(K\) containing all \(N\)-th roots of unity. Cyclic convolution diagonalizes under the DFT. For each frequency \(\omega\), define the \(\binom{k}{2}\times(m-1)\) matrix
\[
\mathcal H(\omega)_{(r,t),\delta}
=
\widehat h_{rt,\delta}(\omega).
\]
For the \emph{relaxed} domain in which each \(q_\delta\) is an arbitrary cyclic length-\(N\) sequence, the frequency-\(\omega\) constraint is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H(\omega)
\begin{bmatrix}
\widehat q_1(\omega)\\ \vdots\\ \widehat q_{m-1}(\omega)
\end{bmatrix}=0.
}
\]
Therefore, if
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}_K\mathcal H(\omega)=m-1\quad\text{for every }\omega,}
\]
then the relaxed kernel is zero, hence the actual triangular-support kernel \(U_L^\perp\) is also zero. This is a sufficient certificate, not a necessary condition for the actual supported problem. It requires \(\binom{k}{2}\ge m-1\), so it may be too strong in the parameter regime of interest.

\paragraph{Why the Fourier step is not a free rank formula.}
The true \(q_\delta\) have supports of lengths \(m-\delta\). Those support restrictions couple their Fourier values across frequencies. Consequently the actual triangular-support rank does \emph{not} decompose as an unrestricted sum of independent frequency ranks. Treating it that way would silently enlarge the domain and can create false conclusions. The safe uses are: (i) the exact block-circulant normal form over \(\mathbb F_2\); and (ii) the all-frequency full-column-rank condition above as a sufficient injectivity certificate for a larger relaxed domain.
