\section{Certified assumption shrinking}
Let $H=G(Y)\wedge\bigwedge_{j=1}^J H_j(Y_j,U_j)\subseteq F$ be a source-verified subformula, with pairwise disjoint private $U_j$ and all shared variables explicitly retained in $Y$. Suppose a complete, source-labelled DPLL refutation $P$ of $H_j$ under assignment $\alpha$ has been independently checked. The new rule attempts to remove boundary assumptions, one at a time, replaying the \emph{same} proof tree under each weakened assignment; failed checks are charged and rejected.
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, restricted proof-replay model]
If the independent proof checker accepts $P$ under $\beta\subseteq\alpha$, then $H_j\models D_\beta$, where
\[
D_\beta=\bigvee_{(v=b)\in\beta}\begin{cases}\neg v,&b=1,\\v,&b=0.\end{cases}
\]
The clause excludes every extension of $\beta$. If the exact master $G\wedge\bigwedge D_\beta$ is UNSAT, then $H$ and $F$ are UNSAT. A master candidate passing all blocks does not prove $F$ SAT if clauses were omitted; full original-source witness replay is mandatory. With at most $|Y_j|$ attempted deletions per proof, discovery cost is bounded by $|Y_j|$ independent checker replays plus original solver acquisition; the checker, not an uncharged minimal-core oracle, supplies soundness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A successful proof replay has verified both children of every split and source-clause conflicts at every leaf without using dropped assumptions. Thus $H_j\wedge\beta$ is UNSAT, so $H_j\models\neg\beta=D_\beta$. All learned clauses follow from $H$. An UNSAT master therefore refutes the subformula and hence the full parent. Conversely, full source replay detects any witness that violates omitted clauses.
\end{proof}
The policy obtains source records, $Y_j$, proof node count, cache, and remaining budget from its current state. It pays for failed recognizers, DPLL calls, checking, condition selection and master switching. A local proof over 16 nodes skips minimization. The master uses exact DPLL with an explicit budget and an exhaustive fallback, and the total procedure has original-source exact DPLL fallback. General proof storage and runtime may be exponential.
