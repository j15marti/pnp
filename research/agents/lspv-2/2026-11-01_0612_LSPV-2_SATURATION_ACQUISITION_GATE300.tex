\begin{theorem}

\[

\begin{proof}
Each pinned variable falsifies exactly two signed occurrences, so every uncovered binary clause, which contains one pinned false literal, contributes to $b\le2k$. Edge-disjoint odd cycles each use at least three original binary clause IDs, so $\nu\le\lfloor b/3\rfloor$. Every covered clause contains a pinned true literal and at most two free occurrences. Hence $\sum(s_x^++s_x^-)\le2c$, implying $\eta\le c$. The canonical identity $3m=4n$ gives $2f-u=2n/3-2k+c$. Therefore
\[
\delta\ge 2n/3-2k+(c-\eta)-\lfloor b/3\rfloor\ge (2n-8k)/3,
\]
and the sharper $L$ inequality is immediate.
\end{proof}
