\section{Missing lemma and falsifiable mechanisms}
CONNECTED-LOG-FLIPS247 constructed an exact radius-$r$ four-category flip histogram in $f(r,d)(n+m)$ indexed-word operations for proper bounded-occurrence ternary CNF, but recovered a model by as many as $n$ full conditioned recompilations, giving $O(nf(r,d)(n+m))$ total work. The missing lemma was whether source identities can be retained algebraically so that only $O(r)$ whole-circuit derivative passes, rather than $O(n)$ independently compiled conditioning tests, suffice to reconstruct one actual model.

The candidate mechanisms tested were the following, each falsifiable: (i) retain individual flip weights $w_i$ in the connected-logarithm compilation without increasing asymptotic circuit size for fixed $r,d$; (ii) obtain the exact number of viable radius-$r$ models containing any selected set of flips from a mixed partial derivative; (iii) recover a witness by selecting a positive first derivative from one reverse sweep instead of trying every source variable; and (iv) check whether fewer abstract inference passes actually reduce fully charged SAT work. The fourth hypothesis fails on the tested SAT and UNSAT examples.

\section{Original source-clause extremes first}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m C_j$ be a proper original 3-CNF; all three literals in each $C_j$ reference distinct original variables. For every signed original occurrence $x_{i,j}$, define the LOCAL target $S_j$ by setting its variable to $1$ if its occurrence is positive and $0$ if negative, making every original literal of $C_j$ TRUE. Define $U_j$ by complementing every one of those local values, making every literal FALSE. Inverting $S_j$ produces $U_j$; inverting $U_j$ produces $S_j$. \emph{These local targets across source clauses need not be simultaneously consistent.} The complete source-indexed lists, both inversions and each occurrence's sign appear in the attached full raw JSON.

For any two distinct original clauses $C_j,C_k$, a source-variable shared with opposite polarity makes $SS$ and $UU$ incompatible, while a same-polarity shared variable makes $SU$ and $US$ incompatible. Both shared types make all four modes impossible and force at least one clause into an intermediate literal-truth state. This does not rule out ordinary satisfaction of both disjunctions. The implementation independently enumerated coherent assignments of each pair's combined original-variable support to check all four modes. The full pair-matrix build is a separate $O(m^2)$ scientific audit; the fast radius compiler does not use it as a free input.

For an actual globally coherent complete assignment $a\in\{0,1\}^n$, $t_j(a)$ is the number of true ORIGINAL literals and $\NN(a)=(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$ counts source clauses in the four categories. Exact source semantics give
\[
 t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\qquad \NN(\bar a)=(N_3,N_2,N_1,N_0)(a),\qquad F(a)=1\iff N_0(a)=0.
\]
The experiment records separate actual $0^n$ and $1^n$, single and multiple coherent flips, every source clause's $t_j$, and whole-assignment inversions. It also returns legitimate original-parent resolvents: from $(x_i\vee A)$ and $(\neg x_i\vee B)$ derive non-tautological $A\vee B$, whose falsifying source-variable cube is impossible by opposite unit demands on $x_i$. Prohibiting a cube is a sound local inference, not an UNSAT decision procedure.

\section{An exact source-weighted generating circuit}
Fix a complete original-variable assignment $a$, a target flip radius $0\le r\le n$, and a maximum original occurrence degree $d$. Put $\Delta\NN(S)=\NN(a\oplus S)-\NN(a)$, and introduce distinct commuting weights $w_1,\ldots,w_n$. Define the formal polynomial
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
 P_{F,a,r}(\boldsymbol w)=\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq[n],\ |S|=r\\N_0(a\oplus S)=0}}\prod_{i\in S}w_i.
\end{equation}
It is homogeneous and squarefree of degree $r$ with coefficient $1$ for every satisfying original-variable flip set. In particular $P(\boldsymbol1)$ is the exact number of such flip sets; no satisfying assignment is supplied to the compiler.

\begin{lemma}[Source-weighted connected logarithm]\label{lem:circuit}
For every proper source ternary CNF with maximum variable occurrence $d$, $P_{F,a,r}$ has a uniformly constructible exact rational arithmetic circuit $\mathcal C$ of at most $C=f(r,d)(n+m)$ arithmetic gates, where $f$ is a computable function independent of $n,m$. The construction includes original-clause parsing, interaction discovery, signed transition calculations and all algebraic joins. The circuit retains a separate input gate for each original flip weight $w_i$. It is a size bound on a circuit for $P$, not a SAT-decision-time lower bound.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $\zz^\delta=\prod_{q=0}^3z_q^{\delta_q}$ for Laurent category labels and form
\[Z(\boldsymbol u,\zz)=\sum_{S\subseteq[n]}\Bigl(\prod_{i\in S}u_i\Bigr)\zz^{\Delta\NN(S)}.\]
The original variable co-occurrence graph $G$ joins variables appearing together in any source clause. Its maximum degree is at most $2d$. If an induced support $H$ is disconnected into $H_1,H_2$, no source clause contains a selected variable from both components. Hence the restricted formal partition function factors $Z_H=Z_{H_1}Z_{H_2}$ and $\log Z_H=\log Z_{H_1}+\log Z_{H_2}$ as formal series around constant term $1$. Thus every coefficient of $\log Z$ whose $\boldsymbol u$-monomial has disconnected support is zero.

Enumerate all connected induced source-variable supports $H$ of size at most $r$, at most $n(1+2d)^{O(r)}$ of them. For each, compute $Z_H$ directly using its at most $2^r$ flipped subsets; source clauses affected by $H$ are found from the at most $d|H|$ incidence entries. Compute $\log Z_H$ through order $r$ via $\sum_{k=1}^r(-1)^{k+1}(Z_H-1)^k/k$, retaining only monomials whose support is exactly $H$. Repeated powers of a variable \emph{must} be retained in the logarithm. Substituting $u_i=t w_i$ and summing over these connected supports constructs coefficients $B_k(\boldsymbol w,\zz)$ of $\log Z$ through order $r$. Recover $E_0=1$ and
\[ E_k=\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^k j B_j E_{k-j},\qquad 1\le k\le r,\]
so $E_r$ is the exact weight-enriched four-category radius-$r$ spectrum. Select and sum its Laurent labels with $N_0(a)+\delta_0=0$ to obtain (\ref{eq:target}). Each selected source flip affects at most $d$ original clauses, so all Laurent exponents lie in a bounded $O(rd)$ box; each truncated local polynomial has a number of terms bounded by a function of $r,d$. Arithmetic-circuit sharing, rather than expanding products of all $w_i$, keeps the size $f(r,d)(n+m)$. Source access, connected-support discovery, finite sums, logarithmic coefficients and exponential reconstruction are all charged.\end{proof}

\section{Derivative witness theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Constructive derivative witness extraction]\label{thm:main}
Let $\mathcal C$ be an explicit arithmetic circuit of size $C$ for (\ref{eq:target}), built before knowing whether the radius-$r$ ball contains a satisfying assignment. There is an exact uniform algorithm that decides whether $P(\boldsymbol1)>0$ and, if so, constructs and checks a specific satisfying original-variable assignment $a\oplus S$ with $|S|=r$ using
\[\boxed{\ O\bigl(r3^r C+n+m\bigr)\ \text{arithmetic operations},\qquad O\bigl(2^rC+n+m\bigr)\ \text{working words}.\ }
\]
In particular, the preceding bounded-occurrence compiler yields the radius-$r$ witness in $g(r,d)(n+m)$ exact indexed-word operations for fixed $r,d$. This replaces the previous $O(n)$ repeated full conditioned compilations with at most $r$ full forward/reverse differentiation passes. For growing $r$, exact rational bit complexity and the possibly large $g(r,d)$ must be charged separately. The procedure is not an all-input polynomial-time SAT algorithm.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any distinct-variable set $T$ write $\partial_T=\prod_{i\in T}\partial/\partial w_i$. The target polynomial is squarefree, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:counts}
 \partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)=\#\{S\subseteq[n]:|S|=r,\ N_0(a\oplus S)=0,\ T\subseteq S\}\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.
\end{equation}
At the start $T=\varnothing$. If the count is zero, there is no radius-$r$ satisfying flip set, and the algorithm returns unresolved for unrestricted SAT. Otherwise, while $|T|<r$, compute simultaneously $\partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)$ and every $\partial_i\partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)$ for $i\notin T$ by one exact arithmetic-circuit forward/reverse pass.

For the forward pass evaluate each circuit gate in the algebra $\mathbb Q[\epsilon_i:i\in T]/(\epsilon_i^2:i\in T)$ after setting $w_i=1+\epsilon_i$ for $i\in T$ and $w_i=1$ for all other original variables. Each gate stores its $2^{|T|}$ coefficients; coefficient $\epsilon_T=\prod_{i\in T}\epsilon_i$ at the output is exactly $\partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)$. Addition takes $O(2^{|T|})$ operations per gate; naive multiplication takes at most $O(3^{|T|})$ operations. Reverse-mode differentiation of this explicit circuit, seeded with the $\epsilon_T$ output coefficient, returns the gradient of $\partial_TP$ with respect to every original $w_i$ in the same $O(3^{|T|}C)$ bound. This is standard reverse automatic differentiation, with the higher mixed partial made explicit by the nilpotent forward extension \cite{BaurStrassen1983}.

Because $P$ is homogeneous squarefree of degree $r$, for $|T|<r$ the exact combinatorial identity
\[ \sum_{i\notin T}\partial_i\partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)=(r-|T|)\partial_TP(\boldsymbol1)>0\]
ensures some $i\notin T$ has positive gradient. Add any such $i$ to $T$; equation (\ref{eq:counts}) remains positive. After exactly $r$ selections, $T$ itself is a valid flip set and source scanning verifies every original clause, reconstructing the full actual assignment. No unverified local occurrence assignment or external answer bit enters the proof. The number of passes is at most $r$, with total arithmetic work $O(r3^rC+n+m)$ and simultaneous coefficient/adjoint storage $O(2^rC+n+m)$.\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Machine restriction.} A word holds $\Theta(\log N)$ bits with an indexed read-only original source $N=\Theta((n+m)\log n)$ where indices use binary encoding. For fixed $r,d$, all exact coefficient numerators have $O(r\log(n+m)+h(r,d))$ bits and denominators are controlled by the finite truncated logarithm; $h$ is a computable function. Thus $g(r,d)$ absorbs multiword arithmetic if needed. No bound is asserted for physical single-tape head crossings without simulating this circuit. This is a constructive upper bound in the stated arithmetic/word model, not a proof of a universal SAT lower bound.

\section{Relationship to primary literature, scope and handoff}
The connected-subgraph coefficient principle is related to Patel and Regts \cite{PatelRegts2017,PatelRegts2019}; differentiating an arithmetic circuit to obtain all first partials is a classical theorem of Baur and Strassen \cite{BaurStrassen1983}. Kullmann's conflict graphs concern actual complementary literal clashes in clauses \cite{Kullmann2003}, whereas the original-indexed $SS/SU/US/UU$ matrix used here describes much stronger all-literals-TRUE/FALSE target compatibility. This report proves an explicit source-variable-weighted application and an $r$-pass extraction bound, not a new general algebraic-complexity theorem or a faster unrestricted SAT solver.

\noindent\textbf{Proof-status ledger.} \textbf{PROVED/EXACT, RESTRICTED}: for the declared source-weighted generating circuit, a radius-$r$ satisfying original assignment can be reconstructed with $O(r3^r C)$ exact circuit operations rather than up to $n$ full conditioned compilations. \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}: the finite independent audits and time/space comparisons. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the expectation that fewer conditioned queries automatically improve actual SAT solver wall time on the measured controls. \textbf{OPEN}: any polynomial dependence on unbounded $r$ for arbitrary strict22 SAT, any general end-to-end dominance claim, and both $P=NP$ and $P\ne NP$.

\noindent\textbf{Next falsifiable lemma:} Construct a persistent \emph{reverse-adjoint cache} for the source-weighted circuit under the successive selected-flip conditions $T$ with provable update cost $o(C)$ per added flip on an infinite bounded-degree family, or exhibit a matched strict22 family on which an $\Omega(C)$ invalidation is necessary for that specified cache representation. Compare against UP-DPLL and XOR/Horn/2-SAT shortcuts with all discovery and replay charged.
