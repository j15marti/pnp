\section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
These four \CAND{} approaches were recorded before the new primary-literature
preflight. They are not claims of completed complexity separation.

\paragraph{I. Canonical-family decision bypass.}
If the clean-core hard formula sequence is produced by a fixed
output-polynomial generator with encoded output length at least the
generator index, then an input can be checked for literal membership in
this sequence by bounded regeneration. Predict a polynomial-time recognizer,
and test whether its trivial NO service bypasses the hard proof system.
Falsifier: the source family may be existence-only, too expensive to generate
relative to actual output length, or require nonuniform choices.

\paragraph{II. Tableau-to-CP by elimination of extensions.}
Starting from a hypothetical SAT decider, compile its execution
and seek a small semantic Cutting Planes refutation of the original CNF,
perhaps eliminating tableau variables. Falsifier: evaluated execution
clauses show only that the machine outputs NO, not that the original clauses
derive contradiction in the same restricted proof system. Uncharged
extension elimination would be an invalid bridge.

\paragraph{III. Restricted certifying-execution transport.}
Define a precise class whose successful UNSAT executions themselves consist
of sound semantic CP inferences. A uniform external log/replay might then
produce a refutation with number of lines bounded by charged execution
events. Falsifier: a decider returning a single bit without exposing these
inferences falls outside the class. The result may be useful but model-scoped.

\paragraph{IV. One fixed family with varying parameters.}
The RS5 ledger has favorable fixed-parameter exponent margins.
Instead of choosing a separate fixed family for each requested exponent,
try the printed growing-parameter regime and the strengthened spread
criterion, charge actual source dimension, and prove a single sequence
with superpolynomial sCP proof size. Falsifier: nonuniform hidden constants,
failure of the strengthened full-image lemma for moving parameters, or a
selector/compiler step only justified with fixed exponents.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
\paragraph{Primary proof-complexity source.}
De Rezende, Fleming, Janett, Nordstr\"om and Pang \cite{deRezende} prove
Theorem~2.3 for \emph{parameter functions} $k(t)$ and $c(t)$ satisfying
$3\le c\le k-1<t/(2\ln t)$. Their source has
\[
2k^2t^{c+1}\le \nu_t\le40k^2(2t)^{c+1},                  \tag{2}
\]
a Resolution refutation of width $k+3$ and size
$O(k^2(4t)^k)$, and a width-$(k+c)$ depth obstruction
$\Omega(t^k)$ in their stated asymptotic convention.
In their Theorem~2.13, a semantic CP proof induces a triangle DAG, and
the printed lifting hypothesis is
\[
b\ge(50\nu/\delta)^{2/\delta}.                          \tag{3}
\]
The originally suggested $b=t^{12\sqrt{k}}$ at
$k=\lfloor t/(4\log t)\rfloor$ does \emph{not} satisfy (3),
because the actual dimension $\nu$ contains $t^{c+1}$.
This is the previously recorded LIFTDIM-FIREWALL obstruction,
not a new criticism of an uninspected theorem.

\paragraph{Spread theorem and existing repair.}
Mossel, Niles-Weed, Sun and Zadik \cite{spread} prove a
probability-normalized spread containment theorem.
ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5, independently audited in RS5REVIEW1, uses it to replace
the dimension-heavy sufficient condition (3) by
\[
 b^\delta\ge 64C_{\rm sp}(w+1)
  \left\lceil\log_2(2\nu)\right\rceil
  \left\lceil\log_2(2b\nu)\right\rceil,                \tag{4}
\]
where $C_{\rm sp}$ is one universal constant, $w=k+c$, and
$0<\delta<1-1/\log_2 b$.
Under (4), a triangle DAG with
$s\le \tfrac12b^{(1-\delta)w}$ and depth $d$
yields a strict Resolution refutation with width $\le w$
and depth $\le wd$. The internal same-row saved-strip and
no-weakening theorem is \EXACT{} at its stated model after RS5REVIEW1.
The complete source-to-signed-$(2,2)$ composition remains \COND{}
pending its separate independent verification.

\paragraph{Proof systems and scope.}
Cook and Reckhow \cite{CookReckhow} distinguish arbitrary polynomial-time
proof verification from p-simulation by a particular proof system.
A hypothetical polynomial SAT decider would enable a short-verifiable
certificate system for UNSAT, but nothing here compels short
semantic CP refutations. CPDEPTHEND1 in this repository \emph{already}
records the published second superpolynomial-depth CP regime.
The proposed variable-parameter specialization is thus a source-matched
repair/check and a \emph{single-family} reformulation, not a claim to
discover the published trade-off for the first time.

\paragraph{Revisions after literature.}

Approach II fails as a generic direct tableau-to-original-CP inference:

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\subsection{Variable-parameter family: the quantitative candidate}
Let the base integer $t$ tend to infinity, and set
\[
 k=\left\lfloor\frac{t}{4\ln t}\right\rfloor,\quad
 c=\lfloor\sqrt{k}\rfloor,\quad
 a=\lceil12\sqrt{k}\rceil,\quad
 \delta=\frac1{4\sqrt{k}},\quad b=t^a,\quad w=k+c.       \tag{5}
\]
These are the parameter functions of the published second illustrative
regime, now tested against the normalized replacement (4). The source
conditions of Theorem~2.3 hold for all sufficiently large $t$.
With the \emph{actual} $\nu$ in (2),
\[
\ell:=\lceil\log_2(2\nu)\rceil=O(\sqrt{k}\log t),\quad
h:=\lceil\log_2(2b\nu)\rceil=O(\sqrt{k}\log t).
\]
Since $k=\Theta(t/\log t)$, this gives
\[
64C_{\rm sp}(w+1)\ell h
=O(C_{\rm sp}k^2(\log t)^2)=O(C_{\rm sp}t^2),
\quad
b^\delta=t^{a/(4\sqrt{k})}\ge t^3.                     \tag{6}
\]
Thus the repaired spread condition holds for sufficiently large $t$
\emph{for every fixed universal} $C_{\rm sp}$.
A finite numerical test with $C_{\rm sp}=1$ cannot certify the
unknown external constant; (6) is the symbolic justification.

Let $H_t$ denote the properized separated-index construction passed
through the actual selector/duplication exact-$(2,2)$ compiler. The
project's fixed-parameter ledger predicts, with source/compiler
constants uniform enough for moving $k,c,a$,
\[
 \log_t L_t\le 4a+c+1+o(\sqrt{k}),\qquad
 \log_t R_t\le a(k+4)+k(1+\log_t4)+O(\log_t k),          \tag{7}
\]
where $L_t$ is the binary encoded length and $R_t$ a supplied
ordinary Resolution refutation size. The admissible sCP cap is
$B_t=\tfrac12b^{(1-\delta)w}$, for which
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \log_t(B_t/R_t)
 &\ge ac-a\delta(k+c)-4a-k(1+\log_t4)
       -O(\log_t k) -o(1)\\
 &=8k-o(k)>0.                                          \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
For any \emph{in-cap} sCP refutation the saved-strip theorem then
forces depth $\ge d_{F_t}(w)/w-O(1)$. Its size is at least its
depth. An \emph{above-cap} proof has size $>B_t$.

\begin{theorem}[Single-family conclusion, explicitly conditional]
\label{thm:single}
Suppose the source width-depth theorem on the functions (5) gives
a uniform quantitative bound
$d_{F_t}(k+c)\ge t^{k-o(k)}$, the separated-index reduction and
properization have the uniform size bound in (7), and the
semantic-CP to triangle reduction and the \emph{actual} selector
compiler retain their size/depth and explicit bit-encoding costs
uniformly for these moving parameters. Then the resulting one
sequence of valid UNSAT signed exact-$(2,2)$ formulas satisfies
\[
 \min_{\tau:\,H_t\vdash_{\sCP}\bot}S(\tau)
      \ge t^{k-o(k)}=L_t^{\omega(1)}.                  \tag{9}
\]
Here $S$ is sCP \emph{line count}, not any SAT-machine time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (6) enables the strengthened spread lift.
Equation (8) places the known Resolution upper witness below
$B_t$ eventually. In-cap refutations have depth at least
$t^{k-o(k)}/w$, and $w=t^{o(k)}$, so their line count is
$t^{k-o(k)}$. Above-cap refutations are even larger since
$\log_t B_t=\Theta(k^{3/2})$.
The denominator $\log_t L_t=O(\sqrt{k})$ in (7), whereas
the numerator lower bound has exponent $k-o(k)$.
Thus $\log S/\log L_t\to\infty$ as claimed.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Status: \CAND{} / \COND{}.}
The original Theorem~2.3 explicitly permits growing parameters, but
the independent RS5 review promotes only the internal triangle lift,
not the full source/compiler composition. In particular, hidden
constants in a fixed-parameter $\Omega(t^k)$ cannot be replaced with
uniform $t^{k-o(k)}$ without checking the original explicit
Cop--Robber bound and all intermediate constructors. Uniform
generator/source code has not been compiled and independently
reproduced for the enormous moving-parameter instances. Therefore
(9) is not labelled PROVED/EXACT for the actual named family.
Even if fully verified, (9) is restricted proof complexity only.

\subsection{An exact polynomial recognizer for a uniform generator}
\begin{lemma}[Output-polynomial generator range]\label{lem:range}
Let $g:\mathbb N\to\{0,1\}^*$ be a fixed deterministic uniform
generator that emits its output as an ordinary bitstream. Suppose
for some fixed $e,C$ and all sufficiently large $t$,
\[
 |g(t)|\ge t,\qquad
 T_g(t)\le C\bigl(|g(t)|+t+2\bigr)^e.                    \tag{10}
\]
Then the \emph{literal} range
$\mathcal G=\{g(t):t\in\mathbb N\}$ is decidable in
deterministic polynomial time on the binary input string.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $x$ with $N=|x|$. The finitely many exceptional small indices
can be handled by a finite table. For every later $t\le N$, simulate
$g(t)$, comparing emitted bits with $x$ as they appear. Abort a
mismatch, an $(N+1)$-st output bit, or at the clock
$C(2N+2)^e$, whichever occurs first; accept only a complete
equal output. If $g(t)=x$, the length bound implies $t\le N$,
and the time bound makes the successful run finish within the
clock. Conversely, any accepted string is a genuine generator
output. There are at most $N$ trials. Clocked simulation,
input reading, generator-code interpretation, resetting,
output-bit comparison and retained memory are all polynomial
in $N$. No unsatisfiability question is asked.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A fast-on-family total decider]
\label{cor:bypass}
If every $g(t)$ is an UNSAT input in a fixed SAT language and $g$
satisfies Lemma~\ref{lem:range}, there exists a correct total
uniform deterministic SAT decider $A_{\mathcal G}$ that uses
polynomial time on the entire range $\mathcal G$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
First test literal membership in $\mathcal G$ and return NO if it
succeeds. Otherwise run an ordinary complete exhaustive SAT
algorithm. This is correct on every string; on $\mathcal G$
it exits after the polynomial recognizer.
\end{proof}
\noindent The decider is \emph{not asserted globally polynomial}.
This proves that proof hardness on an efficiently recognizable
particular family does not imply that \emph{every total correct
decider} is slow \emph{on those same inputs}. It is \emph{not}
a counterexample to a hypothetical theorem quantifying only over
globally polynomial deciders, which may not exist.

\begin{corollary}[A source-specific Cook--Reckhow proof system]
\label{cor:proofsystem}
For any polynomial-time decidable language $\mathcal G$ of UNSAT
formulas, there exists a polynomial-time verifier-defined,
sound and complete proof system $Q_{\mathcal G}$ for UNSAT such
that every $F\in\mathcal G$ has a one-symbol auxiliary proof.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Given $(F,\pi)$, accept if $\pi$ is a distinguished tag and
$F\in\mathcal G$; alternatively accept if $\pi$ is a valid
explicit Resolution refutation of $F$. Both tests are polynomial
in $|F|+|\pi|$. Soundness follows from the UNSAT promise on
$\mathcal G$ and soundness of Resolution. Completeness follows
from completeness of Resolution for unsatisfiable CNFs.
\end{proof}
\noindent Under the additional hypothesis of
Theorem~\ref{thm:single}, $\sCP$ cannot p-simulate
$Q_{\mathcal G}$ on the named family. This is a conditional
\emph{proof-system} separation; it supplies no non-P theorem.

\paragraph{Generator realizability is not granted for free.}
The printed compressed Cop--Robber graph uses modular vertex
classes, and its construction suggests an output-polynomial
generator for the separated-index and selector formulas.
A fully checkable implementation must produce the original
variable quotient, parity clauses, properization, selector
guards, and clone-port gadgets on each index $t$ without
expanding a larger implicit graph or invoking a source-SAT
oracle. It must also verify exact output-length growth.
This concrete implementation remains \OPEN{} and is not
replaced by the general lemma.

\subsection{Transport for a declared certifying machine class}
\begin{lemma}[Restricted proof-producing execution]
\label{lem:certifying}
Consider a uniform deterministic correct UNSAT certifier whose NO-run
physically emits or maintains a sequence of semantic-CP lines,
each new line appearing as a charged computational event, and
whose last line is contradiction. Assume each inference has at most
two legal parents and all source axioms, coefficients, indices,
retained intermediate lines, and final serialized proof are
charged. Then an accepting NO-run of time $T$ produces an sCP
refutation of at most $T$ lines, apart from any explicitly
accounted fixed implementation-constant convention.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The sequence of generated lines is a valid semantic-CP DAG by
the machine's declared invariant. Each new line requires at
least one distinct charged event, so its number does not exceed
the event budget. Outputting or replaying the lines cannot
decrease this count. This is an execution model with the
proof syntax built into the contract.
\end{proof}
\noindent A different correct decider may use Gaussian elimination,
branching, circuits or one unannotated NO bit, and need not
instantiate this certificate class. The lemma does not
convert arbitrary computation into sCP.
