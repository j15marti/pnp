\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
For the FHL source, let X contain shared graph-edge bits, Y the k-clique selectors and Z the (k-1)-colour selectors. The first source side has clauses selecting each slot, preventing duplicate vertices, and asserting edges between selected vertices. The second has clauses requiring one colour per vertex, forbidding two colours per vertex and forbidding adjacent vertices sharing a colour. X occurs \emph{positively only} in the first side and \emph{negatively only} in the second. For each fixed X, the respective sides are satisfiable iff the graph has a k-clique and iff it is $(k-1)$-colourable.

\begin{lemma}[Side-private properization]
Any original clause $(a_1\vee\cdots\vee a_r)$ has a proper ternary existential extension of $O(r)$ clauses using private variables of its original side, with no change in the occurrence sign of X. For r=2 use $(a_1\vee a_2\vee h)\wedge(a_1\vee a_2\vee\neg h)$; for r=1 use all four sign choices for two fresh padding bits; for r greater than 3 use the standard implication OR chain $(a_1\vee a_2\vee h_1)$, $(\neg h_{i-1}\vee a_{i+1}\vee h_i)$, $(\neg h_{r-3}\vee a_{r-1}\vee a_r)$. Every source assignment extends iff it satisfies the source clause. The final $G_n$ has polynomially many proper ternary clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For r greater than 3 set each h_i to 1 exactly when the prefix through $a_{i+1}$ is false. If all source literals are false, the terminal clause contradicts the propagated truth of the last h. Each auxiliary is private and every clause contains three distinct underlying variables. The binary and unary paddings are immediate by checking their possible fresh-bit values.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Binary-semantic substitution projection]
Let T(F) have an explicit map sigma from target variables to source literals or constants; suppose every target clause maps to a source clause or Boolean tautology. Then every m-line binary-semantic CP refutation of T(F) yields one for F with at most m+1 lines and polynomial fully materialized bit cost.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Compose each integer-affine proof line with sigma. Any source assignment induces a Boolean target assignment, so a Boolean-valid two-premise entailment remains valid when evaluated on that assignment. Projected axioms are source axioms or Boolean-valid inequalities, the latter available as semantic axioms or from a seeded input axiom. The final contradiction persists. Coefficient arithmetic and output remain polynomial in the explicit source, sigma and original proof length. No cheap general semantic-proof verification is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-language uncapped hardness]

Every binary-semantic CP refutation of $F_n$ has at least $2^{\Omega((n/\log n)^{1/3})}$ lines. Hence no uniform polynomial upper bound on semantic CP refutations exists over the UNSAT domain of the fixed $L_{22}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
