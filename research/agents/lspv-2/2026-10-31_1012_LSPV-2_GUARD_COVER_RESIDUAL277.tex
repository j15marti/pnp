Put $R=\mathrm{Var}(F)\setminus T$, and for each coherent $b:T\rightarrow\{0,1\}$ define $P(b)=1$ iff $F\!\upharpoonright b$ is satisfiable.
\begin{theorem}
For any $b$ with $P(b)=0$, there is an ordinary-resolution derivation from indexed original clauses of a non-tautological $D_b$ mentioning only variables of $T$ with $F\models D_b$ and $D_b(b)=0$. For any tested set $I$ of rejected $b$, put $Q=\bigwedge_{b\in I}D_b$. Then
\[
(\forall \beta)\quad P(\beta)=1\Longrightarrow Q(\beta)=1.
\]
If a complete ordinary-SAT check refutes $Q$, there is an ordinary-resolution refutation from $F$ by substituting each authenticated $D_b$ proof DAG into the checked refutation of $Q$. If some $F\!\upharpoonright b$ is SAT, a complete original-source SAT witness is recoverable. Exhaustive fallback makes the controller total, not polynomial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Davis--Putnam elimination on the private variables $R$ derives the empty residual clause. Lift each resolution edge to its full indexed original clauses, restoring the discarded $T$-literals. Resolution soundness gives $F\models D_b$, hence the displayed implication. A refutation of $Q$ can be composed with the source resolution derivations of all its premises. A SAT cofactor's private 2-SAT witness together with $b$ is checked against every original $C_j$.
\end{proof}
