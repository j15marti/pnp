\section{Exact spectral separator}
\begin{theorem}[Connected balanced-support spectral firewall]
For every $r\ge1$, the adjacency matrix of $Y_r$ has $-3$ as an eigenvalue
with multiplicity at least two, whereas $A(N_r)+3I$ is nonsingular.  Hence
the equal mixture of $D_1^r,D_0^r$ is decided with zero error on its support
by exact Gaussian elimination.  The predicate is invariant under every
vertex relabelling.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equitable quotient of $Y_r$ is
\[
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&3&3\\3&0&3\\3&3&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
whose eigenvalues are $6,-3,-3$.  Concretely, vectors constant on each colour
class with class values $(1,-1,0)$ and $(1,0,-1)$ lie in
$\ker(A(Y_r)+3I)$.

For $N_r$, let $x_i,y_i$ denote coordinates at $4_i,5_i$, and let the other
five coordinates in block $i$ form its core.  In a hypothetical
$-3$ eigenvector, the five core equations force all core coordinates equal
to a value $q_i$ and
\[
 x_i+y_i=-7q_i.
\]
The $x_i,y_i$ equations reduce to
\[
 16x_i-5y_i+7y_{i+1}=0,\qquad
 -5x_i+16y_i+7x_{i-1}=0.                                 \tag{4}
\]
Diagonalize the cyclic block index with a $(3r)$th root of unity $z$.  The
determinant of the resulting $2\times2$ system is
\[
 256-(-5+7z)(-5+7z^{-1})
 =182+35(z+z^{-1})\ge112>0.                              \tag{5}
\]
Thus every Fourier mode is zero and $A(N_r)+3I$ is nonsingular.  Conjugating
an adjacency matrix by a permutation preserves nullity, proving relabelling
invariance.
\end{proof}

The theorem is \EXACT{}.  It is stronger than the prior component separator
for this candidate because both supports are connected and match all coarse
statistics in (2)--(3).
