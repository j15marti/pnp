\[
 D=(-g_4\vee-g_9\vee-g_{11}),\qquad E=(-g_4\vee-g_8\vee-g_{11}).
\]
Let $G^\circ$ delete both $D$ and $E$. Define $B=(-g_9\vee-g_{11})$, $u=[g_8=0]$ and $v=[-g_4\vee-g_{11}]$. For a source port bit $x$ and source clause-foot bit $a$, let
\[
 P_{xa}=\{(u,v):\exists g_1,\ldots,g_{15}\ (G^\circ\wedge(\neg x\vee B)\wedge(\neg g_4\vee a))\}.
\]

In particular $P_{10}=\varnothing$; for every other input pair there exists an internal extension with $v=1$. The full 35-vertex variable--clause incidence graph of $G$, with precisely the two incidence edges $(g_4,D)$ and $(g_8,E)$ deleted, remains connected.

\section{BUILD II: a universal connected exact22 two-source AND}
\begin{theorem}[Two-source, same-grammar, pointwise conjunction join]\label{thm:join}
Let $H(X)$ and $K(Y)$ be \emph{any} nonempty connected proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNFs with disjoint source variables and clauses of width exactly three. Choose any negative source literal occurrence $\neg x$ in a clause $C_H=(\neg x\vee A)$, and any $\neg y$ in $C_K=(\neg y\vee B)$. The uniform connector $J(H,K)$ described below is connected, proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF, with
\[
 n_J=n_H+n_K+30,\qquad m_J=m_H+m_K+40,
\]
and \emph{for each fixed full original assignment} $(\xi,\eta)$,
\[
 \boxed{\exists\,Z_{30}\ J(H,K)(\xi,\eta,Z_{30})\iff H(\xi)\land K(\eta).}
\]
Neither an SAT answer nor any source-side cofactor is supplied to this constructor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take two disjoint copies $G_H,G_K$ of $G$. Exchange the same-signed negative literal occurrences $\neg x\leftrightarrow\neg g_{4,H}$ between $C_H$ and $D_H$; independently exchange $\neg y\leftrightarrow\neg g_{4,K}$ between $C_K$ and $D_K$. Then exchange the negative literal $\neg g_{8,H}$ in $E_H$ with $\neg g_{8,K}$ in $E_K$. Each operation swaps two negative literal occurrences, preserves exactly two occurrences of both signs of every variable, and cannot introduce a repeated variable within a clause because the two source variable sets and two gadget variable sets were initially disjoint. All new clauses remain distinct: cross-block clauses use signatures on different variable domains and no untouched clause mixes those domains.

For fixed source assignment, set $a=[A(\xi)]$ and $b=[B(\eta)]$. The two independent gadgets satisfy precisely the local relations $P_{xa}$ and $P_{yb}$. The final swap changes their E clauses to
\[
 (v_H\vee u_K)\land(v_K\vee u_H),
\]
where $v_i=(-g_{4,i}\vee-g_{11,i})$ and $u_i=-g_{8,i}$. If either original source clause is false, its $(x,a)=(1,0)$ relation is empty by Lemma~\ref{lem:table}; likewise on the $K$ side. Conversely, if both original source clauses are true, choose the local table witness with $v_H=v_K=1$, making both cross-swapped clauses true. All other clauses of $H,K$ were left unchanged. This proves the displayed pointwise equivalence on both YES and NO assignments.

For connectivity, delete the two chosen source incidence edges. Each resulting source component contains the variable end or the clause end of its deleted edge. Each $G_i$ retains connectedness after deleting both $(g_{4,i},D_i)$ and $(g_{8,i},E_i)$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:table}. The exchanged source--gadget edges attach every source piece to its gadget, and the exchanged $E_H$--$E_K$ edges connect the two gadget blocks. The resulting whole incidence graph is connected.\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Unbounded connected two-answer chains]\label{prop:chain}
For any $k\ge1$, a fixed left-associative sequence of $k-1$ joins of $k$ independent copies of $W_1$ or $G$ produces a connected proper strict exact22 formula with $n=45k-30$ variables and $m=60k-40$ clauses, SAT iff \emph{every} chosen base block is SAT. In particular, there are source-authenticated SAT and UNSAT instances of arbitrarily large size and identical unsigned block connectivity patterns. The constructor is polynomial; its output is not physically shorter than its input.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:join} inductively. Each join adds 30 fresh variables and 40 clauses, and the pointwise existential identities compose. A SAT assignment is obtained by fixed-witness table lookup for each local bridge and each original SAT block. One $W_1$ block forces an UNSAT answer, by its independently enumerated empty model set.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform bounded-width firewall]\label{thm:width}
The above left-associative connected chain of $k$ fixed 15-variable blocks admits a constructible tree decomposition with exactly $2k-1$ bags and width at most $35$, independent of $k$. Its satisfiability is decidable by a uniform deterministic polynomial-time DP over this authenticated decomposition, even when the specified repair seeds all fail.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Give each base $W_1/G$ module one 15-variable bag. At a join, choose a bag from each child that contains the three variables of its selected source clause; such a bag exists by induction. Introduce one central bag containing the six selected source-clause variables and all 30 new gadget variables. Connect this central bag to those chosen child bags, making a tree. Every unmodified source clause is covered inductively. Each modified source clause lies in the central bag because it contains only its two retained source variables and its new gadget variable. Every gadget clause lies entirely there. Each variable shared between a child and the central bag already belonged to the chosen attachment bag, preserving the running-intersection condition. Other variables occur only in their child or central bag. The maximum bag size is $6+30=36$. Starting at $k$ base bags and adding one bag per join gives $2k-1$. The entire decomposition is written and verified from the source construction history; its width bound does not claim that an arbitrary black-box exact22 input has a supplied decomposition. Standard bag-assignment DP runs in $O(k 2^{36}\operatorname{poly}(N))$ bit operations for this fixed interface.\end{proof}

\section{Resource ledgers, barriers, and next proof obligation}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Indexed RAM.} Each join validates source grammar, picks and swaps three pairs of signed clause occurrences, reindexes names, and emits a fully physical $O(N\log N)$-bit child; conservative time $O(N^3)$ and work $O(N\log N)$ bits per join, including failed syntax checks. No constant-time magic random access or uncharged full-size output is assumed. A rank computation on $k\le8$ is a separately paid off-line experiment, not part of the construction.
\item[] \textbf{Read-only-input fixed-$t$ multitape TM.} A conservative realization charges $T_t=O(N^4)$ transitions and $S_t=O(N\log N)$ writable cells with at most $O(N\log N)$ output bits; every sequential source scan, head relocation and re-serialization is included. This is a generous \emph{upper bound for the constructor}, not a SAT solver time bound.
\item[] \textbf{One read/write tape.} A direct scanned simulation conservatively charges $T_1=O(N^5\log N)$ and physical tape footprint $O(N\log N)$ including input, work and output; it does not inherit a random-access index for free.
\item[] \textbf{Two fully writable tapes.} Both source-tape and auxiliary symbol changes are separately chargeable; the above multitape upper bound does not establish any joint write-space lower bound. Changing the native model requires an explicit simulation.
\item[] \textbf{Fixed universal TM.} A fixed interpreter simulates the fixed generator with a polynomial overhead; no specific exponent or oracle step is offered free. SAT/UNSAT are restrictions of one uniform constructor. All failed controller seeds, restart attempts, decomposition construction and full witness verification have been charged within their declared models.
\end{description}
\noindent\textbf{Structural pitfalls.} The original connected-interface obstruction restricts only static syntactic modules. The present join connects the SAT blocks but leaves a polynomially discoverable bounded-width composition tree; it defeats the fixed repair portfolio while remaining easy by DP. Therefore neither connectedness, failed stochastic repair, large unbounded $k$, nor local variable elimination expansion implies a lower bound for \emph{all} adaptive SAT algorithms. A conditional portfolio-doubling scheduler needs an independently proved complete polynomial cover; no such cover is supplied. The quantum one-call architecture needs target-dependent oracle bits; our construction supplies no efficient general oracle or shrinking child. All known time--space lower bounds remain conditional on their native same-machine hypotheses.
