# LSPV-2/452 | Exact gauge-orbit sharing of branching cubic-Tseitin cofactors

2026-11-06 13:27 PST. **PROVED/RESTRICTED (standard GF(2) graph algebra) + COMPUTATIONAL**. Route A primary. P versus NP OPEN.

## Idea, falsifiers, provenance
Hypothesis: distinct unit-propagation-open branches of a published Resolution-hard cubic Tseitin source may become identical signed residuals under *unassigned-edge literal complementations*. Exact falsifiers: (1) equal residual-component syndromes without a valid gauge transformation; (2) an original-clause-UP-open branch having an inconsistent residual tree component; (3) unexpected orbit counts or multiplicities when two or more cyclic components survive. The independent tests rejected all three.

This is a new *project synthesis* of the previously proved LSPV-4/448 branch-rank formulas and classical graph-incidence algebra. We do NOT claim novelty for the linear algebra.

## Family, encoding, and known hardness

Take a connected simple cubic graph G with H vertices and M=3H/2 original edge variables, each with a charge chi_v in GF(2). For a vertex v with ordered incident identifiers a<b<c, take four **original proper 3-CNF** clauses

$$
C_{v,p,q}=\ell_p(x_a)\vee\ell_q(x_b)\vee\ell_{p\oplus q\oplus1\oplus\chi_v}(x_c),\quad p,q\in\{0,1\},\quad
\ell_0(x)=x,\quad\ell_1(x)=\neg x.
$$

These represent the parity equation x_a XOR x_b XOR x_c = chi_v. They contain 4H clauses and 12H literals. Under the previously fixed native bit packet, N=12H(w+1)+4H+12, where w=ceil(log2(M+1)). The archival JSON encodes the same clauses in a *different transport format*, not a native machine input.

For growing 3-regular expander graphs satisfying the published hypotheses, odd-charge Tseitin 3-CNFs have exponential **ordinary shared-DAG Resolution** lower bounds: Urquhart 1987; Ben-Sasson and Wigderson 2001. Pitassi et al. (STOC 2016) establish bounded-depth Frege lower bounds for 3-regular expander Tseitin formulas. The **finite** K4, cube, Petersen, prism16 and K4-chain controls in this run are not individually asymptotic hardness certificates. The known results do not give hardness for Extended Resolution, GF(2), historical R_mix341 or all SAT algorithms.

## Theorem: gauge quotient of all UP-open branches

Select k<=H/2 original edge variables S and fix all 2^k possible values alpha. Delete S from the graph. Let the residual graph consist of r tree components and b cyclic components, c=r+b. Since M-k>=H, b>=1. Let B_S be the vertex-edge incidence submatrix, chi^alpha=chi+B_S alpha, and define component syndrome

$$
\sigma_j(\alpha)=\bigoplus_{v\in C_j}\chi^\alpha_v.
$$

Then:

* The original signed CNF under alpha is unit-propagation-open precisely when every tree-component syndrome vanishes. There are 2^(k-r) such branches (prior LSPV-4/447-448).
* Two residual systems induced by alpha and beta are related by complementing **unassigned** original edge variables if and only if sigma(alpha)=sigma(beta). The same transformation is an isomorphism of the original clauses after substitution.
* The UP-open branches form precisely **2^(b-1) gauge orbits**, each containing **2^(k-r-b+1)** branches. If the total original charge is even, exactly one orbit is SAT; if odd, none is SAT.

### Proof

The image of a connected graph incidence matrix over GF(2) is precisely the even-parity vectors on that component. Thus a difference chi^alpha+chi^beta is generated by complementations of unassigned edges exactly when each residual component sees even parity, equivalently equal syndrome vectors. A spanning forest supplies a concrete transformation by leaf elimination.

Deleting S and contracting the residual components yields a connected multigraph. Its selected-edge incidence map has rank c-1. Hence the c-dimensional syndrome vectors form an affine parity hyperplane with 2^(c-1) values, each with 2^(k-c+1) preimages. Imposing the r tree-component zero conditions leaves 2^(b-1) syndromes and the claimed equal preimage counts. The remaining total-parity condition determines whether the all-zero syndrome occurs.

For unit propagation, tree components are completely eliminated by degree-one stripping and are consistent iff their component charge parity vanishes. Cyclic components retain a nonempty core, so an inconsistent component parity need not be detected by ordinary unit propagation. QED.
