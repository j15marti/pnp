\section{A connected four-partition exact-$(2,2)$ source}
Let $n=6s$ and $r=n/3$. The variables are $x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1}$. A \emph{triple partition} is a partition of all $n$ variables into $r$ unordered three-element blocks. Form four partition layers $A,B,C,D$, each block becoming one clause. Take the two deterministic layers
\[
 A_j=\{x_{3j},x_{3j+1},x_{3j+2}\},\qquad
 B_j=\{x_{3j+1},x_{3j+2},x_{3(j+1\bmod r)}\}.
\]
The union of $A$ and $B$ joins successive blocks into a cycle, so the incidence graph is connected regardless of $C,D$. A random layer means a uniformly random permutation of $n$ names grouped into successive triples. Choose $C,D$ independently. For a prefix variable set $U$, let $\partial U$ contain the variables of $U$ that occur in a clause also containing a variable outside $U$. A clause is \emph{mixed} if it meets both $U$ and its complement.

\begin{lemma}[Capacitated signing, deterministic polynomial-time]
For \emph{every} four-partition clause-scope hypergraph above, one can assign exactly two negative and two positive occurrences to each variable so that every clause has at least one negative literal. Thus the resulting proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF is satisfied by the explicit word $0^n$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Create a bipartite incidence graph with a left vertex for each of the $4n/3$ clauses and two capacity-one copies of each of the $n$ variable names on the right. Every clause is adjacent to the two copies of its three variables. For any set $J$ of clauses, it has $3|J|$ incidence edges, while each neighboring variable is incident to at most four. Hence $|N(J)|\ge3|J|/4$, so the two copies provide at least $2|N(J)|\ge3|J|/2\ge|J|$ neighbors. Hall's theorem~\cite{hall1935} gives a matching assigning each clause to a variable, using at most two clauses per variable. Mark these chosen literal occurrences negative. Each variable has four total occurrences and at most two marked, so fill unmarked occurrences until it has exactly two negatives. Filling cannot destroy any clause's preexisting negative literal. Every clause is therefore true at $x=0^n$. The matching is found by an ordinary polynomial-time bipartite-flow algorithm. This procedure chooses signs \emph{after} scope selection and does not change any scope or frontier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Linear midpoint moving frontier, asymptotic existence]
For every sufficiently large $n$ divisible by six (in particular $n\ge600$ is sufficient under the union bound below), there exist \emph{four mutually triple-disjoint} layers $A,B,C,D$ with the fixed connected $A,B$ above such that for every variable order $\pi$,
\[
\left|\partial\{\pi(1),\ldots,\pi(n/2)\}\right|>\frac{n}{400}.
\]
After the capacitated signing, this is a connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF with an explicit satisfying witness $0^n$. It has exactly $4n/3$ clauses, $4n$ literal occurrences, and physical unpadded DIMACS size $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ bits. The construction is \emph{existential}; no deterministic polynomial-time algorithm selecting good $C,D$ for every $n$ is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a balanced subset $U\subseteq[n]$ with $|U|=n/2$, and let $t=n/3$. For one random triple partition, a mixed block has one or two points of $U$, giving six ordered color patterns, while a nonmixed block has two patterns. For $i$ mixed blocks, the number of positional binary colorings is at most ${t\choose i}2^{t-i}6^i=2^t{t\choose i}3^i$. Uniform permutation maps the fixed set $U$ to a uniform $n/2$-subset of all $n$ positions. Set $\varepsilon=1/100$ and $\alpha=3\varepsilon=0.03$. The probability of at most $\varepsilon n$ mixed blocks is at most
\[
 p_n\le\frac{2^t\sum_{i\le\alpha t}{t\choose i}3^i}{{n\choose n/2}}
 \le(n+1)(t+1)2^{-2n/3+(n/3)h_2(\alpha)+\varepsilon n\log_2 3},
\]
using ${n\choose n/2}\ge2^n/(n+1)$ and the binary-entropy upper bound ${t\choose i}\le2^{t h_2(i/t)}$. Since $\alpha<1/2$, $h_2(i/t)\le h_2(\alpha)$ for the indices used. If some balanced $U$ has at most $\varepsilon n$ mixed clauses in \emph{each} of the independent layers $C,D$, a union bound over at most $2^n$ choices of $U$ gives
\[
 \Pr[\exists U\text{ bad}]
 \le(n+1)^2(t+1)^2 2^{\beta n},\quad
 \beta=-\frac13+\frac23h_2(0.03)+0.02\log_2 3
 <-0.1720.
\]
This tends exponentially to zero. An exact triple of a fixed partition belongs to an independent random partition with probability $2/((n-1)(n-2))$. The five partition pairs involving at least one of $C,D$ therefore have collision probability at most $10n/[3(n-1)(n-2)]$. The fixed $A,B$ have no common triple. For $n\ge600$, the sum of these two error bounds is less than one (and decreases thereafter). Thus some pair $C,D$ has no repeated scope and ensures every balanced $U$ has \emph{more than} $\varepsilon n$ mixed clauses in at least one of $C,D$. Each variable of $U$ belongs to only four clauses total. At least $\varepsilon n/4=n/400$ distinct processed variables are consequently incident to crossing clauses. Every variable order has a balanced prefix, so the lower bound is independent of the order. The Hall-signing lemma finishes the proof. Four literal occurrences per variable and three per clause imply $3m=4n$, and variable identifiers of length $\Theta(\log n)$ give the source-size bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Strict interpretation.} The theorem forces $\Omega(n)$ \emph{live variable names} in a raw clause-scope, variable-ordering dynamic program. It does not prove that $2^{\Omega(n)}$ distinct \emph{feasible semantic residuals} must be stored, nor any time/space lower bound against algorithms using symbolic relations, algebra, direct witness checking, or preprocessing. Indeed this source can be decided by checking the all-zero witness. The signing and partition choice are separate issues; the matching is efficient, whereas finding/certifying an asymptotically expanding pair $C,D$ by naive exhaustive search need not be.

\section{Connected arbitrary-core attachment: an exact projection firewall}
\begin{proposition}[Hard-core retention under a satisfiable tautological bridge]
Let $R(X)$ be any connected proper ordinary 3CNF with at least one variable $x$, and let $G(Y)$ be a disjoint connected all-zero-satisfiable strict exact-$(2,2)$ source from the preceding construction. Choose $y\in Y$ and fresh $t,u,v$. Put
\[
\begin{split}
\mathrm{Force}(t;u,v)= {} &(t\vee u\vee v)\wedge(t\vee u\vee\neg v)\\
&\wedge(t\vee\neg u\vee v)\wedge(t\vee\neg u\vee\neg v),\\
F={}&R(X)\wedge G(Y)\wedge\mathrm{Force}(t;u,v)
\wedge(x\vee y\vee t).
\end{split}
\]
Then $F$ is connected proper ordinary 3CNF and
$\exists_{Y,t,u,v}F(X,Y,t,u,v)\equiv R(X)$, pointwise for all assignments to $X$. The \emph{combined} formula is not exact-$(2,2)$, since the bridge adds occurrences.
For a fixed $G$ of six variables and any connected source sequence $R_q$ with $M_q\to\infty$ physical encoded bits, the child produced by \emph{shell-only stripping} has $M_q$ bits, while the parent has $N_q=M_q+O(\log M_q)$ bits under the canonical sequential DIMACS framing. Hence $M_q>N_q^\lambda$ eventually for every fixed $\lambda<1$. One such stripping step also lacks repeatable closure on unrestricted $R$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The four force clauses jointly imply $t=1$, since $t=0$ requires each of the four possible $u,v$ assignments to violate at least one clause. Conversely $t=1$ satisfies all four for any $u,v$. Therefore the bridge $(x\vee y\vee t)$ is automatically true. Since $G$ is satisfiable, projection of $F$ onto the $X$ variables is exactly $R$. The force clauses join fresh names to each other, and the bridge joins the previously connected variable-clause incidence components $R$ and $G$. All clauses contain three distinct variables. For fixed six-variable $G$, the source header, eight shell clauses, four force clauses and one bridge clause add only $O(\log q)$ bytes using identifiers at most $q+9$; $R_q$ retains its bytes apart from the changed header. Thus $N_q=M_q+O(\log M_q)$, and $M_q/N_q\to1$. Since $N_q^\lambda=o(N_q)$, a child retaining the core cannot achieve power shrinkage. A different semantic algorithm may of course decide or compress $R_q$; no impossibility theorem for arbitrary SAT follows.
\end{proof}

\section{Resource ledgers and publication discipline}
For the \emph{recognized four-partition, all-zero-satisfied source grammar}, the indexed-RAM validator reads the actual $N$ source bits, checks canonical layers $A,B$, both variable-permutation promises for $C,D$, every literal and sign quota, and every clause's negative literal. It uses $O(n)$ writable bits for per-variable counters and layer-seen flags plus $O(\log N)$ for addresses, with $O(N)$ word operations on $\Theta(\log N)$-bit words (or $O(N\log N)$ conservative bit operations). If duplicate-free scopes are additionally required, store or sort the $O(n)$ triples and charge $O(N)$ more bits; avoiding duplicate-free certification does not affect connectivity or the all-zero decision. On this \emph{restricted} accepted grammar the full validator outputs one actual 144-bit proper YES base; malformed source is rejected, not silently accepted as a general SAT input. Above $N\ge755$, the output satisfies $144\le N^{3/4}$. This is a trivial restricted decision shrinker, not a uniform map on all ordinary SAT.

A read-only-input, one-work-tape deterministic TM may keep $O(n)$ finite-alphabet work cells for flags and counters and reach a variable's counter by $O(n)$ work-head moves per literal. Its conservative same-machine ledger is $T_2=O(N+n^2)=O(N^2)$, $S_2=O(n+\log N)$ excluding input, $Q_2,H_2=O(T_2)$, output $L_2=144$ bits. Parsing and invalid-input handling are included. A literal one-read/write-tape simulation with the initial input still present has $P_1=O(N+S_2+\log(T_2+2))=O(N)$ cells and $T_1=O(T_2(N+S_2+\log(T_2+2)))=O(N^3)$ transitions; its head moves and crossings are already charged in transitions. These are safe \emph{upper constructions}, not lower bounds. For a fixed universal interpreter, the program is a constant-size description and interpreting these finite-tape operations takes polynomial time and polynomial total tape footprint with the program/input charged. No Hennie--Stearns two-tape bound is used as a one-tape simulation. No circuit has been materialized here: there is no fictitious gate count, wire-to-gate equality, or uncharged virtual child. The attachment compiler must explicitly emit all $M_q$ bits of its sole child, and if its output tape is reused as readable work, those $M_q$ bits also count toward workspace.
