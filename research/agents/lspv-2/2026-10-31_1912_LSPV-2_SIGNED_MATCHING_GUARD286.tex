\section*{Exact known lemma and concrete original-source proof}
Fix a valid duplicate-free proper monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) original source F,
a coherent partial original-variable assignment p, and f free variables.
Let $R_s$ be the original clauses of polarity $s\in\{+,-\}$ not yet
satisfied by fixed literals. A free original variable covers at most two
clauses in $R_s$ because it has two occurrences per sign. Let
$\tau_s=|R_s|-\nu(H_s)$ be the exact one-sign minimum edge/singleton cover
cost computed using maximum matching. Empty required clauses are immediate
conflicts. Any satisfying extension requires at least $\tau_+$ free
TRUE variables and at least $\tau_-$ free FALSE variables. Thus
\[
 \boxed{\tau_+(p)+\tau_-(p)>f\Longrightarrow
   F\text{ has no satisfying extension of }p.}
\]
Proof: the TRUE and FALSE free-variable sets are disjoint, jointly
of size f, and must separately cover the positive and negative source
requirements. The edge-cover identity is classical. No indexed occurrence
$x_{i,j}$ is independently assignable.

The concrete connected canonical source has original clauses
\[
\begin{split}
C_0&=(1,2,4),\quad C_1=(2,3,5),\quad C_2=(1,3,6),\\
C_3&=(4,7,8),\quad C_4=(5,7,9),\quad C_5=(6,8,9),\\
C_6&=(-1,-2,-7),\quad C_7=(-2,-3,-8),\quad C_8=(-1,-3,-9),\\
C_9&=(-4,-5,-7),\quad C_{10}=(-4,-6,-8),\quad C_{11}=(-5,-6,-9).
\end{split}
\]
On $p=(x_4,x_5,x_6,x_7,x_8,x_9)=(0,0,0,1,1,1)$,
the three positive and three negative required source clauses form
triangles. Each maximum matching has one edge; hence
