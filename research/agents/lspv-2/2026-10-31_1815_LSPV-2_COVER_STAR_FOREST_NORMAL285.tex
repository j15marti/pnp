\paragraph{Target and quantifiers.}
For every well-formed duplicate-free proper monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) original source F with n variables and m=4n/3 clauses, define positive and negative clause-dual cubic loopless multigraphs G_+,G_- (vertices original same-sign clause IDs, edges original variable IDs). Invalid binary inputs are rejected. A complete SAT witness a corresponds to an edge cover of G_+ consisting of its true variables and a simultaneous edge cover of G_- consisting of its false variables.
\begin{theorem}[Two-sided normalization; elementary source-certified theorem]
For every satisfiable target source F there exists a satisfying original-source assignment a^- with at most \lfloor n/2\rfloor ones and a satisfying assignment a^+ with at least \lceil n/2\rceil ones. From any supplied complete source-checked SAT witness a, both can be constructed by one pass through the variable indices, with at most n candidate changes. Given an authenticated signed clause incidence index, the normalization takes O(n+m) additional time and O(n+m) words. Finding the initial witness, checking original source grammar and constructing the index are NOT free.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a^- inspect each true variable x and flip 1 to 0 precisely if both indexed positive source clauses containing x have at least two currently true literals. Every accepted flip leaves all positive clauses true and only improves negative ones. Each rejected flip has an original positive clause with exactly one currently true literal. Later flips cannot make a rejected variable removable, hence the selected edges form an inclusion-minimal edge cover of G_+. Such a cover is a spanning star forest: an edge connecting two selected-degree-2-or-more vertices could otherwise be removed. Every star has degree d\le3 and d/(d+1)\le3/4. As G_+ has 2n/3 clause vertices, at most (3/4)(2n/3)=n/2 selected edges remain. Symmetrically, turn false variables true only if their two indexed negative source clauses have at least two true literals. This gives a minimal negative cover containing at most n/2 false variables. Every step retains source clause and variable identifiers, witnesses are independently checked against every original clause.
\end{proof}
