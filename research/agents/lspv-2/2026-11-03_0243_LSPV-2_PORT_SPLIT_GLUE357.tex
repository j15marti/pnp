Let $P=(x_1,\dots,x_k)$ be any ordered set of $k$ distinct source variables (\emph{ports}), $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^k$, and let $F\upharpoonright\alpha$ be the ordinary restricted formula, dropping clauses satisfied by $\alpha$ and deleting falsified port literals. Define the \emph{blocking clause} $B_\alpha=\bigvee_{i=1}^k(x_i\text{ if }\alpha_i=0\text{ else }\neg x_i)$. \begin{theorem}[Source-authenticated port-gluing inequality]
\label{thm:glue}
For every finite UNSAT canonical general CNF $F$ and every ordered port set $P$ of size $k\ge0$, all $2^k$ residual formulas are UNSAT and
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\ \le\ W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)
\ \le\ \sum_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^k}W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F\upharpoonright\alpha)+2^k-1.}
\]
A uniform effective constructor takes \emph{explicit}, independently checkable residual refutation DAGs and emits one strict, source-indexed RES refutation of $F$ with the displayed upper bound. There is no theorem that arbitrary mixed-system residual proofs lift by this construction: its input certificates use RES only. The proof-length bound is polynomial in the total \emph{supplied} branch proof bytes plus $2^k$ and $N$, not polynomial in $N$ without a restriction on $k$ and the residual proof costs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an assignment $\alpha$ and a strict RES refutation $\pi_\alpha$ of $F\upharpoonright\alpha$. Lift each residual source clause $D=C\upharpoonright\alpha$ to its authenticated original source clause $C$. This source is not satisfied by $\alpha$, hence $C=D\cup E$ for some set $E$ of false port literals, all belonging to $B_\alpha$. Process the residual DAG in topological order. Maintain for each residual derived clause $D$ a lifted original proof clause $L=D\cup E_D$ with $E_D\subseteq B_\alpha$ and all original unassigned-variable literals preserved exactly. If a residual strict RES inference on unassigned pivot $y$ combines $D_1$ and $D_2$, their lifts contain the same complementary $y$ literals, and a single genuine original-source RES inference produces the exact union $D\cup(E_{D_1}\cup E_{D_2})$; it is non-tautological because both residual RES and the uniform false-port set $B_\alpha$ are. Thus every residual paid inference lifts to exactly one original paid RES inference, with shared antecedents reused. At the residual contradiction $D=\bot$, the lifted root is some clause $L_\alpha\subseteq B_\alpha$, possibly already empty.

Build a full binary proof-combination tree on the port variables, in a fixed order. At a node whose left child sets $x_i=0$, its available clause is a subset of the common already-falsified port literals plus $x_i$; the right child's available clause is a subset of the same common literals plus $\neg x_i$. If one child omits the branch literal, inherit that child's already derived clause at zero additional cost; otherwise resolve the two child clauses strictly on $x_i$ at one paid node. The inherited or resolved clause is a subset of the blocker of the common partial assignment. At the root no variables are assigned and the only subset of the empty clause is $\bot$. At most $2^k-1$ merge nodes are needed. Original source leaves are retained once, independently of how many residual proofs refer to them. Consequently the total distinct paid DAG-node count cannot exceed the displayed sum. Since source-authenticated RES is already legal in $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, the mixed optimum is no larger. \hfill$\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Strong Horn/Krom-port necessary-hardness screen]
Let $n$ be the number of occurring source variables and $k$ the size of a \emph{strong} backdoor $P$ such that every restricted formula $F\upharpoonright\alpha$ is either Horn or 2CNF (its choice of class can depend on $\alpha$). Then for UNSAT $F$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le(2n^2+2)2^k-1.}
\]
In particular, if $k=O(\log N)$, the source admits a polynomial-cost refutation even if finding the backdoor is expensive. Hence any superpolynomial-cost sequence in this fixed mixed calculus must have minimum Horn/2CNF-union strong backdoor size $\omega(\log N)$ along a suitable hard subsequence. This is a necessary test, not a sufficient hardness condition.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Complete binary Resolution saturation of any UNSAT 2CNF stays within clause width two. Over at most $n$ names there are exactly $2n+4\binom n2+1=2n^2+1$ possible non-tautological unit, binary and empty clauses; retaining each newly produced resolvent once yields a refutation with at most $2n^2+1$ paid nodes (by Resolution completeness). For UNSAT Horn formulas, Horn unit propagation derives at most $n$ distinct positive units, each by at most $n$ strict source-Resolution steps from its authentic Horn implication, and a final violated purely negative source clause is discharged by at most $n$ further steps. Thus at most $n^2+n\le2n^2+1$ nodes suffice. Substitute this uniform residual bound into Theorem~\ref{thm:glue}; there are $2^k$ branches. For any fixed polynomial threshold, a family with $k\le c\log N$ on all sufficiently large inputs is polynomially bounded, so a genuinely superpolynomial sequence requires an unbounded ratio $k/\log N$ on an appropriate hard subsequence. \hfill$\square$
\end{proof}

\section*{Tightness and a mathematical firewall}
Let $\mathrm{BLOCK}_k$ consist of the $2^k$ distinct width-$k$ clauses falsified by the $2^k$ assignments to $k$ variables. Every clause is indispensable: deleting its unique assignment blocker admits precisely that assignment. Each restriction by all $k$ ports has an explicit empty source, so every residual minimum is zero; the gluing theorem gives $W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(\mathrm{BLOCK}_k)\le2^k-1$. Since the fixed calculus has charged at-most-two-premise inferences, the source-ancestry lower bound yields $W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(\mathrm{BLOCK}_k)\ge2^k-1$. Thus
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(\mathrm{BLOCK}_k)=2^k-1.}
\]
This sharpness is an elementary source-count application, not a new hard family: its encoded source is already of size $\Theta(2^k k\log(k+2))$ bits for signed decimal DIMACS. A small \emph{interface alone} is not a hardness or ease guarantee. The crucial quantity is the complete portfolio of residual proof costs and their authentic gluing; an interface of size zero may still leave any general UNSAT formula unchanged.
