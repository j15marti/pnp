\section{Source primal blocks and the local sign-balance lemma}
Build a primal graph on source variables, adding a triangle for each unsigned proper ternary support. Decompose every connected component into maximal biconnected blocks and articulation-variable nodes. Each clause triangle belongs to exactly one block, as does its sign-reversed support. For a block $B$, write its assigned positive/negative unsigned support sets $P_B,Q_B$, paired core $H_B=P_B\cap Q_B$, unmatched supports $P'_B=P_B\setminus Q_B,Q'_B=Q_B\setminus P_B$, and $q_B=|P'_B|$.

\begin{lemma}[Local signed balance; PROVED/EXACT]
For every $F\in\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$, every block $B$, and every source variable $v\in B$,
\[
\deg^+_{F_B}(v)=\deg^-_{F_B}(v)\le2.
\]
Consequently $|P'_B|=|Q'_B|=q_B$, each block has $q_B=0$ or $q_B\ge2$, and $q(F)=\sum_Bq_B$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
No clause crosses a block, because its three variables form a triangle. At a leaf of the block-cut forest, all vertices except possibly the attachment articulation $x$ occur only in that block, hence have signed degree difference zero. Since every signed clause contains three literals of its sign,
\[
3(|P_B|-|Q_B|)=\sum_{v\in B}(\deg^+_B(v)-\deg^-_B(v))
=\deg^+_B(x)-\deg^-_B(x).
\]
Each term on the right is bounded by two in absolute value, hence this multiple of three is zero. The attachment vertex is locally balanced. Removing that leaf preserves zero signed excess for the remaining graph and cannot increase either sign count. Repeat leaf elimination; this proves balance for all blocks. Equal sign incidence counts imply $|P_B|=|Q_B|$. Removing matched supports leaves equal per-variable signed degrees and equal unmatched cardinalities; a sole positive and negative unmatched triple would have identical incidence and would actually be paired. Clause partition gives the global sum.
\end{proof}
