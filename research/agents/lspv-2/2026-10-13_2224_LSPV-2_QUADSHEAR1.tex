\section{Quadratic payload-shear firewall}
Let $u\in\F^m$, $v\in\F^n$, and let
\[
 P:\F^m\longrightarrow\F^n                              \tag{2}
\]
be any explicitly encoded Boolean polynomial map of degree at most two.
Define
\[
 S_P(u,v)=(u,v+P(u)).                                    \tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[quadratic payload shear]
For all $m,n\ge1$ and every $P$ as in (2), $S_P$ is a quadratic involution.
Let $d=m+n$, $A,B\in\operatorname{GL}_d(\F)$, and $p,q\in\F^d$.  Then
\[
 Q(y)=B S_P(Ay+p)+q                                      \tag{4}
\]
is a quadratic permutation.  Every $b\in\F^d$ has the unique preimage
\[
 y=A^{-1}\left(S_P\!\left(B^{-1}(b+q)\right)+p\right),  \tag{5}
\]
computable in time polynomial in the explicit record length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In characteristic two,
\[
 S_P(S_P(u,v))=(u,v+P(u)+P(u))=(u,v),                    \tag{6}
\]
so $S_P^{-1}=S_P$.  Inverting (4) from right to left gives (5); uniqueness
follows because every factor is bijective.  If $P$ is supplied by its Boolean
ANF, evaluating it costs polynomial time in that explicit encoding.  Gaussian
elimination obtains $A^{-1}$ and $B^{-1}$ in $O(d^3)$ bit operations.  No
inverse, source witness, or normal form is hidden advice.
\end{proof}

The finite schema strengthens the visual obstruction without affecting (6).
It chooses $n$ linearly independent quadratic ANF masks among the
$\binom m2$ squarefree degree-two monomials.  It rejects an output matrix $B$
unless every row has a nonzero payload combination.  Therefore every one of
the $d$ displayed output coordinates of (4) has a nonzero quadratic polar
part.  Invertible input substitution preserves this nonzeroness.  The payload
may be dense and arbitrary subject only to the declared independence check;
the inverse still evaluates that same payload exactly once.

\section{Exact Tseitin realization and costs}
For odd $s\ge5$, take $X=K_{s,s}$ and the star-of-stars spanning tree
\[
 T=\{(0,j):0\le j<s\}\cup\{(i,0):1\le i<s\}.
\]
Its cotree has $d=(s-1)^2$ edges.  Projection to cotree values is a
polynomial-time bijection from every consistent affine coset
$\{x:B_Xx=c\}$ to $\F^d$.  Given target $b$, compute (5), place the result on
the cotree, and solve tree edges leaf-to-root.  The last vertex equation is
consistent exactly when the explicit charge has even parity.

Thus every valid record has one cotree preimage and one complete edge witness
for the fixed charge.  The projected affine interface rank is $\rho=d$.  The
tested payload uses $O(nm^2)$ explicit bits, the two affine matrices use
$2d^2$ bits, and the complete edge output has $s^2$ bits.  Parsing, rank
checks, inversion, payload evaluation, reconstruction, and verification are
all polynomial in the encoded input length.  The generic
$2^\rho\operatorname{poly}(N)$ enumeration bound remains correct but is very
loose on this structured subclass.

\section{Hostile controls and scope}
The construction survives variable renaming, complementation, input/output
phases, arbitrary explicit invertible affine mixing, independent products,
and dense compact quadratic circuits.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP wrappers
cannot remove the inverse when recognition and replay are charged.  The
algorithm uses polynomial workspace and does not emit a DNNF, PC/PCR proof,
Resolution proof, pebbling, or expanded global ANF.

The visible first component of $S_P(u,v)$ exposes $u$; this is exactly the
algorithmic leak.  Consequently QUADSHEAR1 is a counterexample to a
\emph{structural premise}, not a claim that arbitrary MQ is easy.  It does
not solve a source-matched residual that hides $u$, does not define an
NP-complete all-input language, and does not prove $P=NP$ or $P\ne NP$.
Horn, Krom, affine, and bounded-treewidth residuals remain polynomial hostile
controls for their own reasons; the present theorem uniformly permits such
payloads but does not identify their formula representations.

The inverse relativizes and algebrizes.  It provides no nonrelativizing
hinge, and Natural Proofs is not directly invoked because no large useful
circuit property is proposed.  ETH, cryptographic assumptions, random
oracles, and empirical MQ hardness are not used.

\paragraph{Post-quantum technique ledger.}
The payload quadratic-mask space and singleton collision distribution are
\textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFERS} as algebraic diagnostics.  Inferring

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
BIJECTIVEQFW1 gives one explicit triangular quadratic permutation.
QUADSHEAR1 is not another instance of the same recurrence: it proves an
involution for \emph{every} explicit quadratic payload $P$, permits a
linearly growing independent payload-relation space, and forces every
displayed coordinate to remain nonlinear after output mixing.  Older shear
mentions did not provide this typed residual theorem and audit.

There is no contradiction with general MQ hardness or exact-$(2,2)$
NP-completeness.  The structured record exposes a polynomial-automorphism
decomposition.  There is no contradiction with proof-system lower bounds,
which quantify over different resources.  The experiment does not estimate
$H(F)$ by timing.
