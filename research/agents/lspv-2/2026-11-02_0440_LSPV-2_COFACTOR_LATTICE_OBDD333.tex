\section{Exact all-order OBDD recurrence}
Fix any Boolean function $f$ on $n$ named original variables. For a set $S$ of variables already queried and any $v\notin S$, let
\[
 A_f(S,v)=\left|\{f|_a:a\in\{0,1\}^S,\ (f|_a)|_{v=0}\not\equiv(f|_a)|_{v=1}\}\right|,
\]
where the braces count \emph{distinct Boolean continuation functions}, not syntactic pin keys or SAT labels. The two cofactors in this test must agree or disagree on \emph{all future assignments}, not just current satisfiability. For order $\pi$, set $S_k=\{\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_k\}$. Bryant's ROBDD reduction and uniqueness rules imply exactly
\[
   {\rm Nodes}_{\rm ROBDD}(f,\pi)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} A_f(S_k,\pi_{k+1}).
\]
Indeed, at the level testing $v$, a distinct residual depending essentially on $v$ gives one nonterminal node; those independent of $v$ are skipped, and identical residuals are merged. The set of possible residuals after assigning $S$ is independent of the \emph{order} in which $S$ was assigned. Thus, for $V=\{1,\ldots,n\}$,
\[
 L(V)=H(V)=0,\quad
 L(S)=\min_{v\notin S}\bigl(A_f(S,v)+L(S\cup\{v\})\bigr),\quad
 H(S)=\max_{v\notin S}\bigl(A_f(S,v)+H(S\cup\{v\})\bigr).
\]
This optimizes over \emph{every} one of the $n!$ fixed variable orders by $n2^{n-1}$ subset transitions \emph{after} acquiring all edge costs. It does not assert that this acquisition is polynomial or compulsory for SAT.

Let $C_{\min}(V)=C_{\max}(V)=1$ and recursively sum child counts over optimal/minimal or maximal choices. To sum node counts over all orders without explicitly listing them, use
\[
 Q(V)=0,\qquad Q(S)=\sum_{v\notin S}\left(Q(S\cup\{v\})+(n-|S|-1)!\,A_f(S,v)\right).
\]
Then $Q(\emptyset)/n!$ is the exact mean node count over all $n!$ orders. These are elementary, established OBDD/subset dynamic programming facts specialized here to a source-authenticated F15 benchmark, not a novel general SAT lower bound.
