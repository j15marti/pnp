## Theorem 595.1, accepted triplex native suffix union

Let three native /386 certificates share identical SOURCE prefix of length N, the same specification and globally paid gate bank G, with R signed-RES inferences apiece. Suppose every RES parent is a common SOURCE record or earlier in its own suffix. Append the three suffixes after one SOURCE prefix. For lane j, map each old suffix reference p >= N to p+jR, leaving p<N unchanged. Then exact SOURCE membership, signed Resolution parent clauses and pivots are preserved. If the last record is empty and intermediate empties are accepted, the combined record array remains a valid native refutation with paid total 3R+|G|. Proof: chronological induction within each offset-renumbered suffix; the common SOURCE records are unchanged. ## Theorem 595.2, cost-sound chronological provenance simulation

Represent each actual SOURCE occurrence by a unique source atom, each original paid owner by a single owner atom, each defining DEF facet by a node linked to its paid owner, and each signed Resolution result by an AND node of its two exact prior indexed record roots. For every exact signed clause C, retain an OR over all chronologically discovered roots for C. Every native step has a corresponding abstract step preserving its exact original dependency. Charge 1 for the first derived RES appearance of an exact signed clause and 0 for later duplicates; a native RES step always costs 1. Declare each owner once globally. Hence every abstract transition costs at most its actual native transition, and the terminal empty clause remains represented. Each native node introduces O(1) circuit nodes and edges, yielding linear total symbolic circuit size. This is a **forward MAY lemma** only. No reverse lifting, all-rule alpha/gamma closure, or machine-time lower bound is claimed.
