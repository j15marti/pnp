\section{Source-graded restriction floors}
Let $F$ be a proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF: $n$ variables, $m=4n/3$ distinct proper ternary clauses, each source twice positive and twice negative. Let $\rho$ assign $k$ original sources. Define a \emph{raw ordinary child} by deleting satisfied original clause records, then falsified literals in surviving clauses, retaining all surviving records including empty clauses. No global propagation, semantic compression, early UNSAT branch disposal or alternate representation is included in this model.
\begin{lemma}[PROVED/EXACT]
Every raw conditioned child has at least $m-2k$ surviving original clause records, of which at least $m-4k$ are wholly untouched. The latter remain distinct after pure duplicate elimination.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each assigned source makes precisely two of its four signed occurrences true, so at most $2k$ original clauses become satisfied. At most $4k$ original clauses contain any fixed occurrence. Untouched original proper clauses were distinct, and shortening other clauses cannot duplicate an untouched ternary.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Source-dependent full-valuation cover; PROVED/EXACT in declared model]
Let the partial-assignment cylinders of $Q$ raw children cover \emph{all} $2^n$ source valuations. If each child has at most $\ell$ surviving original clause records, then
\[
 Q\ge 2^{\max\{0,(m-\ell)/2\}}.
\]
With only duplicate deletion added (no subsumption or semantic deletions), the always valid weaker bound is
\[
 Q\ge 2^{\max\{0,(m-\ell)/4\}}.
\]
These restrictions may be chosen after examining the source; no formula-independent schedule is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The retention lemma forces each leaf to fix $k_j\ge(m-\ell)/2$ sources, or $(m-\ell)/4$ in the duplicate-cleaned variant. Its cylinder contains $2^{n-k_j}$ full source assignments. Summing the cylinder volumes over a complete cover gives $2^n\le\sum_j2^{n-k_j}$, proving the two bounds.
\end{proof}
Under the project's ordinary four-word encoding,
\[
N=(4m+2)\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil=\Theta(n\log n).
\]
For fixed $b<1$, $\ell\le N^b=o(n)$ makes even the weaker complete-cover fanout $2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$. This is NOT a lower bound on a root-label-only SAT decider, which may terminate or simplify a child without preserving all valuation cylinders.

\section{A stricter SAT-witness cover barrier with a cheap exact solver}
Let $F_q$ consist of $q$ disjoint Boolean parity-even equations on distinct triples, each encoded by the four proper ternary clauses excluding odd assignments. Then $n=3q,m=4q$, the signed exact-$(2,2)$ property holds, and $|\operatorname{Models}(F_q)|=4^q$. This family is non-monotone, unlike D\"ocker's NP-complete monotone subclass.
\begin{theorem}[All-model cover; PROVED/EXACT in raw source model]
Suppose any source-dependent partial assignments $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_Q$ cover \emph{every satisfying witness} of $F_q$. If each conditioned residual has at most $\ell$ surviving original clause records, then
\[
\boxed{Q\ge 4^{q-\ell}.}
\]
For explicitly serialized ordinary children of at most $N^{b+o(1)}$ bits, $b<1$, this forces $Q\ge 2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$ for this particular complete-witness-cover service. This remains true under ordinary duplicate elimination.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If any of a block's three original variables is unassigned, at least one of its four parity-exclusion clauses survives. Thus $\ell$ residual clause records imply at most $\ell$ incomplete blocks. Fully assigned blocks support at most one compatible witness; each incomplete block supports at most four. A leaf therefore contains at most $4^\ell$ of $F_q$'s $4^q$ witnesses. The covering union bound gives the claim. Ordinary explicit CNF encoding costs at least one bit per residual clause and $N=\Theta(q\log q)$.
\end{proof}
For the \emph{different} promise that at most $L$ blocks remain incomplete, the optimum cover has exactly $4^{q-L}$ leaves: select one satisfying triple for each of the first $q-L$ blocks and leave the remaining $L$ unchanged, giving $4L$ residual clauses per child. A single adaptive affine recognizer instead solves $F_q$ in polynomial bit time after source acquisition, avoiding this entire solution relation. Therefore the statement is an explicit warning against promoting enumeration/representation bounds to arbitrary decision time.

\section{ARC and BIT-RESOURCE1 accounting}
An eager, explicit ordinary child must have its bytes/bits written; even an unchanged-value write is a charged primitive work step. Consequently a materialized full-cover service incurs at least $Q$ elementary output operations. It need not incur $Q$ live buffers: one-child-at-a-time streaming can reuse workspace. Total bit flips can be smaller than all writes. A potential vector records primitive bit work, input/workspace reads and writes, actual value-changing flips, peak live writable bits, cumulative live-bit-time and program/representation size, with no automatic time/space product obtained from the fanout alone. Virtual source-backed children require an exact charged random-access bit decoder. An algorithm may instead use source retention, Horn, SCC, affine elimination, learning, caching or direct decision. No proof here shows all such algorithms must use the complete cover.
