\section{Source model and theorem}
Input is explicit width-at-most-three CNF with $n$ original source variables, $m$ clauses, encoded length $N=(4m+2)w$ under a four-field clause convention and $w=\lceil\log_2(16(n+m+1))\rceil$. Tautologies and repeated literals are canonically cleaned with retained source-record mapping.
A support group of four distinct proper ternaries is an XOR quartet iff its falsifying assignments are exactly the four three-bit tuples of parity opposite one fixed bit $b$. Such a group is equivalent to $x\oplus y\oplus z=b$. Source exactness is checked against signed literal records, not a hash.
Split
\[
F=A(X)\wedge C(Y),\qquad
A=\bigwedge_{i=1}^g (x_i\oplus y_i\oplus z_i=b_i),\qquad
C=B_2\wedge\bigwedge_{j=1}^{t}T_j.
\]
$B_2$ has width at most two and $T_j$ are unrecognized proper ternaries. Every unrecognized clause is retained. Let $S=X\cap Y$, $h=|S|$. Exactly $4g+t=k$ genuine ternary clauses are present when all ternaries are in these two groups. $d=|X\setminus S|$ original variables are eliminated by existential projection; a syntactic residual is $r=n-d$ and $v=n/r$ only when $r>0$.
\begin{theorem}[Certified affine-interface join]
A uniform deterministic algorithm computes an affine relation $P(S)$ and source-row-combination certificates such that
\[
P(\alpha)\iff \exists\beta\in\{0,1\}^{X\setminus S} A(\alpha,\beta).
\]
It decides $F$ and reconstructs original source witnesses in $2^{h+t}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time, including all parse, acquisition, normalization, certificate verification and child decision costs. The number of XOR blocks $g$ is unrestricted. For $h+t=O(\log N)$, the entire certified restricted family lies in P.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Perform source-labelled Gaussian elimination on $X\setminus S$, retaining all pivot rows for lifting and the remaining rows over $S$ for the exact existential projection. For each assignment to $S$ check these projected rows. Select one additional original variable from each exceptional ternary not already hit by $S$, giving a backdoor $H\subseteq Y\setminus S$ with $|H|\le t$. Branch on $H$ and solve the remaining 2CNF by implication-graph SCC. If both sides accept, combine witnesses over the same original source IDs and replay the entire original signed CNF. For UNSAT, use either a source-row XOR contradiction or a pair of source-clause-labelled mutually contradictory implication paths for every exhausted branch. An arbitrary width-at-most-three input with no recognized quartet uses $h=0,t=k$, recovering the established $2^k\operatorname{poly}(N)$ bound.
\end{proof}
\section{Charged resource ledger}
With packed GF(2) rows of $(n+g)$ bits and one machine word costing $\Theta(\log N)$ bits,
\[
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{discovery}}&=O((n+m)\log(n+m+2)),\\
C_{\mathrm{normalization}}&=O(gn(n+g)/w+n+m),\\
C_{\mathrm{verification}}&=O(gn(n+g)/w+2^{h+t}(n+m)),\\
C_{\mathrm{continuation}}&=O(2^h(gh+1)+2^{h+t}(n+m)).
\end{aligned}
\]
These are upper bounds. Gaussian row events are charged as multiword work. All failed branches, exact compatibility, reconstruction, source reads, certificate output, and witness replay are counted. Polynomial working memory is available with streamed UNSAT branch proofs; the local audit program retains all branch data and can use exponentially many words. No model-count correspondence is asserted for existential projection. A small TRUE/FALSE output after deciding is not evidence that acquisition was cheap.
\section{Matched SAT/UNSAT source controls}
The original source XOR equations
\[
s\oplus a\oplus b=0,\quad a\oplus c\oplus d=0,\quad b\oplus c\oplus d=0
\]
force $s=0$. Add $g$ connected equations $z_{i-1}\oplus u_i\oplus z_i=0$ on two fresh original bits per equation, with $z_0=a$. The two binary sides
\[
C^+=(\neg s\vee q)\wedge(\neg s\vee\neg q),\quad
C^-=(s\vee q)\wedge(s\vee\neg q)
\]
are each satisfiable individually, yet their exact joins have
\[
\#SAT(A_g\wedge C^+)=2^{g+3},\qquad \#SAT(A_g\wedge C^-)=0.
\]
Both formulas have $n=6+2g$, $m=14+4g$, $k=12+4g$ genuine ternaries and the same bit length. Their interface has $h=1$ and no exceptional ternaries. The solver needs at most two boundary choices. For $g=40$, $n=86$, $k=172$, and $2^{43}$ accepting original tapes on the SAT side. This refutes any universal $2^k$ necessity inferred from surviving ternaries.
\section{NP-complete unrestricted-overlap boundary}
For every positive triple $(a,b,c)$, the conjunction
\[
(a\oplus b\oplus c=1)\ \wedge\
(\neg a\vee\neg b)\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg c)\wedge(\neg b\vee\neg c)
\]
is exactly positive one-in-three: parity one permits one or three true literals; the three binary clauses forbid three. Thus positive one-in-three SAT, known NP-complete by Schaefer, maps exactly and without auxiliaries into affine XOR quartets plus 2CNF. Every source bit may be shared, giving $h=n$. Polynomial solvability of the affine and binary components separately therefore does not imply polynomial solvability of their unrestricted conjunction. Nor does NP-completeness prove a deterministic superpolynomial lower bound.
