\section*{ITERATE: one-node reification lemma}
Let $L=(S,b)$ denote the Boolean GF(2) condition $\bigoplus_{i\in S}x_i=b$, where $S$ consists of original coordinates and $|S|\le3$. Its factor table is
\[
T_L(u)=\mathbf 1\left[\bigoplus_{i\in S}u_i=b\right]\quad(u\in\{0,1\}^{S}).
\]
For $S=\varnothing$, this is the single bit $1$ for $0=0$ or $0$ for $0=1$.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted consumer-preserving affine reification]
Suppose a $q$-node source-authenticated explicit mixed proof already has CANON2R legal rules and references, except that some AND3 inputs may be affine views of GF(2) or AND3 nodes. Each direct affine-input AND3 must satisfy original support union at most three. The proof can be compiled into strict CANON2R using at most $q+g\le2q$ charged nodes, where $g$ counts \emph{distinct affine producer views from GF(2) or AND3} used as AND3 parents without an already support-compatible factor view. The external raw source bytes and final contradiction are unchanged, as are the original node semantics and every original producer/consumer edge up to reindexing.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Traverse the source DAG in topological order. An AND3-produced affine view is equivalent to its same-node factor, but a direct zero-cost redirect is valid only if the factor's full syntactic support combined with the other AND3 parent still has size at most three. Otherwise reify the affine annotation using a paid LOCAL on its smaller essential row support. For each affine view consumed by AND3 that lacks a support-compatible factor view, whether produced by GF(2) or by an AND3 annotation, create one paid LOCAL node with that equation as parent and factor $(S,T_L)$ as output. The implication is an equivalence on all at most eight assignments and is therefore a legal LOCAL check. Cache this factor for all AND3 consumers of the same producer; GF(2) consumers continue to reference the original affine view. Since AND3 sees the exact same truth values, its output factor and any affine annotation are unchanged. All RES pivots, DEF facets, LOCAL and GF(2) descendants preserve their original semantics by induction. The root source identifiers and CANON2R raw wrapper remain identical. The number of inserted LOCAL nodes is $g\le q$, counting each affected affine-producing node at most once. The compiler reads the complete old evidence of length $E$, so physical time is polynomial in $N+E$, not asserted polynomial in $N+q$ for arbitrary unbounded old payload strings.
\end{proof}
This is a complete translation \emph{only for the declared input-type mismatch}; it is neither a proof that direct affine AND3 inputs were historically legal nor a normalization of unbounded private LOCAL wires.


\paragraph{Support-width falsifier and revised repair.}
The naive zero-cost replacement of every AND3 affine annotation by its sibling factor is \textbf{FALSIFIED}. In four original variables, a first AND3 combines source conditions $(x)$ and $(x\lor y\lor z)$. Its factor is syntactically supported on $\{x,y,z\}$, but has exact affine annotation $x=1$ on support $\{x\}$. A subsequent AND3 combines this affine condition with $(w)$ and is semantically supported on $\{x,w\}$. The naive substitution of the full factor makes the support union $\{x,y,z,w\}$ of size four, rejected by strict CANON2. Instead insert one paid LOCAL from the affine $x=1$ to the equivalent factor on support $\{x\}$; the repaired downstream AND3 has two-variable support. An independent Python enumeration checked all sixteen assignments and forty-eight Boolean identities. This countercontrol is why $g$ in the theorem counts \emph{both} GF(2) and AND3 affine producers, except for zero-cost support-compatible aliases.
