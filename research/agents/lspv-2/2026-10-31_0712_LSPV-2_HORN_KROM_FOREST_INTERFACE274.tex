\section{Exact original-source construction}
Let $F(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}C_j$ be an arbitrary ordered proper signed three-CNF, $C_j=(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$ with distinct variables in each clause. Introduce \emph{private} $y_{jk}$ for every signed occurrence $\ell_{jk}$; no two occurrences become independent source variables. Define
\[
 H_j=(\neg y_{j1}\vee\neg y_{j2}\vee\neg y_{j3}),\qquad
 E_{jk}=(\ell_{jk}\vee y_{jk})\wedge(\neg\ell_{jk}\vee\neg y_{jk}),
\]
\[
 T(F)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m \left[H_j\wedge\bigwedge_{k=1}^3E_{jk}\right]=H\wedge K.
\]
The binary pair $E_{jk}$ enforces $y_{jk}=\neg\ell_{jk}$. Every clause of $H$ is purely negative ternary Horn, so $H$ alone is SAT by $y=0$. The $K$ component is 2-CNF, independently SAT for every original assignment of $x$ by the unique induced values of $y$. In its \emph{primal graph}, every $y_{jk}$ has only one neighbor, the original $x_i$ of that occurrence, so this graph is a disjoint union of stars, in particular a forest. Every $y$ occurs in one Horn clause and two binary clauses; no original variable cloning is inferred to be independent.

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise mixed Horn--Krom interface and certificate lift]
For every such original $F$ and every coherent assignment $a\in\{0,1\}^n$,
\[
 \exists y\;T(F)(a,y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F(a),
 \qquad \#\mathrm{SAT}(T(F))=\#\mathrm{SAT}(F).
\]
The existential $y$ extension is unique. $T(F)$ has $n+3m$ variables and $7m$ clauses. Each original clause $C_j$ has a source-indexed, three-step ordinary resolution derivation from four clauses of $T(F)$. Therefore any ordinary resolution refutation of $F$ can be transformed into one of $T(F)$ by substituting its clause leaves with those three-step derivations. This transformation preserves both SAT and UNSAT, and original witness reconstruction is the restriction to $x$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed $x$, both binary clauses in $E_{jk}$ are true exactly when $y_{jk}=\neg\ell_{jk}(x)$, giving one global $y$. Substitution into $H_j$ makes $H_j$ true iff not all three literals of $C_j$ are false, i.e., iff $C_j$ is true. Hence the pointwise equivalence and the count equality, not merely equisatisfiability. Resolving $H_j$ against the three positively-$y$ linking clauses $(\ell_{jk}\vee y_{jk})$ successively on pivots $y_{j1},y_{j2},y_{j3}$ produces $C_j$. Each step is a non-tautological resolution and retains exact original transformed-source clause IDs $7(j-1)+1$ and $7(j-1)+2k$. Replacing a leaf $C_j$ of any resolution DAG with this local source-derived root yields a valid refutation DAG of $T(F)$. A $T(F)$ model restricts to an $F$ model; an $F$ model extends uniquely by the equation above. Membership of the restricted image in NP is immediate, and proper 3SAT reduces linearly to the mixed grammar, so SAT on this syntactically restricted mixed grammar is NP-complete by the standard 3SAT theorem. This does not imply a superpolynomial lower bound for an arbitrary SAT machine.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact forbidden cube.}
For example $(\neg a\vee p)$ and $(\neg p\vee b)$ have resolvent $(\neg a\vee b)$, prohibiting the entire coherent partial cube $a=1,b=0$. In the implementation, a non-tautological original-source resolvent can be lifted by first deriving its source clauses from the tagged $T(F)$ roots and then making the original resolution step. A derived clause does not assert that the whole source is UNSAT. The independent checker validates every parent, pivot, source-index order and derived clause and rejects corrupted DAGs.

\section{A restricted ARC-size and arbitrary-guest firewall}
For a source family with $m$ distinct original clauses on $n=\Theta(m)$ variables, the physical DIMACS encodings satisfy $|T(F)|=\Theta(m\log m)$ and $|F|=\Theta(m\log m)$. The authenticated structural decoder that outputs the original $F$ is thus only a \emph{constant-ratio} shortening, not an ARC child of size $N^{\lambda}$ for any fixed $\lambda<1$. The detector must scan the full source and validate every literal, equation and block; policy choice, failed class recognizers, source reads, emission and guest execution are charged. On heavily duplicated sources, deduplication can produce power-short children, but this is a recognized easy redundancy regime.

\begin{lemma}[Delayed guest test of this particular compiler]
Assume a correct polynomial ordinary-SAT decider $A_0$ exists. Fix $D$ at least its running-time exponent and define a correct wrapper $A_D$ which executes $M^D$ dummy steps before calling $A_0$ on an $M$-bit ordinary input. For the particular compiler which necessarily completes an $A_D$ call on the extracted $F$ of length $M=\Theta(N)$, the guest contribution is $\Omega(N^D)$; it cannot satisfy a uniformly claimed $K'\le\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda D\}$ with $\lambda<1$ and constants independent of $D$, for sufficiently large $D$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose $D$ with $D>\alpha$ and $D>\gamma/(1-\lambda)$. The compulsory delayed call executes $M^D=\Theta(N^D)$ instructions, while both exponents on the claimed right are strictly smaller than $D$. A controller that recognizes and decides this particular easy grammar without calling the delayed guest is not covered by the lemma. It is a \emph{black-box architecture falsifier}, not an all-algorithm lower bound.
\end{proof}
