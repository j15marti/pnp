## IDEA and four falsifiers
Hypothesis: under the *specific* canonical band-separated Koenig–Tsvietkova (KT) 2021 Theorem 1 strict signed 3CNF-to-link diagram, the complete pairwise linking matrix depends on the number of variables and clauses, but **not** on the clauses themselves. Falsifiers tested: (1) clause loops cross each other in the paper's diagram, (2) the commutator loop has a nonzero abelian winding vector, (3) the proposed SAT/UNSAT pair is not proper signed strict 3CNF or has the same label, (4) the published link has different component/crossing counts.

## ITERATE: restricted theorem
For original strict proper three-variable-per-clause formula F with n declared variables and m signed ternary clauses, define L(F) by the **published KT Figure 1/2 diagram** and consistently orient its components. Order these as x1, not-x1, ..., xn, not-xn, c1,...,cm. Then

$$
\operatorname{Lk}(L(F))=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n\begin{pmatrix}0&\epsilon_i\\\epsilon_i&0\end{pmatrix}\oplus 0_{m\times m},\qquad \epsilon_i\in\{+1,-1\}.
$$

The signs ε_i depend on the chosen fixed Hopf orientations, not the SAT clause signs. Hence **the full mod-2 linking rank is exactly 2n**, not merely at least 2n, and every original clause's support/signs are invisible to the entire pairwise matrix. Proof: variable-variable links consist of separate Hopf pairs. Each clause word [a,[b,c]] belongs to the commutator subgroup of the initial link complement, whose abelianization H1 is Z^(2n) with meridian exponent sums recording pairwise linking against all variable components. Consequently each clause-variable linking number is zero. Distinct KT clause loops are confined to separate horizontal diagram bands; no two clause components have mutual planar crossings, so their linking numbers are zero. This last claim is restricted to **the actual band-separated published drawing**, not every arbitrary presentation of the commutator words.

Explicit falsifier to any signature-only SAT algorithm: H contains all eight different 3-literal clauses on x1,x2,x3, one forbidding each assignment. Declared n=4, m=8, F_U=H is UNSAT. Replace the clause (not x1 OR not x2 OR not x3) with (x1 OR x2 OR x4) to form F_S; setting x1=x2=x3=true satisfies its remaining seven H clauses and its replacement, so F_S is SAT. Both are proper original strict signed 3CNF, both have (2n+m)=16 KT components, n+m=12 sublink target, crossing upper <=2136, and identical entire linking matrix of mod-2 rank 8. Their *full link topology* is not claimed equivalent. Therefore **no all-input decider D(Lk(L(F)),n,m)** can decide both; pairwise-matrix-only KT compression fails correctness independently of running time.
