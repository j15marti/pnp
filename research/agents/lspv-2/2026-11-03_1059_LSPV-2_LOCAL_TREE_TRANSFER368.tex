\section*{Established starting theorem and representation}
Buss and Tur\'an, Theorem 1 (1988), prove that every ordinary DAG Resolution refutation of the standard pigeonhole formula $\mathrm{PHP}(p,h)$, for $p>h$, has at least
\[
B(p,h)=\frac12\left(\frac32\right)^{h^2/(50p)}
\]
total proof lines. The formula contains only $p$ pigeon-occupancy clauses and $h\binom p2$ pairwise collision clauses; $m=p+h\binom p2$, $ph$ original variables. For $p=h+1$, an explicit canonical signed-index encoding has bit length $N=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$. The original author PDF, printed page 3 (PDF page index 2), was visually inspected in this run. The result is about proof lines, not SAT machine time.

Fix the already preselected $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$: free authenticated CNF sources and unit-cost distinct shared-DAG RES, AND3, GF(2), justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and conservative, source-independent two-input VARDEF. \section*{New local measure}
Inline all paid source-independent auxiliary definitions of a sound covered proof $\pi$ into Boolean functions of original variables, as justified by predecessor 367. For every non-DEF inference $j$, let $P_{j,1},P_{j,2}$ be its inlined premise conditions, $Q_j$ its conclusion, $S_j$ the declared original-variable support of $Q_j$, and $U_j$ the syntactic union of the premise and conclusion supports. A condition is represented by its exact canonical CNF, consisting of the complete blocking clause for each assignment on its declared support that makes it false.

For every assignment $v\in\{0,1\}^{S_j}$ falsifying $Q_j$, define $\ell_{j,v}$ as the minimum leaf count of a deterministic binary variable-query tree on $U_j\setminus S_j$ which, at each leaf, identifies an \emph{already available canonical premise clause falsified} by the fixed assignment $v$ plus the queried partial assignment. If the local inference is sound, exhaustive branching gives such a finite tree. This is an optimized proof-existence invariant; acquiring it may be computationally expensive.

\begin{lemma}[Falsified-premise tree to strict Resolution]
For a sound local implication, the blocker of any false conclusion assignment $v$ has a Resolution-plus-weakening derivation with at most $2\ell_{j,v}-1$ paid nodes. Weakening may be eliminated without increasing the number of strict Resolution inferences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a leaf the named premise blocker $D$ is false under the partial valuation, hence $D$ is a subclause of the complete blocker for that path together with $v$. Weaken to that full blocker, using one node at most. At each internal query $x$, the two child blockers contain complementary $x$ and the same prefix literals, so one Resolution eliminates $x$. A full binary tree with $\ell$ leaves has $\ell-1$ internal vertices; temporary cost is at most $2\ell-1$. Replace each weakened line by its stronger parent. When resolving, if both pivots survive resolve the stronger representatives; otherwise reuse a parent missing its pivot. Every replacement remains a subclause of its old line and the final empty clause remains empty. This is the standard weakening-erasure argument, not a new R rule.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Instance-sensitive reverse simulation]
For every normalized UNSAT CNF $F$ and every valid proof $\pi$ in the audited paid-wire interpretation, set
\[
\mathcal D(\pi)=\sum_{j\in I(\pi)}\sum_{\substack{v\in\{0,1\}^{S_j}\\Q_j(v)=0}}(2\ell_{j,v}-1),
\]
where $I(\pi)$ consists of non-DEF paid inferences. Then there exists a strict source-only ordinary DAG Resolution refutation of the \emph{same} original formula with
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le \operatorname{cost}_{\mathrm{Res}}(\tau(\pi))\le\mathcal D(\pi)\le\sum_{j\in I(\pi)}2^{|U_j|+1}.}
\tag{1}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Topologically translate every non-definition node. Original source clauses are their own unique canonical blockers. Previously translated derived factors supply all premise blockers needed at each new node, and the lemma constructs all conclusion blockers, preserving shared premises. Paid definitions inline into tautological defining relations and require no blockers. The final false condition yields the empty clause. The sum of local costs is $\mathcal D$. For one node of union support $u$ and conclusion support $s$, at most $2^s$ false output assignments exist, each with an exhaustive at-most-$2^{u-s}$ leaf tree. Thus its local cost is bounded by $2^s(2^{u-s+1}-1)<2^{u+1}$. This is the predecessor 367 scope upper bound, now sharpened with exact local tree costs.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Transfer of the published lower bound, with correct direction]
For every covered mixed proof $\pi$ of $\mathrm{PHP}(p,h)$,
\[
\boxed{\mathcal D(\pi)\ge \tfrac12(3/2)^{h^2/(50p)}-m.}\tag{2}
\]
For balanced PHP, if the mixed paid-node count $q\le h^a$ for a fixed $a$, then for all large $h$ there exists a non-DEF inference $j$ with
\[
\sum_{v:Q_j(v)=0}\ell_{j,v}\ge 2^{\Omega(h)}.
\tag{3}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Buss--Tur\'an counts both original clauses and paid Resolution lines, so $B(p,h)\le m+\mathcal D(\pi)$. For $p=h+1$, $B$ is exponential and $m=O(h^3)$. Since $\mathcal D\le2\sum_{j,v}\ell_{j,v}$ and at most $q$ non-DEF nodes exist, averaging proves (3). The exponential mass may be due to exponentially many canonical blockers of a compact XOR output, so (3) does not bound paid $q$ or SAT decision time.
\end{proof}
