\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let $F$ be a nonempty CNF whose clause widths are one through four, on $\nu$ densely indexed active variables, without tautological clauses or repeated variables in a clause, and let $b\ge2$. Let $r\in[b]^\nu$ and let $y$ be a Boolean $\nu$-by-$b$ table. Introduce $X_{ij},Y_{ij}$. Require exactly one $X_{ij}$ per block using an at-least-one clause and pairwise negative clauses. For every source clause $C$ and tuple $\rho$ on its variables emit the raw clause
\[
 D(C,\rho)=\bigvee_{i\in\operatorname{vars}(C)}\neg X_{i,\rho_i}\ \vee\!\bigvee_{z_i^e\in C}Y_{i,\rho_i}^e.
\]
Build OR chains separately on the row and column literal lists. Gate $o=u\vee v$ has clauses $(\neg u\vee o),(\neg v\vee o),(u\vee v\vee\neg o)$. A binary clause $D$ is replaced by $(D\vee h),(D\vee\neg h)$ with a fresh side-local bit. A unit $L$ is replaced by four clauses $L\vee h^e\vee g^f$ for $e,f\in\{0,1\}$. Join the two side outputs only by a padded binary clause. For side-only structural clauses assert the OR output by the unit encoding. Call the proper-3CNF result $K_b(F)$.
\begin{theorem}
$K_b(F)$ is equisatisfiable with $F$. Its clause and variable counts are exactly
\[
 M_b=\nu(5b-1+9\tbinom b2)+\sum_{C\in F}(10|C|-8)b^{|C|},
\]
\[
 V_b=\nu(5b-1+5\tbinom b2)+\sum_{C\in F}(6|C|-5)b^{|C|}.
\]
Its canonical map assigns $X_{ij}=[r_i=j]$, Bob's values to $Y_{ij}$, semantic OR values to gates, and zero to padding. Every variable is a function of only one party. Every false encoded clause identifies a source clause false on $z_i=y_{i,r_i}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A source model extends by selecting one row index per block and filling each Bob block with its source bit. Conversely a target model selects one index per block, and the corresponding raw clause forces each source clause true. OR equivalences and binary/unit padding are exact existential encodings. Each OR gate costs five clauses and three fresh variables. Each cross terminal costs two clauses and one variable; a unit terminal costs four clauses and two variables. Counting these and the $2\nu b$ base variables yields both formulas.
\end{proof}
A mixed auxiliary is not automatically safe: if $u=x\vee y$ with $x$ row-only and $y$ column-only, the falsification set of $2u-x-y\ge1$ is $\{(0,0),(1,1)\}$, whose singleton row neighborhoods are incomparable. This does not allege that the primary paper uses this defective encoding.

\[
(s\vee a\vee y),\ (\neg y\vee b\vee c),\ (\neg s\vee\neg a\vee z),\ (\neg z\vee\neg b\vee\neg c).

\begin{theorem}[Direct semantic-proof pullback]
Every $s$-line, depth-$d$ semantic Cutting Planes refutation of $J_b(F)=\mathcal T(K_b(F))$ gives a triangle DAG for $\operatorname{Search}(F)\circ\operatorname{IND}_b^\nu$ of at most $s$ nodes and depth at most $d$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the canonical side-separated map with clone identification, $s=0,y=\neg a,z=0$ and zero $Q$ variables. Every target variable remains row-only or column-only. Any affine inequality becomes $A(r)+B(y)\ge c$, whose falsification set has nested row neighborhoods. A valid binary semantic inference gives coverage by the two premise falsification sets. The final contradiction has full falsification set. Every input axiom has either empty falsification set or a correctly labelled source-clause leaf. Reverse proof edges. No helper axiom or intermediate semantic proof is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Explicit upper-proof construction]
If $F$ has a supplied $S$-line width-$W$ strict Resolution refutation, $J_b(F)$ has an explicitly constructible refutation of length $O(M_b+S b^{W+1})$. Generation has polynomial bit time and workspace in the full input and emitted proof lengths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Recover each raw source clause and each raw at-least-one clause from its OR encoding in $O(M_b)$ lines. For a source resolution step on $z_i$ and each tuple $\rho$ for its resolvent, resolve the lifted parents on $Y_{ij}$ for every $j$. This gives $\neg X_{ij}\vee D(C,\rho)$. Resolve these $b$ clauses against the at-least-one clause. There are at most $b^W$ tuples and at most $2b$ inferences per tuple. Apply the selector lift. No unknown source proof is discovered by this procedure.
\end{proof}

The isolated project premise (SL) has integers $b\ge3$, $1\le w\le\nu$, real $0<\delta<1-1/\log_2 b$, and normalized-spread constant $C$. It asserts that if
\[
b^\delta\ge64C(w+1)\lceil\log_2(2\nu)\rceil\lceil\log_2(2b\nu)\rceil,
\]
then any indexed-search triangle DAG of size at most $b^{(1-\delta)w}/2$ and depth $d$ gives source Resolution width at most $w$ and depth at most $wd$. It retains all saved-strip, global-error and same-row obligations. This run does not independently discharge (SL) as a whole.

\[
 N_t=O(t^q\log t),\quad S_{\mathrm{Res}}(J_t)=O(t^u),
\]
\[
 q=4a+c+1,\quad u=a(k+4)+k,\quad z=a(1-\delta)(k+c).

The proof upper follows from the preceding construction, the lower from the direct pullback and (SL). For fixed parameters $a\delta>0$ makes the (SL) threshold eventually hold.
