\section{Language and exact target}
The fixed language $L_{22}$ consists of satisfiable encodings of nonempty,
proper, distinct-clause signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF; malformed and out-of-domain
strings are rejected.  The required theorem is
\[
\forall A\ \forall k\ \forall C>0\ \forall N_0\ \exists G,\quad
|G|=N\ge N_0,\qquad T_A(G)>CN^k ,
\]
for every total deterministic uniform decider $A$ of this one language.
The graph-colouring compiler is an input reduction into $L_{22}$, not this
time lower bound.

\section{Exact composed projection}
Let $F$ be a proper width-2/3 CNF.  Properization $P$ leaves each ternary clause
unchanged and replaces each binary clause $D$ by
\[
(D\lor z_D)\land(D\lor\neg z_D)
\]
for fresh $z_D$.  Define $\tau$ by $\tau(x)=x$ for original variables and
$\tau(z_D)=0$.  One clause in the displayed pair maps to $D$ and the other to
true.

Let $U$ be the transparent seven-clause balancing compiler followed by the
clone-cycle/$Q$ compiler, and let $\rho$ be its proved target-to-$P(F)$/zero
substitution.  The implementation returns precisely this $\rho$ as
\texttt{Compiled.projection}.

\begin{theorem}[Adapter composition]
For $\sigma=\tau\circ\rho$, the nontrue clause images of $U(P(F))$ form exactly
the multiset of clauses of $F$.  Hence the existing PC/PCR projection to
$P(F)$ and the binary-clause adapter to $F$ are a single explicit polynomial
map.  Its acquisition, storage, and application are linear in the target
manifest size up to normal encoding factors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Under $\rho$, compiler source clauses become the clauses of $P(F)$; balancing
clauses, clone implications, and internal $Q$ clauses become true or Boolean
tautologies.  Applying $\tau$ fixes ternary source clauses.  For every
properized binary pair, $\tau(D\lor z_D)=D$ and
$\tau(D\lor\neg z_D)=\mathrm{true}$.  No other nontrue images remain.
\end{proof}

This closes a proof-interface bookkeeping seam, not a complexity seam.
Decoding a target formula and then solving its source is merely another
algorithm with all preprocessing, access, source-solving, and reconstruction
costs charged.  The size-increasing $U$ is not a contraction.  An arbitrary
$L_{22}$ decider need not compute $\sigma$, recognize the compiler image, or
emit a PC/PCR proof.
