\section{Exact gate template and non-interface population}
The source is the original Python \texttt{CIRCUIT-OBLIVIOUS-INVERSE-SORT166\_repro.py}, independently regenerated in this run. Let $w\ge2$, $D=w+3$, $H=7(w+2)$, and $A=H+6D=13w+32$. Every forward bitonic comparator has $H$ less/equality gates followed by $2D$ fixed three-gate MUX modules. Its $A$ gate indices are offset by $cA$ for comparator rank $c$; source proper-CNF variable of gate $g$ is $v_g=\mathrm{gatebase}+9g+2$.

For $0\le k<w+2$, let $g_{k,j}=cA+7k+j$ ($0\le j\le6$). With $\ell_{k-1}$ the preceding comparison bit and $e_{k-1}$ its prefix equality, the exact seven-gate block is
\[
\begin{array}{rclcrcl}
g_{k,0}&=&(-x_k)\land y_k,&&g_{k,1}&=&e_{k-1}\land g_{k,0},\\
g_{k,2}&=&\ell_{k-1}\lor g_{k,1},&&g_{k,3}&=&x_k\land(-y_k),\\
g_{k,4}&=&(-x_k)\land y_k,&&g_{k,5}&=&g_{k,3}\lor g_{k,4},\\
g_{k,6}&=&e_{k-1}\land(-g_{k,5}).&&&&
\end{array}\tag{1}
\]
Minus denotes Boolean negation, not arithmetic negation. The final $g_{w+1,2}$ is the saved switch $\sigma$ treated exactly in LSPV-2 report 167. The final $g_{w+1,6}$ is computed and dead. Each forward MUX $u\in[0,2D)$ has AND gates $m_{u,0}=cA+H+3u$, $m_{u,1}=cA+H+3u+1$ and record-output OR $m_{u,2}=cA+H+3u+2$.

Define the \emph{internal population} $I_c$ by including every one of the $H$ comparison gates except $\sigma$, and the two AND gates of every MUX, while excluding its record-output OR. Then
\[
\boxed{|I_c|=H-1+4D=11w+25.}\tag{2}
\]
Every gate in $I_c$ has all consumers in its own comparator, with zero, one, or two direct signed operand uses. The exact ascending consumer list is the following array; $k<w+1$ guards the next-block entries:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\text{producer} & \text{consumer(s): (gate, operand slot, sign)}\\\hline
g_{k,0}&(g_{k,1},1,+)\\
g_{k,1}&(g_{k,2},1,+)\\
g_{k,2},\ k<w+1&(g_{k+1,2},0,+)\\
g_{k,3}&(g_{k,5},0,+)\\
g_{k,4}&(g_{k,5},1,+)\\
g_{k,5}&(g_{k,6},1,-)\\
g_{k,6},\ k<w+1&(g_{k+1,1},0,+),(g_{k+1,6},0,+)\\
g_{w+1,6}&\varnothing\\
m_{u,0}&(m_{u,2},0,+)\\
m_{u,1}&(m_{u,2},1,+).
\end{array}\tag{3}
\]
There are no other uses of these fresh gates: the comparator returns only $\sigma$ and its $2D$ record-output OR wires, not intermediate scratch gates. The source creates both superficially duplicate $(-x_k)\land y_k$ gates independently, without hash-consing.

\begin{theorem}[Complete internal comparator Rank/Select; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every valid $w,K=2^h$, bitonic layer $\ell$, pair rank $r$, internal gate $g\in I_c$, and legal occurrence ordinal, the direct signed consumer and its inverse rank are given by (3) and the arithmetic $g=cA+\delta$. Both are computed uniformly in $O(\log^3G)$ conservative bit operations and $O(\log G)$ combined writable/index workspace, where $G$ is the \emph{entire} source verifier gate count. No full circuit, permutation table, source occurrence map, or precomputed advice is used by this accessor. The same formulas hold across all comparators independently of source data and saved switch values.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual Python emitter constructs seven fresh gates in exactly the order (1), then $2D$ unshared three-gate MUXs. Inspecting each operand of (1) yields precisely (3), including the negative $g_{k,5}$ use. The only inter-bit references are $g_{k,2}$ and $g_{k,6}$, as shown; the last equality output is unused. Internal MUX ANDs feed only their same-module OR. No internal output is returned to the next comparator. Inversion reduces to a bounded number of row/case tests and quotient-remainder arithmetic on $O(\log G)$-bit indices. Comparator layer/pair addressing uses the closed positional formulas already proved in report 167. Schoolbook multiplication/division and index-tape rewrites fit the conservative $O(\log^3 G)$ bound; storing the temporary register tuple uses $O(\log G)$ bits.\end{proof}

\section{Exact proper-CNF output incidence, not generic CNF}
The source gate compiler replaces each two-input gate by two private operand clones and exactly 13 proper ternary clauses. Its own output variable $v_g$ occurs in five rows. If the gate is AND, its own signs are $(+,-,-,-,-)$; if OR, $(-,+,+,+,+)$, at physical clause/slot locations $(13g+8,2)$ and $(13g+9\ldots12,1)$. Each later signed operand use $(g',s,\varepsilon)$ puts $v_g$ in the four source-CNF rows $13g'+4s+i$ ($0\le i<4$), literal slot 1, with polarity $\varepsilon$ for $i=0,1$ and $-\varepsilon$ for $i=2,3$. These own and use rows are strictly ordered by clause number.

\begin{theorem}[Full base-CNF internal gate OccRank/Select; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every gate in (2), let $f$ be the zero-to-two-element list length in (3). Then its exact number of physical literal occurrences is
\[
\boxed{\deg(v_g)=5+4f\in\{5,9,13\}}.\tag{4}
\]
For AND, $(\deg_+,\deg_-)=(1+2f,4+2f)$; for OR, $(\deg_+,\deg_-)=(4+2f,1+2f)$. The first five occurrences are its own rows, and the remaining groups of four are the ordered consumer-clone rows just stated. This enumeration has a table-free uniform inverse and the same conservative $O(\log^3G)$ bit-time, $O(\log G)$ writable/index space. It is valid for the source compiler's \emph{actual} variable names, clone slots and signs, even if its external comparator field wires happen to alias.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the exact 13-row emitter: each output $v_g$ appears five times in its own gate block. Every direct downstream use occupies a distinct operand-clone block with four source occurrences, balanced two-by-two in sign even for a negative operand. Equation (3) lists every use. All consumer indices exceed the producer, and (3) is ascending, so concatenation gives physical rank order. To invert an occurrence, divide its clause number by 13, detect the own five rows or check the bounded list (3) for a matching consumer and clone row. No scan over $G$ gates or source-dependent cache is needed.\end{proof}

The restricted theorem does \emph{not} cover the saved switch (covered by report 167), MUX record-output wires, inverse-routing record outputs, full scan/carry logic, high-fanout constants, input/witness aliases, final row checkers, output pins, malformed arbitrary SAT encodings, or incidence of every base-CNF variable. In particular it does not complete full-source \texttt{GateAt/Rank/Select}; therefore it does not establish polylogarithmic virtual access to every bit of the two signed regularizer levels.

\section{A GateAt-only inverse lower bound, rigorously model-scoped}
\begin{theorem}[Black-box inverse consumer query bound; PROVED/EXACT]
Let an arbitrary $M$-gate descriptor array be given by \texttt{GateAt}(i), which uses one unknown Boolean parameter $b_i$ per gate and answers a local forward descriptor query in constant word time. There is a family of valid fan-in-two AND circuits with one distinguished gate output $z$ and \emph{at most one} direct consumer of $z$ such that any deterministic exact algorithm returning the first consumer index, or a none sentinel, makes at least $M$ queries in the all-zero case. The same bound applies to finding occurrence rank 5 (zero-based) of $z$ in the actual 13-clause source compiler, since its first five occurrences are its own output clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take constants/input wires $z,u,v$. Define gate $h_i=\operatorname{AND}(z,u)$ when $b_i=1$, and $h_i=\operatorname{AND}(v,u)$ otherwise. Promise the string $b$ has Hamming weight at most one. Each \texttt{GateAt}(i) reveals only $b_i$, and every descriptor is a syntactically valid fan-in-two gate. With $b=0^M$ there is no consumer of $z$; replacing any one unqueried $b_j$ by 1 creates first consumer $h_j$, while retaining the promise. Thus the all-zero path must inspect every coordinate. In the proper compiler, four more source occurrences of $v_z$ appear precisely when a consumer exists; requesting zero-based occurrence 5 distinguishes the cases. This is a lower bound in the \emph{black-box arbitrary-descriptor} input model, \emph{not} a lower bound for the rigid, algebraically indexed bitonic compiler or SAT decision.\end{proof}

\section{Real two-level SAT/UNSAT transport, controls and charges}
An independent comparator emitter constructed actual proper 13-clause gate CNFs with all primary inputs pinned and the final saved switch pinned to its computed value (SAT) or its opposite (UNSAT). Every source variable/clause is explicitly created. For the SAT case, the circuit evaluation extends to a satisfying assignment of every source clause. For the opposite-pin case, the input pins plus gate-equivalence subclauses force a unique switch value, which contradicts the opposite pin; this is a \emph{symbolic UNSAT proof}, not claimed brute-force verification over hundreds of variables.

For each SAT and UNSAT base the reproducer constructs \emph{two actual nested virtual} $R_{15}$ signed exact-$(2,2)$-CNF views using the original independently published $Q_{15}/J$ gadget implementation. It verifies exact $n_1,m_1,n_2,m_2$ recurrence arithmetic, random real clause access, three distinct names per clause, and two positive plus two negative occurrences of sampled second-level variables. Each level retains both SAT and UNSAT labels by the already proved $Q_{15}/J$ projection theorem. The virtual objects are not claimed to be explicitly materialized (for $w=2$ the second level has $6{,}559{,}968$ clauses; for $w=4$, $9{,}441{,}408$ clauses), nor is the recursive accessor logarithmic-space, because this audit intentionally holds a source occurrence table. These are signed, \emph{not} clause-monotone, outputs.

The deterministic standard-library experiment tested $w=2,3,4,5,6,8,12,16,31$: 1,182 internal gate consumer inversions and 10,986 exact source-CNF literal occurrences, with 18,447 source template rows accounted. It also exhaustively rechecked the fixed 15-variable W1 UNSAT core over $2^{15}$ assignments, verified its 19-clause SAT deletion, and built renamed disjoint and connected SAT/UNSAT families for $k=1,2,3,5$. The connected family uses fresh-variable bridge clauses; it remains ordinary proper 3CNF but is \emph{not} exact-$(2,2)$ for $k>1$. The disconnected whole-source clause-hitting number grows at least $k$, while one authenticated fixed UNSAT core has a small local certificate. Total assertion-accounted checks: 63,646, with zero failures, random seed 1681026. The dependency script and raw output are stored beside this report.

In the FLMV bit model, a \emph{single} valid internal incidence query executes fixed-width quotient/remainder, signed slot reconstruction, and encoded output-field writing with $O(\log^3G)$ charged bit operations and $O(\log G)$ peak work/index bits; reads/writes of unchanged values, address arithmetic, and register writes are counted. If a caller emits all its $5+4f$ clauses, the output write bits and time are paid. No claim is made about the time/space of evaluating the entire compiler DAG, reading every virtual child bit, reentering a SAT guest, storing ancestors or constructing a full $C_{A,N}$. Gate count $G=O(B\log^3 B)$ of the \emph{witness verifier} is not the gate count of a full $N$-bit SAT decision circuit.
