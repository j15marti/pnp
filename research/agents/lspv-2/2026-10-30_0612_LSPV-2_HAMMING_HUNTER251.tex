\section{A precise Hamming-layer SAT certificate}
Fix a coherent global original-variable anchor $a\in\{0,1\}^n$ and a radius $r$. Let $\Omega_r(a)=\{z:\operatorname{dist}_H(z,a)=r\}$, $M=\bin nr$, and define the bad event $E_j=\{z\in\Omega_r(a):C_j(z)=\mathrm{FALSE}\}$. The full falsifying assignment to original variables of $C_j$ is exactly its local $U_j$ target. If its current anchor has $t_j(a)$ true literals, exactly those $t_j(a)$ variables must flip and the other source clause variables must remain; therefore
\[
|E_j|=\binom{n-3}{r-t_j(a)},\qquad \binom{u}{v}=0\ \text{for }v<0\ \text{or }v>u.
\]
For two source clauses, the intersection is empty if their $U_j,U_k$ original-variable targets conflict, equivalently if there is opposite-sign sharing; otherwise let $V_{jk}$ be the union of their variable supports and $h_{jk}$ be the Hamming distance from $a$ to their combined consistent $U$ target. The \emph{exact} pair intersection count is
\[
|E_j\cap E_k|=\binom{n-|V_{jk}|}{r-h_{jk}}.
\]
The incident polarity conflicts are the very same original-source conflicts appearing in $SS/UU$. This is a count of actual coherent Hamming-layer assignments, not an independent occurrence-variable experiment.

\begin{theorem}[Hunter-tree Hamming SAT certificate and deterministic witness]
For every proper width-three source CNF $F$, every coherent original-variable anchor $a$, every integer $0\le r\le n$, and every spanning tree $T$ on the $m$ original clause indices, let
\[
H_T(a,r)=\sum_{j=1}^{m}|E_j|-\sum_{\{j,k\}\in T}|E_j\cap E_k|.
\]
Then the number of assignments in $\Omega_r(a)$ falsifying at least one original clause is at most $H_T(a,r)$. In particular $H_T(a,r)<\bin nr$ implies $F$ has an original-variable satisfying assignment at exactly distance $r$ from $a$. A deterministic uniform source-reading procedure constructs and verifies one such witness using only polynomial integer arithmetic and polynomial space, with \emph{no} SAT answer oracle. A maximum-weight spanning tree with weights $|E_j\cap E_k|$ minimizes $H_T$ among tree certificates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root $T$ at any clause index $q$. For any complete coherent assignment $z$, write $e_j(z)=1$ if and only if original $C_j(z)$ is false. Define
\[
B_T(z)=e_q(z)+\sum_{j\ne q}e_j(z)(1-e_{p(j)}(z)),
\]
where $p(j)$ is the parent of $j$. If any $e_j(z)=1$, follow the path from $q$ to that clause: either the root is bad or the first bad vertex has a good parent. Thus $B_T(z)\ge\mathbf 1[\bigvee_j e_j(z)]$ pointwise, including dependencies and overlapping forbidden cubes. Summing over $\Omega_r(a)$ yields $H_T=\sum_{z\in\Omega_r(a)}B_T(z)$ and the claimed upper bound. The exact counts follow by consistency of each original $U_j$ target and choices of remaining flips outside its one/two-clause support. Kruskal's algorithm maximizes the sum of the nonnegative pair weights.

For constructive extraction, fix the computed tree $T$ once, and expose the original-variable flip bits one at a time, always conditioning on exactly $r$ total flips from $a$. At a partial flip cube $\rho$, calculate
\[
W(\rho)=\sum_{z\in\Omega_r(a)\cap\rho}B_T(z),\qquad
M(\rho)=|\Omega_r(a)\cap\rho|.
\]
Both are exact sums of $m+(m-1)$ source-authenticated single/pair event counts, with the same binomial formula after merging the fixed flip cube. For the next bit, $W=W_0+W_1$ and $M=M_0+M_1$, so one feasible child satisfies $W_i/M_i\le W/M$. Choose that child by cross-multiplying integers and pay for both attempted children. Starting with $W/M<1$, this preserves the strict inequality until all original variables are fixed. The final $M=1$ and the nonnegative integer $W=B_T(z)<1$ force $W=0$. A false original clause would force $B_T(z)\ge1$, contradiction. Finally rescan every original clause to verify the reconstructed assignment. This is a constructive conditional-expectation argument, not enumeration of all $\bin nr$ complete states.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact strict22 control, limits and falsifications}
For the displayed 12-clause connected strict22 source, choose the globally consistent anchor $a=0^9$ and radius $r=3$. Direct source target counting gives
\[
\sum_j|E_j|=126\ge\binom93=84,
\qquad\max_T\sum_{\{j,k\}\in T}|E_j\cap E_k|=43,
\qquad H_T=83<84.
\]
The uncorrected first-moment union bound is inconclusive, but the overlap-corrected tree certificate forces \emph{at least one} satisfying assignment in this Hamming layer. The 11-edge chosen tree and all exact pair counts are in the raw results. The deterministic conditional construction returns $z=265$ (bits 1,4,9 TRUE); its nine proof steps have positive remaining Hamming-layer sizes and nonincreasing conditional bound, ending with $W=0,M=1$. A separate exhaustive oracle counted \emph{10} SAT assignments in the 84-state radius-three layer and 61 SAT assignments in the full $2^9$ space, confirming but not supplying the proof.

This is a concrete source-specific gain over the first-moment certificate, \textbf{not} an unrestricted SAT runtime gain. The method only certifies SAT if the computed upper bound is strictly below the layer size; $H_T\ge M$ is \emph{UNRESOLVED}, never UNSAT. Connected strict22 W1 has no models, and the attempted radius-three tree tests are inconclusive and correctly defer to an independently replayed standard UP-DPLL UNSAT proof. The 2-SAT contradictory, affine XOR contradictory, Horn, connected strict22 SAT and polarity-renamed controls were checked without invented answer bits.
