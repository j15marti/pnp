\begin{lemma}[Constant-port implicate compilation]\label{lem:port}
For a CNF $K$ on $d$ named Boolean ports and any entailed clause $C$, exhaustive strict Resolution closure derives a subclause $C'\subseteq C$ in at most $3^d$ distinct inference nodes. If no proper subclause of $C$ is entailed by $K$, the conclusion is exactly $C$. Every bounded-arity Boolean circuit identity or guarded value-consequence therefore admits a uniform fixed strict-Resolution template once all conservative gate views and previous equivalence clauses are included.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the standard implicate-completeness of Resolution (provable by induction by Davis--Putnam elimination): every implicate has a subsuming clause in the Resolution closure. There are at most $3^d$ non-tautological signed clauses on $d$ ports, including the empty clause. Enumerating all valid resolvents until closure produces the required node with source-indexed parent pointers. Entailment of a proper subclause would contradict the second hypothesis. Templates have constant size independent of $h$; specialization to fresh named wires does not invoke a runtime semantic rule.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Fully checkable local guarded XOR row]\label{lem:local}
Let four original guarded clauses encode $t\Rightarrow x\oplus y\oplus z=\chi$, $\chi\in\{0,1\}$. Introduce the six paid signed-AND2 definitions
\[
u=x\wedge\neg y,\ v=\neg x\wedge y,\ w=\neg u\wedge\neg v,\quad
u'=\neg w\wedge\neg z,\ v'=w\wedge z,\ w'=\neg u'\wedge\neg v'.
\]
The signed output $b=\neg w'$ equals $(x\oplus y)\oplus z$. The exact clause $\neg t\vee(b=\chi)$ has a strict Resolution DAG with at most 17 paid Resolution nodes, in addition to the six paid definitions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source quartet forbids precisely wrong-parity assignments. Independently generated clause-DAG witnesses first eliminate $x,y,u,v$ to derive the two guarded clauses specifying the required relation between $\neg w$ and $z$. They then eliminate $w,z,u',v'$ using those two consequences and the second three definition views. For charge zero the output is $(\neg t\vee w')$ using 17 RES and six DEF (total 23); for charge one it is $(\neg t\vee\neg w')$ using 16 RES and six DEF (total 22). Every individual signed source, gate view, pivot, parent and result is present in the separately published raw proof and was independently checked. These are upper witnesses, NOT certified optimal costs.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial native ER guarded Tseitin]\label{thm:main}
For an absolute constant $C$, every finite connected simple cubic $G$ with $h$ vertices and every odd charge vector $\chi$ satisfies
\[
D_{E_{\land2}}(G_t(F_{G,\chi})\vdash\neg t)\le Ch^3,\qquad
W_{E_{\land2}}(H_t(F_{G,\chi}))\le Ch^3+1.
\]
The same guarded UNSAT source $H_t(F_h)$ for a chosen expander family remains $2^{\Omega(h)}$-hard for ordinary unrestricted DAG Resolution, by restriction $t=1$. Hence the established lower bound does not transfer to the paid-ER fragment of the unchanged stronger mixed calculus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each vertex $v$, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:local} using the original quartet to introduce a paid two-XOR circuit with output $b_v=\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e$ and derive its guarded charge literal $\neg t\vee(b_v=\chi_v)$. This uses $O(h)$ nodes. Introduce a left-associated prefix circuit $P_j=\bigoplus_{v\le j}b_v$, three paid AND2 gates per XOR. Constant-port strict Resolution templates applied to the guarded input values and XOR gate views derive $\neg t\vee(P_j=\bigoplus_{v\le j}\chi_v)$ inductively, in $O(h)$ additional nodes. For odd charge, derive $\neg t\vee P_h$.

Flatten this circuit into the XOR left fold $Q_L$ of the $L=3h$ edge-variable occurrences in vertex order. At each vertex, the identity
\[
P_{v-1}\oplus((a\oplus b)\oplus c)
=
(((P_{v-1}\oplus a)\oplus b)\oplus c)
\]
and previously derived prefix equivalence are local implications over at most 23 named ports, counting all signed-AND2 internal gates. By Lemma~\ref{lem:port}, their two output-equivalence clauses have constant-size strict RES derivations from their conservative gate views and prior equivalences. Treat the local input ports independently, so neither side of each output equivalence is forced and weakening is unnecessary. Repeat for $h$ vertices to derive $P_h\leftrightarrow Q_L$ in $O(h)$ paid nodes.

Stable-sort the length-$L$ occurrence list by edge ID using at most $L(L-1)/2$ adjacent swaps. For each swap, introduce a new conservative XOR suffix containing at most $L$ XOR gates. The local identity $((p\oplus a)\oplus b)=((p\oplus b)\oplus a)$ has a fixed template using four XOR gates and at most 15 named ports; propagate each suffix equality one gate at a time by bounded-port strict Resolution from the prior two equality clauses. There are $O(L^3)$ paid gates and inferences across all swaps. The sorted occurrences are $x_1,x_1,x_2,x_2,\ldots$. Repeatedly use the fixed-port consequence of $((q\oplus x)\oplus x)=q$ to derive units stating that every even-index left-fold prefix is zero (the initial $x\oplus x=0$ is a direct constant-port consequence). Thus the sorted output is zero. Apply the derived output equivalences backwards and then the flattening equivalence to derive $\neg P_h$, and one RES step with $\neg t\vee P_h$ gives $\neg t$. In $H_t(F)$ one further RES step with the original unit $t$ yields $\bot$.

All identity templates use only paid acyclic signed-AND2 definitions and legal strict RES steps. Their finite port counts are constants fixed before graph size; the construction itself chooses gates, transpositions, parents and local templates uniformly from the source graph. It permits shared DAGs and temporary growth, and contains no uncharged algebraic inference. Since only $O(L^2)$ swaps each rebuild at most $O(L)$ gates/proof macros, the bound is $O(h^3)$.
\end{proof}
