\section{Source-preserving exact cofactor volume}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^mC_j$ be a proper CNF with source clause IDs $j$,
and $B$ a set of $b$ distinct original Boolean variables. The signed
original occurrence $x_{i,j}$ inherits the value of $x_i$, and is not an
independent Boolean variable. Let $t_j=|B\cap\mathrm{var}(C_j)|$,
$w_j=|C_j|$. For each coherent map $\beta:B\to\{0,1\}$, a
\emph{raw cofactor} deletes a satisfied original clause and deletes
each falsified assigned literal from retained source clauses, including
an empty residual clause if all literals are falsified.
\begin{theorem}[Exact all-cofactor volume]
The sums of the counts of surviving original-clause copies, surviving
unassigned literal copies, and empty clauses over all $2^b$ raw children are
\[
  Q_C=\sum_j2^{b-t_j},\qquad
  Q_L=\sum_j(w_j-t_j)2^{b-t_j},\qquad
  Q_E=\sum_{j:t_j=w_j}2^{b-t_j}.
\]
They can be computed exactly from source incidences without expanding
the $2^b$ children.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A clause survives exactly when all $t_j$ pinned signed occurrences
are false, which has one choice per pinned original variable.
The other $b-t_j$ values are arbitrary. Each surviving copy retains
$w_j-t_j$ literal occurrences; it is empty precisely when $t_j=w_j$.
Summing over original clause indices proves all identities.
This is elementary double counting rather than a new SAT technique.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Restricted shallow raw-child floor]
If every original variable occurs in at most $d$ clauses, any raw
$b$-pin cofactor retains at least $m-db$ completely untouched clauses.
For strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ proper 3CNF,
$m=4n/3$, $d=4$, and any raw child contains at least $m-4b$
untouched three-literal source clauses.
On the declared explicit clause-list representation with
$N=\Theta(n\log n)$, an $N^\lambda$ physical child for any fixed
$\lambda<1$ thus requires $b\ge n/3-o(n)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At most $db$ original clauses are incident to pinned variables.
All others survive in every raw child. In strict exact22,
$m-4b=4n/3-4b$; preserving $O(N^\lambda)=o(n)$ physical
clause records forces $4b\ge4n/3-o(n)$.
The proof assumes no resolution, additional propagation,
semantic compression or change of physical representation.
\end{proof}
A blind expansion of all branches at that linear pinning depth

observation is not an arbitrary-algorithm time lower bound.
