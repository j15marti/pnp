\section*{Fixed calculus fragment and exact quantifiers}
Let \(F\) be any finite valid, explicitly encoded CNF (both SAT and UNSAT labels, and malformed encodings distinguished by the parser). The preselected \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) has free original source clauses, paid unit-weight two-antecedent DAG nodes: RES, local truth-table AND3, GF(2) equation addition, justified LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative charged two-input DEF. All distinct charged nodes count once. The following theorem applies only to proofs in the \emph{extensional-port fragment} \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ext}}\): every semantic node denotes an exact Boolean relation on explicitly named previously available ports, entails its conclusion from its at most two declared antecedents under \emph{every independent port assignment}, and never exports an uncharged internal payload wire. An arbitrary-size LOCAL-CONVERT payload is permissible only as a finite circuit computing a Boolean function of its declared ports; it must be read and evaluated, and its private internal wires cannot be referenced later for free. This is a proper syntactic subgrammar until the authoritative full LOCAL-CONVERT grammar is fixed.

Let \(D\) be a complete acyclic paid DEF bank and \(\pi\) a valid shared-DAG refutation in \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ext}}\), containing \(g\) DEF nodes, \(r\) strict RES nodes and \(s\) other extensional semantic nodes. Let \(T\) be a finite, complete adaptive decision tree querying only original variables, with \(L\) leaves. At every leaf assignment \(\alpha\), every DEF output is identically a Boolean constant or one signed original-variable literal after recursive substitution. For every semantic node \(j\) and every leaf, define \(k_{j,\alpha}\) as the number of \emph{distinct original variables in the union of the exact supports of its substituted antecedent relations and conclusion}; set \(k=\max_{j,\alpha}k_{j,\alpha}\), with zero when \(s=0\). This \(k\) includes the full support of wide XOR rows; merely checking a constant-size gate or output is insufficient.

\begin{lemma}[Bounded independent-port clause reflection]
For any \(k\)-variable clausal antecedent \(\Gamma\) and any CNF consequence \(H\) satisfying \(\Gamma\models H\), ordinary Resolution saturation of all proper clauses on those \(k\) variables creates at most \(3^k\) distinct new non-tautological clauses. For each target clause \(C\in H\), some produced or source clause \(D\subseteq C\) is available.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof} There are exactly \(3^k\) proper clauses, counting the empty clause. Saturate all non-tautological resolvents. Davis--Putnam elimination of variables absent from \(C\) gives a CNF equivalent to the existential projection of \(\Gamma\). Since \(\Gamma\models C\), the assignment falsifying every literal of \(C\) cannot satisfy the projected CNF. One of its clauses \(D\) is falsified and uses only variables of \(C\), hence \(D\subseteq C\). All projection resolvents belong to the saturation. This is the LSPV-3/401 local lemma, reused with explicit attribution.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive semantic-locality translation; restricted]
For \emph{every} UNSAT \(F\), \emph{every} legal proof \(\pi\) and \emph{every} collapse tree \(T\) satisfying the foregoing uniform syntactic assumptions,
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le L(r+s3^k+1)-1.}
\]
There is no instance-dependent answer axiom, no proof-search restriction and no claim of polynomial overhead when \(L\) or \(k\) grows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} For each leaf \(\alpha\), restrict all source clauses and recursively replace all DEF outputs by their constant/signed-original-literal cofactors. Their full defining relations become tautologies. Traverse the original proof DAG topologically. Represent each surviving Boolean factor/equation by derived clauses that are subclauses of its exact canonical CNF. A substituted strict RES step needs at most one RES or inheritance of a stronger parent, by the LSPV-2/381, /397 signed-substitution lemma. For an extensional semantic step, the strong antecedent clauses imply the exact old antecedent relations and hence its conclusion on the \(\le k\) independent original ports. Apply the preceding lemma to obtain all needed strengthened conclusion clauses with at most \(3^k\) new paid strict RES nodes. Thus the restricted original-source refutation needs at most \(r+s3^k\) new nodes. Lift it to a derived subclause of the falsified leaf blocker without free weakening, by the standard guarded restriction argument. Combine the blockers along the complete decision tree using at most \(L-1\) strict RES joins, inheriting an existing stronger child when a pivot disappears. The total is bounded by \(L(r+s3^k)+L-1\). The original sources, not an assumed SAT decision oracle, justify every final clause.
\end{proof}

\section*{ITERATE: certified-hard PHP and width-or-leaves consequence}
For Haken's \emph{explicit unary} pigeonhole CNF \(P_h\), with \(h+1\) pigeons, \(h\) holes, \(h(h+1)\) original variables, \(m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}=\Theta(h^3)\) original clauses and conventional signed-index binary length \(N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))\), the established theorem states
\[
 \exists c>0\ \exists h_0\ \forall h\ge h_0\ \forall
 \text{ ordinary shared-DAG Resolution refutations }\sigma\text{ of }P_h:
 \quad |\sigma|\ge2^{ch}.
\]
This does \emph{not} quantify over ER, Res(\(\oplus\)), \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) or Turing machines. Subtracting the original \(m_h\) free source clauses gives \(W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}-m_h\). The theorem yields for every \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ext}}\) proof with such a collapse tree:
\[
\boxed{L(q+1)3^k\ge 2^{ch}-m_h+1,\quad q=r+s+g.}
\]
In particular, a polynomial-in-\(N_h\) node budget forces
\(\log_2 L+k\log_2 3\ge ch-O(\log h)\).
Thus within this restricted grammar, a polynomial proof must pay either an exponential number of adaptive collapse leaves or at least linear semantic-port width. Wide XOR rows are a legitimate escape and can have polynomial \emph{bit} manipulation cost. This is no lower bound on \emph{arbitrary} machine time, and the statement is silent on a LOCAL-CONVERT that exports uncharged private wires.

\begin{lemma}[Expander cut pressure for XOR aggregation trees]
Let \(G\) be a \(d\)-regular edge expander on \(h\ge3\) vertices with \(|\delta(S)|\ge\epsilon\min(|S|,h-|S|)\). In any full binary tree with each vertex parity row appearing at precisely one leaf and each parent adding its children over GF(2), there exists an intermediate row involving at least \(\epsilon h/3\) distinct edge variables.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof} Every rooted binary tree with \(h\) leaves has a proper descendant subtree with between \(h/3\) and \(2h/3\) leaves: descend through a child with more than \(2h/3\) leaves until this is no longer possible, then one child has at least \(h/3\). For its vertex subset \(S\), the XOR of the vertex incidence rows cancels each internal edge and leaves precisely the boundary \(\delta(S)\). Expansion gives the stated width. This is a restricted \emph{tree-aggregation} width lemma, not a lower bound for arbitrary mixed DAGs or arbitrary algorithms.
\end{proof}
Urquhart (1987) proves exponential unrestricted ordinary Resolution size for bounded-degree expander Tseitin CNFs; the original Rmix341 construction gives the same source a \(4h-1\)-node mixed refutation. The two results coexist because its growing GF(2) rows satisfy the width firewall.
