\section{Exact source-cover theorem}
Let $F(X)$ be an arbitrary finite indexed ordinary CNF with source clauses $(C_j)$. Partition a subset of its \emph{original} proper ternary clauses into disjoint four-clause blocks on three distinct original variables. Accept a block only if its complete eight-row truth table agrees either with one ternary parity equation or with one pinned original variable. Every other original clause belongs to $H$. Let $B=\operatorname{var}(H)$ and $P=X\setminus B$, and let $R(X)$ be the conjunction of the authenticated affine equations. Thus $F(X)\equiv H(B)\land R(B,P)$ pointwise, not merely equisatisfiable. Original signed occurrences $x_{i,j}$ refer to the same $x_i$ throughout.

\begin{theorem}[Exact source-authenticated affine projection]
For all such $F$ and for \emph{every} coherent assignment $\beta\in\{0,1\}^{B}$, a finite sequence of elementary $\mathbb F_2$ row operations and existential private-variable eliminations produces affine equations $Q(B)$ such that
\[
  \exists\pi\in\{0,1\}^{P}F(\beta,\pi)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad H(\beta)\land Q(\beta).
\]
Let $G(B,Z)$ be the physically serialized ordinary CNF consisting of $H(B)$ and a four-clauses-per-ternary-XOR-gate encoding of $Q(B)$, using fresh child-only variables $Z$. Then
\[
  \forall\beta:\quad \exists\pi\,F(\beta,\pi)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists z\,G(\beta,z),
  \qquad \operatorname{SAT}(F)\Longleftrightarrow\operatorname{SAT}(G).
\]
A child witness lifts to a complete original-source witness by solving the authenticated source affine system at its boundary assignment. If $G$ is UNSAT, a complete DPLL/unit-propagation refutation of $G$ together with the complete source authentication and projected-row provenance is a \emph{typed semantic reduction certificate} for $F$ being UNSAT. It is not automatically a pure source-resolution proof.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a clause of width three, its unique falsifying triple is determined by the three original signs. A four-clause block forbids exactly four signed triples. The explicit exhaustive eight-row authentication verifies that the conjunction equals the asserted XOR equation or pin, so replacing the block changes no assignment. Every source clause is either used once or retained in $H$. For a variable $p\in P$, a row $p\oplus L=a$ can be satisfied for either chosen value of its other variables by selecting $p$. With $t$ rows containing $p$, retain the $t-1$ pairwise differences against a pivot and existentially remove the pivot row. This operation preserves all projections onto the other variables, including inconsistent systems. Inductively eliminating all private variables gives precisely $Q(B)\equiv\exists P\,R(B,P)$. Each ternary XOR has an exact four-clause truth table; chaining with fresh internal variables gives an existentially equivalent CNF for each remaining row. Conjunction with $H$ establishes the formula. For a satisfying child model, its $B$-values satisfy $Q$, hence the original affine linear equations have a private solution, which is reconstructed and then checked against every original clause. An UNSAT child refutation plus the established pointwise projection identity excludes all source assignments.\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Degree-two private-incidence monotonicity and source lineage]
Suppose every private variable initially occurs in at most two authenticated affine rows. In a sequence eliminating only private variables, this degree bound persists. For degree two, replacing $(p\oplus L=a)$ and $(p\oplus R=b)$ by $(L\oplus R=a\oplus b)$ removes one row and never increases the occurrence degree of any other variable. If each original row carries its source-block ID and current rows carry XOR-subsets of these IDs, the replacement lineage is the symmetric difference of parent lineages and replays exactly. At most $r$ nontrivial sparse row-XORs occur when there are initially $r$ authenticated rows. This \emph{does not} bound total bit time independently of intermediate support and lineage sizes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two source equations have a common solution in $p$ exactly when their XOR difference holds; then $p$ can be set uniquely. For any other variable $y$, its incidence count among the two parents is $0,1,$ or $2$. Its incidence in the replacement is respectively $0,1,$ or $0$, never larger. A one-row private pivot is dropped because $p$ can satisfy it, and a zero-row pivot does nothing. Hence private degree remains at most two, by induction. Every degree-two elimination reduces the number of live rows by one. XOR source identities combine by symmetric difference over $\mathbb F_2$, proving their soundness and the row-operation count. Sparse support traversals, source-ID traversals, incidence updates, sorting, and physical serialization are separate paid resources.\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Charged word-RAM implementation.} Incidence building reads every accepted source row. Grouping original clauses by unsigned support reads the source and requires sorting/hashing; each attempted four-pattern classifier is paid. The sparse bucket step charges support touches, provenance touches, source-index incidence mutations, private-degree tests and boundary-row reduction. If all live support widths are $\le w$ and lineage lengths $\le p$, the sparse elimination phase uses $O(r(w+p)+n+\mathrm{sorting})$ word operations under constant-time indexed set operations, in addition to source reading and output; without these bounds this expression is not polynomially small relative to the child, and converting it to the FLMV tape model is \textbf{OPEN}.

\section{Matched original-source SAT and UNSAT constructions}
Let $B=(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ and let $H(B)$ be two seven-of-eight signed proper-3CNF blocks, one pinning $(x_1,x_2,x_3)=001$ and the other pinning $(x_2,x_3,x_4)=011$. Thus $H$ has exactly one boundary model $B=0011$, of even parity, but no extracted four-pattern affine block. For each $i\le k$, introduce private gates encoding the XOR chain of the four $B$ variables and a four-clause pin on its output, requiring $\bigoplus B=q$. All $k$ copies use disjoint private variables and complete proper-3CNF source clauses. The source has $4+5k$ variables and $14+16k$ clauses. Its exact projection is
\[
  H(B)\land(\textstyle\bigoplus_{j=1}^4 x_j=q).
\]
For $q=0$ this source is SAT; a complete private source witness is recovered from the child witness. For $q=1$ the \emph{same H} source is UNSAT, and an independently replayed emitted-child DPLL refutation establishes the NO label. With one contradictory charge among the copies, authenticated row XOR produces $0=1$ and the physically emitted ordinary child is the empty clause. All transformations preserve original signs and clause indices and survive random variable renamings and literal complementation.
