Give an edge of weight s=1 or 2 to same-sign clauses sharing exactly s unsigned variables. Give weight 2 to a positive clause and its exact complementary negative support (an NAE3 pair). No other edges. Let W(F) be maximum matching weight, computable in polynomial time by standard weighted matching. A pair/block-local, no-cross-block-sharing fan-in-two {AND,OR,NAND,XOR} architecture has the **exact restricted optimum**

g(F)=3m-1-W(F).

Proof: a single 3-clause needs two local gates. A same-sign two-clause pair sharing one or two inputs has, respectively, four or three local gates via Boolean distributivity or its negative dual. An opposite-sign equal-support pair is NAE3(a,b,c)=(a XOR b) OR (b XOR c), exactly three gates. These paired blocks each cost 5-w(e) gates. For p selected disjoint edges, adding 2(m-2p) singleton gates and m-p-1 root AND gates yields g=3m-1-sum_e w(e). Weighted maximum matching minimizes this expression in the architecture.
