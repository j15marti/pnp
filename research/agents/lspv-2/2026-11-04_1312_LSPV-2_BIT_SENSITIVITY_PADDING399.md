# LSPV-2/399: Source-bit sensitivity gives a genuine uniform query lower bound, but disappears under duplicate-free SAT-equivalent padding

**2026-11-04 13:12 PST, America/Los_Angeles. Status: PROVED/EXACT elementary restricted lemmas, independently checked finite instances COMPUTATIONAL; specialist review pending. P versus NP OPEN.** Owner LSPV-2; no task, role, or calculus mutation.

## IDEA before SEARCH: falsifiable mechanism

**Initial hypothesis:** The number of individually SAT/UNSAT-pivotal polarity bits in a signed-hypergraph source might be an algorithm-independent machine-access invariant, surviving sound changes of representation. Its *input-access* half should be provable by a transcript-coupling argument, but representation invariance may fail.

Falsifiers: (F1) a total correct general-CNF decider can skip a pivotal bit on the declared source; (F2) SAT-equivalent polynomial-time padding retains singleton polarity sensitivity; (F3) a two-sided-error randomized decider can avoid all pivotal bits on the UNSAT input; (F4) a padding that kills singleton sensitivity also kills all bounded-size block sensitivity. Search and tests below falsify F2 and F4, not F1 or F3.

## Model and exact quantifiers

Fix a **binary source encoding**, distinct from legacy ASCII DIMACS but polynomial-time interconvertible: six-bit magic 110101, Elias-gamma codes for n and m+1, then for each clause a gamma code for its length+1 and, for each literal, a polarity bit and a fixed-width w = floor(log2 n)+1 nonzero variable index. Malformed headers, truncated fields, bad IDs and trailing bits are rejected. Each literal polarity occupies a *single independently addressable source bit*. Both SAT and UNSAT are accepted labels on valid codes; malformed inputs are rejected. The fixed proof calculus R_mix341 remains completely unchanged and accepts the equivalent ordinary CNF clauses; this report proves **machine bit-query**, not mixed-derivation, lower bounds.

For every connected simple bridgeless cubic G=(V,E), h=|V|, and odd charge vector chi, let F(G,chi) be the standard full four-clause-per-vertex signed 3-CNF expansion of the GF(2) Tseitin rows. It has m=4h clauses, 12h signed literal positions and n=3h/2 variables. Define s_sign(x) as the number of literal-polarity positions i such that the singly toggled binary input x^i remains a valid CNF and has the opposite SAT label. This is pointwise source-bit sensitivity, not a proof lower bound.

**Theorem A (physical bit-access lower bound, all correct algorithms).** For all such G,chi, s_sign(enc(F(G,chi)))=12h. For **every deterministic total uniform exact SAT decider A**, its execution on this specific UNSAT bitstring must query all 12h distinct pivotal positions. In a bit-probe model T_A(x)>=12h. For every randomized total decider A with two-sided error at most epsilon<1/2 on **every valid input**, the expected number of distinct probed input positions on x satisfies E[Q_A(x)] >= (1-2epsilon)12h. A word-probe model with at most w_mem source bits obtained per probe gives >= (1-2epsilon)12h/w_mem expected probes. Since |enc(F)|=Theta(h log(h+2)), the unconditional bit-probe bound is Omega(N/log N), **not** a superpolynomial lower bound.

**Proof.** The LSPV-2/341 bridgeless-cubic source-deletion theorem establishes, for each local clause C, an assignment satisfying all other original clauses while falsifying C. Reversing any one of the three signed literal polarity bits of C makes that clause true under the witness, so F^i is SAT. Thus all 3m=12h positions are pivotal and the rest of the sign bits do not exist. For deterministic A, if A omits i on x, the entire adaptive trace on x^i is identical, so A would give the wrong answer on a valid opposite-label input. For randomized A couple both runs with identical random coins; before a query of i, traces are identical. The probability of querying i on x is at least the output-distribution total variation, >=1-2epsilon, and linearity of expectation completes the bound. No restriction on the internal representation, adaptive policy, caching, affine elimination, conservative gates, or proof-DAG construction was needed, only the **fixed input bit interface**. Randomness is included in the standard query-model sense, not an all-machine runtime separation. A promise restricted to exact Tseitin encodings excludes the single-bit-flipped SAT neighbors and invalidates this adversary: the guarantee explicitly requires unrestricted valid CNFs.

## ITERATE: exact representation counterexample, not a new hard family

Earlier project /393-/394 supplied the transformation. For any CNF F and fresh z, let

P_z(F)= AND_{C in F} ( C AND (C OR z) AND (C OR NOT z) ).

This is duplicate-free for the studied source and is Boolean-equivalent to F for every assignment of old variables and of z, with at most a constant factor increase in explicitly encoded source length. It is a **new source representation**, not a new free inference rule in R_mix341.

**Theorem B (representation failure).** For every UNSAT F, toggling any one *valid literal-data bit*, whether polarity or variable-ID, of enc(P_z(F)) leaves an UNSAT CNF. Hence s_sign(enc(P_z(F)))=0 despite s_sign(enc(F(G,chi)))=12h. Proof: changing one source clause cannot make P_z(F) satisfiable. If it changes a guard, all original F clauses remain intact. If it changes original C, the two intact guards (C OR z) and (C OR NOT z) imply the original C regardless of z, so all of F remain forced. This is an all-size semantic proof; alterations of headers or clause-count metadata are **not** asserted to retain syntactic validity/UNSAT.

**Theorem C (block sensitivity persists).** If every source clause of UNSAT F is individually indispensable, then at P_z(F) the 3m disjoint two-bit source-sign blocks, each consisting of one original literal polarity and the corresponding polarity in its (C OR z) guard, are all pivotal. For each C take its source-deletion satisfying witness and set z=0. The original flipped C and changed (C OR z) guard are now true; the untouched (C OR NOT z) guard is true because NOT z=1; all other blocks are satisfied. Hence sign-bit block sensitivity of P_z(F) is **at least 12h**, despite all singleton data-bit sensitivity being zero. This does **not** prove that block sensitivity is invariant under arbitrary encodings. Repeated padding P_r from LSPV-2/394 destroys every at-most-r-clause SAT-restoring alteration while leaving SAT equivalence; obtaining its physical encoding and checking any resulting certificate are nonfree operations.

**Theorem D (exact sign-bit Hamming-radius amplification, all r).** For any nonempty clause-minimally-UNSAT CNF F with L signed literal occurrence fields, and any integer r>=1, let P_r(F)=AND_{C in F}[C AND AND_{j=1}^r ((C OR z_j) AND (C OR NOT z_j))] with independent fresh z_j and labelled source records. In the fixed binary grammar, the minimum number of **literal polarity-bit** flips that changes UNSAT to SAT is **exactly r+1**, while P_r(F) has at least L *pairwise-disjoint pivotal blocks, each of cardinality r+1*. Proof: under any assignment of original and z_j variables, some original C is false; then original C and exactly one guard in each z_j pair are false, giving r+1 distinct falsified source clauses. Up to r bit flips modify at most r distinct clause records, leaving UNSAT. For an upper bound, take the satisfying witness of F minus C, set all z_j=0, and flip a selected signed occurrence in C and in every (C OR z_j); all r+1 changed clauses now hold, all (C OR NOT z_j) guards are true, and every other source clause was already true. For each original occurrence the chosen r+1 source sign fields are distinct, and different occurrence blocks are disjoint. This is an exact source-encoding statement, based on the already established LSPV-2/394 repair-amplification mechanism, **not** a new superpolynomial hardness theorem. The input grows by Theta(r) local records; no work is hidden in the cost of creating a new encoding. The 2026-11-04 checker exhaustively measured the minimum number of falsified clauses as r+1 for r=1,2,3 on K4 and K3,3; finite tests do not prove the all-size statement.

**Interpretation:** There is a true all-algorithm lower bound on **reading a particular fixed input**. The source-dependent pivotality statistic itself is **not invariant under sound explicit representation transformations**, and in any event it is at most N and cannot yield superpolynomial time. A source rewrite changes the actual bitstring and its neighboring valid inputs. The two conclusions are compatible. One cannot use source sensitivity or logical DAG cost to infer more than the appropriate bit-access claim without a separately proved machine/proof transfer.
