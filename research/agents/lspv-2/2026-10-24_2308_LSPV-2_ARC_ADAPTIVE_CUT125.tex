\section{Exact source object and adaptive policy}
Let $E$ be a \emph{certified valid closed} sequence of $L$ events OPEN$(v)$, CLAUSE$(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)$, CLOSE$(v)$. Each named variable is opened once and closed once; all three literals of a proper ternary clause refer to distinct currently live variables. A read-only random-access event encoding and its verification/certificate costs are explicit premises, not free consequences of arbitrary ordinary CNF. Let $S_i$ denote the set of live source names after event $i$, for $0\le i\le L$, with $S_0=S_L=\varnothing$; let $w=\max_i|S_i|$.

Fix an integer budget $b\ge2$ and $d=\lfloor b/2\rfloor$. From boundary $i<L$, inspect all cuts
\[
J_i=\{j:\min(L,i+d)\le j\le \min(L,i+b)\}
\]
and choose the \emph{earliest} cut $j$ minimizing the acquired syntactic size $|S_j|$. Begin again at $j$. This selection uses observed variable identities and liveness, never the correct SAT label, child satisfiability or an oracle. Compare with the fixed baseline $i\mapsto\min(L,i+b)$.

For any resulting partition $P=(i_0=0<i_1<\cdots<i_q=L)$ define the source-structural candidate count
\[
C(P)=\sum_{r=1}^q 2^{|S_{i_{r-1}}|+|S_{i_r}|}.
\]
This is an upper bound on completely enumerated boundary-pair candidates, \emph{not} a lower bound on any exact decider, a count of actual SAT calls, or a requirement to compute all pairs.

\begin{theorem}[Lookahead schedule and exact source-named join; restricted]
For every certified valid closed scoped stream $E$ and $b\ge2$, the above policy emits a partition with at most $\lceil L/d\rceil$ blocks, each with at most $b$ events; every nonfinal block has at least $d$ events. There is a source-replay implementation that selects the cuts using $O(L+b)$ event visits and $O(w\log(N+2))$ additional writable bits, given a certified stream and indexed events. Discovery, stream validation and source access remain separately charged. For every such partition, the source-name-preserving pinned-child compiler of SCOPED-BLOCK-REBUILDER92 supplies \emph{ordinary proper 3CNF} children and an exact identity
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(E)\iff
\exists u_0,\ldots,u_q\
\bigwedge_{r=1}^q\left[
u_{r-1}|_{S_{i_{r-1}}\cap S_{i_r}}=
u_r|_{S_{i_{r-1}}\cap S_{i_r}}
\;\land\;
\operatorname{SAT}(\operatorname{Child}_r(u_{r-1},u_r))
\right]
\]
with $u_0=u_q=\emptyset$ and each $u_i:S_i\to\{0,1\}$. The parent can evaluate it by reachability over actual named cut assignments. A child has $O((b+w)\log(b+w+2))$ encoded bits in the declared fixed-record ordinary encoding, including pins, fresh auxiliaries and headers. These are restricted conditional construction bounds, \emph{not} an all-input ARC successor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The selected cut advances by at least $d$ unless it is the final cut and by at most $b$. Thus $q\le\lceil L/d\rceil$. Starting from a known validated active set, scan at most $b$ indexed events to evaluate candidate live sets and retain the earliest minimizing set, its source index and the current best snapshot. Resume at the chosen cut; revisited suffix events are included in the next scan. This requires at most $b\lceil L/d\rceil=O(L+b)$ event visits. Two active-name sets of size at most $w$ plus counters and addresses require $O(w\log(N+2))$ writable bits in this replay controller. This excludes the separately paid validation/certification of a claimed legal stream and excludes any input buffer, parent solver or ancestors. It does not bound the memory of the provided Python implementation, which stores all cut sets.

At any fixed pair of cut assignments, the established pin compiler injectively renames local variables, creates four proper ternaries with two private auxiliaries per unit pin, and preserves precisely the existential extension condition of that source block. Compatible common names on successive cuts agree. Since any source variable is live continuously from its OPEN through its CLOSE, agreements propagate across all blocks containing it; thus the glued local witnesses assign every original variable consistently. Conversely a global witness restricts to all cuts. The worst child contains at most $b$ original events, at most $2w$ unit pins, their auxiliaries, and a correctly serialized header; $O(b+w)$ local names/clauses each take $O(\log(b+w+2))$ bits. No fast validation of an arbitrary ordinary source into this promise is asserted.
\end{proof}
For $b=\lceil\sqrt L\rceil$ and $w=o(\log N)$ the theorem recovers the familiar conditional $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ block/candidate overhead and $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ ordinary child sizes, but it does not guarantee a strictly better worst-case exponent than earlier restricted scoped methods. Its measured gain is input-dependent and can be negative on other streams.
