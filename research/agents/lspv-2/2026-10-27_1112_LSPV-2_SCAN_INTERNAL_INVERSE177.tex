\section{Source emitter, typed gate indexes and exact scan grammar}
Fix $q\ge3$ assignment names and $m\ge1$ proper source-record slots. Let
\[
w=\lceil\log_2(q+1)\rceil,\quad N=m[1+3(w+1)],\quad
K=2^{\lceil\log_2(q+3m)\rceil},\quad h=\log_2K,\quad J=h(h+1)/2.
\]
Each sorted record has $D=w+3$ wires, and the fixed bitonic prefix has
$F=13w+32$ Boolean gates per comparator and
$G_f=KJ F/2$ gates total. All gate IDs are \emph{Boolean gate indexes}; wire IDs equal $b+g$, where $b=N+q+3$ denotes the global first-gate wire base. Distinct source-CNF compiler variable IDs are a different namespace.

The final forward records $\mathrm{rec}[p,c]$ are the indexed wires supplied by the restricted exact theorem of report 173. The scan begins at $G_f$ and has
\[
 L_0=19,\quad L_p=4w+18\ (p\ge1),\qquad
 B_0=G_f,\quad B_p=G_f+19+(p-1)(4w+18)\ (p\ge1).\tag{1}
\]
At every position $p$, two two-bit equality calls produce $\mathrm{isassign}_p$ and $\mathrm{isquery}_p$ in seven gates apiece. For $p>0$, $\mathrm{same}_p$ is a width-$w$ equality to the previous record ID, produced in $4w-1$ gates; for $p=0$ it is the constant false wire. The remaining \emph{five} gates, in their exact emitted order, are
\begin{align*}
 k_{p,0}&=\mathrm{AND}(\mathrm{same}_p,\mathrm{carry}_{p-1}),
&k_{p,1}&=\mathrm{AND}(\neg\mathrm{isassign}_p,k_{p,0}),\\
 k_{p,2}&=\mathrm{AND}(\mathrm{isassign}_p,\mathrm{payload}_p),
&k_{p,3}&=\mathrm{OR}(k_{p,2},k_{p,1}),\\
 k_{p,4}&=\mathrm{AND}(\mathrm{isquery}_p,k_{p,3}).&& \tag{2}
\end{align*}
Take $\mathrm{carry}_{-1}=0$; the previous carry is $k_{p-1,3}$. The scan-output $k_{p,4}$ enters exactly two inverse-layer MUX argument ports, as established and audited independently in report 173.

\section{Indexing the reduction tree without an occurrence table}
For $n$-bit equality, the emitter first produces $n$ independent \emph{three-gate XOR triples}: $(x\land\neg y)$, $(\neg x\land y)$ and their OR. The negated last wire of each triple is an equality leaf. A balanced \texttt{andmany} reduction creates exactly $n-1$ AND gates. Set
\[
 n_0=n,\qquad n_{j+1}=\lceil n_j/2\rceil,
\qquad d_j=\lfloor n_j/2\rfloor.
\]
At reduction level $j$, the $r$th newly allocated AND gate has global Boolean gate ID
\[
 g(j,r)=B+3n+\sum_{u<j}d_u+r,
 \qquad 0\le r<d_j.\tag{3}
\]
The operand \emph{wire} of the $r$th surviving item after $j$ reduction rounds can be calculated by following the unique pass-through chain:
\[
\mathrm{Ref}(0,r)=-(b+B+3r+2),\quad
\mathrm{Ref}(j+1,r)=
\begin{cases}
 b+g(j,r),&r<d_j,\\
 \mathrm{Ref}(j,2r),&r=d_j\text{ and }n_j\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}\tag{4}
\]
This is an \emph{iterative} tail recursion with at most $O(\log n)$ iterations and no stored array of levels. Each step recomputes the level prefix in (3) using one $O(\log G)$-bit counter. In particular \(\mathrm{GateAt}(g)\) of any scan gate, including all internal XOR and reduction nodes, follows from (1)--(4) plus the five tail gates (2) and arithmetic access to the two nearby sorted records. The construction never examines the materialized Boolean gate list.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform scan GateAt; \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}]
For every admissible $q,m$ and every $G_f\le g<G_{\rm scan-end}$, a single fixed deterministic bit-address program computes \(\mathrm{GateAt}(q,m,g)\), namely its exact operator and two signed predecessor wire IDs, with $O(\log^3G)$ bit operations and $O(\log G)$ combined writable/index bits. The bound charges arithmetic, full-width address construction, signed rails, field headers, and every emitted answer bit.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Decode the unique $p$ in (1) from $g-G_f$ by one comparison and quotient. The two kind-equality blocks are constant width. The optional ID-equality block has $n=w=O(\log G)$ leaves, so equations (3)--(4) compute any reduction gate's two predecessors by streaming over at most $O(\log w)$ levels and their prefixes. Only constant many $O(\log G)$-bit integers are live; conservative schoolbook arithmetic and all loop counts fit the stated polylog bound. The remaining five explicit gate formulas require constant many computed wire IDs. Every output is a signed wire ID in the exact original emitter's order, not an alternate circuit that happens to compute the same Boolean function.
\end{proof}

\section{Inverse signed consumers and actual proper source-CNF occurrences}
For a scan gate $g$ in block $p$, every use inside the scan appears in block $p$ or $p+1$. This follows because all key-field predecessor reads use \emph{fixed last-forward-record wires}, while the only value forwarded to the next scan record is the current carry $k_{p,3}$. In the current block every nonterminal equality or carry gate feeds at most one downstream gate, except $\mathrm{isassign}$, which feeds two. The scan output $k_{p,4}$ has no next-scan uses but has exactly two inverse-layer MUX uses. Hence all scan gates have
\[
\boxed{1\le f_g\le2.}\tag{5}
\]
To compute \(\Select_{\rm gate}(g,r)\), stream the at most $L_p+L_{p+1}=O(w)$ gate indexes in those two scan blocks, compute their signed operands by the Lemma, and retain those matching the target signed \emph{wire} $b+g$. For the terminal scan output insert instead its two already-derived inverse-layer MUX uses. The result is sorted by physical gate and argument position. \(\Rank\) is the inverse test of at most two such triples; absent positions are rejected. There is no consumer table, and the two-output temporary list is constant sized.

Each Boolean AND/OR gate is compiled to the project's 13-row proper three-CNF gate module, with fresh operand-clone and padding names. For its output source variable
\[
 v_g=b+9g+2,\tag{6}
\]
five occurrences lie in its own module: the AND output is positive once and negative four times; the OR output is negative once and positive four times. Every signed downstream gate-input use creates four properly padded clone rows, exactly twice with that rail sign and twice with the opposite sign. Therefore
\[
\#\mathrm{Occ}(v_g)=5+4f_g\in\{9,13\}.\tag{7}
\]
The ordered signed \(\mathrm{OccSelect}(v_g,r)\) returns the own-module five plus four rows for each at-most-two signed consumer triples, sorted by physical clause and literal slot. \(\mathrm{OccRank}\) scans the same constant list. Given the declared fixed-width source header and field width, the corresponding output-bit address is
\[
 \mathrm{pos}(c,s)=|\mathrm{header}|+(3c+s)(\mathrm{fieldwidth}+1).\tag{8}
\]
The sign bit and every source variable-ID bit are charged; a wire identifier is never substituted for a CNF variable identifier.

\begin{theorem}[All scan-internal signed inverse and occurrence access; \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}, independent review pending]
In the fixed architecture and notation above, for every scan Boolean gate $g$ and every candidate rank or physical clause/literal coordinate, exact signed gate-consumer $\Select/\Rank$ and proper source-CNF occurrence $\Select/\Rank$ are computed by a uniform table-free algorithm in
\[
\boxed{T=O(\log^4G)\text{ elementary bit operations},\qquad
S=O(\log G)\text{ writable/index bits}}.\tag{9}
\]
The bounds hold for all admissible $q,m$ and both source truth labels because these are structural accessors, independent of the witness values. They do \emph{not} bound evaluation of all $G$ gates or deciding SAT after eliminating the witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equations (1)--(4) give exact $\mathrm{GateAt}$ for any requested scan gate. Only the current and adjacent scan block contain a possible scan consumer; the two inverse-layer uses for a terminal scan output are the already proved final-layer MUX formulas of report 173. The direct fanout bound (5) makes the retained triples constant-sized. Each candidate gate needs at most $O(\log^3G)$ bit operations and there are $O(w)=O(\log G)$ candidates, giving the deliberately conservative upper (9) with a fixed number of $O(\log G)$-bit live registers. Equations (6)--(8), the independently specified 13-row emitter and freshness of operand clones yield exact occurrence signs and locations. Any candidate physical address is checked against this ordered bounded list. This includes negative arguments and duplicate source-value references in distinct operand slots. Neither complete gate construction nor complete source enumeration is used by the proposed accessor.
\end{proof}
