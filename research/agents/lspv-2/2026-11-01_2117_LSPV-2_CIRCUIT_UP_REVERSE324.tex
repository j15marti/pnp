\section{Theorem 324A: UP preservation}
Let $F$ be an arbitrary valid canonical original. Apply the previous source-grouped compiler 323: duplicate same-sign variable pairs define disjoint original-clause pairs; represent each pair of signed clauses by
\[
(A\vee B\vee C)\wedge(A\vee B\vee D)
=(A\vee B)\vee(C\wedge D);
\]
encode unpaired clauses by a private two-gate tree and combine group outputs using a binary AND tree. For negative clauses use the corresponding AND/OR/NAND implementation. Every gate receives a fresh auxiliary variable and the \emph{full} three-clause biconditional Tseitin definition. Add the unit requiring final output one and call the resulting ordinary CNF $E(F)$.

For every coherent partial assignment $p$ of \emph{original} variables,
\[
\mathrm{UP}(F\mid p)=\bot\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathrm{UP}(E(F)\mid p)=\bot.
\]
If neither refutes, their implied assignments projected to original variables are equal.
\paragraph{Proof.} In the paired source, when $A=B=0$ both $C,D$ are forced to $1$; an unknown $A$ is forced to $1$ precisely when $B=0$ and at least one of $C,D$ is $0$, and symmetrically for $B$; contradiction arises exactly when $A=B=0$ and either $C$ or $D$ is $0$. The three-gate full-biconditional factor has these identical unit rules under forced group output $1$. Sign complementation proves the negative-clause case. The single-clause two-gate representation likewise has exactly the source clause's unit rules. The AND tree transmits output $1$ to each group, all group auxiliaries are private, and the signed $(2,2)$ quotas make grouped source-clause IDs disjoint. Hence iteration of the same local unit consequences gives the same least fixed point and conflict status. \(\square\)
This excludes arbitrary auxiliary pinning and arbitrary global circuit minimization. It is not a polynomial SAT decision theorem.

\section{Theorem 324B: a paid 2SAT refutation}
For the \emph{same} valid F9 original, set $x_1=1,x_4=0,x_5=1,x_8=0$. Source UP and factored circuit UP both stall. The original clause IDs yield:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
11&\neg x_2\vee\neg x_9\\
5&x_2\vee x_7\\
12&\neg x_7\vee\neg x_9\\
4&x_6\vee x_9\\
8&\neg x_6\vee\neg x_7\\
6&x_7\vee x_9
\end{array}
\]
Two resolutions along $C_{11},C_5,C_{12}$ derive $\neg x_9$; two along $C_4,C_8,C_6$ derive $x_9$; one final resolution derives $\bot$. All five pivots were independently checked; all $32$ source-original completions fail. Across the complete $3^9=19683$ coherent F9 pin cubes, exactly $9172$ are UP-refuted, three further UNSAT cubes have a 2SAT SCC refutation, and $10508$ are SAT. This reproduces but does not claim novelty over the prior LSPV-4 2SAT observation.
