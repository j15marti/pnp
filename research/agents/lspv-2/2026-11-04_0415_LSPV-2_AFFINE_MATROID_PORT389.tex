\section*{Proved projection criterion}
Represent each affine row by a vector $a_i\in\mathbb F_2^n$ of Hamming weight $d_i\in\{2,3\}$, a bit $b_i$, and \emph{all} $2^{d_i-1}$ signed clauses forbidding the wrong-parity local assignments. The source includes an authenticated row-to-source partition; the partition is checked against the signed clauses, never a new source axiom. Let $F(A,b)$ be the conjunction, $M=\sum_{i=1}^{m}2^{d_i-1}$, $S_i=\operatorname{supp}(a_i)$ and $A_{-i}$ be $A$ without row $i$.

\begin{theorem}[Matroid circuit with full local projection]
For every integer $m\ge3$ and every such explicitly expanded signed system, assume
\[
\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}a_i=0,\qquad
\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}b_i=1,\qquad
\forall i:\operatorname{rank}(A_{-i})=m-1,
\]
and the independently testable local projection condition
\[
\forall i:\quad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}
\left(\operatorname{pr}_{S_i}\ker A_{-i}\right)=d_i-1.
\tag{P}
\]
Then every one of the $M$ original signed CNF clauses is individually indispensable for UNSAT, and the \emph{global} minimum for the unchanged full mixed calculus is
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F(A,b))=M-1.}
\]
This is an all-size theorem for a fixed, rank-checkable promised class, without a search-depth or auxiliary-variable bound; it is not a claim about arbitrary general CNFs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The XOR of all row equations is $0=1$, so $F$ is UNSAT. Removing row $i$ leaves $m-1$ linearly independent equations and hence an affine solution space $U_i$ with direction $\ker A_{-i}$. Since $a_i$ is the sum of the other rows, every point of $U_i$ has the \emph{wrong} row-$i$ parity $1-b_i$. Its projection onto $S_i$ is a coset of the hyperplane $\{z\in\mathbb F_2^{d_i}:\oplus z=0\}$. Condition (P) gives the full dimension $d_i-1$, so the projection is the entire wrong-parity coset. Thus for every original row-$i$ clause $C$, there is an assignment satisfying all other rows and all the other row-$i$ signed clauses but falsifying precisely $C$. Deleting any single original clause makes the source SAT.

Every sound conservative refutation must therefore have all $M$ original source clauses in the ancestry of its contradiction node. A shared proof DAG with $q$ positive-cost derived nodes, at most two premises per derived node and $M$ indispensable leaves has at least $M+q-1$ edges by undirected connectedness but at most $2q$ edges by the arity bound. Consequently $q\ge M-1$. This already quantifies over every permitted mixed derivation, arbitrary adaptive legal choices, auxiliary gates, temporary growth, and sharing.

Conversely, for each row, conjoin its $2^{d_i-1}$ original clauses using $2^{d_i-1}-1$ AND3 nodes; every intermediate has original-variable support $S_i$ of size at most three. The final truth table is exactly the row equation $a_i\cdot x=b_i$ and is deterministically checked on at most eight assignments. XOR the $m$ authenticated equations in $m-1$ further paid binary nodes. Their sum is $0=1$. The constructed cost is
\[
\sum_i(2^{d_i-1}-1)+(m-1)=M-1,
\]
meeting the lower bound. No algebra is a free rule, no proof searches for a shortest proof, and no circuit payload is hidden in a unit-cost inference.
\end{proof}
