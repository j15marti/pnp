\section{Three falsifiers and exact result}
F1: exact source-preserving interface projection supports both SAT and UNSAT with witnesses and refutations: \emph{proved under its explicit partition hypothesis}. F2: symbolic access makes all guest character reads cheap: \emph{falsified for the replaying accessor}. F3: a bounded-interface output is always power-short: \emph{falsified by the measured six-boundary-variable parity controls}.
Let
\[
F(B,P_1,\ldots,P_h)=H(B)\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^h F_i(B_i,P_i),
\quad B_i\subseteq B,\quad P_i\cap P_j=\varnothing\ (i\neq j),
\]
where the partition is computed and verified, every source clause retains its original index and signed occurrences, and $|B|=b$. Let $p(\beta)$ be true precisely when $H(\beta)$ and every $F_i|_\beta$ are satisfiable. Renumber boundary variables \emph{in the ordinary child} as $y_1,\ldots,y_b$ and define
\[
D_\beta=\bigvee_{j=1}^b \begin{cases}
y_j&\beta_j=0,\\
\neg y_j&\beta_j=1,
\end{cases}\quad
G(y)=\bigwedge_{\beta:p(\beta)=0}D_\beta.
\]
When $b=0$, an empty conjunction means true and an empty clause means false.

\begin{theorem}[Same-source both-label correctness]
For every valid verified partition and coherent boundary assignment $\beta$,
\[
G(\beta)\iff\exists P_1\cdots P_h\, F(\beta,P_1,\ldots,P_h).
\]
Consequently $\operatorname{SAT}(F)\iff\operatorname{SAT}(G)$. Satisfying assignments reconstruct independently through the private components and are checked on every original clause. A rejected $\beta$ has an original-clause proof of $F\models D_\beta$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The existential quantifiers over disjoint private sets distribute over the factors. A full-width clause $D_\gamma$ is false at the unique boundary word $\gamma$. Hence $G(\beta)$ holds iff $\beta$ is feasible. A feasible word admits independent private witnesses. If infeasible, either an original boundary clause is false or a component has no satisfying completion. In either case no full source model agrees with $\beta$, proving the blocker. No literal occurrence is treated as a new variable.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Source resolution discharge]
A complete source-indexed DPLL refutation of $F_i|_\rho$ converts into an ordinary original-source resolution DAG rooted at a clause $L$ falsified by the boundary assignment $\rho$. Hence $L\subseteq D_\rho$ and $F\models L\models D_\rho$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each contradiction leaf cites the original clause, which is false under its branch assignment. At a split on private variable $x$, if either child-derived clause omits the pivot in the sign it could have when false, reuse that clause; otherwise resolve the left clause containing $x$ and the right clause containing $\neg x$. The resulting clause is false under the parent assignment. Induction removes all private branch variables. The root contains only boundary literals false under $\rho$. All proof nodes record parents, pivot and original-source clause identifiers. Emitting the full-width $D_\rho$ is a sound weakening of the derived root, not a claim that pure resolution directly derived the widened clause.
\end{proof}

\section{Computed total character accessor and its tariff}
Use ASCII DIMACS serialization with header \texttt{p cnf b q} and one canonical $b$-literal line for each bad mask in increasing order. A character query first recomputes the number of bad masks for the header and then rescans profiles until reaching the target character, returning blank for an out-of-bounds position. Neither the child text nor the set of bad profiles is persisted. If $W_{\max}$ and $S_{\rm profile}$ are the maximum \emph{fully charged} per-mask decision work and space and $S_D$ is a separately charged source/decomposition descriptor, the implementation has
\[
T_{\rm char}=O(2^b[W_{\max}+b\log(b+2)+\log(N+2)]),\qquad
S_{\rm char}=S_D+\max_{\beta}S_{\rm profile}(F,\beta)+O(b\log(N+2)).
\]
A guest making $Q$ character requests may incur $Q$ times the displayed work. Discovery, policy failure/switch, source queries, parent and child simulation, retaining ancestor states and peak writable memory are additional paid obligations. This accessor has a precise and possibly enormous replay tariff, rather than a proved ARC bound.
