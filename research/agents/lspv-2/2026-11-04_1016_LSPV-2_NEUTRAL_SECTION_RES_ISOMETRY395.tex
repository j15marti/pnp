\section*{IDEA before SEARCH}
For CNF $F(X)$ and a conservative definitional bank $D(X,Y)$, conjecture that if a single auxiliary assignment $a$ satisfies every bank clause for \emph{all} old-variable assignments $X$, no strict Resolution refutation can be shortened by adding the bank. Falsifiers: a bank clause surviving the $Y=a$ restriction; a failed SAT projection; a shorter strict-Resolution proof of the enlarged formula; or treating high incidence treewidth as necessary machine work. Contrast with Cook extension variables, which can shorten ordinary Resolution proofs.

\begin{theorem}[neutral-section strict Resolution isometry]
Let $F(X)$ be any UNSAT CNF. Let $D(X,Y)$ be a CNF with $X\cap Y=\varnothing$ and suppose some $a\in\{0,1\}^Y$ makes each clause of $D(X,a)$ identically true for all $X$. Then, counting one node per nontrivial binary Resolution inference in an unrestricted shared-DAG derivation,
\[
W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F\wedge D)=W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F).
\]
In addition, $\exists Y\,(F\wedge D)\equiv F$, for \emph{both} SAT and UNSAT formulas $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every old source clause remains, so the enlarged optimum is at most the old optimum. Conversely fix all $Y=a$ in any refutation of the enlargement. Every $D$-clause becomes true and is deleted, and each old clause stays unchanged. Resolution is closed under restrictions without increasing inference count: inductively retain a subclause of the restricted derived clause, resolving when the complementary pivot survives in both retained parents, and otherwise replacing the node by its surviving stronger parent. If a node becomes true, omit it. At the sink, the empty clause cannot be strengthened, so a refutation of $F$ remains. This proves the reverse inequality. The fixed all-$a$ extension establishes the SAT projection.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Necessary but not sufficient speedup condition.} If a bank shortens strict Resolution, it admits no fixed auxiliary assignment making it a tautology over all old assignments. The converse fails for $F=(x)\wedge(\neg x)$ and $D=(\neg z\vee x)\wedge(z\vee\neg x)$: no fixed $z$ neutralizes $D$, but both minimum Resolution counts equal one.

\section*{Uniform unique-extension grid construction}
Fix an old variable $x$ and integer $k\ge2$. Introduce fresh grid wires $u_{ij}$ for $1\le i,j\le k$ and unit clauses $(\neg u_{ij})$. For each of the $2k(k-1)$ grid edges $(u,v)$ introduce $z_e$ with the three source clauses
\[
(\neg z_e\vee u),\qquad(\neg z_e\vee v),\qquad
(z_e\vee\neg u\vee\neg v),
\]
encoding $z_e\leftrightarrow(u\land v)$. Introduce one bridge $w\leftrightarrow(x\land u_{11})$ with the same three-clause encoding. Every old assignment has \emph{exactly one} auxiliary completion: every new variable is zero. This assignment satisfies the entire bank for any old $X$.

Writing $D_k$ for the bank,
\[
|Y|=3k^2-2k+1,\qquad
|D_k|=7k^2-6k+3.
\]
The clause--variable incidence graph contains a subdivision of the $k$ by $k$ grid: for each grid edge select the length-two path through its ternary defining clause. Thus its treewidth is at least $k$, while the exact Resolution minimum stays unchanged. The bridge connects this graph to the old incidence graph when the latter is connected. Encoded signed-index size added is $O(k^2\log(n+k))$ bits and the uniform construction has polynomial machine cost. No large intermediate is assumed free.

For Haken's standard unary $PHP_{h+1}^h$ with $h(h+1)$ variables, $h+1+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses and $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ explicit signed-index encoding bits, take $k=h$. Haken (1985, TCS 39:297--308) establishes $2^{\Omega(h)}$ lines for \emph{every} unrestricted ordinary shared-DAG Resolution refutation, excluding extension variables and other rules. Isometry preserves that restricted minimum after padding. Cook (1976, SIGACT News 8(4):28--32) gives polynomial Extended Resolution pigeonhole proofs, so no unrestricted SAT algorithm hardness is transferred.

\section*{Mixed-calculus finite optimum corollary}
If $F$ has $m$ individually indispensable original clauses and a previously certified $m-1$-node proof in the unchanged source-authenticated, binary-premise unit-paid $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, then
\[
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F\wedge D_k)=m-1.
\]
Deleting an old clause leaves a satisfiable old formula, and its satisfying assignment extends through the zero auxiliary bank. Therefore every sound refutation must contain at least $m$ distinct indispensable old-source leaves. Any connected proof DAG with $m$ required leaves and paid in-degree at most two needs at least $m-1$ inference nodes. The old matching proof remains available. This uses no assumption of full mixed-system restriction closure. It covers the four-clause blocker (exact $3$), six-clause odd XOR triangle (exact $5$), and previously certified bridgeless cubic odd-Tseitin (exact $4h-1$), not arbitrary nonaffine formulas.
