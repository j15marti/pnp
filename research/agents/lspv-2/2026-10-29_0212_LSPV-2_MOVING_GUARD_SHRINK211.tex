\section{Independent check of the fixed-interface obstruction}
\begin{proposition}[Static modular coverage obstruction, independently reviewed]
Let $F$ be connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF on $n$ variables, with each variable in exactly four clauses and $m=4n/3$. Let $Y$ contain $b>0$ variables; partition every private variable into $k$ nonempty disjoint modules, each containing all clauses that use its private variables, with no clause meeting two private modules. If every module has a syntactic ternary-clause-hitting set $B_i$ of size at most $q$ after accounting for $Y$, then
\[
 k\le4b,\quad n\le3\bigl(b+\sum_i|B_i|\bigr)\le3b+12bq,
 \quad b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-\tfrac14.
\]
For $b=0$, connectedness forces one nonempty private module and $q\ge n/3$. Thus both $b,q=O(\log N)$ are impossible on an unbounded connected exact-$(2,2)$ family with canonical $N=\Theta(n\log(n+2))$ bits.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every private module has at least one clause touching $Y$, or its incidence component would be disconnected. Such clauses are distinct across modules, since no clause contains private variables from two modules. Each of the $b$ boundary variables has four incidences, hence $k\le4b$. By hypothesis $H=Y\cup\bigcup_iB_i$ hits every clause, and each variable hits at most four, so $4n/3=m\le4|H|$. Therefore $n\le3(b+\sum_i|B_i|)\le3b+12bq$. As $b(q+1/4)\ge n/12$, AM--GM gives $b+q+1/4\ge\sqrt{n/3}$. If $Y=\emptyset$, connectedness forces $k=1$ and the same hitting bound gives $q\ge n/3$.
\end{proof}
This uses a \emph{fixed} interface and syntactic clause hitting; moving boundaries or semantic simplification escape it. It is a coverage bound, not SAT complexity, even though local backdoors can be discovered with a $3^q\operatorname{poly}(N)$ search when a partition and $Y$ are supplied \cite{nrs2004}.

\section{Exact closed-chain construction}
Define $\mathcal C$ as the following \emph{tagged ordinary proper 3CNF} inputs. There are core variables $x_0,\ldots,x_{r-1}$, $r\ge3$. For each consecutive scope $\{x_i,x_{i+1},x_{i+2}\}$, $0\le i\le r-3$, there is at least one proper signed clause and at most eight distinct sign patterns. There are no other core clauses. There is also an arbitrary Boolean end relation $G$ on
\[
 E_r=\{x_0,x_1,x_{r-2},x_{r-1}\},\quad |E_r|\le4.
\]
Its at most 16-bit truth table is encoded literally in a canonical DIMACS comment. The same relation is also represented in the actual ordinary CNF clauses, so a SAT parser ignoring comments sees the correct formula. Each forbidden 4-bit end assignment yields a four-literal clause $L_1\vee L_2\vee L_3\vee L_4$, encoded using one fresh auxiliary $u$ and two proper ternary clauses
\[
 (L_1\vee L_2\vee u)\wedge(\neg u\vee L_3\vee L_4).
\]
For at most three distinct ends, output the shorter forbidden-assignment clause directly. The source and child must parse and regenerate byte-identically; this validation reads the full source and charges both the comment and every gadget clause. The at-least-one clause per scope guarantees the physical decimal-DIMACS encoding length $N=\Theta(r\log(r+2))$ as $r\to\infty$. This is a syntactic subclass of ordinary 3CNF, not all SAT.

\begin{lemma}[Exact four-variable moving-interface identity]
For $r>96$ put $h=\max(4,\lfloor\sqrt r\rfloor)$, $c=r-h$, and let $C_{\rm keep}$ contain precisely the core clauses with all indices at least $c$. Let $C_{\rm drop}$ contain the others. Define the new four-end relation on
$E_h=\{x_c,x_{c+1},x_{r-2},x_{r-1}\}$ by
\[
 G'(\alpha)=\exists x_0\cdots x_{c-1}\;
    \left[G(x_0,x_1,x_{r-2},x_{r-1})\wedge
                 \bigwedge C_{\rm drop}\right]_{E_h=\alpha}.
\]
Then, exactly as Boolean functions of the $h$ surviving variables,
\[
 \exists x_0\cdots x_{c-1}\;F(x_0,\ldots,x_{r-1})
 \ \equiv\ G'(E_h)\wedge\bigwedge C_{\rm keep}.
\]
Moreover $G'$ is computable deterministically in polynomial time using at most four rolling truth-value states for each of at most 16 assignments to $E_h$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A consecutive-triple clause meeting both eliminated and retained variables can touch only $x_c,x_{c+1}$ on the retained side; clauses with all variables retained factor out of the existential quantifier. The old guard touches on the retained side only $x_{r-2},x_{r-1}$. Therefore all dependence on the surviving variables of the eliminated portion is captured by exactly the specified four ends. Enumerate their at most 16 assignments. For each, sweep $x_0,\ldots,x_{c-1}$, checking a core clause when its greatest-index variable becomes known; continue through the two fixed variables $x_c,x_{c+1}$ to discharge crossing triples. At every sweep step, retain the set of feasible assignments to the last two variables, at most four tuples. The old guard is checked when $x_0,x_1$ have become known, using the fixed last-two end values. Induction on the sweep index proves that every retained pair extends to a satisfying prefix and every satisfying prefix yields a retained pair. No later dropped clause uses any forgotten variable. Thus the relation is exact. Full clause parsing, endpoint bucketing and table reconstruction are polynomially charged.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Power-short single-child map on $\mathcal C$]
There is an unconditional uniform deterministic algorithm on the explicitly defined \emph{closed} annotated ordinary-3CNF class $\mathcal C$ with a single recursive child of physical bit length at most $N^{19/20}$ for every input above a fixed threshold. It preserves SAT and UNSAT, creates at most 16 guard auxiliaries and 32 guard clauses in a nontrivial projection child, and directly solves bounded-size bases. Its repeated recursion runs in polynomial time. No hypothetical SAT solver is called.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $r>96$, compute $G'$ exactly by the lemma and write the renumbered consecutive-scope child with $h=\lfloor\sqrt r\rfloor$ core variables, its at-most-16-bit relation, at most 16 auxiliaries and at most 32 guard clauses. Its physical size is $O(h\log(h+2))$, including all headers, aliases, literal indices, and regenerated ordinary clauses. The input has length $\Omega(r\log(r+2))$ from its mandatory consecutive-scope clauses. Thus for sufficiently large $r$, $N_{\rm child}\le N^{19/20}$, with no fixed-size table hidden outside the physical output.

For the finitely many smaller sizes where the size test might fail, the single program runs the \emph{already proved} bounded-frontier decision procedure on the parent and outputs the canonical three-core-variable YES or NO formula. These have respectively 54 and 129 bytes in the executed serialization, and are already in $\mathcal C$. Since an input with $r>96$ has at least 95 core-clause lines, hence more than 760 bytes, even the 129-byte base satisfies $8\cdot129<(8\cdot760)^{19/20}\le N^{19/20}$ in physical bits. The unsuccessful candidate and fallback decision are charged. For $r\le96$ use the bounded-frontier decision procedure directly, with no recursive call. This is a fixed, uniform rule, not free recognition of the SAT answer. Every non-base step reduces core count to $\lfloor\sqrt r\rfloor$ or to 3, so termination follows and the child remains in $\mathcal C$. All parser operations, numerical renumbering, guard computation, gadget compilation, and state accounting are polynomial in the physical input length. The one-call recurrence is polynomial.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Scope of constructive progress.}
The algorithm is more informative than mapping a known P-family directly to YES/NO because for sufficiently large members the \emph{ordinary-3CNF child retains a nontrivial suffix} and an exact projected guard. It cannot be generalized by assuming that arbitrary SAT has a constant-width projection or that an unknown good order is supplied. Its exact future-variable interface is given by the rigid clause-scope promise, not discovered for free.

\section{OPEN barrier and targeted next search}
The next load-bearing lemma is an \emph{unconditional, uniformly discoverable} sequence of exact moving/hierarchical SAT summaries for arbitrary ordinary 3CNF whose full physical child representations, changes of order, preprocessing, retries, and all subsequent recursive calls are jointly polynomially bounded and produce a power-short ordinary child. Static global logarithmic hitting cannot provide this on connected exact-$(2,2)$ inputs, but the obstruction does not prohibit moving guards. The present result assumes a consecutive-scope promise that is directly verifiable, and cannot import the same property into unrestricted SAT by a cost-free change of representation. Heterogeneous signed variable degrees, high-width separators, SAT/UNSAT-side discovery and hard arbitrary residual cores remain \textbf{OPEN}. Neither equality nor separation follows.
