\section{Exact source-indexed mixed-interface theorem}
Assume every indexed original clause belongs to exactly one factor in
\[
F(B,P_1,\ldots,P_h)=H(B)\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^h F_i(B_i,P_i),
\quad B_i\subseteq B,\quad P_i\cap P_j=\varnothing\ (i\ne j),
\]
and no private variable occurs in $H$ or any other component. For every boundary assignment $\beta\in\{0,1\}^{B_i}$ with $F_i|_\beta$ UNSAT, discharge assumptions until a partial assignment $\gamma\subseteq\beta$ remains such that $F_i|_\gamma$ is UNSAT. Record a complete source-indexed refutation of that residual and form its blocker
\[
D_\gamma=\bigvee_{v\in\mathrm{dom}\gamma}
\begin{cases}x_v,&\gamma(v)=0,\\\neg x_v,&\gamma(v)=1.\end{cases}
\]
Let $\mathcal D$ collect these blockers for all components and all bad boundary assignments, and define $G$ by conjoining $H$ and $\mathcal D$, deleting duplicate and literally subsumed clauses.

\begin{theorem}[Exact, both-label source-cover antichain]
For every coherent full assignment $b\in\{0,1\}^{B}$,
\[
\exists P_1\cdots P_h\ F(b,P_1,\ldots,P_h)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G(b).
\]
Thus the physically emitted ordinary-CNF child $G$ is SAT iff $F$ is SAT. Every child witness extends to independently verified component witnesses; a complete child UNSAT refutation and all source-authenticated profile proofs constitute a typed UNSAT certificate for the original source. This does not assert a pure original-source resolution proof or any size upper bound for $G$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exact indexed clause coverage and disjoint private-variable sets give
$\exists P F(b,P)\iff H(b)\wedge\bigwedge_i\exists P_i F_i(b|_{B_i},P_i)$.
If component $i$ is satisfiable at its boundary, it cannot falsify an authenticated blocker: such a blocker is false exactly when a proven unsatisfiable guard $\gamma$ is contained in the boundary assignment. Conversely, for each boundary assignment $\beta$ making $F_i$ UNSAT, the exhaustive profile includes its discharged $\gamma\subseteq\beta$, so $D_\gamma$ is false at $\beta$. Therefore each component's blocker conjunction equals its existential boundary projection. Deleting a clause subsumed by another preserves the same truth function. Component witnesses combine since their private sets are disjoint. These arguments apply to both SAT and UNSAT.\end{proof}
