\section*{Independent construction and exact counts}
Use an unrooted tree with $m$ clause leaves, $m-2$ internal degree-three vertices, and $2m-3$ edges.  Give every tree vertex a gauge, and every edge a fresh $e$.  At leaf $i$ add
\[
\operatorname{NAE}(a_i,b_i,u_i),\qquad
\operatorname{NAE}(\neg u_i,c_i,g_i),
\]
and at edge $vw$ add
\[
\operatorname{NAE}(g_v,\neg g_w,e),\qquad
\operatorname{NAE}(g_v,\neg g_w,\neg e).
\]
The edge pair is satisfiable iff $g_v=g_w$, with both $e$ values then free.  Thus all gauges equal one bit $g$ and the source projection is $x=X\oplus g$.  The exact ledger is
\[
n+m+(2m-2)+(2m-3)=n+5m-5,
\qquad 2m+2(2m-3)=6m-6.
\]
Internal gauges occur six times; all triples are proper.  The formula needs the explicit hypothesis $m\ge2$.

For a satisfying source witness $x$, let $h_F(x)$ count clauses whose third literal is true and whose first two literal values differ.  The global gauge contributes two choices, the $2m-3$ edge variables are free, and exactly those $h_F(x)$ frontends have two choices of $u_i$.  Hence the fiber is
\[
2^{1+(2m-3)+h_F(x)}=2^{2m-2+h_F(x)}.
\]
This proves nonparsimonious witness preservation, not hardness amplification.
