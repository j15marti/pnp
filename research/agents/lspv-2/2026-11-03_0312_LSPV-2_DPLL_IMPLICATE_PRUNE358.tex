\section*{Claim card: fixed calculus, metric, quantifiers}
Keep the same preselected, source-authenticated, binary-premise, unit-weight $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. This report uses only its strict non-tautological binary Resolution (RES) and paid conservative, source-independent signed two-input AND VARDEF rules. One genuine distinct RES node costs one; one conservative two-input VARDEF costs one and authenticates its three defining clauses without additional inference weight. Original CNF clauses cost zero. A repeated DAG node is charged once. Optimizer discovery and actual checking/storage work are recorded separately. The theorem does not add any local semantic implication axiom, global truth-table rule, or free weakening.

Take any finite canonical, non-tautological CNF $S$ whose source clauses are nonempty, and any non-tautological target clause $C$. Let $V$ contain the $k$ distinct variables occurring in $S$ or $C$, and let $s=|\operatorname{var}(C)|$. A source empty clause is a separately trivial immediate refutation of charge zero. Every source clause is an explicitly authenticated premise, including when a source appears multiple times in an input encoding. An effective parser for general signed-decimal DIMACS must normalize or reject syntactic duplicates/tautologies with charged time; the mathematical statement does not silently give such parsing free computation.

\begin{theorem}[Subsumption-first implicate compiler; all input sizes]
For every $k\ge1$, every such $S,C$ over the $k$ names, if $S\models C$, then the unchanged RES+VARDEF fragment constructs an authenticated, acyclic shared proof either of the \emph{exact clause} $C$ or of an earlier empty clause, using at most
\[
\boxed{B(k,s)=2^{k-s}-1+3\max\{s-1,0\}}
\]
distinct paid inference nodes. This is a bound on an explicitly constructed derivation, not a claim that it is globally minimum or that its construction takes polynomial time for unrestricted $k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $U=V\setminus\operatorname{var}(C)$, $t=|U|=k-s$, and assign each variable occurring in $C$ to \emph{falsify} its unique target literal. Enumerate the $2^t$ assignments $\alpha$ of the remaining variables $U$. Since $S\models C$, every resulting full assignment $\widehat\alpha$ falsifies some actual nonempty source clause $D_\alpha\in S$. Every literal of $D_\alpha$ is false under $\widehat\alpha$, so its set of literals is contained in $C\cup B_\alpha$, where $B_\alpha$ is the blocking clause consisting of the literals false at $\alpha$ on $U$.

Construct a complete binary decision tree over $U$, bottom-up, retaining one original authenticated clause as the leaf label for every $\alpha$. The invariant at a tree node with assigned prefix $\gamma$ is that the current derived clause $D_\gamma$ is a subset of $C\cup B_\gamma$, where $B_\gamma$ contains only the literals falsified by the \emph{prefix} assignment. At the children split on $x\in U$, the left label is a subset of $C\cup B_\gamma\cup\{x\}$ and the right of $C\cup B_\gamma\cup\{\neg x\}$. If the left omits $x$, inherit the left clause without inference; if the right omits $\neg x$, inherit the right. Otherwise, perform one strict RES inference on $x$. The resulting clause is non-tautological because both branches use the same signs on $C$ and on all variables in the common prefix. This proves the invariant. There are at most $2^t-1$ paid merge nodes, and at the root the resulting clause $D$ satisfies $D\subseteq C$.

If $D$ is empty, we already have a refutation. If $D$ is nonempty, add each literal $\ell\in C\setminus D$ by a \emph{paid} conservative weakening. Choose any currently present literal $u$. Introduce a fresh gate $y\leftrightarrow(u\land\neg\ell)$ at one paid VARDEF node. Its three authenticated defining clauses are $(\neg y\vee u)$, $(\neg y\vee\neg\ell)$, and $(y\vee\neg u\vee\ell)$. Two genuine RES inferences
\[
(D,\;y\vee\neg u\vee\ell)\ \vdash\ (D\setminus\{u\})\vee y\vee\ell,
\quad
((D\setminus\{u\})\vee y\vee\ell,\;\neg y\vee u)\ \vdash\ D\vee\ell
\]
produce the exact one-literal weakening at cost three. New gates are conservative and source-independent, and the fresh $y$ never appears in the resulting clause. Repeat for every missing literal. Because $D\ne\bot$, there are at most $s-1$ missing literals. Thus the additional charge is at most $3\max(s-1,0)$. All certificates use authentic source/definition references and strict binary RES. \hfill$\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact three-variable local clause consequence bound]
For every nonempty-clause canonical CNF $S$ on at most three named variables and any entailed nontautological clause $C$ on the same names, the existing RES+paid-VARDEF calculus either derives $C$ exactly or terminates with $\bot$ after at most \textbf{seven} paid nodes. The per-output-width maxima for $k=3$ are $7$ for $s=0$, $3$ for $s=1$, $4$ for $s=2$, and $6$ for $s=3$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Substitute $k=3$ into $B(k,s)$. Clauses over fewer occurring names can be considered over three names with unused variables, or use their smaller $k$ directly. \hfill$\square$
\end{proof}

\noindent This sharpens the \emph{general single local implicate} upper certificate of 32 RES+VARDEF nodes in LSPV-5 REFEREE310 to 7, under the same canonical support restriction and early-contradiction convention. It does \emph{not} sharpen REFEREE310's separate 48-node bound for one complete eight-clause output factor, because those eight blockers may each require up to six paid nodes. There is no claim that every individual clause requires seven nodes, nor that seven is globally optimal in the full mixed calculus. The all-input worst-case minimum $\overline W_R$ and B3 do not follow.

\section*{Four falsifiers and their dispositions}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{F1.} Could a falsified leaf be a hidden semantic oracle? \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the producer selects an actual source-indexed clause and the independent checker replays that clause; assignment enumeration is external discovery work and carries no proof inference axiom.
\item[] \textbf{F2.} Could a branch fail to produce a valid strict pivot when one child lacks its blocking literal? \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: it inherits the stronger child at zero new nodes. Other cases have genuinely complementary pivot literals and a nontautological exact resolvent.
\item[] \textbf{F3.} Could a final empty subclause be weakened to $C$ for free? \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: no such operation is used. If $D=\bot$, the whole refutation terminates. Otherwise every missing target literal is reconstructed by one paid VARDEF and two RES nodes. Arbitrary downstream typed proof statements are \emph{not} thereby simulated.
\item[] \textbf{F4.} Does a small local compiler imply a polynomial global XOR/circuit compiler? \textbf{OPEN/UNSUPPORTED}: when $k$ is large, the $2^{k-s}$ term is exponential; a global GF(2) row and a circuit-valued CONVERT can involve unbounded support and different statement types. No B1/B2/B3 or global proof-system equivalence is claimed.
\end{description}
