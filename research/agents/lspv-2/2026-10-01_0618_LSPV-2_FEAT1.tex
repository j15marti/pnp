\paragraph{Two current obstacles.}
First, vMS does not uniquely fix the bit-level encoder needed for a unique author-canonical Boolean product-span object. Second, after a legal encoder is declared, one must compute the common degree-two relation space across legal source states; low rank of one slice does not imply low global rank.

\paragraph{Primary-source boundary.}
Shaltiel--Umans (JACM 2005) gives the algebraic extractor and binary conversion architecture; van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski (2025) fixes the RMV/PCPP architecture but not a unique bit-level encoder for the present ANF question. Coding-theory literature treats componentwise powers of linear codes as the exact algebraic object: Cascudo--Cramer--Mirandola show that squares of random linear codes typically saturate near the natural quadratic threshold, while Randriambololona gives Singleton-type product bounds. These are structural comparisons, not SAT-time lower bounds.

\paragraph{Setup from CODE1.}
Fix one declared legal binary code
\[
c:\mathbb F_q\to\mathbb F_2^N
\]
and legal q-ary orbit tuple
\[
a(v)=(a_1(v),\ldots,a_m(v)).
\]
Put
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2
\]
and let \(B_v\in\mathbb F_2^{N\times M}\) be the slice matrix whose columns are
\[
\mathbf 1,\quad c(a_i(v)),\quad c(a_i(v))\star c(a_j(v))\quad(i<j).
\]
CODE1 proved that the global product span is the rank of the vertical stack of all legal \(B_v\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: encoder-square ceiling for every slice.}
Define
\[
W_c=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\bigl(\{\mathbf 1\}\cup\{c(a):a\in\mathbb F_q\}\bigr)
\subseteq\mathbb F_2^N
\]
and
\[
W_c^{\star2}=\operatorname{span}\{x\star y:x,y\in W_c\}.
\]
Every column of every \(B_v\) lies in \(W_c^{\star2}\). Hence
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}B_v\le d_2(c):=\dim W_c^{\star2}\le N.}
\]
If \(d=\dim W_c\), choose a basis \(w_1,\ldots,w_d\). Since the square is spanned by the \(\binom{d+1}{2}\) products \(w_i\star w_j\) with \(i\le j\),
\[
\boxed{d_2(c)\le \min\left\{N,\binom{d+1}{2}\right\}.}
\]
No linearity of the message map is required. If \(c(\mathbb F_q)\) lies in a \(k\)-dimensional linear code \(C\), then \(d\le k+1\), so
\[
\operatorname{rank}B_v\le\min\left\{N,\binom{k+2}{2}\right\}.
\]
If additionally \(\mathbf1\in C\), then \(d\le k\) and
\[
\operatorname{rank}B_v\le\min\left\{N,\binom{k+1}{2}\right\}.
\]
These are slice ceilings only and do not bound the vertical stack across different \(v\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: finite full-rank certificate lower bound.}
For any finite legal state set \(S\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}\begin{bmatrix}B_v\end{bmatrix}_{v\in S}
\le \sum_{v\in S}\operatorname{rank}B_v
\le |S|d_2(c).
\]
Therefore any full-rank certificate for \(\rho(H)=M\) must contain at least
\[
\boxed{|S|\ge \left\lceil\frac{M}{d_2(c)}\right\rceil}
\]
legal source states. The cruder encoder-independent bound is
\[
|S|\ge\left\lceil\frac{M}{N}\right\rceil.
\]
Combined with STACK1's upper certificate bound \(|S|\le M\) whenever full rank holds, this brackets the size of a possible SAME\_REALIZATION falsification certificate. A one-slice full-rank search is impossible whenever \(d_2(c)<M\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: common-kernel formulation.}
Let \(K_v=\ker B_v\subseteq\mathbb F_2^M\). Then
\[
K_2(H)=\bigcap_{v\ {\rm legal}}K_v,\qquad
\rho(H)=M-\dim K_2(H),
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{codim}K_v=\operatorname{rank}B_v\le d_2(c).
\]
Thus the exact global question is whether legal RMV/PCPP states place enough independent codimension-\(\le d_2(c)\) constraints to kill the entire quadratic relation space. Generic locality, list decodability, source length, and field degree do not answer this intersection question.
