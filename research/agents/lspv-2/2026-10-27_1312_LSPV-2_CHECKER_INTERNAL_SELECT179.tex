Fix the exact total \emph{witness verifier} $V_{q,m}(x,z)$ of report 166,
with $q\ge3$ assignment names, $m\ge1$ fixed-width ternary input records,
$w=\lceil\log_2(q+1)\rceil$, $N=m(1+3(w+1))$ source-bit inputs,
$K=2^{\lceil\log_2(q+3m)\rceil}$ record lanes and
$J=\log_2 K(1+\log_2 K)/2$ bitonic layers.
The AND/OR gate count $G$ is the size of the \emph{Boolean witness verifier},
not the count of its conceptual record wires or any SAT decision circuit.
The forward sort has $13w+32$ gates per comparator, the scan has
$19+(K-1)(4w+18)$ gates, and the inverse layer has $3KJ$ gates. Thus the
checker block begins at global gate offset
\[
 B_0={KJ\over2}(13w+32)+19+(K-1)(4w+18)+3KJ.
\]
The $c$th clause checker has size
\[
 Q=3[(w-1)+7w+1+3]+3[3w+(w-1)]+5+2+2
   =36w+15,
\]
and begins at $B_c=B_0+cQ$. The final balanced conjunction of the $m$
row outputs adds precisely $m-1$ gates. Therefore
\[
 G=B_0+mQ+(m-1),\qquad G_{\mathrm{end}}=B_0+mQ.
\tag{1}
\]
These offsets are computed arithmetically and need no netlist construction.

\section{A uniform streaming reduction-tree locator}
For a full binary-fan-in level-by-level reduce with $n$ initial leaves,
let $n_0=n$, $n_{j+1}=\lceil n_j/2\rceil$, and
$P_j=\sum_{v=0}^{j-2}\lfloor n_v/2\rfloor$ for $j\ge1$.
The $r$th gate produced at level $j$ has physical offset $P_j+r$.
To determine its two operands, inspect positions $2r,2r+1$ of level
$j-1$. Repeatedly follow any unpaired odd tail toward earlier levels;
otherwise the operand is the exact previous-level gate index.
At most $O(\log n)$ levels are traversed, keeping one index, count,
level and base address live. No recursion stack, entire tree or
occurrence table is stored. The inverse of any intermediate tree node
is the first higher level at which the node has a sibling; it then has
one direct parent, or none if already the root.

\begin{lemma}[Checker GateAt; \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}]
Given $(q,m,g)$ with $B_0\le g<G$, the fixed program
\texttt{checker\_gateat} returns the exact gate opcode and both
\emph{signed} source-wire operands of original verifier gate $g$.
It uses $O(\log G)$ total combined writable/index bits and
$O(\log^5G)$ conservative elementary bit operations per call.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $g<G_{\mathrm{end}}$, divide $g-B_0$ by $Q$ to locate a unique
clause checker and offset. Its three local request subblocks have
$8w+3$ gates each: $w-1$ balanced OR gates testing a nonzero name,
$7w$ less-than gates comparing the identifier with the binary constant
$q$, one conjunction, and a three-gate XOR comparing source sign and
the exact reverse-MUX lookup. Its three distinct-ID tests have $4w-1$
gates each: three XOR gates per coordinate followed by a $w-1$-gate
balanced AND reduction. The final nine gates are a six-input AND
reduction, a three-input OR reduction, an AND, and an OR with the
negated physical active-bit primary input. All symbols follow the
fixed emission sequence. At any gate location, operand IDs are
computed from the relevant source field ID, reverse-layer
$(\mathrm{stride},\mathrm{rank},\mathrm{side})$, preceding seven-gate
less/equality registers, or the stated reduction-tree formula.
Signed constants $+c_0,-c_0,+c_1,-c_1$ are not simplified away.
For $g\ge G_{\mathrm{end}}$, the same tree locator gives the two
balanced row-conjunction operands. Each case retains only a constant
number of $O(\log G)$-bit integers. Schoolbook integer arithmetic and
bounded $O(\log G)$-level scans give the stated conservative bit bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Checker inverse Select/Rank, \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}]
Every checker gate output has at most two signed Boolean direct uses.
A parameter-only algorithm returns the exact ordered list of triples
$(\text{consumer gate},\text{operand slot},\text{polarity})$, its degree,
$\Select(g,r)$, and $\Rank(g,h,k)$ in
$O(\log^6G)$ elementary bit operations and $O(\log G)$
combined writable/index bits. No source-incidence cache or gate array
is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a gate in a clause checker, query GateAt on all $Q=O(\log G)$
gates in that same block, compare both signed operand IDs, and retain
at most two matching triples. No other clause checker uses this
block's outputs. Only its terminal row gate may enter the final
conjunction tree; its unique parent is obtained by the reduction-tree
locator, including odd-tail carry. A gate in the final tree has at
most one parent computed by the same locator; the terminal Boolean
root has zero direct uses. The seven-gate comparison chain and
three-gate XOR chains fan out at most twice, while each balanced-tree
or local aggregate result feeds at most one higher operation;
thus the two-use cap is structural, not an inference from finite
samples. Rank and Select sort the constant number of matches.
Each GateAt request is polylogarithmic and $Q=O(\log G)$, proving
the conservative bound and constant-register working-space claim.
\end{proof}

\section{The required last-output pin correction}
Each physical AND/OR gate is expanded by the exact original 13-clause
proper 3CNF compiler. Its output variable $v_g$ occurs in five
\emph{own-module} rows. Each signed direct Boolean use at a downstream
gate contributes exactly four signed clone/equality rows, two of
each sign, regardless of the operand polarity. Normally
$\#\mathrm{Occ}(v_g)=5+4f(g)$.
\begin{counterexample}[Terminal pin is not a gate consumer]
Let $g_*=G-1$ be the final Boolean verifier output. It is constrained
TRUE by the original source compiler's 
\emph{four additional pinned source clauses}
$13G+4N+8,\ldots,13G+4N+11$, with $v_{g_*}$ as the positive literal
in field zero. These are not Boolean DAG consumers. Thus even when
$f(g_*)=0$ its physical incidence is $5+4=9$, not $5$.
The smallest tested instance $q=3,m=1$ has
$G=1824$, $g_*=1823$, with exactly nine occurrences.
\end{counterexample}
\begin{theorem}[Complete physical checker-source occurrence Select/Rank]
For each $B_0\le g<G$, the exact source occurrence list is its own
five signed 13-row gate-module entries, four signed entries for each
computed direct Boolean consumer, and \emph{four positive entries in the
terminal pin module when $g=G-1$}. Formally,
\[
 \boxed{\#\mathrm{Occ}(v_g)=5+4f(g)+4[g=G-1]},\quad
 f(g)\in\{0,1,2\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The ordered list, source clause index, literal field, polarity,
$\mathsf{OccRank}$/$\mathsf{OccSelect}$ and the bit index in the
fixed-width emitted ordinary 3CNF are uniform, table-free,
$O(\log^6 G)$ bit time and $O(\log G)$ index/work bits per request.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original 13-row templates contain the output literal at
row 8 field 2 once and in rows 9 through 12 field 1 four times:
AND uses $(+,-,-,-,-)$ and OR $(-,+,+,+,+)$.
A signed Boolean use in operand slot $k$ of gate $h$ contributes
rows $13h+4k+r$, field 1, polarity $(\tau,\tau,-\tau,-\tau)$,
$r=0,1,2,3$. Every checker-output source variable is fresh and
appears nowhere else in other gate templates. The only extra
occurrences are the four final truth-pinning clauses; the source
compiler also pins both Boolean constants, but those use
\emph{different} physical variables. Source clauses are sorted by
physical ordinal and literal field. For fixed-width source record
identifier width $d=\lceil\log_2(n_0+1)\rceil$ and header bit width
$H$, the signed literal's position is $H+(3c+k)(d+1)$, where
$n_0=9G+3N+q+8$ and $m_0=13G+4N+12$.
Input pin values and literal IDs must still be obtained and charged
when evaluating arbitrary source bits. The finite list contains at
most 13 ordinary entries, or 9 for the terminal gate.
\end{proof}

\section{Finite controls, independent source audit, and native ledgers}
The complete Python 3 standard-library reproducer emits the \emph{full}
fixed-network original verifier, independently implements parameter-only
checker GateAt and inverse, independently emits genuine proper signed
13-row source clauses, reconstructs the four source-pin entries, and
compares every source-occurrence triple with a separately built
oracle table \emph{used only for testing}. Eight $(q,m)$
configurations yield 2,724 checker gate records, 2,797 direct uses,
24,840 exact physical occurrences, plus all 8,192 source/witness
semantic combinations for $q=3,m=1$. The original W1 switch
is independently exhaustively evaluated in 131,072 assignment-mode
pairs, giving model counts $772,272,272,0$ and verifying Alice-only
masks $616,4742$. Its two off-diagonal SAT fibers have empty
intersection, so a witness mask independent of \emph{both} input bits
cannot satisfy every SAT mode; this last property has only the
finite-gadget scope. No random seed or SAT oracle is used.

\textbf{NATIVE RESOURCE LEDGERS:}
The Boolean gate count $G$ and every conceptual comparator record
\emph{wire} are counted separately. The proper-source gate compiler
writes $m_0=13G+4N+12$ clauses and $n_0=9G+3N+q+8$ variable IDs.
Writing complete gate records costs at least their physical bit length,
and materializing them occupies writable memory. Virtual bitwise guest
reads each call their actual ranked source-bit locator; repeated reads,
source parsing, output generation, addressing and any failed queries
are not free. With the fixed two-level signed exact-$(2,2)$ Q15/J
regularizer $m_2=4048m_0+5888n_0$, physical output length expands,
not contracts~\cite{floor}. This is the \emph{signed} exact-2P2N
compiler and must not be identified with clause-monotone exact-2P2N.

On $M_1$ one shared read/write tape counts the $N$ input bits in
footprint $P_1$ and all physical head steps/crossings, with every
output bit charged. On fixed-$k$ $M_k$ with one sequential read-only
input tape, visited work cells $S_k$, input probes, work-head
crossings and output bits are separate subcounters, but probes and
crossings are already part of $T_k$ and must not be added twice.
The constructed conservative simulation gives
\[
 T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))),\qquad
 P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)),
\]
\emph{only as an upper simulation}. Hennie--Stearns' fast result is
for TWO tapes. Indexed-RAM and FLMV count explicit binary-address
formation and cannot inherit sequential-head lower surfaces
without a charged model reduction~\cite{flmv,hs}.
