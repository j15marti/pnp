\section{Source-named optimum}
Consider a certified valid closed OPEN/CLAUSE/CLOSE event stream of $L$ events, source-live sets $S_0,\dots,S_L$, with $S_0=S_L=\varnothing$, and widths $w_i=|S_i|$, $W=\max_i w_i$. Its variables and clause signs are retained with original names. Fix $b\ge2$, $d=\lfloor b/2\rfloor$. A legal partition $P=(0=i_0<\cdots<i_q=L)$ has $1\le i_r-i_{r-1}\le b$, and every \emph{nonfinal} edge has length at least $d$. The final edge may be shorter. Define
\[
 C(P)=\sum_{r=1}^q 2^{w_{i_{r-1}}+w_{i_r}},\qquad C_*=\min_{P\text{ legal}}C(P).
\]
$C(P)$ is an upper proxy for exhaustive entry/exit boundary pair testing by one particular relational compiler, \emph{not} unavoidable calls or a lower bound on arbitrary SAT decision. Let $D_0=0$, $D_i=+\infty$ if unreachable. The last-edge recurrence is
\[
 D_j=\min_{i\in[\max(0,j-b),j-d]}(D_i+2^{w_i+w_j})\quad(0<j<L),
\]
\[
 D_L=\min_{\max(0,L-b)\le i<L}(D_i+2^{w_i+w_L}).
\]
The empty-range minimum is infinity.

\begin{theorem}[Exact sliding-width-bucket optimizer, restricted model]
For every certified valid stream with accessible width trajectory, $C_*=D_L$ can be computed in $O(L(W+1)+b)$ integer additions, comparisons and amortized deque operations with $O(b+W+1)$ live numerical/index entries if only the value is required. A complete optimal partition can be emitted by retaining $O(L)$ predecessor entries, with the same arithmetic work. Every cost fits in $B=\lceil\log_2(L+1)\rceil+2W+2$ bits. The value-only controller admits the conservative elementary-bit bound
\[
 O((L(W+1)+b)\,B\,\operatorname{polylog}(L+2))
\]
for its arithmetic/control, and
$O((b+W+1)(B+\log(L+2)))$ writable bits. These bounds exclude separately charged stream certification, original variable identity validation, child compilation, and solving.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a successor cut $j$, every eligible predecessor $i$ of a fixed width $w_i=k$ pays the identical edge increment $2^{k+w_j}$. Only the minimum $D_i$ among eligible predecessors of width $k$ matters. The eligible indices form a sliding window $[j-b,j-d]$. Insert index $i$ when $j=i+d$, expire it when $j>i+b$. Maintain a monotone increasing-cost deque per width: on insertion discard later-redundant entries with cost at least the new cost, then discard expired front entries. A discarded higher-cost predecessor cannot become superior to the lower-cost later predecessor, since equal widths yield equal increments and the later index expires no sooner. The deque front is an optimal candidate at that width. Scanning the $W+1$ fronts and choosing the minimum computes $D_j$ for $j<L$ exactly. Handle $j=L$ by scanning its last $b$ eligible predecessors to respect the terminal exception. Each index is inserted/deleted at most once; at most $b$ active indices reside in the deques. A ring of $b+1$ costs stores the delayed insertion values and the terminal window. Last-edge induction gives $D_L=C_*$. Every edge is at most $2^{2W}$ and there are at most $L$ edges, giving the displayed integer bit length. Arithmetic and addressing costs yield the conservative stated bit-resource bounds.
\end{proof}
