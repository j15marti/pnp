\section{BUILD I: independent static connected-interface referee}
Consider a connected incidence graph of proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF, $n$ variables, $m=4n/3$ clauses, and a supplied boundary $Y$ of $b>0$ variables. The private variables are partitioned into $k$ nonempty modules $Z_i$; each clause contains variables from no more than one $Z_i$. For each module a supplied \emph{syntactic} hitting set $B_i\subseteq Z_i$ of size at most $q$ hits every private clause not already hit by $Y$. These are the exact assumptions of the source audit: no semantic simplification, moving boundary, free discovery, or input-dependent regrouping.
\begin{theorem}[Static-interface capacity bound; previously established, independently checked]
Under the preceding hypotheses,
\[
 k\le4b,\qquad n\le3\Bigl(b+\sum_{i=1}^k|B_i|\Bigr)\le3b+12bq,
 \qquad b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-\tfrac14.
\]
For $b=0$, connectedness gives $k=1$ and $q\ge n/3$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every nonempty private module touches the boundary in a distinct clause: otherwise the full incidence graph would disconnect; clauses shared by private modules are forbidden. Each boundary variable has exactly four clause incidences, so at most $4b$ boundary-touching clauses exist and $k\le4b$. The union $H=Y\cup\bigcup_i B_i$ meets every clause. Since each of its variables can hit at most four clauses, $m\le4|H|$. Together with $m=4n/3$ this gives $n\le3(b+\sum|B_i|)\le3b+12bq$. Writing $s=b+q$, maximize $b(1+4q)=b(1+4s-4b)\le(s+1/4)^2$. Since $n/3\le b(1+4q)$, $s\ge\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$. If $b=0$ all variables must be in one connected module and $n\le3q$.
\end{proof}
Hence, on unpadded canonical $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ encodings, there is no unbounded connected exact22 family with both $b,q=O(\log N)$ under this static grammar. This is a \emph{coverage} obstruction and says nothing about unrestricted SAT decision time. Source-local ternary hitting sets can be discovered in $O(3^q\operatorname{poly}(N))$ if the boundary and partition are already provided; the search for the boundary is not free.

\section{BUILD II: a paid sequential-input streaming frontier}
For $n=3r\ge3$ let the canonical clause order consist of $n$ consecutive signed scopes $\{x_i,x_{i+1},x_{i+2}\}$ (indices modulo $n$), followed by $r$ block scopes $\{x_{3j},x_{3j+1},x_{3j+2}\}$. Every clause has three distinct names. All literal signs may vary subject to two positive and two negative occurrences per variable. A source is encoded in ordinary decimal literal-list form, beginning with the exact count $n$, with all identifiers, signs, separators and all clause-end markers \emph{physical}. Let its bit length be $N=\Theta(n\log(n+2))$. A source not matching the grammar is rejected, after charged parsing.
\begin{lemma}[Bounded frontier; inherited structural argument]
Processing variables $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}$ in order, and retaining precisely prior variables that occur in any clause not yet fully processed, yields at most four frontier names at every stage and at most 16 feasible tuples. For each variable stage at most four clauses have their final variable there; at most $32n$ tuple-and-bit transition candidates are attempted in the entire run.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every non-wrapping cyclic triple uses only its final two processed names. The two wrapping cyclic triples additionally use $x_0,x_1$. The disjoint block triples add no further live name. A just-closing clause has largest numeric variable index equal to the current index, so for every $j$ there are at most the three cyclic clauses closing at $n-1$ plus one block clause. A frontier mask records all feasible assignments to at most four live bits. Appending a new bit and checking every just-closed clause, then existentially discarding no-longer-live names, preserves the invariant that a tuple has a satisfying extension for the already closed clauses. Each mask has at most 16 bits set, hence two continuations each yield at most 32 candidates per stage.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Native bounded-workspace implementation on a fixed two-tape model]
There exists one uniform deterministic machine with one two-way \emph{sequential read-only} input tape and one read/write work tape which, without advice or precomputed occurrence maps, decides every encoded cyclic-scope strict signed exact22 input above, including rejection of malformed inputs, in
\[
 T_2(N)=O(nN\operatorname{polylog}N),\qquad S_2(N)=O(\log N),
 \qquad H_{\mathrm{input}}=O(nN),\quad R_{\mathrm{input}}=O(n).
\]
For valid source encodings, $Q_2,D_2=O(nN\operatorname{polylog}N)$ and the decision output uses one bit. A physically materialized fixed YES/NO base formula uses $O(1)$ additional output symbols, so this also gives a \emph{restricted} one-child shrinker with base cases and closure. It is not an all-SAT shrinker.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First count $N$ with one charged sweep and a logarithmic binary counter, cap each parsed numeric field at $N$, and parse the initial variable count. Scan the entire source once, checking the exact expected triple of absolute identifiers and clause order using an $O(\log N)$ clause-index counter. In order to authenticate the two-per-sign quota without maintaining a length-$n$ count table, for each $v=1,\dots,n$ make one paid full source scan and count its positive and negative occurrences using two constant-size counters. A complete sweep of $N$ physical symbols and its physical rewind each cost $O(N)$ input-head moves. These checks take $O(nN\operatorname{polylog}N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ work cells.

For each variable index $j$, sweep the source again and retain only clauses whose largest absolute variable index is $j$. By the lemma there are at most four, so their literal identifiers and signs occupy $O(\log N)$ writable cells. Derive the next live names from the authenticated public scope grammar and $j$ with constant many $O(\log N)$ counters. Process all up to sixteen previous tuples and their two candidate bits, evaluate the four closing clauses, update a sixteen-bit frontier relation and discard previous live names; no stored occurrence index, source-clause vector or exponential truth table is needed. Rewinding and scanning at most $O(n)$ times costs $O(nN)$ \emph{physical input-head moves}. A fixed finite collection of work-tape counters, indices and masks adds at most a polylogarithmic factor in native work-head actions and bit updates. At the end the relation on the empty frontier is present exactly when the formula is satisfiable. All unsuccessful assignments and all grammar/degree checks have been included. Output a single decision bit or, on the supported restricted class only, a fixed proper-3CNF YES or NO base instance. Bounds are worst-case and apply regardless of the SAT/UNSAT label.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Native machine firewall.} On the ordinary one-read/write-tape model with the source occupying the tape, the conservative standard full-tape-rescan simulation gives
\[
 T_1=O\bigl(T_2(N+S_2+\log(T_2+2))\bigr)
 =O(nN^2\operatorname{polylog}N),\quad
 P_1=O(N+S_2+\log(T_2+2))=O(N).
\]
The initial input is counted in $P_1$; virtual input jumps are not free. On a fixed universal interpreter, compile the \emph{same} finite program including the grammar checks and input movement; its interpreter-dependent polynomial time/space overhead is charged rather than identified with the original two-tape exponent. The indexed random-access model has a distinct cost convention and does not inherit the sequential-input crossing bound. This restricted easy language lies outside an all-ordinary-SAT lower-bound conclusion, regardless of its work-space exponent.

\section{Two hypothetical universes and exact missing arrow}
\begin{proposition}[Universe firewall; inherited]
The restricted cyclic-scope solver exists unconditionally, whether $P=NP$ or $P\ne NP$. Likewise the static counting obstruction and the fixed two-sign counterexample hold in either universe. None implies the absence or existence of a polynomial-time exact decision algorithm for arbitrary SAT.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
All three results are proved for explicit syntactic classes or fixed finite sources, using no complexity assumption. The full SAT language has no established polynomially discoverable collection of such bounded-width source frontiers, and cannot be assumed to have one. Conversely, under $P=NP$, choosing and verifying source summaries may be polynomial via a general decider but may use an unknown exponent and arbitrary workspace. Under $P\ne NP$, the existence of this special-case logspace decider does not contradict NP-completeness, because the special restricted class is decidable independently.
\end{proof}
The previously established one-side timeout theorem and recursively closed one-child contraction equivalence remain logical constraints, not new results here. A genuine improvement to the constructive spine requires \emph{uniformly discovered} hierarchical/moving summaries for \emph{every} ordinary SAT input with one physically shorter decision-equivalent child, paid re-encoding, and a polynomially valued strictly descending rank. The separation spine requires an \emph{unconditional} all-program same-machine resource obstruction or a transformation of a hypothetical polynomial SAT decider into a program in a forbidden native simultaneous time-space region. Neither arrow has been established. The independent source audit's $b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$ is not a runtime lower bound; a scalar memory bit or variable count is not a compulsory resource for every SAT strategy.
