\section{Exact exhaustive-volume theorem, with terminal convention}
Let \(F\in\CM\), let \(P,Q\) denote its sets of unsigned positive/negative clause supports, \(H=P\cap Q\), \(P'=P\setminus Q\), and \(q=|P'|=|Q\setminus P|\). For every \(C\in P'\), choose exactly one of its three \emph{source occurrences} as a TRUE witness. A choice vector \(\sigma\in\{1,2,3\}^{q}\) pins the distinct source variables \(S_\sigma\) occurring in its chosen positions to TRUE. Write \(d_\sigma=|S_\sigma|\le q\).

\emph{The declared protocol here is deliberately not the practical factor algorithm}: it launches \emph{every} one of the \(3^q\) possible witness choices, does not prune failed or previously decided branches, and at each branch independently traverses all \(2^{k-d_\sigma}\) raw assignments to still-unpinned source names, without SAT-based early exits, caching or cross-branch deduplication. The terminal convention is a raw complete assignment (not a solver proof or a factor solution). Under this explicitly chosen protocol, define
\[
\Psi_{\rm raw}(F)=\sum_{\sigma\in\{1,2,3\}^q}2^{k-d_\sigma},\qquad
R_{\rm search}=k-\log_2\Psi_{\rm raw},\qquad
R_{\rm raw}(\sigma)=d_\sigma.
\]
These count hypothetical independent enumeration leaves, not mandatory Turing-machine operations. If the actual adaptive solver chooses another continuation, the definition must be re-declared for the work actually performed, not silently treated as a lower bound.

\begin{theorem}[Unpruned one-sided witness-volume lower bound and equality case]
For every \(F\in\CM\) and \(q\ge1\),
\[
\boxed{\quad
\Psi_{\rm raw}(F)\ge3^q2^{k-q}=2^k(3/2)^q,
\qquad R_{\rm search}\le-q\log_2(3/2)<0.
\quad}
\]
Equality holds if and only if all the positive unmatched triple supports in \(P'\) are pairwise disjoint. Overlapping supports give strict inequality. This theorem is about the declared unpruned procedure, not arbitrary SAT decision.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every choice \(\sigma\) selects \(q\) occurrences but at most \(q\) distinct variables, so \(d_\sigma\le q\). Each of the \(3^q\) leaf families therefore has at least \(2^{k-q}\) raw completions. Summing yields the first inequality, and taking logarithms yields the stated negative \(R_{\rm search}\). If the supports are disjoint, every choice selects exactly \(q\) distinct variables, proving equality. Conversely, if two supports meet at a variable \(x\), a choice selecting \(x\) from both has \(d_\sigma<q\). At least this one contribution strictly exceeds \(2^{k-q}\), proving strict inequality. \(\square\)
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exact multiplicity identity and non-equivalence to SAT complexity]
For an original Boolean assignment \(a\in\{0,1\}^k\), let \(t_C(a)\) be the number of TRUE variables of the unmatched positive support \(C\). Then
\[
\boxed{\qquad\Psi_{\rm raw}(F)=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}^k}\ \prod_{C\in P'}t_C(a).\qquad}
\]
The union of raw completions of all witness choices is exactly the set of original assignments satisfying every clause in \(P'\), with each such assignment appearing with multiplicity \(\prod_Ct_C(a)\). The negative clauses, the paired core and all other constraints can discard assignments afterward.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Count the same pairs \((\sigma,a)\) in two ways, where \(a\) extends every TRUE source pin selected by \(\sigma\). For each \(\sigma\), there are \(2^{k-d_\sigma}\) extensions. For fixed \(a\), each clause \(C\) offers exactly \(t_C(a)\) choices of a TRUE selected position, independently of the choices of other clauses, yielding the product. This equality concerns incidence multiplicities in the enumerator, not the number of distinct SAT witnesses or the cost of a different decision procedure. \(\square\)
\end{proof}

\section{Two real recursive levels, child typing, and encoded sizes}
Take the two disjoint first-cell unmatched positive supports \(P'_{0,1}\) and \(P'_{0,2}\). To preserve the disjunction exactly, level~1 creates three ordinary CNF children \(F_h[x=1]\), one for each \(x\in P'_{0,1}\). Every child is obtained by deleting clauses satisfied by \(x=1\) and deleting the false literal \(\neg x\) elsewhere. Level~2 creates three children per level-1 child, one for each \(y\in P'_{0,2}\), with \(y=1\). These are nine distinct \emph{invocation histories}, although syntactic duplicate residuals or a symbolic decision might permit reuse. As source-choice OR trees,
\[
\SAT(F_h)=\bigvee_{x\in P'_{0,1}}\SAT(F_h[x=1])
=\bigvee_{x\in P'_{0,1}}\bigvee_{y\in P'_{0,2}}\SAT(F_h[x=1,y=1]).
\]
Both equalities are exact, no independent incompatible child assignments are ever conjoined, and the original source names are preserved. This does not provide a uniform compiler back into \(\CS\): already after the first pin, binary clauses appear and the original strict signed-degree quotas fail. An ordinary-SAT solver may receive these CNFs, but a \(\CS\)-only contractor cannot be called without an additional proven, charged adapter.

For a transparent finite representation audit, reserve \(w=\lceil\log_2(k+m+2)\rceil\) bits for every source identifier and header integer, retain the original source namespace, use two \(w\)-bit header integers and one format bit, and serialize each clause as two arity bits followed by \(\ell\) signed \((w+1)\)-bit literals, \(\ell\in\{0,1,2,3\}\). \[
 N_0=2w+1+m[2+3(w+1)],\quad

After at most two fixed source pins, only \(O(1)\) original clauses can be deleted or shortened because each pinned source name appears in four original clauses. In fact the implementation shows at most four clauses deleted on the studied branches. Therefore every such ordinary child satisfies

\section{Checkpoint and primitive bit-resource ledger}
At any declared checkpoint of one fixed uniform adaptive algorithm \(A\), the exact accounting notation is
\[
 T_A(F)=C_A(F,R)+T_{\rm finish,A}(F,R),\qquad
 S_A(F)=\max_{\tau}\{\text{simultaneously live writable/index bits at }\tau\}.
\]
Here \(C_A\) includes all paid source acquisition, parsing, recognizing, strategy selection, failed candidate searches, changes in representation, child construction, cache checking, and already executed reductions. \(T_{\rm finish,A}\) is its \emph{actual}, not modeled, remaining work. The normalized counterfactual \(T_{\rm model}=C(N,R)+2^{k-R}\) and \(R_{\rm net}=k-\log_2T_{\rm model}\), for \(C>0\), obey the exact accounting inequalities
\[
\max\{\log_2C,k-R\}\le\log_2 T_{\rm model}
\le\max\{\log_2C,k-R\}+1,
\]
\[
\min\{R,k-\log_2 C\}-1\le R_{\rm net}
\le\min\{R,k-\log_2C\}.
\]
Neither identity is an all-decider lower bound. The one-sided factor continuation can use \(\operatorname{poly}(N)\) work in each branch, not the deliberately exhaustive \(2^{k-d_\sigma}\) completion. For the actual factor solver \(T_{\rm finish,A}\) must reflect those factor computations and any pruning; the negative \(R_{\rm search}\) of the \emph{other} protocol cannot be inserted as a runtime claim.

For a materialized two-level restriction-only implementation in the declared bit format, each newly emitted child requires at least its \(N_{\rm child}\) output-bit writes, including unchanged-value writes, plus explicit control/index work, and access to original source bits. A naive root reread per child is chargeable; a source-aware indexed accessor may avoid rereading every bit, but its address arithmetic, indexing and cache accesses must still be charged. A fresh zero-initialized writable output array incurs exactly as many \emph{data-bit flips} as one bits in the encoded child string, while every zero output bit still incurs a charged write. No assumption about the full random-access machine transition trace follows from that data-array thought experiment. A physically materialized writable child has a peak-space floor of its complete encoded length; retaining a parent child simultaneously raises the floor to their sum. Conversely, a virtual child could avoid that storage but then must pay to compute every accessed source bit. Parent read-only input is not charged as writable space; output is not a free reusable child-input buffer.

The resource vector to maintain for every actual implementation is
\[
 \bigl(W_{\rm primitive},R_{\rm src},R_{\rm work},W_{\rm work},
 \Delta_{\rm complete\ state},S_{\rm peak},BT,|\mathrm{program}|,|\mathrm{representation}|\bigr),
 \qquad BT=\sum_{\tau}\mathrm{live\ writable\ bits}(\tau).
\]
Our executable audit reports serialized bit lengths, minimal emitted-bit write counts, and output-data one-bit counts for a declared fresh zero array. It does \emph{not} instrument every Turing-machine primitive, unchanged register write, complete-state flip, or cumulative live-bit-time; these fields stay \textbf{unmeasured}, and no physical FLMV exponent is inferred. Child headers/bodies, pinned-source stack, gluing, reconstruction if actually requested, caches, live ancestors and buffers remain explicit outstanding implementation charges for any proposed favorable all-input successor.
