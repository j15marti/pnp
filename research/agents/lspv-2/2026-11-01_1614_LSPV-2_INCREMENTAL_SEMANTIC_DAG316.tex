\[E_x(U_x\setminus\{x\})=\exists x\bigwedge_{g\in B_x}g.\]
Every result is passed to the earliest later remaining variable in its scope. The resulting DAG retains source IDs, scopes and Boolean tables.
\begin{theorem}
Under any sequence of coherent SET/UNSET pin operations on the original variables, updating the unary leaf $p_x$ and recomputing exactly $E_x$ and its successive parent buckets gives the correct SAT/UNSAT decision at every step. No recovery of a satisfying assignment is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}The dependency graph and bucket scopes are fixed independently of unary factor values. By induction in elimination order, each cached table is the exact existential projection of its descendant source factors. A change to $p_x$ changes no dependency subtree outside $x$ and its ancestor path. Recompute that path in order, preserving every unchanged factor. The conjunction of all root constants equals the existential closure of the original source formula under its current coherent pins. Thus both labels are exact. A zero root is independently replayable exhaustive refutation evidence.\end{proof}
\section{Charged work and limitation}

Simultaneous live factor, unary, provenance, incidence and index storage is bounded conservatively by $O(m+n2^w+(m+n)w\log(n+1))$ bits. These are \emph{conditional word-model} bounds; native FLMV source/index/head/writable-peak, failed policy selection, proof/GE work, child/output/guest DELAY-COPY-RECOMP must be separately charged. $w$ may be linear. This is not an all-input polynomial algorithm.
