\paragraph{Priority and scope.} General CNF-SAT, correct on both SAT and UNSAT and malformed strings rejected. Fix the proof system \emph{before} all inputs. The user-approved target is \(W_R(F)\), the minimum generalized cost of a valid acyclic refutation DAG, with best strategy selected freely. Search cost is excluded from this lower-bound relaxation. An arbitrary decider's running time is not identified with the minimum proof cost without a separate efficient-simulation theorem. P versus NP is OPEN.
\paragraph{Exact calculus.} In \(R_2\), source-labelled original CNF clauses are free premises. Each binary resolution node with two previously available parents has unit cost, and shared nodes are paid once. Intermediate clauses may grow or be reused. In \(R_{2\oplus}\), a locally checked pair of binary clauses may be converted to one two-variable XOR row by a two-premise unit-cost rule, and two rows may be XOR-added at unit cost. Conservative fresh-auxiliary definitions are permitted at positive cost, but no inference has more than two incoming premises. No semantic oracle or input-tailored false axiom is permitted.
\paragraph{Family.} For \(h\ge3\), let \(U_h\) encode \(x_i\oplus x_{i+1}=0\) for \(i<h\), and \(x_h\oplus x_1=1\), each binary parity relation using exactly two original 2-CNF clauses. Thus \(U_h\) has \(h\) variables and \(m=2h\) original clauses. Its even-charge counterpart \(S_h\) replaces the final parity 1 by 0. The odd system is UNSAT since summing all parity constraints gives \(0=1\); the even system has exactly two models.
\begin{lemma}Every original clause in \(U_h\) is essential for UNSAT.\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Deleting either of the two clauses for one edge leaves the remaining \(h-1\) parity constraints intact and forces the opposite parity across that edge. The two globally complemented assignments satisfying the remaining equations falsify opposite clauses of the removed parity pair. Choosing the complement that falsifies the deleted clause satisfies all remaining source clauses. Thus every proper one-clause deletion is SAT.\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}For every \(h\ge3\), unrestricted DAG binary resolution has exact minimum
\[
\boxed{W_{R_2}(U_h)=2h-1}.
\]
The same value holds in any stated source-explicit unit-cost, at-most-binary-premise conservative extension admitting the exhibited XOR2 extraction and XOR addition rules, including charged definitional extension.\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Take the ancestry DAG of an arbitrary refutation. By source minimality, all \(a=2h\) original clauses must occur as ancestors: otherwise a model of the used source subset extends to all jointly conservative fresh definitions, contradicting soundness. If there are \(q\) charged inference/definition nodes, the underlying ancestor graph is connected, hence has at least \(a+q-1\) edges. Every charged node has at most two incoming premise edges, while source leaves have none, so the graph has at most \(2q\) edges. Therefore \(q\ge a-1=2h-1\), regardless of reuse, temporary growth, free choice of the winning derivation, or auxiliary definitions. For a matching refutation, eliminate \(x_2,\ldots,x_{h-1}\): each pair of binary XOR constraints composes into a two-clause constraint with exactly two resolution nodes. After \(h-2\) eliminations, the contradictory pair of XOR constraints on \((x_1,x_h)\) yields opposite unit clauses in two further nodes and the empty clause in one. The total is \(2(h-2)+3=2h-1\). An alternative XOR DAG uses \(h\) two-premise extractions and \(h-1\) binary row additions, also \(2h-1\) nodes.\end{proof}
\paragraph{Interpretation and falsifiers.} The statement is an infinite exact optimum within a declared system, not a novel unrestricted SAT lower bound. The source-leaf/fan-in capacity lemma is already recorded in LSPV-2 MINCOST-DAG-FANIN339. This construction proves its \emph{sharpness on an infinite nontrivial family}. Sharing, arbitrary intermediate growth and conservative auxiliary definitions cannot improve the minimum under the stated binary-premise accounting. Changing to a sound higher-premise rule set can alter \(W_R\), so the inference basis must remain explicit. Redundant source padding removes clause minimality and does not increase this proof minimum merely by increasing \(m\).
