## ITERATE 1: all-neighbor-legal exact-root lower bound
Use `p cnf 2 2\n1 0\n1 2 0\n`, i.e. SAT source $F_+=(x)\wedge(x\vee y)$, N=20 ASCII bytes. One paid AND3 infers the factor $S=x$. A second, paid unary LOCAL-CONVERT with one public root, no public internal gates, carries the explicit circuit

$$
C_b(x)=x\vee\neg\left(\bigwedge_{j=1}^k b_j\right),\qquad b_j\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Every one of the $2^k$ payloads is a LEGAL conclusion of $S$, with original support only $\{x\}$. For $b=1^k$, the root is $x$; for each $j$, the individually flipped neighbor $b_j=0$ has root constant TRUE. All are source-entailed; no semantic legality oracle is assumed.

**Lemma (PROVED/EXACT in U).** Any deterministic processor given this unannotated raw circuit that must faithfully return its *exact root Boolean function* has to inspect all k independent constant positions on the legal all-one input, although N=20, paid q=2. For two-sided root-function error $\epsilon<1/2$, expected number of probes at least $(1-2\epsilon)k$.

**Proof.** If a bit position is not inspected, the all-one circuit and its flipped neighbor have identical execution transcripts, but demand distinct output truth tables: $0011$ versus $1111$ in $(x,y)$ order $00,01,10,11$. Contradiction. For randomized processors couple identical random coins on the two inputs; output distributions at distance at least $1-2\epsilon$ require probing the changed position with that probability, then sum across positions. QED.

**Exactly what this does and does not falsify.** This is **not** a native-R lower bound until the original circuit-payload syntax is resolved; the original /341 wording does not serialize a gate budget. It is not a lower bound on the globally shortest proof, nor on SAT-machine time. It qualifies REFEREE349's proposed *raw-input faithful construction/checking* bound `poly(N+q)`: an explicit raw payload evidence-length E or trusted normalized-input promise is needed. In this chain E=Theta(k log(k+2)) under indexed text serialization, output O(1) root truth-table bits. **Existence** of a compact normal form remains fully compatible with REF349.
