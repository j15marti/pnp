Each original variable creates an edge between its two distinct occurrences of sign $s$. Thus each signed graph is a loopless cubic multigraph, with $n$ edges and all vertex degrees three counting parallel edges. This graph is computably extracted by source-index reads.

In the previously proved, restricted source-clause matching architecture from LSPV-2 326, put weight one or two on same-sign clause pairs sharing one or two original variables, and weight two on opposite-sign exact complementary three-variable clauses.
