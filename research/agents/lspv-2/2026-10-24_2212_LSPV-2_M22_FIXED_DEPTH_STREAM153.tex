\section{Uniform fixed-depth theorem}
A CNF is canonicalized by deleting tautologies, merging repeated literals, and retaining a unique sorted copy of each ordinary clause, including the empty clause. \begin{theorem}[Fixed-depth charged projection, restricted exact]
For every \emph{fixed} integer \(h\ge 1\), one uniform deterministic transducer valid on every dense-indexed \(F\in\CM\) selects \(h\) sequential independent-pivot batches (stopping early at terminal formulas), constructs descriptors for
\[
 F=F_0\stackrel{\exists I_0}{\longrightarrow}F_1
 \stackrel{\exists I_1}{\longrightarrow}\cdots
 \stackrel{\exists I_{h-1}}{\longrightarrow}F_h,
\]
and supports arbitrary \emph{charged} indexed output-bit access and complete write-only serialization. Every retained assignment \(z\) obeys
\[
 F_h(z)\ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \exists_{x\in \bigcup_{j<h}I_j}F(x,z).
\]
For each fixed \(h\), all descriptors, graph searches, virtual accesses, and output streaming use \(O_h(\log N)\) simultaneously writable/index bits and \(N^{O_h(1)}\) elementary bit work, where every replay and unsuccessful selector candidate is charged.  Every ordinary explicit \(F_j\) has \(O_h(N)\) bits.  No claim of a favorable all-input ordinary-SAT successor is made.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At stage \(j\), let \(d_j\) be a certified upper bound on the \emph{total} number of occurrences of each active variable and \(w_j\) a bound on clause width.  Its primal graph has maximum degree
\(D_j\le d_j(w_j-1)\).  Begin with \(d_0=4,w_0=3,D_0=8\).  The special source balance \(2^+,2^-\) gives \(d_1\le8,w_1\le4,D_1\le24\).  For every subsequent stage take the safe (not necessarily tight) recurrence
\[
 d_{j+1}=d_j^2,\qquad w_{j+1}=2(w_j-1),\qquad D_j=d_j(w_j-1).
\]
Indeed, independent pivots share no input clause.  Each source occurrence of a surviving variable is either retained once, or copied into at most \(d_j\) resolvents from its unique pivot-containing parent.  Thus its next total occurrence count is at most \(d_j^2\).  Opposite parents of a pivot each have at most \(w_j-1\) other literals, hence the next width is at most \(2(w_j-1)\).  At most \(d_j^2\) candidate resolvents are generated per pivot, giving
\[
 m_{j+1}\le m_j+k_jd_j^2=O_h(k),\quad
 k_{j+1}\le k_j-|I_j|.
\]
All constants depend on fixed \(h\), never on the source length.

For completeness, the independent-set selector is deterministic and logarithmic-space at each fixed \(D\). Rank active original identifiers densely from 1 to \(n\) by paid scans. Choose a prime \(p>\max\{n,(D+1)^3 n\}\), using logarithmic-space primality search by trial division, and enumerate \emph{all} coefficient tuples of degree-\(D\) polynomials over \(\mathbb F_p\).  For each coefficient tuple order every vertex by the pair (its polynomial hash, its dense identifier), and select vertices strictly smaller than each neighbor.  The selected set is independent.  A uniformly sampled degree-\(D\) polynomial has \((D+1)\)-wise independent hash values on these distinct vertices. In each closed neighborhood, a collision has probability at most \(\binom{D+1}{2}/p\); absent collisions, each rank is equally likely to be the local minimum. Consequently the expected selected count exceeds
\[
 \sum_v\frac1{\deg(v)+1}-\frac{nD}{2p}
 \ \ge\ \frac n{D+1}-\frac1{D+1}.
\]
Because the selected count is integral, some enumerated seed has at least \(\lceil n/(D+1)\rceil\) selected vertices. Count all tested seeds, modular operations, and graph probes. Since \(D\) is constant at fixed stage, the seed, counters, and neighbor indices occupy \(O_D(\log N)\) bits and the complete search is polynomial in \(N\).

Finally, \emph{all} ordinary clauses of \(F_j\) have width at most the constant \(w_j\) and use original names \(1,\ldots,k\).  Enumerate the polynomial-size universe of sorted signed clauses of width at most \(w_j\), including the empty clause.  The membership predicate for stage \(j+1\) tests whether a proposed clause (i) survives in \(F_j\) without a pivot, or (ii) is a canonical non-tautological resolvent of two opposite-parent \(F_j\) clauses at a selected pivot.  Membership in \(F_j\), whether a name is active, adjacency, seed evaluation, output rank, and header counts all reduce to nested polynomial loops invoking stage-\(j\) predicates.  For \emph{fixed} \(j\), this has polynomial work and \(O_j(\log N)\) writable space.  Generate the sorted output stream bit by bit; no writable \(O(N)\)-bit child tape is assumed.  Induction proves the claimed accessor and serialization contract.  Classical Davis--Putnam resolution proves the exact pointwise existential identity at every stage \cite{dp}.
\end{proof}
