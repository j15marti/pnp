\begin{theorem}[Exact rowwise essentiality and mixed refutation interval]
For every inconsistent system of $r\ge2$ equations $A_ix=b_i$ over GF(2), each with exactly three distinct occurring variables, assume deleting any whole equation makes the remaining system consistent. Let $T_i=\operatorname{supp}(A_i)$, and encode each row by its four width-three clauses blocking precisely the wrong-parity triples, obtaining $F$. Define
\[
d_i=3-\operatorname{rank}(A_{-i})+
\operatorname{rank}(A_{-i,\overline T_i}).
\]
Then $0\le d_i\le2$, exactly $2^{d_i}$ of row $i$'s four clauses are indispensable, and a sign flip at one literal occurrence makes the formula SAT if and only if its source clause is indispensable. In the same fixed combined calculus,
\[
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^{r}2^{d_i}-1\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le4r-1.}
\]
Moreover, $F$ is clause-minimally UNSAT if and only if all $d_i=2$, in which case the unrestricted shared mixed minimum is \emph{exactly}
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)=4r-1.}
\]
No search cutoff, pure-Resolution restriction, monotonicity assumption, or a cost for finding the best proof is hidden in the global lower bound.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\emph{Proof.}
For any $i$, $S_i=\{x:A_{-i}x=b_{-i}\}$ is a nonempty affine coset of $\ker A_{-i}$. Every $x\in S_i$ violates row $i$, since the full system is inconsistent. Its projection to $T_i$ is therefore inside the wrong-parity affine plane, of dimension two. Rank-nullity and the subspace of vectors zero on $T_i$ give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\dim \pi_{T_i}(\ker A_{-i})
&=\dim\ker A_{-i}-
  \dim\{v\in\ker A_{-i}:v|_{T_i}=0\}\\
&=(n-\operatorname{rank}A_{-i})
-((n-3)-\operatorname{rank}A_{-i,\overline T_i})
=d_i.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus precisely $2^{d_i}$ wrong-parity triples arise from satisfying assignments of the other rows. Deleting the blocker clause for one such triple produces a model if and only if that triple is reachable. Flipping any one sign of the blocker causes it to forbid an adjacent \emph{correct}-parity triple; the recovered model violating the original blocker satisfies the flipped one. Conversely any new model must violate the old blocker, so the same iff holds for sign-flip SAT.

Every sound contradiction DAG must use all $s=\sum_i 2^{d_i}$ indispensable original source clauses. Its pruned connected ancestry has $s+q$ vertices, at most $2q$ antecedent edges, and at least $s+q-1$ edges, so $q\ge s-1$, including shared derivations and charged auxiliaries. Independently, conjoin the four local source clauses for each row with three legal AND3 inferences; the local three-variable truth table is exactly its annotated GF(2) row. Sum the $r$ row equations by $r-1$ legal binary XOR inferences. In a minimally inconsistent affine row system the inconsistent dependency involves every row, so their sum is $0=1$. This authentic shared proof costs $3r+(r-1)=4r-1$. If every $d_i=2$, the bounds coincide. \hfill$\square$

\section*{Explicit six-equation falsifier}
The following six three-variable equations, with first RHS one and all other RHS zero, form a minimally inconsistent GF(2) row system:
\[
\begin{aligned}
x\oplus y\oplus z&=1,&x\oplus u\oplus v&=0,&y\oplus u\oplus v&=0,\\
a\oplus b\oplus c&=0,&a\oplus b\oplus d&=0,&c\oplus d\oplus z&=0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first coefficient row is the sum of the other five, which are independent, so each whole-row deletion is SAT. Nevertheless, under the other five equations, row one has only two reachable wrong triples, because $x=y,z=0$. Likewise row six has only two, because $c=d,z=1$ when it is omitted. The local projection dimensions are $(1,2,2,2,2,1)$, so precisely 20 of the 24 source clauses are indispensable and four are individually redundant. The rank theorem gives
\[
\boxed{19\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_{\rm six})\le23,}
\]
not an exact optimum. The opposite affine label gives 16 satisfying assignments. In particular, a minimally inconsistent row matroid circuit is not necessarily a minimally UNSAT CNF after expanding all local parity patterns.
