\section{Backward semantics and source accounting}
From TRUE at the AND of three-literal OR gates, each clause must be TRUE, so one true occurrence per clause may be chosen as a witness. No original clause is forced to contain exactly one TRUE literal. The same source variable must never be required in opposite signs. Such a selection exists iff the formula is SAT, but enumerating it is not mandatory. For strict proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF, $4n=3m$ occurrence vertices split into $n$ copies of $K_{2,2}$, with $4n$ complement edges; the clause blocks each have size three. \section{Lazy source-coherent join}
Let
\[
H=G(Y)\land\bigwedge_{j=1}^{J}H_j(Y_j,U_j)\subseteq F,\qquad
Y_j\subseteq Y,\quad U_i\cap U_j=\varnothing~(i\ne j),\quad U_j\cap Y=\varnothing.
\]
This is a \emph{source-validated} subformula of original parent $F$, with all common original identifiers in $Y$. An exact master starts with $K=G(Y)$, chooses $\alpha\models K$, and checks $H_j$ under $\alpha|_{Y_j}$. For a failed local check it saves a checked UNSAT proof and learns the clause
\[
D_{j,\alpha}=\bigvee_{y\in Y_j}\bigl(y\ne\alpha(y)\bigr).
\]
For successful local checks it caches a witness keyed by $(j,\alpha|_{Y_j})$. When all local checks are successful, witnesses glue only because they use the \emph{same} shared assignment $\alpha$. A candidate must satisfy the \emph{entire original} $F$ before the algorithm claims SAT. An exhausted or unrecognized state falls back to total exact search.

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT for the declared source-closed model]
Every learned clause is entailed by $H$ and hence by $F$. If $K$ becomes UNSAT, $F$ is UNSAT. If the assembled witness passes full parent replay, $F$ is SAT, even when some parent clauses were not in $H$. At most $2^{|Y|}$ negative refinements occur. With a positive local-result cache and permanent negative blocking, the number of \emph{distinct local SAT calls} is at most
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{J}2^{|Y_j|}.
\]
This is an algorithm-specific call bound, not an unrestricted machine-time bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$H_j\upharpoonright\alpha|_{Y_j}$ being UNSAT says every original model of $H_j$ differs on at least one fixed boundary bit, proving $D_{j,\alpha}$. The clause excludes the current root assignment. The private sources are disjoint, hence successful models agree globally when they share $\alpha$. Each positive local restriction is solved once and cached; each negative restriction is solved once and thereafter blocked by its permanent learned clause. Distinct restrictions number at most $\sum_j2^{|Y_j|}$. Since $H$ is a clause submultiset of $F$, UNSAT($H$) implies UNSAT($F$). Full parent replay establishes the SAT direction.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{RESOURCE LIMITATION.} The implemented master explicitly examines at most $2^{|Y|}$ boundary rows \emph{per refinement}; local solvers can cost exponential time, the cache may retain large witness bitsets, and proofs may be large. All are included in the resource model. No polynomial algorithm for general SAT, unrestricted interface discovery, or mandatory no-good cost is asserted.
