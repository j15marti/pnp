\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
For a finite explicit integer-affine system $P=\{x\in[0,1]^n:a_j\cdot x\ge b_j\}$, a \emph{source automorphism} is a supplied coordinate permutation that preserves the multiset of \emph{original} inequalities. An orbit-Farkas coverage DAG has a root representative $\rho=*^n$; each internal representative $\rho_v\in\{0,1,*\}^n$ branches on one unset coordinate, has both labeled edges $b=0,1$, and each edge supplies an original-source automorphism $g$ mapping the extended context $\rho_v\cup\{x_i=b\}$ to its child representative. Each terminal contains a nonnegative rational Farkas dual of original source rows, box axioms and its own fixes proving $0\ge\epsilon>0$. All edge permutations, row symmetries, dual identities, and acyclicity are checked explicitly.

\begin{theorem}[Sound, complete, polynomial-checkable orbit-Farkas format]
Every accepted DAG certifies Boolean UNSAT of its exact source. Every Boolean-UNSAT finite rational linear source has an accepted DAG, possibly exponentially large. An ordinary uniform deterministic multitape verifier checks a source of length $N$ and a completely materialized DAG/certificate of length $B$ in simultaneous time and space polynomial in $N+B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a Boolean source model existed, begin at the empty root. At every node take the outgoing edge corresponding to the model's queried bit and apply its checked source automorphism. The transported assignment still satisfies the original source and matches the next representative. Acyclicity gives a terminal, whose exact nonnegative Farkas combination rules out a real model, contradiction. Verification uses finite tuples, source-row sorting and exact rational arithmetic, polynomial in the explicitly materialized bit length. For completeness, expand the full binary assignment tree with identity edge transports. At a full falsifying assignment $\rho$, one violated source row $a\cdot x\ge b$ combines with each coordinate's fixed-value inequalities $-a_ix_i\ge-a_i\rho_i$ to give $0\ge b-a\cdot\rho>0$. This may require $2^n$ leaves. It is not uniformly polynomial in source size.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact tree gap and compact orbit quotient]
For $n=2k+1$, let $K_n$ be $2\sum_i x_i\ge n$ and $-2\sum_i x_i\ge-n$ over Boolean variables. Coordinate-only full binary branch trees closing leaves \emph{solely} with LP infeasibility need exactly $2\binom{2k+1}{k}=\Theta(2^n/\sqrt n)$ leaves in the optimum, regardless of adaptive variable order. But $K_n$ has an accepted symmetry-origin DAG of $(k+1)(k+3)$ states and $O(n^3\log n)$ explicit bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a partial assignment with $r$ ones and $z$ zeros, LP feasibility is exactly $r,z\le k$; unassigned coordinates can all be $(n/2-r)/(n-r-z)$. Thus a pure tree must branch until $r=k+1$ or $z=k+1$. Histories first hitting $k+1$ ones with $j$ preceding zeros number $\binom{k+j}{j}$, so their total is $\sum_{j=0}^k\binom{k+j}{j}=\binom{2k+1}{k}$; double for zeros. The next unassigned index cannot change count dynamics. For the DAG, use representatives with $r$ leading ones and $z$ subsequent zeros. Branch on the next index; identity transports a zero, while a coordinate cycle transports a one to the canonical one-block. Every transport preserves the symmetric source. Internal states are $(r,z)$ with $0\le r,z\le k$ and terminal states reach either count $k+1$. At a terminal with $r=k+1$, add the source upper inequality to two times each fixed-one lower bound and two times all remaining lower box bounds to get $0\ge2r-n\ge1$. The dual for $z=k+1$ uses the lower source row and appropriate upper bounds to get $0\ge2z-n\ge1$. These are exact original-source Farkas certificates.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A stronger calculus bypasses the tree.} Syntactic CP divides $2\sum x_i\ge2k+1$ by two to infer $\sum x_i\ge k+1$, and divides $-2\sum x_i\ge-(2k+1)$ to infer $-\sum x_i\ge-k$. Their sum gives $0\ge1$. This is three derived lines beyond the two axioms. Binary-semantic CP can infer $0\ge1$ from both original axioms in one step. The large LP tree is therefore \emph{not} a lower bound on ordinary CP proof size or machine running time.

\begin{theorem}[Two-variable source-origin forgery]
If the source-automorphism check is deleted, a satisfiable two-variable source has a three-node false UNSAT certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The source $x_1\ge1,\,-x_2\ge0$ has model $(1,0)$. Branch on $x_1$. Its zero child is correctly LP-infeasible at $x_1=0$. The one child is falsely sent by the swap $(12)$ to the contradictory representative $x_2=1$. Both leaf Farkas identities are valid, but the swap does not preserve the source. Source-automorphism checking rejects it. A nontrivial swap is impossible with one variable.
\end{proof}
