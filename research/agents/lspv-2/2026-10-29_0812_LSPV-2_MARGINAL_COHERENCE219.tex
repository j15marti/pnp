\section{Complete source construction}
For distinct $v,u,z$, define proper ternary clause gadgets
\[
 {\rm Pad}(l,r;w)=(l\vee r\vee w)\wedge(l\vee r\vee\neg w),\quad
 {\rm Pin0}(v;u,z)=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}(\neg v\vee\epsilon u\vee\delta z).
\]
After existential quantification of fresh auxiliaries, Pad is $l\vee r$ and Pin0 is $\neg v$. A module has data $(a,b,c)$ and a designated positive clause $C=(a\vee b\vee c)$, with puncture $a$ and remainder $d=b\vee c$. Without $C$, module $E$ comprises Pad$(\neg a,b;w_1)$, Pad$(a,\neg b;w_2)$ and Pin0$(c;u,z)$. Module $X$ replaces the first two pads by Pad$(a,b;w_1)$ and Pad$(\neg a,\neg b;w_2)$. Thus their exact puncture relations are respectively
\[
 P_E=\{(0,0),(1,1)\},\qquad P_X=\{(0,1),(1,0)\}.
\]
Each has both separate coordinate supports $\{0,1\}$, the same per-variable signed incidence, and the same unsigned clause scopes. Terminal module $B$ pins all three data names $(a_0,b_0,c_0)$ to zero with three independent Pin0 gadgets, yielding $P_B=\{(0,0)\}$. Choose ordered disjoint modules $B,A_1,\ldots,A_k$, each $A_i$ of type $E$ or $X$, and cyclically exchange their designated positive literal occurrences. In the resulting source the designated clauses are $(a_0\vee b_1\vee c_1)$, $(a_{i-1}\vee b_i\vee c_i)$ for $2\le i\le k$, and $(a_k\vee b_0\vee c_0)$. Denote all-X by $U_k$ and one-E-at-$j$ by $S_{k,j}$.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An $A$ module has three data names, two pad names, two pin names, four padded clauses and four pin clauses, plus one designated clause; hence seven variables and nine clauses. $B$ has nine variables and thirteen clauses. Every data name occurs four times in its own gadgets plus once in a designated clause, and auxiliaries at most four times. The cyclic clauses connect the modules, making the incidence graph connected. Changing $X$ to $E$ flips four occurrences of $a_j$ (two negative and two positive rearranged); every variable's total signed incidence and every unsigned clause scope stay unchanged.
In $X$ modules $c_i=0$, $b_i=1-a_i$, while in $E$ modules $c_i=0$, $b_i=a_i$. Module $B$ has all three data bits zero. The first exchanged clause forces $b_1=1$ and the terminal clause forces $a_k=1$. With every module $X$, $a_1=0$, and the intermediate clauses inductively force all $a_i=0$, contradiction. If module $j$ is $E$, take $a_i=0$ before $j$ and $a_i=1$ from $j$ onwards; set $b_j=1$, $b_i=1-a_i$ otherwise and all $c_i=0$. Every exchanged clause is satisfied and gadgets extend existentially. The binary pads' $a_j$ signs flip in four ternaries, each recorded by exactly one sign bit. This proves every assertion.
\end{proof}
