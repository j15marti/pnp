\begin{theorem}[Unrestricted-DAG, all-input cost equality]
For every finite valid UNSAT CNF $F$,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm macro}}(F)=W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F).}
\]
Thus the two preselected systems also have identical $\overline W_R(N)$ for every $N$ under one fixed source encoding.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take any finite $R_{\rm macro}$ refutation, with arbitrarily shared nodes, and process its derivation DAG in topological order. Replace each $k$-premise macro by the $k-1$ exact binary AND3 steps that successively conjoin those SAME premises. Every intermediate depends on at most the original three variables in $S$, so AND3 is legal. The last intermediate is exactly the macro conclusion, and its equivalent affine annotation is locally checkable without another inference. Redirect every consumer of the old macro node to the last node, preserving sharing and acyclicity. The new unit-cost nodes total exactly the old macro weight $k-1$, and every other operation (including DEF) is unchanged. Therefore $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F)\le W_{R_{\rm macro}}(F)$. The base calculus is a same-weight subsystem of the extension, proving the reverse inequality. The worst-case identity follows by maximizing equal pointwise costs on the same set.
\end{proof}

\section{Small signed bit pigeonhole benchmark}
For $h=2^\ell$, $\ell\ge1$, use $h+1$ pigeons each represented by $\ell$ original bit variables $x_{i,b}$. For every pair $i<j$ and code $a\in\{0,1\}^{\ell}$, write the original source clause
\[
C_{i,j,a}=\bigvee_{b=0}^{\ell-1}\left(x_{i,b}^{1-a_b}\lor x_{j,b}^{1-a_b}\right),
\quad x^1=x,\quad x^0=\neg x.
\]
It forbids precisely the collision in which both pigeons occupy hole $a$. There are $v=(h+1)\ell$ original variables, $m=h\binom{h+1}{2}$ original clauses, and $2\ell m$ independent signed occurrences. The exact clause-pair/hole labels and polarities are retained. The actual text encoding $N$ includes all punctuation and indices.
\begin{theorem}[Every source and occurrence sign is sensitive]
For every $h=2^\ell\ge2$, the CNF $B_h$ is clause-minimally UNSAT. Removing any single $C_{i,j,a}$ yields exactly $(h-1)!$ models; flipping any single signed-literal occurrence within that clause also yields exactly $(h-1)!$ models. Consequently
\[
\boxed{\mu(B_h)=m,\quad W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(B_h)\ge m-1.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every total assignment maps $h+1$ pigeons to $h$ holes and therefore violates at least one collision clause. If $C_{i,j,a}$ is the sole removed clause, the only permitted collision is $i,j$ together in hole $a$, and the remaining $h-1$ pigeons must bijectively fill the other $h-1$ holes, giving $(h-1)!$ assignments. A model after one literal sign flip must also violate the old clause (because the old formula was UNSAT); on all deletion models that inverted literal is true, so the same and only the same $(h-1)!$ assignments survive. Thus all $m$ sources are essential. In any sound conservative binary-premise contradiction DAG with $q$ paid nodes, the undirected source-ancestor graph of all $m$ indispensable leaves has at least $m+q-1$ edges, while its indegree sum is at most $2q$, giving $q\ge m-1$.
\end{proof}
