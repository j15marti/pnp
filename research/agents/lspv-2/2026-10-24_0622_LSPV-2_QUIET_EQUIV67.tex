\section{Target and three falsifiable mechanisms}
The common five-role question is whether an arbitrary exact SAT decider must construct a rich continuation relation or pay a similarly large alternative cost. No such universal dichotomy is assumed. The present mechanism tests a state-adaptive, source-coherent policy that changes its representation from mixed CNF to binary implication classes to a reduced binary CNF, even when no literal is initially forced.

\noindent\textbf{M1, tested and PROVED/EXACT restricted:} a complete, paid SCC computation on a satisfiable binary subformula may collapse linearly many genuine ternary clauses to binary/unit clauses without any initial unit or implied source literal.

\noindent\textbf{M2, tested and FALSIFIED/RETIRED as a universal inference:} a linear minimum \emph{raw syntactic} strong 2-CNF backdoor $h$ necessarily causes $2^{\Omega(h)}$ work for all exact deciders. The matched both-label family below has $h=\Theta(n)$ and a linear-time exact uniform hybrid.

\noindent\textbf{M3, restricted comparator:} a specifically declared policy that fully enumerates every strong-backdoor assignment before calling SCC pays $2^h$ child calls on both labels. This is an exact cost of that policy only. Dynamic DPLL, propagation, direct source inspection, and arbitrary alternative symbolic methods are not covered.

\section{An exact paid SCC-equivalence rule}
Let $F=B\wedge T$, where $B$ is 2-CNF over original source names and $T$ consists of proper ternary clauses. First verify and index the source, then run SCC on the implication graph of $B$. If $B$ is UNSAT, reject with that sound certificate. If $B$ is SAT, assign each signed literal $\ell$ a representative $\rho(\ell)$ from the same implication SCC. Transform each ternary by replacing its literals with their representatives, eliminating duplicate signed literals, and dropping a clause only if syntactically complementary representatives occur. Call the result $T'$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact source-relative equivalence]\label{lem:scc}
For every original-source assignment satisfying $B$,
\[
T(\alpha)=T'(\alpha).
\]
Consequently $F$ and $B\wedge T'$ have \emph{exactly the same satisfying source assignments}. If every clause of $T'$ has width at most two, SCC decides $F$ and reconstructs a source witness in $O(n+m)$ addressed-word operations after the paid source read, with $O(n+m)$ live logical words.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Mutually reachable literals in the implication graph of a satisfiable 2-CNF have identical truth values under every model of that 2-CNF. Replacing a literal by a representative thus preserves each clause's value under $B$. Deduplication preserves OR; syntactically complementary representatives form an always-TRUE disjunction. Conjoin with $B$ and apply the standard linear SCC algorithm. The returned assignment satisfies the original formula; replay every original signed clause before acceptance. Building and scanning all implication edges, SCCs, representatives and clauses is linear. A rejected recognition invokes a separate exact DPLL fallback; no all-input polynomial claim is made.
\end{proof}

\section{Connected scalable SAT and UNSAT controls without binary units}
Fix $q\ge2$, $n=3q$, and original source variables $x_1,\ldots,x_{3q}$. Define
\[
B_q=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{3q-1}
[(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})\wedge(x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1})],
\quad
C_i=(x_{3i+1}\vee x_{3i+2}\vee x_{3i+3})
\]
for $i=0,\ldots,q-1$. Set
\[
F_q^+=B_q\wedge\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-1}C_i,\qquad
F_q^-=B_q\wedge\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-2}C_i\wedge
(\neg x_{3q-2}\vee\neg x_{3q-1}\vee\neg x_{3q}).
\]
These inputs have identical unsigned clause supports and encoded length. Their entire incidence graphs are connected. There are no unit clauses. The binary skeleton $B_q$ has two satisfying assignments, all-zero and all-one, hence it forces no individual source literal; raw unit propagation on the full input has no starting unit.

\begin{theorem}[Unit-free quotient collapse; restricted, peer review pending]
Both labels are exact:
\[
F_q^+\in\mathrm{SAT},\qquad F_q^-\notin\mathrm{SAT}.
\]
The minimum raw syntactic strong 2-CNF backdoor for either formula is $h=q$. Nevertheless, the uniform SCC-equivalence hybrid from Lemma~\ref{lem:scc}, with source acquisition and source witness replay fully charged, solves both in $O(N/w)=O(q)$ word operations and $O(q)$ live logical words. Its trajectory moves from $q$ ternaries to zero without a binary-backbone unit implication.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All clauses of $B_q$ force $x_1=\cdots=x_{3q}$. Every $C_i$ therefore requires the common value $1$; the final negative triple in $F_q^-$ requires it to be $0$. The all-one assignment witnesses $F_q^+$. The $q$ ternary variable supports are pairwise disjoint. Any raw strong deletion backdoor to 2-CNF must meet all $q$ supports, and one chosen variable per support is sufficient, proving $h=q$. The SCCs of $B_q$ identify every positive literal, and separately every negative literal. Each ternary consequently becomes a unit on the same representative, making $B_q\wedge T'$ a 2-CNF. One more SCC finds either a witness or conflicting unit requirements. Neither the $q$ source-variable assignments nor all $2^q$ selector possibilities are enumerated.
\end{proof}

With $m_2=6q-2$, $k=q$, and $m=7q-2$, the fixed-record encoding has
\[
w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil,\qquad N=(4m+2)w.
\]
The assignment-evaluation circuit, \emph{not} a SAT-decider circuit, has $8q-2$ OR2 gates, $7q-3$ AND2 gates and $3q$ source NOT gates, hence $18q-5$ gates and $33q-10$ directed gate-input wires. With a forced-TRUE output and functional trace, it has $51q-14$ Tseitin clauses (including the output unit). Backward output TRUE demands every clause TRUE, but requires only one consistent TRUE witness per clause, not exactly one TRUE original literal.
