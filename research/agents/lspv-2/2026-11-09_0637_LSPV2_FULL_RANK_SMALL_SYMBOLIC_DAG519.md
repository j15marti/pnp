## ITERATE: exact restricted theorem and control
Let G be connected cubic, with H even vertices and E=3H/2 edge variables. At vertex v with incident edges e1,e2,e3 and charge c_v, encode the local parity by four **original** proper ternary clauses forbidding the triples (p,q,1 XOR c_v XOR p XOR q), for p,q in {0,1}. The signs encode forbidden assignments, with fixed source IDs. Fix c_(H-1)=0 and vary other charges d in GF(2)^k, k=H-1. Source packet: 8-bit magic 10110110, unary E plus zero, 4H ordered clauses each containing three (1 sign bit, w-bit positive ID) records, w=ceil(log2(E+1)), plus terminal 01. Exactly
$$
N(H)=8+(E+1)+12H(w+1)+2=\Theta(H\log H).
$$

**Theorem 1 (KNOWN GF(2) incidence fact):** G connected implies its mod-2 incidence matrix has rank H-1, and SAT(F_d) iff XOR(d)=0.

**Theorem 2 (reuses [P2-L2-517], not new all-machine hardness):** Flipping d_j switches SAT label and toggles exactly four distinct original input sign positions, all third literal signs in the four vertex-j clauses. For any total correct deterministic all-CNF SAT decider, on any F_d, at least one of those four positions for each j must be read, else deterministic transition induction would give the wrong answer on F_(d XOR e_j). Thus its completed source-input-read-value transcript has full affine rank k and the 2^k different descriptors cannot all share a complete trace.

**Theorem 3 (new explicit transfer counter-control):** Regardless of those 2^k distinct input-read transcripts, the ENTIRE descriptor-label function is a shared circuit of exactly k-1 two-input XOR gates (balanced span O(log k)). Given the authenticated canonical family, a reflected Gray stream of q=2^r descriptors changing r<=k active bits updates the SAT label with one parity-bit flip per child, retaining an O(r)-bit counter. For r=O(log N), q<=N^(3/5+o(1)), this gives O(N polylog N + q polylog N) time and O(log N) writable space for canonical-family authentication plus q virtual **labels** in an explicitly separate indexed-input model. A family recognizer checks every encoded sign/ID using arithmetic reconstruction of K4/prism graph adjacency and O(log N) counters. This is a promise-family solver, not an all-input SAT contraction. Computing literal completed traces still needs their input reads, and materializing all N-bit children would cost qN. No claim of N^(1/2) child size or same-FLMV-machine compilation is made. A generic correct all-input solver can use exponential fallback on non-family inputs, which does NOT give SAT in P.

**Proof of Gray update:** consecutive reflected binary Gray words differ in one bit; changing one charge reverses exactly the total parity and therefore the output. The single parity register and counter replace repeated guest execution for this recognized promise family. More generally a small source-authenticated algebraic circuit can coexist with exponential completed-trace diversity. **K_trace is not a computational work lower bound.** All original CNF parsing/authentication and output costs remain paid, not free.
