\paragraph{Two obstacles and literature preflight.}
The first obstacle is exact typing: vMS does not uniquely fix the bit-level encoder, so a numerical Boolean product span must be attached to a declared legal encoder. The second is global transversality: a small square for each fixed encoder slice does not imply a small vertical stack across legal source states. The 2025 vMS paper supplies the targeted-HSG/PCPP architecture but no Boolean Schur-square theorem. Recent coding work on HRS and twisted Reed--Solomon variants computes Schur-square dimension by construction-specific determinant/product identities, reinforcing that exact algebraic structure, rather than generic list decodability or field degree, is the relevant mechanism. These coding results do not transfer to unrestricted SAT runtime.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: full Hadamard encoder-square dimension.}
Declare the SU05-sanctioned binary Hadamard conversion
\[
c_{\rm Had}(x)_u=\langle x,u\rangle,\qquad x,u\in\mathbb F_2^k.
\]
Let
\[
W_{\rm Had}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\bigl(\{\mathbf1\}\cup c_{\rm Had}(\mathbb F_2^k)\bigr).
\]
The Hadamard codewords are precisely the linear Boolean functions in the mask coordinates \(u_1,\ldots,u_k\), hence
\[
W_{\rm Had}=\operatorname{span}\{1,u_1,\ldots,u_k\}.
\]
Its Schur square is
\[
W_{\rm Had}^{\star2}
=\operatorname{span}\{1,u_a,u_au_b:1\le a<b\le k\}.
\]
Uniqueness of multilinear ANF on the full Boolean cube makes these functions linearly independent. Therefore
\[
\boxed{d_2(c_{\rm Had})=\dim W_{\rm Had}^{\star2}
=1+k+\binom{k}{2}.}
\]
Thus FEAT1's generic encoder-square ceiling is attained exactly by the full Hadamard conversion.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: one-slice rank formula.}
For a legal source state \(v\), let \(x_i(v)\in\mathbb F_2^k\) be the \(m\) symbol labels supplied to the Hadamard conversion and put
\[
r_v=\dim\operatorname{span}\{x_1(v),\ldots,x_m(v)\}.
\]
The slice columns are \(1\), the linear forms \(\langle x_i(v),u\rangle\), and their pairwise products. The linear forms span an \(r_v\)-dimensional subspace of the dual mask space. After an invertible change of mask coordinates they are generated by \(z_1,\ldots,z_{r_v}\). The squarefree monomials
\[
1,\ z_a,\ z_az_b\quad(a<b)
\]
are independent on \(\mathbb F_2^{r_v}\). Hence
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}B_v=1+r_v+\binom{r_v}{2}.}
\]
This is a slice theorem only. Distinct legal states may have transverse kernels.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: minimum number of Hadamard slices in a full-rank certificate.}
Let
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2.
\]
For any finite legal state set \(S\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{stack}_{v\in S}B_v
\le |S|\left(1+k+\binom k2\right).
\]
Therefore every finite certificate of global full quadratic rank must obey
\[
\boxed{|S|\ge
\left\lceil
\frac{1+m+\binom m2}{1+k+\binom k2}
\right\rceil.}
\]
In particular, if \(m>k\), a one-slice full-rank certificate is impossible for this declared Hadamard realization. This does not preclude full global rank after stacking enough legal states.
