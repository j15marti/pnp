\section{Uniform general certified solver}
For arbitrary $F=B\wedge\bigwedge_{j=1}^kT_j$ with $B$ 2-CNF and at most $k$ genuine ternaries, choose one variable from each as-yet-uncovered ternary. The set $S$ has $h\le k$, and every assignment to it leaves 2-CNF. Enumerate $2^h$ assignments, run SCC, reconstruct a witness for SAT or produce an empty clause / two implication paths for every UNSAT child. Check certificates against regenerated source implication edges. Under four-word clause records with word width $w=\lceil\log_2(16(n+m+1))\rceil$, $N=(2+4m)w$ encoded bits, a charged bound is
\[
C_{\rm discovery}=O(n+m),\quad
C_{\rm normalization},C_{\rm verification},C_{\rm continuation}
=O(2^h(n+m)).
\]
Source reading, failed branches, source replay, proof writes and auxiliary checks are included. Peak live workspace can be $O(n+m+h)$ words with streamed proof output; retained full UNSAT proofs can require $O(2^h(n+m))$. These are \emph{upper} bounds. A constant TRUE/FALSE residual after decide-first preprocessing does not supply a lower-bound argument.
\section{Star-to-choice graph inflation}
Let $p,q\ge1$, $k=p+q$, and use original variables $z$ and distinct triples $(a_i,b_i,c_i)$, $1\le i\le k$. Define
\[
F_{p,q}=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k}(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)
\ \wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{p}(\neg a_i\vee z)
\ \wedge\bigwedge_{j=p+1}^{k}(\neg a_j\vee\neg z).
\]
Here $n=3k+1$ and $m=2k$. All these source variables are original; no auxiliary witness is introduced.
\begin{theorem}[Exact quadratic choice-graph expansion]
The PAIRHELLY67 literal-choice graph has precisely the $pq$ conflict edges $\{(i,a_i)(j,a_j):i\le p<j\}$, with every alternative literal choice isolated. Hence it contains $K_{p,q}$ and has treewidth $\min(p,q)$, while the binary backbone has treewidth one and the whole original CNF primal graph has treewidth two. The minimum strong syntactic direct-restriction 2-CNF backdoor has size $k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choosing $a_i$ on the left forces $z=1$, whereas choosing $a_j$ on the right forces $z=0$; opposite choices conflict. Any other pair extends to a model of the binary backbone. For the primal decomposition use bags $\{a_i,b_i,c_i\}$, $\{a_i,z\}$ and the central singleton $\{z\}$. The connectivity and standard elimination decomposition of $K_{p,q}$ yield treewidth $\min(p,q)$. Disjoint positive ternary supports require one selected variable per group in a strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Representation-specific output and bypass]
A required explicit edge-per-conflict listing has at least $pq$ records. A source-checkable $O(k)$-record representation stores the two lists of marked literals and their common original source $z$. Canonical-grammar recognition by sorting and signed-source comparison, certificate checking and production of a satisfying original assignment require $O(k\log(k+2))$ word operations and $O(k)$ peak words.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are $pq$ distinct conflict edges. The two lists and $z$ answer any conflict query by opposite-list membership; exact regeneration and clause-multiset equality protect source coherence. Setting $b_i=1$ for every group and all other variables zero gives a source-checked witness. Failed recognition triggers the charged generic solver.
\end{proof}
This lower bound concerns only mandatory explicit output. The SAT decision is easy; no unrestricted computational lower bound follows. A complete choice graph can be computed via $O(k^2)$ SCC tests and $O(k^2(n+m))$ word work, including failed tests and certificate verification.
\section{Matched connected SAT/UNSAT controls}
For $t\ge4$, encode one-hot 3-coloring with $t$ groups. Make a $K_4$ core and attach $t-4$ leaves by inequality edges to core vertex $0$. The NO input uses the three normal inequality clauses on every edge. The YES input replaces the three clauses on edge $(0,1)$ with the equally long matching-shift exclusion
\[
\bigwedge_{a=0}^{2}(\neg x_{0,a}\vee\neg x_{1,(a+1)\bmod3}).
\]
Both inputs have $n=3t$, $k=t$, $m=7t+6$, identical $N$ and direct strong 2-CNF backdoor size $t$.
\begin{theorem}[Both labels and path-consistency hostile control]
The NO instance is UNSAT; the YES instance has exactly $6\cdot2^{t-4}$ Boolean source assignments. All singleton domains and pair relations are nonempty, and complete path consistency on the four core vertices deletes no allowed color pair in either case. A source-checked $K_4$ certificate proves UNSAT, and a coloring with independent leaf choices supplies a SAT witness. Canonical recognition, reconstruction and source replay are polynomial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The five ordinary core edges other than $(0,1)$ force the two nonadjacent vertices $0,1$ to share the third color remaining after distinct colors are placed on $2,3$. The usual sixth inequality rejects; the shifted exclusion permits equality. There are six ordered choices for $(2,3)$ and two colors per leaf. Each permitted core edge-color pair has a support at every other core vertex under the two incident relations, proving path consistency. The source-checked core and the replayed coloring are complete certificates. The graph has a width-three clique bag plus pendant edge bags.
\end{proof}
