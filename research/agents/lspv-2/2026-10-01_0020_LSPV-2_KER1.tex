\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: kernel additive-energy identity.}
Let \(A\) be a finite subset of an abelian group \(G\), and let \(L:G\to V\) be a homomorphism with kernel \(K\). Define
\[
r_A(d)=|\{(a,a')\in A^2:a-a'=d\}|.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Coll}_A(L)=\sum_{d\in K}r_A(d)}.
\]
Indeed, \(L(a)=L(a')\) iff \(a-a'\in K\). Hence
\[
\boxed{|L(A)|\ge {|A|^2\over \sum_{d\in K}r_A(d)}}.
\]
This identifies the missing collision quantity as additive concentration of the accepted proof set along the actual kernel directions. It does not create entropy from perfect completeness.

If \(A=a_0+U\) is an affine subspace, then \(r_A(d)=|A|\) for \(d\in U\) and zero otherwise. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Coll}_A(L)=|A|\,|U\cap K|,
\qquad
\boxed{|L(A)|={|A|\over |U\cap K|}}.
\]
An affine accepted-family theorem would thus reduce image size exactly to a kernel-intersection dimension. No such theorem is verified for vMS PCPP acceptance.
