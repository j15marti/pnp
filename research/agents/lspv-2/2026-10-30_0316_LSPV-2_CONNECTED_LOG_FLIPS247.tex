\section{Source-indexed extreme experiment, before the compiler}
Every original ternary clause $C_j$ is retained with signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$, not a separately assignable occurrence variable. Its \emph{local} all-literals-true target $S_j$ requires $x_i=1$ for positive occurrences and $x_i=0$ for negative ones; $U_j$ reverses every such assignment. Inverting $S_j$ yields $U_j$ and inverting $U_j$ yields $S_j$. These requirements generally disagree across clauses; they do not constitute a global assignment. For each original pair $(j,k)$ the recorded $SS,SU,US,UU$ matrix permits $SS,UU$ exactly when no shared variable has opposite signs, and permits $SU,US$ exactly when no shared variable has matching signs. A pair sharing both types makes all four modes impossible, proving only that some clause must have a middle true-literal count $1$ or $2$ on every actual complete assignment. This is \emph{not} a SAT or UNSAT oracle.

We separately evaluate globally coherent $0^n$, $1^n$, their complements and one/multiple source-variable flip trails. For any complete assignment $a$ and original clause $C_j$, $t_j(a)$ is the number of its true literals; $\vv N(a)=(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$ and
\[t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_h(\bar a)=N_{3-h}(a),\quad F(a)\text{ satisfied}\iff N_0(a)=0.\]
Every independent check uses the original source indices, literal signs and coherent variable assignments. The machine-readable audit contains all clause/occurrence targets, inversions, complete pair matrices and endpoint/flip trails. \section{Exact missing lemma: all radii, not just two or three}
Let $F$ be an original proper 3-CNF with $n$ variables, $m$ clauses and each variable occurring in at most $d$ clauses. Fix a genuine complete assignment $a$. Let $G$ be the variable co-occurrence graph, joining two source variables iff they appear in a common original clause. Its maximum degree is $\Delta\le2d$. Let $\vv N(S)=\vv N(a\oplus S)$ for $S\subseteq[n]$ and write $\boldsymbol\delta(S)=\vv N(S)-\vv N(\varnothing)$. For any induced-disconnected $S=U\dot\cup V$ with no $G$ edge joining $U,V$, no original clause sees both, so $\boldsymbol\delta(S)=\boldsymbol\delta(U)+\boldsymbol\delta(V)$.

Introduce independent formal variables $u_1,\ldots,u_n$ and commuting Laurent markers $t_0,\ldots,t_3$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Z}
Z_F(\vv u,\vv t)=\sum_{S\subseteq[n]} \left(\prod_{i\in S}u_i\right)\vv t^{\boldsymbol\delta(S)},\qquad \vv t^{\vv z}=\prod_{h=0}^3t_h^{z_h}.
\end{equation}
$Z_F(\vv0,\vv t)=1$, so its formal logarithm is well-defined. Laurent exponents may be negative, but all calculations are exact and truncated at total $u$-degree $r$. For a nonnegative multiplicity vector $\alpha$ write $\operatorname{supp}\alpha=\{i:\alpha_i>0\}$.

\begin{lemma}[Connected support of logarithmic coefficients]\label{lem:conn}
For every $\alpha\ne\vv0$, if $G[\operatorname{supp}\alpha]$ is disconnected, then $[\vv u^\alpha]\log Z_F(\vv u,\vv t)=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $u_i=0$ outside $H=\operatorname{supp}\alpha$. The coefficient sought is unchanged. If $G[H]$ has nonempty disconnected components $H_1,\ldots,H_s$, the additivity of $\boldsymbol\delta$ implies $Z_{F,H}=\prod_qZ_{F,H_q}$. Hence $\log Z_{F,H}=\sum_q\log Z_{F,H_q}$, and no term contains positive powers of variables from more than one component. The required coefficient vanishes. This also covers arbitrarily many repeated occurrences of the same \emph{formal} variable in the logarithm; these powers are combinatorial book-keeping, not multiple assignments to $x_i$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform exact radius-$r$ aggregate compiler]\label{thm:main}
For every $n,m,r,d$ with $0\le r\le n$, every original proper 3-CNF $F$ on $n$ named variables with occurrence degree at most $d$, and every coherent complete $a$, there is one uniform deterministic algorithm that computes
\[H_r(F,a;\vv q)=|\{S\subseteq[n]:|S|=r,\ \vv N(a\oplus S)=\vv q\}|\]
for every four-category vector $\vv q$, as a compressed histogram in at most $f(r,d)(n+m)$ indexed-word-RAM arithmetic operations and $f(r,d)(n+m)$ working words, for an explicit computable function $f$ independent of $n,m$. For each fixed $r,d$ the time and words are $O(n+m)$; there is \emph{no claim} of a polynomial bound when $r$ grows with $n$. The theorem includes connected signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF with $d=4$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every connected induced vertex set $H$ of size $h\le r$ can be enumerated by starting at one of $n$ roots and adding a neighbor of the previous set at each of $h-1$ steps. The number of candidate histories is at most $n\Delta^{h-1}(h-1)!$, so this enumeration, with deduplication, is $n f_1(r,d)$. For each connected $H$ construct
\[A_H(\vv u_H,\vv t)=\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq H}\vv u_S\vv t^{\boldsymbol\delta(S)},\qquad Z_{F,H}=1+A_H.\]
Every $\boldsymbol\delta(S)$ is computed by scanning at most $d|S|$ original clauses incident to $S$, comparing their original literal-count statuses before and after the coherent flip. Compute the total-degree-$r$ truncation
\[\log Z_{F,H}=\sum_{k=1}^r\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k}A_H^k\pmod{\deg>r}.\]
Retain exactly the monomials whose $u$-support is all of $H$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:conn}, summing these contributions over connected $H$ recovers \emph{all} coefficients of total degree at most $r$ in $\log Z_F$. The maximum work for one $H$ is bounded by a computable function of $r,d$ (for example by explicit enumeration of at most $r(2^r)^r$ ordered factors, times polynomial overhead for signed clause updates and coefficient arithmetic).

Now substitute $u_i=z$ \emph{after constructing the ordinary formal logarithm}, obtaining
\[\log Z_F(z\vv1,\vv t)\equiv\sum_{i=1}^rB_i(\vv t)z^i\pmod{z^{r+1}}.\]
(The substitution must not be performed prematurely in a squarefree quotient: repeated $u_i$ powers in the logarithm matter.) Starting from $E_0=1$, reconstruct the coefficients of $Z_F(z\vv1,\vv t)$ by the exact formal-exponential recurrence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exp}
E_k(\vv t)=\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^k i B_i(\vv t)E_{k-i}(\vv t),\quad 1\le k\le r.
\end{equation}
The coefficient of $\vv t^{\vv q-\vv N(\varnothing)}$ in $E_r$ is exactly $H_r(F,a;\vv q)$, a nonnegative integer; the coefficients sum to $\binom nr$. Since $r$ flips affect at most $dr$ clauses, every shift coordinate lies in $[-dr,dr]$ and sums to zero across four categories. Thus every Laurent polynomial processed has at most $(2dr+1)^3$ possible shift vectors, another bound depending only on $r,d$. Formula scanning, graph discovery, candidate support generation, local clause evaluation, exact rational arithmetic, exponential composition and histogram output are charged. An explicit loose bound is $f(r,d)=\mathrm{poly}(r,d)\bigl(1+\sum_{h=1}^r(2d)^{h-1}(h-1)!\,r2^{r^2}\bigr)(2dr+1)^6$ arithmetic operations per source variable and clause. Exact integer bit lengths, if charged instead of RAM words, add a factor polynomial in $\log(n+m)$ and in $r,d$. The output is aggregated and never enumerates the $\binom nr$ source flip masks.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Certified witness extraction by conditioned compilation]\label{lem:wit}
If $H_r(F,a;\vv q)>0$ for any $\vv q$ with $q_0=0$ (or $q_3=0$), a complete satisfying assignment of $F$ can be found and checked with at most $n+1$ further conditioned invocations of the same compiler, giving $O(n f(r,d)(n+m))$ total indexed-word operations. The $q_3=0$ case complements the recovered flip assignment.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Maintain already-included flips $T$ and eligible variables $E$ disjoint from $T$, initially $T=\varnothing,E=[n]$. Invariant: an extension $T\cup S$ with $S\subseteq E$, $|T\cup S|=r$, has the required good four-category vector. For each current variable $i\in E$, condition on excluding $i$ by running the compiler with genuine base $a\oplus T$, allowed flip-variable set $E\setminus\{i\}$, and radius $r-|T|$. If a good vector remains, discard $i$; otherwise force $i$ into $T$ and remove it from $E$. The invariant is preserved by this exhaustive two-case partition, independent of any SAT answer bit. When $|T|=r$, verify $a\oplus T$ against every original clause, complementing first in the $q_3=0$ case. At most $n+1$ exact compilation calls are used, each charges rebuilding the source-local representation and all unsuccessful feasibility tests. This bound is quadratic in $n$ for fixed $r,d$, \emph{not} a free $O(n)$ witness extractor.
\end{proof}
