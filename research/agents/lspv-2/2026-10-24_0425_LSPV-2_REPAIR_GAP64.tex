\section{Exact integer objective and universal fractional collapse}
For every literal occurrence $o$ let $z_o$ be its selected witness indicator. For each original source $x$, let $u_x^+,u_x^-$ indicate sign use and $b_x$ indicate mixed polarity. Define the linear program
\begin{align}
\min\quad&\sum_x b_x,\\
\sum_{o\in C}z_o&=1 &&(C\text{ a clause}),\\
z_o&\le u_x^{\mathrm{sgn}(o)}&&(o\text{ uses }x),\\
u_x^++u_x^-&\le1+b_x&&(x\text{ a source}),\\
\sum_{o:\mathrm{var}(o)=x}z_o&\le2&&(x\text{ a source}).\tag{1}
\end{align}
In $\mathrm{IP}(F)$ all variables are binary; in $\mathrm{LP}(F)$ they lie in $[0,1]$. POLARITY-REPAIR63 defines $\mu(F)$ as the minimum number of mixed original variables in any capacity selector (one occurrence per clause, at most two per variable), and proves equality with legal-sign-swap distance to satisfiability.

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, integer formulation]
$\operatorname{opt}\mathrm{IP}(F)=\mu(F)$; in particular, the integer optimum is zero iff $F$ is SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An integer feasible $z$ selects one occurrence per clause, at most two per source. Both selected polarities force both $u$ indicators to one and $b_x=1$. Conversely a capacity selector supplies feasible indicators with $b_x=1$ precisely on mixed variables. Hence the optima agree. No mixed variable means a coherent assignment satisfying every selected source occurrence, and every satisfying assignment gives such a selector.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, fractional false positive on EVERY input]
For every valid signed exact-$(2,2)$ formula, SAT or UNSAT,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{opt}\mathrm{LP}(F)=0}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $z_o=1/3$, $u_x^+=u_x^-=1/2$, $b_x=0$. Every three-occurrence clause sums to one. Every coupling row reads $1/3\le1/2$ and $1/2+1/2\le1$. Each source has four occurrences, so capacity is $4/3\le2$. Objective is zero and is nonnegative, proving optimality.
\end{proof}
The Hall two-clone matching polytope is integral; the failure concerns the extra fractional source-polarity coupling, not Hall matching. The analogous uniform witness relaxation for 2-CNF may be feasible on UNSAT inputs while SCC decides those inputs in polynomial time. This is not a lower bound for stronger LP, SDP, Gaussian elimination, learning or arbitrary symbolic methods.

\section{Gap amplification and exact constant-radius hardness}
Write $F\sqcup G$ for a variable-disjoint union with fully preserved signed occurrence records and renumbered source IDs.
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, additive distance]
\[
\mu(F\sqcup G)=\mu(F)+\mu(G).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A capacity selector of the union restricts to one of each component, and the mixed source-variable sets are disjoint. Conversely, optimal component selectors concatenate and remain feasible. These two bounds give equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[KNOWN THEOREM plus PROVED/EXACT reduction]
For every fixed integer $B\ge0$, testing $\mu(F)\le B$ is NP-complete even for strict-monotone proper exact-$(2,2)$ signed sources.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
D\"ocker establishes NP-completeness of strict-monotone proper exact-$(2,2)$ SAT. For each input $H$, output $B+1$ disjoint renamed copies. If $H$ is SAT, $\mu(H)=0$ and the output optimum is zero. Otherwise $\mu(H)\ge1$ and the union optimum is at least $B+1$. Since fixed $B$ preserves polynomial reduction size, NP-hardness follows. An NP witness is a capacity selector with at most $B$ mixed sources, verified by one scan.
\end{proof}
A polynomial additive-$B$ approximation returning a \emph{feasible} repair value on every input would distinguish the two outcomes of this reduction, thus imply $P=NP$. This implication is CONDITIONAL, not an unconditional superpolynomial time theorem. Since the parameter value zero itself is NP-hard, ordinary fixed-parameter tractability in $\mu$ is unavailable unless $P=NP$.
