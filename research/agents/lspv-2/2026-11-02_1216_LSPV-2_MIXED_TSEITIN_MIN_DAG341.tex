No superpolynomial lower bound is claimed. This extends project LSPV-2 ODD-XOR-CYCLE-MIN-DAG340 (a degree-two parity cycle with $2h-1$ minimum) to \emph{degree-three odd Tseitin CNFs}, including cubic graphs useful in known resolution-hardness constructions. \paragraph{One fixed uniform mixed calculus $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.}
An input is an ordinary finite DIMACS CNF, with a declared variable bound, properly terminated clauses and exact header counts; malformed encodings are rejected. Original source clauses are free source-labelled axioms. Every derived node has unit logical cost and at most two antecedent-node identifiers. Shared intermediate nodes cost once, and proof order is any acyclic topological ordering. The fixed rules are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{RES:} binary Boolean-clause resolution on a named complementary pivot, including all ordinary widths. This alone makes the system refutationally complete for CNF.
\item[] \textbf{AND3:} from two conditions whose combined support has at most three original variables, derive their exact conjunction as an eight-bit truth-table factor. A factor may be annotated by a GF(2) row \emph{only if} exhaustive evaluation of all at most eight rows proves exact Boolean equivalence. The annotation is a deterministic local normal-form choice at the same inference node, not a semantic oracle.
\item[] \textbf{XOR:} from two GF(2) equations, infer their XOR sum, including $0=1$. Its variable-mask and RHS computation are algorithmic and its unit \emph{logical} cost does not render the bit manipulation free.
\item[] \textbf{LOCAL-CONVERT:} from one already derived local factor/equation derive a clause or bounded-support Boolean circuit condition only when an explicit constant-support truth-table check establishes logical implication; this has unit cost.
\item[] \textbf{DEF:} at unit cost introduce one fresh variable as a two-input Boolean gate with all defining relations attributed to that same charged node. The definition is conservative: every valuation of previous variables extends uniquely to the new variable; later uses reference its charged node. No independent axiom can encode a formula's SAT label.
\end{enumerate}
Unrestricted auxiliary definitions of other arities and hidden equivalence tests are \emph{not} rules of this fixed $R$. An equivalent sound rule may only be introduced in a later version and separately audited. All rules preserve logical consequence under conservative existential interpretation. RES proves completeness. No derived node has zero weight. The lower-bound argument applies even if any further \emph{conservative, charged, at-most-binary-premise} rules, including temporary growth or representation changes, are added.

\paragraph{Source family.}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite connected \emph{simple bridgeless cubic} graph, $h=|V|$ and $n=|E|=3h/2$. Let $\chi\in\{0,1\}^V$ have odd total parity. For every vertex $v$, its incident-edge variables $x_{e_1},x_{e_2},x_{e_3}$ are constrained by
\[
 x_{e_1}\oplus x_{e_2}\oplus x_{e_3}=\chi_v.
\]
For each of the four local triples of the opposite parity, insert the one three-literal clause falsified \emph{exactly} at that local triple. Thus $F_{G,\chi}$ has $m=4h$ source clauses of width three. Summing all vertex equations cancels each edge twice and yields $0=\bigoplus_v\chi_v=1$, so the formula is UNSAT. For any even-charge $\chi'$, connected incidence rank is $h-1$ and there are exactly $2^{n-h+1}$ satisfying assignments.

\begin{lemma}[Indispensability of all source clauses]
For every $G,\chi$ as above, deleting any one of the $4h$ source clauses makes the remaining CNF satisfiable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In a cubic graph without bridges, no vertex can be a cut vertex: if deleting $v$ made two or more components, its three incident edges would distribute among them and at least one component would have only one edge to $v$, a bridge. Hence $G-v$ is connected for each $v$.
Fix a source clause $C$ at $v$. It forbids one local edge pattern $z\in\{0,1\}^3$ with $\bigoplus z=1\oplus\chi_v$. Pin these three edges to $z$, omit only the parity equation at $v$, and retain all other vertex parity equations. They form the incidence system on the connected graph $G-v$, with fixed-edge contributions moved to right-hand sides. Its only consistency dependency is the XOR of the $h-1$ rows. This dependency is satisfied because
\[
 \bigoplus_{u\ne v}\chi_u = 1\oplus\chi_v = \bigoplus z.
\]
The system therefore has a solution. At $v$ the chosen wrong-parity pattern falsifies precisely $C$ among the four local forbidden-pattern clauses. At every other vertex all four clauses hold. Thus $F_{G,\chi}\setminus\{C\}$ is SAT.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Binary-premise source-capacity bound]
In any sound, source-explicit DAG refutation in which each charged inference/definition node has at most two incoming dependencies, every source clause is essential and all non-source nodes have unit cost, $q\ge m-1$ charged nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All $m$ sources must lie in the ancestry of the contradiction: otherwise the used sources would have a satisfying valuation, extendible across all conservative auxiliary definitions, contradicting soundness. The undirected ancestry graph of $m$ source vertices and $q$ charged vertices is connected and has at least $m+q-1$ edges, while the sum of inference indegrees bounds it by $2q$. Therefore $q\ge m-1$. Shared subproofs, changes of representation, auxiliary gates, and temporary growth do not alter the argument so long as no source-independent unsound axiom or free multi-premise semantic jump is admitted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact global unit-node optimum in the fixed mixed calculus]
For every connected simple bridgeless cubic $G$ and every odd charge $\chi$,
\[
 \boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F_{G,\chi})=4|V|-1.}
\]
The free-choice minimum ranges over \emph{all finite} permitted DAGs, not a depth- or rule-search cutoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemmas give $W\ge 4h-1$. For a matching proof, at every vertex use three AND3 inferences to conjoin its four source clauses into the exact three-variable parity relation. Each AND3 is a two-parent, source-authenticated inference, and the final truth-table factor is locally verified to equal the XOR row. This uses $3h$ nodes. Sum the $h$ rows using $h-1$ binary XOR inferences. Since the total charge is odd, the final row is $0=1$. The constructed shared-DAG refutation has exactly $3h+h-1=4h-1$ nodes. Both sides are attained, so this is a global optimum in the declared calculus, not merely the outcome of a bounded optimizer.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Model-dependence falsifier.} An alternative sound calculus permitting a \emph{unit-cost four-premise} extraction of a whole local XOR3 row admits a proof with $h+(h-1)=2h-1$ nodes. This rule is \emph{not} part of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. Its existence disproves any unqualified inference that the $4h-1$ bound survives arbitrary new multi-premise rules. Another adverse control is a cubic graph formed from two subdivided $K_4$ graphs joined by a bridge: only 36 of its 40 source clauses are independently essential, so the $m-1$ optimality certificate is not applicable, even though an exhibited $39$-node affine refutation remains valid.

\paragraph{Barrier and implication audit.}
Known expander-based odd Tseitin instances can require exponential \emph{resolution} size, as in Urquhart and Ben-Sasson--Wigderson \cite{Urquhart1987,BenSassonWigderson2001}. This run proves a linear \emph{mixed-system} optimum when the cubic graph is bridgeless, so a lower bound from resolution alone does not transfer to the combined calculus. This is a \emph{family failure} of a would-be hard family, not a disproof of worst-case hardness elsewhere. Neither a parity recognizer with exponential fallback nor a short proof on these instances disproves B2 or B3.
The weakest sufficient bridge remains
\[
 \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm{P}\implies \exists C,k\;\forall N:\quad
 \overline W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(N)\le C(N+1)^k.
\]
It is \textbf{OPEN}. Its pointwise and worst-case stronger variants B1 and B2 are also \textbf{OPEN} for this fixed calculus. In particular, Cook--Reckhow's existence theorem for \emph{some} proof system under $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ does not identify this preselected system \cite{CookReckhow1979}. No superpolynomial bound on $\overline W_R$ follows from the present linear family, and $P=NP$ and $P\ne NP$ remain OPEN.
