\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: affine-block rank obstruction.}
Let $H\subseteq\mathbb F_2^m$ and suppose $H$ contains an affine subspace $A=a+S$ of affine dimension $r$. Let
\[
\rho(H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{span}\{1,x_i|_H,x_ix_j|_H:1\le i<j\le m\}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)\ge 1+r+\binom r2.}
\]
Proof: restriction from ambient affine linear forms to $A$ surjects onto the affine-linear functions on $A$. Choose affine coordinates $z_1,\ldots,z_r$ on $A$. Translation by $a$ is an automorphism of the degree-at-most-two Boolean function space, so the restrictions contain
\[
1,\quad z_i,\quad z_i z_j\ (i<j).
\]
These $1+r+\binom r2$ functions are linearly independent by uniqueness of algebraic normal form on $\mathbb F_2^r$. Restriction cannot increase rank, yielding the bound.

Consequently, any claimed coefficient-strong bound $\rho(H)\le C m$ forces every affine block contained in $H$ to satisfy
\[
\boxed{
r\le \left\lfloor { -1+\sqrt{1+8(Cm-1)}\over 2}\right\rfloor.
}
\]
In particular, one legal SAME\_REALIZATION affine block with $r=\omega(\sqrt m)$ falsifies every $\rho(H)=O(m)$ claim for that realization, while $r=\Omega(m)$ yields $\rho(H)=\Omega(m^2)$. If $r=m$, then $\rho(H)=1+m+\binom m2$ and the global quadratic kernel is zero.

\paragraph{Why this is stronger than accepted-family counting.}
This obstruction needs one legal affine block, not exponentially many accepted proofs. It therefore bypasses the proof-multiplicity and collision-entropy feasibility ceiling. It does not assert that an actual SU/vMS accepted block has large affine dimension; that is the remaining SAME\_REALIZATION question.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: shortening lift criterion.}
Let $C\subseteq\mathbb F_q^N$ be linear, let $J$ be shortened coordinates, let $Z_J=\{v:v|_J=0\}$, let $K=C\cap Z_J$, and let $P_J$ delete $J$. Then
\[
S_J(C)=P_J(K),\qquad S_J(C)^{\star2}=P_J(K^{\star2}),
\]
whereas
\[
S_J(C^{\star2})=P_J(C^{\star2}\cap Z_J).
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{S_J(C)^{\star2}=S_J(C^{\star2})}
\]
holds if and only if
\[
\boxed{C^{\star2}\cap Z_J\subseteq K^{\star2}+V_J,}
\]
where $V_J=\ker P_J$ is the coordinate subspace supported on $J$. The reverse inclusion after puncturing is automatic. This gives the exact missing lifting test for any shortening-based Schur-square diagnostic: a relation or rank drop found after shortening is globally relevant only when this quotient condition, or a stronger construction-specific lift, is proved.
