\paragraph{All-graph theorem.}
Let $G$ be connected, simple, bridgeless and cubic, with $h$ vertices and odd vertex-charge vector $\chi$. Let $F_{G,\chi}$ consist of the four forbidden-pattern three-clauses at each vertex, and let $t$ be fresh. Define
\[
H_{G,\chi,t}=t\wedge\bigwedge_{C\in F_{G,\chi}}(\neg t\lor C).
\]
\begin{theorem}
For every such $G,\chi,t$,
\[
\boxed{4h\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_{G,\chi,t})\le8h-1.}
\]
All $4h+1$ source clauses are independently indispensable. The $8h-1$ bound has a uniform source-authenticated construction using only the fixed rules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Deletion of source unit $t$ permits $t=0$. Deletion of guarded source $(\neg t\lor C)$ permits $t=1$ and the previously proved satisfying deletion witness for $F_{G,\chi}\setminus\{C\}$ from LSPV-2/341. Thus every source is essential. Binary-premise ancestry connectivity of $m=4h+1$ source leaves and $q$ paid nodes gives $q\ge m-1=4h$.

For the upper construction resolve $t$ against each of the $4h$ guarded source clauses, paying $4h$ RES nodes, and thereby obtain all unguarded original $C$. At each vertex conjoin the four recovered clauses using three existing two-premise AND3 inferences with exactly three original-edge-variable support, paying $3h$. The verified local factor is the corresponding GF(2) parity row. XOR-add the $h$ rows, paying $h-1$ nodes, and obtain $0=1$ because every edge occurs twice and the total vertex charge is odd. The total is $4h+3h+h-1=8h-1$. No new rule, free guard erasure, or high-support semantic conversion is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Backwards live-ancestor trimming]
Given any valid finite source-labelled proof program, retain only the recursively reachable ancestors of its final contradiction and renumber parent IDs. Every retained inference keeps its parents and justification, and the resulting refutation has no more paid nodes than the original. Any conservative DEF whose view survives remains paid; definitions with no live view can be deleted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Induction over original topological line order preserves all remaining premise and inference checks. The last contradiction is retained by construction.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Hostile controls, physical costs and quantifiers.}
The guard adds a fourth syntactic variable to each source clause, so a direct free AND3 extraction would be illegal. The all-graph upper construction \emph{pays} one RES step per guarded clause before the legal three-variable extraction. The strict-Resolution DP producer has potentially exponential intermediate clauses and no asserted polynomial growing-expander bound; with $n$ edge variables there are at most $3^n$ distinct surviving edge-pattern clauses with fixed $\neg t$, but a naive elimination pass can attempt $O(n9^n)$ pairs.
