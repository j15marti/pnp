\paragraph{Fixed system.} The research target is arbitrary well-formed CNF-SAT, with malformed encodings rejected, and the minimum unit-cost contradiction DAG under a fixed sound rule system R4. R4 has authenticated original clauses, binary resolution, weakening, explicit local four-premise three-variable XOR extraction, binary GF(2) addition, short XOR-to-clause conversion, and fresh conservative XOR/AND gate extensions. All source-dependent steps require explicit premises. Each rule is locally checkable and has at most four premises. Reused nodes are paid once. The optimizer permits one auxiliary and width four, but the theorem below does not need those finite limits.
\paragraph{Theorem (proved).} For every sound source-explicit rule system with at most d premises per inference, jointly conservative fresh definitions, no input-tailored answer axiom, and every clause-minimally UNSAT original F with m clauses,
\[
 W_R(F)\ge \left\lceil\frac{m-1}{d-1}\right\rceil .
\]
Take the DAG ancestry of the contradiction. If it contains a original-source leaves and q inferred nodes, connectivity implies at least a+q-1 premise edges. As each inferred node has at most d incoming edges, (d-1)q is at least a-1. Clause minimality and conservativity force a=m: otherwise a satisfying assignment of the remaining source clauses extends to the definitions and contradicts soundness.
\paragraph{Certified exact optimum.} Let F8 contain the eight three-variable clauses, each forbidding one Boolean assignment. It is minimally UNSAT. Its four odd-parity-forbidding clauses entail XOR(x1,x2,x3)=0; its four even-parity-forbidding clauses entail XOR(x1,x2,x3)=1. Two four-premise extraction nodes and a binary XOR addition refute F8, so W_R4(F8)=3. The theorem excludes every two-node derivation, even with temporary growth, reuse and fresh conservative definitions. Pure binary resolution has exact minimum seven; an alternative auxiliary-XOR route uses six.
\paragraph{Computation.} The exhaustive bounded optimizer generated 26,857 candidate actions, visited 10,670 states and reached the three-node proof. Its first two layers contained 36 and 708 states; 107,660 KiB was the observed all-phase host-process peak, not a logical inference weight. An independently written verifier checked the minimum certificate, resolution and auxiliary proofs, full original-source labels, and corrupted-proof controls.
\paragraph{Hostile controls.} A 16-clause odd K4 Tseitin CNF has an explicit seven-node parity refutation but only the certified mixed-system interval 5 <= W_R4 <= 7. Its even counterpart is SAT with eight models, while a standalone parity half is SAT with four. A full-enumeration UNSAT decider admits a uniform O(n 2^n) resolution/weakening proof translator; on K4 its constructed proof has 163 nodes. Direct UP-DPLL counts are 124 F8 UNSAT, 30 parity-half SAT, 728 odd-K4 UNSAT, 177 even-K4 SAT and 57 easy-equality SAT literal inspections.
\paragraph{OPEN simulation.} The central missing statement is: for every fixed uniform correct all-CNF SAT decider A, does some fixed polynomial p_A bound the cost of an R4 proof derived from A's UNSAT computation by p_A(N+T_A(F))? This is UNPROVED. Source-support and proof-size lower bounds do not imply arbitrary-decider lower bounds. All-phase time, space and exact same-machine FLMV transfer remain open. Both P=NP and P not equal NP remain OPEN.
