\section*{Established hard family and model}
For each integer $\ell\ge32$, put $n=2^\ell$. Original bit-PHP $F_\ell$ has $(n+1)\ell$ Boolean variables $x_{i,t}$ and, for every pair $i<j$ and hole word $a\in\{0,1\}^\ell$, the source
\[
D_{i,j,a}=\bigvee_{t=1}^{\ell}(x_{i,t}\ne a_t)\ \vee\
\bigvee_{t=1}^{\ell}(x_{j,t}\ne a_t).
\]
It has $m=n\binom{n+1}{2}$ clauses of width $2\ell$ and explicit signed ASCII-DIMACS bit length $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$. Braun [1] states that for every $\ell\ge32$, EVERY unrestricted shared-DAG Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of exactly this source, counted in ALL nodes including initial clauses, has
\[
S_\oplus(F_\ell)>\exp\left(\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}\right). \tag{B}
\]
Res$(\oplus)$ lines are disjunctions of affine equations over $\mathbb F_2$. The rule set [2] is single-clause semantic weakening and resolution on complementary affine equations. No regularity or depth restriction is imposed. The published claim is NOT a theorem about arbitrary conservative extensions or arbitrary deterministic SAT computation. No independent Lean kernel replay was performed.

Fix the already preselected $R_{\rm mix341}$ with zero-cost authentic source clauses and unit-cost RES, exact AND3, binary GF$(2)$ row XOR, justified LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative signed two-input DEF. Let $q$ denote DISTINCT paid derived DAG nodes. The present theorem applies to its \emph{locally opaque, wire-defined subgrammar}: every DEF is a source-independent paid two-input Boolean gate, with authenticated constant-size slots, and is acyclic; every non-RES/non-XOR semantic operation is extensional on at most THREE exposed original variables, with an exact checked truth table and no uncharged named internal wires. Arbitrarily many, arbitrarily wide XOR rows remain permitted and may be interleaved with every other legal operation. This is a restriction on proof usage, not a new calculus. LOCAL-CONVERT operations exposing uncharged internal circuit wires are outside the theorem until the original grammar is pinned.

Let $B$ be the union of original variables used immediately as inputs to paid gate definitions, $b=|B|$; for $g$ paid two-input gates, $b\le2g$. Every output gate is determined once $B$ is fixed, including nested gate outputs. These are effective definitional evaluations, not free assignments of auxiliary truth values.

\begin{lemma}[Native affine simulation]\label{local}
Every definition-free, locally opaque proof of an UNSAT CNF $F$ with $m$ authentic source clauses and $q$ paid nodes has a Res$(\oplus)$ refutation using at most $m+64q$ lines, independent of the width of any intermediate GF$(2)$ row.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Represent a factor on at most three original variables by its canonical blocking clauses, at most eight. AND3 and extensional LOCAL-CONVERT output clauses follow from their parent factors by single-clause semantic weakening: for each falsifying output assignment, some premise constraint is false. An output affine unit row can be derived by eight semantic-weakening leaves, one for each assignment to the at most three variables, followed by seven ordinary complementary-variable cuts. On a leaf satisfying the row, row-or-complete-blocker is tautological; on a leaf falsifying it, an authenticated parent blocker is false and may be weakened to row-or-complete-blocker. Tautological row annotations can be omitted. These require fewer than 64 lines per local node. A RES step is one Res$(\oplus)$ cut. Given arbitrary-width equations $E_1$ and $E_2$, their sum $E_3=E_1+E_2$ follows in TWO Res$(\oplus)$ nodes: weaken $E_1$ to $E_3\vee\neg E_2$ and resolve with the unit $E_2$, as in [2]. This does not expand a wide parity row into exponentially many Boolean assignments. Translate topologically and reuse shared lines. Degenerate duplicate/tautological clauses admit standard subclause regularization with no cost increase.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[GF$(2)$-native definition-exposure compiler]\label{compiler}
For every normalized UNSAT source CNF $F$ and every locally opaque, wire-defined $R_{\rm mix341}$ refutation with parameters $(m,q,b)$, a Res$(\oplus)$ proof of the SAME original $F$ exists with
\[
\boxed{S_\oplus(F)\le 2^b(2m+64q+2).} \tag{1}
\]
Proof search, encoded proof bits, actual compilation work and space are not included in this logical line count.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^B$, substitute original exposed variables and evaluate all paid DEF outputs in topological order. Their constant defining clauses become true. Restrict the rest of the proof, dropping true nodes, retaining stronger representatives across fixed-pivot Resolution steps, and preserving exact local factor implications and GF$(2)$ additions. Lemma~\ref{local} produces a Res$(\oplus)$ contradiction for $F|_\alpha$ with at most $m+64q$ lines. Add branch blocker $D_\alpha=\bigvee_{x\in B}(x\ne\alpha(x))$ to every derived line. Res$(\oplus)$ semantic weakening and cuts are stable under this common disjunct. Each restricted source can be lifted from the authentic original source with at most one additional weakening. The branch derives $D_\alpha$. Glue all $2^b$ blockers through a binary variable-splitting tree with $2^b-1$ cuts. Counting the original source lines once and every branch line gives at most $m+2^b(m+64q)+2^b-1\le2^b(2m+64q+2)$. Every intermediate line is source-authenticated; no SAT oracle, unrestricted F-to-false rule or new auxiliary axiom is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Restricted BPHP tradeoff, conditional on (B)]\label{tradeoff}
Every locally opaque, wire-defined mixed proof of the ORIGINAL $F_\ell$ satisfies
\[
\boxed{b+\log_2(2m+64q+2)>
\frac{n}{32768\ell^2\ln2}.} \tag{2}
\]
If $b\le(1-\varepsilon)n/(32768\ell^2\ln2)$ for any fixed $\varepsilon>0$, then $q=\exp(\Omega_\varepsilon(n/\ell^2))$, superpolynomial in $N_\ell$. In particular the entire DEF-FREE, globally XOR-enabled locally opaque fragment has a conditional superpolynomial worst-case minimum. No lower bound for the full $R_{\rm mix341}$ follows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{compiler} upper-bounds the size of a valid proof in the exact model to which (B) applies. Compare and take binary logarithms. With the stated restriction on $b$, rearrangement yields $64q>2^{\varepsilon n/(32768\ell^2\ln2)}-2m-2$. Since $m=\Theta(n^3)$, this is $\exp(\Omega_\varepsilon(n/\ell^2))$. Polynomial-size Extended Resolution proofs may have $b=\Omega(n/\ell^2)$ or greater and therefore do NOT contradict this theorem. Nothing here proves $b$ is small for the cheapest FULL-$R$ proof.
\end{proof}
