\[
\boxed{D_{\rm raw}(G)=h,\qquad D_{\rm recoded}(G)=1.}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For connected $G$, the unsigned vertex-edge incidence matrix over $\mathbb F_2$ has rank $h-1$, and its image is the even-parity subspace. Thus $F_{G,\chi}$ is SAT exactly if $\bigoplus_i\chi_i=0$. Every raw bit is pivotal at every input; not reading $\chi_j$ makes the transcript identical on the flipped, opposite-label input. Reading all bits suffices. The invertible triangular map places that entire parity in $y_1$, proving the one-query bound and its zero-query lower bound. The number of models in the SAT case is $2^{|E|-h+1}$.
\end{proof}
