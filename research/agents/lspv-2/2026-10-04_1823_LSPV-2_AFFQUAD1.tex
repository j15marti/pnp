\section*{AFFINV1}

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{ev}_{T(X)}

\]

\section*{QMINOR1}
Let
\[
B_r=\{0\}\cup\{e_i\}\cup\{e_i+e_j:i<j\}.
\]
If \(B_r\subseteq X\), or an affine image of \(B_r\) is contained in \(X\), then
\[
\boxed{\rho=1+r+\binom r2.}
\]
Proof: if \(p=c+\sum_i a_ix_i+\sum_{i<j}b_{ij}x_ix_j\) vanishes on \(B_r\), evaluation at zero gives \(c=0\), at each \(e_i\) gives \(a_i=0\), and at each \(e_i+e_j\) gives \(b_{ij}=0\). Hence the evaluation kernel is zero. The affine case follows from AFFINV1.

Consequently, under this containment, \(r=\omega(\sqrt m)\) implies \(\rho=\omega(m)\). The containment and growth premise are OPEN for exact SU/vMS.
