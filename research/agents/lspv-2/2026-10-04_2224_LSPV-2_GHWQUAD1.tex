\section*{GHWKERNEL1}
Let \(d_j(C_r)\) be the \(j\)-th generalized Hamming weight of \(C_r\). Then, for every \(j\ge1\),
\[
\boxed{|Y|<d_j(C_r)\Longrightarrow\kappa(X)<j}
\]
and equivalently
\[
\boxed{|Y|<d_j(C_r)\Longrightarrow\rho(X)\ge D_r-j+1.}
\]

\textbf{Proof.}
The kernel
\[
K_X=\{p\in V_r:p|_X=0\}
\]
evaluates injectively on the full Boolean cube to a subcode of \(C_r\) of dimension \(\kappa(X)\). Every such codeword vanishes on \(X\), so the support of this entire subcode lies inside \(Y\). If \(\kappa(X)\ge j\), choose a \(j\)-dimensional subcode. By definition of generalized Hamming weight its support has size at least \(d_j(C_r)\), while containment in \(Y\) gives support size at most \(|Y|\), contradiction. \(\square\)

This is PROVED/EXACT finite-dimensional coding-theoretic accounting. Independent red-team review is still pending, so it is not promoted to a final complexity claim.
