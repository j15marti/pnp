\section{BUILD I: source, four-entry transfer law, and proof}
A \emph{strict signed exact-$(2,2)$} CNF has distinct proper width-three clauses and each variable has exactly two positive and two negative literal occurrences. Let $H$ be such a connected CNF with variable $x$. Select a clause $C=(\neg x\lor A)$, where $A=(\ell_1\lor\ell_2)$ contains two other variables. Let $H^{-}$ denote $H$ without $C$. Define Boolean predicates
\[
 H_0=\left[\exists V\setminus\{x\}:H\wedge\neg x\right],\qquad
 H_1=\left[\exists V\setminus\{x\}:H\wedge x\right],\qquad
 Q_0=\left[\exists V\setminus\{x\}:H^{-}\wedge\neg x\wedge A\right].
\]
Notice $Q_0\Rightarrow H_0$, since $\neg x$ already satisfies the removed clause.

Use the fixed 15-variable, 20-clause $G$ from EXACT22-PORT-WIRES221. Its port is $p=x_4$ and its designated source clause is $D=(\neg p\lor B)$, with $B=\neg x_9\lor\neg x_{11}$. Its exact punctured profile is
\[
 \{(p,B):\exists Z\;G^{-}(p,Z)\}=\{(0,0),(1,0),(1,1)\}.
\]
This is a constant finite lemma, reproduced independently by exhaustive enumeration of $2^{15}$ assignments. In particular $p=0$ forces $B=0$ after puncturing, while both port values have full $G$ witnesses.

Form $F$ by taking disjoint $H,G$ and exchanging only the designated occurrences $\neg x$ and $\neg p$. The resulting clauses are $(\neg p\lor A)$ and $(\neg x\lor B)$; every other source clause is unchanged. The visible two-port relation is
\[
 R_F(x,p)=\exists(V(H)\setminus\{x\})\exists(V(G)\setminus\{p\})\;F.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact triangular two-port transport]
For every connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ source $H$ and every selected negative occurrence as above, the constructed $F$ is connected, proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$, and
\[
\boxed{\quad R_F=\begin{array}{c|cc} & p=0 & p=1\\\hline x=0&H_0&Q_0\\ x=1&0&H_1\end{array}.\quad}
\]
Consequently \(\operatorname{SAT}(F)\iff\operatorname{SAT}(H)\), but generally $R_F$ is not equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Swapping two negative occurrences preserves both variables' exact positive and negative counts and cannot create a duplicate variable within either clause because the source variable sets were initially disjoint. The fixed source $G$ has a crucial nonbridge property: its distinguished occurrence edge $(p,D)$ is not an incidence-graph bridge because the untouched clause $C_3=(\neg p\lor\neg x_8\lor\neg x_{11})$ and the variable $x_{11}$ give an alternate path $p\to C_3\to x_{11}\to D$. Thus $G$ remains connected after deleting $(p,D)$. Remove the incidence edge $(x,C)$ from connected $H$; this makes at most two components, one containing $x$ and one containing $C$. Adding the two cross-incidence edges $(x,D)$ and $(p,C)$ connects both pieces to the still-connected copy of $G$, hence the full new incidence graph is connected. All clauses remain proper and distinct.

For fixed $(x,p)$, the internal assignments to $H$ and to $G$ are independent after fixing the ports. If $(x,p)=(0,0)$, the transported $\neg p$ is true and the $G$ clause $\neg x\lor B$ is true; the remaining condition is $H^{-}\wedge\neg x$, equivalent to $H\wedge\neg x$, giving $H_0$. If $(x,p)=(0,1)$, the first transported clause requires $A=1$, while the second is true; the $G^{-}$ profile admits $p=1$, giving $Q_0$. If $(x,p)=(1,0)$, the first clause is true, but the second requires $B=1$ with $p=0$, which the puncture table forbids. Finally $(x,p)=(1,1)$ requires both $A=1$ and $B=1$. The latter is feasible by the puncture table; the former is exactly the original $H$ requirement at $x=1$, yielding $H_1$.

Since $Q_0\Rightarrow H_0$, the transport is satisfiable iff $H_0\lor H_1$, precisely $\operatorname{SAT}(H)$. \end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: explicit nonaffine implication, all odd sizes}
For odd $r\ge5$, let $H_r$ be the deterministic affine 3CNF from AFFINE-RESOLVENT-FIXEDPOINT216. Its $3r$ variables are $A_i,B_i,C_i$ indexed modulo $r$, with clauses
\[
 (A_i^{\alpha_s}\vee B_{i+s}^{\beta_s}\vee C_{i+2s}^{\gamma_s}),
 \qquad s\in\{0,1,2,3\},
\]
where sign rows are $\alpha=(-,-,+,+)$, $\beta=(+,+,-,-)$, $\gamma=(-,+,-,+)$. It has $4r$ clauses, is connected and strict signed exact-$(2,2)$. Both the all-zero and all-one assignments satisfy $H_r$: every layer contains both a positive and a negative literal in each clause. Select $x=A_0$, and its $s=0$ clause
\[
 C_0=(\neg A_0\vee B_0\vee\neg C_0).
\]
For the all-zero assignment, $x=0$ and $A=B_0\vee\neg C_0=1$, establishing $Q_0=1$. The all-zero and all-one witnesses also give $H_0=H_1=1$. The transport theorem immediately yields:
\begin{theorem}[Explicit nonaffine strict exact22 family]
For each odd $r\ge5$, the splice of $H_r$ with $G$ is a connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF with $n=3r+15$ variables and $m=4r+20$ clauses whose exact two-port relation is
\[
 R_r(x,p)=\neg x\lor p.
\]
This relation is nonaffine as a subset of $\mathbb F_2^2$ because $00,01,11$ belong to it but $00\oplus01\oplus11=10$ does not. Thus a one-swap source change produces implication, not the equality observed in the two-copy $G$ experiment.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}The previous witnesses and the triangular transport theorem prove all four entries uniformly in $r$. The given cardinalities and strict occurrence properties follow by a signed occurrence swap without added or removed clauses.\end{proof}

\section{Resource and model ledger}
Let $L$ be the physical bit length of a canonical unpadded DIMACS source. Signed degree four implies $m=4n/3$ and $L=\Theta(n\log(n+2))$ for the declared encoding. Keep the original variable names $1,\ldots,n$ unchanged and place $G$ on $n+1,\ldots,n+15$. The output retains all original clauses except for one negative-literal replacement and appends 20 fixed clauses with names up to $n+15$. Hence
\[
 \boxed{L'=L+\Theta(\log(n+2))=L+\Theta(\log L).}
\]
This rules out $L'\le L^\lambda$ for every fixed $\lambda<1$ and all sufficiently large inputs \emph{for this specific compiler}. It is not a lower bound on alternative semantic compilers or on SAT decision time.

\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{Indexed RAM.} Canonical source validation and enumeration of four occurrences per variable cost polynomially many word operations, $O(L)$ physical source reads, and $O(L)$ writable bits in a direct parser; output writes cost $\Theta(L')$ bits. No SAT oracle call is made. Word operations include address calculation and header validation.
\item[]\textbf{Fixed multitape deterministic TM.} With read-only input and counted work-tape cells, a conservative explicit parser, clause sorter, connectivity checker, and source-to-child stream takes $T_{\rm multi}=O(L^3)$ steps, $S_{\rm multi}=O(L)$ work cells, and writes $L'$ output bits. This is a provable upper bound for a straightforward implementation, not a measured tight machine trace.
\item[]\textbf{Deterministic one-tape TM.} Simulating the preceding fixed multitape program on one read/write tape including the input within its footprint gives $T_{\rm one}=O(L^4)$ and $P_{\rm one}=O(L)$ using the conservative $O(T_{\rm multi}(L+S_{\rm multi}+\log(T_{\rm multi}+2)))$ simulation; all input, work and output cells are charged.
\item[]\textbf{Fixed universal TM.} A fixed universal interpreter of the above fixed program incurs polynomial compilation/simulation overhead; no particular universal model-specific exponent is claimed without a defined instruction encoding and trace.
\item[]\textbf{Answer classes.} The \emph{same} constructor runs on SAT and UNSAT instances. There is no free classification bit. Its output remains a SAT-hard core plus a constant-size gadget; profile acquisition and arbitrary-core SAT decision have not been performed.
\end{description}

\section{Barrier audit and next role-specific target}
A single literal splice is an exact model-preserving \emph{decision reduction} but not an exact \emph{cofactor-preserving port reduction}. Replacing a bound on source acquisition by a bound on output truth-table size is invalid. The affine control is easy, the $G$ equality control is easy, and the UNSAT $W_1$ control shows soundness on a negative instance. Neither a runtime lower bound nor a general compression algorithm follows. Padding the source, using a semantic oracle, or fixing port values with hidden advice would invalidate the intended uniformity criterion.

The next target is a strict exact22 \emph{tight-foot regulator}: for any arbitrary source $H$, uniformly install an interface that kills the extra $Q_0$ cofactor without altering $H_0,H_1$, including full four-port assignment preservation. It must not cost a free evaluation of $Q_0$, must respect signed quota and fanout, and must be composable across arbitrary SAT-hard modules. The all-input ordinary-SAT one-child $N^\lambda$ contraction remains OPEN, as does its separation alternative. $P$ versus $NP$ remains OPEN.
