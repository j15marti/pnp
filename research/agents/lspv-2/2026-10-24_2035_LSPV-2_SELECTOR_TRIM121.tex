\section{Nonempty selector theorem}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$ be a proper 3CNF. Introduce one Boolean occurrence selector $y_{ij}$ for every literal. Define
\[
P_F=\bigwedge_i(y_{i1}\vee y_{i2}\vee y_{i3}),\qquad
B_F=\bigwedge_{i,j}(\neg y_{ij}\vee\ell_{ij}),
\]
retaining source variables, and the source-free conflict 2CNF
\[
Q_F=\bigwedge_{\substack{u,v:\ \ell_u=x_k,\ \ell_v=\neg x_k}}
(\neg y_u\vee\neg y_v).
\]
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT in declared construction]
For every original source assignment $x$,
\[
F(x)=1\iff \exists y[P_F(y)\land B_F(x,y)].
\]
Also $F\in SAT$ iff $P_F\land Q_F\in SAT$. At least one occurrence, not exactly one, is required from each clause. An original 3-literal OR may have all three literals true.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
From any satisfying $x$, select one true literal per clause and leave the other selectors false. This satisfies $P_F,B_F,Q_F$. Conversely $P_F\land B_F$ selects a source-true literal in every clause. For $P_F\land Q_F$, each source variable has no simultaneously selected opposite polarities. Assign its selected polarity, if any, and choose any value otherwise. Every selected literal is simultaneously true and every clause has one. Thus the reconstructed source assignment satisfies $F$. Exactly-one constraints on original literals are never introduced.
\end{proof}
\section{Exact source count and ordinary proper-3CNF repair}
For strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ proper ternary CNF, $4n=3m$. Complement occurrences form $n$ disjoint $K_{2,2}$, hence exactly $4n=3m$ conflict edges. Every selector occurs once positively (one clause block) and twice negatively (two conflict clauses). The source-free mixed formula has $m$ positive 3-clauses and $3m$ negative binary clauses, with no within-clause triangle clauses. Each half is independently satisfiable, while their common-source intersection is NP-complete by the known NP-completeness of clause-monotone exact-$(2,2)$ 3SAT and this linear reduction.
Each binary $(a\vee b)$ is exactly equivalent (for every setting of the auxiliary) to
\[
(a\vee b\vee z)\land(a\vee b\vee\neg z)
\]
with distinct new $z$. Consequently the full source-free ordinary proper-3CNF has exactly
\[
\boxed{n'=6m,\qquad m'=7m.}
\]
For arbitrary proper 3CNF, the source-retaining $P_F\land B_F$ representation has $n+6m$ target variables and $7m$ proper ternary clauses and exact existential projection onto $F(x)$. Its implication graph is a DAG of longest directed path length at most two: $y^+\to x^\pm\to y^-$. The strict source-free conflict graph has only arcs $y^+\to y^-$, of depth at most one. Isolated $B_F$ and $Q_F$ therefore need no complicated SCC resolution; source consistency with $P_F$ remains the original SAT decision.
