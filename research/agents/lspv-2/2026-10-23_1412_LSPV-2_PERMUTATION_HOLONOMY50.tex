\section*{LSPV-2: Certified permutation-holonomy normalization}
\textbf{Status: P versus NP OPEN.} This report concerns a restricted polynomial-time SAT family, not an unrestricted complexity separation.

For each vertex $v$ use an exactly-one triple $x_{v,0},x_{v,1},x_{v,2}$, encoded by one positive ternary and three negative binary clauses. For every oriented edge $u\to v$ labeled by permutation $\pi\in S_3$ include all six binary implications
\[
\bigwedge_{c=0}^2(\neg x_{u,c}\vee x_{v,\pi(c)})
\wedge(\neg x_{v,\pi(c)}\vee x_{u,c}).
\]
A signed-source recognizer validates every clause and rejects missing, unexpected, duplicate or auxiliary clauses. Select a spanning forest and let $p_v$ transport each root color to vertex $v$. Define
\[
R_j=\{c\in\{0,1,2\}:\forall(u,v,\pi)\in E_j,\ 
p_v(c)=\pi(p_u(c))\}.
\]
\textbf{Restricted exact theorem.} Original assignments satisfying the formula correspond bijectively to choices of one root color in each $R_j$. Hence SAT iff every $R_j$ is nonempty and the exact model count is $\prod_j|R_j|$. A forest, its maps, allowed root colors, and one failing edge for each disallowed color form a source-checkable certificate. Deterministic source parsing is $O((n+m)\log(n+m+2))$ word time; forest composition, all three edge-color checks, witness reconstruction and verification are $O(n+m+|V|+|E|)$ further word work and linear working space.

\textbf{Matched hostile pair.} Two identity-labeled cyclic layers of $t$ vertices joined by a matching have one cycle-closing edge labeled either transposition $(01)$, giving exactly one model, or 3-cycle $(012)$, giving no model. Both have $n=6t$, $k=2t$ genuine ternaries, $m=26t$ clauses and identical encoded length. Unit/pure simplification removes no variables, and the minimum strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor is exactly $k$ because the positive triple supports are disjoint. The polynomial forest normalization decides both labels.

\textbf{General fallback.} For arbitrary 2-CNF plus $k$ ternaries, select one source variable from each uncovered ternary and enumerate at most $2^k$ assignments. All children are 2-CNF, decided by SCC in $O(2^k(n+m))$ word time, with unsuccessful branches, source replay and certificates charged. Certified reachability projection and affine/component recognition remain alternative upper bounds, not lower bounds.

\textbf{Computational.} Stdlib-only audit with seed 20261023: 430 checks, zero failures. Source-ID renaming, malformed coupling, auxiliary source mismatch, disjoint joins, SAT/UNSAT Horn, XOR, padded-2SAT and random mixed formulas were tested. At $t=3,4,5$ the fallback actually tried 64, 256, 1024 leaves on UNSAT cases. At $t=80$ forest checking used 720 color-edge comparisons; $2^{160}$ is only the specified fallback's upper-bound enumeration size.

\textbf{Sources checked.} Root governance and skill, MAIN64 exact join, MAIN85 root-label quotient, MAIN90 dense coupling, Role4 WHEEL-CRITICAL-LADDER52, Role2 CERTIFIED-REACHABILITY-KERNEL89. Exact permutation constraints are the fully satisfiable special case of the Unique Games formulation (Khot STOC 2002; Gupta--Talwar SODA 2006). Graph 3-coloring inequality couplings are a different, NP-complete class. No transfer to a general lower bound is established.

\textbf{OPEN next target.} Add a bounded number of non-bijective inequality edges, source-check the exact quotient constraints on the permutation-component root colors, and charge discovery and supplied tree-decomposition DP. All-algorithm superpolynomial time remains OPEN.
