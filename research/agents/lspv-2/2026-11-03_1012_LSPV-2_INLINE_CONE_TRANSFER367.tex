\section*{New inlined semantic-scope compiler}
For each legal source-explicit shared refutation $\pi$, recursively inline every paid acyclic definition output $y$ into its Boolean function $\phi_y(x)$ on original variables $x$. Every DEF slot becomes a tautology. Boolean implication is preserved for RES (including auxiliary pivots), AND3, GF$(2)$ and any genuinely justified conversion. This is a mathematical representation change, not free physical computation. Let $I(\pi)$ be all non-DEF paid inference nodes. For each $j\in I(\pi)$, let $S_j$ be the \emph{syntactic expanded} original-variable support of its inlined Boolean conclusion. With $S_i$ for its at-most-two condition premises, define
\[
U_j=S_j\cup\bigcup_{i\in{\rm par}(j)}S_i,\qquad u_j=|U_j|.
\]
Syntactic support, rather than an oracle-computed minimal semantic support, is charged.

\begin{theorem}[Scoped paid-gate inlining]
For every normalized UNSAT source $F$ and every legal mixed proof $\pi$ under the audited paid-wire grammar, provided all LOCAL-CONVERT interface conditions admit the inlining just described, a strict source-only ordinary Resolution refutation of the \emph{same} original $F$ exists with
\[
\boxed{W_{\rm Res}(F)\le {\rm cost}_{\rm Res}(\tau(\pi))
 \le\sum_{j\in I(\pi)}2^{u_j+1}.}\tag{1}
\]
There is no separate global $2^b$ factor for the union of original inputs feeding VARDEF. Sharing of previously derived premise blockers is preserved.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent any Boolean condition $P$ on syntactic support $S$ by its complete signed assignment-blocking CNF: for every $a\in\{0,1\}^S$ falsifying $P$, retain its full blocker $B_{S,a}$. An original normalized clause is already its sole blocker and is an authenticated, free source. A tautological DEF slot has no blockers. Work in DAG topological order. To derive any false-output blocker $B_{S_j,\beta}$ for node $j$, examine each extension $\alpha$ of $\beta$ to $U_j$. By the node's soundness, at least one condition premise is false on $\alpha$. Reuse its already authenticated blocker $D_\alpha$, temporarily weaken it to full $B_{U_j,\alpha}$, and resolve the resulting full blockers on the $|U_j\setminus S_j|$ eliminated variables. At most $2^{u_j-|S_j|}$ weakenings and $2^{u_j-|S_j|}-1$ resolutions are needed for that conclusion blocker. Since at most $2^{|S_j|}$ conclusion assignments are false, all its blockers cost strictly fewer than $2^{u_j+1}$ temporary nodes, even if its conclusion is the empty-support false factor. Every shared antecedent blocker is reused.

Erase temporary weakening by stronger-subclause representatives: a weakened clause inherits its stronger antecedent; for resolution, either both pivots survive and an actual strict resolvent is formed, or a stronger parent missing its pivot is reused. Each representative remains a subclause of the corresponding old line; the final empty clause stays empty, no new source appears, and strict paid nodes do not increase. This yields (1).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Expander scope-mass obstruction]
For each sufficiently large cubic expander odd-Tseitin $F_h$ and every covered mixed refutation $\pi_h$,
\[
\boxed{2^{ch}\le 4h+\sum_{j\in I(\pi_h)}2^{u_j+1}.}\tag{2}
\]
If its mixed paid-node cost $q_h\le h^a$ for a fixed $a$ and its largest inlined scope is $s_h=\max_ju_j$, then
\[
\boxed{s_h\ge ch-a\log_2h-O(1)=\Omega(h).}\tag{3}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the published ordinary Resolution total-line lower bound to the translated strict proof from (1), including the $4h$ original clauses. Since $|I|\le q_h$, the right side is at most $4h+q_h2^{s_h+1}$. Rearrangement gives (3). No lower bound on $q_h$ alone follows.
\end{proof}
The previously known XOR shortcut uses large accumulated parity supports, so it \emph{satisfies} the scope obstruction rather than contradicting the theorem.
