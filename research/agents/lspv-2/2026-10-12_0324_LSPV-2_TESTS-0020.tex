Let $\mathcal H^{\pm}_{2,2}$ be proper, duplicate-free, sign-monochromatic
3-CNF in which every variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice
negatively.  D\"ocker proved SAT on this family NP-complete \cite{Docker19}.
For a formula $F$ and width $t$, define the isomorphism-invariant histogram
\[
 \Pi_t(F)(q)=\left|\left\{S\subseteq\operatorname{Var}(F):|S|=t,
 \ \left|\{a|_S:a\models F\}\right|=q\right\}\right| . \tag{1}
\]
The attacked implication was the following proposed intermediate, not a
theorem already established in the corpus:
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\Pi_t\text{ is nonconstant on an NP-hard signed clean family}\\
 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm unif}(\SAT),\\
 &\qquad\text{$A$ must expose, retain, reconstruct, or pay profile scale
 on the corresponding inputs.}
 \end{split} \tag{2}
\]
One exact total decider avoiding the profile on an infinite subfamily is
enough to falsify the unqualified universal conclusion in (2).  The theorem
below does not refute an existential worst-case lower bound with additional
premises.

\section{The signed compatibility easy island}

\begin{definition}[Paired-incidence formula]
For a simple cubic graph $G=(V,E)$ with one variable $x_e$ per edge, put
\[
 \Phi_G=\bigwedge_{v\in V}\left(
   \bigvee_{e\ni v}x_e\right)\wedge
   \left(\bigvee_{e\ni v}\neg x_e\right). \tag{3}
\]
Let $B_0=\Phi_{K_{3,3}}$ and let $B_1=\Phi_P$, where $P$ is the triangular
prism.  Let $\mathcal I$ contain all nonempty disjoint products of canonically
indexed copies of $B_0$ and $B_1$.
\end{definition}

Each block has nine variables and twelve clauses.  Every edge meets exactly
two vertices, so its variable occurs in two positive and two negative clauses.
Simplicity and cubicity give proper clauses; the two polarities in (3) give
sign-monochromatic clauses.  Direct comparison of the two graphs gives unique
clauses.  Hence $B_0,B_1\in\mathcal H^{\pm}_{2,2}$, and disjoint products
preserve the promise.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{SIGNED-COMPATIBILITY-EASY-ISLAND1}]
The following statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $B_0$ has $102$ satisfying assignments and $B_1$ has $86$.
\item Their first different projection histograms occur at $t=4$, where
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrr}
&q=12&q=14&q=16\\ \hline
\Pi_4(B_0)(q)&36&9&81\\
\Pi_4(B_1)(q)&36&21&69.
\end{array} \tag{4}
\]
Thus the semantic profile is nonconstant even between equal-size satisfiable
members of the signed clean family, and the distinction survives variable
renaming and clause reordering.
\item Every $F=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^k B_{b_i}\in\mathcal I$ is satisfiable and
\[
 |\operatorname{Mod}(F)|=102^{|\{i:b_i=0\}|}86^{|\{i:b_i=1\}|}. \tag{5}
\]
\item Under the declared canonical block encoding, membership in $\mathcal I$
is recognized in $O(N)$ time and $O(\log N)$ control space without enumerating
models or computing (1).  For every total uniform exact SAT decider $D$, the
wrapper
\[
 W_D(F)=\begin{cases}1,&F\in\mathcal I,\\D(F),&F\notin\mathcal I,
 \end{cases} \tag{6}
\]
is a total uniform exact SAT decider.  On $\mathcal I$, it performs zero
profile extractions and zero witness reconstructions.
\end{enumerate}
If $D$ is polynomial time (in particular under the counterfactual $P=NP$),
then $W_D$ is polynomial time as well.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For (i)--(ii), exhaustive enumeration of the $2^9$ assignments and all
$\binom 94$ four-coordinate projections proves the displayed finite facts;
the accompanying audit independently checks them.  Histogram (1) ranges over
all subsets, so it is invariant under a variable permutation, and model sets
do not depend on clause order.  For (iii), each factor has a model and the
variables are disjoint, so model sets form a Cartesian product, proving (5).
For (iv), split the indexed input into nine-variable/twelve-clause components
and compare each component to the two constant templates.  A streaming scan
uses a block counter and constant template state.  Equations (3)--(5) prove
that the YES branch of (6) is exact; the other branch inherits correctness
from $D$.  The recognizer, branch, fallback, and output belong to the same
machine, so no profile work is hidden in another realization.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
\texttt{NONCONSTANT-SIGNED-COMPATIBILITY-}\allowbreak
\texttt{OBLIGATION1} is \RETIRED.  Neither
the size equality of the two inputs nor the representation-invariant semantic
distinction in (4) forces an arbitrary exact decider to recover the profile.
This conclusion remains true when the fallback $D$ is wrapped by COPY, DEAD,
DELAY, INTERP, or RECOMP code with the same language.
\end{corollary}

The theorem is intentionally encoding-explicit.  A fully unlabelled graph
recognizer could try the constant $9!$ vertex/edge relabellings per fixed-size
component and remains linear in the number of components.  This does not make
profile extraction free; it shows that it is unnecessary for the answer on
this island.

\section{Barrier, SAME\_REALIZATION, and transfer audit}

The two obstacles identified before substantive work were: (a) source
specificity/nonconstancy does not imply decision necessity, and (b) a
restricted structural or protocol lower bound needs a solver-independent
compiler into the same random-access computation constrained by the endpoint.
Primary literature was checked first.  D\"ocker supplies the exact signed
hard family but no every-algorithm exposure theorem \cite{Docker19}.  FLMV
supplies model-specific time--space lower bounds, not a compiler from semantic
profiles to its charged hypotheses \cite{FLMV05}.  Relativization and
algebrization remain mandatory audits \cite{BGS75,AW09}.

\paragraph{SAME\_REALIZATION.}
The recognizer, input scan, branch, fallback $D$, and one-bit output in (6)
are one uniform deterministic machine.  Template comparison, all input reads,
and the output are charged.  No semantic oracle, offline canonicalizer,
unwritten witness, or uncharged preprocessing is used.  The profile is not
compressed; it is absent.

\paragraph{Barrier result.}
Construction (6) relativizes and algebraizes: replacing $D$ by an oracle or
algebraic-oracle decider leaves the easy branch unchanged.  It therefore
falsifies one route but does not evade the Baker--Gill--Solovay or
Aaronson--Wigderson barriers.  The property is confined to a tiny recognizable
island and is not asserted to be a large useful circuit property, so Natural
Proofs provides no positive bridge.

\paragraph{Cross-domain transfer taxonomy.}
The exact transferable fact from coding/algebra is that a projection, Schur,
quadratic-relation, Hilbert-function, or syzygy invariant can distinguish two
representations.  This is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER} only for
the invariant.  The present construction shows that detectability may be an
algorithmic weakness rather than a hardness source; transferring distinction
to arbitrary-computation cost is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.  Observability,
communication cuts, VLSI routing, proof search, and local-to-global gluing are
\textbf{useful structural analogies requiring a new transfer theorem}; their
restricted state, topology, or proof representation is not automatically the
state of (6).  Cryptographic binding, random oracles, and collision hardness
are \textbf{misleading analogies / failed transfers} for an unconditional
P-versus-NP proof.

\paragraph{Post-quantum ledger.}
The exact collision distribution of a profile map may still be informative,
and low-rank quadratic relations or higher syzygies may distinguish the two
blocks.  Here such structure is externally exploitable and unnecessary for
SAT decision.  Hence these are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge}
to a universal cost statement, and \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} if nonconstancy
alone is taken as that bridge.  No hardness or random-oracle assumption is
admissible.

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}

Any wording that promotes ``$H(F)$ varies on an NP-hard promise'' directly to
``every SAT decider must compute or pay for $H(F)$'' is overstated.  NP-hardness
of the ambient promise is compatible with infinite, recognizable constant-
answer islands.  Any proof that counts the $102$ versus $86$ models as runtime
without a computation transfer is also invalid.  The all-positive exact-two-
occurrence family remains retired as a hard core per LB-0023.

\section{Precise handoff}

The missing implication is \texttt{ANSWER-RELEVANT-TWO-WAY-ONLINE-TRANSFER1}
(\OPEN): construct a fixed pair of signed-clean instances whose required
later responses have disjoint valid fibers in the same realization, prove
that every exact static SAT decider induces the charged online realization
with exponents independent of the unknown solver exponent, and connect that
exact realization to a model-matched unconditional lower bound.  It must
survive the easy island above, constant-output and recomputation wrappers,
Horn/2SAT/affine/bounded-treewidth controls, independent products, padding,
redundant clauses, alternate encodings, semantic oracles, and algebraic
oracles.  Until this transfer and endpoint are proved, P versus NP remains
\OPEN.
