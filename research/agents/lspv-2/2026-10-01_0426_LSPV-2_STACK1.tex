\paragraph{Setup.}
Fix one SAME\_REALIZATION family of legal source states \(s\in\mathcal S\). Under the explicit Hadamard realization, let
\[
X_s\in\mathbb F_2^{m\times b},
\qquad
g(s,u)=X_su,\qquad u\in\mathbb F_2^b.
\]
Let
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2,
\]
and let \(\mathcal Q_m\) be the \(M\)-dimensional space of squarefree Boolean polynomials in \(y_1,\ldots,y_m\) of degree at most two. For each \(s\), define the linear pullback
\[
T_s:\mathcal Q_m\longrightarrow \mathcal Q_b,
\qquad
T_s(Q)(u)=Q(X_su),
\]
where \(u_a^2=u_a\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: stacked-pullback identity.}
Let
\[
H=\bigcup_{s\in\mathcal S}\operatorname{im}X_s.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
I_{\le2}(H)=\bigcap_{s\in\mathcal S}\ker T_s
}
\]
because \(Q\) vanishes on \(H\) if and only if \(Q(X_su)=0\) for every legal \(s\) and every \(u\). Hence
\[
\boxed{
\rho(H)
=
M-\dim I_{\le2}(H)
=
\operatorname{rank}\!\left(\bigoplus_{s\in\mathcal S}T_s\right).
}
\]
This converts the explicit-Hadamard product-span question into ordinary linear algebra and does not require a bound on the Boolean degree of the source-side functions \(s\mapsto X_s\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: one-slice rank.}
Write \(r_s=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}X_s\). The image of \(X_s\) is an \(r_s\)-dimensional linear subspace. Restriction of degree-at-most-two Boolean functions to that subspace has dimension
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}T_s
=
D(r_s):=1+r_s+\binom{r_s}{2}.
}
\]
This is the affine-block obstruction specialized to a linear Hadamard slice.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: accumulation bounds and finite certificate.}
For every finite \(\mathcal S_0\subseteq\mathcal S\),
\[
\max_{s\in\mathcal S_0}D(r_s)
\le
\operatorname{rank}\!\left(\bigoplus_{s\in\mathcal S_0}T_s\right)
\le
\min\!\left\{M,\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_0}D(r_s)\right\}.
\]
Ranks are monotone as legal source states are added. If the full stacked rank is \(M\), then there exists a subset
\[
\boxed{\mathcal S_\star\subseteq\mathcal S,\qquad |\mathcal S_\star|\le M}
\]
whose stacked pullback already has rank \(M\). Proof: start with the zero stack and add only a source state that strictly increases rank; each addition raises the integer rank by at least one, and rank never exceeds \(M\). Thus maximal quadratic rank has a finite SAME\_REALIZATION certificate involving at most \(M\) legal source states.

Conversely, if the legal source family is finite and
\[
\sum_{s\in\mathcal S}D(r_s)<M,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)<M}
\]
and a nonzero global quadratic annihilator exists. This upper-bound criterion is exact linear algebra, although it is expected to be weak when \(|\mathcal S|\) is large.

\paragraph{Explicit pullback equations.}
For
\[
Q(y)=c+\sum_i\alpha_i y_i+\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}y_iy_j,
\]
the identity \(T_s(Q)=0\) is equivalent to vanishing of all coefficients in the Boolean mask \(u\). The constant coefficient is \(c\). For every \(a\),
\[
\sum_i\alpha_i x_{i,a}(s)
+
\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}x_{i,a}(s)x_{j,a}(s)=0,
\]
and for every \(a<c\),
\[
\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}
\left(
x_{i,a}(s)x_{j,c}(s)+x_{i,c}(s)x_{j,a}(s)
\right)=0.
\]
These equations give the exact rows of the stacked matrix. A full-rank \(M\times M\) minor of these rows is an explicit \(\Omega(m^2)\) independent minor and retires the quadratic-annihilator route for this realization.

\paragraph{Sparse-\(Q\) consequence and cost.}
If the exact stacked rank is \(r_2<M\), the existing Singleton argument gives a nonzero quadratic annihilator with at most \(r_2+1\) active ANF monomials. A monomial-list representation uses
\[
O(r_2\log m)
\]
bits and direct Boolean evaluation uses \(O(r_2)\) elementary AND/XOR operations up to constant-factor conventions. Existence does not imply acquisition; constructing this kernel vector from a succinct exponentially large source family remains separately charged.

\paragraph{Collision entropy branch.}
For a finite accepted proof-to-node map \(L:A\to V\),
\[
|\operatorname{im}L|\ge \frac{|A|^2}{\operatorname{Coll}(L)}
\]
remains exact. For a homomorphism the kernel-energy identity remains exact as well. \paragraph{Hostile controls and barriers.}
Affine relabelings of ambient Boolean coordinates preserve degree-at-most-two function space, but arbitrary nonlinear encoder relabelings need not preserve the stacked matrix and must be audited. DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, proof padding, ordinary padding, renaming and auxiliary variables do not convert rank into runtime. A maximal semantic oracle can reveal \(H\) and its rank, and an algebraic oracle can expose the pullback equations, so the theorem is relativizing/algebrizing. Horn, 2-SAT, affine/Tseitin with Gaussian elimination, and bounded-treewidth controls show that maximal product span itself is not SAT hardness.
