\section*{Fixed objects and prior result}
The target is general binary-encoded CNF, including SAT, UNSAT, malformed strings, and an exact declared-variable encoding. $N=|\operatorname{enc}F|$ is \emph{bit length}, not number of variables. For valid UNSAT $F$, $W_R(F)$ is the minimum number of \emph{distinct} paid inference nodes in a shared source-labelled refutation DAG; original clauses are free. $\overline W_R(N)$ is the maximum $W_R(F)$ over valid UNSAT encodings of at most $N$ bits, and zero if none. The unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ comprises strict binary RES, exact three-variable AND3, binary GF$(2)$ XOR, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative source-independent signed two-input VARDEF. No arbitrary SAT-label oracle or unit-cost arbitrary-premise semantic refutation is allowed. We count source/index/serialization work separately from logical nodes. All derived nodes have unit cost.

Here we refine its loss to \emph{each individual inference scope}, not a uniform maximum. This is not a claimed discovery of a new unconditional Resolution or SAT lower bound.

\section*{Finer compiler}
Fix one syntactically valid mixed proof $\pi$ with $q$ paid nodes and $m$ authenticated sources. Let $g$ count paid VARDEF nodes. Let $B$ be the \emph{set} of original variables appearing directly as input literals to any VARDEF in $\pi$, and $b=|B|\le 2g$. On assigning $B$, every VARDEF output is determined topologically, even for nested gates. No auxiliary value is independently guessed.

Let $r$ be the number of ordinary RES nodes, and $J$ the set of all paid nodes whose rule is neither RES nor VARDEF. For each $j\in J$, syntactically evaluate the exposed charged auxiliary wires and $B$, then let $U_j$ be the union of \emph{remaining original variables} occurring in its premise and conclusion conditions, and $d_j=|U_j|$. This is syntactic scope, not a free semantic support-minimization oracle. Set $d=\max_{j\in J}d_j$, with $d=0$ if $J$ is empty.

\begin{theorem}[Per-inference reverse translation, scoped]\label{thm:compiler}
Provided every exposed wire is either original or an output of a charged source-independent conservative two-input VARDEF, and condition factors admit their explicit falsifying-assignment CNFs, there is a strict source-only ordinary Resolution refutation $\tau(\pi)$ of the \emph{same} normalized original CNF such that
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{cost}_{\mathrm{Res}}(\tau(\pi))
\ \le\
2^b\left(m+r+2\sum_{j\in J}2^{d_j}+1\right)-1 .
}
\tag{1}
\]
This is a proof-existence \emph{upper} bound on translated proof nodes. It does not bound discovery time by $q$, does not cover uncharged named internal circuit wires, and does not assert global minimum optimality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every assignment $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^B$, substitute $\alpha$ and evaluate every conservative definition in topological order. Every defining clause is now true, so no unsound independent gate-output assumption enters. Ordinary RES either remains a strict RES inference or simplifies to a previously available stronger clause, using at most one paid step.

For a non-RES/non-DEF inference $j$, take the canonical full-assignment-blocking CNF of every Boolean premise condition over its remaining support. Likewise represent its conclusion condition by the clauses blocking precisely its falsifying assignments. Fix one false conclusion assignment on its support $V\subseteq U_j$. For each completion on $U_j\setminus V$, at least one antecedent condition must be false by soundness. An authenticated blocking clause for that antecedent is therefore available from its previously derived canonical CNF. Weaken it to the full blocking clause for the completion, then eliminate the unassigned variables using a binary Resolution decision tree. The entire tree costs at most $2^{|U_j\setminus V|}$ weakenings plus $2^{|U_j\setminus V|}-1$ resolutions. Across at most $2^{|V|}$ false conclusion assignments, the total is strictly below $2^{|U_j|+1}$. For a clause conclusion its canonical blocking CNF contains the original clause itself; for a factor all blocking clauses are retained and reused as shared proof nodes. Trivial true factors need none.

Lift each restricted branch proof to the original sources by adjoining its $B$-assignment blocker as a guard, using at most $m$ source weakenings. This produces the complementary guard clause for $\alpha$; the $2^b$ guard clauses combine with at most $2^b-1$ binary resolutions. Hence the entire Resolution-plus-weakening DAG has at most the displayed number of paid nodes.

Eliminate weakening with \emph{subclause representatives} in topological order. A weakened clause inherits its antecedent's stronger representative. In each Resolution inference either both pivot literals remain and one takes their strict resolvent, or a pivot disappears from a representative and that stronger premise is reused directly. The representative is always a subclause of the original line, and the empty final clause remains empty. Thus the resulting strict Resolution proof has no more paid nodes than the weakened derivation. Every source remains authenticated. Sharing within each branch is preserved.
\end{proof}

The upper bound (1) is no larger than the previous $2^b(m+q2^{d+1}+1)-1$, because $r+2\sum_j 2^{d_j}\le q2^{d+1}$. It therefore adds information about \emph{which steps} consume the lower-bound budget, not a speculative new proof strength.

\section*{Transfer from established lower bound}
\begin{corollary}[Pigeonhole resource-profile obstruction]
Any proof $\pi$ of the standard $\mathrm{PHP}(p,h)$ covered by Theorem~\ref{thm:compiler} obeys
\[
\boxed{
b+\log_2\!\left(2m+r+2\sum_{j\in J}2^{d_j}+1\right)
\ \ge\
\frac{\log_2(3/2)}{50}\frac{h^2}{p}-1 .
}
\tag{2}
\]
Let $c=\log_2(3/2)/50$. For $p=h+1$, let $d=\max_jd_j$. Then
\[
\boxed{
q\ \ge\
\frac{2^{c h^2/(h+1)-b-1}-2m-1}
{1+2^{d+1}} .
}
\tag{3}
\]
In particular, for any fixed $0<\epsilon<c$, the \emph{restricted class} of proofs satisfying $b+d\le(c-\epsilon)h$ has $q\ge 2^{\Omega_\epsilon(h)}$ for all sufficiently large $h$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply (BT) to the source-only strict Resolution proof from Theorem~\ref{thm:compiler}. Its total source-plus-derived line count is at most $m+2^b(m+r+2\sum 2^{d_j}+1)-1$, itself at most $2^b(2m+r+2\sum2^{d_j}+1)$. Take binary logarithms to obtain (2). Using $r\le q$, $|J|\le q$ and $\sum_j2^{d_j}\le q2^d$, rearrange (2) to obtain (3). For $p=h+1$, $c h^2/(h+1)=ch-O(1)$, and $m=O(h^3)$. When $b+d\le(c-\epsilon)h$, the exponential term in (3) dominates the polynomial $m$ for sufficiently large $h$. This proves the \emph{restricted} exponential lower bound, with constants independent of an individual input but dependent on $\epsilon$.
\end{proof}

For polynomial $q$, (2) forces either a sufficiently large set of original definition inputs or substantial aggregate non-RES scope mass. This is \emph{not} a lower bound for the full free-choice mixed calculus: polynomial Extended Resolution proofs of pigeonhole formulas use the extension escape; parity/Tseitin systems use the high-$d$ GF$(2)$ escape. Nor is (2) a transfer to arbitrary deterministic SAT time, and no inequality is reversed. The minimal sufficient conditional B3 remains
\[
\mathrm{SAT}\in P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists C,k\ \forall N\quad
\overline W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(N)\le C(N+1)^k
\tag{B3}
\]
for the \emph{same} fixed $R$, not some hypothetical replacement proof system. B1$\Rightarrow$B2$\Rightarrow$B3, and none is established here.

\section*{Finite falsifiers, test scope, accounting}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{F1, a few wide algebraic inferences:} using $\max_jd_j$ overcharges every step. Theorem~\ref{thm:compiler} tracks $\sum 2^{d_j}$ separately; it does \emph{not} infer a node lower bound when one broad step is permitted.
\item[] \textbf{F2, uncharged nested auxiliary outputs:} assigning $B$ rather than individual auxiliary bits retains conservative dependencies. An uncharged exposed named wire in LOCAL-CONVERT invalidates the theorem's syntactic premise; this is OPEN for the exact full-system grammar.
\item[] \textbf{F3, Tseitin GF$(2)$ shortcut:} previous exact $4h-1$ node odd bridgeless cubic Tseitin refutations remain valid. Their global XOR scopes can grow with $h$, so (2) cannot be misused to call those instances mixed-hard.
\item[] \textbf{F4, malformed/incorrect local conclusions and erased weakening:} the finite compiler rejects an unsound $(\neg z)$ consequence, duplicate-opposite literals, an out-of-range variable, an even-parity SAT false refutation, and a corrupted final pivot.
\end{enumerate}

Constructing each local decision tree requires reading its premises, source IDs, and heads. A safe work envelope is polynomial in $L+N+q$ multiplied by the total generated tree size, at least $2^b\left(m+r+\sum 2^{d_j}\right)$ in the naive implementation, with additional checking and output costs. The materialized proof contains at most $O((N+Q+1)^2)$ coarse serialization bits (since only $N$ original source bits/indices plus $Q$ new node IDs occur), but actual guest execution/schedules are separate.
