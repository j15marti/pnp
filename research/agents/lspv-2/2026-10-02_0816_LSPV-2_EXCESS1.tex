\paragraph{Setup.}
Fix one SAME\_REALIZATION legal slice family and let
\[
U\subseteq \bigwedge\nolimits^2\mathbb F_2^m,
\qquad d=\dim U.
\]
Choose a full-row-rank matrix \(G\) whose row space is \(U\). Its columns are indexed by the quadratic monomials
\[
E=\{\{i,j\}:1\le i<j\le m\}.
\]
The common alternating quadratic-relation code is
\[
K=U^\perp=\ker G.
\]
For a prescribed Boolean-variable set \(I\subseteq[m]\), write
\[
E(I)=\{\{i,j\}:i,j\in I,\ i<j\}
\]
and
\[
K_I^{\rm var}
=
\{q\in K:\operatorname{supp}(q)\subseteq E(I)\}.
\]

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: excess-local-nullity decomposition.}
By MATCIRC1 rank-nullity on the restricted column set,
\[
\boxed{
\dim K_I^{\rm var}
=
\binom{|I|}{2}
-
\operatorname{rank}G_{E(I)}.
}
\]
Define
\[
\epsilon(I)
=
d-\operatorname{rank}G_{E(I)}\ge0.
\]
Then exactly
\[
\boxed{
\dim K_I^{\rm var}
=
\binom{|I|}{2}-d+\epsilon(I).
}
\]

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The term
\[
\binom{|I|}{2}-d
\]
is a universal dimension baseline. Whenever it is positive, every \(d\)-dimensional subspace of the quadratic-coordinate space has at least that much local nullity on a clique of size \(|I|\). This baseline is not source-specific evidence for SU/vMS, cyclic windows, low degree, local decoding, or computational hardness.

The architecture-sensitive quantity is
\[
\boxed{
\epsilon(I)=d-\operatorname{rank}G_{E(I)}.
}
\]
For two exterior spaces \(U,U'\) having the same dimension \(d\),
\[
\dim K_I^{\rm var}
-
\dim K_I^{\prime\,\rm var}
=
\epsilon(I)-\epsilon'(I).
\]
Thus, after conditioning on \(d\), excess local nullity rather than raw local-kernel dimension is the relevant construction-specific statistic.

\paragraph{Correction to the recent local-relation interpretation.}
The mathematically correct statement
\[
d=O(m)
\Longrightarrow
\text{many relations exist in every sufficiently large }O(\sqrt m)\text{-variable clique}
\]
is not by itself a source-specific distinguisher. Its dominant contribution can be forced solely by ambient dimension. Likewise, the exact support identity
\[
K_I^{\rm var}\cap K_J^{\rm var}
=
K_{I\cap J}^{\rm var}
\]
is generic for any relation code on these monomial coordinates. Persistence of the universal baseline under overlapping shortenings therefore does not become architecture-specific merely because it glues.

Accordingly, the claim that mere abundance or dimension of ubiquitous \(O(\sqrt m)\)-variable relation spaces demonstrates special exploitable SU/vMS structure is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED AS A DISTINGUISHING SIGNAL}. The relations remain explicitly computable once \(G\) is acquired; the correction is only that their baseline abundance adds no information beyond small \(d\).
