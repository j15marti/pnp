\section{Reverse SAT, exact model and source accounting}
Input is proper duplicate-free signed 3CNF with $n$ named original sources, $m$ clauses, exactly two positive and two negative appearances per source. Hence $4n=3m$. Use $w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil$-bit addressed words and input length $N=(4m+2)w$. Charge all parsing, source reads, sorting, failed comparisons, transformation output, bit flips, checking, unsuccessful work, reconstruction and source replay. A fan-in-two evaluation circuit has $2m$ OR2 gates, $m-1$ AND2 gates, $3m-1$ gates and $6m-2$ input wires when signed literal values are supplied. One shared NOT1 per source adds $n$ gates and wires. Requiring output TRUE is equivalent to choosing at least one TRUE literal occurrence per clause with no opposite requirements on the same original source. This does not require exactly one TRUE literal per clause. In exact signed $(2,2)$, the occurrence graph has $4n$ vertices and $n$ disjoint $K_{2,2}$ complement-conflict components with $4n$ edges. Adding clause triangles gives $8n$ edges, degree four, and SAT iff it contains an independent transversal of $m$ occurrence vertices. These are assignment-evaluation and representation facts, not unrestricted time lower bounds.

\section{Signed complementary pairing}
For an unsigned sorted triple $S$, write $c_{S,p}$ for the multiplicity of the clause with sign pattern $p\in\{0,1\}^3$. Let $\mathcal O$ consist of the four complement orbits $\{p,\bar p\}$. A pair of original clauses differing in all three signs is precisely the signed NAE constraint
\[
(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)\wedge
(\neg\ell_1\vee\neg\ell_2\vee\neg\ell_3)
=\operatorname{NAE}(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3).
\]
\begin{theorem}[Optimal extraction in the declared calculus; PROVED/EXACT]
For strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ source 3CNF, the minimum number of unmatched clause records when only full-sign-complement pairs may be extracted is
\[
t_{\pm}=m-2\sum_S\sum_{\{p,\bar p\}\in\mathcal O}
\min(c_{S,p},c_{S,\bar p}).
\]
All locally optimal pairs are simultaneously selectable and every original variable belongs to at most two extracted NAE supports. A source-labelled certificate is found and checked in $O(m\log(m+2))$ word time, $O(n+m)$ live words. This refines the older DUALPAIR61 monotone-only defect $t_{\pm}\le t_{\rm mono}$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof. A pair uses one record from each complementary pattern, giving the displayed independent orbit upper bounds. Select that many pairs per orbit. Every selected pair uses exactly one positive and one negative occurrence of each involved original variable. Two of each polarity exist in the entire source, so all orbit maxima can be attained jointly without violating the at-most-two-support condition. Sorting signed triples and a second clause-record replay certify the output. \hfill$\square$

\section{Direct constructive token algorithm}
Let $\mathcal C$ be $q$ proper signed NAE3 constraints with each original source in at most two constraints. Start with any source assignment and call a constraint BAD iff all three signed literals have the same truth value. Choose any BAD constraint. Flip a source variable distinct from the edge by which this BAD token arrived, choosing the smallest named eligible variable. This repairs the current clause. If the other constraint incident to this source becomes BAD, move the token there, recording the incoming variable. Otherwise stop. Repeat at another BAD constraint.

\begin{theorem}[Signed NAE token repair, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
The walk procedure terminates with a satisfying original-source assignment in at most $q$ walks and $q^2$ original-bit flips. Its time is $O(q^2+n)$ and live space $O(q+n)$ after incidence parsing, with an independently replayable stream of at most $q^2$ flip records.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof. A flip repairs the current BAD constraint, and can affect only one other NAE constraint because its named source appears in at most two. Thus each move either reduces the number of BAD constraints or transfers precisely one BAD token. No walk can repeat a constraint: on leaving a BAD constraint, its flipped departure variable is its unique minority signed literal. Until the first claimed repetition none of its source variables can change again, except on that first incoming edge. Such a return cannot be along its own departure edge, since that edge leads to an already visited successor and would force an earlier repetition; immediate edge reversal is forbidden. Returning along a different edge flips a majority literal, leaving the clause NAE-satisfied, so the token cannot arrive BAD. Thus a walk visits at most $q$ distinct clauses and ends with one fewer BAD clause. Initially there are at most $q$ BAD clauses, giving at most $q^2$ flips. All repairs flip named original bits coherently; each NAE pair corresponds to exactly two source clauses, and replay verifies the whole input. \hfill$\square$

Consequently the $t_{\pm}=0$ strict signed-exact family is SAT and has a uniform witness constructor in $O(m\log(m+2)+m^2+n)$ word time, linear live words. When $t_{\pm}>0$ the previous BLOSSOM-FACTOR60 $3^{t_{\pm}}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ witness/graph-factor fallback remains available, charging all failed branches and matching work; no all-input polynomial-time bound follows.
