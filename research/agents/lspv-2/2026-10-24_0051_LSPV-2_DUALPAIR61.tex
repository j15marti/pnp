\section{Question, hypotheses, fixed source language}
We test (M1) whether the number of unmatched opposite-monotone support clauses is minimized by a source-only polynomial compiler on strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF; (M2) whether that defect and the complementary-pivot contraction defect bound each other; and (M3) whether any two precolored source edges extend through a paired-NAE cubic support. The first two have proofs; M3 is a computational candidate only. The guiding universal representation-versus-other-method cost dichotomy is OPEN and is not assumed.

A valid input consists of $m$ distinct proper three-literal clauses over consecutively numbered $n$ original sources, with two positive and two negative appearances of each source. All clauses retain their signed original source identities. Thus $4n=3m$, and, for $n=3h$, $m=4h$. We use $(4m+2)$ addressed input words of width $w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil$, total bits $N=(4m+2)w$. Parsing, source reads, sorting, construction, every unsuccessful attempt, certificate checking, reconstruction and original source replay are charged at RAM-word cost.

\section{Reverse circuit and occurrence selector}
For $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$, a fan-in-two evaluation circuit has $2m$ OR2 gates and $m-1$ AND2 gates. Its output is TRUE iff a TRUE occurrence can be selected from every clause with no same-named source selected in opposite polarities. A consistent choice assigns each selected signed source literal TRUE and arbitrary values to all unselected source bits. Clauses may have multiple TRUE literals, so these are witness selectors, \emph{not} exactly-one constraints. With signed literal inputs supplied, the evaluator has $3m-1$ gates, $6m-2$ directed gate-input wires, and $9m-2$ functional Tseitin clauses including final output=TRUE. Supplying a NOT1 gate per source adds $n$ gates, $n$ input wires, and $2n$ Tseitin clauses. These counts apply to assignment evaluation and not to a SAT-solving circuit.

In proper signed exact-$(2,2)$, there are $4n=3m$ occurrence vertices, $4n$ complementary-occurrence conflict edges in disjoint $K_{2,2}$s, and $3m=4n$ within-clause triangle edges. The augmented degree-four graph contains $8n$ edges. An independent occurrence transversal of size $m$ exists iff SAT. It need not be explicitly enumerated.

\section{Certified canonical defect}
For each unsigned triple support $S=\{a,b,c\}$, let $p_S$ and $q_S$ be its counts of all-positive and all-negative original clauses. A paired support consists of one original clause of each sign and imposes $1\le a+b+c\le2$. This calculus extracts disjoint opposite-sign identical supports while using any original variable in at most two extracted supports. Every unpaired source clause is a defect.

\begin{theorem}[Source-exact optimal pairing; PROVED/EXACT restricted]
On every valid signed exact-$(2,2)$ formula $F$, the smallest possible number of defects in this declared calculus is
\[
t_{\min}(F)=m-2\sum_S\min(p_S,q_S).
\]
The optimum and a signed-clause multiplicity certificate are constructed and independently checked in $O(m\log(m+2))$ RAM-word time and $O(n+m)$ live words. The parameter is even and invariant under source renaming and clause permutations.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} Every pair consumes one positive and one negative clause of exactly the same support $S$, hence at most $\min(p_S,q_S)$ pairs can use $S$. Selecting all these pairs simultaneously is legal: a source variable has only two original positive and two original negative occurrences, so can occur in at most two selected pairs. Thus the local upper bounds are simultaneously attained. The remaining source-labelled records are precisely the defects. Since $m=4h$, subtracting an even number of clauses makes $t_{\min}$ even; mixed-sign clauses do not invalidate this parity argument. Deterministic signature sorting and a second independent source-record pass certify all counts and the source bijection. \hfill$\square$

The theorem does not claim optimality among arbitrary SAT normalizers, and it need not hold under the same per-variable support cap on general formulas without the exact occurrence promise. BLOSSOM-FACTOR60 previously supplied the graph-factor continuation and $3^t\operatorname{poly}(N)$ upper bound; the contribution here is the optimal parameter discovery guarantee under the stronger exact source grammar.

\section{Two equal-length extreme families}
\begin{theorem}[Opposite defect extremes, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every $q\ge3$ two strict, duplicate-free, source-only signed exact-$(2,2)$ formulas $P_q,Q_q$ have the same $n=3q$, $m=4q$ and identical encoded input length $N$, yet
\[
(t_{\min}(P_q),\delta(P_q))=(0,4q),\qquad
(t_{\min}(Q_q),\delta(Q_q))=(4q,0).
\]
Both are SAT and each has a canonical linear-word recognizer and witness construction. Here $\delta$ is Role 1's distinct syntactic defect after contracting every unique pivot pair $(a\vee b\vee x)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg x)\equiv(a\vee b)$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Construction and proof.} For $P_q$, let the sources be the $3q$ edges of cubic prism $C_q\square K_2$. At each of its $2q$ vertices add the positive OR3 clause on its three incident edge sources and the negative OR3 clause on those same sources. Each edge occurs with both signs at each endpoint, so has two appearances of each polarity. All $2q$ supports pair exactly. No two clauses differ only in the sign of one pivot literal while sharing the other two signed literals: the positive/negative same-support copies differ in all three signs. Thus $t_{\min}=0,\delta=4q$. The assignment making all $2q$ cycle edges TRUE and all $q$ rung edges FALSE gives two TRUE and one FALSE edge at every support, satisfying both signs.

For $Q_q$, use $x_i,y_i,z_i$ with cyclic $i\in\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$. For every $i$ add
\[
(y_i\vee\neg y_{i+1}\vee x_i),\
(y_i\vee\neg y_{i+1}\vee\neg x_i),\
(z_i\vee\neg z_{i+1}\vee x_i),\
(z_i\vee\neg z_{i+1}\vee\neg x_i).
\]
Each $x_i$ occurs exactly twice per sign and each $y_i,z_i$ occurs twice positively in its outgoing pivot pair and twice negatively in the incoming pair. All clauses are proper and distinct for $q\ge3$. No clause is monotone, so $t_{\min}=4q$. Each clause has a complementary pivot partner and $\delta=0$. Contracting the pairs yields two implication cycles on the $y$ and $z$ source variables. Each entire cycle must be constant; all $q$ pivot sources $x_i$ are free, giving exactly $4\cdot 2^q$ original models. In particular every source FALSE is a satisfying assignment. Parsing and signed source replay independently verify each claim in linear word work on these canonical families. \hfill$\square$

At $q=60$, both have $n=180,m=240,w=15,N=14{,}430$ bits. The first has $t=0,\delta=240$; the other has $t=240,\delta=0$. High values of either parameter cannot force every solver to pay an exponential cost.

\section{Charged continuation, independent controls, and cross-role comparison}
Role 2's previously established exact source-coherent solver selects one TRUE literal per defect, checks contradictory source requirements, and extends each consistent branch through polynomial-time degree-interval graph-factor matching on the paired supports. Its worst-case upper bound is $O(N/w+m\log(m+2)+3^{t_{\min}}(n+m)^3)$ under the declared implementation; polynomial peak working words suffice. Role 1's previously established complementary-pivot matching-cover continuation has $O(m\log m+\delta^4+2^{\lfloor5\delta/8\rfloor}(n+m+1))$ time. The improved SIMPLECOVER76 result refines the branching exponent for an additional simple-graph promise, not for arbitrary pivot multigraphs. MAIN101 already proved that alternating two exact deciders attains a factor-two overhead versus whichever halts first. No portfolio claim is newly invented here.

Each family also has a direct linear-time source witness, showing why neither optional normalization is mandatory. In the cost identity $T=C_{\rm discovery}+C_{\rm normalization}+C_{\rm verification}+C_{\rm continuation}$, canonical pairing discovery and verification are $O(m\log(m+2))$, writing original-labelled paired/defect records is $O(m)$, and continuation is charged for \emph{every} branch and matching construction. Reconstructing original variables and rereading $3m$ signed fields to replay a SAT witness is charged; an UNSAT answer may use complete certified search rather than an assumed short certificate. The pairing certificate writes $O(m)$ records and uses $O(n+m)$ peak live words, conservatively $O((n+m)^2)$ when an earlier blossom matching implementation is used. These are algorithm-specific upper bounds and output-format costs, not all-decider time lower bounds.

The deterministic stdlib program with seed 202610230838 performed 1,375 counted assertions and zero failures, tested $q=3,4,5,6,8,12,20,60$ for both families, exhaustively checked 44 small regular-source cases, and audited 60 randomized strict exact-$(2,2)$ formulas. Two source-labelled both-label controls were a full eight-clause three-variable UNSAT cube and a same-size SAT sign flip; they are explicitly \emph{outside} the exact-$(2,2)$ promise. The project's earlier independently reconstructed D\"ocker 198-variable, 264-clause strict-monotone UNSAT and a sign-degree-preserving SAT mutation remain the in-language both-label source controls from DOCKER-QUOTIENT59, but their complete records were not reimported or rerun here. A further exhaustive small-hypergraph experiment examined 863 maximum-degree-two 3-uniform support hypergraphs on at most six sources and all two-bit precolorings; all extended. A universal two-pin extension statement is \textbf{CANDIDATE/OPEN}, not proved and not used in either theorem.
