\section{Exact both-label transaction lemma}
Let $F$ be an original ordered CNF, and suppose a prior sequence of \emph{complete} Davis--Putnam elimination steps has produced $G\equiv\exists P\,F$ pointwise on all remaining variables. Partition $G$ into clauses $G^0$ excluding pivot $x$, positive parents $(x\vee A_i)$ and negative parents $(\neg x\vee B_j)$. Each non-tautological resolvent $R_{ij}=A_i\vee B_j$ has a stored source-parent DAG.

\begin{theorem}[Exact aborted or committed step]
For every (possibly empty) subset $H$ of genuine parent resolvents,
\[
 G\wedge\bigwedge H\ \equiv\ G.
\]
Thus a budget-aborted policy may add the derived $H$ \emph{without deleting any pivot parent}. If all genuine parent resolvents are produced, complete elimination may instead commit
\[
 G^0\wedge\bigwedge_{i,j}R_{ij}\ \equiv\ \exists x\,G.
\]
Every satisfying retained-variable assignment lifts to an original $F$ witness by reverse bucket reconstruction; an UNSAT result on the completed projection refutes $F$. Each derived clause is entailed by the original source and the cube falsifying it is prohibited.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$(x\vee A_i)\wedge(\neg x\vee B_j)\models A_i\vee B_j$, so retaining any subset does not change $G$. For the full set, a model of $G$ satisfies every resolvent. Conversely, with all resolvents satisfied, if some $A_i$ is false, every $B_j$ must be true and $x=1$ satisfies all parents. If all $A_i$ are true, $x=0$ satisfies all parents. Reversing completed buckets reconstructs the source witness. Inducting through saved parent pairs gives ordinary original-clause resolution provenance. The identities hold pointwise for every retained assignment, for both source labels.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Counterexample to unsafe commit.}
\[
F=(x\vee a)\wedge(\neg x\vee b)\wedge\neg a\wedge\neg b
\]
is UNSAT; deleting the two $x$ parents after a zero-resolvent aborted trial yields $\neg a\wedge\neg b$, which is SAT. The missing required resolvent is $(a\vee b)$.
