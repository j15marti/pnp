\section*{Published starting theorem}
For $\ell\ge32$, $n=2^\ell$, let $B_\ell$ be the ordinary bit pigeonhole CNF with $(n+1)\ell$ bit variables and $m=n\binom{n+1}{2}$ original clauses
\[
D_{i,j,a}=\bigvee_{t=1}^\ell(x_{i,t}\ne a_t)\vee\bigvee_{t=1}^\ell(x_{j,t}\ne a_t)
\quad (i<j,\ a\in\{0,1\}^\ell).
\]

\tag{B}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Local semantic expansion]\label{lem}
For each extensional semantic step $j$, the output ordinary clause, unit affine row, or entire canonical blocking-CNF Boolean factor has a source-authenticated Res$(\oplus)$ translation with at most $2^{d_j+1}$ new lines. An unrestricted-width GF$(2)$ unit addition has a two-line translation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Represent each Boolean factor on support $T$ by the clauses $D_a=\bigvee_{z\in T}(z\ne a(z))$ for its falsifying assignments. For any target clause $C$ over $T\subseteq S_j$, fix the variables in $T$ to the unique pattern falsifying $C$, and enumerate assignments $u$ to $S_j\setminus T$. At every full assignment, some premise is false by soundness. Its available canonical blocking clause (or unit affine equation) entails $C\vee D_u$, where $D_u$ blocks that assignment on the unfixed variables. One sound semantic weakening derives each such guarded leaf. Complementary-variable cuts merge the $2^{d_j-|T|}$ leaves to obtain $C$ with at most $2^{d_j-|T|+1}-1$ lines. Summing over at most $2^{|T|}$ target factor blockers yields fewer than $2^{d_j+1}$ lines. A unit affine equation of support at least two uses the full splitting tree, with singleton pivots distinct from that equation; a one-variable affine equation is treated as a literal. Degenerate true/false constraints require no extra cost. For unit affine $E_1,E_2$, put $E_3=E_1\oplus E_2$. Weaken $E_1$ to $E_3\vee\neg E_2$, then resolve with $E_2$. This costs two lines independent of affine width.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Heterogeneous native-affine backward simulation]

\[
\boxed{S_\oplus(F)\le2^{b+d_*}(2m+2q+2).}\tag{2}

\begin{proof}

In each branch, replay the restricted RES clauses, native affine additions and the expansions of Lemma 1. A restricted RES step takes at most two lines including possible subclause regularization. A restricted source is derived from its authentic original source by at most one semantic weakening, so the cost is at most $m$ extra lines per branch. All cuts and semantic weakenings remain valid with the common guard. Finally resolve the $2^b$ derived assignment blockers with $2^b-1$ binary cuts. Count the $m$ original lines once and the branch costs conservatively to obtain (1). Bound each non-DEF step by $2^{d_*+1}$ to obtain (2). Sharing is preserved within each branch. Search time, proof bytes and scheduled memory are separate from logical lines.
