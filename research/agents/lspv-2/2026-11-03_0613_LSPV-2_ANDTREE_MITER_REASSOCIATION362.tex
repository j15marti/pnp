\noindent\textbf{PROVED/EXACT all-size structural bounds, independently tested; specialist review pending.}
Use unchanged source-authenticated, unit-node, at-most-binary-premise $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$; the constructed proof uses only strict binary Resolution.
\begin{theorem}
For all $n\ge2$, let $A,B$ be arbitrary full binary AND trees on the same $n$ input variables, each occurring exactly once in each tree; trees may differ in shape and input order. Use disjoint fresh internal variables. Encode each gate $g=u\land v$ with original clauses $(-g\lor u)$, $(-g\lor v)$ and $(g\lor-u\lor-v)$, and add output disequality $(p\lor q),(-p\lor-q)$, forming $F(A,B)$. Then $F(A,B)$ is clause-minimally UNSAT, with $3n-2$ variables and $m=6n-4$ indispensable clauses, and
\[
\boxed{6n-5\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F(A,B))\le W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F(A,B))\le8n-9.}
\]
For $n=2$ the mixed-system optimum is exactly $7$. With ordinary contiguous DIMACS indices its source has $N=\Theta(n\log(n+1))$ encoded bits, making this a nonaffine linear-cost family in $N$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Proof.}
Both roots compute the conjunction of all common leaves, so required output disagreement is impossible. Every gate source clause is indispensable. Deleting $(-g\lor u)$ or $(-g\lor v)$ permits setting exactly one leaf in the specified child subtree to zero, all other leaves to one, incorrectly setting $g=1$ instead of zero, and faithfully propagating that output to its root. The opposite tree correctly yields zero, satisfying mismatch; all other clauses hold. Deleting $(g\lor-u\lor-v)$ permits all-one input with $g$ incorrectly set to zero and faithfully propagated, while the other tree outputs one. Either mismatch clause is individually indispensable, since evaluating the trees normally on all-one input gives $p=q=1$, while input with a zero gives $p=q=0$. Thus deleting any of the $m$ clauses makes the source satisfiable. Every sound connected root-ancestry DAG with at most two premises per paid node must have at least $m-1$ nodes by the edge inequality $s+q-1\le2q$.

For an explicit upper refutation, derive $(-p\lor x)$ for every nonroot node $x$ of tree $A$. Both children of $p$ have original such clauses. Each of the remaining $2n-4$ parent--child edges costs one Resolution pivot: resolve $(-p\lor\mathrm{parent}(x))$ with $(-\mathrm{parent}(x)\lor x)$. Traverse $B$ bottom-up: at each of its $n-1$ AND gates $g=u\land v$, resolve existing $(-p\lor u)$ and $(-p\lor v)$ successively against the original clause $(g\lor-u\lor-v)$, deriving $(-p\lor g)$ in two paid steps. Thus $(-p\lor q)$ costs $(2n-4)+(2n-2)=4n-6$ nodes. Swap the trees to derive $(-q\lor p)$ at equal cost. Resolve the first mismatch clause with $(-q\lor p)$ to obtain $(p)$, and the second mismatch clause with $(-p\lor q)$ to obtain $(-p)$, then resolve these units. The merge takes three paid pivots. Total $2(4n-6)+3=8n-9$. At $n=2$ this meets the source floor. $\square$
