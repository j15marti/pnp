\[
\mathcal P_{\rm pair}=\{F\in\mathcal C_{M22}:\forall C\in F,\bar C\in F\},

\section{New source co-occurrence gauge theorem}
The unsigned source co-occurrence graph $G_F$ has active source
names as vertices and an edge between names appearing in the same
clause. For $S\subseteq\operatorname{vars}(F)$ define $g_S(F)$
by negating every occurrence of each variable $v\in S$.
The map is SAT-preserving: toggling the assignment bits
$x'_v=x_v\oplus{\bf1}[v\in S]$ preserves the truth
of every signed literal after the corresponding sign toggle.
A per-occurrence sign edit is a different operation,
and need not preserve SAT.

\begin{theorem}[Exact legal M22 source gauges; PROVED/EXACT]
For every $F\in\mathcal C_{M22}$ and $S\subseteq\operatorname{vars}(F)$,
the following are equivalent:
\[
g_S(F)\in\mathcal C_{M22}
\iff {\bf1}_S\text{ is constant on every clause support}
\iff S\text{ is a union of connected components of }G_F.
\]
Consequently there are exactly $2^{c(F)}$ legal source-gauge
subsets, where $c(F)$ counts connected components.
Each legal gauge preserves SAT, all exact signed occurrence
counts, clause uniqueness, paired versus nonpaired membership,
$q$, and the fixed-record indexed $k,m,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Within each original all-positive or all-negative clause,
the source-wide sign toggles leave all three literals
of one polarity precisely when all three names are
switched or none is. This is constancy on every source
co-occurrence edge, equivalently on connected components.
Conversely, switching whole components changes every
clause in them from positive to negative or vice versa.
Each source originally has two occurrences of each sign,
so these quotas swap without changing. The sign toggle
is a bijection of records and cannot introduce duplicates.
Within each component the positive and negative support
collections exchange roles, preserving pairing and $q$.
Assignment bit complementation preserves all clause
truth values. Component choices are independent,
giving $2^{c(F)}$ choices.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Distinct legal orbit members]
Let $d(F)$ count components of $G_F$ that are not
fully complement paired. Among all $2^{c(F)}$
legal source gauges, precisely $2^{d(F)}$ distinct
formulas arise. Thus if $F$ is connected and belongs
to $\mathcal C_\star$, it has precisely the two
distinct legal descendants $F$ and the full
source-sign complement $\bar F$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Componentwise inversion interchanges all its positive
and negative clauses. The resulting set is identical
to its original exactly for fully paired components.
A nonpaired component gives two different alternatives.
Since component name sets are disjoint, their choices
multiply; paired components contribute only stabilizers.
\end{proof}

\section{Charged two-level contraction and normalization}
On connected $F\in\mathcal C_\star$, every legal pure
gauge has the same active source names, clauses and
explicit encoded length $N$; after two consecutive
gauges the group remains the same. Hence no two-level
pure-gauge/direct-copy constructor can create an
$N^{\lambda+o(1)}$-bit ordinary child for fixed
$\lambda<1$. Writing a full ordinary child costs
$\Omega(N)$ elementary output bit operations.
Virtual children represented as source pointers plus
masks require separately charged source access,
index computation, ordinary-input adaptation
or eventual expansion. No source-read or memory
cost is declared free.

If the two distinct legal children on connected
nonpaired input are instead placed in a declared
independent exhaustive list, each still having $k$
source variables, then $B=2$, $R_{\rm raw}=0$,
\[
\Psi=2^{k+1},\quad R_{\rm search}=k-\log_2\Psi=-1.
\]
This optional search budget expands rather than
contracts. A good adaptive solver can avoid both
branches by recognizing the equivalence. With the
user's exact checkpoint convention,
\[
T_A(F)=C_A(F,R)+T_{{\rm finish},A}(F,R),\qquad
S_A=\text{peak simultaneously live writable/index bits}.
\]
For a declared exhaustive residual list $r_j$,
$\Psi=\sum_j2^{r_j}$; the normalized model

\[
|\log_2T_{\rm model}-\max\{\log_2 C,k-R\}|\le1,\qquad
|R_{\rm net}-\min\{R,k-\log_2 C\}|\le1.
\]
These are identities, not all-decider lower bounds.
