\section*{Pinned source-exact selector reflection}
Given any proper signed width-three $F=C_1\land\cdots\land C_m$ on $n\ge3$ named witness variables $y$, introduce one-hot selectors $x_{j,t,\ell}$, for each clause slot $j$, literal position $t\in\{0,1,2\}$, and signed literal $\ell\in\{\pm y_i:1\le i\le n\}$. Include all one-hot source clauses. For every ordered triple $(a,b,c)$ on three distinct original variables include the original guarded source clause
\[
(\neg x_{j,0,a}\vee\neg x_{j,1,b}\vee\neg x_{j,2,c}\vee a\vee b\vee c).
\tag{1}
\]
Write $K=3m$ and list the $K$ positive selectors describing the authentic original signed clauses of $F$ as $s_0,\dots,s_{K-1}$. Add a CNF AND-chain with $K-1$ fresh gate variables, three source clauses per two-input definition, and a final positive unit output. All are explicit source clauses. Call the entire formula $U(F)$. This equality recognizer is not a uniform total correct general-SAT decider.

The formula has exactly
\[
V(U)=n+6mn+(3m-1),\quad
C(U)=3m(1+\binom{2n}{2})+8mn(n-1)(n-2)+3(3m-1)+1.
\]
For cubic Tseitin this gives $V(U_h)=36h^2+(27/2)h-1$, $C(U_h)=\Theta(h^4)$ and actual signed ASCII encoding length $N_U=\Theta(h^4\log(h+2))$ bits.

\begin{theorem}[Source-exact restriction and same-source escape]
Let $\rho_F$ fix all selectors to the unique one-hot signed description of $F$ and every AND-chain gate to one, leaving the original $y$ variables free. Then the surviving source-clause multiset is \emph{exactly} $F$, with no leftover auxiliary contradiction:
\[
U(F)|_{\rho_F}=F.
\]
Models of $U(F)$ correspond bijectively to models of $F$. For every odd-charge simple bridgeless cubic Tseitin $F_h$,
\[
\boxed{
W_{\mathrm{Res}}(U(F_h))\ge W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F_h),\qquad
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(U(F_h))\le40h-3.}
\]
Thus a source-exact reflection can inherit $2^{\Omega(h)}$ ordinary Resolution hardness while having an explicit $O(h)$ mixed proof on the \emph{same new source}. This refutes the proposed unrestricted Resolution-to-mixed hardness transfer for this family; it proves no mixed lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The output unit and all gate equivalences force every selected $s_k=1$. One-hot clauses force every other selector zero, and every gate variable equals one. Every selector-only, gate and output source becomes true. The unique selected guard in each slot reduces to $C_j$; every unselected guard is true. This proves source-exact restriction and model bijection. Ordinary shared-DAG Resolution is closed under restrictions without increasing derived-node cost: topologically substitute assignments, drop true lines, keep a stronger parent when a pivot is fixed, and preserve genuine resolutions on unfixed pivots. The final empty line survives. Therefore any Resolution proof of $U(F_h)$ restricts to one of $F_h$ with at most the same cost.

For the mixed upper certificate, use the source output unit and the two forward-implication clauses from each AND gate, going backwards to derive its two inputs by two strict binary Resolution inferences per gate. This obtains all $K$ positive selector units in $2(K-1)$ paid nodes. Three successive binary Resolution pivots on the appropriate units turn each selected guarded clause (1) into its original signed source clause, using $3m$ nodes. For each cubic graph vertex three paid AND3 operations extract its exact GF$(2)$ row from the four derived authentic clauses, using $3h$ nodes. Sum the $h$ rows in $h-1$ binary XOR nodes. Every edge cancels twice and odd charge yields $0=1$. The total is
\[
2(3m-1)+3m+3h+h-1=9m+4h-3=40h-3.
\]
All inputs and pivots are authenticated, every derived inference is charged, and neither LOCAL-CONVERT nor uncharged circuit-internal names are used.
\end{proof}
