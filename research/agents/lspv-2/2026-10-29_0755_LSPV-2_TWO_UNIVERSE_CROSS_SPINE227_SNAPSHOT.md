## Assumption-switch arithmetic
* SAT parent N -> exact22 entry M=O(N^alpha polylog N) -> child O(M^lambda): guaranteed strict power exponent only if **alpha*lambda<1** plus full source parsing, equivalence and repeated closure. At alpha*lambda=1, a size upper bound does not certify shrink.
* A strict signed exact22 physical output retaining n distinct old ports has a digit-field floor of **32 sum_{j=1}^n (1+floor(log_10 j)) = Omega(n log n) bits**. This holds in *both* worlds. Semantic forgetting can evade it.
* b-port exact existential profile: 2^b extensional entries. Under P=NP, poly-time per-cofactor decision for b=O(log N) permits polynomial full enumeration (subject to ordinary output length), but b=sqrt(N) exact tables are enormous even then. Under P!=NP, a polynomial all-input **zero-port** summary producer would decide SAT and is impossible. Small tables do not imply cheap discovery.
* Hypothetical virtual child simulator with builder cost N^c, child length N^alpha, child solver time M^d, and worst-case access N^u has conservative total time exponent **max(c,alpha*d+u)** if every child step can trigger access. Williams excludes a **correct, same-model** SAT solver with n^{o(1)} *total workspace* and exponent strictly below **2 cos(pi/7)=1.8019377358**. E.g. c=1.1, alpha=1, d=1.6, u=.05 yields 1.65 and would contradict if the uniform low-space solver actually existed; u=.35 gives 1.95 and is not excluded by this bound. A fully materialized N-bit writable child defeats the low-space premise. The unknown hypothetical SAT polynomial exponent K remains unrestricted.
* Separate worst-case T_+,T_-,S_+,S_- maxima need not occur on the same input. Input tape, child materialization, source access, failures and output must be charged in their native TM/RAM model. Head-crossing promises are not RAM lower bounds.
* **Kolmogorov firewall:** for fixed polynomial defining time t=n^e, the time-bounded incompressibility predicate is in coNP; P=NP would put it in P, but the polynomial generator's exponent K(e) need not be <e. Hirahara's unconditional P-immunity concerns **superpolynomial-time-bounded** strings, for which the predicate is not automatically in coNP. Thus PH collapse + immunity does not by itself produce a contradiction. The deterministic time hierarchy is also compatible with a collapse of the whole PH to the *union* P of polynomial exponents.
