\noindent\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, restricted, independent referee pending.} An exact, proof-supported four-category spectrum compresses the $\binom n2$ globally coherent two-variable flips of a proper signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ 3CNF in $O(N)$ indexed word-RAM operations and working words for fixed-width source access. This does \emph{not} count all SAT models, eliminate a general SAT branch, imply an all-algorithm lower bound, or resolve either $P=NP$ or $P\ne NP$; both remain \textbf{OPEN}. The underlying quadratic interaction identity is a standard multilinear expansion, so no priority or novelty of the identity is asserted.

\section{Required source-indexed primary experiment}
Fix a proper source 3CNF $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m C_j$ with $n$ \emph{original global variables}. An occurrence $x_{i,j}$ is the signed occurrence of original $x_i$ in original clause $C_j$, not an independent input bit. Local $S_j$ demands \emph{all three original literals TRUE}, equivalently $a_i=1$ for a positive occurrence and $a_i=0$ for a negative occurrence. Local $U_j$ demands \emph{all three FALSE} and therefore complements the requirements of $S_j$. Both inversions $S_j\to U_j$ and $U_j\to S_j$ are recorded per occurrence. Ordinary source-clause SAT means $t_j(a)\ge1$, not local $S_j$.

For two distinct original clauses, their four extreme compatibility cells are $SS,SU,US,UU$. The diagonal cells are feasible if and only if no original variable is shared with opposite signs; the off-diagonal cells are feasible if and only if none is shared with the same sign. A pair sharing both types has no joint extreme mode, yet can be simultaneously \emph{disjunctively} SAT. Exhaustive checks use coherent original-variable assignments over each source-pair union; 18 sampled overlapping original-clause triples per instance check all eight $S/U$ modes by direct enumeration. For any global $a$, $t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a)$ and $N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a)$. Coherent $0^n$, $1^n$, multi-flip trails, exact clause truth vectors, and the full $(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$ are in the raw records.

A source-parent cube certificate is also retained: from $C_j=(x\vee A)$ and $C_k=(\neg x\vee B)$, a non-tautological resolvent $R=A\vee B$ forbids every original-variable partial assignment falsifying $R$. Under that cube the two unchanged \emph{original} clauses reduce to opposing unit literals on $x$; no SAT-label oracle is used. The raw DAG specifies both original clause indices, pivot, original literal signs and forbidden cube. This is the standard resolution/RUP deduction, not an independent new algorithm.

\section{Compressed four-category two-flip theorem}
For one fixed \emph{coherent} global base assignment $a$, define the clause-status vector
\[
\vN(a)=(N_0(a),N_1(a),N_2(a),N_3(a)),\qquad \sum_{r=0}^3N_r(a)=m.
\]
Let $a^{\{i\}}$ denote the assignment flipping only the original variable $x_i$. Define the signed first and second finite differences
\[
\bd_i=\vN(a^{\{i\}})-\vN(a),\qquad
\bh_{ik}=\vN(a^{\{i,k\}})-\vN(a^{\{i\}})-\vN(a^{\{k\}})+\vN(a).
\]
Write $E_F=\{\{i,k\}:i\ne k\text{ appear together in at least one original clause}\}$. Let $b_{\bd}=|\{i:\bd_i=\bd\}|$ and $B=|\{\bd:b_{\bd}>0\}|$. For a four-vector $\mathbf u$ write $\mathbf z^{\mathbf u}=\prod_{r=0}^3z_r^{u_r}$, allowing Laurent monomials in intermediate arithmetic.

\begin{theorem}[Exact four-category two-flip spectrum]\label{thm:spectrum}
For every proper 3CNF and every coherent complete base assignment $a$, the generating function of \emph{all} distinct globally consistent two-variable flips equals
\begin{align*}
G_{2,a}(\mathbf z)&=\sum_{1\le i<k\le n}\mathbf z^{\vN(a^{\{i,k\}})}\\
&=\sum_{\bd<\mathbf e}b_{\bd}b_{\mathbf e}\mathbf z^{\vN(a)+\bd+\mathbf e}
 +\sum_{\bd}\binom{b_{\bd}}2\mathbf z^{\vN(a)+2\bd}\\
&\quad+\sum_{\{i,k\}\in E_F}\left(\mathbf z^{\vN(a)+\bd_i+\bd_k+\bh_{ik}}-
                     \mathbf z^{\vN(a)+\bd_i+\bd_k}\right).
\end{align*}
Here $\bd<\mathbf e$ is any fixed total ordering of four-vectors. Every coefficient in the final combined polynomial is a nonnegative integer. Its coefficient at exponents $(0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$ counts exactly the distance-two source SAT assignments. Its coefficients with third index $N_3=0$ count exactly the two-flip assignments whose \emph{complements} satisfy $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $g_j(a)$ be the one-hot four-vector of the original clause $C_j$'s number of TRUE literals. The vector $\vN(a)$ is $\sum_jg_j(a)$. The mixed finite difference of $g_j$ on variables $i,k$ is zero if either variable does not occur in $C_j$. Therefore $\bh_{ik}=0$ for $\{i,k\}\notin E_F$, and the displayed identity $\vN(a^{\{i,k\}})=\vN(a)+\bd_i+\bd_k+\bh_{ik}$ is exact, without probabilistic assumptions or repeated-clause independence. Grouping the uncorrected $\binom n2$ pairs by identical singleton vectors yields the first two sums. Correcting the $E_F$ pairs once each yields the final sum. Direct clause-count semantics ensures the final exponents are nonnegative; any intermediate negative exponents cancel in the Laurent ring. Inversion of an original assignment changes every local three-bit truth vector $v$ into $\bar v$, so $\vN(\bar a^{S})=(N_3,N_2,N_1,N_0)(a^{S})$. Hence the $N_3=0$ coefficients, with the same source flip index set, give the opposite endpoint's SAT count.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-degree linear-size histogram and witness]\label{thm:complexity}
In proper signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ 3CNF, each original variable occurs in four source clauses. Consequently there are at most
\[
\boxed{B\le\binom{4+6-1}{6-1}=\binom95=126}
\]
distinct singleton status-change vectors $\bd_i$. The histogram of all $\binom n2$ two-flip vectors, together with the distance-zero and distance-one histograms, and the complement-reversed histograms for the second endpoint, can therefore be constructed in $O(n+m)$ indexed word-RAM work and $O(n+m)$ working words, excluding any demand to emit all $\binom n2$ assignments. If one of these endpoint-radius-two assignments satisfies the source, an original-variable witness can be reconstructed and verified in the same asymptotic resources. For strict22 $m=4n/3$, so the bound is linear in $n$ and in encoded source bits $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ under a word-addressed signed-literal representation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
One flip changes each affected clause count by $+1$ or $-1$. For an individual original occurrence there are six possible one-hot transitions: $0\to1$, $1\to0$, $1\to2$, $2\to1$, $2\to3$, $3\to2$. Since every source variable has exactly four occurrences, its total $\bd_i$ is the sum of four members of these six types. There are at most $\binom95$ multisets. Build $\vN$, all $\bd_i$, and $\bh_{ik}$ by scanning each source clause and its three variable pairs once. At most $3m$ pair appearances are processed and at most $3m$ unique pairs are stored. Evaluate at most $B(B+1)/2$ bucket pairs and make one correction per stored cooccurrence pair. These are $O(n+m+126^2)$ operations. Each variable has at most eight different cooccurrence neighbors. To reconstruct a non-neighbor pair corresponding to a candidate bucket pair, scan the relevant lists and skip adjacent candidates. If both lists are fully cross-adjacent, each has at most eight members; otherwise a non-neighbor appears after $O(8^2)$ skipped candidate comparisons. Neighbor pairs are explicitly scanned; singleton/base cases are directly checked. Thus witness reconstruction is $O(n+m+126^2\cdot8^2)$. A final source pass validates the coherent witness. Each arithmetic count is at most $\binom n2$ in magnitude and fits $O(\log n)$ bits.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Scope caveat.} This is a compressed \emph{bounded Hamming-layer} statistic, not a decision procedure for SAT beyond that layer. A standard width-three bounded-depth search on a falsified clause also provides a linear-in-$N$ decision for the \emph{existence} of a model within any fixed radius; the present additional object is the exact four-category count for every two-flip state \emph{without listing the states}. Known local-search SAT work already includes Hamming-ball algorithms \cite{DantsinHirschWolpert2003}. The occurrence quota and bucket compression do not provide an unrestricted runtime lower bound.

\noindent\textbf{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.} Exact local $S/U$ and pair modes, merge resolvents, parity cycles, and complement counts are already established; they are separately rechecked, not presented as new. The newly proved object is the \emph{second-order four-count distribution and its bounded-occurrence linear-size bucket compilation}. Previous $H_r$ radius-two searches explicitly enumerated $O(n^2)$ flip masks. The compressed histogram does not contradict that implementation; it answers a different, aggregated-output problem. Clause-extreme incompatibility alone remains unable to prune coherent DPLL nodes, consistent with FIREWALL214. Monotone strict balanced exact22 is known NP-complete \cite{DarmannDocker2021}, so no polynomial universal SAT conclusion is drawn from the bounded-radius result. A Horn, 2SAT or affine solver may be much faster on their special families; detecting those representations and converting them is not free.

\noindent\textbf{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN.} LSPV-2's previous syntactic backdoor and signed NAE witness work remains valid and distinct. This result replaces the implicit assumption that auditing every radius-two flip necessarily requires one state evaluation per candidate, but only for aggregate four-count distributions and a small-radius witness. The corpus's open arbitrary-SAT decision and separation arrows are unchanged. The main manuscript may be reconciled only within exact LSPV-2 ownership markers after fresh blob readback.

\noindent\textbf{BARRIER AUDIT.} The compiler's $O(N)$ source-derived $\mathbf N,\bd,\bh$ structure is algorithmically \emph{easy} on signed strict22, not a counting or communication lower bound. Hard clause graph structures may still have trivial two-flip spectra. Randomized algorithms are not needed. The argument does not use nonuniform advice, oracles, cryptographic hardness, natural properties, or restricted proof-system lower bounds, so no arbitrary SAT lower-bound transfer arises. At a deeper Hamming layer $k$ growing with $n$, the number of connected flip interactions and bucket dependencies can grow exponentially; no uncharged generalization is assumed.
