\section*{Universal Boolean construction, and its logical-node count}
For every finite connected simple cubic $G=(V,E)$, $h=|V|$, every odd charge $\chi\in\mathbb F_2^V$ with $r=\sum_v\chi_v$, and fresh guard $t$, let $F_{G,\chi}$ encode the signed local equations $\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=\chi_v$ and define
\[
H_{G,\chi}(t,x)=\bigwedge_{C\in F_{G,\chi}}(\neg t\vee C).
\]
$H$ is SAT at $t=0$ and entails $\neg t$ because $F$ is UNSAT. Buy conservative binary Boolean definitions
\[
y_e\leftrightarrow(t\land x_e)\quad(e\in E),\qquad
p_v\leftrightarrow(y_{e_1}\oplus y_{e_2})\quad(v\in V),
\]
charging exactly $|E|+h=5h/2$ DEF nodes and authenticating all gate CNF views. The original guarded clauses plus the gate views entail the exact affine rows
\[
 A_v:\ p_v\oplus y_{e_3}\oplus(\chi_v t)=0,\qquad
 B_v:\ p_v\oplus y_{e_1}\oplus y_{e_2}=0.
\]
For $t=0$ all $y_e=0$; for $t=1$ the original parity equation applies, proving $A_v$, and $B_v$ is the gate equation. XOR all $2h$ rows. Each $p_v$ cancels and each $y_e$ appears exactly twice, leaving $(\bigoplus_v\chi_v)t=0$, hence $t=0$ for odd total charge. The even-charge counterpart admits a model with $t=1$ and must not entail $\neg t$.
