## Theorem 473.1 | PROVED/RESTRICTED, original signed proper 3-CNF

Let G=(V,E) be a finite **simple connected cubic graph**, with |V|=H and with G-v connected for **every** v in V. Fix charges b_v in {0,1} with XOR_v b_v=1. Assign a Boolean x_e to every edge. Define F(G,b) by the exact four original clauses at each vertex v, one for each triple a in {0,1}^3 with XOR_j a_j != b_v. For the incident edges e_1,e_2,e_3, the clause C(v,a) is the disjunction of literals l_j=(x_{e_j} if a_j=0 else NOT x_{e_j}), so C(v,a) is falsified precisely by local edge vector a. There are 4H original *proper width-three* clauses and 3H/2 variables; with explicit fixed-width binary variable IDs and an independent sign bit on every occurrence, encoded bit length N=Theta(H log(H+2)).

**Claim A (UNSAT and literal-sign sensitivity).** F(G,b) is UNSAT. For every original clause C(v,a) and every j in {1,2,3}, changing **only the sign bit** of occurrence j produces a SAT formula of the same encoded length and still in proper 3-CNF. In particular, each of its 4H clauses is individually indispensable and all 12H occurrence-polarity bits are independently SAT/UNSAT-sensitive.

**Proof.** XOR of all parity equations sums each edge twice, giving 0 on the left and XOR_v b_v=1 on the right, contradiction. Fix v and a violating b_v. Prescribe the three incident edge values to a and delete the parity equation at v. Every other parity equation is satisfiable with those boundary values: since G-v is connected, its GF(2) incidence system has rank H-2 and is consistent iff the XOR of its right-hand sides vanishes. The XOR of the adjusted charges over V\{v} is (XOR_{u != v} b_u) XOR (XOR_j a_j) = (1 XOR b_v) XOR (1 XOR b_v)=0. More constructively, set non-tree edges of G-v to zero and solve its tree edges by postorder parity propagation, with the root equation automatic. The resulting edge vector violates the equation only at v and realizes local vector a. Thus it satisfies all original clauses except C(v,a), which is false. Delete C(v,a) and the vector satisfies all retained clauses. Alternatively flip a single sign in C(v,a): that changed clause now forbids a XOR e_j rather than a, and therefore is TRUE at a. All other clauses remain true. This holds for every clause and sign position. QED.

**Claim B (read-footprint lower bound).** Fix an arbitrary *deterministic, total and correct* SAT/UNSAT/malformed classifier A on explicit fixed-length binary signed 3-CNF records with independently addressable polarity bits. Let R_A(F) be the set of input-bit positions actually inspected when A runs on the particular UNSAT encoding of F(G,b). Then every one of its 12H occurrence-sign positions belongs to R_A(F). Otherwise flipping an unread sign would generate a valid same-length SAT encoding with exactly the same observed input symbols; induction on the deterministic machine's entire configuration gives the same output, contradiction. Consequently |R_A(F)| >= 12H = Omega(N/log N), and any *single-anchor observational footprint cache* cannot reuse A(F)'s UNSAT answer for any of these 12H one-bit SAT neighbors.

**Proof qualification.** The conclusion is only a near-linear **input-bit probe** lower bound for a specific fixed-width encoding. It does not prove Omega(N) when each variable ID costs Theta(log H) bits, superlinear time, a lower bound on working space, an adaptive-branching lower bound, or a universal inability to share other solver subcomputations. In particular, checking all sign bits once and performing GF(2) elimination is a valid polynomial-time method on recognized Tseitin sources. This theorem is not a proof of P != NP.

**Sharp assumption control.** For a cubic graph with a cut vertex, an arbitrary prescribed parity-violating boundary triple need not extend to the remainder: the sum of adjusted charges must vanish in *each* connected component of G-v. Two subdivided K4 graphs joined by a bridge (10 vertices, 15 edges) provide a finite control. Taking odd charge only at one bridge endpoint, of its 12 possible one-occurrence flips six yield SAT and six remain UNSAT. Thus G-v connected is an essential assumption of Claim A, not a disposable proof artifact.
