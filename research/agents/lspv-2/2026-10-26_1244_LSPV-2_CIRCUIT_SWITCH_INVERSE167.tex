not a table precomputed for each physical input. For every allowed
$K=2^h$, record width $w$, comparator layer $\ell$, position $p$,

model. All intermediate arithmetic registers, gate indices, operands,
signs and source accesses are counted. Input-independent formulas are
part of one fixed machine, not nonuniform tables or physical read-only
advice.

\section{Exact comparator gate and inverse indices}
Let $K=2^h$, $L=h(h+1)/2$, $D=w+3$, $H=7(w+2)$,
$A=H+6D=13w+32$. The $L$ bitonic layers use fixed
$(\texttt{size},\texttt{stride})$ pairs; write $s=2^b$ for the
selected layer's stride. For any record position $p$ define
\[
i=p\mathbin{\&}\neg s,\quad j=i+s,\quad
r=\left\lfloor\frac{i}{2s}\right\rfloor s+(i\bmod s),
\quad c=\ell K/2+r.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The partner is $p\oplus s$. Every record position participates in
exactly one comparator of each fixed layer, and (1) inverts its
pair rank with no scan. The forward comparator's \emph{zero-based}
saved-swap gate index is
\[
\boxed{g_\sigma=cA+H-5.}                                  \tag{2}
\]
The minus five is significant. \texttt{LTBITS} uses seven gates
per key bit, but the final \texttt{less} OR gate is the \emph{third}
of the final seven, at offset $7(w+2)-5$, not the final gate.
The independent reproducer reconstructs the actual two-input gate
template to check this index and the signed operand slots.

Each of the two records has $D$ MUX outputs; each MUX has the gate
sequence $\mathrm{AND}(\neg\sigma,a)$,
$\mathrm{AND}(\sigma,b)$, $\mathrm{OR}(\cdot,\cdot)$.
Thus the $4D$ forward signed operand occurrences, indexed by
$0\le u<2D$, $t\in\{0,1\}$, lie at
\[
\boxed{g_F(u,t)=cA+H+3u+t,\qquad
 \operatorname{slot}=0,\quad
 \operatorname{sgn}=(-1)^{1-t}.}                         \tag{3}
\]
The scan has exactly $S=19K+(K-1)(4w-1)$ gates.
The inverse routing section starts at
$I=(LK/2)A+S$. It visits the layers in reverse
order, but keeps ascending pair ranks \emph{within} each layer.
Therefore $c_{\rm inv}=(L-1-\ell)K/2+r$, and the
four inverse operand occurrences are
\[
\boxed{g_R(t)=I+6c_{\rm inv}+t,\quad
t\in\{0,1,3,4\},\quad
\operatorname{slot}=0,\quad
\operatorname{sgn}=
\begin{cases}-1&t\in\{0,3\},\\+1&t\in\{1,4\}.\end{cases}}
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
Every saved swap bit therefore has \emph{exactly}
\[
\boxed{F_\sigma=4D+4=4w+16}                              \tag{5}
\]
signed operand occurrences in this exact compiler. No other gate
reads the saved bit. The use list (3) followed by (4) is strictly
increasing in gate index. Integer division, remainder, fixed
$O(\log K)$-bit products and a constant number of case distinctions
implement both directions in $O(\log^3G)$ conservative bit time
and $O(\log G)$ combined index/workspace, without a table, dynamic
sort, value-dependent swap recomputation or allocating gate records.

\begin{theorem}[Switch-output Rank/Select, restricted,
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT}]\label{th:switch}
For all legal $h,w,\ell,p$ in the fixed bitonic verifier layout,
equations (1)--(4) give a total, mutually inverse
$\operatorname{Select}_{\sigma}$ and
$\operatorname{Rank}_{\sigma}$ on the $F_\sigma$ direct
signed-input occurrences of its saved swap gate. The procedure is
uniform and uses worst-case $O(\log^3G)$ bit operations,
$O(\log G)$ writable/index space, and no source occurrence table.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The bitonic layer consists of independent stride-$s$ pairs.
Removing bit $b$ from $p$ produces their smaller position $i$,
and quotient/remainder modulo $2s$ gives their contiguous pair rank.
There are exactly $7(w+2)$ comparator gates, the final less
OR gate has zero-based local offset $H-5$, and each of the
$2D$ fixed MUXs has exactly two \emph{first-operand} uses
of the saved swap wire. The network's inverse first computes a
scan, then processes layers in reverse order and each pair in the
original order, yielding (4). Other sections do not address the
saved comparator's output. The sectors do not overlap;
within each sector the stated arithmetic bijects the ordinal to
its signed operand occurrence. The forward/backward inversions
are arithmetic on $O(\log G)$-bit integers, with the charged bound.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact base-CNF occurrence Rank/Select}
In the source verifier's \emph{proper} 3CNF compiler,
Boolean gate $g$ has output variable
$v_g=\texttt{gatebase}+9g+2$ and clause block
$13g,\ldots,13g+12$. The swap gate is the final
$\mathrm{OR}(\texttt{less},\cdot)$ of its comparison.
Its output occurs five times in its \emph{own} OR gate
block, once negatively at clause $13g+8$, slot 2,
and four times positively at clauses $13g+9,\ldots,13g+12$,
slot 1. In every downstream consumer whose operand 0 is
$\sigma$ or $\neg\sigma$, the padded equality template uses
the original variable \emph{once in each of its first four}
source clauses, always literal slot 1: two positive and two
negative appearances.

For $F_\sigma$ consumers indexed increasingly by $u$, define
\[
\operatorname{OccSelect}(v_\sigma,k)=
\begin{cases}
(13g_\sigma+8,2,-)&k=0,\\
(13g_\sigma+8+k,1,+)&1\le k\le4,\\
(13g_{\mathrm{use}(u)}+t,1,\operatorname{pol}(u,t))
 &k=5+4u+t,\ 0\le t<4 .
\end{cases}                                                \tag{6}
\]
For signed operand $\varepsilon\in\{+1,-1\}$,
$\operatorname{pol}(u,t)=\varepsilon$ for $t=0,1$
and $-\varepsilon$ for $t=2,3$.
The own gate precedes every consumer, and all consumers are ordered by
gate index, so (6) is \emph{already the ascending source-clause order}.
It has inverse from the clause quotient $g'=\lfloor c/13\rfloor$,
row $t=c\bmod13$, and inverse switch rank for $g'$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact source-CNF switch incidence,
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT}]\label{th:cnf}
For every fixed-comparator saved-switch output variable,
the unregularized proper source CNF has
\[
\boxed{\deg(v_\sigma)=5+4F_\sigma=16D+21=16w+69,}
\quad
\boxed{(\deg_+,\deg_-)=(8D+12,8D+9).}                    \tag{7}
\]
Its complete signed $\operatorname{Rank/Select}$ relation has a
uniform table-free indexed implementation in worst-case
$O(\log^3G)$ bit operations and $O(\log G)$ work/index bits.
This is the \emph{actual 13-clause padded gate template},
not a generic 3CNF transformation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The 13-row template has exactly five occurrences of an
OR gate's output. Every use in (3)--(4) is in operand slot 0 of
a later AND gate. The clone-equality subtemplate for operand 0
contributes exactly four occurrences in rows 0--3,
two of each sign. Since (2) precedes all (3)--(4),
(6) enumerates the entire sorted source occurrence list.
Its inverse is the quotient/remainder computation together
with the inverse of (3)--(4). The exact counts follow.
No other source clause refers to $v_\sigma$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Boundary of the theorem.}
For a comparator \emph{record output wire}, a similar fixed
next-layer pair inversion appears possible, but the present
theorem does not enumerate every internal AND/OR gate, the
high-fanout constants, aliases, scan carry, the first/last
row special cases, final clause checkers, witness bits, and
output-pinning occurrences. Therefore the \emph{complete}
\texttt{GateAt/Rank/Select} needed by two-level R15 for
every variable remains \textbf{OPEN}.
The $O(G)=O(N\log^3N)$ \emph{gate count} does not imply
$O(\log N)$ work space for circuit evaluation or for the
full forward-to-inverse source map. Similarly, storing
$G$ gate records costs $\Omega(G\log G)$ bit writes and
retained space if kept. A small descriptor cannot
be substituted as the entire deterministic SAT
decision circuit $C_{A,N}$.

\section{Connected hostile W1 product and local certificate}
Let $W$ be the fixed 15-variable, 20-clause proper signed
exact-$(2,2)$ UNSAT formula from the project and let
$W^-$ delete its last clause. Direct enumeration checks
$\SAT(W)=0$, and every one-clause deletion is SAT.
For $k$ renamed copies add $k-1$ connector clauses
\[
 (x_{15i+1}\vee x_{15(i+1)+1}\vee z_i),
 \qquad 0\le i<k-1,                                     \tag{8}
\]
where every $z_i$ is fresh. The resulting primal graph
is connected for $k>1$; each formula contains $k$ disjoint
full copies of $W$, hence is UNSAT. Any variable set
hitting \emph{every} ternary source clause must contain
at least one member of each original copy, even after adding
(8), and consequently has size at least $k$.
Replacing all $W$ copies by $W^-$ and setting
$z_i=1$ produces a SAT connected control.

This connected family is \emph{ordinary proper signed 3CNF},
\emph{not} signed exact-$(2,2)$ after inserting the bridges.
A sound local UNSAT certificate can name one 20-clause
source-authenticated $W$ copy and replay its fixed
finite refutation or exhaustive $2^{15}$ evaluation;
its index/header uses $O(\log N)$ bits and fixed pattern
authentication needs only $O(\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work
for random-access clause fields on this explicitly
serialized family. As in the Role-1 185 product result,
this \emph{restricted certificate} does not give
all-UNSAT ordinary-SAT coverage or certify a favorable
general coNTIME premise. The connected construction
refutes the proposed implication that global
clause-hitting backdoor size must be logarithmic
whenever cheap local certificates exist.
