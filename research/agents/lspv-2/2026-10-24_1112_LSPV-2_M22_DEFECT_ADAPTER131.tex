\section{Precise target and receiving proof obligation}
Let \(\mathcal C_{M22}\) be the valid, duplicate-free CNFs with three distinct original variables in every clause, all literals of one clause having the same sign, and exactly two positive and two negative occurrences of every active source variable. We use ordinary OR within clauses. If \(C\) is a clause, \(\bar C\) is obtained by flipping every literal sign while \emph{retaining OR}; it is not Boolean negation of the clause. Define
\[
\mathcal P_{\rm pair}=\{F\in\mathcal C_{M22}:\forall C\in F,\bar C\in F\},
\quad
\mathcal C_\star=\mathcal C_{M22}\setminus\mathcal P_{\rm pair},
\quad
L_\star=\{\operatorname{enc}(F):F\in\mathcal C_\star\land F\in{\rm SAT}\}.

\[
3m=4k,\qquad m_+=m_-=\frac{2k}{3},\qquad N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))

for the declared dense indexed serialization, not \(N=k\). Under D\"ocker's Theorem 1 and Corollary 2 (printed pp.7--9), \(\mathcal C_{M22}\)-SAT is NP-complete and every fully complementary-paired instance is SAT \cite{docker}. The latter statement is about full pairing, not pair-freeness.

\section{Exact cancellation theorem and the missing first defect}
For each unsigned triple \(S\), let \(p_S,q_S\in\{0,1\}\) indicate occurrence of its all-positive and all-negative clause. Put \(P=\{S:p_S=1\}\), \(Q=\{S:q_S=1\}\), \(H=P\cap Q\), \(P'=P\setminus Q\), \(Q'=Q\setminus P\), and \(q=|P'|\).
\begin{theorem}[Canonical residual degree balance, PROVED/EXACT in \(\mathcal C_{M22}\)]
For every \(F\in\mathcal C_{M22}\),
\[
 |P'|=|Q'|=q,\qquad
 \forall x:\quad \deg_{P'}(x)=\deg_{Q'}(x)\in\{0,1,2\}.
\]
Consequently \(q\ne1\), \(\mathcal P_{\rm pair}\iff q=0\), and
\[
F\in\mathcal C_\star\iff q\ge2.
\]
There are exactly \(2q\) unpaired signed clauses, and their common variable support
\(D=\bigcup P'=\bigcup Q'\) satisfies \(|D|\le3q\). All of \(q,D,H\) are independently recognizable from original signed fields in \(O(m\log(m+2))\) indexed-word sorting time and polynomial bit-work, with no SAT-label oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each fully matched support consumes one positive and one negative source occurrence of each of its three named variables. The original per-variable positive and negative counts are each two, so removing all matched supports leaves equal positive and negative residual degrees, each at most two. The total residual positive and negative occurrence counts are equal; dividing by three gives \(|P'|=|Q'|\). If \(q=1\), the unique positive and unique negative unmatched triples have identical per-variable incidence vectors, hence identical supports, contrary to their unmatched definition. Full pairing is precisely \(P=Q\). Every unmatched triple has three distinct sources, giving \(|D|\le3q\); the two residual unions coincide by degree equality. The explicit sorting recognizer charges each source record and signed comparison.
\end{proof}
This sharpens the earlier DUALPAIR61 even-defect observation (the minimum positive defect is four signed clauses), but neither defect count nor graph width is a mandatory SAT runtime.
