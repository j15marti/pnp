\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: relation code as binary column-matroid cycle space.}
Fix one SAME\_REALIZATION legal slice family in \(V=\mathbb F_2^m\), and write
\[
U=\operatorname{span}_v\bigwedge\nolimits^2S_v\subseteq\bigwedge\nolimits^2V\cong\mathbb F_2^{n_2},
\quad n_2=\binom m2,\quad d=\dim U.
\]
Choose a \(d\times n_2\) full-row-rank matrix \(G\) whose row space is \(U\). Let \(E=\{\{i,j\}:1\le i<j\le m\}\) index its columns and let \(M(G)\) be the represented binary column matroid. The common alternating quadratic-relation code is
\[
K=U^\perp=\ker G.
\]
Thus \(K\) is exactly the binary cycle space of \(M(G)\).

For every monomial-coordinate subset \(J\subseteq E\), define
\[
K[J]=\{x\in K:\operatorname{supp}(x)\subseteq J\}.
\]
Restriction to columns \(G_J\) gives
\[
\boxed{\dim K[J]=|J|-\operatorname{rank}G_J.}
\]
Hence \(K[J]\ne0\) iff \(J\) is dependent in \(M(G)\). A nonempty support \(C\subseteq E\) is inclusion-minimal among supports of nonzero relations iff \(C\) is a circuit of \(M(G)\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), a circuit has a unique nonzero dependence on its support, so minimal-support common quadratic relations are exactly circuit vectors.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: DUALDIST1 becomes a circuit theorem.}
Because \(\operatorname{rank}G=d\), every \(d+1\) columns are dependent. Thus every \(J\subseteq E\) with \(|J|=d+1\) contains a circuit and
\[
d_{\min}(K)=\operatorname{girth}M(G)\le d+1.
\]
This recovers DUALDIST1's Singleton consequence while identifying the sparse witness as a minimal column dependency. Locally, \(|J|-\operatorname{rank}G_J\) is exactly the supported relation dimension on \(J\), not a heuristic deficiency score.
