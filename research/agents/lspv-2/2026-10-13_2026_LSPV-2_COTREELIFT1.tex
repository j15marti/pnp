\section{Claim card, quantifiers, and selected obstacles}
The structured input is an explicit encoding of
\[
 G(X,c,Q)=\{(x,u):B_Xx=c\ \wedge\ Q(x_R,u)=1\},             \tag{1}
\]
where $X=(V,E)$ is a finite simple graph, $c\in\F^V$, $R\subseteq E$,

\[
 \forall A\ \forall k\ \forall C>0\ \forall N_0\ \exists G:

for every correct total deterministic uniform decider $A$ of one fixed
NP-complete language.  This report does not prove (2).

and the absence of a model-matched implication from residual structure to

\section{Exact cotree witness bijection}
Let $T$ be a spanning forest of $X$ and $C=E\setminus T$.  Call $c$
consistent when its XOR is zero on every connected component.

\begin{theorem}[Cotree witness bijection; independently reproduced]
For every consistent $c$, projection
\[
 \pi_C:\{x\in\F^E:B_Xx=c\}\longrightarrow\F^C              \tag{3}
\]
is a bijection.  Both directions of the witness map are computable in
polynomial bit time and polynomial workspace from the explicit input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The difference of any two affine solutions lies in the cycle space
$C(X)=\ker B_X$.  A cycle-space vector whose cotree coordinates vanish is
supported on the forest $T$, hence is zero; thus (3) is injective.  The cycle
space has dimension
\[
 |E|-|V|+\operatorname{cc}(X)=|C|,
\]
so a consistent affine coset has exactly $2^{|C|}$ points and (3) is
surjective.  Given $y\in\F^C$, fix $x_C=y$ and solve the remaining tree
equations leaf-to-root in each component.  Conversely, projection is a direct
read.  Forest discovery, charge updates and reconstruction are linear or
polynomial in the explicit bit encoding.
\end{proof}

This theorem is not new relative to the corpus: the lead exploration
\path{research/explorations/2026-10-13_AFFINE-RESIDUAL-RANK1.tex} already
proved the connected-complement bypass.  The present run independently
reproduces it and checks reconstruction on every small case rather than
claiming originality.

\begin{corollary}[Exact residual relocation]
For every explicitly encoded Boolean circuit or CNF $\varphi(y,u)$,
\[
 \exists x,u\,[B_Xx=c\land\varphi(x_C,u)]
 \iff \exists y,u\,\varphi(y,u).                           \tag{4}
\]
YES witnesses map in both directions with polynomial overhead.  Thus a cotree
embedding can carry arbitrary 3SAT exactly, but the affine wrapper supplies no
additional hardness or contraction by itself.
\end{corollary}

For general $R\subseteq E$, the already verified identity
\[
 \rho=\dim\pi_R\ker B_X
      =|R|+\operatorname{cc}(X)-\operatorname{cc}(X-R)       \tag{5}
\]
gives $2^\rho$ feasible projections.  If $Q$ also reads $h$ external free
variables, direct enumeration costs $2^{\rho+h}\operatorname{poly}(N)$.
The factor $2^h$ cannot be silently dropped: a one-edge tree has $\rho=0$,
but $Q(u)=u$ requires choosing $u=1$.

\section{Fresh versus shared nonlinear coupling}
\begin{theorem}[Fresh AND lifting]
Augment every affine edge variable $x_e$ by fresh variables $p_e,q_e$ and the
quadratic relation
\[
 x_e=p_eq_e.                                                \tag{6}
\]
The augmented system is satisfiable exactly when $B_Xx=c$ is satisfiable, and
every affine witness has the uniform linear-time lift $p_e=q_e=x_e$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $x_e=0$, $(p_e,q_e)=(0,0)$ satisfies (6); if $x_e=1$, $(1,1)$ satisfies
(6).  Apply this independently to every edge.  Projection of any augmented
witness recovers the original affine witness, proving the converse.
\end{proof}

Therefore ``quadratic'' or ``nonlinear'' is not a hardness condition when the
new variables are fresh and independently liftable.

\begin{theorem}[Shared-variable failure on the smallest simple cycle]
Let $X$ be a triangle with zero charge.  Its affine solutions are precisely
$000$ and $111$.  Adding
\[
 x_0x_1\oplus x_2=1                                       \tag{7}
\]
makes the system unsatisfiable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The zero-charge triangle equations force $x_0=x_1=x_2$.  On $000$, the left
side of (7) is $0$; on $111$, it is $1\oplus1=0$.  Hence neither affine
solution extends.
\end{proof}

This falsifies a blanket theorem that an affine witness always extends through
shared-variable quadratic coupling.  It does not prove hardness: the example
is constant-size, and general cotree coupling can be exactly arbitrary SAT by
(4).

\section{Hostile controls, wrappers, and barriers}
\paragraph{Controls.}
Ordinary affine Tseitin remains polynomial by forest reconstruction.  The
cotree theorem covers disconnected graphs, isolates, independent components,
renaming, complementation, clause shuffling, planted YES/NO residuals, and
external variables.  The friendship family supplies bounded-treewidth,
high-rank, nonlinear-but-easy controls.  Horn and 2SAT remain covered by the
repository hostile-control suite; nothing here assigns them extra cost.

\paragraph{Wrappers and compact representations.}
DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP can wrap the same explicit decoder,
Gaussian solver, and tree lifter.  They do not force enumeration of every
projection or materialization of a DNNF.  A compact arithmetic circuit for
$Q$ is allowed; its actual evaluation cost is charged.  Conversely, if $Q$
is arbitrary 3SAT on cotree coordinates, (4) gives no shortcut beyond solving
that residual.

\paragraph{Relativization and algebrization.}
The exact bijection relativizes: an oracle for $Q$ can be queried on cotree
assignments, and a semantic SAT oracle decides (4) directly.  Algebraic-oracle
access may evaluate shared residuals without exposing their monomials.  Thus
no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing hinge appears.  No large constructive
circuit property is used, so Natural Proofs is not the immediate barrier.

\paragraph{Post-quantum ledger.}
Puncturing/shortening of the linear cycle code is an exact object transfer and
is algorithmically exploitable.  Quadratic-relation rank, Schur squares,
Hilbert functions, and syzygies do not force search time here; importing their
cryptanalytic success is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} absent a model-matched
theorem.  Collision entropy of $\pi_R$ exactly counts uniform affine fibers,
but fiber size is not decision time.  LWE-style external extraction and
special-soundness remain analogies requiring new unconditional bridges.
