\section{Canonical source, literature and three hypotheses}
A formula in $\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ has unique clauses, each
a proper monotone three-literal OR, and each active original
variable occurs twice positively and twice negatively.
For a clause $C$, $\bar C$ switches all signs but retains OR.
$\mathcal P_{\rm pair}$ comprises only fully complemented
formulas, and $\mathcal C_\star=\mathcal C_{\rm M22}\setminus
\mathcal P_{\rm pair}$. The target language $L_\star$ accepts
valid satisfiable sources and rejects malformed strings.
Partial complementary pairs, disconnected easy formulas and
nonminimal UNSAT formulas are included. With $k=n(F)$,
$m$ clauses and ordinary indexed bit length $N$,
$3m=4k$, $m_+=m_-=2k/3$ and
$N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$, not $N=k$.

Let $P,Q$ be the unsigned supports of positive and negative
clauses, $H=P\cap Q$, $P'=P\setminus Q$, $Q'=Q\setminus P$,
$q=|P'|=|Q'|$. The per-variable cancellation
$\deg_{P'}(v)=\deg_{Q'}(v)$ and $q=0$ or $q\ge2$ are already
proved in M22-DEFECT-ADAPTER131. D\"ocker's Theorem 1 proves
NP-completeness of $\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$, while its fully
paired members are SAT. M22-NONPAIRED1 uses fixed SAT
six-variable $Y$ with positive supports
$123,145,246,356$, negative supports $124,136,235,456$,

$L_\star$ NP-complete. This is not a decision lower bound.

\section{The all-size $q=2$ theorem}
\begin{lemma}[Positive-primal four-colorability]
For every $F\in\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ the simple graph
$G_P$ connecting source variables that share a
positive clause admits a proper four-coloring,
constructible in polynomial time.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A source variable appears in exactly two
positive triples, so $\Delta(G_P)\le4$.
A $K_5$ component is impossible: its five
vertices already exhaust degree four and
have no external neighbors, but exact
positive source degree two would require
$3|P_{\rm comp}|=2\cdot5=10$.
A component with maximum degree at most three
is greedily four-colorable. For a degree-four
component, Brooks' theorem provides a
four-coloring because it is not $K_5$.
The constructive Brooks algorithm and
graph generation are polynomial.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[First nonpaired defect layer; PROVED/EXACT]
For every $F\in\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ with
$q(F)=2$, there is a Boolean original-source
assignment $x$ such that
\[
 \boxed{1\le\sum_{v\in S}x_v\le2
 \qquad\text{for every }S\in P\cup Q.}
\]
In particular $F\in\mathcal C_\star$ is SAT.
A single uniform source-aware algorithm
recognizes and constructs the witness
with polynomial elementary bit work
and polynomial simultaneously live
writable/index bits, including validation.
Hence every UNSAT member of the canonical
target necessarily has $q\ge3$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $P'=\{A,B\}$ and $Q'=\{C,D\}$.
The residual degree equality entails
$\bigcup P'=\bigcup Q'$, hence
$C,D\subseteq A\cup B$.
Properly four-color the graph $G_P$.
Every positive triple has three
distinct colors, so every member
of $H$ also has three distinct colors.
Any three members of $A\cup B$
include two from $A$ or two from $B$,
by pigeonhole. Those two have different
colors. Therefore each $C,D$ uses
at least two distinct colors.

Partition four color names into two
unlabeled pairs. There are three such
partitions: $01|23$, $02|13$, $03|12$.
Every triple with three distinct
colors is bichromatic under each
partition. A triple with at least
two distinct colors is monochromatic
under at most one partition: if it
uses exactly two colors, exactly
one pair-partition groups them
together; if it uses three, none does.
Thus $C$ forbids at most one choice,
and $D$ at most one choice. At least
one of the three choices works.
Converting the four colors to two
bits using this partition makes
every unsigned support in $P\cup Q$
nonmonochromatic, proving the
displayed NAE property and satisfying
every positive and negative OR clause.
Validation, $q$ discovery, Brooks
coloring, three partition tests and
source witness replay are ordinary
charged polynomial operations.
\end{proof}
