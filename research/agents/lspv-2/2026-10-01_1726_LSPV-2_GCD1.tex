\paragraph{Input from WINDOWREC1/SYNCWEDGE1.}
Fix one legal line and the declared SU05/Hadamard realization. Let
\[
a_t=(b_1(t),\ldots,b_k(t))\in\mathbb F_2^k,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z,

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: cyclic-polynomial annihilator theorem.}
Put
\[
R_N:=\mathbb F_2[z]/(z^N-1),\qquad
B_j(z):=\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}b_j(t)z^t\in R_N.
\]
Let
\[
D(z):=\gcd\!\bigl(B_1(z),\ldots,B_k(z),z^N-1\bigr)
\]
with the monic representative in \(\mathbb F_2[z]\), and define
\[
H(z):=\frac{z^N-1}{D(z)}.
\]
Up to the harmless reversal convention relating correlation to cyclic convolution, the full length-\(N\) simultaneous recurrence module is
\[
\boxed{\mathcal A:=\{C\in R_N:C B_j=0\ \forall j\}=(H)\subseteq R_N.}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\mathcal A=\deg D.}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The ideal of \(R_N\) generated by the coordinate words is the principal ideal \((D)\). Indeed Bezout in \(\mathbb F_2[z]\) shows that \(D\) lies in the ideal generated by \(B_1,\ldots,B_k,z^N-1\), and each \(B_j\) is divisible by \(D\). Thus the simultaneous annihilator is \(\operatorname{Ann}_{R_N}(D)\). Writing \(z^N-1=DH\), one has \(HD=0\) in \(R_N\), so \((H)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}(D)\). Conversely, if \(CD\equiv0\pmod{DH}\), then \(DH\mid CD\) in the polynomial ring; cancellation in the integral domain \(\mathbb F_2[z]\) gives \(H\mid C\). Hence \(\operatorname{Ann}(D)=(H)\). The ideal \((H)\) has basis \(H,zH,\ldots,z^{\deg D-1}H\) modulo \(z^N-1\), giving dimension \(\deg D\).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: WINDOWREC1 kernel as a support intersection.}
Let
\[
P_m:=\{C(z)\in R_N:\deg C<m\},
\]
after fixing the WINDOWREC1 origin/reversal convention. Then
\[
\boxed{W_L^\perp\cong P_m\cap(H).}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{\dim W_L^\perp\le \min\{m,\deg D\}.}
\]
Most importantly,
\[
\boxed{D=1\Longrightarrow W_L=\mathbb F_2^m\quad\text{for every }m\le N.}
\]
The converse need not hold for a particular \(m\): the full cyclic annihilator may be nonzero while containing no nonzero representative supported inside the chosen length-\(m\) window.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: exact finite algorithm.}
Once the exact legal word is acquired, compute the \(k\) polynomials \(B_j\), then the single gcd \(D\). If \(D=1\), the linear half of the WEDGE1 certificate is discharged immediately for all \(m\le N\). If \(D\ne1\), compute a basis of \((H)\) and intersect it with \(P_m\) by Gaussian elimination. This returns the complete WINDOWREC1 linear recurrence kernel. Thus hidden cyclic linear structure is algorithmically exploitable, not a hardness source.
