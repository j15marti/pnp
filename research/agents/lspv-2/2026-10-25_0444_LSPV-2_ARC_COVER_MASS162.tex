\section{New exact bounded-occurrence cover obstruction}
Let $F$ be a valid proper explicit CNF with $k$ active
original variable names, $m$ clauses, encoded bit length
$N$, and at most $\Delta$ original clause occurrences of
each variable. A partial original-variable assignment
$\alpha$ has depth $d=|\operatorname{dom}\alpha|$.
Its \emph{raw restriction} substitutes only those named
bits: clauses already satisfied disappear, pinned false
literals disappear, and every clause disjoint from the
pinned-variable set remains present unchanged in the
ordinary fully serialized residual. Source identities,
headers, shortened clauses, and empty clauses are
handled correctly. Additional semantic simplifications,
learned consequences or compressed pointers are not
raw restrictions.

A \emph{tautological pin-cover} is a family of consistent
partial assignments $\mathcal D=\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_B\}$
such that every complete $k$-variable assignment extends
at least one term. The terms may overlap, have different
lengths, be chosen adaptively or from the fixed source code
of a presumed exact solver $A$.
\begin{theorem}[Global raw short-child coverage cost]
Assume all ordinary raw residual children in $\mathcal D$
have complete explicit binary length at most $L$.
For a fixed ordinary encoding in which each retained clause
record uses at least a positive constant number of bits,
\[
 \boxed{B\ge2^{(m-O(L))/\Delta}}.
\]
For canonical $C_{\rm M22}$, namely unique homogeneous
proper OR3 clauses with two occurrences in each sign
per original variable,
$3m=4k$, $\Delta=4$, and
$N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$. If
$L\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ for fixed $\lambda<1$, then
\[
 \boxed{ B\ge 2^{k/3-o(k)}=2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}}.
\]
The result applies equally to SAT and UNSAT inputs and
to $C_\star=C_{\rm M22}\setminus P_{\rm pair}$, which
excludes ONLY fully complementary-paired formulas,
not partial pairs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A term pinning $d$ names touches at most $\Delta d$
original clauses. Hence at least $m-\Delta d$ original
clauses remain completely unchanged. Their output
encoding consumes $\Omega(m-\Delta d)$ bits.
From output length at most $L$, we obtain
$d\ge m/\Delta-O(L/\Delta)$.
Every partial Boolean assignment of depth $d$ covers
exactly fraction $2^{-d}$ of the full $k$-cube.
A tautological cover must satisfy
\[
 1\le\sum_{j=1}^B2^{-|\alpha_j|}
   \le B2^{-m/\Delta+O(L/\Delta)}.
\]
Rearrange. On $C_{\rm M22}$,
$m/\Delta=k/3$, while
$N^{\lambda+o(1)}=o(k)$ for any fixed $\lambda<1$,
giving the announced exponent.
\end{proof}
This is a theorem about the \emph{number of terms in
the complete raw cover plan}, not an unconditional
running-time lower bound on an arbitrary decider.
Its proof does not require a hard instance or an UNSAT
label. A SAT witness can terminate early; a useful
UNSAT certificate may omit large branches altogether.
Semantic source rewriting may eliminate even untouched
clauses, which invalidates the raw-child premise.
A succinct shared DAG need not explicitly evaluate
all terms, but its actual total semantics and operations
then require their own charged proof.

\section{Sharp source fixture and finite comparison}
The canonical $Y_6$ has positive supports
$123,145,246,356$ and negative supports
$124,136,235,456$. It has six original names,
eight unique homogeneous proper ternary clauses,
two positive and two negative occurrences per name,
no complementary pair, witness $111000$, and
exactly 20 Boolean models. Therefore $Y_6\in C_\star$.
Pins $\{3,4\}$ touch all eight original ternaries.
No single name can do so because its degree is four.
In disjoint copies $Y_6^{\sqcup p}$, at least
$2p=k/3$ pins are necessary to touch all source
ternaries, and pinning names $3,4$ in each block
suffices. The complete $2^{2p}$-term cube
partition is therefore sharp for the \emph{necessary
original-ternary-hit condition}. It does NOT
produce a theorem that the fully serialized residual
is power-short: surviving binary/unit clauses may
still occupy $\Theta(N)$ bits.
This product is easy by source recognition.
No claim of per-instance hardness is made.

\section{Semantic pruning and the exact verification trap}
For each clause $C$, define the cylinder of
falsifying assignments
$Z_C=\{\sigma:\sigma\text{ falsifies }C\}$.
The exact identity
\[
 \bigcup_{C\in F}Z_C
    =\{0,1\}^k\setminus\operatorname{Mod}(F)
\]
shows that source clauses already list local
certificates for \emph{all nonmodels}.
Deciding if their union covers the entire cube
is exactly ordinary UNSAT and is coNP-complete for
general 3CNF. Thus a small list of valid exclusions
does not supply a free global tautology certificate.

For the source-recognizable SAT family
$Y_6^{\sqcup p}$, the fraction of all assignments
covered by the source falsification cylinders equals
\[
 1-(20/64)^p=1-(5/16)^p\longrightarrow1.
\]
This high mass gives neither the UNSAT label nor
a time lower bound. The eight proper signed ternaries
$F_8$ on three variables are UNSAT with full coverage;
deleting one leaves one model and coverage $7/8$.

If a separately \emph{proved and charged} pruning
scheme excludes a union of cylinders of normalized
mass $q$, while $B$ remaining live pin terms all
have depth at least $h$ and their cylinders,
together with the pruned union, cover the cube,
then the union bound supplies only
$q\ge1-B2^{-h}$.
Computing and certifying that $q$ and the full
coverage hold is NOT free. This is exactly the
missing semantic-acquisition hinge, not a
contradiction from recurrence algebra.

\section{Two actual recursion levels and all-input fallback}
For any fixed uniform total correct ordinary-SAT
solver $A$, define an exact wrapper $Q_A$:
reject malformed encodings; if fewer than two
active variables call $A$ directly. Otherwise
scan source occurrences to pick a first original
variable, fully construct and serialize the two
ordinary children after its two values, choose a
second variable \emph{separately from each
first-level state}, and fully construct and
serialize the two ordinary grandchildren.
Execute $A$ on all four ordinary children.
OR their exact SAT labels. If a witness is
returned for an accepting child, reconcile its
original named pins and replay the source.
For an UNSAT parent all four ordinary children
are UNSAT. The exact parent equivalence is
Shannon expansion over these two state-dependent
pin choices, not independent incompatible
source-variable joins.

The eager complete-buffer version has simultaneous
conservative bounds
\[
 T_{Q_A}(N)=O(N\operatorname{polylog}N+4T_A(O(N))),
 \quad
 S_{Q_A}(N)=O(N+S_A(O(N))),
\]
hence only
$t'\le\max\{1,t\}$,
$s'\le\max\{1,s\}$.
This is an all-input CORRECT but NONCONTRACTING
algorithm transformation. Invalid strings are rejected.
If descendants leave $C_\star$, they remain
eligible ordinary-SAT inputs. No ancestor, source
read, builder, SAT guest call or output bit is free.
