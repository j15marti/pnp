\section{Fixed-matrix projection and row-refutation lemma}
\begin{theorem}

\[
A_Cz+A_Wy=b\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2.
\]
Let $r=\operatorname{rank}(A_W)$ and let $L$ span the left nullspace, so $LA_W=0$. For each compatible selected-edge vector $\mathbf j$ and its uniquely defined cycle template $z_{\mathbf j}$,
\[
\mathbf j\text{ has SAT extensions}\iff L(b+A_Cz_{\mathbf j})=0.
\]
Every consistent choice has exactly $2^{|W|-r}$ extensions. With $B$ the number of compatible consistent selections,
\[
\boxed{\#\operatorname{SAT}(F\mid p)=B\,2^{|W|-r}.}
\]
The affine coefficient factorization need only be computed ONCE per saturated residual. A row of $LA_Cz=Lb$ whose support has become fully fixed under a partial cycle-template selection and whose parity fails is a sound source-row-XOR proof excluding \emph{all} suffix choices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Delete a selected edge of a simple odd cycle. The remaining even-length path forces alternating values and returns the same assigned value at the opposite endpoint, giving a unique template, which satisfies the selected edge twice and every other edge once. Reject overlapping templates assigning contradictory values. The set of cycle vertices $C$ is independent of selection. Original uncovered noncycle clauses and covered-spill preferences are likewise fixed, hence their matrix coefficients $A_C,A_W$ and RHS $b$ do not change between choices. Gaussian elimination proves that $A_Wy=b+A_Cz$ is consistent precisely when the RHS lies in the column space of $A_W$, equivalently to $L(b+A_Cz)=0$. Its solution kernel has dimension $|W|-r$. The inherited source-conservation theorem gives a unique selected-edge vector for every satisfying source completion. Conversely the cycle template and odd-parity outside rows make every original uncovered clause true; covered clauses stay true by $p$. Branches are disjoint and the formula follows. A violated left-nullspace equation supported only on already assigned template columns cannot be repaired by any suffix; its row-XOR combination is an explicit refutation, conditional on the authenticated saturation premises.
\end{proof}
