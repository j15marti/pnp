\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\begin{theorem}[Global cube-cover soundness]
Let a cube be a partial Boolean assignment. A leaf node $(\alpha,j)$ is legal only if every literal of input clause $C_j$ is assigned false by $\alpha$. A split node $(\alpha,x,L,R)$ has an unset $x$ and two earlier children with cubes precisely $\alpha\cup\{x=0\}$ and $\alpha\cup\{x=1\}$. An empty-cube root of a valid finite DAG certifies that the formula is UNSAT. The certificate is checkable in $O(B\operatorname{poly}(N))$ bit time for $B$ explicit nodes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on node order: each leaf has only falsifying extensions; every extension of a split follows exactly one sound child. The empty root covers all assignments. No polynomial bound on $B$ is asserted.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Charged strict Resolution-to-sCP generation]
Let $F$ have $n$ variables and $m$ clauses. For a supplied variable order $\pi$ of filled-primal-graph elimination width $w$, Davis--Putnam elimination decides SAT and, for UNSAT, emits an explicit strict Resolution and corresponding Boolean-semantic Cutting Planes refutation with at most
\[
S\le m+n9^w,\qquad T_{\rm output}=O((m+n9^w)\operatorname{poly}(N)).
\]
Its parent indices, input access, proof clauses, boolean coefficients, and full serialization are charged. Verification is polynomial in $N+|\mathrm{proof}|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Eliminating pivot $x$ by pairwise opposite-polarity Resolution preserves satisfiability. Every clause containing $x$ has other variables drawn from at most $w$ remaining neighbors. Each polarity bucket has at most $3^w$ different clauses, so at most $9^w$ pairs per pivot. Every retained resolvent carries two earlier proof-line references. If UNSAT, the final empty clause is derived. Clause inequalities $\sum_{\ell\in C}\ell\ge1$ give an sCP refutation one line per Resolution inference with Boolean coefficients 0 or 1. The explicit data structures and recorded lines cost the stated polynomial bit overhead. Polynomiality on a source class requires a \emph{uniformly supplied} order of width at most $c\log N$ for a fixed $c$; no arbitrary $A$ is transformed by this statement.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Exact $2^k$-clause cover obstruction]
A proper $k$-CNF of exactly $2^k$ clauses is satisfiable if $\sum_v p_vq_v<\binom{2^k}{2}$, where $p_v,q_v$ are its positive and negative occurrence counts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The $2^k$ clause-falsification subcubes have total cardinality $2^n$. If UNSAT they must partition all assignments disjointly. Every pair must conflict on some shared variable with opposite signs, yet variable $v$ separates at most $p_vq_v$ clause pairs. The inequality makes this impossible.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Small signed exact-$(2,2)$ sources]
Every valid signed exact-$(2,2)$ proper 3-CNF on three or six active variables is SAT. At six variables, it has at least three satisfying assignments.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $n=3$, $m=4<8$; the clause cubes cannot cover all assignments. At $n=6$, $m=8$, while $\sum_vp_vq_v=24<28$. Thus at least four pairs of clause-falsification cubes overlap. Writing $r(a)$ for clauses falsified at assignment $a$, $\sum_a r(a)=64$ and the number $U$ of satisfying assignments is $\sum_{r(a)\ge2}(r(a)-1)$. Intersections yield $\sum_a\binom{r(a)}2\ge4$. If $U\le2$, this last sum is at most three, contradiction.
\end{proof}
