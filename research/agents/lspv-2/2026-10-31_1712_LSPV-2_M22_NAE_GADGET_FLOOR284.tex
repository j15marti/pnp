\section{An exact typed boundary-replacement API}
Let $F=E(O,B)\wedge H(B,I)$ where $I$ is private to $H$, and let $F'=E(O,B)\wedge H'(B,I')$ with new private variables $I'$. All original signed occurrences and clause indices remain source-authenticated. Write $P_H(b)\equiv\exists i\,H(b,i)$.
\begin{theorem}[Boundary-interface conservation and both-label replacement]
If (i) $F,F'$ pass the full canonical target validator, (ii) the signed multiplicities of each boundary variable in $H$ and $H'$ coincide, and (iii) $P_H(b)=P_{H'}(b)$ for \emph{every} $b\in\{0,1\}^{B}$, then $F$ and $F'$ agree pointwise on every assignment to $O\cup B$ after existentially hiding private variables. In particular $\operatorname{SAT}(F)\Leftrightarrow\operatorname{SAT}(F')$, and any child witness reconstructs a parent witness by solving $H|_b$ and checking all original clauses. An original-source refutation is not automatically transported by the semantic equation alone; a complete NO certificate requires a separately checked proof or correct total exact decider.
For any such proper balanced interface, with $p=|I|$, $m_H^+,m_H^-$ the signed clause counts and $d_H^{\pm}(v)$ the boundary occurrence counts,
\[
  3m_H^{\pm}=2p+\sum_{v\in B}d_H^{\pm}(v),\qquad
  p-p'\equiv0\pmod 3,\qquad
  m_H^{\pm}-m_{H'}^{\pm}=\frac{2(p-p')}{3}.
\]
Thus any quota-preserving private-variable removal occurs in multiples of three. The theorem does not guarantee the existence of a semantics-preserving shrinker or bounded discovery cost.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}The existential projection of $E\wedge H$ on $O\cup B$ is $E(O,B)\wedge P_H(B)$; substitute (iii). Each private variable contributes two occurrences per sign, and each clause contributes three per sign, yielding the two incidence equalities. Subtraction and integrality give the divisibility claim. Witness extension uses the equality of projections and complete verification against the original source.\end{proof}
