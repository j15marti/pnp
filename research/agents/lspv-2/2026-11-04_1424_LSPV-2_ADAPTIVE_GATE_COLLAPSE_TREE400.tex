\begin{theorem}[Exact static-versus-adaptive separation]

Thus the old translation bound is $2^{n-1}(3n)-1$ and the new bound $3n^2-1$, while $W_{\rm Res}(F_n)=n$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} If fewer than $n-1$ inputs are fixed, setting them to one leaves $y_n$ a conjunction of at least two free variables, neither literal nor constant; any $n-1$ variables suffice. For the adaptive upper bound, query $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{n-1}$ until a zero, stopping there, with a final all-ones branch leaving $y_n=x_n$: $n$ leaves. For the lower bound a conjunction of $s$ free variables forces a first query, whose zero child contributes one leaf and one child still needs $s-1$ leaves. By induction the minimum is $s$.
