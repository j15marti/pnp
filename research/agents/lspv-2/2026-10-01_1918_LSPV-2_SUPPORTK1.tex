\paragraph{Setup.}
Let
\[
R_N=\mathbb F_2[z]/(z^N-1),\qquad
E=(R_N)^{m-1},\qquad
F=(R_N)^{\binom{k}{2}}.
\]
For each lag $1\le\delta<m$ and coordinate pair $r<t$, DIAGCONV1 defines the cyclic correlation word $h_{rt,\delta}\in R_N$. Define the $R_N$-linear relaxed convolution map
\[
\widetilde\Phi:E\to F,\qquad
\widetilde\Phi((q_\delta))_{rt}
=\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}q_\delta h_{rt,\delta}.
\]
Let
\[
S=\bigoplus_{\delta=1}^{m-1}P_{m-\delta}\subset E,
\]
where $P_j$ denotes the zero-padded degree-$<j$ streams. Then $\dim_{\mathbb F_2}S=\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}(m-\delta)=\binom m2$.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: support-intersection identity.}
The actual same-shift exterior relation space is exactly

This isolates the only obstruction to direct Fourier decomposition: not the convolution equations, but the support intersection.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: semisimple relaxed-kernel dimension.}
Assume $N$ is odd. Then $z^N-1$ is square-free over characteristic two. Extend scalars to a splitting field $K$. For each $N$-th root $\omega$, let
\[
\mathcal H(\omega)_{(r,t),\delta}=\widehat h_{rt,\delta}(\omega).
\]
DFT gives an isomorphism under which $\widetilde\Phi$ is the direct product of the matrices $\mathcal H(\omega)$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\ker\widetilde\Phi
=
\sum_{\omega^N=1}\bigl((m-1)-\operatorname{rank}_K\mathcal H(\omega)\bigr).
}
\]
The equality of dimensions follows because scalar extension preserves rank and kernel dimension. This is exact for the relaxed cyclic module, not for the triangularly supported kernel.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: support-aware forced-kernel bound.}
Since $S,\ker\widetilde\Phi\le E$ and $\dim E=N(m-1)$, Grassmann's inequality gives
\[
\boxed{
\dim U_L^\perp

\dim\ker\widetilde\Phi-N(m-1)\right\}.

\[
