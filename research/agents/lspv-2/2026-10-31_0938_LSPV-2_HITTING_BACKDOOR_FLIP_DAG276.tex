\begin{theorem}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $b$. Delete clauses containing a fixed true literal; every other fixed literal is false, so the surviving original clauses restrict to width at most two on $R$. For a NO residual, run complete Davis--Putnam elimination on $R$. The final restricted clause set contains an empty clause. For every restricted resolution step, resolve the full original-source descendants on the same variable of $R$, retaining their outside literals. All outside literals are false under $b$, so no complementary outside pair can arise. The full clause corresponding to the restricted empty clause contains only outside literals, all false under $b$. It is the desired original-derived $D_b$. When all $b$ are rejected, the conjunction of $D_b$ excludes every $T$ assignment, and resolving on $T$ produces an empty clause with original-source ancestry. For a YES residual, direct witness reconstruction and checking establishes satisfiability.
\end{proof}
