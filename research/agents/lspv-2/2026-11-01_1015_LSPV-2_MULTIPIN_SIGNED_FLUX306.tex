\section{Exact multi-pin obstruction and proof}
Let $F$ be a connected canonical original of $n$ variables and $p$ a coherent assignment to $t\ge1$ source variables. Include isolated free variables among residual primal components $K$. Write $v_K$ for free variables and $u_{s,K}$ for uncovered original sign-$s$ clauses containing free names in $K$. Let $A_{s,K}$ count sign-$s$ free occurrences in clauses covered by $p$, and $H_{s,K}$ count pinned-false sign-$s$ occurrences in uncovered clauses of $K$. All quantities have authenticated original clause IDs. Put
\[
B_{+,K}=A_{+,K}+H_{-,K},\quad B_{-,K}=A_{-,K}+H_{+,K},\quad B_K=B_{+,K}+B_{-,K}.
\]
\begin{theorem}
For every such $F,p,K$,
\[
3u_{s,K}-H_{s,K}=2v_K-A_{s,K},\qquad
B_{+,K}-B_{-,K}=3(u_{-,K}-u_{+,K}).
\]
Each residual free component has $B_K\ge2$. The complete residual has at most $3t$ components; if all pins are TRUE or all FALSE, at most $2t$. Therefore one component contains at least $\lceil(n-t)/(3t)\rceil$ free source variables, or $\lceil(n-t)/(2t)\rceil$ for one-polarity pins.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Count each of the two occurrences of sign $s$ of each free variable: a slot is either in a covered clause ($A_s$), or in an uncovered clause, which contributes $3u_s-H_s$ free slots. This proves the first identity and subtracting signs proves the second. Every component must touch a pin-boundary source clause because the original incidence graph is connected; that boundary contributes positive $A_s$ or $H_s$. Thus $B_K>0$. The congruence $B_+-B_-\equiv0\pmod3$ forbids $B_K=1$.
Each pinned variable can cause at most four free occurrences in two covered true-sign clauses and at most two pinned-false slots in its two other clauses, so $\sum_K B_K\le6t$. Hence $2c\le6t$. For same-value pins one of $B_+,B_-$ is zero on each component, so each positive $B_K$ is divisible by three and $3c\le6t$. Pigeonhole proves the component-size bounds.
\end{proof}
For every fixed $0<\lambda<1$, $t=o(n^{1-\lambda})$ forces a raw source-identity component with $\omega(n^\lambda)$ names. This does \emph{not} imply any lower bound for semantic compression or arbitrary SAT algorithms, nor a child-bit length statement.
