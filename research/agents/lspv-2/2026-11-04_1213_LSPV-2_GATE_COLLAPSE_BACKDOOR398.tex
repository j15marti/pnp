\section*{Fixed subgrammar and theorem}
Original clauses of any finite explicitly encoded valid UNSAT CNF $F$ are free premises. A paid acyclic conservative gate $y\leftrightarrow(a\land b)$ introduces its exact three defining facets $(\neg y\vee a),(\neg y\vee b),(y\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)$ at cost one. Every distinct strict binary Resolution inference costs one; DAG sharing is allowed. This $E_{\land2}$ is a proper fragment of the same fixed $R_{\rm mix341}$, and this theorem does not erase AND3, native GF(2), or LOCAL-CONVERT.
A set $B$ of $b$ original variables is a \emph{literal-collapse backdoor} for the entire acyclic gate bank $D$ when, for every $\alpha:B\to\{0,1\}$, recursive gate substitution makes each output a constant or a single signed literal of a residual \emph{original} variable. This is syntactically checkable given $B$ and the branch substitution tables; obtaining a best $B$ need not be efficient.
\begin{lemma}[Signed substitution and guarded discharge]
Strict shared-DAG Resolution under a simultaneous variable-to-signed-literal/constant substitution admits a derivation of a subclause of every non-tautological image with no more paid Resolution nodes. For any $\alpha:B\to\{0,1\}$, an $r$-node refutation of $F|_\alpha$ can be lifted from the original sources to a subclause of the guard $G_\alpha$ falsified by $\alpha$ with at most $r$ nodes, using no free weakening.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on source-ordered derived lines. When a mapped pivot remains a complementary pair, resolve once; if it maps to a constant or disappears from a strengthened parent, inherit the stronger parent. A tautological parent can be discarded if its target image is not tautological. For branch lifting, a nontrue restricted source $C|_\alpha$ has $C\subseteq (C|_\alpha)\cup G_\alpha$, so carry original source clauses and replay the same strengthened-parent induction on residual pivots. The projected empty clause lifts to a subclause of $G_\alpha$.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[All-size gate-collapse erasure]
For every finite valid UNSAT CNF $F$, every complete acyclic conservative AND2 bank $D$, every $b$-original-variable literal-collapse backdoor $B$, and every shared-DAG strict Resolution refutation of $F\cup D$ with $r$ derived Resolution nodes,
\[
\boxed{ W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le2^b(r+1)-1. }
\]
In particular the exponent is independent of the number $g$ of paid gate definitions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\alpha$ on $B$. Substitute the declared signed literal or constant for each gate output. Every defining facet is now a Boolean tautology, since its output is exactly its gate function in the cofactor. By substitution closure, the proof reduces to a strict $F|_\alpha$ refutation with at most $r$ nodes. Guard lifting produces a derivation of some subclause of $G_\alpha$ with at most $r$ nodes. Merge the $2^b$ branches in a binary decision tree on $B$, resolving opposite guard literals when present and otherwise inheriting a stronger branch clause. At most $2^b-1$ new inferences are needed. Total $\le 2^b r+2^b-1$, and no gate-derived source survives.
\end{proof}

\section*{ITERATE: direct consequence for certified hard PHP}
Let $P_h$ be Haken's \emph{explicit unary} PHP with $h+1$ pigeons and $h$ holes, $h(h+1)$ variables and $m_h=h+1+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses. Its signed-index encoding is $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. Haken (1985): $\exists c>0,h_0\ \forall h\ge h_0\ \forall$ ordinary shared-DAG Resolution proofs $\pi$ of $P_h$, total lines $|\pi|\ge 2^{ch}$. It \emph{does not} quantify over ER, $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$, $R_{\rm mix341}$ or SAT algorithms. Therefore, for \emph{every} paid AND2 bank and strict Resolution refutation with $r$ derived lines and every backdoor $B$ of size $b$,
\[
\boxed{2^b(r+1)\ge2^{ch}-m_h+1.}
\]
If $r$ is polynomial in $N_h$, then $b\ge ch-O(\log h)$. Thus a polynomial-Resolution-node proof of this established hard family cannot have a gate bank collapsible on only $O(\log h)$ original inputs, even if the number of gates is large. This is a restriction on \emph{this fragment's extensions}, not a general lower bound on mixed minima or machine time. Cook's polynomial ER proofs need not satisfy a small-collapse-backdoor hypothesis.
