\section{Independent proof}
\begin{theorem}[decoded unique-extension composition]
Let $\operatorname{Decode}(I)$ recover $F$ and an ordered list
$R_1,\ldots,R_D$ in time $N^c$, where $N=|I|$ and $D\le N$.  Suppose one
fixed uniform algorithm $E$ satisfies, for every well-typed prefix,
\[
R_j(I,y_{<j},y_j)iff y_j=E(I,j,y_{<j}),                 \tag{2}
\]
and each call, its output, and verification cost at most $N^c$.  Then
projection is a witness bijection
\[
\operatorname{Sat}(G_I)\cong\operatorname{Sat}(F),      \tag{3}
\]
and any source witness finder of time $T(F)$ yields a complete target witness
finder of time $T(F)+O(N^{c+1})$ and polynomial space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Given $y_0$, evaluate (2) in topological order.  Existence follows by setting
$y_j=E(I,j,y_{<j})$; uniqueness follows because (2) forbids every other
value.  Induction gives the unique complete extension and proves (3).
There are at most $N$ layer calls; decoding, verification, and output are
charged, so their total is polynomial.  Conversely, every complete witness
satisfies $F(y_0)$ by (1).
\end{proof}

For the Gold layer, let $d$ be odd, $K=\mathbb F_{2^d}$, and let $X$ be a
connected graph with a declared cotree of size $d$.  The layer relation is
\[
B_Xx=c(y_{<j}),\qquad (\pi_Cx)^3=b(y_{<j}).              \tag{4}
\]
Independent review has promoted the cube theorem to \EXACT{}: cubing is a
permutation with inverse exponent $(2^{d+1}-1)/3$.  The already exact cotree
bijection then gives
\[
z=b(y_{<j})^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3},\qquad
x=\operatorname{TreeLift}_{c(y_{<j})}(z).               \tag{5}
\]
Bidirectional definitional gate clauses give unique circuit auxiliaries.
Thus (4) satisfies (2) for the declared structured encoding.  This
independently closes the mathematical review of PERMEXT1 and the explicit
Gold hierarchy as \EXACT{} at that scope.
