\section{Question and three falsifiable mechanisms}
The universal question is whether every SAT decider must construct a rich backward compatibility representation or pay an equally large alternative cost. No such dichotomy is assumed. We tested three mechanisms: (M1) a smaller exact signed-defect boundary from occurrence conservation, (M2) a decision-only exactly-one binary repair and its probabilistic consequence, (M3) whether this can be transferred to unrestricted SAT. M1 and M2 succeed; M3 stays OPEN because direct source inspection and graph matching defeat the proposed mandatory-work inference.

\section{Adopted source-exact projection and new boundary count}
PAIRPATH73 already proves the following: forced complementary clauses $(a\vee b\vee x),(a\vee b\vee\neg x)$ contract to $(a\vee b)$; with $p$ such pairs, $\delta=m-2p$ unpaired ternaries $T$, contracted binaries $B$, and $U=\operatorname{vars}(T)$, one has $F\equiv B\land T$. Each variable obeys
\[
t_x+2b_x^++u_x^+=2,\qquad t_x+2b_x^-+u_x^-=2.
\]
Eliminate all variables of $B$ outside $U$ along degree-two paths and cycles, giving exact projection
\[
\forall\alpha\in\{0,1\}^U:\quad (T\land B')(\alpha)\ \iff\ \exists y\,F(\alpha,y).
\]
The $P\le\lfloor3\delta/4\rfloor$ projected binaries have pairwise disjoint variable supports. Each of their $2P$ endpoint variables appears twice in $T$, always with polarity opposite its signed occurrence in $B'$.

\begin{lemma}[Sharpened boundary cardinality; PROVED/EXACT]
If $a$ is the number of boundary source variables occurring four times in $T$, then
\[
\boxed{|U|=\frac{3\delta}{2}-a\le\frac{3\delta}{2}.}
\]
In particular $\delta$ is even.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The conservation equations give exactly two possible kinds of variable in $U$: two unmatched appearances (a binary endpoint with two opposite-sign appearances, or a pivot-once variable with one of each sign), or four unmatched appearances (two of each sign, with no contracted binary appearance). Hence $3\delta=2(|U|-a)+4a=2|U|+2a$.
\end{proof}

\section{Decision-only binary normalization and the eight-clause theorem}
\begin{lemma}[Endpoint repair; PROVED/EXACT]
$T\land B'$ is SAT iff it has a satisfying assignment with exactly one true \emph{signed} literal in each disjoint boundary binary of $B'$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If both signed binary literals are true, make either one false. Its appearances in $T$ have only the opposite sign, which change from false to true. The other signed binary literal stays true, and this source variable appears in no other projected binary. Iterate independently over binaries.
\end{proof}
This is equisatisfiability for the one-bit root, not exact preservation of all source assignments or their model counts.

Draw one independent fair bit per binary to choose which endpoint is true, and one per other boundary source variable. Thus exactly $d=|U|-P\le3\delta/2-P$ independent sampled bits suffice to satisfy $B'$. Let $E_i$ mean that unmatched clause $T_i$ is false.
\begin{lemma}[Bad-event conflict bound; PROVED/EXACT]
Every $E_i$ is either impossible or has probability $1/8$. Every possible $E_i$ is incompatible with at most six other ternary bad events.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If a clause contains both endpoints of the same projected binary, their ternary literals are the negations of the two signed binary literals, so one is necessarily true. Otherwise its three different source variables depend on three different fair sampled bits, yielding probability $2^{-3}$. Each falsification requirement can be contradicted by at most two other ternary occurrences: the opposite polarity occurs at most twice for an ordinary boundary variable, while the companion endpoint of a binary occurs exactly twice for a binary endpoint. With three literals, there are at most six other incompatible clause events.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Eight-unmatched-clause SAT; PROVED/EXACT]
Every source-valid signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF with PAIRPATH defect $\delta\le8$ is satisfiable. The exact-one product distribution satisfies
\[
\boxed{\Pr[T\land B'=1]\ge\begin{cases}1-\delta/8&\delta<8,\\1/64&\delta=8.\end{cases}}
\]
Consequently every UNSAT source has $\delta\ge10$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $\delta<8$, union bound on the at-most-$1/8$ bad events suffices. If $\delta=8$ and one event is impossible, the other seven have total probability at most $7/8$. Otherwise all eight have probability $1/8$. Fix any event $E_i$. It conflicts with at most six other events, hence one of the seven remaining events $E_j$ is compatible. Their joint falsification specifies at most six independent fair bits, giving $\Pr(E_i\cap E_j)\ge1/64$. Group this pair in the union bound:
\[
\Pr(\cup_{h=1}^8 E_h)\le\sum_h\Pr(E_h)-\Pr(E_i\cap E_j)\le1-1/64.
\]
The source-exact existential projection and witness lifting establish the original SAT label. The evenness of $\delta$ gives the UNSAT threshold $10$.
\end{proof}

\section{Uniform algorithm, charge ledger and computational audit}
The parser validates genuine distinct three-literal source clauses and exact two-positive/two-negative occurrences. Sort signed canonical triples, discover complementary pairs, contract them, project the degree-two binary paths and cycles with source-labelled resolution, and check all endpoint counts. If $\delta\le8$, the theorem certifies SAT. Optional witness recovery tries at most $2^{|U|-P}\le4096$ fair-bit assignments, then solves original $B$ under the found boundary values by 2SAT SCC; it sets pivot-only source variables arbitrarily and replays every original clause. If $\delta>8$, PAIRPATH73 supplies a $2^\delta\operatorname{poly}(N)$ charged fallback; alternate symbolic algorithms remain allowed.

On the declared word RAM, an optimized certified pipeline has $C_{\rm discovery}=O(m\log(m+2))$, $C_{\rm normalization}=O(m\log(m+2))$, $C_{\rm verification}=O(m+\delta^2)$, and witness $C_{\rm continuation}=O(2^{|U|-P}\delta+m)$, peak reusable $O(n+m)$ words. The executable Python audit deliberately uses repeated live-clause scans, a less efficient $O(m^2)$ projection implementation, and measures 87 resolution attempts with 82 tautology discards. No claimed lower bound follows from summing these costs.

The seeded stdlib audit (202610231312) tested 360 valid random signed-balanced sources; 144 kernel models with all permitted binary pair counts for $\delta=2,4,6,8$; long original-source $\delta=2$ and $\delta=8$ padded families; K4 paired monotone exact-$(2,2)$ SAT products through 120 variables; and both-label $q=3,4,7,20,80$ palette-cycle examples from Role 4. The palette controls are mixed-width and \emph{not} signed exact-$(2,2)$. Proper contradictory eight-clause and 2SAT controls are likewise explicitly out of promise. Measured 840 exact boundary-completion checks, 1,625 implication arcs, 891 kernel attempts, 5,928 source replay literal reads, 48 $K_{2,2}$ conflict edges, 48 clause triangle edges, and 3,712 assignment-gate checks. No final assertion failed; minimum observed kernel success fraction $3/16$. The strict monotone 198-variable/264-clause published UNSAT source was not independently reconstructed.
