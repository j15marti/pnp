\section{Model and repaired curved endpoint}
Fix the ordinary explicit SAT encoding, with bit length \(N\). Let \(E\) consist of exponent pairs jointly attained by ONE fixed uniform exact all-input SAT decider in the FLMV random-access Turing model. The model counts work/index tapes, treats the read-only indexed source separately, and supplies no readable output buffer. A route between different elements of \(E\) may use different finite constructions; its depth can depend on a fixed initial machine but not on input length. A cofinal finite-hit theorem is sufficient, as shown by FINAL-LINK-ARC after correcting its erroneous universal product endpoint.

For \(c>1\), let
\[
 \beta(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).
\]
The published FLMV Corollary 5.8 excludes \(\mathrm{NTIME}[N]\subseteq \mathrm{DTISP}[N^c,o(N^{\beta(c)})]\); Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.2 transfer this to SAT with strict time and space margins. At \(c_0=241/200\),
\[
 \beta(c_0)=\frac{11642479}{23232400}
 =\frac12+\frac{26279}{23232400}>\frac12.
\]
The older formula \(t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)\), when read as a universal SAT time--space-product forbidden region, is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.

\begin{theorem}[Curved multichannel FLMV firewall; PROVED/EXACT under stated premises]
Suppose a single, uniform, all-input exact SAT procedure replaces every recursive SAT call by itself, and on every length \(N\) has \(N^{r+o(1)}\) directly charged work and \(N^{\sigma+o(1)}\) local workspace. It makes, for finitely many channels \(j\), at most \(N^{\eta_j+o(1)}\) sequential ordinary recursive children each of length at most \(N^{\theta_j+o(1)}\), with \(0\leq \theta_j<1\). All discovery, source access, buffers, gluing, verification, and recurrence overhead are included; one child is live at a time and the recursion actually terminates in its correct base cases. Put
\[
 C=\max\left\{r,\max_j\frac{\eta_j}{1-\theta_j}\right\},\qquad
 \rho=\max\{\sigma,\max_j\theta_j\}.
\]
Then the procedure has time \(N^{C+o(1)}\) and peak space \(N^{\rho+o(1)}\). If \(C>1\) and \(\rho<\beta(C)\), it contradicts FLMV, and therefore NO such all-input procedure can satisfy those premises. A convenient sufficient subregion is \(C<241/200\), \(\rho\le 1/2\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The recurrence \(T(N)\le N^{r+o(1)}+\sum_jN^{\eta_j+o(1)}T(N^{\theta_j+o(1)})\) gives \(T(N)=N^{C+o(1)}\), by induction with an arbitrarily small exponent slack. The depth-first peak-space sum is \(N^{\rho+o(1)}\), because powers strictly shrink and only one child, its ancestors, and charged local state are live. For \(\rho<\beta(C)\), continuity and strict monotonicity \(\beta'(c)=-(1+c^{-2}+4c^{-3})/2<0\) permit a constant \(c>C\) with \(\rho<\beta(c)\). Theorem 3.1 carries a hypothetical SAT algorithm into the forbidden FLMV region, absorbing the polylogarithms in both strict margins. The fixed box follows from the displayed rational margin.
\end{proof}
The \(C\le1\) input-reading floor is handled separately. No all-input fractional-channel decomposition has been constructed. For a meta-contractor that invokes an arbitrary existing solver \(A\), solver-dependent channels and representation costs must be accounted for before applying this theorem. The theorem is a \emph{firewall}, not an existence proof.

\section{Exact obstruction to low-width stream reordering}
Let \(h\ge3\), and for every edge \(\{i,j\}\) of the complete graph \(K_h\), create a fresh private source \(z_{ij}\) and one proper clause
\[
 (x_i\vee x_j\vee z_{ij}).
\]
The number of edge clauses is \(M=\binom h2\). Add four source variables \(a,b,c,d\), all eight signed three-literal clauses on \(a,b,c\) for the UNSAT member, or the first seven excluding the all-negative clause plus \((a\vee b\vee d)\) for the SAT member. Finally add \((x_1\vee a\vee d)\) to both. Each resulting incidence graph is connected, all clauses contain three distinct variables and are duplicate free. Both instances have the same \(n=h+M+4\) variables, \(m=M+9\) clauses, and fixed-record bit length
\[
 w_{\rm rec}=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil,\qquad
 N=(4m+2)w_{\rm rec}=\Theta(h^2\log h).
\]
The SAT member is satisfied by ALL TRUE. The UNSAT member contains a direct eight-clause contradictory core. The pair differs in one signed clause support; it is not claimed to be strict exact-(2,2).

\begin{theorem}[All-order source-frontier bound; PROVED/EXACT]
For every ordering of the original clauses into one-open/one-close valid source-scoped stream, the maximum simultaneously live source-variable count is at least
\[
 w_h=\min\{w\in\mathbb{N}:\binom w2\ge\lfloor M/2\rfloor\}
 =\Omega(h)=\Omega\left(\sqrt{\frac N{\log N}}\right).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Consider the instant immediately after the \(\lfloor M/2\rfloor\)-th edge clause in any ordering, ignoring where the nine other clauses were placed. Let \(A,B\) partition edges into the observed and unobserved sets, and let \(W\) be vertices incident to at least one edge of each kind. If some vertex appears only in \(A\) and another only in \(B\), their connecting edge belongs to neither side, impossible in a complete graph. Consequently one side has no exclusive vertices: either all \(A\)-edges or all \(B\)-edges are internal to \(W\). Both sides contain at least \(\lfloor M/2\rfloor\) edges, hence \(\binom{|W|}{2}\ge\lfloor M/2\rfloor\). Each \(x_i\in W\) occurs before and after the cut, so it must remain live in any valid single-interval source stream. Private \(z_{ij}\) and the gadget only increase, not decrease, width.
\end{proof}
For \(h=256\), the bound is \(w_h=182\), versus \(\log_2N\approx21.518\). Thus mere clause permutation fails to normalize these ordinary proper 3-CNFs into the sublogarithmic-width promise used by INTERFACE-PROJECTION8. This is \textbf{NOT} a rejection of arbitrary equisatisfiable symbolic rewriting, auxiliary variables, a wholly different contraction method, or source-aware direct decision.

\section{Constructive adaptive-state and resource ledger}
The tested uniform policy reads explicit source records and first infers \(h=\max|l|-m+5\), not a supplied SAT label. It checks the complete edge/gadget supports using a charged dictionary. On recognized SAT inputs it constructs/replays the all-TRUE witness; on recognized UNSAT inputs it checks the eight source clauses against each of the eight assignments to \(a,b,c\). If recognition fails it switches to an exact general unit-propagation DPLL fallback. Source order may vary. Alternative choices include storing all boundary assignments and performing literal-preserving scoped DP, which on this family can require an exponentially large frontier table. The selected recognizer uses expected \(O(m)\) dictionary work and \(O(m)\) live words (or deterministic sorting in \(O(m\log m)\) work), not the FLMV \(N^{1/2}\)-space ceiling. The fallback is generally exponential. The policy is a constructive family-relative shortcut, not an element of the forbidden \(E\) region.

The source/recognition ledger includes inferring \(h\), reconstructing expected supports, every clause lookup, detection of failed recognitions, fallback invocation, answer replay, and retained source/dictionary space. No external SAT label or free compatibility table is used. The analysis width \(w_h\) is separate from the policy's acquired source parameters and is never treated as a runtime estimate.
