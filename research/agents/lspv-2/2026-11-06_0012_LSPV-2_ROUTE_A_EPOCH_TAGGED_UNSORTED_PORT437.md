## Established hard family and exact signed 3-CNF
For a connected cubic graph G with H vertices and 3H/2 edges, one edge variable per edge and charge vector chi, with incident edges a_v<b_v<c_v, the *four* clauses at vertex v are

$$
C_{v,p,r}(\chi)=\ell_p(x_{a_v})\lor\ell_r(x_{b_v})\lor\ell_{p\oplus r\oplus1\oplus\chi_v}(x_{c_v}),\qquad p,r\in\{0,1\},\quad \ell_0(x)=x,\quad\ell_1(x)=\neg x.
$$

The proper strict-3 encoding has 4H clauses and 3H/2 variables. The historical fixed-field serialization has length

$$
N_H=8+(H+1)+(3H/2+1)+2(3H/2)w+12H(w+1)+2=\Theta(H\log H)
$$

where w=max(1,bitlength(3H/2)). Connectedness yields SAT iff XOR of all charges is zero; odd charge is mathematically certified UNSAT. Published exponential lower bounds for suitable growing cubic *expander* Tseitin families apply to ordinary shared-DAG Resolution (Urquhart 1987; Ben-Sasson and Wigderson 2001), not to arbitrary algorithms, Extended Resolution, or the fixed historical mixed R_mix341. ## Theorem 437.1: exact epoch parity (PROVED/RESTRICTED)
Fix an authenticated base charge b and graph G. Sequential child j=1,...,q is generated by an unsorted sequence of in-range vertex edits u_{j,1},...,u_{j,r_j}. The exact new charge is

$$
\chi_{j,v}=b_v\oplus\bigoplus_{k=1}^{r_j}[u_{j,k}=v].
$$

Reserve one B=ceil(log_2(q+1))-bit tag and one bit of parity for each vertex. Initial blank tags mean zero. Epochs j are distinct nonzero integers, so no wraparound. On edit v, read its tag and parity, logically use zero if tag != j, then write tag j and flip parity. An untouched vertex in epoch j has an older/blank tag and is read as parity zero. By induction on the edit stream, returned parity is exactly the mod-2 count of edits to v. Substitution into the four signed clauses proves *every original-source literal* is exact, including repeated edits and stale tags. Sorting, clearing H bits per child, and retaining qH mutable cells are unnecessary.

This is a standard initializable-array technique adapted to a restricted certified source interface, not a novel general algorithm (Hagerup/Kammer 2017; Katoh/Goto 2017; Loong/Nelson/Yu 2017).

## Theorem 437.2: charged indexed-tape implementation
FLMV, Time-Space Lower Bounds for Satisfiability, JACM 52(6), 2005, section 2.1 (original PDF page 4 visually inspected), permits indexed jumps on non-index **work tapes**, not only on the read-only input. Each indexed jump erases its index tape. Space includes the maximum visited coordinate of non-input work/index tapes.

For H packed tagged cells at addresses 1+v(B+1), an update or bit-port access takes polylogarithmic native bit operations: construct the binary address on the index tape, jump and erase that tape, read/write B+1 tape cells and fixed-size scratch. The tag array is paid at its **full physical high-water**, even when sparse. Let R be total edits and Q total requested exact child bits. Then

$$
T_{\rm port}=O((q+R+Q)\operatorname{polylog}(N+q+H+R+Q)),\quad
S_{\rm port}\le H(B+1)+O(\log(N+q+H+R+Q)).
$$

A wrapper calling A on authenticated virtual children of lengths L_j has **only conditional** work

$$
T_{\rm wrapper}\le V(N)+O((q+R+\sum_jT_A(L_j))\operatorname{polylog}N)+T_{\rm join},
$$

and simultaneous space at most H(B+1)+max_j S_A(L_j)+S_auth+O(log N), where V explicitly includes original-source validation and source-template access, and T_join is not free. This follows by paying child-bit requests during guest emulation; guest index/head jumps and erasures remain charged. For q<=N^{3/5+o(1)}, L<=N^{1/2+o(1)}, H=O(L/log L), B=O(log N), the **port** alone fits N^{1/2+o(1)} space, and if R,V,T_join satisfy their separate budgets its work fits the proposed local envelope.

This is **not** a universal FLMV contraction: no effective recursively iterable child generator or join for arbitrary CNF-SAT/UNSAT/malformed strings is supplied, and the authentication and guest costs cannot be assumed away. The q independent edit lists, if explicitly supplied, contribute their actual encoded bits to the parent length; LSPV-4/433's corrected input-size baseline prevents importing impossible independent portfolios.
