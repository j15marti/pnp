Suppose every parent of a record in $B_i$ belongs either to the immutable SOURCE/facet prefix or to an earlier member of $B_i$. Assume the terminal's parents are retained and the paid-owner bank and all signed clause payloads stay unchanged. Then every permutation of whole blocks before the same terminal, with a bijective remapping of record-parent indices, is accepted by the same mathematical checker and has the same signed SOURCE occurrence sequence, terminal semantic refutation, distinct paid identities and logical work. The complete original CLI acceptance of the six packets below is independently checked rather than inferred from this general mathematical lemma.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The map sends each original RES to exactly one unchanged record, preserving both signed parent clauses, oriented pivot, resolvent and all intra-block dependency identities. The terminal, moved after all blocks, retains its unchanged signed antecedents and empty conclusion. SOURCE and owner tuples do not change, so external source authentication, defining-facet truth conditions and once-paid owner count survive. Every RES occurs once, hence $W=N_{\mathrm{RES}}+N_{\mathrm{paid\ owners}}$ is invariant. Only parent index presentation changes. In the complete original CLI, additional bundled corruption self-tests are program-specific; direct execution verifies acceptance for the concrete construction.\end{proof}
