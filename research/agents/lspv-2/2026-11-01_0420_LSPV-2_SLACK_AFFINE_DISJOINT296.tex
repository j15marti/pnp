\section*{Canonical original source and signed budget}

Let $U$ be the uncovered original clause IDs, $u=|U|$, $W$ the $f$ free variables. For each free $x$, let $s_x^\pm$ be its signed occurrences in clauses already satisfied by $p$, $q_x=|s_x^+-s_x^-|$, and $\eta=\sum_x\min(s_x^+,s_x^-)$. If $q_x>0$, set $b_x=0$ when $s_x^+>s_x^-$ and $b_x=1$ otherwise. Put $r=2f-u-\eta$.
\[
\boxed{r=\sum_{j\in U}(t_j(a)-1)+
\sum_{x:q_x>0}q_x[a_x\ne b_x].}\tag{1}
\]
This follows from two true signed occurrences per free variable, regardless of its assignment. For a SAT extension, all RHS summands are nonnegative; $r<0$ therefore authenticates UNSAT.
\begin{theorem}[Disjoint affine slack profiles]
For every valid $F,p$ with no source empty clause and $r\ge0$, enumerate the unique positive-slack records consisting of (i) nonpreferred free variables $x$ with weight $q_x$ and (ii) uncovered source clauses with exactly two or three true literals, of weights one and two respectively. For the clause record fix its full original free-variable truth pattern, uniquely determined by the assignment. Select profiles with total weight exactly $r$. Each profile induces a GF(2) system by fixing the recorded bits, fixing every preferred variable to either its preferred or selected opposite value, and requiring odd literal-truth parity in every uncovered source clause not recorded. These systems form a \emph{disjoint exact partition} of all satisfying completions. Thus
\[
\#\mathrm{SAT}(F\mid p)=
\sum_{\pi:\ A_\pi z=b_\pi\ {\rm consistent}}2^{f-\operatorname{rank}(A_\pi)}.
\]
The deterministic enumeration uses at most $N^{O(r+1)}$ operations and polynomial workspace on explicit parsed sources, with recognition, failed profiles and row operations charged. Constant-$r$ is polynomial time; this is XP rather than a uniform polynomial SAT algorithm.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textbf{Proof.} Each satisfying assignment has a unique source true-count/preference defect signature. Equation (1) proves its weight is exactly $r$. Clauses with exactly one true literal satisfy odd parity, so every SAT model belongs to its signature's affine system. Conversely, every affine solution satisfies each already-covered clause, every defect clause by the prescribed exact truth pattern, and every other uncovered clause because odd literal-truth parity at residual width at most three implies one or three true literals. Thus all clauses are SAT. Equation (1) and the already spent exact budget $r$ prohibit any unrecorded three-true clause or weighted mismatch. The signature is therefore exact and unique, proving disjointness. Each consistent rank-$\rho$ system has $2^{f-\rho}$ solutions. QED.
