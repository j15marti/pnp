\[
L_i=a_i\land(b_i\oplus c_i),\qquad
R_i=(a_i\land b_i)\oplus(a_i\land c_i).
\]
Use five distinct fresh gate wires per pair, authentic three-clause AND and four-clause XOR Tseitin CNF definitions, distinct intermediate wires for identically shaped balanced XOR trees aggregating $(L_i)_i$ and $(R_i)_i$ to outputs $P,Q$, and \emph{both} source clauses $(P\lor Q)$, $(\neg P\lor\neg Q)$ requiring mismatch. Call the full CNF $D_h$. It is UNSAT, contains precisely $10h-2$ variables and $25h-6$ source clauses, and satisfies
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(D_h)\le W_{\rm Res}(D_h)\le25h+14(h-1)+3=39h-11.}

An explicitly encoded derivation using clauses of width at most four has $O(h\log(h+2))$ certificate bits. This is an upper-bound theorem, not an exact minimum or a lower bound for arbitrary CNF.
\end{theorem}
