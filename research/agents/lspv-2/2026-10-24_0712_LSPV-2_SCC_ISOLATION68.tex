\section{Source and backward circuit}
Fix a proper signed three-CNF with exactly three distinct variables in each distinct signed clause and every original source variable occurring exactly twice positively and twice negatively. With $n$ variables and $m$ clauses, $4n=3m$. Set $w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil$ and encoded bit length $N=(4m+2)w$. Literal inputs followed by $2m$ OR2 gates and $m-1$ AND2 gates evaluate the formula, but this is not a circuit deciding SAT on formula encodings. Backward output TRUE means every clause TRUE and therefore one may select one true literal \emph{occurrence} per clause while keeping all shared sources consistent. This never imposes exactly one TRUE \emph{original literal}. The exact occurrence conflict graph has $4n=3m$ occurrence vertices and $4n$ opposition edges forming $n$ disjoint copies of $K_{2,2}$. Clause triangles add $3m=4n$ edges. This graph need not be materialized by another SAT algorithm.

Contract each source-certified complementary pivot pair
\[
(a\lor b\lor z)\land(a\lor b\lor\neg z)\equiv(a\lor b).
\]
Write $B$ for the $p$ contracted binary clauses, $T$ for the $\delta=m-2p$ unmatched ternaries. For each $x$, let $t_x$ count pivot pairs, $b_x^\pm$ count positive/negative binary appearances, and $u_x^\pm$ count positive/negative unmatched ternary appearances. Exact original-source conservation gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conservation}
t_x+2b_x^++u_x^+=2,\qquad t_x+2b_x^-+u_x^-=2.
\end{equation}
In particular $b_x^\pm\le1$ and $b_x^+=b_x^-=1$ forces $u_x^+=u_x^-=0$.

\section{New zero-gain SCC-isolation theorem}
\begin{theorem}[SCC isolation, PROVED/EXACT restricted]\label{th:scc}
For the $B,T$ above, any original variable whose literal belongs to a nontrivial implication strongly connected component of $B$ is absent from $T$. Replacing ternary literals by implication-SCC-equivalent representatives cannot shorten any clause in $T$. Moreover $B$ is satisfiable and does not force either truth value on an individual source variable occurring in $T$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An incoming implication edge to literal $x$ comes from a binary clause containing $x$; an outgoing edge from $x$ comes from a binary clause containing $\neg x$. In a nontrivial SCC, each vertex lies on a directed cycle, so both signs of the source variable appear in $B$. Equation~\eqref{eq:conservation} then forces the variable absent from $T$. The same reasoning holds for negative literals. Thus no nontrivial SCC can rename or deduplicate any unmatched ternary occurrence.

Interpret the binary clauses as undirected edges between distinct source names. Each source has binary degree at most two, and a degree-two source appears with opposite signs in its two clauses. The components are paths or cycles, including parallel-edge two-cycles. Orient every cycle's edges cyclically and choose each variable to satisfy its incoming clause; this satisfies every binary clause. On a path, either prescribed truth value at either endpoint extends to a satisfying assignment by choosing the next not-yet-fixed variable to satisfy each successive edge if necessary. A source appearing in $T$ has binary degree at most one, by~\eqref{eq:conservation}, hence any individual boundary value extends to $B$; a boundary source absent from $B$ is unrestricted. 
\end{proof}
\textbf{Scope caution.} $B$ can still impose an important binary relation $(a\lor b)$ between \emph{two} boundary variables, even though it forces neither individually. The theorem is not a license to discard $B$. After a partial assignment, additional binary clauses may arise and a later SCC pass may then be productive.

\begin{theorem}[Exact boundary matching corollary; PAIRPATH73 imported]
Existential elimination of every binary-only variable from $B$ produces one binary clause per path over the two distinct source endpoints in $\mathrm{vars}(T)$, and projects a cycle to TRUE. No source endpoint participates in two output binary clauses. These projected clauses form a matching on the ternary boundary. Their implication graph has no nontrivial SCC, and their number is at most $\lfloor 3\delta/4\rfloor$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By~\eqref{eq:conservation}, every internal path source appears in one positive and one negative binary clause and has no ternary occurrence. Thus
\[
\exists z[(A\lor z)\land(\neg z\lor D)]\equiv A\lor D
\]
eliminates it exactly and preserves every fixed assignment to the remaining boundary. The endpoints are precisely degree-one binary sources, which occur at most once in $B$, so they cannot be shared by two paths. A projected matching cannot contain a nontrivial implication SCC. The bound is the PAIRPATH73 source-incidence count: each projected path uses two different endpoint sources, each of which consumes two unmatched ternary occurrences; $4P\le3\delta$. Linear graph traversal and source witness lifting follow from PAIRPATH73, after $O(m\log(m+2))$ source pair discovery.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Renamable-Horn counting control, PROVED/EXACT]
The untouched signed exact-$(2,2)$ ternary source is neither renamable Horn nor renamable dual-Horn when $n>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Complementing any subset of variables leaves exactly two occurrences of each sign per source. Thus there are $2n$ positives and $2n$ negatives after every renaming. Horn and dual-Horn allow at most $m=4n/3<2n$ occurrences of the respective sign across all $m$ clauses. The claim does not apply after restriction or certified non-identity-preserving transformations.
\end{proof}

\section{Uniform state-adaptive policy and fully charged ledger}
A uniform label-blind policy parses and checks the source, identifies source-certified pairs, and either performs path/cycle projection without the provably idle SCC prepass or recognizes an independently soluble component. The available state vector includes current signed source clauses, discovered pair count $p$, defect $\delta$, boundary names, live cache, decomposition and remaining budget. The chosen rule and parameter values may change after every exact source-preserving simplification. A discovered variable hitting all remaining ternaries admits two explicit branches and per-branch SCC; disjoint four-clause parity quartets admit affine reconstruction. Unrecognized instances use a total exact DPLL fallback, without a polynomial claim. Counting a path projection includes source comparisons and successful/failed pair checks, $O(m\log(m+2))$ preprocessing, $O(n+m)$ traversal, and $O(n+m)$ working storage. Witness lifting and complete original-clause replay are charged. Skipping the SCC prepass avoids an otherwise redundant scan of $2p$ implication arcs; the gain is only a linear pass, not a new asymptotic universal upper bound.

\noindent\textbf{Adaptive-state ledger.}
\begin{description}

\section{Relation to the previously proposed lower bound and OPEN arrows}

\[

\qquad r_A(F)/\log_2N\rightarrow\infty

would imply $P\neq NP$ only if proved on appropriate growing inputs against \emph{every} total uniform deterministic exact SAT decider, with the resource parameter defined independently of runtime.
