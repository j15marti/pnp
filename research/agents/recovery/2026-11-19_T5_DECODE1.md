## Exact theorem
Let A be a nonempty binary relational epsilon-expander with nonempty relation, B an explicitly encoded directed source, C a twisted product of A and B, with the explicit projection pi:C->B retained by the compiler. Let epsilon<1/25. For EVERY homomorphism phi:C->T5, choose for each b a most frequent color among phi(pi^{-1}(b)); ties may be resolved arbitrarily. The resulting xi:B->T5 is a homomorphism.

Proof: every chosen color occupies at least |A|/5 elements of its fiber. For any arc (b,b') of B, pull these two color classes back through the tuple's two bijections to subsets S,T of A. Both have size at least |A|/5. Expansion gives
|R(A) intersect (S x T)| >= |R(A)| (|S||T|/|A|^2 - epsilon) >= |R(A)|(1/25-epsilon)>0.
A lifted arc therefore has endpoint colors xi(b),xi(b'). Since phi satisfies C, R(xi(b),xi(b')) holds. This works simultaneously for every source arc and every choice among tied modal colors. QED.

This is the constructive content of Kun Lemma 13, arXiv:0706.1701v2, pp.10-11. Theorem 1's proof fixes t,k; use t=6 for T5 and its epsilon=1/t^(r+1), with r=2. No novelty is claimed for the theorem. The contribution is recovering its decoder and correcting the project ledger.

## Complete cost ledger
Let M=|V(C)|, n=|V(B)|, m=|E(B)|. Given indexed fibers and an already obtained coloring, count five colors per fiber, choose modes, output n colors, and check m original arcs. This costs O(M+n+m) word operations with O(log(M+n+m))-bit words; five counters per source vertex use O(n log(M+1)) bits, plus retained input/projection/witness storage. Bit-machine costs remain polynomial, not automatically linear. Verification of the supplied C-coloring additionally scans all E(C). A malformed projection or noncolor is rejected using the explicit construction metadata and verifier. Acquiring phi still incurs the full solver cost. Constructing A,C, retaining pi, source parsing, storage, and all failed work remain charged. Grouped fibers can reduce auxiliary counters but do not erase retained data.

Global rotations preserve solutions. Minimum-color tie-breaking need not commute with rotations; semantic correctness does not require that symmetry. Canonical root normalization can follow decoding.

Falsifiers checked: failure under ties; dropping expansion; claiming projection can be inferred without paid metadata; importing fixed k into growing k. Only the second gives a genuine counterexample to an overbroad lemma.

## Executed tests
recovery_decode.py exhaustively checks 15,625 assignments on the complete bipartite lift with three vertices per fiber. Exactly 70 are valid lift colorings; all 70 modal-pair checks succeed. This is an epsilon=0 decoder control, NOT a high-girth construction.
The nonexpanding diagonal matching has left colors (0,1), right colors (1,2). Each matched arc is valid, but modal pairs (0,2) and (1,1) are forbidden. Projection alone is insufficient.
The same independent script checks all 510 oriented path words of lengths 1..8, reconstructs nine short-path relations and all 512 relation subfamilies, reproducing irredundant counts 9,26,24,4.
The graph-theoretic 2-connectivity, thread-removal, Moore, and n<=4b^2-3b arguments were checked against the supplied handoff and the preceding audit in this conversation; no new exhaustive graph catalogue or large critical graph generator was executed here. The logical argument uses rotation gluing at cut vertices, four-edge path recoloring, and girth-preserving thread suppression. These remain structural statements, not time lower bounds.
