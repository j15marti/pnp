## EXACT INFINITE SELECTOR-GLUING GAP

For m>=2 let b=m^2, B_{r,c} (0<=r,c<m) be genuine universal seed bits; I in {0,1} is one universal selector bit; X=(X0,X1) is the one original existential candidate shared by both branches. E0 are all m(m-1) horizontal oriented grid edges, E1 all m(m-1) vertical edges.

A_i(B)=AND_{(u,v) in E_i} (NOT B_u OR B_v).
G_i(X,B)=A_i(B) AND (NOT X_i OR B_00) AND (NOT X_i OR NOT B_00).
Phi_m = EXISTS X FORALL I,B NOT G_I(X,B).

THM 1: Each G_i source universal variable-clause incidence graph is a forest with exactly m components and treewidth 1 (m chains plus two unit-clause leaves). All m^2 B bits are semantically essential for each branch, certified by paired seeds differing in one bit.

THM 2: The *naive selector-agnostic union* identifying B across both branches while keeping every source-clause vertex contains a subdivided m-by-m grid. Contract each binary clause vertex to recover the m-by-m grid. Hence union incidence treewidth >= m = sqrt(b) by minor monotonicity and grid treewidth m, even though I is only one bit and both selector branches have width one. This is an infinite extension of TW2FIBER30's finite K4 witness. IMPORTANT: this is NOT asserted about the faithful selector-multiplexed circuit's CNF incidence graph or about arbitrary algorithms' runtime.

THM 3: For all x and selector i, EXISTS B G_i(x,B) iff x_i=0: if x_i=1 the two guarded unit clauses contradict; if x_i=0 the zero seed B=0^b satisfies all implications. Hence H(x):=EXISTS I,B G_I(x,B)=NOT x_0 OR NOT x_1; Phi_m iff EXISTS X (x_0 AND x_1). A source-certified complete universal response f_i(X)=0^b exists for this explicit easy family; no SAT or UNSAT oracle needed.
