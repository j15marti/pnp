## Theorem B: an intrinsically critical three-cut obstruction

```

h1: (0,3) (1,2) (1,3)   [cut {0,1}|{2,3}]
h2: (1,3) (2,3) (0,2)   [cut {0,3}|{1,2}]
```

Across these three partitions every pair of tail variables is separated, hence all four tails must have distinct colors in any satisfying assignment. Let d be the fifth, missing color. Each head must win exactly two of the four tails, so it must not beat d. Exactly three heads have this property, but they induce only two **different** balanced cuts on the four remaining colors (for d=4: heads 0,2,4 yield winner pairs {1,3},{0,3},{0,2}; the first and third are complementary cuts).
