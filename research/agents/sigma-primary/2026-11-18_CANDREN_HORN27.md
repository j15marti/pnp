LIMITATION/COUNTEREXAMPLE. The all-X all-selector renamable-Horn promise is coNP-complete when arbitrary X-only guards are allowed. For an arbitrary F(X), activate the four binary clauses (p OR q), (p OR NOT q), (NOT p OR q), (NOT p OR NOT q) iff F(X)=1. Their renaming-2SAT constraints are contradictory when active, while no clauses activate otherwise. Hence every candidate is renamable Horn iff F is UNSAT. An arbitrary counterexample x and a failed 2SAT test witness nonrenamability, so membership is coNP. Adding the same positive z to each of those four clauses produces a satisfiable (z=1) but nonrenamable source; a compiler treating nonrenamability as G-unsatisfiability would be UNSOUND.
