## PROVED: universal pairwise-overlap consistency fails to refute strict C
The strict-C relation is the 30 ordered permutations of the five consecutive three-color sets {t,t+1,t+2} in Z5. For every physical clause choose its uniform distribution on these 30 permitted tuples. Each unary marginal is 1/5. Each ordered two-variable marginal has numerator 0 for equal colors, 2 for cyclic distance 1, and 1 for cyclic distance 2, all over denominator 30. These distributions project identically onto ANY intersection of two physical clause scopes, even when the source itself is UNSAT. Thus basic CSP LP and even pairwise clause-overlap marginal consistency ALWAYS have a feasible solution on unpinned strict C. This is not a global distribution or SAT witness.
