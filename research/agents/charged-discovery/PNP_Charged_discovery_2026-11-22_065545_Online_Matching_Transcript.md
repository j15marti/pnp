## One proposed transfer

Henzinger, Krinninger, Nanongkai and Saranurak, *Unifying and Strengthening Hardness for Dynamic Problems via the Online Matrix-Vector Multiplication Conjecture*, STOC 2015, full paper, Definition 2.6, Theorem 2.7 and Lemma 4.3. The square specialization of Theorem 2.7 assumes the OMv conjecture. It rules out, for every fixed ε>0, a bounded-error (at most 1/3) algorithm with polynomial preprocessing of an n-by-n Boolean matrix and O(n^(3−ε)) subsequent total computation for n arriving vector pairs. Each Boolean scalar product must be returned before the next pair arrives. Lemma 4.3 realizes the square sequence using incremental or decremental bipartite maximum matching with graph order and size Θ(n²) and n matching queries. The reduction consumes intermediate matching sizes. Our prism graphs may be nonbipartite and ask only whether there is a perfect matching. Neither graph identity nor query equivalence is assumed. [Primary paper, §§2,4](https://people.csail.mit.edu/virgi/6.s078/papers/omv.pdf).

Attempted transfer: concatenate decision-sensitive components, use their joint SAT decision, then extract the component-answer transcript from one post-preprocessing execution. Even allowing an unbounded-time decoder, the extraction fails with a precise advice deficit. This tests the output interface of the proposed transfer. It makes no claim that the prism family implements the paper's hard graph distribution.

**Claim.** For every such q,k and every decoder of d from the complete relative READY suffix, public q,k and program A, exact recovery on all these inputs requires auxiliary information separating at least 2^k−1 possibilities on the Y=0 fiber. In particular a fixed-width binary supplement requires at least k bits in the worst case. This is tight as an information bound by supplying d verbatim. It is not a time lower bound on A.

**Proof.** At READY the entire configuration is a fixed function of Y. Determinism gives identical suffixes for identical Y, including read addresses and termination times relative to READY. Exactly one vector yields SAT. All other vectors yield UNSAT. A decoder receiving the same supplement for two different UNSAT vectors has identical inputs and cannot output both. Thus at least 2^k−1 distinct supplements are necessary, and ceil(log2(2^k−1))=k for k≥2. The two-component collision 01 versus 10 already changes both proposed individual answers while preserving every suffix transition. This fiber argument applies also when the decoder is asked separate coordinate questions with the same supplement and a public coordinate index, since those decoders can be run for all indices.

For a prior with half its mass on all ones and the other half uniform over the UNSAT fiber, H(Y)=1 and H(d|suffix)=½ log2(2^k−1). An exact, conditionally prefix-free auxiliary message given the suffix must have expected length at least this latter quantity by the elementary source coding inequality. Both decision labels have probability 1/2. Concentration on easy UNSAT inputs is unnecessary. Even under the promise of identical input lengths the deficit persists.
