## Exact projection proof

1. Scan pairs of parity rows in input index order. For distinct unconsumed rows (x,h,q) and (y,h,q) sharing exactly h,q, require both shared names to occur in precisely these two original rows and in no factor. Replace the pair by x=y, written as two binary clauses. Mark the two rows consumed. The two original equations imply equality by addition over GF(2). For every equal boundary value extend by h=0 and q=x. Privacy ensures every extension can coexist with the rest of the formula. Used rows cannot be selected twice. Keep equality explicit so endpoints of different factors retain their shared constraint.
2. For each surviving parity row, find an exact-one factor sharing at least two ports with it. If none is found, decline with UNKNOWN. With factor (u,v,w) and row (u,v,x), exact-one implies u XOR v=1-w. The row is therefore equivalent, under the retained factor, to w≠x. Emit its two binary clauses. If the scopes coincide, even parity contradicts exact-one, which certifies UNSAT. The retained factor justifies all its replacements simultaneously.
3. Mark every endpoint of every emitted clause. In each factor choose its lowest-ID unmarked port p. If none exists, decline. With remaining ports a,z, existentially forgetting p yields the single binary clause (¬a ∨ ¬z). Every allowed assignment extends by p=1-a-z. The original factor scopes are disjoint, and p is absent from all binary constraints, so the extensions agree globally. Use exact reachability on the single implication graph of all emitted clauses. The formula is UNSAT exactly when some literal and its complement reach each other [1]. Together the three equivalences establish both decision directions.

In the synchronized cycles the two private rung names qualify in step 1. Every longitudinal check then qualifies in step 2 on its source factor. No external check targets the source b port after the rewrite, since every incoming link targets a or z. Thus step 3 always has a private choice. This argument uses incidence and truth conditions only. Renumbering vertices or changing block order cannot remove these facts. The new method computes no per-column compatibility product, and the implication graph retains global dependencies around both rings.

Four concrete falsifiers are an additional occurrence of a helper, replacing a row without its accompanying factor, forgetting a port still tied across two factors, and interpreting an unhandled row as UNSAT. The first and third trigger occurrence checks, the second is excluded by the order of projection, and the last invokes the fallback. In particular the lower-bound candidate cannot infer separate executions for two occurrences of a shared equality.

## End-to-end ledger

Add the two implications per clause and compute transitive closure by three nested loops over literals. The wrapper has N=Ω(c²) on admitted canonical inputs.
