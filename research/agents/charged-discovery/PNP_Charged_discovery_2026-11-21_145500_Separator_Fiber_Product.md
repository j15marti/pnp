For each i, compute t_i=AND(s,x_i), then q_i=t_i OR NOT(t_i), and conjoin q_i to the running output using the same AND implementation. Pin the final output to true. The q_i stages deliberately retain bivariate witness columns while preserving the source predicate. They are a compiler control, not evidence of scalar hardness.
