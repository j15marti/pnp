## One lemma

There exists a fixed uniform deterministic solver A for all valid explicitly encoded positive Boolean exact-one triple lists such that, for every authenticated triangle-bundle source whose finite simple quotient Q is planar with girth at least five, A decides SAT if and only if every quotient cycle has even matching parity. Forests have infinite girth. The bound is polynomial in the complete direct source length, also on twisted matchings, disconnected graphs, and quotients with arbitrarily many vertices of degree above three in a single block. A outputs one bit and its terminator. No coloring is required.

Use the named triangle places and the full graph-star recognizer from [Construction](../charged-construction/PNP_Charged_construction_2026-11-22_102647_Triangle_Bundles.md). It recovers the expanded graph from the original rows and verifies triangle edges, whole inter-fiber perfect matchings and absence of extra edges. The planar and girth tests apply to the **quotient**. The expanded graph itself contains triangles. The exact-one source is equisatisfiable with proper three-coloring of that expanded graph in both directions by the verified private-leaf construction. Every edge of Q has an actual permutation p from the places of its first triangle to the second.

A proper coloring restricts on each triangle to a bijection f_v from its places to the palette. On edge uv, the requirement is that f_v p f_u^{-1} fixes no palette color. Both derangements of three letters are even. Hence the sign bits must solve s_v = s_u XOR parity(p). This proves rejection for a sign conflict. Conversely propagate signs from a root in each component. Fix either consistent root sign. The list L(v) consists of the three bijections of the prescribed sign. Given f_u, the only forbidden f_v in L(v) is f_u p^{-1}, since the resulting permutation is even and identity is the only even permutation on three letters with a fixed point. This forbiddance is a perfect matching between the two lists. A choice avoiding every matching therefore gives an actual expanded graph coloring and extends to the source. Postle and Smith-Roberge's Theorem 1.9 gives at least 2^(|V(Q)|/282) correspondence colorings on a planar graph of girth at least five with a 3-correspondence assignment. Existence suffices independently on each component. The solver checks the hypotheses and outputs its cached verdict. The bound on the number of colorings gives no exact source count and the solver does no counting. [1]

## Why the high-degree test matters

For every t>=1 join t dodecahedron graphs in a path, identifying one edge of each successive pair and retaining it once. Use disjoint entry and exit edges in every internal copy. Each gluing is planar by placing the new copy in a face incident with the attachment edge, with that edge on its outer boundary. Each copy is 2-connected. Deleting one vertex leaves both sides connected through at least one surviving endpoint, proving 2-connectivity by induction. A simple cycle using edges exclusive to both sides consists of two paths between the common endpoints, each avoiding the common edge and hence each of length at least four. The shortest cycle remains a pentagon. The quotient has q=18t+2 vertices, k=29t+1 edges and exactly 2(t-1) vertices of degree five. The others have degree three. Thus the whole graph survives degree-two peeling, and the number of hubs in its sole block is linear in t. The previous blockwise bound with factor 3 to the number of hubs can grow exponentially here. That is an upper-bound formula for one procedure, with no lower-bound implication for any solver.

All identity matchings on this quotient give SAT. Switching one edge matching to a transposition gives UNSAT since that edge belongs to a cycle. The inputs have identical dimensions. This also tests both labels without a promise of a fixed output. A source change destroying verification triggers a correct fallback. Identity matchings on arbitrary Q give SAT precisely for three-colorable Q: restrict to place zero for necessity, and from any coloring c set f_v(a)=a+c(v) modulo three for sufficiency. Identity K4, for example, is balanced, planar and UNSAT. Thus deleting the girth hypothesis while keeping the parity verdict fails.
