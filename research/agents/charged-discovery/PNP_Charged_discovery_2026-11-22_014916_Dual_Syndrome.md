## Claim and proof

Fix the deterministic solver A below before any input. On **every authenticated mixed-source image** in Construction's canonical encoding, let the port set of the t pairwise disjoint unsigned exact-one triples have size p=3t. Let V be the projection onto those ports of the solution space of all homogeneous parity equations. Write s=dim V and k=p-s. For every fixed C the full admitted path takes polynomial time in the encoded bit length N whenever k<=C log2 N, including when s grows linearly with p. There are same-length SAT and UNSAT images for every t>=4 with k=1 and s=3t-1. Consequently even on UNSAT inputs large projected dimension alone cannot force 2^s candidate checks after paid preprocessing.

Compute a basis G of V by Construction's elimination, projection and second elimination. Compute a full independent basis of the kernel of G (basis vectors of V are rows of G) and place these vectors in the rows of H. Then ker H=V by orthogonality and dimension. Denote column i of H by h_i, a k-bit vector. Any exact-one selection chooses one port per triple. That selection extends to a parity solution exactly when the XOR of its chosen columns is zero. This criterion applies after eliminating all internal variables, including private helpers.

Start R_0={0}. For triple j with columns a,b,c, form R_j as the union of R_(j-1) translated by a, by b and by c using XOR. Induction shows R_j consists exactly of the sums obtainable from one choice in each of the first j triples. Given equal sums, every suffix choice acts identically by adding its own sum, which proves exact sharing even when different histories collide. Membership of zero in R_t gives the decision. Empty factor set accepts and zero check rank accepts every collection of disjoint triples. Factor order is chosen by the algorithm. This two-layer calculation stores reachability alone.
