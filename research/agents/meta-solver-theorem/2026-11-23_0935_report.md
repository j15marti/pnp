## Precise target and constructive theorem

Let MIXED-INDEPENDENT-XOR-2SAT take two explicitly listed subsystems (A,B). A contains positive 3-variable XOR equations u XOR v XOR w=1 whose supports are pairwise disjoint. B is a 2-CNF comprising negative within-triple pair constraints and variable-copy equivalences. Both A and B are guaranteed separately satisfiable. Decide whether A AND B is SAT.

**COMPATIBILITY-BARRIER-1 (PROVED):** This promised mixed-language decision problem is NP-complete under polynomial-time many-one reductions.

**Proof:** Positive monotone ONE-IN-THREE 3SAT is NP-complete [Schaefer 1978]. Given triples e=(x,y,z), make a fresh occurrence variable y_(e,x) for each occurrence, so A consists of m *disjoint* equations y_(e,x) XOR y_(e,y) XOR y_(e,z)=1. For each triple, put (NOT a OR NOT b), (NOT a OR NOT c), (NOT b OR NOT c) into B. For each source variable, equate its copies with a star of binary equivalences (each equality uses two ordinary binary clauses). B is satisfied by all-zero copies. A is satisfied by setting one variable in each disjoint triple to 1. Together, odd parity plus at-most-one enforces exactly one 1 in every triple, and the copy equalities enforce consistent original variables. Thus the combined formula is satisfiable iff the source monotone ONE-IN-THREE instance is satisfiable. The construction has 3m copies, m parity rows, and 3m+2(3m-v)=9m-2v binary clauses, where v is the number of occurring original variables. Expand each ternary XOR to four 3-CNF clauses for 13m-2v total CNF clauses, encoding size O(m log m). Membership in NP is immediate by verifying an assignment. QED.

**Boundary:** This refutes the *unjustified inference* that combining recognized polynomial-time solvers yields a polynomial-time algorithm. It does NOT prove P!=NP, preclude a novel polynomial compatibility algorithm, or refute a properly stated conditional FPT backdoor theorem.
