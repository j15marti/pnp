## 2. Algebraic deficiency-core theorem
Let W_i=row(M_i) after normalization, each row basis independent, and W_D=sum_{i in D} W_i. Define delta(D)=|D|-dim W_D. Let D* be an inclusion-minimal maximum-deficiency subset (including the empty subset). Let rho=dim W_D*. Then there is a deterministic polynomial-time computable certificate that EVERY feasible valuation of W_D* extends to a solution of all F. Consequently the original decision problem is exactly that of the factors in D*, evaluated on rho independent bits. The algorithm has T=2^rho poly(N), S=poly(N), CM<=2^rho poly(N), including discovery and replay, and requires no supplied decomposition or uncharged solver. In particular rho=O(log N) suffices for uniform polynomial decision.

Proof: maximality of delta(D*) implies for every T subset of complement, dim(W_D*+W_T)-dim W_D* >= |T|. For each i outside D*, take its explicitly supplied row basis as a set of labeled elements in the quotient vector space by W_D*. The rank of the union over T is exactly the left side. Rado's independent-representative theorem (linear matroid plus partition matroid) gives a single selected basis row v_i from each outside factor, independent modulo W_D*. Thus a basis of W_D* together with all v_i is linearly independent. For ANY linear valuation f:W_D* -> GF(2), solve the linear system agreeing with f on its basis and giving each v_i the opposite of its corresponding forbidden RHS bit. Full row independence implies consistency for every RHS. This solution avoids every outside forbidden space and retains every observation of the inside factors. Conversely any full satisfying assignment restricts to a feasible inside valuation. No need to count, enumerate or commit to the remaining outside assignments. This is stronger than existence of compatible local representatives: their simultaneous extension for every inside valuation is proved. It is a decision-preserving elimination, not a count-preserving one.

One factor with a private direction (W_i not contained in sum of other W_j) can be eliminated by existentially varying that direction; our criterion removes whole regions even when no factor has a private direction. It is not ordinary variable-disjoint decomposition; the certificate consists of inside row basis, one indexed row per removed factor, and a joint rank check. It can be passed to a WRAPPER or separator module, with all original factor RHS and row maps retained for reverse witness lifting.

## 3. Paid discovery, no exponential subsets
Let nu(U) be the maximum common independent set on labeled basis rows of factors U, under (a) linear independence and (b) at most one row per factor. Matroid intersection computes nu in polynomial time. Then max_{D subset U} delta(D)=|U|-nu(U). Proof: any transversal omits at least |D|-rank W_D from D; conversely add a shared set of delta_max independent dummy directions outside the ambient space, available to every group. Every nonempty group collection now has rank >= its cardinality, so Rado selects one independent representative per group; discarding <=delta_max dummy selections yields the lower bound on nu. Labeled identical rows are parallel linear elements, not independent copies.

Compute Delta=max deficiency on all factors. Starting with U=all, scan factors in a fixed order and remove i from U iff the maximum deficiency on U minus i is still Delta. At most m+1 maximum intersection calls. Rejected deletions cannot become feasible later, since any later U is a subset of the earlier set. Deficiency is supermodular, so maximizers are closed under intersection and union; the resulting minimal maximizer is unique, contained in every maximizer, and minimizes rank among maximizers (rank=|D|-Delta there). One more matroid intersection call modulo W_D* obtains the extension certificate. No exhaustive subset search in the algorithm. Alternatively use strongly polynomial submodular minimization on rank(W_D)-|D| with a cardinality tie-break.

Given explicit N-bit representation, naive shortest augmenting path matroid intersection uses <=N augmentations, O(N^2) independence tests per augmentation, each dense GF(2) rank test O(N^3) bit operations as a conservative bound (ordinary polynomial address overhead can be absorbed). O(N) calls cost O(N^7) before address/log overhead. Conservative bit-RAM bounds T=O(2^rho N^8), S=O(N^3), CM<=O(2^rho N^11), including initial normalization/closure, discovery, enumeration on a basis of the inside span, Gaussian lifting, checking every source factor, and sequential verification. Optional bounded-group closure with fixed g adds its own polynomial degree; these explicit exponents refer to base rank-one closure + the Rado solver, not arbitrary other modules or optional g. A certificate of SAT verifies a full witness. An UNSAT certificate can stream all 2^rho basis valuations and an inside violated factor per valuation; alternatively recompute. Polynomial certificate of the deletion equivalence is a rank check even when the selected core is not optimal; if optimality is requested, replay the polynomial discovery. No parallel work discarded, no physical native CM measurement. With other complete solvers run by fair time slicing, total sequential work is bounded by a polynomial overhead times the minimum of their charged budgets; not by an unproved sum/product of best subproblem parameters.

## 4. Fano core and bounded-group union falsifier
Identify seven points with the nonzero vectors of GF(2)^3, seven lines {a,b,a+b}. For each line {a,b,c}, forbid monochromatic x_a=x_b=x_c: rows e_a+e_b,e_a+e_c, RHS (0,0). These are rank-two forbidden spaces, each complement has full affine hull; rank-one closure does nothing. Total observed rank is 6. Any line-free 4-point set consists of three independent points and their sum, so its complement is a line; a 5-point set cannot be line-free. Hence no 2-coloring; deleting any line yields SAT (that line all one color, other four points the other color). Any proper subset of the seven factors has rank >= cardinality: one line rank2; two rank4; three cover >=6 points; four cover >=6; five/six cover all seven; every nonempty line collection connected through intersections, difference span rank = covered vertex count minus one. Thus the full seven is the unique positive-deficiency core, Delta=1, rho=6; every deletion neighbor has Delta=0 and Rado returns a SAT witness without enumeration.

Two distinct line row spaces have zero intersection because their supports intersect in one point and every form has even support. Each forbidden space has density 1/4, any pair intersection density 1/16, so pair union density 7/16. Any union of at least three has density >=3/4-3/16=9/16. Every proper union misses the deletion-neighbor witness, so density <1. Since affine subspace densities are powers of 1/2, no proper union of two or more Fano forbidden spaces is affine. All seven do cover. Thus bounded-group merging up to six alone cannot finish even this small nonlinear core. The Rado solver does not need to discover that union, instead checks 2^6 observations. A bounded merge of seven does solve it; no claim of difficulty for other workers.

## 5. Infinite surrounding regions, no private directions
For t>=3 add fresh z_0,...,z_(t-1). Put one outside factor per i with row basis {z_i,z_(i+1 mod t),q}, q=x_1+x_2 a core form; arbitrary RHS. Generalization: use a simple 4-regular graph on the t new vertices with a balanced Euler orientation (two outgoing, two incoming), and use {z_i,z_j,z_l,q} for its two out-neighbors. Both have one factor per new vertex. Choosing z_i as the independent representative modulo the Fano span proves every Fano boundary assignment extends; every outside factor adds a fresh distinct representative, but no outside factor owns a private direction, since its coordinates occur in other factors and q is in the core. No Fano factor owns a private direction either since any six lines still span rank6. The core remains exactly the seven Fano factors (outside subsets cannot increase deficiency; minimal maximum remains Fano). After deletion of any Fano factor, the entire instance has Delta=0 and is constructively SAT. At t>=5, finite degree-four examples use i->i+1 and i->i+2 modulo t, not certified expanders. The general 4-regular expander option graph incidence contains the underlying H as minor by contracting each z_i with its own factor; thus raw width Omega(t) for expander choices, without claiming residual width hardness. Rank-four factors have constant-size 3CNF extension encodings (XOR q and OR gate), but all auxiliary clauses/variables must be charged; raw size identities below are for typed HCL input, not a hidden claim about its CNF encoding.

Exact ledger (e=0 or1 optional unused bit, a=1 or2 outgoing edges): d=7+t+e, m=7+t, total coefficient rows K=14+(a+2)t, observable rank R=6+t, observable nullity d-R=1+e, row dependency excess K-R=8+(a+1)t, rho=6 and Delta=1. Each increment t by one changes d,m,K,R,d-R,K-R by (1,1,a+2,1,0,a+1), and rho unchanged. These are family sizes, not measured work. Initial free dimension d (before enforcing any forbidden complements) grows linearly, while observable nullity is small; do not confuse these two parameters. Ordinary affine preprocessing does not fix coordinates in any individual complement of rank>=2. Full semantic closure may solve Fano by another rule; no universal hardness.

General interface: replace q by a k-dimensional independent subspace of the Fano row span, k=0..6. Outside rows then span t+k, so shared cut dimension is k, exact, and same z_i reps suffice; K=14+(a+1+k)t and excess=8+(a+k)t. For k=0 the sides are disjoint; k>0 the cut is genuinely shared. In the k=1 family, and k=2 with H=span(e_1+e_2,e_1+e_4) (points 1,2,4 not collinear), no pair of distinct factors has nested row spaces or equal row spaces; union cannot be affine for any RHS. Proof: two proper affine subsets covering their affine hull must be disjoint equal-sized parallel hyperplanes there. Containment is the other case. This does not claim absence of every higher-group merge in the surrounding region.

Parity: t=3..20 examples both parities; for a=1/2 graph degrees 2/4 even (odd degree not admissible for balanced Euler orientation; odd regular degree forces even vertex count). z-coordinate incidence a+1 is 2 or3; factor count 7+t both parities; deletion flips count parity. R=6+t both; nullity 1+e both; K-R=8+(a+1)t even for a=1, either for a=2. Cut k=0,1,2,3 verified, core rho necessarily even in Fano example, odd rho=3 verified in separate eight-coset cover on three bits (ordinary pair merge easily solves that control). Rank+nullity=d, disallow other parity triples. Fano root charges zero; flip any one RHS bit, same coefficient rank and factor count, SAT using corresponding deleted-line witness, with odd inconsistent dependency in the STACKED FORBIDDEN equations; this is NOT affine inconsistency of required equations. Gauge-switching all b_i by M_i g preserves UNSAT and consistent stacked equations. Separate forced rank-one cycle controls verify actual required affine inconsistency iff cycle XOR is odd, even/odd cycle lengths both. Outside charge does not determine Fano verdict. No use of total parity of unrelated RHS as a decision test.

## 6. Limits and universal gate
Even the computable core parameter has no universal logarithmic bound. For all distinct positive clauses of width 3 on n>=5 coordinate bits, rank of a subcollection is its support size v, and deficiency <= binomial(v,3)-v. The full collection uniquely maximizes deficiency: for v>=3 the expression increases strictly from v=3 onward except the small initial case, and at v=n>=5 it is positive; if v=n only the full collection attains the maximum. Thus rho=n. No distinct group of two or more forbidden coordinate subspaces has affine union: common zero coordinates are the intersection I of their triple supports; the union contains 0 and every unit vector outside I, hence its affine hull fixes exactly I to zero. The vector zero on I and one elsewhere lies in this hull but in none of those distinct triple subspaces. This SAT control is easily solved by all-one assignment/pure literals; it falsifies a universal small rho after only rank-one and affine-union closure, NOT after all conceivable semantic modules. No unrestricted lower bound.

The outstanding theorem must bound the remaining core rank or prove a compensating tractable structure on ALL residuals, with polynomial discovery. Seek a joint potential using (rho, rank-one backdoor beta, algebraic separator width w, local union closures) and exact conditional interactions. This is a genuine additional constructive module, not a proof of universal completeness.
