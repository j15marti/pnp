\section{Primary-literature and model preflight}
Mossel, Niles-Weed, Sun and Zadik state the normalized spread theorem for an
$R$-spread probability measure on sets of size at most $k$: a Bernoulli-$q$
set hits a supported set with probability at least $0.9$ once
$q\ge C\log k/R$ \cite{spread}.  This is the exact external input used by
ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5, after a harmless constant enlargement and a logarithm-base
change.

De Rezende, Fleming, Janett, Nordstr\"om and Pang define Resolution using
binary Resolution, not a system with free weakening, and their Theorem 2.3
supplies the linear-size $4$-CNF width--depth source family used in the
parameter ledger \cite{tradeoff}.  Their Section 7 also uses saved strips
and global deletion in a leaves-to-root chronology.  The present proof
changes the quantitative image argument and makes the no-weakening output
explicit; those changes are therefore not attributed to the paper.

For the independent ordinary-machine endpoint, Fortnow--Lipton--van
Melkebeek--Viglas Corollary 5.8 remains

\section{The audited theorem}
Let $C$ be the absolute constant in the normalized spread theorem.  For an
unsatisfiable CNF $F$ on $\nu$ variables, integers $b\ge3$ and
$1\le w\le\nu$, and $0<\delta<1-1/\log b$, define
\[
 \ell=\lceil\log(2\nu)\rceil,
 \quad h=\lceil\log(2b\nu)\rceil,
 \quad M_w=\sum_{j=0}^{w}\binom{\nu}{j}(2b)^j.
\]
ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5 claims that
\[
 b^\delta\ge64C(w+1)\ell h,
 \qquad s\le\tfrac12 b^{(1-\delta)w}                    \tag{2}
\]
allow any binary triangle DAG of size $s$ and depth $d$ for
$\operatorname{Search}(F)\circ\IND_b^\nu$ to yield a strict Resolution
refutation of $F$ satisfying
\[
                   W\le w,qquad D\le wd,qquad
                   S\le sM_w2^w.                        \tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Independent audit verdict]
Equations (2)--(3) follow from the stated normalized spread theorem.  No
weakening, oracle query, proof search, or DAG unfolding is required in the
existence argument.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof is decomposed into the four points at which a silent model change
could occur.

\emph{Full image.}  For row distribution $X$, map
$x\mapsto A_x=\{(i,x_i):i\in[\nu]\}$.  The hypothesis
$\Pr[x_J=a]\le2b^{-\delta|J|}$ makes this measure
$R$-spread with $R=b^\delta/2$.  Put
\[
 q_0=\frac{2C\ell}{b^\delta},
 \qquad T=\left\lfloor\frac{b^\delta}{4C\ell}\right\rfloor.
\]
Condition (2) gives $q_0\le1/2$ and $Tq_0\le1/2$.  The union of $T$
independent Bernoulli-$q_0$ samples misses every supported $A_x$ with
probability at most $10^{-T}$.  Its coordinate parameter is at most $1/2$,
so monotone coupling gives the same upper bound for a fair set.

If no row realizes every output on columns $Y$, choose a missing output for
each row.  The columns in $Y$ satisfy the resulting signed CNF.  Replacing,
one underlying bit at a time, both signs by its negative literal cannot
decrease the number of satisfying assignments: after all other bits are
fixed, the possible counts change from a conjunction requiring one sign,
the other sign, both, or neither to a conjunction of copies of one sign.
They change respectively as $1\mapsto1$, $1\mapsto1$,
$0\mapsto1$, or $2\mapsto2$.  The final monotone CNF is the event that the
one-set contains no $A_x$, of density at most $10^{-T}<2^{-T}$.  Thus
$\operatorname{def}(Y)<T$ is contradictory.  This proves the robust
full-image lemma with the quantifier order
\[
                    \exists x\in X\ \forall z.           \tag{4}
\]

\emph{Global errors and chronology.}  A node has at most $M_w$ cells.
Rejected cells contribute column density at most $M_w2^{-\Delta}$ for
$\Delta=2(w+1)h+2$; unsecured rows contribute fewer than
$b^{\nu-(1-\delta)w}$.  Process original DAG nodes once, leaves to root,
deleting every accumulated whole row and column before saving the current
strips.  Whole-coordinate deletion preserves nested neighborhoods.  A
current parent point still belongs to a child as that child existed when
saved because it avoids every earlier error.  Hence the children continue
to cover the cleaned parent.  The total row-error density is below $1/2$,
and
\[
 sM_w2^{-\Delta}
 \le2^{wh-1+(w+1)h-2(w+1)h-2}<\tfrac12.                \tag{5}
\]
This uses $s$ original nodes; no tree unfolding or repeated child execution
is hidden.

\emph{One row and strict inference.}  On a parent accepted rectangle, the
nested-neighborhood median argument gives a half-row by half-column
rectangle in one child.  Row density loses at most one bit and column
deficiency rises by at most one.  Equation (2) makes the latter smaller
than $T$, so (4) supplies one row before output patterns vary.  That row is
not in the child's row error, one saved child strip secures it, and all
compatible patterns in that same strip avoid child column errors.  Thus all
required child cells were accepted.  This prevents the invalid swap
$\forall z\,\exists x_z$.

Suppose the already derived child clauses are only subclauses of their cube
clauses.  Eliminate each free cube variable bottom-up.  If both returned
subclauses contain opposite pivot literals, resolve them; if one pivot is
missing, return that stronger clause unchanged.  Induction preserves a
subclause of the remaining cube clause, adds depth at most one per free
variable, and uses at most $2^q-1$ new lines for $q$ free variables.  Every
new line is a legal binary resolvent.  No weakening is simulated or needed.

\emph{Root and accounting.}  The cleaned root row and column sets each have
density at least $1/2$.  For nonempty $J$,
$\Pr[x_J=a]\le2b^{-|J|}\le b^{-\delta|J|}$ because
$b^{1-\delta}>2$.  The empty-pointer root cell is therefore accepted and
its maintained subclause is the empty clause.  Each original edge adds at
most $w$ depth and each node has at most $M_w$ cells with at most $2^w$
lines per cell, giving (3).
\end{proof}

The theorem asserts proof existence.  The potentially expensive discovery
of dense fibers, saved strips, or the row in (4) is not charged to an
arbitrary SAT decider because no such decider is transformed here.

\section{Fixed-parameter squeeze and exact quantifiers}
For fixed rationals $p>1$ and $1<\beta<1+1/p$, choose
\[
 \beta-1<\rho<1/p,
 \quad g=1+\rho-\beta,
 \quad \delta=\frac{g}{2(1+\rho)},
 \quad a=\lceil4\beta/g\rceil,                           \tag{6}
\]
then choose fixed $k$ sufficiently large and set $c=\lfloor\rho k\rfloor$.
With
\[
 q=4a+c+1,\quad u=a(k+4)+k,
 \quad z=a(1-\delta)(k+c),                               \tag{7}
\]
the two required strict margins are
\[
                      k-pq>0,
 \qquad                z-\beta(u+1)>0.                  \tag{8}
\]
Indeed
\[
 H_0=a(1-\delta)(1+\rho)-\beta(a+1)=ag/2-\beta>0,
\]
so the explicit lower bounds on $k$ in ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5 imply (8).  All
parameters are fixed before the source size $t$ grows.  The quantifier is
$\forall(p,\beta)\,\exists(H_t)$, not one family for all $p$.

Consequently the fixed-parameter region is \EXACT{} from the spread-lift,
source width--depth, and compiler/projection hypotheses.  This run assigns
the complete exact-$(2,2)$ statement \COND{} because it did not replay the
separate $58{,}462$-line compiler/certificate batch.  This is a verification
boundary, not a counterexample to that earlier verified interface.
