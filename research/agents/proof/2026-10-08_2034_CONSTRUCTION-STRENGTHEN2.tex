\section{Recovery and the strengthened contract}
FORMAL-SPINE1 charges child count times maximum child size. The recovered
peer theorem replaces that upper envelope by actual emitted and serviced
volume. Let $m_i\ge1$ be encoded lengths of actual child evaluations,
counting repeated executions with multiplicity, and put
\[
 V(N)=\sum_i m_i,\qquad M(N)=\max_i m_i,
\]
with both zero for a direct decision. Retain exactness, uniform recursive
closure, one fixed base algorithm, total local work $N^{1+o(1)}$, and
sequential resumability with local retained space $N^{p+o(1)}$ plus one
child buffer. It suffices to have
\[
 V(N)\le N^{1+o(1)},\quad M(N)\le N^{h+o(1)},\quad 0<h<1,
 \quad 0\le p<1.
\]
Every derived input, cache, suspended frame, rejected route and replay
belongs to this same-machine ledger. Distinct mathematical DAG nodes
cannot replace executed calls without separately proved and charged reuse.

\begin{theorem}[Aggregate-volume composition]
These hypotheses imply one uniform exact algorithm with
$T(N)\le N^{1+o(1)}$ and $S(N)\le N^{\max(p,h)+o(1)}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $\delta>0$, strong induction uses
\[
 \sum_i m_i^{1+\delta}\le M(N)^\delta V(N).
\]
Choose uniform error slack $\epsilon>0$ with
$\epsilon(1+\delta)<\delta(1-h)$ and $\epsilon<\delta$.
The child-time exponent is at most
$1+h\delta+\epsilon(1+\delta)<1+\delta$; local time also fits by
increasing the induction constant on a finite prefix. The machine and
base threshold do not depend on $\delta$. All live sizes along a path
contract by a fixed power above an analysis threshold. Summing local
state and ancestor-held child buffers over $O(\log\log N)$ levels gives
the stated space bound. Full details and independent audit accompany
this report in BRIDGE-STRENGTHEN1 and REDTEAM-STRENGTHEN1.
\end{proof}
This numerical contract is strictly less restrictive than the scalar
fanout condition: $\lfloor N/2\rfloor$ size-one children and one
$\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor$ child have linear total volume but linear child
count. This is a numerical scheduling example, not a SAT construction.
Constant-size encodings larger than one give the same example after
rescaling the number of leaves. Neither volume nor sequential evaluation
alone proves low-space generation.

\section{A concrete candidate: raw Shannon batches}
Try choosing a set $X$ of $t$ variables, enumerating all assignments to
$X$, and emitting their residual CNFs as an OR family. This is exact
Shannon semantics. Here the tested constructor performs only literal
restriction: remove satisfied clauses and assigned false literals.
It does not propagate, factor components, collapse an entire conflicting
formula, merge duplicates, or stop early after deciding a child.
Clause multiplicities are retained. Thus the following lower bounds are
for this specific raw output, not every implementation of Shannon search.

\begin{lemma}[Exact residual-volume identity]
For a proper, non-tautological clause $C$, put $w_C=|C|$ and
$r_C=|\operatorname{vars}(C)\cap X|$. The total literal occurrences in
all raw residual CNFs is exactly
\[
 V_{\rm lit}(F,X)=\sum_{C\in F}(w_C-r_C)2^{t-r_C}.
\]
If widths are at most three, $L=\sum_Cw_C$, and each variable occurs at
most $d$ times, then
\[
 V_{\rm lit}(F,X)\ge 2^{t-2}(L-dt).
\]
Also each individual raw residual has at least $L-3dt$ literals.
Negative right-hand sides give only a vacuous bound.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Exactly one assignment to the $r_C$ selected variables falsifies all
selected literals in $C$. The other $t-r_C$ choices are arbitrary.
Consequently $C$ survives $2^{t-r_C}$ times, each with $w_C-r_C$
literals. Sum over clauses, including zero contribution from empty
residual clauses. If $w_C-r_C>0$, then $r_C\le2$ and
$2^{-r_C}\ge1/4$. Moreover $\sum_Cr_C\le dt$.
This proves the total lower bound. At most $dt$ clauses meet $X$;
these contain at most $3dt$ source occurrences. All other clauses
remain completely unchanged in every raw residual, proving the final
bound.
\end{proof}

For a bounded-degree sparse family with $L=\Theta(n)$ and standard
fixed-width variable-index encoding $N=\Theta(L\log n)$, restricting
$t=O(\log N)$ variables leaves $N^{1-o(1)}$ literal occurrences in
every raw residual. Hence no fixed-power maximum-child bound follows.
In the same encoding, if the raw batch were to have encoded volume
$N^{1+o(1)}$, its literal-volume lower bound forces
$t=o(\log N)$ whenever $dt=o(L)$. An output bit encoding of this explicit
literal list must charge at least its literal count; header and index
bits only add cost. These assertions are not representation-independent.

This recovers the earlier raw-restriction obstruction in a form directly
matched to aggregate volume. It does not newly prove SAT hardness.
Shared representation, propagation, component factoring or certified
pruning may bypass the raw emitter and require a different charged ledger.

\begin{theorem}[No successful raw full-batch choice]
Suppose all $2^t$ raw children are actually serviced with positive explicit
encoding lengths $m_a$, each at least its residual literal count. On a
family with $d\log N=o(L)$ and $N=O(L\log L)$, no choice of $t$ can
simultaneously give $\sum_a m_a\le N^{1+o(1)}$ and
$\max_a m_a\le N^{h+o(1)}$ for fixed $h<1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Positive lengths give $2^t\le\sum_a m_a$, so near-linear volume forces
$t=O(\log N)$. Hence $dt=o(L)$. The average residual literal count is
at least $(L-dt)/4$, so some child has $\Omega(L)$ bits. The encoding
premise yields $L=\Omega(N/\log N)$ (also $L\le N$ here), exceeding
$N^{h+o(1)}$ for fixed $h<1$. This contradicts the second requirement.
\end{proof}
The positive-length condition charges even constant leaves and preserves
execution multiplicity. If branches are discarded before evaluation or
represented collectively, the hypotheses must be re-established; this
theorem cannot simply be applied to the latent assignment tree.

transfers exactly through the moment inequality, whereas its use to
acquire SAT decompositions remains a new-theorem obligation.

\section{Reconciliation and next construction target}

under SAT in P with near-linear actual child volume, fixed-power maximum
child size, near-linear total local time, and sublinear simultaneous
space. The aggregate extension preserves conditional elimination of
$K$. It removes an unnecessarily pessimistic numerical requirement;

parent-scale decision then emitting a constant answer retains that cost.
