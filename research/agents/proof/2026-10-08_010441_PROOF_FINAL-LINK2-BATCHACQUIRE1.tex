\section{Result}

This run constructs a genuine shared \emph{BatchAcquire} object.  Given a
source CNF $F$ and a fixed disjoint route trie, it reads the source once,
propagates every source clause symbolically through the trie, and stores each
stable residual fragment at the highest node where that fragment is shared.
For a source-matched clause cover, one additional source scan chooses the
clauses.  A child is then reconstructed from payloads on one root-to-leaf
path, without rereading $F$.

The exact positive result is therefore stronger than a collection of
separately acquired children:
\[
 \boxed{\text{one shared source acquisition, exact residual recovery, and no
 source reread during recovery}.}
\]
It resolves the narrow parent-reread issue for a fixed route family.

It does not close the final link.  Either the children are emitted as
ordinary CNFs, in which case total emission and recovery costs return, or a
child is represented by a short leaf identifier beside the shared object
$H$, in which case the input is not a smaller ordinary CNF and an arbitrary
SAT running-time bound cannot be applied to the identifier alone.  A hostile
semantic antichain with a large common core made this distinction explicit:
one source scan replaced 64 source scans, yet materializing the 64 ordinary
children emitted 478,208 bits and required 2,204,160 charged recovery units.

The scientific status remains \textbf{OPEN}.  The new theorem is an exact
shared-acquisition theorem, not a general small-instance compiler theorem and
not a P versus NP proof.

\section{Objects and charging model}

Let $F$ be a normalized CNF of encoded size $M$.  A route trie $T$ has a cube
$\rho_v$ at every node $v$, obtained by accumulating edge assignments from
the root.  Its leaves are pairwise inconsistent cubes.  The intended cover
condition is
\[
 F\text{ satisfiable}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \bigvee_{u\in L(T)} F\res\rho_u\text{ satisfiable}.
\]
For witness preservation, a model of $F\res\rho_u$ is combined with $\rho_u$.

The implementation charges the following quantities in the same execution:
\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{Discovery and construction.}  Source literal reads,
normalization, candidate hashing, greedy clause-selection probes, route-trie
construction, assignment-delta probes, symbolic trie visits, payload writes,
and storage-accounting work.
\item[] \textbf{Representation.}  The residual-fragment pool, route trie,
node-to-fragment references, payload offsets, leaf descriptors, and the
read-only source when reporting total logical memory.
\item[] \textbf{Recovery.}  Payload-reference reads, emitted literals, and
canonicalization.  The ledger separately records the total bits of all
standalone children and the maximum encoded child size.
\item[] \textbf{Validation only.}  Independent source replay is performed for
every leaf.  Its reads are reported but excluded from constructor cost because
they are test-oracle work, not part of the proposed procedure.
\end{itemize}

The input is scanned twice for clause-selected covers, once to choose clauses
and once to compile the batch.  Shannon controls require only the compilation
scan.  Storage metadata, including maximum variable, clause count, literal
count, and maximum clause length, is accumulated during that scan rather than
by an uncharged later reread.

\section{Source-matched disjunctive cover}

For a source clause $C=(\ell_1\vee\cdots\vee\ell_w)$, let
\[
 \tau_i=\{\ell_1=0,\ldots,\ell_{i-1}=0,\ell_i=1\}
 \qquad(1\le i\le w).
\]
The cubes $\tau_i$ are disjoint and partition the assignments satisfying
$C$.  Since every model of $F$ satisfies every clause selected from $F$, the
first selected clause gives an exact OR cover of all source models.  Applying
the same rule to the residual of each later selected clause produces a trie
whose leaves still partition all source models.

\paragraph{CLAUSE-TRIE-COVER1 (\Exact).}
For any finite list of clauses selected syntactically from $F$, the resulting
first-true-literal trie is pairwise disjoint, covers every model of $F$, and
has the witness lift described above.

\paragraph{Proof.}
At one node, a source model either already satisfies the selected clause by
the path assignment or has a unique first true literal in its residual.
Thus it continues through exactly one outgoing edge.  Induction over selected
clauses gives unique continuation to one leaf.  Conversely, a model of a leaf
residual extended by the path cube satisfies $F$ by the definition of
restriction.  \hfill$\square$

This rule is source-checkable and makes no SAT call.  Its route count can
still be the product of the selected clause widths, and selecting enough
clauses to force size shrinkage is not known to have a favorable uniform
tradeoff on unrestricted CNF.

\section{The shared residual-delta compiler}

For a source clause $C$ and trie node $v$, let $R_v(C)$ be the residual after
the path cube $\rho_v$.  If $C$ is already satisfied, it contributes nothing
below $v$.  Otherwise, if no variable of $R_v(C)$ is assigned on any edge
strictly below $v$, then the residual is stable throughout that subtree and a
single interned copy of $R_v(C)$ is stored in the payload of $v$.  If it is
not stable, the procedure applies each child-edge delta and continues only
into unsatisfied children.

For a leaf $u$, recovery collects every fragment referenced by payloads on
the root-to-$u$ path and canonicalizes the resulting clause multiset.  The
shared object $H$ consists of the trie, the interned fragment pool, payload
references, and offsets.

\paragraph{TRIE-BATCHACQUIRE1 (\Exact).}
For every CNF $F$, every finite assignment trie $T$, and every leaf $u$,
the recovered formula is exactly the canonical residual
\[
                  \operatorname{Recover}(H,u)
                  =\operatorname{canon}(F\res\rho_u).
\]
Compilation performs one source-literal scan after $T$ is fixed.  Recovery
performs no source-literal read.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Fix a source clause $C$ and leaf $u$.  Symbolic propagation follows the unique
root-to-$u$ branch until an edge satisfies $C$, or until the current residual
is stable below the current node, or until $u$ is reached.  In the first case
$C$ is absent from $F\res\rho_u$.  In either remaining case exactly the
residual $C\res\rho_u$ is stored at one ancestor of $u$.  Hence the path
payloads contain exactly one correct contribution for every unsatisfied
source clause.  Interning and final canonicalization remove only duplicate
clauses, exactly as canonical restriction does.  Applying this argument to
every source clause proves the equality.  Each source clause is introduced
only in the compilation loop, so compilation has one source scan; recovery
accesses only $H$.  \hfill$\square$

The theorem deliberately states the exact access property, not an optimistic
time bound.  A clause may be propagated through many trie nodes, so symbolic
delta work can still scale with the route family.  The compiler's measured
work includes those visits.

\section{Experiment}

The 13-case suite included sparse and dense planted random 3-CNF, a
repeated-clause sharing control, disjoint components, a backdoor-DNF control, satisfiable
and odd-charge-unsatisfiable prism Tseitin encodings, PHP$(5,4)$ through
PHP$(7,6)$, a 2-CNF chain, a 64-way semantic antichain, and the same antichain
with a large common 2-CNF core.  Every Python run had a 25-second internal
alarm and a 30-second shell cap.  The final run took 2.33 seconds.

There were 709 exact residual recovery checks, nine source-witness route
checks, 64 exhaustive source-model cover checks on eligible small instances,
and 8,064 explicit semantic non-implication checks.  No check failed.  The
two antichain controls each had 64 unique residuals, zero equality merges,
and zero clause-subset comparable pairs.

Tseitin, PHP, nor antichain results support a theorem that such opportunities
cover every general input with the required resource bounds.

\section{Correct exponent ledgers}

Write
\[
 q=M^a,\qquad \max_i|G_i|=M^\lambda,\qquad
 W_{\rm acquire}=M^\beta,
\]
and suppose the invoked SAT procedure has exponent $k=2$.  For ordinary
all-block acquisition, the fanout must be charged.  The corrected exponent is
\[
             E_{\rm ordinary}=a+\beta+2\lambda.
\]
Under the common localization relation $a=1-\lambda$, this becomes
$1+\beta+\lambda$, so the $4/3$ target requires
\[
                   \boxed{\beta+\lambda<1/3}.
\]
The old half-scale point $\lambda=1/2$ therefore cannot meet the target.

If the batch construction is genuinely global, a deliberately optimistic
time ledger is
\[
 E_{\rm shared}=\max\{\beta,\rho,a+2\lambda\},
\]
where $M^\rho$ is total recovery work.  This removes repeated source
acquisition but not the cost of solving $q$ ordinary children.  The final
column of the table reports this expression.  Every tested case was above
$4/3$; the plain antichain was closest at 1.351.  These finite failures do not
prove an asymptotic lower bound, but they provide no empirical closure.

More importantly, the optimistic ledger applies only after the children are
ordinary instances.  It cannot treat a leaf identifier as an $M^\lambda$-bit
CNF while silently leaving $H$ beside the solver.

\section{The interface trilemma}

The experiment isolates three choices.
\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{Standalone children.}  Emit each
$G_u=\operatorname{Recover}(H,u)$.  Existing ordinary-CNF SAT bounds apply,
but total recovery and output are charged.  The common-core antichain shows
that these costs can erase source-read sharing.
\item[] \textbf{Parent-aware descriptor.}  Give the consumer $(H,u)$.  The
leaf identifier is short and the common core stays shared, but $(H,u)$ is not
an ordinary CNF of the identifier's size.  A new parent-aware SAT theorem,
including all accesses to $H$, is required.
\item[] \textbf{Self-contained descriptor.}  Copy enough of $H$ into each
child to restore the ordinary interface.  The child size or aggregate output
then inherits the shared object's current-scale information, losing the
claimed descriptor shrinkage.
\end{itemize}

This is not a claim that shared solving is impossible.  It identifies the
exact theorem still needed: a solver or compiler whose interface explicitly
supports the shared object, whose construction and query costs are proved for
the same realization, and whose total exponent beats the target on every
input.

\section{Relation to recovered mechanisms and literature}

Clause equality, oriented dominance, components, Horn, 2-CNF, fixed
backdoors, and backdoor DNFs remain sound partial routes.  Backdoor DNFs are
designed to give tautological OR covers into a tractable base class, but known
detection results are parameterized results for named classes, not a theorem
that every CNF has a cheaply found favorable DNF \cite{ordyniak2021bdnf}.
Backdoor depth and recursive backdoors likewise organize tractable restricted
instances without supplying bounded favorable depth for all CNFs
\cite{dreier2022,maehlmann2021}.

Knowledge compilation explains why a shared compiled representation can
support efficient downstream queries once it exists.  It does not provide a
uniform cheap compiler from unrestricted CNF into a small representation
with the required operations \cite{darwiche2002}.  Sparsification gives exact
disjunctive decompositions for fixed-width CNF, but its exponential-in-$n$
fanout is not the missing polynomial-in-$M$ shared-interface theorem
\cite{ipz2001,calabro2010}.  The present result is consistent with all of
these boundaries.

\section{Status and next theorem}

\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{PROVED/EXACT.}  The source-matched clause trie is a disjoint,
witness-preserving cover of all source models.
\item[] \textbf{PROVED/EXACT.}  For any fixed route trie, one batch source
scan builds an exact residual-delta object; every canonical residual is
recovered without a source reread.
\item[] \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL.}  All 709 residual checks and all cover,
witness, and antichain checks passed on the stated suite with a fully exposed
discovery, storage, recovery, emission, and workspace ledger.
\item[] \textbf{FALSIFIED AS A CLOSURE CLAIM.}  ``The leaf identifier is the
small child'' is invalid under the ordinary-CNF interface because it retains
current-scale shared state.  ``Shared source reads imply cheap standalone
children'' failed on the common-core antichain.
\item[] \textbf{OPEN.}  No construction here proves, for every CNF, a shared
object and consumer with simultaneous favorable construction, total query,
recovery, recursive-size, and workspace bounds.  No P versus NP conclusion
follows.
\end{itemize}

The sharp next target is therefore
\[
 \boxed{\begin{minipage}{0.82\linewidth}
 A source-checkable, witness-preserving shared compiler and parent-aware SAT
 consumer for general CNF, with a same-realization proof charging discovery,
 $H$, every query to $H$, all recovery or emission, total fanout, recursive
 size, and peak workspace, and with total exponent strictly below $4/3$.
 \end{minipage}}
\]
