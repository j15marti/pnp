\paragraph{Definition and correctness.}
For every finite CNF F, fixed consistent partial assignment a and integer
b at least one, normalize each surviving clause by removing repeated
literals and sorting literals, and discard tautologies and satisfied clauses.
Retain an empty residual clause. Repeatedly scan the immutable source,
keeping the b lexicographically least distinct normalized clauses strictly
above the previous block maximum. Emit that block in order.
The invariant after each processed source clause is that the buffer contains
the b least eligible distinct clauses seen so far. Insertion and eviction of
the greatest element preserve it. At pass completion the next block is
therefore exact. Strict advancement excludes duplicates across blocks.
Finite termination and complete enumeration follow.
Two such streams agree exactly if their normalized clause sets agree.
Consequently a SAT answer for one may be reused for the other.
This is syntactic normalization, not general Boolean-equivalence testing.
No subsumption or variable-renaming canonicalization is claimed.

\paragraph{Same-machine resource ledger.}
Let N denote encoded source bits, U distinct residual clauses, w maximum
source width, d assigned variables, and C=O(w log(N+2)) a clause-record bound
for a conventional explicit CNF encoding. A single stream retains
O(b C+C+d log(N+2)+log(N+2)) logical bits, including current clause,
previous maximum, cursor and assignment. Two streams coexist during
comparison. Temporary insertion permits b+1 references; Python object
overhead is not certified by these logical bounds.
No child CNF is materialized inside the comparison consumer.
The test oracle does materialize CNFs and is excluded from candidate cost.
The implementation uses sorted-list insertion, so comparison and movement
costs are additional to the recorded source-read ledger.
At most ceil(U/b)+1 full scans are used by each exhausted stream.
Each scan redoes restriction and normalization. Discovery, failed
comparisons and repeated invocations must all be added by any integrating
wrapper. Ancestor state, complete SAT child executions, and arbitrary
solver random access are not supplied by this module.

\paragraph{Falsification of a favorable inference.}
For bounded-width distinct residual clauses with U proportional to
N/log N and C proportional to log N, choosing b proportional to B/C gives
scan work of order N squared divided by B on this implementation.
At B=N to the power one half this is N to the power three halves,
already exceeding N to the power six fifths before other work.
This is an algorithm-specific obstruction, not a SAT lower bound.
The positive-clause scaling family is easy SAT and demonstrates precisely
that unnecessary canonicalization can itself be expensive.
A capped implementation can stop with UNKNOWN, but a cap proves no
all-input coverage.

\paragraph{Validation.}
The self-contained adjacent Python file has an internal 24-second alarm
and was run under timeout 25s; it completed successfully.
Exhaustive 256 subsets of the eight proper signed three-variable clauses,
three assignment settings and three capacities give 2304 normalization
checks. Controls include duplicates, tautologies, literal/clause reversal,
an empty clause, and distinct residuals with the same UNSAT answer.
Twelve scaling comparisons use 8,32,128,512 distinct clauses and capacities
1,4,16. For 512 clauses, source literal reads are respectively
4202496,1056768,270336. Each stream buffer respects its capacity;
the two-stream peak is not the single-buffer counter.
The independent audit confirmed correctness conditions and the rescan
obstruction. Measurements are source events, not certified total bit time.

\paragraph{Literature and transfer audit.}
Munro and Paterson, Selection and sorting with limited storage,
Theoretical Computer Science 12 (1980), 315--323,
doi:10.1016/0304-3975(80)90061-4, study read-only sequential input
and limited internal storage. The original Warwick report and abstract
were checked at \url{https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/46321/}.
This is a useful model analogy, not a transferred lower bound for arbitrary
SAT machines. Our obstruction follows directly from the implemented scans.
No cryptographic, oracle, physical, or nonuniform assumption is used.
