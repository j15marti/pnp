\section{The construction}
For proper distinct-clause 3-CNFs A and B on disjoint variables, write each A clause as (a or b or c) and output clauses (s or a or y) and (not y or b or c). For every B clause (d or e or f), output (not s or d or z) and (not z or e or f). Denote the conjunction by O(A,B). \begin{theorem}
A syntactic integer Cutting Planes refutation of F can be translated, using guarded axioms L_C(x)+g >= 1 for g=s or g=1-s, into a syntactic CP derivation of g >= 1 with polynomial binary encoding overhead.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof. For each line a.x >= b carry a.x + k g >= b, k >= 0. A clause has k=1 and a Boolean bound k=0. Addition and positive scaling update k. For division by d, pad k to K=d ceil(k/d) using the Boolean bound (K-k)g >= 0. Then divide exactly, obtaining (a/d).x + ceil(k/d) g >= ceil(b/d). For g=1-s the induced constant K is divisible by d and ceil((b-K)/d)=ceil(b/d)-K/d. At the contradictory final line one has k g >= 1 with k > 0, since the guarded axioms all have a Boolean model when g=1. Divide by k to derive g >= 1. Integer bit lengths are polynomial in the explicit source proof. QED.
