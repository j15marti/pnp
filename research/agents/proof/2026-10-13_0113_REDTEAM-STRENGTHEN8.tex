\section{Target, quantifiers, and computation model}

LSPV-4 report:
\[
 \texttt{ALLFIELD22-TO-PREA1}:\quad
 \text{ALLFIELD22 supplies a favorable all-input PRE-A successor.}
\]
The relevant quantifier is not merely existence of a polynomial procedure on
one orbit.  It is
\[
 \exists W\ \exists h,p<1\ \forall F\ \forall\text{ recursively reached }G:
 W(G)\text{ directly decides or emits essential strictly smaller children},
\]
with one uniform machine, encoded bit length, all discovery and failed
attempts, executed child calls with multiplicity, replay, buffers, indices,
and ancestor state charged simultaneously.  No unresolved parent-size SAT
call may be used to start or finish the contraction.

The endpoint remains the deterministic random-access Turing-machine theorem
of Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (FLMV), with read-only random-access
input, charged work and index tapes.  Their Corollary 5.8 states, for every
constant $c>1$,
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
 \operatorname{DTISP}\!\left[n^c,
 o\!\left(n^{d(c)}\right)\right],\qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).
\]
Their Theorem 3.1 supplies locally computed SAT-reduction bits with
polylogarithmic time and logarithmic workspace overhead.  Thus, for example,
strictly below $(6/5,d(6/5))$ transfers to SAT after margins.  This report
does not use the retired unrestricted $2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint.

\section{Independent audit of ALLFIELD22}
For a literal $\ell$, ALLFIELD22 uses
\begin{align*}
N(\ell)={}&(\ell\vee a\vee b)\wedge(a\vee\neg c\vee\neg d)
\wedge(\neg a\vee c\vee\neg e)\\
&\wedge(\neg a\vee d\vee e)\wedge(b\vee\neg c\vee\neg d)
\wedge(\neg b\vee c\vee\neg e)\wedge(\neg b\vee d\vee e).
\end{align*}
Each internal variable has signed occurrence degree $(2,2)$, setting all
five internal variables to one extends either external value, and exhaustive
truth tables give 8 and 14 internal extensions for external values 0 and 1.

For a proper distinct-clause source with $m>0$, write
\[
 r_x=\max(p_x,q_x,2),\quad R_0=\sum_x r_x,\quad
 D=\sum_x(2r_x-p_x-q_x)=2R_0-3m.
\]
Since the deficit of a variable of total occurrence degree $d>0$ is at most
$3d$, $D\le9m$.  Each neutral slot adds ten occurrences of either sign to
the balanced source, hence $R=R_0+10D$.  The recovered balanced compiler
therefore gives
\[
 v=6R=9m+63D\le576m,\qquad
 M=8R=12m+84D\le768m.
\]
The variable/constant substitution maps every monomial to one monomial or
zero.  The two algebraic repairs used by the report are exact over every
field:
\[
 x(1-x)=-(x^2-x),\qquad
 x\bar x=x(x+\bar x-1)-(x^2-x).
\]
Thus the stated degree and expanded-size projection is model-typed; it is
not a claim about arbitrary machine execution.

\begin{lemma}[Audited local support]
For every source vertex of the subset-cardinality source, at most 610 target
clauses imply its signed threshold inequality on at most five chosen edge
representatives, even after independent variable complementation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A degree-four source vertex contributes four source clauses; a degree-five
vertex contributes ten.  Every incident source edge has at most six clones.
Including the complete directed equality cycle therefore uses at most
$4\cdot6=24$ or $5\cdot6=30$ padded implications.  In the worst global
triple grouping, each selected implication can use a different copy of the
19-clause port-forcing gadget $Q$.  Hence the support bounds are
\[
4+24+19\cdot24=484,\qquad
10+30+19\cdot30=610.
\]
The complete cycles equate the representatives, and the full $Q$ copies
force the selected ports zero, so the transformed source clauses imply the
source threshold.  If $x_i=y_i$ or $1-y_i$, the coefficient of $y_i$ is
$+1$ or $-1$ and the right side shifts by the number of complemented
variables.  With at most five variables, every needed right side remains in
$[-5,5]$.
\end{proof}

Enumerating every subformula of at most 610 clauses and every signed-unit
inequality on at most five variables yields
$O((M+1)^{610}(v+1)^5)$ candidate pairs.  Validity of one candidate can be
checked over at most $3\cdot610+5=1835$ Boolean variables, a fixed constant
for this algorithm.  Exact rational LP feasibility of all retained
inequalities is polynomial in the explicit candidate system.  Soundness is
one-sided: every Boolean model satisfies every retained inequality.  On the
orbit, summing left lower bounds and right upper bounds cancels each edge
coefficient, including after complementation, and yields $0\ge1$.

Consequently the report's orbit-wide polynomial partial refuter is
\EXACT{} in its declared ordinary-machine scope.  Its exponent and constants
are enormous, its explicit workspace is only asserted polynomial, and it is
not an FLMV-forbidden simultaneous point.  Mik\v{s}a--Nordstr\"om's Theorem
4.8 supplies the separate PC/PCR degree and size lower bound over the fixed
field of each refutation; their paper explicitly measures expanded monomial
size and states the arbitrary-field model.  The local LP refuter is a
different inference mechanism, so the two statements are compatible.

\section{Why the result cannot be a PRE-A successor}
\begin{theorem}[Essential-child obstruction]
Let $R$ be any sound one-sided procedure returning UNSAT or UNKNOWN, and let
$A$ be any total exact SAT decider.  Define
\[
 D_A(F)=\begin{cases}
 \text{UNSAT},&R(F)=\text{UNSAT},\\
 A(F),&R(F)=\text{UNKNOWN}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then $R$ cannot by itself turn $A$ into an all-input PRE-A successor: on
every satisfiable $F$, soundness forces $R(F)=\text{UNKNOWN}$, so $D_A$
executes the current-size call $A(F)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $R(F)=\text{UNSAT}$ on a satisfiable formula, $R$ is unsound.  Therefore
all satisfiable inputs take the fallback branch.  Satisfiable formulas exist
at arbitrarily large encoded lengths by harmless padding.  The fallback is
not a strict child and is executed with multiplicity one at parent scale.
All work of $R$, including failed discovery and LP storage, is additive:
\[
 T_{D_A}(N)\le T_R(N)+T_A(N),\qquad
 S_{D_A}(N)\le S_R(N)+S_A(N)+O(1),
\]
and the fallback term cannot be erased from a worst-case bound.  Hence the
composition neither eliminates an unknown exponent $K$ nor supplies the
essential strictly smaller SAT child required by PRE-A.
\end{proof}

The compiler $U$ also expands an $m$-clause source to at most $768m$ target
clauses.  Its inverse proof transformation is useful for proof complexity,
but it is not a size-reducing SAT successor.  Adding the orbit refuter as a
parallel wrapper only enlarges the verified terminal promise.  It does not
cover UNKNOWN inputs, make the promise successor-closed for general SAT, or
bound aggregate child volume on every input.  Conditional $K$ elimination
from FORMAL-SPINE1 remains valid only after a favorable all-input compiler is
supplied; ALLFIELD22 does not supply that premise.
