\section{Exact restricted theorem}
Let F be UNSAT on nu variables and m clauses. Search(F) composed with IND_b asks Alice holding pointers and Bob holding bits to output a false clause. A c-bit public-output deterministic protocol generates a same-realization rectangle tree of depth at most c and size at most $2^{c+1}-1$, since each bit partitions a Cartesian rectangle by one side's input. Every leaf has a fixed correct source-clause label, and rectangles are triangles.
Under the original ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5 spread hypotheses, a protocol cost c satisfies
\[c>(1-\delta)w\log_2 b-2\quad\mathrm{or}\quad wc\ge d_F(w).\]
The first branch follows by applying the restricted RS5 lift when $2^{c+1}\le b^{(1-\delta)w}/2$. A split-input search machine with r ownership handoffs and B-bit full configuration gives communication at most $r(B+1)+\lceil\log_2m\rceil+O(1)$.
\section{Counterexample}
For F=(z) AND (NOT z), the source SAT bit is constant UNSAT, while indexed search is INDEX_b(i,y)=y_i. The b pairs $(i,e_i)$ form a monochromatic fooling set: every crossed pair is zero. Hence deterministic public-output communication requires at least $\lceil\log_2 b\rceil$ bits. For a proper exact-(2,2) unsatisfiable F with no empty clauses, no fixed clause is false under all assignments, so source answer alone cannot be a search-label certificate.
