\section{The part that survives}

\textsc{EXTREME-1} tests all-zero and all-one.  If an extreme assignment
fails, it retains one falsified proper width-three clause and tests the at
most three assignments obtained by flipping one of that clause's variables.
It returns TRUE only after a complete verification scan; otherwise it returns
UNKNOWN, never FALSE.

\begin{theorem}[exact terminal bound]
On every valid proper $3$CNF of encoded length $N$, a fixed source-pointer
implementation of \textsc{EXTREME-1} is sound and complete for models at
Hamming distance at most one from an extreme assignment, runs in
$N^{1+o(1)}$ time and $N^{o(1)}$ writable space, and executes no SAT child.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a one-flip model changes variable $x$, then $x$ belongs to every clause
falsified by the underlying extreme assignment.  In particular it belongs to
the first witnessed falsified clause and is tested.  The converse follows
from the verification scan.  At most eight scans occur.  Literal evaluation
uses its sign and a comparison between a read-only ULEB variable identifier
and the retained source substring.  A constant number of
$O(\log N)$-bit pointers and counters suffices.  The Python dictionary used
by the finite runner is not the theorem implementation.
\end{proof}

For pairwise disjoint triples $(a_i,b_i,c_i)$ and hub $x$, set
\[
\Psi_m=\bigwedge_i(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)
\wedge(x\vee\neg a_i\vee b_i)
\wedge(\neg x\vee\neg b_i\vee c_i)
\wedge(\neg c_i\vee a_i\vee b_i).
\]
All-one is a model.  The family is connected, sign-balanced,
non-Horn/non-Krom, has incomplete masks, no guard grammar, and no unit or
pure literal.  Any operational strong-Horn backdoor of size at most two
misses an entire private positive triple for $m\ge2$, so the early-empty
shortcut cannot help.  No singleton hits every width-three clause.
Eliminating $x$ creates non-tautological cross-block width-four resolvents;
eliminating a local variable leaves the remaining blocks in one linear-size
hub component.  Hence every PIVOT exceeds its output/time or square-root
child cap for sufficiently large $m$.  The exact named old controller
returns UNKNOWN while \textsc{EXTREME-1} accepts.

Let $D_+^\star$ be the least typed successor closure after prepending this
terminal to corrected $A_0$.  Recovery9's terminal absorption theorem then
gives
\[
D_0^\star\subsetneq D_+^\star,qquad
(6/5,1/2)\in\A(D_+^\star).
\]
The terminal theorem is used only with finitely many terminal types, one
common $o(1)$ modulus, $c_\tau\ge1$, and all terminal executions from failed
and successful attempts charged.  Its exact envelope is
\[
C=\max\!\left\{1,r,\max_\tau[w_\tau+g_\tau(c_\tau-1)],
                    \frac{v-h}{1-h}\right\},
\quad
P=\max\!\left\{p,h,\max_\tau g_\tau\max(1,d_\tau)\right\}.
\]
This theorem does not make $D_+^\star$ equal to all SAT.

\section{Rejected Horn/dual-Horn promotion}

The singleton scanner's structural lemma is correct.  In a non-Horn formula,
a singleton strong-Horn variable must be one of the at most two positive
literals in the first two-positive clause; a three-positive clause rules it
out.  A full scan checks those candidates.  Literal complementation gives
the dual lemma.

The resource claim does not follow.  ARC7's experimental
\texttt{components()} stores the set of remaining clauses, every discovered
component index, and its support.  This is $\Theta(N)$ derived state before
Horn solving.  A step cap preserves soundness only by returning UNKNOWN; it
does not prove completion on the displayed strict family.  A future proof
must give a concrete read-only component enumerator, charge its rescans, and
share one half-space allowance among serialized component data, Horn work
arrays, indexes and ancestor state.

Two smaller semantic hazards were reproduced.  Restricting
\[
(x)\wedge(\neg x)\wedge(x\vee y)
\]
on either value of $x$ produces an empty residual clause; dropping it causes
a false SAT answer.  Restricting $(x\vee a)\wedge(a)$ at $x=0$ yields two raw
copies of $(a)$; canonical deduplication must be paid for or duplicates kept.
Finally Davis--Putnam elimination on
$(x\vee a\vee\neg b)\wedge(\neg x\vee c\vee\neg d)$ emits a width-four
resolvent.  Root width-three totality is not successor totality.

\section{Exact fallback collision}

ARC6 used
\[
H(F)=G(\widehat G(\mathsf{Iso}(F)))
\]
to map arbitrary proper $3$CNF into the old portfolio's UNKNOWN domain.  Once
\textsc{EXTREME-1} is prepended, this statement is false.

\begin{proposition}[one-clause counterexample]
Let $F=(\neg x\vee\neg y\vee\neg z)$.  Then all-false satisfies $F$ and the
complete old embedding $H(F)$.  Therefore \textsc{EXTREME-1} returns TRUE on
$H(F)$, so $H(F)$ is not in the new UNKNOWN domain.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
All-false satisfies the source clause.  In isolation, every cloned negative
literal is false exactly with its original and each four-clause equality
gadget is satisfied.  Every even-parity guard block $P_0$ is satisfied by
all-false.  Both lifted equivalence pairs and both outer binary equivalence
clauses are satisfied.  Thus the same assignment extends through every
layer.  The bounded executable reproduced an embedded formula with 344
variables and 803 clauses and verified the all-false model.
\end{proof}

Consequently the old claim
$\A(U_{\mathrm{new}})=\A(\SAT)$ cannot be inherited without modifying its
reduction.  The strict promise theorem above survives independently.

\section{Exact shielded fallback repair}

For six fresh variables define the two-block NAE shield
\[
Q=(a\vee b\vee c)\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee\neg c)\wedge
  (d\vee e\vee f)\wedge(\neg d\vee\neg e\vee\neg f).
\]
It is satisfiable, and every model has at least two true and two false
variables.  Put $S(F)=F\wedge Q$ before clause isolation and define
\[
H_S(F)=G(\widehat G(\mathsf{Iso}(S(F)))).
\]

\begin{theorem}[shielded fallback equality]
Let $U_3$ be the UNKNOWN domain of the exact scoped policy consisting of
\textsc{EXTREME-1}, the old ARC5 prefix, GUARD-STRIP, and the corrected
post-strip PIVOT/pure policy.  Then for every valid nonempty proper source
$F$,
\[
\SAT(H_S(F))=\SAT(F),\qquad H_S(F)\in U_3,
\qquad \A(U_3)=\A(\text{proper-3SAT})=\A(\SAT).
\]
The reduction retains ARC6's quasilinear encoded size, polylogarithmic local
bit access and $O(\log N)$ construction state.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$Q$ is a satisfiable formula on fresh variables, so conjoining it preserves
SAT.  Isolation's equality gadgets preserve the six source values, and both
guard layers contain the inner source.  Hence every model at every relevant
terminal boundary retains at least two true and two false variables;
\textsc{EXTREME-1} must return UNKNOWN.

ARC6's universal fallback theorem applies to every nonempty proper source.
Apply it unchanged to $S(F)$ rather than $F$.  Thus every old named member,
including PIVOT, the mask rules and post-strip policy, declines with its
already proved encoded-bit ledger.  The same locally accessible simulation
then proves the achievable-region equality.  The shield is inserted inside
the universal map; attaching raw $Q$ outside would expose two components and
a size-two strong-Krom backdoor and is invalid.
\end{proof}

One NAE block is insufficient: assignment $100$ satisfies it and lies at
Hamming distance one from all-zero.  Two blocks are enough.  The bounded test
checked all eight one-clause sign patterns for SAT equivalence and reproduced
the counterexample's shielded final instance (1,576 variables, 3,695 clauses),
on which \textsc{EXTREME-1} declined.  Larger forced-constant and dual-rail
shields were also tested but are not needed for the theorem.

\section{Literature and endpoint boundary}

Small strong backdoors and bounded recursive-backdoor depth yield
fixed-parameter tractability when the parameter is bounded; no cited theorem
forces bounded size/depth on every input or supplies the present all-input
compiler.  The exact endpoint retained by the project is FLMV Corollary~5.8
for deterministic random-access machines,
\[
\operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
\operatorname{DTISP}[n^c,o(n^{d(c)})],\qquad
d(c)=\tfrac12((c+2)/c^2-c).
\]
At $c=1201/1000$, $d(c)\approx0.509108>1/2$.  No unrestricted
$t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ claim is used.  Nishimura--Ragde--Szeider, SAT 2004,
\url{https://www.satisfiability.org/SAT04/programme/51.pdf};
M\"ahlmann--Siebertz--Vigny, MFCS 2021,
\url{https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2021.73};
Dreier--Ordyniak--Szeider, JCSS 142 (2024) 103520,
\url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2024.103520}; and
Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, JACM 2005,
\url{https://doi.org/10.1145/1101821.1101822}, do not close the missing arrow.
