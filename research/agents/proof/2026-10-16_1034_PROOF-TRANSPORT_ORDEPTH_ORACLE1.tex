\section{Depth-sensitive two-way integer CP OR transport}
Let $A,B$ be unsatisfiable proper disjoint-variable 3-CNFs with $q=m_A+m_B$ clauses. Replace $(a\vee b\vee c)\in A$ by $(s\vee a\vee y)$, $(\neg y\vee b\vee c)$; replace $(d\vee e\vee f)\in B$ by $(\neg s\vee d\vee z)$, $(\neg z\vee e\vee f)$, using distinct fresh local variables. Denote this formula $\mathcal O(A,B)$. It has $2q$ clauses and is SAT iff $A$ or $B$ is SAT.

\begin{theorem}[OR3-CP-DEPTH2]
In syntactic integer Cutting Planes with clause and Boolean-bound axioms, integer addition, nonnegative scaling and legal coefficient-dividing rounding, suppose $A,B$ have refutations of depths $D_A,D_B$ and line counts $S_A,S_B$. Then $\mathcal O(A,B)$ has a refutation of depth at most $2\max(D_A,D_B)+4$ and line count $O(q+S_A+S_B)$, with polynomial binary encoding overhead. Conversely a refutation of $\mathcal O(A,B)$ of depth $D$ and $S$ lines projects into refutations of $A$ and $B$, each with depth at most $D+4$, line count $O(S)$, and polynomial encoded-size overhead.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Adding each local split pair gives its original clause guarded by $g=s$ or $g=1-s$. Lift each source line $a\cdot x\ge b$ to $a\cdot x+kg\ge b$, with $k\ge0$. Source clauses start with $k=1$, Boolean bounds with $k=0$. Addition and scaling update $k$ normally. At source division by $d$, pad $k$ to $K=d\lceil k/d\rceil$ using $(K-k)g\ge0$, then divide by $d$. The case $g=1-s$ is correct because $\lceil(b-K)/d\rceil=\lceil b/d\rceil-K/d$. Thus every source inference adds at most two depth levels; initial guards cost one. At the final $kg\ge1$ ($k>0$), divide by $k$ to derive $g\ge1$. The two guards sum to contradiction. Two last steps give the conservative forward bound.

For backward projection to $A$, set $s=0$, each A-side $y=1-a$, each B-side $z=0$, and unused B variables to zero. To $B$, set $s=1$, each A-side $y=0$, each B-side $z=1-d$, and unused A variables to zero. Every projected shield axiom is either an original source clause or a Boolean-valid affine inequality on at most three source variables. For $a\cdot x\ge b$, Boolean validity means $b\le\sum_{a_i<0}a_i$. Scaled Boolean bounds and a multiple of $0\ge-1$ derive the required inequality in depth at most four. Affine substitution commutes with scaling and addition. For division, the induced constant shift $h$ is a multiple of $d$, yielding $\lceil(b-h)/d\rceil=\lceil b/d\rceil-h/d$. Every inference therefore projects to a legal integer CP inference. Initial repair depth is at most four, and the empty contradiction is unchanged. A bounded number of explicit lines is added per source or target line, with all binary coefficients charged.
\end{proof}
The theorem concerns \emph{syntactic} integer CP, not semantic CP where an arbitrary entailed inequality is admitted as one step. In particular, no semantic CP size-depth result is automatically inherited.

\section{Exact oracle obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[ORACLE-NO-FIXED-PROOF1]
Let $R$ be a fixed oracle-free refutation calculus with an unsatisfiable family $H_n$ requiring superpolynomial \emph{encoded} $R$ proof length. There is an oracle $O$ and a globally polynomial-time oracle SAT decider $A^O$ such that no extractor (even one with access to $O$) can output ordinary $R$ refutations of all $H_n$ of length $\operatorname{poly}(|H_n|+T_{A^O}(H_n))$. The oracle can satisfy $P^O=NP^O=PSPACE$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $O=TQBF$. Polynomially many polynomial-length TQBF queries can be answered in PSPACE, and every PSPACE language reduces to TQBF, so $P^O=PSPACE$. Also $NP^O\subseteq NPSPACE=PSPACE$, hence $NP^O=P^O$. SAT reduces in polynomial time to TQBF, giving a correct globally polynomial-time one-query oracle SAT decider. If the alleged extractor outputs short \emph{ordinary} $R$ proofs for the $H_n$, this directly contradicts their unconditional $R$ proof-size lower bound. The extractor's own oracle calls cannot change the required output calculus.
\end{proof}
For $R=$ Resolution, use the project's expander-Tseitin exact-(2,2) compiler family (original source counts $v=24n$, $M=32n$) with Resolution lower bound $2^{\Omega(v)}=2^{\Omega(L/\log L)}$ in binary input length $L$. This proves a precise \emph{relativizing} obstruction, not the impossibility of a nonrelativizing ordinary-machine bridge. If the output permits oracle axioms, it is no longer an ordinary Resolution proof and the lower bound need not apply. TRACEFRONT1 is a separate, complementary total non-oracle decider counterexample, but its decider is not globally polynomial.

\section{Weakest available solver landing}
\begin{proposition}
If a correct deterministic SAT decider $A$ runs in polynomial time on \emph{all} inputs, then UNSAT has a polynomially bounded \emph{$A$-dependent} Cook--Reckhow refutation system: a proof of $F$ is the explicit valid transcript of $A(F)$ ending UNSAT, checked by deterministic replay.
\end{proposition}
Verification is polynomial in transcript length and input length, and soundness rests on assumed correctness of $A$. Nothing implies that this variable proof system is polynomially simulated by Resolution, CP, PC/PCR, SoS, or a triangle DAG.

A second conditional landing is: if $L_{22}\in P$, then for every explicit $F(X,Y)$ its continuation function $c_F(x)=SAT(F|_{X=x})$ has a uniformly constructible polynomial-size \emph{unrestricted} Boolean circuit, by reducing the restricted formula to $L_{22}$ and unrolling the polynomial-time decider. This does not contradict a lower bound on d-DNNF, OBDD, Resolution or semantic CP. An unrestricted circuit lower bound for this explicit family would itself be a major missing theorem.
