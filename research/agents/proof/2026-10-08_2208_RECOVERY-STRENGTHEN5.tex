\section{PROVED/EXACT: phase-boundary normal form}
Fix a finite set of types.  A \emph{completed phase} begins with a typed SAT
instance $x$ of encoded length $N$, may execute a finite well-founded schedule
of internal typed operations, and ends either with the exact answer or with
ordinary SAT instances $y_1,\ldots,y_q$.  Internal operations may include
same-scale edges.  Every executed operation and child occurrence is counted;
deduplicating addresses does not deduplicate executions.

\begin{theorem}[Charged phase flattening]
Suppose one fixed uniform phase implementation works on every input and every
recursively reached phase entry.  Suppose fixed $h,p<1$ satisfy, at every
entry of length $N$,
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^{q}|y_j|\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 \max_j|y_j|\le N^{h+o(1)}. \tag{1}
\]
All internal discovery, failed attempts, certification, classification,
setup, child-bit generation, repeated access, nested executions, output,
replay and answer combination take $N^{1+o(1)}$ total time.  Peak
simultaneously live state, including all internal descriptors, buffers,
indexes, suspended frames, caches and ancestors, is $N^{p+o(1)}$ bits plus
one explicitly charged phase-exit child buffer.  Phase exit is exact and
strictly descending, with a fixed base case.

Then the phase can be treated as one macro WRAPPER call.  Recursive
substitution yields a deterministic SAT algorithm using
\[
 T(N)=N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 S(N)=N^{\max\{h,p\}+o(1)}. \tag{2}
\]
No strict shrinkage premise is required for an individual internal edge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Replace the entire completed phase by a macro node with its phase-exit
children.  Exactness is unchanged because all internal operations have
already completed.  Premise (1) bounds actual child volume, with
multiplicity.  At recursion level $d$, each node has size at most
$N^{h^d+o(1)}$ and the sum of node sizes is $N^{1+o(1)}$ by induction;
the $N^{o(1)}$ losses over $O(\log\log N)$ levels remain subpolynomial.
Charging the macro's complete local work therefore gives (2)'s time bound.
Depth-first evaluation keeps the declared peak phase state, one child and
the ancestor descriptors.  Geometric power shrinkage makes their total
$N^{\max\{h,p\}+o(1)}$.  Strict descent terminates.  None of this reasoning
inspects an internal edge separately.
\end{proof}

This is a sufficient normal form, not a necessity theorem.  It reconciles
the current aggregate-volume construction target with the original typed
cycle work: a double wrapper may use a recursive or same-scale inner phase
and a forward-only outer boundary, provided the \emph{whole phase} satisfies
the charged premises above.

\section{Scalar and typed recovery}
For the one-type RECURCLOSE1 recurrence with local exponent $r$, query-count
exponent $\eta$ and child exponent $\theta<1$,
\[
 K_{d+1}=\max\{r,\eta+\theta K_d\}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 K_\infty=\max\left\{r,{\eta\over1-\theta}\right\}. \tag{3}
\]
The starting exponent $K$ disappears.  In particular, the aggregate-volume
condition $\eta+\theta\le1$ implies
$\eta/(1-\theta)\le1$.  Equation (3) is the precise place where the old
``divide $2^k$ by $2^x$'' intuition belongs: it is an exponent-contraction
recurrence after an actual smaller SAT return has been constructed, not a
proof that such a return exists.

CYCLEGAIN1 supplies the typed analogue.  For example, an internal edge
$A(N)\to B(N)$ may have scale one, followed by
$B(N)\to A(N^\theta)$ with $\theta<1$.  The completed $A$-to-$A$ phase
contracts.  This route is valid only if the $B(N)$ representation and all
live state are implicit or otherwise sublinear.  Explicitly materializing a
same-scale child costs $\Omega(N)$ bits.  STREAMCOMP1 is therefore a
conditional interface theorem that states what must be charged; it does not
create the needed interface.

ORBITBATCH1 cannot fill that implementation gap.  Its invertibility identity
collapses the union of requested field addresses to one child domain, but it
does not collapse the number of consumers or executions.  It is also
normalized to $p^r$, not to original SAT encoded length, and supplies no
ordinary SAT child.  Address sharing may reduce setup and evaluation work;
it may not be substituted for the executed-volume bound in (1).

\section{All-input implication and surviving gap}
The newly audited guarded family from CONSTRUCTION-STRENGTHEN5 places a
linear, locally addressable copy of every proper 3-CNF inside the current
portfolio's remaining domain.  The phase theorem changes where a candidate
must be measured, but not what it must accomplish.  Applied to that family,
one completed phase must either decide the embedded source exactly or emit
strictly smaller ordinary SAT exits satisfying (1), with the entire internal
route charged.  Merely stripping the guard recovers a $\Theta(N)$ source.

Therefore the strongest recovered route is:
\[
 \boxed{\begin{minipage}{.84\linewidth}
 Construct one uniform all-input completed phase with exact ordinary-SAT
 exits, near-linear aggregate executed volume and work, a fixed-power
 largest exit, and sublinear peak live state.  Same-scale internal edges are
 allowed only inside the charged well-founded phase.
 \end{minipage}} \tag{4}
\]
No original or later reviewed record proves the antecedent of (4).  This is
the missing implementation, not missing recurrence algebra.
