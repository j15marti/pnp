\begin{proposition}[Degree certificate survives every affine stage]
For every field, every polynomial $F$ of total degree at most $\Delta$,
and every finite chain of compatible affine maps over that field, the
composite $F\circ E_1\circ\cdots\circ E_k$ has total degree at most
$\Delta$. If $d_k\le D<\Delta$, the dimensional inequality needed by
vMS Lemma 4.2 also persists. No additional polynomial evaluation is needed
to propagate this existing certificate.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Substituting affine forms into a monomial of total degree $a$ yields
only monomials of total degree at most $a$. Apply this to each monomial
and then to the composed affine map. Dimensions do not increase.
\end{proof}
In the original NORMBLOW normalization, a source with individual degrees
less than $h$ in $r$ variables has total degree at most $r(h-1)<hr=\Delta$.
Thus this certificate works for all same-field affine address stages,
including dense mixing. It is not necessary to multiply the degree budget
by the dimension at every recursive level. Changing the polynomial,
field or encoding requires a new certificate and its own resource ledger.

For supplied individual degree bounds $\delta_i$ and fused map $Mz+b$,
another safe certificate is
\[
 \deg_{z_j}F(Mz+b)\le\sum_{i:M_{ij}\ne0}\delta_i,
 \qquad \deg F(Mz+b)\le\deg F.
\]
The first statement follows monomial by monomial. Testing the support
uses $O(Dd)$ coefficient inspections and additions on $O(\log(1+\sum
\delta_i))$-bit integers, in addition to the already charged matrix
construction/storage. Cancellation in the fused matrix can sharpen this
certificate. None of this verifies an untrusted initial degree claim
from black-box oracle access.

\begin{proposition}[Restriction entropy is a separate exact charge]
Let $p>\Delta$, and let $E:\mathbb F_p^d\to\mathbb F_p^D$ be an
injective affine map. On total-degree-$\Delta$ polynomial coefficient
spaces, restriction $R_E:F\mapsto F\circ E$ is a surjective linear map
with kernel dimension
\[
 q=\binom{D+\Delta}{D}-\binom{d+\Delta}{d}.
\]
For every random polynomial $X$ in the domain,
\[
 H_\infty(R_E X)\ge\max\{0,H_\infty(X)-q\log_2p\}.
\]
For invertible square affine maps this loss is zero. For proper
restrictions it can be complete.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
An affine left inverse $L$ of $E$ exists. For any target polynomial $G$,
$G\circ L$ is an allowed-degree preimage, proving surjectivity. The
dimensions of these polynomial spaces count monomials; $p>\Delta$
ensures the polynomial functions uniquely determine their coefficients.
Rank-nullity gives $q$, and every fiber has $p^q$ elements. Summing the
maximum input probability over a fiber proves the min-entropy bound.
The uniform distribution on the kernel has entropy $q\log_2p$ and
constant restricted output, proving that complete loss is possible.
\end{proof}
This does not assert that the active RMV analysis improperly assumes
entropy preservation. It supplies the check required by any proposed
recursive source interpretation. Merely keeping the output inside the
eligible polynomial class does not meet the extractor's entropy premise.

\paragraph{Smallest odd-field degree counterexample.}
Over $\mathbb F_3$, let $F(x,y)=xy$ and $E(u,v)=(u+v,v)$.
Then $F\circ E=uv+v^2$, which is not individually multilinear, even as
a function. Its canonical degree in $v$ is two, below three. Total degree
remains two. One variable cannot give this counterexample, and three is
the smallest odd prime. This refutes unchanged individual degree, not
the total-degree certificate or vMS's extractor.

\begin{proposition}[The monomial repair conflicts with full orbit]
Over any odd prime field, an invertible linear map preserving the
multilinear polynomial class for every input polynomial is monomial
(one nonzero entry per row and column). For $D\ge2$, no invertible
monomial matrix over $\mathbb F_p$ has order $p^D-1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If two different rows share a nonzero entry in column $j$, the product
of those two coordinate functions contains a nonzero $z_j^2$ term.
Thus each column has at most one nonzero; invertibility forces exactly
one in each row/column. Conversely such maps only scale and permute
variables. For cycle lengths $l_1,\ldots,l_a$ of its permutation part,
the matrix order divides $(p-1)\operatorname{lcm}(l_1,\ldots,l_a)$.
Since $l\le2^{l-1}$ for positive integers $l$,
\[
 \operatorname{ord}(M)\le(p-1)\prod_i l_i
 \le(p-1)2^{D-a}\le(p-1)2^{D-1}<p^D-1.
\]
\end{proof}
This applies to a hypothetical requirement of a full nonzero-vector orbit
(or a field-multiplication generator of order $p^D-1$). It is not claimed
as the source-identified vMS matrix requirement. In particular ORBITBATCH1's
all-seed address-union identity needs only invertibility, so monomial maps
remain adequate for that identity. The right repair here is
the existing total-degree invariant, not restricting every orbit matrix
to be monomial. The characteristic-two functional-multilinearity
exception is deliberate and must not be silently transferred.
