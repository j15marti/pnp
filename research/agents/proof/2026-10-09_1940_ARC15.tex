\paragraph{Sound positive repair.}
For every CNF F and partial assignment rho, a witness satisfying
F restricted by rho can be lifted with rho and forced assignments.
The candidate is checked against every clause of the original F.
Only successful verification permits immediate SAT.
No complete cover is needed to establish existence of one model.
UNSAT still requires a complete source-valid cover with every branch UNSAT.
The original method sometimes discarded a discovered positive branch
because a later child was outside its terminal portfolio.
The harness implements early exit only after full-source witness validation.
This is a standard logical consequence applied to a concrete implementation,
not a new SAT complexity theorem. The original solver file is not overwritten.
