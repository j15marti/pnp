\paragraph{Verdict.}
The proposed embedding is correct with two qualifications.  It is a
source-checkable, linear-size, locally addressable reduction into the
\emph{failure set} of the current partial portfolio, not a favorable all-input
wrapper and not a complexity lower bound.  The local-access conclusion also
requires an explicit fixed-field encoding whose header exposes the source
values $n$ and $m$.  No counterexample was found.  The two additional guard
blocks in the proposal are sufficient but redundant.

\section{Exact statement audited}
Let $F$ be a proper, exactly-width-three CNF over the densely occurring
variables $[n]$; hence a nonempty instance has $n\geq 3$.  Allocate fresh
variables
\[
 h,r,a,b,c,d,w_1,\ldots,w_n.
\]
Let $\Pzero(x,y,z)$ denote the four proper clauses whose satisfying
assignments are exactly $x\oplus y\oplus z=0$.  Define
\[
 H(F)=F\wedge(h\vee\neg r)\wedge(\neg h\vee r)
 \wedge\Pzero(h,a,b)\wedge\Pzero(h,c,d)
 \wedge\bigwedge_{v=1}^{n}\Pzero(h,v,w_v).
\]
Before duplicate elimination, $H(F)$ has exactly $2n+6$ variables and
$m+4n+10$ clause occurrences.  If source clauses are distinct, these are also
the normalized counts.

\paragraph{Equisatisfiability.}
Every satisfying assignment of $H(F)$ restricts to one of $F$.  Conversely,
extend a satisfying assignment of $F$ by setting $h=r=0$, $w_v=v$ for every
$v$, and $a=b$, $c=d$.  Thus
\[
 \SAT(H(F))\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\SAT(F).
\]

\paragraph{The cheap prefix is inert.}
Every variable occurs with both signs in the displayed construction, and all
clauses have width two or three.  Therefore there is initially neither a unit
nor a pure literal.  Exhaustive unit propagation and pure-literal elimination
return the formula unchanged after one complete scan.

\paragraph{Current recognizers all fail.}
The two equivalence clauses make the ARC2 parity-star recognizer return
\textsc{unknown}, since it requires every clause to be proper width three.
The formula is nonconstant, non-Horn, and not 2-CNF.  Its primal graph is
connected through $h$.

It remains to exclude a strong Horn backdoor $B$ of size at most two under the
\emph{operational} restriction procedure, which immediately replaces the
whole residual by the single empty clause if some clause is falsified.  The
positive-pair graph of $\Pzero(h,a,b)$ is the triangle on $\{h,a,b\}$, and the
second guard gives a triangle on $\{h,c,d\}$.  Their union has vertex-cover
number three.  If $B\neq\{h,r\}$, choose a positive pair missed by $B$ and the
corresponding parity clause.  If its third literal is assigned, choose its
value to falsify that literal.  The residual clause still has two positive
literals.  No width-three clause can be emptied by at most two assigned
variables, and neither binary equivalence clause can be emptied because
$B$ does not contain both $h,r$.  Hence the operational absorbing-empty rule
does not intervene.  If $B=\{h,r\}$, the assignment $h=r=1$ satisfies both
binary clauses and leaves $(a\vee b)$ from $(-h\vee a\vee b)$.  Thus every
$|B|\leq2$ has a non-Horn restriction.

\section{Tightening and resource qualification}
The two guard blocks may be deleted.  Because $n\geq3$, the per-source-variable
blocks already contain at least two positive-pair triangles sharing $h$, and
the same proof excludes every backdoor of size at most two.  The tightened
map uses $2n+2$ variables and $m+4n+2$ clause occurrences.  The test checked
both forms.  Keeping the guards is harmless and makes the symbolic proof
independent of the source blocks.

For a canonical encoding with an explicit $(n,m)$ header, a fixed number of
bits per clause, and fixed-width literal identifiers, a requested output bit
can be produced as follows: identify its clause block arithmetically; decode
and re-encode one source literal if it lies in the copied prefix; otherwise
compute one of the displayed fresh labels and signs.  This uses
$\operatorname{polylog}N$ time and $O(\log N)$ workspace on a random-access
bit model.  Since dense occurrence gives $n\leq3m$ and
$\log(2n+6)=\log n+O(1)$, the output bit length is $O(N)$.  Without an explicit
header/fixed-field convention, finding $n,m$ or clause boundaries can require
a source scan, so the polylogarithmic local-access statement must not be made
encoding-free.

There is a second, separate accounting caveat.  On the embedded family the
unit/pure prefix stops after one scan and needs only counters.  A general
exhaustive implementation that retains all derived assignments naively uses
$\Theta(n)$ bits.  This audit does not prove an $O(\log N)$-workspace
implementation of that prefix on arbitrary inputs.  Any global same-machine
claim must charge its assignment state or supply a recomputation theorem.

\section{Bounded falsification}
The independent runner authenticated the pre-existing portfolio by SHA-256
and enumerated all 255 nonempty formulas over the eight proper clauses on the
triple $\{1,2,3\}$.  Both the stated and tightened embeddings were checked.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
check & count\\
\midrule
source formulas & 255\\
semantic equivalence checks & 510\\
unit/pure fixed-point checks & 510\\
exact old-portfolio failures & 4\\
strong-Horn backdoor search & all subsets of size $\leq2$ on representatives\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The special restriction $B=\{h,r\}$, $h=r=1$ was checked explicitly against
the absorbing-empty implementation.  The runner finished in under one second,
well inside its 25-second alarm.

\section{Proof status and handoff}
This result strengthens the negative coverage diagnosis: even after adding
the ARC2 parity-star solver and an inert unit/pure prefix, the exact failure
domain admits a linear local reduction from every proper width-three SAT
instance.  It therefore remains NP-hard, assuming the standard NP-hardness of
proper 3-SAT.  This statement supplies neither an unconditional lower bound
for the portfolio nor the missing implication from $\SAT\in P$ to a jointly
favorable all-input time--space wrapper.  P versus NP remains open.

The next construction target should operate on this embedded residual family,
with the cost of recognizing, acquiring, replaying, and executing all children
charged with multiplicity.  Merely adding more exact partial recognizers does
not establish the required all-input transition.
