\section{A type-correct nested guard}
For a nonempty proper 3CNF $F$ with attained maximum $q$, $m$ clause
occurrences and $L=q+m\ge4$, ARC5's guard is
\[
\begin{split}
G(F)=F&\wedge(h\vee\neg r)\wedge(\neg h\vee r)\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{v=1}^{q}P_0(h,v,q+2+v)
 \wedge\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}P_0(h,2q+2j+1,2q+2j+2),
\end{split}\tag{4}
\]
where $h=q+1,r=q+2$ and $P_0$ is the ordered four-clause even-parity
relation.  The tempting $G(G(F))$ is ill-typed: the outer recognizer requires
its first records to be proper ternary clauses, whereas the inner copy has
the two displayed binary records.

Let $C_+=(h\vee\neg r)$ and $C_-=(\neg h\vee r)$.  Define
$\widehat G(F)$ by replacing these two records in (4) with
\[
 (C_+\vee z_+)\wedge(C_+\vee\neg z_+),\qquad
 (C_-\vee z_-)\wedge(C_-\vee\neg z_-),                  \tag{5}
\]
using two fresh consecutive identifiers, and leaving every source and parity
record unchanged.  Finally define the type-correct nested input
\[
                         X(F)=G(\widehat G(F)).           \tag{6}
\]
Every clause of $\widehat G(F)$ is proper ternary.  Each pair in (5)
existentially projects to its old binary clause, so
\[
 \SAT(X(F))=\SAT(\widehat G(F))=\SAT(G(F))=\SAT(F).       \tag{7}
\]
Both transformations have quasilinear encoded size and polylogarithmic
local random access.  Their composition retains those bounds and requires no
materialized near-parent buffer.

\begin{lemma}[universal PIVOT and pure failure]\label{lem:fail}
On the virtual source $H=\widehat G(F)$, exhaustive pure-literal elimination
returns UNKNOWN, and every completed PIVOT-FACTOR1 candidate exceeds its
maximum-child cap.  Any charged acquisition abort also returns UNKNOWN and
executes no child.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All old guard variables occur with both signs; $z_+$ and $z_-$ each occur
once in each sign.  Hence pure elimination has no first move.

The source $H$ has
\[
 q_H=2L+4,qquad c_H=4L+m+4\le5L+4.                      \tag{8}
\]
If a pivot $z\ne h$ is used, at most one of the $L$ parity blocks is damaged;
the other $L-1$ blocks remain verbatim in one primal component through $h$.
That component contains at least $4(L-1)$ proper ternary records and hence at
least $128(L-1)$ encoded bits.  This includes pivots $z_+,z_-$, which damage
no parity block.  If $z=h$, resolving the two positive-$h$ lifted equality
clauses against the two negative-$h$ clauses of each parity block, and the
two negative-$h$ equality clauses against the two positive-$h$ clauses,
produces eight distinct non-tautological width-four clauses per block.  They
remain connected through $r$ and give a still larger residual.  The current
member deletes tautologies and exact duplicates only.

Put
\[
 \ell=1+\lfloor\log_{128}(5L+4)\rfloor,
 \qquad U_H(L)=8[5L+8+(15L+14)\ell].                    \tag{9}
\]
Canonical serialization and (8) give $|H|\le U_H(L)$.  Also
$\ell\le\log_2L$ for $L\ge4$.  For $L=4,5$, (9) gives respectively
$U_H=816,976$, and direct exact substitution yields
\[
 \sqrt{U_H}\log_2(U_H+2)<277,311<384,512=128(L-1).
\]
For $L\ge6$,
\[
 U_H(L)\le160L\log_2L.
\]
ARC5's proved decreasing ratio
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{160L\log_2L}\,
  \log_2(161L\log_2L)}{128(L-1)}<1
\]
therefore applies.  In all cases the retained component exceeds
$\lfloor\sqrt{|H|}\log_2(|H|+2)\rfloor$.  No PIVOT plan is certified.
\end{proof}

introduce private $y_{jk},z_{jk},w_{jk}$, replace the occurrence by the

\[
 (\neg x\vee y_{jk}\vee\pm z_{jk}),\qquad
 (x\vee\neg y_{jk}\vee\pm w_{jk}).                       \tag{10}
\]
Call the resulting proper formula $\mathsf{Iso}(F)$.  It has $13m$ clauses,

\[

\section{Endpoint, literature and the remaining implication}
If one proved universal root and successor coverage for the controller in
Theorem~\ref{thm:promise}, (1) would be an all-SAT point.  The verified
primary endpoint is Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, Corollary 5.8,
for deterministic random-access Turing machines with read-only indexed input
and charged work/index tapes:
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
\operatorname{DTISP}[n^c,o(n^{d(c)})],\qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).             \tag{13}
\]
At $c=1201/1000$, the time slack above $6/5$ is $1/1000$ and the
space slack above $1/2$ is $26275399/2884802000$.  Thus one actual joint
all-SAT witness at $(6/5,1/2)$ would enter the forbidden region with fixed
finite margin.  Theorem~\ref{thm:promise} does not meet its all-input premise.
Primary source: \url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/tst.pdf}.

E\'en--Biere bounded variable elimination is the exact classical operation
modeled by PIVOT; its practical rule applies elimination only under a growth
bound and does not prove a universally favorable trace
(DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/11499107_5}{10.1007/11499107\_5}).
This agrees with Lemma~\ref{lem:fail}: exact one-variable projection can
leave a near-parent connected residual even after a useful wrapper alias.
No primary source found supplies the missing all-input source-visible
compiler from an arbitrary clocked polynomial SAT decider.
