\section{What these results allow us to stop demanding}
\begin{description}
\item[] We do not need an infinite sequence of separate compiler transformations
$A\mapsto A'\mapsto A''$. One fixed recursively closed reduction suffices.
\item[] We do not need every individual typed call to shrink. Bounded same-scale
phases can be absorbed before the next fractional return.
\item[] We do not need a materialized same-scale child if a charged exact
implicit-access realization exists.
\item[] We do not need independent acquisition of a common parent for every
consumer. Shared retained state or charged recomputation is permitted.
\item[] We do not need to multiply classification by setup merely because both
occur. Their actual total costs are summed, so their exponents combine by a
maximum when separately bounded.
\end{description}
These are real weakenings of unnecessarily restrictive implementation targets.
They do not weaken the need for one uniform all-input implementation satisfying
simultaneous resource bounds.

\section{A precise reusable macro-step corollary}
The following specializes the already recovered typed-cycle and implicit-access
ideas to the FORMAL-SPINE1 endpoint, without assuming a new SAT construction.

\begin{lemma}[Bounded-phase flattening, conditional]
Fix $0<h<1$ and $0\le p<1$. Suppose a uniform exact recursive SAT procedure has
distinguished entry states. Between an entry on $N$ encoded bits and the next
recursive entries, it may run other exact typed procedures, including
same-scale ones. Suppose on every consistent execution:
\begin{description}
\item[] The complete intervening phase terminates; same-scale typed chains have
a fixed bound independent of $N$.
\item[] At most $N^{1-h+o(1)}$ recursive entry calls are made, each of full
encoded length at most $N^{h+o(1)}$.
\item[] The entire phase, outside recursive entry evaluation, uses
$N^{1+o(1)}$ time. This includes every input-generation call, all nested ancestor
access, failed attempts, classification, shared setup and recomputation.
\item[] At suspension, the whole phase retains $N^{p+o(1)}$ bits besides one
$N^{h+o(1)}$ child buffer. All typed frames, evaluators and scratch satisfy that
same simultaneous bound. The current derived entry input is charged to its
caller.
\item[] The same uniform error bounds, source semantics and base procedure apply
at every entry. Explicit child evaluation is sequential.
\end{description}
Then one recursive implementation has
\[
T(N)\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
S(N)\le N^{\max(p,h)+o(1)}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Define one wrapper invocation to be exactly the intervening phase, exposing
only the recursive entry calls as its children. Its exactness follows from
exact typed composition and source-matched child answers. The hypotheses
provide its full local time, child count, child lengths and retained workspace.
Thus it satisfies the contract in FORMAL-SPINE1. Recursive substitution and
depth-first summation give the displayed bounds. This is a change of the
analysis boundary of the same implementation, not a replacement of semantic
calls by a free oracle. In particular, internal same-scale buffers cannot be
discarded from the space accounting by calling them internal.
\end{proof}

An instructive sufficient two-type pattern is
\[
P\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow P.
\]
The first channel may have subpolynomial multiplicity and nonexpanding
same-scale inputs; the second has multiplicity at most
$N^{1-h+o(1)}$ and child lengths at most $N^{h+o(1)}$, in the original entry
normalization. If the combined local phase and simultaneous space satisfy
the lemma, the same-scale edge is harmless. This is a conditional design
pattern, not an implemented general-SAT algorithm. If $Q$'s logical input has
length $\Theta(N)$, materializing it violates the stated sublinear local-space
premise; implicit access must meet the actual aggregate time budget.

\section{Quantitative compatibility, not just valid separate ledgers}
The recovered STREAMCOMP1 sufficient edge inequalities are
\[
 C_i\ge \eta_e+g_e+\theta_e C_j,\qquad
 \rho_i\ge\max(s_e,\theta_e\rho_j),
\]
together with local floors. On a cycle, $J/(1-\Theta)$ bounds the needed
time exponent, where $J$ is the scale-weighted sum of multiplicity and access
exponents. Therefore $K$ can disappear while the resulting exponent is still
too large for the endpoint. Likewise a contracting time recurrence cannot
reduce an unavoidable local space floor.

These inequalities must describe the actual recursively composed access
mechanism. A per-bit bound proved only with free random access to a materialized
parent cannot be reused unchanged when that parent is itself implicit.
A safe implementation must prove total nested access, or replace it with a
separate recurrence and charge its suspended frames.

ORBITBATCH1 supplies the equally important normalization rule. If a source
domain has length $M=N^\lambda$, a child of source length $M^b$ has SAT-normalized
length $N^{\lambda b}$. The relevant contraction is $\lambda b<1$, not $b<1$.
Likewise $M^c$ source work becomes $N^{\lambda c}$ before other costs. This rule
does not assert that $\lambda$ is constant in an arbitrary source construction;
the actual size map must first be proved.

\section{Two recovered statements that cannot close the bridge}
RECURCLOSE1's original semantic-selector lemma is sound at its stated
algorithmic scope: a polynomial-size signature range and a polynomial-time
selector preserving the OR of colliding residuals yield a polynomial-size
self-reduction frontier, hence polynomial-time SAT. Under an assumption
$P=NP$, that conclusion is no contradiction and supplies no small-space or
near-linear compiler.

A stale endpoint occurs repeatedly in that original report:
$C+\rho<2\cos(\pi/7)$ is used as a generic forbidden-region condition. This
audit does not import that unrestricted tradeoff. The original recurrence and
space composition are retained independently of that endpoint shorthand.
Use the model-matched near-linear-time/sublinear-space endpoint already
declared in FORMAL-SPINE1; any alternative quantitative endpoint requires
its own checked theorem and parameter range.

The original Williams self-improvement paper was also checked directly.
Its Section 1.1 explicitly treats extension into the polynomial-time regime
as an open direction. Its concrete circuit-size self-improvement results do
not supply this project's missing simultaneous SAT time-space compiler.

oracle replacement. It provides no nonrelativizing hinge and invokes no

Demonstrating those bounds on all inputs would supply the missing bridge;
merely assigning favorable numerical exponents would not.
