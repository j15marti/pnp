\section{The all-input obligation}
The current strongest sufficient construction may be stated without a
balanced-fanout requirement. There must be fixed $h,p<1$ and one uniform
exact procedure such that, on \emph{every} encoded SAT input of length
$N$ and every recursively reached state, it either decides exactly or
executes smaller SAT children of full encoded lengths $m_i$ satisfying
\[
 \sum_i m_i\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 \max_i m_i\le N^{h+o(1)}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Repeated executions count repeatedly. Discovery, all failed attempts,
certification, writing or locally generating the children, replay,
answer combination and all other local work must total $N^{1+o(1)}$.
Sequential recursion must retain only $N^{p+o(1)}$ bits in addition to
one charged child buffer, including indexes, suspended frames, descriptors,
buffers, caches and ancestor state. The same implementation and closure
must work at every descendant, with strict size descent and a fixed base
procedure.

Construction2 and RedTeam1 prove that these premises compose to one
$N^{1+o(1)}$-time, $N^{\max\{h,p\}+o(1)}$-space SAT machine. The
aggregate-volume relaxation admits uneven children, but it does not make
acquisition or the unresolved domain free. The alternative ARC route may
instead construct a finite sequence of actual joint all-input machines
entering the verified FLMV forbidden region. A partial solver followed
by an unchanged general fallback does neither.

This report asks whether the current partial portfolio can become
all-input merely by prepending two more standard rules: exhaustive unit
propagation and pure-literal elimination. The answer is no in a precise,
source-checkable sense. We do not claim that an arbitrary exhaustive
implementation of those rules uses logarithmic workspace: on the image
below both rules are inert after the first inspection, so the limitation
holds even if the prefix is granted for free.

\section{Authenticated portfolio and robust construction}
The audited code is the exact Git blob
\nolinkurl{51f0a58467370a04284aee4c1cccfd97a4a61f91} at commit
\nolinkurl{e1082f783dd7f237afe8d92bb63dad291afa1aba}. It contains constants,
Horn, 2-CNF, disconnected-component factoring and strong Horn backdoors
of size at most two. ARC2 prepends a scanner for complete parity stars.
Its scanner returns UNKNOWN immediately on a non-width-three clause.
The original restriction routine collapses a residual containing any
empty clause to the singleton empty clause. These executable details are
part of the theorem.

Let $P_0(u,v,w)$ denote the four proper width-three clauses equivalent to
$u\oplus v\oplus w=0$. For every proper exactly-width-three CNF $F$ on
its occurring, densely named variables $[n]$, where $n\geq3$, introduce
distinct fresh variables $h,r,w_1,\ldots,w_n$ and define
\[
 H(F)=F\wedge(h\vee\neg r)\wedge(\neg h\vee r)
 \wedge\bigwedge_{v=1}^{n}P_0(h,v,w_v).
 \tag{2}
\]
Unlike the earlier connector guard, every variable in (2) occurs with
both signs. There are no unit clauses. Thus exhaustive unit propagation
and pure-literal elimination leave (2) unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Robust embedding into the remaining domain]
For every $F$ above,
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(H(F))=\operatorname{SAT}(F),
\]
and the unit/pure-extended ARC2 portfolio returns UNKNOWN/FAIL on $H(F)$.
The output has $2n+2$ variables and $m+4n+2$ clause occurrences when $F$
has $m$ clause occurrences. Under the declared dense fixed-width binary encoding,
$|H(F)|=\Theta(|F|)$, and every requested output bit can be produced
uniformly in $\operatorname{polylog}|F|$ time and $O(\log|F|)$ workspace.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every satisfying assignment of $H(F)$ satisfies the subformula $F$.
Conversely, extend any satisfying assignment of $F$ by $h=r=0$
and $w_v=F(v)$ for each source variable. Every parity
equation and both equivalence clauses are satisfied.

The two binary clauses force the ARC2 scanner to return UNKNOWN. The
formula is nonconstant and not 2-CNF. Every source variable is joined
to $h$ by a parity block, so the primal graph is connected. It is
non-Horn.

It remains to exclude the executable strong-Horn-backdoor member.
Let $B$ contain at most two variables. If $B\ne\{h,r\}$, no assignment
to $B$ can empty a clause: every clause except the equivalence pair has
three distinct variables, and neither binary clause is fully assigned.
The positive-pair obstruction graph of the $n$ connector blocks contains
$n$ triangles sharing $h$, whose minimum vertex cover has size $n+1>2$.
Hence some positive pair is disjoint from $B$. Select its connector
clause and, if its third variable is assigned, choose the value falsifying
that third literal. The residual retains two positive literals and is
not Horn. If $B=\{h,r\}$, choose $h=r=1$. The equivalence clauses are
satisfied while each $P_0(h,v,w_v)$ retains $(v\vee w_v)$. Thus every candidate
$B$ has an assignment producing a non-Horn residual, so the size-two
member fails even with the empty-clause shortcut.

No literal is initially pure: every variable in a parity block has both
signs, and $h,r$ also have both signs in the equivalence pair. All clauses
have width two or three. Therefore the new propagation prefix does
nothing and every old member fails as claimed.

For the resource statement, every source variable occurs, so $n\le3m$.
With a self-delimiting $(n,m)$ header, two-bit arity and signed
$\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$-bit identifiers, the source length is
$\Theta(m\log(n+1))$ and (2) has linear encoded length. Its layout is
the copied source block, two fixed binary records, then four parity
records for each $v=1,\ldots,n$.
Given an output address, fixed-width record arithmetic identifies its
block. A copied payload bit maps to one original input bit, padding or
header arithmetic; a gadget bit follows from counters and fixed signs.
Schoolbook address arithmetic gives the stated local-access bounds.
The generator retains no output array or renaming table.
\end{proof}

The dense-occurring-variable premise is load-bearing. Arbitrarily long
identifiers or a declared universe of unused variables require a separately
charged canonical renaming pass. Such a pass is available in polynomial
time but is not silently inserted into this bit-level theorem.

\section{Why this answers ``what is needed for all input''}
Let $U$ be the domain on which the extended portfolio reaches its fallback.
Suppose one fixed uniform fallback machine is correct on all of $U$ and
has simultaneous bounds $T_U(N),S_U(N)$. It need not recognize membership
in $U$. On arbitrary proper 3-CNF $F$, simulate that machine on the virtual
read-only input $H(F)$. Each input-bit request, including repetitions, is
generated and charged. For a fixed $C$ and monotone envelopes,
\[
 T_{3\mathrm{SAT}}(N)
 \le \operatorname{polylog}N\,(1+T_U(CN)),\qquad
 S_{3\mathrm{SAT}}(N)\le S_U(CN)+O(\log N).
 \tag{3}
\]
This is one same-machine simulation, not separate time and space witnesses.
Thus a favorable theorem about the full remaining domain already yields
a favorable theorem for general proper 3-SAT. The fallback is not a thin
exceptional class.

Equation (3) does not prove a lower bound: under $P=NP$ the fallback may
indeed be polynomial. It proves that adding the tested cheap prefix has
not created the missing contraction. Even a preprocessor that recognizes
and strips the guard only recovers $F$ of size $\Theta(|H(F)|)$, not a
child of size $|H(F)|^{h+o(1)}$ for fixed $h<1$. Constant-factor shrinkage
does not meet (1).

Consequently, an all-input proof now needs one of two substantive objects:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a source-checkable constructor that acts on this general core and,
on every input, proves the aggregate child-volume, fixed-power maximum-size,
near-linear local-time and sublinear retained-space bounds in (1); or
\item an actual all-input joint time-space transition with strict margins
that enters the primary-source-verified FLMV forbidden region in finitely
many stages.
\end{enumerate}
Expanding a finite list of recognizable easy families is useful engineering,
but is not by itself either object.

\section{Primary literature and endpoint guard}
Nishimura, Ragde and Szeider define restrictions and strong Horn backdoors
and distinguish Horn, binary and pure-literal notions. Their length is
literal-occurrence count, not the encoded bit length used above. The
positive-pair proof for (2) is supplied here rather than inferred from a
runtime theorem:
\url{https://www.satisfiability.org/SAT04/programme/51.pdf}.

Biere, J\"arvisalo and Kiesl survey unit propagation, pure-literal
elimination and component detection as classical equisatisfiable
preprocessing. Their survey supports the algorithmic meaning of the two
added rules, but it does not state that these rules cover general SAT or
improve worst-case exponents:
\url{https://cca.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/BiereJarvisaloKiesl-SAT-Handbook-2021-Preprocessing-Chapter-Manuscript.pdf}.

The endpoint was freshly checked in Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas,
Section 2.1, Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 5.8. On their deterministic
random-access model, strict SAT exponents $t<c$ and
\[
 s<d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right)
\]
are forbidden after absorbing the local reduction's polylogarithmic
overhead. No unrestricted $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint is used. The
construction above does not enter this region:
\url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/tst.pdf}.
