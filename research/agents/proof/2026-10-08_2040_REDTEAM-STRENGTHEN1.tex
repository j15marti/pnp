\section{A weaker sufficient volume contract}
Retain every exactness, closure, local-time, uniformity, input-model,
resumption, and simultaneous-space requirement of FORMAL-SPINE1.
Replace the child-count requirement by the following constraints on the
actual list of child evaluations made at a node of length $N$:
\[
 m_i\ge1,\qquad \sum_i m_i\le N^{1+e(N)},\qquad
 \max_i m_i\le N^{h+e(N)}.
\]
An empty list represents direct evaluation; its sum and maximum may be
taken as zero. A repeated query is counted again when it is evaluated
again. Merely identifying two mathematical DAG nodes does not identify
two executions or remove storage and replay costs.

\begin{theorem}[Aggregate-volume composition]
Under this replacement and the retained contract, the same fixed
recursive decider satisfies
\[
 T(N)\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 S(N)\le N^{\max\{p,h\}+o(1)}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\delta>0$. Choose $\epsilon>0$ with $h+\epsilon<1$,
$\epsilon<\delta$, and
$\epsilon(1+\delta)<\delta(1-h)$. Above a fixed threshold all error
terms are bounded by $\epsilon$. Strong induction on the input length,
using $T(m)\le C m^{1+\delta}$, gives
\[
 \sum_i T(m_i)
 \le C (\max_i m_i)^\delta\sum_i m_i
 \le C N^{1+h\delta+\epsilon(1+\delta)}.
\]
This exponent is strictly smaller than $1+\delta$. The local-time term
has exponent $1+\epsilon<1+\delta$. For sufficiently large $N$, their
sum is at most $C N^{1+\delta}$; increasing $C$ covers the finite
remaining inputs. The algorithm is independent of $\delta$.

Space is unaffected by replacing the fanout bound: only one child is
evaluated at a time and all state retained across child answers remains
subject to the original simultaneous-space contract. Put
$s=\max(p,h)<1$. At each path node, suspension plus child buffer is
$O(N^{s+\epsilon}+\log N)$. Power shrinkage gives
$O(\log\log N)$ levels above the threshold, hence total space
$O(N^{s+\epsilon}\log\log N)=N^{s+o(1)}$ as $\epsilon$ is arbitrary.
\end{proof}

The scalar contract implies aggregate volume with error $2e(N)$.
The converse need not hold for fixed $h$: $N$ children of constant
positive size have linear total volume but violate the scalar fanout
bound $N^{1-h+o(1)}$. This is a strict relaxation of sufficient
scheduling constraints, not a construction of such a SAT wrapper.

\section{Failure controls and required qualifications}
\begin{description}
\item[] \emph{Without maximum-size contraction.} A single child of size
$N-1$ has linear volume but, with local work $N$, gives a quadratic
recurrence. Aggregate mass alone does not imply near-linear time.
\item[] \emph{Without charged local work.} One may pay arbitrarily much
before producing a small child. Child sizes do not bound acquisition.
\item[] \emph{Without retained-space control.} Materializing the whole
child list can require linear space despite a sublinear maximum child.
Sequential evaluation does not itself guarantee sequential generation.
\item[] \emph{Without fixed uniformity.} A different machine for each
accuracy parameter does not imply a single near-linear machine.
\item[] \emph{Without multiplicity.} Counting only distinct formulas
misses repeated calls unless an actual charged cache or reuse schedule
supplies the deduction.
\item[] \emph{Without full encodings.} A short identifier into a large
stored parent is not automatically a short independently evaluable SAT
instance. Access costs and the referenced state must be charged.
\end{description}
