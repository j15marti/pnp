\paragraph{Result.}
The exact-byte claim in ARC-STRENGTHEN4 is \textsc{Falsified as written}:
its zig-zag polarity is the reverse of the convention used by the extant
Construction and Recovery artifacts, and its empty-formula header need not be
an attained maximum.  The resource idea survives.  This report gives a
source-checkable repaired dense-CNF1 syntax, an independent total parser, a
corrected fixed-layout adapter, and a fixed-block local Tseitin composition.
Together with the classical local-reduction theorem recorded explicitly by
Jahanjou--Miles--Viola, this discharges the previously isolated Cook-stream
endpoint obligation.  It does \emph{not} construct the all-input favorable
WRAPPER phase.  Consequently $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textsc{Open}, and
conditional elimination of the starting exponent $K$ remains conditional.
The retired unrestricted $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint is not used.

\section{The smallest exact failure}

The repository's Construction/Recovery encoder uses
\[
 \operatorname{zz}(+u)=2u-1,\qquad
 \operatorname{zz}(-u)=2u,                                 \tag{1}
\]
whereas ARC-STRENGTHEN4 equation (6) uses the opposite parity.  Decoding its
stream under (1) complements every literal.  SAT is preserved by globally
complementing every assignment, but the claimed sign preservation, identical
assignment map, canonical bytes, and interoperability are false.  At ULEB
boundaries such as $u=64$ and $u=8192$, the two signs can even have different
byte lengths.  The old test compared its serializer only with a locator using
the same erroneous convention, so it could not expose the conflict.

There is a second independent edge case.  For an empty source with nominal
$q=1$, the old construction emits a maximum-variable header $A+q=17$, while
no literal attains it.  The existing tuple encoder emits maximum zero.  Thus
``largest variable identifier'' and ``upper bound'' were silently conflated.

\section{A total dense CNF1 language}

We now fix the exact syntax used by the endpoint bridge.  A byte string of
length $N$ is in \texttt{DENSE-CNF1} precisely when it has magic
\texttt{CNF1}; every integer is in shortest canonical ULEB128; the header is
$(q,C)$; $q\le128N$, $C\le N$, and every width is at most $N$; every literal
code is nonzero and decodes by (1) to an identifier at most $q$; the maximum
identifier occurring is exactly $q$ (hence $q=0$ for the empty formula); and
the last literal ends exactly at EOF.  Invalid strings denote NO.  Duplicate
literals and tautological clauses are allowed.

\begin{lemma}[total parser]\label{lem:parser}
\texttt{DENSE-CNF1} is recognized in $N\log^{O(1)}N$ time and $O(\log N)$
workspace on the charged random-access model.  On a valid string, its CNF can
be streamed in the same bounds.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Decode each ULEB once while rejecting a nonterminal truncation, a redundant
last zero group, or a value as soon as it exceeds the relevant cap.  All live
values have $O(\log N)$ bits.  Maintain input position, clause and occurrence
counters, and the maximum identifier seen.  Reject a literal outside the
header, trailing data, or a final maximum unequal to the header.  The sum of
consumed token lengths is $N$; standard $O(\log N)$-bit arithmetic contributes
only a polylogarithmic factor.  No formula array is stored.
\end{proof}

The density restriction is harmless for the local reduction below and makes
the total malformed-input policy explicit.  Any broader project CNF1 solver
also solves this sublanguage; this definition therefore does not assume a
promise at the endpoint.

\section{Correct fixed-layout adapter}

\begin{theorem}[repaired indexed-3CNF adapter]\label{thm:adapter}
Let $Q_x$ be an exactly-three-literal CNF with source identifiers in $[q]$,
$q,C\le n\log^{O(1)}n$, and let a deterministic transducer return occurrence
$(j,r)$ in $\log^{O(1)}n$ time and $\log^{O(1)}n$ workspace.  Then there is an
equisatisfiable \texttt{DENSE-CNF1} string $R_x$ of
$n\log^{O(1)}n$ bits whose length and every addressed bit (with blank returned
past its length) are computed within the same polylogarithmic bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $Q_x$ is empty, emit the unique six-byte header
\texttt{CNF1 00 00}.  Otherwise append, for every $v\in[q]$, the tautology
$(v\vee\neg v\vee v)$.  This charges $q$ additional fixed-width clauses,
makes $q$ the attained maximum, and preserves satisfiability.

Choose the least $b\ge1$ such that $q\le2^{7b-3}$ and put
$A=2^{7b-3}$.  Rename $v$ to $A+v$, preserving sign, and encode by (1).
Every encoded literal value lies in
\[
 2A+1\le z\le4A=2^{7b-1}<2^{7b},
 \qquad 2A+1>2^{7(b-1)},                                   \tag{2}
\]
so every literal occupies exactly $b$ bytes.  Each record therefore has
length $1+3b$.  The exact header is $(A+q,C+q)$; it is attained by the padding
clause for $q$.  Since the complete output contains at least $q$ padding
records, its byte length is at least $4q+6$, while $A+q\le129q$ (and is
$17$ for $q=1$); hence $A+q\le128N$.

After the header, division of an address by $1+3b$ identifies a clause,
occurrence and literal byte.  Source clauses use one transducer call; padding
clauses are arithmetic.  Header, EOF and out-of-range cases are explicit.
The output length is $O((C+q)\log(q+1))=n\log^{O(1)}n$, and all live
quantities have $O(\log n)$ bits.  The renaming is injective and all appended
clauses are tautologies.
\end{proof}

\section{The local 3CNF source is a known theorem}

The missing source theorem need not be reproved as a new WRAPPER lemma.
Jahanjou, Miles and Viola state that the classical reduction needed for
time--space tradeoffs maps nondeterministic time $T$ to a quasilinear-size
3SAT formula whose indexed clause is computable in polylogarithmic time and
$O(\log T)$ space.  Their Section~5 makes the gate-to-clause map explicit and
pads short clauses by literal repetition.  This is exactly the
clause-explicit property required here.  The relevant primary source is
\begin{center}
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.3171}.
\end{center}
This invocation is of their explicit account of the \emph{classical}
FLMV-era reduction, not their newer Theorem~1 at $T=n$, whose parameter loss
must not be silently treated as quasilinear.

For completeness, the final serialization composition can be written without
any variable-length gadget ambiguity.  A polylog-uniform quasilinear circuit
has gates densely numbered $1,\dots,G$.  Lipton and Williams, Theorem~3.1,
state that a gate index yields its at-most-two predecessors and type in
polylogarithmic time.  For output variable $y$ and inputs $a,b$, use exactly
three clauses per gate:
\begin{align*}
 y=a\wedge b:&\quad
 (\neg a\vee\neg b\vee y),
 (a\vee\neg y\vee\neg y),
 (b\vee\neg y\vee\neg y);\\
 y=a\vee b:&\quad
 (a\vee b\vee\neg y),
 (\neg a\vee y\vee y),
 (\neg b\vee y\vee y);\\
 y=\neg a:&\quad
 (a\vee y\vee y),
 (\neg a\vee\neg y\vee\neg y),
 (y\vee\neg y\vee y).
\end{align*}
Hardwired input gates use the corresponding repeated unit plus two
tautologies; nondeterministic witness inputs use three tautologies.  One final
three-clause block asserts the output, with two tautological fillers.  Thus
$C=3(G+1)$, $q=G$, and \texttt{divmod} of a clause index identifies one gate
and one slot.  Every child execution is one charged gate-tuple computation;
there is no SAT oracle in acquisition.  The Lipton--Williams source is
\url{https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/qbf-nc-journal-rev.pdf}.

Combining this fixed block with Theorem~\ref{thm:adapter} gives the exact
local endpoint transition:
\[
 L\in\operatorname{NTIME}[n]
 \Longrightarrow R_x\in\texttt{DENSE-CNF1},\quad
 |R_x|=n\log^{O(1)}n,                                      \tag{3}
\]
with each requested bit generated in polylogarithmic time and space.  A
simulated SAT machine retains its work and index tapes while the generator's
state is live, so the joint bound is
\[
 t(n\log^{O(1)}n)\log^{O(1)}n,qquad
 s(n\log^{O(1)}n)+\log^{O(1)}n.                            \tag{4}
\]
This closes the local Cook-stream/serialization obligation.  It says nothing
about obtaining a favorable solver for arbitrary source CNFs.

\section{Primary endpoint and exact exponent slack}

Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas use the same charged random-access
model and prove in Corollary~5.8 that, for constant $c>1$,
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
 \DTISP\!\left[n^c,o\!\left(n^{d(c)}\right)\right],\qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).               \tag{5}
\]
At $c=1201/1000$, $d(c)\approx0.5091082157>1/2$.  Hence a
\texttt{DENSE-CNF1} solver jointly using $N^{6/5+o(1)}$ time and
$N^{1/2+o(1)}$ space would, through (3)--(4), put every language in
$\operatorname{NTIME}[n]$ into time $n^{1.201}$ and
$o(n^{d(1.201)})$ space for all sufficiently large $n$, contradicting (5).
The primary paper is
\url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/tst.pdf}.

\section{What remains open}

The proof spine is now sharper:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textsc{Proved/Exact}: conditional recursive accounting, aggregate
child-volume closure, simultaneous workspace accounting, and conditional
elimination of $K$ once one favorable all-input phase exists.
\item \textsc{Proved/Exact after repair}: the fixed-layout dense-CNF1 adapter
and its total parser; ARC4's opposite-polarity exact-byte statement is retired.
\item \textsc{Known theorem + exact composition}: the local quasilinear 3CNF
transition and the FLMV endpoint contradiction at $(6/5,1/2)$.
\item \textsc{Open and separation-strength}: under $\SAT\in P$, construct on
every unrestricted input a favorable pre-semantic phase before any
parent-scale SAT query, charging discovery, failures, executed children with
multiplicity, replay, buffers, indexes and ancestor state.
\end{enumerate}

The latest hereditary marked construction remains a promise-domain candidate.
Its aggregate recurrence is favorable when
$\sum_i|y_i|\le N^{11/10+o(1)}$ and
$\max_i|y_i|\le N^{1/2+o(1)}$, because
$\sum_iT(|y_i|)\le (\max_i|y_i|)^{1/5+o(1)}\sum_i|y_i|le
N^{6/5+o(1)}$.  But balanced quoting, inherited contexts, dense rebasing,
exact headers, malformed-depth caps and all-input acquisition are not yet
proved.  A fallback to the assumed polynomial solver restores $K$.

The parity-star portfolio also remains non-favorable as implemented: the
four-variable, twelve-clause affine instance on triples
$\{1,2,3\},\{1,2,4\},\{1,3,4\}$ is an UNKNOWN witness, and the infinite
hub-cycle family forces $16m^2$ pair probes in the published scanner.  A
machine-step cap preserves soundness but loses coverage.  These facts prevent
the repaired endpoint from being mistaken for a completed $P\ne NP$ proof.
