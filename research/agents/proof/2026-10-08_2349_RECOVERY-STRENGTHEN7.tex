\section{PROVED/EXACT: aggregate recursive closure}

\begin{theorem}[Aggregate-volume $K$ elimination]\label{thm:aggregate}
Suppose one uniform exact completed phase works on every encoded SAT input and
every recursively reached phase entry.  On an entry of length $N$ it does
$N^{r+o(1)}$ nonchild work, uses $N^{p+o(1)}$ peak local state apart from one
live explicit child, and either decides or sequentially executes children of
lengths $m_1,\ldots,m_q$ satisfying
\[
 M:=\max_i m_i\le N^{h+o(1)},\qquad
 V:=\sum_i m_i\le N^{v+o(1)},\qquad 0\le h<1.              \tag{1}
\]
Every child is a valid immutable ordinary-SAT encoding shorter than its
parent, and the same phase applies recursively down to one fixed base.
Then one oracle-free exact SAT machine has simultaneous bounds
\[
 T(N)=N^{C+o(1)},\qquad S(N)=N^{P+o(1)},                   \tag{2}
\]
where
\[
 \boxed{C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{v-h}{1-h}\right\}},\qquad
 \boxed{P=\max\{p,h\}}.                                   \tag{3}
\]
The exponent $C$ is independent of the exponent of any initial polynomial
SAT decider.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Recursively answer each child call using the same machine.  Strict encoded
length descent proves termination and exactness by strong induction.  For
any $c\ge1$,
\[
 \sum_i m_i^c\le M^{c-1}\sum_i m_i=M^{c-1}V.              \tag{4}
\]
Thus an exponent $c$ closes the time induction whenever
\[
 c\ge r,\qquad c\ge v+h(c-1).                              \tag{5}
\]
The least $c\ge1$ satisfying (5) is (3).  For a rigorous uniform
$o(1)$ statement, fix arbitrary positive exponent slack, choose one length
after which every displayed error is smaller than the corresponding strict
slack in (5), and enlarge one constant for the finite prefix.  Equation (4)
then closes strong induction.  This charges repeated equal children
repeatedly because they occur repeatedly in $V$.

Depth-first evaluation retains one charged parent frame and one live child at
each level.  Their exponents form power-shrinking sequences, so local state,
child buffers and all ancestors sum to $N^{\max\{p,h\}+o(1)}$.  Time and
space are bounds on the same machine.
\end{proof}

RECURCLOSE1 is the count-envelope special case: $q\le N^{\eta+o(1)}$ and
$M\le N^{h+o(1)}$ give $v\le\eta+h$, so (3) becomes
$\max\{1,r,\eta/(1-h)\}$.  The aggregate theorem is strictly more flexible
because it permits unequal child sizes.

\begin{corollary}[The latest numerical ledger really closes]

\[

\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:aggregate} gives the joint pair

This is the recovered role of the old ``divide $2^k$ by $2^x$'' intuition:

\section{PROVED/EXACT: partial members do not create closure}

\begin{lemma}[Finite partial-wrapper closure]\label{lem:portfolio}
Fix a deterministic policy over finitely many source-first partial members.
Each attempted member completes charged source-only validation and returns
DECIDE, EXPAND with the actual child occurrences, or FAIL.  Recursive
substitution defines an all-input solver only if every reachable nonbase entry
is decided or has a selected non-FAIL transition, and every selected
transition satisfies the applicable contraction and resource premises.
RECURCLOSE1, CYCLEGAIN1, STREAMCOMP1 and ORBITBATCH1 do not enlarge this
successor domain.  Calling an assumed $N^{K+o(1)}$ SAT decider at an uncovered
entry restores $K$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the execution graph generated under true child answers.  An
accounting theorem assigns costs to edges that already exist; it supplies no
semantic transition at a FAIL vertex.  An uncovered root makes the policy
partial, and an uncovered executed successor makes recursive substitution
undefined there.  A direct fallback is a current-scale semantic leaf and
contributes its declared exponent $K$.  Address deduplication may reduce
source evaluation work, but it neither changes FAIL into EXPAND nor erases an
executed child occurrence.
\end{proof}

This lemma cleanly separates what was already solved.  COMBINED-REPLAY1 gives
total exact source-backed semantics but has no favorable all-input volume,
maximum-child or peak-state theorem.  MARKED-CUTSET-BLOCK1 and
PIVOT-FACTOR1 meet the aggregate numerical ledger only when they accept.
The former emits ordinary unmarked children; the latter may return UNKNOWN
on those children.  Theorem~\ref{thm:aggregate} cannot manufacture the
missing transitions.

\section{A common infinite residual family for the current suite}

\[
 H_n=F_n\wedge(h\vee\neg r)\wedge(\neg h\vee r)

\begin{theorem}[Guarded pivot obstruction]\label{thm:guard}
PIVOT-FACTOR1 returns UNKNOWN on every sufficiently large $H_n$.  Thus the
current favorable marked/pivot suite does not cover all roots, even before
successor closure is considered.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The repaired ULEB DENSE-CNF1 length is $N_n=\Theta(n\log n)$.  For every
pivot $z\ne h$, only $O(1)$ source or guard records contain $z$.  After
Davis--Putnam elimination, the $n-O(1)$ untouched parity blocks still share
$h$ and form one primal component with $\Theta(n\log n)=\Theta(N_n)$ encoded
bits.  This exceeds the member's
$B(N_n)=N_n^{1/2}\log N_n$ maximum-child cap.

For $z=h$, each parity block contributes two positive and two negative
occurrences.  Pairing a positive clause from one block with a negative clause
from a different block gives a nontautological raw pair, so acquisition has
$\Omega(n^2)$ charged pairs.  Since
\[
 n^2>N_n^{6/5}\log^2N_n
\]
for all sufficiently large $n$, the universal step cap aborts before a child
SAT call.  No pivot accepts, hence the member returns UNKNOWN.
\end{proof}

unstated parser compatibility is assumed.

\section{Recovered PRF3 interface wedge}

PRF3-0034's literal position-block index, recovered and independently
reviewed in CAPPED-INDEX-REPLAY1, partitions $N^{1+o(1)}$ source positions
into blocks of length $N^{\delta+o(1)}$.  One index takes one source scan to
build, retains $N^{1-\delta+o(1)}$ prefix-count bits, and answers a SELECT by
scanning one block in $N^{\delta+o(1)}$ time.  If there are
$N^{\kappa+o(1)}$ sequential builds and $N^{e+o(1)}$ executed SELECTs, the
declared noncaching implementation has
\[
 T_{\rm idx}=N^{\max\{1+\kappa,e+\delta\}+o(1)},\qquad
 S_{\rm idx}=N^{1-\delta+o(1)}.                            \tag{8}
\]
These worst-case terms are tight for this scan implementation when requested
survivors lie at block ends.  They are not a lower bound for all indexes.

The joint $(6/5,1/2)$ target forces
\[
 \kappa\le\frac15,\qquad \delta\ge\frac12,
 \qquad e+\delta\le\frac65,
 \quad\text{hence}\quad \boxed{e\le\frac7{10}}.           \tag{9}
\]
If position blocks service every possible input access in STREAMCOMP1's
conservative no-cache simulation of the saturated recursive edge, then
\[
 e=\eta+hC=\frac35+\frac12\cdot\frac65=\frac65.           \tag{10}
\]
No positive $\delta$ meets the time budget.  Equivalently, multiplying the
aggregate child-work bound by a per-access $N^\delta$ factor gives
$N^{6/5+\delta+o(1)}$.  Therefore the recovered position-block construction
cannot certify the saturated recursive bit interface.  A repair must prove
access sparsity or batching down to exponent at most $7/10$.

PRF3-0035 explains why (9) is implementation-specific.  A supplied survivor
list with $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ entries has access exponent zero and is syntactically
compatible with the target.  But building a fresh full-scan list for each of
$N^{3/5}$ child occurrences costs $N^{8/5+o(1)}$, while retaining all lists
violates half-space.  Cheap shared acquisition, replay and successor closure
remain unproved.  ORBITBATCH1's field-address identity does not merge these
restriction-specific executions or their output lists.

\section{Literature check and endpoint}

The literature supports the distinctions, not the missing implication.
Golynski--Orlandi--Raman--Rao study rank/select in the succinct-index model
with a represented read-only bit vector and a separately charged index; that
does not construct this project's survival vector or child generator:
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.2157}.  Mahaney's sparse-set pruning assumes a
polynomial-time reduction to a sparse hard set rather than deriving a cheap
one from $P=NP$:
\url{https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(82)90002-2}.
Recursive-backdoor algorithms are parameterized by a supplied or discovered
bounded-depth structure, not an all-input half-space acquisition theorem:
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04707}.
Fortnow--Santhanam is a conditional OR-compression barrier, not a compressor
construction; under the counterfactual $P=NP$ its collapse consequence does
not itself yield the required fixed exponents:
\url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2010.06.007}.

The endpoint remains Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas Corollary~5.8 in
their deterministic random-access model:
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
 \operatorname{DTISP}[n^c,o(n^{d(c)})],\qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).
\]
At $c=1.201$, $d(c)\approx0.509108>1/2$.  REDTEAM-STRENGTHEN4 supplies the
local exact-3CNF/DENSE-CNF1 transfer.  Hence a genuine all-input joint
$(6/5,1/2)$ algorithm would contradict the primary endpoint, but no such
algorithm is proved here:
\url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/tst.pdf}.
