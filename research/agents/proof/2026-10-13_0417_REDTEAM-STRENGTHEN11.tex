\section{Primary-literature and endpoint preflight}
The two obstacles checked before construction were the absence of an
unconditional worst-case/average-case bridge for natural NP problems and the
absence of an arbitrary-machine implication from PC/PCR lower bounds.
Bogdanov and Trevisan identify basing average-case hardness in NP on ordinary
worst-case assumptions as a major open problem \cite{bt}.  Holenstein and
K\"unzler refine barriers for broad nonadaptive worst-case/average-case
reductions \cite{hk}.  These are model-matched warnings, not impossibility
theorems for every construction-specific adaptive reduction.

Conneryd, de Rezende, Nordstr\"om, Pang and Risse prove linear PC degree and
strongly exponential expanded-monomial size for random regular graph
colouring over every field \cite{conneryd}.  Their theorem is a
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} in PC/PCR and Nullstellensatz, not a lower bound for
all deterministic deciders.  The newest image equivalence theorem preserves
NP-completeness but does not promote this proof-system endpoint.

The deterministic random-access endpoint remains Fortnow--Lipton--van
Melkebeek--Viglas Corollary 5.8 \cite{flmv}:
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
 \operatorname{DTISP}\!\left[n^c,
 o\!\left(n^{d(c)}\right)\right],
 \qquad
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).              \tag{2}
\]
At $c=6/5$, $d(c)=23/45>1/2$.  Thus a single algorithm with
$N^{6/5+o(1)}$ time and $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ space would contradict a nearby fixed
$c>6/5$ instance of (2).  No retired unrestricted
$t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint is used.

\section{The separation-equivalence firewall}
\begin{theorem}[Average-case all-machine premise already separates]
Let $L$ be any language.  If an ensemble $\{\mu_n\}$ satisfies (1) with
$L$ in place of $\KU$, then $L\notin\mathrm P$.  If $L$ is NP-complete under
polynomial-time many-one reductions, then $\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $L\in\mathrm P$, choose its exact polynomial-time decider $A_L$.  For
every $n$, its error on every distribution is zero, contradicting (1) for
$A=A_L$.  If $L$ is NP-complete and $L\notin\mathrm P$, then NP is not
contained in P, while P is contained in NP.  Hence P differs from NP.
\end{proof}

This does not make distributional research useless.  It fixes its status:
an unconditional proof of (1) is the separation, rather than a weaker lemma
that the current PC/PCR endpoint can complete.  A finite assertion of the
form ``these listed polynomial invariants fail'' has prefix
$\forall S\in\mathcal S_{\rm finite}$ and cannot replace
$\forall A\in\mathrm P$.

\begin{proposition}[Decoder transport]
Let $U$ and $D$ satisfy the verified ordered-image identities
$D(\operatorname{enc}U(F))=F$ on the declared source domain, with $D$
polynomial time.  If a source-support predicate $S$ runs in polynomial time,
then
\[
                  S_U(y)=S(D(y))                           \tag{3}
\]
is a polynomial-time predicate on valid compiler images.  Off-image strings
can be rejected by the same recognizer.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Run the total image recognizer/decoder and then $S$.  Correctness is the left
inverse identity; the time is the sum of two fixed polynomials.  No hidden
source recovery or uncharged oracle is assumed because $D$ is the published
algorithmic interface.
\end{proof}
Consequently, transparent compilation cannot hide any efficiently
recognizable source-support feature.  The compiler is an algorithmic aid in
this direction.

\section{A stronger matched pair and its exact failure}
Let
\[
                   Y=K_{3,3,3},\qquad N=\overline{C_9}.    \tag{4}
\]
Both graphs have nine vertices, 27 edges, degree six, one connected
component, and diameter two.  Starting color refinement from the uniform
color gives one class of size nine forever on either graph, because both are
regular.  Thus one-dimensional Weisfeiler--Leman does not distinguish them.

The displayed tripartition 3-colours $Y$.  In $N$, an independent set is a
clique in $C_9$, so it has size at most two.  Three color classes cover at
most six of the nine vertices; hence $N$ is not 3-colourable.  Nevertheless
the number of triangles separates the supports:
\[
                        t(Y)=27,
             \qquad     t(N)=30.                           \tag{5}
\]
For $Y$, choose one vertex from each part.  For $N$, a direct three-vertex
count gives 30.  Since the degree is fixed, all triangles can be counted in
$O(n)$ adjacency-list operations by intersecting constant-size neighbor
sets.

For every $r\ge1$, take $r$ disjoint copies and uniformly relabel all
vertices.  The YES and NO supports then have the same $9r$ vertices, $27r$
edges, degree sequence, $r$ components all of size nine, component-local
compiler totals, and uniform 1-WL profile.  Yet their component triangle
signatures are respectively
\[
                \{(9,27)^r\},\qquad\{(9,30)^r\}.            \tag{6}
\]
Equation (3) carries this separator to the ordered compiler images.  This
strictly repairs the particular component-size leakage in BALMIXFW1 and
simultaneously gives its next smallest counterexample.  It is not evidence
that every balanced pair has an easy separator.

\section{Accounting, hostile controls, and barriers}
The separator charges source or decoded-input access, component traversal,
triangle counting, and output.  It executes no SAT child and stores only
ordinary graph traversal state.  The compiler-image version charges image
recognition and decoding before the source scan.  It does not count semantic
states as runtime.

Horn, dual-Horn, 2-SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded-treewidth, counting/flow,
compact arithmetic, products, relabeling, clause/literal permutations,
COPY, DELAY, INTERP and RECOMP remain legitimate algorithms or wrappers.
The present pair specifically survives relabeling, equal-size independent
products, component counts and 1-WL but fails triangle-count and spectral
controls.  A stronger candidate must be tested against them, but passing a
finite battery still cannot prove (1).

The firewall and decoder transport relativize and algebrize.  They supply no
nonrelativizing hinge.  No truth-table property is used, so Natural Proofs
is not directly triggered.  Schur squares, quadratic-relation ranks,
Hilbert functions, syzygies and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED
TRANSFERS} for the all-machine quantifier.  Worst-case/average-case
architecture is an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; it becomes an exact transfer
only after a fully charged reduction theorem.  The polynomial decoder is an
\textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}, and its structure is exploitable by
an algorithm.

The HEC/VSC-HEC/G-PPC-HEC/CRS-PPC/CCSPC branch remains consistent with this
result.  Its no-reread information bounds are exact in their online models,
while unrestricted static rereading, recomputation and the missing
static-to-online compiler remain the barrier.  A balanced distribution does
not repair that model transfer.

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}
The phrase ``prove that every currently known polynomial invariant fails''
is valid only as a finite experimental milestone, not a load-bearing theorem.
The stronger phrase ``prove no polynomial support separator exists'' is
exactly an instance of Theorem 1 and therefore already proves P differs from
NP for an NP-complete target.  Matching size, degree, component, 1-WL, or
spectral statistics cannot bridge the quantifier gap by itself.
