\section*{Independent review: guard embedding into the UNKNOWN domain}
\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT on the stated domain; bounded tests are supporting evidence.}
The source formula $F$ is a proper exactly-width-three CNF whose occurring
variables are exactly $1,\ldots,n$, with $n\geq3$. Each clause has three distinct
variables, so it is not tautological. With fresh variables as in the parent
construction, set
\[
G=F\wedge(h\vee r)\wedge P_0(h,a,b)\wedge P_0(h,c,d)
\wedge\bigwedge_{v=1}^n(h\vee v\vee w_v).
\]
Here $P_0$ is the four-clause representation of even parity on its triple.
Every satisfying assignment to $F$ extends by $h=r=a=c=1$, $b=d=0$ and arbitrary
$w_v$. Conversely, $F$ is a subformula of $G$. Thus satisfiability is preserved.

\paragraph{Verified failure of the original portfolio.}
The formula is neither constant, Horn nor two-CNF. Its primal graph is connected.
The positive-pair graph of the two parity guards comprises two triangles sharing
$h$ and has vertex-cover number three. For any candidate backdoor $B$ of size
at most two other than $\{h,r\}$, choose an unhit pair $u,v$ from those triangles.
The guard contains $(u\vee v\vee\neg t)$. If $t\in B$, set $t=1$; otherwise
leave it unassigned. Assign other members of $B$ arbitrarily. This clause
survives with two positive literals. No clause becomes empty: all clauses except
$(h\vee r)$ have three distinct variables, and the exceptional binary clause is
not fully assigned. Thus the implementation's absorbing-empty-clause restriction
rule does not invalidate the witness. For $B=\{h,r\}$ choose $h=1$; the binary
clause is satisfied and $(a\vee b)$ survives. The portfolio therefore fails.
The published ARC2 scanner checks proper width three before any other operation,
so the binary clause forces UNKNOWN even if $F$ is unsatisfiable.

\paragraph{Encoding and same-machine qualification.}
There are $n'=2n+6$ variables and $m'=m+n+9$ clauses. Because every source variable
occurs, $n\leq3m$; fixed-width dense binary identifier encodings therefore give
$|G|=\Theta(|F|)$. A header declaring arbitrarily many unused variables would
invalidate this size argument. A logspace streaming emitter alone does not
establish a joint small-space time bound for a solver on $G$: materialization
costs linear space, and regenerating each requested bit may multiply time by
the source length. The parent repair should specify the regular fixed-width
layout and compute every requested output bit from the corresponding source
literal bit or indexed gadget literal in polylogarithmic time and logarithmic
space. Under that separate local-access proof, the reduction can support
simultaneous resource transfer without materialization. It does not establish
a SAT lower bound or a resource-improving wrapper.
