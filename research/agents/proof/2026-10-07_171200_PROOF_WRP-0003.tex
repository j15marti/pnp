\section*{Exact construction}
For CNFs $A,B$, define $A\Rightarrow_{\rm RUP}B$ iff for every clause
$C\in B$, unit propagation on
$A\wedge\bigwedge_{\ell\in C}\neg\ell$ derives a conflict.  Primary proof
checking literature defines this as $A$ implying $C$ via unit propagation
\cite{wetzler2014drat,reeves2023pr}.  It is a sound, incomplete test for
$A\models B$.

\begin{theorem}[RUP-IMPLICATION-SELECT1]
Given explicit CNFs $R_1,\ldots,R_q$ of encoded length at most $m$, construct
the directed graph $G$ having $i\to j$ iff
$R_i\Rightarrow_{\rm RUP}R_j$.  Condense $G$ into its SCC DAG and retain one
representative from every sink SCC.  For the retained index set $K$,
\[
 \bigvee_{i=1}^{q}\SAT(R_i)=\bigvee_{i\in K}\SAT(R_i).
\]
The construction makes zero SAT-oracle calls, takes $O(q^2m^2)$ time and
$O(qm+q^2+m)$ live space, and emits at most $q$ children of length at most
$m$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $i\to j$, then each clause of $R_j$ is a semantic consequence of $R_i$,
so $R_i\models R_j$.  Every vertex in a finite condensation DAG has a path to
a sink.  Semantic implication composes along that path, hence every model of
every discarded residual is a model of one retained residual.  Conversely,
the retained formulas are a subset of the original list.  The two model
unions, and therefore the two ORs of SAT values, are equal.

For each of $q(q-1)$ ordered pairs, there are at most $O(m)$ target clauses.
Falsify one target clause and perform one occurrence-list queue
unit-propagation run on the $O(m)$-length source, including its initialization.
This gives
$O(q^2m^2)$ time.  Explicit inputs use $O(qm)$ space, the graph uses
$O(q^2)$ bits/words under the declared RAM convention, propagation scratch
uses $O(m)$, and SCC condensation is linear in the graph.  Returning source
indices avoids copying retained formulas; any consumer access to the retained
explicit inputs is already charged by the $O(qm)$ storage term.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Strict extension.}
Let $A=(a)\wedge(\neg a\vee b)$ and $B=(b)$.  Their clause sets are
incomparable, but falsifying $(b)$ in $A$ propagates $a$ and then conflicts,
so $A\Rightarrow_{\rm RUP}B$.  Thus $A$ is deleted from
$\SAT(A)\vee\SAT(B)$.  With fresh $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, all
$A_i=(a_i)\wedge(\neg a_i\vee b)$ collapse to the single $B$ although raw
clause inclusion deletes none.

\section*{Primary literature and transfer audit}
Wetzler, Heule, and Hunt describe efficient RUP/RAT checking in DRAT-trim
\cite{wetzler2014drat}.  Reeves, Heule, and Bryant state the exact RUP
criterion and study the stronger propagation-redundancy system
\cite{reeves2023pr}.  Classification: \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM
TRANSFER} for the local implication certificate $A\Rightarrow C$ and its
soundness.  The sink-SCC OR selector and its WRAPPER ledger are proved here.

Using PR, RAT, local redundancy, or proof logging as a stronger selector is
an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge}: those systems certify
broader redundancy transformations, but the needed theorem must discover
certificates uniformly, orient them as residual implication, bound proof and
access size, and prove favorable density on wrapper-generated frontiers.
Solver performance and proof-checker practicality do not supply that theorem.

The control-theoretic Lyapunov analogy is likewise only structural: SCC sinks
are terminal under the certified preorder, but no scalar potential is shown
to decrease on every SAT frontier.  Post-quantum collision entropy and
Schur-square distinguishers are a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} here; equal hashes
or algebraic similarity do not prove semantic implication.  RUP itself is
exploitable by the algorithm, as required, and uses no hardness assumption.

\section*{Hostile controls and failed density}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{ARC antichain:} for all complete unit assignments on
$1\leq n\leq8$ remaining variables, the selector found zero edges and kept
all $2^n$ residuals; at $n=8$ it kept all 256.  This is the expected semantic
incomparability control.
\item[] \textbf{Renaming and complementation:} random variable permutations
and literal complementation preserved the retained count on every random
portfolio.
\item[] \textbf{Padding:} adding tautological clauses changed neither the
normalized frontier nor the answer.  Padding creates no density.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products:} all four conjunctions of opposite
unit choices on two independent variables were retained, with zero edges.
Easy factorization does not make product assignments imply one another.
\item[] \textbf{Source carry and access:} this implementation explicitly
stores all $q$ residuals and the $q^2$ graph.  Its theorem does not apply to a
purported implicit frontier unless regeneration and random/sequential access
meet a separately stated bound.
\item[] \textbf{Current-scale call:} production makes zero calls to the
hypothetical SAT solver.  Adding such a call would retain its unknown exponent.
\item[] \textbf{Oracle/algebraic replacement:} RUP soundness survives common
oracles and algebraic extensions.  The theorem is therefore useful
infrastructure, not a nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing separation hinge.
\end{description}
No finite experiment is promoted to an asymptotic density claim.
