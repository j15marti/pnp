\section{Exact indexing theorem}
Each input 3-clause has exactly three fixed-width signed literal words.
Let $q$ be total clauses among four leaves and $v$ total source variables.
A balanced tree of two bottom OR gadgets and one root gadget has
$M=4q$ clauses and $V=v+3q+3$ variables.
Every requested root clause rank can be decoded by two comparisons with
child clause counts, two quotients and one request to a source leaf.
No other leaf record is touched. An output bit reads the sign or one
fixed-width identifier bit. Thus with locally indexable hard leaves,
bit access takes polylogarithmic time and logarithmic space.
