\paragraph{Restricted construction.}
For every bounded-width source F, supplied fixed assignment rho and cap B,
scan each clause, retain its unassigned literals only when no assigned
literal satisfies it, and stop UNKNOWN before the encoded residual exceeds
B. Store literal values, not source pointers. Include header, clause widths,
original variable-name widths and padding.
On success the ordinary child is exactly F restricted by rho. In a
random-access fixed-record realization, every child bit is directly
addressable. No ordering or canonicalization is needed.
If s literals and c clauses survive, child length is
O((s+c)log N), with O(log N) current-clause scratch for constant width.
Acquisition is N times polylogarithmic factors with appropriate sparse
assignment lookup. Assignment storage O(|rho|log N), counters, retained
ancestors and other live objects are additional to the child cap.
Thus child shrinkage and cheap access can coexist constructively.
This is an instantiation on successful restrictions, not an all-input rule.

\paragraph{Exact semantic scope.}
A restriction alone need not preserve the parent's SAT value.
For every complete assignment cover on selected variables,
SAT(F) is the OR of the restricted SAT values.
Every attempted assignment, unsuccessful cap check and executed child
must be charged. Recursive closure requires this coverage for every
nonterminal descendant or a separately charged alternative.
Terminal solvers can discharge easy children; failure of this isolated
acquirer is not failure of the full portfolio.

\paragraph{Tests and limitations.}
The adjacent executable uses a declared finite encoding: a 32-bit clause
count and 50-bit records for up to three literals, each with a 16-bit signed
identifier. This is not a variable-length asymptotic encoding claim.
All 256 subsets of the eight signed proper three-variable clauses passed
the exact two-branch SAT-cover identity. Encoding checks covered 67584
bits; those direct checks are consistency checks, not independent machine
verification. Python logical cap accounting does not bound interpreter RSS.
The program completed under 25 seconds with an internal 24-second alarm.
The padded easy control consists of repeated (x1 OR x2 OR x3), followed by
x2 and NOT x2. With source size 25732 bits and sqrt-size cap 160,
rho(x1)=true emits 132 bits; rho(x1)=false exceeds the cap.
At 102532 source bits and cap 320 the same distinction persists.
The small emitted contradiction is easily solved by unit propagation.
Its failed attempt at further empty-assignment shrinkage is not a
hereditary counterexample: base cases and terminals must be respected.
Padding creates these successful ratios and does not establish a general
compression theorem. An independent agent confirmed all scope guards.
