\fbox{\parbox{.92\linewidth}{
\textbf{Result.}  SIGNED-TWIN-MACRO1 is \textsc{Proved/Exact}: a class
of at least two variables having identical signed clause-incidence columns
admits an exact forward OR reduction to at most three residual CNFs.  The
three states are all zero, all one, and one fixed mixed assignment.  All
class occurrences disappear from every child.  A deterministic read-only
scan discovers and generates any threshold-passing class in
$O(M^3)$ time, $O(\log M)$ workspace, and streamed output, so the fully
charged realization has $(a,c,s)=(0,3,0)$ and is not endpoint-favorable.
The rule is a new exact partial macro, not an all-CNF shrinker.  The joint
WRAPPER implication and P versus NP remain \textsc{Open}.}}
\end{center}

\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
Freuder defines full interchangeability of values of a CSP variable and shows
how interchangeable values can compact a solution space
\cite{wrp0006Freuder}.  This is a \textbf{useful structural analogy requiring
a new transfer theorem}: its objects are domain values, whereas the present
objects are Boolean variables whose entire signed clause columns coincide.
The three residual states and their CNF size ledger do not follow from that
paper.

Anders, Codel, and Heule's Definition~2 and Theorems~3.1--3.3 provide an
exact SAT transfer for certified permutation symmetries and orbitopal fixing
\cite{arc0005Orbitopal}.  Their result justifies treating source-visible
symmetries as algorithmically exploitable.  It neither identifies every
signed-twin class nor proves the present mixed-state quotient.  Thus the
symmetry premise is a \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} context, while
SIGNED-TWIN-MACRO1 is proved directly below.

The cross-domain lesson is CSP value abstraction: quotient only after an
explicit interchangeability certificate.  Importing generic symmetry
breaking without the signed-column hypothesis would be a misleading
transfer.  Schur squares, quadratic relations, collision entropy, binding,
and random-oracle techniques do not certify the Boolean case split here;
classification: \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for the active obstacle.

\section{The exact three-state quotient}
For a variable $x$, let its signed incidence column be
\[
 h_x(C)=\begin{cases}
  +1,&x\in C,\\
  -1,&\neg x\in C,\\
  0,&\text{otherwise.}
 \end{cases}
\]
Call $G$ a signed-twin class when $h_x=h_y$ for all $x,y\in G$.
Tautology-freedom implies that every clause meeting $G$ contains either all
members positively or all members negatively.

Fix distinct $g_0,g_1\in G$.  Define assignments
\[
 \alpha_0(G)=0,\qquad \alpha_1(G)=1,
 \qquad \alpha_\star(g_0)=0,\quad\alpha_\star(g_1)=1,
 \quad\alpha_\star(G\setminus\{g_0,g_1\})=0.
\]
Let $F_i=F\mathbin{|}\alpha_i$ for $i\in\{0,1,\star\}$, using ordinary
CNF restriction and canonical duplicate removal.

\begin{theorem}[SIGNED-TWIN-MACRO1]
For every canonical tautology-free CNF $F$ and signed-twin class
$G$ with $|G|\ge2$,
\[
 \SAT(F)=\SAT(F_0)\lor\SAT(F_1)\lor\SAT(F_\star).
\]
Every occurrence of every variable in $G$ disappears from every child, and
the fanout is at most three after residual deduplication.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every assignment to $G$ is all zero, all one, or mixed.  The first two cases
are represented by $F_0$ and $F_1$.  In a mixed assignment, each clause
meeting $G$ is satisfied: a positive incident clause contains a true member,
and a negative incident clause contains the negation of a false member.
Consequently all incident clauses disappear, independently of the outside
assignment, exactly as under $\alpha_\star$.  This maps every model of $F$ to
a model of one displayed child.  Conversely, extending a model of a child by
its fixed assignment gives a model of $F$.  Ordinary restriction removes all
assigned occurrences, and duplicate children may be omitted.
\end{proof}

For a deletion-monotone canonical encoding, every child is no longer than the
parent.  Power shrink is not automatic: an independent outside component may
occupy almost all of $F$.  The partial member therefore emits REDUCE only
after measuring the actual children and verifying
$|F_i|\le B(M)$ for an effective threshold
$B(M)\le M^{b+o(1)}$, $b<1$.  This gives $a=0$ and the requested child bound
on successful instances without promoting finite savings to an asymptotic
claim.

\section{Charged acquisition and generation}
There are at most $M$ variable names.  Equality of two signed columns can be
checked by one complete source scan using logarithmic counters.  For every
candidate leader, compare it with every variable, and simulate each child;
when a literal is encountered, rescan as necessary to decide class membership.
This deliberately simple implementation uses $O(M^3)$ time and
$O(\log M)$ workspace, including a failed search.  A successful second pass
streams the at most three children and charges their output length.

Thus the explicit member has
\[
 (a,b,c,s)=(0,b,3,0),\qquad
 C=\max\{3,0/(1-b)\}=3.
\]
It is not a favorable PRF3-0018 realization.  A materialized incidence index
can improve time but consumes polynomial workspace; no same-realization
endpoint improvement is proved.  On FAIL the unchanged fallback remains, so
PREFIX-FALLBACK-NOEDGE1 also prevents this partial member alone from becoming
an ARC contraction.

\section{Reproducible bounded test}
The command
\begin{verbatim}
timeout 30s python3 experiments/proof_spines/signed_twin_macro_test.py \
  results/raw/proof_spines/2026-10-08_001015_signed_twin_macro.json \
  --seed 20261008

uses a 25-second cooperative alarm and completed in 0.765 seconds.  It

ran 2,000 seeded random formulas, and made 9,338 brute-force SAT evaluations.
All semantic equalities passed.  It also passed 6,314 child-size monotonicity
checks, 500 one-occurrence near-twin rejections, 200 independent-product
checks, and 100 duplicate-padding checks.  Maximum fanout was three.  At the
finite diagnostic threshold $M^{0.8}$, 1,817 random cases passed and 183
failed; this is \textsc{Computational}, not asymptotic evidence.

\section{Hostile controls and model audit}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Easy families.} Repeated columns can be abundant in highly
regular CNFs.  This demonstrates exploitability, not worst-case coverage.
\item[] \textbf{Source carry/current-scale calls.} The transformer makes no
SAT or oracle call.  All discovery scans are charged.  A FAIL branch retains
the original fallback exponent.
\item[] \textbf{Padding.} Duplicate clauses are removed before $M$ and the
shrink threshold are measured.  The test confirms that duplicates do not
counterfeit acceptance.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products.} Exactness survives a disjoint product,
but the untouched factor is carried into every child and can defeat every
fixed power-shrink threshold.
\item[] \textbf{Oracle/algebraic replacement.} The theorem is syntactic and
relativizes.  Algebraic or oracle access may find the class faster, confirming
that this is not a nonrelativizing lower-bound hinge.
\item[] \textbf{Finite versus asymptotic.} The observed $M^{0.8}$ acceptances
do not establish an infinite family or all-input coverage.  Only the exact
case split and stated scan upper bound are promoted.
\end{description}
