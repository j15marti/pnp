\paragraph{Verdict.}
The four original reports contain reusable, proved conditional composition
theorems, but none proves the required all-input constructor.  The smallest
gap is a quantifier reversal: the sources analyze a favorable reduction,
typed graph, generator, or evaluator \emph{after it is supplied}; the proof
needs to derive one jointly favorable uniform implementation from
$\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$.  P versus NP remains OPEN.

\section*{Claim card and corpus}
The target implication is
\begin{align*}
&[\exists A\,\exists K\,\forall x\quad A(x)=\mathrm{SAT}(x),\ 
 T_A(|x|)\le |x|^K]\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
 [\exists W\,\exists h,p<1\,\forall N\,\forall\hbox{ reached states }z
 \quad W\hbox{ satisfies the charged phase contract at }z].
\end{align*}
The same $W$ must provide exact semantics, uniform all-length bounds,
near-linear local work and aggregate executed-child volume, fractional
maximum child length, and simultaneous sublinear retained state on the
endpoint's random-access machine.  Discovery, failures, replay, repeated
executions, generators, buffers, indexes, caches and ancestors are included.

The original GitHub bodies of RECURCLOSE1, CYCLEGAIN1, STREAMCOMP1 and
ORBITBATCH1 were read in full, along with RECOVERY-STRENGTHEN1--3,
FORMAL-SPINE1 and the current construction/red-team evidence.  The current
Fortnow endpoint and Williams self-improvement paper were checked directly.
Fortnow excludes a \emph{single} simultaneous near-linear-time,
$N^{1-\varepsilon}$-space SAT machine in the stated random-access setting;
it does not construct an upper-bound wrapper.  Williams explicitly lists
self-improvement down to the $P=NP$ regime as open.  See
\url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/tst.pdf} and
\url{https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2023/082/download}.

Thus the common logical form is
\[
 \forall X\,[\operatorname{FavorablePremises}(X)\Rightarrow

\]
not
\[
 \forall A\,[A\hbox{ decides SAT in polynomial time}\Rightarrow

\section*{Smallest sanity construction and domain mismatch}
Assuming $A$ decides SAT in time $N^K$, there is a trivial uniform all-input
``wrapper'': run $A$ and make no recursive calls.  It satisfies exactness and
termination, but its local exponent is $r=K$.  RECURCLOSE1 then returns
$C=K$, so the unknown exponent is not eliminated.  This does not prove that a
better wrapper cannot exist; it shows exactly why mere $P=NP$ plus all-input
semantics is weaker than the missing favorable-constructor implication.

The narrowest additional domain mismatches are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item CYCLEGAIN1 covers only a supplied finite typed system with a bounded
same-scale phase/rank.  A recursion whose same-scale chain grows with $N$
does not enter that theorem merely because it eventually shrinks.
\item STREAMCOMP1's $g_e,s_e$ must already include recursive reads of implicit
parents.  A one-level bit oracle bound cannot be reused unchanged at every
nested level.
\item ORBITBATCH1 deduplicates \emph{addresses}, not executed consumers.
Repeated child executions remain counted with multiplicity, and its own
report retains direct output volume $N_{\rm field}^{1+1/r+o(1)}$.
\item $N_{\rm field}=p^r$ is not encoded SAT length.  Contraction measured in
that coordinate cannot be inserted into the endpoint before the complete
original-bit length map is proved.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Reusable closure theorem that survives}
The strongest recovered statement is the aggregate returning-phase closure.
For every reached state of one fixed uniform exact program, let $L(N)$ be all
non-child phase work; let $m_j$ be lengths of \emph{executed} recursive
children, with multiplicity; put
\[
 V(N)=\sum_jm_j,qquad M(N)=\max_jm_j.
\]
If uniformly at every reached state
\[
 L(N),V(N)\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad M(N)\le N^{h+o(1)}\ (h<1),
\]
and all live non-child state is $N^{p+o(1)}$ for fixed $p<1$ plus one charged
child buffer, then depth-first substitution gives
\[
 T(N)\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 S(N)\le N^{\max\{p,h\}+o(1)}.
\]
This theorem legitimately absorbs bounded same-scale typed phases, shared
setup and implicit access \emph{when their complete costs are already inside}
$L,V$ and the live-state bound.  It preserves conditional elimination of
$K$.  It is not an acquisition theorem.

\section*{Falsification status and handoff}
No contradiction was found in the four theorems at their declared scopes.
The attempted promotion of any one of them to an all-input construction is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: each promotion drops an explicit premise.  The
aggregate closure and typed/implicit accounting remain \textbf{PROVED/EXACT
conditional}.  The source-side orbit identity remains \textbf{PROVED/EXACT}
but is not a SAT contraction theorem.

The next target should not be another recurrence.  It is one concrete map
that, for every encoded SAT input and every recursively reached state,
emits the exact child list or direct answer while proving the displayed
$L,V,M$ and live-state bounds in original SAT bits.  The map may use bounded
same-scale phases and shared/implicit evaluators, but their acquisition,
nested replay and multiplicity must be visible in that one execution.

\paragraph{Status.}
Quantifier/domain audit: PROVED/EXACT.  Favorable all-input construction:
OPEN.  P versus NP remains OPEN.
