\section{Target, quantifiers, and model}
The audited implication is
\[
 \mathrm{SAT}\in P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \exists W\,\exists h,p<1\,\forall N\,\forall x\in\mathrm{SATInput}_N
 \,\forall z\in\operatorname{Reach}_W(x):\ \mathcal C(W,z;N,h,p),
 \tag{1}
\]
where one fixed uniform exact machine $W$ must, at every recursively reached
state, decide exactly or issue strictly smaller exact SAT children of encoded
lengths $m_i$ with
\[
 \sum_i m_i\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 \max_i m_i\le N^{h+o(1)},
 \tag{2}
\]
while all local discovery, failed attempts, acquisition, certification,
generation, replay and combination cost $N^{1+o(1)}$, and retained state is
$N^{p+o(1)}$ in addition to one charged child buffer.  Executed children count
with multiplicity.  The model is the same uniform random-access realization
throughout; encoded bit length, not variable count, field-domain size, hash
length, or number of distinct labels, is the size measure.

The recovery question is whether the original RECURCLOSE1, CYCLEGAIN1,
STREAMCOMP1 and ORBITBATCH1 sources prove (1), or only prove consequences of
some of its premises.  They prove only the latter.

\section{What each source actually proves}
\subsection*{RECURCLOSE1}
Fix a deterministic exact SAT decider $A$.  Its premise is one uniform
$A$-derived exact SAT oracle procedure on \emph{every} SAT instance of length
$n$, with local work $n^{r+o(1)}$, at most $n^{\eta+o(1)}$ queries, every
query of length at most $n^{\theta+o(1)}$ for one fixed $\theta<1$, uniform
parameters at every length, and no hidden invocation of $A$ outside the
declared calls.  Under that premise, recursive substitution proves
\[
 T(n)\le n^{C+o(1)},\qquad
 C=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-\theta}\right\},
 \tag{3}
\]
independently of the original polynomial exponent $K$.  With depth-first
execution, local workspace $n^{\sigma+o(1)}$, and one explicit child buffer,
it proves $S(n)\le n^{\max\{\sigma,\theta\}+o(1)}$.

This is the original exact elimination of $K$.  It already has the necessary
all-length uniformity and recursive semantic closure.  It does \emph{not}
derive its shrinker from $A$, and explicitly identifies that derivation as
OPEN.  Its count contract implies the current volume contract only under the
extra numerical condition
\[
 \eta+\theta\le1,
 \tag{4}
\]
because $\sum_i m_i\le n^{\eta+\theta+o(1)}$.  Thus the present near-linear
specialization is $r\le1$, $\theta=h<1$, $\eta\le1-h$, together with the
stated space premises.  The current aggregate-volume theorem is strictly
more flexible than (4), because it permits uneven child sizes.

\subsection*{CYCLEGAIN1}
For finitely many exact procedure types, it assumes charged local exponents
$r_i$ and actual edges $e:i\to j$ with executed multiplicity exponent
$\eta_e$ and encoded-length exponent $0\le\theta_e\le1$.  It proves finite
typed recursive closure when every directed return cycle has
\[
 \prod_{e\in\gamma}\theta_e<1,
 \tag{5}
\]
and every same-scale edge is nonexpanding and decreases a source-proved finite
rank bounded independently of input length (equivalently, in its finite
type-only form, the same-scale subgraph is acyclic).  The inequalities
$C_i\ge r_i$ and $C_i\ge\eta_e+\theta_eC_j$ then give a finite least exponent
vector independent of $K$.

This exactly proves that an individual scale-one edge need not be fatal.
It does not prove that a proposed wrapper has the bounded same-scale rank,
cycle gains, charged local work, or all-input domain closure.  ``Eventually
terminates'' is weaker than its hypothesis.  A materialized scale-one child
also retains a linear-space charge.  For the current contract, a bounded
typed same-scale chain may be flattened into one returning phase only after
\emph{all} executions and retained state inside that phase satisfy (2) and
the local budgets.

\subsection*{STREAMCOMP1}
For a declared actual child string of length $n^{\theta_e+o(1)}$, it assumes a
random-access generator returning each child bit in parent-scale time
$n^{g_e+o(1)}$ and workspace $n^{s_e+o(1)}$.  Its conservative no-cache
simulation proves
\[
 C_i\ge\max\{r_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(\eta_e+g_e+\theta_eC_j)\},
 \qquad
 \rho_i\ge\max\{\sigma_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(s_e,\theta_e\rho_j)\}.
 \tag{6}
\]
Generator construction, preprocessing, repeated accesses, nested parent
access, descriptors, staging, caches, and retained frames remain charged.

This is a genuine solution to the \emph{representation accounting} issue:
a logical same-scale child need not be copied into a fresh linear buffer.
It is not a cheap-acquisition theorem.  It assumes that the source-defined
child and its bit generator already exist.  At the near-linear boundary, a
positive fixed per-access exponent can consume the contraction margin;
shared or batched evaluation needs a separate proved ledger.

\subsection*{ORBITBATCH1}
For one declared SU05/Hadamard realization and an invertible field map $A$,
it proves the exact address-set identity
\[
 \bigcup_{i\in I}\{A^iv:v\in\mathbb F_p^d\}=\mathbb F_p^d.
 \tag{7}
\]
It therefore retires seed-by-orbit multiplicity as a count of distinct field
addresses, permits one already-acquired parent evaluator to be shared across
line restrictions, and keeps classification as a separate total cost.  It
also proves that explicit direct-output volume remains
$N_{\rm field}^{1+1/r+o(1)}$ in its normalization.

This sharing result is reusable against false reacquisition charges.  It does
not acquire the parent evaluator from an arbitrary SAT computation, does not
remove actual consumers or repeated executions, and does not produce SAT
children.  Most importantly, $N_{\rm field}=p^r$ is neither original SAT
encoded length nor PCPP proof length.  No exponent in that normalization can
be inserted into (1)--(2) without a proved, charged length map.

\section{Reconciliation and barrier audit}
\textbf{Adopted:} conditional $K$ elimination; count-form and aggregate-volume
closure; cycle-level rather than per-edge contraction; charged implicit
access; legitimate shared setup and address deduplication.

\textbf{Rejected as sufficient:} existence of a compact representation;
distinct-address counts in place of executed calls; eventual recursion
termination without bounded same-scale rank; field-domain contraction without
SAT-length normalization; and a partial easy-family portfolio with unchanged
general fallback.

\textbf{Retired/overstated:} every same-scale edge blocks closure; every
logical same-scale child requires a fresh linear buffer; every line/seed
requires independent parent acquisition.  The original RECURCLOSE1 report's
historical unrestricted $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint is not used here.

The four accounting results relativize under consistent oracle replacement;
they are upper-bound composition lemmas, not a nonrelativizing or
nonalgebrizing separation hinge.  No runtime lower bound is inferred from
algebraic multiplicity, representation size, or the existence of a semantic
object.  Easy Horn, Krom, affine, bounded-treewidth, padding, COPY, RECOMP,
INTERP, and semantic-oracle controls therefore do not invalidate the
accounting identities, but they prevent promoting those identities into an
unrestricted hardness theorem.

\section{Strongest reusable handoff}
Do not seek another abstract recurrence.  Choose one actual wrapper phase and
publish its complete trace in original encoded SAT length:
\begin{enumerate}
\item exact root and reachable-state domains and satisfiability semantics;
\item every executed successor with multiplicity and encoded length;
\item total child volume and maximum child length;
\item discovery, failed attempts, acquisition, certification, bit generation,
repeated nested access, replay and combination time;
\item peak simultaneous state, including all ancestors, descriptors, indexes,
buffers, caches and shared evaluators; and
\item the CYCLEGAIN well-founded rank for any internal same-scale edge.
\end{enumerate}
If that trace proves (2) and the local/state budgets uniformly on the general
core, the recovered lemmas already provide recursive closure and eliminate
$K$.  Until then, (8) remains OPEN and P versus NP remains OPEN.
