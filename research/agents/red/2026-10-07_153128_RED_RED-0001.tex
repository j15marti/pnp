\begin{abstract}
This run attacks the preceding quantitative sparsification transfer audit.
The predecessor correctly showed that known sparsity bounds do not certify
power shrink at polynomial fanout.  A stronger exact obstruction holds in the
actual same-variable bounded-width solution-cover model: on an independent
2-CNF product, every satisfiable child in an exact union of solution sets must
retain linear occurrence and encoded size.  This retires IPZ-style
solution-cover sparsification as W2, not decision-only semantic wrappers.
The general-SAT inner transformation and P versus NP remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact obstruction}
Let
\[
 F_m=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(x_i\vee y_i).
\]
Its ordinary indexed-CNF length is $M_m=\Theta(m\log m)$.

\begin{lemma}[SPARSIFICATION-SOLUTION-COVER-GATE1]
Let $G$ be a satisfiable width-$k$ CNF over the variables of $F_m$.  If
$\sol(G)\subseteq\sol(F_m)$, then $G$ has at least
$\lceil m/k\rceil$ clauses and at least $m$ literal occurrences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose block $i$ contributes neither $x_i$ nor $y_i$ to $G$.  Choose a
satisfying assignment of $G$ and change only $x_i,y_i$ to zero.  Since neither
variable is read by $G$, the changed assignment still satisfies $G$; it
falsifies the $i$th clause of $F_m$.  This contradicts
$\sol(G)\subseteq\sol(F_m)$.  Hence every block contributes a mentioned
variable.  Each width-$k$ clause can mention variables from at most $k$
blocks, giving at least $\lceil m/k\rceil$ clauses and $m$ occurrences.
\end{proof}

If $\sol(F_m)=\bigcup_j\sol(G_j)$, union equality forces every
$\sol(G_j)\subseteq\sol(F_m)$; no cancellation is possible.  Since $F_m$ is
satisfiable, at least one useful child is satisfiable, and in fact every
satisfiable child obeys the lemma.  Mentioning variables from all $m$ blocks
costs $\Omega(m\log m)=\Omega(M_m)$ bits in the declared indexed encoding.
Thus no fixed $b<1$ child bound can hold on this family, irrespective of the
number of children and irrespective of the rate $C(k,\varepsilon)$.

This strengthens RSR-0001 without contradicting it.  The quantitative theorem
bound was insufficient; the exact model now also supplies a family-specific
output lower bound.  The scope is narrower than arbitrary decision reduction:
the implication uses preservation of all witnesses through a union of solution
sets.

\section{Bounded model and minimal counterexamples}
The command
\[
\texttt{timeout 30s python experiments/proof\_spines/
sparsification\_solution\_cover\_gate\_test.py}
\]
used seed 20261007 and an internal 25-second budget.  It completed 72,605
exact bitset checks in 2.599 seconds.  For widths two and three and up to five
or six independent blocks, respectively, it exhaustively enumerated every
CNF having fewer than $\lceil m/k\rceil$ clauses.  No satisfiable child had
solution set contained in $\sol(F_m)$.  Unit-clause children selecting $x_i=1$
for every block supplied positive controls.  In 256 seeded random cases, 149
satisfiable formulas omitted a block; resetting that block to $00$ preserved
the child and falsified the parent exactly as the proof predicts.

The smallest analytic case is $m=1$: a useful satisfiable child must mention
$x_1$ or $y_1$.  The asymptotic consequence uses independent copies so that
the occurrence lower bound grows linearly.  The finite computation is only a
regression check and is not used to infer the asymptotic theorem.

\section{Hostile-control and model audit}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Decision-only escape:} if correctness requires only
$\operatorname{SAT}(F)=\bigvee_j\operatorname{SAT}(G_j)$, a satisfiable
parent may map to a constant YES child.  The subset step fails.  Acquisition
then carries the burden, so this escape does not prove a wrapper.
\item[] \textbf{Easy-family control:} $F_m$ is monotone 2-CNF and trivially
satisfiable.  The result is representational, not a hardness inference.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products:} the family is itself an independent
product.  Repetition strengthens the occurrence lower bound rather than
creating computational hardness.
\item[] \textbf{Source carry and padding:} the lower bound counts the child's
own mentioned indexed variables; a parent pointer or removable padding cannot
be credited as a smaller ordinary-CNF child.
\item[] \textbf{Current-scale calls:} the sparsification construction makes
none.  The obstruction is W2, not a hidden invocation of the hypothetical
$M^K$ decider.
\item[] \textbf{Auxiliary and algebraic representations:} auxiliary
projection, circuits, polynomials, and unbounded clauses fall outside the
declared same-variable width-$k$ output model.  They may aggregate blocks but
need their own ordinary-SAT return and access proof.
\item[] \textbf{Oracle replacement:} a SAT oracle can emit a constant answer
child and violates witness preservation.  Hence the lemma relativizes as a
solution-cover statement and supplies no nonrelativizing hinge.
\item[] \textbf{Finite versus growing depth:} the obstruction holds at one
inner application.  It neither assumes nor refutes growing-depth scheduling;
no asymptotic claim is promoted from the finite enumeration.
\end{description}
