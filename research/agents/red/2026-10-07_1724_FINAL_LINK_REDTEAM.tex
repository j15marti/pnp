\paragraph{Status.}
The requested final link was not proved.  The strongest recovered candidate
splits into two genuinely different routes.  The older pointwise
SourceLocalization route has a one-child inequality that was already
superseded in the project archive by a global fanout correction.  A shared
intermediate $H$ can avoid that particular multiplication only if it performs
the semantic batch work, child access, and recursive composition, rather than
merely storing handles.  The present run proves exact restricted-model gates,
repairs two endpoint calculations, and builds a larger certified partial
portfolio.  It does not establish a general-SAT coverage theorem.  P versus NP
remains \textsc{Open}.

\section{Evidence recovered}
Fresh inspection covered the current wrapper manuscripts, proof-assembly and
resource audits, the PROVED/EXACT search archive, the exact-hash experiments,
and their executable ledgers.  The decisive historical records are
PROGRESS2-0142, PRF3-0079, BIG\_PICTURE2-0172, NEW\_UNSAT-0114,
LANG2-0063, and PRF3-0080.  They already distinguish a one-child cost from a
whole-cover cost, explicit incidence from shared semantic acquisition, and a
compact dependency representation from cheap aggregate use.  Personal-context
retrieval was unavailable, so this report makes no claim of an exhaustive
private-chat search.

The external literature was checked against primary sources.  Backdoor DNFs
give exact tautological covers and fixed-parameter detection for specified
tractable bases, not favorable unrestricted coverage \cite{ordyniak2021}.
Width-parameterized SAT gives algorithms once a small treewidth/pathwidth
representation is supplied, not a compiler that makes every CNF narrow
\cite{allender2014}.  The sparsification lemma emits $2^{\varepsilon n}$
sparse formulas for fixed $\varepsilon>0$, so it does not directly supply the
polynomial-fanout power shrink required here \cite{ipz2001}.

\section{Recovered Route B and the global correction}
Let $N$ be parent size, $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ the source size of one localized
child, $N^{a+o(1)}$ the number of required children, $\beta\log N$ the
boundary width, and $A_k$ a selected SAT algorithm whose time is
$S^{k+o(1)}$ on an $S$-bit ordinary input.

\subsection{One child is not the whole cover}
Pointwise filling of one child's truth table makes $N^{\beta+o(1)}$ calls to
$A_k$ on $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ inputs.  Its exponent is therefore
\[
 e_{\mathrm{one}}=\beta+\lambda k.
\]
This yields the old inequality
\[
 \lambda<\frac{C-\beta}{k}.
\]
It is exact for one child.  If the global combiner requires all
$N^{a+o(1)}$ children and no semantic work is shared across them, the correct
exponent is instead
\[
 \boxed{e_{\mathrm{global}}=a+\beta+\lambda k.}
\]
For the all-block schedule recovered in PRF3-0079, $a=1-\lambda$.  At
$k=2$ and $C=4/3$ this becomes
\[
 e_{\mathrm{global}}=1+\beta+\lambda<\frac43,
 \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
 \boxed{\lambda+\beta<\frac13.}
\]
Consequently $\lambda=1/2$ cannot meet the global schedule for any
$\beta\ge0$.  The old half-scale example passed only the one-child test.
Near $\beta=1/6$, the corrected requirement is $\lambda<1/6$; as
$\beta\to0$, it is $\lambda<1/3$.

The current COVER--SHRINK target $a=1/3$, $\lambda=2/3$ is also outside this
pointwise Route B:
\[
 a+\beta+2\lambda=\frac53+\beta>\frac43.
\]
This does not refute the recursive wrapper route.  It says that the particular
method of independently filling every child table with $A_2$ cannot be its
acquisition mechanism.

\subsection{Explicit coverage restores the same obstruction}
Suppose every one of $\Theta(N)$ source items must occur explicitly in at
least one child.  There are at most $N^{a+o(1)}$ children and each contains at
most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ source items.  Counting incidences gives
\[
 a+\lambda\ge1.
\]
Combining this with $a+\beta+2\lambda<4/3$ again yields
\[
 \lambda+\beta<\frac13.
\]
Thus incidence separators, explicit clause partitions, and ordinary source
replication do not recover the old half-scale point.  A surviving sparse-cover
route must use a semantic CoverageContract, not omit source items silently.

\section{Independent endpoint correction}
The ARC-style typed recurrence received during this audit is
\[
 t'\le \max\!\left\{r,\max_j(w_j+\lambda_jt)\right\},
 \qquad
 s'\le \max\!\left\{p,\max_j\lambda_js\right\}.
\]
For one recurrent type it has certified fixed point
\[
 C_* = \max\left\{r,\frac{w}{1-\lambda}\right\},
 \qquad S_*=p.
\]
For an all-block channel $w=1-\lambda+\beta+g$.  At
$\lambda=1/2$, $g=0$, and the explicit space floor $p\ge1/2$,
\[
 C_*=1+2\beta,qquad C_*+S_*\ge\frac32+2\beta.
\]
Against $\gamma=2\cos(\pi/7)=1.8019377358\ldots$, strict entry requires
\[
 \boxed{\beta<\frac{\gamma-3/2}{2}
 =0.1509688679\ldots.}
\]
Therefore $\beta<1/6$ is not sufficient for this endpoint.  The concrete
choice $\beta=1/7$ gives $C_*+S_*=25/14=1.785714\ldots<\gamma$.
Any acquisition overhead $g$ consumes the same margin, so the real condition
is $\beta+g<0.1509688679\ldots$ under these other assumptions.

This calculation is separate from the older FLvMV $C=4/3$,
$D_1(C)=2/C-C=1/6$ slice.  The two ledgers must not be mixed.

\section{What a shared compiler would actually have to prove}
A shared compiler can replace the independent term
$a+\beta+\lambda k$ by a joint exponent $h$, but only for operations it truly
performs.  Merely placing the original formula in global storage and issuing
short child handles does not batch the SAT computations.

\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Exact semantic batch.}  $H$ must recover every required
boundary answer or recursively valid child interface with total charged work,
not one selected trajectory.
\item[] \textbf{Counterfactual sufficiency.}  The same $H$ must answer all
branches needed by the exact combiner.  A trace of one execution is not a
branching trace.
\item[] \textbf{Ordinary-input transport.}  If a child is only a pointer into
$H$, calling an ordinary SAT algorithm on the pointer changes the problem to a
succinct or oracle-input version.  Either materialize a standard CNF and charge
it, or prove a model-matched random-access simulation.
\item[] \textbf{Aggregate source access.}  If each of $N^a$ children scans
$N^\lambda$ source cells, the actual source traffic is $N^{a+\lambda+o(1)}$.
Sharing storage alone does not remove those reads.
\item[] \textbf{Live space.}  A newly built $\Theta(N)$ index cannot be
reclassified as read-only input.  It must fit the endpoint space bound, be
streamed, or be regenerated with the time charged.
\item[] \textbf{Recursive closure.}  COMPOSE and PROJECT must not reintroduce
a parent-scale replay at every descendant.  All persistent maps, tables, and
normalizers remain live-state obligations.
\end{description}

These requirements are not a new list invented by this run.  They reconcile
the recovered BatchAcquire, SourceLocalization, STREAMCOMP, and shared-source
audits.  No inspected construction proves all of them on general CNF.

\section{Exact restricted-model gates}
The following results eliminate tempting but stronger formulations of the
missing theorem.  They do not establish a general SAT lower bound.

\subsection{Restriction solution-cover gate}
Let
\[
 F_m=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(x_i\vee y_i\vee z_i)
\]
with disjoint variable triples.  It has $7^m$ satisfying assignments.  Consider
a disjunctive restriction cover that preserves the full solution set, where
each restriction leaves at most $r$ clauses nonempty.  Every eliminated block
must have a literal fixed true, so that restriction covers at most four of the
seven satisfying local assignments in that block.  A remaining block covers
at most seven.  Hence one term covers at most
$4^{m-r}7^r$ satisfying assignments, and every exact cover satisfies
\[
 \boxed{q\ge (7/4)^{m-r}.}
\]
When the residual bit budget is $M^{2/3}$, $r=o(m)$ in the declared indexed
encoding, so the required $q$ is exponential in $m$, not $M^{1/3+o(1)}$.
This retires witness-covering restriction cubes as the universal final link.
It does not retire decision-only VALUE nodes: this particular monotone formula
is easy.

\subsection{Backdoor-DNF gate}
On $n$ variables, take one signed width-three clause on every variable triple.
For a term to leave a 2CNF residual, its assignment domain must hit every
triple.  Thus it fixes at least $n-2$ variables.  Each such term covers at most
four of the $2^n$ complete assignments.  Any tautological 2CNF-backdoor DNF
therefore has
\[
 \boxed{q\ge2^{n-2}.}
\]
The parent has $M=\Theta(n^3\log n)$ indexed bits, so this exceeds
$M^{1/3+o(1)}$.  The finite audit verified the inequality for $8\le n\le20$;
at $n=20$ the lower bound is $262{,}144$ terms while $M^{1/3}<28$.
This is compatible with the positive backdoor-DNF literature: the literature
does not assert a small DNF for every CNF.

\subsection{Dominance, width, and hashing}
Equal-width, duplicate-free families make syntactic subsumption delete nothing.
Petersen Tseitin formulas have connected primal structure, exact primal
pathwidth six in the tested encoding, and minimum strong-2CNF backdoor five.
These are finite controls against the current caps, not asymptotic hardness
claims.

The prior hash experiments remain decisive for merging.  Four-, six-, and
eight-hex-digit hash-only caches produced wrong answers on collision-focused
controls; exact replay produced none.  Increasing the digest changes candidate
collision work, not the number of future-congruence classes.  A fixed public
hash can be used safely only as an index unless a separately proved semantic
congruence theorem governs every bucket.

specialized XOR-system recognizer.  All reference answers are exhaustive on

\section{Why standard compression lower bounds do not close the question}
Dell and van Melkebeek show that an $O(n^{d-\varepsilon})$ communication
protocol for $n$-variable $d$-SAT would imply
$\mathrm{coNP}\subseteq\mathrm{NP}/\poly$ \cite{dell2014}.  Fortnow--Santhanam
and Drucker give strong OR/AND-compression consequences of the same general
kind \cite{fortnow2011,drucker2015}.  These results are valuable controls, but
they do not supply the missing contradiction here for two reasons.

\begin{description}
\item[] Under the proof's contradiction hypothesis $P=NP$, one already has
$\mathrm{coNP}\subseteq\mathrm{NP}/\poly$.  A theorem conditioned on the
opposite assumption cannot be imported as an obstruction inside that world.
\item[] The target decomposition can have total child volume
$N^{1/3}N^{2/3}=N$.  It is not automatically a strong compression whose total
output is independent of the number of encoded source items or source
instances.
\end{description}

Thus kernelization and communication results help type the claim; they do not
prove or disprove the surviving decision-only shared-$H$ construction.

\section{Weakest formulation not contradicted}
The surviving target should not require an exact witness union, ordinary
restriction cubes, a bounded-width representation for every input, or
materialized small CNFs.  A minimally sufficient form is the following.

\begin{quote}
For every ordinary CNF source $F$ of length $N$, a uniform deterministic
generator produces either a source-checkably certified VALUE answer or a
well-founded decision-only Boolean plan.  The plan has an endpoint-compatible
typed recurrence; every child is an ordinary SAT instance or a source-queryable
typed instance with a proved model-matched evaluator; every SUCCESS plan is
exact; the successful domains cover all inputs; and total generation,
counterfactual recovery, child/source access, combination, replay, failed
portfolio attempts, and live state obey one joint resource ledger.  No
parent-scale SAT call lies outside a mandatory contraction cycle.
\end{quote}

For the old TT Route B, this requires a genuine shared semantic compiler
$H$ with $h<4/3$ together with its interface and space inequalities.  For the
ARC wrapper, it requires a channel satisfying the corrected fixed-point
margin, such as the conditional half-scale point with $\beta+g<0.1509688$ and
all other costs included.  Constructing either object on unrestricted SAT is
the final open link.
