# Physical criticality forces unique-blocker rows in strict T5

## Theorem (PROVED)
For any finite-domain, physically clause-minimal UNSAT conjunction F(z,x_1,...,x_B)=AND_j F_j(z,x_j), with disjoint private x_j and each nonempty block containing physical clauses, define E_j(z):=EXISTS x_j F_j(z,x_j). Then for every block j there exists a z_j with E_j(z_j)=0 and E_i(z_j)=1 for all i !=j. Choose a clause e in F_j and a satisfying assignment of F\{e}; all other blocks extend at its shared z, while F_j cannot extend because F was UNSAT. Values z_j are pairwise distinct across j.

For a universal symbolic row cover (U_r,b_r) with union U_r=all z and FORALL z in U_r E_{b_r}(z)=0, b_r a **fixed** original block index, at least B rows are required. Each unique-blocker point z_j forces a row labelled j. Does not apply to a source-verifiable algorithm or circuit with dynamic label b(z) and charged internal branches.

## Explicit strict-C test family
Start with k copies of K6 minus the terminal edge, chained by sharing consecutive terminals t_0,...,t_k, each gadget with four internal vertices forming a K4 adjacent to both its terminals. Add closing edge t_0 t_k. Each gadget forces equal terminal colors under any proper 5-coloring, contradicting the closing edge. Every graph edge is indispensable, with explicit proper 5-coloring after its deletion; make each edge {u,v} a physical strict-C clause C(h_e,u,v) using a fresh head. Existentially eliminating h_e gives u!=v. N=19k+2 original C variables, M=14k+1 physical C clauses. Every physical C-clause deletion has a complete witness with precisely that C clause false, all other C clauses true.

Let all 5k+1 graph vertices be shared z, and group the M private heads into blocks of size floor(N^0.35). For all sufficiently large k, B=ceil(M/floor(N^0.35))<=N^0.65; each block has at most N^0.35 private variables, and B=Theta(N^0.65). Physical criticality implies every fixed-block symbolic cover requires R>=B, contradicting R<=N^0.195. For bit-encoded length L=Theta(N log N), grouping with L^0.35 gives B=Theta(L^0.65/log L)>L^0.195. The family is easy, its incidence girth is six, and its q=Theta(N) shared variables mean it does NOT satisfy the *low-row* promise. This is a structural certificate-grammar barrier, not general complexity hardness.
