## I. New abstract adaptive-orbit theorem (PROVED)
For q>=1, let K=(5^q+5)/10 be the number of orbits of q shared GF(5) colors under the ten strict-C automorphisms a->±a+t. Fix 1<=B<K. A block-refutability oracle D(z,j) is D5 invariant and covers z when some j has D(z,j)=1. For any B publicly identified essential orbits, construct a YES matrix with exactly one 1-entry in every row and one uniquely refutable orbit for every block. Assign every nonessential orbit an arbitrary label f(orbit) in [B]. For each nonessential orbit ω, construct a NO matrix by deleting its sole 1-entry on every row in ω. It is uncovered, D5-invariant, and retains all B essential unique-blocker orbits. Therefore a deterministic entry-query algorithm, even adaptive and with free essential-orbit knowledge, must on the covered matrix query at least K-B distinct **nonessential orbits**: otherwise its transcript is identical on an uncovered hidden-orbit alternative.

**Boundary:** This theorem concerns ABSTRACT D5-invariant matrices. The hidden NO matrices have NOT been shown realizable as physical strict-C formulas. It is NOT an unrestricted SAT lower bound or a hardness result for clause-critical promises. It falsifies the inference that symmetry and unique-blocker rows alone make limited adaptive orbit probes globally sound.

## III. Exact sound one-sided timeout lemma (PROVED)
One uniform deterministic algorithm that outputs an **independently sound verified** SAT witness within a known T(n) bound on **every SAT instance** (including construction/replay/output/clocking) becomes a complete deterministic decider: run for T steps and answer UNSAT on failure to verify a SAT witness. Dually, a uniform deterministic sound UNSAT-certificate specialist whose full verification finishes by T on **every UNSAT instance** becomes a complete decider by the opposite timeout. Neither needs a free polarity bit. The statement fails when wrong-path outputs may be unsound, a supplied nondeterministic path is uncharged, or the promise does not include arbitrary SAT. k nondeterministic bits can require 2^k checked paths; alternation requires all universal branches.
