# AND coverage projection and two-forest compatibility barrier (2026-11-27 PT)

Status: PROVED WITH SCOPE (exact projection, explicit reduction, restricted proof bounds and polynomial parity escape), COMPUTATIONAL checks below; independent mathematical review requested. P vs NP OPEN. Named dependency: can local polynomial Horn/affine/matching modules, even on separate forests, guarantee a polynomial aggregate residual? They cannot be assumed to do so.

## Exact coverage projection
For positive AND gates p_i=AND_{x in A_i} x, with nonempty supports, define U(p)=union of supports A_j with p_j=1. A phase word p is attainable iff p_i=1 exactly when A_i subset U(p). Necessity: true gates force every variable in U; a false gate cannot have its support contained there. Sufficiency: assign exactly U to 1. The attainable words are therefore the fixed points of a monotone extensive idempotent closure operator. For each i and each choice of a covering gate j_x containing x for every x in A_i, introduce the Horn implication AND_{x in A_i} p_{j_x} => p_i. Ignore tautologies; this finite Horn CNF is exactly the attainable output relation. For g gates of maximum arity k fixed, at most g^{k+1} such clauses, acquired polynomially, and at most g^3 for binary AND gates. Add explicit signed phase links p_i XOR p_j=b_ij. Gaussian/graph propagation identifies signed components; compatibility now equals the projected Horn CNF under those signed substitutions, not simply its affine hull. Positive links alone preserve Horn; arbitrary signed links need not. One can condition on all components that use both signs, then choose a common orientation for all remaining components, yielding a polynomial Horn terminal per phase branch. This is an upper bound, not necessary work.

## NP-completeness even on two separate forests
Private input path s-u-v-t with three gates a=u AND s, b=v AND t, c=u AND v has exact existential phase relation (NOT a OR NOT b OR c). Necessity: a=b=1 forces u=v=1. Sufficiency: u=a OR c, v=b OR c, s=a, t=b realizes every allowed phase triple. For an arbitrary source 3-CNF clause (l1 OR l2 OR l3), use a=NOT l1, b=NOT l2, c=l3. Pad shorter nonempty clauses by repeating last literal. Private paths for different clauses are disjoint, every input occurs in at most two gates. If explicit phase ports are desired, use 3m distinct outputs; link occurrences of each original variable by a path with XOR signs chosen to enforce signed consistency. This is an NP-complete decision language even though the input-support graph and the phase-link graph separately are disjoint paths; their union is not asserted to have small width. Both SAT/UNSAT and witness maps preserved. Assuming all n original variables occur, explicit-path representation has 7m variables, 3m AND constraints, 3m-n signed binary links. Direct signed-output CNF uses n'=n+4m variables, M=9m clauses, ell=21m literals. Explicit-path CNF uses 15m-2n clauses and 33m-4n literals. Encoding: unary n',M and widths, sign plus w=bit_length(n') identifier per literal; N=n'+2M+2+(w+2)ell. No claim of parsimonious counting.

## Exact minimally UNSAT core and resolution/tree bounds
Assume F minimally UNSAT, nonempty clauses of width<=3. Each gate p=i AND j is encoded by (-p OR i),(-p OR j),(p OR -i OR -j). For each clause the three key clauses (-a OR u),(-b OR v),(c OR -u OR -v) form core H. Two resolution steps recover the source clause. The core has n+2m used variables, 3m clauses, 7m literals, and is minimally UNSAT. In fact all 3m key clauses individually indispensable even in full G: take source assignment falsifying just the associated clause, so a=b=1,c=0. To violate just first key choose u=0,v=1,s=t=1; second choose u=1,v=0,s=t=1; third choose u=v=s=t=1. Other gadgets extend by the displayed assignment rule. Nonkey clauses are dispensable since H is already UNSAT. Thus full G is gate-minimal but not CNF-clause-minimal. All proofs use ordinary binary resolution inference nodes, not initial clauses. R(H)=R(F)+2m: upper derive every source clause then replay optimal proof. Lower substitute u=a,v=b (literal identification) in trimmed H DAG. 2m indispensable input clauses become tautologies. Taut derived nodes s and non-taut derived boundary nodes c with taut parent satisfy 2m+s<=2s+c by selecting outgoing edges on paths to empty; two taut parents cannot give non-taut resolvent under substitution. Taut nodes delete, boundary nodes collapse to other mapped parent or subclause, and surviving proof can be pruned to ordinary resolution. At least 2m inferences lost. For H, L(F)+2m<=L(H)<=3L(F): substitution simulates each query by a source variable or an already known value, with 2m indispensable key-clause leaves disappearing. Upper: attach two queries u,v to a base false-clause leaf to obtain three terminal leaves. For G same upper, lower L(G)>=3m and R(G)>=3m-1 from indispensable clauses. Therefore if L(F)=m,R(F)=m-1, exact full and core minima L=3m,R=3m-1. General full G exact R and L remain open. Depth upper D(F)+2; no general exact depth claim. Iterated ordinary clause splitting yields arbitrary large bases with L=m,R=m-1, starting at (x),(-x).

## Expander transfer: nonlinear full gates cannot bypass resolution width
Let F be odd-charge Tseitin CNF on a 2-vertex-connected cubic expander of v vertices, n=3v/2,m=4v. Full G n'=35v/2,M=36v,ell=84v, N=Theta(v log v). Every individual gate truth table has full affine hull. Binary envelope admits every source phase word by assigning all private inputs 1 (it includes the binary consequences p=>i,j only); hence no root contradiction. For strict width-3 source clauses, no unit or binary-only refutation exists via the full local binary envelope. Ordinary resolution width transfer: substitute u=a OR c,v=b OR c,s=a,t=b into every clause in a width-W G proof. Each substituted literal depends on at most 2 source variables. Translate each substituted clause to its canonical CNF (one clause for each falsifying assignment to its support). Initial gate clauses translate to tautologies or exactly the source clause (for central AND forward direction); all source clauses have width <=3. For a resolution inference, the translated parents semantically entail translated conclusion; union of their source supports has <=4W variables. Derive each canonical conclusion clause by full assignment resolution on this union restricted to falsify it, with weakening if necessary, and resolve away extra variables. All intermediate widths <=4W. Weakening can be removed by subclause pruning without increasing width. Thus W(F)<=max(3,4W(G)). Known Tseitin linear-width lower bound and Ben-Sasson/Wigderson width-size theorem imply R(G)=2^{Theta(v)} (upper exhaustive full tree; polynomial number of initial clauses). L(G) also exponential since a contradiction tree yields a tree-like refutation. Lower in bit length is 2^{Omega(N/log N)}, not a lower bound on uniform deterministic decisions. This transfer uses constant-locality Boolean substitution, not a free proof conversion preserving proof size.

## History-free escape and same execution resource bound
In direct signed-output encoding all source variable occurrence counts are multiples of 3, all private inputs degree 2 or 4 (occurrences counted with multiplicity). Renaming/complementing variables and permuting clauses preserves this signature. Infer phase set, remove it from co-occurrence graph, then acquire exact existential relation per private component using at most 2^7 complete words (4 private, at most 3 interface bits). For arbitrary inputs this projection is exact whenever the component-size checks pass, even if the inferred phase set differs from intended. Authenticate residual groups of ternary clauses as full XOR tables; permit <=1 exceptional table tested with at most 8 pin patterns. Gaussian elimination decides the rest; unsupported inputs return UNKNOWN. Clean compiled parity YES/NO instances, parity-charge flips, and one source-clause deletion before compilation all admitted. Individual key-clause deletion after compilation is SAT by the proved witness, but role recovery on that deletion model is not established. No claim for arbitrary logically equivalent CNFs. All acquisition and verification polynomial in original encoded N. Conservative deterministic single-tape bound O(N^6) time, O(N^2) peak cells including input, O(N^8) cumulative memory, on same sequential execution. Store explicit input, incidence records, projected tables and dense matrix, scan polynomial times; no exponential cache. Generic signed-phase kernel r number of independent phase bits, or h number of mixed-polarity components, yields poly(N) min(2^r,2^h) time, polynomial live space. For disconnected authenticated residuals sum costs; no universal logarithmic kernel theorem. On parity controls kernel zero after projection. Restricted exact R lower bounds coexist with polynomial decision upper bound.
