### Width-two obstruction theorem
For any simple cubic graph, initial UP has no units. Under zero or one pin no parity vertex has two pinned incident edges. Under two pins, disjoint edges do not propagate; if they meet, exactly one vertex forces its third edge. Its three other endpoints are distinct (simplicity), so none acquires two assigned incident edges. No contradiction, irrespective of charges. Therefore every candidate of width<=2 fails RUP and the deterministic closure has no possible first admission. Fixed-width2 lane returns UNKNOWN on both labels, not a semantic counterexample to unrestricted solving. Exhaustive probes exactly 1+2r^2; all 5833/18433/45001/294913 probes checked at k=3/4/5/8. Sum all vertex equations over GF(2): each edge cancels twice and odd total RHS gives 0=1. ### Growing resolution-width lower bound (companion CNF, unchanged encoding)
For a clause D in a resolution refutation with weakening, let mu(D) be the smallest number of complete vertex parity equations jointly implying it. Input clause mu<=1; empty mu=n because for any proper vertex subset, the corresponding incidence rows are independent over GF(2) in a connected graph (a nonempty proper subset has a nonempty cut). Resolution subadditivity mu(child)<=sum mu(parents), weakening cannot increase. First clause with mu>n/3 has mu<=2n/3. Choose a minimum supporting vertex set S. Every cut edge e between S and its complement must occur in D: otherwise, by minimality, an assignment satisfies S without the incident vertex v and falsifies D, and flipping e as needed satisfies v without changing any other S equation or D. Contradiction. Thus width(D)>=|delta(S)|.

Let M be number of mixed cells (some but not all 4 vertices in S). If M>=k/16, each mixed four-cycle contributes at least two cut edges, giving >=k/8. Otherwise the full-cell set and its complement each have at least k^2/4 cells, since S and complement each have >=4k^2/3 vertices. For a subset of toroidal k by k cells with each side size >=k^2/4: if >=k/4 horizontal rows are mixed, each has >=2 crossing edges, hence boundary>=k/2; otherwise both a pure inside row and pure outside row must exist (fewer than k/4 mixed rows cannot contain either whole side), yielding >=k vertical crossings. At most 4M boundary edges incident to mixed cells can fail to be original full-to-empty cut edges. Hence in this case |delta(S)|>=k/2-4M>k/4. Every refutation width >=k/8. For any fixed RUP width w, a derivation of a width<=w clause can be simulated by unit resolution under its negation followed by lifting with weakening of width<=max(3,w)+w. For sufficiently large k, fixed-width RUP cannot refute the companion chart, independently of enumeration order. This proves an unbounded-width obstruction on the stated encoding, not an unrestricted time lower bound. For the tested native charts the checked literal-bijection transfers the obstruction. A uniform theorem that every future k compiler projection has that chart has not been established; no claimed native all-k resolution-width bound.
