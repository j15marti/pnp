# Parity-hub matching and tensor residual hybrid (2026-11-26 PT)

Status: PROVED WITH SCOPE (elementary parity-lift matching and affine projection); COMPUTATIONAL (verification); CONDITIONAL (external sparse exact-counting theorem); OPEN (universal productive contraction and P vs NP). Independent review requested.

## Source and dependency
Explicit subcubic Boolean edge-CSP, ordered incidence ports and vertex truth tables, loopless edges each occurring at two endpoints. Pad unused ports with zeros. Input length N is the entire graph/table bit encoding. This is NP-complete by ordinary CNF clause OR relations connected to variable equality trees; equality fanout is not eliminated. Named dependency: reduce the size of the residual on which the exponential part of a cooperative decider is spent.

## Exact parity-lift construction
For a ternary relation R define S={(x,y,z,x XOR y XOR z):(x,y,z) in R}, a subset of the eight even four-bit words. Empty S immediately rejects. A nonempty S is realizable by a perfect-matching gadget iff it satisfies even symmetric exchange: for A,B in S and i in A symmetric-difference B, there exists a distinct j in that difference such that A toggled at i,j is in S. Necessity: the symmetric difference of perfect matchings of G-A and G-B has an alternating path from i to another difference endpoint j; flipping the path realizes the toggled set.

Sufficiency for four terminals: translate S by an even XOR mask so it contains 1111. If S contains a complementary pair, normalize using a member of that pair, so 0000 is also present. Let P be the graph whose edges are the allowed weight-two words. Exchange from 0000 to 1111 forces every vertex to belong to some edge; exchange in reverse forces every vertex to be omitted by some edge. Thus P has two disjoint edges: otherwise its edges form a star or triangle, violating one condition. If S has no complementary pair, the normalized S has no 0000 and P has no disjoint edges. Build a four-terminal graph whose edges are the complements of allowed weight-two words. Deleting externally matched terminals yields a perfect matching exactly for S, since deletion of all four always works, deletion of two requires the complementary edge, deletion of none requires disjoint edges. Undo translation by adding one pendant terminal per toggled port (external use of new terminal is opposite internal use of old terminal). Constant-size gadgets suffice.

Connect original edges and all extra parity ports to an even-parity hub: clique on v terminals plus one nonterminal if v odd. Its residual perfect matching is equivalent to even number of externally matched terminals. This parity condition is redundant by summing all original edge incidences. A perfect matching in the assembled graph is equivalent to a satisfying assignment of the CSP. Pinning p at any incompatible vertex leaves a parity-lift support with no complementary pair, hence a matchable gadget. If b incompatible vertices remain, stream all 2^b phase assignments and use deterministic polynomial-time general perfect matching. Both labels decided, no promise of UNSAT. O(v) gadget vertices, O(v^2) edges in simple hub implementation; polynomial acquisition, time and live space per phase, no exponential cache. Same-execution T<=N^C 2^b and S<=N^C, CM<=N^(2C)2^b for some fixed C (including deterministic TM simulation).

## Tensor hybrid
When all but d vertices impose a single full incident parity equation, affine projection retains J=edges incident to defects and c nonempty boundary components; isolated inconsistent components reject. Exact dimension q=|J|-c. The projected tables and disjoint parity equations encode into sparse width-three CNF with O(d) variables and clauses, using linear-size XOR chains. Conditional on the external sparse exact-counting theorem, T<=poly(N)2^{O(d/sqrt(log(d+2)))}. The theorem reported in the OpenAI Oct 25 manuscript is polynomial space; its publicly described Lean result states a weaker polylog factor and also polynomial space. No local Lean replay. Anthropic README states e(9K^16)<=0.9e(K)+O(1/K); iterating this envelope alone yields inverse-polylog decay, not a polynomial guarantee. Uniform cooperative portfolio with polynomial scheduling overhead can take the minimum of the affine enumeration, matching phase and tensor bounds per independent component, summing component costs. Sufficient tensor defect threshold O(log N sqrt(log log N)); not necessary, not a lower bound. Matching decision does not give exact counts.

## Next productive contraction and falsifiers
For any region with at most 3 dangling ports, acquire its exact feasible table by at most 8 boundary-pinned calls to the phase/matching solver. If region has O(log N) incompatible vertices, this is polynomial. Contract region to its table (preserve boundary identities and SAT equivalence); if all original parity lifts matchable, the resulting lift is also matchable by composing gadgets and an even-parity hub with an additional output parity port. Enumerate candidate cuts of <=3 edges polynomially; each successful merge removes >=1 vertex, hence polynomial aggregate acquisition under a fixed phase cap. No universal existence theorem. Prism graphs C_n x K_2, n>=4, have no cut of <=3 separating two regions each of >=2 vertices: rail transition counts are even, two nonconstant rails contribute >=4, one nonconstant rail with cut<=3 differs from constant rail at only one vertex. Equality-heavy prisms can defeat this cut/phase module despite simple Gaussian/propagation escape. A variable equality tree is a second obstruction to universal b bound. Neither is unrestricted hardness. Future: exact multiport matching-interface summaries and compatible collective fanout contractions; no hidden count oracle.
