If all satisfied, it is an exact residual witness; otherwise incrementally impose a's bits on the full residual with indexed unit propagation, in descending original occurrence count, then carrier ID. At conflict trace backward through reason clauses and decision assumptions. This yields original projected clause IDs C and a subguard G of the candidate such that UP on C+G refutes. Independently replay using reverse-order scanning before using. Activate every group intersecting C; optionally export not G as a width<=4 clause. ## Single-pass slicing lemma
Given a finite verified binary contradiction-search tree for F, for each leaf L extract a subset C_L of source clauses and a subguard G_L of its path such that unit propagation on C_L and G_L conflicts. Every total assignment follows exactly one leaf path, and satisfies its subguard. Hence the union of all C_L is UNSAT, and the collection of exclusions not G_L covers the entire original chart. Remove exact duplicate guards and guards implying a shorter excluded guard, retain a checked map from each leaf to a surviving covering guard. Replay the tree on the resulting source subset and on the guard clauses. This avoids enumerating all 2^r assignments or all subsets of guards: verification cost polynomial in supplied tree size and original N. It does not establish polynomial discovery or a universally short tree. Reverse replay handles early conflicts and branches whose pivot is forced; symbolic full leaf cover checked by explicit guard-subset map.
