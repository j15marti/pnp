## New scoped bridge
For distinct overlapping clauses x OR A and NOT x OR B, let M,N be their residual failure indicators in the Boolean ring GF(2)[y]/(y_i^2-y_i). Their parent failure polynomials are (1+x)M and xN. Multiplying by N and M respectively and XORing gives MN. If residual literals conflict, MN=0; otherwise MN is precisely the resolvent failure indicator. The intermediate square-free degree can be one higher than resolvent width. This identity explains how cross-scope inference can supply a lower-degree equation to an algebraic lane. It is ordinary resolution embedded in polynomial calculus, not new universal proof power.

For fixed d, the rows x^S h_i where S is outside the support of the canonical clause h_i and |S|+width(i)<=d span all such bounded-union-degree monomial products (multipliers inside support reduce to zero or the original indicator). A constant 1 in their span is a sound refutation. Gaussian elimination on sum_{j<=d} binom(r,j) features is uniform polynomial in original N for fixed d, with polynomial-size combination witness and bit arithmetic. Failure is only UNKNOWN for this single-epoch span, not for iterative PC, arbitrary-degree cancellation, other representations or all algorithms. Full proof of degree-bounded PC closure would need multiplication of derived rows and is not asserted.

## Transport scope audit
For one-tape fixed finite alphabet/control deterministic machine, cut crossing transcript consists of finite-control states (with direction determined by alternating crossings). If the right-side reconstruction uniquely determines independent uniform left-side payload of d bits conditioned on fixed right input, transcript must distinguish 2^d possibilities. Variable-length transcript coding yields expected crossing count Omega(d) (up to constant alphabet factor); summing across cuts charges time, not parallel span. This does NOT force payload reconstruction for a decision problem. A random-access indexed representation may retain permutation as addresses and retrieve entries without physical transport; even explicit permutation materialization is polynomial with O(r log r) bits and polynomial random-access cost. CM >= N*T only in a model retaining/charging all N input bits; standard work-space can exclude read-only input, so no unconditional CM lower bound.
