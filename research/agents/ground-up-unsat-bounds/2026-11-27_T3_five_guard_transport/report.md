## New transport lemma: affine input-guard rank/cover tradeoff
Let P be the r! permutations encoded as r log2(r) bits for power-of-two r, each value zero based. On the earlier permutation family, UNSAT iff the two maps are identical. A source-parameter affine guard G_i is A_i u+B_i v=c_i over F2, with arbitrary number of equations. Assume each guard is sound as a sufficient condition for UNSAT on all valid permutation pairs, not a guard on SAT assignment variables. D_i={p in P:(p,p) in G_i}. Then A_i restricted to D_i is injective: if p != q in D_i and A_i p=A_i q, substitution gives (q,p) in G_i, an off-diagonal SAT input, impossible. Same for B_i. Hence |D_i|<=2^min(rank A_i,rank B_i). Any cover of every diagonal by k such guards must satisfy sum_i 2^min(rank A_i,rank B_i)>=r!, so some guard has min cross-rank >= log2(r!)-log2(k). In original compiled N=Theta(r log r), polynomial k requires a linear cross-rank guard. With zero cross-rank on either side each guard can cover at most one diagonal. This is a representation-sensitive lower bound independent of physical distance, not an unrestricted time lower bound. A single high-rank equality guard is polynomially expressible and checkable by direct comparison. Arbitrary nonlinear exact sketches have the analogous injectivity/count bound if the guard depends only on the two local sketches. It does not apply to unrestricted source-dependent guards or free global preprocessing. The usual equality fooling-set argument remains the basis of fixed-control one-tape T=Omega(N^2) with D=Theta(N) intervening cuts and h=log2(r!)=Theta(N); physical input-inclusive CM=Omega(N^3) on same execution. Under fixed q sweeps with complete transmitted mutable state, q*s>=h and sum over cuts gives Omega(N^2) input-exclusive state CM. Random access, finite-precision exact comparison circuits and polynomial work can bypass transport. No new transition-level simulation of CHARGED-UNSAT-TRANSPORT1. No unrestricted lower bound.

Exact finite refinement: r=4, 24 codewords of 8 bits. Exhaust all 24^2=576 pairs; their nonzero XOR differences are exactly the 63 nonzero vectors in the six-dimensional space of even parity separately in each bit plane (explicit basis 5,10,17,34,65,130). Thus any injective linear sketch on all 24 permutations has kernel intersecting this space trivially and rank >=6, stronger than ceil(log2(24))=5. Keeping first three identifiers gives rank 6 and is injective. Single sound affine guard covering every diagonal also requires both ranks >=6. Checked all 576 pairs, exactly 24 accepted. This is a finite map-word audit, not a new compiled native benchmark.
