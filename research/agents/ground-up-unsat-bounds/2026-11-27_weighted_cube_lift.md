## Theorem
Let F be any minimally UNSAT CNF of distinct non-tautological clauses C_i on original variables X, m clauses. Let Z be disjoint from X and choose arbitrary possibly overlapping subsets S_i of Z, k_i=|S_i|. Replace C_i by every C_i OR D_{i,a}, where D_{i,a} is the clause on S_i falsified precisely at a in {0,1}^{k_i}. Denote this G. Its conjunction is logically F, and it is minimally UNSAT: a witness violating only C_i extends by selector pattern a to violate only the corresponding new clause. M=sum_i 2^{k_i}; n'=n+|union S_i|; literal count l'=sum_i 2^{k_i}(|C_i|+k_i). With unary n',M and width fields (terminated zeros), signs and fixed w=bit_length(n') identifier bits, N=n'+2M+2+(w+2)l'. Source language includes both SAT and UNSAT, and constant one private selector per source 3-CNF clause gives a linear literal-count NP-complete encoding.

Define L_w(F) as minimum sum of 2^{k_i} over leaves of an original-variable contradiction-search tree, each leaf labeled by a falsified C_i. Trees may continue querying after an original clause has been falsified; with unequal weights this matters. Define D_k(F) as minimum max over leaves of (original query depth+k_i). Exact L(G)=L_w(F) and D(G)=D_k(F). Upper bounds: attach a complete tree querying S_i to each leaf of the original optimal tree. Lower bound: fix all selectors uniformly at random, prune selector queries and retain an original-variable tree. Each leaf of G labeled by group i survives with probability at most 2^{-k_i}, since it fixes every selector of S_i (additional fixed selector queries can only reduce probability). Its expected contribution to the weighted restricted tree is <=1. Every restriction has weight >=L_w. For depth, every surviving leaf has original depth+k_i<=D(G); fix any selector assignment. This works with arbitrary shared selector sets. Uniform k yields L(G)=2^k L(F), D(G)=D(F)+k. Weighted recurrence at partial original assignment alpha is min of all available terminal weights and min_j (W(alpha,j=0)+W(alpha,j=1)); depth analogue uses min terminal k_i and 1+min_j max of children. Independent optimization objectives.

For ordinary binary resolution, counting inference nodes only, exact R(G)=R(F)+M-m. Upper: resolve each cube to C_i in 2^{k_i}-1 steps. Lower: trim arbitrary refutation DAG to ancestors of empty; all M inputs used by minimality. Fix all selectors. Exactly M-m input clauses become satisfied. Let s derived nodes become satisfied and c unsatisfied derived nodes have a satisfied parent. Each satisfied node has a path to the empty clause and thus an outgoing edge to a satisfied node or such a boundary node. At most 2s incoming edges to satisfied derived nodes; at most c incoming edges from satisfied nodes to unsatisfied boundary nodes (two satisfied premises cannot resolve to an unsatisfied clause: their only possible satisfying literals would be opposite pivot signs, impossible). Thus M-m+s<=2s+c. Restriction deletes or collapses at least s+c>=M-m inference nodes, yielding a resolution refutation of F with at most R(G)-(M-m) inferences, allowing standard subclause pruning instead of weakening. Hence equality. Uniform k gives additive m(2^k-1), not multiplicative. No shortest DAG optimizer executed; this is symbolic, and base exact R is unknown for K4.

Reverse construction: after fixing some selectors, each group becomes its same cube on unfixed S_i; exact theorem holds if original F stays minimally UNSAT. If original variables also fixed, tree statements hold for any UNSAT restricted F (possibly duplicate or empty clauses, labels retained); resolution identity requires minimality.

## Masked expander application and polynomial recognition
For 2-vertex-connected cubic expander family with odd Tseitin charge, original n=3v/2, m=4v; minimally UNSAT by extending a local forbidden word through the connected graph minus that vertex. One private selector per original clause produces n'=11v/2, M=8v, l'=32v, N=n'+2M+2+(bit_length(n')+2)l'. All individual 4-clauses have full affine hull and all binary projections full. Even grouping identical supports gives only a single ternary OR clause extended by a free selector, whose affine hull and binary projections remain full. A local unary/binary/affine hull pass stalls. Width/size lower bound for base bounded-degree expanding Tseitin (Ben-Sasson/Wigderson 2001) transfers exactly via R(G)=R(F)+4v, giving 2^{Omega(v)} minimum resolution inference growth, also tree leaf lower bound; this is 2^{Omega(N/log N)}, not unrestricted runtime. Upper 2^{O(v)} by full tree, so resolution exponential in v. No expansion proof or external tensor theorem needed for finite prism tests; prisms not claimed expanders.

History-free scoped decider: resolve complementary width-four pairs sharing the other three literals, deleting the pair and retaining the resolvent, an exact identity. With private selectors and cubic simple graph parity groups, only the intended pairs are possible: selector occurs only in its two clauses, distinct original constraints have no identical triple, and no original variable can be the complementary pivot with same remaining private selector. On a single-clause-deletion neighbor one width-four clause remains; existentially eliminate its singleton selector. Group remaining ternary clauses by support, authenticate full parity truth tables and at most one exceptional table. Enumerate at most eight words of exceptional table and solve the rest by Gaussian elimination, which is sound and complete for admitted residuals. Return UNKNOWN on unsupported structure, not NO. Each pair replacement strictly decreases clause count; scanning and grouping are polynomial in original bit length N. Uniform deterministic single-tape implementation conservative O(N^6) time, O(N^2) cells including input, O(N^8) cumulative memory, same execution; repeated tape scans, O(N^2) matrix storage and at most eight elimination calls suffice. Fixed degree and at most one exceptional table only. General exact pair replacement is equivalence-preserving even if the greedy choice is not intended, but arbitrary shared masks need not normalize to a parity instance. Solver prototype verifies decisions, not emitted SAT or algebraic proof witnesses; theoretical witness reconstruction follows pure selector assignment and Gaussian solution.
