## New coupled family (no short odd core)
Let k>=4 be even, B the k by k square grid plus a chord joining opposite corners. B is triangle-free and contains an odd cycle, with odd girth exactly 2k-1: any odd cycle must use the chord, every grid path between endpoints has length >=2k-2, and a shortest path attains this. Let H=M(B), the Mycielskian: original vertices, shadow u' adjacent to each neighbor of u, and apex adjacent to all shadows. H triangle-free (directly checked for all generated cases). Standard one-hot 3-color CNF: one positive triple and three negative pairs at each vertex; for each edge, three negative same-color pairs. Relabel vertices deterministically before source construction. H has 2k^2+1 vertices, 6k^2+3 Boolean variables. H not 3-colorable: assume a 3-coloring, call apex color 3. Every shadow has color 1 or 2. Recolor each original of color3 using its shadow's color, leaving all others. No adjacent originals had color3; for an edge incident to a recolored vertex, its shadow is adjacent to the other original. Thus a 2-coloring of B, contradiction. This is the classical Mycielski construction, applied to a growing odd-girth base. A short-chord pilot used a fixed 5-cycle and is retained as a separate diagnostic; not evidence for growing core. Long-chord cases primary.

Growing-core theorem: for any UNSAT subset of source clauses, project each original/shadow vertex appearing in its support to the corresponding base vertex, ignoring apex. If the induced base graph on these projected vertices were bipartite, color base and shadows by the two colors, apex by the third; this satisfies all clauses of the subset (indeed the induced lifted graph). Therefore projected support contains an odd cycle of B and has at least 2k-1 vertices. Conversely the full lifted clauses of a shortest odd cycle suffice, so minimum projected vertex support exactly 2k-1. This is not a proof-length lower bound; the certificate is small and easy. The full base grid also has unbounded treewidth (grid subgraph); no universal bounded-width exact elimination claim follows.

Near YES changes precisely ONE sign: flip the apex's color3 literal in the binary exclusion for the shadow of the exceptional corner. The base grid two-coloring with that one corner recolored color3 satisfies the chord; copy to shadows and give apex color3. All original constraints except that one edge-color exclusion hold; modified clause is satisfied. Exact raw witnesses independently checked for k4,6,8,12. Identical ordered unsigned clause scopes, opposite labels, one sign changed. This falsifies scope-only compression regardless of size.

## Four scoped stall mechanisms and cheap bypass
1. Width<=2 RUP no new first admission on triangle-free graph one-hot encoding. Under two negative pins at different vertices no unit; same vertex forces its third color. A single positive pin colors its vertex, excludes other colors there and its own color at neighbors. A second negative pin may color a neighboring vertex with another color; otherwise no new color except direct source conflict. For two positive pins, different colors at distinct vertices force all common neighbors to the third color. Those common neighbors are independent, and a further vertex adjacent to one cannot also neighbor either initial vertex without a triangle. Thus no further colored vertex from distinct-color adjacency. Same-color pins force no further colors. Only conflicts are direct original binary clauses; all width<=2 novel candidates fail RUP.
