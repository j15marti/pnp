## Construction and exact backdoors
For simple 2-vertex-connected cubic G, v vertices and 3v/2 edges, odd total charge Tseitin CNF has 4v clauses and 12v literals. Every single clause deletion is SAT: fix the forbidden local pattern at its vertex, then solve the remaining connected incidence system; the required residual charge sum is even. Paired monotone transform replaces positive x_i by -u_i, negative x_i by -v_i and adds (u_i or v_i). Every satisfying assignment can be lowered to complementary pairs. Clause minimality transfers: deleting a negative clause uses the corresponding source witness; deleting a pair uses a witness for any source clause containing that variable, setting both indicators zero. Other negative clauses stay satisfied. Size 3v variables, 11v/2 clauses, 15v literals. Bit encoding unary n,m and widths, signed fixed-width IDs: N=Theta(v log v).

Define strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor (no semantic simplification beyond restriction): exactly a hitting set of ternary supports. Original requires at least one incident edge at each vertex; paired requires at least two indicators at each vertex (each indicator occurs in only two of four local clauses). One indicator hits at most two vertices. Given a perfect matching, exact optima v/2 and v respectively, achieved by matching edges or both their indicators. Exhaustive fixed-backdoor enumeration has 2^(v/2) or 2^v assignments; this is not a runtime lower bound for a solver allowing early stopping or compression. Prism family arbitrary size; bounded-degree high-treewidth expanders give independent restricted regular-resolution bounds (Itsykson/Riazanov/Smirnov SAT2022). Affine recognition via pair recovery and grouping supports yields polynomial complete solution for both charge parities, plus explicit mixed-table extension below.

## Defect projection
Input: connected graph, degree <=3, one Boolean edge variable, parity at vertices outside defect set D and arbitrary explicit local allowed tables on D. Let J be edges incident to D, and c number of components of G-D with nonempty boundary. Sum charges in each such component C, giving one equation XOR(boundary(C))=XOR(b_v,v in C). Empty-boundary component with odd charge is immediate UNSAT. Conversely every boundary assignment satisfying these equations extends to interior edges, by spanning-tree elimination in each component. Nonempty boundary sets are disjoint, hence independent. Thus exact dimension q=|J|-c, and exactly 2^q assignments extend to good regions, before defect tables are checked. For cubic G, q=3|D|-|E(G[D])|-c. Complete exact algorithm: acquire supports/tables by grouping clauses; recognize parity tables; contract good components; enumerate free boundary bits choosing one pivot per component equation; check defect tables, reconstruct interior using trees. No original construction history required within authenticated graph-table class. q=0 in consistent all-parity case (odd total is detected before enumeration). A single removed local clause on original 2-connected odd graph gives SAT. General arbitrary subcubic local tables are NP-complete: variable copies via equality trees and OR clause nodes, one edge per occurrence; optional NOT is encoded in clause table. Scope: parameterized upper bound, not optimal computation or unrestricted lower bound. Separate components of contracted factor graph can be solved separately at aggregate sum of 2^q_i, not product. Single-tape conservative implementation T=O(N^5 (1+sum_i 2^q_i)), S=O(N^2), CM=O(N^7 (1+sum_i 2^q_i)), same execution, streaming candidates. Acquisition polynomial, not paid oracle. q=O(log N) sufficient; large q not necessary exponential computation.
