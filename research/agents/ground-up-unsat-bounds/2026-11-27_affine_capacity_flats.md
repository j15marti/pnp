## Exact boundary projection
Write boundary image bits as affine forms of independent coordinates y. Normalize remaining binary block images: singleton pins, two correlated words -> shifted equality wire, two words differing in one coordinate -> pin/free, four words -> two private resources, three words -> shifted capacity-one edge (00,01,10). Contract correlated components by XOR of charges and of boundary forms, keeping preimages. For every boundary word, solve quotient resource graph by previous tree criterion: component fails iff tree with no private resource and every quotient vertex odd. Proof: odd demands require distinct resources; rooted spanning tree from an even vertex/private resource or directed cycle with trees attached supplies all odd vertices otherwise. Correlated wire values lift via spanning tree; block preimages lift independently. Consequently the entire exact forbidden boundary set is a union of affine flats F_C, one per tree/no-private component. Its equations are all adjusted charges equal one, A_v y=b_v for v in C. Inconsistent equation sets contribute empty flats, removed by elimination. Cyclic or private-resource components impose no boundary restriction. This is a full support identity, not just a marginal or count. Polynomial acquisition of equations and Gaussian bases.

## Exact flat coalescence and rank certificate
In any current affine ambient space, put each forbidden flat into canonical reduced row form modulo the ambient equations, discard empty ones and duplicates. A rank-zero nonempty flat covers the ambient, proving NO. Rank-one flat forces its complementary equation. For two distinct parallel flats with same coefficient row space, write their canonical systems Ay=a and Ay=b. Their union is exactly Ay in {a,b}; over GF2 the two RHS values form an affine line. Eliminate one pivot equation and add it to the other equations whose RHS differences are one. The resulting flat has rank r-1. This is exact, reduces flat count by one, no enumeration, no requirement that equations be identical syntactic clauses. Iterate with unit closure: polynomial number of merges and independent affine pins. In the ambient space, a nonempty codim-r flat occupies fraction 2^-r. If sum_i 2^-r_i <1, SAT follows, and a deterministic boundary word can be acquired by conditional expectation: fix one coordinate at a time choosing a consistent half with total forbidden fraction <1. Exact dyadic integer arithmetic of O(N) bits. This is a union-bound upper estimate, not an equality for overlapping flats.

Stronger sufficient parameter theorem (symbolic, not benchmarked): choose a subspace U of linear forms of dimension k, with designated heavy flats having row spaces W_i subset U. For every other flat, after any consistent assignment of U, its intersection with the fiber is empty or has relative codim dim(W_i)-dim(W_i intersection U). If sum over other flats of 2^-(dim(W_i)-dim(W_i intersection U)) <1, every fiber avoiding all heavy flats contains an allowed boundary word. Thus only enumerate 2^k syndromes and test the heavy flats, then conditional expectation constructs a witness. Acquire U by choosing a threshold and taking the span of selected row spaces, or any polynomially generated candidate; verify the inequality via Gaussian ranks. No universal existence claim. Branch fallback on an independent parity form if merge/unit/union bound stalls. Each split decreases ambient dimension, polynomial work per node; DFS bound poly(N)2^k for max actual branching depth k. With b nonaffine boundary blocks, sum the costs over 4^b physical assignments, not independent endpoint choices. Independent residual components use a sum, not a product. k=O(log N) sufficient; k=omega(log N) only makes explicit full enumeration superpolynomial if all leaves are traversed, not arbitrary work LB.
