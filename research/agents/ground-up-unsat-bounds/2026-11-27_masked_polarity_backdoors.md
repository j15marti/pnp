## Definitions and source
Simple connected cubic graph G, v vertices, e=3v/2 edges, binary charges b with odd sum, direct Tseitin CNF F: four ternary clauses per vertex. For minimality assume G minus any vertex connected (e.g. prisms and 2-connected cubic expanders). Split each original clause C into (C OR z_C),(C OR NOT z_C) with a distinct private selector. Call masked CNF H. H has n=11v/2 variables, m=8v clauses, ell=32v literal occurrences. Encoding: unary n,m and individual widths (k ones then zero); each literal w=bit_length(n) identifier bits then sign bit. N=n+2m+2+(w+2)ell=Theta(v log v). Lists may contain repeated clauses; sign last is material to final-bit test. No simplification other than truth assignment and deletion of satisfied clauses/false literals in backdoor definition. In particular an empty clause does not erase all other clauses. Strong K-backdoor B iff for EVERY assignment to B the resulting syntactic CNF belongs to K. RH=renamable Horn, orientation may depend on branch. K can be union RH or 2-CNF. Not deletion backdoors, not adaptive propagation, not semantic equivalence classes. Minimum is over all variable sets including private selectors.

## Exact theorem
Let nu be maximum matching size of G; p be minimum number of vertex-disjoint paths (isolated vertices allowed) covering all v vertices. For odd-charge F:
B_2(F)=v-nu; B_RH(F)=v/2+p.
For H (both charge parities):
B_2(H)=B_(2 union RH)(H)=e-nu; B_RH(H)=e.
All original edges are the unique optimal RH set of H. (The correction is explicit here to avoid conflating selected edge cover and remaining matching.)

Proof raw 2-CNF: each vertex must lose at least one incident variable, hence minimum edge cover v-nu. Raw RH: a residual degree3 parity table cannot be made Horn by any orientation (some clause has >=2 positives). Degree2 residual table contains complementary binary clauses; RH iff after renaming they are equality (one positive per clause), equivalently a linear signed condition on the two renaming bits. Degree<=1 places no restriction on renaming. Thus unpinned edges must form maximum-degree-two graph. Any cycle that is a proper subset of connected G has a pinned boundary edge, whose assignment can make the sum of residual charges on the cycle odd; a spanning cycle has original odd total charge. In either case some branch cannot be renamed Horn. Conversely if unpinned edges are a linear forest every branch's equality/inequality conditions on renaming bits are consistent by propagation along paths. A spanning path cover with p paths has v-p edges, proving e-(v-p)=v/2+p. For even charge and Hamiltonian G the Hamiltonian cycle is allowed and achieves v/2, but no general even-charge RH formula needed.

Masked case: partition chosen pins into original edges S and private selectors Z. Residual degree d at vertex is number of unpinned original incident edges. d=3 cannot yield either class: deleting selector literals reveals complete ternary parity table (2-CNF fails by width; RH fails by hereditary deletion of literals). If d=2, two complementary binary base clauses survive each original pin assignment. If either corresponding selector remains free, its two opposite-signed copies force both base literals negative under ANY Horn renaming. The complementary binary clause then has two positives, impossible even if its selector is pinned. Also width3 remains, so neither 2-CNF nor RH holds. Over both values of the pinned original edge all four original local clauses become active, requiring all four selectors at every residual degree2 vertex. Let t be number of such vertices, f number of unpinned edges. Removing at most t unpinned edges gives a matching, so f<=nu+t. Therefore |S|+|Z|>=e-f+4t>=e-nu+3t. Leaving a maximum matching unpinned and pinning only other original edges gives 2-CNF for every branch; proves union and 2-CNF minima. For RH additionally each isolated unpinned edge joins two residual-degree1 vertices. Each endpoint's two other original pins determine its residual unit clause (two possible active selectors per unit sign). Their pin sets are disjoint by simplicity. For either conflicting unit-sign pair, at least one full two-selector group must be pinned: otherwise a branch activates two opposite units each with free mask, and both units cannot be oriented negative. The two opposite conflict directions use disjoint groups, so at least four selectors per isolated unpinned edge. If a is number of isolated unpinned edges, then f<=a+2t (non-isolated path with l>=2 edges has l-1 degree2 vertices; cycles have equal counts). Thus cost>=e-f+4t+4a>=e+2t+3a, strictly above e if f>0. All original edges pinned leaves only mask units, so unique optimum e. For Hamiltonian cubic graphs: raw 2=v/2, raw RH=v/2+1, masked 2 or union=v, masked RH=3v/2. Full explicit assignment enumeration to even the optimal mixed-class backdoor requires 2^v branches, not an all-algorithm lower bound. This also strengthens prior cubic backdoor work.

## Minimality, exact query trees and DAGs

For any forbidden local assignment at vertex u, fix its edges accordingly; remaining parity system has even total adjusted charge and connected G-u, so solve by spanning tree. Each original clause has a unique-violation witness. Extend with its private mask to violate exactly either split copy. H is minimally UNSAT. SAT charge neighbors and deletion neighbors exist.

Depth lower follows by taking a deepest surviving path in restricted tree, adding at least one original selector query. Upper attach one private selector query at each F leaf. Each satisfied input/derived node has a path to empty and an outgoing edge to one of these two groups; satisfied nodes have at most two incoming edges, boundary nodes at most one satisfied parent. Thus m_F+s<=2s+c; remove/collapse at least m_F inferences under restriction (subclause pruning if necessary). Remaining proof refutes F. This independently reconstructs earlier uniform cube lift. ## Acquired polynomial escape and uniform lower bound
Authenticate each width4 clause with distinct identifiers; low-occurrence private selector appears in exactly two complementary clauses with identical three-literal remainder. Replace pair by remainder. Permit at most one singleton or identical same-signed pair: existentially drop it using the private variable. Group recovered ternary clauses by support, check all eight words. Authenticate parity tables; permit one exceptional table, test at most eight permitted words via Gaussian elimination. Correct on all accepted inputs, UNKNOWN on unsupported patterns. This recognizes clean odd/even charges, arbitrary renaming/complementation and clause permutations, single-copy deletion, and same-sign duplicate perturbation. No history needed; not arbitrary logically equivalent CNFs. Track row combination to certify each inconsistent branch and back-substitute/replay SAT assignments on original clauses. At most eight constant-width local pins, no growing exhaustive kernel. Dense matrices incl trace O(N^2) bits; O(N^3) bit operations, each paid by conservative O(N^2) full tape scan plus indexing overhead; uniform single-tape T=O(N^6), peak S=O(N^2), CM=O(N^8) on same execution including input and verification.

New actual machine lower bound (linear only): order any masked UNSAT input so a mask literal is the final sign bit. Flip only that bit so its clause duplicates the opposite-signed partner. Minimality gives SAT (equivalent to removing one copy). Both encodings valid, same N, identical first N-1 bits. Any deterministic sequential-tape decider correct on both must visit last input cell on the UNSAT input; T>=N-1 when head begins at first cell. With space including entire input, CM=Omega(N^2); alternatively visited contiguous cells grow along traversal. No work-space-only lower bound claimed. Cannot assume arbitrary random access has same lower bound, nor extrapolate to superpolynomial. This does not conflict with polynomial recognizer.
