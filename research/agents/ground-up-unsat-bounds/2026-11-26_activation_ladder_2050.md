# Exact additive activation-ladder theorem (2026-11-26 Pacific)
Status: PROVED/EXACT in binary resolution and variable-query contradiction search; independently enumerated small cases, peer review pending. P vs NP OPEN.
For a clause-minimal UNSAT CNF F with m distinct non-tautological clauses and n used variables, set G=(AND_i(C_i OR z_i)) AND !z1 AND (AND_{i=2..m}(!zi OR z_{i-1})), with fresh variables. SAT iff F SAT, and MUS iff F MUS: removing a guarded clause uses a deletion witness with all z=0; removing pin allows all z=1; removing link allows prefix z=0 and suffix z=1, and the proper prefix of F is SAT. Conversely a redundant original clause remains redundant.
Size: n+m variables, 2m clauses, ell+3m-1 literal occurrences. Let L count contradiction-search leaves and R count binary resolution inferences (no extension, no free weakening). Exact L(G)=L(F)+m: query z in order, first 1 identifies violated activation clause, all zero recurse; restriction all z=0 gives a tree for F and each indispensable activation clause requires a distinct discarded leaf. Exact R(G)=R(F)+m: lift every proof clause with union of z guards on contributing premises, then resolve final disjunction of all z against links descending and pin, m steps. Lower bound: trim proof to ancestors of empty. Under z=0, m input nodes are satisfied, s derived nodes satisfied, c crossing inferences have satisfied parent and unsatisfied result. Every satisfied node has outgoing edge to satisfied node or crossing; crossing has at most one satisfied parent. Hence m+s <=2s+c, so s+c>=m. Standard restriction of resolution collapses crossing steps, with remaining unsatisfied nodes mapped to subclauses of restricted clauses, giving refutation with at most R(G)-s-c steps. No tautological premises needed. Thus exact. For any subset of t activators freed with others fixed zero, remaining activation paths are separately pinned, same proof yields additive t. Iterating from F0=(x)&(!x), m_j=2^{j+1}, L_j=m_j, R_j=m_j-1, n_j=m_j-1. Exponential in construction rounds is linear in clauses, not encoded input size.
Uniform algorithm: exhaustive unit propagation (including without knowledge of ladder) forces all z=0 and returns equivalent residual F; correct on arbitrary CNF, including SAT neighbors. For explicit N-bit input, using conservative repeated full scans, comparisons and copying on one tape, preprocessing O(N^4) time, O(N^2) peak including input, CM O(N^6); cost of residual solver extra on same execution, no universal bound. Exact witness counts for ladder deletion are not claimed. General wrapped class is NP-complete by polytime reduction from CNF-SAT even though guard variables are a zero-branch kernel. An unrestricted exponential lower bound does not follow.
Tests: exact memoized clause-level variable-query recurrence and SAT deletion checks on six bases and every suffix restriction; see companion raw output and code. Prior ring values independently verified for (n,k)=(3,1),(4,1),(3,2),(4,2),(3,3). Exact R for simple split bases follows from m-1 lower bound and splitting proof; ordinary rings have m-1 proof by resolving along the two implication chains. No shortest-DAG program or proof assistant independently executed.
Falsifiers: SAT base must stay SAT; deleting any clause from MUS must yield SAT; tree count must match m addition; exponential restricted proof cost must not transfer to a uniform decider. Gates: DISJ not applicable (no communication reduction); relativization/algebrization/Natural Proofs no unrestricted claim; DTISP no simultaneous lower bound; deterministic configurations not used as lower bounds.
