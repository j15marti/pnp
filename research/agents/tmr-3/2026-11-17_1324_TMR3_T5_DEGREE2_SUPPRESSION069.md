For **every** finite explicitly encoded directed graph G=(V,E), with vertices V={0,...,n-1}, compute the exact original-vertex homomorphism count to T5, where R(x,y) iff y-x is 1 or 3 modulo 5. The same run parses and authenticates the source, eliminates degree-at-most-two vertices over exact five-coordinate integer factors, enumerates only a residual core, and outputs the count and the decision bit.

Falsifiers specified before primary-source review: (F1) reversing a directed arc uses W(d) instead of W(-d); (F2) two genuine parallel source paths are combined by convolution instead of pointwise multiplication; (F3) deg-zero or deg-one elimination loses a factor five or a weighted extension; (F4) generated factors require exponential-precision integers or physical state not charged by the ledger. Loop/opposite arcs, empty and disconnected graphs, variable ordering, reversal isomorphism, dense/sparse and high-cycle-rank low-core graphs are controls.

The source can be a canonical binary word containing unary declarations of n and m followed by fixed-width endpoint records, with N>=n+m. The readable executable uses ASCII `n m` plus exactly m ordered pairs; its protective prototype integer caps are not part of the mathematical algorithm. Parsing, endpoint bounds, duplicate-arc removal and mandatory rejection of malformed words are charged. Duplicate identical arcs preserve counts. A self-loop or a pair of opposite arcs has no homomorphism into the loopless asymmetric T5.

## The theorem

Let H be the underlying simple undirected support after source normalization. First peel all vertices of degree at most one to obtain the canonical 2-core H_2. Let h be the number of H_2 vertices of degree at least three (degrees counted in H_2). There is one uniform deterministic blank-start locally headed multitape algorithm with

```
#Hom(G,T5) computed EXACTLY, including counts on UNSAT inputs,
T(N) = O((N+1)^8 (1+5^h)),
S_peak(N) = O((N+1)^3) writable bits
```

for a conservative explicit physical upper envelope. The actual number k of surviving vertices after all elimination satisfies k<=h, and if the surviving weighted support has q nonempty components, component-shift normalization enumerates only 5^(k-q) residual assignments. When no vertices remain, the counting stage is polynomial. These are UPPER bounds on a specifically constructed algorithm, not a lower bound on all algorithms or a fixed-parameter polynomial dependence on h.

### Invariant and exact elimination

Represent each normalized directed original arc (u,v) with the five-entry table W_ab(d), where a=min(u,v), b=max(u,v), d=x_b-x_a mod5. For u=a the entries are 1 for d in {1,3}; for u=b they are 1 when -d in {1,3}. All weights are nonnegative integers. At all times, the current partition function is

```
Z(G) = scalar * sum_{x:active -> Z5} product_{a<b} W_ab(x_b-x_a).
```

Unlisted factors are 1. This is an equality of exact labelled assignment counts, not just a decision-equivalence claim. Every possible unary factor generated by eliminating a component attached at one boundary vertex is constant by translation symmetry.

For degree zero v, sum_{x_v}1=5, so multiply scalar by five and delete v.

For degree one v with neighbour u, sum_{x_v} W_uv(x_v-x_u)=sum_{d=0}^4 W_uv(d), independent of x_u; multiply scalar by that exact integer and remove v and its factor.

For degree two v with distinct neighbours a,b (choose a<b), orient existing tables as F(s)=W_av(x_v-x_a=s) and G(t)=W_vb(x_b-x_v=t). Define the **five-term cyclic convolution**

```
H(d) = sum_{s=0}^4 F(s) G((d-s) mod 5).
```

The sum over x_v at fixed x_a,x_b equals H(x_b-x_a). Remove v and its two factors. If factor P_ab already connects a,b, replace it by **pointwise** P_ab(d)H(d), not another convolution; genuine multiple paths are simultaneous constraints and must all hold. This rule handles triangle closures, cycles, parallel compressed paths, and any directions. It never introduces an arity-three factor: every factor stays a vector of exactly five integers.

To terminate, all residual vertices have degree >=3. Original 2-core preprocessing removes all degree-zero/one vertices before degree-two elimination. A degree-two suppression never increases any surviving vertex degree: if a,b were not already neighbours, deleting av,bv and adding ab leaves their degrees unchanged; if they were neighbours, degrees decrease. Therefore no surviving vertex can have had degree less than three in the original 2-core. Thus k<=h.

For a final weighted connected component, simultaneous translation x_v -> x_v+c preserves every difference and weight. Choose one anchor vertex with colour zero, enumerate the other k_i-1 colours, multiply by five. Across q components the final answer is `scalar*5^q*sum_reduced products`, using 5^(k-q) iterations. This is the exact original count including factors from all eliminated vertices and components.

### Integer precision and physical ledger

All intermediate weights count extensions of some subset of the original n five-colour variables with boundaries held fixed. Thus every nonnegative entry and scalar is at most 5^n, and its binary length is O(n+1) bits. It is important that the factors are actual extension multiplicities rather than arbitrary complex amplitudes; no floating-point cyclotomic arithmetic is necessary.

There are at most n(n-1)/2 active pair factors, five O(n)-bit integer entries each. A dense source-authenticated factor array uses O(n^3) writable bits. The current scalar, accumulated exact answer, n-bit colour registers, head addresses, active-degree table, source-copy buffers, component-DFS stack and output fit the same O((N+1)^3) live bound. One elimination requires at most 25 big-integer products plus additions and O(n^2) factor inspections; there are at most n eliminations. The 5^(k-q) final assignments inspect at most O(n^2) factors each, using O(n)-bit integer multiplications. On locally headed tapes, even with conservatively charged full-array scans, O((N+1)^8) bit steps per enumeration and for total preprocessing suffice. The same actual machine performs every stage; the reference CPython hash-table implementation is not itself claimed as a physical TM microstep trace.

There is no fixed-template 3-SAT lower-bound transfer here. Bang-Jensen, Hell and MacGillivray (1988) prove NP-completeness of H-colouring for a semicomplete target with more than one directed cycle. T5 meets this condition, but that theorem alone supplies no edge/vertex blowup or ETH exponent. This positive algorithm is compatible with NP-completeness.

## Explicit high-cycle-rank witness beating both earlier branches

For K_{2,k} with left vertices a,b and right vertices v_1,...,v_k, orient all 2k edges from the two left vertices to the right, with k>=1. It has n=k+2, m=2k, c=1, cycle rank r=k-1. The /068 generic forest and Fourier methods require up to 2^(k+1) or 5^(k-1) iterations respectively.

For fixed x_a,x_b, each right-hand vertex must lie in the intersection of their two out-neighbour sets. If the left colours coincide, five ordered colour pairs each give 2^k assignments. If distinct, exactly ten of the twenty ordered colour pairs have precisely one common out-neighbour and the remaining ten have none. Therefore

```
#Hom(K_{2,k} -> T5) = 5 * 2^k + 10  (k>=1).
```

The degree-two algorithm contracts all k independent length-two paths into one genuine weighted factor between the two left vertices and then sums the five differences. It uses polynomial bit time and space for all k. For k=120 the exact count is 6646139978924579364519035301401722890 while r=119. This is a counterexample to treating 2^(n-c) or 5^r representation enumeration as mandatory computational work. It does not imply a general subexponential algorithm.

All undirected-support maximum-degree-two graphs also run in polynomial time, including arbitrarily long mixed-direction cycles. All input graph components are counted, not collapsed to one YES bit.
