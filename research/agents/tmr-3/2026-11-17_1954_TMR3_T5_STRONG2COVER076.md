## Authentic source and structural recognition

The executable input is a single ASCII word sequence: n, m, the declared labels 0,...,n-1, then exactly m ordered endpoint pairs in [n]^2. All declarations are validated; thus n,m<=N where N is the actual source length. Duplicate directed arcs are semantically redundant and deduplicated; a self-loop or opposite pair gives the legitimate exact count zero. An invalid token, missing declaration or out-of-range endpoint rejects as malformed. This is an explicit polynomial-overhead counterpart of the manuscript's canonical unary n,m and fixed-width-binary-endpoint convention, not a free source oracle.

For the recognized subclass, every original vertex is either an indegree-zero hub (including isolates), or an outdegree-zero sink of indegree exactly three with three distinct incoming hub vertices; no other arcs are present. Construct the multiset H of triples e recorded by real sink vertices. Duplicate triples from *distinct* sinks are retained separately. If recognition fails on a well-formed graph, run the actual original-arc binary-forest exact counter; do not reject a valid nonmember as NO.

The target is the loopless five-color tournament R(a,z) iff z-a mod 5 in {1,3}, with W(a,b,c)=number of z in Z5 satisfying R(a,z), R(b,z), R(c,z). By enumeration of predecessor sets of size two,

W(a,b,c) = 2 if a=b=c;
W(a,b,c) = 1 if the support is a compatible two-color pair {u,u+2} mod 5;
W(a,b,c) = 0 otherwise.

For a hub assignment f, each original sink chooses its label independently, yielding the *exact original-graph integer* Z(G)=sum_(f:[k]->Z5) product_(e in H) W(f(e)). No promise on hub palette is made.

## Theorem C3-T5-STRONG-TWO-COVER076

Let t=tau_2(H) be the minimum |C| such that |e intersect C|>=2 for every recorded triple e (multiplicity does not affect t). Then the exact all-original-vertex count is given, for *any* such cover C, by

Z(G) = SUM_(a in Z5^C) [ PRODUCT_(e subseteq C) W(a|e) ] *
       PRODUCT_(v notin C) [ SUM_(s=0)^4 PRODUCT_(e in H, e\C={v}) W(a|e∩C, s) ].

The products over empty collections equal one, so isolated hubs contribute exactly five. The formula counts separate sink vertices through repeated factors and all hub palettes, including three or more distinct colors.

**Proof.** Fix all colors on C. Each triple has either three cover vertices, contributing a constant W, or exactly two cover vertices and one outside vertex v, contributing a unary weight in f(v). No triple contains two outside vertices. Exchange finite sums and products: each outside variable is now independent and may be summed locally over all five colors. Finally sum over all 5^|C| cover assignments. Every original hub assignment occurs exactly once and each original sink's possible labels have already been integrated exactly. The formula therefore equals the original #Hom, not a projected Boolean count or merely an existence test.

**Charged cover discovery.** Search t'=0,1,... until the first feasible cover. At every state, locate the first triple containing fewer than two selected vertices. If it contains no selected vertices, branch on its three possible pairs. If it contains exactly one selected vertex, branch on either of its two unselected vertices. Each branch adds at least one vertex, so a budget t' has at most O(3^t') nodes. If a size-t' extension exists, at least one branch is contained in that extension; inductively the search finds it. The first success has size precisely tau_2(H). Across all failed smaller budgets and the final success, O(poly(N) 3^t) source-work is charged. This is a real, discoverable cover, not preloaded decomposition advice.

**One physical execution.** Retain the authenticated original source and graph, discovered sink-to-triple table, branch stack/selected-set bitvector, one cover coloring, one five-entry weight vector for each outside hub, O(N)-bit arithmetic accumulators, source positions/addresses and final output. The search performs O(poly(N)3^t) nodes; the sum performs exactly 5^t cover colorings and O(poly(N)) physical arithmetic and seek work per coloring. All intermediate integer counts are bounded by 5^(original vertex count), so each has O(N) bits. Conservative same-realization bounds are

T(N)=O((N+1)^12(1+3^t+5^t))=O((N+1)^12(1+5^t)),
S_peak(N)=O((N+1)^4) writable bits.

There is no preloaded checkpoint, unpriced query, uncharged materialized exponential table, hidden hypergraph acquisition or separate per-stage peak-space model. For valid nonmembers, including the failed family recognition, the deterministic binary-forest fallback has T=O((N+1)^12(1+2^(n-c))), S_peak=O((N+1)^4). Thus the overall implementation is total on every declared graph.

## An infinite minimum-degree-three family outside /075

Let k>=5. Create three anchors a,b,c and k-3 outside hubs v. For every v and every anchor pair ab, ac, bc, create a distinct sink with arcs from those three hubs. Call the source graph D_k. Its present triples do not cover any pair of distinct outside hubs; the /075 pair-cover requirement fails. All original vertices have undirected degree at least three; the ordinary 2-core is the whole graph.

The original number of vertices n=4k-9 and arcs m=9(k-3); the underlying graph is connected and cycle rank m-n+1=5k-17. The *ordinary* underlying-graph minimum vertex-cover size equals k for k>=5: the k hub vertices give a cover, while a matching of size k is obtained by matching every outside hub v to its distinct sink (a,b,v), and matching a,b,c to (a,c,v_1), (b,c,v_1), (a,c,v_2), respectively, for two distinct outside hubs v_1,v_2. Hence any standard 5^vertex-cover bound remains exponential in k while tau_2(H)=3. The latter equals 3 because the three anchors form a strong two-cover, and no two hub vertices meet every present triple twice.

For fixed anchor colors a,b,c define
K(a,b,c)=SUM_(s=0)^4 W(a,b,s)W(a,c,s)W(b,c,s).
Directly from the template, K=10 when all anchors agree (5 ordered triples), K=3 when exactly two compatible colors occur (30 triples), and K=0 in the remaining 90 cases. The outside hubs are independent after fixing the three anchors. Consequently the exact count is

**Z(D_k)=5*10^(k-3)+30*3^(k-3).**

For k=40 this is 50000000000000000013508517176729920890 valid original full-graph colorings, with n=151 original vertices, m=333 arcs, cycle rank 183 and strong cover 3. The /069 degree-two suppressor has no degree<=2 vertex to eliminate. Exhaustive finite module tests found D_5 and D_6 prime, but *no unproved all-k modular-primality assertion* is made.

A concrete legitimate three-hub-color assignment is anchor colors all zero, outside hub v_1=2 and another v_2=3, with remaining outside hubs zero. Each actual present triple contains two anchor zeros and one outside color 0,2 or 3, and has a target sink extension. Thus global two-color rigidity genuinely fails. This is not another coefficient tuning of the /075 two-color formula.

A simpler sunflower with present triples {a,b,v} has tau_2=2 and count 5*4^(k-2)+10*2^(k-2); it is included only as a transparent diagnostic because prior degree-two suppression already efficiently handles it.
