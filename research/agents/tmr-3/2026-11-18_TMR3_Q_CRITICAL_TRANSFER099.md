# TMR-3/099: exact-one T5 projection, physically critical controls, and charged recurrences
Date: 2026-11-18 Pacific. Role TMR-3 / PNP Time-Space Tradeoffs. Status: PROVED (structural), COMPUTATIONAL (finite), CONDITIONAL (time-space transfer), OPEN (unrestricted hardness).

## Exact fixed relation and paired-projection theorem (PROVED)
Colors are Z5 and R(a,b) iff (b-a) mod5 belongs to {1,3}, with R(a,a)=0. Q(h,u,v)=R(h,u) XOR R(h,v); P(h,u,v)=R(h,u) AND R(h,v). Every constructed clause has three distinct variable names, although names may share colors. For an undirected graph G, attach a **fresh private head h_e** to every edge e={u,v}. Summing over each head independently, define q(d)=#h Q(h,0,d), p(d)=#h P(h,0,d). Exhaustive local enumeration and direct tournament symmetry give
```
 d:          0   1   2   3   4
 q(d):       0   4   2   2   4
 p(d):       2   0   1   1   0
 q(d)+2p(d)=4 for every d.
```
Hence Z_Q(G)=sum_{c:V->Z5} product_{uv in E} q(c(v)-c(u)) and analogously Z_P with p. **Z_Q(G)>0 iff G is 5-colorable**, whereas **Z_P(G)>=5*2^|E|>0 for every G**, by giving all original vertices a common color and independently giving each private head one of its two in-neighbors. This is an exact matched-scope Q-vs-P separation on a restricted family, not a general algorithmic dominance. Removing a Q clause leaves its private head isolated, multiplying the exact surviving assignment count by 5. The local accepted triple counts are Q=60/125 and P=20/125, disjoint.

## Uniform physical critical family with easy deletion witnesses (PROVED)
Let G_r=K3 join C_(2r+1), r>=1. It has v=2r+4 vertices and m=8r+7 edges, and chi(G_r)=3+3=6. Deleting a clique edge makes K3 2-colorable; deleting a cycle edge makes C_(2r+1) a 2-colorable path; deleting a cross-edge (k,c) allows the uniquely colored cycle vertex c to share k's color. Thus **every** G_r-e is 5-colorable, so its fresh-head Q encoding is connected, clause-minimal UNSAT and all m deletion witnesses exist. Each head occurs in one clause and its independent parity graph is one edge, hence the parity filter passes every instance. The incidence girth is 6, fixed, not growing.

A concrete uniform no-advice witness procedure colors G_r-e by one of the three explicit cases, selects a valid head for every surviving edge by scanning 5 possibilities, emits every original and private-head label, and verifies all surviving Q clauses. It uses O(v+m) constant-alphabet operations for a recognized canonical encoding. Arbitrary input authentication, output, and possible fallback are charged; on read-only random-access input, a conservative repeated-scan recognizer for canonical structure costs O(N^2) time and O(log N) writable bits, with write-only output of Theta(N) symbols. This is an easy family countercontrol, not a general Q solver.

## Unbounded-girth physical critical family: existence only (PROVED)
Erdos's high-girth/high-chromatic theorem supplies for every g a graph with girth >=g and chromatic number >=6 [1]. Delete edges until chi first becomes 6 (one deletion changes chi by at most one), then retain a minimal connected 6-chromatic edge subgraph H_g. It is edge-critical: chi(H_g-e)<=5 for every e, and its girth remains >=g. Its private-head Q instance is physically clause-critical UNSAT; every deletion has a SAT witness; its incidence girth is >=2g; every per-head parity graph is a single edge. Each H_g vertex has degree at least 5: if deg(v)<=4, a 5-coloring of H_g-e restricted to H_g-v extends to v, a contradiction. **The existence theorem supplies no efficient family generator or witness-finding lower bound.** No fixed-girth hardness theorem was used to infer a growing-girth complexity result.

## Constant-width exact counting and operation/space accounting (PROVED)
The cycle has treewidth 2; add the K3 vertices to each bag to get treewidth <=5. Each private head is attached by a new size-three bag, so ternary Q/P primal treewidth stays <=5. The exact integer transfer algorithm enumerates 25 clique patterns with one clique color pinned at 0, five possible first cycle colors, and 5x5 state transitions along the cycle. Edge weights are q or p. For deleted clauses omit exactly that edge factor and multiply by five for the isolated head. It computes all assignment counts exactly in O(r) constant-size integer operations on O(r)-bit values, hence conservative O(r^2) bit-time and O(r) peak bit-space including final count. No lower bound follows.

Independent original-vertex brute enumeration verified all full Q/P counts and three representative deletion counts for r=1,2 (ten validations). For r=1: Z_Q=0, Z_P=250880; all Q deletion counts 1258291200. For r=2: Z_Q=0, Z_P=62405120; Q deletion counts are 30064771072000 (clique), 5153960755200 (cycle and cross). Larger transfer cases up to r=30 are retained in the local reproducibility bundle.

## Charged row/block recurrences and correct-path distinction (CONDITIONAL)
For authenticated F(z,y)=AND_{j,i} B_{j,i}(z,y_{j,i}), private y disjoint after z, let s=|z|, R<=N^0.195 rows, M<=N^0.65 blocks, child size <=N^0.35, and child time ell^(alpha+o(1)). A sound exhaustive deterministic specialist requires
`T_auth + T_pre + O(2^s [sum_{j,i}(T_child+T_verify+T_replay)+T_route+T_output])`
and must count every failed child, both polarities of child calls, all routing computation and child materialization. The simultaneous writable peak includes live parent states and any unreleased child, routing state and certificates. If M is the total count, the direct child exponent is 0.65+0.35alpha; target 1.33 needs alpha<=1.942857. If EACH of R rows contains M blocks, it becomes 0.845+0.35alpha and requires alpha<=1.385714. For s=N^0.195, direct 2^s enumeration is superpolynomial regardless.

A free correct polarity route is not a computable uniform routing algorithm. A supplied k-bit successful path is nondeterministic advice whose validity, reading, replay, wrong-path rejection, full quantified UNSAT coverage, and any universal branches must be charged. For SAT, a complete Q coloring is efficiently checked; for general UNSAT, polynomially complete certificates would imply NP=coNP. A deterministic sound one-sided specialist with an enforceable uniform cap on every input of its promised polarity gives a full deterministic decider after timeout, so published SAT deterministic random-access exclusions apply to **unrestricted** such algorithms: at N^(1/2+o(1)) writable space, c<1.34070 is excluded by Buss-Williams [2]; at N^o(1) space, c<2cos(pi/7)≈1.80194 is excluded [3]. These require the exact machine model and fine-grained source reduction. They do not apply to the restricted join or critical-deletion promise. For nondeterministic TAUTOLOGIES, Diehl/van Melkebeek/Williams give c<cube_root(4)≈1.5874 with some positive theorem-dependent small space exponent, **not a licensed c=1.5874 exclusion at space N^0.5** [4]. Existential SAT guesses are verified and have no analogous lower bound from that result. Proof-system-specific and representation lower bounds are not same-machine results.
