## Exact Fourier-cutset theorem

Fix $0\le s\le\rho$ and put $h=\rho-s$. Split each $\gamma_j=(u_j,v_j)\in\mathbb F_5^s\times\mathbb F_5^h$. For each streamed high-frequency $\omega\in\mathbb F_5^h$, define a weighted low-residue DP over the fifth-cyclotomic integer ring:
\[
F_{\omega,0}(a)=[a=0],\quad
F_{\omega,j+1}(a)=F_{\omega,j}(a)+
\zeta_5^{\langle\omega,v_j\rangle}F_{\omega,j}(a-u_j).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{Z(G)=\frac{5^c}{5^h}
\sum_{\omega\in\mathbb F_5^h}\sum_{a\in\mathbb F_5^s}
\sum_{b\in\mathbb F_5^h}
 A(a,b)F_{\omega,t}(a)\zeta_5^{-\langle\omega,b\rangle}.}
\]
The proof is finite Fourier character orthogonality: $5^{-h}\sum_\omega\zeta_5^{\langle\omega,v-b\rangle}=[v=b]$. Thus the inverse transform selects the actual high residue of every forest-bit subset. Multiplication by $5^c$ restores all original root-color assignments. All operations are exact, using four signed integer coefficients in $\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]$, reduced by $1+\zeta_5+\zeta_5^2+\zeta_5^3+\zeta_5^4=0$. No probabilistic hashes or floating-point approximations.
