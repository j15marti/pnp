\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let $N=2^n$. Counting size-$s$ circuits gives
\[
 B(n,s)=\lceil\log_2(s+1)\rceil+
 \lceil\log_2(n+s)\rceil+
 s(2+2\lceil\log_2(n+s)\rceil).
\]
\begin{theorem}
An exact deterministic MCSP[$s$] decider that
reads source bits adaptively must probe at least
$N-B(n,s)$ source bits on the zero truth table.
This includes stand-alone, many-to-one reductions
to any target language.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Proof. If $q<N-B(n,s)$, more than
$2^{B(n,s)}$ inputs agree with zero on those
$q$ positions. One is circuit-hard, yet has
the identical adaptive transcript. Contradiction.
A randomized two-sided-error algorithm needs at
least $N/(6B+6)$ source-bit probes: randomize
$2B+2$ sparse positions, couple with zero, and
compare correctness gap $1/6$ with divergence
probability at most $q(2B+2)/(2N)$.
