\section*{Exact theorem and proof}
Let $\mathcal Y_s$ and $\mathcal Y_t$ be two subsets of a finite truth-table universe $\mathcal U$, and let independent public randomness $Z$ select a map $\Phi_z:\mathcal U\to\mathcal U$ that is injective for every $z$.
\begin{theorem}[Seed-independent YES-cardinality obstruction]
\[
\boxed{\min_{f\in\mathcal Y_s}\Pr_Z[\Phi_Z(f)\in\mathcal Y_t]\le
\frac{|\mathcal Y_t|}{|\mathcal Y_s|}.}
\]
Consequently, if $|\mathcal Y_t|/|\mathcal Y_s|<2/3$, this transformation fails the two-sided-error source-YES completeness requirement even with unlimited computing time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed seed $z$, injectivity implies
$|\{f\in\mathcal Y_s:\Phi_z(f)\in\mathcal Y_t\}|\le|\mathcal Y_t|$.
Average over arbitrary source-independent distribution $Z$ and then over uniform $f\in\mathcal Y_s$. Some fixed $f$, chosen before the public seed, succeeds with probability no greater than the displayed ratio.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact threshold asymptotics for one explicit gate library.}
Use fan-in-two NAND gates, with $n$ freely supplied inputs, constants $0,1$, and one output pointer, for truth tables of length $N=2^n$. Let $\mathcal Y_s$ denote the functions computable by at most $s$ NAND gates. Fix
\[
k=\min\left\{N,\left\lfloor\frac{s-n}{2n+1}\right\rfloor\right\}.
\]
For $n\ge2$ and $k\ge1$, choose any $k$ distinct input assignments. Every subset of those assignments is the support of a different function, expressible as an OR of minterms. Sharing at most $n$ inversions, each minterm takes at most $2(n-1)$ NAND gates and each OR uses at most three; thus total gates $\le n+2k(n-1)+3(k-1)\le n+(2n+1)k\le s$. The empty subset is the supplied zero constant. Hence $|\mathcal Y_s|\ge2^k$. Counting NAND gate descriptions, predecessor pointers and the chosen output gives
\[
|\mathcal Y_t|\le(t+1)(n+t+2)^{2t+1}.
\]
For any fixed $0<\beta<\mu<1$, $s=\lfloor N^\mu\rfloor$ and $t=\lfloor N^\beta\rfloor$, the ratio is therefore at most
\[
\frac{|\mathcal Y_t|}{|\mathcal Y_s|}
\le(t+1)(n+t+2)^{2t+1}2^{-k}
=2^{-\Omega(N^\mu/\log N)}.
\]

\paragraph{Actual FK recurrence shape.}
For $r=\lceil\log_2N\rceil\ge2$, replace only the final additive term by $D_{r-1}:=f$, leaving independent public seeds and the preceding stages fixed. The output is
\[
\Phi_Z(f)=f\oplus[T_{r-1}(Z)\wedge G_{r-1}(Z)]
=f\oplus B_Z.
\]
This is a permutation on all $N$-bit truth tables for each fixed seed, so the theorem applies without knowledge of the seed distribution or security of $G$. Hence no source-independent randomized insertion of this form maps all source YES instances of threshold $N^\mu$ into target YES at threshold $N^\beta$ with $2/3$ probability for any fixed $\beta<\mu$. This specifically eliminates the same-length, final-additive input-injection candidate. It does not refute the original CHMY PRG, earlier noninjective insertions, source-dependent seeds, or changed output length.
