# TWO-AXIS-DESCENT2 (restricted exact corollary)

For an exact, stand-alone, deterministic reduction from MCSP[N^beta] to MCSP[(N')^beta'] with N'=N^(1+o(1)) and fixed beta'<beta, two necessities follow.

**Massive YES fibers:** THRES-ENTROPY1 gives at least 2^(c_beta N^beta) source YES truth tables, while the target YES class has only 2^(N^(beta'+o(1))) possibilities. Therefore some target YES instance has at least 2^(Omega(N^beta)) distinct source YES preimages.

**Near-full input read:** LOSSY-PROBE-NOGO2 forces the transducer to inspect at least N-O(N^beta log N) source bit positions on the all-zero input, even with an arbitrary downstream target solver, provided that solver has no direct access to unobserved source bits.

Thus any small-parameter same-scale descent must be both massively lossy and source-input exhaustive. Neither property proves hardness preservation or a superlinear one-tape bound. Exact NO preservation, time/workspace accounting, and a certified rho>lambda remain OPEN. P versus NP OPEN.
