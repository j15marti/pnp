\section*{THEOREM}
Let $G_{i+1}=D_i\oplus(T_i\wedge G_i)$, $1\le i<r$, and inject $f$ additively at layer $j$: $D_j\mapsto D_j\oplus f$. With a shared public seed and $k=r-1-j$ mask layers above the injection,
\[
\Phi(f)\oplus\Phi(f')=(f\oplus f')\wedge\bigwedge_{i>j}T_i .
\]
Each coordinate of the difference survives with probability $\le 2^{-k}$ (independent layers, marginal $(1-2^{-h})/2$). Hence (i) $k=0$ gives $\Phi(f)=f\oplus P(\zeta)$, a one-time-pad by a seed-computable string: promises are preserved up to the circuit size of $P$, but the map is complexity-preserving and decodable from the seed; (ii) for $k\ge1$, YES instances stay small ($\mathrm{size}(f)+\mathrm{poly}(n)k$), and a random far source $f_1$ on a $\delta N$ set stays far from size-$N^\mu$ circuits on $\mathrm{supp}(\wedge T)$ of density about $2^{-k}$ iff $\delta N2^{-k}\gg N^\mu\log N$, i.e.\ $k\le(1-\mu-\epsilon)\log_2N$ (Chernoff plus counting, as in the prior report).

\textbf{Consequence.} The two-promise obstruction of the base-slot route disappears for $k\le(1-\mu-\epsilon)\log N$, but the output is still $N$ bits with the instance complexity parameter unchanged: there is no shrinking $\beta'\le\lambda\beta$, and the CHMY lower bound is not transferred to a new source problem. The late-layer map is a masked-selection reduction $f\mapsto(f\wedge T)\oplus D$; given the seed, $f|_{\mathrm{supp}\,T}$ is read off directly. Classification: promise preservation EXACT OBJECT TRANSFER; usefulness for threshold descent FAILED TRANSFER pending a shrinking mechanism.
