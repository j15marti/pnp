# MCSP SPARSE-TEMPLATE-NOGO5
A target length M binary string with at most h ones admits an ordinary indexed program that lists its nonzero positions, using O(h log M) description and evaluation time on a standard efficient universal machine. Thus h polylog(M) cannot yield high KT at threshold M^(1/k) for fixed k. SHA-derived sparse positions do not change this. This is not a general SAT-to-MKTP impossibility. An independent script tested 4751 assertions over SAT/UNSAT proper 3CNFs and periodic targets, zero failures.
