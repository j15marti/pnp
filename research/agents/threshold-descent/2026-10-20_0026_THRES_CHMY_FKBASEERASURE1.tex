\section*{BUILD: EXACT ERASURE THEOREM}

\[
\Phi_{i+1}(f;\zeta)=D_i(\zeta)\oplus(T_i(\zeta)\wedge\Phi_i(f;\zeta)),\quad 1\le i<r.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Base-layer two-promise obstruction]
For every $f,f'$ and a shared public seed,
\[
\Phi_r(f;\zeta)\oplus\Phi_r(f';\zeta)
=(f\oplus f')\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{r-1}T_i(\zeta).
\]
If $f,f'$ differ only on $S$, then
$\Pr_\zeta[\Phi_r(f;\zeta)\ne\Phi_r(f';\zeta)]
\le |S|/2^{r-1}=2|S|/N$.
For every fixed $0<\delta<1/2$ and $\mu<1$, for sufficiently large $N$ there is a source YES instance $f_0=0^N$ and a source NO instance $f_1$ differing only on $\lfloor\delta N\rfloor$ coordinates, with $f_1$ at distance $\Omega(N)$ from every circuit of size $N^\mu$. With $\delta=1/16$, no even unlimited-time downstream classifier given only public seed and the output can distinguish both source promises with error $\le1/3$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
XOR two recurrences: the $D_i$ terms cancel and the difference is multiplied by $T_i$ coordinatewise. Induction gives the first identity. Since $a_i^j$ is nonzero for $a_i\ne0$, uniform $b_i$ makes the inner product a fair bit; $a_i=0$ forces zero. Thus each one-bit mask marginal is $(1-2^{-h})/2\le1/2$. Independent layers yield survival probability at most $2^{-(r-1)}$ per coordinate; union bound over $S$ proves the display without claiming coordinate independence.
Choose a fixed set $S$ of $\lfloor\delta N\rfloor$ positions and let $f_1$ be independently fair on $S$ and zero elsewhere. For each size-$s$ circuit with $s=N^\mu$, its Hamming distance from $f_1$ on $S$ is $\mathrm{Bin}(|S|,1/2)$; Chernoff bounds give probability $\exp(-\Omega(N))$ of distance less than $|S|/4$. There are $2^{O(s\log(n+s))}=2^{o(N)}$ such circuits. Their union is not enough to cover every such $f_1$. Take one far from all circuits. When $\delta=1/16$ the coupling makes outputs and public seeds identical with probability at least $7/8$; their total variation is at most $1/8$. Any randomized classifier's acceptance gap is likewise at most $1/8$, whereas $1/3$ two-sided error on opposite labels requires gap at least $1/3$. Contradiction.
\end{proof}
