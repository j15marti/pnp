\section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
\paragraph{A. Full-entropy local-generator descent.}
Reparameterize a known one-tape local PRG by lowering the circuit-size
threshold of every generated truth table, while retaining its
distinguishing lower bound.  Proposed falsifier: too many distinct
generator outputs to fit into circuits below the new threshold.

\paragraph{B. Lossy semantic quotient with recovery.}
Allow many source YES truth tables to collapse to one target YES table
but insist that provenance or source-free advice can recover the source.
Quantify the required advice before claiming a cheap replay mechanism.
The non-reconstructing quotient is not ruled out by this approach.

\paragraph{C. Direct sums, input inflation and source restrictions.}
Try constant-copy composition and coordinate projection.  Audit whether
threshold reduction is genuine or only a change in \(N\), and whether
the one-way one-tape simulation pays for every output bit.

\paragraph{D. Implicit target truth tables.}
Represent the larger output by an evaluator rather than materialize it,
then ask whether the original MCSP model and magnification theorem still
apply.  Missing premise: an exact model-preserving compiler.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
McKay--Murray--Williams' Theorem 1.2 gives one-pass
search-MCSP with \(N\widetilde O(s)\) total time,
\(\widetilde O(s^2)\) update time, and \(\widetilde O(s)\)
space using short \(\Sigma_3^{\mathrm P}\) oracle queries
\cite{MMW2019}.  Under \(P=NP\), their fixed oracle procedure
yields a uniform \(s^{O(1)}\) resource envelope.
This is an \emph{upper} bound, not a lower bound.

Cheraghchi--Hirahara--Myrisiotis--Yoshida's
Theorem 15 is the stronger parameterized one-tape
\emph{lower} bound: for every \(1/2<\mu'<\mu<1\),
\[
  \mathrm{MCSP}[N^\mu]\notin
  \mathrm{BPTIME}_1^{O}
     [N^{2(\mu'-o(1))}],
\]
where the arbitrary oracle receives queries of length
\(N^{o(1)}\) \cite{CHMY2020}.
Their introductory Theorem 3 states a weaker \(N^{1+\delta}\)
form.  Theorem 15's \(\sqrt{T}\)-scale local-PRG seed
is the point of contact for the present result.
Its locality and the oracle machine hypotheses cannot be discarded.

Oliveira--Pich--Santhanam's magnification is for a
particular \emph{Gap-MCSP promise} and general circuits, not the
above one-tape decision model \cite{OPS2018}.  We must not
transfer its bound without a separate promise and model bridge.
Goldberg--Juvekar--Kabanets (June 2026) study
\emph{implicit} gap MCSP under stated cryptographic/proof-complexity
assumptions and randomized reductions \cite{GJK2026}; it does not
supply an unconditional explicit-truth-table threshold descent.

The prior \texttt{PROMISESLOPE1} already rules out
claiming a growing slope merely by replacing \(N\) by \(N^q\).
The current argument instead isolates the independent obstruction
present even before output materialization: \emph{a lower circuit-size
budget has fewer distinct Boolean functions available.}

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let \(N=2^n\), let \(0<\beta<1\) be fixed,
and let \(s=\lfloor N^\beta\rfloor\).
Write
\[
  \mathcal S_{n,s}:=
  \{f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}\colon
       \mathrm{CC}(f)\le s\},
\]
where \(\mathrm{CC}\) is gate count for unrestricted-fan-out,
fan-in-two \(\{\wedge,\vee,\neg\}\)-circuits.

\begin{theorem}[YES-class entropy capacity, \texttt{YES-ENTROPY1}]
For every fixed \(0<\beta<1\), there is \(c_\beta>0\)
such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
  \boxed{\log_2|\mathcal S_{n,s}|\ge c_\beta s.}
\]
Moreover, for all integers \(r\ge1\), \(m\ge1\),
\[
  \boxed{
   \log_2|\mathcal S_{m,r}|
   \le r\log_2(3(m+r)^2)
      +\log_2((r+1)(m+r)).
  }
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put \(t=\lfloor s/(2n)\rfloor\).
For each \(A\subseteq\{0,1\}^n\) with \(|A|=t\),
define \(f_A(x)=1\) iff \(x\in A\).
Precompute the at most \(n\) negated input literals.
Every equality indicator takes at most \(n-1\)
binary AND gates, and OR-ing the \(t\) indicators
takes \(t-1\) gates.  Thus \(f_A\) has at most
\(n+t(n-1)+(t-1)=n+tn-1\le s\) gates
for large \(n\). Distinct \(A\)'s give distinct functions.
Therefore
\[
  |\mathcal S_{n,s}|
     \ge {N\choose t}
     \ge (N/t)^t,
\]
and since \(t=(1+o(1))s/(2n)\) and
\(\log_2t=\beta n-O(\log n)\),
\[
 \log_2 {N\choose t}
 \ge ((1-\beta)/2-o(1))s
 \ge c_\beta s.
\]
For the upper bound, an \(m\)-input ordered
circuit of \(k\le r\) gates has at most
\([3(m+r)^2]^k(m+r)\) syntactic descriptions:
each gate chooses one of three types and at most two
predecessor wires, and one final output wire is chosen.
Summing \(k=0,\ldots,r\) yields at most
\((r+1)(m+r)[3(m+r)^2]^r\) circuits.
Their computed functions are no more numerous.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Source-free bounded-fiber threshold obstruction,
\texttt{FIBER-DESCENT1}]
Let \(E:\mathcal S_{n,s}\to\mathcal S_{m,r}\) be any map
(not necessarily efficient), and suppose every target value
has at most \(2^h\) preimages under \(E\).
Then
\[
\boxed{
h+r\log_2(3(m+r)^2)+\log_2((r+1)(m+r))
\ \ge\ c_\beta s.
}
\]
In particular, if \(m=O(n)\), \(r=N^{\gamma+o(1)}\)
with \(\gamma<\beta\), then
\[
   \boxed{h=\Omega(N^\beta).}
\]
If \(h=0\), so that \(E\) is injective on the
source YES class, then
\[
  \boxed{r=\Omega(s/\log(m+r)).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Count pairs consisting of a target value and one of its
at most \(2^h\) preimages:
\[
 |\mathcal S_{n,s}|
 \le 2^h|\mathcal S_{m,r}|.
\]
Insert the bounds from \texttt{YES-ENTROPY1}.
When \(m=O(n)\) and \(r=N^{\gamma+o(1)}\), the entire
target-description term is
\(N^{\gamma+o(1)}=o(N^\beta)\), so \(h=\Omega(N^\beta)\).
Setting \(h=0\) yields the last bound after absorbing
the additive logarithmic term.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No injective exponent descent at polynomial output size]
Suppose \(m=an+o(n)\) for a fixed \(a\ge1\), and
\(r=(2^m)^{\beta'}\).  An injection from all
source \(\mathcal S_{n,N^\beta}\) into target
\(\mathcal S_{m,r}\) is impossible whenever
\[
  \boxed{a\beta'<\beta.}
\]
If the target size is \(N'=N^{a+o(1)}\), the
inequality \(\beta'\ge\beta/a-o(1)\) is necessary
for any such all-YES injective map.  The only
simple decrease in \(\beta'\) left by this count
can therefore be input-length inflation, which
the independent \texttt{INFLATION-SLOPE-NOGO1}
time-exponent audit already shows cannot improve
the certified \(g/\beta\) slope.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If \(a\beta'<\beta\), the chosen \(r\) is
\(N^{\gamma+o(1)}\) for \(\gamma=a\beta'<\beta\).
An injection has \(h=0\), contradicting
\texttt{FIBER-DESCENT1}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Local generator image-capacity guard,
\texttt{PRG-ENTROPY1}]
Let \(G:\{0,1\}^d\to\{0,1\}^{2^m}\) have at least
\(2^{h}\) \emph{distinct outputs}, and suppose every
output \(G(z)\) has a circuit of size at most \(r\).
Then
\[
 \boxed{h=O(r\log(m+r)).}
\]
If a proposed full-entropy seed-hardwiring construction
has \(h=\Omega(d)\) and \(d=N^{(1+g)/2-o(1)}\)
on \(N\)-bit truth tables (\(m=\log_2N\)), the
condition \(r=N^{\beta+o(1)}\) forces
\[
 \boxed{\beta\ge(1+g)/2-o(1).}
\]
Thus the \(\sqrt{T}\)-seed, full-image-entropy
local-generator template cannot certify a
superlinear \(N^{1+g}\) time lower bound for
\(\beta<1/2\).  This is a \emph{restricted
template obstruction}, not a lower bound on
all generators or all MCSP algorithms.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
All distinct outputs belong to
\(\mathcal S_{m,r}\); apply the upper counting
bound, divide by \(\log N\) in the exponent
and compare powers of \(N\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Crucial quantifier guard]
An arbitrary many-one reduction deciding the large-threshold
YES/NO partition need \emph{not} be injective on YES inputs.
It may merge exponentially many YES instances.  Therefore
\texttt{FIBER-DESCENT1} is not a prohibition on every
decision-preserving threshold descent.  Source-backed
reconstruction can also bypass the source-free counting premise,
but its rereads, indexing, replay, workspace, and query
costs must be charged separately.  Also, a PRG seed length \(d\)
does \emph{not} imply \(|\mathrm{im}(G)|=2^d\).
Full image entropy must be verified, not inferred.
\end{remark}

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
A standalone deterministic integer-audit runner is recorded at
\texttt{experiments/meta/2026-10-18\_threshold\_entropy1.py};
the checked raw results are at
\texttt{results/raw/2026-10-18\_threshold\_entropy1.json}.
For \(n\in\{16,24,32,40,48,64\}\),
\(s=2^{3n/4}\), \(r=2^{n/2}\),
and \(m\in\{n,2n\}\), the audit checks the exact
elementary log-count bounds, with no floating point.
There are \(12\) controls; the explicit upper and lower
counts by themselves certify impossibility of injectivity in
\(8\), beginning at \(n=32\).
For example, at \(n=m=32\), the
source log-cardinality lower bound is \(3{,}670{,}016\)
bits and the target upper bound is \(2{,}359{,}330\)
bits.  At \(n=m=64\), they are
\(50{,}577{,}534{,}877{,}696\) and
\(292{,}057{,}776{,}194\) bits, respectively.
The remaining finite cases are inconclusive,
not counterexamples to the asymptotic theorem.
These are counting tests, \emph{not} experiments
proving MCSP machine-time lower bounds.

\paragraph{Cross-track conclusion.}
The strong Cheraghchi--Hirahara--Myrisiotis--Yoshida
one-tape lower bounds remain valid high-parameter
seeds.  The present result narrows the missing
\texttt{THRESHOLD-DESCENT1} arrow: an injective,
source-recoverable, seed-full-entropy threshold
reparameterization cannot supply the desired
parameter shrinkage at polynomial output size.
A successful mechanism must instead use an
\emph{essentially lossy} semantic quotient
with a decision-preserving NO guard, or a
genuinely stronger local PRG with a better
seed/fooling-time relationship, while still
preserving the ordinary explicit-truth-table
one-tape model and all charged costs.

\paragraph{Highest-value next lemma.}
Specify a candidate many-to-one YES quotient \(E\) and
an exact NO-separation condition for the
fixed decision threshold \(\mathrm{MCSP}[N^\beta]\);

deciders back to the source with
cost \(N^{c+o(1)}\) where \(c\) is strictly below
the known source lower-bound exponent.
Compute the true \((\lambda,\rho)\) after
every output/access/setup cost.  If the
quotient needs to decide the source MCSP
partition beforehand, reject it as circular.
