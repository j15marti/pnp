THEOREM POLYNOMIAL-FEATURE-FIBER10. Let N=2^n; let P_j:GF(2)^N ->GF(2) have Boolean ANF degree d_j, with D=sum_j d_j. For each y in the image of F=(P_1,...,P_B), there exists an N-bit truth table g satisfying F(g)=y, wt(g)<=D and CC(g)<=n(D+1).

PROOF. Pick a solution x to F(x)=y with inclusion-minimal support S. The fiber indicator Q(X)=product_j(1+P_j(X)+y_j) has degree at most D. Fix X outside S to zero. By minimality, Q is one exactly at the all-ones assignment on S, so its unique multilinear ANF equals product_{i in S}X_i, of degree |S|. Restriction cannot increase ANF degree; hence |S|<=D. A weight-w truth table has a point-DNF with at most n+w(n-1)+(w-1)<=n(w+1) gates. QED.
