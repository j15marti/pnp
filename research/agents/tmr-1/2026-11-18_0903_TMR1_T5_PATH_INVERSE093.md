## Problem and exact theorem

Take the precise regular 5-vertex tournament with vertices Z5 and R(a,b)=1 iff b-a mod 5 is 1 or 3. Let S be the 5x5 permutation matrix S[a,b]=1 iff b=a+1 mod 5, so R=S+S^3=S(I+S^2). A path of length ell has ell oriented arcs in any order. Traverse from a to b, with f forward arcs and g backward arcs (ell=f+g). Let M(a,b) count extensions to its internal vertices. Then

**Theorem C1-T5-FULLRANK-PATH-TRANSFER093.**
1. M=R^f (R^T)^g, independently of arc ordering, and every row and column sums to 2^ell.
2. det(R)=2 and det(M)=2^ell. Thus M has rank 5 over Q for every finite ell, despite the fact that all 25 entries of M are strictly positive for every orientation when ell>=4.
3. The exact rational inverse is R^{-1}=(-I-S+S^2+S^3+S^4)/2. Consequently M^{-1}=2^{-ell} times an explicitly computable integer circulant matrix.
4. Writing phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2, the five singular values of M are exactly {2^ell, phi^ell, phi^ell, phi^{-ell}, phi^{-ell}}. The 2-norm condition number is (1+sqrt(5))^ell.

**Proof.** Because R and R^T are polynomials in S, they commute, and usual path matrix multiplication proves item 1. Each factor has row and column sum 2. The 5-cycle S is an even permutation. The matrix S^2 is also a 5-cycle, so det(S)=1 and det(I+S^2)=2 (evaluate the characteristic polynomial t^5-1 at t=-1). Thus det R=2, and det R^T=2, proving item 2. Put A=-I-S+S^2+S^3+S^4. Directly expanding (S+S^3)A and using S^5=I yields 2I. The transposed relation proves item 3.

For item 4, Fourier diagonalization of every circulant matrix gives R eigenvalues lambda_k=zeta^k+zeta^{3k} for zeta=e^{2 pi i/5}. Their magnitudes are 2 for k=0, phi for k=2,3, and phi^{-1} for k=1,4. Each reversed arc conjugates the corresponding eigenvalue; every path therefore has singular values depending only on ell. The largest-to-smallest ratio is (2 phi)^ell=(1+sqrt(5))^ell.

To prove positive support sharply, for ell=4 the possible first-row transfer vectors, for forward count f=0,1,2,3,4, are respectively
(4,1,6,1,4), (1,6,1,4,4), (6,1,4,4,1), (1,4,4,1,6), (4,4,1,6,1).
Every value is positive. Multiplication by either R or R^T preserves strict positivity, so all paths of length >=4 have full positive support. The all-forward 3-edge path has first row (3,0,3,1,1); hence length 4 is the sharp uniform saturation threshold. QED.

## What this eliminates, and what remains

An exact free-independent endpoint transfer has rank one and is a scalar multiple of the all-ones matrix. No finite oriented path over this template implements it, even though every endpoint pair becomes extendible after four arcs and transfer entries become almost uniform on long paths. Numerical/spectral mixing is not exact loss of endpoint information. Conversely, rank five does NOT establish hard computation: matrix multiplication evaluates each finite path in polynomial bit time. Thus neither boundary rank alone nor support saturation alone is a sound all-algorithm SAT lower-bound obstruction.

The matrices have at most ell+1 bits per nonnegative entry (each row sums to 2^ell). A left-to-right exact 5x5 BigInt transfer calculation requires O(ell) fixed-size matrix products and polynomial bit-time/peak bits on the same sequential bit-machine model. The inverse numerator can likewise be calculated by O(ell) products of fixed 5x5 integer matrices, with entries of O(ell) bits. No arbitrary precision real/floating-point operations are necessary, and no nonuniform/precomputed input-dependent advice is assumed. Any source-path decomposition supplied as an algorithmic promise must be authenticated against the complete canonical directed input; the theorem itself is unconditional for an actual path.
