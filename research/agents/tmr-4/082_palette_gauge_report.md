## Precise source model and theorem

Write \(R(a,b)\) iff \(b-a\in\{1,3\}\pmod 5\). Let \(H\) be a connected, triangle-free undirected graph with \(b\ge2\) vertices. For every base vertex \(i\), create an induced five-vertex \(T_5\) tournament \(\{(i,a):a\in\mathbb Z_5\}\). Each undirected base edge \(\{i,j\}\), \(i<j\), has an offset \(d_{ij}\in\mathbb Z_5\) and is replaced by exactly ten *original, ordinary, unweighted* directed constraints:

- \((i,a)\to(j,a+1-d_{ij})\), one for every \(a\in\mathbb Z_5\);
- \((j,a)\to(i,a+1+d_{ij})\), one for every \(a\in\mathbb Z_5\).

All additions in the second coordinate are modulo five. The source presents **only** the canonical original graph, not the palette partition, potential labels, orientation mapping, or a favorable variable ordering. The compiler discovers and verifies all of them using original source arcs.

**Exact theorem:** Any homomorphism from a five-vertex \(T_5\) tournament to itself is one of the five cyclic translations, since a loopless complete tournament enforces injectivity and the fixed 5-element automorphism group consists exactly of translations. Thus each block's colors are \(x_{i,a}=a+t_i\). The forward five cross-arcs constrain \(\Delta=t_j-t_i\) to \(\{d,d+2\}\); the reversed five constrain it to \(\{d,d-2\}\). Their intersection is exactly \(\{d\}\). BFS propagation of these \(\mathbb Z_5\) potentials and all signed-cycle checks therefore produces exactly **5 satisfying assignments** for a consistent connected base, and **0** otherwise. No approximations are used.

On these same unweighted sources, a complete, pointwise original-symbol five-way OBDD has width at most **6** and at most **6n** nonterminal nodes: there are at most five full accepted assignments, and a sixth rejecting state consumes all remaining variables. Under any fixed variable order, both accepting and rejecting input multiplicities are preserved, namely \((5,5^n-5)\) or \((0,5^n)\). This OBDD is also an adaptive FBDD upper bound; it cannot be interpreted as a lower bound for any stronger machine.

**Source-only partition recognition:** In the underlying undirected neighbor sets, two distinct vertices in the same palette share at least three neighbors, whereas two *adjacent cross-palette* vertices share exactly two. The latter have precisely one shared neighbor in each of their two palettes, and no shared third-palette neighbor because the base is triangle-free. The four same-palette neighbors of any vertex are therefore exactly its neighbors with at least three common neighbors. The implementation finds these by charged adjacency intersections, checks the internal directed \(T_5\) orientation against all relevant five-element permutations, verifies one and only one offset per ten-arc cross pattern, verifies base triangle-freeness and accounts for **all source arcs**. It is a polynomial-time, graph-only recognizer; rejected out-of-class graphs are not misrepresented as solved instances. A strict canonical-source parser and necessary pre-allocation \(m\ge2n\) guard prevent a succinct vertex-count header from hiding exponentially many isolated variables. A source SHA-256 is only a content-identity fingerprint, not authentication of external provenance.

For connected \(H\), the underlying original graph is at least **5-vertex-connected**: removing at most four vertices leaves every internal five-clique nonempty and preserves at least one edge of a size-five inter-palette matching across every base edge. The full digraph is strongly connected because each palette and each pair of neighboring palettes have directed paths in both directions. There are no directed false twins.
