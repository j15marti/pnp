## Main theorem: sound and extensionally complete local-certificate cover (PROVED/EXACT, restricted syntax)

Let the authenticated formula for one row be
\[
F_r(z,y_{r1},\ldots,y_{rB})=\bigwedge_{b=1}^B B_{rb}(z,y_{rb}),
\qquad
y_{rb}\cap y_{rb'}=\varnothing\quad(b\ne b').
\]
All variables shared by two blocks MUST belong to z, and the equivalence to the original CNF must be verified, not assumed. Rows can be alternative SAT candidates: \(F=\bigvee_{r=1}^{R}F_r\); for a single conjunctive source, take R=1. Under this explicit row-OR semantics,
\[
F\in\mathrm{SAT}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ 
\exists r\exists z\forall b\exists y_{rb}\,B_{rb}(z,y_{rb}),
\]
\[
F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\forall r\forall z\exists b\forall y_{rb}\neg B_{rb}(z,y_{rb}).
\]
The quantifier interchange for the SAT line is justified ONLY by independence of private variable sets. The UNSAT line is its exact logical negation.

Define a local leaf certificate \(L=(r,Q,b,\pi)\), where Q is a Boolean cube over shared variables z, and \(\pi\) is a checked refutation of \(Q(z)\land B_{rb}(z,y_{rb})\). Checking \(\pi\) against *the authentic source block* must be sound. Leaf L certifies that no z extending Q makes block b satisfiable. Define a combination rule: certificates covering cubes Q0 and Q1 may certify Q whenever the verifier establishes \(Q\subseteq Q_0\cup Q_1\). In particular, splitting on z_i and combining the 0 and 1 children is sound; the children MAY refer to different blocks. An accepted UNSAT proof has a verified root Q=true for EVERY row. This is sound, by induction on the certificate DAG and the quantified equivalence above.

Converse/coverage: if F is UNSAT, for every (r,z) at least one block B_rb[z] is UNSAT. Given any complete, sound and verifiable local refutation system, the finitely many singleton cubes Q=(z=z*) admit leaves; combine all 2^h leaves for each row to obtain a root. Thus exact local cover is extensionally complete, NOT polynomially bounded. Leaves may be refutations in resolution, DRAT, or any other independently verified complete refutation format. The completeness argument supplies no small proof, cheap proof discovery, or small verifier workspace.

SAT dual: an accepted SAT certificate consists of one row r, one complete z assignment, and satisfying private assignments for ALL of that row's blocks; the verifier checks every original clause. Accepted SAT and UNSAT certificates cannot coexist. A local refutation at ONE z is never a global UNSAT certificate. A false block selection, invalid local proof, uncovered z, unverified source mapping or incorrect witness is rejected as UNKNOWN, never translated to an answer of the opposite polarity.

## Minimal falsifiers and barriers (PROVED/EXACT)

1. For z=0, B0=(z OR x) AND (z OR NOT x) is UNSAT, but with B1=(z OR y), the conjunction is SAT at z=1. Therefore a valid point-local certificate does not imply UNSAT of the source.
2. B0=(z OR x) AND (z OR NOT x), B1=(NOT z OR y) AND (NOT z OR NOT y): for z=0 B0 fails, and for z=1 B1 fails. The full formula is UNSAT yet neither block is UNSAT without fixing z. A fixed globally refutable block is not necessary.
3. If supposedly private variables overlap, the theorem fails: B0=x and B1=NOT x are individually SAT while their conjunction is UNSAT, even when z is empty. The source partition is a real proof obligation.
4. Combining a purported OR of different local failure cases without proving that their cubes cover every z is UNSOUND. A successful local proof may be reused across cubes only after proving the stronger cube-refutation statement.

## Distinct polarity/access models (PROVED for logical implications, CONDITIONAL for complexity transfer)

**Free correct-polarity routing:** an external choice of YES on satisfiable sources and NO on unsatisfiable sources already contains the original answer. Its existence proves neither a uniform SAT decider nor P=NP. If both one-sided specialists are truly sound, a deterministic dovetailer may run both and accept only verified YES or NO; total decision requires guaranteed termination of at least one, with all calls/failed attempts charged.

**Uniform deterministic sound specialist:** no input-dependent answer advice, all paths and source reads generated by the same machine. Output YES only after full SAT witness replay, NO only after full refutation/cover replay, otherwise UNKNOWN. Soundness is proved here; polynomial total coverage/acquisition is OPEN.

**Supplied nondeterministic correct path of k bits:** require a *single* verifier and an explicit bound on ALL bits representing the transcript, selectors, local refutations, row/cube labels and adaptive strategy. On SAT, existential path with a verifiable complete witness yields an NP-style accepting branch, provided time/space costs are borne by that same machine. On UNSAT, existential proof of a complete universal-z cover yields coNP-style membership if the proof is polynomially bounded and polynomially verifiable on EVERY UNSAT input. In particular an all-CNF polynomial UNSAT certificate system implies NP=coNP, a major additional hypothesis. Wrong guessed branches MUST reject/UNKNOWN, never assert false opposite outputs. A sole good branch for each z is a \(\forall z\exists\) fact, not a uniform existential proof: either encode a verifiable whole strategy or explicitly branch over each universal z and charge all children. With K total binary choice/certificate bits, deterministic exhaustive enumeration of the 2^K possibilities may take \(\widetilde O(2^K(T_{verify}+K))\) and peak \(O(S_{verify}+K)\) if streamed (plus retained state). One k-bit witness cannot conceal a separate free input-specific advice string or unchecked child answers.

**Read-only versus writable space:** a verifier with random-access read-only provided advice is a different model from standard nondeterministic small-space RAM, whose nondeterministic guess bits cannot be reread without writing them into charged work space [3]. Do not transplant NTISP/DTISP lower bounds to a machine with free random-access certificate or oracle. Include certificate generation or exhaustive replay/access costs and certificate representation. Output, input address counters, recursion, caches and retained ancestor state all count on the same execution.

## Resource ledger for the supplied layout (CONDITIONAL upper bound, NOT an achieved exponent)

Let at most R=n^{.195} rows, B=n^{.65} blocks per row, authenticated block description length m=n^{.35}, and shared assignment length h=|z|. For physically separate blocks the raw description can be \(N=\Theta(RBm)=\Theta(n^{1.195})\); if rows are implicit/reused, their construction and reads must still be counted. With a deterministic general SAT *decision* seed on m-bit blocks running in \(m^{p+o(1)}\) time and \(m^{s+o(1)}\) work space, naive full pointwise coverage checks have the conservative bound
\[
T_{all}\le T_{parse+materialize}+2^h n^{.845+.35\max(p,1)+o(1)}
 +T_{failed+child+replay+cover+output},
\]
where no overhead summand is free. Space for a streaming one-block verifier is at least the actual seed's \(n^{.35s+o(1)}\), source buffers, recursive state, coverage materialization and indices. A full original SAT witness must also be constructed and validated. If h<=\kappa log_2 n and all nonseed overhead stays below the target, the bare seed exponent required for time at most n^{1.33+o(1)} is \(p\le (1.33-.845-\kappa)/.35\); at \(\kappa=0\), p<=1.385714..., while p=2 alone yields exponent 1.545. An unspecified polynomial-time SAT seed does NOT imply the claimed n^1.33 upper. If h=n^.195, naive 2^h is superpolynomial. The space target n^.5 polylog requires s<=10/7 for a single materialized block before other state, NOT merely that p is finite. Standard witness extraction from a decision-only seed may multiply seed calls per witness by m, increasing the worst-case exponent.

The displayed inequality is a possible exhaustive upper algorithm under precise assumptions, not a lower bound on clever adaptive coverage or a proof that such proof objects can be found. A compact source representation, BDD cube merge, dynamic programming or algebraic elimination remains legitimate; each must supply its own acquisition and SAME_REALIZATION costs. If alternative row semantics are intended, the verifier must rederive the exact outer Boolean composition; do not use the row-OR theorem without a source-certified equivalence.

## Sourced simultaneous time-space comparison and scope

Random-access machine, input **bit length N**, no answer advice. Fortnow and van Melkebeek [1] give an explicit deterministic SAT forbidden region \(T=N^a,S=N^b\) for a<\phi and \(b<(((a+2)/a^2)-a)/2\). For a=1.33 it yields b<0.276263; it does NOT by itself exclude (1.33,.5). Van Melkebeek's survey [2, Thms 1.2--1.5] gives deterministic SAT time exponent <2cos(pi/7) approximately 1.8019 at subpolynomial space, plus a two-machine theorem: if \((c-1)d<1\) OR \(cd(d-1)-2d+1<0\), some e>0 excludes BOTH a co-nondeterministic SAT N^c machine and a deterministic SAT N^d,N^e machine. The theorem does not specify e=.5.

For the opposite polarity, nondeterministic TAUT (=co-nondeterministic SAT) admits no \(N^d\)-time \(N^{o(1)}\)-space algorithm for d<cube_root(4) ~1.5874, as in Diehl--van Melkebeek--Williams [3]. Their two-machine theorem rules out BOTH nondeterministic TAUT N^c (unrestricted space) and nondeterministic TAUT N^d,N^e when c^2 d<4 for some e>0. The extended survey [2, Thm 1.5] also lists \((c^2-1)d<c\). Fortnow [2, Thm 1.1] rules out a near-linear-time and a polynomial-time \(N^{1-\epsilon}\)-space (co-)nondeterministic SAT pair. These exclusions do NOT say a generic nondeterministic SAT witness verifier in N^1.33,N^.5 is forbidden; nondeterministic SAT and nondeterministic TAUT have fundamentally different semantics. A restricted proof-system refutation lower bound is neither of the above arbitrary-machine lower bounds.

If all separate block descriptions are truly N=Theta(n^1.195), the proposed n^1.33,n^.5 becomes N^1.11297,N^.41841. In that normalization, the explicit deterministic forbidden-region boundary is b<0.70006. Thus a hypothetical *uniform arbitrary N-bit-CNF SAT decider* at this pair would contradict [1]. This does NOT exclude a solver restricted to the promised layout: a verified near-linear reduction from all N-bit CNFs into such layouts, preserving all costs and length, is MISSING. Conversely if n already is original source bit length, one cannot reinterpret it as n^1.195. No unconditional P/NP conclusion follows.
