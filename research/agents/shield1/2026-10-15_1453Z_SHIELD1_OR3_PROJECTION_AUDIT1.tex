\section{The explicit gadget}
Let $A,B$ be proper 3-CNFs on disjoint source variables, with ordered
clauses $C=(a\vee b\vee c)$ and $D=(d\vee e\vee f)$, respectively.
Give each occurrence a fresh private variable $y_C$ or $z_D$, and add
one fresh selector $s$. Set
\[
\begin{split}
{\cal O}(A,B)={}&\bigwedge_{C\in A}
[(s\vee a\vee y_C)\wedge(\neg y_C\vee b\vee c)]\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{D\in B}
[(\neg s\vee d\vee z_D)\wedge(\neg z_D\vee e\vee f)].
\end{split}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Semantic and syntactic shield]
The formula is proper and clause-distinct; its numbers of clauses and
variables are $2(m_A+m_B)$ and $v_A+v_B+m_A+m_B+1$.
For all fixed source assignments $(\alpha,\beta)$,
\[
 \exists s,\mathbf y,\mathbf z\ {\cal O}(\alpha,\beta,s,\mathbf y,\mathbf z)
 \iff A(\alpha)\vee B(\beta).
\]
More strongly, literal/constant substitutions
\[
\rho_A:\ s=0,\ y_C=\neg a,\ z_D=0,\qquad
\rho_B:\ s=1,\ y_C=0,\ z_D=\neg d
\]
fixing source variables send \({\cal O}\) clause-by-clause to
$A$ plus Boolean tautologies and to $B$ plus Boolean tautologies.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $s=0$. A private pair is equivalent under existential elimination
of $y_C$ to $(a\vee b\vee c)$; the disabled $B$ pair is satisfied by
$z_D=0$. For $s=1$, the roles reverse. Under $\rho_A$ the active
pair is exactly $(a\vee\neg a)\wedge(a\vee b\vee c)$,
and every inactive clause is true. The other case is symmetric.
Every clause contains its own fresh auxiliary and two other distinct
underlying variables. These facts also prove properness, distinctness
and the stated occurrence counts.
\end{proof}

\section{Proof-system closure}
\begin{theorem}[Resolution]\label{res}
A general Resolution refutation with $L$ nodes maps to a Resolution
refutation of a selected leaf with at most $L$ nodes, and no greater width.
Tree-likeness is preserved if present.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Maintain for each target proof node either a tautology marker or a
derived subclause of its literal-substituted clause. If the image
of a resolvent is tautological, use the marker. Otherwise, if its
pivot maps to a Boolean constant, the parent whose pivot becomes
false subsumes the image. If the pivot maps to a literal $\ell$,
either one parent loses its pivot and subsumes the image, or both
parents retain complementary copies of $\ell$ and one Resolution
step derives a subclause of the image. A parent made tautological
through its pivot is unnecessary, while a tautology unrelated to
the pivot makes the resolvent tautological. Thus the empty
root survives. Variable identification need not preserve
regularity; general Resolution hardness suffices to lower-bound
the regular subsystem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Binary semantic CP and syntactic CP]
Every $L$-line binary-premise semantic CP refutation of the shield
projects to an $O(L)$-line semantic CP refutation of a selected leaf,
with polynomial coefficient bit overhead. The same holds for
syntactic CP with integer rounding.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A source Boolean assignment maps to a target Boolean assignment,
so each valid two-premise semantic inference remains valid.
The only non-source axiom images in the local shield are
$0\ge0$, $x\ge0$ and $x+y\ge0$, which follow in constantly
many steps from Boolean bounds. Syntactic sums and nonnegative
scalings commute with affine literal substitution.
At a division step by $d$, all original coefficients are
multiples of $d$. Hence the induced constant shift $\kappa$
is a multiple of $d$ and
\[
\left\lceil (b-\kappa)/d\right\rceil
=\left\lceil b/d\right\rceil-\kappa/d.
\]
Thus projected division is legal, and coefficient bit lengths
grow by at most polynomial factors.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic scope and counterexample]
PC, PCR, and equality-style SoS \emph{degree} lower bounds
transfer to the shield by these substitutions. Explicit-monomial
PCR proof size transfers up to constant local axiom repair.
General explicit-monomial PC size does not admit a linear-size
substitution transfer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the active $A$ pair, the Boolean clause polynomials become
$a(1-a)=-(a^2-a)$ and $(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)$; inactive images are
zero. Every Boolean axiom maps to a Boolean axiom or zero.
Substitution cannot increase degree. A PC multiplication by
a variable mapped to $1-x$ can be simulated using multiplication
by $x$ and subtraction without increasing degree. SoS sums of
squares remain such after substitution. In PCR, map the rail for
$y_C$ to the complementary rail of $a$ and vice versa.
Every monomial maps to one monomial or zero, with
$a\bar a$ repaired using $a+\bar a-1$ and $a^2-a$.
In contrast, the one-monomial PC expression
$\prod_{i=1}^k y_i$ projects to
$\prod_{i=1}^k(1-a_i)$, with $2^k$ distinct monomials.
Hence a blanket constant-factor PC monomial-size claim is false.
\end{proof}

\section{Four-leaf family and source uniformity}
Write $\Phi(A,B,C,D)={\cal O}({\cal O}(A,B),{\cal O}(C,D))$.
With $M_0=\sum_i m_i$ and $V_0=\sum_i v_i$,
\[
m(\Phi)=4M_0,\qquad v(\Phi)=V_0+3M_0+3.
\]
Every leaf is a literal/constant image of the same formula.
Thus, if all four leaves are UNSAT, the one shield inherits
all available lower bounds of the listed proof systems,
with \emph{maximum}, not sum, combination.

Let $U_0$ consist of all eight sign-pattern clauses
over three variables, an explicit proper UNSAT anchor.
The canonical structurally tagged four-leaf shield language
is NP-complete, since
$H\mapsto\Phi(H,U_0,U_0,U_0)$ preserves SAT and outputs
$4m_H+96$ clauses and $v_H+3m_H+84$ variables.
In fixed-width binary clause-list encoding, dense proper
distinct-clause source inputs have $m_H\le8\binom{v_H}{3}$,
so bit size grows only by a constant factor.

\noindent\textbf{Uniformity firewall.}
The NP-hardness reduction uses only fixed $U_0$. In contrast,
proof-hard same-instance witnesses may use existentially
chosen all-field graph-colouring cores from a random
high-probability theorem. Such existence is not a polynomial
deterministic hard-core generator. Keep these statements separate.
The stronger LOCALSHIELD1 random-access model transfer
remains to be audited.

\section{Quantitative lower-bound controls}
The FHL semantic CP theorem for clique-versus-colouring gives
$2^{\Omega((r/\log r)^{1/3})}$ proof lines, where $r$ counts
graph vertices. Side-private properization preserves the
signed shared-variable interpolation hypothesis; this is
recorded and separately tested in project PROJECTIONCOVER1.
The properly encoded formula has polynomial, not linear,
size in $r$, so $2^{\Omega(N)}$ in the full shield bit length
is \emph{not} justified. Expander Tseitin supplies the
general Resolution hard core; all-field graph-colouring
supplies the PCR hard core. Their scope is restricted
proof systems, not unrestricted decider runtime.
