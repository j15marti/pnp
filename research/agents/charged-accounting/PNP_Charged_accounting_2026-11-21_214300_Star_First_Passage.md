## Domain, quantifiers and four falsifiers

Fix a deterministic globally correct Boolean compatibility decider A first. One writable finite-alphabet tape initially contains the N encoded bits in consecutive cells 0,...,N−1, all of them nonblank, and the head starts at the left origin. Each primitive transition reads the current cell, may write it, and moves at most one. There is no separate source, free clock, precomputed index or remote-read facility. Output bit and terminator start at the origin. No requirement that the head travel back after its final observation is used. Storage is the baseline occupied span containing origin, head and nonblanks; physical CM sums these spans after each transition, in cells up to the fixed bits-per-cell conversion. Descriptive CM sums K(C_t|A) for the full configurations with a fixed prefix-universal machine. Neither t nor the original input is free. Choose k≥1, then the input distribution, and optionally a center checkpoint. A putative global bound fixes K and a≥1 independently of k.

Use the exact Construction revision [1]. The center's first seven proper clauses exclude every assignment on three variables except 000. Its remaining k clauses are copies of (¬x1,x2,x3). Leaf j changes only the first literal of copy j and is UNSAT. The center is SAT. For c=7+k,d=3, r is the least odd integer greater than c, L=10c, n0=d+(L+1)r+2, m0=(2L+4)r, n=n0+m0+3, m_clause=2m0+4, w=ceil(log2(n+1)). The target layout is unary n and delimiter, gamma(m_clause+1), signed fixed-width triples, two n-bit local witnesses and the w-bit release identifier. Thus

    N = 3n + 2 + 2 floor(log2(m_clause+1)) + 3m_clause(w+1) + w.

Each original clause's controlled word occupies 40r consecutive triples. Changes preserve all public dimensions, other words, both witnesses and release information. Let D_j be the exact set of differing bit offsets and p_j=min D_j, with max D_j<min D_(j+1). Nonemptiness also follows from the opposite decisions. p_k<N−1 since the trailer is shared. For growing k, p_k=Theta(N): the last editable word is preceded by 10(c−1) instructions, each contributing 4r triples of 3(w+1) bits, while N=Theta(c² log(c+1)). These are literal tape addresses under the declared physical layout.

Four falsifiers: (1) a legal earlier transition can read a remote bit; (2) an edit changes earlier metadata or overlaps another edit; (3) the conditional record ends by an uncharged stopping signal or an unreported address; (4) a post-cache record is charged a second time despite containing no first read. The first is excluded only by the local model. The second is excluded by the compiler proof and checked numerically below. The last two are addressed by the code and checkpoint definitions.

## The actual decoder and exact event charge

For each k give the center prior mass 1/2 and each leaf mass 1/(2k). Index J=0 denotes the center; J=j denotes leaf j. On the center all D_j must be read. If some D_j were missed, induction step by step on actual head reads and writes gives identical behavior on the corresponding opposite-label leaf. The output cells are outside D_j. On leaf j the entire run up to the first scan of D_j agrees with the center up to the symbol read there. Since the head starts to the left and moves locally, its first scanned cell of D_j is p_j. On the center these first scans occur in order j=1,...,k. Earlier writes, caches, copies and computations cannot change a cell before its first scan.

Fix any integer tau on the center from 0 through halt. Let v be the number of p_j already scanned by then. Condition on **survival**, meaning J=0 or J>v. This conditioning selects exactly the inputs whose observed source and work histories through tau are the center histories. Here a history includes scanned symbols; equal last control states alone do not suffice. On the selected runs the head position x_tau is common. Put m=k−v. Conditional on survival, the center has probability k/(k+m), and each surviving leaf probability 1/(k+m). Checkpoints in this statement are fixed on the center beforehand; the receiver is not given an input-dependent stopping time for free.

Use one obligation U=J on this conditional domain. Public side information B is the fixed construction, k and v, with survival part of the distribution. Tau and x_tau are needed to state the travel bound but not to decode. No actual input, retained source, state or answer is given to the decoder. E consists of first-scan transitions for p_(v+1),p_(v+2),... up to and including the first discrepant read, or up to p_k if none. Include each event at most once on that execution. On these scans the source bit is still original. Record 0 for equality with the public center bit there and 1 for inequality. Pi ends at the first 1 or after m zeros. For m=0 the unique record is empty.

The fixed prefix-free dictionary is the center word 0^m and the surviving leaf words 0^(i−1)1, 1≤i≤m. The decoder returns 0 on the center word and v+i on the other word. Hence the one-obligation constants are **b=1, h=0, rho=1**. The address of each recorded bit is determined by k,v and its ordinal in the record: it is p_(v+i). To reconstruct it without an oracle, regenerate the public center and hypothetical leaf v+i and find their first differing offset. These are explicitly specified templates independent of the hidden J. A standalone decoder can obtain the whole list by O(k N³) sole-tape work and O(N+k log N)=O(N) span by [1]. Decoder runtime is not credited as a compulsory solver operation. A's real movements to those addresses are charged below. If absolute addresses are transmitted literally, W=ceil(log2(N+1)) extra bits per event suffice, with a weaker capacity W+1. The sharper code has no address-selection or END channel. A need never write this transcript.

For m=0 both quantities below are zero. For general m:

    H(U|B, survival) = log2(k+m) − (k/(k+m)) log2 k.
    E[|E| | survival] = [km + m(m+1)/2]/(k+m).

The first identity uses the posterior above. The second averages the code lengths m,1,...,m with weights k,1,...,1. Kraft's inequality gives H≤E|Pi|=E|E|≤E[T−tau | survival]. This is the requested event inequality with real events and no virtual service. At entry E|E|=(3k+1)/4 and H=1+(log2 k)/2. The exact cardinality does not assert that a solver halts at the first discrepancy. If several checkpoints are summed, the same transition may appear in several E sets and rho is the maximum number of such memberships. This proof takes one checkpoint at a time.

## Travel, physical span and minimum descriptions

For m>0 let P=p_k on the center and P=p_J on a surviving leaf. Since p_(v+1) has not been scanned, x_tau≤p_(v+1). The head could already be sitting there ready to read. It must take at least P−x_tau+1 further transitions through the scan of P. Up to and including that scan, cell N−1 is the original nonblank trailer cell. Reaching it sooner from the origin would already have required a scan at P. Thus on **the exact same execution**, measuring spans in cells,

    T−tau ≥ P−x_tau+1,
    CM_phys(suffix, cells) ≥ N(P−x_tau+1).

This multiplies actual per-transition retention by a common interval of time on that run. It does not multiply unrelated worst-case time and storage. Conditional expectation replaces P by [k p_k + sum_(j>v) p_j]/(k+m). At entry E[T]=Omega(N) and E[CM_phys]=Omega(N²), already from the half-mass center. These statements survive erasure behind the head. At m=0 the first-passage ledger has zero remaining demand; preprocessing may have compared everything. This argument never assumes another visit to the original cut, a fixed query order chosen by a programmer, or a compulsory return trip.

There is a tighter descriptive upper bound on this **star** than replay from N incompressible source bits. A decoder can generate the whole original input from self-delimiting k,J using O(log(k+2)) bits in total, pay for the transition index in O(log(t+2)) further bits, and simulate A. Thus CM_min≤O_A(T(log(k+2)+log(T+2))). On each star input, T≥k up to a fixed constant: the center scans k distinct sites, and even the first possible discrepant site lies past the unary header of length n>k. A leaf must reach its own site. Consequently CM_min≤O_A(T log(T+2)). This charges the input description and time; a short description can take arbitrarily long to decode.

Conversely along any deterministic halting execution without an external clock, the T configurations after transitions 1,...,T are pairwise distinct. A repeated nonhalting configuration would repeat forever. Their distinct shortest descriptions for fixed side information A form a prefix-free set. Kraft and convexity give sum_t K(C_t|A)≥T log2 T. Consequently **CM_min=Theta_A(T log(T+2))** on these star inputs (T≥2), including solvers that deliberately take longer. This is a cost identity in terms of the actual T. It supplies no independent superpolynomial time demand. It also explains why the large physical trailer charge cannot be transferred to descriptive CM. The full NP-hard image can have inputs of much higher description complexity than this special star.
