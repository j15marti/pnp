## Fixed solver and quantified lemma

Fix one deterministic finite-alphabet one-head, one-writable-tape solver A_G before the input. Each transition reads before writing and moves at most one cell. Original input, addresses, retained matrices, copies, caches and fixed-origin terminated output are charged. On every valid encoding, A_G authenticates the canonical compiler image by exact regeneration, uses the admitted mixed-source solver below when its tests pass, and uses exhaustive Boolean SAT fallback otherwise. Internal UNKNOWN is a failed shortcut admission. Malformed encodings are rejected by validation.

The admitted source has c proper ternary clauses on d<=3c occurring names. Exact support tables must be homogeneous parity relations or unsigned exact-one triples, with the exact-one triples variable-disjoint. Let P be their union, p=|P|<=d, and let V be the projection of the parity nullspace onto P. Gaussian elimination and a second reduction produce a deterministic reduced basis b_0,...,b_(s-1) of V, in increasing pivot order. Thus s<=p. Each projected vector extends to a parity solution, and all possible parity-compatible port vectors occur in V.

For every admitted input, the algorithm maintains y=0 initially. Enumerate coefficient strings g(i)=i XOR floor(i/2), i=0,...,2^s-1. At i>=1 set k to the position of the least significant one of i and update y by XOR with b_k. Check every factor triple on y, recording the conjunction of its exact-one tests. Stop at the first success. Return UNSAT if the entire list fails. A full checking sweep is retained even when an early triple fails, so the event membership below is explicit.

Proof of enumeration follows from binary arithmetic. Incrementing i-1 to i changes a suffix of its bits, and XOR with the shifted number cancels every changed coefficient except k. The map i to g(i) is invertible by repeated XOR of shifted bits. Therefore every coefficient string occurs exactly once. Independence of the projected basis makes its linear image injective. Each candidate is consequently visited once, and satisfaction of the factor triples is equivalent to joint feasibility. For s=0 the sole vector is zero. For p=0 the empty conjunction accepts.

An s-bit binary counter, trailing-zero scan and loop control use O(s) bit work per iteration. The basis row has p bits. Update y coordinate by coordinate and scan all factors once, using O(p+c) operations. Since s,p=O(c), the per-candidate bound is O(c), including counter work. No unit-cost operation on a 2^s-bit object is used. Basis row selection, physical addresses and movement are priced separately below.

Four falsifiers are unreduced dependent projected rows causing duplicate candidates, an omitted counter/index tariff, premature UNSAT before all vectors are examined, and multiplication of candidate count by simultaneous storage of every candidate. The second elimination, explicit counter cost, exhaustive stopping rule and single retained vector respectively address them. Alternative solvers may perform less work, and no universal enumeration requirement is derived.
