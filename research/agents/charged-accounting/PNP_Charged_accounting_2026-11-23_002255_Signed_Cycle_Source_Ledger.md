## Claim and scope

Fix a uniform deterministic machine \(A\) first, correct for both answers on all valid explicitly encoded positive Boolean exact-one triple instances. For every finite simple publicly fixed base graph \(G=(V,E)\), every binary edge signing \(z\), and every reachable prefix of the execution on its canonical diamond source, let \(F\) be the edges whose signal the machine has physically read by that point. A signal is an original source bit whose value changes when that edge alone is toggled. We prove below that every such bit has exactly one owner and that one read reveals its owner's binary label. The machine may keep, combine or discard everything it has obtained. We place no checkpoint-factorization requirement on its work tape.

On an UNSAT input, the exact minimum number of further edge signals sufficient to certify that verdict against all completions of the same source family is

\[
d_-(z,F)=\min_{C:\ \bigoplus_{e\in C} z_e=1}|C\setminus F|,
\]

where \(C\) ranges over simple cycles. Every correct execution has at least this many **distinct future first reads**. A SAT execution must first read every as-yet-unread nonbridge edge. This is an exact certificate statement with a one-way implication for all-input runtime. Finding a cheapest cycle or authenticating the input can cost more than exhibiting its signals. A paid global cache survives in full. If an odd cycle was already observed, \(d_-=0\).

On one odd \(d\)-cycle, let \(r=d-|F|\). Pointwise, at least \(r\) further source transitions occur. Under uniform odd signings and a prefix recoverable by replay, the unread source information is exactly \((r-1)_+\) bits conditional on that prefix. At its actual first-read events we also prove a physical cumulative lower bound \(\Omega(Nr)\) pointwise and a minimum-description cumulative lower bound of \(\sum_{i=1}^{r}(r-i-1)_+\) bits in conditional expectation. These three measures charge different things.

Four ways to falsify the argument are a source field that leaks a function of two tags, a cache containing an unread tag obtained without a charged source event, a stopping rule whose definition reveals a future tag, and multiple charges to the same transition as a cycle member. The first two are excluded by the raw-layout and replay proofs. The entropy claim requires an adapted stopping rule. Each event below has one owner even on overlapping cycles.

## Encoded source and support proof

Use the existing canonical generator and dense names, with the exact bit order used by the two peer encoders. An edge \(u<v\) of index \(j\) gets private fibers \(a=q+2j,b=a+1\). The five matchings are identity on \(ua,ub,ab\), and the same tag \(p_e\) on \(va,vb\). Here \(p_e\) is identity or a fixed transposition \((01)\). Each fiber is a triangle. The star generator turns its coloring graph into positive Boolean exact-one triples. The established local diamond relation is \(f_v=f_u\circ p_e\) for the two terminal bijections. Therefore consistency is exactly \(z_{uv}=x_u\oplus x_v\) for some vertex bits, even with disconnected components. Rooted paths give the converse and extend the interiors independently. Both input labels are allowed to \(A\), including when we analyze an UNSAT-only distribution. [1,2]

With \(q=|V|,\ k=|E|,\ h=3(q+2k)\), the source has \(n=33q+111k\) declared variables and \(m=21q+87k\) rows. The physical stream is unary \(n\) followed by zero, Elias gamma of \(m+1\), then three incremented IDs per ordered row, each of width \(w=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil\). Thus \(N=n+2+2\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor+3mw\). Alphabet symbols 0 and 1 are distinct from blank. There is no preloaded graph, index, checksum or tag array on the machine. The public graph is decoder side information for a fixed input class. [1,2]

**Why the compiler does not mix two edges into one physical bit.** The star generator emits \(4h\) tag-independent rows first. It then sorts expanded graph edges \((x,y)\) lexicographically and writes three rows per edge with fields \(6x+c,6y+c,8h+3j'+c\), before the common ID increment. For fixed \(x\), destinations in distinct fibers lie in disjoint ordered intervals. Inside its own fiber the destinations and their order are fixed. In each adjacent higher matching fiber there is exactly one destination for \(x\). Changes of its place can never change the relative order of **two different fibers**. Thus the sorted slot \(j'\), source endpoint, private identifier, width, and every header bit are tag-independent. A varying second-endpoint field depends on precisely one original edge, even though its tag occurs in two private matchings. A bit with two different values at that slot equals \(z_e\) or its complement. Every edge has at least one varying bit since some destinations change and equal-width binary IDs distinguish them. Let \(P_e\) be all such addresses. The \(P_e\) are nonempty and pairwise disjoint. Arbitrary input reorderings are outside this raw-layout result. An all-input-correct solver remains free to reorder its tape while running. [2]

## Actual-event and certificate proof

Read-before-write single tape, unit-speed head, fixed finite control, no external clock or original-input oracle, terminated verdict at the left origin. Use posttransition configurations. For edge \(e\), \(\tau_e\) is the first transition that scans any address in \(P_e\), if it occurs. Until that transition all cells in \(P_e\) retain their original values. Silent destruction or copying of a still-unseen signal is impossible on this machine. No work cell or control register can already contain data depending on that tag when all other independent input tags are fixed. Logical deductions under a restricted analysis prior are allowed. The correctness domain still includes the opposite-label alternatives.

Induct on the transitions. Given the fixed program and graph, simulate from the original layout with unresolved cells marked symbolically. On first physical access to a new \(P_e\), consume one bit of the record, invert the known polarity if needed, and resolve that tag throughout the original template. Honor every write generated by the simulation, including overwrites and relocation. Rereading an earlier cell uses its current content. Seen and overwritten locations, head positions, directions, all control, addresses, auxiliary state, storage allocation, time-dependent loops internal to the machine, and cache contents observed or created by the solver follow from replay. Every other source cell is public or previously resolved. In particular **exactly** the edge words agreeing on observed labels have the same stopped history within this family, if the stopping rule is determined by that history. The suffix lower bound on a given run also follows by replay of the complete run, without assigning probabilities to a checkpoint. Unopened source cells remain physical unknowns available for later charged access. Giving their values freely to the decoder would erase the entropy demand.

If an observed subgraph has odd parity around a cycle, its labels exclude every balanced completion. Conversely, if its every cycle has even parity, assign a root bit in each observed component, propagate potentials along a forest, and label all remaining edges by the XOR of endpoint potentials. This balanced completion agrees on all observed labels. A halted rejecting run must therefore contain the observed edges of an odd cycle. Applying this to the *same run's final observed set* proves the lower bound \(d_-\) from every prefix. Revealing the missing labels of a minimizing cycle attains the additional family certificate size. On SAT, flipping an unseen nonbridge alone creates an odd cycle and contradicts a putative identical accepting run. Knowing all nonbridges suffices because every cycle avoids bridges. The SAT certificate increment is exactly the number of unseen nonbridges. An individual bit in each \(P_e\) recovers the corresponding label, so these certificate minima apply to informative raw bits as well. [3]

As an independently computable description of the minimum, assign weights zero to observed edges and one to unobserved edges. Duplicate each vertex into two parity states, crossing layers precisely on negative edges. Minimize the distance from \((v,0)\) to \((v,1)\) over all \(v\). Every odd cycle lifts to such a path. Any resulting odd closed walk contains a simple odd cycle with no greater weight, by splitting at repeated vertices and dropping backtracks. This shows equality even with shared edges and zero-weight walks. The calculation takes the full signing as input. Its answer is not advice supplied to \(A\).

## Conditional decoder, capacity and reuse

Specialize to the cycle of length \(d\ge3\), with all \(2^d\) inputs in the correctness domain. Analyze a uniformly chosen odd signing. Let \(\sigma\) be any publicly specified stopping rule determined by already simulated transitions and observed values, reached on the inputs considered. Include halting as a terminal case, or use a bounded total rule. No selection by a future answer or an oracle for the last necessary read is used.

For a single suffix let \(U=z\), \(B=(A,G,\text{encoding, prior, stop rule, ordered prefix signal payloads})\). The past tape, including all data-dependent work and known source content, is reproducible from \(B\) with unopened input represented symbolically. Supply the conditional record \(\Pi\) of raw first-signal payloads at \(E=\{\tau_e:\tau_e>\sigma\}\) in chronological order. Every edge on the unique cycle must be seen on **both** labels, since flipping that single tag changes the verdict. Therefore \(|E|=r\), and replay maps the raw payloads to all remaining indexed tags and ultimately \(U\). For each fixed \(B\), \(r\) is known. All suffix records have that fixed length, including the empty record when \(r=0\). They are conditionally prefix-free. Addresses and timing need no fields because replay computes them from actual preceding transitions. The decoder is an information decoder, without a polynomial running-time assumption.

Every selected transition reads precisely one original binary symbol and belongs to exactly one edge set. The effective capacity is \(b=1\), additional framing \(h=0\), and maximum time-event reuse \(\rho=1\). Input framing is physically read whenever \(A\) reads it. Its length and location are determined by \(G\) for replay. When \(r>0\), exactly \(2^{r-1}\) equally likely odd completions remain. At \(r=0\) there is one. For any stopped history \(B=b_0\),

\[
\mathbb E[T-\sigma\mid B=b_0]\ \ge r \ \ge H(U\mid B=b_0)=(r-1)_+ .
\]

The last unknown bit has zero conditional entropy on the odd prior yet still requires a read to distinguish the legal even completion. This is the distinction between reconstructing a random label vector and deciding correctly on every input.

For any fixed finite partition of the execution by such ordered stopping times (repeat the halt time for empty intervals), let \(U_j\) be the chronologically ordered sequence of newly revealed edge identities and labels in interval \(j\). \(B_j\) includes the previous intervals' complete payloads and public rules. The interval's \(\Pi_j\) is its ordered raw signal bits. Replay stops at its next boundary, making the possible records prefix-free given \(B_j\). A proper extension of a completed record would have stopped at the same earlier step. Conversely, \(U_j,B_j\) determine \(\Pi_j\), including each raw polarity. Thus conditional entropies agree. The partition covers the whole cycle execution, so the combined transcripts determine \(z\) and are determined by \(z\). Their conditional entropies telescope to \(d-1\). The disjoint event sets and the conditional binary coding inequality give the requested form [4]:

\[
\mathbb E T\ \ge\
\frac{\sum_j H(U_j\mid B_j)-\sum_j\mathbb E h_j}{b\rho}
=d-1,\qquad T\ge d\ \text{pointwise}.
\]

This multi-interval equality is for a whole execution starting before the first signal on the uniform odd cycle. Applying the one-suffix formula at many nested prefixes and adding those values with reuse one would charge overlapping transitions.

## Cumulative costs on the same execution

Let \(t_i\) be the \(i\)-th distinct new signal transition **after** the chosen stop, for \(1\le i\le r\). Fix \(B=b_0\) and the first \(i\) signal payloads. Replay determines which tags are still unopened. Every such edge has a fixed representative address in \(P_e\) which has never been scanned. Its symbol in the actual posttransition configuration therefore still determines that tag. Conditional on these records, the \(2^{(r-i-1)_+}\) equally likely completions give distinct complete configurations at \(t_i\). Together with determinism this is a bijection onto this conditional support. It remains true if earlier cells have been destroyed, data moved, the parity accumulated in control, or paths selected adaptively.

Selecting one shortest prefix program per complete configuration yields a prefix code for fixed \(A\). Even within a conditioned fiber, Kraft's inequality lower-bounds its expected length by the log of that fiber's cardinality. The scored descriptions themselves do not get \(B,z,t_i\) free. Summing charges at distinct times on each run gives

\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{r}K(C_{t_i}\mid A)\ \middle|\ B=b_0\right]
\ge\sum_{i=1}^{r}(r-i-1)_+
=\begin{cases}(r-1)(r-2)/2&r\ge2,\\0&r<2.\end{cases}
\]

This is a lower bound on residence of information, not a demand to transmit fresh bits at every step. In particular \(H(\text{verdict}\mid C_t)=0\) is consistent with it. No positive term is claimed for a specific compressible input solely from this expectation. A member of each positive-probability stopped fiber meets the average bound while satisfying the pointwise time and physical bounds.

Separately take \(s_t\) to be one plus the tape interval length covering origin, head and nonblanks, in cell units or their fixed bit conversion. The public prefix through the \(4h\) fixed rows has \(L=n+2+2\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor+12hw\) bits. Every \(t_i\) reads at position at least \(L\) (zero origin). The posttransition head is at least \(L-1\) even if the cell is erased. Thus \(\sum_i s_{t_i}\ge rL\). On a cycle \(q=k=d,\ h=9d,\ n=144d,\ m=108d\), giving \(L\ge N/3\). Hence physical suffix CM at these events is at least \(Nr/3\) in the stated cell convention. The physical bound does not require random data. Other transitions can dominate the true cumulative cost.
