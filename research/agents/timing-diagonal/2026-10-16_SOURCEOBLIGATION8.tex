\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let $A$ be a correct deterministic SAT decider; construct $A_d$ by performing $d(|x|)$ deterministic no-op steps before its output. For every $x$, $A_d(x)=A(x)=\mathrm{SAT}(x)$, and for polynomial $d$, polynomial running time is preserved. However, completion time and full labeled trace differ. With a logical event-indexed external bitstream, a challenge may differ. Thus no universal function $h(x,\mathrm{SAT}(x))$ can equal an encoding that distinguishes actual traces of all correct implementations. If a wrapper returns different SAT bits on the same $x$, and both wrapper correctness claims require equality to $\mathrm{SAT}(x)$, those claims cannot both be true. Any correctness theorem must connect the wrapper answer to the root formula's satisfiability for all schedules and padding choices.
