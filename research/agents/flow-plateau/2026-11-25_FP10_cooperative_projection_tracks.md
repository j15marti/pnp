## A. Mixed rotation/swap palette terminal
At every proper vertex compare proposed output permutation with the three distinct original colors. The six permutations split into identity, three transpositions (change exactly two edges), and two nonidentity cyclic rotations (change all three). For each nonzero edge encode its new color by two binary activation coordinates indexed by the two nonzero change directions unequal to its original color; 00 means unchanged, 10/01 the two alternatives, 11 forbidden. Share these bits at its endpoints. For a given repaired e0 color r, each defective endpoint has precisely two proper local permutations, hence its activation relation is bijunctive (two words admit a Boolean selector and all coordinates are constant or that selector/complement).
At each good vertex supply one type: S permits identity and any of the three transpositions; R permits identity and either rotation. For S the six activation bits form three disjoint pairs, one pair for each possible transposition; bits of each pair are equal and at most one pair is active. For R they form two disjoint triples, one triple for each rotation; bits of each triple are equal and at most one triple active. Each relation is expressible exactly by 2-CNF. Intersection across all edges and fixed-r endpoints is therefore one exact polynomial 2SAT computation. Arbitrarily many vertices can rotate; their actual branch syndrome need not be specified. Any proper target is in the palette assigning S to odd local permutations and R to even (identity can use either). A supplied type partition is not a semantic contraction. Type discovery remains open. Exact local pair-closure enumeration confirms S=4 words, R=3; the union of their pairwise consequences admits 18 words, only 6 proper local permutations.
Partial type assignments: leave untyped vertices at their common pairwise relaxation. This is a necessary relaxation; an SCC contradiction soundly excludes all completions. Given a failed full word, deletion-minimize its partial-type conflict by at most n queries. If every failed word has an acquired conflict on at most two type literals, accumulate binary nogoods and solve their 2SAT, with at most O(n^2) distinct additions (each excludes current candidate), plus polynomial oracle calls. A larger core can instead be retained in a bounded-width factor DP, with no unconditional complexity bound. If ternary projection derives signed orientation equivalences, those are necessary on canonical type assignments, and can be used to group or constrain type choices; an identity is allowed in either palette, so such constraints on canonical choices are for witness coverage, not claims every relaxed output has that sign. Always verify actual coloring. Source UNSAT only from complete sound cover/refutation, never from a failed type word.

## B. Exact long type-conflict barrier, and parity collaboration
Choose r different from the repeated original color at BOTH defective endpoints. Then each endpoint's remaining two edges lie on its r-direction projected cycle. Fixing e0 to r requires exactly one of its two r-activations to be 1, the other 0 (either endpoint permutation allowed). Suppose those endpoints belong to distinct r-cycles. Under any partial assignment selecting only S types, activation propagates in direction r through each typed vertex (equalities of the pair), and stops at an untyped vertex. Untyped relaxation contains every subset of a single-transposition activation pair, as the common pairwise restrictions forbid only pairs of simultaneous 1's. Other directions can remain zero. Distinct cycles cannot collide. Hence this relaxed 2SAT oracle is infeasible EXACTLY when one of the two endpoint r-cycles has all of its good vertices typed S: full equalities would identify its two contradictory endpoint bits. Otherwise assign each equality component containing the endpoint's chosen active seed to 1 and all others 0, giving a compatible relaxation. Minimum cardinality of an all-S conflict is the smaller number of good vertices on the two cycles.
FP01 START has no zero-branch target, so endpoints cannot be on same r-cycle for such r (a bichromatic path between them could repair e0 using only transpositions). In the dense serial FP04/FP05 lift each protected original wire crosses L successive Petersen four-poles. In proper initial module coloring any bichromatic path entering along a wire exits at its same-pair terminal, by exact pair relation and Kempe exchange (FP06). Thus the two cycles in distinguished copy remain distinct and each length grows Theta(L). Lift n=2312 L^2+408L, cyclically four-edge-connected by FP04 construction. Therefore every all-S partial-type conflict at chosen r has Omega(sqrt(n)) pins, despite a two-branch proper repair. This falsifies a universal bounded direct conflict-width claim for this specific relaxation, not general proof complexity. The binary syndrome parity track bypasses the long conflict: each endpoint cycle requires odd sum of actual h on its good vertices, immediately requiring at least one rotation there. Long conflicts may be easy algebraically; no general progress proof.

## C. Collective domain-closure tracks
Maintain exact local domains D_v subsets of six permutations under the source and current guard. Iterate local edge support propagation and three necessary relaxations:
- Binary: acquire all pairwise consequences of each local activation-word domain. A global 2SAT refutation is sound; if SAT, verify all local words. To delete a candidate local permutation, pin its activation bits and refute by SCC. For a fixed e0=r the local encoding is injective. All tests paid.
- Matching: for each output color c, allowed edges are those with a compatible occurrence of c in both endpoint domains. Every proper coloring gives a perfect matching in this allowed graph. If none exists reject guard. For each allowed edge not in an acquired matching, force it by removing its endpoints and solving the remaining matching problem. If impossible delete that color at its endpoints. Each probe is polynomial.
- Ternary: use local labels 0,1,2 in incidence order and exact permutation pairs (s_v,t_v) over F3 with s_v nonzero. Every edge equality is linear. For each current local domain add all affine equations valid on its at most six pairs. Solve the combined linear relaxation. Project its affine solution space to each local pair and remove any unsupported permitted permutation. This is stronger than relying on global rank alone and can use additional certified sign-equivalence constraints. No assumption affine hull is exact.
At most 6n strict local-value deletions, a full sweep costs polynomial (constant-size domain tables, polynomial SCC/matching/linear calls). A common witness is verified. Infeasibility of a necessary relaxation refutes that guard. Otherwise stagnation DECLINE, not UNSAT. Can combine with adaptive palette branching and exact width DP, charging every executed branch; only a bounded aggregate tree or log branching depth is a polynomial terminal, not established universally.

## D. Exact two-view labeled T-join relaxation
A good vertex is an edge of a bipartite multigraph Q whose endpoints are its containing projected cycles in two chosen directions. Its third-cycle incidence is a binary label l_e. Selection h satisfies the two chosen parity equations iff boundary in Q is the set T of odd-demand cycles. Since the original near flow has two defective endpoints, |T|<=4. Third-view condition is XOR of labels equal to a prescribed b. Choose a spanning forest of Q and assign vertex potential L_v as XOR of labels along root-tree path. Replace label l_e by l_e+L_u+L_v; tree labels vanish and target becomes b+XOR_{v in T}L_v. This preserves equality for every T-join by incidence cancellation. The resulting labels span exactly the cycle-label image (fundamental cycles), dimension k, and all labels/target can be reduced to k binary coordinates by Gaussian elimination, rejecting a target outside that span.
The minimum cardinality (or nonnegative weighted cost) of an h satisfying all three parity views can then be found in 2^{O(k)} poly(N): build lifted graph (Q vertex, k-bit label state), each edge updates label by XOR and retains cost. For each possible terminal pairing (at most3 for |T|=4), obtain minimum path walks for every label between paired terminals. Also compute least cost closed walk for each label by shortest paths between (v,0) and (v,l) over all v. Dynamic programming over group labels (shortest paths on label group for arbitrary sum of closed walks) combines terminal paths and cycles to hit target. Correctness: a feasible T-join decomposes into paths pairing terminals and cycles, hence the computed optimum walk cost is no larger than its cost. Conversely XOR of recovered walks is a valid edge subset of correct boundary/label, with cost no greater than walk cost for nonnegative weights. These inequalities give exact minimum and witness. Disconnected Q handled by unreachable pairs. All searches and explicit edge masks charged; k=O(log N) suffices polynomial. This is a parity-only relaxation; pass candidate to FP06 fixed-h SCC or mixed type terminal, not a standalone source decision. For each graph three choices of view pair give different ranks and candidate tie choices.
