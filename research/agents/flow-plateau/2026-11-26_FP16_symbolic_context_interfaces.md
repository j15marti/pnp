## 1. Exact implicit matching interface
Let a region consist of disjoint groups with nonempty explicit FP15 context-safe perfect-pairing tables R_g, independent phase witnesses, internal wire matching W, and p exposed ports B (precisely those unmatched by W). Union of legal transition edges U_g. Complete region relation R_B consists of boundary pairings realized by a choice of one legal word per group, tracing paths through W; internal closed circuits unmarked and irrelevant. R_B can have factorial size. Do not enumerate it. For query distinct i,j in B and partial matching C on B minus {i,j}, delete unused exposed ports B minus ({i,j} union V(C)) from the relaxed graph with edges W union C union all U_g. The baseline W union C covers every remaining vertex except i,j. Perfect matching in this reduced graph iff there is a simple alternating i-j path: symmetric difference with baseline gives the path in one direction, toggling it gives matching in the other. Legal phase choices imply such a path. Conversely apply FP15 contextual rerouting to each constituent group once. Remove its path transitions; exterior path segments give a partial matching context on that group's ports, with first and last entry attached to i,j. Select a legal table word maintaining connectivity; path may change but transitions in previously processed groups remain a subset of their already chosen words. If group unused choose any word. Restore all deleted ports and selected legal words, trace boundary pairing, retain actual original phase witnesses. The restored graph has boundary degree1 and interior degree2, and still i-j connected in the queried context. Thus exact answer including NO and lift for every partial context; no claim of simultaneous multiple terminal-pair demands. Graph size O(explicit primitive table incidence + internal wires), deterministic polynomial matching and linear scans per word. This is a compositional implicit interface for FP15, not a new universal terminal for unsafe primitives. Pin independent unsafe originals before applying, charging all branches.

## 2. Safe composition need not permit flat marginal replacement
Two four-port square switches on 0..3 and 4..7, options {(0,2),(1,3)} or {(0,3),(1,2)} and shifted copies. Internal wire (3,4). Boundary B=(0,1,2,5,6,7). Exact four boundary words:
((0,2),(1,6),(5,7)); ((0,2),(1,7),(5,6)); ((0,6),(1,2),(5,7)); ((0,7),(1,2),(5,6)).
The union contains (0,6),(1,7); with external C={(6,7)}, relaxed flat union connects 0 to1. No actual word connects 0 to1 under C: the single internal wire cannot carry both crossing transitions. Retaining internal gadget correctly rejects. Source-derived square group can be closed to a simple connected cubic bipartite singleton chart by adding wire (2,5), new s attached to 0,1, t attached to 6,7, z=st, a union of square edges, b all wires except z plus one phase per square. C is a hypothetical external partial context for testing flattening, NOT the full actual chart of that closure. Refutes closure of context safety under replacing an arbitrary composition by the union of individual boundary pairs; not closure under retaining the actual gadget.

## 3. Polynomial certificate and witness dictionary for complete multipartite union
Let U_B be the union of exact boundary transitions (acquire by p choose2 queries from section1 with C empty). Suppose U_B complete multipartite, not necessarily bipartite. Its part partition is recoverable as connected components of complement and verified by exact edge comparison. Then R_B is context-safe iff for each part A, every distinct i,j in A and distinct u,v outside A have an attainable word containing pairs i-u,j-v or i-v,j-u. Necessity: relaxed path i,u,v,j with C={(u,v)} exists, and i-j direct transition absent; a legal perfect pairing with one external edge must use just those two crossing pairs. Sufficiency: for endpoints in different parts use direct pair; for endpoints in same A take any simple relaxed alternating path. Every transition joins different parts so at least as many outside-A as A vertices on path. All outside-A vertices are incident to context edges (both endpoints in A), whereas only interior A vertices are; therefore at least one context edge joins two outside-A vertices. The required attainable word reconnects endpoints through that one edge. Thus polynomial certification using O(p^4) exact queries, each paid via section1; store full boundary word and actual primitive phase witness from every successful direct/single-edge context. At most O(p^4) distinct words, even if original relation factorial. This dictionary is NOT the exact full pairing relation, but has same union and is equivalent for all single-terminal-pair contexts. Can replace a certified group in outer FP15 solver and lift without enumerating original R_B. Failure of promise or certificate: retain implicit gadget, no source rejection. If only a sufficient known set of actual pairings is acquired and union not authenticated, use witness-only algorithm or retain exact gadget; never use missing transitions as UNSAT authority. No claim context safety of arbitrary marginal union.

## 4. General short-context bound
For graph U on p ports define tau(U) as largest number of ordered oriented vertex-disjoint transition pairs (x_1,y_1),...,(x_l,y_l) in U with x_i y_j NOT in U for every i<j. If tau(U)<=k, each relaxed i-j connection has a simple alternating path with at most k transitions: select shortest such path, write its transitions in traversal order. Any shortcut x_i y_j, i<j, skips intermediate transitions/context edges without repeated vertices and yields a shorter legal relaxed path; thus path length <=tau. The original context may be arbitrarily large, but the path uses at most k-1 of its edges. It suffices to test all contexts with <=k-1 edges: if relaxed YES implies legal YES there, choose such short subcontext of any longer one and its legal witness remains connected in the full context. Conversely safety implies all short tests. For fixed k, test absence of ordered matching length k+1 by explicit O(p^(2k+2)) tuple scan and enumerate O(p^(2k)) short queries. This gives exact certification under promise, including dictionary of at most O(p^(2k)) actual words. If tau>k, decline this certificate, not infer unsafe. The adjacency matrix has rank >=tau over any field (triangular submatrix with diagonal1), but rank bound not needed. Complete multipartite graphs have tau<=2: length3 would imply x1,y2,y3 same part, then x2,y3 same part, contradicting x2-y2 edge. Hence section3 is a special case. No universal fixed k claim.

## 5. Authenticated large-boundary realization
Use r tracks, r passes of adjacent two-track swaps (0,1),(1,2),...,(r-2,r-1) as a fixed bubble-sort network. Every swap is a cubic square with two input ports, two output ports and cycle K2,2; phase matchings straight/cross. Connect previous outputs to next inputs on each track, leaving r first inputs A and r last outputs B. Every path goes forward in switch order, so boundary relation consists of bipartite permutations. For any desired permutation, route by bubble-sorting input labels according to desired output order; realizes every r! permutation, with O(r^2) switches. Source closure: attach s to first two A, t to first two B, match remaining A_j-B_j by track, add z=st. Distinct square occurrences, r>=2 gives simple connected cubic bipartite graph of n=4r(r-1)+2 vertices. Let a be union of all square-cycle edges, b all non-a edges except z plus any phase per square. Validate conservation, sole zero z, b spanning even factor by bipartiteness. Only s,t odd components in a, so minimum fixed-direction deficiency1 and z unique singleton T-join. 2^(r(r-1)) phase assignments, r! distinct boundary pairings. Certificate section3 acquires compact single-path dictionary with no full table, polynomial witness storage and queries. Alternatively directly construct required direct/pair witnesses by sorting a chosen permutation. This is a source realization of large pair-cover interfaces, not a novel rearrangeable network theorem (Beneš precedent) and not a new class of hard source graphs: all primitive squares safe, source bipartite. Actual full certificate ran r2..5; r8,16 symbolic proof and sampled independent routing only, not full matching certificate. Source graph generation uses known construction; blind chart extraction tested separately below.

## 6. Affine guard obstruction (arbitrarily long minimal bad contexts)
Take k>=3 disjoint square switches, no internal wires between them, guard XOR of their binary phase bits =0. Guard compact O(k); exact table has 2^(k-1) legal words, same marginal union as unrestricted disjoint squares. All contexts with <=k-2 wires pass: a relaxed simple alternating path has <=k-1 transitions, hence touches <=k-1 squares. Disjoint used transitions within a square always belong to a common phase. Fix touched phases, choose an untouched phase to satisfy parity. Conversely choose an odd word, take one transition x_j-y_j in each square from its chosen phase, context wires y_j-x_(j+1) for j=1..k-1, endpoints x1,yk. All other ports unmatched. The only possible connection requires every one of the designated transitions (square union has no cross-square edge; each square has just two active ports in this context), and hence the excluded odd word. Minimal bad context exactly k-1. This falsifies unrestricted short-context certification for implicitly guarded groups and dropping guard correlations when applying section1. Not an exponential-time lower bound: Gaussian elimination knows this parity guard.
Source authentication: k selected squares A_j and k auxiliary squares B_j, wire both outputs of A_j to inputs of B_j, both outputs of B_j to inputs of A_(j+1 mod k). Remove one A->B wire from each of j=0,1; attach freed outputs to s, inputs to t, add z=st. n=8k+2, simple connected cubic bipartite, a all cycles, b all wires except z and straight phase per square; conservation/sole zero verified. A-region has 4k exposed ports, no internal wires; guard on A phases explicit. Abstract external contexts above are universal interface tests, not claimed to be actual contexts of this specific closure. Arbitrary coupled guards must be retained, not factored into independent groups.
