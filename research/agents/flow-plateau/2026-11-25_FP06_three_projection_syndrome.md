## Exact construction
Input simple cubic graph, conserved F2^2 flow z with sole zero e0=uv. For each nonzero direction c=1,2,3 form G_c of all nondefective edges with z_e!=c. Every proper vertex has degree 2 in every G_c, endpoints degree 0 or 2; each view is disjoint cycles and isolates. Cut cycles into directed paths retaining parity closure. Each nondefective edge belongs to exactly two projections c,d, with c XOR d=z_e. Let x_{e,c} be activation. Require not(x_{e,c}=x_{e,d}=1); then w_e=z_e XOR c*x_{e,c} XOR d*x_{e,d} remains nonzero. At a proper vertex define h_{v,c} as XOR of its two incident activations in G_c. Conservation iff all three h_{v,c} equal shared h_v. With exclusivity, h_v=1 iff all three incident edges change; h_v=0 iff zero or two change. Fix new color r of e0. At each endpoint with duplicated original nonzero color t, h_{v,t}=0 and the other two bits uniquely represent r as XOR of their directions. This enforces conservation of w with w_e0=r.

For each cycle C in G_c, sum its h entries =0. Stack these equations A h=b(r) on proper vertex coordinates. Each column has exactly 3 ones, one per view. For a supplied h satisfying all equations, one phase p_C determines all x along C via prefix XOR; x_{e,c}=p_C XOR ell_{e,c}(h,r). The edge exclusion becomes a 2-CNF clause on the two relevant phases. SCC decides and reconstructs in polynomial time (linear indexed graph operations for a fixed h). Equivalence both directions: a proper target defines exclusive activations and syndrome; satisfying phases reconstruct a valid conserved nowhere-zero flow. No semantic absorption, target guess, plateau traversal or module identification. This gives 2^k poly(N) for rank-nullity k of the acquired affine intersection. Affine feasibility alone is not sufficient: on a bridgeless graph, a cycle through e0 with constant change r is a relaxed conserved flow, allowing both permitted directions when change equals z_e. Hence the affine domain is nonempty for every r, even on Petersen.
