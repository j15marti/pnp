Self-contained existence: let B be all reduced words of length <=max(2,2K) on d involutive generators; define permutation g_i by pairing each word with its reduced right append of i when both lie in B, fixing unmatched boundary words. ## Universal budget obstruction
At any conserved flow, each module defines mismatch d_j on its edge of R. Sum conservation at original vertices of a copy; e5 cancels, and telescoping along each of its protected chains gives XOR of all incident module mismatches zero. Thus mismatches are an F2^2-flow on R. Nonempty support has no degree-one vertex, hence contains a cycle. Each nonzero mismatch requires a zero internal or boundary edge; each zero can charge at most two modules. For Psi<=K, support <=2K<girth(R), forcing every mismatch zero. Hence every protected chain has L+1 identical wire colors. Zero would require L+1=K+1 distinct zero edges, impossible. Project each chain to its common nonzero virtual edge and keep e5. This gives conserved core flows, each with at most one zero. Every step retaining a,b,a+b projects to a step retaining the same direction; global color relabeling can be implemented by projection-retaining transpositions. Therefore a successful lifted trajectory at budget K would give a successful protected core trajectory from START, contradicted by the exact certificate. All K quantified, unbounded absolute overshoot even at one starting zero. No upper bound on required overshoot in n or proof of superpolynomial computation.

## Cyclic 4-edge connectivity (symbolic, not exhaustive large-lift cut search)
Contract each Q to a degree-four skeleton vertex. Each core copy is now a subdivision of G0; paths are edge-disjoint across copies, sharing only module vertices. Any skeleton cut of size <=3 splits original vertices of at most one copy, since the core is 3-edge-connected and each separated core endpoint pair forces a distinct crossing. If one mixed copy v, all others have zero crossing edges and their vertices/modules lie on common side by connectivity of R-v. Every module incident to v therefore lies on common side too. Opposite-side originals in v form a singleton or 11-set by the core cut audit. 11-set contains both endpoints of some protected edge, whose path must cross twice in addition to the three edges forced by the base cut; impossible. Thus only singleton cut. If no mixed copy, paths each have an even number of crossings. At most one exceptional path crosses. All other paths are constant. Delete from R the interaction edges incident to that path in its copy v; R-v remains connected and v has other incident labels, so the resulting R remains connected. All originals have same side and every module also has same side via its other copy, contradicting nonempty cut. If no exceptional path same conclusion. Skeleton small cuts are exactly original singleton stars.

For a split Q let k be number of cut internal edges, t number of its terminals on one side, r=min(t,4-t). Exhaustive 254 proper nonempty subsets: k>=2; for k<=3 the (k,r) multiplicities are (2,1):8,(3,0):8,(3,1):16,(3,2):8. Whenever k+r<=3 the minority vertex set (or its complement) is a singleton. In a lift cut <=3, at most one module is split. Rounding it to majority terminal side decreases cut by at least k-r>=1 (newly crossing external edges at most r). Skeleton cut then <=2 so trivial. All other vertices are on rounded side. Original cut is k+r and therefore isolates one module vertex. If no split module, skeleton analysis applies. Hence no nontrivial cuts <=3, cyclic 4-edge-connected. 

## Source-checkable semantic recovery
Q has 4 internal 5-cycles, their edge union covers all 10 Q edges and is connected; no two ports adjacent. No cycle of length <=5 can pass through >=2 Q segments: each segment between different ports needs >=2 internal edges and at least one external edge, total >=6. A cycle through just one Q must leave via original vertices and return without visiting another Q; originals have only the one unprotected edge e5 and cannot provide that path: two ports of a module never attach to one original vertex, nor to endpoints of e5 (protected label differs; distinct copies cannot be connected through originals). No cycle wholly among originals. Thus the 5-cycle edge union consists exactly of disjoint Q modules. Discover from unannotated cubic graph by all bounded-length walks, O(n) on indexed adjacency RAM, conservative polynomial bit cost. Verify boundary table and exact port pairing on each module; replace each by two virtual equality edges simultaneously, tracing wire paths. This preserves SAT and proper witness recovery (independent local extensions); recovers disjoint 12-vertex cores. Enumerate each constant-size residual, lift and verify. Polynomial total on this family despite unbounded required overshoot. General theorem that every barrier admits such recognition/absorption OPEN; arbitrary four-port relations need not be independent equality pairs.
