## A. Complete local palettes and strong activation backdoor

Exact local table is six permutation words. A Boolean relation is bijunctive iff closed under coordinatewise majority. Relative permutation gauge matters; no claim arbitrary encodings preserve this list.

Necessity: missed triple survives common pins, majority still absent. Sufficiency: any majority violation in a restricted table was an original violation whose varying coordinates avoided pins. Greedily select maximal disjoint supports, take their union and remove redundant elements if desired: packing gives lower bound k* and guard size <=6k*. All acquisition polynomial; no target. ## B. Minimum whole-edge guard cover
Let U be vertices with nonbijunctive current activation domains before coordinate quotient, and fix repaired edge color r (defective endpoints have <=2 words). Pin any incident edge's color; a vertex retains <=2 permutations, hence bijunctive. To achieve syntactic strong backdoor with whole-edge pins must hit every vertex of U, since missing it leaves its original local relation unchanged. Minimum count is |U|-nu(G[U]), nu maximum matching size. Upper bound by matching then one incident edge per unmatched U vertex. Lower bound: assign each U vertex to one selected incident edge; edges assigned two vertices form matching, so |U| <= guard count+nu. ## D. Asymptotic obstruction to global guard count, even with perfect signed-equivalence closure

Every other replica can independently apply ANY of six global color permutations, with all other original core vertices unchanged. Each original vertex of these replicas has all six local permutations among actual proper source witnesses. Thus even PERFECT unary-domain support closure under that guard cannot remove any of their local words. No activation bit at such vertex is constant; no two of its six bits are equal or complementary on all witnesses (each bit has exactly 2 of 6 words, distinct supports). Between independent replicas no nonconstant original activation bits are equal/complementary, since one replica can vary independently. Applies to syntactic strong guards after support/equality closure, not to arbitrary existential factoring, choice of representative witness, affine substitution with more than two terms, or a decision algorithm.
