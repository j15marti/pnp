## 1. Exact elimination of tractable regions
Choose unresolved vertex set U and a supplied S/R palette for the other good vertices. Fix repaired edge color r, or leave its endpoints in U and check r there. The FP10 palette relations, nonzero edge exclusions, and any fixed-r endpoint relations outside U are 2-CNF. Let Q(B,P) be their conjunction, retaining all activation bits incident to U in B; local non-bijunctive domains at U are not replaced by their hulls. Existential projection R(B)=exists P Q(B,P) is exactly 2-CNF. Variable elimination by resolution preserves width two, is existentially exact, and there are O(|B|^2) distinct non-tautological clauses on B. Alternatively, test each unary/binary forbidden tuple by a 2SAT query with its pins; the resulting conjunction equals the projection by resolution closure. If Q is unsatisfiable reject only the guarded chart. For satisfying projected boundary and exact remaining local domains, solve Q with pins to recover private activation values, verify the original full coloring. All work polynomial in original explicit bit length, including failed queries and lifting. If the projected constraint graph (binary clauses plus local hyperedges at U) has independently solvable components each containing O(log N) unresolved vertices, enumerate each component's local domains and solve independently, polynomial sum of costs. Alternatively use bounded-width DP. Conditional on palette choice and U; no universal acquisition claim. In contrast to arbitrary semantic region absorption, only ordinary 2SAT projection is needed. A restriction S/R is not automatically safe for the unrestricted source.

## 2. Exact raw ternary cut-rank identity
For each vertex give its three edge incidences distinct labels 0,1,2 in F3. Unrestricted local affine coordinates (s_v,t_v) yield a triple summing to zero, including s=0 in the linear relaxation. M is the edge-agreement matrix with row for e=uv: ell(u,e)s_u+t_u-ell(v,e)s_v-t_v=0. For X subset V let M_X contain both columns of vertices in X. Define lambda(X)=dim(im M_X intersect im M_(V-X)), let b(S) be number of bipartite connected components of G[S], and delta(X) the crossing edges. Then
lambda(X)=|delta(X)|-b(X)-b(V-X)+b(V).
Proof: intersection vectors vanish on edges internal to either side. They correspond, up to per-edge signs, to boundary color words extendible on both sides in the unrestricted affine relaxation. In any connected induced region C, unknown internal edge colors satisfy the unsigned incidence system with RHS supplied by boundary colors. Its left nullity is one for bipartite C (alternating +/- signs), zero otherwise. Each bipartite C contributes one boundary equation with a nonzero coefficient on every incident cut edge. For a connected component of G meeting the cut, the quotient of induced components on both sides is connected. A linear dependence among the boundary equations must have coefficients propagated along every cut edge, with nonzero coefficients on both ends or neither. If one component is nonbipartite no dependence is possible; otherwise a nonzero dependence exists iff alternating signs glue across the cut, precisely global bipartiteness, in which case dimension one. Whole components not meeting cut contribute equally to b(X)+b(V-X) and b(V); formula follows. For connected 3-edge-connected cubic G and proper nonempty X, every induced component has at least three boundary edges, hence b(X)+b(V-X)<=2|delta(X)|/3, lambda>=|delta(X)|/3. This concerns both local columns per vertex BEFORE phase elimination or new constraints/quotients; no claim for every possible projection. It holds under invertible changes of local affine coordinates. A leaf decomposition of vertices with max lambda=w has all cut sizes <=3w and yields an ordinary tree decomposition with bags of endpoints of edges crossing incident leaf partitions (size <=18w+O(1)). Each vertex occurs on the connected subtree joining its leaf to neighbor leaves; each edge endpoints share a bag. Consequently tw(G)=O(w), so raw ternary branch-width cannot evade large graph width in this representation.

## 3. Simultaneous asymptotic stress family

For d>=1 set q=3^d, use two sets of q^2 replica indices indexed by Z_q^2, connect them by labeled perfect matchings for each protected source edge (all except sole potentially defective edge). The first five labels use shifts (0,0),(1,0),(-1,0),(0,1),(0,-1); remaining labels use shift (0,0). Parallel *interaction* edges permitted; each has its own disjoint eight-vertex Petersen four-pole Q, so final cubic graph simple. Each pair of seams for a replica's virtual edge attaches to that copy's original endpoints. Every replica retains its unprotected edge. Only distinguished replica uses the near coloring, other replicas use proper core colorings. The proper Q table gives authentic lift and all-SAT target. By FP06 same-pair bichromatic path swap, changing one pair leaves the other fixed and adds no branches. Exactly one zero initially; proper target requires >=2*3^d branches and >=2*3^(d-1) common-interval controls in distinguished replica, with matching upper bounds via singletons. Private cycle signatures persist through Q splices. FP10 parity-only syndrome minimum stays 2, since its lift selects only core branch vertices with corresponding root signature, and RHS size 2 or4 excludes zero and singleton. No claim all joint domain closures stall.

Cyclic four-edge connectivity of final graph: contract Q to degree-four node. Any cut<=3 splits originals in at most one replica by edge-disjoint paths for the 3-edge-connected core. If one mixed, all other copies have zero crossings and R-v connects them, forcing every Q node on same side. Cut for opposite-side original set S in mixed replica is 3|S|-2*1[e0 internal to S], <=3 iff singleton. If none mixed, any different copy sides incur >=2 per crossing interaction edge, so >=4; same sides force all Q nodes to agree. For an actual split Q, FP09's exact internal cut audit gives k>=2, and for k<=3 its minority terminal count t<k; k+t<=3 only singleton/complement. At most one Q split; rounding it to majority terminal side decreases cut by >=k-t, yielding trivial skeleton cut<=2, hence original singleton. This proves no nontrivial cut<=3. 
Each Q can be split into vertex-disjoint paths (4,9,7,5) and (2,3,8,6), with at least one edge between them; allocate each path to its replica. Each replica's resulting branch set is connected (it contains all its source edges as paths). Thus tw>=q-1 (grid lower bound), so min max raw ternary cut rank Omega(q). Lift vertex count n=(n0+4*(3*n0/2-1))*2*q^2=140*81^d+20*9^d. It does NOT refute every adaptive projection or unrestricted polynomial solver.

## 4. Graph-only cooperative repair of the simultaneous stress family
An explicit uniform solver for the above family without supplied source partition or target. Mark all vertices on 5-cycles by bounded-degree walks. Q's internal pentagons cover all Q vertices. No pentagon crosses two Q modules because their ports are nonadjacent and at least two internal edges per traversed module are required; plus external edges length>=6. No pentagon crosses just one Q: outside Q, originals have only e0 in each copy, and the two same-copy terminals of any Q are endpoints of a different edge; no return path among originals. Thus exactly Q vertices marked. This is only a structural tag for proposing S palettes, not recognition of each module or its exact full relation.
Use swap-only 2CNF at marked vertices; leave every original vertex untyped, impose edge exclusions. Initial activation all-zero satisfies this relaxation (defective endpoints untyped). In Q, exact proper pair relation forces equality of both same-pair seam bits. Each bit is nonconstant: changing one pair to either other color while leaving other pair fixed is possible by a bichromatic path swap, with all internal vertices still S. Different pairs are not equated because either pair can change alone. These observations and edge nonzero constraints imply: in the SCC equivalence relation of the implication graph, two seam activation bit pairs at untagged stubs match (in direction order) exactly when they belong to the same virtual edge. Standard 2SAT implication fact: for two nonconstant literals, mutual logical implication is equivalent to mutual graph reachability; if adding two literal assumptions leads to contradiction, their closures conflict or one alone conflicts, and contraposition gives the implication path. The unprotected colored edges in other copies share their original variables, and the unique defective edge is joined directly. Build candidate graph of paired stubs from SCC keys, validate degree/simple/cubic and matching near color, choose component containing defective edge. In this family the candidate graph recovers all the cores, and the chosen component is exactly the distinguished C_d.
Now solve that core by generic six-state vertex CSP using bounded treewidth (FP09 tw<=35 and known Bodlaender 1996 fixed-width acquisition DOI 10.1137/S0097539793251219); no target needed. Freeze other untagged vertices at their original colors. Pin selected core boundary colors and solve full original guarded 2CNF, reconstruct and independently verify original graph. Every proper coloring of the selected core extends by independent one-pair Kempe-path swaps in Q, all S-compatible, while other replicas remain unchanged. Therefore this graph-only proposal is uniformly polynomial on this entire family, despite simultaneous asymptotic obstruction in section 3. On arbitrary graphs it is a sound witness-only terminal (DECLINE if tags, keys, candidate, width or final lift fails), not a universal solver. The proof uses known family geometry, but the executed solver receives only original graph+near flow; it never computes a four-pole's complete semantic boundary table or needs supplied module IDs. It still uses algebraic projection and reconstructs a candidate reduced graph, not a claim of zero semantic operations.
