## 1. Authenticated wire network
Let G explicitly encoded simple cubic, a in ker D, singleton zero z=st outside supp(a). b fixed on F=E\supp(a) except zero z. To extend b to a proper pair it must be a spanning 2-factor; solve each support(a) cycle's integer equations x_prev+x_next=2-deg_bF(v), trying initial bit0/1, and check isolates. A marked cycle has at most one feasible phase; an unmarked even cycle has two alternating matchings. A marked vertex on a b-cycle has two selected edges of the same A/F type; all others one of each. Cycle length parity therefore equals parity of its count of s,t. Hence even-factor extension iff s,t on same selected cycle. Splitting each marked vertex into its two incident selected ends and suppressing fixed paths gives wire matching W on switch ports plus four immutable terminals s0,s1,t0,t1. Fixed cycles with no marks may be ignored (even by parity). Each original switch has at most two exact local perfect matchings, with stored source phase choices. Only independent choices or individual pins are admitted; arbitrary coupled affine guards are NOT dropped. No choice of direction or source coloring assumed.

## 2. Exact group composition (new acquisition)
For any disjoint groups X,Y whose stored relations are exact sets of attainable perfect pairings of their exposed ports, glue any pair of table words along ALL W wires internal to X union Y. The combined graph has internal degree2 and boundary degree1, hence each component is a closed circuit or a path between two exposed ports. Record the resulting boundary perfect matching and one actual combined source phase witness. Enumerating the Cartesian product of the two stored tables is exact: every original phase assignment maps to a table pair; every pair has independent source lifts in disjoint groups; internal circuits contain no marked ends. This preserves all terminal connectivity continuations within this chart, not arbitrary cross-direction flow continuations. Internal self-wires of one original switch are handled by the same one-table operation. Zero-port groups may be dropped with a stored witness; two-port groups retain their unique boundary pair. Common pairs may additionally be suppressed as CEC06, but not required for the new theorem.
Greedy rule: merge any adjacent groups if the number of remaining exposed ports of their union is <=4. Each initial group has at most two table words even when its arity is large. Each merged group has at most three (the three pairings on four ports). Thus at most 9 word-pair trials per merge, even merging two long switches with many internal wires. At most k-1 merges among k initial switches, no hidden exponential table acquisition. Use actual port identity and explicit wire edges for every trial. Store backpointer or one full phase witness per row. No assumption of pairwise marginals at six ports.

## 3. Canonical acquisition theorem under a verifiable promise
Define interaction multigraph J with a vertex per original switch and root r representing the four immutable terminal ends; W wires are its edges, loops ignored in cuts. Terminal-free components can be discarded with arbitrary legal choices by endpoint parity. Let c(S)=|delta_J(S)| counting multiplicities for nonempty S of switch vertices, excluding root. Promise: c(S)>=4 for every nonempty S, equivalently all root-separating cuts at least4. This can be checked by ordinary mincut (contract the terminals to the root and run unit-capacity r-v maxflow for every switch). It is a property of the projected wire graph, not of G's ordinary cyclic edge connectivity.
Run greedy binary unions only when c(X union Y)<=4. Under the promise every such union has exactly4 ports. For disjoint current blocks X,Y,Z, direct edge counting gives
c(XY)+c(XZ)=c(X)+c(XYZ)+2 e(Y,Z).
If XY and XZ are both legal, c(XYZ)<=8-c(X)-2e(Y,Z)<=4. Thus either overlapping first merge can be followed by a legal merge with the third block (adjacency is automatic for legal union of two blocks with cuts>=4). Disjoint merges commute. Every step strictly decreases block count. Local diamonds imply unique final partition by induction on the maximum remaining number of merges: from any two immediate successors take their common successor, whose unique terminal partition by induction is shared. Exact boundary relations of the same final block are unique irrespective of merge order because they are the attainable pairings of its original independent phases; chosen representatives need not be identical. Hence if any sequence of binary unions of boundary<=4 absorbs all long switches into short groups, EVERY sequence run to stagnation does so. No supplied decomposition or exponential order search. This is a restricted constructive acquisition lemma, not a universal small-port theorem.
Outside the promise partition confluence need not hold: X has two wires, one each to Y,Z; Y and Z each have three additional wires to root. c(X)=2,c(Y)=c(Z)=4,c(XY)=c(XZ)=4 but c(XYZ)=6. The two legal first merges stall at different partitions. This is an abstract cut counterexample (six root wires; not claimed as a singleton-chart source). Algorithm remains exact with either order; no canonical claim without promise.

## 4. Exact polynomial terminal and witness
After paid group closure let L be count of residual groups with >4 ports (these are still original single switches, with <=2 choices). Enumerate their choices, at most 2^L. Add both/all legal transition edges for the short groups to W, and the fixed pairings for this branch. For each x in {s0,s1}, y in {t0,t1}, compute ordinary perfect matching after deleting x,y. By CEC06, symmetric difference with W gives a simple alternating x-y path; its disjoint used edges at every <=4-port group always belong jointly to one allowed pairing. Indeed two disjoint pairs on four ports force one another, and one pair belongs to a legal table word by construction. Long switches were fixed. Lift each chosen group table witness into original phases, reconstruct b and verify exact z, degree2 and all-even factors. Obtain c in ker D by setting c=1 on complement of b and alternating on b-cycles; verify Psi(b,c)=0 and conservation. Conversely any feasible phase assignment gives such an alternating path and hence a matching by toggling W along it. Therefore exact chart decision/witness in 2^{O(L)} poly(N), <=4 matching calls per branch. If L=O(log N), polynomial. A capped run with excessive L returns DECLINE, not NO. Can enumerate <=m singleton charts of a min-one direction and apply independently; failure of all is a fixed-direction two-step NO only, not source UNSAT nor full H1 reachability. Global color relabeling does not change the original zero mask; no uncharged switching.

## 5. Explicit infinite family, both chart labels on SAT sources
Use k modules, each two disjoint hexagons with local vertices 0..5 and 6..11, cyclic edges in natural order. Two boundary ports on left (0,1), two right (6,7). Four wires join first hex vertices 2,3,4,5 to second hex vertices 8,9,10,11 in order for good module; for bad module instead join to 9,8,11,10. Chain right pair of each module to next left pair in order. Attach two extra vertices s,t to two leftmost and rightmost ports respectively; add edge z=st. G is simple connected cubic on n=12k+2 vertices. a consists of all hexagon cycle edges; s,t are isolated in a, the only odd components. All other edges selected in b except z, and either alternating matching on each hexagon selected. Thus every one of 2^(2k) choices is source-conserved with sole zero z, min_a=1 and z unique singleton T-join. No original switch has internal wires or common phase pairs; all 2k initial effective arities are six. (There are small source cuts and short cycles; no high-girth or cyclically 4-edge-connected claim.)
Local exact table: good module permits cap pairing (01)(67) for phase words00,01,10; through pairing (06)(17) for 11. Bad module permits only cap for all four words. Independent direct traversal verified table. Consequently all-good chain admits a spanning even factor by choosing all through; a chain with one bad module has no compatible factor in this fixed-a chart, regardless of all other phase choices. Both source families are nevertheless 3-edge-colorable: for k=1 the edge order is twelve cycle edges, z, four internal wires in listed order, s0,s1,t6,t7. Proper one-hot color words (1,2,4) good [1,2,1,2,1,4,1,2,1,2,1,4,1,4,4,4,2,2,4,2,4], bad [1,2,1,4,2,4,1,4,1,2,4,2,1,4,2,1,1,2,4,4,2]. Both have distinct boundary pair colors {2,4} on each end, z color1. Relabel 2/4 on successive modules to match the preceding outgoing ordered pair, keeping 1 fixed, yielding a proper coloring of every k. Thus chart NO never inferred as source NO.
Interaction graph after root contraction is a cycle with alternating four-wire internal bundles and two-wire intermodule/terminal bundles; for k=1 also a three-vertex cycle. Every nonempty root-excluding vertex set has >=4 cut wires. Merge each pair of hexes across its 4 wires to form a 4-port group, then merge adjacent modules across 2 wires until one group with 4 boundary ports remains. By confluence all greedy orders reach that partition. Exactly 2k-1 merges, <=9 trials each, L=0. Initial long count 2k=Theta(n) and complete phase-word space exponential; new deterministic polynomial terminal decides both chart labels without enumerating words. This is NOT a lower bound on prior adaptive algorithms or general two-step acquisition.
