The last arc c->v is legal precisely when v-u is 4 or 1 modulo 5, which is undirected C5 adjacency. Every C5 coloring of H therefore has exactly one extension on all original T5 gadget vertices:

#Hom(D(H),T5) = #Hom(H,C5).

The T5 graph has n+3m vertices and 5m arcs. Its topological ranks are 4i for original vertex i and 4u+1,4u+2,4u+3 for each private triple on u<v. Every arc goes to a higher rank, including c->v.
