## Exact primary-source verification

Original source, not a secondary review: Jahanjou, Miles and Viola, **Local reductions**, ECCC TR13-099 **revision 3**, March 31/April 4, 2014, https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/099/revision/3/download/ . I inspected the original PDF text and rendered pages 2 and 3. Its Theorem 1 (printed page 2) states a uniform poly(n, log T) circuit D from an x, of address length ell and **3v+3 output bits**, where each variable name has **v bits** (not v total variables). Its output bits have decision-tree depth O(log r), ell=max(log T,n/r)+O(log n)+O(loglog T), and formula satisfiability is equivalent to existence of a length-T witness accepted by M(x,y). This independently confirms the source interpretation used by TMR-1/064 and TMR-5/063-/064.

More significantly, **JMV Corollary 2(1), printed page 2**, concludes a language f in E^NP not computed by *linear-size general circuits* under the stipulated circuit-SAT speedup. **Corollary 3(1'), printed page 3**, explicitly substitutes **ETH is false**, i.e. for every epsilon>0 3SAT is in time 2^(epsilon n), for the assumption of item (1). The target is precisely E^NP; neither NP nor NE nor a particular superlinear function is stated. The printed text expressly says strengthening the IKW route for the linear-size classes would be needed to obtain the different NE consequence. Thus the direct JMV published implication `not ETH => E^NP not subset SIZE(O(N))` is supported, with its original scope.

## Conditional ordinary-machine/ETH bridge, with same-execution ledger

Let n be the number of original declared variables and restrict the usual explicit 3SAT convention to input formulas with at most O(n^3) clauses of width<=3. Read the *entire* input, reject malformed binary syntax and names outside range, remove identical ordered clauses and remap the actually occurring variable names. Duplicate outer clauses do not alter SAT; deleting unused declared variables does not alter SAT (but would alter a literal #SAT count, hence this fallback is **decision-only**). The new n'<=n. If n'<4 or fewer than two distinct clauses, brute force the bounded-variable or trivial-clause cases. Otherwise the resulting genuine `encClauses 3 φ'` has AllOccur if desired, NoDup, a unary n' header and B_A=O(n^3 log(n+2)) actual bits. The physical parse/dedup/remap/serialization costs at most a fixed polynomial in the original polynomially bounded input length B; O(B^4) sequential bit time and O(B^2) peak work are ample conservative bounds for a straightforward locally headed implementation.

Under the independently unverified pinned `MainTheorem`, for fixed k=3, all promised n'>=4,m'>=2, the counting time is bounded by
`t <= 2^(9 C n' log2 log2(n')/sqrt(log2(n')))` since the theorem uses the minimum of the m and n exponents. For *every* epsilon>0, eventually the exponent is <=epsilon n uniformly over 4<=n'<=n: for large n' its coefficient tends to zero, while finitely many small n' have bounded cost. The fixed FinTM2 stack-machine physical translation costs O_M(B_A+t+1) time and O_M(B_A+t+1) peak writable bits on one matched run, including input loading, stack head moves, output, and a zero-versus-positive count decision. Thus under the *formal theorem's truth* the resulting **ordinary deterministic** algorithm has `T(n)=poly(n)+2^o(n)=2^o(n)` and `S_peak=O(poly(n)+2^o(n))`. No small-space or FLMV forbidden pair is asserted.
