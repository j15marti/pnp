## Theorem A: global cofacial three-interval groups
Let n be the number of source variables (also clauses); N the explicit encoded bit length. Choose two partitions A,B to retain. Delete the third-partition clause vertices and their incident edges from a fixed plane embedding of the source. Suppress the resulting degree-two variable vertices to labeled multiedges e directed from A to B, or keep the subdivision for computing rotations. The resulting graph H may be disconnected. Use its GLOBAL faces on the sphere, not one independent exterior per component. These faces are polynomially computable: traverse source face walks, then union the face IDs on opposite sides of each deleted edge. Deleting an edge merges its incident faces (no change if already identical); deleting the resulting isolated omitted vertex does not split the complement. Suppressing subdivisions preserves faces. This obtains all faces, including multiply connected ones. Euler check f=e-v+c+1 for c components.

An omitted clause vertex and its three deleted incident edges lie in ONE face f of H: the deleted star minus its endpoints is connected in the complement, and its arcs approach the three variable midpoints. Thus the three distinct labeled matching edges are incident to f. Each edge contributes at most one other face. This gives at most FOUR global faces in its exact height factor, including the center f, even across components. The claim is not made about independently rooted component-face coordinates without translating them.

A cubic bipartite multigraph has a perfect matching m by Hall's theorem. Compute one using the underlying simple bipartite graph and choose an actual labeled edge for each matched pair. Every other perfect matching x differs from m by an integer circulation. Fix one global face height zero. An integer circulation is uniquely represented as x_e-m_e=h_right(e)-h_left(e). To prove existence, integrate along dual walks; each closed dual walk has zero sum, by decomposing into simple closed curves crossing primal edges and summing primal conservation inside each curve. Dual connectivity implies uniqueness up to one constant; the converse is telescoping around primal vertices. This proof works for disconnected H; bridges have equal face on both sides and zero difference. Edge bounds -m_e <= h_right-h_left <= 1-m_e give Boolean occupancies. Conservation gives one selected edge at every retained clause. Original pins replace intervals by singletons. Root distances bound heights in [-n,n]. All difference bounds are integer and polynomially acquired, with negative cycles certifying infeasibility. As in AGE01, the all-pairs shortest distance matrix D gives exact projection to any face subset plus the root: a permitted pinned vector extends by h_v=min_u(h_u+D[u,v]), where u ranges over pinned faces including the root. Triangle inequality proves feasibility and the pairwise projected inequalities force the specified values.

Now fix an omitted clause c and a choice s of its unique selected variable. For every incident edge e, set the signed difference to 1[e=s]-m_e. Since all incident edges share f, the heights of every other face g in this factor are necessarily h_g=t+delta_g for t=h_f, delta_f=0 and delta_g a known signed integer in {-1,0,1}. Repeated faces require identical offsets; equal-side edges require zero difference. Reject inconsistent offsets. Pairwise inequalities between these faces depend only on the offsets and can be checked in constant time with D. The root inequalities reduce to a single closed integer interval
  max_g(-D[g,root]-delta_g) <= t <= min_g(D[root,g]-delta_g).
Empty intervals reject that phase. Hence each omitted clause factor is exactly a union of at most THREE interval families, each with one integer coordinate and fixed offsets plus its original selected edge label. No matching enumeration or high-dimensional table acquisition is needed. Each family can be stored in O(log N) bits beyond the constant number of face/edge IDs; it has at most 2n+1 rows. A whole chart has O(n^2) explicit rows, and the compact pre-expansion factors use O(n log N) bits. This replaces AGE01's O(N^6) per-factor bound by O(N), and retains every true witness, not just a relaxation. Full conjunction of difference bounds and local factors is bijective to original satisfying assignments. This is a finite type/interval classification, NOT proof that interacting intervals are globally easy.

Corollary: use pure height factors in AGE01-C's static strong median-backdoor detector. A violating median triple has a coordinate on which the three rows differ; a further strong backdoor must pin at least one such coordinate, or the witness triple and its absent median survive in the same slice. There are at most four coordinates. For fixed c>=0 and product of pinned face domain sizes <=N^c, the detector has depth <=c log2 N and at most O(N^(2c)) search nodes; then enumerate <=N^c guard assignments and use threshold 2SAT. All tests polynomial in O(n^2) acquired rows. This improves the branching base and acquisition cost, not the existence guarantee. A growing number of disjoint nonmedian scopes still defeats the static parameter. Detector improvement proved, not implemented in this audit.

## Theorem B: shared-source three-chart CSP saturation

A chart's difference bounds and threshold chains are exact 2CNF (implications t[j,k]->t[i,k-b] for h_j-h_i<=b; use constant sentinels). Include each original exact-one triple as its explicit 3-row relation, plus at-most-one clauses. For each omitted clause factor of each chart append its three x_e columns to the threshold word of every interval row; these columns are its exact selected edge pattern. This is a channel relation, not an assumption that x_e is a single threshold. All true source solutions lift into the joint network because each chart has a unique rooted height representation of its corresponding matching. Conversely, the exact joint network projects to source solutions. Auxiliary charts are redundant but permit deductions through a common x_e interface.

For any current explicit table, include every forbidden unary/binary assignment as a clause. Conjoin across all tables, charts, source pins and any certified necessary clauses. If the master 2CNF is contradictory, reject. Check the master's source candidate and every decoded chart candidate directly against the original source and guards; accept only a verified witness. Otherwise, discard any table row whose complete word is infeasible in the master 2CNF. Pinned feasibility is exact by implication reachability: a set of literals is extendible iff no pinned literal reaches the complement of a pinned literal, including itself. Iterate. An empty table is UNSAT. A stationary nondecisive state is OPEN, never UNSAT. Invariance by induction: every true source solution has all its chart lifts and table rows surviving; any projection clause or deleted row is therefore safe. ## Theorem C: clocked exact AND/OR integration
Represent a state by authenticated source IDs, actual guards, declared block partition/representation, exact interface tables or certified necessary relaxations, and witness maps. An OR edge is an alternative exact representation or a sound partial terminal for the SAME state, not a union of arbitrary subproblems. A cover edge partitions the parent's solutions among finitely many explicit guards; accept on a lifted witness from any child, reject only if all children reject. A genuine AND product requires disjoint private variables and exact agreement on the exported boundary; use the prior footprint join or explicit finite row join with bounded table size, not independent decisions on overlapping groups. In an OR of alternative solvers, any decisive sound answer suffices; OPEN is not evidence for NO. Guards are part of the cache key. Merge only literal identical normalized states or independently verified equivalences with witness maps; no free semantic canonicalization. Exactness follows by bottom-up induction on an explicitly acyclic catalogue.

For fixed exponent d and at most N^d paid acquisitions, vertices, edges, table entries, join candidates and candidate attempts, with every acquisition/evaluation/witness operation polynomially bounded or explicitly clocked to OPEN, the whole evaluation is polynomial. This includes alternative decompositions, failed lanes, guards, compatibility and lift. This is CONDITIONAL on the polynomial catalogue/admission, not universal. Crucial uncovered implication: for every SAT source, at least one uniformly discoverable, polynomial-clocked lane or cover succeeds. If that is proved, soundness plus timeout-to-NO at the global fixed polynomial cap gives a total deterministic decision algorithm on all valid inputs (SAT must be accepted; UNSAT cannot be accepted). It is sufficient to cover SAT but not sufficient merely to show existence of a witness-guided decomposition with no acquisition procedure. No claim of that implication here.
