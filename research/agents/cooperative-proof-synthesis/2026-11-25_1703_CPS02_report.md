## 1. Exact matching recognition for a MATCHING4 mask
Use FCG02's signed ports (+,+,-,-), so legal raw words satisfy z0+z1=z2+z3. Twist the two negative ports: y=(z0,z1,1-z2,1-z3). Every normalized word has weight exactly two. For any exact mask R let B be its family of two-subsets of {0,1,2,3}. Empty mask is immediate NO.
Theorem A: R is realizable by an ordinary matching gadget (boundary selected vertices retained and covered internally, other boundary vertices deleted) iff B satisfies rank-two matroid basis exchange. In fact two internal slots suffice.
Necessity: take two perfect matchings of the internal-plus-selected-port graphs for A,B. Their symmetric difference is alternating paths and cycles; starting at a port a in A minus B yields an alternating path to another changed port. Flipping it changes exactly these two ports, leaving a valid matching on a new boundary set. Since every permitted set has size two, the other port must belong to B minus A. This is basis exchange. Argument works for arbitrary size matching gadgets, with independent port twists (symmetric exchange is invariant under twisting); not just the proposed two-slot graph.
Sufficiency: nonloop elements of a rank-two matroid are partitioned into parallel classes, and bases are exactly cross-class pairs. For completeness, if distinct nonloops a,b have no base {a,b}, and b,c likewise, but {a,c} is a base, choose a base {b,d}. Exchange from {b,d} against {a,c} for d would force {b,a} or {b,c}, contradiction. Thus nonadjacency among nonloops is transitive, and the base graph is complete multipartite. There are at most two nonsingleton classes on four ports. Put each nonsingleton class on its own exclusive slot, all singleton classes on both slots, loops on neither. If one nonsingleton class, use slot 1; if two, use different slots. Every cross-class pair and no same-class pair admits a bijection to the two slots. Take edges slot->port accordingly. If rank-two family nonempty, both slots can be covered on some pair. This proves exact realization, not just an analogy to a delta-matroid. Explicit constant-time discovery can also enumerate the 256 pairs of slot-neighborhood bitmasks.
Every nonempty subfamily obtained by fixing any one port bit is matroidal: for a fixed included port all bases share it, and for a fixed excluded port all bases lie on three elements, hence any distinct bases intersect in one. Exchange is immediate in either case. Further pins preserve exchange. A rejected/empty branch is not counted as feasible.

Exhaustive finite classification of the 64 subsets of six legal words: empty 1; both matching-realizable and bijunctive 21; matching only 15; bijunctive only 15; neither 12. Bijunctive tested by Boolean majority (equivalently exact binary projections); independent coordinate twists preserve majority. Thus 36 nonempty matching masks, 36 nonempty bijunctive masks. The 12 neither masks are exactly triangle-plus-pendant base graphs (paw); this finite characterization can also be verified by listing 4-edge graphs on four vertices. No claim that arbitrary mixtures of the two individually tractable languages are tractable.

## 2. Wire-repair terminal (Theorem B)
Let the explicit atom be an edge CSP: each Boolean wire has two factor-port incidences, parent word treated as pinned unary factors; transit wires retain their original IDs. Factors are original EQ3/EO3 and exact submasks of FCG02 EQ3, EO3, PAIRED4, MATCHING4, ONEHOT4. Every relation arity <=4. A locally admitted factor is one that after an independent coordinate twist has a nonempty constant-cardinality matroid basis table. For ranks 0/4 singleton, rank 1 any nonempty subset, rank 2 on <=4 by A, rank 3 on <=4 by the complement of rank 1: all elements outside the possible omission set are mandatory and get dedicated slots, the possible omission set gets |U|-1 common slots. General singleton tables use fixed coordinate twists. Uniform finite acquisition: enumerate <=16 twists and <=2^16 slot-incidence tables, compare exact table, no hidden semantic oracle. These are constants. A good factor stays good under arbitrary pins (basis deletion/contraction with fixed elements retained as coloops; or the explicit <=4 rank characterization).
For every remaining factor define eligible repair ports for which BOTH nonempty conditioned tables become good. All EQ3 have any port (pin forces singleton), EO3 are already good, MATCHING4 have all ports by A, PAIRED4 any port (one duplicated pair fixed, remaining pair has at most two equality values, twist one port to normalize), ONEHOT4 at least either duplicated singleton-indicator port p or q (p=q and p+r+s=1, pin p makes singleton or an exact-one relation on r,s). All statements hold for arbitrary submasks; empty branches reject. Eligible ports can be checked exhaustively on the explicit table, potentially more than these sufficient choices. Additional factor languages require their own recognition and nonempty eligibility, or decline.
Construct graph H on bad factors, edge whenever one original wire joins two bad factors at eligible ports at both ends. Compute maximum cardinality matching M. Pin one representative wire per matched edge, then one eligible wire per unmatched bad factor. Distinct matched factors covered jointly. No two unmatched factors are joined at eligible ends (maximality); hence selected wires distinct. If b is number of bad factors, k=b-|M|. Optimality for this static repair task: given any covering set of wires assign each bad factor to a covering wire; groups of size two are disjoint pairs in H and form a matching. If s wires cover b factors, at least b-s double groups exist, so s>=b-|M|. This does not optimize arbitrary semantic regrouping or arbitrary branch-dependent repairs.
Enumerate the 2^k assignments to selected wires. Every model survives its own guard. Restrict BOTH endpoint masks, reject empty, acquire matching gadgets for every remaining factor (at least one eligible pin for each bad factor, extra pins preserve goodness). Match the internal slots to exactly the selected boundary ports y=1. For a wire with equal endpoint twists, join its ports by an edge; it must be used iff both port bits y=0, enforcing equality. For unequal twists use a length-two path with a fresh middle vertex, which must match exactly one endpoint; y values are complementary, hence original wire bits x are equal. Perfect matching of whole gadget graph iff a compatible factor word exists. Read internal matching status of every port to recover x, verify all original masks and pins. Graph can be nonplanar; ordinary deterministic blossom suffices. No counting bijection is claimed, as a feasible tuple may have several slot matchings.
T(N,k)<=2^k poly(N), including optimal cover discovery, compilation and witness reconstruction; S=poly(N) serial even if k large. Applies independently to each atom with its exact acquired masks; if k<=c log2(N+2) for fixed c per atom, this is polynomial. Does NOT bound k on arbitrary source. FCG02 2-SAT pairing-lane parameter can be improved by recognizing bijunctive masks first: only nonbijunctive MATCHING4 need pairing split, with other source EO3 owners handled by the existing phase portfolio. Do not join a matching-feasible projection with a separately 2SAT-feasible projection without exact common interface.

## 3. Genuine scalable obstruction to universal matching-mask closure (Theorem C)
Use the explicit 24-variable source C,P in audit.js, archived square-medial control. Its incidence graph has 48 vertices, 72 edges and an explicit sphere embedding (26 oriented faces). Exhaustive exact cut test: among 62,269 edge-deletion sets of size <=3, precisely 48 disconnect, all isolate one vertex on a 3-cut. Thus it is 3-edge-connected with no nontrivial 3-bond. A cubic 3-edge-connected simple graph is 3-vertex-connected: a cut vertex implies a bridge; two separating vertices require at least three attachments per component, hence exactly two components, separator vertices nonadjacent, and one has at least two attachments to one component; adding that vertex to the component creates an edge cut <=2. This is impossible. Source partition and distinct signatures checked.
Choose two disjoint pairs of same-color incidences A={(x0,c0),(x15,c15)} color 1, B={(x1,c1),(x10,c10)} color 0. In given embedding A is cofacial on [0,24,4,28,15,39,13,37], B on [1,25,6,30,10,34,3,27] (clause IDs offset 24). Neither pair touches the other's chosen face. Make k copies of the base. For consecutive copies alternate A and B. Replace each of the two matching-index internal incidence edges u_i-v_i in both copies by cross edges u_i(left)-v_i(right) and u_i(right)-v_i(left), preserving all variable/partition and clause degrees. The attachment order around each face is u1,v1,u2,v2; cross mapping reverses the order cyclically, allowing disjoint annular arcs by reflecting the new component if needed. The unused next attachment face is untouched. Thus planarity inductively.
3-edge connectivity: for any cut that splits only one old component, the number of crossed old removed edges is compensated by cross edges to the unsplit component, yielding >=3. If both components are split, each retains >=1 internal crossing after two removed edges. The fresh base retains >=2, since a cut of size 3 containing both removed disjoint edges would be a nontrivial 3-cut of the base. Thus >=3. If neither split but sides separate copies, four cross edges. By induction and cubic connectivity lemma the chain is 3-connected. No two variables share signatures: each modified variable has one foreign clause and two native ones, foreign clauses distinct within its copy; across copies, two native clauses in different copies cannot fit in the same triple. Unmodified variables inherit distinct signatures. Thus authenticated source family, n=24k.
Every prefix/suffix bond between copies has two positive and two negative incidences all same color. Order ports as two outgoing bits followed by two incoming bits. At a splice, neighboring sides exchange these halves. Compute each fixed-size base region's exact boundary table by checking all six conservation words at each active boundary, substituting fixed incoming variables and pinning outgoing values; deterministic exact-one unit propagation plus binary branching on unassigned local bits. Full table (left,right), with - absent:
 left absent, A right: (-,0000),(-,1111).
 A left,B right: (0000,0000),(0000,1111),(0110,0110),(1001,0110),(1001,1001),(1111,0000).
 B left,A right: (0000,0000),(0000,1111),(0110,0110),(0110,1001),(1001,1001),(1111,0000).
 A left,right absent or B left,right absent: (0000,-),(1111,-).
These finite facts are reproducible without randomization; the repository standalone script checks all 90 candidate boundary rows, 113 recursive calls, and source embedding/cuts. Inducting using the two middle tables, every prefix at every nontrivial chain bond has exact mask {0000,1111}. Both remain feasible; root closes, so every chain SAT. Normalization gives two disjoint bases {0,1},{2,3}; exchange fails. This disproves universal matchability of genuine source MATCHING4 masks, even after arbitrary coordinate twist or larger ordinary matching gadget. The mask IS bijunctive, and exports one Boolean equality bit. This is not a family resisting the complete portfolio; bounded exact cut DP solves it. It specifically motivates switching representation at the interface, rather than branching on every failed local matching test. Do not infer a large repair count in any single prime atom from linearly many nested cuts.

## 4. Cooperative AND/OR evaluation and clock firewall
A partition of factors into disjoint blocks, with one shared Boolean wire per quotient-forest edge, can be solved if every block has a uniform exact terminal closed under arbitrary unary pins. More generally each shared interface may be explicitly certified to consist of a common Boolean bit with known deterministic port functions; exact projections/lifts must be supplied, not inferred from the number of possible states alone. Leaf emits its two-bit membership table via at most two calls. Parent receives unary allowed-value sets on its child interface bits, imposes them together and solves its local terminal once per parent value. Store local witness and child choices. Induction proves exact masks and original witness lift; no product of child state counts. This permits arbitrarily many matching and 2SAT blocks, including EQ4 messages in C. For multi-bit interfaces, arbitrary child relations can destroy a parent's tractability: apply A/B or another certified terminal, else UNKNOWN. Bounded local interface arity alone is not a bound on total interface complexity.
A clocked portfolio can evaluate a supplied acyclic catalogue of exact guarded queries and transformations: key includes exact source factor ownership, complete boundary/guard relation, representation identity and lift map (not merely a G0-G3 type). AND combines disjoint owned factors via explicit shared-bit tables; OR alternatives must be equivalent representations or an exhaustively covering set of guards. SAT witness verified; UNSAT only from exact rejection of a complete equivalent query or all exhaustive guard children. Decline/timeout propagates UNKNOWN.
