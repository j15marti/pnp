# PARITY-FIBERS45
Date: 2026-10-23 KST

Restricted theorem: For faithful uniform XOR/AND/NOT circuits, suppose a rank-t span of original-source affine linear forms includes one syntactically affine operand at every AND. Find a certificate in 2^O(t) poly(S,m) time by rank-increasing binary branching. Each parity fiber becomes affine. A full deterministic hitting set has at most 2^t(m-t+1) genuine original tapes; exact counting and NO-case fidelity follow. Polynomial for t=O(log n).

New obstruction: A Boolean function affine on every rank-t parity fiber has ANF degree <=t+1. Thus proposed rank-one high-degree examples fail. A repeated-parity recurrence has t=1 while the old coordinate-support certificate needs n variables, but its degree is only 2. Audits: 180 small random circuits, 48 source-scanned 3CNF YES and 48 UNSAT controls, shared functional Tseitin extensions, all without assertion failures.
