FORMAL CLASSIFICATION: Let U mean 'every hypothetical fixed polynomial-time SAT decider has a polynomial-time complete ordinary Resolution proof extractor for all UNSAT CNFs.' In the other direction, the assumed polynomial decider plus U contradicts the unconditional exponential Resolution lower bound. The assertion U is therefore separation-strength itself, NOT an independently verified transport lemma and NOT a separation proof.
