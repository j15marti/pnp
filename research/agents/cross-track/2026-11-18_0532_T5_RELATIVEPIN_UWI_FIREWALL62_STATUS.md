## New proof: T5 relative-color gadgets
On Z5, let R(a,b) iff (b-a)%5 in {1,3}. With one fresh vertex w, define
G1(u,v;w) = R(u,v) AND R(u,w) AND R(w,v);
G3(u,v;w) = R(u,v) AND R(u,w) AND R(v,w).
Exact modular path intersections prove
EXISTS w G1 iff v-u=1 mod5, and EXISTS w G3 iff v-u=3 mod5.
Reversing u,v gives differences 4 and 2. Difference zero is equality by original-variable identification. No named colors, oracle constants, or non-T5 constraints are needed. Both nonzero gadgets have three ordinary directed arcs and one existential vertex. Source-isomorphism/gauge is preserved.
