## 1. Conservation for every source cut

Write source incidence graph G=(X,C;E), C=C_0 disjoint union C_1 disjoint union C_2. For S subset V(G), each edge e crossing S has original variable endpoint x_e and clause endpoint c_e with color j(e); sign sigma_e=+1 if x_e in S, -1 otherwise. Let b_j be the signed count of crossing edges of color j, a=|S intersect X|, c_j=|S intersect C_j|. Then

    b_j = a - 3 c_j.

Proof: count color-j incidences at variables in S and at clauses in S; internal incidences cancel. For a locally feasible edge-bit assignment z, with equality at every variable vertex and exactly one at every clause vertex, put t=sum of selected internal variable vertices. The same count with weights z gives

    s_j = sum_{e crossing S, color j} sigma_e z_e = t - c_j.
    3 s_j - b_j = 3t - a, independent of j.

This is a necessary identity for any local extension, under arbitrary original pins; no hidden SAT or matching oracle. The complementary side has signs negated and the same permitted conservation relation. Each crossing bit is the value of its original variable endpoint in a complete source solution. Color count congruence b_0=b_1=b_2 mod 3 follows before any computation on truth values. Conservation may be strictly weaker than the actual extendibility mask. Never replace a whole unsolved region by this necessary relation alone.

## 2. The five-type classification (proved)

For a three-edge cut, sum_j b_j is a multiple of 3, is odd and has absolute value <=3. Therefore all signs agree. Color multiplicities are either (1,1,1) or (3,0,0) up to permutation. In the first case s_j must agree and the conservation relation is EQ3={000,111}. In the second case the absent colors have s=0 and the occupied color has signed sum of selected bits equal to the common sign; the relation is EO3={100,010,001}. This proof also includes singleton-vertex cuts (which are not needed for decomposition).

For a four-edge cut, sum_j b_j is even, a multiple of 3 and has absolute value <=4, so is zero. There are exactly two signs of each kind. The b_j are in [-2,2], congruent modulo three, sum zero; hence either (0,0,0), or a permutation of (1,1,-2) or (-1,-1,2).

* PAIRED4: b=0 and two colors each occur once with each sign. The absent color forces 3s_j-b_j=0. The two matched color pairs have equal bits. Four words; arbitrary subrelation is a relation on two Boolean bits with duplicated coordinates and is exactly 2-CNF by forbidding the unwanted assignments.
* MATCHING4: b=0 and all four ports have the same color. The other colors force s=0. The two positive bits sum to the two negative bits. Six words. They form the union of the two perfect pairings between the two positive and two negative ports. On either pairing, only two Boolean bits remain. Hence ANY exact subrelation of the six-word support is a union of at most two bijunctive relations; intersect the actual mask with each pairing and encode each resulting two-bit relation exactly. Empty lanes are rejected. A switch can be represented with guarded binary clauses, or enumerated explicitly; it is not generally possible to encode the unsplit full relation by 2-CNF.
* ONEHOT4: assume b=(1,1,-2) without loss of sign reversal. Both positive ports have distinct colors, both negative ports share the third color. Let the positive bits be p,q and the negative bits r,s. Conservation gives p=q and p+r+s=1. Three words. Sign reversal has the same relation on the corresponding ports. The type reduces to one exact-one triple with a duplicated coordinate.

For EQ3, EO3 and ONEHOT4, every nonempty subrelation is expressed by the same conservation relation with unary pins (select a common value for equality; eliminate unavailable one-hot phases by setting their indicators to zero). The empty mask is immediate infeasibility. In ONEHOT4 the original repeated coordinate must be retained. All five classifications concern actual edge-bit interfaces, not arbitrary graph separators or post-quotient invented ports.

Useful exact output without any pinned solver: enumerate all 3/4 bonds of the original graph, derive every coordinate equality and every three-port exact-one identity valid on the appropriate conservation relation, map ports back to original variables and add those constraints. All original source solutions survive, and retain the original clauses, so both labels and all source witnesses are unchanged. If a derived triple repeats a variable, keep multiplicity: 2x+y=1 forces x=0,y=1, and 3x=1 is inconsistent. Do not silently deduplicate literals. MATCHING4 also exports the signed balance equality; its union-of-two lane decomposition is exact. This supplies constraints to FCG01 clique saturation, G0-G3 phase grouping and AGE01/CPS01 height/CSP tracks without needing any true global assignment. These identities are consequences of original real-linear incidence equations, not a claim that cut enumeration subsumes all Gaussian reasoning.

For a fixed k<=4, all bonds can be enumerated by testing each k-edge subset: remove it, require exactly two connected components and every removed edge crossing them. Since m=O(N), polynomial discovery with O(N^4) subsets and polynomial BFS and verification. In the plane one can instead enumerate simple dual 3/4 cycles and verify the corresponding bonds (the bond-cycle duality is standard). The executed Python audit used the dual version, not an unproved optimal decomposition finder.

## 3. Exact finite-mask atom tree

Every 3-edge cut of the cubic 3-connected source is minimum. Its sides are connected, since disconnection on either side would give disjoint cuts with total size at least 6. Choose a root vertex and orient each cut by its side not containing the root. All these sides are laminar. Self-contained proof: if S,T cross, the four nonempty corners A=S intersect T, B=S minus T, C=T minus S, D=complement have cuts >=3. The identity |delta(S)|+|delta(T)|=|delta(A)|+|delta(D)|+2|E(B,C)| forces diagonal B-C absent and cuts A,D both three; the complementary identity similarly forces A-D absent and cuts B,C both three. The surviving corner graph is a weighted four-cycle. All four corner degrees equal three, so opposite edge multiplicities equal; |delta(S)| is twice an integer, contradicting three. Thus an inclusion tree of all nontrivial 3-bonds is acquired uniformly. 4-bonds may cross; one may greedily add a maximal laminar compatible subfamily, never claim they all form a tree or that this is the optimal choice.

For each tree node S, its local original vertices are S minus its child sets. Keep edge-bit variable IDs on all local incident edges, on the parent cut, and on every child cut. An edge passing through an atom with no endpoint is still present through its boundary IDs. For each parent word (<=3 for 3-bond, <=6 for 4-bond), impose those boundary bits as pins, impose equality/EO3 at local original vertices and all original variable guards there, and impose the exact masks of each child on the same original edge IDs. Decide that local factor network using any exact terminal. This computes the exact feasible mask of S; store one local witness and compatible child words per accepted parent word. Empty root mask is UNSAT; nonempty root mask lifts recursively. Proof by induction: restrict any subtree model to the local factors and child masks; conversely merge any accepted local witness with the selected child extensions. All shared edges agree via IDs (including transit edges); private factors disjoint; thus full source constraints and guards hold. This is a true AND decomposition, unlike calling independent solvers on arbitrary overlapping projections.

For each atom with b matching4 child masks, split each such mask into its two bijunctive pairing lanes. All other child masks and the original local factors compile into 2-CNF plus explicit exact-one ternary owners; pins and repeated coordinates processed exactly. Thus at most 6*2^b pinned calls per atom to the existing 2-SAT/three-phase/phase-conflict/group portfolio, not a product over all atoms. If every called residual has a complete polynomial terminal (or a sound SAT-only terminal covers every satisfiable required pinned query with a common polynomial cap), and b<=c log_2 N uniformly per atom for fixed c, then the tree evaluation is polynomial. If a necessary local query declines, its mask entry is UNKNOWN, not absent; never issue NO based on an uncompleted mask. A simpler unconditional parameterized terminal: if the maximum number q of local Boolean edge-bit equality classes is <=c log N, exhaustive local enumeration computes exact masks in sum_i 2^q_i poly(N) time. Constant or log local scope may allow arbitrarily many atoms. The Python implementation uses this latter bounded-local-scope terminal, not the full heterogeneous matching4 lane theorem.

This gives a new exact compositional lane for C/P/S without replacing any previously valid solver. A finite type count (five) says nothing by itself about the size or complexity of a prime local atom.

## 4. Scalable genuine source controls and obstruction

A 12-variable fixture from the archived genuine-source benchmark has clauses:
(0,5,6),(0,1,7),(1,2,8),(2,5,9),(3,4,10),(3,4,11),(0,3,6),(1,7,8),(2,8,9),(7,9,10),(4,10,11),(5,6,11).
Partition labels [0,1,0,2,1,0,2,2,1,0,2,1]. It is planar and 3-connected, distinct signatures; exact models bitmasks 212,594. A rainbow cut encloses variable indices 0,3,4,5,6,10,11 and clause indices 0,4,5,6,10,11. The cut ports have colors 0,1,2 and inside variable IDs 10,0,5 respectively. Its unique local feasible word in the listed variable order is 0010100 (i.e. [0,0,1,0,1,0,0]); it exports only 000. This is a forced-zero gadget, NOT a generic two-state equality gadget. The local DP correctly rejects the opposite pin.

Replace variable 0 by this gadget, identifying the external incidence at each color with the matching port, then repeat at the color-1 port, which reuses index 0. Each step adds 6 variables and 6 clauses. Because the replaced variable is forced zero in both original models, both extend by the unique gadget word. A SAT witness remains valid and a pin on the deepest color-1 port to 1 is UNSAT. Planarity and cubic 3-connectivity follow from splicing along a vertex: cap the gadget side of the nontrivial three-bond with a new vertex (for graph structure only). Its three attachment endpoints are distinct: otherwise deleting the repeated endpoint and the other endpoint disconnects a nontrivial side, contradicting 3-connectivity. The cap graph is 3-connected: if the cap remains, connectivity after at most two internal deletions follows by contraction in the original; if cap and at most one internal vertex are removed, every remaining component must attach through at least two original external ports (otherwise delete that single port endpoint too), so three ports permit at most one component. Both sides are connected and the ports are cofacial after cutting, so a three-port permutation can be matched by rotation/reflection. Splice of two such cubic 3-connected graphs remains 3-connected: one deletion on each side leaves each side connected (cap plus one deletion) and at least one cross-edge intact; two deletions on one side leave each surviving component connected to an intact port via connectivity with the cap, and the opposite side connected. Partition incidence is correct by construction, and distinct signatures preserved; the finite audit checks them explicitly. This is a scalable tractable control, not an NP-hard family or unbounded number of feasible phases.

Counterexample to universal bounded-atom acquisition by nontrivial 3-bonds: FCG01 square-medial source from J, cubic bipartite polyhedral octahedron-dual refinement, has no nontrivial 3-bond. Its squares expand vertices of 4-edge-connected L(J). Proof: if a three-cut splits no square, it is a cut of L(J), impossible. At most one square can split since each split square contributes at least two internal crossing edges. With one split square Q and full squares on both sides, L(J)-q has at least two edges between the two sides: at least one side has at most two of the four adjacencies to q, so its cut in L(J) has >=4 and >=2 survive. Then >=2 internal + >=2 external crossings, impossible. If all other squares are on the same side, the smaller side lies in Q; its internal crossing count is at least two and each of its vertices has one external edge, so a three-cut isolates just one vertex. Therefore the whole large source is a single 3-bond atom. FCG01 independently solves it by saturated triangles; the obstruction excludes only the 3-cut lane, not the combined portfolio. This same family has many 4-bonds of ONEHOT4 type in the executed controls. It motivates finding a laminar 4-bond decomposition whose prime atoms are handled by FCG01/AGE01/CPS01; no such universal theorem proved.
