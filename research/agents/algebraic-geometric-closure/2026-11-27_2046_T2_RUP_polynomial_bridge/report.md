## Exact RUP to Boolean polynomial calculus
In the square-free F2 Boolean ring x^2=x, encode clause C by its falsity polynomial g. Under the assumptions falsifying C, for any known false literal q its truth bit satisfies g q=0. Suppose a premise has false literals q_1,...,q_s and remaining literal falsity bit t, with t=1 in a conflict. The exact telescoping identity is
g t prod_i(1+q_i) + sum_i [(g q_i) t prod_{j<i}(1+q_j)] = g t.
The first term is a multiple of the premise falsity polynomial; remaining terms are multiples of already acquired zero identities. Thus every propagation and final conflict yields g=0. The proof degree is at most w+k for a width-w RUP conclusion and maximum active premise width k. Multiplication by complemented variables is simulated by multiply and add; all operations interpreted in the Boolean quotient. For fixed w,k the local expanded terms and number of operations per propagation are bounded; balanced summation gives logarithmic depth locally, NOT globally across dependent propagation steps. ## Full degree-d Boolean closure: finite polynomial algorithm
Let V_d be the square-free monomial space up to fixed degree d, D=sum_{i=0}^d binom(n,i). Maintain basis W of derived rows. For each variable x, project W onto degree-d monomials not containing x. Kernel K_x consists exactly of rows whose multiplication by x remains degree<=d: those forbidden monomials map injectively to distinct degree-(d+1) monomials, so no other cancellations can remove them. Compute a basis of K_x by GE; multiply by x and adjoin to W. Sweep to closure, detect 1, else fixed-point refusal in this named calculus. At most D rank-increasing sweeps plus one no-change sweep. Dense elimination, row multiplication and recorded proof DAG have fixed polynomial bounds for fixed d in n (including failed sweeps); for polynomial-sized chart n=poly(N), bounded-degree procedure is polynomial in original N. Credit D-dim W cannot be reset without accounting; at fixed chart each productive insertion decreases it. Exact full chart or a sound necessary relaxation required. This does not prove a fixed d suffices, nor a universally polynomial chart acquisition, nor a universal portfolio deadline.
