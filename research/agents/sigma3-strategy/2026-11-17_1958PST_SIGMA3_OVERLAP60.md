## THEOREM A, PROVED/EXACT: 3 local trace bits, globally NP-complete
For any 3CNF F(x)=AND_j(ell_j1 OR ell_j2 OR ell_j3), encode
Phi_F=EXISTS x FORALL j EXISTS z in {0,1}^3:
AND_{i=1..3} [z_i XOR x_{var(ell_ji)} = 1_{ell_ji positive}]
AND (NOT z_1 OR NOT z_2 OR NOT z_3).
The j challenge indexes one of m clauses (ceil(log2 m) bits); invalid codes use the unique default z=000. Each fixed-x,j affine trace z is UNIQUE and z_i=1 exactly when literal i is false. Local Horn then accepts iff the clause is satisfied. Therefore Phi_F TRUE iff F SAT. This syntactically restricted quantified family is NP-complete (not Sigma3^P-complete). The source translation is uniform and polynomial-size, and a complete deterministic gate trace can be added without changing the result.
Both components independently accept: Horn admits z=000, affine equations uniquely prescribe z for any x; their joint compatibility carries SAT. Each fixed challenge has a 3-bit shared Horn/XOR interface, but acquiring ONE global x remains NP-hard by the standard reduction. Thus local fractional-size SAT questions do not, by themselves, provide global resource contraction.

TRUE: valid global x* plus computed adaptive Skolem triple z(x*,j). FALSE: against a specified x select first actually falsified j(x); its affine equations force 111, contradicting the negative Horn clause; this is a genuine local universal challenge and checkable local NO. However validating that j(x) beats EVERY x is coNP-complete (equivalent to UNSAT F); a compact Herbrand circuit is not a free global negative-answer proof.

## THEOREM B, PROVED/EXACT: shared-variable parameter k
Let H be any explicit Horn formula and E an affine GF(2) system, sharing precisely k source variables S. Enumerate all 2^k assignments to S. For each, check Horn by forward chaining and XOR by Gaussian elimination; conditioned variable sets become disjoint. YES merges two witnesses agreeing on S. NO supplies, for EACH boundary assignment, a replayable Horn derivation ending in contradiction or a GF(2) row-dependency vector with zero left side and right side one. A second pass streams certificates instead of storing exponentially many. Conservative deterministic source costs: T=O(2^k N^3), peak writable S=O(N^2) bits including parsing, each generated branch and certificate. For k<=beta*log2 N, time <=N^(beta+3+o(1)), space <=N^(2+o(1)). This is an upper bound on this source grammar, NOT a forbidden time-space pair. One-bit restrictions preserve Horn and XOR grammars and cannot increase k, but do NOT force N^lambda-size physical children.

In Theorem A's aggregated mixed system, 3m occurrence bits appear in both the global Horn and XOR components, even though a selected clause exposes only 3. A small per-challenge k cannot be substituted for small global k.
