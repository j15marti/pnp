## THEOREM A, EXACT: r-restriction-surjective majority lift
Given arbitrary explicit 3CNF F(u_1,...,u_n) of length M, replace each u_i by h_i(v)=MAJ_{2r+1}(v_{i,1},...,v_{i,2r+1}). For EVERY adaptively chosen partial assignment rho fixing at most r distinct clone bits, h maps its completions onto ALL {0,1}^n: for target u set every unpinned clone in group i to u_i. At most r pinned clones leave at least r+1 free copies per group, forcing majority u_i. Thus F(h(v))|rho is SAT iff F is SAT, for every such rho, including all two-stage histories. Sharp for this gadget: pin r+1 copies of one group opposite desired bit, forcing that majority. This is a restriction-invariance theorem, NOT a lower bound on arbitrary algorithms.

An explicit dynamic-programming threshold circuit for MAJ_{2r+1} needs O(r^2) fan-in-2 gates per group. With r=Theta(log M), the translated circuit has N=M polylog M=M^{1+o(1)} encoded bits while preserving SAT status under Theta(log M) arbitrarily chosen clone-pin steps. Source structure remains syntactically coupled to F, not to a sublinear-size hard query.

## THEOREM B, EXACT: QBF with unique mixed Horn/GF(2) local services
Let outer x=v clones; universal y=(j,s) with binary clause index j and mode s in {0,1}; existential continuation z=(b1,b2,b3,t0,t1). For valid j, force each b_k to the FALSE-literal indicator for F's clause j evaluated at h(v), t0=0, and t1=s AND [h_{i_{j,1}} XOR h_{i_{j,2}} XOR (j mod 2)]. For s=0 additionally require the single negative Horn clause NOT b1 OR NOT b2 OR NOT b3. For s=1 the parity response is always valid. Invalid j binary codes have unique z=00000 and are vacuous YES. Thus the trace is uniquely forced for every x,y, whether or not its final Horn condition is satisfied. The exact continuation returns YES iff s=1 or selected clause is satisfied; NO means three forced b's equal 1 contradict the Horn clause.

Phi_F = EXISTS v FORALL (j,s) EXISTS z C(v,j,s,z) is TRUE iff F is SAT; the same equivalence holds after ANY <=r clone-bit restrictions. The promised restricted class is NP-complete. The FULL circuit is nonlinear due to majority, indexing and selector, and is NOT claimed to be globally Horn or affine. Only the fixed-input local services are tractable.

YES: set clones to any satisfying original u, then evaluate the unique 5-bit adaptive Skolem trace. FALSE: for each proposed v, universal Herbrand counterstrategy selects first falsified clause j(v) and s=0. This is a polynomial-size circuit that gives a checkable losing continuation. Verifying that this candidate wins against ALL v is coNP-complete, equivalent to F UNSAT. Do not treat the global negative claim as a free certificate. These challenges are polynomially many, not an exponential-count lower-bound claim.

## K,L RESOURCE AND PADDING LEDGER
For ONE hypothetical uniform SAT decider A with time q^{K+o(1)} (K>=1) and writable space q^{L+o(1)}, and original M, lifted N=M^{1+o(1)}, deciding Phi needs one actual A(F) call, including NO:
T_dec=O(N+M^{K+o(1)})=N^{K+o(1)};
S_dec=O(N+M^{L+o(1)})=N^{max(1,L)+o(1)} using one reusable query/source buffer.
Global YES witness extraction by <=n<=M sequential SAT calls A(F|prefix) has
T_extract=O(N+M^{K+1+o(1)})=N^{K+1+o(1)};
S_extract=O(N+M^{L+o(1)})=N^{max(1,L)+o(1)}.
For false, actually compute SAT(F)=NO before validating universal y(v). The original hard query remains size M=N^{1+o(1)}, so lambda=1. Enlarging repetition to r=M^alpha can make N=M^{1+alpha+o(1)} and apparent lambda=1/(1+alpha), but delta*lambda=1 on the original M: padding gives no exponent speedup. All access, serialization and peak workspace charged on same machine. No forbidden time-space region is crossed.
