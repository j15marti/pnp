## Theorem A: simple selector, hard global validity (PROVED)

With precisely the supplied tournament `A->{B,D},B->{C,E},C->{A,D},D->{B,E},E->{A,C}`, self-comparisons false, and `Q(h,u,v)=R(h,u) XOR R(h,v)`, a fresh private head for graph edge uv satisfies
`exists h Q(h,z_u,z_v) iff z_u != z_v`. Thus
`Phi_G=exists z forall edge e exists h Q(h,z_u,z_v)` is TRUE exactly when G is 5-colorable.

A uniform deterministic counterstrategy `p_G(z)` returns the *first* equal-colored source edge or NONE. A local NO is verified by testing all five possible head values (or the proven exact projection), not presumed free. The program works on both SAT and UNSAT graphs in O(|E|) edge probes and O(log |E|+log |V|) writable control bits given read-only random-access source and row. Yet `forall z: p_G(z) != NONE` holds iff G is NOT 5-colorable and is coNP-complete as a global validity problem. The small program does not decide its own global validity.

## Theorem B: critical edge coverage does not force a large strategy (PROVED)

If G is edge-critical non-5-colorable, every G-e has a 5-coloring c_e, with exactly one monochromatic edge e in the original G. Hence `p_G(c_e)=e` for every edge: the strategy's image covers the entire critical source, but the single uniform program still scans and selects. For the SAT sibling G-e, `p_{G-e}(c_e)=NONE`; wrong-path soundness requires a full verified witness, never a false NO.

## Theorem C: K6 exact critical-witness transport (PROVED)

From a five-coloring of K6-f whose unique color collision is f, obtaining a K6-e proper coloring with exactly one collision at e throughout one-vertex recoloring steps takes exactly **one step iff e intersects f**, and **two steps iff they are disjoint**. Transfer the duplicate along one or two edges. There are 120 ordered intersecting and 90 disjoint (f,e) pairs. Complete independent BFS checked all 210 pairs. This concerns **SAT siblings** and says nothing about deciding the UNSAT parent.

Quantifier trap: `exists z forall edge exists h` over a triangle is TRUE, whereas `exists z exists h forall edge` is FALSE. Ties do not count as wins; per-edge projection is not valid for reused heads.
