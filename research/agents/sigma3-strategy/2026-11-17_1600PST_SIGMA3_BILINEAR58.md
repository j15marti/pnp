## R58-B: exact bilinear Sigma3 extension
For each label t supply A_t,B_t,C_t,D_{t,j},d_t over GF(2), with R_t(x,y)=B_t*x+C_t*y+d_t+SUM_j y_j*D_{t,j}*x. The product x_i*y_j makes the matrix nonlinear *jointly*, but affine in y at fixed x. Define Phi=exists x forall y exists z [ A_{g(y)} z=R_{g(y)}(x,y)]. Let rows L_t span ker(A_t^T). With genuine frames (p_t,q_{ti}), Phi TRUE iff ONE common x satisfies every L_t R_t(x,p_t)=0 and L_t R_t(x,q_{ti})=0. Each is linear in x. Proof: A z=b solvable iff L b=0, and affine functions vanish on a fiber iff they vanish on its affine frame. Guard depends on y only; A_t can vary with t. TRUE: compute x*, z_{t0} for anchor and z_{ti} for RHS differences, then z(y)=z_{t0}+SUM_i c_i(y)z_{ti}, where coefficients express y+p_t in genuine basis. FALSE: for each proposed x, scan genuine frame representatives to find legal losing y and dual u satisfying u^T A_t=0, u^T R_t(x,y)=1. A global linear inconsistency certificate also produced by Gaussian elimination. Gate trace uniqueness does not entail unique Skolem witnesses when A_t has kernel.
