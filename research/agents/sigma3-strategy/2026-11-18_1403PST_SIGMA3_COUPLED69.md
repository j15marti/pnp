## THEOREM A: DISJOINT-MAJ3-AFFINE-0 is NP-complete (EXACT)
Input: affine equations A v=b over GF(2), each involving at most three variables, and **pairwise disjoint** ordered triples of variables; demand MAJ3 of each triple equals zero. Every MAJ target is the same all-zero pattern. Feasibility is NP-complete.

Reduction from GRAPH 3-COLOR: create a vertex triple (a_v0,a_v1,a_v2) for each vertex with XOR(a_v0,a_v1,a_v2)=1 and MAJ3=0. Thus exactly one of three colors is active. For each edge {v,w} and color c, create a fresh triple (l_ec,r_ec,q_ec), with XOR(l_ec,a_vc)=0, XOR(r_ec,a_wc)=0, q_ec=0, and MAJ3(l_ec,r_ec,q_ec)=0. Because q_ec=0, the majority is a_vc AND a_wc; this prohibits equal endpoint colors. Triple inputs are disjoint: sharing is exclusively through affine equations. Construction has |V|+3|E| majority blocks, 3|V|+9|E| Boolean variables, |V|+9|E| affine equations, all rows of arity <=3. Graph 3-colorable iff instance feasible. Membership in NP is immediate.

Let h(v) be the vector of all these independent local majorities, and A(v) the affine conjunction. Even deciding whether the **fixed all-zero vector** belongs to Q_A={u:exists v A(v) & h(v)=u} is NP-complete. Thus semantic removal of majority blocks must retain or solve the image predicate. It cannot be replaced by independent free output bits without a sound certificate.

## THEOREM B: Certified majority clone removal, and coupling falsifier (EXACT)
If h_i=MAJ_{2r+1} of distinct copies used nowhere except through h_i, and at most r copies per group are pinned, all original u_i in {0,1} are realizable: set every remaining clone to u_i. Thus SAT(F(h(v))|rho)=SAT(F(u)) exactly under these syntactic promise conditions. A polynomial-time parser can remove the repetition, but only returns original F, not a smaller hard SAT query.
Counterexample to unsound removal: pin v0=0, impose affine equation v1 XOR v2=1, require MAJ3(v0,v1,v2)=1. The original coupled source is UNSAT, while the uncoupled quotient u=1 is SAT. Actual compiler rejects coupled or overpinned sources; it does not drop A.

## QBF LIFT AND STRATEGY POLARITIES
Enumerate every MAJ=0 and affine equation as a local indexed check t_j(v)=0, with invalid binary indexes assigned t=0. Define
Phi_G = EXISTS v FORALL j EXISTS z: (z=t_j(v)) AND (z=0).
The one-bit local gate trace is uniquely forced. YES when t_j=0 with z=0; otherwise NO with forced z=1 contradicting z=0. Phi_G TRUE iff G is 3-colorable. This special quantified language is NP-complete, NOT claimed Sigma3^P-complete.
For NO instances the polynomial-size candidate Herbrand strategy j(v)=first violated constraint defeats every v, but checking its **global validity** is coNP-complete (non-3-colorability); no negative answers counted for free. Swapping the outer quantifiers to FORALL j EXISTS v,z makes even K4 TRUE, because each local test is individually satisfiable. Controls also checked z=y and z=x XOR y.

## SINGLE-MACHINE K,L LEDGER
For one hypothetical deterministic uniform SAT decider A using q^{K+o(1)} time and q^{L+o(1)} writable bits on queries q, K>=1, take M as original graph length and N=M^{1+o(1)} as indexed circuit source length. A 3-color CNF query has length q=O(M)=N^{1+o(1)}. One actual SAT call, including a NO result, decides Phi with time N^{K+o(1)}, conservative peak writable space N^{max(1,L)+o(1)} with reusable source/query buffering. Extract a global YES witness through at most O(M) actual sequential SAT pinning calls in time N^{K+1+o(1)} with the same peak-space exponent. Local adaptive z(j) is cheap once v is known. A counterchallenge j(v) can be computed by a complete scan in O(N), but proving every v fails requires an actual global NO decision or legitimate proof.
The hard query exponent remains lambda=1. Source padding N=M^delta only gives nominal lambda=1/delta with delta*lambda=1 against original M. No forbidden simultaneous time-space bound is obtained.
