\paragraph{Result and status.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT scoped guard; COMPUTATIONAL audit; ARC premise OPEN.}
The exact clause-first partition from FINAL-LINK-HYBRID1 is useful semantic
infrastructure, but an unmerged tree made only from that partition cannot by
itself provide a polynomial-fanout, fixed-power-shrink ARC successor.  On an
independent product of positive 3-clauses, polynomial tree fanout removes only
$O(\log M)$ clauses, whereas fixed-power shrink requires removing
$m-o(m)$ clauses and creates $3^{m-o(m)}$ leaves.  Exact residual merging
collapses this particular tree, and a tagged variant that prevents equality
merging is still easy by component decomposition.  Thus the statement is a
guard on one realization, not a SAT lower bound.  No achievable pair is added,
the SAT-specific finite-hit premise remains \textsc{Open}, and P versus NP
remains open.

\section*{The exact guard}
For
\[
 F_m=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(x_i\vee y_i\vee z_i),
\]
all $3m$ variables are distinct.  Let $M_m$ be the length of the usual
explicit indexed-CNF encoding, so $M_m=\Theta(m\log m)$.

\begin{theorem}[CLAUSE-FIRST-FANOUT-SHRINK-GUARD1]
Fix $0<b<1$.  Consider a tree realization which applies
\texttt{CLAUSE-FIRST-COVER1} to $k$ distinct clauses of $F_m$, retains every
resulting branch, and does not merge semantically or syntactically equal
residuals.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The tree has exactly $3^k$ nonempty leaves, and every leaf retains
exactly $m-k$ untouched 3-clauses.
\item If the fanout is at most $M_m^a$ for a fixed $a$, then
$k\le a\log_3 M_m=O(\log M_m)$ and
\[
 \frac{M_{m-k}}{M_m}\longrightarrow1.
\]
In particular, no fixed $b<1$ can satisfy $M_{m-k}\le M_m^b$ for all large
$m$.
\item Conversely, if every leaf has encoded length at most $M_m^b$, then
\[
 k=m-O\!\left(\frac{M_m^b}{\log M_m}\right)=m-o(m),
\]
and the fanout is $3^{m-o(m)}=2^{\Omega(M_m/\log M_m)}$, hence exceeds every
fixed power of $M_m$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On each selected clause the three first-true terms are nonempty and disjoint.
Because the clauses use disjoint variables, choices multiply and no unselected
clause changes.  This gives exactly $3^k$ leaves and $m-k$ residual clauses.
The first implication follows from $3^k\le M_m^a$.  Since
$k=O(\log M_m)=o(m)$ and $M_r=\Theta(r\log r)$, the residual-to-source ratio
tends to one.  For the converse, $M_{m-k}\le M_m^b$ and the explicit encoding
give $m-k=O(M_m^b/\log M_m)=o(m)$; substituting into $3^k$ yields the stated
superpolynomial bound.
\end{proof}

The theorem is exactly a tree statement.  On $F_m$, all three children of one
clause normalize to the same remaining formula, so exact residual equality
can merge them.  To ensure this escape was not being hidden, the bounded audit
also uses a tagged constant-size block.  Besides
$(x\vee y\vee z)$, each decision variable has two polarity guards on fresh
tag variables.  The three first-true residual clause sets are pairwise
distinct and incomparable, and $k$ independent tagged blocks therefore have
$3^k$ distinct product signatures.  Even after all main clauses are processed,
every residual has $\Theta(m\log m)$ explicit size.  But the source is a
conjunction of constant-size variable-disjoint components, so component
decomposition and direct solving bypass the tree.  This is the required easy
family and independent-product control.

\section*{Failed controls and scope}
\begin{description}
\item[Equality merging.]  On the untagged product all three residuals are
equal, so equality sharing defeats the raw tree count.  The theorem therefore
does not lower-bound the combined hybrid DAG.
\item[Direct solving and components.]  The positive product is solved by a
linear scan.  The tagged product separates into constant-size components.
Neither is a hard SAT family.
\item[Padding.]  Tautological or duplicate padding is removed before $M$ is
measured.  Raw padded length cannot create a valid contraction.
\item[Source carry.]  A pointer into the parent is not an ordinary standalone
CNF child.  A virtual-source escape remains allowed only with its decoder,
access traffic, retained state, and endpoint simulation charged.
\item[Current-scale calls.]  A call to an $N^K$ machine at parent scale costs
$N^{K+o(1)}$ and retains the unknown $K$.  The test records this separately
from a genuine $N^{2/3}$ call.
\item[Oracle/algebraic replacement.]  Direct evaluation bypasses the plan on
the easy controls.  No tree-count statement is promoted across that model
change.
\end{description}

\section*{Primary literature and transfer audit}
IPZ's sparsification lemma represents fixed-width CNF as a disjunction of
$2^{\varepsilon n}$ sparse formulas in $2^{\varepsilon n}\poly(n)$ time
\cite{ipz2001}.  It is an exact theorem transfer only as a warning that
branching can trade fanout for sparsity; it does not give polynomial fanout
with fixed-power encoded shrinkage.

Dell--van Melkebeek Corollary~1 excludes, assuming
$\mathrm{coNP}\nsubseteq\mathrm{NP}/\mathrm{poly}$, reductions making
$O(n^a)$ queries of $O(n^c)$ bits when $a+c<d$ for $d$-SAT
\cite{dellvanmelkebeek2014}.  That is model-matched calibration for dense
sources, not a lower bound usable inside the assumption $P=NP$, where its
noncollapse hypothesis fails; it also supplies no equality-case constructor.

Samar--Vinyals--Ganesh give a polynomial deterministic CDCL configuration on
Ordering Principle formulas while DPLL/tree-like resolution is exponential
\cite{samar2026cdcl}.  This is a useful proof-complexity transfer boundary:
tree behavior does not transfer to stronger proof search, much less to an
arbitrary RAM SAT algorithm.  Treating a clause-first tree guard as a machine
lower bound is therefore a \textsc{Failed Transfer}.

The repository's backdoor-DNF and component mechanisms are exact interfaces
on their stated classes, but no reviewed source proves favorable all-CNF
coverage and acquisition within the charged ARC envelope
\cite{ordyniak2021bdnf}.  No post-quantum ledger technique provides a direct
object correspondence here; hashes and collision arguments would label the
branches without reducing their number or their ordinary-CNF size, so that
analogy is rejected for this arrow.
