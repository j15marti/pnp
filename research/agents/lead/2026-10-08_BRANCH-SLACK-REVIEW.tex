\section{A weaker sufficient target (conditional)}
Fix constants $0<h<1$, $\eta\ge0$, $p<1$ with $\eta+h<1$.
Suppose under $P=NP$ a fixed uniform deterministic constructor, on every
ordinary $N$-bit CNF, produces an exact OR of at most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$
ordinary CNFs of length at most $N^{h+o(1)}$. Suppose its total local
construction, combination and regeneration time is $N^{1+o(1)}$ and
its live local workspace is $N^{p+o(1)}$. All bounds must be uniform over
inputs of each length, recursively closed and charged, including failed
routes, source access, solver calls and output. Children are processed
sequentially; no free external storage is allowed.

Given one fixed polynomial seed with exponents $K,L$, finite-depth
composition has upper-exponent recurrences
\[
t_0=K,\quad t_{d+1}=\max(1,\eta+ht_d),\qquad
s_0=L,\quad s_{d+1}=\max(p,h,hs_d).
\]
For $q=\eta/(1-h)<1$, induction gives
$t_d=\max(1,q+h^d(K-q))$ (taking $K\ge1$).
Also $s_d=\max(p,h,h^dL)$ for $d\ge1$.
Consequently a fixed depth, depending on the fixed seed but not $N$,
reaches simultaneous exponents $(1,\max(p,h))$, with the second strictly
below one. Fortnow's deterministic SAT endpoint excludes this pair on
the matched random-access model. This relaxes the single-child $h=2/3$
target; it does not construct a qualifying OR compiler.

At the boundary $\eta+h=1$, $t_d=1+h^d(K-1)>1$ for every finite
$d$ when $K>1$. A growing-depth construction needs a separate uniform
error, termination and workspace argument; it is not this fixed-depth proof.
\section{Literature transfer audit}
Fortnow, Time--Space Tradeoffs for Satisfiability (2000), supplies the
near-linear-time/sublinear-space endpoint:
\url{https://lance.fortnow.com/papers/files/npvsnl.pdf}.
Williams, Simulating Time With Square-Root Space (2025), Theorem 1.1,
supplies a multitape time-to-space inclusion, not the simultaneous time
bound required here. Remark 1.6 additionally limits random-access transfer:
\url{https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/time-vs-space.pdf}.
Thus its direct use to close this compiler is a failed transfer, not a
refutation of the simulation theorem.
\section{Controls and limitations}
The exact-rational script branch\_slack\_audit.py tests strict-slack
recurrences and the boundary countercontrol. It does not test a SAT
algorithm or establish asymptotic acquisition. Read-only parent inputs
at the root do not make recursively materialized inputs free; the $h$
space floor charges them. Serial OR evaluation charges total work, not
parallel elapsed time. Hash widths cannot replace exact OR semantics.
The composition argument is relativizing bookkeeping, not an independent
nonrelativizing separation. No circuit property or algebraic mechanism
is constructed, so Natural Proofs or algebrization barriers are not bypassed.
The missing bridge remains an all-input constructor meeting these bounds.
