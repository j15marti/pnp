\section{The precise target}
The target is to match actual constructors to the established composition
theorems on one realization. On the direct contradiction route the missing
implication is
\[
 P=NP\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\mathcal W\ \forall F\quad
 \mathcal W\text{ meets a model-matched favorable joint contract on }F.
\]
Here $\mathcal W$ may be a finite typed system, a covered portfolio, or one
fixed favorable member of an exact family. It need not be one per-step
shrinker. Every state reachable from a root must be supported, not every
mathematically possible state of every type. The original read-only input
is excluded from workspace; derived tables, formulas, caches and suspended
states are charged. Sizes are actual encoded bits, not variable counts,
hash lengths or route-identifier lengths.

\section{How the established theorems compose}
For each actual transition $e:i\to j$, record input domain, output encoding,
exact semantic role, $\eta_e,\theta_e,g_e,s_e$, any retained parent state,
and the concrete successor entry point. Require the output to satisfy that
entry point's preconditions, or insert a proved and charged adapter.
For example, a trie leaf identifier cannot simply be relabelled a short CNF.

Fix finitely many types, $0\le\theta_e\le1$, and worst-case uniform
exponent errors. After local discovery, failed attempts, shared setup and combination have
been included in $r_i,\sigma_i$, STREAMCOMP1 supplies the sufficient ledger
\[
 C_i\ge\max\{r_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(\eta_e+g_e+\theta_e C_j)\},
 \qquad
 \rho_i\ge\max\{\sigma_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(s_e,\theta_e\rho_j)\}.
\]
These formulas presuppose the charged simulation interface. A local
generator bound on a materialized parent cannot be transplanted unchanged
to an implicit parent if nested access is more expensive. Persistent
representations must be added to the space floors. Shared work may instead
use the sharper already-proved batch bound; these are sufficient upper
certificates, not lower bounds forbidding reuse.

For a cycle $e_1,\ldots,e_d$, put $\Theta=\prod\theta_{e_k}$ and
$J=\sum_k(\prod_{q<k}\theta_{e_q})(\eta_{e_k}+g_{e_k})$.
CYCLEGAIN1 and STREAMCOMP1 permit $\Theta<1$, with requirement
$C_i\ge J/(1-\Theta)$. All cycles and local floors matter. In particular
\[
 C_A\ge\max(1,C_B),\qquad
 C_B\ge\max(1,\tfrac13+\tfrac23 C_A)
\]
has least solution $(1,1)$. The same-scale edge is legitimate if its source
interface and bounded same-scale rank are proved. Adding generator cost
$1/12$ to the second edge changes the least time pair to $(5/4,5/4)$.
An explicit same-scale buffer independently raises the space floor to one.
An identity fallback without contraction supplies no exponent-erasure
certificate, even if its charge intercept is zero.

\paragraph{Why root coverage alone can be insufficient.}
If all ordinary CNFs are covered by a uniform portfolio whose outputs are
ordinary CNFs, recursive domain coverage follows. No extra semantic closure
theorem is then needed. If members accept special types, metadata or
promises, coverage must instead be proved for the reachable successor
states as well. This is a compatibility check, not a demand that every
individual member handle every CNF.

\section{The recovered strongest proof spine}
On the direct route, the critical-boundary report [B] already proves that a
uniform resumable closed constructor with
\[
 q(n)\le n^{1-h+o(1)},\quad m(n)\le n^{h+o(1)},\quad
 A(n)\le n^{1+o(1)},\quad S_{\rm local}(n)\le n^{p+o(1)}
\]
for fixed $0<h<1$, $p<1$, yields
$T(n)\le n^{1+o(1)}$ and $S(n)\le n^{\max(p,h)+o(1)}$.
Full recursion removes $K$ even at equality $\eta+h=1$. Fortnow's
model-matched endpoint excludes this all-input algorithm [F]. Thus the
unproved arrow is its derivation under $P=NP$, not the subsequent recurrence
calculation. Proving this constructor unconditionally would already
contradict that lower bound.

The alternative achievable-region route only needs a justified movement
between jointly achievable time-space pairs into a forbidden region.
It need not transform every supplied padded or delayed program. It still
needs one algorithm witnessing both coordinates of each pair, and a
source-grounded improvement implication. A portfolio may supply that
implication, but exactness and cheap behavior on a union of tested examples
do not establish it for every required starting pair.

\section{Tests and independent review}
\texttt{composition\_ledger\_audit.py} exhausts 3,840 two-type rational
parameter cases and performs 38,408 assertions with zero failures.
The grid is $\{0,1/3,2/3,1\}$ for both scale factors, charged intercepts
and local floors, excluding product-one cycles. Closed-form least solutions
are checked against their max-affine equations and eight monotone iterates.
Additional controls exercise a contracting same-scale cycle, regeneration
cost, explicit storage, a root cover lacking successor coverage, a route-ID
interface mismatch, identity cycling and input-dependent same-scale rank.
These are arithmetic and abstract interface checks, not new SAT runs or a
SAT-family coverage test. Runtime was below one second, in one bounded
invocation. No random seed is used.

An independent agent freshly read [R,C,S], checked this reconciliation and
confirmed the bounded-rank and nested-access qualifications. It also
recovered the earlier implementation-to-bound audit: the double wrapper
already has general-input semantics and recursive leaves, but its stored
DAG/cache does not implement the no-persistent-child interface. No new
asymptotic SAT theorem is promoted.

\paragraph{Overstatements corrected.}
``Charged reductions are missing'' is too broad. So are requirements that
every edge shrink or every logical same-scale child occupy linear space.
Conversely, existing exact accounting is not proof that its hypotheses hold
jointly for one general constructor. The old RECURCLOSE1 report's universal
$2\cos(\pi/7)$ product endpoint is not reused; its recurrence survives
independently of that historical endpoint error.

\paragraph{Literature and transfer.}
Fortnow [F] is a direct endpoint transfer only on the matched uniform
random-access time-space model. Ben-Amram's size-change/ranking-function
work [L] motivates a separate termination certificate, but does not supply
SAT coverage or favorable quantitative exponents. That is an analogy,
not a compiler theorem. No cryptographic or random-oracle assumption is
used. The accounting identities survive consistent oracle replacement;
they alone supply no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing separation step.
No constructive large circuit property is proposed. Easy SAT families
retain their direct VALUE routes; no representation-specific cost is
promoted to a lower bound for arbitrary SAT algorithms.
