\paragraph{Result.}
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL reconciliation; general quantitative implication OPEN.}
The double wrapper already implements exact general-input decision semantics,
forward construction without a SAT oracle, small standard-CNF leaves, recursive
termination and normalized-formula sharing. These are not missing constructions.
The inspected implementation does not establish a favorable joint construction,
branching, access and workspace bound. The ARC alternative does not require
normalizing every supplied program. This audit separates those facts rather
than reopening all components under a generic compression label.

\section*{Two distinct meanings of general compression}
Assuming $P=NP$, fix an exact SAT decider $A$ with time $N^{K+o(1)}$.
One can output a fixed satisfiable CNF when $A(F)=1$ and a fixed unsatisfiable
CNF otherwise. This is a uniform SAT-preserving constant-size compression on
all inputs under that assumption. Its construction still costs
$N^{K+o(1)}$ and its space is whatever that realization of $A$ uses.
Thus general polynomial-time semantic compression is not the missing
consequence under $P=NP$. The missing quantitative implication is a strict
improvement of jointly achievable resources. This elementary observation
also explains why a construction that finishes deciding SAT before emitting
zero leaves cannot be credited with free compression.

For the actual double wrapper, the emitted frontier is small per leaf
unconditionally, through branching and simplification. Small output therefore
has two different realizations in this discussion; neither alone implies cheap
acquisition. The conditional canonical compressor and the implemented forward
DAG must not be treated as the same machine.

\section*{Actual source and resource interface}
The inspected source is
\path{experiments/DOUBLE_WRAPPER_2026-10-07/double_wrapper.py}.
Its \texttt{inner} stores \texttt{nodes}, \texttt{keys} and \texttt{todo}.
Every node contains a full normalized formula. OR nodes additionally retain
route assignments, prime labels and hashes. The \texttt{outer} solver retains
its plan while evaluating children, and its unbounded memoization cache uses
formula-valued keys. The source-bit metric is a logical encoding count, not
Python memory. Input exclusion does not make separately allocated normalized
formulas, adjacency maps, intern keys or answer caches free.

Consequently the direct application of STREAMCOMP1's no-persistent-child-
materialization space premise fails for this implementation. This is an
implementation mismatch, not a proof that no equivalent low-space realization
exists. The source-scanning backends avoid residual formula storage but retain
assignments or trails and have different execution bounds; transferring their
space result to this DAG without a simulation theorem would mix realizations.

A correct per-invocation ledger is
\[
T(F)\le A(F)+V(F)+\sum_{H\in Q(F)}T(H),
\]
where $A$ includes all inner construction (including states later collapsed to
constants), $V$ includes charged combination/cache operations, and $Q(F)$ is
the set of recursive requests actually serviced without a previously available
answer. Pre-existing cached answers may reduce this sum, but their acquisition
and retained storage are charged to the enclosing execution. This is not a
claim that all nominal branches must be independently solved.

If one realization proves $A+V\le N^{r+o(1)}$, at most $N^{\eta+o(1)}$
recursive requests of size at most $N^{\theta+o(1)}$ with $\theta<1$, and
uniformly controlled recursion overhead, the familiar sufficient closure is
\[
C=\max\{r,\eta/(1-\theta)\}.
\]
The prototype supplies its measured leaf cutoff, but no uniform favorable
$r,\eta$ and joint workspace certificate. Measuring finite counts is not a
substitute for these bounds.

For an actual implicit child generator the recovered sufficient budgets are
\[
 C_i\ge\max\{r_i,\chi,\delta+p,\eta+g+\theta C_j\},\qquad
 \rho_i\ge\max\{\sigma_i,s_e,\theta\rho_j\}.
\]
Here shared setup is charged once per acquired setup, classification is its
own total cost, and $g,s_e$ charge generation time and space. The formula is
a conservative upper certificate, not a lower bound prohibiting batching.
Nested access and persistent live state must fit the stated interface.
Neither swapping in ORBITBATCH1's field normalization nor setting $g=0$
because a child has a short description is justified.

\section*{Fresh regression and hostile checks}
A new runner uses the recovered double-wrapper source with its reference
solver disabled after independent expected answers are computed. It tests
flat and fast-separator variants on all 296 original cases. All 592 complete
solver checks pass; all measured leaves satisfy their cutoffs. The batch takes
about 0.55 seconds with a 25-second alarm.

Sharing is observed in 492 inner plans. In 377 plans, the leaf count is zero
while construction literal visits are nonzero. All 592 tested plans retain
root formulas at the measured input scale; these cases have no initial unit
simplification reducing that scale. This finite observation is not a universal
memory lower bound. Formula payload, DAG nodes, cache hits, separator trials,
outer metrics and hypothetical uncached full-traversal visits are recorded.
The latter are only a comparison count, not unavoidable runtime.

On the planted seed-17 no-bridge case, input size is 384 measured bits.
The flat plan has three leaves, 19 nodes, 2520 stored-formula bits and 2260
construction literal visits. The fast-separator plan has 15 leaves, 20 nodes,
1551 stored-formula bits and 2232 visits. Both meet the 52-bit leaf cutoff.
This demonstrates why leaf count alone does not rank overall acquisition or
storage. Counters omit sorting, hashing and allocation and cannot certify a
near-linear machine bound.

The original test suite includes Horn, XOR, independent products, pigeonhole,
planted-separator and seeded random controls. These are exact finite tests.
No representation-specific expense is promoted to arbitrary SAT hardness.

\section*{ARC route: the weaker sufficient target}
Let $\mathcal R_{\rm SAT}$ contain time-space pairs witnessed by one uniform
exact algorithm each. For a model-matched forbidden region $\mathcal B$, ARC
needs an implication of the form
\[
 \forall x\in\mathcal R_{\rm SAT}\setminus\mathcal B\quad
 \exists y\in\mathcal R_{\rm SAT}:\quad y\le Mx+b,
\]
with controlled exponent slack, nonnegative $M$, spectral radius below one,
and a fixed point strictly inside an appropriate downward-closed forbidden
region. The recovered iteration argument does not require discovery of the
starting exponent or a transformer acting on every padded/delayed program.
Different algorithms may witness successive pairs. One machine must witness
both coordinates of each pair.

The double-wrapper implementation is independent of the assumed solver $A$.
Its correctness alone therefore does not prove this achievable-region
implication. A constant-size compressor obtained by first running $A$ also
preserves the original exponent rather than establishing a strict improvement.
The needed implication remains OPEN in the inspected ARC and power-shrinker
sources. No new conclusion about the validity of the lower-bound endpoint is
made in this audit.

The accounting and SAT identities supply no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing

interface contracts is organizational only. No new external theorem transfer
or full literature review is claimed.

The exact remaining handoff is to exhibit one original-SAT-scale realization

bound on total replay. If it cannot maintain favorable work, the ARC route
remains logically available and should not inherit this implementation's
failure as a universal obstruction.
