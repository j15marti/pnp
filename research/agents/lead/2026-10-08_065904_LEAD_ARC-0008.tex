\section{Primary literature and transfer boundary}
Williams's model-matched general product endpoint is
\(T(N)S(N)=\Omega_{\mathrm{i.o.}}(N^{\phi-\varepsilon})\), where
\(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), under the hypotheses of Theorem~4.11
\cite{williams2010}.  Fortnow's separate theorem excludes near-linear SAT
time with strictly sublinear space \cite{fortnow2000}.  These are exact
endpoint transfers only after machine, encoding, simultaneity, and input
measure match; neither theorem turns a prefix max-envelope into a successor.

Gomes and Selman study algorithm portfolios whose advantage comes from
runtime distributions, randomized copies, parallel execution, or
interleaving \cite{arc0008GomesSelman}.  This is a useful structural analogy
for practical portfolios, but a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} to the deterministic
worst-case ARC claim: expected or distributional acceleration does not imply
that every input receives a smaller simultaneous exponent pair.  The exact
transfer used here is instead the elementary worst-case maximum accounting
already proved by GLOBAL-PREFIX-LEDGER1.

\section{The certified envelope}
Let a uniform exact partial member use pair \(p=(c,u)\) on every outcome,
including FAIL, and let the unchanged fallback use \(z=(t,s)\).  The proved
sequential ledger is
\[
 F_p(z):=z\vee p=(\max\{t,c\},\max\{s,u\}).
\]
This is an upper bound certified for one sequential realization.  It does not
say the program needs those resources, and therefore cannot support a lower-
bound conclusion.  The question is narrower: can this very certificate
instantiate the sufficient contraction inequalities of the ARC spine?

\begin{theorem}[PREFIX-FALLBACK-NOEDGE1]
Let \(p\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^2\), \(0<\lambda<1\), and \(c_0<\infty\).

\noindent(i) For every \(v\in\mathbb R_{>0}^2\), the inequality
\[
 v^{\mathsf T}F_p(z)\le\lambda v^{\mathsf T}z+c_0
 \tag{1}
\]
fails on the cofinal set \(\{z:z\ge p\}\).

\noindent(ii) Let \(q_0\to q_1\to\cdots\to q_{k-1}\to q_0\) be a directed
cycle and let \(v_{q_i}\in\mathbb R_{>0}^2\).  If each edge is certified only
by \(F_{p_i}\), then no fixed \(\lambda<1\) and finite edge drifts can satisfy
the PHASE-MULTINORM-ARC1 inequalities on a cofinal set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For \(z\ge p\), \(F_p(z)=z\).  Fix \(u>0\) and put \(z=ru\), increasing
\(r\) until \(z\ge p\).  Equation~(1) would imply
\[
 (1-\lambda)r v^{\mathsf T}u\le c_0,
\]
which is false for sufficiently large \(r\).  This proves (i).

For an edge \(q_i\to q_{i+1}\), substitute \(z=ru\) above every fixed guard.
After division by \(r\) and letting \(r\to\infty\), its phase inequality
requires
\[
 v_{q_{i+1}}^{\mathsf T}u
 \le\lambda v_{q_i}^{\mathsf T}u
 \quad\text{for every }u\ge0.
\]
Testing the coordinate vectors gives
\(v_{q_{i+1}}\le\lambda v_{q_i}\) componentwise.  Chaining around the cycle
gives \(v_{q_0}\le\lambda^k v_{q_0}\), impossible because every coordinate
of \(v_{q_0}\) is positive and \(\lambda^k<1\).
\end{proof}

Because \(\R\) is upward closed, any one finite achievable pair witnesses
cofinally many larger exponent bounds.  That cofinal domain lies outside the
bounded Williams half-plane
\(t+s<\phi\), so this is directly relevant to that ARC specialization.  The
same proof applies when different fixed guards \(p_i\) occur on different
edges.  A finite directed graph in which every outside state has an outgoing
pure-prefix edge must contain a directed cycle; hence pure-prefix edges alone
cannot form the ARC-0007 certificate.  An acyclic prefix chain may still
precede a genuinely contracting edge, but then that new edge carries the
load-bearing progress.

\section{What the theorem does and does not retire}
The theorem retires the inference
\[
 \text{finitely many partial recognizers + unchanged fallback}
 \Longrightarrow \text{contracting ARC successor}.
\]
It does not retire the partial recognizers, their exact answers on covered
families, their one-child reductions, or their use in a complete portfolio
once genuine all-input coverage is proved.  Nor does it show that the
sequential implementation has exact exponents \(F_p(z)\); a tighter analysis
could prove a smaller achievable pair.  Such an improvement would be new
information beyond the present max-ledger and must charge source access,
failed attempts, representation, construction, and live space.

For the existing density-gated member \(p=(1,2/3)\).  The current ledger
already observed that its baseline sum \(5/3\) exceeds \(\phi\).  The new
content is the phase-cycle obstruction: relabeling the same identity-leading
map at finitely many graph nodes cannot manufacture contraction.

\section{Hostile controls and barrier audit}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Easy families.} A member may return VALUE in constant or
linear cost on pigeonhole, Horn, 2-SAT, affine, or bounded-treewidth inputs.
The FAIL branch on uncovered inputs still invokes the unchanged fallback, so
easy successes do not establish worst-case contraction.
\item[] \textbf{Source carry/current-scale call.} Carrying or rereading the
source and then calling the original-scale solver retains \(z\) in the max
envelope.  This is the exact obstruction, not an uncharged-acquisition
argument.
\item[] \textbf{Padding.} Canonical encoded length is fixed.  Padding can
change coordinate presentation but cannot turn the identity-leading max map
into a proved strict contraction without a model-preserving rebasing theorem.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products.} A factor not recognized by the
partial member can force the fallback branch.  Coordinatewise maxima preserve
rather than erase its ledger.
\item[] \textbf{Oracle/algebraic replacement.} The envelope proof relativizes
and algebrizes.  A semantic or algebraic oracle may make the actual fallback
cheaper, which confirms that this audit is not the missing nonrelativizing
hinge.
\item[] \textbf{Wrappers.} DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP can inflate
or disguise a fallback but do not make the max-envelope contract.  A new
implementation-specific upper bound remains admissible if proved and charged.
\item[] \textbf{Post-quantum ledger.} Schur squares, quadratic relations,
syzygies, collision entropy, binding, and random-oracle methods do not alter
this order identity.  Classification: \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for this
barrier.
\item[] \textbf{Natural Proofs.} No constructive largeness property of
Boolean functions is used, so that barrier is not invoked.
\end{description}

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
CERTIFIED-PARTIAL-PORTFOLIO1 already proves exact first-success scheduling
under full coverage.  GLOBAL-PREFIX-LEDGER1 already proves the max-envelope
when coverage is absent and a fallback is retained.  WRP-0005 and RSR-0005
already state that the concrete prefix adds no endpoint pair.  ARC-0008 does
not duplicate those results: it closes the remaining abstract escape in which
the same noncontracting envelope is placed at multiple phase nodes and then
mistaken for a phase-dependent contraction.

No contradiction with PHASE-MULTINORM-ARC1 exists.  That theorem permits
genuinely different nonnegative leading maps and explicitly assumes a legal
SAT successor.  PREFIX-FALLBACK-NOEDGE1 says only that identity-leading max
maps cannot supply all edges of its closed certificate.
