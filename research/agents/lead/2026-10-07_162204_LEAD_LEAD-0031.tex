\paragraph{Status.}
This run recovers several components that earlier summaries described too
broadly as open, combines them in one executable realization, and proves two
conditional bridge lemmas.  It does not prove the remaining all-input
resource implication.  P versus NP remains \textsc{Open}.

\section{Material reviewed}
The review covered the current GitHub proof spines and protocol, prior agent
reports, source-backed and double-WRAPPER implementations, frontier and
backdoor experiments, recurrence files, available conversation artifacts,
and the project documents marked \textsc{Proved/Exact} or
\textsc{Computational}.  The decisive recovered records are PRF3-0018,
MULTICHANNEL1, CYCLEGAIN1, STREAMCOMP1, ORBITBATCH1, the capped-restart
lemmas, FRONTIER-JOINT1, BATCHFRONTIER1, and the double-WRAPPER audits.

The primary-literature search checked Williams's SAT time-space endpoint and
2024 self-improvement theorem, parameterized self-improvement, bounded-width
and separator-based circuit/query-DAG evaluation, p-selective
self-reducibility, sparsification and compression limits, backdoors,
succinct representations, and deterministic DAG pebbling
\cite{williams2010,williams2024,jansensarma,gharibianrudolph,
buhrmantorenvliet,paultarjan}.  These sources support restricted mechanisms
and exact transfer guards.  None supplies the missing resource implication
from bare $P=NP$.

\section{Recovered solved components}
The project already proves the downstream exponent algebra.  If one exact
recursive realization has at most $M^{a+o(1)}$ children, child length at most
$M^{b+o(1)}$ with $b<1$, total local work $M^{c+o(1)}$, and charged live
space $M^{s+o(1)}$, then fixed-depth composition eliminates a hypothetical
solver's finite exponents $K,L$ and approaches
\[
 C=\max\left\{c,{a\over1-b}\right\},
 \qquad \sigma=\max\{s,b\}.
\]
Same-scale control edges are permitted when complete return cycles contract.
Implicit children are permitted when every generated-bit request is charged.
Shared setup may be paid once.  A fixed unknown good family member may be
handled by capped sequential restart.  ARC may use different uniform
algorithms at successive stages and does not require a universal source-code
compiler.

The concrete double WRAPPER already proves exact forward AND/OR semantics,
ordinary-CNF leaves below its cutoff, recursive termination, and
decision-preserving normalized sharing.  Source-backed versions avoid stored
residual CNFs.  Frontier equality is an exact future-extension congruence.
These semantic and accounting interfaces are not wholly open.

\section{New conditional bridges}
\begin{theorem}[Forward-tree bridge]
Assume $P=NP$.  Let one exact source-queryable AND/OR tree for an explicit
CNF of length $N$ have $Q=N^{q+o(1)}$ evaluated nodes, total charged
generation and combination work $G=N^{g+o(1)}$, subpolynomial depth and
generator space, and explicit leaf length $M=N^{o(1)}$.  If a depth-first
frame has $N^{o(1)}$ bits, then SAT is decidable in
\[
 T=N^{\max\{q,g\}+o(1)},\qquad S=N^{o(1)}.
\]
Thus $\max\{q,g\}<\gammaTS$ contradicts Williams's endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix the polynomial SAT decider supplied by $P=NP$, with finite exponents
$K,L$.  Each leaf call costs $M^{K+o(1)}=N^{o(1)}$ time and
$M^{L+o(1)}=N^{o(1)}$ space.  Multiply the time by $Q$, add $G$, and retain
only one leaf plus one root-to-current-node stack.  The claimed bounds
follow.  All quantities belong to one implementation.
\end{proof}

For a shared DAG, acyclicity alone is insufficient.  General DAG
recomputation can have severe pebbling tradeoffs \cite{paultarjan}.
Bounded treewidth or small query-DAG separator number is a source-checkable
alternative \cite{jansensarma,gharibianrudolph}, but published generic
transformations do not fix the time exponent below $\gammaTS$.  The wrapper
must supply and charge an evaluator with the required exponent.

A recovered ARC guard also yields a precise two-stage target.  If a first
phase maps time exponent $t$ to one joint witness
$(t_1,s_1)\le(qt,\alpha t)$, and a second phase gives
$(t_2,s_2)\le(a+bt_1+cs_1,d+s_1)$, let
$\theta=bq+c\alpha$.  When $\theta<1$, the return envelope has fixed point
\[
 t_*={a\over1-\theta},\qquad s_*=d+\alpha t_*.
\]
The sufficient endpoint condition is
\[
 { (1+\alpha)a\over1-bq-c\alpha}+d<\gammaTS.
\]
This is \textsc{Proved/Exact} conditional arithmetic.  Neither general-SAT
phase is presently established.

\section{Where $2^k/2^x$ fits}
For a declared population $D_0=2^k$ and exact factors $G_i$,
\[
 D_r={2^k\over\prod_iG_i}=2^{k-x},\qquad
 x=\sum_i\log_2G_i.
\]
Polynomial residual count is equivalent to
$x\ge k-O(\log N)$, and subpolynomial residual count requires
$x\ge k-o(\log N)$.  The calculation becomes a proof step only after a
source-checkable map connects $D_r$ to evaluated nodes or explicit leaf
bits.  Hashes, prime products, terminal reconvergence, and counts of nominal
assignments do not supply that map.  An injective label for $2^d$ histories
itself carries at least $d$ bits.

\section{Combined realization and tests}
The new \texttt{combined\_replay\_wrapper.py} executes source replay,
streaming unit propagation, block-batched frontier dynamic programming,
component AND, Shannon OR, and collision-checked bounded caching in one
solver.  Cache hashes only select candidates; exact normalized-stream replay
verifies equality.  The implementation stores neither residual CNFs nor a
formula DAG and makes no SAT-oracle call.

The 25-second guarded run completed in under two seconds.  It checked 286
answers, 261 cache-disabled comparisons, three forced-collision runs, and 125
rejected unequal collision candidates, with zero errors.  PHP$(7,6)$ required
4,183 recursive calls, 18,284 source passes, and 3,792,865 literal reads.
The corrected logical workspace ledger peaked at 12,440 bits in the
sequential-source model and 13,637 bits with a charged clause index.  These
are finite instrumentation values, not asymptotic RAM bounds.

BATCHFRONTIER1 separately checked 1,728 completed answers on 289 formulas,
with six sound UNKNOWN cap exits.  On a 256-variable chain, block size 16
reduced literal reads from 195,077 to 4,847 while retaining an eight-byte
frontier table.  Its sparse bounded-width envelope is
\[
 C=\max\{2-\beta,1+\omega\},\qquad
 \sigma=\max\{\beta,\omega\},
\]
and always has $C+\sigma\ge2$.  This retires the full-suffix-scan
implementation as an endpoint solution, while preserving frontier merging
as a correct restricted mechanism.

The exact-arithmetic audits performed 635 polynomial-regime bridge checks
and 1,602 two-stage ARC checks.  Williams's fixed-$\alpha$ theorem improves
the exponential variable coefficient, but when the input size is
$M=2^{r(n)n}$ with $r(n)\to0$, its fixed-$\alpha$ output exponent in $M$ is
$(K-1)\alpha/r(n)+o(1/r(n))$.  It therefore does not produce a uniform
polynomial encoded-size exponent.  Williams explicitly lists extension to
the $P=NP$ regime as open \cite{williams2024}.

\section{Strongest present spine}
The endpoint is the product theorem
\[
 T(N)S(N)=\Omega_{\mathrm{i.o.}}
 \left(N^{\gammaTS-\varepsilon}\right)
\]
for every $\varepsilon>0$ and polynomially bounded deterministic SAT time
and space in the stated model \cite{williams2010}.  Hence the valid spine is
\[
 P=NP
 \overset{\textsc{Open}}{\Longrightarrow}
 (C,\sigma),\ C+\sigma<\gammaTS
 \overset{\textsc{Known theorem}}{\Longrightarrow}\bot.
\]
For the fixed-power route, the exact remaining implication is the existence,
under $P=NP$, of one uniform recursively closed realization satisfying
\[
 \max\left\{c,{a\over1-b}\right\}+\max\{s,b\}<\gammaTS,
\]
with every acquisition, output or regenerated bit, branch, equality test,
combination operation, ancestor state, cache, and representation map charged.
A clean sufficient target is
\[
 b=s=\frac23,\qquad a=\frac13,\qquad c=1,
\]
which yields joint exponent $5/3$.  For $b=s=2/3$, it is enough separately
that $a<0.378424\ldots$ and $c<1.135271\ldots$.

For the forward-tree route, the exact remaining implication is the five-way
bound on $Q,G,D,R,M$ stated above.  A p-selector would solve the semantic
choice part, because p-selectivity plus self-reducibility characterizes P
\cite{buhrmantorenvliet}; however, its uncontrolled polynomial cost is the
same missing resource theorem in another form.

\section{Failed controls and handoff}
Current-scale calls preserve the unknown $K$.  Fixed-power child size without
total fanout and construction bounds is insufficient.  A width cap has no
general coverage theorem.  Random bitstreams do not fit the deterministic
endpoint unless every stream is correct or a seed is generated uniformly.
Succinct leaf descriptors are not ordinary SAT instances.  Generic shared
DAG recomputation lacks a favorable guarantee.  The combined realization's
dense assignment masks and component scopes can have $N^{1-o(1)}$ payload on
sparse CNFs.

The next proof attempt should target one of two falsifiable statements:
construct the fixed-power interface near $(a,b,c,s)=(1/3,2/3,1,2/3)$, or
construct a source-queryable forward object with subpolynomial explicit
leaves and total evaluated-node plus generation exponent below $\gammaTS$.
A bounded-treewidth or bounded-separator certificate is useful only together
with an explicit evaluator ledger.  Any future claim should first be tested
on pigeonhole formulas, independent-clause products, sparse unique-clause
families, forced hash collisions, and source-retaining encodings.
