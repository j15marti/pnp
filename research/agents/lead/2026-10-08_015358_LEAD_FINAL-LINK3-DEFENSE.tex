\section{Executive determination}

P versus NP remains \Open{}.  The current project contains enough exact
material for a defensible conditional proof spine, but not for an
unconditional separation.  This audit makes four material repairs:
\begin{description}
\item[] It retires the use of $2\cos(\pi/7)$ as a universal polynomial-space
product exponent.  The published Williams full version retracts that claim
and proves the golden-ratio product bound instead \cite{williams2010}.
\item[] It preserves the direct $(1,2/3)$ landing by citing Fortnow's
near-linear-time, sublinear-space SAT theorem \cite{fortnow2000}.
\item[] It replaces unbounded typed recursion by fixed finite depth followed
by total seed solvers, so termination is proved rather than inferred from a
Bellman ledger.
\item[] It replaces the quadratic generated-child verifier by a
quasilinear canonical verifier, proving closed generator--shrinker
equivalence and eliminating the former condition $2b\le h$.
\end{description}

The canonical detailed statement is \texttt{main10.tex}.

\section{Exact direct spine}

Under $P=NP$, fix one total uniform exact SAT solver with finite exponents
$K,L$.  The remaining open statement is the existence of one fixed uniform
constructor $W$ such that, for every sufficiently large canonical input
$F$ of actual encoded length $M$,
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(W(F))=\operatorname{SAT}(F),\quad
 L(W(F))\le M^{2/3+o(1)},\quad
 T_W(M)\le M^{1+o(1)},\quad
 S_W(M)\le M^{2/3+o(1)}.
\]
All parsing, malformed rejection, route discovery, failed alternatives,
hashing, prime arithmetic, collision handling, equivalence tests, backdoor
search, recursive calls, source scans, replay, random bits, certificates,
output bits, retained parents, materialized queries, and decoder state are
included.  The output is an ordinary CNF in the same domain, so the
constructor is recursively applicable.

If that statement holds, choose fixed $d$ so
$K(2/3)^d\le1$ and $L(2/3)^d\le2/3$.  Fixed-depth composition gives time
$M^{1+o(1)}$ and space $M^{2/3+o(1)}$, contradicting Fortnow.  The original
``divide the $2^K$ scale by repeated $2^x$'' reasoning is exactly this seed
elimination step.  It is not a proof of the first successor.

\section{Exact typed alternative}

For a fixed refined type graph, write $R_i,P_i$ for complete nonrecursive
time and space floors and assign each edge $e:i\to j$ a child scale
$\theta_e$, recursive time weight $w_e$, and retained-state exponent $h_e$.
At finite depth use
\begin{align*}
 C_i^{(0)}&=K_i,&
 C_i^{(d+1)}&=\max\{R_i,\max_e(w_e+\theta_e C_j^{(d)})\},\\
 \rho_i^{(0)}&=L_i,&
 \rho_i^{(d+1)}&=\max\{P_i,\max_e h_e,
                         \max_e\theta_e\rho_j^{(d)}\}.
\end{align*}
If every root-reachable directed cycle has scale product below one, seed
terms vanish geometrically.  A strict landing
\[
 \max_{i\in I_0}C_i^*+\max_{i\in I_0}\rho_i^*<\phi
\]
therefore holds at some fixed terminating depth and contradicts Williams.
The open item is the constructor realizing that favorable ledger on every
input, not the recurrence algebra.

\section{No-borrow audit}

The literature does not fill the barrier by citation.  Dell--van Melkebeek
gives a conditional multi-query sparsification boundary, not an
equality-boundary constructor \cite{dellvanmelkebeek2014}.
Fortnow--Santhanam and Drucker give compression consequences under their
precise hypotheses, not a general SAT shrinker
\cite{fortnowsanthanam2011,drucker2015}.  Buss--Hay normalizes bounded
truth-table access but does not make queries power-smaller
\cite{busshay1991}.  IPZ sparsification is a fixed-width, parameterized
disjunction with a different quantitative envelope \cite{ipz2001}.
Recent CSP redundancy, streaming, and core theorems remain
representation-specific and do not supply the ordinary-CNF successor
\cite{brakensiekguruswami2026,sharmavelusamy2026,brakensiekputterman2026}.

\section{Hash, prime, and portfolio audit}

Shortening a hash from eight hexadecimal digits to six or four increases
opposite-answer collision risk and cannot support a general sound merge.
Lengthening the hash until histories are injectively represented can consume
the bits the compression sought to save.  Assigning unique primes changes
the labels, not this tradeoff: prime bit lengths and operations are charged,
and unique names do not prove semantic equivalence.

Parallel wrappers can improve empirical coverage but their deterministic
worst-case work is serialized.  A disjunctive return is exact only after all
fanout, acquisition, child-size, OR encoding, and combination costs are
charged.  A double wrapper helps only if its collapsed finite epoch reaches a
valid endpoint.  These are accounting rules, not reasons to abandon the
architectures.

\section{Defense disposition}

The defensible manuscript should make the following statements prominently:
\begin{description}
\item[] P versus NP is \Open{}.
\item[] The $1.8019\ldots$ universal product claim is \Retired{}; the general
product constant is $\phi$.
\item[] The direct $(1,2/3)$ endpoint is still \Exact{} forbidden, by
Fortnow.
\item[] Direct fixed-depth and typed fixed-depth compositions are \Exact{}.
\item[] Closed generator--shrinker equivalence is \Exact{} and removes
$2b\le h$.
\item[] The charged all-input successor or favorable typed epoch is \Open{}.
\item[] Experimental wrapper successes are \Comp{} unless promoted by a
source-checkable worst-case theorem.
\end{description}

This disposition maximizes the strength that survives hostile review while
removing conclusions that depended on a retracted endpoint or an unproved
acquisition step.
