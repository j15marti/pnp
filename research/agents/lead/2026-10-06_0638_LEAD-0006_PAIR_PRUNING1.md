# LEAD-0006 — Pair-pruning / collision-selector hostile test
Timestamp: 2026-10-06 06:38 PT

**GLOBAL STATUS: OPEN.** P versus NP remains open.

## Target
Test whether existing-style algebraic signatures, applied to pair objects F OR G, can provide the semantic equality/deletion primitive required by a Mahaney-style polynomial-frontier argument.

## Literature correction
Mahaney pruning does not follow from equality of arbitrary structural signatures. It uses a polynomial many-one reduction h, so h(X)=h(Y) guarantees X and Y have the same membership status. The relevant pair objects are disjunctions of residual formulas. Ogiwara-Watanabe extends sparse-hard-set collapse to bounded truth-table reductions, but the analogous sparse disjunctive-truth-table question is a longstanding open problem. Therefore a generic disjunctive sparse-image theorem would itself be unexpectedly strong; our target must exploit additional source structure.

## COMPUTATIONAL: selector-CNF pair test
Seed 20261006. Generate 180 random 3-CNFs with n=7, m=28. Determine truth exactly by brute force over 2^7 assignments.

Encode F OR G using fresh selector s:
- for each clause C of F, add (NOT s OR C);
- for each clause D of G, add (s OR D).
The resulting CNF is satisfiable iff F OR G.

Sample 3000 pair objects. Compute the surrogate signature:
1. clause count and clause-length multiset;
2. F2 rank of unsigned clause-variable incidence;
3. F2 rank of signed-literal incidence;
4. number of distinct signed clause-incidence points;
5. constant-aware degree-at-most-two Boolean evaluation rank on those points, analogous to AFFQUAD1.

Result: 11 signature classes; 6 classes contained both SAT and UNSAT pair-disjunctions. A representative mixed class had 52 distinct points and degree-two evaluation rank 52, yet contained both a pair whose two constituents were UNSAT and a pair whose two constituents were SAT.

**FALSIFIED/RETIRED at this surrogate scope:** equality of these coarse incidence/quadratic-rank pair signatures is not a SAT-semantic invariant and cannot instantiate the Mahaney collision rule.

This does not falsify an exact source-transcribed SU/vMS pair invariant, which was not available in this experiment.

## PROVED/EXACT easy-family control
For
F_k = AND_i [(x_i OR y_i) AND (NOT x_i OR z_i)],
branching on x_1,...,x_k leaves 2^k residuals AND_i w_i with w_i in {y_i,z_i}. These residuals are mutually incomparable under clause inclusion. Hence cheap syntactic implication deletion can fail exponentially even on 2-SAT.

## Architectural consequence
The pair-pruning route needs more than a sparse structural signature. It needs a source-derived membership-preserving reduction or a directly sound deletion certificate. A generic disjunctive reduction to a sparse image risks colliding with the longstanding sparse-dtt open problem.

## PAIR-REDUCTION-FORCING1 — CANDIDATE/OPEN
For every hypothetical polynomial-time exact SAT decider A, construct without current-scale execution of A a source-derived pair map h_A such that:
1. h_A is SAT-membership preserving on the relevant pair-disjunction objects, or yields a directly sound OR-deletion certificate;
2. its image/collision behavior on every restriction frontier is strong enough to prune to polynomial width through n-n^theta levels for fixed theta<1;
3. acquisition/comparison cost is independent of A's unknown polynomial exponent;
4. it is source-matched and wrapper-covariant;
5. intended oracle replacement fails for an explicit reason, and a separate nonalgebrization audit is supplied.
