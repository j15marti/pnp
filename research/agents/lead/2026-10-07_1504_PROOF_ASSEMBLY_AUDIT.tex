\paragraph{Finding.} Several obligations previously grouped under ``OPEN'' have already been addressed, conditionally proved, or shown unnecessary. The retrieved results support a substantially narrower conditional proof architecture, but do not currently compose into an unconditional separation. This is an audit of recovered evidence, not a proof that no missing historical argument exists. P versus NP remains OPEN.

\section{Scope and evidence quality}
Four targeted conversation searches covered wrapper constructions, normalization/provenance, exponent elimination, achieved-region closure, and the recent source-selector experiments. Retrieval returned summaries rather than complete raw transcripts; those summaries were used as discovery leads, not mathematical authority. Fresh reads covered repository main2--main5, CYCLEGAIN1, STREAMCOMP1, LEAD-0029, and six native project documents: NR-001, 27E, REV-0030, REV-0031, PROOF-SYNTHESIS-0015 and the optimal backdoor-cost recurrence. Three named Library JSON results were read in full. Earlier PRF3-0018, PRF2-0155 and ARC0013 reads remain relevant.

The earlier PROVED audit was used only as a locator. Its historical search covered 753 repository text files and 1,568 native Drive documents returned by that search; this run did not reread that corpus in full. Its own coverage excludes an exhaustive private-chat archive and unindexed sources. Fresh current manuscript reads are authoritative over the old index. Repository skill preflight was run on the available partial governance snapshot; it is not evidence of a full local checkout. Primary literature verification included Williams's self-improvement paper, whose Section 5 explicitly leaves extension to the P=NP regime open.

\section{What is already addressed}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Path exposure: PROVED/EXACT.} WRAPPED-SELFRED1 in main2/main3 supplies an answer-preserving restriction path using decision calls for every exact SAT decider. No inspection of its internal representation is needed. This should not remain an open requirement.
\item[] \textbf{Assignment-domain compression: PROVED/EXACT.} Fixing $x$ variables changes $2^n$ assignments to $2^{n-x}$. PRF2-0145 separately gives aggregate logarithmic compression. Neither identifies this count with required arbitrary-machine work.
\item[] \textbf{Exponent composition: CONDITIONAL.} RECURCLOSE1 and PRF3-0018 supply the recurrence machinery under their interface hypotheses. The starting solver exponent is not an independent unresolved algebra problem.
\item[] \textbf{Finite hitting: PROVED/EXACT meta-result.} REV-0030 already states $\forall A\,\exists j(A)<\infty$ suffices. Uniform affine contraction, an A-independent iteration count, and growing-depth execution are stronger sufficient devices, not mandatory requirements. Fixed code growth depending only on A and a fixed finite iterate is an asymptotic constant; input-dependent acquisition is not.
\item[] \textbf{Universal syntactic compilation: unnecessary.} ARC0013 permits language-level achievable-region implications. Successive resource pairs may have different witnesses, while each individual pair must have one joint witness.
\item[] \textbf{Same-scale edges: CONDITIONAL cycle solution.} CYCLEGAIN1 permits a scale-one typed edge in a well-founded recursive graph when every return cycle contracts. A direct unresolved call to the original decider remains distinct.
\item[] \textbf{Implicit child access: CONDITIONAL accounting.} STREAMCOMP1 charges bit generation and workspace explicitly. Logical child length alone does not force materialization space.
\item[] \textbf{Backdoor execution cost: PROVED/EXACT in its model.} The weighted AND/OR functional charges discovery, leaves and combination. The open claim concerns favorable general coverage, not whether discovery should be charged.
\item[] \textbf{Canonicalization and real quotients: established in scope.} NR-001 constructs canonical minimum SAT circuits in polynomial time under P=NP; 27E recovers a declared equality-copy source by union-find. Neither guarantees an endpoint-compatible general-input speedup.
\end{description}

\section{Attempted assemblies and the precise unmatched premises}
\paragraph{Path exposure plus recursive closure.}
The first result supplies correct restrictions after semantic decision calls. The second consumes cheaply generated children with fixed power shrinkage. The former does not certify the latter's acquisition bound. A conservative implementation ledger carries the original solver's exponent in early calls. This is an upper-bound certification failure, not a theorem that every actual residual requires worst-case time. An upper bound $T_A(N)\le N^K$ must never be silently converted into an $\Omega(N^K)$ lower bound for an individual call.

\paragraph{Canonical minimum circuit plus quotient.}
NR-001 proves existence of some polynomial construction exponent, not a fixed small exponent, a small workspace bound, or useful recursive structure. A minimum circuit can still have unknown polynomial size. 27E removes identifiable equality-copy expansion and returns the original source; it does not ensure arbitrary SAT instances possess such redundancy. Padding an $n$-bit source to $N=n^r$ and recovering an $N^{1/r}=n$-bit source restores the original problem. It creates no fractional reduction measured in the original $n$.

\paragraph{Charged backdoors plus portfolio.}
The charged functional gives a valid upper bound for each admitted route. A finite portfolio can exploit its fastest correct member, subject to scheduling and joint-space costs. The existence of an unrestricted polynomial SAT solver does not imply that one of the listed tractability representations is small and cheaply discoverable on every input. Enlarging the portfolio repairs specific examples, not the universal quantifier.

\paragraph{Cycle contraction plus streaming.}
This is a genuine improvement over the single-type spine. For edge $e:i\to j$, let $\eta_e$ be multiplicity exponent, $\theta_e$ logical size exponent, $g_e$ per-bit generation exponent, and $s_e$ generator workspace exponent. Feasible upper certificates satisfy
\[
C_i\ge\max\{r_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(\eta_e+g_e+\theta_e C_j)\},
\]
\[
\rho_i\ge\max\{\sigma_i,\max_{e:i\to j}(s_e,\theta_e\rho_j)\}.
\]
These are constraints on certified upper bounds, not machine lower bounds. Preprocessing, retained states, nested access and all unresolved original-solver calls must be included. The old results solve the bookkeeping once a semantically correct typed generator system exists. They do not instantiate that system for general SAT with favorable parameters.

\paragraph{Witness-preserving pruning plus ARC.}
PROOF-SYNTHESIS-0015 is a useful recovered mediator. Sound pruning preserving a satisfying extension, uniformly bounded frontier width, cheap certificate generation, final verification and bounded storage would yield an improved solver. If number of levels, width, certificate multiplicity and charged per-object cost have exponents $\eta,w,r,d$, respectively, the simplified time bound is $\eta+w+r+d$ only when branching, final checks and bookkeeping are absorbed. Otherwise their exponents must also be charged. To obtain ARC, the resulting joint resource pair must improve enough to reach the endpoint. Labeling a node by its SAT answer gives a tiny image but obtains that image by the very decision computation whose cost must be improved.

\paragraph{Parallel orbit search plus finite hitting.}
REV-0031 supplies a time-only dovetail theorem for an effective family of exact solvers. It removes the need to locate the good fixed iterate. It does not manufacture that iterate or automatically preserve its space bound. For the existential contradiction, one can simply select the good finite witness without running the whole race; this avoids imposing an unnecessary scheduler obligation on ARC.

\section{Recent experimental evidence}
The full Library files read were SOURCE\_SELECTOR\_WRAPPER, AFFINE\_SOURCE\_SELECTOR\_TEST, and EPOCH\_WRAPPER\_FULL\_RESTRICTION\_LATTICE. They record four source bits and aggregate counts of future-service equivalence classes. For example, the source-selector file records average class counts 14 for 3SAT and 5.4 for 2SAT; the affine file records 16 distinct affine maps but average future-service counts about 11.67 and 4.33. These are finite statistics. The JSON files do not contain a general acquisition bound, an asymptotic coverage theorem, or a complete proof of a source-forcing implication. Their omitted methods cannot be reconstructed from averages.

LEAD-0029 separately reports that removing generic DEAD/COPY/DELAY inflation retains a useful abstract $N^K$ skeleton, and lossy hash collisions do not certify semantic equivalence. Its no-go scope is generic local/signature quotienting, not every possible SAT-specific semantic transformation. A raw-state counterexample must not be promoted to a lower bound for SAT.

\section{Strongest surviving combined structure}
\[
\begin{aligned}
P=NP&\Rightarrow\text{finite achievable exact SAT resource pair}\\
&\Rightarrow\text{canonicalization/path interfaces in their proved scope}\\
&\xRightarrow{\text{OPEN: quantitative SAT-specific bridge}}
\text{favorable joint improvement}\\
&\xRightarrow{\text{conditional finite hitting/composition}}
\text{forbidden joint SAT pair}\\
&\Rightarrow\bot.
\end{aligned}
\]
Canonicalization and path exposure are available tools, not mandatory steps in every possible proof. The missing bridge could be an existential ARC implication, a concrete typed shrinker, or cheap sound bounded-width pruning with joint resource bounds. No combination audited here supplies that bridge without retaining its premise as an assumption. The direct source-cost route instead needs an independently forced consumer and unrestricted resource transfer; it does not become complete merely by counting assignment histories.

\section{Bounded model checks and next handoff}
The companion assembly\_audit\_test.py performs 1,149 checks in under one second. It exhausts 276 Boolean truth tables on one to three variables, verifying a decision-guided answer-preserving path; checks a two-type cycle with one same-scale edge; checks that a charged direct K-term persists in a conservative certificate; checks padding-scale transport; and checks strict boundary approach without finite crossing. Status: COMPUTATIONAL. No cheap general SAT transformation is inferred.

The team should label the solved items above as available components or conditional lemmas, not reopen them wholesale. The immediate concrete target is one actual SAT-preserving operation with a complete typed channel or pruning certificate and all acquisition/access/space exponents. Alternatively prove a direct existence-level improvement. Do not require a universal syntactic compiler, strict shrinkage on every edge, or growing-depth execution merely to obtain finite endpoint entry.
