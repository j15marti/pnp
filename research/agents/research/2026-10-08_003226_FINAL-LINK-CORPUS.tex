$q\le M^{1/3+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT children, child length at most
$M^{2/3+o(1)}$, and total construction work $M^{1+o(1)}$.  It does contain

\section{The exact target and where the old \texorpdfstring{$K$}{K}-elimination fits}

Let one invocation on an $M$-bit CNF perform $M^{c+o(1)}$ charged local work,
emit at most $M^{a+o(1)}$ recursively closed SAT children, and make every
child have length at most $M^{b+o(1)}$, where $b<1$.  The standard recurrence
has the fixed exponent
\[
 C=\max\left\{c,\frac{a}{1-b}\right\}.
\]
With streamed explicit children and local workspace exponent $s$, the
recovered sufficient peak-space bound is
\[
 \rho=\max\{s,b\}.
\]
The Williams landing condition is
\[
 C+\rho<\gamma,
 \qquad \gamma=2\cos(\pi/7)=1.8019377358\ldots.
\]
For the desired cell
\[
 (a,b,c,s)=\left(\frac13,\frac23,1,0\right),
 \qquad (C,\rho)=\left(1,\frac23\right),
 \qquad C+\rho=\frac53<\gamma.
\]

The remembered removal of the hypothetical solver exponent $K$ is genuine,
but downstream.  If the recursion is stopped after fixed depth $d$ and the
original $M^{K+o(1)}$ decider is used only at the leaves, the exponent has the
form
\[
 A_d+K b^d,
 \qquad
 A_d=a\frac{1-b^d}{1-b}
\]
up to the local-work maximum and uniform $o(1)$ terms.  Since $b<1$, a finite
$d$ can make $Kb^d$ smaller than any fixed landing margin.  Equivalently,
full recursive closure gives the fixed point above.  This removes $K$ only
\emph{after} a genuine $b<1$ interface has been constructed without first
paying a current-scale $M^K$ computation.  It is not a method for discovering
that interface.

\section{Strongest overlooked composition}

The useful overlooked composition is not a hidden compressor.  It is a normal
form that removes several auxiliary gaps and isolates one remaining theorem.

\begin{proposition}[FINAL-LINK CERTIFIED PORTFOLIO NORMAL FORM]
Assume $P=NP$ and fix one exact deterministic polynomial-time SAT decider
$A$.  Suppose a fixed finite family of uniform procedures
$W_{A,1},\ldots,W_{A,t}$, effectively obtained from the code of $A$, has the
following properties on every $M$-bit ordinary CNF.
\begin{description}
\item[Safe outcome.] Every member halts with FAIL or an exact forward plan;
$q=0$ constant plans are allowed.
\item[Coverage.] At least one member succeeds on every input.
\item[Charged attempts.] The total time of every attempted member, including
shared setup, search, certification, discarded work, generation, access,
correspondence, and combination, is $M^{1+o(1)}$; peak local live space is
$M^{o(1)}$ apart from one streamed child.
\item[Favorable success.] Every SUCCESS plan has
$q\le M^{1/3+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT children of size at most
$M^{2/3+o(1)}$, with a streamed representation and exact combiner.
\item[Closure.] Every unresolved child is in the same ordinary-SAT domain,
and replay/project/combination needs no parent-scale call to $A$ and no
unaccounted root reread.
\end{description}
Then SAT has a deterministic random-access realization with time
$M^{1+o(1)}$ and space $M^{2/3+o(1)}$.  Subject to the standard encoding and
model transport, this contradicts the Williams product lower bound.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
CERTIFIED-PARTIAL-PORTFOLIO1 turns sequential first-SUCCESS into one exact
inner interface; fixed finiteness preserves the maximum exponent.  The
successful member has $(a,b,c,s)=(1/3,2/3,1,0)$ up to $o(1)$.  Recursive
closure gives $C=\max\{1,(1/3)/(1/3)\}=1$ and
$\rho=\max\{0,2/3\}=2/3$.  Therefore $C+\rho=5/3<2\cos(\pi/7)$.
\end{proof}

This proposition makes four recovered mechanisms genuinely complementary:
zero-child members absorb certified easy regions; disjunctive/backdoor members
return exact OR covers; component members return exact AND decompositions;
and dominance/deduplication may prune a proposed frontier when their own
certificates fit the charged budget.  One root scan or shared $H$ may serve all
members if its construction and retained state are charged once.  No semantic
winner predictor is required.

The exponent pair has a favorable output-volume coincidence:
\[
 M^{1/3}\cdot M^{2/3}=M.
\]
Thus an explicit child family with constant source-occurrence multiplicity
could, in principle, be emitted in near-linear total volume.  The explicit
cover barrier $a+b\ge1$ does not reject this boundary point.  This is the best
constructive clue recovered.  It redirects effort from hash length and route
count to the exact compatibility theorem: how can the source be distributed
or summarized so that the resulting ordinary SAT children preserve the
parent answer without boundary explosion?

The proposition is not itself the missing proof.  The Coverage plus Charged
Attempts premises jointly contain the open link.  Moreover, if every member
were a solver-independent source-only rule, universal success would already
give the forbidden SAT algorithm unconditionally.  Therefore the
contradiction program needs at least one worst-case member whose existence is
derived non-black-boxly from the assumed code of $A$.  Using $A$ only as a
black-box oracle on smaller instances is blocked by BLACKBOX-MACRO-NOGO1, and
naive symbolic specialization recreates an $M^{K+o(1)}$ tableau by
PARTIAL-EVAL-K-CARRY1.

An alternative surviving composition is a single symbolic descendant that
represents the whole child family and remains closed through the recursive
consumer.  It could remove explicit fanout from the recurrence.  But then it
is no longer a family of ordinary independently recursed SAT instances; its
SAT semantics, representation size, update/project operations, and model
transport must be proved directly.  The corpus labels this route a candidate,
not a construction.

\section{Interpretation of the latest finite hybrid}

The local \texttt{final\_link\_hybrid\_builder.py} combines unit
propagation, tractable base solvers, bounded backdoor attempts, clause-first
disjunctive covers, components, equality deduplication, and
CLAUSE-DOM-SELECT1.  It is a valuable executable instance of the normal form.
The preserved JSON reports zero semantic errors and target $q$/child success
on all 27 heterogeneous controls.  It does not put construction work inside
the success predicate.  The largest displayed case uses 3,518,492 work-proxy
operations for $M=2,282$, and retained logical workspace includes full formula
objects.  Consequently it currently demonstrates semantic composability and
finite coverage only.  The next experimental revision should make SUCCESS
conditional on a hard per-attempt work cap and a streamed-space cap, then
search for small formulas on which every capped member safely returns FAIL.
That would test the exact theorem premise rather than only the leaf budgets.

\section{Literature calibration}

Williams' self-improvement paper explicitly leaves open whether its method can
be pushed to the $P=NP$ regime; it does not supply the compiler recovered here
as missing.  IPZ sparsification gives $2^{\varepsilon n}$ sparse fixed-width
CNFs.  Making this fanout polynomial by taking
$\varepsilon=O(\log M/n)$ does not yield the desired fixed-power encoded
shrink, and the sparsity constant deteriorates as $\varepsilon\to0$.

Mahaney-style sparse-set pruning explains why an exact selector can remove one
of two branches when a reduction maps them to the same sparse target.  The
project's clause-dominance rule is a valid local analogue.  The literature
does not give a sparse reduction from an arbitrary assumed SAT algorithm with
the required acquisition cost.  Fortnow--Santhanam and Drucker show that
strong instance compression is consequential, but their standard collapse
consequences are absorbed under $P=NP$.  Backdoor-DNF theorems provide exact
restricted covers and parameterized detection for specified base classes;
they do not assert that every CNF has a small cheaply found cover.  SAT
preprocessing supplies exact syntactic reductions and practical reuse, not a
universal $M^{1/3}$-by-$M^{2/3}$ theorem.

\section{Final conclusion}

The prior work has solved more of the proof spine than a generic OPEN label
suggests.  Exact plan semantics, recursion, $K$-decay after contraction,
portfolio scheduling, zero-child answers, local dominance, route lineage,
and charged consumer interfaces are all available.  The recovered records
also explicitly explain why the most tempting claims do not close the gap:
the SourceLocalization half-scale cell is only pointwise; shared $H$ is not
cheap merely because it is compact; clause dominance can have zero density;
and semantic compression can be acquired only after paying the unknown solver
cost.

The strongest source-checkable statement still missing is therefore:
\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.91\textwidth}{\centering
From the code of one assumed polynomial SAT decider, construct a fixed finite
certified partial portfolio that covers every CNF, with total attempt work
$M^{1+o(1)}$, at most $M^{1/3+o(1)}$ children, child size at most
$M^{2/3+o(1)}$, and streamed same-machine space.}}
\end{center}
No searched conversation artifact, GitHub file, Library record, Drive record,
program output, or primary literature theorem proves this statement.  The
present proof spine is therefore sharply conditional but not complete.
