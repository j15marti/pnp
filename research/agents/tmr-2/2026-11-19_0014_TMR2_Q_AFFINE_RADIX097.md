# TMR-2/097 | Affine palindromic global Q compatibility and counting extraction

## Exact model and proof status

Alphabet \(\mathbb F_5=\{0,1,2,3,4\}\); \(R(a,b)\) iff \(b-a\bmod5\in\{1,3\}\); \(Q(h,u,v)=R(h,u)\mathbin{\oplus}R(h,v)\). Distinct clause variable names may receive identical letters. All findings below called PROVED are elementary derivations; numerical results are COMPUTATIONAL. The existing /096 five-class forbidden-pattern basis, parity-cycle syndrome and unbounded-girth critical existence are retained without claiming rediscovery.

**Theorem 1, exact private-head factor.** For a graph \(G=(V,E)\), let every edge \(uv\) have its own distinct private head \(h_{uv}\), with clause \(Q(h_{uv},u,v)\). For any endpoint letters \(a,b\), its head-extension multiplicity is \(w(a,b)=0\) when \(a=b\), \(4\) when \(a-b\equiv\pm1\bmod5\), and \(2\) when \(a-b\equiv\pm2\bmod5\). This follows by enumerating the five possible head values with equality counted as a nonwin. For each proper 5-coloring \(f\) of \(G\), write \(s(f)\) for the number of edges of difference \(\pm1\), and \(W_G(X)=\sum_{f\ {\rm proper}}X^{s(f)}=\sum_{s=0}^{m}c_sX^s\). Then **\(Z_Q(G)=2^mW_G(2)\)** and the decision problem is *exactly* graph 5-colorability. A source-aware solver can eliminate all private heads, recognize graph structure and reconstruct heads from a coloring. The reduction is not count-parsimonious.

**Theorem 2, affine orbit signature.** For every graph, **\(W_G(X)=X^mW_G(X^{-1})\)**, i.e. \(c_s=c_{m-s}\). The affine group \(a\mapsto ua+b\), with \(u\in\mathbb F_5^\times\), \(b\in\mathbb F_5\), acts on proper colorings. Multipliers \(1,4\) preserve the edge-difference classes \(\pm1,\pm2\); multipliers \(2,3\) exchange them. If \(m>0\), every proper coloring contains two distinct values, and its 20-element affine orbit is free. Hence each \(c_s\) is a multiple of 10 and, for even \(m\), the central coefficient \(c_{m/2}\) is a multiple of 20. Empty-edge graphs are excluded from the divisibility assertion, since they have \(c_0=5^{|V|}\). These conditions give inexpensive exact cofactor sanity checks, not a hardness measure.

**Theorem 3, ONE-QUERY metric counting reduction.** Let \(v=|V|\), \(m=|E|\), set \(t=3v+1\), and for each edge \(uv\) introduce \(t\) fresh independent Q-head variables (same followers). Then \(B=2^t>5^v\) and the resulting polynomial-size instance \(F(G,t)\) has \(v+tm\) variables and \(tm\) clauses. Its exact #Q answer is
\[
Z_Q(F(G,t))=2^{tm}\sum_{s=0}^{m}c_s B^s.
\]
Since \(0\le c_s\le5^v<B\), the \(m+1\) base-\(B\) digits of \(Z_Q/2^{tm}\) are exactly \(c_0,\ldots,c_m\). Summing those digits returns the number of proper 5-colorings of \(G\) using a **single counting-oracle query**, polynomial construction and polynomial-bit-length postprocessing. As fixed-5-coloring counting is #P-complete, #Q is #P-hard under polynomial-time metric/counting reductions and is in #P. This is NOT a parsimonious reduction and does NOT yield an unrestricted time-space lower bound. An ordinary SAT decision seed does not automatically compute counts. Bit-level input/output, big integer shifts/divisions, repeated heads and read-only versus writable storage must be fully charged.

**Theorem 4, restricted critical-edge necessity.** If \(G\) is edge-critical non-5-colorable, every edge \(e\) has a 5-coloring \(f_e\) of \(G-e\), with **exactly that edge** monochromatic in \(G\). Thus every fixed nonadaptive selection of original edge-violation probes that purports to detect a violation for EVERY candidate coloring must contain all \(m\) edges. This applies only to the named probe interface: direct source inspection, coloring computation, chromatic certificates, recognizing K6/Mycielski, and all other algorithms remain legal. The high-girth existence theorem previously recorded in /096 demonstrates unbounded local obstruction size, but is not a uniformly polynomial-time hard source family.

## Correct-path, RAM and open problem safeguards

A guessed edge violation is a valid counterexample to one *proposed* coloring, not proof that every coloring violates some edge. SAT accepts with a full verified coloring and legal head assignments. UNSAT requires actual exhaustive coverage or a sound global proof; no free polarity or k-bit favorable path can replace universal quantifiers. Both polarities must account for all child calls, wrong branches, replay, preprocessing, verification, read-only certificate and peak writable live space. With \(N^{.195}\) rows already enumerating shared \(z\), \(N^{.65}\) blocks per row, size \(N^{.35}\) per child, deterministic child exponent \(s\) gives conditional composition exponent \(.845+.35s\). At \(s=1.33\), it is \(1.3105\); an unspecified polynomial-time child does not suffice. Existing random-access SAT alternation-trading restrictions cover subpolynomial or limited positive space, not the proposed full simultaneous \(N^{1.33+o(1)}\)-time and \(N^{.5}\operatorname{polylog}N\)-space target.
