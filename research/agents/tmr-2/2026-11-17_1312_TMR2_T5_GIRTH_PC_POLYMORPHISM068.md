## Exact T5 walk calculation

Identify Sigma with Z/5Z. A forward arc contributes an increment in {1,3}=1+{0,2}; traversal against an arc contributes {-1,-3}={2,4}=2+{0,2}. For an oriented source walk W of length ell with f forward steps and b backward steps (f+b=ell), let
    d(W)=f+2b (mod 5).
Its T5 endpoint relation is exactly
    P_W(x,y)=1 iff y-x in d(W)+2*{0,1,...,ell} modulo 5.
Proof: at each of ell edges choose independently either the base increment or base+2. Every j in 0..ell is attained, and these exhaust all possible endpoint differences. Consequently every signed length-ell path has 5*min(ell+1,5) ordered endpoint pairs when ell<=4 and **all 25 pairs for ell>=4**. This is independent of edge orientation. A step immediately followed by its reverse has a path relation containing the diagonal, since each color has at least one in/out neighbor. All path relations are translation-invariant, left-total and right-total.

## Theorem A: exact all-pairs path-consistency closure on girth >= 7

For EVERY finite simple undirected H with girth >=7, and for EVERY orientation G of its edges, set D_ii=IDENTITY. For distinct i,j with undirected distance t<=3, take the T5 endpoint relation of the UNIQUE simple shortest i-to-j path in H; for t>=4 or disconnected pairs set D_ij=Sigma^2. Then:

(i) Every D_ij is nonempty, contains only original allowed tuples on an actual arc, and is reverse-consistent D_ji=D_ij^T.
(ii) For ALL i,j,k, D_ij subseteq D_ik composed with D_kj. Thus D is a fully path-consistent subnetwork of the original CSP's unrestricted nonedge and R/reverse edge relations.
(iii) The standard monotone complete all-pairs path-consistency closure of the initial relations equals D, exactly. Hence each unary label remains possible, each adjacent ordered pair retains all 10 R tuples, distance-two ordered pairs retain 15 tuples, distance-three ordered pairs retain 20, and distances >=4 retain all 25; diagonal pairs retain five.

Proof: a distinct pair of simple paths of lengths at most three between the same endpoints creates a closed cycle of length at most six, excluded by girth. For consistency, if one of D_ik,D_kj is full, its composition with the other (which is left- and right-total) is full. If both are path relations of lengths a,b<=3 and a+b>=4, their composition is full by the walk formula. If a+b<=3, concatenate the two paths. Any immediate return along the same edge may be erased while retaining a subset of the composed endpoint relation, because the two-step return contains the identity. After erasure, the walk is a simple path of length <=3, which must be the unique short shortest path i-to-j; thus D_ij is contained in the original composition. This establishes (ii). The initial network contains D; all path-consistency deletions preserve any path-consistent subnetwork, giving D subseteq closure. Conversely repeated path compositions along each source shortest path of length <=3 remove all tuples outside D, giving closure subseteq D. This proves exact equality, not only non-emptiness. No source homomorphism is assumed to construct D.

## Theorem B: infinite path-consistent UNSAT family, without an NP assumption

For every h>=7 there exists a finite simple undirected H_h with girth >=h and chromatic number >=h (Erdős 1959). Let H_h be the first such graph in a specified length/lexicographic enumeration of finite graph encodings, and orient every edge from smaller to larger index, obtaining authentic G_h. The graph properties are decidable by finite exhaustive testing, so this is a **total computable** family; the exhaustive generator is NOT certified polynomial time or size-efficient.

Every homomorphism of G_h into the loopless five-vertex T5 template would assign unequal symbols to undirected adjacent vertices, giving an ordinary proper five-coloring of H_h. As chi(H_h)>=h>=7>5, this is impossible. Hence #Hom(G_h,T5)=0. By Theorem A the full path-consistency closure nonetheless contains 10,15,20 or 25 endpoint tuples at all distinct pairs, and all five singleton colors survive. The underlying treewidth satisfies tw(H_h)>=chi(H_h)-1>=h-1, yet the FULL original-assignment indicator tensor is identically zero (every full flattening rank zero). Thus large treewidth, local constraint density, and empty global tensor can coexist with a PC-consistent local certificate. **No lower bound on source-aware SAT, counting, OBDD, tensor, or general-circuit work follows.**

For clarity, the elementary random-graph proof supplies the existence premise independently: choose fixed h, n arbitrarily large and p=n^(-1+1/(2h)). Expected cycles of length <h are O_h(n^((h-1)/(2h)))=o(n). With probability tending to one, no independent set of n/(2h) vertices exists, by binomial-union bound 2^n exp(-Omega(n^(1+1/(2h)))). Delete one vertex from each of fewer than n/4 short cycles; the remaining >3n/4 vertices have independence number <n/(2h)+1, so chromatic number exceeds h for sufficiently large n. Iterating h yields unbounded distinct sizes. Selection/generation and source-validation costs are not donated to the CSP algorithm.

## Secondary finite polymorphism result

Exhaustive AC+branch enumeration of idempotent binary polymorphisms f:T5^2->T5 yields **exactly the two coordinate projections**. The 25 source points (a,b) have 100 product arcs. Enforce diagonal f(a,a)=a, and revise every product arc until arc-consistent. The initial 20 undetermined locations collapse to domains exactly {a,b} for a!=b (60 supported-value deletions beyond the diagonal assignments). Branching only on f(0,1) in {0,1}, one more propagation sweep forces all 24 other cells to the first or second projection, respectively. Each candidate is checked against all 100 actual arcs. The procedure is complete because removing a value without supporting value on a product arc cannot delete a homomorphism. Both projections preserve R. This finite certificate rules out nontrivial **binary idempotent** polymorphisms; it does not classify ternary/higher polymorphisms or by itself establish the CSP dichotomy.
