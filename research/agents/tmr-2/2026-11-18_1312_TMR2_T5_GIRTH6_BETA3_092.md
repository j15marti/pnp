# TMR-2/092: girth-six cycle-rank-three satisfiability theorem

**Date:** 2026-11-18 (America/Los_Angeles). **Owner:** TMR-2, PNP Information Necessity. **Template:** five-symbol regular tournament with R(a,b) iff (b-a) mod 5 in {1,3}. **Status:** analytic finite-reduction proof with two independent finite enumerators, not a general complexity lower bound. ## Theorem 092-A

For every finite simple oriented graph G without loops or digons, if the undirected girth is at least six and the total cyclomatic number beta(G)=|E|-|V|+c(G) is at most three, then G admits a homomorphism into T5.

The preceding /091 theorem covered girth at least five and beta at most two and constructed a girth-five beta-three UNSAT counterexample. The present result proves that this obstruction cannot be moved to girth six at the same cycle rank. Sharpness at beta four for girth six is **not claimed**.

## Finite-core reduction and reproducible certificate

Prune all degree-zero/one vertices, which are freely extendible since every label has two incoming and two outgoing neighbors. In each connected component with beta=3, suppress every maximal degree-two chain, recording its exact transfer between remaining branch vertices. The resulting connected multigraph H permits loops and parallel edges, has minimum degree at least three, and satisfies |E(H)|=|V(H)|+2. Its degree sum gives |V(H)|<=4. Exhaustive enumeration up to vertex permutations yields exactly **15** unlabeled cores, including loop, bridge, and articulation cases. Components with beta<=2 are handled by /091.

For a path of length l with r reversed source arcs, its transfer is A^l S^r, A=S+S^3. Exact existential support depends only on (l,r), irrespective of the order of the reversals because A and A^T commute. There are exactly 2+3+4=9 short states for l=1,2,3. Every l>=4 has full support on all 25 endpoint pairs, so collapse it to a single unrestricted state represented by artificial length six for conservative girth screening. This wildcard **represents all** actual l>=4 transfer supports; its chosen screening length is not a claim about source arc count.

For each multigraph core, each assignment of these ten path states must satisfy the necessary girth-six condition on every elementary cycle composed of short paths. Using artificial length six on wildcard paths only admits additional cases, so the tested set is a superset of genuine girth-six sources. Pin branch label 0 to zero by global cyclic symmetry, then construct a bitmask of all 5^(v-1)<=125 branch-label assignments. For each path, intersect the bitmask with its exact transfer support. **No admissible completed intersection is empty**. The assignment to branch labels extends independently through every suppressed path, and through pruned trees, proving the theorem.

This is a proof by exhaustive **fixed-size** finite combinatorial enumeration after an analytic reduction; it does not derive superpolynomial asymptotic behavior. Reproducible standard-library Python and separately compiled C++ implementations agreed on all totals.
