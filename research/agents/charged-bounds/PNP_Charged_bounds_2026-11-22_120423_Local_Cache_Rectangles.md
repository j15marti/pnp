**Model and quantifiers.** Fix one deterministic total Boolean decider A before selecting inputs, with a sole writable finite-alphabet tape, unit-speed read-before-write head, fixed origin and terminated output. Fix d≥3 and a public partition of its d permutation tags into r nonempty consecutive blocks. Each block word x_i ranges independently over S3 to its block length. Denote its ordered product by g_i. The direct canonical source F(x) from Accounting is SAT precisely when g_1 ... g_r=e. It has n=144d, m=108d and N=n+2+2 floor(log2(m+1))+3m ceil(log2(n+1))=Θ(d log d). These are Boolean exact-one sources. No strict symmetric T5 count or ordered R assertion is made.

Assume a reachable checkpoint C(x) for every word, including both answer classes. Its head, control, output cells and all public cells are fixed for this d. The other cells lie in fixed finite disjoint regions Z_1,...,Z_r. The entire contents of Z_i equal a fixed computable function f_i(x_i), including every retained source copy, summary, padding, address and metadata there. Layout and region capacities are public and input independent. All regions outside this finite union have fixed contents. The functions may be redundant and computationally expensive. The checkpoint times can differ, but the model has no external clock. Every suffix from C(x) must give the correct answer for F(x).

This is an explicit representation hypothesis. Sole-tape semantics alone do not imply factorization. A particular all-input-correct machine realizes it by authenticating the canonical family, computing each block product, erasing other sample-dependent data, placing those products in prescribed cells and resetting its head and control. Each action is paid. Correct exhaustive fallback handles valid nonimages after internal UNKNOWN.

**The actual record.** Let E(x) be the set of suffix transitions that first read a cell in Z=union_i Z_i. Record that cell's initial symbol, in a fixed b=ceil(log2|Gamma|)-bit alphabet code. Later reads of it are omitted. Let Pi be these payloads in actual order. B gives A, d, the partition, region positions, f_i, public initial cells and fixed control/head/output. B excludes sampled values f_i(x_i) and x_i. Framing h=0 and reuse rho=1 for this single obligation.

The decoder simulates the suffix. It knows every public cell. On the first read of a region cell it requests one code, then tracks all subsequent writes and reads of that cell. It determines all event addresses and its stopping time from the same available information. Read-before-write ensures that overwriting an unknown initial symbol first records it. The complete records are prefix-free at fixed B, since a deterministic replay halting on a shorter record cannot later request another symbol. There is no hidden selection-time channel.

**Rectangle lemma.** For a completed record pi, let X_i(pi) be the block words whose encoding agrees with every recorded initial cell value in region i. The set of tuples yielding pi is exactly the Cartesian product of these X_i. One inclusion follows from recorded reads. For the reverse inclusion replay the deterministic transitions on any combination of compatible blocks. Every first read agrees by construction, every previously observed or written cell agrees by induction, and the same halt occurs. Adaptive movement and writes do not invalidate this argument.

If pi accepts, fix compatible words in every region. If two compatible choices in region i had distinct products g and g', the two global products LgR and Lg'R would be distinct by cancellation. They cannot both equal e. Both combinations would follow the accepting record, contradicting correctness on the full family. Thus each nonempty X_i(pi) has exactly one product. The decoder enumerates the finite block domains and computes those unique products. Enumeration is an information decoder, with no claim of efficient inversion or free work by the solver.

Define U(x)=(g_1,...,g_r), independently of the execution. For any distribution supported on accepting words, Pi and B decode U exactly. The distributional restriction does not restrict the solver's correctness promise. The proof explicitly uses valid rejecting hybrids outside that distribution. Without those hybrids the conclusion fails for an always-SAT machine.

Choose the first r−1 products uniformly and choose the last to make the total identity. Realize each block by its product in its first position and identities elsewhere. Then

    H(U | B) = (r−1) log2 6,
    E[T_suffix] ≥ E[|E|] ≥ (r−1) log2 6 / b.

For completeness, the prefix-code bound follows from Kraft's inequality for the completed binary records and nonnegativity of relative entropy, giving H(Pi|B)≤E|Pi|. Since U is decoded, H(U|B)≤H(Pi|B). Here |Pi|=b|E|. This is exactly the shared ledger with one j, h=0 and rho=1. For multiple proved phase records, the sum formula additionally needs their actual event overlap bounded by rho. A new region partition on paper supplies no new execution phase.

**Four falsifiers and the resulting boundary.**

1. Add a single globally computed bit D=[g_1...g_r=e] near the origin. Read it and halt. All accepting tuples can have one record even though their product vectors differ. The initial bit depends jointly on the blocks, so factorization fails.
2. Give B the actual sampled local caches or full configuration. The stated entropy can disappear. The theorem gives B the encoding functions and public geometry only.
3. Supply correctness solely on the accepting promise. Constant acceptance defeats the hybrid proof.
4. Apply the decoder to rejecting records. Even for two raw three-bit product tags, reading the first tag and comparing the second tag with its inverse can stop at the first differing bit. Some rejecting records leave four possible second tags. Unique block-product decoding is then false.

The r=1 bound is zero, as expected after aggregating the entire word into one block. Product summaries require three physical bits per block and are sufficient for decision. Moreover each f_i must itself determine g_i under the all-word contract. If it identified two different products, choose the other blocks to complete one to identity and obtain two identical complete checkpoints with opposite answers. Therefore the ordered block products are the coarsest possible independent local summaries, up to encoding, for r≥2. Computing them is a constructive way to meet the restricted interface, and computing their joint product then leaves this interface entirely.
