## Independent lower-demand invariant

In a positive count 10·2^(E+d), the one-bits occur exactly at positions E+d+3 and E+d+1. Therefore output bit E+1 equals one precisely when d=0 and the masks are disjoint. Equivalently
    output_(E+1) = (AND_i B_i) AND (AND_i NOT A_i)
on X_k. It depends essentially on all 2k mask coordinates: at A=0,B=all ones, flipping any one coordinate changes the bit.

Take its ancestor cone in any correct circuit for F_(N_k), or for the promise map. A cone with g noninput gates of fan-in at most two can involve at most g+1 distinct input nodes. This follows by counting edges in the connected underlying ancestor graph: if it has l input vertices and g internal vertices, connectivity requires at least l+g−1 edges while fan-in bounds them by 2g. Sharing can only reduce the number of newly reached leaves. By literal locality each such input node supplies at most one mask coordinate after restricting to X_k. Essential dependence on 2k coordinates forces g≥2k−1. This argument includes unary gates and constants and permits arbitrary shared circuits. It gives
    C_(N_k) ≥ 2k−1 = Ω(N_k/log N_k)
for the all-valid-input map. It does not assume that identifying an encoded bit carries a free oracle answer.

## The explicit upper circuit

Recover the 2k coordinates from their chosen wires. Compute q=NOT OR_i(A_i AND B_i), with at most 2k+1 gates including a simple accumulator. Accumulate d=Σ_i(1−B_i) into ℓ binary bits. Each increment has a one-bit carry; each position computes old XOR carry and old AND carry. XOR uses four AND/OR/NOT gates, so there are at most 5kℓ gates plus k input negations. Since d≤k<2^ℓ, final overflow is harmless and always zero.

Decode the binary number into predicates e_j=[d=j]. A shared complete prefix tree, reading one of the ℓ digits or its negation per level, uses at most 2^(ℓ+1)−2 AND gates and ℓ NOT gates, hence at most 4(k+1) AND gates. Its root is constant one. Retain leaves j=0,...,k. For output position E+1+r, with 0≤r≤k+2, emit q AND (e_r OR e_(r−2)), omitting invalid indices; all other output wires are zero. These are the two one-bits of 10 shifted by E+d. They are combined by OR because a fixed d cannot select both alternatives at that position. At most 2(k+3) gates suffice. The decision output is simply q.

Adding the two charged constant nodes, the extraction, intersection, popcount, decoder and output gates is bounded by U(k). The circuit depends on length and the public generator, never on the particular masks of the evaluated input. This establishes the claimed uniform promise circuit. It is a constructive proof of circuit existence with paid construction, not an assumption that a short answer specifies its own computation.
