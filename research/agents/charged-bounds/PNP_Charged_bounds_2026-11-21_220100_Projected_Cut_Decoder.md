**DYNAMIC-PROJECTED-CUT-DECODER1. PROVED/EXACT** restricted representation theorem, constructive characterization and offline decoder counterexample. Independent adversarial review pending. Finite checks are **COMPUTATIONAL**. P versus NP remains OPEN.

The concrete target is Construction's shared-selector Boolean family at 9d5005e4b727f0e3483a8b6e98b99d57ce4f3383, following Discovery's cut algorithm at c5018c0ba81f31c58ea5b9c9ec3d00877ebe1ada. Discovery proved a polynomial solver for energies admitting a coordinate switch to submodularity. ## Exact target and falsifiers

For every nonempty relation R on d Boolean source coordinates, consider any finite directed s-t graph with nonnegative finite capacities, any number of auxiliary vertices, and d distinguished vertices. A fixed coordinate permutation/complementation identifies those vertices with the source variables. Require R to be exactly the projection of all globally minimum cuts onto those coordinates. An equivalent requirement is that every partial assignment has an extension in R exactly when clamping the corresponding distinguished vertices leaves the original global optimum achievable. Fully assigned queries already force equality of the projected relation. Arbitrary objective values away from the optimum are permitted. There is no bound on graph size in this negative statement.

For the actual-execution counterexample first fix the uniform globally correct solver A described below, then q≥1 and the distribution on the nine same-length inputs. A uses the transport model, one writable tape with a fixed finite alphabet and one head, at most one-cell movement, source/work/address/caches charged, terminated output at the left origin, no separate original source or free clock. Its checkpoint is a designated canonical READY state after paid recognition, decision and cleanup. This is a reachable state on every input in the finite support. A general lower-bound claim may instead fix any correct solver first; it must in particular survive this A. Any proposed cap uses fixed K>0 and a≥1 in T≤K N^a.

Four falsifiers guide the claim: a projection of actual minimizers may fail lattice closure; some switch may make a three-literal clause's seven models closed; an uncharged nonlinear interpretation may replace the specified coordinate projection; a decoder may obtain the erased source through side information or unrecorded input-dependent event selection. The first two are ruled out by the proof. The last two are outside the restricted claim and must be charged and justified separately.

## Characterization and proof

Subsets encode source sides of cuts, including s and excluding t. Each arc's cut indicator is submodular, so the nonnegative weighted sum C obeys C(S)+C(T)≥C(S∩T)+C(S∪T). If S,T have common minimum lambda, each term on the right is at least lambda. Both are therefore minimum cuts. Coordinate projection commutes with meet and join. Projected minima are a nonempty sublattice of the Boolean cube. Equivalently, if a submodular energy H(x,z) has minimizing extensions for x and y, take the meet and join of the full minimizing assignments and project. Arbitrarily many auxiliary vertices cannot remove this closure requirement. This elementary argument agrees with the residual-relation description of minimum cuts in [3] and the graph-representability condition in [4].

Conversely, every nonempty finite meet/join-closed R is definable by unary pins and implications x_i→x_j. Let a be the meet of all models and b their join. Pin coordinates in a to one and coordinates outside b to zero. For each i occurring as one in any model, let P_i be the meet of all models containing i. Add i→j for j in P_i. Every original model satisfies the constraints. If z satisfies them, each P_i for i in z is contained in z. The join of a and these P_i is in R and equals z, proving sufficiency. Unit-capacity arcs for implications and terminal arcs for the pins have zero cost exactly on R. This also gives a graph with O(d²) arcs once the implications are known. Empty relations can instead be represented as having no feasible zero-cost cut by an always-positive cost, but they cannot be the projection of a nonempty set of global optima.

This is an existence characterization. Constructing the P_i from an explicit list of models is polynomial in that list's length. Obtaining them from an arbitrary compact CNF can require answering SAT queries. No such oracle is free. A supplied implication-and-pin instance is uniformly solvable by reachability: propagate forced ones, reject if a forced zero is reached, otherwise set all remaining bits to zero. Partial assignments add pins. Reading, graph construction and each requested query are polynomial in the supplied list length. No enumeration of the model set is needed in that declared representation.

For one proper three-variable clause the models are all eight cube vertices except a single forbidden vertex v. Any coordinate switch or permutation simply changes v. Among its three coordinates, at least two are zero or at least two are one. If at least two are zero, change each of those coordinates separately to one, obtaining two allowed vertices whose meet is v. If at least two are one, change them separately to zero, obtaining two allowed vertices whose join is v. Thus no switch works. For example x OR y OR z permits 100 and 010 but excludes their meet 000. This defeats even a different objective preserving only the optimum relation, strengthening Discovery's narrower obstruction for the exact violation energy.

The restriction matters. A graph producing only a SAT/UNSAT scalar, or a graph with an arbitrary polynomial-time nonlinear decoder for its cuts, is not covered by this theorem. A general SAT-to-mincut decision reduction with polynomial construction would imply P=NP; this report neither supplies nor rules out one. Even our single-clause obstruction has a trivial polynomial SAT algorithm. A relation handle supporting all future clamped queries is a stronger service than the requested original decision.

## Same-length reachable counterexample to compulsory residual service

Let C_v be the proper clause on x1,x2,x3 falsified exactly by v. For q≥1 set c=8q,d=3. Define F_v to contain c copies of C_v, for each of the eight v. Define G to contain q copies of each C_v in a fixed public order. Each F_v is SAT with seven source models. G is UNSAT. Encode each through Construction's ten-instruction dihedral words and the adjacent-witness compiler. Both labels are actual valid compatibility inputs.

Set r to the smallest odd integer greater than c, L=10c. The exact source and target dimensions are

    n0 = 3+(10c+1)r+2,       m0 = (20c+4)r,
    n = n0+m0+3,            m = 2m0+4,
    w = ceil(log2(n+1)),
    N = 3n+2+2floor(log2(m+1))+3m(w+1)+w.

The tape has unary n and delimiter, gamma(m+1), ordered sign-plus-ID target triples, two n-bit local witnesses and a w-bit release ID. All nine inputs have identical N and metadata dimensions for fixed q. The coefficient-function or target extension count is not being counted as a Boolean solution set. Construction proves existential projection onto the common selector assignment. Symmetric strict C, ordered R and historical S/Q play no role.

A fixed solver first parses the entire target, discovers the canonical parameters, extracts the source triples from instruction positions 0,1,4, regenerates and compares every target bit as in [1,2]. It admits this nine-family only after checking d=3, c divisible by eight, and either constant repeated triples or the prescribed complete block repetitions. Otherwise it uses exhaustive SAT on the retained original target. Thus failed admission is UNKNOWN, not a negative decision. On admission either direct pattern recognition or checking the eight source assignments correctly decides both labels. It stores the answer in finite control, erases input and scratch with delimiter-controlled sweeps and returns to READY at the origin with just that bit and a fixed marker. This is implementable with a fixed expanded alphabet and no live timestamp. It then emits the required answer and terminator. All SAT inputs have the same actual READY configuration and suffix. The UNSAT input has the other canonical configuration.

Give G mass one-half and each F_v mass one-sixteenth. Let U be the truth vector of all eight full source assignments, a fixed-order list of SAT answers to these eight residual queries. Let B contain q, the public generator, the solver and its cached original decision Y. Given Y=SAT, U is uniform on eight distinct vectors with exactly one zero. Given Y=UNSAT, U is the all-zero vector. Therefore H(U|B)=3/2 bits. Even a single fixed full-assignment query has positive conditional entropy: at assignment 000 it is (1/2)h_binary(1/8).

Any Pi obtained solely by observing the READY suffix, with event selection determined by B and the observed trace, is a function of B. Its information about U is zero. There is no actual decoder D(B,Pi)=U. For the concrete empty suffix observation E=empty, Pi=empty, h=0, b=1 and rho=1 by convention, conditional prefix freedom holds but the demanded decoder fails. Allowing all actual suffix events does not help. Sending an event list chosen using v would transmit missing side information and must be explicitly charged.

The corrected general code inequality is

    H(U|B) − H(U|B,Pi) ≤ H(Pi|B) ≤ E|Pi|.

For a conditionally prefix-free record with |Pi|≤b|E|+h, summing over obligations and counting actual overlap gives

    E T ≥ [sum_j I(U_j;Pi_j|B_j) − sum_j E h_j]/(b rho).

Replacing mutual information by H(U_j|B_j) is justified only by exact decoding. In this counterexample the numerator's information contribution is zero. If B freely includes the retained original source, the entropy can already vanish before reading any record. Neither choice establishes compulsory work. Past recognition and first reads remain real paid work; their repetition is not inferred.

For any fixed polynomial cap the general ceilings remain S=O(N^a), physical CM=O(N^(2a)), descriptive CM=O(N^(a+1)). The count-map O(N^a log N) simulation applies only to counting. Here the failed post-cache demand contributes zero, so it excludes no fixed exponent. Construction size and the different original-input length must be charged before a reduction-based comparison.
