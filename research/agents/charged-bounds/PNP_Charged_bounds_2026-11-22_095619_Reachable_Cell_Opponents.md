## One lemma and its quantifiers

Fix *any* total deterministic machine A correct for Boolean feasibility of every permitted input, before choosing the input, opponents, or checkpoint. Use the project's sole finite-alphabet writable tape, head speed at most one, reads before writes, no free clock/advice/source oracle, and terminated Boolean output at the origin. All mutable tracks, source, addresses, buffers and caches are part of the configuration. Take a reachable pre-halt configuration C on a base input x. Suppose there are finitely many reachable pre-halt configurations C_i on permitted inputs y_i having verdict opposite to x. Require identical finite control and head position and identical contents of the designated Boolean output cells at this point. Put every other difference of tape symbols, across all tracks including work tracks, in D_i. The checkpoints on the different runs need not have the same elapsed time. Any clock actually used by A must be represented in its state or tape.

Let R be the set of distinct addresses read on the actual continuation from C through halt. Then R meets each D_i. In particular, for any nonnegative weights w_i with sum of incident weights at each cell at most one,

    remaining transitions >= |R| >= sum_i w_i.

Proof. Induct on transitions of the two continuations. Until A first reads D_i, the equal head position and control give equal reads, writes, moves and next control. No cell in D_i can be overwritten unobserved in this model. If no such read occurred, the two executions would halt together with identical verdicts, including identical origin output. This contradicts correctness. Count a first hit for each i, grouping by the address actually read on the base run. Its total assigned weight is at most one. This proves the inequalities on a single continuation, independently of which alternative runs are longer. Reachability on correctly labelled inputs, not just validity of invented tape strings, is essential.

Physical movement supplies a separate bound on the same suffix. If D_i is at distance d from the current head, the first read of D_i takes at least d moves followed by a read. For several sets these distance bounds may be maximized. Adding them without disjoint travel intervals would double-charge motion.

For *one* opponent pair choose the reached configuration by a fair coin. Let U be the original Boolean verdict, B the public machine and ordered pair of hypothetical configurations with their labels, but without the random selection or elapsed time. The decoder can simulate the common path up to the first address p in D_i. The two old symbols at p differ. E is the singleton consisting of the actual first read of D_i after the chosen checkpoint. Pi is 0 or 1 according to which of the two publicly specified symbols it read. Identity of the chosen configuration, hence U, is decoded. The record has fixed length one, capacity b=1 for this projected two-symbol record, h=0 and rho=1. The common deterministic simulation specifies p and the event, so no private address or framing bit is free. The side information contains *both possibilities*, not the selected configuration. Thus E[remaining transitions]>=H(U|B)=1. The multi-opponent bound above uses incidence counting on x. It does **not** add these pairwise entropies from different distributions. For the packed family below the pairs can simply be described by q, i and the public input generators and checkpoint rule.

## Instantiation on exact encoded inputs

At Construction c28d84d5, graph coloring stars reduce G's 3-colorability to Boolean positive exact-one feasibility. Fix q>=1. Take q disjoint six-cycles, vertices 6j,...,6j+5 in rim j, and one apex joined to every rim vertex. Each rim gets one chord from 6j to 6j+3 on the base input. Opponent i replaces only that rim's chord endpoint by 6i+2. The apex consumes one color. The rest must be bipartite, and a bipartite coloring extends by assigning the apex the third color. All base chords join opposite parities. An edited rim contains a triangle. Thus the base is SAT and each opponent UNSAT. For arbitrary simultaneous edits feasibility is exactly absence of edits.

There are h=6q+1 vertices and e=13q edges. Emit the 4h station/bridge rows from the peer generator, followed by three edge/color leaves per edge. The order is fixed: all common edges first in rim order, all chord slots last in rim order. Private leaf IDs depend only on slot and color. Sorting edges anew on each input is unnecessary and could invalidate a bit-layout argument. Concretely the three leaves of edge u<v in slot a are (6u+c,6v+c,8h+3a+c) for c=0,1,2 in zero-based names. Private flags extend exactly when no color is chosen at both endpoints. Each real station chooses one color, dummy middle choices extend any proper coloring across bridge rows. This proves both-label equivalence without requiring A to process the rows in generator order.

The packed direct source has n=8h+3e=87q+8 names, m=4h+3e=63q+4 triples, w=ceil(log2(n+1)), and N=n+2+2 floor(log2(m+1))+3mw bits. Layout is unary n plus zero, gamma(m+1), then three fixed-width IDs plus one per row (each zero-based ID incremented before writing). Hence N=Theta(q log(q+1)). There is no certificate, compiled wrapper or hidden truth table. Relative to the base, opponent i changes only bits inside the middle ID fields of its own three chord rows. The difference support is nonempty, and these q supports are pairwise disjoint. They exclude the origin output cells.

For every time t on the base run, retain the indices I_t of the original difference supports no part of which A has yet read. For each such i the true opponent run reaches step t with the same head and control and identical tape outside the original D_i. This follows by the same read-before-write induction, even with joint preprocessing, copies, adaptive addressing and shared computations. On the base continuation there must be at least |I_t| more transitions that read distinct surviving supports. Use unit weights. In particular at t=0 there are q compulsory reads. The last differing field lies within O(log N) cells of the end for the last opponent. Starting at the origin, unit head speed therefore gives Omega(N) initial time on the base. If a checkpoint lies near that last field this origin-based distance argument cannot simply be restarted.

The single-tape model permits a full left-to-right input scan in O(N) time and O(N) charged span, plus return. It sets I_t empty irrespective of which semantic answer has been computed. Construction's authenticated solver goes further and actually decides all these graphs: their complements separate the apex from the rims. One can then pay O(N^4) or less on admitted runs to sweep and normalize the O(N²)-span workspace, keeping only the answer and fixed metadata, positioning the head at the origin. For example mark both workspace ends during allocation and erase to the endpoints and back on finite tracks. At a common READY instruction with decision in a separate origin work cell/track, all remaining differences for all opposite-label opponents can be concentrated at that one symbol (or in finite control in another implementation). The output cells can be reset to a fixed neutral value first. In the common-symbol-location case all weights incident there sum to at most one. Different elapsed times are unavailable to the continuation. This is a constructive counterexample to the inference that the original q disjoint obligations must survive every paid prefix.
