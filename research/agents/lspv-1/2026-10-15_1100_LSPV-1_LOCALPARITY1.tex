\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
For every integer $r\ge0$, choose $L$ divisible by four with $L\ge100(r+1)$; write $a=L/4$, $b=L/2$. Make two cycles $X=(x_0,\ldots,x_{L-1})$ and $Y=(y_0,\ldots,y_{L-1})$. Each edge $(u,v)$ requires $u\oplus v=e$, encoded by $(u\vee\neg v)\wedge(\neg u\vee v)$ for $e=0$, or $(u\vee v)\wedge(\neg u\vee\neg v)$ for $e=1$. Properize any 2-clause $D$ by $(D\vee h_D)\wedge(D\vee\neg h_D)$ with unique $h_D$. Finally encode $x_0\oplus y_0\oplus z=0$ by its four proper clauses prohibiting odd triples. The bridge is nonvacuous but, for any pair of initial cycle values, determines exactly one $z$. Both incidence graphs are connected.

Source $F_L^+$ has one-edge defects at $(X,a)$ and $(X,b)$; $F_L^-$ at $(X,a)$ and $(Y,b)$. Each has $6L+1$ variables and $8L+4$ proper three-clauses; total explicit input length $N=\Theta(L\log L)$. None has a pure variable or unit clause.

\begin{theorem}[Exact truth-value switch]
$F_L^+$ is satisfiable with exactly $4\cdot2^{4L}$ full assignments; $F_L^-$ is unsatisfiable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The sum of edge equations around a cycle is consistent exactly when its charge parity vanishes. The plus-source has charges $(2,0)$ and therefore two independent initial-cycle bits; the minus-source has odd parity on each cycle. The bridge uniquely determines $z$ and the $4L$ private twin-clause variables are free in the satisfiable source.
\end{proof}

Let $G(F)$ denote the typed variable/clause incidence graph, with signed literal edges. Let $\mathcal P_r(F)$ be the multiset of rooted typed, sign-preserving isomorphism classes of all induced radius-$r$ balls, ignoring numeric identifiers and clause ordering.
\begin{theorem}[Exact local-profile collision]
For all $r\ge0$ and $L\ge100(r+1)$ as above,
$\mathcal P_r(F_L^+)=\mathcal P_r(F_L^-)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The sources differ only by exchanging the two parity gadgets at edge index $b$ between otherwise identical cycles. These neighborhoods lie farther than $2r+O(1)$ graph steps from the fixed $(X,a)$ defect and the connector at index zero. For roots whose radius-$r$ balls avoid both changing gadgets the balls are literally the same. For roots whose balls meet them, exchange the corresponding roots in the two cycles. Their full balls cannot reach either distinguished distant feature, and the two gadgets are uniform signed local replacements. This root bijection preserves exact typed signed-ball isomorphism and its multiplicities, including every auxiliary and clause root.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Profile-only terminal-coverage firewall]
For every $R(N)=o(N/\log N)$, no deterministic total terminal SAT classifier receiving only $\mathcal P_{R(N)}(F)$ is correct on all proper 3CNFs. A sound partial classifier receiving that profile must say UNKNOWN on both members of some pair. For a randomized profile-only classifier, one member incurs error at least $1/2$. The same holds for a generated portfolio of profile-only terminal runners even when the favorable index moves with the input.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The equal-length instances have equal complete information-interface strings for sufficiently large $L$, while their correct Boolean answers differ. Every deterministic/profile-generated sequence of terminal outputs is identical on both. Soundness forces all entries to be UNKNOWN. Two opposite labels cannot each occur with probability strictly above $1/2$ in the same output distribution. This says nothing about a runner that sees the full source or calls a hypothetical SAT decider.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Certified global-affine escape]
A single ordinary multitape source-reading machine on every explicit proper 3CNF outputs SAT with witness, UNSAT, or OUTSIDE; every definite answer is correct and every relabeling and clause permutation of the pair above receives a correct definite answer. A conservative charged simultaneous implementation has $T(N)=O(N^8)$ and $S(N)=O(N^2)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Canonicalize clauses; repeatedly eliminate an auxiliary $h$ occurring exactly twice with opposite signs in twin clauses and nowhere else, using $(D\vee h)\wedge(D\vee\neg h)\equiv D$. Group the residue by underlying variable pair/triple. Accept only if every pair has the exact two-clause truth table of one XOR2 equation and every triple the exact four-clause truth table of one XOR3 equation. Otherwise return OUTSIDE. On acceptance decide the resulting GF(2) system by Gaussian elimination and lift a model by setting erased auxiliaries to zero. Every step is a semantic equivalence on all inputs, hence sound. $O(N)$ rounds, explicit clause storage, sorting, dense $O(N^2)$-bit matrix, exact row operations, tape-head access and witness output all fit the stated conservative bounds on one machine. On the displayed sources the extraction is complete. This polynomial algorithm is a direct counterexample to any proposed inference from local indistinguishability to arbitrary-source superpolynomial runtime.
\end{proof}
