\section{Canonical target and fully source-authenticated residual}
For every valid original formula $F$ of D\"ocker's duplicate-free proper monotone 3-SAT-(2,2), each source clause $C_j$ is a triple of distinct same-sign literals, each original variable occurs twice per polarity, and no clause repeats. Malformed encodings must reject. This specific language is NP-complete \cite{doecker}. Ordinary cofactors may violate the grammar. A coherent partial source assignment $p$ survives sound unit propagation with $f$ free variables and $u$ uncovered original clauses. Its signed-source binary residual multigraph $G_p$ has one edge labelled by the original clause ID for each uncovered width-two clause. Let $r_3$ count its uncovered ternary clauses and $e=2f-u$. The unresolved issue is whether a paid new source-variable parameter improves the earlier $e+1$ edge-disjoint odd-cycle certificate without assuming semantic SAT/UNSAT labels.

For each free original variable $x$, let $s_x^+$ (respectively $s_x^-$) be the number of its positive (respectively negative) occurrences in original clauses already satisfied by pinned literals. Put
\[
\eta(p)=\sum_{x\ {\rm free}}\min(s_x^+,s_x^-),
\quad \Delta(p)=\sum_{x\ {\rm free}}|s_x^+-s_x^-|.
\]
Both are syntactic quantities acquired by paid original-source scanning.

\begin{theorem}[Signed-spill conservation and source-indexed cycle budget]
For every valid original canonical source $F$, every UP-stable coherent cube $p$, and every satisfying full extension $a\supseteq p$,
\[
\boxed{e=\sum_{j:C_j\ {\rm uncovered}}(t_j(a)-1)
+\sum_{x\ {\rm free}}\{s_x^+a_x+s_x^-(1-a_x)\}.}\tag{1}
\]
Independently of SAT/UNSAT, with $h=\sum_x(s_x^++s_x^-)$,
\[
\boxed{2e=r_3+h,\quad 2(e-\eta)=r_3+\Delta.}\tag{2}
\]
For any explicitly authenticated collection $\mathcal C$ of edge-disjoint odd cycles of the original-source binary graph $G_p$,
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{SAT}(F|_p)\Longrightarrow |\mathcal C|+\eta\le e.}\tag{3}
\]
Therefore $|\mathcal C|+\eta>e$ refutes the entire partial cube. Failure to find such a certificate returns UNRESOLVED and has no UNSAT authority.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each free original variable has two positive and two negative occurrences, hence every complete assignment makes exactly two of its original occurrences true. Its total true free occurrences across the entire source is therefore exactly $2f$. The true occurrences in uncovered original clauses are $\sum_j t_j(a)$, since no assigned literal satisfies those clauses. In already-covered source clauses they are $\sum_x(s_x^+a_x+s_x^-(1-a_x))$. If $a$ is SAT, every uncovered $t_j(a)\ge1$; subtracting $u$ yields (1) with nonnegative terms. The $4f$ original free occurrence slots partition into $2(u-r_3)+3r_3$ uncovered slots and $h$ covered slots. This gives $4f=2u+r_3+h$ and $2e=r_3+h$. The identity $2\min(b,c)=b+c-|b-c|$ gives $2\eta=h-\Delta$, hence the second equality of (2). In any satisfying assignment a binary monotone original clause has exactly one true literal if its endpoints are different, and exactly two if the endpoints agree. Every odd cycle requires a same-valued edge, spending one unit of the first nonnegative sum in (1). Edge-disjoint cycles spend distinct units. The second sum is at least $\eta$. Thus $e\ge|\mathcal C|+\eta$. All original source clause IDs and signed occurrences are retained and independently checkable.
\end{proof}
The counting uses and credits LSPV-1 OCCURRENCE-SLACK290 and ODD-CYCLE-BUDGET293. Greedy BFS cycle packing is known graph reasoning and need not find a maximum packing \cite{guo}. Computing $\eta$ or detecting a cycle must not be treated as a free semantic oracle.
