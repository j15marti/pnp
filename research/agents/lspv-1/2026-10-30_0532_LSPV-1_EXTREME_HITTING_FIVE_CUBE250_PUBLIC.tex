\section{Original signed extreme controls}
For every ORIGINAL source clause $C_j$ and original occurrence $x_{i,j}$ with sign $\sigma_{ij}=1$ (positive), $0$ (negative), define $S_j$ by $x_i=\sigma_{ij}$ and $U_j$ by $x_i=1-\sigma_{ij}$ for each occurrence. Thus $S_j$ requires all literals TRUE ($t_j=3$), $U_j$ requires all FALSE ($t_j=0$), and inversion exchanges them. One global variable is assigned consistently across its occurrences; ordinary disjunctive SAT only requires $t_j\ge1$. For clauses sharing same-sign variables $s_{jk}$ and opposite-sign variables $o_{jk}$, the four-mode matrix is
\[
SS,UU\ \mathrm{feasible}\Longleftrightarrow o_{jk}=\emptyset;\qquad
SU,US\ \mathrm{feasible}\Longleftrightarrow s_{jk}=\emptyset.
\]
For coherent complete $a$ and original-variable flip set $H$,
\[
t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a),\quad
t_j(a\oplus H)-t_j(a)=|H\cap L_j^0(a)|-|H\cap L_j^1(a)|.
\]
The full raw audit retains original indexed S and U, both inverted target sets, four pair modes, triple checks, actual $0^n/1^n$, per-clause four statuses, complements, flip trails, and source-resolvent forbidden cubes.
\section{Exact minimal local obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[Four-clause barrier]
For every proper residual CNF $R$ with at most four clauses of width two or three, UNSAT implies that all four clauses have width two and their local all-FALSE cylinders are pairwise disjoint. Consequently the binary-only residual 2SAT is UNSAT. Therefore a residual contradiction not caught by unit propagation or its binary-only 2SAT subformula requires at least five residual clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A width-$w$ clause has falsification fraction $2^{-w}$. For at most four residual clauses the union measure is bounded by $\sum_j2^{-|C_j|}\le1$. UNSAT requires equality, forcing exactly four width-two clauses, and equality in the union bound forces pairwise-disjoint falsification events. Their binary conjunction is UNSAT.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Five-clause hitting certificate]
Given a coherent original-variable cube $\rho$, suppose five residual clauses have widths $(2,2,2,3,3)$ and every pair has an opposite-polarity shared free original variable. Then $\rho$ has no satisfying extension in the source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The five all-FALSE events are pairwise disjoint and have combined measure $3/4+2/8=1$. They partition the entire coherent residual assignment cube. Every extension violates exactly one of the five clauses. This is established hitting clause-set reasoning rather than a novel inference rule.
\end{proof}
\section{Connected proper strict signed (2+,2-) 3CNF}
Take original variables $x=x_1,y=x_2,z=x_3,g_1=x_4,g_2=x_5,g_3=x_6$ and original indexed source
\[
\begin{array}{rcl@{\qquad}rcl}
C_0&=&x\vee y\vee g_1&C_1&=&\neg x\vee z\vee g_2\\
C_2&=&\neg y\vee\neg z\vee g_3&C_3&=&x\vee\neg y\vee z\\
C_4&=&\neg x\vee y\vee\neg z&C_5&=&\neg g_1\vee\neg g_2\vee\neg g_3\\
C_6&=&g_1\vee g_2\vee\neg g_3&C_7&=&\neg g_1\vee\neg g_2\vee g_3.
\end{array}
\]
Every variable occurs twice in each sign, each original clause is proper, and the incidence graph is connected. Both globally consistent $0^6$ and $1^6$ FAIL, with histograms $(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)=(1,3,3,1)$. The coherent $0^6\to x=1\to x=y=1\to x=y=z=1$ trail has indexed clause-status vectors
\[
(0,1,2,1,2,3,1,2),\ (1,0,2,2,1,3,1,2),\
(2,0,1,1,2,3,1,2),\ (2,1,0,2,1,3,1,2).
\]
The full formula has 12 complete satisfying assignments, including $(1,0,0,0,1,0)$. Thus endpoint failure is not UNSAT.
Under $\rho:(g_1,g_2,g_3)=(0,0,0)$, source $C_5,C_6,C_7$ are satisfied, leaving exactly
\[
(x\vee y),\ (\neg x\vee z),\ (\neg y\vee\neg z),\
(x\vee\neg y\vee z),\ (\neg x\vee y\vee\neg z).
\]
Original-source unit propagation derives no contradiction, and the first three binary clauses have two models $(x,y,z)=(0,1,0),(1,0,1)$. Those models falsify respectively the fourth and fifth clause. All ten U-pairs are disjoint, covering all eight coherent $xyz$ assignments. Hence
\[
\boxed{F\models(g_1\vee g_2\vee g_3)}.
\]
An independent conventional six-step resolution proof, from source parents, is
\[
\begin{array}{rcll}
R_1&=&y\vee z\vee g_1\vee g_2&\operatorname{Res}_x(C_0,C_1),\\
R_2&=&y\vee\neg z\vee g_1&\operatorname{Res}_x(C_0,C_4),\\

\]
All resolvents are non-tautological and replayed against the indexed original parent clauses.
\section{Charged bounded-degree discovery and infinite SAT controls}
\begin{theorem}
On an original strict signed exact-(2+,2-) proper 3CNF source with input encoding length $N$, a deterministic indexed word-RAM procedure enumerates in $O(N\log N)$ time and $O(N)$ indexed words all motifs that have (i) two complementary ternary source clauses on a three-variable support $W$, and (ii) three source clauses each sharing exactly two variables of $W$ plus a distinct external guard literal, whose projected two ternary plus three binary clauses form the five-U-hitting partition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Verify all source occurrences and sort source-clause three-variable support keys. Each variable in $W$ has four total occurrences, and two are consumed by the complementary pair. Hence at most six distinct source clauses remain adjacent to $W$, so at most $\binom63=20$ guard-triple candidates exist per anchor. All checks (same/opposite source signs, ten U conflicts, small truth table, optional resolution certificate) take constant time per candidate; there are at most $O(m)$ anchors. Source scanning, failed candidates, proof checking and bounded output are charged. The Python implementation uses hash maps; deterministic sorting is the specified theorem model.
\end{proof}
For every $q\ge1$, cyclically join $q$ copies of the eight-clause source by permuting the positive $g_1$ occurrence of $C_6$ between successive copies. This preserves strict22 signed quotas and connects the incidence graph for $q\ge2$. Each local five-clause core survives; the formula is SAT with a model repeating $(x,y,z,g_1,g_2,g_3)=(0,0,0,1,0,1)$ in each copy. This is an infinite restricted deduction family, not a general SAT algorithm.
