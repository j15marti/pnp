\section{The candidate and its exact quantifiers}
Fix a total correct deterministic SAT decider $A$.  A trace proof of an UNSAT
formula $F$ is the complete accepting computation history
$\pi=\mathrm{trace}_A(F)$ ending with the bit UNSAT.  The verifier checks the
input configuration, every transition, and the final bit.  It runs in
$O(|F|+|\pi|)$ elementary transition checks (or the corresponding polynomial
under a standard encoded machine model).  Thus this is a sound, complete,
$A$-dependent proof relation for UNSAT.

Write $\operatorname{Sim}_H(A)$ for the weakest size consequence needed by
the proposed bridge:
\[
 \exists c_A,k_A\ \forall n\ \exists R_n\quad
  R_n\text{ is a Resolution refutation of }H_n,
 \quad |R_n|\le c_A(L_n+|\mathrm{trace}_A(H_n)|)^{k_A}.       \tag{2}
\]
Requiring a polynomial-time translator (p-simulation) would only strengthen
(2).  Define
\[
 U_{\poly}:=\forall A\,[A\text{ is a uniform deterministic polynomial-time
 exact SAT decider}\Rightarrow\operatorname{Sim}_H(A)].      \tag{3}
\]

\paragraph{Theorem (quantifier collapse).}
Assuming the explicit Resolution lower bound (1),
\[
                         U_{\poly}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad P\ne NP.
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
If $P\ne NP$, no uniform polynomial-time exact SAT decider exists, so (3) is
true vacuously.  Conversely assume $P=NP$.  Then some such $A$ exists and
$|\mathrm{trace}_A(H_n)|\le L_n^{d_A}$ for a constant $d_A$.  If
$\operatorname{Sim}_H(A)$ held, (2) would give polynomial-size Resolution
refutations of every $H_n$, contradicting (1).  Therefore the implication in
(3) fails for that $A$, so $U_{\poly}$ is false.  This proves (4).\qed

Two neighboring quantifier forms make the obstruction explicit:
\[
 \exists A\in\mathrm{PolyDec}(\SAT)\;\operatorname{Sim}_H(A)
 \quad\text{is unconditionally false},                         \tag{5}
\]
because the conjunction alone contradicts (1), whereas
\[
 \forall A\in\mathrm{TotalDec}(\SAT)\;\operatorname{Sim}_H(A)
 \quad\text{is unconditionally false}.                         \tag{6}
\]
For (6), define $A^\star$ to recognize canonical $H_n$ by generation and
comparison, return UNSAT there, and run exhaustive search elsewhere.  It is a
uniform total exact decider with polynomial traces on $H_n$; (2) would again
contradict (1).  All recognition, comparison, fallback, and trace-verification
costs are charged.  The construction says nothing about polynomial worst-case
time, because its fallback is exponential.

Consequently, the proposed universal transfer cannot be used to prove the
separation: on polynomial deciders it already states the separation, and on
all total deciders it is false.  This is the additional all-algorithm
implication requested by the integration memo, and it is retired rather than
assumed.
