\paragraph{Setup.}
Let \(V=\mathbb F_2^m\). For every legal SAME\_REALIZATION Hadamard slice let \(S_v\le V\) be its image. Define
\[
W=\operatorname{span}_v S_v,\qquad U=\operatorname{span}_v\bigwedge\nolimits^2 S_v\le \bigwedge\nolimits^2V.
\]
Let
\[
\mathcal Q_2=\{q(x)=c+\ell(x)+\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}x_ix_j\},\qquad M=1+m+\binom m2,
\]
and let \(K_2(H)\) be the common degree-two annihilator space.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: dimension-sum theorem.}
Let \(\pi:K_2(H)\to(\bigwedge^2V)^*\) send \(q\) to its alternating polar form \(b_q\). Since \(b_q\) vanishes on every \(\bigwedge^2S_v\),
\[
\operatorname{im}\pi\subseteq U^\perp,\qquad
\dim\operatorname{im}\pi\le\binom m2-\dim U.
\]
If \(q\in\ker\pi\), then \(q\) has no quadratic cross term, hence is linear because \(q(0)=0\). Its restriction to every \(S_v\) is zero, so its linear functional lies in \(W^\perp\). Conversely every \(\ell\in W^\perp\) defines a common annihilator. Thus
\[
\ker\pi\cong W^\perp,\qquad \dim\ker\pi=m-\dim W.
\]
Rank-nullity gives
\[
\dim K_2(H)\le(m-\dim W)+\left(\binom m2-\dim U\right),
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)\ge1+\dim W+\dim U.}
\]
For every finite legal subfamily \(R\), likewise
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)\ge1+\dim W_R+\dim U_R.}
\]
