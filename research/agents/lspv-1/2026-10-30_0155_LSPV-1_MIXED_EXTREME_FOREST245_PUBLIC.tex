\section{Signed original-clause targets}
For original proper 3CNF clause $C_j$, occurrence $x_{i,j}$ has polarity $\nu_{ij}$ (0 positive, 1 negative). Local $S_j$ pins $x_i=1\oplus\nu_{ij}$ and local $U_j$ pins $x_i=\nu_{ij}$. Their complements exchange them. These are \emph{local} targets, never independent global occurrence assignments. For coherent $a$,
\[
t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a),\quad
t_j(a\oplus H)=t_j(a)+|H\cap L_j^0(a)|-|H\cap L_j^1(a)|.
\]
Two local extreme modes $SS,UU$ are compatible iff no opposite signed variable is shared; $SU,US$ iff no same-signed variable is shared. A clause is ordinarily SAT when $t_j\ge1$, not only $t_j=3$. All source-indexed targets, inverted assignments, four-mode pair matrices, coherent endpoints, and multi-flip four-class traces were independently checked.
\section{Two-sided mixed-event composition}
Define $E_{jk}=U_j\cap S_k$ for distinct original clause indices $j,k$. Retain nonempty coherent cubes, deduplicate identical cubes with the full list of original source pairs as provenance, and call the resulting events $E_1,\dots,E_M$ ($M\le m(m-1)$). For any forest $T$ on event IDs set
\[
H_T(a)=1-\sum_u [E_u(a)]+\sum_{uv\in T}[E_u(a)\wedge E_v(a)],
\qquad B_T=\mathbb E H_T.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact restricted source-to-witness certificate]
For every original coherent assignment $a$,
\[
\boxed{H_T(a)\le [F(a)\ \lor\ F(\bar a)].}
\]
Hence $\#\mathrm{SAT}(F)\ge\max\{0,2^{n-1}B_T\}$. If $B_T>0$, a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm constructs and checks a full SAT witness from original source clauses, without an answer oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both $F(a),F(\bar a)$ fail iff $N_0(a)>0$ and $N_3(a)>0$, iff at least one $U_jS_k$ event holds. If $q>0$ such events occur, their induced subgraph in a forest has at most $q-1$ edges, so $H_T(a)\le1-q+q-1=0$. If none occurs, $H_T(a)=1$. Taking expectations and noting that a successful orientation is either a model or the complement of a model gives the model-count bound. Exact cube-intersection probabilities conditional on original-variable pins $\rho$ satisfy $B_T(\rho)=[B_T(\rho,x=0)+B_T(\rho,x=1)]/2$. Starting with $B_T>0$, repeatedly choose a positive child, then check the final coherent assignment and its complement against every source clause. All choices are computed, not supplied.
There are at most $O(m^2)$ bounded-width event cubes. Prim's maximum-intersection spanning tree uses $O(m^4)$ constant-width source-event comparisons and polynomial workspace; all failed edges and source reads are charged. Conditional selection takes $O(nm^2)$ exact cube probability evaluations; integers have polynomial bit length. This is a sufficient restricted SAT criterion, not an arbitrary-algorithm lower bound.
\end{proof}
The graph/probability inequality is an application of Hunter's established 1976 spanning-tree union bound, not a claim of discovery of a new probability theorem.

\section{A negative cube theorem}
\begin{proposition}[One original pivot is UP-dominated]
For proper source parents $(x\lor A)$ and $(\neg x\lor B)$ with a non-tautological resolvent $R=A\lor B$, every original-variable partial cube falsifying $R$ is already inconsistent under source unit propagation. Conversely, direct opposed-source-unit conflict on an unassigned pivot falsifies that resolvent.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Falsifying $R$ pins every literal of $A,B$ false. The parents then reduce to $x,\neg x$ if $x$ is unpinned; if pinned, one parent is empty. Conversely, opposed units require all other source-parent literals false. A tautological resolvent cannot be falsified by a coherent cube.
\end{proof}
Four incompatible clause-pair \emph{extreme} modes do not mean the source disjunctions are mutually UNSAT. E.g. $(x_1\lor x_2\lor x_3)$ and $(x_1\lor x_2\lor\neg x_3)$ are jointly SAT, yet their extremes cannot coexist. Their resolvent $x_1\lor x_2$ excludes the cube $x_1=x_2=0$ through ordinary UP, not a new inference principle.
