\section*{Exact fixed system, quantifiers, and assumptions}
Keep unchanged $R=R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, on valid general binary-encoded CNF with authentic zero-cost source clauses, strict binary RES, exact three-variable typed AND3, justified GF$(2)$ XOR and LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative source-independent signed two-input VARDEF. Each distinct derived inference node costs one; all DAG sharing, auxiliary extensions and free hindsight proof selection are allowed. Let $E=\mathrm{ER2}=\{\mathrm{RES},\mathrm{VARDEF}\}\subseteq R$. In both systems define
\[
 W_S(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_S\bot}\sum_{v\in\pi_{\rm derived}}1,\quad
 \overline W_S(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ {\rm valid\ UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_S(F).
\]
The empty maximum is zero; $N$ is bit length. The sought \emph{weakest sufficient} B3 is
\[
  \mathrm{SAT}\in P\ \Longrightarrow\
  \exists C,k\ \forall N\quad \overline W_R(N)\le C(N+1)^k.
\]
This is \textbf{OPEN}, as are stronger B1/B2.

\section*{Universal small-domain rejection soundness with a global mixed optimum}
For every integer $b\ge0$ put $m=2^b$ and introduce primary bits $y_0,\ldots,y_{b-1}$ and mask bits $u_j$ for $j\in\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$. Let
\[
 B_j(y)=\bigvee_{i=0}^{b-1}
 \begin{cases}y_i,&\text{bit}_i(j)=0,\\
                \neg y_i,&\text{bit}_i(j)=1.\end{cases}
\]
$B_j$ is false only on the assignment $y=j$. A mask $u$ describes the signed clause family $F_u(y)=\bigwedge_{j:u_j=1} B_j(y)$, which is UNSAT iff \emph{every} mask bit is 1. The fixed total decider for this restricted input grammar parses exactly $m$ bits, returns MALFORMED otherwise, and rejects iff all mask bits are one. Its time is $O(m)$ in the mask-encoded input length; it is not a general-CNF SAT decider. The universal no-false-rejection CNF is
\[
 U_b=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{m-1}
 \bigl[(u_j)\wedge(\neg u_j\vee B_j(y))\bigr].
 \tag{1}
\]
Indeed the units assert rejection, and the guarded blockers assert that a purported witness $y$ satisfies the selected mask formula.

\begin{theorem}[Exact all-size, full-mixed rejection/evaluation fixture]
For every $b\ge0$, $U_b$ is minimally UNSAT with exactly $2m$ indispensable authentic source clauses; every individual source literal-sign flip makes it SAT; and for the \emph{unchanged complete} $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$,
\[
 \boxed{W_R(U_b)=2m-1=2^{b+1}-1.}\tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All units force $u_j=1$. On a witness $y=j$ its own guarded blocker is then false, proving UNSAT. If unit $(u_j)$ is deleted, set $y=j$, $u_j=0$ and all other $u_i=1$. If guarded clause $(\neg u_j\vee B_j)$ is deleted, set $y=j$ and all $u_i=1$. Each assignment satisfies every other source. Flipping the one literal in the unit is repaired by the former assignment; flipping any occurrence in its guarded clause is repaired by the latter assignment. Thus every source and signed occurrence is essential.
Any sound source-explicit contradiction ancestry with at most two actual premises per charged node must incorporate all $2m$ indispensable sources. Starting from $s$ source leaves, binary merging to one sink needs at least $s-1$ charged nodes even with shared DAGs, unary rules, zero-premise conservative definitions and temporary growth. Formally the connected ancestry has $E\ge V-1$ and $E\le2q$, where $V=2m+q$; hence $q\ge2m-1$.
Conversely resolve $(u_j)$ with $(\neg u_j\vee B_j)$ to derive each $B_j$, paying $m$ genuine RES nodes. Pair adjacent blockers on $y_0$, then pair their outputs on $y_1$, and continue through $y_{b-1}$. These are $m-1$ genuine non-tautological RES pivots, ending in the empty clause. All derivations use authenticated sources and cost $2m-1$. This proves the \emph{global} mixed optimum, not just a Resolution optimum.
\end{proof}

\section*{Conditional one-family B3 sufficiency inside ER2}
Fix \emph{one} externally correct uniform polynomial-time general-CNF decider $A$ handling SAT, UNSAT and malformed strings. For each bit length $N$ choose one polynomial-size, uniform bounded-fanin Boolean circuit $D_N(s,y)$ computing
$\operatorname{Valid}(s)\land\operatorname{Reject}_A(s)\land\operatorname{Eval}(s,y)$,
where $s$ has exactly $N$ bits and the witness vector $y$ has at most $N$ bits. Define $H_{A,N}$ by authentic bounded-fanin Tseitin gate clauses for $D_N$ plus a unit asserting its output is true. Each $H_{A,N}$ is UNSAT by the externally assumed correctness of $A$, with bit length at most $g_A(N)=(N+1)^{O_A(1)}$.

\begin{lemma}[ER-only universal specialization suffices, \textbf{CONDITIONAL}]
Assume there is a polynomial $q_A$ such that for \emph{every} $N$, the above exact $H_{A,N}$ has an authenticated ER2 refutation with at most $q_A(N)$ paid nodes and polynomially encoded intermediate clauses/gates. Assume the standard polynomial Extended-Frege/Extended-Resolution simulations in the declared signed gate grammar, including polynomial closure under substitution of source input bits, and a uniformly polynomial signed-CNF-to-evaluation circuit compilation. Then
\[
 \forall\ F\ {\rm valid\ UNSAT}:\quad
 W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)
 \le W_E(F)\le (\,|{\rm enc}(F)|+q_A(|{\rm enc}(F)|)\,)^{O_A(1)},
 \tag{3}
\]
and hence B3 holds for this \emph{same} preselected $R$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $s=\operatorname{enc}(F)$ with $|s|=N$. Translate the assumed $E$ proof of $H_{A,N}$ to Extended Frege, substitute all $N$ source bits by their actual values, and polynomially simplify the authenticated circuit-defining clauses. This is a proof transformation, not a semantic SAT oracle. Since $F$ is valid and $A(F)$ actually rejects, the specialized parser and machine-circuit outputs have explicit gate-evaluation certificates of polynomial length. The resulting proof derives that the propositional predicate $\operatorname{Eval}(s,y)$ is false for all witness bits $y$. Independently the genuine source clauses of $F$ imply the corresponding evaluation circuit output true: for each source clause, derive the output of its OR-of-literals gate using its actual clause and paid small-fanin gate identities, then AND all clause outputs. These are polynomial in $N$ under the stated uniform encoding. Combining the two yields a signed Extended-Frege contradiction from $F$. Translate back to signed $E$ using the assumed standard polynomial simulation; charge every fresh definition and RES inference. Since $E\subseteq R$, (3) follows, with explicit polynomial composition $N\mapsto g_A(N)\mapsto q_A(N)$ and the simulation overhead.
\end{proof}
\noindent This avoids the earlier \emph{full mixed} restriction/retyping obligation, because the universal proof is assumed to live in ER2 and goes through Extended Frege substitution. It is \emph{not} an unconditional B3 proof: neither $SAT\in P\Rightarrow W_E(H_{A,N})\le\operatorname{poly}(N)$ nor $SAT\in P\Rightarrow$ a formal rejection-soundness proof has been shown. It is \emph{not} the converse of B3 for $R$ without an all-rule mixed-to-ER simulation. Unlike a mere reformulation of worst-case $R$-hardness, it identifies a single uniform $H_{A,N}$ family and a strictly specified \emph{ER-only} additional certificate obligation.
