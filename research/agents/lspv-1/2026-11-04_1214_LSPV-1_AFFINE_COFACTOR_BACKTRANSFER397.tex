\begin{lemma}[Typed local Res$(\oplus)$ simulation]
Temporarily admitting each complete paid DEF facet bank as an explicit premise, every $r$-non-DEF-node fragment proof has a Res$(\oplus)$ simulation with no more than $m+4g+128r$ lines.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A strict RES is one affine pivot. For unit affine equations $E_1,E_2$, let $E_3=E_1+E_2$. Weaken $E_1$ to $E_3\lor\neg E_2$, then cut with $E_2$; for constant or duplicated rows normalize first and bypass degenerate pivots through inherited stronger lines. A local Boolean output on at most three named inputs has at most eight canonical blocking clauses. To establish any output clause $C$ from its parent conditions, split syntactically on all at most eight assignments $\alpha$. At a falsifying assignment for $C$, soundness supplies a parent blocking clause falsified at $\alpha$, which weakens to $C\lor B_\alpha$, with $B_\alpha$ the complete assignment blocker. When $\alpha$ satisfies $C$, $C\lor B_\alpha$ is tautological. At most eight leaves and seven binary cuts give $C$ using at most 15 nodes. Repeating for at most eight clauses costs at most 120 lines. Allow 128 per local paid node, leaving original-source authentication intact.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Affine or one-bit cofactor removal]
A Res$(\oplus)$ refutation with $S$ lines over $F$ plus $j$ acyclic complete gate definition banks can remove the final bank at no line-growth cost when the gate is affine; a nonaffine final bank can be removed with no more than $2S+2M+1$ lines, where $M=m+4g$ bounds temporary source clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For affine output $z=f(a,b)$, substitute the affine expression for $z$; all defining facets become tautologies, affine cuts and semantic weakening remain valid after normalization. For a nonaffine output, fix the underlying named variable of a signed input $a$ at $0$ or $1$. Then the resulting function $z=f(\alpha,b)$ is constant, $b$ or $\neg b$, hence affine. Substitute in both branches, deleting final facets. Earlier banks are still authentic premises even when $a$ is an earlier paid output. Lift each restricted genuine-source clause by adjoining the disagreeing guard literal; common guards preserve Res$(\oplus)$ weakening and complementary pivot, producing both opposite guards. Merge by one binary cut. Each branch has at most $S+M$ lines. No gate premise survives after reverse-topological elimination.
\end{proof}
