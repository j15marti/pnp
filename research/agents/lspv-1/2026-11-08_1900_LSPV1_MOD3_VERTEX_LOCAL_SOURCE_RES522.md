No additional axiom, gate, extension, branch, weakening, or case-discharge rule is used.

Let A_Z = z0 OR z1 OR z2 be the ORIGINAL positive one-hot clause and
D_abj = NOT(u_a) OR NOT(v_b) OR NOT(z_j), for every a+b+j != c modulo 3, be the ORIGINAL forbidden-tuple clause. For each a,b, write l=c-a-b modulo 3 and let {j,k}=F3\{l}. Two correctly oriented binary Resolution steps yield:

2. RES on pivot z_k from Q_ab and D_abk: C_ab = NOT(u_a) OR NOT(v_b) OR z_l.
