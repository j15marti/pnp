\section{One integrated research target and four falsifiers}
For an arbitrary fixed uniform total correct polynomial-time ordinary-SAT
decider $A$ with unknown finite same-machine exponents $K,L$, the proposed
unconstructed compiler $\Phi$ would have to preserve both labels on every
original source and, on the \emph{same} FLMV indexed read-only-input
random-access Turing machine, achieve
\[
 K'\le\max\{11/10,3/5+K/2\},\qquad
 L'\le\max\{1/2,L/2\}.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The selector, discovery, parsing, source and indexed-tape reads, unsuccessful
policies, output bit access, complete ordinary children, guest simulation,
proof checking, reset/replay, and simultaneous retained work/ancestor state
belong inside the implementation.  Equation (1) is a \emph{target}, not an
achieved recurrence.

The present test concerns one certified policy inside the adaptive portfolio:
joining source-authenticated Horn-projected modules over a shared original
boundary. Before computation, four falsifiers were fixed:
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{F1 (pairwise shortcut).} Pairwise compatibility of projected
modules guarantees global SAT. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: already three
meet-closed projections over two shared bits have pairwise intersections
but empty total intersection.
\item[] \textbf{F2 (unrestricted small core).} Every arbitrary $k$-bit
interface with no global compatible assignment has an inconsistent
subfamily of at most $k+1$ modules. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: eight signed clauses
over three variables have empty full intersection and every seven intersect.
\item[] \textbf{F3 (free projection acquisition).} The existence of a
$k+1$-module obstruction makes its structural discovery, projection
construction, certificate authentication or source/tape traffic free.
\textbf{FALSIFIED as a deduction from the theorem}: the example's
proper-ternary source gadgets must still be read, and the full source
and per-module boundary information must be verified. No general
$N^{11/10}$ discovery bound follows.
\item[] \textbf{F4 (semantic versus physical shrinkage).} Correct two-child
Shannon joins imply that both physical ordinary-SAT children have length
$N^{1/2+o(1)}$. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the tested same-source children:
on the $k=4$ UNSAT source, parent and child DIMACS lengths are
191 and 172/152 bytes, not square-root scale.
\end{description}

\section{Deterministic certified residual semantics}
Let $F$ be the ordered, signed original clause list with original-variable
IDs; $x_{i,j}$ denotes an occurrence of \emph{one} original Boolean
variable $x_i$ in source clause $C_j$, not a new variable. A residual
controller state is the finite source-encoded tuple
\[
 z=(F,\rho,\mathcal D,\mathcal I,\mathcal C,\mathcal H,
       \mathcal P,\mathcal R,\mathcal L).
\]
Here $\rho$ is a coherent partial assignment, $\mathcal D$ contains
authenticated derived clauses and proof dependencies, $\mathcal I$ is the
current source/index-head configuration, $\mathcal C$ contains checked
certificates and cache, $\mathcal H$ is the branch/continuation stack,
$\mathcal P$ records attempted and selected policies, $\mathcal R$ is the
physical representation and child-access descriptor, and $\mathcal L$
contains measured time/space/event charges. Its \emph{computed and
certified} parameter vector records residual physical length, original
variables/clauses, recognized Horn/Krom/dual-Horn/XOR status, discovered
separators and projection support, source reads, guest-query lengths,
child access/serialization, failed attempts, replay and simultaneous peak
writable position. Neither recognition nor a successful answer is a
free oracle. For any unfixed $x$, correct ordinary branching is
\[
 \SAT(F\!\upharpoonright\!\rho)=
 \SAT(F\!\upharpoonright\!(\rho,x=0))\lor
 \SAT(F\!\upharpoonright\!(\rho,x=1)).                         \tag{2}
\]
A NO answer requires both NO descendants or another genuine authenticated
contradiction; a bounded unsuccessful search is UNKNOWN, not UNSAT.

\section{Exact sharp theorem for Horn interface projections}
Let $B=\{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}$ be $k$ original shared variables.  Let
$F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{q} H_i(B,U_i)$, where the private variable sets
$U_i$ are pairwise disjoint, every original clause has one authenticated
module owner, and the complete shared set has been proved (not guessed).
Define the full boundary relation
\[
 P_i=\{b\in\{0,1\}^k:\exists u_i\,H_i(b,u_i)\}.
\]
Assume every $P_i$ is closed under coordinatewise meet:
$b,c\in P_i\Rightarrow b\wedge c\in P_i$.
For example, existential projection of any Horn CNF is meet-closed.
A proper-3CNF \emph{encoding} may fail to be syntactically Horn even when
its existential projection is meet-closed; certification of the semantic
relation is part of the policy's cost.

\begin{theorem}[Sharp Horn-projection Helly bound]\label{th:helly}
Under the above source-authenticated module and meet-closure hypotheses,
\[
 \SAT(F)\iff\bigcap_{i=1}^{q}P_i\ne\varnothing.              \tag{3}
\]
If $F$ is UNSAT, some collection of at most $k+1$ source module IDs
already has empty projected intersection. This bound is optimal for
every $k\ge1$, even when each module has a physically explicit proper
width-three source encoding and the whole original source has both
SAT and UNSAT one-module-deletion controls.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (3) follows by projecting any global satisfying assignment to
the \emph{same} original shared tuple $b$; conversely, for a common $b$,
select independently one private witness $u_i$ for each module. Private
disjointness makes the witnesses jointly coherent.

For the obstruction size, a finite relation on Boolean coordinates is
definable by a (possibly long) Horn CNF precisely when it is closed under
coordinatewise meet; this includes the empty relation. Choose such a
Horn description for every $P_i$, associating each description clause
with the module ID that logically entails it. The union is UNSAT by
(3). Run complete Horn forward chaining, initially with every boundary
variable false. Each successful positive-head implication introduces
a \emph{new} true variable, so at most $k$ distinct implication clauses
are needed along the trace. UNSAT is witnessed when a negative Horn
clause (possibly empty) is violated by the resulting least model.
The $k$ triggering clauses plus the final negative clause are jointly
UNSAT, and each has a source module owner. At most $k+1$ distinct
modules suffice. This is an \emph{existence} argument, not a free
construction of the projected Horn descriptions.

For sharpness use the $k+1$ boundary conditions
$P_j=\{b:b_j=1\}$ for $j=1,\ldots,k$ and
$P_{k+1}=\{b:\neg b_1\vee\cdots\vee\neg b_k\}$.
Each is meet-closed: the latter excludes only the top Boolean vector.
All $k+1$ have empty intersection; deleting any one positive condition
allows its bit to be zero, and deleting the final negative condition
allows the all-ones tuple. Therefore every proper subfamily is
satisfiable. Each positive unit has an exact proper-3CNF existential
encoding on two private variables $p,q$:
\[
 \operatorname{Pin}(\ell;p,q)=
 (\ell\vee p\vee q)\wedge(\ell\vee p\vee\neg q)
 \wedge(\ell\vee\neg p\vee q)\wedge(\ell\vee\neg p\vee\neg q).
\]
It is equivalent to $\ell$ under existential quantification of $p,q$.
For $k=1$ the negative condition is another Pin gadget; for $k=2$ it
uses $(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_2\vee z)\wedge
(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_2\vee\neg z)$; for $k=3$ the negative clause
is proper ternary; and for $k=4$, two proper ternaries
$(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_2\vee t)$ and
$(\neg t\vee\neg x_3\vee\neg x_4)$ existentially realize the
negative four-literal disjunction. A standard private chain extends
this to all larger $k$. Thus the sharp examples have honest
source indices, original-variable consistency and source-preserved
SAT/UNSAT deletion polarities. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Non-Horn firewall]\label{prop:nonhorn}
Without meet closure, the $k+1$ module-core bound fails even for
$k=3$: the eight distinct proper ternary clauses, one falsified
by each of the eight assignments on $x_1,x_2,x_3$, are UNSAT together,
while every seven admit the omitted assignment.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each Boolean triple exactly its opposite-sign clause is false,
and all seven others are true. Thus all eight exclude every triple
and deleting one leaves precisely the omitted triple as witness.
The projections of some modules are not meet-closed. \qed
\end{proof}
A broader $2^k$ sharp unrestricted boundary obstruction was already
proved in project LSPV-3 SOURCE-BOUNDARY-AFFINE-HELLY129; this proposition
is an explicit consistency control, not a novelty claim.

\section{Original-clause extreme and both-label adversarial controls}
For each original proper ternary $C_j$ and its \emph{signed occurrences},
the raw file records its local $S_j$ requirement (every literal TRUE),
its inverted $U_j$, the local $U_j$ requirement (every literal FALSE),
and its inverted $S_j$. These are partial assignments to shared
\emph{original} variables. For original clause IDs $j<r$ the complete
matrix records $SS,SU,US,UU$ compatibility. The first and last modes
are feasible exactly when no opposite-sign variable is shared;
the middle two exactly when no same-sign variable is shared.
Both global coherent $0^n,1^n$ assignments, the intermediate flip trail,
original $t_j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ and its $N_0,\dots,N_3$ are retained.
Independent enumeration checked
\[
 t_j(\neg a)=3-t_j(a),\qquad
 t_j(a\oplus S)-t_j(a)=
 |S\cap\operatorname{false}_j(a)|-
 |S\cap\operatorname{true}_j(a)|.
\]
Every original-source non-tautological resolvent recorded with its
source parents forbids exactly its falsifying partial cube.
The four UNSAT sources additionally have complete original-clause
resolution refutations ending in the empty clause; each SAT source
has an original-clause-checked full witness. Two physically serialized
ordinary Shannon children per parent were separately checked, with
parent SAT exactly the OR of the two child answers. Failure at both
global endpoint assignments was never treated as UNSAT.

naive exhaustive projection table may require $2^k$ boundary rows
and exponential private-witness search. Materializing all $q2^k$
support rows costs $\Omega(q2^k)$ bits, while retaining all pair-mode
records costs $\Theta(m^2)$ records; these are \emph{not} within an
automatic $N^{1/2}$ workspace budget. Streaming, independent on-demand
recognition, or certified compressed summaries must be proved, not
declared free. Failed policies, alternative strategy selection, parent
tape/source reads, proof checking and the retained cache/ancestors
must enter \emph{one} machine realization.

For $k=4$ UNSAT, the source/child DIMACS lengths are 191 and 172/152
bytes. Child length is $\Theta(N)$ in the displayed direct-cofactor
architecture; no physical $N^{1/2}$ contraction was observed.
As in earlier same-source portfolio experiments, a correct total
fallback may restore SAT/UNSAT semantics but its unbounded time/space
charges remain, and no tested controller beats the baseline by a
uniformly comparable instruction accounting. In particular no
worst-case polynomial speedup follows.

\section{Duplication, contrast, machine-model barriers and root update}
\noindent\textbf{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.}
The $k+1$ witness mechanism is standard Horn logic, via the least-model
forward-chaining proof (Dowling--Gallier); no new Horn complexity
lower bound is claimed. LSPV-3 SOURCE-BOUNDARY-AFFINE-HELLY129
previously obtained the \emph{affine} $k+1$ obstruction and
\emph{arbitrary} $2^k$ sharp obstruction. LSPV-3 EXTREME-HELLY-WITNESS252
established 2-Helly only for unary local source extremes; those are
not ordinary disjunction SAT. LSPV-5 HITTING-TREE-COMPOSITION-REFEREE222
and SMALL-INTERFACE-EXACT-JOIN64 already established source-consistent
separator joins with charged decomposition discovery. The present
new \emph{project-scoped} result adds the sharp meet-closed/Horn
intermediate case with complete proper3CNF same-source proof transcripts.
LSPV-2 HORN-KROM-FOREST-INTERFACE274 demonstrates that syntactic
Horn and Krom tractability of components does \emph{not} permit
conjoining their answers. It is consistent with this theorem because
an exact common-boundary projection must be checked.

\noindent\textbf{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN.}
The available restricted compatibility witnesses distinguish Horn
projected relations, affine projections and arbitrary boundary relations;
the source-authenticated module count $k+1$ does not imply a fast
uniform selector. This is an incremental portfolio and resource
firewall, not a new overall proof spine.

\noindent\textbf{ADOPTED / REJECTED / RETIRED.}
Adopted: exact original-variable interface, complete clause ownership,
Horn closure certification, authentic resolution proofs, both-label
Shannon OR, charged discovery. Rejected: pairwise SAT tests, accidental
private-variable aliases, free projection descriptors, conflation
of semantic source compression with physically smaller ordinary
instances, and transfer of restricted Helly counts to arbitrary
algorithms.

\noindent\textbf{PRIMARY LITERATURE / BARRIERS.}
Horn tractability is classical \cite{DG,Schaefer}. Source-compatible
module joins are standard database/project--join reasoning
\cite{Yannakakis}. Backdoor recognition can have nontrivial cost
\cite{DOS}. The actual FLMV theorem forbids a simultaneous time--space
point on the matched indexed-input Turing machine \cite{FLMV},
not a combination of independently realized numbers. Neither
relativization/algebrization nor circuit natural-proof concerns is
resolved by this restricted theorem. A DELAY wrapper on any
compulsory parent-scale guest call violates the desired unknown-$K$
coefficient under the established opaque completed-call firewall;
the controller may avoid the call only by another fully charged,
correct strategy. The affine-small-bias MCSP theorem is retained
as a \emph{restricted semantic no-go}, not SAT hardness.

\section{Exact missing arrow and reproducibility}
If a uniform hereditary arbitrary-decider compiler really satisfied
(1), its conditional fixed point would be $(6/5,1/2)$, strictly
inside the independently certified FLMV region at
$c=241/200$, $d=11642479/23232400$.
That is algebra, \emph{not an implementation}. The sole load-bearing
\textbf{OPEN} arrow is a single, uniform, source-authenticated,
same-machine, all-input and both-label-correct controller that
produces certified terminals or power-short ordinary-SAT children
with $\le N^{3/5+o(1)}$ completed calls, pays for discovery,
selectors, source reads, failed attempts, replay and physical
input/index/work tapes, respects peak writable space, and is
hereditary under repeated $\Phi$. No such child-or-terminal
construction is available. Both $P=NP$ and $P\ne NP$ remain OPEN.
