\section*{Exact source and lower bounds}
Let $P_h$ be canonical signed unary PHP with $h+1$ pigeons and $h$ holes, each pigeon assigned at least one hole and no two pigeons assigned the same hole. For explicitly binary-indexed signed literal source encoding, its parameters are
\[
v_h=h(h+1),\quad m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2},\quad
L_h=h(h+1)^2,\quad N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2)).
\]
The input encoding is fixed, self-delimiting, and malformed sources are rejected. Haken (1985) proves
\[
\exists c>0\,\exists h_0\,\forall h\ge h_0\,
\forall\rho\in\mathrm{ResDAG}(P_h):|\rho|\ge2^{ch}.
\]
This includes unrestricted ordinary Resolution pivots, width and shared DAGs, with original source axioms free and at most $m_h$ free lines. It does not include Extended Resolution, Res$(\oplus)$, Rmix341, or any unrestricted SAT machine.

Cook (1976) constructs polynomial-size Extended Resolution refutations of the same original $P_h$. Binary-gate ER2 with fully paid two-input conservative DEF plus binary RES is a subgrammar of the preselected Rmix341, certified in REFEREE299/336. Therefore for some $C,d$ and every $h$, there exists an Rmix341 proof $\pi_h$ of the SAME $P_h$ with
\[
\mathrm{cost}_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(\pi_h)\le C(h+1)^d,\qquad
|\langle\pi_h\rangle|\le C(h+1)^d.
\]
This is a complete proof-bit bound, not only a node bound. No uncharged LOCAL-CONVERT internal wire is needed.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial same-source backtranslation is impossible]
There is no polynomial $p$ such that for every UNSAT source $F$ and every valid $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ refutation $\pi$ of it there exists an ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution proof $\rho$ of the identical $F$ satisfying
\[
|\rho|\le p(|\langle F\rangle|+|\langle\pi\rangle|).
\]
Even an uncomputable ideal backtranslator cannot achieve this bound. The assertion with paid mixed nodes in place of full mixed proof bits also fails.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $F=P_h$ and take the polynomial-bit mixed $\pi_h$ from Cook/ER2. Any fixed polynomial of $N_h+|\langle\pi_h\rangle|$ is $h^{O(1)}$. Haken says all ordinary DAG Resolution refutations of the same $P_h$ have at least $2^{ch}$ lines. Contradiction for all sufficiently large $h$. Replacing mixed proof bits by the polynomial mixed node count gives the same result.
\end{proof}
This proves a source-preserving proof-format separation, not an SAT computational lower bound. Required explicit ordinary Resolution output on these inputs has at least $2^{ch}-m_h$ derived records and bits, equivalently $2^{\Omega((N_h/\log N_h)^{1/3})}$ in the specified encoding. Output and checking work are exponentially large if that restricted format is demanded, while the original mixed proofs remain polynomial in bits and checkable in polynomial evidence-time. Intermediate encoded lengths, input and index reads, construction, output, verification, DAG scheduling and peak writable space must be separately charged.
