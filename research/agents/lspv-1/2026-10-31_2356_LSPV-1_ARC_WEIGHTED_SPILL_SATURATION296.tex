\section{Weighted saturation theorem}
Let $p$ be any coherent original-variable partial assignment, $f$ its free-variable count, and $u$ the number of original clauses not yet satisfied by pinned literals. Put $e=2f-u$. Let $s_x^\pm$ count the positive and negative free occurrences of variable $x$ in original clauses already satisfied by pins. Define
\[
\eta=\sum_{x\text{ free}}\min(s_x^+,s_x^-),\quad
w_x=|s_x^+-s_x^-|,\quad
b_x=\begin{cases}0&s_x^+>s_x^-\\1&s_x^->s_x^+\\ *&s_x^+=s_x^-.\end{cases}
\]
Construct the source-clause-indexed graph of uncovered monotone binary clauses, and certify any edge-disjoint packing $\mathcal C$ of $\nu$ odd cycles. Let $\delta=e-\eta-\nu$. The policy must pay to acquire every original-source count and cycle, including failed searches.
\begin{theorem}[Weighted signed-spill forbidden cubes]
For every valid canonical original source $F$, every coherent $p$, every authenticated edge-disjoint odd-cycle packing $\mathcal C$, and every satisfying full original-source extension $a\supseteq p$,
\[
\boxed{\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x\,\mathbf1[a_x\ne b_x]\le\delta.}\tag{1}
\]
If $\delta<0$, the entire cube $p$ is UNSAT. If $\delta\ge0$ and $w_x>\delta$, every satisfying completion forces $a_x=b_x$. More generally any further cube taking the opposite value on a set $X$ with $\sum_{x\in X}w_x>\delta$ cannot be extended. Failure to find a packing returns UNRESOLVED, not SAT or UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every free original variable has exactly two true signed occurrences under every full assignment. In any satisfying completion, source-occurrence conservation yields
\[
e=\sum_{j\in U(p)}(t_j(a)-1)+
\sum_{x\text{ free}}\bigl(s_x^+a_x+s_x^-(1-a_x)\bigr).
\]
Every uncovered clause has $t_j(a)\ge1$. The covered-occurrence sum equals $\eta+\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x\mathbf1[a_x\ne b_x]$. A satisfied uncovered monotone binary clause with equal-valued endpoints has both literals true and contributes one unit to $t_j(a)-1$. Each odd cycle requires an equal-valued edge; edge-disjoint cycles require distinct original clauses. Thus the first sum is at least $\nu$. Rearranging proves the theorem.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Zero-gap reverse propagation]
If $\delta=0$, every satisfying extension obeys all sign-imbalanced preferred $b_x$ values; each uncovered ternary clause is exact-one; each binary edge outside the selected cycle union has opposite-valued endpoints; and each chosen odd cycle has exactly one same-valued, both-true edge. These are sound necessary implications, not independently sufficient to assert SAT.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}All nonnegative terms in the preceding counting inequality must attain their minimum simultaneously.\end{proof}
