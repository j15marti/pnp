\section{A declared, fully charged eager two-level stage model}
For a fixed correct ordinary-SAT guest $A$ of joint exponents $(t,s)$,
consider source programs for stages $i,j$ with their own fixed parameters
$(a_k,\lambda_k,r_k,g_k,\eta_k)$, $0<\lambda_k<1$.
On \emph{each eligible ordinary input} of encoded length $L$, stage $k$
generates at most $L^{a_k+o(1)}$ ordinary self-contained children of
encoded length at most $L^{\lambda_k+o(1)}$; it incurs at most
$L^{r_k+o(1)}$ other work, $L^{\eta_k+o(1)}$ other peak state, and
$L^{g_k+o(1)}$ work per \emph{emitted} child bit including generation
and child-address logic. The two stages are correct \emph{as a composed
source-to-SAT decision algorithm}: both root labels must be reconstructed
from child labels with exact common source-name compatibility, and \emph{all}
grandchildren must actually satisfy the next stage's eligibility promise.
These semantic premises are \OPEN{} on unrestricted ordinary SAT.

Charge source parsing and rereads, all writable bit traffic including
unchanged writes, work and index head/address/control/arithmetic,
source-state discovery, switching and failed alternatives, every ordinary
child header and payload, source-name maps, guest calls, and any joins.
Place all independent parent work in $r_k$, actual generation cost in
$g_k$, stage scratch in $\eta_k$ and concurrently retained ancestor,
policy, cache and label state in $N^{\kappa+o(1)}$; exclude no parent-scale
SAT query from this ledger. Child buffers are writable/readable tape,
\emph{never} FLMV's free one-way output tape. Retain one child and one
grandchild along a depth-first path and discard previous siblings. All
space components are present in \emph{one} realization.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional two-stage eager ledger]\label{th:ledger}
Writing $A_{ij}=a_i+\lambda_i a_j$ and $H=\lambda_i\lambda_j$, the
composition, \emph{provided the preceding semantic and machine hypotheses
hold}, has a fixed uniform total ordinary-SAT machine implementation with
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 u_{ij}&\le\max\{r_i,\ a_i+\lambda_i+g_i,\
                a_i+\lambda_i r_j,\ A_{ij}+H+\lambda_i g_j,\
                A_{ij}+Ht\},\\
 v_{ij}&\le\max\{\kappa,\eta_i,\lambda_i,\
                \lambda_i\eta_j,\ H,\ Hs\}.
\end{aligned}}\tag{2}
\]
This is an \emph{upper certificate}. It is neither a necessary work lower
bound nor an unconditional ARC edge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the root, $N^{a_i}$ first children of length $N^{\lambda_i}$
are eagerly generated in at most $N^{a_i+\lambda_i+g_i+o(1)}$
work. There are $N^{a_i+\lambda_i a_j+o(1)}$ second-level calls,
whose input size is at most $N^{H+o(1)}$. Their eager generation requires
at most $N^{A_{ij}+H+\lambda_i g_j+o(1)}$ work and running the
actual guest requires $N^{A_{ij}+Ht+o(1)}$ work. The initial local
work is $N^{r_i}$ and the local work at all first-level children totals
$N^{a_i+\lambda_i r_j}$. Two live child buffers contribute
$N^{\lambda_i}$ and $N^H$ bits. Concurrent generator workspaces
contribute $N^{\eta_i}$ and $N^{\lambda_i\eta_j}$, the guest
$N^{Hs}$, and retained state $N^\kappa$. A depth-first schedule
reuses sibling buffers and charges all headers and renamings. Taking
maxima of this \emph{one execution's} contributions proves (2).
\end{proof}

\section{Conditional exchange rule and exact joint counterexample}
\begin{theorem}[Adjacent interchange; guest term only]\label{th:exchange}
For any fixed finite sequence of conditionally valid, \emph{semantically
interchangeable} stages with parameters $(a_k,\lambda_k)$ and
$0<\lambda_k<1$, let $P$ be the product of $\lambda$ values before
some adjacent pair $(i,j)$. The exponent of the terminal guest
invocation count changes under exchange by exactly
\[
  A_{\cdots ij\cdots}-A_{\cdots ji\cdots}
     =P\,[a_i(1-\lambda_j)-a_j(1-\lambda_i)].\tag{3}
\]
The leaf-input exponent $\prod\lambda_k$ is unchanged. Consequently,
\emph{among genuinely allowed reorderings}, sorting by
\[
                 \rho_k=\frac{a_k}{1-\lambda_k}
\quad\text{in nondecreasing order}\tag{4}
\]
minimizes the \emph{conditional terminal guest time exponent}
$A+ (\prod\lambda_k)t$. It does \emph{not} necessarily minimize
(2)'s total time or simultaneous space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each stage contributes its fanout exponent multiplied by the product
of all preceding length exponents. In two adjacent positions, the two
contributions are $P(a_i+\lambda_i a_j)$ or
$P(a_j+\lambda_j a_i)$. Later contributions have identical preceding
scale $P\lambda_i\lambda_j$, independent of order, proving (3).
Since $P>0$, the nondecreasing-ratio order removes all inverted
adjacent pairs without increasing $A$. Repeated adjacent swaps
terminate at the sorted order. This is an exchange proof for one
specified \emph{upper-bound functional}; it grants neither permission to
reorder semantic programs nor a favorable all-input machine.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A fully charged ordering reversal.}
Let stage $X$ have
$(a,\lambda,r,g,\eta)=(3/5,1/2,1,0,0)$,
and stage $Y$ have
$(1/10,4/5,2,0,0)$. At guest pair $(t,s)=(2,1)$ and
$\kappa=0$, both schedules have hypothetical leaf scale $H=2/5$.
The guest-count exponents are $13/20$ for $XY$ and $29/50$ for $YX$;
indeed $\rho_Y=1/2<\rho_X=6/5$, so sorting chooses $YX$.
But inserting \emph{all} stage costs in (2) yields
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
\text{order}&A+Ht&u_{\rm eager}&v_{\rm eager}\\\hline
XY&29/20&8/5&1/2\\
YX&69/50&2&4/5
\end{array}\tag{5}
\]
Thus the guest-optimal ordering is \emph{strictly worse in both}
charged time and peak space for this declared profile. The pair is
an exact counterexample to a policy \emph{certificate dominance}
claim, not a lower bound on optimal implementations.

\paragraph{Practical ARC consequence.}
A state-dependent strategy should
optimize a \emph{vector of fully charged upper certificates},
not a single branch-count statistic. The rule (4) can prune candidate
stage orders only after checking that exchange preserves exact SAT/UNSAT
semantics, contracted ordinary input encodings, local child eligibility,
and retained-stack bounds. An ordering heuristic cannot choose the
low-cost branch by asking an uncharged SAT oracle. Distinct stages and
fixed route depths may depend on the fixed source machine $A$ or its
finite diagonal $K$, never on uncharged length-specific advice.

\section{Two REAL ordinary-CNF recursion levels and hostile controls}
For two \emph{distinct} original source variables $x,y$, unit
restriction satisfies the identity
\[
 \SAT(F)=\bigvee_{b,c\in\{0,1\}}
            \SAT(F\restriction[x=b,y=c]),\tag{6}
\]
which is unchanged if $y$ is restricted before $x$. A satisfying
full assignment supplies one leaf witness; conversely a satisfying
leaf extends with its pinned original-name values. UNSAT means
\emph{every} leaf is UNSAT. This is exact even for shared-source
names because the leaf pin values are explicit. It does \emph{not}
validate joining independent modules by merely ANDing separate SAT bits.

The reproducer physically serializes the root as ordinary DIMACS CNF,
then produces \emph{two} serialized/reparsed first-level ordinary SAT
children and \emph{four} serialized/reparsed second-level ordinary SAT
grandchildren for \emph{each} order. It invokes an independent exhaustive
SAT evaluator on the four leaf formulas and on the root.
The 20 roots include explicit SAT and UNSAT guarded-quadrant families,
Horn/dual-Horn, 2-SAT, an eight-clause 3-variable parity-style UNSAT
cube, independent components, tautological padding, empty and unit
