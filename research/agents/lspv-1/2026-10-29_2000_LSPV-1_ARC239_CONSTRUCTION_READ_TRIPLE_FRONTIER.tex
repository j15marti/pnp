\section{Quantifiers and machine contract}
For arbitrary SAT modules $F=A(X,Y)\land B(X,Z)$ the boundary function is $f_A(x)=\exists y\,A(x,y)$. A reusable summary built before $B$ arrives is a different contract from a joint solver reading $(A,B)$ together. Its resource vector includes complete offline preparation time $P$, stored source-dependent bits $L$, online time $R$, raw original-source access $Q$, peak writable space $S$, communication rounds $M$, physical head crossings $H$ and cumulative writes $D$ on a specified machine. Retaining and sending the original formula are legal.

Our restricted control source is $A_a(X)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{b}(x_i=a_i)$, where $a\in\F^b$ appears in $b$ fixed-width authenticated unary source records, total physical input size $N_A=\Theta(b\log(b+2))$. The future continuation is $B_{w,t}=[w\cdot X=t]$, with a charged polynomial-size XOR3-chain CNF conversion if required. Thus $\operatorname{SAT}(A_a\land B_{w,t})\iff w\cdot a=t$. This is \emph{not} strict signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF. The declared indexed bit-RAM exposes individual original sign fields through counted named-coordinate reads; address arithmetic, input validation, output, and all other bit operations cost additional time. The original source remains read-only and available. There is no free answer-class bit, no source-dependent decoder advice, and no silently retained preprocessing transcript.

A deterministic total offline algorithm $E$ makes at most $p$ (possibly adaptive, repeated) original sign-field reads, then retains at most $L$ source-dependent bits. A single deterministic online decoder, receiving the state, arbitrary mask $w$, and target bit $t$, may make at most $Q$ additional adaptive source sign-field probes. It must be exact for \emph{every} $a,w,t$. No limit is placed on other polynomial workspace unless separately stated.
\section{All-adaptive same-source read coverage}
\begin{theorem}[Read coverage, including nonlinear adaptive preparation]\label{th:adaptive}
Every such exact encoder/decoder satisfies $\boxed{p+Q\ge b}$, irrespective of $L$. More strongly, for every fixed source $a$, if the \emph{actual} offline execution reads $r(a)$ distinct coordinates, there exists a later parity mask $w_a$ whose online execution on that same $a$ reads at least $b-r(a)$ previously untouched coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a source $a$ and its entire deterministic offline transcript. Let $R$ be the set of distinct coordinates read, $|R|=r(a)$; put $U=[b]\setminus R$. Every other source agreeing with $a$ on $R$ produces exactly the same offline transcript and persisted state, since each future offline query and response remains identical. Thus the state is constant on the full free subcube of $2^{|U|}$ choices for untouched coordinates. Choose future mask $w_a=1_U$. Suppose the online algorithm stops on source $a$ without reading some $j\in U$. Flip only $a_j$. The resulting source has the same offline and online transcripts, so the returned answer remains unchanged while the requested parity flips. Contradiction. Thus it reads every coordinate in $U$ on this same realization, so $Q(a,w_a)\ge b-r(a)$ and $p+Q\ge b$.
\end{proof}
The lower bound is sharp merely as a source-acquisition \emph{necessary condition}: reading and storing $p$ literal source bits and subsequently reading the requested $b-p$ others attains $(p,L,Q)=(p,p,b-p)$. It does not characterize every feasible triple, nor lower-bound arbitrary joint SAT computation.
\section{Fixed offline read set: a stronger nonlinear triple frontier}
Impose the \emph{additional explicit restriction} that all offline source dependence comes from a public source-independent set $R\subseteq[b]$ of size $p$. Write $U=[b]\setminus R$, $u=b-p$, and $\mathrm{Vol}(p,q)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(p,q)}\binom pj$ for $q\ge0$, zero otherwise.
\begin{theorem}[Fixed-read nonlinear preparation]\label{th:fixed}
For every such fixed-read encoder $E:\F^p\to\{0,1\}^L$, however nonlinear, and every exact deterministic adaptive online decoder,
\[
\boxed{Q\ge b-p,\qquad 2^L\mathrm{Vol}\bigl(p,Q-(b-p)\bigr)\ge 2^p,}
\]
and hence $L\ge p-\lfloor\log_2\mathrm{Vol}(p,Q-b+p)\rfloor$. The stronger $L$ inequality is \emph{not} claimed for an offline preprocessor whose read positions adapt to source contents.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a nonempty encoder fiber $F_\sigma\subseteq\F^p$ and any future mask $v$ on $R$, while selecting all-one mask on $U$. The summary is independent of $U$, and a one-bit flip argument forces every online transcript to read all $u$ coordinates of $U$. Fix the actual $U$ vector to zero and prune these forced reads from the online decision tree. The remaining tree computes every parity character $(-1)^{v\cdot a_R}$ on $F_\sigma$ using depth at most $q=Q-u$ coordinate reads from $R$. Every depth-$q$ coordinate decision tree expands as a real linear combination of Fourier characters supported on at most $q$ input bits, since each leaf indicator is a product of at most $q$ factors $(1\pm(-1)^{a_i})/2$. Thus all restricted parity characters lie in a space of dimension at most $\mathrm{Vol}(p,q)$. But restrictions of the full $2^p$ Walsh characters span all real functions on $F_\sigma$ by invertibility of the Walsh matrix, giving $|F_\sigma|\le\mathrm{Vol}(p,q)$. At most $2^L$ fibers partition $\F^p$, yielding $2^p\le2^L\mathrm{Vol}(p,q)$.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Sharp q=0,1 endpoints, known-code construction]
At $Q=b-p$, $L=p$ is necessary and sufficient. For every $p\ge1$, at $Q=b-p+1$ the exact nonlinear optimum is
\[
\boxed{L_\star=p-\lfloor\log_2(p+1)\rfloor.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first endpoint stores the $p$ read bits. For the second, let $r=\lfloor\log_2(p+1)\rfloor$ and construct a public $r\times p$ binary matrix $H$ whose columns include all $2^r-1$ nonzero $r$-bit syndromes. The nullspace $C=\ker H$ has dimension $p-r$. Read all $p$ offline source bits, store dot products against a public basis of $C$, and discard any other source-dependent work state. For future mask $v=w_R$, compute $Hv$. If $Hv=0$, $v\in C$ and its parity is computable from the summary. Otherwise select a column $He_i=Hv$; then $v+e_i\in C$, so its parity is known from the summary and one additional source probe of $a_i$ recovers $v\cdot a_R$. Probe the needed untouched coordinates of $U$, at most $u$ more. Theorem~\ref{th:fixed} gives the matching nonlinear lower bound. The Hamming construction itself was already established in ARC237.
\end{proof}
For a fixed linear summary with rowspace $C\subseteq\F^p$, the exact worst-case online probes are $u+\rho(C)$, where $\rho$ is covering radius. The upper bound follows by nearest-codeword reconstruction; the lower bound follows because each transcript must first read $U$, and a queried set $J\subseteq R$ can determine target parity only if $v$ lies in $C+\operatorname{span}\{e_j:j\in J\}$. This uses LSPV-2's established distance theorem and adds forced unprepared reads.
\section{Frontier crossings, charged upper bounds, and context value}
For the one-extra-probe branch $1\le p\le b$,
\[
L(p)=p-\lfloor\log_2(p+1)\rfloor,\quad Q(p)=b-p+1.
\]
A weighted \emph{probe/storage proxy}, not measured runtime, is $C(p)=\alpha p+\beta L(p)+\gamma Q(p)$. Its exact discrete increment is
\[
C(p+1)-C(p)=\alpha+\beta-\gamma-\beta\mathbf 1_{\{p+2\text{ power of }2\}}.
\]
The smooth relaxation has $C_{\mathrm{cont}}'(p)=\alpha+\beta-\gamma-\beta/((p+1)\ln 2)$ and $C_{\mathrm{cont}}''(p)=\beta/((p+1)^2\ln2)\ge0$. Check integer points rather than claiming the continuous optimum is realized. Concrete attainable point $(b,p,L,Q)=(15,11,8,5)$; its integer storage lower bound is eight although $2^8\mathrm{Vol}(11,1)>2^{11}$, so there is no false claim of unrounded sphere equality.

The explicit indexed bit-RAM implementation authenticates input records, reads all $p$ chosen original signs, builds $H$ uniformly, stores $L$ parity bits, decodes a future query by syndrome and basis arithmetic, probes at most $Q$ sign fields, and verifies output. A conservative charged upper bound is $P=O((N_A+b^2)\log^2(b+2))$ offline and $R=O((N_B+b^2)\log^2(b+2))$ online, $S=O(b^2)$ peak writable bits, with $L$ persistent source-dependent bits; the read-only original source occupies $N_A$ physical bits. One-tape crossings, two-tape changes, fixed UTM interpretation, and native exponents are separate, not inferred from bit-RAM probes. Failed strategies and adaptive switches, if introduced, must be charged.

For contextual data $I\mid_{v_\Phi}$, define independently $\Phi_W(K)$ on authenticated facts and $v_\Phi(I\mid K)=\Phi_W(K)-\Phi_W(K\cup\{I\})$, retaining separate acquisition, verification, scoring and storage costs. The identities $v_\Phi(I\mid K,I)=0$ (no changed state), $\sum_jv_\Phi(I_j\mid K_{j-1})=\Phi(K_0)-\Phi(K_m)$, and
\[
v_\Phi(I\mid K,J)-v_\Phi(I\mid K)
=\Phi(K,I)+\Phi(K,J)-\Phi(K)-\Phi(K,I,J)
\]
are direct exact algebra. They do not make initial singleton values additive. For target $t=u\oplus v$, either certified fact $(u=0)\mid_{0}$ or $(v=0)\mid_{0}$ initially leaves the target binary, but the second after the first has value one. For realized Shannon entropy, a one-true-in-eight target has entropy $h_2(1/8)$; a certified event selecting that true world and one false world increases posterior entropy to one bit, so realized value $h_2(1/8)-1\approx-0.456435557$. This does not contradict nonnegative *expected* information gain. No target entropy or value label is a lower bound on SAT runtime without an independently proved step-cost and endpoint criterion.
