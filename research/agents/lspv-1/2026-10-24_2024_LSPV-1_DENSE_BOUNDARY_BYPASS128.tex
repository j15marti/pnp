\section{Exact proper-3CNF generator}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(p_i\vee q_i\vee r_i)$ be proper ordered 3CNF with $n$ used IDs and $m\ge1$. Introduce guard $g$ and private $a_i$:
\[
\Gamma_g(F)=\bigwedge_i[(\neg g\vee p_i\vee a_i)\wedge(\neg a_i\vee q_i\vee r_i)].\tag{1}
\]
For $z_1,\ldots,z_d$, enforce $g=z_1\vee\cdots\vee z_d$. The proper-3CNF padding identity is
\[
{\rm Pad}(u,v;e)=(u\vee v\vee e)\wedge(u\vee v\vee\neg e)
\]
with fresh $e$. At $d=1$ pad $(\neg g\vee z_1)$ and $(g\vee\neg z_1)$. For $d\ge2$, let $h_1=z_1$, $h_d=g$ and use fresh intermediate $h_i$ for $2\le i<d$. Each $h_i=h_{i-1}\vee z_i$ is encoded by
\[
{\rm Pad}(\neg h_{i-1},h_i;e_i)\wedge
{\rm Pad}(\neg z_i,h_i;f_i)\wedge(h_{i-1}\vee z_i\vee\neg h_i).\tag{2}
\]
Call this $D_{F,d}$. Every clause has three distinct IDs, all original shared variable IDs are preserved and the non-isolated incidence graph is connected. Exact counts:
\[
d=1:(n_D,M_D)=(n+m+4,2m+4);
\]
\[
d\ge2:(n_D,M_D)=(n+m+4d-3,2m+5(d-1)).\tag{3}
\]
Canonical bit length is $O((m+d)\log(n+m+d))$ after removing unused IDs. All output headers, literals, source reads, auxiliary IDs and construction work are charged. This is not signed exact-(2,2).
\begin{theorem}[Dense projected hardness, trivial root]
For boundary $b\in\{0,1\}^d$ let $Q_{F,d}(b)$ be satisfiability after pinning $z=b$ and existentially quantifying every other variable. Then
\[
\boxed{Q_{F,d}(b)=[b=0^d]\vee{\rm SAT}(F).}\tag{4}
\]
If $F$ is UNSAT, the feasible boundary support has one word, while the $2^d-1$ nonzero memberships equal SAT($F$); if $F$ is SAT, the support is all of $\{0,1\}^d$. Nevertheless $D_{F,d}$ is SAT with every variable set to zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Padding enforces the binary clause and the OR chain forces $g=\bigvee b_j$. For $g=0$, $a_i=0$ satisfies each guarded pair regardless of $F$. For $g=1$, eliminating $a_i$ from its pair yields the source clause $p_i\vee q_i\vee r_i$. All displayed proper-3CNF clauses contain a negative literal, so every clause holds at the all-zero assignment.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Shared-query ceiling]
One evaluation of $b_F={\rm SAT}(F)$ determines the entire boundary function via the $O(d)$-gate circuit $[z=0^d]\vee b_F$. Thereafter a pinned query requires $O(d)$ bit operations; an explicit truth table costs $O(2^d d)$ further output work, not $2^d$ independent source decisions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Equation (4) gives the circuit directly; no speed bound on acquiring $b_F$ is assumed.\end{proof}
