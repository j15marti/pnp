\section*{Fixed calculus, evidence and quantifiers}
Preserve unchanged sound, complete, source-authenticated $R=R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$: free original signed general-CNF clauses; every distinct permitted RES, exact local AND3, binary GF$(2)$ XOR, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and source-independent conservative signed two-input VARDEF inference node costs one. Shared DAG nodes cost once. Adaptive rules, auxiliaries and temporary growth are allowed. No hidden SAT oracle or free semantic output is introduced. Define
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}|\pi_{\rm derived}|,\quad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ {\rm valid\ UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F),
\]
with an empty maximum of zero; $N$ counts input \emph{bits}. The positive integral weights and complete Resolution fragment guarantee a minimum.

\textbf{Normalization premise.} Use LSPV-4 NODE-EVIDENCE-DOMINATION342 for the preselected \emph{bounded-output} grammar: every $q$-node valid refutation admits a normalized certificate of at most $C(N+q+2)^d$ bits, with a uniform verifier polynomial-time in its input and certificate length. Every accepted certificate is genuinely sound. Do not silently extend this premise to arbitrary unit-cost circuit outputs of unbounded implicit gate count: the LSPV-4 LOCAL-CONVERT typing challenge remains a separate unresolved grammar issue.

\begin{theorem}[Unary-budget optimum and certificate extraction]
For each binary $x$ and unary integer budget $1^b$, let
\[
\mathcal B_R=\{(x,1^b):x=\operatorname{enc}(F)\text{ valid and }W_R(F)\le b\}.
\]
Under the stated normalization premise, $\mathcal B_R\in NP$ unconditionally. Assuming $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$, a fixed total deterministic algorithm, polynomial in $|x|+b+1$, decides $\mathcal B_R$ and, when YES, emits a \emph{minimum-cost} genuine $R$ contradiction DAG of exactly $W_R(F)$ paid nodes. For NO, it reports only that no proof meets the budget, without classifying $F$ as SAT; malformed sources are independently rejected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Parse $x$, guess an at-most-$C(|x|+b+2)^d$-bit normalized proof record, check every source reference, legal rule, auxiliary gate, signed pivot, contradiction, and the charged count $\le b$. Soundness rejects forged refutations; normalization supplies a witness whenever $W_R(F)\le b$. The relation is in NP. Under a polynomial SAT decider $A$, Cook--Levin constructs in polynomial $|x|+b$ time a satisfiable CNF precisely when the polynomial proof-verifier circuit accepts some witness. A polynomial number of self-reduction calls to $A$ recovers all witness bits. Extract the certificate and independently rerun the verifier. Binary search on the \emph{integer budget interval} $[0,b]$ finds the least feasible value, and witness extraction at that value produces a minimum-DAG refutation. A given empty original source clause needs zero paid nodes. SAT and malformed sources never generate a sound false contradiction. This is a conditional implementation whose acquisition, indexing, conversion and checking work are charged in its polynomial-in-$|x|+b$ bound. \end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional existence-to-effective-production transfer]
If $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ and B3 for this same fixed $R$ hold, there is one polynomial-time total solver which emits a checked satisfying assignment for every valid SAT input, a checked $R$ refutation for every valid UNSAT input, and an error on malformed input. Conversely, any such polynomial-time checked proof-producing solver implies $\overline W_R(N)=N^{O(1)}$, even without assuming $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$ separately.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
B3 supplies constants $C,k$ with $W_R(F)\le C(N+1)^k$ for all UNSAT $F$. Hardcode budget $\lceil C(N+1)^k\rceil$ and apply the theorem, using $A$ plus standard witness self-reduction for SAT inputs. Conversely, printing the entire genuine certificate in polynomial time bounds the number of paid nodes by output bit length and therefore bounds $\overline W_R$ polynomially. \end{proof}

\textbf{Quantifier firewall.} Under $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$, B3 also implies B1 for every correct decider $A$, because $W_R(F)\le C(N+1)^k\le C(N+T_A(F)+1)^k$, regardless of the actual runtime of $A$. B1 or B2 for one polynomial-time SAT decider conversely implies B3 by substitution of its all-input worst-case polynomial runtime. Thus, conditional on SAT in P, all three have equivalent truth values, but this \emph{does not establish any of them} from SAT in P. Proof existence, effective acquisition, and arbitrary-machine decision cost are not equated unconditionally.
