\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, independent review pending.}
A probabilistic verifier with constant local proof queries does not supply a globally sound accepting certificate.
The necessary observed-information budget for a deterministic verification is the physical union over all random seeds, not a per-seed maximum.

\section{Falsifiers proposed before source inspection}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] H1. Accepting one random seed is a sound UNSAT certificate.
\textbf{FALSIFIED} on a signed exact-(2,2) source.
\item[] H2. One proof query per seed yields one global proof-bit query.
\textbf{FALSIFIED} by disjoint proof addresses.
\item[] H3. Deterministic all-seed aggregation preserves soundness with a charged $R$ multiplier. \textbf{PROVED} below.
\item[] H4. The resulting first-read enumeration composes with the Williams cosine bound for subpolynomial space. \textbf{PROVED under explicit conditions}.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{PRIMARY LITERATURE.} Arora--Safra (1998) and Dinur (2007)
provide probabilistically checkable proof constructions for NP.
Their local-query property is probabilistic, not accepting-seed soundness.
Under hypothetical P=NP, UNSAT is in NP and inherits some PCP, but
no favorable exponent is thereby fixed. Williams (2013), in the
author's corrected random-access time-space analysis, gives the
deterministic SAT $N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}$ lower bound for
$N^{o(1)}$ space. Its earlier universal cosine product claim is retired.
FLMV (2005) supplies a different positive-space strict indexed region.

\section{Uniform indexed machine and exact quantifiers}
For each ordinary CNF source $F$ of physical length $N$, fix ONE
uniform total deterministic indexed-RAT checker $V(F,\pi,z)$ with
binary proof $\pi$ of publicly fixed length $L(N)\le N^p$,
and random seed $z\in\{0,1\}^{h(N)}$.
Its physical proof-store plus bit-address map is stable, uniform,
and charged. Public polynomially bounded indices and a uniform clock
prevent hidden advice channels. Write
\[
R=2^{h(N)}\le N^{\rho+o(1)},\qquad
T_V\le N^{v+o(1)},\qquad S_V\le N^{s+o(1)}. \tag{1}
\]
All three ceilings apply together to the SAME $V$, on every
source, seed and proof, including failed branches.
Let fixed $0\le\sigma<c\le1$ satisfy
\[
\begin{split}
\forall F\in{\sf SAT}\;\forall\pi:
&\quad \sum_{z=0}^{R-1} V(F,\pi,z)\le\sigma R,\\
\forall F\in{\sf UNSAT}\;\exists\pi:
&\quad \sum_{z=0}^{R-1} V(F,\pi,z)\ge cR .
\end{split} \tag{2}
\]
For a fixed proof, let $Q_z(F,\pi)$ be the set of distinct physical
binary proof cells read during seed $z$. The \emph{global} observed
proof budget is
\[
Q_{\rm all}(F,\pi)=\left|\bigcup_{z=0}^{R-1}Q_z(F,\pi)\right|. \tag{3}
\]
Assume independently, for EVERY ordinary UNSAT source, that
there is a proof satisfying (2) \emph{and}
\[
Q_{\rm all}(F,\pi)\le(a+o(1))\log_2 N. \tag{4}
\]
Local per-seed queries do not imply (4). A child-local proof position
is not a physical key unless its store and offset mapping is
injective and paid. All source authentication, local discovery,
virtual access, child creation, resets and failed trials count
within $V$.
