\section*{Proof of a restricted affine-sketch interface theorem}
Use $B\ge1$ earlier authenticated Boolean wires $u_j$ and an \emph{explicit syntactic} affine sketch of size $k$:
\[
s_i(u)=c_i\oplus\bigoplus_{j=1}^{B}a_{ij}u_j,\quad a_{ij},c_i\in\{0,1\}.
\]
An acyclic private Boolean DAG accesses \emph{only} these $k$ sketch wires. Let $r\ge1$ named observed outputs be sketch or private wires. All later operations must be typed, source-justified, and extensional in their entire public vector, with no hidden source/identity access. Signed-AND2 conservative definitions are paid one per gate; negated references are aliases.

\begin{theorem}
For every such $B,k,r$ and every valid two-stage circuit, there exists one shared signed-AND2 replacement bank with the identical entire observable output vector for every $u\in\{0,1\}^{B}$, including all cross-output correlations, and paid definition count
\[
d(Q)\le1+3k(B-1)+rk2^k,
\]
plus at most $r$ separately charged aliases if required by the target grammar. This is independent of the private circuit's size, and does not presume upstream boundary-wire independence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
One paid gate $u_1\wedge\neg u_1$ supplies false. Signed refs and three paid gates $t_1=\neg a\wedge\neg b$, $t_2=a\wedge b$, $z=\neg t_1\wedge\neg t_2$ compute each XOR. Thus all $k$ affine rows require at most $3k(B-1)$ paid gates. Evaluate the private DAG on all $2^k$ sketch inputs. For each observed function, realize its exact truth table by DNF: each true row costs at most $k-1$ AND gates, and signed-De-Morgan OR unions cost at most one per join, for at most $k2^k$ paid gates per output; false reuses the shared false gate. All public values are identical on every sketch input, hence on every original boundary assignment, with joint correlations preserved. Sum distinct gates once.
\end{proof}
