\section*{Exact published lower bound, source, and representation}
Set $n=2^\ell$, $\ell\ge32$. The standard bit pigeonhole CNF $B_\ell$ has $n+1$ pigeons each represented by $\ell$ Boolean bits. For each pair of pigeons and each of the $n$ possible holes a single proper $2\ell$-literal source clause forbids their simultaneous assignment to that hole. The source therefore has $v=(n+1)\ell$ named original variables and $m=n\binom{n+1}{2}$ authenticated clauses. Its explicit signed ASCII-DIMACS encoded bit length is $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$; $N_\ell$ is neither $v$ nor the number of clauses. Each source clause is also a legitimate disjunction of affine literals in Res$(\oplus)$.

Braun (2026, arXiv:2609.23015) reports, for every $\ell\ge32$ and every \emph{unrestricted DAG} Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of this particular original source, including source lines,
\[
 L_{\oplus}(B_\ell)>\exp\left(\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}\right).\tag{1}
\]
This is not limited to tree-like, regular or bounded-depth proofs. It is a preprint with a linked Lean formalization, not an independently rechecked peer-reviewed theorem in this run. Standard Res$(\oplus)$ lines are disjunctions of affine $\mathbb F_2$ equations, with semantic weakening and resolution on complementary affine literals. Itsykson--Sokolov (2020) prove that its semantic two-premise variant is polynomially equivalent to standard Res$(\oplus)$ and that the system is implicationally complete.

The unchanged \emph{full} project system $R_{\rm mix341}$ retains zero-cost authentic source clauses, unit-paid ordinary signed RES, exact AND3 on at most three variables, global binary GF$(2)$ row addition, justified LOCAL-CONVERT and charged conservative two-input signed VARDEF. A shared derived DAG node costs one regardless of whether it shrinks a state; a minimum is taken over all legal proofs with free ideal proof selection. The lower bound (1) is not initially a bound on this full calculus.

\section*{Pin an audited proper subgrammar, not a new full system}
Define $R_{\rm aff3}$ to be the following \emph{restricted legal fragment} of the already fixed $R_{\rm mix341}$, with all names drawn only from the original variables. It permits strict RES between ordinary signed clauses; GF$(2)$ row addition between two explicitly represented \emph{unit affine equations}; and the existing sound local AND3/LOCAL-CONVERT factor steps \emph{only} when the union of all input/output supports is contained in one common set of at most three original variables, with explicit truth tables and legal output typing. No fresh variables, paid VARDEF, uncharged internal circuit wires, arbitrary large-support factor conversion, hidden semantic implication rule or instance-dependent axiom is permitted. The fragment includes genuine wide GF$(2)$ rows but excludes the extension/circuit features that defeat pigeonhole hardness. Native strict RES is still complete for UNSAT; minima exist with positive integer charges. Ordinary clause and affine equation nodes are represented by their linear clause, and a three-variable factor is \emph{translated} to its exact false-row blocker CNF of at most eight ordinary linear clauses. This translation does not add a free R-rule.

\begin{lemma}[Absolute bounded-support compilation]
There exists a finite, effectively precomputable constant $C_3$ such that any single legal paid $R_{\rm aff3}$ inference, given translated authenticated antecedent lines, can be compiled to at most $C_3$ additional \emph{syntactic} Res$(\oplus)$ inferences yielding the translated conclusion lines. The same derived bundles may be shared by later DAG nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Native ordinary RES is one Res$(\oplus)$ resolution. From unit affine equations $(f=\alpha)$ and $(g=\beta)$, weaken the first to
\[
 (f+g=\alpha+\beta)\vee(g=1-\beta)
\]
and resolve with $(g=\beta)$, costing exactly two Res$(\oplus)$ steps. Every other permitted local inference involves at most three names total, and at most two premise factors of at most eight false-row blockers each. Each required output blocker (at most eight), or the local output clause/affine row, is a consequence of this finite set of input linear clauses, by the legal rule's soundness. Implicational completeness of Res$(\oplus)$ supplies a finite derivation of each output. Up to three-variable renamings there are only finitely many factor relations, antecedent configurations and linear-clause conclusions. Therefore the maximum minimum syntactic derivation length over all legal local cases exists and is finite; exhaustive legal-proof enumeration gives an effective, uniform constant table. Let $C_3$ dominate that maximum and two. This reasoning does not assert an optimized numerical value or an instance-specific oracle, and never inspects the entire SAT source semantically.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Published hardness transfers to the affine/local subgrammar]
Every $q$-paid-node $R_{\rm aff3}$ refutation of an $m$-source UNSAT CNF $F$ induces a Res$(\oplus)$ refutation of \emph{the same original $F$} with at most $m+C_3q$ total lines. Consequently, conditional on Braun's stated theorem, every such refutation of $B_\ell$ obeys
\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
q>\frac{\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))-n\binom{n+1}{2}}{C_3}
=\exp(\Omega(n/\ell^2))
=\exp\!\left(\Omega(N_\ell^{1/3}/(\log N_\ell)^{8/3})\right)}
\tag{2}
\]
for sufficiently large $\ell$. Hence $\overline W_{R_{\rm aff3}}(N)$ is superpolynomial \emph{only for this fixed restricted calculus}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Topologically replay each paid shared node once via the lemma, retaining each original source clause and the previously derived bundle for every used parent. The target proof has at most $m+C_3q$ total lines. Apply (1), then subtract the polynomial $m$. Since $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ and $\ell=\Theta(\log N_\ell)$, the stated input-bit form follows without any source encoding change. All legal shared proofs are covered, not only an optimizer's selected derivation. The production/verification work of a compiler is separate from the logical minimum.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Precise missing transfer.} $R_{\rm aff3}\subset R_{\rm mix341}$ implies $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F)\le W_{R_{\rm aff3}}(F)$, the \emph{opposite} inequality from that required to infer full mixed hardness. Existing polynomial paid-ER bit-PHP refutations and the exact $4h-1$ mixed Tseitin XOR proof are mandatory countercontrols. Thus any polynomial-node full mixed proof of this bit-PHP source must use some excluded rule/interface; we do not lower-bound the number of such operations. A polynomial-source/charge-preserving compiler eliminating \emph{all} paid definitions and large circuit converters remains unproved, as does any arbitrary-SAT machine runtime lower bound. B1, B2 and B3 for $R_{\rm mix341}$ are OPEN.
