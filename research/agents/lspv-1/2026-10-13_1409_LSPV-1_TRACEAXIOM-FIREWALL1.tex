\section{Primary-literature and obstacle preflight}
The two current obstacles are: (i) a one-bit deterministic run is not a proof
in a preselected lower-bounded system; and (ii) elimination or translation of
computation extension variables can be exponentially expensive.

Cook--Reckhow provide the correct framework for propositional proof systems
and polynomial simulations, but do not identify arbitrary decision traces
with proofs in a fixed weak system \cite{cookreckhow,krajicek}.  G\"o\"os,
Koroth, Mertz, and Pitassi prove that finding Cutting Planes proofs can be
NP-hard even relative to shortest-proof length, separating proof verification,
search, and decision \cite{gkmp}.  Buss--Tur\'an record generalized
pigeonhole formulas with exponential Resolution lower bounds but polynomial
extended-Resolution proofs, so auxiliary definitions cannot be silently
eliminated from Resolution with polynomial overhead \cite{bussturan}.
Fleming et al. obtain translations between named branch-and-cut systems only
under explicit hypotheses and quantitative losses \cite{fleming}; such a
translation is a theorem to prove, not a property of a trace.

\section{Exact fixed-trace theorem}
Let \(C\) be a finite acyclic Boolean circuit with inputs fixed to \(y\),
output variable \(o\), and gates from
\(\{\mathsf{AND},\mathsf{OR},\mathsf{XOR},\mathsf{NOT},\mathsf{COPY}\}\).
Let \(b=C(y)\).  Let \(T(C,y)\) be the standard Tseitin CNF containing input
unit clauses and constant-width gate-equivalence clauses.  Let \(\bar o_b\)
be the unit literal asserting \(o=1-b\).

\begin{theorem}[fixed-trace axiom collapse]
For every \((C,y)\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(T(C,y)\wedge\bar o_b\) has a unit-Resolution refutation using
  \(O(|C|+|y|)\) clauses and propagation steps;
\item under the actual evaluation substitution \(\rho_{C,y}\), every clause
  of \(T(C,y)\) is true and \(\bar o_b\) alone becomes the empty clause;
\item the statement is independent of whether \(y\) encodes a YES or a NO
  instance of any external language.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The input units assign the inputs.  In topological order, each constant-width
gate equivalence unit-propagates the unique correct output once its arguments
are assigned.  Thus the propagation derives \(o=b\), contradicting the unit
\(o=1-b\), with constant work per gate.  Under \(\rho_{C,y}\), the input units
and all equivalences hold by definition of circuit evaluation, while the
opposite-output unit is false.  No fact about an external formula was used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[premise firewall]
The natural evaluation projection does not produce a derivation of
unsatisfiability from the clauses of an input formula \(H\).  It deletes all
computation axioms as true and leaves only the false output assumption.  The
same short contradiction can be produced on a satisfiable input by asserting
the opposite of its correct output.
\end{corollary}

The corollary does not rule out every machine-to-proof theorem.  It falsifies
the specific natural candidate \textsf{FIXED-TRACE-IS-INPUT-PROOF1}.

\section{The genuinely additional implication}
Let \(z\) range over assignments to the variables of \(H\).  The fixed
circuit \(C_{A,y}\) has no free \(z\)-variables.  A proof of \(H\)'s
unsatisfiability must instead add:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{correctness internalization:} a quantitatively bounded
  derivation connecting ``\(z\) satisfies \(H\)'' to the correct output of
  \(A(y)\), rather than accepting the terminal bit as an axiom; and
\item \textbf{extension elimination/model translation:} a theorem mapping
  that circuit derivation to the exact lower-bounded \(\sCP\)/triangle system
  with controlled size, depth, coefficients, construction time, replay, and
  joint workspace.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the proof map is
\[
 \underbrace{\text{fixed execution}\Rightarrow
 \text{wrong-output trace contradiction}}_{\EXACT}
 \not\Rightarrow
 \underbrace{\text{proof of }\neg H}_{\OPEN}
 \not\Rightarrow
 \underbrace{\sCP\text{/triangle proof}}_{\OPEN}.              \tag{3}
\]
DIAGTRANSFER1 shows that exponent-indexed families are sufficient after a
transfer with depth exponent \(q(K)\) is proved.  Equation (3), not the later
diagonal choice, is the present load-bearing gap.

\section{Hostile controls and barriers}
Horn, 2-SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded-treewidth, and independent-product
computations all receive the same easy wrong-output trace contradiction, so
its length cannot measure semantic input hardness.  Padding and alternative
encodings only alter the fixed circuit.  COPY, DELAY, DEAD, INTERP, and RECOMP
do not add the missing correctness derivation.  Compact circuits and integer
counting/flow are allowed; no expanded-monomial PC/PCR size is imported.

With oracle gates, fixing realized oracle replies preserves the fixed-trace
theorem while leaving reply correctness external.  The firewall therefore
relativizes.  Algebraic gate equations likewise vanish under the actual
evaluation, leaving the false output equation; there is no nonalgebrizing
hinge.  No constructive circuit property is proposed, so Natural Proofs is
not the immediate obstruction.

Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, syzygies, and collision entropy are
not the object in (3), hence are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} here.  External
black-box extraction inspired by LWE is an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} needing
a new unconditional reduction.  No cryptographic hardness or random-oracle
assumption is used.
