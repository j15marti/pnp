\section{Machine and semantic contract}
An original Boolean instance is a finite signed clause list $F$, with original
occurrence $x_{i,j}$ denoting variable $x_i$ in source clause $C_j$ and
\emph{not} a new independent variable. At every step the residual state is
\[
 z=(F,\rho,\text{source indexes},\text{authenticated implications},
       \text{cache},\text{policy history},\text{representation},\text{ledger}).
\]
The dynamic parameter vector records residual clause and bit size, observed
maximum occurrence degree, certified tractable grammar, discovered interfaces,
source-access count, child lengths, failed methods, retained bits and peak
writable space. Computing any such entry is paid; a semantic selector or
answer bit is never free.

For a variable $x$ not pinned by $\rho$, correct ordinary-child branching is
\[
 \SAT(F\mid\rho)
 =\SAT(F\mid\rho,x=0)\lor\SAT(F\mid\rho,x=1). \tag{1}
\]
A parent NO requires both children NO. A unit-propagation inference $F\mid\rho\models
(x=b)$ means that the opposite cube $\rho\cup\{x=1-b\}$ is certified NO by
original-source clauses; its mass cannot silently disappear. A complete guest
$A$ on a physical ordinary residual preserves both labels. An incomplete
strategy returning UNKNOWN is neither a NO answer nor an UNSAT certificate.

For the structural theorem only, use fixed-width source clause records of
$h_n=2+3(1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil)$ bits and a separately charged
$O(\log N)$ header, where $N=m h_n+O(\log N)$ is parent bit size. Original
three-literal clauses retained untouched after restriction occupy an entire
$h_n$-bit record in an ordinary clause-list child. The input is read-only;
materialized child payloads, transcript, cached answers, writable source
indices and stacks are counted as writable space. This is a \emph{declared
restricted representation}, not an invariant of equivalent circuits,
Tseitin gadgets, semantic compression, or arbitrary SAT machines.

\section{Exact source-certified prefix-cover theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Adaptive direct-cofactor Kraft bound]\label{th:main}
Let $F$ contain $m$ proper width-three clauses, each variable occurring in
at most $\Delta\ge1$ source clauses, with $n$ original variables. Let
$0<\lambda<1$ be fixed, and define
\[
 k_N=\max\left\{0,\left\lceil
 \frac{m-\lfloor N^\lambda/h_n\rfloor}{\Delta}
 \right\rceil\right\}.                                           \tag{2}
\]
For every deterministic \emph{explicit finite} source-authenticated
binary decision/forced-literal tree for an \emph{UNSAT} $F$, assume every
nonterminal guest leaf queries an exact ordinary SAT decider on the pure
syntactically restricted $F\mid\rho$, physically encoded with unchanged
three-literal source records, and its physical length is at most
$N^\lambda$. Every other leaf has a verified source-only NO derivation;
all forced-literal opposite branches are counted as source-certified NO.
If $\mathcal G$ is the set of \emph{logical guest leaves}, then the
uniform Boolean-cube measures satisfy
\[
 \mu_{\rm guest}=\sum_{g\in\mathcal G}2^{-|\rho_g|}
 \le |\mathcal G|2^{-k_N},\qquad
 \boxed{\mu_{\rm source\,NO}\ge1-|\mathcal G|2^{-k_N}.}            \tag{3}
\]
In particular, if no source-only NO leaf exists, the tree has at least
$2^{k_N}$ logical guest leaves. For an explicit tree that visits and records
every guest leaf with at least one counted step, an exact local time bound
$T_{\rm local}(N)$ yields the weaker but cache-resilient
\[
 \mu_{\rm source\,NO}\ge1-T_{\rm local}(N)2^{-k_N}.     \tag{4}
\]
These statements do not hold with $|\mathcal G|$ automatically replaced by
the number of \emph{distinct completed physical guest calls} in a memoizing
or symbolic-DAG implementation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any path pinning $r$ distinct original variables affects at most
$\Delta r$ original clause records. Hence at least $m-\Delta r$
three-literal records remain untouched, and its ordinary restricted child
has at least $(m-\Delta r)h_n$ physical bits. The guest-size premise forces
$r\ge k_N$ at every guest leaf.

Expand each certified propagation $F\mid\rho\models x=b$ into a virtual
binary split. The $x=1-b$ branch is a source-authenticated NO leaf by the
original implication; the $x=b$ branch continues. Ordinary decisions
explicitly retain both branches. A source-only refutation is a NO leaf.
This expanded tree partitions all complete original assignments into
pairwise-disjoint prefix cubes. Therefore its leaf-cylinder probabilities
sum to one:
\[
 \sum_{g\in\mathcal G}2^{-|\rho_g|}
 +\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}2^{-|\rho_b|}=1,
\]
where $\mathcal B$ denotes source-only NO leaves. Every guest leaf has
$|\rho_g|\ge k_N$, giving (3). If $\mathcal B$ is empty, (3) implies
$|\mathcal G|\ge2^{k_N}$. If each distinct logical guest leaf is explicitly
visited, it consumes at least one local operation, so
$|\mathcal G|\le T_{\rm local}$, giving (4). This argument is combinatorial;
no guest running-time or lower bound against another architecture is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Physical calls versus cached logical aliases]
If physical completed guest call $i$ is reused for $r_i$ logically separate
prefix leaves, (3) implies only
\[
 \mu_{\rm guest}\le2^{-k_N}\sum_{i=1}^{q_{\rm physical}}r_i.
                                                                    \tag{5}
\]
The substitution $\sum_i r_i\mapsto q_{\rm physical}$ is valid only
when there is no cross-leaf reuse. A DAG with a source-authenticated compact
quotient can represent many paths without enumerating their leaves, so
(4) also requires \emph{explicit} leaf visits.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Partition the logical leaves by the physical call whose
result is used, and apply the bound $2^{-|\rho|}\le2^{-k_N}$ to each.
The time bound counts only actually visited logical leaves, not a
hypothetical exponential unfolding of a compressed DAG.
\end{proof}

\section{Strict signed $(2,2)$ consequence and actual FLMV comparison}
In connected proper signed exact-(2-positive,2-negative)-3CNF, each of $n$
variables occurs in exactly four original clauses and $m=4n/3$. Thus
$\Delta=4$ and $N=\Theta(n\log n)$. For every fixed $\lambda<1$,
\[
 k_N\ge \frac n3-O(N^\lambda/\log n)-1=\frac n3-o(n).       \tag{6}
\]
If a no-memo direct-cofactor tree has at most $q=N^\gamma$ logical guest
leaves, its source-only NO cubes must cover
\[
 \mu_{\rm source\,NO}\ge1-N^\gamma 2^{-n/3+o(n)}.
                                                                    \tag{7}
\]
Even if memoized, (7) follows with $\gamma$ replaced by $\alpha$ for
an explicitly enumerated tree whose non-guest operations take at most
$N^{\alpha+o(1)}$ time. A controller that instead recognizes a source-wide
algebraic/DP/symmetry summary, shares a child across exponentially many
symbolic contexts, or changes the representation is \emph{outside} these
tree-specific statements.

\section{Two falsifying families}
\paragraph{Near-total source-pruning mass need not be useful.}
Let $G(Y)$ be \emph{any} proper 3CNF. Add, for each $i=1,\dots,t$,
fresh variables $x_i,u_i,v_i$ and four proper ternary clauses
\[
 (x_i\vee u_i\vee v_i)\land(x_i\vee u_i\vee\neg v_i)
 \land(x_i\vee\neg u_i\vee v_i)
 \land(x_i\vee\neg u_i\vee\neg v_i).                         \tag{8}
\]
Two resolutions on $v_i$ derive $(x_i\vee u_i)$ and
$(x_i\vee\neg u_i)$; a final resolution on $u_i$ derives the
source-authenticated unit $x_i$. The $x_i=0$ partial cube is therefore
forbidden, and the union of these cheap excluded regions has fraction
$1-2^{-t}$ of the entire Boolean assignment cube. Yet the resulting formula
is satisfiable \emph{exactly when $G$ is}, since $x_i=1$ satisfies
its four clauses for any $u_i,v_i$. Thus arbitrarily high forbidden-cube
mass is compatible with an arbitrary unresolved core $G$; no universal
runtime conclusion follows. This counterfamily is proper 3CNF but \emph{not}
strict signed exact-(2,2).

\paragraph{Physical guest-call count need not equal logical leaf count.}
For arbitrary $G(Y)$ and disjoint fresh triples $(z_i,p_i,q_i)$, add
\[
 (z_i\vee p_i\vee q_i)\land(\neg z_i\vee p_i\vee q_i).
                                                                    \tag{9}
\]
For all $2^t$ assignments to the $z_i$, the normalized ordinary
cofactor is exactly $G(Y)\land\bigwedge_i(p_i\vee q_i)$. A cache can answer
all $2^t$ logical leaves with a single completed call. The child here
is still linear in $t$, \emph{not} $N^{1/2}$ physical bits, so this is
not a counterexample to the power-shrink theorem. It is a counterexample
to replacing logical leaf occurrences by physical guest calls without a
reuse bound. Source discovery and normalization of the cache key are
charged in any proposed actual ARC machine.
