F1. A length-N explicit CNF can contain Omega(N) different short variable IDs that all have to be branched on, even after choosing a logarithmic cutoff.

F2. After branching on the short IDs, a residual formula may still contain Omega(N) long-identifier literal occurrences, necessitating Omega(N) fresh variables in a 3-CNF conversion.

F3. Standard clause-chain 3-CNF conversion, parsing, source mapping or malformed-input completion has an exponential hidden overhead; or the guest's machine bound applies only to promised inputs not generated by our canonicalizer.

F4. The selected hard family is hard only for tree-like rather than shared-DAG proofs, lacks certified UNSAT, or needs an unproved lower-bound-preserving CNF-to-3CNF translation.

All four are answered within the stated explicit binary literal-list model and the **conditional external guest-algorithm premise**. This is an encoding and resource-transfer lemma, NOT a historical mixed proof-system theorem.

## ITERATE: exact conditional transfer theorem

**Theorem 534.1 (proved reduction, conditional algorithmic premise).** Let explicit binary CNF be encoded by a fixed valid literal-list grammar in which every occurrence of a variable carries its canonical nonempty binary identifier as an actual input substring and parsing is polynomial time. Assume a ONE uniform deterministic at-most-3CNF SAT decider G, total on its canonical grammar, with

$$
T_G(m,n)\le (m+1)^c 2^{g(n)},\qquad g(n)=o(n),
$$

where m is the encoded guest length, c is constant and malformed guest inputs are handled in polynomial time. Then one uniform TOTAL deterministic decider A for arbitrary explicit binary CNF and malformed strings satisfies

$$
T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N)=2^{o(N/\log N)}.
$$

If G has all-prefix polynomial visited space, A has polynomial peak space (not a FLMV contraction).

**Proof.** Set N=|x|. Parse x exactly. On malformed input return MALFORMED; for empty clauses return UNSAT; for trivially satisfied empty conjunction return SAT. For N below a fixed threshold handle by finite brute force. For all larger N set

$$
L=\left\lfloor\log_2\left(\frac{N}{(\log_2 N)^2}\right)\right\rfloor.
$$

Let S contain all *occurring* variable names whose binary length is strictly below L. The total number of possible such binary names is less than 2^L, even if each occurs many times. Therefore

$$
|S|<2^L\le\frac{N}{(\log_2 N)^2}.
$$

Enumerate every assignment a to S and simplify the ORIGINAL signed CNF by ordinary literal evaluation, tautology deletion and duplicate-literal removal. This is paid work, not an oracle. In the resulting residual formula every occurring literal retains an original identifier of length at least L. If Q is the number of its literal occurrences, their explicit source substrings are disjoint, giving

$$
QL\le N,\qquad Q=O(N/\log N).
$$

In particular every nonempty residual clause contains at least one such occurrence; there are at most Q nonempty clauses. Renumber the remaining original variables densely. Split each clause of length d>3 by the standard existential Tseitin chain with at most d-3 globally fresh auxiliary variables and d-2 output clauses; retain shorter clauses verbatim. The 3-CNF has at most 2Q variables and O(Q) clauses. The fresh variables are existential witnesses; no implication about proof-system source authentication is asserted. Clause-local witnesses prove equisatisfiability in both directions for each residual formula. Thus

$$
F\text{ SAT}\iff\bigvee_{a\in\{0,1\}^S}G(\operatorname{3CNF}(F|a))=\text{SAT}.
$$

All input reads, assignments, canonicalization, short-ID table, long-ID comparison, clause conversion and guest serialization cost poly(N) per branch. Guest descriptions are polynomial in N bits even if the guest formalization uses unary headers or a less compact encoding. The number of calls is <=2^{|S|}, and each has n'<=2Q=O(N/log N). For every g(n)=o(n), the uniform maximum of g(n') over n'<=C N/log N is o(N/log N). Therefore

$$
T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N)\le\operatorname{poly}(N)\,
2^{O(N/(\log N)^2)+o(N/\log N)}
=2^{o(N/\log N)}.
$$

No semantic guessing, nonuniform advice, unsound malformed fallback or free SAT call occurs. A uses G as its explicitly assumed uniform subroutine. The source is immutable and every copied/dense-renamed literal is paid. Peak simultaneous space is polynomial in N plus G's polynomial space; no child-size FLMV inequality is claimed. QED.

**Explicit rate under the October 25 OpenAI statement, if its implementation and conventional machine-cost simulation are valid:**

$$
T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N)\le \operatorname{poly}(N)\,
2^{O\left(\frac{N}{(\log N)^2}+
\frac{N(\log\log N)^2}{(\log N)^{3/2}}\right)}
=2^{o(N/\log N)}.
$$

**Theorem 534.2 (conditional B2 falsification for a DISTINCT fixed calculus).** Assume G as in Theorem 534.1. Choose R=Res(PC_1/F_3), with its original syntactic axioms, permitted inferences, arbitrary shared DAG and unit cost per distinct proof line. By Pang Theorem 7.2(a), for arbitrarily large n there exists an UNSAT random-family member F_n with exactly 8n proper signed 3-clauses and

$$
W_R(F_n)\ge 2^{\kappa n}\quad(\kappa>0),\qquad
|\operatorname{enc}(F_n)|\le C n\log(n+1).
$$

Let N_n=ceil(C n log(n+1)). Theorem 534.1 gives

$$
T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N_n)=2^{o(n)}.
$$

For any fixed polynomial p, p(N_n+T_A^{wc}(N_n))=2^{o(n)}, eventually smaller than W_R(F_n). Hence **B2 is false for this particular R and this one uniform all-input A under the stated guest premise**:

$$
\exists A\ \forall p\ \exists N:
\overline W_R(N)>p(N+T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N)).
$$

The random theorem supplies an existential hard sequence, not a certified explicit list. Distinct source encodings are related only by their declared polynomial-time literal-list serialization; no proof-lower-bound reduction is invoked. The derivation is conditional on a sound, truly uniform subexponential G, independent of whether G implies P=NP (it does not). QED.

**Why this does NOT settle the main proof chain.** The selected R is NOT R_mix341. The historically legal mixed AND3, GF(2), LOCAL-CONVERT, paid DEF, extension and sharing shortcuts may make W_Rmix much smaller. In addition, failure of B2 for another R does not refute B3 for the historical R. B3 is not asserted either true or false. This theorem improves an important countercontrol: genuine worst-case B2, not merely the earlier faster-on-one-family B1, can fail in a sound fixed system if the external guest algorithm is correct.
