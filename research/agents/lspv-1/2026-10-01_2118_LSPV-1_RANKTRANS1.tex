\paragraph{Literature search.}
The Schur-product literature warns against inferring maximal square dimension merely from a large evaluation field. Generalized Reed--Solomon codes have highly structured small Schur squares, while recent hyperderivative Reed--Solomon work obtains maximal square dimension only under additional derivative/evaluation hypotheses. Recent monomial-Cartesian evaluation-code work identifies Schur products with exponent-set Minkowski sums when the evaluation algebra permits it. These are useful structural comparisons but do not directly prove the synchronous binary exterior-rank theorem needed here.

\paragraph{Why ordinary RS square theorems do not settle the question.}
For ordinary evaluation codes, componentwise multiplication corresponds to polynomial multiplication before reduction, and GRS squares can have dimension only about $2k-1$. Our object instead applies a binary label to odd-characteristic field values, takes Hadamard mask coordinates, forms same-shift exterior products, and spans over all legal cyclic shifts. The binary label destroys direct field-linearity. Thus standard RS Schur-square dimension is neither a lower nor upper bound for the required $\dim U$ without a new transfer theorem.

\paragraph{Candidate quantitative hinge.}
The right target is now:
\[
\exists c>0\ \forall\text{ source-legal nondegenerate leaves in the vMS regime},\qquad
\dim U\ge m^{1+c},
\]
or even any $\omega(m)$ lower bound. By DIMSUM2,
\[
\rho(H)\ge1+\dim W+\dim U,
\]
so such a theorem would retire global $O(m)$ product span for the SAME_REALIZATION. Maximal rank $\binom m2$ is unnecessary.

\paragraph{Possible proof mechanism from the literature.}
The most promising transferable idea is not the usual RS square formula but a filtration argument: expose binary label coordinates one at a time, shorten on previously exposed coordinates, and lower-bound the number of genuinely new synchronous wedge directions contributed at each layer. Hyperderivative-RS results show that derivative/evaluation layers can drive square dimension to its maximal combinatorial bound under quantitative hypotheses. This is an ANALOGY/CANDIDATE until the binary-label orbit is matched exactly.
