## Exact family, model and theorem ledger
For `n>=3`, let `F~F(n,3,8n)` have `m=8n` iid with-replacement signed proper 3-clauses, uniformly chosen among `8*binom(n,3)` possibilities, as in Pang Section 7.1. The binary indexed source length is `N=Theta(n log(n+2))`. This is *already strict original-source 3-CNF*, so no 3-CNF equisatisfiability shortcut or hardness-transfer gadget is used. Pang Theorem 7.2(a) (arXiv:2610.00837v2) states that for each fixed field prime p and fixed r>=1, with probability 1-o(1) every DAG `Res(PC_r/F_p)` proof requires exp(Omega(n)) nodes, invoking random-CNF linear PC degree from Alekhnovich--Razborov. With p=2,r=1 the system is syntactic `Res(XOR)` (disjunctions of GF(2) affine equations), and Theorem 1.1 reads

$$
\mathcal{Q}(F)\not\vdash_D1,\ D\ge32
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\ln S_{\mathrm{syn}}\ge \frac{D^2}{2048n}.
$$

**Distinct restricted calculus $R^{\mathrm{noDEF,loc3}}$:** source-labelled original CNF clauses; ordinary oriented RES of arbitrary clause width; exact AND3 conjunction and factor-to-row annotation on at most three original-variable coordinates; binary GF(2) row addition at arbitrary row width; sound single-parent LOCAL-CONVERT whose nontrivial factor/row is on at most three original variables; a local condition may export a wide, logically weaker clause by literal weakening. No DEF, hidden extension ports, oracle, free splitting/discharge, or arbitrary-arity jump is authorized. Every derived node has the original historical positive unit weight, not a new weight. This is a **restricted fragment**, NOT the complete historical byte parser.

### Theorem 481.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED: direct syntactic compilation)
There exists an absolute constant `K_3` independent of the formula, proof DAG and maximum affine-row width, such that every source-authenticated `q`-paid-node refutation in the above fragment of a source `F` with `m` clauses yields a **syntactic** `Res(PC_1/F_2)` refutation of the **same original F** using at most `K_3(m+q+1)` nodes. Sharing and reconvergence are retained.

**Proof:** Topologically represent an original/derived ordinary clause as one disjunction of affine literal equations, and every three-original-variable truth-table factor as the conjunction of its canonical full-assignment blocking clauses (at most 8). A nontrivial AND3 or LOCAL relation `E` is a consequence of one or two existing parent relations `P` whose total input/output support is at most 3. For each of at most 8 canonical clauses of `E`, the bounded implication `P |= C` has, by Pang Fact 7.1 and its elementary bounded-tableau proof, a **syntactic** Res(PC1/F2) derivation with `O(3*2^3)` nodes from the parent canonical clauses and Booleanity axioms. A local row is a degree-one equation, so the same fact directly derives the singleton affine equation from its parent-factor CNF; a row parent is handled by the identical bounded assignment-tree argument with that equation retained as an antecedent. Thus every bounded local step contributes at most `O(3*4^3)` syntactic nodes. The constant is not claimed numerically optimized. A RES step is the usual affine-literal cut, and a GF(2) row sum is Pang's allowed linear-combination inference, each constant-cost. When a local factor entails a wider clause `C`, either `C` is tautological or its restriction to the factor's support is entailed; derive that local clause and use one syntactic weakening to add the outside literals. When a single high-support **noncontradictory** affine row or proper clause has a variable outside a proposed three-coordinate conclusion, its projection onto those coordinates is the whole Boolean cube: fix the three coordinates arbitrarily and use an outside variable to satisfy the row/clause. Thus it cannot generate any nontrivial local consequence under the stated single-parent rule. Retain the bounded output clauses for later uses; never multiply by their number of consumers. Summing constant cost per source/paid node gives `K_3(m+q+1)`. A wide contradictory row ends the proof without local conversion. QED.

### Corollary 481.2 (PROVED/RESTRICTED, conditional on Pang's theorem)
For every such UNSAT F with n variables, m clauses and `\mathcal Q(F)\not\vdash_D1` at `D>=32`, any legal proof with q paid distinct nodes obeys

$$
\boxed{\ln\big(K_3(m+q+1)\big)\ge \frac{D^2}{2048n}.}
$$

For the published `F(n,3,8n)` family, `D=\Omega(n)` with high probability; therefore **every** proof in this DEF-free, 3-local, RES/GF2 shared-DAG mixed fragment needs `q=\exp(\Omega(n))=\exp(\Omega(N/\log N))` nodes. The coefficient `1/2048` is transferred from the cited theorem, not an independently established new sharp lower bound. The unknown `K_3` is a fixed multiplicative constant.

**Falsifier/limitation:** Paid conservative DEF may put high-support Boolean circuit functions behind a one-variable interface. A compiler inlining those gates can grow exponentially; this theorem makes NO assertion about proofs using such gates, unauthenticated LOCAL export/private wires, or freely assumed case splits. The full `R_mix341` has DEF, so this remains a strict restricted-system lower bound. A previously published GF2 shortcut proves that odd Tseitin is unsuitable as a mixed-hard family; the random strict-3CNF choice avoids that immediate parity case but leaves all DEF/LOCAL provenance questions OPEN. No claim about arbitrary algorithms follows.
