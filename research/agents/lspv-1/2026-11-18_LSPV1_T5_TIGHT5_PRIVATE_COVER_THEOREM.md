## Proposed candidate and four falsifiers
Candidate: in an intrinsically edge-minimal UNSAT T5 source H, one coloring of the reduced branching core with a weight-five vertex v removed is sufficient to construct independently verified witnesses for **every** original-arc deletion on a thread incident with v.
Falsifiers: (1) for a critical H and weight-five v, some nonincident-valid reduced coloring gives overlapping forbidden-color sets; (2) an authentic path endpoint support differs from L+1 for L<=3; (3) reconstructed source-level H-e witness violates any retained original arc or satisfies the deleted arc; (4) attempting the same guarantee without the weight-five hypothesis encounters a reduced coloring lacking private colors. ## Native model
T5 uses colors Z5 and R(a,b) iff (b-a) mod 5 belongs to {1,3}. H is finite, connected, edge-minimal T5-UNSAT with its **actual** original arcs. Its degree-two threads between distinct degree>=3 branching vertices B have lengths L=1,2,3 (previous independently established structural result). Thread P has positive endpoint-completion multiplicity K_P(a,b); |{b:K_P(a,b)>0}|=L+1.

For a branch v, write I(v) for its incident threads. Choose any tau:B\{v}->Z5 satisfying all threads with neither endpoint v. Define the forbidden set E_P(tau)={c in Z5: P is impossible after extending tau by v=c}, of size f_P=4-L(P). Define the **private forbidden set**
A_P(tau)=E_P(tau) \ union_{Q in I(v),Q!=P} E_Q(tau).

**Theorem A (exact private-cover criterion, PROVED/EXACT).** For any original arc e on incident thread P, tau extends to a satisfying branch assignment for H-e iff A_P(tau) is nonempty. Precisely its viable choices for v are the elements of A_P. H-e is SAT iff there exists such a reduced tau with A_P nonempty. This is independent of which original arc of P is deleted.

Proof: All intact incident Q require v notin E_Q. Deleting any original arc in P disconnects its internal path into two rooted trees, so all branch endpoint pairs are realizable. Therefore the viable v values are Z5 \ union_{Q!=P} E_Q. Since H is UNSAT, union_{Q in I(v)} E_Q = Z5 for every valid tau; otherwise adding v would solve H. Hence viable values are E_P \ union_{Q!=P}E_Q=A_P. For each such value reconstruct intact paths using positive K_Q and the broken path using two outgoing choices per remaining arc. QED.

**Theorem B (tight-five saturation and batch reuse, PROVED/EXACT).** If w(v)=sum_{P in I(v)}(4-L(P))=5, then for *every* reduced nonincident-valid tau the sets E_P partition Z5. Thus A_P=E_P is nonempty for every incident P. One tau yields complete H-e witnesses for **all** original arcs on incident threads. If Q_v is the number of reduced branch assignments tau, deleting a thread P yields exactly (4-L(P))*Q_v **branch-color assignments**. This last expression does NOT count full original-vertex witnesses: intact K_Q multiplicities are nonuniform and broken paths have 2^(L-1) internal completions.

Proof: UNSAT forces the union of forbidden sets to have cardinality five. Their total cardinality equals w(v)=5, hence they form a disjoint partition. Any tau exists because H-e is SAT by criticality and its restriction is nonincident-valid. Apply Theorem A, with internal path reconstruction and full original-arc verification. QED.
