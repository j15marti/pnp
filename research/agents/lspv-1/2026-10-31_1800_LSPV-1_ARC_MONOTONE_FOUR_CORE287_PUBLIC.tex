\section{Complete four-core classification}
Let $F|\rho$ be the ordinary residual under coherent $\rho$, with each clause retaining its original source index. A minimal UNSAT core is UNSAT but each single-clause deletion is SAT.
\begin{theorem}
For every monotone CNF with distinct literals per clause and clause width at most three, every inclusion-minimal UNSAT subcollection of exactly four clauses is, up to variable permutation and simultaneous global literal complementation, exactly one of
\begin{align*}
\mathbf A:\ &(x)\land(y)\land(z)\land(\neg x\lor\neg y\lor\neg z),\\
\mathbf B:\ &(x)\land(y\lor z)\land(\neg x\lor\neg y)\land(\neg x\lor\neg z),\\
\mathbf C:\ &(x)\land(\neg y)\land(y\lor z)\land(\neg x\lor\neg z).
\end{align*}
All three are inclusion-minimal UNSAT and are completely refuted by UP. Thus any UNSAT core of size at most four in a monotone residual is detected by UP.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The established Tarsi lemma states that any minimally unsatisfiable CNF with $m$ clauses has at most $m-1$ variables. Hence every four-clause minimal UNSAT core has at most three variables. There are exactly $2(\binom31+\binom32+\binom33)=14$ possible nonempty monotone clauses on three variables. Enumerate all $\binom{14}{4}=1001$ distinct four-clause subsets, evaluate all eight assignments on each, and require each of the four three-clause deletions to have a witness. The exact exhaustive finite certificate produces 14 labeled minimal UNSAT subsets, comprising precisely three variable-permutation/global-complement orbits of sizes $2,6,6$, with the representatives above. The independent compact checker exhausts this entire fixed finite universe and also tests all 230300 four-subsets over five variables. There is no asymptotic extrapolation of samples: Tarsi proves three-variable completeness. UP refutes A by forcing all three variables to TRUE, B by forcing $x=1$ then $\neg y,\neg z$ against $y\lor z$, and C by forcing $x=1,y=0$ and then opposite units $z,\neg z$. The earlier ARC-MONOTONE-THREE-CORE286 proves the complete empty/pair/three cases.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}
Every monotone CNF with at most five clauses, each of width at most three, is decided exactly by UP. Six is the minimum clause count for an UNSAT unit-free monotone residual, and the bound is sharp.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If UP reaches a unit-free nonempty residual without contradiction and it were UNSAT, a minimal UNSAT subcollection would have at most five clauses and, by Tarsi, at most four variables. The universe of nonempty monotone width-two or width-three clauses over four variables has $2(\binom42+\binom43)=20$ members. All $\binom{20}{5}=15504$ five-clause subsets have a satisfying assignment by complete independent finite enumeration; so do all smaller subsets. This contradicts UNSAT. Sharpness: the six clauses $(x\lor y),(\neg x\lor\neg y),(y\lor z),(\neg y\lor\neg z),(z\lor x),(\neg z\lor\neg x)$ are a minimal UNSAT odd-cycle NAE instance, yet have no unit. It is only an abstract residual countercontrol, not a canonical-source hardness claim.
\end{proof}
