\section{Exact finite conditional phase-cycle certificate}
Let
\[
 b=(6/5,1/2),\quad c=241/200,\quad
 d=11642479/23232400.
\]
For exponent envelopes \(z=(t,s)\ge b\), define
\[
 P(z)=b+\operatorname{diag}(2,1/5)(z-b),\qquad
 Q(z)=b+\operatorname{diag}(1/5,2)(z-b).
\]
Their composition is
\[
 Q(P(z))=b+\frac25(z-b).
\]
\begin{theorem}
Suppose that, from an arbitrary achieved simultaneous
\((K,K)\), \(K\ge2\), every fixed alternating \(P\) and
\(Q\) stage is witnessed by an actual fixed uniform deterministic
total correct ordinary-SAT decider in the same FLMV indexed
read-only-input random-access Turing model, and every edge
has its full bit, discovery, guest, and peak writable/index-space
costs already included in the displayed bounds.
Then finitely many stages yield a machine with joint
time and space exponents \(t<c,\ s<d\), contradicting
the published FLMV endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
After \(j\) two-stage cycles the achieved upper envelope is
\[
 t_j=\frac65+\left(\frac25\right)^j
 \left(K-\frac65\right),\qquad
 s_j=\frac12+\left(\frac25\right)^j
 \left(K-\frac12\right).
\]
The time margin is \(c-6/5=1/200>0\);
the space margin is \(d-1/2=26279/23232400>0\).
Both excesses converge to zero, so a fixed finite \(j\)
satisfies the two strict inequalities. Every hypothesized
stage is a same-machine achievable-pair transformation, so
the final machine would contradict FLMV Corollary 5.8.
\end{proof}
With \(K=10\), \(j=10\) gives
\((t,s)=(1.20092274688,0.5009961472)\).
These are exact-rational conditional upper-envelope calculations,
not implemented SAT transformations.
The historic two unstable diagonal phase matrices were
already present in ARC-0007; the current statement changes
their center and applies the valid FLMV rectangle.
The retired universal cosine endpoint is not used.
\section{A charged effective-interface discontinuity}
Let \(F=E\land B\) where \(E\) is an arbitrary consistent
affine equation system over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(B\)
is a 2-CNF. Decompose the affine components, including
E-free singleton variables, and let \(q_i\) be the affine
projection rank onto the component coordinates actually
appearing in \(B\). Put
\[
 k=\sum_i\max(0,q_i-1).
\]
\begin{lemma}
There is a uniform exact deterministic procedure deciding
this declared mixed language in
\(2^k\operatorname{poly}(N)\) time with
\(\operatorname{poly}(N)\) workspace.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Detect inconsistent \(E\) and reject. Gaussian elimination
finds a parameter basis for each component's projection
to \(B\). Enumerate values of all but one of the
\(q_i\) projected parameters, paying \(2^k\) choices
total. Every resulting literal in \(B\) is then a constant
or a literal of the remaining selector for its component.
Thus the residual is an ordinary 2-CNF on these selectors.
Use a charged exact 2-SAT decider on each enumerated
choice; the disjunction of all outcomes is exact for both
SAT and UNSAT. Free affine parameters invisible to \(B\)
can be completed after a successful branch. Reuse working
memory for successive choices.
\end{proof}
If \(q_i\le2\) for all components and the
cross-component 2-CNF interaction graph is a forest,
the ordinary constant-domain tree-CSP join is polynomial:
there are at most four projected assignments per component.
Consequently \(k=\Theta(N)\) need not imply exponential
work. A class-wide tractability discontinuity does not
supply a per-instance lower bound.
