\section{All-valid-source signed cut-defect theorem}
Let $p$ be any coherent original-source partial assignment, $f$ its free-variable count, $U(p)$ the set of original clauses not yet satisfied by pinned literals, $u=|U(p)|$, and $e=2f-u$. For each free $x$, let $s_x^+$ and $s_x^-$ be its positive and negative occurrences in already-satisfied original clauses. Define
\[
\eta=\sum_{x\text{ free}}\min(s_x^+,s_x^-),\qquad
w_x=|s_x^+-s_x^-|,\qquad
b_x=\begin{cases}0&s_x^+>s_x^-\\1&s_x^->s_x^+\\ *&s_x^+=s_x^-.\end{cases}
\]
Construct the source-clause-indexed residual binary multigraph $G_p$: each original clause reduced to exactly two free literal occurrences is an edge labeled with its distinct original clause ID. For any edge-labeled $H\subseteq G_p$ define its cut defect
\[
q(H)=|E(H)|-\operatorname{MaxCut}(H)
=\min_{\sigma:V(H)\to\{0,1\}}\#\{uv\in E(H):\sigma(u)=\sigma(v)\}.
\]
The identity is standard Max-Cut combinatorics \cite{goemans1995}. Obtaining $q(H)$ for an unbounded graph is NOT a free operation. A constant-size $H$ permits a verified exhaustive certificate.

\begin{theorem}[Paid original-source signed cut-defect packing]
For every valid canonical $F$, coherent $p$, authenticated family $\mathcal H$ of residual binary subgraphs with pairwise disjoint original source-clause edge IDs, and every full satisfying original-source extension $a\supseteq p$,
\[
\boxed{\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x{\bf1}[a_x\ne b_x]
\le \delta_{\mathcal H}:=e-\eta-\sum_{H\in\mathcal H}q(H).}
\]
If $\delta_{\mathcal H}<0$, the entire partial cube is UNSAT. If $\delta_{\mathcal H}\ge0$ and $w_x>\delta_{\mathcal H}$, every satisfying extension has $a_x=b_x$. Any opposite assignment set with total weight exceeding $\delta_{\mathcal H}$ is a forbidden subcube. A zero or positive budget alone does not certify SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every free original variable has exactly two true signed literal occurrences among its original two positive and two negative positions. For every original-source satisfying completion,
\[
e=\sum_{j\in U(p)}(t_j(a)-1)
+\eta+\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x{\bf1}[a_x\ne b_x].
\]
Every term $t_j(a)-1$ is nonnegative. A satisfied original-source monotone residual binary clause whose two variables have equal truth values must have two true literals: both false would violate the clause. Thus each equal-endpoint edge contributes one to the first sum. For each $H$, every coloring has at least $q(H)$ such edges. Since their original clause edge IDs do not overlap, the lower bounds sum without double counting. Rearrangement gives the displayed inequality and its exact logical consequences.
\end{proof}
