\section*{Fixed target and polynomial family}
Keep one preselected authenticated source-independent-VARDEF binary-premise unit-weight $R_{\rm mix341}$ with RES, exact AND3, XOR, CONVERT and charged conservative VARDEF. All distinct paid DAG nodes cost one; original sources cost zero; the optimal proof is chosen freely. Put
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\sum_{v\in\pi}1,\qquad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ \mathrm{UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F),
\]
with empty maximum zero. Input lengths are encoded \emph{bits}, not variable counts. A general decider must distinguish valid SAT, valid UNSAT and malformed sources.
\begin{theorem}[Polynomial universal counterexample CNF]
For any fixed uniform total \emph{polynomial-time correct} CNF-SAT decider $A$, there is an effective uniform family $H_{A,\ell}$ of CNFs of bit length $\ell^{O_A(1)}$, where input bits $s$, padded assignment bits $y$, and paid definitional-circuit auxiliary variables express
\[
\operatorname{ValidCNF}(s)\ \wedge\ [A(s)=\mathrm{UNSAT}]
\ \wedge\ [y\models F_s].
\tag{1}
\]
Every $H_{A,\ell}$ is UNSAT by \emph{external correctness of $A$}; the theorem does not assert any short $R$-refutation or the existence of a P-time $A$ unconditionally.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A fixed binary parser plus evaluation of $F_s(y)$ has uniform polynomial-size Boolean circuits on $\ell$ input bits. An assumed polynomial-time deterministic $A$ likewise has polynomial-size uniform unrolled circuits. Form a conjunction of the valid-source, rejecting-output and satisfaction bits, assert that conjunction and apply a fixed conservative Tseitin encoding. All lengths and construction costs are polynomial in $\ell$. A correct decider cannot reject a valid formula having the exhibited witness; invalid strings are excluded by the validity circuit. A rejecting trace without the external correctness hypothesis would not suffice.
\end{proof}
\section*{Two explicit obligations, not yet proved for the full mixed calculus}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{RT, rule-by-rule restriction/retyping.} Any $q$-node legal $R$ refutation of $H_{A,\ell}$ specializes by setting $s=\operatorname{enc}(F)$ into a legal $R$ refutation of the specialized CNF at cost bounded by one fixed polynomial $p_A(\ell+q+1)$. This MUST audit every AND3, XOR, CONVERT, source-independent VARDEF, shared edge, fresh variable and charged constant substitution. Ordinary Resolution restriction closure proves only a subsystem case.
\item[] \textbf{SC, authenticated source/extension compiler.} For each valid UNSAT $F$ of exactly $\ell$ bits, every specialized source of $H_{A,\ell}$ needed by the restricted proof must be legally derived from the actual clauses of $F$ with charged gate definitions, RES and independently checkable constant circuit evaluation, at an additional cost $q_A(\ell)$ polynomial in $\ell$. The result of $A(F)$ is not a free axiom.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional one-family B3 reduction]
Assume one correct P-time $A$ and RT+SC for the \emph{same fixed} $R$. Let $Q_A(\ell)=W_R(H_{A,\ell})$. There exists a polynomial $P_A$ such that
\[
\boxed{\overline W_R(N)\le\max_{1\le\ell\le N}
 P_A\bigl(\ell+Q_A(\ell)+1\bigr).}\tag{2}
\]
Thus polynomial $Q_A$ suffices for B3 under RT+SC. Conversely, under SAT$\in P$, B3 already implies that $Q_A(\ell)$ is polynomial since $H_{A,\ell}$ is a genuine UNSAT formula whose encoded bit length is polynomial in $\ell$. Under RT+SC this is a \emph{one-length-indexed-family equivalence}, not a completed unconditional B3 implication.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take an optimum $q=Q_A(\ell)$ refutation and restrict its signed source-index bits by RT. SC supplies every required specialized circuit/source clause from authenticated original $F$ with at most polynomial added paid work; append the DAGs and count shared nodes only once. This gives $W_R(F)\le p_A(\ell+q+1)+q_A(\ell)$, uniformly for any UNSAT $F$ of length $\ell$. Maximize over $\ell\le N$ and absorb the sum into one polynomial. For the converse, B3 applies at $|H_{A,\ell}|=\ell^{O_A(1)}$ and yields $Q_A(\ell)=\ell^{O(1)}$. Any polynomial length rescaling is charged. Proof acquisition/optimizer cost is separate.
\end{proof}
\section*{Exact signed three-variable control}
The restricted signed-unit grammar is $F_{a,b}(x)=(x\oplus a=1)\wedge(x\oplus b=1)$, correctly rejected iff $a\oplus b=1$. Its false-rejection-plus-witness CNF is the six source clauses
\[
(a\vee b),\quad(\neg a\vee\neg b),\quad(x\vee a),\quad
(\neg x\vee\neg a),\quad(x\vee b),\quad(\neg x\vee\neg b).
\]
They represent $(a\oplus b=1)\wedge(x\oplus a=1)\wedge(x\oplus b=1)$. All six clauses are indispensable: deleting each produces exactly one three-bit SAT witness. Therefore binary-premise source ancestry gives $W_R\ge5$. Three AND3 nodes conjoin each two-clause parity row and two legal XOR nodes derive $0=1$, proving
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(H_{\rm toy})=5.}
\]
All four signed-unit inputs yield the correct two SAT/two UNSAT labels. Upon specializing a genuinely rejecting pair, original units $(x)$, $(\neg x)$ have a one-node Resolution refutation. An unsound reject-all comparator has two satisfying counterexamples and is NOT a soundness proof.
