\section*{LSPV-1 exact RMV output typing}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open.

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT.} In van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski, Definition 20 makes the local extractor an oracle function. Lemma 21 supplies a local extractor for degree-$\Delta$ Reed--Muller polynomials over $\mathbb F_p$, with seed length $O(r\log p)$ and output length $\Delta$. Definition 22 defines MV lines. The RMV construction then takes the length-$m$ prefix, $m\le\Delta^{1/100}$, of $E^{\widehat g_L}(y)$ and recursively repeats on $\widehat g_L$.

For one fixed SAME\_REALIZATION polynomial $\widehat g$, an output word is therefore indexed by a recursion node $\sigma$, an MV line $L$, and seed $y$. Writing $\widehat g_{\sigma,L}$ for the composed restriction, the exact final coordinates exposed by the active paper are
\[
g_j(\sigma,L,y)=\pi_j(E^{\widehat g_{\sigma,L}}(y)),\qquad 1\le j\le m,
\]
where $\pi_j$ selects bit $j$ of the prescribed prefix. Any adaptive query address is internal to the deterministic oracle computation $E$: it is a function of $y$ and prior oracle answers, not a free source coordinate.

\textbf{OPEN.} The checked primary statements specify the oracle interface, locality and parameters, but do not expose the Boolean ANF of $[E^X(y)]_j$ or a multiplication law after $X=\widehat g_{\sigma,L}$. Consequently the Source-Degree Rank Lemma is not applicable. The only certified general bound remains
\[
\rho=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_i g_j\}
\le \min\{|U|,1+m+\binom m2\}.
\]
Neither $O(m)$ nor an $\Omega(m^2)$ independent minor is proved for the actual realization.

\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED.} Reed--Muller linearity in polynomial coefficients does not imply affine Boolean dependence on the varying index $(\sigma,L,y)$. Short seed, locality, field degree, canonicity and list-decodability remain insufficient. Pure restriction-order monodromy is retired by extractor extensionality. An arbitrary post-hoc binary relabeling is not an architecture-preserving attack because $E$ is fixed.

\textbf{Hostile audit.} SAME\_REALIZATION is preserved by fixing $\widehat g$. A maximal semantic oracle can expose the range/rank and an algebraic oracle can expose low-degree field structure, so a rank theorem alone would not be the final nonrelativizing hinge. DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP, padding/auxiliary variables, affine/Tseitin, Horn, 2-SAT and bounded-treewidth controls forbid interpreting rank as unrestricted runtime.

\textbf{Handoffs.} LSPV-2: use the typed domain $(\sigma,L,y)$, not polynomial coefficients. LSPV-3: acquisition of any $Q$ remains separate. LSPV-4: attack any claimed low Boolean degree through the actual query-address dependence.

\textbf{Single next target.} Recover the explicit algorithm implementing the local-extractor lemma cited as implicit in Shaltiel--Umans 2005, list every oracle query and binary projection, substitute one legal RMV restricted polynomial, and compute symbolic Boolean degree/product span. If the source does not expose that implementation, record the exact missing definition rather than substitute a surrogate.

\textbf{Primary sources.} Shaltiel--Umans, JACM 52(2), 2005, pp.172--216. Shaltiel--Umans, SIAM J. Comput. 39(3), 2009, pp.1006--1037. van Melkebeek--Mocelin Sdroievski, CCC 2023, Defs.20,22 and Lemma21.
