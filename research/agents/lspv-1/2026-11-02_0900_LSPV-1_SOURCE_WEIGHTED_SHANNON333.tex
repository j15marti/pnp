\section{Precise source theorem}
Let $\mathcal D$ consist of all valid proper duplicate-free D\"ocker monotone 3-CNF originals with exactly two occurrences of each polarity per variable. Such an original $F$ has $n$ variables, $m=4n/3$ clauses and maximum primal degree eight. A deterministic greedy maximal independent set $I$ has $k\ge\lceil n/9\rceil$ and no original clause contains two pivots. Let $H$ consist of source clauses avoiding $I$. For each $x\in I$, let $P_{x1},P_{x2}$ be the two positive width-two clause remainders and $N_{x1},N_{x2}$ the two negative remainders. For an assignment $z$ to the surviving original variables, define
\[
A_x(z)=\prod_{j=1}^2 \mathbf1[P_{xj}(z)=1],\qquad
B_x(z)=\prod_{j=1}^2 \mathbf1[N_{xj}(z)=1].
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact paid weighted source-independent Shannon projection]
One uniform polynomial-time source-authenticated compiler computes $I$ and a fan-in-two mixed Boolean/arithmetic circuit for
\[
\boxed{W_I(z)=\mathbf1[H(z)]\prod_{x\in I}(A_x(z)+B_x(z))
=\#\{b\in\{0,1\}^I:F(z,b)=1\}.}
\]
Hence $\#SAT(F)=\sum_z W_I(z)$ and $SAT(F)\iff\exists z: W_I(z)>0$. Under the declared unsimplified source-private architecture, this is exactly $3m-4k-1$ mixed fan-in-two OR/AND/ADD/MUL operators, with input negations free; $W$ has at most $k+1$ bits. This is NOT an unrestricted gate minimum or a SAT decision-time lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original clause touches at most one $x\in I$. After fixing $z$, $x=0$ satisfies all its four incident clauses iff $A_x=1$, and $x=1$ iff $B_x=1$. Thus the number of permitted values of $x$ is $A_x+B_x$, independently for different pivots CONDITIONAL ON THE SAME COMPLETE $z$. Multiplication, then summing or testing positivity over $z$, proves the formulas. The original primal graph has degree at most eight, so deterministic greedy selection gives $k\ge n/9$. Let $h=m-4k$. If $h>0$, evaluating $H$ requires $3h-1$ fixed source-private Boolean operators. Each pivot requires four OR, two AND, one integer ADD and one multiplication, so the total is $3h-1+8k=3m-4k-1$. If $h=0$, seven local operators per pivot plus $k-1$ multiplications also yields $8k-1=3m-4k-1$. The integer result is bounded by $2^k$.
\end{proof}
