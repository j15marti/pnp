\section{Model and corrected primary endpoint}
Fix a binary ordinary SAT encoding and the deterministic random-access Turing-machine model of Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek and Viglas (FLMV): indexed read-only input, counted work and index tapes, and one-way write-only output. Define
\[
\E=\{(t,s):\exists\text{ one fixed total uniform all-input exact SAT decider }A,\
T_A(N)\le N^{t+o(1)},\quad S_A(N)\le N^{s+o(1)}\}.
\]
Every pair is witnessed by a single realization, although different pairs may have different programs. The coordinates are analysis parameters and need not be computable by the program. The set $\E$ is upward closed.

FLMV Theorem 3.1 reduces nondeterministic linear time to SAT with polylogarithmic time and space overhead in the same random-access machine model. Corollary 5.8 states
\[
\mathrm{NTIME}[n]\not\subseteq
\mathrm{DTISP}\bigl[n^c,o(n^{\beta(c)})\bigr],
\qquad
\beta(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right),\quad c>1.
\]
This is a \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} in its published model, not an unrestricted polynomial-time SAT lower bound.

\begin{lemma}[Corrected FLMV curved forbidden region, PROVED/EXACT]
Let
\[
\B_{\rm FLMV}=\{(t,s): \exists c>\max\{1,t\}
\text{ with }s<\beta(c)\}.
\]
Then $\E\cap\B_{\rm FLMV}=\varnothing$. The region is downward closed in the coordinatewise order. For $1<t<c_0$, where $c_0$ is the positive root of $c^3-c-2=0$, the condition $s<\beta(t)$ is equivalent to $(t,s)\in\B_{\rm FLMV}$. For $0\le t\le1$, the condition $s<1$ suffices and is necessary for membership in this region.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $(t,s)\in\E$ and $c>\max\{1,t\}$ with $s<\beta(c)$, the same machine's time $N^{t+o(1)}$ has strict margin below $N^c$ and its space is $o(N^{\beta(c)})$. FLMV Theorem 3.1 absorbs polylogarithmic factors and contradicts Corollary 5.8. If $(t',s')\le(t,s)$ the same $c$ witnesses $(t',s')\in\B_{\rm FLMV}$, giving downward closure.
Moreover $\beta'(c)=-\bigl(1+(c+4)/c^3\bigr)/2<0$, and $\beta$ is continuous for $c>0$. Thus an admissible $c>t$ exists precisely when $s<\lim_{c\downarrow t}\beta(c)=\beta(t)$ for $t>1$. For $t\le1$ use $\lim_{c\downarrow1}\beta(c)=1$. The positive $\beta$ range stops at $c_0$, since $\beta(c)=0$ iff $c^3-c-2=0$.
\end{proof}

Let $\mathcal K\subseteq\mathbb N$ be unbounded. Assume that, for every $K\in\mathcal K$ with $(K,K)\in\E$, there exists a \emph{finite} route
\[
(K,K)=x_0,\ x_1,\ldots,x_r\in\E
\qquad\text{with}\quad x_r\in\B_{\rm FLMV}.
\]
Every vertex must be witnessed by a uniform total all-input exact SAT machine with \emph{joint} resources. Every claimed route edge must be proved by a valid existence theorem or a fully charged finite uniform construction. The route may depend on fixed $K$ but not on input length without an additional uniformity theorem. Then $P\ne NP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume $P=NP$. Some fixed uniform polynomial-time exact SAT algorithm has a finite simultaneous exponent pair $(t_0,s_0)\in\E$. Choose $K\in\mathcal K$ larger than both exponents. Upward closure implies $(K,K)\in\E$. The assumed finite route gives $x_r\in\E\cap\B_{\rm FLMV}$, contradicting the lemma.
\end{proof}
