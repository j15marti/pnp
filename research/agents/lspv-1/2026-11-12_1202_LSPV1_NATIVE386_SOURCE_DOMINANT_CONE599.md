## Theorem 599.1: universal complete-/386-CLI clause-cache/cone normal form

Let \(P\) range over **all finite parsed, full-CLI-accepted ordinary certificate arrays** of original archived \`/386\`. The original \`spec\` is considered fixed, including its exact signed guarded cubic graph and parity charges. Its \`gate_defs\` owner bank is fixed and globally visible; each owner is charged once. The full original \`/386\` grammar has precisely SOURCE, zero-additional-charge DEF facets with no parents, and unit-paid strict signed RES records with two earlier parents, **without** assumptions, branch contexts, locality scopes or proof-history-sensitive conversion rules.

Define \(N(P)\) deterministically:
1. Copy every original mandatory SOURCE record without changing its original relative SOURCE order. Copy each distinct zero-parent DEF facet to the prefix, or map it to an earlier equal signed clause. Preserve *all* global paid \`gate_defs\` declarations regardless of facet usage.
2. Traverse the original paid RES records in original chronological order, maintaining the earliest available representative for each **entire canonical signed clause**, irrespective of whether it was a SOURCE, a DEF facet, or a RES. For any nonterminal RES with an already cached conclusion, eliminate this paid record and map its future consumers to that earlier clause. For a genuinely new conclusion, retain the original pivot and clause and remap only its parent indices. **Always retain the original terminal RES with its original pivot**.
3. From the retained last RES, traverse its full predecessor graph. Delete every unneeded nonterminal RES and every unneeded DEF facet. Retain all mandatory SOURCE occurrences and the full paid \`gate_defs\` bank. Reindex all chronological parent references and recompute every \`summary\` count.

Then for **every** such \(P\):
\[
\operatorname{CLI}_{386}(N(P))=\mathrm{ACCEPT},\qquad
N(N(P))=N(P),\qquad W_{386}(N(P))\le W_{386}(P).
\]
More precisely, with \(d\) the count of nonterminal paid RES nodes skipped by the clause cache and \(a\) the count of distinct remaining nonterminal paid RES nodes removed by terminal-cone pruning,
\[
W_{386}(N(P))=W_{386}(P)-d-a.
\]
The original signed SOURCE multiset, signed final contradiction, \`spec\`, owner tuples, owner charges and each retained paid RES's signed premise clauses/pivot are unchanged. All **remaining paid RES** are ancestors of the final empty sink. No statement is made about actual physical memory, evidence bits, span monotonicity, proof discovery, or optimal work over *other* proof DAGs.

**Proof.** SOURCE and DEF rows have zero parents and all paid owner declarations occur in a separate globally available array, so moving these zero-parent rows ahead of RES is a legal permutation under the exact source-code tests. Each removed RES row has a strictly earlier representative producing the **same full signed clause**. The archived /386 RES checker depends on a parent only through that clause, tests its ordered signed pivot incidence, and computes its exact canonical signed resolvent. Replacing a parent by an equal clause therefore preserves every retained inference exactly; it cannot create a forward edge because the representative is earlier. All source and gate checks remain identical. Backward traversal deletes only nodes not used by the terminal RES and does not delete any mandatory SOURCE or global paid owner declaration. Thus the mathematical core still accepts. The spec and graph-size control tests are unchanged. Since the input was **complete CLI** accepted, its records were an Array, at least one owner exists and the last pivot is not numeric 999999 (the corrected archived-wrapper predicate from /577 and /505). The transformed packet has all three properties, so all eleven original CLI corruption controls continue rejecting their mutations. Each deleted RES eliminates one original paid occurrence; no previously separate owners are merged or recharged. The output has no redundant nonterminal RES conclusion, no dead nonterminal RES/DEF, all mandatory SOURCE rows at the front and each needed DEF facet once, so rerunning the transformation leaves it identical. QED.

**Important limit.** The proof depends on context-free, globally available premise semantics and is **not valid automatically** for private/exported wires, contextual assumptions, discharge, branching joins, source-history-sensitive LOCAL-CONVERT or any rule with provenance-checked parent identities.
