\section*{Established lower bound and exact target}
For a fixed sound effective preselected $R$,
\[
 W_R(F)=\min_{\pi\ {\rm refutes}\ F}
       \sum_{v\in{\rm distinct\ paid\ nodes}(\pi)}w_R(v),\quad
 \overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ {\rm UNSAT,\ valid}\\
                   |\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F).
\]
The empty maximum is zero. Source bit length $N$ is not variable
count $n$. Take standard unary
$P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^h$ with $n_h=h(h+1)$ variables and
\[
 m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}=\Theta(h^3),\qquad
 N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))
\]
in signed ASCII DIMACS. Haken (1985) proves: there exist $c>0,h_0$
such that for all $h\ge h_0$, \emph{every unrestricted shared-DAG
ordinary Resolution} proof of $P_h$ has at least $2^{ch}$ lines.
This is \emph{restricted proof hardness}, not arbitrary SAT runtime
hardness. Extended Resolution and Frege have polynomial proof
shortcuts for these same sources (Cook--Reckhow, Buss).

\section*{A proof-acquisition theorem in the correct bit metric}
Let $V(F,e)$ be any fixed sound, complete, polynomial-time
\emph{bit-verifiable} proof relation for UNSAT, rejecting malformed
encodings, and define the shortest encoded evidence length
\[
  \ell_V(F)=\min\{|e|:V(F,e)=1\}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Output-sensitive optimal evidence]
If $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$, one fixed uniform algorithm rejects malformed
sources, produces a satisfying assignment on SAT sources and,
on each UNSAT source, produces a \emph{bit-shortest} $V$ proof
in time
\[
  (|\operatorname{enc}(F)|+\ell_V(F)+2)^{O_V(1)}.
\]
It receives neither an optimal bound nor proof advice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For unary $1^b$, existence of $e$ of length at most $b$
accepted by $V$ is an NP predicate, uniformly polynomial in
$|F|+b$. SAT in P implies NP=P. Try $b=0,1,2,4,\ldots$
until successful; the first successful bound is at most twice
the optimum (or one when the optimum is zero). Binary search
for the exact minimum bit length and use standard bit-by-bit
prefix extension with the same polynomial NP predicate to
recover an actual certificate. There are polynomially many
queries and all lengths are polynomial in $|F|+\ell_V(F)$.
Handle SAT first with the assumed decider and polynomial
assignment self-reduction, and malformed inputs by parsing.
\end{proof}
This generalizes the evidence-safe aspect of LSPV-1
OUTPUT-SENSITIVE-OPT365 beyond ER2. It does \emph{not} optimize
logical node cost $q$ in time polynomial in $N+q$ unless there
is a separately proved evidence normalization
$|e|\le\operatorname{poly}(N+q)$. For unrestricted LOCAL-CONVERT
the latter normal form and even the precise fixed proof grammar
remain an outstanding audit. Under SAT in P a circuit-implication
legality check is polynomial via a polynomial-size SAT reduction,
provided the circuit payload is explicitly serialized, but this
does not bound its size.

\section*{A fixed binary-premise toy calculus and a forced payload}
Define $R_{\mathrm{table}}$ as follows, \emph{for methodological
counterexample only}. Authenticated original CNF clauses are free.
Strict ordinary Resolution derives clauses at unit cost.
One additional \emph{binary-premise, unit-paid} TAB-AND operation
takes two authenticated source/Resolution clauses or previous
Boolean factors and returns their exact conjunction, explicitly
as a vector of all $2^n$ Boolean values over the declared source
variables, with no lazy/sparse/circuit substitute. A factor cannot
be used as a Resolution clause for free. Contradiction is
the verified all-zero vector or the empty Resolution clause.
Every operation is checked directly, row by row for TAB-AND;
the verifier is deterministic polynomial in \emph{encoded proof
length}. There is no hidden SAT/equivalence oracle. This
separate system is sound, complete, effective and source-preserving.
It is \emph{not} a legal new rule of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, which
forbids uncharged global truth-table inference.

\begin{lemma}[All-source binary DAG lower bound]
If deleting any of the $m$ source clauses makes $F$ satisfiable,
every sound, authenticated at-most-binary-premise refutation DAG
has at least $m-1$ distinct paid internal nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each source is indispensable, so each has a directed path to
the contradiction; otherwise the same derivation would refute
its satisfiable deletion. Prune unused nodes. The resulting
underlying connected graph has $m+t$ vertices and at least
$m+t-1$ edges, and at most $2t$ edges since $t$ internal
nodes have at most two parent edges. Thus $t\ge m-1$.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Polynomial node optimum, forced exponential bits]
For every $h\ge1$,
\[
 \boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{table}}}(P_h)=m_h-1.}
\]
For all sufficiently large $h$, every refutation attaining this
node optimum has encoded evidence of length at least
\[
 \boxed{2^{n_h}=2^{h(h+1)}\ {\rm bits}.}
\]
Thus even if SAT were in P, a producer that must \emph{write}
a minimum-node $R_{\mathrm{table}}$ proof of $P_h$ cannot run in
time polynomial in $N_h+W_{R_{\mathrm{table}}}(P_h)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Deleting a pigeon-occupancy clause permits omitting that
pigeon and injecting the other $h$ pigeons into $h$ holes.
Deleting a collision clause for pigeons $p,q$ in hole $j$
permits placing both in $j$ and all $h-1$ remaining pigeons
in the other $h-1$ holes. Every clause is indispensable.
The binary-DAG lemma yields $q\ge m_h-1$.
A binary TAB-AND tree (or chain) conjoining the $m_h$ clauses
attains $q=m_h-1$, hence the minimum is exact. For sufficiently
large $h$, Haken's bound excludes any Resolution-only derivation
with so few derived nodes. Therefore every minimum-node
derivation contains at least one TAB-AND record, which by the
\emph{fixed toy grammar} explicitly contains $2^{n_h}$ bits.
The output-length lower bound is independent of all algorithms
for finding the proof.
\end{proof}
Indeed $\overline W_{R_{\mathrm{table}}}(N)\le N$, because every
UNSAT formula with $m$ clauses has the $m-1$-node conjunction
proof. Thus B3 for this \emph{toy} is true unconditionally;
this result does not refute or prove B3 for real $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.
It instead rigorously falsifies the attempted transfer from
polynomial node count and polynomial-in-evidence verification
to polynomial-in-node-cost proof output. A source-preserving
derivation with free hindsight optimization is not automatically
an efficient computational certificate generator.
