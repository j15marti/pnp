\section{Target, obstruction and quantifiers}
The sole NP-complete hardness target is every valid duplicate-free proper clausewise-monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) input of D\"ocker, with three distinct same-sign literals per clause and exactly two source appearances per variable per polarity. A grammar checker must reject malformed strings. Broader proper strict-2P2N is a comparison superset. We seek one correct paid adaptive residual-SAT controller and an all-input hereditary compiler $A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ for arbitrary correct polynomial-time guest deciders $A$ of unknown exponents $(K,L)$, not a family-only source splitter. The exact obstruction is whether the previously proved observable slack $e=2f-u$ decreases on every branch. The following answers this \emph{no for every valid canonical source}, without asserting arbitrary-machine hardness.
\section{The exact forward/backward sibling law}
For a coherent original-source partial assignment $p$, let $f$ be the free original variables and $u$ the uncovered original clauses. An uncovered original clause is not yet satisfied by any pinned literal, even if it contains no free variables. Put $e=2f-u$. For any free original variable $x$, let $a_\pm(x,p)$ count its uncovered original positive/negative occurrence clauses, and $h_x=4-a_+-a_-$ its covered occurrences. Each $0\le a_\pm\le2$.
\begin{theorem}[Sibling conservation]\label{thm:sib}
On both ordinary residual children,
\[
\boxed{e(p,x=1)=e(p)-2+a_+(x,p),\quad
 e(p,x=0)=e(p)-2+a_-(x,p),}
\]
\[
\boxed{e(p,x=1)+e(p,x=0)=2e(p)-h_x.}
\]
Both child slacks are at most the parent. Both equal the parent iff all four occurrences of $x$ are uncovered. The identities are syntactic, valid for SAT and UNSAT parents/children, and hold even before UP. Correct cofactor decision is SAT by OR and UNSAT by AND; failure of one child does not refute the parent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pinning $x$ decreases $f$ by one; pinning $x$ TRUE covers exactly the $a_+$ uncovered positive original clauses, while FALSE covers exactly $a_-$ uncovered negative original clauses. Thus $u_b=u-a_b$, giving the first equations. Summing and using $a_++a_-=4-h_x$ yields the second. Bounds and the simultaneous equality criterion follow from each polarity's exact source quota of two.
\end{proof}
\section{An explicit flat-slack path of linear length}
Define the primal graph $G_F$ on original variables, connecting variables sharing an original clause. Its degree is at most eight, because each variable belongs to four width-three clauses.
\begin{theorem}[Every canonical source has a polynomially discoverable plateau]\label{thm:plat}
Every valid source with $n$ variables and $m=4n/3$ original clauses has an independent set $I\subseteq V(G_F)$, constructible by minimum-ID greedy closed-neighborhood deletion, with
\[
\boxed{|I|\ge\lceil n/9\rceil.}
\]
For any $0\le t\le |I|$, any $t$ distinct chosen variables of $I$ and any assignment of those variables, the original-source cofactor is unit-propagation-stable and satisfies
\[
\boxed{f=n-t,\;\; u=m-2t,\;\; r_3=m-4t,\;\;
r_2=2t,\;\;h=4t,\;\;e=2n-m=2n/3.}
\]
The parameter $e$ stays exactly constant for at least $\lceil n/9\rceil$ legal branch decisions and for \emph{both} values of every selected variable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The primal maximum degree is at most $4(3-1)=8$. Greedy deletion of one closed neighborhood per selected vertex removes at most nine vertices, hence outputs at least $\lceil n/9\rceil$ variables. Independence means every source clause has at most one pinned variable. For each chosen variable its four source clauses are distinct and untouched by earlier chosen pins. Exactly two become satisfied, each contributing two covered free occurrence slots; the other two become uncovered binaries. Therefore $2t$ clauses are covered, $2t$ are binary and $m-4t$ remain ternary. No clause is unit/empty. Thus $u=m-2t$, $h=4t$, and $e=2(n-t)-(m-2t)=2n/3$. No SAT/UNSAT label or policy oracle was used.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Explicit-child corollary.} Since $t\le |I|$ and in particular the first $\lceil n/9\rceil$ pivots leave $m-4t=\Omega(n)$ untouched ternary clauses, a materialized fixed-width indexed literal-list cofactor has $\Omega(n\log n)=\Omega(N)$ bits relative to the standard $N=\Theta(n\log n)$-bit original. Thus these raw children cannot meet $M=O(N^\lambda)$ for $\lambda<1$. This is \emph{not} a bound on semantic compression, alternative encodings, hereditary target gadgets, or arbitrary SAT algorithms. The raw cofactor does not preserve the original target grammar.
