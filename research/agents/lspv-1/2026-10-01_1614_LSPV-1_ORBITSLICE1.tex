\paragraph{Primary-literature preflight and obstacles.}
vMS Definition 4.1/Lemma 4.2 specifies a local Reed--Muller extractor existentially, then RMV evaluates it on every MV line and every seed. PAIR2COVER1 and WEDGE1 reduce the global quadratic kernel to legal slice geometry but do not identify those slices. SU05 supplies a source-sanctioned realization:
\[
E_q(x,v)=\widehat x(Av)\circ\widehat x(A^2v)\circ\cdots\circ\widehat x(A^t v).
\]
SU05 Lemma 4.16 converts every q-ary symbol with the same binary-code coordinate and explicitly permits Hadamard. vMS states that for every MV line \(L\), \(E^{\widehat g_L}(y)\) is included for every seed \(y\), recursively on every legal line. Thus the formula below is exact for the declared SU05/Hadamard instantiation, but not asserted to be the unique vMS implementation.

\paragraph{Exact declared realization.}
Fix one legal RMV line \(L:B\to B^2\), where \(B=\mathbb F_p^d\), \(d=r/2\), and put \(f=\widehat g_L:B\to\mathbb F_p\). Let \(A\in GL_d(\mathbb F_p)\) be the SU05 generator matrix satisfying
\[
\{A^jv:1\le j<p^d\}=B\setminus\{0\}\quad\text{for every }v\ne0.
\]
Let \(\kappa:\mathbb F_p\to\mathbb F_2^k\) be the fixed binary label map, with \(k=\lceil\log_2p\rceil\). For seed \(v\in B\) and Hadamard mask \(u\in\mathbb F_2^k\),
\[
\boxed{g_i(L,v,u)=\langle \kappa(f(A^iv)),u\rangle_{\mathbb F_2}},\qquad1\le i\le m.
\]
Define
\[
\boxed{
X_{L,v}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\kappa(f(Av))^T\\
\kappa(f(A^2v))^T\\
\vdots\\
\kappa(f(A^mv))^T
\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb F_2^{m\times k},
\qquad S_{L,v}=\operatorname{im}X_{L,v}.
}
\]
Then \(g(L,v,u)=X_{L,v}u\), so this is exactly the Hadamard output slice used by PAIR2COVER1/POLAR1/WEDGE1.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: cyclic-window reduction.}
Fix \(v_0\ne0\), set \(N=p^d-1\), and define
\[
a_t=\kappa(f(A^tv_0))\in\mathbb F_2^k,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z.
\]
Every nonzero seed is uniquely \(A^sv_0\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{
X_{L,A^sv_0}=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{s+1}^T\\
a_{s+2}^T\\
\vdots\\
a_{s+m}^T
\end{bmatrix}.
}
\]
Thus the complete nonzero-seed legal slice family for one MV line is exactly the family of length-\(m\) cyclic windows of one explicit binary-label word. The zero seed is separate:
\[
X_{L,0}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\kappa(f(0))^T\\
\vdots\\
\kappa(f(0))^T
\end{bmatrix},\qquad \dim S_{L,0}\le1.
\]
This is source-specific, not a generic locality or short-source argument.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: exterior-incidence target in window form.}
Define
\[
W_L=\operatorname{span}_{s}S_{L,A^sv_0},\qquad
U_L=\operatorname{span}_{s}\bigwedge\nolimits^2S_{L,A^sv_0}.
\]
By WEDGE1, \(W_L=\mathbb F_2^m\) and \(U_L=\bigwedge^2\mathbb F_2^m\) suffice to kill every common quadratic relation for this legal line family. By PAIR2COVER1, to falsify an \(O(m)\) global product-span theorem it suffices to extract an independent direction set \(T\) from these window images whose legal co-occurrence edge set \(E\) satisfies
\[
|T|+|E|=\omega(m).
\]
Legal co-occurrence is now exact: two directions are adjacent precisely when they lie in the image of the same legal window matrix.

\paragraph{Boolean source degree remains OPEN.}
For fixed \(L\), the source-seed coordinate is \(v\mapsto\kappa_j(f(A^iv))\). Bounded degree of \(f\) over the odd-prime field does not, by any reviewed theorem, imply low ANF degree over the binary bits encoding \(v\). Hadamard makes only the mask dependence linear. Generic field-degree, locality, list-decoding, short-source and canonicity shortcuts remain retired.
