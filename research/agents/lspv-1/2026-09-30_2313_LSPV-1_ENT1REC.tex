\section*{LSPV-1: accepted-proof entropy audit and canonical single-proof target}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. This report recovers the substantive body of the preceding LSPV-1 run after its GitHub write failure; it does not strengthen any claim from that run.

\paragraph{Accepted-proof entropy is not presently a universal route.}
The exact PCPP/PCP completeness statements checked in the preceding run have the quantifier form
\[
x\in L \Longrightarrow \exists \pi\; \Pr_R[V^\pi(x;R)=1]=1,
\]
rather than a lower bound on the number of perfectly accepted proofs. Perfect completeness is existential and does not itself give proof multiplicity.

The construction contains canonical algebraic structure without yielding proof multiplicity. The verified logical shape is
\[
P \longrightarrow \text{canonical algebraic tables} \longrightarrow \exists\text{ accepting recursive auxiliary proofs},
\]
not a theorem of the form
\[
\#\{\text{perfectly accepting proofs}\}\ge 2^{\Omega(L)}.
\]

\paragraph{Status of the previous entropy implication.}
The SAME\_REALIZATION implication
\[
\log_2|\mathcal A|>h^{r/2}\left(\Delta^5+1+m+\binom m2\right)\Longrightarrow\rho=M
\]
remains \textbf{CONDITIONAL}. The grid-slice entropy inequality remains \textbf{PROVED/EXACT}, but its multiplicity premise currently has no verified BGH+/vMS lower bound. Accepted-family entropy is therefore not presently load-bearing.

\paragraph{Canonical single-proof route.}
The revised SAME\_REALIZATION chain is
\[
\boxed{\text{one canonical honest PCPP proof}\rightarrow\widehat y\rightarrow\widehat y|_L\rightarrow E_{\mathrm{SU}}\rightarrow\{g_i\}}.
\]
The target is whether legal RMV restrictions contain
\[
0,\qquad e_i,\qquad e_i+e_j.
\]
If they do, then
\[
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_ig_j\}=1+m+\binom m2=\Theta(m^2),
\]
retiring quadratic contraction for that canonical realization without an accepted-proof multiplicity assumption.

\paragraph{Hostile and barrier audit.}
