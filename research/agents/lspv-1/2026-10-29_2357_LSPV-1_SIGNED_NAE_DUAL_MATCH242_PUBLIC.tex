\section{Original source extrema and coherent assignments}
For each original proper ternary clause $C_j$, let $\nu_{ij}$ denote the original signed occurrence of variable $x_i$ ($1$ for negative, $0$ for positive). Local $S_j$ means ALL three literals true and requires $a_i=1\oplus\nu_{ij}$; $U_j$ means ALL false and requires $a_i=\nu_{ij}$. Inversion swaps $S_j,U_j$ on every occurrence; the same $x_i$ is shared, never an independent occurrence variable. $SS,UU$ for distinct original clauses $j,k$ are compatible iff there is NO opposite-signed common variable. $SU,US$ are compatible iff there is NO same-signed common variable. Ordinary source SAT needs only $t_j(a)\ge1$, not three true literals. Coherent complete assignments obey
\[
t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a),\quad
t_j(a\oplus S)=t_j(a)+|S\cap L^0_j(a)|-|S\cap L^1_j(a)|.
\]

\section{An explicit balanced-signed dual-to-matching SAT theorem}
\begin{theorem}
Suppose $F$ has proper width-three source clauses paired exactly as $(D_p,\neg D_p)$, and every original variable appears in exactly two distinct source pairs. Let $G$ have one vertex per pair and an edge $i=pq$ for every original variable appearing at the two pairs $p,q$. Give it parity $\delta_i=\nu_{pi}\oplus\nu_{qi}$, from the designated first source member of each pair. If every signed dual cycle has parity XOR zero, $F$ has a uniform deterministically constructible satisfying assignment in $O(N\log N+r^2)$ indexed word-RAM operations and $O(N+r\log N)$ bits, where $r$ is the number of clause pairs and $N$ their physical source bit encoding length. On any failed recognition test, the algorithm returns UNKNOWN, not UNSAT. Every accepted $F$ has precisely two positive and two negative ORIGINAL occurrences per variable and $3r=2n$, $m=2r$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Check source-pair matching and all original signed occurrences. Solve the linear parity constraints $e_p\oplus e_q=\delta_i$ by BFS on the signed dual graph, including parallel-edge 2-cycles. Balancedness is exactly the condition for consistency. Define $g_i=\nu_{pi}\oplus e_p$ using either source pair containing $i$. Orient pair $p$ by choosing $D_p$ or its complement according to $e_p$, and rename $a_i=y_i\oplus g_i$. The formula becomes the monotone not-all-equal conjunction
\[
\bigwedge_{p=1}^{r}\left[(\bigvee_{i\in E_p}y_i)
 \land(\bigvee_{i\in E_p}\neg y_i)\right],\qquad |E_p|=3.
\]
The dual graph is cubic with no loops (parallel edges allowed). Construct a bipartite double cover with left and right copies of its vertices, and replace every dual edge $i=pq$ by directed left-right edges $p_Lq_R$ and $q_Lp_R$, both indexed by the real original variable $i$. This is a 3-regular bipartite multigraph. For every subset $U$ of its left vertices, $3|U|$ incidences meet at most three incidences per right vertex, proving $|\Gamma(U)|\ge|U|$. Hall's theorem gives a perfect matching, found by deterministic augmenting paths. Let $R$ be the set of underlying original-variable edge IDs used by that matching and set $y_i=1$ iff $i\in R$. Every dual vertex $p$ has one matching edge leaving its left copy and one entering its right copy. These represent one or two DISTINCT original incident variables, hence precisely one or two of its three literals $y_i$ are true. Every paired original source clause is satisfied. Undo the variable rename and check every original clause. With $O(r)$ graph edges, naive augmenting paths use $O(r^2)$ indexed operations after deterministic $O(N\log N)$ parsing and balancedness tests. Input, graph records, matching, witness, and verification occupy $O(N+r\log N)$ bits.
\end{proof}
\section{Concrete falsifiers, forbidden cubes and charging}
Connected cubic duals (for example cubic prisms) yield arbitrarily large connected strict signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF inputs in this tractable class; both coherent $0^n$ and $1^n$ fail. For $K_4$ dual: $n=6,m=8$, the four category vectors at $0^6$, one flip, two flips, and $1^6$ are respectively $(4,0,0,4)$, $(2,2,2,2)$, $(1,3,3,1)$, $(4,0,0,4)$, while the matching witness is a full SAT model. A single flipped sign on just one source-pair occurrence creates an UNBALANCED signed dual with 20 independently counted SAT models; the method correctly returns UNKNOWN. Thus the converse of the theorem is FALSE.

The complementary positive/negative clauses on every 3-subset of five variables form a constant-size 20-source UNSAT core: every 2-coloring of five variables has a monochrome triple. It can be source-verified by the ten positive/negative original clause pairs and 32 complete local colorings. This $K_5^{(3)}$ UNSAT control is OUTSIDE strict22 (six occurrences of each polarity per variable), so must not be transferred to strict22. Fano-paired UNSAT is another outside-class control.

A real forbidden original-variable partial cube arises from source $C_0=(x_1\vee x_2\vee x_3)$ and original $C_3=(\neg x_1\vee\neg x_4\vee\neg x_5)$. Their non-tautological source-parent resolvent on $x_1$ is $(x_2\vee x_3\vee\neg x_4\vee\neg x_5)$, excluding all assignments with $x_2=x_3=0,x_4=x_5=1$. Under this cube the two source clauses reduce to contradictory units $x_1,\neg x_1$. This is KNOWN resolution, not new inference soundness.
