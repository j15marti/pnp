\section{Rank quotient and two exact algorithms}
Use the paid AFFINECORE69 parser to replace each complete four-clause parity quartet with its XOR-3 equation, retaining $\kappa$ residual clauses. Solve the affine equations. If consistent, the entire affine solution space is uniquely $x=x_0+Vy$, $y\in\mathbb F_2^D$. A residual clause $C_i$ is false on an affine coset $B_i=\{y:M_i y=b_i\}$, possibly empty, of codimension $c_i=\operatorname{rank}M_i\le3$.
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT]
Let $t$ be the rank of all restriction rows $M_i$ for nonempty $B_i$. There is a surjective linear map $\pi:\mathbb F_2^D\to\mathbb F_2^t$ such that every bad-set predicate factors through $\pi$. Hence
\[
\#SAT(F)=2^{D-t}|\{z\in\mathbb F_2^t:z\text{ avoids all }B_i\}|.
\]
For $J\subseteq[\kappa]$, if the stacked system $M_Jy=b_J$ has rank $r_J$ and consistency bit $\epsilon_J$, then
\[
\#SAT(F)=\sum_{J\subseteq[\kappa]}(-1)^{|J|}\epsilon_J2^{D-r_J}.
\]
Thus exact SAT decision is available in $(1+\min\{2^t,2^\kappa\})\operatorname{poly}(N)$ word time and polynomial peak words. Every multiword operation and failed trial is charged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose independent basis rows for the exceptional restriction rowspace to define $\pi$. All its fibers have size $2^{D-t}$ and all clause statuses are constant along each fiber. For the second expression, intersect the forbidden affine cosets and apply inclusion--exclusion and rank-nullity. Gaussian row operations implement both methods; the smaller exponent is selected after charged normalization. These are upper bounds, never mandatory decision costs.
\end{proof}
\section{Exact obstruction, amortization, and first interactions}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, declared clause-insertion calculus]
A source-complete normalizer reads and inserts original residual clauses one at a time into the affine solution universe. Define the independent semantic potential
\[
\rho_j=\left|\mathbb F_2^D\setminus\bigcup_{i\le j}B_i\right|.
\]
For every nonempty bad coset of codimension $c_j$,
\[
0\le\rho_{j-1}-\rho_j\le2^{D-c_j},\qquad
\rho_j\ge2^D\left(1-\sum_{i\le j}2^{-c_i}\right).
\]
If all cosets have codimension at least $c$, UNSAT needs at least $2^c$ charged insertions; equality requires disjoint codimension-$c$ cosets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The assignments newly removed by clause $j$ belong to $B_j$, which contains precisely $2^{D-c_j}$ elements. Telescope and use equality throughout for the disjointness condition.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT: two-clause compatibility]
Two residual clauses that are individually satisfiable over a nonempty affine core are jointly inconsistent if and only if their bad sets are complementary affine hyperplanes of the core.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each proper nonempty affine subspace contains at most half the core. Two such sets cover it exactly only if both contain half and are disjoint; disjoint affine hyperplanes over $\mathbb F_2$ are complementary fibers of one nonzero linear form. The reverse is immediate.
\end{proof}
The three clauses $(x)$, $(\neg x\vee y)$, $(\neg x\vee\neg y)$ are jointly UNSAT, but every pair is SAT. Eight signed width-three clauses forbidding all assignments to three independent variables attain the sharp rank-three coset-cover threshold: seven cannot cover the universe, eight can. This constant restricted bound does not imply exponential solving work.
