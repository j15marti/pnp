\section*{Fixed model and quantifiers}
For every integer $h\ge1$, let $P_h$ encode $h+1$ pigeons in $h$ holes, with original Boolean variables $x_{i,j}$, positive source clauses $\bigvee_{j=1}^{h}x_{i,j}$ for each pigeon $i$ and negative pair clauses $\neg x_{i,j}\vee\neg x_{k,j}$ for every distinct pigeons $i,k$ at the same hole. There are $h(h+1)$ variables and $(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ source clauses, binary-indexed signed input bits $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$.

Haken's theorem in its \emph{ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution} model, with no auxiliaries and no non-Resolution inference rules, states:
\[
\exists a>0\ \exists h_0\ \forall h\ge h_0\ \forall\text{ Resolution DAG refutations }\rho\text{ of }P_h:
\quad |\rho|\ge 2^{ah}.
\]
The measure is number of clauses/lines in an explicitly encoded ordinary Resolution proof. The statement is not about all SAT deciders, Res$(\oplus)$, ER or the mixed calculus.

An \emph{additive RUP log} is a finite explicitly encoded sequence of added clauses $(C_1,\ldots,C_s)$, ending with the empty clause, such that UP on the original source plus preceding additions and units negating each literal of $C_i$ produces a conflict. No RAT, PR, newly asserted definitions or semantic oracles are allowed. DRUP may additionally delete existing clauses.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial RUP/DRUP logs on all UNSAT inputs are impossible]
There are constants $c>0$ and $h_1$ such that every additive RUP refutation, and every deletion-augmented DRUP refutation, of the original $P_h$ for $h\ge h_1$ has complete encoded proof length $E_h\ge 2^{ch}$. This holds independently of whether SAT belongs to P.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $L=N_h+E_h$. The LSPV-1/412 elaborator, equivalently the standard RUP-to-Resolution construction, replaces each RUP-added clause $C_i$ by an original-source-authenticated strictly RES-derived subclause $D_i\subseteq C_i$. A unit-propagation contradiction under $\neg C_i$ can be replayed against the strengthened previous database $\{D_j:j<i\}$; resolving recorded unit reasons backwards produces a derived subclause, without Weakening. Each of at most $L$ log lines requires at most $L$ unit rounds, each scanning at most $L$ literal positions, so a deliberately naive elaborator emits at most $K L^3$ ordinary RES nodes for an absolute $K$, including its final empty resolvent. Retaining all clauses in a DRUP proof instead of deleting them cannot invalidate any RUP-UP contradiction, so the same bound covers DRUP. The output contains all parent IDs and signed pivots and uses at most a fixed polynomial in $L$ bits; strict verifier and constructor work similarly have polynomial input-output cost.

Haken now forces $2^{ah}\le K(N_h+E_h)^3+N_h$, whence
$E_h\ge K^{-1/3}2^{a h/3-o(h)}-N_h=2^{\Omega(h)}$.
Since $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log h)$, this is superpolynomial in the original encoded length. This uses an unconditional proof-format lower bound only. The solver's behavior on other inputs is irrelevant.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[RUP logging branch of the proposed B3 transfer fails]
No fixed uniform total exact SAT algorithm with worst-case polynomial output-inclusive runtime on all valid SAT, UNSAT and malformed strings can emit a valid additive RUP/DRUP refutation on \emph{every} UNSAT input. Even under the hypothetical SAT-in-P premise, it must use a different proof language, non-RUP additions, or non-polynomial output on some sources.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
On $P_h$ the required log alone uses $2^{\Omega(h)}$ output bits, whereas polynomial runtime in the source length $N_h$ writes at most $\mathrm{poly}(h)$ bits. Contradiction.
\end{proof}

\section*{ITERATE: interpretation and exact non-results}
This strengthens the practical verdict on /412: its conditional restricted B1 inequality remains true for any output-logging solver, including slow solvers, but \emph{the additional all-input polynomial-runtime logging hypothesis is unconditionally impossible} for the specified additive RUP/DRUP protocol. It therefore cannot be a noncircular proof of B3. Its bridge is not refuted; its hoped-for polynomial-time antecedent is eliminated. The earlier /398 theorem already ruled out \emph{all-mixed-to-ordinary-RES} polynomial backtranslation using Haken/Cook; this run's more specific increment directly targets the successor /412 RUP solver-output architecture.

The result does NOT preclude polynomial ER, RAT or PR proofs of PHP. Cook's ER proofs are polynomial; Buss--Thapen give stronger PR/RAT distinctions. No backtranslation is claimed for those. The original fixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ admits richer rules, so this theorem does not establish a superpolynomial mixed proof cost or refute B3 for the SAME calculus. Need a sound source-authenticated polynomial generator for richer native certificates plus an independent reason that such certificates exist under SAT in P, rather than only a stronger logging convention.
