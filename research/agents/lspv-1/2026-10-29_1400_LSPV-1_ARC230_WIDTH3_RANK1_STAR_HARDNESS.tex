\section{BUILD: exact rank-one ternary-clause leaf}
\begin{lemma}[Signed ternary-clause projection]
For any three source literals $\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3$ on distinct names, introduce three fresh bits $a,b,c$ and set
\[
G_\ell=(\neg a\vee\ell_1)\land(\neg b\vee\ell_2)
\land(\neg c\vee\ell_3)\land(a\oplus b\oplus c=1).
\]
Then for all assignments to the source names,
\[
\exists a,b,c\,G_\ell\ \equiv\ \ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If the parity is odd at least one selector is true, so the corresponding implication forces a true source literal. Conversely select one true source literal, put one on its selector and zero on the other two. The parity and all three implications hold. The three binary clauses and one rank-one affine row are exactly source-checkable.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[NP-completeness of a verified width-three rank-one star class]
Let $\mathcal T_3$ consist of explicitly encoded and verifiable trees of modules, with each source constraint owned exactly once inside its owner's variable bag, connected occurrence bags for every shared name (running intersection), only unary/binary CNF and at most one affine row per module, and separators of at most three variables per edge. SAT for $\mathcal T_3$ is NP-complete, even restricted to supplied star trees whose centre owns no constraints and whose leaves own precisely three signed implications and one odd XOR3 row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any proper 3CNF $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$ over $n$ source variables, make a root bag of all $n$ source variables with no constraints. For each clause $j$, make a leaf with the three source variables of that clause, private selectors $a_j,b_j,c_j$, and the constraints from the lemma. Connect each leaf directly to the root. The intersection of a leaf bag with the root is exactly its three source variables. Source variables occur in the root and any subset of adjacent leaves, hence satisfy running intersection; private selectors occur in one bag. The root affine rank is zero and every leaf affine rank is one. The entire construction and its authenticated clause-indexed manifest have polynomial bit size: $n+3m$ variables, $3m$ binary clauses, $m$ affine rows, $m+1$ modules and $m$ edges. The lemma shows that existential elimination of all $3m$ fresh selectors recovers precisely $F$. This is a uniform polynomial many-one reduction from proper 3SAT. An assignment to all variables is polynomially checkable, so the class belongs to NP.
\end{proof}
\begin{proposition}[An exact three-port relation leaves the base grammar]
For a single positive clause the leaf projects to $R(x,y,z)=x\vee y\vee z$, which has seven of eight tuples. No conjunction of 2CNF clauses and affine equations on those same three names represents $R$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Any nonzero affine equation has only four satisfying assignments, so a seven-model conjunction cannot contain one; it must be 2CNF. But the majority of the three models $100,010,001$ is $000$, which $R$ excludes. Every 2CNF solution relation is majority-closed. The argument forbids only auxiliary-free representations on the exposed ports; the leaf itself gives an exact representation with three auxiliary names.
\end{proof}
