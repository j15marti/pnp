\section*{PAIRDEG1 exact theorem}
Let \(T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_r\}\) be an independent legal direction set and let
\[
M_{s,a}=\mathbf 1[t_a\in S_s].
\]
Write
\[
q_s=\sum_{a=1}^r M_{s,a},
\qquad
\mu_{ab}=\sum_s M_{s,a}M_{s,b}\quad(a\ne b).
\]
Thus \(q_s\) is the number of selected independent directions contained in slice \(s\), and \(\mu_{ab}\) is the number of legal slices witnessing the edge \(\{t_a,t_b\}\).

For each vertex \(a\), define its total witnessed pair incidence
\[
I_a=\sum_{s:M_{s,a}=1}(q_s-1).
\]
Double counting triples \((s,a,b)\) with \(a\ne b\) and \(t_a,t_b\in S_s\) gives the exact identity
\[
\boxed{
I_a=\sum_{b\ne a}\mu_{ab}.
}
\]
Let
\[
\lambda_a=
\begin{cases}
\max\{\mu_{ab}:b\ne a,\ \mu_{ab}>0\},& I_a>0,\\
1,& I_a=0.
\end{cases}
\]
If \(d_a\) is the degree of \(t_a\) in \(E_{\rm full}\), then
\[
I_a=\sum_{b:\{a,b\}\in E_{\rm full}}\mu_{ab}
\le \lambda_a d_a.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
d_a\ge \left\lceil\frac{I_a}{\lambda_a}\right\rceil.
}
\]
Summing degrees and using \(2|E_{\rm full}|=\sum_a d_a\) yields the exact computable certificate
\[
\boxed{
|E_{\rm full}|
\ge
\left\lceil
\frac12\sum_{a=1}^r
\left\lceil\frac{I_a}{\lambda_a}\right\rceil
\right\rceil.
}
\]
Consequently PAIR2COVER1 gives
\[
\boxed{
\rho(H)\ge
1+r+
\left\lceil
\frac12\sum_{a=1}^r
\left\lceil\frac{I_a}{\lambda_a}\right\rceil
\right\rceil.
}
\]
All statements above are PROVED/EXACT finite-dimensional identities or inequalities for one fixed declared legal realization.

\paragraph{Relation to a global multiplicity bound.}
If \(\lambda=\max_{a<b:\mu_{ab}>0}\mu_{ab}\), then \(\lambda_a\le\lambda\) for every active vertex. Hence the vertex-wise certificate is at least as informative as replacing every local overlap by the same worst-case \(\lambda\) before summing. It can be strictly stronger when high witness multiplicity is concentrated on only a few vertices.
