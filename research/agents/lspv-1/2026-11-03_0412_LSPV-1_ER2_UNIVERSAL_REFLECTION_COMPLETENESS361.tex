\section*{One fixed calculus and length measure}
Preserve the preselected $R=R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$: zero-cost authenticated signed CNF sources, unit-weight distinct derived shared-DAG nodes, strict binary Resolution, typed AND3, GF(2) XOR, LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid source-independent conservative signed two-input AND VARDEF with clauses $(\neg q\vee a)$, $(\neg q\vee b)$, $(q\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)$ and $|a|\ne|b|$. Let $E$ be the already-legal strict RES plus VARDEF fragment. Signed-polarity Boolean gates can be synthesized with polynomially many such definitions and strict RES steps. No new rule is authorized. For sound complete $S=E,R$ write
\[
W_S(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_S\bot}|\pi_{\mathrm{paid}}|,\qquad
\overline W_S(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ \mathrm{valid\ UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}F|\le N}} W_S(F),
\]
with an empty maximum zero. The minimum exists by completeness of RES and integral nonnegative costs. $N$ denotes bits in the fixed binary signed-source encoding, not variable count.

Fix \emph{one} total uniform externally correct polynomial-time three-label SAT decider $A$, including MALFORMED. At each exact source length $\ell$ build a fixed uniform polynomial-size Boolean circuit asserting
\[
\mathrm{Valid}_\ell(s)\ \land\ \mathrm{Eval}_\ell(s,y)\
\land\ \mathrm{Reject}_{A,\ell}(s).
\]
Let $H_{A,\ell}$ be its authenticated signed two-input AND Tseitin CNF, with the three outputs asserted. There is $d_A$ with $M_A(\ell)=|\operatorname{enc}H_{A,\ell}|\le(\ell+1)^{d_A}$ after changing constants. External correctness of $A$ makes every $H_{A,\ell}$ UNSAT. This alone proves no short $E$ proof.

\begin{lemma}[Proof-node to evidence-bit normalization]
An $M$-bit input's $q$-node $E$ refutation admits polynomial bit serialization and polynomial-size EF simulation in $M+q+1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There are at most $M$ distinct source identifiers and $q$ fresh extension outputs; rename the latter by first use. Every non-tautological clause has at most $M+q$ signed literals. Every proof record has at most two parents, a rule/pivot, or two signed gate inputs. This gives $O((M+q)^2\log(M+q+2))$ explicit evidence bits. Standard EF simulates both strict Resolution and conservative signed two-input extensions with polynomial overhead. The proof optimizer is not being charged to $W_E$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[ER2 universal-proof specialization]
There is one polynomial $p_A$ such that
\[
\forall\ell\ge1\quad\forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}_{\mathrm{valid}}
\ (|\operatorname{enc}F|=\ell)\quad\forall\pi:
\left[\pi\text{ is an }E\text{-refutation of }H_{A,\ell}\right]
\Rightarrow W_E(F)\le p_A(\ell+|\pi_{\mathrm{paid}}|+1).
\]
The polynomial does not depend on $F$, its optimum, or a nonuniform choice of $A_\ell$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Normalize $\pi$ and turn it into a polynomial-size EF refutation of the signed Tseitin CNF $H_{A,\ell}$. Substitute the literal constants $s=\operatorname{enc}F$ throughout this EF proof, and also the correctly evaluated internal bits of $\mathrm{Valid}_\ell$ and $A$ on those fixed bits. EF substitution, including simplification of constant gates and fresh relabeling of extension wires, has polynomial overhead. Since $F$ is valid and $A(F)=\mathrm{UNSAT}$, the fixed validity and rejection output assertions become true, leaving a short EF refutation of the Tseitin CNF asserting $\mathrm{Eval}_\ell(\operatorname{enc}F,y)=1$.

Now authenticate that specialized evaluator from the \emph{actual} original clauses $F$: for each source clause $C_j$, introduce paid conservative signed AND gates for a De Morgan OR tree computing its truth value. Strict RES from the original signed clause $C_j$ and the defining slots yields the unit output literal in $O(|C_j|)$ inferences. For example, if $C_j=(a\vee b)$ and $u=(\neg a\wedge\neg b)$, resolving $C_j$ with $(\neg u\vee\neg a)$, then with $(\neg u\vee\neg b)$ derives $(\neg u)$. Conjoin clause output literals by paid gates and derive the evaluator-output unit. Empty input clauses already refute, and duplicate literals/constants are simplified. All specialized evaluator gate-source clauses are reproduced by \emph{paid} VARDEF, never imported as free source axioms. Standard polynomial EF-to-ER simulation, using exactly the fixed signed two-input conservative extensions and strict RES, now composes the EF refutation with these authenticated premises. Its paid node count is polynomial in $\ell+|\pi_{\mathrm{paid}}|+1$. No mixed XOR/AND3/CONVERT restriction theorem is assumed. A rejecting machine trace without a separately justified universal proof is not an UNSAT certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One-family completeness, exact scope]
For \emph{every fixed externally correct polynomial-time} $A$, let $Q_A(\ell)=W_E(H_{A,\ell})$. Then
\[
\boxed{\ Q_A(\ell)=(\ell+1)^{O_A(1)}\text{ uniformly in }\ell
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\overline W_E(N)=(N+1)^{O(1)}\text{ uniformly in }N.\ }
\]
Either equivalent condition also implies polynomial $\overline W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}$, because $E$ is a subcalculus of $R$. No implication from bare SAT$\in P$ to either condition has been proved.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $Q_A(\ell)$ is polynomial, apply the preceding lemma separately to every valid UNSAT $F$ of exact length $\ell$ and maximize over $\ell\le N$. Conversely $H_{A,\ell}$ is a genuine UNSAT CNF with $M_A(\ell)=\ell^{O_A(1)}$ input bits. A polynomial bound on $\overline W_E$ at that size bounds $Q_A(\ell)$ polynomially. Every polynomial rescaling is explicit. The equivalence is about the stronger $E$ family condition, not about B3 for the larger $R$.
\end{proof}

\section*{Exact falsifiers and finite hostile controls}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: External correctness of $A$ forces short $H_{A,\ell}$ proofs. \textbf{OPEN/UNSUPPORTED}, still the missing nonrelativizing implication.
\item[] F2: Specialization makes every Tseitin source clause free in a proof of $F$. \textbf{FALSIFIED}; evaluator definitions and source-to-output derivations are explicitly paid.
\item[] F3: The lemma proves substitution closure for \emph{all} mixed AND3/XOR/CONVERT proof DAGs. \textbf{FALSIFIED AS AN INFERENCE}; it only compiles the fixed $E$ subsystem.
\item[] F4: For a fixed correct P-time $A$, polynomial one-family $E$ proofs are intrinsically a strictly weaker condition than all-instance $E$ polynomial boundedness. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the equivalence.
\end{enumerate}
The independently checked finite toy uses two signed unit-presence bits $a,b$, witness $x$, and a three-label total parser: $11\mapsto\mathrm{UNSAT}$, $00,01,10\mapsto\mathrm{SAT}$, and all malformed codes$\mapsto\mathrm{MALFORMED}$. The universal counterexample CNF is
\[
H=(a)\land(b)\land(\neg a\vee x)\land(\neg b\vee\neg x).
\]
All four clauses are indispensable. Its strict RES proof derives $(x)$ from $(a),(\neg a\vee x)$, then $(\neg x)$ from $(b),(\neg b\vee\neg x)$, then $\bot$, proving $W_R(H)=3$ by indispensable-source binary ancestry. On rejecting $11$, the actual source $(x)\land(\neg x)$ refutes in one node. All other valid codes have witnesses. Independent JS verified 63 assertions: four clause deletions, six sign repairs, nine label records including malformed, two malformed DIMACS controls, and three corrupted proofs. This is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL} illustration, not a general short-reflection theorem.
