\paragraph{Input from ORBITSLICE1.}

Write a_t=(b_1(t),\ldots,b_k(t)).

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: synchronous column-window normal form.}
For 1\le j\le k define
\[
c_{s,j}:=(b_j(s+1),\ldots,b_j(s+m))^T\in\mathbb F_2^m.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{X_s=[c_{s,1}\ \cdots\ c_{s,k}],\qquad S_s=\operatorname{span}\{c_{s,1},\ldots,c_{s,k}\}.}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{W_L=\operatorname{span}_{s,j}c_{s,j}.}
\]
Thus dim W_L is the rank of one explicit m by Nk block-circulant/window matrix. This uses no locality, field-degree, short-source, or list-decoding shortcut.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: synchronous exterior matrix.}
Because exterior square is generated by wedges of a spanning set,
\[
\bigwedge^2 S_s=\operatorname{span}\{c_{s,j}\wedge c_{s,\ell}:1\le j<\ell\le k\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{U_L=\operatorname{span}_{s,j<\ell}\{c_{s,j}\wedge c_{s,\ell}\}.}
\]
Let \(\mathcal W_L=[c_{s,j}\wedge c_{s,\ell}]_{s,j<\ell}\) in the standard basis of \(\bigwedge^2\mathbb F_2^m\). Then
\[
\boxed{\dim U_L=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}\mathcal W_L.}
\]
The word synchronous is load-bearing: only coordinate windows with the same shift s may be wedged for free. Wedges with distinct shifts are not legal unless a separate legal slice contains both directions. This blocks the invalid rectangularization previously rejected by CROSSSPAN.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: incidence budget and necessary maximality condition.}
Each nonzero-seed slice contributes exterior dimension at most \(\binom{k}{2}\). Hence
\[
\boxed{\dim U_L\le\min\{\binom m2,\ N\binom k2\}.}
\]
Thus WEDGE1 maximality requires
\[
\boxed{N\binom k2\ge\binom m2.}
\]
This is only a dimension-budget necessary condition, not sufficient and not a runtime lower bound. It also shows why full linear window span is inadequate: for k=1, W_L may be large while U_L=0.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: finite certificate formulation.}
For shifts R define
\[
W_R=\operatorname{span}_{s\in R,j}c_{s,j},\qquad U_R=\operatorname{span}_{s\in R,j<\ell}c_{s,j}\wedge c_{s,\ell}.
\]
The ranks are monotone as shifts are added. A finite R with
\[
\dim W_R=m,\qquad \dim U_R=\binom m2
\]
is an exact WEDGE1 certificate that the common quadratic kernel for this line family is zero. PAIR2COVER1 remains available when an independent legal T and actual same-slice edge set E are explicit, giving \(\rho(H)\ge1+|T|+|E|\). The synchronous exterior matrix is computationally preferable because it avoids enumerating exponentially many vectors inside each S_s.
