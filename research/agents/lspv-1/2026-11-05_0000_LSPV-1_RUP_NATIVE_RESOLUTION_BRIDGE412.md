**PROVED/RESTRICTED theorem.** Let valid signed original CNF F have encoded length N. Let a supplied explicit **additive RUP** refutation P of length E end with empty clause. Every line C has unit propagation contradiction from the previous clauses plus assumptions ¬C. There is a uniform deterministic elaborator producing an original-source-authenticated **strict binary Resolution** refutation π with paid nodes q at most H, where H is the total literal inspections in a repeated-full-scan UP run against the stronger derived database. Both H and the generated proof bit length, generation time, independent checking time and all-phase peak space are polynomial in N+E. Thus
$$
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le q_{\mathrm{RES}}(\pi)\le H\le\operatorname{poly}(N+E).
$$
The compiler uses only the already-legal RES fragment and does not assume named DEF facets, GF(2) exports, or private LOCAL wire axioms.

**Proof.** Induct over RUP additions. The compiler stores authenticated D_j ⊆ C_j. Unit propagation refutability is monotone under replacing any prior clause by a subclause: each original unit-reason step either still forces that unit or gives an earlier contradiction. Re-run propagation on this strengthened database. Given assumptions ¬C, each propagated literal u has a strict RES proof of u∨E_u with E_u ⊆ C. Start with its authentic source/reason clause and resolve away literals falsified by previous propagations; assumption-falsified literals are members of C and stay. The conflict reason resolves analogously to D ⊆ C. Repeat. The final line C=∅ yields D=∅. No weakening and no semantic oracle. Each binary pivot consumes a literal occurrence from a recorded reason, hence at most H. A deliberately naive full-scanning implementation has polynomial H in explicitly sized N+E; name/index/source reads and output bits are charged.
