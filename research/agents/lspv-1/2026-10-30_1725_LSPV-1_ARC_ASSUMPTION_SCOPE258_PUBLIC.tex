\section{Four falsifiers and a precise rule}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: Exporting a conditional residual clause unguarded across a policy switch is safe. \textbf{FALSE}.
\item[] F2: Guard-lifted verified clauses change the set of original source models. \textbf{FALSE}.
\item[] F3: Timeout or failed bounded search on one residual proves parent UNSAT. \textbf{FALSE}.
\item[] F4: Saving DPLL nodes necessarily saves total charged computation. \textbf{FALSE on finite controls}.
\end{enumerate}
For an arbitrary ordinary original CNF \(F\), a coherent original-variable partial assignment \(\rho\), and any clause \(D\) including the empty clause, define \(B_\rho\) as the disjunction of the literal opposite to every assignment in \(\rho\), with \(B_\varnothing=\bot\). It is false exactly on full valuations extending \(\rho\).

\begin{theorem}[Contextual clause export, known propositional deduction]
For all \(F,\rho,D\),
\[
\boxed{F\upharpoonright\rho\models D\ \Longleftrightarrow\ F\models B_\rho\vee D.}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{F\upharpoonright\rho\text{ UNSAT}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ F\models B_\rho.}
\]
Once a source-replayable resolution/RUP/complete branch refutation of \(F\wedge\rho\wedge\neg D\) is checked, the clause \(B_\rho\vee D\) may be retained across policy switches without changing \emph{any original source model}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix any full valuation \(a\). If \(a\not\supseteq\rho\), then \(B_\rho(a)=1\); otherwise \(B_\rho(a)=0\), and the exported clause is true exactly when \(D(a)=1\). Therefore all source models satisfy \(B_\rho\vee D\) iff all source models extending \(\rho\) satisfy \(D\). Put \(D=\bot\) for the UNSAT-blocking conclusion. A checked refutation proves the required entailment, so conjoining the entailed clause removes no source model and adds none.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Shortest unsafe source.}
Let \(F=(x\vee y)\wedge(x\vee\neg y)\). The original has two models \(x=1\), yet the residual \(\rho=(x=0)\) has clauses \(y,\neg y\) and is UNSAT. Exporting those \emph{local} units \emph{unguarded} makes the originally SAT source falsely UNSAT. The correct global export is \(B_\rho=x\), or separately the clauses \(x\vee y,x\vee\neg y\). The clauses share same-polarity \(x\) and opposite-polarity \(y\), so none of SS/SU/US/UU all-TRUE/all-FALSE extreme pairs is compatible, while both source clauses are disjunctively SAT under both genuine models.

\paragraph{Bounded-local converse fails.}
All eight proper signed clauses on three variables form an UNSAT source. UP initially has no unit. After one split, every residual has all four signed two-clauses on the remaining pair, again without a unit, so UP with at most one split cannot prove parent UNSAT. Two split layers expose opposite units and suffice. Thus source-certified bounded UP is sound but incomplete; the contextual entailment \emph{equivalence} itself is exact.

\paragraph{Certified adaptive policy semantics.}
A policy may return SAT only with an original-source-checked complete witness; NO under \(\rho\) only with an independently checked original-source refutation, then export \(B_\rho\); or UNRESOLVED with no negative semantic claim. Partial clauses inferred under \(\rho\) receive their assumption guard. A global NO needs certified coverage of all branches, never merely failure of both complete endpoints. A separate complete solver fallback ensures correctness but does not imply polynomial runtime. This complements the LSPV-2 transactional lemma for \emph{unconditional} genuine resolvents retained after aborted elimination, while preserving exact original-clause provenance.
