\paragraph{Setup.}
Let $S_{s_1},\ldots,S_{s_q}\le V=\mathbb F_2^m$ be any ordered family of legal SAME\_REALIZATION slice spaces, with
\[
W_i=\sum_{j\le i}S_{s_j},\qquad
U_i=\sum_{j\le i}\bigwedge^2S_{s_j},
\]
and define the linear innovation
\[
d_i=\dim W_i-\dim W_{i-1}.
\]

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: block-innovation lemma.}
For every such ordered legal family,
\[
\boxed{\dim U_q\ge\sum_{i=1}^q\binom{d_i}{2}.}
\]
Indeed, quotient $W_i$ by $W_{i-1}$. The image of $S_{s_i}$ in $W_i/W_{i-1}$ has dimension $d_i$, hence its exterior square has dimension $\binom{d_i}{2}$. The quotient-induced map
\[
\bigwedge^2 W_i\longrightarrow\bigwedge^2(W_i/W_{i-1})
\]
kills $\bigwedge^2W_{i-1}$ and therefore kills $U_{i-1}$. The image of $\bigwedge^2S_{s_i}$ has dimension $\binom{d_i}{2}$, so
\[
\dim U_i-\dim U_{i-1}\ge\binom{d_i}{2}.
\]
Summing proves the claim.

Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
\dim U_q\ge
\frac12\left(\sum_i d_i^2-\dim W_q\right),
}
\]
because $\sum_i d_i=\dim W_q$.

\paragraph{Connection to DIMSUM2.}
DIMSUM2 gives
\[
\rho(H)\ge1+\dim W+\dim U.
\]
Consequently, on one SAME\_REALIZATION legal family, it is enough to establish
\[
\sum_i d_i^2=\omega(m)
\]
together with the corresponding legal span, to obtain superlinear product-span rank. A spanning $m$-clique and maximal exterior rank are sufficient but no longer necessary.

\paragraph{Useful conditional corollary.}
Suppose legal slices have dimension on the order of $k$ and one can greedily select $\Theta(m/k)$ legal slices, each contributing $\Theta(k)$ new linear dimensions. Then
\[
\sum_i\binom{d_i}{2}=\Omega(mk),
\]
so $\dim U=\Omega(mk)$. In the declared SU05/Hadamard realization $k$ is at most the binary label width $\lceil\log_2p\rceil$. This is only a conditional structural target: no theorem here says the actual SU/vMS legal windows achieve such block innovation.

\paragraph{Interpretation relative to PAIR2COVER1.}
PAIR2COVER1 searches for an independent direction set $T$ and a large legal pair set $E$, yielding
\[
\rho(H)\ge1+r+|E|.
\]
BLOCKINNOV1 is complementary. Rather than selecting individual directions and certifying pair co-occurrence, it lower-bounds the exterior contribution of each legal slice from how many genuinely new linear directions that slice introduces. The active obstruction becomes architecture-specific: an $O(m)$ exterior theorem would require the legal slices to have sufficiently fragmented innovation, for example predominantly $d_i\le1$, or another mechanism keeping $\sum_i d_i^2=O(m)$ for every useful ordering.
