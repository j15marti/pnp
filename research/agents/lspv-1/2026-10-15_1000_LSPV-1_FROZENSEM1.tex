Define \(\mathsf{SAMEBIT}(F,z)\) to be equality of the two SAT-answer bits of \(F[z=0]\) and \(F[z=1]\), not syntactic equality or equality of their solution sets.
\begin{theorem}[One-bit SAT switch]
For every proper 3CNF \(H=\bigwedge_i(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)\), with fresh \(z,u,v,s_i\), set
\[
 W^+(H,z)=\bigwedge_i[(z\vee a_i\vee s_i)\land(\neg s_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)]
 \land(z\vee u\vee v)\land(\neg z\vee u\vee v).
\]
It has \(2m+2\) proper 3-clauses, both signs of \(z\), and
\(\mathsf{SAT}(W^+[z=0])=\mathsf{SAT}(H)\),
\(\mathsf{SAT}(W^+[z=1])=1\).
Hence \(\mathsf{SAMEBIT}(W^+,z)=\mathsf{SAT}(H)\) even though the whole parent is always SAT. Add four proper clauses
\((\neg z\vee r\vee p)\),
\((\neg z\vee r\vee\neg p)\),
\((\neg z\vee\neg r\vee q)\),
\((\neg z\vee\neg r\vee\neg q)\)
to obtain \(W^-\) for which
\(\mathsf{SAMEBIT}(W^-,z)=\neg\mathsf{SAT}(H)\).
Each output record bit is computed from at most a constant number of indexed \(H\) fields using polylog bit time and logarithmic scratch.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

At \(z=1\), setting all \(s_i=0,u=1\) satisfies \(W^+\). The four extra clauses are vacuous at \(z=0\) and demand both \(r=1\) and \(r=0\) at \(z=1\). Indexed output row \(2i\) or \(2i+1\) uses original row \(i\), with constant local arithmetic and fixed trailing records.
\end{proof}
