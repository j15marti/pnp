\section{Exact prime-chain projection and minimal source support}
For every integer $h\ge2$ use pairwise distinct variables
$a_1,\ldots,a_{h+2},x_1,\ldots,x_{h-1}$, and define the original
\emph{proper} width-three clauses
\[
 C_1=(x_1\vee a_1\vee a_2),\quad
 C_j=(\neg x_{j-1}\vee x_j\vee a_{j+1})\ (2\le j<h),\quad
 C_h=(\neg x_{h-1}\vee a_{h+1}\vee a_{h+2}).
\]
Put $F_h=\bigwedge_{j=1}^h C_j$ and
$R_h=\bigvee_{i=1}^{h+2}a_i$.

\begin{theorem}[Tight source-leaf support for a prime chain]
For every $h\ge2$ and every complete boundary assignment $b$,
\[
 (\exists x_1\cdots x_{h-1})\,F_h(b,x)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R_h(b).
 \tag{2}
\]
The clause $R_h$ is a prime implicate of $F_h$.
It has a source-authenticated tree-resolution derivation using exactly
$h$ \emph{distinct} original clauses and $h-1$ resolution steps.
Every sound resolution proof of $R_h$ from \emph{only the clauses of}
$F_h$ must use each of its $h$ original clauses as a source leaf.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If every $a_i=0$, $C_1$ forces $x_1=1$.
Each intermediate clause forces $x_{j}=1$ from $x_{j-1}=1$.
The final clause forces $x_{h-1}=0$, contradiction.
If some $a_i=1$, at least one chain clause is satisfied independently
of the $x$ variables. The remaining implication path has a break:
set all $x$ variables on the left of that break to $1$ and those on the
right to $0$ (before the missing link the implications propagate TRUE,
after it the final negative endpoint permits FALSE). This satisfies all remaining clauses. Hence (2).

Resolving $C_1$ with $C_2$ on $x_1$, then the result with $C_3$
on $x_2$, and so on produces exactly $R_h$. For every $i$,
the boundary with $a_i=1$ and all other $a$ zero satisfies $F_h$,
but falsifies the subclause $R_h\setminus\{a_i\}$.
Thus $R_h$ is prime. Finally, remove any one source clause $C_j$.
Under all $a=0$ the implication chain has a broken link, hence
there exists a satisfying assignment of the remaining $h-1$ clauses
with $R_h=0$. Those clauses do \emph{not} entail $R_h$.
By resolution soundness, any proof of $R_h$ using only original
clauses must include $C_j$. Since $j$ was arbitrary, all $h$
original source leaves are indispensable.
\end{proof}

\noindent The statement is specifically about proof leaves for this
specified \emph{conclusion}. It does not lower-bound arbitrary decision
algorithms or even fast discovery of this recognizably easy chain.
For any fixed leaf budget $k<h$, exhaustive resolution limited to
$k$ distinct original clauses cannot derive $R_h$, while a
source-aware scan recognizes and resolves the chain in linear
number of signed occurrences. This is an \emph{existence versus
acquisition} distinction, not global hardness.

\section{Both-label original-source realization}
For each $a_i$, append four original proper 3-clauses
\[
(\neg a_i\vee u_i\vee v_i),\
(\neg a_i\vee u_i\vee\neg v_i),\
(\neg a_i\vee\neg u_i\vee v_i),\
(\neg a_i\vee\neg u_i\vee\neg v_i)
\tag{3}
\]
with private $u_i,v_i$. Two resolutions on $v_i$ and one on $u_i$
derive $\neg a_i$. Resolving all these units against the authenticated

$F_h^{\rm pin}$ is therefore UNSAT, with a direct full-source refutation.
The original $F_h$ is SAT. Both are proper three-literal CNFs,
with all gadget leaves indexed in the published raw data.
No failed local cube is misreported as a global refutation.
