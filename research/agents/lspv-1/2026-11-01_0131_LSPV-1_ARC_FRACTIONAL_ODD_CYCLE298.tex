\section{All-valid-source conservation and fractional lemma}
For any coherent partial source assignment $p$, let $f$ be free variables, $U(p)$ uncovered original clauses, $u=|U(p)|$, and $e=2f-u$. For free $x$, let $s_x^+,s_x^-$ count its positive and negative occurrences in original clauses already satisfied by a pinned literal. Define
\[
\eta=\sum_{x\text{ free}}\min(s_x^+,s_x^-),\qquad
w_x=|s_x^+-s_x^-|.
\]
For $w_x>0$, let $b_x=0$ when $s_x^+>s_x^-$ and $b_x=1$ when $s_x^->s_x^+$. In a complete satisfying extension $a\supseteq p$ the earlier source-verified signed-spill identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:budget}
e=\eta+\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x{\bf1}[a_x\neq b_x]
 +\sum_{j\in U(p)}(t_j(a)-1).
\end{equation}
It follows by counting exactly two true original signed occurrences per free variable; each uncovered clause in a satisfying completion has $t_j(a)\ge1$.

Form the binary residual multigraph: each original clause retaining exactly two unassigned same-sign literals becomes an edge with its original immutable clause index. Let $\mathcal C$ be any explicitly listed family of source-indexed \emph{odd cycles}. Give each cycle a nonnegative rational $\theta_C$, and require the capacity constraints
\[
\sum_{C\in\mathcal C:j\in E(C)}\theta_C\le1
\quad\text{for every binary-clause source index }j.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Fractional odd-cycle signed budget, all valid target sources]
For every valid canonical source $F$, every coherent $p$, every authenticated cycle certificate satisfying the displayed capacities, and every complete original-source satisfying extension $a\supseteq p$,
\[
\boxed{\sum_{x:w_x>0}w_x{\bf1}[a_x\neq b_x]
\le e-\eta-\left\lceil\sum_{C\in\mathcal C}\theta_C\right\rceil.}
\]
A negative right side proves the entire partial cube UNSAT. A nonnegative right side smaller than some $w_x$ forces $x=b_x$ in every satisfying completion. A group of opposed values with total weight exceeding the budget defines a forbidden partial cube. Neither a positive budget nor failure to find a certificate proves SAT/UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any two-coloring of an odd cycle has at least one equal-colored edge. In an original residual \emph{same-sign} binary clause satisfied by a full assignment, equal endpoint values imply that both literals are TRUE, contributing one to the final excess sum in (\ref{eq:budget}). Let $D$ be the set of source-ID binary edges with equal-colored endpoints. Then
\[
\sum_C\theta_C\le
\sum_C\theta_C\sum_{j\in C}{\bf1}[j\in D]
=\sum_{j\in D}\sum_{C\ni j}\theta_C\le |D|.
\]
The number $|D|$ is integral, so $|D|\ge\lceil\sum_C\theta_C\rceil$. The excess sum in (\ref{eq:budget}) includes every distinct member of $D$. Rearranging proves the inequality; the logical consequences follow from its nonnegative summands. Source clause IDs avoid double counting even if two original clauses have the same variable pair.
\end{proof}

The cycle/capacity proof is an elementary instance of established fractional graph-packing and cut-polytope methods \cite{barahona1985,boros1992,geelen2002}, not new graph theory. Given an explicit certificate of total bit length $B$, source-ID, cycle parity, endpoints and rational capacities can be checked in $\operatorname{poly}(N+B)$ time. Obtaining the certificate is a separate paid problem. A constant-size K5 motif is a polynomially recognizable conditional action on a materialized bounded-degree adjacency structure, not yet a native-machine small-space action.

\section{Exact 15-variable canonical K5 adversarial source}
Take free variables $x_1,\ldots,x_5$, and pinned variables $a_i=x_{5+i}$, $b_i=x_{10+i}$, $1\le i\le5$. Split the ten K5 edges into two complementary five-cycles $P$ and $Q$. For each $(i,j)=P_k$, include $(x_i\lor x_j\lor a_k)$; for $Q_k$, include $(\neg x_i\lor\neg x_j\lor\neg b_k)$. Add the ten closure clauses $(a_i\lor b_i\lor b_{i+1})$ and $(\neg b_i\lor\neg a_i\lor\neg a_{i+1})$ with cyclic indices. The resulting $F_{15}$ has $n=15,m=20$; all original clauses are distinct proper same-sign triples, and every variable has exactly two occurrences of each sign. The compact producer and independent checker publish and independently reconstruct the exact signed source list.

Set $p_U$: all $a_i=0,b_i=1$, leaving $x_1,\ldots,x_5$ unassigned. All ten closure clauses are satisfied; the ten remaining binaries form K5. Ten distinct triangles each receive weight $1/3$, every edge belongs to three triangles, and therefore the edge capacities are exactly one. The total is $10/3$, rounded up to $4$. Yet $e=2(5)-10=0$ and $\eta=0$. The theorem proves the entire cube UNSAT, while the maximum integer edge-disjoint odd-cycle packing is only two. Independently, $\operatorname{MaxCut}(K_5)=6$, so the true cut deficit $10-6=4$ shows the fractional certificate is exact on this motif.

Every single flip of one $a_i$ or $b_i$ removes precisely its associated K5 binary edge, while all ten closure clauses stay covered. Thus all ten neighboring pinned cubes have $f=5,u=9,e=1,\eta=0$ and binary graph K5 minus one edge. Their seven triangles, each at weight $1/3$, give bound $\lceil7/3\rceil=3$, so $1-3=-2<0$. Hence the eleven entire pinned cubes in the radius-one Hamming ball are certified UNSAT without enumerating their five free variables.

For the same original source, define $p_S$ by flipping only pinned $x_6$ and $x_{15}$ relative to $p_U$. This removes two disjoint K5 edges and leaves eight binary clauses, with $f=5,e=2,\eta=0$. Two edge-disjoint triangles give a valid certificate of weight two, saturating the budget. Its signed covered-occurrence costs force
\[

Either value of $x_4$ gives a full original-source SAT witness. Thus $p_U$ and $p_S$ have opposite labels inside the SAME legal parent, which has exactly 770 complete satisfying assignments. Neither $0^{15}$ nor $1^{15}$ is a witness.
