\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: Hasse-jet conjugacy.}
Write \(N=2^\nu N_0\), \(N_0\) odd, and let \(K\) split \(z^{N_0}-1\). For each root \(\omega\), the Hasse--Taylor identity
\[
f(\omega+\epsilon)=\sum_{r\ge0}D^{[r]}f(\omega)\epsilon^r
\]
and the Hasse Leibniz rule give
\[
\boxed{J_\omega(fg)=J_\omega(f)J_\omega(g)\pmod{\epsilon^{2^\nu}}.}
\]
Taking all roots yields
\[
\boxed{K[z]/(z^N-1)\cong\prod_{\omega^{N_0}=1}K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2^\nu}).}
\]
Thus the relaxed cyclic convolution operator is exactly conjugate to multiplication by Hasse-jet matrices over local Artin rings.

When \(N_0=1\), the sole root is \(1\) and
\[
T_N(f)=(D^{[0]}f(1),\ldots,D^{[N-1]}f(1))
\]
is an algebra isomorphism
\[
\boxed{\mathbb F_2[z]/((z-1)^N)\cong\mathbb F_2[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^N).}
\]
Its coefficient matrix is the mod-2 Pascal matrix \((\binom{i}{r})\), triangular with diagonal one.

\paragraph{Supported operator and implementation correction.}

\paragraph{Interpretation and exact filtration.}
The test validates the repeated-root coordinates and correlation convention, but falsifies the generic inference
\[
\text{repeated roots}\Longrightarrow \dim U=O(m).
\]
Each local ring \(J_\omega=K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2^\nu})\) has the exact filtration
\[
J_\omega\supset\epsilon J_\omega\supset\cdots\supset\epsilon^{2^\nu-1}J_\omega\supset0.
\]
The Hasse matrix of the local operator is block lower-triangular by derivative order. This is useful computational structure, but triangular support \(S\) need not split across local factors, so layer ranks alone do not determine the supported kernel.
