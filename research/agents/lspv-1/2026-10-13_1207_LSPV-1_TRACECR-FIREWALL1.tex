\section{Fixed target and the candidate implication}
Let $\mathcal C_{22}$ be the nonempty proper distinct-clause signed
exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF domain under the declared dense binary encoding.  The
total language
\[
 \Ltwo=\{\operatorname{enc}(G):G\in\mathcal C_{22},\ G\text{ satisfiable}\}
\]
rejects malformed and out-of-domain strings and is NP-complete by
IMAGE22COMP1.  The desired direct theorem is
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}(\Ltwo)\ \forall k,C,N_0\ \exists x:\quad
 |x|\ge N_0\ \land\ T_A(x)>C|x|^k.                        \tag{1}
\]
Hard inputs may depend on $A$.  A proof of (1) would settle $P\ne NP$; it
remains OPEN.

The candidate tested here is the weakest exact general-machine consequence
of assuming a polynomial $\Ltwo$ decider.  Fix a polynomial-time many-one
reduction $r$ from proper 3-SAT to $\Ltwo$ with
\[
 |r(F)|\le c|F|^q,\qquad T_r(|F|)=O(|F|^a).                \tag{2}
\]
Suppose a deterministic total exact decider $A$ obeys
\[
 T_A(N)\le C N^k                                                    \tag{3}
\]
for arbitrary fixed $k$.  Add a linear COPY prefix if necessary so its
charged runtime is at least the input length.  No internal compatibility
object, algebraic proof, or canonical solver structure is assumed.

\section{Exact decider-indexed proof system}
For a source CNF $F$ and a string $\pi$, let $V_{A,r}(F,\pi)$ perform the
following deterministic check:
\begin{enumerate}
\item compute $y=r(F)$, rejecting malformed reductions;
\item parse $\pi$ as the complete sequence of configurations of the fixed
machine for $A$ on $y$;
\item check the initial configuration, every transition, and a terminal NO
state.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Decider-indexed Cook--Reckhow endpoint]
If $A$ is a correct total decider for $\Ltwo$, then $V_{A,r}$ is a sound and
complete polynomial-time refutation relation for proper 3-CNF UNSAT.  If
(3) holds, it is polynomially bounded.  Under a standard multitape encoding,
every UNSAT $F$ of length $n$ has a proof of length
\[
 |\pi_F|=O\!\left((n^a+n^{qk})^2\right)
           =n^{O(\max\{a,qk\})}.                          \tag{4}
\]
Verification time is polynomial in $n+|\pi_F|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $F$ is UNSAT, reduction correctness makes $r(F)$ a NO instance of
$\Ltwo$; total determinism supplies its unique rejecting computation, which
the local transition checker accepts.  If $F$ is satisfiable, $r(F)$ is a
YES instance and correctness of $A$ excludes any valid rejecting trace, so
the relation is sound.

Let $T=O(n^a+n^{qk})$ charge reduction, input construction, COPY, and the
solver.  A $T$-step multitape computation visits at most $O(T)$ cells, so a
conservative complete configuration uses $O(T)$ bits and the $T+1$
configurations use $O(T^2)$ bits.  The verifier recomputes (2) and scans
adjacent configurations, establishing (4).  This is deliberately loose;
compression is unnecessary for polynomial boundedness.
\end{proof}

Composing with the standard polynomial reduction from TAUT to 3-CNF UNSAT
gives a Cook--Reckhow propositional proof system.  This is consistent with
the Cook--Reckhow theorem that a polynomially bounded propositional proof
system exists exactly when $\mathsf{NP}=\mathsf{coNP}$ \cite{cr,razborov}.
The assumption $\Ltwo\in P$ already implies $P=NP=coNP$; no contradiction
has appeared.

\section{Fixed-system promotion firewall}
The trace relation depends on the particular program $A$.  It is not
Resolution, PC/PCR, Cutting Planes, Frege, or a compact arithmetic-circuit
proof.  Let $S$ be a fixed proof system with a known superpolynomial lower
bound on an explicit UNSAT family $H_n$.  A translation satisfying
\[
 V_{A,r}(H_n,\pi)=1\Longrightarrow
 \exists\sigma\ [S(H_n,\sigma)=1\ \land\
 |\sigma|\le\poly(|H_n|+|\pi|)]                           \tag{5}
\]
for every polynomial-time $A$ would turn (4) into polynomial $S$ proofs and
contradict that endpoint.  Thus (5), together with the endpoint, already
excludes every polynomial-time $\Ltwo$ decider and proves (1).  It is not a
weaker intermediate lemma.

The recovered exact-$(2,2)$ minimally-UNSAT family illustrates the point:
Resolution size is $2^{\Omega(v)}=2^{\Omega(L/\log L)}$, but a hypothetical
polynomial $\Ltwo$ decider would still give polynomial $V_{A,r}$ traces.
TRACESIMQ1 already proves that universal trace-to-Resolution promotion has
the wrong quantifier status.  CPDEPTHEND1 similarly identifies a sufficiently
strong CP depth endpoint but leaves computation-to-CP OPEN.  The present
result supplies the exact positive endpoint available from arbitrary
computation---a tailored Cook--Reckhow system---and shows why fixed-model
lower bounds do not attach to it.

\section{Barriers, hostile controls, and PQ ledger}
The exact transfer is
\[
 \text{polynomial }\Ltwo\text{ decider}
 \longrightarrow \text{decider-indexed p-bounded CR system}.       \tag{6}
\]
This is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}.  Promotion from (6) to a
fixed system with a known lower bound is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} unless a
new simulation theorem is proved; with universal polynomial-decider
quantifiers it is separation-strength.  Proof search/verification and
automation remain distinct in the current literature \cite{atserias}.

Horn, 2SAT, affine/Gaussian elimination, bounded treewidth, compact circuits,
integer counting/flow, decoder front doors, padding, products, alternate
encodings, wrappers, and one-bit output are all allowed: each simply induces
its own trace relation.  A single rejection bit is insufficient, but its full
charged computation is locally checkable.  Soundness uses the fixed program's
mathematical correctness; it is not inferred from the trace alone.

The construction relativizes and algebrizes and supplies no Natural-Proofs
escape.  Schur squares, quadratic-relation spaces, syzygies, collision
entropy, worst-case/average-case cryptographic architecture, special
soundness, hash functions, and random oracles do not provide (5).  They are
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} or \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}, not proof claims.
