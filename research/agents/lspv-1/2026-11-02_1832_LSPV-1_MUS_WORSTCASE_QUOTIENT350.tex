\section*{Fixed model}
Use the already fixed, binary-premise, unit-node-cost $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ with source-labelled RES, AND3, XOR, local CONVERT, and charged conservative two-input variable DEF. No free global semantic inference or uncharged multi-premise macro. The proof cost counts distinct paid DAG nodes, with hindsight-optimal derivation choice free. Let $\mu(F)$ be the least number of original clauses in an UNSAT subformula. Define
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}|\mathrm{paid}(\pi)|,\qquad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\\|\mathrm{encode}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F).
\]
The input length $N$ is bits, not variable count. Fix explicit canonical ASCII DIMACS-like serialization with signed decimal literals and a correct header. Deleting clauses and renumbering remaining variables in increasing order never increases encoded bit length. The formula-to-proof relation is source-hereditary: the same derivation on an UNSAT subformula is available on any source superset.

\section*{Main theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Exact minimally UNSAT worst-case quotient]
For every integer input-bit budget N,
\[
\boxed{\overline W_R(N)=
\max_{\substack{U\text{ clause-minimally UNSAT}\\|\mathrm{encode}(U)|\le N}}W_R(U).}
\tag{1}
\]
If $\mathcal O$ ranges over signed variable-renaming/polarity-complement orbits of minimally UNSAT formulas and $d(\mathcal O)$ is the minimum encoded bit length in the orbit, then
\[
\boxed{\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\mathcal O:\ d(\mathcal O)\le N}W_R(\mathcal O).}
\tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any UNSAT F choose a minimum-cardinality UNSAT source subset U, which is necessarily clause-minimally UNSAT. Compact its variable IDs into a source U' without increasing input length. Since R ignores unused premises, every R refutation of U is a refutation of F, giving $W_R(F)\le W_R(U)=W_R(U')$. The equality uses exact renaming-equivariance of each existing R rule, already checked in LSPV-1 SIGNED-ORBIT348. Hence every F in the left maximum is dominated by a minimally UNSAT U' of no greater input length. The reverse inequality holds because minimally UNSAT sources are among all UNSAT sources. Signed-orbit invariance then yields (2). Proof discovery and minimum-core discovery are excluded from W but would be charged to any actual algorithm.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Core-size sandwich]
For every valid UNSAT general CNF F,
\[
\boxed{\mu(F)-1\le W_R(F)\le 3^{\mu(F)-1}.}\tag{3}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The source-ancestry lower bound from LSPV-1 SOURCE-EXCESS-EULER349 applies to any sound source-authenticated binary-premise DAG. If r original source clauses occur in the pruned ancestry, $r\ge\mu(F)$; connectedness and at most two premise edges per paid node yield $q\ge r-1\ge\mu(F)-1$. For the upper bound, take a minimally UNSAT core U with m clauses. The classical Tarsi lemma gives at most m-1 occurring variables. Binary Resolution is complete, and any repeated derived clause can be redirected to its first occurrence and erased. At most $3^v$ distinct nontautological clauses on v variables exist, including the empty clause, so one can choose a Resolution refutation with at most $3^v\le 3^{m-1}$ paid nodes. This Resolution proof is legal in fixed R and on the original F.
\end{proof}
\noindent \textbf{Exact candidate screen:} if $W_R(F)>C(N+1)^k$ for an input of length N, then
\[
\mu(F)>1+\log_3C+k\log_3(N+1).
\]
Thus a superpolynomial worst-case lower bound requires candidates with $\mu(F)/\log N$ unbounded along a sequence, but this is only necessary. Odd Tseitin, Horn chains and full assignment-blocking sources have large minimum cores and short combined proofs. A bounded core does not make finding it efficient; no global P-time algorithm follows.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: a redundant-clause formula might be strictly harder than every within-budget minimally UNSAT source. Ruled out by source heredity and input-size monotonicity.
\item[] F2: an UNSAT tiny source core embedded in many unused variables requires an unbounded proof. Ruled out by Tarsi and at-most-$3^v$ distinct resolution clauses.
\item[] F3: $\mu$ large forces W superpolynomial. Refuted by the existing exact linear odd-Tseitin mixed proofs and Horn controls.
\item[] F4: a multi-premise unit-cost semantic rule or clock-gated source-dependent calculus obeys these inequalities without separate justification. False in general, as LSPV-2 PROOF-PAYLOAD347 and LSPV-1 CLOCK-SUBCORE339 show. Neither rule changes the preselected R.
