\[

 \quad\text{RES pivot }z

\begin{proof}

The second pivot cancels $z$ against $\neg z$ while reintroducing $\ell$, leaving exactly $C\vee t$. Freshness of $z$ and nontautology of $C\vee t$ make both intermediate clauses clean, and the two actual Resolution records each have exactly two earlier parents. No weakening, oracle, assumption discharge, or free new definition is invoked. For repeated extensions use the same surviving pivot literal $\ell$ and globally distinct paid owners.
