\section{Occurrence-aware independent-resolution lemma}
Let $H$ be a normalized CNF of maximum width $w\ge2$, maximum $P$ occurrences of each polarity at any variable, and primal maximum degree $d$. Acquire a maximal primal independent set $I$ by deterministic ascending-name greedy selection, or take any other authenticated independent set.
\begin{lemma}
The exact existential Davis--Putnam projection $H_I=\exists_I H$ admits a normalized ordinary CNF with
\[
\boxed{\Delta(H_I)\le d+2P\bigl(P(w-1)-1\bigr)},\quad
P_{\rm out}\le P^2,\quad
w_{\rm out}\le2(w-1).
\]
The equivalence is pointwise for every complete assignment to the surviving names; no original-variable witness extraction or semantic oracle is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix surviving $y$. Let $r$ count selected pivots adjacent to $y$ before elimination. Independent pivots cannot share a clause, so each uses a distinct clause occurrence of $y$ and $r\le \mathrm{occ}_H(y)\le2P$. Every removed pivot $x$ was an old neighbor. Every new $y$-neighbor produced through an $x$-parent must originate from an opposite-sign $x$-parent. There are at most $P$ of these and each has at most $w-1$ other names, giving at most $P(w-1)$ new neighbors per $x$. Thus
\[
\deg_{H_I}(y)\le d-r+rP(w-1)\le d+2P(P(w-1)-1).
\]
Each signed occurrence of $y$ survives unchanged or is replicated into at most $P$ resolvents, yielding at most $P^2$ signed output occurrences. Each resolvent contains at most $2(w-1)$ literals. Independence ensures selected pivots have disjoint parent clauses, so resolutions commute. Non-tautological Davis--Putnam elimination is exactly existential quantification; deduplication preserves this identity. QED.
\end{proof}
