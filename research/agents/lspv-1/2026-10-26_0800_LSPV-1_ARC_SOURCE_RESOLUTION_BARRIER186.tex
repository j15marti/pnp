\section{Authenticated grammar and conflict clauses}
Let \(F=C_1\wedge\cdots\wedge C_m\) be a well-formed read-only source CNF of at most three literals per clause, with original immutable byte offsets authenticating every claimed source axiom. At any node let \(\sigma\) be the consistent assignment to distinct \emph{original} variables fixed on that branch. Put
\[
A_\sigma=\bigvee_{\sigma(x)=0}x\;\vee\bigvee_{\sigma(x)=1}\neg x.
\]
Thus every literal of \(A_\sigma\) is false under \(\sigma\). The exact accepted grammar, inherited from peer ADAPTIVE-SOURCE-POINTER-STREAM131, has:
\begin{description}
\item[] \(\mathsf{BRANCH}(x,P_0,P_1)\) on an unassigned original variable, checking \emph{both} children with \(x=0,1\).
\item[] \(\mathsf{EMPTY}(p)\) when the actual original source clause at authenticated physical offset \(p\) is false under \(\sigma\).
\item[] \(\mathsf{PATH}(x,\gamma_+,\gamma_-)\), when both \(x\leadsto\neg x\) and \(\neg x\leadsto x\) are authenticated paths in the implication graph formed by an \emph{actual subcollection} of original source clauses whose residuals under \(\sigma\) are unit or binary. Surviving unrelated ternaries may be ignored. Each edge cites an original byte offset and its endpoints; no syntactic claims about derivations from large clauses are accepted.
\end{description}
Let \(B\) count binary branch nodes, \(L=B+1\) terminals, \(E\) authenticated implication-edge records over the whole proof, \(h\) branch depth, and \(R=B+L+E\). The grammar was already sound and complete with an exponential unbounded construction. The new theorem addresses its \emph{minimum possible} size.

\section{Local proof compilation}
\begin{lemma}[Source implication lifting]\label{lem:path}
For every accepted PATH leaf at assignment \(\sigma\), there is a general-resolution derivation of \(A_\sigma\) from genuine original-source clauses, allowing weakening, with at most twice the number of leaf edge records plus a constant number of lines. Maximum clause width is at most \(h+2\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a source-authenticated residual implication \(u\to v\), its source clause \(C_p\) consists of literals already false under \(\sigma\), together with the unit or binary clause implying \(u\to v\). Consequently \(C_p\subseteq A_\sigma\vee\neg u\vee v\), and one source weakening derives \(A_\sigma\vee\neg u\vee v\). Unit residuals are the case \(u=\neg a,v=a\), producing \(A_\sigma\vee a\). Resolve these lifted implications successively along a path \(u_0\to\cdots\to u_k\), yielding \(A_\sigma\vee\neg u_0\vee u_k\). If a unit creates a stronger endpoint clause early, that clause may be used directly. A cyclic path can be shortened by deleting loops, without increasing its length. The two paths yield \(A_\sigma\vee\neg x\) and \(A_\sigma\vee x\); resolve on \(x\) to obtain \(A_\sigma\). These are original-variable operations, never a free SAT answer.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Empty leaves and exhaustive joins]\label{lem:join}
Every accepted EMPTY leaf yields \(A_\sigma\) by weakening its authentic source clause. If branch children at \(x=0\) and \(x=1\) yield \(A_\sigma\vee x\) and \(A_\sigma\vee\neg x\), one resolution on \(x\) yields \(A_\sigma\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every literal in the EMPTY source clause is false under \(\sigma\), so the clause is a subclause of \(A_\sigma\). The join is exactly the resolution rule. The two branch restrictions cover all assignments to the original name \(x\). At the unassigned root \(A_\varnothing\) is empty.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial simulation, new restricted theorem]\label{th:sim}
For every canonical proper source CNF \(F\) and every accepted source-authenticated adaptive BRANCH/EMPTY/PATH certificate \(\pi\), a fixed deterministic compiler outputs a valid general-resolution refutation, allowing weakening, with at most
\[
m+B+L+2E=O(m+R)
\]
axiom/weakening/resolution records, width at most \(\max\{3,h+2\}\), and explicit bit length
\[
O((m+R)(h+2)\log(N+R+2)).
\]
The compiler runs in time polynomial in \(N+\lvert\pi\rvert\), and never calls the guest SAT decider on the full parent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Emit the actual \(m\) original clauses, then traverse the authenticated proof tree depth first. For each PATH terminal apply Lemma~\ref{lem:path}, for each EMPTY terminal Lemma~\ref{lem:join}; a branch contributes one resolution at its join. Every lifted clause contains at most \(h\) ancestor conflict literals and two current edge literals, with identifiers and proof indexes logarithmic in \(N+R\). The topmost join derives \(A_\varnothing\), the empty clause. All source, ancestor, pivot, output and proof indices must be checked and charged for actual implementations. The stated simulation is a mathematical polynomial-time algorithm, not a separately calibrated physical FLMV resource bound.
\end{proof}

\section{Connected hard-source counterexample}
Let \(G_v\) be an infinite family of connected cubic vertex expanders, with \(v\) vertices, and \(f:V(G_v)\to\{0,1\}\) of odd total parity. Introduce one Boolean variable for every edge. Encode the three-edge XOR parity equation at each vertex by its four forbidden assignments, giving four proper signed ternary clauses per vertex. The resulting Tseitin CNF \(T_v\) has
\[
n_v=3v/2,\quad m_v=4v,\quad N_v=\Theta(v\log(v+2)),\quad \UNSAT(T_v).
\]
Each edge occurs twice positive/twice negative at \emph{each} endpoint, hence four times \emph{per sign globally}: the source is signed \emph{exact-(4,4)}, not the target signed exact-(2,2) or clause-monotone exact-(2,2) family. Odd parity causes UNSAT because the XOR sum over vertices has each edge twice.

\begin{theorem}[Resolution-hardness barrier, known theorem plus new deduction]\label{th:barrier}
Every accepted certificate of the exact BRANCH/EMPTY/PATH grammar for some connected odd-charge cubic-expander Tseitin source \(T_v\) has
\[
\boxed{R(\pi)\ge 2^{\Omega(v)}
       =\exp(\Omega(N_v/\log N_v)).}
\]
Consequently, for every fixed \(b<\infty\), the proposed all-input coverage statement
\[
\forall N\ \forall F\in\UNSAT\cap\{0,1\}^N\
\exists \pi:\ V(F,\pi)=1\wedge R(\pi)\le N^b
\]
is \textbf{FALSIFIED for this exact source-authenticated grammar}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Ben-Sasson and Wigderson, \emph{Short Proofs Are Narrow: Resolution Made Simple} (JACM 2001), Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.5, imply \(2^{\Omega(v)}\) minimum size for \emph{general} resolution refutations of these connected odd-charge Tseitin 3CNFs on cubic expanders. If the authenticated grammar had a certificate of \(R\) records, Theorem~\ref{th:sim} would output a valid general-resolution refutation with \(m+O(R)\) records. Here \(m=4v\), hence \(R\ge 2^{\Omega(v)}\). Since \(N_v=\Theta(v\log v)\), the stated encoded-size bound follows. This is a \emph{lower bound for one proof grammar}, not a deterministic decision-time or circuit-size lower bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Hardness transfer firewall.}
The proof does not apply to polynomial calculus, Gaussian elimination, extended resolution, cutting planes, arbitrary DRAT, semantic or algebraic source-authenticated proofs, or an unrestricted deterministic SAT solver. The Tseitin instances are easy for an explicitly parity-aware algorithm, despite being exponentially hard for resolution. There is no justified reduction preserving this resolution lower bound in the separate signed exact-(2,2) or clause-monotone grammars. A near-linear general SAT witness \emph{verifier circuit} remains distinct from an efficiently constructible full SAT \emph{decision circuit}.
