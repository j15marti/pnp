\section{Charged adaptive multi-channel lemma}
Assume an actually correct fixed uniform wrapper $W[A]$ decides both SAT and UNSAT on \emph{all} ordinary inputs. Its policy may inspect actual states and change parameter vectors. Across finitely many declared call types $e$, it makes at most $N^{a_e+o(1)}$ sequential ordinary-SAT invocations of $A$, each on at most $N^{\theta_e+o(1)}$ logical encoded bits; an indexed bit probe of a virtual child costs at most $N^{g_e+o(1)}$ actual bit work. Let $N^{r+o(1)}$ pay all other operations: source and workspace reads, writes including unchanged writes, arithmetic/control/indexing, rule discovery/switching, failed attempts, headers, source rereads, caching, gluing and reconstruction. Let $N^{p+o(1)}$ cover all simultaneous generator, policy, retained-ancestor and cache workspace/index bits, including scratch for virtual addressing. One solver call at a time uses and reclaims its writable state. This is a \emph{conditional ledger hypothesis}, not an implemented all-input wrapper.

\begin{theorem}[Paid multi-channel successor and finite hit, CONDITIONAL]
For $(t,s)\in\EE$, if the stipulated actual implementation exists, its simultaneous upper bounds satisfy
\[
t'\le f(t)=\max\{r,\max_e(a_e+g_e+\theta_e t)\},\qquad
s'\le h(s)=\max\{p,\max_e\theta_e s\}.
\]
For explicit child tapes that are materialized in counted writable memory, additionally charge the emitted bit work and add $\max_e\theta_e$ to the space upper envelope. If $\rho=\max_e\theta_e<1$, let
\[
T_*=\max\{r,\max_e(a_e+g_e)/(1-\theta_e)\},\quad S_*=p.
\]
The numerical iterates obey
\[
t_j\le T_*+\rho^j(t_0-T_*)_+,\qquad
s_j\le p+\rho^j(s_0-p)_+.
\]
If $T_*<c_*$ and $p<d_*$ and a fully charged exact hereditary uniform machine transformation genuinely realizes every iteration, every fixed initial achievable diagonal enters the forbidden strict box after finitely many iterations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each channel executes at most $N^{a_e+o(1)}$ solver calls taking at most $N^{\theta_e t+o(1)}$ steps, with at most constant input probes per simulated step. Charging $N^{g_e+o(1)}$ for every virtual probe gives the stated conservative time exponent. Live solver scratch is combined with parent scratch and retained ancestor state in a \emph{single} simultaneous maximum, not selected from different machines. The maximum of finitely many polynomials yields the time and space exponents. With $\rho<1$, the monotone map $f$ is $\rho$-Lipschitz and fixes $T_*$; $h$ similarly fixes $p$. Induction gives the displayed iterates. A strict positive margin and a finite fixed composition suffice only if the machine-producing premise really holds.
\end{proof}
This max-affine theorem is already implicit in FINAL-LINK-ARC; the incremental focus is exact \emph{policy query accounting} as a distinct paid channel.

\section{A mandatory parent-scale query obstruction}
\begin{proposition}[Padding no-go for obligatory black-box lookahead, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Assume $P=NP$ and take any fixed polynomial-time exact ordinary-SAT decider $D$. For any sufficiently large fixed integer $K$, form a fixed uniform total exact solver $A_K$ that executes $\Theta(M^K)$ dummy steps on inputs of bit length $M$ before running $D$. It has polynomial writable space. Any wrapper architecture $W[A]$ that on at least one parent input of length $N$ for infinitely many $N$ \emph{actually executes} $A$ on a query of length $M\ge cN$ for a fixed positive $c$, satisfies
\[
T_{W[A_K]}(N)\ge\Omega(N^K)
\]
on those inputs. It therefore cannot certify an exponent strictly less than $K$ for \emph{every supplied} exact polynomial-time solver.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed $K$ above the exponent of $D$, an $O(\log M)$-bit counter drives the $\Theta(M^K)$ padding loop, then $D$ returns the correct label. This is a uniform total solver for all input lengths with time $\Theta(M^K)$. A mandatory executed call on $M\ge cN$ requires at least $(cN)^K$ real steps, regardless of what the wrapper does afterward.
\end{proof}
The result does \emph{not} rule out a route tailored to one chosen $A$, a wrapper able to skip that query, or an existential route with other algorithms. It only eliminates a compulsory parent-sized lookahead as a claimed universal black-box exponent-reduction method.

\section{Exact numerical selector slack and resource firewall}
Take the illustrative but still OPEN recursive channel $(a,\theta)=(3/5,1/2)$, local time $r=11/10$, peak extra space exponent $p=1/2$. Add an adaptive policy channel of at most $N^b$ ordinary queries of length $N^\rho$, with per-probe accessor exponent $g$. The two-channel conditional ledger becomes
\[
t'\le\max\{11/10,3/5+t/2,b+g+\rho t\},\quad
s'\le\max\{1/2,s/2,\rho s\}.
\]
For $0\le\rho<1$, its \emph{numerical time upper-envelope fixed point} is
\[
\boxed{T_*=\max\{6/5,(b+g)/(1-\rho)\}}.
\]
Thus this envelope reaches a \emph{strict} FLMV time bound from finite diagonals precisely when
\[
\boxed{b+g<\frac{241}{200}(1-\rho)}
\]
given its other declared assumptions and the space requirement. For $\rho=1/2$, the effective policy fanout plus accessor exponent must be $b+g<241/400=0.6025$. At the reference $b=3/5$, there is only $1/400=0.0025$ exponent slack. At $\rho=3/5,b=12/25$ or $\rho=3/4,b=3/10$, the numerical fixed point remains $6/5$, but eager materialization of even one policy query costs $N^\rho$ counted writable bits and fails the strict FLMV space box. These cases require \emph{genuine paid on-demand virtual input access} to remain numerically promising. At $\rho=1$ the algebra does not contract $t$ and the obligatory-query proposition applies.

The result does not claim a genuine SAT transformation from these exponents. A fast algorithm on Horn/2SAT/affine, a publicly witnessed large-width formula, or a M22-only source may avoid all policy calls; if a lower-bound claim insists it must execute them, it needs an explicit source-dependent proof. Bit flips, cumulative live-bit-time, hardware gates/wires, and static program size remain separate resources from counted bit work and simultaneous space.
