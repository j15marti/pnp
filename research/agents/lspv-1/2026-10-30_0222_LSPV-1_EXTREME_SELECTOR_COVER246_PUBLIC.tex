\section{Eight-clause hitting and strict22 firewall}
\begin{theorem}[Exact eight-cube criterion]
For any proper original ternary CNF with exactly eight clauses, write $E_j=\{a:t_j(a)=0\}$ for globally coherent all-false source cubes. The formula is UNSAT if and only if every distinct pair $E_j,E_k$ has empty intersection, equivalently every $UU$ source pair conflicts. At most seven proper ternary clauses are always SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every $E_j$ has uniform measure $1/8$. The sum of eight measures is one. If their union covers all assignments, equality in the union bound forces every pair intersection empty; conversely eight pairwise disjoint cubes with total measure one cover the sample space. For at most seven events the union measure is at most $7/8$. This is a standard hitting-clause-set principle, not a novel unrestricted solver.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Original-occurrence-quota obstruction]
For any eight original proper ternary clauses with $p_i\le2$ positive and $q_i\le2$ negative occurrences per original variable, at least four $UU$ clause pairs are compatible and the formula is SAT. Thus a strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ original UNSAT subformula uses at least nine ORIGINAL clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A shared opposing-polarity variable $i$ can conflict at most $p_iq_i\le p_i+q_i$ unordered source clause pairs. Therefore the total number of conflicting pairs is at most $\sum_i(p_i+q_i)=24<\binom82=28$. \end{proof}
