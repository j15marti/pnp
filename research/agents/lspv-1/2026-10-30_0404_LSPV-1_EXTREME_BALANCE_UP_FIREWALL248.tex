\section{Target, falsifiable mechanisms, and exact model}
Given an original proper Boolean CNF $F=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{m-1} C_j$, test these three explicit mechanisms with one globally consistent assignment to each original variable:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] (M1) A balanced-occurrence ``true-literal excess budget'' might rule out an original-variable partial cube $\rho$ beyond unit propagation.
\item[] (M2) A sign-sensitive upper bound on how many uncovered original clauses can be repaired might rule out a cube beyond unit propagation, even when M1 cannot.
\item[] (M3) Pairwise source-indexed extreme incompatibilities and non-tautological resolvents might authenticate the rejected cubes or reveal the missing cross-clause correlations.
\end{enumerate}
The target is a sound logical distinction, \emph{not} any equity/valuation score or a SAT-time lower bound. The complete experiment is a deterministic indexed-word-RAM/Python implementation with explicit source reads; the measured counters are selected Python logical operations, not physical Turing-machine steps or industrial CDCL instructions. All input-dependent preprocessing, successful and unsuccessful checks, source verification, branch search, and peak allocations are reported per execution.

\section{Original signed occurrences and coherent extreme experiments}
For original signed literal $\ell_{ij}\in\{x_i,\neg x_i\}$ in original $C_j$ (and sign bit $\sigma_{ij}=1$ iff positive), define the local all-literals-TRUE target
\[S_j:\quad x_i=\sigma_{ij}\quad(\ell_{ij}\in C_j),\]
and local all-literals-FALSE target
\[U_j:\quad x_i=1-\sigma_{ij}\quad(\ell_{ij}\in C_j).\]
Inversion sends $S_j\mapsto U_j$ and $U_j\mapsto S_j$ \emph{on every occurrence}. The indices $x_{i,j}$ refer to one globally shared original variable $x_i$, not independent bits. For distinct original clauses $C_j,C_k$, $SS,UU$ modes are consistent exactly when there is no opposite-polarity shared original variable; $SU,US$ are consistent exactly when no same-polarity variable is shared. If both kinds occur, all four extreme modes fail, but the two original disjunctions may simultaneously be true with one or two true literals each.

For width three under globally coherent complete $a$,
\[
t_j(a)\in\{0,1,2,3\},\qquad t_j(\overline a)=3-t_j(a),\qquad
N_r(\overline a)=N_{3-r}(a),\]
\[
F(a)=1\iff N_0(a)=0,\qquad
F(\overline a)=1\iff N_3(a)=0.
\]
For any subset $H$ of original variables,
\[
t_j(a\oplus H)-t_j(a)=|H\cap L^0_j(a)|-|H\cap L^1_j(a)|,\]
where $L^0_j,L^1_j$ are original variables whose signed clause occurrences are initially FALSE/TRUE. These are accounting identities, never independent-occurrence assignments. The primary raw JSON lists \emph{every original occurrence}, its $S/U$ and inversions, every pair's indexed $SS/SU/US/UU$ compatibility and conflicting original variables, independently checked triple extremes, coherent $0^n/1^n$, complements, intermediate one/multiple flips, all $t_j$ and four $N_r$ categories, and indexed resolvent forbidden cubes.

\section{Capacity of sign-consistent clause repairs}
Fix any coherent partial assignment $\rho$ to \emph{original} variables. Let $R_\rho$ consist of original clauses with no pinned-TRUE literal, shortened only by deleting pinned-FALSE literals. Let $Z=|R_\rho|$. For each unassigned original variable $v$, let $p_v$ and $q_v$ be the numbers of its positive and negative literal occurrences in $R_\rho$. Define
\[
H(\rho)=\sum_{v\notin\mathrm{dom}(\rho)}\max\{p_v,q_v\}.
\]
This counts a deliberately optimistic supply of true literal occurrences: variable $v$ is allowed to choose the more common of its two signs. It may count a clause more than once and is therefore an upper bound on how many of the $Z$ residual clauses any extension can satisfy.

\begin{theorem}[Sign-capacity test cannot exceed original-source UP, any proper CNF]\label{thm:cap}
For every proper CNF $F$ of arbitrary widths and every coherent original-variable partial cube $\rho$, $H(\rho)<Z(\rho)$ is a sound forbidden-cube certificate. More strongly, if this strict inequality holds, repeated \emph{ordinary source unit propagation} from $\rho$ necessarily derives the empty clause after at most the number of initially free variables in forced unit assignments, followed by an empty-clause detection; the number of clause scans is charged separately. At every noncontradictory UP-fixed residual $H(\rho)\ge Z(\rho)$. This is true even when $H$ is recomputed for \emph{any chosen subfamily} of residual clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An assignment to free variable $v$ can make only positive or only negative occurrences of $v$ true, contributing at most $\max\{p_v,q_v\}$ true literal occurrences. Satisfying all residual clauses would require at least $Z$ true occurrences, so $H<Z$ forbids every extension.
If every residual clause has at least two unassigned literals, the number of residual literal occurrences is $\sum_v(p_v+q_v)\ge2Z$, hence
\[
 H=\sum_v\max\{p_v,q_v\}\ge\tfrac12\sum_v(p_v+q_v)\ge Z.
\]
Thus $H<Z$ forces an empty or unit residual source clause. The empty clause refutes $\rho$. Otherwise suppose the residual unit is the signed literal $\ell$ of original variable $v$. Unit propagation sets it TRUE. Let $r=p_v$ or $q_v$, according to $\ell$'s sign, counting the residual clauses newly satisfied; properness ensures these are distinct. The new residual clause count is $Z'=Z-r$. Variable $v$ disappears; all other residual signed occurrence counts only decrease. Hence $H'\le H-\max\{p_v,q_v\}$, and
\[
 Z'-H'\ge Z-H+\max\{p_v,q_v\}-r\ge Z-H>0.
\]
The strict deficit persists through every forced unit. Each propagation fixes a free original variable, so eventually an empty source clause is reached. For any subfamily, the same $\sum(p_v+q_v)\ge2|J|$ argument proves no subset-specific capacity deficit at a UP-fixed residual.
\end{proof}
The only possible computational advantage of calculating $H$ before a full UP scan is an implementation-specific cost saving from selecting unit clauses more efficiently, not a logically stronger pruning rule. Computing $H$ after source-UP fixed point can never eliminate an additional DPLL node.

\section{Balanced literal-conservation frontier and exact dominance chain}
An original proper uniform width-$k$ clause-set is \emph{sign-balanced} when each original variable $v$ has exactly $d_v$ positive and $d_v$ negative occurrences; $d_v$ may differ across variables. Then $\sum_vd_v=km/2$, and every globally coherent complete assignment has exactly $km/2$ true literal occurrences. In particular for strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ proper 3CNF, $d_v=2$, $m=4n/3$ and $\sum_jt_j(a)=2n=3m/2$ for every $a$.
For a partial $\rho$ let $a_j(\rho)$ be the count of already pinned-TRUE signed source literals of $C_j$ and define
\[
B=\frac{m(k-2)}2,\qquad
\Theta(\rho)=\sum_j\max\{0,a_j(\rho)-1\}.
\]
A full satisfying assignment has $\sum_j(t_j-1)=B$, so any satisfiable extension must obey $\Theta(\rho)\le B$.

\begin{theorem}[Sign-balanced conservation is subsumed by sign-capacity and UP]\label{thm:dom}
For any proper uniform width-$k\ge2$ sign-balanced original CNF and coherent partial cube $\rho$, put $D(\rho)=\sum_{v\text{ free}}d_v$ and let $Z(\rho)$ be the number of original clauses with no pinned-TRUE literal. Then
\[
\boxed{\Theta(\rho)=B-D(\rho)+Z(\rho).}\]
For the same $\rho$, with sign-sensitive residual capacity $H(\rho)$ as in Theorem~\ref{thm:cap},
\[
\boxed{\Theta(\rho)>B\ \Longrightarrow\ H(\rho)<Z(\rho)
\ \Longrightarrow\ \UP(F\mid\rho)\text{ refutes }\rho.}
\]
Consequently a deterministic UP-DPLL with an extra conservation check \emph{after} UP reaches a fixed point visits exactly the same nodes and returns the same answer as the otherwise identical solver without that check. The theorem requires no supplied SAT/UNSAT answer bit and no restriction on subsequent methods; it is only a firewall for these two specified counting tests.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The number of already pinned-TRUE signed original occurrences is $\sum_{v\text{ pinned}}d_v=km/2-D$. Exactly $m-Z$ original clauses have a positive pinned-TRUE count. Thus
\[
\Theta=\sum_ja_j-(m-Z)=km/2-D-m+Z=B-D+Z.
\]
Each free original variable $v$ has at most $d_v$ positive and $d_v$ negative occurrences among residual clauses. Therefore $H\le\sum_{v\text{ free}}d_v=D$. Hence $\Theta>B$ is equivalent to $Z>D$ and implies $Z>H$. Theorem~\ref{thm:cap} supplies the explicit unit refutation. At a noncontradictory UP fixed point neither capacity nor conservation can trigger, so appending the latter check changes no branch, unit or witness of a fixed-policy UP-DPLL; only its charged execution cost changes.
\end{proof}
For a proper strict exact22 3CNF, write $u$ for free original variables, so $D=2u$ and
\[
\boxed{\Theta(\rho)=\frac m2-2u+Z(\rho).}
\]
For any SAT assignment in this class, the global four-state identity reduces to $N_1=N_2+3N_3$ and $N_2+2N_3=m/2$. These are genuine identities but do not decide SAT. The converse of the theorem's pruning implication fails: UP can reject a source cube with $\Theta\le B$ and $H\ge Z$.

\section{Clause-indexed countercontrol and sound resolution cube}
Use the proper connected strict22 source with zero-based original clause indices
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
C_0=(\neg x_1\vee x_2\vee\neg x_5),& C_1=(\neg x_3\vee\neg x_2\vee\neg x_5),\\
C_2=(x_1\vee\neg x_6\vee\neg x_4),& C_3=(x_6\vee\neg x_3\vee x_5),\\
C_4=(x_6\vee x_3\vee\neg x_4),& C_5=(x_1\vee x_2\vee x_4),\\
C_6=(\neg x_6\vee\neg x_1\vee x_5),& C_7=(\neg x_2\vee x_3\vee x_4).
\end{array}
\]
Every source variable has two positive and two negative occurrences, $m=8,n=6,B=4$. Original $C_3,C_4$ share $x_6$ with the \emph{same} sign and $x_3$ with \emph{opposite} signs: none of $SS,SU,US,UU$ source extremes can be globally combined. Yet the formula has exactly four satisfying complete assignments, so this pair incompatibility is not a formula-level UNSAT certificate. Resolve these \emph{original} clauses on $x_3$:
\[
\frac{(x_6\vee\neg x_3\vee x_5)\qquad(x_6\vee x_3\vee\neg x_4)}
{\boxed{x_6\vee x_5\vee\neg x_4}}.
\]
Thus $\rho=\{x_4=1,x_5=0,x_6=0\}$ is a prohibited \emph{original-variable cube}. It reduces $C_3$ to $\neg x_3$ and $C_4$ to $x_3$, contradictory opposing demands. Here $Z=2,H=1$ while $\Theta=0\le B=4$. This witnesses strict containment of the first inclusion in Theorem~\ref{thm:dom}, but the stronger capacity test is still UP-dominated. Conversely pinning $\{x_1=1,x_2=0,x_5=1\}$ immediately falsifies original $C_0$, although $\Theta=0$ and $H=3\ge Z=2$, showing neither test is complete even for UP.
At the globally coherent endpoints both $0^6$ and $1^6$, the original source has $[N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3]=[1,3,3,1]$ and fails. Flip original $x_1$ from $0^6$: the new assignment satisfies all eight original clauses, with $[0,6,0,2]$. The two endpoint failures are not an UNSAT certificate.
