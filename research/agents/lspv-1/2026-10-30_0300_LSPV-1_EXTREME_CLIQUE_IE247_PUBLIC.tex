\section{Original signed occurrences, extremal targets, complements}
For each \emph{original} proper clause $C_j$ and signed occurrence $\ell_{i,j}$ of original global variable $x_i$, let $\sigma_{ij}=1$ for $x_i$, $0$ for $\neg x_i$. The local all-literals-TRUE target $S_j$ pins $x_i=\sigma_{ij}$; the local all-literals-FALSE target $U_j$ pins $x_i=1-\sigma_{ij}$. Inverting the pinned target exchanges $S_j$ and $U_j$. These are local cubes, not independently assignable occurrences. Ordinary source-clause SAT requires one or more TRUE literals; $S_j$ requires all three. For distinct source clauses $j,k$, $SS,UU$ are feasible iff no opposite-polarity variable is shared, and $SU,US$ iff no same-polarity variable is shared. If both kinds are shared, none of the four extreme pairs is feasible; this does not imply either source disjunction is false. Under coherent complete global assignment $a$,
\[
t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a),\quad
t_j(a\oplus H)-t_j(a)=|H\cap L^0_j(a)|-|H\cap L^1_j(a)|.
\]
The complete raw JSON in the reproducibility bundle contains each indexed occurrence's local S, inverted U, local U, inverted S, all four pair modes with source indices, coherent all-zero/all-one masks and intermediate global-variable flip trails with per-clause truth vectors and class counts $N_0,\ldots,N_3$.

\section{Exact conditional source-cube model-count theorem}
Let $\rho$ be any coherent partial assignment to ORIGINAL variables. Restrict the source and remove satisfied source clauses; an empty residual clause certifies $\rho$ impossible. Otherwise let $R=F|_\rho$ have $r$ residual clauses and $u$ remaining free variables. For each residual clause $D_j$, let $E_j$ denote its local all-literals-FALSE cube. Construct conflict graph $H_\rho$ with edge $jk$ exactly when $E_j\cap E_k=\varnothing$, which is equivalent to an opposite-polarity shared original variable. Let $\mathcal I(H_\rho)$ contain every independent set, including empty; define $V(J)=\cup_{j\in J}\operatorname{var}(D_j)$.
\begin{theorem}[Exact conditional model count]
For every source and partial cube $\rho$,
\[
\boxed{M(\rho)=\#\{a\supseteq\rho:F(a)=1\}
=\sum_{J\in\mathcal I(H_\rho)}(-1)^{|J|}2^{u-|V(J)|}.}
\]
In particular $\rho$ is a forbidden partial cube precisely when the exact integer on the right is zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All original source U targets are conjunctions of unit demands on the \emph{same} original Boolean variables. Pairwise consistency of fixed unit demands is equivalent to consistency of the entire family. Thus $J$ is an independent set precisely when $\bigcap_{j\in J}E_j$ is a nonempty cube, fixing exactly $|V(J)|$ free variables, with $2^{u-|V(J)|}$ globally coherent completions. Inclusion--exclusion counts assignments not in the union of source-falsifying events. This is classical inclusion--exclusion, not a new proof system or a SAT complexity breakthrough.
\end{proof}
The certificate records $A_d=\sum_{|J|=d,\,J\in\mathcal I}2^{u-|V(J)|}$ and checks $\sum_d(-1)^dA_d$ by reenumerating the source-indexed compatibility graph. A positive count may be turned into a witness by charged self-reduction or by a complete UP-DPLL backend; zero establishes no extensions without being given an UNSAT answer bit.

\section{Explicit-enumerator barrier}
\begin{theorem}[Restricted architecture lower bound]
Every algorithm that \emph{explicitly} visits every compatible source-U subfamily for a proper exact-width-$k$ input with $m$ clauses has at least $2^{\lceil m/2^k\rceil}-1$ nonempty terms to visit. For proper original strict exact-$(2+,2-)$-3CNF this improves to $2^{\lceil m/7\rceil}-1=2^{\lceil 4n/21\rceil}-1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An average coherent assignment falsifies $m/2^k$ original clauses, so some assignment falsifies at least its ceiling. Every subset of those false-clause U targets is compatible. For strict22, each source clause conflicts with at most two opposite-sign source clauses on each of three variables, so the conflict graph has maximum degree at most six. Greedy independent-set selection yields an all-U-compatible family of at least $\lceil m/7\rceil$ clauses and all its subsets.
\end{proof}
This is \emph{not} an arbitrary-algorithm SAT lower bound. Cancellations, compact summaries, connected-component factoring, 2SAT/Horn, Gaussian elimination for affine/Tseitin, treewidth-DP and adaptive methods may avoid the explicit subset expansion. The known Bennett--Sankaranarayanan inclusion--exclusion paper studies precisely such cancellations.
