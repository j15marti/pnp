\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: coefficient-kernel theorem.}
Fix one legal SAME\_REALIZATION source-state set $S$. For output position $i\in[m]$, let
\[
x_i:S\to\mathbb F_2^b,\qquad x_i(s)=(x_{i,1}(s),\ldots,x_{i,b}(s))
\]
be the binary label of the symbol delivered to the Hadamard conversion. For mask $u\in\mathbb F_2^b$,
\[
g_i(s,u)=\langle x_i(s),u\rangle=\sum_{a=1}^b x_{i,a}(s)u_a.
\]
Let $M=1+m+\binom m2$ and
\[
Q(z)=c+\sum_i\alpha_i z_i+\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}z_iz_j.
\]
Substitution gives the unique multilinear expansion
\[
Q(g(s,u))=c+\sum_a A_a(s)u_a+\sum_{a<c}B_{ac}(s)u_au_c,
\]
where
\[
A_a(s)=\sum_i\alpha_i x_{i,a}(s)+\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}x_{i,a}(s)x_{j,a}(s)
\]
and
\[
B_{ac}(s)=\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}
\bigl(x_{i,a}(s)x_{j,c}(s)+x_{i,c}(s)x_{j,a}(s)\bigr).
\]
Because $1,u_a,u_au_c$ are linearly independent functions on $\mathbb F_2^b$,
\[
Q(g(s,u))\equiv0\ \forall(s,u)
\iff c=0,\quad A_a\equiv0\ \forall a,\quad B_{ac}\equiv0\ \forall a<c.
\]
Define the linear coefficient map
\[
\Psi:\mathbb F_2^M\to
\mathbb F_2\oplus\operatorname{Fun}(S,\mathbb F_2)^b
\oplus\operatorname{Fun}(S,\mathbb F_2)^{\binom b2}
\]
by $\Psi(c,\alpha,\beta)=(c,(A_a)_a,(B_{ac})_{a<c})$. Then
\[
\boxed{K_2(H)=\ker\Psi}
\]
and exactly
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_ig_j\}
=\operatorname{rank}\Psi=M-\dim\ker\Psi.}
\]
This is architecture-specific for the declared Hadamard realization and is equivalent to the earlier weight-at-most-two mask-basis theorem.

\paragraph{Corollary: scale required for quadratic contraction.}
Since $M=\Theta(m^2)$, an architecture-specific bound $\rho(H)=O(m)$ requires
\[
\boxed{\dim K_2(H)=M-O(m)=\Theta(m^2).}
\]
One or a few quadratic relations cannot establish the desired contraction. The source functions must force a quadratic-dimensional family of independent relations among the $A_a$ and $B_{ac}$. Conversely, injectivity of $\Psi$ gives $\rho(H)=M=\Theta(m^2)$ and retires quadratic contraction for that SAME\_REALIZATION.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT conditional Boolean-degree ledger.}
Represent a legal source state by Boolean variables $s_1,\ldots,s_n$, and suppose every label bit $x_{i,a}(s)$ has ANF degree at most $d$. Then for fixed $u$,
\[
\deg_s g_i(\cdot,u)\le d,\qquad
\deg_s(g_ig_j)(\cdot,u)\le\min(n,2d).
\]
Jointly in $(s,u)$,
\[
\deg_{s,u}g_i\le d+1,\qquad
\deg_{s,u}(g_ig_j)\le\min(n,2d)+2.
\]
These implications are exact once $d$ is known. They do not bound $d$ from the odd-prime Reed--Muller field degree. The active RMV construction uses $\mathbb F_p$ for a large prime $p$, so field-to-binary labeling remains a genuine characteristic-change boundary.
