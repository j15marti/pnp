\section*{Fixed original proof calculus and known hard family}
The unchanged source-authenticated $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ has free labelled CNF source leaves, and distinct unit-paid strict RES, exact AND3, GF$(2)$ row additions, justified LOCAL-CONVERT and conservative source-independent signed two-input VARDEF operations, with shared inference nodes counted once. The present theorem is about the existing proper subgrammar $E=\{\mathrm{RES},\mathrm{VARDEF}\}$ only, not about all of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. A signed paid gate $z\leftrightarrow(a\land b)$, where $a,b$ are signed distinct named input literals, has three authenticated slots
\[
(\neg z\vee a),\qquad(\neg z\vee b),\qquad(z\vee\neg a\vee\neg b).
\]
Gate outputs are globally fresh; later gates may depend on earlier gates. The proof must charge every new gate and every RES inference. For a proper valid UNSAT CNF $F$, define $W_R(F)$ as the minimum distinct paid-DAG-node count, and $\overline W_R(N)$ as the maximum over UNSAT encoded sources of at most $N$ bits, with empty maximum zero. Free best-proof selection does not mean free proof acquisition or free gate definitions.

Let $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ be the standard unary pigeonhole CNF on $h(h+1)$ occurring variables, with
\[
m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}=\Theta(h^3),
\quad N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))\text{ bits}
\]
in canonical ASCII DIMACS. By Haken (1985), there exist constants $c>0,h_0$ such that for every $h\ge h_0$ every \emph{unrestricted shared-DAG ordinary Resolution} refutation has at least $2^{ch}$ lines. This is a restriction on Resolution proofs, NOT on the computation of arbitrary SAT algorithms. Cook (1976) gives polynomial Extended Resolution proofs of these very formulas, a mandatory hostile control.

\section*{New scoped lemma: remove a paid AND2 gate}
\begin{lemma}[Uniform single-gate RES substitution]
Given an authenticated $E$ proof and its last introduced paid gate $z\leftrightarrow(a\land b)$, eliminate $z$ using only ordinary Resolution plus syntactic weakening, with at most a factor $64$ more represented lines and no additional source axioms. All earlier definitions remain legal. The transformation is uniform in formula size, clause width, and the signs of $a,b$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Substitute $z\mapsto a\land b$ and $\neg z\mapsto\neg a\vee\neg b$ in every proof line, distributing just this one substitution into CNF. A clause $z\vee C$ becomes $a\vee C$ and $b\vee C$; a clause $\neg z\vee C$ becomes $\neg a\vee\neg b\vee C$. No more than two non-tautological clauses replace one line. All three authenticated slots defining $z$ become Boolean tautologies and need not remain as premises. Since this is the latest output gate, no older definition depends on it.

For a Resolution pivot $x\ne z$, the transformed premises resolve pairwise on $x$ in at most two steps, unless the original resolvent was tautological, in which case it was not a strict inference. For a pivot $z$, the transformed premises are $a\vee C$, $b\vee C$, and $\neg a\vee\neg b\vee D$; resolving on $a$ and then $b$ yields the original resolvent $C\vee D$. When contexts contain $a,b$, tautologies, duplicates or subsuming clauses, simply drop tautologies or retain a stronger subclause and use a syntactic weakening. A prior weakening similarly becomes at most two weakenings. Thus a universal constant below $64$ suffices for each represented line, including tautology bypass and slot accounting. The proof retains a derived representative of each desired clause without an additional SAT or equivalence oracle. Repeat in reverse definition order.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Removing syntactic weakening]
A Resolution-with-syntactic-weakening refutation with $L$ represented lines has a strict Resolution refutation with no more than $L$ derived lines.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Maintain a derived subclause of each original proof line. A weakening inherits its predecessor. When resolving two represented subclauses, genuinely resolve if both pivot signs remain, otherwise inherit the parent missing the pivot. At the final empty line the representative must be empty. Sharing can be retained.
\end{proof}

\section*{Unconditional source-preserving translation and extension budget}
\begin{theorem}[Paid definition elimination]
For \emph{every} proper valid UNSAT original CNF $F$ with $m$ source clauses and \emph{every} legal $E$ refutation with $q$ distinct paid nodes, of which $d\le q$ are fresh signed AND2 definitions,
\[
\boxed{\quad L_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le64^d(m+4q+1).\quad}\tag{1}
\]
The output refutes the \emph{same original source $F$}, not a new equisatisfiable encoding.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expand each paid VARDEF for the purpose of the translator into at most three authenticated defining slots. The represented transcript has at most $m+q+3d\le m+4q$ lines. Sequentially remove the latest remaining gate using the first lemma, at most multiplying the transcript line count by $64$ each time. After all $d$ eliminations only original $F$ sources remain, along with ordinary RES and syntactic weakening. Remove weakening using the second lemma, without increasing line count. The estimate is conservative and includes all shared copies.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Haken-to-ER2 definition-count lower bound]
For each sufficiently large $h$, every legal paid $E$ refutation of the authentic unary pigeonhole $P_h$ with $q$ paid nodes and $d$ conservative definitions obeys
\[
\boxed{\quad d\ge\frac{ch-\log_2(m_h+4q+1)}{6}.\quad}\tag{2}
\]
For every fixed $K>0$, if $q\le(N_h+1)^K$, then $d=\Omega_K(h)=\Omega_K((N_h/\log N_h)^{1/3})$. Equivalently, $q\ge (2^{ch}/64^d-m_h-1)/4$. When $d\le ch/12$ and $h$ is sufficiently large, $q=2^{\Omega(h)}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine Haken's $L_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}$ with (1), take base-two logarithms, and use $\log_2 64=6$. Under a polynomial node budget, $\log(m_h+4q+1)=O_K(\log h)=o(h)$. The remaining inequalities follow by elementary rearrangement.
\end{proof}

This is an \emph{unconditional quantified lower bound on the number of paid AND2 extension definitions required in polynomial-length $E$ proofs of a published hard family}. It is NOT a new lower bound on the full combined $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, because its other legal operations were excluded. It does not imply any arbitrary SAT computation lower bound or B3.
