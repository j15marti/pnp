\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: kernel intersection.}
Let
\[
g_i(s,u)=\langle x_i(s),u\rangle,\qquad u\in\mathbb F_2^b,
\]
and $M=1+m+\binom m2$. For each mask $u$, let $E_u$ be the degree-at-most-two evaluation map on source states $s$, with columns indexed by $1,g_i,g_ig_j$. Let $E_{\rm full}$ evaluate on all legal pairs $(s,u)$. Then
\[
\boxed{\ker E_{\rm full}=\bigcap_{u\in\mathbb F_2^b}\ker E_u},
\qquad
\boxed{\rho_{\rm full}=M-\dim\bigcap_u\ker E_u}.
\]
Hence global maximal rank does not require any one fixed mask to have maximal rank.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: weight-at-most-two masks suffice.}
For coefficient vector $q=(c,\alpha_i,\beta_{ij})$,
\[
Q_q(g(s,u))=c+\sum_a A_a(s)u_a+\sum_{a<c}B_{ac}(s)u_au_c,
\]
where
\[
A_a=\sum_i\alpha_i x_{i,a}+\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}x_{i,a}x_{j,a},
\]
\[
B_{ac}=\sum_{i<j}\beta_{ij}(x_{i,a}x_{j,c}+x_{i,c}x_{j,a}).
\]
A multilinear Boolean polynomial of degree at most two is identically zero iff it vanishes at $0$, every $e_a$, and every $e_a+e_c$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\ker E_{\rm full}
=
\ker E_0\cap\bigcap_a\ker E_{e_a}\cap\bigcap_{a<c}\ker E_{e_a+e_c}.
}
\]
Only $1+b+\binom b2$ masks are required, rather than all $2^b$ masks.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: stacked certificate.}
Stack the evaluation matrices for $0,e_a,e_a+e_c$ vertically into $\mathcal E_{\le2}$. Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\mathcal E_{\le2}=\rho_{\rm full}}.
\]
Thus an $M\times M$ nonsingular minor anywhere in this stack proves $\rho_{\rm full}=M=\Theta(m^2)$. Rows may come from different masks. This strictly strengthens the single-full-mask sufficient criterion in MASK2 and explains how individually deficient label-bit masks can collectively give full global rank.
