\section{Construction}
Let \(H\) be a deduplicated, monotone CNF with clause width one through three
and at most two occurrences per sign per variable. Use stable source-order
deduplication. The empty conjunction maps to a fixed six-variable SAT M22
formula \(Y\); an empty clause maps to \(U\). Otherwise, let \(n,m,L\) be the
numbers of active variables, clauses and literal occurrences, and set
\(E=3m-L\), \(D=4n-L\).

Make three disjoint copies of \(H\). For each triple of corresponding clauses
of width \(\ell\), add \(3-\ell\) fresh \(F_0\) factories, or their sign-duals for a
negative clause. A factory's three ports pad the three corresponding clauses.
All added literals are forced false. Existing variables retain their original
occurrence counts. For each original variable and each missing signed
occurrence, attach a fresh \(G_+\) or \(G_-\) to its three copies. Each filler
is placed immediately after its ports' final source use. Finally append
 disjoint \(Y\), whose positive triples are 123,145,246,356 and negative
triples are 124,136,235,456. It has 20 models and guarantees a missing
complementary clause partner. Denote the completed formula by \(R(H)\).

\begin{theorem}
The output \(R(H)\) is a unique proper monotone 3-CNF with exactly two positive
and two negative occurrences per variable, is not fully paired, and satisfies
\[
\SAT(R(H))=\SAT(H).
\]
For the nonconstant cases,
\[
n_R=3n+198E+6D+6,\qquad m_R=3m+263E+9D+8.
\]
Consequently \(n_R\le417m+6\) and \(m_R\le556m+8\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every factory port fills precisely its missing occurrence in a padded clause.
Each source copy fills exactly its signed deficits through neutral gadgets.
All private variables have balanced quotas; freshness and source
deduplication prevent repeated clauses. The disjoint \(Y\) guarantees
nonpaired membership. A model of the output restricts to a model of each
source copy because all padding literals are false. Conversely, any three
satisfying copy assignments extend through the fixed factories, all
universally extendible fillers, and \(Y\). Both labels are preserved without
first solving \(H\). Counting the \(E\) factories and \(D\) fillers gives the
identities. Since \(n\le L\) and \(L\ge m\),
\[
n_R=27n+594m-204L+6\le417m+6,
\quad m_R=792m+36n-272L+8\le556m+8.
\]
\end{proof}

An explicit ordinary width-at-most-three adapter is also implemented.
Give each literal occurrence a fresh copy; equate copies by a directed
cycle of binary implications. Each copy now has at most two occurrences
per sign. Replace every mixed clause \(P\vee N\) by
\((P\vee z)\land(N\vee\neg z)\) with fresh \(z\). This preserves existential
satisfiability and the occurrence cap, and leaves a monotone short-clause
formula. Applying \(R\) gives a linear record-count ordinary-3CNF-to-M22
reduction. Its near-linear-sized writable construction buffer is charged;
it is NOT a small-workspace locally indexed FLMV transport theorem.

\section{Block size, recursive eligibility, and named joins}
For a \(q\)-clause block of an original M22 formula, let \(B\) be the variables
also used outside it, \(b=|B|\), and \(h\) its boundary-literal occurrences.
Then \(h\le3b\). Fix a boundary assignment, simplify, and compile the actual
residual. This retains fixed-branch semantics, unlike bare renaming.
For a nonconstant residual, \(E\le h\) and \(D\le2(h-E)\): a satisfied or
deduplicated original clause consumes at least one remaining boundary
incidence and loses at most two internal occurrences. Therefore
\[
 m_R\le3q+263h+8\le3q+789b+8.
\]
The empty-clause case uses the fixed \(U\). With the specified grouped
serializer and last-use deficit placement, the active frontier obeys
\(w(R(H))\le3w(H)+210\). This conservative constant allows a whole private
factory and its pending ports; it is not unchanged width.

Every query is genuinely in the requested class, including at the next
recursive level. Global SAT is reconstructed by compatible assignments to
original named boundaries and the conjunction of the corresponding block
answers. Independent incompatible SAT answers cannot be joined.
For a paid or supplied ordering of cut width \(w\), square-root blocks have
at most \(2w\) boundary names, \(O(\sqrt m\,2^{2w})\) queries, and
\(O(\sqrt m+w)\) clauses per compiled child. Logarithmic boundaries therefore
preserve the \(N^{1/2+o(1)}\) child-size exponent. No such cheaply acquired
boundary bound has been proved for all inputs. Generation, reads, unchanged
writes, arithmetic, selection, failed routes, guest work, joins, buffers,
caches and ancestor peak storage remain charged. Generic polynomial
reductions are not free FLMV transfers.

\section{Executed controls and limitations}
The dependency-free full reproducer passed 582 explicit checks, with 44
small ordinary-CNF controls (41 SAT, 3 UNSAT), 113 constructed satisfying

and the checked \(U\) proof. Five actual first-level and nine actual

Their labels were controlled by the \(U\) certificate, \(Y\) truth tables and
a verified \(V\) witness path, together with the compiler proof. Large
outputs were NOT all solved by an independent general SAT solver.

The connected SAT mate \(V\) swaps variable occurrences 194 and 5 in positive
source clauses 1 and 7: \((194,196,197),(5,7,8)\) become
\((5,196,197),(194,7,8)\). All quotas, properness and monotonicity are
preserved; the stored \(F_0\) witness satisfies \(V\). Both \(U\) and \(V\)
have 198 variables and 264 clauses, and their connectedness was checked.
Two full-formula pin/restoration levels grow \(264\to1356\to4632\) clauses,
verifying closure, NOT contraction. Another 32 projected block assignments
were sampled and compiled.

For the original \(U\) clause order, 17-clause blocks have boundary sizes
\((21,23,23,9,9,22,24,24,12,7,20,25,25,13,12,27)\).
Full enumeration would issue 259015808 queries; it was NOT executed.
This diagnoses this partition and controller only, not arbitrary SAT
algorithms. The largest sampled child had 2776 clauses, so gadget constants
dominate at these small sizes. No physical FLMV machine measurements are
claimed.
