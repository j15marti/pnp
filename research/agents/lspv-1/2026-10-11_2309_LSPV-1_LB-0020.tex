could contribute.  If a total source procedure represents satisfiability of

 C=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-\theta}\right\},\qquad

most a polynomial SAT algorithm.  It is not an intermediate structural

assumption $\SAT\in\mathsf P$, without importing the unknown solver exponent.
The audit passed 14,498 exact checks.  P versus NP remains open.

\section{The charged hereditary-pass theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{HEREDITARY-OR-PASS-COMPILATION1}]
Fix $0\le\eta,r,p$ and $0<\theta<1$.  Suppose one total deterministic
uniform procedure $P$ has the following contract for every sufficiently large
$F\in\mathcal D$ of encoded length $N$.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item It emits $q(F)\le N^{\eta+o(1)}$ ordinary encoded formulas
$G_1,\ldots,G_q\in\mathcal D$, each of length at most
$N^{\theta+o(1)}$.
\item Exact semantics is
\[
 \SAT(F)=\bigvee_{i=1}^{q(F)}\SAT(G_i).                 \tag{2}
\]
\item Total local time, including source access, route discovery, failed
routes, translation, child construction and output, is $N^{r+o(1)}$; local
writable space is $N^{p+o(1)}$.
\item Children are generated and evaluated one at a time.  The actual current
child is materialized in writable space.  Addresses, recursion state, replay
and retained ancestors are charged in the same realization.
\end{enumerate}
Then there is one ordinary deterministic uniform SAT decider on $\mathcal D$
with
\[
 T(N)=N^{C+o(1)},\qquad
 C=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-\theta}\right\},          \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 S(N)=N^{\rho+o(1)},\qquad \rho=\max\{p,\theta\}.       \tag{4}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Recursively apply $P$, evaluating children depth first and stopping at the
first YES child.  Correctness follows inductively from (2).  The maximum
nonconstant depth is $O(\log\log N)$ because lengths follow
$N,N^{\theta+o(1)},N^{\theta^2+o(1)},\ldots$.

Ignoring subpolynomial factors until the final line, the time recurrence is
\[
 T(N)\le aN^r+N^\eta T(N^\theta).                       \tag{5}
\]
At level $j$, aggregate local work has exponent
\[
 r\theta^j+\eta\sum_{i<j}\theta^i,
\]
and the leaf multiplicity has exponent
$\eta\sum_{i<d}\theta^i$.  The geometric sum is at most
$\eta/(1-\theta)$, giving (3); equality contributes only a subpolynomial
depth factor.  The standard fixed-$\varepsilon$ expansion absorbs all stated
$o(1)$ terms.

For space, a depth-first realization retains only local workspace, the one
current child, and $O(\log N)$ bits of indices per level.  Thus
\[
 S(N)\le aN^p+N^{\theta+o(1)}+S(N^{\theta+o(1)}),        \tag{6}
\]
whose geometrically decreasing stack gives (4).  This is the same actual
execution used for (3), so SAME\_REALIZATION is satisfied.
\end{proof}

The materialized-output contract also forces the consistency check
$r\ge\eta+\theta$ when all children of worst-case size are written in full.
A virtual child may evade this volume, but only by replacing item 4 with a
precise random-access generator; its per-bit time, generator space, repeated
queries and parent retention must then be inserted into (5)--(6).  A succinct
descriptor is not an uncharged ordinary SAT input.

\begin{corollary}[\texttt{FORBIDDEN-PASS-COROLLARY1}]
If the machine and encoding in Theorem 1 match an unconditional SAT
time--space lower bound and the resulting $(C,\rho)$ lies strictly in its
forbidden region, no such hereditary pass exists.  In particular,
$C+\rho<\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ contradicts the matched FLMV product endpoint.
The point $(\theta,\eta,r,p)=(1/2,1/2,1,1/2)$ yields
$(C,\rho)=(1,1/2)$ and is excluded.  The point
$(2/3,1/3,1,2/3)$ has $C+\rho=5/3>\varphi$ and is not excluded by that
product inequality; it needs the separately matched near-linear-time,
sublinear-space theorem.  Endpoint models may not be interchanged.
\end{corollary}

This corollary is a lower bound on a declared source-pass interface, not a
proof of (1).  Conversely, if $\SAT\in\mathsf P$ with time exponent $K$, a
trivial pass may first decide $F$ and emit a fixed YES or NO formula.  It has
$\theta=\eta=0$ but $r=K$ (and the solver's space exponent).  Therefore
$\SAT\in\mathsf P$ supplies semantic contraction but not the favorable
$K$-independent numerical landing needed for contradiction.

\section{What the theorem falsifies and what remains}

The synthesis phrase ``externally bounded hereditary source pass'' can now be
read exactly.  Once (2), heredity and all costs are present, the pass is not a
resource that an arbitrary SAT execution must be proved to expose.  It is
already executable algorithmic machinery.  If its recurrence lands in a
forbidden region, its existence is unconditionally false; if not, it merely
constructs a polynomial SAT algorithm.  Thus
\texttt{HEREDITARY-PASS-AS-INTERMEDIATE1} is \RETIRED.

The load-bearing arrow is instead
\texttt{FAVORABLE-PASS-ACQUISITION1}: under the counterfactual
$\SAT\in\mathsf P$, derive one fixed pass satisfying Theorem 1 with numerical
bounds independent of the unknown exponent $K$, without first solving the
parent at scale $N$.  This is separation-strength and \OPEN.  LB-0016 and
LB-0017 still constrain literal-restriction realizations: all branches have
exponential volume, while one power-shrunk YES child fixes a linear partial
witness.  LB-0019 supplies only a linear source-probe floor.  None transfers
to arbitrary one-bit computation.

\section{Hostile controls and barrier audit}

DIRECT, DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP and RECOMP cannot change (2), but their
actual construction time and space change $r,p$ and are charged.  Padding,
redundant clauses and alternate encodings change $N$; de-padding,
translation and random access are charged before any endpoint comparison.
Independent products may make (2) cheap.  Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin and
bounded-treewidth descendants can be solved by semantic shortcuts, showing
again that contraction geometry alone is not hardness.  A final one-bit
answer is sufficient for the recursion; witness reconstruction is not
silently required.

The theorem and its recursion relativize: the same oracle may be supplied at
every node.  Algebraic access can only make local construction or child
solution easier, so no nonalgebrizing hinge is present.  Natural Proofs is not
the active obstacle because no large constructive circuit property is used.
FLMV and Fortnow are directly transferable only after the exact random-access
machine and simultaneous resource accounting match.  Chavrimootoo et al.'s
critique is directly relevant to the missing acquisition arrow: an arbitrary
SAT algorithm is not forced to implement a chosen downward self-reduction.

Across other domains, divide-and-conquer master recurrences are a
\textbf{directly transferable theorem} because (5) is the same mathematical
object.  VLSI routing, distributed cuts, observability, proof-search and
pebbling are \textbf{misleading analogy / failed transfer} without a compiler
to the unrestricted random-access SAT model.

\section{Post-quantum technique ledger}

Schur squares, quadratic-relation ranks, Hilbert functions and syzygies are

external black-box extraction but supplies no unconditional NP transfer.
Special-soundness or collapse-binding would require two openings to yield an
