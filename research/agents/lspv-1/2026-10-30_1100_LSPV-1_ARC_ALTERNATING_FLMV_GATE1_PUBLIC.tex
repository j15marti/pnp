\[
\begin{aligned}

&=\max\{28/25,239/200,89/100+K/4\},\\
L_W&\le\max\{1/2,\tfrac12\max(3/4,L/2)\}=\max\{1/2,L/4\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The maximum constant time term is $239/200$. Because $\frac{89/100}{1-1/4}=89/75<239/200$, the fixed upper pair is $(239/200,1/2)$. Starting from arbitrary finite $K,L$, after $j$ composites:
\[
