Strict slack handles $N^{t+o(1)}$ time and $N^{s+o(1)}$ work space. A failed test does NOT show an achievable point or survival of all higher-level FLMV exclusions.

\begin{theorem}[Exact two-level certificate]
For any $t\ge1$ and $s\ge0$,
\[
(t,s)\in B_2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s<\beta(t).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$\beta(c)=1/(2c)+1/c^2-c/2$ is continuous and strictly decreasing for $c>0$, since
\[
\beta'(c)=-1/(2c^2)-2/c^3-1/2<0.
\]
The forward implication follows from $c>t$ and monotonicity. For the reverse, continuity of $\beta$ allows $c>t$ sufficiently close to $t$ to preserve a strict gap $s<\beta(c)$; because $t\ge1$ the guard $c>1$ holds. Equality at $s=\beta(t)$ is not certified.
\end{proof}
