\section{Exact phase-sandwich theorem}
Fix any Boolean language $L\subseteq\{0,1\}^*$ and a
deterministic total uniform decider $A$ on all bit strings.
Malformed SAT strings are included in the language's ordinary
NO semantics. Assume polynomial worst-case time $T_A(N)$ and
counted writable/index space $S_A(N)$, and a charged
indexed input-access model. For any length-$N\ge1$ source
$x=x_0x_1\cdots x_{N-1}$, define $x^{(b)}$ by replacing
$x_0$ with $b$, leaving every other bit unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[PHASE-SANDWICH136; PROVED/EXACT]
For every fixed such $A$ there exist two fixed, uniform,
total exact all-input machines $A_{\mathrm{early}}$ and
$A_{\mathrm{late}}$ for $L$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{early}}(N),T_{\mathrm{late}}(N)
  &=O((N+T_A(N))\operatorname{polylog}N),\\
S_{\mathrm{early}}(N),S_{\mathrm{late}}(N)
  &=O(S_A(N)+\log(N+2)).
\end{aligned} \tag{1}
\]
The first physically reads every input bit before performing
the computation of $A$. The second computes both exact
bits $A(x^{(0)})$ and $A(x^{(1)})$ before physically reading
$x_0$. It reads $x_0$ as its \emph{last previously unread}
source bit and halts with the correct output after
$O(\log N)$ further charged elementary bit operations.
Both machines eventually physically read every input bit.
For any $(t,s)\in E$ with $t\ge1$ and $s\ge0$,
both machines separately realize the \emph{same simultaneous
exponent upper bounds} $(t,s)$; neither strictly improves them.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the early machine, perform a paid indexed sweep of all
$N$ source bits, holding only an index and temporary
register of logarithmic size, without doing source-dependent
SAT computation. Then run $A$ on the unchanged read-only
source and output its one bit. The scan costs at most
$O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit operations and $O(\log N)$
writable/index bits.

For the late machine, sweep physical positions
$1,\ldots,N-1$ without inspecting position zero.
Run a virtual simulation of $A$ on $x^{(0)}$, and another on
$x^{(1)}$. Each simulated input probe at position zero
receives the stated constant from finite control; this
is a charged internal action, not a physical read of $x_0$.
All other virtual input probes are paid source reads.
The simulated work/index tapes are ordinary charged
writable tapes. Retain two answer bits. Two sequential
simulations may use disjoint work areas, with total
peak space at most $2S_A+O(\log N)$, so no uncharged
workspace reset is needed. The uniform simulation incurs
at most polylogarithmic per-step overhead for polynomial
$T_A$. Pay for source rereads, control, comparisons,
addressing, output storage, unchanged writes and selection.

Now prepare physical source address zero \emph{before} the
last read, physically acquire $x_0$, choose the already
stored value $A(x^{(x_0)})$, and halt. The latter equals
$A(x)$ on every input, valid or malformed. Only a constant
number of fixed-control steps, conservatively $O(\log N)$
charged bit work, follows this read. The empty string is
handled separately. This proves (1). For $t\ge1$ and
$s\ge0$, fixed simulation factors and polylogarithms
preserve the jointly witnessed exponent bounds.
There is no nonuniform advice.
\end{proof}

\section{Direct lower-bound consequence and weakest repair}
Let $\Phi_N(C)$ be a noncircular potential on complete
machine states, with $\Phi_N(C_{\rm halt})=0$. The sought
all-policy local decrease condition is
\[
\Phi_N(C)-\Phi_N(C')
\le L(N)\,u(C,C') \tag{2}
\]
for every permitted adaptive transition, where
$u(C,C')$ counts the complete elementary bit work.
Let $C_{\rm full}$ be the earliest state after
\emph{all physical source bits} have been read.

\begin{corollary}[Deferred-pivot firewall; PROVED/EXACT]
For the uniform late machine from the theorem,
\[
\boxed{\Phi_N(C_{\rm full}^{\rm late})
  \le O(L(N)\log N).} \tag{3}
\]
Thus a proposed universal initial bound
$\Phi_N(C_{\rm full})\ge H(N)$ \emph{for every exact
decider on a hard family} is incompatible with (2)
when $H(N)/(L(N)\log N)\to\infty$. In particular
a superpolynomial initial $H$ and polynomial $L$
cannot coexist with that universal phase assumption.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
All work after the last previously unread physical
bit in the late machine is $O(\log N)$. Sum (2)
over those final transitions and telescope to
$\Phi_N(C_{\rm halt})=0$. The alleged universal initial
lower bound must also apply to this correct
decider, yielding the contradiction.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precisely what survives.}
The theorem does \emph{not} show that total SAT decision
is easy. Both hypothetical hard computations occur
\emph{before} the last physical bit is read.
It also does not refute a potential initiated at
a \emph{declared paid clean scan-first state};
the first construction gives that normal form
at no exponent loss for $t\ge1$.
A proof using this repair would still need an
independently large initial $\Phi$ on that clean
state and bounded drop under \emph{all} later
permitted adaptive transitions. That is separately
\textbf{OPEN: POST-POTENTIAL-136}.
Defining $\Phi$ as unknown optimal remaining work
would be circular. The existing source-observation
hitting potential $\tau\le N$ cannot be summed
over later stages and supplies only acquisition work.
A one-Lipschitz function on an unrestricted
$s$-bit Hamming cube has range at most $s$;
legal state transitions and the cost of selecting them
must be represented explicitly.

\section{Contraction spine and full bit-resource ledger}
The new normal forms have successor exponent upper
bounds $(t',s')\le(\max\{1,t\},s)$ for $s\ge0$.
They give no strict contraction, ordinary shorter
children or semantically favorable parent merge.
In particular they do \emph{not} fill ARC-EDGE-90.
For the late machine, in the same bit-access model,
the itemized ledger is:
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Source acquisition:}
$N-1$ initial physical reads and one final pivot read.
Every additional actual source read during both
simulations is paid, at most $2R_A(N)$.
\item[] \textbf{Virtual probes:}
At most $2R_A(N)$ including synthetic position-zero
answers. A synthetic answer is computed in finite
control, not a real source read and not an oracle.
\item[] \textbf{Work reads and writes:}
$O((N+T_A)\operatorname{polylog}N)$, including
unchanged-value writes and two saved output bits.
\item[] \textbf{Control, addressing, selection and retries:}
$O((N+T_A)\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work.
Two complete simulations are explicitly included;
no free failed trials or hidden strategy selection.
\item[] \textbf{Ordinary children, gluing, ancestors and caches:}
No SAT child is generated, no implicit input
serialization is used, no parent glue is needed.
Retain at most two simulated workspaces and two
saved result bits, with no uncharged cache.
\item[] \textbf{Peak live writable/index bits:}
$O(S_A+\log N)$ jointly with the work bound;
read-only physical source is excluded exactly as in FLMV.
\item[] \textbf{Cumulative occupied bit-time:}
$\sum_{\text{actual steps}}S_{\rm live}$
is separately bounded by
$O((N+T_A)\operatorname{polylog}N)\cdot O(S_A+\log N)$.
\item[] \textbf{Complete-state flips:}
Not equated with write count; head, index and
control flips are distinct and charged.
No physical transistor-switch count is claimed.
\item[] \textbf{Program/representation size:}
A fixed compiler adds $O(1)$ controller description
around $A$; the input remains length $N$ and
no advice is allowed.
\end{description}
