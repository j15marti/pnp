\section*{IDEA and falsifiers}
\noindent Hypothesis: a hypothetical unit-cost LOCAL-CONVERT that projects unbounded many local factors onto just one public bit has a potentially global coNP-hard checking requirement. Falsifiers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: zero is missing from a source projection.
\item[] F2: output one exists without a positive one-in-three satisfying assignment or vice versa.
\item[] F3: falsely forced zero survives independent semantic replay on a SAT control.
\item[] F4: the historical LOCAL rule excludes unbounded global factor bundles, in which case this reduction is inapplicable to a LEGAL native rule.
\end{enumerate}
\section*{Exact reduction}
\noindent Set $R(a,b,c)$ iff $a+b=c$ under integer addition on bits, equivalently $R=\{000,011,101\}$. The forbidden five triples give a five-clause signed 3-CNF definition. For every positive exactly-one-in-three instance $G$ with triples $(a_j,b_j,c_j)$, fresh internal wires $s_j$ and ONE shared public wire $t$, let
\[
Q_G(x,s,t)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m (R(a_j,b_j,s_j)\wedge R(s_j,c_j,t)).
\]
Each conjunct has exactly three literal ports. Its total signed source encoding has polynomial bit length in $G$.
\begin{theorem}
$\{t:\exists x,s\, Q_G(x,s,t)\}=\{0,1\}$ when $G$ has a positive exact-one solution, and $\{0\}$ otherwise. Consequently, checking $Q_G\models(t=0)$ is coNP-complete on this restricted always-satisfiable, one-export-bit, ternary-factor family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two factors force $s_j=a_j+b_j$ and $t=s_j+c_j$, so their conjunction is equivalent to $a_j+b_j+c_j=t$ for every $j$. All-zero original and internal values with $t=0$ satisfy every factor. $t=1$ holds precisely if every triple has exactly one true variable. By Schaefer (STOC 1978), positive 1-in-3 SAT is NP-complete. The complement property is in coNP because a violated implication has an explicit polynomial-length witness $x,s,t=1$. No unconditional superpolynomial time lower bound follows.
\end{proof}
