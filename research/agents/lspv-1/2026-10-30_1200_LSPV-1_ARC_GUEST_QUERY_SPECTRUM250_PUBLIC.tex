\begin{theorem}[Completed-call guest-query spectrum, restricted]
Suppose a correct polynomial-time SAT decider \(A_0\) exists on FLMV's indexed random-access read-only-input Turing machine, with unknown finite exponents \(K_0,L_0\). For every fixed positive integer \(D\), form a correct decider \(A_D\) that executes at least \(m^D\) physical steps on each \(m\)-bit ordinary SAT input and then runs \(A_0\).
Let one fixed source-correct hereditary compiler \(\Phi\) use an \emph{opaque, timing-insensitive, completed-CALL} interface: its completed guest-query list on source \(F\) depends only on the original source and the Boolean answers, not guest code, running time, timeouts or unfinished calls. Assume for all these \(A_D\) a simultaneous bound
\[
T_{\Phi(A_D)}(N)\le N^{\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda\max(K_0,D)\}+o(1)},\quad
S_{\Phi(A_D)}(N)\le N^{\max\{\sigma,\lambda L_0\}+o(1)},\quad 0<\lambda<1 .
\]
If for some infinite source sequence there are \(N^{\theta-o(1)}\) completed guest calls on physical ordinary children each of size at least \(N^{\eta-o(1)}\), necessarily, for every fixed integer \(D>0\),
\[
\boxed{\theta+D\eta\le\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda\max(K_0,D)\}.}
\]
Consequently \(\eta\le\lambda\), and when \(\eta=\lambda\), \(\theta\le\gamma\). More generally, if \(m_{i,N}\) are all actual completed guest-query lengths on such a source, then
\[
\limsup_{N\to\infty}\log_N\bigl(\sum_i m_{i,N}^D\bigr)\le
\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda\max(K_0,D)\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The completed-query transcript is independent of \(D\) because every guest replies with the same SAT answer. Every physically completed call of length \(m\) spends at least \(m^D\) guest steps, and these sequential time costs add. Therefore the claimed source-run lower bound is \(\sum_i m_{i,N}^D\), which cannot exceed the assumed worst-case upper bound. Taking logarithmic exponents proves the inequalities. Fix \(D>K_0\) and then let its fixed integer value grow: the coefficient of \(D\) forces \(\eta\le\lambda\). At equality choose \(D\) large enough that \(\gamma+\lambda D\) dominates \(\alpha\); subtract \(\lambda D\) to get \(\theta\le\gamma\). No \(D=D(N)\) is used.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Firewall.} This result does not apply to a compiler inspecting the guest code, preempting it, using timing, refusing completed calls, or directly deciding from its own source proof. It proves no hardness for arbitrary SAT and no existence of \(\Phi\).

\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous payload storage]
If one physical FLMV execution keeps \(q\) disjoint, independently stored ordinary child payloads of length at least \(m\) on counted writable tapes at the same time, its writable space is at least \(qm\) bits. Thus \(q=N^{\theta-o(1)},m=N^{\eta-o(1)}\) imply \(S\ge N^{\theta+\eta-o(1)}\). The illustrative \(q=N^{3/5},m=N^{1/2}\) buffering uses \(N^{11/10-o(1)}\) bits, not \(N^{1/2+o(1)}\). Streaming, recomputation and virtual descriptions are not excluded but must pay source/bit-access and reconstruction costs.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Disjoint simultaneously materialized payloads occupy at least \(qm\) counted cells. Their peak is a single same-execution quantity; a write-only output cannot be recycled for free read access.\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Direct fixed-width strict22 cofactor floor]
For a proper signed exact-(2-positive,2-negative)-3CNF with \(n\) original variables and \(m=4n/3\) clauses, pinning \(k\) distinct variables and \emph{only} syntactically restricting clauses leaves at least \(m-4k\) untouched width-three source clauses. With fixed original literal-ID width and \(h_n=2+3(1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil)\), a physical clause-list child has \(N_\rho\ge\max(0,m-4k)h_n\) bits. If original \(N=mh_n+O(\log n)\) and fixed \(0<\lambda<1\), child size \(N^\lambda\) requires \(k\ge n/3-O(N^\lambda/\log n)=n/3-o(n)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Every variable touches exactly four source clauses. At most \(4k\) clauses are touched by \(k\) pins. Every untouched clause appears unchanged in this declared direct cofactor encoding, giving the bit and pin bounds.\end{proof}
\noindent This syntactic architecture bound does not constrain algebraic compression, resolution, globally recognizable easy exact22 or arbitrary correct SAT deciders. It cannot establish a universal ARC lower bound.
