\paragraph{Source construction.}
Let $b\ge2$, $n=3b$, and use disjoint indexed triples $X_j=(x_{3j+1},x_{3j+2},x_{3j+3})$, $0\le j<b$. Let $p_j=j\bmod2$. For $u\in\{0,1\}^3$ define $K_{j,u}$ as the single proper ternary clause falsified by $X_j=u$:
\[
K_{j,u}=\bigvee_{k=1}^3\begin{cases}x_{3j+k}&u_k=0,\\ \neg x_{3j+k}&u_k=1.\end{cases}
\quad
F_b=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{b-1}\ \bigwedge_{\oplus_k u_k\ne p_j}K_{j,u}.
\]
Every block contains four original clauses. Every source variable has exactly two positive and two negative signed occurrences. Hence $F_b$ is proper signed exact-$(2^+,2^-)$-3CNF, with $4b$ source clauses, $12b$ signed occurrences, and explicit source input length $N=\Theta(b\log b)$ under logarithmic identifiers. Its models are precisely $X_j$ of XOR parity $p_j$, independently: $|\operatorname{Mod}(F_b)|=4^b$. Since both parity values occur among the $p_j$, the complete globally consistent endpoints $0^n$ and $1^n$ each falsify a block and are not witnesses. A witness is obtained by setting the first variable of each odd-target block to 1 and all other variables to 0. Signed polarity renaming simply translates the corresponding target parity and does not affect the argument.

For a same-source UNSAT control, conjoin the four opposite-parity clauses on $X_0$ to $F_b$. This companion remains proper 3CNF but is not claimed strict-$(2,2)$ on $X_0$. Its eight clauses on the first block yield a seven-step ordinary resolution refutation: resolve four pairs on $x_3$ to the four binary signed patterns on $(x_1,x_2)$; resolve two pairs on $x_1$ to units $x_2$ and $\neg x_2$; resolve those units to the empty clause. This establishes an authentic UNSAT certificate, not a failed search timeout.

\begin{lemma}[Exact residual clause and model counts]
For any coherent source-variable cube $\rho$ consistent with at least one model of $F_b$, let $k_j$ denote the number of variables fixed inside $X_j$. Under \emph{raw syntactic restriction only}, the remaining original clauses from block $j$ number $c_j\in\{4,2,1,0\}$ respectively for $k_j=0,1,2,3$. The number of compatible satisfying block assignments is respectively $v_j\in\{4,2,1,1\}$. Consequently
\[
|\operatorname{Mod}(F_b)\cap \operatorname{Cube}(\rho)|
=\prod_jv_j,\qquad
2\log_2v_j\le c_j,\qquad
|\operatorname{Mod}(F_b)\cap\operatorname{Cube}(\rho)|\le2^{\lfloor\sum_jc_j/2\rfloor}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every partial assignment with $k_j<3$ has both an allowed and a forbidden completion of its last unset bit(s). Among four clauses excluding exactly the forbidden parity triples, exactly $2^{2-k_j}$ remain unsatisfied by the fixed literals, reducing to nonempty clauses. The number of satisfying extensions is $2^{2-k_j}$ for $k_j=0,1,2$. With all three variables fixed consistently, all four clauses disappear and exactly one model remains. Thus $(c_j,v_j)=(4,4),(2,2),(1,1),(0,1)$. The displayed inequality holds in all four cases, and multiplication over disjoint source triples proves the claim. For a cube with no model, the model-cover contribution is zero, so it may be discarded.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Power-short raw-cofactor cover barrier, restricted]
Let $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_q$ be any adaptive, overlapping or repeated coherent source-variable cubes, and assume the \emph{source-relative model-cover premise}
\[
F_b\models\bigvee_{i=1}^q\operatorname{Cube}(\rho_i).
\]
If each model-compatible raw syntactic cofactor $F_b|_{\rho_i}$ retains at most $s$ nonempty original clauses, then
\[
\boxed{q\ge\frac{4^b}{2^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}}.}
\]
If every such ordinary physically encoded child has length at most $N^\lambda$ bits, for any fixed $0\le\lambda<1$, then $s\le N^\lambda=o(b)$ and
\[
\boxed{q\ge2^{2b-o(b)}.}
\]
Thus a polynomially bounded \emph{certified source-cover OR decomposition using only raw restricted children} cannot at once give physical power-short children on this infinite, easy affine family. This does not bound arbitrary $A$ or representations obtained by semantic simplification.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each child with at most $s$ residual clauses, the lemma shows that its cube intersects at most $2^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}$ source models. Every one of the $4^b$ source models lies in at least one child cube by the authenticated cover hypothesis. The union bound therefore gives $4^b\le q2^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}$. Each surviving nonempty clause occupies at least one physical bit, so $s\le N^\lambda$. For an explicit source $N=\Theta(b\log b)$, $N^\lambda=o(b)$ if $\lambda<1$. Substitution proves the power-short bound. Repeated/overlapping cubes can only weaken coverage efficiency; adaptivity does not change this final realized-cover counting. The statement has \emph{no premise} covering semantic child transformations or direct source recognition.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Original-clause S/U, complement and proof provenance.}
For every signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$ in each original ternary clause $K_{j,u}$, its local ALL-literals-UNSAT target is exactly the forbidden triple $u$; its ALL-literals-SAT target is $\neg u$ on those same original variables. Complement inversion swaps S and U, but local targets from distinct clauses are not independent global assignments. Within a single parity block, any two distinct forbidden triples have Hamming distance two: they share one same-polarity occurrence and two opposite-polarity occurrences. Therefore SS, SU, US, UU are \emph{all infeasible} for that pair; nevertheless $F_b$ is SAT with $4^b$ models and those clauses can be disjunctively SAT in middle states. Between distinct blocks, all four extreme pair modes are feasible. Globally, each original clause directly proves that the cube assigning all three of its literals FALSE is forbidden: the clause itself is the source-parent proof. All-zero/all-one counts, every complement, coherent flip trails and the four 3CNF true-literal categories are checked in the raw producer. No hypothetical occurrence-wise incompatible target is treated as a global assignment.
