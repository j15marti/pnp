\section*{Exact theorem and proof}
Let $D$ be the exact ordered image recognizer/decoder of IMAGE22DECODE1.  It runs in deterministic polynomial time and satisfies
\[
 D(y)=\bot\iff y\notin I_U,
 \qquad D(\operatorname{enc}U(F))=B(F),
\]
where $B$ is the balanced source normalizer.  For every $G\in\mathcal C_{22}$, exact balance gives $B(G)=G$.

Choose one fixed malformed string $z_\bot$ (the bytes for \texttt{[]}); it is rejected by both languages.  Define
\[
 q(y)=\begin{cases}y,&D(y)\ne\bot,\\z_\bot,&D(y)=\bot,
 \end{cases}
\qquad
 r(x)=\begin{cases}
 \operatorname{enc}U(G),&x=\operatorname{enc}(G),\ G\in\mathcal C_{22},\\
 z_\bot,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Both functions are total.  Since $U$ preserves satisfiability and $D(\operatorname{enc}U(G))=G$, one has
\[
 x\in\Ltwo\iff r(x)\in\KU,
 \qquad
 y\in\KU\iff q(y)\in\Ltwo.
\]
The second equivalence includes off-image satisfiable $\mathcal C_{22}$ strings: they are sent to $z_\bot$, as required by the source language $\KU$.

For a $\mathcal C_{22}$ formula with $v$ variables and $m=4v/3$ clauses, let
\[
 S(v)=\sum_{i=1}^{v}\bigl(1+\lfloor\log_{10}i\rfloor\bigr).
\]
Its exact compact JSON byte length is
\[
 J(v)=5m+2v+4S(v)+1=\frac{26v}{3}+4S(v)+1.
\]
The transparent compiler applied to an already balanced $G$ has $12v$ variables and $16v$ clauses, hence output length $J(12v)$.  Grouping each $j\in[12v]$ with $i=\lceil j/12\rceil$ gives
\[
 \operatorname{digits}(j)\leq \operatorname{digits}(i)+2,
 \qquad S(12v)\leq12S(v)+24v.
\]
Therefore
\[
 J(12v)\leq200v+48S(v)+1<\frac{520v}{3}+80S(v)+20=20J(v),
\]
because $S(v)\geq v$.  Parsing, domain validation, $D$, and $U$ admit a conservative uniform $O(N^3)$ implementation.  Thus a time-$O(N^k)$ decider on either language yields a time-$O(N^{\max\{3,k\}})$ decider on the other (with the constant $20^k$ charged explicitly in the $r$ direction).  This handles every fixed exponent $k$, not merely a preselected exponent.
