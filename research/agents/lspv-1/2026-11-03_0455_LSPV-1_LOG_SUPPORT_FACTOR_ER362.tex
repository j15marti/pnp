A named Boolean factor $P$ on support $S_P$ is represented by its exact complete CNF of assignment-blocking clauses, one clause per assignment falsifying $P$. A source clause is already its single complete blocker on its literal support. Consider a legal one- or two-parent factor inference with exact predecessor relations $P_1,P_2$ and exact output relation $Q$ satisfying $P_1\land P_2\models Q$ (omit $P_2$ in a unary step). \begin{theorem}[Authenticated factor compiler]
For every $w\ge1$, given authenticated exact blocker-CNF presentations of $P_1,P_2$, the complete blocker-CNF presentation of any implied $Q$ over union support of size $w$ has an explicit legal $E$ derivation with at most
\[
\boxed{K(w)=(3w-1)2^w}
\]
additional paid nodes, preserving signed names and provenance. This includes identically true and identically false output factors. Offline witness-row selection is not assigned a free machine-time interpretation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a false output assignment $\beta$ on $S_Q$. Each extension $\alpha$ of $\beta$ to $S$ falsifies at least one predecessor factor, hence falsifies one of its already authenticated full blocker clauses $D_\alpha$ on support $T\subseteq S$. If $D_\alpha$ is empty, the empty clause is already proved. Otherwise $D_\alpha$ is a subclause of the full $S$-blocker $B_\alpha$. Write $d=|T|$. If $d=w$, reuse it at zero cost. If $1\le d<w$, the pre-existing LSPV-2 PAID-CLAUSE-WEAKENING352 theorem extends $D_\alpha$ to $B_\alpha$ using exactly $3d+2(w-d)-2=2w+d-2\le3w-2$ paid conservative definitions and genuine RES steps. All extra names are fresh.
Pairwise resolve the $2^{w-|S_Q|}$ full blockers extending $\beta$ on the variables outside $S_Q$, requiring $2^{w-|S_Q|}-1$ genuine RES steps and deriving exactly the blocker of $\beta$ on $S_Q$. Repeat for at most $2^{|S_Q|}$ false output rows. Total cost is at most $(3w-2)2^w+(2^w-1)<(3w-1)2^w$. For $Q$ identically true no clause is needed; for $Q$ false with empty support, the same elimination yields the empty clause. Every intermediate clause is non-tautological. All inference nodes are ordinary paid operations of the unchanged $E$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Scoped all-shared-DAG simulation]
Let $\pi$ be any $q$-paid-node valid $R$ refutation whose non-$E$ nodes all satisfy the preceding exact factor grammar, introducing no unnamed fresh variables. If $q_E$ of its nodes belong to $E$ and its non-$E$ nodes have union supports $w_v$, then
\[
W_E(F)\le q_E+\sum_{v\notin E}K(\max\{1,w_v\})+O(1).
\]
All shared intermediate factors are compiled once. Thus if $q$ is polynomial in encoded source length $N$, each $w_v=O(\log(N+q))$, and factor records have polynomial explicit bit descriptions, the translated $E$ proof has polynomial paid-node and canonical evidence-bit size.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Process the DAG topologically, retaining authenticated source clauses and paid conservative definitions. Translate each exact factor to its equivalent full blocker CNF with the preceding compiler; replay strict RES clauses and definition slots, with globally fresh translator gate names. Exact equivalence of each parent-factor CNF preserves the semantic validity of later factor inferences, and every shared reference targets a previously compiled representation. The final false factor yields the empty clause. Sum paid distinct nodes. The logarithmic-support conclusion follows from $2^{c\log_2(N+q+2)}=(N+q+2)^c$ and polynomial name/reference indexing. This makes no claim for arbitrary huge inline circuit syntax with uncharged gate records.
\end{proof}
