## 1. Published hard-family anchor and research question

The starting published theorem is Haken's 1985 lower bound on the *unrestricted shared-DAG ordinary Resolution* proof of the standard **unary pigeonhole** signed CNF \(P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^h\). For constants \(c>0,h_0\), every \(h\ge h_0\) and every ordinary Resolution DAG refutation \(\pi\) of \(P_h\) satisfy \(|\pi|_{\mathrm{lines}}\ge 2^{ch}\). Its source has \(h(h+1)\) variables, \(m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}\) signed clauses, and conventional explicit indexed signed source encoding length \(N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))\) bits (using a standard bounded-overhead signed-literal list). With free source axioms, derived-Resolution-node cost is at least \(2^{ch}-m_h\). The quantifiers cover **all Resolution DAGs on each sufficiently large source**, not Extended Resolution, Res(oplus), the combined calculus, or uniform SAT machines. Cook (1976) gives polynomial Extended Resolution PHP proofs. Braun (arXiv:2609.23015, posted September 19, 2026) reports a \(>\exp(n/(32768\ell^2))\) lower bound for every unrestricted DAG-like **extension-free Res(oplus)** proof of bit PHP with \(n=2^\ell,\ell\ge32\), with \(\Theta(n^3\ell)\) signed literal occurrences and \(\Theta(n^3\ell^2)\) bits in a standard explicit signed binary-index encoding (each literal carries an \(O(\ell)\)-bit identifier). It is recent, Lean-reported research not a machine-time lower bound. Neither result is regenerated here.

The **unchanged preselected** \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) [LSPV-2/341 blob c87acd11] has zero-cost authenticated original clauses and unit-cost paid RES (binary premises), AND3 (at most three *original* support variables), binary GF(2) XOR, unary LOCAL-CONVERT with an authenticated local implication check, and conservative two-input DEF with its own paid node. A refutation is a shared DAG, each paid node counted once. Crucially the source text is ambiguous about whether circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT internal wires may become externally addressable for free. We do **not** settle that ambiguity by stipulation.

For valid UNSAT input \(F\) of encoded length \(N\), \(W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\sum_{v\text{ paid in }\pi}1\). Let \(\overline W_R(N)=\max_{F\text{ valid UNSAT},\,|\langle F\rangle|\le N}W_R(F)\), empty maximum zero. B1: \(\forall A\exists p_A\forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}, W_R(F)\le p_A(N(F)+T_A(F))\); B2: \(\forall A\exists p_A\forall N,\overline W_R(N)\le p_A(N+T_A^{wc}(N))\); B3: \(\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)=N^{O(1)}\), for the **same fixed R**. All remain OPEN. B1 implies B2 implies B3.

## 2. New compositional lemma: only the observed constant-support interface need survive

Consider a **typed interface fragment** of the existing mixed syntax under these *additional restrictions*:

1. Every circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT node has an explicitly authenticated common support \(S\) of at most three original Boolean variables. Its circuit is a finite acyclic, explicit fan-in-two Boolean circuit with constants and no other independent external inputs. An arbitrary number of its internal wires may be declared as ports.
2. A later paid inference may address at most a fixed constant \(a\) of those exported ports, in addition to its at-most-two paid ancestor node pointers. Every later use and every rule-legality test is **extensional** in the Boolean values of the addressed ports, source clauses and authenticated DEF wires, not intensional in circuit gate syntax, names, unused outputs, or total payload length. Exported names may be consistently alpha-renamed. A separate DEF wire used globally remains separately paid. This does not assert that arbitrary many public ports are legal under the original R_mix341.
3. Each condition/row/definition is explicitly typed; clauses are literal *sets* over source variables and separately charged DEF variables; GF(2) equations are reduced affine masks. RES, AND3, XOR, and DEF retain their original source-local semantics, charges and at-most-two-premise DAG references. A circuit-output condition can be presented by an extensionally equal constant-size Boolean circuit on its declared support without a new paid node. These are fragment hypotheses, **not a change to the original proof system**.
4. The verifier rejects malformed source encodings, ill-typed public ports, unauthorized free variables, cyclic gate DAGs, forged source references, and invalid implications. All proof evidence is explicit; no semantic SAT oracle, answer axiom or zero-cost multi-premise inference exists.

**Theorem 386 (conditional-fragment polynomial bit compactness).** There exists an absolute constant \(K_a\) depending only on the fixed typed grammar and fixed observation arity \(a\), such that for every valid UNSAT \(F\) of input length \(N\), every **finite valid** \(q\)-paid-node refutation \(\pi\) of \(F\) in the above fragment, with *arbitrarily long* serialized private gate payloads, admits a refutation \(\widehat\pi\) of the **same source** with at most \(q\) paid nodes, the same authenticated outer dependency DAG up to alpha-renaming, and full encoded proof length

$$
|\langle\widehat\pi\rangle|
 \le K_a\Bigl[N+q(v+q+1)(N+\lceil\log_2(q+2)\rceil+1)+q+1\Bigr]
 \le K'_a(N+q+2)^3,
$$

where \(v\le N\) is the number of distinct *appearing* original source variables. Constants are independent of \(F,q\) and of **all original gate-payload lengths**. Proof-finding and normalization work are *not* bounded by this output-size statement.

**Proof.** Read the entire finite original DAG *mathematically* (the theorem asserts existence, not inexpensive discovery). For each circuit producer \(C_i\), collect exactly the wire ports referenced by the remaining proof. At most \(aq\) references occur across all paid continuations. Each referenced port denotes one Boolean function on the same \(\le3\)-bit support. There are at most \(2^{2^3}=256\) distinct possible truth vectors. For each producer, replace its huge internal circuit with a canonical bank: at most \(256\cdot26=6656\) Boolean gates, plus one alias for each *referenced* port, using the shared DNF construction of prior LSPV-1/379 (blob 615e4497). Remove unused outputs and private gates. The bank reproduces the **joint** vector of observed ports for every original-support assignment. Thus all correlations under later shared use are preserved, not merely the final root. Induct on topological paid node order: every extensional local legality judgment and every downstream condition has the same Boolean meaning; signed sources, GF(2) masks and conservative DEF equations retain their semantics and original paid charges. This gives a legal proof with the same paid node count. No original support is broadened and no uncharged independent variable is introduced.

Alpha-rename the at most \(q\) fresh DEF variables in first-definition order to successive unused identifiers above both the declared source variable bound and the largest appearing source identifier. Original signed source labels remain unchanged. In explicit signed binary or decimal-index encodings, the largest source identifier and declared variable bound are already written using at most \(O(N)\) bits, and a fresh identifier needs \(O(N+\log(q+2))\) bits. A clause or affine equation has at most \(2(v+q)\) distinct signed variable occurrences, each at most that bit width. Every paid record has O(1) source/ancestor/port references aside from its clause or mask, and each circuit bank has at most 6656 gates with O(aq) aliases across the *entire* proof. Topological indices cost \(O(\log(N+q+2))\) bits. The stated uniform upper bound follows by summing over \(q\) records plus the unchanged source and gate banks. Since \(v\le N\) and \(\log(q+2)\le q+1\), the expression is \(O((N+q+2)^3)\). The only potentially enormous operation is computing the original port truth vectors from a very long supplied circuit; the theorem does not charge that work to \(W_R\). QED.

**Corollary 386A.** In this typed bounded-observation fragment, \(W(F)\le q\) has an equivalent certificate of at most \(K'_a(N+q+2)^3\) bits. The unary-budget proof language is decidable, is in NP when the fragment verifier runs in polynomial time in the supplied full evidence, and the exact minimum \(W(F)\) on valid UNSAT sources and its worst-case profile \(\overline W(N)\) are total computable functions by finite enumeration. This combines prior LSPV-1/385's abstract compactness criterion with LSPV-1/379's public-vector quotient and an explicit all-proof **bounded-use** accounting. It asserts no polynomial-time proof search and no B3.

## 3. Four falsifiers

**F1, root-only replacement with exported wires: FALSIFIED.** A circuit with root \(x\) and exported \(u=y\) differs from one with root \(x\) and exported \(u=\neg y\) on every assignment, though the two roots agree. Preserve all *observed* port vectors; the prior root-only quotient/376 is insufficient.

**F2, hidden long circuit implies long physical normalization: CORRECT PHYSICAL CONTROL, NOT LOGICAL LOWER BOUND.** An explicit length-\(L\) constant-support circuit can require \(\Omega(L)\) input-bit probes by a correct deterministic validator on a sensitive record, even when its canonical eight-bit truth vector is O(1). Constructing the bank by direct full gate evaluation takes \(O(8L)\) gate operations plus parsing and output, with potentially \(O(L)\) live explicit gate storage or repeated indexed rescans. A short *existing* proof does not imply an implementable fast SAT algorithm.

**F3, arbitrary exported wires and syntactic consumers: OPEN FOR ACTUAL GRAMMAR.** If a paid continuation can access unbounded independent input wires, many ports through uncharged syntax, or require syntactic gate identities, the theorem's common-support/constant-observation/extensional hypotheses no longer hold. This is a hypothesis violation, not an asserted legal R_mix341 counterexample. LSPV-4/363 proves circuit-valued legality checking under an explicit uncharged-gate interpretation may require length-dependent work; LSPV-1/384 proves an unrelated coNP-complete factorization problem when the boundary width grows. Neither supplies a native all-R normalization theorem.

**F4, bit length versus nodes versus P-time SAT: FALSIFIED AS A TRANSFER.** The upper cap on certificate bits is polynomial in \(N+q\), not a proof that \(q=N^{O(1)}\), not a uniform algorithm for obtaining a certificate, and not a SAT machine-time lower bound. LSPV-5/REFEREE334 (blob 961e58ad) shows even polynomially equivalent *bit-encoded* systems can differ exponentially in paid-node count via a proof-packing rule. The current theorem fixes the typed node grammar instead of inserting an uncharged packing rule.
