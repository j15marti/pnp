## Model and precise restricted theorem
Fix a distinct, sound typed calculus $R_{\mathrm{loc3,DEF2}}$ with original indexed strict proper 3-CNF source clauses; charged oriented RES; paid exact 3-exposed-wire AND3/LOCAL with no hidden or uncharged ports; binary GF(2) addition; and globally fresh, conservative, acyclic **paid two-input** Boolean definitions. Gate wires may be exposed to local operations only through the declared bounded interface. No free oracle, case split/discharge/weakening is added to **R**. The target compiler, not the source calculus, may use the actual syntactic rules of Res(XOR). Branch contexts are carried explicitly. The historical accepted-byte R_mix341 remains unauthenticated.

For a source $F$ on $n$ original variables with $m$ clauses and any source-authenticated shared-DAG $R_{\mathrm{loc3,DEF2}}$ refutation with $q$ paid nodes, let $g$ count the paid gates whose binary truth table is **nonaffine** (among 16 Boolean tables, eight are affine). Then for some absolute constant $C$ depending only on the fixed local interface,

$$
S_{\mathrm{syn\,Res}(\oplus_2)}(F)
\le C(n+1)4^g(m+q+1).
$$

The bound counts all original source and translated proof nodes, allows global DAG sharing, and explicitly charges a polynomial number of native target inferences per affine parity guard. It does **not** bound proof search, machine time for SAT, or any inference using unauthenticated historical ports.

### Construction and proof
1. Topologically evaluate all conservative DEF gates symbolically. Affine two-input gates preserve affine functions of original variables on each branch and do not cause splits. For each nonaffine gate, split on the values of its two *current affine input forms*, at most four children; specialize the gate to a constant. Gaussian elimination prunes inconsistent affine-test conjunctions. Across $g$ nonlinear gates there are at most $4^g$ terminal branches, each with a list $\Gamma$ of affine equations. All DEF outputs on a leaf are affine forms/constants over original variables.
2. On every branch substitute these outputs into the source-authenticated proof. Ordinary RES and binary GF(2) remain syntactic affine cuts/combinations. Each other declared local step depends on at most three exposed affine forms. Choose an independent affine basis (rank $k\le3$); a local truth-table implication is a constant-size entailment over at most $k$ independent Boolean coordinates, so Pang Fact 7.1 and ordinary finite Resolution give a constant-size syntactic certificate **in the basis**. Translate the basis coordinates to their affine original-variable forms. This requires the next Booleanity lemma; no assumption of a semantic inference in one step is made.
3. **Explicit affine Booleanity lemma:** For any affine $A(X)$ with $t$ nonzero coordinates, the syntactic target can derive
$$
B(A):=(A=0)\lor(A=1)
$$
from the original-variable Boolean axioms in $O(t)$ nodes. Inductively derive $B(a+b)$ from earlier $B(a),B(b)$ by four syntactic cases $u,v\in\{0,1\}$. For each case weaken $B(a)$ and $B(b)$ by opposite-value guards, combine their selected $a=u$ and $b=v$ equations to obtain $(a+b=u+v)$ under the guards, weaken by the other parity value, then eliminate the two complementary $b$ guards and finally the complementary $a$ guards using linear combination and false-equation deletion. The explicit construction is 22 paid target records for two inputs; iterating over nonzero coordinates proves the $O(t)$ claim. The same finite-basis method supplies any bounded-rank local semantic entailment through **syntactic** steps after affine substitution.
4. **Guard lifting:** Write $G_\Gamma$ for the disjunction of *opposites* of the affine tests in a leaf. Every source clause $C$ yields $C\lor G_\Gamma$ by syntactic weakening. An assumed affine equation $E\in\Gamma$ yields $E\lor G_\Gamma$ by first deriving $B(E)$ as above and weakening with the remaining guards. All inference rules then preserve the common guard by syntactic context addition. At the leaf derive $G_\Gamma$ without retaining assumptions. Complementary affine-guard clauses from all children are joined by a binary linear-combination cut yielding $(1=0)$ and a paid deletion. ALL required children are discharged; infeasible affine branches are discharged by their inconsistent test combinations. No branch-local lemma crosses an incompatible context.
5. Every branch adds $O((n+1)(m+q+g+1))$ nodes, and there are at most $4^g$ branches plus fewer than $4^g$ joins. Since $g\le q$, absorb constants into $C(n+1)4^g(m+q+1)$. All paid proof nodes, tautology constructions, guards, references, and child joins are counted. An *implemented* translation additionally pays parsing, source access, Gaussian elimination, retained/recomputed affine masks, evidence serialization, verification and peak space; this ideal proof-size estimate is not an actual algorithm runtime bound.

## Published hard strict-3CNF family and lower-bound corollary
Use Pang's explicit sampling model $\mathcal F(n,3,8n)$: $m=8n$ iid with-replacement proper signed width-3 clauses uniformly selected from $8\binom n3$ options. Encoded source length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$. Theorem 7.2(a), via random-CNF linear PC degree, gives with probability $1-o(1)$ a constant $\kappa>0$ such that **every** syntactic shared-DAG Res(XOR) refutation needs $\exp(\kappa n)$ nodes. This is proof-complexity hardness, not deterministic SAT machine hardness or a certified named finite hard sample. With the above compiler,

$$
\boxed{g\ln4+\ln\big(C(n+1)(8n+q+1)\big)\ge\kappa n.}
$$

Consequently every polynomial-size proof of a suitable member of this established family in the **restricted** typed calculus needs $g=\Omega(n)$ genuinely **nonaffine** paid definitions, while proofs with $g=o(n)$ require exponential $q$. This strengthens /483's direct-source-input **total-DEF** budget only within the explicitly permitted affine-wire interface. It is a repaired synthesis of /371 and /481, not an unrestricted mixed-system lower bound. Cook Extended Resolution and arbitrary paid nonlinear gates remain legal shortcut controls.
