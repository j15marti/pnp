\section*{Original lower-bound anchor, quantifiers, and what it does not say}
Fix the project's source-authenticated combined calculus $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ of LSPV-2/341 with free original signed CNF leaves and unit cost for each distinct paid shared-DAG inference node: strict signed binary Resolution (RES), typed at-most-three-variable AND3, binary GF(2) row XOR, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative signed two-input DEF. $E=\mathrm{ER2}$ is the already present RES+DEF fragment; $X_3$ is the normalized at-most-three-variable local-factor and explicit-row fragment from LSPV-1/359,375. No new rules are inserted in the target.

The original certified lower-bound anchor is Haken's 1985 result for the \emph{standard unary} pigeonhole CNFs $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}^{h}_{h+1}$ with $h+1$ pigeons, $h$ holes, $v_h=h(h+1)$ source variables, $m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses and ordinary explicit signed-literal input length $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$. There exist $c>0,h_0$ such that
\[
\forall h\ge h_0\ \forall\pi\in\operatorname{Ref}_{\rm Res}(P_h):
\quad |\pi|_{\rm lines}\ge 2^{ch}.
\]
These are unrestricted \emph{DAG Resolution} lines, not arbitrary SAT machine steps. The bound on paid Resolution inference nodes is $2^{ch}-m_h$. Cook's and Buss's polynomial Extension/Frege proofs of the same PHP sources defeat transfer of that exponential bound to $E$ or the full mixed calculus. Independently, Urquhart's Resolution-hard expander Tseitin sources have a linear $4h-1$ mixed AND3/GF(2) proof on the previously certified cubic odd-charge subfamily. Published lower bounds are not re-proved here.

\section*{The exact root-only fragment}
We do \emph{not} resolve the unspecified full mixed LOCAL-CONVERT circuit grammar. Instead explicitly hypothesize interpretation U from LSPV-4/363: a legal circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT may serialize an acyclic, arbitrarily long, fan-in-two Boolean gate DAG whose \emph{external root} denotes a function on at most three declared named inputs. Define $X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^{U}$ to be a usage fragment of the unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ under U satisfying all of the following syntactic/semantic conditions:

\begin{description}
\item[] Every circuit's internal gates and wire IDs are \emph{private syntax}. They are not premises, independently addressable names, inputs of a later paid DEF, or row operands. Only the declared output/root function on at most three named variables may be referenced later.
\item[] Legality of a local output and all permitted downstream uses are extensional in the actual Boolean function on the declared support, not in its chosen circuit serialization. All nonlocal XOR rows are explicit signed affine masks, as in $X_3$; every new globally usable variable is introduced by a separately paid conservative DEF. Source typing and correct clause/RES operands are preserved.
\item[] Replace any arbitrary circuit-valued local output by its exact eight-or-fewer-row truth table and obtain a legal record of the already defined $L_3$ subgrammar with \emph{the same paid node}. All other uses satisfy the declared $X_3$ grammar.
\end{description}

The third point is a \emph{typed representational requirement}, rather than an unproved fact about every intended $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ record. If full U permits exporting internal wires, intensional syntax tests, or source support expansion, the theorem below does not cover that record. Since $E\subseteq X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U$, ordinary Resolution guarantees completeness and unit integer costs guarantee attained finite minima. Let $N=|\operatorname{enc}(F)|$.

\begin{theorem}[Cost-independent existential semantic quotient]\label{thm:main}
Under the root-only/extensional premises above, there is one fixed polynomial $p(z)=C(z+3)^4$, with $C$ independent of the instance, proof, and opaque circuit payload lengths, such that
\[
\forall\,F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}_{\rm valid}:
\qquad W_{X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U}(F)
\ \le\ W_E(F)\
\le\ p\!\left(N+W_{X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U}(F)\right).
\]
More strongly, for \emph{every} legal $X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U$ shared-DAG refutation $\pi$ of $F$ with $q$ distinct paid nodes and arbitrarily large aggregate serialized circuit payload length $M$, there \emph{exists} an $E$ refutation of $F$ using at most $C(N+q+3)^4$ paid nodes, regardless of $M$. The theorem concerns proof existence and logical cost, \emph{not} an $O(\mathrm{poly}(N+q))$ physical algorithm for reading the original $\pi$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Traverse the \emph{finite} original proof DAG in topological order. For each opaque circuit output $C$ on declared support $S$, $|S|\le3$, evaluate its root on all $2^{|S|}\le8$ assignments. Replace it by the exact truth table, and retain the same authenticated signed source names, paid-node ID, parent references, conservative DEF dependencies and declared local support. Its Boolean function is \emph{identical}. By root-only access and extensional legality, each subsequent node, including any separately charged DEF or explicit affine row, remains legal on the new records. Induction on the topological order constructs an $X_3$ refutation with at most $q$ paid nodes. This is an \emph{existence} proof: arbitrary circuit payloads may be very large, but every one has a finite truth vector.

LSPV-1/375's restricted all-shared-DAG affine-row compiler gives, for a fixed constant $C$ independent of $F$ and any $q$-node $X_3$ proof, an actual signed $E$ refutation with at most $C(N+q+3)^4$ paid nodes, using paid parity-prefix two-input gates and genuine Resolution, rather than exponential false-row expansion. That result already incorporates LSPV-1/359's constant-support $440$-node local compiler. Compose the translations; the presence of $M$ disappears only from the \emph{output proof's size bound}. As $E$ is a subfragment of $X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U$, the reverse weak inequality is immediate. Take the freely selected minimum proof for each UNSAT $F$ to finish. All quantifiers are for one fixed calculus and one fixed polynomial, with no instance-specific program or varying proof system.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Quantified restriction on possible hard-family transfers]
Within this restricted fragment, a superpolynomial worst-case minimum for $E$ is equivalent to one for $X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U$ after explicitly charging the polynomial input-length rescaling in $p$. In particular an $E$ lower bound may transfer to $X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U$ only through this \emph{actual polynomial cost simulation}; Haken's Resolution lower bound cannot be used, since $E$ already has polynomial PHP proofs. This provides no bound for full $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, for which $W_R(F)\le W_{X_{3,\mathrm{root}}^U}(F)$.
\end{corollary}

\section*{Countercontrols: physical work is not semantic quotient cost}
A circuit of $g$ explicitly serialized gates can compute one of only $2^8=256$ functions on three input variables. The resulting canonical truth vector is constant length, regardless of $g$, \emph{but computing it from an arbitrary given serialized proof and checking its legality still requires processing that evidence}. Let authenticated local source be $(x)$ and let proposed root be
\[
C_b(x)=x\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k}b_i
\]
where each $b_i$ is one separately encoded Boolean constant. The all-ones record is legally entailed by $(x)$. Flipping any $b_i$ makes its output identically false, so $(x)\not\models C_b$. Any deterministic exact single-bit-probe validator must read all $k$ source payload bits on the legal all-ones record. Nevertheless, the resulting truth table in the legal case is just $x$ and costs $O(1)$ normalized local clauses. This reconfirms LSPV-4/363 and LSPV-5/315; it does not contradict Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. For any materializing normalizer, $M$ bytes imply at least $\Omega(M)$ acquisition/output-sensitive work and up to $O(M)$ live storage in a direct gate-array implementation; a separate annotated random-access/rescan verifier may trade polynomial time for logarithmic writable space, subject to paid annotation construction. No same-machine FLMV exponent is inferred. In $s'\le\max\{\rho,\eta,\lambda,\lambda s\}$, $\rho$ covers \emph{every} source parsing, payload production and verification phase.

There is also an exact scope counterexample. Two internal gate DAGs can both have root $1=t\vee\neg t$ while one internal $t$ denotes $x$ and the other $\neg x$. A later inference referring directly to the \emph{internal} $t$ distinguishes these records despite identical root truth tables. Therefore any exported internal wire, unless separately charged/authenticated as an original/DEF variable, invalidates the root-only quotient argument. This is a typed limitation, not a claim that such a wire is legal in the unchanged $R$.

\section*{B1/B2/B3 quantifiers and next gap}
For every fixed uniform total correct general SAT decider $A$, including SAT, UNSAT and malformed binary strings, set $T_A^{\rm wc}(N)=\max_{|x|\le N}T_A(x)$. The three distinct \textbf{OPEN} bridges for the unchanged full $R$ are
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
B1: & \forall A\ \exists p_A\ \forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}:\
 W_R(F)\le p_A(|F|+T_A(F)),\\
B2: & \forall A\ \exists p_A\ \forall N:\
 \overline W_R(N)\le p_A(N+T_A^{\rm wc}(N)),\\
B3: & \mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\exists C,k\ \forall N:\

\end{array}
\]
This lemma does not convert a rejecting machine trace into UNSAT evidence; neither soundness reflection for an arbitrary correct $A$ nor superpolynomial $W_R$ is established. A \emph{precise next obligation} is a typed, globally scoped LOCAL-CONVERT signature showing either (i) internal wires are never exported without paid DEF, making this root quotient applicable, or (ii) an explicit legal exported-wire record exhibiting a real counterexample to extension.
