Write $G_F$ for the unsigned variable co-occurrence graph, whose edge $\{i,j\}$ is present when two names occur together in some clause, of either polarity.

\begin{theorem}[Five-vertex clique obstruction, restricted exact law]
Let $F$ be any proper clausewise-monotone three-CNF. If $G_F$ contains a clique of size $r\ge5$, there is no global law on the original source variables whose restriction to \emph{every} clause support is the fixed symmetric $\mu_S$. There is also no positive-semidefinite degree-two moment matrix realizing the one- and two-coordinate moments prescribed by these laws.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $Z_i=2X_i-1\in\{-1,1\}$. The six-row local law has $\mathbb E Z_i=0$, $\mathbb E Z_i^2=1$, and for distinct names co-occurring in a clause, $\mathbb E Z_iZ_j=-1/3$. Every pair among clique $Q$ of size $r$ co-occurs, possibly in different clauses, so its fixed correlation submatrix is
\[
M_Q=I_r-\frac13(J_r-I_r)=\frac43I_r-\frac13J_r.
\]
The all-ones eigenvector has eigenvalue $(4-r)/3<0$. Equivalently a global law or PSD second-moment pseudoexpectation would require
\[
0\le\mathbb E\left[\left(\sum_{i\in Q}Z_i\right)^2\right]
=r-\frac{r(r-1)}3
=\boxed{\frac{r(4-r)}3}<0,
\]
contradiction. The proof does not use SAT or UNSAT labels.
\end{proof}

This rejects only the \emph{particular symmetric fixed-law global extension}. It does not reject an arbitrary alternate local law, a general low-degree SoS relaxation, a SAT assignment or a high-level hierarchy. A satisfying assignment yields its own deterministic marginals, which are not usually the chosen $\mu_S$. The clique condition is necessary, not sufficient when absent. Finding a five-clique is polynomial by trying all five-tuples, and is not a lower bound on SAT time.

\begin{theorem}[All valid six-name M22 formulas are SAT]
