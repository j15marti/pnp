\section*{Prior lower bounds and precise model}
\noindent \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM (Haken 1985):} For sufficiently large $h$, every ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG \emph{Resolution} refutation of the canonical signed unary pigeonhole CNF $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ has at least $2^{ch}$ lines for some $c>0$. This $P_h$ has $h(h+1)$ variables and $m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}=\Theta(h^3)$ explicit source clauses; decimal ASCII DIMACS length $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$. Hence the unit-charged derived-node minimum of ordinary Resolution is at least $2^{ch}-m_h$, \emph{not} a general SAT machine-time lower bound. Cook (1976) proved polynomial Extended Resolution refutations of PHP. Odd expander Tseitin is Resolution-hard but GF(2) has compact authenticated shortcuts in the preselected mixed system. Haken and Tseitin are restricted controls, not carriers of an unrestricted computational result.

The fixed project calculus $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ retains individually signed source leaves at zero charge, paid binary RES, bounded-original-support AND3, GF(2) addition, conservative paid two-input DEF, and source-local LOCAL-CONVERT; a shared derived inference node costs one. The precise gate-payload and named-wire interface of LOCAL-CONVERT remain incompletely fixed in its published grammar (see LSPV-4/363, LSPV-1/376, LSPV-5/328). We therefore \emph{do not} change or silently complete $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.

\section*{All-size multiwire theorem}
Fix a finite acyclic Boolean circuit $C$ with $b$ named external Boolean inputs $x_1,\ldots,x_b$ and constants, and \emph{no other free inputs}. It may contain an arbitrarily large number $g$ of binary AND/OR/XOR/NOT gates and arbitrary internal sharing. Let $O=(w_1,\ldots,w_r)$ denote a list of \emph{observable} internal or output wires. A continuation $K$ is called \emph{extensional at this interface} if, after substituting the Boolean values of these $r$ wires, all its conclusions and legality predicates depend only on the induced Boolean functions of the external inputs and any other independently declared continuation inputs, not on internal gate IDs, circuit source text, hidden wire contents, certificate lengths or particular representations. The continuation may share, reread, and correlate the observable wires arbitrarily.

\begin{theorem}[bounded-common-support, many-observed-wire quotient]\label{th:bank}
For every fixed $b$, every such $C$ and every observation list $O$ of length $r$, there exists a shared canonical Boolean circuit bank $Q_{C,O}$ together with $r$ aliases that has exactly the same joint interface function
\[
 \forall a\in\{0,1\}^{b}:
 (w_1(C,a),\ldots,w_r(C,a))
 =
 (w_1(Q,a),\ldots,w_r(Q,a)).
\]
The bank needs no more than
\[
  \min\{r,2^{2^b}\}\bigl(b+b\,2^b-1\bigr)
\]
binary/NOT gates, plus $r$ output aliases and their identifiers (constants are counted as at most one primitive each). Its structure depends on the \emph{functions observed}, not on $g$ or the original internal names. For $b=3$, at most $256\cdot26=6656$ bank gates and $r$ aliases suffice. Every extensional continuation $K$ has exactly identical Boolean behavior after substitution. Internal wire sharing and correlations are retained at the interface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each observed wire $w$ compute the Boolean function $f_w:\{0,1\}^b\to\{0,1\}$, represented by its $2^b$-bit truth vector. There are at most $2^{2^b}$ distinct vectors. Construct one shared DNF circuit for each distinct vector: share the $b$ input negations; form a minterm of at most $b-1$ binary AND operations for each of the at most $2^b$ satisfying inputs; OR the minterms using at most $2^b-1$ gates. Counting a separate bank per function overcharges the shared negations and gives at most $b+(b-1)2^b+(2^b-1)=b+b2^b-1$ gates per function; true and false use constants. Alias each $w_i$ to the bank root implementing $f_{w_i}$. Thus the whole vector agrees for every $a$, not merely the circuit's final root. Every extensional continuation, by definition, sees the same values simultaneously and cannot distinguish the original from the bank. The construction neither assumes a bound on $g$ nor needs to retain unreachable or unobserved gates. The proof is by exact functional identity on every external assignment.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[restricted logical-DAG proof transfer, explicitly conditional]
Consider any \emph{fixed} calculus completion in which (i) a constant-common-support circuit interface may be replaced by an extensionally equivalent bank, (ii) its paid logical charge is independent of the serialized private gates and observable aliases, (iii) all downstream wire references can be consistently alpha-renamed, and (iv) implication/source-side checks are strictly local and extensional. Every proof in this completion has a proof with the same outer paid-node DAG and with the bank bound in Theorem~\ref{th:bank} at each interface. If its syntax also admits the converse truth-table-to-circuit realization, the circuit and bank presentations have the \emph{same} minimum paid-node cost.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof} Replace one interface at a time, updating only typed references to its $r$ public aliases. By Theorem~\ref{th:bank} all source-local inference predicates and output relations are unchanged, so all outer nodes remain sound and have identical charge. Reverse via DNF circuits. The syntactic hypotheses are necessary and \emph{not yet certified} for the actual published $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ LOCAL-CONVERT grammar. \end{proof}

\section*{Three necessary firewalls}
\noindent\textbf{FALSIFIER F1, root-only quotient fails on observable wires.} A circuit $C_+$ has public root $x$ and exposed internal wire $u=y$; $C_-$ has the same public root $x$ but exposed $u=\neg y$. A downstream continuation reading $u$ distinguishes them, despite their identical root truth tables. Thus LSPV-1/376 and LSPV-5/328 private-root normalization cannot simply erase exposed internal names; the vector-valued quotient here repairs precisely this loss.

\noindent\textbf{FALSIFIER F2, no common bounded support.} A linear-size XOR chain on $t$ \emph{independent} original inputs has a compact circuit, but a full truth vector on its common $t$-variable support requires $2^t$ bits. Theorem~\ref{th:bank}'s constant depends doubly exponentially on $b$ and cannot be called uniform polynomial if $b$ grows. Distinct wires each having at most three supports also do not guarantee that their \emph{union} has bounded support.

\noindent\textbf{FALSIFIER F3, arbitrary semantic support assertions.} Checking that an arbitrary succinct circuit has at most a fixed $k$ essential original inputs is coNP-complete (LSPV-3/382). Theorem~\ref{th:bank} assumes syntactically fixed $b$ inputs or a separately authenticated support certificate; it does not provide a free support oracle.

\noindent\textbf{FALSIFIER F4, physical work is not the paid-DAG optimum.} When given an explicitly serialized circuit of $g$ gates, constructing the vectors by direct evaluation costs $O(2^b(g+r))$ gate operations plus mandatory source bit access. A straightforward topological replay uses $O(g+r2^b)$ working bits, and the final bank's bit output costs at least its serialized length. A lazy representation may reduce materialization but still must authenticate accesses and source provenance. The logical existence of a tiny bank does not imply cheap \emph{conversion of a supplied huge circuit}, a physical time-space lower bound, or an algorithm for finding a shortest refutation. For any solver upper bound also charge parsing, indexing/head movement, failed policies, caches, conversions and all-phase peak space; use $s'\le\max\{\rho,\eta,\lambda,\lambda s\}$ with $\rho$ including every local phase.

\section*{Reproducibility and controls}
\noindent\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL, two independently coded checkers.} The deterministic xorshift32 producer builds typed three-input DAG circuits of 20, 130 and 1030 wires, exposing 5, 17 and 73 wires respectively. Their distinct observed Boolean functions number 5, 11 and 18. It compares all observed wires on each of eight assignments, tests 512 nested AND/OR/XOR suffix continuations per fixture, and independently reconstructs all 256 three-input DNF vectors. Its recorded total is 31,357 assertions and 2,048 DNF mask identities. A separate recursive/memoized evaluator, not a topological replay, checks the same three deterministic fixtures and all ordered pairs of exposed wires as XOR suffixes; it reports 47,963 assertions and 2,048 DNF identities. Both independently count $K_4$ even-charge edge-label solutions as eight and odd-charge as zero. Each rejects five malformed circuit records and tests a corrupted alias; neither benchmark claims a specific native-machine FLMV resource bound. Tests were re-executed from source in a sandbox, and raw records published. These are finite controls, not an asymptotic theorem by experiment. \textbf{Source label controls}: assignment truth vectors include both Boolean outputs at every input, and malformed/wrong-reference controls are rejected.
