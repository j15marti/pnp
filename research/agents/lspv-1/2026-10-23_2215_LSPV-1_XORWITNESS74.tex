\section{Falsifiable mechanisms, model, and corpus}
We test three mechanisms: (i) replacing the three-choice matching-edge sampler by a two-choice exclusive-one sampler may reduce the false-clause probability from $8/27$ to $1/8$; (ii) occurrence-count constraints may prevent eight false-clause cylinders from partitioning the sample cube; (iii) neither a sampler nor a backward-table obstruction need impose work on every direct one-bit SAT decider. Mechanisms (i) and (ii) yield an exact positive theorem, while (iii) remains the all-algorithm firewall.

\section{Source-coherent exact binary kernel}
Two complementary source clauses $(a\vee b\vee u),(a\vee b\vee\neg u)$ contract exactly to $(a\vee b)$. Clause uniqueness and two appearances of each signed literal force pair disjointness (PAIRRIGID72). Let $p$ be the pair count and $\delta=m-2p$ the remaining proper ternaries. The binary contractions project along their degree-two paths/cycles by existential Davis--Putnam elimination, yielding
\[
K_F(U)=T(U)\wedge B(U)\equiv\exists(V\setminus U)F(V),\quad |T|=\delta,\quad |B|=P\le\lfloor3\delta/4\rfloor.
\]
Each projected binary is on distinct boundary variables, different projected binaries are variable-disjoint, and each variable in a projected binary appears in all unmatched ternaries only with polarity opposite to its projected binary polarity.

The last claim follows from the exact original occurrence identities, with $t_x$ counting pair pivots, $b_x^\pm$ the signed contracted binary occurrences and $u_x^\pm$ the unmatched signed occurrences:
\[
t_x+2b_x^++u_x^+=2,\qquad t_x+2b_x^-+u_x^-=2.
\]
If $b_x^+=1$, then $t_x=u_x^+=b_x^-=0$ and $u_x^-=2$; the negative-positive interchange is symmetric. Projection along an internal binary path preserves endpoint polarity. This kernel theorem and its charged $O(m\log(m+2))$ construction are adopted from PAIRPATH73.

\section{New opposite-sign sampler}
For each projected binary $(\ell_a\vee\ell_b)$ independently choose exactly one of $(\ell_a,\ell_b)=(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ equiprobably. Choose an independent unbiased Boolean value for each boundary variable absent from projected binaries. Call the resulting finite nonempty space $\Omega_F$. Every sample satisfies all projected binaries. For residual $C\in T$, let $E_C$ be its set of falsifying samples.

\begin{lemma}[One-eighth cylinders and six conflicting partners; PROVED/EXACT]
Each $E_C$ is empty or fixes exactly three independent unbiased sampler coordinates, hence $\Pr(E_C)=1/8$. A nonempty $E_C$ is disjoint from the falsification event of at most six other residual clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $C$ contains both endpoints of a projected binary, both ternary polarities are opposite to the binary signs and exactly one ternary literal is true in every exclusive-one sample, so $E_C$ is empty. Otherwise $C$ has three distinct source variables in three independent coordinates, whose prescribed false values specify a probability-$1/8$ cylinder.

Two nonempty falsification cylinders are disjoint only if they prescribe opposite values on some shared sampler coordinate. For a binary-edge coordinate, if $C$ uses endpoint $x$, a conflicting clause must use the other endpoint $y$ with opposite coordinate demand; exactly two unmatched $y$ occurrences exist. For an isolated variable, an opposite coordinate demand requires the opposite original signed literal, occurring at most twice in the original exact-$(2,2)$ source. Thus each of $C$'s three coordinates can be responsible for at most two conflicting other clauses, or at most six in total.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[XORWITNESS74; PROVED/EXACT]
Every valid $L_{2,2}$ source with $\delta(F)\le8$ is SAT. Quantitatively,
\[
\Pr_{\omega\sim\Omega_F}[K_F(\omega)=1]\ge
\begin{cases}1-\delta/8,&\delta\le7,\\1/64,&\delta=8.\end{cases}
\]
Since $\delta$ is even, every UNSAT valid source has $\delta\ge10$, therefore $m\ge12$ and $n\ge9$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $\delta\le7$, apply the union bound to at most $\delta$ events of probability $1/8$. For $\delta=8$, if one event is empty, the same argument leaves success at least $1/8$. Otherwise there are eight probability-$1/8$ events. Take any $E_C$. The lemma shows one of the other seven, say $E_D$, is not disjoint from it. Their joint demands fix at most six independent coordinates, so $\Pr(E_C\cap E_D)\ge1/64$. Group this pair before using a union bound on the other six:
\[
\Pr(\cup_{C\in T}E_C)
\le\sum_{C\in T}\Pr(E_C)-\Pr(E_C\cap E_D)
\le1-1/64.
\]
A surviving sampled kernel assignment extends to a source witness by exact projection. Finally $m\equiv0\pmod4$ makes $\delta=m-2p$ even.
\end{proof}
A positive decision uses source validation, pair detection and path normalization in $O(m\log(m+2))$ word operations. Optional witness production uses the previously established at-most-$2^\delta\le256$ source-coherent SCC branches, reverse reconstruction and full source replay; peak memory is $O(n+m)$ words. This strengthens the previous PAIRPATH73 sufficient test $\delta+2P<8$, but is not an all-input polynomial SAT algorithm.

\section{Independently defined amortization and universal firewall}

\[
