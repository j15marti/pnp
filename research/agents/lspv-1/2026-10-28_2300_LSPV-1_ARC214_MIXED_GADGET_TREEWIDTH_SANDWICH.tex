\section{BUILD: the exact treewidth sandwich}
For a proper signed ternary clause $C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$ on three distinct variables $a,b,c$, define the ARC213 mixed gadget
\[
 G_C=(\ell_1\vee y_1)\wedge(\ell_2\vee y_2)\wedge(\ell_3\vee y_3)
 \wedge(y_1\oplus y_2\oplus y_3=0),
\]
where $y_1,y_2,y_3$ are private to that clause. For $F=\bigwedge_C C$, let $G_F=\bigwedge_C G_C$. The \emph{primal constraint graph} $P(F)$ has one vertex per source variable and an edge between variables co-occurring in a clause. $P(G_F)$ includes both binary-clause edges $\{a,y_1\}$, $\{b,y_2\}$, $\{c,y_3\}$ and the three edges among $y_1,y_2,y_3$ demanded by the \emph{ternary parity constraint}. Omitting parity edges would define a different graph and invalidate the theorem. Isolated original names, if any, are retained in both graphs. Treewidth is the minimum over ordinary tree decompositions with connected vertex-bag supports and a bag containing each edge.

\begin{lemma}[Local exact projection, ARC213]
For every valuation $x$ of source variables, $F(x)=1$ if and only if there is a valuation $y$ of all fresh gadget variables with $G_F(x,y)=1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix one clause. If all three literals are false, its binary constraints force $y_1=y_2=y_3=1$, violating even parity. If one literal is true, set the other two $y$ bits to 1 and choose the free bit to make total parity even; the remaining binary clauses hold. Separate clauses have disjoint fresh variables, so the existential conditions distribute over their conjunction.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact ARC214 treewidth sandwich]
For every proper signed 3CNF $F$ with $n$ variables and $m$ clauses,
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{tw}(P(F))\ \le\ \operatorname{tw}(P(G_F))\ \le\ \max\{\operatorname{tw}(P(F)),3\}.}
\]
Moreover, the upper inequality is witnessed by a uniform algorithm that takes \emph{any supplied valid tree decomposition} of $P(F)$ of width $w$ and physically builds and validates a decomposition of $P(G_F)$ with width at most $\max\{w,3\}$, adding exactly three bags per source clause. Consequently, whenever $\operatorname{tw}(P(F))\ge3$ the two optimal primal treewidths are \emph{equal}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Lower inequality.} In $P(G_F)$, the private auxiliary vertex $y_i$ is adjacent to the corresponding original variable of its clause. Contract all $3m$ pairwise distinct edges $\{a,y_1\}$, $\{b,y_2\}$, $\{c,y_3\}$ gadget by gadget. After contracting one clause's three edges, its parity triangle creates exactly the three source primal edges $\{a,b\}$, $\{a,c\}$, $\{b,c\}$. The private-gadget disjointness prevents identifications of distinct original variables. After all contractions the resulting simple graph is exactly $P(F)$, including isolated source names. Treewidth cannot increase under edge contraction or vertex/edge deletion, proving the lower inequality. There is no algorithmic lower bound claimed here.

\emph{Upper inequality.} Take any valid decomposition $\mathcal T$ of $P(F)$ with bags $D$ of size at most $w+1$. For each clause $C$ on $a,b,c$, those three vertices form a triangle in $P(F)$, hence the connected subtrees of bags containing each original variable intersect pairwise. The Helly property for subtrees of a tree supplies a bag $D_C$ containing all $a,b,c$. Attach a \emph{path} of three new bags to $D_C$:
\[
 B_0=\{a,b,c,y_1\},\qquad
 B_1=\{b,c,y_1,y_2\},\qquad
 B_2=\{c,y_1,y_2,y_3\},
\]
with edges $D_C-B_0-B_1-B_2$. All new edges are covered: $\{a,y_1\}$ by $B_0$, $\{b,y_2\}$ by $B_1$, $\{c,y_3\}$ and the $y$-triangle by $B_2$. All old source edges retain their original bags. Every original vertex's new occurrences attach along a connected path to its old subtree, and each private auxiliary vertex occurs in a contiguous suffix of its unique three-bag path. Thus the running-intersection condition is preserved; each new bag has size four, proving the upper bound. Scanning supplied bags to locate $D_C$, appending and serializing three bags per clause, and independently checking their scopes are polynomial in the \emph{physical} source and decomposition lengths. No optimal decomposition is assumed to be computable for free.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Restricted exact joint compatibility and what it cannot establish]
Given a verified source decomposition of width $w$, the mixed constraints $G_F$ can be decided on both answer classes by one uniform deterministic Boolean-factor dynamic program in
\[
 T,S_{\rm peak}\ \le\ \operatorname{poly}(N+B+M)\,2^{\max(w,3)+1},
\]
where $N$ is original encoded length, $B$ the physical decomposition encoding, and $M$ the physically constructed mixed-gadget encoding. This is the known treewidth dynamic program applied to the explicit lifted decomposition. For $w=O(\log N)$ with a polynomial-size supplied/constructible decomposition, it supplies a restricted polynomial-time answer-preserving constant-YES/NO child. No such $w$ or cheap decomposition is promised on arbitrary SAT inputs. The gadget does not reduce optimal primal width when $w\ge3$ and produces $3m$ new variables, $3m$ binary constraints, and $m$ parity constraints. In particular it is not a single-child $N^\lambda$ compressor.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Treat each constraint as a Boolean table on its at most three scoped bits; the displayed three-bag path already covers its scope. Apply exact conjunct-and-project bag transitions with at most $2^{\max(w,3)+1}$ assignments per bag, charging table materialization, message generation, reuse/backpointers, failed partial assignments, source verification, and full YES/NO output. The root is satisfiable exactly when the original formula is by the local projection lemma. An effective decider can follow the same fixed code on SAT and UNSAT; $A_{\rm SAT}$ and $A_{\rm UNSAT}$ denote restrictions of its traces, not different programs with side hints. The polynomial prefactor includes the whole input/decomposition/gadget serialization and verification.\end{proof}
