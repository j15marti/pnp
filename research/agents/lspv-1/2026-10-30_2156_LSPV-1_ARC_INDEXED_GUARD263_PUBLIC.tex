\section{Source-authenticated regeneration theorem}
Let F be a length-N original CNF, rho a coherent partial assignment, and Pi a fully specified length-P topologically indexed resolution DAG. Leaves are numbered exact original-source clauses or unit assumptions in rho. Internal nodes have ordered earlier parents, exact pivot and canonical resolvent. The final clause is D. Let U be all assumption leaves in the DAG and B_U the disjunction of their opposites.
\begin{theorem}[Charged indexed-regeneration certificate export]
Assume a fixed uniform deterministic transducer G(F,rho,i) supplies any physical bit i of Pi, returning blank outside its declared size, using worst-case time t_G(N,P) and writable space s_G(N,P), including every source access and index operation. There is a deterministic verifier that checks each proof leaf, parent, pivot and exact resolvent and emits B_U OR D, with
\[
T\le(N+P)^{O(1)}(t_G+1),\qquad
S\le s_G+O(\log^2(N+P))
\]
temporary writable bits. All accepted outputs satisfy F entails (B_U OR D). If D is empty then U is a forbidden cube. If U is empty as well, F is UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restart G for each requested indexed proof symbol and charge the complete generation. Validate syntax and increasing parent indices. Compare original clause IDs and actual signed literals by rescanning read-only source. To check a resolvent enumerate the proposed literals and rescan both parents to verify exact membership after pivot deletion, using logarithmic counters and polynomially many source/proof symbol queries. Variable-length records can be found by rescanning the P-bit proof. For each source variable rescan assumption leaves, output its opposite literal if used, and deduplicate by fixed variable order. This uses a polynomial number of paid G invocations and logarithmic (conservatively squared-logarithmic) auxiliary counters. By resolution induction, F AND U entails D. Any model of F falsifying B_U satisfies U and therefore D, so F entails B_U OR D.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Limits.} A physically writable rho, any retained exported clause, and all retained ancestor and generator state must ALSO be charged. An exported tautology is sound but supplies no pruning. Materializing Pi requires its physical bits; generating each index on demand can be much slower and cannot be presumed available. A DELAY-wrapped parent call in G destroys any asserted cheap bound for that strategy. A supplied indexed read-only second input is not a free ordinary source-only SAT certificate. This theorem does not supply a bounded-size proof, universal proof discovery, or an ordinary SAT child shorter than N to the lambda.
\begin{lemma}[Sharp four-clause gate]
Let H consist solely of a subset of the four distinct proper 3-clauses (ell OR +/-y OR +/-z), with distinct variables. H entails ell if and only if all four occur. In that case three resolution steps derive ell; under the assumption not-ell one further resolution yields empty and exports blocker ell.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Setting ell false makes each clause forbid exactly one of the four (y,z) assignments. All four are required to forbid all assignments. Resolve the opposite-z pairs to obtain (ell OR y) and (ell OR not-y), then resolve on y. The necessity is only for this isolated grammar, not an arbitrary larger CNF.
\end{proof}
