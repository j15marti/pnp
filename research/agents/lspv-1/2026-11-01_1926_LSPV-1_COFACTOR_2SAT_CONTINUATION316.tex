\section{Exact quantified theorem}
Fix any valid canonical original $F$, any coherent original-variable pins $p$ excluding variable $x$, and let $H_x$ be all original clauses \emph{not incident on $x$}, simplified under $p$. The two positive $x$ source clauses give x-free remainders $R^+_1,R^+_2$ and the two negative ones give $R^-_1,R^-_2$. An already covered clause contributes $\top$ and is omitted; each remaining remainder is a source-indexed clause of width at most two. Put $A=\bigwedge R^+_i$, $B=\bigwedge R^-_j$. For every complete future assignment $z$ on the remaining original variables,
\[
F(p,z,x=0)=H_x(z)\land A(z),\qquad F(p,z,x=1)=H_x(z)\land B(z).
\]
Write $\mathsf{Same}(F,p,x)$ when these functions agree \emph{pointwise} for all $z$, not merely when both existential SAT decisions have the same label.
\begin{theorem}[Complete four-query source-continuation gate]
If the fully paid source-acquired $H_x$ has residual clause width at most two, pointwise cofactor equivalence is decidable by at most four exact 2SAT queries:
\[
H_x\land A\land\neg R^-_j\quad (j=1,2),\qquad
H_x\land B\land\neg R^+_i\quad (i=1,2).
\]
Every $\neg R$ is at most two unit assumptions. All queries UNSAT is necessary and sufficient for $\mathsf{Same}(F,p,x)$, and the conclusion remains sound under arbitrary subsequent coherent pins of other source variables. A SAT query instead yields a concrete continuation-difference counterassignment. Source grammar, discovery, every failed attempt, clause/bit/index/head reads, 2SAT implication-graph work and proof checking incur polynomial bit work and polynomial live writable space on the recognized subclass. If the width gate fails, return UNRESOLVED, not SAT or UNSAT; a separate exact fallback makes the all-input controller complete without guaranteeing polynomial total work.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cofactors differ exactly when $(H_x\land A\land\neg B)\lor(H_x\land B\land\neg A)$ is satisfiable. De Morgan reduces $\neg B$ and $\neg A$ to disjunctions of at most two negated remainders. Each resulting test is 2CNF, since $H_x$ is 2CNF, $A,B$ consist of width-two clauses, and each negated remainder is a conjunction of unit assumptions. The classical implication-graph SCC algorithm decides each query, and a contradictory SCC provides source-indexed implication paths; a successful query has a complete difference witness for the two cofactors. The test explores every case of the disjunction, so it is necessary and sufficient. Pointwise equality is preserved under arbitrary future restrictions of $z$. Replaying original clauses verifies results; no assignment recovery is assumed for a decision-only solver. \qed
\end{proof}
