\begin{theorem}[Forced source-entailing step with increasing ticket budget]\label{thm:one}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The uncovered source IDs at $p$ are $1,2,4,8,10$, whose residual clauses are
\[

(\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3\vee\neg x_4),\

\[
H_p(z)=(3+z^2)(1+z)^2(1+3z+z^2),
\qquad [z^0]H_p=3,\qquad [z^1]H_p=15.
\]
At $q$, uncovered original IDs are $(4,8,10)$, all binary; two free variables have weight-one sign penalties and no odd binary cycle survives. Therefore
\[

Independent direct enumeration verifies that precisely three assignments satisfy either cube. The $p$ partition has three consistent cycle tickets; the $q$ partition has five tickets of which three are consistent and two are row-refuted. \end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Unbounded one-step amplification and exponential multi-step amplification]\label{thm:product}

\[
\boxed{B_{p_t}=3^t,\qquad
 B_{q_t^{(1)}}=5t\,3^{t-1},\qquad

\]
Thus one forced assignment increases the ticket budget by a factor $5t/3$, unbounded in $t$, and $t$ forced assignments increase it by at least $(5/3)^t$ despite preserving \emph{every} satisfying model. In fact
\[
\frac{B_{q_t}}{B_{p_t}}\sim
\sqrt{\frac{5}{8\pi t}}\left(\frac{3125}{768}\right)^t.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
