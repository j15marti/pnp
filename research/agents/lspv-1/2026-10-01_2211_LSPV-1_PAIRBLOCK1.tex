\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: pair-block certificate.}
Take any ordered legal slice family $S_1,\ldots,S_q\le V=\mathbb F_2^m$. Put
\[
W_i=\sum_{j\le i}S_j,\qquad d_i=\dim W_i-\dim W_{i-1}.
\]
For each $i$, choose vectors
\[
t_{i,1},\ldots,t_{i,d_i}\in S_i
\]
whose cosets form a basis of $(S_i+W_{i-1})/W_{i-1}$. Define
\[
T=\{t_{i,a}:1\le i\le q,\ 1\le a\le d_i\}.
\]
Then $T$ is linearly independent and
\[
\boxed{|T|=\sum_i d_i=\dim W_q.}
\]
Moreover, every two distinct vectors selected in the same block $i$ lie in the same legal slice $S_i$. Hence the PAIR2COVER1 legal co-occurrence graph on $T$ contains the disjoint union of block cliques
\[
K_{d_1}\sqcup K_{d_2}\sqcup\cdots\sqcup K_{d_q},
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{|E|\ge\sum_i\binom{d_i}{2}.}
\]
PAIR2COVER1 immediately yields
\[
\boxed{
\rho(H)\ge
1+\dim W_q+\sum_i\binom{d_i}{2}
=
1+\frac{\dim W_q+\sum_i d_i^2}{2}.
}
\]

\paragraph{Proof of independence.}
Suppose a nontrivial linear relation exists among $T$, and let $i$ be the largest block index having a nonzero coefficient. Reducing the relation modulo $W_{i-1}$ leaves a nontrivial relation among the chosen cosets $t_{i,a}+W_{i-1}$, contradicting their construction as a quotient basis. Thus $T$ is independent. Pair legality follows directly from $t_{i,a},t_{i,b}\in S_i$.
