Fix an encoded language $L\subseteq D\subseteq\Sigma^M$ with $M$ addressed input words and positive per-first-query costs $c_j$. Uniform total randomized algorithms may use arbitrary computation, source rereads, learned clauses, SCC, affine/Horn reasoning, DPLL, restarts, memoization, representation changes, and direct one-bit decision; all internal work is charged in overall machine time. The theorem below counts \emph{only} distinct informative source-word reads, and assumes independent random coins and per-input error at most $\epsilon<1/2$. All parameters are defined from the actual encoded input and opposite-label sources, not measured running time or the desired conclusion. Their values need not be available to the algorithm.

For $x,y\in D$, put $\Delta(x,y)=\{j:x_j\ne y_j\}$ and $\mathcal Y_x=\{y\in D:L(y)\ne L(x)\}$. Define
\[
\rho_c^*(x)=\min_{\substack{z_j\ge0\\
\sum_{j\in\Delta(x,y)}z_j\ge1\ (\forall y\in\mathcal Y_x)}}\sum_j c_j z_j.
\tag{1}
\]
The integer version $\tau_c(x)$ restricts each $z_j\in\{0,1\}$.
\begin{theorem}[Fractional source-obstruction theorem: PROVED/EXACT]
Every bounded-error total adaptive randomized decider $A$ on $D$ satisfies
\[
\boxed{\mathbf E\left[\sum_{j\ {\rm first\ read}}c_j\right]\ge
(1-2\epsilon)\rho_c^*(x).}
\tag{2}
\]
Every deterministic exact decider has source-read cost at least $\tau_c(x)\ge\rho_c^*(x)$. No assumption on a finite solver menu, fixed variable ordering, static circuit representation or emitted witness is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every opposite-label $y$, couple runs on $x$ and $y$ with identical random tapes. Up until a source address in $\Delta(x,y)$ is read, the complete trajectories and outputs agree. Correctness on both inputs implies that the run on $x$ hits $\Delta(x,y)$ with probability at least $1-2\epsilon$. Let $p_j$ be its probability of visiting word $j$ at least once. The union bound gives $\sum_{j\in\Delta(x,y)}p_j\ge1-2\epsilon$. Thus $z_j=p_j/(1-2\epsilon)$ is feasible in (1), and linearity of expectation proves (2). The fixed deterministic transcript must visit some differing word for every $y$, proving the integer bound.
\end{proof}
A conditional fractional cover after any transcript $\sigma$ uses only opposite-label sources compatible with previously observed responses. If a new word $j$ is queried then
\[
0\le\rho_c^*(x\mid\sigma)-\rho_c^*(x\mid\sigma j)\le c_j.
\tag{3}
\]
Deleting constraints cannot raise the optimum; adding $z_j=1$ to any post-query cover covers all deleted constraints. This is a telescoping potential, not an additive lower bound across successive states. After complete source acquisition it is zero. The parameter therefore has an \emph{input-information ceiling}, and gives no post-acquisition computational lower bound.

\section{Projective-plane source family, exact representation and SAT labels}
Let $p$ be prime and $q=p^2+p+1$. Points of $\operatorname{PG}(2,p)$ are normalized nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_p^3$ modulo nonzero scaling. Nonzero covectors similarly define $q$ lines. Each line has $p+1$ points, each point belongs to $p+1$ lines, and any two distinct lines intersect at exactly one point. A canonical ordering sorts normalized triples. For each point $i$, use Boolean $x_i$ and orientation $b_i\in\{0,1\}$. Introduce $u_i,v_i$, and the four proper three-literal clauses
\[
(\ell_i\vee u_i\vee v_i)\wedge(\ell_i\vee u_i\vee\neg v_i)
\wedge(\ell_i\vee\neg u_i\vee v_i)
\wedge(\ell_i\vee\neg u_i\vee\neg v_i),\qquad
\ell_i=\begin{cases}x_i&b_i=1\\\neg x_i&b_i=0.\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]
These four clauses require $\ell_i$ to be TRUE. For each line $R$, add
$\bigvee_{i\in R}\neg x_i$. Encode a clause of width $p+1$ by the standard proper ternary chain, with $p-2$ fresh private variables and $p-1$ clauses. Use fresh identities and fixed ordering; no shared $x_i$ gets duplicated as an independent Boolean value. The resulting proper unique 3CNF $F_{p,b}$ has
\[
n=q(p+1),\quad m=q(p+3),\quad
w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil,\quad N=(4m+2)w.
\tag{5}
\]
Different orientations retain exactly the same variable IDs, unsigned supports, word counts, metadata and bit lengths. A flip of $b_i$ changes exactly four signed literal words for its $x_i$, disjoint from alterations for distinct points.
\begin{lemma}[Exact two-label statement: PROVED/EXACT]
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{SAT}(F_{p,b})\iff
\text{no projective line has all its point orientations equal to one}.}
\tag{6}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The four forcing clauses are equivalent to the required unit $\ell_i$, under existential elimination of $u_i,v_i$. A private ternary chain is satisfiable precisely when its original long line clause is. Substitution $x_i=b_i$ yields (6). One explicit witness sets $u_i=v_i=0$ and uses prefix-carry assignments to the private chain variables. For an all-one line, its line clause fails and the chain is UNSAT; this conclusion is assignment-preserving for original variables. Independent source replay confirms witnesses in the computational audit.
\end{proof}
The \emph{full} proper-3CNF language is NP-complete, but this source family has a polynomial-time recognition and decision procedure. It is \emph{not} proper signed exact-$(2,2)$, since its variables do not satisfy the twice-each-polarity budget. Any transfer to that fixed grammar is OPEN.

\section{Fractional bound strictly beyond disjoint sensitivity}
Compare the all-zero SAT source $x=F_{p,0}$ with valid UNSAT neighbors obtained by setting precisely the $p+1$ orientations on a projective line $R$ to one. Because each pair of projective lines shares one point, any two opposite-label source differences within the full orientation promise family have overlapping changed-word supports. Thus the \emph{promise-family} disjoint block sensitivity is exactly $1$; no such assertion is made for arbitrary full-language opposite-label 3CNFs.
Aggregating the fractional weight across the four sign words for each point, a feasible cover must obey
$\sum_{i\in R}z_i\ge1$ for every line. Summing all $q$ line inequalities gives
$(p+1)\sum_i z_i\ge q$. The uniform solution $z_i=1/(p+1)$ is feasible, so its exact fractional value is $q/(p+1)$. An integer hitting set of at most $p$ points covers at most $p(p+1)<q$ lines, while all $p+1$ points of any one line intersect every line. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\beta^{\mathrm{promise}}=1,\qquad
\rho^{*,\mathrm{promise}}=\frac{q}{p+1}
=p+\frac1{p+1},\qquad
\tau^{\mathrm{promise}}=p+1.}
\tag{7}
\]
These values are valid \emph{lower bounds} for the corresponding full-language parameters, not exact equalities there. Every total full-language randomized decider obeys the explicit line-neighbor lower bound
\[
\boxed{\mathbf E Q_A(F_{p,0})\ge(1-2\epsilon)\frac{p^2+p+1}{p+1},}
\tag{8}
\]
and every deterministic exact decider reads at least $p+1$ differing-source sign fields (one source word suffices to observe each field). Since $N=\Theta(p^3\log p)$, the source bound is only
$\Omega((N/\log N)^{1/3})$ and cannot separate P from NP.

\section{Adaptive two-label switching, resources and Circuit-SAT}
A total exact hybrid first parses and validates the canonical source grammar, reconstructs $p$ and the projective incidence relation with all parameter discovery charged, and then solves the recognized family by checking whether any line has every observed orientation equal to one. SAT requires one source-verified assignment, while UNSAT needs only one line implication; otherwise fall back to full exact exhaustive SAT search on the original source. The recognized family admits $O(q^2+m\log(m+2))$ word time and $O(n+m)$ live words. The exponentially slow fallback remains legal and openly charged.
In a \emph{promise-only} diagnostic, first inspect a predetermined line. If all its $p+1$ fields are zero, projective intersection proves SAT immediately. Otherwise switch to a full scan and search for an all-one line, returning UNSAT if found and otherwise SAT. No true label was supplied: the rule is chosen from observed source bits. The diagnostic costs $p+1$ orientation reads on the all-zero SAT source and up to $q$ on UNSAT line-flip sources; it does not bypass the source validation cost of the full-language total solver.
The tested adaptive state is $(\text{source grammar},\text{known sign fields},p,q,\text{cached fields},\text{current rule},\text{remaining budget})$. At $S_0$ only source length is known; canonical validation and incidence construction are charged. At $S_1$ observed sign fields select line-probe or complete-scan continuation; at $S_2^+$ a source-checked witness is reconstructed and replayed; at $S_2^-$ a certified all-one line yields UNSAT. Time pays for policy evaluation, failed recognition, source reads, switching, reconstruction, replay and output; peak space includes source cache and all simultaneously live state.
For proper 3CNF with $n$ source variables and $m$ clauses, a shared-NOT1/OR2/AND2 assignment-evaluation circuit has $G=n+3m-1$ gates and $W=n+6m-2$ gate-input incidences in this explicit basis. Backwards from output TRUE every clause OR must have a true literal, subject to global source-coherence. The source recognizer does not build an all-context backward relation or independent transversal. These circuit counts are not SAT-decision-circuit lower bounds.
