\section{Native resource ledger and control frontiers}
Let $N$ be the full \emph{physical source bit length}, including variable roster, source identifiers, clause signs, XOR right-hand sides, and encoding. Let $n\le N$ be the variable count. For a fixed machine model $\mathcal M$, let $T_{\mathcal M}(F)$ count every step on \emph{one execution} and let $S_{\mathcal M}(F)$ count its simultaneously live peak footprint. Define
\[
 t_{\mathcal M}(F)=\log_N(\max\{2,T_{\mathcal M}(F)\}),\qquad
 s_{\mathcal M}(F)=\log_N(\max\{2,S_{\mathcal M}(F)\}).
\]
No independent worst-case maximum is substituted for a same-instance pair. A RAM count is not a one-tape, two-tape, or universal-machine count. The native accounting includes parsing, failed separators, all speculative 2SAT calls, provenance tests, compatibility joins, cached witnesses, reconstruction, rejected strategies, and full output.

\subsection{Static signed exact-(2,2) coverage is only a structural proxy}
Under the original \emph{specified static} global boundary and disjoint-private-module assumptions, and \emph{only} those assumptions,
\[
 n\le3b+12bq,\qquad
 q\ge\max\{0,\frac{n}{12b}-\frac14\},\qquad b>0.
\]
Its illustrative enumeration and explicit-table proxy exponents are $E_T=b+q$ and $E_S=b$; no algorithmic lower bound is implied. In the active continuous branch $b<n/3$,
\[
 E_T(b)=b+\frac{n}{12b}-\frac14,\qquad
 E_T'(b)=1-\frac{n}{12b^2},\quad E_T''(b)=\frac{n}{6b^3}>0,
\]
so $b^*=\sqrt{n/12}$ and $E_T(b^*)=\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$. The continuous proxy tangent slope in the $(E_S,E_T)$ plane equals $1-n/(12b^2)$; it approaches $-\infty$ as $b\to0^+$ (at fixed $n$) and approaches $1$ as $b$ increases within the active branch, with a kink at $b=n/3$. For canonical $N=\Theta(n\log n)$, the proxy normalized exponent is
\[
 t_{\mathrm{proxy}}=c_T+\frac{E_T\ln2}{\ln N},\qquad
 s_{\mathrm{proxy}}=c_S+\frac{b\ln2}{\ln N},
\]
where $c_T,c_S$ denote the \emph{unquantified} polynomial prefactors; neither is a measured native exponent. At $n=1200$, exact integer relaxation gives $E_T=20$ at $(b,q)=(9,11),(10,10),(11,9)$. Of these, only $(9,11)$ is nondominated in explicit table space. The earliest proxy equality $E_T=E_S$ occurs at $(b,q)=(400,0)$. These points are \emph{permitted by the necessary inequality}, not proved realizable. The normalized optimal enumeration overhead $\Theta(\sqrt n/\log n)$ diverges, but only for this proxy, not for SAT in general.

\subsection{Competing algorithmic upper envelopes}
Given a verified width-$w$ primal tree decomposition, generic Boolean DP may retain $O(N2^{w+1})$ table cells and use $O(\operatorname{poly}(N)2^{w+1})$ operations, with decomposition discovery $D_w$ charged. Those are attainable \emph{algorithmic upper bounds}; small width is not a necessary condition for polynomial SAT. Given a verified mixed 2CNF/affine module tree with \emph{full} interfaces $b_u$ and local codimensions $e_u$, the known fixed-codimension solver supplies the upper envelope
\[
 T\le D+\operatorname{poly}(N)\sum_u 2^{b_u}N_u^{O(e_u)},
\]
with peak storage charged for materialized tables, source data and local workspace. Neither expression is a universal lower bound. A state-dependent portfolio executing bounded attempts $A_j$ has
\[
 T_{\mathrm{controller}}(F)\le D_{\mathrm{select}}(F)+\sum_{j\text{ attempted}}B_j(F)
   +T_{\mathrm{chosen}}(F)+T_{\mathrm{verify}}(F),
\]
where a correct return on an input is not guaranteed merely by a heuristic success rate. The controller's peak space is the actual simultaneous live cache plus active solver space. The lower Pareto envelope across hypothetical algorithms is not established by these inequalities.

\section{Constructive theorem: charged adhesion-two rank-one compiler}
The typed source grammar consists of indexed signed unary/binary clauses and indexed XOR equations; rows may have any arity. Its physical encoding records every variable and source index. Rows of arity at most two are compiled \emph{exactly} into 2CNF, preserving original source ownership and byte offsets. An empty XOR row with right side one is an empty clause; an XOR of one variable is one unit clause; an XOR of two variables is equivalent to two binary clauses. We call rows of arity at least three \emph{genuine affine rows}.

\begin{lemma}[At most two bits is the exact bijunctive closure threshold]\label{lem:2cnf}
Every $R\subseteq\{0,1\}^S$ with $|S|\le2$ has an exact 2CNF representation of at most $2^{|S|}\le4$ clauses. The statement is false at $|S|=3$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}For each excluded assignment $\alpha\notin R$, add the single clause $\bigvee_{v\in S}(v\text{ if }\alpha_v=0\text{ else }\neg v)$. This clause excludes exactly $\alpha$; their conjunction defines $R$ exactly. For three variables, $R=\{001,010,100,111\}$ is odd parity. Its first three listed members have coordinatewise majority $000\notin R$, whereas every 2CNF relation is closed under majority.\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Separator graph compiler]\label{lem:split}
There is a deterministic polynomial-time procedure which, for every canonical typed source, produces a tree of terminal bags and source assignments such that: every original source has exactly one owner; every source scope is contained in its owner bag; every variable's bag occurrences form a connected subtree; and adjacent bags overlap in at most two variables. The procedure performs no SAT calls and may yield bags whose genuine affine-row rank is arbitrarily large.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Form the primal graph using clique edges on every original scope. On each connected component enumerate all candidate separating vertex sets of size at most two. If removing a set $S$ separates the graph, choose the lexicographically first, partition the components into two nonempty sides, and recursively process $S\cup A$ and $S\cup B$. Include a \emph{virtual clique edge} on $S$ in both recursive graph skeletons (not in the Boolean source). Each original constraint scope lies entirely on one side: otherwise its primal clique would have an edge between $A$ and $B$. A constraint supported entirely on $S$ is deterministically assigned to one side. At recombination choose a leaf bag containing $S$ on each side (the virtual clique guarantees this for $|S|=2$), and join those bags. This establishes exact source ownership and running intersection inductively.

All candidate cuts are enumerated in $O(N^2)$ choices per subproblem and connectivity and source-scope tests use polynomial work. A split makes both child vertex sets strictly smaller. A polynomial node bound follows from $\sum(|V_{\text{active}}|-2)$ across connected nontrivial subproblems: a two-separator split conserves this nonnegative potential and has children of size at least three, while a one-separator split reduces it by one and creates at most two size-two leaves. Initial disconnected components create at most $n$ roots. Consequently there are $O(n)$ terminal bags and splits, and the total compilation and validation is polynomial. The exhaustive implementation deliberately uses no claimed linear-time Hopcroft--Tarjan subroutine.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform restricted SAT/UNSAT decision with paid discovery]\label{th:main}
Run the deterministic compiler of Lemma~\ref{lem:split} on an arbitrary typed source $F$. If the coefficient rank of the genuine affine rows owned by \emph{every terminal bag} is at most one, a single deterministic algorithm decides $F$ exactly in polynomial physical input length, on both SAT and UNSAT inputs, without receiving an answer-class bit. If any terminal bag has rank at least two, this implementation may return \texttt{UNKNOWN}. All original sources, separator searches, failed tests, exact message constructions, local solve calls, and reconstruction are charged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root the verified decomposition. Let $S_u$ be a child's intersection with its parent; then $|S_u|\le2$. Compute by postorder the exact subtree-extension table $R_u\subseteq\{0,1\}^{S_u}$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:2cnf}, compile each child $R_v$ into at most four 2CNF clauses over $S_v$, including an empty clause when $R_v=\varnothing$. For each of at most four assignments to $S_u$, pin those variables in the local source and run an exact 2CNF-plus-one-affine-row algorithm. Its polynomiality is the known $e=1$ case of \cite{AG2021}; the accompanying executable independently implements the meet-minimal-generator method audited in ARC227. Pinning variables does not increase the rank of residual original affine equations. Store whether the pinned local source has a model, and one model when it does. The child's tables are exact by induction, and running intersection says all child subtrees are mutually independent conditional on the parent's variables. Hence each computed $R_u$ is exact. At the root, the empty assignment is feasible exactly when $F$ is satisfiable. Reconstruct an original model by descending through compatible stored witnesses and check every original indexed source byte. Otherwise the exhaustive, deterministic table computation constitutes an exactly replayable infeasibility transcript (not an ordinary-resolution proof).

The separator compiler is polynomial in $N$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:split}. There are $O(N)$ bags, at most four pinned calls per bag, and the $e=1$ local algorithm uses at most $O(v^2)$ ordinary 2SAT calls on a $v$-variable bag, each polynomial in the complete local source and child-message input. This proves polynomial total work including unsuccessful tests, discovery and rechecking. A deliberately loose conservative word-RAM accounting for the explicit straightforward implementation is $T_{\rm RAM}=O(N^6\operatorname{polylog}N)$ and $S_{\rm RAM}=O(N^3\operatorname{polylog}N)$, including recursion copies and temporary adjacency; a tighter implementation is possible but not claimed. Numerical machine exponents are not transported to a fixed multitape, single tape, or universal interpreter without a separate charged simulation.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exact bag occurrence and witness amortization]\label{prop:bags}
For any verified decomposition tree with $k$ bags $V_u$, full edge separators $S_{uv}=V_u\cap V_v$, and $n$ distinct variables,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{u}|V_u|=n+\sum_{uv\in E(\mathcal T)}|S_{uv}|.}
\]
For the algorithm in Theorem~\ref{th:main}, at most four witnesses are cached per bag, so the number of stored witness \emph{bits} is at most $4(n+2(k-1))$. This does not bound its total peak workspace.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}For each variable $x$, the bags containing $x$ form a connected subtree. If $x$ occurs in $c_x$ bags it belongs to precisely $c_x-1$ tree edges. Sum $c_x=1+(c_x-1)$ over variables. Each cached witness on a bag uses at most $|V_u|$ bits, and at most $2^{|S_u|}\le4$ witnesses are retained.\end{proof}

\section{Hereditary scope and a discovered high-width family}
An \emph{annotated} class consisting of typed sources with a verified adhesion-two decomposition whose every bag has genuine affine rank at most one is closed under deletion of whole source constraints \emph{when its certificate is retained and updated}. Rank can only decrease and each source stays owned by the same bag. Parsing and validating the supplied certificate are charged polynomially, not free advice. The unannotated greedy recognition class of Theorem~\ref{th:main} is a different matter: we have \emph{not} proved that an arbitrary deletion preserves recognition by the same greedy policy. Early finite testing found a counterexample if unary XOR rows were not first absorbed into 2CNF; the repaired program did not fail on 890 sampled deletion descendants, but this is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not a hereditary proof.

Define $F_{q,k}$ as a chain of $k$ dense 2CNF cliques, each with $q$ private variables, adjacent cliques overlapping in exactly two distinct shared variables, and each clique carrying one ternary XOR equation on three private variables. The parity rows are independent and the positive binary clique sources admit all-ones assignment when each XOR right-hand side equals one. The primal graph has clique size at least $q+2$, hence $\tw(F_{q,k})\ge q+1$, while its global affine rank equals $k$. The deterministic pair-separator compiler discovers $k$ rank-one bags and solves it. A hostile UNSAT variant forces an overlap bit in opposite directions using \emph{different owned private-variable binary clauses} on its two sides, so each side separately remains feasible. This forces actual composition and UNSAT message propagation.

For $k=q$,
\[
 n=q^2+2(q-1),\qquad N=\Theta(q^3\log q),\qquad
 D_{\rm brute}=O(q^8\operatorname{polylog}q).
\]
A conservative RAM count for the explicit implementation is $T=O(q^8\operatorname{polylog}q)$ and $S=O(q^4\operatorname{polylog}q)$, which yields restricted attained \emph{upper-bound} normalized exponents $\limsup t\le8/3$ and $\limsup s\le4/3$. These are not demonstrated tight, and source encoding and word/bit conventions are accounted in the polylogarithmic terms. Replacing brute discovery with a correctly integrated Hopcroft--Tarjan/SPQR implementation is an implementation target, not a claimed completed source-native solver. The structural $n\le3b+12bq$ inequality applies to a \emph{different strict signed exact-(2,2)} grammar and cannot be imported to this typed construction.

\section{Independent computational controls}
The executable uses canonical ASCII source encoding with exact source IDs, a variable roster, and recoverable byte offsets. The independently reimplemented one-parity subroutine passed all $3,584$ combinations of 256 three-variable 2CNF subsets, seven nonempty parity supports, and two parities: $1,482$ SAT and $2,102$ UNSAT comparisons, with 735 generated minimal models and no discrepancy. Random small typed instances passed 400 brute-force comparisons, 393 recognized and seven correctly reported UNKNOWN. The $q=4,6,8,10,12,14,16$ clique-chain instances passed for \emph{both} SAT and UNSAT, with largest $q=16$ instance having $n=286$, 48,500 or 48,560 input bytes, 16 bags, maximum bag size 20, global parity rank 16, and peak Python-tracemalloc bytes 4,406,604 on SAT and 4,407,940 on UNSAT. These byte counts are \emph{Python measurements}, not a fixed TM's $S$.

Ten intentionally corrupted source IDs were rejected. A pre-normalization nine-variable deletion test changed a recognized UNSAT parent into an UNKNOWN child; after compiling unary/binary XOR sources to 2CNF the same descendant was recognized SAT. The repaired finite deletion test admitted all 890 sampled descendants of 178 recognized parents. This repairs an observed coverage weakness; no unbounded heredity follows from sampling. Code and raw input-independent seeds are attached to the reproducibility bundle.

\section{Cross-model safeguards and precise remaining gap}
The semantic source grammar includes original source bytes even when a low-arity affine constraint is compiled to 2CNF. The formula encoding is not automatically strict signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF; such a reduction would need to be explicit and all physical size costs tracked. A two-writable-tape machine, a read-only-input multitape machine, a shared single tape, an indexed RAM, and a fixed universal interpreter have different native access and storage charges. The program has a word-RAM polynomial implementation and can be simulated with polynomial overhead by standard fixed-tape machines, but no model-specific numeric crossing lower bounds are inferred. Its SAT and UNSAT traces are restrictions of \emph{one} correct deterministic program; neither receives a free answer bit.
