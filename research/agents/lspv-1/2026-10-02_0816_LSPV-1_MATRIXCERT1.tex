\section*{Exact matrix certificate}
Let the legal slices in one fixed \same\ realization be represented by binary matrices
\[
 X_s\in\Ftwo^{m\times k_s},\qquad S_s=\operatorname{im}X_s\subseteq\Ftwo^m.
\]
For a legal subfamily \(Q=\{s_1,\ldots,s_q\}\), define the horizontal concatenation
\[
 X_Q=[X_{s_1}\mid X_{s_2}\mid\cdots\mid X_{s_q}].
\]

\paragraph{Lemma 1 (concatenation span).}
Exactly,
\[
 \operatorname{im}X_Q=\sum_{s\in Q}S_s,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}X_Q=\dim\!\left(\sum_{s\in Q}S_s\right).
\]
This is immediate because the columns of \(X_Q\) are precisely the union, with multiplicity allowed, of the generating columns of the participating legal slices.

Let \(X_{\rm all}\) concatenate every legal slice in the declared realization and put
\[
 R=\operatorname{rank}X_{\rm all}.
\]
Then the legal slice-cover number has the matrix-native form
\[
 \boxed{\tau_L=\min\{|Q|:\operatorname{rank}X_Q=R\}}.
\]
This is an exact identity, not an algorithmic complexity claim.

\paragraph{Lemma 2 (pivot-block innovations).}
Fix an ordering \(s_1,\ldots,s_q\) and write \(Q_i=\{s_1,\ldots,s_i\}\). Define
\[
 d_i=\operatorname{rank}X_{Q_i}-\operatorname{rank}X_{Q_{i-1}},
 \qquad X_{Q_0}=0.
\]
Gaussian elimination may select exactly \(d_i\) columns from the new block \(X_{s_i}\) whose residue classes are independent modulo \(\operatorname{im}X_{Q_{i-1}}\). Call the corresponding vectors \(T_i\). Then
\[
 T=\bigcup_{i=1}^qT_i
\]
is linearly independent and
\[
 |T|=\sum_i d_i=\operatorname{rank}X_Q.
\]

\paragraph{Lemma 3 (certified legal pair set).}
Every pair of distinct vectors in \(T_i\) lies in the same legal slice \(S_{s_i}\). Hence
\[
 E_{\rm cert}:=\bigcup_i\binom{T_i}{2}
\]
is a certified subset of the legal-slice co-occurrence graph on \(T\), with
\[
 |E_{\rm cert}|=\sum_i\binom{d_i}{2}.
\]
PAIR2COVER1 therefore yields the directly computable exact lower bound
\[
 \boxed{
 \rho(H)\ge
 1+\operatorname{rank}X_Q+
 \sum_i\binom{d_i}{2}.
 }
\]
The full legal co-occurrence graph may contain additional cross-block edges; ignoring them only weakens this lower bound.
