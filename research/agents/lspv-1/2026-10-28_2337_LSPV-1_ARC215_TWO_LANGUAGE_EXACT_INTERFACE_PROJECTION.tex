\section{PROPOSE: one missing lemma and four falsifiable mechanisms}
The missing constructive lemma is a \emph{uniform, total, all-input, answer-preserving, polynomially charged and repeatedly closed} single-child map whose physical encoded length shrinks by a fixed power (or whose polynomial-value rank decreases with polynomial complete states). The following mechanisms were fixed before the targeted source search.
\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{M1, 2CNF private projection.} Existentially removing variables used only by the 2CNF subsystem can be compiled in polynomial time into 2CNF on a shared interface. \textbf{PROVED.}
\item[]\textbf{M2, affine private projection.} Existentially removing variables used only by the XOR subsystem can be compiled in polynomial time into at most $b$ independent GF$(2)$ rows on that same interface. \textbf{PROVED.}
\item[]\textbf{M3, first-step compression versus hereditary closure.} The joint interface has $O(b^2\log(b+2))$ physical bits, potentially power-short for $b\le N^{1/2-\epsilon}$, but a second application may leave it unchanged. \textbf{First part PROVED, universal second-level shrink FALSIFIED for this compiler.}
\item[]\textbf{M4, logarithmic overlap tractability.} A projected mixed instance can be solved by enumerating $2^b$ assignments, with all evaluation costs charged. This is \textbf{PROVED/RESTRICTED}. No general $b=O(\log N)$ guarantee is given.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD I: exact interface projection}
An instance is a pair $F=(C,A)$, where $C$ is a conjunction of clauses of length $0,1,2$ over Boolean variables, and $A$ is a conjunction of equations $\bigoplus_{v\in S_i}v=r_i$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Length-zero clauses/equations are allowed to represent falsity. Let $V_C,V_A$ be the mentioned variable sets and put
\[
 Z=V_C\cap V_A,\qquad X=V_C\setminus Z,\qquad Y=V_A\setminus Z,\qquad b=|Z|.
\]
The three sets are disjoint. Syntactic occurrence tables compute them without knowing whether $F$ is satisfiable. Let $N$ be the complete unpadded physical input length including signs, indices, separators and all right-hand sides.

\begin{lemma}[Paid 2CNF projection, a known Davis--Putnam closure fact]
There is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm constructing $C_Z(Z)$ with at most $O(b^2)$ distinct clauses of width at most two, such that for every assignment $z$,
\[
 C_Z(z)\iff\exists x\in\{0,1\}^{X}\ C(x,z).
\]
Throughout elimination, no more than $O(|V_C|^2)$ distinct width-two-or-shorter clauses need be stored.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a private variable $x$, partition its clauses into those of form $(x\lor P_i)$, those $(\neg x\lor Q_j)$ and all remaining clauses $R$. Existential elimination yields
\[
 \exists x\, C\equiv R\wedge\bigwedge_{i,j}(P_i\lor Q_j).
\]
The residual $P_i,Q_j$ each has length at most one, so every resolvent has length at most two. Drop tautologies and deduplicate after each step; on $v$ remaining variables the number of distinct binary, unary and empty clauses is $O(v^2)$. Eliminate each $x\in X$ and retain only clauses involving $Z$. The identity holds \emph{pointwise on every assignment $z$}, including contradictory unit clauses and empty clauses. Computing all generated pairs and checking clause identities can be done in polynomial time without SAT calls.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Paid affine projection, a known Gaussian-elimination fact]
There is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm constructing $A_Z(Z)$ with either one inconsistent row or at most $b$ independent GF$(2)$ equations, such that for every $z$,
\[
 A_Z(z)\iff\exists y\in\{0,1\}^{Y}\ A(y,z).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a private variable $y$ that occurs in a row. XOR that pivot row into every other row containing $y$, and then delete the pivot row: for any assignment to the remaining variables, the pivot uniquely determines a suitable $y$. Repeat over $Y$. Gaussian elimination on the remaining $Z$ columns detects $0=1$ and retains at most $b$ independent rows. Each operation preserves the exact projection, and all row operations are explicitly charged.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform source-authenticated two-language projection]
For every valid encoded mixed instance $F=(C,A)$, the deterministic constructor
\[
 \mathcal P(F)=(C_Z,A_Z)
\]
produces \emph{exactly one} explicitly written mixed child on at most $b$ locally renumbered variables, satisfies
\[
 \forall z\quad \mathcal P(F)(z)\iff
   \exists x,y\,[C(x,z)\wedge A(y,z)],
\quad\text{and}\quad
 \SAT(F)\iff\SAT(\mathcal P(F)),
\]
and has physical output length $M=O(1+b^2\log(b+2))$. Its total parsing, table construction, unsuccessful resolvent trials, XOR row operations, deduplication, reindexing and output are polynomial in $N$; simultaneous peak memory is polynomial in $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Because $X$ occurs only in $C$ and $Y$ only in $A$, existential quantifiers distribute at every fixed $z$:
\[
 \exists x,y\,[C(x,z)\wedge A(y,z)]
 \iff (\exists x\,C(x,z))\wedge(\exists y\,A(y,z)).
\]
Apply the preceding two constructive lemmas. At most $O(b^2)$ clauses use at most two $O(\log(b+2))$-bit locally numbered variable indices, and at most $b$ independent affine equations each have at most $b$ such indices. The complete explicit binary encoding has the claimed bound. The constructor is one fixed deterministic program for SAT and UNSAT, never receives a free answer-class bit, and never solves the mixed parent. Malformed representations are rejected by a deterministic polynomial parser, outside the declared encoded language.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[First-step power shrink under a genuine syntactic promise]
Fix $\epsilon>0$. On any mixed subfamily with $b\le N^{1/2-\epsilon}$, for every fixed $\lambda$ with $1-2\epsilon<\lambda<1$ and all sufficiently large $N$, the explicitly constructed child satisfies $M\le N^\lambda$. If $b=O(\log N)$, the constructor can also decide the original mixed instance by checking at most $2^b$ boundary assignments against its $C_Z$ and $A_Z$ output, in total deterministic polynomial time.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Insert $b\le N^{1/2-\epsilon}$ into the physical size bound. The logarithm is absorbed by the strict exponent slack. For each $z$, evaluate all $O(b^2)$ constraints directly. The $2^b$ enumeration, including failures and the final output of a fixed YES/NO base formula, is polynomial when $b=O(\log N)$. The decision side does not come for free: an accepting case may be the last candidate, and a rejecting case may require all candidates.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: a hereditary fixed point and the exact missing arrow}
\begin{theorem}[FALSIFIED: repeating this projection necessarily shrinks]
For every $b\ge3$, there are mixed instances $F_{b,t}$ with $b$ shared variables and arbitrarily large additional private-variable padding, whose first projection $K_b=\mathcal P(F_{b,t})$ is strictly shorter, but
\[
 \mathcal P(K_b)=K_b,\qquad |K_b|\longrightarrow\infty\quad(b\to\infty).
\]
Thus no uniform strict-power contraction at \emph{every hereditary level} follows from this componentwise projection procedure, even when the first step power-shrinks its parent. This does not prohibit other algorithms from deciding or compressing $K_b$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On shared variables $z_1,\ldots,z_b$ set
\[
 C_b=\bigwedge_{i=1}^b(z_i\lor z_{i+1}),\quad z_{b+1}=z_1,
 \qquad A_b=\bigwedge_{i=1}^b(z_i=1).
\]
Each variable occurs in \emph{both} components, and neither has a private variable. In the chosen deterministic canonical representation, $\mathcal P(K_b)=K_b$ for $K_b=(C_b,A_b)$: there is nothing to eliminate, and each XOR row and clause is already distinct. For each $z_i$ add private 2CNF variables $u$ with $(z_i\lor u)\wedge(\neg z_i\lor\neg u)$, which existentially projects to TRUE, and private affine variables $v$ with $z_i\oplus v=0$, which also existentially projects to TRUE. Repeating those attachments $t$ times yields $F_{b,t}$, whose full source becomes arbitrarily long as $t$ grows while $\mathcal P(F_{b,t})=K_b$. In fact choosing $t=b^K$ with a sufficiently large fixed integer $K$ makes the first child power-short for any prescribed $0<\lambda<1$ (since $|K_b|=O(b\log b)$ while $|F_{b,t}|=\Omega(bt)$). Under the same canonical serialization, after the first shortening, the next step is a length-preserving fixed point. The family is intentionally easy and SAT; it falsifies only the compiler's hereditary guarantee, not general SAT hardness. The same structural fixed point can be made UNSAT by replacing $A_b$ with the rows $z_i=0$, leaving $C_b$ unchanged.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Connection to the preceding ARC213 gadget.} The ARC213 reduction has $3m$ fresh variables, each shared between its binary and XOR layers. If $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$ on a standard source encoding, the interface $b=3m$ is much too large for the present first-step power-shrink guarantee. In particular the new theorem does not collapse arbitrary 3SAT through ARC213. More generally an arbitrary ordinary-3SAT-to-mixed compiler which always produced $b=O(\log N)$, with polynomial output and total uniform costs, would imply P$=$NP by the restricted algorithm. That compiler remains OPEN; claiming its existence would insert the desired conclusion as a hypothesis.

\section{Separate native resource ledgers and proof DAG}
Let the complete source have $N$ physical bits, not just $n$ logical variables. There are at most $N$ syntactic variable names, and at most $O(N^2)$ distinct binary clauses at each elimination stage. A dense indexed-RAM implementation performs at most $O(N^5)$ resolvent-pair tests plus polynomial matrix operations, using $O(N^2\log(N+2))$ bits of simultaneous workspace; a generous native upper bound is $T_{\rm RAM}=N^{6+o(1)}$, $S_{\rm RAM}=N^{3+o(1)}$. A fixed-$k$ multitape read-only-input implementation with sequential scans of complete tables has conservative \emph{upper} bounds $T_{\rm off}=N^{9+o(1)}$, $S_{\rm off}=N^{3+o(1)}$, with distinct work cells, accesses and output all included. A two-fully-writable-tape machine can emulate that construction without altering its first tape. A single shared read/write tape can simulate it by a complete sweep of $N+S_{\rm off}$ cells per step, obtaining $T_1=N^{12+o(1)}$ and total visited footprint $P_1=N^{3+o(1)}$, including input and output. This verifies only the stated conservative simulation arrow
\[
 t_1\le t_{\rm off}+\max(1,s_{\rm off})=9+3=12,
\]
not any optimal lower bound, same-time square-root-space transport, or claim about indexed-RAM hardness. A fixed UTM pays a constant program prefix and its interpreter overhead polynomially. These deliberately loose upper bounds are for the \emph{projection compiler}, not a SAT solver. If the interface is enumerated, add $2^b\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time, and keep the SAT-side and UNSAT-side maxima as restrictions of the \emph{same fixed program}. All failed branches, parser work, row/selector access, physical output and simultaneous peak tables are charged; no NC or polynomial-depth oracle is presumed free.

The proved, restricted dependency chain is
\[
\text{2CNF projection}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{affine projection}
\;\longrightarrow\; O(b^2\log(b+2))\text{-bit joint summary}.
\]
Its conditional first-step power-shrink edge assumes $b\le N^{1/2-\epsilon}$.
The proposed automatic second-level contraction is \textbf{FALSIFIED} for
this compiler by an unbounded fixed-point family. The all-input,
polynomially discovered interface and alternative repeated successor are
\textbf{OPEN}, as is the polynomial-value descending-rank alternative.
The independent ARC/FLMV separation route still lacks its all-input,
source-authenticated, same-machine strict forbidden-region implication.
No sequential-head numerical exponent is transferred to indexed RAM.
