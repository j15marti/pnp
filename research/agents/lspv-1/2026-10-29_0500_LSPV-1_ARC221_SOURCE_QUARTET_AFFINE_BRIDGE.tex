\begin{abstract}
A single, unconditional deterministic polynomial-time source auditor recognizes
exactly the class of proper 3CNF formulas whose clauses partition into complete
four-clause encodings of ternary parity equations.  It discovers either a full
source-checked satisfying assignment or a complete source-checked inconsistent
GF(2) row combination.  Combining this recognizer with the earlier paid
bounded-width and strong-2CNF-backdoor selector extends a \emph{restricted}
answer-preserving, one-child physical contraction.  An explicit cubic Tseitin
family is connected, proper, has $4$ positive and $4$ negative occurrences per
Boolean variable, and has both large primal treewidth and large syntactic
2CNF-backdoor size.  Yet it is covered by the affine arm.  When the total
vertex charge is odd, \emph{every} refutation consisting solely of XOR sums
of the extracted source equations must use every original equation.  This is
a grammar-specific certificate-support statement, not a running-time lower
bound for SAT.  Arbitrary mixed 2CNF+XOR inputs remain outside the proven
compiler.  \textbf{P versus NP and the general all-input contraction are OPEN.}
\end{abstract}

\section{PROPOSE: missing implication and falsifiable mechanisms}
The current missing lemma is an all-input, uniformly constructible and
hereditarily progressing answer-preserving successor.  The ARC220 selector
recognizes bounded greedy elimination width and bounded strong syntactic
2CNF backdoors, but gives an identity child outside those islands.

\paragraph{M1 (source quartets).} Test whether complete signed ternary
clause quartets can be recognized in polynomial time, with clause offsets and
source indices, and converted \emph{exactly} to a GF(2) linear system.
\emph{Outcome: PROVED/RESTRICTED.}
\paragraph{M2 (certificate acquisition).} Test whether one Gaussian-elimination
run can produce both SAT witnesses and UNSAT refutations authenticated by the
original quartets; check complete proof discovery costs, not just verification.
\emph{Outcome: PROVED/RESTRICTED.}
\paragraph{M3 (hostile old-branch controls).} Construct a connected family
whose treewidth and syntactic 2CNF backdoor both exceed ARC220's $\log N$
threshold, while the affine branch still decides both answers.  Require some
UNSAT instances to demand a \emph{global} affine certificate using linearly
many distinct source constraints.  \emph{Outcome: PROVED/RESTRICTED.}
\paragraph{M4 (unrestricted escape).} Test whether adding the affine branch
makes the portfolio all-input and therefore a P-time SAT decider.
\emph{Outcome: FALSIFIED for this concrete recognizer, by mixed
2CNF+XOR inputs and general non-quartet 3CNFs.  No general impossibility
claim follows.}

\section{BUILD: exact clause-quartet syntax}
For an ordered scope $u<v<w$ and $b\in\{0,1\}$ let
\[
 Q_{u,v,w}^b\ :=\ \bigwedge_{a\in\{0,1\}^3:\ a_1\oplus a_2\oplus a_3\ne b}
\Bigl(\bigvee_{j=1}^3 L_j(a_j)\Bigr),\quad
L_j(0)=x_j,\quad L_j(1)=\neg x_j.
\]
Every conjunct excludes exactly its tuple $a$, so the quartet is true
precisely when $x_u\oplus x_v\oplus x_w=b$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact source-quartet extraction, PROVED/RESTRICTED]
For any canonical proper signed 3CNF $F$ whose full clause multiset
can be partitioned by unsigned three-variable scope into exactly four
\emph{distinct} clauses per scope, and where each foursome equals
$Q_{u,v,w}^0$ or $Q_{u,v,w}^1$, a uniform deterministic polynomial-bit-time
procedure outputs an indexed list $E(F)$ of GF(2) equations, with exactly
identical satisfying assignments on all original variables:
\[
 \forall a\in\{0,1\}^{n},\qquad F(a)=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ E(F)(a)=1.
\]
It returns UNKNOWN rather than guessing if any scope is incomplete, mixed,
duplicated, or contains non-ternary clauses.  Every output row retains its
four clause-source indexes and their byte offsets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The canonical parser reads and validates the entire byte stream, then groups
clauses by sorted unsigned scope.  For each group it compares its signed
clauses to both explicitly enumerated parity quartets, each of constant size.
It accepts only if each source clause is used exactly once.  A single quartet
is false exactly on its four excluded assignment tuples and therefore equals
its claimed affine equation pointwise.  Conjoining over groups proves the
identity.  Source grouping, canonicalization and writing the row and index
lists require at most polynomially many bit operations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructed SAT/UNSAT certificates, PROVED/RESTRICTED]
On the recognized class, deterministic GF(2) elimination returns either an
assignment $a$ satisfying every original source clause or a nonempty list
of equation indexes $I$ such that
\[
 \bigoplus_{i\in I}A_i=0,\qquad
 \bigoplus_{i\in I}b_i=1.
\]
The certificate is checked using the four original clause indexes associated
with each row, and can be discovered, serialized, and checked in deterministic
$N^{O(1)}$ bit time and simultaneous workspace on one fixed program.  A
constant-length YES or NO base formula is then a physical one-child successor
for recognized inputs above a fixed threshold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Augment each row with a unit vector over its original equation indexes.
Every GF(2) row operation also XORs these provenance vectors.  Reduction to
$0=1$ gives a valid combination of source-authenticated rows.  If elimination
is consistent, back substitution produces an assignment and full clause
checking verifies it.  There are at most $N$ equations and $N$ variables,
and rows including provenance have $O(N)$ bits, so ordinary polynomial
bit-time Gaussian elimination, input checks, proof checks, and output writing
suffice.  No SAT oracle or uncharged side-of-answer hint appears.
\end{proof}

\section{An explicit global-proof control with cubic Tseitin equations}
Let $L\ge3$.  For each toroidal grid cell $(r,c)\in(\mathbb Z/L)^2$,
construct four port vertices $(r,c,p)$, $p\in\{N,E,S,W\}$, in a cycle.
Connect its $E$ port to the next cell's $W$ port and its $S$ port to the
next row's $N$ port, with toroidal wraparound.  Denote the resulting
connected cubic graph by $G_L$.  It has
\[
 V=4L^2\ \text{vertices},\qquad E=6L^2\ \text{edges}.
\]
Use one Boolean variable $x_e$ for each graph edge.  Each cubic vertex
$v$ supplies a ternary equation
$\bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v$, encoded by its complete four-clause quartet.
Write $F_L(b)$ for the resulting proper signed 3CNF, with
$n=6L^2$ variables and $16L^2$ clauses.

\begin{theorem}[Global affine support and old-selector evasion]
For every $L\ge3$, $F_L(b)$ is connected and proper, and every variable
has exactly four positive and four negative literal occurrences.  It is
SAT exactly when $\bigoplus_v b_v=0$.  If the total charge is odd, then
\emph{every} inconsistent XOR-of-original-equations certificate contains
\emph{all} $4L^2$ source equations.  Its primal graph has
$\tw\ge L-1$, and every syntactic strong 2CNF backdoor has size at least
$2L^2$.  Thus, for all sufficiently large $L$, both the width and
backdoor tests of ARC220 fail at threshold $\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor$,
while the affine source-quartet arm accepts SAT and UNSAT members.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each graph edge has two endpoints, hence its Boolean variable occurs in
exactly two XOR3 quartets.  Within a quartet, any variable appears twice
positive and twice negative.  The resulting signed incidence is $(4,4)$.
All clauses have three distinct variables; connectedness follows from
connectedness of $G_L$.

The coefficient of $x_{uv}$ in the XOR of equations over a vertex set $U$
is $1$ exactly when $|U\cap\{u,v\}|=1$.  Thus the left side vanishes
iff $U$ is a union of connected components.  Because $G_L$ is connected,
the only combinations yielding zero left side are $U=\varnothing$ and
$U=V(G_L)$.  The all-vertex sum has right side $\bigoplus_v b_v$.
If it is even, the graph incidence system is consistent (choose a spanning
tree and solve edge values successively); if odd, its unique nontrivial
zero-left-side combination is $0=1$, using every equation.

For the backdoor bound, every scope includes four ternary clauses that
survive untouched if the backdoor misses all three variables of that scope.
Any strong syntactic 2CNF backdoor must therefore cover every cubic vertex
by selecting at least one incident graph-edge variable.  Each chosen edge
covers only its two endpoints, giving at least $|V|/2=2L^2$ variables.

For the primal width bound, take the line graph whose vertices are Boolean
edge variables, with adjacency when two graph edges share a cubic vertex.
Within each grid cell, its four cycle-edge variables form a connected
branch set.  Each external graph edge corresponds to a distinct additional
line-graph vertex adjacent to a cycle-edge variable at each endpoint cell.
Contracting these disjoint paths and branch sets yields the $L\times L$
toroidal grid as a minor; it contains the ordinary $L\times L$ grid.
Grid treewidth is at least $L-1$, and treewidth is minor-monotone.
Therefore every elimination ordering, including greedy min-fill, has
induced width at least $L-1$.  Finally, physical canonical $N$ is
$\Theta(L^2\log L)$, making $L-1$ and $2L^2$ both larger than
$\log_2 N$ eventually.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Answer-side input acquisition on the matched promise]
Let $\mathcal P_L$ consist of the zero-charge SAT formula and one odd-charge
UNSAT formula for each possible location of a single flipped charge, with
identical scopes and equal physical group lengths.  Any one correct
uniform deterministic bit-query decision tree restricted to this promise
requires $4L^2$ distinct charge-group probes on the SAT input and
$4L^2$ in the worst case over UNSAT inputs.  This is only an
\emph{input-acquisition} lower bound for the promise, not a RAM
post-acquisition lower bound and not a SAT lower bound.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If a zero-charge execution fails to inspect group $v$, flipping only that
charge gives an indistinguishable UNSAT input.  Thus it must inspect all
$4L^2$ groups.  The UNSAT input with its single flipped group last on the
zero-charge decision path likewise requires that many group inspections.
Each quartet has two positive and two negative occurrences per variable,
so a charge flip changes only bytes \emph{inside} that one equal-length
quartet block; there is no total-length or shifted-suffix side channel.
The same deterministic program decides both labels without an answer hint.
\end{proof}

\section{Charged tribranch selector and formal dependency chain}
One fixed deterministic portfolio first parses the full source, tries the
authenticated affine-quartet recognizer, and, if it returns UNKNOWN, runs
the previously constructed ARC220 min-fill width arm and then its exact
strong syntactic 2CNF backdoor arm, each with $h=\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor$.
If a branch decides, a verified witness or complete authenticated UNSAT
certificate is produced, checked, and the answer is written as one constant
YES or NO base instance.  Each base is recognized as terminal at the
next level.  If all arms fail, the only honest successor is the unchanged
source, labeled UNKNOWN: \emph{no strict contraction is asserted}.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted three-arm closure, not P=NP]
The resulting \emph{one-program} selector terminates in polynomial bit time
on every encoded input.  On the union of the three recognized islands it
has one answer-preserving, constant-sized physical child and directly
solvable bases, including both labels of the family $F_L(b)$.
On other inputs, no answer or contraction guarantee follows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The affine branch has polynomial bit-time source checking, Gaussian
elimination, proof checking, and output.  For the second branch, $9^h$ is
polynomial in $N$; for the third, $3^h$ backdoor discovery and $2^h$
2SAT-leaf enumeration are polynomial, with all failures included.
Branch selection is deterministic, no separate SAT/UNSAT programs are
chosen, and the serialized children are constants.  The counterexample is
any mixed 2CNF+XOR gadget not in the pure quartet language and exceeding
both other structural thresholds, or any unrecognized general 3CNF.
These cannot be declared YES or NO by this selector.  The theorem does
not say that all such inputs \emph{necessarily} evade the older arms;
that remains instance-dependent.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Uniform charged resource ledgers.}
For a fixed read-only-input multitape TM, the clause stream, indexes,
full failed strategy search, all row XORs, provenance vectors, candidate
2SAT branches, verification, certificate output, and base serialization
fit polynomial bit time and work space.  A deterministic single shared
read/write tape counts the $N$ input cells in the footprint and can
simulate the concrete fixed multitape program with a conservative
polynomial (for example quadratic-time) overhead; all head travel and
tape reshuffling are charged.  A fixed universal TM has one fixed
interpreter and fixed program, with the full program interpretation,
input, and output access charged at polynomial overhead.  Indexed RAM
has separate $O(\log N)$-bit addresses, polynomial total word operations,
polynomial traffic, and simultaneous peak memory.  \emph{These are upper
bounds for explicit algorithms, not machine lower bounds.}  The
model-native polynomial exponents are not interchanged.

\paragraph{Five-role dependency chain.}
LSPV-1 checks all-child correctness, certificate acquisition and closing
recursion.  LSPV-2 must test source-preserving circuit summaries on
signed-geometry and linear-frontier counterfamilies.  LSPV-3 must test
adaptive/moving interfaces after a mixture of affine and 2CNF language
constraints, without assuming a pure quartet partition.  LSPV-4 must
preserve SAME-RUN SAT/UNSAT-side and tape/RAM/UTM resource typing.
LSPV-5 must independently referee the exact minor and unique global
affine-dependency proofs, plus root-\texttt{main.tex} governance.
All roles remain on their existing schedules; this report does not create
or modify any task.

\section{OPEN implication and next refutation tests}
An unrestricted all-input affine extractor is impossible to assume: mixed
2CNF+XOR already expresses arbitrary SAT by the earlier ARC213 exact
reduction, so polynomial compatibility decision on all such mixtures
would itself prove $P=NP$.  Equally, recognizing just a source quartet
without constructing the Gaussian certificate is insufficient to cover
arbitrary CNF.  At present there is no polynomial-time deterministic
noncircular transition that guarantees a smaller child or a strictly
smaller \emph{polynomial-valued} rank on every SAT and UNSAT input.

The highest-value next falsifier is a connected proper mixed-affine+
2CNF source with no pure-quartet cover, superlogarithmic primal width
and superlogarithmic strong syntactic 2CNF backdoor, for which source-
authenticated partial affine elimination plus 2SAT must jointly
maintain compact, repeatedly constructible compatibility summaries.
The alternative separation target still requires a single-model,
all-algorithm lower bound and a strict correctly quantified ARC/FLMV
contradiction.  Restricted query, tape-crossing, and syntactic-width
phenomena cannot be imported as unrestricted RAM hardness.
