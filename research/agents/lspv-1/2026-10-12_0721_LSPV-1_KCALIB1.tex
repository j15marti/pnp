\section{Fully charged theorem}
Let a total uniform front end $R$ take arbitrary CNF $x$ of length $N$ to
$F=R(x)\in\Hsigned$ with
\[
 |F|\le N^{\lambda+o(1)},\quad T_R(N)\le N^{u+o(1)},\quad
 S_R(N)\le N^{v+o(1)}. \tag{2}
\]
Here $v$ includes the actual output-access realization: explicit buffering,
random-access generation, rereading, or recomputation is not free.

Fix an exact ordinary-CNF SAT decider $A$ and valid finite bounds
\[
 T_A(L)\le L^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(L)\le L^{D+o(1)}. \tag{3}
\]
A compiler $C_{A,K,D}$ on a clean-core input of length $M$ streams at most
$M^{\eta+o(1)}$ ordinary CNF children of length at most
$M^{\beta+o(1)}$, with
\[
 F\in\SAT\Longleftrightarrow\bigvee_{Q\in C_{A,K,D}(F)}Q\in\SAT, \tag{4}
\]
and charged local costs $M^{r+o(1)}$ time and $M^{p+o(1)}$ space. Any call to
$A$ and any virtual-input access is included in those costs.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{K-CALIBRATED-CLEANCORE-LANDING1}]
The composition that runs $R$, $C_{A,K,D}$, then $A$ sequentially on the
children is one uniform exact SAT decider with
\[
 c=\max\{u,\lambda r,\lambda(\eta+\beta K)\},\qquad
 \rho=\max\{v,\lambda p,\lambda\beta D\}. \tag{5}
\]
If $c+\rho<\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ and the encoding/model match FLMV, the
premises are inconsistent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The front end contributes $(u,v)$. Scaling compiler costs from $M$ to $N$
gives $(\lambda r,\lambda p)$. At most $M^\eta$ sequential calls on inputs of
length $M^\beta$ give time $\lambda(\eta+\beta K)$ and peak space
$\lambda\beta D$. Exactness is (2),(4). The finite code of
$R,C_{A,K,D},A,K,D$ is one uniform program. Apply the model-matched FLMV
endpoint.
\end{proof}

\section{Quantifier correction}
Under the contradiction premise,
\[
 \exists A\ \exists K,D<\infty\quad\text{such that (3) holds}. \tag{6}
\]
Once these witnesses are fixed, a proof may instantiate one finite compiler.
The sufficient prefix is therefore
\[
 \forall A\ \forall(K,D\text{ valid for }A)\ \exists C_{A,K,D}:
 c+\rho<\varphi, \tag{7}
\]
not the stronger
\[
 \exists C,r,p,\eta,\beta\ \forall A,K,D:\ c+\rho<\varphi. \tag{8}
\]
Uniformity of the final machine does not require common numerical parameters
over all different program texts.

\begin{corollary}
Every fixed $\beta>0$ is defeated by delay wrappers because
$c\ge\lambda\beta K$. After finite $K,D$ are fixed, the illustrative
single-child ledger
\[
 u=\lambda,\ v=0,\ r=1,\ p=\beta,\ \eta=0
\]
lands below FLMV whenever $\lambda<\varphi$ and
\[
 0<\beta<\min\left\{\frac1K,
 \frac{\varphi-\lambda}{\lambda\max\{1,D\}}\right\}. \tag{9}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Equation (9) gives $c=\lambda$ and
$\rho<\varphi-\lambda$. The fixed-$\beta$ failure follows by increasing $K$.
\end{proof}

This is accounting, not a compressor. An all-input compiler producing one
$M^\beta$ SAT-equivalent child is separation-strength. A compiler that first
runs $A$ and emits a fixed YES/NO formula must charge $(K,D)$ into $(r,p)$.
Thus \texttt{SOLVER-INDEPENDENT-PARAMETERS-NECESSARY1} is \RETIRED, while
\texttt{K-CALIBRATED-ALL-INPUT-COMPRESSOR1} remains \OPEN.

input-dependent advice would not. Schur squares, quadratic-relation ranks,
Hilbert functions, syzygies, and collision entropy are \textbf{FAILED

and special-soundness extraction are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; no
unconditional SAT compiler satisfying (7) is known. Hash/random-oracle
binding is inadmissible, and hidden algebraic structure may aid an algorithm.
