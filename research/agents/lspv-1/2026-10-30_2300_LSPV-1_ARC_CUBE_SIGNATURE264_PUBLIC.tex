\section*{Exact family and same-source both-label theorem}
Fix $n\geq5$, variables $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$, and
$\mathcal T=\binom{[n]}3$ of size $T=\binom n3$.
Let $H_8(u,v,w)$ be all eight signed three-variable proper clauses.
Let $H_7$ omit exactly $(u\vee\neg v\vee w)$; it has unique
model $(u,v,w)=(0,1,0)$. Both complete all-zero and all-one
assignments falsify at least one original $H_7$ clause.
For $S\subseteq\mathcal T$ of size $m$, set
\[
F_S=H_7(u,v,w)\land
\bigwedge_{\tau\in S}\left(\bigvee_{i\in\tau}x_i\right).
\]
Every parent $F_S$ is SAT, witnessed by $X=1^n$ and $u v w=010$.
For $\tau\in\mathcal T$, define the coherent partial assignment
$\rho_\tau$ setting precisely the three $x$ variables of $\tau$ to zero.
\begin{theorem}[Exact three-pin signature]
For every $S$ and $\tau$,
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(F_S\!\upharpoonright_{\rho_\tau})
\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \tau\notin S.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When $\tau\in S$, the original positive clause indexed by $\tau$
is falsified, providing a source-indexed UNSAT leaf.
When $\tau\notin S$, set every unpinned $x$ to one and $uvw=010$.
Each original positive clause differs from $\tau$ and thus contains
an unpinned true variable. Every original clause is checked under
this coherent complete witness. No occurrence is separately assigned.
\end{proof}
\section*{Source-free artifact lower bound}
A source-free artifact $D(F_S)$ is a finite bitstring containing
\emph{all} source-dependent bits given to a fixed decoder $V$.
The decoder receives $(n,m,\tau,D)$ and answers the exact three-pin
residual SAT status, without rereading $F_S$.
Auxiliary-variable encodings, child lists, Boolean joins and any
source-dependent decoder program bits count toward the artifact length.
\begin{theorem}[Counting gate]
If one fixed decoder answers every triple correctly for all
$S\subseteq\mathcal T$ of size $m$ using artifacts of at most $b$ bits,
then
\[
2^{b+1}-1\geq {T\choose m},\qquad
b\geq\left\lceil\log_2({T\choose m}+1)\right\rceil-1.
\]
For $m=n$ and the declared fixed-width indexed 3CNF encoding,
$N=3(n+7)(1+\lceil\log_2(n+3)\rceil)+O(\log n)$ and
\[
b\geq(2-o(1))n\log_2n=(2/3-o(1))N.
\]
Thus no such all-three-pin-cube-faithful source-free total artifact
has length $O(N^\lambda)$ for fixed $\lambda<1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $S\neq S'$, a triple in their symmetric difference yields opposite
correct answers under the previous theorem. Equal artifacts would
give the same deterministic decoded answer, impossible.
Hence $S\mapsto D(F_S)$ is injective; there are only
$\sum_{i=0}^b2^i=2^{b+1}-1$ strings of lengths at most $b$.
Finally $\log_2{T\choose n}=2n\log_2n-O(n)$ and
$N=(3+o(1))n\log_2n$.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Scope and countercontrols.}
This is a restricted information theorem, not an all-SAT algorithm
lower bound, nor the actual ARC compiler requirement.
Decision-only compression may output the constant YES bit on this
family. Source rereads evade its premise. The allowed aggregate
$q=N^{3/5}$ children of size $N^{1/2}$ have up to $N^{11/10}$
total encoded bits, exceeding the lower bound. Neither the
FLMV forbidden region nor universal hardness follows. The H8
companion of the same gadget is UNSAT and admits a short source-axiom
resolution refutation. Every H7 source contains both YES and NO
three-pin residuals. A canonical sorted original-clause table can
answer $\tau\in S$ by $O(\log m)$ charged source-record comparisons
with logarithmic index storage; this is an explicit legal escape.
