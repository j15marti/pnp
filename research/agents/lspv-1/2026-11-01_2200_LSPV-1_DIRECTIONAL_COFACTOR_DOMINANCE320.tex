\begin{theorem}[every valid original, every source variable]
Write the four original clauses containing $x$, with their original source IDs, as
\[
(x\vee a_1\vee b_1),\ (x\vee a_2\vee b_2),\
(\neg x\vee\neg u_1\vee\neg v_1),\ (\neg x\vee\neg u_2\vee\neg v_2).
\]
Here all $a_i,b_i,u_j,v_j$ name original variables distinct \emph{within} each clause. Other source clauses form $H_x$. Put
\[
P_i=a_i\vee b_i,\quad N_j=\neg u_j\vee\neg v_j,\quad
A=P_1\wedge P_2,\quad B=N_1\wedge N_2,\quad
f_0=F|_{x=0}=H_x\wedge A,\quad f_1=F|_{x=1}=H_x\wedge B.
\]
For every total assignment $z$ of the other original variables,
\[
D_{0\setminus1}(z)=f_0(z)\wedge\neg f_1(z)
=\bigvee_{j=1}^{2}\left(F|_{x=0}(z)\wedge u_j(z)\wedge v_j(z)\right),
\tag{1}
\]
\[
D_{1\setminus0}(z)=f_1(z)\wedge\neg f_0(z)
=\bigvee_{i=1}^{2}\left(F|_{x=1}(z)\wedge\neg a_i(z)\wedge\neg b_i(z)\right).
\tag{2}
\]
Hence $f_0\Rightarrow f_1$ pointwise iff \emph{both} of the original-source partial contexts
$\{x=0,u_j=1,v_j=1\}$ ($j=1,2$) have zero satisfying completions; reverse dominance uses $\{x=1,a_i=0,b_i=0\}$. These are sound tests under \emph{every future coherent pin} of variables other than $x$, without maintaining syntactic pin keys or a free SAT-label oracle. They do not assume two branches of the same current SAT label are continuation equivalent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two positive clauses reduce to $P_1,P_2$ under $x=0$ and the two negative clauses become true. Under $x=1$ the roles reverse. Thus $f_0=H_x A$, $f_1=H_x B$. On a satisfying assignment of $f_0$, failure of $f_1$ is exactly $\neg B=\neg N_1\vee\neg N_2$, and $\neg N_j=u_j\wedge v_j$. The reverse statement is sign-symmetric. Every witness is a full coherent assignment of original names; the disjunctive union need not be disjoint. QED.
\end{proof}
