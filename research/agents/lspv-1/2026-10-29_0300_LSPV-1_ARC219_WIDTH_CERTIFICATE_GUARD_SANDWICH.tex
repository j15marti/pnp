\section{BUILD I: effective bounded-primal-width certificate generation}
\begin{lemma}[Exact Davis--Putnam projection]
If $F=R\wedge\bigwedge_i(v\vee A_i)\wedge\bigwedge_j(\neg v\vee B_j)$ and $v$ does not appear in $R,A_i,B_j$, then
\[
\exists v\,F\equiv R\wedge\bigwedge_{i,j}(A_i\vee B_j).
\]
Every nontautological displayed resolvent is an ordinary resolution inference from the two indexed source-parent clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed assignment to the other variables, any false $A_i$ forces $v=1$, and any false $B_j$ forces $v=0$. No value is feasible exactly when both exist, which falsifies a displayed resolvent. Tautological resolvents contribute no restriction.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Paid source-authenticated certificates, restricted parameter]
For a fully encoded CNF $F$ with $n$ source variables, $m$ clauses, and a supplied fully encoded variable ordering $\sigma$ of induced \emph{primal} width $w$, one fixed deterministic algorithm returns on SAT inputs a verified satisfying assignment and on UNSAT inputs an independently replayable indexed ordinary resolution refutation of the actual source. The number of distinct new derived resolution clauses is at most $n3^w$ and the number of examined opposite-polarity clause pairs at most $n9^w$. Its total deterministic bit-time, including source/order validation, parsing, every candidate (including unsuccessful and tautological pairs), dictionary operations, proof creation, identifiers and output, is bounded by
\[
\operatorname{poly}(N+n9^w),
\]
with peak materialized clause storage at most $m+n3^w$ distinct bodies and their physically written parent references, where $N$ counts the complete physical input including $\sigma$. These are \emph{upper bounds} for this one algorithm, not lower bounds for arbitrary SAT programs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Eliminate pivots in the supplied order, retaining a source index for each original clause and two prior-parent indices and actual pivot for every newly derived clause. Eliminate duplicate bodies but keep one sound derivation. A current pivot has at most $w$ still-live filled-graph neighbors. In either polarity bucket, for each neighbor there are three possibilities: positive, negative or absent, hence at most $3^w$ distinct bucket clauses and at most $9^w$ pairs. Their nontautological resolvents have at most $3^w$ distinct neighbor-only bodies. Sum over at most $n$ pivots. Each symbolic operation, parent-index access, full clause comparison and physical print is polynomial in the total materialized data. Exact projection by the lemma proves that an empty clause appears iff source $F$ is UNSAT. Replay of actual clause IDs and actual pivots verifies every line. Otherwise, reverse the elimination stages. Each saved pivot's positive/negative buckets cannot simultaneously require opposite values under the already reconstructed suffix valuation, since their resolvent would be false in the projected residual. Choose the allowed value, continue and verify the final input assignment. The same fixed code determines both outcomes, with no answer-class advice.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Charge structural discovery and distinguish restricted from unrestricted]
If $\operatorname{tw}(P(F))\le c\log N$ for fixed $c$, apply Bodlaender et al.'s deterministic $2^{O(k)}n$ approximation with $k=\lceil c\log N\rceil$, obtain a width-$5k+4$ decomposition, extract/verify an induced-width order and execute the preceding theorem. All operations are then polynomial in physical $N$ on this \emph{restricted} language. The proof-finder makes the discovery step effective in principle; the present Python finite audit directly tests supplied orders and does not implement the sophisticated width finder. General SAT has no $O(\log N)$ primal-width guarantee.
\end{remark}

\section{BUILD II: global-pinned guard treewidth}
Let source $F$ be a proper signed 3CNF on $n$ variables and $m$ clauses. For each source clause $C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$, introduce private $y_1,y_2,y_3,t$, common global $g$, three binary clauses $(\ell_i\vee y_i)$ and even-parity constraints
\[
y_1\oplus y_2\oplus t=0,\qquad t\oplus y_3\oplus g=0.
\]
Pin $\neg g$ to obtain $P_F$. The primal constraint graph includes all pairwise edges of \emph{each parity triple}. This globally pinned $P_F$ is SAT iff source $F$ is SAT. The unpinned system is always SAT and must not be confused with the pinned reduction.
\begin{theorem}[Guarded primal treewidth sandwich]
For every such $F$,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{tw}(P(F))\le\operatorname{tw}(P(P_F))
\le\max\{\operatorname{tw}(P(F))+1,4\}.}
\]
The lower bound is a graph-minor inequality and is \emph{not} a decision time lower bound. The upper bound has a uniform polynomial-time bag constructor given a valid complete source decomposition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each gadget contract its private-to-original edges $a-y_1,b-y_2,c-y_3$ and then $c-t$ after the first contractions. The parity triangle on $y_1,y_2,t$ induces the source triangle on $a,b,c$. After doing this for all clauses, delete $g$ and any extra edges. The source primal graph is a minor.

For the upper bound, add $g$ to every original decomposition bag, adding at most one to bag cardinality. Each source clause's three original variables $a,b,c$ form a clique, so their connected bag supports share an original bag $D$. Attach to $D$ a path of five new bags:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
B_0=\{a,b,c,g,y_1\},&B_1=\{b,c,g,y_1,y_2\},\\
B_2=\{c,g,y_1,y_2,t\},&B_3=\{c,g,y_2,t,y_3\},\\
B_4=\{c,g,t,y_3\}.&
\end{array}
\]
Every binary edge and both parity-triple scopes are covered, $g$ is globally connected and each old and new variable has connected bag support. The added bags have at most five vertices, giving width at most $\max(w+1,4)$. The bag-to-source identity, output length and graph-edge validation are polynomial in the physically supplied source/decomposition.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proof lift after paying global discovery]
If the pinned source $F$ is UNSAT and the preceding eliminator discovers a refutation having $L\le n3^w$ inferences, the ARC218 five-step clause derivation transports it, without a new SAT oracle, to a source-authenticated resolution refutation of $P_F$ with exactly $11m+1$ pinned CNF axioms and $5m+L$ inferences. The proof and source are fully printed and independently checkable. This does \emph{not} establish a universally polynomial proof for arbitrary primal width.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Encode both ternary even-parity constraints into four forbid-assignment clauses each, with the three binary clauses and common pin: $3m+8m+1$ axioms. ARC218 derives every exact original clause from its own gadget and the pin in five source-indexed resolution steps, all of width at most three. Replace each source-axiom reference in the already \emph{discovered} source proof by its five-step derived clause and replay its original inferences with their actual parent rows and pivots. The empty clause follows. Charge output of all labels, source identifiers, clauses, pivots, indices, failed candidate pairs, validation and work storage.
\end{proof}
