\section*{Preselected calculus and definitions}
Fix the already declared Rmix341 with authenticated zero-cost original CNF sources and unit-paid at-most-binary-premise RES, exact AND3, GF(2) XOR, CONVERT and conservative source-independent VARDEF. Shared DAG nodes count once. The best legal derivation is selected freely. Write
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\#\{\text{paid inference nodes of }\pi\},\quad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ \mathrm{UNSAT}\\|\mathrm{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F).
\]
Malformed strings are excluded from this maximum and must be rejected by a general SAT decider. Encoded input length N counts bits, not variables.

For a CNF F, a heterogeneous strong Horn/2CNF backdoor is a variable set B such that for \emph{every} assignment alpha of B, the syntactically simplified F under alpha is either Horn or 2CNF, with the class allowed to depend on alpha. Let b(F) be the least cardinality.

\begin{lemma}[Genuine Resolution lifts a restricted refutation]
Given any partial assignment alpha and any q-node authentic Resolution refutation of F restricted by alpha, the original sources of F derive in at most q genuine binary Resolution nodes some subclause of the blocker of alpha.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Lift each residual source to its original authenticated clause, which adds only literals made false by alpha. For every resolvent of the restricted proof, resolve the lifted parents on the same unassigned pivot. All added assigned-variable literals have a unique sign fixed by alpha, so lifting cannot create a complementary pair on those variables. The restricted resolvent was nontautological on unassigned variables. Induction over the shared DAG preserves each legal Resolution step, and the empty final restricted clause lifts to a clause made solely of alpha-false literals. No weakening, semantic oracle, auxiliary variable, or unproved mixed restriction rule is needed.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Third necessary filter]
There exists an absolute c such that for every valid UNSAT general CNF F with encoded bit length N,
\[
\boxed{W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F)\le W_{\rm Res}(F)\le
2^{b(F)}\bigl(c(N+1)^2+1\bigr).}
\]
Thus any growing family with b(F)=O(log N) has polynomial mixed proof cost.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each of 2^b assignments to a minimum B, F restricted to that assignment is UNSAT and lies in Horn or 2CNF. Ordinary Horn unit propagation has a polynomial-size genuine Resolution refutation (each unit is derived at most once, charging each antecedent pivot); UNSAT 2CNF has opposed implication paths yielding opposite units with polynomially many genuine pivots. A uniform O((N+1)^2) bound suffices. Lift each leaf refutation using the lemma, producing from the original sources a subclause of its assignment blocker. Combine sibling blockers bottom-up: resolve on their opposite decision literals if both occur; otherwise retain whichever stronger clause already omits the pivot. Each merged clause is a subclause of the parent's partial-assignment blocker, and the final root is empty. At most 2^b-1 combining pivots are needed. Every step belongs to the RES subsystem of the \emph{same} mixed calculus. The cost of discovering B or constructing/checking this proof is separate from W_R.
\end{proof}

\section*{An explicit 3CNF family that passes both earlier filters but is easy}
For integer $k\ge3$ use original variables $x_{ij}$, $0\le i,j<k$, except $(0,0)$. Make a Horn CNF $H_k$: a positive unit for every boundary cell $i=0$ or $j=0$; each interior cell has clause
\[
(\neg x_{i-1,j}\vee\neg x_{i,j-1}\vee x_{ij});
\]
finally add the negative unit $(\neg x_{k-1,k-1})$. This $H_k$ has $k^2-1$ variables and $k^2$ clauses and is minimally UNSAT. Deleting a boundary unit permits it to be false and propagates zero to the sink; deleting an interior implication permits its head to be false with both parents true and propagates zero; deleting the final negative unit permits all variables true. All other implications are satisfied by the AND propagation.

Guard every original clause C twice with (z OR C) and (NOT z OR C), using a fresh selector z. For any guarded four-literal clause
\[
(g\vee\neg a\vee\neg b\vee c),\qquad g\in\{z,\neg z\},
\]
replace it by the private width-three pair
\[
(g\vee\neg a\vee u)\wedge(\neg u\vee\neg b\vee c).
\]
Guarded units stay binary. Name the result $G_k$. Every signed source is indispensable: choose the selector that activates its guard, use the $H_k$ deletion witness, and set each private u to satisfy all remaining pairs. For an active unsatisfied original guarded four-clause, deleting its first split clause permits u=0 and deleting its second permits u=1. A source-deletion witness falsifies all literals in the removed clause and thus repairs every single occurrence-sign flip.

\begin{theorem}[High deficiency and treewidth, constant backdoor, linear proof]
For every $k\ge3$, the signed width-at-most-three $G_k$ is minimally UNSAT, not itself Horn, and satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
m_k&=2k^2+2(k-1)^2,&v_k&=k^2+2(k-1)^2,\\
\delta(G_k)&=k^2,&\mathrm{tw}_{\rm primal}(G_k)&\ge k-1,\\
b_{\rm Horn}(G_k)&=1,&m_k-1\le W_R(G_k)&\le 4(k-1)^2+k^2+1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Its canonical signed-DIMACS encoded length is $N_k=\Theta(k^2\log k)$ bits, up to the constant factor eight for ASCII bytes. Thus both deficiency and treewidth are superlogarithmic in $N_k$, yet $W_R(G_k)=O(N_k)$. At $k=2$ the matching upper and source-fan-in lower bounds give exact $W_R(G_2)=9$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are k^2 guarded original Horn clauses in each selector direction and 2(k-1)^2 private ternary splits, each adding one source and one new variable. Every assignment to z leaves each active split Horn (positive head at most one) and renders the other branch's first clause true, while its second clause is still Horn. A positive z-guarded root clause has two positives, so the source without assignment is non-Horn. Contract every private u in one selected interior split into its output head $x_{ij}$; the primal graph then contains both dependency edges from the cell's two parents. Its interior vertices contain a (k-1)-by-(k-1) grid minor, proving treewidth at least k-1.

A source-authenticated genuine Resolution refutation uses 2(k-1)^2 pivots to undo private splits, k^2 pivots to eliminate opposite guards, 2(k-1)^2 unit pivots for interior Horn propagation, and one final conflicting-unit pivot. This gives 4(k-1)^2+k^2+1 paid nodes. Since every original source is indispensable, any sound conservative binary-premise DAG, including any mixed/VARDEF DAG, must connect all $m_k$ source ancestors through at least $m_k-1$ paid nodes. For $k=2$ these bounds coincide. Counting signed decimal source symbols yields $N_k=\Theta(k^2\log k)$.
\end{proof}
