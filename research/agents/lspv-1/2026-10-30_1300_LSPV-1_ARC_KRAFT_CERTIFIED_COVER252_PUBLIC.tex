\begin{theorem}[Kraft mass bound for explicit adaptive direct-cofactor trees]
Let an UNSAT $F$ have $m$ proper width-three source clauses on $n$ original variables, with each variable in at most $\Delta$ clauses. A fixed-width physical source record has $h_n=2+3(1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil)$ bits and parent length $N=m h_n+O(\log N)$. Consider an explicitly unfolded deterministic decision tree with adaptive branching and source-certified forced literals. Every terminal is either a source-authenticated NO partial cube or a completed exact guest $A$ on the purely syntactically restricted ordinary CNF child. A child retains every untouched clause as an unchanged $h_n$-bit record. Assume every guest child has physical length $\le N^\lambda$, $0<\lambda<1$. Define
\[
k_N=\max\bigl(0,\lceil(m-\lfloor N^\lambda/h_n\rfloor)/\Delta\rceil\bigr).
\]
If there are $g$ \emph{logical guest leaves}, the fraction of complete Boolean assignments covered by source-only NO leaves satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\mu_{\mathrm{source\,NO}}\ge1-g\,2^{-k_N}}.
\]
If there are no source-only NO leaves, then $g\ge2^{k_N}$. For an explicit tree visiting every guest leaf with a separately charged local operation, $g\le T_{\mathrm{local}}$, whence $\mu_{\mathrm{source\,NO}}\ge1-T_{\mathrm{local}}2^{-k_N}$. Neither statement identifies $g$ with the number of distinct physical guest calls under memoization.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A path pinning $r$ distinct variables changes at most $\Delta r$ original clause records. Its purely syntactic child consequently has at least $(m-\Delta r)h_n$ physical bits, so any short guest child requires $r\ge k_N$. Expand each certified forced literal into a virtual decision: its excluded opposite-value branch is a source-certified NO prefix; its permitted branch continues. Expand ordinary decisions in both polarities. The resulting finite, explicit leaves are pairwise-disjoint Boolean prefix cylinders partitioning $\{0,1\}^n$, so their weights $2^{-|\rho|}$ sum to one. Every guest leaf weighs at most $2^{-k_N}$; hence the guest mass is at most $g2^{-k_N}$ and the complementary source-NO mass has the claimed lower bound. Visiting each guest leaf consumes a counted operation. No lower bound against a differently encoded or globally recognizing algorithm follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Caching and logical-path alias firewall]
When one physical completed query $i$ serves $r_i$ logically distinct guest prefix leaves, only
$\mu_{\mathrm{guest}}\le2^{-k_N}\sum_{i=1}^{q_{\mathrm{physical}}}r_i$
follows. Substituting $q_{\mathrm{physical}}$ for $\sum_i r_i$ is invalid unless no cross-leaf reuse is permitted. Compact decision DAGs also evade the explicit-leaf-visit premise.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Partition logical leaves by the physical query whose answer is reused; the Kraft weights still count each logical source cube once, whether or not the physical guest is executed again.\end{proof}
