\begin{theorem}[Elementary exact mixed-extreme layer moment]

\[
M_k^F(w):=\mathbb E_{|a|=w}\left[\binom{N_0(a)}k\binom{N_3(a)}{3-k}\right]
=\frac{\sum_{u,h}H^F_k(u,h)\binom{n-u}{w-h}}{\binom nw}.
\]
Out-of-range binomial coefficients are zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

For fixed clause-mode triple, an inconsistent repeated variable cannot extend; a compatible triple fixes $u$ variables, including $h$ ones, leaving exactly $\binom{n-u}{w-h}$ completions. Swap sums and divide by $\binom nw$. No implication about SAT decision complexity is used.
\end{proof}
