\section{BUILD: exact signed gadget}
For a signed clause $C=\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3$ introduce three fresh Boolean bits $y_1,y_2,y_3$ and let
\[
G_C(x,y)=\left(\bigwedge_{i=1}^{3}(\ell_i(x)\vee y_i)\right)
\wedge(y_1\oplus y_2\oplus y_3=0).
\]
\begin{lemma}For every assignment $x$ to source variables,
$C(x)=1$ if and only if $\exists y\in\{0,1\}^{3}:G_C(x,y)=1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}If all three literals are false, the binary clauses force every $y_i=1$, contradicting even parity. If any literal is true, choose that index as a free parity bit and set the other two bits to one. Both directions hold, even for repeated or complementary source literals.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}There is a uniform polynomial-time reduction of every $n$-variable $m$-clause 3CNF $F$ to a mixed 2CNF-plus-XOR instance $G_F$ with $n+3m$ variables, exactly $3m$ binary clauses and $m$ ternary XOR constraints, such that
\[
\forall x\quad F(x)\iff\exists y\ G_F(x,y).
\]
Each fresh auxiliary variable occurs in exactly one binary clause and one XOR constraint, and the XOR triples are disjoint. Satisfiability of this restricted mixed language is NP-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Conjoin the locally projected gadgets with fresh disjoint triples. Their existential quantifiers distribute over their conjunction. The local lemma proves correctness on SAT and UNSAT inputs without an answer-class bit. The explicit construction is polynomial and 3SAT-hardness transfers. Membership in NP follows by checking a joint assignment. If the original incidence graph is connected, adding its attached gadget variables preserves connectedness; every original variable's signed binary occurrence count equals its source count.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Falsification of isolated closures]Let $F_\bot$ contain every one of the eight possible signed 3-clauses over $x_1,x_2,x_3$. The source $F_\bot$ and its mixed image are UNSAT, but the 2CNF portion alone is SAT (all fresh $y=1$), and the XOR portion alone is SAT (all $y=0$). Deleting any one source clause makes the source and mixed image SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Each assignment to three source bits falsifies exactly one of the eight clauses. The gadget theorem transfers this exact answer. The displayed separate witnesses are explicit. Thus independent subsystem-consistency answers are insufficient. This does not imply a lower bound for properly charged adaptive combined solvers.\end{proof}
