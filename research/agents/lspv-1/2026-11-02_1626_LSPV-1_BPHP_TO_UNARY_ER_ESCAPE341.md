**PROVED/EXACT (gate-RES prefix).** For the same strict signed three-CNF bit pigeonhole instances G_n from LSPV-1 report 340, with n=2^ell holes and n+1 pigeons, construct ordinary unary-PHP axioms using only binary resolution and explicit, charged conservative two-input AND-gate definitions. Every source is authenticated and every rule is fixed before instances. The mixed-calculus source-capacity floor M3-1 remains valid, where M=n*binom(n+1,2) and M3=M*(2ell-2).

**EXPLICIT CONSTRUCTION.** For each pigeon i, binary label prefix u, introduce p(i,u) meaning the first |u| hole-label bits equal u. Level-one prefixes are signed original bit literals; deeper prefixes are fresh charged two-input AND gates. Each gate q<->(a AND b) supplies exactly three attributed definitional clauses (-q OR a),(-q OR b),(q OR -a OR -b). Resolve the two sibling gate upward clauses on the new bit to derive (-parent OR left OR right). Recursively expand to obtain the unary row (OR_z p(i,z)); the two first-bit branches resolve without any tautology axiom. Resolve each private 3-CNF chain into its original signed 2ell-literal collision clause in 2ell-3 steps. A leaf gate implies each matched label bit by a chain of O(ell) authenticated resolution steps; cancel all 2ell mismatch literals to obtain (-p(i,z) OR -p(j,z)). Thus every unary PHP axiom is derived with O(n^3 ell + n^2 ell^2) charged gate-plus-resolution nodes. No semantic SAT/equivalence oracle or free multi-premise rule is introduced.

**KNOWN THEOREM:** Ordinary unary PHP_(n+1)^n admits polynomial-size extended-resolution refutations (Cook--Reckhow, 1979; Buss, 1987; Grosof--Zhang--Heule, 2022 discusses Cook's O(n^4) ER and a polynomial DRAT construction). All standard ER extension gates can be decomposed into constant-many 2-input AND/OR/NOT gates. Consequently in the *explicit fixed gate-RES calculus* G (source clauses, binary RES, paid 2-input DEF with clause-addressable defining clauses) the strict bit-PHP family has W_G(G_n) = n^{O(1)}. Since N=|enc(G_n)| >= M3, this is polynomial in encoded input length.
