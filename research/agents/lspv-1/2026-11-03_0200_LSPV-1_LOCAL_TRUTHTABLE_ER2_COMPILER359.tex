\begin{lemma}[Exact charged local implicate compiler]
For a finite authenticated non-tautological CNF \(\mathcal S\) over at most three named variables, every implied non-tautological clause \(C\), including \(C=\bot\), has a valid RES+paid conservative signed-AND-VARDEF derivation from \(\mathcal S\) with at most \(\boxed{55}\) additional charged nodes. The names may be original or auxiliary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each complete assignment \(\alpha\) falsifying \(C\), at least one authenticated premise \(D_\alpha\in\mathcal S\) is false. If an empty premise occurs, the proof already ends. Otherwise \(D_\alpha\) is contained as a literal set in the complete assignment-blocking clause \(B_\alpha\). The already verified LSPV-2 PAID-CLAUSE-WEAKENING352 compiler uses \(3k+2r-2\) paid conservative signed gates and genuine RES nodes to extend a \(k\)-literal premise by \(r\) compatible literals, whenever \(r>0\); if \(r=0\), use the original clause without additional charge. For a three-variable blocker \(r=3-k\), hence at most \(k+4\le6\) paid nodes. There are at most \(2^{3-|C|}\le8\) falsifying assignments and thus at most 8 derived blockers. Pair the complete blockers on variables not mentioned in \(C\). A strict binary RES tree requires at most \(2^{3-|C|}-1\le7\) additional pivots and ends in exactly \(C\). Therefore \(8\cdot6+7=55\) suffices. A smaller support uses the smaller cube or already available padding names. If the entire original UNSAT formula has at most one variable, it has a direct zero- or one-node RES proof. All extra gates are globally fresh and conservatively charged; the translation never calls typed AND3.
\end{proof}
