## Lemma 424.1: original-premise strict Resolution row compiler
Let locally represented conditions A1,A2 have a combined support of at most r named variables, with A1 AND A2 implying output B. Represent a condition by its canonical CNF, with one blocker clause per violating assignment (empty for false, no clauses for true). Each canonical output blocker D is derivable as a *subclause* D'⊆D by **strict Resolution only**, using <=2^r-1 inferences and no new definition.

**Proof:** Fix the falsifying assignment of D. For each completion on the remaining <=r input variables, A1 AND A2 must be false, so choose one actual parent canonical blocker falsified by that completion. Process completions bottom-up as an assignment binary tree. For sibling subclauses differing on pivot x, strictly resolve if both complementary x literals remain; otherwise inherit the subclause lacking pivot. Non-pivot complements cannot arise because both clauses are subsets of blockers of assignments agreeing on those other bits. The root is a subclause of D; when D is empty the root is empty. All leaves cite the actual authenticated input clauses or their stronger already-derived representatives. Every branch is discharged in this **compiler's external metatheory**, not added as a native R inference. Each original proof node is compiled once with DAG references retained.

Therefore one extensional K-local step costs O_K(1) ordinary RES, while a GF(2) sum of rows of maximum width b, with two-parent union support <=2b and at most 2^b canonical output clauses, costs at most 2^b(2^(2b)-1)<=2^(3b) new ordinary RES. No extra definitions are needed. Res parent clauses with strengthened representatives use strict pivot or missing-pivot inheritance, not weakening. All original paid DEF facets remain owned by paid nodes.
