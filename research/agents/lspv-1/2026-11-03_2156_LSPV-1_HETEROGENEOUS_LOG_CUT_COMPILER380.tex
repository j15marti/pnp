\section*{Established hard family, precise scope}
\noindent\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM (Haken, 1985):} For the unary signed CNF $\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^h$ with $h(h+1)$ variables, $(h+1)$ positive width-$h$ occupancy clauses, and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative collision clauses of width two, standard explicit signed DIMACS serialization has $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. There exist $c>0,h_0$ such that for every $h\ge h_0$ and \emph{every ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution refutation} of this CNF, at least $2^{ch}$ lines are required. Unit charged distinct derived resolvents therefore number at least $2^{ch}-m_h$, where $m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$. This is NOT an all-algorithm SAT time bound. Cook (1976) gives polynomial Extended Resolution proofs of the same family. Expander Tseitin is Resolution-hard but short using paid GF(2) operations in the existing $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$. Neither lower bound transfers to mixed inference or unrestricted computation.

\noindent The unchanged project calculus charges one per distinct paid binary RES, constant-three-original-support AND3, binary XOR, local unary LOCAL-CONVERT, and paid conservative two-input DEF; individual signed source clauses cost zero. The actual full LOCAL-CONVERT circuit payload / public-wire syntax remains incomplete (LSPV-4/363 and Red Team/328). The theorem below is confined to a predeclared, smaller typed conservative signed-AND2 subgrammar.

\section*{Typed heterogeneous public cut}
\noindent Fix an acyclic signed-AND2 graph $C$ with $B\ge1$ earlier authenticated boundary wires $u_1,\dots,u_B$. Every private gate defines exactly one fresh wire $w=(\pm a)\wedge(\pm b)$ using only earlier named wires; the definition is conservative and one paid inference. The complete list of publicly observable outputs is $O=(o_1,\dots,o_r)$. For every $j$, let $S_j$ be the set of boundary wires reached by syntactic reverse paths from $o_j$. These sets may differ and the union need not be small. Assume $|S_j|\le b$; an empty cut may be padded with one existing boundary wire. Downstream operations can reuse, correlate and share the entire output vector, but their legality and conclusions depend on the Boolean values of these named outputs, not on private gate identities, source text, proof metadata, private wire names or uncharged source-semantic tests. Replacing an observed wire name must be checked as a typed alias. No source-to-false oracle is admitted.

\begin{theorem}[heterogeneous cut, all-size, representation-extensional]
For every $B,r,b\ge1$ and every valid acyclic typed circuit and observation list as above, there exists one acyclic conservative signed-AND2 replacement bank $Q$ and $r$ public aliases for which
$$
\forall a\in\{0,1\}^{B}:\quad O(C,a)=O(Q,a)
$$
coordinatewise, including every joint cross-output correlation. The replacement needs no more than
$$
d(Q)\le \sum_{j=1}^r |S_j|\,2^{|S_j|}\le r b2^b
$$
paid gate definitions, independent of the original number of private gates and of $|\cup_jS_j|$. If public aliases are separately paid in an applicable formal grammar, add at most $r$ charged steps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each output $o_j$, induction along its fan-in DAG shows that it computes a Boolean function $f_j:\{0,1\}^{S_j}\to\{0,1\}$. Put $k=|S_j|$. Construct an exact DNF for $f_j$ over the $k$ boundary wires. If $t$ input words make $f_j$ true, each of its $t\le2^k$ signed minterms needs at most $k-1$ paid AND2 gates. Combining these terms by OR via signed De Morgan AND2 uses at most $t-1$ more paid gates. For $0<t<2^k$ the total is $tk-1\le k2^k-1$. Constant functions use one paid $u\wedge\neg u$ gate and the signed complement, within $k2^k$ for $k\ge1$. Take disjoint internal fresh names for all outputs, assign every public alias, and concatenate banks. For every boundary assignment each output computes exactly its old function, so the joint vector, not merely its marginals or final root, agrees. Any strictly extensional continuation therefore has identical Boolean conclusions. The external boundary wires themselves can be arbitrary correlated functions of original SAT variables, since the identity holds for \emph{all} formal boundary assignments.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[logarithmic cut, physical compiler]
Let $L$ be the entire \emph{supplied serialized circuit and output-list bit length}, not the SAT source length $N$ or logical paid-node count $q$. For $r\le L$ and $b\le c\log_2(L+2)$ with fixed $c$, the replacement has $O(L^{c+1}\log(L+2))$ encoded logical gates and aliases. A uniform deterministic compiler can parse the whole explicit DAG, reject forward/zero/malformed references, acquire each syntactic cut, evaluate at most $r2^b$ complete gate arrays and emit the bank. For a safe output-bit budget $Q=O(rb2^b\log(L+rb2^b+2))$, deliberately loose all-phase bit-machine upper bounds are
$$
T=O((r2^b+1)(L+Q+2)^3),\qquad S=O(L+Q+2).
$$
These charge source parsing, indices, reads, checking, output materialization, memory, and every conversion phase. They are polynomial in \emph{actual evidence length} $L$, not automatically polynomial in $N+q$. If $L$ is exponential in $N+q$, this theorem cannot be used as a polynomial physical-runtime transfer.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Since $2^b\le(L+2)^c$, substitute in the preceding bound. Determine each syntactic frontier by source-DAG reachability, then independently evaluate its $2^{|S_j|}$ boundary assignments, setting irrelevant boundaries to zero. Use exhaustive indexed rescans to justify the stated conservative cubic overhead per pass and materialize input, gate tables, emitted bank and simultaneous aliases within the stated storage bound. The compiler is one uniform algorithm and uses no SAT/equivalence oracle or per-instance advice. If instead the supplied graph were charged one paid inference for every gate with conventional bounded-index serialization, $L$ could be separately bounded polynomially by $N+q$; that premise is NOT known for arbitrary opaque LOCAL-CONVERT payloads.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[raw DAG inflation is not intrinsic]
For every $t\ge1$, the $t$-gate conservative chain $z_1=u\land u$, $z_{i+1}=z_i\land u$ with public value $z_t$ computes precisely $u$ and has a zero-gate equivalent alias. Thus unbounded private gate counts in a supplied representation cannot be interpreted as invariant minimum logical work or physical SAT decision time.
\end{corollary}
