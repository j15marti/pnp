\section*{One candidate and precise falsifiers}
\textbf{Candidate P2-C3 (conditional on P2-D1):}
A finite abstract reachable-state graph, with at most $M$ states and maximum nonnegative abstract transition charge $\kappa$, cannot itself certify a source-to-refutation paid-work lower bound exceeding $\kappa(M-1)$ by sound MAY shortest-path potentials. This is a \emph{ceiling on a selected proof method}, not an upper bound on the genuine optimal native proof work.

The exact falsifiers registered before the finite experiment were:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[F1.] A finite directed graph with $M$ reachable states, a reachable abstract goal, all edge costs in $[0,\kappa]$, yet shortest goal distance greater than $\kappa(M-1)$.
\item[F2.] A nonnegative zero-cost cycle is indispensable to every cheapest goal path, so it cannot be shortened to a simple path without increasing work.
\item[F3.] A feasible nonnegative goal-zero Bellman potential exceeds the shortest abstract goal distance at the initial state.
\item[F4.] An alleged application to $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ silently assumes that the unknown full native rule relation is authenticated, that arbitrary historical weights are known to be bounded by the asserted $\kappa$, or that a symbolic state of $b$ bits contains at most $b$ abstract states.
\end{enumerate}
F1--F3 are excluded by the elementary proof below and by exhaustive finite tests for F1--F2. F4 remains a mandatory source-authentication and model-accounting obstacle; no full-native application is asserted.

\section*{Exact statement, proof and sharpness}
Fix an external original CNF $F$ and \emph{one} completely specified source-bound, context-aware native shared-DAG calculus $R$. In its concrete construction semantics, the state contains all original source occurrences, distinct already-paid node identities, global sharing, exact charges, definitions, fresh/private/exported wires, assumptions, branch-AND obligations, discharge and joins. Free optimal proof selection is over \emph{all} complete legal shared DAGs. Let $W_R(F)$ be the infimum of sum of distinct paid inference/owner weights. Evidence bits, discovery, physical time, span and peak space are separate quantities.

Suppose a sound MAY abstraction with state map $\alpha$ and concretization $\gamma$ (with every concrete state in its abstract preimage) has a finite set $A_F^+$ of abstract states reachable from $\alpha(s_F)$ and size $M$. Every legal concrete step must have an abstract successor of charge no greater than the actual first-use/reuse charge, and every concrete completed refutation must map to an abstract goal. Suppose in addition every MAY abstract edge has charge $c\in[0,\kappa]$, where $\kappa$ is a finite constant for this abstraction, and some abstract goal is reachable from the initial state.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-state MAY certificate ceiling]
Let $d^\#(a,G^\#)$ denote the shortest abstract goal-path charge. For every nonnegative abstract potential $h$ with $h(g)=0$ at abstract goals and
$h(a)\leq c+h(b)$ on every MAY edge $a\xrightarrow{c}b$,
\[
0\ \leq\ h(\alpha(s_F))
\ \leq\ d^\#(\alpha(s_F),G^\#)
\ \leq\ \kappa(M-1).
\]
Provided the stated all-step and terminal soundness hold, also $d^\#(\alpha(s_F),G^\#)\leq W_R(F)$.
Consequently, if such a finite-state method certifies $h(\alpha(s_F))\geq L>0$, it necessarily has
\[
\boxed{\qquad M\geq 1+\left\lceil L/\kappa\right\rceil
\qquad(\kappa>0).\qquad}
\]
If $\kappa=0$, no positive finite lower bound is certifiable by this method.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Because the goal is reachable and all edge charges are nonnegative, deleting a repeated-state cycle from any candidate path does not raise its charge. A least-cost path can therefore be chosen simple. It has at most $M-1$ edges, each of cost at most $\kappa$, proving $d^\#\leq\kappa(M-1)$. Bellman inequalities telescope along that path, yielding $h(a_0)\leq d^\#$. The native-to-MAY cost-sound path simulation and goal inclusion yield $d^\#\leq W_R(F)$ by the earlier LSPV-1/589 argument, with no added source-dependent or historical-rule assumption. Rearranging the ceiling proves the required state count.
\end{proof}

For every $M\geq2$ the bound is tight: the directed unit-weight chain on $M$ states, with the final state as its only goal, has shortest goal cost exactly $M-1$. One additional conservative MAY edge of zero cost from the initial state to that goal collapses the certified lower bound to zero, although it does not assert any legal native zero-cost step.

\textbf{Quantifier consequence.} With a genuinely fixed uniform $\kappa$, a family of superpolynomial \emph{certified} abstract lower bounds would require superpolynomial $M$ along that family. This is only a necessary condition; it is not a proof that $W_R(F)$ itself is polynomial, and not an exponential RAM-space lower bound: $2^b$ abstract states may have $b$-bit symbolic descriptions. Free context/discharge, zero-cost facets, globally shared once-paid nodes and unknown MAY transitions may create \emph{additional} short abstract paths, never invalidate the ceiling.
