\section{Matched model and fixed-image endpoint}
Let $E$ remain the original upward-closed set of pairs $(t,s)$ achieved
\emph{simultaneously by one fixed uniform deterministic total exact ordinary-SAT
decider on every encoded input}, with indexed read-only input and counted
writable/index bits in FLMV's random-access Turing model. Under $P=NP$ there
exists a finite integer $(K,K)\in E$. Define
\[
c_\star=\frac{241}{200},\qquad
d_\star=\beta(c_\star)=\frac{11642479}{23232400},
\qquad\beta(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).
\]
FLMV Corollary 5.8 states
\[
\NTIME[n]\not\subseteq\DTISP[n^{c_\star},o(n^{d_\star})].
\tag{1}
\]
FLMV Section 3.1 and Theorem 3.1 provide, for each fixed
$L\in\NTIME[n]$, a uniform SAT reduction $R_L$ with encoded length
$M(n)\le n\log^{O(1)}n$, and each addressed output bit uniformly computable
in $\log^{O(1)}n$ time and $O(\log n)$ extra work/index space. The reduction
may be virtual but every addressed bit is actually computed and charged.

\begin{theorem}[One fixed hard reduction image is enough]\label{th:single}
There exist one fixed language $L^\dagger\in\NTIME[n]$ and its fixed locally
indexed SAT reduction $R^\dagger=R_{L^\dagger}$ such that, for
\[
\mathcal H^\dagger=\{R^\dagger(x):x\in\{0,1\}^{*}\},
\]
no fixed deterministic uniform program $B$ can be correct and halt on
\emph{every} $y\in\mathcal H^\dagger$ and simultaneously have
$T_B(y)\le |y|^{t+o(1)}$, $S_B(y)\le |y|^{s+o(1)}$ there for constants
$t<c_\star$ and $s<d_\star$. There is no obligation on $B$ outside
$\mathcal H^\dagger$, including no need to recognize that image.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By (1) fix
$L^\dagger\in\NTIME[n]\setminus\DTISP[n^{c_\star},o(n^{d_\star})]$
and a fixed nondeterministic machine for it. Simulate a hypothetical
$B$ on the virtual ordinary SAT encoding $R^\dagger(x)$, on source input $x$.
Whenever its read-only guest input is accessed, compute the addressed bit by
the FLMV local reduction with polylogarithmic time and $O(\log n)$ additional
writable/index space. Every guest instance is in $\mathcal H^\dagger$, so
its promise is always honored. Exact SAT/UNSAT reduction semantics make
this one fixed simulator a correct decider for $L^\dagger$ on every $x$.
The \emph{simultaneous} bounds are
\[
T_D(n)\le n^{t+o(1)},\qquad
S_D(n)\le n^{s+o(1)}+O(\log n).
\]
Strict margins put $D$ in
$\DTISP[n^{c_\star},o(n^{d_\star})]$, contradiction.
\end{proof}
This is a sourced \emph{known-theorem consequence}, not a new
decision-time lower bound or a constructed ARC improvement.
The choice of $L^\dagger$ may be nonconstructive, but once its machine is
fixed all programs above are uniform across source lengths.

\section{Typed-promise recursion is the correct weaker objective}
For a subset $\mathcal H$ of ordinary encodings, define
$E_{\mathcal H}$ to denote pairs simultaneously achieved by one uniform
machine \emph{only on every input of $\mathcal H$}. It is not $E$.
No conversion $E_{\mathcal H}\subseteq E$ is inferred.

\begin{lemma}[Finite typed recursion]\label{lem:typed}
Fix finite ordinary-input domains $\mathcal H_0,\ldots,\mathcal H_d$.
Suppose $B_0$ is exact and uniform on $\mathcal H_0$.
At each level $j\ge1$ a fixed uniform contractor $Q_j$ has a
semantically exact SAT/UNSAT gluing rule on \emph{every}
$F\in\mathcal H_j$, makes finitely many calls \emph{only} on
children proved to belong to $\mathcal H_{j-1}$, and charges all
bit work and peak writable/index storage.
Then the resulting $B_j$ is uniform and exact on $\mathcal H_j$.
At fixed finite depth the valid declared simultaneous resource
envelopes compose. No off-domain correctness is required.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induction on $j$: every child is promised and therefore its correct
SAT bit and charged resource bounds follow from the preceding level.
The exact parent join gives the correct bit for both labels. A fixed
finite composition of fixed programs is uniform; it carries no
input-size-dependent advice. Source/workspace reads, all writes including
unchanged values, arithmetic and addressing, policy selection, failed
routes, caching, child headers/bodies or per-probe virtual generation,
live ancestors, buffers and gluing all remain charged.
\end{proof}
If one level makes $N^{a+o(1)}$ sequential ordinary child calls of
length $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, with local work exponent $r$, child-bit
generator exponent $g$, generator workspace $\eta$ and live ancestor
workspace $\kappa$, the conditional eager ledger is
\[
t'\le\max\{r,a+\lambda+g,a+\lambda t\},\quad
s'\le\max\{\lambda,\lambda s,\eta,\kappa\}.
\]
The fully lazy per-request ledger is
\[
t'\le\max\{r,a+\lambda t+g\},\quad
s'\le\max\{\lambda s,\eta,\kappa\}.
\]
Here $g$ is paid for each written output bit in eager mode and each
requested guest bit in lazy mode. These are conditional upper envelopes,
not machine-producing theorems. A fallback to $A$ on a child outside
the promise must be separately and fully charged.

Our single ARC route can now start with an all-input polynomial $A$ under
$P=NP$ as $B_0$, pass through proved typed domains $\mathcal H_j$,
and finish on $\mathcal H_d=\mathcal H^\dagger$ inside the strict
FLMV box. This is weaker than an all-input successor at each stage.
The old cofinal finite-hit arithmetic remains CONDITIONAL:
the displayed illustrative successor
$(t,s)\mapsto(\max\{11/10,3/5+t/2\},\max\{1/2,s/2\})$
has not been established as a typed machine transformation.
Fixed route depth may depend on the chosen fixed program and exponent,
not on uncharged growing input advice.

\section{A real two-level counterexample to automatic image inheritance}
For $x=x_1\cdots x_n$, let ordinary 2CNF $R_\oplus(x)$ have variables
$u_0,\ldots,u_n$, unit clauses $\neg u_0,\neg u_n$, and two clauses per
constraint $u_i=u_{i+1}$ if $x_{i+1}=0$, or
$u_i\ne u_{i+1}$ if $x_{i+1}=1$.
Then $\SAT(R_\oplus(x))$ iff $x$ has even parity.
A fixed-width indexed encoding consists of a 32-bit header and
$2n+2$ clause records, each
$1+2(1+\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil)$ bits long. Its addressed bits are
computable directly from the source with logarithmic-sized arithmetic
and without copying the formula.

Define uniform total $B_\oplus$ to recognize this exact image and
answer the parity there, but return UNSAT on all off-image inputs.
For the SAT root $R_\oplus(0000)$, a true restriction by $u_0=0$
remains SAT yet leaves the image. The second real restriction by
$u_1=0$ also remains SAT and outside the image. $B_\oplus$ gives
wrong UNSAT answers to both descendants. Thus legitimate SAT-preserving
recursion does not imply child promise eligibility. The example is
easy 2CNF and NOT an FLMV hard image.

The deterministic standalone JavaScript verifier
\path{research/agents/lspv-1/2026-10-25_0130_ARC_SINGLE_HARD_IMAGE161_audit.js}
tested all $1022$ words of lengths $1$ through $9$,
$232320$ individual virtual indexed bits against full serialized
encodings, $254$ independently brute-forced SAT instances of lengths
at most $7$, and two real pin levels. All assertions passed.
Raw values are in the paired JSON artifact. These are finite
COMPUTATIONAL results, not measured physical TM cycles, bit flips,
cumulative bit-time or a new asymptotic lower bound.
