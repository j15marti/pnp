\section{Canonical target and three falsifiable links}

\[
\mathcal P_{\rm pair}=\{F\in\CM:\forall C\in F,\bar C\in F\},\quad
\Cs=\CM\setminus\mathcal P_{\rm pair},\quad
L_\star=\{\operatorname{enc}(F):F\in\Cs,\ \SAT(F)\}.
\]
Invalid encodings are NO. Partial complementary pairs remain permitted, as do disconnected formulas, easy formulas, and nonminimal UNSAT sources. Write \(k=n(F)\), \(m\) for clause count and \(N=|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\) for physical bit length. On valid sources \(3m=4k\), \(m_+=m_-=2k/3\), and in dense indexed clause-list encoding \(N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))\), not \(N=k\). D\"ocker's Theorem 1 proves \(\CM\)-SAT NP-complete; the previous role-2 fixed-Y reduction proves \(L_\star\) NP-complete. This does not prove any SAT decision-time lower bound.
