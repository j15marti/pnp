\section{Original-source graph and theorem}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{m-1}C_j$ be any valid canonical source on $n$ variables ($m=4n/3$), and $p\in\{\ast,0,1\}^n$ a coherent original-variable partial assignment, $f$ free coordinates. For sign $s\in\{+,-\}$ let $R_s$ be original clauses of sign $s$ not satisfied by fixed literals, $q_s=|R_s|$. An unmet clause with no free literal is an immediate source-indexed contradiction. Otherwise define graph $H_s$ with vertices original clause IDs in $R_s$ and edge $\{j,k\}$ exactly when some \emph{free original variable} occurs in both clauses. Every free variable covers at most two unmet clauses of one sign. Its exact minimum one-sign variable cover number is $\tau_s=q_s-\nu_s$, with $\nu_s$ maximum graph matching size, including singleton coverage of unmatched vertices.

\begin{theorem}[Two-sided Tutte source-cube refutation]
For any subsets $S_s\subseteq R_s$, let $o_s$ be the number of odd-sized connected components of $H_s-S_s$ and define $B_s=(q_s+o_s-|S_s|)/2$. Then
\[
\boxed{B_+(S_+)+B_-(S_-)>f
\ \Longrightarrow\ F\wedge p\text{ is UNSAT}.}
\]
Whenever the pre-existing exact matching cut $\tau_++\tau_->f$ applies, some $S_+,S_-$ prove this inequality. Given such original-clause-ID sets on read-only certificate input, a uniform total deterministic verifier checks all grammar and certificate requirements in polynomial time and $O(\log N)$ writable space, with $N$ the combined explicit input/certificate length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every selected free variable covers at most two clauses on either single-sign side. The minimum edge-plus-singleton-cover identity $\tau_s=q_s-\nu_s$ follows by a maximum matching plus singleton choices and the converse pairing bound. Tutte--Berge gives $\nu_s\le(q_s+|S_s|-o_s)/2$, so $\tau_s\ge B_s$. Any full satisfying extension of $p$ uses \emph{disjoint} free TRUE and FALSE original-variable sets to meet the two signs. It requires $f\ge\tau_++\tau_-\ge B_++B_-$. The stated strict opposite inequality rules out every completion of $p$. Equality in Tutte--Berge supplies $S_s$ attaining $B_s=\tau_s$, proving completeness only within this existing matching-cut rule, not for all UNSAT residuals.

The verifier checks original 3-literal supports and signs, duplicates and two positive/two negative quotas using nested source scans and logarithmic counters. For specified original clause IDs $j,k$, the predicate that an unfixed original variable is shared by both is computable with constant-many $O(\log N)$ registers, rescanning the read-only source. Therefore $H_s-S_s$ has a logspace-computable implicit adjacency predicate. By Reingold's deterministic undirected connectivity in L, every pairwise component-membership test uses $O(\log N)$ writable bits. Enumerate each retained vertex $j$; test whether some smaller retained ID is connected, and if not count all component vertices by a separate connectivity scan. Increment $o_s$ when that component size is odd. The reused counters, head indices and connectivity work bits total $O(\log N)$; finally check the integer inequality. The original formula, partial cube and $S_\pm$ are read-only and their scanning costs time. This does not claim low-space \emph{acquisition} or a native FLMV-useful time exponent.
\end{proof}
