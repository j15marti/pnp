## ITERATE: fixed separate affine-DEF / original-local3 calculus

Define the distinct sound, complete, effective system $R_{\mathrm{affDEF+local3},513}$ **before** choosing sources. Free source records are exact signed original CNF clauses $F(X)$, verified by original source IDs. Every derived record costs at least one node. Permitted paid rules:

1. **RES**: unrestricted ordinary binary clause Resolution, including arbitrary clauses over paid auxiliary wires and original variables, with directed source-authenticated shared DAGs.
2. **XOR**: binary addition of any previously justified affine GF(2) equations over original or auxiliary wires, regardless of support.
3. **DEF_aff**: each globally fresh, acyclic, conservative two-input $z=u\oplus v\oplus c$ gate with its four exact defining facets and one charged owner. Unrestricted nesting and sharing. **Nonlinear gates excluded.**
4. **AND3 and LOCAL**: exact semantic local conjunction/implication or local factor/affine/clause conversions, on the union of **at most three ORIGINAL variables after full auxiliary expansion**, with all input/output records source-backed and an explicitly checkable 8-row truth table. A factor is a conjunction represented canonically by the at most eight clauses excluding its falsifying local assignments. Both original-local affine-to-factor and factor-to-affine directions are included. A gate alias whose **expanded** support is >3 is NOT eligible for these rules even if the record names just one wire.
5. **No** branch/discharge, free oracle, nonconservative gate, arbitrary-width semantic LOCAL, public uncharged internal gate, hidden weakening or axiom depending on the source SAT label.

Original Rmix341's actual grammar is **not replaced** by this declaration. The separate system remains complete by RES.

**Proof sketch with native-rule witnesses.** Topologically replace each paid affine wire by its original-variable affine form $\sigma(z)=\sigma(u)+\sigma(v)+c$ over $\mathbb F_2$. (\sigma(u)+1-a=0)\vee(\sigma(v)+1-b=0)
