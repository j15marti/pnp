\section{The even-degree cube}
Let $d\ge4$ be even, let $K=\mathbb F_{2^d}$ in a declared polynomial basis,
and define
\[
P_d:K\to K,\qquad P_d(z)=z^3.                            \tag{1}
\]
Because squaring is $\F$-linear, every coordinate of (1) is a quadratic
Boolean polynomial.

\begin{theorem}[image, fibers, and zero polar kernel]
For even $d\ge4$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $|\operatorname{im}P_d|=1+(2^d-1)/3$;
\item $P_d^{-1}(0)=\{0\}$ and every nonzero image point has exactly three
preimages;
\item the scalar-polar map
\[
K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Alt}_{\F}(K),\qquad
\lambda\longmapsto\beta_\lambda,
\quad
\beta_\lambda(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\bigl(
 \lambda(x^2y+xy^2)\bigr)                               \tag{2}
\]
has kernel zero.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The group $K^*$ is cyclic of order $2^d-1$.  Even $d$ gives
$2^d\equiv1\pmod3$, so $\gcd(3,2^d-1)=3$.  The homomorphism
$z\mapsto z^3$ on $K^*$ therefore has a kernel of size three and image of
size $(2^d-1)/3$, proving the first two claims after adjoining zero.

For the third, suppose $\beta_\lambda=0$.  Nondegeneracy of the trace pairing
and trace transposition of Frobenius give, for every $y\in K$,
\[
(\lambda y)^{2^{d-1}}+\lambda y^2=0.                    \tag{3}
\]
Putting $y=1$ shows $\lambda^{2^{d-1}}=\lambda$.  Since
$\gcd(d,d-1)=1$, the fixed field is $\mathbb F_2$, hence
$\lambda\in\{0,1\}$.  If $\lambda=1$, (3) says the Frobenius maps
$y\mapsto y^{2^{d-1}}$ and $y\mapsto y^2$ agree on $K$.  That would require
$d\mid(d-2)$, hence $d\mid2$, impossible for $d\ge4$.  Thus
$\lambda=0$.
\end{proof}

The map consequently has maximum scalar-component independence in the
zero-kernel sense, but it is neither injective nor surjective.  In particular,
there is no total inverse $K\to K$.

\section{Canonical source-matched compiler}
Let $F(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$ be a source CNF.  Choose an odd $s$ with
\[
d=(s-1)^2\ge\max\{4,n\};                                \tag{4}
\]
then $d$ is even.  Use the canonically encoded graph $X=K_{s,s}$ and its
star-of-stars spanning tree.  The cotree $C$ has $d$ edges.  Let
$\iota_d(u)\in\F^d$ be zero padding of $u$, let $A_d$ be a fixed
syntactically generated unit-lower-triangular matrix, and let $p_d$ be a
fixed syntactically generated phase.  Define the polynomial-time section
\[
s_d(u)=A_d\iota_d(u)+p_d\in K.                           \tag{5}
\]

For a syntactically even charge $c$ define
\[
G_F(u,x)=F(u)\land[B_Xx=c]\land
 \bigl[(\pi_Cx)^3=s_d(u)^3\bigr].                       \tag{6}
\]
All field multiplication gates in (6) are encoded by full definitional
bi-implications.  The source block, field modulus, graph, tree/cotree order,
matrix generator, phase rule, and gate layout are tagged canonical syntax.
Recognition recomputes them in polynomial time; malformed/off-image strings
are rejected.

\begin{theorem}[planted-section neutrality]
For every source witness $u\models F$, the value
\[
z=s_d(u)                                                   \tag{7}
\]
satisfies the residual equation in (6), and $z$ extends uniquely through the
declared tree to a full edge assignment $x$.  Conversely every witness of
$G_F$ projects to a witness of $F$.  Therefore SAT and witness search on the
canonical compiler image are polynomial-time equivalent to their source
counterparts, with a charged polynomial source-to-target extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (7) makes the two sides of the cubic equation identical; it requires
no inversion or search.  Assign $z$ to the cotree.  Subtract its incidences
from $c$ and solve the remaining tree equations leaf-to-root.  Even parity of
$c$ is the unique consistency condition, so this constructs exactly one
edge assignment with the prescribed cotree.  Topological gate evaluation
then supplies all auxiliary values.  Parsing, evaluating (5), two field
multiplications for cubing, tree lifting, writing, and verification are
polynomial in the explicit encoded length.  Projection is immediate from the
literal conjunct $F(u)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[fixed-language consequence]
Let $K_{\rm plant}$ contain satisfiable canonical encodings of (6), rejecting
all malformed or off-image strings.  Then $K_{\rm plant}$ is NP-complete and
\[
\operatorname{SAT}\le_m^p K_{\rm plant}\le_m^p
\operatorname{SAT}.                                     \tag{8}
\]
The forward reduction is $F\mapsto G_F$; the reverse reduction returns the
embedded $F$ on valid records and a fixed NO formula otherwise.
\end{corollary}

The theorem and corollary have complete elementary proofs and are retained as
\CAND{} pending independent review.  They do not put SAT in P: the source
conjunct may retain all original difficulty.  They show only that this
residual layer adds no compulsory asymptotic work after a source witness is
available.

\section{Why the candidate lower-bound repair fails}
CUBEPERMFW1 used odd $d$, where cubing is a permutation and a total inverse is
explicit.  PLANTEDCUBEFW1 uses even $d$: two thirds of nonzero targets are
off-image, every nonzero accepted target has three residual witnesses, and no
total inverse exists.  Yet the compiler never needs a global inverse.  It
requests only $s_d(u)^3$, and the same source witness computes the planted
preimage $s_d(u)$.

Thus each of the following is \RET{} as a sufficient residual-only premise,
even in conjunction with the others:
\begin{itemize}
\item zero scalar-polar kernel;
\item non-surjective residual image and a positive density of NO targets;
\item lack of a total inverse;
\item nonunique three-element nonzero fibers;
\item full projected cotree rank and unbounded treewidth;
\item dense source-to-section mixing and source-matched outputs.
\end{itemize}
The repair required for a future candidate is exact: prove that no uniform
polynomial source-witness section exists.  Merely failing to find a
target-only inverse is insufficient.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
FIELDTRACEFW1 already used $Q(x,y)=xy$ to retire a growing full-rank polar
pencil.  CUBEPERMFW1 used odd-degree $z^3$ to combine zero polar kernel with
singleton fibers and a total inverse.  PERMEXT1 and PROMISEGATE1 treated
source-matched unique extension.  The new result occupies the missing
intersection: even-degree $z^3$ has zero polar kernel, a proper image,
off-image NO targets, and three-to-one nonzero fibers, while a planted
source-matched section still gives a charged polynomial lift.

No contradiction arises with general MQ hardness, SAT NP-completeness, or
the exact-(2,2) restricted proof-system lower bounds.  The family is a
tractable residual extension of an arbitrary source, not an algorithm for the
source itself.  Repository search found no prior report proving this exact
even-cube planted-section firewall.

\section{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN}
Root MAIN already retires total-invertible polynomial automorphisms and

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}
The phrase ``choose a source-matched output with no known inverse'' is
overstated as a lower-bound direction unless it also rules out a section
computed from the source witness.  Global inversion solves more targets than
the compiler needs.  A future report must state whether it lower-bounds a
target-only inverse, image membership, or the actual source-to-residual
section; these are distinct problems.
