\paragraph{Primary-literature preflight.}
DIAGCONV1 and SUPPORTK1 reduce the exterior problem to cyclic convolution plus triangular support, but their ordinary DFT decomposition is conditional on odd orbit length \(N\). Repeated-root coding theory treats the complementary case using generalized DFT/Mattson--Solomon coordinates with Hasse derivatives to retain multiplicity information \cite{LingNiederreiterSole,GenMS}. Strong ordinary Fourier uncertainty is unavailable over finite fields in general \cite{BorelloSole}. Schur-product theory remains the correct ambient coding object \cite{Rand}.

\paragraph{Exact SAME\_REALIZATION input.}
ORBITSLICE1 has nonzero orbit length
\[
N=p^d-1
\]
for odd prime \(p\), while the label/window algebra is binary:
\[
R_N=\mathbb F_2[z]/(z^N-1).
\]

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: semisimple Fourier is unreachable.}
Since \(p\) is odd,
\[
\boxed{N=p^d-1\text{ is even}.}
\]
Thus in characteristic two
\[
(z^N-1)'=Nz^{N-1}=0,
\]
so \(z^N-1\) is not square-free. Therefore the hypothesis \(N\) odd in the semisimple DFT portions of DIAGCONV1/SUPPORTK1 can never hold for the actual ORBITSLICE1 orbit. Their convolution and support identities remain exact, but the ordinary semisimple frequency decomposition is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED for this SAME\_REALIZATION}.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: repeated-root decomposition.}
Write
\[
N=2^\nu N_0,\qquad N_0\text{ odd},\quad \nu=v_2(N)\ge1.
\]
Frobenius gives
\[
\boxed{z^N-1=(z^{N_0}-1)^{2^\nu}.}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{R_N\cong\mathbb F_2[z]/((z^{N_0}-1)^{2^\nu}).}
\]
After extending to a splitting field \(K\),
\[
\boxed{
K[z]/(z^N-1)\cong
\prod_{\omega^{N_0}=1}K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2^\nu}).
}
\]
The correct local fibers are therefore Artin rings with nilpotent jet coordinates, not fields.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: ordinary root values lose information.}
Let \(r(z)=z^{N_0}-1\). Its class is nonzero modulo \(r^{2^\nu}\), but
\[
r(\omega)=0
\]
for every \(N_0\)-th root \(\omega\). Thus ordinary evaluation at the distinct roots is not injective on \(R_N\). Root-value matrices alone can miss nonzero nilpotent convolution directions.

\paragraph{Exact surviving operator and replacement.}
DIAGCONV1 remains valid:
\[
\widetilde\Phi:(R_N)^{m-1}\to(R_N)^{\binom{k}{2}},
\qquad
\widetilde\Phi((q_\delta))_{rt}
=\sum_{\delta=1}^{m-1}q_\delta h_{rt,\delta}.
\]
SUPPORTK1 also remains valid:
\[
\boxed{
U_L^\perp\cong S\cap\ker\widetilde\Phi,\qquad
S=\bigoplus_{\delta=1}^{m-1}P_{m-\delta}.
}
\]
For each \(\omega^{N_0}=1\), put
\[
J_\omega=K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2^\nu}).
\]
Chinese remaindering gives local \(J_\omega\)-linear maps
\[
\boxed{
\widetilde\Phi_\omega:J_\omega^{m-1}\to J_\omega^{\binom{k}{2}}.
}
\]
Their entries are multiplicity-aware truncated Taylor/Hasse jets of \(h_{rt,\delta}\), not merely ordinary values. Repeated-root coding theory provides generalized DFT/Hasse machinery for this kind of object \cite{LingNiederreiterSole,GenMS}; deriving the exact supported block-Hasse rank formula for \(\widetilde\Phi\) is \textbf{OPEN}.
