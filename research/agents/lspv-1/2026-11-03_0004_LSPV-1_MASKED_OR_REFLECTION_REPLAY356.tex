\section*{All-width signed sparse-mask certificate}
Fix \(m\ge2\) signed-literal positions \(L_1,\ldots,L_m\) over distinct source variables, with a nonempty set \(S\subseteq[m]\), \(k=|S|\), and authenticated proper nontautological original clause \(C=\bigvee_{i\in S} L_i\). Let each mask \(s_i\) be true exactly for \(i\in S\). Introduce paid term gates \(t_i\leftrightarrow(s_i\land L_i)\), and an OR chain represented by
\[
q_2\leftrightarrow(\neg t_1\land\neg t_2),\qquad
q_j\leftrightarrow(q_{j-1}\land\neg t_j)\ (3\le j\le m),\qquad
o=\neg q_m.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Authenticated paid sparse-mask unit]
There is a uniform original-source RES+VARDEF derivation of unit \(o\) with
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{paid}(C\vdash o)\le mk+5m+5}
\]
nodes, \emph{including} fresh masks, gate definitions, selector units and every resolution. No SAT oracle, free weakening, or uncharged defining clause is introduced.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take two distinct source variables \(x,y\). Pay for \(u=x\wedge y\), \(v=\neg x\wedge\neg y\), and \(z=u\wedge v\). Three strict RES steps yield unit \(\neg z\): from the named clauses derive \(\neg z\vee x\) and \(\neg z\vee\neg x\), then resolve. For every slot introduce a distinct paid gate \(a_i=z\wedge x\) and infer unit \(\neg a_i\) in one RES step. Set signed mask \(s_i=\neg a_i\) for selected positions and \(s_i=a_i\) for inactive positions; no two selector variable names are identified. Pay for \(t_i=s_i\wedge L_i\). From its defining slots and unit \(\neg a_i\), derive \(\neg L_i\vee t_i\) for selected positions, or unit \(\neg t_i\) for inactive positions, one RES each. Pay for \(m-1\) OR-prefix gates. For each selected \(i\), use their actual signed slots to derive \(\neg q_m\vee\neg t_i\) by at most \(m-2\) RES steps along the prefix chain. Resolve it with \(\neg L_i\vee t_i\), obtaining \(\neg q_m\vee\neg L_i\); resolve these \(k\) clauses successively against the authentic \(C\) to yield unit \(\neg q_m\). All pivots and intermediate clauses are non-tautological under the proper-clause hypothesis. Charged gates: \(3+m+m+(m-1)=3m+2\). Charged resolutions: at most \(3+m+m+k[(m-2)+2]=3+2m+mk\). The sum is \(mk+5m+5\).
\end{proof}
\noindent This handles arbitrary widths, sparse masks and literal signs. Normalization of repeated/tautological source clauses, active empty clauses, inactive/padded CNF rows, full source-parser correctness, and a full arbitrary-decider circuit are separate end-to-end obligations; no incorrect universal conclusion is inferred from this local theorem.

\section*{Complete finite two-label reflection-copy falsifier}

\[
g=\neg w\wedge\neg z,\qquad k=\neg g\wedge\neg w,\qquad t=e\wedge z,\qquad h=k\wedge\neg t.

\[
k,\quad\neg t,\quad\neg g,\quad\neg w,\quad(g\vee z),\quad z,\quad(t\vee\neg z),\quad t,\quad\bot.
\]
Those gate clauses are \emph{original sources} in \(U\); hence its displayed original-source refutation charges nine RES nodes, not the four gate introductions. The standalone typed physical record separately charges four DEF and nine RES for an alternate interpretation.

For the genuine UNSAT original source \(F^-=(w\vee z)\wedge(\neg w)\wedge(\neg z)\), derive a distinct signed selector as the negative of a fresh, paid, false-output gate. Introduce the four universal gates topologically as fresh VARDEF outputs. Strict RES from \(F^-\) derives the verifier unit \(h\), and the selector-pin gadget derives the rejecting unit \(e\). Append the entire nine-line universal proof \emph{without any rule-by-rule restriction}. The resulting complete original-source certificate has \textbf{eight DEF plus twenty RES, 28 charged nodes}.
