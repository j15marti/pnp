\section*{Construction and directly matched primary graph theorem}
Fix a conventional dense literal-index ordinary-CNF encoding with logarithmic-width variable fields, fully specified headers, and $n_F\le L^{O(1)}$ for an ordinary source $F$ of length $L$. Choose
\[
q=\left\lfloor\sqrt{\frac{L}{\log_2(L+2)}}\right\rfloor,\qquad v=q^2=\Theta(L/\log L)
\]
for sufficiently large $L$ (handle finite exceptions with a fixed direct parser). Use the Margulis--Gabber--Galil 8-regular undirected multigraph on $(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^2$, giving four forward undirected slots from each $(x,y)$ to
\[
(x+2y,y),\quad (x+2y+1,y),\quad
(x,y+2x),\quad(x,y+2x+1)\pmod q.
\]
Including multiplicities, there are $4v$ undirected slots and degree eight. The matching \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM} bounds the nontrivial adjacency eigenvalues from above by $5\sqrt2<8$ \cite{gabber,afp}; no spectral result is inferred from the samples. For every vertex introduce its own original graph variable $u$, distinct from $F$. For each nonloop slot $\{u,v\}$ insert the proper three-name all-positive clause $(u\vee v\vee z_e)$ with private $z_e$; for each loop slot $(u,u)$ insert $(u\vee a_e\vee b_e)$ with two private names. Call this public formula $G_q$ and define
\[
K(F)=F\wedge G_q.
\]
Every padding clause is true if its private literal is true, independently of $F$. Thus
\[
\SAT(K(F))=\SAT(F)
\]
for both labels. The padding has $4v$ clauses and $O(v)$ names, so its dense bit length is $O(v\log L)=O(L)$ and the fixed image is size $\Theta(L)$. The $j$th fixed-width padding bit is obtained by quotient/remainder to its slot, modular arithmetic on $O(\log L)$-bit integers, and its literal field, in $\operatorname{polylog}L$ time and space. The original source portion is indexed from the read-only input with charged source probes. This is a virtual random-access bit generator, not a free writable copy.

\begin{theorem}[Nearly linear touched-live width of faithful clause streams]\label{th:width}
For all sufficiently large $L$ and every ordinary $F$ of length $L$, \emph{every} faithful OPEN/CLAUSE/CLOSE stream containing exactly the ordinary clauses of $K(F)$ as written, under arbitrary legal clause ordering and legal OPEN/CLOSE placement, has graph-original touched-live width
\[
w\ \ge\ \left(1-\frac{5\sqrt2}{8}\right)\left(\frac v4-O(1)\right)
\ =\ \Omega(v)\ =\ \Omega(|K(F)|/\log |K(F)|).
\]
The claim remains true when arbitrary source clauses are interleaved.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the clause order onto the $4v$ padding slots and stop immediately after the first slot for which the set $U$ of graph vertices already mentioned has size at least $v/2$. Each slot mentions at most two graph vertices, hence $v/2\le |U|\le v/2+1$. Every multiedge crossing $U$ to its complement has its slot \emph{not yet output}, since outputting it would touch its outside endpoint. Its endpoint inside $U$ is already touched and must remain live across the cut. Each vertex is incident to at most eight slots, so at least $|\partial U|/8$ distinct touched vertices remain live.

Write the indicator vector $\mathbf1_U=(|U|/v)\mathbf1+x$, $x\perp\mathbf1$. The matched 8-regular graph bound gives
\[
|\partial U|=8|U|-\mathbf1_U^\top A\mathbf1_U
\ \ge\ (8-5\sqrt2)|U|\left(1-\frac{|U|}{v}\right)
\]
because $x^\top Ax\le5\sqrt2\|x\|^2$. Consequently
\[
w\ge \frac{|\partial U|}{8}
\ge\left(1-\frac{5\sqrt2}{8}\right)(v/4-O(1)).
\]
Loops never cross and source clauses cannot eliminate an un-emitted future incidence. Distinct private names can only increase the count. This is precisely the faithful \emph{raw representation} statement, not a semantic normalizer statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Near-linear locally indexed completeness despite large raw width]
For each fixed ordinary-SAT decider $A$ achieving simultaneous exponents $(t,s)$, $t\ge1$, in the matched FLMV model, its restriction to $\operatorname{im}K$ achieves the same pair at exponent scale. Conversely, if \emph{one fixed} exact uniform indexed-input machine solves every member of $\operatorname{im}K$ in simultaneous time $N^{t+o(1)}$ and writable/index space $N^{s+o(1)}$, then one total ordinary-SAT machine achieves $(t,s)$ at exponent scale by an indexed simulation of $K(F)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Restriction is immediate. In the reverse direction, first validate the ordinary input's dense encoding with counted $O(L\operatorname{polylog}L)$ time and $O(\log L)$ additional space, returning the declared result for malformed input. Simulate every indexed guest input probe by generating that virtual image bit from $F$ and the public MGG slot formula; all guest arithmetic, source/index probes, changed or unchanged writes, simulator control, and live guest work are charged. No output tape is reused freely as a guest input buffer.
\end{proof}
