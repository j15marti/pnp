\section{Exact signed payment}
For coherent original-variable pins $p$, let $f$ count free variables and $u$ uncovered original clauses. Set $e=2f-u$. For free $y$, let $s_y^+,s_y^-$ count occurrences in source clauses already satisfied by pinned literals; define $\eta=\sum_{y\ {\rm free}}\min(s_y^+,s_y^-)$ and $r=e-\eta$.
\begin{theorem}[All-child payment identity]
For any free source variable $x$ and value $b$, let $q=p\cup\{x=b\}$ and $s_x^b=s_x^+$ if $b=1$, otherwise $s_x^-$. For other free $y$, write
\[
\Delta_y=\min(s_y^+(q),s_y^-(q))-\min(s_y^+(p),s_y^-(p)).
\]
Then for both child signs, even if either source cube is UNSAT,
\[
e(p)-e(q)=s_x^b,\qquad
\boxed{r(p)-r(q)=s_x^b-\min(s_x^+,s_x^-)+\sum_{y\ne x,\ y\ {\rm free}}\Delta_y\ge0.}
\]
Thus $r$ is nonincreasing under every source-variable assignment. The first term is the selected-variable signed-preference payment, and each $\Delta_y$ is a newly activated overlapping-covered-sign payment.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exactly $2-s_x^b$ uncovered clauses become covered when fixing $x=b$, since each source sign occurs twice and original clauses are proper. Thus $f$ drops by one, $u$ by $2-s_x^b$, and $e$ drops by $s_x^b$. Fixing $x$ removes $\min(s_x^+,s_x^-)$ from $\eta$. Covering more original clauses cannot decrease either $s_y^+$ or $s_y^-$ for other free $y$, so their minimum rises by $\Delta_y\ge0$. Subtract the resulting $\eta$ identity from the $e$ identity. No SAT label or selected strategy is used.
\end{proof}
For authenticated edge-disjoint odd binary-cycle packing sizes $\nu(p),\nu(q)$, define $\delta(z)=r(z)-\nu(z)$. Exactly,
\[
\delta(q)-\delta(p)=\nu(p)-\nu(q)-[r(p)-r(q)].
\]
A rise in cycle-adjusted $\delta$ is precisely a loss of cycle credits exceeding the nonnegative signed payment, not an increase of $r$.
\section{Universal signed-and-cycle plateau}
\begin{theorem}[Distance-three all-sign plateau]

\[
\boxed{f=n-t,\quad u=m-2t,\quad e=r=2n/3,\quad \eta=0,\quad r_2=2t,\quad\nu=0,\quad\delta=2n/3.}
\]
Here $r_2$ is the number of residual binary clauses, and $\nu=0$ includes the maximum original-clause-edge-disjoint odd binary-cycle packing. The residual has no unit or empty original clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A source variable has at most eight distinct primal neighbors, so a closed distance-two neighborhood contains at most $1+8+8(8-1)=65$ vertices. Minimum-ID greedy removal of such neighborhoods outputs at least $\lceil n/65\rceil$ vertices at pairwise distance at least three. No source clause contains two selected variables. Each selected variable covers two clauses matching its assigned sign and shortens the other two into binaries; its clauses are disjoint from those of other selected vertices. Therefore $u=m-2t$ and $e=2(n-t)-(m-2t)=2n/3$, with no units. Every free $y$ is adjacent to at most one pinned selected variable. Source clause monotonicity implies that all covered occurrences of such a $y$ have a common polarity, so $\min(s_y^+,s_y^-)=0$ and $\eta=0$. The binary edges from each pinned selected variable number at most two; edges created by distinct selected vertices cannot share a free endpoint. Thus every binary component contains at most two edges and is acyclic. The maximum odd-cycle packing is zero, proving the remaining identities.
\end{proof}
