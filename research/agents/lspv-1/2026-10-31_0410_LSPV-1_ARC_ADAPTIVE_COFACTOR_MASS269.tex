\section{General deterministic source-encoded state}
Fix an original non-tautological CNF $F=C_1\land\cdots\land C_m$ on $n$ shared Boolean variables, each source clause having \emph{distinct} variables and width $1\le |C_j|\le w$. Source-occurrence notation $x_{i,j}$ identifies the sign-aware occurrence of the \emph{same} variable $x_i$ in original indexed clause $C_j$, not a separate variable.

A finite deterministic residual state may store
\[
z=(F,\rho,h_{\rm source},h_{\rm child},P_{\rm source},
  \Pi_{\rm guarded},\mathcal C,\mathcal H,\mathcal Q,\mathcal W),
\]
where $\rho$ is a coherent partial assignment, $P_{\rm source}$ is original signed-clause provenance, $\Pi_{\rm guarded}$ authenticated proofs, $\mathcal C$ certified class/structural descriptors, $\mathcal H$ history and failed trials, $\mathcal Q$ query/child descriptors, and $\mathcal W$ all simultaneously retained writable workspace and ancestor state. An evolving vector $\kappa(z)$ contains measured residual clause counts/lengths, certified class/rank/backdoor/interface parameters, source acquisition and policy-selection work, materialized child sizes, guest access, failures, switching, retained cache and peak space. \emph{Every parameter's discovery is charged.} This vector is not a supplied oracle.

For a clause $C_j$, define $R_j(\rho)=1$ iff no literal has yet been set TRUE by $\rho$; such a clause survives in the \emph{raw} original-source cofactor, even if it has become the empty clause. Put $R(\rho)=\sum_jR_j(\rho)$. For a surviving clause let $u_j(\rho)$ count its unassigned literals.

A deterministic source-aware binary decision tree may choose the next variable by \emph{any} function of its full charged history and the original source. Along a root-to-leaf path, variables are queried at most once, and every complete input assignment reaches exactly one leaf $\ell$ with coherent cube $\rho_\ell$. The tree can stop early on certified SAT/UNSAT or at a guest frontier. Set $d_\ell=|\rho_\ell|$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact adaptive cofactor-survival mass]\label{thm:mass}
For \emph{every} such finite deterministic complete adaptive decision tree, regardless of source-aware pivot selection and stopping behavior,
\[
\boxed{\ \sum_{\ell}2^{-d_\ell}R(\rho_\ell)
 =\sum_{j=1}^{m}2^{-|C_j|}
 +\sum_{\ell}2^{-d_\ell}
   \sum_{\substack{j\\R_j(\rho_\ell)=1}}
        (1-2^{-u_j(\rho_\ell)})\ .\ }\tag{2}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{\quad\max_\ell R(\rho_\ell)
\ge\sum_{j=1}^{m}2^{-|C_j|}
\ge m/2^{w}.\quad}\tag{3}
\]
The nonnegative remainder in (2) vanishes precisely when every surviving original clause at every positive-measure leaf has zero unassigned literals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose $a\in\{0,1\}^n$ uniformly. Coherence and completeness partition the cube, so $\Pr[a\text{ reaches }\ell]=2^{-d_\ell}$, including early stopping. If a clause $C_j$ is already satisfied under the reached cube, it cannot be false under $a$. If it survives and $u_j(\rho_\ell)$ of its distinct variables are unassigned, then, conditional on that leaf, the probability that $a$ falsifies $C_j$ is exactly $2^{-u_j(\rho_\ell)}$. Consequently
\[
\mathbb E_a R(\rho_{\ell(a)})
-\mathbb E_a \#\{j:C_j(a)=0\}
=\sum_\ell 2^{-d_\ell}
  \sum_{j:R_j(\rho_\ell)=1}(1-2^{-u_j(\rho_\ell)}).
\]
Each proper clause is falsified under uniform $a$ with probability $2^{-|C_j|}$, independently of how $\ell(a)$ was chosen. This gives (2). Every remainder is nonnegative, proving (3); the mean cannot exceed the maximum. All leaves have positive probability and $1-2^{-u}=0$ exactly when $u=0$, establishing the equality criterion.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[No all-leaf power-short \emph{raw} cofactor-only cover]\label{cor:raw}
Any complete adaptive coherent branch partition that retains every unsatisfied original clause record at each leaf, without terminal elimination, semantic quotienting or re-encoding, has a leaf with at least $m/2^w$ records. Hence it cannot make \emph{all} such raw residuals contain $o(m)$ records. For ordinary explicitly numbered source encodings with $m=\Omega(N/\log N)$ and fixed $w$, the $\Omega(m)$-byte raw record floor is larger than $N^\lambda$ for any fixed $\lambda<1$ and sufficiently large $N$.
\end{corollary}
\noindent The claim is \emph{not} about the number or length of actual children after certified simplification. A terminal contradictory source clause can be discharged with its original index; a recognized Horn/XOR/backdoor source may be solved directly; a different representation may be short. Nor does (3) imply that an unrestricted SAT machine must actually visit or materialize the indicated leaf. The theorem is a direct sharpened accounting of classical uniform clause-falsification probability, not a novel computational hardness theorem.

\section{Exact SAT/UNSAT semantics, proof scope and physical children}
If any original clause becomes empty under $\rho$, it is a \emph{source-indexed UNSAT terminal for that cube only}; exporting the finding globally requires the assumption-blocking clause $B_\rho$. If every original clause has an assigned TRUE literal, the cube is a \emph{SAT terminal} and every completion is a source witness. Otherwise a physical ordinary CNF child is obtained by removing already satisfied source clauses and false assigned literals, retaining original variable indices; its ASCII DIMACS header, each literal and each final zero are written and parsed. Its SAT answer equals extendibility of $F$ under $\rho$. At an internal pivot $x$,
\[
\SAT(F|_\rho)
=\SAT(F|_{\rho,x=0})\lor \SAT(F|_{\rho,x=1}),
\quad
\neg\SAT(F|_\rho)
=\neg\SAT(F|_{\rho,x=0})\land\neg\SAT(F|_{\rho,x=1}).\tag{4}
\]
The second equality is the indispensable UNSAT polarity. Failure of both globally complete complement endpoints never substitutes for proof of both \emph{branch residuals}.

For $(x\lor A)$ and $(\neg x\lor B)$, the non-tautological resolvent $A\lor B$ forbids exactly the coherent cube falsifying all its literals, subject to additional stronger source deductions. The experiment stores the original clause indices, pivot and blocker assignments, and verifies that no complete source model extends any asserted blocker. This is established resolution, not a new proof rule.
