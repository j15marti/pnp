\section*{Exact gate reconstruction}
For $z=a\wedge b$, use three standard clauses, with each binary clause padded by a fresh $q$ as $(D\vee q)\wedge(D\vee\neg q)$.  Thus AND costs five proper triples and two padding variables.  XOR costs four proper triples and no padding; equality $z=y$ costs four proper triples and two padding variables.  Each source violation bit uses two AND gates.  The anchored network has $3m-1$ ANDs, $2m-1$ XORs, $m$ feedback inputs, and $8m-2$ padding bits, giving
\[
(3m-1)+(2m-1)+m+(8m-2)=14m-4,
\]
and $5(3m-1)+4(2m-1)+4m=27m-9$ clauses.  The closing shear adds one AND and one XOR, giving $14m$ and $27m$.  Gate outputs are unique given source and feedback bits; padding bits and the $m$ feedback bits remain free, yielding multipliers $2^{9m-2}$ and $2^{9m}$ on satisfying source assignments.

\section*{All-field PC/PCR replay}
Let $v_i$ be the degree-three polynomial that is $1$ exactly when source clause $C_i$ is false.  Over an arbitrary field use
\[
\operatorname{AND}(a,b)=ab,\qquad
\operatorname{XOR}(a,b)=a+b-2ab.
\]
Set all feedback and padding variables to zero, recursively substitute gate outputs, and map PCR complements to $1-\sigma(z)$.  Anchored changed bits have degree at most $7$; the wraparound changed bit has degree at most $11$.  In characteristic two the $-2ab$ term vanishes and the observed maxima improve to $4$ and $5$, so there is no field-two escape from the upper bounds.

Every substituted gate-clause polynomial vanishes on the Boolean cube and therefore lies in the source Boolean ideal; multilinear reduction provides a degree-preserving ordinary-ring certificate.  A substituted target Boolean axiom has degree at most $2q$.  Recursively, each changed bit has an explicit certificate $h_i=\sum_j A_{ij}v_j$; hence every closing equality image lies in the source clause ideal with degree at most $q$.  For a target refutation of degree $D\geq3$, all repairs fit within $qD$.  Addition is preserved directly.  A multiplication step maps to multiplication by the polynomial $\sigma(z)$; expanding it into monomials and replaying each monomial by legal variable multiplications gives an ordinary PC derivation (and analogously PCR).  Consequently, for every field,
\[
 \operatorname{Deg}_{\rm PC/PCR}(F)\leq

and, for $m\geq2$,

These are degree statements, not shortest-size or machine-time statements.

\section*{Boundary defect and repair}
For $m=1$, two-shear defines $t_0=v_0$ and $h_0=t_0\oplus(a_0\wedge t_0)$.  If $v_0=a_0=1$, then $h_0=0$, so a violated source clause has a fixed point.  The bounded audit found eight such assignment/source-polarity controls.  For $m\geq2$, the unchanged outputs $h_i=t_i$ for $i\geq1$ prevent this self-cancellation, as in the archived proof.  Thus the unrestricted $m\geq1$ statement is \status{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}; the $m\geq2$ theorem is retained.  A uniform reduction can duplicate a one-clause input before applying two-shear, but that repaired preprocessor must be stated explicitly.
