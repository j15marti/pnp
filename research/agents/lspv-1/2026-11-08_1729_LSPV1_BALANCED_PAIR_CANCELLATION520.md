## Fixed source family, encodings, and proof-model ledger

Let G=(V,E) be any connected simple cubic graph with even h=|V| and e=3h/2, orient each edge once, and let b in F3^V satisfy sum_v b_v != 0. For each edge e create **three original onehot indicators** x[e,a] for a=0,1,2. Edge onehot source clauses are the one positive ternary clause and three distinct negative binary clauses. At every vertex, include one negative signed ternary source clause for each of the 18 forbidden triples of incident edge labels whose signed mod3 sum differs from b_v. Deduplicate unordered uniqueness pairs: original **canonical clause-set** has 9h/2 old variables, 24h clauses, of which 9h/2 are binary. Replace each binary B with (B OR z) and (B OR NOT z) for one globally fresh ORIGINAL z. The resulting **proper signed strict 3-CNF** has 9h/2+1 variables, 57h/2 clauses and source length N=Theta(h log h). Either restriction of z recovers the original CNF.

Original-source RES derives each old binary B from its two padded sources on z, paying 9h/2 ordinary inference nodes. All old source ternary clauses remain free axioms. Uniform conservative onehot mod3 adders use constant numbers of **paid globally fresh signed two-input Boolean AND gate definitions**, each with its three paid-definition-attributed facets, and ordinary paid two-parent Resolution. ## Theorem 520.1 (balanced pair-elimination upper bound; PROVED/RESTRICTED)

For the declared **ER2** Boolean gate/Resolution calculus there exists a uniform source-authenticated acyclic shared-DAG proof schema on the preceding strict sources satisfying

$$
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{ER2}}(F^z_{G,b})=O(h\log h),\qquad |\mathrm{encoded\ proof}|=O(h\log^2 h).}
$$

This is an **upper bound** with an unspecified fixed macro constant, not an exact optimum. It improves LSPV-1/519's O(h^3) declared-ER2 upper bound, and rules out this *published family* as a superpolynomial lower-bound witness in any fixed calculus that can authentically simulate the whole signed-ER2 fragment with polynomial overhead. No such simulation is yet proved into actual historical R_mix341.

### Proof

**A. Balanced original-incidence tree, source charge.** Place the 3h signed incidence group elements as leaves of a binary tree T. Group the three occurrences belonging to each vertex into a fixed two-adder subtree and combine these h vertex roots in a balanced binary tree. Height is at most ceil(log2 h)+2. Each original edge variable occurs once with sign + and once with sign -. Each vertex's 18 forbidden source clauses plus its edge-onehot sources force its three-leaf sum to the known constant b_v. The fixed local gate identities and a bounded-support source-indexed Resolution saturation macro derive the onehot clauses asserting this equality. Propagating vertex equalities through the h-vertex balanced addition tree, with paid fixed-support congruence macros, derives the root onehot value B=sum_v b_v !=0 in O(h) work.

**B. One arbitrary opposite pair per stage, without sorting.** At stage j, pick an unremoved edge e=(u,v). In the current tree all previously removed occurrences have been replaced by explicit group-zero leaves. The two occurrences of e are in two different child subtrees of their lowest common ancestor w. Denote the encoded signed value at the first leaf by a and the second by -a. Write P and Q for the respective sums of all *other* leaves in those two child subtrees. The two child outputs before deletion can be derived to equal P+a and Q-a. After replacing precisely these two leaves by paid group zeros and rebuilding their ancestor paths with **globally fresh paid gates**, the new child outputs equal P and Q. Therefore, in F3,

$$
(P+a)+(Q-a)=P+Q.
$$

The middle identity involves only **three group inputs P,Q,a**, with onehot and definitional wires of bounded total Boolean support. It has a fixed truth-table certificate, hence a fixed finite ordinary Resolution derivation from the signed-AND gate facets, previously proved context clauses, and onehot input clauses. The context P (respectively Q) is constructed from sibling aggregate outputs along the path from the leaf to w, requiring O(log h) paid group additions. Associativity/commutativity identities locally establish the child equalities by induction along both paths, with O(log h) additional fixed-support Resolution macros. Above w, the old and new root equality is propagated through O(log h) unchanged sibling aggregates by fixed-size congruence macros. These are ordinary unconditional implications, not a proposed split/discharge rule. All reused old gates are referenced and charged once. This yields **O(log h)** fresh gates plus paid Resolution nodes per removed edge.

**C. Finish.** There are e=3h/2 original edges. After e cancellations every incidence leaf is the same paid neutral zero, and fixed local onehot-adder consequences derive the last root equals zero in O(h) steps. Together with the independently source-derived old root equals nonzero B, ordinary RES derives the empty clause. Therefore paid work is O(h)+e O(log h)=O(h log h). Each gate/output/premise/source index has O(log h) encoded bits, so the fully materialized source-authenticated proof DAG has O(h log^2 h) bits. All paid rules are from declared ER2 and every local Boolean fact has a constant-size *syntactic Resolution* macro, not a global SAT/equivalence oracle.

**D. Uniformity and source/assumption controls.** The proof generator needs only G, b and source indices. Each macro schema is a fixed finite propositional deduction on a bounded number of onehot group wires; exhaustive finite implication closure can be precompiled *once from the fixed gate basis* and then instantiated with fully paid references. No machine-specific solver code, nonuniform per-instance optimizer, unsound zero axiom, free weakening or uncharged branch lemma is used. Acyclic dependency order uses source facets first, then older tree values, then new stage contexts and equalities. Constructed proof existence is separate from any external checking cost. This is not a generic R_mix341 normalization: the historical accepted-byte grammar for DEF port polarity, repeated signed inputs, exported/private wires, source facets and LOCAL-CONVERT is still OPEN.

### Important qualification on the fixed-support macro

The exact numeric O(1) bound per Boolean inference macro follows from finite implicate completeness of ordinary Resolution over at most a fixed K Boolean variables. If a local relation H logically implies a clause C, Resolution derives C or a **subclause** of C via at most a fixed finite number of non-tautological clauses; subclauses must be carried through later deductions by monotone strengthening, not silently weakened back to C. The identity/equality is represented by a fixed number of clausal facets. All source onehot axioms are available as already sourced or recovered clauses; newly introduced gate facets are attributed to their paid conservative DEF. This semantic-to-syntactic completeness argument certifies existence of a finite macro library, **not** an actual byte-level historical checker execution.
