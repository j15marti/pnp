## Exact obstruction and four falsifiers

We test whether an exact one-variable boundary feasibility relation is universally cheap inside the sole NP-complete target, duplicate-free proper monotone 3-SAT-(2,2). ### Exact theorem, including quantifiers

Let C be the language of well-formed Döcker originals and let SAT(F) be its 0/1 satisfiability predicate. Define the *total* function SUP1(H,b) to output INVALID for malformed sources or invalid b, and otherwise output the two Boolean predicates

\[
\rho_H(v)=1[\exists a\models H\ \text{with}\ a_b=v],\quad v\in\{0,1\}.
\]

For **every** F in C, form the canonical source H(F)=F ⊔ G6, renaming G6's six variables above F's n original variables, and take b=n+1. Both components have disjoint variable names; thus H has n+6 variables, m+8 clauses, exact 2P/2N quotas, no duplicate clauses, and source length Theta(N) in the declared fixed-width indexed binary encoding. Then

\[
\boxed{\rho_{H(F)}(0)=\rho_{H(F)}(1)=SAT(F)}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\#SAT(H(F)\mid b=v)=9\,\#SAT(F)\qquad(v=0,1)}.
\]

**Proof.** Every source assignment of F independently combines with exactly nine gadget satisfying assignments in either b-fiber. There are no cross-component occurrences, preserving source canonicality. An all-input polynomial SUP1 machine, even promised the equal-fiber image, decides SAT(F) by grammar-checking F, producing H(F) with charged source/bit translation, and reading either output bit. Döcker's canonical SAT language is NP-complete. Conversely if P=NP, grammar checking is polynomial and the two pinned ordinary-CNF residual satisfiability queries are decidable in polynomial time. Therefore the total function **SUP1 is in FP if and only if P=NP**. This is a *conditional* function-complexity equivalence, not an unconditional superpolynomial lower bound. A root-only decider need not compute SUP1 or retain assignments.

The result also warns that width-one relation size, equality of its two bits, or width-zero connected-component interfaces do **not** establish a polynomial-time acquisition step on arbitrary canonical originals. All input source work, policy failures, memoization, matrix/proof work and guest operations remain payable.
