\section{Claim card and exact clean core}

Let $K=\Hsigned$ be the duplicate-free proper sign-monochromatic 3-CNF
family in which every variable appears exactly twice positively and twice
negatively.  The encoding is canonical and complete; $N=|F|$ is encoded bit
length.  D\"ocker proves that SAT restricted to this exact family is
NP-complete \cite{Docker19}.  Let $T_A(F)$ and $S_A(F)$ be time and peak work
space of one total deterministic uniform exact SAT decider $A$ on a standard
read-only-input ordinary-machine realization.

The two superficially parallel targets are
\begin{align}
 \mathsf{WC}_T(K):\quad&
 \forall A\;\forall d\;\forall N_0\;\exists F\in K:\
 N\ge N_0\land T_A(F)>N^d,                                  \tag{1}\\
 \mathsf{WC}_S(K):\quad&
 \forall A\;\forall d\;\forall N_0\;\exists F\in K:\
 N\ge N_0\land S_A(F)>N^d.                                  \tag{2}
\end{align}
The candidate under test was that both coordinates, or a generic
``state/exposure'' surrogate, can serve as the same universal obstruction.

\section{Exact resource-coordinate theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{CLEANCORE-RESOURCE-COORDINATE1}]
For $K=\Hsigned$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\mathsf{WC}_T(K)\Longleftrightarrow\SAT\notin\mathsf P
\Longleftrightarrow P\ne NP$.
\item $\mathsf{WC}_S(K)$ is false unconditionally.
\item No lower bound larger than one output bit follows merely from SAT's
external decision semantics.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For (i), if (1) holds, a polynomial SAT decider contradicts it by choosing
$d$ above the decider's clock exponent.  Conversely, if (1) fails, one total
exact decider $A$ has a polynomial time bound on every sufficiently long
$F\in K$.  Compose $A$ with D\"ocker's polynomial many-one reduction from
SAT to $K$.  Reduction length is polynomial, and the finitely many outputs
below the threshold contribute only a constant maximum running time because
$A$ is total.  The composition decides SAT in polynomial time.  This is the
exact biconditional independently stated in COREEQUIV1.

For (ii), use the direct exhaustive decider $E$.  On input $F$, retain one
$n$-bit assignment counter, scan the clauses, and reuse logarithmic indices
and a constant number of flags.  Increment the assignment until a satisfying
one is found or all $2^n$ assignments are exhausted.  Because $n\le N$,
\[
 S_E(F)=O(n+\log N)=O(N)                                    \tag{3}
\]
on every CNF, including every $F\in K$.  Taking $d=2$ and sufficiently large
$N_0$ negates (2).  The exponential time of $E$ is irrelevant to this
workspace counterexample.

For (iii), $E$ and every exact decision implementation may erase reusable
work tapes and return only the membership bit.  Internal traces can be
inflated or rewritten by COPY, DEAD, DELAY, INTERP and RECOMP wrappers while
the external result remains one bit.  Thus a larger state obligation needs a
declared online, streaming, communication, proof or representation interface;
it is not a consequence of decision semantics alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Simultaneous endpoint guard]
Neither (1) alone nor any semantic-state count places one machine in an FLMV
forbidden simultaneous time--space region.  The time target is already the
separation; the space target is false; a simultaneous conclusion needs a
separate SAME\_REALIZATION compiler with both coordinates charged.
\end{corollary}

\section{Hostile controls, wrappers, and PQ ledger}

Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin and bounded-treewidth families are tractable
shortcuts; paired-block products are recognizable all-YES islands.  They do
not refute (1), whose negation requires one decider polynomial on the entire
NP-hard $K$.  They do refute attempts to infer hard decision or large memory
from model count, parity structure, interface profiles or independent-product
volume.  Padding, redundant clauses and alternate encodings change $N$ and
must be charged; none defeats the linear-space upper bound in the complete
encoding.

Schur squares, quadratic relation ranks, Hilbert functions, syzygies and
graded Betti numbers are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} absent a forced algebraic
representation; the structure may instead support a shortcut.  Collision
entropy is exact only for a declared map.  LWE-style extraction,
special-soundness forks and collapse-binding are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}
routes needing a construction-specific unconditional bridge.  Hash and
random-oracle assumptions are inadmissible for (1).
