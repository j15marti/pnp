\section*{Fixed definitions and quantifiers}
Preserve the unchanged binary-premise unit-cost $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ and source-independent VARDEF convention: authenticated original signed CNF source clauses have zero cost; binary RES, local AND3 of Boolean factors supported on at most three \emph{original variables}, authenticated XOR, LOCAL-CONVERT and charged conservative two-input signed VARDEF are permitted. A gate $q\leftrightarrow(a\wedge b)$ has fresh output and two signed distinct-ID inputs, and its three clause slots are $(\neg q\vee a)$, $(\neg q\vee b)$, $(q\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)$. One gate node pays for all three slots. No gates introduce an instance-specific answer axiom, free semantic SAT test, or implicit multi-premise deduction. The proof can adapt, share nodes, grow, introduce further conservative auxiliaries and select its optimal legal derivation without paying discovery cost.

\[
W_S(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_S\bot}\sum_{\text{distinct derived }v\in\pi}1,\quad
\overline W_S(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ {\rm valid\ UNSAT}\\|\langle F\rangle|\le N}}W_S(F).
\]

\section*{Universal ER$_2$ charged padding sandwich}
Fix any valid UNSAT signed CNF $F$ and an acyclic list $D$ of $g$ conservative source-independent two-input VARDEF gates, each with output fresh relative to $F$ and preceding gates, and inputs referring only to distinct original variables or earlier gate outputs. Write $F^D$ for the \emph{ordinary-source} CNF obtained by appending all $3g$ named gate-definition clauses to the original source $F$. In $F^D$, those gate clauses are free original \emph{sources}, not paid derivation steps.

\begin{theorem}[All-instance ER$_2$ source-padding sandwich]
For every such $F,D$, including arbitrary shared and internally extended optimal ER$_2$ refutations,
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(F)-g\le W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(F^D)\le W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(F).}\tag{1}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the right inequality, take a shortest proof of $F$ and alpha-rename all its internal fresh gate variables away from the new original names used by $D$. All old source clauses are present in $F^D$, so the identical RES+VARDEF proof works, ignoring the appended clauses.

For the left inequality, take any ER$_2$ proof of $F^D$. Working over authentic $F$ sources only, create and pay for the $g$ source-independent gates in their acyclic order. Replace every occurrence of an appended gate-source clause in the old proof by that paid gate node's named defining-clause slot. Reuse the remaining old sources and every RES pivot exactly. Injectively rename any old internal gate variables to avoid collisions. This is legal in the type-blind-with-respect-to-original-status ER$_2$ fragment, and increases the number of distinct paid nodes by at most $g$, even with sharing. Minimize over padded proofs. The compiler is polynomial in input/proof \emph{evidence length}, but its construction work is not charged to the free-minimum quantity $W$. \end{proof}

If $g(N)\le N^{O(1)}$, usual canonically renumbered ASCII DIMACS expands input length by $O(g\log(n+g+2))$, a polynomial. Therefore any hypothetical growing family having superpolynomial $W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}$ remains hard after this exact conservative padding by the \emph{lower} inequality. No such hard mixed family is established here; (1) does not apply to arbitrary clause-replacement gadgets or claim mixed-calculus lower-bound transport.

\section*{Exact full-mixed typing firewall}
The converse in (1) is \emph{not proved for all mixed proofs}. For $H_2$ pad with $q\leftrightarrow(x_1\wedge x_2)$. When $q$ is an \emph{original} variable of the padded source, a legal AND3 node may exactly combine the source factors represented by $(\neg q\vee x_1)$ and $(\neg q\vee x_2)$, whose combined support is $\{q,x_1,x_2\}$, three originals. After replacing those two source clauses by a paid VARDEF gate on $H_2$, $q$ is an \emph{auxiliary}, not an original variable; the verbatim same AND3 node is not licensed by the original-support restriction. Thus a naive mixed rule-by-rule replay is \textbf{FALSIFIED as a compiler}.
