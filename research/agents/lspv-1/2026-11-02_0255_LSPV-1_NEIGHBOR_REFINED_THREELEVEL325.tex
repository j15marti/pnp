\begin{lemma}[First-batch degree ceiling]
For every valid canonical original $F$ and every source-primal independent set $I$, exact DP elimination gives a noncanonical $F_I=\exists_I F$ whose primal graph has maximum degree at most $20$, width at most $4$, and at most four occurrences per surviving variable of either polarity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every original name $y$ occurs in four ternary source clauses and has at most eight primal neighbors. Let $r$ of those neighbors be selected pivots; source-clause independence implies $r\le4$. An eliminated pivot $x$ has two positive and two negative ORIGINAL clauses. If $y$ belongs to $a\ge0$ positive and $b\ge0$ negative $x$ clauses, $a+b\ge1$ and $a,b\le2$. A new neighbor of $y$ after resolution through $x$ must occur in an opposite-polarity $x$-parent not already containing $y$. For $a>0,b=0$ or $a=0,b>0$ there are at most two such parents, each containing at most two other names. For $a,b>0$ there are at most $(2-a)+(2-b)\le2$ such parents. Therefore each pivot introduces at most four previously absent neighbors. The $r$ old pivot neighbors disappear, so $\deg_{F_I}(y)\le8-r+4r=8+3r\le20$. Independent pivots have disjoint original parent clauses, so their resolutions commute. The previously proved polarity-squared lemma supplies width four and at most four occurrences per sign. QED.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Valid sharpness fixture]
A connected proper duplicate-free canonical source on 30 original names and 40 original clauses, explicitly serialized in the published compact Python reproducer, admits the maximal primal-independent batch $I=\{3,9,13,16,22,26\}$. The original $y=2$ has degree 8 and its exact DP residual has degree 20.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Independent source-grammar verification checks two occurrences per sign, original clause uniqueness and distinct same-sign source triples. All displayed pivots are pairwise source-primal nonadjacent and the batch is maximal. Four neighbors of $y=2$, namely $13,16,22,26$, disappear and four new distinct neighbors arise from each. Hence $\deg_{F_I}(2)=8-4+16=20$. Full independent DP recomputation gives 36 projected ordinary clauses and 128 literal occurrences. This proves sharpness for SOME maximal independent selector, not necessarily for the fixed greedy order.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Uniform charged three-stage upper bound]

\]
Combined peak space is polynomial, not necessarily below the FLMV half-power threshold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Authenticate every original clause and occurrence. Apply ascending-name maximal source-primal independent-set DP exactly three times. At the root $p_0^+=p_0^-=2$, width $3$ and degree at most $8$, so $v_1\le(8/9)n$. The new lemma gives degree $20$ and hence $v_2\le(20/21)v_1$. Thus $v_3\le(8/9)(20/21)(160/161)n=(25600/30429)n$. Fixed-round resolution, selectors, acquisition, source/index/head, tautology/dedup, proof provenance, caching and intermediate serialization incur polynomial bit costs and polynomial simultaneous space, because all per-name clause occurrence and width ceilings depend only on the fixed three phases. Exhaustively evaluate the final $2^{v_3}$ surviving assignments. Existential DP is continuation-exact, so this decides SAT and UNSAT. No canonical hereditary child, witness-recovery requirement or free best-policy oracle is used. QED.
\end{proof}
