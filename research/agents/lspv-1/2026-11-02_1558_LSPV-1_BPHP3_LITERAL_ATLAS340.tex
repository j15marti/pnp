\section{Strict signed three-CNF chain}
For $n=2^\ell\ge4$ and $m=n+1$, let $b_{it}$ denote the $t$th Boolean hole-label bit of pigeon $i$. For each $0\le i<j<m$ and $z\in\{0,1\}^\ell$, the original pigeon collision-forbidding clause is
\[
C_{ijz}=\bigvee_{t<\ell}[b_{it}\ne z_t]\ \lor\ \bigvee_{t<\ell}[b_{jt}\ne z_t],
\]
with $[b\ne0]$ the positive literal $b$ and $[b\ne1]$ the negative literal $\neg b$. The source is UNSAT by pigeonhole counting. For each width-$k=2\ell$ clause with ordered signed literals $(a_1,\ldots,a_k)$, choose $k-3$ \emph{private fresh} variables $y_1,\ldots,y_{k-3}$ and substitute
\[
(a_1\lor a_2\lor y_1)\land
\bigwedge_{r=1}^{k-4}(\neg y_r\lor a_{r+2}\lor y_{r+1})
\land(\neg y_{k-3}\lor a_{k-1}\lor a_k).
\]
Every resulting clause is proper strict three-CNF. Write $G_n$ for the resulting formula. Exact counts:
\[
M=n\binom{n+1}{2},\quad
M_3=M(2\ell-2),\quad
V_3=(n+1)\ell+M(2\ell-3).
\]
The private chain is satisfiable under a fixed original assignment iff its original disjunction is true. If all original literals are false, the chain is $y_1\land(\neg y_1\lor y_2)\land\cdots\land\neg y_{k-3}$, unsatisfiable. Otherwise set each $y_j$ true iff the first $j+1$ source literals are false; this satisfies the chain.

\begin{theorem}[Every literal-exact 3-CNF source clause is essential]
For all $n=2^\ell\ge4$, $G_n$ is clause-minimally UNSAT; every individual literal-occurrence sign is sensitive. In particular, deleting one three-clause or flipping the sign of any one literal-occurrence makes the full result SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pigeonhole counting and chain equisatisfiability show UNSAT. For any chain clause $D$ of parent $C_{ijz}$, place pigeons $i,j$ at the same label $z$ and biject the other $n-1$ pigeons with the remaining $n-1$ labels. This falsifies exactly the parent $C_{ijz}$ and satisfies every other original clause, with satisfying private chain extensions. The sole false parent's chain has every $a_r=0$. To make its $r$th chain clause (zero-based, $0\le r\le k-3$) false and every other chain clause true, set its first $r$ auxiliary variables to true and all remaining auxiliary variables to false. Thus $D$ has a source-deletion witness. Every literal in $D$ is false under this witness; flipping any one literal's sign satisfies $D$ while retaining all other clauses.
\end{proof}
