\section*{WRAPPERCHARGE1: exact hash-filter lemma}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, restricted.} A child-result cache filtered by a hash and additionally guarded by exact canonical-string equality is sound, because string equality entails identical SAT status. Hash equality alone is not sound.
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, direct implementation.} A wrapper that first calls a hypothetical fixed SAT decider A on the full parent has time at least T_A(N). Polynomial padding A_d with T_{A_d}(N) >= N^d falsifies any universal claimed bound t' <= max(r,mu+gamma d) with gamma<1 for sufficiently large d.
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL.} Enumeration of 256 subsets of eight signed ternary clauses found 120 ordered, hash-equal (two-bit SHA-derived) pairs with different SAT status, and 256 exact-equal pairs that agree.
\textbf{OPEN.} All-input, hereditary fully charged resource contraction. Source-retained replay and exact cache reuse remain partial-domain optimizations.
