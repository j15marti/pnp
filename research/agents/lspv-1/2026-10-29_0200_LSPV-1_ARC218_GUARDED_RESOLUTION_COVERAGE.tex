\section{PROPOSE: one missing arrow and four falsifiable mechanisms}
The current missing theorem is a uniform polynomial-time, all-input successor for SAT and UNSAT, with physical output, hereditary closure, and source-authenticated certificates, or a same-machine resource contradiction. We deliberately target a smaller dependency: can a \emph{local} guard-to-source-clause derivation be authenticated without a SAT call, and does that imply universally affordable UNSAT refutations? Before search or coding, mechanisms M1--M4 were preregistered in \path{PROPOSE.txt}.
\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{M1, refuted as universal hereditary rank.} Projected affine nullity alone need not become logarithmic on arbitrary 3SAT; the exact ARC213 gadget has nullity $2m$. A language-changing elimination also need not preserve 2CNF (ARC216).
\item[]\textbf{M2, falsified.} The explicit all-YES witness of an \emph{unpinned} guarded mixed formula does not decide whether a satisfying extension with $g=0$ exists. ARC217 reduces ordinary SAT to that extension question.
\item[]\textbf{M3, partly resolved.} A source clause admits five authenticated constant-width resolution steps from its pinned guarded gadget; the whole source UNSAT refutation is \emph{not} thereby discovered. Universal polynomial-size or affordable global proofs have complexity-class implications.
\item[]\textbf{M4, not a constructive theorem.} A decreasing polynomial-value rank controls runtime only when full states, every transition, terminal checking and source access have uniform polynomial bounds. A succinct virtual child with uncharged decoding does not satisfy the hypothesis.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD: local guard clause and constant-width proof}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m C_j$ be a proper signed 3CNF, $C_j=(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$. The ARC217 uniform guarded image $H_F$ introduces $g$ and fresh $y_{j1},y_{j2},y_{j3},t_j$ for each clause, imposing
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gadget}
\bigwedge_{i=1}^3(\ell_{ji}\vee y_{ji}),\qquad
 y_{j1}\oplus y_{j2}\oplus t_j=0,\qquad
 t_j\oplus y_{j3}\oplus g=0.
\end{equation}
Encode each even three-variable parity equation as its four forbidding ternary clauses. In the source-authenticated \emph{pinned} formula $P_F:=H_F\wedge\neg g$, the $m$ gadgets have $3m+8m+1=11m+1$ CNF axioms; pin $\neg g$ is common. Every axiom receives its source-clause index and row index.
\begin{lemma}[Five-step source-clause derivation]\label{lem:five}
For every signed proper original clause $C_j$, resolution derives $C_j$ from the CNF clauses of its own gadget and the global unit $\neg g$ with \emph{exactly five explicit inferences}, all clauses of width at most three. No SAT call, semantic truth table, or other source clause is used.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppress the subscript $j$ and write $\ell_i,y_i,t$. The even-parity CNF for $t\oplus y_3\oplus g=0$ contains $(\neg y_3\vee t\vee g)$; the even-parity CNF for $y_1\oplus y_2\oplus t=0$ contains $(\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2\vee\neg t)$. All displayed steps are ordinary resolution:
\begin{align*}
(\neg y_3\vee t\vee g),\;(\neg g) &\ \vdash_{g}\ (\neg y_3\vee t), \\
(\neg y_3\vee t),\;(\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2\vee\neg t)&\ \vdash_t\ (\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2\vee\neg y_3),\\
(\ell_3\vee y_3),\;(\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2\vee\neg y_3)&\ \vdash_{y_3}\ (\ell_3\vee\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2),\\
(\ell_2\vee y_2),\;(\ell_3\vee\neg y_1\vee\neg y_2)&\ \vdash_{y_2}\ (\ell_2\vee\ell_3\vee\neg y_1),\\
(\ell_1\vee y_1),\;(\ell_2\vee\ell_3\vee\neg y_1)&\ \vdash_{y_1}\ (\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)=C_j.
\end{align*}
All literals remain actual original source literals, including signs. Every parent is either a source-indexed parity/binary axiom, the source-independent guard pin, or an earlier derived clause. In particular, the identity $C_j$ is not supplied by a nonlocal compatibility oracle.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Conditional authenticated resolution-proof lift]\label{prop:lift}
Suppose an ordinary resolution refutation of $F$ has $L$ resolution inferences and $B$ physically written literal occurrences. Then a deterministic uniform compiler given \emph{both $F$ and that original refutation} constructs a sound source-identified resolution refutation of $P_F$ using exactly $5m+L$ resolution inferences, $11m+1$ CNF axioms, and physical proof size polynomial in $|F|+B+L$. It does \emph{not} construct the source refutation or a SAT decider from $F$ alone.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Compile all $11m+1$ pinned axioms and append the five-step derivation of Lemma~\ref{lem:five} for each source clause. Map every original resolution proof's source axiom to the derived copy of that exact clause, and replay its $L$ inferences with their original ordered parent references and pivot literals. The last derived clause is empty, by the source proof. This uses one finite program for every $F$ and both answer classes; it has no free source-instance verdict. The initial clauses and all inference parents, pivots and conclusions have indices of $O(\log(m+L+2))$ bits and are physically emitted. The source refutation's complete clauses are likewise copied, so the cost cannot be expressed using $L$ alone if its width is large.
\end{proof}

\section{The separate global-coverage barrier}
\begin{theorem}[Guarded promise proof-size and acquisition implications]\label{thm:cover}
Let $\mathcal P=\{P_F:F\text{ is a proper }3\mathrm{CNF}\}$ be the syntactically recognizable constructed class. Suppose $V(P_F,\pi)$ runs in deterministic polynomial time in its full physically encoded input and is \emph{sound} whenever it accepts a purported UNSAT certificate. Fix a polynomial $p$.
\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{(i) Polynomial-size coverage.} If for every UNSAT $F$ there exists $\pi$, $|\pi|\le p(|P_F|)$, accepted by $V$, then $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$.
\item[]\textbf{(ii) Affordable discovery.} If a fixed deterministic procedure emits such a checkable $\pi$ on every UNSAT $F$ within $p(|P_F|)$ computation steps (it may loop or fail on SAT inputs), then $P=NP$.
\end{description}
These are implications, not proven impossibility claims or unconditional separations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pointwise ARC217 identity implies $P_F$ is UNSAT if and only if $F$ is UNSAT: under $g=0$, each gadget can be extended exactly when its original clause is true, and distinct gadgets have disjoint private variables. The map is polynomial and injects no answer label. In (i), a nondeterministic verifier computes $P_F$, guesses the bounded $\pi$ and checks $V$; completeness holds on all UNSAT $F$, and soundness excludes accepting SAT $F$. Hence $3\mathrm{UNSAT}\in NP$, so $\mathrm{coNP}\subseteq NP$ and by complement $NP=\mathrm{coNP}$. This specializes the classical proof-system observation~\cite{cook1979}. In (ii), truncate the asserted fixed generator after the uniform polynomial bound, accept UNSAT precisely when it produces a certificate accepted by $V$, and otherwise accept SAT. The time cap is valid on every UNSAT input; soundness prevents a false UNSAT result on SAT inputs. This is a total deterministic polynomial-time decider for 3SAT, so $P=NP$.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}[What the theorem does and does not say]
A polynomial source-authenticated \emph{local} derivation of every $C_j$ is proved; a uniformly short source \emph{global} UNSAT refutation is not. Even the existence of a polynomial-bounded checked proof system for every source UNSAT formula is a major open possibility equivalent to $NP=coNP$. There is no unconditional lower bound for all proof systems here. The theorem concerns the pinned $g=0$ instances; the unpinned $H_F$ is always SAT, so a correct answer-only compressor can output a fixed YES without learning $F$'s verdict. A lower bound for resolution on $F$ does not automatically transport to $P_F$: added variables can shorten proofs, and Proposition~\ref{prop:lift} gives an \emph{upper} bound only.
\end{remark}

\section{Finite hostile controls and the resource ledger}
The standalone Python verifier \path{ARC218_guarded_resolution_lift.py} constructs proof axioms from numbered original source clauses, checks every initial axiom's source and parity-row identity, and independently replays every actual parent/pivot/resolvent. It generated five local steps for each of 1024 clause occurrences across all 256 subsets of eight proper signed 3-clauses, validating 5120 local resolution steps. It also constructed a complete original-source refutation for the eight-clause UNSAT source, added the five-step lift for all eight clauses, and checked a pinned proof with 89 numbered axioms and 47 resolution inferences, ending at the empty clause. Its $47$ steps comprise $40$ local and $7$ global steps. It rejected mutated axiom, source-index, and pivot controls. The run tested 2048 source assignments, 16 local guard truth rows, and 160 seeded mixed SAT/UNSAT random source formulas. There is no theorem-extraction from these finite tests.

Let $P$ be the \emph{physically serialized} verification input length, including the original source, pinned gadget, complete proof, source labels, all indices, pivots, caches and final output. For the actual code's proof format, a deterministic checker can be implemented with time $P^{O(1)}$ on each of: \emph{single shared read/write tape} (input cells included in footprint), \emph{one read-only input head plus fixed work tapes}, \emph{two fully writable tapes}, \emph{one fixed universal deterministic interpreter}, and \emph{indexed RAM}. A deliberately conservative direct implementation bound is $O(P^3)$ time and $O(P)$ simultaneously visited tape cells on the one-tape model; $O(P^3)$ and $O(P)$ work-cell bound on the fixed multitape model, with input length recorded separately, and polynomial emulation overhead for the fixed UTM. These are \emph{upper} bounds for the proof checker, not optimal complexity, and cannot be paired with FLMV exponents without a single fixed machine and correct model simulation. In the RAM model the verifier remains polynomial with indexed proof-line access, also without transferring tape crossing lower bounds. A generator given only $F$ may need an unknown amount of time to \emph{discover} a source refutation, whose physical bit length is not bounded in this report.

The archive audit's syntactic bound $b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$ and the separate exact-(2,2) sequential-head restrictions cannot imply a general SAT lower bound. The rank/descent criterion still requires polynomial \emph{value}, effective transitions, bounded physical states, and closure through two hereditary levels. Independent SAT-side and UNSAT-side quantities refer to restrictions of \emph{one} correct program, not two solvers with a free verdict bit.
