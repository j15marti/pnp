## Theorem 560.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED): source-driven equal-cost retraction

Let `P` be **any parsed packet accepted by the exact pinned recovered fixture checker**. For each included graph fixture `f` require the following *additional* conditions:

* **G0:** graph dimensions/edge endpoints are proper safe integers, with a connected simple cubic graph of `V` vertices and `E=3V/2` original edge-variable positions; the verifier's remaining accepted source-deletion, graph, metadata and summary conditions hold.
* **G1:** an independently supplied, immutable original signed clause sequence `F` agrees at each ordered literal occurrence with the fixture's `4V` clauses, and these are primitive safe nonzero signed integers. Source identity is equality of the independently authenticated clause sequence, not a claim that the packet chose its own trusted source.
* **G2:** each vertex charge is a numeric 0 or 1, with odd total parity. They are not strings or objects. The recovered checker's SOURCE pattern/vertex checks remain required to pass.

**No G3 typed-AND3-support condition or G4 bounded-affine-mask condition is required on the input packet.** In particular its paid derivation may contain recovered-checker-accepted support strings, unbounded paired ghost masks, unusual parsed IDs and irrelevant metadata.

Then there is an effective source-preserving transformation `R(P,F)` that changes only paid proof records (and their newly selected paid IDs), retains every original clause, charge, graph, original source identity and deletion-witness metadata, and satisfies

$$
\operatorname{Accept}_{341}(P)\land G0\land G1\land G2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Accept}_{341}(R(P,F))\land
\operatorname{SoundRefutation}(F).
$$

Its exact charged paid-node count satisfies, **for every fixture**,

$$
W(R(P,F))=4V-1=W(P).
$$

Every newly created AND3 support is a genuine numeric array. Every affine mask is bounded by the original `E` variables. New paid-node names are fresh among original source IDs and among each other. `R` uses no original proof inference as an oracle and no original SAT label.

**Proof.** The actual recovered checker authenticates four distinct clauses at each cubic vertex, each excluding one assignment of local edge parity opposite to numeric `chi_v`. G1/G2 prevent the charge, literal and provenance coercions used in the accepted SAT forgeries. Independently from the original paid proof, assign to each source clause its exact eight-row Boolean factor on the three incident edge positions. For each vertex conjoin its four factors in any fixed order using exactly three AND3 records with newly distinct paid IDs. Every conjunction is a checked at-most-three-original-variable table. Its last record has exactly the truth table of the vertex parity equation, so annotate that record by the in-range row `xor_{e incident v} x_e = chi_v`. The complete graph contains every edge at exactly two vertices. XOR these `V` rows with exactly `V-1` new paid binary XOR records. The terminal mask cancels to zero and its RHS equals odd `xor_v chi_v = 1`; hence the output is `0=1`. Induction over this topological DAG verifies every SOURCE/AND3/XOR inference. The source, metadata, and deleted-source records were already accepted and remain identical. The checker's required paid count is `4V-1`, exactly the new DAG count. Replacing the proof leaves every other accepted test invariant. This proves both checker acceptance and semantic UNSAT under the independently fixed `F`. QED.
