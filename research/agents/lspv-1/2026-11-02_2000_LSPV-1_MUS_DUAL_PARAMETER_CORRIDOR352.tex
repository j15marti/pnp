\section*{Fixed calculus, encoding and quantifiers}
Preserve exactly the existing source-authenticated binary-premise, unit-cost mixed calculus $R_{\rm mix341}$: binary Resolution (RES), exact small-support AND3, checked GF$(2)$ XOR, checked local CONVERT, and charged fresh conservative two-input DEF. The two-premise source-indexed RES subsystem alone supplies every new upper bound. Every original source is free; every paid derived DAG node is charged once; optimal proof selection is free and separate from the cost of finding an optimal proof. No new rules, semantic oracles, or instance-dependent axioms are introduced. Use the monotonically clause-deletable, compact signed-ASCII DIMACS encoding fixed in LSPV-1 MUS-WORSTCASE-QUOTIENT350. Validity includes the header and signed variable index checks; malformed strings are not formulas.

For valid UNSAT $F$, set
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\sum_{\text{distinct derived }v\in\pi}1,\quad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\text{ UNSAT}\\|\mathrm{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F),
\]
where empty maxima are zero. A clause-minimally unsatisfiable (MUS) $U$ has $m$ distinct indispensable clauses on $v$ occurring variables; write $\delta(U)=m-v$. Define $\tau(U)=\mathrm{tw}(P(U))$, the minimum induced elimination width of its \emph{primal variable graph}, where two variables are adjacent if they occur in one clause. \emph{Primal width is not incidence width.} The optimal order may be chosen for free in the minimum $W_R$; an implemented solver must discover or receive it and pay all costs.

\section*{Original inputs: two established one-parameter certificates}
\begin{theorem}[Project-verified original ingredients]
There are absolute constants $C>0,A\ge1$, independent of $U$ and its encoded length $L$, such that every MUS $U$ obeys
\[
\boxed{W_R(U)\le C(L+1)^A2^{\delta(U)}}
\tag{1}
\]
and, for $v=|\mathrm{var}(U)|$,
\[
\boxed{W_R(U)\le v3^{\tau(U)}.}
\tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For (1), by the Hall-injection MUS stability lemma of LSPV-1 351, maximum clause-subset deficiency of $U$ is its ordinary deficiency $\delta(U)$. Apply Szeider's \emph{previously published} fixed-parameter algorithm, whose UNSAT output is a regular binary Resolution refutation with at most $2^{\delta(U)}\mathrm{poly}(L)$ output operations, and compile its indexed RES format with polynomial overhead. (2) reuses ARC219 / LSPV-3 BUCKET-PROVENANCE153: fix a minimum-width primal elimination order. At pivot $x$, all live other variables in positive/negative parent clauses lie in a set of at most $\tau(U)$ names; only $3^{\tau(U)}$ distinct nontautological resolvent clauses can be produced. Eliminate $v$ pivots by complete Davis--Putnam resolution, retaining each newly derived clause once with its actual two parents and signed pivot. Exact existential projection guarantees an empty clause on UNSAT $U$. The DAG uses at most $v3^{\tau(U)}$ charged RES nodes; deleting unused derived nodes only decreases its cost. Both proofs remain legal in the unchanged combined R.
\end{proof}
\noindent The $v3^{\tau}$ proof also has a fully charged \emph{implemented} alternative: with a valid supplied order, at most $v9^\tau$ candidate parent pairs plus polynomial input/index checking; a low-width order can be found by known parameterized treewidth algorithms at $2^{O(\tau)}\mathrm{poly}(L)$ cost. These optimizer and conversion costs are \emph{not} inserted into $W_R$.

\section*{New joint worst-case MUS corridor}
For fixed positive real $a,b$, let $\mathcal H_{a,b}(N)$ be the set of \emph{individually specified, signed} clause-MUS formulas $U$ with
\[
|\mathrm{enc}(U)|\le N,\quad
\delta(U)>a\log_2(N+1),\quad
\tau(U)>b\log_3(N+1).
\]
The stringent parameter bounds use the \emph{global budget N}, not $|U|$; thus there is no concealed change of input scale.

\begin{theorem}[Simultaneous hard-core localization]
For every $N$ and any fixed $a,b>0$,
\[
\boxed{
\max_{U\in\mathcal H_{a,b}(N)}W_R(U)
\;\le\;\overline W_R(N)
\;\le\;
\max\!\left\{
C(N+1)^{A+a},\;
N(N+1)^b,\;
\max_{U\in\mathcal H_{a,b}(N)}W_R(U)
\right\}.
}
\tag{3}
\]
All empty maxima are zero. In particular, any genuinely superpolynomial $\overline W_R$ requires a single unbounded sequence of MUSes $U_j$ with
\[
\frac{\delta(U_j)}{\log(|\mathrm{enc}(U_j)|+1)}\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad
\frac{\tau(U_j)}{\log(|\mathrm{enc}(U_j)|+1)}\longrightarrow\infty,
\tag{4}
\]
\emph{simultaneously on those same formulas}, and with superpolynomial $W_R(U_j)$ relative to their encoded lengths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the exact source-hereditary MUS quotient LSPV-1 350, the maximum defining $\overline W_R(N)$ is attained on a clause-MUS $U$ within the same bit budget. If $U\notin\mathcal H_{a,b}(N)$, either $\delta(U)\le a\log_2(N+1)$ and (1) gives the first polynomial envelope, or $\tau(U)\le b\log_3(N+1)$ and (2), with $v\le N$, gives the second. Otherwise it is included in the remaining maximum. The reverse inequality is immediate from set inclusion. For (4), choose for each growing integer $j$ a sufficiently large budget $N_j$ with $\overline W_R(N_j)$ exceeding both fixed polynomial envelopes with $a=b=j$, and exceeding $(N_j+1)^j$; the defining MUS maximizer must satisfy both strict inequalities and have superpolynomial cost. Select its actual encoded length $L_j\le N_j$. The lower cost inequality rules out bounded $L_j$; moreover $\log N_j\ge\log L_j$, so the ratios to $\log L_j$ diverge. No same-formula assumption is added: both filters were applied to the same maximizing MUS.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Precisely localized weakest B3 obligation, still OPEN]
For any fixed $a,b>0$, proving
\[
{\rm SAT}\in P\ \Longrightarrow\
\max_{U\in\mathcal H_{a,b}(N)} W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(U)
\le (N+1)^{O(1)}
\]
would suffice for the user's weakest conditional B3 bridge, by (3). This \emph{localizes} the missing implication but does \textbf{not} prove any portion of it. B1 and B2 remain unproved as well, and no universal runtime lower bound follows from treewidth, deficiency, or certified proof cost.
\end{corollary}
