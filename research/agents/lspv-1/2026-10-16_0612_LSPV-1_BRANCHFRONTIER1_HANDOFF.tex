\section*{Restricted frontier theorem}
For proper 3-CNF with m clauses and each variable occurring at most Delta times, assigning k variables leaves at least m-Delta*k untouched clauses. A full binary variable-restriction tree with q total leaves has at most q-1 branch nodes on each path, hence every unresolved explicit residual retains at least m-Delta*(q-1) untouched clauses. On bounded-occurrence m=Theta(n), N=Theta(n log n), q<=N^{0.401+o(1)} leaves imply every unresolved explicit residual is Omega(N/log N) bits, not N^{0.6+o(1)}. Restrictions: no unit propagation, semantic termination, resolution or compressed-language consumer. Tovey's bounded-four-occurrence NP-complete 3-SAT is covered only at this interface level.
