\section*{Precise project target and published starting theorem}
Fix unchanged $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, whose authentic original clauses are zero-cost source leaves and whose distinct derived RES, bounded exact AND3, GF$(2)$, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and conservative paid-definition nodes each have unit cost. The full LOCAL-CONVERT circuit syntax remains under review. For any valid UNSAT CNF $F$ define
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi\text{ valid shared refutation DAG of }F}
 \sum_{v\in\mathrm{paid}(\pi)} w(v),\qquad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\text{ valid UNSAT}\\|\mathrm{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F).
\]
The empty maximum is zero. Proof choice is free; the cost of producing, serializing, or verifying a selected proof is not its logical inference-node cost.

For an integer $h\ge1$, let $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}^{h}_{h+1}$ denote the ordinary unary pigeonhole CNF with $h+1$ pigeons, $h$ holes, variables $p_{i,j}$, positive pigeon clauses $\bigvee_{j=1}^h p_{i,j}$ and negative collision clauses $(\neg p_{i,j}\vee\neg p_{k,j})$ for each $i<k,j$. Then
\[
n_h=h(h+1),\quad m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2},\quad
N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))
\]
for canonical explicit signed-literal binary or ASCII records with $\Theta(\log h)$-bit variable names. Haken's 1985 theorem states that there exist $c>0,h_0$ such that for all $h\ge h_0$, every ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution refutation of this standard pigeonhole family uses at least $2^{ch}$ lines (under the standard line-count convention). The theorem concerns \emph{Resolution only}. The same sources have polynomial Extended Resolution or Frege proofs, and this fact already defeats a naive transfer to $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.

\section*{A fully uniform and invertible counterexample}
Let $\mathcal H=\{P_h:h\ge1\}$ in the \emph{canonical ordered encoding} above. There is a fixed deterministic polynomial-time predicate $K(F)$ that parses an arbitrary CNF $F$, tests whether its counts agree with some $h$, and checks every ordered literal against the generated $P_h$ schema. It accepts exactly $\mathcal H$ and therefore accepts only UNSAT formulas. It is not a general-SAT decider and invokes no SAT oracle. On valid $F$ with declared variables $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ define fresh $z=n+1$ and
\[
 E(F)=\begin{cases}
 F\land(z)\land(\neg z),&K(F)=1,\\
 F\land(z),&K(F)=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Preserve the original clause order and append the indicated one or two unit clauses. The output header declares $n+1$ variables and updates the clause count. On malformed input strings, define $E(x)=x$ with the original invalid label; a total parser rejects these strings. The decoder $D$ identifies the fresh terminal variable and its uniquely prescribed final one- or two-clause suffix, strips the suffix, decrements the variable count and validates by regenerating $E$. Thus $D(E(x))=x$ for \emph{every} valid or malformed input string. The two maps on their declared domain/image run in time polynomial in input bit length and introduce $O(\log(N+2))$ bits on top of the serialized original record; in particular $|E(F)|=O(|F|+1)$.

\begin{theorem}[Conservative reversible recoding with exponential Resolution-proof collapse]\label{th:trap}
The single, instance-independent polynomial-time encoder $E$ above obeys, for every valid CNF $F$, every assignment $a$ to its original variables,
\[
 F(a)\iff \exists z\,E(F)(a,z).
\]
The original clauses occur verbatim as the initial prefix of $E(F)$, and the whole encoding can be recovered by one fixed polynomial-time inverse $D$ on its image. Nevertheless, for every $h\ge1$,
\[
 W_{\mathrm{Res}}(E(P_h))=1,
\]
whereas Haken's theorem yields $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}$ for all $h\ge h_0$. Therefore no polynomial $p$ can satisfy
\[
 W_{\mathrm{Res}}(F)\le
 p\bigl(|\operatorname{enc}(F)|+W_{\mathrm{Res}}(E(F))\bigr)
\]
for every valid UNSAT $F$, even though $E$ is computable, conservative and invertible in polynomial time.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When $K(F)=0$, $E(F)=F\land z$ and existential projection gives exactly $F$. When $K(F)=1$, $F=P_h$ is UNSAT by the pigeonhole principle, and $E(F)$ is also UNSAT due to the two appended units. Thus both predicates are false for each original assignment. Distinct original formulas have distinct preserved clause prefixes; fresh $z$ occurs only in the terminal suffix, so suffix deletion is an inverse, and rechecking its exact syntactic legality is polynomial.

For $F=P_h$, the two source clauses $(z)$ and $(\neg z)$ resolve to the empty clause with one paid Resolution inference, so $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(E(P_h))\le1$. No source clause of $P_h$ or the appended suffix is empty. Thus every valid refutation must derive at least one clause, and the minimum equals one. Haken supplies $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}$. Since $|P_h|=\Theta(h^3\log h)$, the right-hand side of any fixed polynomial applied to $|P_h|+1$ is $h^{O(1)}$, a contradiction for all sufficiently large $h$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Mixed-calculus qualification]
In the unchanged unit-paid $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, the \emph{transformed} family $E(P_h)$ also has $W_R(E(P_h))=1$, provided source leaves cannot silently assert the empty clause and all actual inference nodes carry unit weight. This is an easy-family observation only. It gives no new lower bound for the \emph{original} $P_h$ in the mixed calculus, which already admits Extended Resolution shortcuts.
\end{corollary}

\section*{Why this addresses the proposed intrinsic signed-hypergraph/DAG invariant}
The source signed hypergraph is fully retained inside $E(F)$; merely reconstructing or preserving it, preserving its Boolean semantics, or having polynomial-time reversible code is not enough to preserve the minimum Resolution DAG cost. The extra two-unit tiny core is sound \emph{because} a polynomial recognizer certifies that the selected family has label UNSAT. The encoder exploits a mathematically established family property, not a hidden general SAT oracle. Thus an intrinsic quantity that claims to control proof cost across representations must specify \emph{which} representation changes are admissible and prove a cost-bounded backtranslation of their justifications. This is a negative result for an overbroad invariance axiom, not evidence that no useful representation-aware invariant exists.

More precisely, the needed lower-bound direction is
\[
 W_Q(F)\le p\bigl(N+W_R(F)\bigr).
\]
An efficient \emph{input} decoder, or even exact existential equivalence, does not imply this \emph{proof} simulation. Here $Q=R=\mathrm{Res}$ and the two presentations are $F$ and $E(F)$; the proposed direction already fails on $P_h$. A computational conversion of any purported $R$-proof must also charge its encoded evidence size, construction, proof checking, output, all bit reads and peak space. Exponential intermediate representations are not forbidden but cannot be assigned unit runtime, and compact alternatives must be tested.

For arbitrary correct uniform SAT deciders, the representation switch $E$ has polynomial preprocessing and inverse cost, so the two \emph{decision problems on these presentations} have polynomially equivalent uniform decision complexity. This is fully consistent with the exponential separation in \emph{restricted} Resolution proof complexity. A single general SAT decider can recognize the known pigeonhole family quickly and use exponential fallback on other inputs. This does not say its global worst-case running time is polynomial.
