\section{Exactly scoped adaptive self-reducer}
Let $N$ be \emph{encoded input bit length}. Suppose one fixed, total, uniform deterministic random-access Turing machine $R$ decides ordinary SAT through a finite number $d$ of sequential \emph{ordinary SAT child decisions}. The machine must decide both labels exactly on \emph{every} input. It may adaptively choose, abandon, reorder and switch channels using any computable policy, but the following worst-case envelopes hold for all reachable states on sources of length $N$.

For channel $j$, there are at most $N^{\eta_j+o(1)}$ calls, each with a fully ordinary, self-contained encoded SAT child of length at most $N^{\theta_j+o(1)}$, where $0\le\theta_j<1$ are fixed constants. The entirety of local work, \emph{including} parsing, recognition, failed attempts, query construction, output writes, compatibility, policy selection and answers integration, is $N^{r+o(1)}$ time and $N^{\sigma+o(1)}$ work/index space. All readable children are explicitly generated and charged, and at most one is live at a time. Parent/ancestor state, caches, and all bookkeeping are counted within that workspace bound. The call graph terminates at a fixed finite base size, decided by an explicitly finite truth table. No nonrecursive use of a supplied arbitrary SAT algorithm, SAT oracle, nonuniform advice or inaccessible parent pointer is permitted.

\begin{lemma}[Charged adaptive multichannel closure, \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} under this contract]
Set
\[
 C=\max\!\left\{r,\max_{1\le j\le d}\frac{\eta_j}{1-\theta_j}\right\},
 \qquad
 \rho=\max\{\sigma,\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_d\}.
\]
The full uniform exact self-recursion decides SAT in joint time $N^{C+o(1)}$ and space $N^{\rho+o(1)}$ on the same machine.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
With recursive time $T$, the complete local-work and adaptive call envelopes imply
\[
 T(N)\le N^{r+o(1)}+
   \sum_{j=1}^{d}N^{\eta_j+o(1)}T(N^{\theta_j+o(1)}).
\]
For every fixed $\epsilon>0$ and sufficiently large $N$, substitution of
$T(M)\le M^{C+\epsilon}$ makes every channel exponent
$\eta_j+\theta_j(C+\epsilon)+o(1)
 \le C+\theta_j\epsilon+o(1)<C+\epsilon$.
The positive slack $\epsilon(1-\theta_j)$ absorbs a fixed number of channels, constant overhead and finite base cases. Since every $\theta_j<1$, recursion bottoms out, giving $N^{C+o(1)}$ time. Depth-first evaluation retains local state at each ancestor and a single explicit child. The sizes shrink geometrically in exponent, and the combined peak is
$N^{\rho+o(1)}$, accounting for index tapes, ancestor state and child buffers. Correctness follows by induction on strictly decreasing child length with exact SAT responses. Rule selection may depend on every prior result; the envelopes hold on each adaptive execution. The result concerns this \emph{restricted recursive-policy class}, not every uniform SAT algorithm.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FLMV channel-feasibility firewall, \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} from known theorems]
If the preceding contract holds for \emph{ordinary all-input SAT} and some fixed $c>\max\{1,C\}$ obeys $\rho<\beta_2(c)$, the procedure $R$ cannot exist. More generally, Theorem 5.7 forbids any profile satisfying $\rho<\beta_\ell(c)$ for some fixed integer $\ell\ge1$, where
\[
 f_0(c)=1,\quad f_{\ell+1}(c)=\frac{c f_\ell(c)}{1+f_\ell(c)},\qquad
 \beta_\ell(c)=\min\!\left\{\frac{f_\ell(c)^{-1}-c}{\ell},\,c^{-\ell}\right\}.
\]
In particular for $1<C$, every such procedure must satisfy the necessary inequality $\rho\ge\beta_2(C)$ wherever $\beta_2(C)>0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The lemma supplies one ordinary all-input exact SAT machine with a simultaneous exponent pair $(C,\rho)$. For a fixed strict witness $c$, the polylogarithmic transfer of FLMV Theorem 3.1 yields the forbidden NTIME inclusion of FLMV Corollary 5.8, or Theorem 5.7 for fixed $\ell$. Contradiction. For the last assertion, continuity and strict decrease of $\beta_2$ permit $c>C$ sufficiently close to $C$ whenever $\rho<\beta_2(C)$.
\end{proof}
