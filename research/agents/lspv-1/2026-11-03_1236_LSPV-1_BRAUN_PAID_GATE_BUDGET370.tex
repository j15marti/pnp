\section*{Published theorem first; exact fixed model}
Fix $n=2^\ell$ holes with $\ell\ge32$ and standard original-source bit pigeonhole CNF $B_\ell$ on $(n+1)\ell$ Boolean variables. Each of the $\binom{n+1}{2}$ pigeon pairs and each of $n$ holes yields a width-$2\ell$ collision-blocking clause, so
\[
 m_\ell=n\binom{n+1}{2},\qquad N_\ell=|\operatorname{enc}(B_\ell)|=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)\text{ encoded bits}.
\]
Braun (September 19, 2026, arXiv:2609.23015) reports that every unrestricted shared-DAG Res($\oplus$) refutation, source nodes included, satisfies
\[
L_{\oplus}(B_\ell)>\exp(t_\ell),\qquad t_\ell:=\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}. \tag{1}
\]
Itsykson--Sokolov's standard syntactic Res($\oplus$) has semantic weakening and resolution on complementary affine equations. It is implicationally complete, and Lemma 4.1 therein shows closure of weakening/resolution under adding a common disjunct. This is a claim about a proof calculus, not about SAT Turing-machine time. The Braun preprint's claimed Lean verification has not been independently replayed here.

Retain the \emph{unchanged} original $R_{\rm mix341}$: signed original CNF source clauses at zero proof-leaf charge, genuine unit-paid RES, exact local AND3, binary GF$(2)$ row addition, justified LOCAL-CONVERT, and fresh source-independent conservative signed two-input paid VARDEF gates. Every distinct paid proof-DAG inference is counted once, and free optimal proof selection is intentional. For valid UNSAT $F$,
\[
 W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\#\pi_{\rm paid},\quad
 \overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ \mathrm{valid\ UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F),
\]
with empty maxima zero. Positively charged RES completeness ensures minima exist.

\section*{Audited proof grammar, not a changed calculus}
The predecessor LSPV-1 369 defines the \emph{proper} fragment $R_{\rm aff3}$ containing strict ordinary RES, global binary GF$(2)$ row sum on unit affine equations, and exact local AND3/CONVERT factors whose full named-variable support is at most three, but no new variables or high-support conversions. Its already reported compiler supplies an absolute constant $C_3$ so that $q$ paid $R_{\rm aff3}$ nodes translate to at most $m+C_3q$ syntactic Res($\oplus$) nodes for the exact original source.

Let $R_{\rm aff3}+\mathrm{DEF}$ denote \emph{the fragment of legal original $R_{\rm mix341}$ proofs} that additionally permits paid, fresh, acyclic, signed AND2-VARDEF nodes, each with exactly the source-independent conservative slots $(\neg z\lor a),(\neg z\lor b),(z\lor\neg a\lor\neg b)$, where $|a|\ne|b|$ and $a,b$ are signed original variables or previously paid gate outputs. Local factor steps may use at most three named original/gate variables; all their signed slots, input/output scopes, and original/auxiliary legality remain subject to existing Rmix341 rules. No uncharged gate or arbitrary circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT wire may be created. This is a restriction on \emph{proofs}, not a new source-dependent proof calculus. Write $g(\pi)$ for the number of paid gate definitions within a proof DAG of total paid cost $q$.

\begin{lemma}[Direct-fanin restriction]
For every such proof with $g$ paid gates, let $S$ be the original variables appearing \emph{directly} as either signed input of any paid gate. Then $s:=|S|\le2g$. Under every assignment $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^S$, all paid gate outputs have uniquely determined Boolean values, and every signed definition slot becomes a tautology after restricting both its original direct-fanin variables and these determined outputs.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every paid output is an AND of signed literals over either original variables from $S$ or earlier paid outputs. Evaluate gates in their acyclic definition order. The induction assigns a constant to every output based solely on $\alpha$. Because a signed AND truth table satisfies its three actual defining clauses, these become true under that substitution. There are at most two direct original inputs per paid gate. No assumptions about the other original variables or the actual SAT/UNSAT status of $F$ are needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Gate-budgeted Res($\oplus$) backtranslation]
There is an absolute constant $C\ge C_3$, independent of the source, gate count, proof length and named-variable count, such that every legal $q$-paid-node $R_{\rm aff3}+\mathrm{DEF}$ refutation $\pi$ of an original $m$-clause CNF $F$, with $s$ direct original gate-input variables, yields a source-authenticated syntactic Res($\oplus$) refutation of \emph{exactly the same original $F$} with at most
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle L_{\oplus}(F)\le m+2^s(m+Cq+1)\le m+4^g(m+Cq+1).}\tag{2}
\]
This is a proof-to-proof \emph{upper} construction and does not assert an unrestricted SAT algorithm lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^S$. Replace all originals in $S$ by $\alpha$ and every paid gate variable by its recursively determined value. Each definition slot is true by the lemma and disappears. Remaining source clauses are precisely restrictions of genuine original $F$ clauses. All non-gate inferences remain sound under substitution: ordinary RES may degenerate to a single-premise semantic weakening, GF$(2)$ additions remain affine identities after constants are substituted, and every permitted local factor step has at most three surviving named variables. If one input becomes true while the output is nontrivial, the other input alone implies the output; if all antecedents become true, a sound output is true and may be omitted. Thus the same finite R-aff3 compiler yields a syntactic Res($\oplus$) refutation of $F|_\alpha$ at cost at most $m+Cq$ (taking $C$ large enough to cover all bounded local cases and simplifications). No source definition has been inserted as an extra axiom.

For every original source clause $D$ with a nontrivial restricted counterpart $D|_\alpha$, let
\[
 B_\alpha=\bigvee_{x_i\in S}(x_i\ne\alpha_i).
\]
Since $D$ implies $(D|_\alpha)\vee B_\alpha$, one ordinary semantic-weakening step in Res($\oplus$) derives this guarded source from the \emph{authentic original} $D$. Guard every line of the restricted Res($\oplus$) derivation by $B_\alpha$. Its weakening and complementary-affine-pivot rules commute with the addition of this fixed disjunct (Itsykson--Sokolov, Lemma 4.1). The guarded final empty clause is $B_\alpha$. This adds at most $m$ weakened source nodes and at most $Cq$ derived nodes per assignment. The $m$ unguarded original sources are shared across all branches.

All $2^s$ complete blockers $B_\alpha$ now have source-authenticated proofs. Resolve them in the ordinary binary decision tree on variables of $S$, costing $2^s-1$ further Res($\oplus$) nodes. Its root is empty. Accounting conservatively gives $m+2^s(m+Cq)+(2^s-1)$, which is no more than (2). DAG sharing could only decrease cost. If $s=0$, the same proof is immediate without a branching tree. Proof acquisition and encoded proof bits are not equated with source- or machine-time cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Quantified extension-count barrier, conditional on Braun]
Assume (1). For every $\ell\ge32$ and every legal $R_{\rm aff3}+\mathrm{DEF}$ refutation of the original $B_\ell$ with $q$ paid nodes and $g$ paid AND2 gates,
\[
 \boxed{m_\ell+4^g(m_\ell+Cq+1)>\exp(t_\ell).}\tag{3}
\]
Consequently, for every fixed $a$ and every sequence of such refutations with $q\le N_\ell^a$ for all sufficiently large $\ell$,
\[
 \boxed{g\ge \frac{n}{32768\ell^2\ln4}-O_a(\log N_\ell)=\Omega(n/\ell^2).}\tag{4}
\]
If instead $g\le t_\ell/(2\ln4)$, then for all sufficiently large $\ell$,
\[
\boxed{q\ge \frac{\exp(t_\ell/2)}{2C}.}\tag{5}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine Braun's original-source lower bound (1) with the upper translation (2). Rearranging (3), using $m_\ell=\Theta(n^3)$ and $N_\ell=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$, proves (4), because the logarithm of a polynomial $m_\ell+Cq+1$ is $O_a(\log N_\ell)$ whereas $t_\ell$ grows as $n/\ell^2$. For (5), $4^g\le\exp(t_\ell/2)$, and the polynomial $m_\ell$ plus $\exp(t_\ell/2)(m_\ell+1)$ is eventually less than $\exp(t_\ell)/2$. Therefore $Cq4^g>\exp(t_\ell)/2$, giving (5). These statements apply to this restricted grammar, not to arbitrary $R_{\rm mix341}$ derivations.
\end{proof}

\section*{Explicit falsifiers, countercontrols and cost discipline}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{F1, checked:} two gate inputs hide more than $2g$ distinct direct original names. Impossible under the exact two-named-input paid slot grammar; 128 deterministic signed-gate networks and their fibers were independently tested at small support. Uncharged circuit-valued named wires would invalidate the model and are explicitly excluded.
\item[] \textbf{F2, checked:} source specialization needs an invented unit assumption. No: guarded restricted source clauses are derived from the actual original clauses by Res($\oplus$) weakening, with all branch assumptions discharged by genuine resolutions.
\item[] \textbf{F3, mandatory:} full Rmix341 must inherit (3). \textbf{REJECTED}: unrestricted named circuit-valued LOCAL-CONVERT has not been compiled here, paid VARDEF may be coupled with nonlocal operations, and the known odd-Tseitin $4h-1$ mixed XOR shortcut is valid. Neither B3 nor an unrestricted computational-time lower bound follows.
\item[] \textbf{F4, falsifiable against future explicit proofs:} construct $q=\operatorname{poly}(N_\ell)$ and $g=o(n/\ell^2)$ in precisely this restricted grammar. Such a certificate would contradict either the translation lemma or Braun's published premise, and therefore merits independent exact grammar/sources/semantics verification before any promotion.
\end{description}

The deterministic Python producer (seed 3702026) checked 128 separately constructed signed acyclic paid-gate networks with $1\le g\le4$ on $2\le k\le5$ original names and every original assignment. Each direct-fanin restriction uniquely determined all paid outputs and satisfied every signed definition slot. These are \emph{COMPUTATIONAL} controls for the symbolic theorem, not verification of Braun or a global optimum. The original toy's $q=6$ is an upper certificate, not its minimum.
