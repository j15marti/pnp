\section*{New exact graph-operation lemma}
Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite connected simple cubic graph with \(h=|V|\), and let \(M\subset E\) be a matching with \(|M|\ge2\). Suppose \(G-M-v\) is connected for \emph{every} vertex \(v\). Retain one original Boolean variable for every edge outside \(M\), but identify all matched edge variables with a \emph{single} original variable \(g\). This preserves three distinct incident variables at every vertex because \(M\) is a matching. Let \(A\) be the resulting \(h\times(|E|-|M|+1)\) incidence matrix over \(\mathbb F_2\). Take charges \(b_v\) with \(\bigoplus_v b_v=1\). Encode each equation \(A_v x=b_v\) by its four original proper width-three clauses blocking the four local wrong-parity triples; call the resulting formula \(F(G,M,b)\).

\begin{lemma}[Punctured rank and full local repair]
The matrix \(A\) has row rank \(h-1\) with unique nonzero row dependence \(\sum_vA_v=0\). For every \(v\), the projection of \(\ker A_{-v}\) onto the three coordinates \(S_v=\mathrm{supp}(A_v)\) has dimension two.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every unmerged column contains two ones, and the merged column has \(2|M|\) ones; hence \(\sum_vA_v=0\). The unmerged graph \(G-M\) is connected, so its incidence columns span the even-parity subspace of \(\mathbb F_2^V\) of dimension \(h-1\). Therefore \(\mathrm{rank}(A)=h-1\).

Fix \(v\). Consider a vector in \(\mathrm{rowspan}(A_{-v})\) supported entirely within \(S_v\). It is the sum of rows indexed by some \(U\subset V\setminus\{v\}\). All unmerged columns on edges of \(G-M-v\) outside \(S_v\) vanish, so the indicator of \(U\) is constant on every edge of that connected graph. Thus \(U=\emptyset\) or \(U=V\setminus\{v\}\). The corresponding row sums are \(0\) and \(A_v\). Therefore
\[
 \mathrm{rowspan}(A_{-v})\cap\mathbb F_2^{S_v}=\mathrm{span}\{A_v\}.
\]
Orthogonal-complement duality gives
\(\dim\mathrm{proj}_{S_v}\ker A_{-v}=|S_v|-1=2\).
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The XOR of all equations reads \(0=1\), so \(F\) is UNSAT. For fixed \(v\), the \(h-1\) remaining rows are independent, so \(A_{-v}x=b_{-v}\) has a solution. Its projected local triples have parity \(1\oplus b_v\) because the XOR of all rows is zero while the XOR of all charges is one. The lemma gives projected affine dimension two, so every one of the four wrong-parity triples is realized. For each source clause blocking such a triple, choose a solution of the other equations realizing that triple. It falsifies exactly that one clause, proving deletion-SAT and satisfying any one-literal sign-flipped version of that source.

Add all \(h\) rows using \(h-1\) legal binary XOR nodes. The last row is \(0=1\). Cost \(3h+h-1=4h-1\). No free four-premise or semantic contradiction rule is used.
\end{proof}
