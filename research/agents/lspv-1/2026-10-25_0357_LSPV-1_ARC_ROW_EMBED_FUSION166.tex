\section{Exact row-membership construction}
Fix one well-formed proper 3CNF scoped stream $H$ with event syntax
$\mathrm{OPEN}(v),\mathrm{CLAUSE}(C),\mathrm{CLOSE}(v)$, every used variable
opened/closed exactly once, and every clause referencing three distinct
currently live names.  Let its maximum number of live names be $w$.
Divide its $m$ clause records into consecutive blocks of size at most
$\lceil\sqrt m\rceil$.  At block $i$, let $L_i$ and $R_i$ be the live
name sets at the cuts immediately before and after that block.  Write
$\mathcal R_i\subseteq\{0,1\}^{L_i}$ for the \emph{exact reachable row}:
the boundary assignments extendible to satisfy all preceding blocks.
The initial row is $\{\varnothing\}$.

For a finite named boundary $L$ and exact explicit row
$\mathcal R\subseteq\{0,1\}^L$, define its forbidden-assignment CNF
\[
\operatorname{Tab}(L,\mathcal R)
:=\bigwedge_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{L}\setminus\mathcal R}
\left(\bigvee_{v\in L}(v\ne\alpha_v)\right).
\tag{1}
\]
A length-zero forbidden clause is contradictory.  An empty conjunction is
true. Each clause in (1) is converted to \emph{proper} 3CNF by the
standard existentially equisatisfiable private extension: a one-literal
clause uses four proper 3clauses with two fresh helpers; a two-literal
clause uses two proper 3clauses with one fresh helper; a length-$\ell\ge4$
clause uses an $\ell-2$-clause implication chain of fresh auxiliary names.
Empty clauses use a fixed proper unsatisfiable cube.
All old names retain their identities, and all helpers are fresh and local.

\begin{lemma}[Row embedding is an exact existential join]
For each assignment $\beta\in\{0,1\}^{R_i}$, let
\[
C_i(\mathcal R_i,\beta)
:=\operatorname{Proper3}\left(
\operatorname{Tab}(L_i,\mathcal R_i)\ \wedge\
\bigwedge\operatorname{Block}_i\ \wedge\
\operatorname{Pins}(R_i,\beta)
\right).
\tag{2}
\]
Here $\operatorname{Pins}$ are exact proper-3CNF unit gadgets.
Then
\[
\boxed{\beta\in\mathcal R_{i+1}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\SAT\bigl(C_i(\mathcal R_i,\beta)\bigr).}\tag{3}
\]
The method needs at most $2^{|R_i|}\le2^w$ \emph{actual}
ordinary-SAT child decisions for this block, instead of
$2^{|L_i|+|R_i|}\le2^{2w}$ separate paired decisions.
The table and its full proper-CNF encoding are charged, not oracle metadata.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For any Boolean assignment $\gamma$ to $L_i$, the formula (1) holds
exactly when $\gamma\in\mathcal R_i$: a forbidden $\gamma$ violates its
own unique rejecting clause; an allowed $\gamma$ differs from every
forbidden assignment and satisfies all rejecting clauses.  Fresh 3CNF
helpers existentially preserve that predicate and the unit-pin predicate.
Thus (2) has a witness exactly when one allowed incoming assignment
$\alpha\in\mathcal R_i$ extends through the current block to outgoing
$\beta$.  Recursing over blocks yields exactly the global satisfying
assignments, because every cross-cut original name is contained in the
corresponding live interface. The same argument applies when the
reachable row is empty, to unsatisfiable parents, and to arbitrary
shared original variables.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Hereditary scoped eligibility.}
The child (2) can be emitted in the order
\emph{table prefix, original block, outgoing pins}.
At the table prefix only the incoming $L_i$ names are carried live
and at most two fresh 3CNF-chain helpers are simultaneously live;
at the block the source's original live-name set stays within $w$;
near its end at most the $R_i$ names are live, plus the constant-number
pin helpers. All helpers are closed locally. Therefore the child has a
\emph{construction-known}, checkable legal scoped ordering of width
$\le w+O(1)$, with an actual complete ordinary 3CNF body and header.
The same reasoning applies at each \emph{fixed} recursive depth.
This construction-dependent ordering avoids charging an unproved
arbitrary-CNF low-space pathwidth recognizer. No fresh run of the
unknown all-input normalizer is needed for the descendants.

\section{A sharper charged one-pass fusion theorem}
Fix any one total deterministic uniform normalizer/transducer $G_A$,
which is allowed to depend on the \emph{fixed source program} $A$.
On every ordinary SAT source $F$ of length $N$, including malformed
inputs under an agreed reject convention, it must either reject
invalid encodings correctly or output one exact equisatisfiable
well-formed closed \emph{proper 3CNF} scoped stream of length
$M\le N^{\mu+o(1)}$ with named live width
$w\le\delta\log_2 N+O(1)$.  The names must have
$O(\log N)$-bit canonical indices; $G_A$ declares its clause count
and is \emph{pauseable} with all live state retained.  Its own complete
charged running time and simultaneously live writable/index space are
$N^{r+o(1)}$ and $N^{\eta+o(1)}$, respectively.
In particular, any $A$ calls by $G_A$ are already charged and
may not become free parent-scale decisions.

\begin{theorem}[One-boundary row-embedded fixed-depth fusion]
Fix an integer $d\ge2$ independent of $N$ and suppose
\[
\boxed{\delta<\mu 2^{-d}.}\tag{4}
\]
Given any fixed uniform deterministic total ordinary-SAT decider $A$
with one jointly realized pair $(t,s)$, and the foregoing $G_A$, there
is one fixed uniform deterministic total correct ordinary-SAT decider
$B_{A,G_A,d}$ whose simultaneously realized resource exponents obey
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
t_d&\le\max\left\{r,\ \mu+d\delta,\
\mu(1-2^{-d})+d\delta+\mu2^{-d}t\right\},\\
s_d&\le\max\left\{\eta,\ \mu/2,\ \delta,\ \mu2^{-d}s\right\}.
\end{aligned}}\tag{5}
\]
All bounds have $o(1)$ slack. In the same declared source interface,
the earlier two-boundary-pair method supplied a $2d\delta$ time
surcharge. Equation (5) reduces this to $d\delta$ by moving
incoming-row existential quantification into each ordinary SAT child.
No \emph{unproved} shorter encoding of an arbitrary Boolean row is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each table (1) has at most $2^w$ forbidden assignments, each expanded
into $O(w)$ proper 3CNF clauses of bounded length; canonical serialized
bit length is $O(2^w w\operatorname{polylog}N)=N^{\delta+o(1)}$.
The $O(\sqrt m)$-clause original block and exact proper pins have
serialized size $O(\sqrt M\operatorname{polylog}N)$, so a child of a
parent of encoded exponent $x_i$ has exponent
\[
x_{i+1}=\max\{x_i/2,\delta\}.
\]
By (4), at each of the $d$ fixed stages
$x_i=\mu2^{-i}$ and
$x_{i+1}=\mu2^{-(i+1)}$.  At stage $i=0,\dots,d-1$, each
node forms at most $N^{x_i/2+\delta+o(1)}$ children: one for
each of $O(N^{x_i/2})$ blocks and each of the $2^w=N^{\delta+o(1)}$
outgoing assignments, \emph{not} each pair of incoming/outgoing values.
Thus the number of depth-$d$ leaf instances is bounded by
\[
N^{\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}(x_i/2+\delta)+o(1)}
=N^{\mu(1-2^{-d})+d\delta+o(1)}.
\]
The sole leaf guest is $A$ on a \emph{fully serialized ordinary} input
of length $N^{\mu2^{-d}+o(1)}$.  Summed guest time gives the last term
of (5).  Across all nodes, actually producing, parsing, validating,
scanning and discarding each child and its row table requires
$N^{\mu+d\delta+o(1)}$ charged deterministic bit work, since the
stage-$i$ output-bit total is
\[
N^{\mu(1-2^{-i})+i\delta}\cdot
N^{x_i/2+\delta}\cdot N^{x_i/2}
=N^{\mu+(i+1)\delta+o(1)}.
\]
This conservatively pays repeated writes of \emph{unchanged} bits,
address/index/control arithmetic, table scans, failed SAT replies,
helper literals, header construction, outgoing row updates, and all
actual nested child serialization/access.  The one pass through
$G_A$ contributes its separate $N^{r+o(1)}$ time without stream replay.

A depth-first stack pauses $G_A$ without copying its state, holds
at most one entire root child of $N^{\mu/2+o(1)}$ bits, all
geometrically shorter ancestor/descendant child buffers, a reachable
row of at most $2^w=N^{\delta+o(1)}$ bits at each of fixed $d$
levels, and one leaf guest using
$N^{\mu2^{-d}s+o(1)}$ writable/index bits.
Input heads, row-table addresses, child headers, and recursion/control
state are simultaneously charged. This proves the \emph{same
machine} joint space bound in (5). No per-input adaptive choice of
an uncharged algorithm is made; adaptive row values are computed by
previous real SAT calls.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional six-level numerical margin from a large diagonal]

\left(\max\{r,19/16\},\max\{\eta,1/2\}\right).
\]
The earlier two-boundary method instead gives a conservative time

\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Cofinal finite-depth certificate ceiling.}
With $\mu=1$ and starting diagonal $(K,K)$, this particular
\emph{upper-certificate} requires
\[
1+d\delta+\frac{K-1}{2^d}<c_*,\qquad
\frac{K}{2^d}<d_*,\qquad \delta<2^{-d},
\]
besides $r<c_*$, $\eta<d_*$ and $1/2<d_*$.
If one insists on a \emph{single fixed positive} $\delta$ over
arbitrarily large $K$, these inequalities cannot certify cofinal
strict entry: $d\delta<c_*-1$ bounds $d$, while
$K<2^d d_*$ bounds $K$.  This limits the present
\emph{certificate}, not true algorithmic running time.
The normalizer parameters may depend on the fixed $A$ and chosen
integer $K$; an independently justified width schedule or a
fundamentally different edge remains possible.

chains, and a malformed header guard.  Maximum checked scope width
was four for a root and six for a recursive child.

A shared-variable proper-3CNF pair of opposing unit gadgets has each
child SAT but its conjunction UNSAT.  Deleting the input reachable-row
constraints from the fused child yields a reproducible \emph{false
SAT positive} on that separated conflict. The row-embedded
construction returns UNSAT.  Affine/2SAT structure and tiny
full-cube contradictions are deliberately easy hostile controls;
they falsify any claim of an input hardness result.

Python function counts and byte lengths are \emph{not} a trace of
FLMV random-access Turing-machine work, writable peak, changed-bit
flips, unchanged writes, physical transistor switching, or hardware
size.  The theorem's $N^{\cdot+o(1)}$ bit-work and space bounds derive
