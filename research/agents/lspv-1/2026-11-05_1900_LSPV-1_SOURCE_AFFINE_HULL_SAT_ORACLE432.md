## Theorem 432.1 (relative to an exact SAT-search oracle)

For any well-formed signed CNF F with r occurring variable identifiers and N input bits, there is one uniform deterministic algorithm returning either EMPTY (if F is UNSAT) or at most r+1 *actual models of F* whose affine span is the full GF(2) affine hull of all models, plus a basis of every entailed affine equation. It uses at most r+1 calls to a SAT **search** oracle. Each query consists of F plus an explicit O(r^2)-size XOR/OR Tseitin circuit encoded in width-at-most-three CNF. Signed padding makes every shorter clause proper exact-width-three when required, with constant overhead per shorter clause and preserving existential projection. If SAT is in P, standard self-reduction replaces search by a polynomial number of decision calls: exact affine-hull acquisition is then polynomial-time on **all binary inputs**, with parsing and malformed-input rejection included.

**Proof.** Parse and compact the actually occurring variable IDs, preserving a reversible original-ID map. Ask the oracle for a model a. If none exists, return EMPTY. Otherwise store genuine models w and form V=span{w xor a}. Compute via Gaussian elimination the basis v_1,...,v_(r-d) of the orthogonal complement, d=dim V. The current candidate hull is a+V and its equations are v_i.(z xor a)=0. If d<r, construct an explicit functional Tseitin circuit for

$$
\mathrm{Outside}(z)=\bigvee_{i=1}^{r-d} [v_i\cdot(z\oplus a)\ne0].
$$

Query SAT on F AND Outside. A returned model lies outside a+V and strictly raises d. An UNSAT response proves *semantically* that all source models lie inside a+V; the reverse inclusion holds because the collected models are actual source models. Return the basis (v_i,v_i.a) in that case. If d=r, the full space is already spanned. At most r rank-increasing positive queries plus at most one final failed query occur after the initial model, and in either case the total is <=r+1. XOR/OR gates are uniquely extendible and preserve the original models under projection. QED.

**Encoded-size discipline:** r is bounded by the number of literal occurrences, hence O(N). Header maximum ID M may be 2^{Theta(N)}; no loop or vector over the M unused variables is needed. They are represented implicitly as unconstrained and have coefficient zero in every entailed affine equation. The explicit per-query bit length is O(N+r^2(log M+log r)); query production, source-map reads, gate construction, witness verification and all-phase memory are polynomial in N. An assumed SAT decision decider must pay each call, witness self-reduction and its actual resources. The Python finite oracle below is *exponential*, not a P algorithm.
