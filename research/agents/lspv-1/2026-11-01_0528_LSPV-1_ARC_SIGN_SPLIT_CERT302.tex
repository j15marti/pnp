\section{Exact original-source two-sign conservation}
Let $p$ be any coherent original-variable partial assignment, $X$ its set of free variables, and $U^\pm$ the sets of positive/negative original clauses with no pinned-TRUE source literal. Let $u_\pm=|U^\pm|$. For each free original $x$, write $s_x^\pm$ for its occurrences of each sign inside original clauses \emph{already satisfied by pinned-TRUE literals}. Let $t_j(a)$ count all TRUE original source literals of $C_j$ under complete $a\supseteq p$; pinned literals inside any $j\in U^\pm$ are FALSE.
\begin{theorem}[Two-sign exact occurrence identity]\label{thm:split}
For every well-formed canonical $F$, every coherent $p$, and \emph{every} completed original-variable assignment $a\supseteq p$, even when $a$ is not SAT, define $E_\pm(a,p)=\sum_{j\in U^\pm}(t_j(a)-1)$. Then
\begin{align}
E_+(a,p)&=\sum_{x\in X}(2-s_x^+)a_x-u_+,\label{eq:ep}\\
E_-(a,p)&=\sum_{x\in X}(2-s_x^-)(1-a_x)-u_- .\label{eq:em}
\end{align}
For every satisfying $a$, $E_\pm\ge0$. If a \emph{committed} complete certificate branch fixes the two surplus parities $\epsilon_\pm\in\{0,1\}$, every original-source SAT model in that branch obeys the authenticated GF$(2)$ equations
\begin{align}
\bigoplus_{x\in X:s_x^+\ \mathrm{odd}}a_x&=\epsilon_+\oplus(u_+\bmod2),\\
\bigoplus_{x\in X:s_x^-\ \mathrm{odd}}a_x&=\epsilon_-\oplus(u_-\bmod2)\oplus(|\{x\in X:s_x^-\ \mathrm{odd}\}|\bmod2).
\end{align}
Neither conditional row is a valid prefix invariant before its relevant parity and completion domain are committed, unless a separate source-authenticated proof establishes its universal scope.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Exactly two positive and two negative source occurrences belong to each free variable. Of its positive occurrences, $s_x^+$ were covered by already pinned TRUE literals, leaving $(2-s_x^+)$ uncovered positive occurrences, all TRUE iff $a_x=1$. The analogous negative count is $(2-s_x^-)$, TRUE iff $a_x=0$. Summing and subtracting one per uncovered clause yields (\ref{eq:ep})--(\ref{eq:em}). In a SAT extension every uncovered clause has at least one TRUE original literal, so both excesses are nonnegative. Reducing these exact equations modulo two and replacing $(1-a_x)$ by $(1+a_x)\bmod2$ yields the stated GF$(2)$ rows. A later ticket may change $\epsilon_\pm$, and an uncommitted row cannot be imported across sibling choice domains (REFEREE264).
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Canonical root layer bound and parity]\label{cor:root}
For $p=\varnothing$ and full assignment Hamming weight $w=\sum_i a_i$, for \emph{every} complete assignment:
\[
E_+(a,\varnothing)=2(w-k),\qquad E_-(a,\varnothing)=2(2k-w),\qquad E_++E_-=2k.
\]
Every original-source SAT witness therefore has $k\le w\le2k$ and \emph{even} positive and negative excesses. If $w=k$, each positive clause has exactly one TRUE literal; if $w=2k$, each negative clause has exactly one TRUE literal. Complementing the assignment interchanges the two excesses. None of these necessary conditions proves a witness exists.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
At the root $s_x^\pm=0$ and $u_\pm=2k$. Substitute in Theorem~\ref{thm:split}; both signs have nonnegative excess on SAT assignments. Vanishing sign excess is a sum of nonnegative clause excesses and makes every term zero.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact syntactic sign-even filter of full root tickets}
In the inherited positive-defect affine description at the root, $\eta=\nu=0$ and the total ticket cost equals $2k$. Every uncovered ternary original clause has one compressed cost-zero odd-parity GF$(2)$ option, three cost-one positions corresponding to exactly two TRUE literals, and one cost-two option with all three TRUE literals. Let
\[
P_k(z)=(1+3z+z^2)^{2k},\quad
B_k=[z^{2k}]P_k(z)^2,\quad
B_k^{\rm split}=\sum_{j=0}^k[z^{2j}]P_k(z)\,[z^{2k-2j}]P_k(z).
\]
\begin{lemma}[Necessary parity pruning of original-root ticket descriptions]
Every consistent complete root ticket affine system has \emph{even} positive and negative total ticket cost. Thus $B_k-B_k^{\rm split}$ descriptions are syntactically excluded \emph{before} individually solving their affine equations, and every actual SAT model survives with exactly the inherited unique ticket signature. The numbers are abstract ticket descriptions, not distinct SAT models, machine query lower bounds, or a guaranteed reduction of real solver time.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In an inherited complete ticket branch, each sign's declared cost is the sum of true-count excesses of its clauses, modulo two. The compressed cost-zero ternary odd-parity row could otherwise allow three rather than one TRUE literals, but that difference is two and does not change sign parity; exact global-budget saturation moreover excludes any uncharged excess from a consistent complete branch. Corollary~\ref{cor:root} makes both actual sign excesses even for every source model. Odd-sign-cost descriptions therefore cannot yield a source model. Count exactly the remaining two-sign even costs by multiplying the two clause-option polynomials. The inherited disjoint union's all-model soundness and uniqueness are unchanged.
\end{proof}
