\section*{Fixed source and combined-rule theorem}
For odd $r\ge3$, let $W_r$ be an odd rim $1,\ldots,r$, hub $0$, all rim edges and all spokes. For each vertex $v$ and color $c\in\{0,1,2\}$ use one-hot original Boolean input $z_{v,c}$. Source ALO: $z_{v,0}\vee z_{v,1}\vee z_{v,2}$; AMO: $\neg z_{v,a}\vee\neg z_{v,b}$ for $a<b$; and each graph edge $uv$ yields all three BAN clauses $\neg z_{u,c}\vee\neg z_{v,c}$. There are $3(r+1)$ appearing Boolean variables, $10r+4$ authentic source clauses, and $N_r=\Theta(r\log(r+2))$ explicit signed-index DIMACS-style bits.
\begin{lemma}[Individual source indispensability]
All $r+1$ ALO clauses and all $6r$ edge BAN clauses are indispensable: deleting any one makes the source CNF satisfiable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the hub ALO, leave hub uncolored, alternate 0/1 around the first $r-1$ rim vertices and give the last color 2. For rim ALO at $i$, leave $i$ uncolored, give the hub color 2 and alternate 0/1 along the other $r-1$ rim vertices, which form a path. For spoke BAN at $i$ and color $c$, give $i$ and hub color $c$, alternate the two other colors along the rim path obtained by removing $i$, and rotate color labels as needed. For rim-edge BAN $i,i+1$ at color $c$, color both endpoints $c$, alternate colors $c,c+1$ along the remaining path of even length $r-1$, and give the hub the third color. In every case all AMO clauses hold and exactly the selected original source clause is false. The original source contains $s=7r+1$ individually indispensable clauses.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Established /341 source-capacity lemma]
Any sound conservative refutation DAG with $s$ individually indispensable original source axioms and $q$ unit-paid inference nodes of at most two explicit premises must have $q\ge s-1$, including when the fixed permitted rules are mixed adaptively and subproofs are shared.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every indispensable source belongs to the contradiction's ancestry, or its deletion witness would satisfy the used sources and, by source-local soundness and conservative definitions, all descendants. The connected undirected ancestry has $s+q'$ vertices and at least $s+q'-1$ dependency edges, but at most $2q'$ edges by native inference indegree. Thus $q\ge q'\ge s-1$.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Mixed odd-wheel linear bracket]
For \emph{every} odd $r\ge3$ and every legal native $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ refutation, including arbitrary RES/AND3/GF(2)/LOCAL and separately paid conservative DEF mixtures,
\[
\boxed{7r\le W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(W_r)\le18r-6.}
\]
Hence this nonaffine infinite family has $W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(W_r)=\Theta(r)=\Theta(N_r/\log(N_r+2))$, not superpolynomial. The exact minimum is not certified.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The indispensable-source lemma and native source-capacity lemma give $7r$. For the upper bound, use only strict source-authenticated binary Resolution. Fix hub color $h$ and rim-$1$ color $c\ne h$. On each rim edge $i,i+1$, resolve the at-least-one clause at $i+1$ with the hub-spoke BAN for color $h$ and with the rim-edge BAN for color $c$ to produce the conditional implication
$(\neg z_{0,h}\vee\neg z_{i,c}\vee z_{i+1,c'})$,
where $c'$ is the other rim color not equal to $h$ or $c$. Chain these implications by one strict pivot on each later rim vertex, then resolve with the closing rim-edge BAN. Since the odd rim leaves $r-1$ even alternations, it yields $(\neg z_{0,h}\vee\neg z_{1,c})$ after $3r-3$ paid steps per pair $(h,c)$. There are six such pairs. For each of three $h$, resolve rim-$1$ ALO against its two blockers and spoke BAN in three more nodes to obtain $(\neg z_{0,h})$. Finally resolve the hub ALO against three such negative units in three steps. The total is $6(3r-3)+9+3=18r-6$. Every intermediate uses the exact original signed sources or earlier certified resolvents; no DEF export or LOCAL syntax is needed.
\end{proof}
