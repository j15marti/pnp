\section{BUILD I: exact source grammar and affine projection}
Take a canonical physical source $F=A\wedge C$ on $n$ Boolean variables,
where each width-three clause belongs to exactly one \emph{complete} group
of the four signed clauses falsifying $x_i\oplus x_j\oplus x_k=b$,
with three distinct names, and all other clauses have width one or two.
The source indices and starting byte offsets of all four signed members
are authenticated by a fresh deterministic parse; unsupported encodings
return UNKNOWN or INVALID. Let $Z$ contain precisely the variables appearing
in $C$, and let $Y$ be the remaining variables.

Gaussian elimination either produces a source-indexed XOR inconsistency,
or an affine parametrisation of every full satisfying assignment of $A$:
\[
 x_i=c_i\oplus\langle v_i,t\rangle,\qquad
 t\in\mathbb F_2^r,\qquad r=n-\operatorname{rank}(A).
\tag{1}\label{eq:param}
\]
Here $c_i\in\mathbb F_2$ and $v_i\in\mathbb F_2^r$ are computed from the
original row system. Put
\[
 \mathcal V=\{v_i\ne0:i\in Z\}\quad\text{as a set of distinct vectors},
 \qquad R_Z=\{z:\exists y\ A(y,z)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Copies and complements of one form correspond to the same vector.

\begin{theorem}[Exact affine-bijunctive projection characterisation]
\label{thm:char}
For a consistent affine system $A$, the following conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] (i) The distinct nonzero vectors in $\mathcal V$ are linearly independent.
\item[] (ii) $R_Z$ is definable by a conjunction of unary and binary
parity equations $z_i=d$, $z_i\oplus z_j=d$, and the tautology.
\item[] (iii) $R_Z$ is closed under coordinatewise Boolean majority.
\end{enumerate}
These equivalences concern the \emph{affine projection alone}; they are not
necessary for the conjunction $A\wedge C$ to be easy for other reasons.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume (i). Introduce one new coordinate $u_v=\langle v,t\rangle$
for each distinct $v\in\mathcal V$. Linear independence makes
$t\mapsto(u_v)_{v\in\mathcal V}$ surjective, so all $2^{|\mathcal V|}$
combinations occur. Every observed variable is either the constant $c_i$
or equals $c_i\oplus u_{v_i}$. Thus $R_Z$ has exactly the constraints that
pin the constant coordinates and equate or complement coordinates with
identical $v_i$, proving (ii). Unary and binary affine equalities are
preserved by majority, so (ii) implies (iii).

For (iii)$\Rightarrow$(i), suppose distinct nonzero vectors are dependent.
Choose an inclusion-minimal dependency $v_1\oplus\cdots\oplus v_s=0$.
Distinctness and nonzero vectors force $s\ge3$; minimality makes the rank
of these $s$ vectors exactly $s-1$. Therefore the projection of $R_Z$
onto one representative variable per vector is a full affine parity
hyperplane $\sum_{j=1}^s z_j=d$. This hyperplane fails majority closure:
translate a fixed satisfying row by the three even-parity vectors
$1100\cdots0$, $1010\cdots0$, and $0110\cdots0$. All three translated
rows satisfy its parity equation, while their coordinatewise majority
differs from the fixed row in the first three coordinates, changing total
parity. Projection preserves majority closure, giving a contradiction.
Thus $\mathcal V$ is independent.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: uniform source-certified SAT/UNSAT selector}
\begin{theorem}[Charged independent-affine-interface selector]
\label{thm:selector}
One fixed deterministic total polynomial-time program recognises the
specified XOR3-quartet-plus-2CNF grammar, computes~\eqref{eq:param},
and checks independence of $\mathcal V$. On every recognized formula
with (i) of Theorem~\ref{thm:char}, it decides \emph{both} SAT and UNSAT,
produces a source-checked full SAT assignment or a complete independently
replayable source-indexed GF(2)/2SAT refutation, and writes one 18- or 19-byte
terminal YES or NO child preserving the decision answer.
For input length $N\ge512$, this child satisfies
\[
 |K(F)|\le N^{3/4}.
\]
The recognized class enlarged with both terminal formulas is closed and
terminates after the subsequent direct base check. On unrecognized sources
the program returns UNKNOWN, without claiming a contracted child.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Authenticate and recover all quartets; Gaussian elimination computes
~\eqref{eq:param} or a full-row XOR contradiction whose source indices
are checked by replaying their sum. Recovering the forms and their rank
is polynomial in the complete physical bit length $N$.

If $\mathcal V$ is independent, create $u_v$ for each distinct nonzero form
and replace every literal of every original clause of $C$ by either a constant,
$u_v$, or $\neg u_v$, using $x_i=c_i\oplus u_{v_i}$. Remove true and
tautological clauses and detect a false empty clause. The resulting $C'(u)$
is an ordinary 2CNF with at most $N$ clauses and at most $N$ names.
Theorem~\ref{thm:char} gives \emph{exactly}
\[
 \exists x\,\bigl(A(x)\wedge C(x)\bigr)
 \iff \exists u\,C'(u),
\tag{3}
\]
with no dependence on an uncharged SAT oracle. Ordinary implication-graph
SCC decides $C'$ and, on NO, supplies a cycle through $u_j,\neg u_j$:
explicit source-identified implication paths in both directions.
Each path edge is rederived from its specific source clause under the
recomputed affine projection. On YES, solve the full-row linear equations
$\langle v,t\rangle=u_v$ (consistent because of independence), reconstruct
$x$ and check every original clause. On NO, the source quartet identities,
affine elimination and implication paths certify inconsistency.

There are polynomially many source clauses, rows, matrix bits, graph edges,
failed elimination/rank tests, path entries and witness bits. All parsing,
source-offset validation, row provenance, intermediate storage, lookup,
verification, and physical output are polynomial in $N$ on both answer
classes for the \emph{same} deterministic program. The program is total:
failed source recognition or dependent forms return UNKNOWN, not a guessed
label. A base source is decided without recursion. The fixed YES and NO
encodings have lengths 18 and 19, both below $512^{3/4}$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Semantic firewall.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:char} explains why the ARC216 majority counterexample
does \emph{not} preclude basis changes: $(u\oplus v)\vee w$ is not 2CNF
in coordinates $(u,v,w)$, but after using $q=u\oplus v$ it becomes
$q\vee w$. This alone does not handle inputs with a minimal affine-form
dependency of length at least three \emph{among the variables in $C$}.
Arbitrary XOR3-plus-2CNF is NP-complete by the exact 3SAT reduction in
ARC213; a blanket polynomial extension to that entire language would
settle $P=NP$, which is not asserted here.

\section{Connected, high-width, large-backdoor controls without units}
Let $L\ge2$ and $m=L^2$. At grid site $j$ introduce six distinct names
$x_j,y_j,p_j,a_j,b_j,c_j$ and four source-authenticated XOR3 groups
\[
 x_j\oplus y_j\oplus p_j=0,\quad
 p_j\oplus a_j\oplus b_j=0,\quad
 p_j\oplus a_j\oplus c_j=0,\quad
 p_j\oplus b_j\oplus c_j=0.
\tag{4}
\]
The last three equations force $p_j=0$ and $a_j=b_j=c_j$;
the first then forces $x_j=y_j$. Put signed positive binary clauses
$(x_j\vee x_k)$ and $(y_j\vee y_k)$ across every horizontal and vertical
grid edge $\{j,k\}$. The full formula is connected, has \emph{no unit
clauses}, and contains $16m$ signed 3-clauses plus $4L(L-1)$ binary clauses.
Its affine nullity is $2m$, while the distinct nonzero affine forms on the
alien support number $m$ and are independent. All-positive assignments
on the $x,y$ coordinates are extendible, so the base family is SAT.
The UNSAT controls add all four signed binary clauses on $x_0,x_1$,
or alternatively a longer path of equality clauses and one disequality.

\begin{proposition}[Syntactic-structure adversarial separation]
\label{prop:grid}
For every $L$, the source family in (4) has primal treewidth at least $L-1$
and minimum \emph{syntactic} strong-2CNF clause-hitting backdoor at least $m=L^2$.
It contains more than $m$ nonentailed binary clauses, and for $L\ge8$ has
more than $\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor$ nonentailed alien clauses.
Nevertheless Theorem~\ref{thm:selector} decides the family and its declared
UNSAT controls in deterministic polynomial time.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The primal graph contains an $L\times L$ grid as a subgraph on
$x_0,\ldots,x_{m-1}$, yielding the standard grid treewidth bound.
Each site contains a proper ternary quartet on a set of names disjoint
from all other sites. A syntactic strong-2CNF hitting set must meet at
least one ternary scope in every site, hence has size at least $m$.
The grid binary clauses are not entailed by the affine equations because
all $x_j$ values may be chosen freely; each can be individually falsified.
The same is true of the additional contradictory binary controls.
The recovered affine projection onto the observed $x,y$ names imposes only
$x_j=y_j$ and no relation between different sites; its $m$ distinct
nonzero coordinate forms are linearly independent. Thus the new solver
applies regardless of high raw width, raw alien count and backdoor size.
These static measurements say nothing about lower bounds for arbitrary
adaptive solvers.
\end{proof}

recognition, affine projection, 2SAT SCC, and certificate validation
are each charged on both sides; neither restriction receives a free
answer bit. Each class has polynomial simultaneous time and peak space
bounds, but maximizers of time and space across distinct inputs are
not combined into a fictitious same-run contradiction.
