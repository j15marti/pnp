\section*{Preselected model and four falsifiers}
Use the original unit-weight binary-premise Rmix341 calculus with free authenticated original clauses, charged RES, AND3, XOR, LOCAL-CONVERT, and conservative source-independent two-input VARDEF with three addressable defining clause slots. All statements below use only RES and VARDEF, including \(q\leftrightarrow(a\land b)\) for signed literals \(a,b\). No semantic oracle, source-factor DEF, unit-cost PARITY4 or free selector pin is inserted. Let \(W_R(F)\) minimize distinct paid shared-DAG inference nodes over all legal refutations (free optimal choice), and \(\overline W_R(N)\) maximize \(W_R\) over valid UNSAT sources of encoded bit length at most \(N\), empty maximum zero.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] F1: arbitrary selector bits can only be fixed with uncharged axioms. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: conservative paid false-wire lemma below.
\item[] F2: all selectors can be identified with one variable. \textbf{REJECTED}: destroys syntactic freshness; retain distinct outputs and use a signed injective renaming.
\item[] F3: every XOR/AND3/CONVERT proof is invariant under replacing original selector inputs with auxiliary wires. \textbf{OPEN}. Avoid mixed restriction completely by replaying only the RES+VARDEF ER subsystem.
\item[] F4: a source-to-satisfaction-output proof would certify UNSAT on a SAT input. \textbf{FALSE}; it merely authenticates the clauses already asserted. Both labels tested.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{lemma}[Paid conservative selector pins]\label{pins}
Given two distinct occurring source variables \(x,y\), and any desired \(b\)-bit selector word, one may introduce \(b\) fresh distinct selector output variables, each provably false, with \(b+3\) charged VARDEF nodes and \(b+3\) charged binary RES nodes; no defining clause used is tautological.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Pay for \(u\leftrightarrow x\land y\), \(v\leftrightarrow\neg x\land\neg y\), and \(z\leftrightarrow u\land v\). The verified slots \((\neg z\vee u)\), \((\neg u\vee x)\), \((\neg z\vee v)\), and \((\neg v\vee\neg x)\) yield \((\neg z\vee x)\), \((\neg z\vee\neg x)\), and then unit \(\neg z\), using three actual RES steps. For each selector introduce one new \(q_i\leftrightarrow z\land x\). Its slot \((\neg q_i\vee z)\) and \(\neg z\) yield \(\neg q_i\) in one actual RES step. All Boolean definitions are conservative; the signed image of selector value 0 is \(q_i\), and of selector value 1 is \(\neg q_i\). Distinct \(q_i\)'s preserve injectivity and gate freshness. A valid UNSAT source with fewer than two occurring variables already has the empty source clause or opposite unit clauses, giving a constant-cost Resolution proof.
\end{proof}

\section*{The universal source and its authenticated compiler}
Conditional on the existence of one fixed uniform total correct polynomial-time general CNF-SAT decider \(A\), use a length-\(b=O(N^2)\) selector matrix \(s\) to encode any normalized general CNF with at most \(N\) clause slots and \(N\) variable positions. Let \(D_A(s)\) decode it and run \(A\); malformed matrix strings are recognized separately. Build uniform polynomial-size Boolean circuits with signed-input AND gates and signed negated output literals representing OR, for
\[
V(s)=\mathrm{Valid}(s),\quad
U(s)=[D_A(s)=\mathrm{UNSAT}],\quad
Q(s,y)=\bigwedge_{j\ {\rm active}}\bigvee_{\lambda\ {\rm selected\ in}\ j}\lambda(y).
\]
Define the single family \(H_{A,N}\) as all 3-slot Boolean definitions of these circuits plus the output units \(V,U,Q\). External correctness of \(A\) proves that every \(H_{A,N}\) is UNSAT, but does not give short R refutations. Its encoded length is \(N^{O_A(1)}\).

\begin{lemma}[Authenticated source compilation]\label{compiler}
For every valid UNSAT original CNF \(F\) with encoded length at most \(N\), there is a signed injective renaming of all original variables of \(H_{A,N}\) and an actual paid RES+VARDEF derivation from the authentic clauses of \(F\) of every signed-renamed source clause of \(H_{A,N}\), at additional cost at most one fixed polynomial \(p_A(N)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Empty-clause and one-variable UNSAT sources are refuted directly. For other \(F\), remove tautological and duplicated rows, preserving its satisfiability and citing authentic retained clauses; injectively rename its occurring variables into the assignment positions of the selector circuit. Reserve distinct conservative copy-gate outputs \(w_i\leftrightarrow x\land x\) for unused witness variables, rather than illegally identifying different original names. Instantiate each selector bit by the signed distinct false pins of Lemma~\ref{pins}. Every Boolean gate of \(H_{A,N}\) is now introduced topologically as one charged source-independent VARDEF, exposing all its checked slots. In particular, no defining clause is taken as a free source. The outputs \(V\) and \(U\) have fixed values 1 on this selected input because the input is valid and the assumed correct \(A\) rejects it; derive these units by at most two actual RES steps per gate, propagating previously derived signed input units along the concrete deterministic circuits. A bare decider rejection is never an axiom.

For each active original source clause \(C_j=\bigvee_{\lambda\in C_j}\lambda(y)\), use the selected indicator gate \(t_{j,\lambda}\leftrightarrow s_{j,\lambda}\land\lambda(y)\). A derived selector-one unit plus the actual gate slot gives \(\neg\lambda\vee t_{j,\lambda}\). Resolve these implications against original authenticated \(C_j\) to derive \(\bigvee_{\lambda\in C_j}t_{j,\lambda}\). The OR tree is built from signed AND gates and contains for each masked literal a polynomial-size RES derivation of \(\neg t_{j,\lambda}\vee c_j\), where \(c_j\) is that row's output. Resolving all leaves against their implications yields unit \(c_j\). Padded/inactive rows have outputs proved by their selector-zero units and fixed padding gates, with no extra presumed original source. A paid AND tree over the \(c_j\) units derives unit \(Q\). Hence all original clauses of \(H_{A,N}\) are either already-paid DEF slots or genuinely derived output units from \(F\). The uniformly implemented per-gate unit verification, selector pinning, source references, gate construction and clause implications use polynomially many nodes and bits. Free selection of a shortest proof is not used to build this source compiler.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[No mixed-proof restriction assumption]\label{bridge}
Let \(\mathrm{ER}_2\) be the same authenticated binary RES and source-independent signed-input VARDEF rule with one charge for each gate and each resolution node. For every one fixed total correct polynomial-time SAT decider \(A\), the explicit \(H_{A,N}\) satisfies, for one polynomial \(p_A\),
\[
\boxed{\forall N\ge1:\qquad \overline W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(N)
\le W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(H_{A,N})+p_A(N).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each UNSAT original \(F\) with \(|\mathrm{enc}(F)|\le N\), use Lemma~\ref{compiler} to produce every source clause of a signed injective renaming of \(H_{A,N}\), at the stated polynomial cost. Take any shortest shared-DAG \(\mathrm{ER}_2\) refutation of \(H_{A,N}\) and replay every RES and VARDEF instruction \emph{without restricting its rule or its original-variable support}, with sources replaced by the compiler's derived units/DEF slots and all new outputs alpha-renamed to preserve freshness. Genuine RES pivots and signed gate semantics are invariant under injective signed renaming. Existing LSPV-5 simulation REFEREE299 confirms these are legal nodes of the unchanged Rmix341. The replay adds at most \(W_{\mathrm{ER}_2}(H_{A,N})\) charged nodes, shared steps once, and ends at the empty clause. Maximize over all \(F\) of length at most \(N\); no arbitrary mixed XOR/AND3/CONVERT proof has been restricted or retyped.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Exact missing implication:} polynomial \(\mathrm{ER}_2\) proofs of \(H_{A,N}\) for some fixed polynomial-time correct \(A\) would imply B3, but the \emph{external} correctness of \(A\) does not establish those proofs. This is a stronger short-proof hypothesis than the prior LSPV-1 353 theorem, which instead required both unproved arbitrary-mixed restriction RT and source compilation SC. The present ER-only route removes RT and supplies a charged SC, but is not known equivalent to B3.
