\section{Source-exact four-variable lift}
For each original $x_i$ introduce four distinct target source variables $a_i,b_i,p_i,q_i$; set $q_0=q_n$. Replace source literal $x_i$ by $\neg a_i$ and $\neg x_i$ by $\neg b_i$, obtaining an all-negative ternary conjunction $T_F$. Add
\[
C_i^\pm=(a_i\vee b_i\vee(\pm p_i)),\qquad
D_i^\pm=(q_{i-1}\vee\neg q_i\vee(\pm p_i)).
\]
Define
\[
G(F)=T_F\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^{n}
(C_i^+\land C_i^-\land D_i^+\land D_i^-). \tag{1}
\]
All signs here refer to the same named original source coordinates when translated; $p,q$ are explicitly charged fresh target variables.

\begin{theorem}[Valid connected exact-regular lift; PROVED/EXACT]
For every nonempty valid $F$ (necessarily $n\ge3$), $G(F)$ is proper, duplicate-free, incidence-connected, signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF with
\[
n_G=4n,\quad m_G=m+4n=16n/3,\quad k_G=m_G.
\]
It is constructed and source-validated in $O(m\log(m+2))$ word operations, including all output writes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two positive occurrences of $x_i$ become two negative occurrences of $a_i$ and its two negative occurrences become two negative occurrences of $b_i$; $C_i^\pm$ contribute the two required positives of each. Each $p_i$ occurs once of each sign in each of $C_i^\pm,D_i^\pm$. Each $q_i$ is positive in both $D_{i+1}^\pm$ and negative in both $D_i^\pm$. No new clause repeats a variable when $n\ge3$, necessarily true for any valid proper source. Different gadgets have distinct supports; the source ternaries are all-negative. The $q$-cycle connects the $D$ gadgets, the $p_i$ connect each $C_i$ to $D_i$, and the $a_i,b_i$ connect source residual clauses to this network. Thus the incidence graph is connected. Sorting and validation are polynomial and every output record is charged.
\end{proof}

\section{Sharp exact existential projection}
The assignment-wise identity $(A\vee u)\land(A\vee\neg u)\equiv A$ gives
\[
G(F)\equiv T_F\land
\bigwedge_{i=1}^n(a_i\vee b_i)\land
\bigwedge_{i=1}^n(q_{i-1}\vee\neg q_i).\tag{2}
\]
The last cycle is SAT, e.g. all $q_i=0$, and the $p_i$ disappear. Thus exact existential projection to the $2n$ boundary variables is
\[
K(F)=T_F\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^n(a_i\vee b_i). \tag{3}
\]
The positive binaries are pairwise variable-disjoint. All negative ternaries are proper and unique; each boundary variable occurs \emph{exactly twice} in those ternaries and never positively there. A ternary never contains both $a_i,b_i$ for one $i$.

\begin{theorem}[NP-complete saturated pair/path kernel; PROVED reduction + KNOWN source hardness]
The polynomial-time recognizable image language of $G(F)$ is NP-complete. So is the image kernel language of $K(F)$ subject to its displayed exact grammar. Canonical complement contraction yields
\[
p=2n,\quad \delta=m_G-2p=m,\quad P=n=3\delta/4,
\quad |K(F)|=\delta+P=7\delta/4,\quad |U|=2n=3\delta/2.
\]
Therefore the PAIRPATH73 upper bounds on projected binary clauses, boundary cardinality, and final clause count are attained on an NP-complete sublanguage even when every unmatched ternary variable appears just twice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any source assignment $x$, set $(a_i,b_i)=(1-x_i,x_i)$ and $q_i=p_i=0$. Under this canonical assignment every replaced literal has the original truth value, so $F$ SAT implies $G(F)$ SAT. Conversely, given an assignment satisfying (3), each $(a_i,b_i)$ is one of $(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$. Replace every $(1,1)$ by $(1,0)$. This preserves its positive binary and can only \emph{increase} the truth of the all-negative ternaries. Then $x_i=1-a_i$ is a satisfying original assignment. Hence $F$, $K(F)$ and $G(F)$ have identical SAT labels. Canonical source-image membership is polynomially verifiable by reading all clauses, validating paired gadgets and inverting the name/sign substitution; malformed originals can be mapped to one fixed valid NO image. NP-hardness follows from the known NP-completeness of proper unique monotone signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-SAT \cite{docker}. The original all-negative $T_F$ clauses have no complementary partners, whereas the four gadget clauses per $i$ form precisely two pairs. The $q$-cycle projects away and leaves $P=n$ disjoint positive binaries, proving every count.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact inverse and canonical witness; PROVED/EXACT]
Source-image recognition, recovery of the original $F$, and extraction of \emph{one} original witness from a satisfying $K(F)$ assignment are uniform polynomial-time operations. They do not compile the full backward occurrence-transversal relation. The $3^n$ assignments satisfying positive binary pairs can be reduced without loss of SAT to the $2^n$ exclusive-one assignments $(a_i,b_i)\in\{(0,1),(1,0)\}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In state $(1,1)$ set $b_i=0$; this cannot falsify any negative ternary. All other states remain unchanged. The inverse signed source is read directly from the negative ternary records. The exact backward Circuit-SAT characterization still selects one true literal occurrence per original OR3 clause with shared source identity, but no single-bit decider is forced to construct all such choices. In exact-$(2,2)$ sources opposite-occurrence conflicts are disjoint copies of $K_{2,2}$, not a mandatory representation.
\end{proof}

\section{Noncircular amortization and uncovered algorithms}
Fix $\mathcal C_{\rm split}$ to validate the source, contract \emph{every} canonical complementary pair, existentially project away $p,q$, then explicitly emit one record for each of the $n$ disjoint positive binaries and $m$ negative ternaries. Its objective and allowed records precede its cost measure. If $j$ distinct required positive binary records have been emitted, set $\rho_j=n-j$. Each charged record write decreases $\rho$ by at most one, so at least $n$ binary outputs and $m+n=7\delta/4$ total clause outputs are necessary and sufficient in this calculus.
