\section{All-input sign-specific recursion and full decision construction}
Fix any coherent ordinary signed CNF $G_j$ whose active variables are original source names. It may be nonmonotone, noncanonical, contain shorter clauses, and have no equal sign quotas. Let $v_j$ be the number of occurring variables; let $p_j$ be a bound on \emph{each} sign-specific occurrence count of any variable, and $w_j$ a bound on clause width. Define the source-authenticated primal graph on active variables and choose a deterministic ascending-ID maximal independent set $I_j$. No input clause contains two selected pivots. For every selected pivot, perform ordinary Davis--Putnam elimination using all positive-negative parent pairs, discard tautological resolvents and exact duplicates, retain original-name and clause provenance, then iterate. This is an explicitly computed policy; no favorable oracle is supplied.

\begin{lemma}[Polarity-squared conservation, any signed residual]
If every active variable of $G_j$ has at most $p_j$ positive and at most $p_j$ negative occurrences, each clause has at most $w_j$ literals, and $I_j$ is primal independent, then $G_{j+1}=\exists_{I_j}G_j$ pointwise for \emph{every} remaining-variable assignment and every later compatible pin, and
\[
 p_{j+1}\le p_j^2,\qquad
 w_{j+1}\le2(w_j-1),\qquad
 \Delta(\mathrm{primal}(G_j))\le2p_j(w_j-1),\qquad
 |I_j|\ge\Big\lceil\frac{v_j}{1+2p_j(w_j-1)}\Big\rceil .
\]
For a variable that has become semantically or syntactically absent, subsequent existential quantification is free of constraints but not a grant of uncharged computational work.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix an occurrence of a surviving literal $y^\sigma$ in a parent clause. If the parent survives, this occurrence is retained once. Otherwise its clause contains exactly one chosen pivot $x$, and that parent produces at most $p_j$ non-tautological resolvents because $x$ has at most $p_j$ opposite-sign parent clauses. Every such resolvent retains $y$ with the SAME sign $\sigma$ or is discarded. No parent clause can be used by two chosen pivots. Summing over the at most $p_j$ original $\sigma$-occurrences of $y$ yields at most $p_j^2$ output $\sigma$-occurrences, even if clauses become mixed-sign or repeated. Resolving two width-$w_j$ clauses removes both pivot literals, giving at most $2(w_j-1)$ surviving literals. Each variable has total occurrence count at most $2p_j$, hence at most $2p_j(w_j-1)$ distinct primal neighbors. Greedy maximal independent selection covers at most $1+2p_j(w_j-1)$ vertices per pivot, establishing the cardinality bound. The standard single-pivot resolution identity $\operatorname{DP}_x(G)\equiv\exists_xG$ is exact pointwise; independent pivots have disjoint parent-clause sets, so the substitutions commute. Ordinary clauses may be mixed, and no canonical child assertion is used.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform three-stage all-input upper bound]
There exists ONE deterministic uniform source-authenticated, malformed-rejecting decision algorithm $D$ on the canonical language that acquires and applies the three primal-independent Davis--Putnam batches and then exhaustively evaluates the remaining ordinary CNF. For all valid canonical originals $F$ with $n$ variables and original input bit-length $N$, its final active variable count and fully charged total decision time satisfy
\[
 v_1\le\frac{8n}{9},\qquad v_2\le\frac{64n}{75},\qquad
 \boxed{v_3\le\frac{2048n}{2415}=0.848033126\ldots n},\quad
 \boxed{T_D(F)\le P(N)2^{2048n/2415}}
\]
for one fixed polynomial $P$ independent of $F$. Explicit intermediate CNFs, provenance and simultaneous index/selection buffers use at most polynomial peak writable space. This is \emph{not} the FLMV half-power workspace regime.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The canonical initial counts are $p_0=2,w_0=3$. The lemma gives the exact valid upper parameter vector
\[
 (p_0,w_0,\Delta_0)=(2,3,8),\quad
 (p_1,w_1,\Delta_1)=(4,4,24),\quad
 (p_2,w_2,\Delta_2)=(16,6,160).
\]
Thus each residual retains at most the fractions $8/9$, $24/25$ and $160/161$ of its preceding occurring variables. Their product is $2048/2415$. In contrast, the earlier coarser total-occurrence recurrence $d_{j+1}\le d_j^2$ used $d_2=64$, degree $320$ and the weaker $(64/75)(320/321)$. The sign-specific bound gives $d_2\le32$ and degree $160$. With only three fixed phases, all occurrence, width and possible resolvent multiplicity bounds are absolute constants ($p_3\le256,w_3\le10$). There are $O(n)$ generated clauses per fixed phase; source grammar validation, primal graph discovery, failed selection checks, resolution pairs, tautology/dedup checks, source IDs, index/head movements and output serialization have polynomial native bit complexity and polynomial peak combined space. Finite exhaustive evaluation of at most $2^{v_3}$ remaining assignments decides SAT/UNSAT exactly. On SAT, a reverse pass can optionally reconstruct eliminated pivot values, and a full original-clause scan verifies a witness. On UNSAT, the exhausted ordinary-CNF table plus the sequential resolution identities supplies a complete, possibly exponential proof. Neither witness output nor a particular machine transcript is assumed as a lower-bound obligation for a one-bit decider.\end{proof}

\section{Same-original forward-circuit contraction versus true decision work}
The original source-private fan-in-two evaluator uses $g_0=2m+(m-1)=3m-1$ binary gates, counting unary input NOT as free. For the first source-disjoint batch $I$ with $k=|I|$, write $H$ for the $m-4k$ untouched clauses. For every $x\in I$, write $A_{x,1},A_{x,2}$ for its positive two-literal source-clause remainders, and $B_{x,1},B_{x,2}$ for its negative two-literal remainders. The actual quantified factor has the exact forward circuit
\[
 Q_x=(A_{x,1}\land A_{x,2})\lor(B_{x,1}\land B_{x,2}),\qquad
 \exists_I F=H\land\bigwedge_{x\in I}Q_x.
\]
Each $Q_x$ is compiled with four two-input OR gates and three AND/OR gates, seven in total. With the same non-sharing source-private architecture, this source-authenticated forward projection has exactly
\[
 g_1=2(m-4k)+7k+(m-3k-1)=3m-4k-1.
\]
Thus $g_0-g_1=4k\ge4n/9$ within THIS declared topology. This does not compare unrestricted minimum circuit sizes, and it does not charge the computation of $\exists z\,(\exists_I F)(z)$. The original A9 already admits a \emph{29-gate} matched-pair evaluator (LSPV-4 A9-PETERSEN328), versus the 35-gate private evaluator and 31-gate private first-projection circuit. Therefore a private-template gate saving cannot establish a representation-independent circuit decrease.

\begin{lemma}[Tiny unrestricted minimum, not SAT hardness]
In the previously authenticated nine-variable affine-plane canonical $A9$ with positive clauses $123,456,789,147,258,369$ and negative clauses $159,267,348,168,249,357$, eliminating original $x_1$ gives the local factor
\[
 Q_{x_1}=((x_2\lor x_3)\land(x_4\lor x_7))\lor
 ((\neg x_5\lor\neg x_9)\land(\neg x_6\lor\neg x_8)).
\]
In an \emph{unrestricted} acyclic fan-in-two Boolean gate model with unary NOT/free wires, this eight-variable local factor has exact minimum binary gate count seven.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The displayed circuit has four OR gates, two AND gates, and one final OR gate, giving seven. Exhaustively or directly, each of its eight distinct variables is essential: choose the other literal of its pair false, its partner conjunctive pair true, and disable the opposite disjunct. For any circuit whose output essentially depends on $r$ distinct input variables, contracting free unary gates gives a connected undirected dependency graph with $r$ necessary input nodes and $g$ binary-gate nodes. The graph has at least $r+g-1$ edges but at most $2g$ gate-input edges, so $g\ge r-1$. Taking $r=8$ establishes the lower bound seven. This small local gate minimum is not the unrestricted minimum of $A9$, nor a complexity lower bound on SAT decision.\end{proof}
