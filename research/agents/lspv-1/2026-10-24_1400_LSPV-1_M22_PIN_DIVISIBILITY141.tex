\section{Exact theorem and proof}
\begin{lemma}[Counting and properness]\label{count}
Every nonempty \(F\in\CM\) has \(3m=4k\), \(m_+=m_-=2k/3\), \(3\mid k\), and \(k\ge6\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every original name occurs in two positive and two negative literals, while every clause has three literals of one polarity. Hence \(3m_+=3m_-=2k\). The next smaller positive multiple \(k=3\) would require two distinct positive clauses on only three names, impossible under duplicate-free proper syntax.
\end{proof}
Let \(F\in\CM\), \(|\operatorname{vars}(F)|=k\). Pin \(d\) distinct names \(D\). Suppose an explicit normalized child \(G\in\CM\) admits an injection
\[
\iota:\operatorname{vars}(F)\setminus D\longrightarrow
\operatorname{vars}(G)
\]
whose images are all \emph{active distinct child names}. The injection is a contractor-specific hypothesis. In particular, it is not imposed on all possible deciders.
\begin{theorem}[Survivor-retaining child floor; restricted exact]\label{floor}
The child's active name count \(k'\) and clauses \(m'\) obey
\[
\boxed{k'\ge\max\{6,3\lceil(k-d)/3\rceil\}
=\max\{6,k-3\lfloor d/3\rfloor\}},\qquad
m'=4k'/3.
\]
For \(d=1,2\), \(k'\ge k\) and \(m'\ge m\). For \(d=o(k)\), an ordinary explicit dense indexed child has length \(\Omega(k\log k)=\Omega(N)\), so it cannot be \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) bits for any fixed \(\lambda<1\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The injective survivor map implies \(k'\ge k-d\). Lemma~\ref{count} applied to \(G\) requires the next multiple of three not smaller than \(k-d\), with minimum six. The stated formula follows since \(3\mid k\). Each child clause count is \(4k'/3\); the \(d=1,2\) lower bounds equal \(k\). Dense indexed encodings require \(\Theta(k'\log(k'+2))\) bits, proving the asymptotic length statement for \(d=o(k)\). This argument is syntactic and does not assume or prove conditional SAT preservation.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Sequential one-pin two-level guard]\label{two}
For valid children \(G_0,G_1,G_2\in\CM\) where each \(G_{i+1}\) results from pinning one active name of \(G_i\) and normalizing with an injective map retaining all other current active names, \(k_2\ge k_1\ge k_0\) and \(m_2\ge m_1\ge m_0\). The same holds by induction at any finite depth. Explicit monotone dense record bit lengths cannot decrease.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{floor} separately with \(d=1\) at each \emph{actual} level. No shortcut uses an uncharged ancestor or a different decider.
\end{proof}
For clarity, one illustrative fixed-width indexed convention, not asserted to match the repository production input or FLMV machinery, has
\[
b(k)=\lceil\log_2(4k/3+1)\rceil,\quad
L(k)=3b(k)+1+(4k/3)(1+3b(k)).
\]
Every proper child under the one/two-pin survivor assumption has \(L(k')\ge L(k)\). At \(k=198\), \(L(198)=7420\) bits; with \(d=1,2\) the floor remains 7420, while \(d=3\) allows only the lower-bound size \(L(195)=7308\).
