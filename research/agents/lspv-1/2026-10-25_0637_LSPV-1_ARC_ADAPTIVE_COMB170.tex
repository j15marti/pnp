\section{Exact raw-pin obstruction and adaptive branching distinction}
\begin{proposition}[Literal retention; architectural limitation only]
If a proper ternary CNF has $m$ original clauses and each variable occurs in at most $\Delta$ clauses, fixing $q$ distinct variables and performing only raw clause/literal restriction leaves at least $m-\Delta q$ original, unchanged ternary clauses. For a standard explicit ordinary-CNF encoding where $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$, obtaining a fully encoded child of size $O(N^\lambda)$, fixed $\lambda<1$, requires
\[
q\ge (m-O(N^\lambda))/\Delta=\Omega(N/\log N).
\]
This statement does not cover resolution, learning, semantic compression or direct SAT computation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At most $\Delta q$ original clauses touch any selected variable. Every untouched clause survives as a distinct original ternary clause. The ordinary explicit child must encode each such clause; with fixed arity and a standard dense index encoding, its length is at least the number of survivors.
\end{proof}
An exhaustive \emph{Cartesian} enumeration of $q$ pins has $2^q$ branches, but a fully adaptive pruned binary Shannon tree can have a single depth-$q$ spine and just $q+1$ leaves. Moreover the number of guest-SAT calls may be less than the number of leaves if off-spine branches have locally \emph{paid} refutations. Thus the earlier exact M22 Cartesian fanout lemma cannot legitimately be promoted to an all-adaptive-tree lower bound.

\section{A proper-3CNF forced-choice comb}
Let $x_i,a_i,b_i$ be pairwise fresh names. Define
\[
 U_i=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\zeta\in\{0,1\}}
(x_i\vee a_i^\epsilon\vee b_i^\zeta),
\quad z^0=z,\ z^1=\neg z.
\]
Each gadget contains four proper ternary clauses; each of its three names occurs exactly four times. Checking all four $(a_i,b_i)$ possibilities or resolving pairs shows that $U_i\equiv x_i$. Accordingly the $x_i=0$ branch has an explicit constant-work UNSAT refutation from four residual binary clauses, and the $x_i=1$ branch deletes those clauses. For an ordinary proper-3CNF $H$ disjoint from all gadgets set
\[
 \Phi_{k,H}=\left(\bigwedge_{i=1}^k U_i\right)\wedge H,
\qquad \SAT(\Phi_{k,H})=\SAT(H).
\]
Choose $k=u^4$, and an $H$ of encoded size $u^{1+o(1)}$ or less. Under fixed dense serialization $N=|\Phi_{k,H}|=u^{4+o(1)}$.

First genuine typed level: certify the forced choices for $u^4-u^2$ gadgets and serialize/reparse the ordinary child
\[
 C_1=\Phi_{u^2,H},\qquad |C_1|=u^{2+o(1)}=N^{1/2+o(1)}.
\]
Second genuine typed level: certify the remaining $u^2$ forced choices and serialize/reparse ordinary $C_2=H$,
\[
 |C_2|\le u^{1+o(1)}=N^{1/4+o(1)}
      =|C_1|^{1/2+o(1)}.
\]
Both levels preserve the SAT and UNSAT labels exactly, have verified syntax/promise membership, and admit one original-name-consistent path. The off-spine UNSAT tests are \emph{not} free queries.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The gadget identity proves both-label correctness for root, first child and terminal child, including when $H$ is UNSAT. On an off-promise input, the validator's correctness-preserving fallback to $A$ uses parent-scale resources; there is no free parent A call.
\end{proof}

\section{NP-complete padded promise is not a near-linear FLMV-hard reduction image}
\begin{proposition}[Polynomial padding cancels the apparent improvement]
Restrict $H$ to the NP-complete proper monotone-3SAT-4 language of Darmann and D\"ocker, where source variables occur at most four times \cite{docker}. Then the corresponding canonical comb family is NP-complete and all its variables occur in at most four clauses. For a source $H$ of length $L$, a standard polynomial reduction to the comb family taking $u=\max\{L,2\}$ has output length $N=L^{4+o(1)}$. Composing a direct on-demand generator for that reduction with (1), without materializing the entire quartic parent, gives the conservative certificate
\[
 T_H(L)\le L^{\max\{4,t\}+o(1)},\qquad
 S_H(L)\le L^{\max\{2,s\}+o(1)}.
\]
Therefore NP-completeness of the promised comb family supplies \emph{no FLMV strict-box transport} via this quartic reduction.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The gadget identity gives a many-one reduction preserving both SAT and UNSAT. Its $4u^4$ constant-arity clauses are generated by a fixed polynomial transducer; the tag/identifier serialization contributes only logarithmic factors. The comb member validates in polynomial time; NP-completeness follows from the cited source language. For the declared algorithm, a streaming/indexed generator can expose gadget fields on demand using logarithmic index state; scanning or validating the $L^{4+o(1)}$ virtual parent costs $L^{4+o(1)}$ time, while the simultaneously retained largest  ordinary child uses $L^{2+o(1)}$ bits and the source decider uses $L^{s+o(1)}$. This yields the displayed virtual-input composition envelope. If one instead first eagerly materializes the  entire quartic reduction output in writable memory, its peak storage is $L^{4+o(1)}$ rather than the displayed $L^{\max\{2,s\}+o(1)}$; this alternative must be charged separately. Neither implementation produces a strict FLMV exponent pair. This is a compiler/algorithm \emph{upper certificate}, not a lower bound on every conceivable algorithm or alternative local reduction.
\end{proof}
