\section*{The escape-owner cut}
For the archived checker, admitted signed views of a paid owner
$z=a\wedge b$ are the clean members of
\[
  (-z\vee a),\qquad(-z\vee b),\qquad(z\vee-a\vee-b).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Restricted complementary escape-owner cut]
Assume an accepted archived-/386 packet whose original SOURCE clauses omit
fresh auxiliary $z$, and whose paid owner declares $z=a\wedge\neg a$.
If any record descended from one of that owner's admitted facets is an ancestor
of the terminal empty clause, then the terminal ancestry contains a later
authenticated DEF facet with literal $+z$. That facet belongs to a later paid
owner whose declared inputs depend on $z$ in some polarity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Original SOURCE contains only edge literals and the guard, so it omits $z$.
For $z=a\wedge\neg a$, the positive-$z$ view is tautological and the checker
filters it. The two admitted facets contain only $-z$. A strict Resolution
result is the canonical union of its parents after deleting the chosen pivot
pair, so Resolution never introduces a literal absent from both parents.
Therefore the first primitive clause containing $+z$ cannot be SOURCE, RES, or
a facet of the complementary owner. In this checker it must be a DEF facet of
another paid owner. Gate inputs must be original variables or already declared
outputs, so this owner is later in declaration order and depends on $z$.

Along a path from a facet containing $-z$ to the empty clause, $-z$ must
eventually disappear. Only strict Resolution can remove it, using a parent
containing $+z$. Backward tracing reaches the primitive positive facet just
identified, which is therefore in terminal ancestry. \qed
\end{proof}

\section*{CEGAR and cost-sound abstraction consequence}
Both live and dead packets are concrete accepted paths. A CEGAR loop may not
refine away the live packet as spurious. An abstraction recording only the
Boolean fact $z=0$, or normalized clauses without owner provenance and terminal
ancestry, merges materially different states. A sound restricted abstraction
must retain at least paid owner order, signed input ports, authenticated facet
identity, chronological parents, terminal ancestry, and count-once cost. It may
use /494's dead-cone invariant only after proving absence of a later $+z$ escape
facet.

Concrete-step inclusion and completed-refutation preservation remain unproved
for the full historical mixed calculus. No lower-cost abstraction is
authenticated for AND3, GF(2), LOCAL-CONVERT, branch, context, discharge, join,
weakening, deletion, learned clauses, or compact affine rows. Thus P2-D1,
P2-C3 and P2-B1 remain OPEN, and P2-B2/P2-B3 remain separately OPEN.
