\section{A max-space SAT-side restart race}
Fix a total deterministic exact proper-3CNF decider $A$.  At a satisfiable
prefix of original variables, construct the two proper-3CNF children
$G_{i,0},G_{i,1}$ using the exact four-clause force gadget
\[
\operatorname{FORCE}(\ell;a,b)=
\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{+,-\}}
(\ell\vee a^\epsilon\vee b^\delta).
\tag{1}
\]
At least one child is SAT.  For a run of $A$ on $G$, write $t_A(G)$ for its
halting time and $S_A^{\le B}(G)$ for the maximum live guest space during its
first $B$ steps.

\begin{theorem}[Geometric serial restart race, PROVED/EXACT]\label{th:race}
There is a label-blind deterministic selector which, given two children of
which at least one is SAT, returns a SAT child after fewer than $8t$ guest
steps, where
\[
t=\min_{v:G_{i,v}\in SAT}t_A(G_{i,v}).
\tag{2}
\]
At no time are two guest configurations live.  If $B<2t$ is the final
power-of-two budget, peak working space beyond the two read-only child
encodings is
\[
O\!\left(\log t+max_{v\in\{0,1\}}S_A^{\le B}(G_{i,v})\right).
\tag{3}
\]
All restarts and discarded work are charged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For budgets $1,2,4,\ldots$, run child zero from its initial configuration for
the current budget, then discard its configuration; do the same for child
one.  Stop only on an actually computed SAT answer.  A child already observed
to halt UNSAT can be marked dead by one bit.  Let $B$ be the first budget at
least $t$, so $B<2t$.  Earlier rounds charge less than
$2(1+2+\cdots+B/2)<2B$ guest steps.  The final round charges at most $2B$.
Thus the total is below $4B<8t$.  The runs are serial, so their guest spaces
are maximized rather than added; a binary budget and simulator counter use
$O(\log t)$ bits.  The procedure never receives the correct label or winning
child in advance.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Search-to-decision with adaptive resource ledger]
On SAT input $F$ with $n$ original variables and child length at most $M$, a
total witness machine $W_A$ exists with
\[
T_{W_A}(F)=O_A\!\left(nM+sum_{i=1}^n
\min_{v:G_{i,v}\in SAT}t_A(G_{i,v})+t_A(F)\right)
=O_A(nM+nT_A^+(M)).
\tag{4}
\]
Its peak live space is
\[
O\!\left(M+S_A(F)+\log t_{\max}+
\max_{i,v}S_A^{\le2t_i}(G_{i,v})\right),
\tag{5}
\]
with maxima rather than simultaneous-state sums.  It emits and verifies one
$n$-bit witness.  On UNSAT input it returns after the initial exact decision.
\end{corollary}
The construction charges child materialization, both failed and successful
simulations, restarting, scheduling, retained prefix, reconstruction and
verification.  It neither asserts that $A$ internally self-reduces nor forces
an UNSAT certificate.

\section{Exact lower-bound transport remainder}
Suppose a compatible theorem lower-bounds every total SAT witness generator
by $L_{\rm wit}(F)$ on a selected satisfiable $F$.  Applying that theorem to
$W_A$ yields, for an algorithm-dependent constant $c_A$,
\[
L_{\rm wit}(F)
\le c_A\left(nM+nT_A^+(M)+T_A(F)\right).
\tag{6}
\]
Absorbing $T_A(F)\le T_A^+(M)$ gives only
\[
T_A^+(M)\ge
\frac{L_{\rm wit}(F)-c_A nM}{c_A(n+1)}.
\tag{7}
\]
As in every valid transport inequality, the numerator must be positive.
Known source-query bounds are at most input order and are consumed by the
$nM$ child-construction term.  Equation (7) is therefore an exact
\textbf{conditional transport lemma}, not a new lower bound.

Role 3's timeout dualization is also correct: if one fixed exact decider is
polynomially bounded on all sufficiently long instances of either label
alone, a deterministic cutoff decides both labels in polynomial time.
Consequently, the assertion that every decider has superpolynomial sequences
on both labels is equivalent to $P\ne NP$, not independent evidence for it.

\section{Independent CUTFLOW85 audit}
For $u,v\in\{0,1\}^k$, Role 1's grouped source is
\[
E_k(u,v)=\bigwedge_iQ(\lambda_{u_i}(x_i);a_i,b_i)
\wedge\bigwedge_iQ(\lambda_{v_i}(x_i);c_i,d_i),
\tag{8}
\]
where each four-clause $Q(\ell;\cdot,\cdot)$ is equivalent to $\ell$.
Thus $E_k(u,v)$ is SAT exactly when $u=v$, with $n=5k$, $m=8k$ and
$W=32k+2$ fixed-width record words.

In the declared model the source head moves one adjacent word per charged
step, and every persistent bit outside the input is part of one of at most
$2^\sigma$ portable configurations.  At the cut after the first $j$ source
blocks, accepted diagonal strings with different $j$-bit prefixes must have
different complete crossing transcripts; otherwise deterministic splicing
accepts an unequal pair.  The same argument from the opposite side gives
dimensions $j$ and $k-j$.  Fixed-length configuration codes with an endmarker
give
\[
\mathbb E T_A(E_k(X,X))ge
\frac{(k-1)(k-2)}{\sigma+1}.
\tag{9}
\]
Together with travel to a label-critical last record,
\[
T_A^{\max}(k)=\Omega\!\left(k+\frac{k^2}{s+c_A+1}\right)
\tag{10}
\]
under Role 1's convention that $s$ counts the complete live portable state.
The splice, distinct-cut summation and arithmetic check out.

The scope boundary is decisive.  Addressed access compares all $k$ pairs in
$O(k)$ probes and $O(\log k)$ workspace.  Interleaving the same constraint
blocks likewise permits a one-sweep comparator.  Hence the cut profile is a
physical representation/access parameter, not a SAT-semantic obstruction.
Moreover equality is in $P$, and (10) is polynomial even in its native model.
It is the strongest surviving restricted rung here, not an NP-complete
all-decider amplification.

\section{CANCEL-JOIN69 and trajectory hostile control}
The Role-2 cyclic strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ family independently replays as
$n=6q,m=8q$, with $2q$ complementary pairs and $4q$ unmatched ternaries.
Its raw unmatched-clause strong-2CNF deletion backdoor is $h=q$.  Nevertheless
the source quota identities force each single-use pivot into two opposite
unmatched occurrences.  Exact Davis--Putnam elimination produces $2q$
tautological resolvents, deletes all unmatched ternaries, and leaves $2q$
binary clauses.  A paid source recognizer, elimination trace, SCC solver and
witness replay run in $O(m\log(m+2)+n)$ word time.

Thus an initially large vector $(\delta,h)=(4q,q)$ can collapse to $(0,0)$
under a sound adaptive trajectory.  A negative-socket twist makes some
resolvents non-tautological, so pivot count alone does not predict even this
shortcut.  This independently supports the user's requirement that parameters
be state variables, not a fixed scalar lower-bound formula.
