\section*{Executive verdict}
P versus NP remains open.  The strongest current circuit/certificate/ARC chain has two
independent load-bearing gaps.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The weighted inequality
\[
  \sum_i m_i^d\le M^{d-1}\sum_i m_i,\qquad M=\max_i m_i, d\ge1,
\]
is exact arithmetic, but it bounds verification cost only \emph{after} a sound and
complete all-UNSAT certificate tree has been supplied.  It does not prove that every
UNSAT input has such a tree of affordable total leaf volume.
\item The recovered signed exact-(2,2) compiler is linear-size at the materialized
word level, but the corpus does not yet prove a polylogarithmic-time, polylogarithmic-
workspace random-access bit accessor in the FLMV model.  Eager materialization retains
an input-scale buffer; a naive lazy accessor can rescan the source to rank occurrences.
Thus FLMV is the correct ordinary-SAT endpoint \emph{only conditionally on a proved
same-machine local-access transport theorem}; NP-completeness of $2,2$-CNF-3 alone is
insufficient.
\end{enumerate}

The assertion that $P=NP$ produces a deterministic $N^{1.802}$-time,
$N^{o(1)}$-space SAT algorithm is \retired.  $P=NP$ gives some finite polynomial
exponent $K$, not this particular quantitative pair.  Assuming the near-boundary pair
is an additional hypothesis; refuting only that pair would not separate P from NP.

\section*{Primary-source preflight and model match}
The two major obstacles are (i) all-UNSAT certificate coverage and (ii) bit-local
same-machine transport.  The following primary-source checks were made.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item van Melkebeek's survey, Theorem~1.3, states that for
$ (c-1)d<1$ or $cd(d-1)-2d+1<0$, SAT cannot simultaneously have a
co-nondeterministic random-access $N^c$ algorithm and a deterministic random-access
$N^d$-time, $N^e$-space algorithm.  These are two explicit machine hypotheses, not a
consequence of $P=NP$.  See
\url{https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~dieter/Papers/sat-lb-survey-fttcs.pdf}.
\item Theorem~6.3 in that source instead pairs full SAT decision circuits of size
$N^c$ with a deterministic small-space algorithm for $\Sigma_2\mathrm{SAT}$.  It does
not concern a circuit that represents a satisfying assignment for one formula or a
per-instance relation.  The claimed numerical $q\ge1.444997\ldots$ was not located in
the primary theorem text or in the accessible continuation artifacts and remains
\openstatus; any valid use must first derive it and supply a full all-length SAT
decision-circuit family.
\item Bathie--Williams proves a depth lower bound for polylogtime-uniform NAND circuits
solving SAT.  It is a \known{} result in that circuit/uniformity model, not a generic
lower bound for arbitrary RAM computations or arbitrary certificate trees:
\url{https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2024.10}.
\item FLMV is a time--space lower bound for ordinary SAT on its random-access model.
A promise solver for signed exact-(2,2) formulas reaches that endpoint only through an
explicit reduction with charged length, bit access, time, and workspace:
\url{https://doi.org/10.1145/1101821.1101822}.
\end{itemize}

Barrier-transfer classification: the van Melkebeek and FLMV statements are directly
transferable only after exact machine and reduction premises are proved.  Proof-system
coverage, VLSI routing, and cryptographic collision/binding are useful structural
analogies requiring new transfer theorems.  Treating them as unconditional arbitrary-
algorithm lower bounds is a failed transfer.

\section*{Exact coNTIME polarity lemma}
Let $V(F,y)$ be a deterministic verifier with an explicit time bound.  Define malformed
or timed-out evaluations to return $0$.  Then the following is the correct certificate
orientation for SAT in coNTIME:
\[
\begin{aligned}
F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}&\Longrightarrow \exists y\;V(F,y)=1,\\
F\in\mathrm{SAT}&\Longrightarrow \forall y\;V(F,y)=0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The co-nondeterministic machine guesses $y$ and \emph{rejects} exactly when
$V(F,y)=1$; it accepts malformed/time-out branches.  Hence all branches accept on SAT,
whereas at least one branch rejects on UNSAT.  This is \proved{} by the definitions.
The opposite treatment of malformed/time-out guesses creates false UNSAT evidence.

For a branching certificate, each internal node must name a sound semantic partition,
and every resulting child must be covered.  A complete certificate includes the tree,
all child formulas or reproducible access procedures, every leaf certificate, and the
work needed to validate source indices, gate accesses, compatibility, and coverage.

\section*{Smallest proper-3CNF coverage counterexample}
Define the proper 3-CNF
\[
U(x;a,b)=
(x\vee a\vee b)\wedge(x\vee a\vee\neg b)\wedge
(x\vee\neg a\vee b)\wedge(x\vee\neg a\vee\neg b).
\]
It is exactly equivalent to the unit clause $x$.  Consequently the parent is SAT,
the child $x=0$ is UNSAT, and the child $x=1$ is SAT.  A purported parent-UNSAT
certificate containing only a valid UNSAT proof of the $x=0$ child is therefore
unsound.  This is a concrete counterexample to any leaf-volume rule lacking a verified
complete partition; it does not attack a rule that explicitly pays for complete
coverage.

The reproducer exhaustively checked all eight assignments and found parent models
$100,101,110,111$, no $x=0$ models, and all four $x=1$ continuations.  It also checked
the weighted inequality on 46,650 finite integer cases.  Status: \comp{} finite
verification plus \proved{} symbolic inequality; the global affordable-coverage
existence theorem remains \openstatus.

\section*{Weighted-volume lemma: exact scope}
For $d\ge1$ and $m_i\le M$,
\[
m_i^d=m_i\,m_i^{d-1}\le m_i M^{d-1};
\]
summing proves the inequality.  A sound conditional upper bound is therefore
\[
T_V(F)\le T_{\mathrm{tree}}(F)+T_{\mathrm{coverage}}(F)
+\sum_i\bigl(|\pi_i|+T_{\mathrm{leaf}}(F_i,\pi_i)\bigr),
\]
with every term charged.  If the final summand is $m_i^{d+o(1)}$, total leaf volume is
$V$, and maximum leaf size is $M$, the leaf contribution is
$M^{d-1+o(1)}V$.  This is \cond{} on certificates for \emph{all} UNSAT inputs.

The decisive missing lemma is:
\begin{quote}
For every ordinary UNSAT input $F$ of length $N$, there exists a uniformly verifiable,
sound, complete adaptive certificate tree whose complete encoding, construction checks,
and leaf verification fit the claimed time and space budget, with all children forming
an exact partition and no parent-size SAT call hidden in strategy selection.
\end{quote}
No reviewed proof-system, polynomial-calculus, parity, Tseitin, or model-specific
terminal result establishes this statement for all UNSAT formulas.

\section*{Exact arithmetic at $d=1.802$}
Put $d=901/500$.  The second strict condition of Theorem~1.3 requires
\[
c<\frac{2d-1}{d(d-1)}=\frac{651000}{361301}
=1.801821749732\ldots .
\]
At $c=d=901/500$ the second expression is
\[
d^2(d-1)-2d+1=\frac{32201}{125000000}>0,
\]
and the first expression has $(d-1)d=361301/250000>1$.  Thus the exact
diagonal $(1.802,1.802)$ satisfies neither strict premise.  This arithmetic is
\proved{}; it says nothing by itself about P versus NP.

\section*{When FLMV is the correct endpoint for signed exact-(2,2) 3-CNF}
Let a source of length $N$ be mapped to a target of length
$M=N^{\lambda+o(1)}$.  Suppose an addressed target bit can be generated in
$N^{g+o(1)}$ time and $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ writable/index space, and the target decider uses
$M^{t+o(1)}$ time and $M^{s+o(1)}$ space.  A fully lazy simulation has the charged
bound
\[
t_{\rm source}\le\max\{r,g+\lambda t\},\qquad
s_{\rm source}\le\max\{\eta,\lambda s,\kappa\},
\]
where $r$ covers source validation and $\kappa$ the retained controller/ancestor state.
An eager simulation has
\[
t_{\rm source}\le\max\{r,\lambda,\lambda t\},\qquad
s_{\rm source}\le\max\{\eta,\lambda,\lambda s,\kappa\}.
\]
These are \proved{} accounting identities for the declared simulations, not a theorem
that a favorable compiler exists.

The recovered PC22 compiler maps a proper 3-CNF with $m$ clause occurrences to a
distinct-clause signed exact-(2,2) formula with $72m$ variables and $96m$ clauses,
using a 15-variable/19-clause fixed padding gadget.  This is not the clausewise-monotone
M22 target.  The separately mentioned 348/463 gadget was not found in the accessible
source bundle, Drive search, or current GitHub corpus and is not substituted for 15/19.
The constants do not change $\lambda=1$ under dense indexed encodings, but they do
matter to exact child indices and two-level grammar.

Current blocker: the compiler pairs occurrences and densely renumbers clones.  The
available proof establishes a polynomial-time materialized compiler, not an FLMV-local
bit accessor.  Eager storage has exponent $1$, outside the desired strict space bound.
A naive lazy accessor can require a source scan ($g=1$), making a target $t=6/5$
simulation cost $11/5$ in the source.  Only a proved $g=o(1)$, $\eta=o(1)$ accessor
would preserve the pair $(6/5,1/2)$ up to $o(1)$.  Two genuine recursive levels also
require the second-level input to retain the promised grammar or a paid adapter.

Therefore: FLMV is the correct endpoint for $2,2$-CNF-3 only \cond{} on this
same-machine, all-input, bit-local transport.  Without it, the endpoint application is
\openstatus, not false.

\section*{Hostile controls}
The surviving target must permit direct decisions, Horn/2SAT/SCC, Gaussian elimination
for affine/Tseitin instances, graph-factor algorithms, DPLL, learning, caching, replay,
source rereads, alternate representations, and strategy switching.  Resolution or
polynomial-calculus hardness for a restricted proof system does not imply all-UNSAT
certificate hardness.  An easy padded family or an oracle-oblivious direct decider does
not refute worst-case hardness, but it does refute a universal per-instance resource
claim.  SAT and UNSAT soundness are independent obligations.
