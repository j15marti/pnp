\section*{Exact restricted theorem}
Let $P$ be the 45-record seed, with 17 SOURCE and 28 signed RES records, one
declared AND2 owner $[0,8,1,4]$, no displayed DEF facet, and total charge
\[
W(P)=28+1=29.
\]
There are 45 pre-record gaps.  For a multiset $S$ of $k$ gaps, insert in each
chosen gap the valid zero-paid facet $[1,-8]$ and rebase every later parent
index.  Independently choose $e\in\{0,1\}^{28}$; for each selected RES
occurrence exchange its two parents and negate its signed pivot.  Independently
choose $b\in\{0,1\}$; if $b=1$, exchange the two inputs of the same owner to
$[0,8,4,1]$.

For every $k$ in the checker's admitted finite input domain (that is, the
constructed JSON parses, its indices are representable, and execution
completes), all three transformations commute.  Their result is accepted by
the pinned checker, retains the same SOURCE multiset and \texttt{spec}, ends in
the empty clause, and has $W=29$.  For fixed $k$ the number of distinct raw
presentations is
\[
\boxed{\binom{k+44}{44},2^{29}}.
\]

Proof: identical inserted facets occur in maximal runs separated by original
non-DEF records, so their run lengths recover the gap multiset.  Every RES
choice is recoverable from its nonzero pivot sign (and parent order), and the
owner bit is recoverable from its ordered input pair; hence presentations are
distinct.  Reindexing touches only chronological parent coordinates.  A signed
RES exchange accompanied by pivot negation leaves its exact resolvent
unchanged.  AND input exchange leaves all three defining facets invariant as a
set, in particular $[1,-8]$.  Thus the transformations commute record for
record.  Inspection of the pinned checker shows that each inserted facet is
zero-paid, while RES count and the single owner charge remain fixed.  This is a
source-code theorem about this declared transition relation, not about the
missing historical mixed dispatcher.
