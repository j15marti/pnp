\section{Target and falsifiers stated before search}
\textbf{Target claim.}  Independently reproduce the strongest new exact-one result after the quotient-template theorem: for separated head-only and follower-only variables, with each follower lying in at most two per-head parity components, fixing head colors reduces full Q realizability to exact 2-SAT; determine whether the mechanism extends through degree three or high-girth critical families.

\textbf{Falsifiers.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A prescribed pair of head colors for which the six-clause gadget disagrees with adjacency in $C_5$ (difference $2$ or $3$ mod 5).
\item A supported separated-head formula on which independent direct T5 enumeration and independent component/2-SAT reconstruction disagree, or a reconstructed assignment failing an original Q clause.
\item A claimed critical core with a nonwitness deletion, or a four-variable induced UNSAT subinstance.
\item A degree-three compatibility relation still closed under the Boolean majority operation, or an incidence-girth claim contradicted by a local gadget cycle.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Exact degree-two reconstruction}
For each head $x$, let $B_x$ be the graph on follower variables with an edge $uv$ for every clause $Q(x,u,v)$.  An odd cycle in $B_x$ is a sound UNSAT certificate.  Otherwise each connected component $C$ has a fixed bipartition side $\sigma_{x,C}(u)$ and one Boolean selector $s_{x,C}$, so
\[
 R(a_x,c_u)=s_{x,C}\oplus\sigma_{x,C}(u).
\]
Fix a color tuple for the $h$ heads, normalizing one head to zero under the five rotations.  A follower in $r\le2$ components permits some of its five colors for each selector tuple, or forbids that tuple.  Each forbidden tuple becomes a CNF clause of width $r\le2$.  Linear-time SCC 2-SAT either supplies all selectors, after which a follower color is recovered and every source clause is reverified, or rejects that head tuple.

\paragraph{Theorem (PROVED/EXACT).}
On the declared separated grammar with follower component degree at most two, this deterministic procedure is sound and complete, outputs a full original-label witness on SAT inputs, and returns UNSUPPORTED rather than UNSAT outside the degree guard.  With explicit indexed triples it uses
\[
 O\!\left(N/w+(n+m)(1+5^{h-1})\right)
\]
word operations and $O(n+m)$ retained words, including source indices, parity components, the live implication graph, SCC workspace, and the final witness.  These are upper bounds, not lower bounds.  For $h\le2$, parity feasibility alone suffices: choose head colors $(0,2)$, whose joint comparison signature contains all four Boolean pairs.

\section{Six-clause edge gadget and exact restricted hardness}
For head colors $a,b$, define
\[
S(a,b)=\{(R(a,c),R(b,c)):c\in\mathbb Z_5\}.
\]
Independent enumeration proves $S(a,b)=\{0,1\}^2$ iff $b-a\in\{2,3\}\pmod5$.  Four private followers represent the four signatures.  Three Q clauses headed by $a$ form a parity path through the followers by first coordinate; three headed by $b$ form a path by second coordinate.  The gadget is satisfiable for prescribed $a,b$ exactly when those heads are adjacent in the undirected $C_5$.  For every positive ordered head pair the independent checker finds exactly eight follower assignments.

Replacing every edge of an undirected graph $K$ by a fresh gadget yields $|V(K)|+4|E(K)|$ variables and $6|E(K)|$ clauses.  The Q source is SAT iff $K\to C_5$.  Hell--Ne\v{s}et\v{r}il prove fixed-$H$ homomorphism NP-complete for every non-bipartite $H$, so this declared Q subclass is NP-complete.  This is a decision reduction and constructive SAT-witness transfer; it does not give critical-deletion promise hardness or a time lower bound.

\section{New degree-three NOR boundary}
Take three incident head colors $(0,1,2)$.  The five possible follower letters induce the selector-signature relation
\[
\mathcal R_{012}=\{100,101,001,010\}
=\{(x,y,z):y=\operatorname{NOR}(x,z)\}.
\]
This equality is direct and exact, including ties.  The following tuples give explicit closure failures:
\begin{align*}
100\wedge001&=000\notin\mathcal R_{012},&
100\vee010&=110\notin\mathcal R_{012},\\
\operatorname{maj}(100,001,010)&=000\notin\mathcal R_{012},&
100\oplus101\oplus001&=000\notin\mathcal R_{012}.
\end{align*}
Also $000,111\notin\mathcal R_{012}$.  Hence it is neither Horn, dual-Horn, bijunctive, affine, 0-valid nor 1-valid.  Of all 125 ordered triples of head colors, 60 give non-bijunctive signature relations.

\paragraph{Consequence and firewall.}
Schaefer's theorem classifies the unrestricted Boolean CSP using $\mathcal R_{012}$ as NP-complete.  This proves that ``replace every degree-three follower by 2-CNF'' is impossible: the degree-two proof has a genuine algebraic boundary.  It does \emph{not} yet prove NP-hardness for the Q subclass with three precolored heads.  Q selector variables are typed by their head components; arbitrary NOR-circuit wiring, selector equality across types, and internally forcing the relative colors $(0,1,2)$ have not been constructed.  That typed same-source transfer is the next exact target.

\section{Incidence-girth transfer fails for this gadget}
The head-interaction graph may have arbitrary girth, but the Q incidence graph of one six-clause edge gadget already has girth four: two consecutive clauses in a per-head path share both their head and one follower, giving head--clause--follower--clause--head.  Independent BFS finds incidence girth four for both one gadget and the minimized 15-clause critical core.  Therefore the $K\mapsto Q$ reduction cannot establish growing-incidence-girth critical Q hardness, even when $K$ itself has high girth.  This is a model-matched falsification of that transfer, not evidence that another gadget is impossible.

\section{Correct paths and row--block accounting}
The exact head tuple is a useful correct-path parameter, but it is not free.  Encoding one of $5^{h-1}$ normalized tuples needs
\[
k=\left\lceil(h-1)\log_2 5\right\rceil
\]
bits.  Given a candidate tuple, the verifier still pays parity preprocessing, 2-SAT construction/SCC, follower reconstruction, source rereading and witness output.  On a wrong SAT path it must return UNKNOWN or a locally proved tuple failure, never global UNSAT.  On an UNSAT input, one failed tuple proves nothing about the other $5^{h-1}-1$ tuples; deterministic advice removal enumerates all tuples or verifies a separate covering certificate.

For a block of length $m$ with $h\le\alpha\log_5m+O(1)$, the restricted inner cost is $m^{\alpha+1+o(1)}$ under explicit linear per-tuple work.  For $h=\Theta(m)$ it is exponential.  Substitution into the authenticated row--block recurrence remains
\[
T(n)=n^{.845+.35t+o(1)},\qquad S(n)=n^{\max\{.195,.35s\}+o(1)}.
\]
The desired one-step $1.33/.5$ point still requires child exponents $t\le97/70$ and $s\le10/7$.  The current exact solver has $s=1$ in source words (within the numerical child-space gate) but does not have a uniform polynomial $t$ when $h$ grows, and it applies only to the declared Q grammar, not arbitrary CNFs.  Materializing all blocks simultaneously, skipping failed tuples, or receiving SAT/UNSAT polarity would invalidate the accounting.

Buss--Williams' $1.34070$ point concerns deterministic general-SAT decision in its random-access DTISP model.  Nondeterministic SAT witnesses, nondeterministic UNSAT/TAUT branches and proof-system refutations have different theorem scopes.  The present restricted upper bound and correct-path verifier do not inherit any of those lower bounds without a same-machine reduction preserving size, time, space and polarity.
