\section{Arbitrary-balance anytime blocking}
Fix once and for all a rational $\theta\in(0,1)$.  A deterministic total
producer emits $M\ge1$ self-delimited records and EOF, without supplying
$M$, its exponent, or an upper envelope.  Epoch $k$ contains record positions
$2^k,\ldots,2^{k+1}-1$.  Inside epoch $k$, close a block after
\[
 b_k=\left\lceil 2^{\theta k}\right\rceil
\]
records, and close any nonempty partial block at the epoch boundary or EOF.
For fixed rational $\theta=p/q$, $b_k$ is computed uniformly as the least
integer $b$ with $b^q\ge2^{pk}$ using charged polylogarithmic arithmetic.

\begin{theorem}[Asymmetric anytime partition]\label{th:asym}
The schedule is one-pass, never restarts the producer, and uses
$O(\log(M+2))$ live counter bits.  Its contiguous blocks satisfy
\[
 \max_i |B_i|\le M^\theta+1,                              \tag{1}
\]
\[
 q\le C_\theta M^{1-\theta}+\lfloor\log_2M\rfloor+1,
 \qquad C_\theta=\frac{2^{1-\theta}}{2^{1-\theta}-1}.    \tag{2}
\]
Consequently, if analysis proves $M=N^{\mu+o(1)}$, the program---which does
not know $\mu$---has block exponent $\mu\theta$ and block-count exponent
$\mu(1-\theta)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $K=\lfloor\log_2M\rfloor$.  Every block in epoch $k\le K$ has at most
$b_k\le2^{\theta k}+1\le M^\theta+1$ records.  The epoch contributes at most
$2^{(1-\theta)k}+1$ blocks.  Summing the geometric progression gives
\[
 \sum_{k=0}^{K}2^{(1-\theta)k}
 \le C_\theta 2^{(1-\theta)K}
 \le C_\theta M^{1-\theta},
\]
and the additive one per epoch gives (2).  Each record is consumed once;
binary counters, comparisons and the fixed-rational root computation have
polylogarithmic bit overhead.
\end{proof}

The scoped SAT/UNSAT join is partition-oblivious.  A source name open at two
separated records remains live across every intervening boundary, so adjacent
local witnesses that agree on live named boundaries glue to one global
witness.  Conversely a global witness restricts to every block.  Therefore
the theorem preserves both labels for arbitrary epoch boundaries and through
any fixed number of genuinely serialized recursive levels.

\section{The honest asymmetric fusion envelope}
Suppose the explicit closed scoped stream has actual length
$N^{\mu+o(1)}$, maximum live width $\delta\log_2N+O(1)$, producer time
$N^{r+o(1)}$, and producer writable/index state $N^{\eta+o(1)}$.  Applying
the existing boundary-pair construction with Theorem~\ref{th:asym} gives at
one level
\[
 t_\theta\le
 \max\{r,\ \mu+2\delta,\
 \mu(1-\theta)+2\delta+\mu\theta t\},                    \tag{3}
\]
\[
 s_\theta\le
 \max\{\eta,\ \mu\theta,\ \delta,\ \mu\theta s\}.     \tag{4}
\]
The third time term pays all $N^{\mu(1-\theta)+o(1)}$ blocks, all
$N^{2\delta+o(1)}$ boundary pairs, and the same base solver on complete
$N^{\mu\theta+o(1)}$ ordinary children.  The second term pays complete
child generation/checking over all records and boundary pairs.  Space
retains the live producer, one block/child, one boundary assignment and the
guest state.  Source/workspace reads, changed and unchanged writes, indices,
headers, failed pairs, parsing, compatibility, caches and ancestors are not
hidden.  At fixed depth $d$ with common $\theta$, the conservative analogue is
\[
 t_{d,\theta}\le\max\{r,\mu+2d\delta,
 \mu(1-\theta^d)+2d\delta+\mu\theta^d t\},
\quad
 s_{d,\theta}\le\max\{\eta,\mu\theta,\delta,
 \mu\theta^d s\}.                                       \tag{5}
\]
These are implementation upper bounds, not lower bounds on other algorithms.

\section{Direct frontier DP strictly subsumes the fusion target}
Role~1's ARC-DP-DOMINANCE167 was read in full and independently rederived.
On the same explicit valid scoped stream, keep the set of extendible
assignments to the currently live named frontier.  OPEN duplicates rows,
CLAUSE removes falsifying rows, and CLOSE existentially projects the closed
coordinate.  At width $w\le\delta\log_2N+O(1)$, at most
$N^{\delta+o(1)}$ rows are live.  This proves the following.

\begin{theorem}[Same-stream direct-DP dominance]\label{th:dom}
For the producer above, one fixed fused producer/frontier-DP machine decides
the source formula exactly on both labels with
\[
 t_{\rm DP}\le\max\{r,\mu+\delta\},\qquad
 s_{\rm DP}\le\max\{\eta,\delta\}.                       \tag{6}
\]
For every $d\ge1$, every $\theta\in(0,1)$ and all nonnegative declared
parameters, (6) is coordinatewise no worse than (3)--(5).  Thus no choice of
asymmetric blocks, ordinary guest solver or fixed recursive depth can make
this explicit-stream fusion subspine load-bearing before direct DP already
enters the same FLMV box.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The row invariant is exact existential feasibility of the processed prefix
for each live named assignment.  The three event updates preserve it, and the
final empty row is present exactly when the entire formula is satisfiable.
Processing $N^{\mu+o(1)}$ records against at most
$N^{\delta+o(1)}$ rows proves (6), including headers and polylogarithmic
indices.  Equations (3)--(5) all contain $r$ and $\eta,\delta$; their time
contains $\mu+2d\delta\ge\mu+\delta$, and their space contains
$\max\{\eta,\delta\}$.  Hence coordinatewise dominance follows without any
assumption on the guest exponents.
\end{proof}

This is not a lower bound.  It is a positive algorithm and a proof-spine
simplification.  In particular, at $\mu=1$ strict entry follows from the
still-unproved simultaneous normalizer conditions
\[
 \boxed{r<c_\star,qquad \eta<d_\star,qquad
 \delta<\min\{c_\star-1,d_\star\}=\frac{41}{200}.}       \tag{7}
\]
The prior square-root two-level guest condition $\delta<31/800$ is sufficient
for that more elaborate implementation but is no longer the weakest known
receiving obligation.  No ordinary children, solver calls, recursive type
inheritance or block buffers are needed after the stream is available.

An $A$-dependent normalizer in (7) remains a meaningful conditional target:
