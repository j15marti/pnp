\section{Preregistered falsifiers and sources}
Four attacks were fixed before the targeted literature search.
\begin{description}
\item[H1.] Polynomial all-UNSAT certificate coverage is itself a minor
structural lemma.  \status{FALSIFIED}: for an unrestricted ordinary-CNF
proof system it is exactly the Cook--Reckhow $NP=coNP$ boundary.
\item[H2.] Assuming $P=NP$ supplies coverage but also the favorable exponent
needed by van Melkebeek.  \status{FALSIFIED}: the assumed decider has an
unknown finite exponent $K$.
\item[H3.] A fast restricted grammar plus parent-decider fallback retains the
fast branch's exponent.  \status{FALSIFIED}: coNTIME charges the slowest
branch, giving $\max\{c,K\}$.
\item[H4.] A compact selector or witness circuit bypasses certificate
verification.  \status{FALSIFIED as an inference}: wiring size is distinct
from global correctness/totality authentication and from a full decision
circuit.
\end{description}

The source audit used Cook--Reckhow's original proof-system paper
\cite{cr}, van Melkebeek's Theorem~1.3 model and hypotheses \cite{vm}, and
FLMV's indexed random-access model \cite{flmv}.  Current peer material and

\section{The exact qualitative boundary}
Call $V(F,\pi)$ an ordinary-CNF UNSAT proof verifier if it is one fixed
deterministic polynomial-time program satisfying
\[
 F\in\UNSAT\iff\exists\pi\;V(F,\pi)=1.                         \tag{1}
\]
It is polynomially bounded when one polynomial $p$ satisfies
\[
 \forall F\in\UNSAT:\quad\exists\pi,quad |\pi|\le p(|F|),
 \quad V(F,\pi)=1.                                             \tag{2}
\]
Malformed records reject and the runtime is polynomial in the complete
input pair.  This is the verifier form of a Cook--Reckhow proof system,
after the standard polynomial translation between UNSAT CNFs and tautologies.

\begin{theorem}[Cook--Reckhow boundary, specialized to UNSAT]
There exists a verifier satisfying (1)--(2) on all ordinary CNFs if and only
if $NP=coNP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If it exists, $\UNSAT\in NP$.  Since UNSAT is coNP-complete, this gives
$coNP\subseteq NP$; complementing gives the reverse inclusion.  Conversely,
if $NP=coNP$, then $\UNSAT\in NP$, whose definition supplies a polynomial
witness relation and one polynomial length bound.  This is precisely
(1)--(2).
\end{proof}

The theorem is qualitative.  It neither chooses a small degree nor bounds
simultaneous writable/index space.  A grammar restricted to M22, affine
Tseitin, SCC leaves, resolution, polynomial calculus, or a recognized
product family does not meet the theorem's all-ordinary-input premise unless
a paid sound and complete reduction is separately supplied.

\section{Empty-proof absorption under $P=NP$}
Assume only that $P=NP$.  Then a fixed uniform deterministic total exact SAT
decider $A$ exists with, for some finite but unspecified $K$,
\[
 T_A(N)le N^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}.               \tag{3}
\]
Define the zero-bit UNSAT verifier
\[
             V_A(F,\epsilon)=1-A(F).                            \tag{4}
\]
It is sound and complete, has proof cap zero, and its branchwise complement
is exactly $A$.  Thus $P=NP$ does imply polynomial all-UNSAT coverage, even
without any structural certificate.  But its coNTIME exponent is $K$, not a
universal constant extracted from proof length.

\begin{theorem}[Fallback exponent firewall]\label{th:fallback}
Let a fast restricted verifier have all-branch time
$N^{c+o(1)}$.  Extend it to all UNSAT inputs by including one proof mode that
runs (4).  The resulting nondeterministic UNSAT verifier, and hence its
branchwise complemented co-nondeterministic SAT machine, has worst-branch
time
\[
                         N^{\max\{c,K\}+o(1)}.                  \tag{5}
\]
No proof-length reduction changes (5).  If $K\ge2$, this fallback cannot
enter the project threshold
\[
 c_*={651000\over361301}=1.8018217497\ldots.                   \tag{6}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The machine has both the restricted-verifier branches and the fallback
branch.  The complexity class charges maximum branch time, so the upper
envelope is (5); the fallback branch also gives the matching obstruction to
claiming the smaller proved bound for this construction.  Since every
integer $K\ge2$ exceeds the strict threshold (6), that branch lies outside
the desired premise.  Zero proof bits eliminate guess cost, not computation
or work/index storage.
\end{proof}

Padding $A$ with inert steps yields empty-proof verifiers of arbitrarily
large polynomial degree for the same language.  Conversely, a long proof
may have a nearly linear streamed verifier.  Proof length, verifier time,
peak space, circuit size and proof-search time are therefore distinct
resources.  This formalizes why the now-falsified global logarithmic header
cannot be repaired by saying ``use $A$ on uncovered inputs.''

\section{Adaptive certificate and circuit consequences}
LSPV-3's partial-2CNF SCC and paired-affine terminals, and LSPV-4's balanced
stream dictionary, remain sound restricted verifiers.  If every UNSAT input
had a canonical proof of bit length $P\le N^{a+o(1)}$ and the Role-4 verifier
bound applied on every record, its conditional coNTIME exponent would be
$\max\{1,a\}$.  Cook--Reckhow explains why polynomial coverage alone is a
major qualitative statement; Theorem~\ref{th:fallback} explains why the
assumption $P=NP$ does not supply the required quantitative exponent via a
fallback.

The same firewall applies to current circuit and selector objects.
LSPV-1's $J_F$ has compact, locally reconstructible wiring, but the statement
that it is total/correct for all assignments is the missing universal
authentication.  Running the parent decider verifies that statement only at
exponent $K$.  LSPV-2's $O(N\log^3N)$ circuit is a witness verifier, not a
full SAT decision circuit; a short description does not certify its
existential closure.  LSPV-4's exact pinned-output floor remains a theorem
about one explicit compiler, not a lower bound for all decision circuits.
None of these objects supplies the full circuits required by van Melkebeek
Theorem~6.3 or the deterministic small-space machine paired with
Theorem~1.3.

\paragraph{Next decisive lemma.}
The target is no longer qualitative polynomial proof coverage.  It is one
fixed all-ordinary-input adaptive proof verifier with a uniform cap and
worst-branch time exponent strictly below (6), together with the separate
matched deterministic small-space SAT seed.  The proof must exclude
parent-decider fallback, charge malformed and timeout paths, and authenticate
every selector/circuit/affine/SCC terminal from source bits.
