\section{Comparator selector and proper-3CNF encoding}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$ be any
ordered proper 3CNF on $n$ named variables.  For integers $d\ge1$ and
$1\le h\le2^d$, interpret $b\in\{0,1\}^d$ as an unsigned binary integer and
put
\[
 C_{d,h}(b)=[b<h].                                                 \tag{1}
\]
A standard prefix comparator computes $C_{d,h}$ with $O(d)$ fan-in-two
Boolean gates.  Explicitly, scan the bits from most significant to least
significant while maintaining prefix-equality $e$ and already-less $l$:
when the corresponding bit of $h$ is zero update
$e\leftarrow e\wedge\neg b_i$; when it is one update
\[
 l\leftarrow l\vee(e\wedge\neg b_i),\qquad
 e\leftarrow e\wedge b_i.                                        \tag{2}
\]
The output is $l$ (with the constant cases handled directly).

Encode each gate by its bidirectional Tseitin clauses.  A binary clause
$(u\vee v)$ becomes
$(u\vee v\vee z)\wedge(u\vee v\vee\neg z)$, and a unit $u$ becomes its
four-clause proper ternary forcing gadget.  Fresh auxiliaries make every
clause contain three distinct variable identifiers.  Call the resulting
proper formula $K_{d,h}(b,c,y)$, where $c$ is the comparator output and
$y$ denotes its auxiliaries.  For every fixed $b$, the Tseitin variables
are forced consistently and
\[
 \exists y\,K_{d,h}(b,c,y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 c=C_{d,h}(b).                                                     \tag{3}
\]

Now append, with fresh clause-local $a_i$,
\[
 (c\vee\ell_{i1}\vee a_i)\wedge
 (\neg a_i\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})                            \tag{4}
\]
for every source clause.  Denote the complete ordinary proper 3CNF by
$U_{F,d,h}$.

\section{Boundary-support universality}
\begin{theorem}[Exact arbitrary-density support; PROVED/EXACT]
For every proper 3CNF $F$, $d\ge1$, $1\le h\le2^d$, and boundary word
$b\in\{0,1\}^d$,
\[
 \boxed{\SAT(U_{F,d,h}[b])=[b<h]\vee\SAT(F).}                     \tag{5}
\]
Consequently:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U_{F,d,h}$ is always SAT, with the source-independent boundary
choice $b=0$;
\item if $F$ is UNSAT, its feasible boundary support has exactly $h$
points and exactly $2^d-h$ memberships are SAT-hard;
\item if $F$ is SAT, all $2^d$ boundary points are feasible;
\item as $h$ ranges from $1$ through $2^d$, the number of hard projected
memberships ranges over every integer from $2^d-1$ down to zero.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (3) fixes $c$.  If $c=1$, set every $a_i=0$; the first clause of
(4) is true and the second contains $\neg a_i$.  If $c=0$, existentially
eliminating $a_i$ from (4) gives exactly
$\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}$: a true first literal permits
$a_i=0$, a true second or third literal permits $a_i=1$, and when all are
false the pair demands both values.  Conjoining over $i$ proves (5).  Since
$h\ge1$, $b=0$ makes $c=1$, proving the root and support claims.
\end{proof}

The two previous extremes are recovered exactly.  Role~1's
DENSE-BOUNDARY-BYPASS128 is the $h=1$ case: all but one memberships carry
the source bit.  GUARD-CONTINUATION-FIXEDPOINT126 is the
$h=2^d-1$ case after naming the last word $1^d$: one membership carries the
source bit.  Equation (5) interpolates every density between them.

\begin{theorem}[Correlation ceiling; PROVED/EXACT]
The complete boundary function has the $O(d)$-gate representation
\[
 Q_{F,d,h}(b)=C_{d,h}(b)\vee z_F,qquad z_F=\SAT(F).                \tag{6}
\]
Thus one acquisition of $z_F$ determines all $2^d-h$ hard memberships.
They do not constitute $2^d-h$ independent SAT decisions.  Explicitly
writing a truth table still costs $\Omega(2^d)$ output bits, but that is a
typed compilation/output cost, not mandatory one-bit root-decision work.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
This is (5) with the single source-dependent bit named $z_F$.
\end{proof}

\section{Size and ARC transport}
The comparator Tseitin/padding part has $O(d)$ variables and clauses, while
(4) has exactly $m$ new auxiliaries and $2m$ clauses.  Hence
\[
 n(U_{F,d,h})=n+m+O(d),\qquad M(U_{F,d,h})=2m+O(d).                \tag{7}
\]
Under the fixed-width proper-3CNF encoding its length is
$O((m+d)\log(n+m+d))$.  Pinning a hard word $b\ge h$ by ordinary proper
unit gadgets adds $2d$ variables and $4d$ clauses.  For fixed or
$d=o(m)$ boundary length, the ordinary pinned child has
\[
 N_{\rm child}=N_{\rm parent}^{1+o(1)}.                            \tag{8}
\]
It is source-equivalent, so its continuation exponent is $\lambda=1$, not
$1/2$.  A one-call transport through that child gives only
\[
 t'\le\max\{r,t\},                                                 \tag{9}
\]
with compiler/recognizer exponent $r$.  Enumerating the table introduces
the call/output factor but cannot be charged to an adaptive root decider
that selects the known $b=0$ witness.  Therefore neither density nor
support cardinality supplies the missing HEREDITARY-SCOPED10 power shrink.

\section{Adaptive-state and BIT-RESOURCE1 ledger}
The tested uniform policy begins with the ordinary read-only source and no
label oracle.  It charges syntax recognition, comparator validation,
source/header reads, address formation and failed alternatives.  On this
recognized family it chooses $b=0$, evaluates or verifies the comparator,
sets $c=1,a_i=0$, and checks the resulting witness.  It may instead switch
to Horn, SCC, affine elimination, decomposition, caching, direct source
inspection or exact fallback on unsupported inputs.  Favorable bounds are
claimed only on this family; the fallback supplies total correctness, not
an all-input contraction.

The resource vector is
\[
 (U,R_s,R_w,W_w,\Delta_{\rm state},S_{\rm peak},BT,|P|,|R|),      \tag{10}
\]
where primitive work includes unchanged writes, control, addressing,
recognition, selection, switching, conversions, generated pins, solver
calls and verification.  Actual state flips remain distinct from writes;
peak space includes counters, Tseitin/comparator state, buffers, caches and
live ancestors; $BT$ is actual cumulative live-bit time; $|P|,|R|$ record
program and representation sizes.  SAT can return one witness; UNSAT of a
pinned hard child need not output a fixed proof.  The unpinned parent never
requires that UNSAT child to be solved.

This state-adaptive bypass is the decisive quantifier correction: a
component proving exact boundary membership answers a stronger question
than the parent SAT decider is asked.
