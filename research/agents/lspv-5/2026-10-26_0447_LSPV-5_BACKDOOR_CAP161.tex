\paragraph{Quantifier target.}

\[
 \forall F\in\{0,1\}^{N}:\quad

\[

\section{Uniform cap-and-clock normalization}

\begin{theorem}[CAP-CLOCK normal form, \status{PROVED/EXACT}]\label{th:cap}
Let $L$ be a language and $V$ a fixed deterministic proof verifier.  Suppose
there are time-constructible functions $r,T$ such that, for every $x$ of
length $N$,
\begin{enumerate}
\item $x\in L$ iff some proof record $\pi$ of length at most $r(N)$ makes
      $V(x,\pi)$ accept; and
\item every well-formed, malformed, accepting, rejecting, and unfinished
      record of length at most $r(N)$ is forced to halt within $T(N)$ steps.
\end{enumerate}
Then $L\in\mathrm{NTIME}(O(r+T))$, and its complement is decided by the
branchwise complemented co-nondeterministic machine in the same bound.
Conversely, a constant-branching nondeterministic $T(N)$-time machine yields
such a verifier with $r(N)=O(T(N))$ by using its transition choices as the
proof record.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The nondeterministic machine guesses at most $r(N)$ bits, including an
explicit length tag when variable-length records are allowed, then runs the
clocked verifier.  Its acceptance condition is existential, and every branch
has the stated time bound.  Flipping the final result of every halted branch
turns the existential acceptance condition for $L$ into universal acceptance
for $\overline L$ without changing branch time or work space beyond $O(1)$.
For the converse, record the at-most-constant-way transition choice at every
one of the $T(N)$ steps and deterministically replay it, rejecting malformed
or impossible transitions.  No proof finder is used in either direction.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Variable-length repair.}
Raw zero padding is unsound without suffix invariance.  The one-line verifier
``accept iff $\pi$ is exactly the one-bit string 1'' accepts $1$ but rejects
its raw length-two padding $10$.  A canonical length tag repairs this: encode
$\pi$ as $1^{|\pi|}0\pi$ followed by zeros to a common width, reject all
noncanonical encodings, and then run $V$ on the decoded record.  This costs
$O(r)$ time and bits and preserves the exact proof relation.  Therefore H2
does not block normalization, but it forbids silent padding.

\paragraph{Minimum-cost firewall.}
Define
\[
 k_*(x)=\min\{j:V(x,\pi_j)=1\},\qquad k_*(x)=\infty
 \text{ if no proof accepts},                                      \tag{3}
\]
for any fixed enumeration.  Then $k_*(x)<\infty$ iff $x\in L$.
Consequently an exact favorable $k_*(x)$ is a semantic label, not a uniform
machine parameter.  A proof of a length-only upper bound
$\sup_{|x|=N,x\in L}k_*(x)\le r(N)$ is legitimate and does not require
computing $k_*$; merely writing $r(x)=k_*(x)$ into the algorithm is not.
Similarly,
\[
 \min_{\pi:V(x,\pi)=1}t_V(x,\pi)
 \quad\text{does not bound}\quad
 \max_{|x|=N,\,|\pi|\le r(N)}t_V(x,\pi).                         \tag{4}
\]
This is the exact H1/H3 distinction.

\section{Capped backdoor/SCC theorem}

Adopt only the verified Role-3 grammar.  A proof header is a distinct-variable
list $B$ hitting every ternary clause.  For each
$\sigma\in\{0,1\}^{B}$, the verifier forms the source-ID-preserving residual
$F|_\sigma$, which has width at most two, and checks an empty clause or the
standard implication-graph SCC contradiction.  For every valid $B$, Role 3
proved
\[
 V(F,B)=1\iff F\in\UNSAT,qquad
 T_V(N,|B|)\le 2^{|B|}N^{1+o(1)},qquad
 S_V(N,|B|)\le N^{1+o(1)}.                                      \tag{5}
\]
This is a restricted verifier theorem, not a deterministic SAT algorithm.

\begin{theorem}[BACKDOOR-CAP161, \status{PROVED/EXACT}]
Fix a length-only cap $r(N)$ and define $V_r$ to reject immediately unless
$B$ is a well-formed clause-hitting header with $|B|\le r(N)$, and otherwise
to run the clocked verifier in (5).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V_r$ is sound on every SAT input for every cap.
\item Its branchwise complement is a correct co-nondeterministic SAT machine
      iff
\[
 \forall F\in\UNSAT\cap\{0,1\}^{N}\;\exists B:
 B\text{ hits every ternary clause and }|B|\le r(N).             \tag{6}
\]
\item Under (6), its time is at most
      $2^{r(N)}N^{1+o(1)}$ and its peak work/index space is at most
      $N^{1+o(1)}$ in the reported straightforward implementation.
\item No deterministic algorithm for finding $B$ is required for this
      coNTIME conclusion.  Such acquisition becomes necessary only if the
      same object is repurposed as a deterministic ARC contraction step.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Soundness follows directly from (5), including malformed-header rejection.
For completeness, $V_r$ has an accepting branch on exactly those UNSAT
inputs having a valid header below the cap, which is statement (6).  Apply
Theorem~\ref{th:cap} and maximize (5) over $|B|\le r(N)$.  The final claim is
the existential semantics of nondeterministic guessing; it does not excuse
the guess length, verifier time, source reads, malformed branches, or clock.
\end{proof}

\[

\]
with strict slack and with every additional exponent included.  This yields
the exact trilemma:
\begin{enumerate}
\item impose (9): time is favorable, but all-UNSAT coverage (6) is

\item allow $B=\operatorname{Var}(F)$: finite completeness is immediate,

\end{enumerate}

some fixed polynomial exponent, not $d=901/500$, and no theorem here supplies
