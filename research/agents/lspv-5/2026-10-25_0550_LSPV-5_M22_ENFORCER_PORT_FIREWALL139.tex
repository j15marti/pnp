\section{Receiving obligation and falsifiable candidates}
The current contraction spine needs a paid map from an intermediate ordinary
CNF residual back into $\CS$ before a $\CS$-only solver can be called.  The map
must preserve the fixed branch semantics, be uniform, and transport time and
space in one declared machine.  Three archive-derived candidates were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Enforcer repair:} replace every residual clause by D\"ocker's
  monotone enforcer; prediction: semantics and target type are both restored.
\item \emph{Padding choice:} for each of the two binary clauses produced by a
  pin, duplicate one of its two literals; prediction: one of the four choices
  restores every external $(2,2)$ quota.
\item \emph{Two-level closure:} if the first lift is not target-valued,
  re-enforce its clauses; prediction: the second level erases the defect or at
  least contracts the charged representation.
\end{enumerate}
Candidate 1 is 
\textbf{PARTIAL/RESTRICTED}: branch semantics is preserved, but target type is
not.  Candidates 2 and 3 are \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} for this exact
interface.

\section{Primary-source interface}
D\"ocker, arXiv:1912.08032, printed pp.~8--9, constructs a monotone clause
enforcer $S(v_1,v_2,v_3)$ with 99 fresh variables and 133 proper monotone
clauses.  Each port $v_i$ occurs once, every fresh variable occurs exactly
twice positively and twice negatively, and
\[
 \exists\,\text{fresh variables }S(v_1,v_2,v_3)=1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_1\lor v_2\lor v_3=1.
\]
The paper uses this interface to replace duplicate clauses while retaining the
source variables' occurrence counts.  This report imports that theorem; it
does not reconstruct or reverify all 133 internal clauses.

\section{Exact port-conservation theorem}
For a proper monotone 3-multiset clause $C$ (literal multiplicity is allowed
only at the padding stage), let $L_S(C)$ be a fresh copy of $S$ with its three
ports identified, occurrence by occurrence, with $C$.  Complement the copy
for a negative clause.

\paragraph{Lemma 1 (external signed-degree conservation).}
For every external variable $u$ and sign $\sigma$, the signed multiplicity of
$u^\sigma$ in $L_S(G)$ equals its signed multiplicity in the padded source
multiset $G$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Each source literal occurrence is attached to exactly one port occurrence, and
each port occurs exactly once.  All other occurrences belong to fresh internal
variables.  Summing source occurrences proves equality. \(\square\)

\paragraph{Lemma 2 (pin deficit).}
Let $F\in\CM$, pin an occurring variable $x=b$, delete satisfied clauses, and
remove the falsified literal from the remaining clauses.  Exactly two
$b$-true-sign clauses are deleted and exactly two opposite-sign clauses become
binary.  Padding each binary clause by duplicating either surviving literal
adds only opposite-sign occurrences.  At least one co-variable from the two
deleted clauses therefore has true-sign degree below two after every padding
choice.

\paragraph{Proof.}
The $(2,2)$ quota gives the two deleted and two shortened clauses.  A surviving
literal in a shortened clause has the sign of that clause, opposite the sign
of the deleted clauses.  The two deleted proper triples contain four
co-variable slots; since neither remaining binary clause contains a
true-sign literal, padding cannot replace any lost true-sign occurrence.
At least one co-variable lost such an occurrence. \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theorem 3 (enforcer port firewall).}
For every $F\in\CM$, every pin $x=b$, and all four binary-padding choices,
$L_S(G)\notin\CM$; hence it is not in $\CS$.  Any number of further applications
of the same port-once enforcer preserves the external deficit.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Lemma 2 supplies an external signed-degree deficit.  Lemma 1 preserves it at
each lift. \(\square\)

The conclusion is deliberately narrow.  It does not rule out an adapter that
introduces additional clauses incident to external variables, uses a different
gadget, or performs a global semantic reduction.

\paragraph{Materialized implementation ledger.}
Parsing and validation read every source record and charge all comparisons,
address arithmetic, loop control, and index writes.  Selecting a pin charges
the discovery scan or built index.  Simplification charges source reads,
satisfied-clause tests, copied residual headers and literals, and every output
write, including unchanged-value writes.  Padding charges two selection bits,
two reads and two output literal writes.  Each enforcer instance charges
template addressing, 133 clause headers, 399 literal records, variable-index
arithmetic, and all output writes.  A stored level has peak writable storage at
least its materialized output plus live input index, counters, and buffers;
serial generation can reduce buffers but not output traffic.  The displayed
dense-bit counts are therefore output-size lower bounds for a materialized
builder, not complete machine-step counts.  Cumulative bit-time is the actual
sum of live writable bits over charged steps and is not inferred from output
size alone.

\paragraph{Virtual accessor ledger.}
A virtual representation can avoid storing the expanded output, but every
query still pays address decoding, division/remainder locating a source clause
and template position, source rereads, sign handling, and returned-bit control.
It also must expose or compute ordinary headers and indices.  More importantly,
the accessor describes a non-M22 formula, so a $\CS$-only recursive solver
cannot consume it without another verified adapter.  Caches and source-readable
templates trade repeated work for peak writable bits; neither changes Lemma 1.

Actual full-state flips, physical transistor switches, active gates/wires,
code/template size, primitive logical work, traffic, peak live bits, and
cumulative bit-time remain distinct coordinates.  No one-bit semantic SAT
choice is treated as a primitive.

\section{Direct lower-bound spine}
This experiment supplies no post-acquisition lower bound.  ``Number of clauses
in the optional lift'' is not a valid initial potential: the decider is not
required to construct that representation.  A terminal direct answer can
bypass it, while a globally Lipschitz potential on unrestricted live states
has only the range permitted by their Hamming diameter.  ADAPTIVE-COVER75
still accounts only for input acquisition.  No independent structural
$\Phi_{\rm initial}$ with bounded decrease for every legal adaptive transition
is obtained here; that implication remains \OPEN.
