\section*{Lemma (source-redirection simulation; PROVED/EXACT, restricted model)}
\begin{lemma}
Let \(\pi\) be a strict-RES DAG proof with read-only free SOURCE, \(v\) a paid node whose clause equals a SOURCE clause \(s\), and \(\pi'\) obtained by
redirecting every use of \(v\) to \(s\) and deleting every paid node that no longer reaches the sink.  Then for every capacity \(c\) and every legal
schedule \(\sigma\) of \(\pi\) (with recomputation, overwrite and free deletion), deleting the executions of removed nodes and erasing them from all
resident sets yields a legal schedule \(\sigma'\) of \(\pi'\) with \(W(\sigma')\le W(\sigma)-|\text{removed}|_{\ge1}\), \(A(\sigma')\le A(\sigma)\), \(B(\sigma')\le B(\sigma)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Removed nodes have no surviving consumer, so deleting their steps leaves every surviving step's paid parents among its (shrunk) old residents, except
parents equal to \(v\), now read from free SOURCE.  Kept sets only shrink, so \(|{\rm keep}'\cup P'_j|\le|{\rm keep}\cup P_j|\); if the original step needed an
overwrite of \(v\), then \(v\notin P'_j\) and the peak drops by one, so no overwrite is needed.  Each surviving post-set loses only removed nodes and each
deleted step contributes a nonnegative area term, so both areas are nonincreasing; at least one execution of \(v\) is removed.
\end{proof}
So the cost-sound quotient is ``collapse to the free read-only representative'', not ``collapse to the earliest paid occurrence''.  /563 correctly
showed the latter is not jointly cost-sound; the former is, for every schedule, but only for clauses literally equal to a SOURCE clause.
