\section{Canonical target and independent anchor recheck}
An instance of $\CM$ is an OR-CNF with unique clauses, three distinct
variables in each clause, every clause entirely positive or entirely
negative, and exactly two positive and two negative occurrences of every
active variable.  If $\overline C$ flips all literal signs in $C$ while
retaining disjunction, then $\Cs$ excludes only formulas in which every
clause has its partner $\overline C$.  Partial complementary pairs remain
allowed.  Invalid encodings are rejected.

\section{Exact renaming-repair theorem}
Let $F\in\CM$, let $x$ be active, let $b\in\{0,1\}$, and let $z$ be fresh.
Simplify $F$ under $x=b$.  Exactly two incident clauses are satisfied and
deleted, and exactly two opposite-sign incident clauses become binary.
Define $Q_{x,b,z}$ by the following support-faithful slot operations:
\begin{itemize}
\item append the literal on $z$ having the same sign as the removed false
literal on $x$ to each binary record; and
\item reinsert each satisfied record, replacing its true literal on $x$ by
the same-sign literal on $z$ and preserving its other two variables.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Theorem (one-alias repair collapse).}
For every $F,x,b,z$ as above,
\[
 Q_{x,b,z}(F\mid x=b)=\rho_{x\mapsto z}(F),
\]
where $\rho$ is the sign-preserving variable renaming.  In particular the
result is independent of $b$, remains in $\CM$, preserves fully-paired versus
nonpaired status and model count, and has the parent's $k$ active variables
and $m$ clauses.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Partition clauses into those not containing $x$, those containing the literal
made false by $b$, and those containing the literal made true by $b$.
The first group is unchanged.  A clause in the second group loses its
$x$-literal and the repair inserts the same-sign $z$-literal.  A clause in the
third group is deleted and then reinserted with exactly the same substitution.
Thus every record is precisely its image under $\rho$, with no other record
added or removed.  Sign-preserving alpha-renaming preserves all stated
properties. \qed

The repaired relation on the retained variables is therefore
$\exists x\,F$, not the fixed branch $F\mid x=b$.  Fixing $z=b$ recovers the
branch but immediately recreates the non-M22 residual.  A target-only solver
called on the repaired formula answers $\SAT(F)$ and does not provide the two
branch labels.

After $d$ distinct pins followed by this repair, every nominal child still
has $k$ active variables.  If the $2^d$ children are completed independently,
\[
 \Psi=2^d2^k=2^{k+d},\qquad \Rsearch=k-\log_2\Psi=-d.
\]
If a cache merges alpha-isomorphic children, only one parent-equivalent
instance remains, but its one answer still does not recover the fixed branch
answers.  Caching prevents an artificial blow-up; it does not create a
contraction.

\section{Two-level BIT-RESOURCE1 ledger}
The table separates logical work from traffic and storage.  One record carries
$\Theta(\log k)$ identifier/sign bits in a dense indexed encoding and the full
input has $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$ bits.  Bounds below are for one fixed uniform
implementation choice; they are not mixed across machines.

\begin{longtable}{p{.20\linewidth}p{.24\linewidth}p{.23\linewidth}p{.23\linewidth}}
\toprule
Item & primitive bit work / traffic & peak live writable bits & audit consequence\\
\midrule
Parse and validate & read all $N$ source bits; counters, comparisons, duplicate and quota checks are charged & streaming may use polynomial index/counter space; a materialized incidence table uses $\Theta(N)$ & required for a total $L_\star$ decider; no free promise recognition\\
Locate four records & scan costs $\Theta(N)$ reads; a maintained index charges its construction, queries and addresses & $O(\log N)$ extra for a scan, or the paid index size & source rereads are traffic, not free access\\
Pin and simplify & four record reads, sign tests, control and address arithmetic; two delete/two shorten actions & $O(\log N)$ control plus any live parent/index & this state is generally outside $\Cs$\\
Materialized repair & copy/write every ordinary child bit, including unchanged values: $\Theta(N)$ reads and writes & $\Theta(N)$ child bits, plus live parent/index/buffer & child has the original $k,m,N$ scale\\
Virtual repair & store alias and branch-control metadata; every later access charges selection, address rewrite and source read & $O(\log N)$ per alias plus downstream state & avoids payload storage, not semantic parent equivalence\\
Second real level & repeat discovery, pin, access and repair on the first representation; failed trials and switching remain charged & live ancestors, two aliases, buffers and cache entries counted simultaneously & four nominal children are full-size alpha-renamings\\
Cache/canonicalize & hash/equality reads and cache operations are charged; complete recognition may inspect $\Theta(N)$ bits & cache key/table and collision-verification buffers & merges duplicates but returns only the parent decision\\
Finish/reconstruct & branch-specific labels or witnesses require separate valid work; the repaired decision alone is insufficient & all live solver, cache and reconstruction state & no hidden branch oracle\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Unchanged-value writes count as writes.  Actual complete-state flips are at
most the writes but depend on the prior bit values and are recorded separately;
physical transistor switches, active gates and wires are not identified with
logical bit work.  Cumulative bit-time is the sum of actual live writable bits
over actual charged steps and cannot be inferred from the static child count.
Code, representation and any hardware table size are separate resources.

Consequently, neither materialization nor virtualization yields a successor
simultaneous pair better than the parent in the required all-input FLMV model.
There is no ARC edge here.  The same analysis also blocks interpreting
$c+U(C')<U(C)$ for some chosen enumeration budget as a universal saving.

\section{Direct post-acquisition spine}
A potential that counts these nominal repaired branches, distinct repaired
formulas, or their static table sizes is not an unavoidable-work measure:
alpha-canonicalization and caching reduce all of them to one parent-equivalent
object.  Conversely, charging the missing fixed-branch answers as if they were
already present assumes the desired computation.  This is a concrete rejection
of this candidate family only.  It does not refute a structural potential
$\Phi$ on complete legal machine states.

The remaining direct obligation is unchanged: define $\Phi$ independently of
optimal running time, prove a large initial value after acquisition and
$\Phi_{\rm terminal}=0$, and prove for every legal adaptive transition
\[
 \Phi(C)-\Phi(C')\leq \Gamma(N)\,w(C,C')
\]
with charged elementary bit work $w$.  Input hitting number $\tau\leq N$ is
not counted again.  No such post-acquisition $\Phi$ is proved here.
