\section{PROPOSE: falsifiers fixed before search}
The missing lemma is not another structural width parameter.  It is an
effective, arbitrary-input theorem that constructs complete moving semantic
summaries, charges their discovery and updates, and re-enters a closed
ordinary-SAT grammar with one fixed-power smaller child.  Before searching
the new peer corpus, this run fixed four falsifiers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a large grid-minor frontier can coexist with a constant-state semantic
transfer if the grid portion is answer-neutral;
\item an UNSAT source proof may have to touch linearly many modules while a
constant-interface transfer still decides the instance cheaply;
\item even the conjunction of the preceding two properties may remain easy,
so neither can authenticate an arbitrary-SAT lower bound;
\item affine/XOR elimination, SCC/2CNF closure, caching, and compiled circuits
may erase a syntactic obstruction unless acquisition of the compact
representation is itself charged on all ordinary inputs.
\end{enumerate}

\section{BUILD I: four exact 15-variable transfer templates}
Use the authenticated W1 source as template $U$.  The long-chain source gives
templates $C,D$ with exact predecessor/own-$x_3$ relations
\[
R_C=\{(0,0),(0,1)\},\quad
R_D=\{(0,0),(0,1),(1,1)\},\quad
R_U=\{(0,1)\}.
\]
Starting from the satisfiable W1-ring template $S$, exchange the signs of its
first-clause occurrence $-x_3$ and a later $+x_3$.  Call the result $E$.
The exchange preserves properness and two occurrences of each sign for every
variable.  Exhaustive enumeration gives
\[
 R_E=\{0,1\}^2.
\]
Four chosen negative private-literal ports of $E$ have value zero in a local
witness for every boundary pair.  Consequently same-sign occurrence swaps
among those ports preserve the selected witnesses, although no generic
splice-invariance principle is assumed.

\section{BUILD II: the joint countercontrol}
For $H\ge2$, put $L=H^2$.  Form a chain of $L$ modules
\[
 C D^{L-2}T,
 \qquad T=E\ \hbox{for the SAT member},\quad T=U\ \hbox{for the UNSAT member},
\]
then append $H^2$ further $E$ modules.  Link all $2H^2$ modules in the usual
$x_3$ predecessor ring.  On the appended $E$ modules, use the four selected
negative ports to exchange occurrences along every horizontal and vertical
edge of an $H\times H$ grid.  There are
\[
 n=30H^2\quad\hbox{variables},\qquad 40H^2\quad\hbox{clauses}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Joint frontier/support easy family]
For every $H\ge2$, the two formulas above are connected proper strict signed
exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNFs.  They have the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Their variable primal graphs contain an $H\times H$ grid minor.  Hence every
variable ordering has a syntactic processed-to-unprocessed frontier of size
at least $H/16$.
\item The $T=E$ member is satisfiable and the $T=U$ member is unsatisfiable.
\item Deleting all clauses of any one of the first $H^2$ modules from the
UNSAT member leaves a satisfiable formula.  Therefore every UNSAT subset of
original source clauses touches all $H^2$ chain modules.
\item One fixed deterministic program recognizes the grammar, recomputes the
four constant relations, and decides both members in polynomial time.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each module is proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$.  Ring substitution replaces
one positive $x_3$ occurrence by a positive predecessor occurrence.  Grid
patches only permute negative private occurrences.  Thus every signed count
remains two, and no clause repeats a variable.  The ring makes the source
connected.  Each appended $E$ module induces a connected branch set, and the
patched ports realize all grid adjacencies, giving the stated minor.  The
elementary grid-ordering lemma used in GRID-FRONTIER-SCC151 then yields the
$H/16$ frontier floor after the constant-size branch-set translation.

For satisfiability, compose the exact two-bit relations.  Because $E$ is
universal, the $C D^{L-2}E$ chain closes.  In $C D^{L-2}U$, $C$ forces its own
bit to zero; every subsequent $D$ can be one only if its predecessor is one;
hence the bit before $U$ is zero, while $U$ requires predecessor one.  This is
impossible.  The chosen $E$ witnesses make every patched negative grid port
true, so the occurrence exchanges do not disturb either conclusion.

If chain module $i$ is deleted, omit its relation from the cyclic product.
Direct relation composition has a closing path.  Selecting the corresponding
local witnesses, including the grid-port-zero witnesses, gives a satisfying
assignment to every remaining clause.  Therefore an UNSAT subset omitting
all clauses from module $i$ would be a subformula of a satisfiable deletion,
a contradiction.  This proves source-module support $H^2$.

Finally, a single parser checks the finite templates and patch grammar,
computes the constant relations by exhaustive local enumeration, and
multiplies $2\times2$ Boolean matrices.  The same code handles $T=E$ and
$T=U$; no answer bit or oracle table is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Structural conjunction firewall]
Neither a grid-minor syntactic frontier, nor linear source-module support,
nor their conjunction implies a decision-time, decision-space, proof-size,
or semantic-state lower bound for arbitrary SAT algorithms.
\end{proposition}
The theorem is an explicit counterexample to that implication.  It does not
show that either measure is useless inside a separately proved algorithmic or
proof-system model.

\section{Separate machine ledgers}
\paragraph{Indexed RAM / word model.}
Grammar recognition scans the explicit clause list, authenticates constant
templates and endpoints, and composes constant relations.  Arithmetic and
address widths cost $O(\log N)$ bits.  This is a restricted polynomial upper
bound, not a RAM lower bound.
\paragraph{Fixed-$k$ multitape with read-only input.}
A sequential pass can authenticate canonical records; relation composition
uses constant live Boolean state plus $O(\log N)$ counters.  If random source
cross-checks are implemented by rescanning, their head travel is charged and
remains polynomial.  Input cells are not work space.
\paragraph{One read/write tape containing the input.}
The initial $N$ source cells count in footprint.  Applying
\[
T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))),\qquad
P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))
\]
is only a conservative upper simulation.  It is not a lower bound, and no
two-tape Hennie--Stearns statement is substituted.
\paragraph{Fixed universal machine.}
Program-prefix length is constant and interpreter, alphabet, head movement,
and source access are charged.  Only polynomial preservation is asserted;
no FLMV exponent is transported.  The finite Python checker is evidence for
the construction, not the native implementation ledger.

\section{Answer-side separation and hostile shortcuts}
The SAT and UNSAT executions are restrictions of one fixed program.  On the
SAT side, witness recovery selects transfer pairs and local assignments.  On
the UNSAT side, the same complete relations show that no closing path exists.
The program must first authenticate the selector module; it receives no free
side bit.  Side-specific source support is therefore not borrowed from a
different machine.

Affine/XOR elimination, SCC/2CNF closure, Horn propagation, caching, BDD/SDD
sharing, Gaussian elimination, and direct template recognition all remain
valid hostile controls.  The present family deliberately demonstrates a
direct semantic shortcut.  Source-premise support is not proof bit length,
Resolution width, proof space, cumulative bit-time, or decision time.  The
grid frontier is syntactic and need not survive projection or compilation.
Padding, succinct encodings, and moving interfaces change the size ledger and
cannot be imported without a closed physical grammar.

\section{Corpus reconciliation}
\paragraph{Adopted.}
The exact long-chain relations, grid-minor control, affine/parity shortcut,
same-program answer separation, physical source support, and direct semantic
transfer are adopted only at their declared restricted scopes.
\paragraph{Retired.}
Any root language suggesting that frontier width, source-module support, or
their conjunction authenticates arbitrary SAT hardness is retired.  A compact
summary is not free on arbitrary input; conversely, an explicit large
syntactic object need not be semantically hard.
\paragraph{No contradiction with CONNECTED-INTERFACE-REFEREE186.}
That result excludes one static small-boundary/small-local-backdoor
architecture.  The present family uses a moving two-bit summary and therefore
acts as its intended escape control, not a refutation.
\paragraph{No P-versus-NP conclusion.}
The family is syntactically recognizable and does not contain arbitrary SAT.
Its polynomial decider is a promise-family upper bound.  The construction
neither proves P=NP nor P$\ne$NP.

\section{Single next target}
Force semantic, not merely syntactic, state growth while preserving arbitrary
ordinary-SAT coverage and charging discovery.  A falsifiable next lemma would
exhibit an all-input recursive constructor for which every compact candidate
summary is either (i) effectively authenticated and updated in polynomial
total cost and re-entered as one $N^\lambda$ child, or (ii) defeated by two
source instances with identical candidate summaries but different answers.
The same test must survive affine, SCC, compiled-circuit, and direct-recognizer
shortcuts and must be repeated at a genuine second recursive level.
