\section{Claim card and preregistered falsifiers}
\paragraph{Target and quantifiers.}
Fix a source $x^0$, a family of legal perturbations that reverse its label,
one fixed partition of source coordinates into input cells, and one actual
deterministic computation or sound certificate path.  The target is a
necessary source-authentication inequality for that same realization.  It is
not a lower bound on an arbitrary SAT algorithm unless a separate theorem
forces that algorithm to use the interface.

Before the peer/source audit, four falsifiers were fixed.
\begin{description}
\item[H1: one neighbor, one probe.]  Count every label-changing perturbation
as a separate required probe.  \textbf{FALSIFIED}: overlapping perturbations
may share one queried coordinate or cell.
\item[H2: disjointness is cosmetic.]  Drop the disjointness hypothesis from
the balanced-pair version of ARC144.  \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the star family has
$b=n-1$ two-bit blocks and one common-coordinate transversal.
\item[H3: layout-free word transfer.]  Preserve a bit transversal unchanged
under arbitrary word packing.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the relevant
quantity is the transversal after cell quotient, and it can decrease.
\item[H4: two levels reuse one charge.]  Apply a parent sensitivity bound to
a nested child without charging shared preprocessing, virtual requests and
the final consumer.  \textbf{FALSIFIED as an inference}: all three terms in
the inequality above are load-bearing.
\end{description}

\section{Perturbation hypergraph and exact cell quotient}
Let $f:D\to\{0,1\}$ be a partial Boolean problem, $x^0\in D$ with
$f(x^0)=0$, and let $\mathcal E$ be a nonempty family of coordinate sets
$E\subseteq[N]$ such that $x^0\oplus\mathbf 1_E\in D$ and
$f(x^0\oplus\mathbf 1_E)=1$.  Let $\mathcal P$ partition $[N]$ into input
cells.  The quotient perturbation hypergraph has vertex set $\mathcal P$ and
hyperedge
\[
 B_{\mathcal P}(E)=\{P\in\mathcal P:P\cap E\ne\varnothing\}.
\]
Define
\[
 \tau_{\mathcal P}(\mathcal E)=
 \min\{|Q|:Q\subseteq\mathcal P,\;
 Q\cap B_{\mathcal P}(E)\ne\varnothing\text{ for all }E\in\mathcal E\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact cell-transversal replay theorem]\label{th:tau}
Every correct deterministic computation that outputs $0$ on $x^0$ probes at
least $\tau_{\mathcal P}(\mathcal E)$ distinct source cells.  The same holds
for every sound accepting UNSAT-certificate path, and for every rejecting
branch under the co-nondeterministic SAT polarity
\[
 F\in\SAT\Rightarrow\text{all branches accept},\qquad
 F\notin\SAT\Rightarrow\text{some branch rejects}.
\]
Malformed and timeout paths must obey the same uniform total clock and
polarity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $Q$ be the set of cells actually returned on the fixed baseline path.
If $Q$ misses $B_{\mathcal P}(E)$, every probed cell has identical contents
on $x^0$ and $x^0\oplus\mathbf1_E$.  Inductively, all adaptive addresses,
cache states, guest computations and control states agree, so the terminal
NO behavior is reproduced on a YES input.  This contradicts correctness or
soundness.  Hence $Q$ is a transversal and $|Q|\ge\tau_{\mathcal P}$.
\end{proof}

This is an exact lower bound for distinguishing the baseline from the
specified neighbor set: if a transversal $Q$ is known and the baseline is
$0^N$ with perturbations that flip zeros to ones, the restricted promise is
decided by OR of the coordinates/cells in $Q$.  It need not decide any other
input, so it cannot be promoted to full SAT.

\begin{corollary}[Disjoint blocks and bounded cell incidence]
If $b$ perturbation sets are pairwise disjoint, then the bit-partition has
$\tau=b$.  More generally, if each source cell meets at most $c$ of these
blocks, then $\tau_{\mathcal P}\ge\lceil b/c\rceil$.
\end{corollary}
This recovers SIGN-SENSITIVITY-WORD-FIREWALL171.  For one-bit sensitive
positions spaced by $\Delta$ in a contiguous $W$-bit layout,
$c\le\lfloor(W-1)/\Delta\rfloor+1$.

\section{Two-level virtual-child repair}
Use ARC144's declared one-realization interface.  First-level shared
preprocessing reads at most $p_1$ source cells.  Each requested first-level
bit makes at most $q_1$ further source-cell probes.  Second-level shared
preprocessing requests at most $p_2$ first-level bits.  Each of the $L_2$
final bits requests at most $q_2$ first-level bits.  Addresses, descriptors,
headers, failed requests, cache acquisition and replay use these budgets or
are charged separately.

\begin{theorem}[Transversal form of ARC144]\label{th:two}
If the complete fixed-layout two-level generator preserves the baseline and
every neighbor label, then for the actual union $Q$ of distinct source cells
read on the baseline execution,
\[
 \boxed{\tau_{\mathcal P}(\mathcal E)\le |Q|
 \le p_1+q_1p_2+q_1q_2L_2.}
\]
Repeated/cached probes count in actual time but cannot increase $|Q|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The upper bound is the union bound through both virtual levels.  If the
actual union misses a perturbation hyperedge, couple every deterministic
descriptor, virtual address, cache and emitted bit exactly as in
Theorem~\ref{th:tau}.  The final child strings coincide despite opposite
required labels, contradiction.  Thus the union is a transversal.
\end{proof}

ARC144's two displayed results follow: individual sensitive bits give
$\tau=m$, and its $b$ disjoint quota-preserving pairs give $\tau=b$.  The
proof and hypotheses are therefore independently upheld.  The correct
generalization is $\tau$, not the raw number of possibly overlapping legal
neighbors.

\paragraph{Hostile overlap controls.}
For coordinates $0,\ldots,n-1$, take star perturbations
$E_i=\{0,i\}$, $1\le i<n$.  There are $n-1$ legal neighbors but
$\tau=1$; reading coordinate zero distinguishes all of them from $0^n$.
For all two-subsets, there are $\binom n2$ neighbors but $\tau=n-1$.  A
matching has $\tau$ equal to its number of edges, while an $n$-cycle has
$\tau=\lceil n/2\rceil$.  Thus overlap structure, not neighbor count, is
load-bearing.

\section{Independent audit of the newest peer claims}
\paragraph{INDEXED-LOCATOR-GAP140.}
Role 3's separated-equality family uses $m$ pairwise disjoint four-sign
groups $P_i$.  On the equal SAT baseline, correctness against every
singleton-defect UNSAT neighbor forces the deterministic run to hit every
$P_i$.  Choosing the group whose first hit occurs last gives one UNSAT
neighbor whose run agrees until $\Omega(m)$ earlier group hits.  This is a
valid same-algorithm worst-input adversary.  In the declared canonical
layout, each group spans four successive records of
$3(w+1)+1=\Theta(\log N)$ bits.  A contiguous $W=O(\log N)$ cell meets only
$O(1)$ groups, so the quotient transversal remains $\Omega(m)=
\Omega(N/\log N)$ cells.  The theorem is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted}
for deterministic acquisition/decision in that layout.

It does not apply to a guessed rejecting coNTIME branch: the branch may name
the bad coordinate in $O(\log N)$ bits.  It is not a superlinear time or
space lower bound.  The disconnected family is signed exact-$(2,2)$; the
new equality-bridged connected extension is proper ordinary 3CNF but not
signed exact-$(2,2)$.  Its two PIN descendants remain $\Theta(N)$ length, so
they do not contract ARC.

\paragraph{ARC-JUMP-REVERSAL-CERT192.}
The report correctly separates a one-jumping-input-head reversal/space
obstruction from the constant local UNSAT proof and from FLMV.  Its corrected
record factor is $S+\log(N+TJ+2)+1$, and its time transport through an
ordinary-head simulation is explicitly \textbf{CONDITIONAL} on the native
HEADMOVE product.  This run adopts that restricted status; it does not turn
reversal count into indexed time.

\paragraph{CIRCUIT-SCAN-REVERSE-SELECT173.}
The new table-free consumers are an architecture-restricted source-access
result.  The finite gate/occurrence counts are agent-reported rather than
rerun here.  Even complete Rank/Select would not quantify away the witness,
construct a full decision circuit, evaluate it in small space, or make the
explicit two-level child smaller.  The verifier-to-decision firewall remains.

\section{Finite controls, barriers and model ledger}
The dependency-free audit exhaustively checked all $32{,}767$ nonempty
hypergraph families on four coordinates, every aligned cell width, all
sub-transversal failures, and star/clique/matching/cycle families through
$n=12$.  It tested 64 tight two-level OR configurations and the exact
fixed-record spacing declared by Role 3 for $m=2,4,8,16,32$ and word factors
$c=1,2,4$.  At $m=32,c=4$, the crude incidence lower bound is 16 cells and
the exact interval transversal is 21, still $\Theta(m)$.  Total: 781,937
assertions, zero failures, no randomness.

The result relativizes only to oracles that still expose source cells through
the declared interface.  A maximal semantic oracle answers the label without
forming a transversal, so transfer to arbitrary oracle algorithms fails.
Likewise an algebraic/global-OR primitive can collapse the star control.
DEAD, COPY and DELAY wrappers do not create unavoidable probes; re-encoding or
packing changes the cell quotient and its construction must be charged.
These are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS}, not evidence for $P=NP$.
