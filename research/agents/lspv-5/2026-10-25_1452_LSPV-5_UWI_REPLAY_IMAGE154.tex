\section{Canonical target and attacks fixed before search}
For a satisfiable ordinary SAT instance $F$ of full bit length $N$, retain
\[
 b(N)=b_0+\lfloor\log_2(N+2)\rfloor,
 \qquad
 \Hmin(F)=\min_{|p|\le b(N)}\{t_p(F)+q(N)+1\}.
\]
The program may be selected with free hindsight and need not work on any other
formula, but its actual witness output and all verification cost are charged.
The corrected UWI theorem is
\[
 P=NP\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists C,d\ \forall F\in\SAT:\ \Hmin(F)\le C(N+2)^d.
\]
Consequently the missing direction requires one common satisfiable input defeating
every eligible program at each fixed polynomial budget.

Before archive or literature search I fixed four falsification attacks.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A public polynomial witness-extensible generator with a fixed or
$O(\log N)$ seed may be replayed by one fixed short program.
\item A source-readable seed, planted witness, affine basis, Horn/2SAT certificate,
or graph-factor recognizer may give an immediate input-aware witness program.
\item Statements of the form $\forall p\,\exists F_p$ may be silently substituted
for the required $\exists F\,\forall p$ common-input statement.
\item DNNF, proof-system, hash/timer, oracle, circuit-size, switching, routing, or
entropy bounds may be transferred to all witness algorithms without a theorem.
\end{enumerate}
The first attack is resolved below by a new explicit replay lemma.  The other three
remain standing rejection tests.

\section{Short-seed replay theorem}
Fix a canonical SAT encoding and verifier $V$ of cost $q(N)$.  A \emph{public
replay presentation} consists of fixed deterministic programs $I,G,W$ and constants
$a,c$.  The header $h=I(F)$ is computed in polynomial time.  For every formula
$F$ in a family $\mathcal S$ of length $N$, some binary seed $z$ of length at most
$c\log_2(N+2)$ satisfies
\[
 G(h,z)=F,\qquad V(F,W(h,z))=1.
\]
For every enumerated seed of that length, $G$ and $W$ halt within $(N+2)^a$ work
and produce at most $(N+2)^a$ bits.  The equality $G(h,z)=F$ is ordinary exact
encoding equality, not a semantic oracle.

\begin{theorem}[Public short-seed image replay, PROVED/EXACT]
For every public replay presentation there are constants $C,d,N_0$ and one fixed
program $p_{G,W}$ such that for every $F\in\mathcal S$ with $N\ge N_0$,
\[
 |p_{G,W}|\le b(N),\qquad
 t_{p_{G,W}}(F)+q(N)+1\le C(N+2)^d.
\]
Hence $\Hmin(F)$ is polynomially bounded throughout $\mathcal S$.
Injectivity of $G$ is not required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The fixed program computes $h=I(F)$, enumerates every string $z$ of length at most
$L=\lfloor c\log_2(N+2)\rfloor$, computes $G(h,z)$, and compares the complete
ordinary encoding with $F$.  On the first exact match it computes $W(h,z)$ and
outputs it.  There are fewer than $2^{L+1}\le2(N+2)^c$ seeds.  Parsing, each
generation, equality comparison, witness generation, output, and final verification
are polynomial, so the total is polynomial in $N$.  The code of this procedure,
$I,G,W,c$, and the scheduler has one constant description length $L_0$.  Since
$b(N)\to\infty$, choose $N_0$ with $b(N)\ge L_0$ thereafter.

If several seeds generate $F$, the program computes $W$ and runs $V$ on each
matching opening, continuing unless the witness verifies.  At least one valid
opening exists by definition, and there are only polynomially many seeds, so this
remains polynomial.  When every opening is valid, the first exact match suffices.
The program may fail outside
$\mathcal S$, which is expressly allowed in the definition of $\Hmin$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Recoverable-seed replay, PROVED/EXACT]
If a fixed polynomial-time extractor $E(F)$ returns any opening $z$ with
$G(I(F),z)=F$ and a polynomial-time witness lift $W(I(F),z)$, the same conclusion
holds without an $O(\log N)$ seed-length assumption.
\end{corollary}

This includes source-readable witnesses, fixed public seeds, and polynomial-depth
witness-extensible chains whose seed/opening is recoverable from the target.  It
also includes logarithmic-seed planted generators even when the seed is not written
in the formula: enumeration is polynomial.  Thus such a family cannot establish
the UWI hard-input statement unless a specific replay hypothesis above is broken.

\section{Charged resource ledger}
The theorem is a time upper bound, but the implementation does not hide resource
costs.  For fewer than $2(N+2)^c$ candidates it charges:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{.98\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
resource & charged operations / peak objects \\
\midrule
source reads & parsing $F$ and complete byte/bit comparisons; worst case
$O(N^{c+1})$ reads \\
workspace reads/writes & seed counter updates including unchanged writes,
generator and comparison state, control, addressing, branch selection \\
output & every generated candidate bit when materialized or every streamed bit,
plus the final witness bits \\
failed trials & full actual cost of every earlier seed; no hindsight selects the
correct seed for free \\
verification & the declared $q(N)$ final verifier; internal validation, if used,
is additionally paid \\
peak live writable bits & $O(\log N)$ seed/address state plus the actual workspace
of $I,G,W$ and at most one ordinary candidate/witness buffer; source remains
read-only \\
caches / replay & no cache is assumed; caching only adds charged writes and live bits \\
cumulative bit-time & the sum of actual live writable bits at every step, bounded
by time times the declared peak; not confused with bit flips \\
program / representation & one constant program description; each ordinary
candidate has its full paid encoding \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
Actual full-state bit flips are at most the charged writes but remain a separate
metric.  Gates, wires, transistor switches, DNNF size, and proof length are not
substituted for logical work.

\section{Post-quantum and barrier-transfer ledger}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER:} lossiness/collision counting applies
to the finite seed map $z\mapsto G(h,z)$.  Collisions do not impede replay; they
reduce the image and multiple valid openings only give more witnesses.  Collision
entropy is therefore not a hardness source here.
\item \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge:} the LWE lesson of externally
extracting structure from black-box solver behavior resembles replay, but no
worst-case/average-case theorem transfers to arbitrary SAT witness programs.
\item \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER:} Schur squares, quadratic-relation rank, syzygies,
hash/random-oracle binding, and active-gate energy have no object-level map to the
UWI common-input runtime obligation in the reviewed proposals.
\end{itemize}
Always test the opposite algorithmic use: hidden algebraic or generator structure
often makes witness extraction easier.
