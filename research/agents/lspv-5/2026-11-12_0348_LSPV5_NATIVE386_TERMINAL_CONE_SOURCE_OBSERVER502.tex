\begin{theorem}[Full-CLI verdict is not a terminal-cone function]

\[

For \(P_2\), \(h=12,E=18\), this global self-test is skipped.
The odd-charge source is still UNSAT because the first connected component has odd total charge.
Thus the A/R/A phase is caused by a real global source observer outside the proof ancestry, not a forged clause.
\end{proof}
