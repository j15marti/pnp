\section{Restricted theorem and CEGAR consequence}

\textbf{Theorem 522.1 (accepted goal is not a semantic UNSAT goal).}
Let $C_{341}$ be the pinned recovered checker above and let $P_{613}$ be the
exact pinned packet.  Then $C_{341}(P_{613})$ accepts, while the authenticated
source clause set carried by $P_{613}$ is satisfiable.

\textbf{Proof.}  The first assertion is the unchanged-checker OS execution.
The second follows by direct evaluation of the displayed witness against all
48 in-packet source clauses; exhaustive enumeration independently gives 64
models. \hfill$\square$

\textbf{Corollary 522.2 (UNSAT-goal abstraction no-go).}
No abstraction that includes every concrete execution accepted by $C_{341}$
and preserves its accepted terminal state can both (i) map every accepted
terminal state to ``the carried source is UNSAT'' and (ii) be semantically
sound for the carried source.  In particular, CEGAR may not remove
$P_{613}$ as spurious: authoritative replay makes it a genuine operational
path.  A sound abstraction must instead distinguish operational acceptance
from semantic UNSAT, or restrict its concrete domain by a separately proved
historical-authority condition.  Such a restriction would define a different
domain unless it is source-authenticated.

This corollary is about goal semantics, not proof-cost magnitude.  It neither
constructs a cheap sound refutation nor proves a lower bound.  It blocks only
the unsound step ``accepted terminal $\Rightarrow$ UNSAT source'' for all
accepted recovered-/341 bytes.

\section{Literature and transfer classification}

Cook--Reckhow proof systems require soundness and completeness for the stated
tautology language; successful execution of an unrelated checker is not that
theorem.  Cousot--Cousot supplies the inclusion discipline for sound abstract
interpretation.  Clarke et al. require real counterexample paths to be kept and
only replay-proved spurious paths to be refined.  ECMAScript specifies the
actual coercions used by the checker, including \texttt{ToInt32} for number
bitwise operations and strict equality without coercion.  These are
\textbf{directly transferable} only to the pinned program semantics.
Importing them as a complete $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ proof system is a
\textbf{failed transfer}.  Topological, coding, post-quantum and circuit
analogies supply no missing authority or all-rule invariant in this run.
