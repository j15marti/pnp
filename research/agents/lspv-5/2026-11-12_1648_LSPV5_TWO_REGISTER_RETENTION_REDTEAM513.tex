\section*{3. Red-team scope findings for $\overline W_R(N)$}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cost must be a declared scalar. (Observation; KNOWN THEOREM context.)} On these \emph{trees}, paid work $W=4V-1$ does not depend on the order. So minimizing over $\pi$ optimizes $W$ and $A_{\mathrm{slot}}$ \emph{at the same time}, and there is no tradeoff. For DAG proofs with sharing this fails in general. Resolution has proven length--space tradeoffs (Ben-Sasson--Nordstr\"om), and Beame--Beck--Impagliazzo prove superpolynomial length lower bounds for superlinear-space proofs. So $\min_\pi \operatorname{cost}_R$ must fix a single cost coordinate, or an explicit weighted combination, before the max--min is taken. The /572 frontier must not be read as evidence that joint time--space minimization is free in a mixed DAG calculus.
\item \textbf{Overwrite saves exactly one register. (KNOWN-THEOREM consistency check.)} The register-allocation pebbling literature reports that architectures allowing overwriting instructions can save precisely one register. That matches the corpus: the fresh-slot DFS peak is 3 (/523), and the overwrite peak is 2 (/572). This is a consistency check, not a new result.
\item \textbf{No transfer to the bridge.} Area and register peak of a \emph{chosen} proof are proof-side coordinates. They do not touch the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm{P}\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)$, which stays OPEN for any $R$ that has no unconditional superpolynomial lower bound. For weak fixed $R$ such as resolution, $\overline W_R$ is already unconditionally exponential (Haken; Urquhart for Tseitin), so the bridge's hypothesis cannot be contradicted through such an $R$. This is the standing firewall and is unchanged.
\item \textbf{Knot/link handoff.} No knot/link object enters this run. The finding is recorded only as the smallest available falsifier attempt for the /572 claim, as the 2026-11-09 handoff asks of LSPV-5.
