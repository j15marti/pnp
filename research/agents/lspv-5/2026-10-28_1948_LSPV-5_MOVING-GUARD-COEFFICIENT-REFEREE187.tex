\section{Claim card and PROPOSE before search}
\textbf{Claim ID:} MOVING-GUARD-COEFFICIENT-REFEREE187.  The desired weak
result is an exact physical-size condition under which a supplied moving
frontier relation can be re-entered as an ordinary proper 3CNF child.  The
strong all-input result remains open.

\textbf{Quantifiers and realization.}  For every source of physical length
$N$, suppose one fixed deterministic program has already computed an exact
relation on $w$ named boundary variables and selected a retained suffix.  The
same program must compile the relation, write the complete child, and recurse.
Existence, acquisition, compilation and recursive decision cost are separate.

The following falsifiers were fixed before search.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A logarithmic frontier can still have $N^{\alpha}$ rows, where the
hidden constant $\alpha$ exhausts the desired child exponent.
\item A small frontier relation can be cheap as a circuit but expensive under
a required ordinary-3CNF re-entry, or conversely; representation switching
must be charged.
\item The LSPV-2 four-end construction may omit auxiliaries, canonical bytes,
or a crossing clause and fail closure at the second level.
\item A decomposition implementation may consume a supplied order/tree and
therefore give no polynomial discovery theorem for arbitrary SAT.
\end{enumerate}

\section{BUILD: exact relation compiler and coefficient theorem}
Let $R\subseteq\{0,1\}^w$ be an exact boundary relation.  For every forbidden
assignment $a\notin R$, form the width-$w$ clause
\[
 C_a=\bigvee_{i=1}^w \ell_{a,i},\qquad
 \ell_{a,i}=x_i\ \hbox{if }a_i=0,
 \quad \ell_{a,i}=\neg x_i\ \hbox{if }a_i=1.
\]
This clause is false exactly on $a$.  Let $f=2^w-|R|$.

\begin{lemma}[Proper ternary chain]
For distinct literals $L_1,\ldots,L_w$, $w\ge3$, introduce fresh
$u_1,\ldots,u_{w-3}$ and write
\begin{align*}
 &(L_1\vee L_2\vee u_1),\\
 &(\neg u_{j-1}\vee L_{j+1}\vee u_j),\quad 2\le j\le w-3,\\
 &(\neg u_{w-3}\vee L_{w-1}\vee L_w).
\end{align*}
The existential projection over the $u$ variables is exactly
$L_1\vee\cdots\vee L_w$.  It uses $w-3$ auxiliaries and $w-2$ proper
ternary clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If every $L_i$ is false, the first clause forces $u_1$, each middle clause
forces the next $u_j$, and the last clause is false.  If some $L_i$ is true,
choose the prefix of $u$ values true until the first satisfied literal and
false thereafter (with the evident endpoint conventions).  Every chain
clause is then satisfied.  All names in each clause are distinct.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Moving-guard physical coefficient]
The direct forbidden-row compiler produces a proper 3CNF whose existential
projection is $R$, with
\[
 f(w-2)\ \hbox{clauses},\qquad f(w-3)\ \hbox{auxiliaries},
\]
and canonical physical length
\[
 L_{\rm guard}=O\!\left(fw\log(N+fw+2)\right)
 \le O\!\left(w2^w\log(N+w2^w+2)\right).
\]
If $w\le\alpha\log_2N+O(1)$, then
$L_{\rm guard}\le N^{\alpha+o(1)}$.  Therefore this compiler certifies a
child of length $N^\lambda$ only when the retained encoded suffix is also at
most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ and the guard has a strict coefficient margin
$\alpha<\lambda$ (or a separately proved sharper physical bound).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Conjoin one chain from the lemma for every forbidden row, using disjoint
auxiliaries.  Existential quantifiers distribute over these independent
chains, so the projection is $\bigwedge_{a\notin R}C_a$, exactly the
characteristic relation $R$.  The counts are immediate.  Each signed index
uses $O(\log(N+fw+2))$ bits, yielding the physical bound.  Substitute
$2^w\le O(N^\alpha)$ and absorb polylogarithms into $N^{o(1)}$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precise falsifier.}
The statement $w=O(\log N)$ without its constant is insufficient.  With
$w=\lceil\log_2N\rceil$ and half the table forbidden, the direct compiler has
$\Theta(N\log N)$ ternary clauses/literal records up to index encoding, so it
cannot certify any fixed $N^\lambda$, $\lambda<1$, output.  This falsifies a
\emph{proof rule about the direct compiler}, not the existence of a different
compact circuit or semantic shortcut for a particular relation.  Counting
truth-table rows is not an arbitrary-SAT lower bound.

\section{Independent audit of MOVING-GUARD-SHRINK211}

a four-variable end relation, and retains $h=\lfloor\sqrt r\rfloor$ core

charges at most sixteen forbidden rows, sixteen auxiliaries and thirty-two
ternary clauses.  The retained source is $O(\sqrt r\log r)$ bits while the
parent is $\Omega(r\log r)$.

The tag/comment is duplicated by actual CNF guard clauses and validated, so a
standard SAT parser sees the correct formula.  Children preserve the
consecutive-scope promise; bounded bases are decided by the same fixed
program.  No answer-class bit or general SAT oracle is called.  Accordingly,
the restricted one-child theorem survives this independent audit.  It is a
real closed contraction on a P-decidable syntactic class, not progress on
embedding all ordinary SAT into that class.

\section{Machine, barrier, and SAT/UNSAT ledgers}
On indexed RAM, relation acquisition costs $A(N)$ before compilation; direct
compilation costs $O(2^w w\operatorname{polylog}N)$ time and stores or streams
the complete physical child.  On a fixed-$k$ sequential-input multitape
machine, charge all source rescans, order validation, boundary addresses,
relation rows and output head traffic.  On one shared writable tape, the
original $N$ cells count in footprint and simulation may add full-span scans;
the conservative simulation is an upper construction, not a lower bound.  On
two writable tapes, changes and head positions on both tapes remain separate.
A fixed universal machine charges its interpreter and carries no oracle table
in its program prefix.  None of these ledgers imports a tape exponent into
FLMV RAM.

SAT and UNSAT executions are restrictions of one fixed program.  A favorable
small relation on one side does not provide the side bit, the order, or a
worst-case all-input bound.  A semantic oracle collapses the boundary relation
to a constant answer bit, so syntactic guard growth relativizes and cannot
prove hardness.  Tseitin conversion can make a compact circuit linear-size,
which is an escape from truth-table growth when such a circuit is uniformly
constructible.  Padding, dead variables and recomputation wrappers do not
repair an uncharged acquisition step.  No Natural-Proofs or algebrization
claim is used.
