\section{Three falsifiable mechanisms, checked in order}
\noindent\textbf{M1 (PROVED/EXACT ceiling).}
For a total Boolean label function on $M$ explicit source fields,
deterministic adaptive query depth satisfies
\[
D(f)\le \min\{M,C_0(f)C_1(f)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The Role-3 grid function has $C_0=C_1=q$ and $D=M=q^2$.
Thus its quadratic certificate/minimax separation is real, but it saturates
complete source acquisition and leaves no forced post-read work.

\par\noindent\textbf{M2 (PROVED/EXACT restricted constructive bypass).}
The newest Role-4 family has a linear raw strong 2CNF backdoor, yet a charged
uniform policy discovers one guard SCC and switches between affine elimination
and 2SAT in linear word time.  The label, gate functionality, counts, backdoor
packing, and Schaefer endpoint were independently checked below.

\par\noindent\textbf{M3 (OPEN repair, sharply falsifiable).}
Replace independent explicit fields by a succinct shared interface and prove
that, after its entire encoding is read, every exact uniform decider either
(i) materializes a charged superpolynomial compatibility object or
(ii) performs superpolynomial continuation.  A recognizable switch, direct
evaluation, SCC quotient, affine elimination, Horn closure, clause learning,
or decide-first compression falsifies a proposed instance.  No such universal
post-acquisition invariant is currently known.

\section{Exact certificate-product and source ceilings}
For a nonconstant total $f:\{0,1\}^M\to\{0,1\}$, let $C_b(f)$ be the
maximum, over inputs of label $b$, of the smallest number of fixed coordinates
that force that label.  Let $D(f)$ be optimal worst-case deterministic adaptive
field-query depth.

\begin{theorem}[KNOWN THEOREM plus immediate source ceiling]\label{th:product}
For every total Boolean function,
\[
D(f)\le C_0(f)C_1(f),\qquad D(f)\le M.
\tag{2}
\]
Constants have $D=0$; for nonconstants (1) follows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof audit]
The first inequality is the standard certificate-product decision-tree bound:
iteratively query a monochromatic certificate consistent with the current
subcube; opposite certificates must intersect it in a conflicting coordinate,
and the usual induction charges at most one factor of $C_0$ and one of $C_1$.
This construction permits the next certificate and queried coordinate set to
depend on the complete current transcript.  It therefore already covers
state-dependent switching inside the deterministic query model.  The second
inequality queries each source field once and evaluates $f$ from the complete
input.  Taking the better decision tree proves (1).
\end{proof}
The theorem concerns query depth, not unrestricted machine time.  Internal
computation for choosing certificates can be arbitrarily expensive unless a
uniform implementation is separately supplied.  Conversely, after all $M$
fields are known, the query model charges no further reads and cannot prove
any positive continuation cost.

For
\[
f_q(b)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^q\bigvee_{j=1}^q b_{ij},\qquad M=q^2,
\tag{3}
\]
a SAT certificate selects one $1$ in every row and an UNSAT certificate gives
one all-zero row, so $C_1=C_0=q$.  The two-label adversary of Role 3 answers
zero until the last unread position of each row; every proper prefix has both
labels as completions.  Hence
\[
D(f_q)=q^2=C_0(f_q)C_1(f_q)=M.
\tag{4}
\]
Equation (4) is extremal for both terms of (1).  The proper-3CNF transport
therefore proves $q^2=\Omega(N/\log N)$ sign-group acquisition and no more.
Its canonical source scan matches the bound.  A purported amplification from
``certificate $q$ versus minimax $q^2$'' to superlinear post-acquisition work
is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.

\section{Independent audit of HETEROGENEOUS--GATE72}
Role 4 starts from $q$ cyclic equations
$e_{i-1}\oplus e_i\oplus h=\beta_i$ for even $q$ and a binary chain $F_b$.
Every parity clause $C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$ is replaced by
\[
(\neg X\vee t)(\neg\ell_1\vee t)
(X\vee\ell_1\vee\neg t)(t\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3).
\tag{5}
\]
The first three clauses are exactly $t=X\vee\ell_1$.  An implication cycle
forces all $4q$ copies $X$, all $q+4$ binary-clause guards $Y$, and a switch
$x$ equal.  Eliminating these functional auxiliaries gives
\[
(x\vee A_{q,\beta})\wedge(\neg x\vee F_b).
\tag{6}
\]
For $x=0$, XOR summation cancels every $e_i$ twice and $h$ an even number of
times, so $A$ is satisfiable exactly when $\oplus_i\beta_i=0$.  For $x=1$,
$F_b$ is satisfiable exactly when $b\ne1^q$.  Thus
\[
D_{q,\beta,b}\in SAT\iff(\oplus_i\beta_i=0)\lor(b\ne1^q).
\tag{7}
\]
Counting independently gives
\[
n=11q+9,\quad m_2=13q+5,\quad k=9q+4,\quad M=22q+9.
\tag{8}
\]
Indeed, $4q$ functional OR gadgets contribute $8q$ binary and $8q$ ternary
clauses; $q+4$ guarded chain clauses are ternary; and the cycle contributes
$5q+5$ binaries.  Selecting one top ternary from every other parity quartet
gives $q/2$ pairwise-disjoint supports, proving the claimed raw strong-2CNF
backdoor lower bound $h_2\ge q/2$.

The fixed constraint language $\{\mathrm{OR}_2,E_3\}$ is outside every
Schaefer tractable class: OR is neither 0-valid nor affine, and even ternary
parity is neither 1-valid, Horn, dual-Horn, nor bijunctive.  The endpoint is
therefore NP-complete by Schaefer's theorem.  This class theorem does not make
the recognizable family (7) hard: a direct sign scan decides it in $O(q)$.

\section{Adaptive/hybrid test and state ledger}
The mechanism actually tested was a total policy over the grid source and the
heterogeneous family.  It receives no label.  On a recognized grid it scans a
row until seeing $1$, switches rows, and stops UNSAT upon completing a zero
row.  On a recognized heterogeneous target it parses the grammar, computes
binary SCCs, tries the affine child, and only after inconsistency switches to
the 2SAT child.  Unrecognized formulas use a complete generic fallback.

\section{Transport and coverage ledger}
For a source lower bound $L_{old}$ transported through recognition/reduction
$R$, other overhead $H$, $Q$ target calls of size at most $g$, and simulation
factor $\tau$, the only valid subtraction is
\[
T_{new}^{\le}(g(n))\ge
\frac{L_{old}(n)-R(n)-H(n)}{Q(n)\tau(n)}.
\tag{9}
\]
The remainder must be positive.  The grid compiler reads and writes
$\Theta(q^2)$ fields, consuming its $q^2$ query bound.  Direct inclusion in
proper-3CNF avoids paying a reduction but preserves only
$\Omega(N/\log N)$ target acquisition.  The heterogeneous family similarly
has $2q$ sensitive sign groups and a matching $O(q)$ source-aware algorithm.

\section{Exact proof spine and next missing lemma}
The current chain is
\begin{align}
&\text{explicit opposite-label source fields}
  \Longrightarrow \text{charged acquisition up to }M,
  &&\text{PROVED},\notag\\
&\text{charged acquisition up to }M
  \not\Longrightarrow \text{expensive post-read continuation},
  &&\text{OPEN},\notag\\
&\text{expensive post-read continuation}
  \not\Longrightarrow \text{all-decider superpolynomial time},
  &&\text{OPEN}.
\tag{10}
\end{align}
The highest-value missing lemma is therefore not a larger pointwise
certificate gap.  It is a \textbf{post-acquisition adaptive dichotomy} for one
fixed NP-complete encoding: for every total uniform exact decider and suitable
growing inputs, after charging full source access, either the cumulative
compatibility/representation objects actually produced have superpolynomial
construction or verification cost, or the remaining continuation has
superpolynomial cost.  The invariant must be defined independently of runtime,
survive changing representations and rule choices, and permit distinct SAT
witness and UNSAT decision invariants.  Decide-first normalization under a
hypothetical polynomial decider remains the decisive hostile control.
