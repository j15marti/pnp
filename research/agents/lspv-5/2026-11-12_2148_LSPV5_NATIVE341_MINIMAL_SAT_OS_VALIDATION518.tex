\section*{Restricted threshold theorem}
Let \(V\) and \(E\) denote the effective graph sizes.  Cubicity gives
\[
  3V=2E.
\]
Hence \(V\) is even and \(E=3V/2\).  If \(V<12\), then \(V\leq 10\) and \(E\leq 15\).  The pinned checker explicitly exhausts all \(2^E\) edge assignments when \(E\leq15\), counts odd-vertex solutions of the effective signed SOURCE, and requires that count to be zero.  Therefore no accepted fixture in the stated sublanguage can have a satisfiable source and \(V<12\).

The /609 graph has \(V=12\), \(E=18\), is connected, simple and cubic, and its exact packet is accepted.  Exhaustive independent enumeration of all \(2^{18}\) assignments finds exactly 64 models.  Thus the lower threshold is attained.

\[

whereas the accepted proof claims the row
\[
  x_0\oplus x_1\oplus x_{10}=0.

Consequently all 64 genuine source models falsify the claimed row.  The first model in lexicographic bit-string order is
\texttt{000000010010000001}.
