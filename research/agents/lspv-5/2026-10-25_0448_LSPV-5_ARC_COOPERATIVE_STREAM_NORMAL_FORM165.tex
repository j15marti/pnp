\section{One target and falsifiers fixed before search}
The only active target is an assumption-dependent, fully charged finite route
from cofinally many achievable integer diagonals into the strict FLMV box
\[
t<c_\star=\frac{241}{200},\qquad
s<d_\star=\frac{11642479}{23232400},\qquad
d_\star=\frac12\left(\frac{c_\star+2}{c_\star^2}-c_\star\right).
\]
Here $(t,s)\in E$ means that one fixed uniform deterministic total exact
ordinary-SAT decider on every ordinary $N$-bit input simultaneously uses
$N^{t+o(1)}$ time and $N^{s+o(1)}$ writable/index space in the indexed
read-only-input random-access Turing-machine model of FLMV.  Under
$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$, some finite diagonal $(K,K)$ lies in $E$.

The previous ARC-STREAM-SCOPE-FUSION164 theorem assumed a total uniform
\emph{pauseable} scoped-stream transducer and used its exact output length to
choose a balanced block capacity.  Before literature and archive search, this
run registered four falsifiers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item suspension may require copying an entire configuration or may not be
legal in the matched machine model;
\item a missing exact output-count header may force replay or a full materialized
output before balanced blocking can begin;
\item restart-from-origin may add a hidden $M^{3/2}$ event-generation term at
$B=\sqrt M$;
\item two recursive levels may retain two producer configurations and invalidate
the claimed peak-space exponent.
\end{enumerate}
The first, second and fourth attacks are repaired exactly under the hypotheses
below.  The third is a genuine counterexample to restart-based implementations.
None constructs the missing normalizer.

\section{Model-matched cooperative suspension}
Fix a deterministic random-access transducer $G$ whose input is read only.  Its
complete configuration consists of finite control, every live work/index-tape
cell, every work/index head or address, and its output protocol state.  Suppose
$G$ emits self-delimited events of $O(\log N)$ bits.  The event length can be
replaced by any $N^{o(1)}$ bound without changing the exponents below.

\begin{theorem}[Cooperative suspension normal form]\label{th:coop}
Let $G$ be one fixed total uniform deterministic transducer with charged time
$T_G(N)$ and peak live writable/index state $S_G(N)$.  Let a fixed host $H$
consume each event and, between consecutive events, run an arbitrary fixed
deterministic guest computation using time $T_H$ and peak state $S_H$ over the
entire execution.  There is one fixed deterministic machine $M_{G,H}$ that
interleaves the computations without restarting $G$ and satisfies
\[
T_{G,H}=O(T_G+T_H+M\operatorname{polylog}N),\qquad
S_{G,H}=O(S_G+S_H+\operatorname{polylog}N),                 \tag{1}
\]
where $M$ is the number of emitted events.  All source and workspace reads,
writes (including unchanged-value writes), index/control operations and event
bits are charged.  For any fixed recursion depth $d$, retaining $d$ producer
configurations multiplies the state by at most a constant and does not change
its exponent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inline the transition table of $G$ into the fixed control of $M_{G,H}$.  Replace
each output instruction by ordinary charged writes into one event buffer.  At
an event boundary the scheduler records $G$'s finite control and head/address
registers, leaves all of $G$'s work regions untouched, and switches to disjoint
tagged work regions for $H$.  On return it restores those $O(\log T_G)$-bit
addresses and executes the next transition of $G$.  A fixed-track or tagged
address encoding simulates the disjoint regions with constant-factor tape and
polylogarithmic address overhead.  No configuration is copied: it remains live
and is therefore charged in $S_G+S_H$.  Every transition of $G$ is executed once,
and scheduler/event operations contribute the last term of (1).  At fixed depth
there are only $d$ untouched producer regions.  The same construction applies
if $G$ is paused inside a call to the assumed SAT decider: a nested call uses a
disjoint region, and both states are simultaneously counted.
\end{proof}

Thus ``pauseable'' is not an additional semantic promise once the object is an
explicit deterministic machine.  It \emph{is} still invalid for an extensional
existence statement that gives only the final stream and no machine, or for an
external service whose hidden state is not included in the declared resources.

\section{An exact count header is unnecessary}
Let $M(N)$ be the actual number of emitted clause records.  Assume a fixed,
explicitly computable certified bound
\[
M(N)\le U(N)=N^{\mu+o(1)}.                                 \tag{2}
\]
No exact $M$ header is supplied.

\begin{lemma}[Headerless certified-bound blocking]\label{lem:header}
Set $B(N)=\lceil\sqrt{U(N)}\rceil$.  Read until either $B$ records have arrived
or $G$ signals end-of-stream.  Then every block has at most $B$ records and the
number $q$ of blocks obeys
\[
q=\left\lceil\frac{M}{B}\right\rceil\le \sqrt U+1.         \tag{3}
\]
The stream is consumed once; at most one $B$-record block is materialized.
Consequently FUSION164's fixed-depth envelope remains
\[
t_d\le\max\{r,\mu+2d\delta,
\mu(1-2^{-d})+2d\delta+\mu2^{-d}t\},
\quad
s_d\le\max\{\eta,\mu/2,\delta,\mu2^{-d}s\},               \tag{4}
\]
when $(2)$, rather than an exact count header, determines the block size.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Equation (3) is immediate from $M\le U$ and $B\ge\sqrt U$.  The block payload
and number of blocks each have exponent at most $\mu/2$; replacing the exact
square-root split by these upper bounds changes only constants and subpolynomial
factors in the FUSION164 derivation.  End-of-stream is tested by executing $G$,
not by a semantic query.  The producer state is retained by
Theorem~\ref{th:coop}, and the event buffer, block, children, boundary tables,
ancestors and guest state remain charged exactly as in (4).
\end{proof}

The requirement in (2) is substantive.  An unknown asymptotic exponent cannot
be used as uncomputed changing-length advice.  For an existential ARC proof it
is enough to supply one fixed program-specific computable envelope; a universal
compiler or automatic exponent discovery is not intrinsically required.

At $\mu=1,d=2,(t,s)=(6/5,1/2)$, the same strict entry calculation is
\[
r<\frac{241}{200},\qquad
\eta<\frac{11642479}{23232400},\qquad
\delta<\frac{31}{800}.                                    \tag{5}
\]
The finite arithmetic is exact.  Existence of an all-input or paid fixed-hard-
image normalizer satisfying (2) and (5) remains \OPEN.

\section{Restart replay is a real failed implementation}
Suppose instead that block $j$ is obtained by restarting $G$ from its initial
configuration and discarding the first $(j-1)B$ emitted records.  Complete
consumption with $q=\lceil M/B\rceil$ visits
\[
R(M,B)=\sum_{j=1}^{q}\min\{jB,M\}=\Theta(M^2/B+M).          \tag{6}
\]
When $B=\sqrt M$ and $M=N^{\mu+o(1)}$, merely emitting the visited records
requires $N^{3\mu/2+o(1)}$ charged operations.  More generally, rerunning the
full prefix machines gives the safe upper envelope $qT_G$, with exponent
$r+\mu/2$.  For $\mu=1$, the lower event-count exponent $3/2$ already exceeds
$241/200$.  Therefore restart-from-origin cannot justify the favorable fusion
bound.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT for the declared restart architecture},
not a lower bound on every stream consumer: Theorem~\ref{th:coop} removes the
replay by retaining ordinary machine state.

At two genuine recursive levels the parent producer stays live while the child
producer and guest execute.  Their bit counts add, but a fixed sum of
$N^{\eta+o(1)}$ regions still has exponent $\eta$.  This same-machine statement
does not borrow time from one solver and space from another.  Program size is
constant because $G,H,d$ are fixed; physical transistor switching, circuit
gates/wires, actual changed-cell flips and cumulative bit-time remain separate
metrics and are not inferred from (1)--(6).

\section{Finite reproducible audit}
The Python artifact imports the independently published FUSION164 proper-3CNF
codec, exact DPLL leaf and scoped block semantics.  Its new producer exposes
only a program counter and an EOF step---no exact-count header.  A certified
power-of-two upper bound chooses the block capacity.  During every recursive
child computation the parent producer counter is asserted unchanged.  Every
child is serialized and reparsed as an ordinary proper 3CNF.

The audit covers equality-path SAT and UNSAT formulas for lengths $2$ through
$5$, plus a disconnected SAT-plus-UNSAT control, at depths one and two: 18
cases, 118 assertion groups, zero failures.  It separately checks exact restart
counts at $M\in\{16,64,256,1024,4096\}$ with $B=\sqrt M$; the replay ratios are
$2.5,4.5,8.5,16.5,32.5$.  Runtime was below one second.  Python object counters
are not FLMV primitive traces, producer records are prebuilt, and the test does
not construct a normalizer; its status is only \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}.

\section{Barrier-transfer and post-quantum preflight}
FLMV's original JACM paper fixes the indexed read-only-input random-access
model and the simultaneous endpoint; Williams' original paper confirms the
same local-reduction/random-access setting.  Neither grants a free semantic
stream, a readable write-only output tape, or a favorable normalizer.  The
present suspension proof is an internal machine construction, not an imported
systems theorem.

Coroutine scheduling, backpressure and checkpoint/restart in operating systems
are a \textbf{useful structural analogy requiring a new transfer theorem}; they
do not establish (1) in FLMV's model.  The exact transfer instead follows from
the deterministic configuration semantics above.  Hardware context-switch and
VLSI results are \textbf{misleading/failed transfers} unless their physical
memory and switching measures are translated into the declared bit model.
Schur squares, quadratic relation spaces, Hilbert functions, collision entropy,
LWE-style worst/average reductions, special soundness and random-oracle methods
have no object-matched arrow here and are classified \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}
for this subgap.  The retained machine state is also exploitable by the
algorithm, which is why restart replay is not a hardness source.
