\section{Exact22 module and finite reconstruction}
Let \(W_1\) be the following twenty signed clauses:
\begin{align*}
&(-1,3,-14),(2,-8,9),(10,-13,14),(3,7,-13),(-4,-9,-11),\\
&(5,8,-14),(4,-8,-11),(1,7,13),(1,-7,10),(-5,-6,-15),\\

&(2,-9,11),(4,8,-10),(-3,12,15),(-5,-12,15),(-1,13,14).
\end{align*}
Every clause has three distinct variables, clauses are distinct, and each variable occurs twice with each sign. Define \(H(a,b)\) by flipping slots \((6,0),(11,0)\) when \(a=0\), and \((17,2),(9,2)\) when \(b=0\), using zero-based indices. Each pair flips two occurrences of one variable, preserving exact-(2,2) balance and unsigned supports. Exhausting all \(2^{15}\) assignments gives
\[
(\#H_{00},\#H_{01},\#H_{10},\#H_{11})=(772,272,272,0). \tag{1}
\]
For \(d\ge1\), take disjoint renamed copies
\[
F_d(a,b)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^dH(a_i,b_i).
\]
Then \(F_d(a,b)\in\SAT\) iff \(a\wedge b=0^d\). It has \(15d\) variables, \(20d\) clauses and fixed-width physical length \(N=\Theta(d\log(d+2))\). This is a disconnected promise family, not full SAT.

\begin{theorem}[Communication--certificate firewall]\label{th:firewall}
For \(F_d\): (i) exact one-way state and deterministic communication complexity are at least \(d\); (ii) the UNSAT matrix has rank \(2^d-1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\); (iii) its exact XOR/AND/NOT multiplicative complexity is \(2d-1\); yet (iv) SAT on the promise is in uniform coNTIME \(N\operatorname{polylog}N\) with \(O(\log N)\) work for one two-way sequential read-only input head.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Distinct \(a,a'\) give distinct rows by choosing \(b\) supported on a differing coordinate, so all \(2^d\) rows must be distinguished. Moebius elimination of the nonempty-intersection matrix gives GF(2) rank \(2^d-1\).

Its multilinear polynomial is \(1+\prod_i(1+a_ib_i)\), of degree \(2d\). With \(r\) AND gates algebraic degree is at most \(r+1\), hence \(r\ge2d-1\). A matching circuit computes \(d\) products and joins them by \(d-1\) OR gates \(x\vee y=x+y+xy\).

For (iv), guess \(i\) in \(\lceil\log_2(d+1)\rceil\) bits. Invalid or malformed guesses accept. A valid branch sequentially locates the two switch positions of module \(i\) and rejects iff both are one. A uniform length clock accepts on timeout. On SAT all branches accept; on UNSAT some coordinate branch rejects. A constant number of full scans and fixed-width counters cost \(N\operatorname{polylog}N\) time and \(O(\log N)\) work.
\end{proof}

Because \(d=\Theta(N/\log N)\), the exact \(2d-1\) promised circuit lower bound is only \(\Omega(N/\log N)\). Fixing nonswitch encoding bits can restrict a full decision circuit to this promise, but the direction cannot be reversed and the exponent is far below the van Melkebeek full-decision threshold \(q\ge1.444997\ldots\).
