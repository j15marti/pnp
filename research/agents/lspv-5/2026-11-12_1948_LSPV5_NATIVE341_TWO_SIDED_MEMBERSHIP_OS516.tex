\section{Two-sided result}

\begin{theorem}[Recovered-checker two-sided membership failure]
For the pinned artifacts above:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V_{341}$ accepts a 47-paid-node packet whose 18-bit assignment
\texttt{101001000000100000} satisfies all 48 signed source clauses.  Adding an
external field claiming the source is UNSAT changes the accepted bytes but leaves
checker stdout byte-identical.
\item $R_{\rm decl}$ admits the one-paid-step Resolution refutation
$[1],[-1]\vdash []$, while $V_{341}$ rejects the corresponding \texttt{RES}
opcode as \texttt{unknown rule}.
\item A fresh sixteenth AND3 node, duplicating an already accepted truth function,
is exact on all eight assignments to its three-support set, yet $V_{341}$ rejects
the packet solely with \texttt{proof candidate length}.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently $L(V_{341})\not\subseteq L(R_{\rm decl})$ and
$L(R_{\rm decl})\not\subseteq L(V_{341})$ for the published interpretation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first statement is an exhaustive evaluation of the 48 clauses followed by a
fresh OS checker run: 48/48 clauses are true and the checker reports 2,273 checks.
The ordinary and externally mislabeled packets yield the same stdout digest
\texttt{7ae1477f0044\ldots 1329}; therefore the metadata is not authenticated.
The second statement is the elementary Resolution inference on complementary unit
clauses, while the exact executable exits 1 with \texttt{Error: unknown rule}.
For the third, the runner evaluates both the existing node and its fresh duplicate
on all eight assignments, obtaining equality in every case.  The only changed
structural quantity is paid length $15\to16$, and the unchanged executable exits 1
with \texttt{Verifier: proof candidate length}.  These are witnesses for the two
strict non-inclusions.  The conclusion is restricted to the recovered executable
and the separate declaration; it does not assert that a missing authoritative
historical checker had either defect.
\end{proof}

The full raw run contains 12 native OS invocations: seven accepts, five rejects,
and 40,864 accepted assertions.  The exact accepted external-claim packet has
SHA-256 \texttt{f9767ad782562ef5d7e4cc7cc8642d2e61c3bd9a60abccf40665b7852387cbfe}.
The rejected semantically valid redundant-AND3 packet has SHA-256

\section{CEGAR and concrete-semantics consequence}

The accepted SAT-source execution is a genuine concrete path of $V_{341}$ and may
not be removed by refinement.  Conversely, a may-abstraction for the declared
calculus must include general Resolution; excluding it is not a proved-spurious
path but a concrete-step omission.  A usable P2-D1 deliverable therefore needs one
source-authenticated dispatcher for every actual accepted operation, typed parents,
contexts, freshness, exported/private wires, discharge, joins, deletion, weakening,
paid DEF facets, and all costs.  It must prove concrete-step inclusion, preserve
completed refutations, and assign abstract cost no greater than concrete cost.
No current artifact supplies that theorem.
