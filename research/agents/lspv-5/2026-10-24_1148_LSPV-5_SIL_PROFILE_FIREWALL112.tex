\section{Target, claim card and mechanisms}
The ultimate target is still
\[
\forall A\ {\rm(total,uniform,deterministic,exact)}\ \forall c>0\ \forall N_0\
\exists F\quad |F|\ge N_0\land T_A(F)>|F|^c,                         \tag{1}
\]
for one fixed NP-complete language and encoding.  The tested predecessor is
the interaction-cluster profile from AFFINE-EXCESS-NULLITY111.  For affine
component $i$ with nullity $d_i$, let $e_i=\max(d_i-1,0)$; in interaction
cluster $C_j$ let $K_j=\sum_{i\in C_j}e_i$.  That profile supports a valid
\emph{upper} algorithm.  The falsification target is the stronger inference
that a large raw $K_j$ is an unavoidable or representation-neutral cost.

Three mechanisms were tested.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{M1 (\PROVED{} counterexample):} a private star of exact
complementary pairs changes profile $(1,\ldots,1)$ to $(q)$ while preserving
the entire visible relation and even its extension count.
\item \textbf{M2 (\PROVED{} algorithm):} the SIL-RC trace
\texttt{CP2}$^q\to$\texttt{CL3}$^{q-1}\to$\texttt{VE1} removes the star in
linear charged work; the equal-metadata SAT/UNSAT anchor then collapses by two
more \texttt{CP2} applications and Gaussian continuation or \texttt{UP3}.
\item \textbf{M3 (\OPEN{} repair):} replace raw profile by a semantic or
normal-form obstruction invariant under every affordable exact rewrite.
Existence, acquisition and minimality of such a normal form are unproved;
minimizing over a finite SIL catalogue would not cover arbitrary algorithms.
\end{itemize}

\section{Equal-metadata two-label family}
For $q\ge1$, use $q$ disjoint triples
$(a_i,b_i,c_i)$ with odd parity
\[
a_i\oplus b_i\oplus c_i=1.                                          \tag{2}
\]
In CNF, each is represented by its four falsifying-assignment clauses.
Introduce fresh $u,v,z$.  Both labels contain the first anchor pair
\[
(a_0\vee u)\land(a_0\vee\neg u),                                    \tag{3}
\]
which forces $a_0=1$.  The SAT source repeats the same head with $v$,
whereas the UNSAT source uses the opposite head:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_q^+:&(a_0\vee v)\land(a_0\vee\neg v),\\
S_q^-:&(\neg a_0\vee v)\land(\neg a_0\vee\neg v).
\end{array}                                                          \tag{4}
\]
Finally add the private star wrapper
\[
W_q(z)=\bigwedge_{i<q}
[(z\vee a_i)\land(z\vee\neg a_i)].                                  \tag{5}
\]
Let $F_q^\pm$ denote parity blocks plus (3)--(4), and
$G_q^\pm=F_q^\pm\land W_q(z)$, with $z$ private.

Both labels have identical unsigned supports, record widths, headers and
length; only two literal-sign fields in (4) differ.  Exact counts are
\[
n=3q+3,\qquad k=4q,\qquad m_2=2q+4,\qquad M=6q+4.                    \tag{6}
\]
In the established four-word-per-clause grammar,
\[
N=(24q+18)\left\lceil\log_2(64(9q+8))\right\rceil.                  \tag{7}
\]

\begin{theorem}[SIL projection-profile firewall; \PROVED]\label{th:firewall}
For every $q\ge1$ and either label,
\[
\exists z\,G_q^\pm\equiv F_q^\pm                                  \tag{8}
\]
on all visible variables.  Before simplification, the affine interaction
graph is a connected tree and has the single excess profile $(q)$.  After
the exact SIL-RC trace
\[
\texttt{CP2}^q\to\texttt{CL3}^{q-1}\to\texttt{VE1},                \tag{9}
\]
the profile is $(1,\ldots,1)$ with $q$ clusters.  The rewrite and all pattern
checks take $O(q)$ word operations and $O(q)$ peak live records under an
indexed fixed-width representation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every $i$,
\[
(z\vee a_i)\land(z\vee\neg a_i)\equiv z                           \tag{10}
\]
by \texttt{CP2}; this identity remains valid although $a_i$ occurs in its
parity block.  Thus $W_q(z)\equiv z$.  Duplicate units contract by
\texttt{CL3}, and $\exists z\,z\equiv\top$ by the private degree-one
projection \texttt{VE1}, proving (8).  Indeed each visible satisfying
assignment has exactly one extension, $z=1$.

Recognizing (2) gives $q$ affine components of nullity two, hence excess one
each.  Variables $u,v,z$, absent from the affine equations, are singleton
nullity-one components with excess zero.  Binary clauses (5) make a star from
$z$ to all $q$ parity components; (3)--(4) attach $u,v$ to component zero.
This graph has $q+3$ vertices and $q+2$ edges and is connected, hence is a
tree and its unique cluster has $K_1=q$.  After (9), $z$ and all star edges
are gone.  The component containing $a_0,u,v$ has excess one; every other
parity component is isolated with excess one, giving $(1,\ldots,1)$.
An index keyed by common clause tail and opposite pivot sign finds all pairs
after one source scan; sorting gives a deterministic $O(q\log q)$ fallback.
All writes and retained maps are linear.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Both labels and direct adaptive decision; \PROVED]
$G_q^+$ is SAT with exactly $2^{2q+1}$ assignments, while $G_q^-$ is UNSAT.
A label-blind uniform exact policy recognizes and decides both in $O(q)$ word
work and $O(q)$ peak live words on the canonical family, with complete exact
fallback outside it.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Applying \texttt{CP2} to (3) yields unit $a_0$.  On the plus source the
second anchor yields the same unit; odd parity then leaves two assignments in
block zero and four in each other block.  Variables $u,v$ are free and $z$ is
forced one, giving $2\cdot4^{q-1}\cdot4=2^{2q+1}$ assignments.  On the minus
source the second pair yields $\neg a_0$, so \texttt{UP3} detects conflict.
The SAT branch may reconstruct one witness, e.g. every parity block $100$,
$u=v=0,z=1$, and replay every source clause.  UNSAT needs no model enumeration
or prescribed proof output.
\end{proof}

\section{Two independent bypasses and Role 4 audit}
The raw profile $(q)$ would make the unnormalized AFFINE-EXCESS111 enumeration
ceiling $2^q\operatorname{poly}(N)$.  This is not a contradiction: it was an
upper bound, not the best algorithm.  The family has two paid polynomial
bypasses.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The SIL trace (9) normalizes the profile to $q$ clusters of excess one.
\item Even without that trace, the raw interaction graph is a tree with
maximum local affine dimension $h=2$.  Independently checked Role~3
TREEJOIN79 message passing uses at most $2^{2h}=16$ local state pairs per
edge, hence polynomial total work.
\end{enumerate}
This proves that neither connectedness nor one large raw $K_1$ is sufficient.

Role~4's affine-bijunctive criterion also checks out.  For a projected affine
relation $R=\{c+At\}$ of rank $d$, let $s$ be the number of distinct nonzero
coordinate covectors.  All unary/pairwise consequences identify constants
and equal/negated duplicate covectors.  If $s=d$, the distinct covectors are
independent and these clauses exactly define $R$; if $s>d$, their maximal
2-CNF relaxation has $2^s$ rows while $R$ has $2^d$, so no same-boundary
2-CNF defines $R$.  One odd XOR3 block has $d=2,s=3$ and is not bijunctive,
yet the present family remains easy through interaction structure and SIL
rewrites.  Local nonbijunctivity is therefore expressive power, not a time
lower bound.
