\section{Quantifiers and preregistered falsifiers}
The ultimate ARC claim concerns one fixed, uniform, total, exact ordinary-SAT decider on
\emph{all} physical inputs in the indexed read-only-input FLMV model, with one simultaneous
time/space pair.  The present theorem is only about a declared residual summary and cannot
be promoted to a runtime lower bound.

Four attacks were registered before the targeted archive and primary-source search.

\item[H3.] The collision also invalidates LSPV-3's authenticated partial-2CNF SCC terminal.

\item[H4.] The PC-BRANCH-COLOUR134 barrier silently imports a lower bound for the wrong

\end{description}

\section{Exact collision and selector parent}
For a unique-clause proper 2CNF $G$ over active names $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, define the
\emph{sign-blind pair interface}
\[
 \Sigma(G)=\left(
   ((d_i^+,d_i^-))_{i=1}^n,
   \left[\!\left[\{i,j\}: C\in G\text{ has variable support }\{i,j\}\right]\!\right]
 \right).                                                    \tag{1}
\]
It retains the sign count at every variable and the entire multiset of unordered clause
supports, but forgets the correlation of the two signs within each clause.

Let
\begin{align*}
 A={}&(\neg x\vee\neg y)\wedge(\neg x\vee y)
      \wedge(x\vee\neg z)\wedge(x\vee z),\\
 B={}&(\neg x\vee\neg y)\wedge(x\vee y)
      \wedge(\neg x\vee\neg z)\wedge(x\vee z).
                                                               \tag{2}
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}[Sign-correlation collision]\label{th:collision}
The formulas in (2) have unique proper binary clauses and
\[
 \Sigma(A)=\Sigma(B)=((2,2),(1,1),(1,1);
        \{x,y\},\{x,y\},\{x,z\},\{x,z\}).                    \tag{3}
\]
Nevertheless $A\in\UNSAT$, while $B$ has exactly the two models
$(x,y,z)=(0,1,1),(1,0,0)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first two clauses of $A$ resolve to $\neg x$, while the last two resolve
to $x$.  For $B$, the first support-pair enforces $y=\neg x$ and the second
enforces $z=\neg x$, giving exactly the stated models.  Direct inspection gives
the identical occurrence vector and support multiset (3).
\end{proof}

Introduce a fresh selector $s$ and the proper ternary source
\[
 P=\bigwedge_{C\in A}(s\vee C)\ \wedge\
   \bigwedge_{D\in B}(\neg s\vee D).                          \tag{4}
\]
All eight clauses are distinct and contain three distinct variables.  Restriction $s=0$
gives exactly $A$ after satisfied clauses are deleted; restriction $s=1$ gives exactly $B$.
Consequently $P$ is SAT with exactly two models, both having $s=1$.  A checker that merges
the two children solely because their $\Sigma$ labels agree and reuses the UNSAT proof of
$A$ at the $B$ child would falsely refute the SAT source $P$.  This is a common-input,
same-parent counterexample, not a comparison of two unrelated programs.

\paragraph{What repairs the interface.}
Keeping each signed implication edge, or equivalently the complete signed clause, separates
$A$ from $B$ and makes the usual SCC criterion sound.  A hash or compact label is also safe
only if exact injectivity on every residual that may be merged is independently proved and
its construction, source reads, collision handling, retained bits and replay are charged.
Bounded-error fingerprints do not satisfy exact certificate soundness.

\section{Independent peer and quantifier audit}
\paragraph{Role 3 SCC and PC leaves.}
The source-pointer SCC grammar checks actual source offsets and signed endpoints of both
paths $u\leadsto\neg u$ and $\neg u\leadsto u$; (3) is therefore not its interface.
The later PC-BRANCH-COLOUR134 theorem uses a different, explicit unshared tree grammar.
Its properization replaces every binary clause $a\vee b$ by
$(a\vee b\vee z)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg z)$ with a fresh $z$; the uniform
restriction $z=0$ recovers the original clause and satisfies the mate.  This preserves the
claimed PC/PCR degree lower bound in the required direction.  Conneryd et al. state that their
lower bound applies to the standard CNF polynomial translation and that the size--degree
relation also applies to PCR.  Thus this red team found no encoding mismatch in that arrow.

\paragraph{coNTIME polarity and clocks.}
For a fixed sound verifier $V(F,\pi)$ and a computable length-only cap $P_*(N)$, the correct
co-nondeterministic SAT machine guesses at most $P_*(N)$ proof bits and \emph{rejects iff}
verification succeeds.  On SAT, soundness makes every branch accept; on UNSAT, completeness
provides some rejecting branch.  Malformed proofs, cap overruns and uniform-clock timeouts must
therefore accept, not reject.  This orientation is exact but supplies no cap, no polynomial
coverage, no deterministic proof discovery, and no same-machine FLMV pair.  A minimum short
proof on some sources cannot replace the required all-UNSAT maximum bound.

\paragraph{Current circuit/ARC ledger.}
LSPV-2's record-output inverse is an exact architecture-restricted accessor but still belongs
to a witness verifier, not a full SAT decision circuit.  LSPV-4's pinned output floor prevents
the displayed two-level regularizer from creating power-smaller children.  LSPV-1's new
ARC-SOURCE-RESOLUTION-BARRIER186 proves that the BRANCH/EMPTY/PATH grammar polynomially
simulates Resolution and therefore lacks polynomial all-UNSAT coverage.  Role 3's affine/PCR
augmentation escapes Tseitin but is itself exponentially hard on the properized random
graph-colouring family in its stated unshared fixed-field model.  None of these results gives
the extra $d=901/500$ subpolynomial-space deterministic seed from $P=NP$.
