\section{The general incidence floor}
Let $F$ be a proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ three-CNF: every active variable
appears in exactly two positive and two negative literal occurrences, and
each clause has three distinct variable names.  Write $n=n(F)$ and $m=m(F)$.
Then $3m=4n$.  Let $\tau(F)$ be the minimum number of variable names meeting
every clause.

\begin{lemma}[Exact-$(2,2)$ incidence floor]\label{lem:floor}
Every such $F$ satisfies
\[
                  \tau(F)\ge m/4=n/3.                 \tag{1}
\]
This is independent of satisfiability, monotonicity and connectedness.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Properness puts each of a variable's four occurrences in a distinct clause,
so one selected name meets at most four clauses.  Covering all $m$ clauses
therefore requires at least $m/4$ names.  Double counting literal
occurrences gives $3m=4n$.
\end{proof}

This already shows that a growing exact-$(2,2)$ family cannot have a global
clause-hitting header of logarithmic size.  To refute an \emph{all-UNSAT}
coverage statement, however, one must also supply growing UNSAT inputs.
The next two sections do so, first entirely finitely from $W_1$, then inside
the connected clause-monotone target using one published base theorem.

\section{Disjoint $W_1$ product: exact obstruction}
Use the exact twenty signed clauses
\[
\begin{split}
&(-1,3,-14),(2,-8,9),(10,-13,14),(3,7,-13),(-4,-9,-11),\\
&(5,8,-14),(4,-8,-11),(1,7,13),(1,-7,10),(-5,-6,-15),\\
&(-2,-6,12),(-4,9,-10),(-2,5,-12),(-3,6,-15),(6,-7,11),\\
&(2,-9,11),(4,8,-10),(-3,12,15),(-5,-12,15),(-1,13,14).
\end{split}                                                     \tag{2}
\]
Call this formula $W_1$.  The independent auditor checked every one of its
$2^{15}$ assignments, all signed occurrence counts, properness and clause
uniqueness.  It is UNSAT.  Exhaustion of subsets of the fifteen names through
size five found the unique minimum transversal
\[
                   \{2,4,7,14,15\},\qquad \tau(W_1)=5.          \tag{3}
\]
The source is proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ but \emph{not clause-monotone}; this
distinction is retained.

Let $F_k=\dot\bigcup_{i=0}^{k-1}W_1^{(i)}$, using source name
$x_{15i+j}$ for local name $x_j$ and source clause ID $(i,r)$ for local
clause $r$.  Renaming preserves every signed literal, proper clause and
unique source ID.  Clauses from different copies have disjoint name sets,
so they cannot duplicate.  Each copy is an UNSAT conjunct; hence $F_k$ is
UNSAT.

\begin{theorem}[Exact global-header failure]\label{th:product}
For every $k\ge1$,
\[
n(F_k)=15k,\quad m(F_k)=20k,\quad \tau(F_k)=5k.          \tag{4}
\]
Under the declared dense indexed encoding,
$N_k=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$, and therefore
\[
                 \tau(F_k)=\Theta(N_k/\log N_k).        \tag{5}
\]
In particular, for every fixed $C$, sufficiently large $k$ has
$\tau(F_k)>C\log N_k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every global hitting set restricts in copy $i$ to a hitting set of that
copy, because no name outside copy $i$ occurs in its clauses.  Equation (3)
gives the lower bound $5k$; the union of the $k$ translated five-sets gives
the matching upper bound.  There are $60k$ literal records and identifiers
range to $15k$.  A fixed-field binary indexed encoding costs
$\Theta(k\log(k+2))$ bits; the same asymptotic follows for canonical DIMACS
decimal records.  Inverting this relation gives (5), which dominates
$\log N_k$.
\end{proof}

Thus condition (6) of \texttt{BACKDOOR-CAP161}, with any
$r(N)=O(\log N)$, is \status{FALSIFIED}, not OPEN.  This retires only the
global-header route to the claimed coNTIME exponent.

\paragraph{Why adaptive/local refutation survives.}
The first twenty authenticated clause IDs already form $W_1$.  A fixed
five-variable branch/SCC certificate, or any fixed verified refutation of
that component, proves the entire conjunction UNSAT.  Its structural proof
does not inspect or hit clauses in the remaining copies.  Authentication may
still require a charged parser/source scan under a general input grammar,
but the proof tree is constant after the selected component is found.  Hence
large $\tau(F_k)$ is not an adaptive-certificate lower bound and says nothing
about an arbitrary deterministic SAT decider.

\section{Connected clause-monotone exact-$(2,2)$ control}
Disconnectedness is not the essential obstruction.  Let $U$ be D\"ocker's
published connected 198-variable, 264-clause monotone exact-$(2,2)$ UNSAT
formula from Appendix C \cite{docker}.  The archived source record has blob
\texttt{f9dda41a810ad8e02bd553f2711a26a4050861f9}.  This run independently
checked its 264 exact records for properness, uniqueness, monotonicity,
two-positive/two-negative degrees, absence of complementary partners and
incidence connectedness.  Its UNSAT status is imported from the primary
theorem, not from brute force.

For pairwise disjoint UNSAT formulas $U_i$, select clauses
$C_i=(\ell_i\vee D_i)$ and replace them by
$(\ell_{\pi(i)}\vee D_i)$ for a permutation $\pi$.
\begin{lemma}[UNSAT port permutation]\label{lem:port}
The resulting conjunction is UNSAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If an assignment satisfied it, it would satisfy every $U_i\setminus C_i$.
Since $U_i$ is UNSAT, all literals of $C_i$ must then be false, including
$\ell_i$ and all of $D_i$.  Permutation makes every moved literal
$\ell_{\pi(i)}$ false as well, so every replacement clause is false, a
contradiction.
\end{proof}

In copy $i$ of $U$, take zero-based clause 1,
$(x_{194,i}\vee x_{196,i}\vee x_{197,i})$, and cyclically replace its first
literal by $x_{194,i+1}$.  Call the result $G_k$.  Direct source checks for
$k\in\{2,3,4,8,16,32\}$ verified that each $G_k$ is connected, proper,
unique, clause-monotone, exact-$(2,2)$ and fully unpaired.  Symbolically,
the same-sign cyclic permutation preserves every signed quota; crossed
clauses connect the source blocks, and the checked local source stays
connected after removal of the outgoing occurrence.  Lemma~\ref{lem:port}
and published $\UNSAT(U)$ prove $\UNSAT(G_k)$ for all $k\ge2$.

By Lemma~\ref{lem:floor},
\[
n(G_k)=198k,quad m(G_k)=264k,quad
\tau(G_k)\ge66k=\Theta(N_k/\log N_k).                   \tag{6}
\]
This is a connected clause-monotone exact-$(2,2)$ counterfamily to global
logarithmic coverage.  It remains an easy recognized family with a fixed
port-permutation proof; (6) is not a runtime lower bound.  For comparison,
adding fresh bridge clauses to intact $W_1$ copies also gives connected
ordinary proper 3CNFs with an obvious local UNSAT core, but independently
checked signed quotas fail.  Such bridges must not be mislabeled exact-$(2,2)$.

\section{coNTIME polarity, streamed verification and peer arrows}
Let $R(F,\pi)=1$ mean that the source-authenticated verifier accepts an UNSAT
proof.  With a length-only cap and a total clock, malformed records and
timeouts return $R=0$.  Soundness/completeness are
\[
F\in\UNSAT\iff\exists\pi\ R(F,\pi)=1.                 \tag{7}
\]
Branchwise complementation gives the correct co-nondeterministic SAT
orientation:
\[
F\in\SAT\Rightarrow\forall\pi\ [1-R(F,\pi)=1],\qquad
F\in\UNSAT\Rightarrow\exists\pi\ [1-R(F,\pi)=0].       \tag{8}
\]
The Role-3 source-pointer grammar and Role-4 balanced-dictionary repair obey
this polarity: malformed/timeout branches accept after complement, while one
valid UNSAT proof gives a rejecting branch.  The streamed bound is a
\status{PROVED/EXACT restricted verifier upper bound}; it is useful only
under a uniform all-UNSAT proof-size cap.  The present result falsifies the
special case where every proof begins with one global clause-hitting header.
It does not falsify adaptive partial-2CNF leaves; indeed $F_k$ is their
intended bypass control.  A favorable minimum proof on selected inputs is not
worst-case all-UNSAT coverage.

The other peer arrows survive only at their stated types:
\begin{itemize}
\item LSPV-1's compact canonical selector $J_F$ does not authenticate its
universal totality.  A full-parent call to the guest $A$ retains the parent
exponent and cannot be booked as contraction.
\item LSPV-2's $O(N\log^3N)$ object is a witness-verifier circuit.  Its newer
source Rank/Select theorem covers saved switch outputs, not every gate or a
full deterministic SAT decision circuit.  Van Melkebeek's circuit threshold
cannot be applied across that type gap.
\item LSPV-4's arithmetic floor
$m_2=105616G+33856B+5888q+95680\ge33856B$ is exact for the specified pinned
compiler.  It is not an algorithm-independent circuit lower bound.  Also
$P=NP$ supplies an unknown finite polynomial exponent $K$, not the extra
$d=901/500$ subpolynomial-space seed.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Next decisive lemma.}
Discard global clause-hitting coverage.  Prove or falsify one fixed canonical,
source-authenticated \emph{adaptive} UNSAT proof grammar satisfying, for all
ordinary UNSAT $N$-bit inputs, a constructible length-only cap
$R(\pi)\le N^{a+o(1)}$ and worst-branch verification
$N^{c+o(1)}$ below the exact van-Melkebeek threshold.  The proof must include
malformed/timeout branches, source-offset access, dictionary state, complete
SAT and UNSAT quantifiers, and cannot call a parent-size SAT solver.  A
separate same-machine deterministic small-space seed is still required.
