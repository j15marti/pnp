\section{Canonical target and audit questions}
An instance of $\CM$ is a CNF with unique clauses, every clause containing
exactly three distinct variables and having either three positive or three
negative literals, and every active variable occurring exactly twice with
each sign.  For a clause $C$, $\bar C$ flips all signs but retains disjunction.
Let
\[
 \mathcal P_{\rm pair}=\{F\in\CM:(\forall C\in F)\,\bar C\in F\},\qquad
 \Cstar=\CM\setminus\mathcal P_{\rm pair}.
\]
Thus only \emph{fully} paired formulas are excluded.  Partial pairs,
disconnected formulas, easy instances and nonminimal UNSAT instances remain.
The language $L_\star$ accepts exactly valid encodings of satisfiable members
of $\Cstar$ and rejects malformed strings.

If $F$ has $k$ active variables and $m$ clauses, literal counting gives
$3m=4k$ and $m_+=m_-=2k/3$.  In the dense indexed encoding used by the peer
reports, $N=\Theta(k\log(k+2))$.  These identities are syntactic; they are not
running-time lower bounds.

Three falsifiable possibilities were tested.
\begin{description}
\item[H1.] The announced six-variable $Y$ fails the exact M22 grammar,
the witness, the model count, or the nonpairing test.  \textbf{FALSIFIED}.
\item[H2.] Adjoining $Y$ can also repair arbitrary restricted recursive
children back into $\Cstar$.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} by an exact iff lemma.
\item[H3.] Branching on every variable in the unmatched-clause support yields
positive net search exponent saving under exhaustive independent completion.
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the exact budget remains $2^k$.
\end{description}

\section{Independent check of $Y$ and the exclusion reduction}
Let
\[
 P=\{123,145,246,356\},\qquad Q=\{124,136,235,456\},
\]
and let $Y=P^+\cup Q^-$.  Direct inspection gives eight unique proper
monotone ternary clauses.  In both signs every variable occurs exactly twice.
No support belongs to both $P$ and $Q$, so no clause of $Y$ has its complement.
The assignment $111000$ satisfies $Y$.  Exhaustive evaluation of all
$2^6=64$ assignments gives exactly 20 models.

Döcker's Theorem~1 proves NP-completeness of Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2), the present
$\CM$, and Section~3 proves that the fully complementary-paired subfamily is
satisfiable.  Full pairing is polynomially recognizable.  Therefore the total
map on valid $\CM$ instances
\[
 f(F)=\begin{cases}F,&F\notin\mathcal P_{\rm pair},\\Y,&F\in\mathcal P_{\rm pair}
 \end{cases}
\]
preserves satisfiability and lands in $\Cstar$.  Malformed strings must still
be recognized and rejected by the source language's total reduction.  Hence
$L_\star$ is NP-complete.  This is a class-completeness theorem, not $P\ne NP$
and not a quantitative lower bound.

Role~4 recovered the stronger valid-source no-test anchor
$A(F)=F\mathbin{\dot\cup}Y$ on fresh variables.  It always lies in $\Cstar$,
preserves the SAT bit, and multiplies the model count by 20.  The following
red-team lemma states exactly what this anchor does \emph{not} repair.

\begin{theorem}[Anchor grammar equivalence; PROVED/EXACT]
Let $G$ be any finite CNF whose variables are disjoint from a fresh copy of
$Y$.  Then
\[
       G\mathbin{\dot\cup}Y\in\CM\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\in\CM.
\]
Moreover, whenever these equivalent conditions hold,
$G\mathbin{\dot\cup}Y\in\Cstar$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $G\in\CM$, disjoint union preserves clause uniqueness, proper monotone
ternarity and the per-variable two-positive/two-negative counts.  Since the
fresh $Y$ component contains a clause lacking its complement, the union is not
fully paired.  Conversely, if the union belongs to $\CM$, restrict the grammar
conditions to variables and clauses of the disjoint $G$ component.  No $Y$
literal can repair an arity, sign, duplication, or occurrence-count failure in
$G$; hence $G\in\CM$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Restriction nonadapter; PROVED/EXACT]
The $Y$ anchor is not an adapter from ordinary simplified pin children to
$\Cstar$.  In particular, setting $x_1=1$ in $Y$ deletes the two positive
$x_1$ clauses and turns the two negative $x_1$ clauses into binary clauses.
The residual is not in $\CM$, and adjoining another fresh $Y$ leaves it outside
$\CM$ and therefore outside $\Cstar$.
\end{corollary}
This distinction is load-bearing for contraction: a solver promised only
$\Cstar$ cannot be called recursively on such a child.  A separate type-restoring
compiler, its ordinary output bits, correctness, compatibility, reconstruction,
time, and peak space are still required.

\section{Residual budget and direct-lower-bound firewall}
For declared independent exhaustive residual completions $r_j$, write
\[
 \Psi=\sum_j2^{r_j},\qquad R_{\rm search}=k-\log_2\Psi.
\]
If all $d$ variables of a support are branched both ways, there are $B=2^d$
children with $r_j=k-d$.  Therefore
\[
 \Psi=2^d2^{k-d}=2^k,qquad R_{\rm search}=0.
\]
This is exact for the stated exhaustive budget and was rechecked for
$k=24$, $0\le d\le12$.  It does not forbid a symbolic affine, Horn, SCC,
factor, learned-clause, cache, or direct-decision continuation; those may be
faster precisely because they do not execute the exhaustive budget.

The unmatched-support number $q$ is an independently defined syntactic
parameter on $\CM$, but it is undefined after the ordinary restriction above
and it is not a complete-state progress potential.  The complete six-variable
audit has easy SAT instances at $q=2,3,4$.  A template recognizer may finish
them after acquisition without enumeration.  Consequently, neither $q$ nor
$2^{3q}$ is an independently proved mandatory post-acquisition work measure.

Role~1's PHASE-SANDWICH136 supplies a second scope firewall: an arbitrary
decider can be normalized to do its work before the final previously unread
bit and halt shortly after that acquisition.  A potential initiated at a
\emph{paid clean scan-first state} survives this objection in principle, but it
still needs an independently large initial value and bounded drop for every
adaptive transition.  ADAPTIVE-COVER75's hitting number $\tau\le N$ counts
input acquisition only and is not added again here.

The newest Role~2 executable, M22-GAUGE-FIREWALL132 at commit
\texttt{d3839a0e}, independently tests a related representation switch:
variablewise sign switching preserves the clausewise-monotone grammar only
when the switched set is a union of connected components.  Arbitrary
individual sign flips therefore cannot serve as a hidden repair for the pin
children above.  This is a restricted grammar theorem, not a time lower bound.

\section{Two-level adaptive-state and bit-resource ledger}
The tested policy is deliberately concrete: validate/recognize the current
representation, consider direct template decision and symbolic factor methods,
otherwise try a pin child, simplify it, test its type, and only call a
$\Cstar$-promised solver if that type test succeeds.  The representative
source is the SAT anchor $Y$; the published large fixture supplies the UNSAT
syntax control.  No true-label oracle is used.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.15\linewidth}p{.78\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Item&Charged resource/accounting\\\midrule\endhead
Initial state&Read-only encoding of $Y$ (or the 198-variable fixture), empty
workspace, no label, grammar and parameters not yet trusted.\\
Acquisition&Read every source bit used by validation.  Charge source reads,
index updates, comparisons, duplicate tests, occurrence counters, unchanged
writes, and failed recognizers.  A simple low-space nested-scan validator uses
$O(N^2\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work and $O(\log N)$ live writable/index
bits; hash tables may reduce time but their full storage and collision logic
must be charged.\\
Rule selection&After validation, compute pairing/unmatched supports with paid
source rereads.  Alternatives considered are direct $Y$ recognition, the
known factor algorithm, and a pin.  Selection and switching are ordinary work.\\
Level 1 pin&For $x_1=1$, read all eight clause records and their 24 literal
records, evaluate literal signs/IDs, delete two satisfied clauses, and emit four
ternary plus two binary residual clauses with a fresh header.  Every emitted
bit, including unchanged copied IDs, is a write.  A materialized child occupies
its full generalized-CNF encoding at peak; a streaming builder saves space but
does not make it a legal $\Cstar$ input.\\
Level 1 adapter&Appending $Y$ reads/updates header and width fields and emits 24
fresh literal records.  If materialized, all output bits are live until the
call; if virtually addressed, every target query pays address selection and
source/fixed-table access with $O(\log N)$ extra live bits.  In either mode the
two binary clauses remain, so the promised recursive call is rejected.\\
Level 2 pin&For every second distinct variable and bit, repeat scan, simplify,
serialize/test, and reanchor.  Across all 120 ordered two-level cases, every
residual and reanchored residual remained outside $\CM$ because of nonternary
clauses.  Failed type restoration is charged; it yields no recursive SAT call.\\
Traffic vector&Keep separately: source reads; workspace reads; workspace and
output writes including unchanged values; control/addressing/comparison work;
serialized child/header bits; cache operations; retained ancestor/buffer bits;
and output reconstruction.  Their sum is primitive bit work, not literal-flip
count or physical transistor activity.\\
Peak space&Nested scans require $O(\log N)$ counters beyond any serialized child.
Materialization adds the complete child/output size; streaming adds only the
largest live record/buffer but still pays all traffic.  Read-only source is
excluded only under the same convention as the receiving machine.\\
Bit-time and flips&Cumulative bit-time is $\sum_t S_{\rm live}(t)$ on actual
steps.  Full-state Hamming flips are logged separately from all writes; no
gate/wire/switch lower bound is claimed.  Program and fixed $Y$ table size are
$O(1)$ for this fixed construction and are recorded separately.\\
Correctness condition&A child solver is invoked only on a verified member of
$\Cstar$; otherwise the parent must choose another exact rule or fall back.
The experiment proves rejection of this adapter, not hardness of the source.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

For total valid-source anchoring rather than recursive repair, the dense
encoding of Role~4 has
\[
N=3w+2+3m(w+1),\quad
N'-N=3(m+1)(w'-w)+24(w'+1),
\]
where $w=\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil$ and
$w'=\lceil\log_2(k+7)\rceil$.  Materialization pays all $N'$ output writes and
their storage; virtual access pays each requested output bit.  This preserves

route from cofinally many achievable integer diagonals into a verified

 t<241/200,qquad s<11642479/23232400,
\]

ordinary all-input SAT decider into a jointly favorable successor, and a
$\Cstar$-promised algorithm does not automatically lie in FLMV's ordinary-SAT
box.  The exact all-input reduction-size, bit-access, time and peak-space
