\section{Exact finite formulation}
There are $\binom{12}{3}=220$ candidate supports per sign.  For each support
$e$, let $x_e^+,x_e^-\in\{0,1\}$.  Degree equalities force
\[
 \sum_{e\ni v}x_e^+=\begin{cases}1&v\in P,\\2&v\in A,\end{cases}
 \qquad
 \sum_{e\ni v}x_e^-=2.
\]
Consequently every feasible block has exactly seven positive and eight
negative clauses.  For each nonconstant port word $z$ and each
$y\in\{0,1\}^9$, rejection is the linear covering constraint
\[
 \sum_{e:\,(z,y)|_e=000}x_e^+
 +\sum_{e:\,(z,y)|_e=111}x_e^-\ \ge 1.
\]
For fixed witnesses $y^0,y^1$, every clause falsified by either complete
constant-word assignment is fixed to zero.  These constraints are equivalent
to the declared projection requirement.

\begin{lemma}[Complete witness-orbit reduction]
Under simultaneous permutation of the nine private auxiliaries, a witness
pair $(y^0,y^1)$ is classified by
$(n_{00},n_{01},n_{10},n_{11})$, a weak composition of nine.  Hence there are
exactly $\binom{12}{3}=220$ orbits, and feasibility is constant on each orbit.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Two coordinates are in the same orbit precisely when their ordered witness
bit-pairs agree.  A simultaneous permutation can map any two witness pairs
with the same four multiplicities.  Clause supports and all degree and
coverage constraints are invariant under that permutation.
\end{proof}

\section{Two-spine resource audit}
\subsection*{Contraction ledger}
Had a fixed block existed, a compiler over $g$ independent three-port groups
would perform the following disjoint charged work: scan and validate the
source; find signs and occurrence ranks; select the rule; compute fresh-name
addresses; generate seven positive and eight negative clause records per
group; write every header and all 45 indexed literal fields, including
unchanged-value writes; maintain counters and error/fallback control; and
either retain the ordinary child or pay for a virtual accessor on every
recursive source query.  Dense indexed output has
\[
 N'=\Theta(g\log(g+2)),
\]
so construction traffic and bit work are $\Theta(N')$.  Materialization
retains $\Theta(N')$ writable bits for random access; recomputation can reduce
peak live bits to $O(\log N)$ but makes every access pay address arithmetic,
source reads and replay.  Code/template size is constant; actual full-state
bit flips, logical writes, cumulative bit-time and physical gates/wires are
separate measures.

The tested block is infeasible before level one.  Hence there is no genuine
first ordinary child, no second recursive level, no SAT/UNSAT reconstruction
ledger and no favorable same-machine exponent pair.  Even a hypothetical
fixed linear block would have length exponent one, not a power shrink.  This
finite negative result does not establish ARC-EDGE-90.
