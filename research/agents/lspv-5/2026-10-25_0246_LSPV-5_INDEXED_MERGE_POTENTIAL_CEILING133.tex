\section{The common proper-3CNF family}
For even $q$, balanced $A,B\subseteq[q]$, and private helpers $u_j,v_j$,
write
\[
 \operatorname{Pin}(\ell;u,v)=
 \bigwedge_{\sigma,\tau\in\{+1,-1\}}
 (\ell\vee \sigma u\vee\tau v).
\]
The ordinary proper-3CNF $J_q(A,B)$ first contains positive pin blocks for
the sorted names in $A$, then negative pin blocks for the sorted names in
$B$.  Helpers are private and canonical.  Its fixed declaration is
\[
 n=3q,\quad m=4q,\quad
 w=\lceil\log_2(64(7q+1))\rceil,\quad
 N=(16q+2)w,
\]
even if a primary name occurs in both halves and another declared primary is
unused.  Thus SAT and UNSAT controls have identical length and metadata.
Exactly
\[
 \SAT(J_q(A,B))\iff A\cap B=\varnothing.
\]
On balanced subsets the compatibility matrix is a permutation matrix of
rank
\[
 R_q=\binom q{q/2}\ge \frac{2^q}{q+1}
\]
over every field.  This is a global property of the represented relation,
not a property of the current writable machine state.

Role 4's paid recognizer validates the full grammar and sorting.  It then
uses two indexed pointers and at most $q-1$ name comparisons, requiring
$O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ total bit work including validation and
$O(\log N)$ writable bits.  Neither the label nor the name sets are supplied
free.  Unsupported inputs require a total exact fallback, for which no
improved exponent is asserted.

\section{The exact post-acquisition ceiling}
Fix one input and one deterministic execution after a paid phase in which
all source bits have been acquired at least once.  The read-only source may
still be indexed and reread, with those reads charged.  Let
$C_0,C_1,\ldots,C_T$ be the complete machine states and let $u_i\ge0$ be the
elementary bit work charged to transition $C_i\to C_{i+1}$.

\begin{theorem}[POST-ACQUISITION-TELESCOPING-CEILING133;
PROVED/EXACT]\label{thm:tel}
Suppose a potential $\Phi_N$ is defined independently of the unknown optimal
running time, satisfies $\Phi_N(C_T)=0$, and for every permitted transition
of the tested machine satisfies
\[
 \Phi_N(C_i)-\Phi_N(C_{i+1})\le L(N)u_i.
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\Phi_N(C_0)\le L(N)W_{\rm post}},
 \qquad W_{\rm post}=\sum_{i<T}u_i.
\]
This remains true when the policy changes representations, rereads source,
uses caches, or selects different rules adaptively, provided every such step
is present in the charged execution.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sum the transition inequalities.  The left side telescopes to
$\Phi_N(C_0)-\Phi_N(C_T)=\Phi_N(C_0)$ and the right side is
$L(N)W_{\rm post}$.  No monotonicity assumption is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Raw-rank potential firewall; PROVED/EXACT]
On the recognized $J_q$ family, the indexed merge has
$W_{\rm post}=O(q\log q)$ under the declared bit schedule.  Hence no
potential satisfying Theorem~\ref{thm:tel} with
$L(N)=q^{O(1)}$ can also satisfy $\Phi(C_0)\ge R_q$ for all large $q$.
The candidate $R_q\mathbf1[\text{not halted}]$ already drops by $R_q$ at
its terminal toggle and therefore violates the local bound.  By contrast
$\log_2R_q=\Theta(q)$ is compatible with the fast path and can yield at most
an input-order lower bound here, not a superpolynomial one.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Combine Theorem~\ref{thm:tel}, $R_q\ge2^q/(q+1)$, and the polynomial
upper bound on $L(q)W_{\rm post}$.  The final statement follows from
$\log R_q=\Theta(q)$ and the existing $O(q\log q)$ execution.
\end{proof}

This corollary does not say that every useful potential is small.  It says
that a proposed large initial value must be established independently and
must survive comparison with every legal cheap execution.  Defining
$\Phi$ as minimum remaining running time would make the desired conclusion
circular.  A family-wide matrix rank is unchanged when a fixed computation
halts and therefore is not, by itself, a terminal-zero state potential.

\section{Two-spine resource audit}
\subsection*{Direct indexed merge}
The declared elementary basis charges one source/workspace bit read, one
workspace bit write (including unchanged writes), or one bit step of
addressing, control, selection, or comparison as one unit of work.  One live
writable bit costs one unit of space; the read-only input is excluded exactly
as in the receiving model.  Actual data-register flips are counted
separately from writes; complete-state flips lie between measured data flips
and total work because the finite checker does not emulate the program
counter or head encoding bit by bit.

For a full validating scan of an $N$-bit source using $w$-bit registers, one
explicit conservative schedule has: exactly $N$ source reads; at most $N$
buffer writes; at most $2N$ buffer/counter reads; at most
$(12w+32)N$ control, address and selection bit steps; peak live writable
space at most $6w+32$ bits; and bit-time at most the resulting work times
that peak.  This deliberately loose schedule proves acquisition is paid and
near-linear up to logarithms; no free family tag is used.

After validation, each merge comparison rereads two $w$-bit names.  The
finite counter charges disjoint categories
\[
 R_{\rm src}=2cw,\quad
 R_{\rm wk}=c(2w+2b+8),\quad
 W_{\rm wk}=c(2w+2b+8),
\]
\[
 C_{\rm addr/select}=c(12w+12b+32)+1,\qquad
 S_{\max}\le4w+2b+24,
\]
where $c$ is the actual number of comparisons and
$b=\lceil\log_2(q/2+1)\rceil$.  Their sum is the reported post-acquisition
work upper bound.  Unchanged writes are included.  The conservative
cumulative bit-time is $W_{\rm post}S_{\max}$.  There are no children,
ancestors, witness tables, learned clauses, or hidden cache.

\subsection*{Eager named-child alternative}
The comparison policy converts the source to a scoped event stream, chooses
an exact union-cost block schedule, enumerates compatible named boundary
assignments, fully constructs every ordinary proper-3CNF child, and calls an
exact child solver.  For a child containing $m_c$ clauses and $n_c$ actually
used variables, its independent ordinary input length is
\[
 (4m_c+2)\lceil\log_2(64(n_c+m_c+1))\rceil.
\]
Every emitted bit therefore causes at least one charged write, including
zeros and repeated values.  The experiment records both ordinary bits and
the larger tagged scoped-stream representation.  Its reported serialization
is a lower bound on full work: source reads, planning, pin construction,
addressing, SAT calls, cache lookups and control remain additional.  If a
machine streams instead of materializing a whole child, this particular
peak-space lower bound no longer applies; access, recomputation and target
simulation must then be charged in the alternative execution.

Role 3's PIN-BIT-FLOOR98 proves that on all-order cubic expander Tseitin
sources, every sublinear-block unchanged-name eager pin compiler of the
specified kind produces some ordinary child of $\Omega(N)$ bits.  That is a
valid contraction firewall for this compiler.  The same sources admit a paid
affine parity recognizer on recognized inputs.  Thus the floor neither forces
all deciders to generate the child nor supplies an all-input lower bound.

\section{Adaptive-state ledger and hostile controls}
Initial state contains the indexed ordinary source, headers not yet trusted,
no label, no name sets, empty caches, the exact fallback entry point and the
remaining joint resource budget.  Paid grammar acquisition supplies
$(q,w,n,m)$ and sortedness.  The rule selector considers 2-SAT SCC, Horn
propagation, affine elimination, indexed merge, nested unsorted comparison,
scoped child generation and general exact fallback.  Valid $J_q$ selects
merge; failed recognition selects fallback.  The new state contains either
one observed opposite-polarity name (UNSAT) or exhausted disjoint sorted
lists (SAT).  Correctness is set intersection; a SAT witness is unnecessary
for one-bit decision and would be charged if requested.

The hostile controls explain why M1 is not repairable by renaming the same
quantity.  Affine Tseitin inputs permit a parity scan after acquisition;
2-CNF permits SCC; Horn permits propagation; cached repeated subproblems may
be answered by a key lookup; a recognized UNSAT family may be mapped to a
constant-false representation; a recognized SAT source can reconstruct one
witness in polynomial time without materializing its entire relation.
Reordered elimination and source rereads are legal.  Any potential that
ignores these transitions does not quantify over the permitted adaptive
machine.  A globally one-Lipschitz potential on an unrestricted $s$-bit
Hamming state cube has range at most $s$; a larger range must exploit proved
legal-transition restrictions, and the cost of choosing those transitions
cannot be omitted.
