\section{PROPOSE: current missing lemma and four falsifiers}
The missing constructive lemma is a \emph{repeatable} occurrence-balanced
interface compiler: after one legal strict exact-$(2,2)$ splice, can another
uniform splice suppress the extra $Q_0$ cofactor while retaining the source's
SAT/UNSAT answer and eventually producing one power-short child?

Before search and build, four mechanisms were fixed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Repeat the same splice at the transported distinguished clause and test
whether the triangular relation becomes equality.
\item Alternate source occurrences or splice signs and test whether the
unwanted cofactor can be intersected away without solving it.
\item Use ARC220's source-certified selector to compute the cofactor and choose
the effective splice in polynomial time on arbitrary sources.
\item Supply both positive and negative port certificates so that a compact
relation is also efficiently acquired on both answer classes.
\end{enumerate}
Mechanism 1 is now exactly falsified. Mechanisms 2--4 remain open; ARC220 is a
restricted selector, and the two-sided certificate route meets an NP/coNP
acquisition firewall rather than supplying an unconditional compiler.

\section{BUILD: exact idempotence theorem}
Write $H_0$ and $H_1$ for satisfiability of the source under $x=0$ and $x=1$.
Delete the distinguished clause $(\neg x\vee A)$ and conjoin $A$; let $Q_0$
be satisfiability of that punctured source under $x=0$. Then
$Q_0\Rightarrow H_0$ because any $Q_0$ witness also satisfies the deleted
clause.

\begin{theorem}[Transported-foot idempotence]\label{thm:idempotence}
Apply the LSPV-2 negative-occurrence splice once, then repeat it any number of
times at the transported clause $(\neg p_j\vee A)$. For every $r\ge1$, the
exact relation between the original source variable $x$ and the final port
$p_r$ is
\[
 R_r=\begin{pmatrix}H_0&Q_0\\0&H_1\end{pmatrix}=R_1.
\]
Equivalently, this triangular Boolean matrix is idempotent: $R_1^2=R_1$.
Every physical level adds a fresh fixed module, so this iteration grows the
formula rather than contracting it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the final port is zero, the fixed module forces its distinguished foot
false, which recursively forces every preceding port to zero. Thus the only
possible endpoint is $(x,p_r)=(0,0)$, present exactly when $H_0$ holds.
For $(0,1)$, the final splice requires $A$ at the source-facing end; this is
exactly $Q_0$. Conversely a $Q_0$ witness extends through the fixed module at
each level by choosing the appropriate fixed-module witnesses. The endpoint
$(1,0)$ is impossible because the first triangular relation forbids it.
Finally $(1,1)$ is present exactly when $H_1$ holds, again extending through
all fixed copies.

Algebraically, Boolean matrix multiplication gives
\[
 R_1^2=
 \begin{pmatrix}H_0&H_0Q_0\lor Q_0H_1\\0&H_1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The diagonal entries are unchanged because Boolean squaring is idempotent.
The upper-right entry is $Q_0$: if $Q_0$ holds then $H_0$ holds, while the
physical fixed module used by the splice has the required accepting extension
on the $p=1$ side. This is also immediate from the preceding witness
construction. Induction proves the statement for every $r$.
\end{proof}

The algebraic statement does hold for every Boolean triple satisfying
$Q_0\Rightarrow H_0$, independently of $H_1$: the upper-right entry is
$Q_0(H_0\lor H_1)=Q_0$. What remains narrow is the \emph{physical}
realization theorem: it concerns only the declared transported-foot splice,
not arbitrary occurrence choices or arbitrary abstract matrices presented
without an authenticated SAT encoding.

\section{Corrected theorem, constructive and separation implications}
The corrected usable statement is:
\begin{quote}
One legal negative-occurrence splice gives a complete triangular port
relation. Reapplying the same splice to its transported foot preserves that
relation exactly. It neither authenticates $Q_0$ nor converts implication to
equality, and its physical encoding grows linearly in the number of repeats.
Any useful regulator must therefore change the interface, combine independent
cofactor constraints with proved semantics, or acquire enough source profile
information to choose a different operation---all at charged polynomial cost.
\end{quote}

Constructively, alternating signs or composing genuinely different complete
relations could still shrink a supplied relation-chain grammar. What remains
missing is an unconditional uniform arbitrary-instance compiler with closed
ordinary-SAT output and $N^\lambda$ physical size. Separationally, the failure
of this one regulator is an architectural falsifier only. It is not a lower
bound against other SAT algorithms, representations, RAM programs, or
multitape machines.

\section{Separate machine-resource ledgers}
The reproducer is a RAM-level finite audit. A fixed-$k$ read-only-input
multitape implementation can validate and print $r$ supplied splices in time
polynomial in the physically written formulas, charging every output bit and
work-tape cell. A two-fully-writable-tape implementation must separately
charge changes on both tapes and auxiliary footprint; no D2-only theorem is
imported. A single-tape implementation counts the writable input footprint,
head travel, and crossings. The conservative simulation
\[
T_1=O\!\left(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))\right),\qquad
P_1=O\!\left(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)\right)
\]
is used only as an upper construction. A fixed universal machine also pays
its fixed interpreter and program-prefix overhead. No tape lower bound, RAM
exponent, or FLMV forbidden-region conclusion is transferred from the
relation theorem.

The one-splice triangular theorem belongs to LSPV-2 and is adopted, not
reclaimed. Report191 already proved idempotence of a different compiler:
fixed-boundary 2CNF/affine projection. The present theorem is new only in its
exact repeated physical occurrence-splice architecture. Both results expose
