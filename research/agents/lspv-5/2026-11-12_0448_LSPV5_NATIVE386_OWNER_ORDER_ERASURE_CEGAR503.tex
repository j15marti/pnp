\section{Counterexample: order erasure creates a spurious zero-cost edge}
Let \(\alpha_{\mathrm{set}}\) copy every packet field byte-for-byte after JSON parsing, except that \texttt{gate\_defs} is sorted by owner ID.  Then for every one of the 120 permutations \(\pi\),
\[
 \operatorname{SHA256}(\alpha_{\mathrm{set}}(P_\pi))
 =\texttt{8830fe8553ec76d906890f6d7098c12e38492fa67bbaa5597e087b6e5cc51924}.
\]
This common representative is exactly the accepted 01234 packet.  In particular,
\[
\alpha_{\mathrm{set}}(P_{01234})=\alpha_{\mathrm{set}}(P_{40123}),
\]
but the checker accepts the former and rejects the latter at owner 4 because producers 2 and 3 have not been declared.

If the coarse abstract transition system treats canonicalization as a free transition, it contains
\[
P_{40123}\xrightarrow{\;0\;}\alpha_{\mathrm{set}}(P_{40123})=P_{01234},
\]
yet the concrete grammar contains no owner-sorting transition.  Concrete replay therefore classifies this path as \st{SPURIOUS}.  The accepted 01234 proof remains genuine and must not be excluded.  This is the required CEGAR polarity: refine only the rejected preimage path, not the valid shortcut or its W33 cost.

This result is a transition-discriminability failure, not a violation of the inequality ``abstract cost no greater than concrete cost.''  A MAY abstraction may deliberately include the extra edge.  It then cannot support a positive lower bound above the new cheaper path until the edge is either concretely replayed or refined away.

\section{Repaired restricted lemma}
At owner-declaration prefix \(i\), retain
\[
S_i=(D_i,V_i,H_i),
\]
where \(D_i\) is the set of declared owner IDs, \(V_i\) is the set of their available output wires together with original wires, and \(H_i\) is the once-paid owner set.  A declaration \((o,z,a,b)\) is legal only if every owner-produced input among \(|a|,|b|\) has its producer in \(D_i\), \(o,z\) are fresh under the native checks, and the transition adds exactly one unit when \(o\notin H_i\).  Genuine DEF facets remain tied to the recorded owner operands and add no second owner charge.

\textbf{Lemma 503.1 (restricted).} For the five owners above, iterating this prefix rule accepts exactly the six linear extensions of the owner dependency DAG, rejects the other 114 at their first missing producer, and reproduces the native declaration verdict.  For 40123 the first step requests owner 4 while producers 2 and 3 are absent.  The refinement separates the collision immediately.

This lemma proves neither completed-refutation preservation nor cost soundness for Resolution, AND3, GF(2), LOCAL-CONVERT, branching, weakening, contexts, discharge, joins, deletions, exported/private wires, or any other unrepresented historical transition.  A full P2-C3 theorem still requires source-authenticated concrete-step inclusion, completed-refutation preservation, and \(W_{\mathrm{abs}}\le W_{\mathrm{concrete}}\) for every actual accepted rule.
