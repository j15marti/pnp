\section{Target claim and predeclared falsifiers}
The single target claim was that every SOURCE coordinate accepted by the
historical native front end has one authenticated byte spelling, so source
identity may reason directly about the raw coordinate lexeme.
The predeclared falsifiers were: (F1) a dead deletion or padding changes a
wrapper self-test; (F2) a normalizer merges coordinates or chronology; (F3)
a pruned packet is accepted only by an in-memory core rather than the CLI;
(F4) a genuine accepted path is discarded as abstractly spurious.  The
experiment found a sharper F2 witness: distinct raw numeric lexemes decode
to the same SOURCE coordinate and are accepted by the full CLI.  F3 is
excluded by operating-system replay.  F4 remains mandatory CEGAR discipline.

\paragraph{Restricted repair (PROVED/EXACT for this interface).}
Let $P$ be the exact parser and $V$ the checker.  A parsed-object abstraction
may quotient byte strings $b_1,b_2$ after proving $P(b_1)=P(b_2)$, while
charging physical parse/input costs separately.  A theorem about accepted
bytes may instead require one canonical serializer $C$ and accept only
$b=C(P(b))$.  The archived CLI implements the first semantic quotient, not
the second raw-byte language.  Raw SOURCE-coordinate bytes therefore are not
an injective source identity.  No minimum-cost conclusion follows.

\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
RFC 8259 Section 6 permits optional fraction and exponent parts and forbids
leading zeros in the integer component:
\url{https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8259.html}.  ECMAScript
\texttt{JSON.parse} produces Number values and may not extend the JSON
grammar:
\url{https://tc39.es/ecma262/multipage/structured-data.html}.  These are
\textbf{DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE} parser facts.

Cousot--Cousot (POPL 1977, DOI
\texttt{10.1145/512950.512973}) supports explicit concrete and abstract
domains, but does not prove checker coverage or a complexity lower bound.
Clarke et al. (CAV 2000, DOI
\texttt{10.1007/10722167\_15}) requires concrete counterexample replay
before refinement.  These are \textbf{METHODOLOGICAL TRANSFERS}.  Treating a
parsed-object quotient as preserving raw provenance or physical memory is a
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}.
