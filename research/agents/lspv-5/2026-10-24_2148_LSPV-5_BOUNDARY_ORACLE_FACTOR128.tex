\section{Claim card, target and three falsifiable mechanisms}
\textbf{Target.}  Audit whether the exponentially many pinned decisions in
ROOT-HARD-PROJECTION129 become independent when the threshold support is
replaced by more complicated boundary geometry.

\textbf{Exact prefix.}  For every proper 3CNF $F$, every $d\ge1$, and every
explicit Boolean circuit $C:\{0,1\}^d\to\{0,1\}$ of size $g$ with nonempty
support, we construct one proper 3CNF $H(F,C)$.  For every boundary word $b$
we prove an exact projection identity.  For every deterministic adaptive
controller making any finite sequence of projection queries, we prove a
same-transcript simulator with at most one query to the bit $z=\SAT(F)$.
Circuit evaluation, source acquisition, caching and all state are charged;
$z$ is not supplied for free.

\textbf{Model and SAME\_REALIZATION.}  The construction is an ordinary
fixed-width proper-3CNF encoding.  The projection service and simulator use
the same explicit circuit $C$ and the same source $F$.  This is not the FLMV
random-access bit-TM successor: it is a restricted service theorem and a
red-team control on candidate ARC progress measures.

Three mechanisms were preregistered conceptually before the audit:
\begin{description}
\item[(M1)] arbitrary succinct support geometry might make pinned answers
independent; \textbf{falsified} by the factor theorem below;
\item[(M2)] root hardness might remove the earlier easy-root bypass and thereby
force projection compilation; \textbf{falsified}: the root is source-hard, but
all pinned labels still share one bit;
\item[(M3)] lazy pinning might turn one supported boundary point into a strict
power-shrunk ordinary child; \textbf{falsified in the direct-copy calculus} by
LAZY-PIN-SOURCE93 and independently reflected here by exponent-one child size.
\end{description}

\section{Construction and exact semantics}
Let $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$
be proper.  Translate $C$ into a functional proper-3CNF Tseitin relation
$T_C(b,y,c)$, where $c$ is the output and each internal gate has a unique
extension once its inputs are fixed.  Standard AND/OR/NOT encodings use
$O(1)$ unit, binary or ternary clauses per gate; every unit or binary clause
is converted to proper ternaries with fresh split variables.  Thus
$T_C$ has $O(g)$ variables and clauses and
\[
 \exists y\;T_C(b,y,c)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c=C(b).
 \tag{1}
\]
Force $c=1$ using the four proper ternaries
$(c\vee u^{\epsilon}\vee v^{\delta})$ for
$\epsilon,\delta\in\{0,1\}$.  For every source clause add a fresh $a_i$ and
the two clauses
\[
 (\neg c\vee\ell_{i1}\vee a_i),\qquad
 (\neg a_i\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}).
 \tag{2}
\]
Call the conjunction $H(F,C)$.  Every clause is proper.  The source gadgets
all meet at $c$, so the clause-variable incidence graph of the nontrivial
formula is connected even when $F$ has independent components.

\begin{theorem}[Root-hard succinct-support projection; PROVED/EXACT]
For every $F,C$ above and every $b\in\{0,1\}^d$,
\[
 \boxed{\SAT(H(F,C)[b])=C(b)\wedge\SAT(F).}
 \tag{3}
\]
If $C^{-1}(1)\ne\varnothing$, then
\[
 \boxed{\SAT(H(F,C))=\SAT(F).}
 \tag{4}
\]
The construction has $n+d+O(g)+m$ variables and $2m+O(g)$ proper ternary
clauses.  For $g=O(n+m)$ its encoded length is within a polylogarithmic
factor of the source length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $b$.  By functionality of $T_C$, the forced literal $c=1$ is compatible
with the circuit exactly when $C(b)=1$.  When $c=1$, eliminating $a_i$ from
(2) gives precisely
$\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}$: if $\ell_{i1}$ is false then the
first clause forces $a_i=1$ and the second requires
$\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}$; if $\ell_{i1}$ is true, choose $a_i=0$.
Independence of the $a_i$ proves (3).  Existentially choosing a supported
$b$ proves (4), and the counts follow directly.
\end{proof}

Threshold supports recover ROOT-HARD-PROJECTION129 up to a different
functional connector implementation.  The present statement also permits
succinct singleton, co-singleton, subcube, parity-hyperplane, disconnected
two-corner and union-of-subcubes supports.  It does \emph{not} assert that
every arbitrary subset has a small circuit: $g$ is explicit and charged.

\section{Adaptive oracle factorization}
Write $z=\SAT(F)$ and $Q_z(b)=\SAT(H(F,C)[b])$.
Equation (3) says $Q_z(b)=C(b)\wedge z$.

\begin{theorem}[One-source-bit transcript factor; PROVED/EXACT]
Let $A$ be any deterministic adaptive controller that chooses its next
boundary query from its complete current state and the preceding answers.
For every finite execution of $A$ against $Q_z$, there is an exact simulator
that evaluates $C$ on every requested word and queries $z$ at most once.
It returns the identical answer transcript and hence drives $A$ through the
identical states.  The statement holds pointwise for each fixed random tape
of a randomized controller.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On a query $b$, if $C(b)=0$ return $0$ without reading $z$.  At the first
query with $C(b)=1$, read $z$, return it and cache it.  Thereafter return the
cached bit on supported queries and $0$ elsewhere.  Induction on the query
number proves equality of transcripts and controller states.  At most one
read of $z$ occurs.
\end{proof}

This is an information factorization, not a fast SAT algorithm.  Obtaining
$z$ can be exactly the original hard problem.  Its use is negative and
precise: $2^d$ individually source-complete pinned formulas do not certify
$2^d$ independent source decisions.  After a paid source decision, the
whole boundary relation is represented by $C$ plus one cached bit.  On an
UNSAT source it is the constant-false function; on a SAT source it is $C$.

\section{Transport and child-scale ledger}
Choose any public supported word $b^*$.  The map
$F\mapsto H(F,C)[b^*]$ is a one-call proper-3CNF reduction, but it retains
all $m$ source constraints.  Whether boundary constants are substituted or
encoded by proper pin gadgets, the ordinary child length is
\[
 N_{\rm child}=N^{1+o(1)}+O(g\log N),
 \qquad\lambda=1
 \tag{5}
\]
for linear-size $C$.  Consequently a source solver obtained from a target
solver obeys only
\[
 L_{\rm old}(N)\le R(N)+H(N)+\tau(N)
 T_{\rm new}^{\le}(N^{1+o(1)}),
 \tag{6}
\]
and no strict exponent contraction follows.  Materializing the target also
costs source order; lazy indexed simulation avoids the buffer but does not
change the target's ordinary encoded length.  This independently accepts
LAZY-PIN-SOURCE93's distinction between a short source-backed handle and a
short ordinary child.

Padding a valid closed child as in PAD-FLOOR-SCOPED87 is semantically sound
inside that restricted stream calculus, but padding raises a lower length
floor; it cannot turn the exponent-one continuation (5) into a square-root
child.  TREE-AFFINE-JOIN122 is likewise accepted only as a polynomial
algorithm for its certified affine/tree language.  Its growing rank control
reinforces that boundary rank is not necessary unrestricted work.

\section{Hostile controls and reproducible discrimination}
The auditor exhausts all 255 nonempty subsets of the eight signed clauses on
three variables, then tests affine/XOR SAT and UNSAT, Horn, dual-Horn,
padded 2SAT SAT and UNSAT, independent components and 48 seeded random
sources.  Six support circuits cover cardinality, Hamming connectivity and
ANF-degree extremes.  For each construction it checks properness, the root
label, every boundary word and five adaptive policies on both available
labels.

\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL:} seed 128; 310 bases (307 SAT, three UNSAT); 1,860
constructions; 17,520 complete boundary checks; 9,300 adaptive transcripts
containing 203,100 queries; 54,024 properness checks; 301,304
assertion-accounted checks total; zero failures.  Every transcript simulator
used at most one source-label call.  Script SHA-256 is
\path{6b0485e369444d53ee66e27257e07f1c2952a3faa0d078993be481d5c18d15ca}.
Finite checks validate the implementation only; the asymptotic statements
are supplied by the proofs above.

Source-readable witnesses, affine elimination, SCC, direct recognition and
decide-first normalization remain legal bypasses.  Constant-false
representation handles every UNSAT boundary relation.  A SAT decider need
not reconstruct witnesses, a DNNF or all contexts.  Dead gates, copied gates,
padding and alternate circuit forms change $g$ and evaluation cost but not
the factor identity.  The theorem relativizes and algebrizes: replacing the
source decision by a semantic or algebraic oracle only makes the single-bit
factorization easier.  It is therefore a barrier/control, not a route around
known relativization barriers.  VLSI, proof-system and post-quantum binding
analogies supply no same-realization transfer here.
