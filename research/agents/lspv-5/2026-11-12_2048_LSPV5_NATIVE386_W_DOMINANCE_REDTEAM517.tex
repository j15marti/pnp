\section{Results}
\begin{proposition}[PROVED/EXACT, restricted to the pinned spec and checker]
Let $\Sigma$ be the guarded K4 source ($h=4$, charge $(1,0,0,0)$, guard $7$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item W29 and W38 have identical \texttt{spec}.
W29's single paid gate $8=1\wedge4$ has zero DEF views, and variable 8 occurs in no
record.
W38's gates $9=8\wedge8$ and $10=9\wedge9$ are idempotent copies, $z\leftrightarrow a$.
\item Deleting W29's dead gate gives a 45-record packet with 28 RES and paid 28.
The unchanged \texttt{checkProof} body (hosted evaluation, \texttt{control=true}) accepts
it and confirms 0 odd models.
The unchanged CLI rejects it only with \texttt{accepted a corrupted proof}.
That rejection is the \texttt{gate\_defs.shift()} sentinel already identified by
LSPV-1/590, not a logical rejection.
\item Over all $7!=5040$ Davis--Putnam elimination orders, the needed-ancestor cone of
$\bot$ has a RES count in $\{28,29,31,32,33,35,39,43,47\}$ with multiplicities
$144,144,144,576,576,720,720,720,1296$.
The minimum is 28, at order $(1,2,4,3,5,6,7)$.
After renumbering, the cone is byte-identical to the dead-gate-deleted W29
(SHA-256 \texttt{d39d73ab\ldots083e0e45}).
\item The unchanged native CLI \emph{accepts} two inflated packets.
The first is literal tree unrolling of W38's index-23 shared RES: the record is duplicated
and its second user (record 31) is rerouted, giving paid 39 (36 RES + 3 gates).
The second is W29 with a dead duplicate RES inserted before $\bot$, giving paid 30.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently, on $\Sigma$, under the native CLI's charge
$\mathrm{cost}=|\mathrm{RES}|+|\text{gate owners}|$:
\[
\min_{\pi\ \text{core-accepted}}\mathrm{cost}(\pi)\ \le 28
\quad\text{and}\quad
\min_{\pi\ \text{CLI-accepted}}\mathrm{cost}(\pi)\ \le 29 < 30 < 38 < 39 .
\]
The checker certifies no minimality, and it charges dead and duplicated work.
\end{proposition}

\section{Bridge red team (literature-checked)}
Two points bear on the bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM.} Haken (1985) and Urquhart (1987) prove that
$\overline W_{\mathrm{Res}}(N)=2^{\Omega(N)}$ unconditionally.
For $R=$ Resolution, the bridge is therefore equivalent to $\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$
itself. The bridge is ``weak'' only if $R$ is strong enough that its p-boundedness is
plausible under $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$. This was already recorded in the corpus
(the Haken entry of \path{research/coordination/2026-11-02_1204_WORST_CASE_MINIMUM_DERIVATION_FIVE_ROLE_REFOCUS.md},
together with that file's Urquhart and fixed-Rmix341 escape entries) and is re-adopted here.
\item \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM + OPEN.} $\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow
\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$, which by Cook--Reckhow gives \emph{some} p-bounded system.
For a \emph{fixed} $R$ such as Extended Frege, ``$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\Rightarrow R$
p-bounded'' is open and is tied to p-optimality.
Pich--Santhanam (arXiv:2405.02232) obtain ``EF not p-bounded $\Rightarrow\mathrm P\ne
\mathrm{NP}$'' only under additional hypotheses. That is the nearest literature instance
of the bridge, and it is \textbf{CONDITIONAL}.
Atserias--M\"uller (J.~ACM 2020) shows that approximating the minimum Resolution
refutation is NP-hard. This supports the directive's choice not to charge for
strategy search, but it does not give the bridge.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Post-quantum ledger.} We considered item 4 (collision and lossiness). The
proof-to-cost map is many-to-one, with inflated packets giving collisions at higher cost.
FAILED TRANSFER: collisions of $\pi\mapsto\mathrm{cost}$ carry no lower-bound information
about the minimum. \textbf{Barrier transfer.} Dead-code elimination and common-subexpression
sharing from compiler and circuit design are an EXACT OBJECT TRANSFER for normalizing
accepted packets: cone pruning gives a minimum-preserving normal form. They give no lower bound.
