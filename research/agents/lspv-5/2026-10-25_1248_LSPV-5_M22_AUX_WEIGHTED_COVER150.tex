\section{Scope and recovered hypotheses}
The canonical class $\CM$ consists of unique proper three-distinct-variable CNFs, every clause entirely positive or entirely negative, with every active variable occurring exactly twice in each sign.  The target $\CS$ removes only the fully complementary-paired formulas.  D\"ocker proves NP-completeness of the source class and satisfiability of its fully paired special case \cite{docker}; this is class complexity, not a time lower bound.

AUX-PORT-CONGRUENCE148 proved that a standalone pure-sign completion block with three source ports and private auxiliaries can meet signed $(2,2)$ quotas only when the number $a$ of auxiliaries is divisible by three and all three pre-used source occurrences have the same sign.  Its $a=3$ search and the subsequent AUX6-ORBIT149 $a=6$ MILP found no equality projection.  The latter model's continuous relaxation was already infeasible.  This run asks whether that numerical obstruction has a short exact proof.

Three falsifiable proposals were tested.  (H1) the MILP failure is merely integral or numerical: \textbf{false}.  A rational weighted-cover inequality proves impossibility.  (H2) the constant port words are the difficult part: \textbf{false}.  The proof never assumes either constant extends.  (H3) the obstruction yields compulsory SAT work: \textbf{false}.  It is a static finite gadget-design theorem, not a complete-state lower bound.

\section{Notation}
Assume each port has one pre-used positive occurrence; global sign complementation handles the negative orientation.  A completing gadget must give every port gadget-degree $(1^+,2^-)$ and every auxiliary degree $(2^+,2^-)$.  Let $P_t$ and $N_t$ be the numbers of selected positive and negative clauses containing exactly $t\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ ports.  Properness means the other $3-t$ names are distinct auxiliaries.  The signed incidence identities are
\begin{align}
 \sum_t tP_t&=3,& \sum_t(3-t)P_t&=2a,\\
 \sum_t tN_t&=6,& \sum_t(3-t)N_t&=2a.\label{eq:degrees}
\end{align}
No uniqueness assumption is needed below.

For a full assignment with port Hamming weight one and auxiliary Hamming weight $h$, a fixed positive $t$-port clause is false on
\[
 c^+_{a,h}(t)=\binom{3-t}{1}\binom{a-3+t}{h}
\]
assignments of that orbit.  A fixed negative clause is false on
\[
 c^-_{a,h}(t)=\binom{3-t}{1-t}\binom{a-3+t}{h-(3-t)},
\]
where out-of-range binomial coefficients are zero.

\begin{theorem}[Exact weighted-cover obstruction]
For $a\in\{3,6\}$, every proper pure-sign ternary formula satisfying the degrees in \eqref{eq:degrees} has a satisfying full assignment whose three-port word is nonconstant.  Consequently its existential port projection is not exactly $\{000,111\}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose every full assignment with a nonconstant port word falsifies at least one selected clause.

For $a=3$, assign weight one to every assignment with port weight one and auxiliary weight one, and weight two when the auxiliary weight is two.  The total assignment weight requiring coverage is
\[
 3\binom31+2\cdot3\binom32=27.
\]
The weighted falsification capacities of a positive clause for $t=0,1,2,3$ are $(0,2,4,0)$; for a negative clause they are $(0,2,0,0)$.  Coordinatewise these are bounded by $(0,2,4,6)=2t$ and $(3,2,1,0)=3-t$.  Therefore all clauses together have weighted capacity at most
\[
 2\sum_t tP_t+\sum_t(3-t)N_t=2\cdot3+6=12,
\]
contradicting the required coverage 27.

For $a=6$, assign weights one and three at auxiliary weights three and four.  Required coverage is
\[
 3\binom63+3\cdot3\binom64=60+135=195.
\]
The positive clause capacities are $(3,14,25,0)$ and the negative capacities are $(30,22,0,0)$.  They are bounded coordinatewise by
\[
 (3,14,25,36)=12t+(3-t),\qquad
 (33,22,11,0)=11(3-t).
\]
Using \eqref{eq:degrees}, total clause capacity is at most
\[
 12\sum_t tP_t+sum_t(3-t)P_t+11\sum_t(3-t)N_t
 =12\cdot3+12+11\cdot12=180,
\]
contradicting 195.  Hence some weighted assignment is uncovered by every falsifying set, meaning it satisfies every clause and has a nonconstant port word.
\end{proof}

The proof is an explicit symmetry-averaged Farkas certificate.  The strict deficit is 15 in both cases.  It strengthens the preceding computations: uniqueness, integrality search, and witnesses for $000$ or $111$ are unnecessary.  It does not address $a\ge9$, cross-variable clauses, shared auxiliaries, contextual ports, repeated literals, mixed-sign clauses, or another semantic reduction.

\section{Contraction and bit-resource ledger}
The rejected mechanism is a fixed adapter template, not a SAT decider.  Had it existed for $g$ source groups, it would still emit a constant number of fresh names and ordinary clauses per group.  With dense index width $\ell=\Theta(\log(g+2))$, parsing/ranking reads, group and sign selection, address arithmetic, literal/header writes including unchanged values, and consumer reads total $\Theta(g\ell)$ bit traffic.  A streaming implementation can keep only logarithmic local control beyond the source accessor; materialization retains $\Theta(g\ell)$ writable bits.  Actual temporal flips, cumulative bit-time, program size and physical gates remain separate metrics.

Thus even a successful fixed block would be linear normalization, not an input-power contraction.  The present blocks fail before a first typed adapter level, so two recursive ARC levels cannot be formed.  LOCAL-COOK-TAGGED-SIGNED22-1 still requires an all-input semantic compiler with paid source tags, occurrence ranks, inverse incidence, malformed-input rejection, output traffic, peak state and same-machine access.
