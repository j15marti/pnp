REDTEAM32 explicit counterexample. For n>=2, over 2^(n+1) states, increment from 0 through 2^n-1, then jump to state 2^(n+1)-1 and halt/loop. Final endpoint is a hardwired constant (zero gates). Whole indexed trajectory for time 0..2^n has exact multioutput circuit size n: low output bit j is time_j OR time_n, top bit time_n. Each of n low functions is a different non-primary function, requiring at least n distinct gates; n OR gates attain the bound. Explicit fast-forward remains poly(n). The oracle pointer-chase t-query bound cannot transfer to short explicit transitions.
