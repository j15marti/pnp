## Theorem: EXPTABLEAU-NOCOMPACT25
Fix any language L in EXP minus P (unconditionally exists by deterministic time hierarchy). Fix its total deterministic 2^{poly(n)}-time decider M. For input x, construct the standard succinct 3-CNF F_x of M's entire accepting computation, with exponentially many variables/clauses. There is a polynomial-time local generator G(x,i) that returns the i-th clause and binary addresses of its variables. Functional transition and Tseitin equivalence constraints can force one canonical global tableau assignment on an accepting run. F_x is satisfiable iff x in L.

Assume P=NP. Then for EVERY fixed integer k>=1, infinitely many x in L have no circuit W_x of size <= |x|^k that maps all encoded tableau-variable addresses to a common satisfying assignment. Proof: Suppose for one fixed k only finitely many accepted x lack such a circuit. The language L is then in Sigma2^P: existentially guess W (polynomial-size code), universally quantify the polynomial-bit clause index i, compute G(x,i) and each of the three W-addressed literal values, and verify the clause in polynomial time. Soundness on NO inputs follows from F_x unsatisfiability. Hardwire finitely many exceptional YES inputs. Under P=NP, Sigma2^P=P, whence L in P, contradicting the unconditional deterministic time hierarchy. QED.

This is a theorem of the form (P=NP) => (some succinct YES tableaux do not possess any n^k shared witness circuit, for every fixed k). It does NOT prove P!=NP, DOES NOT prove an unconditional circuit lower bound, and DOES NOT show noncompactness of the particular project diagonal family. The latter needs an independent source-specific lemma. The unique full assignment, one endpoint, original planted witness, per-index satisfiability witnesses, and one shared W are logically different objects.

## Finite hostile controls (executable checker and raw JSON)
A deterministic 3-bit increment machine was encoded for T=8 time indices, 7 transitions, into 94 CNF clauses of width <=3 over 31 tableau and auxiliary variables. For each of 8 initial states the canonical valuation satisfies every clause and unit propagation forces every variable, certifying a unique solution. On the initial-zero tableau, the indexed 32-bit assignment word is 0x08fac688. Enumerating all 14,392 <=2-gate AND/OR/NOT descriptions on 5 address bits, with constants 0 and 1 free, yields 172 distinct truth tables; none equals that word. This is only a bounded small-s witness failure. The structured increment itself is efficiently fast-forwardable; black-box pointer chasing cannot be imported.

For each of 256 CNFs formed from subsets of 8 three-variable clauses, A_x tests equality with a hardcoded x, instantly returns the correct stored SAT(x) if equal, and otherwise falls back to exhaustive SAT. All 256 x 256 = 65,536 machine/input pairs were verified correct. Pointwise optimality with a different A_x for each x supplies no uniform fastest polynomial SAT decider and can require n bits of x-dependent program description.

Audit totals: 1,081 tableau assertions, 65,536 specialized-decider checks, zero failures. The asymptotic theorem is mathematical; the numerical experiments do not prove EXP!=P, which follows separately from classical hierarchy, nor decide P vs NP.
