# MAGRED-SEEDMASS15 | 2026-10-18 20:40 KST

Status: PROVED/EXACT restricted theorem, independent review pending; finite controls computational. P versus NP open.

Scope: A source-independent public seed S with arbitrary probabilities p_s selects hashes h_s from M=2^N inputs to q=2^b outputs. For every distinct x,y assume P[h_S(x)=h_S(y)]<=1/q. Let L_s be the largest output bucket of h_s.

THEOREM: p_s<=1/L_s<=q/M, so H_inf(S)>=N-b. Proof: choose L=L_s inputs colliding under s. For any other seed t the fraction of distinct pairs inside that bucket colliding under t is at least a=(L-q)/(q(L-1)), by sum of squared bucket occupancies. Consequently 1/q>=p_s+(1-p_s)a. Rearranging yields p_s<=1/L. With fixed r-bit public seed identifiers, r+b>=N. The latter is Stinson's KNOWN key-length lower bound and sharp for finite-field affine hashing.

VARIABLE LENGTH: For any injective binary seed code c, without requiring prefix-freeness, Pr[|c(S)|+b<=N-Delta]<2^(1-Delta) for Delta>=1 (or zero if no code can fit). Count at most 2^(N-b-Delta+1)-1 possible short binary codewords, each seed weighing at most 2^(b-N). Hence output length N^lambda, lambda<1, has exponentially small probability if seed and b-bit digest are literally serialized.

HOSTILE EXCEPTION: Nonprefix codes need not have expected seed+digest length >=N. At N=3,b=1, four equiprobable seed IDs "",0,1,00 average total length 2<N=3, while worst-case is N. For prefix-free codes expected total length>=N. If collision tolerance relaxes to 1/q+delta, the bucket proof adds delta*q*(L-1)/((q-1)L), allowing short-seed almost-universal families.

SHARPNESS: For N=b(m+1) and q=2^b, write x=(a,v) in GF(q)xGF(q)^m, choose uniform s in GF(q)^m, and output a+<s,v>. Collision is 0 or 1/q, and r=N-b exactly.

Model: Requires input-independent exact universality plus recoverable serialized seed identity; does not apply to external coins, source-adaptive selection, short-seed almost-universal hashes or unrestricted nonlinear quotients. No CHMY output entropy or tape-time conclusion. Stage-uniform K and valid source YES guard remain open. P versus NP open.

Literature: Stinson as quoted in https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/files/749/uhf.pdf ; CHMY https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2021.23 ; Naor-Naor https://doi.org/10.1137/0222053 .
