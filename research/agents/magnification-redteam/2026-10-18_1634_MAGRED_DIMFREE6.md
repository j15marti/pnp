# MAGRED-DIMFREE6

2026-10-18. PROVED/EXACT restricted lemma, pending independent review. P versus NP OPEN.

DIMENSION-FREE-PARTIAL-INTERPOLATION6: Any m distinct consistently labeled Boolean input points, regardless of input dimension n, have a fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit agreeing with every label and using at most 4(m-1)+O(1) gates. Proof: recursively split on a variable distinguishing two opposite-label examples. Every split has two nonempty children, hence at most m-1 internal nodes. Each node is one 4-gate Boolean multiplexer. Repeated addresses with the same labels deduplicate. Opposite labels on one address are inconsistent, a separate syntactic NO case.

Thus an explicit partial table of m<=L records at circuit threshold t cannot be a NO when t>=4m+O(1), independent of address width. With L_j<=N^{lambda^j+o(1)} and t_j>=N^{beta-o(1)}, choose fixed j with lambda^j<beta: every consistent possible output is YES. This strengthens ADAPTIVE-PARTIAL-CASCADE-FLOOR1 by removing its n_j=N^{o(1)} premise. It does not cover implicit exponentially many constraints, non-coordinate hashes, circuit-description minimization, changing t_j, or nonstandard input-access models.

COMPUTATIONAL: 6,561 exhaustive 3-bit partial tables and 4,681 duplicated-record lists gave zero discrepancies; n=1024,m=2 needed one split. Verified raw: results/raw/meta/2026-10-18_MAGRED_DIMFREE6.json. Other construction and model-preservation obligations remain OPEN; no P versus NP proof.
