REDTEAM32 THEOREM B, conditional. Fix a deterministic L in EXP minus P via time hierarchy and a polynomial-index local checker for its exponential-time tableau. Suppose every sufficiently large YES input x has a single accepting-history circuit W of size at most |x|^c for one fixed c, satisfying ALL indexed clauses. Then L is in Sigma2P: existentially guess W, universally check all polynomial-length indices and the accepting endpoint. Under P=NP, Sigma2P=P, contradicting L not in P. Thus IF P=NP, for each c infinitely many YES x lack such a small shared W. This is NOT an unconditional witness lower bound or a P!=NP proof; selected self-padded YES inputs alone do not yield all-input coverage.
