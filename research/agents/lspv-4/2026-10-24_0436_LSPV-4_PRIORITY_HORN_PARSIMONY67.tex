\section{The unique first-true Horn lift}
Let $\Phi=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$ be proper 3-CNF on $n_0$ source variables. Source clause literals have distinct identifiers. Introduce fresh selectors $s_{j1},s_{j2},s_{j3}$, and for each clause put
\[
\begin{split}
P_j={}&(s_{j1}\vee s_{j2}\vee s_{j3})\\
&\wedge(\neg s_{j1}\vee\ell_{j1})
\wedge(\neg s_{j2}\vee\ell_{j2})
\wedge(\neg s_{j3}\vee\ell_{j3})\\
&\wedge(\neg s_{j2}\vee\neg\ell_{j1})
\wedge(\neg s_{j3}\vee\neg\ell_{j1})
\wedge(\neg s_{j3}\vee\neg\ell_{j2}).
\end{split} \tag{1}
\]
Every binary clause is Horn irrespective of the original literal sign. The unique positive ternary of each group contains only its three fresh selectors, and these ternaries are disjoint.
\begin{theorem}[Exact parsimonious witness transport, PROVED/EXACT]
For every fixed assignment $\alpha$ to the source variables, $H_{\rm pri}(\Phi)=\bigwedge_jP_j$ has exactly one extension of $\alpha$ if $\alpha\models\Phi$, and none otherwise. Consequently,
\[
\#SAT(H_{\rm pri}(\Phi))=\#SAT(\Phi).
\]
The output has $n=n_0+3m$, $m_2=6m$, $k=m$ genuine ternaries, and $M=7m$ total clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The selector ternary requires at least one active selector. An active $s_{ja}$ forces its associated source literal true. Selectors in positions two and three additionally force all preceding literals false. Hence the only possible active selector is the first true literal. That selector suffices. Selector triples are pairwise disjoint, giving unique simultaneous extension. This is a source-local construction, not a decide-first encoder.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Degree trade-off.} The literal in source position one has three target occurrences, position two has two and position three has one; two, one, and zero of those are complemented. The selectors in positions one, two, and three have respectively two, three, and four occurrences with both signs. Hence the decoded source degree-four language remains NP-complete by the source-preserving inverse parser, but physical target occurrence degrees need not remain four. The earlier nonparsimonious Horn selector preserved the source degree more directly.
\section{Sharp minimality in an explicit restricted calculus}
Retain the three implications and positive selector ternary above. The only allowed extra guards are binary Horn clauses $(\neg s_i\vee\neg\ell_j)$ for $i\ne j$ (six available directed guards). Objective: have zero selector extensions for truth triple $(0,0,0)$ and exactly one for each of the other seven source truth triples.
\begin{theorem}[Six optimal priority orders, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
Exactly three guards are necessary and sufficient. Every successful guard set orients each of the three source-literal pairs exactly once and forms an acyclic tournament. Exactly $3!=6$ guard sets succeed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a source valuation with exactly positions $i,j$ true, precisely one of selectors $s_i,s_j$ may remain available. At least one directed guard on this pair is required, while two opposite guards would reject both choices. Every pair therefore needs exactly one direction, yielding exactly three guards. With all source literals true, a selected vertex must have zero incoming blockers. A three-vertex tournament has exactly one such vertex precisely if it is transitive. A transitive tournament is a total order; its earliest true literal is the unique selector for every truth pattern. There are six total orders.
\end{proof}
This is \emph{not} a claim of absolute minimum binary clauses across all Horn encodings, variables, representations or algorithmic models.
\section{One-to-one proper-3CNF baseline transport}
For the prescribed binary implication-chain $F_b$, $q\ge1$ and $b\in\{0,1\}^q$, the endpoint pairs force $z_0=1,z_q=0$. All $b_i=1$ propagates $z_q=1$ and is UNSAT. A first zero permits a transition to $z=0$ and SAT. Thus $F_b$ is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$, with $n_F=q+3$ and $m_F=q+4$. The all-one member has deterministic orientation-query depth exactly $q$.
Instead of free literal padding, introduce globally fresh $(t,a,c)$ and seven proper ternary clauses, one forbidding each of the seven assignments other than $(0,0,0)$. The resulting anchor forces uniquely $(t,a,c)=(0,0,0)$. Replace each binary clause $(u\vee v)$ by $(u\vee v\vee t)$. Denote the proper 3-CNF output by $G_b$. Every source satisfying assignment extends \emph{uniquely}, so
\[
n_G=q+6,\quad m_G=q+11,\quad \#SAT(G_b)=\#SAT(F_b).
\]
Now put $J_b=H_{\rm pri}(G_b)$. Its counts are
\[
n_J=4q+39,\quad m_{2,J}=6q+66,\quad k_J=q+11,\quad
M_J=7q+77,
\qquad \#SAT(J_b)=\#SAT(F_b).
\]
Flipping original orientation $b_i$ changes only the signs of the consequent and its one priority blocker in that lifted group, in two input words disjoint from other orientations. Thus every deterministic exact decider for the full canonically encoded priority-Horn language, when given $J_{1^q}$, must read at least one affected word for each $i$ or its read transcript would be the same after an opposite-label single-flip. The lower bound is $q$ addressed-word reads on that UNSAT input and is matched by a linear-time canonical source recognizer. There is no claim of the same per-input bound on all SAT members.
