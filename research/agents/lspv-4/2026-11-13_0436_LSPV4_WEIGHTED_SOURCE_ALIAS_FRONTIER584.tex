\[

 L(\lambda)=\min_{\pi}(Q(\pi)+\lambda S(\pi))

\]

\sum_{d>0}H(d)=167,\qquad \sum_{d>0}dH(d)=9{,}462.
\]
The minimum-$Q$ corner is $(Q,S)=(29{,}238,823)$; the minimum-$S$ corner is $(Q,S)=(38{,}700,656)$. At $\lambda=1$,
\[
L(1)=30{,}061 > 29{,}238+656=29{,}894.
\]
There are exactly 49 distinct positive kinks of $L$, with one-sided slopes decreasing from 823 to 656. All choices at the kinks are legitimate tied aliases, not forbidden by this optimization.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\emph{Proof.}
For each triple of distinct variables, direct insertion sort on three items takes exactly two or three key comparisons. At each clause $c$, one can independently choose either best order with two comparisons or best with three. With $\delta_c>0$, the latter order saves exactly $\delta_c$ probes but requires one more comparison. Therefore
\[
\min_{\pi_c}(q_c(\pi_c)+\lambda s_c(\pi_c))
=\min(q_{c,2}+2\lambda,q_{c,3}+3\lambda)
=\min(q_{c,2},q_{c,3})+2\lambda+\min(\lambda,\delta_c).
\]
When $\delta_c=0$, the same equality holds, including cases $q_{c,2}<q_{c,3}$. Exact clause-truth preservation under permutation leaves the proof nodes and existing witnesses unchanged. The total minimization distributes over the Cartesian product of all 328 independent legal orders, so summing the clause identity proves the formula. The independent, executable source reconstruction enumerates 328$\times$6 options, checks their signed truth tables and canonical deleted-source assignments, and yields the reported $H$ histogram. The 167 positive savings all exceed one and total 9,462. Each positive saving supplies a kink at its value; the 49 distinct values and both endpoints follow immediately. \(\square\)
