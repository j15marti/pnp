\paragraph{Scope of the restricted proof grammar.} Let \(\mathcal L\subseteq R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) be evaluator-source derivations using original clauses, strict binary RES, paid conservative two-input DEF and native AND3/LOCAL-CONVERT whose conditions are \emph{assertion consequences} on a common set of at most three named \emph{original evaluator} wires. Outputs may be represented by stronger entailed clauses, and every local-factor consumer is another such local assertion or clause RES. No output of a local factor is consumed by native GF(2) XOR; there are no exact newly named functional wire identities produced by local inference, no unrestricted semantic-support oracle, and no other source. DEF gate facets are exact, not assertion aliases. This is a substantial restriction on downstream proof shape and does not alter the full fixed calculus.

For normalized \(F=\bigwedge_i C_i\) with total \(L=\sum_i|C_i|\ge1\), let \(T(F)\) be REFEREE341's clause-private binary OR/AND evaluator with \(L-1\) fresh gates, every signed defining clause and a positive root-unit source. Let \(W_{\mathcal L}(T(F))\) minimize charged nodes in \(\mathcal L\).
\begin{theorem}[Restricted retyping-safe source retraction]
For every UNSAT normalized signed CNF \(F\),
\[
 \boxed{W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(F)\le3(L-1)+27W_{\mathcal L}(T(F)).}
\]
This is an upper simulation, not a superpolynomial proof or machine lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a legal \(q\)-node \(\mathcal L\) refutation of \(T(F)\). Before retyping any evaluator variables, apply LSPV-3/386's source-authenticated implicate compiler to every local assertion step. There are \(3^3=27\) distinct non-tautological clauses, including the empty clause, on three named wires. Binary Resolution saturation therefore adds at most 27 new clauses per local step. For each falsifying row of an output factor, an inferred subclause of the associated blocker exists by Resolution implicate completeness. Strengthened clauses can replay later ordinary RES: either their opposed pivot survives and is resolved, or one strengthened parent already subsumes the target resolvent. By the stated grammar restriction, no local output must subsequently be used as an exact DEF identity or GF(2) row. Thus the entire evaluator refutation compiles to RES plus DEF for at most \(27q\) paid nodes.

Now apply REFEREE341's source replay. From the original \(F\), introduce the same \(L-1\) conservative paid DEF gates, derive every clause-output and the root-unit by \(2(L-1)\) strict RES nodes, and map each old free evaluator gate facet to its charged DEF view. Rename proof-private variables away from those gates. All downstream nodes are now only RES or DEF, whose named wire typing is preserved when the evaluator's former source variables become auxiliary gates. The result is a valid source-authenticated contradiction with cost \(\le3(L-1)+27q\). Minimize over \(q\). All conversions are explicit and use no SAT oracle, nonuniform answer axiom or free semantic jump.
\end{proof}
