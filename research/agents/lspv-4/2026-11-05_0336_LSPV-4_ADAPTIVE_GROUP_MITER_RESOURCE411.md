## ITERATE: exact group-miter lemma and query theorem
Fix an explicit syntactically legal acyclic list D of t individually paid, conservative, total Boolean two-input DEF gates, an output h, and m distinct ORIGINAL variables in its syntactic transitive cone (m<=2t for t>=1). Let Ess(h) be globally essential original coordinates. For subset I of these coordinates, form an explicit two-copy CNF M_I: both copies of all defining gates; equalities x_j=x'_j only for j outside I; and output disequality h(x) != h(x'). **Inputs in I are free to differ, not required to flip.**

**Lemma (PROVED):** SAT(M_I) iff I intersects Ess(h). If output changes between x,x' differing only on I, change variables in I one at a time; one change must affect h, giving an essential coordinate. Conversely an essential-coordinate witness differing only at i in I extends uniquely over both conservative gate banks to satisfy the miter.

**Theorem (PROVED):** Balanced binary splitting, querying each group only if its parent was positive, returns exact support for s<=3 with at most 1+2s ceil(log2 m) queries, and returns four distinct essential coordinates if s>=4 with at most 1+8 ceil(log2 m) queries. For m=0 it makes zero queries, and for m=1 at most one. Each expanded internal group contains an essential singleton subsequently found. At height H at most min(s,4) H such groups expand, each causing two child tests; add the root test. This is an elementary adaptive group-testing method, not a novel lower-bound theorem.

**FALSIFIED alternative:** Requiring EVERY bit in I to flip fails for h=x0 XOR x1, I={0,1}: simultaneous complement never changes XOR, despite both coordinates being essential. **FALSIFIED alternative:** Under source F=(x0), output h=x0 AND x1 and replacement h'=x1 agree on all F-models but differ globally, so source-model equality cannot authorize arbitrary typed GF(2) or exported-wire substitutions.
