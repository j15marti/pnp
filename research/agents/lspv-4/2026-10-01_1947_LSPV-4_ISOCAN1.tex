\section*{LSPV-4 hostile audit: isolation and canonical-witness escape after PADINV1}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. This report proves hostile closure facts for the Search--G/FP--FNP branch. It proves no SAT lower bound, arbitrary-algorithm transfer, or nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing hinge.

\paragraph{Top obstacles and corpus reviewed.} The immediate questions are whether uniqueness/isolation/canonical witnesses repair PADINV1's representation defect, and whether any such witness forces more than polynomial recomputation under $P=NP$. Surveyed AGENTS.md, CODEX.md, the pnp-research skill, root main.tex, current tasks including TASK-006/007, MAIN 1558, materially relevant LSPV-1--5 reports, and HEC/CRS-PPC/CCSPC/RCCN/Search--G/search-spine artifacts surfaced by repository search. PADINV1 is verified. LSPV-3 SGLALT1/SGLAUD1 are substantive and sentinelled, so predecessor preflight passes.

\paragraph{Primary-literature preflight.} Valiant--Vazirani prove a randomized reduction from SAT to the zero-versus-unique-solution regime; uniqueness therefore does not generically trivialize SAT. Mulmuley--Vazirani--Vazirani give the related isolation lemma. Toda and Toda--Ogiwara establish the power of counting classes relative to PH, not a selector-to-counting forcing theorem. Aaronson--Wigderson remains the applicable barrier reference.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: canonical witness closure.} Let $R(x,y)$ be polynomial-time decidable and polynomially balanced, $|y|\le p(|x|)$. Define
\[L_R=\{(x,a):\exists y\,[R(x,y)\wedge a\preceq y]\}.\]
Then $L_R\in NP$. Under $P=NP$, decide $L_R$ in polynomial time. Starting with the empty prefix, query whether prefix $a0$ extends to a witness; choose 0 on YES and 1 otherwise. After at most $p(|x|)$ queries this returns
\[y_{\min}(x)=\min_{\rm lex}\{y:R(x,y)\}.\]
Hence
\[\boxed{P=NP\Longrightarrow y_{\min}\in FP}\]
for every polynomially balanced P relation, with failure reported when no witness exists.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: delayed canonical audits remain P-closed.} If $H(x,y,g)$ is polynomial-time and $g$ is any polynomial-length future challenge, then under $P=NP$
\[A(x,g)=H(x,y_{\min}(x),g)\]
is polynomial-time: recompute $y_{\min}(x)$ by prefix self-reduction after $g$ is known and evaluate $H$. Polynomially many delayed audits remain polynomial. Thus canonical witness plus future polynomial audit does not imply superpolynomial reacquisition under $P=NP$. This extends REACQ1 from a preselected deterministic FP selector to a canonical selector defined by the chosen representation.

\paragraph{Representation guard.} PADINV1's COPY wrapper still attacks naive canonicity. For $R_p(x,(y,z))\iff R(x,y)$, the standard product encoding has least padded witness $(y_{\min}(x),0^{p(|x|)})$. But an efficiently computable bijective relabeling of witness strings can change which semantic witness is lexicographically least while preserving polynomial selector equivalence. Lexicographic canonicity is therefore representation-canonical, not automatically a semantic invariant. Any audit must fix the source-sanctioned encoding and survive message/code relabeling.

\paragraph{Isolation does not supply the missing arrow.} Valiant--Vazirani isolation is a KNOWN THEOREM about randomized reduction to unique solutions, not a theorem forcing an arbitrary selector to materialize a superpolynomial resource. Under $P=NP$, SAT decision and prefix witness recovery are polynomial. RANDCLOSE1 already closes bounded-error polynomial causal computation with adaptive NP calls under the counterfactual. Randomized hashing/isolation can be algorithmically useful, but uniqueness alone does not repair Search--G's selector-to-resource gap.

\paragraph{Counting boundary retained.} PADINV1 remains exact: raw multiplicity and parity can be changed by irrelevant witness padding without changing selector complexity. Exact counting, PP, and parity are not known to collapse merely from $P=NP$, but importing such an aggregate does not show an FNP selector is forced to compute it. ISOCAN1 adds that moving in the opposite direction, from many witnesses to one isolated/canonical witness, also fails to force a superpolynomial resource under the counterfactual.

\paragraph{Hostile controls.} DEAD/COPY/DELAY inflate traces or encodings without changing semantic search. INTERP/RECOMP and static reread implement reacquisition. Relabeling changes naive lexical canonicity. Memoization, pointer jumping, and group compression can only reduce special-case costs. Gaussian/Tseitin, Horn/2-SAT, and bounded-treewidth controls show that complicated witness presentations need not imply hard global computation. Polynomial stagewise nondeterminism does not create NEXP merely because the latent tree is exponential. Superpolynomial explicit histories must be charged against ordinary SAT input size.

\paragraph{SU/vMS, acquisition, reconstruction, and PQ status.} No structural claim is strengthened. Exact SU09/vMS invocation, fixed-code stacked Schur rank, ACQ1 legal-state separation/compiler exponent, sparse-Q normalization, reconstruction quantifiers, arbitrary-algorithm transfer, and TC4 retain their prior statuses. Fixed-code Schur products, quadratic kernels, polarization, correctly typed puncturing/shortening, and explicit Gaussian elimination are EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFERS. Syzygies, Betti invariants, minimum-rank relations, collision/special-soundness extraction, and LWE-style black-box extraction are ANALOGY/CANDIDATE. Cryptographic hardness/random oracles, VLSI locality, distributed cuts, online no-reread bounds, and proof-system-specific lower bounds are FAILED TRANSFERS to unrestricted static SAT runtime.

\paragraph{Barrier audit.} ISOCAN1 relativizes: an NP/semantic oracle only makes prefix extension and witness recovery easier. The Schur/polar branch remains algebraic. No line here crosses Baker--Gill--Solovay or Aaronson--Wigderson.

\paragraph{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.} REACQ1 proved rerun for a deterministic FP selector. PADINV1 proved representation instability of raw count/parity. The new theorem is the canonical-selector specialization: under $P=NP$, the lexicographically least witness is polynomial-time recoverable by prefix queries. This does not contradict Valiant--Vazirani, Toda, RCCN, or the SGL reconstruction ledger.
