\begin{abstract}
An all-input, implementation-blind oracle controller that exactly decides SAT using only a bounded number of strictly shorter SAT truth-bit queries can have its oracle eliminated by recursion. The single-query-class theorem is already established in the project as EXTENSIONAL-DOWNWARD-ORACLE-ELIMINATION1 (MODELBRIDGE-0011, 11 October 2026); it is not claimed as new. Here we extend its simultaneous FLMV accounting to two independent child/selector query classes, derive an exact forbidden selector region at the canonical $241/200$ endpoint, and audit actual two-level SAT and UNSAT promised-family controls. The resulting exclusion is unconditional \emph{for this narrowly defined black-box protocol}; it does not rule out an intensional, hypothesis-dependent ARC compiler and proves no P versus NP separation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact source and receiving contract}
Let $E$ consist of joint pairs $(t,s)$ of one fixed, deterministic, uniform, total, exact ordinary-SAT random-access Turing machine in the FLMV indexed read-only-input model. The machine uses $N^{t+o(1)}$ time and $N^{s+o(1)}$ writable/index-tape space \emph{simultaneously} on every ordinary input. Under $P=NP$ some finite diagonal $(K,K)$ lies in $E$; no two algorithms' coordinates may be combined. The strict published exclusion used here is
\[
c_0=\frac{241}{200},\qquad d_0=\beta(c_0)=\frac{11642479}{23232400},\quad
\beta(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right),\quad
t<c_0,\ s<d_0.
\]
This is a sourced strict box via FLMV Theorem 3.1 and Corollaries 3.2, 5.8, not the retired $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ universal endpoint.
\paragraph{Extensional two-channel oracle controller.}
One fixed ordinary-input algorithm $D^{\SAT}$ is uniformly correct for every binary string (malformed strings included) and sees each query only as an ordinary encoded bit string plus its \emph{correct SAT membership bit}; no oracle program, internal trace, completion time, hidden clock, or free advice is visible. On source length $N$, it may make adaptively at most $N^{a+o(1)}$ child calls of length at most $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ and $N^{b+o(1)}$ selector calls of length at most $N^{\rho+o(1)}$, with fixed $\lambda,\rho<1$. All nonoracle work and metadata, including parsing, source reads, every unchanged/changed write, rule discovery/switching, unsuccessful options, child headers, gluing, query creation, output, and cache/replay work, cost at most $N^{r+o(1)}$ bit operations. Peak simultaneously live writable/index bits, \emph{including} all retained ancestors and ordinary query buffers, are $N^{h+o(1)}$. Program size is fixed independent of $N$.
\begin{theorem}[Two-channel downward elimination and simultaneous ledger]
Under the preceding total, uniform, extensional hypotheses, there is a \emph{single ordinary deterministic total exact SAT decider} $B$ with
\[
T_B(N)\le N^{\tau+o(1)},\quad
S_B(N)\le N^{\sigma+o(1)},\qquad
\tau=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{a}{1-\lambda},\frac{b}{1-\rho}\right\},\quad
\sigma=\max\{h,\lambda,\rho\}.
\]
If the declared $h$ already includes readable query buffers, the last two arguments in $\sigma$ are redundant. Every exponent belongs to the same machine.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On inputs below a fixed threshold, a uniform finite lookup/base algorithm gives the exact SAT bit. Above it, run the controller and answer each of its SAT-oracle questions by recursively running \emph{this same} algorithm on that strictly shorter ordinary string. The strict inequalities $\lambda,\rho<1$ make this a well-founded size recursion. Strong induction on the input length proves that every adaptive answer equals the true SAT bit, hence every subsequent queried string and the final answer coincide with $D^{\SAT}$, for both SAT and UNSAT sources.

One realized recursion path obeys
\[
T(N)\le N^{r+o(1)}+N^{a+o(1)}T(N^{\lambda+o(1)})
                     +N^{b+o(1)}T(N^{\rho+o(1)}).
\]
For any $\epsilon>0$ take $t>\tau$ and then sufficiently small uniform error envelopes so that $r<t$, $a+\lambda t<t$, and $b+\rho t<t$. Induction gives $T(N)\le N^{t}$ up to a constant and subpolynomial loss, proving the displayed exponent. Depth-first execution retains one suspended parent at each size $N,N^{\mu+o(1)},N^{\mu^2+o(1)},\ldots$, where $\mu=\max(\lambda,\rho)<1$. The geometric-size sum of paid frames, generator buffers and recursive child tapes is $N^{\sigma+o(1)}$. FLMV indexed accesses to a materialized child are simulated on counted work/index tapes with at most polylogarithmic address overhead; a write-only output tape is \emph{not} reused as a free readable input. The fixed interpreter and stack are uniform. No SAT oracle remains.
\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[Unconditional extensional selector exclusion at ARC-90]
Set $a=b_0=3/5$, $\lambda=1/2$, $r=11/10$ and $h=\max\{1/2,\rho\}$, with an additional selector fanout $N^b$ and selector length $N^\rho$, $0\le\rho<1$. If
\[
\boxed{\quad b<\frac{241}{200}(1-\rho),\qquad
\rho<\frac{11642479}{23232400},\quad}
\]
the proposed all-input extensional two-channel controller cannot exist with the declared resource bounds.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The recursive decider would have
$\tau=\max\{11/10,6/5,b/(1-\rho)\}<241/200$
and $\sigma=\max\{1/2,\rho\}<d_0$ and would therefore contradict FLMV on \emph{ordinary SAT}. No assumption $P=NP$ enters this nonexistence result. The first query class was independent of the second; identical selector/child queries may be cached and charged once if actually shared, but this only improves the upper bound and cannot evade the contradiction.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Numerical boundary.}
At $\rho=1/2$, the conservative strict-box time threshold is $b<241/400=0.6025$; at $b=3/5$ the time requirement is $\rho<1-(3/5)/(241/200)\approx0.50207469$, but the materialized selector buffer forces the tighter $\rho<d_0\approx0.50113114$. An extensional selector with $(b,\rho)=(.600,.501)$ is therefore forbidden by the cited box; at $(.600,.502)$ the \emph{same test} is inconclusive on space, and at $(.610,.500)$ it is inconclusive on time. Inconclusive is not feasible. For $\rho=1$, a parent-scale SAT query prevents the downward recursion proof; its actual hypothetical solver time $N^{t+o(1)}$, lookup and live space must be charged.

\paragraph{Genuine two-level semantic controls.}
Form an \emph{ordinary, not M22} 3CNF from $p$ disjoint three-variable blocks. All eight possible signed clauses make a block UNSAT; any seven of the eight are SAT. For square $p$, partition into $\sqrt p$ groups of $\sqrt p$ blocks and recursively AND the independently computed child SAT bits over two true levels, renumbering each child and charging every emitted ordinary literal. At $p=256$, all-SAT has 1792 clauses and 59200 bits in the declared finite width-tagged encoding; after two rounds there are 16 first-level child calls, 256 second-level calls, 272 total. Maximum single child is 2416 bits and 71168 child bits are emitted in the full two-level tree. Replacing one block by its eight-clause UNSAT counterpart produces an UNSAT source with 1793 clauses, 59233 bits and correct rejection. Two UNSAT blocks also reject. Smaller $p=16,64$ pass both labels. This is a \emph{promised disconnected easy family}, not a universal ARC decider or a measured FLMV work-space execution.
\paragraph{Finite diagonal arithmetic only.}
For the hypothetical recurrence $t_{j+1}=\max(11/10,3/5+t_j/2)$, $s_{j+1}=\max(1/2,s_j/2)$, the exact audit finds finite safe-box entries after 8, 10, 11, 12 and 13 steps from diagonal seeds $K=2,4,8,16,32$. This arithmetic does not construct a single valid edge or justify charging an oracle as free. Program/state introspection, runtime-dependent strategies, hashes and clock-dependent policies are \emph{outside} the extensional theorem. To contribute to ARC they require a hypothesis-dependent, same-realization, uniform and fully charged transport, not merely wrapper obscurity.
