\section*{Established lower bound and fixed target}

\[
\exp\left(\frac{n}{32768\ell^2}\right)
\]
lines, sources included. This is a 2026 preprint reporting Lean formalization, not an independently replayed or peer-reviewed result here. Its lines are disjunctions of affine $\mathbb F_2$ equations with its declared affine-cut and semantic-weakening rules. In canonical signed ASCII DIMACS, the original input has $m=n\binom{n+1}{2}=\Theta(n^3)$ clauses of width $2\ell$ and $N=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$ encoded \emph{bits}. Haken (1985) establishes a distinct exponential \emph{ordinary Resolution} bound for unary pigeonhole; Cook (1976) gives polynomial Extended Resolution proofs of unary pigeonhole. Neither lower bound concerns arbitrary SAT algorithms or every stronger calculus.

\section*{New exact circuit lemma}
\begin{theorem}[Affine-first one-bit conditioning]
Let $C$ be any acyclic circuit on original Boolean inputs, in a fixed binary Boolean-gate basis (all 16 binary truth functions permitted), with paid named gates and $g_{\rm nl}$ gates whose two-input truth tables are nonaffine. There exists an \emph{explicit adaptive partition} of the original input assignments into at most $2^{g_{\rm nl}}$ disjoint affine subspaces, each specified by at most $g_{\rm nl}$ consistent affine equations over original inputs. On every partition cell, every named gate output equals an affine form of the original input bits. This holds with shared circuit gates, signed inputs, constants, repeated arguments, and arbitrary sequences of affine gates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every two-input Boolean truth function has a unique algebraic normal form
\[
f(u,v)=c\oplus\alpha u\oplus\beta v\oplus\gamma uv,
\quad c,\alpha,\beta,\gamma\in\{0,1\}.
\]
It is affine precisely when $\gamma=0$, which accounts for eight of the sixteen binary tables. Process the gate DAG topologically. Inductively, on each current affine cell, the two gate inputs are affine forms $A(Y),B(Y)$ in the original variables. If $\gamma=0$, the gate output $c\oplus\alpha A\oplus\beta B$ is affine and no split is needed. If $\gamma=1$, branch on \emph{only one} affine equation, $A(Y)=0$ or $A(Y)=1$. On the $A=a$ child, the exact output is
\[
f(a,B(Y))=c\oplus\alpha a\oplus(\beta\oplus a)B(Y),
\]
again affine. Drop inconsistent branches by standard GF$(2)$ consistency checking, which cannot increase the leaf count. Complementary tests give disjoint exhaustive children. Each paid nonaffine gate at most doubles the number of cells; every affine gate leaves it unchanged. Induction proves the bound and the affine annotations on every cell. The rule is a \emph{proof translation technique}; it is not an additional Rmix341 inference rule.
\end{proof}
The $2^{g_{\rm nl}}$ leaf count is attained by this stated construction on $g_{\rm nl}$ independent AND gates whose first operands are independent original bits. No claim of optimality among \emph{all} possible circuit decompositions is made. If two gate operands are identical or an affine test is already forced, fewer branches can result. The previous $4^{g_{\rm nl}}$ method needlessly fixes both operands.

\section*{Sharper scoped conditional proof-size transfer}
Adopt precisely the additional hypotheses of LSPV-2 BRANCH371's \emph{source-authenticated guarded Res$(\oplus)$ compiler} for its proper fixed-grammar fragment. Let $m$ be source clause count, $r$ RES nodes, $x$ native XOR nodes, $J$ the other extensional local nodes, $k_j$ the maximum independent affine rank exposed to local node $j$ after conditioning, $k_*=\max_j k_j$ or zero, $q$ all charged nodes, and $g_{\rm nl}\le q$ paid nonlinear two-input DEF gates. \[
\boxed{S_{\oplus}(F)\le m+2^{g_{\rm nl}}\left(m+2r+2x+\sum_{j\in J}2^{k_j+1}+2\right)}
\tag{1}
\]
and hence the conservative uniform simplification
\[
\boxed{S_{\oplus}(F)\le 2^{g_{\rm nl}+k_*}(4m+8q+8).}
\tag{2}
\]
This is a conditional \emph{upper} bound on the output size of a restricted proof-system compiler, and not a lower bound on $W_R$ for the full calculus. Its correctness depends on source-preserving guards, the precise LOCAL-CONVERT wire grammar, legal Res$(\oplus)$ weakenings/cuts, and the prior compiler's typed replay. The affine partition lemma itself does not assume those issues away.

If Braun's stated theorem and the complete scoped compiler are both valid, every covered proof of authentic bit-PHP must satisfy the new tradeoff
\[
\boxed{
g_{\rm nl}+k_*+\log_2(4m+8q+8)>
\frac{n}{32768\ell^2\ln2}.}
\tag{3}
\]
For bounded $k_*$ and polynomial $q$, this forces $g_{\rm nl}=\Omega(n/\ell^2)$ and thus $q=\Omega(n/\ell^2)$. Since $N=\Theta(n^3\ell^2)$, that is only a \emph{polynomial} lower bound in encoded length.
