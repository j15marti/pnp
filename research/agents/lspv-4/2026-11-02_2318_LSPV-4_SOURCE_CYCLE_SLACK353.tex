\section{Exact cycle--slack identity}
For any sound contradiction DAG $\pi$, keep only the ancestry of its concluding contradiction. Let $s\ge1$ count distinct source clause nodes, $q$ count distinct charged derived nodes, and $k_v$ count explicit antecedent references of a derived node $v$, including parallel references. The ancestry's underlying undirected multigraph is connected. Put
\[
E=\sum_v k_v,\quad \beta=E-(s+q)+1,\quad \delta=\sum_v(2-k_v).
\]
The integer $\beta\ge0$ is its undirected cycle rank. If $k_v\le2$ for all $v$, then $\delta\ge0$.
\begin{theorem}
For any source-explicit at-most-binary-premise proof DAG,
\[
\boxed{q=s-1+\beta+\delta.}
\]
For any nonnegative weights and even unbounded arity, the exact weighted generalization is
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{cost}(\pi)=s-1+\beta+\sum_v\left[\omega(v)-(k_v-1)\right].}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
As the graph is connected, $E=s+q-1+\beta$. The binary-slot definition gives $E=2q-\delta$. Equating gives the first formula. For the second, add and subtract $\sum_v(k_v-1)=E-q=s-1+\beta$ from $\sum_v\omega(v)$.
\end{proof}
For clause-minimally UNSAT $F$ with $m$ source clauses, soundness plus conservative auxiliary definitions require $s=m$ in every refutation: otherwise the unused-clause deletion witness satisfies every used source. Therefore
\[
W_R(F)=m-1+\min_{\pi}\{\beta(\pi)+\delta(\pi)\}.
\]
A cost-$m-1$ proof must be an undirected tree with two antecedents at every derived node. No source can be reused in such a proof; no unary or zero-antecedent conversion can occur. The baseline counts indispensable sources; the excess counts necessary sharing/cycles or nonbinary deficits, not arbitrary-machine runtime.

\[
