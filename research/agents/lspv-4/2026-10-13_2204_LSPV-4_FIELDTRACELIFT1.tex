\section{Exact affine elimination and witness maps}
Index edges of $K_{s,s}$ by $(i,j)$, $0\le i,j<s$.  Take
\[
 T=\{(0,j):0\le j<s\}\cup\{(i,0):1\le i<s\},
 \quad C=\{(i,j):1\le i,j<s\}.                           \tag{4}
\]
For a cotree vector $y\in\F^d$, assign $y$ to $C$, subtract its incidences
from $c$, process the leaves $i>0$ and $j>0$, and finally assign $(0,0)$.
Call the resulting edge vector $\operatorname{Lift}_c(y)$.  Even parity of
$c$ is necessary and sufficient because $X$ is connected.

Define
\[
 a=\operatorname{Lift}_c(0),\qquad
 L_j=\operatorname{Lift}_0(e_j).                         \tag{5}
\]
The leaf equations give, without a hidden existential choice,
\[
 B_Xa=c,qquad B_XL=0,qquad \pi_C L=I_d,
 \qquad \operatorname{Lift}_c(y)=a+Ly.                  \tag{6}
\]
Thus (5) is an explicit particular solution and kernel basis.  A program may
materialize $L$ in polynomial space, but the witness finder needs only one
forest pass.

\begin{theorem}[typed field-trace Tseitin lift]
Every valid record for (3) is satisfiable.  A full edge witness is computable
uniformly in polynomial time and polynomial space.  Moreover every nonzero
$\F$-linear combination of the $r$ coordinate residuals has polar rank $d$
on cotree coordinates, before and after the declared affine mixing.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent $b$ in the polynomial basis of $K$ and set
\[
 z=(1,b)\in K^2\cong\F^d,qquad
 y=A^{-1}(z+p),qquad x=\operatorname{Lift}_c(y).         \tag{7}
\]
Then (6) gives $B_Xx=c$ and
\[
 Q_f(A\pi_C(x)+p)=Q_f(z)=1\cdot b=b.                    \tag{8}
\]
This proves the selector and witness map.

For $\lambda\in K^\times$, the corresponding scalar component is
$q_\lambda(u,v)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\F}(\lambda uv)$.  Its polar form is
\[
 \beta_\lambda((u,v),(u',v'))
 =\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\F}\!\left(\lambda(uv'+u'v)\right). \tag{9}
\]
If $(u,v)\ne(0,0)$ and $u\ne0$, nondegeneracy of the finite-field trace
pairing supplies $v'$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(\lambda uv')=1$.  If $u=0$,
then $v\ne0$ and the symmetric argument supplies $u'$.  Hence (9) has zero
radical and rank $2r=d$.  Translation by $p$ does not alter the polar form,
and composition with $A$ replaces its matrix by $A^{\mathsf T}M_\lambda A$,
which has the same rank because $A$ is invertible.

The recognizer checks the exact key set and dimensions, odd $s$, degree and
irreducibility of $f$, parity and ranges, and $\operatorname{rank}A=d$.
Polynomial arithmetic, Rabin-style irreducibility testing, Gaussian inversion,
field multiplication, the forest lift, output, and verification are all
polynomial in the encoded record.  With $A$ explicit, the binary input length
already contains $d^2$ matrix bits; naive $O(d^3)$ elimination is therefore
polynomial.  The complete edge output has $s^2$ bits.
\end{proof}

\section{Fiber and collision ledger}

\[
 |Q^{-1}(0)|=2q-1,qquad |Q^{-1}(b)|=q-1\quad(b\ne0).      \tag{10}

\[
 \operatorname{Coll}(Q)

 |\operatorname{im}Q|\ge \frac{q^4}{q(q^2+q-1)},
