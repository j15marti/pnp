## ITERATE: precise restricted cost lemma and proof

Fix **one** uniform total SAT decider $A$ with all-input worst-case time $T_A(m)=m^{t+o(1)}$ and space $S_A(m)=m^{s+o(1)}$ on the actual FLMV indexed read-only-input machine. For a **hypothetical**, source-authenticated, both-label-and-malformed aware compiler, suppose at most $N^{a+o(1)}$ sequential children of encoded length at most $m=N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ are issued. Parent local work including discovery/failed choices, parsing, conversion, cache and combination costs at most $N^{d+o(1)}$. Each child has $N^{u+o(1)}$ one-time setup and each *simulated guest step* has paid additional cost $N^{v+o(1)}$ in the specified interpreter. All-phase local writable/index peak is $N^{\rho+o(1)}$; simultaneously retained state is $N^{\eta+o(1)}$; a materialized child buffer has $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ bits.

**PROVED/EXACT for this interpreter architecture, conditional on its existence and correctness:**
$$
t'\le\max\{d,\ a+u,\ a+v+\lambda t\},\qquad
s'\le\max\{\rho,\eta,\lambda,\lambda s\}.
$$
Proof: each guest invocation performs at most $m^{t+o(1)}$ native steps; its interpreter charges at most $N^{v+o(1)}$ per step. Sum over $N^{a+o(1)}$ calls, their $N^{u+o(1)}$ setups and $N^{d+o(1)}$ local work; bound simultaneous space by the maximum because calls are sequential with buffers reused. If $r(m)$ is the number of actual guest source reads, a tighter charged bound is $\sum_i[T_A(m)+r_i(m)N^{v+o(1)}]$, never *automatically* $r_i=T_A$. If the accessor is charged once per input read and the guest makes far fewer reads, the coarse envelope need not be tight; this lemma does not impose unavoidable per-step overhead on every solver. Space counts all phases including preprocessing and guest head/index tapes. A native output-only stream is not a free read-only child.

For the proposed Route A envelope $a=3/5$, $\lambda=1/2$, $u=1/2$, $d=11/10$, $\rho=\eta=1/2$, the **bound** becomes
$$
t_{k+1}=\max\{11/10,\ 3/5+v+t_k/2\},\qquad s_{k+1}=\max\{1/2,s_k/2\}.
$$
Starting from sufficiently large finite exponent pair, the limiting upper envelope is $t_\infty=6/5+2v$, $s_\infty=1/2$. FLMV's relevant strict $k=2$ forbidden region is $s<\beta(t)$ with
$$
\beta(t)=\frac12\left(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\right).
$$
$\beta(6/5)=23/45$ gives strict gap $1/90$. The unique root $c_\star>1$ of $c^3+c^2-c-2=0$ is $1.2055694304005903\ldots$, hence the interpreter's upper **guarantee** enters this sufficient $k=2$ region precisely when $v<(c_\star-6/5)/2=0.00278471520029515\ldots$ (assuming other parameters stay fixed and finite iterability holds). At equality the endpoint is not strictly forbidden. This is not a lower bound excluding every other machine, alternate FLMV envelope, or a smarter compiler; a bad upper bound does not imply actual machine infeasibility.

With initial $(t,s)=(10,6)$, exact rational simulation gives $v=0$: first strict iteration 11; $v=1/400$: iteration 14 with limiting $t=241/200$, still strictly admissible; $v=1/300$: limiting $t=181/150$, so this particular upper guarantee does not enter the half-space region. Note prior LSPV-4/343 already calculated a different, larger $0.60556$ threshold for a **one-per-child** accessor/setup exponent. This report isolates the **per guest step** location, not a contradictory new universal lower bound.
