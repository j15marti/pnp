\section*{Exact machine and proof}
Fix a \emph{uniform deterministic total} converter with a read-only $M$-bit input, fixed finite control $q_0$, a fixed number $k$ of writable work/index tapes of total used space $s$ over a constant alphabet of size $a\ge2$, and an append-only write-once output \emph{port}. The converter cannot read the output or query its head position; each bit emitted costs one step. Source parsing, authenticated proof checking, source/index movement, caches and all local phases are included in $s$.

\begin{lemma}[Internal configurations]
For every halting run printing $L$ bits in $T$ steps,
\[
L\le T\le q_0(M+2)(s+2)^ka^s.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The right side bounds all observable internal states: finite control, input-head position, writable-tape symbols and work-head positions. The output port cannot affect later transitions. Revisiting any internal configuration would deterministically repeat forever and prevent halting. Therefore no internal state repeats, and every printed bit consumes a native step.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Fully explicit conversion space lower bound]
Any such total converter mapping every valid Extended Resolution source-plus-proof certificate to an \emph{explicit same-source ordinary Resolution} refutation obeys, on polynomial-length Cook PHP certificates,
\[
T_C\ge\exp(\Omega(h)),\qquad
S_C=\Omega(h)=\Omega((N_h/\log(N_h+2))^{1/3}).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Haken forces exponentially many explicit output lines. Cook gives input length $M_h=h^{O(1)}$. Thus $\log L=\Omega(h)$, while the lemma gives $\log L\le O(\log M_h)+k\log(s+2)+s\log a+O(1)$. If $s<h$, the non-$s$ terms are $O(\log h)$, forcing $s=\Omega(h)$. If $s\ge h$, the conclusion is immediate.
\end{proof}
