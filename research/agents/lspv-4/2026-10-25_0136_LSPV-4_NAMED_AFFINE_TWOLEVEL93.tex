\section{Original ordinary proper-3CNF source, encoding and witnesses}
Fix $q\ge3$, orientation $b\in\{0,1\}^q$, and original variables
$x_0,\ldots,x_{q-1}$. Edge $i$ demands
$x_i\oplus x_{i+1\bmod q}=b_i$. If $b_i=0$, use the proper binary halves
\[
(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1}),\qquad(x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1});
\]
if $b_i=1$, use $(x_i\vee x_{i+1})$ and
$(\neg x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1})$. Replace \emph{each} binary clause $C$ by
$(C\vee u)\wedge(C\vee\neg u)$ with its own distinct free helper $u$.
Call the resulting ordered proper-3CNF $P_{q,b}$.

The exact resources are $q$ original variables, $2q$ free helpers,
zero functional auxiliaries, $n=3q$, $m=4q$ distinct proper ternary
clause occurrences, and (fixed-width signed-literal records)
\[
 h_q=\left\lceil\log_2(64(7q+1))\right\rceil,\qquad
 N_q=(16q+2)h_q.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Changing a single $b_i$ changes four signed-literal source fields, with
all unsigned incidence, identifiers, headers and encoded length fixed.
Every original solution has $2^{2q}$ lifts by the free helpers. Therefore
\[
 \#P_{q,b}=
 \begin{cases}2^{2q+1},&\bigoplus_i b_i=0,\\0,&\bigoplus_i b_i=1.
 \end{cases}                                           \tag{2}
\]
Every strict subset of the $q$ complete edge blocks has exactly
$2^{3q-|S|}$ models \emph{with the original declared variable universe}.
Moreover, on an odd-parity parent removing \emph{any} of the $4q$
ternary clauses makes it SAT. These are prior restricted source theorems,
used here as adversarial scalar-oracle controls, not as complexity claims.

\section{Actual pinned ordinary child and exact count matrix}
Unroll a contiguous edge interval $[a,c)$ containing $k=c-a\ge1$
edges as a \emph{path} with local endpoint variables $z_0,z_k$,
$k-1$ internal original-path variables, and two private clause helpers
per edge. The final copy $z_q$ of the cycle's $x_0$ remains a
\emph{separate local endpoint} until the global closing trace imposes
equality. All local variable identifiers are freshly, injectively
renumbered.

For a local literal $\ell$ enforcing $z=v$, define the proper
four-clause pin
\[
 \operatorname{Pin}(\ell;p,r)=
 \bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{0,1\}}
 (\ell\vee p^{\epsilon}\vee r^{\delta}),\qquad
 p^0=p,\quad p^1=\neg p
                                                        \tag{3}
\]
(and analogously for $r$), with fresh auxiliaries $p,r$.
This enforces $\ell$ but leaves four assignments to its two helpers.
Put one pin on each path endpoint and call the resulting fully
serialized ordinary child $H_{a:c}(u,v)$ for $u,v\in\{0,1\}$.
There are
\[
 \begin{split}
 n_{\rm child}&=k+1+2k+4=3k+5,\\
 m_{\rm child}&=4k+8,\\
 L_k&=(16k+34)\left\lceil\log_2(64(7k+14))\right\rceil .
 \end{split}                                           \tag{4}
\]
All clauses are proper. Both labels have identical $L_k$.

Let $p_{a:c}=\bigoplus_{i=a}^{c-1}b_i$ and let
\[
 P_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 P_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Write $D_{a:c}(u,v)=\#H_{a:c}(u,v)$, counting all declared
auxiliary variables.

\begin{theorem}[Pinned count and Boolean relation, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every $q,b,0\le a<c\le q$ and endpoint bits $u,v$,
\[
 \boxed{D_{a:c}=16\cdot4^k P_{p_{a:c}},\qquad
 B_{a:c}=[D_{a:c}>0]=P_{p_{a:c}}.}                     \tag{5}
\]
The $2$-by-$2$ matrices have rows $u$ and columns $v$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Once $z_0=u$ is fixed, the $k$ XOR equations uniquely determine
$z_1,\ldots,z_k$. The final endpoint condition holds precisely when
$u\oplus v=p_{a:c}$. Every one of the $2k$ clause-padding helpers
is free, giving $4^k$ extensions. The two four-clause pin gadgets
introduce four additional free helpers, giving $2^4=16$ extensions.
If the condition fails, no extension exists. This proves every entry
of (5), including exact zero on UNSAT pinned children.
\end{proof}

\section{Two levels of same-source gluing and the missing factor}
Ordinary Boolean matrix multiplication below uses $\vee$ and
$\wedge$; ordinary integer multiplication is indicated by juxtaposition.

\begin{theorem}[Named two-level transport and multiplicity correction,
PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every $a<d<c$ the complete Boolean relations and the \emph{integer}
child count matrices satisfy
\[
 \boxed{B_{a:c}=B_{a:d}\odot B_{d:c}},\qquad
 \boxed{D_{a:c}=\frac1{16}D_{a:d}D_{d:c}}.             \tag{6}
\]
For any partition $0=i_0<i_1<\cdots<i_r=q$,
\[
 \boxed{\#P_{q,b}
 =16^{-r}\operatorname{tr}
   \left(\prod_{j=1}^{r}D_{i_{j-1}:i_j}\right)}.         \tag{7}
\]
All division results in (6)--(7) are exact integers.
The equations may be applied recursively to arbitrary fixed
two-level subdivisions, without knowing the parent SAT label.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$P_{\alpha}P_{\gamma}=P_{\alpha\oplus\gamma}$ over the
Boolean semiring and over the integers, since these are permutation
matrices. For $k_1=d-a$ and $k_2=c-d$, equation (5) gives
\[
 D_{a:d}D_{d:c}
 =16^2\,4^{k_1+k_2}P_{p_{a:d}\oplus p_{d:c}}
 =16D_{a:c}.
\]
This proves both formulas in (6). The shared cut source variable is
summed \emph{once}; the four private pin helpers of each subchild
are not original-source variables. Omitting their multiplicity gives
a wrong model count even though the SAT Boolean join is correct.
Multiplying the $r$ count matrices gives
$16^r4^qP_{\oplus b_i}$. Closing the cycle identifies $z_q=z_0$,
implemented by the trace. The result is
$2\cdot4^q$ for even parity and $0$ otherwise after division by
$16^r$, proving (7) and the source count (2).
\end{proof}

Equation (6) is a concrete source-to-target witness-map rule.
A naive model-count join \emph{without} the factor $1/16$
overcounts by exactly $16$ at every binary split. The Boolean SAT
decision relation is correct without the factor, because only
positivity matters. The integer count values have
$\Theta(k)$ bits when nonzero; they need not be explicitly stored:
the pair $(k,p_{a:c})$ is a logarithmic-bit symbolic representation.
Even a complete $2^r$ array of child SAT answers would be a
different object from this constant-size named relation.
