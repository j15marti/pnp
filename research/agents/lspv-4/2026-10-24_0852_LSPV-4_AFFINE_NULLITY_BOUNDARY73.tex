\section{Polynomial side: one affine free bit per component}
For arbitrary Boolean variables $X$, let $E$ be a system of $\mathbb F_2$ equations and $B$ an arbitrary 2-CNF. Make the affine incidence components by connecting variables appearing in the same equation. Unused variables form singleton components. For a consistent component $U_i$ put $\delta_i=|U_i|-\operatorname{rank}(E|_{U_i})$. Inconsistent $E$ terminates UNSAT.
\begin{theorem}[Affine-component quotient; PROVED/EXACT]
If $\delta_i\le1$ for every consistent affine component, $B\wedge E$ is decidable in polynomial time by a uniform deterministic exact algorithm, even if the sum $\sum_i\delta_i=\Theta(n)$ and $B$ arbitrarily couples all components. The algorithm constructs a bijective witness map to an explicit 2-CNF on at most one quotient variable per component.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Parse the equations and discover the components. Charged Gaussian elimination yields $x=a_x\oplus b_xt_i$ for each variable of a nullity-one component, where $t_i$ is its single free bit and $a_x,b_x\in\{0,1\}$. Rank-full components give constants. Substitution changes each signed source literal into a constant or a signed literal on exactly one $t_i$. Every clause of $B$ remains a tautology, contradiction, unit or binary clause. An implication-graph SCC solve is exact; back-substitution recovers the unique source assignment corresponding to any quotient assignment and verifies every original constraint. Discovery, elimination, substitutions, SCC, output and replay are charged. Dense elimination gives a conservative $O(N^3)$ bit-operation and $O(N^2)$-bit workspace bound, with linear SCC work thereafter.
\end{proof}
The key invariant is the \emph{maximum local} affine nullity, not the total affine dimension, a syntactic residual, or solver time. No promise that all adaptive deciders internally use this representation is made.
\section{NP-complete side: affine nullity two}
For every vertex $v$ of an input graph $G=(V,E_G)$ introduce three Boolean color indicators $x_{v,1},x_{v,2},x_{v,3}$ and the equation
\[x_{v,1}\oplus x_{v,2}\oplus x_{v,3}=1.\]
Include the three binary NAND clauses $(\neg x_{v,i}\vee\neg x_{v,j})$ for $i<j$. For every edge $\{u,v\}$ and color $j$, add $(\neg x_{u,j}\vee\neg x_{v,j})$. Every binary is negative-only and thus Horn. Odd XOR permits $001,010,100,111$; local NANDs remove $111$. The remaining three assignments are exactly one-hot colors. Cross-edge NANDs prohibit equal colors.
Represent one odd ternary XOR $(a\oplus b\oplus c=1)$ by the four proper ternary clauses
\[
(a\vee b\vee c),\quad(a\vee\neg b\vee\neg c),\quad
(\neg a\vee b\vee\neg c),\quad(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee c).
\]
\begin{theorem}[Sharp local boundary; PROVED/EXACT reduction, KNOWN NP-completeness]
The mixed affine-plus-binary language with maximum affine-component nullity at most two is NP-complete even if each affine component consists of exactly one disjoint odd 3-XOR equation and all binaries are NAND clauses. The graph-to-formula map is parsimonious: there is exactly one Boolean solution per proper 3-coloring.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding local truth table gives the bijective reduction from GRAPH 3-COLORABILITY, known NP-complete by Stockmeyer and Garey--Johnson--Stockmeyer. Each component has three variables, one independent equation, hence nullity two. Verification of an assignment is polynomial. The reduction is uniform and writes constant-many source-indexed records per vertex and edge, with no SAT oracle or guessed color witness.
\end{proof}
Writing $v=|V|,e=|E_G|$, the exact ledger is
\[
n=3v,\quad m_2=3v+3e,\quad k=4v,\quad M=7v+3e,\qquad
N=(28v+12e+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(10v+3e+1))\right\rceil.
\]
There are no auxiliary Boolean variables and no free target bits. Construction, source reads, inverse graph/color decoding, verification and output ($4M+2$ addressed words) are $O(v+e)$ word operations; fixed-width identifiers and signs are included. The minimum raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor has size $v$, one variable from each disjoint ternary support, giving $2^v\poly(N)$ as an UPPER bound only.
\section{Scalable matched SAT/UNSAT and source-query lower bound}
Let $q\ge3$ be odd and the graph be the $q$-cycle. Add for each vertex $v$ a fresh auxiliary $u_v$ and two binary anchor clauses
\[
(s_v x_{v,3}\vee u_v)\wedge(s_v x_{v,3}\vee\neg u_v),\quad
s_v=-1\ \text{if }b_v=0,\quad s_v=+1\ \text{if }b_v=1.
\]
The anchor forces $x_{v,3}=b_v$ with no initial written unit. The $u_v$ are FREE on every satisfying source coloring. The unsigned clause supports, identifiers and bit length are independent of $b$. Exact counts:
\[
\boxed{n=4q,\quad m_2=8q,\quad k=4q,\quad M=12q,\quad
N=(48q+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(16q+1))\right\rceil.}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Two-sign sensitivity; PROVED/EXACT in the addressed-word model]
For odd $q$, the all-zero orientation $D_{q,0^q}$ is UNSAT. Changing exactly one anchor-pair polarity $b_v:0\to1$ gives SAT with precisely $2^{q+1}$ complete assignments. Every deterministic exact addressed-literal-word decider on the all-zero input must read at least one of the two changed literal-sign words from EACH of the $q$ independently sensitive anchors, hence $\Omega(q)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ word probes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All-zero anchors forbid color three, so an odd cycle cannot use only two colors. One selected color-three vertex leaves a path with exactly two 2-colorings. The $q$ auxiliaries $u_v$ are each free, multiplying by $2^q$. An algorithm skipping both sign words of some anchor has the same deterministic read transcript after the opposite-label single-pair flip, a contradiction. The claim is encoding/model-specific acquisition, not superlinear post-read computation.
\end{proof}
All $q$ ternary triples are initially disjoint and the minimum raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor is $q$. A source-aware signed-cycle recognizer also decides the family in $O(q)$ word time by checking adjacent chosen color-three vertices and bipartiteness of the remaining induced graph. This falsifies any inference of unavoidable $2^{\Omega(q)}$ computation from that raw backdoor alone.
\section{Adaptive-state ledger and restricted policy audit}
The actual tested uniform solver takes an arbitrary CNF, reads all source clauses, searches for binary resolution pairs $(L\vee u)$ and $(L\vee\neg u)$, derives certified forced $L$, restricts the source, chooses SCC if all residual clauses have width at most two, otherwise discovers distinct ternary supports and enumerates representatives if the observed set has size at most eight, otherwise performs exact DPLL fallback. It does not know the true SAT label or receive the optimal backdoor. SCC and DPLL are exact; source witnesses are independently verified. Its finite menu does not cover all possible algorithms.

\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{state}&\text{live }k&\text{observed/analysed parameter}&\text{chosen action}\\\hline
S_0&4q&h_{\rm raw}=q\text{ (analysis only)}&\text{input and pair scan}\\
