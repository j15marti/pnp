\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If two different $b,b'$ led to the same working configuration after the sign block, append one identical suffix including the proposed conclusion $y=b$. Therefore all $2^m$ prefixes must have different configurations. The final fixed source records in the SAT and UNSAT cases do not change the argument. Storing all $m$ earlier signs provides the matching upper bound.

\begin{proof}
