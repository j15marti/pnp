\begin{proof}

A ROW contributes 1 if its triple contains the currently selected variable, while XOR outputs the XOR of its two already-computed parent bits. One $K$-bit scratch vector plus logarithmic source/proof/index pointers suffices, including all phases and output. Repeated work-tape seeks and read-only scans contribute polynomial time. \qedhere
\end{proof}
