\section{Declared machine resource regions and peer-refereed bound}
Use one sequential two-way read-only input head, a fixed number of sequential finite-alphabet work tapes, and charged head jumps of reach at most $J(N)$, with the landing symbol read only after the destination has been selected. Set $R$ to changes of sign among nonzero input-head movements, $S$ to all visited writable work cells, and $T$ to all bit transitions. A complete transcript crossing a middle-gap cut records control, direction, future-readable work configuration and head positions, and source and destination jump coordinates. Its record size is
\[
B_*=O_k(S+\log(N+T+2)+1).
\]
For the $2^m$ YES pairs $(a,\neg a)$, identical accepting crossing transcripts would splice into a mixed NO pair. The already published HEADMOVE141 proof counts $\Omega(m/B_*)$ mean crossings for each gap cut; summing cuts before choosing a common input yields one accepting computation with $\Omega(Gm/B_*)$ traversed cuts. Every jump crosses at most $J$ such cuts, and successive crossings of any single cut alternate directions. Thus the native simultaneous inequalities are
\[
TJ(S+\log(N+T+2)+1)=\Omega(N^2/\log N),
\]
\[
(R+1)(S+\log(N+T+2)+1)=\Omega(N/\log N).
\]
For polynomial exponents $T=N^{t+o(1)}$, $S=N^{s+o(1)}$, $J=N^{j+o(1)}$, $R+1=N^{r+o(1)}$, they imply $t+s+j\ge2$ and $r+s\ge1$. These are \emph{restricted deterministic} results, not an independent new unrestricted SAT lower bound. The Python reproducer checks all intermediate source/landing coordinates, per-cut jump counts, conservation of total cut traversals, and injective complete source-buffer transcripts for 3,048 genuine SAT runs. Its full raw data is separately provided.

\section{Adversarial polarity, model transfer and next gap}
A co-nondeterministic SAT machine on the same promised encoding can guess an index $i$, scan forward to $a_i$ and $b_i$, and reject iff both equal one. On SAT every guess accepts; on UNSAT some guess rejects. Its time is $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$, writable/index space $O(\log N)$ and input-head reversals zero. Thus the deterministic reversal bound does not constrain certificate guessing. Two independent input heads or indexed RAT input likewise bypass the single-head transport premise.
