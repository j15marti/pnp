## Exact obstruction and theorem

If there are a new variables and m_+,m_- positive/negative monotone clauses, incidence counting gives 3m_+=P+2a, 3m_-=Q+2a. Consequently **P-Q is divisible by 3**. A single mixed 2P1N or 1P2N source clause has signed port imbalance ±1, so no standalone such tile exists, of ANY size or internal computational sophistication.
