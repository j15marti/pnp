\section{Actual indexed FLMV resource and universal-ARC audit}
FLMV Section 2.1 works on a deterministic indexed random-access Turing machine with read-only input and work/index tapes. An indexed jump atomically erases the index tape's \emph{contents}; it does not imply the index head is at cell zero. Multiple bits can therefore be erased in one native transition, invalidating a naively port-counted full-index-tape XOR-to-time lower bound. An offline binary or Gray encoding of an index-head coordinate is not a tape-cell write. At every stage we charge grammar recognition, source bits and heads, acquisition and signed-pair sorting, clause-count/index-head writes, failed methods, every switch, circuit/output bits, proof or GF(2) checking, cache equality, explicit child access and simultaneous live guest DELAY/COPY/RECOMP high-water writable/index space. The simple materialized Gray enumeration gives a conservative indexed-bit simulation upper bound $O((N+2^n)\log^4(N+2^n))$ time and $O(N\log^2(N+2^n))$ writable/index peak under a standard fixed finite tape configuration; this is a proposed generous compilation budget \emph{not independently measured in native FLMV transitions} and is exponentially slow. It is not a numerical FLMV lower-bound contradiction.

For a source-authenticated adaptive residual state $z$, with actual executed successors, the mandatory ledger is
\[
\begin{split}
W(z)\le{}& W_{\rm grammar,source,discover,select}(z)+
\sum_{q\in {\rm failed}(z)}W_{\rm trial,verify,switch}(z,q)
+ W_{\rm compile,lookup,cache}(z)\\
&+\sum_{z'\in{\rm executedSucc}(z)}
\left(W_{\rm reencode,childbit,check}(z,z')+W(z')\right)
+W_{\rm proof,guest,output}(z).
\end{split}
\]
DAG-shared acquired work is counted once, actually executed child calls with multiplicity, and peak space is the maximum over \emph{simultaneously live} original and retained-ancestor representation, work/index tapes, cache, proof, and guest storage. Without a polynomial all-input bound on this aggregate or a power-short arbitrary-$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ with separately established tight source-to-target \emph{same-machine} numerical FLMV transport, no unrestricted separation follows.
