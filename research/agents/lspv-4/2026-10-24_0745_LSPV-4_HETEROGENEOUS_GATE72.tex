\section{Source, compiler, witness map and exact counts}
Fix even $q\ge4$ and orientation vectors $\beta,b\in\{0,1\}^q$.
Introduce parity variables $h,e_0,\ldots,e_{q-1}$ with cyclic indices and
\[
A_{q,\beta}=\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-1}
(e_{i-1}\oplus e_i\oplus h=\beta_i). \tag{1}
\]
Write each equation as its exact quartet of proper ternary clauses
excluding the four wrong-parity assignments.
Let $B_b=F_b$ be the specified $q+4$-clause proper binary
chain on $z_0,\ldots,z_q,u,v$.

For each source XOR ternary $C=(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$
create fresh $X_C,t_C$ and emit
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
 (\neg X_C\vee t_C)\land(\neg\ell_1\vee t_C)
 \land(X_C\vee\ell_1\vee\neg t_C)\\
 {}\land(t_C\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3).
\end{split}\tag{2}
\end{equation}
The first three clauses make $t_C=X_C\vee\ell_1$
functional. For each binary $(a\vee b)\in B_b$, add a private guard
$Y_{a,b}$ and ternary $(\neg Y_{a,b}\vee a\vee b)$.
Let $(G_j)_{j=1}^{5q+4}$ be all $4q$ copies $X$ and all
$q+4$ copies $Y$. Introduce switch $x$ and one binary implication cycle
\[
x\to G_1\to\cdots\to G_{5q+4}\to x. \tag{3}
\]
Write $D_{q,\beta,b}$ for the conjunction.
Every ternary is proper; binary clauses may have both positive signs,
so the backbone is 2CNF, not necessarily Horn.

\begin{theorem}[Exact label and resource identity, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every even $q\ge4$,
\[
D_{q,\beta,b}\in SAT
\iff \left(\bigoplus_i\beta_i=0\right)
\ \lor\ (b\ne1^q). \tag{4}
\]
The formula has
\[
n=11q+9,\quad m_2=13q+5,\quad k=9q+4,\quad M=22q+9. \tag{5}
\]
It retains $2q+5$ original variables, adds $9q+4$ functional
auxiliary variables, no free auxiliaries, and eliminates none.
Every satisfying original assignment has exactly one auxiliary extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The binary cycle (3) forces all guards equal $x$.
Each local OR gadget then uniquely forces $t_C=x\vee\ell_1$.
Existentially eliminating auxiliaries gives the guarded conjunction
$(x\vee A_{q,\beta})\land(\neg x\vee B_b)$, where the first
disjunction distributes across every clause of $A$.
For $x=0$ this is $A_{q,\beta}$; summing its $q$ XOR
equations over $\mathbb F_2$ cancels each $e_i$ twice and $h$
an even number of times, leaving $0=\oplus_i\beta_i$.
For even syndrome take $h=e_0=0$ and propagate around the cycle,
giving a satisfying assignment. For $x=1$ the formula is $B_b$,
which is SAT iff some orientation bit is zero.
There are $4q$ OR gates, hence $8q$ binary plus $8q$ ternary
clauses; the guard cycle adds $5q+5$ binaries and $B_b$
adds $q+4$ ternaries. New variables are $4q+q+4+4q=9q+4$.
All guard and OR values are uniquely determined by $x$ and
the original values.
\end{proof}
For each consistent parity vector, $\operatorname{rank}(A)=q-1$ on
$q+1$ variables, hence precisely four affine assignments.
The exact target model-count identity is
\[
\#SAT(D)=[\oplus\beta=0]\,2^{q+5}
+[b\ne1^q]\,2^{q+1}\#SAT(F_b). \tag{6}
\]

\section{Structural parameter separation}
Let $h_2$ denote the minimum \emph{raw strong syntactic 2CNF}
backdoor, the minimum set of variables hitting all ternary clauses
before equivalence normalization or propagation.
For each even $i$, select one top clause
$(t_C\vee e_{i-1}^{\pm}\vee e_i^{\pm})$
from the $i$-th parity quartet.
These $q/2$ ternary clauses have pairwise-disjoint variable supports,
so
\[
\boxed{q/2\le h_2(D)\le k=9q+4.} \tag{7}
\]
The binary-only subformula has no initial units and forces no variable:
all guard copies can be $0$ with all $t_C=1$ and arbitrary source
values, while setting any chosen $t_C=0$ is possible by taking its
$\ell_1$ false. All guard copies can also be $1$.
An SCC computation nonetheless recovers their one equivalence class
of size $5q+5$, without shortening any ternary clause.

On $x=0$, the $4q$ top ternaries remain genuine after restricting
guards; functional elimination of $t_C=\ell_1$ restores a certified
affine quartet encoding with rank $q-1$.
On $x=1$, all OR gates are satisfied, and the $q+4$
guarded clauses become exactly 2CNF.
Thus the discovered representative $x$ is a size-one backdoor into
\emph{normalized affine} $\cup$ \emph{2CNF} for this
recognizable grammar. It is not a size-one raw 2CNF backdoor.
No single initial residual scalar measures the decision cost.

\section{Input-query bound and exact NP-complete endpoint}
For $\beta^*=(1,0,\ldots,0)$ and $b^*=1^q$,
$D_{q,\beta^*,b^*}$ is UNSAT.
Flipping any one of its $q$ parity bits or $q$ chain orientations
produces SAT without changing $n,M,N$ or any unsigned literal IDs.
The changed sign words lie in disjoint local clause groups.
On this UNSAT input, every exact deterministic addressed-word decider
of the fixed language must inspect at least one word in each of
the $2q$ groups, or the read transcript would not change on an
opposite-label neighbor. The orientation-oracle deterministic query
depth is exactly $2q$; the unrestricted full-word input lower bound
is $\Omega(N/\log N)$, matched by a linear source scan.
This is not superlinear post-acquisition time.

\begin{theorem}[KNOWN THEOREM: Schaefer dichotomy]
The Boolean constraint language
$\Gamma=\{\mathrm{OR}_2(x,y),\,E_3(a,b,c):a\oplus b\oplus c=0\}$
has NP-complete satisfiability, although $\mathrm{OR}_2$ alone is
2SAT and $E_3$ alone is solved by Gaussian elimination.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Verification of the six dichotomy tests]
The language is not 0-valid since $\mathrm{OR}_2(0,0)=0$,
nor 1-valid since $E_3(1,1,1)=0$.
$E_3$ is not Horn: $011\land101=001$ is invalid;
not dual Horn: $011\lor101=111$ is invalid;
and not bijunctive: the coordinatewise majority of
$000,011,101$ is $001$, invalid.
$\mathrm{OR}_2$ is not affine because its valid tuples
$01,10,11$ have XOR $00$, invalid.
Thus no polynomial case of Schaefer's dichotomy applies,
and $SAT(\Gamma)$ is NP-complete. Each $E_3$ relation compiles
exactly to four proper ternary clauses. This is a fixed
NP-complete mixed-width endpoint, not a universal time bound.
\end{proof}
