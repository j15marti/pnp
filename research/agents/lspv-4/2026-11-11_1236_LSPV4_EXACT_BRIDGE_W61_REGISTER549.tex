\section*{Exact model and theorem}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be the exact paid Resolution dependency DAG of LSPV-1/579, with $|V|=60$; original SOURCE clauses remain read-only and the one paid but unused AND2 definition is globally charged once but requires no derived-result register. Let $D\subseteq V$ be a downset of previously executed paid nodes, and
\[
 L(D)=\{u\in D:\ \exists v\notin D,\ (u,v)\in E\}.
\]
For any ready $v\notin D$ whose paid parents lie in $D$, put
\[
 r(D,v)=\mathbf{1}\!\left\{\exists u\in L(D)\cap\operatorname{parents}(v):
    \{w\notin D:(u,w)\in E\}=\{v\}\right\}.
\]
A last-use parent may be read, then overwritten atomically by the new clause value. For the byte-nonexpanding policy, additionally require
$|\mathrm{JSON}(C_u)|\geq|\mathrm{JSON}(C_v)|$ for that parent. Write $r_{\rm byte}$ for this restricted test.

\begin{proof}
Any result in $L(D)$ must be retained because some later node depends on it and recomputation is forbidden. A ready node consumes a new register unless a last-use paid parent is overwritten after its premises are read. Therefore the necessary and sufficient one-step capacity test is $|L(D)|+1-r(D,v)\leq b$; the fresh policy sets $r=0$, and the nonexpanding policy uses $r_{\rm byte}$. Induction on $|D|$ reduces existence of a $b$-slot complete schedule to reachability of $V$ from the empty downset in the directed state graph whose edges pass this capacity test. No inference occurrence or shared result is charged twice.

The exhaustive order-ideal search for $b=5$ in the fresh model visits precisely $993{,}927$ downsets, examines $2{,}185{,}554$ admitted transitions and can finish at most 52 of 60 nodes. For $b=4$ in the unrestricted last-use-overwrite model, it visits $181{,}387$ downsets, examines $590{,}930$ admitted transitions and finishes at most 51 of 60 nodes. The nonexpanding model is a subset of unrestricted overwrite, so four slots are impossible there as well.

\end{proof}
