\section{Exact model and quantifiers}
One fixed total uniform indexed-bit random-access verifier $V(F,\pi,z)$ reads every ordinary encoded source formula $F$ of length $N$ on a read-only indexed input. Its publicly fixed, immutable binary proof store has length $L(N)\le N^p$, each proof address and bit is charged, and $z$ ranges through $R=2^{h(N)}\le N^{\rho+o(1)}$ public seeds. Guest work and index space $S_V=N^{s+o(1)}$ and seed time $T_V=N^{v+o(1)}$ are simultaneous ceilings for all $F,\pi,z$ including failed calls. Physical proof keys distinguish stores and bit offsets; virtual child locators, source authentication, ordinary-CNF parsing, index generation, source probes, seed enumeration, guest resets and ancestor state are charged. No word of unrestricted size or semantic oracle is free.

For constants $0\le\sigma<c\le1$, define
\[
A=\lfloor\sigma R\rfloor,\quad B=\lceil cR\rceil,\quad 0\le A<B\le R.
\]
Require on all ordinary sources
\[
\begin{split}
F\in\mathsf{SAT}&\Rightarrow \forall\pi:\ \sum_zV(F,\pi,z)\le A,\\
F\in\mathsf{UNSAT}&\Rightarrow \exists\pi:\ \sum_zV(F,\pi,z)\ge B.
\end{split}\tag{1}
\]
This is an all-seed completeness/soundness gap, not single-seed exact soundness.

\begin{theorem}[Optimal deterministic black-box seed-gap depth]
For a promise input $y\in\{0,1\}^R$ with $|y|\le A$ or $|y|\ge B$, the exact minimum deterministic worst-case number of indexed seed-bit queries is $K=R-(B-A)+1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Ask successive seeds in any order. Output HIGH immediately after $A+1$ ones or LOW after $R-B+1$ zeros. If neither criterion fires, at most $A$ ones and $R-B$ zeros have been observed, so the next query (at latest $A+R-B+1$) must stop, and neither promised class can trigger the wrong criterion.

For the matching lower bound, answer any deterministic adaptive strategy with $A$ ones, followed by $R-B$ zeros at queried positions. After these $K-1$ replies, $B-A$ coordinates remain unseen. Filling every unseen coordinate with zero yields a legal LOW vector of weight $A$; filling them all with one yields a legal HIGH vector of weight $B$. The entire observed adaptive transcript is identical. One more query is necessary. This lower bound is for an unrestricted outcome-vector \emph{black box}, not for an arbitrary SAT verifier allowed to exploit additional seed structure.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sound, complete early-stopped verifier]
Run $V(F,\pi,z)$ on seeds in fixed public order, with fresh guest work and the same immutable proof. Define $W_*$ to accept at $A+1$ accepting seeds and reject at $R-B+1$ rejecting seeds. Then
\[
F\in\mathsf{SAT}\Rightarrow\forall\pi\,W_*(F,\pi)=0,\qquad
F\in\mathsf{UNSAT}\Rightarrow\exists\pi\,W_*(F,\pi)=1.
\tag{2}
\]
It performs at most $K$ calls; time $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N+KT_V\operatorname{polylog}N)$, and peak indexed work $O(S_V\operatorname{polylog}N+\log R)$, including charged resetting.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For SAT every proof has at most $A$ accepting seeds, so the accepting threshold is impossible. For UNSAT take the fixed proof with at least $B$ accepting seeds; it has at most $R-B$ rejecting seeds, making the rejecting threshold impossible. A deficient UNSAT proof with a seed weight strictly between $A$ and $B$ may be accepted or rejected depending on seed order; this is permitted by the existential UNSAT quantifier. All calls run under a uniform clock and are restored to genuinely blank guest work by epoch tags or a charged sparse reset.
\end{proof}

Let $Q_z(F,\pi)$ be the physical proof cells read by the actual seed-$z$ execution, and define
\[
Q_*(F,\pi)=\bigcup_{z\ \mathrm{executed\ by}\ W_*} Q_z(F,\pi)
\ \subseteq\ \bigcup_{z<R}Q_z(F,\pi). \tag{3}
\]
The \emph{stopped} union is the relevant information budget. Local child labels, per-seed maxima and unverified logical aliases are not valid substitutes.

\begin{theorem}[Stopped-bit first-read compilation; conditional]
Assume additionally that for every ordinary UNSAT formula there exists a successful $W_*$ proof with at most $q(N)\le(a+o(1))\log_2N$ \emph{distinct physical cells} in $Q_*$, with public efficiently computed cap. Put $K=N^{\kappa+o(1)}$. Then one fixed deterministic ordinary-SAT solver in the \emph{same indexed} model has
\[
T_D=N^{\max(1,\kappa+v+a)+o(1)},\qquad
S_D=N^{\max(0,s)+o(1)}.\tag{4}
\]
If $S_V=N^{o(1)}$, the published Williams SAT lower bound implies the necessary inequality
\[
\boxed{\kappa+v+a\ge 2\cos(\pi/7)=1.8019377358\ldots .}\tag{5}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each of the $2^q$ binary strings, simulate $W_*$ with a fresh proof-bit map. On the first query to any physical proof store/bit-address key, consume the next candidate bit, retaining it under that key; repeat visits return the saved bit. Reject an over-budget $(q+1)$st first read. Every accepting map extends to a genuine immutable proof, so SAT soundness of (2) excludes false acceptance. Each UNSAT good proof has its first-read sequence enumerated. Epoch-tag guest work by $(\mathrm{candidate},\mathrm{seed})$, costing $O(\log(2^q K+1))$ bits per previously reached indexed guest cell; the current candidate's $q$ map entries cost $O(q\log L)$ bits. Input parsing costs $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ once. Every source query, guest reset, proof map operation and seed transition is executed and paid. The Williams theorem excludes a strictly smaller deterministic SAT time exponent only when the \emph{same resulting machine} has subpolynomial indexed space. Equality is not excluded.
\end{proof}

For fixed $c-\sigma<1$, $K=(1-c+\sigma)R+O(1)=\Theta(R)$; a constant-factor improvement does not remove exponent $\rho$. At the exceptional perfect-gap endpoint $(\sigma,c)=(0,1)$, $K=1$, so this black-box compilation legitimately removes the seed enumeration exponent. This does not provide a universal perfect-gap verifier.

\begin{counterexample}[Full union larger than stopped union]
Take $R=8,A=2,B=6$. On an easy authenticated UNSAT promise, let seeds $0,1,2$ all query physical bit $p_0$, and seeds $3,\ldots,7$ query distinct bits $p_1,\ldots,p_5$. An all-ones proof produces eight accepting seeds, but $W_*$ accepts after the first three calls with physical proof union size one; full eight-seed evaluation reads six different cells. On the separate SAT promised source return acceptance on exactly the first two seeds independent of proof; all proofs are rejected by $W_*$. This is an \emph{abstract restricted verifier}, not an all-input SAT method. It proves that ARC201's all-seed log-union sufficient premise can be strictly stronger than the stopped-run premise.
\end{counterexample}
\begin{counterexample}[Probabilistic seed shortcut lacks exact soundness]
With $R=8,A=2,B=6$, a legal SAT seed vector can have two accepted coordinates. A single existential accepting seed is unsound. Sampling two distinct uniform seeds and requiring both to accept has false-positive probability $\binom22/\binom82=1/28>0$. Statistical sampling alone cannot replace a zero-error proof checker.
\end{counterexample}
