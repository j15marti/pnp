\noindent\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT with explicit finite-certificate dependency; independent research review pending. P versus NP remains OPEN.}

\begin{abstract}
The recovered occurrence-halving compiler preserves not only resolution hardness but also polynomial-calculus degree, up to a constant ceiling of four. For every unsatisfiable proper exact-$(4,4)$ source $F$, and every field $\F$,
\[
\degpc^{\F}(F)\le \degpc^{\F}(C(F))
\le\max\{\degpc^{\F}(F),4\}.
\]
The upper bound uses a freshly generated and replayed width-three resolution derivation of the actual padding gadget's three forced ports. Its ancestor subproof has 69 nodes. Thus the existing compiled cubic Tseitin contradictions have ordinary polynomial-calculus refutations over $\F_2$ of degree at most four and polynomial expanded size, including after variable renaming and complementation. A self-contained bounded-degree search argument gives a polynomial-time sound refuter without a compiler decomposition. This removes global decomposition ambiguity as a necessary obstacle to refuting this specific family; it does not solve compiler-image recognition or arbitrary SAT.
\end{abstract}

\section{Recovered foundations and what changes}
The original LBO3SAT-0031 compiler replaces each source variable by four clones, pairs positive and negative occurrences, adds a directed four-cycle of implications, and pads each triple of implications using one fresh copy of $Q$. Here $Q$ is the actual 15-variable, 19-clause gadget obtained by deleting $(x_1\lor x_7\lor x_{13})$ from CLAUDE-0021's witness $W_1$. Its three ports are forced false. For a proper exact-$(4,4)$ source with $n$ variables,
\[
 v=24n,\qquad m=32n=4v/3.
\]
The output is proper exact-$(2,2)$, not necessarily clause-monotone. A literal/constant substitution identifies each clone group with its source variable and sends gadget variables to zero. PROOF-0034 proves the resolution projection; LBO3SAT-0032 separately proves minimality for a structured cubic Tseitin compilation with specified endpoint-local pairing and connectivity hypotheses. Those results are retained, not re-proved as new claims here.

LBO3SAT-0084 recognized fixed $Q$ copies in polynomial time and inverted some small anonymized compiler instances. It left global decomposition ambiguity open. The new point is that deciding the known odd-charge images does not require recovering a unique global decomposition: bounded-degree algebraic proof search suffices. Conversely, the same compiler preserves degree lower bounds when a suitable hard algebraic source is supplied. The two uses share one exact transport theorem.

\section{Precise model and theorem}
A Boolean variable has values $0$ (false) and $1$ (true). Work in the ordinary polynomial ring $\F[X]$, with Boolean axioms $x^2-x$. The polynomial for a clause $D$ is
\[
 p_D=\prod_{x\in D^+}(1-x)\prod_{\neg x\in D^-}x.
\]
A polynomial-calculus derivation starts with clause and Boolean axioms, and uses linear combinations and multiplication by a single variable. Degree is the largest total degree of any expanded monomial appearing on any line. Size counts expanded monomial occurrences, not just proof lines. This is the ordinary PC model underlying Definition 2.1 of Mik\v{s}a--Nordstr\"om, after reversing their Boolean truth-value convention. No implicit multilinearization is charged as a free inference here.

\begin{theorem}[Constant-ceiling degree retraction]\label{thm:retraction}
Fix the actual gadget $Q$ specified in Appendix A. For every field $\F$, every unsatisfiable proper exact-$(4,4)$ 3-CNF $F$, and every instance $C(F)$ of the historical four-clone compiler,
\[
\degpc^{\F}(F)\le\degpc^{\F}(C(F))
\le\max\{\degpc^{\F}(F),4\}.
\tag{1}
\]
For the uncomplemented compiler representation, a supplied source proof lifts with $O(n)$ additional lines and expanded monomial occurrences. No minimality or expander hypothesis is needed for (1). The constant four is a certified upper ceiling, not a claim of optimal degree for $C(F)$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The fixed gadget certificate}
The fresh exhaustive witness audit confirms that $W_1$ is UNSAT, each of its 20 clause deletions is satisfiable, and $Q$ has exactly 244 models, all setting its ports $1,7,13$ to zero. More specifically, bounded-width resolution saturation derives
\[
 Q\vdash\neg x_1,\quad Q\vdash\neg x_7,\quad Q\vdash\neg x_{13}
\]
using clauses of width at most three. Pruning to the three target ancestors leaves 19 input clauses and 50 resolution inferences, or 69 nodes total. The complete explicit finite certificate is included in the reproducibility package. This certificate, rather than a claim that all semantic consequences are cheaply available, is the fixed dependency used below.

For an inference resolving $A\lor x$ and $B\lor\neg x$ into the non-tautological clause $R=A\cup B$, extend each parent polynomial by the missing false-literal factors. They become
\[
 (1-x)p_R,\qquad x p_R.
\]
Their sum is $p_R$. Each extension is a sequence of variable multiplications and linear combinations implementing multiplication by $(1-y)$. Its degree is at most $|R|+1\le4$. This identity holds over the integers, hence over every field. The implementation checks all 50 retained identities with integer coefficients independently of its characteristic-two proof generator. We therefore obtain the three port equations in PC degree at most four over every field.

\subsection{Forward simulation: remove padding and identify clones}
A padded implication $(\neg a\lor b\lor p)$ has polynomial
\[
 a(1-b)(1-p).
\]
Using the derived port equation $p=0$, obtain $ap(1-b)$ and add it to this axiom. The result is $a(1-b)=0$. Degree never exceeds four, and the new operations after deriving $p$ need degree at most three.

Transitivity along a directed clone cycle follows from the exact identity
\[
 (1-c)a(1-b)+a\,b(1-c)=a(1-c).
\tag{2}
\]
The intermediate degree is at most three. Paths in the four-cycle have constant length. For a representative clone $r$, derive both $a(1-r)$ and $r(1-a)$ and subtract:
\[
 a(1-r)-r(1-a)=a-r.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus each clone can be replaced by its representative in a source-clause polynomial. A telescoping product identity performs the replacement one literal at a time, multiplying a linear difference by the two other false-literal factors. Proper source clauses have three distinct source variables, so this needs degree at most three. All renamed source axioms, and their Boolean axioms, are now available. Copy the supplied source proof on representative variables. Local work uses a constant number of bounded-size lines per clone/gadget/clause, giving the upper bound and the $O(n)$ overhead.

\subsection{Backward simulation: projection needs Boolean axioms}
Apply the historical substitution $\sigma$ to a target PC proof. A source-clause image is its source axiom. An internal $Q$ axiom maps to zero because the all-zero assignment satisfies $Q$. A padded implication maps to
\[
 x(1-x)=-(x^2-x).
\tag{4}
\]
\emph{It is not the zero polynomial in the ordinary polynomial ring.} It is a scalar multiple of a source Boolean axiom. Target Boolean axioms similarly map to source Boolean axioms or zero. Every projected linear combination remains legal; multiplication maps to multiplication by the source variable or to zero. Variable identification and constants do not increase degree. This proves the first inequality of (1). Equation (4) is the necessary correction to a purely semantic tautology-deletion argument in this algebraic model.\qed

\section{A uniform consequence for the original Tseitin family}
\begin{corollary}[Degree-four algebraic refutation]\label{cor:tseitin}
Let $T(G,c)$ be the standard 3-CNF encoding of an odd-charge Tseitin system on a simple cubic graph. Then
\[
 \degpc^{\F_2}(C(T(G,c)))\le4.
\tag{5}
\]
There are $O(v)$ proof lines and $O(v^2)$ expanded monomial occurrences. These bounds persist under arbitrary variable permutations, independent variable complements, clause reorderings and literal reorderings. They apply, in particular, to the structured minimally-UNSAT compiler family in LBO3SAT-0032.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
At each vertex, the sum over $\F_2$ of its four clause polynomials is the linear equation
\[
 \sum_{e\ni u}x_e+c_u.
\]
This is an exact polynomial identity of degree at most three during the summation. Summing over all vertices cancels every edge twice and leaves the constant $\sum_u c_u=1$. Thus the source has a degree-three PC refutation. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:retraction}.

A sequential sum of vertex equations has $O(v)$ lines but intermediate linear polynomials can have $O(v)$ terms; the safe total-size bound is $O(v^2)$, not $O(v)$. Renaming preserves degree. Complementing a variable substitutes $1-y$ for it. A multiplication by $1-y$ is simulated using one variable multiplication and one linear combination. At fixed degree four, expansion produces only a constant factor in monomial count. All operations remain ordinary-ring PC operations.
\end{proof}

For suitable cubic expanders, the recovered resolution result still gives
\[
 \Res(C(T(G,c)))\ge2^{\Omega(v)}.
\tag{6}
\]
The encoded bit length is $L=\Theta(v\log v)$, so this is $2^{\Omega(L/\log L)}$. Equations (5) and (6) concern two different proof models and are compatible. The source-level ease of mod-two equations is established background; the new project-specific content is its degree-controlled transport through the actual forced-port compiler, including anonymization and proof search without a supplied decomposition.

\section{Why global gadget decomposition is not needed for refutation}
\begin{lemma}[Fixed-degree search over a finite field]\label{lem:search}
For fixed $d$ and a fixed finite field, the existence of an ordinary PC refutation of degree at most $d$ is decidable, and such a refutation can be generated, in polynomial time in the encoded input length.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There are $M={v+d\choose d}$ ordinary monomials of degree at most $d$, including powers. Identify degree-$d$ polynomials with coefficient vectors of length $M$. Start with the span $V$ of input axioms of degree at most $d$. Compute, by elimination, a basis of
\[
 V_{<d}=V\cap\{p:\deg p\le d-1\}.
\]
Multiply every such basis vector by every variable and add the resulting vectors to $V$. Repeat until the span stops growing. There are at most $M$ strict dimension increases. All vector spaces and matrices have polynomial dimension for fixed $d$. Finite-field coefficients have constant bit size.

Soundness follows because every added vector has a PC derivation, and degree does not exceed $d$. At the fixed point, induction on the lines of any degree-$d$ PC proof shows that every line belongs to $V$: linear combinations are in the span, and multiplication uses a member of $V_{<d}$. Test whether $1\in V$. Provenance can be retained as polynomial-size elimination and multiplication instructions. Importantly, compute the full intersection $V_{<d}$; filtering an arbitrary basis for low-degree basis elements is not equivalent.
\end{proof}

Taking $d=4$ yields one fixed uniform sound refuter for all the scrambled images in Corollary~\ref{cor:tseitin}. Its input is just the ordinary CNF. It needs no graph, charge vector, gadget partition or clone map. The proof of completeness on those images uses a compiler map, but the searching algorithm does not receive that map. This is a distinction between proving that short proofs exist and explicitly supplying a general procedure to find them; both are provided here.

On arbitrary formulas the refuter may return UNKNOWN. Attaching an exhaustive fallback produces an exact SAT decider that is polynomial-time on this entire promised family, without being claimed polynomial on all SAT. No time-space contradiction follows: the bounded-degree search can use a large fixed polynomial of time and space. Full compiler inversion and exact membership in its image remain separate questions.

\section{How this strengthens the lower-bound construction}
The positive transfer is not limited to parity sources. Equation (1) states, for every field and every eligible UNSAT source, that a PC degree lower bound survives the compiler. In particular, a family of proper exact-$(4,4)$ sources with $\degpc(F_n)=\Omega(n)$ would yield exact-$(2,2)$ targets with linear PC degree. Applying the degree-to-size theorem in Mik\v{s}a--Nordstr\"om, Theorem 2.2 (attributed there to Impagliazzo et al.), would then give exponential PC/PCR size for this target family. This is a conditional source-instantiation consequence, not a claim that such an eligible source was constructed here.

The next useful task is to select a non-affine source with a genuine algebraic degree lower bound and verify a degree-preserving route into the compiler's exact source syntax. The graph functional pigeonhole results in the cited paper are relevant literature, but their clauses and occurrence counts must be checked rather than silently treated as exact-$(4,4)$. Even a successful stronger proof-system lower bound would not, by itself, exclude every polynomial-time SAT algorithm. Non-affineness alone is not a hardness theorem.

\paragraph{Failed approach retained.}
A deliberately restricted static span of disjoint-monomial multiples of the $Q$ clause axioms did not derive the ports at tested caps 4,5,6,7. That is not full PC search: it forbids iterated cancellation followed by multiplication of newly derived lower-degree polynomials. It is not a degree lower bound. The subsequent width-three resolution derivation and degree-four ordinary-PC certificates demonstrate why that restricted failed search must not be promoted to a hardness claim.

\paragraph{What was not benchmarked.}
The full metadata-free degree-four search of Lemma~\ref{lem:search} was not run on the 720-variable formulas. These experiments build certificates with metadata, then verify them without metadata. The polynomial-time metadata-free consequence rests on the explicit finite-dimensional search proof, not a reported runtime of that generic search.

\paragraph{Barrier and cross-domain audit.}
This is an algebraic proof-system result, not a nonrelativizing separation. No escape from relativization, algebrization or Natural Proofs is claimed. Coefficient-size issues in real/rational sum-of-squares automatability do not transfer to our fixed finite field. The characteristic-two upper bound must not be asserted for all characteristics; only the degree sandwich is field-general. An all-algorithm lower bound for one fixed NP decision problem would suffice for P versus NP, with no extra input-family broadening step.
