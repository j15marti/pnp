\paragraph{Fixed rules and charged evidence.}
Source CNF clauses are free axioms. Each unit-cost binary AND3/XOR inference contributes one node to $W_R$. Each source-authenticated \texttt{ROW} macro explicitly names the four clauses over three distinct variables whose eight-row conjunction is exactly $a\oplus b\oplus c=d$. It expands to \emph{three} charged binary AND3 nodes, never one free four-premise step. Every \texttt{XOR} record names two earlier ROW/XOR records; a repeated parent is counted \emph{twice} and cancels over GF(2). With $r$ ROW records and $t$ XOR records, the macro certificate has $K=r+t$ records but $q=3r+t$ paid logical nodes. Signed row variables, RHS values, source indices, antecedents, declaration counts and proof grammar are checked against the original source without importing an SAT oracle. The final equation must equal $0=1$. This fragment is sound within $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ but does not remove its other rules RES, LOCAL-CONVERT or conservative DEF.

\begin{theorem}[Fully charged low-space affine certificate replay]
Let $M=N+P$ be the binary length of an input CNF together with a supplied ROW/XOR certificate. There is a fixed deterministic indexed-input verifier that accepts exactly the authenticated contradiction proofs in this fragment with \emph{all-phase} peak writable space $O(\log^2(M+2))$ bits and worst-case native time $M^{O(\log(M+2))}$. The cost of finding a proof is not included; the theorem does not imply polynomial-time SAT decision or a machine-time lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First validate the entire source and certificate grammar by rereading the read-only input. Count clauses and validate signed decimal variable IDs by retaining substring pointers and lengths instead of allocating an array indexed by the possibly enormous declared variable bound. Check every ROW locally by all eight valuations of its exact three named variables. Validate each XOR operand as a backward macro reference. This needs only logarithmically many writable pointer, counter and truth-table bits.

Number records in topological order $0,\dots,K-1$ and let $f$ be the final index. Define a conceptual strictly upper-triangular $K\times K$ adjacency matrix $A$ over $\mathbb F_2$ whose entry $A_{uv}$ is the \emph{parity} of antecedent-slot occurrences from $u$ to $v$. In particular, duplicate operands give $A_{uv}=0$. Because $A^K=0$, each source ROW $i$ contributes to the final row with coefficient
\[
 c_i=\sum_{\ell=0}^{K-1}(A^\ell)_{i,f}\pmod2.
\]
This counts all directed source-to-output proof paths modulo two, including sharing and cancellations. Form the conceptual $2K\times2K$ matrix
\[
 B=\begin{pmatrix}A&I_K\\0&I_K\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad c_i=(B^K)_{i,K+f}.
\]
The identity crossing gives one and only one padded path for each original source-to-output path of length less than $K$. Evaluate the requested entry of $B^e$ recursively with
\[
 P_0(u,v)=[u=v],\quad P_1(u,v)=B_{uv},\quad
 P_e(u,v)=\bigoplus_{z=0}^{2K-1}
 P_{\lfloor e/2\rfloor}(u,z)
 P_{\lceil e/2\rceil}(z,v).
\]
The recursion has $O(\log K)$ levels, with $O(\log M)$ bits for its endpoints, middle-index counter, exponent and accumulator at each level. All $B_{uv}$ bits are obtained by charged proof rescans. Hence all phases stay within $O(\log^2(M+2))$ writable bits; the number of recursive calls, including paid indexed source queries, is $M^{O(\log M)}$.

Finally iterate over actual source-literal occurrences. For each named variable, recompute the $c_i$ and XOR the coefficients of ROWs containing it; the resulting final row must have zero coefficient at every variable. Separately XOR ROW RHS bits weighted by $c_i$ and require one. Unused declared variables never occur in an authenticated ROW. Every accepted certificate therefore derives precisely $0=1$ from the named original clauses. Conversely every correctly formatted ROW/XOR contradiction passes these calculations.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Falsifiers and precise classifications.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[] \textbf{F1, FALSIFIED (restricted):} a supplied ROW/XOR certificate inherently needs $K$ simultaneously writable coefficients for deterministic checking. Matrix-power rescanning gives a quasipolynomial-time checker with $O(\log^2 M)$ writable bits.
\item[] \textbf{F2, FALSIFIED:} duplicate XOR antecedent references may be collapsed into one ordinary graph edge. Multiplicity must be reduced modulo two.
\item[] \textbf{F3, FALSIFIED:} an even-charge SAT source or a sign-flipped SAT neighbor with a forged local row can be accepted. Authenticated row truth tables and final RHS reject these controls.
\item[] \textbf{F4, OPEN:} this restricted verifier implies a polynomial-time $N^{1/2}$-space universal SAT decider or B2/B3. It is quasipolynomial in proof size, requires the proof to be supplied, and does not cover other mixed rules.
\end{enumerate}
The all-phase materialized-child space envelope still is $s'\le\max\{\rho,\eta,\lambda,\lambda s\}$, where $\rho$ includes \emph{every} parsing, source access, order discovery, proof materialization and conversion phase. A representation that first stores all $M$ source/certificate bits writable has peak exponent at least one in $M$, regardless of later deallocation. An indexed-read-only proof certificate makes the present restricted verifier's low-space bound possible; neither it nor a family-local Tseitin recognizer establishes B2. The latter requires $T_A^{\mathrm{wc}}(N)$ maximized over \emph{all} binary strings up to length $N$, not over the tested formulas.
