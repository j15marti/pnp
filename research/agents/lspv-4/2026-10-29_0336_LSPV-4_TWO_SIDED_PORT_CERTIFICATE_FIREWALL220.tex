\section{PROPOSE: four falsifiable mechanisms}
\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{M1, proof-carrying semantic repair.} Perhaps every constant-output exact port relation has a polynomial-size, polynomial-time checkable certificate, even if its constructor is expensive. This would imply $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ for the guarded family below; no unconditional construction is established.
\item[]\textbf{M2, SAT-side witnesses.} A witness proves the port value $1$ feasible. This is \textbf{PROVED}, but cannot by itself prove port value $1$ infeasible.
\item[]\textbf{M3, affine dual repair.} For XOR systems, an inconsistency row combination certifies exclusion. This is \textbf{PROVED/RESTRICTED} with explicit linear-time-in-certificate verification, demonstrating that the obstruction is not universal across tractable languages.
\item[]\textbf{M4, recursive certificate bookkeeping.} A chain with polynomial-size certificates and power-short physical children has polynomial total verification cost, but only if certificates are actually provided/generated within the claimed budget. This is \textbf{PROVED/CONDITIONAL}, not a general SAT construction.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD: exact projection can be tiny but its negative fiber is coNP-complete}
Let $F(x)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(\ell_{i1}\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3})$ be an arbitrary proper 3CNF. Remove inactive variable names and introduce port $y$ and private $u_i$. Set
\[
G_F(x,u,y)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}(\neg y\vee\ell_{i1}\vee u_i)\wedge(\neg u_i\vee\ell_{i2}\vee\ell_{i3}).
\]
All clauses have three distinct variable names and the incidence graph is connected for $m\ge1$. The source is always satisfiable at $y=0,u_i=0$, while
\[
R_F(y):=\exists x,u\,G_F(x,u,y)\quad\text{satisfies}\quad
\boxed{R_F(0)=1,\ R_F(1)=\operatorname{SAT}(F).}
\]
This follows by existentially eliminating each $u_i$ from its two clauses at $y=1$; it reconstructs the original ternary clause. Thus the port-one query is NP-complete, and its complement, port-one infeasibility, is coNP-complete. The complete interface has only two output bits. The source $G_F$ remains in the SAT side on both source answer classes.

\begin{theorem}[Two-sided exact-port certification barrier]\label{thm:certificate}
Suppose a deterministic algorithm $V$ runs in time polynomial in $|G|+|R|+|\pi|$ and is sound for certificates of \emph{exact} one-port relations $R$ on every authenticated $G=G_F$: if $V(G,R,\pi)$ accepts, then $R=\{y:\exists z\,G(z,y)\}$. Suppose also that for every such $G_F$, its correct relation has an accepted certificate $\pi$ of bit length at most a fixed polynomial in $|G_F|$. Then
\[\boxed{\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}.}\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Given $F$, construct $G_F$ in polynomial time. Guess a certificate $\pi$ for the two-bit reported relation $R=\{0\}$. Accept iff $V(G_F,\{0\},\pi)$ accepts. Soundness ensures acceptance only when $R_F(1)=0$, namely $F$ is unsatisfiable. Completeness and the polynomial length bound ensure some accepted guess on every unsatisfiable $F$. Thus UNSAT lies in NP. Since UNSAT is coNP-complete under polynomial many-one reductions, $\mathrm{coNP}\subseteq\mathrm{NP}$ and, by complement, equality follows.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.} A solver may use \emph{decision-only} equivalence and bypass the entire port. The theorem does not prohibit superpolynomial certificates, polynomial checking in certificate length with exponential proof length, restricted input grammars, or non-uniform oracle access. It does not establish $\mathrm{NP}\ne\mathrm{coNP}$. A polynomially efficient \emph{constructor} of the exact port would imply the stronger $P=NP$ by the previous report. The present theorem instead rules out assuming universally short and sound certificates without confronting the separate $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$ question.

\section{Positive control: exact two-sided certificates for GF(2) systems}
\begin{proposition}[Affine complete certificates]
For $A\in\mathbb F_2^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb F_2^m$, an exact decision certificate can always be constructed in polynomial time. If $Ax=b$ is consistent, the certificate is a vector $x$ with $Ax=b$. If inconsistent, it is a vector $\lambda\in\mathbb F_2^m$ with $\lambda^TA=0$ and $\lambda^Tb=1$. Both are polynomial-size and verifiable with $O(mn)$ bit operations. Hence a guarded port whose $y=1$ fiber is $Ax=b$ has a polynomially constructible exact two-bit interface and a two-sided proof object.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Gaussian elimination over $\mathbb F_2$ constructs a satisfying vector or derives the contradictory augmented row $(0\mid1)$. Tracking the row operations expresses the latter as a linear combination of original rows, with combination coefficients $\lambda$. The two certificate checks are direct matrix--vector products. The input language restriction is essential: no comparable general dual certificate for arbitrary 3CNF was established.
\end{proof}

\section{Resource ledgers and recursive proof accounting}
Write $N$ for the canonical unpadded physical length of $F$, $C$ for the entire printed length of $G_F$, and $m$ for clause count. The construction uses exactly $2m$ ternary clauses and $n+m+1$ variables, so $C=O(N\log(N+2))$ including headers, binary names, signs, and output. An indexable RAM with $\Theta(\log N)$-bit words streams the output in $O(C\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit-operation time. A fixed-k multitape TM with a sequential read-only input head, a writable output tape and a separate bounded counter tape does so in $T_k=O(C\operatorname{polylog}N)$, counting $S_k=O(C)$ visited writable cells if output is counted. A conservatively implemented read-only-input-plus-one-work-tape machine may take $O(C^2\operatorname{polylog}N)$ if counters must be revisited along the growing output tape; a two-fully-writable-tape machine can leave the source intact and likewise use a conservative quadratic-time construction. These are \emph{construction upper bounds}, not the cost of solving the port query.

Under the explicit project simulation convention,
\[
T_1=O\bigl(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))\bigr)=O(N^2\operatorname{polylog}N),
\quad P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))=O(N\operatorname{polylog}N).
\]
These are upper bounds for a single-tape simulator of the stated fixed-k constructor, including original input and physical output. A fixed universal TM has an additional model-specific interpretation cost which has not been assigned a numerical exponent. None of the restricted sequential-head DISJ lower bounds, RAM forbidden regions, or same-run SAT/UNSAT resource claims transports to this construction.

For a hypothetically \emph{supplied and sound} chain $N_{i+1}\le N_i^\lambda$, $0<\lambda<1$, of physical children with certificates $|\pi_i|\le N_i^d$ and per-stage verification time $O((N_i+|\pi_i|)^e)$, the number of levels is $O(\log\log N)$ and total verification time is
\[O\!\left(N^{e\max(1,d)}\log\log N\right),\]
with conservative peak physical space $O(N^{\max(1,d)}\log\log N)$ if all ancestors and proofs are retained. Producing or discovering the certificates, or repairing a failed trial, is not covered by these verifier-only estimates and must be charged independently. On the guarded general-3CNF family, supplying polynomial-size two-sided semantic certificates is precisely the unproved hypothesis of Theorem~\ref{thm:certificate}.


\section{Additive independent referee: strict exact-(2,2) port-wire chain}
LSPV-2's contemporaneous \texttt{EXACT22-PORT-WIRES221} source archive was inspected separately from the guarded construction above. Its published family has $15k$ variables and $20k$ proper ternary clauses, with each variable occurring twice with each sign. The reported exact source-port relation is equality of $k$ designated bits, and the source is all-SAT. The fixed four-clause ordinary-3CNF child has $384$ encoded bits in the selected ASCII DIMACS convention, but is \emph{not} strict exact-$(2,2)$. No NP-hard contextual port is embedded by this family.

\begin{proposition}[Logarithmic-workspace syntactic recognizer for the chain grammar]
For the explicit $20$-clause W1 template and adjacent-block negative-occurrence swaps in the LSPV-2 generator, a fixed deterministic machine can authenticate the \emph{exact canonical sorted} $k$-block source representation using $O(\log N)$ work cells and $O(N^3\operatorname{polylog}N)$ steps on a sequential read-only input, where $N=\Theta(k\log k)$ is the complete source length. It can then output the constant-size ordinary equality child. The claim concerns recognition and construction on the authenticated promise class; it does not prove the separately required exact port semantics from scratch.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
From the header verify that $n=15k$ and $m=20k$, with $k\ge2$. For each of the $20k$ candidate clauses identify its block and constant template slot. Apply the prescribed local sign-preserving swap using the predecessor/successor block index, then sort its three signed literals by the published canonical clause key. All candidate clauses are distinct: unmodified clauses have disjoint block-local names; each cross-block clause contains two local nonsignal names that identify its own block and slot, and its foreign signal name cannot cause a duplicate. To compute the clause of global lexicographic rank $r$, scan the $20k$ candidates and, for each, count how many candidates precede it. One candidate has count $r$. Compare its three literals to the next source clause, validating delimiters, headers, and EOF as well. This requires a constant number of $O(\log k)$-bit indices and clause names, not a stored list. There are at most $O(k^3)$ candidate comparisons over the whole input. Even with a conservative polylogarithmic overhead for arithmetic on a fixed tape, the time is $O(N^3\operatorname{polylog}N)$, and the read-only input head traverses the source once. Reject malformed inputs before printing the child. The child is four clauses on four variables and has a constant printed length. The input remains charged in physical footprint.
\end{proof}

An independently written rank-selection validator checked each of the 280 canonical clauses for $k=2,3,4,5$ against the archived LSPV-2 generator, with zero mismatches. The original LSPV-2 test script was independently rerun and its raw JSON reproduced byte-identically, SHA-256 \texttt{03fd2e0cbeb0e1e6}\allowbreak\texttt{6ed2d97f50c916ef}\allowbreak\texttt{54525339573d3636}\allowbreak\texttt{c6061c42a1a6c1e8}. This is an independent check of grammar generation and the model-level authentication route, not a Coq formalization of the relational gadget. Under the stated one-tape simulation, the recognizer has $T_1=O(N^4\operatorname{polylog}N)$ and physical footprint $P_1=O(N)$. No RAM-native lower bound follows.
