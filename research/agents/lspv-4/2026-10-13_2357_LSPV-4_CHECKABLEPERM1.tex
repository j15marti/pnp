\section{Literature and transfer classification}

\textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to an unconditional time lower bound absent a new
model-matched theorem.

\section{Exact implementation defect}
Let a record contain a graph $X=(V,E)$, an ordered tuple $T$ of declared tree
edge indices, a tuple $C$ of declared cotree indices, odd target dimension
$d$, and a polynomial-basis modulus.  The PERMEXT1 recognizer checked that
$X$ possessed \emph{some} spanning tree and that $|E|-|V|+1=d$.  It did not
check that $T$ itself was a spanning tree, that $T$ and $C$ were disjoint and
partitioned $E$, that their entries were distinct and in range, or that graph
endpoints were safe.

Therefore the published executable recognition claim is \RET{} at code level.
This does not refute the semantic theorem on promised-valid inputs.  It shows
that its prior off-image behavior was not established and, for one malformed
endpoint, was not total.

\section{Strict recognizer theorem}
Define \textsc{StrictGoldRecord} to accept exactly when:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $d\ge3$ is odd, the target width equals $d$, and the declared degree-$d$
polynomial is irreducible over $\F$;
\item every edge has two distinct endpoints in $V$ and no undirected edge is
duplicated;
\item $|E|-|V|+1=d$;
\item $T$ has $|V|-1$ distinct in-range indices, $C$ has $d$ distinct
in-range indices, and $T\cap C=\varnothing$, $T\cup C=\{0,\ldots,|E|-1\}$;
\item union--find on the edges indexed by $T$ finds no cycle and one component.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[strict typed recognition]
\textsc{StrictGoldRecord} is decidable in time polynomial in the encoded record
length.  Every accepted record has exactly the declared connected spanning
tree and cotree partition, cycle rank $d$, safe endpoints, and a valid field
encoding.  Every string violating one of the five typed conditions is rejected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Items 1--4 are finite parsing, sorting/set, arithmetic, and polynomial
irreducibility checks.  In item 5, union--find rejects precisely when a declared
tree edge closes a cycle.  An acyclic set of $|V|-1$ edges with one resulting
component is a spanning tree.  Conversely every valid declared spanning tree
passes those checks.  Items 3--4 then make the remaining $d$ edges exactly the
cotree.  All operations are polynomial in the explicit bit length.  Every
failure branch returns rejection before any endpoint is dereferenced by the
witness lift, so recognition is total.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[polynomial witness lift on the accepted subtype]
Let $c\in\F^V$ have even parity, let $z\in\F^d$ be the declared cotree value,
and let $b=z^3$ in the accepted field encoding.  There is exactly one
$x\in\F^E$ with $B_Xx=c$ and $\pi_Cx=z$, and it is computable by assigning
cotree edges and eliminating tree leaves.  For odd $d$, cubing permutes
$\mathbb F_{2^d}$ and
\[
 z=b^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3}.
\]
Thus the strict recognizer preserves PERMEXT1's uniform polynomial source
witness extension.  Malformed/off-image strings are rejected.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
After cotree values are fixed, the incidence equations restricted to the
spanning tree have rank $|V|-1$; even charge is exactly their one consistency
condition.  Leaf elimination gives existence, uniqueness, and a linear-time
tree solve.  For odd $d$, $\gcd(3,2^d-1)=1$ and the displayed exponent is the
inverse of $3$ modulo $2^d-1$.
\end{proof}

Parity must be guaranteed by the typed syntax used in PROMISEGATE1: encode the
first $|V|-1$ charge bits and define the last as their XOR.  An arbitrary
declared even-charge circuit or inverse circuit remains a semantic promise and
is not admitted by this repaired subtype.
