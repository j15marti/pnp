\section{Single target, model and four preregistered falsifiers}
The all-input achievable exponent set \(E\) is upward closed: \((t,s)\in E\)
if \emph{one fixed uniform deterministic total exact ordinary-SAT decider}
\(A\), on \emph{every ordinary \(N\)-bit input}, jointly satisfies
\(T_A(N)\le N^{t+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N)\le N^{s+o(1)}\).
We retain the original Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (FLMV)
indexed, read-only-input random-access Turing machine with counted writable
and index tapes, charged source/workspace reads, \emph{all} writes including
unchanged writes, and actual sequential bit work. A write-only output is not a
free readable child-input buffer. Under \(\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\), some finite
\((K,K)\in E\) exists.

The verified strict sufficient FLMV forbidden box is
\[
t<c_*=\frac{241}{200},\qquad
s<d_*=\frac{11642479}{23232400}=\beta(c_*),\qquad
\beta(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right).
\]
A finite, uniform, source-checkable, \emph{jointly} charged route from
cofinally many achievable diagonals into this box would suffice. Route depth
may depend on fixed \(K,A\), not on varying input length or free advice.
The illustrative \( (t,s)\mapsto
(\max\{11/10,3/5+t/2\},\max\{1/2,s/2\})\)
remains \OPEN. The older universal cosine-product endpoint is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.

Before consulting the original FLMV/Williams primary papers, the run fixed:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{H1 (composition improvement):} give an explicit
source-read and workspace ledger for \emph{nested} virtual child accessors,
instead of adding only the outermost generator surcharge.
\item \textbf{H2 (test):} exhibit two semantically correct, power-short
\emph{ordinary SAT encodings} where literal repeated reconstruction of
virtual child bits makes even one complete leaf input scan exceed the
FLMV time threshold.
\item \textbf{H3 (falsifier):} allow a source-specific retained summary and
test whether the apparent compulsory nested access cost disappears.
\item \textbf{H4 (alignment):} test whether the result proves an
all-input favorable successor or fixed FLMV-hard-image coverage.
\end{enumerate}
H1 is proved for a declared simulation; H2 has an exact asymptotic example
and reproducible finite controls; H3 \emph{does} eliminate the apparent
universal lower bound by a cheap parity summary; H4 fails. The improvements
are \emph{accounting precision and a scoped counterexample}, not an ARC edge.

\section{Two-level virtual-input resource lemma}
A parent \(F\) has actual encoded size \(N\). The first virtual ordinary
SAT string \(Y(F)\) has length
\(L_1\le N^{\theta_1+o(1)}\) with \(\theta_1<1\).
Its \(j\)-th bit is served by \(G_1(F,j)\), with at most
\(N^{g_1+o(1)}\) elementary bit operations and
\(N^{\eta_1+o(1)}\) writable/index space, \emph{including the source
addresses and the entire preprocessing charged on each invocation}.
A second virtual ordinary SAT string \(Z(Y)\) has length
\(L_2\le L_1^{\theta_2+o(1)}\). Its bit-service code \(G_2(Y,i)\)
takes \(L_1^{g_2+o(1)}\) steps, counting its own source-indexed
accesses to the \emph{virtual} \(Y\), but not the cost of implementing
those accesses through \(G_1\); its local peak index/work state is at most
\(L_1^{\eta_2+o(1)}\). The final one fixed ordinary SAT decider \(A\)
takes \(L_2^{t+o(1)}\) time and \(L_2^{s+o(1)}\) writable/index space
on \emph{every} ordinary input. Parent and intermediate policy use
\(N^{r+o(1)}\) bit work and \(N^{h+o(1)}\) space.
Let \(N^{a+o(1)}\) be the total number of sequential final guest calls,
including failed strategies; ancestors/cache buffers contribute peak
\(N^{\kappa+o(1)}\).

\begin{theorem}[Literal fully lazy nested access: conditional upper ledger]
If the declared implementation actually generates each requested bit of
\(Z\) by \(G_2\), and each of \(G_2\)'s \(Y\)-probes by a fresh \(G_1\)
invocation, with no separately omitted setup or access costs, then
one \emph{uniform and exact} implementation of the specified semantic parent
policy, if that policy exists, has a conservative upper envelope
\[
\boxed{\begin{split}
t'&\le\max\{r,\ a+\theta_1\theta_2 t
                     +\theta_1g_2+g_1\},\\
s'&\le\max\{h,\kappa,\eta_1,\theta_1\eta_2,
                      \theta_1\theta_2s\}.
\end{split}}
\tag{1}
\]
If readable ordinary child bodies, policy selectors, \(N^b\) guest
calls of length \(N^\rho\), or separate lookahead guests are actually
used, they require \emph{additional} charged terms and their own
simultaneous buffer peaks. In particular a lookahead channel has time
\(b+\rho t+g\) for a per-probe exponent \(g\), and peak guest
space \(\rho s\) plus all live policy/ancestor state.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each guest takes at most \(L_2^{t+o(1)}\) steps, hence at most this many
unit-addressed logical input probes. To answer one probe, \(G_2\) does
at most \(L_1^{g_2+o(1)}\) elementary operations, including at most
that many \(Y\)-bit probes. Each such probe costs at most
\(N^{g_1+o(1)}\) operations. This yields
\(N^{\theta_1\theta_2t+\theta_1g_2+g_1+o(1)}\)
per final call, and multiplying by \(N^{a+o(1)}\) gives (1).
Depth-first reuse permits the \emph{maximum} of concurrently live,
separately charged tapes and generator/index states, not their independent
coordinatewise selection from different realizations. Fixed program
descriptions and loop constants are finite and uniform; machine
interpretation and source addresses are charged. This is an \emph{upper}
bound for the exact specified lazy implementation, not a lower bound
against caching, partial evaluation, direct recognition, or another solver.
\end{proof}
When a generator retains an acquired source summary rather than repeating
\(G_1\), its paid acquisition and live summary must replace the repeated
\(g_1\) term. Neither a free cached bit nor a free materialized intermediate
string is permitted. An eag\-er generator is instead governed by the
original EAGER-LAZY-FRONTIER34 ledger, including emitted body/header bits
and one simultaneous readable child buffer.

\section{Explicit SAT/UNSAT ordinary input chain}
For a bit-vector \(b=(b_1,\dots,b_q)\), \(q\ge3\), take original
variables \(u_1,\dots,u_q\), indexed cyclically, and for each edge
\((u_i,u_{i+1})\) append the two usual 2CNF clauses enforcing
\(u_i\oplus u_{i+1}=b_i\). The ordinary 2CNF \(F_b\) is SAT precisely when
\[
\bigoplus_{i=1}^{q}b_i=0.
\tag{2}
\]
This follows by summing equations round the cycle; conversely a chosen
value for \(u_1\) propagates consistently exactly when (2) holds.
Both labels occur at every \(q\). A deterministic SCC 2SAT solver and a
simple parity scan decide the \emph{promised source} efficiently.
Source encodings use a declared self-describing fixed-slot binary
ordinary-CNF grammar: unary bit width, variable/clause counts, and full
literal records. Its length is \(N=\Theta(q\log q)\).

Define the first ordinary proper-3CNF target \(Y_b\) with seven of the
eight signed 3clauses on original names \(1,2,3\).
For odd parity the eighth is the missing eighth signed clause, so the
eight clauses jointly exclude all eight assignments and are UNSAT.
For even parity replace that eighth clause by
\((v_4\vee v_5\vee v_6)\), so the first seven have a satisfying
assignment and the replacement is independently satisfiable.
Append \(m_1=\lfloor\sqrt N/\log_2(N+2)\rfloor\)
disjoint positive three-literal clauses with fresh variables.
The target is ordinary SAT, with \(\SAT(Y_b)=\SAT(F_b)\).
Its exact indexed bits can be produced by \(G_1\): scan the original
\(F_b\), compute its parity, then address one canonical output header,
clause, sign, ID and padding field. In the \emph{literal deliberately
uncached implementation}, each addressed bit recomputes parity using
\(N^{1+o(1)}\) source work and \(O(\log N)\) scratch.

Repeat identically with \(m_2=\lfloor\sqrt{L_1}/\log_2(L_1+2)\rfloor\),
but obtain the status indicator by scanning the actual first encoded
\(Y_b\), \emph{servicing every scanned first-child bit through \(G_1\)}.
The second ordinary proper-3CNF \(Z_b\) satisfies
\(\SAT(Z_b)=\SAT(Y_b)=\SAT(F_b)\), on both labels,
and the two genuine logical lengths obey
\[
L_1=N^{1/2+o(1)},\qquad
L_2=L_1^{1/2+o(1)}=N^{1/4+o(1)}.
\tag{3}
\]
The asymptotic formula concerns \(q\to\infty\); fixed base gadget and
headers can dominate small instances. The \(G_2\) service takes
\(\Theta(L_1)\) calls to \(G_1\) per second-child bit and
\(O(\log N)\) live generator control/index bits when served sequentially.
A final exact guest whose first action scans its whole \(Z_b\) input
once therefore performs, under this \emph{specified literal nested
emulator},
\[
\boxed{\Theta((N+q+O(\log N))L_1L_2)
        =N^{7/4+o(1)}}
\tag{4}
\]
source-bit reads before \emph{any} substantive SAT work.
Equation (4) is exact counting of \emph{this emulator's} probes:
it proves neither a lower bound on all implementations nor a
black-box lower bound on a hypothetical polynomial SAT decider.
Every unchanged source-bit fetch, indexing step, counter update,
copy/rewrite and guest computation still contributes paid bit work.
The source and both virtual lengths are real ordinary-SAT
encodings; a short edit descriptor is never substituted for a guest length.

\section{An elementary fusion falsifies a universal tax}
The one-bit summary
\(p=\bigoplus_i b_i\) is acquired in \(O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)\)
charged bit work, retaining \(O(\log N)\) indexes and a one-bit parity
register. With this \emph{paid} summary, every bit of \(Y_b\) or \(Z_b\)
can be generated by direct indexed arithmetic on known fixed clause
templates, without recomputing \(Y_b\) or rereading all \(F_b\).
A full final ordinary \(Z_b\) scan is therefore possible in
\(O(N\operatorname{polylog}N+L_2\operatorname{polylog}N)\)
bit work and \(O(\log N)\) generator scratch for this \emph{easy}
source promise, in addition to the guest's own counted state.
This proves H3 and refutes any claim that (4) is an unavoidable
\emph{SAT decision} cost.

Crucially, here the one-bit summary is \emph{already the source SAT
answer}. It cannot be invoked as free semantic preprocessing for
arbitrary ordinary SAT without circularity. The source recognizer,
the promised cycle syntax and its proof (2) are extra hypotheses.
They do not imply that an arbitrary FLMV-hard SAT-reduction image
is in this family, nor that its true ordinary children inherit
the promise. Thus H4 fails as an ARC implication.

\paragraph{Barrier/hostile-control ledger.}
Source family is affine 2SAT, admits SCC and one-scan XOR direct
recognizers on SAT and UNSAT; fully paired, Horn, dual Horn,
independent products and source renamings cannot promote this
restricted generator to general hardness.
An extensional SAT oracle or algebraic extension oracle could
bypass the repeated cost, so this is not a nonrelativizing
or nonalgebrizing hardness result.
Caching/fusion corresponds \emph{analogically} to computing
circuit common subexpressions or memory hierarchy reuse;
there is no theorem transfer implying an arbitrary-SAT
time-space contraction.
Cryptographic Schur powers/collision entropy and circuit/VLSI
flips provide no model-matched arrow and remain analogies, not
imported lower bounds.
Peak simultaneous writable/index bits are separately retained
at each execution, while bit operations, actual flips and
cumulative bit-time are not conflated.
