\section{Three exact lemmas and witness maps}
\begin{lemma}[All literal signs are critical on a minimally UNSAT source]
Let $F$ be a clause-minimally UNSAT CNF with distinct variable IDs within every clause. Flipping the sign of any one occurrence creates a SAT formula. Therefore an exact deterministic SAT decider, running on $F$ in a fixed-width addressed-literal representation, must inspect every source literal field.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For any $C\in F$ choose $\alpha\models F\setminus\{C\}$. As $F$ is UNSAT, $\alpha$ falsifies all literals of $C$. Reversing any one sign in $C$ makes $\alpha$ satisfy the whole modified formula. If that input word was unread, deterministic execution would have an identical transcript on opposite-label inputs. Repeat for all literal positions.
\end{proof}
Thus every one of the $48$ literal words in $U$ must be read on the UNSAT input. Applying the same lemma to the \emph{existing growing} clause-minimal UNSAT cubic-parity prism family of CONNECTED-CRITICAL-BACKDOOR58 strengthens its earlier $2t$ independent RHS-sensitivity positions to all $24t$ literal fields on odd-charge proper-3CNF members. This remains input-order acquisition, not computation lower bounds.

\begin{theorem}[Horn selector transport preserves minimality]
For any clause-minimally UNSAT proper 3CNF $\Phi=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$, introduce fresh selectors $s_{ja}$ and define
\[
H(\Phi)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^m\left[(s_{j1}\vee s_{j2}\vee s_{j3})\wedge
\bigwedge_{a=1}^3(\neg s_{ja}\vee\ell_{ja})\right].
\]
Then $H(\Phi)$ is clause-minimally UNSAT. It has $n=n_0+3m$ variables, $m_2=3m$ Horn binary clauses, $k=m$ disjoint positive genuine ternaries, $M=4m$ total clauses. A source resolution refutation with $R$ binary inferences transports to a target refutation of $3m+R$ binary inferences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For deletion of a positive selector triple choose $\alpha\models\Phi\setminus\{C_j\}$ and set its three selectors false; select true source literals in all other groups. For deletion of one implication $(\neg s_{ja}\vee\ell_{ja})$, use the same $\alpha$ and set only $s_{ja}=1$ in group $j$; all other groups choose true source literals. Thus every clause deletion becomes SAT. Resolving the three selector implications with each positive selector triple derives $C_j$ in three inferences. Replay the source resolution proof.
\end{proof}
For a satisfying source assignment $\alpha$, put $t_j=\#\{\text{true literals in }C_j\}$; its exact selector witness-extension count is $\prod_j(2^{t_j}-1)$. All selectors are witness choices, not functional auxiliaries.

\begin{lemma}[Optimal $M-1$ resolution bound for a minimally UNSAT input]
Every ordinary binary-resolution DAG refutation of a clause-minimally UNSAT source containing $M$ distinct clauses has at least $M-1$ inference nodes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every input clause is needed in any refutation DAG, since deleting an unused clause would leave a proof of an actually satisfiable formula. If $I$ inference nodes each have two premise edges, the connected ancestral graph contains $M+I$ nodes and $2I$ edges. Hence $2I\ge M+I-1$, so $I\ge M-1$.
\end{proof}
Our split-tree source proof has $15=16-1$ inferences. $H(U)$ has $63=64-1$, also optimum. Padding each of the 48 binary implications $(a\vee b)$ with a private free $y$ into $(a\vee b\vee y)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg y)$ preserves clause-minimality; it adds one resolution inference per binary and gives a proper-3CNF proof of $111=112-1$ inferences, again optimal.
