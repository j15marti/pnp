\section*{Published hard-family anchor and model}
Let $F_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^h$ be the \emph{unary} pigeonhole CNF with variables $x_{ij}$ ($h+1$ pigeons, $h$ holes); $h+1$ positive occupancy clauses $\bigvee_{j=1}^h x_{ij}$ and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative collision clauses $(\neg x_{ij}\vee\neg x_{kj})$. There are $n=h(h+1)$ variables, $m=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses, and $L=h(h+1)^2$ literal occurrences. Explicit indexed signed-bit/DIMACS encoding has $N=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$ bits. Haken (1985) proves that for some $c>0,h_0$ and \emph{every} $h\ge h_0$, \emph{every ordinary unrestricted DAG Resolution refutation} of this source has at least $2^{ch}$ lines. Counting only distinct paid derived binary resolvents changes this by at most $m=\operatorname{poly}(h)$. This excludes extension, XOR, general circuits and arbitrary SAT machines. Cook's polynomial Extended Resolution and Buss's Frege proofs are mandatory stronger-system countercontrols. In the fixed mixed $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, a known GF(2) shortcut makes odd Tseitin proofs linear, so no Resolution-hard-family claim automatically transfers.

Fix the \emph{strict} ordinary shared-DAG Resolution subsystem, free original clauses, each non-tautological distinct derived resolvent charged one, no weakening and no positive selector axioms. This theorem concerns this restricted subsystem, not full opaque LOCAL-CONVERT.

\section*{Exact clause-private lifting}
For arbitrary indexed CNF $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i$, introduce fresh, different selector bits $s_1,\ldots,s_m$ and set
\[
G(F,s)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(\neg s_i\vee C_i),\qquad D(s)=\bigvee_{i=1}^m\neg s_i.
\]
For $t\in\{0,1\}^m$ define the \emph{complete projected boundary relation}
\[
B_F(t)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists x\,G(F,t,x)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{SAT}\Big(\bigwedge_{i:t_i=1}C_i\Big).
\]
The second equivalence holds for every $F,t$, not just for one output bit. $G$ is always satisfiable by $t=0^m$.

\begin{theorem}[strict Resolution isometry]
For any UNSAT $F$, the minimum paid Resolution nodes for deriving \emph{some} clause consisting only of negative $s$-literals from $G$ equals the minimum nodes for refuting $F$. If $F$ is clause-minimally UNSAT, the only possible non-tautological entailed selector-only clause is $D$, and
\[
\boxed{\min_{\pi:G\vdash_{\rm Res}D}|\pi_{\rm derived}|
=\min_{\tau:F\vdash_{\rm Res}\bot}|\tau_{\rm derived}|}.
\]
Every premise of a contradiction refutation of a minimally UNSAT $F$ is indispensable; the equality includes the zero-node case of an original empty clause when $m=1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
No positive $s_i$ occurs in the guarded premises, so no Resolution derivation can pivot on a selector. Deleting all negative selector literals maps every guarded pivot on an original variable to the identical ordinary pivot over $F$. A derived selector-only clause maps to $\bot$. Thus stripping any $q$-node guarded proof yields an ordinary refutation with at most $q$ paid nodes. Conversely take any ordinary $q$-node refutation of $F$. Replace each original premise $C_i$ by $\neg s_i\vee C_i$ and repeat each pivot, carrying the union of the participating negative selector literals. This uses at most $q$ paid nodes and finishes at a selector-only clause. If $F$ is minimally UNSAT, every source clause must occur in the underlying refutation, so the selector union at the root is all $m$ guards, namely $D$. Equivalently, any shorter proper guard-only subclause of $D$ cannot be entailed because the omitted activated original subset is satisfiable. Hence both inequalities and the exact equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[compact full projected relation with exponential strict-Resolution proof]
For every clause-minimally UNSAT $F$,
\[
B_F(t)=\neg(t_1\wedge\cdots\wedge t_m)=D(t).
\]
The standard $F_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^h$ is minimally UNSAT: deleting an occupancy clause lets one pigeon remain unplaced; deleting a collision clause permits exactly that pair to share its specified hole while the remaining pigeons occupy other holes. Hence the entire selector-projected relation of $G(F_h,s)$ is the \emph{single explicit clause} $D(s)$, yet every strict-Resolution derivation of $D$ takes $2^{\Omega(h)}$ paid nodes.
\end{corollary}
This exposes the exact gap: semantic output length and acquisition from a \emph{recognizable known family} can be polynomial while authenticated derivation cost in a restricted proof system is exponential. A single uniform parser recognizes canonical PHP grammar, checks all source and guard bits and emits $D$ for every such instance in polynomial time, without recognizing arbitrary UNSAT. The compiler is not itself a sound Resolution rule and a certificate that $F$ is canonical PHP is not a derivation of $D$ in Resolution. The existence of this family-specific compiler does \emph{not} refute B2 or B3 for any calculus.
