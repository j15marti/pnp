\section{Matched scalable proper-3CNF formulas}
For integer $p\ge2$ let source variables be $x_1,\ldots,x_p,a,b,c$, with
\[
 C_p=\bigwedge_{1\le i<j\le p}(x_i\vee x_j\vee a).
\]
Let $Q_8(a,b,c)$ consist of all eight signed clauses over $a,b,c$. Define $U_p=C_p\land Q_8$. Define $V_p$ by replacing the positive clause $(a\vee b\vee c)$ in $Q_8$ with a second copy of $(\neg a\vee b\vee c)$. Each clause is proper with three distinct variables, although the SAT source intentionally has one duplicate \emph{whole clause}. The formulas are connected through $a$ and have identical identifiers and lengths:
\[
 n=p+3,\quad m=\binom p2+8,\quad
 h=\left\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\right\rceil,\quad
 N=(4m+2)h=\Theta(p^2\log p).
\]
There are no source auxiliaries. The exact chosen encoding is one common fixed-width header and four fixed-width fields per clause; changing a sign cannot change length.

\begin{theorem}[Exact SAT and UNSAT controls]
For all $p\ge2$,
\[
 \#\operatorname{SAT}(U_p)=0,\qquad
 \#\operatorname{SAT}(V_p)=p+1.
\]
Every model of $V_p$ has $(a,b,c)=(0,0,0)$ and at most one zero among $x_1,\ldots,x_p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every assignment to $a,b,c$ falsifies exactly one of the eight complete signed clauses, proving $U_p$ UNSAT. The seven distinct remaining clauses in $V_p$ have exactly one common model, $(a,b,c)=0^3$. Duplicating another clause does not alter this. At $a=0$, every clause of $C_p$ is $(x_i\vee x_j)$, so at most one $x_i$ can be zero, giving $p+1$ models. One verified witness is all $x_i=1$, $(a,b,c)=0^3$.
\end{proof}

\section{Source-preserving scope width is exactly $p+1$}
A \emph{source-preserving closed scoped stream} consists of \texttt{OPEN}(v), \texttt{CLAUSE}$(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)$ and \texttt{CLOSE}(v) events. Every source variable is opened once and closed once, clauses are emitted only while their source identities are live, and every original clause is emitted unchanged, with arbitrary reordering permitted. Fresh disjoint satisfiable three-variable gadgets may be appended. The \emph{live width} $w$ counts simultaneously open variables; it is syntactic and is not machine-space cost.

\begin{theorem}[Exact all-order scope width, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For either $F=U_p$ or $F=V_p$, and every source-preserving scoped stream representing $F$, $w\ge p+1$. This is attained by a valid closed stream, so
\[
 \boxed{w_{\min}(U_p)=w_{\min}(V_p)=p+1.}
\]
The lower bound persists under all source-clause reorderings and any quantity of independent fresh satisfiable padding clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each two distinct members of $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_p,a\}$ occur together in an original clause. Thus their two live intervals must intersect at the corresponding clause event. Finite intervals have the elementary pairwise-intersection Helly property: if $L$ is the largest interval left endpoint and $R$ the smallest right endpoint, pairwise intersection implies $L\le R$. Hence all $p+1$ intervals share an event. For an attaining stream, open $a$ and all $x_i$, output every clique clause, close the $x_i$, then open $b,c$, output the eight signed core clauses, and close $c,b,a$. The peak is $\max\{p+1,3\}=p+1$. Open and close each fresh independent padding triple afterward.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Polynomial pad-floor no-go, restricted]
For every fixed $C\ge1$, a clause/name-preserving padded stream of encoded length $N'\le N^C$ has
\[
 \frac{w}{\log_2 N'}\ge\frac{p+1}{C\log_2 N}
 =\Omega\left(\frac p{\log p}\right)\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Consequently no fixed number of polynomial-size disjoint-padding stages can establish $w=o(\log N')$ for this family. Here $w=\Theta(\sqrt{N/\log N})$. This excludes padding/reordering alone, not semantic rewriting, signed source-variable splitting with paid equality, clause replacement, or direct decision.
\end{corollary}

\section{Adaptive one-bit SAT decision defeats any machine-space inference}
A total uniform solver tries a paid recognizer for the canonical ordered $U_p$ or $V_p$. It scans and validates every expected positive pair clause and the entire eight-record core, then returns the label and, for SAT, the explicit source witness. It is not given the label; the source content determines the branch. If recognition fails, it enters a separate total exact SAT fallback. A bit-level implementation of the successful recognized branch uses $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work and $O(\log N)$ live writable controller bits with indexed read-only source and a fixed finite program. This proves an easy both-label high-width family, not an all-input polynomial SAT solver.

For comparison, a deliberately nonpruning fixed frontier-table policy stores a row for every $(x_1,\ldots,x_p,a)$ state, hence $2^{p+1}$ rows, and spends $2^{p+1}\binom p2$ pair-clause tests if it tests every candidate against every clique clause. The table is a cost of this \emph{restricted policy}, not a lower bound on arbitrary exact SAT decisions, and recognizing the source is always an allowed alternative.
