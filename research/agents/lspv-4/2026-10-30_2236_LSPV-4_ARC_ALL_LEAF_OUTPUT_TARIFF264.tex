\section*{One target, quantifiers, and prior work}
Target the one integrated adaptive state-dependent residual-SAT controller and the unconstructed arbitrary polynomial SAT decider $A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ with hypothesized stage recurrences $K'\le\max\{11/10,3/5+K/2\}$, $L'\le\max\{1/2,L/2\}$ on the \emph{same} FLMV deterministic indexed random-access Turing-machine model. These recurrences are not proved for any all-input transformation. This run explicitly isolates an inexpensive-looking but defective implementation: a fully unfolded, unpruned adaptive binary tree, where each leaf materializes and queries its direct original-source cofactor. Every leaf is an ordinary SAT or UNSAT guest query; there are no sound semantic terminal certificates, sharing aliases, or nonlocal re-encodings. The construction is a \emph{restricted architecture} and cannot lower-bound all SAT machines.

Project predecessors LSPV-2 COFACTOR-VOLUME-ARC252 already proved that one $b$-pin raw cofactor retains at least $m-\Delta b$ untouched clauses; LSPV-1 ARC-KRAFT-CERTIFIED-COVER252 and LSPV-3 ARC-BRANCH-MASS-GATE261 already proved Kraft-based guest-mass conditions \emph{including} certified P/S leaves. These are credited, not claimed as novel. This report adds the explicit \emph{all-leaf physical-output tariff} and the sharp tree-depth $\le q-1$ condition, with separately paid leaf outputs on both labels and against maximally unbalanced adaptive trees. It does not improve those predecessors' certified-branch generality.

\section*{Four falsifiers}
\begin{description}
\item[] F1: Can an unbalanced adaptive decision policy with at most $N^{3/5}$ complete leaves avoid a parent-size output in every guest query? No, under the raw direct-cofactor and no-pruning assumptions, by the theorem below.
\item[] F2: Can materializing $q=N^{3/5+o(1)}$ separate ordinary raw-cofactor inputs be an $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ parent stage? No. Even the mandatory write-only output bits alone take $N^{8/5-o(1)}$ on this restricted architecture.
\item[] F3: Can the same structural tariff separate SAT and UNSAT from original unsigned supports? No. W1 UNSAT and S15 SAT have identical unsigned supports; the cofactor clause/literal volumes aggregated over all value assignments for each fixed pinned-variable set agree, although labels differ.
\item[] F4: Does the output tariff extend to an arbitrary exact SAT controller with proofs, global recognition, memoization, streaming accessors and algebraic solution policies? No. A polynomial recognizable easy family, a certified root-UNSAT proof, and a virtual child with a separately charged accessor lie outside the theorem's hypotheses.
\end{description}

\section*{New restricted lemma and proof}
\begin{theorem}[All-leaf direct-cofactor output tariff]
Fix $0<\gamma<1$. Let $F_n$ be proper distinct-clause 3CNF with $n$ named variables, $m=\Theta(n)$ clauses and maximum number $\Delta=O(1)$ of original clauses incident to each variable. Let the source be an explicit fixed-width ordinary signed-CNF array with each untouched original ternary clause requiring $h_n=\Theta(\log n)$ physically emitted bits and $N=m h_n+O(\log N)=\Theta(n\log n)$.

Consider any complete rooted full binary decision tree branching on previously unassigned original variables, adaptively and with arbitrary deterministic discovery policies. Every leaf $\ell$ emits a separate ordinary, direct syntactic cofactor $F_n\upharpoonright\rho_\ell$ containing every untouched original clause as its own $h_n$-bit record; no leaf terminates with a source proof or checked witness instead of a guest child. Let $q$ be the number of leaves and $d_\ell$ its distinct pinned-variable depth. Then
\[
\max_\ell d_\ell\le q-1,\qquad
 |F_n\upharpoonright\rho_\ell|_{\rm physical}\ge h_n\max\{0,m-\Delta(q-1)\},
\]
and the total physically emitted guest input obeys
\[
\boxed{B_{\rm output}\ge qh_n\max\{0,m-\Delta(q-1)\}.}
\]
For $q\le N^\gamma$ and $m=\Theta(n)$, $q=o(n)$; therefore \emph{each} emitted guest input has $(1-o(1))N$ bits under this fixed-width source encoding. If $q=N^{\gamma+o(1)}$, $B_{\rm output}\ge N^{1+\gamma-o(1)}$. The materializing implementation spends at least $\Omega(B_{\rm output})$ transitions writing output even though the FLMV output tape is excluded from its \emph{space} measurement.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A finite full binary tree with $q$ leaves has $q-1$ internal nodes. Any root-to-leaf path traverses at most $q-1$ internal nodes, hence pins $d_\ell\le q-1$ distinct variables. Each pinned variable occurs in at most $\Delta$ distinct original clauses. At most $\Delta d_\ell$ source clauses touch any pin; every other clause survives literally as an unchanged ternary source record. Thus each child has at least $h_n\max\{0,m-\Delta d_\ell\}\ge h_n\max\{0,m-\Delta(q-1)\}$ bits. Summing the separate physical output records over $q$ leaves proves the inequality. Since $N=\Theta(n\log n)$ and $\gamma<1$, $q\le N^\gamma=o(n)$ while $m=\Theta(n)$, so $\Delta q/m=o(1)$. This gives the asymptotic conclusion. A write-only output tape can avoid charging these bits to \emph{workspace}, but emitting them still costs at least one transition per output bit. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact strict signed $(2+,2-)$ consequence.} Each source variable occurs in exactly four distinct original clauses, $\Delta=4$ and $m=4n/3$. With a declared fixed-width payload of $h_n=3(1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil)$ bits per ternary clause,
\[
 B_{\rm output}\ge q\,3(1+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil)\max\{0,4n/3-4(q-1)\}.
\]
The inequality holds for the original W1 and S15 opposite-label, identical-unsigned-support sources and their disjoint-copy families, whether or not a specialized solver would solve those families much faster. The source encoding is declared; arbitrary general-purpose compression is not excluded.

\paragraph{Kraft comparison and label correctness.} The same direct-cofactor tree has $\sum_{\ell}2^{-d_\ell}=1$ and
\[
 \mathrm{SAT}(F_n)\iff\bigvee_{\ell=1}^q \mathrm{SAT}(F_n\upharpoonright\rho_\ell),\qquad
 \mathrm{UNSAT}(F_n)\iff\bigwedge_{\ell=1}^q\mathrm{UNSAT}(F_n\upharpoonright\rho_\ell).
\]
These are exact on both labels for each fixed source and do not assume an oracle that tells which branches succeed. If there are certified source-terminal leaves or shared guest calls, the premise ``every leaf emits a separate ordinary child'' fails. The earlier project's mass/gate theorems are then the appropriate weaker framework. If a controller tests a SAT witness and stops early, the executed path is no longer a complete tree, also outside this output-volume conclusion.

\section*{Matched FLMV audit and resource ledger}
Theorem 5.7 [FLMV 2005] uses indexed read-only input and finitely many work/index tapes; the output tape is write-only and not charged to space. Its exact rational function is $d_*(c)=\max_{k\ge1}\min\{(1/f_k-c)/k,c^{-k}\}$ with $f_0=1$, $f_k=cf_{k-1}/(1+f_{k-1})$. A computer-free finite-tail certificate at a rational $c>1$ checks $k\le K$ and then $d_j(c)\le c^{-j}\le c^{-(K+1)}<\max_{k\le K}d_k(c)$ for all $j>K$. Independently implemented exact fractions gave
\[
 d_*(6/5)=23/45\ (k=2),\qquad
 d_*(241/200)=11642479/23232400\ (k=2).
\]
In both cases $K=3$ suffices, with strict tail bounds $625/1296<23/45$ and $1600000000/3373402561<11642479/23232400$. At $(6/5,1/2)$ the proposed conditional fixed point has strict margins $1/200$ time and $26279/23232400$ writable space relative to $c=241/200$. Exponent equality does \emph{not} enter the $o(N^d)$ space exclusion without an additional little-$o$ proof. The newer finite arithmetic checker also confirms the broader sample points $c=7/5,3/2,8/5$ with $k=3,4,8$ respectively. No retired $t+s$ curve is used.

For complete materialization, every leaf incurs original-source scan/access and selector/path work, emission of child bits, a guest call, and its own guest index/work-tape state. Parent physical storage of one child costs $\Omega(N)$ high-water writable space if materialized on work tape; reusing that same work-tape address prevents $qN$ cumulative space but cannot reduce one full child's $\Omega(N)$ high-water. Direct sequential emission to a write-only output tape avoids the latter workspace cost but cannot in this model magically give each independent ordinary SAT guest a free read-only input tape. An on-demand virtual-child implementation changes the simulation contract and must account for every guest read, child-bit accessor invocation, source-index head move, replay, and retained parent/ancestor state. Multiple work/index tapes and source-head recovery are jointly charged on the same guest-simulator execution. Nothing in this report proves an input-accessor time lower bound against all possible virtual-child representations.

Exact original-clause endpoint scans cost $O(m)$ literal tests with $O(\log m)$ counters if not retaining all per-clause counts; keeping every clause's 0/1/2/3 count costs $\Omega(m)$ bits. With an in-memory occurrence index, one flip updates at most four source-clause counters on strict22 but acquiring/storing that index costs $\Theta(n\log n)$ description bits in a straightforward representation. With no such index, an honest source rescan can cost $\Theta(N)$ per flip. Materializing all $\binom m2$ four-mode pairs costs $\Theta(m^2)$ records; checking only shared-variable pairs can be sparse but requires paid discovery. Resolution steps, proof checking, failed branches and policy switching are additive costs. These are \emph{upper accounting examples}, not mandatory lower bounds on arbitrary algorithms. The experiments count guest DPLL clause visits, literal tests and nodes as separate instrumented logical events, not native FLMV machine transitions.

\section*{Limits, project integration and handoff}
This is an elementary corollary combining known binary-tree size, bounded clause occurrence and the earlier project's source-faithful raw-cofactor floor. It establishes only the failure of the specified fully unfolded, unpruned, separately materializing ARC design. It is not a theorem about a total optimal controller, restricted SAT hardness, a universal source-probing lower bound, or the computational complexity of a general SAT instance. If $q$ counts only \emph{physical guest calls}, shared logical leaves can invalidate the tree-depth inference. If short source-proved P/S terminals exist, the total number of logical leaves can be exponential even when guest count is small. Such optimizations are the exact remaining mechanism to analyze.

Relativization and algebraic-access audit: the combinatorial output-floor proof is syntactic and survives semantic oracle augmentation \emph{only for a controller forbidden to use oracle answers}. Allowing a SAT oracle, Gaussian elimination, a short proof, or nonlocal source-dependent encoding escapes this restricted model. Consequently neither relativization nor algebrization can transport the result to arbitrary SAT decision trees. A DELAY-wrapped guest on a large child adds $\Omega(N^D)$ guest work but is not needed to prove the materialized-output defect. A source-only recognizer of the product family returns a checked answer without emitting these children.

\textbf{Single OPEN proof arrow:} an all-input, hereditary, uniform, arbitrary-$A$ ordinary SAT contractor whose adaptive certified terminal leaves, shared source-authenticated queries or nonlocal compression actually achieve $q\le N^{3/5+o(1)}$, child length $\le N^{1/2+o(1)}$, fully charged parent stage $\le N^{11/10+o(1)}$, counted space $\le N^{1/2+o(1)}$, and strict FLMV entry for a single deterministic indexed random-access TM. This report does not provide it. $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textbf{OPEN}.
