\section*{Published hard theorem: exact quantifiers and model}
The unary pigeonhole CNF $P_h=\mathrm{PHP}_{h+1}^{h}$ has variables $p_{ij}$, the $(h+1)$ positive clauses $\bigvee_{j=1}^h p_{ij}$ and $h\binom{h+1}{2}$ negative collision clauses $(\neg p_{ij}\lor\neg p_{kj})$. It has $m_h=(h+1)+h\binom{h+1}{2}$ clauses and $h(h+1)^2$ literal occurrences, with explicitly indexed signed binary source length $N_h=\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$. Haken (1985) proves there exist $c>0,h_0$ such that $\forall h\ge h_0$, $\forall$ ordinary unrestricted \emph{shared-DAG Resolution} refutations $\pi$ of $P_h$, $|\pi|\ge2^{ch}$. Hence, counting only paid derived nodes, $W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}-m_h$. This does not quantify over Extended Resolution, $\mathrm{Res}(\oplus)$, $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ or arbitrary uniform SAT machines. Cook's polynomial Extended Resolution PHP proofs are mandatory countercontrols.

\section*{Equal complete selector profiles}
For an ordered $m$-clause CNF $F$, let
\[
B_F(s)=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \bigwedge_{j:s_j=1}C_j\ \mathrm{SAT}
\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists_x\,\bigwedge_j(\neg s_j\lor C_j).
\]
The $\{s_j\}$ are \emph{source-indexed} selectors; this is the full $2^m$-point source-deletion SAT relation, not only single-clause tests.

\begin{theorem}
For every integer $h\ge1$, put $m=m_h$ and define the Horn 2-CNF
\[
H_m=(x_1)\land\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m-2}(\neg x_j\lor x_{j+1})\land(\neg x_{m-1}).
\]
Both $H_m$ and $P_h$ are clause-minimally UNSAT, with exactly $m$ source clauses. For every selector vector $s\in\{0,1\}^m$,
\[
\boxed{B_{P_h}(s)=B_{H_m}(s)=\neg\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}s_j.}
\]
Nevertheless,
\[
\boxed{W_{\mathrm{Res}}(H_m)=m-1,\qquad
W_{\mathrm{Res}}(P_h)\ge2^{ch}-m\quad(h\ge h_0).}
\]
The signed binary source lengths of both families are $\Theta(h^3\log(h+2))$. Thus no invariant solely of source-clause count, the \emph{complete} clause-subset SAT profile, and polynomially comparable source length can be polynomially equivalent to both ordinary Resolution costs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $P_h$, remove a positive occupancy clause and assign the other $h$ pigeons injectively to the $h$ holes; or remove collision $(\neg p_{ij}\lor\neg p_{kj})$ and place both $i,k$ in hole $j$ and the remaining $h-1$ pigeons injectively in the other holes. Every deleted-clause source is SAT, while full $P_h$ is UNSAT. In $H_m$, delete the first unit and set all variables 0; delete the final unit and set all 1; delete implication $(\neg x_j\lor x_{j+1})$ and set $x_1,\ldots,x_j$ to 1 and the remainder 0. Full $H_m$ is UNSAT. Any proper subset omits one clause and is SAT, proving the selector identity for \emph{all} $2^m$ vectors. Every sound binary-premise refutation of a minimally UNSAT $m$-source CNF needs all $m$ leaves, so a connected backward DAG requires at least $m-1$ inference nodes. The Horn unit-propagation refutation attains $m-1$. Apply Haken for PHP. Both lengths grow as $\Theta(m\log(m+1))$ under binary literal indexing.
\end{proof}

\section*{All-phase physical accounting}
The canonical-source authenticator scans and verifies expected clause templates and produces the compact boundary in polynomial bit time. A streaming reader and write-only output transducer can use $O(\log N)$ writable bits (counters and indices), with paid rescans when needed.
