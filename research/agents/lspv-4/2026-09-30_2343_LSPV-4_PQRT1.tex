\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: collision-effective entropy.}
For finite accepted set $A$ and extensional map $L:A\to B$, let $n_v=|L^{-1}(v)|$ and
\[
\operatorname{Coll}(L)=\sum_v n_v^2,\qquad h_2(L)=2\log_2|A|-\log_2\operatorname{Coll}(L).
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
|\operatorname{im}L|\ge {|A|^2\over\operatorname{Coll}(L)}=2^{h_2(L)}.
\]
Thus $h_2>\kappa+M$, $M=1+m+\binom m2$, is sufficient for the existing extractor counting attack. Under ignored decorative proof padding $A'=A\times\{0,1\}^t$,
\[
|A'|=2^t|A|,\qquad \operatorname{Coll}(L')=2^{2t}\operatorname{Coll}(L),
\]
hence $h_2(L')=h_2(L)$ exactly. Since $\operatorname{Coll}(L)\le\beta|A|$, this image lower bound is at least as strong as $|A|/\beta$.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: completeness does not supply the entropy premise.}
Perfect completeness has form $x\in L\Rightarrow\exists\pi$ accepted with probability one. This premise is compatible with $|A|=1$, in which case $\operatorname{Coll}(L)=1$ and $h_2=0$. Therefore completeness alone cannot imply positive asymptotic accepted entropy, collision-effective entropy, or image size. This does not assert that the actual BGH+/vMS accepted set is singleton. It falsifies deriving the threshold from completeness alone. Weakest repair: prove an actual $h_2$ lower bound for the concrete verifier/map, or use LSPV-1's canonical-single-proof minor route.

\paragraph{PQ Schur/syzygy toolkit.}
For $C_H=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{1,g_1,\ldots,g_m\}$,
\[
\rho(E_H)=\dim C_H^{\star2}
\]
is literally a code Schur-square invariant: \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}. Shortening, puncturing, dualization, and Schur-power identities transfer when their hypotheses hold for the exact reachable code.

Randriambololona's 2025 syzygy distinguisher uses graded Betti numbers of the homogeneous coordinate ring of a shortening of the dual code, extending distinguishability beyond the square-code regime. Betti/syzygy diagnostics are therefore an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} for $C_H$. Hostile direction: in PQ cryptanalysis, detectable algebraic structure is an exploitable weakness, not evidence of hardness. Recoverable low-rank relations or syzygies could help acquire sparse $Q$.

The algorithmic transfer is presently \textbf{FAILED}. PQ distinguishers receive an explicit length-$N$ code representation and charge time in $N$. The natural SU/vMS evaluation code may have one coordinate per source input, exponentially many relative to a succinct source description. No reviewed theorem converts polynomial/subexponential time in explicit code length into polynomial time in the succinct SU/vMS input, and hardness of computing these invariants does not imply unrestricted SAT hardness.

\paragraph{Quadratic relations and higher syzygies.}
The kernel of the degree-two multiplication/evaluation map is exactly the quadratic-relation space, so its rank is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT TRANSFER}. Higher Hilbert/Betti/syzygy invariants are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}. Missing bridge: a theorem about efficient computation or an unconditional lower bound in the actual succinct encoder. Full Schur square only retires quadratic annihilation; it is not a SAT lower bound.

\paragraph{LWE-style reduction lesson.}
External extraction from black-box solver behavior is preferable to demanding a chosen internal resource, but this is \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}. Bogdanov--Trevisan give barriers to nonadaptive worst-case/average-case self-correction for NP-complete problems; Trevisan--Vadhan give limitations of black-box uniform reductions. LWE reductions use problem-specific algebra/distributions and cannot be imported as unconditional SAT reductions. Repair requires an exact solver interface and a source-specific reduction without average-case or cryptographic assumptions.

\paragraph{Collision extraction and binding.}
