\section{Target and obstruction}
The sole selected hardness target is all valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2), each clause three distinct same-sign literals and every variable twice per sign, with malformed strings rejected. CONTRACT1 warns that a polynomial factor multiplying \emph{every guest step} must have exponent $\rho<1/400$ to enter the fixed reference box. That numerical warning does not apply identically to a one-time cost paid \emph{once per child}. The distinction is the current precise resource obstruction.
\begin{theorem}[Conditional setup/guest split]
Let A be any uniform deterministic total exact target-language decider on the same FLMV indexed random-access Turing machine, with $T_A(M)\le M^{K+o(1)}$, $S_A(M)\le M^{L+o(1)}$. Suppose a uniform exact hereditary arbitrary-$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ compiler has actually been constructed on every target string, making at most $q\le N^{\gamma+o(1)}$ sequential guest calls of length $M_i\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, $0\le\lambda<1$. Assume all machine work is bounded by
\[
T_{\Phi(A)}(N)\le N^{\alpha+o(1)}+
\sum_{i=1}^{q}\left(N^{\delta+o(1)}+
N^{\rho+o(1)}T_A(M_i)\right).
\]
Here $\delta$ is additive per-child setup covering discovery, failed policies, source/index access, encoding and output descriptor construction. $\rho$ is the multiplicative per-guest-step cost of input-bit access, replay and simulation. Every source and guest uses the same physical representation. Then
\[
K'\le\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\delta,\gamma+\rho+\lambda K\}.
\]
If the separately \emph{proved} simultaneous writable/index-tape bound is
$S_{\Phi(A)}(N)\le N^{\max\{\sigma,\eta,\lambda L\}+o(1)}$
including guest, ancestors, descriptors and index tapes, then $L'\le\max\{\sigma,\eta,\lambda L\}$.
For a fixed uniform hereditary compiler, the conditional iteration fixed point is
\[
K_*=\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\delta,(\gamma+\rho)/(1-\lambda)\},
\qquad L_*=\max\{\sigma,\eta\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sum at most $N^{\gamma+o(1)}$ child terms, taking the larger of the two exponent costs. Iteration satisfies $K_{j+1}\le\max(P,B+\lambda K_j)$, where $P=\max(\alpha,\gamma+\delta)$ and $B=\gamma+\rho$. By induction, $K_j\le K_*+\lambda^j\max(0,K_0-K_*)$. The space recurrence analogously contracts to $L_*$. Serial execution does not prove the required peak-space hypothesis and no such all-input compiler has been constructed.
\end{proof}
