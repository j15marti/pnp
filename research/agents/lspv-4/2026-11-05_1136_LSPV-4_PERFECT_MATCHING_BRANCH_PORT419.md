Then:
1. There are exactly 2H width-two clauses, on exactly H unassigned edge variables. No other widths occur.
2. The 2^(H/2) different matching assignments induce pairwise **different original-source-ID-labelled** residual clause streams.
3. For odd total charge every cofactor is UNSAT. For even total charge the original source is SAT and some matching cofactor extends a model, though not necessarily every branch.
4. With vertex-major original quartets, the j-th surviving clause (0-indexed) comes from vertex floor(j/2), local surviving slot j mod 2. With indexed source adjacency and the matching assignment state, four local checks suffice to generate a requested clause.

**Proof.** Exactly one fixed matching edge meets each vertex. No other matching edge meets u; therefore the full source-ID-labelled residuals differ. Odd total parity yields 0=1 by summing all vertex equations; restriction cannot restore satisfiability.
