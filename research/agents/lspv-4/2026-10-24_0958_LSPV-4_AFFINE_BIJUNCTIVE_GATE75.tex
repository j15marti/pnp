\section{A complete affine-to-2-CNF representation criterion}
Let $E$ be a consistent system of linear equations over $\mathbb F_2$,
$X=(x_1,\ldots,x_h)$ a supplied boundary, and $Y$ the remaining variables.
The exact projected relation is $R(X)=\exists Y\,E(X,Y)$.
Choose an affine parameterization of this nonempty relation:
\[
R=\{c+At:t\in\mathbb F_2^d\},\qquad
c\in\mathbb F_2^h,\quad \operatorname{rank}(A)=d. \tag{2}
\]
Let $v_i$ be row $i$ of $A$, let
$s=|\{v_i:v_i\ne0\}|$ count \emph{distinct} nonzero row vectors, and
define the structural \emph{coordinate-covector excess}
\[
\epsilon(R):=s-d\ge0. \tag{3}
\]
This is defined from the affine relation independently of runtime and is
invariant under invertible reparameterization of $t$. It need not be
available to a solver before a charged projection/elimination.

\begin{theorem}[Exact affine-bijunctive criterion; \PROVED]\label{th:criterion}
For every consistent finite Boolean affine relation $R$,
the following are equivalent:
\[
R\text{ is definable by 2-CNF}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
s=d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\epsilon(R)=0. \tag{4}
\]
In the yes case a 2-CNF of at most $2h$ unit/binary clauses is
constructible from the parameterization in polynomial time. In the no
case no 2-CNF over the \emph{same boundary variables} expresses $R$,
regardless of the number of clauses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each zero row $v_i=0$ fixes $x_i=c_i$. For any two coordinates whose
nonzero row vectors coincide, $x_i\oplus x_j=c_i\oplus c_j$.
Distinct nonzero row vectors over $\mathbb F_2$ are linearly independent
\emph{as a pair}, so their two-bit projection is all four assignments.
These are exactly all unary and pairwise consequences of $R$.

Let $Q$ be the 2-CNF consisting of the fixed-coordinate units and
the equality/disequality clauses that connect each set of equal
nonzero row vectors to one representative. If the $s$ different
nonzero rows are linearly independent, their induced bits range over
all $2^s$ choices, hence $Q=R$. There are at most $h$ units and
$2(h-s)$ binary clauses, thus at most $2h$ clauses.

If $s>d$, $Q$ permits $2^s$ different boundary assignments, whereas
$R$ permits only $2^d$. Every unit/binary clause true on $R$
is true on $Q$, since it refers to at most two coordinates and their
projected value pairs coincide. Therefore no conjunction of such
clauses can distinguish $R$ from $Q$. This proves nondefinability.
\end{proof}
An inconsistent $E$ is detected first and represented by FALSE.
The case of an empty boundary is also handled directly.

\begin{theorem}[Charged adaptive Gaussian-to-SCC decision; \PROVED]
For $F=B(X)\wedge E(X,Y)$, with $B$ an arbitrary 2-CNF and $E$ an
arbitrary binary linear system, compute the implication SCCs of $B$
and substitute every discovered signed equivalence into $E$ and $B$.
Let $X'$ denote the quotient boundary. If the resulting affine system
is inconsistent, return UNSAT. If its projection $R(X')$ satisfies
$\epsilon(R)=0$, a total uniform exact algorithm decides $F$ in
$\operatorname{poly}(N)$ time and space, with a 2-CNF intermediary
and exact witness reconstruction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
SCC equivalences preserve the full solution set of $B$ under a
bijective quotient assignment map; the residual $B'$ is still 2-CNF.
Charged Gaussian elimination constructs $R(X')$.
When $\epsilon=0$, Theorem~\ref{th:criterion} gives $Q_R(X')$
such that $B'\wedge Q_R$ is equisatisfiable with the original $F$.
One implication-graph SCC solve decides it. A satisfying quotient
assignment extends through the affine equations by another charged
linear solve, and inverse SCC substitution recovers the original
witness, which can be replayed against the source. On inconsistency
either an SCC contradiction or a GF(2) inconsistent row supplies a
verifiable certificate. If the test fails, exact DPLL fallback still
makes the algorithm total; no polynomial bound is claimed on that
fallback. Parsing, SCC recognition, Gaussian projection, policy
selection, failed tests, output production, replay and peak workspace
are all charged. Conservatively one may use a polynomial $O(N^4)$
bit-time implementation with $O(N^2)$ bits of live matrix storage.
\end{proof}
The theorem is a sufficient polynomial-time criterion for \emph{observed
states}, not a bound for all algorithms or all mixed affine/2-CNF formulas.
There may be cheaper direct, Horn, DP or branch-and-cache solutions
even when $\epsilon>0$.

\section{A scalable cyclic parity interface and both labels}
Fix even $q\ge4$ and $\beta\in\{0,1\}^q$.
Use $q$ source variables $x_0,\ldots,x_{q-1}$, $q$ auxiliary
variables $y_0,\ldots,y_{q-1}$ and cyclic indices. Let
\[
E_{q,\beta}=\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-1}
(y_{i-1}\oplus y_i\oplus x_i=\beta_i),\qquad
B_q=\bigwedge_{i=0}^{q-2}(x_i\leftrightarrow x_{i+1}).
\tag{5}
\]
Encode each XOR3 as its four proper ternary falsifying-assignment clauses,
and each binary equality as the two Horn/bijunctive binary clauses
$(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})$ and $(x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1})$.
Call the resulting CNF $H_{q,\beta}$.

\begin{theorem}[Parity label, exact witnesses, and raw backdoor; \PROVED]
For every even $q\ge4$,
\[
H_{q,\beta}\in SAT\iff\bigoplus_{i=0}^{q-1}\beta_i=0,\qquad
\#SAT(H_{q,\beta})=
\begin{cases}4,&\bigoplus_i\beta_i=0,\\0,&\bigoplus_i\beta_i=1.\end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
The minimum raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor has size $h_2=q/2$.
The affine projection $\exists y\,E_{q,\beta}$ on $x$ is the
$q$-ary parity relation $\bigoplus_ix_i=\bigoplus_i\beta_i$,
of dimension $d=q-1$, $s=q$, and $\epsilon=1$.
Its minimum \emph{semantic} strong 2-CNF backdoor under fixing
boundary coordinates is exactly $q-2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Summing the $q$ affine equations cancels each $y_i$ twice,
giving the displayed parity condition on $x$. Conversely,
choosing any $x$ of the required parity and either $y_0$ uniquely
determines all other $y_i$ by the equations, so projection is exact.
The binary equalities force $x_i=t$ for all $i$. Since $q$ is even,
$\bigoplus_i x_i=0$ irrespective of $t$, and when the parity charge is
even, both choices of $t$ and both choices of $y_0$ are valid.

Every ternary block has the support $\{x_i,y_{i-1},y_i\}$.
Any backdoor that lowers every raw ternary to width two must hit all
$q$ supports; any variable hits at most two, giving $h_2\ge q/2$.
Choosing alternate $y_i$ meets all supports, proving equality.
For the affine projection, $q-1$ free $x$ bits determine the last,
so the $q$ distinct coordinate covectors have one dependence,
giving $\epsilon=1$. Fixing fewer than $q-2$ boundary bits
leaves a parity equation on at least three variables, which is not
2-CNF by Theorem~\ref{th:criterion}. Fixing $q-2$ leaves binary
parity, expressible by two binary clauses, on every branch.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact resources and source/witness mapping.}
The representation has $n=2q$, $m_2=2q-2$, $k=4q$ genuine ternary
clauses and $M=6q-2$. In the common fixed-width four-record grammar,
\[
w=\left\lceil\log_2(64(n+M+1))\right\rceil,\qquad
N=(24q-6)\left\lceil\log_2(64(8q-1))\right\rceil. \tag{7}
\]
The $x$ variables are original. The $y$ variables include one
free affine seed $y_0$; for specified $x,y_0$ the other $q-1$
are functional. No variable is silently eliminated in the input
($d_{\rm elim}=0$). After SCC substitution the $q$ original $x$
coordinates become one representative, eliminating $q-1$
syntactic variables; after affine reconstruction the consistent
state has precisely two free bits $(t,y_0)$.
The source is constructed by $4q$ certified XOR3 quartets and
$2q-2$ explicitly written binary clauses in $O(q)$ addressed-word
time, $O(q)$ peak words. An optional witness is constructed and
verified in $O(q)$ words. The raw (unreduced) affine subsystem and
the raw binary subsystem are each SAT on both combined labels;
the UNSAT result arises from their compatibility.

\section{A one-link compatibility theorem and strict acquisition bound}
Delete the equality link between $x_{r-1}$ and $x_r$,
$1\le r<q$. The binary quotient now has two free representatives
$t_L,t_R$. The projected affine relation is
\[
(r\bmod2)t_L\oplus((q-r)\bmod2)t_R
=\bigoplus_i\beta_i. \tag{8}
\]
For even $q$, if $r$ is odd, this is the binary parity
$t_L\oplus t_R=\bigoplus_i\beta_i$, always satisfiable,
with exactly four full extensions. If $r$ is even, this
is the constant condition $0=\bigoplus_i\beta_i$, giving
eight extensions when even and zero when odd. These claims
are \PROVED{} by substitution; tested source labels and model
counts are recorded below. A single missing cross-block link
can therefore change UNSAT to SAT, and cannot be omitted from
a detached compatibility summary.

For both the even- and odd-charge original $H_{q,\beta}$,
flipping any one $\beta_i$ changes the SAT label. In a canonical
quartet ordering indexed by $(y_{i-1},y_i)$ falsifying bits,
a flip of $\beta_i$ changes exactly the sign of the $x_i$
literal in each of the four private ternary clauses, preserving
all absolute variable identifiers, headers and total length.
Any deterministic exact addressed-word decider of the full
fixed mixed-width language must inspect at least one of those
four source words for every $i$, or else have an identical
transcript on a distinct-label neighbour. Therefore
\[
T_A(H_{q,\beta})\ge q
=\Omega(N/\log N) \tag{9}
\]
in that \emph{specific} addressed-word input-access model,
on \emph{both} labels. A recognized parity scan matches this
order. This is not an encoding-independent $\Omega(N)$ theorem
or post-acquisition superlinear lower bound. Grouped bit-packed
access must charge its actual word and extraction costs.

\section{Adaptive-state, switching, and charged ledger}
The tested uniform policy does not receive the SAT label.
On supported inputs it first parses source clauses, recognizes
the XOR quartets and binary implication graph, and attempts
SCC quotient. It then substitutes signed SCC equivalences
into the affine equations, checks consistency, computes
the projected covector signature if needed, and switches
to Gaussian, 2-CNF SCC or exact fallback. For the canonical
ring $H_{q,\beta}$ the quotient is source-checkable in $O(q)$
word time. A complete generic recognizer uses sorting and
full clause verification, with polynomial cost on other sources.

\section{2-SAT-to-NP-complete ledger and transport}
For the mandated baseline $F_b$, $n=q+3$, $m_2=q+4$,
$N_F=(4q+18)\lceil\log_2(64(2q+8))\rceil$,
and $F_b$ is UNSAT exactly when $b=1^q$.
Its all-one deterministic orientation-query depth is $q$,
matched by a linear source scan. The new cyclic family
is not claimed to be a SAT-label-preserving reduction of
the chain, so its independently proved lower bound is not
an uncharged transport of the chain bound.

For arbitrary 2-CNF plus $k$ genuine ternaries, choose one variable
from each ternary. Assigning their union $S$, $|S|\le k$,
reduces every branch to 2-CNF and gives the standard
$2^{|S|}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ \emph{upper} bound.
Fixed $k$ and $k=O(\log N)$ remain polynomial.
The present $H_{q,\beta}$ occupies a mixed affine/2-CNF
subclass with $k=4q$ and linear raw backdoor, but source-aware
SCC-to-affine cancellation decides it in $O(q)$ words.

At the explicitly NP-complete endpoint, use the Role~4
AFFINE-NULLITY-BOUNDARY73 graph-coloring compiler:
for each graph vertex introduce three indicators, one
odd XOR3 quartet (four proper ternaries), three local
negative NAND binaries, and for every edge three
cross-vertex negative NAND binaries. Then for a graph
of $v$ vertices and $e$ edges,
\[
n=3v,\quad m_2=3v+3e,\quad k=4v,\quad
M=7v+3e,\quad N=(28v+12e+2)
\left\lceil\log_2(64(10v+3e+1))\right\rceil.
\tag{10}
\]
The source graph has a proper 3-coloring iff the target
is SAT; witness mapping is bijective, with $O(v+e)$
source-indexed word construction and reconstruction.
This endpoint is \textbf{KNOWN NP-complete}, not a time
lower bound. The ring has no 2-CNF projection before
using the binary equivalences, but its easily discoverable
compatibility eliminates that obstruction. The important
missing theorem would have to survive every state-adaptive
policy, not a fixed scalar $\epsilon$, $h_2$ or $k$.
