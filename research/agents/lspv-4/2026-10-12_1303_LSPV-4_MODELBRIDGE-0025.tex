\begin{abstract}
P versus NP remains \OPEN.  This run closes a typing gap in the semantic
compatibility route.  A decider for the fixed NP-complete clean-core
\emph{language} cannot generally be called directly on
\(F\upharpoonright\alpha\), because literal restriction leaves the syntactic
promise.  Nevertheless a fixed polynomial many-one reduction back into the
core gives an exact external evaluator
\[
 E^{A_K}(F,\alpha)=A_K\!\left(r(F\upharpoonright\alpha)\right).
\]
The adapter is \EXACT, but its reduction and oracle call belong to a new
composed execution.  If \(A_K\) has unknown exponent \(K\) and the reduction
output has exponent \(a\), the charged composite retains \(aK\); it does not
show that the original call \(A_K(F)\) exposed compatibility or paid for the
adapter.  A paired \(K_{3,3}\) core instance gives an explicit counterexample
to the untyped direct call.  Thus the adapter repairs external evaluation but
not SAME\_REALIZATION transfer or a model-matched contradiction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim card: objects, quantifiers, and encoding}

Let \(K\subseteq\{0,1\}^*\) be the polynomial-time recognizable set of
canonical encodings of duplicate-free proper signed Monotone
\(3\)SAT-\((2,2)\) instances.  Define the total language
\[
 L_K=\{x:x\in K\text{ and the encoded formula is satisfiable}\}.
\]
Malformed and off-core strings are outside \(L_K\).  D\"ocker's theorem makes
\(L_K\) NP-complete \cite{Docker}.  Hence fix one polynomial many-one
reduction
\[
 r:\{0,1\}^*\longrightarrow\{0,1\}^*,
 \qquad
 G\in\SAT\Longleftrightarrow r(\operatorname{enc}G)\in L_K.       \tag{1}
\]
The reduction is part of the theorem, not an uncharged oracle.

For a valid CNF \(F\) on indexed variables and a consistent partial
assignment \(\alpha\), set
\[
 H(F,\alpha)=1
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \exists\beta\succeq\alpha\;[\beta\models F]
 \Longleftrightarrow
 F\upharpoonright\alpha\in\SAT.                                  \tag{2}
\]
The exact prefix proved here is
\[
 \exists r\in\mathsf{FP}\;
 \forall A_K\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm det,unif}(L_K)\;
 \exists E^{A_K}\;
 \forall(F,\alpha):
 E^{A_K}(F,\alpha)=H(F,\alpha).                                  \tag{3}
\]
Every machine is total on a complete self-delimiting encoding.  Input length
is \(L=|\operatorname{enc}(F,\alpha)|\).  Parsing, consistency checking,
restriction, reduction, serialization, the actual \(A_K\) call, output, and
peak workspace are charged.

\paragraph{SAME\_REALIZATION.}
The adapter and subcall form one ordinary realization of
\(E^{A_K}(F,\alpha)\).  They are not the original one-input execution
\(A_K(\operatorname{enc}F)\).  Equation (3) is therefore an external
black-box construction, not an internal exposure theorem.

\section{Exact adapter theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\claim{CORE-RESIDUAL-ADAPTER1}]
For the fixed reduction \(r\) in (1) and every total exact decider \(A_K\) of
\(L_K\), the machine
\[
 E^{A_K}(F,\alpha)
 =
 A_K\!\left(r(\operatorname{enc}(F\upharpoonright\alpha))\right) \tag{4}
\]
computes (2) on every valid pair \((F,\alpha)\).

Suppose, after converting all bounds to the input measure \(L\), restriction
and encoding take \(L^{u+o(1)}\) time, the reduction takes \(L^{b+o(1)}\)
time and produces at most \(L^{a+o(1)}\) bits, and
\[
 T_{A_K}(m)\le m^{K+o(1)},\qquad
 S_{A_K}(m)\le m^{S+o(1)}.
\]
Then the explicit-buffer realization satisfies
\[
 T_E(L)\le L^{\max\{u,b,aK\}+o(1)},                              \tag{5}
\]
and, if all non-subcall local buffers use \(L^{v+o(1)}\) cells,
\[
 S_E(L)\le L^{\max\{v,a,aS\}+o(1)}.                             \tag{6}
\]
Streaming may reduce the \(a\) buffer term when the chosen reduction and
machine interface permit it, but never removes the actual reduction work or
the \(aK\) time term.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Construct \(G=F\upharpoonright\alpha\).  By (2),
\(H(F,\alpha)=1\) exactly when \(G\in\SAT\).  Equation (1) makes this
equivalent to \(r(\operatorname{enc}G)\in L_K\), and exactness of \(A_K\)
proves (4).  The machine is uniform because restriction, \(r\), and \(A_K\)
are fixed uniform procedures.  Summing the three charged time terms gives
(5).  The explicit-buffer implementation retains only the local state,
the reduction output, and the active subcall workspace, giving (6).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Endpoint boundary]
A polynomial-time \(A_K\) makes (4) polynomial, and a polynomial-time
\(A_K\) exists exactly when \(\SAT\in\Pclass\), hence exactly when
\(\Pclass=\NPclass\).  The adapter is therefore not a weaker separation
intermediate.  Its exponent retains the unknown finite \(K\).
\end{corollary}

\section{Concrete direct-call counterexample}

Let \(F_{3,3}\) have one variable for each edge of \(K_{3,3}\).  For each of
the six vertices include the positive clause on its three incident edges and
the negative clause on the same three edges.  Then \(F_{3,3}\in K\), with
nine variables and twelve distinct proper clauses; each variable occurs twice
positively and twice negatively.  Perfect matchings satisfy it.

Fix any edge variable \(x_e\) to either bit.  The residual contains two
binary clauses and eight ternary clauses, so
\[
 F_{3,3}\upharpoonright(x_e=b)\notin K.                          \tag{7}
\]
Both bits extend to satisfying assignments.  Hence
\[
 H(F_{3,3},x_e=b)=1,
 \qquad
 A_K(\operatorname{enc}(F_{3,3}\upharpoonright(x_e=b)))=0,       \tag{8}
\]
because the residual encoding is outside the total language \(L_K\).
Thus the unqualified identity
\(A_K(F\upharpoonright\alpha)=H(F,\alpha)\) is false.  This retires
\claim{DIRECT-CORE-RESIDUAL-CALL1}.  Applying (1) before \(A_K\) repairs
correctness, but only in the external realization (4).

This does not contradict HEVALLB1.  Its direct restriction call uses a
\emph{global SAT decider}; its clean-core reverse direction uses a reduction
into the core.  MODELBRIDGE-0017 already noted that ordinary restriction
leaves the exact-occurrence family.  The present increment is the fully typed
core-language adapter, the explicit wrong-answer direct query (8), and its
time--space ledger.

\section{Implication for the project target}

The distinction between input scope and algorithm scope is now exact.
A direct theorem that every uniform decider for \(L_K\) is superpolynomial
already proves \(L_K\notin\Pclass\), hence \(P\ne NP\); it needs no transfer
to every unrestricted SAT input family.  By contrast, a lower bound proved
only for a chosen representation, protocol, proof system, HEC strategy,
communication interface, or residual service still needs a compiler from
\emph{every} decider for \(L_K\) into that model.

Equation (4) supplies only external semantic access.  It does not imply that
\(A_K(F)\) materializes \(H\), maintains a compatibility table, exposes a
witness, communicates across a cut, or pays for reduction calls.  Retrospective
charging would violate SAME\_REALIZATION.  DELAY, COPY, DEAD, INTERP and
RECOMP wrappers leave (4)'s answer correct but alter actual executions; the
source exponent and every adapter cost remain charged.  Therefore
\claim{ADAPTER-TO-ORIGINAL-EXPOSURE1} is \RETIRED.

\section{Hostile controls}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0cm,labelwidth=0cm]
\item[] \textbf{Horn, dual-Horn, 2SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded treewidth.}
Restriction and specialized polynomial algorithms make the semantic service
easy.  The adapter theorem still holds, but hidden structure is exploitable
rather than a hardness source.
\item[] \textbf{Independent products and repeated components.}
The one-bit answer can share work or use memoization; (4) does not prove a
direct sum.
\item[] \textbf{Padding, redundant clauses, alternate encodings.}
Parsing, normalization and reduction are charged in \(u,b,a\).  Inflating the
source or changing addresses cannot create a free favorable exponent.
\item[] \textbf{One-bit output and wrappers.}
The original run returns one bit.  An external transcript or compatibility
answer belongs to the composed machine, exactly as MODELBRIDGE-0022/0023 and
WRAPORBIT1 require.
\end{description}

\section{Barrier-transfer and primary-literature preflight}

The two blocking obstacles were (i) promise nonclosure under restriction and
(ii) transfer from external semantic access to arbitrary original execution.
Cook and Karp supply the directly transferable reduction architecture
\cite{Cook,Karp}; D\"ocker supplies NP-completeness of the chosen core
\cite{Docker}.  This proves (4), but does not prove original-run exposure.
Schaefer's dichotomy is a directly relevant source of easy hostile controls,
not an arbitrary-SAT lower bound \cite{Schaefer}.

FLMV and later time--space work give unconditional lower bounds only after
one declared random-access realization meets their simultaneous resource
hypotheses \cite{FLMV,MW}.  No theorem found transfers the adapter's external
work to the original \(A_K(F)\) call.  VLSI routing, control observability,
distributed cuts, branching programs, pebbling and feasible interpolation
are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} without an interface-preserving compiler.
Local decoding and black-box worst-case/average-case architecture are
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} requiring a new bridge.

The theorem and counterexample relativize: use the same oracle in the
reduction and deciders.  They also survive algebraic-oracle access and hence
do not cross the Baker--Gill--Solovay or Aaronson--Wigderson barriers
\cite{BGS,AW}.  Natural Proofs does not supply the missing compiler
\cite{RR}.  Nonrelativization and nonalgebrization remain \OPEN.

\paragraph{Post-quantum technique ledger.}
Schur squares, puncturing/shortening, low-rank quadratic relations, Hilbert
functions, syzygies and graded Betti numbers are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}
unless an arbitrary decider is forced to realize the relevant representation.
Collision entropy is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER} only for a
declared proof-to-node map and gives no runtime price for (4).  LWE-style
black-box extraction is an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge}.
Special soundness/collision extraction is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} without
two conflicting valid openings yielding an unconditional forbidden witness.
Hash and random-oracle methods remain engineering analogies.  In every case,
visible algebra may make the instance easier.
