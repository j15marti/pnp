\section{PROPOSE: the missing realizability lemma}
The active research asks for simultaneous time--space $(T,S)$ frontiers from combinatorial reduction inequalities. A necessary static-interface inequality is useful only when its permitted integer points are not confused with realizable decompositions or unrestricted runtime bounds. The specific missing lemma is whether the improved quotient-incidence inequality $k+h\le3b+1$ can be \emph{attained by genuine connected proper strict signed exact-(2,2) 3CNFs} with true syntactic private hitting sets, or whether signed incidence adds a further combinatorial deficit.

The falsifiable mechanisms are: (M1) an exact22 occurrence/parity constraint forbids saturating $3b+1$; (M2) signed saturation is possible but requires $q$ to grow with $b$; (M3) a connector-tree plus constant-size private gadgets saturates the incidence count with $q=2$; (M4) charging local-backdoor discovery changes the time proxy near its continuous minimum. We construct a counterexample to M1 and M2, establish M3, and calculate M4 without promoting a proxy to a SAT lower bound.

\section{Known connected-component inequality: independent short proof}
Let a connected proper signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF have $n$ variables, each of degree four. Let $Y$ be the fixed boundary of $b$ variables, $Z_1,\ldots,Z_k$ disjoint nonempty private modules, with no clause containing private names of distinct modules. Let $h$ count clauses lying wholly inside $Y$, and let $B_i\subseteq Z_i$ hit every clause of module $i$ disjoint from $Y$, with $|B_i|\le q$.

\begin{lemma}[Already known: quotient incidence edge budget]\label{lem:known}
One has $k+h\le3b+1$ and
\[n\le3b+3kq\le3(b+q)+9bq.\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Contract every private module together with its clauses containing private variables to one vertex of the source incidence graph. Retain each boundary variable and each of the $h$ boundary-only clause vertices. The connected quotient has $b+k+h$ vertices and exactly $4b$ boundary-incidence edges, counted with multiplicity. Hence $b+k+h-1\le4b$. Every original clause is hit by $Y\cup\bigcup_iB_i$, and each hit variable meets four clauses, giving $4n/3\le4(b+\sum_i|B_i|)$; substitute $k\le3b+1-h$.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD: a source-realizing equality family}
\begin{theorem}[New, restricted: incidence bound is sharp inside strict22]\label{thm:sharp}
For every integer $b\ge1$ there is an explicitly constructible connected proper signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF $F_b$, with a fixed boundary of $b$ variables, such that
\[
 k=3b+1,\quad h=0,\quad n=15b+6,\quad m=20b+8,
 \quad\min |B_i|=q=2\ \text{for every private module.}\]
Every $F_b$ is satisfiable by the all-true assignment. The source and claimed decomposition are uniformly constructible and verifiable in polynomial time. Thus the coefficient $3b+1$ and additive $+1$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:known} cannot be improved using only its stated hypotheses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Arrange boundary vertices $y_1,\ldots,y_b$ as a path. Between $y_i$ and $y_{i+1}$ put one \emph{connector} private module. Attach three leaves to each endpoint (when $b>1$), two leaves to each internal boundary vertex, and four leaves when $b=1$. Thus there are $b-1$ connectors and $2b+2$ leaves, so $k=3b+1$. Every boundary appears in exactly four module edges and therefore four source clauses. Each connector has four private names $a,b,c,d$, with clauses
\[
(y_i\vee a\vee b),\ (y_{i+1}\vee c\vee d),\
(a\vee b\vee c),\ (a\vee b\vee d),\ (a\vee c\vee d),\ (b\vee c\vee d).
\]
The displayed signs are placeholders for occurrence-specific sign choices below. Each leaf has five private names $a,b,c,d,e$, one boundary clause $(y_i\vee a\vee b)$, and six private clauses
\[(a,c,d),\ (a,c,e),\ (a,d,e),\ (b,c,d),\ (b,c,e),\ (b,d,e).\]
Again, notation here lists unsigned scopes. Choose the \emph{positive} occurrence indices of each private variable (zero-based, in displayed order) as follows: connector $a:0,2$, $b:0,2$, $c:1,4$, $d:3,5$; leaf $a:0,1$, $b:0,4$, $c:1,2$, $d:1,3$, $e:5,6$. Every other private occurrence is negative. Each boundary $y_i$ is positive on any two of its four incident clauses and negative on the other two. Thus all original variables have exactly two positive and two negative appearances, and every clause has a positive private literal. All-true satisfies the whole source.

Every scope has three different names. Each module's private incidence graph is connected; after deleting all boundary variables each module remains a distinct connected component. No clause lies wholly in $Y$. The leaves have six internal clauses hit by $\{a,b\}$, and connectors have four internal clauses hit by $\{a,c\}$. No singleton hits all these internal clauses, since the intersection of all private scopes is empty. So the exact minimum in every module is two. The number of variables is $b+4(b-1)+5(2b+2)=15b+6$ and the number of clauses is $6(b-1)+7(2b+2)=20b+8=4n/3$. The contracted incidence graph is precisely the path-with-leaves tree with $b+k=4b+1$ vertices and $4b$ edges. Therefore $k=3b+1$ realizes equality, for every $b$.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Scope firewall.} Our family does not attain equality in the $n\le3(b+q)+9bq$ clause-hitting bound, and its static $q=2$ grows neither with $b$ nor with $n$. The all-true witness defeats any attempt to convert this static boundary burden into an intrinsic SAT-time lower bound. The theorem addresses only the \emph{quotient component bound}.

\section{Normalized time--space proxy geometry, old and refined}
For a declared structural relaxation with $b>0$, the older necessary inequality gives
\[
 q_{\rm old}(b)=\max\left(0,\left\lceil\frac{n-3b}{12b}\right\rceil\right),
 \quad t_{\rm old}=b+q_{\rm old},\quad s_{\rm table}=b.
\]
The already-known connected sharpening gives
\[
 q_{\rm sharp}(b)=\max\left(0,\left\lceil\frac{n-3b}{9b+3}\right\rceil\right),
 \quad t_{\rm sharp}=b+q_{\rm sharp}.\tag{1}
\]
The associated illustrative time and materialized-boundary-table space are $T_{\rm proxy}=2^{t_{\rm sharp}}\mathrm{poly}(N)$ and $S_{\rm table}=2^{b}\mathrm{poly}(N)$; they are \emph{neither model-wide lower bounds nor evidence of an actual runtime frontier}. A one-shot assignment enumerator can omit the $2^b$ table and stream in $\mathrm{poly}(N)$ bits.

In the positive-$q$ continuous relaxation,
\[
 t(b)=b+\frac{n-3b}{9b+3},\quad s(b)=b,\quad
 \frac{dt}{ds}=1-\frac{9(n+1)}{(9b+3)^2},\quad
 \frac{d^2t}{ds^2}=\frac{162(n+1)}{(9b+3)^3}>0.
\]
The unique continuous minimum is at $b^*=q^*=(\sqrt{n+1}-1)/3$, with minimum $t^*=\frac23(\sqrt{n+1}-1)$; $t^*=\frac23\sqrt n+O(1)$. At fixed $n$, the derivative tends to $-n$ as $b\downarrow0$ and approaches $1$ for $b\gg\sqrt n$ before the $q=0$ truncation; the full curve becomes $t=s=b$ for $b\ge n/3$. For exact22 sources $n$ is a multiple of three; the optimal integer arithmetic relaxation is $\lceil t^*\rceil$, verified independently for 2000 admissible $n$ values. This is \emph{arithmetic permitted}, not a proof of realizability for the optimizing $(b,q)$.

For $n=1200$, old integer time exponent $20$ occurs at $b=9,10,11$, while the sharp exponent $23$ occurs at $b=9,10,11,12,13,14$. For a materialized table only $b=9$ among those equal-time minima is nondominated. The first \emph{condition-permitted} equality $t=s$ is $b=400,q=0$, and the log-proxy intersection $(400,400)$ is dominated by $(23,9)$; a separate LSPV-5 construction witnesses a $q=0$ endpoint but no entire proxy curve has been shown realizable. Rounded arithmetic values and SHA-reproducible raw points are in \texttt{n1200\_frontier.csv}.

\paragraph{Paid local discovery.} If the full boundary, module partition and clause ownership are supplied and verified, but the private hitting sets are not, ternary hitting search uses at most $3^q\mathrm{poly}(N)$ work per module. A deliberately exhaustive one-pass static decision implementation has the conservative upper bound
\[
 T\le \mathrm{poly}(N)\,k\bigl(3^q+2^{b+q}\bigr),\qquad
 \log_2 T=O(\log N)+\max\{q\log_2 3,b+q\}.
\]
This includes unsuccessful search branches, $2^{b+q}$ local assignments, all local 2SAT calls, joins, verification, and witness construction. For $n=1200$, the paid-discovery continuous crossover is near $b=8.57$; its integer permitted minimum exponent remains $23$ at $b=9$ through $14$. Uncharged \emph{global} boundary/partition discovery is not inferred, and in general is the missing constructive bridge.

\section{Separate machine-native upper-bound ledgers}
Write $A=k(3^q+2^{b+q})$ and let $N$ count the explicit source plus proposed decomposition encoding. All bounds below are conservative \emph{constructive upper ceilings for a supplied and authenticated decomposition}, not measured transition counts or lower-bound exponents. Using standard bitwise scans, sorting and repeated head movement, sufficiently generous fixed model bounds are:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.36\linewidth}p{0.28\linewidth}p{0.27\linewidth}}\toprule
Model & Time upper ceiling & Peak physical memory ceiling\\\midrule
Fixed $r$-work-tape TM with read-only source & $O(AN^5)$ transitions & $O(N^2)$ writable visited cells; source separately $N$\\
Fixed fully writable two-tape TM & $O(AN^7)$ transitions & tape 2 $O(N^2)$ cells; original source remains on tape 1\\
Deterministic single read/write tape & $O(AN^7)$ transitions & $O(N^2)$ total visited footprint, including initial input $N$\\
Indexed RAM, $O(\log N)$-bit words & $O(AN^4)$ elementary word steps & $O(N^2)$ bits of work plus source\\
Fixed universal TM & polynomial overhead from compiled finite machine & no certified precise exponent; tape/input included\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
A canonical single-tape rescan simulation of $T_r=O(AN^5)$ and $S_r=O(N^2)$ yields $T_1=O(T_r(N+S_r+\log(T_r+2)))=O(AN^7)$, since $\log A=O(N)$ under explicitly encoded parameters; its footprint is $O(N+S_r+\log(T_r+2))=O(N^2)$. The two-writable-tape ceiling can conservatively emulate that single-tape computation. Counters for source probes, tape crossings and failed choices are components of transition time, not extra multipliers. If storing all $2^b$ boundary truth rows, peak space instead adds $\Omega(2^b)$ output/table bits for that specific materialized architecture; streaming avoids this. No single-tape lower bound is transferred to RAM, and the signed exact22 promise is not a general SAT hardness lower bound.
