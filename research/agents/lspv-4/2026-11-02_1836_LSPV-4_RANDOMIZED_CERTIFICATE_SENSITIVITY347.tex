\section*{Exact obstruction and randomized theorem}
An inference DAG can be short without authenticating its claimed source. Fix one structurally valid CNF $F$ whose $L$ literal-occurrence sign fields are independently encoded as distinct source bits, such that reversing any one sign gives a valid SAT formula $F^{(i)}$. Fix an arbitrary proof string $\pi$. Consider any randomized sound certificate verifier $V(F,\pi;r)$, with the same randomness space for all inputs and independently chargeable source-bit queries. Assume
\[
\Pr_r[V(F,\pi;r)=1]\ge c,\qquad
\Pr_r[V(F^{(i)},\pi;r)=1]\le s<c
\quad (1\le i\le L).
\]
The soundness requirement is for the \emph{same fixed proof} on the SAT neighbor. The checker may adaptively read the source and proof, move heads and indices, switch proof representations, or recompute shared DAG nodes.

\begin{theorem}[Same-certificate randomized source-probe bound]
For each sign field $i$, the probability that $V$ probes that field on the original pair $(F,\pi)$ is at least $c-s$. Hence
\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_r[\#\text{ distinct original sign fields read}]\ge(c-s)L.}
\]
When one source access reveals at most $w$ sign fields, expected ordinary source probes are at least $(c-s)L/w$. A perfectly sound deterministic verifier accepting $\pi$ reads all $L$ sign fields. Under completeness $1-\delta$ and soundness error $\epsilon$, the bound is $(1-\delta-\epsilon)L$ when positive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Couple the two executions on $(F,\pi)$ and $(F^{(i)},\pi)$ with the same random tape. Unless the first run reads changed sign field $i$, both runs have identical replies to every query and follow identical transcripts, so they produce the same output. With acceptance indicators $A,A_i$ and original-run event $Q_i$ of querying the field,
\[
\Pr(Q_i)\ge\mathbb E|A-A_i|\ge|\mathbb EA-\mathbb EA_i|\ge c-s.
\]
Sum over fields by linearity of expectation. A $w$-bit probe accounts for at most $w$ field observations. No independence among queries or random coins is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
Every clause-minimally UNSAT CNF with independently encoded literal-occurrence signs satisfies the theorem for any fixed accepted proof. For each source clause $C$, a witness for $F\setminus\{C\}$ falsifies every literal in $C$; reversing any one of them makes the whole original formula SAT.
\end{corollary}
