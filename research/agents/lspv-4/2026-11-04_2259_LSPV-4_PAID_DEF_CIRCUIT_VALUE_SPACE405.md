## ITERATE: proved restricted semantic-query lemma
Fix SAT source F+=(x) AND (x OR y). One source-authenticated AND3 factor A=x has two satisfying assignments. Consider a legal acyclic sequence of q individually charged conservative two-input DEF nodes; each fresh defined wire is ultimately a function of x alone. Define CV-DEF as the question: is the last DEF output equal to 1 when x=1? This **semantic output query** is P-complete under deterministic logspace reductions.

Proof: membership in P by topological gate evaluation. For hardness take Goldschlager's monotone Circuit Value instance (input bits plus AND/OR DAG). Introduce two paid total DEF gates c0=XOR(x,x) and c1=XNOR(x,x), giving 0 and 1 under every original assignment. Map each specified input bit to c0 or c1. Translate each original binary AND/OR gate into one fresh paid conservative two-input DEF node with corresponding earlier inputs; add a paid identity if output is a leaf. This is a uniform logspace reduction, q<=m+3 for m gates, and explicit encoding size O((m+b+1)log(m+b+2)) for b bits. All outputs have support at most {x}; the designated output equals the original CVP answer. Hence P-hardness. No free semantic oracle, no unpaid gates and no nonuniform per-N advice. QED.

If the conditional query had a deterministic polytime and O(log M)-writable-space algorithm on M-bit supplied traces, it would imply P=L. ## Exact established-hardness anchors, and their limits
**Haken (1985), ordinary Resolution:** For the explicitly signed unary pigeonhole CNF PHP_(h+1)^h, with h+1 pigeons, h holes, Boolean indicators p_(i,j), one at-least-one-hole clause per pigeon and one binary no-collision clause for every pigeon pair and hole, every ordinary shared-DAG Resolution refutation has at least 2^{Omega(h)} lines for sufficiently large h. This is a quantified *Resolution proof-size* lower bound, not an unrestricted machine-time bound. In a standard signed textual encoding the source has Theta(h^3) literal occurrences and N=Theta(h^3 log h) bits; the exact byte count depends on the chosen encoding. Native R_mix341 additionally has AND3, GF(2), LOCAL and paid DEF; Haken's result does not quantify over those proofs.

**Braun, arXiv:2609.23015 (September 2026 preprint), Res(oplus):** The stated theorem covers every DAG-like refutation in Resolution over Parities of the *bit* pigeonhole formula with n+1 pigeons and n=2^ell holes, ell>=32, and requires more than exp(n/(32768 ell^2)) clauses. Lines are disjunctions of GF(2) affine equations with that system's resolution/weakening grammar. The preprint is not an all-algorithm SAT lower bound and is not a bound on the distinct fixed R_mix341. Cook's polynomial-size Extended Resolution PHP proofs and direct GF(2) Tseitin refutations are mandatory controls against any naïve hardness transfer.
