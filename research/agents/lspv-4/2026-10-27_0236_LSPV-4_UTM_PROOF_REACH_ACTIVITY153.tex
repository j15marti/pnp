\section{Single target, exact quantifiers and four pre-search falsifiers}
Fix \emph{one} deterministic interpreter $U$ with $q$ control states, \emph{one} two-way sequential read-only SAT-source head, \emph{one} independent two-way sequential read-only proof head, and $k\ge1$ fixed sequential initially blank read/write work tapes over a fixed alphabet of size $g$. Both read-only tapes have fixed left endmarkers; the proof-head starts at its leftmost content cell, moves by at most one cell per charged transition and has a visible endmarker. It cannot teleport and cannot read a cell beyond the position it reaches. Output is decision-only (or write-only one-way and never reread). No other writable storage or auxiliary moving head is free. Proof strings may be arbitrarily long and may be selected adaptively with hindsight, but every accepting run must be \emph{globally sound} on NO source inputs for the chosen proof. There is no separate source-left input-dependent program in the theorem; the interpreter itself is fixed. This avoids a hidden program-portfolios hypothesis.

For the authenticated W1 signed exact-$(2,2)$ family $F_m(a,b)$, $\SAT(F_m(a,b))\iff\forall_i\neg(a_i\land b_i)$. For each $a$, the diagonal $F_m(a,\bar a)$ is SAT; for $a\ne a'$, at least one cross-source $F_m(a,\bar a')$ or $F_m(a',\bar a)$ is UNSAT. Each source has physical bit length $N=\Theta(m\log(m+2))$ with a fixed central source gap containing $G=\Theta(N)$ cuts. The exact target quantifier order is
\[
\exists U\;\forall m\;\forall a\in\{0,1\}^m\;\exists\pi_a:\;
U(F_m(a,\bar a),\pi_a)=\mathrm{accept},
\quad \forall b,\pi:\;\neg\SAT(F_m(a,b))\Rightarrow U(F_m(a,b),\pi)\ne\mathrm{accept},
\]
subject to common \emph{simultaneous} resource ceilings on all selected diagonal accepting runs. This is a \emph{restricted implication}, not an assumption about arbitrary ordinary SAT input coverage. The exponent labels below refer to counts of changed \emph{cells}, not an additional additive time charge beyond the TM transitions already counted.

\begin{description}

\section{Visible-prefix splicing and two independent epoch caps}
On a selected accepting run let $S$ be the \emph{total visited} work cells (blank included), $D$ the total number of individual work-cell symbol changes across every work tape (so simultaneous changes on distinct tapes count separately; rewriting an unchanged symbol does not), $R$ the number of source-head reversals, $T$ the number of transitions, $H_\pi$ the number of genuine proof-head one-cell moves, and $J_\pi$ the maximal proof-head coordinate measured from the leftmost content cell. Let $P$ be the common cap on physical proof content bits, with a unique endmarker; all these ceilings hold on the \emph{same} accepted run. Put
\[
h=2S+3,\quad V=2qh^k,\quad A=\min\{P+1,J_\pi+1\},\quad
\Gamma=2^{A+1}-1,
\quad C_R=2+VA+khg,\quad C_H=4+V+khg.
\tag{1}
\]
The cap $A$ is on the observable initial proof prefix, including any endmarker reached. The $\Gamma$ expression safely counts all such binary prefixes, allowing a visible early endmarker or a length-$A$ binary prefix. All constants $q,k,g$ are fixed; $A,h,V,C_R,C_H$ are \emph{uniform} upper bounds, not per-input minima used as caps.

\begin{lemma}[Visible-prefix reduction and splicing]\label{lem:prefix}
For each fixed central gap cut $c$, diagonal accepted sources grouped by their visible proof prefixes have pairwise different \emph{complete} source-cut crossing sequences. Consequently the whole family has at most $\Gamma$ such groups and the number of diagonal accepted inputs is at most $\Gamma$ times the maximum number of complete crossing histories per group.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The proof head starts at coordinate zero and can consult only the $A$-cell initial segment it reaches. Its behavior on a given source is unchanged under replacement of any longer unvisited suffix. A crossing label includes source crossing direction, finite control state, $k$ work-head coordinates, the entire mutable work-tape content, and the proof-head coordinate. Two accepted diagonal sources using proofs with the same visible prefix and identical complete crossing histories admit the standard deterministic shore splice: retain Alice's source-left shore and the proof of the first accepted run, and Bob's right shore from the second accepted run. Every segment of the resulting execution follows a segment of one original run, because the work configurations and proof-head coordinates match at the splice, and the proof bits at every reachable proof cell agree. This accepts an off-diagonal NO cross-source, contradicting soundness. A swapped orientation yields a NO in at least one direction. The number of binary strings with a visible endmarker before cell $A$, plus length-$A$ binary prefixes, is $(2^A-1)+2^A=\Gamma$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Work-frozen and doubly-frozen cut crossings]\label{lem:epochs}
At every fixed gap cut, the number $L_c$ of source-head crossings satisfies
\[
 L_c\le R+1,\qquad
 L_c\le(D+1)VA+D,\qquad
 L_c\le(D+H_\pi+1)V+D+H_\pi.
\tag{2}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Successive crossings alternate direction, establishing the first bound. At most $D$ transitions with work changes divide the halting run into at most $D+1$ epochs of fixed work contents. At a crossing during one such epoch, finite state, direction, work-head coordinates and proof-head position admit at most $VA$ possibilities. Repeating one would repeat a complete deterministic machine configuration at the same source cut while both read-only tape contents and work tape contents are fixed, forcing an infinite loop. Account conservatively for at most $D$ crossings coincident with a work-changing transition. This proves the second bound. Partition more finely at any genuine work change \emph{or} genuine proof-head move: at most $D+H_\pi$ eventful transitions and $D+H_\pi+1$ quiet epochs. The proof-head coordinate is fixed within each quiet epoch, so only $V$ crossing labels remain. Charge at most $D+H_\pi$ eventful-transition crossings. This proves the last bound.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Two exact finite activity/reach chronicle inequalities]\label{th:packing}
Under the displayed global soundness, finite input-family and same-run resource hypotheses,
\[
\boxed{2^m\le\Gamma C_R^{(D+1)VA+2D+1}},\qquad
\boxed{2^m\le\Gamma C_H^{(D+H_\pi+1)V+2(D+H_\pi)+1}}.
\tag{3}
\]
There are also the separately charged source-reversal and gap-averaged time bounds
\[
\boxed{2^m\le\Gamma C_R^{R+D+2}},\qquad
\boxed{2^m\le\Gamma G C_R^{\lfloor T/G\rfloor+D+1}}.
\tag{4}
\]
No assertion transfers these formulas to a machine with a second \emph{source} head or uncharged indexed source/proof addresses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the first transcript code, record every actual work-symbol change by (tape index, absolute visited coordinate, new symbol), and record every cut crossing by (direction, finite state, all work-head coordinates, proof-head coordinate). Replay from initially blank tapes to reconstruct all full work contents at every crossing. With a fixed visible proof prefix, Lemma~\ref{lem:prefix} makes these event words injective; each is at most $D+L_c$ letters over an alphabet of size $C_R$. Hence at most $C_R^{D+L_c+1}$ words. Apply the second bound in Lemma~\ref{lem:epochs} and multiply by $\Gamma$.

For the second code, additionally record each proof-head $\pm1$ move in chronological order, and omit the proof coordinate from the crossing letter because the move history determines it. Its word length is at most $D+H_\pi+L_c$ with alphabet bounded by $C_H$. The third bound in Lemma~\ref{lem:epochs} yields the second inequality. If an edit, proof move and source crossing coincide, use the fixed canonical order edit, proof move, crossing; post-transition crossing labels are recorded. For (4), use $L_c\le R+1$, and for each accepted source select a gap cut with at most $\lfloor T/G\rfloor$ crossings; encode its cut ID among $G$ choices and apply the first transcript. The source head moves only one cell per charged transition, so $\sum_{c\text{ in gap}}L_c\le T$.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strict exponent consequences; same machine]\label{th:exponents}
Suppose, on all selected diagonal accepted runs, $D+1\le N^{d+o(1)}$, $S+1\le N^{s+o(1)}$, $A\le N^{\beta+o(1)}$, $H_\pi+1\le N^{a+o(1)}$, $R+1\le N^{r+o(1)}$, and $T\le N^{t+o(1)}$. Then the restrictions include
\[
\boxed{d+ks+\beta\ge1},\qquad
\boxed{\max\{d,a\}+ks\ge1},\qquad
\boxed{\max\{r,d\}+\beta\ge1},\qquad
\boxed{\max\{t-1,d\}+\beta\ge1}.
\tag{5}
\]
For $\beta<1$, the last two can be sharpened to
\[
\boxed{\max\{r,d\}\ge1,\qquad\max\{t-1,d\}\ge1}.
\tag{6}
\]
When $\beta\ge1$, the advice-prefix factor itself may consume $\Theta(2^m)$ possibilities, so no contradiction is asserted from (6). Equality on any exponent face is not an exclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$ and $\log C_R,\log C_H=O(\log N)$ under the polynomial resource assumptions, take logarithms of (3)--(4). The $\log\Gamma=O(A)$ term is $o(m)$ if $\beta<1$; otherwise the stated $+\beta$ inequalities are vacuous. In the first inequality, $(D+1)VA$ has exponent $d+ks+\beta$. The second has exponent $\max(d,a)+ks$. For the two bounds in (4), the stated weaker $+\beta$ bounds are always necessary and the stronger claims follow for $\beta<1$ because the advice term is negligible. All factors and exponents refer to one fixed interpreter and one bound on its diagonal accepted runs.\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Quantitative novelty.} If $P=N^2$ but the proof head reaches only $N^{.2}$ cells and moves at most $N^{.3}$ times, while $S=N^{.3}$ and $D=N^{.1}$ with one work tape, the earlier proof-\emph{length} face $d+ks+\ell\ge1$ is vacuous ($2.4\ge1$). The new \emph{reach} face is $.1+.3+.2=.6<1$ and the \emph{activity} face is $\max(.1,.3)+.3=.6<1$, both strictly excluded within this stronger two-read-head \emph{promise} model. This is not an unrestricted SAT lower bound.
