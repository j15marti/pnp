\section{PROPOSE: four falsifiable mechanisms fixed before source search}
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{M1, logical equivalence.} A uniformly paid deterministic, answer-preserving power shrinker on an encoded NP-complete SAT class with fixed YES/NO representatives is \emph{equivalent in existence} to SAT in $P$. Falsifier: a class/encoding violating the fixed-representative, closure, or total-computation hypotheses.
\item[] \textbf{M2, weaker progress measure.} A single-child successor that decreases physical bit length by only one, rather than a fixed power, already gives a polynomial-time decision procedure if each paid step is polynomial and the bounded bases are decidable. Falsifier: a legal computation with superpolynomially many such decreases.
\item[] \textbf{M3, polynomial-value rank.} Power shrinkage is unnecessary when every complete intermediate state is polynomially bounded and a nonnegative integer rank of \emph{polynomial value} strictly decreases. Falsifier: an exponential run satisfying all resource and rank-value hypotheses. A polynomial-bit-length rank alone is deliberately tested as a negative control.
\item[] \textbf{M4, semantic moving guard.} The recent LSPV-2 four-variable moving-guard projection preserves the entire Boolean function of surviving variables, rather than merely the current SAT label, for its consecutive-triple grammar. Falsifier: a small signed chain plus arbitrary end guard whose projected function differs from the child.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD: exact logical equivalence and weaker sufficient descent}
Fix a canonically encoded total decision language $L$ such as ordinary proper-3CNF-SAT. Assume valid instances are polynomial-time recognizable and contain two fixed, valid encodings $y\in L$ and $z\notin L$; both classes of labels occur. Define \textsc{PowerChild}$(L)$ to assert that constants $0<\lambda<1$, $N_0$ and a \emph{uniform deterministic} polynomial-time transducer $f$ exist such that for every valid $x$ with $|x|>N_0$, $f(x)$ is valid, $|f(x)|\le|x|^\lambda$, and $x\in L\iff f(x)\in L$. Inputs of length at most $N_0$ are decided directly by one fixed machine.

\begin{theorem}[Existential equivalence, no SAT resolution]\label{thm:equiv}
For every $L$ satisfying the preceding explicit encoding and representative hypotheses,
\[
\boxed{\textsc{PowerChild}(L)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L\in P.}
\]
In particular, on ordinary proper-3CNF, existence of the stipulated unconditional single-child shrinker is equivalent to $P=NP$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If \textsc{PowerChild}$(L)$ holds, iteratively apply $f$ to the single physical child until the fixed base threshold is reached. Answer preservation composes, and $N_{i+1}\le N_i^\lambda$ implies at most $O(\log\log N)$ steps. Each step runs in time polynomial in its current bitlength, including all source access, failed attempts internal to $f$, output generation and parsing. Thus the total is polynomial and decides $L$.

Conversely, if $L\in P$, choose one fixed polynomial-time decider $A$. Define $f(x)=y$ when $A(x)$ accepts and $f(x)=z$ otherwise. The fixed $y,z$ are legitimate members of the same encoded class, so $f$ is uniform, deterministic, correct on \emph{both} answer classes and makes no uncharged oracle call. Choose $N_0$ large enough that $\max(|y|,|z|)\le N^\lambda$ for all $N>N_0$. The transducer's cost includes the full decision by $A$. Therefore \textsc{PowerChild}$(L)$ holds. This converse is a conditional logical implication from $L\in P$, \emph{not} an unconditional construction of $A$.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Interpretation.} The power-short architecture is not necessary as the \emph{form} of a polynomial SAT algorithm. Nevertheless, the \emph{existence claim} is necessary and sufficient under the above very permissive specification, because an already known polynomial decider can emit fixed answer-labeled bases. Proving the shrinker without first assuming an SAT decider would therefore solve the original open problem, not evade it. To formulate a genuinely more structural target, one must add an independent, checkable non-decision-first restriction and justify why that restriction can be met.

\begin{theorem}[Strict physical-length descent suffices]\label{thm:linear}
Replace $N_{i+1}\le N_i^\lambda$ by $N_{i+1}\le N_i-1$ above, and assume all source reentry, computation, child construction, failed trials and parsing cost at most $C N_i^c$ for a fixed $c\ge1$. Then iterative single-child evaluation terminates in at most $N-N_0$ steps and uses
\[
T(N)\le C\sum_{j=N_0+1}^N j^c+\mathrm{poly}(N)=O(N^{c+1}).
\]
If one can implement each step in a fixed multitape model with two reusable physical buffers and $O(N_i^s)$ work cells, the combined implementation can use $O(N^{\max(1,s)})$ \emph{peak} work cells. It need not retain its ancestors. With an alternative recursion that retains every old frame, the safe upper bound may instead be $O(N^{s+1}+N^2)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Physical length is a nonnegative integer and drops at least one, so the trajectory has at most $N-N_0$ nonterminal states; each has length at most $N$. Sum the charged step budgets. A two-buffer implementation erases or reuses a predecessor after writing its successor, whose length is at most $N$; preallocated work for the step is bounded by $O(N^s)$. An implementation that does retain all frames must add their footprints, hence the more conservative sum bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Paid polynomial-rank descent with temporary growth]
Suppose all reachable complete states from an $N$-bit input have at most $N^\alpha$ physically represented bits, an integer-valued rank $R$ initially at most $N^\rho$, and $R$ decreases by at least one on each transition. If parsing, selection, unsuccessful attempts, source access, output, and rank updates cost at most $O(N^{\alpha c})$ \emph{per transition}, and final zero-rank states are directly decidable, then time is $O(N^{\rho+\alpha c})$ plus initialization. Neither a one-child power shrink nor monotone intermediate bitlength is required.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
There are at most $N^\rho$ transitions. Each is charged by hypothesis. The bound on complete state size prevents hidden exponential physical objects. Correctness follows by composing the exact transition invariant. The claims are conditional on proving this rank and transition discipline for the chosen class.
\end{proof}

A short \emph{encoding} of the rank does not suffice. The $n$-bit binary counter initialized at $2^n-1$ decreases by one for $2^n-1$ transitions. This finite-state counterexample falsifies the claim that polynomial rank bitlength alone ensures polynomial termination.

\section{Model-native ledgers: no forbidden-region transfer}
Let $c\ge1$ be a natively compiled fixed-multitape step exponent and $s\ge0$ the per-step work-space exponent. This section is a \emph{conditional construction ledger}; no unrestricted SAT transducer has been supplied.
Put $u=\max(1,s)$. A conservative summary in the exponent ledger is
\begin{center}\small\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Scenario & Fixed-$k$ resource ceilings & Simulated single-tape ceilings\\\midrule
Power shrink & $t_k\le c,\quad s_k\le u$ & $t_1\le c+u,\quad p_1\le u$\\
Strict length decrease & $t_k\le c+1,\quad s_k\le u$ & $t_1\le c+1+u,\quad p_1\le u$\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
The stated fixed-$k$ implementations require enough \emph{fixed} tapes to emulate the transducer while physically holding two successive children; their reentry/copy costs are included in $c$ or the displayed polylog factor. The one-tape formulas follow the conservative direction
\[
T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))),\qquad
P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))=O(N^{\max(1,s)}).
\]
Input contributes at least $N$ to one-tape footprint. The one-tape time exponent is an \emph{upper} bound produced by a simulation; it is not a native RAM exponent and does not imply a reverse simulation or a SAT lower bound. Distinct work-cell visits, output materialization, head crossings and input probes must be logged but are not added again to time if already counted as transitions. A fixed universal machine supplies polynomial simulation with a separately charged overhead, not automatically these numerical exponents. No FLMV, Williams or Buss--Williams numerical endpoint is applied to the hypothetical shrinker.

\section{Independent finite checks and remaining gap}
An independently implemented Python reproducer exhaustively checked 16,382 parity instances and 196,610 \emph{single-bit length-decrease} steps; each step preserved the decision while shrinking one bit. It tested 256 three-variable proper-3CNF YES/NO replacement controls. For LSPV-2's moving guard it evaluated 375 random signed consecutive-triple/guard instances ($345$ SAT, $30$ UNSAT) and compared \emph{all} 6,000 surviving assignments against direct existential projection, not merely matching current answers. It verified 35 bounded-update horizon cases and 94,662 continuation evaluations, and ran 17 exponential-value rank counters totaling 262,125 decrements. Zero assertions failed. These are reproducible finite tests; no claim of all-size correctness or native TM timing is inferred from the counts.

The original Library audit separately reproduced its exact 4,680 boundary-subset checks, 7,191 local discovery checks and 139 brute-force comparisons. Its source and result hashes were retained. The restricted moving-guard proof remains contingent on its precise consecutively scoped grammar, canonical validation, and fully charged guard computation; extending it to arbitrary ordinary SAT is \textbf{OPEN}.
