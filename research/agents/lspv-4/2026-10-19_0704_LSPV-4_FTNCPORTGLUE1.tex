\section{DECLARED PORT MODEL}
Let $T(F)$ be the unrooted LAMINAR-GAUGE-TREE24 signed-NAE target for a proper
source 3-CNF $F$ with $n$ variables and $m\ge2$ clauses.  It has
\[
 V_B=n+5m-5\quad\hbox{Boolean variables},\qquad
 M=6m-6\quad\hbox{signed NAE predicates}.
\]
For this audit only, define $P(T(F))$ by replacing each predicate
$C=\operatorname{NAE}(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)$ with one cell on ports
\[
 \{s_C,v(\ell_1),v(\ell_2),v(\ell_3)\},
\]
where $s_C$ is fresh and the sign is retained in the cell record.  Equal
Boolean variables are identified.  No additional occurrence wire, fold-state
identification or face rule is silently added.  This is the weakest literal
reading of FTNCNAESLICE1's displayed ports; it is not asserted to be the
missing full FTNC construction.

\begin{theorem}[Port-incidence ledger, \EXACT]
For every $F$ in the declared domain, $P(T(F))$ has
\[
 \boxed{V_P=n+11m-11},\qquad
 \boxed{M=6m-6},\qquad
 \boxed{I_E=36m-36},
\]
where $I_E$ counts $K_4$ edges with cell multiplicity.  An internal gauge has
signed-NAE occurrence six, $K_4$ multidegree eighteen, and simple one-skeleton
degree twelve.  A leaf gauge has occurrence three and simple degree seven;
an equality-edge variable has occurrence two and simple degree four.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There is one fresh selector for each of the $M$ predicates, giving
$V_P=V_B+M=n+11m-11$.  Each cell contributes six $K_4$ edges, hence
$I_E=6M=36m-36$.  Each occurrence of a Boolean port contributes three incident
cell edges with multiplicity.  An internal degree-three gauge occurs in the
two equality predicates on each of its three tree edges, giving $6\cdot3=18$.
On one tree edge, the two cells have distinct selectors but the same other
gauge and edge-variable neighbors, so their simple union contributes four
neighbors; three tree edges give twelve.  The leaf and edge-variable counts
follow by the same direct enumeration.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Forced face sharing, \EXACT]
Exactly $2m-3$ pairs of cells in $P(T(F))$ share three Boolean ports.  They are
the two equality cells for each gauge-tree edge.  Explicitly,
\[
 \operatorname{NAE}(g_v,\neg g_w,e_{vw}),\qquad
 \operatorname{NAE}(g_v,\neg g_w,\neg e_{vw})
\]
have the same unsigned support $\{g_v,g_w,e_{vw}\}$ and distinct selectors;
their two $K_4$ cells therefore share the full literal triangle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The unrooted binary tree has $2m-3$ edges and every edge emits exactly this
pair.  Frontend splitters, leaf gauges and every edge variable are fresh, so
no other pair can share three ports, even when source clauses repeat or are
permuted.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Aligned cut in the port-only model, \EXACT]
For a cut displayed by a tree edge, assign its two equality cells to one
side.  If the source clause boundary has width $w$, then the Boolean-port
boundary of $P(T(F))$ has width exactly $w+1$.  No selector port crosses.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
All noncut tree cells and all frontends lie in one component.  The selected
cut cells introduce the opposite endpoint gauge on their assigned side, while
their fresh edge variable and selectors occur nowhere on the opposite side.
All other shared ports are exactly the original source boundary.
\end{proof}

\section{THE COMPOSITION GAP}
FTNCNAESLICE1 proves only an isolated-cell quotient: existentially choosing one
of three local matchings yields one NAE predicate.  A conjunction of these
quotients composes automatically if the matching/fold variables of different
cells are disjoint and the only shared interface is the declared Boolean port
value.  The equality pair above instead shares an entire face.  The accessible
corpus does not say whether face-sharing identifies any fold state, edge state,
orientation, or matching resource.

\begin{lemma}[Isolated projections do not determine a glued projection,
\EXACT]
There are two gadgets with the same one-port existential projection as the
always-true relation, but whose conjunction becomes empty when their hidden
state is identified.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For port $p$ and hidden bit $h$, let $R_0(p,h)$ mean $h=p$ and $R_1(p,h)$ mean
$h=1-p$.  For each $p$, both $\exists h\,R_0(p,h)$ and
$\exists h\,R_1(p,h)$ hold.  With fresh $h_0,h_1$, the conjunction is
satisfiable for both $p$.  If the hidden variables are glued to one $h$, the
conjunction requires $p=1-p$ and has no Boolean solution.
\end{proof}

Therefore the inference
\[
 \bigwedge_C \exists h_C\,R_C(x_C,h_C)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \exists H\,\bigwedge_C R_C(x_C,H|_C)
\]
is invalid without a disjointness or compatibility theorem.  This does not
show the actual FTNC gluing is unsound; it shows that the available isolated
quotient is logically insufficient to certify it.

The minimum repair is one of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item supply the immutable FTNC cell/occurrence specification and prove a
face-gluing fiber-intersection theorem for the displayed equality pair; or
\item replace the equality pair by a verified FTNC macro whose cells intersect
only in the declared Boolean vertex ports, then redo counts and separators.
\end{enumerate}

\section{ALGORITHMIC SHORTCUT AUDIT}
In the signed-NAE representation, the equality network is affine and easy.
A linear scan recognizes the $2m-3$ complementary clause pairs; union--find or
Gaussian elimination collapses all gauges to one bit, and every $e_{vw}$ is
free.  With typed tetrahedron records, direct decoding costs
$O(m\alpha(m))$ word operations after an $O(m)$ scan and
$O(m\log(n+m))$ bit traffic.  What remains is the original 3-CNF under one
global complement gauge, not a new hard residual.  2-SAT does not solve the
general frontend/source constraints, but it can verify the equality portion.
Thus the tree is an exact routing aid and separator display, not a hardness
amplifier.  Without typed selectors/ports, recognition from an unlabeled simple
graph is a separate \OPEN{} decoding problem and must not be treated as free.

The canonical record encoding stores four vertex identifiers plus three signs
per cell and has length $O(m\log(n+m))$ bits.  Parsing, validating proper cells,
detecting duplicate selectors, recognizing complementary equality pairs,
constructing the source decoder and rejecting malformed records are all
polynomial and must be charged.  No unrestricted SAT lower bound follows from
these counts.
