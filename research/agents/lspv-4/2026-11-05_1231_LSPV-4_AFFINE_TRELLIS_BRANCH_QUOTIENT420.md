## PROVED/RESTRICTED theorem: exact minimum future-state count
For any binary matrix M with c rows and k columns, right-hand side u, and a fixed input-bit order, split at j: M=[P_j | Q_j]. Every prefix p has continuation f_p(q) = [Q_j q = u + P_j p]. Let r = rank(M), r_P = rank(P_j), r_Q = rank(Q_j), d_j = r_P+r_Q-r.

If u is outside im(M), all continuations are FALSE, so there is exactly ONE future-function class at every cut. Otherwise precisely 2^d_j **distinct nonempty** continuations occur, and the total number including FALSE when present is

$$
C_j=2^{d_j}+\mathbf1[d_j<r_P].
$$

**Proof.** Feasible residual right-hand sides form (u+im(P_j)) intersect im(Q_j), a nonempty affine coset of im(P_j) intersect im(Q_j) of dimension d_j. Every distinct feasible RHS has a nonempty, disjoint suffix-solution set, hence a different continuation. Every infeasible RHS yields the same constant FALSE. The number of feasible prefixes is 2^(j-r_P+d_j). Some prefix is infeasible exactly if d_j<r_P. QED. This is the exact minimal width of *continuation-function classes* for a deterministic layered ordered branch evaluator (not necessarily actual nodes of a reduced OBDD with skipped levels); it is NOT an arbitrary-algorithm lower bound or a native R_mix341 inference rule.

## Known hard 3-CNF family and shortcut
Choose connected simple cubic graph G with H vertices and 3H/2 edge variables; each vertex parity equation is encoded by its four forbidden-assignment width-three clauses. Thus 4H clauses, N=Theta(H log H) encoded bits. Odd total vertex charge gives UNSAT. On suitable cubic *expander* sequences, Urquhart (JACM 1987) and Ben-Sasson-Wigderson (JACM 2001) give 2^Omega(H) **ordinary shared-DAG Resolution** size. This does not constrain arbitrary SAT machines or the fixed mixed calculus. Prior LSPV-2/341 proves a legal mixed AND3/GF(2) upper bound and exact optimum of 4H-1 paid nodes on connected bridgeless cubic odd-charge instances.

For edge subset S, deleting S yields components C_i. Define u_i as the XOR of original charges in C_i, and column M_{i,e}=1 exactly if selected edge e crosses C_i. Prior LSPV-2/424 proves a branch a is SAT exactly when Ma=u and rank(M)=number_of_components-1. The theorem above supplies the **new prefix/suffix continuation-equivalence formula** for arbitrary ordered S. Exponentially many distinct raw cofactors need not require exponentially many distinct continuation functions. For odd charge the global GF(2) contradiction is a paid/justified shortcut rather than a free branch answer.
