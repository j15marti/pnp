\section*{Original literature and matched resource interpretation}
FLMV~\cite{FLMV}, Section~2.1, counts writable and index tapes
and permits indexed read-only input and a one-way write-only output.
The input-access index is charged, including its writing and erasure.
Neither a free RAM word nor a free \emph{readable} child body is assumed.
FLMV Theorem~3.1, Section~3.1 and Corollary~3.2 provide
model-compatible locally addressable near-linear SAT reductions;
Corollary~5.8 gives the stated $\beta$ simultaneous
time--space exclusion for $\operatorname{NTIME}[n]$.
Williams~\cite{Williams}, Section~2.1, has separately specified
machine models and indirect-diagonalization results.
Neither source proves a favorable universal recursive ordinary-SAT
contractor, and a theorem for another computational model is not
silently substituted for the FLMV result.

We count \emph{every} source/workspace read, index/address computation,
write (including an unchanged write), actual branching or control step,
child-header/body construction, auxiliary-variable renaming,
validation, discovery, selection/switching, failed route, cache
insertion/eviction/miss, replay, output, source-named compatibility
and gluing. The complete source is on a read-only indexed tape;
stored intermediate ordinary child strings are on counted writable
work tapes, never on the nonreadable output tape.
Peak storage includes \emph{simultaneously} live guest, generator,
child, ancestor, index, cache and gluing state.
We do \emph{not} equate write operations to changed-cell flips,
cumulative live-bit time or physical switches/transistors; those
latter quantities need separate instrumentation.

\section*{Exact conditional two-level four-mode theorem}
Fix $a_1,a_2\ge0$, $0<\theta_1,\theta_2<1$,
$g_1,g_2,\eta_1,\eta_2,r,\kappa\ge0$.
Suppose a \emph{pre-existing, exact, uniform} semantic parent policy
exists, with the following \emph{all-input} or explicitly typed
FLMV-hard-image promises:
\begin{description}
\item[] \textbf{Level 1.} On each eligible source $F$ of length $N$ it
selects no more than $N^{a_1+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT child strings
$Y$ with encoded length at most $N^{\theta_1+o(1)}$.
Each addressed $Y$ bit including ordinary header/body and
name bookkeeping is computable from $F$ in
$N^{g_1+o(1)}$ elementary operations, including \emph{all}
addressed source bits, with $N^{\eta_1+o(1)}$ live workspace.
\item[] \textbf{Level 2.} On each \emph{actually eligible}
ordinary $Y$ it selects no more than
$|Y|^{a_2+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT grandchildren $Z$,
each of encoded length at most $|Y|^{\theta_2+o(1)}$.
An addressed $Z$ bit requires at most
$|Y|^{g_2+o(1)}$ \emph{local} elementary
operations and no more such $Y$-bit probes, with
$|Y|^{\eta_2+o(1)}$ local writable/index workspace,
\emph{before} the additional cost of serving those $Y$ probes.
All headers, source-name maps and gluing interfaces are real bits.
\item[] \textbf{Semantic join.} The finite child and grandchild SAT bits,
combined with a \emph{uniform fully charged parent computation},
exactly determine $\SAT(F)$ on \emph{both} SAT and UNSAT sources.
Every recursive guest input lies inside its claimed domain.
All required compatibility data are generated within the stated
bounds; no SAT oracle bit is provided free.
\item[] \textbf{Nonchild costs.} Validation, family recognition,
adaptive choice, strategy changes, failed searches and
joins take at most $N^{r+o(1)}$ elementary work.
Ancestry, retained summaries, indices, route descriptions and
other nonchild live state occupy at most $N^{\kappa+o(1)}$
writable/index bits. The fixed ordinary SAT guest $A$ runs in
$L^{t+o(1)}$ time and $L^{s+o(1)}$ space on \emph{every}
ordinary $L$-bit input, with one realization of both bounds.
\end{description}
These semantic and resource hypotheses are \emph{not established}
for any general ARC contractor.

Write $Q=a_1+\theta_1a_2$, $H=\theta_1\theta_2$,
and $G=Q+Ht$. Interpret the first letter of a protocol as
whether $Y$ is eagerly materialized (\textsf E) or accessed on
demand (\textsf L); the second denotes the same choice for $Z$.
Eager buffers are written in \emph{full} and retained until the
guest finishes. Depth-first scheduling discards earlier siblings;
any policy requiring all sibling bits to remain live must include
them in $\kappa$.

\begin{theorem}[Four exact conditional upper envelopes]
\label{th:modes}
If the preceding hypotheses hold, each fixed protocol is a
uniform exact machine on the declared source domain with
the simultaneous upper exponents shown below, where
$u_j$ is time, $v_j$ peak counted space:
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
u_{\EE}&=\max\{r,\ a_1+\theta_1+g_1,\
                    Q+H+\theta_1g_2,\ G\},\\
v_{\EE}&=\max\{\kappa,\ \theta_1,\ H,\ \eta_1,\
                       \theta_1\eta_2,\ Hs\},\\[1pt]
u_{\EL}&=\max\{r,\ a_1+\theta_1+g_1,\
                    G+\theta_1g_2\},\\
v_{\EL}&=\max\{\kappa,\ \theta_1,\ \eta_1,\
                       \theta_1\eta_2,\ Hs\},\\[1pt]
u_{\LE}&=\max\{r,\ Q+H+\theta_1g_2+g_1,\ G\},\\
v_{\LE}&=\max\{\kappa,\ H,\ \eta_1,\
                       \theta_1\eta_2,\ Hs\},\\[1pt]
u_{\LL}&=\max\{r,\ G+\theta_1g_2+g_1\},\\
v_{\LL}&=\max\{\kappa,\ \eta_1,\
                       \theta_1\eta_2,\ Hs\}.
\end{aligned}}\tag{1}
\]
The equations are conservative \emph{upper-bound implementations},
not necessary-work lower bounds. If the source policy issues
$N^{b+o(1)}$ additional invocations of $A$ on
$N^{\rho+o(1)}$-bit policy probes, time must also include
$b+\rho t$ plus full guest-input generation/access surcharges;
peak must include $\rho s$, any materialized $\rho$-length
ordinary input and simultaneously live ancestors.
A parent-scale guest invocation or arbitrary failure fallback
is not contractive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The total number of terminal guest invocations is at most
$N^{Q+o(1)}$, their ordinary encoded lengths at most
$N^{H+o(1)}$; guest work has exponent $G$ and guest workspace
$Hs$. If the level-one children are eager,
writing all $N^{a_1}$ child bodies entails at most
$N^{a_1+\theta_1+g_1+o(1)}$ bit work and a
$N^{\theta_1+o(1)}$-bit live input.
If level two is eager, generating all
$N^Q$ grandchildren writes at most $N^{Q+H+o(1)}$
bits, each costing $N^{\theta_1g_2+o(1)}$
when its parent is already materialized, or at most
$N^{\theta_1g_2+g_1+o(1)}$ when every parent-bit
probe re-enters the source accessor.
If level two is lazy, each guest operation may request
one $Z$ bit; across all leaves there are at most
$N^{G+o(1)}$ such operations, each costing
$N^{\theta_1g_2+o(1)}$ if $Y$ is materialized or
$N^{\theta_1g_2+g_1+o(1)}$ otherwise.
Adding parent nonchild work $N^{r+o(1)}$
proves the four time maxima. Depth-first reuse leaves
at most one eager $Y$, one eager $Z$, one active
guest and one of each active generator, alongside
counted retained nonchild state. Their sizes are the
listed space terms; the maximum, \emph{not a
separately optimized machine for time and for space},
bounds their sum at exponent scale. Full buffer
allocation, reads and writes, all indices and control
are part of the hypotheses and of the upper work
bounds; nothing is charged to a write-only
output tape as reusable memory.
\end{proof}

\section*{An exact numerical window, not a constructed ARC successor}
Set $a_1=a_2=3/5$, $\theta_1=\theta_2=1/2$,
$t=6/5$, $s=1/2$, $g_1=g_2=1/10$.
Then $Q=9/10$, $H=1/4$, $G=6/5$.
Suppose also, \emph{conditionally}, that
$r\le6/5$ and $\kappa,\eta_1,\theta_1\eta_2\le1/8$
so those terms do not dominate any listed peak.
Equation~(1) gives:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc|c}
\text{protocol}&u&v&\text{strict FLMV box?}\\\hline
\EE&6/5&1/2&\text{numerically yes}\\
\EL&5/4&1/2&\text{no (time)}\\
\LE&13/10&1/4&\text{no (time)}\\
\LL&27/20&1/8&\text{no (time)}
\end{array}
\]
All four rows are \emph{different actual implementations};
no time coordinate from one row is combined with the space
coordinate from another. The \EE{} row has
$6/5<241/200$ and $1/2<11642479/23232400$,
a \emph{strict numerical window only}. To turn it into a
forbidden machine, the parent must be correct on the
whole required source domain with two genuinely shorter
eligible ordinary-SAT levels, all joins and policies paid.
No such parent is supplied by this theorem.

The source-first write traffic in this cell alone has
exponent $a_1+\theta_1+g_1=6/5$; both level-two
ordinary bit generation and the guest work also have exponent $6/5$.
Thus even a small extra \emph{fixed-power} expense beyond
$6/5$ can exhaust the $1/200$ time-exponent margin.
For example, if an implementation stores all
$N^{Q+o(1)}=N^{9/10+o(1)}$
distinct grandchild SAT flags before the parent join,
that explicit retained table has writable peak exponent
$\kappa\ge9/10>d_\star$ and fails the same-machine
space box. A \emph{proved} streaming join may avoid
this table on a restricted family; general compatibility
joins do not inherit that property for free.

The separately checked $21\times21$ exact-rational
$(g_1,g_2)\in\{0,.01,\ldots,.20\}^2$ grid finds respectively
$121,11,36,1$ strict-time-feasible cells for
$\EE,\EL,\LE,\LL$ with $r\le6/5$ and nonbinding
metadata. This is finite arithmetic under (1),
not a lower bound or an adaptive strategy theorem.

\section*{Two actual ordinary-SAT levels and the deliberate easy control}
A standalone, deterministic Node.js reproducer parses and writes
a \emph{concrete} ordinary finite CNF bit encoding:
a 32-bit header containing a five-variable limit and a 16-bit
clause count, followed by 15-bit records for up to three literals.
Tautological filler clauses $(x_4\lor\neg x_4\lor x_5)$
make one root with $k^4$ records, its two $x_1$-restricted
children (each repadded to $k^2$ records), and their two
$x_2$-restricted grandchildren each (repadded to $k$
records). Brute-force semantics handles empty clauses.
At $k=8,9,10,11$ the \emph{actual serialized bit lengths}
are $N=32+15k^4$, $L_1=32+15k^2$,
$L_2=32+15k$.

Six unambiguous controls are applied at each size:
satisfiable ternary SAT, all-sign proper ternary UNSAT,
Horn SAT, even-parity affine SAT, SCC/2SAT UNSAT,
and a directly recognizable opposite-unit UNSAT.
Across 24 roots (12 SAT and 12 UNSAT), two
first-level and four second-level encoded descendant
program objects per root are actually generated and
decoded. Both-level Shannon identities are checked:
\[
\SAT(F)=\bigvee_{b\in\{0,1\}}\SAT(F|_{x_1=b}),
\quad
\SAT(Y_b)=\bigvee_{c\in\{0,1\}}
\SAT(Y_b|_{x_2=c}).
\]
Tautological padding preserves both labels.
The 48 first children and 96 grandchild calls
are independently brute-force checked.
The reproducible run counts \textbf{1,086,745
assertions}, zero failures, \textbf{3,261,756
explicit read-bit events} and an equal count of
\emph{finite-body serialization writes}.
Those writes comprise \textbf{1,741,882 zero bits}
and \textbf{1,519,874 one bits}. A zero bit written
to a fresh zero-initialized cell may be
an \emph{unchanged write} but it still costs a write.
These are \emph{instrumented JS serialization events},
not FLMV machine cycles. No claim is made that
all logical CPU/address/erasure events, flips or hardware
resources were measured by JavaScript.

The fixed 16-bit finite header is \emph{not} an
asymptotic ordinary-SAT encoding for unbounded $k$.
An extensible header and $\Theta(\log k)$-bit
literal identifiers could preserve the \emph{schematic}
$k^4:k^2:k$ exponent ratios, but that
different uniform machine has not been
implemented or measured here. In the finite
prototype, power shrink is created by \emph{removal
of an easy tautological padding component}.
This is precisely why the example \emph{fails}
to prove any nontrivial hard-source contractor,
despite exact SAT/UNSAT equality at two real
recursive levels. Horn, affine, SCC and
direct recognition beat any proposed universal
resource lower bound inferred from these examples.
