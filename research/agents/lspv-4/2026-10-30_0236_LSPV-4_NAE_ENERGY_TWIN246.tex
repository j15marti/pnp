\section{Original clause-indexed experiment and global consistency}
Let a proper width-three source clause be $C_j=(\ell_{i,j}\vee\ell_{k,j}\vee\ell_{h,j})$ with original signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$ denoting the \emph{same original Boolean variable} $x_i$ that occurs in every other clause. A literal with sign $\sigma_{ij}=+1$ is $x_i$ and one with sign $-1$ is $\neg x_i$. The local target $S_j$ sets all three original literals TRUE, requiring $x_i=(1+\sigma_{ij})/2$; local $U_j$ sets all three original literals FALSE, requiring $x_i=(1-\sigma_{ij})/2$. Componentwise inversion swaps these local targets. Ordinary disjunctive SAT is $t_j(a)\ge1$, not necessarily $t_j(a)=3$. For distinct original clauses $C_j,C_k$, a pair of local extremes is feasible under a globally coherent assignment iff its shared-variable demands agree: $SS,UU$ iff no shared original variable has opposite polarity; $SU,US$ iff no shared original variable has the same polarity. The primary raw JSON records every signed occurrence, all four local/inverted targets, all $190$ pairs per source, selected three-clause modes and each shared-variable conflict, with source indexes preserved.

Separate \emph{globally coherent} complete assignments $0^n$, $1^n$, their complements, and intermediate flip masks are evaluated using one Boolean value per original variable. Write $t_j(a)=\#\{\text{TRUE literals of }C_j\}$, $N_r(a)=\#\{j:t_j(a)=r\}$, and $a\oplus H$ for a simultaneous flip set. Then $t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a)$ and $N_r(\bar a)=N_{3-r}(a)$; all are checked independently in the finite experiment. The user-proposed initial ``flip all nonconflicting UNSAT demands'' is ordinary pure-literal elimination, and a strict signed $(2+,2-)$ source has no such original variables.
\section{Exact complement-energy identity}
\begin{theorem}[Source-clause phase invariance, PROVED/EXACT]
For every proper width-three CNF $F$, every subset $T$ of original clause indices and every coherent full assignment $a$, let $F^T$ be obtained by negating all three literal signs in each selected original clause. Then
\[
 E_{F^T}(a)=E_F(a),\qquad E_F(a):=N_0^F(a)+N_3^F(a).
\]
Moreover $E_F(a)$ is exactly the number of signed original clauses violating the NAE predicate. For $s_i=2a_i-1\in\{-1,+1\}$ and
$W_{ik}=\sum_{j:\,i,k\in C_j}\sigma_{ij}\sigma_{kj}$,
\[
 \boxed{E_F(a)=\frac m4+\frac14\sum_{i<k}W_{ik}s_is_k.}
\]
Thus it has only pairwise signed overlap terms, and neither the complete $E$ function nor its strict-gradient trajectory can distinguish two sources related by whole-clause sign inversions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each original ternary clause, the contribution to $E$ is $1[t_j(a)=0]+1[t_j(a)=3]$, which is invariant under $t_j\mapsto3-t_j$, the operation induced by inverting all three signs. It is also precisely the NAE failure indicator. If $z_1,z_2,z_3$ are the three original signed literal spin values $\sigma_{ij}s_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, the indicator is
$\frac14(1+z_1z_2+z_1z_3+z_2z_3)$. Sum over the source clauses. This is an exact multilinear expansion, not a complexity assertion.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Complement-orientation SAT certificate]
For a proper ternary CNF, if a coherent full $a$ satisfies $E_F(a)\le1$, at least one of $a,\bar a$ is a valid SAT witness. If $E_F(a)=0$, both are SAT witnesses. In contrast, $E_F(a)\ge2$ does not establish UNSAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Because $E=N_0(a)+N_3(a)=N_0(a)+N_0(\bar a)$, if $E\le1$ at least one nonnegative summand vanishes. If $E=0$ both vanish. For the converse failure, the SAT twin in the next proposition has $E\ge2$ for every coherent assignment.
\end{proof}
