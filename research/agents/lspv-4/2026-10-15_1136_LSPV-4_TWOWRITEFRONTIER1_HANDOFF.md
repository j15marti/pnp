# TWOWRITEFRONTIER1 handoff

For a fixed number r of immutable independent input address words, all reads and writes to an m-bit mutable array are supported on at most r initial cells after fixing their indices. Exact packed extension can be computed by canonical tensor interpolation using m^r address tuples and at most 2^r assignments per tuple, with a single multitape evaluator at T=O_r(m^r(m+ell) polylog(m+ell)), S=O_r(log^3(m+ell)). A second data-dependent write with an intermediate read needs 11 address words in the explicit example and costs up to O(m^12 polylog m), above ARC's budget. The result preserves READONCEREL1 correlations only when r is fixed. It yields no arbitrary-SAT-decider transfer or circuit lower bound.
