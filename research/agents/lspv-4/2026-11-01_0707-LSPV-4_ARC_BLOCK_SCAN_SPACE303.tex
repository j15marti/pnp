\begin{theorem}[Charged block acquisition]
Let $F$ be a valid target original, $n$ variables, $m=4n/3$ original clauses, encoding length $N=\Theta(m\log m)$, and let $p$ be a coherent original-variable partial assignment. The multigraph of original-ID-labelled residual binary clauses has maximum degree four. For block capacity $B$, a deterministic algorithm can acquire and emit \emph{every} residual original-ID triangle exactly once using at most $O(B\log N)$ incremental writable/index cells plus $p$ and all other live state, with
\[
T=O\!\left((m+m^2/B)\operatorname{polylog}N\;Q_p+T_{\rm grammar}+T_{\rm output}\right).
\]
Here $Q_p$ is the \emph{paid} original pin-query cost. A write-only output is not a free reusable list of triangles. Building a greedy edge-disjoint packing from this stream can require a separately charged used-edge index or replay.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each free variable retains at most its four original occurrences. Partition original clause IDs into $B$-record blocks. Retain a block's residual binary clauses, their original IDs and both endpoints; sort their at most $2B$ endpoint entries. Rescan \emph{every} source record, and for each residual binary edge $(v,w)$ append its original ID and other endpoint to cached edges incident at $v$ or $w$. Maximum degree four bounds incident-neighbor lists by three per endpoint. A cached edge $(u,v)$ forms a triangle precisely when one neighbor at $u$ and one at $v$ have a common third endpoint $w\notin\{u,v\}$, with three distinct original clause IDs. Emit only when the cached edge's ID is the smallest of those three; every triangle then has exactly one owner. There are at most $\lceil m/B\rceil$ full scans, and the sorted lookup and constant-degree neighbor comparison require the displayed paid bound. \end{proof}
