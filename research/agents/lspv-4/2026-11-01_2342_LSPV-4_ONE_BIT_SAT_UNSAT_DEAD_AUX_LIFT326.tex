\begin{lemma}[Explicit acquisition and exact 2CNF Tseitin rebuild]
Let $R$ be an explicitly acquired source-labelled residual with
$q\ge1$ binary clauses, no unit or empty clauses.
Build a private OR gate for each binary clause, AND the outputs,
assert the root one and use complete biconditional Tseitin encodings.
Exactly $2q-1$ gates and $6q-2$ encoded clauses are created.
UP leaves exactly the original $q$ binary clauses, preserving
their original-input meaning with a unique full gate trace.
Source acquisition and gate writing are fully charged polynomial work.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An AND root fixed at one forces both child signals to one, inductively.
For each binary OR output $z=1$, the definition
$(\neg a\vee z)\wedge(\neg b\vee z)
\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg z)$ reduces to $(a\vee b)$.
There are $q$ binary OR gates, $q-1$ AND gates and one
forced-output unit, so $3(2q-1)+1=6q-2$ clauses.
No original unit clause remains to propagate. Gate values
are unique by topological evaluation. The parser,
source/index/head reads, memory and encoded writes are not free.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
