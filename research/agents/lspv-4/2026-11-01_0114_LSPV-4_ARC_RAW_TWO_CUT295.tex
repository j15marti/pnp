\section{Formal theorem and proof}
\begin{theorem}
For every connected valid canonical formula $F$ on $n$ variables, every pivot $x$ and value $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$, the raw cofactor $F|_{x=\epsilon}$ retains all $n-1$ other variable identities and has at most \emph{two} incidence-connected components. One component contains at least $\lceil(n-1)/2\rceil$ original variables. For each residual component $K$, let $a_K$ be the number of its neighboring literal occurrences lost with the two satisfied pivot clauses, and $b_K$ the number of its residual binary pivot clauses. Then $a_K+b_K\in\{3,6\}$, $\sum_K a_K=4$, and $\sum_K b_K=2$. The bound is sharp even where the source primal graph minus the pivot remains connected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $x=1$; the other case exchanges signs. The two positive pivot clauses disappear, deleting four nonpivot occurrences, and the two negative clauses shorten to binary clauses. Each other variable loses at most two of its four occurrences and survives. In residual component $K$ with $v$ variables, let $P$ and $Q$ count surviving positive and negative clauses. Positive clauses are ternary while exactly $b_K$ negative clauses are binary. Exact original signed occurrence quotas yield
\[
3P=2v-a_K,\qquad 3Q-b_K=2v.
\]
Thus $a_K+b_K=3(Q-P)$. Every residual component contains some original neighbor of $x$, since the original graph is connected and no source edge outside the four pivot clauses was removed. Every such neighbor contributes to $a_K$ (if its pivot clause was deleted) or $b_K$ (if the pivot clause was shortened). Therefore $a_K+b_K>0$ and is divisible by three. Total boundary flux is $4+2=6$, so at most two components exist. Pigeonhole proves the largest size.
For sharpness use $x=9$ and, for each four-variable block $(a,b,c,d)=(1,2,3,4)$ or $(5,6,7,8)$, include
\[
(x\vee a\vee b),(a\vee c\vee d),(b\vee c\vee d),
(\neg x\vee\neg c\vee\neg d),(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee\neg c),
(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee\neg d).
\]
This is valid connected canonical T9 with two four-variable cofactor components. Swap the two positive occurrences $2$ and $5$ between clauses $(9,1,2)$ and $(9,5,6)$. The canonical T9C remains valid; its source primal graph minus $9$ is connected through the crossed clauses, but those clauses disappear when $9=1$, leaving two cofactor components.
\end{proof}
This proves a restriction of raw source-preserving one-pivot recursion only: its largest child has $\Omega(n)$ original names, and its ordinary residual is not itself canonical. It does not exclude noninjective semantic compression, auxiliary gadgets, more pivots, certified easy policies or arbitrary polynomial algorithms.
