\section*{One target, four falsifiers, and the exact machine}
The target is one integrated universal adaptive parameterized residual-SAT controller, fully charged on the \emph{same} deterministic indexed read-only-input random-access multitape Turing machine used by Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, and Viglas (FLMV). Input-index \emph{contents} are erased by an indexed jump; the index-tape \emph{head position} is preserved. The guest's input tape is read-only and does not count toward writable space. The host's materialized child buffer is a work tape and \emph{does} count. Every guest work and index tape, controller tape, address register, source index and retained ancestor must be charged.

\noindent\textbf{F1 (far-blank access).} A guest input-head jump past $O(M)$ requires a readable host child buffer proportional to the \emph{address}. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as a general implementation claim for logarithmic input-index head extent: a binary address register and a blank guard suffice even for input address $\Omega(M^2)$.

\noindent\textbf{F2 (automatic logarithmic index extent).} Every polynomial-time correct SAT decider has input-index head extent $O(\log M)$. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}, already by the previous BOUNDED-GUEST-INPUT273 $M$-cell index-head sweep and far blank wrapper. That adversary is retained, not claimed as new.

\noindent\textbf{F3 (free parent normalization).} Deduplication or strict exact-$(2,2)$ normalization makes every SAT source into a power-short ordinary child with negligible parent memory. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by unchanged length on unreplicated controls and by the explicit $O(N)$ auxiliary storage of the test's simple deduplication map. CP22LIFT1 transports \emph{Cutting Planes proofs} at polynomial cost; it is not a free arbitrary-algorithm space contraction.

\noindent\textbf{F4 (endpoint UNSAT).} Failure of both all-zero and all-one original-source assignments proves UNSAT. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the seven-clause source omitting the clause false at $101$ has that unique middle-layer SAT witness. \section*{Exact strengthened restricted simulation lemma}
Let $w\in\Sigma^M$ be a \emph{genuine} ordinary-SAT input already materialized on one host indexed readable work tape, with all $M$ cells charged. Let $A$ be any fixed deterministic total FLMV guest decider, and consider a run of duration $T=T_A(M)$ for which its \emph{input-index tape head} visits positions at most $h=h_A(M)$, counting zero as the first position. Do \emph{not} assume the guest's read-only input-head coordinate is $O(M)$.

\begin{theorem}[Dynamic far-blank guard, restricted]
A fixed FLMV host with a separate original-source read-only input and a counted $M$-cell child work buffer can exactly simulate this one guest run, preserving each guest indexed jump, erasure of the entire input-index contents, unchanged index-head position, ordinary input-head movements, blank reads, and all other guest work/index tapes. Its charged bounds, including initial child storage, are
\[
T_{\rm host}=O\left(M+T[h+\log(M+T+2)+1]\right),
\]
\[
S_{\rm host}=O\left(M+S_A(M)+h+\log(M+T+2)\right).
\]
The constants may depend on the fixed guest machine but not on $M$, input label, or an unprovided guest exponent $K$. In particular, $h=O(\log M)$ and polynomial $T_A(M)$ yield $T_{\rm host}=O(M+T_A(M)\log M)$, $S_{\rm host}=O(M+S_A(M)+\log M)$ \emph{without any $O(M)$ input-coordinate premise}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initially the guest input-head coordinate is a constant. An indexed jump reads only the guest input-index tape's finitely many cells at positions at most $h$, so its new nonnegative coordinate is below $2^{h+1}$ (the harmless $+1$ absorbs delimiters or indexing conventions). During at most $T$ intervening ordinary input-head moves, its coordinate increases by at most $T$. Thus every coordinate has binary representation length
\[
O\bigl(h+\log(T+2)\bigr).
\]
Keep this \emph{virtual coordinate} in counted host work cells. Mirror the guest input-index tape as a separate host work tape, including its current index-head coordinate. On a guest indexed input jump, scan and decode its first $h+1$ cells, erase those cells \emph{physically}, preserve the index head, and update the virtual input-head coordinate. This requires $O(h+1)$ charged host operations. Ordinary guest input movements use a charged binary increment or decrement on the virtual coordinate, costing $O(h+\log(T+2)+1)$ in the worst case. Before a guest input-symbol read, compare the virtual coordinate with the true child length $M$. If it is at least $M$, return the blank symbol without moving the host child-buffer tape. Otherwise write its $O(\log M)$-bit address onto the \emph{host child-buffer index tape}, execute the indexed move and read the actual child character; the host index contents are erased exactly as required. The host child's input-buffer index-head position and all temporary coordinates also occupy counted work/index space. Mirror each of the guest's finitely many other work and index tapes with corresponding host tapes, preserving their indexed moves and head locations; the guest's own work-space high-water bound $S_A(M)$ pays for those. Induct on guest transitions to obtain identical configurations of the \emph{guest}, in particular the same accepting or rejecting label on every ordinary input. Summing charged operations proves the bounds. No nonmember promise, SAT oracle, or free input head teleportation is invoked.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope and exact non-implications.}
This theorem covers \emph{one initially blank guest execution}. For multiple sequential children, all source-authentication work $P(N)$, child acquisition/output, policy selection and failed trials, and \emph{actual} guest-workspace reset/replay costs $R_i$ must be added:
\[
T_{\rm parent}\le O\left(P(N)+\sum_{i=1}^{q}
 \bigl[M_i+T_A(M_i)(h_i+\log(M_i+T_A(M_i)+2)+1)+R_i\bigr]\right).
\]
This is a \emph{conditional upper ledger for this architecture}, not a universal ARC recurrence. A physical eager reset can cost $R_i=\Omega(S_A(M_i))$ on some guests and therefore add a $\gamma+\lambda L$ term to the parent \emph{time} exponent. Any proposed $K'$ inequality independent of $L$ must explicitly use and prove a cheaper reset or fresh-workspace mechanism. Sequential calls can reuse buffers and avoid multiplying peak \emph{space} by $q$, but not erase total time charges. The unbounded-index-head guests of BOUNDED273 are still outside the logarithmic-$h$ specialization. The theorem does not construct genuine power-short children on all sources or give the arbitrary-$A$ uniform hereditary $\Phi$.

\paragraph{Matched correctness-preserving far-blank wrapper.}
For a child of actual length $M$, let $h=2\lceil\log_2(M+2)\rceil+4$. A deterministic guest prefix writes ones at input-index cells $0,\dots,h-1$ and jumps to address $2^h-1=\Omega(M^2)$, reads blank, restores a near address through a second indexed jump, and later performs a second far jump and returns to address zero. Erasure and preservation of index-head position are essential. The prefix executes in $O(\log M)$ guest steps, restores the guest's input and input-index starting configuration, and can precede \emph{any} exact SAT decider $A$. Therefore both SAT and UNSAT decisions of the wrapped decider equal those of $A$ on the \emph{same} ordinary child. An incorrectly resetting input-index head or an emulator that physically jumps its readable child buffer to the far blank address is not an exact resource-matched implementation.

\section*{Actual FLMV Theorem 5.7 and strict boundary}
The published FLMV theorem has $k\ge1$; the project must not silently omit $k=1$. With
\[
f_0=1,\qquad f_{k+1}=\frac{cf_k}{1+f_k},\qquad
d_k(c)=\min\left\{\frac{1/f_k-c}{k},c^{-k}\right\},
\quad d_*(c)=\max_{k\ge1}d_k(c),
\]
our independently executed rational program and separately implemented checker confirm
\[
d_*(6/5)=23/45,\qquad
d_*(241/200)=11642479/23232400.
\]
In both cases the unique maximizing index is $k=2$. After checking $k=3$, the exact $c^{-4}<d_2(c)$ bound excludes \emph{all} $k\ge4$, not just finitely sampled terms. Checks at $c=7/5,3/2,8/5$ attain $k=3,4,8$, respectively, so $k=2$ is \emph{not} a global maximizer across the full envelope. At $c=241/200$, the unconstructed fixed point $(K_*,L_*)=(6/5,1/2)$ lies strictly inside the FLMV exclusion only if \emph{one and the same} total deterministic indexed-input SAT machine has simultaneous worst-case bounds with exponent slacks
\[
c-K_*=\frac1{200},\qquad
d_*(c)-L_*=\frac{26279}{23232400}.
\]
The theorem excludes space $o(N^{d})$, so merely achieving a non-strict $O(N^{d})$ boundary does \emph{not} suffice. The old retired $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$ shortcut is not used.
