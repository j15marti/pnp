\paragraph{Result and status.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT symbolic constructions and consequences within specified proof systems;
independent review pending.} A seven-clause transparent balancing gadget extends
PC22\_TRANSFER1 from balanced sources to every nonempty proper distinct-clause
3-CNF. Its projection preserves PC/PCR degree and explicit monomial size over
every field. Applying this to sparse graph-colouring formulas gives an
exact-$(2,2)$ family requiring exponential PC/PCR monomial size even in
characteristic two. The lower-bound source is the published graph-colouring
theorem, not the finite experiments. No general minimal-unsatisfiability claim
is made for the new family. \textbf{Neither $P=NP$ nor $P\ne NP$ is proved.}

\section{Relation to the recovered results}
LBO3SAT-0031/0032 and PROOF-0034 supply the original occurrence-halving compiler,
its resolution projection, and minimality for the structured Tseitin source
\cite{LBO}. PC22\_TRANSFER1 extended the compiler to sources with variable-specific
balanced counts $(r_x,r_x)$, $r_x\ge2$, and supplied its PC/PCR/SoS transfer
\cite{PC22}. Its characteristic-two upper bound is specific to the compiled
Tseitin family. It does not say every exact-$(2,2)$ formula has a short
characteristic-two refutation.

The present change is a fully specified balancing interface and a different
source. The output is signed exact-$(2,2)$, not necessarily clause-monotone.
The old minimally-UNSAT theorem is retained with its old hypotheses; it is not
silently transferred to these new outputs. General NP-completeness of the
occurrence class and generic substitution methodology are established background,
not novelty claims of this report.

\section{An explicit transparent occurrence gadget}
For a literal $\ell$ over an existing variable and five fresh variables
$a,b,c,d,e$, define
\begin{align*}
J(\ell)={}&(\ell\lor\neg a\lor\neg b)\land(a\lor c\lor\neg d)
\land(a\lor d\lor\neg e)\\
&\land(b\lor e\lor\neg c)\land(b\lor\neg d\lor\neg e)
\land(\neg a\lor c\lor d)\land(\neg b\lor\neg c\lor e).
\end{align*}
\begin{lemma}[Transparent signed-port padding]\label{lem:pad}
Every auxiliary variable in $J(\ell)$ occurs exactly twice positively and twice
negatively. The original variable receives exactly one additional occurrence,
with sign $\ell$. The seven clauses are proper and distinct. Setting all five
auxiliaries to zero satisfies every clause, irrespective of $\ell$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $a$, the positive occurrences are in clauses 2 and 3 and the negative
occurrences in clauses 1 and 6. For $b$, they are in clauses 4 and 5 versus
1 and 7. For $c$, they are in clauses 2 and 6 versus 4 and 7. For $d$,
they are in clauses 3 and 6 versus 2 and 5. For $e$, they are in clauses
4 and 7 versus 3 and 5. Freshness proves properness and prevents collisions
with old clauses. Every displayed clause has a negative auxiliary literal,
so the all-zero auxiliary assignment satisfies all of them. In particular,
$\exists a,b,c,d,e\,J(\ell)$ is the constant-true relation on the boundary.
\end{proof}

This is a transparent addition, not a forcing gadget. It does not compute the
truth value of the source formula. The finite truth table has 5 auxiliary
extensions when $\ell=0$ and 9 when $\ell=1$, so it is \emph{not} a
model-count-preserving transformation.

\section{All-input proper-3CNF regularization}
Let $F$ have $m\ge1$ proper, distinct 3-clauses and dense active variable IDs
$1,\ldots,n$. Write $p_x,q_x$ for the signed occurrence counts and set
\[
r_x=\max(p_x,q_x,2),\qquad
 g(F)=\sum_x(2r_x-p_x-q_x).
\]
Add $r_x-p_x$ fresh copies of $J(x)$ and $r_x-q_x$ fresh copies of $J(\neg x)$.
Call the result $B(F)$. Its variables are balanced: the original variable $x$
has counts $(r_x,r_x)$ and each of the $5g$ fresh variables has counts $(2,2)$.
Consequently,
\[
n_B=n+5g,\qquad m_B=m+7g,\qquad
 R=\tfrac32(m+7g)=\sum_{z\in\operatorname{Vars}(B(F))}r_z.
\]
All auxiliary-zero assignments leave precisely the original source constraints.
Thus $B(F)$ is equisatisfiable with $F$, and its added clauses project to true.

Apply the balanced-source compiler of PC22\_TRANSFER1: each variable with
$r_z$ occurrences of each sign gets $r_z$ clones, paired by occurrence order,
and a directed equality cycle. There are $R$ clones and $R$ implications.
Partition the implications into triples and pad each triple with the three
forced-false ports of a fresh copy of
\[
Q=W_1\setminus\{x_1\lor x_7\lor x_{13}\}.
\]
The recovered $Q$ has 15 variables and 19 clauses and is satisfied by zero.
The exact occurrence arithmetic ensures $R$ is divisible by 3. Denote the
composed compiler by $U(F)=C(B(F))$.

\begin{theorem}[Universal proper-source compiler]\label{thm:universal}
For every $F$ in the stated domain, $U(F)$ is a proper, distinct-clause,
exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF with
\[
 v(U(F))=9(m+7g),\qquad M(U(F))=12(m+7g),
 \qquad \SAT(U(F))=\SAT(F).
\]
There is an explicit variable/zero substitution $\rho$ whose clause images
are exactly $F$ plus true clauses. There is no source-solving call.
Furthermore $g\le9m$, so $v\le576m$ and $M\le768m$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The balanced-source occurrence argument gives $v=6R$ and $M=8R$, yielding
the displayed formulas. Each clone has one positive and one negative source
occurrence and one incoming positive and outgoing negative cycle occurrence.
A $Q$ port has its missing positive occurrence filled by exactly one padded
implication. All other $Q$ occurrences remain balanced. Cycles have length at
least two, so no implication repeats a variable. The fresh gadgets and
source-clause structure guarantee proper, distinct clauses.

Every model has all $Q$ ports zero, hence has equal values on each clone cycle.
It therefore induces a model of $B(F)$ and hence $F$. Conversely, every model
of $F$ extends by setting all balancing and $Q$ variables to zero and by
copying each source value around its clone cycle. This gives the exact
satisfiability equivalence.

Compose the clone-identifying substitution with the map sending all balancing
auxiliaries to zero. Original source clauses survive. Added balancing clauses
have a negative zeroed auxiliary; internal $Q$ clauses are satisfied by zero;
and cycle implications become tautologies or constants. This proves the
projection statement.

For $d_x=p_x+q_x\ge1$, one has
$2\max(p_x,q_x,2)-d_x\le3d_x$. Summing and using $\sum_xd_x=3m$
gives $g\le9m$. This proves the claimed bounds.
\end{proof}

The compiler has a polynomial, explicit implementation. With validated dense
IDs, arrays and linked occurrence lists use $O(m)$ word operations and
$O(m\log(m+2))$ bits of workspace/output. A conservative deterministic
bit-cost bound allowing sorting/renumbering is $O(m\log^2(m+2))$.
The Python implementation uses ordinary in-memory dictionaries and lists;
these are not a subpolynomial-space implementation. The output is
$O(m\log(m+2))$ bits. For proper distinct densely encoded 3-CNF this is
$O(|\operatorname{enc}(F)|)$, since $n/3\le m\le8\binom n3$.

\section{The proof transfer survives the new interface}
Use Boolean 1 for true, clause polynomials
\[
p_D=\prod_{x\in D}(1-x)\prod_{\neg x\in D}x,
\qquad x^2-x=0,
\]
and the usual PC rules of linear combination and multiplication by a
variable. Size counts explicit monomial occurrences, not arithmetic circuit gates.

\begin{theorem}[All-field degree and monomial-size transfer]\label{thm:PC}
For every unsatisfiable source $F$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:universal} and every field
$\mathbb F$, each PC or PCR refutation of $U(F)$ of degree $D$ and explicit
monomial size $S$ can be transformed into a refutation of $F$ of degree at most
$D$ and size $O(S)$. In particular,
\[
\Deg_{\PC_{\mathbb F}}(F)\le\Deg_{\PC_{\mathbb F}}(U(F)),\qquad
\Deg_{\PCR_{\mathbb F}}(F)\le\Deg_{\PCR_{\mathbb F}}(U(F)).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply $\rho$ to each proof line. An original source clause becomes its source
polynomial. Every balancing-gadget polynomial and every internal $Q$
polynomial becomes zero. A padded implication on clones of an original
variable $x$ becomes $x(1-x)=-(x^2-x)$; an implication on clones of a
balancing auxiliary becomes zero. Thus image axioms are source axioms,
zero, or constant-size Boolean consequences.

Linearity commutes with substitution. Multiplication by a target variable
becomes multiplication by an original variable or gives zero. Every monomial
maps to a single monomial or zero; variables are never replaced by nonconstant
sums. Degree and monomial count cannot increase. Remove zero and duplicate
lines and insert the constant-size Boolean derivations when needed.

For PCR, send complementary rails consistently to the complementary source
rail or to 1 when the underlying variable maps to zero. A surviving padded
polynomial is $x\bar x$, derived in degree 2 using
\[
x\bar x=x(x+\bar x-1)-(x^2-x).
\]
The same argument applies to all remaining axioms and rules. A nontrivial
refutation uses an initial degree-3 clause axiom, so these degree-2 repairs
are harmless. The final image of 1 remains 1.
\end{proof}

The identity $x(1-x)=0$ is used via the Boolean axioms, not asserted as a formal
identity in the ordinary polynomial ring. This preserves the prior axiom audit.
For equality-style sum-of-squares certificates over $\mathbb R$, the same
substitution preserves squares and maps extra axiom terms to zero or Boolean
multiples. Thus the prior SoS-degree transfer also extends to $U$; no SoS
lower bound for the graph-colouring family is inferred from a PC bound.

\section{A source without a privileged characteristic}
Let $G$ be a simple graph on $h$ vertices with $e$ edges. The standard Boolean
3-colouring CNF $F_G$ has variables $x_{v,i}$, $i\in\{1,2,3\}$, clauses requiring
at least one colour at each vertex, three binary clauses excluding two colours
at one vertex, and three binary clauses excluding a shared colour on each edge.
It has $3h$ variables, $h$ ternary clauses and $b=3h+3e$ binary clauses.

Make every binary clause $D$ proper ternary by replacing it with
$(D\lor z_D)\land(D\lor\neg z_D)$ for fresh $z_D$. Call this $P(F_G)$.
Setting all $z_D$ to zero projects back to $F_G$, and one source assignment
satisfies both new clauses exactly when it satisfies $D$.

The published source system $\Col(G,3)$ uses $x_{v,1}+x_{v,2}+x_{v,3}-1$
instead of an at-least-one clause polynomial. This is not silently identified
with the CNF. On three indicators $a,b,c$,
\[
(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)=-(a+b+c-1)+ab+ac+bc-abc.
\]
The right-hand side is derivable in degree 3 from the colour-sum axiom and
the pair-exclusion axioms, since $abc$ follows from $ab$ by multiplication.
Hence a degree-$D$ refutation of the CNF would yield a degree-$\max(D,3)$
refutation of the published source system, over every field.

\paragraph{Primary lower-bound input.}
Conneryd, de Rezende, Nordstr\"om, Pang and Risse prove all-field PC degree
bounds for sparse random graphs \cite{Coloring}. In their full version,
Corollary 6.2 states an asymptotically almost-sure lower bound
$d^{-Cd}h$ for random $d$-regular graphs, with the displayed condition
$6d^3\le\log h$. Fixing $d\ge6$ gives linear degree at sufficiently large
sizes; the non-3-colourability qualification is retained. Their
Theorem 6.1 supplies the underlying deterministic graph-property statement.
This report checks the theorem, encoding, and quantifiers, not an independent
rederivation of their full lower-bound proof.

Define $K_G=U(P(F_G))$. The exact counts are
\begin{align*}
m_P&=7h+6e, & n_P&=6h+3e,\\
g(P(F_G))&=15h+18e,\\
v(K_G)&=1008h+1188e, & M(K_G)&=1344h+1584e.
\end{align*}
To check the padding count, each original colour indicator has counts
$(1,2(\deg_G(v)+2))$ after properization, requiring $2\deg_G(v)+3$
new occurrences. Each properization variable has counts $(1,1)$ and needs
two new occurrences. Summing gives $15h+18e$ exactly.

\begin{corollary}[All-characteristic hardness in exact-$(2,2)$]\label{cor:all}
Fix $d=6$. For a uniformly sampled simple $d$-regular graph $G$ on $h$ vertices,
asymptotically almost surely $K_G$ is an unsatisfiable proper exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF,
with
\[
v=4572h,\qquad M=6096h,
\]
that requires PC/PCR refutation degree $\Omega(v)$ and explicit monomial
size $2^{\Omega(v)}$ over every field, including characteristic two.
For standard indexed bit length $L=\Theta(v\log v)$ this is
$2^{\Omega(L/\log L)}$. The constants are independent of the field.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The source bound, the degree-3 encoding adapter, binary properization, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:PC} give degree $\Omega(h)$ for $K_G$. Since $v=4572h$,
this is $\Omega(v)$. The PC/PCR size-degree theorem gives
\[
 \Size_{\PCR}(K_G)\ge
 \exp\!\left(\Omega\!\left(\frac{(\Deg(K_G)-3)^2}{v}\right)\right)
 =2^{\Omega(v)}
\]
\cite{MN2015}. The same bound holds for PC. All compiler identities are valid
over any field; the source's good-graph event is field-independent. The final
bit-length translation follows from the explicit constant-occurrence encoding.
\end{proof}

This is a pushforward distribution of random graphs, \emph{not} the uniform
random exact-$(2,2)$ CNF distribution. Small tested graphs are not certified
asymptotic hard instances. The larger constants are construction overhead,
not progress on an exponential running-time base. These outputs are not
asserted to be minimally unsatisfiable; transparent padding need not preserve
clause criticality.

\section{What does and does not follow for $P$ versus $NP$}
The specific characteristic-two short-proof escape in the previous Tseitin
family is absent from Corollary~\ref{cor:all}. Merely choosing a different
single coefficient field no longer avoids the stated PC/PCR bound.
This is a genuine strengthening of the proof-system benchmark.

It is not an arbitrary-algorithm lower bound. Monomial count and arithmetic
circuit size remain different: $\prod_{i=1}^t(1+x_i)$ has $2^t$ distinct
monomials but an arithmetic circuit with $O(t)$ gates. This example is not
claimed to be a short refutation of $K_G$; it shows why expanded monomial
counts do not alone lower-bound general computation or circuit size.
Mixed-field systems, general Boolean circuits, arbitrary valid preprocessing,
and other proof systems are not covered by the theorem.

Under $P=NP$, a polynomial SAT decider need not output a PC refutation at all.
A transfer from its computation to a short PC/PCR proof would be an additional
load-bearing theorem, not part of the input compiler proved here. Conversely,
$U$ is a size-increasing exact input reduction, not a map to $N^b$-size inputs
with $b<1$. It supplies no ARC contraction and erases no hypothetical solver
exponent. The existing ARC/WRAPPER resource-contract gaps therefore remain
OPEN.

A direct non-$P$ theorem for a fixed NP-complete core would still suffice;
no extension to broader input families would be needed afterward. The current
result strengthens one possible ingredient, while neither a uniform
polynomial decision algorithm nor an all-algorithm obstruction has been
constructed.
