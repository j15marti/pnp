\section{New all-input restricted gate upper bound}
The prior LSPV-5 theorem gives $g_{\rm match}(F)=3m-1-W(F)=4n-1-W(F)$ in the \emph{declared} architecture of disjoint same-sign matched-clause factors and a binary output AND tree. $W(F)$ is a maximum weighted matching in the signed original-clause intersection graph, where an edge of weight one or two records the common original-variable support.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform cubic-matching upper bound]
Every valid canonical original has a polynomially constructible fan-in-two AND/OR/NAND evaluator, in that architecture, with
\[
g(F)\le 4n-1-\lceil n/2\rceil=\lfloor 7n/2\rfloor-1.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Create a signed multigraph with one vertex for each original clause ID. Each original variable contributes two labelled edges, joining its two positive-clause occurrences and its two negative-clause occurrences. Each clause vertex has degree exactly three, and the total edge count is $2n=3m/2$. No loop is possible; at most two parallel edges join any two same-sign clauses, since sharing all three variables would make duplicate clauses. Shannon's edge-coloring theorem colors any degree-three multigraph with at most $\lfloor3(3)/2\rfloor=4$ colors. One color class is therefore a matching with at least $\lceil2n/4\rceil=\lceil n/2\rceil$ edges of weight at least one. Thus $W(F)\ge\lceil n/2\rceil$ and the old $3m-1-W$ formula yields the claim. Construction, coloring, matching, gate emission, source/index/head reads and checking are paid polynomial work; no satisfiability answer is consulted.
\end{proof}
Greedy five-edge-coloring supplies the independent simpler bound $4n-1-\lceil2n/5\rceil$. Finite exact maximum matching/gate results are Y6 $(n,W,g)=(6,4,19)$, F9 $(9,7,28)$, and F15 $(15,11,48)$; the new upper bound respectively gives $20,30,51$. These are gate \emph{upper} bounds for a particular architecture, not decision-time lower bounds, not unrestricted circuit minima, and not guaranteed reductions in total acquisition work.

\section{New declared binary non-shortening lemma}
Source serialization is Elias-gamma $n,m$ plus $m$ records each of one sign bit and three $b=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$-bit original-variable IDs. A topologically ordered gate circuit serializes gamma $n,g$ plus $g$ records of a two-bit AND/OR/NAND opcode and two $b'=\lceil\log_2(n+g+1)\rceil$-bit predecessor IDs. Hence
\[
N_F=|\gamma(n)|+|\gamma(m)|+m(1+3b),\quad
N_C=|\gamma(n)|+|\gamma(g)|+g(2+2b').
\]
Matching weight satisfies $W\le m$; consequently $g=3m-1-W\ge2m-1$, $b'\ge b$, $|\gamma(g)|\ge|\gamma(m)|$, and
\[
N_C-N_F\ge (2m-1)(2+2b)-m(1+3b)
=m(3+b)-2(1+b)>0
\]
for all valid originals. The large-$n$ ratio is at least $4/3-o(1)$. Constructed actual serialized lengths: Y6 92/242, F9 170/408, F15 276/690 source/circuit bits. This theorem concerns \emph{only} the specified encoding, not optimal encodings, implicit programs, or arbitrary SAT solvers. In particular it cannot establish the power-short canonical successor demanded by ARC.

\section{Actual indexed FLMV resource firewall}
FLMV Section 2.1 uses a deterministic read-only-input indexed random-access Turing machine. A single indexed jump \emph{atomically clears all index-tape contents}, without implying the index head resets. Thus a full-index-tape Hamming change of $b$ does not require $b$ distinct native write steps, invalidating the naive $T\ge\sum d_{\rm phys}/k$ for arbitrary index contents. Index preparation still costs work, and a $b$-bit indexed address can access work position $2^b$: FLMV work space charges the largest accessed work-tape cell location, not merely the number of nonblank bits. A selected-primitive meter sampled $b=4,8,12$ with largest accessed positions $16,256,4096$ and illustrative event counts $7,11,15$. This is \emph{not} a full FLMV native simulator, circuit-to-machine compiler, or arbitrary-SAT lower bound.

An exact controller recurrence includes acquisition and every actual failed trial and successor:
\[
T_D(z)=T_{\rm parse/grammar/source/index/head}+T_{\rm discover/compile/match}

+\sum_{z'\in E(z)}T_D(z').
\]
Peak writable space is simultaneous parser, high-water work positions, index tape, source copy, gate output, cache, proof, ancestor, child and guest DELAY/COPY/RECOMP storage.
