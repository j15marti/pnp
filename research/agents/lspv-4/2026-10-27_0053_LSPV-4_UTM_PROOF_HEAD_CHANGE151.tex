\section{One fixed machine, two read-only heads and a fully charged ledger}
Fix the original 15-variable/20-clause proper signed exact-$(2,2)$ W1
source with sign selectors $H(a,b)$ and satisfiable assignment counts
$772,272,272,0$ for $(a,b)=00,01,10,11$. Concatenate $m$ disjoint
modules, printing all Alice-controlled rows, the $16m$ fixed rows,
then all Bob-controlled rows, in the standard fixed-width binary source
layout. For $w=\lceil\log_2(15m+1)\rceil$,
\[
 \SAT(F_m(a,b))\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a\mathbin{\&}b=0,\quad
 N=60m(w+1)+O(\log m),\quad
 G=48m(w+1)=\Theta(N),\quad m=\Theta(N/\log N).
\]
The $G$ source-input cuts in the central fixed block have
identical physical layout for every diagonal $F_m(a,\neg a)$.

Let $U$ be ONE fixed deterministic finite-control machine with
$q$ states, one \emph{bounded, two-way sequential read-only source head},
$k\ge1$ fixed sequential work heads over a fixed $g$-symbol
alphabet, and initially \emph{completely blank} work tapes.
A program prefix $p$ of length at most $b$ is an ordinary read-only
source prefix. An \emph{independent} two-way reusable read-only proof
tape contains $\pi$ of length at most $L$ and a bounded endmarker.
The proof head has its own charged transitions and positional capacity;
its bits are not silently placed on a work tape.
At each accepting run jointly charge $T$ total transitions,
$S$ all visited work cells including initial cells,
$D$ actual work-symbol changes (not same-symbol writes),
and $R$ source-head reversals. Work contents, positions, proof
pointer, source head and finite control must belong to the
\emph{same accepting run}. A write-only output track is not readable state.

The exact quantified target is
\[
\begin{split}
&\forall p,\pi,F\in\UNSAT:\ U(p,F,\pi)\ne\textnormal{accept},\\
&\forall a\in\{0,1\}^{m}\ \exists p_a,\pi_a:\ 
U(p_a,F_m(a,\neg a),\pi_a)=\textnormal{accept},\\
& |p_a|\le b,\quad|\pi_a|\le L,\quad
(T_a,S_a,D_a,R_a)\le(T,S,D,R)
\quad\textnormal{simultaneously}.
\end{split}\tag{1}
\]
Global soundness in the first line is intentionally stronger than
promise-only soundness. Any malformed source or proof, clock failure
or nonaccepting branch must not be confused with accepted SAT.
The theorem below uses only soundness on the crossed family.

\section{Combined finite packing and change-epoch theorem}
Put
\[
 h=2S+3,\quad
 A=2q h^k(L+3)
 \sum_{j=0}^{\min\{D,kh\}}
 \binom{kh}{j}(g-1)^j,\quad
 V=2q h^k(L+3),
 \qquad
 K_c=\min\{R+1,(D+1)V\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Here $A\ge2$ bounds complete \emph{crossing-event labels}, including
source-cut direction, finite control, work-head positions, changed work
cells and contents, and the \emph{proof-head position}. The proof
\emph{contents} are held fixed when splicing two accepting runs
with the same $\pi$.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-UTM source/program/proof/change bound]\label{th:combined}
For every $m,b,L,T,S,D,R$ obeying the exact declaration (1),
\[
\boxed{\
2^m\ \le\ (2^{b+1}-1)(2^{L+1}-1)
 A^{K_c+1}\ },
\tag{3}
\]
and, separately, with $K_T=\min\{\lfloor T/G\rfloor,K_c\}$,
\[
\boxed{\
2^m\ \le\ (2^{b+1}-1)(2^{L+1}-1)\,
G\,A^{K_T+1}\ }. \tag{4}
\]
No claim that (3) or (4) holds for two independent \emph{source}
heads, a random-access source, a writable proof tape with uncharged
writes, a machine family of growing control/alphabet, or varying
program soundness is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every central cut, crossing from either direction carries the
complete work configuration and proof-head position.
From initially blank work cells, at most $D$ cells can be
nonblank. Every visited work-head position lies in an interval
of length $O(S+1)$, bounded by $h$ for each of $k$ tapes.
The elementary support count therefore bounds every label by $A$.

Between two changes of \emph{work symbols}, work contents remain fixed.
At one fixed source cut, repeating the same control state,
crossing side, work-head position vector and proof-head position
would repeat the complete configuration of a deterministic machine
on the \emph{same source and fixed proof}. It would then loop,
contrary to halting acceptance. Each of the $D+1$ no-change epochs
therefore has at most $V$ crossings of that cut.
Consecutive source crossings at one cut alternate direction,
giving the separate cap $R+1$.

For each diagonal $a$, choose one sound accepted $(p_a,\pi_a)$.
For fixed $p,\pi,c$, identical crossing histories for different
diagonals splice the Alice source shore of one accepted run to the
Bob shore of the other while keeping the \emph{same proof tape}.
At least one crossed source is UNSAT, contradicting (1).
Thus $a\mapsto(p_a,\pi_a,\mathrm{trace}_c(a))$ is injective at every
fixed cut. There are at most $2^{b+1}-1$ program strings and
$2^{L+1}-1$ proof strings. For each pair, the total number
of histories of length at most $K_c$ over alphabet $A$ is
$\sum_{j=0}^{K_c}A^j\le A^{K_c+1}$, proving (3).

For each accepted $a$, total crossings summed over the $G$
central cuts cannot exceed $T$ physical source-head steps, so
some (possibly $a$-dependent) cut has at most
$\lfloor T/G\rfloor$ crossings. Encoding that cut takes one of
$G$ choices; the same fixed-$(p,\pi,c)$ injection gives (4).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Explicit slack and a novel proof-head exponent]\label{cor:face}
Fix $\varepsilon>0$ and suppose $b+L\le(1-\varepsilon)m$ for all
sufficiently large $m$. Set
\[
 M=\min\{S+1,(D+1)\log_2(S+2)\}+\log_2(L+3).
\]
Then $M=O_U(\log_2 A)$ is not asserted; rather the upper estimate
$\log_2 A=O_U(M)$ gives the \emph{necessary} simultaneous conditions
\[
  T M=\Omega_\varepsilon(N^2/\log N),\qquad
  (R+1) M=\Omega_\varepsilon(N/\log N),
\tag{5}
\]
\[
  (D+1)(S+1)^k(L+3)M
  =\Omega_\varepsilon(N/\log N).
\tag{6}
\]
For polynomial ceilings $T=N^{t+o(1)}$,
$S=N^{s+o(1)}$, $D+1=N^{d+o(1)}$,
$R+1=N^{r+o(1)}$ and $L+3=N^{\ell+o(1)}$,
and fixed $k$, this forces
\[
 \boxed{\
 t+\min(d,s)\ge2,\quad
 r+\min(d,s)\ge1,\quad
 d+ks+\ell+\min(d,s)\ge1.\ }\tag{7}
\]
These are \emph{necessary} faces only in the declared model;
the equalities do not yield strict contradictory margins.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The support sum in (2) is bounded both by $g^{kh}$ and by a
constant times $(khg)^{D+1}$, so
$\log_2 A=O_U(\min\{S+1,(D+1)\log(S+2)\}+\log(L+3))$.
Take logarithms of (3)--(4). The advice cap
$b+L\le(1-\varepsilon)m$, together with
$\log G=o(m)$, leaves a positive $\Omega_\varepsilon(m)$
gap after subtracting the code and cut choices.
For every nonzero diagonal $a$, a sound accepted run with a fixed
$p,\pi$ must actually reach the Bob-sensitive source shore:
otherwise flipping a Bob bit where $a_i=1$ produces an
uninspected UNSAT source with the same accepting run.
Thus the common accepting time ceiling has $T\ge G$,
allowing the $+1$ in $\lfloor T/G\rfloor+1$
to be absorbed. Similarly $R+2\le2(R+1)$,
and $(D+1)V+1\le2(D+1)V$.
Since $G=\Theta(N)$ and $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$,
(5)--(6) follow from $\log A=O(M)$.
For $L\le m$ one has $\log(L+3)=N^{o(1)}$;
taking only genuine exponent \emph{ceilings} proves (7).
\end{proof}

\section{Independent native-model firewall, resource transfer and ARC}
The original deterministic ordinary one-tape model M1 counts
physical input footprint $P_1\ge N$ and all source-head
moves/transition steps. The ordinary deterministic fixed-$k$
sequential multitape model Mk counts one read-only
\emph{sequential} source input head and $S_k$ visited
writable cells. For a fixed $k$ and a conservative construction,
\[
 T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))),\qquad
 P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)),
\]
so only $t_1\le t_k+\max(1,s_k)$ and
$p_1\le\max(1,s_k)$ are certified.
For $t_k\ge1$, the one-tape $t_1\ge2$ stripe
yields no nontrivial $t_k,s_k$ constraint through this
simulation. Pippenger--Fischer's oblivious/two-tape
$O(T\log T)$ result is \emph{not} a one-tape simulation.
The read-only proof tape in Theorem~\ref{th:combined}
is an \emph{additional uncounted storage channel} with
a \emph{counted} length $L$ and head moves; its simulation
into an ordinary Mk must also charge materialization,
copying, access and peak storage.

FLMV Section~2.1 instead uses \emph{indexed random-access}
source and work tapes with separately counted index tapes;
its work-space measure includes the largest work position
indexed, subject to an explicit sparse simulation.
Corollary~5.8 concerns one deterministic indexed-input
SAT machine, with strict $s<\beta(t)$,
$\beta(t)=\bigl((t+2)/t^2-t\bigr)/2$, not the
sequential-head work-change face.
Williams (2006, Section~2.1 and Corollary~1.5)
also gives a \emph{distinct hybrid} lower bound:
indexed read-only source, $n^{o(1)}$ indexed
auxiliary bits, exactly one unbounded sequential
read/write work tape, and SAT time exponent
$1.268$ excluded at the paper's stated $O(n^{1.268})$
ceiling. This hybrid is not M1, Mk, $M_{k,\pi}$
or unrestricted FLMV RAT. Its source head jumps by
charged index generation, and a short guessed proof
is not a deterministic SAT decision routine.
