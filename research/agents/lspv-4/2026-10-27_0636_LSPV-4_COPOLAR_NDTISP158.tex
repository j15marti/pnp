\section{One native indexed machine, exact quantifiers}
Fix one total uniform deterministic verifier $V(F,\pi,z)$ with indexed read-only CNF source, indexed read-only immutable binary proof $\pi$ of publicly bounded length $L(N)\le N^p$, and counted indexed work, proof-address and source-address registers. Physical proof keys include exact store identity and actual bit address, not child-local names. Public seed set $z<R(N)$ satisfies $R\le N^{\rho+o(1)}$. Each seed runs in simultaneous time $\tau\le N^{v+o(1)}$ and space $S\le N^{s+o(1)}$, including parsing, index generation, child locating, cache resets, proof reads and all failed computations. Let $0\le\sigma<c\le1$ and define
\[
 A=\lfloor\sigma R\rfloor,\quad B=\lceil cR\rceil,\quad A<B.
\]
All ordinary valid CNFs must satisfy
\[
F\in\mathsf{SAT}\implies\forall\pi:\sum_{z<R}V(F,\pi,z)\le A,\quad
F\in\mathsf{UNSAT}\implies\exists\pi:\sum_{z<R}V(F,\pi,z)\ge B. \tag{1}
\]
The stopped aggregator $W_*$ accepts after $A+1$ accepting seeds and rejects after $R-B+1$ rejecting seeds. Its black-box worst-case seed-call cap from GAP--STOP156 is $K=R-(B-A)+1\le N^{\kappa+o(1)}$. For each ordinary UNSAT source require \emph{some successful stopped run} with at most $q(N)$ distinct actual physical proof cells queried across all executed seeds and hereditary children. For a same-machine construction require a computable cap $\widehat q=O(\log N)$ that covers these good paths; a fixed-positive-slack cap suffices for limiting lower-bound conclusions if only an existential $(a+o(1))\log N$ assertion is supplied. No proof length, endmarker, seed identity, or guest-state carryover may act as free advice.

\begin{lemma}[Nondeterministic stable-map simulation]
With the assumptions above, one uniform indexed nondeterministic recognizer for ordinary UNSAT runs on every branch in
\[
T_{\mathrm{ND}}=O\!\left(N\operatorname{polylog}N+
K(\tau+1)\operatorname{polylog}(N+L+K+\tau)\right),\tag{2}
\]
\[
S_{\mathrm{ND}}=O\!\left((S+1)\log(K+2)+
(\widehat q+1)(\log(L+2)+1)+\log(N+K+2)\right).\tag{3}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Simulate $W_*$ and maintain a dictionary of exact physical proof keys. At the first read of a new key, choose its bit nondeterministically, store that key/value and return the bit. Every later read of that key, including another seed or a second hereditary level, receives the same stored value. Abort on the $(\widehat q+1)$st new key. Seed-epoch tags or a charged sparse reset restore blank guest work between calls without erasing the persistent proof dictionary. The address to query is computed by $V$ from source, public seed and previous coherent proof replies; it is not an additional nondeterministic free choice. Tag, address, dictionary, head and guest work costs give (2)--(3).

Every accepting branch defines a finite coherent partial proof that extends to a full public-layout proof; deterministic replay gives exactly the same seed results. SAT soundness in (1) forbids $A+1$ successes. For UNSAT, follow the promised fixed good proof's replies: at most $\widehat q$ distinct cells are observed and $B$ successful seeds force stopped acceptance. Every branch has a finite uniform clock and halts. This is a \emph{nondeterministic} machine, not deterministic proof acquisition.
\end{proof}
