\section{Barrier and primary-literature preflight}
The two blocking arrows are (i) transferring a structural count for \(H_F\)
to a resource paid by the \emph{same execution} of every SAT decider, and
(ii) transferring that resource to an unconditional lower bound in the same
general-machine model.  Bryant's reduced ordered BDD theory gives an exact
representation theorem for a fixed order: distinct residual functions require
distinct OBDD nodes~\cite{Bryant86}.  That theorem transfers only after an
OBDD realization is supplied; it does not make an arbitrary RAM or Turing
machine materialize those residuals.  Baker--Gill--Solovay and
Aaronson--Wigderson warn that relativizing or algebrizing semantic counts do
not by themselves resolve \(P\) versus \(NP\)~\cite{BGS75,AW09}.
Bogdanov--Trevisan similarly blocks treating black-box worst-case/average-case
extraction as free~\cite{BT06}.

The useful knowledge-compilation analogy is residual-state sharing.  The
missing theorem would be
\[
 \text{many }H_F\text{ residuals}\Longrightarrow
 \text{many paid states in every unrestricted realization}.              \tag{1}
\]
This run falsifies (1) when the antecedent merely counts compatibility
functions across an easy clean-core layer.

\section{Exact compatibility injection}
Let \(\beta=(0,*,0,*,0,*)\).  Exhaustive evaluation gives
\[
H_{G_0}(\beta)=1,\qquad H_{G_1}(\beta)=0.                 \tag{5}
\]
For \(i\in[k]\), let \(\alpha_i\) place \(\beta\) on block \(i\) and leave
all other blocks unset.

\begin{proposition}[\texttt{COMPATIBILITY-FINGERPRINT-INJECTION1}]
For every \(u\in\{0,1\}^k\),
\[
(H_{F_u}(\alpha_1),\ldots,H_{F_u}(\alpha_k))
 =(1-u_1,\ldots,1-u_k).                                  \tag{6}
\]
Hence \(u\mapsto H_{F_u}\) is injective and
\[
|\{H_F:F\in D_k\}|=2^k=2^{\Theta(N_k/\log N_k)}.          \tag{7}
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Compatibility of a disjoint conjunction factors over blocks.  Every
unrestricted block is satisfiable by \(001011\), while the restricted block
has value \(H_{G_{u_i}}(\beta)=1-u_i\) by (5).  Thus (6) separates all
\(u\ne v\), and (3) yields (7).
\end{proof}
On this \(k\)-point probe set, fingerprint distance is exactly
\(d_H(u,v)\), stronger than mere cardinality.

\section{Wrapper theorem}
The union \(D=\bigcup_kD_k\) is recognizable in \(O(N)\) time by parsing the
fixed blocks.  For any total exact SAT decider \(A\), define
\[
W[A](F)=\begin{cases}1,&F\in D,\\ A(F),&F\notin D.\end{cases} \tag{8}
\]
\begin{theorem}[\texttt{COMPATIBILITY-CARDINALITY-NO-COST1}]
\(W[A]\) is a uniform exact SAT decider.  On every \(F\in D\), it makes zero
calls to \(A\), performs zero compatibility queries, and need not evaluate,
represent, store, or reconstruct \(H_F\).  If \(A\) is polynomial time, then
so is \(W[A]\).  Therefore
\[
\operatorname{SAT}\in\mathsf P\Longrightarrow
\exists\text{ a uniform polynomial-time SAT decider violating (4).}       \tag{9}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every member of \(D\) is satisfiable, so the first branch is correct; the
second inherits correctness from \(A\).  Recognition costs \(O(N)\).  On
\(D\), the fallback is not invoked, despite the exact recovery in (6).
\end{proof}
Equation (9) is the required model-matched consequence under the hypothetical
\(\operatorname{SAT}\in\mathsf P\); no \(P\ne NP\) premise is hidden.
