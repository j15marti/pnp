\section{The restricted parameterized theorem}
Let $G^-(F)$ have one vertex per original source clause and an edge $\{j,h\}$ precisely when $C_j,C_h$ share a variable with opposite polarity, equivalently when their local source-extreme SS (and UU) mode is infeasible. This is the simple nonzero conflict graph in Kullmann's terminology. For a full globally coherent SAT assignment $a$, define its set of middle clauses
\[
 M(a)=\{j:t_j(a)\in\{1,2\}\}.
\]
\begin{lemma}[Mandatory middle-clause cover]
For every ordinary SAT assignment $a$ of $F$, $M(a)$ is a vertex cover of $G^-(F)$. Consequently, for every matching of size $h$ in $G^-(F)$, every SAT assignment has at least $h$ middle clauses. The statement holds for general proper 3CNF, without balancing assumptions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every edge $\{j,h\}$, local SS is impossible because a shared original variable would be required to equal both $0$ and $1$. An ordinary SAT assignment cannot have either clause in local U. If neither endpoint is middle, each is therefore in local S, a contradiction. Hence at least one endpoint is in $M(a)$; disjoint matching edges require distinct middle clauses.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Bounded-middle ordinary SAT is FPT]
For input $(F,k)$, the restricted question
\[
 \mathsf{MiddleSAT}_3(F,k):\quad \exists a\in\{0,1\}^n\quad
 [\ \forall j,\ t_j(a)\ge1\ \wedge\ |M(a)|\le k\ ]
\]
is decidable deterministically in $O(N^2+2^k N+k16^k)$ indexed word-RAM time and $O(N+m^2+k)$ indexed working words if its rejection trace is streamed. Materializing the whole branching/candidate rejection transcript costs additional exponential output. Every YES answer produces an original-source checked full SAT witness. NO answers exclude only the stated middle-clause budget, not ordinary unrestricted SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Build the original opposite-polarity conflict graph by scanning source occurrences and producing all $d_v^+d_v^-$ opposing parent pairs for each original variable $v$, retaining the original clause indices. Since $\sum_v(d_v^++d_v^-)=3m=O(N)$, $\sum_vd_v^+d_v^-=O(N^2)$. An indexed Boolean adjacency array deduplicates edges deterministically in $O(N^2)$ time and $O(m^2)$ bits. A greedy matching with $k+1$ vertex-disjoint source conflicts proves the budget impossible; the certificate lists the original opposite-sign shared variables.

Otherwise, choose any uncovered graph edge $(j,h)$ and branch on declaring $j$ or $h$ exceptional (allowed to be middle). Every SAT witness with at most $k$ middle clauses follows at least one branch, as the preceding lemma forces one endpoint into $M(a)$. Branch depth is at most $k$, hence there are fewer than $2^{k+1}$ branch nodes. If all edges are covered, every original clause outside the chosen exceptional set $K$, $|K|\le k$, is forced into its local $S_j$ mode. These source S demands are globally consistent: an inconsistency would make some opposite-signed source pair outside $K$, an uncovered edge. A variable remaining unassigned after applying these source requirements appears only in one of the $|K|$ exceptional proper 3-clauses, hence there are at most $3k$ such original variables. Exhaust all $2^{3k}$ globally consistent completions, check the at most $k$ exceptional clauses, and finally verify any model against every ORIGINAL clause. These procedures cost at most $O(N+ k2^{3k})$ indexed operations per cover leaf and therefore $O(N^2+2^kN+k16^k)$ overall. A streamed depth-first proof search retains the adjacency array, one working assignment, an $O(k)$ recursion stack and constant-sized per-edge source references. A NO is a complete bounded-middle search, not a full formula contradiction.
\end{proof}

\section{Sharp strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ firewall}
\begin{theorem}[Middle budget must be linear on strict22 SAT]
If a proper 3CNF has exactly two positive and two negative original occurrences per variable, with $m=4n/3$, then \emph{every} complete ordinary SAT witness has
\[
 \boxed{|M(a)|\ge \frac{3m}{4}}.
\]
The coefficient $3/4$ is attained by the connected, proper strict22 SAT instance $H_1$ of LSPV-4 ENDPOINT-PLATEAU243, which has $m=8$, four satisfying assignments, and exactly six middle clauses in every model. Thus the above FPT regime $k=o(m)$ cannot contain \emph{any} SAT source of this balanced family for sufficiently large $m$; the NO is only a parameter-budget NO and must never be upgraded to full UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every coherent assignment each original variable has two TRUE signed occurrences, so $\sum_j t_j(a)=2n=3m/2$. For an ordinary SAT witness, $N_0=0$ and
\[
 N_1+N_2+N_3=m,\qquad N_1+2N_2+3N_3=\frac{3m}{2}.
\]
Subtract to obtain $N_2+2N_3=m/2$, hence $N_3\le m/4$ because $N_2\ge0$. Therefore $|M|=N_1+N_2=m-N_3\ge3m/4$. Direct original-source enumeration of $H_1$ verifies four models with $(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)=(0,6,0,2)$. This is a sharp information/resource boundary, not an NP-hardness lower bound.
\end{proof}
