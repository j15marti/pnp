\section{Exact Davis--Putnam elimination with bounded source degree}
Given CNF $G$ and literal variable $x$, let $P_x=\{(x\lor A_i)\}$ and $Q_x=\{(\neg x\lor B_j)\}$ be its positive and negative parent clauses. Define
\[
 {\rm DP}_x(G)=
 \bigl(G\setminus(P_x\cup Q_x)\bigr)\wedge
 \bigwedge_{i,j:\,A_i\lor B_j\ {\rm non\!-\!tautological}}
 (A_i\lor B_j).
\]
Repeated literals within a resolvent are suppressed. Duplicate \emph{clauses} may remain; they do not affect SAT. Standard resolution elimination establishes the stronger pointwise identity
\[
        {\rm DP}_x(G)(z)=\exists x\,G(x,z)
        \quad\text{for every assignment }z\text{ of the surviving names}.
        \tag{1}
\]
The proof uses elementary Boolean distributivity:
$(x\lor A)\wedge(\neg x\lor B)$ is existentially satisfiable in $x$ precisely when $A\lor B$ is true; applying this simultaneously to all opposite-sign parent pairs yields the stated conjunction \cite{dp,eenbiere}.

\begin{theorem}[Sparse two-pivot exact projector, PROVED/EXACT in a valid-source promise]
For every valid $F\in\CM$ with a declared original indexed name order, choose distinct original names $x,y$ (including a no-occurrence pivot if necessary). Define
\[
           G_1={\rm DP}_x(F),\qquad G_2={\rm DP}_y(G_1).
\]
Then $G_2(z)=\exists x\,\exists y\,F(x,y,z)$ for \emph{every} surviving assignment, and in particular $\SAT(G_2)=\SAT(F)$ on both labels.
At most twelve original clause records are suppressed; at most twenty new (not necessarily unique) clauses are retained by a two-stage source patch; every output clause has width at most six, and
\[
              m(G_2)\le m(F)+8.                  \tag{2}
\]
All modified source records can be represented by $O(\log N)$ writable/index bits. In a self-delimiting indexed \emph{ordinary}-CNF output with length-tagged clauses, a deterministic uniform address decoder can answer an arbitrary output bit in $\operatorname{polylog}N$ elementary bit work and $O(\log N)$ live writable/index bits from the original read-only source and this patch. A source-acquisition scan builds the patch in $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work and $O(\log N)$ peak writable/index bits. Both the logical output and its ordinary encoded length remain $N^{1+o(1)}$; neither assertion implies $G_2\in\CM$ or $G_2\in\CS$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first pivot $x$ has exactly $p_x=q_x=2$. Its four source clauses are removed, and at most $2\cdot2=4$ resolvents remain, each of width at most four. Initially the second pivot $y$ occurs twice per sign. Every original occurrence of $y$ that lies in one of the four removed $x$ parents participates in at most two opposite-parent resolvents. Thus after the first pivot the number of positive or negative $y$ occurrences is at most four each, irrespective of whether the resolvents are tautological, duplicate, or otherwise shortened. Therefore the second step deletes at most eight current clause records and produces at most $4\cdot4=16$ resolvents, each of width at most six. The cumulative number of \emph{original} records marked removed is at most $4+8=12$, while at most $4+16=20$ generated records need temporary or retained storage. Counting clauses before tautology deletion gives
\[
 m(G_1)\le m-4+4=m,\qquad
 m(G_2)\le m(G_1)-(|P_y|+|Q_y|)+|P_y||Q_y|.
\]
For $0\le |P_y|,|Q_y|\le4$,
$|P_y||Q_y|-|P_y|-|Q_y|\le8$, proving (2).
Two applications of (1) give exact pointwise projection.

Use a fixed $w=\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil$-bit variable ID and one sign bit per literal, a constant-size clause-width tag for widths at most six, and a logarithmic-size header specifying the name universe and clause count. Original, untouched width-three clauses remain in source order. The patch stores at most twelve original clause indices and twenty width-at-most-six clauses, hence $O(\log N)$ mutable bits, including constant-size loop counters, addresses, tags and arithmetic scratch. Sorted suppressed indices implement source-rank/select: for requested surviving original record ordinal, correct for each of at most twelve deleted indices, then fetch the corresponding original source clause. Requested generated records come from the patch. The output-bit position is mapped to its header, regular-width original block, or one of at most twenty short variable-width generated records through a fixed number of arithmetic and comparisons on $O(\log N)$-bit words. Each source bit is read through the original FLMV-style read-only indexed input; there is no second oracle input, precomputed advice, or free occurrence index. Both preprocessing and each query charge their actual source/workspace reads, writes including unchanged-value writes, control, addressing and conversions. Source acquisition scans all $m=\Theta(N/\log N)$ records at most a constant number of times, with at most polylog bit operations per index. The constant-record edits change serialized output by at most $O(\log N)$ bits relative to the declared tagged input, yielding output $N^{1+o(1)}$. No output materialization is required. \qedhere
\end{proof}
