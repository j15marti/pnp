\section{Neutral-slot normalization}
For a literal $\ell$ and fresh variables define
\begin{align*}
N(\ell)={}&(\ell\lor a\lor b)\land(a\lor\neg c\lor\neg d)
\land(\neg a\lor c\lor\neg e)\\
&\land(\neg a\lor d\lor e)\land(b\lor\neg c\lor\neg d)
\land(\neg b\lor c\lor\neg e)\land(\neg b\lor d\lor e).
\end{align*}
Every internal variable has exactly two occurrences of each sign, and all internal variables one satisfies every clause for either external value. Five auxiliaries is minimal for this exact one-external-occurrence gadget class: $3k=4t+1$ requires $t=2\pmod3$, and $t=2$ gives only three clauses, insufficient for four occurrences of an internal variable. This is a scoped minimum, not a global gadget-optimality claim. Conditional internal model counts are 8 and 14, so it is not parsimonious.

For any proper distinct-clause 3-CNF $F$ with $m>0$, let
\[
r_x=\max(p_x,q_x,2),\quad R_0=\sum_xr_x,\quad
D=\sum_x(2r_x-p_x-q_x)=2R_0-3m.
\]
Fill every missing signed occurrence with a private $N(x)$ or $N(\neg x)$. This gives a balanced source with $m+7D$ clauses and positive-occurrence total $R=R_0+10D$. Apply the recovered balanced compiler: pair opposite occurrences into clones, add directed clone cycles, and pad triples of implications with the actual fixed 15-variable/19-clause gadget $Q$. It is all-zero satisfiable and forces its three ports zero.

\begin{theorem}
The resulting uniform polynomial-time map $U$ is equisatisfiable and proper distinct-clause exact-$(2,2)$, with
\[
v=6R=9m+63D\le576m,\qquad M=8R=12m+84D\le768m.
\]
For every UNSAT source and every field, PC/PCR degree and expanded proof size project back without degree increase and with only constant-factor size overhead. Real equality-style SoS refutation degree also does not increase.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All clone cycles have length at least two. Forced-zero ports enforce equality. A source model extends by setting neutral auxiliaries one and $Q$ variables zero; every target model projects to a source model. Fresh gadgets and distinct signed clone occurrences prevent duplicate clauses. For an active variable of total degree $d$, its deficit is at most $3d$; summing gives $D\le9m$.

Map old-variable clones to their source variable, neutral-auxiliary clones to one and $Q$ variables to zero. In the encoding $p_C=\prod_{x\in C^+}(1-x)\prod_{\neg x\in C^-}x$, source clauses map to source axioms, neutral and $Q$ clauses to zero. A padded implication maps to $x(1-x)=-(x^2-x)$ or zero. It is not silently discarded as a zero formal polynomial. Boolean axioms map to Boolean axioms or zero. PCR uses the repair $x\bar x=x(x+\bar x-1)-(x^2-x)$. Linear combinations commute with substitution, and multiplication becomes multiplication by a variable, identity or zero. Each monomial maps to one monomial or zero, proving the ordinary-PC and PCR size assertions. In SoS, substitute throughout $-1=\sum s_i^2+\sum h_Cp_C+\sum b_x(x^2-x)$: squares remain squares and complete-summand degrees do not increase.
\end{proof}
The map is not claimed smallest: PC22\_ALLFIELD1's different $72m/96m$ map may be smaller, but has complemented images and did not claim ordinary-PC expanded-size preservation. No general minimality or clause-monotonicity assertion is made.

\section{All-field source, adopted with a new image}
Take a simple 4-regular bipartite graph with $q$ vertices per part and add a previously missing edge. The subset-cardinality CNF places every positive triple at each left vertex and every negative triple at each right vertex. It has $4q+1$ variables and $8q+12$ clauses. Eight edge variables have signed counts $(6,3)$ or $(3,6)$, the added edge $(6,6)$, and the others $(3,3)$. Thus $D=24$ and
\[
v_q=72q+1620,\qquad M_q=96q+2160.
\]
Mik\v{s}a--Nordstr\"om, Theorem 4.8 in the retrieved 24-page arXiv version, gives degree $\Omega(q)$ over every field when the base graph is a $(\gamma q,5/2+\delta)$ bipartite vertex expander for fixed positive constants. Their Theorem 2.2 gives exponential PC/PCR monomial size. Projection therefore gives $2^{\Omega(v_q)}=2^{\Omega(L_q/\log L_q)}$ on the exact-$(2,2)$ targets, with $L_q=\Theta(v_q\log v_q)$.

Invertible variable complementation preserves PC/PCR degree: multiplication by $1-x$ is simulated by multiplication by $x$ and subtraction. Apply the size-degree theorem again after complementation rather than assuming monomial-size preservation under an unbounded-degree affine substitution. Thus the exponential bound holds throughout the renamed/complemented orbit. No new SoS lower bound or explicit deterministic expander generator is asserted.

\section{Orbit-wide ordinary-machine upper bound}
\begin{theorem}
A single deterministic sound polynomial-time partial refuter rejects every independently complemented, variable-renamed, clause-reordered and literal-reordered image of $U(\mathrm{SC}(G+e))$, without supplied graph or compiler decomposition. An exact exhaustive fallback makes a total SAT decider polynomial on this entire orbit, but not proved polynomial on all SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original edge has at most six clones. For one source vertex include its at most ten transformed source clauses, the at most thirty padded implications comprising all cycles of its incident variables, and every $Q$ copy supplying those ports. There are at most thirty such copies, so at most $10+30+30\cdot19=610$ clauses suffice. These enforce one representative value per edge and the source threshold inequality on at most five representatives.

The left thresholds require at least $2q+1$ selected edges in total; right thresholds allow at most $2q$. Writing all inequalities in greater-than form and summing cancels all coefficients and yields $0\ge1$.

Here is a source-free uniform algorithm. On an input 3-CNF with $v$ active variables and $M$ clauses, enumerate subformulas of at most 610 clauses. For each enumerate inequalities $\sum_{i\in I}a_ix_i\ge b$ with $|I|\le5$, $a_i=\pm1$, and $-5\le b\le5$. Retain an inequality exactly when every Boolean model of the selected subformula satisfies it. This can be decided by at most $2^{1835}$ assignments, a constant. Test rational LP feasibility of the retained inequalities and $0\le x_i\le1$. Infeasibility returns UNSAT; feasibility returns UNKNOWN.

There are $O((M+1)^{610}(v+1)^5)$ candidate pairs. All generation, failed tests, storage and rational LP work are polynomial in encoded length. The huge constants and exponents are not practical performance claims. Soundness holds because any model of the entire formula satisfies all retained inequalities. On the claimed family, the 610-clause supports guarantee inclusion of the contradictory thresholds.

Under variable complementation, the threshold coefficients remain $\pm1$, support remains at most five, and the new right sides stay in $[-5,5]$. All local supports have unchanged size. Renaming and clause/literal order change only enumeration order. The algorithm therefore finds adequate inequalities without recovering a layout, and exact rational LP detects their contradiction.
\end{proof}
This refuter uses a different inference system from PC/PCR. The theorem refutes an arbitrary-decision-cost interpretation of the all-field benchmark, not its valid algebraic proof-size lower bound. No standard cutting-planes simulation is assumed.
