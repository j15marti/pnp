## IDEA and four falsifiers

**Candidate:** in the canonical full-$E$-bit test grammar, an original UNSAT source of $N$ bytes may have constant **minimum paid inference work** but require exponentially many bytes for **every accepted proof**. Thus polynomial paid W cannot by itself imply polynomial physical certificate-output time. Test:

1. **Alternative legal proof:** another topological order or shared proof DAG terminates without the full $E$-digit contradiction mask.
2. **Implicit/sparse shortcut:** the actual canonical parser accepts an omitted, sparse, compressed or one-byte all-zero terminal mask.
3. **Source artifact:** the odd K4 is SAT, the source requires length $\Omega(E)$, or unused declared coordinates are rejected.
4. **Paid-source bypass:** a sound binary-premise proof can omit an indispensable original clause, use an unauthenticated free multi-premise jump or reach the sink with fewer than 15 unit-paid nodes.

All falsifiers are excluded for the EXPLICIT /531 grammar by the theorem and independent tests. F2 is a VALID possible shortcut for other, more expressive languages, especially the still unauthenticated historical calculus. No prohibited shortcut is excluded from the target full R_mix341.

## Theorem 533.1 (shortest-certificate barrier)

**PROVED/RESTRICTED.** Fix only the /531-/532 source-bound canonical line grammar: original signed strict proper width-three CNF has declared variable count $E$; all derived AND3/XOR records are unit-paid, each has exactly two earlier distinct parent IDs, and the **terminal contradiction requires the fully expanded $E$-ASCII-digit all-zero mask** and RHS=1. Only SOURCE, AND3 and XOR are accepted. No compressed terminal encoding or source-independent axiom is accepted.

Let $F_E$ be signed K4 odd-Tseitin with 4 cubic vertices, 6 active edge variables, 16 original three-literal clauses, and an external DIMACS declaration of $E\ge6$ variables (the rest unused). Define source bytes $N(E)$, minimum paid inference work $W_{\min}(F_E)$, and **minimum byte length over all accepted proof certificates** $B_{\min}(F_E)$.

Then:

$$
N(E)=162+\operatorname{digits}_{10}(E),\quad W_{\min}(F_E)=15,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{E\le B_{\min}(F_E)\le 7E+340.}
$$

In particular for $E=10^k$, integer $k\ge1$,

$$
\boxed{N=163+k,\quad B_{\min}\ge 10^k=10^{N-163},\quad W_{\min}=15.}
$$

Therefore NO polynomial $p$ satisfies $B_{\min}(F)\le p(|F|+W_{\min}(F))$ for all F in THIS test calculus.

**Proof:** Odd-charge K4 local parity equations XOR to $0=1$, so $F_E$ is UNSAT; even charges permit exactly $2^{6-4+1}=8$ active models. Each of the sixteen forbidden-pattern source clauses is indispensable for UNSAT: deletion permits an assignment extending the remaining vertex equations, by bridgeless K4 source essentiality proved in /341. For any *sound* at-most-binary-premise source-explicit refutation DAG, all sixteen source occurrences must occur in the sink ancestry. With $q$ paid non-source nodes, a connected undirected ancestry graph on $16+q$ vertices has at least $15+q$ edges, while binary arity permits at most $2q$ edges; thus $q\ge15$. The canonical AND3/XOR construction from /531 has 12 paid AND3 and 3 paid XOR nodes, attaining 15. Independently of the chosen accepted DAG, the final line *must explicitly contain the $E$ zero-mask ASCII bytes*, hence every certificate has $B\ge E$. The exhibited canonical proof contains seven full-coordinate masks and 340 remaining bytes, giving $B\le 7E+340$. The header of $F_{10^k}$ uses only $k+1$ digits to write $10^k$, yielding $N=163+k$ and the exponential shortest-certificate bound. QED within the stated test grammar.

**Critical scope:** Pure RES, conservative extension-variable proofs, compact GF(2) rows, zero-run encoding, assumption discharge, alternate end markers and source preprocessing may completely destroy this $B_{\min}$ bound in a DIFFERENT calculus. They are required as shortcut controls and cannot be ruled out to support the desired all-rule hardness. The result is a representation barrier, not a SAT decision-time lower bound, a shortest historical R_mix341 proof-size theorem or an FLMV machine lower bound.
