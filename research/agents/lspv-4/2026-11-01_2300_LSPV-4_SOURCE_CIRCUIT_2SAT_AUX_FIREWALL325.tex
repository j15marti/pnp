(\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3\vee\neg g_{26}).
\]
The $C_9$ second OR gate is already forced true by the fixed input $\neg x_4$, so neither $x_2$, $x_3$ nor the now-dead $g_{26}$ is forced by that gate. No other remaining gate unit propagation forces these three variables. Consequently the displayed three-literal gate-defining clause survives.
