\section{BUILD: exact joint relation, not independent marginals}
Write an arbitrary finite system over $\mathbb F_2$ as
\[
A_x x+A_y y=b,
\qquad x\in\mathbb F_2^p,\quad y\in\mathbb F_2^w,
\]
where $x$ are eliminated variables and $y$ are retained. Row-reduce the augmented matrix $[A_x\ A_y\mid b]$, pivoting first through \emph{all} $x$ columns. The nonpivot rows have zero $x$ coefficients. Put their remaining coefficients and right-hand sides into $Cy=d$, and row-reduce this system once more.

\begin{theorem}[Full-port affine projection; standard linear algebra]
For every $y\in\mathbb F_2^w$,
\[
\boxed{\exists x:\ A_xx+A_yy=b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Cy=d.}
\]
The reduced projected system has at most $w$ nonzero independent equations, unless it consists of the single contradictory row $0=1$. A deterministic machine obtains this exact joint relation using polynomial-time Gaussian elimination, for both SAT and UNSAT sources.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Invertible row operations preserve solutions. After the $x$ pivots, every lower row is a necessary constraint on $y$. If these rows are satisfied, the upper pivot equations can be solved successively for the pivoted $x$ coordinates, setting remaining free $x$ coordinates arbitrarily. Thus the lower constraints are sufficient as well. Their row reduction yields a basis of rank at most $w$, or a zero-left-hand-side contradiction. No coordinatewise independence assumption is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Physical proper-3CNF reentry]
A system of at most $w$ independent rows of length at most $w$ has an explicitly writable proper ordinary 3CNF with $O(w^2)$ clauses and fresh auxiliaries, of physical length $O(w^2\log(w+2))$ bits, whose existential projection onto the $w$ core variables equals the joint affine relation exactly.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a triple $u,v,z$, parity $u\oplus v\oplus z=d$ is represented by the four proper signed ternary clauses excluding precisely the four assignments of the wrong parity. For a row with $k\ge3$ variables, create $k-3$ fresh intermediate XOR sums and $k-2$ ternary XOR constraints, totaling $4(k-2)$ proper clauses. A binary XOR equals two binary clauses, each padded by the existentially equivalent pair $(a\vee b\vee t)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg t)$. A unary condition is compiled into four ternary clauses over two fresh names. For $0=1$, use all eight signed ternary clauses on three fresh names; $0=0$ emits no clauses. Fresh auxiliaries are disjoint between rows, so existential quantification distributes and exactly preserves the boundary relation. Each name uses $O(\log(w+2))$ bits and there are $O(w^2)$ clauses and identifiers.
\end{proof}

\section{Counterexamples to illicit universalization}
A two-variable joint equality relation $E=\{00,11\}$ and disequality $X=\{01,10\}$ have the same two independent coordinate supports $\{0,1\}$ but different joint meaning. A join can distinguish them, so separate one-bit marginals cannot replace a complete summary; this is consistent with and independent of LSPV-2 MARGINAL-COHERENCE219. Every solution set of a pure XOR system is an affine subspace, hence is empty or has $2^d$ points. Existentially quantifying any number of fresh XOR-only auxiliaries preserves affinity. The seven-point satisfying relation of $u\vee v\vee z$ is therefore not expressible by such a projection. Even graphically complete pairwise XOR source scopes (primal $K_n$) are solvable by Gaussian elimination. Thus both ``width forces hardness'' and ``Gaussian solves arbitrary mixed SAT'' are false transfers.

\section{Same-machine time, peak space, and access ledgers}
Let $N$ count complete source bits, including the physical 3CNF body, tag, and core list. Source authentication can copy all $N$ bits to a work tape; the augmented matrix has at most $N$ rows and columns and $O(N^2)$ bits. Direct naive Gaussian elimination uses $O(N^3)$ bit operations; every operation can be executed by a fixed finite-tape machine with a crude $O(N^2)$ tape-seek overhead. Parsing, failed candidate checks, all rows, generated clauses, and answer-side fallback fit this ceiling. This intentionally loose implementation gives a single deterministic program:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{Indexed word RAM:} & T_{\rm RAM}=O(N^3\operatorname{polylog}N),\quad S_{\rm RAM}=O(N^2)\text{ bits};\\
\text{Read-only input, fixed-$k$ work tapes:} &T_k=O(N^5),\quad S_k=O(N^2)\text{ visited cells};\\
\text{Single read/write tape:} & T_1=O(N^7),\quad P_1=O(N^2)\text{ physical cells}.\end{array}
\]
The last row is a \emph{simulation upper bound}: $T_1=O(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)))$, $P_1=O(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))$. It counts the original input in $P_1$. With a fixed number of tapes, input/work probes, head moves, crossing events, and symbol changes are at most a fixed multiple of native transitions and must not be \emph{added again} to those transitions. Output is fully materialized in work cells. The peak bound uses reusable input/child buffers rather than summing old ancestors. Separate SAT-side and UNSAT-side restrictions share this \emph{one} program and the same upper ceilings; no answer bit is given. On a fixed universal TM, interpretation adds a machine-dependent polynomial simulation cost; no numerical exponent is imported without an exact implementation.

The above ceilings are \emph{not} FLMV/Williams/Buss--Williams lower-bound model points for unrestricted SAT. The source language is in $P$, the fixed-$k$ and one-tape bounds are construction \emph{uppers}, and neither a native RAM lower bound nor a reverse simulation is inferred. The OpenAI oracle is likewise not a free deterministic classical primitive.
