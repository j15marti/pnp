\section{PROPOSE: four falsifiable mechanisms, fixed before searching}
\begin{description}
\item[]\textbf{M1, mixed exact port.} Adding $r$ arbitrary signed width-three OR clauses to a GF$(2)$ linear system admits exact existential projection onto any chosen $w$ retained variables as a union of at most $3^r$ affine relations, with branch generation and Gaussian elimination fully charged. \textbf{PROVED/RESTRICTED}.
\item[]\textbf{M2, polynomial affine-piece universality.} Every easy proper 3CNF has a polynomial-size union of affine projected subspaces on its original variables. \textbf{FALSIFIED} for the explicit union-of-affine-piece architecture, even on disjoint trivial OR3 clauses.
\item[]\textbf{M3, projected disjointness.} Distinct first-true branches remain disjoint after eliminating private variables, permitting naive addition of projected model counts. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by one proper ternary clause.
\item[]\textbf{M4, closed ordinary-SAT power shrink.} The polynomial-size mixed-affine compiler automatically yields an indefinitely reapplicable single-child $N\mapsto N^\lambda$ ordinary-3SAT shrinker. \textbf{NOT ESTABLISHED}: typed-output closure, parameter discovery and a persistent selector-size floor must be addressed. In the promised logarithmic-$r$ subclass the decision problem is already in $P$, and may instead emit fixed YES/NO bases.
\end{description}

\section{BUILD I: exact projected mixed-affine semantics}
For an explicitly encoded formula over $n$ active Boolean variables, write
\[
 F(x,y)\;=\;[A_xx+A_yy=b\text{ over }\mathbb F_2]\ \wedge\ \bigwedge_{i=1}^{r}C_i(x,y),
\]
where every $C_i$ is a signed disjunction of at most three literals, the $x$ variables will be eliminated and the $y$ variables ($w$ in number) retained. Active variable names are explicitly present in the source; after renumbering $n\le N$ for physical source length $N$. All parsers, equations, row reductions and outputs are finite and uniform.

For $C_i=\ell_{i1}\vee\cdots\vee\ell_{it_i}$, $1\le t_i\le3$, define
\[
 B_{ij}\equiv\neg\ell_{i1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\neg\ell_{i,j-1}\wedge\ell_{ij}
 \quad (1\le j\le t_i).
\]
Each literal condition is one GF$(2)$ unit equation; $B_{ij}$ are disjoint and have union precisely $C_i$. For a choice vector $\sigma\in\prod_i[t_i]$, put
\[
 A_\sigma(x,y):=[A_xx+A_yy=b]\wedge\bigwedge_i B_{i,\sigma_i}(x,y).
\]
\begin{theorem}[Exact mixed-affine full-port projection]
The complete existential relation, including SAT and UNSAT sides, satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\exists x\,F(x,y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \bigvee_{\sigma\in\prod_i[t_i]}\Pi_\sigma(y),\qquad
 \Pi_\sigma(y):=\exists x\,A_\sigma(x,y).}
\]
There are at most $M=3^r$ branches. Each nonempty $\Pi_\sigma$ is an affine relation described by at most $w$ independent XOR rows over $w$ port variables; empty pieces can be removed. Every row and all inconsistent branches are found deterministically by Gaussian elimination. In particular SAT for this encoded hybrid class is decided in $O(3^r\operatorname{poly}(N))$ time, with no supplied answer-class bit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed complete assignment, each satisfied clause has exactly one first true literal, so the product of its clause-partitions partitions precisely the satisfying assignments of the CNF part. Distribute the finite existential quantifier over the corresponding disjunction. Every branch is a linear system, and Gaussian elimination with the eliminated columns first produces its exact affine projection. At most $w$ linearly independent nonzero equations remain; if $0=1$ appears the branch is empty. This proves equality on \emph{every} port assignment and not only equality of global SAT labels.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Explicit proper-3CNF reentry cost]
For a list of $M'$ nonempty affine port pieces, $M'\le3^r$, there is a uniformly writable \emph{proper} ordinary 3CNF with selector and XOR/Tseitin auxiliaries that has exactly the same existential projection on the $w$ retained names, with
\[
 O\bigl(M'(w+1)^2\bigr)\text{ clauses and auxiliary names},\quad
 \boxed{C=O\bigl(M'(w+1)^2\log(M'(w+2)+2)\bigr)}\text{ physical bits.}
\]
The empty union has a fixed contradictory eight-clause proper 3CNF.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Introduce one selector $s_j$ for each piece and force their OR by a binary OR-gate tree. For each affine row of each piece, chain fresh binary XOR gates (four proper ternary clauses each) and guard its parity output with $\neg s_j$. Pad any binary guard clause $(a\vee b)$ using fresh $t$ as $(a\vee b\vee t)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg t)$. Force the selector-tree root using a four-clause ternary unit gadget. Each gate has an independent fresh output and padding variables, so every clause has three distinct variables. For a fixed port assignment, an auxiliary satisfying assignment exists exactly when some selector witnesses a projected piece satisfied by that assignment. There are at most $w$ independent rows per piece, at most $w$ terms per row and $O(M')$ selector gates; every auxiliary name and header is physically printed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Polynomial corridor, not a general construction.} If $r\le\beta\log_3 N$ and $w\le N^\alpha$, then $M\le N^\beta$ and
\[
 C=O(N^{\beta+2\alpha}\log N).
\]
For any fixed $\beta+2\alpha<\lambda<1$ this is $\le N^\lambda$ for sufficiently large $N$. However the compiler's output is a selector-bearing 3CNF, not automatically a source of the \emph{same bounded-$r$ XOR-plus-OR grammar}. Even a typed two-stage grammar would require authenticated parsing and a proven output policy at every level. The original promised hybrid class is already polynomial-time decidable when $r=O(\log N)$, so it can always use a charged Gaussian-branch decider and emit fixed YES/NO bases. No implication for arbitrary SAT follows.

\section{BUILD II: an exponential firewall for explicit affine covers}
Let $R_r\subseteq\mathbb F_2^{3r}$ consist of all assignments satisfying $r$ disjoint positive ternary clauses:
\[
 R_r=\left\{a:\bigwedge_{i=1}^r(a_{3i-2}\vee a_{3i-1}\vee a_{3i})\right\},\qquad |R_r|=7^r.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Affine-piece representation lower bound]
If $R_r=\bigcup_{j=1}^{M}U_j$ with every $U_j$ an affine subspace of $\mathbb F_2^{3r}$, then
\[
 \boxed{M\ge\left\lceil\left(\frac74\right)^r\right\rceil.}
\]
The same statement holds if any $U_j$ is originally presented using extra \emph{pure XOR} auxiliary variables and existentially projected onto the $3r$ original names. Yet the underlying 3CNF has $r$ clauses and is trivially satisfiable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $U_j$ must be contained in $R_r$. Project it onto any disjoint coordinate triple. An affine projection of an affine space is affine, and its range is contained in $\mathbb F_2^3\setminus\{000\}$. It therefore has dimension at most two and at most four points, since a three-dimensional affine subspace would be all eight points. Since the $r$ triple projections together determine every full assignment, $|U_j|\le\prod_{i=1}^r4=4^r$. Covering all $7^r$ points requires $M4^r\ge7^r$. Existential projection of a system containing only XOR equations is again affine, so extra XOR-only variables do not circumvent this bound. Conversely the affine subspace fixing each first literal to one and leaving the remaining $2r$ variables free has $4^r$ satisfying points, showing the per-piece capacity bound is tight.
\end{proof}

This is an \emph{architecture-specific} size obstruction, not a SAT time bound: the same function has an $O(r\log r)$-bit ordinary 3CNF and a trivial witness. For an explicit-pieces-and-selectors architecture, output must record $M$ pieces and cannot evade an $\Omega(M)$-bit branch floor merely by shrinking port dimension; equivalent branches may of course be merged or replaced by a different semantic representation. The discrepancy illustrates why pure-affine full-port compression cannot be naively generalized to arbitrary nonlinear conjunctions.

\begin{proposition}[Projected first-true branches need not be disjoint]
For $F=x\vee y\vee z$ and port $(y,z)$, the first-true branches $x=1$ and $x=0,y=1$ have overlapping projections. At $(y,z)=(1,0)$ both are feasible, while $\exists x\,F$ has a single Boolean truth value. Summing projected branch counts overcounts models.
\end{proposition}

\section{BUILD III: full native time-space and side-specific ledgers}
Let $M=3^r$ and let $C$ be the actual physical output bound, including selectors, XOR gadgets, digits, delimiters, full headers and intermediate representations. The branch/Gaussian algorithm uses $O(N^2)$ bits for one dense matrix and $O(C)$ bits for the materialized output; it processes at most $M$ branches sequentially, so its peak space is $O(N^2+C)$, not the sum of $M$ live matrices. A conservative implementation with word size $\Omega(\log(N+C))$ has
\[
 T_{\rm RAM}=O\!\left(MN^3\operatorname{polylog}(N+C)+C\operatorname{polylog}(N+C)\right),\quad
 S_{\rm RAM}=O(N^2+C)\text{ bits}.
\]
For one read-only input and at least three fixed work tapes, implement each nominal matrix bit operation by a physical scan of at most $O(N^2)$ cells, dedicating a separate work tape to accumulated output. This deliberately loose but uniform ceiling is
\[
 T_k=O\!\left((MN^5+C)\operatorname{polylog}(N+C)\right),\quad
 S_k=O(N^2+C)\text{ visited writable cells}.
\]
With read-only input plus \emph{one} work tape (2-OFF), or two fully writable tapes (2-RW) with the original input left intact, a safe coarse implementation scans a combined $O(N^2+C)$-cell working area per abstract operation:
\[
 T_{2\text{-OFF}},T_{2\text{-RW}}=O\!\left((MN^3+C)(N^2+C)\operatorname{polylog}(N+C)\right).
\]
The one-tape read/write simulation of the fixed-$k$ algorithm obeys the \emph{one-way} conservative ceiling
\[
 T_1=O\left(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))\right),\qquad
 P_1=O\left(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)\right),
\]
with the initial input counted in $P_1$. If $M\le N^\beta$, $C\le N^\lambda$ with $\lambda<1$, then $T_k=O(N^{\beta+5}\operatorname{polylog}N)$, $S_k=O(N^2)$, $T_1=O(N^{\beta+7}\operatorname{polylog}N)$ and $P_1=O(N^2)$. All head movements, crossing events and symbol updates are charged within these native transition ceilings and must not be counted twice. On a fixed universal machine, simulation/interpretation adds a machine-dependent polynomial; no unjustified precise exponent is assigned.

The same \emph{one} decision/projection procedure is used on satisfiable and unsatisfiable inputs. Writing $T_+(N),T_-(N)$ for restrictions of its runs, both obey the displayed global upper bound; in UNSAT cases every branch must be ruled out, while SAT may be witnessed earlier when decision alone is requested. These are not independent solvers with a supplied answer bit, and maxima for different resource axes need not occur on the same input. No FLMV, Buss--Williams or Williams native RAM lower bound is inferred for this P-solvable promise class. Tape-only transport inequalities are not general SAT hardness results.

\section{Reproducibility, publication safeguards and next obligation}
The dependency-free independently written Python audit generated 235 random mixed inputs, including 188 SAT and 47 UNSAT sources; checked 1,434 exact retained-port assignments against full brute-force existential quantification and 177 port assignments against the \emph{physically compiled} proper 3CNF using a separately implemented DPLL; checked affine-cover capacity and a projected-overlap counterexample. In total \textbf{2,587 deterministic assertion groups passed, zero failures}. The disjoint-OR3 physical expansion controls at $r=1,\ldots,5$ compiled from $18,26,34,46,57$ source bytes to $301,1382,6081,23152,92873$ bytes for the specific affine-union compiler. These are \emph{implementation sizes}, not minimal proper 3CNF sizes or universal SAT complexity. No mathematical referee or Coq kernel completion is claimed.

The requested remaining bridge is a representation invariant that controls the \emph{number of necessary nonlinear semantic cases} across adaptive elimination while retaining full port correlations, both answer classes, generation and failure costs and actual recursion closure. A mere bounded width, a tractable source pattern or a bounded number of XOR rows does not imply such an invariant. The counterexample shows precisely why enumerating nonlinear branches need not give a polynomial output on general SAT; it does not prove that a different polynomial compression is impossible. LSPV-1 should referee the affine-cover abstraction against mixed 2SAT/XOR hardness; LSPV-5 should test nested selectors, physical output, malformed source authentication and two-level closure.
