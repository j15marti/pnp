\section{BUILD I: actual encoded length, not number of variables}
Let $A_i=a_i\lor b_i$ and $B_j=c_j\lor d_j$ over $4t$ pairwise distinct original variables, and
\[
P_t=\bigwedge_{i,j=1}^t(A_i\lor B_j),\qquad
F_t=\bigwedge_{i=1}^t(z\lor A_i)\land
\bigwedge_{j=1}^t(\neg z\lor B_j).
\]
For every assignment $x$ of the originals,
\[
P_t(x)\iff\exists z\,F_t(x,z)
\iff(\bigwedge_i A_i(x))\lor(\bigwedge_jB_j(x)).
\]
The printed four-literal CNF $P_t$ has $t^2$ clauses, the printed ternary selector $F_t$ has $2t$ clauses, and the shared fan-in-two Boolean circuit has $4t-1$ gates. These representation sizes are not interchangeable. A canonical expanded source has $N=\Theta(t^2\log t)$ bits and the selector child $\Theta(t\log t)$ bits, a genuine single step for every fixed exponent $\lambda>1/2$ above a threshold. The child is not in the expanded source grammar, hence this alone yields \emph{no} closed recurrence or SAT solver.

\begin{lemma}[Context pass-through firewall]
Fix $t\ge3$. Let $R_k$ contain $k$ independent width-three clauses over $3k$ fresh explicitly numbered variables, none using $z$. If a rewrite replaces only $P_t$ by $F_t$ and transmits $R_k$ unchanged, the actual binary serialization lengths $N_k,M_k$ satisfy $N_k=\Theta(k\log k)$ and $M_k/N_k\to1$. Thus for every fixed $\lambda<1$ and sufficiently large $k$, $M_k>N_k^\lambda$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The unchanged context has $k$ constant-width clauses whose literal labels have logarithmic bit width. Replacing the fixed-$t$ prefix and its header changes a negligible number of serialized bits compared with $\Theta(k\log k)$. This says nothing about an algorithm that also simplifies or decides $R_k$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Materialized NP-hard source transport]
In LSPV-2's previously proved many-one embedding of an arbitrary $m$-clause proper 3CNF $G$ into $E_m=P_{2m}\land R(G)$, the physical lengths are $|G|=\Theta(m\log m)$, $|E_m|=\Theta(m^2\log m)$ and $|C(E_m)|=\Theta(m\log m)$. A pipeline physically writing $E_m$ before contracting spends $\Omega(m^2\log m)$ output work. If it then uses a solver on the child with time $L^{K+o(1)}$, a natural total upper ceiling is $m^{\max(2,K)+o(1)}$, not an improved exponent for $K\ge2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The expansion contains $4m^2$ full clause rows. The selector child has $O(m)$ rows. Counting all rows, literal index bits and the hypothetical solver gives the claims. Fusing construction with contraction may avoid expanded materialization, but then the expanded formula is not an actual intermediate child. This is not a lower bound for arbitrary SAT algorithms.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: exact model-native child and virtual access ledgers}
Suppose, \emph{conditionally}, one fixed correct map on a closed ordinary-SAT class generates one actual child of size $n_{i+1}\le n_i^\lambda$ for fixed $0<\lambda<1$, with generation time $O(n_i^{c+o(1)})$ and extra work $O(n_i^{\sigma+o(1)})$, including all parsing, failed choices, output, input transfer, retained ancestors, and decoding. The depth is $O(\log\log N)$. When children are explicitly stored and reread in visited writable cells, the conservative fixed-$k$ multitape bound is
\[
T_k(N)=N^{c+o(1)},\qquad
S_k(N)=N^{\max(\lambda,\sigma)+o(1)}.
\]
If one step invokes a separately compiled guest solver with unknown time/space exponents $K,s$ on an actual child of size $N^\lambda$, the same-machine upper ledgers, conditional on that implementation, are
\[
 t_k\le\max\{c,\lambda K\},\qquad
 s_k\le\max\{\sigma,\lambda,\lambda s\}.
\]
$P=NP$ supplies unknown finite $K$, not the special time exponent $1.802$ or space exponent $o(1)$.

\begin{lemma}[Uncached virtual bit requests]
Suppose bit $j$ of virtual child $X_i$ takes $a_i$ local operations plus at most $q_i$ bit accesses to $X_{i-1}$, with $X_0$ concrete. Without memoization, per-bit access satisfies
\[
 G_0=1,\qquad G_i\le a_i+q_i G_{i-1}.
\]
A guest making $Q$ reads may therefore cost $O(QG_h)$ rather than $O(Q+G_h)$. The product of $q_i$ can be realized by a parity-of-all-parent-bits virtual interface.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the level and charge every recursive parent bit read. Caching and materialization change the algorithm, and their initialization, storage, work-head motion, address routing and replay are separate mandatory costs.
\end{proof}

For deterministic one-read/write-tape TM with initial source counted, physical footprint $P_1\ge N$. For fixed-$k$ read-only-input sequential multitape machines $S_k$ excludes the source but includes visited writable output/child cells and joint peak work. The conservative simulation has
\[
 T_1=O\bigl(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))\bigr),\quad
 P_1=O\bigl(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)\bigr),
\]
and exponent upper arrows $t_1\le t_k+\max(1,s_k)$, $p_1\le\max(1,s_k)$. These do not transfer native tape lower bounds to indexed RAM. Hennie--Stearns' $O(T\log T)$ targets \emph{two} tapes, not one. Under two fully writable tapes count changes $D_1$ and $D_2$ separately. On a fixed UTM, charge interpretation, source-bit access, oracle evaluation and memory. Coq's proof objects do not waive implementation costs.
