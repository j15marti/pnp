\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Fix $K=\mathbb F_2[\alpha]/(\alpha^2+\alpha+1)$, so $\alpha(1-\alpha)=1$.
For any three-CNF $F(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ with $m$ clauses and $n\ge1$, put
\[
 H_F(x,y)=F(x)\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^n
  ((x_i\lor y_i)\wedge(\neg x_i\lor\neg y_i)).
\]
Every satisfying $x$ admits exactly one $y=\neg x$ and hence precisely $n$ ones and $n$ zeros among the $2n$ controls. Empty clauses can be detected and mapped directly to a constant-zero source.
Construct an explicit layered four-state controller $(B,S)\in\{0,1\}^2$ with sticky failure $B$ and current-clause OR $S$. For each clause of $H_F$, read its three literals in consecutive fixed-address layers; repeat the last for clauses shorter than three. First layer sets $S$ to the first literal, second updates $S$ by OR, third sets $B\gets B\lor\neg(S\lor \mathrm{literal})$ and resets $S$; all transitions are specified for all four states and two read values. A last layer sets the single DATA bit $z\gets1-B$, an affine constant update. Controller branching and all addresses are DATA-independent. The serialized source length is $L=O((m+n)\log(m+n+2))$, $r=2n$ control bits, $T=3(m+2n)+1$ layers and width four; compiler time $O((m+n)\operatorname{polylog}(m+n+2))$.
Let $f_P:\{0,1\}^{2n}\to\{0,1\}$ be its output, and $\widehat f_P$ its unique bitwise multilinear extension:
\[
\widehat f_P(a)=\sum_{v\in\{0,1\}^{2n}}f_P(v)
\prod_i[v_i a_i+(1-v_i)(1-a_i)].
\]
Every contributing satisfying vertex has weight $\alpha^n(1-\alpha)^n=1$.
\begin{theorem}[Four-state fixed-field parity]
For every three-CNF $F$,
\[
\widehat f_{P_F}(\alpha,\ldots,\alpha)
=\#\mathrm{SAT}(F)\bmod2\in\{0,1\}\subseteq K.
\]
The coefficient-of-one decision problem for canonical interpolation over arbitrary explicitly given typed width-four, one-DATA-bit layered sources is $\oplus P$-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The controller's sticky failure is precisely the disjunction of clause failures, giving $f_{P_F}=H_F$. The complement-pair clauses give a bijection between satisfying $F$ and satisfying $H_F$ vertices. All such vertices have constant weight one, so their field sum equals their count mod 2. Hardness follows from parity-3SAT via the polynomial compiler. Membership follows from a nondeterministic machine guessing a Boolean control assignment, evaluating the typed source and its field coefficient for that assignment in polynomial time, and accepting on coefficient one. The parity of accepting paths is the designated output coefficient because $\mathbb F_4$ addition is coordinatewise XOR.
\end{proof}
The general counting or parity hardness of canonical MLE was already known; this is a more specific small-controller construction. It does NOT lower-bound an ordinary SAT decider, and it does NOT apply directly to Williams's distinct packed-symbol local low-degree extension.
\begin{theorem}[Source-tagged explicit scalar DAG]
For an explicit topological scalar arithmetic DAG of $R$ bounded-indegree nodes over $\mathbb F_{2^e}$, where every node $i$ supplies a last-use tag $\ell_i\in[i,R-1]$, require that each edge $i\to j$ obey $j\le\ell_i$. Let
\[
\lambda=\max_j|\{i\le j:\ell_i\ge j\}|.
\]
A deterministic streaming machine validates the tags on access, evaluates a designated final output, and runs in $O(R\operatorname{poly}(e,\log R))$ bit time and $O(\lambda(e+\log R)+\log R)$ conventional writable bits. Input serialization of $\Theta(R(e+\log R))$ bits is explicitly charged and read on an input tape.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Stream records in order, using a dictionary of active values and a priority queue by expiry. For each record look up earlier operands, rejecting absent/future indices, evaluate the field operation and insert its result. After consuming record $j$, evict entries expiring at $j$. Legal operands remain present by induction; an illegally late reference is detected. At most $\lambda$ tagged values are simultaneously live. Dictionary identifiers cost $O(\log R)$ bits, field values $O(e)$ bits, and per-record operations are polynomial in $(e,\log R)$. The designated output is computed exactly.
\end{proof}
No polynomial compilation of every SAT decider into a \emph{narrow} explicitly annotated graph is known. If the graph contains $2^{\Omega(n)}$ records, the bound is exponential in original input length. Constant controller width does not imply few live repeated control bits.
