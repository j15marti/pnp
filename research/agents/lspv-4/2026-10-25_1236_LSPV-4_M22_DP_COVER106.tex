\section{Exact linear-pivot necessary threshold}
A \emph{bare Davis--Putnam sequence} selects $d$ distinct original
source variables. Each elimination removes all current clauses
containing the pivot, adds the cross-product of positive and
negative parent tails as non-tautological resolvents, and may
coalesce duplicate clauses. It does \emph{not} apply arbitrary
subsumption, unrelated clause deletion, semantic compression,
propagation, representation change or free source solving.
Actual source access, pivot choice and elimination costs are paid.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted original-clause cover]
For every valid $F\in\CM$ and every bare-DP sequence on $d$ distinct
original names, the output retains at least
\[
\boxed{m-4d=4k/3-4d}
\]
unchanged original proper ternary clause records whenever the right
side is positive. If $d\le(1/3-\epsilon)k$ for fixed $\epsilon>0$,
at least $4\epsilon k$ original clauses survive, involving at least
$3\epsilon k$ distinct surviving original names.
Their ordinary dense indexed serialization costs
$\Omega_\epsilon(k\log k)=\Omega_\epsilon(N)$ bits.
Therefore an \emph{explicit ordinary clause-list} DP output
of length $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, $\lambda<1$, necessitates
\[
\boxed{d\ge k/3-o(k).}
\]
The same necessity holds over two successive DP levels for the
\emph{total} number of distinct original variables eliminated.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each original pivot belongs to exactly four original clauses.
An unchanged original clause is removed only if a selected pivot
occurs in it; bare DP cannot otherwise erase or rewrite it.
The union of touched original clauses therefore has cardinality
at most $4d$. For $d\le(1/3-\epsilon)k$ at least $4\epsilon k$
records survive with $12\epsilon k$ literal positions. Each
original variable appears in at most four such positions, so at
least $3\epsilon k$ names remain. Their dense binary IDs require
$\Omega(\log k)$ bits per literal. The original encoding is
$\Theta(k\log k)$, establishing the necessary pivot fraction.
The touched-source union argument survives any grouping of
sequential levels.
\end{proof}
The estimate $m-4d$ itself was already in Role~2's original
M22-DP-LOCALITY151 \cite{role2}; the present contribution is the
asymptotic \emph{encoded-length} threshold and the sharpness
counterfamily. It concerns \emph{only} bare DP and explicit
ordinary serialization, not every exact SAT solver.

\section{Sharp syntactic counterfamily}
For each $r\ge5$, take distinct $a_i,b_i,c_i$ for
$i\in\mathbb Z/r\mathbb Z$ and define
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_r=\bigwedge_i
 &(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)
 \wedge(a_i\vee b_{i+1}\vee c_{i+2})\\
 {}\wedge&
 (\neg a_i\vee\neg b_{i+2}\vee\neg c_{i+1})
 \wedge(\neg a_i\vee\neg b_{i+3}\vee\neg c_{i+3}).
\end{aligned}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Sharp original cover does not force size reduction]
Every $F_r\in\CS$ is connected and has $k=3r$, $m=4r$,
no complementary clause pairs (defect $q=2r$), and a simple SAT
witness $a_i=1$, $b_i=c_i=0$.
Eliminating any $d\le r$ of the original $a_i$ by bare DP creates
exactly four non-tautological unique width-four mixed-sign
resolvents for every four deleted original clauses and has
\[
m_d=4r,\qquad L_d=12r+4d,\qquad k_d=3r-d,\qquad
m_{\rm unchanged}=4(r-d).
\]
For $d=r=k/3$, no original clause remains but the ordinary
encoded size stays $\Theta(r\log r)=N^{1+o(1)}$ and the ratio
of leading lengths tends to $4/3$, rather than a power shrink.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $a_i$ has two uses of each sign. Each $b_i,c_i$
has the same signed quotas by cyclic index shifts. Properness
and uniqueness follow since one $a_i$ belongs to every clause
and all signed $b,c$ patterns are distinct for $r\ge5$.
Positive $b$ offsets are $0,1$ and negative offsets $2,3$,
excluding complementary supports. Shared $b$ variables join
neighboring $a$ blocks, giving connectedness. The displayed
assignment satisfies positive clauses via $a$ and negative
clauses via the zero $b,c$ coordinates. No original clause
has two $a$ names. Each eliminated $a_i$ has two positive
and two negative parents whose $b,c$ tails have disjoint
same-variable signs, yielding four proper non-tautological
mixed width-four resolvents. Distinct source indices make
all resolvents unique. This proves the counts. At $d=r$,
$4r$ surviving clauses each contain four literals on
$2r$ names, so the ordinary input still needs
$\Theta(r\log r)$ bits and its leading literal field
coefficient increases from $12r$ to $16r$.
\end{proof}
These are easy SAT formulas with a source-readable witness,
not a proposed hard family. Exact existential projection holds
for each step, but mixed-sign width-four outputs generally
\emph{leave} $\CS$, so a target-only recursive solver needs a
separate charged grammar adapter.
