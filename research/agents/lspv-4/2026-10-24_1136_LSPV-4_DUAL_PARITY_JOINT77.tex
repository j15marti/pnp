\section{Exact construction}
Fix $q\ge1$, $e\in\{0,1\}^q$. Introduce source variables $x,y,z_1,w_1,\ldots,z_q,w_q$. The proper binary CNF $B_e$ encodes, for each $j$,
\[
w_j=z_j\oplus e_j,\qquad (x\vee z_j),\qquad(y\vee\neg z_j).
\tag{2}
\]
Each signed equality/disequality is exactly two proper binary clauses. Impose two long affine conditions:
\[
x\oplus\bigoplus_{j=1}^q(z_j\oplus w_j)=0,\qquad
y\oplus\bigoplus_{j=1}^q(z_j\oplus w_j)=0.
\tag{3}
\]
Each of the two ordered operand lists $(a_1,\ldots,a_{2q+1})$ has a separate fresh functional prefix $(t_1,\ldots,t_{2q-1})$, constrained by
\[
t_1=a_1\oplus a_2,\quad t_i=t_{i-1}\oplus a_{i+1}\ (2\le i\le2q-1),
\quad t_{2q-1}=a_{2q+1}.
\tag{4}
\]
Encode each XOR3 row by four proper ternary clauses forbidding the wrong-parity rows, and the final equality by two proper binaries. Call the full explicit CNF $D_{q,e}$. All $4q-2$ new auxiliaries are functional; there are no new free auxiliaries. Source variables remain $2q+2$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact labels, model fibers and dimensions: PROVED/EXACT]
For every $q\ge1,e\in\{0,1\}^q$, with $p=\bigoplus_j e_j$,
\[
\boxed{n=6q,\quad m_2=4q+4,\quad k=16q-8,\quad M=20q-4.}
\]
\[
\boxed{D_{q,e}\in SAT\iff p=1,\qquad
\#SAT(D_{q,e})=2^q\text{ if }p=1,\ 0\text{ otherwise}.}
\]
The binary subformula is SAT on both labels and has $2^q+2$ models. Both long equations are individually compatible with $B_e$. Their attainable syndrome relation, evaluated on $B_e$ models, is exactly
\[
\mathcal R_e=\mathbb F_2^2\setminus\{(p,p)\}.
\]
Both one-coordinate projections are full, and $\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal R_e)=\mathbb F_2^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$B_e$ forces $z_j\oplus w_j=e_j$, hence each long XOR expression equals $p$ and both equations require $x=y=p$. If $p=0$, the cross clauses require $z_j=1$ and $z_j=0$ simultaneously. If $p=1$, both cross clauses are true for every $j$, leaving arbitrary $z\in\{0,1\}^q$ and forced $w_j=z_j\oplus e_j$. The prefix auxiliaries are unique. Under $B_e$ alone, $(x,y)$ can be $01$ (unique $z=1^q$), $10$ (unique $z=0^q$), or $11$ (all $2^q$ choices of $z$), proving the model count and the three-point syndrome image. Each prefix contains $2q-1$ XOR3 rows, $2q-1$ auxiliaries and one final binary equality, giving the dimensions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp initial strong backdoor: PROVED/EXACT]
The minimum \emph{raw syntactic} strong 2-CNF backdoor of the actual encoded clause list is
\[
\boxed{h_2(D_{q,e})=2q-1}.
\]
The formula is connected in its primal graph and has no initial unit clause.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Its ternary clauses form exactly $4q-2$ distinct absolute-variable supports, each repeated with the four appropriate signs. Every variable meets at most two distinct supports: one in each parity chain for a shared source operand, or two adjacent supports for a prefix variable. Thus any hitting set, equivalently any raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor, has size at least $(4q-2)/2=2q-1$. The $2q-1$ original variables $z_1,w_1,z_2,w_2,\ldots,z_q$ (excluding $w_q$) each occur in the corresponding row of both prefix chains and collectively hit every support. All binary cross clauses connect the blocks to $x$ and $y$.
\end{proof}

\section{Adaptive-state and complete resource ledger}
An explicit total solver first compares the source against the complete canonical grammar. On nonmatches it uses exact DPLL fallback; hence it never assumes a promised SAT label. On matches it regenerates and verifies every clause, pays for its binary implication graph and SCC analysis, reads all $q$ signed orientation fields, substitutes $w_j=z_j\oplus e_j$, cancels both affine rows to $x=p$ and $y=p$, and resolves $(x\vee z_1),(y\vee\neg z_1)$ into $(x\vee y)$. If $p=0$ it derives a sound contradiction; if $p=1$ it writes and checks one complete source-plus-auxiliary witness. Thus $O(q)$ addressed-word time and $O(q)$ peak logical words hold only for the canonical family; general fallback is exact but potentially exponential. Recognition, template reconstruction and comparison, source reads, SCC traversal, cancellation, switching, conflict/witness verification and failed recognizers are charged, not given by an oracle.

The evolving independent state parameters are initially $(k,h_2)=(16q-8,2q-1)$; after paid source-aware XOR extraction and signed SCC substitution they become two short equations $x=p,y=p$; after normalization the ternary count and strong 2-CNF backdoor are both zero. The syntactic residual after eliminating $4q-2$ prefix variables and $q$ duplicate $w$ variables has $d=5q-2$, $r=n-d=q+2$, while after fixing $x,y$ it has $d=5q,r=q$ and $v=n/r=6$. These are representation descriptors, not necessary-machine-work measures. For SAT the $q$ free $z_j$ suffice to reconstruct one witness. For UNSAT the binary resolvent plus the two parity identities suffice; no solver is forced to emit a particular proof object.

The canonical four-fixed-width-word-per-clause grammar plus two header words uses
\[
w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+M+1))\rceil
=\lceil\log_2(64(26q-3))\rceil,
\qquad
\boxed{N=(80q-14)w}.
\]
On $e=0^q$ the formula is UNSAT, and flipping any one $e_j$ makes it SAT, changing only two private literal-sign words. Thus every total deterministic exact decider on the full encoded SAT language must inspect at least $q=\Omega(N/\log N)$ addressed words on this UNSAT instance, with direct $O(q)$ source scan matching that order. This is a query acquisition bound, not an encoding-independent machine-time theorem or superlinear continuation lower bound. Grouped memory access requires separately charged grouping costs; paid source materialization consumes rather than amplifies this bound.

\section{Ladder endpoint and further three-check firewall}
The prescribed growing 2-CNF chain $F_b$ has $n=q+3,m=q+4$, and is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$; its $q$ sensitive fields have only an input-order query lower bound and a matching linear SCC/direct scan.
