\section*{LSPV-4 red-team report 1844R}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open.

\textbf{Reviewed.} Repository protocol/skill, root main.tex, MAIN 1759, LSPV-1 1737A, LSPV-2 1723, LSPV-3 1833A-R1, LSPV-4 1749, GROK BOOL v2.8, CLAUDE-0266, and primary literature including Shaltiel--Umans/vMS, Cook--Reckhow, Baker--Gill--Solovay, and Aaronson--Wigderson.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: proof-padding multiplicity no-go.}
For any verifier $V(x,w)$ and $t\ge1$, define
\[
V_t(x,w,z)=V(x,w),\qquad z\in\{0,1\}^t.
\]
The language is unchanged, while every accepting $w$ becomes $2^t$ accepting pairs $(w,z)$. Thus accepting-proof count, unique-proof singularity, or Merlin-strategy multiplicity is not invariant under a polynomially equivalent verifier wrapper. Unused PCP coordinates and duplicate Merlin strategies give the same attack. Therefore
\[
\text{proof multiplicity/singularity}\Rightarrow\text{unavoidable unrestricted runtime}
\]
is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} generically.

\textbf{Weakest repair.} Quotient ignored coordinates and polynomial-time proof-system simulations, or provide a polynomial-time canonicalization $C_x$ that preserves acceptance, is idempotent, and is invariant under proof padding. Even then, acquisition and transfer to unrestricted runtime remain OPEN. Cook--Reckhow proof complexity is relative to a fixed proof system and does not supply this invariance.

\paragraph{Product-span boundary.}
LSPV-1's interpolation construction is a serious \textbf{CANDIDATE}: in the broad SU05 orbit-polynomial family it prescribes rows $0,e_i,e_i+e_j$, yielding the full quadratic-feature dimension $M=1+m+\binom m2$. Its finite tests remain \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}. The SAME\_REALIZATION gap is decisive: the actual vMS object is a PCP-derived low-degree extension/restriction, and its reachable orbit-value patterns may be a strict subset. Thus architecture-only low-rank claims are attacked, but low rank for the actual PCP-LDE subclass is still \textbf{OPEN}.

Weakest repair for low rank: prove a dependency specific to reachable PCP-LDE restrictions. Cleanest retirement: embed the $M$ prescribed orbit-value rows into one legal PCP-LDE SAME\_REALIZATION while preserving the exact binary sampler.

\paragraph{Other attacks.}
Sparse-Q normalization remains \textbf{OPEN}. If $w(Q)\le\gamma m+1$ and compilation costs $S(Q)\le aw+b$, big-O sparsity does not imply $S(Q)\le m$ without coefficient slack. DEAD padding cannot shrink an oversized target, and changing $m$ changes the range.

LSPV-3's direct SAT-prefix absorption remains \textbf{OPEN}; the fourth-block dependency obstruction survives, so the conservative $k^4$ ledger remains \textbf{CONDITIONAL}. TransportCert/TC4 and ordinary switching remain \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as universal lower-bound bridges. SWITCH+SURVIVE+BIND remains \textbf{OPEN}: maximal semantic/algebraic oracles can supply extensional answers, while INTERP/RECOMP can erase exposed provenance. A surviving BIND theorem must force a nonextensional, wrapper-invariant acquisition event.

\paragraph{Barrier audit and P=NP evidence.}
Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson remain active barriers. Current rank, proof-multiplicity, TC4, and reconstruction bookkeeping do not exhibit the required oracle-replacement failure. No genuine positive evidence for $P=NP$ was found. Failure of a proposed $P\ne NP$ route is not evidence for equality.
