\section*{New theorem and constructive proof}
\begin{theorem}[585.1: exact integer budget and Pareto normal form]
For every integer $k$ with $0\le k\le167$, the minimum of $Q$ among all legal aliases with at most $S=656+k$ comparisons is
\[
\boxed{Q^\star(656+k)=38700-\sum_{i=1}^{k}d_i.}
\]
The bound is attained by an actual alias having \emph{exactly} $S=656+k$. These yield precisely 168 distinct nondominated resource pairs, from $(Q,S)=(38700,656)$ to $(29238,823)$. Every concrete legal alias $p$ is weakly dominated in $(Q,S)$ by a \emph{concrete} legal normalized alias $N(p)$, without changing signed original SOURCE clauses, the original fixed proof dependencies or stipulated paid logical work. The statement holds only within the fixed SOURCE-order alias class.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Proof.}
First independently replace each original clause order with its minimum-$Q$ two-comparison order. If the clause had a positive saving $d_c$, using its optimal three-comparison order exchanges one extra comparator for precisely $d_c$ fewer witness probes. If $d_c\le0$, the two-comparison order is weakly cheaper in both coordinates. For a budget of $k$ extra comparisons, the best exchange is the $k$ largest positive savings: any selected smaller $d$ and unselected larger $d'$ can be swapped without increasing the comparator count and with a strict decrease in probes. Repeating swaps gives the sorted prefix; it is explicitly attainable by choosing those SOURCE permutations independently. The all-two-comparison baseline has $S=656$ and $Q=38700$, so the displayed formula follows. Each $d_i>0$, so every one of the 168 adjacent pairs improves $Q$ while worsening $S$: they are nondominated. Any original alias has $S_0\ge656$; replace it by the sorted-prefix alias with $k=\min(S_0-656,167)$. This legal alias has comparison count at most $S_0$ and $Q$ at most the original, since the original was among the feasible aliases under its own budget. Signed OR semantics and all reused original witnesses are invariant under permutation; the proof DAG and paid records are unchanged. Thus normalization is concrete-realizable and weakly coordinatewise dominating. \(\square\)

\noindent\textbf{Caveat:} this is a \emph{whole-packet} normalizer in a fixed alias fiber, not a cost-sound one-step abstract transformer for missing mixed rules. Serialized UTF-8 bytes, native V8 sorting calls, verification time, scratch, write space and memory allocation may increase and are not dominated. The full-checker source uses implementation-dependent sorting not equal to this insertion-sort comparator metric; LSPV-5/524 independently established that resource boundary.
