\begin{theorem}[Four signed-bit edits, exponential virtual Hamming difference]
For each connected simple cubic graph $G_h$ with even $h$ and $n=3h/2$ edges, take the zero-charge SAT Tseitin 3-CNF $F_h^0$ and the odd-charge UNSAT Tseitin 3-CNF $F_h^1$ differing at one vertex charge. They admit identical-length fixed signed-literal binary encodings of
\[
N_h=4w+1+12h(w+1),\quad w=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil,
\]
with exactly four polarity-bit changes. Their full $2^n$-bit satisfying-assignment truth vectors differ at exactly $2^{n-h+1}=2^{h/2+1}$ positions. One fixed uniform total correct all-CNF decider processes both in polynomial time and all-phase writable space, and individual virtual-vector membership bits are computable in polynomial source work.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The connected graph incidence matrix has GF(2) rank $h-1$. Zero charges give $2^{n-h+1}$ solutions. The odd-charge vertex-system XOR is $0=1$, so gives none. For a parity-three constraint the forbidden triple lists under opposite charges are $(001,010,100,111)$ and $(000,011,101,110)$. Lexicographic pairing changes only the final sign field of each of the four clauses. Our fixed source header is unary $w$ followed by $w$ bits for $n$ and $2w$ bits for $m$; $3m$ signed literal records use $w+1$ bits each. An effective recognizer parses and checks every original clause, groups exact four-clause parity constraints, checks graph incidence/connectedness and applies GF(2). Otherwise it invokes exhaustive correct SAT search. It handles valid SAT, UNSAT and malformed strings with one fixed code. Individual assignment membership tests evaluate the source without writing its $2^n$-bit vector.
\end{proof}
