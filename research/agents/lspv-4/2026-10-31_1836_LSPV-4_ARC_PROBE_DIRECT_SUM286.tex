\section{Same-target both-label source-probe theorem}
The independently certified $U$ is a 198-variable, 264-clause valid
UNSAT instance; its six private modules are refuted and all 64 boundary
assignments are excluded. The valid SAT counterpart $V$ is formed by
exchanging first positive literals of original zero-based clauses 1 and 7.
A complete 198-bit witness satisfies every original clause of $V$.

For $k\ge1$, make disjoint renamed copies
$S_k=\bigwedge_{i=1}^k V_i$ and
$N_{k,j}=U_j\wedge\bigwedge_{i\ne j}V_i$.
All strings are in the selected target grammar; $S_k$ is SAT and
each $N_{k,j}$ is UNSAT. Use source-indexed fixed-width bit encoding with
$n=198k$, $m=264k$, $w=\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor+1$, header
$1^w0\mathrm{bin}_w(n)\mathrm{bin}_w(m)$, and sign plus $w$
identifier bits for each original occurrence. Thus the length is
$N_k^{\mathrm{bits}}=3w+1+3m(w+1)=\Theta(k\log k)$.
Define $B_j=\{b:S_k[b]\ne N_{k,j}[b]\}$. Each $B_j$ is nonempty
and they are pairwise disjoint.
\begin{theorem}[Restricted deterministic query direct sum]
Any deterministic total exact decider of the selected NP-complete
language must, when accepting $S_k$, query at least one source bit
from each $B_j$. Consequently it performs at least
$k=\Omega(N_k^{\mathrm{bits}}/\log N_k^{\mathrm{bits}})$
source probes. This entails neither a superlinear time lower bound
nor a writable-space lower bound for arbitrary SAT machines.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the accepting execution on $S_k$ skips $B_j$, on $N_{k,j}$
the same machine receives identical responses to every indexed input
read. Induction on native steps gives identical control, writable/index
tape contents, head positions, strategy selection and guest behavior,
so it would accept the UNSAT $N_{k,j}$, a contradiction.
Therefore every $B_j$ is queried; disjointness yields $k$ queries.
\end{proof}
This is a known decision-tree/block-sensitivity argument, not
a new lower-bound method; it applies to the designated SAT source
and not to every SAT or UNSAT input. Sequential scans can avoid
the indexed-address rebuilding costs.
