\section*{Exact recurrence}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be the fixed acyclic DAG with $|V|=60$. For an executed order ideal $D\subseteq V$, let
\[
L(D)=\{u\in D:\ \exists\,w\notin D,\ (u,w)\in E\},\qquad \ell(D)=|L(D)|.
\]
For each ready $v\notin D$, define $r_{\mathrm{byte}}(D,v)=1$ if and only if some paid parent $u$ of $v$ has no future consumer except $v$ and its canonical \texttt{JSON.stringify} clause-array length is at least that of $v$. Such a last-use parent may be read and then replaced atomically by $v$ without increasing that slot's byte capacity. No stores into other paid slots, alternate representations, or recomputations are allowed. The exact legal-step predicate at five slots is
\[
\ell(D)+1-r_{\mathrm{byte}}(D,v)\leq 5.
\]
For reachable ideals, set $A(\varnothing)=0$ and update
\[
A(D\cup\{v\})=\min_{\substack{v\text{ ready at }D\\
\ell(D)+1-r_{\mathrm{byte}}(D,v)\leq5}}
\left[A(D)+\ell(D)\right].
\]
Every state has increasing cardinality, so this is a shortest-path recurrence on a finite DAG of order ideals, not a heuristic greedy scheduler.
