\section{Exact source-to-1-factor reduction and proof}
Let $n$ be original variables, $q=2n/3$ positive clauses, $m=2q=4n/3$ original clauses. Recognize originals whose every positive source triple has exactly its negative source partner. The $q$ positive original-clause IDs form vertices of a loopless cubic \emph{multigraph} $G$, and each original variable is an edge joining its two positive source occurrences. Parallel edges keep distinct original names. Both original source clauses at each vertex are satisfied exactly when one or two incident variable edges are true.

For a coherent original pin map $p$, let $t_v$ be pinned TRUE degree and $r_v$ the number of unpinned incident edges at $v$. Put
\[
 \ell_v=\max(0,1-t_v),\qquad u_v=\min(r_v,2-t_v),\qquad
 b_v=u_v-\ell_v,\quad B=\sum_v b_v\le q.
\]
If $\ell_v>u_v$, source-pinned local contradiction is immediate. Otherwise the FREE original edges $X$ must have $\ell_v\le\deg_X(v)\le u_v$.

\begin{theorem}[Pinned paired original SAT/UNSAT with source-checkable general-graph certificates]
For EVERY valid fully paired canonical original $F$, and EVERY coherent original-variable partial assignment $p$, $F|p$ is satisfiable iff one of at most $B+1$ explicitly constructible \emph{ordinary simple graphs} $P_h$ has a perfect matching, where
\[
0\le h\le B,\qquad h\equiv \sum_v u_v\pmod2.
\]
Each $P_h$ has $O(n)$ vertices, $O(n^2)$ edges, and polynomially addressed labels. A perfect matching gives a full original-name SAT witness. For an UNSAT pin map, either some $\ell_v>u_v$ or a complete externally verifiable NO certificate exists: for EVERY admissible $h$, a named subset $U_h\subseteq V(P_h)$ with
\[
\operatorname{odd}(P_h-U_h)>|U_h|.
\]
There are at most $q+1$ such subsets; their total length and source-based verification are polynomial. Bipartiteness is not required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Build $H$ from all free original variable edges and a new slack vertex $z$ with precisely $b_v$ separately named parallel slack edges $vz$ for each source vertex $v$. For each listed $h$ prescribe exact factor degree $f_h(v)=u_v$ and $f_h(z)=h$. A feasible free-edge set $X$ can be extended by $u_v-\deg_X(v)\in[0,b_v]$ slack edges at each $v$. Their total is $h=\sum_v u_v-2|X|$, in the stated range/parity. Conversely any such exact factor yields $X$ with the required interval degrees. Source variable names and both clause IDs are retained.

For every individually named edge $e=vw$ of $H$, make endpoint port vertices $(e,v),(e,w)$ and join them. For every $v$ make exactly $d_H(v)-f_h(v)$ dummy vertices and connect each dummy to all incident ports at $v$. In any perfect matching every dummy uses a different port; the remaining $f_h(v)$ ports are matched to opposite edge ports, selecting precisely the desired exact factor. Conversely any such factor extends to a perfect matching by matching unused ports to local dummies. This graph has at most $4|E(H)|=O(n)$ vertices. The one slack vertex may create $O(n^2)$ dummy-port edges, so size remains polynomial. The usual deterministic general matching algorithms decide and reconstruct a perfect matching in polynomial total work (all copies, failures and source/name accesses charged).

If every $P_h$ lacks a perfect matching, Tutte's classical 1-factor theorem supplies for EACH $h$ a subset $U_h$ whose removal leaves more odd-order connected components than $|U_h|$. A verifier re-creates $P_h$ from the source and pins, deletes $U_h$, counts odd components, and checks this strict inequality, for all admissible $h$. These independent polynomial-size checks prove UNSAT without trusting an asserted scalar factor result. Conversely any original completion would supply a perfect matching for some admissible $h$, contradicting its Tutte witness. A matching yields original variable values and every original clause is checked before accepting SAT.
\end{proof}
