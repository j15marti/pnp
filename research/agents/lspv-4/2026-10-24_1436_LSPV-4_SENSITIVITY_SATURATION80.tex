\section{Explicit matched source controls}
For q>=1 and b in {0,1}^q let
\[
F_b=(z_0\vee u)\wedge(z_0\vee\neg u)\wedge
(\neg z_q\vee v)\wedge(\neg z_q\vee\neg v)
\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^q C_i(b_i),
\quad
C_i(1)=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee z_i),\
C_i(0)=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee\neg z_i).
\]
There are n=q+3 variables and m=q+4 proper binary clauses. Endpoint pairs force z_0=1 and z_q=0. All-one orientations propagate 1 to z_q and are UNSAT. A first zero at index i permits z_j=1 before i and z_j=0 from i onward; the formula is SAT, with u,v free. Thus the all-one UNSAT input U has q equal-length one-bit-flip SAT neighbors V_i. In the declared format a clause has four fixed-width addressed word slots and the formula has two header slots, with w=ceil(log_2(64(n+m+1))) bits per word. Its encoded bit length is
\[
N_F=(4q+18)\lceil\log_2(128(q+4))\rceil=\Theta(q\log(q+2)).
\]
Every orientation sign occupies a distinct addressed field, without changing unsigned identifiers or metadata when flipped. There are q necessary orientation-field probes on U. Bit addresses, word grouping and FLMV index-tape movement require separate stated costs; one W-bit word exposing p independent signs permits only ceil(q/p) forced word probes. No encoding-independent Omega(N) time theorem follows.

\section{Universal sensitivity saturation under explicit-query transports}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT: exact source-field transport floor]
For each fixed q let R be an arbitrary deterministic, halting source-probing machine whose output on every member of {U,V_1,...,V_q} is a fully serialized self-contained target string G(F), such that a fixed target language L obeys L(G(F))=SAT(F). The output size may be constant, polynomial, or arbitrarily computable. Then R probes every orientation field on U. The statement also holds for any deterministic exact adaptive oracle Turing reduction with fully serialized ordinary target queries, whose only access to the changing source record fields is through its own charged source probes and whose oracle responses depend solely on the query strings.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If R does not query orientation field i on U, compare the run on V_i. Induction on the deterministic execution shows every requested source location has equal contents, all internal states and emitted bytes coincide, and, in the adaptive oracle case, every oracle query and reply coincides. The two runs therefore have the same output string or final decision. But U is UNSAT and V_i is SAT, and truth preservation requires opposite target labels or opposite decisions. Contradiction. This applies independently to every i.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT: the q-field source bound has no positive reduction remainder]
Suppose a correct explicit-query target simulation on U charges the reducer's source reads R_q(U), other nonnegative work H_q, at most Q_q>=1 target calls with child lengths at most g(q), and target-time conversion factor tau_q>0. The original q-field lower bound and the known positive-remainder transport inequality yield
\[
T_{\rm target}^{\le}(g(q))\ge
\frac{q-R_q(U)-H_q}{Q_q\tau_q}.
\]
But the preceding theorem gives R_q(U)>=q, so this numerator is nonpositive for every such exact explicit source reduction. This is a firewall only for the existing q-field source lower bound, not for any stronger future post-acquisition lower bound or a non-reduction-based existential ARC closure.
\end{theorem}

\section{Two fully specified transports and resource ledgers}
\paragraph{Ordinary padding.}
For each binary clause (a or b), choose one fresh variable h_j and emit the two proper 3-clauses (a or b or h_j), (a or b or not h_j). The target has n_P=2q+7, M_P=2q+8, encoded length
\[
N_P=(8q+34)\lceil\log_2(256(q+4))\rceil.
\]
Its source witness projection is exact; every source satisfying assignment has 2^{q+4} target extensions, because all newly introduced h_j are free. The encoder reads q orientation fields, emits 2(q+4) ternaries and incurs O(q) construction time. Its q acquisition debt already saturates the q lower bound.

\paragraph{Constant-size, equal-metadata decide-first control.}
Let U_4 be the conjunction of these eight distinct proper ternaries:
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
(a\vee b\vee d),&(a\vee b\vee\neg d),&
(a\vee\neg b\vee d),&(a\vee\neg b\vee\neg d),\\
(\neg a\vee b\vee c),&(\neg a\vee b\vee\neg c),&
(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee c),&(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee\neg c).
\end{array}
\]
Their falsifying edges partition the sixteen assignments of a,b,c,d, so U_4 is UNSAT. Replacing solely the first clause with (a or b or c) gives S_4, with the unique satisfying assignment (a,b,c,d)=(0,0,1,0). Both n=4,m=8 clause-distinct proper-3CNFs use all four variables and have encoded bit length 34*ceil(log_2(64*13))=340, regardless of label. A uniform source-family compiler scans orientations until it sees the first zero, then emits S_4; if all are one, it emits U_4. On U it reads all q, despite constant output. On SAT sources it may stop at the first zero. The inverse from a target witness to a source witness requires a separately charged source scan and trajectory reconstruction if a source witness is requested; a one-bit decider has no such mandatory output.

\section{Adaptive-state ledger and hostile controls}
Before work the state is the canonical source grammar and an unknown vector b. Acquiring the header q and certifying that the input has the promised q+4 clauses costs O(q) addressed-word scans, and rejects or delegates malformed instances to a correct unrestricted fallback. The restricted promise policy scans b left to right; observing a zero changes state to SAT with a recoverable z-transition witness, otherwise the unresolved suffix shortens by one. Upon seeing all q ones it derives an endpoint contradiction and outputs UNSAT. A policy may choose constant-output normalization or clausewise padding after this observation, charging every source read, clause write, selection, failed shortcut, and witness reconstruction. Time O(q) addressed-word steps and conservative peak O(q) work words suffice for both specified encoders; memory may be improved for a streaming implementation but was not measured on the FLMV tape model. The fallback policy is correct on arbitrary inputs but no polynomial all-input improvement is asserted.

SCC 2-SAT gives a matching linear-time source algorithm, affine/XOR and Horn families remain easy controls, and disconnected products or source-readable witnesses do not raise the query ceiling. Oracle target strings that contain a pointer back into the original source are not ordinary self-contained SAT instances; their dereferences, indexing, replay, caches and live ancestors must be charged before any FLMV transfer. Proof-system-size bounds do not force a one-bit decider to produce CP, PC, DNNF or every witness.
