## Theorem 542.1: universal at-most-one unit repair

Do not add or change any proof record. This owner is unused, but real and paid, with inputs 1 and 2 representing existing edge variables. For a core-accepted input E=3h/2>=6, the new z is safe, strictly greater than E, distinct from the guard, previously undeclared, and its ID is fresh because g=0. Second, if the final RES pivot p equals the numeric +999999, negate p and reverse its two parent indices. The old oriented parent clauses contained p and -p; the new order contains -p and p. Their canonical empty resolvent and all parent identities are unchanged. Both operations commute because the added owner does not occur in any proof record. If p differs from +999999, do nothing.

The original in-band source specification and every signed SOURCE record stay exactly the same. The terminal RES parent list may be reversed, but its underlying dependency edges and depth are unchanged. The construction adds precisely one paid owner if g=0, and otherwise adds no paid deduction:

$$
\boxed{W(Q)=W(P)+\mathbf{1}_{\{g=0\}}}
\qquad
\boxed{R(Q)=R(P)=2r,\quad N_{\rm records}(Q)=N_{\rm records}(P).}
$$

The signed core proof and sink parent-set are preserved; count-once owner accounting is exact, with no duplicate facet charge. QED.
