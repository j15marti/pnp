\section{Source occurrence, inversion and actual global assignments}
For each source occurrence $x_{i,j}$ of Boolean variable $x_i$ in original proper ternary clause $C_j$, the sign bit $\nu_{ij}$ is zero for a positive literal and one for a negative literal. It is not a separate Boolean variable. The local all-literals-true target $S_j$ requires $a_i=1\oplus\nu_{ij}$, and the all-literals-false target $U_j$ requires $a_i=\nu_{ij}$. \section{Falsifiable lemma and exact proof}
\begin{theorem}
For a proper signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF source with $n$ variables and $m=4n/3$ clauses, let $M$ count distinct original-clause pairs sharing at least one same-sign and at least one opposite-sign source variable. Then $M\le2n$. Equality is attained for every $n=3r$, $r\ge1$, by a SAT formula with $4^r$ models and no non-tautological first-round source resolvents.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every variable has two positive and two negative original occurrences. Thus it contributes exactly $\binom22+\binom22=2$ same-sign occurrence-pair witnesses. There are $2n$ in total, and every distinct mixed pair counted by $M$ consumes at least one such witness.
For equality, use the four source clauses
\[
C_1=(x\vee y\vee z),\quad C_2=(x\vee\neg y\vee\neg z),\quad
C_3=(\neg x\vee y\vee\neg z),\quad C_4=(\neg x\vee\neg y\vee z).
\]
Each of their six pairs shares exactly one same-sign and two opposite-sign variables, so $M=6=2n$. Every source resolution pivot leaves the other opposite-sign variable and hence gives a tautology. The conjunction is exactly the parity equation $x\oplus y\oplus z=1$, with four satisfying assignments. The disjoint product of $r$ copies has $n=3r$, $M=6r=2n$ and $4^r$ satisfying assignments, with no non-tautological source resolvents. The product is not claimed connected.
\end{proof}
For $C_1,\ldots,C_4$, local $S$ targets in variable order $(x,y,z)$ are $111,100,010,001$ and their inverted local $U$ targets are $000,011,101,110$; inverting those $U$ targets recovers the corresponding $S$. All six four-mode matrices contain four false entries. Nevertheless global $111$ has per-clause true counts $(3,1,1,1)$ and $N_{0123}=(0,3,0,1)$, so SAT; complement $000$ has $(0,2,2,2)$ and $N=(1,0,3,0)$, so not SAT. All-extreme incompatibility is not an UNSAT proof.
\section{Sound source-parent cut and computational model}
A distinct original pair $(x\vee y\vee z)$ and $(x\vee\neg y\vee w)$ has one same-sign and one opposite-sign shared variable. Source-parent resolution on $y$ gives $R=(x\vee z\vee w)$. Falsifying this resolvent sets $x=z=w=0$ and forces opposing source units $y$ and $\neg y$, excluding the entire subcube by a standard RUP proof. At $z=w=0$ alone, original saturated unit propagation is silent on $x$ while adding $R$ forces $x=1$. Resolution is a KNOWN THEOREM, not a novel rule. A mixed pair with two opposite shared variables gives only tautological pivot resolvents.
On an indexed word-RAM with $\Theta(\log n)$-bit words, the complete occurrence lists can be built while scanning the encoded source $N$, and at most $6n$ same-variable occurrence pairs need be inspected. A constant-width clause comparison per candidate gives an $O(N+n)$ word-operation sparse four-mode representation with $O(N+n\log n)$ source-plus-index bits and at most $2n$ mixed entries. A dense materialized $m$-by-$m$ matrix instead uses $\Theta(m^2)$ bits. This is not an arbitrary SAT lower bound; single-tape head movement and other models are kept separate.
