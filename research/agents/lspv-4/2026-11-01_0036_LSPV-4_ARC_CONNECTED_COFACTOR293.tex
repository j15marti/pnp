\section{Quantified theorem and proof}
Let $\mathcal D$ consist of all nonempty duplicate-free proper clausewise-monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) formulas: each original clause has three distinct same-sign variables and each original variable appears exactly twice positively and twice negatively. Let $I(F)$ be the original variable--clause incidence graph. For $F\in\mathcal D$ and any coherent partial assignment $p$, the \emph{raw syntactic} cofactor $F|p$ deletes clauses made true and deletes falsified assigned occurrences; it adds no auxiliary clauses or new variables.
\begin{theorem}
For every $F\in\mathcal D$ and every coherent $p$, the nonempty raw cofactor $F|p$ belongs to $\mathcal D$ (up to bijective relabeling) \emph{if and only if} it consists exactly of a nonempty union of original incidence-connected components that $p$ leaves completely untouched, and $p$ satisfies every original clause outside those components. In particular, if $I(F)$ is connected, no nonempty partial assignment has a nonempty target-valid raw cofactor child.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each variable $x$ surviving in a target-valid $G=F|p$ has exactly the same two positive and two negative source occurrences in $F$ and $G$. Restriction deletes occurrences and never creates them. Hence all four occurrences of $x$ survive, every source clause incident with $x$ survives, and the proper width-three requirement means none of its other source literals was deleted. Its two neighboring variables thus also survive unassigned and with all four occurrences. Propagating this implication across incidence edges retains the entire original connected component of $x$ unchanged. Every other original clause was deleted rather than shortened and hence is satisfied by an assigned true literal. The surviving components are nonempty, proving necessity. Conversely, deleting only fully satisfied components while leaving an unchanged nonempty union of source components preserves clause monotonicity, proper distinct-literal triples, nonduplication and exact signed occurrence quotas, proving sufficiency. For a connected $F$, a nonempty $p$ touches the only component, so the required untouched nonempty union is impossible.
\end{proof}
A fully satisfied empty cofactor is a SAT terminal, not a canonical child; an empty clause is an ordinary-CNF UNSAT terminal, not a canonical child. The theorem does \emph{not} exclude semantic compression, target reencoding, auxiliary gadgets, a recognizably easy connected family, or an arbitrary polynomial SAT solver. It strengthens the previously verified LSPV-4 SURVIVOR-FRONTIER292 single-pivot source-identity bound only in the raw syntactic model.
