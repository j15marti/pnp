\section*{PRIMARY LITERATURE PREFLIGHT}
Valiant's 1986 theorem states that for an $N$-variable Boolean slice function, monotone circuit complexity exceeds unrestricted circuit complexity by at most a constant multiplicative factor plus $O(N(\log N)^2)$. Wegener's fixed-slice work warns that standard monotone lower-bound methods can fail on slices and records canonical slices, including CLIQUE, with linear-size circuits. Hrube\v{s}--Pudl\'ak prove strong monotone-real-circuit lower bounds for approximate satisfiability of random $k$-CNFs only when $k=\omega(1)$, becoming exponential around $k\approx\log n$; this does not close fixed $k=3$. Razborov--Rudich applies to broad constructive-large properties for unrestricted circuit lower bounds under its pseudorandom-function hardness hypothesis; it is a required audit if the slice proof induces such a property, not an automatic refutation of every monotone proof.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: SLICE-KW LEAF PROTOCOL}
Let $A$ be an unsatisfiable exact-layer 3-CNF clause set and $B$ a satisfiable exact-layer clause set on the same $q$ variables. In the monotone Karchmer--Wigderson relation, the task is to output a clause in $A\setminus B$. Bob sends any satisfying assignment $\beta$ of $B$ using $q$ bits. Since $A$ is unsatisfiable, Alice can choose a clause $C\in A$ falsified by $\beta$. Because $\beta$ satisfies every clause in $B$, $C\notin B$. Thus
\[
D(R_{q,t})\le q+O(\log M_q)
\]
for the ordinary deterministic communication formulation, with the output index charged if needed. Therefore ordinary communication depth cannot yield the desired superpolynomial monotone circuit-size lower bound. Any surviving KW route must control DAG protocol size, not merely communication depth.

This agrees with the newest TASK-008 SLICEKW1 update and independently red-teams any attempt to infer size hardness from transcript length.

\section*{PROVED/EXACT: SLICE TRANSFER CALIBRATION}
Assume the exact Valiant slice theorem in its published form:
\[
C_{\rm mon}(f)\le c\,C(f)+O(N(\log N)^2)
\]
for an $N$-variable slice function $f$. Hence a superpolynomial lower bound
\[
C_{\rm mon}(U_{q,t})=N^{\omega(1)}
\]
implies $C(U_{q,t})=N^{\omega(1)}$. If, independently, arbitrary 3-SAT reduces by a polynomial-size many-one reduction to the chosen exact layer, then polynomial-size circuits for SAT would compose with the reduction and complement to give polynomial-size circuits for $U_{q,t}$. Therefore the two ingredients together imply
\[
NP\not\subseteq P/poly.
\]
This is stronger than merely $P\ne NP$. The strength is a calibration warning: TASK-008 is targeting a major unrestricted circuit lower bound.

The padding reduction by itself does not transfer monotone lower bounds. The current corpus already proves the elementary guard that a coordinatewise-monotone map with constant output Hamming weight is constant. Thus the ordinary fixed-weight padding map is not a monotone projection, and full-cube monotone hardness cannot be imported through that padding.

\section*{HOSTILE AUDIT OF SLICE-UNSAT}
The new branch avoids the arbitrary-algorithm acquisition problem only if Valiant's established slice simulation applies exactly to the chosen total slice extension. That is a genuine advantage over HEC/CCSPC. But the burden moves to an unrestricted-circuit lower bound in disguise.

Easy-slice controls remain mandatory. Wegener's result shows that canonical hard-looking monotone functions may have easy slices. Horn, 2-SAT, bounded-treewidth, Tseitin/parity, padding, duplicate/dead coordinates, and independent products therefore remain falsification controls for any proposed combinatorial measure.

The ordinary KW depth route is now FALSIFIED/RETIRED as a route to superpolynomial size by the explicit $q$-bit satisfying-assignment protocol above. The DAG-KW-size target remains OPEN.

Natural-Proofs audit: a direct combinatorial monotone lower bound for this one explicit slice is not automatically a Razborov--Rudich natural proof. However, because Valiant converts the conclusion into a superpolynomial unrestricted-circuit lower bound, any proposed proof measure that extends to a polynomial-time recognizable property holding for a large fraction of Boolean functions must be checked against the Natural-Proofs barrier before promotion. No such largeness/constructivity theorem is currently supplied, so Natural Proofs is a guard, not a present contradiction.

Semantic and algebraic oracles do not directly refute the Valiant transfer, because this branch is circuit-theoretic rather than an arbitrary-machine acquisition argument. They do refute attempts to reuse product-span or causal acquisition claims as if they were forced resources inside the slice proof.
