The existence of an authenticated easy source attaining a large syntax proxy again falsifies promoting that proxy to an algorithm-independent SAT lower bound.

\section{BUILD I: a stronger necessary structural inequality}
Let $F$ be a connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF with $n$ variables and $m=4n/3$ clauses. Let $Y$ be a fixed boundary with $|Y|=b\ge1$. Removing $Y$ leaves nonempty connected private components $Z_1,\dots,Z_k$. No clause contains private variables of two components. Let $h$ count clauses wholly inside $Y$. For each $i$, let $B_i\subseteq Z_i$ hit every clause disjoint from $Y$, with $|B_i|\le q$. Let $a_i\ge1$ count \emph{boundary-variable occurrences} in clauses containing a private variable from component $i$; these occurrences may belong to clauses with one or two boundary names.

\begin{lemma}[Known quotient bound; not new]\label{lem:quotient}
One has $k+h\le3b+1$ and $\sum_i a_i=4b-3h$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Contract each private component and its associated private-containing clause vertices in the connected clause-variable incidence graph. Keep $b$ boundary variable vertices and $h$ boundary-only clause vertices. The quotient has $b+k+h$ vertices and $4b$ boundary-incidence edges, counted with multiplicity, so connectivity yields $b+k+h-1\le4b$. Every boundary-only width-three clause consumes exactly three boundary incidences, giving the sum formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[New mod-four module surplus]\label{lem:parity}
Writing $v_i=|Z_i|$ and $r_i=|B_i|$, every module satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local}
 \boxed{v_i\le3r_i+2a_i-3.}
\end{equation}
The bound is sharp for both $a_i=1$ and $a_i=2$ within genuine signed exact-$(2,2)$ clauses.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $c_i$ be the number of all clauses containing private variables from $Z_i$. Such clauses are hit by $Y\cup B_i$. Define the nonnegative incidence surplus
\[
 \delta_i:=\sum_{C\text{ in module }i}\bigl(|C\cap(Y\cup B_i)|-1\bigr)\ge0.
\]
The term counts \emph{distinct literal positions} in each proper clause. Each private hitting variable occurs exactly four times, so $c_i=4r_i+a_i-\delta_i$. Counting all private literal positions gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mod4}
 4v_i=3c_i-a_i=12r_i+2a_i-3\delta_i,
 \qquad \delta_i\equiv2a_i\pmod4.
\end{equation}
For $a_i=1$ the congruence forces $\delta_i\ge2$. For $a_i\ge2$ nonnegativity implies $\delta_i\ge0\ge4-2a_i$. Thus universally $\delta_i\ge4-2a_i$. Substitute in \eqref{eq:mod4} and divide by four to obtain \eqref{eq:local}. The explicit leaf and connector constructions below respectively attain equality at $a_i=1$ and $a_i=2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp parity-refined static bound]\label{th:bound}
Under the stated connected, clause-owned, residual-hitting hypotheses, if $q\ge1$ then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:newbound}
 \boxed{n\le(9b+3)q-3-3(q+1)h\le(9b+3)q-3.}
\end{equation}
For $q=0$ one instead has the known separate necessary condition $n\le3b$. These are \emph{structural} inequalities only.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:parity} and Lemma~\ref{lem:quotient},
\begin{align*}
 n=b+\sum_i v_i&\le b+3kq+2\sum_i a_i-3k\\
 &=9b+3k(q-1)-6h\\
 &\le9b+3(3b+1-h)(q-1)-6h\\
 &=(9b+3)q-3-3(q+1)h.
\end{align*}
The inequality using $k+h\le3b+1$ requires $q-1\ge0$, explaining the case split. If $q=0$, every clause is hit by $Y$, hence $4n/3=m\le4b$, or $n\le3b$.
\end{proof}

\section{BUILD II: exact equality via a $K_5$ signed connector}
For any $b\ge1$, arrange $b$ boundary variables along a path, attaching one private connector between adjacent boundary variables and enough leaves to make boundary degree exactly four. This uses $b-1$ connectors and $2b+2$ leaves, giving $k=3b+1$ and $h=0$. Every boundary occurs in four crossing clauses and receives two positive and two negative signs. The leaf gadget is the known five-private-variable, seven-clause construction \cite{component}; its single boundary clause has one boundary name and two private names, its six private clauses have minimum private hitting number two, its module surplus is $\delta_i=2$, and it has an all-true private witness for either boundary value.

The new \emph{connector} uses two private hitting variables $p_1,p_2$ and five private ordinary variables $u_0,\ldots,u_4$. Take every one of the ten unordered edges $\{u_i,u_j\}$ of $K_5$ once, and make a three-literal clause consisting of those two endpoints and one leading literal. Assign two distinct edges to boundary variables $y_L,y_R$, four distinct edges to $p_1$, and the remaining four to $p_2$. Every $p_j$ receives exactly two positive and two negative appearances. Orient $K_5$ as the regular cyclic tournament: the endpoint $u_i$ receives positive sign on edge $\{i,j\}$ precisely when $(j-i)\bmod5\in\{1,2\}$, and the other endpoint receives negative sign. Every $u_i$ has exactly two positive and two negative appearances, and \emph{every connector clause has one positive $u$ literal}. The two boundary clauses and eight private clauses are proper, distinct, connected in the private primal graph, and have exact minimum private hitting number two: $\{p_1,p_2\}$ hits all eight private clauses, while any singleton hits at most four. There are seven private variables, ten clauses, $a_i=2$, and $\delta_i=0$, attaining equality in \eqref{eq:local}.

For a desired local hitting parameter $q\ge2$, attach to each leaf or connector a balanced signed cyclic gadget on $3r$ fresh variables, where $r=q-2$, containing $3r$ cyclic clauses $(x_i\vee x_{i+1}\vee\neg x_{i+2})$ and $r$ negative block clauses $(\neg x_{3j}\vee\neg x_{3j+1}\vee\neg x_{3j+2})$. When $r>0$, swap one positive ordinary private literal of an internal base clause with positive $x_1$ in cyclic clause $C_0$. Every literal occurrence retains its sign and degree, every clause remains proper, the component remains connected, and the hitting set extends by $\{x_0,x_3,\ldots,x_{3r-3}\}$. To show it is optimal, observe that the $r$ disjoint negative block clauses require $r$ distinct cyclic names. The five unchanged old private leaf clauses, or seven unchanged old private connector clauses, require \emph{at least two} original private names since no one base name occurs in more than four clauses. Therefore $\min|B_i|=r+2=q$. Every boundary assignment still extends to the explicit witness assigning old private names TRUE and each cyclic block periodically $110$.

\begin{theorem}[New complete equality family]\label{th:family}
For every $b\ge1$ and $q\ge2$, the preceding construction uniformly produces a connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$-3CNF with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exact}
 \boxed{k=3b+1,\quad h=0,\quad n=(9b+3)q-3,\quad m=4n/3,\quad \min|B_i|=q\ \forall i.}
\end{equation}
It attains equality in \eqref{eq:newbound}. Its exact projected relation onto all $b$ boundary variables is identically TRUE, with polynomial source authentication and an explicit satisfying assignment.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The quotient tree is connected and uses every boundary occurrence exactly once. Leaves contribute five private variables each; connectors contribute seven. The base source therefore has $n=b+5(2b+2)+7(b-1)=18b+3$ names. Each of the $k=3b+1$ modules gains $3(q-2)$ fresh names, giving
\[
 n=18b+3+3(3b+1)(q-2)=(9b+3)q-3.
\]
The signed-degree, connectivity, exact hitting number, and universal boundary witness arguments are given above. Together with Theorem~\ref{th:bound}, the construction proves equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Globally minimizing realized integer proxies]\label{cor:optimum}
For each integer $j\ge2$, choose $b=q=j$. Then $n=9j^2+3j-3$ and the actual source realizes the globally smallest \emph{permitted integer enumeration exponent} $t=b+q=2j$ under \eqref{eq:newbound}; the optional boundary-table exponent is $s=b=j$. By adjusting two independent cyclic amplification lengths from $11$ to $10$ in the $b=10,q=13$ construction, one obtains an authenticated strict exact-$(2,2)$ source with \emph{exactly} $n=1200$, $m=1600$, $k=31$, maximum local hitting number $q=13$, and $t=23,s=10$. This realizes the minimum integer proxy exponent permitted by the parity-refined bound at $n=1200$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
In the positive-$q$ continuous relaxation, $t(b)=b+(n+3)/(9b+3)$ has minimum $\frac23\sqrt{n+3}-\frac13$. At $n=9j^2+3j-3$, that value lies strictly between $2j-1$ and $2j$, so the minimum integer exponent is at least $2j$; the constructed point attains it. The $q=0$ branch requires $b\ge n/3>2j$ for $j\ge2$. For $n=1200$, exact integer enumeration gives minimum $23$, attained arithmetically at $b=10,11,12$. The base $b=10,q=13$ construction has $n=1206$; omitting one three-variable cyclic block from each of two modules yields $1200$ variables and preserves the maximum local hitting number $13$, since the other 29 modules are unchanged. Sign balance, connectivity and the explicit witness remain valid. The accompanying code constructs and checks this exact source.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact integer crossings, derivatives, slopes and realizability}
Write the old necessary $q$ and the known connected quotient improvement as
\[
q_{\rm old}(n,b)=\max\{0,\lceil(n-3b)/(12b)\rceil\},\quad
q_{\rm quotient}(n,b)=\max\{0,\lceil(n-3b)/(9b+3)\rceil\}.
\]
The new condition is
\[
q_{\rm parity}(n,b)=\begin{cases}0,&n\le3b,\\\max\{1,\lceil(n+3)/(9b+3)\rceil\},&n>3b.\end{cases}
\]
The arithmetic logs $t=b+q$ and $s_{\rm table}=b$ describe a particular optional enumeration and table materialization, not actual same-machine $(\log T,\log S)$ and not decision lower bounds. On the $q>0$ continuous branch,
\begin{gather*}
 t(b)=b+\frac{n+3}{9b+3},\quad s(b)=b,\\
 \frac{dt}{ds}=1-\frac{9(n+3)}{(9b+3)^2},\qquad
 \frac{d^2t}{ds^2}=\frac{162(n+3)}{(9b+3)^3}>0,\\
 b^*=\frac{\sqrt{n+3}-1}{3},\qquad
 t^*=\frac23\sqrt{n+3}-\frac13.
\end{gather*}
For $b=\alpha\sqrt n$, $t/\sqrt n\to\alpha+1/(9\alpha)$ and $s/\sqrt n\to\alpha$, with limiting slope $1-1/(9\alpha^2)$. The leading optimum $t^*/\sqrt n\to2/3$ and $b^*/\sqrt n\to1/3$. The two log proxy curves first coincide \emph{conditionally} at $q=0$, earliest $b=\lceil n/3\rceil$, and this intersection remains strongly dominated in the optional $(t,s)$ coordinates. The canonical unpadded bit encoding has $N=\Theta(n\log n)$. Even at $t=\Theta(\sqrt n)$, $2^t$ is superpolynomial in $N$; this remains a cost of one optional enumeration architecture only.

\section{SAME-RUN T/S ledger and algorithm-independent firewall}
For a \emph{supplied and polynomially authenticated} boundary and private partition, a deterministic depth-bounded ternary clause-hitting search discovers each $B_i$ in at most $3^q\operatorname{poly}(N)$ attempts. Then one fixed uniform controller enumerates $2^b$ boundary assignments, tests up to $2^q$ local backdoor assignments per module, and invokes exact 2-SAT on the residual at each leaf. The same code processes both SAT and UNSAT; all failed choices, joins, parsing, source scans, witness verification and output are charged. With
\[A=k\bigl(3^q+2^{b+q}\bigr),\]
one conservative fixed read-only-input multitape implementation has $T_k=O(AN^5)$ transitions and writable visited space $S_k=O(N^2)$ cells. The stipulated input-counting one-tape scan simulation yields
\[
T_1=O\bigl(T_k(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2))\bigr)=O(AN^7),\quad
P_1=O\bigl(N+S_k+\log(T_k+2)\bigr)=O(N^2).
\]
For the two-fully-writable-tape model, emulating this one-tape algorithm gives the conservative ceiling $T_2=O(AN^7)$ and $S_2=O(N^2)$ auxiliary cells, with input still physically present. In an indexed RAM with $O(\log N)$-bit words and explicit bounded addresses, one generous ceiling is $O(AN^4)$ word operations and $O(N^2)$ work bits; fixed universal-machine overhead is polynomial but no precise exponent is certified. These are \emph{constructive ceilings}, not certified optimal exponents or exact instruction traces. Discovery of an arbitrary suitable boundary and partition is \emph{not free}: the formulas are not known to admit one uniformly, and any all-input controller must charge this acquisition separately.

A full $2^b$-row boundary table has $\Omega(2^b)$ bits of \emph{representation-specific} storage. A depth-first streaming decider can use polynomial writable memory and no table. On the displayed family a polynomial authenticator simply outputs the periodic witness and the exact projection TRUE, bypassing both syntactic exponential proxies. Under a fixed single-tape model the initial source footprint is always charged, unlike read-only-input auxiliary-work conventions. Hennie--Stearns two-tape acceleration and FLMV random-access exclusions cannot be substituted into the other models. Neither $P=NP$ nor $P\ne NP$ follows.
