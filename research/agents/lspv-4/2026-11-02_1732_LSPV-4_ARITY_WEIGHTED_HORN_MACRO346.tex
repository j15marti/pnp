\section*{Scope and existing duplication}
The target is general encoded CNF-SAT and a fixed, sound, complete, effectively checkable mixed calculus $R$. Let
\[
W_R(F)=\min_{\pi:F\vdash_R\bot}\sum_{v\in\pi}\omega(v),\qquad
\overline W_R(N)=\max_{\substack{F\ {\rm valid\ UNSAT}\\|\operatorname{enc}(F)|\le N}}W_R(F).
\]
The optimal derivation may be selected freely after the fact. No optimizer-search charge is assigned to this lower-bound minimum. LSPV-2 DEF-SUPPORT-COVERAGE346 already proved the binary-premise unit-cost source floor and identified an unresolved source-bearing-factor DEF syntax. The contribution here is an explicitly weighted all-arity extension and a sound polynomially checkable macro that collapses the unweighted node count. No change to the preselected $R_{\rm mix341}$ is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[All-arity source-fan-in conservation]
Suppose every inference node $v$ explicitly names its $k_v$ distinct source or proof premises, with every relied-upon source input represented by a premise edge, and carries cost $\omega(v)\ge\max\{1,k_v-1\}$. In any sound proof calculus of this form, every clause-minimally UNSAT CNF with $m$ labelled source clauses has contradiction-DAG cost at least $m-1$, even with sharing, conservative fresh definitions, auxiliary variables, temporary growth and variable-arity macros.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Discard non-ancestors of contradiction. Because every source clause is indispensable and the calculus is sound, all $m$ sources must occur in the proof ancestry. If $q$ derived nodes are reached, the undirected dependency graph on $m+q$ vertices is connected. Thus $E=\sum_v k_v\ge m+q-1$, so
\[
\sum_v\omega(v)\ge \sum_v(k_v-1)=E-q\ge m-1.
\]
A semantic macro with undeclared source reads is not covered by this premise-edge theorem. A zero-premise conservative definition is permitted but has positive cost.
\end{proof}

\section*{Exact infinite test family and a valid one-step macro}
For every $m\ge2$ set
\[
H_m=(x_1)\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m-2}(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})\wedge(\neg x_{m-1}).
\]
Define a fixed comparative calculus $R_H$ with ordinary binary resolution and an optional Horn-UP macro. The macro references an explicit list of $k\ge2$ source/derived clause premises, includes a transcript of unit steps with source/pivot references, and infers $\bot$ only if replaying its transcript yields a falsified premise. This is sound, polynomial-time checkable, complete when combined with resolution, and not a free semantic SAT oracle. Its unweighted variant charges one node; the weighted variant charges $k-1$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact optima and independent sign sensitivity]
Every $H_m$ is clause-minimally UNSAT; its $L=2m-2$ separate occurrence signs are each independently sensitive. For the declared macro and resolution calculi,
\[
W_{R_H^{\mathrm{unit}}}(H_m)=1,\quad
W_{R_H^{\mathrm{weighted}}}(H_m)=W_{\mathrm{RES}}(H_m)=m-1.
\]
Under the strict fixed authenticated at-most-two-premise positive-unit-cost reading of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$, the same exact $m-1$ result holds, subject to the existing DEF-source-input syntax caveat.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Unit propagation on all $m$ clauses produces $x_1,\ldots,x_{m-1}$ and conflicts with $\neg x_{m-1}$; the checked one-node macro is valid. Resolution successively eliminates $x_i$ in exactly $m-1$ binary inferences. Deleting the first clause admits all-zero variables; deleting the last admits all ones; deleting internal clause $i$ admits $x_1=\cdots=x_i=1$ and $x_{i+1}=\cdots=x_{m-1}=0$. The omitted clause is the unique false clause, and all its literals are false. Flipping any of those occurrence signs makes that clause true, preserving all other clauses. Hence every clause is indispensable and every occurrence sign sensitive. The first theorem supplies the weighted $m-1$ lower bound, and the resolution path is the matching upper bound.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Physical input-bit consequence (model-specific).}
In a read-only encoding containing independently addressable occurrence-sign bits, every deterministic total correct all-CNF-SAT decider must read all $2m-2$ sign fields on $H_m$. Otherwise a single unobserved bit could be flipped to a SAT neighbor with an identical deterministic execution. This is $\Omega(m)$ source-bit probes, or $\lceil(2m-2)/w\rceil$ ordinary $w$-field probes, not an unrestricted exponential decision bound. It does not imply that arbitrary deciders emit Horn-UP refutations.
