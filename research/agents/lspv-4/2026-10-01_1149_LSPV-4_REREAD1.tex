\section*{LSPV-4 hostile audit: static reread versus causal pointer cascades}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. No unrestricted SAT lower bound is proved here.

\paragraph{CORPUS REVIEWED.} I surveyed the repository tree and read the governing protocol, root main, current tasks, newest verified MAIN and relevant historical synthesis surfaced by repository search, materially relevant LSPV-1--5 work on SU/vMS product span, acquisition, reconstruction and causal random challenges, the HEC/CRS--PPC/CCSPC branch, governance material, and cited result paths. In particular I checked HADCOEF1, ACQ1, DEPDEF1, TASK-001, TASK-004, TASK-005, the CRS--PPC theorem index, and CCSPC1. LSPV-3 DEPDEF1 is substantive, correctly headed/sentinelled, and readable from GitHub, so predecessor preflight passes.

\paragraph{Two blocking obstacles.} First, the source-sanctioned SU05 Hadamard branch has an exact coefficient map $\Psi$, but the actual vMS/SU09 invocation still lacks source-transcribed label-bit functions, so neither $\operatorname{rank}\Psi$ nor the ACQ1 verifier exponent is known. Second, HEC/CRS--PPC/CCSPC still lacks a static-to-online compiler: static full-input visibility permits reread, recomputation, pointer jumping and preprocessing unless a model-preserving theorem charges them.

\paragraph{Literature and barrier preflight.} SU05 supplies the algebraic extractor and permits Hadamard in the relevant binary-conversion regime, but does not force a unique vMS encoding. Bogdanov--Trevisan shows strong collapse consequences for generic nonadaptive NP self-correction, so LWE-style extraction is not a free import. Aaronson--Wigderson shows that many arithmetization techniques algebrize. Recent syzygy cryptanalysis reinforces the algorithmic warning that hidden code structure may be exploitable rather than hard.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: static reread simulation.} Let a fixed static input be an array $X$ of $N$ cells of $w$ bits with unit-cost random access. Let a length-$T$ deterministic cascade satisfy
\[
a_0=A_0(x),\qquad y_t=X[a_t],\qquad a_{t+1}=F_t(x,a_t,y_t),
\]
where $F_t$ runs in time $c_t$ and workspace $s_t$. A static RAM evaluates the exact cascade in
\[
\boxed{O(T+\sum_{t<T}c_t)}
\]
time and
\[
\boxed{O(w+\log N+\max_t s_t)}
\]
extra workspace, apart from read-only input and output. Store only $t,a_t,y_t$; probe $X[a_t]$, compute $F_t$, discard $y_t$, and iterate. Induction gives the reference cascade. Thus causal dependence alone cannot imply an $\Omega(Tw)$ persistent-state requirement in this model.

\paragraph{Fixed-transcript corollary.} If a CRS/CCSPC transcript $G$ is fixed into a static instance and each stage challenge is explicitly addressable by the recurrence above, future freshness is no longer an information-theoretic storage obligation. Sequential work may remain, but a superpolynomial SAT lower bound needs a separate theorem against pointer jumping, shortcut representations, memoization and alternate algorithms. Fixing a positive-probability hard transcript is not resource preserving by itself.

\paragraph{Hostile controls.} DEAD/COPY/DELAY inflate a chosen implementation only. INTERP/RECOMP instantiate the simulation. Memoization can improve repeated-address cases. Group/affine cascades may compress further by composition. Horn, 2-SAT, bounded-treewidth and Gaussian/Tseitin controls show that long syntactic dependency chains can have cheap global methods. A semantic oracle bypasses SAT semantics; an algebraic oracle can bypass algebraic transitions. Stagewise nondeterminism can wait for the current challenge and guess its witness. If $X$ is succinct and random access itself is hard, that access cost must be charged; confusing an explicit exponential array with a succinct input risks changing the problem toward succinct/NEXP complexity rather than ordinary SAT.

\paragraph{CRS--PPC/CCSPC status.} Fresh-block stopping-time lemmas and no-reread state bounds remain \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in their declared online models. Importing them to static SAT is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} without a compiler preventing or charging the simulation above. Schedule externalization, fixed-transcript resource preservation, and static-to-CRS--PPC compilation remain \textbf{OPEN}. CCSPC pointer causality is a \textbf{CANDIDATE} sequential-work source, not a static-memory lower bound.

\paragraph{PQ toolkit.} Schur squares, puncturing, fixed-code quadratic kernels, Hilbert functions and syzygies are \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFERS} on a declared Boolean code. Shortening/syzygy filtrations as succinct separation algorithms, LWE-style black-box extraction, and special-soundness collision extraction are \textbf{CANDIDATE}. Cryptographic hardness, random oracles, VLSI/distributed locality, control potentials and proof-system lower bounds are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} to unrestricted RAM SAT absent a model-preserving bridge.

\paragraph{SU/vMS, ACQ1, reconstruction.} HADCOEF1 survives: for the declared Hadamard realization $K_2(H)=\ker\Psi$ and $\rho(H)=\operatorname{rank}\Psi$, but the actual vMS label functions remain open. ACQ1 survives: once legality and $B_vq$ are polynomial-time decidable, a violating legal state is NP search and its SAT-decider cost must be charged. DEPDEF1's dependency-uniformization obstruction remains open. None of these steps is nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing.

\paragraph{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.} The corpus already records that a late permutation is defeated by one static reread and cyclic/affine histories compress. The new theorem generalizes those bypasses to every explicit deterministic pointer recurrence of the stated form. It does not contradict HEC's no-reread theorem because the models differ exactly at reread access.

\paragraph{STALE OR OVERSTATED MAIN CLAIMS.} Root main's LSPV-4 region is stale: it points to a 2026-09-30 report and omits ACQ1, the Hadamard coefficient theorem, the causal static-reread bypass, and newer reconstruction dependency work. Executive OPEN status is correct.

\paragraph{ADOPTED / REJECTED / RETIRED.} Adopted: source-sanctioned Hadamard branch, coefficient-kernel theorem, charged ACQ1 separation, DEPDEF1 uniformization, and causal lemmas only in their restricted models. Rejected as insufficient: proof multiplicity, free acquisition, fixed-transcript freshness, generic cryptographic binding and physical locality. Retired: static persistent-storage lower bounds based solely on explicit deterministic causal pointer dependence; late-permutation storage; cyclic/affine accumulation.

\paragraph{COMPONENTS CONSISTENT WITH OR SUGGESTIVE OF $P=NP$ WITHOUT CLAIMING IT.} Static reread, memoization, group compression, stagewise nondeterministic witness choice, and NP-search acquisition under an assumed SAT decider are legal bypass behaviors compatible with a $P=NP$ world. They are not evidence that $P=NP$.

\paragraph{Strict status.} \textbf{PROVED/EXACT:} explicit static pointer-cascade reread simulation and workspace bound. \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} SU05 extractor/binary conversion; Bogdanov--Trevisan barrier; Aaronson--Wigderson algebrization barrier. \textbf{CONDITIONAL:} ACQ1 compilation exponent after evaluator transcription. \textbf{CANDIDATE:} succinct syzygy acquisition, collision extraction, CCSPC sequential work. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED:} static storage lower bounds from causal explicit pointer dependence alone; uncharged fixed-transcript freshness; physical/cryptographic runtime transfers. \textbf{OPEN:} exact SU09/vMS Hadamard trace; rank of $\Psi$; ACQ1 evaluator exponent; sparse-$Q$ normalization; reconstruction uniformizer/exponent ledger; arbitrary-algorithm transfer; CRS/CCSPC static compiler; nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing hinge; final diagonal.

\paragraph{Next falsification target.} For CCSPC/CRS, strengthen the resource from persistent storage to a sequential-access quantity that static reread cannot erase, then attack the proposed staticization with pointer doubling, memoization and group compression. In parallel, source-transcribe the exact SU/vMS label-bit evaluator needed to instantiate ACQ1.
