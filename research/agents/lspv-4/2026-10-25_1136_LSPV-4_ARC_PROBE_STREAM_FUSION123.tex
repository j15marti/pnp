\section{Primary theorem: a charged selector-to-scoped-stream bridge}
\begin{theorem}[Probe-separated normalizer and frontier consumer]
Fix any ONE globally correct total deterministic ordinary-SAT RTM $A$
with joint pair $(t,s)\in E$. On each source $F$ of ordinary encoded
length $N$, suppose a fixed total machine $G[A]$, allowed to inspect
the finite source code of $A$, produces online a syntactically valid,
closed \texttt{OPEN/CLAUSE/CLOSE} CNF event stream satisfying
\[
  \SAT(F)\iff\SAT(\operatorname{stream}(G[A](F))).
\]
The output has $M\le N^{\mu+o(1)}$ complete serialized bits and named
live width $w\le\delta\log_2 N+o(\log N)$.
The \emph{complete} policy, selection, verification, rejected candidates,
failed fallback, source and workspace traffic, control, arithmetic,
header/body emission and compatibility joins, excluding separately
accounted guest calls and their input service, cost at most
$N^{r+o(1)}$ time, $N^{\sigma+o(1)}$ retained writable/index space.
It makes at most $N^{b+o(1)}$ \emph{sequential} guest calls on
\emph{virtual ordinary SAT} inputs, each of declared actual length
$L\le N^{\rho+o(1)}$. A virtual input-bit service costs at most
$N^{g+o(1)}$ time and $N^{\eta+o(1)}$ simultaneous workspace,
inclusive of source/workspace reads/writes, unchanged writes and
index/control operations. Suppose, \emph{in addition to} $(t,s)$,
a valid $L^{q+o(1)}$ bound is proved on ALL actual guest input probes.
Here $q\le t$ can always be conservatively taken equal to $t$.

For fully lazy access, ONE fixed total exact ordinary-SAT machine
$D[A,G]$ achieves the simultaneous joint exponent envelope
\[
\boxed{\quad
 t_{\rm lazy}'\le\max\{r,\mu+\delta,\ b+\rho t,\ b+\rho q+g\},\qquad
 s_{\rm lazy}'\le\max\{\sigma,\delta,\rho s,\eta\}.
 \quad} \tag{1}
\]
For sequential eager guest-input materialization, the corresponding
valid envelope is
\[
\boxed{\quad
 t_{\rm eager}'\le\max\{r,\mu+\delta,\ b+\rho+g,\ b+\rho t\},\qquad
 s_{\rm eager}'\le\max\{\sigma,\delta,\rho,\rho s,\eta\}.
 \quad} \tag{2}
\]
If another phase retains an ancestor, stream, cache, certificate or
workspace of exponent $\kappa$, add $\kappa$ to the \emph{appropriate
simultaneous} space maximum; if it executes extra selector calls or a
root-scale $A(F)$ fallback, add their time and space terms.
These are conditional upper \emph{machine-simulation} theorems,
not established all-input semantic normalizers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A fixed simulation of $G[A]$ pauses it after each output bit. The
consumer parses online and maintains all extendible assignments to
the currently live, explicitly named variables. \texttt{OPEN}
branches a row, \texttt{CLAUSE} deletes violating rows, and
\texttt{CLOSE} existentially projects. The scope contract proves
both-label correctness by induction. A named row has
$2^{w+o(w)}$ entries and its update costs
$2^w\operatorname{polylog}N$ elementary operations on the matched
indexed RTM. The producer's write-only, one-way output is \emph{intercepted
at issuance}; it is neither reused as an input tape nor counted as a
free readable buffer. Full streams never need be stored.

Within each guest call, ordinary guest steps total
$L^{t+o(1)}$. Each of at most $L^{q+o(1)}$ input probes is replaced by
a complete virtual-bit service. Thus the \emph{separate} guest
time terms are $N^{b+\rho t+o(1)}$ and
$N^{b+\rho q+g+o(1)}$, not necessarily
$N^{b+\rho t+g+o(1)}$. The guest writable/index tape, producer
state, bit generator and DP table coexist when called, costing at
most the sum of $N^\sigma$, $N^{\rho s}$, $N^\eta$ and $N^\delta$,
whose exponent is their maximum. The input-source tape remains
read-only; all accesses cost time.

In eager mode emit every guest input bit, including unchanged bits,
into an actually readable local buffer of length $L$ before running
$A$. Generation costs $N^{b+\rho+g+o(1)}$ time; the buffer contributes
$N^\rho$ peak counted space, in addition to the caller/guest state.
Each term refers to this same fixed machine, with no free
maximizing selector, different-machine coordinate borrowing, or
output-as-workspace assumption.
\end{proof}

\section{Two exact virtual recursion levels}

charged successor interface with constant parameters $b,\rho,g,r,\mu,
\delta,\sigma,\eta$ at \emph{every current ordinary source size}.
Set $c=\max\{r,\mu+\delta\}$, $z=\max\{\sigma,\eta,\delta\}$.
Then the lazy exponent \emph{upper certificates} obey
\[
  f(t)=\max\{c,b+\rho t+g\},\qquad
  h(s)=\max\{z,\rho s\}.
\]
Two genuine invocations of the \emph{same fixed compiler}, including
all guest source-access taxes, yield
\[
\boxed{\ f(f(t))=
\max\{c,b+\rho c+g,\ b(1+\rho)+\rho^2t+(1+\rho)g\},\quad
h(h(s))=\max\{z,\rho^2s\}. \ } \tag{3}

The $g+\rho g$ in the last time branch cannot be deleted.

fixed route depth. In particular, at the \emph{purely conditional}

\section{All-input parent-scale SAT decision hidden behind two short children}
\begin{lemma}[Noncontractive exact quotient, a constructive firewall]
For any fixed total exact ordinary-SAT decider $A$, there is a
fixed uniform machine $Q_A$ which, for EVERY ordinary formula $F$
of length $N$, first runs $A(F)$ and thereafter emits an ordinary
proper 3CNF $Y$ of length $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ satisfying
$\SAT(Y)=\SAT(F)$. Running the same $Q_A$ on $Y$
emits a second proper ordinary 3CNF $Z$ of length
$N^{1/4+o(1)}$, preserving the same answer.
Despite both genuine ordinary power shortenings and both-label
heredity, the specified $Q_A$ \emph{performs the full original
root-scale $A(F)$ call} and therefore does not prove any improvement
over $A$'s worst-case joint exponents.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $C_1=(x\lor y\lor z)$, a satisfiable proper ternary core.
Let $C_0$ be the conjunction of its eight signed versions, one
falsified by each assignment to $(x,y,z)$; $C_0$ is UNSAT.
After $A(F)$ returns bit $u$, take $C_u$ and append $k$
independent positive ternary clauses on fresh triples, where
$k=\max\{8,\lceil\sqrt{N}/(1+\log_2N)\rceil\}$.
The complete ordinary encoding, including self-delimiting
headers, names, signs and records, has length
$\Theta(k\log(k+2))=N^{1/2+o(1)}$.
Fresh clauses are jointly satisfiable and independent
of $C_u$, so both labels are preserved. The next call to
$Q_A$ has source length $|Y|$ and yields
$|Z|=|Y|^{1/2+o(1)}=N^{1/4+o(1)}$.
One may also emit a width-one stream
$\mathrm{OPEN}(x)$, $\mathrm{CLAUSE}(x)$,
optionally $\mathrm{CLAUSE}(\neg x)$, $\mathrm{CLOSE}(x)$
according to the root SAT bit. A frontier DP decides this
stream in constant size \emph{after} the expensive root
decision. The eager $Y$ buffer, headers, source reads and
every $A$ execution are charged. In particular the
root $A(F)$ call gives $t'\ge t$ as a bound for this
declared construction when $t$ denotes the resource
envelope charged for that call; it does not prove that
any alternate faster SAT algorithm is impossible.
\end{proof}
This lemma expressly tests all-input ordinary SAT, not
only a low-width or affine promise. Its \emph{output structure}
is efficiently directly recognizable. No hard-image
compression or lower bound follows.
