\section{Primary literature and exact target language}
Let $\Ctwo$ be the set of nonempty proper 3-CNFs with unique clauses, dense
active variable identifiers, and exactly two positive and two negative
occurrences of every active variable.  Clauses may mix signs.  Fix a
self-delimiting canonical binary encoding and define the total language
\[
 \Ltwo=\{\operatorname{enc}(G):G\in\Ctwo\text{ and }G\text{ is satisfiable}\},
 \tag{1}
\]
rejecting malformed and out-of-domain strings after a polynomial syntactic
check.

D\"ocker's primary theorem states NP-completeness for the narrower problem in
which each clause is sign-monochromatic, proper and unique and every variable
appears twice with each sign \cite{Docker}.  This is a literal subset of
$\Ctwo$, not the all-positive family previously retired in
CLEANCORE-DEFINITION-SEAM1.  Hence:

\begin{proposition}[Fixed-language completeness]\label{prop:l22}
$\Ltwo$ is NP-complete under polynomial many-one reductions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Membership in NP uses the assignment as witness after the syntactic check.
For hardness, retain the encoding of D\"ocker's sign-monochromatic instances.
They satisfy every condition defining $\Ctwo$, and satisfiability is unchanged.
Thus the identity-on-formulas map reduces the published NP-complete language
to (1).
\end{proof}

Cook's official problem description records the standard consequence: if an
NP-complete language lies in P, then $P=NP$ \cite{Cook}.  Proposition
\ref{prop:l22} is therefore a complete fixed-language formulation of the
project objective.  It does not prove the objective.

\section{Compiler-image language with total off-image semantics}
Let $\mathcal S$ be the nonempty proper distinct-clause 3-CNFs with dense IDs.
Let $U$ be the deterministic compiler in PC22\_ALLFIELD1: transparent
seven-clause balancing followed by the clone-cycle/$Q$ occurrence compiler.
For $F\in\mathcal S$ with $m$ clauses and $g$ balancing gadgets, its output is
in $\Ctwo$ and has
\[
 v(U(F))=9(m+7g),\qquad M(U(F))=12(m+7g),\qquad g\le9m,
 \tag{2}
\]
with $\SAT(U(F))=\SAT(F)$.

Define the total canonical image language
\[
 K_U=\{y:\exists F\in\mathcal S\ [y=\operatorname{enc}(U(F))
       \ \wedge\ F\in\SAT]\}.                                  \tag{3}
\]
Every string not having such a preimage is a NO instance.  This definition
does not assume that image recognition is in P and does not ask an arbitrary
decider to reveal a preimage.

\begin{theorem}[Compiler-image completeness]\label{thm:image}
$K_U$ is NP-complete.  Every YES instance has a polynomial-length certificate
consisting of a source preimage and a satisfying source assignment.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For membership, the verifier checks that $F\in\mathcal S$, computes $U(F)$,
compares the complete canonical output with $y$, and verifies the assignment.
If $y=\operatorname{enc}(U(F))$, then (2) gives $M(U(F))\ge12m$ and the dense
source has at most $3m$ active variables.  Under the declared indexed
encoding,
\[
 |\operatorname{enc}(F)|=O(m\log(m+2))
 =O(|y|\log(|y|+2)),                                      \tag{4}
\]
so the preimage and assignment are polynomial in $|y|$.

For hardness, start with D\"ocker's NP-complete monotone exact-$(2,2)$
language, whose formulas lie in $\mathcal S$, and map
$F\mapsto\operatorname{enc}(U(F))$.  The compiler is uniform polynomial time,
and its semantic equivalence proves
$F\in\SAT\Longleftrightarrow U(F)\in\SAT$.  This is a many-one reduction to
(3).
\end{proof}

Because independent red-team review of the complete proof above is pending,

\CAND{} in the living synthesis.  Proposition~\ref{prop:l22}'s underlying
NP-completeness is a \KNOWN{} plus an exact subset corollary.

\section{Weakest useful arbitrary-computation transfer}
The compiler acts externally.  It does not inspect the target algorithm and
does not demand proof extraction.  Let $A$ be any total deterministic decider
of $\Ltwo$ satisfying
\[
 T_A(N)\le c_A N^{d_A}                                   \tag{5}
\]
for fixed but arbitrary $c_A,d_A$.  If source encoding length is $s$, the
reported implementation constructs and writes $U(F)$ in $s^{O(1)}$ bit time
and its output has length $s^{O(1)}$.  More explicitly, if construction time
is $O(s^a)$ and output length $O(s^q)$, then
\[
 T_{A\circ U}(s)
 \le O(s^a)+c_A O(s^{q d_A})
 =s^{O(\max\{a,q d_A\})}.                                \tag{6}
\]
Thus every fixed polynomial exponent remains polynomial and is fully charged;
it is not erased.  The same composition holds for a total decider of $K_U$.

Equation (6) is the exact general-computation arrow supplied by the
construction:
\[
  \Ltwo\in P\Longrightarrow\text{D\"ocker's language}\in P
  \Longrightarrow P=NP.                                  \tag{7}
\]
It advances the input-reduction/interface arrow only.  It supplies neither
\[
 \text{arbitrary target execution}\Longrightarrow
 \text{short PC/PCR or Resolution proof},                 \tag{8}
\]
nor an unconditional superpolynomial endpoint in the ordinary-machine model.
The missing implication remains exactly
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}(\Ltwo)\ \forall d,C,N_0\ \exists x:
 |x|\ge N_0\ \land\ T_A(x)>C|x|^d.                       \tag{9}
\]
Equation (9) is the desired separation, not a subsidiary compiler lemma.

\section{Falsification first and hostile controls}
The strongest possible overreading of Theorem~\ref{thm:image} is false:
NP-completeness of an image language does not make its particular proof-hard
NO benchmarks computationally hard.  PC22's graph-colouring images are hard
for expanded-monomial PC/PCR over every field, while a representation-aware
algorithm may use compact arithmetic, counting, flow, decoding, or other
preprocessing.  The 00:10 cardinality artifact gives a concrete counting/flow
escape on its own benchmark.  PCRETRACT1 gives a low-degree $\mathbb F_2$
escape for the different compiled Tseitin family.  These results coexist
because proof system, source, and representation differ.

Horn, properized 2SAT, affine/Tseitin, bounded-instance exhaustive checks,
independent products, padding, byte-level target mutation, and
COPY/DEAD/DELAY/RECOMP exponent ledgers were included in the audit.  Renaming
or clause reordering changes the chosen canonical byte string but does not
change Proposition~\ref{prop:l22}; $K_U$ deliberately uses exact canonical
image equality.  A decider of $K_U$ may recognize images, decode them, or use a
completely different representation.  No preimage is forced into its state.

\section{Barrier and cross-domain audit}
The syntactic composition (6) continues to work if the same source and target
machines receive an oracle; however, ordinary NP-completeness of a fixed
unrelativized language does not make it complete for every $NP^O$.  Baker,
Gill and Solovay construct oracles on both sides of the relativized question
\cite{BGS}.  Hence (7) supplies no nonrelativizing hinge.  Low-degree extension
access likewise does not invalidate the input map, and no nonalgebrizing step
appears; Aaronson--Wigderson's barrier remains applicable to any proposed
general separation route \cite{AW}.  No large constructive circuit property
is defined, so Natural Proofs is not invoked.

PQC-inspired black-box extraction, collision binding, Schur squares,
quadratic-relation spaces, syzygies, VLSI routing, and communication cuts are
\textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} here: they do not strengthen a Karp reduction into
an unrestricted time lower bound.  The exact theorem transfer is simply
standard many-one composition.  The LWE-style external-reduction analogy adds
nothing beyond (6) unless it reaches a genuinely different unconditional
ordinary-machine endpoint.
