\section*{LSPV-4 hostile audit: size-normalization coefficient barrier}
\textbf{STATUS: OPEN.} P versus NP remains open. No positive evidence for $P=NP$ is claimed.

\textbf{State reviewed.} Root \texttt{main.tex}; historical MAIN 54501-R1; LSPV-1 1707; LSPV-2 1723; LSPV-3 42301-R1; LSPV-4 104601; GROK BOOL v2.8; CLAUDE-0266. Primary barrier/control literature rechecked: Baker--Gill--Solovay (1975), Schaefer (1978), Aaronson--Wigderson (2008).

\textbf{New hostile result: size normalization is quantitatively stronger than sparse-$Q$.}
Fix generator parameter $m$ and range $H(z,m)$. Suppose an architecture-specific theorem eventually gives
\[
\rho(E_{H(z,m)})\le c m.
\]
Singleton yields an annihilating quadratic with ANF weight
\[
w(Q_m)\le \rho(E_H)+1\le cm+1.
\]
Let $S(Q)$ be actual circuit size in the target convention. If compiling a $w$-monomial quadratic has only the certified upper bound
\[
S(Q)\le a w+b,
\]
then the proved consequence is merely
\[
S(Q_m)\le acm+(a+b).
\]
This does not imply $S(Q_m)\le m$ unless the coefficient inequality $ac<1$ with finite-size slack is separately proved. Big-$O$ sparsity is insufficient for same-size normalization.

Padding cannot repair the bad direction. DEAD gates can increase size $s<m$ to $m$, but cannot turn $s>m$ into size $m$. Replacing the generator parameter by $m'=S(Q_m)$ changes the range to $H(z,m')$; annihilation of $H(z,m)$ gives no generic annihilation of $H(z,m')$. Hence
\[
\rho=O(m)\Rightarrow O(m)\hbox{-monomial }Q
\]
may hold while
\[
\rho=O(m)\Rightarrow \exists Q\in K_2(H(z,m))\text{ with }S(Q)\le m
\]
remains OPEN.

\textbf{Countermodel to the inference, not to the construction.}
If the only established constants permit $\rho(E_H)=m$ and the adopted fan-in-2 realization charges at least two gates per active nonconstant monomial, Singleton certifies only $w\le m+1$, compatible with $S(Q)>m$. This refutes deduction of same-size normalization from the available asymptotic inequalities. It is not a lower bound on the canonical SU/vMS annihilator.

\textbf{Weakest repair.}
Either prove
\[
\rho(E_H)\le\gamma m-O(1),\qquad a\gamma<1,
\]
with an exact compiler and size convention; directly prove a $Q_m\in K_2(H(z,m))$ with $S(Q_m)\le m$; or prove reconstruction tolerates a constant-factor larger target without changing the range being annihilated. Until then common-$Q$ size normalization is an independent OPEN arrow.
