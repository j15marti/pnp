\begin{proof}

The two forward gate clauses permit derivation of the positive clause-output literal from its original source clause with exactly $2(w-1)$ Resolution steps, by replacing two input literals with their OR output. Join all $m$ positive clause outputs with $m-1$ AND definitions. Two Resolution steps per AND using its backward definition clause derive its positive output. The root is therefore established as one by $L-1$ paid gate definitions and $2(L-1)$ Resolution nodes. CoNP-hardness follows by the uniform linear-gate compilation of arbitrary CNF UNSAT into the question whether its output circuit is identically zero. Malformed records are rejected separately.
\end{proof}
