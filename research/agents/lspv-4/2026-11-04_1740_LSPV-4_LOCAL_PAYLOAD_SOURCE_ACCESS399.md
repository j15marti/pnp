## ITERATE: precise conditional lemma
**Declared test grammar R_chain, not the normative R_mix341 grammar.** Original source is satisfiable unit CNF F=(x). A single authenticated LOCAL-CONVERT inference has premise x and exports one circuit condition C(x), with *unpaid* internal AND gates. Original support is the one variable x. The gates are g_0=x and g_i=AND(g_(i-1),ell_i), where ell_i is either x or NOT x. The inference is legal iff x logically implies its sole output g_m, checked over both x values. Its complete payload includes every gate's signed second operand and its predecessor index; the statement has one external output.

**Lemma (PROVED/EXACT for R_chain).** Let ell_i=x at all m gates. Then x=>g_m is valid, the original source has constant length, and the inference has q=1 paid node under this explicitly permissive grammar. Negating *any one* ell_j yields g_m=0, invalidating x=>g_m. Consequently, any exact deterministic checker accepting the valid supplied certificate must read all m signed operand fields. For a two-sided-error randomized checker with error <=epsilon<1/2, the expected number of distinct fields read is at least (1-2epsilon)m. Proof: a checker skipping any field on the valid chain sees an identical transcript on the one-field mutation, incorrectly accepting it. For randomized checking couple the coins and sum the per-field probabilities.

**CRITICAL native qualification.** The literal R_mix341 definition says LOCAL-CONVERT may derive a "bounded-support Boolean circuit condition" with an explicit constant-support truth-table check but does not fully state the internal gate serialization, paid-gate policy, exported-wire lifetime or whether signed literal operands are permitted at that interface. Thus this is NOT certified as a *legal native* counterexample. If each AND gate must be separately introduced as paid DEF, q is Omega(m) and the gap disappears. Additionally, the valid long chain is equivalent to x, so a short normalized proof exists. No lower bound on W_R, no B3 refutation and no proof of P!=NP follows.

## Separate B3 falsifier (KNOWN THEOREM consequence, not native counterexample)
Fix oracle O=TQBF. Since TQBF is PSPACE-complete and NPSPACE=PSPACE, P^O=NP^O=PSPACE, so SAT is in P^O. Ordinary **unrelativized** Resolution nevertheless requires 2^{Omega(h)} lines for explicit unary PHP(h+1,h) for every sufficiently large h (Haken 1985), of signed-input length Theta(h^3 log h). Hence the *relativized analogue* "SAT in P^O => polynomial Wbar_Resolution" is FALSE. Oracle access belongs to the algorithm, not to the proof grammar. This bars a generic relativizing derivation from polynomial SAT decisions to polynomial proofs in arbitrary fixed weak systems. It does NOT falsify unrelativized B3 or B3 for stronger R_mix341. Cook 1976 polynomial Extended Resolution PHP proofs and short GF(2) Tseitin contradictions remain the stronger-system controls.
