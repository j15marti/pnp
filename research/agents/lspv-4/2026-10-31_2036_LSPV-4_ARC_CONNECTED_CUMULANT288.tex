\section*{Exact restricted theorem}
For a uniform independent fair-bit full assignment $a$, define $I_j(a)$ as the indicator that original clause $C_j$ has zero true literals, and $X=N_0(a)=\sum_j I_j$. Put $p=1/8$. Build the source-overlap graph $G$ on original clauses, joining $i,j$ exactly if they share a source variable. Let $E$ denote its edges and $\mathcal T$ its connected unordered distinct triples. For any original-clause set $J$, $p_J=\Pr[\bigwedge_{j\in J}I_j=1]$ is zero when falsifying variable demands conflict and otherwise equals $2^{-|\cup_{j\in J}\operatorname{var}(C_j)|}$. Define $B=\sum_{ij\in E}(p_{ij}-p^2)$.
\begin{theorem}
If $n$ is the source variable count, $m=4n/3$ and canonical indexed source length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$, then
\[

For any fixed positive integer $r$, the same bounds hold for the $r$th unconditional moment, with constants depending on $r$. These programs do NOT solve SAT.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.}
Every clause has three distinct variables, each occurring in four source clauses, so the overlap graph has maximum degree nine. Summing degrees bounds edges; every connected triple has a center adjacent to its two companions, yielding $m\binom92$. The joint cumulant of clause indicators from independent disjoint variable supports vanishes. In the ordered triple expansion of $\kappa_3(\sum I_j)$, repeated $iii$ positions contribute $m p(1-p)(1-2p)=21m/256$, two-equal $iij$ arrangements contribute $6(1-2p)(p_{ij}-p^2)=(9/2)(p_{ij}-p^2)$ per overlapping pair, and three-distinct connected positions contribute six times their three-variable cumulant. The raw moment follows from standard cumulant identities.

A fixed-width indexed source parser checks length, signed occurrence quotas, duplicate clauses and proper monotonicity through repeated authenticated source scans with only logarithmic counters. Enumerate pairs $i<j$ and, only for overlapping pairs, scan possible third indices $k$. Keep three original records, check whether $k$ overlaps $i$ or $j$, and charge the connected triple only to the smallest of its overlapping edges. There are $O(m^2)$ source-record queries because $|E|=O(m)$. Every indexed address construction, index-head restoration and atomic erasure, source-bit read, failed comparison and counter update costs at most a polylogarithmic factor; peak simultaneous tape and index data are $O(\log N)$ bits for the declared fixed-width representation. More generally, fixed-$r$ cumulants sum only connected support tuples of size at most $r$: bounded graph degree gives $O(m)$ supports for constant $r$, a charged scan finds each next neighbor, and partition formulas use $O(\log N)$ space. The fixed-Hamming-weight distribution is EXCLUDED because disjoint supports become dependent. \hfill$\square$
