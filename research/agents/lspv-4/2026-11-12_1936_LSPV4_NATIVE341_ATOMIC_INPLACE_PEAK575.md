The /574 **fresh-before-parent-release** model allocated a third result before freeing parents and gave
$$
P_{\rm fresh}^*=\max(R+2F,F+2R,3R),\qquad
A^*=2FV+(5V-4)R-\max(0,2R-3F).
$$

Change only the instantaneous output policy: after source and paid inputs have been read, the output may atomically replace a paid parent **at its last use**, including when the new value is larger. An unoccupied register may receive an output. Define the *in-place payload peak* as the maximum sum of the occupied results' nominal F/R bytes immediately before or after every atomic transition. This ideal operation assumes a resizable value register and excludes temporary copies, conversion scratch, machine head/index accesses, original JSON, input/packet buffers, and runtime heap allocation. It is NOT a physical-memory theorem.

**Theorem 575.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED).** For every fixed original /341 tree template with `V>=3`, positive fixed packed sizes F and R, and the above two-register atomic in-place machine:
$$
\boxed{P_{\rm inplace}^*=\max(F+R,2R).}
$$
Furthermore, **the same chronological paid execution** can jointly attain this minimum peak and the /574 minimum retained byte-step area
$$
\boxed{A_{\rm inplace}^*=A^*=2FV+(5V-4)R-\max(0,2R-3F).}
$$

**Proof (lower bound).** Consider the first XOR, which must combine the completed first two local vertex rows. Immediately before this XOR, two R-weighted rows coexist, so the payload peak is at least `2R`. During construction of those two completed rows, one unfinished F-weighted local factor and one completed R-weighted row must coexist at some transition: either the first row is completed before the other local chain starts, or two unfinished local chains coexist and one becomes a row first. Thus `F+R` is unavoidable. No paid recomputation or alternative rule is available *in this fixed-tree theorem*.

**Proof (matching upper bound and joint area).** For `2R<=3F`, use the depth-first /574 area-optimal schedule: complete a vertex chain to R, compute the next vertex chain using the other register, XOR the two rows in place, and iterate. Live payload is only a single F/R, one F plus one R, or two R values; its peak is `max(F+R,2R)`. For `2R>3F`, /574's area-optimal prefix interleaves the first two chains' first two F-values before finishing their R outputs. During that prefix two F-values can coexist, but `2F<F+R<2R` follows from `R>3F/2`. Thus the peak is again `2R`. The remainder is the same one-accumulator depth-first schedule. Every operation either fills an empty register or overwrites a dead paid parent (last consumer), so no extra result slot is required. The retention area is exactly the previously proved optimum under the SAME representation, and the constructed schedule attains both bounds. QED.
