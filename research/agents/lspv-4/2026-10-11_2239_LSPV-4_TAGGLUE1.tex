\begin{abstract}
TESTS-0015 retires the semantic use of LB-0019's disjoint satisfiable tags
and proposes actual gluing clauses as the smallest repair.  This report builds
that repair.  A proper, duplicate-free, bounded-occurrence 3CNF enforces every
binary parent tag as the AND of its children and pins supplied leaf/tag values.
The result has a trilemma: disjoint tags erase; consistent promised tags expose
the answer at the root; arbitrary pinned tags reduce to local consistency plus
a true root, decidable by one linear pass and logarithmic address space.
Thus gluing repairs semantic coupling but does not create a compatibility,
memory, communication, or superpolynomial-time lower bound.  A bounded audit
performed 610,828 checks with zero failures.  The arbitrary-decider transfer
and P versus NP remain \OPEN.
\end{abstract}

\section{Primary-literature and transfer preflight}

The obstacles are the semantic gap in the original tags and the absent
arbitrary-computation transfer.  Tseitin's definitional transformation is a
direct theorem type: a fixed Boolean gate is represented by constantly many
CNF clauses with extension variables \cite{Tseitin}.  It supplies coupling,
not a lower bound.  Buss places Boolean formula value in ALOGTIME

logDCFL and prove large branching-program bounds only under declared

restricted bounds do not transfer to unrestricted SAT machines.

Baker--Gill--Solovay and Aaronson--Wigderson remain direct barrier theorems
for relativizing and algebrizing strategies \cite{BGS,AW}.  VLSI routing and
distributed cuts are \CAND{} analogies only because physical locality is
absent from this model.

\section{Explicit proper 3CNF repair}

For children \(x,y\), parent \(z\), and fresh padding \(p,q\), define
\[
\Gamma_{\wedge}=
(\neg x\vee\neg y\vee z)
\wedge(x\vee\neg z\vee p)\wedge(x\vee\neg z\vee\neg p)
\wedge(y\vee\neg z\vee q)\wedge(y\vee\neg z\vee\neg q).
\tag{1}
\]
For literal \(\ell\) and fresh \(a,b\), let
\[
\Pi(\ell)=
(\ell\vee a\vee b)\wedge(\ell\vee a\vee\neg b)
\wedge(\ell\vee\neg a\vee b)\wedge(\ell\vee\neg a\vee\neg b).
\tag{2}
\]
Every clause has three distinct variables, clauses are duplicate-free, and
occurrence is bounded by a constant.  Direct truth tables give
\[
\exists p,q\;\Gamma_{\wedge}\Longleftrightarrow z=x\wedge y,
\qquad
\exists a,b\;\Pi(\ell)\Longleftrightarrow \ell=1.
\tag{3}
\]

Fix a complete binary tree with \(L=2^d\) leaf bits \(s\) and \(I=L-1\)
supplied internal tags \(\tau\).  Pin every leaf and tag to its supplied value,
add (1) at every node, and assert the root true using (2).  Call the proper
3CNF \(H_d(s,\tau)\).  Its variables, clauses, and encoded characters are
linear in \(L+I\), up to ordinary identifiers.  This concretely realizes
TAG-GLUING-GADGET1, though not exact Monotone 3SAT-\((2,2)\).

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{TAG-GLUING-SEMANTICS1}]
\EXACT, independent review pending.  For every \(d,s,\tau\),
\[
H_d(s,\tau)\in\SAT
\Longleftrightarrow
\tau=\operatorname{Eval}_{\wedge}(s)
\ \land\ \bigwedge_{i=1}^{L}s_i=1.
\tag{4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each pin forces its advertised bit.  Equation (3) forces each internal tag to
be the AND of its children.  Bottom-up induction makes the tag vector unique
and the root equal \(\bigwedge_i s_i\); the root assertion proves necessity.
Conversely, under the displayed conditions every local gadget has padding
witnesses, so all clauses are simultaneously satisfiable.
\end{proof}

\section{The gluing trilemma}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{TAG-GLUING-TRILEMMA1}]
\EXACT, independent review pending.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Variable-disjoint satisfiable tags erase, as in TESTS-0015.
\item If \(\tau=\operatorname{Eval}_{\wedge}(s)\) is promised and the root is
locally addressable, one root-tag probe decides \(\bigwedge_i s_i\).
\item With arbitrary pinned \(s,\tau\), a flat pass checks every local
equation and the root in \(O(L+I)=O(N)\) time, one Boolean accumulator,
\(O(\log N)\) address state in heap layout, zero compatibility-table
materialization, zero reconstruction, and one output bit.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first item is component erasure.  The second is the definition of a
consistent AND tree.  For the third, scan each record, compare its supplied
parent with its two supplied children, reject on the first mismatch, and
finally check the root.  Equation (4) proves correctness.  Heap indices
compute child addresses from one \(O(\log N)\)-bit index.
\end{proof}

The theorem does not claim every implementation follows this scan.  It gives
one explicit algorithm, which retires (TG-H) for this island.  Under the audit
hypothesis \(\SAT\in P\) with total decider \(A\), a total wrapper recognizes
\(H_d\), runs the charged \(O(N)\) validator on-island, and invokes \(A\)
otherwise.  The fallback keeps \(A\)'s unknown polynomial exponent, so no
contradiction or exponent elimination follows.

\section{Wrappers and barriers}

DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP alter traces without changing the
function.  Layouts change addresses, not the linear upper bound when locator
metadata is charged.  Padding weakens bounds stated in padded length.  Compact
circuits and branching programs help: the tree is already a linear circuit.

The trilemma relativizes.  A semantic oracle bypasses the validator; algebraic
access evaluates local degree-two gates without materializing our state.
There is no nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing hinge.  No large constructive
circuit property is used, so Natural Proofs is not active.  Nonuniform
circuits also evaluate the fixed-layout tree in linear size.  No
solver-to-protocol theorem forces local consistency messages.

\section{Post-quantum technique ledger}

Tseitin gate encoding is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER}.  Collision
lossiness is exact for the root map:
\[
\operatorname{Coll}(\operatorname{AND})=(2^L-1)^2+1,
\]
but on the consistent promise it is a shortcut, not storage.  Black-box
worst-case/average-case extraction is an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a

syzygies, Betti numbers, and special soundness are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER}:
no matching representation or conflicting openings are forced.  Hash and
random-oracle assumptions remain inadmissible.
