\section*{New target: make source-occurrence UNSAT requirements coherent}
The user proposes initially placing each \emph{source-indexed} occurrence into its UNSAT literal state, inverting nonconflicting occurrences, then reversing globally coherent assignments of previously unused conflicting variables whose literals are currently satisfied. This suggests four falsifiable mechanisms: (i) inversion of a pure-sign variable is safe; (ii) a coherent single-use SAT-literal-key flip walk can reach any witness; (iii) conflicting UNSAT targets that remain at the end imply formula UNSAT; (iv) original-clause endpoint statuses and aggregate source conflict patterns can distinguish SAT/UNSAT even if the walk fails. We formalize and test all four, without turning separate occurrences into independent Boolean variables.

Let $F=\bigwedge_{j=0}^{m-1}C_j$ be proper source 3CNF. The original occurrence $x_{i,j}$ of $x_i$ has sign $\sigma_{ij}\in\{+1,-1\}$. Define the \emph{local all-TRUE} $S_j$ and \emph{local all-FALSE} $U_j$ targets at every occurrence by
\[
 S_{ij}=\mathbf{1}[\sigma_{ij}=+1],\qquad U_{ij}=1-S_{ij};
 \quad\overline{S}_{ij}=U_{ij},\ \overline{U}_{ij}=S_{ij}.
\]
They are partial targets attached to ORIGINAL clause IDs, not globally independent variables. A clause ordinarily satisfies $C_j$ when at least one literal is TRUE, not necessarily all three. For globally coherent $a$,
\[
 t_j(\bar a)=3-t_j(a),\quad N_h(\bar a)=N_{3-h}(a),\quad
 t_j(a\oplus M)-t_j(a)=|M\cap L_j^0(a)|-|M\cap L_j^1(a)|.
\]
For two original clauses, with $s$ same-sign and $o$ opposite-sign shared variables, $SS,UU$ are feasible exactly when $o=0$, and $SU,US$ exactly when $s=0$. The raw experiment records \emph{every} source index, occurrence, four target values, inverted targets, same/opposite compatibility witnesses, coherent endpoint $0^n,1^n$ truth vectors, intermediate single/multiple flips and per-clause $t_j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$.

\begin{lemma}[Safe nonconflicting inversion: known pure-literal rule]
If every occurrence of a variable $x$ has the same sign, assigning $x$ the literal-TRUE value cannot falsify any clause that was true before. When $x$ occurs in both signs, its complete family of local $U$ targets is incompatible, but this does not restrict any globally coherent assignment without additional clause information. In strict signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ 3CNF \emph{every} variable is of the latter type: the proposed nonconflicting-variable stage is vacuous.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Setting a pure positive variable to $1$ or a pure negative variable to $0$ only changes affected literals from FALSE to TRUE. Conversely, a mixed variable occurs once with a $U$ target $x=0$ and once with target $x=1$; at most one target may hold in a global assignment. This is a conflict of local all-FALSE targets, not of the disjunctions themselves. Strict22 gives two occurrences of each sign at every original variable.\end{proof}

\[
 C_9:\ -6\mapsto+6,\qquad C_{13}:\ +6\mapsto-6,

leaving every other signed literal and every unsigned source-clause support unchanged.

For a source $G$, define $\mathcal{D}(G)$ as the ordered unsigned clause-incidence lists, the per-variable $(d_i^+,d_i^-)$ degrees, the two four-class endpoint histograms, and the \emph{aggregate multiset} of source-pair $(s,o)$ overlap signatures. Crucially, $\mathcal{D}$ does \emph{not} contain every indexed pair-matrix entry or every signed source literal.

\begin{theorem}[Matched SAT/UNSAT diagnostic twins, PROVED/EXACT restricted]
For every integer $r\ge1$, let $W_r$ and $V_r$ be the disjoint unions of $r$ original-variable-relabeled copies of $W$ and $V$, respectively. Both are proper signed exact-$(2+,2-)$ 3CNF with $n=15r,m=20r$ and identical $\mathcal{D}$, yet $W_r$ is UNSAT and $V_r$ is SAT. Thus no universally correct SAT/UNSAT classifier can depend \emph{only} on $\mathcal D$ or on which variables possess opposing local $U$ targets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $r=1$, each variable has exactly two positive and two negative original occurrences in both sources. The endpoint class vectors are \emph{identical}:
\[
 (N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)(0^{15})=(1,10,7,2),\qquad
 (N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)(1^{15})=(2,7,10,1).
\]
The exact pair-signature counts in both sources are
\[
 \#(s,o)=(0,0):117,\quad(0,1):43,\quad(1,0):13,\quad(1,1):17.
\]
The UNSAT source $W$ has a complete original-source DPLL refutation containing 76 checked conflict leaves, 75 binary branch nodes and 228 source-indexed unit propagations. An independent checker replays every unit consequence and every falsified original source clause. A separate exhaustive check finds zero models among all $2^{15}=32768$ coherent assignments. Source $V$ has 54 models; one explicitly verified full model is mask $19186$, setting $x_{2},x_{5},x_{6},x_{7},x_{8},x_{10},x_{12},x_{15}$ to TRUE. All other original variables are FALSE.

Disjoint unions preserve unsigned incidence and the degree quota. The endpoint four-class histograms scale by $r$. Each source-pair signature count scales by $r$, except $(0,0)$ additionally gains $400\binom r2$ clause pairs belonging to distinct copies; the extra term is equal in both sources. A model of $V_r$ is the repeated explicit $V$ witness; no model of $W_r$ exists because restricting it to any copy would satisfy $W$.\end{proof}

\section*{Sound deductions, construction and total-work firewall}
A sound new clause (not an assumption that all clauses must reach their all-TRUE local extremes) follows from ORIGINAL $C_7=(x_1\vee x_7\vee x_{13})$ and $C_{19}=(\neg x_1\vee x_{13}\vee x_{14})$:
\[
 \operatorname{Res}_{x_1}(C_7,C_{19})=x_7\vee x_{13}\vee x_{14}.
\]
The cube $(x_7=x_{13}=x_{14}=0)$ is impossible: the two original parents reduce to contradictory unit clauses $(x_1)$ and $(\neg x_1)$. This excludes a genuine \emph{partial-assignment subcube}. The opposite local $U$ requirements for $x_1$ alone do not exclude that cube or force the entire formula to be UNSAT.

The implemented corrected \emph{single-use key walk} chooses one of two coherent endpoint masks, assigns each pure-sign variable its safe SAT value, and then flips globally one unused mixed-sign variable at a time, either (A) the first currently-TRUE literal belonging to a satisfied source clause or (B) a variable in the first currently-false source clause. Each flip changes every ORIGINAL occurrence of the selected $x_i$, not one occurrence in isolation. All current source clause counts and $N_0,\ldots,N_3$ are updated through an occurrence index. After at most $n$ key flips, failure means UNKNOWN, never UNSAT. Two endpoints and both policies are tried; complete fixed-order UP-DPLL is the correctness-preserving fallback. The source-parent resolvents, four-mode matrices, all failed walks and witness/refutation checks are charged.

For $T=\sum_j|C_j|$, source ingestion, local target construction, pure/mixed classification and endpoint counting cost $O(T+n)$ indexed word-RAM actions. Naive explicit all-pairs four-mode construction costs $O(m^2k^2)$ for width $k$; strict22 permits a previously proved sparse $O(N+n\log n)$ representation, but the current experiment \emph{pays for} the dense path. A coherent flip of $x_i$ changes exactly $d_i$ source counters and $2d_i$ histogram buckets, $d_i=4$ for strict22. The four walks visit at most $4(n+1)$ full masks and use $O(nT)$ scanning work with $O(T+n)$ streamed state; storing every intermediate per-clause vector adds $O(nm\log k)$ bits. DPLL fallback and a complete retained UNSAT proof may be exponential. All bound claims include policy selection, source access, all failed paths and witness/UNSAT replay; no exponential cost is hidden in a free oracle.
