\section{Exact bottom-up growth}
\begin{theorem}
Let $F$ be UNSAT, let $C$ be indispensable ($F\setminus\{C\}$ is SAT), and let $z$ be fresh. Put
\[
F'=(F\setminus\{C\})\cup\{C\vee z,C\vee\neg z\}.
\]
Then $W_{res}(F')=W_{res}(F)+1$. If $F$ is minimally UNSAT, so is $F'$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Resolve the two new clauses to recover $C$ and append a shortest proof of $F$: this gives the upper bound. Every refutation of $F'$ uses a split clause, since all other sources are satisfiable. A path from that clause to contradiction must eliminate its $z$ literal, hence some inference pivots on $z$. Restrict the entire proof to $z=0$. Standard topological restriction retains derived subclauses, removes satisfied lines and assigned-pivot steps, and resolves remaining parents only when necessary. This yields a refutation of exactly $F$ with at most $q-1$ inferences if the original had $q$. Thus the matching lower bound holds. Minimality follows by extending witnesses for deletion of an unchanged source arbitrarily in $z$, and extending a witness for $F\setminus\{C\}$ with the value satisfying the remaining split clause.
\end{proof}
Starting at $\{\bot\}$ and performing $s$ fresh splits gives $m=s+1$ and exact $W_{res}=W_{mix341}=m-1$. The lower bound follows because all $m$ sources must occur in the connected contradiction ancestry: $m+q-1\le E\le2q$. This is an exact growing lower bound but only linear in source clause count and encoded length.

\section{Seed, mathematical optimality certificate, and growth}
Use
\[
F_*=(\neg b\vee\neg c)\wedge(\neg a\vee b)\wedge(a\vee c)
\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee c)\wedge(a\vee b\vee\neg c).
\]
Adding these clauses in the displayed order leaves $8,6,4,2,1,0$ models. Every source is indispensable. A five-step proof, numbering source clauses in order, is
\[
D_1=Res_c(C_1,C_4)=\neg a\vee\neg b,
\quad D_2=Res_b(D_1,C_2)=\neg a,
\]
\[
D_3=Res_b(C_1,C_5)=a\vee\neg c,
\quad D_4=Res_c(D_3,C_3)=a,
\quad D_5=Res_a(D_2,D_4)=\bot.
\]
Four steps are impossible: equality in the source-arity bound would force a tree with each of five sources used once. Its final parents are complementary units. No source is a unit and no pair of sources resolves to a unit, so each unit subtree needs at least three source leaves, requiring six in total. Consequently $W_{res}(F_*)=5$, whereas $W_{mix341}(F_*)=4$.

\[
W_{res}=5+s,\qquad W_{mix341}=4+s.
