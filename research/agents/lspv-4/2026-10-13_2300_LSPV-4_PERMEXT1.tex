\section{Primary-literature and transfer preflight}
Tseitin's definitional translation encodes every Boolean gate by a constant
number of CNF clauses \cite{Tseitin}; Cook's circuit-to-SAT construction is
the foundational polynomial simulation \cite{Cook}.  The only property used
here is proved directly below: once a gate's inputs are fixed, its
bi-implication clauses force one output value.  This is a directly
transferable encoding fact, not a runtime lower bound.

Gold power maps and Nyberg's differential-uniformity framework supply the
finite-field context for the cube residual \cite{Gold,Nyberg}.  The required
permutation fact is elementary: for odd $d$,
$\gcd(3,2^d-1)=1$ and the inverse exponent is
$(2^{d+1}-1)/3$.  This is an exact algebraic transfer and an algorithmic
shortcut, not a cryptographic hardness premise.

De Colnet and Mengel prove representation/proof-complexity results for
Tseitin formulas \cite{dCM}.  Their DNNF and regular-Resolution statements do
not imply witness-search time.  Likewise, circuit locality, VLSI routing,
communication cuts, control-theoretic observability, and pebbling supply no
model-matched theorem forcing an unrestricted solver to materialize this
extension.  Those are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFERS} for the present target.

The two obstacles are therefore (i) separating source difficulty from work
introduced by the residual layer, and (ii) obtaining an all-program lower
bound after polynomial witness lifting is allowed.  This run resolves (i) in
the negative for one explicit family and leaves (ii) \OPEN{}.

\section{Typed construction and off-image semantics}
Let $F(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$ be a CNF.  A valid typed record contains:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an odd $d\ge3$, a connected graph $X=(V,E)$ of cycle rank $d$, a
declared spanning tree $T$, and its cotree $C=E\setminus T$;
\item a circuit $b:\F^n\to\F^d$ and a circuit
$c:\F^n\to\F^V$ whose output always has even parity;
\item a circuit for a permutation $P_d:\F^d\to\F^d$ and a declared uniform
polynomial-time inverse algorithm.
\end{enumerate}
The semantic coupled relation is
\[
 G_F(u,x)=F(u)\land[B_Xx=c(u)]
             \land[P_d(\pi_Cx)=b(u)].                 \tag{1}
\]
The encoding expands $b,c,P_d$, the incidence parities, and output equalities
by topologically ordered definitional CNF gates.  For a fan-in-two circuit,
AND uses three clauses, XOR uses four, and NOT uses two.  If the combined
circuit has $g$ gates, there are $g$ gate variables and at most $4g+O(d)$
clauses.  With a standard bit representation,
\[
 |G_F|=O(|F|+|E|+g_b+g_c+g_P+d).                       \tag{2}
\]
For the cube map in a polynomial basis, schoolbook field arithmetic gives
$g_P=O(d^2)$; better circuits are permitted but not needed.

The recognizer checks the version/key set, dimensions, odd $d$, connectedness,
$T$ spanning, $C=E\setminus T$, $|C|=d$, field-modulus irreducibility in the
cube specialization, circuit well-formedness, and the even-charge output
contract.  Malformed or off-domain records are rejected.  This is a promise
subclass with explicit rejection; it is not silently promoted to an
NP-complete language.  Compiler output from an arbitrary $F$ is always valid.

\section{Unique-extension theorem}
For $y\in\F^d$ and an even charge $q\in\F^V$, let
$\operatorname{Lift}_q(y)$ put $y$ on $C$ and solve the tree-edge equations
leaf-to-root.  Define
\[
 a(u)=\operatorname{Lift}_{c(u)}(0),\qquad
 L_i=\operatorname{Lift}_0(e_i).                       \tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 B_Xa(u)=c(u),\quad B_XL=0,\quad\pi_CL=I_d,
 \quad\operatorname{Lift}_{c(u)}(y)=a(u)+Ly.           \tag{4}
\]

\begin{theorem}[parsimonious permutation-residual extension]
For every valid record above, projection $(u,x,\gamma)\mapsto u$, where
$\gamma$ denotes all definitional gate variables, is a bijection
\[
 \operatorname{Sat}(G_F)\longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Sat}(F). \tag{5}
\]
Its inverse is uniform and polynomial time:
\[
 u\longmapsto
 \left(u,
   \operatorname{Lift}_{c(u)}(P_d^{-1}(b(u))),
   \operatorname{EvalGates}(u,x)
 \right).                                               \tag{6}
\]
Consequently, any source witness finder can be wrapped with only polynomial
time/space and fixed program-description overhead to find a coupled witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $u$.  Evaluate $b(u)$ and $c(u)$.  Since $P_d$ is a permutation, exactly
one cotree vector satisfies the residual, namely
$z=P_d^{-1}(b(u))$.  Equation (4) gives exactly one full edge vector
$x=a(u)+Lz$ satisfying $B_Xx=c(u)$ and $\pi_Cx=z$.  Existence follows from
even charge parity; uniqueness follows because a homogeneous solution with
zero cotree coordinates is a forest circulation and hence zero.

For fixed primary inputs, each definitional gate's bi-implication clauses
force its truth-table output.  Induction in topological order therefore gives
exactly one $\gamma$.  Hence every $u$ has exactly one complete extension
through the residual layer, and that extension satisfies $G_F$ exactly when
$F(u)=1$.  Conversely every satisfying assignment of $G_F$ contains
$F(u)$, so projection yields a satisfying assignment of $F$.  This proves
(5) and (6).

Parsing and evaluating $b,c$ cost $O(g_b+g_c)$ gate operations.  The declared
inverse costs $\operatorname{poly}(d)$, tree lifting costs $O(|V|+|E|)$,
gate evaluation costs $O(g)$, and writing/verifying the witness costs its
explicit length.  All workspace is polynomial in (2), and no source witness,
inverse table, tree solution, or gate trace is advice.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[source-matched Gold-cube family]
For every odd $d\ge3$, take $K=\mathbb F_{2^d}$ and $P_d(z)=z^3$.  Then
\[
 P_d^{-1}(w)=w^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3}.                        \tag{7}
\]
Theorem~1 applies for arbitrary polynomial-size source circuits $b(u)$ and
even-charge circuits $c(u)$, including $K_{s,s}$ with even $s$ and
$d=(s-1)^2$.  Thus the family includes shared-variable quadratic coupling,
full cotree rank, singleton fibers, and unbounded graph treewidth while
remaining a polynomially transparent extension of its source.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For odd $d$, $2^d-1\equiv1\pmod3$, while
$3(2^{d+1}-1)/3=2(2^d-1)+1$.  Therefore (7) inverts cubing on $K^\times$ and
both maps fix zero.  Cubing is quadratic because $z\mapsto z^2$ is
$\F$-linear and field multiplication is bilinear.  The graph statement
follows from $|E|-|V|+1=(s-1)^2$ and (4).
\end{proof}

The theorem is \CAND{} pending independent review.  Its proof is symbolic;
the experiment below is a separate \COMP{} audit.  It does not make $F$ easy:
it proves only that the new residual layer contributes no superpolynomial
overhead once a source witness is available.

\section{Short-program and amplification accounting}
Let $p$ be any program which, on the explicit source record, outputs a
satisfying $u$.  A fixed wrapper $\widehat p$ extracts the embedded source,
runs $p$, and applies (6).  For one constant $c_0$ independent of $F$,
\[
 |\widehat p|\le |p|+c_0,
 \qquad
 t_{\widehat p}(G_F)
 \le t_p(F)+\operatorname{poly}(|G_F|).                \tag{8}
\]
If a UWI encoding needs the description budget preserved, a fixed constant
factor of transparent padding provides the $c_0$ extra logarithmic budget;
the padding and its scan are charged.  Equation (8) is an upper-bound
compiler, not an unconditional estimate of $H(F)$.

Therefore this transform cannot justify a claim that the residual extension
itself supplies a growing factor $r(s)\to\infty$.  Any such factor must come
from the embedded source problem or from a different construction that
defeats (6).  This does not contradict the conditional growing-factor lemma,
nor does an additive polynomial upper bound alone falsify every possible
multiplicative inequality when the source cost is unknown.

\section{Hostile controls, barriers, and post-quantum ledger}
The coupled family inherits the source's YES and NO cases rather than being
an all-YES benchmark.  Horn, Krom, affine, bounded-width, independent-product,
renaming, complementation, padding, and deterministic gate auxiliaries all
preserve the explicit lift.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY/INTERP/RECOMP wrappers add only
their charged explicit work and cannot destroy the source projection or (6).
Compact field arithmetic and polynomial workspace are permitted.

The wrapper and bijection relativize and algebrize; they are upper bounds and
offer no nonrelativizing hinge.  Natural Proofs is not invoked.  No ETH,
cryptographic, random-oracle, or average-case premise is used.  A successful
trace is not compiled into a DNNF or proof-system refutation.

In the post-quantum ledger, the singleton-fiber collision identity
$\operatorname{Coll}(P_d)=2^d$ is an \textbf{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER};
it exposes losslessness but not work.  The construction-specific unique
extension in (5) is an exact binding statement, yet interpreting binding as
hardness is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} because (6) computes the opening.
Schur powers, syzygies, graded Betti numbers, and rank spectra are
\textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE} diagnostics for a different residual and require a
new bridge.  The LWE worst-case/average-case analogy does not apply: (8) is a
deterministic source-to-extension wrapper, not an average-case reduction.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}
Repository search found prior cotree bijections, field selectors, quadratic
permutations, hidden-shear recovery, and the Gold-cube firewall.  PERMEXT1
does not claim a second discovery of those maps.  Its new contribution is the
fully source-matched construction: arbitrary $F(u)$, public $b(u),c(u)$,
unique complete extension, parsimonious CNF encoding, selector-wrapper bound,
and explicit YES/NO/off-image semantics.

There is no contradiction with NP-completeness of arbitrary SAT or MQ: the
source $F$ is retained verbatim and may carry all difficulty.  There is no
contradiction with exact-$(2,2)$ compiler or proof-system lower bounds, which
measure other encodings/models.  Nor does the theorem imply that every
nonlinear residual is easy.
