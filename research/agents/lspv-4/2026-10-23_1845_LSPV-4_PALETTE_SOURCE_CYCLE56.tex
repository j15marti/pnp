\section*{Prerecorded hypotheses and model}
Test (1) source-field sensitivity for both labels after a proper-3CNF graph-color encoding; (2) whether dense chords among two-valued palette-pinned vertices create exponential hardness; and (3) whether the AND-based 2-SAT baseline can transport to the XOR family through independent field transformations. Outcomes: (1) PROVED restricted, (2) FALSIFIED as an amplification, (3) impossibility of such a local transport PROVED. Input: explicit four-word fixed-width clause records plus two header words, $w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil$, $N=(2+4m)w$, one addressed input-word access per word operation. All input reads, failed branches, parsing, recognition, replay and workspace are charged.

\section*{Exact 2-SAT baseline and ternary ladder}
For $q\ge1$, set
\[
F_b=(z_0\vee u)(z_0\vee\neg u)(\neg z_q\vee v)(\neg z_q\vee\neg v)
\bigwedge_{i=1}^q (\neg z_{i-1}\vee z_i^{(b_i)})
\]
with $z_i^{(1)}=z_i$, $z_i^{(0)}=\neg z_i$, and juxtaposition denoting conjunction. Then $n=q+3$, $m=q+4$, $N=(4q+18)w$; $F_b$ is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$. If $b$ has a zero at position $j$, put $z_i=1$ for $i<j$, $z_i=0$ for $i\ge j$. The all-ones instance requires all $q$ distinct orientation-field probes by deterministic transcript indistinguishability; a source scan matches $O(q)$ operations. Each $u,v$ is free in SAT witnesses.

Padding every binary clause $(a\vee b)$ into $(a\vee b\vee t)(a\vee b\vee\neg t)$ with a distinct free $t$ gives $n'=2q+7$ and $2q+8$ genuine ternary clauses, with exactly $2^{q+4}$ extensions of each original satisfying assignment; its source is recognizable 2-SAT. For general 2-CNF $B$ plus $k$ ternary clauses, select one original variable per ternary into $S$, $|S|\le k$; restricting $S$ leaves 2-CNF, giving a charged $2^{|S|}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ upper bound. Fixed or logarithmic $k$ is polynomial, with NO corresponding exponential necessity theorem.

\section*{Palette-cycle construction and exact primary theorem}
For $q\ge3$ and $\sigma\in\{0,1\}^q$, create a palette triangle $B,T,F$ and nodes $x_i,\bar x_i$ for $i$ modulo $q$. Edges: $BT,TF,FB$; and $Bx_i,B\bar x_i,x_i\bar x_i$ for all $i$; and for each $i$ the edge $x_{i+1}x_i$ if $\sigma_i=1$ or $x_{i+1}\bar x_i$ otherwise. The graph has $V=2q+3$ vertices, $E=4q+3$ edges, no loops or duplicates, canonical edge order independent of $\sigma$.
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT]
The graph has exactly 12 proper three-colorings when $\bigoplus_i\sigma_i=0$, and none otherwise. Flipping any single orientation changes the label but no other source edge record.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The palette uses all three colors. Each pair $x_i,\bar x_i$ must take the two colors different from $B$, in opposite order. Write its first color as bit $X_i$. The connector edge enforces $X_{i+1}=X_i\oplus\sigma_i$, so the cycle closes iff $\bigoplus_i\sigma_i=0$. In that case six palette permutations and two initial choices give 12 colorings.
\end{proof}

One-hot encode every graph vertex using a positive ternary requiring one of three colors plus three negative binaries prohibiting multiple colors; each graph edge adds three negative binary clauses forbidding matching colors. The one-hot CNF models are in bijection with graph three-colorings, with no free or independently sampled auxiliary variable. Its exact counts are
\[
n=6q+9,\quad k=2q+3,\quad m_2=18q+18,\quad
m=20q+21,\quad \ell=42q+45,\quad N=(80q+86)w.
\]
No initial units or pure literals exist. Flipping $\sigma_i$ changes \emph{exactly three literal words}, one in each binary clause of the $i$th connector gadget; different $i$ affect disjoint address sets. Header, length, clause arities, and all other records are unchanged.
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT: all-algorithm input access]
On every YES or NO member of this proper-3CNF family, every deterministic exact decider $A$ of \emph{unrestricted} proper-3CNF SAT must read at least one of the three orientation-dependent words at each $i$, hence at least $q=\Omega(N/\log N)$ input words. A uniformly recognized source-aware algorithm matches $O(q)$ word operations for the family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $A$ reads none of the three $i$th words, flip $\sigma_i$. Its entire observed transcript is unchanged by determinism, yet the correct SAT answer reverses, contradiction. Source-address groups are disjoint across $i$. A charged $O(q)$ template scan and XOR propagation decides and reconstructs satisfying assignments on the recognized family; a total exact decider can fall back to exhaustive search on other inputs.
\end{proof}
This is an \emph{input acquisition} bound on an NP-complete language, not a lower bound exceeding the cost of reading the input. It does not imply $P\ne NP$.

\section*{Exception parameter, affine controls, and transport failure}
Fix the palette triangle and let $t$ be the number of remaining vertices \emph{not} adjacent to $B$. These are unrestricted three-color variables; every other vertex has only two available colors. Enumerating $3^t$ exception-color choices leaves an affine/XOR inequality system on the two-valued vertices. Signed component propagation gives an exact $O(3^t(V+E))$ word-time algorithm, $O(V+E)$ peak space, charging all failed choices. Arbitrarily many chords entirely among pinned vertices remain linear-time solvable. This is an upper bound, not a lower bound.

Every graph $H$ reduces to a connected graph with palette by adjoining the triangle and an edge $Bv$ to one representative vertex in each connected component. Each component's coloring may permute its three colors to avoid $B$ at the representative, so the reduction preserves three-colorability. This reaches the known NP-complete endpoint of arbitrary graph three-coloring with a connected graph, but attaching an arbitrary hard component is not asserted to amplify the source lower bound.

A precise local reduction barrier holds: the original baseline UNSAT indicator is $\prod_i b_i$ (AND), while the palette-cycle UNSAT indicator is $\bigoplus_i \sigma_i$ (XOR). For $\sigma_i=g_i(b_i)$ independently, XOR of unary Boolean maps is affine over $\mathbb F_2$, while AND on $q\ge2$ inputs is not affine. Thus no such fieldwise label-preserving map exists. A global construction can read all $q$ bits, compute AND, and write one output orientation, but the paid $O(q)$ acquisition consumes the old $q$ lower bound.
