\section{Target and source reconciliation}
The preceding continuation identified a spectral polynomial-time shortcut on some random graph-coloring instances having exponential PC proof complexity. We test three mechanisms: an exact algebraic certificate for that shortcut, an NP-complete class excluding it, and a degree-sensitive auxiliary-gate extension. The first two succeed below. The auxiliary-gate branch is not independently recertified or strengthened in this run.

Let $\mathcal C$ be the nonempty finite simple triangle-free graphs of maximum degree at most four. The fixed language $L_*$ consists of valid encodings of the 3-colorable graphs in $\mathcal C$; malformed and out-of-class sources are rejected. Maffray--Preissmann prove that this is NP-complete. This classification is KNOWN, not new.

The bounded corpus review read root main.tex and original MAIN DEGREE-BOUNDARY96 and reconciled the supplied prior proof-system/source-accounting context. Repository searches for triangle-free spectral and Maffray returned no matches; that is not exhaustive novelty evidence. MAIN96 already supplies the degree-three/four boundary and an input-order acquisition lower bound. We retain those results, the source-channel limitations, and the prohibition against equating proof output with every decider's work. No full-repository preflight or unperformed peer review is claimed.

Write $p=|V|$, $e=|E|$, $A$ for adjacency and $D=\operatorname{diag}(\deg(v))$. Use $n$ for Boolean variables, $k$ for genuine ternaries and $N$ for encoded bits. Matrix $D$ is not an eliminated-variable count.

\section{Explicit feasible vector-coloring certificate}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT within the stated relaxation]
For every $G\in\mathcal C$,
\[
 X=\frac18\bigl((A-2I)^2+(4I-D)\bigr)
\]
is positive semidefinite, has diagonal one and has $X_{uv}=-1/2$ on every edge. Thus the basic strict vector 3-coloring relaxation is feasible even on every UNSAT member of this NP-complete class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The square of symmetric $A-2I$ is positive semidefinite, as is $4I-D$. Since $(A^2)_{vv}=\deg(v)$, the diagonal numerator is eight. Adjacent vertices in a triangle-free graph have no common neighbor, so on edges $(A^2)_{uv}=0$ and the numerator is minus four.
\end{proof}
The Gram factors $A-2I$ and diagonal $4I-D$ use $O(p+e)$ words. Sparse construction costs $O(\sum_v\deg(v)^2+p)=O(p+e)$ word events after graph validation. Dense $p^2$ output is optional. The vector relaxation is classical (Karger--Motwani--Sudan); no literature-wide novelty claim is made for this elementary specialization. It does not rule out stronger SDPs, rounding, additional constraints, or alternative algorithms.

\section{An exact degree-two pseudoexpectation}
Use Boolean one-hot variables $x_{v,c}$, $c=1,2,3$, and equations
\[
\sum_c x_{v,c}-1=0,\quad x_{v,c}^2-x_{v,c}=0,\quad
x_{v,c}x_{v,c'}=0\ (c\ne c'),\quad
x_{u,c}x_{v,c}=0\ (uv\in E).
\]
Define $\widetilde E[1]=1$, $\widetilde E[x_{v,c}]=1/3$ and
\[
\widetilde E[x_{u,c}x_{v,c'}]=
\begin{cases}(1+2X_{uv})/9&c=c',\\(1-X_{uv})/9&c\ne c'.\end{cases}
\]
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT constant-degree obstruction]
This functional annihilates all quadratic equations and every linear multiple of a color-sum equation, and is nonnegative on every square of a linear polynomial. Consequently every UNSAT source in $\mathcal C$ lacks a total-degree-at-most-two equality-style sum-of-squares refutation in this encoding.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $\mu=\mathbf1/3$, $P=I_3-J_3/3$. The moment matrix indexed by $1,x$ is
\[
\mathcal M=\begin{pmatrix}1&\mu^T\\\mu&\mu\mu^T+X\otimes P/3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its Schur complement is positive semidefinite. The diagonal and edge identities for $X$ verify Booleanity, within-vertex and edge exclusions. Summing pair moments over a color gives $1/3$, verifying multiplied color sums. Applying this functional to a degree-two identity $-1=\sum_j q_j^2+\sum_i h_i f_i$ would make its right side nonnegative, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
There is an integer certificate of positivity. Let $X_8=8X$ and $w=(3,1,\ldots,1)^T$. Then
\[
72\mathcal M=8ww^T+\operatorname{diag}\bigl(0,X_8\otimes(3I_3-J_3)\bigr).
\]
Use $3I_3-J_3=\sum_{c<c'}(e_c-e_{c'})(e_c-e_{c'})^T$ and the explicit Gram factors of $X_8$. All weights and entries can be integers. No floating-point SDP is required.

This is not a true distribution on complete colorings, and is not a SAT certificate. No degree-four pseudoexpectation is asserted. The CNF at-least-one polynomial is cubic, whereas this equality encoding uses a linear color sum; conventions must not be silently identified.

\section{Spectral rejection fails hereditarily}
For any nonempty graph, $M=D+2A\succ0$ certifies non-3-colorability. Represent the three colors by $(2,-1,-1)$, $(-1,2,-1)$ and $(-1,-1,2)$. A proper coloring matrix $U$ would satisfy
\[
\operatorname{tr}(U^TMU)=6\sum_v\deg(v)-12e=0,
\]
contradicting positive definiteness.

For every $G\in\mathcal C$, take $z_v=2$, $z_u=-1$ on $N(v)$ and zero elsewhere. There are at most five nonzero coordinates, and
\[
z^T(D+2A)z=\sum_{u\in N(v)}\deg(u)-4\deg(v)\le0.
\]
Triangle-freeness removes neighbor-neighbor contributions. This holds on every nonempty subgraph after recomputing degrees. A zero value is not a claim that $z$ is a null vector. Thus the old test is excluded without appending irrelevant easy components.

\section{Exact low-degree certificate for the old shortcut}
For $M\succ0$ choose rational $\epsilon>0$ with $Q=M-\epsilon I\succeq0$. Define
\[
B_v=\sum_c(x_{v,c}^2-x_{v,c}),\quad S_v=\sum_cx_{v,c}-1,
\quad E_{uv}=\sum_cx_{u,c}x_{v,c},\quad t_c=3x_{\cdot,c}-\mathbf1.
\]
Direct polynomial expansion gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
-1={}&\frac{\sum_c t_c^TQt_c}{6\epsilon p}
-\frac{9\sum_v(\deg(v)-\epsilon)B_v}{6\epsilon p}\\
&+\frac{\sum_v(9\deg(v)+3\epsilon)S_v}{6\epsilon p}
-\frac{36\sum_{uv\in E}E_{uv}}{6\epsilon p}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Rational $LDL^T$ supplies positive rational weighted squares. A safe choice is $\epsilon=[2(3\Delta)^{p-1}]^{-1}$; determinant/eigenvalue bounds give polynomial bit lengths. For three indicators,
\[
a+b+c-1=-(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)+(1-c)ab+ac+bc.
\]
Substitution yields degree at most three in the mixed-width coloring CNF. Binary properization replaces $R$ by $R(1-z),Rz$, whose sum is $R$; the final proper-3CNF certificate has degree at most four. Four actual rational certificates and their original axiom membership were independently replayed.

\section{Ladder and exact resource ledger}
The original implication-chain baseline has $n=q+3,m=q+4$ and is UNSAT iff every orientation bit is one. A first zero supplies the transition witness. Every deterministic orientation-query decider reads all $q$ independent fields on all ones, only $\Omega(N/\log N)$ bit probes in the fixed-width indexed representation. Separate sign words give the same word count; packed access changes word costs.

For arbitrary 2-CNF plus $k$ ternaries, selecting one variable per ternary gives a strong 2-CNF backdoor $S$ with $|S|\le k$. Enumerating it gives a $2^{|S|}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ upper bound, not a lower bound. SCC, Horn, affine, component and source-readable shortcuts remain legal.

For a graph in $\mathcal C$, the parsimonious one-hot encoding has
\[
n=3p,\quad k=p,\quad m_2=3p+3e,\quad m=4p+3e\le10p.
\]
Properizing binaries uses $3p+3e$ fresh FREE variables, not functional gates:
\[
n'=6p+3e\le12p,\quad m'=k'=7p+6e\le19p.
\]
Each source model has $2^{3p+3e}$ target extensions. Construction, witness lifting, projection and replay use $O(p+e)$ word work in the prescribed compiler. Four-word clause records and two headers give $N=(4m+2)w$ with $w=\lceil\log_2(16(n+m+1))\rceil$. Peak sparse source/certificate storage is polynomial; dense moment output, when chosen, costs quadratic entries.

\section{Matched controls and actual tests}
The UNSAT fixture has vertices $0,\ldots,11$ and edges
\[
\begin{gathered}
01,04,06,09,12,15,17,23,26,28,34,37,39,45,48,\
5{,}10,5{,}11,6{,}10,6{,}11,78,7{,}11,8{,}10,9{,}10,9{,}11.
\end{gathered}
\]
Its source code lists unambiguous integer endpoint pairs. Removing $(0,1),(2,6)$ and adding $(0,2),(1,6)$ gives the SAT fixture. Both have 12 vertices, 24 edges, degree four and no triangles. Independent complete coloring counts are zero and 108. One SAT witness is $(0,0,1,0,1,2,1,1,2,1,0,0)$.

Both mixed CNFs have $(n,m,k)=(36,120,12)$ and $N=5784$ bits. Both proper CNFs have $(n',m',k')=(144,228,228)$ and $N'=11882$ bits. Moment dimension is 37 with 37 positive-factor rows. Evaluator counts under shared NOT, binary OR and binary AND conventions are 287 gates/538 input wires mixed, 827/1510 padded. These are not solver lower bounds.

The main graph audit screened all 33,867 labelled simple graphs through six vertices; 6,222 qualified, and all 36,828 local-star checks passed. The Gram audit checked all 439 qualifying graphs through five vertices plus the matched pair, with 70,758 counted checks and no unexpected failures. It rejected two deliberately corrupted moments. Baseline controls tested all 254 orientation strings through $q=7$, with 247 SAT and seven UNSAT, plus padding, Horn, XOR and products. Four proper-CNF spectral certificates for $K_4,K_5,K_6$ and $K_6$ minus an edge passed a separate exponent-vector polynomial verifier with 1,094 checks; four deliberate corruptions were rejected. The initial proposed positive fixture $K_5$ minus an edge correctly failed the spectral guard and was replaced, not counted as a successful positive test. These are finite implementation checks, not asymptotic hardness measurements.

\section{Status, limitations, and peer handoff}
\noindent\textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted:} Gram and moment identities, absence of degree-two equality-SoS refutations, hereditary spectral failure, old-certificate algebra and encoding ledger.\par
\noindent\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} NP-completeness of the chosen triangle-free maximum-degree-four endpoint; classical vector relaxation and SCC.\par
\noindent\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL:} finite exact audits; independent external review remains pending.\par
\noindent\textbf{OPEN:} growing higher-degree proof obstruction; separately, every-decider superpolynomial time. Earlier random-graph PC/PCR lower bounds are NOT transferred to this class merely by NP-completeness.

Role 5 should audit the Schur-complement proof, polynomial identities, fixed encoding and all-field-versus-real-proof distinction. Roles 1--3 should consume the matched pair and the entire canonical Gram-certificate class as source-aware controls. The immediate next experiment is degree-four moment consistency/positivity on growing UNSAT families, seeking either an exact extension or a replayable refutation. This alone would still be a restricted proof-system result.

No ETH, SETH, cryptographic hardness, random oracle, nonrelativization or Natural-Proofs escape is assumed. A semantic oracle can still return the root answer. The arbitrary-decider bridge must allow source retention, arbitrary algebra, caching, decomposition, shared DAGs and decide-first normalization. The target remains
\[
\forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}_{\rm uniform}(L_*)\ \forall c>0\ \forall N_0\
\exists G:\ N=|\operatorname{enc}(G)|\ge N_0,\ T_A(G)>N^c.
\]
Nothing in this report proves that assertion. No schedules are modified. This body does not assert publication of the separate local code bundle or unperformed root reconciliation.
