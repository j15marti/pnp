\section*{Target}
ROBUSTGAP1 proves that ordinary static SAT correctness does not automatically entail all-future-challenge continuation robustness. The weakest plausible repair is therefore an explicit compiler theorem. COLLAPSEGATE1 asks what such a compiler would already imply before attempting to prove it.

Let \(W\) be a polynomial-horizon global continuation problem. Assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(W\) is PSPACE-hard under polynomial-time many-one reductions;
\item for every deterministic polynomial-time SAT decider \(A\), a uniform polynomial-time compiler \(C\) using \(A\) as its declared subroutine produces a deterministic polynomial-time decider \(D_A\) for \(W\), with all preprocessing, strategy evaluation, rereading, recomputation, and output costs included.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{PROVED/EXACT collapse gate}
\paragraph{COLLAPSEGATE1.}
Under the hypotheses above,
\[
P=NP\quad\Longrightarrow\quad P=PSPACE.
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Assume \(P=NP\). Then a deterministic polynomial-time SAT decider \(A\) exists. By hypothesis 2, \(C(A)\) yields a deterministic polynomial-time decider \(D_A\) for \(W\), hence \(W\in P\). Since \(W\) is PSPACE-hard under polynomial-time many-one reductions, every language in PSPACE polynomial-time reduces to \(W\), and therefore lies in \(P\). Thus \(PSPACE\subseteq P\). The reverse inclusion \(P\subseteq PSPACE\) is standard, so \(P=PSPACE\). \(\square\)

Equivalently, unless the project independently proves
\[
P=NP\Longrightarrow P=PSPACE,
\]
it cannot treat a uniform polynomial-cost static-SAT-to-PSPACE-hard-global-continuation compiler as a routine consequence of \(P=NP\).

\section*{Why this sharpens ROBUSTGAP1}
ROBUSTGAP1 establishes a semantic gap: the compiler is not inherited from static SAT correctness. COLLAPSEGATE1 assigns a complexity-theoretic price to one strong repair. If the target continuation is genuinely PSPACE-hard and the compiler makes it polynomial-time whenever SAT is polynomial-time, the repair itself bridges the entire NP-to-PSPACE gap under the counterfactual.

This does not prove such a compiler impossible. It proves that the compiler is already a major load-bearing theorem and cannot be justified by freshness, causal chronology, SAME\_REALIZATION terminology, or SAT self-reducibility alone.

\section*{Hostile compiler audit}
\textbf{RECOMP.} A static solver may answer each realized SAT instance after it is presented without maintaining a reusable all-futures strategy.

\textbf{Full preprocessing.} If all future challenge data are placed in the static input, preprocessing may exploit them; if they are not present, ordinary static SAT correctness supplies no obligation to answer all future branches.

\textbf{Compact strategies.} Explicit strategy-tree size is not a valid charge. A compiler may use circuits or another succinct representation. COLLAPSEGATE1 therefore charges evaluation time, not explicit tree size.

\textbf{INTERP.} Universal interpretation changes native trace and provenance while preserving extensional SAT behavior. Any compiler depending on native code locations needs a wrapper-invariant transport theorem.

\textbf{DEAD/COPY/DELAY.} These change chronology or multiplicity without adding the PSPACE-hard semantic obligation.

\textbf{Semantic-oracle replacement.} Giving the realization a SAT oracle still does not supply a PSPACE oracle. The proposed compiler would have to create the missing global power from its additional structure.

\textbf{Algebraic-oracle extension.} Low-degree or algebraic access does not by itself establish the compiler. Any final contradiction must still identify a nonalgebrizing line.
