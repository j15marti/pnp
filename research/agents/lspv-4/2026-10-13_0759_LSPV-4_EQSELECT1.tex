\section{Exact objects and quantifiers}

Let $F$ be a CNF over variables $V(F)$ and let $\mathcal P(F)$ be a declared set of partial assignments (probes).  Define the clean-core compatibility bit
\[
 H_F(\alpha)=1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists z\in\{0,1\}^{V(F)}\;[z\supseteq\alpha\ \wedge\ F(z)=1].
\]
For a variable permutation $g$, let $gF$ and $g\alpha$ be the transported formula and probe.  Direct substitution gives the exact covariance identity
\[
 H_{gF}(g\alpha)=H_F(\alpha).                                      \tag{1}
\]

A deterministic single-probe selector is a total map
\[
 Q:\mathcal F\longrightarrow\bigcup_{F\in\mathcal F}\mathcal P(F),
 \qquad Q(F)\in\mathcal P(F).
\]
It is \emph{equivariant} when, for every encoded input $F$ in the domain and every permitted variable permutation $g$,
\[
 Q(gF)=gQ(F).                                                       \tag{2}
\]
The quantifier in the candidate repair is therefore
\[
 \exists Q\ \forall F\ \forall g\quad (2),
\]
not a per-instance existential choice $\forall F\exists\alpha$.

\begin{theorem}[EQUIVARIANT-SELECTOR-FIXED-POINT-GUARD1]
Let a group $G$ act on formulas and their candidate probes, and let $Q$ satisfy (2).  For every formula $F$,
\[
 Q(F)\in \Fix_{\mathcal P(F)}(\Aut(F)),
 \qquad
 \Aut(F)=\{g\in G:gF=F\}.
\]
Consequently, if $\Fix_{\mathcal P(F)}(\Aut(F))=\varnothing$, no deterministic single-valued equivariant selector exists at $F$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For every $h\in\Aut(F)$, $hF=F$.  Equivariance gives
$Q(F)=Q(hF)=hQ(F)$.  Hence $Q(F)$ is fixed by every $h$.  If the common fixed-point set is empty this is impossible.
\end{proof}

This theorem is representation-aware.  It applies when $gF=F$ in the declared normalized encoding, or at the semantic-object level followed by a charged normalization.  An encoding that places an external total order on otherwise identical blocks can select the least label, but that selector is not equivariant under the block action.  Breaking the premise is a valid algorithmic escape, not a refutation of the theorem.

\section{Explicit clean-core counterfamily}

Let $G_0$ have variables $x_0,\ldots,x_5$, positive clauses
\[
012,\ 034,\ 135,\ 245
\]
and negative clauses
\[
\overline0\overline2\overline4,\ 
\overline0\overline3\overline5,\ 
\overline1\overline2\overline5,\ 
\overline1\overline3\overline4.
\]
Every clause has three distinct variables and each variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice negatively, so $G_0$ is proper signed monotone $3$CNF-$(2,2)$.  For
\[
 \beta=(0,*,0,*,0,*),
\]
brute force verifies $H_{G_0}(\beta)=1$.

For $k\ge2$, form the disjoint product
\[
 F_k=G_0^{(1)}\wedge\cdots\wedge G_0^{(k)}
\]
and let $\beta_i$ apply $\beta$ to block $i$ and leave all other blocks unset.  With clauses canonically sorted, every block permutation in $S_k$ fixes the encoded clause set $F_k$ and sends $\beta_i$ to $\beta_{g(i)}$.  Thus
\[
 \Aut(F_k)\supseteq S_k,
 \qquad
 \mathcal P(F_k)=\{\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k\}
\]
is a transitive orbit with no fixed point.  The theorem rules out a deterministic equivariant $Q(F_k)\in\mathcal P(F_k)$.

The bit length of canonical DIMACS encoding is $N_k=O(k\log k)$: $6k$ variables, $8k$ clauses, and $24k$ literal occurrences.  The full orbit has only $k$ elements and can be streamed in $O(k)$ block operations.  Uniform randomized selection needs at most $\lceil\log_2 k\rceil$ random bits (with standard rejection for nonpowers of two).  Therefore the obstruction does \emph{not} imply superpolynomial time, memory, communication, or materialization.

\section{Arbitrary solver, SAME\_REALIZATION, and barrier audits}

Fix an arbitrary uniform deterministic polynomial-time SAT decider $A$ under the counterfactual $P=NP$.  EQSELECT1 proves neither
\[
 \forall A\ \exists\text{ charged extractor }E^A:\ E^A(F)=Q(F),
\]
nor any lower bound on $A$.  On $F_k$, $A$ may simply return the one bit $1$; it need not name a block, enumerate the orbit, expose $H_F$, retain a state family, or communicate a compatibility transcript.  COPY, DEAD, DELAY, INTERP, and RECOMP wrappers can preserve the same decision while changing every internal trace.  This is exactly the missing solver-to-protocol transfer.

\paragraph{SAME\_REALIZATION.}
Normalization, automorphism discovery, orbit construction, compatibility queries, extraction, and reconstruction must all be charged to the same computation if later used for an endpoint contradiction.  This run measures only its own finite audit.  It does not attach a time-space bound for any resource to the same run of arbitrary $A$.

\paragraph{Relativization and algebrization.}
The fixed-point proof is purely extensional and relativizes.  It is compatible with either $P=NP$ or $P\ne NP$ and therefore cannot be the nonrelativizing hinge needed for separation.  No arithmetization is used, so the result also supplies no escape from the algebrization barrier.  A later proof may use this guard, but must contain an additional model-matched nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing implication.

\paragraph{Natural Proofs and nonuniformity.}
No large constructive property of Boolean functions is asserted, so the Razborov--Rudich Natural Proofs framework is not the operative obstruction here.  Allowing a separate advice-selected block for each $k$ can choose a label but remains representation-dependent and does not yield an equivariant point of the symmetric object.  Conversely, forbidding such advice does not prove a uniform SAT lower bound.

\paragraph{Post-quantum ledger.}
Schur squares, low-rank quadratic relations, syzygies, and cryptographic binding are \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} for this barrier.  The orbit quotient has an exact collision calculation---all $k$ probes map to one orbit, so $\mathrm{Coll}=k^2$ and the Cauchy--Schwarz image bound is $1$---but this is a benign symmetry collapse, not hardness.  Black-box worst-case/average-case extraction remains an \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE needing a new bridge}; no such reduction is known here.  Hash/random-oracle methods are inadmissible for an unconditional conclusion.

\section{Smallest repair and handoff}

The smallest sound repair is to replace a single-valued covariant selector with one of the following explicitly typed interfaces:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an orbit-valued selector $\mathcal O(F)$ satisfying $\mathcal O(gF)=g\mathcal O(F)$;
\item an invariant aggregate of $\{H_F(\alpha):\alpha\in\mathcal O(F)\}$;
\item an equivariant probability distribution, with randomness and error charged; or
\item a label-dependent canonical choice, with normalization and loss of equivariance stated and charged.
\end{enumerate}
None alone is P-vs-NP relevant.  The highest-value missing implication is now:
