# AFFINE-NULLITY-BOUNDARY73: Role 5 and Roles 1-3 handoff

Status: PROVED/EXACT in the specified reduction/algorithm model, KNOWN THEOREM for graph 3-color NP-completeness, COMPUTATIONAL for 1570 checked test assertions (zero failures). The universal separation is OPEN. All five research schedules remain unchanged.

**Role 5 mathematical checks.** For arbitrary GF(2) system E, form connected affine-variable components and compute each consistent component's nullity. If each nullity is at most one, Gaussian elimination gives x=a_x XOR b_x*t_i with at most one free bit t_i per component. Substituting every literal of arbitrary binary CNF B yields exact 2-CNF over these t_i, even when all components are coupled through B. This is a polynomial-time quotient with bijective witness reconstruction; total nullity may be Theta(n). Independently verify inconsistent systems, isolated variables, rank discovery and costs.

The sharp opposite endpoint is graph 3-colorability: for every graph vertex impose an odd XOR3 equation and three negative NAND binary clauses, and for every edge impose one negative NAND for each of three colors. Exactly the assignments 001,010,100 remain per vertex (111 is excluded), so SAT iff 3-colorable, with one Boolean assignment per coloring. Every affine component has exactly three variables, rank one, nullity two; all binaries are Horn. For v source vertices and e edges the exact target counts are n=3v, m2=3v+3e, k=4v genuine ternaries and M=7v+3e. No SAT auxiliary variables. This establishes an NP-complete restricted language, not a superpolynomial lower bound.

**Role 5 and Roles 1-3 signed controls.** For odd cycle C_q, introduce a fresh u_v and two anchor clauses (s_v*x[v,3] OR u_v), (s_v*x[v,3] OR NOT u_v), with s_v=-1 for b_v=0 and +1 for b_v=1. Counts: n=4q, m2=8q, k=4q, M=12q, N=(48q+2)*ceil(log2(64*(16q+1))). Every u_v is free on satisfying colorings. The all-zero b string gives UNSAT. Flipping one anchor pair (exactly two sign words) gives SAT with exactly 2^(q+1) witnesses; two adjacent 1s give UNSAT. Every exact addressed-word decider must read one of two sign words per vertex on all-zero UNSAT, an Omega(N/log N) input acquisition bound, not a post-acquisition computational bound.

**Adaptive-state test.** Initial syntactic strong 2-CNF backdoor is q despite zero written unit clauses. A label-blind solver reads and matches the two anchor records for each vertex, derives sound units and eliminates all 4q ternaries before switching to SCC. If h anchors are absent, the residual has 4h ternaries and choosing one variable from each distinct support gives an exact 2^h*poly(N) upper bound, with discovery charged. The tested uniform exact policy selects SCC, support branching for at most eight uncovered supports, or DPLL fallback, not a universal menu. At q=1023, n=4092, M=12276, N=982120 bits, its instrumented events were 120718 UNSAT and 149367 SAT, with peak logical-word estimates 92070/92076. A deliberately non-pruning static backdoor enumerator at q=9 visited 512 leaves, reading 98778 literal records on each label; adaptive events were 1066 UNSAT and 1323 SAT. These event measures are not equivalent physical instructions, and comparisons are restricted policy experiments.

The local-nullity-two endpoint provides no such inequality.
