\begin{theorem}[Exact type restoration; PROVED/EXACT]
For every $F\in\mathcal C_{\rm M22}$ and original clause $C$, define $D_C(F)=(F\setminus\{C\})\land G_{\operatorname{sgn}(C)}(C)$ with private fresh auxiliaries. Then
\[D_C(F)\in\mathcal C_\star,\quad \operatorname{SAT}(D_C(F))\iff\operatorname{SAT}(F\setminus\{C\}),\quad (k',m')=(k+6,m+8).\]
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} Deletion removes one old signed occurrence from each of the three ports. The gadget restores each and gives each fresh variable two occurrences of each polarity. All new clauses are proper, monochromatic and distinct. A cross-port clause contains an old and a fresh name and has no negative complementary partner, establishing nonpaired membership. Every model of the retained source clauses extends by a gadget witness, and every completed model restricts to a model of the retained clauses. The negative case is symmetrical.
\begin{theorem}[Optimal local add-only size; PROVED/EXACT]
Any repair of a single deleted positive clause that retains all other original clauses and names, adds only fresh variables and proper unique monochromatic triples, and restores exact signed quotas either reinserts the original clause with no new variables, or requires at least six new variables and at least nine newly added clauses. $G_+$ attains both bounds.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} If $h$ is the number of fresh active names and $p,q$ are new positive and negative clause counts, signed incidence equations give $3p=3+2h$, $3q=2h$. Thus $h=3u$, $p=1+2u$, $q=2u$. For $u=0$ the deleted clause is the unique possible repair. For $u=1$, both negative triples must consist of the same three fresh names, violating distinctness. Therefore a genuine repair has $u\ge2$ and $h\ge6,p+q\ge9$.
\noindent\textbf{Mod-three charge lemma.} In each connected component $K$ of the newly added clause-variable incidence graph, the old deficit satisfies $\sum_{x\in K\cap V_{\rm old}}(d_x^+-d_x^-)\equiv0\pmod3$: every fresh name has zero net sign charge and each monotone triple contributes $\pm3$. After deleting one positive or negative clause, each of its three ports contributes charge $\pm1$; they must all occur in one newly added component. This rules out independent one-port or two-port repairs in this exact model.
