\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: acquisition is NP search under an explicit succinct evaluator.} Fix a declared legal encoder. Suppose legal states are strings $v\in\{0,1\}^s$ recognized by deterministic predicate $L(v)$, and given $v$ and $q\in\mathbb F_2^M$, the value $T_v(q)=B_vq$ is deterministically computable in polynomial time with polynomial output length. Define
\[P_q(v):=L(v)\wedge[T_v(q)\neq0].\]
Then $\exists v\,P_q(v)$ is in NP, with witness $v$. Its complement says that $q\in K_2(H)$, but is only a coNP statement from this syntax.

Under the project counterfactual, a fixed deterministic SAT decider $A$ runs in time $S^{k+o(1)}$. A model-matched Cook--Levin/Robson-style compilation of the verifier for $P_q$, followed by $A$, decides whether a violating legal state exists. Standard SAT self-reduction recovers such a $v$, when it exists, with polynomially many decision calls. Complementing $A$ handles the no-state case at the same exponent. Thus acquisition is not independently impossible under $P=NP$, but it is not free.

If a verifier of running time $T_q$ compiles to SAT size $T_q^{b+o(1)}$, the charged decision cost is
\[\boxed{T_q^{bk+o(1)}}\]
up to polynomially many self-reduction calls, provided those calls do not alter the leading exponent. Any proof ledger invoking a rank-growth oracle without this charge is invalid.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: at most $M$ successful rank-growth rounds.} Start with $K^{(0)}=\mathbb F_2^M$. At round $j$, choose nonzero $q_j\in K^{(j)}$. If acquisition returns legal $v_j$ with $B_{v_j}q_j\neq0$, update
\[K^{(j+1)}=K^{(j)}\cap\ker B_{v_j}.\]
Because $q_j\in K^{(j)}$ but $q_j\notin\ker B_{v_j}$,
\[\dim K^{(j+1)}\le\dim K^{(j)}-1.\]
Hence at most $M$ successful rounds occur. If a round returns NO, the current $q_j$ is a genuine global quadratic relation for the declared legal-state family.
