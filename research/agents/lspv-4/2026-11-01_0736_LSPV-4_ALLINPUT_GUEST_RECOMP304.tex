\section{New scoped theorem and rigorous proof}
Fix a correct uniform polynomial target-decider $A$ of time exponent $K$, \emph{conditionally if P=NP}. For any fixed positive integer $h$, let $A_h$ issue $M^h$ indexed input-bit queries cyclically, discard their answers and then run $A$. It remains an exact polynomial decider on every valid target and malformed string, with exponent $\max(K,h)$ up to polylog factors.

\begin{theorem}[Uncached original-source replay tariff]\label{thm:tariff}
On an infinite family of parent lengths $N$, suppose a particular faithful simulator of $A_h$ makes $B(N)\ge N^{\gamma-o(1)}$ target-valid child calls of lengths $M_i\ge N^{\lambda-o(1)}$, and its explicitly declared no-cache child-bit accessor performs $R(N)\ge N^{\chi-o(1)}$ \emph{fresh original-source reads on every guest query}, with no sharing, suppression or amortization. Then
\[
Q_{\rm original}\ge \sum_{i=1}^{B(N)} M_i^h R(N)
\ge N^{\gamma+\lambda h+\chi-o(1)}.
\]
On the fixed FLMV read-only input tape this entails at least that many native input-cell inspections up to the fixed number of read heads. If $\chi>0$, choose $h\ge K$ sufficiently large that the guest term dominates independent setup: this architecture cannot certify the proposed exponent $K'\le\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda \max(K,h)\}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The wrapper is correct because ignored reads do not change $A$'s answer. Every simulated query incurs the specified $R(N)$ fresh reads. Multiplying their quantities proves the bound. A fixed input tape cannot inspect an unbounded number of different cells in one native step. The strict exponent gap is $\chi$ when $h\ge K$. The conclusion is solely about the declared faithful no-cache simulation. A different compiler might remove redundant guest work, cache, batch or use a better local-bit accessor, and is not ruled out.
\end{proof}
No theorem here asserts that every canonical source must induce $N^\gamma$ children, or even that a universal canonical child generator exists. The finite demonstration below uses raw noncanonical cofactors and is therefore a component-control, \emph{not} an instance of the theorem's hereditary child premise.
