\section{Uniform source and canonical encoding}
For $q\ge1$ and $b\in\{0,1\}^q$, retain original variables
$z_0,\ldots,z_q$ and introduce $a,\beta,c,d$ plus $u_i,v_i,w_i$
for $0\le i\le q$.  For three \emph{distinct} variables,
$Q(x,y,z;e)$ is the conjunction of the four proper three-literal
clauses falsified by the assignments with $x\oplus y\oplus z\ne e$.
It is logically equivalent to $x\oplus y\oplus z=e$.

The Horn binary backbone is
\[
 B_{q,b}=(\neg a\vee\beta)\wedge(\neg\beta\vee a)
 \wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{q}(\neg z_{i-1}\vee L_i),\qquad
 L_i=\begin{cases}z_i&b_i=1,\\\neg z_i&b_i=0.\end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]
The five central parity equations (each encoded by its quartet $Q$) are
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
 a\oplus c\oplus z_0=1,&a\oplus c\oplus z_q=0,&
 a\oplus d\oplus z_0=1,\\
 \beta\oplus c\oplus d=0,&c\oplus z_0\oplus z_q=1.&
\end{array} \tag{2}
\]
For every $0\le i\le q$ add
\[
z_i\oplus u_i\oplus v_i=0,\qquad
z_i\oplus u_i\oplus w_i=0,\qquad
u_i\oplus v_i\oplus w_i=0. \tag{3}
\]
Let $K_{q,b}$ be the conjunction of all corresponding clauses.

\begin{theorem}[Counts, label, and exact witness map]
For every $q\ge1$ and every $b\in\{0,1\}^q$,
\[
\boxed{n=4q+8,\quad m_2=q+2,\quad k=12q+32,\quad M=13q+34,}
\tag{4}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{K_{q,b}\in\UNSAT\iff b=1^q.} \tag{5}
\]
Every satisfying source chain assignment with $z_0=1,z_q=0$ has
exactly one extension:
\[
 a=\beta=c=d=0,\qquad u_i=0,\qquad v_i=w_i=z_i. \tag{6}
\]
There are no free new auxiliary variables relative to a fixed original
$z$ assignment, and no original variables are eliminated.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Subtract central equations one and three to obtain $c=d$.
Equation four then gives $\beta=0$.
Equations one and two give $z_0\oplus z_q=1$; the fifth then gives
$c=d=0$. Hence $z_0=1\oplus a$, $z_q=a$.
The binary equivalence $a=\beta$ forces $a=0$, so
$z_0=1,z_q=0$.
Subtract local equations one and two to obtain $v_i=w_i$,
then local equation three gives $u_i=0$, and local equation one
gives $v_i=w_i=z_i$. If all $b_i=1$, the oriented chain propagates
$z_0=1$ to $z_q=1$, contradicting $z_q=0$.
If $b_j=0$, choose $z_i=1$ for $i<j$ and $z_i=0$ for $i\ge j$.
All constraints hold and (6) supplies the unique extension.
The formula counts follow from the explicitly enumerated variables,
$q+2$ binaries, five central quartets, and $3(q+1)$ local quartets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Backdoor, SCC quotient, and affine rank]
Let $E_q$ be the affine system encoded by all ternary quartets and
let $h$ be the minimum raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor,
equivalently the smallest variable hitting set of all ternary supports.
Then
\[
\boxed{h=q+3,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(E_q)=3q+8,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(E_q\cup\{a\oplus\beta=0\})=3q+9.}
\tag{7}
\]
The binary-only formula is SAT on both labels. Its implication SCC
recognizes $a\equiv\beta$, but no ternary contains both variables;
there are no initial implied binary units or contradictions, and
\emph{zero} ternary clauses shorten under SCC equivalence substitution.
The affine-only system is SAT on both labels and does not force either
$z_0$ or $z_q$. After the SCC equation is imported, exactly $q+11$
variables have forced values, and restricting them makes every
ternary clause binary or satisfied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The central five XOR rows have rank five, with the single free
parameter $a$ and the solutions
$\beta=c=d=0,z_0=1\oplus a,z_q=a$.
Adding $a\oplus\beta=0$ raises central rank to six.
For fixed $z_i$, the three local equations uniquely determine
$u_i,v_i,w_i$; thus each contributes rank three and no further
constraint on $z_i$. The ranks are $5+3(q+1)$ and $6+3(q+1)$,
leaving respectively $q$ and $q-1$ affine dimensions.
The forced values after merging SCC are six central variables,
$q+1$ local $u_i$ variables, and the four $v_i,w_i$ variables at
the two pinned endpoints: $q+11$ total. Every ternary contains at
least one of them. The binary signed chain contains no nontrivial
SCC equivalences between distinct $z$ variables. Its SCC with
$a\leftrightarrow\beta$ has no ternary support with repeated
equivalence class, so it shortens no ternary.

For the backdoor upper bound choose each $u_i$ and choose
$a,c$; this hits all local and central supports. The five
central supports have empty total intersection, so require at
least two selected variables. Each local block independently
requires at least one variable; its sole one-variable cover is
$u_i$. If the central choice uses $z_0$ or $z_q$, the corresponding
local block needs a second selected variable, since its third
support $(u_i,v_i,w_i)$ excludes $z_i$. Consequently no sharing
can save a selection and every hitting set has at least
$(q+1)+2=q+3$ variables.
\end{proof}

\section{State-adaptive policy and full resource ledger}
The tested algorithm is total, uniform, deterministic, and
label-blind. It parses and validates the entire CNF; uses
deterministic three-pass integer radix grouping to recognize complete
four-clause XOR quartets; constructs the binary implication graph,
runs SCC, and obtains certified signed equalities; adds the equalities
to a sparse $\mathbb F_2$ elimination procedure; restricts by
\emph{only} the derived unit consequences; runs SCC on the residual
if it is binary; and otherwise calls a total exact DPLL fallback.
If SAT, it verifies the reconstructed original-source witness.
UNSAT can instead terminate with an SCC contradiction.
Switches, failed recognition, parsing, output, and verification
are charged. The finite menu is an experimentally tested policy,
not a claim about every SAT algorithm.
