\section{Claim card, exact model, and three falsifiers}
The endpoint sought by this project is
\[
\forall A\ \text{total uniform deterministic exact}\quad
\forall c>0\ \forall N_0\ \exists F:
|F|=N\ge N_0,\quad T_A(F)>N^c. \tag{1}
\]
It remains \textbf{OPEN}. The source/target bit encoding is explicit signed-literal clause lists: four addressed words per clause, two header words, and word width
\(w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+M+1))\rceil\). One addressed word contains one signed literal or clause metadata field; sign flips do not alter record width. The RAM word width is \(\Theta(\log N)\). We count reads, comparisons, policy tests, copying, failed recognition, Gaussian/XOR operations, reconstruction, output and peak storage. No per-instance advice or uncharged decide-first compression is permitted.

\noindent\textbf{M1, PROVED/EXACT restricted:} For all \(t\ge3\), a 2-CNF \(B\) with one binary clause and an invertible \(t\)-coordinate GF(2) transformation has a syndrome image with \emph{every strict coordinate projection full}, a full affine hull, and a missing prescribed syndrome. Thus all proper marginals, even jointly queried at order \(t-1\), do not certify feasibility of that particular full syndrome.\\
\textbf{M2, PROVED/EXACT restricted:} A proper binary/ternary CNF realizes both SAT and UNSAT pinned syndromes at the same length \(N=\Theta(t\log t)\), with \(k=4t-4\) genuine ternaries and a deterministic exact recognizer/solver of \(O(t)\) word time. An all-decider \(t\)-word acquisition bound is matched by this solver.\\
\textbf{M3, FALSIFIED/RETIRED as an amplification claim:} Strict-projection completeness, growing syndrome rank, a growing raw syntactic backdoor and failure of an affine-hull test do not force superlinear post-acquisition work. The corresponding Boolean syndrome predicate is quadratic after a paid invertible change of basis.

\section{Exact high-order projection firewall}
For \(t\ge3\), let \(x=(x_1,\ldots,x_t)\), and let the ordinary proper 2-CNF be
\[
B_t(x)=(x_1\lor x_2).
\]
Define an invertible syndrome change of coordinates \(L_t\) by
\[
P(x)=\bigoplus_{j=3}^t x_j,\qquad
y_1=x_1\oplus P,\quad y_2=x_2\oplus P,\quad
y_j=x_j\quad(3\le j\le t).                  \tag{2}
\]
Its inverse, entirely specified by the observed syndrome \(b\), is
\[
P_b=\bigoplus_{j=3}^tb_j,\quad
x_1=b_1\oplus P_b,\quad x_2=b_2\oplus P_b,\quad
x_j=b_j\quad(j\ge3).                       \tag{3}
\]
Let \(\mathcal R_t=\{L_t x:x\models B_t\}\subseteq\mathbb F_2^t\).

\begin{theorem}[Order-\((t-1)\) projections are all full: PROVED/EXACT]
For every \(t\ge3\),
\[
|\mathcal R_t|=3\cdot2^{t-2},\qquad
\operatorname{aff}(\mathcal R_t)=\mathbb F_2^t,\qquad
0^t\notin\mathcal R_t,                           \tag{4}
\]
and for \emph{every} strict subset \(S\subsetneq[t]\),
\[
\pi_S(\mathcal R_t)=\mathbb F_2^{S}.             \tag{5}
\]
Every unit vector \(e_i\), \(1\le i\le t\), lies in \(\mathcal R_t\). The complement \(\mathbb F_2^t\setminus\mathcal R_t\) is a codimension-two linear subspace.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The invertible map (2) transports the three admissible pairs
\((x_1,x_2)\in\{01,10,11\}\) and leaves \(t-2\) free source bits, so
\(|\mathcal R_t|=3\cdot2^{t-2}\). The image is the set of \(b\) obeying
\[
a(b)\lor c(b)=1,\quad
a=b_1\oplus P_b,\quad c=b_2\oplus P_b.         \tag{6}
\]
Its complement is given by the two \emph{independent} homogeneous equations
\(a=c=0\); in particular \(0^t\) is excluded. The allowed original points
\(e_1,e_1+e_2,e_1+e_3,\ldots,e_1+e_t,e_2\)
include \(t+1\) affinely independent vectors; invertibility preserves their
affine span. For any fixed \(b\) on all but one coordinate \(j\), flipping
\(b_j\) changes at least one of \(a,c\): if \(j=1\) it changes \(a\), if \(j=2\)
it changes \(c\), and otherwise it changes both. Thus the two completions
cannot both satisfy \(a=c=0\), establishing every \((t-1)\)-coordinate
projection as surjective. Smaller projections inherit surjectivity. Finally
\(b=e_1\) gives \((a,c)=(1,0)\), \(e_2\) gives \((0,1)\), and every \(e_j\) for
\(j\ge3\) gives \((1,1)\). \(\square\)
\end{proof}
The image-membership predicate is a Boolean quadratic polynomial:
\[
\mathbf1_{\mathcal R_t}(b)=a(b)+c(b)+a(b)c(b)\pmod2.  \tag{7}
\]
High marginal order therefore does not imply high algebraic degree or expensive decision. This is a source-checkable \emph{counterexample}, not a candidate machine-universal lower-bound invariant.

\section{Proper mixed-CNF realization, fibers and all charges}
For \(b\in\mathbb F_2^t\), introduce source variables \(x_1,\dots,x_t\),
syndrome variables \(y_1,\dots,y_t\), \(t-3\) prefix variables computing
\(P=x_3\oplus\cdots\oplus x_t\), and one fresh shared pin variable \(u\).
Retain the binary source clause \((x_1\lor x_2)\). Add two binary clauses
for \(x_j\leftrightarrow y_j\), \(3\le j\le t\). Build the prefix with
\(t-3\) functional XOR3 gates, and append the gates
\(y_1=x_1\oplus P\) and \(y_2=x_2\oplus P\). Encode each XOR3 gate by the
four proper ternaries excluding assignments of incorrect parity. For each
\(j\), let \(\lambda_j=y_j\) if \(b_j=1\) and \(\lambda_j=\neg y_j\) otherwise, and insert
\[
(\lambda_j\lor u)\land(\lambda_j\lor\neg u).  \tag{8}
\]
These two proper binary clauses force \(y_j=b_j\) without an explicit unit.
They leave \(u\) free on SAT fibers. Denote the complete formula \(G_{t,b}\).
All identifiers, clause ordering, clause widths and headers are independent
of \(b\); only the two sign words in each pin group change.

\begin{theorem}[Realization, exact labels and resources: PROVED/EXACT]
For \(t\ge3\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
n&=3t-2, &m_2&=4t-3,&k&=4t-4,&M&=8t-7,\\
w&=\left\lceil\log_2(64(11t-8))\right\rceil,&
N&=(32t-26)w=\Theta(t\log t).
\end{aligned}                                                  \tag{9}
\]
There are exactly \(t\) original variables, \(2t-3\) functional
auxiliaries (\(t\) syndrome and \(t-3\) prefix), and one free variable \(u\);
no source variables are eliminated (\(d=0\)). The exact number of target models is
\[
\#SAT(G_{t,b})=
\begin{cases}2,& b\in\mathcal R_t,\\0,&b\notin\mathcal R_t.\end{cases}         \tag{10}
\]
Thus \(G_{t,0^t}\) is UNSAT, while every \(G_{t,e_j}\) is SAT, with the same input length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are \(t-3+2=t-1\) XOR3 gates, each introducing four distinct
ternaries, and there are \(1+2(t-2)+2t=4t-3\) binaries.
The source assignment is uniquely recovered from \(y=b\) by (3).
Every XOR prefix variable is then uniquely fixed; \(u\) may take either
Boolean value. The binary source clause accepts precisely when (6) holds.
All counts follow directly. \(\square\)
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Explicit construction and transport ledger.}
The input-to-CNF compiler uses \(O(t)\) word time, including all \(t\)
pin reads, \(t-1\) XOR gate constructions and \(O(t)\) emitted words.
The canonical recognizer independently re-generates and compares the
complete signed-literal list, so parsing, failed pattern tests, shortcut
discovery, rule selection, and even redundant reconstruction are charged.
After recognition, (3) determines a SAT witness or a canonical restricted
UNSAT contradiction in \(O(t)\) word time; certificate or witness
verification scans the source record in \(O(t)\), with \(O(t)\) peak words.
The generic policy falls back to SCC for 2-CNF, Horn propagation for Horn
CNF, GF(2) elimination for pure recognized XOR3 quartets, and exact DPLL
otherwise. This \emph{uniform computable policy} has no advance label
advice; only its canonical-family running time is claimed linear.
The fallback is exact but can be exponential for arbitrary 3-CNF.

\begin{theorem}[Matched input-word acquisition: PROVED/EXACT, restricted model]
For any total deterministic exact decider \(A\) of the full canonically
encoded mixed-CNF language, its execution on \(G_{t,0^t}\) must inspect
at least one of the two private pin-sign words belonging to each \(j\):
\[
\operatorname{wordreads}_A(G_{t,0^t})\ge t
 =\Omega(N/\log N).                           \tag{11}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If both signs in pin group \(j\) were unread, changing \(b_j=0\) to
\(b_j=1\) would leave the deterministic execution transcript unchanged,
while the exact SAT label changes by (4). A word contains only one signed
literal in the declared grammar; the \(t\) private pairs are disjoint.
\(\square\)
\end{proof}
This is not an encoding-independent \(\Omega(N)\) bit-cost theorem and
does not prove superlinear post-acquisition work. A pre-transform
materializing reduction spends \(\Theta(t)\) source reads/writes and cannot
transfer a positive amplified lower-bound remainder from a weaker
input-order source query floor.

\paragraph{Growing syntactic backdoor, but simple trajectory.}
There are \(t-1\) distinct XOR3 supports; each variable belongs to at
most three of them. Therefore the initial minimum raw strong syntactic
2-CNF backdoor obeys
\[
\lceil(t-1)/3\rceil\le h_2(G_{t,b})\le t-1.   \tag{12}
\]
The actual minimum is not computed or given as algorithmic advice.
Although the number \(k=4(t-1)\) grows, the strong-backdoor enumeration
upper bound \(2^{h_2}\operatorname{poly}(N)\) is only an upper bound, not
a mandatory runtime. The unrestricted \(k\)-ternary language contains
ordinary 3-SAT and is NP-complete; its canonical subfamily (9) is easy.
The binary padding control \((a\lor b\lor z)\land(a\lor b\lor\neg z)\)
preserves satisfiability but adds a free extension, not hardness.

\paragraph{Restricted-policy comparison.}
The fixed baseline deliberately enumerates and records every one of
\(2^t\) source assignments and its complete syndrome table; it is a
\emph{restricted full-table policy}, not the fastest fixed or adaptive
SAT solver. The tested hybrid policy chooses source-verified
normalization when the grammar matches and switches to SCC/Horn/Gaussian
or DPLL for general inputs. At \(t=11\) the table baseline visits
2048 assignments, while the hybrid incurs 522 counted logical events
for the UNSAT control and 731 for the SAT control. These event counters
include literal reads, emitted reconstruction words, XOR operations,
rule evaluations and switches, but are \emph{not} cycle-accurate CPU
instruction totals. At \(t=4096\), the hybrid incurs 208857 and 294851
events on UNSAT and SAT, respectively; it does not construct a full
syndrome table. Recognition at tiny \(t\) may have worse constants.
