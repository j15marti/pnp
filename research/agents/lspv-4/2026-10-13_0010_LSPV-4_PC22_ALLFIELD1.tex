\section{Recovered components and conventions}
The original LBO3SAT-0031/0032 and PROOF-0034 provide the four-clone occurrence-halving compiler, resolution projection, and minimality on the specified cubic Tseitin sources. The attached PC22\_TRANSFER1 report extends its input to variable-specific balanced sources: if variable $w$ occurs $r_w$ times positively and negatively, $r_w\ge2$, then with $R=\sum_w r_w$ the target has $6R$ variables and $8R$ clauses. It also supplies algebraic degree transport and the Tseitin-specific characteristic-two upper bound. These results are adopted; their restricted proof-system scope is retained.

A proper 3-clause uses three distinct variables. Intermediate formulas below are ordered occurrence lists, not sets; repetitions are intentional. Final clauses are distinct. Densely renumber active variables. The truth convention is $x=1$ for true. Clause equations over a fixed field are
\[
 p_D=\prod_{x\in D}(1-x)\prod_{\neg x\in D}x=0,
 \qquad B_x=x^2-x=0.
\]
PC uses linear combinations and multiplication by variables, with formal total degree. PCR also has complementary rails and $C_x=x+\bar x-1=0$. Size counts expanded monomial occurrences, not arithmetic circuit gates. Real equality-style SoS uses
$-1=\sum_j s_j^2+\sum_D h_Dp_D+\sum_x b_xB_x$, with degree measured on complete summands.

\section{All-input compiler and full proof}
\begin{theorem}
Every nonempty proper 3-CNF $F$ with $m$ clause occurrences has a uniform polynomial-time computable equisatisfiable proper, distinct-clause exact-$(2,2)$ formula $\mathcal T(F)$ with
\[
 v=72m,\qquad M=96m.
\]
There is an explicit literal/constant substitution projecting every clause axiom to a source clause, a signed Boolean axiom, or zero, and containing every source axiom among its images. For every UNSAT $F$ and every field $\mathbb F$,
\[
 \Deg_{\mathrm{PC}_{\mathbb F}}(F)\le\Deg_{\mathrm{PC}_{\mathbb F}}(\mathcal T(F)),\qquad
 \Deg_{\mathrm{PCR}_{\mathbb F}}(F)\le\Deg_{\mathrm{PCR}_{\mathbb F}}(\mathcal T(F)).
\]
PCR expanded proof size projects with $O(1)$ multiplicative overhead. Equality-style real SoS refutation degree also does not increase under projection. No general minimality or clause-monotonicity assertion is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write the source clauses as $(a_j\lor b_j\lor c_j)$, with letters denoting literals. Introduce a common selector $s$ and fresh $y_j,z_j$. For each clause emit
\begin{align*}
 &(s\lor a_j\lor y_j),&& (\neg y_j\lor b_j\lor c_j),\\
 &(\neg s\lor\neg a_j\lor z_j),&& (\neg z_j\lor\neg b_j\lor\neg c_j).
\end{align*}
Call the result $U(F)$. Eliminating the two auxiliaries restores a choice between $F(x)$ at $s=0$ and $F(\neg x)$ at $s=1$, which have the same satisfiability status. It does not demand that one assignment satisfy both.

If $x$ occurs $d_x$ times in $F$, it occurs $d_x$ times with each sign in $U(F)$. The selector has $m$ occurrences of each sign and each auxiliary one of each. Duplicate the whole list, forming $B(F)=U(F)\uplus U(F)$. Every active variable now has balanced counts at least two. There are $8m$ clauses and $R=12m$ occurrences of either sign in total. Deduplicating this intermediate list would invalidate the construction.

Pair each variable's positive and negative occurrences in list order and give each pair a fresh clone. There are $R$ clones. Put each variable's clones on one directed implication cycle, giving $R$ binary implications. Every clone now has two occurrences of each sign after the cycle is included.

Use the recovered fixed gadget $Q=W_1\setminus\{x_1\lor x_7\lor x_{13}\}$. It has 15 variables, 19 clauses, an all-zero model, and three forced-zero positive ports with one unused positive occurrence each. Pad every triple of cycle implications by one fresh $Q$ copy. Since $R=12m$, there are $R/3$ copies. Each implication becomes $(\neg u\lor v\lor p)$ with a distinct fresh port. Thus
\[
 v=R+15R/3=72m,\qquad M=8m+R+19R/3=96m.
\]
All target variables have exact signed degree $(2,2)$. The cycles have at least two distinct clones. All clauses remain proper. A given signed clone occurs in only one transformed source occurrence, so two transformed source clauses cannot coincide even though the intermediate list had duplicates. Padded clauses have distinct ports. Fresh gadget supports and the different clause types exclude other coincidences. Hence output clauses are distinct.

Every model of the target has all ports zero and equal clones on every cycle, so it projects to a model of $B(F)$. Conversely a source model lifts using $s=0$, $y_j=\neg a_j$, $z_j=0$, equal clones, and all-zero gadgets. This proves equisatisfiability.

Apply those same assignments symbolically after identifying clones. The first split clause has polynomial image $(1-a_j)a_j=-B_{\operatorname{var}(a_j)}$, and the second maps to $(1-a_j)(1-b_j)(1-c_j)=p_{D_j}$. The other two split clauses map to zero. A padded implication maps to $t(1-t)$ for a literal or constant $t$, hence a signed Boolean axiom or zero. Every internal $Q$ clause maps to zero since it contains a negative literal. Clone Boolean axioms map to source Boolean axioms or zero. In particular a projected semantic tautology is not silently discarded as the zero formal polynomial.

For PC, apply this ring homomorphism line by line. Linear combinations commute with substitution. Multiplication by a complemented source variable is implemented by deriving $xP$ and then $P-xP$. This needs no higher degree than the original multiplication step. Every image axiom is available in degree at most three; a refutation of a proper 3-CNF already uses a degree-three clause axiom. Final $1$ remains $1$.

For PCR, swap rails for a complemented image. A monomial maps to one monomial or zero, so it does not expand. The needed repairs have degree two and constant size:
\[
 x\bar x=xC_x-B_x,\qquad
 B_{\bar x}=(\bar x-x)C_x+B_x.
\]
All other inference images are the corresponding source inferences or removable repeated/zero lines. This gives both degree preservation and $O(S)$ expanded-size projection. Ordinary PC may expand a complemented product into exponentially many monomials, so no ordinary-PC size-preservation claim is made.

For SoS substitute into the certificate identity. Squares remain squares, and each axiom term becomes a source-axiom multiple, a Boolean-axiom multiple, or zero. Literal/constant images are affine, so complete-summand degrees do not increase. This proves the theorem.
\end{proof}

\section{One family hard over every field}
Let $G=(U\mathbin{\dot\cup}V,E)$ be a simple 4-regular bipartite graph with $h$ vertices on either side. Add an edge between two previously nonadjacent vertices, giving $G'$. Put one Boolean variable on each edge. For each left vertex include every positive triple of incident edges; for each right vertex include every negative triple. Denote the resulting proper 3-CNF by $\mathrm{SC}(G')$.

It has $4h+1$ variables and $8h+12$ clauses. The ordinary left vertices require at least two selected edges and the unique degree-five left vertex at least three. Every right vertex permits at most two. Therefore a model would require
\[
 |T|\ge2h+1\quad\hbox{and}\quad |T|\le2h.
\]
These local threshold constraints are not affine: the degree-four relation has eleven models.

In the retrieved 24-page version of Mik\v{s}a--Nordstr\"om, Theorem 4.8 states that if $G$ has bipartite vertex expansion
\[
 |N(X)|\ge(5/2+\delta)|X|\quad
 (X\subseteq U,\ |X|\le\gamma h)
\]
for fixed $\gamma,\delta>0$, then $\mathrm{SC}(G')$ has degree $\Omega(h)$ and exponential PC/PCR size. Section 2 specifies arbitrary field characteristic. Their Theorem 2.2 gives the size-degree tradeoff. These are the theorem numbers of the retrieved version, not substituted journal numbering. Appropriate graph families are supplied by the literature; no new deterministic explicit expander generator is claimed here.

\begin{corollary}
Applying $\mathcal T$ gives a single field-independent exact-$(2,2)$ family with
\[
 v_h=576h+864,\qquad M_h=768h+1152,
\]
and PC/PCR degree $\Omega(v_h)$ and expanded monomial size $2^{\Omega(v_h)}$ over every fixed field, including characteristic two.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Degree transfers by the theorem and $v_h=\Theta(h)$. Initial degree is three. The cited size-degree bound gives
$S\ge\exp(\Omega((\Deg-3)^2/v_h))=2^{\Omega(v_h)}$.
For standard indexed input length $L_h=\Theta(v_h\log v_h)$ this is $2^{\Omega(L_h/\log L_h)}$, not automatically $2^{\Omega(L_h)}$.
\end{proof}
The field is fixed during each refutation. Hybrid systems are not covered. The new family is not asserted MU or clause-monotone. It does not retract the old Tseitin family's short characteristic-two proofs. No new SoS lower bound is asserted for this replacement source.

\section{A constructive algorithmic escape}
For an explicitly supplied bipartite graph, the positive/negative triple constraints reduce to integral maximum flow. Source-to-left capacities are $\max\{d(u)-2,0\}$, graph-edge capacities are one, and right-to-sink capacities are two. Feasibility means saturating all left quotas. Indeed a satisfying edge set can be trimmed to exact left quotas without violating right upper bounds, yielding an integral flow; conversely such a flow supplies a satisfying edge set. Integral augmenting paths give a polynomial-time algorithm. For $G'$ the quota is $2h+1$ and total right capacity is $2h$; without the extra edge a 2-factor of the 4-regular bipartite graph is a satisfying solution.

The new compiler has a checked canonical inverse. From $M=96m$ recover $R=12m$. The $R$ padded-cycle clauses after the $8m$ transformed-source prefix reveal directed clone cycles and hence parent variables. There are $n+1+2m$ parent groups, recovering the original variable count. Collapse the prefix, check its duplicate halves and every four-clause selector template, extract $F$, rebuild $\mathcal T(F)$, and compare every clause including every $Q$ copy. Reject this fast path on any mismatch.

On canonical subset-cardinality inputs, parse positive then negative blocks as all triples on four or five variables. Four-variable blocks have four clauses; five-variable blocks have ten, and their first four lexicographic triples already have five-variable support. Verify each edge label's two endpoints, simplicity, degree counts, and the unique added edge. This establishes the integer contradiction after polynomial work on every canonical member. No supplied layout is needed. It is not an inverse for every arbitrary renaming or clause permutation.

Use exhaustive SAT search when the verified fast path fails. The resulting total uniform exact decider is polynomial on this canonical all-field-PC/PCR-hard family, but not proved polynomial on all inputs. Thus field-independent proof complexity does not supply an arbitrary-algorithm lower bound. This is a checked counterexample to promoting the new benchmark's proof size to its decision time, not a $P=NP$ algorithm.
