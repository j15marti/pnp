\section{Exact recovered Boolean gadgets}
\begin{lemma}[False pin, minimal within this four-clause template, \EXACT]
Let
\[
 P(f,r,s)=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}
 (\neg f\vee r^{\epsilon}\vee s^{\delta}),
 \qquad r^{1}=r,\quad r^{-1}=\neg r.
\]
Then $P(f,r,s)$ is true exactly when $f=0$, independently of $r,s$.
Deleting any one of its four clauses admits $f=1$ and some $r,s$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $f=0$, every displayed clause is true.  If $f=1$, the four
clauses list every possible binary disjunction of $r$ and $s$, one of which
is false for every pair $(r,s)$.  After deletion of one clause, choose
$(r,s)$ that falsifies exactly the deleted clause.  Then the remaining
three are all true.  With source literal row $(a,b,c)=(0,0,0)$, setting
$t=f=1$ makes both $\NAE(a,b,t)$ and $\NAE(\neg t,c,f)$ true,
so the truncated pin block admits a spurious model.\qed
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Boolean selector tetrahedron, \EXACT]
For three signed literals $p_1,p_2,p_3$ on distinct variables, introduce
selectors $M_1,M_2,M_3$ and impose: exactly one $M_j$ is true;
$M_j\Rightarrow p_j$; and
$M_j\Rightarrow\neg(p_k\land p_l)$ for the two other ports.
Encode exactly-one with one positive ternary clause and three at-most-one
binary clauses; encode the selected-port implications by three binaries;
encode the unselected-pair implications by three proper ternaries.
Replace each of the six binaries $D$ by
$(D\vee q)\land(D\vee\neg q)$ using a fresh $q$.
The result has nine auxiliaries (three selectors plus six padders), sixteen
proper three-literal clauses, and projects exactly to $\NAE(p_1,p_2,p_3)$.
For a fixed satisfying triple with $k$ true ports, it has exactly $k$
matching selectors ($k=1,2$) and $2^6 k$ full extensions.
Every selector has eight occurrences, every padder two occurrences, and
every literal port four occurrences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The selected matching index must be true and must leave at least one
other literal false.  Such a choice exists exactly when the three literal
values are neither all zero nor all one.  Every satisfied binary clause
has exactly two extensions in its new padding variable, while a false
binary has none.  The count and properness follow by inspection of the
six original binary and four original ternary clauses.  For one selector
$M_j$: one at-least-one occurrence, four from the two padded at-most-one
clauses, two from the padded selected-port clause, and one from the
unselected-pair clause, totaling eight.\qed
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Recovered $T_{\rm loc}$, \EXACT]
For every proper signed 3-CNF $F=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)$
with $n$ source variables and $m\ge1$ clause occurrences, let $T_{\rm loc}(F)$
introduce fresh $t_i,f_i,r_i,s_i$, impose $P(f_i,r_i,s_i)$, and add a fresh
selector tetrahedron encoding of each of
\[
 \NAE(a_i,b_i,t_i),\qquad\NAE(\neg t_i,c_i,f_i).
\]
Then $T_{\rm loc}$ has
\[
\boxed{N=n+22m,\quad M=36m,\quad \max\operatorname{occ}_{\rm aux}=8.}
\]
Every source model extends to a target model and every target model
restricts to a source model.  Target source-variable occurrence is exactly
four times source occurrence.  The compiled indexed bit length and witness
maps take $O((n+m)\log(n+m))$ bits/time with conventional indexing.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pin lemma forces $f_i=0$.  Direct case analysis yields
\[
 a\vee b\vee c\iff\exists t\,[\NAE(a,b,t)\land\NAE(\neg t,c,0)].
\]
The two matching-selector gadgets have independent internal variables,
so their existential projections compose.  Per source clause, the four
local variables $t,f,r,s$ and eighteen matching-gadget auxiliaries give
22 variables.  Two groups of sixteen proper clauses plus the four pin
clauses give 36; all introduced names are fresh, even on repeated clauses.
The splitter $t$ occurs four times in each matching gadget, hence eight;
$f$ occurs four times in its matching gadget and four times in the pin;
selectors occur eight times; $r,s$ occur four times; padding variables twice.
Thus the maximum auxiliary occurrence is exactly eight.  Source literal
ports each occur four times per original occurrence.
A witness is lifted by choosing an accepted $t$ for every source clause,
$f=0$, arbitrary $r,s$, a legal selector per NAE and arbitrary padding bits;
projection discards all auxiliaries.\qed
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact cut and face-free port ledger, \EXACT{} in declared model]
For \emph{every} partition of the source clause occurrences into $L$ and
$R$, the common Boolean variables of the compiled blocks are precisely
$\operatorname{vars}(F_L)\cap\operatorname{vars}(F_R)$.
In particular, $6\to6$ and $8\to8$ source Boolean boundaries hold without
a laminar-hierarchy assumption.  If each of the $2m$ NAE subclauses is
represented by a fresh four-port $K_4$ cell consisting of its three literal
port variables plus a private center, then two such cells intersect in
at most two ports (even when source clauses repeat).  The number of such
cells is $2m$ and their $K_4$ edge incidences with multiplicity are $12m$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Every auxiliary of block $i$ is fresh and appears only in block $i$.
Thus the only potential cross-block shared variables are the original
source variables.  Each first NAE subclause has exactly two original
source variables and one block-local splitter; each second subclause has
exactly one original source variable and two block-local variables.
Within a block, the pair shares only its splitter.  Private centers do not
create any additional intersection.  In particular no pair has a common
three-literal face.\qed
\end{proof}

\section{New exact weighted matching-quotient fiber}
Let $w(a,b,c)$ be the number of pairs of allowed matching selectors,
summed over both choices of splitter $t$, with $f=0$.  Exhaustive local
case splitting gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
(a,b,c)&000&001&010&011&100&101&110&111\\\hline
w(a,b,c)&0&1&1&4&1&4&2&4
\end{array}
\]
The two matching gadgets together contain twelve binary padding variables.
The local pin adds two independent bits $r,s$.  Therefore every allowed
selector/splitter pattern has exactly $2^{14}$ extensions.

\begin{theorem}[Nonparsimonious but exact model-count formula, \EXACT]
Let $\ell_i(x)$ denote the ordered three-literal truth tuple of source
clause $i$, and define
\[
 Z_F=\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^n}\prod_{i=1}^m w(\ell_i(x)).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\#\SAT(T_{\rm loc}(F))=2^{14m}Z_F.}
\]
For a single positive source clause, $\#\SAT(F)=7$,
$Z_F=17$, and $\#\SAT(T_{\rm loc}(F))=17\cdot2^{14}$.
Thus local matching multiplicity is not constant and is not a lower bound
resource: permuting the literals of a clause can change $w$ from two to four
while preserving the clause's Boolean relation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pin has four assignments $(r,s)$ when $f=0$; each matching gadget has
six arbitrary pad bits for any chosen valid selector.  Their per-clause
auxiliary sets are disjoint, so local weights multiply for fixed source
$x$.  Summing all source assignments proves the formula.  The example
follows by summing the seven positive local weights.
\end{proof}

\section{Reversible nonlinear feedback with actual recovered REVFIX2}
For a proper source $G$ with $Q\ge2$ clauses, set $v_j$ to each gate-encoded
clause-violation bit and let $a_j$ be the underlying variable of the first
literal of the $j$th source clause.  The recovered two-shear network is
\[
 t_0=v_0,\quad t_j=v_j\oplus(a_j\land v_{j-1})\ (1\le j<Q),
 \qquad h_0=t_0\oplus(a_0\land t_{Q-1}),\ h_j=t_j\ (j>0).
\]
Introduce feedback flags $y_j$ and require
$y_j\oplus h_j=y_j$.
The map $v\mapsto h$ is bijective for every fixed vector $a$:
recover $t_j=h_j$ for $j>0$, then
$t_0=h_0\oplus(a_0\land t_{Q-1})$, and then
$v_0=t_0$ and $v_j=t_j\oplus(a_j\land v_{j-1})$ successively.
Hence $h=0\iff v=0$.  The reversible flag update is a translation
and is an involution.  Standard AND, XOR and equality Tseitin clauses,
including pair-padding every binary clause, produce exactly $14Q$
additional variables and $27Q$ proper ternary clauses, with $9Q$
free feedback/padding bits on each satisfying input $G$-assignment.

\begin{corollary}[REVFIX2 after local pin, \EXACT{} Boolean composition]
Set $G=T_{\rm loc}(F)$, with $Q=36m\ge36$.  This avoids the $Q=1$ cyclic
cancellation defect.  For every proper source $F$,
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\#\mathrm{variables}(R_{\rm cyc}(G))&=n+526m,\\
\#\mathrm{clauses}(R_{\rm cyc}(G))&=972m,\\
\#\SAT(R_{\rm cyc}(G))&=2^{338m}Z_F.
\end{aligned}}
\]
The alternative anchored, non-wraparound REVFIX encoding gives
$n+526m-4$ variables, $972m-9$ clauses, and $2^{338m-2}Z_F$ models.
All target SAT decisions are the same as the source $F$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Substitute $N_G=n+22m$, $Q=36m$ into the recovered ring ledger
$(N_G+14Q,27Q,2^{9Q}\#\SAT(G))$, and then apply the weighted-fiber
theorem.  The anchored formula follows by using its
$(N_G+14Q-4,27Q-9,2^{9Q-2}\#\SAT(G))$ ledger.\qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Hostile controls and boundary limitation.}
The naive direct cyclic use on just one violation bit has
$h_0=v_0\oplus(a_0 v_0)$, which vanishes incorrectly at
$v_0=a_0=1$; the present composition is valid because $Q=36m\ge2$.
For a contiguous source-block partition the cyclic feedback links have
two across-block dependencies (one at the linear-chain boundary and the
wraparound edge).  For $m$ even clause blocks in alternating sides they
have $m$ across-block dependencies.  This counts explicit raw circuit
couplings, \emph{not} minimum graph separator size and not a solver lower
bound.  It prevents promoting the source's $6\to6$ Boolean cut ledger
unchanged through the raw feedback circuit without another placement/
elimination theorem.  Directly decoding the typed gates is polynomial-time.
