## ITERATE: theorem 501.1 (PROVED/RESTRICTED exact-configuration replay)

Condition on existence of any uniform total three-label polynomial SAT decider B. For fixed integer k, construct B_mark on arbitrary binary input x of length L. A deterministic, bounded public-header recognizer optionally locates r=O(log L) marker bits; B_mark reads and retains them in an immutable r-bit WORK register, increments a counter through K=L^k distinct states, then runs B(x) unchanged. Bad/short headers simply skip the marker; B(x) still decides SAT/UNSAT/MALFORMED. Thus B_mark is total, uniform, polynomial time, with extra O(log L) writable/index bits and at most O(L^k polylog L) extra charged transitions.

Take q=2^r equal-length inputs with distinct observed binary markers j. Authenticated deterministic checkpoint replay may share read-prefix transitions. The entire binary prefix trie contains exactly 2q-1 distinct prefix states, 2q-2 edges, versus q*r naive bit reads. But after all r marker reads, the immutable WORK register differs across every pair of children. During the subsequent K counter transitions the marker remains unchanged and counter values do not repeat. Hence the union of all *actual exact machine configurations* contains q(K+1) distinct post-marker states and at least **qK distinct paid post-marker transitions**, even with free optimal checkpoint selection and shared-DAG reconvergence whenever legal. This is a lower bound on *faithful exact-configuration replay*, not on code-aware semantic compilation or arbitrary SAT algorithms. Semantic removal of dummy loops, nonnative state quotienting or genuine SAT shortcuts are explicitly unruled-out.

## Family/theorem/encoding/known-method/transfer ledger

Established family: odd-charge Tseitin on connected cubic expanders G_H, original 4H proper signed three-literal clauses on 3H/2 edge variables, encoding Theta(H log H). Total odd charge implies UNSAT. Urquhart (1987) and Ben-Sasson/Wigderson (2001) establish exponential lower bounds in ordinary shared-DAG Resolution on appropriate expanding families. Append r=ceil(log2 q)=O(log H) *positive fresh-variable* proper clauses at the FRONT; each clause selects between two adjacent fresh variable IDs according to one marker bit, and all fresh clauses are satisfied by setting every fresh variable TRUE. Restrict any Resolution refutation by this TRUE assignment: padded clauses vanish, leaving original Tseitin, so min Resolution size cannot fall. Ignoring padding gives the reverse bound: exact ordinary Resolution size is preserved. This does NOT imply mixed R_mix341/ER/GF(2)/all-machine hardness. Valid shortcuts include source-visible GF(2), extension variables and shared reasoning and remain permitted.

Take q_H=2^floor((6/5)log2 H)=Theta(H^(6/5)), child M=Theta(H log H), formal parent N=M². Then q=N^(3/5-o(1)), M=N^(1/2), and writing q explicit children costs Theta(qM)=N^(11/10-o(1)); there is NO parent SAT decomposition here. In general cubic sources, n=3H/2+4r and m=4H+r, so candidate header reconstruction H=(8m-2n)/29, r=m-4H is uniform, bounded and checked; invalid headers fall back to B. The finite program uses a fixed-width 16-bit n/m header purely for bounded tests, not as an unbounded complexity-family serialization.

## Resource ledger and unresolved implications

Source parsing, input INDEX addressing, range checking and marker retention cost O(log M) bits and O(polylog M) per observed marker; K counter increments cost Ω(K) and at most O(K polylog M), including INDEX head resets. Guest B costs its entire original time/space.
