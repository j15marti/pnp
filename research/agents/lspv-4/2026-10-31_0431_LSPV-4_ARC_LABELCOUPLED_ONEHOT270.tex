\section{Exact seven-variable, both-label original-source family}
Fix an even integer $m\ge4$, an index $h\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$, and seven \emph{original} variables $x,u,v,a,b,c,d$, numbered $1,\ldots,7$. Define the eight source-indexed clauses
\[
G(u,v,a,b,c,d)=\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{\pm1\}}
(\neg u\vee\epsilon a\vee\delta b)
\ \wedge\
\bigwedge_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{\pm1\}}
(\neg v\vee\epsilon c\vee\delta d).
\]
Here $\epsilon a$ denotes a positive literal for $\epsilon=+1$ and negative otherwise. Each four-clause block entails respectively $\neg u$ and $\neg v$, by resolving once on each of its two private variables. Let $s_h=+1$ if $h$ is odd and $s_h=-1$ if $h$ is even, and let
\[
F_{m,h}=G\ \wedge\
\bigwedge_{j\ne h}^{m}(x\vee u\vee v)\
\wedge\
(\neg x\vee s_hu\vee v).
\tag{1}
\]
Every original clause is proper width three, and original indices are retained. Some distinct original indices have \emph{duplicate clause content}; the formula is not an exact-$(2+,2-)$ instance and not an unrestricted hard family. For a fixed-width signed-record grammar, $N=\Theta(m)$ and the physical length of every $F_{m,h}$ with fixed $m$ is exactly the same; all differences lie in the marked source record, not in its header or total length. The seven-variable source makes this linear rather than the $\Omega(N/\log N)$ variable-ID regime of predecessor ONEHOT253.

\begin{lemma}[Exact paired source/child semantics]
Every $F_{m,h}$ has exactly $16$ satisfying full assignments when $h$ is even and none when $h$ is odd. Under $x=1$, every unmarked source clause is satisfied. Introduce one fresh variable $w$ and define the ordinary proper 3CNF
\[
C_h=G\land(s_hu\vee v\vee w)\land(s_hu\vee v\vee\neg w).
\tag{2}
\]
For every $h$, $F_{m,h}\in\SAT$ iff $C_h\in\SAT$, and
$F_{m,h}\in\UNSAT$ iff $C_h\in\UNSAT$. The child has $10$ proper clauses and fixed physical length under the declared encoding, independent of $m$ and $h$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The gadget $G$ forces $u=v=0$, independently of the four private variables. Because $m\ge4$, at least one unmarked $(x\vee u\vee v)$ forces $x=1$. The marked clause then evaluates to $(s_hu\vee v)$, which is false for odd $h$ and true for even $h$. In the even case, $a,b,c,d$ are arbitrary, giving $2^4=16$ witnesses. In (2), existentially eliminating $w$ gives exactly $(s_hu\vee v)$, since $(P\vee w)\land(P\vee\neg w)\equiv P$. The gadget constraints are unchanged. Thus both labels are preserved, and an even child has $2^6=64$ complete assignments when counting its declared variables including unused $x$ and free $w$. No SAT oracle is used in the construction.
\end{proof}

\section{A restricted SAT-label-coupled bit-probe theorem}
Let a deterministic exact policy be promised that its original indexed source is some $F_{m,h}$ with $m$ even but hidden $h$. The only source-dependent information is obtained through charged indexed source-bit probes. Source-dependent preprocessing summaries, head/address state and all cached bits are \emph{not} free; a later continuation may adaptively probe more source bits. Its requested physical output bit is the sign of the first literal of the first child clause in (2). That bit determines the SAT versus UNSAT label for this promise. A source probe to any fixed field outside the original marked-record positions gives the same bit for all $h$. A query to a marked-record field, with the sign-record alignment known, is at most as informative as an oracle telling whether one candidate index equals $h$.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted label-coupled one-hot acquisition floor]
For any exact deterministic two-stage policy with $P$ preprocessing and $Q$ continuation source-bit probes in the worst case, whose combined persisted information depends only on inspected bits,
\[
  P+Q\ge m/2=\Omega(N).
\tag{3}
\]
The bound is tight for this one physical output bit. It does not constrain unrestricted SAT algorithms, and it does not imply $\Omega(m/2)$ \emph{new} probes at every repeated query when an authenticated summary can be retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Consider the transcript in which every inspected marker-sign location returns unmarked. After fewer than $m/2$ distinct candidates have been excluded, there are still an uninspected odd and an uninspected even candidate index. Choosing either as $h$ produces the same full observed transcript, including any deterministic source-derived summary and head state, but opposite required physical child-bit values and opposite SAT labels. Exactness would fail. Probing all $m/2$ even indices gives a matching policy: if the one mark is found the child bit identifies even; otherwise it identifies odd. All preprocessing source probes and carried information are charged.
\end{proof}

This is a \emph{promise-family input-acquisition} theorem. Recognition of the grammar on arbitrary inputs, including the forcing gadgets, must scan and check the source and can take $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit work with small counters. A policy may simply recognize the promised source and decide SAT without calling the child. Prior LSPV-1 ONEHOT253 gave a source-probe theorem for a marker-indexed short-child bit but did not couple the source SAT label to the mark. Prior LSPV-4 PIN-DISCOVERY-GATE268 gave a $\Omega(N/\log N)$ block-pin acquisition floor on a different balanced parity grammar. The present fixed-variable one-hot coupling is a different restricted control, not a new general SAT lower bound.

For $h$ odd, resolve the unmarked original $(x\vee u\vee v)$ against $(\neg x\vee u\vee v)$ to obtain the \emph{non-tautological} resolvent $(u\vee v)$, falsified by $u=v=0$, already derived independently from the eight original gadget clauses. This is a sound UNSAT certificate with original indices and a short resolution DAG. For $h$ even, the corresponding resolvent $(u\vee\neg u\vee v)$ is tautological and cannot prohibit a cube. The complete assignment $x=1,u=v=0$ with arbitrary private variables is a checked SAT witness.

\section{Do not multiply cold-query costs without a cold-state hypothesis}
Let $q$ distinguishable guest input-read requests for the same child sign bit be made. In a declared \emph{cold stateless} adapter that loses all source-derived state between requests, (3) gives $q m/2$ indexed source-sign probes in the worst-case independently reset model. This is a bound on that \emph{implementation restriction} only. A single paid scan of the original source, retaining one authenticated parity-of-marker bit plus $O(\log m)$ addressing state, reduces subsequent queries to $O(1)$ elementary bit retrieval overhead each. A child physically emitted to a work-tape buffer requires paying for the entire buffer and its non-free indexed-input simulation. Source-probe counts, word operations, work/index tape movements, reset/scrubbing, and peak space are separate ledgers.

The constant-length 10-clause child in (2) cannot make a fixed guest issue $q(N)$ indexed queries solely because the parent length $N$ grows. To give the wrapper adversary a genuinely growing ordinary input, append
\[
p(N)=\left\lfloor\frac{\sqrt N}{\log_2(N+2)}\right\rfloor
\tag{4}
\]
independent all-positive proper ternary clauses on fresh variables to $C_h$. Their conjunct is satisfiable and independent of the core, hence does not change either label. Under standard explicit fixed-slot fields whose IDs use $O(\log p)$ bits, the resulting physical child has
$M=\Theta(\sqrt N)$ bits asymptotically. Define a fixed guest $B_A$ that, on every ordinary input of physical length $M$, performs $\lceil M^{6/5}\rceil$ well-formed indexed dummy reads of the same child sign bit (with address writes and index erasure paid) and then invokes \emph{the supplied exact guest} $A$ unchanged. It preserves both SAT/UNSAT on every ordinary guest input and is polynomial if $A$ is polynomial. The cold per-read reconstruction implementation pays
\[
 \Omega\!\left(m M^{6/5}\right)
 =\Omega\!\left(N^{8/5-o(1)}\right)
\tag{5}
\]
raw source probes on this promise family. The once-authenticated cache avoids the multiplication. Therefore (5) is neither an all-input lower bound nor a barrier to a sufficiently designed ARC compiler. It is an exact restriction-specific falsifier of \emph{free repeated cold access}.
