## Exact-one coloring without padding
For simple graph G, vertex variables x_v,c and private edge slack s_e,c satisfy integer exact-one equations sum_c x_v,c=1 and x_u,c+x_v,c+s_e,c=1. On Boolean inputs this is exactly proper 3-coloring; each coloring has unique slack extension. Q=3n+3m variables, M=n+3m rows, 4M ordinary CNF clauses (one positive triple, three negative pairs per row). Distinct private slack pivots and disjoint vertex rows imply rank M in EVERY field, nullity 2n, hence no nontrivial row dependency or charge obstruction. All-1/3 feasible in LP. Integer witness x_v,1=1, other x=0; s_e,1=-1, other slack=1, so all moduli consistent. Split positive ternary XOR rows vs binary exclusions: former consistent, latter SAT at all zero. Eliminate slack and vertex parity, use free coordinates (1,0),(0,1),(0,0), retain nonlinear domain and edge inequalities. Source graph recognition from supplied graph straightforward; arbitrary CNF provenance detection not assumed.

## Bipartite affine saturation
For any bipartite edge region H including isolated vertices, begin with two-coloring using colors 1,2. Recolor one vertex to 3, valid, yielding a difference in one of its two free coordinate directions. Globally swap colors 1,2 and repeat to obtain its other direction. The span of differences of proper colorings thus has dimension 2n. Slack coordinates are affine determined over GF(2). Therefore ALL affine consequences of a bipartite region are already in its base affine equations. No local extra affine contraction, independent of the search procedure used. If girth g, every subset of fewer than g edges is a forest, so the same result holds for every such source-local deduction. More generally odd girth suffices for bipartiteness of small edge regions. This does not preclude affine deductions on larger or non-bipartite supports or conditional branches.

## Strong LP support obstruction
Every 3-colorable region has uniform all-1/3 in the convex hull of its satisfying Boolean assignments: average the six global permutations of one coloring (slacks are the remaining color). For an edge-minimal non-3-colorable H, every proper edge subset is colorable, including with all vertex domain constraints retained. Consequently any valid linear inequality derived solely from any proper subset of edge bundles (on its observed variables) holds at the uniform point. This extends to the union of ALL such inequalities, including cutting planes of arbitrary local rank and arbitrary support size less than full graph. If retaining unselected slack variables without their bundle constraints, assign them independently with mean 1/3. A CP refutation whose lines carry source-edge provenance must have its first inequality violated by the uniform point supported on the entire edge set. This is a support restriction, not a proof-size or time lower bound. Global CP can combine local cuts, and Boolean branching may destroy the uniform symmetry. Every edge-deleted neighbor SAT by minimality. Existence at arbitrarily large girth (even or odd thresholds) follows Erdos high-girth high-chromatic graphs; take edge-minimal non-3-colorable subgraph, remove isolates. Actual minimal graph discovery by deletion uses SAT calls, not a free polynomial generator. Reference Osthus et al. 2001 doi:10.1002/jgt.1017.

## Unpadded algebraic cut lower bound and expander existence

For edge partition A,B, row-space intersection dimension exactly 2|S|, S=vertices incident to both. Vertex-domain factors if separate cannot reduce this bound. Invertible change of basis does not change it. For d-regular graph with edge expansion h>0 (|delta U|>=h|U| for |U|<=n/2), any balanced edge cut |A|,|B|>=m/3 has |P|+|S|,|Q|+|S|>=n/3, where P,Q exclusive incident vertices. If |S|>=n/6 done; otherwise P,Q each >=n/6, smaller <=n/2, and has all its exiting edges into S. Hence d|S|>=h*n/6. Every subcubic leaf decomposition has a balanced edge cut (centroid argument); if vertex factors are leaves too, use edge leaf weights. Therefore w>=2 min(1,h/d)n/6 = Omega(n) for fixed d,h. ## Constructive repair: exact forest projection
For any graph and designated boundary B, a private vertex of degree <=2 can be existentially removed along with incident edges: every coloring of remaining vertices leaves at least one of three colors available. No fill constraint needed; recover a witness by reversing removal. Iterate by queue in O(n+m) graph word operations, or poly bit overhead. On a forest, final private vertices all have degree >=3. For each nontrivial component with b boundary and i private, 3i+b <= 2(i+b-1) implies i<=b-2. Isolated boundary nodes retained, no private isolated nodes. Thus final size <= b+max(0,b-2), O(b) edges, exact existential compatibility including for empty/singleton B. This symbolic message may be much smaller than 3^b table. Boundary constraints in remaining graph are retained; auxiliary branching vertices not eliminated symbolically may require further solving. General 3-core can be arbitrarily large, and conjunction of forest messages can reproduce arbitrary graph coloring (disjoint single-edge messages). No universal closure claim. This operator specifically helps where local affine saturation stalls. Charge finding source regions separately; degree peel itself linear, each edge removed once. Exact message proof via reverse extension, replay linear graph operations. For explicit N-bit graph/source encoding and supplied region, a conservative sequential bit model with naive scans can use T=O(N^4), S=O(N^2), CM<=O(N^6) including parsing, verification, queue simulation and witness replay. For arbitrary CNF parser, global decomposition discovery, local message composition, add respective costs; no free parallel workers or oracle. Model counts not preserved by this projection unless extension multiplicities retained.
