## Statement
There is a deterministic polynomial-time reduction from graph 3-colorability of maximum degree four to connected positive exact-one instances in which all triples are distinct-variable ternary rows, every variable occurs either two or three times, all rational equations have an integer witness (hence are consistent modulo every integer), the box-constrained rational LP is feasible, the bare affine and binary subsystems are each SAT, both the number k of degree-three variables and rational nullity d grow linearly in the variable count, and the incidence graph has linear treewidth. All properties hold for SAT and UNSAT images alike. Thus deciding Boolean compatibility on this promised class is NP-hard (and the underlying decision problem is in NP). No inference of exponential compulsory work is valid.

## Step 1: neutral expander overlay
Given a simple maximum-degree-four graph G on g vertices, pad with isolated vertices to p=s^2 where s=ceil(sqrt(g)), for g>=2. On the p terminals use the simple underlying graph X of the Margulis-Gabber-Galil 8-regular multigraph: for each (x,y) modulo s include the four forward edges to (x+2y,y), (x+2y+1,y), (x,y+2x), (x,y+2x+1) and regard edges as undirected; discard loops and parallel copies. For every distinct edge uv of X add a fresh vertex w_uv and edges uw_uv, w_uv v. Keep G's original edges. Call the resulting graph H, with v vertices and e edges. Every terminal coloring extends along each length-two path, since at most two colors are forbidden for w. Therefore H is 3-colorable iff G is. H is connected, has degree <=12, v,e=Theta(g) for the infinite family, and contains X as a minor. The MGG spectral gap (second eigenvalue <=5sqrt(2)<8) implies constant edge expansion; deleting loops and coalescing edges loses at most a constant multiplicity factor. A balanced separator of X must have linear size: if the separator is already large this is immediate; otherwise one can union remaining components into a set of size between p/4 and p/2, or take the complement side of a component of size between p/2 and 2p/3. Expansion then forces linearly many neighbors in the separator, using bounded degree. Standard treewidth/balanced-separator facts give tw(X)=Omega(p). Consequently tw(H)=Theta(g). This establishes linear treewidth, not a claim that every induced residual expands. Neutral overlay padding can be removed by sufficiently strong semantics-aware simplification; this is intentional.

## Step 2: exact-one coloring encoding
For each H vertex u use 3 Boolean variables x[u,1..3] and row sum_c x[u,c]=1. For each H edge uv and color c, add a private z[uv,c] and row x[u,c]+x[v,c]+z[uv,c]=1. Boolean solutions are exactly proper 3-colorings of H, with uniquely determined z. Conversely every solution is such a coloring. Base dimensions m=v+3e rows, q0=3v+3e variables. Rows have rank m over Q and every field: each edge-color row has a unique z pivot, and the remaining vertex rows have disjoint variable scopes. Base nullity d0=2v. Integer witness: set all vertices to color 1, regardless of adjacency, and z=1-x[u,c]-x[v,c], taking values -1 or 1. It is an integer equality witness, NOT necessarily Boolean. All coordinates 1/3 are a feasible LP witness. These distinct witnesses demonstrate why consistency of both relaxations is insufficient.

## Step 3: bounded occurrence and no private variables
Make three copies of each base row using fresh occurrence variables. For every original variable, cyclically order its occurrence copies; each cycle has length at least 3. For each adjacent pair a,b on the cycle add fresh p_i,q_i and the two distinct ternary rows
a+p_i+q_i=1, b+p_i+q_i=1.
Their difference forces a=b over every field and over the integers. For equal Boolean a=b, the auxiliary pair has a legal extension: one true if a=0, both false if a=1. Projecting the pair therefore gives exactly the binary equality relation. Each occurrence variable lies in one base-copy row and two equality rows (degree three); each auxiliary variable lies in two equality rows. No variable occurs once. All rows are distinct and their three variables are distinct.

Let L=9m be the number of occurrence variables/links. Final formula F has Q=3L=27m variables, M=3m+2L=21m rows, k=L=9m degree-three variables. All original-variable rings are connected; the three color rings at each H vertex are connected via a vertex row, and each H edge has a color-edge row linking its endpoint branch sets. These disjoint vertex branch sets and edge rows certify H as an incidence-graph minor. Hence the final incidence graph is connected and has tw=Theta(g)=Theta(Q), although we do not claim an exponential lower bound on arbitrary symbolic representations.

## Step 4: exact nullity and scaling
The affine solution spaces are isomorphic to the base affine space times one free coordinate per link: substitute equal occurrence variables; choose p_i freely, set q_i=1-a-p_i. This works over Q and every field, so
d=Q-rank_Q=2v+L=2v+9m; rank=18m-2v.
An integer witness lifts with p_i=0, q_i=1-a. The LP witness all 1/3 remains feasible. Odd-XOR alone has the all-one witness; binary at-most-one alone has the all-zero witness. If encoded by one positive ternary plus three negative binary clauses per row, no nontrivial SCC equalities can arise in the binary implication graph: every implication goes positive literal to negative literal, and no edge leaves negative literals. Thus the peer's syntactic exact-one + binary SCC + rational rank parameter is at least this linear d (in fact equal for the stated recognizer; there are no other positive ternary clauses). If encoded with parity, the extra ternaries do not add an independent certified exact-one equation unless one has proved a new constraint.

Exact recurrences: adding a neutral length-two path on already present distinct H endpoints adds one vertex and two edges, hence Delta m=7, Delta Q=189, Delta M=147, Delta k=63, Delta d=65. Adding one original edge on existing vertices adds Delta m=3, Delta Q=81, Delta M=63, Delta k=27, Delta d=27. For explicit binary encodings N=Theta(Q log(Q+1)) on this sparse family, so k,d=Theta(N/log N), rather than literally Theta(N) bits. The fixed min(k,d) enumeration budget is exactly 2^(9m), since d>k. That is an upper-bound budget, not a lower bound for arbitrary implementations or early UNSAT shortcuts.

Counting: for any fixed proper 3-coloring of H, each base row has one true bit. Across three copies, exactly 3m occurrence variables are true and 6m false. Each link has exactly 1 extension if its endpoint bit is true and 2 if false. Thus #F=2^(6m) * #3Color(H). Neutral overlay paths have 2 extensions if endpoint colors equal, 1 otherwise; do not claim a uniform multiplicity from G to H or infer decision hardness from counting.

## Signed-hypergraph DAG repair and precise gate
Use a source-indexed rule: two exact-one nodes with a shared pair p,q entail equality of their other variables (row subtraction). A proof edge cites both rows and the subtraction. The pair can be projected only if it has no remaining external occurrence. For these instances pair-key sorting discovers all ring equalities; union-find merges the copies; deduplicating identical base rows is sound; private pair projection leaves the original coloring encoding. No semantic oracle, and all these steps are polynomial. The affine nullity falls from 9m+2v to 2v after projecting auxiliary pairs. Projecting each edge slack z yields x[u,c]+x[v,c]<=1, while vertex rows retain exactly-one. Thus the residual is precisely graph 3-coloring. The graph matching theorem for pure degree-two exact-one rows cannot be applied to the nonlinear mixed residual just because its remaining exact-one rows are disjoint. A certificate DAG must retain the binary cross-color exclusions. This gives an exact obstruction even after successful local structural simplification: generic polynomial compatibility of those residuals implies P=NP.

An explicit frontier DP on graph vertices has <=3^w candidate color states for width w; unrestricted graph coloring still admits exponential upper bounds. The high initial treewidth is padding-robust as a raw statistic but not necessarily a lower bound on semantic width after discovering neutral paths. No lower bound on proof DAG size, shortest resolution, cutting planes, or unrestricted algorithms is proved here.

## Cutting-plane repair: K4 and K4-free control
On K4 source, for a fixed color c all pair inequalities x_i+x_j<=1 are valid. For every three-vertex subset, sum its three pair inequalities and divide/round to get sum of the three x_i<=1. Sum the four resulting inequalities, divide/round by 3 to get sum over all four vertices<=1. Across 3 colors the total <=3 contradicts four vertex exactly-one rows requiring total 4. These are two rounds of elementary Chvatal-Gomory cuts after the certified quotient. Verify arithmetic and source indices. This is a short proof for K4 and invalidates a lower-bound claim based merely on our large d,k,treewidth. A K4-free odd wheel W5 is also UNSAT (hub forbids its color, leaving an odd cycle to 2-color). Clique cuts alone do not supply that specific K4 proof. Do not infer general incompleteness of all cut systems. ## Charged ledger and exact remaining gate
Uniform reduction builds O(g) symbolic objects, O(g log(g+1)) output bits, with deterministic polynomial bit time (including overlay edge sorting, padding, naming and all source access); NP membership uses one Boolean witness checked against every original row. Pair-key sorting, equality proof construction, union-find and projection checks are polynomial in N, including conservative polynomial path replay and proof storage. Exact field rank has polynomial cost; rational arithmetic requires coefficient bit lengths and fraction-free elimination. On any fixed explicit bit model a polynomial-time prefix with polynomial workspace has polynomial physical CM under faithful representation; for complete fallback enumeration the upper bound remains exponential, with polynomial workspace if streamed. No measured physical CM, minimum-description CM, or unrestricted lower bound is claimed. If output or saved proof grows exponentially its storage and construction must be charged. No free parallel workers or semantic merge oracle. The decision result is distinct from counting.
