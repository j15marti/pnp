## 1. Rank-one backdoor
Explicit input: d Boolean free coordinates (d<=N), and forbidden affine spaces A_i={x:M_i x=b_i}, total encoded length N. Normalize each by GF(2) elimination; remove inconsistent forbidden systems, reject immediately if a forbidden system is universal. Let W_i=row(M_i), ranks r_i<=r for fixed r. Define beta as minimum dimension of a subspace H of the dual satisfying dim(W_i/(W_i intersect H))<=1 for all i. Unlike a variable backdoor, H may contain arbitrary parity forms. This is a syntactic condition on the supplied normalized representation, not a minimum over equivalent formulas.

Discovery with budget k: start H=0. If every factor meets the condition, return H. Otherwise choose a factor W_i with residual rank>=2. If dim H=k fail. Branch on each nonzero v in W_i outside H, replace H by H+span(v), and recurse. Completeness: if a feasible H* of dimension <=k extends H, then W_i intersect H* has dimension >=r_i-1 while W_i intersect H has dimension <=r_i-2; at least one candidate v lies in H* outside H. Each branch increases dimension by one. Soundness follows from checking all ranks on a returned basis. At most (2^r-1)^k times polynomial(N) bit work (r>=2; r<=1 trivial). Iterative deepening through beta preserves this bound for fixed r>=2. For rank two the base is 3; rank three base 7. The search is paid once, NOT once per evaluation fiber. No guessing of an optimum is free.

Evaluation: enumerate the 2^dim(H) possible values on a basis of H. In each nonempty affine fiber, each forbidden system is either empty, the entire fiber, or a relative hyperplane. Empty drops, entire rejects, and avoiding a relative hyperplane is a single opposite affine equality. Solve all these equalities plus fiber equations by Gaussian elimination. This decides each branch and gives exact count 2^(d-rank of combined equations) for a consistent branch, zero otherwise. Sum counts across disjoint fibers. If starting from x=x0+Kt, count is preserved for an injective parameterization; retain lifting map. For r fixed, total T=poly(N)[(2^r-1)^beta+2^beta], S=poly(N) via DFS and sequential branch evaluation; CM<=T*S. A deliberately conservative explicit bit-RAM bound is T=O(N^8[(2^r-1)^beta+2^beta]), S=O(N^4), CM=O(N^12[(2^r-1)^beta+2^beta]) for dense explicitly encoded matrices, d<=N, fixed r, using polynomial-time rank checks, DFS depth <=d and polynomial-bit model counts. Verification replays discovery if a negative budget certificate is requested; for a supplied feasible H its rank conditions are polynomially verified; UNSAT proof streams a Gaussian inconsistency or universal-forbidden certificate for each fiber. All work of parallel workers would be added, not discounted; no native physical CM measured. No source discovery cost hidden: theorem applies to supplied explicit affine-obstruction input, plus separately charged source extraction (direct 3CNF translation is polynomial). beta=O(log N) suffices for polynomial discovery, decision and counting. Arbitrary 3CNF has r<=3 but no small beta guarantee.

## 2. Simultaneous cut monotonicity
The discovery adds only vectors from original individual W_i. For EVERY cut A|B of any fixed factor tree, H is generated by vectors lying in W_A or W_B, so H=(H intersect W_A)+(H intersect W_B). Under quotient by H the shared rank becomes s'=s-dim(H intersect W_A intersect W_B)<=s. Proof by dimension formula for images under the quotient map: dim image W=dim W-dim(W intersect H); use H subset W_A+W_B and the displayed decomposition. This also applies to an affine fiber since constant offsets do not affect row spaces. Further dropping empty factors cannot increase the cut rank. Thus the backdoor is safe across all cuts, unlike arbitrary cross-cut affine deductions. One may stop discovery early and use exact cut-table gluing on the residual; a supplied tree width w bounds the resulting join work by 4^w poly(N) per fiber for bounded-rank leaves. Charge H discovery, 2^k fibers, actual joins and tree discovery separately. Korhonen/Oum arXiv:2601.04756 gives 2^{O(w^2)} poly(N) discovery using the rank oracle, not polynomial for arbitrary logarithmic optimal width. No claim of universal small beta or w.

## 3. Infinite collective-cut family: exact large beta, cheap SAT
For a simple graph G=(V,E), n vertices,m edges,c(G) connected components (including isolates), give each vertex bit x_v and each edge private bit p_e. For each edge uv prescribe charge c_e in GF(2); let a=x_u, b=x_v xor (1 xor c_e). Impose NAE(a,b,p_e) AND NAE(a,b,not p_e). The two NAE factors forbid the two cosets of the same rank-two row space W_e=span(x_u+x_v,x_u+p_e), with RHS first 1 xor c_e and second 0 or 1 respectively. Their union is exactly {x_u+x_v=1 xor c_e}; avoiding both forces x_u+x_v=c_e. Hence 2m rank-two factors, 4m ordinary width-three clauses on d=n+m bits; all-half vector meets all standard clause LP inequalities, and there is no supplied affine equality to contradict modular consistency. The raw modular blindspot is deliberately limited to supplied equations, not integer or Boolean closure. This is a nonlinear pair that exposes an exact rank-one consequence.

Cutting-plane certificate: each NAE yields 1<=a+b+p<=2 and 1<=a+b+1-p<=2 (the four original CNF inequalities). Sum lower and upper pairs, giving 2(a+b)>=1 and <=3; integer division/rounding yields a+b=1. The equivalent parity equation is then certified and fed to Gaussian elimination. Each edge uses constant many cuts and local affine transfer; closure cost polynomial. All graph factors have full affine hull individually because the six allowed points in their rank-two observation space lift three noncollinear points, or directly the six satisfying triples span GF2^3. The two-factor aggregate is essential.

Exact beta=n-c(G), before collective merging. Upper: span all edge differences. Lower: any feasible H must choose a nonzero v_e in H intersect W_e for every edge. Either v_e is x_u+x_v or it contains the unique private coordinate p_e. The chosen vectors containing private coordinates are independent modulo the vertex-only forms and each other. Thus their rank is their number plus rank of the remaining chosen edge differences. Replacing private vectors by the corresponding edge differences cannot increase that rank upper bound, so rank of all edge differences n-c(G) <= dim H. This is invariant under invertible coordinate changes, not under rewriting the factors. For connected cycles or expanders beta=n-1, while after the certified cuts beta=0 and Gaussian solves every charge vector. Large beta is NOT a computational lower bound.

Exact row-space gluing on edge bundles: rank W_A=|A|+n-c(A), where c(A) counts components of (V,A). Therefore cut rank s(A,B)=n+c(G)-c(A)-c(B) for disjoint complementary edge sets. Private dimensions cancel. This is graphic-matroid connectivity; each nontrivial cut of a simple cycle has s=1, so low cut width can also bypass high beta. Graphs may have larger s; no universal width lower bound claimed for this family. Connected expander G works equally for the beta formula, and a balanced charge c_uv=t_u xor t_v is SAT; flip one nonbridge edge to obtain an UNSAT matched neighbor with identical dimensions and graph structure. No need to claim expansion for finite controls. Arbitrary charges are consistent iff each cycle XOR is zero (not parity of total edges in a general graph). SAT count is 2^(m+c(G)); UNSAT count zero. For a cycle with odd total charge, deleting any one NAE factor gives exactly 2^n models (n=m), deleting any one of the 4n CNF clauses gives exactly 2^(n-1) models. Proof: remaining edges fix a unique relative assignment along the path, two global orientations; the broken edge has a=b. A remaining NAE fixes p_e uniquely, while deletion of only one CNF clause further fixes the orientation; other p_e are free. Balanced and unbalanced controls differ by one edge charge, both possible for either cycle length. On all-one charges, even cycle SAT, odd cycle UNSAT; flipping one reverses.

Parity ledger: n,m both parities admitted (cycles/path/other graphs); degrees 1,2,3 tested, odd regular degree forces even n by handshake. Factor count 2m and clause count 4m are necessarily even in complete pair encoding; one-factor deletion gives odd factor count, one-clause deletion gives odd clause count. Each W_e rank 2 necessarily even. Whole row rank n+m-c(G), nullity c(G), both parities possible (cycles odd rank and nullity 1; path even rank; two disconnected nontrivial components even nullity). beta=n-c(G) both parities. Cut dimensions 0,1,2 and higher possible in general, both parities tested. Odd dependency charge means inconsistency after parity extraction; ordinary pair of forbidden cosets is a *union* not a conjunctive equation system, and its differing RHS is not an inconsistent input. No claim that an odd number of constraints determines UNSAT. Parity relation d=rank+nullity disallows the other four of eight triples. Both odd and even cycle lengths with even and odd charge were tested.

## 5. Constructive repair and exact open gate
Before searching backdoors, group forbidden systems by canonical row space, merge complementary cosets with matching parent; use rank-one outputs as certified affine equations, reproject and update all cut ranks. Each exact merge reduces factor count and each independent affine equation reduces dimension; scanning all pairs per productive round is polynomial in the supplied explicit representation if no other operations enlarge it. For rank-two pair case this gives precisely the CP+Boolean+Gaussian cooperative repair, even on an expander. It can be extended to bounded-size groups by enumerating the constant-dimensional joint observation space of a fixed number of bounded-rank factors; discover group candidates in polynomial time for fixed size, certify union equals an affine subspace, then replace the union. No universal bound on required group size is known. This does not justify the universal macro-potential recurrence. Seek families with large beta, high cut rank, no bounded-group merges and no other known closure; then either show an alternate symbolic repair or isolate restricted failure. Need a proved, paid universal tradeoff to get P=NP; failure of any fixed family of rules does not get P!=NP. Prior meta report audited sound; independent review requested for beta discovery, cut monotonicity and CP transfer.
