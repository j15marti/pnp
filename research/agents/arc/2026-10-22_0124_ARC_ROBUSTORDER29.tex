\section*{Literature check}
Kashyap, \emph{Matroid Pathwidth and Code Trellis Complexity}, DOI:10.1137/070691152, already relates linear-code trellis width to matroid pathwidth. The phenomenon is not asserted to be novel generally. Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, \emph{Time-Space Lower Bounds for Satisfiability}, JACM 52(6) 2005, Section 2.1 and Corollary 5.8, supply a much stronger \emph{machine-specific} target. Their original corollary forbids $\mathrm{NTIME}[n]\subseteq\mathrm{DTISP}[n^c,o(n^{d(c)})]$, where $d(c)=((c+2)/c^2-c)/2$ and $d(1.203)=0.5051127974604911$.
\begin{lemma}
For the affine relation $R(u,v)=[H_Pu+H_Qv=0]$, the number of distinct nonempty suffix residual functions after the variables $u$ have been assigned equals $2^{\lambda(P,Q)}$, with $\lambda(P,Q)=\operatorname{rk}(H_P)+\operatorname{rk}(H_Q)-\operatorname{rk}(H)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The residual indexed by $u$ is $\{v:H_Qv=H_Pu\}$. It is nonempty exactly for $H_Pu\in\operatorname{im}(H_P)\cap\operatorname{im}(H_Q)$. Distinct right sides give disjoint nonempty residual solution sets. The intersection dimension is $\lambda(P,Q)$.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}
For all sufficiently large $n$ there exists an $n$ by $2n$ binary matrix $H$ such that, for every ordering of the $2n$ boundary variables, every exact ordered residual decision DAG for $R_H(x)=[Hx=0]$ has width at least $2^{n-2\lceil2\sqrt n\rceil+2}$ just after the $n$th boundary variable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $k=\lceil2\sqrt n\rceil$. For a uniformly random matrix, every fixed $n$-column submatrix $M$ is uniform $n$ by $n$. If $\operatorname{rk}(M)\le n-k$, some $k$-dimensional subspace lies in its left nullspace. The count of $k$-subspaces is at most $\binom nk2^{k(n-k)}\le2^{n+k(n-k)}$. A fixed such subspace lies in the left kernel with probability $2^{-kn}$. Hence $\Pr[\operatorname{rk}(M)\le n-k]\le2^{n-k^2}$. Union over fewer than $2^{2n}$ balanced column subsets gives failure probability at most $2^{3n-k^2}\le2^{-n}<1$. Fix a matrix passing every test. At any balanced cut, $\lambda\ge 2(n-k+1)-n=n-2k+2$ because $\operatorname{rk}(H)\le n$. Every order contains a balanced cut after $n$ variables. Apply the lemma.
\end{proof}
\section*{Ordinary 3CNF and the limitation}
Each parity row is encoded as a chain of XOR gates. The gate $z=a\oplus b$ has four proper three-literal CNF clauses; unary output conditions are converted into four proper three-literal clauses using private auxiliaries. The existential source CNF uses $O(n^2)$ clauses and $N=O(n^2\log n)$ encoded bits. The projected relation on its original boundary variables therefore has fixed-order OBDD width $2^{\Omega(\sqrt{N/\log N})}$ in every order. This does NOT yield an unrestricted SAT lower bound. The original matrix is a compact $O(n^2)$-bit affine description. Checking full-assignment contexts $x=a$ by calculating $Ha$ takes $O(n^2)$ time and logarithmic scratch space. Affine contexts also admit Gaussian elimination; arbitrary nonaffine parent contexts are not established easy. The probabilistic proof does not give a uniform polynomial-time family of witnessing matrices.
