## Quantifiers, model and certificate

Fix the deterministic solver A below before any input, with one head and one finite-alphabet writable tape containing the input, scratch, addresses and output. One transition reads/writes the scanned cell and moves at most one position. Output and its terminator begin at the left origin. For every nonempty proper ternary source F with c clauses on d densely named occurring variables, let I(F) be its canonical compiled image, N its full length. The target asks whether the entire formula is satisfiable. The supplied adjacent assignments certify the two local parts. All other valid inputs get an exact fallback.

Group clauses by their sorted triple of unsigned IDs S. Tabulate the conjunction R_S on its eight assignments by clearing the unique forbidden row per clause. Multiple clauses on one triple count once for incidence. Write H for the names on more than two distinct original supports. Define h(F) as the minimum |B| over sets satisfying both of the following tests.

1. H is contained in B.
2. For every original S and every binary assignment to B intersect S, the allowed rows on the remaining names form the empty relation, the full cube, or one complete Hamming-weight layer. Weight uses the original unsigned names. Check even a group whose slice is hidden by a contradiction elsewhere.

Full pinning qualifies. This definition allows universal and false sections, keeps each original group identified, and is checked before any cross-group simplification. A semantic backdoor that becomes tractable after discarding redundant factors or exposing an unrelated contradiction can be smaller.

## Construction and acquisition proof

Start from H. For budgets ell=0,1,... for extra names, run depth-first search in a fixed order. At a node P check all local sections (at most eight per support). If all qualify, return P. Otherwise choose the first failing S. If the budget remains, try each name in S minus P and backtrack on failure. At budget zero report search failure. A failing section survives in every extension disjoint from S minus P: the same assignment on S intersect P witnesses it, whatever values are assigned outside S. Thus every successful extension hits the branch. Conversely further pinning of any qualifying section preserves its type: a weight layer loses the pinned ones from its demand, becoming empty if impossible. Full and empty sections remain full or empty. This proves by induction on the budget that search succeeds exactly when a qualifying extension within budget exists. The first successful depth gives a minimum. With e=h-|H| the sum of all visited nodes across budgets is bounded by the sum over ell<=e of (3^(ell+1)-1)/2, hence O(3^e), including failed trials.

Try assignments to the returned B sequentially. In a branch, any false group implies UNSAT and full groups can be dropped. Every remaining factor equates a sum of distinct bits to its required degree. Each free name occurs in at most two retained labelled groups. Identify a twice-used name with an edge between its groups, keeping parallel edges distinct. A singly used name connects to a common reservoir. Try reservoir demand t=0,...,D for D singly used names. A satisfying assignment selects true edges and gives a factor of exactly the specified degrees for one t. Conversely a factor gives values to every used name and satisfies every retained group. Zero-incidence names are free. This remains true if two groups restrict to the same scope. In a perfect-matching implementation each original edge has paired endpoint ports, and each degree-d vertex of demand b has d-b private dummies adjacent to all its ports. Exactly b ports must match across edges. This proves the decision equivalence for each branch, including zero and saturated demands. The disjunction over branches gives both labels on F. The acquisition proof uses no matching oracle. The matching algorithm and its polynomial implementation bound here are inherited from [1].

Three concrete falsifiers are a failing local slice repaired by pinning only outside its support, an accepted branch giving incompatible assignments to a twice-used name, and a source that disproves logarithmic h under the stated test. The local witness and shared edge identity rule out the first two. The third is realized: t variable-disjoint positive ternary clauses require at least two pins each (pin one to zero and the remaining binary OR is neither full nor a single layer). Two suffice, so h=2t even though all-ones satisfies the entire source. This disproves logarithmic coverage for this parameter. It leaves open the effect of separately paid simplifications, including private-leaf elimination.

## A compact, both-label selector cycle

For every even k>=6 and p,q in {1,...,k-1}, use a gate g=k+1 and k bits x_i, cyclically indexed from zero with physical IDs i+1. Group i has scope (g,x_i,x_(i+1)). At g=0 mark positions 0,p; at g=1 mark 0,q. A marked position requires its two bits to sum to zero. Every other position requires sum one. The generator emits groups in increasing i and lexicographic gate/bit order. For each forbidden assignment it emits the unique ternary clause false on that assignment. Each group has eight possible rows. The endpoint ID wraps explicitly at k. The table for each edge depends on the same gate.

Fix a gate branch with second mark s. Both incident bits at position zero are zero. Each unmarked position toggles the next bit. Reaching the zero required at s requires s-1 toggles to be even. The return arc requires k-s-1 toggles to be even. As k is even these conditions coincide. When they hold, propagating the two arcs constructs a witness. Otherwise the forced zero disagrees on the first arc. Therefore SAT holds exactly when p is odd or q is odd; UNSAT holds exactly when both are even. Each variable other than g occurs on two supports and H={g}. The two sections after pinning g are exact degree layers, hence h=1. The same-source gate enforces compatibility around a cycle but a single parity disjunction decides it. In particular the syntax imposes no sequence of required branch queries on a correct solver.

**Layout and generation.** In either branch the sum of allowed row counts over the k positions is 2k-2. Thus c=4k+4, d=k+1. Apply the established shared-selector compiler [2] to that exact ordered list. Each clause emits ten controlled dihedral instructions. On a fixed source assignment each violated clause contributes translation by four modulo r=c+1 (odd), each satisfied clause contributes identity. The initial-state pin and forbidden nonzero final states therefore allow the run exactly for zero violations. The adjacent-witness compiler first emits four release clauses, then two clauses per ordered intermediate triple with its own auxiliary name; see the actual serializer [2,3]. Thus lengths are
     
    L=10c, r=4k+5, n0=d+(L+1)r+2, m0=(2L+4)r,
    n=n0+m0+3, m=2m0+4, w=ceil(log2(n+1)),
    N=3n+2+2 floor(log2(m+1))+3m(w+1)+w.

The bit stream is unary n and a zero, gamma(m+1), m ordered triples of sign plus w-bit ID, two n-bit assignments, and the w-bit release ID. Hence n,m=Theta(k²), N=Theta(k² log k). On general sources r is the least odd integer greater than c, at least three. The explicit generator enumerates O(k) constant-size rows, never a 2^k table. Reuse the ordered intermediate pins and transitions of [2], with the canonical compiler equivalence for the adjacent local Horn/dual-Horn witnesses. No separation of variable positions across physical cuts is assumed. Generating the full target by indexed sequential shuttling costs O(N³) transitions, O(N) span, measured separately from solving it. The experiment actually generated two full targets.
