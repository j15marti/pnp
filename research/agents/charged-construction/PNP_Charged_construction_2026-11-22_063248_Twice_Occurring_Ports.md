## Costs of the single fixed solver

The finite-alphabet deterministic machine reads before writing and moves its sole head at most one cell per transition. No graph or prior solution is given for free.

Implement graph, adjacency, tree/blossom labels and endpoints by dense indexed records of O(log(m+2)) bits. Counts and indices are polynomially bounded and graph entries and labels require no large numeric weights. A generous O(log²(m+2)) indexed bit operations per elementary comparison, address computation, or update covers arithmetic on these words. The dense graph workspace fits O(m² log(m+2))=O(N), together with input and all recovered records. With a scanning tape simulator costing O(N²) moves per indexed bit operation, the matching stage fits O(m⁴ log²(m+2) N²)=O(N⁴). Allocate, clear and reuse labels while charging movement.
