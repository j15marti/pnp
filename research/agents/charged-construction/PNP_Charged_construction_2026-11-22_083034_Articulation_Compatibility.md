## One lemma

Fix a uniform deterministic solver before the input. On m positive exact-one triples on v occurring Boolean names, distinct within each triple, let s=m+v. Form the primal graph by joining all pairs within each triple. Recursively split disconnected pieces, then split a connected piece at any vertex x whose removal disconnects it. Give each resulting component its own rows and put x back wherever it occurred. Each triple belongs wholly to one part because its other two names are joined by an edge. Continue until there are no cuts. Choose cuts deterministically by increasing name. Empty input is SAT.

For every such source whose terminal pieces P satisfy either (i) |V(P)| <= 9+floor(log2(s+1)), or (ii) each variable in P occupies a circular interval in the inherited order of P's rows, the solver decides exactly in polynomial total work. Only (i) is needed for the family. The order in (ii) can also be discovered by the earlier PC-tree method, but the stated guard just verifies the given order. Failed guards yield UNKNOWN and then an exact SAT fallback. This proves a restricted polynomial decomposition that admits connected sources with arbitrarily large global deletion number.

**Compatibility proof.** Join each terminal piece to its shared variables in a bipartite graph, with a single node per shared name. This graph is a forest. Induct on splits. At a cut x the child subinstances share only x. In each child's forest the x node is already present or can be attached as a leaf to its unique containing piece. Identifying these x nodes joins trees at one vertex. Disconnected splits introduce no edges. Every original row occurs once.

Root each tree at a piece. From a piece to its parent shared variable send the subset of {0,1} that extends to an assignment of the entire descendant subtree. At a variable, intersect the subsets returned by all child pieces. An empty subset is infeasible, both bits are unrestricted, and a singleton pins the variable. Compute a piece's output by testing its local feasibility with all incoming domains and each candidate parent bit. At a root only local feasibility is needed. Disconnected roots combine by AND. Soundness and completeness both follow by gluing local solutions on the specified shared bit. Subtrees have no other common names. Intersection of child domains retains precisely the compatible values. The induction proves this assertion even when many children meet at one variable.

In (i) evaluate all local assignments once and project feasible ones onto the parent bit. The sum of local assignment counts is at most 512(s+1)m. In (ii) apply all singleton pins simultaneously. Two true names in one row reject. Every row already satisfied by a true pin forces its other names false globally. Propagate these zeros, rejecting any conflict, remove satisfied rows and all pinned columns, and reject an empty unsatisfied row. The remaining circular intervals survive deletion of entire rows and columns. A feasible residual chooses one of at most three arcs covering its first row and tiles the remaining gap by forward endpoint reachability. A full-circle arc alone suffices. This is the prior arc proof for widths up to three. Thus a high-degree piece can receive many distinct unary messages without enumerating their Cartesian product. The same argument can use separately acquired local deletion sets with the per-piece budget of Synthesis, but no such search is needed for this lemma.
