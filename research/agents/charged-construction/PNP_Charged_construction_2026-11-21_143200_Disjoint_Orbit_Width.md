## Proof from the shuffled bytes

**Authenticate and contract.** Parse the unary declaration, gamma code and fixed-width list. Authenticate every link with the WRAPPER routine [3]. In a genuine split packet, its middle two private names alone have degree three; they occur together twice. The other two private names have degree four. A copy has degree 1, 2, 4 or 5. The two common rows identify the side names. Their two shared rows identify the ports. Compare all six rows as an exact multiset against the oriented frame-and-pin template, validate distinctness, privacy, and disjoint row use, and check every remaining copy is in exactly one core row and that directed links form disjoint paths. The asymmetric pin distinguishes the two ports; reversing the cyclic frame is allowed. Reject a failed authentication as UNKNOWN. On the orbit the tests recover the original occurrence partition up to relabeling. On any success they prove Z=2^E Z(J), where J consists of the remaining rows with one name per path. This also follows directly by fixing path colors and multiplying the private link fibers. Singleton paths are retained. No hash equality certifies a merge.

**Seven deletions leave width two.** In the primal graph of J delete the five anchors, the wire x0, and the output wire T of the initial tautology. After anchor removal each domain helper only touches its wire. The output pin adds no edge. At the underlying gate level, after deleting x0 and T, both four-gate selectors for each coordinate are trees: z has children u and one of s,h, while u has the other child. The only exterior continuation is through s. Each remaining primary x_i attaches to its own middle OR. The middle OR and B selector meet at the AND construction, whose two negated operands meet once at an OR and then a NOT. Different coordinates have disjoint wires up to the fold. Their outputs are attached one at a time by that fold; the final negation and three tail negations form a path. The initial NOT and the remnants of the OR that produced T attach only to each other. Consequently the bipartite graph of remaining wires and gate occurrences is a forest, including a gate with a deleted operand as a partially attached node. This is an assertion about this fixed circuit and holds for either mask setting.

More explicitly, replacing a NOT node by its edge and an OR node by its connected clause graph preserves a tree of pieces meeting in single surviving wire vertices. The OR piece has triangle (X,Y,Z) and pendant operand edges (P,Y),(Q,Z); missing terminals are deleted. It admits a width-two decomposition with bags {X,Y,Z}, {P,Y}, {Q,Z}; restrictions of those bags work after deletion. Disconnected parts and isolated helpers pose no difficulty. Glue bags at a shared wire and append the unary-domain helper leaves. This gives treewidth at most two after deletion. Adding the seven vertices to all bags gives quotient treewidth at most nine. The argument does not bound the primal width of the split formula.

**Discover an order without a source certificate.** Try minimum-degree elimination with fill; accept its full order only if every pivot has at most nine live neighbors. If it fails, enumerate all subsets of seven quotient names (all names when fewer than seven exist). On the remaining graph repeatedly choose a vertex of degree at most two, make its neighbors a clique, and erase it. Reject that candidate if no such vertex exists. For width at most two, a leaf of a reduced width-two tree decomposition supplies a vertex of degree at most two (a single bag also suffices). Every such elimination preserves width at most two: degree zero or one is deletion, and suppressing a degree-two vertex with a missing neighbor edge is an edge contraction followed by simplification. Width cannot increase under contraction since the two intersecting subtrees of bags unite. Thus the *first* allowed choice is safe. The named seven vertices certify that at least one candidate succeeds on every permuted input. Append the deleted vertices last. When this order runs on the full graph its remaining undeleted neighbors evolve exactly as in the induced subgraph; eliminating an undeleted pivot can add at most the seven extra live neighbors. Its full width is at most nine. Failure of all candidates gives UNKNOWN.

**Count and decide.** Start with the actual five-color C indicator of each quotient row. In each bucket multiply all factors involving the pivot pointwise on their joined scopes, then sum out its color. Distributivity preserves the number of completions conditional on the remaining variables. Empty support gives zero, isolated scalar factors multiply, and the last scalar product is Z(J). The largest joined scope has ten names. Emit Z(J) shifted by E binary places, or emit its positivity for decision. Every computed entry is a count of partial assignments to a subset of the eliminated variables, with remaining boundary values fixed. The underlying eliminated-variable sets of multiplied incoming factors are disjoint by single-use bucket consumption. Their integer bit lengths and the shifted output length are O(N). A total all-input solver can switch to exact enumeration upon UNKNOWN. No negative label is inferred from failed discovery.

## Same-execution cost and forbidden-region check

Keep the full N-bit input, O(N) row/packet/factor records, O(log N)-bit identifiers and an O(N²)-entry adjacency matrix. For each of O(N^7) candidate subsets restore one scratch graph and scan degrees and fill entries for at most N pivots. Only the current candidate and its order are retained. A conservative O(N^4) addressed elementary operations per candidate covers matrix copying, repeated full matrix scans, fill and bookkeeping. Local packet verification can also use direct scans to build and cross-check incidences and exact multisets in O(N^4) elementary operations, aborting a nonmatching packet. There are O(N) active factors of at most 5^10 entries each. Each entry has O(N) bits. Table multiplication, summation, real lookups, writing messages, and output shifts have polynomial bit cost; even repeated pairwise table scans take a constant number of comparisons per pair of factors up to the fixed domain/width constant. O(N³) bits of contiguous storage more than suffice, including counters, old/new tables, copied input and addresses.

For an explicit loose one-head budget, replace each addressed operation by a bounded number of scans and bit copies on this O(N³)-bit region, or O(N⁶) transitions even if each bit requires a separate round trip. An O(N)-bit multiplication may instead use schoolbook O(N²) individual bit updates, included separately in the O(N⁴) count of evaluation operations (O(N²) bucket visits/joins with fixed-size scopes). Input and final output at the left origin are included. These charges fit O(N^20) time, O(N³) physical peak, O(N^23) physical CM, and O(N^21) minimum-description CM by replay of input and time index. These are intentionally generous upper bounds on an implementation, separate from the unmeasured Python runtime. Rejected subsets, the failed first order, and reconstruction all count. There are no oracle calls. A uniform cap on the UNKNOWN fallback outside the orbit has not been derived.

For any fixed cap T≤KN^a on the handoff machine, input included, its general ceilings are O(N^a) peak, O(N^(2a)) physical CM, O(N^(a+1)) descriptive CM and O(N^a log N) simulated count-circuit gates on the specified domain. An example with O(N²) time and O(N) total space would have O(N³) physical CM; that budget is compatible with the handoff's cubic matching-family count lower bound. A bound of o(N³) on the same faithful configuration charge for all such count inputs would conflict. The handoff's separate decision family has Ω(N²/log N) time and Ω(N³/log² N) configuration CM. The number of Boolean candidates retained by a reducer gives none of these time or residence bounds. A SAT algorithm also needs completeness on UNSAT, and an exact counter needs multiplicities if candidates represent many assignments.
