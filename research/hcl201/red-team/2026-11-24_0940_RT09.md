## 2. Automatic rejection of RT08, without cyclic labels
Use n=8k and ordinary triples z=x+y mod n plus k specials (0,y,y+2), 0<=y<k. As in RT07/RT08, one per Y and odd X/Z counts force all three part counts exactly one. Drop one X and one Z coordinate since their totals are n; the resulting zero-sum integer difference space is isomorphic to Z^(2n-2) by deleting the final coordinate in each part. Target remaining counts all 1. This works with arbitrary permutation of labels and row order.

For consecutive ordinary-only Y owners a,a+1 (exist among n-k ordinary-only owners), let D_x(a)=(e_x-e_0,e_(x+a)-e_a) in the two zero-sum parts. Their differences yield (0,g_(a+x)-g_a), g_j=e_(j+1)-e_j. The cyclic g_j generate the zero-sum Z lattice with only relation sum g_j=0; quotient by differences g_j-g_a is cyclic order n. Correct arbitrary X first via D_x(a), then Z modulo this quotient. Equivalently L is precisely the kernel of w(u,v)=sum j(v_j-u_j) mod n on the zero-sum ambient. Thus rank full, index n, every ordinary choice has identical residue within each owner and specials differ by +2. Relative to all-ordinary baseline the target has residue h=n/2=4k. The projected support consists of 0,2,...,2k; h absent. Arbitrary baselines shift both support and target equally. Consequently pair scan with cap n finds a rejection directly from permuted incidence data. It need not identify w or n's cyclic generator; canonical basis keys suffice. This closes RT08's family-specific potential discovery gap. The generic group-relaxation idea is classical, not claimed novel; the seed selection guarantee and partition counterexample are the project contribution.

## 3. New infinite obstruction: spread specials across every owner
For every n=8k include all n^2 ordinary triples and now n specials (0,y,y+2 mod n) for ALL y. They share X=0. A perfect matching uses at most one special. Exact counts require total defect h mod n, while feasible total defect is 0 or 2. Since h>=4, UNSAT. Add (n-1,h,n-1) of defect h to obtain a SAT neighbor: (x,x,2x) for x<h; (x,x+1,(2x+1) mod n) for h<=x<n-1; and the added triple. X,Y,Z each covered once. Both labels compiled into the same unit owner/tag model, with only one arc added.

**Theorem:** every full-rank two-Y-owner seed has index 2 and its projected target is attainable. Thus the entire two-owner full-rank quotient scan on the original Y partition is inconclusive for this infinite UNSAT family, even without any index cap.

Proof: pick Y owners a,b, d=b-a mod n, g=gcd(d,n). Ordinary differences D_x(b)-D_x(a) have Z part e_(a+x+d)-e_(a+x) -(e_(a+d)-e_a), zero X. The step-d edges preserve residue class modulo g. After eliminating the X part by D_x(a), the ordinary span therefore has rank at most (n-1)+(n-g). The two special differences both have zero X part and the SAME class-sum image (one unit moved from class a to class a+2); they add at most one dimension to the quotient by the full within-class Z subspace. For g>2 the resulting rank is strictly below 2n-2. For g=2 the specials preserve classes and also fail full rank. For g=1, step-d edges are one cycle. As in section 2 they generate the zero-sum lattice with sole cyclic sum relation, and their pair differences have index n. Weighted defect mod n annihilates all ordinary differences and is surjective, so the ordinary lattice is its kernel. Adding a defect-2 special difference gives precisely the kernel modulo 2, index 2. All other ordinary and special differences lie in that lattice, and the target also lies there since h even. Projected support is a singleton (zero in difference coordinates). Hence no full-rank pair detects the contradiction. Rank-deficient pair quotients or other algorithms may detect; this is not a runtime lower bound. The same global lattice index 2 and target parity show global signed lattice acceptance. The ordinary two-consecutive-owner binary span and projected-witness argument of RT08 remain valid: all special differences preserve the three affine parity equations, and disjoint intact pairs furnish independent directions for rank r<=n/2-p under p compatible pins. The root LP has a strictly positive rational witness by the same RT08 perturbation with k replaced by n: start ordinary weight 1/n, special epsilon, subtract epsilon from x=0 ordinary and correct Z using ordinary pair differences over Q; epsilon sufficiently small preserves positivity. This LP and guarded projection extension is symbolic here, not re-executed.

## 4. Exact owner repartition restores polynomial rejection
The exact integer count lift allows any of the three parts to be used as the owner partition. No assignment set is changed if the remaining two part count equalities remain in the source problem; projected tests then relax those equalities only through the chosen quotient. In the spread family, use X as owners. Only owner X=0 contains specials, all other X owners ordinary-only. Choose any consecutive ordinary-only pair. Exchange X/Y in section 2; rank full, index n. Projected support has just two residues, 0 and 2 relative to ordinary baseline, because specials are all one owner. Target h absent. Thus scanning the three authenticated part partitions, and all pairs in each, rejects both RT08 and this new hostile family in deterministic polynomial time. It is a cooperative parity-to-integer-to-owner-repartition-to-group-contradiction rule. This is not a universal owner selection theorem; the exact three-part structure must be authenticated or separately discovered and its cost charged.
