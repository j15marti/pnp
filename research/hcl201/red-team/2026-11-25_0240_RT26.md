\begin{lemma}[Eight-partition critical family]\label{lem:rt26-eight}
There are arbitrarily large inclusion-minimal UNSAT signed NAE-3 registries on $n$ available variables, $O(n)$ owners, maximum occurrence eight, and incidence girth $\Omega(\log n)$. For some absolute $\delta>0$, every set of $1\le s\le\delta n$ owners has more than $7s/4$ incident variables. Every single-owner literal flip gives a SAT neighbor on identical scopes. The roots pass the raw MS27 support-coupled triple test, and every ordinary resolution refutation of the paired CNF has size $2^{\Omega(n)}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take $n$ divisible by three, eight independent uniform permutations, each partitioned into disjoint ordered triples, giving $m=8n/3$ labeled owners. For any fixed $s$ owner slots and any $u$-set of variables, the probability that all chosen owners lie in that set is the product over partitions of $(u)_{3s_i}/(n)_{3s_i}$, at most $(u/n)^{3s}$. Here $(a)_b$ denotes the falling factorial, with impossible events assigned probability zero. Put $u=\lfloor7s/4\rfloor$. As in DA20 the union bound is at most $[C(s/n)^{1/4}]^s$ for an absolute constant $C$; take $\delta$ small enough that $C\delta^{1/4}<1/2$. Splitting at $\sqrt n$ shows failure probability $o(1)$.

For a simple incidence cycle of length $2\ell$ with distinct variable labels and owner labels, choose its ordered labels in at most $n^\ell m^\ell$ ways. Within each partition, the two designated variables for each chosen owner must occupy two distinct slots in that triple. Inconsistent repeated variable requirements have probability zero; otherwise at most $6^\ell$ choices of ordered slots and probability at most $(n-2\ell)^{-2\ell}$ for each choice, by uniform permutation and independence across partitions. For $\ell\le n/4$ the expected number of such cycles is therefore at most $64^\ell$. With $g=\lfloor\log n/(4\log64)\rfloor$, the expected number for $2\le\ell\le g$ is $O(n^{1/4})$. By Markov, with probability $1-o(1)$ at most $n^{1/2}$ such cycles exist. Delete one owner from each listed cycle, retaining expansion and degree bound, and more than $5n/2$ owners for all sufficiently large $n$. Random independent literal signs on the remaining incidences give expected model count $2^n(3/4)^m<1$, so some signing is UNSAT. Extract an inclusion-minimal UNSAT owner set. This extraction is existential and is not a polynomial SAT subroutine.

Every small owner set has more than $s/2$ variables of degree one within it, by counting $3s$ incidences, so is satisfiable by recursive private-variable extension. The minimal UNSAT core has more than $\lfloor\delta n\rfloor$ owners and $\Omega(n)$ used variables (maximum occurrence eight). Removal of any owner admits a witness that violates that owner; flipping any one of its three signs makes that witness legal. Root singleton closure has no pins and the girth admits the MS27 triple-support construction for both labels. The DA20 minimum-entailing-owner measure proof applies unchanged to signed NAE: subadditivity under resolution, initial measure at most one, and the first line with measure above $\lfloor\delta n\rfloor/2$ has measure at most $\lfloor\delta n\rfloor$. Every degree-one variable of a minimum supporting set must occur in that clause, since otherwise its bit can be repaired while preserving the falsifying assignment. Width is $\Omega(n)$; Ben-Sasson--Wigderson width-size with initial width three gives exponential general resolution size. This is a restricted proof-system bound for a sparse encoded family, not an unrestricted time lower bound.
\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Mixed binary/ternary degree-three transport]\label{lem:rt26-split}
Replace each variable by one copy per owner incidence and impose equality on consecutive copies using signed binary NAE. This is a uniform linear-owner-size parsimonious transport to a mixed binary/ternary registry of maximum occurrence three. Every resolution refutation of the transported paired CNF yields a resolution-with-weakening refutation of the original with no more than a constant factor in line count. The same exponential resolution bound therefore holds on the transported family.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each legal copy chain is constant, so the projection and lift are inverse. Identify every copy with its original variable in a resolution DAG. Equality axioms become tautologies; original ternary clauses become original clauses. Discard tautological lines. For a non-tautological image of a resolution child, either the images of the two parents resolve on the image pivot (possibly followed by weakening), or a parent image subsumes the child because the image pivot or its complement already occurs in a residual parent clause. If a parent becomes tautological, its complementary pair must involve the pivot image, since otherwise the child would be tautological too; the other parent then subsumes the child. Both parents cannot be tautological with a non-tautological child. Weakening maps to weakening. Hence the projected refutation is valid and size-linear in line count. The equality-chain discovery and contraction here is polynomial by explicit binary owner closure and returns the raw degree-eight chart. This is not a strict E4/B4 physical compiler or a raw four-perfect-partition ternary family; MS27 is applied after certified equalities are contracted, not directly to binary owners.
\end{proof}
