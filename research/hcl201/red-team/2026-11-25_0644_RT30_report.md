For every field of fixed characteristic q (including characteristic 0), there is a branch p in {2,3} with p distinct from q. Restrict s to activate p, assign arbitrary constants to all other branch variables; the other gated axioms vanish. Any polynomial-calculus (PC) refutation restricts to a PC refutation of Pp without degree increase.
