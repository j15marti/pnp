## Lemma A: biconnected completion
For any simple linear R registry from BH16 with n>=3 vertices, m owners, retain all owners and actual seed s!=t. For each consecutive pair u,v on a cycle through the original vertices attach a private five-variable gadget p,q,r,s',t' with four owners
R(u;p,q), R(v;r,s'), R(p;r,t'), R(q;s',t').
For any boundary assignment take p=r=0, q=s'=1 and t'=0. Thus all original assignments extend, and restriction is exact. The all-zero word also extends to all-zero, preserving R semantics. Complement symmetry is retained. Host gadget edges contain the cycle u-p-r-v-s'-q-u and ear p-t'-q; every gadget is biconnected. The union over the cyclic sequence of old vertices is biconnected: removing an internal vertex leaves that gadget connected, and removing an old vertex leaves the chain of remaining gadgets connected, with each incident gadget attached at its other old endpoint. Adding original edges preserves connectivity. Each new variable occurs in exactly two owners, each old variable in at least two added owners. Linearity is preserved because each added triple meets another in at most one variable, and contains at most one old variable. Repeat gadgets on arbitrary distinct old pairs with fresh private variables until k>=max(n,m-n). New n'=n+5k, m'=m+4k, |E|=2m'<=2n'. All host edges are distinct and each belongs to exactly one local conflict pair. Construction, exact projection, witness lift and verification are linear in explicit augmented size; k polynomial. BH16 seeded hardness therefore transfers by many-one reduction to simple biconnected sparse hosts with local perfect-matching conflicts and minimum source owner-degree two. No bounded maximum degree or strict native port claim. A polynomial algorithm merely on one full block would imply P=NP; the neutral gadgets remain peelable by stronger methods and this is not a simultaneous normalization obstruction.

Infinite controls: actual Fano source 012,034,056,135,146,236,245, anchor bits (1,1,1,0,0,0,0), bad owner 012. Good R registry with seed (0,1) is UNSAT, same registry with seed (0,3) SAT. For every k multiple of seven >=7 the augmented registry has opposite answers with identical geometry and all owner signs; only seed endpoint changes. Root certified singleton support closure under either designated seed orientation is paid; tested UNSAT pins retain consistency. This is not an assertion about full semantic closure.

## Lemma B: adaptive exact two-variable interface contraction
Let A and B partition the owners, each nonempty, sharing at most two variables u,v, with both designated seed vertices contained in A and no additional independent guard. All R relations are complement-invariant and accept constant assignments. A one-vertex/empty interface is freely extensible by constants. For two vertices, the exact projected support of B contains 00 and 11; it contains 01 iff it contains 10. One exact seeded subquery on B, u!=v, determines its entire interface: SAT -> all four words, UNSAT -> equality. On SAT store actual B witness and its complement, plus constants; on UNSAT use constants and merge u,v in A. If the merge identifies the designated seed endpoints, reject. Simplify repeated variables: R(a;a,b) or R(a;b,a) tautological; R(a;b,b) forces a=b. Perform certified equality and singleton closure to a fixed point, retaining source map and guards. Then make just one exact seeded A subquery, on the unchanged A or equality quotient as appropriate. Its positive witness lifts by choosing the stored B witness or constant of the same boundary word. Its negative answer is exact. A B result under seed u!=v is NOT unconditional on the full parent; only the derived support summary is. UNKNOWN is not UNSAT; decline if exact B decision cannot be obtained.

Find eligible splits by scanning vertex pairs of the primal graph (clique per owner), connected components after removal, checking ownership and seed locations; singleton cuts analogous. Never discard a constraint crossing the interface. For nonempty subregistries use positive credit mu=2m-1, for zero-owner terminal use 1. Since each side contains at least one parent owner, the two sequential subcalls (even if A contracts to no owners) satisfy mu(B)+mu(A')<=2m-2=mu(parent)-1. This is an adaptive ordered procedure, not literally a static list of children as in thm:aggregate; its same credit telescopes on the actual execution tree regardless of B outcome, with a proof of each conditional composition. No duplicate A calls. Bound applies only when all subsequent subqueries either have a similarly charged normalization or a polynomial terminal. Search O(n^2(n+m)) adjacency work plus explicitly charged owner scans, source maps, witness storage, closure, equality quotient and exact child decisions; each per-call overhead polynomial in original N. At most 2m-1 occurrences for this restricted recursive policy when every terminal succeeds within a fixed cap. Same-execution serial peak space polynomial; CM<=(T+1)S. All B/A answers, failed scans, maps, actual guards, reconstruction and source verification charged. Absolute seed orientation can be set by global complement; one extra absolute pin is harmless only when the seed is relative (not independently oriented). Arbitrary additional guards, multiple seed demands or a separator splitting the seeds are not covered. Do not assume constant-time complete semantic closure.
