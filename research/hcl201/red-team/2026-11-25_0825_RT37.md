## Universal transverse plane theorem at strict d1
For any surjective linear F:C_K->F2^2 on dependency space of strict projected d1 geometry, acquire a nondegenerate 2D plane E of actual dependencies with F|E bijective. Represent F by two tags on labeled row directions. Use one RT32 lift of first tag, delete a supporting row from unique projected dependency to obtain independent quotient basis T with omitted owner o having nonzero residual tag image. Residual tag = original tag XOR tags of its expression in T. If image rank2 finish; else line L={0,l}. Selected owner's two alternate directions share residual. Some owner has residual outside L by total augmented rank r+2. Form original directed exchange graph: i->j if a direction of i has nonzero coefficient at selected basis slot j. From omitted o all reachable, since a proper closed reachable set R would have all rows spanned by |R|-1 slots, contradicting Hall. Take shortest path to outside-line owner. At each exchange the next slot coefficient and the nonzero residual on L are nonzero linear functionals of two-bit local coefficients, so one of three choices hits both. Such swap changes every residual only by an element of L; newly omitted owner's old selected row has residual l. At intermediate owners rank stays1; at target rank2. Shortest path has no forward shortcuts, so replacing each slot leaves coordinates of all later slots unchanged; next arc survives. At most h-1 swaps. Fixed r basis rows + any omitted direction gives a full-rank transversal; dependence plane linear, nondegenerate because basis owners lie on lines and omitted block has standard pairing. This proves deterministic section acquisition without enumerating bases, even with degenerate projected planes.
For w2 compressed puncture, F is boundary sum of K dependency rows. Surjective since B subset span K. Choose symplectic section sigma. Replace each contracted selected seam direction by actual K transversal from corresponding sigma value; dependence lifts linearly (zero maps zero), full rank preserved, pairing preserved. Orthogonal triangles in contracted chart lift. Also any quotient K triangle lifts using fixed independent J basis. When quotient K admits UNSAT signing, original geometry admits UNSAT signing iff contracted geometry does: any seam puncture can be attained by sign shift; other choices give full boundary. When K is all-SAT, original all-SAT. Thus triangle conjecture reduces through w2 seams, but no general generation or converse triangle-transfer theorem.
Defect repair: given hypothetical satisfying witness x*, start from basis near-model with omitted owner. Choose direction at omitted owner satisfied by x*; its discrepancy with near-model is one, hence some discrepant selected equation has coefficient one. Swap that equation out and recompute near-model, decreasing number of selected equations disagreeing with x* by one. At most r swaps until SAT. This is an existence theorem, not discovery (uses unknown witness).
