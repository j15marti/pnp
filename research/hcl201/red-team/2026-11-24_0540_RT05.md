## 2. Strong NP-completeness for rank-one unit-budget cycle blocks (PROVED)
Model: explicitly listed disjoint undirected cycle multigraphs, distinguished distinct terminals a_i,z_i per cycle, target parity one on both and zero elsewhere. Each edge has one owner and unit cost. Every owner has exactly three edges, at most one in each cycle, and budget 2. No additional tags, no source occupancy. Ask for a subset J of edges with the prescribed parity and each owner cost <=2.

For any 3-CNF with distinct variables per clause, first iteratively assign pure literals and delete satisfied clauses; discard unused variables, keeping a reconstruction record. Every surviving variable has occurrences of both signs. If the formula is empty, output a fixed feasible instance, e.g. opposite 3-clauses on three variables. For variable i make two internally vertex-disjoint paths P_i^0 and P_i^1 from a_i to z_i. On P_i^0 place one edge for each positive occurrence, and on P_i^1 one for each negative occurrence. Owner is its clause ID. Each path nonempty. Length-one parallel pairs allowed by I05. All vertices are private to this cycle. A clause owner has exactly three distinct edges in three different cycles; its budget is 2.

At every internal degree-two vertex parity zero forces equality of the two adjacent edge indicators. Thus each path is selected entirely or not at all. At a_i the parity one forces exactly one path. The same holds at z_i. Hence the two possible coefficient rows correspond bijectively to x_i=0,1. A clause owner's cost equals the number of its false literals, so budget 2 is precisely clause satisfaction. This proves both directions; witnesses lift by selecting whole paths, and pure assignments are restored. Every original residual satisfying assignment gives one and only one parity join; count is preserved for the residual formula, not claimed for the original after pure removal.

Construction edges = residual literal count L, vertices = L, each cycle has nullity 1, degree two, explicit rows=2 per cycle. Encoded length O(L log L) with binary IDs, constant weights/budgets; preprocessing polynomial even naive repeated scans; construction and verification polynomial, linear in occurrence records plus indexing. Membership in NP by checking all edge bits and budget sums. Hence NP-complete with all numerical data bounded by 2 (strong NP-completeness). I05's binary PARTITION example can therefore be strengthened to unit weights and rank-one blocks, not merely a weakly NP-hard numerical barrier. This is not a compiler into the stricter geometric wheels or actual physical PM supports; that transfer is OPEN. Exact I05 coefficient model only. The test compiler additionally accepts unbalanced occurrence inputs by adding a private unit edge with private budget 1 for empty paths; this generalization is not needed for the theorem.

## 3. Infinite SAT/UNSAT neighboring family (PROVED)
Use cubic bipartite toroidal graph G_s, s>=2: vertices A_ij,B_ij for i,j mod s; connect A_ij to B_ij,B_(i+1)j,B_i(j+1). It is connected, simple and cubic. n_v=2s^2, m_v=3s^2. One Boolean variable per source edge. Prescribe charge 1 at A_00, 0 elsewhere. For every vertex encode its 3-bit parity by the four distinct 3-clauses forbidding the four assignments of wrong parity. XOR all vertex equations: every source edge occurs twice, while charge XOR is 1. UNSAT.

Compile by section 2. Each variable occurs in eight clauses, four of each sign, so each cycle has two paths of four edges. Counts: b=3s^2 blocks, o=8s^2 owners, e=24s^2 coefficient edges and vertices, each block ell=1. Every owner appears once in each of three blocks. Change just the budget of the clause forbidding 000 at A_00 from 2 to 3. The all-zero source assignment, lifted as the zero paths, is SAT. No topology, target parity or edge weight changes.

Stronger: relaxing ANY one owner budget from 2 to 3 is SAT. At its source vertex v fix the forbidden tuple. Its parity is opposite the charge. G_s-v is connected: contracting the intact cell edges A_ijB_ij away from the deleted cell gives a toroidal square grid minus one vertex, which is connected; the surviving half-cell has edges to other cells. Adjust the remaining charges by the fixed boundary edges. Their XOR is zero because total original charge was one and the chosen wrong tuple has parity 1+charge(v). Forest leaf elimination therefore constructs a parity assignment on remaining edges. Exactly the relaxed clause can fail. In fact each such neighbor has 2^(m_v-3-(n_v-1)+1)=2^(s^2-1) solutions. Only the designated all-zero neighbor was tested at large sizes; the all-owner generalization follows from this proof. For s=2, full 4096 row assignments tested, UNSAT 0 and designated neighbor 8 solutions.

## 4. Simultaneous scoped parameter stagnation (PROVED)
Initial I05 closure: every edge cost 1<=2, each owner has total cost 3>2, no free edges or loops; no bridges, no pure cycle block; component parity valid. Each owner has three incident block choices with local costs independently 0/1, budget 2. Its attainable local row-bit relation is the cube minus one point, affine hull full and all two-coordinate projections full. Row-minimum budget propagation removes nothing. Under a single block-bit probe, each affected clause reduces to either no restriction or a binary clause on two remaining independent bits; each has full affine hull and both unary projections full. Thus separate owner local affine-hull + row-minimum/domain closure does not propagate beyond the probe. Complementary probes supply no new unconditional coefficient equation beyond the existing cycle parity equations, when using these scoped operations only. No claim against extracting joint relations of several owners, global implication certificates, or full cooperative closure. Integer pooled capacity also passes: every variable selects four edges, total cost 12s^2 <= sum budgets 16s^2; the fractional half-row assignment costs 1.5 per owner.

Each block has 8 distinct owners so I05 p=sum_B(|B|-max_i|B∩E_i|)=7b=21s^2. In MS05 owner-block graph, every incidence cost domain {0,1}. The incidence graph is connected (each source vertex's four clause nodes link its three edge variables; source graph connected). It has 24s^2 incidence edges and 11s^2 nodes. Any spanning tree cuts 13s^2+1 incidences, so the optimized raw cut product C=2^(13s^2+1), irrespective of forest selection. This is an upper enumeration parameter, NOT a lower bound on actual cut tuples visited: filtering repeated pins can collapse many prefixes and the tests confirm that effect. No claim of exponential work for all solvers. log C and p are omega(log N) on this fixed representation even after the explicitly scoped closure.

Also H (owner-block graph) contains a subdivision of G_s as a subgraph by retaining just one owner per source vertex. Contract the A_ijB_ij edges of G_s to obtain the toroidal square grid, which contains an s-by-s grid. Every tree decomposition of H therefore has a bag of size at least s: treewidth is minor monotone; the row/column crosses of a square grid are connected, pairwise intersect, so their associated bag subtrees have common intersection by tree Helly; any bag hitting all crosses has size >=s since fewer than s vertices miss a row and a column. This bounds structural bags in the raw owner-block graph, not arbitrary algebraic joint widths or post-rewrite decompositions.

## 5. Exact qualitative-to-affine escape (PROVED)
Do not infer hardness for the exhibited Tseitin family. For the explicit two-row blocks, compute actual owner cost functions of row choice, retaining block labels. Group owners by identical scope of block variables (sort IDs). For scopes of size at most 3 enumerate all <=8 row assignments; check every owner constraint simultaneously. Empty group is contradiction, full/affine group can be replaced by exact GF(2) equations after computing its affine hull AND comparing the cardinality with 2^rank (or exhaustively checking every point). Keep every nonaffine group as an exact truth table; let U be union of its scopes. Condition U, test these tables, solve remaining affine equations by Gaussian elimination under the same pins, lift to source block rows, verify owner budgets and parity. No oracle, hidden decomposition, or source charge labels required. Time 2^|U| poly(N), polynomial streamed space, |U|<=3 times the number of nonaffine scope groups; group discovery polynomial. The UNSAT family has all affine groups, U empty; every single-budget neighbor has one nonaffine group, |U|=3. Thus both labels have cheap deterministic terminals despite p and log C linear in graph size. This is an exact multisign fusion between qualitative owner contradictions and algebraic information. It does not guarantee U small for general 3-CNF, where a single clause per scope is already nonaffine.

Mandatory closure: perform certified coefficient parity, owner bound and local support closure before branching; extract every available same-scope group equation unconditionally and renew closure with Gaussian elimination; a rejected pin remains conditional. If using group rows of an already guarded source factor, guard the result until globally justified. No witness representative is treated as logically forced. For decision no protected tags were added; all group equations are equivalent on row bits, not arbitrary physical features. Full physical lifting and all-channel closure not executed.
