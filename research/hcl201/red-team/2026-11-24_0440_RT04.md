## 2. Exact isotropic codimension for the RT03 fixed singleton map
Quantifier: every finite simple connected graph G=(V,E), n>=2, m=|E|, with RT03 target zero and duplicate singleton map f(s,t)=(s,1+s+st). For disconnected graphs the analogous result counts nonisolated vertices. First perform the exact affine closure s_e=s_u+s_v and substitute. The remaining m independent quadratic equations are
 P_e=1+s_u t_u+s_v t_v+(s_u+s_v)t_e=0.
Write variables as s in F2^n and t in F2^(n+m). For edge subset J, the polar form of sum_{e in J} P_e has block matrix [[0,C_J],[C_J^T,0]] with C_J=[diag(deg_J mod 2) | incidence columns of J, zero elsewhere]. Thus polar rank = 2 rank(C_J). A left kernel vector z of C_J must be constant on each component of (V,J) and zero at each odd-degree vertex. In particular if J is a forest, every nontrivial component has a leaf. Therefore rank(C_J) is exactly the number of nonisolated vertices in that forest.

Take J a spanning tree. Then rank(C_J)=n and the sum has polar rank 2n. Every common-isotropic direction space must also be isotropic for this sum. A rank-2n alternating form requires isotropic codimension at least n (pass to the nondegenerate quotient by its radical, where isotropic dimension<=n). Conversely set all s direction coordinates to zero; all P_e become affine on every coset, and that direction subspace has codimension n. Consequently the MS04 optimum k is EXACTLY n, not merely lower bounded by a matching number. For disconnected G take spanning trees of its nontrivial components, proving k=#nonisolated vertices. This is a fixed parametrization, not an invariant of the original decision problem; a different nonlinear encoding or structural preprocessing may do better. MS04 approximation b/2<=k<=b<=2k is consistent and sound.

## 4. No S03 anchor-shadow contraction on girth>=5 critical graphs
Let G be any finite simple 4-critical graph of girth>=5 (not 3-colorable, every proper subgraph 3-colorable), and a any anchor. Use precisely S03's data B subset V minus {a}, H=G[B], T subset B, forward certificate F: each v in T has proxy p(v) in N_G(a) covering N_H(v), and reverse R: retraction r:G-a -> H fixing B and mapping N_G(a) into T.

If v in T has deg_H(v)>=2, then p(v)=v: distinct v,p sharing two distinct neighbors form a 4-cycle (the common neighbors cannot equal either endpoint in a simple graph). Therefore every such v is itself adjacent to a. Any cycle in H[T] would consist entirely of such vertices, so all its vertices would be neighbors of a, contrary to triangle-freeness. S03 forward-only odd-cycle contradiction is impossible for EVERY choice of anchor and base, not just the obvious base.

Now suppose F and R both hold and B is a proper subset of V minus {a}. By 4-criticality G[B union {a}] has a proper 3-coloring. Permute the colors so a has color3. All v in T with deg_H(v)>=2 are anchor neighbors, hence already binary (colors1/2). Any remaining v in T colored3 has degree<=1; recolor it to a color in {1,2} different from its sole current neighbor, if any. Recolor sequentially, preserving every edge. This yields the S03 required list coloring of H. R lifts it to a 3-coloring of G, contradiction. Thus every valid F/R certificate has B=V minus {a}. Since 4-criticality implies minimum degree>=3, every vertex of H then has degree>=2. Hence T subset N(a). R is now the identity and requires N(a) subset T. We obtain EXACTLY T=N(a), U=V minus N[a], with no proper-base contraction.

Each u in U has at most one neighbor in N(a) (otherwise a 4-cycle), so deg_{G[U]}(u)>=2. Each neighbor of a has at least two neighbors in U and no two anchor neighbors share a U neighbor. Hence |U|>=2 deg(a), and |U|>=2(n-1)/3. If C is any vertex cover of G[U], k_C=|C|, every vertex outside C has two distinct neighbors in C; choosing such a pair is injective, since a repeated pair creates a 4-cycle. Therefore |U|<=k_C+binom(k_C,2), so k_C=Omega(sqrt(n)) for ALL anchors, even with unbounded degree. If G has maximum degree Delta, counting edges from U\C to C gives 2(|U|-k_C)<=Delta k_C, so k_C>=2|U|/(Delta+2)>=4(n-1)/(3(Delta+2)). In bounded-degree critical graphs this is linear. S03's H[T] odd-cycle rejection cannot trigger and H bipartite cannot hold on these UNSAT inputs (it would provide a list coloring). Its sufficient cover parameter is large even with optimal certificate and anchor choice. This audits the theorem without refuting its stated restricted correctness. Other list-coloring algorithms, multi-anchor procedures, separator decomposition or global structural recognition remain eligible.
