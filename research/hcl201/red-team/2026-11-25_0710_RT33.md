## Degree-band theorem (white-box PIT)
For a supplied owner order, let A_i=sum prefix U_j, C_i=sum suffix U_j, B_i=A_i intersect C_i, w_i=dim B_i, e_i=dim A_i-i, h_i=w_i-e_i=i-r+dim C_i. For a selector prefix word a_1,...,a_i define F_i(b) to be parity of all linear valuations on A_i extending valuation b on B_i with ell_{ja_j}=1 for all prefix owners. Over F2 this is a Boolean polynomial in a fixed basis of B_i. F_0=1. Let D_i=B_{i-1}+U_i, containing B_i. Then
F_i(b)=sum_{psi in D_i^*, psi|B_i=b} F_{i-1}(psi|B_{i-1}) ell_{ia_i}(psi|U_i).
This follows from exact linear gluing of A_{i-1} and D_i (intersection B_{i-1}); a compatible pair defines precisely one valuation on A_i. Choose a basis of D_i extending a basis of B_i. The number k_i of eliminated coordinates equals dim C_{i-1}-dim C_i, at most two: D_i+C_i=C_{i-1}, D_i intersect C_i=B_i. Substitution of binary linear forms does not increase Boolean degree, multiplication increases by at most one, summing over k_i coordinates decreases degree by at least k_i (all other monomials cancel). Hence degree <=h_i by induction, since h_i=h_{i-1}+1-k_i. Terms below h_i-d can be deleted: after the next map they are still below the next cutoff. At end B_m=0,h_m=d; precisely the constant survives. This proves an exact projected transfer on degree bands [max(0,h_i-d),min(w_i,h_i)].
K_i=sum of binom(w_i,j) on that band = sum of binom(w_i,j) for max(0,e_i)<=j<=min(w_i,e_i+d). Let K=max K_i.

## Deficiency-one determinant support-containment formulation

Its coefficient is one iff the selected system has full observation rank r and is inconsistent. Let Q_i(z) be det[A(z)|c_i(z)] with c_i zero outside row i and c_i=sum_a z_ia at row i.
