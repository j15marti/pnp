## A. Intrinsic control plane theorem (PROVED)
All arithmetic GF(2). Input is an explicit affine chart and an explicit registry of Boolean quadratic equations f_i(t)=0, each of polar rank at most two after chart substitution, plus all affine constraints. N is complete encoded length, including chart, source IDs, all coefficients, guards and reconstruction maps. This is a promise on EACH supplied equation, not on arbitrary sums or the semantic relation. A Boolean quadratic is reduced with t_j^2=t_j. Its alternating bilinear polar form is B_f(a,b)=f(a+b)+f(a)+f(b)+f(0). Rank zero means affine; rank two has a unique 2D rowspace U. Given any basis u,v of U, the polar form is u(a)v(b)+v(a)u(b); indeed the induced alternating nondegenerate form on the two-dimensional quotient has only one nonzero coefficient over GF2. Thus f(t)=u(t)v(t)+ell(t)+c, for an affine remainder recovered by subtraction. Polar rank and rowspace computed and verified by Gaussian elimination, not assumed factorizations. An equation can equivalently use the pair u,u+v or v,u+v after adjusting its linear term, since u(u+v)=u+uv. Affine chart restrictions can reduce rank to zero; general rank>2 equations are outside this terminal.

Let K be a dual subspace of the free chart; pin a basis of K, so fiber direction H=ann(K). f restricted to any such fiber is affine iff its polar form on H x H is zero. For rank two this occurs iff the restrictions of u,v to H are linearly dependent, iff U intersects K nontrivially. Necessity holds on EVERY nonempty fiber since second differences are offset-independent; sufficiency also follows from explicit substitution. Therefore the minimum dimension h of a subspace meeting each rank-two observation plane in a nonzero vector is exactly the minimum number of independent linear observations that make EVERY supplied equation affine on EVERY fiber. This is invariant under invertible affine chart changes and local refactorizations, not under arbitrary equation-registry changes, nonlinear substitutions or semantic simplification. Do not equate it to the minimum rank required by an arbitrary solver, or to affineness of the global feasible set.

## B. Discovery and decision (PROVED)
Exact budget search: start K=0. If all U_i meet K, succeed. Otherwise choose an unhit plane with basis u,v and branch on adjoining u, v or u+v (the three nonzero elements). Each strictly increases rank. If an optimum extension K* containing current K exists, at least one of these three choices belongs to K*. Thus depth-h search is complete; iterative budgets 0,1,... discover optimum in O(3^h poly(N)) total bit time including failed searches. Polynomial-space depth-first implementation; the search tree for acquisition is NOT a split of source models. Separate polynomial 2-approx: whenever an unhit plane is found, append its full basis. It adds <=2 dimensions and increases intersection with any fixed optimum by >=1. At most h additions. Both acquisition bounds include all membership scans and basis updates. For coordinate planes indexed by graph edges, h equals vertex-cover number: in the quotient by K adjacent nonzero coordinate images must agree, so choosing one original vertex from each distinct nonzero image gives an independent set of size at least quotient dimension. Conversely span of a vertex cover hits all planes. Thus optimum is NP-hard, but fixed-parameter acquisition is exact. No novelty claim for general backdoors.

## C. Infinite opposite-label family: written-factor rank versus true rank (PROVED)
For n>=2 use free bits a,x_1,...,x_n,z_1,...,z_n, affine constraints XOR_i x_i=0 and XOR_i z_i=1. Gates z_i=x_i(x_i+a) (RT14 stars with affine input copy). Since x_i(x_i+a)=(1+a)x_i, XOR of outputs must be zero. UNSAT. Changing a single gate to z_j=x_j(x_j+a)+1 yields SAT with exactly 2^n chart assignments (a arbitrary, x even parity, z determined). Removing any one gate also yields SAT. The same geometry and observation planes are retained. On the root affine chart, projection to a,x_i,z_i is surjective for each i; each gate's graph has full three-dimensional affine hull, even after output sign flip. The specified local gate-hull closure stagnates for both labels.
The raw factor-pair cover parameter of RT14 is n-1: modulo span(a), either written factor has image x_i, and these span dimension n-1 after the parity chart restriction. Conversely span of all x_i covers. RT14's greedy can use n directions (actual ordered test uses n). But every intrinsic plane contains the SAME form a, so h=1 exactly. Thus no constant-factor bound on the raw optimum in terms of intrinsic optimum; not a flaw in RT14's proved approximation relative to its own parameter. The pair registry can suggest exponential guard enumeration where two affine fibers suffice. This is an easy family: adding gate polynomials and using the parity equations also solves it directly; unrestricted closure may succeed. No assertion of lower bound, or that it defeats all portfolio terminals. For strict physical transfer authenticate the sparse copies, parity and original wheel multiplicities separately.

## D. Regional gluing extension (PROVED symbolically)
For a supplied finite tree on a partition of the actual equation registry into regions, let V_i span ALL linear forms used by region i (including linear remainders and external observations, not just polar factors). For each tree edge e define S_e=span of V_i on one side intersect span on other side. Require S_e subset of BOTH endpoint V_i, verified by Gaussian ranks. Region i boundary B_i=sum incident S_e; b_i=dim B_i. After fixing B_i all regional polar planes are taken modulo B_i; let h_i be optimal intrinsic control rank in that quotient. These ranks do not depend on the chosen boundary RHS prior to further local deductions.
Compute a tree DP over actual boundary functionals. At a node enumerate all 2^b_i assignments to a basis of B_i, check every child edge message on its restriction, and use the local exact control terminal to decide whether the assignment extends to a regional satisfying functional on V_i. At a leaf send support on parent S_e. For each accepted value keep one verified witness and child references or recompute with charged costs. For root accept iff some boundary assignment and local witness exists. Soundness of gluing: by induction the functional on a child subtree span and that on the remaining part agree on S_e, their exact intersection; the endpoint containment hypothesis ensures the two values checked are the actual cut interface. To glue a sequence of children, the current other span is a subset of the complete opposite span; hence every possible overlap is in S_e and agreement suffices. Linear functionals agreeing on intersection uniquely glue on the sum; extend to full dual by Gaussian elimination and verify original registry. Necessity is restriction of any global witness. This proof does not require pairwise independence or erase any observed coordinate.
One control acquisition on each quotient costs 3^h_i poly(N), and at most 2^b_i local jobs with 2^h_i affine guarded solves; conservative T<=poly(N) sum_i (3^h_i+2^(b_i+h_i)). Space <=poly(N)(1+sum_i 2^b_i) using actual boundary tables and local sequential DFS, CM<= (T+1)S. Charge all tree verification, chart substitution, closure under each scoped guard, table cells and child edges, lift and final verification. No claim of count-preserving gluing here. If all b_i,h_i logarithmic this is a polynomial decision terminal. Disjoint direct-sum regions have b_i=0; their intrinsic minimum ranks add because K intersects each independent region in independent subspaces, but decision costs add rather than multiply. Small regional h_i alone is insufficient (RT14/QC01); universal cheap tree discovery and bounded interface acquisition OPEN. General tree backend NOT executed.
