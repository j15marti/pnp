## A. Exact coordinate-incidence support (PROVED)

A disjoint K4 per edge realizes this EXACTLY: assign the three edges incident to its distinguished vertex tags 0,e_u,e_v respectively; all other edges tag 0. There are exactly three perfect matchings, one for each distinguished edge. The three local incidence vectors are affinely independent over GF2. This is a general simple tagged PM input to I20, not a claim of strict E4/B4 physical realization. Tags are explicit and the components are visibly disjoint; no hidden guards, pins or quotas. Let D be the vertices whose target bit is one. Then target is achievable iff D has a matching into the incident edge set (each edge can serve at most one demanded vertex). Necessity: each demanded vertex has an odd number, hence at least one, of selected incident edge contributions; chosen representative edges for different vertices are disjoint because an option contributes to at most one vertex. Sufficiency: use one edge per matched vertex and zero on all unused edges. This proves equivalence, including arbitrary cancellation in candidate sums. One can also give an exact graph classification: in each connected tree component, all target patterns except the all-ones pattern are attainable; choose a zero vertex as root and assign each nonroot vertex its parent edge, selecting it iff demanded. All-ones impossible by edge count. In each connected component containing a cycle, every target pattern is attainable: take a spanning unicyclic subgraph, orient the cycle consistently and assign each cycle vertex one distinct cycle edge; assign off-cycle vertices their parent edge. Remaining edges contribute zero. Isolated vertices must be zero (tree of size one). Hence on the span of incident coordinates, saturation iff every nontrivial connected component contains a cycle.
