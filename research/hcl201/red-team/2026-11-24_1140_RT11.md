## 1. Precise barrier (PROVED)
For each bundle j let all class widths be 2, class parity rows p_j affine in independent generator bits z, W_j=2K_j where K_j is the number of class coordinates, and shifted net H_j even. By M10/M11 the local bad event E_j is wt(p_j)>H_j OR wt(p_j XOR omega_j)>W_j-H_j, omega_j=0. We work in that exact fixed-net coefficient model (distinct labeled classes, including equal affine row functions); no unannotated physical-wheel compiler is assumed.

Given two such bundles in an explicitly supplied common affine chart, deciding whether E_1 intersects E_2 is NP-complete and computing its cardinality is #P-complete under parsimonious reductions, even with q=0 protected probes, max LOCAL generator support component size c<=3, only width-two classes, no large coefficients, legal base known SAT and its exact affine hull the entire chart. Both separate bad-event masses and entire separate weight histograms are polynomially obtainable. The claim concerns acquisition of a pair correction, not the difficulty of avoiding both events, and not arbitrary basis optimizations. A pair oracle that answers polynomially on all these encoded inputs implies P=NP; the counting version further solves #3SAT in FP.

Proof. Start with proper 3-CNF F, all source variables occurring (unused variables can be accounted for separately). If a variable occurs d>3 times, replace its occurrences by distinct variables a_1,...,a_d and append the cyclic implications (not a_i OR a_{i+1}), indices modulo d. These force all copies equal, preserve satisfying assignments bijectively, and each new variable appears exactly three times. Clauses of widths 1,2,3 are allowed in this intermediate source; no reliance on hardness of exact-width-3 occurrence-3 SAT. Let G be the resulting formula. Now give each literal occurrence its own independent Boolean bit z_o representing the underlying variable value (literal sign is carried in the row constant). These occurrence bits are NOT synchronized by a shared affine equation; consistency is a local score condition for the second queried bad event. Add four independent switch bits a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2.

Bundle 1: for a clause of k distinct occurrences, form every nonzero XOR of its k literal truth values. Use each affine row twice. If the truth vector is zero, the score (sum of these bits as integers) is zero; otherwise exactly 2^(k-1) of the nonzero XORs equal one and the duplicated score is 2^k. The maximum for each clause is attained iff it is satisfied. Different clauses use disjoint occurrence sets of size <=3. Include the duplicated unary rows for a_1,a_2. Bundle 2: for each variable with occurrence bits z_1,...,z_d, use twice each row 1 XOR z_1 XOR z_i, i=2..d. Its maximum 2(d-1) is attained iff all copies agree. These components also have size <=3. Include duplicated unary rows for b_1,b_2. Variables absent from one bundle are independent singleton zero-score components.

For each bundle let L_j be the sum of its component maxima, H_j=L_j-2. Then H_j is nonnegative even and <=K_j, every attainable score is even, and W_j-H_j>=K_j. The second bad inequality is therefore redundant, the first holds iff the score equals its maximum. All class parity sums are identically zero mod2 by row duplication, so no hidden parity restriction on the free generator chart. H_j lies in [0,W_j]. M10 pair-fill gives exact fixed-net feasibility iff the bad event is avoided. We have
 E_1 = {all G clauses satisfied on occurrence bits AND a_1=a_2=1},
 E_2 = {all variable copies consistent AND b_1=b_2=1}.
Their intersection has exactly one word per satisfying assignment of F, since all copies and all four switches are determined. Conversely every intersection word gives a satisfying source assignment. This is a linear-size sparse compiler up to index bits, including the occurrence splitting. A candidate intersection word is polynomially verifiable; counting is in #P. NP/#P hardness follows by the explicit reduction from SAT/#SAT (standard #3SAT completeness). No repeated physical class label is needed; duplicated affine functions belong to distinct width-two classes.

Each individual weight histogram is obtained by multiplying component polynomials of degree <=constant (plus free bits); total degree O(N), coefficient bits O(N), deterministic polynomial. Its mass is <=1/4 of chart size because of its two independent switches. In fact M11 certifies base SAT by strict dominance U<=M/2. At zero and at every unit basis word neither pair of switches can both be one, so BOTH bad events are false. These actual legal witnesses certify the full affine hull and no coordinate pins; base unconditional affine closure is exact and stagnant. Even all unary observations can be realized. No positive pair mass has been assumed to imply a legal witness: the pair query counts rejected words. Under the explicit guard E_1 AND E_2, switch pins and consistency relations may be inferred; source SAT remains the conditional decision. Do not export those guarded deductions to the legal base.

## 2. Infinite neighbors with identical marginals (PROVED)
For n>=2 use F_n=(x_1) AND all (not x_i OR x_{i+1}), i=1..n-1, AND (not x_n). The contradiction is immediate by propagation; deleting any one clause yields exactly one satisfying assignment (the appropriate step function, including all-zero or all-one endpoints). Changing the sign of the final literal to x_n instead gives exactly one model, without changing any occurrence, clause scope, or bundle dimension. Every clause score histogram is invariant under literal sign flips because its occurrence bits are independent. The second histogram is unaffected. Thus the full two marginals (not only masses) are identical across this sign-only SAT/UNSAT pair, while the joint bad mass is 0 versus 1. Base legal model is SAT on both sides. This family is easy for the alternative forest algorithm below, not hard instances for arbitrary solvers.

Stronger symbolic high-cycle-rank family: begin with all eight proper three-variable clauses and iteratively replace (a OR b OR c) by (a OR b OR z),(not z OR c OR d),(not z OR c OR not d) with fresh z,d. The conjunction projects exactly to the replaced clause. For deletion of a new clause, fix old variables to a witness falsifying the replaced clause and satisfying all others. Deleting the first allows z=0; deleting either of the latter allows z=1 with the appropriate d. Deleting an unchanged clause lifts its old satisfying witness. Thus every step preserves minimal UNSAT, connected incidence, and n=3+2j,m=8+2j. Flipping any one literal of any clause gives a SAT neighbor: the deletion witness falsifies all its original literals, hence satisfies the flipped clause. The factor graph below has cycle rank beta=3m-(n+m)+1=14+2j before high-occurrence splitting. Each split of a d-occurrence variable adds 2d incidences and 2d-1 vertices, preserves connectivity and increases beta by one. The same split is used on both sign neighbors. Both c<=3 and identical full marginals hold; beta grows linearly with j. This is a symbolic construction; it was not included in executed tests. Structural/minimal-core shortcuts remain allowed. Large beta is not a runtime lower bound.

## 3. Constructive escape: joint factor-graph cycle rank (PROVED)
General pair-histogram adapter: use the common affine chart, with each bundle separately partitioned into disjoint generator-support components (including singleton unused bits). Form a bipartite multigraph: one node per component on each side, one binary edge per generator variable between its unique two component nodes. Parallel edges are retained. Node degree <=c. This graph can be connected even if c is constant. A spanning forest is discovered by DSU; beta=number of edges minus number of nodes plus number of connected components. Condition on the beta feedback edges (all 2^beta bit words, disjoint guards). On the remaining forest, exact counting uses standard tree messages. For an edge to the parent, retain its bit, both integer weight coordinates for BOTH bundles (four coordinates if including the omega-shift weights of M11) and the protected XOR label of q bits. Local node enumerations evaluate the actual affine rows. Count each generator bit's label contribution exactly once, e.g. on side 1, including feedback-edge values. Offsets and constant rows assigned once. Combine child messages by addition of weight indices and XOR of labels, matching shared edge bits. Multiply exact integer counts, sum alternatives. Independent forest components are joined with the same convolution. Add across cutset assignments, never take a Boolean OR of counts. This is a bijective enumeration by tree induction; no independence assumption about the original events. A specified affine guard is included as an additional paid XOR label; fixed pins filter local tables.

For explicitly given rows, each weight coordinate is <=number of classes, so four-dimensional weight tables have polynomial cardinality in original N times 2^q. Each count is <=2^n, O(N) bits; intermediate partial products and sums likewise count disjoint partial assignments. Each node enumerates <=2^c local words, and each child convolution by naive pair scanning costs <=4^q poly(N) bit operations. Therefore a conservative bound including all beta guard words, DSU, row evaluation, all exact arithmetic and failed acquisition/verification is
 T <= 2^(beta+c+2q) poly(N), S <= 2^q poly(N)+2^c poly(N) (or stream local rows), CM <= (T+1)S.
Retain polynomially many node tables per guard, stream between guards; no exponential predecessor history required for count output. A witness of an attained histogram key can be reconstructed by a positive-count prefix self-reduction with at most 2n additional calls, or stored positive local transitions. A pair-event witness is a rejected word, not a satisfying assignment for the original problem. A global positive Hunter credit witness requires MS11's separate additive-credit procedure with exact pair terms and certified closure. Discovery of a minimum cycle rank across arbitrary bases is not assumed. This gives an alternate polynomial regime beta+c+q=O(log N), even when the union support component is large. It generalizes the one-dimensional maximum-score intersection forest specialization actually tested here; the full four-weight/tag histogram implementation was NOT executed.

Closure protocol: compute base affine consistency and certified facts first; on the specific base family the explicit zero/unit witness basis proves exact full affine hull. Before a pair-guard branch, use exact local maximum-score tables and propagate forced bits; restart after each insertion and repeat at stagnation. In the executable each cutset word is a separate guarded summand, with local forced closure before the forest count. It is not a claim to implement every certified rule in the complete HCL portfolio. Full single-event affine closure, strict physical reconstruction, general weighted pair histograms, complete cross-channel closure and measured bit-machine CM are unexecuted. No guard-local result promoted to unconditional base information.
