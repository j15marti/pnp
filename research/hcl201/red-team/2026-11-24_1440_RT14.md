## A. One-star AND compiler (PROVED)
Work with signed four-color relative-exclusion graph and explicit affine exterior chart. A private vertex h has four exterior leaves with colors 0, a=(x,z), b=(1,y), a+b (all arithmetic GF2). All four incident edges forbid equality. The private hub can be colored iff the exterior colors fail to cover GF2^2. Because b is nonzero, they have exactly two distinct values when a,b linearly dependent, and four otherwise. Hence
    exists h legal iff det(a,b)=xy+z=0.
Every legal triple x,y,z has exactly TWO hub colors, every illegal triple zero. This is an actual local signed-color graph, not a symbolic unsupported oracle. Each boundary bit is an affine expression involving at most two chart variables. The projected good and bad predicates each have a disjoint union of two affine events: fix x=0 or x=1, and enforce z=xy or z=1+xy respectively. Individual rank <=3, individual observation trellis width <=3 and deterministic mask automaton <=16 states. Each physical star graph is a tree of treewidth one. These bounds refer to the graph BEFORE adding the shared affine chart. The entire source including chart may have high width.

For any proper 3-CNF F on n variables with m clauses, introduce one auxiliary p_i per clause. Let a_i,b_i,c_i be the falsity bits of its literals. Two stars enforce p_i=a_i b_i and 0=p_i c_i. A source assignment extends iff all clauses true; the chart assignment of p_i is unique. Independent private hub choices then yield exactly 2^(2m) lifts per source model. The graph is a disjoint union of 2m degree-four stars with 8m distinct leaves. All shifts zero, chart sparsity <=2 per observed bit. Source/chart encoding and graph creation polynomial (O((n+m) log(n+m)) bits using sparse identifiers); verification and reverse witness projection polynomial. Thus decision on disjoint affine-synchronized stars is NP-complete, exact projected count equals #SAT(F), and exact star-graph count with deterministic exterior chart divided by 2^(2m) equals #SAT(F). This is not a strict E4/B4 compiler; any strict physical transfer requires an independently authenticated chart and lift adapter, including multiplicities. No L/L2 tags or budgets erased.
Consequence: constant-size positive AND negative affine normal forms, small single-event trellis width, and trivial local geometry are jointly insufficient for a universal polynomial composition theorem. MS14 needs its actual credit and joint-prefix hypotheses; I14 its authenticated chart width; BD08 its actual spectral widths; DS08 its charged joint cuts. Do not infer any bound on those parameters from the star geometry. This is a complexity reduction, not an unconditional time lower bound.

## B. Infinite neighboring family and local closure (PROVED)
Start with all eight proper clauses on three bits. Replace a clause (a OR b OR c) by (a OR b OR u), (!u OR c OR v), (!u OR c OR !v), fresh u,v. Existential equivalence follows by combining the last two into (!u OR c); if old clause false then deleting any one of the three new clauses admits u,v satisfying the other two. For deletion of another old clause extend its deletion witness through the split. Induction gives minimally UNSAT F_j, n=3+2j,m=8+2j. Flip the first literal sign in any one clause: a witness of deletion falsifies all original literals in that clause and hence satisfies the changed one. Every such neighbor is SAT. The two-star compiler preserves this property and only a constant number of chart coefficients/offsets change per literal flip.

Root closure repertoire: affine consistency, then for every local gate enumerate its at most eight triples consistent with current chart; test actual four-color lifts; an empty local support rejects; infer every affine identity of surviving triples and restart after each new equation. This is sound unconditional closure on root; child deductions belong only to their retained guard. The AND graph {(0,0,0),(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(1,1,1)} has full 3D affine hull. The pinned-zero gate has full relative 2D hull on its two free inputs (00,01,10). Each proper clause uses distinct variables and a fresh p_i, so every such local identity is already represented by the chart. Consequently root closure stagnates on every F_j and all its signed neighbors. This does not cover arbitrary multi-gate or other cooperative deductions. The split chain is an easy structural family, not a diagonal-hard family.

## C. Control-subspace cover, polynomial discovery (PROVED)
After affine closure, rechart remaining variables as free generators t in GF2^d. Consider a system of explicit affine equations and factored quadratic equations; each nonlinear product is (u_i*t+c_i)(v_i*t+e_i). This includes A's gates and determinant expressions by using both product pairs. Keep all actual linear terms and constants. Define h as the minimum dimension of a subspace K of the dual chart containing at least one of u_i,v_i for every product. Zero forms are already covered. No assumption that a minimum K is provided. With a coordinate-form registry, h equals minimum vertex-cover size: the set of basis vectors contained in K must be a vertex cover and is linearly independent; conversely span of a cover is valid. Thus exact minimum acquisition is NP-hard for arbitrary pair registries, even though the construction below does not require it.

Greedy algorithm: S=0; scan product pairs; if neither form lies in S, append both and update Gaussian basis. Previously covered pairs stay covered. On each such iteration, some endpoint belongs to any fixed optimal K, and this endpoint is outside the old S. Therefore dim(S intersect K) increases by >=1. At most h iterations; each adds <=2 dimensions. Hence
    h <= r=dim S <=2h.
Discovery is deterministic polynomial, including failed containment tests. No optimum or favorable chart oracle. If one already has affine equations, work in their quotient chart; in the checker, include their homogeneous row span in the maintained basis and count only newly added independent forms.

Branch only on a basis of S, with all trial assignments as scoped affine guards. Run certified affine/gate closure before branching, after every deduction, and at stagnation. On any guard fixing S, at least one factor of each product has constant value, so all quadratic equations become affine. Solve by Gaussian elimination, recover an actual chart assignment, and verify original gates and color lifts. Reject only when every guard is inconsistent; exact projected counting adds 2^nullity over the disjoint consistent linear fibers. Existing affine restrictions may force some control coordinates, allowing redundant branches to be skipped. For general factored equations, symbolic linearization is exact; a new nonlinear protected constraint requires a separate adapter. For gate-only specialization local hull closure itself recovers the linear gate equation once a control form is fixed.
T <= 2^r poly(N) <=4^h poly(N); S_peak <= poly(N) using sequential DFS with at most d chart snapshots, original registry, guard equations and verified reconstruction. CM <=(T+1) S_peak in same execution. Count bit lengths O(d) plus physical multiplicity exponent if A applies. DAG can be generated and discarded depth-first; retaining every visited node costs <=2^r poly(N), explicitly not charged as polynomial peak space for that optional implementation. No assumed sharing, no free replay or complete semantic closure. Polynomial for h=O(log N), and fixed-parameter in h. General cooperative closure operations have their own costs; theorem covers stated local closure and affine system. A supplied chart might improve h; discovery of universally small h or alternative geometry is OPEN. Large h not necessary for difficulty (independent products can be solved separately).
