## Exact core and signed polynomial
Over F2, a rank-two packet excludes L_i x=s_i, with U_i=rowspan L_i. Work on independent coordinates for span of all U_i after the actually acquired unconditional equations have been substituted; no hidden guards or nonlinear constraints. If a guard remains, include it in the actual chart or decline. The max deficiency is d*=max_J(|J|-rank(sum U_i)). For a maximizing C, every S outside C has quotient rank rank(U_C+sum_{i in S}U_i)-rank(U_C)>=|S|. Rado independent representatives in the quotient therefore give a simultaneous lift of every satisfying core word to all outside owners, preserving core observations. DS15 gives polynomial matroid-intersection acquisition. d*=0 implies SAT. In C write m=|C|, r=rank(U_C), d=m-r=d*>0. Every J subset C satisfies |J|-rank(U_J)<=d. The same result holds without minimality for any such chart satisfying this Hall condition.

For every owner i and each of its three nonzero directions a, let ell_{ia}(x)=a(x)+a(s_i) (the RHS denotes the corresponding observation-space character of s_i). The packet is satisfied iff some ell is 1; for a satisfying x exactly two directions have ell=1 and they span U_i. Let z_{ia} be independent indeterminates, f_i(x,z)=sum_a z_{ia}ell_{ia}(x), and P(z)=sum_{x in F2^r} prod_i f_i(x,z) as a formal characteristic-two polynomial.

THEOREM: on the Hall core, P is nonzero iff SAT. UNSAT makes every product zero. For a satisfying x*, restrict each owner's three directions to its two satisfied ones; their spans stay U_i. Rado's maximum independent transversal formula gives rank r (the full set has deficiency d and no subset exceeds d). Choose r independent satisfied directions from distinct owners and choose a satisfied direction for each of the remaining d. The selected equations ell=1 have unique solution x*, so the corresponding z monomial has coefficient exactly 1. Conversely any nonzero coefficient is a unique full-rank consistent selection (a consistent affine system of rank <r has even size), hence certifies SAT. This is exact formal identity, not a free identity oracle.

## Low-deficiency evaluation and witness extraction
Evaluate weights in K=GF(2^k), |K|>=2m (and >=2). Then f_i(x)=c_i+sum_j A_ij x_j. Reduce product by x_j^2=x_j; sum on the binary cube kills every monomial except x_1...x_r in characteristic two. For d=1 (m=r+1), the desired coefficient is
  sum_i c_i det A[rows except i, all cols]
  + sum_j sum_{i<h} A_ij A_hj det A[rows except i,h; cols except j].
Every term that hits all r columns either uses one constant or repeats exactly one column twice. Remaining bijections are permanents which equal determinants in characteristic two. For general d enumerate constant rows and disjoint repeated-column row groups of sizes h_j>=2 with c+sum_j(h_j-1)=d; remaining rows must biject to remaining columns. Multiply the selected coefficient entries by the corresponding determinant. Order repeated columns once to avoid duplicate counting. At most 2d exceptional rows and d repeated columns, giving N^{O(d+1)} bit operations per evaluation, including field construction, determinants, discovery and failed enumeration. For d=1 the displayed direct formula uses at most m+r*binom(m,2) determinants of order <=r, O(m^6) field operations with naive Gaussian elimination. Field degree O(log m); deterministic search for an irreducible polynomial of that degree and its standard finite-field tests are polynomial. Stream exceptional choices: polynomial peak space (also for variable d) and same-run CM <= (T+1)S, with fixed polynomial factors for source maps and chart/lift work.

By Schwartz-Zippel, a random independent uniform weight evaluation of a nonzero degree-m P is nonzero with probability >=1-m/|K|>=1/2. UNSAT always gives zero. Once nonzero, fix each owner's weight group to one of its three unit vectors that keeps the evaluation nonzero: linearity in that group guarantees at least one. At most 3m additional evaluations. At end solve the chosen binary affine equations by Gaussian elimination, lift through the core and source compiler, verify actual witness. This is a restricted RP decision algorithm, or a sound SAT-or-UNKNOWN consumer in the exact cooperative scheduler. A zero trial or even repeated zeros must NOT be exported as a certified UNSAT or unconditional deduction. Certified closure runs before admission, after accepted deductions and at stagnation. No random semantic oracle. Runtime N^{O(d+1)} including witness extraction; fixed d is polynomial, log d or d=O(log N) is not a general polynomial guarantee. No deterministic hitting-set claim.

## Limitation and concrete closure target
The earlier RT21 exponential number of signed-transversal words does not imply exponential work: the coefficient formula aggregates those words. But rank-Hall deficiency can itself grow even on an easy family with full solution affine hull: all monotone pair clauses x_i OR x_j, i<j, n>=4. There are m=binom(n,2), rank n, unique maximum-deficiency set is the full registry (any rank k subset has <=binom(k,2) owners), d=m-n. The full solution set is all-ones plus single zeros, so its affine hull is full. A common-coordinate 2-SAT terminal solves it, not an unrestricted obstruction. This invalidates universal small raw d, including after mere affine-hull closure. The two new precise obligations are a deterministic identity test for P (even d=1 merits independent study) and uniform polynomial coverage of arbitrary d through another exact reduction/terminal or improved aggregate evaluation. A 2^{O(d)} poly(N) evaluator plus a logarithmic residual d bound would suffice for cost, but neither is proved. Finite-field random evaluation cannot be treated as exact rejection. General coverage remains open. This is a direct attack on U-PROGRESS, not a claim that P=NP is near proved.
