## 2. New theorem: minimum binary-fiber variation rank = vertex cover
Let tau(G) be minimum vertex cover size. For every exact binary-affine representation S_e=(a_e0+W_e0) union (a_e1+W_e1), allow empty fibers, and set W=sum_e,a W_ea and r=dim W. Then
    min over all such representations r = tau(G).
Proof: A nonempty affine subset of a three-point support has size one or two. At least one fiber must have size two, hence W contains one of e_u, e_v, e_u+e_v for every edge. Let q:F2^n -> F2^n/W. For every edge either q(e_u)=0, q(e_v)=0, or q(e_u)=q(e_v). Select vertices whose images are a basis of the quotient (images of standard basis span it). They form an independent set: both images nonzero and distinct for every pair, so no edge can join them. Their complement is a vertex cover of size n-(n-r)=r. This proves the lower bound and constructs a cover from any admissible W by Gaussian elimination. Conversely, take a vertex cover C. For each edge choose an endpoint u in C; use pair {0,e_u} and singleton {e_v}. All variation lies in span{e_u:u in C}, giving r<=|C|. Minimize both sides. QED.

Thus the exact minimum-rank acquisition problem is NP-complete in decision form (rank <= k), by the classic NP-completeness of NODE COVER / VERTEX COVER. The input-to-support mapping is polynomial; the certificate selects one of three directions per edge (the corresponding pair plus leftover singleton), and rank is verifiable by Gaussian elimination. The hardness remains even when the target is fixed to zero, so the original decision instance is always SAT. No lower bound on computing SAT follows. In particular, NP-hard exact optimization does not rule out an O(log N) approximation target: endpoints of a maximal graph matching give a polynomial 2-approximation for this special rank-minimization class. One can use such a cover directly without exact optimization. No analogous 2-approximation for arbitrary two-dimensional subspaces is asserted here.

Physical tagged matching realization: for each source edge make a private simple K4 on vertices 0,1,2,3. Put labels 0,e_u,e_v on 01,02,03 respectively; all other edges label zero. Exactly three perfect matchings exist, selecting exactly one of those edges. Thus their syndrome support is exactly S_e, and a tag determines a unique local matching. Union the components; full occupancy is constant and independence is exact. The union is a simple tagged matching graph, not claimed to be a strict four-regular signed E4/B4 graph or a geometric wheel embedding. Binary guards are new exact local selectors of these matching choices, not automatically original tetrahedral occupancy coordinates. If a binary two-port affine representation is supplied, I02 can implement it, but any external guard/occupancy constraints must still be preserved. The reduction addresses the explicit tagged-matching/fiber model in the portfolio and cannot transfer geometry for free.

## 3. Infinite paired controls, exact global support, and why the portfolio must keep alternatives
For a connected tree T on n>=2 vertices, sum_e S_e = F2^n minus the all-ones vector. Necessity: selecting at most one endpoint from each of n-1 edge factors cannot produce n odd vertex coordinates. Sufficiency: for any target not all ones, choose a root absent from its support. Each demanded vertex chooses its parent edge, distinct for distinct vertices; each other edge chooses zero. This realizes the target exactly and reconstructs the corresponding K4 matching choices. For connected cyclic G, take a spanning unicyclic subgraph, assign each cycle vertex its distinct incoming cycle edge, and each off-cycle vertex its parent edge towards the cycle. Every vertex has its own distinct edge, so any target is realizable. Isolated vertices are tree components of size one. On general G, supports factor by connected components. Thus exact decision and witness take O(n+m) graph operations plus parsing and explicit label/output costs: each tree component must omit at least one target coordinate. This solves the entire rank-optimization reduction class, without computing tau.

For path P_n, tau=floor(n/2) by alternate-vertex cover and floor(n/2) disjoint edges. Choose UNSAT c=all ones and SAT c'=c+e_1 (Hamming-distance-one targets); in fact every proper subset is SAT. Each local hull has rank two and no nonzero translation (odd support cardinality). The global hull is F2^n and the global support has odd cardinality 2^n-1, so even global translation absorption cannot remove a nonzero direction. Translation by a nonzero vector partitions a stable set into two-element orbits, impossible for odd cardinality. Every grouped nonempty subforest likewise has odd support cardinality, including isolated coordinates constrained to zero. Bare S01 rank-one peeling has no eligible factor at W=0. Every exact binary-affine decomposition of these fixed factors has r>=floor(n/2). MS02's literal full character loop would thus use 2^floor(n/2) characters on this representation if run without alternate pruning. This is superpolynomial in dense encoded N=O(n^2). It is NOT a compulsory time lower bound; tree recognition, integer capacity, or local target-conditioned propagation/DP resolve the family cheaply. In particular target-conditioned mixed closure can expose leaf consequences, so do not report stagnation of the entire cooperative closure. Prefix cut width is at most one in path order, an explicit positive control for I01/GD01. Complete graphs have tau=n-1 but all targets SAT, so rank optimization can be expensive even when every decision is easy.

Optional grouping observation: for n>=3, a punctured n-cube cannot be a union of just two affine cosets. Any proper affine coset has <=2^(n-1) points. If one has dimension <=n-2, the union has <=3*2^(n-2) points; two hyperplanes either coincide, are complementary (union full, forbidden here), or meet in dimension n-2, again <=3*2^(n-2). But 2^n-1 exceeds this bound. Thus grouping a whole tree into one factor cannot simply rescue the exact binary-affine premise. The punctured support has a compact exact Boolean predicate instead. This is a representation obstruction, not a lower bound on arbitrary grouping or guarded DAGs.
