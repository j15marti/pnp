## A. Sharp positive separated cover theorem (PROVED)
For a nonempty affine packet projected onto disjoint incident edge blocks Y_e, let J be the projection and B_e its exact marginal. After subtracting marginal constraints write J={y in product B_e : XOR_e H_e y_e=b} with k independent residual coupling rows. Let r_e=rank(H_e on dir B_e). Images H_e B_e are affine; XOR on their product has rank k. The number of feasible local syndrome tuples (H_e y_e)_e is therefore exactly 2^c, c=sum_e r_e-k. MS18-E correctly constructs a disjoint positive Cartesian refinement of that size.

New lower bound: any nonempty rectangle product_e C_e contained in J, even with arbitrary nonaffine C_e, must have constant H_e y_e on each C_e. Otherwise hold other blocks fixed and vary just e, contradicting the required syndrome. Thus every rectangle covers only one feasible syndrome tuple; every such tuple must be covered. The minimum number of arbitrary positive rectangles is EXACTLY 2^c. This applies to overlapping covers and nonnegative weighted separated indicator representations: nonnegative summands cannot have support outside J. For a single parity constraint on d>=2 independent bits, k=1 and r_e=1 for each edge, so c=d-1. Every rectangle is a point on syndrome coordinates, even though signed character expansion uses two terms. Equality is not a lower bound on decision work, and auxiliary-state/factored positive representations are not ruled out. The count is for an individual packet, not an arbitrary union where overlap may simplify the final support.

## B. Exact positive syndrome-streamed affine-hull theorem (PROVED)
Assume a supplied and authenticated owner forest on independent edge coordinate blocks Y_e and private coordinate blocks X_i (plus unused block), with each owner's accepting support an explicit union of actual affine packets A_it. No hidden signed terms, no arbitrary external guards, and all observers/owners retained. For each packet compute J_it, B_ite, H_ite and b_it as in A using Gaussian elimination. Let k_it be its residual coupling rank. Let K_it be the private kernel of A_it projected to J_it. Choose an anchor (x0,y0) in A_it and a linear section T:dir J_it -> X_i. Extend T linearly to the product of homogeneous marginal directions (basis extension), or the full ambient edge space. Split into edge maps T_e. Then every legal edge tuple y in J_it has private realizations exactly
x_i=x0+sum_e T_e(y_e+y0_e)+K_it.
For y outside J_it this formula need not describe a legal lift; never accept it unless the syndrome equation is met. Set local contribution anchor x0+sum_e T_e y0_e with private kernel K_it; each edge contributes its actual y_e plus both endpoint private shifts T_e y_e embedded into global chart. This is a direct generalization of MS18-B; extension choices may alter partial contributions but not the set of completed legal words.

Root the forest. For fixed packet t at i, store a map M_i(t,s), s in F2^k_it, for attainable contribution sums in the subtree excluding its incoming edge, conditional on the incoming edge contributing syndrome s. At a leaf, only s=b_it is admissible, with local private anchor/kernel payload. For a child j and edge e, fixed t at i and child packet u, acquire by Gaussian elimination the edge set
C_e(t,u,a,s)= B_ite intersect B_jue intersect {H_ite y=a} intersect {H_jue y=s}.
It is empty or affine. Map it to actual y plus both correlated private shifts, obtaining exact affine hull payload D_e(t,u,a,s). For each parent syndrome a, union_hull over u,s of D_e + M_j(u,s). Starting at the local private payload at i with cumulative child syndrome 0, process children one at a time using XOR convolution of syndrome keys, Minkowski-add payloads and union_hull when keys collide. After all children, cumulative syndrome c corresponds to incoming s=b_it+c. At root require c=b_it and union over root labels. Forest components and unused coordinates Minkowski-add.

Payload H(S)=(actual anchor r, span{z+r:z in S}), empty distinct. For union use one anchor, both bases and anchor difference; for independent Minkowski sums use XOR anchor and span of both bases. These identities are exact (MS18-C). The independence required is conditional on fixed packet and syndrome keys: distinct child blocks are disjoint, and edge intersections use one identical y at both endpoints. Induction establishes that each key contains the exact hull of attainable partial contributions, not that every hull point is attainable. All anchor choices remain actual partial contributions and the root anchor is a real full satisfying word; verify on original factors. Empty root iff UNSAT. At nonempty root all forced affine rows are exactly annihilator of the root difference space with RHS evaluated on root anchor. Overlapping packet labels are harmless for existence/hull; this does NOT count distinct source models. It does not preserve exact boundary support by replacing it with its hull. No signed labels used, no 2^c packet materialization, no query oracle. ## D. Infinite neighboring controls with closure stagnation (PROVED)
Center on n>=2 edge bits s_i imposes XOR_i s_i=b. Each child has two accepting affine packets: s_i=0 with private outgoing two-bit edge y_i arbitrary, OR s_i=1 with y_i=11. Its leaf accepts y_i!=11, as the overlapping positive union y_i,1=0 OR y_i,2=0. Thus every complete solution has s_i=0. The two inputs b=0 and b=1 differ in one RHS bit, same geometry, all local ranks, label counts and center coupling rank one. For b=0 exactly 3^n distinct chart words; hull has dimension 2n. For b=1 none. Every child and leaf accepting hull is full; on the center parity guard every individual s_i is still free for n>=2, so the child and leaf hull intersected with that guard span the entire guarded chart (other s values can be chosen to meet parity; equivalently the independent remaining coordinates are free). The center parity is the only owner-local affine row: closure stagnates on both labels. The center minimum flat positive refinement is 2^(n-1); the streamed table has at most two syndrome keys, each child has two labels and the leaf two labels. This is a restricted representation obstruction, not computational hardness: ordinary local message passing also resolves it. No claim of a strict degree-four physical realization of the high-arity owner; a parity accumulator can encode the coupling in a linear-sized affine DAG, but source adapter and physical transfer must be authenticated separately.
