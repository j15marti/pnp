## A. Local cross-equation theorem (PROVED)
For every explicit authenticated collection of Boolean or nonnegative integer factors f_i=sum_t c_it [A_it], with signed integer coefficients and affine sets, assume BA11's forest and independent registry of edge blocks E_e and private blocks P_i. Each factor depends only on its incident E_e and P_i. All blocks are independent in the common chart; unused bits independent. Verify the tree and direct-sum rank test and source block containment by Gaussian elimination, or decline. Authenticate the registry and all actual consumers; Booleanity is checked by BD11's sum(f_i^2-f_i)=0, but that alone is not source authentication. No implicit arbitrary circuit/OR expansion, matching IDs, physical multiplicities or occupancy/syndrome budgets are discarded. A supplied affine chart is parameterized first.

For each nonempty affine local term A, eliminate private coordinates. Its image J on the incident edge blocks is affine and its nonempty private fibers have constant size 2^p. For each edge block e let J_e be its marginal. Put C=product J_e. Then J subset C and k=dim C-dim J >=0 is the exact number of additional independent cross-equations needed within C. Compute marginal equations by Gaussian projection; extend their combined independent rows by k independent rows of a defining system for J. Write these cross-equations as sum_e H_e y_e=b with k rows. Therefore pointwise
 [J(y)] = 2^-k sum_{lambda in F2^k} (-1)^(lambda dot b) product_e ([J_e(y_e)] (-1)^(lambda dot H_e y_e)).
This is the ordinary character identity for the indicator of zero; no probability independence is assumed. Proof of k: the marginal systems have disjoint supports and define C; a basis extension to the rowspace of J has dimension codim(J)-codim(C)=dim C-dim J. Empty terms dropped by consistency check. Coefficient c_it*2^p multiplies the expansion. BA11 is exactly the k=0 special case.

Let L'_i=sum_{nonempty terms t}2^k_it. Each new label is an original signed term ID and lambda; attach coefficient a_i(alpha) and per-edge affine support J_i,alpha,e and character mask q_i,alpha,e. The exact edge interaction is
 K_e(alpha,beta)=sum_{y in J_i,alpha,e intersect J_j,beta,e intersect G_e} (-1)^((q_i,alpha,e+q_j,beta,e) dot y).
Here G_e is an actual edge-local affine guard, if any. Gaussian elimination computes this sum: for nonempty affine coset a+U it is zero if the character fails to annihilate U, otherwise sign (-1)^(q dot a) times 2^dim U. The tree recurrence M_i(alpha)=a_i(alpha) product_child j sum_beta K_ij(alpha,beta) M_j(beta) is exact by expansion and independent edge summation. Sum root messages and multiply independent unused-coordinate count. Negative intermediate coefficients are not witnesses. With D_i=max_t k_it scale every local coefficient by 2^D_i so all arithmetic is integer; divide final result by 2^sum D_i, exactly. This scaling is not an enumeration of global lambda tuples.

In particular max k_it=O(log N) suffices even if total cross rank grows linearly and edge dimensions are unbounded. Storage may stream matrices, but the stated bound allows materialization and predecessor/reconstruction buffers. ## B. Arbitrary single parity probe without recertification (PROVED)
The product chart coordinates split an arbitrary affine parity q into characters on independent edge/private/unused blocks, with an overall offset sign. For edge blocks insert q_e into K_e once, not once per endpoint. For each local term and private character q_p, the weighted fiber sum is zero if q_p does not annihilate the common private fiber kernel. Otherwise choose a Gaussian affine section p(y) on J; the weighted sum is 2^p times a sign character on y. Distribute its linear part to incident edge characters and its offset to the coefficient. Thus private probes do not add cross rows or labels. For an unused block use the ordinary affine character sum. Private-local pins may be intersected with each term before projection; new k must be charged (or use prefix pins for reconstruction as below). For exact root count Z and character sum C(q), the count at parity q=b is (Z+(-1)^b C(q))/2. This handles ONE arbitrary parity query, without asserting arbitrary cumulative parity guards are free. ## C. Sharp restricted obstruction and infinite neighboring controls (PROVED)
Across two independent edge blocks, let J={(x,y):x=y}, x,y in F2^r. Both marginals full, k=r. The characteristic matrix is the 2^r by 2^r identity. Any flat separated signed representation sum_{j=1}^L a_j g_j(x)h_j(y) has real matrix rank <=L, so L>=2^r, even if g,h arbitrary real functions. Character expansion attains 2^r. This proves a representation barrier for the specified packet-label terminal, NOT a runtime lower bound; direct Gaussian equality or direct message transport solves it cheaply. For a general nonempty affine J in two blocks, its cross rank k gives the same lower bound: row/column partition by surjective cross-syndromes on the marginals, a permutation of 2^k nonempty disjoint all-ones blocks, hence rank 2^k. Multiway case only claim the upper bound above.

Infinite explicit path family with n edge blocks of r bits each: left endpoint forces first bit 0, every internal node equates consecutive first bits, right endpoint forces first bit b. Root b=1 UNSAT; flip only endpoint b to 0, SAT count 2^{n(r-1)}. Every internal term has k=1 with two full marginals. Raw BA11 Cartesian test on this declared block registry declines, but RT17 needs two labels per internal owner for arbitrary n,r. A parity comparing the two endpoint first bits has character sum Z. This is a deliberately easy affine family (ordinary affine closure resolves it); the executed scaling bypasses that shortcut to exercise the kernel, not to claim necessity of RT17. Large r here is a conservative interface, not semantic minimum. Strengthen symbolically for r>=3 by also requiring every tail (r-1 bits) nonzero at both adjacent factors. Each internal factor has four signed affine terms from multiplying by the two complements of tail-zero, each k=1; endpoints two terms. Each edge observation space now has full semantic dimension: any nonzero tail shift changes the tail-zero predicate; first-bit shift changes an equality or endpoint pin. Count for b=0 is (2^{r-1}-1)^n, b=1 zero. The signed expansion still uses at most eight labels per internal factor. This strengthened family was not executed. Neither family is diagonal-hard; both have cheap alternative affine plus local counting solutions. The adversarial rank bound above is for representation only.
