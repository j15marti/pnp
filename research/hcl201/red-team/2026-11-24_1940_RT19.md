## A. Fixed-girth signed compiler (PROVED)
Input finite explicitly indexed simple graph G=(V,E), each edge uv forbids x_u XOR x_v=s_e in GF2^2, with no additional constraints. Pins can be transported to a chosen copy, but unpinned low-order statistics below must then be re-evaluated. For every fixed integer g>=3 choose an edge-minimal non-4-colorable finite simple graph H of girth >=g. Existence follows from Erdos 1959 (RT12 gives elementary probabilistic proof); choose a finite graph then delete redundant edges and isolates. Fix edge ab of H. Set J=H-ab, with terminals a,b. J is 4-colorable; every proper coloring has a=b, else H is 4-colorable. By permutation symmetry of four colors, for each fixed equal terminal color there are exactly K>0 colorings of J, independent of color. For unequal terminals there are none. A path between terminals in J has length >=g-1, since adding ab forms a cycle. J may be authenticated by fixed exhaustive search; for fixed g this is a constant, not a free oracle, and no polynomial bound in variable g is asserted.

Replace each vertex of degree d>0 by an ordered chain of d distinct copies (one per incidence); keep one copy of each isolate. Replace each consecutive equality by a fresh J with independent private interiors. Retain each original edge as one signed edge between its actual incidence copies; all gadget edges have shift zero. One may insert q additional copies anywhere strictly between consecutive incidence copies, with no external edges on extra copies. Let L=sum_v max(d_v-1,0)+q. For every assignment of original colors, all copies must agree; every legal source assignment has precisely K^L lifts, and every illegal source assignment has none. Hence projected support is exact and
    Z_new=K^L Z_source.
This also preserves a one-edge sign edit on an original edge pointwise, with the same K^L. A source witness lifts using a fixed representative J coloring and common XOR translation; a compiled witness decodes by reading any copy and verifying source constraints. No uncontrolled multiplicity or semantic merge.

Maximum degree <=2 Delta(H)+1, independent of source degree; every copy belongs to at most two equality gadgets and at most one original edge. The graph is simple. Any simple cycle either lies inside a gadget (length >=g), or traverses a gadget between its distinct terminals (length >=g-1) and at least one edge outside it: original edges alone form a matching, and an excursion from and to the same terminal cannot be part of a simple cycle leaving that gadget. Thus girth >=g. For each fixed g, construction and lift are uniform polynomial in explicit input length N, with O_g(n+m+q) vertices and edges, O_g((n+m+q)log(n+m+q+2)) indexed bits; q is chosen polynomial or supplied in explicit length. Constant-g exhaustive construction may be enormous. 4-colorability is NP-complete (reduce 3-coloring by adding one universal vertex), so even bounded-degree, girth>=g, zero-shift instances are NP-complete for a degree bound depending on g. Signed version is in NP as well. The large-girth coloring hardness itself is classical; the contribution here is exact source-fiber multiplicity, sign-edit transport and next all-edge family in our model.

## B. Infinite all-edge-critical family (PROVED)
Apply A to K5, with q arbitrary additional copies between the first and second incidences of vertex 0. Let h=|V(H)|, f=|E(H)|. The all-zero root has
    vertices = 20+q+(15+q)(h-2),
    edges = 10+(15+q)(f-1).
It is UNSAT by the equality macro. We show every edge deletion has an explicit coloring, hence the entire expanded graph is edge-critical, not just the ten original edges.
1. Deleted original edge: color its source endpoints equally, other three vertices distinctly. Lift each intact gadget.
2. Deleted internal edge f' of a copy of J: H-f' is 4-colorable by criticality and includes ab, so a,b have different colors. Its deleted edge endpoints must agree. Give the four other source vertices four distinct colors. The affected chain splits at that gadget into two nonempty sets of ORIGINAL incidence ports (true even at inserted copies), with disjoint nonempty neighbor sets. Assign the left segment a color used by a right neighbor, and the right segment a color used by a left neighbor. These colors are distinct and each segment avoids all its external neighbor colors. Keep every other source chain constant. Lift intact J copies and lift the defective J using H-f' coloring relabeled by a four-color permutation mapping its distinct terminal colors to the desired pair. All nondeleted edges legal and the deleted endpoints agree. This proves edge criticality for all q and all fixed g.

For each root, shifting ANY one edge from 0 to ANY of the three nonzero two-bit words creates a SAT neighbor. The same deletion witness works for all three shifts because its changed endpoints are equal. Conversely every such SAT word has equal endpoints on that edge, or it would color the original UNSAT root. Thus its satisfying relation is exactly the deletion relation, not merely equisatisfiable. There are exactly 3|E_new| distinct one-sign neighbors. This family is infinite for EACH fixed g as q grows, uniformly generated given the finite gadget. Unlike RT12's existential critical-graph family, this gives an explicit scalable recipe and reconstruction for internal as well as external sign changes. It is deliberately an EASY adversary with a supplied macro manifest; no unrestricted runtime lower bound follows.

## C. Exact local moment firewall and closure
For any selected F of fewer than g edges, (V_new,F) is a forest. Every assignment of edge differences extends to 4^components vertex words. Therefore, for ALL shifts, the entire joint bad-event indicator law on F is independent Bernoulli(1/4). This reproduces RT12, with stronger uniform source compilation and explicit all-edge neighbors. In particular every intersection probability is 4^(-|F|). Under common relative edge pins P, the same claim holds on disjoint F if |P|+|F|<g, by the same forest argument. Every individual allowed edge relation has full affine hull (three allowed relative colors span the two-bit difference plane); root owner-local affine closure is stagnant. Under pins in a forest, reclose the actual affine chart; never export a guard-specific fact as unconditional. A method reading only these bounded-order laws and unsigned geometry cannot decide the opposite labels. An algorithm reading the shifts, graph or exact macro relations is not restricted this way.

The positive escape is exact equality macro contraction: for authenticated intact J copies with private interiors, verify fixed finite template, terminal IDs, signs and absence of external private consumers, then insert the two necessary terminal equality rows, reclose, and contract while retaining witness/multiplicity records. A changed internal gadget must NOT be contracted as intact; use its actual guarded terminal table. For fixed g such a table has only 16 entries and can be obtained by finite exhaustive acquisition. Supplied manifests give polynomial discovery and reconstruction; unauthenticated instances may be searched for finite templates at O(n^h) cost, with each proposed replacement checked, but no universal disjoint-cover discovery theorem is claimed. This is an alternative escape to BA13 graphic guarded projection and I19 matching, not a general tractability theorem.
