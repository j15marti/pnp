## PROVED: exact complement-component characterization
Let H be a connected 3-uniform hypergraph of distinct variable triples, n occurring vertices, m owners; define M:F2^n -> D=(F2^2)^m by Mx_i=(x_a+x_b,x_a+x_c) at owner i=(a,b,c). Each signed NAE owner permits exactly three of four local relative words, T_i. Let Q=D/im M, pi the quotient, and U_i the projected coordinate slot. SAT iff zero belongs to sum pi(T_i). Original source lift follows from solving Mx equal to the chosen legal tuple. For nonempty J subset of owner IDs, D_J denotes coordinate slots supported on J. Let c_J be number of connected components on ALL n vertices after deleting J (isolated vertices count). Mx in D_J iff x is constant on each remaining component. Since H is connected, ker M consists exactly of the global constants. Hence dim(D_J intersect im M)=c_J-1, dim pi(D_J)=2|J|-c_J+1. A nontrivial kernel occurs precisely when J disconnects H. If min owner cut lambda >=2, each U_i is injective; if lambda>=3, distinct pairs have zero intersection. Common adapted basis on the entire family cannot exist when lambda>=3: pairwise disjoint coordinate subspaces would have direct sum, whereas total kernel has dimension n-1. Root hull restriction is stagnant since each local word is attained by some source word when every other owner is relaxed to its full local hull. No claims about stronger closure.

## PROVED: all-subgroup exact support obstruction, stronger than MS29/BH22 sufficient-test failure
Assume lambda>=8, all slots punctured. For any J of k>=2 owners, the affine hull of the actual sum S_J=sum pi(T_i) is pi(D_J) (each three-word table spans its slot). There are only 3^k legal tuples, so |S_J|/2^(2k-c_J+1) <= 2^(c_J-1)(3/4)^k. If c_J=1 this is (3/4)^k and the actual map is injective, leaving >=7 holes. If c_J>=2, every component has at least lambda original crossing owners; each deleted owner counted at most three times, giving 3k>=lambda c_J. Thus density <= (1/2)(2^(3/8)*3/4)^k <1/2, since 2^3*3^8<4^8. In both cases the hull misses more than one word; for growing k the number missing is exponential in k (rank >=13k/8, and k>=3 gives density <1/2). Therefore no group of two or more original slots can be exactly replaced by a full or singly punctured affine table, regardless of subgroup discovery, coefficient signs, affine basis or method of proving exactness. Even a polynomial-size explicit list of missing points cannot represent the full support of growing groups in this form. This rules out the result of BA22/MS29/BH22 style fusion, not only their certificates. A sound such fusion is impossible at root; hull closure does not change these slots. Does not forbid separate nonlinear summaries, guard changes, partial target queries, matching/other terminals or nonlocal deductions. Cut minimum can be acquired in polynomial time: replace every triple by its three undirected pair edges (with multiplicity); each crossing triple contributes exactly two cut edges, so use ordinary weighted global mincut.
