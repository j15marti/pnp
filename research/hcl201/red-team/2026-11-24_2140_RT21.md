## Constructive repair and actual costs
All three unshifted owners together have exact affine allowed support L0. Generic discovery enumerates every subset of at most three owners, computes its actual joint observation row basis (rank <=6), enumerates at most 64 observations, and derives an actual necessary affine row or a contradiction. Substitute globally, discharge inactive packets, reapply singleton closure, then repeat pair/triple scans at stagnation. At most d independent deductions plus m inactive deletions; O((m+d+1)(m+1)^3 poly(N)) bit operations, polynomial peak space and same-execution CM <= (T+1)S. Charge all failed scans, actual row construction, storage, chart substitutions, reconstruction and verification. No DAG branching, no complete semantic hull oracle. This is a polynomial preprocessing even if it stagnates on general charts. At stagnation each unaltered unshifted triple has been discharged: under any accrued affine chart its allowed preimage is still affine, so if nontrivial it either rejects or supplies a further row. Induct through the expansion; for root and shift-propagated base-sign neighbor the remaining rank <=6. For arbitrary single sign change, only its ancestor path can be exceptional; all other private observations participating in surviving packets are fixed, so live observation rank <=6+3k, and exact enumeration is polynomial in m. No hierarchy supplied to the scan. The exceptional-path variant is symbolic, not executed. A recognized full four-owner macro reduces rank mass by six; individual affine deductions need not all decrease rank mass. This does not prove a universal terminal or universal credit. In fact the Fano base can be decided independently by MS21 PSD. On guarded charts the true guard is preserved, but the family-wide terminal guarantee here is for unguarded roots and stated neighbors.
