## 1. Reference-free complementarity reduction

Rado/matroid intersection yields an independent transversal a_j of the other r owners. Solve a_j*x=1+c_j(s) to get anchor x0 satisfying those owners. If omitted owner accepts x0, SAT. Otherwise each retained owner has exactly one other satisfied nonzero observation b_j at x0. In coordinates y_j=a_j(x+x0), let M_j be b_j in basis a. ## 2. Exact span for block-symmetric and simple-cycle SCCs
Graph arcs j->k for off-diagonal M_jk=1. If M is symmetric on a connected component C, summing equations cancels off-diagonal pairs, giving d_C*y=0, d_C=diagonal. This is the entire span. For each zero-diagonal vertex the singleton is legal. Connect one diagonal-one vertex to each other via a shortest path, split at intermediate diagonal-one vertices. Each resulting induced path has exactly two marked endpoints and zero-marked interior, so its indicator is legal. Adding zero-diagonal singletons cancels path interiors, yielding all even marked-coordinate differences. Thus span=ker(d_C), or full if d_C=0. Connected symmetric chart always satisfies any omitted rank-two owner, since the codimension is at most one.

For a simple directed cycle of length >=3, local constraint at selected j is successor bit=d_j. If all d_j=1, support is empty or all vertices: span{1}. If exactly one zero at z, successor(z) cannot be selected (otherwise propagation wraps around to z and contradicts its successor requirement); legal suffix paths ending at z span every other coordinate. If >=2 zeros, legal paths starting at any vertex and ending at first zero span full coordinates by adjacent path differences. Initial checker detected the single-zero exception; corrected before final successful run.

Now allow arbitrary directed arcs between SCCs, each SCC internally symmetric or simple directed cycle. Process sink SCCs first. For each fixed actual downstream word v, the effective local diagonal is d_C+M_CD v. Current block zero is always legal. Thus span of all extensions = direct sum of the downstream span and span of all local legal words over attainable diagonal shifts (subtract actual witnesses with identical downstream word). At zero downstream shift, symmetric block has full span or one hyperplane; simple cycle has full span, span{1}, or one coordinate hyperplane. Whenever base span not full and any nonzero downstream shift is attainable, these two local spans together are full: distinct nonzero diagonal labels give distinct hyperplanes in symmetric case (zero gives full); cycle nonfull labels give distinct coordinate hyperplanes or span{1} plus a coordinate hyperplane, otherwise full. Since shift map linear, some actual downstream basis witness produces nonzero shift iff one exists. ## 3. Exact principal-minor containment for the unrestricted missing subproblem
For any binary M and row ell, define H_M(z)=det(I+diag(z) M), squarefree coefficient for S equals det M[S,S]. Put M_j=M+e_j ell. Then ell annihilates W iff supp H_{M_j} subset supp H_M for every j. Proof: if ell kills every legal word then for each S any vector in ker M[S,S] extended by zeros is legal, so ell_S kills kernel. If principal minor singular, adding e_j ell cannot make it full rank (kernel remains). Conversely choose legal x with ell*x=1 having minimum support S. Then x_S=1 is in kernel; nullity >=2 would supply nonzero y in kernel with ell*y=0; x+y has smaller support and same ell-value, contradiction. Hence rank deficiency exactly one. Its nonzero right kernel x, left kernel u imply adj(M[S,S])=x u^T over F2. Choose j in S with u_j=1; rank-one determinant update gives det=ell_S adj(M[S,S]) e_j=1. So containment fails. This is exact reformulation, not a polynomial support-containment algorithm. Ordinary principal-minor equivalence (Chatterjee, Ghosh, Gurjar, Raj, STOC 2025, https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2024/150/) tests equality, not one-sided inclusion. A difference on an already nonsingular minor need not certify a legal target witness. Also see Berczi/Schwarcz rainbow circuits, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2022.112830 ; independent transversals alone do not supply labeled circuits.
