## 2. Exact projection and affine edge-color model
Let H=F2^2. A tree of t>=1 private vertices has total degree exactly four at each private vertex, with distinct independently observed exterior leaves, each incident to exactly one private vertex. Every edge uv forbids x_u+x_v=s_uv; no extra private pins, budgets or tags. The whole tree including leaves has 2t+2 leaves, so exterior boundary has d=4t+4 bits. Since it is a tree, affine switching eliminates all signs (choose a root gauge and propagate along edges); hence suffice zero signs. Signs translate the bad set, preserving all results.

Root at a private vertex incident to a distinguished exterior leaf; orient from that leaf. Each private vertex has one incoming edge and three child edges. Let B be boundary words with NO legal private color lift. DA07's mask recurrence independently proves: a nonroot subtree always has >=1 supported color; if its three incoming forbidden words from below are distinct, all forced, its only supported color is their XOR. If any collision occurs or a child has >=2 supported colors, it has >=2 colors, and the lack of a forced exclusion propagates upward. The distinguished leaf forbids one more root color. Thus B consists precisely of the boundary restrictions of proper 4-edge-colorings of the tree (all four incident edge colors distinct at each private vertex). For each bad boundary word the internal EDGE colors are uniquely reconstructed by XOR of the three descendant edge words. This is a linear map on the full boundary chart; root consistency and all-distinct at every private vertex characterize B. These edge colors are certificates of contradiction, not a legal vertex-color lift. Conversely every such proper edge-coloring gives a bad boundary word by the same induction.

At the distinguished incoming leaf choose any of four colors; at every private vertex choose an arbitrary permutation of the other three on its child edges, independently. Therefore |B|=4*6^t. For the free exterior chart B is not empty and |B|/2^d<1/2. The legal boundary relation has >half the words, hence full affine hull and no nontrivial unconditional affine identities. Its forced-affine closure cannot detect the nonlinear obstruction. This does not rule out richer certified inference.

## 3. Sharp minimum affine rejection cover (PROVED)
Theorem: for every such tree and every choice of signs, the largest affine subspace contained in B has dimension t+2. The minimum number of affine events on the original boundary chart whose UNION equals B is exactly 3^t, allowing arbitrary overlap and arbitrarily many affine equations per event.

Upper bound on affine dimension: lift any affine subset A of B by the linear edge reconstruction (injective since boundary coordinates retained). Reveal the distinguished incoming edge, then the three child edge colors at each private vertex in topological order. Each nonempty fiber of A under previously revealed coordinates is affine. The distinguished edge allows at most four values. Given a fixed incoming color, the allowed ordered child triples are precisely the six permutations of the other three words. Their bitwise XOR sum is zero (each color appears twice at each position); any four distinct triples have nonzero XOR, as their XOR equals that of the remaining two distinct triples. A binary affine plane has four distinct points with zero XOR. Therefore an affine subset of this six-element set has at most two elements. Projecting any conditional affine fiber to the next triple thus yields at most two possibilities. All edge colors eventually revealed, giving |A|<=4*2^t, dim A<=t+2.

Sharp construction: fix nonzero d0 in H and e0 outside span(d0). At every vertex choose which of its three child positions receives parent_color+d0. The two remaining child positions receive parent_color+e0 and parent_color+e0+d0 in either order. For each of the 3^t possible position-choice vectors, the incoming root word (two bits) and one swap bit per vertex parameterize the entire edge word affinely; recovery is injective via edge colors, then via boundary. These are disjoint affine subspaces of dimension t+2 whose union is B. The volume lower bound |B|/(4*2^t)=3^t matches. In particular the smallest codimension of a nonempty affine event contained in B is 3t+2, in boundary dimension 4t+4. A conjunction of rank-at-most-two punctured affine constraints on the boundary alone can never express the legal support: every forbidden event would have to lie in B but is too large. This holds already at t=1; adding more such boundary-only constraints does not help. A single forbidden observation may have rank zero or one, making its nonempty bad event still larger. No restriction on auxiliary existential variables, nonlinear gates, signed cancellation or unrestricted algorithms follows from this theorem.

## 4. Sharp exact linear observation width (PROVED)
For any Boolean function f with period subgroup P={h:f(z+h)=f(z) for all z}, a linear/affine observation quotient preserving all f values must have kernel contained in P. Conversely quotient by P is sufficient. This is BA07's characterization, here computed directly without spectral enumeration.

Every uniform shift of all boundary colors is a period of B. Conversely a period h lifts by the homogeneous XOR reconstruction to a shift on all edges, mapping the set of proper edge-colorings to itself. At any private vertex every permutation of H across its four incident edges extends to a proper edge-coloring of the full tree: extend away from that vertex by independently permuting the three remaining colors at each step. If two incident edges had unequal shifts h_i,h_j, choose their original distinct colors with XOR h_i+h_j, then extend the local permutation. The translated colors would coincide, contradiction. All incident shifts must equal each other. Connectivity forces one common shift globally. Thus P is exactly the two-dimensional diagonal color space. The minimum linear observation rank for the Boolean legal support (equivalently B indicator) is d-2=4t+2, even under arbitrary invertible affine relabeling. This is not the rank of an arbitrary nonlinear observation, nor a lower bound on circuit size. Signed switching leaves periods unchanged. Even a single private degree-four star requires six exact binary observations, not two.

## 5. Infinite opposite-label neighbors and constructive escape
Choose any proper edge-coloring by the top-down permutations, and pin its boundary. The resulting original vertex-coloring extension instance is UNSAT. Change any one exterior leaf color by any nonzero word. In the rooted tree, if the changed leaf is not the distinguished leaf, the affected vertex now has a collision among its three child edge words, or is the root with a repeated forbidden color; the collision propagates to permit a root color. If the distinguished leaf changes, the forced root color is no longer forbidden. Therefore each of the 3(2t+2) one-leaf changes yields SAT. More directly, the XOR parity of all boundary words of every proper edge-coloring is zero; a single nonzero change violates it, hence leaves B. This proves infinite pinned neighboring families for arbitrary tree size. They are easy, not diagonal-hard. Shared affine exterior charts can retain the same hard gluing issue as DA07/BW07/I13, but no universal portfolio stagnation is claimed.

The exact legal projection has a small alternative: use DA07 formal XOR descendant messages and OR of pair collisions among the three child words at each nonroot, and among the four root incoming words (including distinguished leaf). There are 3t+3 affine collision events, rank<=2 each, with linear-size factored XOR DAG on sparse boundary input. The earlier collision may invalidate a later formal forced message but already guarantees a lift; this is why the OR is exact. A fixed boundary can be decided and lifted by exact four-color tree support DP; a SINGLE tree with explicit affine exterior chart/guard can be solved by scanning 3t+3 Gaussian systems, choosing any consistent collision, and constructing actual private colors. Its complete external affine hull, if desired, is the span of the union of these consistent affine fibers; this follows from their particular solutions and nullspace bases, not enumeration. Multiple trees under a shared chart require actual simultaneous compatibility, not separate witness choices. Flat bad events blow up, but an OR of GOOD affine events stays small. Preserve polarity, guard and actual lift; do not force the bad set into a flat affine-event list for MS/BD/DS.
