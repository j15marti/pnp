## Sharp obstruction to cap

Stronger symbolic obstruction on a connected bounded-degree path graph: forbid both endpoints zero for each edge, i.e. monotone 2-CNF. Choose an induced matching of linear size (every third edge). For every subset of these matching edges, its intersection fixes exactly the selected endpoints to zero. No other graph edge has both endpoints in that endpoint set, and no smaller subset can cover it. Thus only that subset of edges yields this flat, so its final coefficient is nonzero.
