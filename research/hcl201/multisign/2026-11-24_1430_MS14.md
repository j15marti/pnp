## A. Signed affine trellis (PROVED)

Accept means BAD. Rejecting sink or complement of a total deterministic acceptor can be used without expanding a union of affine events. Transition tables or explicitly evaluated bounded block predicates must have paid size/time. Let a_i be maximum number of automaton states at a layer. No nondeterministic path counting is permitted unless determinized at charged cost. Other constraints must be among these predicates or factor through protected z.
For fixed offset o=B_i z+b_i, put y=A_i x, r=rank A_i, P rows a basis of ker A_i^T. Then y is realizable iff Py=0, and every realizable word has 2^(n-r) private lifts. At an observation cut j, attainable prefix syndromes that can be completed lie in im P_left intersect im P_right; its dimension equals w_ij=rank A_left+rank A_right-r by the kernel dimension identity in I14. Write w_i=max_j w_ij. Maintain automaton state and prefix syndrome; for each next block y_j (<=2^k choices), update syndrome, reject if outside suffix span, and update automaton with y_j XOR o_j. End at syndrome zero and bad-accepting state.
For private character chi_h(x)=(-1)^(h*x): if h outside row A_i, some k0 in ker A_i flips its sign; pairing x and x+k0 proves the conditional signed moment exactly zero, even with arbitrary offset. Otherwise Gaussian elimination finds lambda with A_i^T lambda=h. Weight each block transition by (-1)^(lambda_j*y_j), add signed integers at merged keys, multiply along transitions. The terminal integer Z equals sum_{y in im A_i} f_i(y+o)(-1)^(lambda*y). Therefore E_x[e_i chi_h]=Z/2^r. Different choices of lambda give same value because differences annihilate im A_i. Signed cancellation is used only for a moment, not as a SAT/UNSAT test except exact unsigned cover counts. Counts are bounded in bit length O(number of observation bits + n); no physical multiplicity assertion.
Cost per moment <=a_i 2^(w_i+k) poly(N), including construction of P, suffix bases, sign solution, failed transitions and exact integer arithmetic. ## D. Actual RT13 polarity adapter and scope
RT13 gives exact bad boundary of a private signed degree-four tree: after switching signs, the formal internal edge colors are XORs of descendant leaf colors, and at each vertex all four incident EDGE colors must be distinct for the boundary to be bad. Include the root XOR consistency (two equations on the full boundary chart); otherwise all-distinct tests on formal colors alone need not be exact. Encode each vertex's 8 bits as a local observation block, accept iff each is one of 24 permutations; deterministic monitor is one live state plus sink. Root consistency can be a separate block. On a path of t private vertices use parity-zero boundary chart with independent 2-bit colors s_0,...,s_t and a_1,...,a_t; four colors at vertex i are s_(i-1),s_i,a_i,s_(i-1)+s_i+a_i. This chart is a bijection to the boundary words of XOR zero: internal s_i recovered recursively from incoming leaf and preceding exterior leaf pairs, s_t is final leaf. The root parity restriction is explicit; on the full free chart bad probability is one quarter of the parity-zero chart probability. Path cut overlap is exactly two bits (shared s_i) at internal block boundaries. Thus signed bad-event moments cost polynomial even though flat cover is exactly 3^t by RT13 (also on parity-zero chart, since every bad word lies there).
