## Proved forest certificate
Let T_i=(a_i+U_i) minus at most one hole h_i. A is the sum map on direct-sum coefficients, K=ker A. For target t in affine hull, uniform affine fiber c+K, B_i is local coefficient block selecting its hole. For any forest F on punctured owners, Q=sum_i 1_{B_i}-sum_{ij in F}1_{B_i intersect B_j} is the component count of the induced forest on violated owners. Thus Q is nonnegative integer and zero iff no holes. Exact event probabilities by Gaussian consistency/rank: an affine condition on K of rank r has probability 2^{-r} if consistent, zero otherwise. E Q<1 proves a target witness. A maximum-weight forest on pair intersection probabilities is optimal for this bound (Hunter, 1976; not a novel probability inequality). ## Uniform target-independent fusion
Let r_i and r_ij be ranks of kernel projections onto one/two local blocks. Individual event probability <=2^{-r_i}. If deleting both blocks i,j leaves sum_{l notin {i,j}} U_l equal to sum_l U_l, the pair-hole event is feasible for every t in the affine hull (holes in the local affine hulls); its probability is exactly 2^{-r_ij}. Otherwise use lower bound zero. Choose maximum-weight forest on these certified lower bounds, define beta=sum_i 2^{-r_i}-sum_edges lower_ij. If beta<1, full exact affine sum and paid reverse lift follow for every target. This certificate is sufficient not necessary.
