For a non-tautological resolvent R=A union B from (A or p),(B or not p), every falsification of R falsifies exactly one parent, regardless of pivot bit. Two parents of a genuine resolution step cannot both be violated under the same assignment; useful selection should compare multiple starts or neighboring assignments, not require same-assignment co-violation. This is a sound local guide, not a universal progress guarantee. Each strict improvement reduces integer violated count by >=1 within a fixed clause table, hence at most m flips even without free-carrier cap. Number of distinct clauses of width <=w on r carriers is at most sum_{j=0}^w 2^j binom(r,j), polynomial for fixed w; exact parent replay and re-scoring polynomial under fixed chart/caps. Neither guarantees a productive deduction or polynomial-size full refutation. Mod3 signed zero for a width-three row may occur at k=0, k=2 with opposite satisfied signs, or k=3 with all satisfied signs equal. k mod3 zero also collides at k=0,3.
