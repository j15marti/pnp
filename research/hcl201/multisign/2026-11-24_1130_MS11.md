## 1. Theorem: correlated sparse Walsh certificate
Let A be a nonempty explicitly given affine space over F2, and P protect external output and all retained event forms. Gaussian elimination gives affine coordinates (x,z), x in F2^k, z in F2^q, with each z uniquely indexing a fiber of P(A). Each event E_i is a conjunction of affine equations in x,z. A point is legal iff no E_i is true. For any supplied tree T on these events, define
  B(z)=sum_i Pr_x(E_i|z)-sum_ij in T Pr_x(E_i and E_j|z).
Pointwise the corresponding indicator sum minus edge intersections counts connected components of the active induced forest. Hence B(z) >= Pr_x(any E_i|z). No independence assumed.

For each signed term I (single or tree pair), eliminate x first in its affine equations. Inconsistency gives zero. Otherwise r_I is the private rank, and C_I z=d_I is a consistent independent system of c_I external equations. Exactly
  Pr_x(E_I|z)=2^(-r_I) 1[C_I z=d_I]
               =2^(-r_I-c_I) sum_{a in F2^c_I} (-1)^(a dot d_I) chi_{C_I^T a}(z),
where chi_w(z)=(-1)^(w dot z). Proof: character orthogonality and uniform size of consistent private fibers. Add every signed coefficient at the IDENTICAL external frequency before absolute values. Let F(w) be the combined coefficients, and
  U = F(0)+sum_{w != 0}|F(w)|.
Then max_z B(z)<=U. In particular U<1 guarantees every z has a legal lift; the exact projected legal relation is all P(A). No semantic claim from a single zero term. Conditional guards stay attached.

Compute the sparse polynomial in  sum_I 2^c_I poly(N) bit work and space; coefficients may be scaled by 2^(k+q), all magnitudes have O(k+q+log m) bits. Even q linear in N is harmless if c_I bounded (rank-two events imply pair c_I<=4). For an alternative exact maximum, take a basis of the span of surviving nonzero frequencies (rank d); evaluate over 2^d character assignments, all realized because basis independent. The exact max of B may be lower than U. Optional refinement: partition frequencies into direct-sum components by Gaussian basis provenance (BS04); each component can maximize independently with sum 2^d_j evaluations, not product. These optional maxima are proved symbolically, not implemented. If expansion exceeds a paid cap, retain MS10 or UNKNOWN; no universal logarithmic codimension.
