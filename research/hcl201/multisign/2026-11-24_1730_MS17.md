## A. Pointwise fractional certificate (PROVED)
On a common binary affine chart G, owner i accepts iff at least one of its explicitly listed affine packets A_ij holds. All exterior observations and any retained factor consumers are protected by a linear map P. No hidden nonlinear consumers are dropped. Integer tags/quotas must be kept separately or authenticated by the lift. Define s_i(x)=sum_j 1_Aij(x), M_i=number of packets (or a certified positive upper bound on multiplicity), fixed integer d>=1, b_i(x)=(1-s_i/M_i)^d. Empty owner rejects directly. Then 0<=b_i<=1 and b_i=1 iff owner rejects. Neither b_i nor s_i is asserted Boolean. For a fixed forest T on the owners,
    W(x)=1-sum_i b_i(x)+sum_{ij in T} b_i(x)b_j(x).
For every x, W(x)<=1_legal(x). Proof: root each tree at any owner. Sum b_i minus sum edge products equals sum of root b values plus sum over nonroots b_child(1-b_parent), all nonnegative. If any owner rejects, root its tree there, so the sum is >=1. This proves pointwise domination without exact union counts or exact Boolean pair intersections. Equivalent to applying Hunter's forest bound to auxiliary independent Bernoulli events with probabilities b_i at fixed x; no independence assumption on source events. Negative credit is allowed.
Optional marginal-only PSD: b_i b_j >= h(b_i+b_j)-h^2/2-(b_i^2+b_j^2)/2, by half the square (b_i+b_j-h)^2. Any fixed h, including a conditional Fourier projection, yields another sound pointwise credit. Exact fractional pair products are already polynomial here, so no PSD optimization required; this optional variant not tested.

## B. Sparse Fourier certificate, uniform across protected fibers (PROVED)
Each affine packet of rank <=k has <=2^k Walsh terms by orthogonality. Expand only b_i (degree d in s_i) and pair products along T (degree 2d); never expand the true Boolean OR. If M=sum_i M_i, a crude bound on arithmetic operations and stored terms is O((1+M 2^k)^(2d) poly(N)), including convolution, signs, forest choice and Gaussian operations. One can instead evaluate signed intersections of <=2d affine packets with Gaussian elimination directly; for a single fixed guard mean this yields M^O(d) poly(N) even when packet ranks are large. For uniform external Fourier, the exponential k factor is charged. All dyadic/rational denominators have polynomial bit length for fixed d (product of per-owner denominators, O(d sum_i(k+log M_i)) bits), numerator growth polynomial bits. Fourier convolution keys are actual row vectors and equal keys combine before absolute values.
Parametrize the current nonempty chart G as a+Kx+Jz, where columns of K span ker(P restricted to the chart direction space), and J spans a section of the image. In each Fourier character, substitute a+Kx+Jz. Average over x: terms with nonzero private frequency vanish; others become signed exterior frequencies. Combine equal frequencies into C(z)=E[W|z]=sum_v c_v chi_v(z). If c_0-sum_{v!=0}|c_v|>0, every admissible exterior fiber contains a legal original word. No enumeration of exterior assignments. This condition is sufficient, not necessary, and certifies Boolean support not exact counts. Forest can be supplied or choose a maximum spanning forest for a fixed-chart mean; no claim of optimizing worst-fiber credit. Code uses a path forest and d=2.
If P includes all retained consumers, eliminate this owner group existentially while preserving exact compatibility on the remaining chart. Physical projection P authenticated on L and L2 gives delta mu=dim(ker P intersect L)-dim(ker P intersect L2). This is a conditional rank test, not a measured physical reduction.

## C. Closure and lift (PROVED, executed on small chart cases)
For each owner intersect each actual packet with current guard, remove impossible packets conceptually; empty union rejects. Affine hull of union is a representative plus span of each surviving kernel and differences of representatives; impose its annihilator equations on the current chart. These are necessary deductions (not arbitrary choices). Restart after each rank increase and at stagnation before branching. At most n rank additions per consistent guard; complete local closure for this repertoire, not global semantic closure oracle. Conditional equations never leave their chart. Keep the original certificate fixed; restricting to a consistent subguard does not invalidate pointwise domination. Positive total credit cannot decrease when deleting only illegal words (each has W<=0). For any independent Boolean split, unnormalized child credits sum to parent credit; positive parent implies a positive child. Exact conditional mean on a guard is obtained by Gaussian testing whether each character annihilates the guard kernel and applying its anchor sign. Child positive mean equivalent to positive total credit on nonempty child. ## D. Strict example and barrier (PROVED)
Four owners, each accepts OR of h independent private bits XOR their distinct protected bits. Different owners have disjoint private blocks. h even >=4. For d=2, mean b=(h+1)/(4h)=u in every fiber, pair mean u^2. Union credit=1-4u=-1/h. Three-edge path credit=1-4u+3u^2=(1-u)(1-3u)>0. External rank 4h; conditional credit constant (one coefficient), though full exact Boolean Fourier expansion of one OR has 2^h terms. A high-rank single affine rejection packet is another cheap representation; independent pivots also solve this family, so improvement is specifically over the fractional union bound, not all peer methods. Authenticated signed rim specialization: BW11 section C generalizes directly to doubled odd cycle of h+1 vertices with one fixed direction 1, h directions 1(false)/2(true), first signs zero. All false: odd XOR of J(1) outside span(1); any true: full span. Thus exact OR and local four-state DP lift, within BW11's supplied affine-sign model; full strict physical E4/B4 source not compiled. Scaling tests exercised the chart, not the rim DP. The h private coordinates per owner can be eliminated on every protected fiber; physical quotient transfer remains conditional.
For fixed d and h, disjoint identical OR owners have mean b=u>0 (also u<1). Every forest has at most m-1 edges; best tree credit 1-mu+(m-1)u^2=(1-u)(1-(m-1)u), <=0 for sufficiently many owners even though every fiber is SAT. Thus fixed-degree forest success is not universal. Component factorization/direct pivots bypass this easy barrier. Exact count of arbitrary rank-two escape OR already encodes complement of independent-set count (one packet x_u=x_v=1 per graph edge); this is not an exact count algorithm. No need to Booleanize OR lists for this certificate, but no assertion that it succeeds on all such lists. BW11's NP-complete synchronized pentagon chart remains a direct firewall.
