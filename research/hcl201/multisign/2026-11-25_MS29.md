## Theorem and proof
For any explicit family of binary affine tables T_i = a_i+U_i with optional single hole h_i in that affine space, normalize empty and rank-one punctured tables. Let A: direct sum U_i -> sum U_i be summation, K=ker A, r_i=dim projection_i K. If sum over punctures 2^(-r_i)<1, the exact XOR sum is the whole affine sum. For each target t in the affine sum, Gaussian elimination gives one decomposition x and basis for K. On the uniform fiber x+K, each hole event x_i=h_i is empty or has probability 2^(-r_i). Expected number of violations <1; hence a legal decomposition exists. Fix coefficients of a kernel basis in sequence, choosing the bit minimizing the conditional expected number of violations. A remaining event is empty or has exactly 2^(d-r) realizations where d is the number of unassigned coefficients and r is the rank of the projected remaining kernel columns. Gaussian membership and rank tests and exact integer additions compute both choices, whose mean is the prior expectation. At the end the number of violations is a nonnegative integer <1, hence zero. This is deterministic and polynomial in explicit total basis size, including target bit width, reconstruction and checks. No enumeration of a full affine support or a high-rank hole fiber.

Special case: pairwise disjoint rank-two spaces U,V,W with W the graph of an isomorphism U->V, so K dimension 2 and all projections rank 2. Three punctures give 3/4<1. Pair fusion declines; no common adapted basis (three rank-two subspaces with pairwise zero intersections span rank four), yet multiway fusion gives a full rank-four affine table. Disjoint copies give unbounded families. Test targets inside/outside affine sum, not arbitrary UNSAT NAE hardness.
