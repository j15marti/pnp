## Theorem A: odd support obstructs linear quotient of a local relation
For a nonempty odd-cardinality subset S of F2^r, every unnormalized Walsh coefficient of its indicator is an odd integer, hence nonzero. Therefore the Fourier span has rank r; no nonzero translation fixes S (translation by nonzero d partitions invariant S into two-element orbits). More generally, S is invariant under H iff every nonzero Walsh frequency lies in H^perp, by translation of characters and uniqueness of Fourier inversion. A basis of the frequency span gives a minimal exact *linear* response, and each fiber is constant. This is not a barrier to sparse support lists, nonlinear functions, affine hull with exact exclusions or compact clauses. In particular a table with three points can have maximal response rank. No claim about minimum global energy spectrum or all algorithms.

## Theorem B: exact factor-tree composition by linear-form interfaces
Let finite factors f_i be arbitrary explicitly given allowed tables on evaluations of polynomially many binary linear forms on x in F2^r (affine offsets absorbed into tables after a checked guard parameterization). For a binary tree of factor leaves, W_U is the span of forms in subtree U; I_U=W_U intersect W_outside. Store precisely the restrictions to I_U of linear functionals on W_U satisfying all factors of U. At a leaf enumerate allowed response words, check linear consistency by GE and restrict to I_U. For children L,R, two stored labels glue iff their evaluations agree on W_L intersect W_R, a subspace of both interfaces. Chosen extensions to W_L and W_R then glue to a unique functional on their sum. The parent interface is contained in I_L+I_R: if u=l+r is in W_outside, l=u+r lies in W_L intersect (W_R+W_outside), and similarly for r. Hence the parent label is uniquely determined by the child labels, independent of extensions; agreement makes all decompositions consistent. Conversely every satisfying functional restricts to a compatible pair. Inductively root support is nonempty iff feasible. Save a pair of child labels for each retained label, then recover leaf responses and solve the original linear equations by GE; this lifts exactly. Empty root label set proves UNSAT. With m leaves, cut intersection rank <=w, explicit local support tables and polynomial-size supplied decomposition, at most 2^w labels per node and 2^(2w) pairs per merge; each label and pair check uses polynomial bit work via GE in r and total explicit input size. Backpointers and verifier use the same bound. For w<=c log2 N and fixed c, this is a polynomial terminal conditional on paid acquisition of tables, decomposition and guard adapter. No free discovery of logarithmic width. Jeong/Kim/Oum arXiv:1711.01381 (SIAM JDM 2021 DOI 10.1137/19M1285895) defines this exact subspace branchwidth and gives FPT acquisition for fixed k; its fixed-k statement alone does not yield polynomial acquisition at k=O(log N). Kashyap arXiv:0711.1383 covers related linear-code tree realizations. Our nonlinear table DP proof does not require the factors themselves to be linear. General tree algorithm proved here, not implemented this run.
