## 1. Gauge quotient lemma (PROVED)
For each event e_i on x in F2^n and z in F2^q, assume its exact class parity vector p_i=R_i x+S_i z+b_i, and e_i is the fixed-net bundle bad indicator 1[wt(p_i)>H_i OR wt(p_i XOR omega_i)>W_i-H_i]. An original affine guard is first solved into a product chart respecting the protected projection (a particular linear section plus basis of its kernel); costs and resulting ranks/components charged. Arbitrary integer costs, nonlinear guards and additional observations cannot silently be dropped.

Let t_i=rank([R_i S_i])-rank(R_i). Gaussian elimination constructs
  S_i=R_i T_i+V_i Q_i,
where columns of V_i are independent modulo im R_i, Q_i has t_i independent rows, and T_i is a private translation matrix. Verify the equality and ranks directly. For fixed z, substitute y=x+T_i z, u=Q_i z. Then e_i(x,z)=f_i(y,u) where f_i uses R_i y+V_i u+b_i. For a private frequency w, conditional moment
  m_i(w,z)=E_x[e_i(x,z)(-1)^(w*x)]
           =(-1)^((T_i^T w)*z) a_i(w,Q_i z),
  a_i(w,u)=E_y[f_i(y,u)(-1)^(w*y)].
Expand the t_i-bit function a_i in Walsh characters. Exact m_i(w,.) has at most 2^t_i terms at external masks T_i^T w+Q_i^T alpha. All coefficients are dyadic rationals with denominators dividing 2^(n+t_i). In particular t_i=0 gives a SINGLE character per moment, even when rank S_i and rank of the collective external phases grow linearly. Different events use different translations; they are NOT assumed to have a common private repair, and they are not independently sampled.

For each u, M11/MS12 signed local histograms acquire a_i(w,u): connect private variables cooccurring in a row of R_i; enumerate each component of size <=c and convolve integer bivariate weight totals with signs. Changing u changes only row offsets; no union of event components needed. Thus per feature 2^(c+t_i) poly(N) arithmetic/bit work, using a fast Walsh transform in t_i (the executed naive transform costs 4^t_i). No exact pair count.
