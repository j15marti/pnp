## 1. Source-SCC generator theorem (constructive)
First close all available certified unconditional constraints and reject contradictions. Freeze F,H,c for the following exact module; if F alone is UNSAT reject by SCC. Obtain one ordinary model a. Rename y=x+a, so y=0 satisfies F'. Every clause in F' is a negative unit, an implication (not y_i OR y_j), a negative pair (not y_i OR not y_j), or tautological. Let D be the digraph of positive-variable implications, and contract SCCs. An assignment to y is feasible iff its selected SCC set is forward closed in the resulting DAG, avoids all negative units, and avoids every negative-pair conflict. For an SCC C let q_C=H*1_C (column XOR). A feasible selected set U has target XOR sum q_C=t=c+Ha.

Let L_F be the span of all differences x+a for ordinary models of F. Put k=dim H(L_F), at most rank H. If t is outside H(L_F), reject immediately. Otherwise, if a target exists, select an inclusion-minimal feasible U attaining t. Let R be the source SCCs of the induced DAG on U (no incoming arc from another selected SCC). Any subset of R may be removed while preserving forward closure and all negative restrictions: no remaining node has an arc into a removed source, and negative constraints are hereditary. For each source C, both U and U minus C are ordinary feasible, so 1_C belongs to L_F and q_C belongs to H(L_F). If |R|>k, their labels are linearly dependent. A nonempty subset of these sources has XOR zero; removing it preserves target, contradicting minimality. Thus |R|<=k, and their labels are independent. Every node of U is reachable from a source of U; by forward closure, U is exactly the union of the full descendant closures of these at most k SCCs.

Consequently enumerate unions of at most k feasible single-SCC closures, test all negative constraints and compute the actual XOR of the UNION. Return the first matching target witness or reject after exhausting these candidates. Do NOT XOR closure labels blindly; intersecting closures would be counted twice. Each union is forward closed by construction, and original F and H are rechecked. No recursion through full hub assignments required. A single generator can force arbitrarily many variable changes. This is a compact generator characterization, not a claim that n^k candidates are necessary.

## 2. Exact ordinary affine closure without enumerating models
Each SCC whose descendant closure is feasible has one witness difference d_C (its closure indicator in original variables). Every feasible model difference is a union of feasible SCC closures. To see that the latter indicators linearly span all feasible differences, order the feasible SCCs reverse topologically. For each C, d_C=1_C+sum of indicators of its strict descendants (distinct SCCs); descendants of a feasible C are feasible by heredity and closure. Induction expresses every feasible 1_C in the span of the d_C. Conversely d_C is an actual witness difference, so the span is exactly L_F. This yields full affine equations of the ordinary 2CNF, not only pair SCC equalities, and an exact effective rank k via Gaussian elimination. A single closure is computed by reachability and validated, not guessed. This is polynomial preprocessing, and all exported equations are unconditional for F. Exact initial rank and target exclusion precede enumeration. The code uses literal-graph reachability to obtain feasible flips and deduplicates identical closure masks; equivalent to the SCC construction.

## 3. Mixed affine feedback and defect reduction
The exact solver above permits complete affine extraction from the mixed fiber F and Hx=c via GD-01's witness-growth method. Find one witness b. Maintain W=span of witnessed differences x+b. For a basis of W^perp, test the opposite bit u*x=1+u*b by appending only that row to the frozen H. A successful query adds a witness and increases dim W; if all fail, b+W is the exact affine hull. At most n(n+1) queries, each increases effective rank by at most one; no accumulated query rank. Conditional branches retain their guards; only after both alternatives are accounted for or opposite query fails unconditionally is a relation exported. This batch is the complete affine closure of the exact mixed subsystem; repeating same queries cannot give further affine information unless another certified channel changes the base. On a larger source, insert only verified new equations and renew available matching/integer/implication closure before branching or further queries. Full arbitrary cooperative closure not implemented in this run.

Transport equations through source observation maps to physical port direction space L. From HCL-196, for added matrix E the number of independent quotient-preserving combinations is rank(E|L)-rank(E|L2). Compute by Gaussian elimination before applying the batch; those equations reduce mu without splitting parity blocks. Other equations are retained and defect recomputed. A strict mu decrease is not automatic. Example: two groups of four equal physical ports controlled by x and y, with x+y=0; L has a single weight-eight direction, L2=0, mu=1. Add the source-certified binary exclusion not x OR not y (e.g. M01 rank-two hole). Ordinary affine closure of the exclusion is full; parity alone leaves x=y. Mixed solver forces both zero, giving an independent quotient equation and mu=0. This is an abstract interface example, not a certified strict wheel embedding or globally hard residual.

A rank-one mixed condition is deterministic polynomial even if closure size is linear. A local exact 2CNF factor with a fixed number of protected affine observations can therefore be tested and its witnesses reconstructed without Pfaffian randomization. ## 4. Parameter barrier
This explicit mixed class is W[1]-hard parameterized by the number k of independent parity rows. From multicolored clique on k nonempty color classes, use one variable per vertex. Include negative pair clauses for pairs in the same color and cross-color nonedges. Add one XOR row per color requiring parity one. The clauses enforce at most one per color, the XOR forces exactly one, and pairwise compatibility is precisely a multicolored clique. Conversely a clique gives a solution. Rows are disjoint nonempty supports, rank exactly k. F alone has all-zero and all singletons feasible, so L_F is full and effective rank k too. Reduction is polynomial in graph encoding and preserves parameter; already applies to negative 2CNF with no implication arcs. Therefore a uniform f(k)poly(N) algorithm for the entire class implies FPT=W[1]. This is conditional parameterized evidence, not an unrestricted P vs NP lower bound, nor an obstruction to source families with a narrower certified compiler promise. Cooperative closure may shrink individual instances. At k=O(log N), n^k is quasipolynomial rather than polynomial; no stronger bound follows.
