## Theorem: exact translation-response basis
For any explicit Boolean relation R on k bits, H={d:R xor d=R} is a vector subspace, since translations compose and invert. Compute basis rows of H-perp, L, and T=L(R). For every x, x in R iff Lx in T: equality of images implies x differs from a permitted row by ker L=H. This is the maximal linear translation quotient: any exact representation depending only on Ax has ker A contained in H. For a family of local relations embed all L rows in common coordinates; joint linear map Q has rank r. A tuple of local response codes is realizable iff it belongs to image Q AND every local code belongs to its exact T. Necessity immediate; sufficiency solve Qx=y by Gaussian elimination, then each local relation accepts all preimages. Keep all cross-owner linear equations defining image Q; never choose local codes independently. Exact lifting polynomial once compatible codes acquired. For fixed k, computing all periods, allowed codes and verifying both directions is polynomial in explicit input. For unbounded k the simple exhaustive period search is exponential in k, and no acquisition bound for succinct arbitrary R is claimed.
A distinct-carrier signed B4 has exactly periods 0000 and 1111. Odd-weight periods change parity; a weight-two translation sends the matching weight-two legal word to 0000; complement preserves weight two. Signs translate the support and do not alter periods.
