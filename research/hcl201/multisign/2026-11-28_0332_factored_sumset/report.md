## Theorems
1. For Boolean f,g, the translations d with f(x+d)=g(x) for all x are empty or a coset of the translation stabilizer of f. Split at the next ordered BDD coordinate: d_i=0 intersects the two like-cofactor transporters; d_i=1 intersects the two crossed-cofactor transporters. Their union, after prefixing the chosen bit, is exactly the transporter and therefore is affine if nonempty. Represent intersections by binary affine linear systems and union by the affine span of anchors and bases. Memoizing (level,f-node,g-node) gives O(r M^2) states when input diagrams have <=M nodes; each affine operation is polynomial in r. This includes fixed-zero translations on a prescribed coordinate suffix. For a prefix interface in a reduced ordered BDD, the distinct reachable suffix functions give the exact nonlinear response-class count; its prefix translation kernel is computable by the same recursion. Acquisition of small BDDs from arbitrary inputs is not proved.
2. For independent support sets S,T, compute the exact XOR sumset characteristic function recursively. On target bit 0 the suffix is OR(sum(S0,T0),sum(S1,T1)); bit 1 uses crossed cofactors. This follows by existentially choosing the two operand bits. Memoization and ordinary Boolean diagram APPLY produce an exact result subject to charged intermediate size. To reconstruct a decomposition, choose a pair of operand bits whose suffix sumset contains the required remaining target, and recur. If the fused support is invariant under H, the global sum constraint can be quotiented by H, keeping all observations in its codomain; lift by correcting the fused response by the resulting discrepancy in H and reverse the sumset decomposition. Arbitrary non-coordinate quotient compilation must be charged. This is not a claim that symbolic fusion always stays small.
3. If the direction spaces U=span(S+a), V=span(T+b) have trivial intersection, the sumset is a Cartesian product under a linear isomorphism. Its translation group is exactly H(S)+H(T); a translation preserving the product preserves each projection and conversely. Thus when individual periods vanish only positive-overlap pairs can yield a new period. Find collective dependencies using Gaussian elimination with provenance across the concatenated local direction bases; each dependent column gives a fundamental circuit and a candidate group. This is not enumeration of all circuits or all groups.
4. Exact ambient rebase: choose a_i in each nonempty dictionary S_i and U=span(union_i(S_i+a_i)). If b+sum_i a_i is outside U, reject. Otherwise replace every x in S_i by the exact basis coordinates of x+a_i and replace b by coordinates of b+sum_i a_i. Injectivity of the basis map proves exact preservation of every compatible tuple and a direct inverse for each option. Under T3's existing fixed credit Phi=(B+d)(B0*K+1)+sum_i|S_i|, if all dictionary rows and blocks are retained and d drops to q=dim U, the decrease is (d-q)(B0*K+1), with original B0,K unchanged. This is a representation-rank contraction only: intrinsic cut intersections are isomorphic and their ranks do not decrease just from rebasing. No reset or duplicate credit after the same rebase.
