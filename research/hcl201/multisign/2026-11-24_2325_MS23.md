## Target and theorem (PROVED, restricted)
BD17 (commit 4a6f11453e361d311410571da87c18db9f50acea, thm:bd17-mask) proves NP-completeness for a designated-side common mask. Test whether allowing either side, or any number of common masks followed by a final unilateral defect flip, escapes the hardness. It does not. The reduction below has known ordinary SAT witnesses on BOTH target labels, even with d fixed to either orientation. Therefore no decision-only solver may be required to execute this particular repair. This is a genuine four-perfect-partition result, not a P != NP result.

Given an arbitrary seeded R-instance from BD17/BH16, with R(a;b,c) iff a=b or a=c and demand s != t, make two disjoint copies. Each admits all-zero and all-one without the demand. Make positive occurrence triples for NAE(not a,b,c) in color 0. Add a demand owner (d,u0,v0) in color 0 with positive terminals for seeds of copy 0, and (d,u1,v1) in color 2 with positive terminals of copy 1. d is fresh. In each signed variable occurrence path put the demand terminal first when present. As in BD17, consecutive same-sign terminals are synchronized by two I23 opposition wires of colors 1,2, opposite signs by three of colors 1,3,2. In particular the color-2 demand terminals have only an outgoing color-1 wire; there is no repeated color slot. All intermediate vertices and affine-plane wires are private. All ordinary occurrence terminals use color 0 and at most one incoming color-2 and outgoing color-1 wire. d has two distinct slots; every pair is fresh except pairs of a removed plane line used once by its attachment. Pad to a multiple of three and apply DS16 transparent completion, with fixed opposite filler bits. This is a uniform linear-size construction in explicit occurrence count (constant gadget factors). All wire/completion maps are explicit and lift every old word satisfying the old owners. Use pairing A={0,1}, B={2,3}. Start from old copy 0 all-zero, copy 1 all-one, extend with common wire/filler bits and let x_d=1, y_d=0. The only d owner on each side with a monochromatic other pair is its own demand owner; its d bit is minority on its side. Other owners at d are transparent fillers. Thus x satisfies A, y satisfies B, exactly one blocker each, exactly one disagreement, root guard empty.

A common mask h with h_d=0 followed by flipping d on A gives common w with d=0. Both before and after A must satisfy its demand owner; hence w_u0 != w_v0, and the signed copy map gives a seeded R model. Similarly flipping B requires copy 1 seeds opposite. Conversely lift any seeded model in copy 0 and all-one in copy 1 with d=0, or all-zero in copy 0 and seeded model in copy 1 with d=1. Both orientations on the designated side are legal, so the common mask from the actual start followed by one defect flip works, with fixed filler mask bits. Therefore either-side feasibility (indeed both individual labels agree) iff the original seeded R problem is SAT. Membership in NP is direct checking. A sequence of legal common masks at fixed d before the last flip is equivalent to its XOR, because the endpoint just before the final flip is itself a feasible pair, so multiple such stages do not change the result. This does not cover moving d, arbitrary unilateral plateau paths, or simultaneous disagreement-eliminating batches.

Ordinary SAT is trivial on the compiled family: both copies all-one with d=0, or both all-zero with d=1, each lifts. Consequently ordinary SAT and the existence of an either-side repair are not interchangeable; requiring the repair can give false negatives even on known SAT instances. Full semantic closure is not assumed free. At root, ordinary local coordinate closure of positive NAE triples has no unary forces; a known witness and its complement certify no globally forced absolute bit. Guarded target constraints may force bits, but must never be exported as unconditional source facts. A decision algorithm may simply accept a verified common word without any particular path from x,y. If a common word is acquired by some other method, S21 applies to its actual flow lifts, not to these restricted masks.
