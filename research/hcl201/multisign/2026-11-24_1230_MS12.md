## 1. Pointwise PSD identity (PROVED)
Let e_i,e_j be 0/1 bad-event indicators, s=e_i+e_j. For ANY real-valued function h on source words define
    ell_h = h*s - h^2/2 - s/2.
Since s^2=s+2e_i e_j,
    e_i e_j - ell_h = (s-h)^2/2 >=0.
This is pointwise, with no independence, monotonicity, or joint-count assumption. It is a rank-one square/PSD certificate, not a general SDP. Alternatives ell=0 and ell=s-1 are pointwise valid too. Fix one minorant per selected edge.

On the uniform original Boolean chart z in F2^n, take an explicit set H of distinct frequency masks (e.g. all weight <=2, size O(n^2)). Let chi_w(z)=(-1)^(w dot z). Choose h=sum_(w in H) a_w chi_w, a_w=E[s chi_w]. Orthogonality maximizes the EXPECTED minorant among this linear span, by completing squares:
    E[ell_h]= (sum_(w in H) a_w^2 - E[s])/2.
No exact pair count is needed. The gap is half the omitted Fourier energy of s, and equality holds iff s lies in the feature span. Constant-only h does not necessarily beat Frechet; retain the best of the three choices. Coefficients are exact dyadic rational numbers. This discovery rule is deterministic. Arbitrary selected masks also valid, and no optimal feature discovery promised. M12's Harris certificate may be better; it remains a separate terminal for its valid prefix chart.

## 2. Pointwise forest transport (PROVED)
Fix a forest T and a chosen minorant ell_ij on each edge. Define
    w(z)=1-sum_i e_i(z)+sum_(ij in T) ell_ij(z).
Since the exact edge product correction yields 1 minus number of components of the active induced forest, w(z)<=1[legal(z)], and w(z)<=0 on illegal words. Thus for ANY guard B, C(B)=sum_(z in B)w(z)<=#legal(B). Under ANY disjoint exhaustive guard partition C is exactly additive. If certified closure discards only illegal words, C cannot decrease. Positive credit implies a legal word; nonpositive credit means UNKNOWN absent another certified contradiction. Pick a positive child, retaining guards, repeat closure, and verify the final word. No coordinate independence required for this transport identity. Unlike M12's lower bounds that may change under affine chart reset, the pointwise certificate stays fixed, even on a dense parity guard. Negative child credit is allowed; a positive parent guarantees at least one positive child, not both. Never discard an unchosen child as globally impossible.

For discovery evaluate the three alternatives for each pair on the starting product chart and use maximum-weight spanning forest (Kruskal); root edge weights nonnegative after comparison with zero. No optimum over all certificates or feature sets claimed. Keep the chosen edge functions and original chart for reconstruction; optional reoptimization must not silently replace a positive fixed certificate with a weaker one.

## 3. Paid single-event moments (PROVED)

Build its LOCAL support components by unioning variables appearing in the same row. Let c be maximum local component size. To obtain sum e_j chi_w, enumerate each component's 2^|C| local assignments, accumulating SIGNED counts indexed by the two weight totals (a,b). Constant rows and fixed pins contribute offsets and sign. Convolve tables across disjoint independent components, and sum precisely the union of bad thresholds once. Character signs multiply; this computes the exact moment by distributivity. Isolated free bits included. No graph union across pairs. Counts and intermediate coefficients have O(n+log N) bits. For the bare character sum use the same construction with event identically one or Gaussian elimination.

On a guard of rank q affine equations Pz=d, multiply by its exact indicator 2^-q sum_lambda (-1)^(lambda dot d) chi_(P^T lambda). Thus each guarded signed moment takes <=2^q unguarded moment calls (after checking guard consistency and reducing independent rows). Prefix pins can be substituted first without increasing c.
