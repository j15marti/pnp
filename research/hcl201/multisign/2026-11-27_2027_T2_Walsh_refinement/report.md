## Theorem (proved)
For a real/integer-valued violation polynomial E(x)=sum_a c_a (-1)^(a.x) on F2^r, group equal frequencies before truncation. Let H be a subspace of frequencies, P_H the terms in H, T=sum_{a outside H}|c_a|. Define L_H=min_x P_H(x)-T. E>=L_H uniformly. If dim H<=k, compute the minimum by enumerating 2^k possible character response words; a basis is independent, hence every word occurs. All arithmetic exact. For nested H subset K, L_K>=L_H by min(P_H+added)>=min P_H-||added||_1 and the corresponding removal of mass from T. Thus a persistent nested refinement cannot reduce its bound. If an affine chart restriction x=b+B y is certified, send frequency a to B^T a with sign (-1)^(a.b), group coefficients, and retain H'=B^T H. The original retained polynomial pulls back with minimum at least its old minimum; some formerly discarded terms enter H' and have absolute grouped mass U, while the new discarded mass is at most T-U (triangle inequality, including cancellations). Consequently the transported L_H' is >= L_H and the discarded mass cannot increase. This holds for any affine map, including a rank-decreasing restriction; applying it to the feasibility question requires a certificate that every solution survives the map and a checked lift. The proof does NOT establish a corresponding invariant for nonlinear chart changes or for adding arbitrary implied-clause energies. For those changes retain old certified bounds in their scope and use separate paid credit; do not reset the ledger. For nonnegative integer energy a strictly positive lower bound proves NO; a nonpositive bound is UNKNOWN.
For fixed local width w, M source rows produce <=M 2^w raw character terms; rank k<=c log N yields polynomial enumeration. Use r-bit masks, signed integer coefficients after common dyadic scaling 2^w, coefficients O(w+log M) bits. Sorting/grouping and basis elimination are polynomial. Explicit cap on r, M, width and rank is necessary. Pay every rank refinement and failed candidate, all maps and reconstruction; under a fixed spectrum at most r independent directions may be added, but only logarithmically many may be explicitly enumerated under a fixed polynomial cap. Affine transport charges O(poly(r,M,2^w,2^k)) bit operations, not free. No bound on number of nonaffine chart changes or acquisition of useful frequencies follows.

## Hostile family and first missing implication
On an n-cycle of equality/opposition binary relations, take one inconsistent sign (NO) versus all equality (YES). Clause-pair energy has one nonconstant frequency per edge, equal absolute coefficient, and unique dependence involving all n edge frequencies. Any frequency span of rank k<n-1 contains at most k of these n frequencies; those retained are independent, so can attain all preferred signs, yielding L=0 for either label. No rank-k tail certificate can separate them. Tests n=5,9,13,17, cap8, both labels: NO separated at 5,9, not 13,17. Affine hull GE rejects all NO and not YES, so restricted spectral obstruction has a cheap bypass; no unrestricted hardness. This family is chart-level, not newly authenticated native instances. First OPEN arrow: from an arbitrary post-paid-closure NO state to a polynomially discoverable productive affine restriction or a sparse signed packet with positive bound or some other charged terminal. Monotonicity alone admits plateaus. Need a common non-resetting aggregate budget for branches, chart changes, all lanes and precision, plus all-input coverage. Neither P=NP nor P!=NP established.
