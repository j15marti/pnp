## New theorem: factored nonaffine chart can defeat every polynomial-size explicit global Walsh tail
For k>=1 let f=AND_i(u_i OR v_i), h,t free bits, g=f XOR h. Hard gate semantics, three unit-cost requirements t=f, t=g, h=b. Let E_b=[t!=f]+[t!=g]+[h!=b]. Both f values realizable. XOR first two violation bits equals h; XOR all three equals b. Thus min E_b >= b; all inputs zero give E_b=b. Eight exact response words (f,t,h) suffice, with forward gate evaluation and explicit reverse representatives. Exact circuit encoding O(k log k) bits, O(k) gates, linear gate evaluation, 0/1 arithmetic; exact checks polynomial. This is an easy family for factored transport, not a hard family for all solvers. Invertible triangular map t'=t XOR f turns the energy into [t'!=0]+[t'!=h]+[h!=b].

Walsh OR has constant 3/4 and three coefficients of magnitude 1/4. On disjoint pairs f has 4^k nonzero coefficients, degree 2k and l1 norm (3/2)^k. Write chi_f=(-1)^f=1-2f and chi_t=(-1)^t. E_b=3/2-(chi_t chi_f + chi_t chi_f chi_h + (-1)^b chi_h)/2. The two shifted frequency blocks have disjoint support, also disjoint from the constant/h terms; the total energy has exactly 2*4^k+2 nonzero Fourier terms. The mass from nonconstant f frequencies alone is M_k=2*((3/2)^k-(3/4)^k), each such energy coefficient magnitude <=a_k=3^(k-1)/4^k. Every retained set of at most m frequencies leaves tail T>=M_k-m*a_k. The retained polynomial has mean 3/2 (constant retained); its min <=3/2. Hence the global triangle-tail certificate L=min P-T is negative for all sufficiently large k and every m bounded by any fixed polynomial in original encoded N. This applies to all choices of retained frequencies, not just greedy/rank-8; coefficient cancellation cannot help across these disjoint blocks. For rank O(log N), retained support is polynomial, so the same obstruction applies. Explicit exact expansion also exponential. Proof is restricted to this particular global Walsh tail rule after this nonaffine representation; does not exclude grouped/factored checkers, affine transport on original t' chart, gate constraints, other lower bounds, or unrestricted algorithms. BigInt exact numerator/denominator have O(k) bits even while coefficient list is exponential. Fixed bit/degree cap must retain factored objects rather than assume flat expansion.
