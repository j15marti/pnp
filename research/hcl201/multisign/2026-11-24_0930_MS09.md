## 3. Theorem: reserve-layer quotient composition
Choose a sequence of subgraphs J_i of the variable arcs and let H_i be the tag image of their integral circulation lattice. W_0={0}, W_i=W_{i-1}+H_i. Let L_i be the exponent of the image of H_i in G/W_{i-1}; it is 1 exactly when H_i subset W_{i-1}. Drop such zero-progress layers. If for each arc e,
  sum_{i:e in J_i}(L_i-1) <= r_e,
then phi(f)+W_s subset phi(F). Every tag in this lower coset has a constructive integral bounded-flow lift in polynomial bit time given polynomially many encoded layers, independently of |G|. If W_s=H, the support is exactly that coset.

Proof: take delta=t-phi(f) in W_s. Work in reverse i=s,...,1, maintaining delta_i in W_i. Solve the group equation for d_i an integral circulation supported in J_i with phi(d_i) congruent to delta_i modulo W_{i-1}; such a preimage exists by W_i=W_{i-1}+H_i. By network integrality find integer circulation z_i supported on J_i between coordinate floors and ceilings of d_i/L_i. Set v_i=d_i-L_i z_i. Then A v_i=0, |v_i(e)|<=L_i-1, and phi(v_i)=phi(d_i) mod W_{i-1} since L_i annihilates the image of H_i. Thus delta_{i-1}=delta_i-phi(v_i) belongs to W_{i-1}. At the end delta_0=0. The reconstructed x=f+sum_i v_i satisfies conservation and bounds by the reserve inequality, with the exact original tag t. In particular physical edge overlap is permitted; independence of the layers is neither asserted nor needed. Earlier layers correct the rounding error in the previous subgroup, and the reserve ensures those corrections coexist. The outer inclusion follows because differences of actual flows are integer circulations supported on E_var. QED.

Corollary: SAT if t-phi(f) in W_s, UNSAT if outside H, otherwise UNKNOWN (unless another exact method applies). If W_s=H, all targets are decided. This is an exact terminal on a larger promise than full-block MS08. A certified subgroup growth reduces residual *group index* [H:W_i] by an integer factor >=2; for elementary binary H this reduces quotient dimension at least one. It is not automatically a reduction of physical matching defect dim(L/L2).

## 4. Polynomial discovery algorithm
Maintain the remaining reserve a_e. For each distinct positive value t among a_e, form J={e variable:a_e>=t}; compute a fundamental cycle basis and its image H_J, and calculate L=exp((H_J+W)/W). If 1<L<=t+1, reserve L-1 on every arc in the union of the basis supports (bridges unused), append the layer and update W. Restart threshold discovery. Stop when W=H or no candidate passes. Deterministic order: descending thresholds. Trying each cycle block separately is also sound but not required here; not executed. Candidate thresholds need not enumerate integers up to a capacity. There are at most m candidates per successful round and one final failed round, and at most floor(log2 |G|) successful rounds since every subgroup enlargement multiplies order by >=2. These quantities are polynomial in the bit encoding. The method is not claimed to find every possible reserve decomposition or optimal baseline.

Represent W as column lattice Lambda_W=diag(d_j) Z^q + span_Z(its label generators) in Z^q. Group quotients G/W are represented by Z^q/Lambda_W. A full rank HNF basis of Lambda_W has polynomial bit encoding; the order of a vector h modulo W is the lcm of denominators of the rational coordinates B_W^{-1}h. Exponent L is lcm of the generator orders. Retain polynomially many original cycle columns and subgroup generators for preimage, use a polynomial-bit integer normal-form/linear equation algorithm, with previous subgroup columns and modulus diagonal slack. No factorization of a large modulus or enumeration of group elements is necessary. Exact arithmetic and ordinary bounded-flow rounding give polynomial T and S, including failed thresholds, fixed-coordinate closure and verification. Cumulative memory CM<=(T+1)S for that same sequential execution.
