## 1. New exact saturation criterion
Let centered explicit response dictionaries S_i contain 0. For a block subset G, choose at most one nonzero v_i in S_i for each i in G, with all chosen vectors linearly independent. If every option in every S_i of G belongs to H=span(chosen vectors), then sum_{i in G} S_i=H. One inclusion is the span test. For the other, use the unique binary basis expansion of h and select either v_i or 0 in each owner block, and zero in all unmatched blocks. This proves exact group replacement without enumerating the sumset; the same coefficients reconstruct original choices. It applies even where every local and pairwise sumset has trivial translation stabilizer.

Acquire a maximum independent transversal by matroid intersection (partition on block IDs, binary linear independence). Each option has its original owner; a shortest augmenting path alternates a fundamental-circuit exchange and a partition exchange. Recheck each augmentation by independent high-pivot GE and distinct owner test. For a selected basis I, mark a block bad if any option lies outside span(I); give every other block outgoing edges to owners of nonzero basis coordinates of its options. Propagate bad backwards. The remaining largest closed set G for this particular basis satisfies the criterion: all options lie in the span of the selected vectors owned by G. Verify directly by independent GE. A maximum transversal also has the Rado upper bound rank(union of options of R)+number of blocks outside R, for the reachable block set R from unselected owners. This upper bound is checked independently; no claim of complete saturated-subset recognition under arbitrary basis choices. Discovery is polynomial in the explicit dictionary encoding (at most m augmentations, polynomial exchange and elimination operations; no 2^rank enumeration), conditional on paid acquisition of the dictionaries. Checkers for rank, membership and reconstruction are polynomial in that same encoding.

If G lies on one side P of a cut, quotient by H subset U_P. Let U_P,U_Q be original direction spans. Exact cut loss is dim(H intersect U_Q), since lambda=dim U_P+dim U_Q-dim(U_P+U_Q) and quotient changes these dimensions by dim H, dim(H intersect U_Q), dim H, respectively. For local periods distributed across both sides H=H_P+H_Q, H_P subset U_P,H_Q subset U_Q, the exact loss is dim(H intersect U_P intersect U_Q), as already reported by Finite Group Classification #7239051133. This extends its safe quotient operation to **jointly saturated** groups, not just periods in individual dictionaries. No ambient-rank decrease is assumed to be cut decrease. Quotient is exact: lift the chosen exterior representatives, correct the discrepancy in H with the verified independent-transversal choices, reverse prior maps. Absorb all of G as an implicit affine block, then quotient; do not explicitly write 2^dim H rows. Under existing fixed Phi=(B+q)(B0*K+1)+sum |S_i| this is a nonrenewable strict decrease if it removes nonempty group or positive dimension, provided the explicit chart/credit is the one previously admitted; no reset, no independent credit for unchanged coordinates.
