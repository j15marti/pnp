## Exact counterexample: nonlinear reversible rechart resets rank credit
For any fixed d>=1 set n=d+2. Compare plane y_n=0 with the graph x_n=prod_{i<n} x_i, via invertible triangular change y_n=x_n+prod_{i<n}x_i, y_i=x_i. Both have 2^(n-1) points and exact polynomial-size forward/reverse maps. Every degree<=d polynomial uniquely A(x_<n)+x_n B(x_<n), deg A<=d, deg B<=d-1. On plane W consists of A=0, arbitrary B. On graph restriction is A+prod(x_i) B(1,...,1); the monomial degree d+1 exceeds d, hence A=0 and B(1,...,1)=0. The full degree-d vanishing rank drops by exactly one going from plane to graph, Phi increases by one although r unchanged. Reverse switching can generate false credit indefinitely.
