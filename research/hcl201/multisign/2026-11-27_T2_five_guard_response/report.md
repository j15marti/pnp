## General theorem (proved)
In common free coordinates x in F2^r, let k certified impossible affine guards G_i = {A_i x=b_i}, each consistent and reduced to q_i<=r independent equations (drop inconsistent guards; a whole-carrier guard directly refutes). Let U be the complement of their union. For each guard choose one defining equation that is violated; reverse its RHS. Intersect the selected equations, omitting inconsistent intersections. The union of these at most product_i q_i <= r^k affine flats equals U, by the elementary distributive law. For each flat write x=a+Bt with B a basis of its directions. Evaluate all squarefree monomials of degree <=d at t of weight <=d. The restriction of any such polynomial to the flat has degree <=d after Boolean reduction. Its coefficients are recovered from the low-weight evaluations by Boolean subset inversion: coefficient at S is sum over T subset S of f(1_T). Hence a polynomial vanishes on all these test points iff it vanishes on the entire flat. Stack evaluations across flats and use exact GF2 nullspace; obtains ALL degree<=d identities on U, not just the affine hull. The full evaluation span is obtained from those points, which are only relaxation points, not asserted source models. Exact reverse mapping for flat coordinates; for arbitrary equations the extracted low-degree identities need not alone characterize U. For fixed k,d, O(r^(k+d)) points and D=sum_{j<=d} binom(r,j) features; dense elimination polynomial in original explicit chart size (e.g. O(r^(k+d) D^2) bit operations plus evaluation and flat elimination). Stream flat/point rows; charge all failed systems, rank and basis extraction, guards/maps and arithmetic. No semantic SAT oracle. For supplied irredundant full binary affine covers, Brouwer 1986 stronger known theorem gives joint rank of ALL defining normals <= k-1 (translate common carrier and quotient intersection of directions). Thus a complete five-guard cover depends on at most four common Boolean linear responses, not merely five separate rank<=4 guards. Obtain minimal cover by paid constant-size tests and actual response basis by GE. This is a post-acquisition theorem, not a uniform search over exponentially many possible normals. Primary printed theorem https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/2507/2507D.pdf p315, DOI 10.1016/0012-365X(86)90177-9; precise hypotheses checked; no new attribution. T1's weaker individual bound also follows by choosing an exclusive witness and one reversed equation from every other guard. Complement of one codim q>d affine flat has no nonzero degree<=d vanishing polynomial: in adapted coordinates any polynomial supported only on that flat is its degree-q indicator times a polynomial on free coordinates. Thus partial exclusions can deliver zero rank credit at every fixed degree, even though exact cover count decreases; no unrestricted hardness follows.
