## 1. Qualitative obstruction and exact repair
For any edge region i, let S_i consist of combined vectors z=(o,s) of local matchings saturating vertices incident exclusively to region i. o is the full binary occupancy vector; s is XOR of labels of selected original edges. A perfect matching gives sum_i z_i=(1,c). The converse is FALSE if a vertex appears in at least three regions: on K_1,3 put each edge in its own region and tag zero. Every leaf must be occupied locally, all three center occupancies sum to 1 mod 2, yet no perfect matching exists. This falsifies *unguarded XOR gluing*, not HCL-198/199's stated recurrence when it separately enforces no double matching. The same failure occurs for any odd-degree star d>=3. It does not by itself establish a strict four-valent post-closure counterexample.

**Incidence splitting theorem.** For each vertex v incident to d>=3 different regions, replace v by d clones c_1,...,c_d, assign the old edges of region i to clone c_i (renumber the incident regions), and introduce d-1 private vertices a_1,...,a_(d-1). Put the alternating path c_1-a_1-c_2-a_2-...-a_(d-1)-c_d in one new selector region, all its labels zero. Vertices incident to at most two regions can be left alone. All new clones occur in exactly two regions and path auxiliaries in one.

Proof: each of the d-1 private vertices must use a distinct clone in a PM; no edge joins two clones inside the path. Thus exactly one clone must be matched externally. Conversely for any one externally matched clone c_i, deleting it splits the path into two even paths (possibly empty), each with a unique perfect matching. There is a bijection of original and expanded PMs, preserving all original edge choices and label XOR. A vertex of degree zero is rejected immediately. The number of extra vertices is 2(d-1) and edges 2(d-1) per replaced vertex; sum d <= 2|E|, so construction is polynomial with linear vertex/edge growth. Reconstruct by dropping zero-tag paths and identifying clones; validate original matching.

After splitting, **pure XOR composition is exact**: each vertex has local occupancy 0/1 in at most two regions, and odd total then means exactly one. Thus sum_i S_i contains (1,c) iff original graph has a compatible PM. No independent double-occupancy bookkeeping remains necessary at joins. The selector region has exactly d feasible boundary occupancy vectors (all but one clone locally occupied); each has a unique internal witness. Do not enumerate 2^d subsets of selector edges. This repair applies to the derived matching graph, not a claimed four-valent-preserving compiler on the original source constraint network.

## 2. Exact local joint affine hull without enumerating occupancy
Given a region graph H=(V,E), with B the vertices allowed to remain locally unmatched, make private dummies d_b for every b in B, add b--d_b, join all dummies by a clique, and add one extra clique dummy if |V|+|B| is odd. Require a perfect matching in the expanded graph. For any partial matching covering V-B, unmatched boundary vertices must match to their own dummies. The remaining dummy count is even because the number of unmatched original vertices has parity |V|. Complete the remaining dummy clique arbitrarily. Conversely any PM restricts to an allowed partial matching. The projection onto original edges is surjective; it need not be one-to-one, so no count preservation claim here.

Attach observation vector (e_u+e_v,g_uv) to original edges and zero to dummy edges. Extend observations with a forced disjoint edge of tag (0,1) and use Lovasz finite-field PM incidence span (El Maalouly, Steiner, Wulf, arXiv:2207.09797v2, Thm 20; Thm 19 gives exact parity search). The span of projected observations with final coordinate 1 equals the affine hull a_i+V_i of S_i, exactly. First ordinary PM detects empty support. Graph overhead O(|B|^2); GF(2) rank and image computation polynomial. Thus unconditional complete *local joint affine closure* is deterministic polynomial without enumerating boundary occupancy. This generalizes M01's perfect-matching affine hull to partial matchings. It is only a necessary condition for membership, not sufficient. All observations intended for later joins must remain in the map.

## 3. Target-conditioned branch decomposition and exact leaf predicates
Let U_X=sum_(i in X) V_i, a_X=sum_(i in X) a_i; target z*. Every globally extendible partial sum at cut X belongs to
J_X=(a_X+U_X) intersect (z*+a_complement+U_complement).
If nonempty, J_X is an affine coset of U_X intersect U_complement, of dimension k_X. Compute by Gaussian elimination; if the root target is outside the sum hull, reject before any branching. This is certified unconditional necessary closure; a removed candidate is impossible in any global solution. Keep complete local affine equations; later conditional deductions retain their guards.

For a leaf i, enumerate only J_{i} (at most 2^w points for tree width w), NOT all local occupancy/syndrome combinations, even if physical boundary or local span is huge. For each point test exact membership in S_i: delete locally unoccupied boundary vertices; require a PM of the remaining induced region, including all occupied boundary and all interior vertices; check full prescribed syndrome. Selector leaves use the direct all-but-one rule. M01 supplies deterministic exact membership when fixed-occupancy syndrome affine dimension <=1 or its exact factor-component peeling resolves the fiber, with witness. Otherwise the general multitag fiber remains OPEN for deterministic polynomial evaluation, or use a charged exact enumeration / randomized Fourier-Pfaffian method. A sufficient precheck is dim(V_i intersect (0 x GF(2)^r))<=1, because any fixed-occupancy support differences lie in that kernel. Even this sufficient bound need not be necessary. All target-conditioned fiber tests must check original constraints; no use of the whole affine hull as exact support. GD-01 can use this predicate on fixed observations, but if only selected linear observations are supplied, fixing them is not the same as fixing occupancy; retain missing observations or charge a more general exact test.

For a supplied rooted binary tree, each leaf table contains exactly the tested attainable points in its J. At internal node X=A union B, XOR pairs of child entries; retain only sums in J_X. Induction: any globally extendible solution restricts to both child tables; every accepted pair concatenates feasible leaf matchings (by incidence splitting and final root occupancy). Some intermediate pairs can occupy a vertex twice, but no eventual root target can have that collision because there are at most two occurrences; rejecting it early is optional. At root require z*. Store one witness pointer per entry; reconstruct original PM and verify. This is exact decision/search, not counting. The tree DP is equivalent to the earlier HCL-197/198 sum-space algorithm after the new occupancy repair. A leaf of rank w can have a hard local predicate, so width alone is not sufficient.

Given exact membership cost C(N), leaf tests <=h*2^w; binary joins <=h*2^(2w); Gaussian maps, witness storage and reconstruction polynomial per state/transition. T=2^w poly(N) C(N)+2^(2w) poly(N); S=2^w poly(N)+S_C(N) (sequential local calls, stored witnesses of polynomial size, retained all tables), CM<=T*S for that same implementation. No exponential local support table is required. If local C polynomial and supplied w=O(log N), deterministic polynomial terminal. If the local tester returns OPEN for a needed state, return OPEN unless other certified evidence settles the target; cannot treat OPEN as UNSAT.

**Automatic discovery (known theorem transfer):** the computed V_i are a subspace arrangement. Jeong, Kim, Oum, Finding branch-decompositions of matroids, hypergraphs, and more, arXiv:1711.01381, Thm 1.1, explicitly constructs a tree of width <=k or reports none for this arrangement, in O(r' m^2+(k+1)r' m h+k^3 h^3+f(2,k)h^2), where r' ambient dimension, m total spanning vectors and h number of regions. These are polynomially bounded input parameters except the dependence on k. This yields automatic deterministic polynomial discovery for each FIXED k. It does not establish polynomial discovery for k=O(log N), nor discovery of a better initial region partition. Tree discovery algorithm not implemented this run; result is a theorem transfer. A failure for one arrangement is not an unrestricted obstruction. The result addresses GD-01's request for an exact interface predicate and extends M01's independent-component peeling to a framework that safely permits shared vertices.

## 4. Boundary gauge no-go and useful conditional gauge
For common vertex potential P mapping occupancy to syndrome, gauge (o,s)->(o,s+Po) is an invertible linear shear on the entire joint ambient space. Shift target identically. For every fixed partition and cut, V'_i=T(V_i), and intersection dimensions are invariant under T. Thus an ordinary common vertex gauge CANNOT reduce true joint affine width by itself, though it may reduce syndrome-only rank and computation in a chosen coordinate representation. The minimum over trees for the same partition is also invariant. Region-specific independent gauges require compensating interface variables; their naive use is unsound. Augmented integer/cardinality gauges may be nonlinear in occupancy and are not covered by this invariance statement; no guarantee of reduction without charging their representation.

For a fixed local occupancy, however, the syndrome variation kernel V_i intersect ker(occupancy projection) is gauge invariant and can be much smaller than the projected syndrome rank. It gives the sufficient rank-one fiber condition above, or a bound on conditional Fourier filter cost. To reduce true joint width, seek actual support restrictions, exact independent products/quotients, or new region decompositions, not mere invertible change of basis.
