## 1. Model and polynomial recognition
For every explicitly encoded graph G with required vertices H and optional ports partitioned into disjoint owner groups P_i, let X be the source matchings covering H, with fixed graph-representable guard. Input has binary interval counts L_i<=k_i(M)<=U_i and disjoint pairs of original tag coordinates with arbitrary four-state masks. Unary constraints allowed. Tag t(M)=t0+XOR_{e in M}g_e over GF(2)^q (t0 a supplied constant), and d(M) optional defect indicator. No extra uncompiled couplings, arbitrary budgets or source oracles. All additional protected linear equalities can be represented as singleton tag restrictions if encoded in this input; arbitrary other conditions are not assumed compiled. N includes graph, bounds, masks, source IDs, tags, and guard. Infeasible X certified by ordinary matching. I09 gives exact affine hull for bipartite source. For general source use I08 required-cover completion with zero dummy labels except optional defect indicators on original-to-dummy edges and GD10 forced marker + Lovasz GF(2) span. Project to joint (d,t); synthetic basis vectors are not witnesses.

Write U=direction space of aff{(d,t)} and K={z:(0,z) in U}, eta=dim K. Choose eta coordinate indices J such that coordinate projection K->GF(2)^eta is bijective (pivot columns). Put r=t_J. For each j compute unique linear coefficient a_j with z_j=a_j.z_J for every z in K. Then h_j=t_j+a_j.r annihilates vertical directions and is pointwise affine in d by I09. This is independent of which source matching is chosen. Determine constant h_j by the exact joint hull, store its value; otherwise compute all owner identities h_j=c_ji+b_i(d), b_i=sum_{v in P_i}d_v mod2, by testing direction annihilation and anchor offset. Build bipartite graph from nonconstant h_j to owners with certified identities. Maximum matching gives maximum injective assignment for this fixed J and supplied partition. Let B be unmatched coordinates, k=|B|. Do not assume that this is globally minimum over all J or partitions. Chosen J coordinates themselves have h_j=0 and are not counted in B.

Define Y=(t_J,t_B), dimension eta+k before compression. Compute its exact affine hull by projection of U; let rho be its affine rank <=eta+k. Choose rho actual coordinate positions in Y with injective projection on its direction space. They form a residual tag vector R(M) whose value uniquely determines Y(M) on X. Enumerate only rho-bit words; reconstruct full Y by Gaussian affine decoding. This accounts even for repeated/linearly dependent exceptional tags. The resulting bound is in rho, and <=4^(eta+k) poly(N). Neither small eta nor low physical overlap individually suffices as a universal assertion.

## 2. Exact mask specialization, no false mixing
For each fixed residual word w, all unmatched original tag values are constants decoded from Y. Every matched coordinate is its owner's physical parity plus known offset c_ji+a_j.r. All constant h_j tags also become constants. Substitute into ORIGINAL paired masks, without changing them by an arbitrary global basis rotation. Every surviving binary mask uses two distinct owners; no owner is reused because the assignment is injective and original tag pairs are disjoint. Unary mask can pin or leave a port optional; constant mask rejects or disappears. Any owner interval remains in force. Apply I08 slot/filler/triangle gadget to build a required-cover graph preserving EVERY source matching satisfying these specialized masks (not just existence). Complete to a simple perfect-matching graph G_w, retaining original source edges and tagging them by the rho selected original coordinates of Y, zero tags on all added edges, and track original affine tag constants separately. In a fixed w, enforce residual target w minus its constant using these ORIGINAL edge tags. Therefore the exact source constrained matching exists iff at least one G_w has a perfect matching with the specified residual XOR tag. A source matching lifts to that very branch by its R(M), and any successful branch projects to an actual source matching satisfying every original constraint. No arbitrary affine-hull realizability assumption or one-witness-per-marginal shortcut.

## 3. Deterministic terminal for rho<=1, randomized for arbitrary rho
For rho=0 ordinary perfect matching suffices. For rho=1 invoke deterministic Correct Parity Matching (CPM), El Maalouly, Steiner, Wulf ISAAC 2023 Theorem 19, based on Lovasz span Theorem 20/Corollary 22. Witness recovery by edge deletion is polynomial. At most two branches. This is a deterministic polynomial adapter even though a nonphysical tag or an unmatched owner coordinate may remain. Use GD10 affine hull in each fixed state before backend; affine hull is exact for testing one parity, not general joint parity.

For general rho let v=|V(G_w)| even. Orient each undirected edge by a fixed vertex ordering. Over odd prime field F_p assign independent formal variables X_e. For a character u in GF(2)^rho let A_u be skew symmetric with upper entries X_e*(-1)^(u.g_e), zero absent; with parallel edges sum distinct variables (or use private length-3 paths). For target b,
 P_b(X) = 2^(-rho) sum_u (-1)^(u.b) Pf(A_u).
Expansion by perfect matchings plus character orthogonality gives P_b = sum_{M:g(M)=b} sign(M) product_{e in M}X_e. Distinct matchings use distinct monomials with nonzero coefficients +/-1, so P_b is identically zero iff infeasible, regardless of Pfaffian orientation or matching counts. Do not use determinant squared in place of Pfaffian; it can lose the XOR tag. Each character evaluated by skew elimination with pivot row/column swap, sign correction and Schur complement, polynomial field ops O(v^3) per Pfaffian. All characters use SAME random edge values within the evaluation. Choose prime p>2v (can find by elementary trial division and Bertrand in polynomial time), choose independent uniform values from a power-of-two subset of nonzero field elements of size >=v (choose p>4v for slack). Degree v/2, Schwartz-Zippel false-zero probability <=1/2. Nonzero evaluation certifies existence; zero alone is not a deterministic proof. Given nonzero evaluation, expand along first vertex, evaluate coefficients for subgraphs and adjusted target using SAME weights. At least one incident edge has nonzero weighted contribution; choose it, recurse, and verify original match, tags, intervals and masks. At most O(v^2) calls, no additional probabilistic reconstruction failure. Retain guard identity. Independently repeat per branch O(log(1/delta)) times. If input SAT at least one true branch, probability all its trials zero <=delta; if UNSAT no false SAT. This is an RP-type bounded-error terminal, NOT deterministic normalization, nor an exact UNSAT certificate. Use UNKNOWN for randomized all-zero in a proof-producing solver (or explicitly label Monte Carlo NO in an RP solver). Never insert random negative into unconditional DAG.

The rank compression gives at most 2^rho graph states and at most 2^rho character evaluations per query. Time <=4^rho poly(N) log(1/delta) incl. source acquisition within explicit model, validation, Gaussian maps, injection matching, graph construction, every failed state, field arithmetic, all self-reduction and final witness checks. Work is sequential, not parallel span. All states/characters streamed; peak S=poly(N,log(1/delta)); physical cumulative memory <=(T+1) max(1,S) with retained encoded input, not minimum-description CM. No explicit 2^rho table needed. For rho=O(log N) this gives polynomial randomized time; for rho<=1 deterministic polynomial. Source acquisition from strict wheel E4/B4, global guard count and full cooperative work not covered.

## 5. FALSIFIED: eta alone is sufficient (unless P=NP; or NP=RP for randomized)
Given arbitrary simple graph H for 3-colourability, create 2|V(H)| independent optional K2 source edges. Selecting each edge sets a bit x=1, not selecting sets 0; all words feasible. Equivalently x=1+d at a chosen endpoint. For each source vertex put two fresh tag coordinates equal to its two bits, mask {00,01,10}; for each edge ij put two fresh coordinates equal to x_i XOR x_j, mask {01,10,11}. All pairs disjoint as TAG positions, but physical port observations overlap. All individual tags are affine in physical occupancy; indeed each tag uses at most two port bits and affine constant. Thus K=0, eta=0 even before constraints; any physical owner partition may be singleton with unconstrained intervals. Original graph is disjoint edges, bipartite, max degree one. Every proper 3-coloring gives one original matching, and any feasible matching gives a proper 3-coloring; polynomial explicit labels (dense size O(n(n+m)) or sparse), NP membership by direct witness. Hence NP-complete at eta=0 in explicit tagged-mask model. This does not imply P!=NP and does not transfer to strict wheel source absent reduction. In this construction every original mask has three values, full affine hull, every pair projection of the unrestricted source is all four words. Repeated joint affine mask closure cannot start (all projected masks yield no equations), independent of label. K4 UNSAT and K4 minus an edge SAT with six labelled 3-colorings; disjoint copies give arbitrarily large examples on both labels. The barrier is independent of high nonphysical rank: even perfectly physicalizable tags can encode globally coupled overlap. Do not treat quotient rank zero as sufficient for ordinary matching unless owner injection or a different valid composition theorem is established.
