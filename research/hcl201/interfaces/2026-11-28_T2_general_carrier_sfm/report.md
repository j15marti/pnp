## Generalization of carrier cut oracle
Normalize native equality and signed edges using parity union-find. Each normalized carrier has a bit; convert each B owner's local four-bit physical words into normalized occurrence words using the sign offsets. Remove words inconsistent on repeated carriers and any actually admitted local pins. An empty dictionary certifies local NO. For nonempty dictionaries D_i, let V_i be the span of differences from a fixed member in the four-dimensional occurrence space, d_i=dim V_i. Represent V_i by all nonzero dual equations; substitute carrier indices, cancel repeated indices modulo two, yielding rows H_i. Let H be their concatenation, R=rank H. Define C (as a space) to be the consistent-carrier subspace of occurrence space; use a star map M with ker M=C. Its coordinates are differences of each nonfirst occurrence with the first occurrence of its carrier. This is an exact syndrome chart: independent local choices sum to zero under M iff they agree on every carrier. Signed port assignments lift using the normalization map.

For a cut P|Q, let I_P, I_Q be carriers with all occurrences on the named side, and b=r-|I_P|-|I_Q|. Write U_P=M(direct sum_{i in P} V_i), similarly Q. The kernel of M on the direct sum supported on P consists exactly of carrier assignments on I_P satisfying H[P,I_P]. Hence
dim U_P=sum_{i in P} d_i-|I_P|+rank H[P,I_P].
The corresponding total is sum_i d_i-r+R. Subtract total dimension from the two side dimensions:
lambda(P)=b+rank H[P,I_P]+rank H[Q,I_Q]-R.
No distinct-carrier hypothesis is needed. No use of affine hull as a substitute for actual feasibility. For guard-local filtered words the same direction calculation works; general cross-owner guards must be encoded exactly as additional constraints before claiming equivalence.

If every local dictionary is complement-closed, the all-ones occurrence vector lies in V_i. Let K be the owner-constant subspace and use the exact quotient by M(K); a quotient witness discrepancy in M(K) is solved by tagged GE and the corresponding whole-owner complements. Let c be the number of components of the owner/carrier incidence graph, counting only carriers touching B. dim M(K)=m-c, since C intersect K consists of one flip per incidence component. For a side Q, merge carriers co-occurring at an owner in Q; let c_Q be the resulting class count, including isolated carriers; R_Q=rank H after identifying columns. To have c_vector+k_vector supported on P, each Q owner forces equality of its carrier bits and fixes its owner constant; P imposes H c_vector=0 since ones lies in V_i. There are c_Q-R_Q free carrier bits and |P| free owner constants, with representation kernel dimension c. Thus
dim U'_P=sum_{i in P}(d_i-1)-c_Q+R_Q+c.
Subtract the whole quotient dimension sum_i(d_i-1)-r+R+c to get
lambda_quot(P)=r-c_P-c_Q+R_P+R_Q-R+c.
Works with repeated carriers and locally complement-closed restricted dictionaries, not with an arbitrary pin destroying the period. The actual input, not an asserted symmetry, determines whether the quotient is legal. Local dictionary tables have <=6 words; H has <=15 rows per owner. Explicit binary Gaussian elimination and sparse column substitution give polynomial bit cost in original N for each cut, e.g. conservative O(N^4) with simple dense storage and indexing. All ranks refer to actual direction spaces, not raw ambient rank alone.

## Approximation and SFM audit
Read Korhonen-Oum arXiv:2601.04756v2 section 2 and section 5 (HTML retrieved after initial fetch failure), generalized Oum-Seymour approximation. For f(P)=lambda(P), or lambda_quot(P) when eligible, f(P)=rank(P)+rank(Q)-rank(all) is integer, symmetric, normalized and submodular because the sum-of-subspaces rank function is submodular. Singleton upper bounds are d_i<=3, quotient <=d_i-1<=2. For k>=max singleton width, approximation returns width <=3k+c' or certifies bw>k with O(2^(3k+c') m^2 SUB(m,km,gamma)); no exact optimum needed. k=ceil(a log2(N+2)) for fixed a>0 makes this polynomial with a deterministic exact integer SFM subroutine and paid bit costs. Do not import the exact optimum 2^O(k^2) algorithm at log width. Classical deterministic SFM (Iwata-Fleischer-Fujishige, arXiv:math/0004089; Schrijver DOI:10.1006/jctb.2000.1989) suffices for the polynomial claim without relying on randomization in a faster implementation. The cited Korhonen-Oum SUB tariff is O(gamma m M^3 log m); not measured here. Bound f by km using subadditivity. The constrained interpolation min_{A subset Z subset V-B} f(Z) is ordinary SFM on remaining free elements with g(X)=f(A union X)-f(A). Restriction preserves submodularity; range remains polynomial. An exact lower-bound check on a proposed minimizer uses a convex combination of greedy base vectors for g: for a permutation, successive prefix differences give x(S)<=g(S) by diminishing returns; therefore g(X)>=sum_j min(0,x_j). A rational combination of these vectors has the same bound. Equality with candidate value gives a checkable optimality certificate, using actual rank queries and arithmetic; no assumption that a single greedy order always certifies a minimum. For small controls the single greedy certificate happened to be tight. General SFM and approximation tree construction not implemented. This is a paid theoretical interface, not a completed universal acquisition algorithm.
