## 4. Order independence and strict extension (PROVED)

If part of a previously eligible batch was absorbed by another run, its witness lists project to zero, so the remaining subset is still eligible (size <=c). Consequently all maximal greedy sequences absorb the same component set: induction simulates each step of any sequence in the other's final state. W at saturation is consequently unique for fixed lists/chart. This does NOT imply invariance to choice of acquired basis or to a different authenticated decomposition.
