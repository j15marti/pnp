## A. General fiber-density saturation lemma (PROVED)
Work over F2. For independent factors S_i=(a_i+U_i) minus one specified hole h_i (or full a_i+U_i), assume rank>=2 for punctured factors, exact support and actual source lift. Affine factors may have any rank. All relevant protected XOR observations are included in the full vector, with guard fixed; no other constraints silently omitted. For a proposed group B, define W=sum U_i, A=sum a_i. For every z in A+W the ambient sum fiber F_z={(x_i in a_i+U_i): XOR x_i=z} is a nonempty affine coset of ker(sum). Its coordinate-i projection is a coset of R_i=U_i intersect sum_{j in B-i} U_j. Proof: a kernel vector with i-coordinate v exists iff v lies in the sum of the other spaces; all fibers are translates of the kernel. Let d_i=dim R_i=dim U_i+rank(other spaces)-rank(W). Under the uniform distribution on F_z, a specified hole is either unreachable or attained with probability 2^(-d_i); all elements of a linear projection have equal preimage count.

Hence if sum over punctured i of 2^(-d_i) < 1, every z has a legal preimage. This proves EXACT sum S_i=A+W. No independence assumption. All rank tests Gaussian, compare dyadic rationals exactly. For an actual target z, a constructive deterministic lift for large symbolic punctured spaces: Gaussian parameterize F_z, sequentially pin its free coordinates, maintaining conditional expected number of hole events <1. Each hole event is an affine system in the remaining variables, with exact probability zero or 2^(-rank); at least one child expectation <= parent. At the final word expectation is an integer <1, hence zero. Polynomial time and bit space in the explicit basis representation, not in the number of ambient points. This general symbolic lifting is proved but not executed; source preimage acquisition is a separate obligation for any succinct matching factor. The executed algorithm uses small explicitly listed rows only.

Pairwise case: if two rank-two punctured planes coincide, d_1=d_2=2 and full support follows. Stronger pair rule (symbolic): for two punctured affine spaces with intersection dimension one and unique nonzero intersection vector h, the sum is its ambient affine sum minus exactly h_1+h_2+h; all other fibers contain at least one legal pair. Indeed any fiber has two pairs; both are bad only when the two distinct pairs respectively contain the holes. If intersection dimension >=2 full by density. Full+punctured with nonzero intersection is full. This symbolic rule can fuse a connected overlap graph into one full or punctured affine support by growing a connected component, retaining a polynomial basis and one hole. Not implemented or claimed as a universal algorithm. Disjoint spaces can retain multiple holes.

## B. Three-plane saturation without pairwise overlap (PROVED)
Let q1,q2 be independent binary scalar columns and q3=q1+q2. Embed GF2^2 relative colors d via q_i tensor d. For each i allow any 3 of 4 colors (one forbidden s_i). The three direction planes have zero pairwise intersection but each is contained in the sum of the other two. Thus d_i=2 for all i, and the density sum is 3/4<1. Their exact collective support is the entire rank-four span, independent of the signs. Each local factor is period-free and has exactly three actual matching witnesses by I11. This proves new affine period discovery after nonlinear fusion, without row enumeration at the global level. A fixed target can also be lifted directly: choose the common two-bit kernel parameter among four possibilities, each hole excludes at most one, so a legal parameter exists.

Sharpness: for a scalar circuit of length four, take independent q1,q2,q3 and q4=q1+q2+q3, all holes zero. Write the target by its three color coefficients t1,t2,t3. Its legal preimages require d4 not in {0,t1,t2,t3}. Exactly six target words (permutations of the three nonzero colors) are absent out of 64. The affine hull is full. With other signs the missing set translates. Every fiber in the four-circuit has four possible lifts, so absence is real, not counting cancellation. No unconditional four-factor saturation rule.

## C. Automatic quotient and strict resource ledger (PROVED for explicit small rows)

All constraints represented by one XOR target t in q bits. Projection of a triple is a triple, pair, or singleton; projected affine factors remain affine. Before any action, exhaust row deletion: delete x of factor i if t+x is outside the affine sum of all others. Restart after each deletion, reject empty factor. This is a certified unconditional affine closure, not semantic closure using a SAT oracle. No source branching occurs.
1. Find a full affine factor by rank/cardinality, or enumerate all pairs and triples of current triple factors and certify the density condition by ranks. Search stops if none exists (UNKNOWN, not UNSAT).
2. For the selected group with exact sum A+W, map all other rows through a Gaussian quotient pi with ker pi=W, deduplicate only after preserving original preimages, set t'=pi(t+A). Store current group, rest, and t. The quotient is SAT iff original target can be reached: choose any original preimages of surviving other rows, then remaining target lies in A+W and must have an actual group lift. Protected coordinates included in target/tag before projection, so full compatibility is verified during lift. For guard changes recertify; no merging across conditional guards.
3. Restart closure after every rewrite. At empty residual accept iff target zero. Reverse the archive, choose each preimage of its actual projected row, then choose an actual tuple for the group (<=4^3 constant candidates). Reconstruct and verify original full tuple. An UNSAT at closure lifts through exact equivalence; no rejected candidate search proves UNSAT.

## D. Graph specialization: exact <=3-edge-cut deletion (PROVED)
I11 fundamental-cycle quotient has scalar column matrix Q_c with kernel the cut space; color version tensors with F2^2. For a nonempty bond C of size k<=3, its indicator is in ker Q_c, and minimality makes its restricted columns a binary circuit. For k=1 bridge col=0; k=2 parallel nonzero cols; k=3 the three distinct nonzero cols of a rank-two scalar space. B certifies exact collective affine support for k=3 and A certifies k=2. Equivalently, independently solve each side of a cut. Translate every color on one side by the same two-bit word; interior relative colors unchanged, each cross edge forbids exactly one translation. <=3 edges cannot exhaust four choices. This gives exact pointwise feasibility for the retained relative colors, not for fixed absolute colors on both sides.

Quotienting by the span of removed edge columns induces the cycle quotient of the remaining graph up to invertible coordinates: Q_c(x) belongs to span of removed columns iff some extension on removed edges has zero Q_c, iff x is the restriction of a cut-space vector, precisely the cut space of the remaining graph. Hence actual factor independence follows across the split, rather than assuming decorrelation. Find all candidate cuts by enumerating <=3 edges and testing connectivity; choose a minimal disconnecting subset within a connected component, recursively split and archive. Discovery polynomial O(m^4(n+m)) loose; total archived graph and lifts polynomial. Decide graphs fully decomposed into tractable pieces, and otherwise use other certified terminals or UNKNOWN. Protected absolute assignments can obstruct the shift; the augmented rank test in A is safe, graph-only shortcut is not. No arbitrary overlapping quotas, no graph-to-unannotated-physical compiler.

Counts differ: number of permissible relative shifts is 4 minus number of distinct forbidden shifts, depends on retained tuple. BW06 balanced physical multiplicities do not make this count uniform. A simple three-cut with identical forbidden shifts admits three lifts, with all distinct shifts admits one. No exact count transfer or signed coverage assertion from saturation alone.

## E. Restricted-model barrier, same-geometry controls
Any simple 4-edge-connected graph has no zero columns, no pair dependency and no triple dependency in Q_c (nonempty cuts have size>=4). Tensor planes of any <=3 columns form a direct sum, so no candidate qualifies. Affine row closure also stagnates on every loopless signed graph whose edge planes have full affine hull: any individual edge lies in a cut, thus its plane is in the sum of the other planes (the cut indicator tensor either bit). Therefore all its rows pass the complement affine test. Infinite family K_n, n>=5, uniform forbidden shift 0 is UNSAT (cannot assign n distinct four-colors), uniform forbidden shift 1 is SAT (all colors equal). Same geometry, all factors rank two, no periods, same rank, no pair or ternary saturation, closure stagnates. This falsifies universal progress for THIS certificate repertoire, not all solvers, not a complexity separation. These examples are deliberately easy via alternative algorithms.
