## Main finding
For explicit tables on exactly the same ordered coordinate scope, replace them with their intersection and keep union of all original owner provenance. Truth-table replay checks equality; witness is unchanged on these coordinates. For two nonempty tables, row mass strictly decreases (at least the smaller table size), and factor count decreases by one. At fixed maximum width, discovery by dictionary and exact intersection polynomial in explicit representation; no new coordinates, and no branch. This is a certified local credit decrease on the existing chart, not a universal availability result.

Over the Boolean quotient ring GF(2)[x]/(x_i^2-x_i), the indicator of the unique forbidden word of a width-three non-tautological clause has degree three with top monomial the product of its three variables. A function vanishing on all satisfying words of that clause is supported on that one word, so no nonzero polynomial of degree <=2 vanishes. For a four-coordinate signed NAE4 factor, the two forbidden words are complementary. Their indicators have degree four, and the XOR has degree three (all four cubic monomials have coefficient one). Thus no nonzero degree<=2 annihilator exists individually. With two distinct NAE4 factors on identical scope, XOR of the two degree-three forbidden indicators cancels cubic terms and yields a nonzero degree<=2 polynomial valid on their common allowed words. The holes are disjoint, so this polynomial is exactly the forbidden indicator of their conjunction. For two width-three clauses on identical support, their degree-three terms likewise cancel; distinct forbidden words ensure a nonzero quadratic-or-linear relation. Such a relation need not yield a binary exclusion.

Pairwise binary-exclusion lemma: two distinct exact width-three clauses on three distinct variables each imply a non-tautological binary clause iff their supports coincide and their signs differ at precisely one coordinate, with the binary clause consisting of their two common literals. To see necessity, falsify the proposed binary clause by fixing its two distinct variables. No width-three source clause becomes empty. The two residual clauses can jointly be inconsistent only when both become opposite singleton literals on the same remaining variable. Thus their two other literals must be exactly the two falsified literals. Sufficiency is resolution. This does not claim the result for more than two clauses or arbitrary tables.

A fixed-degree local-polynomial lane can derive annihilator bases by linear nullspace of evaluations of all degree<=d monomials on explicit allowed words. At fixed d and explicit chart length polynomial in original N, dimension D=sum_{j<=d} binom(n,j) is polynomial. For degree2 one may also multiply every local affine identity by every variable, reducing squares, and take a common exact GF(2) row span. Every row combination is valid; a constant 1 is a contradiction and x_i+b is a forced bit under the same guard. Provenance vectors of polynomial length and independent word checks give polynomial acquisition and verification. The implemented bounded local coefficient enumeration is only justified for fixed small arity; a general nullspace algorithm is not implemented here. Rank credit cannot be reset under representation changes. There is no proof every unresolved chart has a useful identity or common-scope pair.
