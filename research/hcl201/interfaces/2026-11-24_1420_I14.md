## B. Exact linear compatibility trellis and width (PROVED)
Let m=2L be the number of scalar observation rows in the specified offset order, A the stacked homogeneous rows, r=rank A, d the free chart dimension after certified pins. Compute P with rows forming a basis of ker A^T; thus rank P=m-r and y in im A iff Py=0. Affine constants are used by the automaton, not accidentally mixed into homogeneous P. At cut i between two-bit observations let P_L,P_R be the column blocks, U_L=im P_L,U_R=im P_R. A partial syndrome p=P_L y_L is extendible to some full observation word iff p belongs to U_R. It then lies in U_L intersect U_R. The interface dimension
  w_i=rank P_L+rank P_R-rank P
     =rank A_L+rank A_R-rank A
     =dim(row A_L intersect row A_R).
Proof: the kernel of P_L is the subcode im A supported wholly on L, of dimension r-rank A_R (projection onto R has rank rank A_R). Thus rank P_L=|L|-r+rank A_R, and symmetrically on R. This establishes the displayed equality. All suffix bases and ranks are Gaussian-computable in polynomial time for the supplied order. No enumeration of individual full bundle support tables.

The trellis starts at syndrome 0; for symbol y_i in {0,1,2,3} update p by the corresponding two columns of P and reject if p is outside the suffix span. End at p=0. Multiply by the deterministic support automaton in A. At each layer at most 256*2^w_i live product keys (rejecting sink excluded). Sum counts when keys agree. This is exact because all prefixes with the same syndrome and mask pair have precisely the same allowed continuation language; the two components enforce different constraints and neither may be forgotten. Final accepted observation words count C; exterior chart count Z=C*2^(d-r) by constant kernel multiplicity. This proves
  T <= 2^w poly(N), S <= 2^w poly(N),
where w=max_i w_i, N includes all chart rows, offset/bundle manifests, reconstruction maps, bit lengths and guards. In particular deterministic polynomial for any supplied order with w=O(log N), not merely low total observation rank. The algorithm discovers w and verifies the order, not an optimum order. A fixed cap may return UNKNOWN. Different orders can have radically different width. The linear part is the standard code trellis principle; our scoped contribution is the exact product with I13 support determinization, the projected-count distinction, and the next closure augmentation. No novelty claim about classical trellises.

Restricted minimality: if p!=q are two extendible prefix syndromes, their admissible suffix sets for linear validity are disjoint and nonempty, since a suffix cannot have two distinct syndromes. Thus exact deterministic linear-validity summaries need at least 2^w_i distinguishable states at that cut before considering the nonlinear predicate. Nonlinear acceptance may merge or eliminate many of these states; this is NOT an unrestricted runtime or combined automaton state lower bound.
