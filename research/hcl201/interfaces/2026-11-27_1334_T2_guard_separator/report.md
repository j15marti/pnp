## Lemma: affine orbit section coverage

Let F be a finite Boolean constraint system, A a certified affine carrier containing its solutions, and u a certified translation symmetry of F and A. If a linear functional l satisfies l(u)=1, the hyperplane S={x in A:l(x)=0} intersects each two-element orbit {x,x+u} exactly once. If each of k affine guards G_i has a checked contradiction of F AND G_i, and their union covers S, then F is UNSAT. Proof: any model is either in S or translates into S, still a model, then lies in one refuted guard. For k<=5, inclusion-exclusion with at most 31 affine intersection counts in S checks coverage, using Gaussian elimination, integer counts of at most n+1 bits, fixed k. This is not proof of polynomial guard acquisition. A gauge representative restriction is equisatisfiability, not an unconditional forced literal. For explicit bounded-arity relations symmetry can be verified by checking local row invariance. Native strict E/B has u=all ones unconditionally: E equalities and XOR transport unchanged, B integer sum 2 becomes 4-2=2. Do not apply to general CNF, pinned asymmetric guards, or merely a relaxation without checking invariance.
