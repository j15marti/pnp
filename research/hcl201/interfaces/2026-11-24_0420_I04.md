## 1. Model and local theorem
Factor i has h_i distinct incident hub blocks (each two Boolean coordinates). For each of at most four local guards g, its exact attainable additive joint tag support is
  S_ig = a_ig + { D_ig 1_J : J subset E_ig, sum_{e in J} w_e <= K_ig }.
D_ig is an undirected graph incidence matrix over GF(2), with exactly the two parity vertices per hub (unused vertices may be isolated). Multiple labeled edges may be distinct coefficients. Weights and budgets are nonnegative binary integers. Uniform cardinality support w=1 is the M04 case when its source certificate is verified. Guard choice here is local; a shared guard must be explicitly transmitted, e.g. via a dedicated pair of isolated vertices whose offset is that guard, or an equality hub below. No omitted coupling, no uncharged source acquisition. I01 physical occupancy after splitting is allowed only when encoded in these disjoint paired coordinates with exact local and global consistency. Arbitrary extra protected linear features across groups are not silently discarded.

Given independent masks A_j subset GF(2)^2 at all h ports, seek J with cost <=K and pair parities in A_j (first subtract offset). This is deterministic polynomial even when every mask is nonaffine, the number of ports is unbounded, and dictionary nullity is large.

Proof: split each original graph edge e=uv into two incidence nodes i_eu, i_ev, joined by a cross edge of cost w_e; selecting the cross edge represents selecting e. For each original vertex v, make a zero-cost clique on its d_v incidence nodes plus port t_v. All incidence nodes and t_v must be matched exactly once in the assembled graph. If k_v incident cross edges are chosen, the port is matched inside its clique iff d_v-k_v is odd. Thus occupancy of t_v on the mask-gadget side is exactly 1+d_v+k_v mod 2. This is both necessary and sufficient: remaining incidence nodes form an even clique after the indicated port choice, so pair arbitrarily. Nonnegative weights are needed for the later affine hull formula; the matching construction itself is an exact weighted correspondence for any finite integer weights with a suitable absolute bound.

For a pair v,u, transform its allowed parity set A into allowed mask-side occupancy set B=A+(1+d_v,1+d_u) over GF(2). Attach one private third port, allowed to remain unmatched. Lift B to the even triples E={(b1,b2,b1+b2): b in B}. If B is empty reject. Pick any s in E, translate by s; E+s contains 000 and only even triples. A zero-cost triangle on three terminals with an edge for every allowed nonzero pair realizes exactly E+s: there is either no edge or exactly one edge in a matching. For every bit of s that equals 1, make its old terminal a required internal node and attach a fresh external port by one edge; requiring the old terminal to be matched complements the boundary occupancy. This shifts support back to E. Identify first two external terminals with t_v,t_u; third external terminal is optional. All other nodes required. Therefore the assembly realizes exactly the desired two-bit mask without enumerating masks across hubs. The optional third ports are private; no constraints between them. If a single-coordinate message is needed, pad its pair with an isolated parity vertex and take an appropriate mask.

Let L=1+sum_e w_e. Assign every assembled edge weight L times its number of required endpoints minus its original cost (zero for all gadget edges). Since a matching's total original cost lies between 0 and L-1, maximizing weight lexicographically maximizes the number of covered required vertices and then minimizes original cost. Reject if any required vertex remains uncovered or minimum cost>K. Otherwise read selected cross edges to recover J, verify budget and mask membership. Conversely every qualifying J extends to a matching saturating all required nodes by the clique and mask constructions. No assumption that the partial matching relation is affine. This uses ordinary maximum-weight matching, not a tagged matching oracle. Polynomial deterministic blossom is classical. Graph nodes O(m+h), edges O(sum_v(d_v+1)^2+h+m), weights have polynomial bit length. The argument also covers all sixteen masks, including empty by explicit rejection. For any independent pairwise bijective affine recoding, pull each mask back before building; do not treat a general global gauge as preserving this pair partition.

Boundary caution: arbitrary three-bit mask {000,111} cannot be realized by an ordinary matching gadget even if it has private optional terminals. In a realizing pair of matchings, the symmetric difference decomposes into alternating paths/cycles; choose a path with one of the three differing designated ports as endpoint. It changes at most two designated occupancies, at least one, preserving coverage of required internal vertices. That gives a forbidden intermediate support. This is a barrier for this arbitrary-mask gadget route, not for all algorithms; the pair theorem does not imply arbitrary high-dimensional masks.

## 2. Exact global forest algorithm and automatic discovery
Incidence graph A has factor nodes and hub nodes, edges for factor contributions to that hub. At each XOR hub j require xor of incident two-bit contributions = c_j. Optionally an equality hub requires all incident contributions equal and in a supplied mask (the proof changes only its convolution to intersection; this variant not tested).
For a rooted forest, each directed incidence message is the subset of four colors extendible in the corresponding subtree under that cut contribution. At a hub, combine child masks by repeated four-state XOR convolution, then translate by c_j. At a factor, fix parent color and use all child masks simultaneously in the local theorem, scanning at most four guards. Root factor needs no parent pin; isolated hub checks target. Induction: cutting an incidence edge disconnects the remaining choices, so child witnesses can be combined independently and the local matching returns compatible colors. Store one witness per feasible message value or backtrack using saved masks and recompute local matching. For each selected factor guard, reconstruct J, and if a physical M04 robust-Hall certificate supplied, use its partial transversal and slack completion to reconstruct source perfect matching and verify tags. This last physical lift was not executed here.

An upward pass is sufficient for decision and a witness; both directions (reroot or repeated runs) give all globally supported individual incidence colors by intersecting opposite messages. Guard-specific support queries are possible by fixing the local guard. Empty support is a certified qualitative contradiction, singleton a forced color, arbitrary masks nonaffine feedback to adjacent regions. Matching infeasibility is relative to the supplied masks and guard; only promote unconditional consequences when these assumptions are certified or all remaining guards exhausted. The exact four-color boundary relation of a cut is computed without listing joint assignments over the potentially huge degree of its factor node. This is stronger than requiring small local rank or small local port count in HCL-198.

For cyclic incidence graphs, automatically construct a spanning forest and take its non-tree edges (f of them, f=|E(A)|-|V(A)|+components). Fix the contribution on each such incidence to one of four values; remove the edge, retain its factor-side mask pin and adjust its hub XOR target. Equality hubs instead restrict their common color by the pin. Exact forest evaluation now applies to each residual; union of results is exact. Deterministic T<=4^f poly(N) with polynomial peak space by streaming the branches. For forest f=0, any degree/rank/nullity is permitted. This extension is proved, not executed. It is only an FPT terminal; no guarantee f=O(log N). Factor forests recognized by DFS, no decomposition oracle. Cyclic core selection based on other separator methods may be advantageous; do not infer a lower bound from f.
