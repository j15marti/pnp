Every port has one table and one parity incidence. Each table touches at most three checks. Source lifts and reverse readout are immediate. This is a reduction for the interface representation before further affine restructuring, not a lower bound on unrestricted SAT or a claim that every stronger closure fails.

A fixed-parity matching gadget with one external edge per port has even symmetric exchange: compare two partial internal matchings realizing port states; the symmetric difference consists of alternating paths/cycles. From any differing port follow its path to another differing port and switch the path, obtaining the two-bit changed feasible state. Parity completion of the actual BW22 imperfect ternary tables has supports {0,9,10,12,15} and {0,9,10,15}, little-endian pad bit 3. First fails exchange from 15 toward 0 at coordinate 3 (all allowed weight-two words retain that bit); second fails from 0 toward 15 at coordinate 2. E3 support {0,7} completes to {0,15}, also fails. Thus blindly padding every ternary owner and gluing with one port per bit cannot be universal. BW22's actual component map merges the three bits into ONE parity summary, escaping the obstruction.
