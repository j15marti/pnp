## Scoped mathematical refinement
Let D_F(G) be refutable guards in an explicit fixed finite guard registry G under UP of F. If each of a family of clauses C is individually RUP on F, then every guard directly falsifying any such clause is already in D_F(G). Proof: negating the clause gives a subset of the guard's literals, and unit conflict is monotone under adding compatible assumptions. Thus all new activation from a set of individually root-RUP clauses is indirect. For sequential RUP, new direct rows may occur because later clauses depend on prior admissions. No claim that any two newly indirect rows are independent.

Activation can have increasing marginal returns even with individually root-RUP seeds. Let F consist of (a OR u),(a OR NOT u),(b OR v),(b OR NOT v),(NOT a OR NOT b OR NOT g OR z),(NOT a OR NOT b OR NOT g OR NOT z), all six variables distinct. Seeds A=(a), B=(b) each RUP on F (assume the opposite, two opposing units). Guard g=1. UP with neither seed has no unit, with A alone yields a=1 but no conflict; with B alone analogously. With both seeds, z and NOT z are forced. Guard g=0 never conflicts under any seed subset, and F is SAT (a=b=1,g=0). On registry {g=0,g=1}, activation count satisfies f(empty)=f(A)=f(B)=0,f(A,B)=1, violating submodularity. This is a symbolic counterexample to greedy singleton activation as a universally adequate selector, not a new native benchmark, and early failed-literal closure would discover the two units. It does not falsify UCAC or all closure. Suggest pair seeds under paid fixed budget and independent replay; even pair completeness is OPEN. Fixed polynomial explicit registry admits polynomial row deletion closure, but a general all-input positive seed acquisition lemma remains the first OPEN arrow. Fixed root-coordinate rank credit is applicable only for correctly checked guarded lifts of bounded degree; these row exclusions not automatically independent rank gains or global credit in transformed charts.
