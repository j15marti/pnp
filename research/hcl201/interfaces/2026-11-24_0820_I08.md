## 1. Explicit defect-port model
Let G=(V,E) be a finite simple loopless graph (possibly a disjoint union of independently acquired matching regions). Partition V into mandatory interior R and pairwise disjoint optional port sets P_i, i=1..h. A source matching M must cover R. Define k_i=|P_i minus vertices covered by M|. Require L_i<=k_i<=U_i and, for each member (i,j) of a vertex-disjoint family of owner pairs, (k_i mod 2,k_j mod 2) in an arbitrary specified mask A_ij subset of GF(2)^2. There is no assumption on graph cycles, matching rank, number of regions, or local support size. Each port has exactly one owner. Equal per-owner binary-encoded costs d_i>0 and fixed offsets can be converted to count bounds by ceil/floor; general nonuniform port costs cannot. A fixed guard may restrict source edges, force occupancy, and set bounds and masks; retain its identifier in every conclusion. If acquisition of this representation or projection from L/L2 is not established, return UNKNOWN for that upstream step.

## 2. I08.1: Implicit exchange on maximum slices
Assume there exists a matching covering R, and let r be the maximum size of such a matching. All its defect sets have size |V|-2r and consist of ports. For any two such matchings M,N their symmetric difference is a disjoint union of alternating cycles and paths. No odd-length path can occur: flipping an odd path in the matching having fewer edges on the path augments that matching without uncovering any already covered vertex, contradicting maximal size subject to covering R. Each even path swaps an exposed endpoint of M with one exposed endpoint of N. Both are ports, since R is covered in both. For each endpoint exposed in M but not N, flipping its path exchanges precisely one defect for another. Thus these defect sets are bases of a matroid by the ordinary basis exchange axiom.

Moreover their owner-count vectors form an integer base-exchange family, without enumerating them. For any vectors x,y choose actual M,N. Regard each alternating path as a directed owner arc from its M-exposed endpoint to its N-exposed endpoint; net outflow at i is x_i-y_i. If x_i>y_i, the set reachable from i must contain a deficit owner j with x_j<y_j: otherwise the sum of net outflows over this closed reachable set is strictly positive, which is impossible. Choose a simple directed path i to j, and flip all corresponding vertex-disjoint original alternating paths simultaneously. All intermediary owner changes cancel, leaving x-e_i+e_j in the exact support. This establishes the exchange premise of I07 with an implicit physical graph certificate. Fixed mandatory coverage is respected throughout. A defect-set independence query can be implemented by requiring the queried ports exposed and checking whether the original maximum r is still achievable with R covered. No oracle access is free.

When only upper defect bounds are present, restriction to maximum matchings is safe for decision: repeatedly augment any feasible matching along an augmenting path, preserving covered vertices, so counts only decrease. When lower bounds or parity masks exist, maximum restriction is NOT safe. For one edge between two optional ports of different owners, the empty matching gives parity 11 while the unique maximum matching gives 00. Do not drop smaller matchings in that case. The next theorem handles the entire relation.

## 3. I08.2: Exact occupancy quota and tetrahedral parity-port gadget
Clip U_i to |P_i| and reject negative or inconsistent intervals. Discard from each pair mask colors inconsistent with the parity values in its intervals; reject empty masks. For each owner introduce U_i required slots S_i, U_i-L_i required filler vertices D_i, and one parity port p_i. Add all P_i-to-S_i edges, all S_i-to-D_i edges, the clique on D_i, and all D_i-to-p_i edges. Make all original vertices required in the enlarged graph; original edges remain. p_i is optional if unpaired, otherwise shared with the mask gadget as described below.

Any enlarged matching covering its required vertices uses a slot edge at precisely those original ports unmatched by the restriction to E. Thus k_i distinct slots are occupied by original ports; the other U_i-k_i slots must use distinct fillers. Consequently k_i<=U_i and U_i-k_i<=U_i-L_i, i.e. k_i>=L_i. The remaining k_i-L_i fillers must pair within their clique, except possibly one matched to p_i. Their parity forces
  a_i = 1[p_i matched on owner side] = (k_i-L_i) mod 2.
Conversely every specific source matching with L_i<=k_i<=U_i lifts: assign its uncovered ports injectively to slots, cover remaining slots by fillers, pair remaining fillers and use p_i exactly if their number is odd. This preserves each original matching, not just marginal count support.

For each owner pair choose s in A_ij, and translate the desired mask by XOR s so it contains 00. On two triangle terminals t_i,t_j and an optional private q, allow no edge (00), and include only the edges (t_j,q) for 01, (t_i,q) for 10 and (t_i,t_j) for 11 whose colors belong to A_ij XOR s. Triangle matching support is exactly the translated mask. The desired triangle-side occupancy bit for owner i is a_i XOR (L_i mod 2) XOR s_i. If the latter constant shift is 1, identify t_i with p_i and require it covered: its two sides have complementary occupancy. If shift is 0, keep t_i distinct, connect p_i-t_i and require both covered; the edge is selected precisely when neither side locally matches its terminal, giving equal side occupancies. Do the same for j. Since pairs are disjoint these gadgets never consume a parity port twice. Private q may be unmatched.

This gives both directions: every enlarged required-cover matching restricts to an original feasible matching; every original feasible matching extends. No enumeration of regional count vectors, no conditioning on all hub colors, no relaxation of nonaffine masks. The result is exact even for varying source matching cardinality. All 16 masks, including empty, are handled.

The graph has O(n+h) vertices and O(m+n^2+h) edges for n=|V|, m=|E|, since sum U_i<=n after clipping. Binary budget magnitudes do not control expansion. Maximize the sum of required-endpoint weights (0,1,2) over ordinary matchings. The maximum equals the number of required vertices iff feasible. Alternatively, reduce required cover to perfect matching by adding |O|+(|R'| mod 2) dummy vertices, where O,R' are optional and required vertices of the enlarged graph. Join every optional to every dummy and make dummies a clique. Every required-cover matching leaves at most |O| optional vertices unmatched; remaining dummy count is even after pairing them. Conversely restrict a perfect matching to the enlarged graph. Hence deterministic polynomial time by ordinary blossom; a Tutte deficient odd-component set in the final graph is a verifiable contradiction (certificate extraction not executed here).

## 4. Certified closure and exact composition
First enforce interval clipping, empty-mask rejection and any authenticated source pins, then invoke the exact terminal. For a SAT result, exact individual defect support is obtained by two queries per port: force defect by deleting its source incident edges; force nondefect by deleting its slot edges. Exact pair color support is obtained by intersecting its mask with each of four singletons and querying. Unsupported colors/values are unconditional under the current complete fixed guard, and may be conjoined back because all feasible witnesses already obey them. In the proof one can also pin source edges by deleting conflicting incident edges and blocking slot edges at their endpoints, or forbid the edge; owner min/max counts by binary search in bound changes. These latter probes not executed. Since all supported single values and colors are computed against the full instance, jointly inserting these consequences does not change the feasible set. Recompute on any external deduction or changed guard before attempting another terminal or branch. The executed checker applies interval/parity closure and exact port/color probes, not the entire cooperative source-level closure. No branching is needed within the terminal.

Nonaffine example (symbolic): a mandatory center with three optional leaves of owners 1,2,3 has exactly one matched leaf. Its parity pair (1,2) takes 01,10,11; its projected affine hull is all four colors, yet mask {00} rejects. The third owner's parity is unrestricted. The gadget preserves this nonlinear contradiction without a full local row list.

## 5. Cost and barriers

Construction, matching, source reconstruction and verification polynomial in this encoded length N. A polynomial number of exact probe calls remains polynomial; retain only witnesses and exclusions, reuse working memory. Critical falsifiers/limits: overlapping owner classes, arbitrary protected XOR labels on source matching edges, multiple overlapping hub pairs, and arbitrary multiowner cost per physical defect are not supported. A general compact matching quotient need not have this physical-port form. RT07 multi-tag hardness and DR02 acquisition barrier remain. General factor literature already establishes polynomial interval/parity-interval matching realizability; project-specific contribution is explicit parity export plus arbitrary disjoint paired masks, exact source lift, and bridge to I07 implicit exchange.
