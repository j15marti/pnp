## Lemma T2-E (exact separator response)
For a factor collection F, let F_v be all factors containing v, with union scope U, boundary B=U minus v. Replace F_v by R(b)=OR_{t in {0,1}} AND_{f in F_v} f((b,t)|scope(f)). Other factors contain no v, so this replacement is existentially exact for every external assignment and any existing guard included in F. Remove an inessential boundary coordinate only if its two cofactors are identical on all remaining assignments. Every row extends to the eliminated coordinate by one of the two trials; reverse order reconstructs a model with saved incident buckets and map. Equality BFS/DSU, local integer-balance support, duplicate factor removal and support pinning are sound. Reclose after every projection and at caps. No free semantic closure.

Conditional aggregate theorem: for any fixed c, if every remaining nonterminal state admits a discoverable complete bucket of raw boundary <= c log2(N+2) after the implemented closure, a deterministic scan of all variables finds one. Each table costs O(N^c) bits before scope/index overhead, each step removes one variable and does not add variables, at most O(N) eliminations, at most O(N) active factors (one replaced by at most one). Full original data, reverse maps, every candidate scan, table construction, verification, closure pinning, and witness recovery can be charged a fixed polynomial in original N. ## Lemma T2-F (exact Cartesian factorization)
For nonempty explicit Boolean response R on B, its finest disjoint Cartesian factorization is unique and can be acquired polynomially in the explicit row length, not necessarily native N. This is the known relational prime-factorization idea, specialized to boundary truth tables. Choose coordinate v, split rows into 0/1 slices. If one slice is empty, factor out the constant singleton and recurse. Otherwise recursively factor both slices after removing v; factor out each identical prime factor (same coordinate IDs and rows) present in both. The remaining coordinates, including v, form one prime factor obtained by projecting original rows onto them. Induction: every prime factor avoiding v appears unchanged in both slices, and a common slice factor is independent of v and all other coordinates. If the remainder split, some factor avoiding v would have been common, a contradiction. Base empty scope is unit. Every recursion depth partitions rows across nodes; depth <= |B|, so O(|B||R|) nonempty nodes, polynomial processing per node, polynomial storage. This is a direct constructive argument, not a claim of novel database mathematics. Check a proposed product by verifying each factor equals its projection, scopes partition B, and product of factor row counts equals |R| (inclusion plus cardinality). Bit length of count <= |B| when scopes partition. To check finest factorization replay recursive algorithm, not all partitions. For k>=2 the even-parity relation has full projection on every proper subset and 2^(k-1) rows, so no nontrivial disjoint Cartesian split and every coordinate is essential; yet a single affine equation and a two-state OBDD suffice. Thus even exact prime/essential width is not a decision lower bound or a barrier to overlapping/affine representations. A parity-chain 3-CNF and the pinned compiler realize such guarded responses with polynomial native input; no specific new registered instance claimed.
