## I22-RELATIVE-WIDTH (proved)
Take any simple 4-regular bipartite edge expander G with sides A,B of size k >= 12, edge expansion h>0 independent of k; infinitely many exist (Brito–Dumitriu–Harris, arXiv:1804.07808, random regular bipartite case). Decompose its edges into four perfect matchings by repeated bipartite matching. Let C be a third class of k vertices. In matching j choose a bijection assigning a C vertex to each A-B edge, avoiding previously used A-C and B-C pairs. Each row and column of this assignment problem forbids at most 2(j-1)<=6 entries (j=1..4). The allowed balanced bipartite graph has minimum degree >=k/2 and a perfect matching: any nonempty left set of size <=k/2 has enough neighbors, and any larger set meets the neighborhood of every right vertex. Hence a deterministic augmenting-path algorithm constructs the four triple partitions. Each vertex occurs once per partition, no pair repeats, all signs positive. Graph object count O(k); existence does not need a uniform expander search because the lower bound concerns all inputs, but the adapter from supplied G is uniform polynomial.

Every class-indicator word is a model. For each vertex v choose an indicator of a different class; both that word and its flip at v are models, since v is a majority bit in every incident triple. The differences contain every unit vector, certifying full affine hull of the exact joint SAT support using O(k) explicit witnesses and polynomial verification. Thus even complete global unconditional affine closure cannot add a nonzero affine equation. Complement all bits when necessary to normalize seed x0=b. The normalization's linear part is the projection x -> x+x0*1, so the pinned model hull is the entire seed hyperplane. These are paid witness certificates, not a semantic oracle. A verified class indicator is a trivial SAT bypass when the class dictionary is supplied; no universal dictionary-acquisition assumption.

For owner {a,b,c}, choose its relative observations e_a+e_b and e_a+e_c in the original dual coordinate space; the owner rejects precisely the zero relative word. For owner cut L|R let U_L,U_R be their summed row spaces, w=dim(U_L intersection U_R). Changes of basis inside owners or invertible global reparameterization do not affect w. Expand each owner leaf into a two-leaf fork. Internal owner cuts are unchanged; new single-row cuts have width <=1. Delete all e_a+e_c leaves and suppress degree-two nodes. Each cut of the restricted tree comes from a cut of the refined tree, and intersection dimension cannot increase on deletion. The surviving e_a+e_b columns represent the binary cycle matroid of G. Its conventional connectivity width is dim intersection +1. By Mazoit–Thomasse (Branchwidth of graphic matroids, 2007/2010, https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/surveys-in-combinatorics-2007/bandwidth-of-graphic-matroids/C9CA233149A3C3A818092BF277B25614), this equals graph branchwidth for bridgeless G. Each component of a 4-regular graph has no bridge by even degree; G is connected by expansion.

Any subcubic leaf decomposition has an edge splitting the 4k graph edges between one third and two thirds (centroid argument). Let Bnd be its shared endpoint vertices. Pure L and pure R endpoint sets each have size at least 2k/3 - |Bnd| by degree four. The smaller pure set has <=k vertices and all its outgoing edges enter Bnd. Expansion gives h(2k/3-|Bnd|)<=4|Bnd| (trivial if lower bound nonpositive). Therefore |Bnd|>=2hk/[3(4+h)]. Graph branchwidth is Omega(k), and every owner decomposition has a cut of relative width Omega(k), despite exact affine closure. This is a basis-invariant lower bound for the **specified raw owner subspace model**, not arbitrary equivalent rewriting, merging/solving owners, nonlinear summaries or SAT runtime.

For an actual consistent affine guard with row space H of rank r, restrict observations to the free chart, i.e. quotient dual row space by H. The image of U_L intersection U_R is contained in the intersection of the images, so w_guard >= w-r. This applies to every cut and thus every adaptive owner tree: logarithmic width requires total rank Omega(k), including any derived affine equations, if all original owners remain represented. It is not a bound on branch count or on finding one witness. For a single actual coordinate pin H=span(e_s), all relative rows have even weight and H intersects their total span trivially; quotient is injective on that span and every cut width is **exactly unchanged**. Global complement gauge fixing cannot remove the obstruction.
