## A. Mixed-group coset packet theorem (PROVED)
Let G=F2^q x product Z/(2^k_i), encoded in binary, equivalently a product of cyclic 2-groups (allow trivial factors). For exact integer quotas, first apply S15: shift target into leaf contributions; from certified absolute subtotal bound B_i choose modulus M_i>2B_i. Then zero residue is equivalent to exact zero for every legal tuple. For modular constraints no bound is needed. No claim that a compact residue distribution is an authenticated source distribution without this certificate. Let each independent region supply its exact nonnegative integer multiplicity function f:G->Z_nonnegative as a list of signed integer coefficients times coset indicators c 1_(a+H). A single region may have arbitrary mixed tag/quota correlations. The sign of an individual term is NOT its feasibility. Nonnegativity and interpretation as actual counts or positive support multiplicities require a paid source certificate. Integer weights can be binary encoded. Keep source, guard, owner, and consumer identities. For existential decisions positive multiplicities suffice, not necessarily Boolean counts.

For packets p=c 1_(a+H), q=d 1_(b+K), convolution is exactly
  p*q = cd |H intersect K| 1_(a+b+H+K).
Proof: at z outside a+b+H+K no pair exists. At any z inside, the set of pairs summing to z is a translate of {(h,-h):h in H intersect K}, of constant cardinality. Distribute bilinearly over signed packet lists. Pointwise restriction to a coset is either zero or one coset indicator with coefficient unchanged, because nonempty intersection of cosets is a coset of the intersection subgroup. A complement restriction equals the original minus the positive restriction; charge resulting terms. Canonicalize subgroups and offsets, merge exact keys, remove zero coefficients. This is not semantic minimization. Order-two phase packets can be converted to at most two unsigned-indicator terms with signed coefficients: chi on H equals 2*1_kernel(chi)-1_H, with the phase relative to the coset representative. No complex arithmetic needed.

Polynomial bit operations per packet: let D=diag(M_i), lattice L_H=D Z^r + span_Z of supplied subgroup generators. HNF gives canonical full-rank basis of L_H. Coset representative is its canonical remainder, sum subgroup from concatenated lattice generators, intersection and nonempty coset witness by integer linear Diophantine solve using Smith form with multipliers. For instance solve L_H u - L_K v=b-a; any solution gives intersection point a+L_H u; integer kernel images generate L_H intersect L_K. Cardinality |H|=det(D)/det(HNF(L_H)). Alternatively |H intersect K|=|H||K|/|H+K|. All matrices have polynomial dimensions; canonical lattice pivots and representatives bounded in bit length by log det D, and polynomial-bit algorithms exist (Kannan-Bachem). For phases use kernel subgroup or two-term expansion. No enumeration of residues or characters. General skew-subgroup lattice implementation is symbolic, not executed.

For a supplied tree of independent regions, convolve each child list then optionally restrict to the safe boundary subgroup H_J intersect H_out, where H_J is any verified subgroup containing all possible shifted subtotals on side J. ## B. Exact forced support, reconstruction and cost (PROVED)
For given parent subtotal t, child functions f,g, and coset guard A on left subtotal, define
  m(A)=sum_(x in A) f(x)g(t-x).
For packet pair, intersect a+H, t-b+K, A. Multiply its cardinality by cd. Sum over all pairs. Exact integer mass, nonnegative for actual tables; zero excludes all legal pairs in that guard. If m(A)=0 or equals m(G), the complement or A respectively is forced within the current target/guard. The same formula handles leaf observations in any fixed context by using the exact outside table. Recompute closure after every new deduction and at stagnation before branching. A finite registered set of coset predicates can be closed with polynomially many passes relative to the packet cap; not complete semantic or all nonlinear closure. No local pin exported to a relaxed context. Retain guard records and original IDs. Do not infer universal equality from a few moments. Full packet-function identity on the same group is decidable by expanding the squared difference and summing pairwise coset intersection cardinalities; zero iff equal, but consumer and decoder equivalence is separate.

For a positive node target, find an actual x by fixing low-to-high bits of each cyclic coordinate. Each partially fixed low-bit prefix is a coset; its two next-bit children partition it. Compute exact masses and record forced bits when one mass zero. Restart registered closure after a deduction; when both positive, select one as a CONDITIONAL witness guard after closure. At most sum k_i bits per node. At completion f(x)>0 and g(t-x)>0, recurse with these actual targets using saved child lists. No search over exponentially many candidate subtotals. A local source decoder still must be supplied and charged; the theorem reconstructs actual region subtotal tuples, not arbitrary unannotated wheel assignments. Conditional state changes use their guards and cannot rewrite root tables for other consumers. ## C. Antipodal representation obstruction (PROVED)
Let G=Z/(2^k), k>=2, h=2^(k-1). Define Delta f(x)=f(x)-f(x+h), for 0<=x<h. Every nontrivial subgroup of G contains h. Its coset indicator is therefore invariant under translation by h, so contributes zero to Delta. A singleton packet affects at most one antipodal pair. Hence ANY signed coset-indicator representation requires at least the number of nonzero entries of Delta, even allowing overlap and negative coefficients. In particular the ordinary half-interval indicator 1_[0,h-1] requires >=h=2^(k-1) packets. Singleton representation attains equality. Allowing order-two phases does not improve this bound: a character on a cyclic subgroup of order >=4 is trivial at its order-two element; order-two subgroups only affect one pair. For mixed groups, push forward by summing over the other coordinates; each unsigned coset packet projects to one coset packet with constant fiber multiplicity, so the same lower bound applies whenever the projected marginal has this shape. For a nonnegative integer support contained in a half-open interval of length h, Delta has one nonzero entry per occupied value. Thus signed cosets cannot automatically compress exact integer quota ranges, even under the safe-modulus promise.

Explicit safe exact-quota family: independent binary choices of weights 1,2,...,2^(k-2) produce uniform attainable subtotals [0,h-1]. With target 0 and bound B=h-1, modulus 2^k>2B is safe. Its unpruned region subtotal function needs h packets despite O(k^2) input bits and a trivial zero-target decision (all choices zero); an alternate grouping or interval/generating-function terminal can escape. This is a restricted representation lower bound, not a runtime lower bound, not hardness of arbitrary strict E4/B4. In a target-pruned tree the table may be much smaller. Do not confuse abstract modular dense coset scaling with authenticated large integer interval compression.
