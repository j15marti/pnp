## 2. Exact half-integral anchor (PROVED)
Shift lower bounds to zero. Create row copies i+ and i-, and one reservoir R. Demand at i+ is shifted b_i, at i- its negative, and R demand zero; incoming minus outgoing convention. A column with occurrences (i,s),(j,t) produces two directed arcs from j(-t) to i(s), and from i(-s) to j(t). Each has the shifted upper capacity and cost w_j. A singleton (i,s) produces R->i(s) and i(-s)->R. Repeated same-row same-sign occurrences give two parallel arcs. Normalize canceled occurrences to zero columns, which are independent and can be optimized at an endpoint.

A real feasible x gives a symmetric cover flow assigning its shifted coordinate to both corresponding arcs, satisfying all demands. Conversely any cover flow f projects to x=l+(f_a+f_a')/2: subtracting the minus-row balance from the plus-row balance shows Bx=b; the two capacities imply the original bounds. Costs are doubled up to the known lower-bound constant. Thus an integral optimal cover flow gives an exact half-integral optimal real point a. The cover is a standard integral capacitated min-cost flow on 2r+1 nodes and <=2m indexed arcs; use a polynomial binary-input algorithm (e.g. Orlin, 1993). Negative costs and repeated arcs are legitimate. An infeasible cover certifies infeasible real system and hence UNSAT. A feasible cover alone does NOT certify integer feasibility.

## 3. Conformal integer cycle generators (PROVED)
For any real d in ker B, lift d symmetrically onto both arcs of every nonzero column; the lift is a signed circulation by the row-balance identity. Reverse arcs carrying negative differences and take absolute values. This nonnegative directed circulation decomposes by repeatedly subtracting the minimum residual value on a directed simple cycle. Each step removes at least one nonzero arc, so at most 2m cycles, including the independent zero-column directions counted separately (at most 2*m_nonzero+m_zero).
For a cycle take signed SUM (not average) of its uses of the two original indexed arcs per variable. Its projection g is integral, Bg=0 (subtract the two row balance equations), |g_j|<=2, and it is conformal to d (coordinate signs agree or vanish). Each cycle contributes its magnitude divided by 2 to the projected real difference. Zero-column directions have norm 1. Hence d=sum_{k<=2m} lambda_k g_k with lambda_k>=0, no coordinate cancellation and |g_k|_infty<=2. This is an existential comparison, not a procedure requiring an unknown integer solution.

## 4. Residue-stable weighted clipping (PROVED)
Let a be the optimal real anchor. For any attainable coordinate residue class p mod Q (positive integer Q), choose an integer optimum x for objective w in that class, minimizing ||x-a||_1 among optima. Compact finite bounds ensure existence. Decompose d=x-a as above. If lambda_k>=Q then x-Q*g_k is integer, stays coordinatewise between a and x by conformality, has Bx=b and same full residue vector. Since a+epsilon*g_k is feasible for sufficiently small positive epsilon, w*g_k>=0 by real optimality. Therefore subtracting Q*g_k either strictly improves the integer objective or preserves it and strictly decreases distance, both contradictions. So all lambda<Q and
  ||x-a||_infty < 4m Q  (nonzero m).
Intersect the original bounds with [floor(a_j)-4mQ, ceil(a_j)+4mQ] per coordinate. The box contains one optimum of EVERY attainable residue class, simultaneously for the same anchor. Reverse containment trivial because it is an actual subbox. No enumeration of classes required to acquire it.

Q=1 preserves feasibility and untagged weighted optimum. Q=2 gives radius 8m and retains the COMPLETE attainable coordinate parity support, hence all joint linear tags Gp, and the optimum of every such syndrome fiber (take the best residue class in that fiber). Constant Q gives polynomial-size unary expansion; arbitrary binary-large Q does not. The theorem concerns a single specified objective; new objectives may need new anchors. The original integer domain is NOT equal to the clipped domain: x+y=1000 and objective x yields anchor (0,1000), yet (1000,0) is feasible outside the clip. No unconditional integer pins from clipping. All arbitrary parity guards are preserved existentially, not with multiplicity; arbitrary integer-value pins can require rebuilding the anchor. With w=0, any feasible half-integral anchor suffices.

## 5. Exact matching and tags (PROVED)
Shift clipped lower endpoints L, expand each variable into U-L unit switches having its original <=2 signed occurrences. Total copies O(m^2 Q); row demands after shift are bounded by the remaining occurrences on any feasible system; if quotas are outside range reject. Use S12 slots/ports: positive activation is bit, negative activation complement; same sign two ports have internal edge; opposite sign share mandatory middle; singleton attaches optional leaf, all optional leaves clique plus dummy if needed. Zero-column unit uses an isolated 4-cycle with two perfect matchings, one distinguished edge signaling bit=1. Every feasible integer x in clip admits a unit decomposition and matching, and every matching lifts to actual x. Label slot edges from one designated port of each switch with the column's binary tag, other edges zero. If designated sign negative, XOR a constant tag offset for the switch (activation complement). Add lower-bound parity offset. Linear objective similarly weights designated slot edges with sign-adjusted cost and adds constant; 4-cycle distinguished edge carries its tag and cost. This is exact on syndrome support and optimum value per fiber, not count preserving. For Q=2 graph has O(m^2) vertices and O(m^4) edges, indices and bit lengths polynomial in N. Weighted matching without tags solves the untagged optimum, including coordinate extrema, deterministically. For UNSAT, ordinary matching deficiency/Tutte witness or cover infeasibility cut suffices, with the proof of clipping and checked anchor optimality; standard weighted matching dual and min-cost residual potentials certify objectives. Weighted-plus-parity optimum is transferred to weighted tagged matching, NOT asserted polynomial here.

## 6. Complete unconditional affine parity closure and a one-bit terminal (PROVED transfer, not executed with polynomial basis backend)
El Maalouly, Steiner, Wulf, arXiv:2207.09797v2 (2023), section 4 Theorems 19-20, explicitly provide deterministic polynomial correct-parity perfect matching and Lovasz finite-field linear span of perfect matchings. Checked primary PDF pp. 14-16. Import only ordinary correct parity, no bounded correct parity or multi-tag claim. [Primary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.09797).
Apply to the Q=2 compressed graph with w=0. Append a disjoint mandatory edge so that its incidence coordinate is 1 for every perfect matching. Compute the linear span over F2 of these perfect matching incidence vectors using Lovasz's theorem. Map each vector linearly to (constant-edge coordinate, original coordinate parity) using the unit-tag construction, including affine offset times the constant-edge coordinate. The image is exactly the span of (1,p) over attainable original p; its slice at first coordinate 1 is precisely aff{p}. Empty matching domain handled first. Thus all unconditional parity identities of the original UNTAGGED signed system can be extracted in polynomial time (one span call plus Gaussian elimination). No arbitrary SAT counting or randomized Pfaffian needed. One arbitrary additional global binary observation is an ordinary correct-parity query (and a witness via edge-deletion self-reduction). Known full coordinate residue assignments p can also be imposed directly by x=2y+p, divisibility of b-Bp, and shifted integer bounds; same column norm. On a domain already restricted by arbitrary parity equations, complete conditioned affine hull is NOT obtained from the unconditioned span by intersecting affine spaces. Such a probe may require simultaneous parity tags, remain guarded, and must use an eligible exact backend or UNKNOWN. Counterexample x1+x2+x3=1, 0<=x<=1, tags 00,01,10: support misses 11 but its affine hull is full.
Complete integer-coordinate forced closure on untagged system: run min/max original-box optimization for every coordinate (2m queries), never derive pins from a single clipped box. One can similarly recover full rational affine hull by orthogonal linear probes and extrema: either find a new witnessed direction or a new identity, <=m independent probes. Re-run eligible closure first, after each actual deduction and at stagnation before any external branching. Closure outputs are already consequences, so a full domain-preserving closure need not recompute unchanged identities after every identity insertion; if domain/guard changes, rerun under that guard. In arbitrary parity-guarded jobs, sound closure on a superset is valid but incomplete; retain guard and use exact one-tag or fixed-coordinate residue queries when eligible. No claim of universal forced-information closure on unrecognized residuals.
