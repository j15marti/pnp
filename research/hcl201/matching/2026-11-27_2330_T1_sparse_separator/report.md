## Scoped exact response theorem
Assume disjoint signed-literal demand rows, a partition into certified exclusive bins, at most one literal from each demand in each bin (not needed for the negative direction), and a boundary assignment disabling a known subset of bins. A Hall-deficient subset certifies rejection of that boundary row. If the entire interior consists only of positive disjoint demands, within-bin negative-pair clauses and the selector-to-bin blockers, every saturating matching gives an interior witness by choosing one literal per demand and setting other interior cells false. Thus the matching response is exact for that explicitly recognized class. For other constraints it is only an outer relaxation. For b boundary bits enumerate 2^b assignments, compute matching, and intersect with an exact independently checked consumer table. A zero intersection proves UNSAT; a positive intersection proves SAT only with compatible lifts. Per query matching and checking are polynomial; if b <= fixed c log N, full table acquisition is polynomial (including all queries), provided consumer response, recognition and maps also have fixed polynomial cost. This does not prove such boundaries exist or are acquirable in arbitrary networks. For b=3 there are eight entries. A guard clause exported from one gauge needs the corresponding guarded clause and its complemented mate in the unpinned chart, not unguarded identification of both rows. Rank and operational credits are different; no new global rank bound is proved.
