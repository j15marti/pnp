## 2. Exact class-parity reduction (PROVED)
For every explicitly encoded directed multigraph with integral bounds, signed incidence, net demands and additive GF(2)^q tags, choose a set of parallel/antiparallel indexed arcs between two vertices. Treat their required signed net as h, with an authenticated interval [hlo,hhi] if external flow varies. All nonlocal owner, occupancy and guard constraints must be compiled or retained. This reduction assumes no other unrepresented constraints inside the chosen bundle. For a standalone bundle, if signed arc intervals are [l_e,u_e], L=sum l_e, U=sum u_e, reject a fixed h outside [L,U]. For an interval contained in [L,U], exact individual signed-coordinate extrema over all allowed integer h are
  l'_e=max(l_e,hlo-(U-u_e)), u'_e=min(u_e,hhi-(L-l_e)).
Each such endpoint is attainable by independent integer interval filling; all tightenings preserve the domain, so rerunning range closure gives no further changes. For external networks obtain ordinary feasibility and safe net extrema by min/max-cost integral flow before grouping; a standalone interval can also be an outer interval. No arbitrary protected constraint is discharged by ordinary closure. Revalidate after actual pins/guards; guard conditional facts, reapply all applicable closures at stagnation. Full cooperative closure is not executed.

Convert signs (negative orientation does not change parity), shift each tightened signed lower bound, offset both net and tag. Group by identical full binary tags g_i, i=1..k; remove zero-width groups, retaining original IDs and offsets. Width w_i=sum individual shifted widths >0, total W=sum w_i; required shifted net H=h-L'. The class sum y_i can attain EVERY integer in [0,w_i], by greedy filling. Its tag is (y_i mod2)g_i. Let p_i=y_i mod2, omega_i=w_i mod2. For each p, possible class sums are p_i,p_i+2,...,w_i-(p_i XOR omega_i). Consequently, for H integral, EXACT feasibility of p is equivalent to
  Gp=t, sum p_i=H mod2 (GF2),
  wt(p)<=H, wt(p XOR omega)<=W-H.
Here t is target after lower-bound offset. Necessity immediate; sufficiency fill the remaining even net by integer pairs until H, then greedily distribute each class sum to original arcs. Restore original orientations and verify. No capacity expansion into unary copies; all arithmetic uses binary encoded values. This is an exact physical-flow witness, not a strict tetravalent lift absent a source compiler.

## 3. Exact thin terminal by enumerating the complement (PROVED)
Let A_t(H) be the affine solution set of the augmented matrix [G;1] with RHS [t;H mod2]. If consistent, size M=2^nu where nu=k-rank([G;1]). Let F(H) be the union of the two violating Hamming balls: center 1^k and radius k-H-1; center 1^k XOR omega and radius k-(W-H)-1. Negative radius means empty. Filter to required parity; deduplicate. Define d=max(0,k-H,k-(W-H)). For d<=D fixed,
  |F(H)| <= B_D=2 sum_{j=0}^{D-1} binom(k,j).
Exact target decision: compute f=|F(H) intersect A_t(H)| by scanning this list and evaluating the full augmented equations. The target is feasible iff M>f (or reject inconsistent A). If feasible, enumerate at most f+1 distinct affine solutions using a Gaussian anchor/kernel basis; at least one lies outside the list. Lift it as in section 2. This is polynomial bit time and space for each fixed D, independent of both rank and nullity. It is an XP bound in d, not an FPT claim. Outside the chosen cap decline or apply section 5; do not claim UNSAT.

Exact target-conditioned class affine hull: if M>2f, every proper affine subspace has at most M/2 points, so A minus F has full affine hull A. Otherwise M<=2f, enumerate the whole polynomial-size A, retain feasible points, compute their difference span. This yields all class-parity identities, not all original individual arc identities. New affine observations on the SAME class parity variables are just additional rows in A; the same F remains valid, so arbitrary simultaneous class parity observations preserve this terminal. Use one-bit probes if actual witnessed directions are needed; synthetic kernel vectors need not themselves be feasible. Appending an arbitrary ORIGINAL arc observation can split classes, increase k and break eligibility. Reaggregate and recertify or keep the guard and decline. The I09 physical pullback is a separate obligation.

## 4. Exact uniform saturation and quotient (PROVED)
For fixed H, the full linear tag image on the parity hyperplane is
  (H mod2)g_0 + W_tag, W_tag=span{g_i+g_0},
for any reference class g_0 (if k=0, only H=0 and zero shifted tag). Every target in this affine image has exactly M=2^nu class words. Bucket F(H) by their Gp; saturation iff no bucket reaches M. This is necessary and sufficient for the supplied bundle and fixed net, not merely an inner coset. In the interval [Hlo,Hhi], all H in [k,W-k] have empty F, and outside that band there are at most 2D integers when d<=D uniformly. Test just these boundary nets; if all pass, the exact tag fiber is the stated affine image for EVERY allowed net. No enumeration of the exponentially large binary encoded interval. Failed saturation does NOT imply UNSAT; preserve missing targets with the exact thin terminal where applicable.

For an accepted interval replace bundle by one aggregate arc of shifted net H, with bounds [Hlo,Hhi] and label g_0. Adjust vertex demand by lower-bound signed net L'; add constant original tag offset. Project global labels/target modulo W_tag. A quotient witness specifies H and a required tag in the certified coset; use section 3 or 5 to reconstruct the actual original arc tuple. Keep all old labels, IDs, basis, offsets, and protected constraints; reverse contractions in order. Different accepted bundle contractions may be composed sequentially with paid verification and reconstruction. No joint terminal for arbitrary MANY nonsaturated bundles claimed; independent forbidden lists can produce a difficult global constraint. Fixed guards or source pins require revalidation.

## 5. Stronger count-dominance certificate: no enumeration even for linear d (PROVED)
Define B(H) as the SUM of the sizes of the two forbidden balls restricted to parity H (overlap may be double-counted). Unlike f, these are ambient counts ignoring the tag equations. Each count is a binomial tail with a parity filter; compute using Pascal's triangle, O(k^2) arithmetic on O(k)-bit integers. If M>B(H), every consistent tag coset has at least one actual feasible point. This gives a sufficient full-saturation certificate for arbitrary d, including d=Theta(k); no list enumeration. If M>2B(H), the full affine hull also equals A for every consistent target.

**Deterministic witness construction without enumerating B or M.** For a fixed prefix of p, let a be its Hamming weight and b its weight after XOR with the corresponding prefix of omega. For the remaining n coordinates compute exact size M_prefix of the affine solution set by Gaussian elimination, after subtracting the fixed prefix contribution. Compute B_prefix as the sum of the binomial tails with inequalities a+j>H and b+j>W-H and respective total parity constraints H mod2 and (H mod2) XOR wt(omega) mod2. These counts are additive under splitting the next bit (even with double-counting). The affine count is additive as well. If M_prefix>B_prefix, at least one of the two child prefixes has the same strict inequality. Choose that child by two explicit Gaussian/count computations. At depth k, count is 1 and B_prefix=0, yielding a genuine feasible word, lifted by section 2. This is conditional-expectation-style deterministic witness extraction from a counting inequality, not a counting oracle: the two easily counted objects are the affine coset and ambient Hamming balls. No general #SAT computation is used. This search retains the whole original target and guard; no speculative exponential branching. At most 2k+1 Gaussian calls plus binomial computations. If inequality fails, return UNKNOWN (except affine inconsistency gives UNSAT); use section 3 if eligible.

Uniform interval certificate: separately for parity eps in {0,1}, take smallest Hmin_eps and largest Hmax_eps in the interval with that parity (skip absent parity). Lower violation count is maximal at Hmin_eps and upper at Hmax_eps. Sum these two counts, using the appropriate parity filter. If M exceeds this sum for each present parity, saturation holds at every H in interval. This is sufficient, not necessary. It is polynomial even when both interval length and forbidden list cardinality are exponential. Append arbitrary class-level parity rows and recompute M and counts; margin may be lost. If M>2B, actual witness probes for any nonconstant single row retain M/2>B and can be used for GD10-style witnessed basis acquisition. If neither strong hull certificate nor bounded-complement exact hull applies, full hull is OPEN, not silently asserted. The fixed original tag domain remains guarded through every probe.

## 6. Families and hardness boundary (PROVED)
A. Let k>=8, two unit parallel arcs per distinct class, w_i=2, H=k-2. Exactly one parity-compatible word violates capacity: 1^k. If g_i are independent, that word has a unique tag and is UNSAT; toggle two bits gives SAT. Full affine image is not support. If instead g_i are 0,e1,e2,e1+e2 and k-4 further independent labels, augmented nullity is 1, every tag coset contains 2 words, only one forbidden word total. Full syndrome saturation holds although the parity support is not affine. Its dimension is k-2. Original arcs all unit, so M08/M09 interior certificates fail for this entire bundle even after grouping (interior class values all 1 require H=k). S09's single common-tag increasing-switch scheme has recourse width 2, insufficient for rank k-2>=6; its full-bundle certificate declines. This does not exclude partial contractions or other solver paths.

B. Let k be a power of two >=32, r=k/16, take k-r distinct labels from log2(k) base coordinates (include zero) and r independent new coordinates. Two unit arcs per label, H=3k/4, W=2k, d=k/4. Augmented rank <=log2(k)+r+1 and >=r, so both rank and nullity grow linearly in k. The upper forbidden ball is empty; the lower ambient count is bounded by sum_{j<=k/4} binom(k,j). For x=1/3, this is <= (1+x)^k x^(-k/4) = ((4/3)*3^(1/4))^k, whose base is strictly smaller than 2^(15/16). Since M>=2^(15k/16-log2(k)-1), the strict count certificate holds for all sufficiently large k, without enumerating an exponentially large ball or coset. Every affine-image target is feasible, other targets reject by Gaussian elimination. The family is explicitly generated and has no claimed unrestricted hardness. S09 full-bundle common-tag width 2 is insufficient as rank grows.

C. General tagged single-bundle decision is NP-complete, so the hypotheses cannot be replaced by a universal claim without a major breakthrough. Reduce binary syndrome decoding (find p with Ap=t, wt(p)<=K) by one unit arc per input column plus a zero-tag slack arc of capacity K; required net K. Every feasible join gives a decoding witness and conversely fill slack by K-wt(p). For K<=number of columns, replace slack by K unit arcs to keep all arcs unit and polynomial encoded length. General decoding NP-completeness is established by Berlekamp, McEliece, van Tilborg (1978); see https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/aw9vs-ann16 and https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19780054314. Membership in NP via binary flow witness. This is hardness of the explicit tagged-flow domain, NOT a strict physical source reduction or P!=NP proof. Arbitrary tags cannot be discarded or assumed to arise from a physical tetravalent network without authentication.
