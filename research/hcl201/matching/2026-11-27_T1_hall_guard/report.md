## Infinite obstruction and cooperative escape
For every odd k>=3 take three parts each of size 2k, k vertices of each label 0,1. Include exactly triples of even XOR of labels. Every perfect 3-matching selects all vertices, whose total label parity is 3k mod 2=1, but every selected triple has parity 0. Contradiction by XOR sum of the exact-one vertex equations over odd-labeled vertices. Thus UNSAT. Every pair projection is complete: for each pair there is at least one vertex of required label in the third part. Even after fixing a disjoint positive guard of at most d triples, if k>d then both labels remain in all three residual parts; every pair graph remains complete on remaining vertices, and pinned triples isolated. Every projected edge belongs to a perfect matching. Therefore bounded-depth positive guard Hall closure cannot delete any unpinned triple or reject these UNSAT instances (for fixed d choose k>d).
