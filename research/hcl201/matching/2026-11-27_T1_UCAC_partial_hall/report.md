## New theorem: binary-free matching activation obstruction
Suppose F is a non-tautological CNF with no explicit binary conflict clauses (e.g. all clauses width>=3), and H has consistent unit-propagation closure without contradiction. In this specified direct-conflict resource language, the only possible nontrivial cliques are opposite rails of a single variable. For ANY selection of literal-disjoint demand rows, choose one true literal per already satisfied demand; all chosen variables are distinct (a Boolean variable has only one true sign and demand rows are literal-disjoint). The remaining unsatisfied demand rows each have at least two distinct free literals by closure; each free variable occurs at most twice in the entire literal-disjoint collection. For every subset S of remaining demands, the neighbor variable set has >=|S| members (count >=2|S| distinct literal occurrences, at most two per variable). Hall gives a matching, independent of the particular packing order. Assigned and free variable resources are disjoint. This lifts to any disjoint collection of opposite-pair/singleton resources plus private literal dummies. Thus no direct-conflict partial-Hall deficiency is possible after consistent UP, even if ALL possible demand packings are searched. A nontrivial derived binary conflict or richer capacity proof can be an activation prerequisite. This is a negative theorem about a named representation, not an unrestricted lower bound. If guard-reduced binary rows are added, or arbitrary entailed pair conflicts are allowed, the hypothesis need not hold. No universal simultaneous stall asserted.
