## 1. Exact deletion-component factorization
Input: explicitly acquired bipartite unit-demand shell H=(L,R,E), with a matching M saturating L (otherwise a checked Hall cut refutes the shell). Orient resource arc v->w when v is matched to demand i in M and i is adjacent to w. Let U be unmatched resources, T(v) the reachable free resources including the length-zero path. Partition U by the equivalence relation generated by all co-occurrences in T(v). Each v with nonempty T(v) belongs to the component of T(v); let L0 be the resources with empty T. These are indispensable. For each component G retain its resource set R_G and the demands matched by M to R_G; keep their incident edges within R_G.

Claim: deletion set D is feasible iff D misses L0 and each restricted component admits a matching avoiding D. Proof: along any arc v->w, T(w) is contained in T(v). Thus if w is nonloop, v is nonloop in the same group. Loop-matched demands have only loop neighbors, all loops are originally matched, and these demands exhaust the loop resources in any saturating matching. No nonloop demand can use a loop resource in such a matching. Its other neighbors are in the same group. Restrict any global witness to its groups. Conversely unite independent group matchings and original M on loop demands. This proves exact equivalence and witness reconstruction, including overlapping external selectors only when the same deletion word is used consistently across all groups. Do not treat external selectors as independent.

Each group has slack k_G=|U intersect R_G|. Rank-one groups give the earlier at-most-one class. More generally for fixed K, if every k_G<=K, enumerate all subsets of size <=k_G and check matching; total <=sum_G sum_{j=0}^{k_G} binomial(|R_G|,j) polynomial for fixed K. Store exact feasible deletion options and a matching witness per option; preserve additional observations jointly or refuse compression. Global slack may be linear. This is a standard-style decomposition of deletion independence in the dual transversal matroid, not a claimed novel general matroid theorem. Polynomial construction: one augmenting-path matching, |U| reverse graph searches, unions and checked edge partition; each local query is a matching computation. A proposed arbitrary sequence of externally changed deletions can still cycle. Bound repair only under a fixed polynomial schedule or the monotone option-deletion protocol below; no universal global extension oracle.

## 2. Pair-support-to-affine terminal (fixed explicit syndrome chart)
Let S_i be nonempty explicit finite sets of binary syndrome vectors, with a local witness for each vector, and exact feasibility iff sum_i s_i=b over F2, s_i in S_i. No hidden nonlinear coupling outside these exact dictionaries. For distinct i,j, choose anchors a_k in S_k for exterior k; define a=sum_{k not i,j}a_k and V=span{v+a_k: v in S_k,k not i,j}. A necessary pair condition is b+s_i+s_j+a in V. Compute exact pair candidates within this relaxation by enumerating the explicitly listed local options. Remove an option only if it has no surviving partner. A zero pair support refutes the chart. The check is necessary, not in general sufficient for a globally extendible pair. For private coordinates owned only by i,j, exact equality to b on those coordinates is an even simpler necessary test. A full fused dictionary must preserve complete pair sums and witness choices, not only marginal supports.

Proof of safe filtering: every exact global solution has an exterior sum in a+V and hence preserves both of its local options. Conversely, surviving options were already in the original dictionaries, so satisfaction of the exact global sum after pruning is sufficient. On any FIXED cut P|Q, centered span U_P=sum_{i in P}span(S_i+a_i) shrinks under deletion (choice of anchor within each S_i does not change its difference space), similarly U_Q. Therefore lambda(P)=dim(U_P intersect U_Q) is nonincreasing. Strict shrinkage of local option count does not automatically imply strict rank loss. If all remaining dictionaries are affine cosets (check cardinality=2^rank and membership, exact distinct options), solve the global sum by tagged GE; lookup local witnesses and reconstruct. This is a uniform exact terminal even with large residual cut rank. For arbitrary nonaffine surviving dictionaries the algorithm may stall. At least one drop reduces Phi=sum_i |S_i| by one. On a fixed explicit chart of L encoded bits and d-bit vectors, repeated complete pair scans with deterministic order take polynomial work: at most Phi_initial successful sweeps plus one final unchanged sweep; each of O(n^2) pairs constructs an exterior basis with O(Phi_initial d^2) bit operations, and tests at most |S_i||S_j| candidates at O(d^2) each, plus polynomial table/index/witness costs. On a straightforward bit RAM this is O(Phi_initial*(n^2 Phi_initial+Phi_initial^2)*d^2) core bit work before indexing overhead. Conservative polynomial memory and cumulative bit-memory follow from explicit storage and the same work bound. If chart is acquired with size polynomial in original N, this fixed-chart phase is polynomial in N. No credit replenishment after fusion/guard changes assumed. This is not UCAC completeness: strict decrease may stop with nonaffine surviving dictionaries.
