## A. Exact joint cut theorem (PROVED)
Input: ANY explicit loopless multigraph G with n vertices, m edge IDs, labels g_e in F2^q, k signed integer quota weights h_e in Z^k, required syndrome t and quota h, and optional edge pins in the actual guard. A rooted binary tree partitions its edge IDs into singleton leaves. For each edge define a joint column a_e=(incidence(u,v),g_e) in F2^(n+q); target a*=(1^n,t). For each node edge subset I define U_I=span{a_e:e in I}, U_bar=span{a_e:e outside I}, w_I=dim(U_I intersect U_bar), w=max w_I. Compute ranks by Gaussian elimination; no interface word enumeration required for this acquisition. Pins are enforced in leaf choices and do not need to change raw spaces. Reject inconsistent root target outside U_E.

Each table entry represents partial matchings M subset I with key (v,j), v=sum a_e over M in F2, j=sum h_e in Z^k. Retain only v in U_I intersect (a*+U_bar). This is either empty or one affine coset of U_I intersect U_bar, with at most 2^w_I possible v. The occupancy component of v is the ACTUAL 0/1 occupancy vector because every partial configuration maintained is a matching. This is stronger than a linear parity relaxation. Integer quota j stays joint, not independent marginal.

Leaves: consider absent/present, check pins and coset membership. At a binary join I=L disjoint union R, combine child keys only if their actual occupancy masks are disjoint (bitwise AND zero). Then v=v_L XOR v_R and j=j_L+j_R. Apply parent coset filter. Root accepts only (a*,h). A global matching with correct target survives every coset filter, since its complement contributes a*+v. Conversely every assembled object is a partial matching by induction and at the root covers every vertex exactly once with actual requested tag and quota. Distinct edge IDs preserved including parallel edges. Key sufficiency: cross-boundary matching conflicts depend ONLY on exact occupancy, and all remaining compatibility depends ONLY on sum of tag and quota. No planarity, Pfaffian, randomized PIT, tight-cut or affine local-support promise is needed. Nonaffine support holes remain absent in the table.

A necessary occupancy check: the three edges of K1,3 have XOR incidence all ones, even though all three cannot constitute a perfect matching. Thus pure parity gluing without the disjointness test is false. This is a proof counterexample, not a separately executed control.

## B. One-pass full affine closure (PROVED)
At every nonempty key store exact positive integer count C, actual representative edge-incidence vector r and a basis D of the span of all differences x+r of its represented configurations in F2^m. This does NOT identify the support with its hull. Cartesian merge of disjoint child ID sets gives count C_L*C_R, representative r_L+r_R and basis span(D_L union D_R) (zero-extend). The difference space equality follows by varying either child alone, and every joint difference is their sum. If multiple compatible child-key pairs land on one parent key, their configuration sets are disjoint (each full partial matching uniquely restricts to its children). Add their counts, keep one r and insert every incoming difference basis plus r+r_incoming. This gives exactly the affine hull of the union: inclusion both ways follows from actual representatives and the difference decomposition. Induction yields complete affine hull of the actual target-conditioned graph solutions at the root, as well as exact count and one actual witness.

Every unconditional affine identity on original matching-edge bits is p dot x=p dot r for p in D^perp. All coordinate pins are immediately extractable. This computes all such identities in a single terminal pass, unlike M17's up to m parity decision probes. No additional equality is inferred from an empty target: that is a contradiction. Under an explicit guard, identities are only guard-relative. Archive the entire hull with its guard; adding any subset of these already-proved identities cannot change the solution set or its hull, so closure is a fixed point after every such deduction, including stagnation. This is not a license to feed a basis vector itself to the witness decoder; it need not be a solution difference between the retained representative and an individual witness. Store the real representative separately and verify it. Source affine identities can be transported only through a verified affine source decoder (otherwise retain edge-level statements).

Pre-closure: ordinary matching can find feasibility and forced/absent edges by exact inclusion/exclusion queries, and Lovasz matching span (M13/M17) supplies the full untagged affine hull; these are polynomial theorem transfers. Alternatively apply the same tree DP dropping tags and quotas, whose raw width cannot exceed the original? Do NOT assume this: projection can increase an intersection dimension. Charge its actual width or use the polynomial ordinary matching-span preclosure. In the executed checker we used exact untagged DP before target solving, measured actual operation totals, then enforced its pins and performed exact target closure. Recheck after adding all target pins, with full hull equality. No decision branch before closure; witness acquired from the retained real representative. General proof allows ordinary matching-span preclosure regardless of raw projected width. Reusing a fixed-point archive avoids unnecessary reencoding of all affine identities as new tags.
