## Theorem: a polynomial exact response without logarithmic slack
Let H=(L,R,E) be an explicitly acquired exact matching shell, |L|=d, |R|=q, and M a matching saturating L. A deletion set D of resource bins is admissible iff H-D admits a matching saturating L. If M cannot be found, a checked maximum-flow/mincut certificate rejects the shell. Otherwise let U be the unmatched bins in R, |U|=k=q-d. On R draw directed arc v->w whenever v is matched in M to a demand adjacent to w. For each v compute T(v), the set of reachable free bins, allowing a path of length zero. Suppose every T(v) has size at most one. Define loops with empty T and classes C_u={v:T(v)={u}}.

Then D is admissible iff D contains no loop and |D intersect C_u|<=1 for each u in U. Necessity: compare M with any saturating matching avoiding D. Every deleted matched bin starts an alternating path to a distinct bin free in M and not deleted. Its endpoint lies in T(v). A deleted free bin already uses the only deletion allowance in its class. Sufficiency: choose a simple directed path from each deleted matched bin to its unique free bin; paths from distinct classes cannot intersect because a common vertex would reach two free bins. No other deleted bin lies on the chosen path. Shift assignments backwards along each path. The resulting matching saturates all demands and avoids D. This is a direct special case of deletion independence in the dual of a transversal matroid, not a claim of novel general matroid theory.

Recognition uses one saturating matching and k reverse reachability searches, O(k(q+|E|)) graph events after matching, plus explicit original-ID maps and polynomial bit overhead. Path certificates and witness reconstruction are polynomial (at most q resource vertices on disjoint paths), space O(kq+|E|) indexed words for explicit tables. Alternatively test every singleton and pair deletion by flow; these determine loops and parallel classes, and if the number of nonloop classes equals k, the deletion matroid is the direct sum of rank-one classes (a basis must meet each class). Reachability recognition avoids the quadratic number of maxflow calls. A vertex reaching multiple free bins is a scoped refusal of this criterion, not an impossibility of another response compression.

If h_j(z) is an explicitly certified signed-literal bin-disabled predicate, exact shell elimination exports units NOT h_j for loops and binary clauses NOT h_i OR NOT h_j inside each class. With an exact 2CNF consumer the complete problem is solved by standard 2SAT regardless of slack k. More generally conjunctive predicates export the corresponding union-of-negated-literals clause; this remains 2CNF only when each non-tautological export has width <=2. A different tractable consumer (e.g. a wholly affine joined response) is permitted if its own hypotheses hold. No general mixed affine/bijunctive tractability is assumed. Discharged region must have exactly the declared interior grammar and all boundary/exterior incidences checked. Scope is a sufficient constructive terminal, not universally acquired.

## Higher-order overlap and repair

A region forbidding odd words has exact support {0,3,5,6}; a region forbidding even words has {1,2,4,7}. Each has uniform 1/4 full-region distribution on its own four valid words (matching lifts), every singleton half and every pair cell 1/4.
