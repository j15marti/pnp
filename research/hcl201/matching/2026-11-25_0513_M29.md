For reverse, obtain a rank-revealing unimodular minor of D_J (row and column pivot sets of full rank, determinant ±1). Then s-z lies in (k-1)F. Induct. This also preserves fixed coordinates. Consequently h is a difference of sums of actual vertices, and modulo 2 a sum of differences from z0.
