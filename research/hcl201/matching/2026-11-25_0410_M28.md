## PROVED: regular augmented syndrome consumer
Let H be a binary matrix, t target, nonnegative binary-encoded integral costs w. First Gaussian consistency/forced-coordinate closure as applicable (this is not semantic closure). The exact minimum of sum w_e y_e over Hy=t, y binary, is the minimum circuit weight (excluding a distinguished element *) through * in the binary matroid represented by [H|t]. Any feasible support plus * is a binary cycle; it decomposes into disjoint circuits and one contains *. Nonnegative weights let us discard the others. Conversely such a circuit gives a feasible word. A zero target yields the empty word; infeasible linear systems are rejected first.
If the AUGMENTED matroid is regular, polynomial binary regularity recognition and TU signing of a row-reduced standard representation give a TU matrix B representing the same binary matroid (not necessarily the original H). With columns B_e and B_* solve the rational LP
min sum_e w_e (u_e+v_e), B_E(u-v)=-B_*, 0<=u,v<=1.
The constraint matrix [B_E,-B_E] with bounds is TU; an integral optimum can be acquired by an exact polynomial rational LP and deterministic lexicographic vertex extraction. Reduction of u-v mod 2 gives a feasible word of weight at most LP optimum. Conversely a minimum binary circuit through * has a signed real dependence with coefficients +/-1, including coefficient +1 at *: choose a basis of the independent columns of the circuit minus *, use a nonsingular square submatrix and Cramer's rule; total unimodularity makes every nonzero coefficient +/-1. This yields a feasible LP with exactly the circuit cost. Thus exact equality, witness and polynomial bit complexity. This is a theorem transfer/application of regular matroid circuit optimization, not a new general matroid theorem.
For loopless syndrome-labeled perfect matching, take H as stacked mod-2 vertex incidence and syndrome labels, t=(all ones,target). Any feasible support has all degrees odd, hence at least |V|/2 edges, and equality holds iff it is an actual perfect matching. Min cardinality solves exact tagged matching on this regular augmented domain. For a nonnegative weighted objective use edge cost M+w_e, M=1+sum w_e, to minimize cardinality then cost. No extra exact quota/count guarantee. Each decision can verify actual matching and syndrome. The number of syndrome rows may grow arbitrarily, and no rank enumeration is required. Regularity of H ALONE is insufficient.
