## 2. Graph-level acquisition theorem
Let G=(U disjoint Z, E disjoint F; A) be balanced bipartite, with active rows U, slack rows Z, real columns E (each carries a distinct coordinate index from a common dictionary, missing indices are loops), filler columns F. Distinct indices need not have linearly independent tags. Write k=|U|. Conditions:
1. Every slack row is adjacent to every column.
2. The subgraph U--F admits a matching saturating U (ordinary matching test).
3. For every existing edge ij there are vertex potentials p_i,p_j in GF(2)^d such that
   g_ij = p_i+p_j + (D_e if i in U and j=e in E, else 0).
   Here D_e is the supplied shared dictionary column. Check potentials by DFS on edge discrepancies (or Gaussian elimination); all edges checked. No assumption about absent active edges.
These three conditions are sufficient, not necessary. Given partition and D, verification is polynomial. Recover gauge potentials by setting one root per connected component to zero, propagating the residual edge equations, and rejecting inconsistent edges. The offset a is XOR of potentials over all vertices; independent of gauge choices when PMs exist.

Let I be the subsets J of E matchable into distinct active rows (partial transversals). The exact source syndrome support is
    S(G) = a + {D 1_J : J in I}.
Proof: every perfect matching uses active rows on a subset J of real columns and fillers; slack edges contribute no nonconstant tag. Conversely take any matching of J into U. Extend in the restricted column graph J union F: the current matching saturates J, and the maximum size is k by condition 2. Successive augmenting paths to maximum preserve saturation of every previously matched column, so all J remain selected, and exactly k columns now cover U. Since |Z|=|E|+|F|-k and slack rows are complete, biject remaining columns arbitrarily to slack rows. This is an actual original PM, with tag a+D1_J. Each local independence query and lift uses polynomial ordinary matching. This is a complete exact acquisition certificate for a compact nonaffine support, not a claim that its affine hull equals its support. Edmonds-Fulkerson (1965), section 1, identifies partial transversals as a matroid; the direct augmenting proof here also supplies the missing source lift.

## 3. Deterministic product terminal and contradiction
For independent factors i with certified supports, define a=sum_i a_i and common dictionary D. S02 cancellation: every coordinate with even multiplicity can be deleted from all local selections and every coordinate with odd multiplicity retained once. Each local subset remains legal by heredity, and XOR is unchanged. Conversely disjoint assignments are legal. Construct B with coordinate vertices e and ALL active row copies (i,u); edge iff real column e adjacent to u in factor i. Then the complete joint support is
    S_total = a + {D 1_J : J matchable into B's active slots}.
No general matroid intersection or oracle nesting needed. Any matching of target indices in B partitions them among factors; apply Section 2 lift independently, then verify original PMs and target. First reject c+a outside span of D columns with a nonempty neighborhood. This is the EXACT global affine hull because zero and all nonloop singletons are attainable and every support point lies in their span. Retain this unconditional information in the ledger before branching. For each remaining target coefficient lift solve D z=c+a over GF(2), after removing loop columns. With q=nullity(D restricted to nonloops), at most 2^q coefficient lifts. For each, find a matching saturating T=supp(z) in B. If it fails, a maximum matching and alternating reachability from unmatched T vertices produce X subset T with |N_B(X)|<|X|. This is a qualitative contradiction, a Hall deficiency; it need not be visible in binary linear algebra. A failure is scoped to that coefficient lift, and to its guard if present. All lifts must fail to conclude UNSAT. With q=0, a single Hall check is complete; q=O(log N) yields deterministic polynomial time even with high global syndrome rank. Exact support as a transversal matroid image can be stored as B and D, without listing its sets.
 
Additional exact rank-one acquisition (proved, not implemented in this run): for any explicit undirected tagged graph with PMs, after a certified gauge choose a canonical zero label and give weight 1 to all nonzero reduced-label edges, 0 otherwise. If maximum PM weight <=1, the support is a subset of {0} union the distinct nonzero labels; test zero via minimum weight=0 and test each label by forcing one of its edges (delete endpoints and query ordinary PM). Store actual witnesses. If zero is unavailable, pick any attainable label d0 as offset; the new support is {0} plus the differences d+d0. This is a rank-one transversal on distinct nonzero dictionary differences and can be merged with the Section 2 factors when their dictionary agrees. If max>1 recognition fails, not UNSAT. RT02's K4 with all nonzero tags incident on a single pivot meets the max<=1 test, supplying physical extraction without support enumeration. General weighted PM theorem is classical; this additional certificate has not been executed.

For each extendible crossing edge uv, remove its endpoints from the two induced sides; conditional sides are independent, fixed tag g_uv contributes an offset. Run these recognition and closure tests inside each such guard, then use the above exact terminal whenever both sides qualify. There are at most |delta(C)| guards, so a single cut with all extendible fibers certified and q=O(log N) is a deterministic polynomial terminal; if a fiber is unrecognized, no global UNSAT conclusion unless separately resolved.
