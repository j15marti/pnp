## PROVED: planar structural crossover obstruction
Let a finite planar graph be embedded in a disk with four distinct boundary terminals t1,t2,t3,t4 in cyclic order. Let an energy be a sum of arbitrary pair terms depending only on xor (plus constants; no fixed field), or a conjunction of complement-invariant local hard relations whose primal graph is planar with these terminals. Suppose its nonempty optimum/feasible boundary support is exactly {0000,0101,1010,1111} (two independent crossing equalities). Choose full assignments a,b with boundary 0000,0101. Complement of b is also an optimum/feasible full assignment. For two optimum assignments, each connected component of their disagreement-induced graph can be switched independently from a: all affected edge energy deltas are additive because no edges join distinct components, each delta is nonnegative by optimality, and the sum is zero. For hard relations every owner touches at most one disagreement component, so the same switch preserves feasibility. Apply to D={a!=b} and D^c={a!=complement(b)}. If t2,t4 are in distinct D components, switching one gives an illegal boundary word. Thus a D path connects t2,t4; similarly a D^c path connects t1,t3. These paths are vertex-disjoint, impossible for alternating boundary terminals in a disk. Contradiction. The argument is unconditional and applies to arbitrary internal size, signs and auxiliary variables within this model. Ternary owner incidence embeddings allow local triangle expansion; arbitrary four-ary owner incidence embeddings need not give planar primal cliques. The result does NOT exclude nonplanar lifts, fields/pins, arbitrary higher-arity E4/B4 models, affine/nonlocal decoder interfaces, or syndrome-tagged matching.
