## A. Explicit tree extended formulation (PROVED)
For every finite explicit loopless tagged graph with integer additive costs, target syndrome and pins, and a supplied verified binary edge partition tree, run M18 untagged closure first (ordinary matching affine span or paid untagged tree), then actual tag-conditioned coarse support/hull. Verify actual occupancy disjointness and syndrome at each join. Remove unreachable states/joins by bottom-up positive support and top-down root reachability. Root accepts only all-ones occupancy and actual target syndrome. No quota is discarded from the registry. Additional nonlocal guards must be key-determined or encoded and charged, else decline.
Let T be the resulting indexed transition list (leaf alternatives and legal binary child-key pairs). A transition tau produces state s(tau) and consumes one state per child. Assign its additive quota contribution c_tau (leaf costs; zero at internal joins). Define the incidence matrix A with +1 for produced state, -1 for consumed states. Let b have 1 at the accepting root state, 0 elsewhere. Define
 P = {y >= 0 : A y = b}.
For each complete derivation D let chi_D be its transition incidence vector. Each tree node is visited exactly once. Then P = conv{chi_D}.
Proof: any derivation satisfies balances. Conversely sample a root transition in proportion to its flow, then at a reached child state s choose a producing transition with probabilities y_tau/sum_{tau:s(tau)=s}y_tau. The flow balances imply inductively that the probability of reaching s equals the total producing flow of s; positive states have a well-defined conditional distribution, zero states are never reached. Each sampled derivation is actual, and its expected incidence is y. This proves equality, not just integrality of an unrelated graph-flow relaxation. All variables <=1 by conservation across each fixed tree level/node. Duplicate choices remain indexed; matching configurations decompose uniquely under the actual edge tree. No tree independence is assumed beyond extension-sufficient coarse keys. C maps each transition vector to its quota vector by columns c_tau.
Let K=C P=conv{cost(D)}, the exact convex hull of coarse-feasible quota vectors. It can have exponentially many vertices but the transition formulation is explicit. Impose exact rational equations Cy=Q. Let P_Q be the resulting slice. If P_Q is empty, the original integer problem is UNSAT. This is a certified necessary test, not an integrality claim about the slice.

## B. Automatic minimal-face closure theorem (PROVED)
If P_Q is nonempty, for every tau solve the rational LP maximize y_tau on P_Q. Keep tau precisely when the optimum is strictly positive; let E_Q be this set. A polynomial number of rational LP solves suffices, independently of the numerical weight range. For every deleted transition, an exact dual optimum zero certifies that no exact-target derivation uses it. Infeasibility requires a Farkas certificate. For retained transitions record exact positive primal witnesses. A single certified batch deletion can be applied and immediately reclosed (actual support/hull, no branch); individually applying deductions with reclosure is also polynomial. These are Q- and guard-scoped facts, not unconditional outside Q.
Let F be the unique minimal face of K containing Q, so Q is in its relative interior. Then:
  {D : every transition of D lies in E_Q} = {D : cost(D) lies in F}.
Proof forward: average the positive primal witnesses for every retained tau to get y* in P_Q with positive coordinates exactly E_Q. For any coarse-valid D using only E_Q, choose 0<epsilon<min(1,min_{tau in D} y*_tau). Then z=(y*-epsilon chi_D)/(1-epsilon) lies in P by nonnegativity and balances. Hence Q=epsilon cost(D)+(1-epsilon)Cz. Since F is a face containing Q, both costs belong to F. No invalid recombination occurs even though different transitions may have been witnessed by different fractional mixtures.
Proof reverse: if p=cost(D) in F, relative interior gives Q=epsilon p+(1-epsilon)p' for some 0<epsilon<1 and p' in F (if p=Q take p'=Q). Lift p' to y' in P. Then epsilon chi_D+(1-epsilon)y' is in P_Q and all transitions of D have positive coordinates. Thus D uses E_Q.
This is the complete minimal face, with no enumeration of facets, supporting normals, exact quota words or matchings. Re-run actual affine hull/count/witness on pruned DAG using M18 positive payload. It computes all affine GF2 identities of the face-supported derivations, hence sound necessary identities of exact solutions. No fractional anchor or GF2 hull member is treated as actual. If repeated closure on the same slice without new constraints, E_Q is a fixed point: every old slice witness uses only kept transitions; after pruning nothing changes in P_Q. Exact face identities may be archived as implied, not added as costly global tags unless remeasured.

## C. Vertex terminal and exact row reduction (PROVED)
If dim aff(F)=0, then F={Q}. EVERY remaining derivation has exactly Q. Ordinary positive M18 payload on retained transitions now gives the exact integer decision SAT, exact graph count, actual witness and complete target-conditioned GF2 affine hull. No numeric quota DP and no randomization. Conversely, if Q belongs to K and is a vertex, dimension is zero, so this procedure is a complete deterministic terminal for that promise, even with arbitrary binary integer weights and polynomially many quota rows. Infeasible LP also decides UNSAT without that promise. Verify actual witness against graph, syndrome, quotas, pins and original source decoder (if supplied). If the LP is feasible and F has positive dimension, a verified anchor at Q may certify SAT only; it does not establish exact count or complete quota-conditioned hull.
For general F, acquire an actual cost anchor a and basis V=span_Q{cost(D)-a : D retained} by union and additive-product affine payload (the rational analogue of M18/MS19). For joins add child anchors and spans; for unions add the anchor difference. Use exact Gaussian/rational arithmetic and bounded actual difference vectors. Since Q in aff(F), choose r=dim V linearly independent coordinate rows of any basis matrix for V. Projection onto these quota rows is injective on V, so on all remaining actual configurations:
  cost(D)=Q iff cost(D)_J=Q_J.
This drops k-r quota rows without any enumeration, and without modifying the registered tag chart. Retain full quota decoder/verification. After each certified removal reclose or archive the logically equivalent row set; no branch required. M19/S19 may run on the remaining r exact rows, with their actual residual/lattice capacities; MS19 may add necessary parity with actual remeasured width. Rank one does not imply small residual, as ordinary subset sum already illustrates. No universal bound on r or on the remaining state volume.

An exposed-face certificate can also be supplied directly: for rational lambda, compute max_D lambda^T cost(D) by exact scalar DP. If lambda^T Q exceeds it, reject; if equality, retain precisely transitions in optimal root derivations, by max-plus recurrence and reverse reachability. Repeat with fresh supporting normals if known. This is a cheap specialized path, not a free oracle for general normals. LP support acquisition above removes the need to guess them.
