## 3. Theorem A: exact rank-one robust recourse (PROVED)
If dim aff(D_C)<=1, we can decide whether EVERY integral quotient y has an integral interior H_C completion in polynomial bit time, with at most two endpoint flow feasibility checks after rank/extremum discovery.

Proof: If rank zero, D_C is a singleton. Otherwise choose a vertex-coordinate j nonconstant on D_C. This coordinate is injective on its affine line. Minimize and maximize d_C(y)_j over P by min-cost integral flow on Q, giving integral y^- and y^+; their images d^- and d^+ are the endpoints of D_C. Necessity: any uniform completion certificate must work on these actual quotient witnesses. Sufficiency: any d_C(y) is a convex combination of d^- and d^+. If both endpoint flows satisfy unit slack, their convex combination satisfies that balance and the same slack bounds as a real flow. For each integral y, d_C(y) is integral; network integrality gives an integral flow with the same bounds. Compute it by an ordinary bounded-flow query when lifting y. No requirement to retain the same chord flows, to use a fixed tree, or to round each coordinate independently. If every H component passes, independent completions give a global interior lift for every y. No correlation among components is assumed beyond the common fixed y.

Efficient rank discovery: find one feasible quotient flow, test each coordinate for a deviation in each direction and mark exactly the fixed coordinates. In the affine subspace A_Q y=b_Q with fixed coordinates removed/frozen, averaging finitely many feasible witnesses yields a point strict between both bounds in every nonfixed coordinate. Thus this subspace is exactly aff(P); its direction space is the kernel of A_Q with fixed coordinates zero. Compute a rational basis C0 by Gaussian elimination; rank of (A_K C0)|_C is dim aff(D_C). Zero-capacity and fixed arcs treated explicitly. Min-cost calls optimize a coordinate linear form including original arc IDs even if contracted loops are present. Bit-polynomial integral network optimization, not unit augmentations in numerical capacity.

## 4. Theorem B: higher-dimensional demand boxes (PROVED, not executed)
For rank r_C, choose r_C actual coordinates whose restriction to the affine direction space is injective. Minimize and maximize each selected coordinate over P. Form the enclosing coordinate box and lift its 2^r_C vertices through the affine inverse. Some lifted corners need not belong to D_C and may be rational. If each corner admits a real unit-slack flow, convexity certifies every actual d_C(y), and integral recourse follows as above. Failed corner test is UNKNOWN (not necessarily actual failure); rank-one is exact because corners are actual endpoints. Cost is sum_C 2^r_C poly(N), not exponential in the total rank across components. A sufficient deterministic polynomial certificate acquisition condition is max_C r_C=O(log N) for explicitly selected H and polynomially many candidates. Rank-one failure rules out the specified all-y interior property but does not prove infeasibility of the original tagged network. Arbitrary H discovery remains OPEN.

## 6. Theorem C: exact tag quotient stable under arbitrary additive parity probes (PROVED)
Assume every C has uniform interior recourse by A/B or another certified procedure. Choose a spanning forest of H, vertex potentials h_v in GF(2)^q such that g'_e=g_e+h_head+h_tail is zero on tree edges. Set a=sum_v (b_v mod2)h_v and W=span of gauged H chord labels. Conservation makes the total tag a+sum_e (x_e mod2)g'_e. Given ANY y in P integral, construct an interior lift z by bounded flow. The fiber tag is exactly
  a + sum_{e in K}(y_e mod 2)g'_e + W.
All completions lie in that coset by gauge. Conversely, express any required difference from z as a combination of chord cycle labels (Gaussian preimage); XOR the corresponding fundamental-cycle incidence vectors; orient the even subgraph Eulerian and apply signed unit changes. Feasible by interior margin. Quotient all target/tag coordinates by W, retain cycle preimages and original arc IDs. No counting claim.

Crucially the uniform interior certificate depends only on the balance/capacity domain, NOT on the tag dictionary. For any additional original coefficient parity observation p^T(x mod2), append p_e to every arc tag; recompute gauge and W in q+1 dimensions and preserve every original target coordinate. Same H and recourse certificate valid, same quotient graph and chord parity dimension, projected tag rank may rise by at most one (quotient dimension changes by 0 or 1). ## 7. Strict improvement over all fixed trees on one infinite family
For integer h>=4, H is h parallel arcs 0->1 of capacity 4, first tag zero and remaining h-1 independent tags. K consists of one 0->1 arc capacity h-1 tag zero, two unit arcs 0->2 with labels 00 and 01 in new bits, and one unit arc 1->2 with label 10. Demands (-2h-1,2h,1). Let y be the K loop flow and c the selection of 1->2. Q permits y in [0,h-1] and exactly one exit arc. H must carry total 2h-y+c, ranging from h+1 to 2h+1. Every such integer total is in [h,3h], the interior capacity range. Hence endpoint test certifies all fibers. No fixed tree recourse in S08 can work: every tree has exactly one H arc, correction width h, but its interior interval [1,3] has width 2. This disproves necessity of fixed-tree recourse, not S08's sufficient theorem. All H and K arcs variable. Original beta=h+2, quotient beta=3 (one loop plus two independent parallel exit differences). H cycle-label rank h-1. Exact global support is GF(2)^(h-1) x {00,01,10}. Missing 11 lies inside full affine hull. The zero-tag loop can also be freely optimized away, but not needed. This family demonstrates strict representation improvement, not general hardness. Candidate H supplied explicitly; capacity threshold alone need not find it because K has a large capacity loop.
