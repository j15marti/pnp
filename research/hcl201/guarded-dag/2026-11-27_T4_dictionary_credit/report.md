## Proven lemma: consumer-safe causal guard forgetting
Let F be one exact authenticated chart under fixed base guard B. Let L_i be a topologically ordered list of verified consequences of F under B. A leaf unit-propagation conflict under decision guard H has a reason DAG obtained by recursively following the antecedent clauses of the falsified literals of its final conflict. Stop at decision literals. Denote those decisions by K subset H and the used clause indices by S, all indices referring to F or already verified L_i. Replaying only S under K establishes contradiction: induct in the original chronological order of propagated assignments; every needed antecedent occurs earlier or is a decision in K. Thus F AND B entails the clause OR_{l in K} NOT l. Verify it before admission and reclose. In every later context on the same chart with base B (or a stronger guard) and monotone admitted registry, this clause can be reused without rechecking its proof, though finding consumers, checking applicability and all propagation still cost work. Different charts or guards need checked transport. A source-only gauge-fixed conclusion remains relative to its base gauge until complement symmetry is discharged.

Store sorted lists of support indices by exact prefix trie nodes (parent index, next index). Check parent index < child index, full equality of the decoded list to the declared support, and every referenced clause precedes its consumer. Hash equality is not an inference. This is lossless ordered support-list compression, not general semantic DAG isomorphism or optimal LZ. If total explicit support entries S_tot, dictionary D entries, M consumers and index bit width b, building the dictionary with ordered-map lookup costs O(S_tot poly(b,log(S_tot+2))) bit work; checking all decoded lists costs O(S_tot poly(b)), plus actual RUP replay. The encoded dictionary and pointers take O((D+M)b) bits, not necessarily smaller than raw supports. Charge guards, clauses, trace, source maps, learned proof lines and multiplicity separately. A polynomial D does NOT imply a polynomial S_tot or polynomial discovery.

Counterexample to dictionary-size-only progress: four clauses (a OR b),(a OR NOT b),(NOT a OR b),(NOT a OR NOT b), plus k disjoint clauses (u_i OR v_i). Under unit closure alone, branch on each u_i first, then a; both assignments to each u_i are live before a and negative u_i forces v_i. At all 2^(k+1) leaves one of two constant conflict supports suffices. Dictionary size is constant but explicit consumer guards and search remain exponential. Learning the causal unit exclusions a and NOT a collapses this example. It is not a counterexample after stronger failed-literal closure, nor a lower bound on arbitrary solvers. This refutes only the inference from small support dictionary to paid global progress.
