## 2. Known theorem and new adapter
Primary source: El Maalouly, Steiner, Wulf, Exact Matching: Correct Parity and FPT Parameterized by Independence Number, ISAAC 2023, arXiv:2207.09797, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.09797, section 4, Theorems 19/20, Lemma 21, Corollary 22 (PDF pages 14-16 in one-based numbering; inspected text and page images). Lovasz, Matching structure and the matching lattice, JCTB 43 (1987), 187-222. Theorem 20 supplies a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm to compute the linear span of perfect matching incidence vectors over a fixed finite field, including GF(2), on simple general graphs. Corollary 22 tests one red-edge parity; Theorem 19 obtains a witness via edge-deletion self-reduction. This is not deterministic exact-weight matching. We apply the known theorem; do not claim a new Lovasz algorithm.

Let G be a nonempty explicit simple graph with perfect matchings X. Add a disjoint forced edge e*, whether or not original graph is empty. Let L=span_F2{chi(M): M perfect matching of G+e*}. Its coordinate ell(x)=x_e* is 1 on every actual matching. Then aff(X_aug)=L intersect {ell=1}. Proof: a sum over GF(2) lies on ell=1 iff it uses an odd number of matching generators, exactly the affine combinations over GF(2). Empty support yields no ell=1. Given a basis of L, choose any basis vector a with ell=1; direction basis spans {v+ell(v)a: v in basis}. This computes the full affine hull without assuming basis vectors are actual matchings. For an authenticated affine output feature map f(M)=f0+P chi(M), the projected hull is f0+Pa+P(ker ell intersect L). Affine images commute with affine hull. The output is a hull, not a complete support relation; a synthetic anchor need not be a source witness.

## 4. BAD-count hull and witness theorem
Within a fixed count state and masks, a BAD group has f forced-one blocks and h flexible blocks. Let l=k-f. If l not in [0,h], reject; forced choices are constants. If l=0 or l=h, exactly one choice word. Otherwise choose a baseline l-subset A of flexible blocks; all swaps i in A,j outside A are actual feasible words with differences e_i+e_j. Their span is exactly the even-weight subspace of F2^h: the complete bipartite graph on A and its complement is connected, and its incidence differences span the even-weight space. Conversely every valid word differs in an even number of bits. This proves exact affine hull of the fixed-cardinality choice words, and hence of arbitrary affine features of actual BAD row indicators. To witness an opposite bit, either baseline already has it or some single swap has odd feature difference; test at most h^2 pairs. Multiple independent groups and GOOD assignments combine by Minkowski sum of affine hulls because costs and base tags are fixed by counts, conditional on no hidden shared constraints. For a single parity probe, if baseline parity fails, change ONE group or GOOD matching that has an attainable opposite bit; no mixed incompatible choices. Reconstruct and verify actual rows, masks, owner budgets, original tag and source map.
