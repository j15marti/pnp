## Theorem
On the BS15 ring of q >= 2 blocks, for either fixed value of the unique seed coordinate, any exact union of families closed under coordinatewise meet and join after separate fixed coordinate complements (different complements permitted in each family) has at least (3/2)^(q-1) nonempty families. The families may have arbitrary auxiliary bits before coordinate projection. In particular this holds for projected global minimizers of nonnegative-capacity directed s-t cuts or submodular energies, regardless of their auxiliary graph size. Each piece must project only to legal repairs, and the union must cover every legal repair in the pinned seed fiber. It does not cover arbitrary affine decoding, general threshold sublevel sets, decision-only reductions, or factored unions.

Proof: a nonempty minimizer family of a submodular function is closed under intersection and union, by the defining inequality and optimality; fixed pins preserve this property. Coordinate projection preserves meet/join because two projected points have full lifts and their meet/join lifts project correctly. With exactly one initial disagreement, any strict monotone improvement is a common feasible word z, and any such word can be reached by a cycle batch (possibly using several components) with both endpoint flows, by S21. In particular the full repair relation projected onto one actual bit per synchronized coordinate is exactly the common support. Flips relative to the initial word are just coordinate complements.

Connectivity: consecutive-layer triples connect each layer's three variables via shared successors, and consecutive layers are joined around the ring.

Any complemented NAE3 excludes a complementary pair of words. A meet/join closed subset has at most 4 members: for excluded 001 and 110 the disjoint pairs (011,101) and (010,100) respectively meet/join to those excluded words, so at least one word of each pair must be absent; permute coordinates and globally complement for any mixed pair. For excluded 000 and 111 use disjoint pairs (001,010) and (101,110). A piece projected onto q-1 disjoint triples therefore has at most 4^(q-1) points, even with correlations and differing signs. Covering all 6^(q-1) projected points requires at least (3/2)^(q-1) pieces. No assumption of efficient discovery was needed. Explicit flat dispatch of that many pieces, even given free per-piece acquisition, cannot meet any polynomial occurrence credit on this family. At q=32 the lower bound exceeds 2^18. The source encoded length O(q log q) is polynomial in q.

## Constructive repair of this obstruction
Do not flatten the cover. With supplied BS15 block ordering, tabulate each block's feasible actual 36-bit words conditional on its first three bits and the next block's first three bits. There are 8x8 seam choices, constant block size; impose the actual seed pin only on block zero. Each clause is either internal or crosses the seam to the next first layer, so this is exact. A guarded cyclic DP fixes first seam, advances 8-state seam messages and closes the ring; retain a predecessor and a private local witness for reconstruction. All local search is paid (even exhaustive enumeration of <=39 bits is a fixed constant), every edge and failed candidate charged. A full relation can also be represented without flattening by local transitions labelled by their accepted local words, with exponentially many paths and polynomial-size factored graph. The finite transition table can be obtained by actual local DPLL with pin closure and retained branch assignments; no complete semantic closure assumed. For the supplied block plan, time O(poly(N)), peak and cumulative memory polynomial; for naive stored full path copying O(q^2) word references suffices, not free. This is an exact alternative on the adversarial family, not universal block-order discovery. A universal algorithm could find one verified witness without representing all repairs, so the cover bound is NOT a time lower bound for SAT, nor for P vs NP.
