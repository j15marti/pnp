## Exact weighted observation trellis (proved)

Canonicalize equal characters and constants, only within the actual guard. The objective is c+sum_i f_i(a_i y), where f_i is an explicitly bit-encoded two-entry integer cost, in particular f_i(b)=w_i(-1)^b. Given an order, write P_j=span(a_1,...,a_j), R_j=span(a_{j+1},...,a_m), B_j=P_j intersect R_j. A state retains the minimum processed cost and an actual realizing word on B_j. If a_{j+1} belongs to P_j it belongs to B_j and its value is fixed by the state. Otherwise both values extend every prefix functional; a Gaussian homogeneous solve finds a direction orthogonal to P_j toggling that character. The next B_{j+1} is contained in B_j+span(a_{j+1}): write its element as p+e a_{j+1}; p belongs to P_j and R_j. Thus the new state depends only on old boundary values and the selected bit, and two histories with the same state have identical continuations. Inductively minimize costs per state; retain an actual realizing witness, using the toggling direction to preserve all previously processed costs, not merely the boundary. Root minimum is exact and source witness is reconstructed through Q and verified.

## Exact all-sign weighted factorization rank (proved, scoped)
Fix labelled observation masks on a consistent affine guard, partition them A,B, and choose positive weights d_i. Consider M(p,q)=min_{x satisfying guard} sum_{i in A} d_i [a_i x != p_i]+sum_{j in B} d_j [a_j x != q_j], indexed by all independent preferred-bit words on each side. Let U,V be the observation spans restricted to ker G, w=dim(U intersect V). The min-plus separable rank (least k in M(p,q)=min_{t<=k}(u_t(p)+v_t(q))) is exactly 2^w. Upper bound: condition on the shared linear functional (subtract the fixed affine offset); each side can minimize independently and agreeing functionals glue on U+V, extend to a full word. Lower bound: pick one full realization for each shared functional and take its left/right observed words as row/column indices. Diagonal entries zero; every off-diagonal positive because different shared functionals cannot glue, and weights strictly positive. Every min-plus summand dominates M everywhere, and cannot attain zero on two diagonal cells: the sum of its two crossed entries equals zero, while both must dominate positive entries. This also allows +infinity terms. Hence at least 2^w terms. The result is a positive-weight affine-guard extension/application of Sheshadri 2026 arXiv:2607.23471, Theorem 1 (unweighted linear codes). NOT a fixed-instance decision lower bound; independent preferred signs include choices outside correlated NAE-owner signs. An implicit or nonseparable representation can bypass this.
