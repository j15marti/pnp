## New audit: residue gauge dependence
A signed variable reparametrization x_i'=1-x_i reverses every occurrence's sign while preserving truth, so exact k is invariant. For a satisfied occurrence its contribution s changes to -s; signed sum changes by -2s (congruent to s mod3). Example satisfied (+,+,-) row has k=3, k mod3=0, signed residue1; invert the third coordinate and its sign, now (+++) gives residue0 without changing truth.
