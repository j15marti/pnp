## Exact proof
In a chart split into disjoint actual blocks, L is the explicit tuple registry, L_j the local projected list, H=(product L_j) minus L. For assignment x, V_j(x_j) is its complete set of locally violated packet IDs. Parent accepts iff product V_j is disjoint from L. Either some V_j is empty, or the nonempty box product V_j is contained in H. For every such box B=product D_j, solve local avoidance F_j(D_j) omitting exactly D_j. SAT parent iff OR of full local avoidance children OR of the AND of local deletion children for each box. The AND glues actual independent block witnesses; the OR child extends by arbitrary other block assignments. No uniqueness assumption for violation sets, no independent resampling of shared coordinates, no model-count transfer.

With h=|H| <= fixed C log2(N+2), compute product cardinality first; enumerate the ambient product only after cap passes (size |L|+h). Enumerate nonempty subsets of H, take their coordinate projections, retain iff the resulting box is contained in H. Reject projected products of cardinality >h before enumeration. Every true box is generated by choosing the box itself as subset. At most 2^h-1 proposals, polynomial bit cost. Use exact local (block, deleted set) child keys; one child result can fan out to multiple Boolean composition consumers, all edges and accesses charged. Distinct child formulas are inputs of the explicit composition, not recursively recomputed per syntactic occurrence. Verify sum child (1+retained rank mass) <= parent rank mass, or decline. One missing tuple: credits 2s+2 sum M_j-R; parent mass sum M_j product_{k!=j}m_k-R, so sufficient iff sum M_j(product_{k!=j}m_k-2)>=2s. This holds for two blocks of >=3 positive-rank packets or >=3 blocks of >=2 packets. Fano double root 196->30, one hole 192->56. This is a true weighted child contraction, including the full child list, not a selected branch.

Closure: certified local Gaussian/unit closure before acquisition, after strict deductions and at child entry/stagnation; any known affine guards must be substituted into the chart or kept exactly, and the disjoint block condition checked in that chart. No semantic closure oracle. In the executed rank-two controls every local complement has full affine hull (size > half), and every product packet has codimension >=2, so singleton affine scans cannot force an equation; no strict local deductions arose. This does not say the conjunction has full hull.
