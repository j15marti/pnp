## Scoped theorem: verify a compressed monotone reason grammar without expansion

A leaf (i,o) is an implication using clause C_i: if o is a literal of C_i its prerequisite set R is the complements of all other literals and output O={o}; if o=0 (contradiction symbol), R is the complements of every literal and O={0}. Require each atom to have valid row and designated output, no hash as a semantic proof. For a straight-line acyclic grammar of concatenated leaves, assign
R(AB)=R(A) union (R(B) minus O(A)); O(AB)=O(A) union O(B).
Also store maximum row ID and exact expanded occurrence count, added under concatenation. For each proof consumer at chronological row j, check max referenced row < j, root prerequisites contained in the complement of the learned clause, and 0 in root outputs. Every expansion is a sound proof by ordinary unit implication: the first child yields every output under its required external premises; the second child's required literals are either in the initial premises or supplied by the first child. A conflict makes all later consequences vacuous; repetition is sound even when output already established. By structural induction every summarized consumer is sound; strict LRAT trace ordering is not asserted for redundant repeated outputs.
