## Lemma (scoped, proved): naturality of complete-bucket elimination
Let F=E AND P AND Q, where E does not contain pivot v, P=AND_i(v OR A_i), Q=AND_j(not v OR B_j), and each A_i,B_j is pivot-free. All clauses are normalized, without tautologies. Let C be all non-tautological A_i OR B_j, deduplicated. For every boundary assignment, if a positive and negative clause both require opposite pivot values, a member of C is false. Otherwise choose v=1 if any A_i is false, and v=0 otherwise. Thus exists v F=E AND C and the stated choice lifts any projected witness. The conclusion is existential feasibility, not count preservation.
