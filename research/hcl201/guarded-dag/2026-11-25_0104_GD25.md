## Exact block theorem
Let H be any nonempty block of owners in an authenticated binary selector CSP with all actual guards enforced by the registry. Each internal owner has a supplied injective Boolean row code of polynomial total size. Assume each internal domain and every internal allowed-pair relation is exactly 2-CNF in the disjoint owner code bits. For each exterior row r, its compatibility with each internal owner is either empty or exactly the domain restricted by a conjunction of code-bit literals. Combine internal relations into a 2-CNF T; let U(r) be the union of cross pins. Exterior rows remain actual row IDs. If T is UNSAT, the root is UNSAT. Otherwise remove H and delete r if T and U(r) is UNSAT (or any cross support empty); delete exterior pair r,s if T and U(r) and U(s) is UNSAT. Keep every other exterior constraint. This represents the EXACT existential projection, not just a relaxation.

Proof: Any old solution survives. Conversely a feasible exterior transversal after these deletions has every single and pair of assumption sets feasible in T. Any inconsistent set of unit literals over a satisfiable 2-CNF has an inconsistent subset of at most two literals: 2-CNF solutions are closed under majority; if all proper subsets of a minimal inconsistent pin set of size >=3 have models, choose three models omitting three distinct pins and take their majority, contradiction. Therefore all exterior pins jointly extend. Solve T under their union and decode actual rows, check all original pair edges and use upstream source lifts. Pair assumptions are not new unconditional facts beyond the actual guard. The projection preserves exterior support, not internal counts or internal pins added later. Guards on eliminated bits require replay/recompilation; never transport representative restrictions as impossibility.

## Arbitrary-code obstruction
Even allowing arbitrarily long auxiliary code bits, suppose a nonempty bijunctive domain D decodes onto a relation on actual bits, and for every actual bit both value fibers are definable by conjunctions of code literals on D. Every nonconstant actual bit is a code literal on D: its two pin sets U,V are individually feasible and jointly infeasible. The preceding 2-pin lemma gives u in U, v in V with D entails not(u and v). All first-fiber words satisfy u, all second-fiber words falsify u. Constant bits are harmless. Hence the decoded relation is majority closed (apply majority to code words then decode). Signed NAE3 is not majority closed (for ordinary NAE the majority of 001,010,100 is 000; complement signs as appropriate). Therefore no code, not merely no coordinate basis, makes a six-row NAE owner both bijunctive and fully two-sided pin-interfaced. In raw linear signed NAE of minimum degree two every owner coordinate is exposed separately by another owner with both values, so every singleton fails in any code. More generally an all-exposed block with a nonbijunctive actual projection cannot meet this adapter. This does not exclude non-unit exterior consumers, larger private-coordinate elimination, or unrestricted algorithms. DO19 OR guard distribution is a distinct operation, and DA19 class-label choices do not evade this two-sided pin condition.
