## Mathematical result
Work in the square-free Boolean ring over F2; V_d(r) has dimension D_d(r)=sum_{j=0}^d binom(r,j). Normalize one independent affine split coordinate t, retaining the exact source/guard/reconstruction map. Let W_0,W_1 be certified child vanishing subspaces of V_d(r-1), valid under t=0 and t=1 respectively. The image of the injective map (f0,f1) -> (1+t)f0+t f1 is the linear span of their guard lifts. Its degree-at-most-d part has dimension
dim W0 + dim W1 - dim(pi_d(W0)+pi_d(W1)),
where pi_d extracts the degree-d homogeneous component (zero if d>r-1).
Proof: the multilinear expression is f0+t(f0+f1). Its only possible degree d+1 component is t*pi_d(f0+f1), so cancellation is exactly membership in the kernel of the top-component sum map. Restriction to the two slices recovers f0,f1, giving injectivity. Rank-nullity proves the formula. Gaussian elimination produces a basis and parent coefficient vectors in fixed-degree polynomial bit work after the child spans and their certificates have been acquired. This is maximal ONLY for the given linear span, not an ideal or semantic closure. All lifted rows remain valid only under the common base guard. A nonaffine chart change requires separate verification and accounting.
For independent affine guards of codimension b, any nonzero polynomial supported entirely inside the guard is g(z)q(y) after an invertible affine change, where g is the indicator of the fixed b-bit word. The disjoint variable groups make the exact degree b+deg(q). Invertible affine substitution preserves degree (apply inverse for the opposite inequality). Thus for b>d no individual nonzero guard-supported polynomial fits degree d, though factored certificates can remain compact. Sibling cancellation g*t+g*(1+t)=g forgets one guard bit; a complete acquired partition sums to 1. No free leaf acquisition or implication about general algorithms.
For a live parent (r,W), with restrictions contained in W_i, the difference of the two child credits from parent credit Phi_d=D_d(r)-dim W+r is exactly
binom(r-1,d) + r-2 - (dim W0+dim W1-dim W).
This is Pascal's identity; strict integer decrease requires child rank gain >= binom(r-1,d)+r-1. Applies to two retained charts of dimension r-1, not retired terminals, unequal affine contractions, or aliased identical objects. Even a split with empty spans inflates the bank for r>=3. The parent may not be silently discarded and the child ledger may not reset. A conditional budget for explicit polynomial chart and row sizes plus verified acquisition is possible, not UCAC. First OPEN arrow: deterministic acquisition of productive splice or alternative terminal/contraction for every reachable unresolved state with a single global bank.
