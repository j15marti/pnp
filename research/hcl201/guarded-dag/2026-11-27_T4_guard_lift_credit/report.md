## Mathematical result (scoped, not a P=NP claim)
Let F be a fixed authenticated Boolean chart with r variables and polynomial size in original encoded N. Let H be a consistent conjunction of signed coordinate pins. A local RUP derivation C_1,...,C_t against F plus H lifts linewise to D_i=not H OR C_i (where not H is the disjunction of negated pins). Discard tautologies and duplicate lines. In the RUP check of D_i, falsifying D_i supplies H and falsifies C_i; prior lifted clauses then behave as the earlier local clauses. Thus the local unit-propagation contradiction replays globally. An empty local clause becomes the guard exclusion. This does not forget the original native complement gauge; that gauge is discharged by the verified global symmetry. For affine/nonliteral guards the simple width formula is inapplicable without a charged exact encoding.

For fixed w, the global dictionary of distinct non-tautological clauses of width <=w (including empty) has K=sum_{i=0}^w 2^i binom(r,i) entries. A persistent RUP-only admission consumes one previously unused entry. Deterministic repeated exhaustive candidate scans, reclosure after each admission and deduplication decide whether a width-w RUP refutation is obtainable over that fixed chart: if a refutation with learned lines <=w exists, induct on its lines and use monotonicity of unit-propagation contradiction under added clauses. If saturation reached without contradiction, report UNKNOWN relative to the restricted method. At most K+1 complete passes, each <=K probes. With L0 initial literal occurrences and indexed variables, elementary scalar UP costs O((r+1)(L0+wK)) literal visits per probe; extra bit-cost for addressing, parsing, proof retention, all failed attempts, verification and reconstruction is polynomial for fixed w and polynomial chart size. Peak and cumulative storage polynomial under that same charged serial execution. This removes the discovery gap *within* the fixed-width RUP proof class; it does not prove that every NO instance has such a proof. For a guarded proof with learned width q and guard length g, lifted width <=q+g; if q+g grows, fixed-width credit no longer automatically applies. Actual width-two RUP is stronger than resolving only width-two clauses.

Restricted obstruction: a RUP admission of C of width <=w into a bounded-initial-width k CNF can be simulated by resolution plus weakening of width <=w+max(k,w)+1. Under negated C, simulate each propagated literal l as C OR l, resolving its reason clause against previous C OR not l_j and using weakening where needed. A unit-propagation conflict similarly yields C. The intermediate clauses contain at most the original reason's literals and C. Weakening is dispensable via subclauses. Therefore fixed w,k cannot give all UNSAT refutations: bounded-degree expander Tseitin contradictions require unbounded resolution width (Ben-Sasson and Wigderson 2001, section 4.1). More elementary width-two obstruction: in a simple cubic graph's three-variable local parity CNF, at most two pinned edge bits can cause at most one additional propagated bit, at their shared vertex; other endpoints are distinct, no second vertex has two fixed incident edges. Thus no width<=2 RUP clause is acquired initially, even with odd total charge. Gaussian elimination rejects odd charge immediately. This is a scoped simultaneous-stall falsifier for the narrow pool alone, not an unrestricted lower bound or a falsifier of the full portfolio.
