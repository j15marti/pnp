## 2. Theorem A: exact weighted transition with no local-rank exponent (PROVED)
Take ANY explicit directed acyclic multigraph with affine-conjunction guards on one common affine chart. Each edge is a single event; order edges so every incoming edge of a vertex is processed before any outgoing edge (topological tail order suffices). Parameterize a consistent chart by x=x0+Kz, z in F2^d, absorb offsets into guard right-hand sides. An inconsistent chart is empty. N includes the full encoded input, chart, event/edge identities, and guard descriptions; acquisition/authentication from an earlier source is charged separately. Source/target endpoints are held live; sink reach can be retained as a bit. Safe means target not reached. If compilation is exact iff, safe is SAT support; if sound only, uncovered words are merely candidates until verified.

For edge i, U_i is the rowspace of its guard. Define prefix P_i=sum_{j<=i} U_j, suffix S_i=sum_{j>i} U_j, W_i=P_i intersect S_i, w_i=dim W_i. P_0=S_m=W_0=W_m=0 where applicable (P_m and S_0 generally nonzero). Let delta_i=dim P_i-dim P_(i-1). Use a key (lambda,R) at cut i-1: lambda is the actual evaluation functional of z on W_(i-1), and R is exact processed-prefix reachability restricted to live vertices. Keep source/sink bits, and any vertex incident to both processed and future edges. Nonlive first-use vertices are false unless a source. p_i is the size of this live registry. Each key has an integer mass C of DISTINCT restrictions z|P_(i-1) (i.e. linear functionals on P_(i-1)); one actual representative word suffices for lifting. At start C=1 on the empty observation and initial reach state.

Let B_i=W_(i-1)+U_i. Two exact identities:
  P_(i-1) intersect B_i = W_(i-1)
  W_i subset B_i.
First: U_i subset S_(i-1), and W_(i-1) subset S_(i-1); both sides follow by intersection. Second: write w in W_i as p+u, p in P_(i-1), u in U_i. Then p=w+u lies in S_(i-1), hence W_(i-1). Consequently dim B_i=w_(i-1)+delta_i. For EVERY prefix functional with the given lambda, its extensions to P_i are in bijection with functionals on B_i agreeing with lambda, by unique linear gluing on the intersection. This proves identical extension multiplicities across merged histories, not merely equality of future support.

Enumerate only next interface functional eta in W_i*. Let H be the joint equations prescribing lambda and eta on their respective bases (actual chart rows). Let G_i be the affine conjunction defining the edge guard. For any consistent equation set H' with all rows in B_i, define
  C_B(H')=2^(w_(i-1)+delta_i-rank(H'));
and C_B(H')=0 when inconsistent. The transition weights are exactly
  c_1(lambda,eta)=C_B(H AND G_i),
  c_0(lambda,eta)=C_B(H)-C_B(H AND G_i).
No exponent in the number or rank of local guard rows is needed in the algorithm: large powers of two are stored as integers of at most d+1 bits. A conflicting pair of syndrome prescriptions gets zero mass. Counts are nonnegative and sum to total local compatible extensions.

For e=(u,v), update R by setting reach(v) to reach(v) OR (reach(u) AND truth), then discard expired vertices. Add C*c_truth to the new key (eta,R'). This is deterministic reachability, not counting enabled paths. Induction using the linear gluing bijection shows the exact mass invariant; every distinct prefix functional contributes once, and each full chart word is counted by multiplying final masses by 2^(d-dim P_m). Safe count zero iff coverage. With multiple designated source/sink pairs on disjoint copies, the same chart and joint order gives exact joint reachability; accept arbitrary polynomial-time predicate of final sink bits. For identity, count XOR of two reach bits, zero iff equivalent. This is NOT a promise that individually narrow graphs have a narrow joint schedule.

Proof of lift: for one representative x of the old key prescribe the actual values of every row of a basis of P_(i-1), prescribe eta, and impose G_i for truth=1. If truth=0, try each guard row's complement with those prescriptions until a consistent system is found. Positive weight and gluing guarantee one. Gaussian solve returns x' retaining the complete old observation and exact truth. Store one representative for each new key; no need to retain exponentially many original words. Verify final x against actual graph and authenticated decoder. The proof covers arbitrary high rank, inconsistent/empty guards, parallel edges, reused or dense affine rows, and all consistent source chart offsets.
