## 1. Partial stability, not number of unstable factors (PROVED)
For available color g let W_i(g)=proj_E J_i(g), where R_i(g)=a_i(g)+J_i(g). Detect all-empty factors. Compute C_i=intersection_g W_i(g), W=sum_i C_i, V=sum_i,g W_i(g). W subset V. Define a=dim V-dim W. All these spaces and projection maps are computed by polynomial Gaussian elimination on the explicit tables, not guessed. Individual instability may be extensive even if a=1.
For each i choose a fixed basis D_i of C_i; extend it separately in each color by residual syndrome columns E_i(g). Choose lifts of both sets of columns into J_i(g), with protected matrices B_i(g), F_i(g), and pure protected kernel H_i(g). Pad residual and kernel columns by zero to the local maximum. Thus every joint tag is exactly
 s_i=a_i^s(g)+D_i w_i+E_i(g)u_i,
 p_i=a_i^p(g)+B_i(g)w_i+F_i(g)u_i+H_i(g)t_i.
Availability enforced separately; arbitrary table entries at empty colors harmless. Both directions follow by projecting then subtracting lifts, leaving pure kernel.

This gives every protected tuple and only feasible ones when the residual equations hold, with free z,t. The section is guard-independent even when protected lift matrices change.

## 2. Deterministic sparse-selector feature theorem (PROVED)
For a nonempty affine cell x=x0+A y, let d be an upper bound on the degree in y of every interpolated local table entry (including the chosen lifts and offsets), with d=2 always valid, d=1 when these entries are affine on the cell. Fix u,z,t to constants. Each protected coordinate after the above substitution is degree <=2d in y: the section applied to v+E u is degree <=d, then multiplied by a lift entry of degree <=d. Residual coordinates Q(x)u+vbar(x) have degree <=d in y.

Let f be any degree<=k polynomial in observed coordinates. If it fails as a zero identity on any feasible tuple of this cell, choose the actual parameters u0,z0,t0 of a violating tuple. Form the Boolean polynomial
 P(y)=f(obs(y,u0,z0,t0)) product_{j=1}^a [1+(Q(x)u0+vbar(x))_j].
This equals 1 on the chosen witness, so it is nonzero, and degree(P)<=d(2k+a). Outside feasible pairs the mask is zero; the extended section causes no false witness. By Boolean subset Möbius inversion, a nonzero multilinear polynomial of degree <=D cannot vanish at every point of weight <=D. Therefore a violating tuple exists at some selector of weight <=D=d(2k+a) in the cell's free-coordinate basis (cap by dimension). This argument works simultaneously for every f, without enumerating f or the fixed u0,z0,t0.
At each sampled selector solve the ORIGINAL joint syndrome linear equation by Gaussian elimination, retaining its full affine solution fiber, or reject if inconsistent. The protected fiber is affine. Extract its exact degree-k feature span from actual lifts of free-coordinate points of weight <=k (GD03/GD05 argument). Merge all sampled bases across cells by Gaussian span. If the span missed any true feature direction, a separating coefficient vector f would contradict the preceding masked polynomial theorem. Constant feature 1 ensures exact decision as well. All retained points are genuine joint tuples; keep coefficient provenance for certified source lifts. No need to materialize the proof's masked polynomial, only the sparse grid and fiber bases.

For n free selector bits per cell, M=sum_{j<=k} binom(Dobs,j), m free coefficient bits per fixed-selector fiber, time
 T <= acquisition+closure+2^b [sum_{j<=min(n,d(a+2k))} binom(n,j)] poly(N,M) [sum_{j<=k} binom(m,j)] + charged lifts/verification.
For fixed k, T<=2^b N^{O(a+k)} with polynomial streamed peak space in N,M excluding separately charged source workspace. CM<=(T+1)S on the same sequential execution. Thus for fixed a and k and b=O(log N), exact all-feature extraction is polynomial even for t=Theta(N) unstable factors and unbounded stable syndrome rank. This is XP in a, NOT deterministic FPT in a; a=O(log N) alone gives at best quasipolynomial by this grid argument. For a=0 it contains the GD05 low-degree selector mechanism. A family of a independent optional directions active iff their selector bit is 1 and target all a directions forces weight a, so the dependence on a cannot simply be omitted from this grid argument (analytic control, not benchmarked).

## 3. Randomized FPT feature discovery, not certification (PROVED, not executed)
A nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree <=D is nonzero on at least fraction 2^{-D} of the cube (cap D by dimension). Proof by induction on variables: write P=P0+x_n P1. If P1 nonzero, at least one value of x_n is nonzero for each input where P1=1, and deg P1<=D-1; otherwise reduce dimension. Apply to the mask polynomial from section 2. For any missing identity candidate f that is not a genuine identity, there is a cell where a uniform selector has probability >=2^{-D} of a compatible violating fiber. Exact fiber feature extraction detects it. A union bound over at most 2^M coefficient vectors gives failure <=epsilon if independently sampling at least ceil(2^D (M ln 2+ln(1/epsilon))) selectors per cell; choose a fixed witnessing cell for each f in the union bound. Sampling and exact fiber computations take 2^{b+d(a+2k)} poly(N,M,log(1/epsilon)) time for fixed k; stream samples, polynomial workspace.

## 4. Deterministic rank-a Fourier decision (PROVED, not executed)
Let chi range over (V/W)^*. On all available x with v in V and external selector equations, define Z=sum_chi sum_{x: chi Q(x)=0} (-1)^{chi dot vbar(x)}. Character orthogonality gives inner sum over chi at a fixed selector equal to 2^{a-rank Q(x)} if target is in im Q(x), else zero (sum over annihilator). Hence Z>0 iff SAT, with nonnegative mass; no dependence on stable rank or number of unstable factors. Each survival equation and phase is quadratic in x. MS04 or the MS03 equation-rank character expansion gives exact 2^{a+max_chi min(q_chi,b_chi)} poly(N) streamed, with q/b measured after paid per-character affine simplification and including availability/outside-V constraints. Signed integer bit lengths polynomial because total mass bounded by 2^{a+n}; intermediate character sums and inclusion-exclusion denominators have polynomial bit length. This is a specialization/extension of MS03 to the common-core quotient, not an arbitrary protected-feature signed count or a universal FPT guarantee in a alone. Affine selector pins allow witness search by polynomially many reruns, then exact original linear fiber lift. Do not confuse this decision terminal with a complete feature basis.
