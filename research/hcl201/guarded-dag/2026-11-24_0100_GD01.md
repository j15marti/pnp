## 1. Coverage barrier, even with three-edge paths
For every 3CNF F over hub bits h, with clauses C_j=l_j1 OR l_j2 OR l_j3, construct fresh Boolean implication variables s,t and a_j,b_j for each clause. Require s=1,t=0. Add three guarded implications s->a_j, a_j->b_j, b_j->t, enabled respectively when l_j1,l_j2,l_j3 are false. Each guarded implication is a conditional 2CNF clause, with both implication arcs and their contrapositive. Guards are single signed coordinate observations. The forward graph is a parallel union of internally disjoint length-three paths, a two-terminal series-parallel graph. For fixed h the active formula is UNSAT iff some path is complete: complete path forces t=1; otherwise assign all positive vertices reachable from s to true and all others false. Then every active implication and the endpoint units hold. Contrapositive edges preserve the same constraints. Hence its SCC contradiction circuit C(h) equals NOT F(h) pointwise. This gives a polynomial many-one reduction from 3UNSAT to universal coverage C(h)=1 for every h, even with these shallow paths and full hub affine space. Coverage is in coNP since a noncovered h can be checked by ordinary SCC in polynomial time (or exhibit an extending assignment). Thus coverage is coNP-complete in this explicit class; finding a noncovered hub is NP-complete. Size O(m+n) Boolean objects, O((m+n) log(m+n)) ordinary indexed bits; dense row encodings polynomial. This proves no unconditional superpolynomial lower bound. It falsifies the idea that small reachability-DAG size, short implication paths, or simple forward-graph geometry ALONE ensures efficient coverage unless P=NP. It does not falsify a theorem on a more restricted four-valent realizable guard class without a paid embedding. Arbitrary occurrence counts and guards are allowed in this reduction. Tetravalent realization and post-cooperative-closure promises must be independently checked.

## 2. Exact row-space evaluation of guarded clauses
First run available certified unconditional closure. Normalize forbidden affine systems by elimination: inconsistent forbidden systems drop, rank-zero consistent system rejects, rank-one forbidden forces the opposite equality, substitute and repeat. Source-certified matching, SCC, integer and other modules may also contribute, with their costs charged. The experiments implement this rank-one baseline, not the full cooperative solver.

Parameterize the surviving affine ambient space x=a+Kz with independent z, include internal implication bits among z as necessary. Convert a guarded implication (Mz=b) => (u=>v) into a forbidden affine space: Mz=b, u=1, v=0 (affine literals absorbed into RHS). A guard that is an arbitrary Boolean circuit can instead be Tseitin-encoded with exact gate constraints and fresh variables, and root truth constraints; each gate becomes a constant number of bounded-rank forbidden factors. No distribution into exponential DNF. This works for exact circuits, not for incomplete obstruction messages as a decision equivalence. To decide coverage, ask for a noncovered assignment by setting the exact contradiction output to zero, or use the original guarded clauses directly without constructing cubic symbolic reachability. The SCC semantics require complementary implication edges and fixed hub assignments.

Represent each factor by a subspace W_i of the dual of GF(2)^d (row span) and a predicate on linear valuations W_i -> GF(2). A forbidden affine system disallows precisely one valuation after normalization. More generally a leaf predicate can be evaluated on each basis valuation with a charged local test; matching-derived exact predicates require additional proof/cost. Let q=max dim W_i, m=#factors. For a supplied order, U_i=sum_{j<=i} W_j, V_i=sum_{j>i}W_j, B_i=U_i intersect V_i, w=max dim B_i. A live state at i is a linear functional on B_i which extends to a functional on U_i satisfying all processed factors. Base state at i=0 is the empty functional.

Transition: D_i=B_{i-1}+W_i. Enumerate all 2^dim D_i valuations. Retain those agreeing with a live state on B_{i-1} and satisfying factor i; project onto B_i. Both restrictions exist because B_i is contained in D_i: if v in B_i, write v=u+w_i with u in U_{i-1}; v and w_i are in V_{i-1}, hence u is in B_{i-1}. Also U_{i-1} intersect W_i is contained in B_{i-1}. For any accepted valuation of D_i, take a witnessing functional on U_{i-1} of its prior state. These functionals agree on U_{i-1} intersect D_i=B_{i-1}; thus they extend uniquely to the sum U_i. Conversely each satisfying prefix restricts to an accepted transition. Induction proves exactness. At the last cut B_m=0: nonempty table iff a global functional satisfies every factor, and every such functional is the restriction of dot product by some z (extend a basis to the ambient dual). Thus no free compatibility or enumeration of global hub bits. This is the dual (observation-space) analogue of the HCL-197/198 syndrome intersection algorithm. It is not necessary to assume independent coordinate supports.

With a supplied binary factor tree, the previous peer tree-gluing proof generalizes directly: for children A,B and exterior E, store functional restrictions on U_A intersect (U_B+U_E) and U_B intersect (U_A+U_E). Join only pairs agreeing on U_A intersect U_B, then compute the parent's functional on (U_A+U_B) intersect U_E by decomposing each parent basis form into child forms. Each such component lies in its child's boundary. Agreement makes the value independent of decomposition; all accepted pairs extend. Enumerate leaves in 2^q, joins in at most 4^w. This extension is proved here but only the ordered path recurrence was executed.

## 3. Exact full affine backbone via one extra interface bit
Given a fixed supplied factor order of width w, append ONE unary predicate on an arbitrary nonzero dual direction u, requiring u(z)=b. Across each old cut the suffix row span gains at most one dimension; the intersection with the unchanged prefix gains at most one. The additional last cut has width zero. Thus the queried system has width <=w+1, leaf rank max(q,1). Local rank-one forcing is a quotient by vectors lying in individual factor row spaces and cannot increase any cut rank by the peer cut-monotonicity lemma; retain the query constraint explicitly if preferred. An arbitrary cross-factor inferred direction is not necessarily cut-monotone.

For a satisfiable system, compute one witness a using backpointers. Maintain L=span of witnessed differences z+a. For each basis vector u of L^perp, ask the above single-direction query u(z)=1+u(a). If any is feasible, recover its witness, enlarge L by >=1 and restart. If none is feasible then every solution lies in a+L, and the witnessed points already span that affine space. Hence a+L is the EXACT affine hull of all solutions, not merely a relaxation. At most d+1 rounds, each with <=d queries, each on the SAME frozen certified base with only ONE appended condition. No accumulation of rank costs in the query instances. The full resulting affine equations are an unconditional batch for the multisign ledger; renew closure after incorporating the batch, recalculate widths, do not assert arbitrary cross-cut additions preserve width. Empty base gives immediate UNSAT. This extraction is deterministic, complete for affine consequences in the explicit model, and polynomial when w+q=O(log N) and the supplied decomposition is polynomial. It does not imply universal small width, nor complete nonlinear backbone. Witness reconstruction: retain one previous state and local valuation per accepted state, trace backward, collect consistent factor-row evaluations and solve by Gaussian elimination; lift through saved rank-one substitutions and original affine parameterization. The executable below tests decisions and the coverage reduction, not backpointer or full-affine-hull extraction. These latter results are mathematical proofs pending independent implementation.

## 4. Resource accounting and discovery
Dense GF(2) bases for prefix, suffix, intersection, transition and coordinate maps use polynomial(N) bits and time (ordinary Gaussian elimination, no supplied rank oracle); conservative preprocessing O(N^6) bit operations including address overhead suffices with explicit matrices. Each transition evaluates polynomial(N) bits and uses a dictionary keyed by <=w bits (direct bit table or trie). At most m*2^(w+q) candidates; deterministic T=2^(w+q) poly(N), S=2^w poly(N), CM<=T*S=2^(2w+q) poly(N) on the same execution with retained state tables, including witness pointers, conversion, output checking and d^2 repeated queries. For a tree replace time by 2^O(w+q) poly(N); no physical CM measured. Storing explicit symbolic reachability gates adds their full polynomial size to N; computing their universal identity is not free. Polynomial terminal condition assumes supplied, verified width bound. Discovery via the known FPT subspace branch-decomposition algorithm is polynomial for fixed w, not automatically for w=O(log N). Recent Korhonen/Oum arXiv:2601.04756 gives 2^O(w^2) poly(N) oracle work with computable rank oracle; at logarithmic width this upper bound is quasipolynomial, not polynomial. Do not silently assert polynomial discovery. Rado core reduction (peer 43308a2d) may precede this decision routine; to preserve a queried boundary, independent representatives for deleted factors must be independent modulo the protected observation span plus retained core, not merely modulo core. If the stronger rank certificate fails, do not delete that region for projection. This is immediate from the peer simultaneous extension argument. Rado elimination is decision-preserving, not in general count-preserving.
