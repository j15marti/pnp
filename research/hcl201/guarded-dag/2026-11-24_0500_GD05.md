## 2. Stable projection theorem (PROVED)
Write local joint support R_i(g)=a_i(g)+J_i(g) in E x P_i, or empty, with g=h_i(x) in GF(2)^2. E includes the entire additive compatibility tag; P_i is the local protected observation space. The new hypothesis is that for all NONEMPTY colors of a given factor i, projection of J_i(g) onto E is the SAME subspace W_i, although the joint space J_i(g), protected kernel and offsets may vary freely with g. Detect equality by Gaussian elimination on projected bases; all-empty factor is UNSAT. This is a paid recognition of supplied exact fibers, not automatic source support acquisition.

Choose a fixed independent column basis D_i for W_i. For each available g and each column of D_i, solve for a lift in J_i(g). Write its protected part as columns of B_i(g). Also find a basis of K_i(g)={p:(0,p) in J_i(g)} and write it as columns of C_i(g), padded with zero columns to the maximum kernel dimension for that factor. Thus EVERY joint tag is exactly
    s_i=a_i^s(g)+D_i u_i,
    p_i=a_i^p(g)+B_i(g)u_i+C_i(g)t_i,
with free coefficients u_i,t_i; for empty colors tables may be arbitrary as long as availability is enforced. Proof: subtract the chosen lift of the syndrome projection, leaving precisely a pure protected kernel vector. Both inclusions follow. No claim of injective parametrization (padding and projection may duplicate witnesses).

Let D be the concatenation of all D_i, W=im D, r=rank D. Compute by Gaussian elimination a linear right section L:W-> coefficient space and kernel basis matrix K for ker D. Extend L linearly to E arbitrarily. Let v(x)=c+sum_i a_i^s(h_i(x)). Then x is compatible iff external affine equations, all guard availability constraints, and
    pi_W(v(x))=0
hold. All these constraints are Boolean polynomials of degree <=2: every scalar table on two bits has a unique squarefree interpolation of degree <=2, composed with affine guards; availability is exact (a forbidden color gives an indicator equation). There is NO exponential character enumeration over r. For compatible x, the entire coefficient solution is
    u=L v(x)+K z.
Substitute in all protected tables. Each protected bit is polynomial of degree <=4 in x,z,t, because the guarded B_i coefficients have degree <=2 and Lv has degree <=2. More sharply its degree in selector bits is <=4 and its degree in all variation parameters (z,t) jointly is <=1. All compatible protected tuples (including x itself if desired) are produced, and each output is a feasible tuple. The same holds for any affine combination of protected local bits. The two-bit guards can overlap or be dense; no independent selector assumption.

Contradiction alternative: if pi_W(v(x)) != 0 for a particular x, a separating linear character annihilating W certifies the failure of that selector. This is an exact quotient equation, not a conditional Fourier sign cancellation. If the projected polynomial equations have no solution, the entire original product is UNSAT. For variable projected spaces the replacement by their union is unsound: one factor with W(0)=0, W(1)=span(e), zero offsets and target e requires selector=1; union W would falsely admit 0. This is a failed hypothesis check, not a barrier to other terminals.

Every coordinate of Phi_k(F) has degree <=4k in y and <=k in v=(z,t). Evaluate F only at the Cartesian grid of y with weight <=4k and v with weight <=k, in their own independent free coordinate bases. This grid spans the evaluation vectors of Phi_k on the entire cell. Proof: for any coefficient vector a annihilating the grid, the pulled-back Boolean polynomial a dot Phi_k(F) has the two separate degree bounds. For each fixed low-weight v, its coefficients in y are recovered by the subset Möbius inversion on weight<=4k; hence all vanish. This proves equality of orthogonal complements and hence spans over GF(2). Equivalently use the usual Reed-Muller information set; self-contained proof above. No random sampling or counting oracle. Retain only a Gaussian basis of actual output witnesses per streamed union of cells; each basis row stores its actual lift parameters. Constant coordinate means empty span iff UNSAT. A proposed degree<=k equation holds exactly iff it pairs to zero with every row; a nonzero pairing returns an actual violating tuple. All degree<=k equations are the orthogonal complement.
