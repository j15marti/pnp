## 3. Theorem A: quadratic-lift reduction (PROVED)
Each scalar table entry is a degree <=2 Boolean polynomial in x because the local guard consists of two affine bits. Availability can be encoded by its 0/1 forbidden-color polynomial; outside-V equations are linear projections of v. Expand the multilinear ANF of all needed scalar table entries, availability and offsets. Form the matrix of their homogeneous degree-two coefficients (one column per distinct x_i x_j, i<j). Its rank h and basis q_1,...,q_h are computable by ordinary Gaussian elimination in poly(N). This is not a guessed optimal backdoor. Set y_j=q_j(x), xi=(x,y). Replace each original polynomial by the corresponding affine polynomial in xi. The h defining equations y_j+q_j(x)=0 are quadratic. On their exact graph, no table value has changed.

Now availability, outside-V and external affine selector constraints are affine in xi. Keep these constraints, eliminate them by Gaussian substitution, and reject if inconsistent. It is convenient to describe the rest before that substitution. Let E(xi)=E_0+sum_nu xi_nu E_nu. Define the variable stable leakage space
Rspace=span of all columns of L E_nu, nu nonconstant.
Let ell=dim Rspace. Compute a column basis R and coefficient matrices A_nu with L E_nu=R A_nu. Introduce ell new bits eta, with equations
eta=sum_nu xi_nu A_nu u.
These ell equations are quadratic; the stable coefficient formula becomes affine:
w=L v(xi)+L E_0 u+R eta+Kz.
(The constant part of E is not charged to ell.)
The remaining a quotient equations Q(xi)u=vbar(xi) are quadratic as well. Thus after all affine constraints have been eliminated there are at most m=h+a+ell quadratic equations. The observations p_i are degree <=2 polynomials in the surviving free variables because B_i,F_i,H_i,a_i^p are affine in xi and w is affine. Affine substitution preserves degree <=2.

Exactness: every original joint tuple gives compatible x,u,z,t and unique y,eta, and satisfies these equations. Conversely every solution of the lifted system fixes y=q(x) and eta exactly; reconstruct w by the displayed expression, yielding D w=v+E u by the outside-V and quotient conditions, and recover original available local tuples by the original tables. No spurious protected observations. Padding may produce multiple preimages of a tuple, so the following count is of lifted coefficient assignments, NOT the physical matching count. For decision and identity existence positive preimage multiplicity is harmless.

## 4. Theorem B: deterministic FPT affine feature span (PROVED)
Write the quadratic equations after affine substitution as H_j(z)=0, 1<=j<=m. For every additional quadratic phase/test equation P(z)=0, the number of solutions is
2^(-m-1) sum_{lambda in GF(2)^(m+1)} sum_z (-1)^(sum_j lambda_j H_j(z)+lambda_(m+1)P(z)).
Without P omit the last character. Integer character orthogonality proves equality. Exact quadratic Gauss sum: if Q=z_i z_j+z_i A+z_j B+C with A,B affine in other variables and C quadratic, summing i,j gives 2*(-1)^(C+AB). Repeat until affine; its sum is zero if a nonzero linear coefficient remains, otherwise signed power of two. Each elimination is polynomial on an explicit quadratic ANF; integer accumulator bit lengths O(number of variables+m). Stream characters, polynomial workspace, no exponentially large circuit or table. This is a self-contained proof of the counting primitive, not a black-box counting assumption.

Let Phi=(1,x,p) (or chosen affine images), dimension M. Any affine parity violation f dot Phi=1 is one extra quadratic equation. Maintain a GF(2) basis B of actual feature rows, initially empty. Compute a basis of B^perp. For each such f ask if a violating tuple exists, via the exact count above. If yes, recover a witness by fixing each free variable using the same exact feasibility count under affine substitutions; zero counts on one side permit the other side. Pins are conditional and do not increase nonlinear equation count. Verify the tuple against source tables, insert its feature row (necessarily independent), restart. If all annihilator basis probes have zero count, then every feasible feature vector is orthogonal to all of B^perp, hence belongs to B. At most M insertions and O(M^2) probes; at most polynomially many additional pin queries. Feature constant 1 detects UNSAT exactly. Return actual witness basis and a certified identity basis B^perp. Every zero claim follows from exact arithmetic, not random sampling.
A four-color guard restriction is two affine equations, so guarded identity and reachability queries reuse this bound. A conjunction of additional quadratic tests costs one character bit per test. No claim that arbitrary disjunction coverage or arbitrary circuits are easy. For degree-two features the same argument applies IF the protected observation maps themselves are affine in surviving lift coordinates; generally quadratic protected maps yield quartic probes, which are not covered.

### Probe-local improvement (PROVED, not executed)
Decision requires only h for the feasibility tables and a; it need not introduce eta, since w is reconstructible and protected values are unconstrained. For an affine protected probe let its coefficient on w be b(xi)=b_0+sum xi_nu b_nu. Write w=w_aff+R lambda(xi,u), with lambda=sum xi_nu A_nu u. The term b_0 R lambda is already quadratic. Only the remaining products (xi_nu b_nu R lambda) require auxiliary bits. Choose a basis H_f of the rows b_nu R and introduce eta_f=H_f lambda, at most ell_f=rank{b_nu R}<=ell quadratic equations. The probe becomes quadratic in eta_f. Recover all w from original lambda afterward. Therefore per-probe exponent can use a+h+ell_f+1; no need to materialize all eta for each query. This requires computing the actual probe-specific row rank and charging it. No uniform logarithmic ell_f guarantee.

## 5. DO01 adversarial control (PROVED representation-specific)
A monotone NAE triple on x_i,x_j,x_k can be encoded by one two-bit affine guard (x_i+x_j, x_i+x_k) forbidding 00, with no syndrome or protected fiber restrictions. Its forbidden polynomial is
(1+x_i+x_j)(1+x_i+x_k)=1+x_i+x_j+x_k+x_i x_j+x_i x_k+x_j x_k.
For a linear 3-uniform hypergraph each pair appears in at most one edge. Each edge therefore has three quadratic monomials disjoint from those of other edges. The quadratic coefficient vectors are linearly independent: h=m_edges on this original guard representation. Here a=ell=0, yet h grows linearly or faster on DO01 critical families. Thus small unstable rank alone cannot guarantee this lift terminal. High h is not a runtime lower bound; different representations, common-isotropic methods, nonaffine global reasoning, and exact conjunction reductions remain eligible. This is a precise bridge from DO01's explicit owner-row family to a pure guard model, not a proof of strict four-valent physical realization. The Fano test checks h=7, full UNSAT, and each deleted-edge neighbor SAT (10 labeled solutions). General NAE complexity is known via Schaefer's dichotomy; no new unrestricted separation is claimed.
