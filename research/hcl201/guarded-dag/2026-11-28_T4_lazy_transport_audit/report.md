## Exact result
Let B(v,z) be a fixed complete bucket with positive tails A_i and negative tails B_j, P(z)=AND_i A_i, Q(z)=AND_j B_j. Then B(0,z)=P and B(1,z)=Q. For any explicit consumer guard G(v,z) and exterior E(z), exact projection is
E(z) AND ((P(z) AND G(0,z)) OR (Q(z) AND G(1,z))).
Choose v=0 when first disjunct holds, otherwise v=1; check original bucket/guard. This is an O(M+|G|) Boolean circuit with two guard cofactors and shared tails for one pivot, not a guaranteed compact CNF after repeated elimination. For outside-only guard, projection commutes with exterior/guard conjunction. A fixed bucket has <=p*q distinct parent-pair corrections, permanently valid across arbitrary exterior changes with matching signed parent identities. Each correction from a relaxed model falsifying both tails is new because that model satisfied every active correction. This bank does not pay for repeated solving, arbitrary exterior contexts, rollback or changes of bucket. If a guard touches pivot, unrestricted projection may lose witness extension; keep the two cofactors or restore incidence. NO under weaker guard stays sound under stronger guard, even when exact response reuse fails. Signed complement swaps the two flags with the corresponding signed map. No new universal acquisition theorem.
