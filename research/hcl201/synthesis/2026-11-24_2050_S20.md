## A. Exact theorem (PROVED)
For every explicitly indexed list of independent owners i=1,...,m with nonempty finite option list A_i, every option selects exactly one coordinate j and contributes a tag g in GF(2)^r. Option identities and actual source lifts are registered. Quota k_j counts owners choosing j. Require l_j <= k_j <= u_j and k_j=l_j mod 2, with nonnegative binary-encoded endpoints; require XOR tags=t. More generally normalize no-choice/idle as an actual coordinate 0, including distinct tags on idle alternatives. If the real coordinate parities are fixed, idle parity equals m minus their sum modulo 2; clip its unconstrained interval accordingly. All integers k_j <= m, so clipping endpoints is polynomial and does not expand binary magnitude. If a supplied quota has no consistent endpoints, reject. An arbitrary guard, two-hot option, non-unit signed contribution, or correlated owner choices is NOT silently encoded.

Let L=sum_i |A_i|. Make one vertex o_i per owner and a distinct port p for every option, with o_i--p edge carrying its actual tag. For each coordinate j let d_j be number of its ports. Clip u_j downward to min(u_j,d_j) with parity l_j, reject if u_j<l_j. Create d_j-u_j mandatory slots and u_j-l_j optional slots. Connect every port for j to every slot for j; connect all optional slots into a clique. No other edges; slots and internal edges carry zero tag.

Proof: every perfect matching selects exactly one owner-port edge for each owner. At coordinate j the d_j-k_j unselected ports must match distinct slots. All d_j-u_j mandatory slots have no other possible partner, so d_j-k_j >= d_j-u_j. Total slots d_j-l_j imply d_j-k_j <= d_j-l_j. The remaining k_j-l_j optional slots must match each other, so their number is even. Conversely for any legal owner word, match its selected owner-port edges, fill all mandatory slots with unselected ports, use enough optional slots for the remaining unselected ports, and pair the unused optional slots arbitrarily. This constructs an actual matching preserving exactly the option tags. Duplicate options have distinct ports. Internal multiplicities vary; no count preservation. Graph simple, O(m+L) vertices and O(L^2) edges, construction and decoder polynomial. Pins remove disallowed options before compilation, or force the designated owner edge. A source lift follows registered option witnesses; if witness acquisition isn't paid, theorem is conditional on that input.

## B. Arbitrarily many redundant tags, at most one independent extra channel (PROVED)

Gaussian elimination on augmented [H;G] detects inconsistent linear necessities. If consistent, choose independent G rows modulo row(H). When rank increase is zero, all tag requirements follow from owner and quota parities; ordinary perfect matching decides. When it is one, every other tag equation is a certified combination of H and a representative additional row, with compatible RHS. ## D. Sharp boundary: two-hot choice is NP-complete (PROVED)
Reduce perfect 3-dimensional matching with three sets X,Y,Z each of size n and allowed triples T. Owner x selects one allowed (y,z) with x fixed; its option contributes 1 to each of two coordinates y,z (one in each of Y,Z). Require odd occupancy at every coordinate, with no other tags or constraints. Total occupancy exactly 2n, and 2n odd coordinates require >=1 each, so every coordinate is occupied exactly once. Legal word iff perfect 3DM. Optional idle choices do not change equivalence since any idle loses two contributions. Membership in NP immediate. Thus one-hot arbitrary arity parity intervals plus a rank-one extra channel is in P, but the natural two-hot parity-only extension is NP-complete. This is an explicit option-model barrier, not a proof that every strict native network contains such an obstruction or a lower bound against arbitrary machines. General exact counts and two independent residual tags are not resolved by this proof.

RT20 marked cycles with two marked same-orientation owner choices have exactly two legal all-ones occupancy assignments, both tag 0: total quota forces every owner nonidle and one selection at each vertex forces uniform orientation. Our compiler resolves both labels on ANY graph in the one-hot domain, not just cycles, without bounded batch tests or a supplied tree. The unrestricted all-one graph characterization without tags in RT20 remains a cheaper special case. Multi-hot 3DM is a direct falsifier of universal extension. Other falsifiers checked symbolically: idle parity, interval holes, duplicated option identities, arbitrary hidden cross-guards (excluded), variable internal multiplicity (not counted).
