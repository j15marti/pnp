## 1. Exact robust cut theorem
For every explicitly encoded directed multigraph on V, let C be indexed unit switches with values epsilon in {0,1}^r, R recourse arcs with integral upper capacities u and lower zero (shift known lower bounds), A signed incidence (head +1, tail -1), b integral with sum zero. All extra constraints must be represented, fixed, or separately retained. Recourse has zero protected tag in a verified gauge; each switch has an independent binary label for exact full-support equivalence. A fixed external flow is absorbed into b. Fixed nonbasis arcs are absorbed into b and tag offset. No claim about arbitrary dependent labels or arbitrary nonaffine guards.

The robust property is forall epsilon exists integral x_R: A_R x_R=b-A_C epsilon, 0<=x_R<=u. For any S subset V, write R_in(S) for incoming recourse capacity, C_out(S) for number of switches leaving S, b(S)=sum b_v. Define
    F(S)=R_in(S)-C_out(S)-b(S).
Then robust property holds iff min_S F(S)>=0.
Proof: for fixed epsilon, bounded integral demand flow is feasible iff d(S)<=R_in(S) for all S, d=b-A_C epsilon. This follows by adding a super-source at negative-demand vertices and super-sink at positive-demand vertices, applying max-flow/min-cut and integral augmentation; complementary S handles lower net cut condition. Max over epsilon of d(S) is exactly b(S)+C_out(S), attained by setting all leaving switches to 1, entering and other switches to 0. Universal quantifiers commute. A violating cut gives an explicit missing switch word, checked against the original demand cut. If all cuts pass, every word lifts with a single ordinary integral flow. With independent tags, this is equivalent to full affine fiber support, not a hull surrogate. General S09 independent switch basis contraction is sound when its other controllable tags lie in the absorbed span and its baseline is preserved; our exact recognition applies to the fixed-basis subproblem, not selection of the basis itself. For independent integer external demand intervals represented by additional signed incidence directions, the same formula uses interval widths in C_out after shifting lows; exact for the whole independent box, only sufficient for a correlated subset. Failure for an outer box cannot reject a real target.

## 2. Deterministic signed interaction cover terminal
Let w_uv=sum capacity of R arcs u->v minus count of C arcs v->u. With x_v=1 for v in S,
    F(S)=sum_(u,v) w_uv (1-x_u)x_v - sum_v b_v x_v.
For each unordered pair u<v set c_uv=w_uv+w_vu. Its two directed terms equal
    c_uv (1-x_u)x_v + w_vu(x_u-x_v).
Collect all linear terms. Negative directed coefficients are NOT in themselves the obstruction; only a negative pair sum is. Construct the undirected conflict graph with edges c_uv<0. Take endpoints Z of a maximal matching, a vertex cover with k=|Z|<=2 tau(conflict graph). Enumerate all 2^k memberships of Z. Every remaining two-free-variable term is nonnegative. Substitute fixed vertices, converting incident terms to constants/unaries. A nonnegative pair term is an ordinary directed cut arc u->v. For unary h x_v, h>=0 use source->v with capacity h; h<0 add constant h and v->sink capacity -h. Convention: membership 1 is sink side. One mincut per membership therefore finds the exact minimum F and a minimizing S. No optimization of the cover or demand rank required. k=0 needs one mincut even with arbitrarily many independent demand directions. Proof covers negative unary values, nonzero b, parallel and antiparallel arcs. Signed pair graph and original witness map retained.

Time O(2^k poly(N)) including bit-length arithmetic, deterministic cover construction, preprocessing and witness or violating-cut extraction; streamed S=poly(N). Edmonds-Karp supplies a polynomial augmentation bound independent of capacity magnitudes. If k=O(log N), recognition polynomial; general k not claimed small. This is not a 2^k bound for arbitrary tagged-flow target decision outside the independent-basis recourse model. In fact here target decision for a particular word is one maxflow even when *full-support recognition* is hard. Prefer the cheaper fixed-target path when it is enough; do not universally require saturation recognition.

## 3. Exact coNP-completeness of unrestricted full-support recognition
Reduce complement of Simple MAX CUT (unweighted). Given graph G=(V,E), threshold K>=1, ask maxcut(G)<=K. Trivial thresholds can be separately handled, so K<=|E|. Add vertices s,t. For each undirected edge add two opposite indexed unit switches, each with its own independent tag. Recourse: s->v and v->t of capacity M=|E|+1 for every v in V, t->s capacity K. All demands zero. For S containing s but excluding t, only t->s enters from recourse and C_out(S)=|delta_G(S intersect V)|. Hence F(S)=K-|delta_G(S intersect V)|. For any other nontrivial S, at least one capacity-M arc enters S: both outside with a vertex inside, both inside with a vertex outside, or s outside/t inside. Therefore F(S)>=M-|E|>0. Empty and full cuts give 0. Thus min F=min(0,K-maxcut(G)), and robust iff maxcut<=K. The complement has a polynomially verifiable violating cut, so full-support recognition is coNP-complete.

All capacities can be expanded to polynomially many distinct parallel unit arcs: 2|V|M+K recourse arcs, 2|E| switches. Every arc in that unit-expanded system has attainable 0 and 1 when K>=1: zero circulation is feasible; each recourse arc can be used in an s->v->t->s cycle; each switch u->v can be used with v->t->s->u. Thus ordinary coordinate-range closure can be wholly stagnant. Switch tag support includes zero and each unit vector, hence full affine hull. More strongly any assignment pinning at most K switch bits extends by setting other switches zero and routing each selected switch with its return path through t->s (at most K units, enough capacity M). For the star family with |E|=r and K=r-1, every projection onto <=r-1 switch coordinates is full while a full word is missing. This is a limitation of scalar/bounded joint support probes, not a lower bound on arbitrary decision algorithms. In the hard reduction all original budget systems are SAT (zero flow), every specified word is polynomially decidable, and no strict E4/B4 compilation or hardness transfer is claimed. The unit expansion and general reduction are symbolic; scaled tests use aggregate capacities.
