## Theorem A (uniform slack certificate, PROVED)
For every finite explicitly encoded directed multigraph with indexed arcs E=H disjoint union K (loops and parallels allowed), signed incidence A (net inflow), integer upper bounds u, demands b, and additive binary tags g_e in GF(2)^q, consider Ax=b, 0<=x<=u, x integral. Let H be a candidate removable set. Choose an undirected spanning forest T of H, including isolated vertices as components. Contract H components to obtain a graph Q on K, retaining all arc IDs, including loops. Aggregate demands into b_Q. Let Y={y integral: A_Q y=b_Q, 0<=y<=u_K}. If Y empty then original budget domain empty. Otherwise choose any y0 in Y via bounded integral flow. No source independence assumption beyond exact flow encoding.

For every tree arc e, let S_e be the side of its component tree cut containing the original head. Define c_e(y)=sum_{v in S_e} [A_K(y0-y)]_v. These are signed integer linear forms plus constants. For every y in Y the vector d(y) supported on T with d_e=c_e(y) satisfies A_H d=A_K(y0-y): component sums are zero by Q feasibility and tree cut equations uniquely determine the correction. Edges outside T have d=0.

Compute m_e=min_{y in Y} c_e(y), M_e=max_{y in Y} c_e(y) with two min-cost integral flow optimizations per tree edge on Q; coefficients are 0,+1,-1, even if contracted loops have nonzero cost. m<=0<=M. Solve one bounded integral flow on H at demand b-A_K y0, with tree bounds
  1-m_e <= f_e <= u_e-1-M_e
and non-tree bounds 1<=f_e<=u_e-1. If no such f, return UNKNOWN for this certificate, not UNSAT. If found, then for every y in Y, z(y)=f+d(y) is an actual integral H completion with margin at least one on EVERY H arc. Conversely every original feasible x projects to Y. This is a sufficient certificate for uniform interior recourse. It is also necessary for a single fixed chord-vector tree-recourse policy with that forest and margin; not necessary for arbitrary adaptive recourse.

## Theorem B (exact tag quotient, PROVED)
Choose vertex potentials h_v in GF(2)^q by h_root=0, h_v+h_u=g_e on T. For each arc e=u->v define g'_e=g_e+h_u+h_v. Tree tags become zero. Put W=span{g'_e:e in H minus T} and offset a=sum_v (b_v mod 2)h_v. For any original x, its tag equals a+sum_e (x_e mod 2)g'_e, because incidence conservation cancels vertex potentials.

For each y in Y, the complete fiber tag support is EXACTLY
  a + sum_{e in K}(y_e mod 2)g'_e + W.
Inclusion follows from zero tree tags and chord labels in W. For reverse inclusion, take the certified z(y). Every desired adjustment in W is a linear combination of fundamental H cycle tags, found by Gaussian elimination. XOR their edge incidence vectors; resulting undirected even edge set admits Euler circuit orientations. Give each traversed edge signed unit change relative to its original direction, including loops. This is an integer circulation d with the chosen parity, |d_e|<=1. Adding it to z(y) preserves margins/bounds and all demands, and realizes the requested tag. Retain original chord preimages and source arc IDs. Do not replace actual flow fibers by an affine hull without Theorem A.

Consequently original target t is feasible iff contracted Q admits y with projected tag pi(sum_K y_e g'_e)=pi(t+a), pi quotient by W. Witness reconstruction uses the stored f, tree cuts, Gaussian preimage and Euler orientation; output original flow then S07 source lift. This is exact for arbitrary K capacities, arbitrary q and arbitrarily many H cycles. It is decision/witness, not a counting reduction. Does not preserve extra unencoded observations; add them to the protected tag dictionary before construction. A fixed guard restricting feasible arcs must be included before testing certificate. New source restrictions require certificate revalidation. No arbitrary-mask promise.

## Falsifier and nonaffine scaling family
Naive midpoint at one y fails: two H arcs 0->1 of capacity 2 with tags 01,10; two thin arcs same endpoints capacity 1 and zero tag; net inflow demand 2. Baseline H=(1,1), K=(0,0) has unit slack. At K=(1,1) H is forced (0,0), not all tags from W={00,11}. Incorrect fiber tag 11 rejected by exact enumeration. The uniform certificate refuses this H because the tree correction range exceeds available slack. A failed certificate is not a proof of overall UNSAT.

For each r>=1, H has r+1 parallel 0->1 arcs of capacity 8, tree tag zero and r independent chord tags. K contains unit 0->2 arcs with tags 00,01 in two new coordinates, and unit 1->2 arc with tag 10. Set demands (-4(r+1)-1,4(r+1),1). One undirected cycle block (all edges variable), original beta=r+2, no whole-block integral midpoint (unit arcs), tag hull rank r+2. Contract H to two vertices, exactly one of three unit arcs must be chosen. For each y, take chord flow 4 and tree flow 4+y_{1->2}; lies in [4,5]. Thus W of dimension r is exactly absorbable. The original support is W times {00,01,10}; missing quotient 11 lies inside full affine hull. Quotient beta=2 for every r, so M08 terminates after at most 4 parity checks. This is a strict escape from whole-block interior and rank-one peeling as stated, not a hard family for all algorithms: direct exclusivity, combined tag pins and small local matching may also solve it.
