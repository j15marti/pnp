For an explicitly registered positive linear four-perfect-partition ternary NAE source, A and B are the two pairs of partitions. Fix the SAME authenticated guard G, if any (or empty), source variables and owner-pair registry E containing every pair in every clause. Define d_uv(x)=[x_u != x_v], and P_A=conv{d(x): x satisfies A and G}, P_B similarly, P_AB from all owners. Empty convex hull is empty. For each actual clause e, V_e(d)=1-(d_uv+d_uw+d_vw)/2 is 0 for a legal word and 1 for a monochromatic word. It is affine, nonnegative on all Boolean cut vectors and their convex hulls. P_A intersect P_B = P_AB = P_A intersect {L_B=0}.
Proof: every joint model projects into both. If p in both, L_B(p)=0. Write p as a convex combination of genuine A/G words. ## Unconditional extension-complexity obstruction on easy genuine sources
For t>=2 form groups indexed 0,...,t. For each unordered pair i<j allocate fresh occurrence bits u_ij in group i, v_ij in group j and dummy a_ij, and a color-0 clause (u_ij,v_ij,a_ij). For each group connect its t occurrence vertices in a path; each adjacent link (u,v) has fresh dummy b and a clause (u,v,b), alternating colors 2 and 3. Color 1 initially empty. All partial partitions are disjoint within color: occurrence vertices have at most one link of either color; all dummies fresh. Linearity holds because links join distinct target clauses, target clauses disjoint, and any two link clauses share at most one vertex. Add <=2 isolated vertices to make total divisible by three. Apply DS16 lem:ds16-complete, exactly two 21-vertex templates per missing color triple, preserving every old word that satisfies the partial owners and retaining all existing pair IDs. This constructs genuine linear perfect four partitions of O(t^2) vertices, explicit input O(t^2 log(t+2)) bits. Each group bit vector beta freely extends to a full joint satisfying word: set all group occurrences equal beta_i, each dummy opposite its first endpoint, use fixed filler word. Thus the instance is trivially SAT with an explicit witness. Complement also supplies no unconditional singleton pins.

Consider the face of P_A defined by sum of d_uv on the link pairs =0. Every link coordinate is nonnegative, so every positive-weight atom on this face has equal copies along each group path. Hence its target-pair coordinates form exactly the complete-graph cut vectors on t+1 group bits; converse follows from the explicit full joint lift for every beta. The argument holds identically for P_B and P_AB (all target and link pair IDs are registered even if the owner is in the other side). For i,j=1,...,t, set d_ii=0 and map to X_ij=(d_0i+d_0j-d_ij)/2. At every atom X_ij=(beta_i XOR beta_0)(beta_j XOR beta_0). All t-bit words occur. The affine image of this face is precisely CORR(t). The extension complexity of a face or its affine image is at most that of the original polytope (restrict an extension to the preimage face; equations need no additional inequalities). Kaibel--Weltge, arXiv:1307.3543, theorem 2.1, xc(CORR(t)) >= (3/2)^t. Thus each of P_A,P_B,P_AB has extension complexity at least (3/2)^t, superpolynomial in explicit source bit length. This is an unconditional barrier to a universal explicit polynomial-size linear extended formulation of the FULL authenticated pair-moment hull, even with arbitrary real coefficients and even on these easy SAT sources. It is not an unrestricted time/space lower bound, not a PSD or arbitrary nonlinear/factored representation bound, not a lower bound for targeted zero-face decisions, and not a claim about residuals after all possible exact projections. A solver may bypass the whole polytope, as matching itself illustrates. No claim that the constructed family has supplied one-mismatch witnesses or remains hard after all closure.
