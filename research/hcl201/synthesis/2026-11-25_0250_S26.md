\begin{lemma}[Paid rank-one private block elimination]\label{lem:s26-dual}
Parameterize the actual consistent affine guard, and apply certified rank-zero/one closure. On the free chart write coordinates $(u,z)$, where $u$ has dimension $h>0$. Suppose every live forbidden packet has private coefficient rank at most one, and all other consumers are independent of $u$ (or exactly included in the registered packets). Every private-rank-one packet can be put by row operations into the form $G_i(z)=g_i$, $a_i u=\beta_i+c_i z$, where $a_i\ne0$. Avoiding it imposes $a_i u=1+\beta_i+c_i z$ precisely when its activation equations $G_i(z)=g_i$ hold. Rank-zero packets remain. Put $b_i=1+\beta_i$. For every binary left-kernel vector $\lambda\ne0$ of the matrix with rows $a_i$, the following affine exterior packet is forbidden:
\[
  G_i(z)=g_i\quad(i\in\operatorname{supp}\lambda),\qquad
  \left(\sum_i\lambda_i c_i\right)z=1+\sum_i\lambda_i b_i .
\]
Avoiding all such packets, together with the unchanged rank-zero packets, is exactly the existential projection. Every accepted exterior word lifts by Gaussian elimination, without preserving counts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $z$, an active owner's private forbidden value is one linear equation and its avoidance is the complementary equation. Inactive owners impose nothing. The active system is inconsistent iff some subset of active rows has XOR-zero coefficient and XOR-one right-hand side, by ordinary Gaussian elimination with tracked row combinations. Extending that subset's indicator by zeros is exactly a displayed dependency, and conversely each displayed forbidden packet certifies an inconsistent active subsystem. Inconsistent activation equations and empty affine packets may be deleted; a whole-space forbidden packet certifies contradiction. For a consistent system solve the actual active equations, reconstruct the original affine guard parameterization and verify every original owner. Conditional activations are not globally forced facts.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Block-distance-three escape and explicit-cover obstruction]\label{lem:s26-barrier}
There exist rank-one private projection instances on which no nonzero private direction affects fewer than three owners, yet the preceding rule eliminates a linear-dimensional private subspace with only linearly many candidates. Conversely, there are polynomial-size rank-one private block instances, all satisfiable and stagnant under single-packet affine closure, on which exact explicit forbidden-affine-packet projection onto the designated exterior requires exponentially many packets.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the first assertion take a binary Hamming code of length $m=2^k-1$ with parity-check columns all distinct nonzero $k$-bit vectors. Its kernel has dimension $m-k$ and minimum distance three (no one or two columns sum to zero, and a column pair and their sum give three). Use a generator of that kernel for $u\mapsto(a_i u)_i$, and forbid $z_i=0,\ a_i u=\beta_i$ with arbitrary constants. The private direction supports have size at least three, while the left-kernel dimension is $k$, so at most $m$ candidates suffice. This is a supplied protected private subspace statement, not a claim that DS20 cannot exploit some exterior direction.

For the second assertion use $s\ge3$ row vectors $a_1,\dots,a_s$ consisting of a basis of dimension $s-1$ and its sum, so the sole nonzero dependency on distinct rows uses every row. Duplicate each owner twice with independent activation bits $z_{i,0},z_{i,1}$; an active copy requires $a_i u=b_i$ with $\sum_i b_i=1$. The original forbidden packets are $z_{i,j}=0,\ a_i u=1+b_i$. All have rank two, and setting all activation bits to one makes the source trivially SAT. Every nonzero private direction changes an even positive number of distinct row values, hence affects at least four owners. The exact exterior rejection relation is
\[
 \prod_{i=1}^s\{00,01,10\},
\]
since inconsistency occurs iff every distinct row is activated by at least one copy. Its cardinality is $3^s$. An affine flat contained in it has affine projection onto each pair of dimension at most one, because the projection omits $11$. Its cardinality is therefore at most $2^s$, since its pair projections jointly identify every word. Any explicit union of forbidden affine flats giving this rejection relation needs at least $\lceil(3/2)^s\rceil$ members, even allowing arbitrary affine directions and overlap. This is a designated-projection representation lower bound, not a runtime lower bound or a universal portfolio counterexample. The rejection relation itself has a short factored Boolean representation and a simple decision escape. It refutes a universal cap for this particular exact explicit projection, not every alternative representation.
\end{proof}
