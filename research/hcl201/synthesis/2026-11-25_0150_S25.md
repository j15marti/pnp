## PROVED: coherent forest-closure support (bounded actual-variable scope)
Fix integer k>=6 and a raw signed NAE-3 registry whose Berge girth is >k, with ordinary distinct variables in every triple. For S of <=k actual variables, grow C(S) by including every triple with at least two present vertices (and adding its missing vertices) until stable. Count even all-present triples as edges. The construction starts with |S| isolated vertices. Every newly included edge meets >=2 distinct previous components: otherwise a preexisting path between two of its vertices, together with the new edge, forms a Berge cycle of length at most |S| (inductively <=|S|-1 prior edges). Each step reduces components by >=1, adds at most one vertex, and creates no cycle. Thus <=|S|-1 edges, <=2|S|-1 vertices for nonempty S; C(empty)=empty. This also proves induced triple forest, monotonicity C(S) subset C(T), and independence of processing order (least fixed point).

Let P(S) consist of assignments on S extendable to all triples induced on C(S). It is nonempty, and P(T) projects EXACTLY to P(S) whenever S subset T, |T|<=k. Restriction is immediate. For extension, start with a legal coloring of C(S). Every extra owner in C(T) touches <=1 old vertex by closure. Delete old variables from the forest incidence graph; every remaining owner has >=2 free neighbors. Root each remaining tree at a free variable. Choose its bit, and for each owner choose child bits satisfying signed NAE with the parent and any fixed bit; at least one free child exists. This extends every old coloring. A raw row survives on its triple; any compatible pair of raw rows survives on its scope union: after including the two pinned edges, the closure forest can add at most one bridge joining them, and if such bridge exists it has a new free third vertex (linearity and no cycle). All unary assignments and all assignments to two variables survive.
