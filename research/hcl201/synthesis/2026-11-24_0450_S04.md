## 2. Discoverable two-adhesion terminal (PROVED)
Let G be any explicit finite simple graph, the primal graph of a fixed-domain finite CSP with polynomially checkable local constraints (scopes induce cliques; all global restrictions included in the primal graph, not silently omitted). Let s be the minimum achievable maximum bag cardinality among tree decompositions whose adjacent bags intersect in at most two vertices. No supplied decomposition required. Algorithm: recursively find a vertex separator S of size 0, then 1, then 2, in current structural graph H with >3 vertices. Split into two nonempty sides A,B of H-S; in each induced child H[A union S], H[B union S] complete S to a clique. For |S|=2 only proceed after failure of 0/1 separators. Stop at <=3 vertices or no such separator. Fill edges are STRUCTURAL ONLY, never disequality constraints. For each split recursively glue the two resulting bag trees at bags containing S. Such bags exist because S is a clique (edge coverage or a vertex). All original edges covered; running intersection follows because the sides meet precisely in S. All original constraint scopes lie in a bag by the Helly property of connected subtrees of a tree; verify decomposition explicitly. Empty input handled directly.

Proof of size guarantee: the class admitting bag cardinality <=s and adhesion <=2 is minor closed. Delete vertices/edges from the decomposition; contract uv by replacing both endpoints by one vertex. The endpoint subtrees intersect since uv is an edge, so union connected. Across any tree edge, new vertex cannot appear on both sides unless at least one old endpoint was already in the overlap: otherwise endpoints would lie in disjoint strict sides, contradicting the common bag. Thus adhesion and max bag size do not grow. With a 0/1 separator, children are induced minors. With a 2 separator in a 2-connected H, each component of H-S touches both separator vertices; a path through the opposite side realizes the fill edge by contractions, so both children are minors. Hence every child inherits the promise. A >3 vertex leaf with no separator <=2 is 3-connected. In a tree decomposition of adhesion <=2, remove redundant leaf bags contained in their neighbor. If >1 bag remains, some leaf has a private vertex and its neighbor side also has a vertex outside their separator (or prune contained bags throughout by contracting a containment tree edge); this would be a <=2 separator. Therefore every 3-connected leaf fits in one promised bag. Small leaves have size <=3. The discovered maximum bag size w <= max(3,s), independent of which minimum-size separator is chosen.

Termination: potential max(|H|-2,0). For every split, child sum <= parent; splits producing a child of zero potential decrease it by at least one (parent >3). Positive leaves at most n-2; zero-potential leaves at most n-2; binary tree has O(n) nodes (small n handled directly). Naive BFS over O(n^2) candidate cuts per node gives O(n^3(n+m+ n^2)) conservative polynomial work, including structural fills; O(n^3) conservative storage. No exponential search for decomposition order. For each bag, enumerate local assignments over D colors; attach exact child separator messages (<=D^2 states), verify all actual constraints, and keep supported states. T=D^w poly(N), polynomial peak space with streaming; root empty means UNSAT; witness via stored actual local choices. In pure 3-coloring a two-port message reduces to equality/disequality support (four possibilities: neither, equality, disequality, both) by global color symmetry. Keep all D^2 states if labels or pins break symmetry. Local proof of empty cells is conditional on separator pins; only unconditional at root or after verified global propagation. This is a classical separator-DP route with explicit discovery guarantee, not a novelty claim for graph decomposition itself.

RT04 Hajós chain: one bag of 21 original vertices for each seed, connector bag {a,b,d} at each join (identified a=c, removed ab/cd, inserted bd), intersections {a,b},{a,d}; outgoing edge remains in newest seed bag, so induction yields a tree of bags with adhesion <=2, bag size <=21. Edge deletion preserves this decomposition. Thus every RT04 chain and all one-edge deletion neighbors are solved in polynomial time without the supplied join history, seed recognition or minimization of RT04 rank parameters. This is a general structural bypass, not refutation of RT04's correctly scoped bounds. RT03 grid obstruction not claimed resolved by this terminal.

## 3. Protected polynomial feature closure (PROVED)
For any fixed degree d, encode each local color by a fixed number of bits, total bit count l. Let E_d(x) consist of all squarefree monomial evaluations through degree d, INCLUDING the constant 1, so M=sum_{j=0}^d binom(l,j). For each separator assignment keep a GF(2) basis of evaluation vectors of all feasible subtree assignments; retain an actual witness for every selected basis vector. Not just the affine hull of colors. Empty span iff no assignment because of constant coordinate.

Join two conditioned subtrees: fixed separator values agree. Assign each shared coordinate to exactly one side (project out from other side; projection on monomial coordinates linear). For disjoint remaining variables, a monomial in union factors uniquely as a left monomial times a right monomial. Thus E_d(union) = B(E_d(left), E_d(right)) for a fixed bilinear map B (constant coordinates handle monomials on only one side). Every feasible pair has image in span of at most M^2 products of input basis witnesses, by bilinearity. Each product is itself a feasible actual joined witness. This proves exact span, not a relaxation. Bag assignment fixed: begin with its singleton witness, successively join each child basis for its compatible separator cell. Union over bag assignments by linear-span union; reduce incrementally, never discard an actual selected witness. Induction yields root span of the full feasible set. Test any polynomial p of degree <=d for identity p=0 by pairing coefficient vector with the basis. Failure yields an actual counterexample. All identities obtained by orthogonal complement. Both values of any Boolean p can be tested by p and 1+p; all pairwise implications between degree-e observations via identities of degree <=2e (d=4 for quadratic observations). This avoids Fourier signed mass or arbitrary guard enumeration for this structural class. It does NOT compute signed masses or #SAT, nor allow arbitrary extra global constraints to be added without changing the primal graph. For observations on source PM witnesses, require verified bounded-degree pullback or local encoding; do not infer full physical feature preservation from decision-only equivalence.

At most M independent witnesses per cell; each child join considers <=M^2 pairs, each evaluated/reduced in O(poly(l,d) M^2) bit work. T<=D^w poly(N,M) including discovery, checks, all bag enumeration and repeated joins; conservative factor O(n D^w M^4 poly(N)). Space polynomial in N,M for O(n D^2) tables, actual assignments, bases, and temporary streamed products. For fixed d and D, w=O(log N) implies deterministic polynomial total work and peak space; cumulative memory <=(T+1)S in a bit-step realization. Basis dimension cannot exceed M; no branch recursion beyond charged local enumeration. Exact available feature closure is returned unconditionally from root, and any derived identity can be retained without changing solution set; conditioning must remain guarded. Full cooperative matching/Fourier/geometry closure not executed. Proof does not rely on those channels being complete.
