## A. Safe finite-group exactification (PROVED)
Input N is the encoded authenticated independent block system, supplied binary factor tree, all side constraints and local decoder/guard manifests. A local state contributes an integer vector q_i, binary tag s_i, and nonnegative integer multiplicity. Explicit states or paid S14 acquisitions are allowed. Count bit lengths and local acquisition costs must be charged. Require sum_i q_i=b and XOR_i s_i=t. If a quota has no contributor check b_a=0. For each remaining quota subtract b_a in one contributing leaf. Choose B_a=sum_i max |q_ia|+|b_a| from certified bounds and M_a a power of two strictly greater than 2 B_a (M_a=1 allowed for zero bound). All shifted partial totals lie in [-B_a,B_a], inject into Z/M_a, and a root residue 0 is equivalent to exact integer zero. Never replace a binary-encoded large quota by a unit-cost residue array. Its M_a factor is explicitly charged. Shift binary target by decomposing t as sum of elements in leaf tag spaces (Gaussian elimination, reject if impossible). This leaves all local linear spaces unchanged. Thus the residual is exact zero-sum compatibility in G=F2^r x product_a Z/M_a. A modular zero without the bound is unsound: actual total 8 and modulus 8 is the simplest counterexample.

For each tree side J define H_J=U_J x product of quota groups with at least one registered contributor in J; U_J is span of its leaf binary tag spaces. Other quota coordinates are 0. H_J is a safe superset of attainable subtotals, not their support. Retain only subtotal in H_J intersect H_out, using actual counts. This automatically verifies every completed quota exactly before forgetting it, and applies the binary outside-span filter. A leaf with omitted local constraint, decoder, or nonlocal guard is not authenticated. A guard that is a mask of one actual table key can be applied before/after a join. An arbitrary guard on a pair of child states does NOT commute with convolution: even for Z2 with f=g=(1,1), a guard accepting only (0,0) changes the zero coefficient from 2 to 1. Such guards need their own exact representation or the algorithm must decline.

## B. Mixed common-fiber join (PROVED)
At a trivalent node let A,B,C be binary tag spaces for the three incident disjoint leaf sets. Let R={(x,y,z) in A x B x C: x+y+z=0}, t0=dim R, D=A intersect B intersect C, d=dim D. Gaussian elimination gives these and the quotient R/D^2 of dimension t0-2d, where D^2 denotes {(u,v,u+v):u,v in D}. Choose one ambient linear complement to D, not unrelated local representatives, so quotient triples sum to zero in that complement. Enumerate its kernel basis combinations, NOT the full Cartesian product of child quotient domains.

For each quota coordinate record which of the three sides contributes. A coordinate present in only one side must be 0 in its boundary message. If exactly two sides contribute, choose one residue; its opposite is forced in the other side by the zero-sum equation. If all three contribute, leave this coordinate inside the common-fiber group
  H = D x product_(a present all three) Z/M_a.
At fixed binary quotient triple and fixed pair-sector residues, f and g are actual joint tables indexed on H. Their contribution to the outgoing message is exactly the cyclic convolution on H (reverse sign on the outgoing group key when using the zero-sum convention). The common representative guarantees no binary carry, and cyclic integer addition is the exact group law, not XOR. Every compatible pair belongs to exactly one enumerated fiber. Hence summing these convolutions produces precisely the S14 joint join, with all correlations preserved.

Let K_A=2^w_A product_(a meeting A and its complement) M_a, w_A=dim(A intersect (B+C)); similarly K_B,K_C. Let Q2 be the product of moduli of coordinates present on exactly two sides, Q3 the product for all three. Empty products are 1.
  Number of fibers = 2^(t0-2d) Q2.
  Common group cardinality = 2^d Q3.
  E = 2^(t0-d) Q2 Q3.
A tensor radix-two FFT (Walsh on D) computes each convolution in |H| poly(N) bit time, under the exactness construction below. Input/output table capacities <= E by w_A<=t0-d (I15) and coordinate inclusion. Thus join time E poly(N), scratch+input+output space max(K_A,K_B,K_C) poly(N); group size <= every incident K. All failed fibers are charged. This is a dense capacity bound, not a guarantee of improvement over sparse enumeration; choose the cheaper certified execution with charged selection.

I15's pair-transfer dimension proof gives t0-d <= (w_A+w_B+w_C-d)/2. Since pair quotas enter two K's and triple quotas enter three,
  E <= sqrt(K_A K_B K_C / (2^d Q3)).
In particular for three identical boundary groups K with common group size K, E=K rather than K^2. The conservative moduli may enlarge S14's local interval capacities; do not claim a universal speedup relative to a tighter sparse or interval implementation. No new universal tree discovery theorem is claimed.

## C. Bit-exact transform, not a floating-point oracle (PROVED symbolically)
All cyclic orders are powers of two. Tensor radix-two transforms require O(|H| log |H|) butterflies, including Walsh axes. Coefficients are integers bounded in absolute value by B with log B polynomial in encoded matching size (or charged separately for supplied weights). Use complex fixed-point dyadic arithmetic; approximate each root of unity to absolute error at most 2^-p by rational Taylor evaluation of sin/cos with a certified pi approximation (Machin arctan series is sufficient), and round each real component after a multiplication. The FFT has depth log2 |H| for each complete transform, and intermediate exact absolute values are at most |H| B forward, |H|^2 B^2 after pointwise multiplication and |H|^3 B^2 during inverse before division. In a butterfly, error grows by at most 4 times prior error plus a constant times 2^-p times the bound on intermediate magnitude (assuming prior error <=1). Three transforms and the intervening multiplication therefore have a conservative absolute error bound 2^-p (16 |H|)^12 (B+1)^4 before rounding, by iterating this recurrence and bounding the pointwise product error by (2|H|B+1) times the maximum input error. Set p=100(1+ceil(log2 |H|)+ceil(log2(B+1))) so the error remains <1/4 throughout; divide by |H| and round to the nearest integer. All precision, roots, intermediate bit lengths and scratch are polynomial in N and log |H|, assuming charged coefficient bit bounds. This gives uniform deterministic exact integer convolution, including signed coefficients if desired; no random prime or unverified numerical sign. The executed backend below instead uses a fixed NTT prime with a small-case no-overflow bound. General dyadic FFT and general Gaussian quotient discovery were not executed. A fixed prime cannot certify arbitrary positive support (a count divisible by the prime aliases zero); that backend is restricted to the checked count range.

## D. Bidirectional closure, replacement, reconstruction (PROVED)
Compute messages in both directions of each edge using B, on the complete fixed registry. At leaf i, its environment E_i is exact outside subtotal count on its actual interface. Then for any authenticated local state a, the full extension count is f_i(a) E_i(-a) (with zero for excluded coordinates). All impossible states are therefore certified in one scan once environments are acquired. For a replacement table g_i with same registered observations and bounds, exact global mass is sum_a g_i(a) E_i(-a). This is the joint integer/binary analogue of DP10, and gives batched one-region substitution without recomputing other regions. A replacement removing a constraint may invalidate earlier root-derived pins; keep variant guards separate, use unpinned original environments or rebuild; no reuse by summary alone.

First run applicable ordinary matching/range/Gaussian closure, after every deduction and at stagnation before any guarded trial. Once exact messages are acquired, use the environment scan as complete support closure over the supplied local state registry. A zero-support state can be deleted unconditionally in its parent context; repeat closure after each deduction. At most the number of registered table entries can be removed, or at most polynomially many actual edge/coordinate pins when using local re-acquisition; cost is charged. No arbitrary semantic inference is assumed free. Conditional decisions retain local key/edge guard. Closure pinning restricts existing states and does not expand the registered group spaces; new nonlocal observations must be charged and widths recomputed.

Fast convolution does not provide a child-pair backpointer for every output key. For SAT lift, at a selected positive parent key enumerate ONE child boundary and look up the uniquely required opposite child key in saved tables. Nonnegative exact counts ensure a positive pair exists. Descend once at each tree node, then invoke paid local S14/M15 lift or explicit row witness. No quadratic pair materialization or signed cancellation shortcut. Reconstruct and verify all original integer sums and binary tags, plus authenticated source decoder separately. Counts are indexed matching/adapter counts, not necessarily source #SAT.

With L local acquisition total, fixed tree, per-node E_v and per-edge K_e, one two-pass execution has
 T <= poly(N)[L+sum_v E_v+sum_e K_e],
 S <= poly(N)[input+local retained tables+sum_e K_e],
 plus charged local lift, coefficient arithmetic and variant costs. Restart closure and all permitted support probes incur their explicit number of reruns; under polynomial local acquisition and K_e these are polynomial. Save tables once per directed context and charge all consumers, root scans, failed tests, masks, reconstruction, guard IDs, and adapter accesses. DAG/trace size bounded by charged tables/jobs; no semantic cache merging. CM <= (T+1)S on same serial realization, not measured. Sum over all conditional guards separately unless exact shared execution established. DP10's forward-only bound does not cover reverse sparse packet expansion; here dense registered K_e bounds both directions by definition. This theorem does not supply a cap on the number of externally created guards.
