## 2. Uniform basis recourse theorem (PROVED)
Choose an arc set H to contract, K=E-H. Contract each undirected connected component of H into a quotient vertex. Let P be the bounded real integral flow polytope on K with aggregated demand, original bounds and loops retained. Empty P rejects. Within each H-component choose a connected spanning recourse subgraph R and let C=H-R. Components handled independently for each fixed y in P. Find vertex potentials h_v in F2^q such that g'_e=g_e+h_head+h_tail=0 for EVERY recourse arc. This is equivalent to zero tag sum on its cycle space; solve by tree traversal and verify all arcs. The total tag is a+sum_e (x_e mod 2)g'_e, with a=sum_v (b_v mod 2)h_v. Fixed nonzero-tag H arcs are permitted in C and contribute constants. Define W=span{g'_e: e in C, l_e<u_e}. Select an independent basis B from these actual arc labels by Gaussian elimination, with r=dim W. Select a baseline z_e for each C arc and signs sigma_e in {-1,+1} for e in B, such that z_e and z_e+sigma_e are within bounds; other C arcs stay at z_e. Baseline need not be a feasible full flow until recourse checked.

Assume the following certificate, separately for each component:
for EVERY integral quotient y and EVERY epsilon in {0,1}^r there is an integral recourse flow f_R with
 A_R f_R = b_H - A_K y|_H - A_C z - sum_{e in B} epsilon_e sigma_e A_e,
 l_R<=f_R<=u_R.
Then for EVERY integral y the entire tag fiber is EXACTLY
 a + sum_{e in K}(y_e mod 2)g'_e + sum_{e in C}(z_e mod 2)g'_e + W,
with W the sum of the component subspaces (offsets summed). Proof: all original completions give a tag in this coset because R has zero gauged labels and differences on variable C arcs lie in W; fixed C differences vanish. Conversely any required W difference has a binary preimage over the selected basis labels (across components use concatenated bases). Set the switches accordingly and solve bounded recourse flow. Both signs toggle the parity of the arc. Thus the selected state exists with its exact target tag, even if the switches share bottleneck capacity or physical vertices. No counting or arbitrary pin-support preservation. Project K tags and offset modulo W, retain full original arc IDs, potentials, basis preimages, z and sigma. Lift by a Gaussian preimage and one recourse flow per component. Recheck all original guards in authenticated domain. This is exact existential elimination, stronger than a lower coset at one baseline.

## 4. Exact polynomial discovery for a parallel bundle (PROVED; executed)
Let H consist of parallel arcs between two vertices, oriented either way. Orient the net-flow coordinate from vertex 0 to vertex 1; s_e=+1 for forward, -1 for reverse. Let external quotient demand across H range over [h_min,h_max], with extrema obtained by min-cost flow on the quotient. Choose ANY common original binary tag g0 for R (all arcs of that class), gauge h_0=0,h_1=g0. Thus R tags vanish. C is the complement. For each arc use signed range [min(s*l,s*u),max(s*l,s*u)]. Let [C_min,C_max] be the sum of signed ranges on C, and [R_min,R_max] on R. Choose any basis of nonfixed gauged C labels, rank r. For each selected arc choose sign sigma=s_e so its switch increases the signed net flow by 1, whether forward or reverse. Choose baseline signed values from the original signed range except that each basis arc's upper endpoint is reduced by one. All integers in the resulting sum interval [C_min,C_max-r] are attainable as a baseline net L; use greedy interval filling, and convert signs back to original flow. The possible signed C contribution after switches is [L,L+r]. Every external demand and every switch choice has a completion in R iff
  L >= h_max-R_max  AND  L <= h_min-r-R_min.
Combining with baseline existence gives the EXACT criterion for this directed-increasing switch scheme:
  max(C_min,h_max-R_max) <= min(C_max-r,h_min-r-R_min).
Proof: the extreme combinations are actual (h_min with all bits one, h_max with all bits zero) and demand minus switch net is an integer; all integers in the R signed interval are achievable by independent bounded parallel arcs. Conversely every baseline in the intersection provides recourse at all such demands. Hence any basis of the same span gives the same interval criterion, with no rank-minimization problem, no exponential search over switch words or baselines. Fixed arcs included correctly in intervals and offset. Enumerate at most |H| distinct tag classes, rank and test each, choose maximal certified rank, retain actual witness baseline. Group parallel arcs by unordered endpoints to discover H candidates in polynomial time (can include all arcs in the group; revalidate after contraction). This is not an exact characterization of all saturation mechanisms or arbitrary subgraph choice; failure of all classes is UNKNOWN.
