## Conditional theorem: protected per-input coverage

Fix a uniformly generated list of k(N)<=K N^a partial solvers and a single fixed transition model on finite binary tapes. Each lane includes source parsing, checking, lifting, and output verification in its own computation. Each accepts only independently checkable correct YES or NO terminals; it may also suspend/return UNKNOWN. Give every lane a protected copy of its initial source and persistent execution; optionally run additional import-consuming replicas, but never require them to overwrite a protected baseline. Put checking, import validation, and communication into resumable paid lanes, not blocking calls. No external oracle. Fix a deterministic round-robin order. If for every x of length N some protected lane reaches a correct checked terminal within B(N)<=C N^b of its own primitive bit steps, then at most k(N) B(N) primitive simulated steps suffice. The solver does not need to know the winning lane, C, or b. A uniform machine can implement the schedule with explicit configuration tapes and scanning, without unit-cost random access: after H simulated steps each initial configuration plus written bits is O(N+H) (fixed finite descriptions, or charge polynomial total descriptions G(N)); total live representation O(G+kN+H). Scanning the entire serialized configuration store per transition gives a conservative O((G+kN+H+1)^2) time upper bound per simulated transition, including index/counter arithmetic, thus O(G+kN+H*(G+kN+H+1)^2) total with H=kB (and polynomial cumulative space via time*peak space). This is deliberately loose, uniform, and polynomial under the hypothesis. A fair protected-lane schedule can include a polynomial number of bounded cooperative/verification jobs as additional lanes with their own finite steps; if dynamically spawned, limit their total number by a fixed polynomial or use reserved slots. No free resets. For a cooperative-only winner, require coverage in the **actual** schedule, including the arrival/validation of all dependencies, not existence of a favorable message order.

For NO-only coverage, every NO must have a checked rejection by a known fixed polynomial deadline and every YES must be safe from rejection; only then may timeout mean YES. Without that theorem timeout is UNKNOWN. For a fixed clock B, the uncovered residual class is the set of exact source/guard/residual states on which no protected lane has a checked terminal within B and no certified globally charged contraction is acquired by the prescribed cooperative schedule. A restricted method's refusal is not evidence of membership in NP-complement or of unrestricted hardness. First OPEN arrow: prove this class empty for an explicit polynomial B and all reachable states, or add a paid complementary lane and prove coverage. A priori choosing the fastest lane is unnecessary. A useful stronger next test is a bounded nonrenewable root-credit adaptive selector with protected lanes; charge its failures and representation growth. The theorem does not assert B exists.

## New exact rational pair-overlap result and constructive repair

On RANDOM, group all identical scopes by intersection of their allowed words. For each of 183 scopes, choose a full parity coset contained in the group support and put mass 1/4 on each of its four words. Every pair of overlapping groups was checked by integer cross multiplication: 3574 pairs, no inconsistency (32500 named checks). Hence a feasible rational marginal packet exists for the pair-overlap local LP. This is a **survival certificate**, not a native assignment. It falsifies the proposed universal pair-LP infeasibility-only module on this NO instance. For JOINT the particular parity-coset packet disagrees on 155 overlaps; unrestricted pair-LP status UNKNOWN, not infeasible. 1979 comparisons, 44942 named checks.

For width-three factors with distinct scopes, each full parity coset has uniform marginal on every proper subset. Thus if a parity coset survives every grouped constraint, all pair intersections of size <=2 agree automatically. This proves a family of exact relaxation witnesses, without a solver. A stronger regional relaxation can enforce correlations absent from pair intersections.

Original chart root ports are zero-based. First two local distributions have probability one on their parity equations, so any common joint distribution forces x33 XOR x49=1. The third assigns mass 1/2 to words 0 and 3 with x33 XOR x49=0. Therefore these three particular marginals have no joint extension. This is an exact proof via support, not floating point, found after 399 candidate third-scope tests. Does not imply the unrestricted three-region LP is infeasible; alternate marginals might work. This is a concrete dependence across scopes, not an unconditional parity identity of original constraints.
