\section{Span-rank credit with exact table continuations (S23)}\label{sec:s23}
DS17 (Theorem~\ref{thm:ds17-one-shot}) rules out treating joint one-mismatch acquisition as a free primitive. DP18 (Lemmas~\ref{lem:dp18-seam} and~\ref{lem:dp18-credit}) and RT23 motivate a different charged representation: retain a joint interface as one explicit relation, and use the dimension of the entire observation span, not the sum of individual ranks. This is exact bucket-style elimination on algebraic observations, not a new unrestricted SAT algorithm.

\begin{lemma}[Span-rank seam credit]\label{lem:s23-span}
After actual affine guard absorption, permit owners given by independent observation rows and an explicit accepted-word table, or a single forbidden word on independent rows. Let $U,V$ be the observation spans of a partition $A,B$, let $I=U\cap V$, $a=\dim U$, $d=\dim I$, $t=\dim V-d$, and $r=\dim(U+V)=a+t$. If $t>0$ and
\[
2^d(t+1)^4+(a+1)^4<(r+1)^4,
\]
then exact recursive acquisition of $B$'s interface table followed by one continuation satisfies the aggregate credit with potential $\mu=1+r$ and fixed exponent four. This is a conditional dispatch rule, not a universal availability theorem.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each of $Q=2^d$ seam words, impose the corresponding independent affine equations, perform certified closure, and decide $B$ in that actual chart. The quotient of its entire observation span has rank exactly $t$ before further closure, hence at most $t$ afterward. Retain a witness for every positive answer; UNKNOWN is not a negative answer. If no words are accepted reject. Otherwise replace all of $B$ by one table on $I$, with the acquired accepted words. Because $I\subseteq U$, the continuation span has rank at most $a$; no extra span rank is introduced. Gluing is precisely the functional argument of Lemma~\ref{lem:dp18-seam}: two witnesses agreeing on $I$ define a functional on $U+V$ and extend by Gaussian solution; verify the original chart and guard. The stated strict integer inequality pays all $Q$ occurrences and the continuation. It also implies $Q<(r+1)^4$, so the actual table and its retained witnesses have polynomial size in the original input. The number of owners along each path does not increase: replacing a nonempty $B$ by one table cannot increase it. A table under a further affine guard is restricted by enumerating its existing accepted words, checking consistency with the affine image, and re-expressing them in independent quotient coordinates. This does not require enumerating unregistered words. A forbidden-word owner can instead be restricted by Gaussian consistency and rank reduction. Local affine-hull closure of a table is computed from its registered accepted words, with all deductions reclosed; an empty table rejects. No new ambient variables are required. Across recursively covered charts the root credit bounds occurrences, each explicit table has polynomial size, and every stored witness/map and consumer is charged. Thus bit time, peak space and cumulative live storage are polynomial on the same serial realization, provided discovery, all remaining terminals and per-occurrence work satisfy the uniform bounds of Theorem~\ref{thm:aggregate}. Preflight precedes dispatch; no uncharged fallback from an incomplete recursive acquisition is permitted.
\end{proof}
The tempting replacement of $t$ by the original sum of owner ranks minus $d$ is FALSE. For two rank-one owner spaces $\langle e_1\rangle,\langle e_2\rangle$ and interface $\langle e_1+e_2\rangle$, each owner retains rank one after quotienting, although the global span loses one dimension. Our first checker failed on this inference; the corrected reference found 26 such rank-mass failures among 240 cases. This is not a defect in DP18, which deliberately charged its query children at the original rank mass.

\paragraph{Direct acquisition special case.}
If $d+t\le C\log_2(N+2)$ for one fixed $C$, enumerate functionals on $V$ directly, evaluate all $B$ owners and record the interface table and witnesses. This costs $2^{d+t}\operatorname{poly}(N)$, without recursive calls. For $t>0$, one continuation strictly decreases global span rank. A connected genuine positive linear four-partition source has an initial variable-star elimination with at most eight neighbors, and, if the remaining observation graph is connected on the other vertices, $d=7,t=1$. This is exact on the given source, not a guarantee that later tables have bounded width. Keeping the table avoids DP18's forbidden-packet expansion; expansion back to native owners requires a separate charge. Neither a table's independent row basis nor the rank credit by itself bounds subsequent seam width. U-PROGRESS remains the requirement that every actual nonterminal residual has a paid move or terminal under one fixed uniform policy. A balanced full-query split with $a,t=\Theta(r)$ and unbounded $Q$ again defeats any fixed power of span rank.

## Scope and audit
This is a focused conditional proof, not a final manuscript audit. DS17 ordinary acquisition is NP-complete; this new rule supplies paid progress for some actual cuts without presuming a common solution. Universal table-language progress, strict native adapter, universal correctness and unconditional COST remain open. DISJ: exact AND/projection/glue only; no claim arbitrary disjunction is free. Relativization, algebrization and Natural Proofs: no unrestricted separation or collapse claimed. DTISP: same serial realization only conditional; configuration counting not invoked as a lower bound. The first attempted rank-mass bound failed and was corrected; 26 counterexamples in final checker. The unused gates counter fields in raw results are zero-initialized placeholders, not a numerical gate sweep. Closure at root and after accepted deductions, all failed conditional branches guarded; local closure is not semantic closure. No native replay, integrated recursive solver, kernel, measured CM or local preflight executed.
