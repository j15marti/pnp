## New lemma A: plateau-directed exact resolution

Flipping its variable repairs at least E. Let R,B be exact repaired and broken clause counts. Plateau implies B>=R>=1. Every broken clause D has -p as its unique true literal under a. Resolving E,D on p gives a clause false under a: all remaining literals from both parents false, hence cannot contain a complementary pair. Its width <=|E|+|D|-2.
