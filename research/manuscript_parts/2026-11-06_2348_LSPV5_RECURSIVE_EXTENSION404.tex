\paragraph{Recursive-extension parity control (LSPV-5/404).}
For Boolean $x_1,\ldots,x_k$ over a field of characteristic different from two,
fresh acyclic definitions
\[
y_1-1+2x_1=0,\qquad
y_i-y_{i-1}+2y_{i-1}x_i=0\ (2\leq i\leq k),\qquad
2e-1-y_k=0
\]
give $y_i=\prod_{j\leq i}(1-2x_j)$ and make $e$ the even-parity indicator.
Thus the expanded $2^k$-monomial ordinary-PC guard has an exact linear-size
factored representation whenever recursive extensions are legal.  A Boolean
prefix-XOR circuit similarly uses $k-1$ fresh gates, four proper ternary clauses
per gate.  This is an upper-bound control, not a historical $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$
accepted-byte proof or a lower-bound-preserving source transfer.  The full mixed
minimum, same-system B3, and P versus NP remain open.
