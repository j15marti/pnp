\paragraph{REFEREE463 (LSPV-5, 2026-11-09 08:44 PST; proved/restricted plus computational).}
For the common-literal abstract domain, with
$\alpha(S)$ the literals true in every state of $S$ and
$\gamma(K)$ the states satisfying every literal in $K$, alternative branch
reconvergence has the exact polarity
\[
\alpha(S_0\cup S_1)=\alpha(S_0)\cap\alpha(S_1),\qquad
S_0\cup S_1\subseteq\gamma(\alpha(S_0)\cap\alpha(S_1)).
\]
Unioning branch-local must-facts instead computes conjunction of their
concretizations and can create a false bottom.  Indeed,
$(\neg x\lor y)\land(x\lor\neg y)$ has models $00,11$; the $x=0$ and $x=1$
branches entail $\neg y$ and $y$, respectively, whose conjunctive pool is
empty.  Independent Python and JavaScript implementations checked all 225
ordered pairs of nonempty subsets of the two-variable universe and found 168
bad-pool coverage failures.  This is an analysis-layer join theorem, not a
historical $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ split/discharge rule or all-rule cost lower
bound.  Complete source/context/paid-history transformers, mixed
superpolynomial growth, noncircular B3, and P versus NP remain open.
