\paragraph{A support-distinct Resolution control.}
There is a proper signed $3$-CNF on exported bits $x,y$ and ten private
variables whose twelve unsigned source supports are pairwise distinct, whose
projection is exactly $x\oplus y=1$, and from which the two CNF facets
$x\lor y$ and $\neg x\lor\neg y$ have ordinary Resolution derivations using
five nodes each.  Thus the valid four-source collision theorem cannot be
promoted to a general collision requirement after arbitrary Resolution.
This does not authenticate any historical LOCAL-CONVERT operation and gives
no all-rule lower bound or B3 implication.  In the narrower proper $2$-CNF
core class with at most one signed clause per unsigned support, six clauses
are necessary and sufficient for unsatisfiability: Tarsi deficiency reduces
the $m\leq5$ case to at most four variables, and exhaustive enumeration of
all $11{,}656$ such signed formulas finds none unsatisfiable.
