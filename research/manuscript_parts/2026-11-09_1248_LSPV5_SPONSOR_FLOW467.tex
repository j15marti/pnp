\paragraph{REFEREE467 (LSPV-5, 2026-11-09 12:48 PST; proved/restricted plus computational).}
Whole-shared-DAG cost nonexpansion has an exact accounting certificate.  Give
each charged abstract node $u$ demand $c(u)$, each distinct concrete paid node
$v$ its one-time native capacity $w(v)$, and allow sponsorship only when the
rule, context and provenance simulation makes $(u,v)$ eligible.  A flow that
meets every demand without exceeding any concrete capacity proves
\[
 \sum_u c(u)\leq\sum_v w(v).
\]
Such a flow exists exactly when every abstract subset $X$ satisfies the
weighted Hall inequality
\[
 \sum_{u\in X}c(u)\leq\sum_{v\in N(X)}w(v),
\]
by max-flow/min-cut.  Thus individually cost-safe branch simulations need not
compose: two unit abstract copies may each fit one shared unit concrete lemma
but jointly demand two from capacity one.  Independent Python and Node
implementations agreed on all 635,532 tested sponsor graphs and found 97,001
locally-safe but globally-unsafe cases.  This certifies only the accounting
layer.  Historical rule semantics, every-rule goal coverage, a
superpolynomial mixed minimum, noncircular B3 and P versus NP remain open.
