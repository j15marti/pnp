\paragraph{Context-typed affine union repair (restricted).}
For two complementary affine assumptions $A_0+A_1=1$, let $q$ count
distinct inference vertices in one acyclic union DAG, where every vertex has
an immutable parent tuple and a branch scope.  If a common vertex has no
branch-assumption ancestry, tagging each row by the parity of assumption uses
and replacing $r_v$ by $r_v+\tau(v)A_b$ retracts each branch with at most one
inference per original inference vertex.  Two tag-one terminals derive
complementary rows and one additional XOR yields contradiction, for a bound
of $q+1$.  Equal affine labels with different parents or incompatible branch
contexts are distinct vertices and cannot be deduplicated.  Native
$R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ simulation, full-calculus lower bounds, and B3 remain open.
