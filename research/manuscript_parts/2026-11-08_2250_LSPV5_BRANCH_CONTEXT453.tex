\paragraph{Explicit branch contexts and source covers (restricted).}
In a sound source-explicit acyclic proof DAG, label each node $\Gamma\vdash C$.
A split on $x$ is an AND hyperedge requiring both
$\Gamma,x\vdash\bot$ and $\Gamma,\neg x\vdash\bot$ before deriving
$\Gamma\vdash\bot$.  Any node shared by the two branches must have a proved
context contained in their intersection, hence contained in $\Gamma$; a
branch-local lemma cannot leak to the incompatible sibling branch.  The set
of original sources in an empty-context false root's ancestry is necessarily
unsatisfiable.  Thus every semantic source-cover bound remains valid under
legal branching and reconvergence.  With $r$ required original sources, $q$
paid distinct nodes, and maximum inference indegree $a>1$, connected ancestry
gives $r+q-1\le aq$, hence
\[
q\ge\left\lceil\frac{r-1}{a-1}\right\rceil.
\]
This is a theorem for an explicit control calculus, not a new case-split or
discharge rule in historical $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.  Historical byte
authentication, the all-rule superpolynomial transfer, and B3 remain open.
