\paragraph{Context-typed affine multisplit retraction (restricted).}
Fix affine queries $A_1,\ldots,A_k$ and valid binary-XOR refutations in
all $2^k$ contexts $A=b$.  In one acyclic context-typed union DAG, let $q$
count distinct paid inference vertices with immutable parents.  Tagging every
branch row by its assumption-ancestry vector and adding back the tagged
assumptions maps each false terminal to an unconditional affine row $U_b$
with $U_b(b)=1$.  Thus the equations $U_b=0$ exclude the whole cube and are
inconsistent.  Their augmented vectors lie in dimension $k+1$, so at most
$k+1$ rows span the constant contradiction and at most $k$ additional binary
XORs join them.  Hence the common affine source has work at most $q+k$.
This does not authenticate historical mixed rules, adaptive branch DAGs, B3,
or a full-calculus lower bound.
