For each variable fiber v, a candidate ternary word has symbol multiplicities a_v,b_v,c_v and M_v=q!/(a_v!b_v!c_v!). There are prod_v M_v words of prescribed types. ## B. Exact forest local closure (PROVED)
For any residual satisfying ordinary pin closure, every nontrivial owner has either three unpinned variables (NAE) or two unpinned variables (one pinned, giving a binary clause excluding one equal pair). Let R be any subset with forest incidence graph. R has full affine hull on its unpinned coordinates, constructively: for any coordinate v, root its tree at v. At each incident root owner with two children choose opposite child values; at an owner with one child and one pinned coordinate choose child opposite to the pin. Both root values now satisfy all its incident owners. Recursively extend each child subtree with parent value fixed, choosing at least one child opposite the parent for a ternary NAE, or a satisfying child for the binary clause. Trees have disjoint descendant sets so no conflict. Satisfy other components similarly. The two full assignments differ only at v; all unit differences occur. Isolated coordinates are free. Consequently no nonzero affine equality can follow from R and the current pins; every nonzero linear observation on the free coordinates takes both values on actual models of R. This proves exact local affine closure saturation, not just rank slack. Gives linear scan per forest witness, polynomial acquisition if requested. Girth>2s makes any <=s-owner residual region a forest (pinning/deleting cannot create an incidence cycle). Hence adding all exact bounded-region affine deductions to N4FN05 does not increase its closure on these sources.
