## Theorem ES15A (PROVED): exact cut-rank connectivity
Let G=(V,E) be an edge-bit factor graph (parallel edges allowed, no loops required); each actual bit lies on exactly one edge joining two factors. Each factor has a finite explicit relation R_v. Compute its affine hull by differences of its listed rows; conjoin these LOCAL affine equations on actual IDs. Empty relation or inconsistent affine system rejects; otherwise L=x0+W is a sound relaxation. Additional certified row deletions allowed; recompute hulls and Gaussian elimination after deletions until fixed point. For each edge let b_e be its coordinate functional on W. For S subset V let U_S be the span of all b_e incident to S, C the crossing edges, and r(S)=rank{b_e:e in C}. Then
r(S)=dim(U_S intersect U_(V-S))=dim U_S+dim U_(V-S)-dim W.
Inclusion of crossing span in intersection immediate. Conversely let h be in intersection, and w in W with zero boundary. Split w into w_A supported on internal A edges and w_B supported on internal B edges. Every homogeneous equation is local at a vertex, so both split vectors satisfy all equations and belong to W. On w_A, the representation of h using B incident bits vanishes, and on w_B the representation using A bits vanishes. Hence h(w)=0 for every w in kernel of boundary projection. The annihilator of that kernel is exactly span of crossing coordinate functionals (Gaussian elimination), proving reverse inclusion. All edge functionals span W*, dimension dim W. Affine offsets irrelevant.
Function f(S)=dim U_S is submodular since U_(S union T)=U_S+U_T and U_(S intersection T) subset U_S intersect U_T. Thus f(S)+f(V-S)-dim W is symmetric submodular. The actual physical crossing rank, not just proxy, is therefore submodular. No row sparsity, connected coordinate supports or bound on rank required. In raw NAE4 the local EQ hull gives independent stars, while cap and guarded graphs can give arbitrarily correlated forms.

## Theorem ES15B (PROVED): mass-sensitive exact rank objective acquisition
For disjoint fixed anchors A,B of two vertices each, restrict S to contain A and avoid B. Then alpha*r(S)+beta*|S| is integer submodular (alpha>0,beta>=0). A rank evaluation is polynomial Gaussian elimination; use deterministic polynomial value-oracle submodular minimization (Iwata/Fleischer/Fujishige, arXiv:math/0004089, JACM 2001 DOI 10.1145/502090.502096; their strongly polynomial theorem). This is an ordinary polynomial algorithm with paid rank oracle, not SAT oracle. Enumerate O(V^4) anchor pairs to require nontrivial shores. Exact minimum nontrivial rank in P even if logarithmic or larger. Fixed K>=1, mu=V-2, u=a-1 for smaller shore size a>=2, v=mu-u. K-admitted if 2^r u^K<=mu^K. Enumerate guessed u0=1..floor(mu/2), r0=0..floor(K log2 mu) passing integer admission and anchor pairs; minimize u0*r(S)+r0*|S|. True witness's probe yields u0*r'+r0*(|S'|-1)<=2u0*r0. If r0=0 get rank0. Otherwise r'<=2r0, u'<=2u0. Since L'=log2(mu/u')>=1 and >=log2(mu/u0)-1, obtain r'<=4K L'. Return any cut passing exact 4K-admission check; if none, no K-admitted seam exists in current local chart. This is bicriteria, not exact cardinality-constrained optimum. O(V^5 log V) minimizer calls, each polynomial with exact integer ranks and polynomial bit coefficients. No sparsity bound from FN10 needed. SFM theorem is used, not implemented in this audit.
