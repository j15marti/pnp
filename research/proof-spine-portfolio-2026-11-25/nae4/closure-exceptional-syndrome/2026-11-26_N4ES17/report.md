## ES17A | Exact owner response, without pair-saturation (PROVED)
Partition actual variables into disjoint blocks. An internal clause is contained in one block. A 2+1 crossing clause has unique pair-owning block; a 1+1+1 clause remains an explicit NAE factor. Define E_B as distinct actual variables in B which appear in another block's owned clause or in a retained 1+1+1 clause. Define Z_B as distinct actual foreign singleton IDs in clauses owned by B. Local unary guards on B are included in its internal system. Other retained explicit guards must be on exported variables; mark additional variables as exported if needed. Cross-block 2-CNF guards can then be retained verbatim; arbitrary guards cannot be discarded or silently approximated. Define R_B on E_B union Z_B: an assignment (e,z) is admitted iff there exists an actual B word with exports e satisfying every internal clause and local pin, AND every crossing clause owned by B when evaluated at the actual foreign singleton values z. Store a witnessed B word per admitted row. Distinct repeated foreign IDs share one coordinate, never independently chosen. This definition includes internal clauses, nonfull projections and conditional pair requirements, not merely pairwise marginals.

Necessity: any physical model projects to a row of every R_B, satisfies retained 1+1+1 factors and retained guards. Sufficiency: choose a global model of the exact R_B factors and retained constraints; for each B, lift its selected row using the recorded B word. These B words are disjoint and preserve every exported value; thus each external singleton used in a local word's test equals its actual value in the lifted parent. Retained clauses and guards hold by export preservation. Hence exact existential elimination, both directions, with polynomial-size witness map under the bounds below. No requirement that a local pair be simultaneously unequal, no assumption of symmetry or affine hull. ES16 full saturation gives a special case in which R_B is independent of Z_B. Nonfull R_B can nevertheless be bijunctive, as in FN13.

Source acquisition: try each of four omitted partitions, compute components of the other three via union-find, check actual scopes. All crossing clauses belong to the omitted partition, so each variable occurs in at most one crossing clause. With no extra export guards, |E_B|+2|O_B|<=|B|, where O_B is the list of owned crossing clauses; |Z_B|<=|O_B|. Thus |E_B|+|Z_B|<=|B|, and enumeration of all local B words and foreign singleton patterns costs at most 2^(3|B|/2) times polynomial work (allow weaker 2^(2|B|) if extra exports). For fixed C and all |B|<=C log2 N, all tables, witnesses and failed enumeration costs polynomial in N. Even an arbitrary supplied partition of raw degree-four source into log-size blocks gives |Z_B|<=2|B| and scope <=3|B|, still fixed polynomial. Explicit cross-guard sizes are paid and must meet any required terminal conditions. No universal existence of such blocks is asserted.

## ES17B | Contextual binary-support terminal (PROVED)
Given actual explicit response factors R_i on original shared Boolean coordinate scopes V_i, build H(R) by adding one unary/binary clause for every absent one-/two-coordinate pattern of each R_i, and retain any explicit 2-CNF guards. Empty table gives NO. Every actual joint model satisfies H(R). If H is contradictory by implication SCCs, reject, soundly. If a model of H satisfies all actual tables and guards, lift and verify, soundly. Otherwise for each current table row r, test H conjoined with pins for every coordinate of r. Delete r only if this is 2-CNF UNSAT. Rebuild H and repeat. Every deletion is sound since an actual parent model containing r would itself satisfy H and the pins. A failed query's conditional contradiction discharges only the exact row, not another guard. At most M strict rounds for M initial rows. All retained rows remain original attainable local words (actual witness stored). At a fixed point a missing tuple test gives an exactness certificate: if every absent full tuple on each factor scope is incompatible with H by SCC, then every H model satisfies every actual factor; if a test is SAT it produces an actual global relaxation model violating that factor. Thus this is a deterministic exact recognition of equivalence of H and the current explicit network, not a free general equivalence oracle. Requires enumerating all full scope tuples, paid under log scope bound. If H SAT but no verified original witness, and no certified complete contradiction, return UNKNOWN. In particular, row support fixed point is not global compatibility.

For admitted table scopes O(log N), total rows and total full scope patterns polynomial. Each H has O(sum_i |V_i|^2) clauses plus guards, built by row scans. At most M+1 rounds of <=M row pin probes, each ordinary 2SAT (deterministic SCC); optional absent-pattern recognition adds polynomial queries. Thus fixed polynomial total, even including failed partition candidates, ID compaction, bit costs, storing witnesses, all probes, repeated scans and final original check. No exponential branch tree; no free SAT oracle or canonicalization. Peak storage polynomial on same serial execution; CM <=(T+1)S. If some other retained factor is not bijunctive, it stays actual, and cannot be replaced by H absent certified equality. This terminal also gives an exact sound preprocessing for FN11 virtual-cap network: feed retained rows, not H alone, unless equivalence proved. For arbitrary affine charts, independent coordinate pins may not express the actual guard; use this lane only on actual Boolean IDs with admitted guards, or retain explicit chart constraints as factors with charged scopes.

## ES17C | Raw one-defect gate obstruction (PROVED)
For any genuine positive linear NAE4, choose any actual clause D and distinct p,q in D. Pin p=0,q=1 (or complement). D is satisfied, and by linearity every other clause contains at most one pinned variable. Ordinary unit propagation cannot force any other coordinate. Clauses with no pins have full binary projections. The other clauses each produce one binary clause: OR of two free members if pin is zero, NAND of two if pin is one. Among clauses through p, the free-member pairs are vertex-disjoint by linearity, hence form a matching; likewise through q. The union of two matchings is bipartite (paths and even alternating cycles, no coincident pair here by linearity). Assign opposite bits on every edge via BFS; every OR and NAND then holds. Therefore the raw residual 2-CNF envelope is always satisfiable under each of the six two-opposite-bit guards of D, independently of the source SAT label or baseline. Also the elementary clause LP for original bits 1<=sum of triple<=2 admits p=0,q=1, every other bit 1/2. Both are exact relaxation obstructions, not lower bounds on arbitrary algorithms or on grouped response closure.

For a baseline w with one defect D, set x=w XOR y. A previously satisfied clause with minority index m and majority indices u,v is preserved iff y_m is one of y_u,y_v; equivalently (y_m=y_u) OR (y_m=y_v). Proof: original unequal pairs are (m,u),(m,v); satisfaction after flip requires at least one remain unequal. If neither remains unequal all three final bits equal. D becomes NAE(y). Thus exact repair is a cut separating at least two D terminals while not cutting both incident candidate edges of any prior satisfied clause. This is an exact joint disjunctive equality formulation, not a graph minimum cut algorithm; the two paired edges cannot be optimized separately. Can retain explicit guards in y coordinates by substituting w. FN13 gate hardness (below) makes arbitrary exact repair NP-hard, not solved by knowing D or by these raw relaxations.
