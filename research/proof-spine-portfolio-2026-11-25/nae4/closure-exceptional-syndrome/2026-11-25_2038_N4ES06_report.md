## 2. Constant module H, with exact signed boundary
Use points (x,y) in GF(3)^2, integer ID 3x+y. Four partitions are the three vertical lines x=b and the three lines y=ax+b for each a=0,1,2. Each pair of points is on exactly one line. Let L0=(0,1,2) and L1=(3,4,5), two vertical lines. Delete both; H has ten retained owners.

Lemma: no 2-coloring of the full affine plane has fewer than two monochromatic lines. For zero: take five points of a majority color (a subset if necessary); if they contain no line, their ten pairs determine ten distinct lines with two selected points each. These consume all 20 incidences of the five points; the other two lines avoid the five points and so their union (>=5 points) lies in the four-point complement, impossible. If exactly one monochromatic line L, the two other parallel lines must both be mixed. Pick a point of L's color on each; their connecting line crosses L at a third point, another monochromatic line, contradiction.
Any two distinct lines can be the exact monochromatic set: affine automorphisms are transitive on unordered parallel pairs and unordered intersecting pairs. Representatives L0,L1 have color-1 set {0,1,2,6}; L0 and y=0 (0,3,6) have color-1 set {0,1,2,3,6}. Direct check of the twelve lines proves these two witnesses, and translations/invertible linear maps give all pairs. Thus deleting any two lines permits a model.

Every H model has L0 and L1 monochromatic. They have opposite colors: if same, every point of the third parallel line of that color would complete another monochromatic transverse line; hence the whole third line has the opposite color, also forbidden. Conversely the parallel-pair witness above and its complement lift both opposite choices. Therefore its EXACT projected six-port relation is (c,c,c,1+c,1+c,1+c), c in GF(2). These are actual lifts, not just a hull. For a deleted interior line K, a coloring whose exact mono lines are L1,K gives a mixed L0 while satisfying every retained internal owner except K; it can be complemented to set any designated L0 singleton to either value.
