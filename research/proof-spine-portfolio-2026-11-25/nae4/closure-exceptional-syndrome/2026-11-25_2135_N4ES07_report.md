## 2. A general rank-slack certificate (PROVED)
Let R be rank-two affine avoidance owners with actual row spaces U_i and arbitrary forbidden local values, acting on ambient GF(2)^v. Assume for every nonempty S subset R, rank(U_S)>=|S|+1. For ANY nonzero linear form h and either prescribed bit e, quotient rows by span(h). Every nonempty S loses at most one rank, so rank in the quotient is >=|S|. Rado's independent-transversal theorem (finite matroid, one representative per set, rank of every subunion at least number of sets) supplies one independent nonzero projected observation per owner. Choose actual nonzero preimage from owner space; imposing its opposite forbidden value suffices to satisfy that owner. These chosen rows together with h are independent, so Gaussian elimination attains all those RHS and h=e simultaneously. This gives actual local satisfying witnesses for both values of every nonzero h. Consequently the full local feasible affine hull is GF(2)^v and its projection onto ANY coordinate subset has full affine hull. It does NOT follow that its actual projection is full.

The slack condition is polynomially recognizable without enumerating subsets: duplicate one owner with a fresh labeled copy, test independent transversal existence by partition/linear matroid intersection on labeled row choices (parallel copies retain linear dependence). For each possible duplicated owner i, Hall holds iff all subsets containing i have slack >=1 and all other subsets have slack >=0 (subsets with just one of its copies also satisfy the ordinary Hall condition). Therefore all |R| tests succeed iff strict one-unit slack holds. Rado witness acquisition with a specified h is one further matroid intersection and Gaussian solve. All owner spaces have <=3 nonzero directions; use rank oracle by elimination. This is an actual deterministic polynomial test of the sufficient condition, not a complete test of affine-hull fullness in arbitrary geometry. It is closely related to historical guarded Rado/AB05; the new application is the simultaneous nonaffine-boundary obstruction below.

## 3. A missing actual boundary word (PROVED)
For raw owner set R define I=variables all four incident owners in R (private), B=V(R) minus I (ports). If I empty, choose a clause of R and set its three ports to 0; no local completion. Otherwise, suppose some v in I has two incident clauses in which v is the only private variable. By linearity the other two variables of those clauses are four distinct boundary variables. Set the pair of the first clause to 0 and the pair of the second to 1. The first forces v=1, second forces v=0; no completion. Other ports arbitrary. This gives an original-ID certificate with at most four pins, not an affine-hull statement. Both tests together imply actual port projection is a nonempty proper subset of the full cube with full affine hull, hence nonaffine. Thus reject this proposed affine-region substitution, NOT the source. Even zero-credit/full-response neutral affine contraction is excluded on admitted groups. Search for these certificates is polynomial once the region is specified. General nonaffine tables remain usable as exact interfaces.

## 4. Infinite connected SAT family with no small affine region (PROVED, probabilistic existence only)
Use independent uniform permutations B_j,C_j on p positions for each j=0,1,2,3. Partition j consists of p triples (A_i,B_{B_j(i)},C_{C_j(i)}). These are genuine perfect partitions; assigning all A=0, all B=1, C arbitrary gives a SAT witness. Prove that, with positive probability for all sufficiently large p, the source is linear AND both following properties hold for delta=10^(-8) (conservative fixed constant):
(P1) every owner set S with 2<=s=|S|<=delta p has |V(S)|>=s+2.
(P2) every nonempty variable subset I of size i<=delta p contains a vertex with at least two incident clauses having exactly one variable in I.
No randomized algorithm or efficient construction of the asymptotic family is claimed.

P1: fix s, and possible support sizes a,b,c of the three parts with a+b+c<=s+1. Since each variable occurs in four clauses, a,b,c>=s/4 and <=s for any such actual support. Number of candidate vertex sets is at most (4 e p/s)^(a+b+c) <= (4 e p/s)^(s+1) (binomial bound, e Euler's constant). Select s of the 4a possible A slots, at most (4e)^s possibilities. For a fixed slot set, the chance all B and C images land in selected supports is <= (b/p)^s(c/p)^s <= (s/p)^(2s), using sampling without replacement within each partition, independence between colors and partitions. Thus the bad probability at size s is bounded by
 s^3 (4 e p/s) (16 e^2 s/p)^s.
Sum s=2..floor(delta p) tends to 0: fixed s=2..5 vanish as O(p^(1-s)); for 6<=s<=sqrt(p), prefactor O(p^2), ratio <=16e^2/sqrt(p), geometric tail O(p^(-1)); beyond sqrt(p), prefactor O(p^3), ratio <=16e^2 delta<1/2. Singletons have exactly 3 support variables. For every nonempty subset of an admitted region, apply P1 to each connected support component (singleton also satisfies the bound). Since rank on each is v-1, rank(U_S)>=|S|+number of components>=|S|+1. This verifies Section 2 for every such region, independent of signing.

P2: fix I of i variables; for each partition consider the occupied A-index slots corresponding to its A vertices and to the inverse B and C images of its B and C vertices. Expose the latter two sets in random order without replacement within their own color. A collision is an exposed slot already occupied by another color. There are at most 4i draws total and conditional collision probability <=i/(p-i)<=2i/p, regardless of earlier exposures. Across partitions, collision count is sum over occupied clauses of (number of I incidences minus 1). If every I vertex had at most one incident single-I clause, total single-I incidences <=i, so >=3i incidences lie in clauses with >=2 I vertices. Each such clause contributes at least half its incidence count to collisions. At least ceil(3i/2) collisions follow. Union bound over subsets of exposure times gives probability <=2^(4i)(2i/p)^(3i/2) for the fixed I. There are <=(3ep/i)^i possible I. Thus bad probability at size i is <=[48e*2^(3/2)*sqrt(i/p)]^i. Sum i=1..floor(delta p) tends to zero by splitting at sqrt(p): first ratio O(p^(-1/4)), second ratio <1/2. This proves P2 with probability 1-o(1).

Linearity has probability bounded below uniformly by a positive constant. Elementary switching proof (no asymptotic independence assumption): in a uniform p-permutation with forbidden board of at most d entries per row and column (d fixed, p>=8d), let A_k count permutations with exactly k forbidden positions. For 1<=k<=p/2, choose one of k bad rows and a good row with both switched entries allowed; at least p-k-2d>=p/4 choices of good row. Every swap produces a permutation counted by A_(k-1). Reverse choices are at most pd (choose bad row and forbidden column, then swapped row is unique), hence A_k <= (4d)^k A_(k-1)/k. Expected bad positions <=d, so at least 3/4 of permutations have k<=p/2 by Markov. Summing gives A_0/p! >=(3/4) exp(-4d). Choose B_j in order avoiding all previously used A-B pairs (board degree <=3). Conditional on this actual permutation, choose C_j avoiding previous A-C and B-C pairs (board degree <=6 on both sides, since each previous partition is a bijection on each color pair). Each of six nontrivial choices has a uniform positive lower probability, so probability of linearity >= [(3/4) exp(-24)]^6. P1/P2 hold with probability tending to one in the original independent model; union bound proves positive intersection with linearity. The switching argument is self-contained; Lu/Szekely random-injection LLL is related background, not an assumed dependency.

For any nonempty raw region R with |R|<=delta p, if I nonempty then 4|I|<=3|R|, hence |I|<=delta p. P2 gives the original-ID forbidden boundary word of Section 3. P1 gives full affine hull. Therefore EVERY such region (including disconnected ones) has a nonaffine exact boundary projection. If original source disconnected, take any connected component; every partition restricts to a perfect partition on it, and color sets have equal size p'<=p. A full component has deficiency p'+1, hence violates P1/slack if it has <=delta p owners. Thus each component grows, and the selected connected component with m'=4p' owners retains the obstruction for all nonempty regions <=(delta/4)m'. Let p grow. This is an infinite family of connected authentic SAT sources with a linear-size obstruction, not merely a logarithmic bound for one fixed exponent. For every fixed c, all regions of size <=c log N eventually lie in the excluded range (ordinary explicit encoded N polynomial in m' with standard integer IDs). No unproved universal affine-normalization premise survives this family. Not an UNSAT lower bound: the source is SAT and planted witness proves it; the tripartition is not required in source encoding.
