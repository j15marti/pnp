## 3. Spectral compression theorem (PROVED)
For partition j, let A_j be the adjacency matrix of its disjoint union of triangles on the actual variable IDs: A_j(u,v)=1 iff distinct u,v share a j-owner. Put A=sum_j A_j; it is symmetric 8-regular on a linear NAE4 source. This is the pair-cooccurrence graph, not a planar graph or a replacement of ternary NAE semantics. For an incidence-permutation lift of a base with b variables and common fiber size p, the n-by-b fiber indicator X obeys A_j X = X B_j for the corresponding base triangle adjacency B_j. Every lifted variable has one neighbor over each other variable of its unique base clause in partition j. Thus A X=X B, B=sum B_j, and the column space W=im X (fiber-constant vectors) is invariant under every A_j. This is an exact equitable partition, not approximate eigenvector clustering.

For any fixed integer polynomial f with f(B)=0, f(A)X=X f(B)=0. Work over a fixed prime field F_l (l=101 in code); X still has rank b because every fiber is nonempty and disjoint, even if p divisible by l. Obtain K0=ker f(A) by exact modular elimination, then acquire the greatest simultaneous invariant subspace K contained in K0: K_{t+1}=K_t intersect all_j A_j^{-1}K_t. Equivalently start row space R0=row f(A) and saturate R_{t+1}=span(R_t union all_j R_t A_j), using Gaussian elimination. At most n strict rank increases; stop when stable. W is contained in all K_t by induction, hence W<=K. If dim K=b, K=W. Compute an arbitrary basis as columns of n-by-d matrix Y. Coordinates u,v have equal rows of Y iff every vector in K agrees on them. This criterion is basis independent. If K=W, this partitions actual original IDs into exactly the true b fibers, independent of their labels. Modular exactness suffices; no float clustering, random choice or assumed spectral gap.
