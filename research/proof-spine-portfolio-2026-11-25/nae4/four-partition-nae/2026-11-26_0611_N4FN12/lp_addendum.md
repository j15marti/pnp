# N4FN12 addendum | Odd-cycle covering LP remains at the packing bound
PROVED scoped; independent review pending.

Let H be the simple subcubic graph from FN12 Section C, with m supplied disjoint clause triangles and n variable wire components. Consider the ordinary fractional odd-cycle vertex-cover relaxation: minimize sum_v x_v, x_v >= 0, with sum_{v in C} x_v >= 1 for each simple odd cycle C. The triangle constraints give lower bound m. Assign x_v=1/3 to every clause-triangle vertex and x_v=0 to every wire vertex. This has cost m. Every odd cycle entirely in one clause triangle contains three charged vertices. The induced graph outside all clause triangles is bipartite (disjoint 4-cycles with pendants). Any simple cycle that uses both wire vertices and triangle vertices must traverse at least two distinct clause triangles: if only one triangle is visited, an excursion through a wire component would need two distinct attachment edges from that same triangle to that component, impossible because each original clause has three distinct variables and the compiler has exactly one occurrence per variable in that clause. Each visited triangle in a cross-region simple cycle contributes at least two vertices, since every triangle vertex has only one edge to a wire. Thus every such cycle has at least four charged vertices. All odd-cycle inequalities are satisfied, including those not explicitly enumerated. Therefore fractional optimum is exactly m on BOTH SAT and UNSAT compiler images, whereas integral OCT optimum equals m iff the source is SAT. No assumption of efficient LP separation is required for this representational obstruction; even exact optimum cannot classify the source. No unrestricted lower bound follows. This narrows the missing step to an exact integrality/repair mechanism beyond ordinary odd-cycle covering and disjoint-cycle packing. Symbolic derivation only; no LP solver executed.
