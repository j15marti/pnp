## 5. Uniform cyclic spectral extension (PROVED)

The variable cooccurrence adjacency Fourier block B(z) is b-by-b with Laurent polynomials of exponent range [-H,H]. For kth roots z, Fourier diagonalization gives all eigenvalues of the original adjacency. Assume no constant eigenvalue of B(z) as a Laurent matrix: det(B(z)-lambda I) not identically zero for every complex lambda. Sufficient certificate: the characteristic polynomials at two rational nonzero z values are coprime. A rational resultant nonzero modulo a prime avoiding denominators certifies this.
