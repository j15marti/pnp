## 2. Deficit-paydown lemma (PROVED)

If B empty, global NO. If B is a nonempty affine set, there exists a subspace A<=W and functional b on A such that B={f on W:f|A=b}. Acquire A and RHS by Gaussian elimination on actual B rows; certify exactness by containment plus equal cardinality, or another proof. Remove R, restrict exterior functionals by these equations, substitute them using a particular solution and quotient E/A, retaining original observation IDs and guard signs. If working with raw coordinate boundary instead of W, the same proof applies whenever exact extension certified and A<=U_R (e.g. connected positive block, complement-closed affine coordinate-boundary relation implies each annihilator row has even weight). Conversely any exterior assignment satisfying projected condition has a locally feasible functional agreeing on W; two functionals glue uniquely on U_R+U_O by f(a+c)=f_R(a)+f_O(c), well-defined by intersection agreement, then extend to E by Gaussian elimination.
