## L1 PROVED: reachable infinite four-bond primes with no literal pins
Let G be any simple 3-vertex-connected cubic plane graph, with a coherent clockwise rotation. At each vertex of G, cyclically direct its three incident edges to their successors. This gives a directed plane medial graph Q=L(G) on the edges of G; every vertex has two incoming and two outgoing arcs, alternating in its rotation. Each G vertex gives a directed facial triangle; the other checkerboard face class represents G faces. For every e in E(G), replace Q vertex e by a disk square with two variable vertices and two clause vertices alternating. Incoming Q arcs attach to variable vertices; outgoing arcs attach to clause vertices. An arc from e to f is physically the native incidence edge from f's variable to e's clause (reverse of native variable-to-clause orientation). Preserve every edge ID and cyclic order. The resulting native graph is simple planar cubic bipartite with 2|E(G)| variables and equal clauses. In a square, its two internal variable bits a,b and external clause-input bits p,q obey a+b+p=a+b+q=1 over integers, so its complete table is p=q=1-a-b. Conversely each permitted port word uniquely lifts to the original square. Thus t_e=p=q, and the whole source is exactly t_e+t_pred(u,e)+t_pred(v,e)=1 for e=uv. Every native variable is the t of its external predecessor block. This fixes the orientation explicitly; reversing all abstract arcs also works with the reversed coherent rotation, not an arbitrary mixed reversal.

Every square is a nontrivial 4-bond with connected complement since Q minus one vertex is connected (G minus one edge connected); sequentially contract all squares using AB09 exact response tables, maintaining cap port order and original IDs. All these cuts have two connected shores and the square is the smaller shore (at least six Q vertices). Q is now a generalized degree4 prime. For S subset of E(G), the Q edge cut equals 2 times the number of G vertices incident to both S and its complement: at a cubic vertex with 1/2 split exactly two pairs cross. If <=4, at most two mixed vertices X. G-X connected by 3-vertex-connectivity, so all nonmixed vertices have the same edge color. Every minority edge must have both endpoints in X, and since G simple there is at most one. Therefore every <=4 cut of Q has a singleton shore or is trivial. No claim that every contraction order reaches Q; this is an explicit permitted order, falsifying geometric hereditary four-bond availability in the full generalized language.

Even k prism G=C_k square K2 (k>=4) has exactly three facial covers (top+bottom, or either alternating set of side squares). See L2. They give three perfect matchings collectively containing every G edge exactly once. Hence every original variable and cap phase attains both bits in actual models, all sound unconditional literal closure leaves all bits live. Even exact global affine closure cannot force a literal; it can of course reduce chart rank (actual affine dimension <=2). Thus this is NOT an obstruction to the AB09 low-rank terminal or to treewidth; it kills only hereditary geometry without more operations. For general G the prime may have large width, not required by proof, not tested. Native square uniqueness: any external edge set is a matching on square ports; a 4-cycle using external edges would use two external and two internal edges and require two parallel Q edges, impossible. Hence the intended squares are exactly the graph's 4-cycles. Their complements connected implies facial in any plane embedding, by Jordan separation. Similarly each star triangle in Q is facial in any embedding since deleting its three vertices leaves L(G-v) connected. Thus the source-only acquisition below does not need supplied G labels or a special embedding.

## L2 PROVED: exact facial-cover terminal (at most three actual models)
For the SAME Q, let M={e:t_e=1}. At any G vertex, every pair of incident edges appears consecutively in one direction around its 3-cycle. The equation for the successor forbids both from being selected. Hence M is a matching. Summing the phase equations over all e gives 3|M|=|E(G)|=3|V(G)|/2, so M perfect. Each vertex has one matched edge and two complementary edges. Mark the successor of the matched edge at that vertex. Every unselected edge is marked at exactly one endpoint by its phase equation. Orient each complementary edge away from its marking endpoint; every vertex now has one entering and one leaving complementary edge. Thus G-M is a directed disjoint union of cycles. Because the successor convention is coherent clockwise at every vertex, the matched edge is on the same local side of every directed complementary cycle (cyclic order matched,outgoing,incoming). The other side contains no edge leaving any vertex of the cycle and, by connectedness and Jordan separation on the sphere, no graph at all. Every complementary cycle bounds a face. Conversely, given a vertex-disjoint set of facial cycles covering every G vertex, their complement is a perfect matching. Orient each face boundary with its empty side on the prescribed side; the matched edge is then followed by the outgoing complementary edge in the chosen rotation. Thus each unselected edge has exactly one marked endpoint and each selected edge has neither predecessor selected. All phase equations hold and the unique square lifts are native witnesses. Facial cover <-> source model bijective; no count transfer to general source.

Now assign Boolean z_F to every face of G with exactly one incident selected face at each vertex. ## L3 PROVED: same three-state table with adjacent port order is already general NP-complete
Do not conflate abstract relation p=q=1-a-b with cyclic geometry. Every native connected cubic bipartite incidence graph has a perfect matching by Hall: 3|S| edges from any subset of variables meet at least |S| clauses. Acquire by ordinary augmenting paths in polynomial time. Contract each matched variable-clause incidence (disjoint edges). At its merged vertex, outgoing p,q are the two other variable incidences and incoming a,b are the two other clause incidences, with cyclic port order (p,q,a,b) up to rotation/reversal. The matched bit t equals p=q, and the clause equation is t+a+b=1. Thus EXACTLY the same abstract three-state relation but adjacent equal ports, not the alternating square's cyclic order. All edge IDs, multiplicity, rotation and original matched bits retained. The quotient is loopless planar degree4 multigraph; parallel edges allowed. Every source model induces a cap assignment and every cap model uniquely reconstructs all source variables. Thus the general adjacent-port-only four-valent factor language is NP-complete by explicit polynomial parsimonious compilation from P1IN3, with degree4 multigraph promise, not necessarily four-bond-prime. This is a scoped complexity equivalence, not P!=NP. In AB09 sign convention outgoing p,q and incoming a,b give normalized weight 1 or4. In L1 square actual native sign is opposite with weight0 or3; both belong to signed ternary cap language. Ignoring cyclic order or assuming an alternating medial model for adjacent caps would silently assume the hard step. General solver for this language would suffice for P=NP; no claim it is impossible.
