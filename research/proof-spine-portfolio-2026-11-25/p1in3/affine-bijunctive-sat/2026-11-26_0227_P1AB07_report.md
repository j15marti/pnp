## L1 (PROVED): degree-h actual evaluation basis for q-Horn
Use a certified polarity and partition of variables into K (Horn) and B (binary). In renamed coordinates clauses are either Horn on K alone (at most one positive literal), or a disjunction of negative K literals and one or two B literals of either sign. Units allowed, empty clause rejects. This is the q-Horn class. Polynomial acquisition: solve LP in literal weights w(l) in [0,1], w(l)+w(not l)=1, sum over each clause <=1. Round w<1/2 to 0, w>1/2 to 1, keep equality at 1/2. In any clause at most one weight >1/2, and if one exists no half weight; otherwise at most two half weights. This gives a valid polarity and partition, and conversely such a certificate gives feasible weights. Remove tautologies and duplicate literals first. LP rational input polynomial bits, known deterministic polynomial bit-time algorithm; verify certificate. The general LP recognizer is not executed here.
For each seed set J subset K of size <=h, acquire its least Horn closure C(J) using forward chaining of K-only clauses, or reject if a negative Horn clause fails. Infeasible seeds never occur in a model. For a closure C let r(C) be the minimum seed size among enumerated J giving C. Deduplicate closures, retaining min r. The active binary formula H_C consists of B portions of mixed clauses whose negative K antecedents all fail (K bits all 1). It is ordinary 2CNF. Discard C if H_C is inconsistent. Acquire its actual degree-(h-r(C)) evaluation samples by P1AB06 2SAT seed closure: obtain an H_C model by deterministic 2SAT, flip to make that model zero, enumerate seeds of bounded size, forward-chain and discard conflicting closures, undo polarity. No original target model is supplied. Each combined sample (C,b) is an actual q-Horn model.

Proof completeness of identity samples over ANY field: after polarity change expand multilinear polynomial f(k,b)=sum_J k_J a_J(b), deg a_J<=h-|J|. On actual models k_J=1 iff C(J) subset of k. Seeds discarded by Horn inconsistency or binary inconsistency never occur: active binary clauses are monotone increasing in K. Group coefficients g_C=sum_{J:C(J)=C}a_J; degree <=h-r(C). Order feasible closures by size. If f vanishes at acquired samples for C, all terms g_E with E strictly contained in C already vanish on every b satisfying H_C, since H_C implies H_E. Thus g_C vanishes at the acquired degree-(h-r(C)) basis for H_C and hence at every H_C model. Induct. At arbitrary actual model (U,b), each contributing feasible C subset U has b satisfying H_C, hence all terms vanish. Conversely identity on all models implies identity on samples. Empty sample set iff formula unsat (if any model then least closure of empty seed also has feasible B assignment).
The 2SAT basis used above is proved by grouping monomials by least implication closure in coordinates complemented relative to an acquired 2SAT model: all clauses are then Horn, every feasible closure is an actual model, and the containment matrix indexed by closures is triangular with unit diagonal.
