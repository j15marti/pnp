## L1: exact forest exchange classification (PROVED)
Replace every source incidence by an indexed occurrence bit shared between an EX22 clause factor and an equality factor EQ_d={0^d,1^d} for each variable of degree d. Each wire participates in precisely two factors. Group any nonempty connected induced tree of factor vertices, exposing each crossing edge as a distinct physical port; no extra projection, coordinate coding or guard. Assume no EQ_1 in group. Then its exact attainable boundary relation is a delta-matroid iff it contains no EQ_d with d>=3. In the positive case it is even and matching-realizable. Delta-matroid axiom (Kazda–Kolmogorov–Rolinek Definition 4): for feasible words a,b and i in their symmetric difference D, a flip of {i,j} for some j in D must be feasible (j=i denotes single flip).

Positive: represent each clause by 4 terminal vertices and 2 private vertices adjacent to all 4 (K_{2,4}); selected port means terminal matched outside or deleted from local gadget. Matching remaining vertices forces exactly 2 ports selected; all such choices extend. EQ_2 has 2 terminals connected by an edge. Glue pairs of terminals across internal wires with a single edge, keeping original IDs. Selected bit is use of this edge. Each remaining local gadget has the required row, and each row assignment extends. For two matchings with boundary subsets deleted, symmetric difference paths connect differing ports; switching the path from a chosen differing port realizes a two-port flip, and no single flip is possible by parity. Thus matching-realizability implies even exchange. This direction holds on cyclic groups too.

Negative: choose a branching equality vertex r, take one satisfying labeling of all group edges and boundary stubs with r=0 and another with r=1. Both exist by rooting the tree: every incident bit can be extended to an allowed local row, and branches are independent. Choose the pair A,B minimizing boundary Hamming distance across the two root values (existence argument; constructive alternative below). Their changed edges form a forest with no internal vertex of degree one: a changed EX22 factor has 2 or 4 changed ports, and a changed equality factor has all d ports changed. Any changed component has at least two boundary leaves. If a changed component does not contain r, restore its labeling from A in B; all factors still valid, root unchanged, strictly fewer boundary differences. Thus the changed edges form a connected subtree K containing r of degree at least 3. Every path between two boundary leaves of K contains a branching equality OR a clause with degree 4 in K. To ensure the path specifically crosses an equality, use the following constructive choice instead of merely relying on minimality: from any labeling A, flip all edges at r and recursively propagate away from r. At each reached clause the incoming bit flips, so choose exactly one other port with the opposite A bit and flip it (always exists since A has two ones and two zeros); at EQ flip every other port. The tree ensures no conflicts or revisits. Obtain actual B with changed subtree K having degree 2 at changed clause nodes, degree d at changed equalities. Its root branches. Any path joining two of its boundary leaves necessarily passes a branching equality, since otherwise every internal node on that path has degree 2 in K and the path would be a whole connected component, contradicting root branching. There are >=3 leaves. For ANY purported feasible word A' differing from A at only one leaf, the corresponding changed forest must have a component with >=2 leaves, impossible. For two leaves the changed forest must be exactly the unique path between them (a tree with all interior changed degrees >=2 and only two boundary leaves is a path); this is the same path in K, passing through a branching equality at which two changed incident bits cannot satisfy EQ. Thus for every differing i no permissible j exists. This gives a constructive witness in linear local graph work; no truth assignment for the exterior used. It refutes even the ordinary delta-matroid axiom, invariant under coordinate flips and permutations. An arbitrary minimum-distance pair also has no clause degree4 in its changed component: if a clause has 4 changed ports, both assignments are complementary there; one can pair opposite A bits into two paths and detach the branch not containing r, decreasing distance. This follows by separating the tree at that clause and restoring the detached two subbranches, which changes exactly an opposite pair at that clause, preserving EX22. The constructive proof alone suffices.

Consequence: any partition of an unguarded source with all variable degrees >=2 and at least one degree>=3 into connected acyclic factor groups leaves at least one non-delta-matroid group. It cannot be made into an ordinary physical matching graph merely by exact raw forest grouping, regardless of group sizes. This is a representation obstruction, not a source decision lower bound. Conditional pinning, nonlocal overlapping or cyclic grouping, semantic coordinate retractions, and exact tagged matching joins are not excluded. In particular an equality label can be encoded as an extra parity obligation, but that is not ordinary physical matching.

## L2: all-source paired-occurrence matching compiler and exact chart join (PROVED)
For each original variable arbitrarily partition its indexed occurrences into pairs and at most one singleton, by source ID order. Create a copy bit for each group and discard equality *between* group bits temporarily, keeping pair equality. Every clause is still exact two. Build one K_{2,4} gadget per clause and one interterminal edge per pair. For each singleton create a private dummy joined to its corresponding terminal by an edge. Make all dummies a clique, adding one extra dummy if their number is odd. There are O(m) vertices and O(m^2) edges; no need for planarity. Every perfect matching chooses exactly 2 selected ports per clause and each paired port is selected at both ends or neither. Conversely for any relaxed satisfying labeling, choose pair and singleton edges according to its bits, complete two unmatched ports in each clause with the two private vertices; selected singleton count is even since total selected ports is 2m minus twice the number of selected pairs. Pair all remaining dummies arbitrarily (even count). Thus exact equivalence with split relaxation. The relaxation is decidable by ordinary deterministic perfect matching (Edmonds); guarded original bit fixes all corresponding link edges present or absent, done by endpoint deletion for forced edges after checking conflicts, and deleting forbidden edges. This is a polynomial necessary-condition oracle with explicit guard provenance, not a SAT oracle. If every original degree <=2, it is already exact. For arbitrary degree, retain for each original variable comparisons of its first link indicator with all its other link indicators; every group equal iff these XOR equations are zero. Original solution iff some actual perfect matching satisfies all comparisons, with source lift by reading any copy, then checking every original clause. No marginal acceptance.

For two actual perfect matchings M0,M1 of this SAME graph and SAME guard, decompose their symmetric difference into disjoint alternating even cycles. Every independent subset switch gives actual perfect matching. Write each link indicator as a_g + z_{cycle(g)} over GF2, if it changes; otherwise constant. For each original copy comparison the resulting equality is a linear equation with <=2 nonzero phase variables. Solve all at once by Gaussian elimination or signed parity graph. Any solution gives an actual switched matching and lifts to source; any source witness in the two-matching chart corresponds to some switch pattern satisfying all equations. This is exact chart compatibility, not an affine hull of unrelated matchings. A chart inconsistency rejects only that chart. Guarded forbidden/forced edges hold in both anchors and all switches. Anchor acquisition can use deterministic blossom, then polynomially many force/delete individual edges or different weighted objectives (no completeness shown); compare all pairs, charge failed calls, no target witness required. This is an explicit EX22 adapter of the historical alternating-cycle technique, not a claim about the other primary source languages.

Conditional fallback: pin all but at most one copy per high-degree source variable by fixing its original bit (one Boolean branch per such variable); residual degree<=2 is matched exactly. With b variables of degree>2, enumerate both guards for each, applying paid closure and matching per leaf, O(2^b poly(N)) time, poly peak space. Independent factors additive; no universal bound on b. L1 says a physical forest partition alone cannot remove the synchronization, L2 identifies that synchronization exactly as parity of actual matching edges. ## L3: scalable genuine obstruction to local grouping (PROVED)
For any prime p>3 take clause vertices (i,t), variable vertices (j,u), i,j in {0,1,2,3}, t,u mod p, with edge u=t+ij mod p. Each vertex has degree4; clause has four distinct variables, and no two clauses share two variables: the equality for two distinct j would imply (i-i')(j-j')=0 mod p. Thus incidence girth >=6, all clauses unique. Connected: using row i=0 and i=1 allows moving t to t+1 via j=0,1; every j is attached. The assignment selecting variables with j=0,1 satisfies every row exactly, as does its complement. Hence no sound unconditional unary forced bit exists. Every connected induced group of <=5 factors is a tree; any group containing a variable factor fails exchange by L1. In fact any raw forest partition fails regardless of size. For arbitrary girth g, start with K4,4 with two of four variable types set to 1. Given a finite simple bipartite graph of girth h, take a covering graph with fiber F2^{|E|}, voltage on each base edge a distinct coordinate vector (undirected). Every cycle of length h in a cover would project to a nonbacktracking closed walk of length h in base, necessarily a simple shortest cycle; its voltage sum is nonzero, so impossible. No shorter cycles can appear. Any connected component covers the connected base, retains degree and planted balanced coloring. Repeat to exceed max(4,g); thereafter clauses are proper and unique. For arbitrarily large size at fixed g, connected cyclic covers via voltage 1 on any nonbridge edge, 0 elsewhere; the associated cycle generates the fiber, girth never decreases. This is a finite existence construction (not a polynomial-time large-girth construction claim). All are SAT, not proof of regular-subclass NP-hardness. No assertion of resistance to semantic grouping or alternative exact solvers.
