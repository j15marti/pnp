## 1. Known boundary graph and cycle theorem
For a 5-pole with five indexed dangling edges at cubic internal vertices, model dangling endpoints as degree-one leaves in path traversals. Summing conservation over all internal vertices gives boundary xor=0 in F2^2, so the three nonzero color multiplicities are odd and hence (3,1,1). Up to permutation a type is exactly the unordered pair of solitary port positions, giving 10 possible edges in the complete graph K5. Let S(P) be its actual type support. Two poles glue iff S(A) intersects S(B), since equal types can be aligned by a global permutation. No guards or fixed color choices preserved by this contextual quotient.

For any actual witness of edge uv in the support graph, take a Kempe path of the solitary color at u and the majority color from port u. Its other boundary endpoint is one of the three majority ports (only u has its color, v has the excluded solitary color). Switching the path replaces u by a different solitary port, retaining v. Consequently no vertex of a nonempty coloring graph has degree one. This is known; the path gives constructive discovery of a new neighbor of a degree-one vertex in any stored witness subgraph. Further, any simple graph on five vertices with no degree-one vertices has no bridge: a bridge would separate its connected component into two pieces, each containing a cycle (otherwise a tree piece has a leaf other than its possible bridge endpoint, or that endpoint has degree one). These cycles require >=6 distinct vertices. Therefore every edge belongs to a simple cycle. This proof also works for disconnected graphs with isolated vertices.

K5 has exactly 37 undirected simple cycles: 10 triangles, 15 four-cycles, 12 five-cycles. Define signature sigma(S) as the set of those cycles disjoint from S. For every possible context B, S intersects S(B) iff there is a simple cycle in S(B) not in sigma(S): one direction pick cycle containing common edge, other immediate. Thus equality of signatures is sufficient for contextual equivalence, and necessary because all 37 single-cycle masks have explicit actual caps (see below). This is decision equivalence, NOT equality of full support, count or guarded feasibility.

## 3. Exact acquisition and constructive reverse lift
Given connected simple cubic G with a supplied cut of exactly five indexed edges into connected sides A,B, choose smaller A. Query all 37 caps C, each a simple-cycle support; each query is a recursive ordinary CEC decision-and-witness call on A attached to C (plus simplicity adapters). If NO then C is disjoint from S(A); if YES keep actual A witness for its solitary edge e in C. A child UNKNOWN propagates UNKNOWN (not NO). Thus exact signature acquired; witness dictionary W is subset of S(A) and hits every cycle hit by S(A). If W empty return NO, since any edge belongs to some tested cycle. Otherwise select representative R by signature dictionary. One recursive call on B attached to R yields exact decision equivalent to G. No need to query larger side for all 37 cycles or acquire its full support.

Reverse lift if YES: restrict returned coloring to B, with solitary type e in S(R). Start a dictionary V={e: actual B witness}. If a vertex v has degree one in V, its only edge uv has a stored witness. Apply the above Kempe path from u to a majority port, obtaining a new edge incident with v; store its actual B coloring. Every iteration adds a previously absent edge, so <=9 switches (10 edges total). When no degree-one vertex remains, e lies on a cycle C of V. Since e in S(R), C hits S(R); signature equivalence implies C hits S(A). W therefore intersects C, hence V. Pick a common edge, align global colors, and join actual original indexed seam edges. Verify whole graph. This does NOT assume that the initial representative type appears in A or that arbitrary Kempe classes are connected. In 43 finite audits the initial exterior type was absent from W, but constructive repair worked. No count transfer. Stored witnesses preserve all original IDs; arbitrary precoloring, a pinned defect, or pinned port states may destroy the no-degree-one premise, so no guarded transfer without separate proof.
