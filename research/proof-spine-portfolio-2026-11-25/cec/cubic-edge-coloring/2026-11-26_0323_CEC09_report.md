## 1. Exact one-way square removal and source witness lift

An alternating square on four ordinary vertices has two opposite W edges ab,cd and two opposite A edges ac,bd. Each vertex has one other A edge outside square. For vertex-disjoint selected squares, remove square vertices, reconnect the two outside incidences at a,b into one edge and at c,d into another. Simultaneous suppression traces outside A paths via W at deleted vertices, preserves distinct parallel edge IDs; if path fails to reach retained endpoints, overlapping squares, loops, or invalid residual, return UNKNOWN in this implementation. Keep two original terminals. Every coloring of the reduced cubic multigraph lifts: assign raw edge color alpha to every outside A edge along its recorded path; within a square let alpha,beta be its two path colors (possibly equal). Choose gamma distinct from both. Color ac,bd gamma, W edges ab and cd by the third color distinct from alpha,gamma and beta,gamma respectively. Every deleted vertex sees exactly three distinct colors. All untouched incidences preserved. This extends CEC08 beyond reverse chronological order; no dependence on original splice history. ## 2. Recognizing precisely the compiler squares without compiler metadata
CEC07 raw layered program has l>=3 stages, each w>=7 input/output vertices on separate sides. Raw P0 and P1 are perfect matchings from inputs to outputs in each stage; W wires outputs to next-stage inputs cyclically. The two marked closing wires are subdivided at u,v and joined by z. CEC07 synchronization operation removes two P0 edges, adds four new vertices with two P1 and two W edges forming a square, and replaces each removed P0 pair by an outside P0--W--P0 path. Later splices only remove P0 edges; P1 and W inside every inserted square are permanent. Every raw P0 edge is therefore a path with dummy W subdivisions, and all raw P1 edges persist.

Claim all alternating squares detected by searching two W and two A edges on ordinary vertices are exactly the inserted squares:
* Two original W edges: their endpoints are original raw vertices; any A edge between original vertices lies within one stage and joins input to output. Following alternating A/W goes consistently forward (or backward) by stage, and a two-W-edge closed circuit would require l dividing 2, impossible.
* A square containing a dummy W edge ab and a P1 edge incident to a or b: that P1 mate is in the same inserted square, and its W mate is the fourth vertex of that inserted square. The final A edge must be the other internal P1 edge; no P0 ever connects two vertices created in the same splice (all P0 edges to fresh vertices connect them to old ones, and later operations delete rather than add edges between existing vertices).
* Otherwise a square containing a dummy W edge must have both A edges P0. A P0/W circuit on ordinary vertices before the marked-wire insertion traces a raw stage permutation orbit, length at least 2l. Each splice subdivides paths in this phase rather than creating new circuits. Inserting u,v cannot create a new ordinary 4-cycle. Contradiction.
Thus all and only disjoint genuine synchronization squares recovered. Enumerate via each W edge and its two-by-two A-neighbor pairs, deduplicate using exact vertex IDs. Their simultaneous contraction restores raw P0 edges, with actual path maps, and raw P1 edges. No A phase orientation known or used. Original marked endpoints retained. Restoring marked W wires after deleting u,v and z yields a bipartite cubic multigraph H with 2-factor A_raw and wire matching W_raw. Parallel raw edges retained as indexed edges (some original P0=P1 endpoint pairs), no conversion to a simple graph or free equality assumption.

## 3. Exact two-edge common-cycle equivalence (unguarded residual)
For any bipartite cubic loopless multigraph H and two distinct nonincident edges e,f, let H+ be obtained by subdividing e,f once and adding edge z between subdivisions. Then H+ colorable iff H has a spanning 2-factor containing e and f in the SAME cycle. Forward: take color c of z. Remove the subdivisions and restore e,f; no c-colored seam edge is incident to a subdivision, so the c-color class off z is a perfect matching of H missing e,f. Complement is a 2-factor containing e,f. If they are on different cycles, each would gain one vertex in H+ and become odd (H bipartite), contradicting that complement of the c class in H+ is all even. Reverse: the common cycle gains two vertices, remains even, other cycles unchanged; alternate two colors and color complement (including z) third. Actual witness both ways. This equivalence does not force the chosen perfect matching to be one of the raw A phase options; section 4 supplies sufficient search, not a general solver. General two-edge common-cycle theorem on arbitrary nonplanar bipartite H not imported from Diwan. If no perfect matching avoids both edges then H+ is NO, but this does not imply original G NO after one-way square contraction.

## 4. Layered shortest-path theorem without knowing the layers
Acquire raw H from section 2, compute a bipartition, orient A edges from side0 to side1, W edges from side1 to side0 (reverse orientation also tested). Let start be the input endpoint of marked closing wire e, end be the output endpoint of marked f. Directed path alternates A,W and begins/ends with A. For compiler's original stage orientation, all paths from start to end use a positive number k of A edges divisible by l; every stage step advances mod l. By CEC08 row-occurrence lemma, there EXISTS independent occurrence phase word taking lane o to marker in one pass of l gates (and vice versa). This is used only as an existence proof of a directed path with l A edges, not as an input or computed source solution. BFS acquires a shortest path; k=l. Hence it visits each original stage exactly once, so every raw A component at most once. Each raw A cycle has exactly two alternating perfect matchings (including length2 parallel edge cycles). Choose its phase containing the path A edge, arbitrary phase on unused components. Can recover phases by alternating traversal of actual indexed A edges, no original stage/variable names. More generally any directed path whose pinned choices agree within each A component is sufficient, and that check is explicit. Wrong bipartition orientation may be tried and rejected; correct one is among two. If both markers in same component, bipartition side choice is consistent; existence path ensures they are. Selected A matching plus W forms spanning factor containing both marked wires in same circuit, because path plus marked wires belongs to that circuit. By section 3, H+ colorable; lift through all squares (section 1) and verify every original incidence. This gives a deterministic polynomial algorithm to color EVERY CEC07 compiled G from G,A,z alone, including guarded NO charts, with no use of compiler history or original H.
