## 1. Exact threshold acquisition for all optimal cuts of a fixed matching
Let M be any verified perfect matching; C_1,...,C_q are cycles of G-M, k odd. CEC11 established Phi(x,P)=|D(x)\P|, with D(x) monochromatic edges, and min_x Phi(x,M)=k. Every optimal x for M has exactly one monochromatic edge on each odd cycle and none on even cycles. Choose a set D consisting of one indexed edge per odd cycle. Acquire offsets h_v by traversing cycles, requiring h_u xor h_v=0 for D and 1 for all other factor edges. Consistency follows from parity of each cycle length minus its chosen defect. All optimal cuts with this particular defect pattern are precisely x_v=h_v xor p_i for v in C_i, where the q phases p_i are unconstrained bits. This handles all even-cycle phase choices, without flattening them.

For threshold t>=0, enumerate a set T of <=t original edges permitted to be uncovered defects. For a fixed (M,D,T), seek a perfect matching P and cut of the above form satisfying D(x)\P subset T. This holds iff:
(a) P contains D\T;
(b) for each original edge uv in M\(T union P), impose p_i xor p_j = 1 xor h_u xor h_v.
Proof: all non-M edges have fixed crossing status from D; all remaining requirements are exactly old matching edge crossings. Both directions and actual witness x follow directly. Enumerating all D and T covers all optimal cuts and all threshold witnesses, no guessed target. Cost at most n^k * sum_{j=0}^t binom(m,j) outer queries. For t=0 positive query gives actual proper 3-edge-coloring by alternating even cycles of G-P; negative excludes only this M-optimal-cut chart, even if all D tested. For t<k, positive gives strictly lower oddness of P; for t=k can give nonstrict or neutral movement. No inference that chart NO means source NO.

## 3. Charged fixed-rank bound
Let r be the cycle rank |E(Q)|-|V(Q)|+components of the bundled signed multigraph after initial saturation for a fixed (M,D,T). Upon branch, a whole selected bundle vanishes from an inconsistent circuit, so rank decreases by at least one. Subsequent closure only deletes bundled edges, contracts hard equations (with gauge change), removes loops, or merges identical signed edges. These operations never increase cycle rank. In particular an old group cannot split under contraction because endpoints in each old hard component undergo the same potential change. Hard conflicts reject. Inherited hard relations remain sound since descendant matching family only shrinks. Each closure round either adds at least one of O(m) forced edges or at least one independent hard relation, so polynomial total node work, even when inspecting all old edges/bundles each round. Depth <=r (and <=n/2 by forced disjoint edges). Branch factor <=m. Hence per guard O((m+1)^(r+1) poly(N)) deterministic work; all outer queries O(n^k (m+1)^(t+r+1) poly(N)) with maximum r over tried guards. For fixed k,t,r this is a fixed polynomial. A balanced quotient terminal requires one node even at high r, and arbitrarily many phases can be acquired by parity traversal. This is XP in k,t,r, NOT FPT or a polynomial at logarithmic rank automatically. More precise actual tree accounting charges sum of all nodes including failed probes, no free parallelism.
