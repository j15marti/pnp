B. PROVED exact majority overlay theorem. Let A1,A2,A3 be actual indexed XY,XZ,YZ perfect matchings, each cardinality n. Let F consist of triples occurring in at least two copies. At each X owner, any majority triple belongs to A1 or A2; these select exactly one incident tuple each. Thus degree <=2, likewise Y/Z. Empty row rejects F; degree-one forces its tuple; degree-two imposes complement; bipartite component coloring with pin checks is necessary and sufficient for exact F-cover. D. PROVED infinite stationary SAT family. k octahedra (all binary triples), separate part labels.
