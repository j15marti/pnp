## 1. Infinite actual source family
For integer k>=1 subdivide each triangular face of the octahedron into k^2 elementary triangles by the triangular lattice. Use integer lattice vertices (x,y,z) with |x|+|y|+|z|=k. Within each octant take lattice coordinates i=|x|,j=|y|, remaining k-i-j; color (i+2j) mod 3. Adjacent lattice vertices differ by one transfer between coordinates, so colors differ; colors agree across glued octant boundaries. Every face has all 3 colors. The six original corners have degree 4; all other vertices degree 6. Total triangular faces F=8k^2. This is a simple sphere triangulation.

Take the dual graph D of the barycentric subdivision. Its vertices are flags (f,a,b) where f is an oriented triangular face, a is a vertex of f, b is the other endpoint of an incident edge. Black flags take the orientation of (a,b,c) in f, white the reverse. Three neighbors of (f,a,b) are (f,b,a), (f,a,c), and (g,a,b) where g is the opposite face incident to edge ab. Each adjacency reverses flag orientation, including the last because adjacent oriented faces have opposite directions on shared edges. Graph is planar, connected, cubic, bipartite, and 3-connected (dual of simple sphere triangulation; classical polyhedral dual theorem). Each f has three flags of each bipartition. Give white flags color of b. Black flags with colors (a,b,c) have neighbor colors (a,c,b), hence form original P3DM triples with one element in each color. Every white color occurs once per triangle, thus n=F, m=3F=24k^2. Distinct black flags in a face have different pairs of white neighbors in that face, and flags in different faces cannot have equal neighborhoods (each has exactly two neighbors in its own face and one outside). Explicit source encoding O(m log m) after deterministic ID relabeling. Each element has degree exactly 3.

Give black flags color of b too. The reverse adjacency argument shows each white flag has exactly one black neighbor of each color. Hence three pairwise disjoint actual exact covers partition T. All exact integer occupancies verified by construction. Each variable occurs true and false among these witnesses, so NO sound unary deduction can fix any variable, even with complete semantic unary closure. In particular exact-one and all-three indexed matching support leave source unchanged, and after reclosure at stagnation still unchanged. This does not imply no nonliteral equivalences or contractions.
