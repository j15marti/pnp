## 1. Phase-component absorption PCA (PROVED)
Consider a finite plane tensor network with binary wires, equality contraction, each vertex supplied with a cyclically ordered exact nonnegative integer response (or rational of polynomial bit length), and actual local witness access for source lifting. The network and its exact region responses must be acquired from source under a fixed polynomial total budget. Allow two types of vertices: (i) certified planar matchgate signatures with polynomial-size rational realizations, (ii) exact responses supported on at most two words, both of the same parity (possibly singletons after pins). This includes arbitrarily many native nonmatchgate vertices of type ii, and does not assert all P3DM instances have such a chart. Other exceptions can be handled only with separately paid exact methods.

Initially and after guards, propagate all fixed coordinates from singleton tables or coordinates common to both supported words, conditioning the adjacent factor exactly, including matchgate factors by attaching a unary pin. This removes fixed wire incidences and pays for their scalar factors; if a conditioned two-word table loses both words reject, if one remains continue. A fixed wire between matchgates need not be discovered by semantic support oracle. Certified native deductions can enter as local pins through actual witness adapters; no unproved forcing or marginal support is used. Every live two-word vertex now has a phase bit p and all live incident wire bits have form p XOR a_e. Its two weights are retained separately. Fixed parity implies even live degree. Along an edge between two phase vertices, equality becomes a signed phase equation. Self-loop consistency checked as well. Traverse the connected components of the phase-induced subgraph, assign root phase 0 and propagate signed equations; any inconsistent cycle rejects. For consistent component every internal phase is determined by root phase; the two root choices are exactly all internal assignments, with weights products of actual local weights. All internal wire bits determined. Thus its exact external response has two complementary words, with one actual internal configuration of local word IDs per root phase (the product of local multiplicities accounts for actual source choices). Sum of even degrees minus twice internal edges proves the number b of external variable wires is even. Fixed incidences previously removed contribute no live bits.

- b=0: replace by scalar sum of the two weights.
- b=2: replace by an exact binary parity signature (weighted equality or weighted disequality). A weighted equality (f00,f11) with both positive can be realized by two external vertices joined by an edge of weight f00/f11, multiplied by scalar f11 under removal convention. Disequality follows by flipping one port with a unit-weight binary disequality gadget (two arms sharing one internal vertex). Zeros are unary pins. Realization uses exact rationals; multiply global scalar explicitly.
- b>=4: the two-word response is NOT a matchgate in this basis, regardless of weights. For support u,v at Hamming distance b, choose i in their difference, alpha=u XOR e_i and beta=v XOR e_i. The matchgate identity sums alternating terms f(alpha XOR e_j) f(beta XOR e_j) over j in difference(alpha,beta). Only j=i is nonzero: for j!=i two positions changed from u, neither u nor v if b>=4. Contradiction. This is a representation barrier, not a decision lower bound. Enumerate the root phase of the entire component, not each of its constituent vertices. Each branch fixes all its actual boundary bits and contributes its actual weight.

For b=2 the replacement preserves planarity even if the component is not a disk or encloses other network parts: select a simple embedded path inside the component connecting the two outer incidences; remove the component and route its constant-size binary gadget along a thin neighborhood of that path and the old outer incidences. No crossing of other retained edges. b=0 deletion and b>=4 pinning also preserve planarity. Separate components are disjoint and use disjoint neighborhoods. No unproved cofacial assumption for b>=4 because those are pinned, not replaced by a multiport gadget.

Let h be the number of consistent phase components with b>=4 after fixed-port closure. Every branch of their 2^h root-phase assignments leaves a planar matchgate network, weighted count via exact rational FKT, multiplied by scalar factors. Branch assignments are disjoint and exhaustive, so sum is the original count; no cancellations at source word level (local signature entries nonnegative even if internal realizing edges signed). Uniform deterministic time 2^h poly(N), peak poly(N) by streaming, including preprocessing, source-to-chart acquisition within admitted cap, conditioning, failed branches, FKT arithmetic and reconstruction. Products of polynomially many local rational weights have polynomial bit length; common denominators and determinants of polynomial-size rational matrices have polynomial bit lengths. For witness choose positive branch and use source wire self-reduction with pins, polynomial additional paid FKT evaluations, then local original-ID witness lookup. No signed graph matching witness used directly without verifying the source. Decision only if decision-preserving (rather than count-preserving) earlier pruning used. Arbitrary binary-encoded weighted optimization not claimed from counts; no large generating-polynomial evaluation assumed. Fixed logarithmic h suffices, no universal h bound. This improves AGE14's per-defective-region exponent to a parameter of CONNECTED high-fanout phase components, not total number of exceptional atoms. ## 2. Native crossing-square region (PROVED)
