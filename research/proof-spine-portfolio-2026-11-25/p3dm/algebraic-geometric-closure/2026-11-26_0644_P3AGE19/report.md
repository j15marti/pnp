## 2. Exact quadratic feasibility criterion (PROVED)

Necessity: coefficient of the cubic Walsh character equals (sum_even P - sum_odd P)/8, hence if one parity is absent from B, equality to zero is impossible with positive penalties. Sufficiency: assign penalty o to every forbidden even word, e to every forbidden odd word, zero on R. Cubic Walsh coefficient cancels exactly. All unary/binary tables use unit forbidden penalty; full relations zero, empty reject. Thus 225 of 255 nonempty ternary relations (including full) have an exact quadratic penalty, 30 do not.
