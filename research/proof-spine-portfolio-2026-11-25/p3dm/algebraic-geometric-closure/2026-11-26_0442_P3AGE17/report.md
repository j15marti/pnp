## 1. Leaf elimination theorem (PROVED scoped)
Take a plane tensor network on binary wires with equality contraction, explicitly acquired exact nonnegative integer source response tables and actual local witness access. Every atom is either an ordinary planar matchgate with polynomial-size rational realization or an exact fixed-parity two-word atom. The chart and all witness adapters must cost a fixed polynomial in N; no claim of universal acquisition. Apply AGE16 signed phase synchronization and fixed-port closure. A live phase component P has one bit p and actual incident bits x_e=p XOR a_e; its two weights are w_P(0),w_P(1). Every ordinary connected component M consists of matchgate realizations joined by wires. Distinct components and their internal variables are disjoint. In the incidence graph H, vertices are phases and ordinary components; collapse parallel adjacencies to a single graph edge but store all actual wires and their signs. No expansion of an ordinary component's potentially exponential boundary table.

Operation M: if ordinary component M has exactly one phase neighbor P, compute its exact conditional partition functions F_M(p) for p=0,1 by pinning ALL incident wires to p XOR a_e, not independently optimizing ports. Each is an ordinary planar closed matching network: unary pins are matchgates, equality contraction is joining terminal pairs by edges, and the source response is nonnegative. Evaluate via exact weighted planar Pfaffian (FKT). Multiply w_P(p) by F_M(p) and delete M and those wires. If M has no neighbors, evaluate scalar and delete. All internal configurations in M are summed exactly; disjoint factors guarantee product, not overlap. If a phase weight becomes zero, condition or reject as appropriate, propagate fixed wires and reclose. The two remaining words of P retain their correlation. Removing an odd number of wires need not leave fixed parity in a general formal tensor network, but in the live two-phase case F_M is supported on a single total boundary parity, so an odd-sized bundle to the one phase would kill one phase; reclosure handles that case. We do not silently apply even-port replacement to odd live fanout.

Operation P: if P has no external wires, sum its two weights and delete as scalar. If it has precisely two remaining external wires, its response is binary weighted equality or disequality (two complementary words). Use removal-bit convention, where port bit1 deletes that terminal. For equality weights (A,B) on 00,11, realize by path a-c-d-b with edge weights A,B,1 and terminals a,b. Deleting neither gives A, deleting both gives B, odd removals zero. For disequality weights (A,B) on 10,01 use path a-c-b with weights B,A; one deletion gives the corresponding weight, others zero. Both gadgets use no division, permit zeros. One fixed external bit is pinned. These gadgets are exact even with nonuniform weights. Replace P by the gadget, using a thin neighborhood of a simple path within its original connected phase region and the two external incidences; no cofacial assumption on the remaining ordinary component or disk assumption on P. Remove absorbed descendants; they have no other connections. Replacement may join two ordinary components; recompute connected components after each operation. General planarity maintained on the original embedding, since paths lie entirely inside removed phase regions. The resulting ordinary network can be arbitrary large/high-width, yet closed weighted planar matchings are polynomially evaluable. Reclose after deductions and at stagnation.

Choose deterministic ordering of operations, e.g. canonical sorted IDs. Each removes at least one nontrivial phase/ordinary component or pins a live variable; binary substitution may create one ordinary component but strictly decreases number of phases, and ordinary removal strictly decreases number of ordinary components. At most O(chart size) operations plus polynomial reclosure/reindexing, each ordinary leaf needs at most two FKT evaluations. All exact source counts preserved. Let h_* be the number of surviving live phases at stagnation after these operations. Branch over their 2^h_* root assignments, pin actual wires, multiply corresponding phase weights, evaluate all ordinary components via FKT. These guards are disjoint and exhaustive. Thus uniform deterministic time 2^h_* poly(N), polynomial peak space streaming the branches, including chart acquisition under its stated cap, discovery scans, failed work, full arithmetic, reconstruction. Source counts at every partial contraction bounded by 2^m for disjoint original tuple sets (and fixed physical boundary conditions), hence polynomial bits. Rational gadget constants of polynomial bit length have a common denominator whose bit length is at most the sum of input denominators' bit lengths; exact matching sums/determinants have polynomial bit complexity. Reconstruction by recording eliminated regions and two-word state IDs, then conditioning original wires one at a time with the same exact evaluation schedule (can replay certified unguarded chart with added local pins), uses polynomial additional calls, or source self-reduction rebuilding and pinning the chart under fixed guards. No assumption that arbitrary newly guarded source retains a preferred unguarded recognition criterion: use recorded exact chart with local source witness adapters; in general if these adapters cannot answer internal source pins, use positive wire assignments followed by paid local witness access as assumed. Do not use a signed matching witness as an original tuple witness without adapter. Additive arbitrary weighted optimization not obtained from counting with large binary encoded exponents.

Sufficient structural corollary: H is a forest after collapsing multiple wire adjacencies, and every phase-ordinary adjacency bundle has exactly two wires. Leaf M has two conditioned evaluations; leaf P has binary realization; induction removes all phase vertices. An ordinary component can have unbounded degree, so *ordinary table enumeration is not needed*. The same proof works for an orientation toward any root in each tree if each phase's parent bundle contains two wires (a root phase needs no parent), other child bundles can be arbitrary. Leaf ordinary evaluation pins all parent wires; phase leaf after its descendants are absorbed has two or zero wires. It is sufficient to use the stronger all-bundles-two condition for a simple deterministic acquisition guarantee. Cyclic chart cores may survive with h_* large. The theorem is a specialized exact sum-product elimination using FKT as a high-degree factor evaluator and planar binary gadget realization, not a novelty claim for ordinary tree message passing. This strengthens AGE16's initial h exponent; h_* can be zero with h linear.

## 2. Native scalable counterexample to necessity of low initial h (PROVED scoped)

Its host has 2n directed incidence edges (including exterior completion), is connected, and each cube has certified C6 matching response with ports in the source cyclic order. Retain a deterministically chosen spanning tree of n-1 host edges (ignore orientation for tree). For each of the remaining n+1 edges, insert the following six-tuple patch into the corresponding actual source incidence wire, preserving original core tuple ID. On one original tuple t's incidence to old X label x, replace x in t by a fresh X label e. Add four tuples q0,q1,q2,q3 with YZ endpoints (0,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,0) in fresh two-by-two local labels, cyclic order. Their X labels are x,e,a,b, respectively, with a,b fresh. Add two tuples u,v with a shared fresh Y and Z pair, their X labels a,b respectively. Thus exact-one rows enforce q0+q1=q1+q2=q2+q3=q3+q0=1, u+v=1, q2+u=q3+v=1. Entrance t+q1=1. Therefore q0=q2=v=t, q1=q3=u=1-t. For every bit of t there is exactly one patch extension and q0 replaces t at old owner x, including all its other competitors. Reverse restriction restores the original source, exact count-preserving. Six new tuples, three owners per part, distinct (u,v differ on X), equal part sizes. No narrowed hardness claim.

The leaf two-tuple component is a 4-cycle, joined by the two degree-two X owners a,b in a disk on the leaf side of the square. Place the entire patch within a disk surrounding a segment of the old incidence edge, attach its two core ports in order to original endpoints (one via new e). This is a planar disk substitution, with old rotation preserved, even for exterior host edges. Core stays connected without patches via the retained spanning tree. Each square separately is fixed-parity two-word nonmatchgate, four live ports, not synchronized with other square phases: leaf and entrance exact-one rows are ordinary W2 matchgates; leaves are binary exact-one on the two indexed tuples. The sole large ordinary component is the original connected core matching network with those edges cut and entrance W2 factors; each leaf is a separate ordinary component. The simple component graph is a subdivided star: each phase has two ordinary neighbors (central and private leaf), each bundle contains two wires. Operation M multiplies phase weights by leaf counts (both 1 unguarded), then operation P replaces with binary response and restores the original incidence. This is a native instance of the theorem, not an abstract synthetic signature assumption.
