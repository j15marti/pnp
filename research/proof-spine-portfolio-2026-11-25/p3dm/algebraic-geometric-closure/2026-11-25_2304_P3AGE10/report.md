Any exact cover selects disjoint elements on its two sides. Its left occupancy z lies in C_S = U intersect (b+V). If b not in U+V, reject globally. Otherwise C_S=z0+(U intersect V); dimension lambda=rank(A_S)+rank(A_complement)-rank(A). Obtain z0 by a single Gaussian solve with provenance. Project a basis of ker A onto left occupancy (A_S times restriction to S) and row reduce; image exactly U intersect V (every common direction can be produced by kernel by adding two left/right preimages). Enumerate 2^lambda candidate bit vectors, not assignments. Exact occupancy, not mere parity: for target z, forbid every tuple in S touching a zero coordinate; require exactly one at all coordinates with z=1. If remaining counts of X,Y,Z unequal, reject this target. Otherwise delete zero rows and compact the three label sets while preserving original IDs.
