## 3. Strengthening AGE07 on the same authenticated flag family (PROVED)
Use AGE07 triangulated octahedral mesh and barycentric dual flag graph, m=24k^2, n=8k^2. Each original triangle f contains three black flags and three white flags forming a hexagonal cycle. Each two of the three black flags share one of the white elements, hence form a conflict triangle. These groups partition all black flags, so global integer quota implies exactly one selected flag per original triangle in EVERY cover. No reconstruction of supplied mesh coordinates needed by the terminal: enumerate nonowner triangles (triangles not identical to any owner row) and check that they form a partition of n triangles. For this family these are exactly the groups: the properly 3-colored triangulation has bipartite triangle dual (orientation of the three colors reverses across an edge), so a conflict triangle cannot meet three distinct mesh triangles. Along a mesh edge there is one outgoing incidence each direction. A black flag outgoing across that edge is one of the two internal black neighbors of the white flag receiving the other external incidence. Thus the cross-conflict edges are the union of two stars with distinct centers on opposite sides; a conflict triangle spanning two groups is precisely one of the two owner triangles. Within a group the three black flags do not share one common white neighbor. Hence the certificate is acquired from actual triples alone.

## 4. Sharp obstruction to universal nonowner partition acquisition and complementary bypass
Let m=6r>=12. Take planar prism C_m square Cartesian product K2, bipartition by alternating rim index and layer. One black tuple t_i and one white element w_i per column i. The black tuple touches w_(i-1),w_i,w_(i+1) (mod m). Color white w_i by i mod 3. Every tuple has one element of each color and n=m/3; distinct triples, genuine planar cubic incidence. This is a genuine source family already related to PS05 prism controls, not a new hard family. Conflict graph C_m^2: every triangle consists of three consecutive indices for m>=12 and is an owner row. Therefore the nonowner partition acquisition fails even though there are three exact covers (each residue mod 3), every tuple globally true and false, and source unary closure cannot change anything. Direct owner row subtraction gives s_(i-1)=s_(i+2) at each i. Hence exactly three equality classes, and each exact cover is precisely one of them. This proves that the complementary row-difference lane solves the same failure of partition acquisition, without supplying a target. It refutes universal nonowner clique-cover admission, not the larger quotient method, arbitrary algorithms or P=NP.
