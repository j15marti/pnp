## 2. Exact Boolean demand theorem (PROVED)
Let F be conjunction of explicitly acquired exact finite Boolean tables (P_j,R_j), including all conflict factors and original guards in its representation. Select variable x. Replace EVERY factor containing x by its existential projection onto P_j minus x, leaving all other factors intact; call the resulting formula G. For each projected word u in such a factor compute its nonempty fiber B_j(u) subset {0,1} of x values. If B_j(u)={b}, call u a demand for b and record the corresponding partial assignment to P_j minus x. Let D_b be the list of all b-demands. Both-value fibers impose no demand.
For every assignment a to remaining variables satisfying G, there exists a joint extension in F iff it does NOT match both a member of D_0 and a member of D_1. Necessity: matched demands require opposite x values. Sufficiency: all incident fibers are nonempty, and a finite family of subsets of {0,1} with no opposite singleton fibers has nonempty intersection. The same x is chosen for every incident factor. Thus the exact projected formula is G AND the nogood for each compatible opposite-demand pair (the union of their pinned assignments). No enumeration of Cartesian products across incident tables is needed. If each such pair is CERTIFIED incompatible with G, its nogood may be omitted and G itself is the exact projection, preserving EVERY attainable word on the remaining coordinates. This is stronger than CPS18 decision-only reference overwrites; no complete model/reference or preselected guard truth table required. No claim of preserving multiplicity or arbitrary weights.
Acquire pairs explicitly from exact local support rows; direct conflict (same coordinate, opposite bit) excludes immediately. Otherwise test the union of pins against every actual factor of G; a factor with no compatible row excludes that pair. If inconclusive, build H, the conjunction of all necessary unary and binary projections of G's individual factors. Exact SCC and implication reachability certify that H AND the union of pins is unsatisfiable. Since G implies H, this excludes that pair even if G itself is non-bijunctive. No inference of feasibility from H. If H is itself UNSAT, F is UNSAT; if any pair remains unexcluded, decline this elimination (OPEN), not UNSAT and not free projection. More generally another verified polynomial UNSAT certificate can exclude a pair; never use a SAT oracle. A matched demand-1 pattern is sufficient to reconstruct x=1; if none matches choose 0. This DNF circuit has size at most the explicit input row-list length, even if it depends on many guards. Lift in reverse order, assigning freely discarded unconstrained variables an arbitrary default. Source Hall witness table backpointers then recover original tuple IDs and verify all three colors and guards.
2SAT pinned exclusion detail: assume H satisfiable. Its implication graph is closed under contrapositive. For pins p,q (including p=q), if p reaches NOT q, no completion. Conversely a union of implication closures inconsistent contains l and NOT l reached from p and q; contrapositive gives p -> NOT q. If union closed under implication and consistent, can extend to a model of H by the ordinary SCC construction with pins. Only sufficiency is used in executed eliminator. Cache reachability in SCC condensation, no expensive SAT queries.
Counterexample to naive separate projections: x=y AND x=z. Both projections are full but y!=z cannot extend; CDP correctly declines. Another counterexample to treating local binary projections as full relation is NAE(a,b,c): all binary projections full while 000 forbidden. Our exact-table exclusion can use NAE directly, not its hull. The theorem is related to known Davis-Putnam elimination and bounded variable elimination (BVE); no novelty claim for existential Boolean elimination. New integration target is a polynomial-size, source-authenticated certificate for zero-new-factor projection through mixed P3DM Hall charts.
