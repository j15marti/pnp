## 4. New exact nonlinear messages without child-state Cartesian products (PROVED)

Let D(v)=F(q0 XOR v)-F(q0), finite on shifted support S, with D(0)=0 and all D>=0. If S closed under XOR and D subadditive, encode with one virtual column v of weight D(v) for each nonzero v in S. Any collection of such columns has combined vector v in S and sum of costs >=D(v); one column (or none for zero) attains D(v). Decode by lookup of the ACTUAL stored child witness for that combined v, not by taking union of multiple alternative witnesses. This generalizes prior P3ES04 subadditivity sufficient rule to affine shifted context with a direct paid check. Rank-one messages always satisfy it (after shift); rank-two need not.
Counterexample (FALSIFIED universal subadditivity for signed affine responses): three binary columns (100,101,110) over F2^3, all costs -2; private first coordinate required zero.
