## 2. Greatest explicit-table reference overwrite (PROVED)
Let F be conjunction of exact explicit finite relations R_j on coordinate sets P_j, with Boolean variables indexed globally. Fix arbitrary supplied/acquired reference a (NOT necessarily a solution), and allowed overwrite set U. A mask S subset U is locally safe if replacing all coordinates S by their reference values preserves each R_j for ALL its words. Consequently it maps every global solution to another global solution; the restricted formula (pin S to a, project pinned coordinates) is equisatisfiable, and every reduced solution lifts by reinsertion. This is decision-only; it does not preserve every solution or weight, and safe pins are not semantic forced bits. In particular never export a decision retraction as a universal original-variable implication.

Safe masks are closed under union: overwrites to the same reference commute and compose to their union. Empty safe; unique greatest within U. One can compute it polynomially even if arity is unbounded, measured in EXPLICIT row-list length, without enumerating 2^arity masks. For each factor encode every row by its deviation set D={i in P: row_i != a_i}. Denote deviation family H. Overwrite S maps D to D minus S. For each D in H, let H_D={E in H:E subset D}. Acquire a small explicit auxiliary mask table L_D on coordinates D: enumerate candidates M=D minus E for E in H_D, retaining iff Z minus M belongs to H_D for every Z in H_D. Every possible valid mask on H_D is enumerated, since D itself must be mapped to H_D. At most |H| candidates for each D. L_D union-closed (composition) and includes empty. A global S is safe for the original factor iff S intersect D belongs to L_D for every D: necessity follows from all subrows being preserved; sufficiency follows by testing the original row D itself. Thus even a width-w table with r sparse rows uses <=r^2 stored local candidate masks of width at most w. No subset enumeration or oracle.

Initialize S=U. For every D,L_D compute G_D=union of all M in L_D with M subset S. This union is itself admissible; remove D minus G_D from S. Repeat to fixed point. Every feasible mask initially contained in U remains contained in S by induction (its projection was one of the candidates). At fixed point S intersect D=G_D for all constraints; hence S itself feasible and greatest. At most |U| strict deletion rounds plus final scan. Deterministic bit implementation using sorted row bit vectors and membership binary search costs O(sum_j r_j^3 w_j log(2+r_j)+(m+1)sum_j r_j^2 w_j) elementary bit/vector operations, with polynomial ID/access overhead; simple slower direct scans also polynomial. Empty original relations reject initially. Factor projection after fixing is row filtering and coordinate deletion, never increases explicit row count. Original exact local witnesses remain valid for retained words. If factors came from exact disjoint private source regions, restore stored original tuple IDs once and check all three colors. References may come from matching, affine or 2SAT relaxation; no requirement they be SAT. This is a polynomial certificate construction for fixed-reference LOCAL closure, NOT acquisition of the greatest semantically safe mask over all global solutions (coNP-hard in general CSP).
