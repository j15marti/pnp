## 2. W4F weighted flow compiler (PROVED scoped)
Let E be a nonempty table of finite costs f(B) for fixed-size subsets of U, omitting infeasible B. Remove loops (owners in no finite word); they cannot be selected here. For rank two, min-valuated symmetric exchange means for disjoint finite pairs A,B and a in A there exists b in B such that both exchanged pairs are finite and their summed costs <=f(A)+f(B). Overlapping pairs exchange trivially. It implies the ordinary basis axiom; nonloops partition into parallel classes whose within-class pairs are forbidden and every cross-class pair is finite. Verify this complete multipartite support directly. Each class has capacity one and can send flow to either of two mandatory slots.
Case <=3 classes on <=4 owners: costs are additive per owner. With two classes, every 2x2 rectangle of cross-pair costs has equal opposite sums: exchange in both directions forces equality since within-class pairs forbidden. Thus row+column representation (also if one class singleton). With three classes and four owners at most one duplicated class; comparing the two members' costs to either other class gives equal differences by same exchange. Reduce to three singleton class costs, which solve three pair-sum equations; extend by member differences. With fewer owners the same argument or trivial one-word representation applies. Acquire rational additive weights a_x by exact elimination on a_i+a_j=f(ij). Denominator at most two: in each connected cross-class graph propagation from a chosen vertex expresses all potentials as +/- one free parameter plus integer, odd cycle fixes twice parameter; free value zero is valid for bipartite component. Charge bit operations.
Case four classes on four owners: all six pairs finite. Define S1=f12+f34, S2=f13+f24, S3=f14+f23. Valuated exchange equivalent to the minimum being attained >=2 times. Relabel so S1>=S2=S3. Put delta=S1-S2>=0, subtract delta from f34 only. The resulting six pair costs have equal opposite sums; therefore are exactly a_i+a_j, acquired as above (e.g. 2a1=g12+g13-g23; other equations verified). Construct two slots. Every owner can occupy slot 0 at cost a_i, slot 1 at cost a_i+delta for owners 3,4 and a_i otherwise. Each owner may be used at most once. Choosing any pair incurs min assignment cost a_i+a_j+delta*1_{pair=34}; the penalty appears iff both chosen owners are in {3,4}. Exactly original f, no forbidden pair introduced. Double costs to keep all chart weights integral. This is a direct weighted representation, not a claim that ordinary matroid support implies valuation.
Rank1: one mandatory slot with cost f({x}). Rank0: constant. Single finite word: dedicated slots forcing its owners. Corank1: every word omits exactly one owner; if omission allowed for subset H, force U-H through dedicated slots, choose |H|-1 of H through interchangeable slots with per-owner costs -f(U-{x}); add constant sum_{x in H}f(U-{x}). Then cost for omitting x is exactly f(U-{x}). Includes rank2 on three owners. Arbitrary-size additive rank2 complete multipartite tables also admitted by explicit rational check. No general nonadditive rank2 chart claimed beyond four nonloops.
Global compile source -> each remaining actual X owner cap1 -> region parallel-class in/out cap1 -> region slots -> sink cap1. Total demanded flow equals number of remaining owners and total slots; hence all owners and slots saturated. Edge costs as above. A selected word in each region yields a flow at its exact local cost; conversely any integral flow selects admissible words and costs at least their table minima. Local minimum routing independently attainable, so global min equals source min. Decode via stored ID witness, verify all three colors and original guards. Cycles/high fanout in regional interaction graph do not affect this proof. This is compatible with paid exact weighted transversal charts (FCG16) by inserting their own slots and actual owner-slot costs directly, but large implicit weighted DDR acquisition NOT implemented here.
Algorithm uses successive shortest augmenting paths on initially acyclic directed network, with signed costs and unit capacities. Zero flow optimal at value0; difference between a minimum value-j flow and a feasible value-(j+1) flow decomposes in residual into one augmenting path plus cycles, no negative residual cycle at optimum. A shortest residual path yields min value-(j+1), no negative cycle by optimality. No residual path => no full flow. At most |X| augmentations, deterministic Bellman-Ford, polynomial bit work and capacity bound independent of weight magnitude.

## 3. Matching representation falsifier and exact fallback

Their symmetric difference decomposes into alternating cycles/paths. For a port a matched internally in M_A and not M_B, toggle the path from a in both matchings. All internal mandatory vertices still saturated. Its other endpoint cannot be optional internal (would change cardinality of port set) or a port with same selection direction (changes cardinality by two). Hence ends at b in B-A. Swapped realizations are feasible with same combined edge cost, so f(A-a+b)+f(B-b+a)<=f(A)+f(B).
