## 2. Lemma FC: fractional clique allocation

Form conflict graph: l,l' conflict iff they cannot both be active in B. Remove impossible types. All conflicts acquired by SCC pins, or in local full binary hull by PM. For each partner b enumerate cliques Q of at most fixed h eligible literal-options, each of which permits b. Include singletons, opposite choices of same slot where allowed (disjoint domains imply not both eligible at b). Seek rational nonnegative weights w_{b,Q} with sum_{b,Q containing l}w>=1 for every supported l, and sum_Q w_{b,Q}<=1 for every b.
PROOF: for each B-model choose its one option per slot. At each b, no clique contains two active options. Send each active l at least one unit available in capacities w across eligible b; contributions of different active l to b use disjoint clique weights, total <=1. ## 3. Lemma ZB: small characteristic evaluation basis for fixed-degree identities on arbitrary 2CNF (PROVED)
Let F satisfiable 2CNF over p bits; choose any SCC model r, reorient variables y_i=1 iff t_i differs from r_i. All-zero satisfies reoriented F. Every clause is then an implication y_i=>y_j, a negative exclusion (!y_i OR !y_j), or a negative unit (tautologies discarded). For each S subset of [p] of size <=d, take implication closure C(S). If it violates a negative clause/unit, no F model contains S. Otherwise the word with exactly C(S) set to one is a model and is the least F model containing S. Acquire and deduplicate these words by reachability in polynomial time for FIXED d. Number <=sum_{j=0}^d binom(p,j), not number of full F words.
For any multilinear monomial m_S=prod_{i in S} y_i of degree <=d, its restriction to F is identically zero if closure inconsistent, else equals the indicator [C(S) subset support(y)]. Merge monomials with equal closure; choose a total order extending inclusion on distinct feasible closures. The evaluation matrix of these distinct indicators at their own closure words is triangular with diagonal 1: entry [D subset C]. Thus indicators linearly independent on the sample, and a degree<=d polynomial vanishes on all F models iff it vanishes on this acquired sample. Same after expanding complemented literals and reducing y_i^2=y_i. This is an elementary constructive evaluation proof, not an assumed Groebner/SAT oracle, and works over Q. No need to enumerate F models or optimize arbitrary quadratic functions. d fixed is essential.

## 4. Lemma PL_d: acquire positive bounded-degree fractional recourse (PROVED)

where alpha is a partial pin pattern of <=d bits, and if a is a port each alpha includes the literal selecting the class that permits b (more generally one may certify that alpha implies that literal in F). Nonports need no literal condition. Every term is a nonnegative polynomial of degree <=d on the Boolean cube; f_e is identically zero on incompatible words. Remove patterns impossible in F by SCC (or test on ZB basis, using nonnegative indicator and ZB).
