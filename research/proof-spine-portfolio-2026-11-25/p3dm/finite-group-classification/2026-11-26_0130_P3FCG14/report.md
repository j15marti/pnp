## 1. Conservation on an arbitrary two-incidence bond (PROVED)
In a connected original incidence graph, delete a pair of nonbridge edges forming a bond; two connected shores result. Let b_j be the Boolean selection of the original tuple incident to cut edge j. For shore U, n_c is its number of owners of color c. Let s be number of chosen tuples in U, and epsilon_j=+1 if the edge's tuple is in U (outgoing), -1 otherwise (incoming). Count selected incidences of color c: s=n_c+sum_{j of color c} epsilon_j b_j. This is integer conservation for actual covers, not merely parity. At least one of the three colors is untouched. If the cut colors differ, the untouched color fixes s, and both individual bits are forced; possibly inconsistent. If same color, both other counts must agree, and epsilon_1 b_1+epsilon_2 b_2=s-n_c. The equation has at most two Boolean words. If there are two, they are 00,11 for opposite orientations, or 01,10 for identical orientations. Every actual cover obeys this. Conservation alone is necessary, not sufficient. Evaluate both remaining words exactly on the paid side: outgoing bits pin its original tuple IDs, incoming bits subtract from its owner targets (reject negative target); if a tuple has two outgoing edges, require consistent pins. Local owner occupancy must be exact, including zero targets. A zero/one target instance reduces to ordinary native P3DM by removing zero-target owners and deleting incident tuples, and applying selected outgoing pins and covered rows; all remaining crossing tuples are removed. Unequal residual color cardinality rejects. No arbitrary source model assumed.

## 2. Native constant-size replacement, avoiding both branches on the large shore (PROVED)
Let L be eliminated shore, R retained shore. Compute the exact nonempty response table of L by local brute force under fixed cap, or a paid exact conditional terminal (CPS12 HGO for arbitrary 0/1 row targets is one candidate). A declined call must not be treated as infeasible. Keep one actual witness for every feasible word, or all for counting if independently paid. If table empty, source NO. If one word, substitute it on R: pin outgoing tuples, deduct incoming occupancy, remove covered rows and competing tuples, reject conflicts. This is a single ordinary residual, with selected tuple IDs saved.

If both words feasible, they necessarily have the same color c. Three native patches on R:
(a) one outgoing and one incoming: attach the missing c incidence of the outgoing tuple to the incoming owner in R. This directly realizes equality 00/11: the outgoing tuple's bit is the virtual incoming occupancy.
(b) both outgoing: add one fresh c owner and attach both dangling tuple incidences to it. Its exact-one row realizes complement 01/10.
(c) both incoming: add one fresh owner of each other color and two new alternative tuples, each containing both fresh owners and one of the two old incoming c owners. Exactly one alternative is selected, supplying the corresponding incoming bit. This realizes complement 01/10. If the incoming owner repeats, both alternatives have identical coordinates but distinct computational IDs. More generally any patch can create identical indexed tuples. Retain their identities for full lifts; no assertion that an indexed internal chart is a distinct-tuple hardness source. For decision-only external calls one may deduplicate after literal guards, keeping a representative and its state decoder; do not deduplicate if exact count/support of all original IDs is needed.
