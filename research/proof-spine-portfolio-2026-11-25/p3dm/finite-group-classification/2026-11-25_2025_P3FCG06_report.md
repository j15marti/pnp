## Lemma CS (PROVED): common-scaffold exact transversal oracle

For each a in A let L(a)={z: some original residual triple uses a,z in C}, and N(a,z)={b: (a,b,z) is an actual triple in C, reordered if A=Y}. Construct unlabelled graph H_C with a--b iff b belongs to intersection over z in L(a) of N(a,z). If H_C has a perfect matching f:A->B, store f and label graph J_C=(A,Z_C; edges a--z iff z in L(a)). This certificate is acquired by intersections and one ordinary perfect matching per orientation; no target assignment or oracle.

Claim: the exact size-|A| family of distinct Z-label sets achievable by indexed local XY perfect matchings equals the bases of the transversal matroid of J_C, provided J_C rank=|A|. If rank smaller, no local distinct-label matching exists (sound NO). Necessity: every local indexed matching with distinct labels gives a matching of A to those labels in J_C. Sufficiency: any injection g:A->Z using J_C lifts to actual triples (a,f(a),g(a)), since f(a) lies in every relevant N(a,z). All A and B are covered exactly once, labels distinct, with actual source IDs. No assumption that all XY perfect matchings are label-injective; certificate only needs a single universal scaffold.
