## 5. Remaining universal gap and precise obstruction
The parity-gluing theorem does not absorb arbitrary fanout. Three-way equality of literal ports R={000,111} has R^+={0000,1111}; from one word no two-coordinate flip reaches another permitted word. Direct matching deletion signatures of graphs have even exchange (symmetric difference of two PMs pairs changed ports by alternating paths). Even permitting existential optional deletion ports cannot realize equality3 with those original literal ports retained: take two lifts differing on all three; the alternating path starting at an external changed port ends at another changed port (external or optional), flipping only one or two external bits, forbidden. Thus copying a high hub to three factors and hoping parity completion or a matching gadget will enforce equality is invalid. This does not prohibit exact context-dependent contraction, nonliteral re-encoding, or guarded joins. Some nonbijunctive ternary relations pass, not all: R on bitmasks {0,1,2,5} has even extension {0,9,10,5}; u=10,v=5,i=2 has no valid two-flip exchange. A failed exchange returns OPEN, never UNSAT.
