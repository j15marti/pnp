For a satisfiable 2CNF, a clause (a OR b) is entailed iff the opposite pins cannot coexist, equivalently one of four implication reachability conditions: not-a -> a, not-b -> b, not-a -> b, not-b -> a. Proof: if both pins individually consistent and their implication closures conflict, a literal and its complement reached from the two pins gives a path from one pin to negation of the other by contraposition. If closure consistent, residual 2CNF extends by SCC. Thus compute SCC and reachability in each satisfiable slice; discard only exactly inconsistent slices. If none survive, genuine NO (unavoidable Hall circuit). A binary clause is true on the union iff entailed by every surviving slice.
