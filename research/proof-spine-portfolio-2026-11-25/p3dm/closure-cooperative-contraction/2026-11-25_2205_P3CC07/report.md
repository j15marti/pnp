## 1. Signed-majority bound (proved)
Let R be a nonempty relation of genuine zero-energy Boolean words; F any real quadratic multilinear polynomial, F=0 on R and F>0 on every non-solution in the intended domain. A hard 2CNF domain may be imposed; its clauses have zero violation mass on all three words and their median. Let u,v,w in R and m=majority(u,v,w) coordinatewise. If m is not in R, define the signed measure
  nu = (delta_u + delta_v + delta_w - delta_m)/2.
It has total mass 1. It is not a probability distribution on whole words, but every unary and pair marginal is a probability table: only the median's pattern is subtracted, and at least one of u,v,w realizes that pattern. Indeed at each of two coordinates at least two of the three words agree with the median, so the two index sets intersect. All unary and pair marginals are consistent and nonnegative. They are feasible for the standard pairwise local marginal polytope, including every true unary/binary consequence of R. For every quadratic F, the corresponding objective value is exactly -F(m)/2. Hence the ordinary roof-duality relaxation value lambda(F) <= -F(m)/2, and CC06's doubled mincut bound L=2lambda satisfies
  L <= -F(m).
For an exact nonnegative integer penalty, the roof deficit k=-L is at least F(m)>=1. This applies to ANY quadratic penalty on the same chart bits, not merely diagonal owner weights, signed gauges, occupancy cross terms or added valid binary penalties. For a 2CNF hard domain, its median closure ensures m is still in the domain. Hard violations have zero pseudomass. Proof uses no algorithm for finding the three witnesses; their acquisition is a separate dependency.

Roof/local LP equivalence: for each pair i,j let x_i be unary probability and y_ij=P(1,1), with max(0,x_i+x_j-1)<=y_ij<=min(x_i,x_j). On unrestricted pairs minimize q_ij*y_ij by the appropriate endpoint. The usual doubled submodular extension has same optimum after dividing by two. From any doubled labeling, average the two unary labels and choose the aligned pair coupling for negative q, crossed pair coupling for positive q, obtaining a feasible local distribution of exactly its half objective. Conversely for any local point take a_i=1[x_i>=t], 1-b_i=1[x_i>=1-t] for uniform t in [0,1]; aligned pair product has mean min(x_i,x_j), crossed mean max(0,x_i+x_j-1). The expected half doubled objective is no greater than the local value. Thus both minima agree. A finite rational hard clause penalty B larger than four times the absolute coefficient sum of the base objective enforces zero local violation at a relaxed optimum whenever the hard 2CNF has a model: doubled minimizers yield half-integer pair probabilities, any positive violation is at least 1/2, and base absolute cost is bounded by its coefficient sum. Alternatively treat hard clauses as zero-mass constraints directly. Integer scale rational weights before exact cut.
