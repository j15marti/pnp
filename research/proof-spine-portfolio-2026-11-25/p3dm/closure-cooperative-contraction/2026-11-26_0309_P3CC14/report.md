## 3. Dual-price optimal face contraction DPOF (PROVED)
For exact local table costs introduce lambda_(i,B)>=0. Configuration LP:
min sum_(i,B)c_i(B) lambda_(i,B)
subject to sum_B lambda_(i,B)=1 for every i,
sum_(i,B contains x)lambda_(i,B)=1 for every x.

Let L=sum alpha_i+sum p_x, and F_i^0 be the tight minimizing words. Every globally compatible choice from ALL tight tables has actual cost exactly L; hence globally optimal, even if price was not obtained optimally. If no such compatible choice, this does NOT reject original problem. In particular lack of tight feasibility is not an UNSAT certificate.
Stronger uniform sufficiency: solve explicit LP exactly and acquire optimal dual. If the primal is feasible and every nonempty tight family F_i^0 satisfies ordinary matroid exchange, then a global compatible tight choice MUST exist. Proof: by complementary slackness an optimal fractional primal is supported only on tight words. No integral marginal assumption needed. Cost equals L by tightness, actual witnesses reconstruct original optimum. No appeal to arbitrary weighted matroid intersection on nonlinear costs.
