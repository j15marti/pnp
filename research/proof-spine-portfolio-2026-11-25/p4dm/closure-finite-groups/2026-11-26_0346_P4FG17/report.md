## 1. Exact weighted four-port theorem
Let f:{0,1}^4 -> integers union infinity have nonempty support of fixed parity. Four distinct ports of a weighted undirected graph are deleted when their bit is one; every remaining vertex must be matched. Additive constant allowed. Define S_i=f(i)+f(i XOR15), i=0..7. Then f is realizable iff min S_i is attained at least twice, or all S_i infinite.

Necessity: take minimizing matchings for a finite minimizing complementary pair. All four ports are covered in exactly one matching; every other vertex covered in both. Symmetric difference has two alternating paths pairing the four ports, plus cycles. Switch one path in BOTH matchings. The two resulting complementary words belong to a different antipodal pair, and total cost is unchanged. Their optimal costs cannot exceed that total or be smaller than global minimum. Thus a unique finite min is impossible.

Sufficiency: choose p in a minimizing complementary pair if finite, or any finite p otherwise. Set c=f(p) and g(z)=f(z XOR p) for even z. Four required vertices, edge ij of cost g({i,j})-c when finite, omitted otherwise. Empty present set has cost0; two present vertices exact edge cost; four present vertices minimum over three perfect pairings. Because the chosen complementary sum ties another (or all sums infinite), this minimum equals g(15)-c. For actual deletion y, make old vertex the external port if p_i=1, otherwise attach a required leaf and make that leaf the port. Remaining old vertices available after forced leaf edges are exactly z=y XOR p. Add c. At most 8 vertices. Invalid parity impossible because odd remaining vertex count. Lower arity constant parity tables are padded by fixed-zero deletion bits and unused ports internalized; all have no finite complementary pair, so automatically admit matching. Empty table is NO. A pin deletes a port if bit1 or internalizes if bit0; remaining table stays realizable. No requirement the gadget be planar, and no unrestricted equivalence of tropical Wick with arbitrary graph realization beyond arity4.

## 2. Unweighted equivalence to even delta-matroids (new exact bridge for PS14)
Set every finite cost zero. Every matching-admitted support is EDM: for two words, symmetric difference alternating paths pair their differing ports, and swapping the path incident to any requested differing port gives the required two-bit exchange. Conversely if a support has exactly one complete complementary pair, twist to make that pair 0 and15; remaining words are two-subsets of four with no disjoint pair. A pairwise-intersecting family of two-subsets is contained in a star or triangle (if two distinct edges share v, any edge not using v must be the edge joining the other endpoints, and then only triangle edges possible).
