## 1. Split-monomial universal compression (PROVED)
Given any finite rational boundary optimum F on k spins s_0..s_{k-1}, invariant under simultaneous sign reversal, take reference s_0. Set d=k-1 and binary x_i=(1-s_0*s_i)/2. F is exactly a table on d bits. Acquire its unique multilinear coefficients c_T by Boolean-lattice Mobius inversion in O(d 2^d) rational operations. Partition these d variables into L,R of sizes floor(d/2),ceil(d/2). For each subset U of either group of size at least two introduce one bit y_U representing product_{i in U} x_i. Singleton features are original bits, empty feature is constant1. Fix a deterministic parent decomposition U={i} disjoint union V; enforce y_U=x_i*y_V using the nonnegative quadratic penalty p(u,v,y)=uv-2uy-2vy+3y, zero precisely when y=uv (all eight assignments checked).
Every original monomial T splits uniquely into at most two features, so Q=sum_T c_T y_{T intersect L} y_{T intersect R} is quadratic on expanded variables. Choose M=1+sum_T |c_T|. Maximize Q-M sum_U p. Any violation costs at least M and cannot be optimal at fixed boundary, since Q varies by at most sum|c_T| over all feature assignments. Satisfying all penalties recursively fixes every auxiliary feature and evaluates exactly F. Both directions, no guessed optimum. Homogenize each auxiliary bit by its own spin y=(1-s_0*t)/2, products expand into constant and pairwise zero-field spin terms since s_0^2=1. Preserve actual conditional private witnesses from boundary table; auxiliary assignments need not be original spins. This works for MAX; negate for MIN. General upper bound A(k)=2^floor((k-1)/2)+2^ceil((k-1)/2)-(k-1)-2. k=4,5,6,7,8 gives 1,2,5,8,15. For sparse tables, omit features unused by nonzero monomials and their deterministic parent chains; try all 2^d partitions, use best actual count. This is paid fixed-k work, or polynomial in supplied explicit table size. No claim output preserves planarity, counting, or arbitrary native incidence promise.

## 3. Representation obstruction (PROVED, not native lower bound)
A generic finite complement-symmetric k-spin table has 2^(k-1) independent values. A pairwise zero-field graph on k+r spins has at most D=1+binom(k+r,2) coefficients including constant, even with all possible edges. Choose one maximizing auxiliary word for each of the 2^(k-1) boundary words. For each such choice, map from coefficients to table is linear of rank <=D. There are finitely many choices; representable tables lie in a finite union of subspaces. If D<2^(k-1), the union cannot fill real space (nor rational/integer space: choose rational point outside the finitely many rational proper subspaces and clear denominators). Thus some rational tables require r with 1+binom(k+r,2)>=2^(k-1). k=5 lower1 upper2; k=6 lower3 upper5; k=7 lower5 upper8; k=8 lower9 upper15. Asymptotically r=Omega(2^(k/2)) for arbitrary tables, matching split upper order. No claim such generic tables arise natively or lower bound on arbitrary algorithms. A fixed handful of auxiliaries cannot universally handle unbounded interfaces just because four-spin compression is small.
