## 1. Source cut theorem
Use FG13 opposite-face identification on the planar incidence dual, retaining indexed edge per original tuple. Homogeneous parity kernel equals cut space: an even incidence-edge word constant around each tuple is a planar cycle-space word, hence a dual cut; equality of four incident dual edge values imposes identification of opposite faces. All-ones tuple word satisfies parity since element degree=3. Thus x_e=1 XOR z_u XOR z_v. Each element maps to a closed length-three walk, possibly degenerate. Any spin cut crosses at most two of its three edges. Each tuple appears in four element rows. Hence |cut|<=2n, and equality iff every row has exactly two cut edges, i.e. precisely one selected original tuple. Both directions and actual witness lift follow. This is not a new general MaxCut algorithm.

A proper 4-coloring, interpreted as GF2^2 labels, produces three nonzero linear projections. Every indexed edge appears in exactly two projected cuts; sum cut sizes=6n, so each reaches 2n. Complementary edge words are three disjoint exact covers partitioning all tuples. Conversely, two of three disjoint covers have spin charts whose pair distinguishes every edge. For loop-free K5-minor-free simple quotient (aggregate parallel weights), the constant fractional cut vector 2/3 satisfies all cycle inequalities: in the form sum_{e in F}(1-y_e)+sum_{e in C\F}y_e>=1 for odd F, each summand>=1/3 and cycle length>=3. Barahona–Mahjoub (1986), DOI 10.1007/BF02592023, identifies that polytope with convex hull of cuts for K5-minor-free graphs. The weighted value 2n is attained by an actual cut, so SAT. Exact weighted optimization on this class is polynomial by cycle-inequality separation and rational LP, including signed weights; reconstruct an integral cut by coordinate self-reduction on the optimum face. The source incidence being planar does not imply its spin quotient is planar or K5-minor-free. Fixed-minor recognition is a known polynomial algorithm, not implemented here.

## 3. Rank-graph bridge and constructive coverage target
On native three-variable exact-one factors modulo original parity plus literal guards, a nondegenerate unresolved triangle offers its three spin-edge vectors as guard directions (pair XOR equals remaining singleton modulo parity). A minimal hitting span can be generated by one selected physical edge per bad triangle. Its rank is the size of a forest spanning the selected edges, hence equivalently minimum sum_B(|B|-1) over partitions with at least two vertices of each bad triangle in the same block. Nontrivial blocks contain at most 2r vertices. Their union is a vertex cover of the graph H consisting of all edges of bad triangles. Conversely any vertex cover of H contains two vertices of every triangle, so select an edge within it per triangle; rank <=cover size. Thus r<=tau(H)<=2r. A maximal matching yields a polynomially acquired cover and rank bound and a checkable lower bound |M|/2 on this physical rank. This bridge is scoped to native triangle factors and a graphic observation quotient; arbitrary extra affine equations/virtual tables need separate treatment.

For signed weighted spin optimization a separator S of size 1..3 gives at most four relative boundary words (global complement symmetry). On a smaller shore with a private vertices, evaluate each conditional optimum after signed contraction of S into one vertex. With b>=a private vertices on the other shore, n=a+b+|S|, the exact finite response is a constant plus pairwise XOR costs on S (for three vertices four complementary classes give a nonsingular four-by-four linear system). Replace once on larger shore. Uniformly acquire separator by checking all vertex sets of size <=3. Strict recurrence 4(a+1)^4+(n-a)^4<=n^4-1: n>=2a+1, n^4-(n-a)^4 increasing in n; at n=2a+1 it suffices that (2a+1)^4>5(a+1)^4, true since ratio>=3/2 and (3/2)^4>5. Independent components compose additively. Handle hard literal guards by signed union-find first; every remaining relative spin word is feasible in the unconstrained signed objective. Aggregate parallel edges, retain dyadic exact weights and conditional lifts. Coefficient magnitude grows by at most polynomial factors per node, denominator exponent at most depth, so polynomial bit length on the charged recursive run. Each node separator discovery and exact rational arithmetic is polynomial. This is a conditional polynomial recurrence if every reachable residual has an admitted terminal or separator; no universal coverage theorem. The graph need not remain native after virtual reductions. Related existing work: SW06 degree<=3 spin elimination, FG17 weighted bond recurrence, P3PS04/ Likhosherstov et al. arXiv:1910.11142 three-adhesion Ising decomposition.
