## 3. Weighted ternary matching theorem
A mandatory-cover weighted graph has three distinct required deletion ports; bit1 means the corresponding vertex is externally matched/deleted. Other vertices are required or optional. Cost is minimum sum of signed integer edge weights of matching covering all remaining required vertices; infinity if impossible. Allow additive constant. For f:{0,1}^3 -> integers union infinity, define s_i=f(i)+f(i XOR7), i=0..3. Exact realizability iff min s_i is attained at least twice or all s_i infinite.

Necessity: take optimal matchings for a finite minimizing antipodal pair. Their symmetric difference is alternating paths and cycles. All three ports are endpoints (covered in exactly one), so some path has exactly one port; its other endpoint is optional, since all nonport required vertices covered in both. Switch that path in BOTH matchings. The new pair of complementary words differs from the old pair and total matching costs unchanged. Their optimal costs sum to at most the old minimum, so equal. No unique finite min. This holds for any graph size and arbitrary signed integer weights.

Sufficiency (constructive, no external theorem required): empty support uses isolated required internal vertex. Otherwise pad f to F on four deletion bits y of even parity by F(y)=f(low three bits), all odd words forbidden. Choose even p from minimizing complementary pair if finite; if all pair sums infinite, choose any finite p. Set g(z)=F(z XOR p), c=g(0) finite. On four required vertices use edge ij of weight g({i,j})-c if finite, omit otherwise. Interpret z as PRESENT set. Empty matching cost0; two-vertex matching has required cost; full matching cost min over three pairings. Since chosen p,p XOR15 is a tied finite minimizing complementary pair, g(0)+g(15)=min_{three pairings} [g(pair)+g(complement)], and full cost is g(15)-c. If all pair sums infinite, g(15)=infinity and all pairings infeasible. Add constant c. To map original deletion bit y_i to present bit z_i=y_i XOR p_i: when p_i=1 use old vertex directly; when p_i=0 make old vertex internal required and attach a new required leaf as external port, forcing old vertex consumed iff leaf is present. At most 8 vertices. Make fourth external port optional; a matching either covers it or leaves it uncovered, which is equivalent to deleting that optional port. Since total size before deletions is even (p even), required coverage of all other vertices selects exactly the correct fourth bit = parity of original three. Thus exact f. Signed integer weights are legitimate; use required-coverage reward M>2 sum absolute costs in maximum-weight matching, or optional-only zero clique and parity dummy with ordinary min-cost perfect matching. Decode actual external wire identities. Proof recovers FG10 unweighted 148/256 count (unique full complementary pair = 4*3^3=108 bad) without relying on library. Any weighted binary/unary response admitted by padding missing coordinates with fixed zeros. Any exact gadget survives port pins by deletion/internalization. Every nonadmitted ternary factor is admitted after fixing one port. Consequently FG10's actual-wire affine hull rank cover extends to weighted tables by retaining costs; necessary pins do not justify dropping cost differences. In particular SW05's odd-three local parity relation is weighted matchable on its three physical wires; that alone does not give an arbitrary syndrome chart a physical two-occurrence wire representation.
