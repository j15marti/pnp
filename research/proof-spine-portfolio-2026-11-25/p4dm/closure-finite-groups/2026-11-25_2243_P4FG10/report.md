## 2. Theorem A: complete ternary mandatory-cover classification
A three-port gadget is an arbitrary finite simple graph with distinct mandatory ports 0,1,2 and any mix of other mandatory and optional vertices. A boundary word x in {0,1}^3 is accepted iff deleting ports marked 1 leaves a matching covering all remaining mandatory vertices (optional may be unmatched). This is the FG08 notion. A table R is realizable iff it does NOT contain exactly one antipodal pair {x,x XOR 7}. In particular 148 of the 256 labeled masks, including the empty table, are realizable; 108 are not.

Necessity for arbitrary graph size: take matchings for an admitted complementary pair x,y=x XOR 7. Their symmetric difference is alternating paths and cycles. Each of the three ports is covered in exactly one matching and is an endpoint. A path has at most two endpoints, so at least one path contains exactly one port p. Its other endpoint cannot be a common mandatory vertex because both matchings cover that vertex; it is optional. Toggle this path in each matching, leaving all other components unchanged. Common mandatory coverage stays valid; only p changes external deletion status. Hence both x XOR 2^p and y XOR 2^p are accepted, a second antipodal pair. (All ports shared by both matchings or deleted by both, for general noncomplementary pairs, pose no issue.) For ternary words, any distinct antipodal pair is obtainable by a singleton flip of one member of the first, possibly exchanging the two endpoints. Thus the criterion is precisely exclusion of exactly one antipodal pair. Count bad masks: choose the unique fully present pair in 4 ways; each of the other three pairs has 3 possibilities (neither or one endpoint). 4*3^3=108.

Sufficiency: the table below is a compact constructive certificate under coordinate permutations and coordinate complementations. Mask M=sum_(x in R) 2^x, x encoded bit0+2*bit1+4*bit2. For each row, ports 0,1,2 mandatory, other vertices 3,4,5 optional except listed; unlisted edges absent. The sixteen representatives are canonical minimum masks of the orbits of the 148 good masks under the six coordinate permutations and eight coordinate flips. Their orbit sizes sum to 148; a finite loop over masks/permutations/flips verifies coverage. Each witness is checked by the elementary matching subset recurrence P(0)=true; for nonempty subset S, choose least u in S and P(S)=OR_{uv edge,v in S} P(S minus {u,v}); accept boundary w iff some matchable covered set S avoids deleted ports, contains all present ports and mandatory auxiliaries. ## 4. Theorem B: rank-charged local matching
For a bag and one fixed parent word, each event factor has at most two explicit signed words, row factor at most d one-hot words (or all-zero singleton), child factor at most eight words. Thus local factor descriptions polynomial even at large degree. Compute the exact affine HULL of each factor's current explicit word set by a difference basis and orthogonal complement. Conjoin these necessary GF2 equations on actual local wires. Gaussian consistency and forced wire bits, filter actual factor tables by those pins, repeat to fixed point (at most number of wires strict pins). If empty/inconsistent reject. This is necessary closure ONLY: preserve all original tables, never infer feasibility from affine hull. These virtual bits can be partial occupancies, not independent physical tuples.

Let each remaining wire be x_j=a_j+v_j*z in Gaussian chart. For each nonmatching ternary factor, candidate observations are its at most three free-wire directions. For an event factor with >3 live ports, two remaining words are bitwise complements: affine hull entails equality/complement, so all free-wire observation directions coincide and any one fixes the entire factor. Ternary event equality is also bad and handled by same rule. All row factors remain realizable: zero quota forces all wires zero; exact-one on d ports is represented by d mandatory ports and d-1 mandatory internal vertices joined completely bipartite (after external coverage, exactly d-1 ports must remain), even for arbitrary d. Already admitted ternary factors use Theorem A; admitted relations remain admitted after pinning. Any new pin-triggered filtering only fixes coordinates, so matching realizability preserved. Do not confuse general arbitrary wide masks with these particular row/event relations.

Define r* as minimum dimension of a binary subspace containing at least one ACTUAL candidate direction for each bad factor (not merely intersecting their spans). Acquire H greedily. Whenever a bad factor has no candidate contained in H, add all its candidate directions, retaining an independent basis of actual wire IDs. At most three new dimensions per addition. For any optimum H*, that factor has an actual chosen vector in H* outside the previous H; the vectors charged on successive additions are independent, because all previously charged vectors belong to previous H. Therefore acquired rank r<=3r*. All acquisition paid Gaussian tests, not a minimum-rank oracle. Pure high-degree event exceptions require only their rank (not count), no ternary multiplier.

Enumerate 2^r assignments to independent basis wires. In each branch rerun local affine/actual-word pin closure. Every original bad ternary factor has a wire forced by basis, hence at most two free coordinates; every bad equality is fixed. All other factors remain admitted. Construct mandatory-cover gadget per factor (<=6 vertices for <=3 ports with padded coordinates fixed zero; row complete bipartite if wide). For each free wire join its two named factor ports by a single edge. Ports are mandatory; wire bit1 iff that exact external edge is chosen. All optional internal vertices may be connected into a clique, adding one optional dummy if total vertices odd. A mandatory-cover matching extends to perfect matching by pairing remaining optional vertices; conversely remove optional-only added edges from a perfect matching, preserving all mandatory coverage. Ordinary deterministic maximum matching decides and supplies witness. All external wire endpoints have distinct identities, so exact external-edge selection decodes actual words. Any matching yields accepted word at every factor; any accepted joint word yields matching by local gadgets.
