## 3. Exact contextual binary consensus
Let K be the current sound 2-CNF on actual source or exterior bits, initialized by all unary/binary projections of exact acquired tables. Let Bad(a,b) mean K AND literal a AND literal b is unsatisfiable (self pair allowed). If K satisfiable, any finite set U of unit literals extends to K iff every pair in U is compatible. Proof by eliminating other variables from 2-CNF with Davis-Putnam resolution: all non-tautological resolvents have width<=2, so the exact projection to the variables in U is 2-CNF; any false clause witnesses at most two assumptions. Equivalently, implication graph closure of unit assumptions conflicts only from two units. Acquire Bad by SCC/condensation reachability: for feasible individual a,b, they conflict iff a reaches not b; a unit impossible iff a reaches not a. Include all impossible literals in every bad row.
For each exact local table R, discard any mask s incompatible with K (safe, because all source solutions satisfy K). For each remaining mask define O_s = literals individually compatible with every literal of s. Then exact binary support in K AND R is
Good_R(a,b) iff NOT Bad(a,b) AND exists surviving s with a,b in O_s.
Proof: all pairs in the combined unit set s union {a,b} must be compatible; criterion exactly tests them. No global source SAT query. Use union of outer-product bitsets O_s x O_s, then intersect with current pair support. Add all missing pairs as 2-CNF exclusions, repeat. If K inconsistent or any table loses every mask -> exact NO. Every new clause is a consequence of K AND one actual table and therefore of all source solutions. At most O(m^2) strict new pair exclusions and at most O(total table size) deletions; SCC closure, all support loops, exact bitset word costs polynomial. This is exact strongest binary consequence acquisition for K AND a single table, not completeness of iteration for arbitrary joint CSP.

Then K entails that table, so remove from further nonlinear compatibility without losing any model.
