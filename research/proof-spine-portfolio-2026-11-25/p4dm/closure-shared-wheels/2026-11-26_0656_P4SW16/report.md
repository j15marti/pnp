Here "cut" means edge boundary of a vertex partition, not vertex deletion. K=0 trivial by connectivity.

Proof: at most K disks are mixed, since each such disk contains at least one internal crossing edge by connectedness and disk edge sets are disjoint. Otherwise both shores cannot contain a full row, since then each column contributes a distinct crossing edge, giving >=d>L. Choose shore without a full row. Each nonempty row is mixed and contributes at least one horizontal cut, so there are <=L nonempty rows. As L<d, no column is full in that shore. Each nonempty column contributes a vertical cut, so <=L nonempty columns. Thus the chosen macro shore has <=L^2 vertices. Its corresponding raw shore is contained in those disks plus the at most K mixed disks. Multiplying by b or t proves the two bounds.
