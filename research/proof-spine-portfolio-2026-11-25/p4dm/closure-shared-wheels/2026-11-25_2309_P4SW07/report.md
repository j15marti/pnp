## 2. Exact low-degree contextual evaluation (recovered closure toolbox)
Let B be any satisfiable 2CNF on k bits, with an SCC model z0. Orient y_i=z_i XOR z0_i. The resulting formula is satisfied at zero and consists of negative unit clauses, negative-negative clauses, and implications. For any seed S, take its closure under directed implications. If this closure violates a negative clause, no B model contains S. Otherwise it is itself a B model, the least one containing S. Thus any squarefree monomial y_S is identically zero or equals the indicator that cl(S) is contained in a B model. For fixed degree d, collect all distinct consistent closures with <=d generators including empty. Evaluate these corresponding closed-support monomials on those same least models. Hence these functions form a basis of the degree<=d functions on B models; evaluation on this sample set is injective over ANY field. Orienting z_i= y_i or 1-y_i does not increase degree. No guessed satisfying source model: only a 2SAT relaxation model.
