## 2. Even-check pairing theorem
Let H have columns h_i, t in span(H), nonnegative binary-encoded integer weights. A nonzero functional l on span(H) with l(t)=0 has support D={i:l(h_i)=1}. Replace D by all unordered pair columns h_i XOR h_j of weights w_i+w_j. Other columns and target unchanged. Zero columns may be deleted and parallel columns reduced to cheapest, since an even number of parallel copies cancels and costs nonnegative. These are optimizer reductions, not source-variable pins.
Forward: every feasible word uses an even subset of D; pair its selected indices disjointly, then take the virtual pairs with identical cost and syndrome. Reverse: XOR actual pairs (not union), keeping other selected IDs. Syndrome exact; cancellations cannot increase nonnegative cost. Hence both optimum inequalities and equality, with actual reverse optimizing witness. New span equals ker l in old column span: every old representation of a vector in ker l uses an even number of D and can be paired; reverse inclusion immediate. Rank falls exactly1, even after zero/parallel simplification. For d=|D|<=4, new column count <=m-d+binom(d,2). Define mu=m+3 rank(H). Every such move decreases mu by at least1, since worst fill is +2. No physical planar K4 asserted. With all weights nonnegative one can always drop zero target with optimum0.
General multi-hub extension (symbolic): for a column set D let W=span(H_outside,t), ell=rank(H)-dim W. Project columns of D into span(H)/W. For ell>0, any feasible local selection projects to zero, hence partitions into disjoint minimal nonempty zero-sum subsets (circuits, including singleton projected zero). Replace D by one column for each such circuit, equal to XOR of its original columns with sum of their costs. Every forward word partitions, reverse XOR cancellation as above. New span is W (all W representations split outside plus projected-zero inside); rank drops ell. For fixed d bound enumerate all 2^d subsets and minimality; q<=2^d-1, credit mu_d=m+2^d rank strictly decreases. No variable-size exhaustive subset oracle, no claim all residuals contain such bounded D. This generalizes even pairing to multiple homogeneous wheel directions, not a new source promise.
