## 1. Structural distinctness theorem (PROVED)
Consider ANY planar simple bipartite incidence graph with tuple vertices having exactly one neighbor of each of four row colors and every row degree exactly 3. Do not initially require distinct tuple signatures or unequal occurrence sets. Closed connected components automatically have equal color sizes by degree counts (t=3r_c). For any vertex subset S, not necessarily connected, t-3r_c=o_c-i_c where o,i count boundary edges going from internal tuple to exterior row and vice versa. Thus if some color has no boundary edge then all color imbalances divisible by 3. This does not require any satisfying assignment.
(a) Two duplicate tuple signatures would create embedded K_{2,4} using their four differently colored row neighbors. Its faces are four quadrilaterals, each incident to just two row vertices (Euler, simple bipartite, 2-connected). Tuple vertices have no spare edge; every row has exactly one unused edge to an external tuple. Every such edge lies within one face. Take the set S of all external vertices within a face with at least one such edge. No edge connects interiors of distinct faces except through core vertices; all boundary edges of S go from its tuples to core rows. At most two such incidences, at most one per color, and at least two colors untouched. Their imbalances cannot be multiples of 3. Contradiction. Applies even to indexed input with duplicate signatures. No planarity-preserving repair required.
(b) Two rows with identical occurrence sets necessarily share three tuple neighbors; if same color, impossible because each tuple has one neighbor per color. Otherwise embedded K_{2,3} with three quadrilateral faces, two tuple vertices on each face boundary. Rows have no spare incidence; each tuple has exactly two spare incidences of the two other colors. Choose face with spare edge. All boundary edges of external vertices in it go from core tuples to internal rows, at most two per color and only these two colors. Again unused colors imply imbalances multiples of3, contradiction. Thus original stronger exclusion of identical occurrence sets holds automatically in any closed planar native graph, including gluing descendants. This does not assert 3-connectivity or linearity. The proof uses cyclic embedding, not Boolean consistency.
These lemmas strengthen native closure under capping: gluing open disks by indexed ports preserves simple incidence because a tuple has one stub per color, a row has three incidences, and no paired port can create repeated adjacency from a tuple to one row without repeating its color. Closed degree/count and planarity imply strict distinctness above. No duplicate-signature guard or auxiliary indexed language required.
