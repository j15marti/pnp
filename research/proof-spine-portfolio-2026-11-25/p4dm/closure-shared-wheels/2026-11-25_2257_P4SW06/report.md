## 2. Universal source-specific cut chart (PROVED)

Thus any parity solution x differs by y=x XOR 1 in ker A. On each original incidence edge (tuple i, element e), put value y_i. This is a binary Eulerian subgraph: at every tuple degree4 all four values equal; at each element degree3 the sum is zero exactly when Ay=0. Conversely any Eulerian edge word with all four values equal at each tuple comes from such a y.
In a plane connected graph the binary Eulerian edge space equals the dual cut space: a face bit assignment s assigns edge value s_f XOR s_g for its incident faces (bridges have equal faces). This follows because each face-boundary vector is Eulerian and the bounded-face vectors span the cycle space (dimension E−V+1 by Euler, or decompose cycles using Jordan separation). Every Eulerian edge word is realized, uniquely up to global face flip per connected component. Retain repeated corners when a face occurs multiple times.
At tuple i with cyclic face corners f1,f2,f3,f4, the four edge bits are s_f1 XOR s_f2, s_f2 XOR s_f3, s_f3 XOR s_f4, s_f4 XOR s_f1. Equality of all four holds iff s_f1=s_f3 and s_f2=s_f4. Union all opposite faces at all tuple nodes. ## 3. Native triangle-connectivity and a stronger initial event-chart theorem (PROVED)
For an unguarded connected source, suppose quotient G has no loop (otherwise forced selected tuple available). At every element row, the three distinct tuple edges are a closed 3-edge walk in the quotient, equivalently their endpoints have even incidence, because all cut words have even row parity. Without loops the three unordered endpoint pairs must be distinct and form an ordinary triangle. All tuple edges occur in such triangles. After aggregating parallel edges, the source triangles are edge-connected through shared edges: a path in the original connected bipartite incidence graph gives a sequence of rows joined by common original tuple edges. Hence the simple quotient is a single edge-connected union of triangles. If two distinct triangles share an edge, their other four edges form a simple four-cycle. If no such pair exists, the connected triangle family consists of one triangle, with exactly three spin vertices and a trivial exact optimum. Therefore every nontrivial loopless connected UNGUARDED native event quotient has an acquired four-cycle on four distinct event edges; with the all-one reference its four-bit XOR is zero. In the SW02 same-event chart before other projections this is a genuine homogeneous support-4 check, not just the raw source support theorem of SW05. Discovery by group row triangles by edge and compare distinct third vertices. Guarded signed charts need a separate RHS check; subsequent virtual graphs need not retain triangle connectivity. The theorem is initial, not hereditary under arbitrary eliminations.
Optional useful criterion: a proper four-coloring of the loopless G is equivalent to a partition of all original tuples into three disjoint exact covers. Two color bits give a nowhere-zero pair of cut differences; each row triangle has the three nonzero differences in GF(2)^2, so grouping by those values gives three covers. Conversely three covers assign the three nonzero values with row XOR zero in both coordinates, acquire two face charts and combine bits into four colors. Thus a four-coloring suffices for SAT, but mere SAT or both-value support does not establish four-colorability. Q,V,C verified have 1,4,40 such unordered partitions; W has four covers but no partition and two loops, so is not a loopless counterexample. Universal loopless four-colorability OPEN and not assumed.
