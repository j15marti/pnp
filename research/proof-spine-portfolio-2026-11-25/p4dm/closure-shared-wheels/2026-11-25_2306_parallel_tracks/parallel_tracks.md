## Track D: target-free weighted circuit descent
For any current feasible GF2 word s with nonnegative weights, assign signed flip gain g_i=w_i(1-2s_i). Any better feasible s' differs by d in ker H; the support of d partitions into disjoint inclusion-minimal nonempty zero-sum column sets (remove a minimal nonempty dependent set, repeat). Since gain of d is negative, some minimal circuit has negative gain. Thus s optimal iff no negative-gain circuit exists. For original unit-cost P4DM start from all-ones parity solution when unguarded, or any Gaussian parity solution when guarded; at most m strict integer decreases before optimum if a negative-gain circuit can be acquired deterministically in polynomial time or certified absent. This is a scoped exact descent characterization, not an algorithm for arbitrary binary matrices; arbitrary negative-circuit acquisition is the named OPEN obligation. On graphic factors use paid exact T-join optimizer and difference, on non-graphic factors seek short-check/separator/gain interaction. Virtual weights can be exponentially large in value even with polynomial bit length; a bound by total numeric weight is not a polynomial bit-time guarantee, so apply unit-cost descent on original source or prove another potential. At every improvement export actual witness upper bound, not unsupported source pins.
