## 4. Exact finest independent affine factors and shared hub conditioning
For any consistent H u=b', compute particular word and RREF fundamental homogeneous kernel basis, one vector per free column with that unique free coordinate. Join every coordinate appearing in one basis vector; connected components give independent affine factors. Proof: basis vectors each supported within one component, hence every combination of component restrictions is in the coset, and every coset word splits this way. Finest: if a product partition exists, projection of each basis vector to each product block lies in kernel; all but its unique free-coordinate block have no free coordinate and must be zero (RREF uniqueness). Thus no product partition can split that basis support. Objective is additive across coordinates, so compute independent minima and add, NOT enumerate Cartesian combinations.
