## 2. Stronger counterexample: Fano-path all-cut blindness even for planar cographic charts (PROVED)
Let Fano lines L_j={j,j+1,j+3} mod7 for j=0..6. Any two lines meet, and for every set of <=2 points some line is disjoint (each point lies on3 lines; two distinct points share one line, at most5 hit). These facts also checked exhaustively. Construct a planar simple graph H with two terminals s,t connected by seven internally vertex-disjoint length-three paths; color/label the three edges of path j by the distinct points of L_j (last edge j). The seven edge groups are points. There are 16 vertices,21 edges. After deletion of any two color groups, at least one entire s-t path remains. Every internal degree-two vertex can be isolated by deleting its two distinct incident colors. Therefore B-perp is exactly span{e_s+e_t}: internal entries must vanish, total parity is even, and s,t always remain in same component. Delta=1.
Any edge subset having boundary e_s+e_t must contain at least one complete s-t path because each internal degree2 vertex has even degree in that subset. Hence e_s+e_t belongs to I_S iff S contains a Fano line. No S and its complement can both contain lines, since all lines intersect. Thus ALL 63 nontrivial complementary cut quotients are zero although delta1. This is an explicit cographic and planar graph counterexample to the general visibility conjecture (even arbitrary cuts). It does not depend on approximate rank tests or arbitrary nongraphic subspaces.

Scalable family A (small regions, connected): replace each edge of a tree with the same seven-path gadget, using private seven groups per tree edge. Gluing at terminals gives a connected plane graph. Every internal vertex isolated by deletion of its two colors, every original tree vertex connected after removal of any two groups, so B-perp is all even vertex words supported on original tree vertices; delta=|V(tree)|-1. For any cut of the groups, each base edge has complete path on at most one shore. Every boundary supported on base vertices is the boundary of a subset of base edges with complete paths on that shore (local partial paths cannot occur at degree2 interiors). A tree has injective edge-to-boundary map; disjoint base edge subsets cannot share a nonzero boundary. Thus ALL cuts blind, even with arbitrarily large delta and groups of exactly three indexed edges. This is a connected direct-sum-like example, not a hard global interaction.

For any cut, one shore contains no Fano line, hence no complete path anywhere, so ALL63 cuts blind. ## 3. Bounded-size visibility obstruction with known productive retirement
Independent square construction: four corners cyclic, each side replaced by two internally disjoint length-L paths, L>=2. First edge on path p of side i has common label 2*(i mod2)+p; remaining edges have globally private distinct labels. There are g=8L-4 groups and 8L edges. Removing two groups cannot disconnect adjacent base sides: their path label sets are disjoint, and breaking a side requires hitting both disjoint paths. If two sides lost they must be opposite; corners remain in even-size components. Internal degree2 vertices each isolatable. Deleting labels {0,1} and {2,3} gives two crossing two-pair partitions of corners, so B-perp=span of all-four-corner word. Delta1. Any boundary realization of this word contains at least two full paths; their groups intersect in at most one label. Thus any exposing shore requires >=2L-1=g/4 distinct groups. Equality achieved by choosing the two opposite paths with same common label; complement has the other two parallel paths. Every singleton and pair cut blind, and any fixed bounded-size cut search fails for large L. Yet the exposing cut can have small full syndrome width; no intrinsic computational obstruction.
FG13 physical-star retirement remains valid and is a robust bypass. Square corner star e_v not in B, since dot product with corner word1. It touches four groups; merge three into one to leave at most two support groups. Its image dies, delta drops to0. At most two binary merges, initial groups size<=2 and merged group size<=6, constant raw rank cap. Fano tree: one star basis per non-root terminal can equivalently use physical branch-cut words; simpler coarsen each gadget's seven groups into two (five merges per edge, combined group <=18 edges). Because the cut space splits across edge-gadgets of a tree, K/K2 then zero. This retires delta in O(number of base edges) merges with constant local group sizes on projected chart; actual nonlinear interface acquisition still separate. Fano grid: coarsening seven groups to two also retires delta, but groups large and no bounded cost claim. Tests independently recompute delta after coarsening. Therefore cut-quotient visibility is not necessary for retirement; retirement alone still not a complete solver or proof of low full boundary rank.
