Acquire/check embedding, orient edges left-to-right. For each connected component, choose one dual root face and set its height to zero.
By SW10 and planar integral circulation: for any PM word x, x-M has zero divergence. Integrate on dual paths to obtain unique integer heights after gauge fixing; differences around closed dual walks vanish by primal cut conservation. Conversely face differences have zero divergence (cyclic telescoping), and binary edge bounds make them an actual PM. For oriented edge e, x_e=M_e+h_p-h_q, where p,q are incident signed faces (a bridge has p=q). Binary bounds are h_p-h_q<=1-M_e and h_q-h_p<=M_e. These bounds are attained as individual extrema, not asserted jointly attainable.
Define threshold Boolean t_(f,k) iff h_f>=k, for L_f<k<=U_f; extend by constants below/above range. Monotonicity t_(f,k+1)=>t_(f,k). Any difference inequality h_p-h_q<=c is exactly the set of implications t_(p,k)=>t_(q,k-c) for k=L_p..U_p. Forward immediate, reverse take k=h_p. Constants handled by unit clauses (true fixed). Heights reconstruct uniquely as L_f plus number of true thresholds. Thus the raw matching chart is exactly a 2CNF with O(F^2) Boolean variables and polynomial clauses, no PM enumeration. Fixed physical edge x_e=b becomes two difference inequalities h_p-h_q<=b-M_e, h_q-h_p<=M_e-b, hence 2CNF, including arbitrary signed/unsigned literal guards. ## 3. Polynomial constructive majority obstruction from an exact binary hull
Let S be the projection onto a chosen scope of K AND one explicit mode union, with membership by 2SAT over modes. Pair hull H consists of all Boolean words whose unary/binary restrictions are attained in S. For a word w in H minus S, obtain actual pair witnesses p_ij in S agreeing with w on positions i,j (via feasible mode and self-reduction). Then construct a triple of members with majority outside S in polynomial time, not exponentially many model queries.
Invariant a_(i,j) agrees with w on prefix 1..i and coordinate j>i. Initialize a_(1,j)=p_(1,j). To obtain a_(i,j), take coordinatewise majority of a_(i-1,i), a_(i-1,j), p_(i,j). First two agree on prefix i-1, first and third at i, second and third at j. If this majority is outside S, return the three actual predecessor members and their invalid majority. Otherwise retain it. If no violation occurs, a_(B-1,B)=w in S, contradiction. O(B^2) pair witnesses and majority membership tests, with explicit B-bit storage and paid polynomial self-reductions. This is a constructive proof of the standard Boolean majority characterization of bijunctivity: every 2CNF (also existential projection of 2CNF with auxiliaries) closed under coordinatewise majority; conversely any majority-closed relation equals its binary hull. The trivial scopes of size <=2 require no triple. Lift scope witnesses separately to actual full K+mode words; full majority still satisfies K but violates the factor when scope majority invalid. This is a stronger diagnostic than affine-rank or missing pair alone.
Any strong guard making every nonempty local fiber bijunctive must separate the three witnesses (some guarded Boolean coordinate nonconstant on them), since otherwise the counterexample persists. For physical literal guards, fixed-bit fibers are 2CNF in thresholds, so full majority preserves those guards too. This yields concrete guard-obstruction targets, not a universal small guard or acquisition recurrence.
