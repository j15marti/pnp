\section{Review: faster partial acquisition is not an ARC successor}
SIGNED-TWIN-REFINE1 replaces the signed-twin macro's cubic certification scan
by a source-linear partition-refinement implementation using
$O(n\log M)$ work bits.  This is a genuine same-realization improvement for
that partial member, not a new achievable all-SAT pair.  With
$n=M^{u+o(1)}$ and explicit children of exponent $b$, its local recursive
ledger is $C=1$ and $\sigma=\max\{u,b\}$; the Williams product test would
require $\max\{u,b\}<\phi-1$.  On formulas with no accepted signed-twin class
the member safely fails, and the unchanged fallback remains identity-leading
by PREFIX-FALLBACK-NOEDGE1.  Consequently the SAT-specific ARC successor and
P versus NP remain \textsc{Open}.
