\paragraph{Fixed preprocessing portfolios are not universal transfers.}
MODELBRIDGE-0009 formalizes a partial-controller wrapper.  If a polynomial
front end $P$ soundly answers some formulas and returns $\bot$ on the rest,
then any hypothetical uniform polynomial SAT decider $B$ yields a uniform
polynomial wrapper $P$-else-$B$; this composition does not turn $P$ into an
all-input extractor.  A seeded finite audit of eight explicit rules found 26
noncovered formulas among 36 exact Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ samples.  That
noncoverage is not hardness and is not extrapolated.  The surviving obligation
is an all-input, representation-invariant extraction theorem with acquisition,
simulation, and reconstruction costs charged in the same realization.
