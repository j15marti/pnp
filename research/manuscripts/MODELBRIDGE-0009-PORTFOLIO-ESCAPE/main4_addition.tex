\paragraph{Fixed-portfolio bridge guard (MODELBRIDGE-0009).}
Let a sound polynomial partial controller $P$ return a correct SAT bit or
$\bot$.  For every total correct uniform decider $B$, the wrapper that uses
$P$ on its success domain and otherwise calls $B$ is total and correct, with
runtime $N^{\max\{d_P,c_B\}+o(1)}$ when the component exponents are $d_P$ and
$c_B$.  Thus, even under $\mathsf{SAT}\in\mathsf P$, correctness does not make
a proper fixed portfolio cover all inputs or provide a successful portfolio
transcript on a $\bot$ instance.  This blocks source-local extraction only:
it does not prove that $B$ internally avoids every analogous semantic object.
A universal lower bound still requires a representation-invariant, fully
charged extractor from every decider execution.  P versus NP remains open.
