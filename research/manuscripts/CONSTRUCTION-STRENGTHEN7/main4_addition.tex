\section{Resolution acquisition enlarges PRE-A but not the ARC region}

The endpoint repair now makes the remaining distinction especially sharp.
A new source-first partial member, \textsc{PIVOT-FACTOR1}, tries one charged
Davis--Putnam variable elimination, factors the implicit residual primal
graph, and completes an exact dry run before any SAT call.  It accepts only if
\[
 \max_i|Q_i|\le N^{1/2}\operatorname{polylog}N,qquad
 \sum_i|Q_i|\le N^{11/10}\operatorname{polylog}N,qquad
 T_{\rm local}\le N^{6/5}\operatorname{polylog}N.
\]
Every failed pivot, raw resolution pair, exact duplicate comparison,
connectivity step, header pass, replay and repeated child execution is
charged.  One child buffer and all live state fit
$N^{1/2+o(1)}$ space.  Davis--Putnam projection and AND over independent
components prove exact SAT semantics.  This is a new \textsc{Proved/Exact}
PRE-A member on a nonvacuous infinite family; it is not an all-input machine.

The obstruction is exact.  For
\[
 X_m=\bigwedge_i(x\vee a_i)\wedge\bigwedge_j(\neg x\vee b_j),
\]
eliminating $x$ creates $m^2$ binary resolvents in one component, while
eliminating any leaf leaves a connected component of size $N-O(\log m)$.
Thus every pivot violates the fixed-power maximum-child cap.  More generally,
connected regular 3-XOR formulas have no unit or pure literals and no
component split; standard non-growing BVE replaces twelve incident
width-three clauses by twenty-four distinct width-four resolvents at every
variable.  A six-variable parity instance grows from 70 to 78 canonical
bytes for every first pivot.

Consequently this construction adds no uniform all-length ARC point.  Its
accepted phase again realizes
$K\mapsto\max\{6/5,3/5+K/2\}$, but ordinary emitted children may fall in the
UNKNOWN region, so conditional elimination of $K$ cannot be invoked.
The local endpoint transition is closed; all-input acquisition remains open,
and $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textsc{Open}.
