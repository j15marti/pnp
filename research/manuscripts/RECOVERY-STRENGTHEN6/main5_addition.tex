\subsection*{RECOVERY-STRENGTHEN6: the strongest valid WRAPPER composition}

Earlier WRAPPER work already provides (i) a terminating exact general-input
source-backed solver with replay and bounded collision-checked sharing, and
(ii) exact conditional recursive accounting that removes the inherited
polynomial exponent $K$.  MARKED-CUTSET-BLOCK1 now provides (iii) a favorable
source-first one-level compiler on a supplied marked subclass.  These are
three different achievements; no reviewed implementation has all three
properties with favorable all-input bounds.

The exact reusable theorem is promise-relative.  If one uniform marked grammar
is hereditary under every emitted child, and if nested annotations, headers,
renumbering, verification, replay and ancestors all fit the one-level ledger,
then recursive evaluation gives
\[
 T(N)=N^{6/5+o(1)},\qquad S(N)=N^{1/2+o(1)}
\]
on that hereditary domain.  This is the recovered RECURCLOSE1 fixed point
with $r=11/10$, $\eta=3/5$ and $\theta=1/2$.  It is not an all-input SAT
algorithm.

The current flat grammar fails the hereditary premise on an asymptotic family.
For any growing ordinary unmarked child $G_m$, a valid marked parent with one
cut variable, one block and $\Theta(m^2)$ charged tautological padding has
length $N=\Theta(m^2)$ and emits $G_m$ within the $\sqrt N\log N$ cap under
both assignments.  The successor lacks the magic marker and returns UNKNOWN.
Both identical child occurrences count unless separately deduplicated by a
charged exact mechanism.

The hoped-for two-type double wrapper therefore still lacks its
ordinary-CNF-to-hereditary-marked edge.  A trivial one-block annotation with
linear overhead fails the shrink check; padding to acceptance expands to
$\Omega(m^2/\log^2m)$ and yields no strict complete-cycle contraction.  A
fallback to the assumed SAT decider restores $K$.  Exact general-input replay
does not cure this: its clause scopes, source rereads, Shannon multiplicity
and ancestor state have no proved favorable worst-case bounds.

The next construction target is one causal all-input transition that either
acquires a recursively valid certificate, source-decides within the local
ledger, or emits another exact strict contraction.  P versus NP remains OPEN;
conditional $K$ elimination and the exact FLMV endpoint guard are preserved.
