\section{Mixed answers do not repair pointwise necessity}

RT-0017 proves \texttt{RECOGNIZABLE-EASY-ISLAND-BARRIER1}.  If a subfamily
$S$ is uniformly recognizable and SAT restricted to $S$ has a uniform
polynomial-time classifier, then for every total exact SAT decider $A$ the
recognize-first wrapper
\[
 A_{R,B}(x)=\begin{cases}B(x)&R(x)=1,\\A(x)&R(x)=0\end{cases}
\]
is total and exact, while on $S$ it pays only the fully charged recognizer and
classifier costs and never reaches $A$.  The proof is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT}
and uses one SAME\_REALIZATION execution.

The theorem is not confined to constant-answer sets.  Canonical paired
$K_{3,3}$ blocks give infinitely many satisfiable ordinary exact Monotone
3-SAT-$(2,2)$ formulas.  Adjoining D\"ocker's fixed 198-variable,
264-clause unsatisfiable exact-$(2,2)$ component gives infinitely many
unsatisfiable formulas.  The union is syntactically recognizable, has a
polynomial classifier, contains both answers, and has encoded length
$\Theta(k\log k)$.  The fixed unsatisfiable component is a \textbf{KNOWN
THEOREM}; the wrapper and paired-block verification are independent.

Therefore \texttt{NONMONOCHROMATIC-REPAIR1} is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: excluding only monochromatic islands cannot yield
pointwise all-decider compatibility extraction, state exposure, memory,
communication, or superpolynomial-time necessity.  The worst-case target
\texttt{SOURCE-PASS-ACCESS1}, arbitrary-exponent elimination,
nonrelativization, nonalgebrization, and P versus NP remain \textbf{OPEN}.
