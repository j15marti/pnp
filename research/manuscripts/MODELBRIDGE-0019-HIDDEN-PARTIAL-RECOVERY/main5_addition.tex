\subsection{Hidden partial recovery and the white-box boundary (MODELBRIDGE-0019)}
\paragraph{Status: PROVED/EXACT in the declared hidden-source query model; generic SAT transfer FALSIFIED/RETIRED; P versus NP remains OPEN.}
If an unknown $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ is accessible only through binary answers, every deterministic exact procedure that outputs $r$ certified coordinates of $x$ needs exactly $r$ answers.  Indeed, each leaf output fixes at least $r$ bits, so its fiber has size at most $2^{n-r}$; a depth-$q$ tree has at most $2^q$ leaves and therefore $q\ge r$.  Coordinate queries meet the bound.  This does not transfer generically to explicit SAT input: the unique-witness formula
\[
\bigwedge_{i:x_i=1}(z_i\vee z_i\vee z_i)\wedge
\bigwedge_{i:x_i=0}(\neg z_i\vee\neg z_i\vee\neg z_i)
\]
reveals all $n$ bits by syntax parsing with zero SAT calls.  The example is a hostile control, not a proper exact-$(2,2)$ core member.  Thus query count does not imply arbitrary-decider time, memory, communication, or internal exposure.  The surviving target is a white-box, source-specific, hereditary, $K$-independent pass charging direct inspection, preprocessing, oracle calls and peak workspace.
