\section{ARC phase-potential review}
PHASE-MULTINORM-ARC1 adds a finite-state, phase-dependent potential theorem to
the ARC spine.  It changes no WRAPPER construction status.  The existing
double wrapper, STREAMCOMP1, ORBITBATCH1, density-gated member, and
source-visible rectangular-pigeonhole recognizer retain their proved or
conditional interfaces.  None supplies the new premise that every
general-CNF achievable pair has an outgoing original-scale successor
satisfying one certified graph edge.

In particular, a WRAPPER implementation cannot claim the phase theorem by
tagging its control locations.  The same uniform exact machine must justify
each joint time--space pair, every affine edge bound must include acquisition,
access, generation, and failed work, and the phase graph must be fixed rather
than grow with input length.  The all-input original-SAT-scale realization,
the joint endpoint implication, and P versus NP remain \textsc{Open}.
