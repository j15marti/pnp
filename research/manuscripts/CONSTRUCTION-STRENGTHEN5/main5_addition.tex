\section{Robust general core in the remaining WRAPPER domain}
Let $P_0$ be the complete four-clause even-parity block. To a proper
3-CNF $F$ append two binary clauses forcing $h=r$ and one parity
connector $P_0(h,v,w_v)$ for
every source variable $v$. The image is satisfiable exactly when $F$ is.
Every variable has both signs and there are no units, so unit propagation
and pure-literal elimination leave it unchanged. The exact existing
portfolio still returns UNKNOWN/FAIL. The $n$ connector triangles share
$h$ and have positive-pair obstruction vertex-cover number $n+1>2$;
the separate candidate $\{h,r\}$ is defeated by assigning $h=r=1$.
This includes the published empty-clause shortcut semantics. No claim
that general exhaustive unit/pure preprocessing is logspace is needed:
on this image the rules are inert after inspection.

The output has linear encoded size and regular local random access in the
declared dense encoding. Consequently any exact fallback bound holding on
the entire remaining domain transfers jointly to general proper 3-SAT.
This does not prove that such a fallback is slow. It proves that finite
prefix coverage has not supplied the required all-input contraction.
Recognizing and removing the guard exposes a source core of comparable
size, not a child of size $N^{h+o(1)}$ for $h<1$.

The next construction must act on that general core: either solve it or
emit genuinely smaller SAT children whose actual aggregate encoded volume,
largest child, construction/replay work and simultaneously retained state
meet the charged recursive contract on every input. Executed calls count
with multiplicity. Conditional elimination of $K$ is unchanged. P versus
NP remains OPEN.
