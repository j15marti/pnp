\section{The exact all-input target after robust fallback auditing}
The all-input route must use one uniform exact implementation at every
recursively reached state. On length $N$, actual executed child lengths
$m_i$ (with multiplicity) may be uneven, but must satisfy
\[
 \sum_i m_i\le N^{1+o(1)},\qquad
 \max_i m_i\le N^{h+o(1)}
\]
for fixed $h<1$. Total discovery, failed attempts, certification,
construction, access, replay and combination must be $N^{1+o(1)}$;
simultaneously retained indexes, buffers, descendants and ancestor state
must be $N^{p+o(1)}$ for fixed $p<1$, plus one charged child buffer.
These are sufficient conditions, not a claim that every valid route has
this scalar form. The ARC alternative must instead produce actual joint
all-input machines entering a verified forbidden region at a finite stage.

CONSTRUCTION-STRENGTHEN5 proves that the current portfolio remains far
from this target even after exhaustive unit propagation and pure-literal
elimination. Every proper 3-CNF $F$ has a linear-size, locally indexable,
SAT-equivalent guarded image with no unit or pure literals. It is connected,
contains binary clauses that make the ARC2 parity scanner return UNKNOWN,
and defeats constants, Horn, 2-CNF, components and every size-two strong
Horn backdoor. Therefore a favorable simultaneous bound for the whole
remaining domain transfers, with only polylogarithmic time and logarithmic
space overhead, to general proper 3-SAT. Stripping the guard merely recovers
a $\Theta(N)$ source core and is not fixed-power shrinkage.

This is a PROVED/EXACT limitation of the declared portfolio, supported by
finite tests, not an unrestricted SAT lower bound. Conditional $K$
elimination and aggregate-volume composition are preserved. P versus NP
and the all-input favorable transition remain OPEN.
