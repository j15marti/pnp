\section{Flattening a virtual source into a finite partial portfolio}
An exact virtual-source wrapper can be flattened into a fixed ordered
portfolio without multiplying child volume by failed attempts.  Each member
must finish a source-first dry run and return FAIL, DECIDE, or an immutable
PLAN before any SAT child executes.  Alias acquisition, per-bit access,
failed scans, state, replay, buffers and winner multiplicity are charged.
For alias exponent $\alpha$ the combined domain is the alias preimage of the
union of member domains, with
\[
 R=\max\{a,\max_j(g+\alpha r_j)\},\quad
 B=\max\{b,s,\max_j\alpha p_j\},\quad
 H=\max_j\alpha h_j,\quad V=\max_j\alpha v_j.
\]
Recovery7 applies only if all emitted ordinary-SAT boundary children are
covered.  Internal same-scale aliases require finite acyclic flattening, not
their own hereditary wrapper type.

For GUARD-STRIP, adding pure-literal DECIDE after PIVOT preserves the favorable
ledger and accepts the earlier lifted-cross control: its $a_i,b_j$ variables
are pure positive.  This repairs the claim that the family defeats the whole
portfolio.  A sign-balanced lifted control makes both members return FAIL,
so collective all-input coverage, recursive closure and P versus NP remain
OPEN.
