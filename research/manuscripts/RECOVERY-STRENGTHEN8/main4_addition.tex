\section{Alias--portfolio flattening and boundary-only closure}
RECOVERY-STRENGTHEN8 recovers the exact composition rule missing between the
typed accounting and the new concrete double wrapper.  If a source-only alias
of size $N^{\alpha+o(1)}$ has acquisition/access exponents $(a,g)$ and a
fixed sequential portfolio has member ledgers $(r_j,p_j,h_j,v_j)$, completing
every dry run before child execution gives
\[
 R=\max\{a,\max_j(g+\alpha r_j)\},\quad
 B=\max\{b,s,\max_j\alpha p_j\},\quad
 H=\max_j\alpha h_j,\quad V=\max_j\alpha v_j.
\]
Failed members contribute local work only; just the first certified winner
contributes executed child volume.  If every actual boundary child re-enters
the covered domain, aggregate recursive closure gives
\[
 C=\max\{1,R,(V-H)/(1-H)\},\qquad P=\max\{B,H\}.
\]
Same-scale acyclic aliases are internal phase nodes and do not themselves
need recursive coverage.

The recovery also corrects an overbroad reading of Construction8.  Its
lifted-cross family defeats PIVOT and pure affine treatment, but after guard
stripping a pure-literal member decides it with zero children.  Sign-balanced
controls still defeat both members.  No all-input or successor-coverage
theorem follows; conditional $K$ elimination and P versus NP remain OPEN.
