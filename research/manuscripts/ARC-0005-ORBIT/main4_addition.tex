\section{Certified orbit acquisition and a restricted low-space VALUE cell}
The prior WRAPPER already supplies exact AND/OR semantics, ordinary small leaves,
and semantic recursive closure. The present addition targets acquisition and
live storage rather than reopening those interfaces.

For a source CNF $F$, accept a signed permutation only after verifying that it
commutes with negation and maps the whole clause set to itself. If $R$ contains
one literal from each orbit meeting a clause $C\in F$, then
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(F)=\bigvee_{\ell\in R}
 \operatorname{SAT}(F\upharpoonright(\ell=1)).
\]
This is the orbit-cover extension of the known clausal-fixing rule; a literal
and its negation in one orbit give negation fixing
\cite[Theorems~3.2--3.3]{arc0005Orbitopal}. Future restrictions must be
transported too. Fixed-label antichains therefore remain valid controls.

The new source-scanning balanced-pigeonhole VALUE member has a proposed joint
resource proof, pending independent RED/WRITE review. Its first pass checks
$p$ positive rows of width $h$, $n=ph$, balance $h/2\le p\le2h$, and
$e=h\binom p2$ distinct negative binary clauses. Only then are variable-indexed
arrays allocated. Since $e\ge n^{3/2}/(4\sqrt2)$, those arrays fit
$M^{2/3+o(1)}$ space even on subsequently rejected inputs. A second pass and
an $n$-record incidence sort verify complete negative components with one
variable from each row. The exact zero-child answer is $p\le h$, in
$M^{1+o(1)}$ time. No SAT call, source pointer, or hidden setup is used.

This realizes a favorable \emph{restricted} cell, not an achievable general
SAT pair. It cannot be inserted after a materializing parser and retain its
space claim. The orbit prefilter separately passed 1,776 answer checks inside
the byte-verified original WRAPPER. Four-variable rigid SAT/UNSAT controls
have only the identity signed automorphism, so neither universal symmetry
coverage nor ARC contraction follows. The SAT-specific finite-hitting and
all-CNF joint-resource implications remain \textsc{Open}; P versus NP
remains open. Detailed code, proofs, ablation, and pending-sync records are in
\path{research/agents/lead/2026-10-08_050150_LEAD_ARC-0005-ORBIT.tex}.
