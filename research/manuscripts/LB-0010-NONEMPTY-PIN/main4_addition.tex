\paragraph{LB-0010 nonempty-pin equivalence guard.}
Excluding the empty restriction does not by itself repair the forced-service
route.  For a proper monotone clean source $F$ and anchor $x$, append
$(z\vee a\vee b)\wedge(\neg z\vee\neg x\vee\neg c)$ with fresh variables.
Pinning $z$ to either value leaves a factor that extends every assignment of
$F$, so the requested nonempty coordinate equals $\mathsf{SAT}(F)$ with
linear encoded-size overhead.  Conversely, one pin compiles linearly to
ordinary proper 3-CNF.  Thus the unrestricted one-coordinate service is
linearly interreducible with its source SAT language; a superpolynomial lower
bound for it is already separation-strength.  The result adds no ARC point.
