\paragraph{LB-0010 nonempty-pin accounting.}
Requiring a genuinely nonempty restriction does not remove the decision
coordinate.  A linear proper-monotone two-clause pin lift makes either query
$z=0$ or $z=1$ equal the original SAT bit.  An evaluator for that coordinate
therefore gives a source decider, while a supplied SAT decider evaluates it
after a linear residual/proper-3-CNF compilation and retains its exponent
$K$.  Buffering and virtual input access remain separately charged.  No
strict successor, exponent contraction, or time--space endpoint is added.
Balanced-source preservation, a fixed restricted service compiler, and P
versus NP remain OPEN.  See
