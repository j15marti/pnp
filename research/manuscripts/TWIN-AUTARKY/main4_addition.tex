\section{Signed-twin autarkies and the remaining coverage barrier}
The TWIN-AUTARKY review strengthens the existing three-way macro to one
equisatisfiable residual: a fixed mixed assignment satisfies every
clause touching an exact signed-twin class. All disjoint nontrivial
classes may be eliminated together. The prior gated partition-refinement
costs are retained, while two unnecessary branches are removed.
This improves a restricted member, not the all-input ARC relation.
Minimally unsatisfiable formulas admit no nontrivial autarky, so
autarky-only contraction cannot establish general coverage.
P versus NP remains \textsc{Open}.
