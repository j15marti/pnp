\subsection{Predecision call-order laundering (SYNTHESIS-0015)}
\paragraph{Status: PROVED/EXACT for the declared call-relative theorem,
independent review pending; P versus NP remains OPEN.}
For any total decider $B$, an ordinary transducer may inline $B$ on the source
and emit a fixed satisfiable or unsatisfiable constant CNF from the answer.
It makes zero calls to a separately named SAT program and has constant child
size and access cost, but inherits $B$'s full time and space.  Hence
``predecision'', ``before the supplied solver'', and ``no current-scale SAT
call'' do not imply a $K$-independent source bound when interpreted only by a
named-call trace.  The repaired target is
\texttt{EXTERNALLY-BOUNDED-SOURCE-PASS1}: one fixed pass with explicit bounds
on all source computation, hereditary bit-local semantics, complete
SAME\_REALIZATION charging and a model-matched forbidden landing.  That target
and P versus NP remain OPEN.  The bounded audit passed 12,442 checks.  See
