\section{Aggregate child volume and the raw-restriction test}
The independently reviewed BRIDGE-STRENGTHEN1 replaces scalar fanout by
actual serviced child volume. With encoded lengths $m_i\ge1$, it suffices
to have $\sum_i m_i\le N^{1+o(1)}$ and $\max_i m_i\le N^{h+o(1)}$,
retaining every charged local-time, sequential-generation, space and
recursive-closure requirement. Repeated evaluations count repeatedly.
The inequality $\sum_i m_i^{1+\delta}\le(\max_i m_i)^\delta\sum_i m_i$
preserves near-linear recursive time and conditional elimination of $K$;
space remains $N^{\max(p,h)+o(1)}$ under the same live-state contract.

CONSTRUCTION-STRENGTHEN2 tests a concrete raw Shannon emitter. For proper
clauses, total residual literal volume is exactly
$\sum_C (w_C-r_C)2^{t-r_C}$. At width at most three it is at least
$2^{t-2}(L-dt)$. If every raw child is serviced, positive child lengths
force $t=O(\log N)$ under near-linear volume. For
$d\log N=o(L)$ and $N=O(L\log L)$, at least one raw child then has
$\Omega(N/\log N)$ bits, violating fixed-power shrinkage.
Certified pruning, propagation, factoring and shared representation are
outside this raw-emitter obstruction and must be separately charged.

This broadens the sufficient resource contract and eliminates one
specific construction, not an arbitrary SAT algorithm.
