\section{Red-team audit: strict promise step and shielded fallback repair}

The \textsc{EXTREME-1} terminal is exact: it tests the two extreme
assignments and the at most three one-bit repairs named by one falsified
clause on each side.  Source-pointer implementation takes
$N^{1+o(1)}$ time, $N^{o(1)}$ writable space and zero child calls.  It
strictly enlarges the corrected recursively covered promise on the family
$\Psi_m$, and terminal absorption preserves the joint $(6/5,1/2)$ promise
point.  This is not a PRE-A phase and gives no all-input or UNSAT coverage.

The componentized strong-Horn/dual-Horn proposal is not promoted: its current
component map uses $\Theta(N)$ derived state.  Empty residual clauses, raw
duplicates, shared component/Horn buffers and width-four PIVOT successors are
also unresolved proof obligations.

Adding \textsc{EXTREME-1} invalidates ARC6's unqualified fallback equality.
For $F=(\neg x\vee\neg y\vee\neg z)$, all-false satisfies the complete old
embedding $G(\widehat G(\mathsf{Iso}(F)))$, so the new terminal catches it.
Thus no equality $\mathcal A(U_{\rm new})=\mathcal A(\SAT)$ is inherited
without changing the reduction.

The exact repair is to conjoin, before isolation, two disjoint NAE triples
\[
Q=(a\vee b\vee c)\wedge(\neg a\vee\neg b\vee\neg c)\wedge
(d\vee e\vee f)\wedge(\neg d\vee\neg e\vee\neg f).
\]
Every model of $Q$ has at least two true and two false variables, so
\textsc{EXTREME-1} declines throughout the equality-preserving guard map.
ARC6's universal theorem already handles every nonempty proper source; apply
it to $F\wedge Q$.  Therefore the scoped shielded UNKNOWN domain again has
$\mathcal A(U_3)=\mathcal A(\SAT)$ with the old local-access ledger.  The
all-input PRE-A compiler and P versus NP remain
OPEN; conditional $K$ elimination is unchanged.
