\paragraph{REDTEAM-STRENGTHEN10: exact-occurrence modular firewall.}
For the infinite disjoint three-variable parity family $B_k$ in the signed
exact-$(2,2)$ domain, either one-variable residual depends essentially on
$3k-1$ source variables.  Any existential proper exact-$(2,2)$ repair $G$
must contain those variables, and $3M(G)=4v(G)$ forces
$v(G)\ge3k$ and $M(G)\ge4k$, exactly the parent counts.  A four-clause
XOR/XNOR gadget with one auxiliary attains both bounds and has identical
compact dense-encoding length.  Thus the natural compiler repair is not
minimal, but no arbitrary exact-$(2,2)$ repair gives strict contraction on
this family.  The family is affine and Gaussian-easy; this is a hostile
recurrence control, not hardness evidence.  Conditional $K$ elimination
remains uninstantiated, and P versus NP remains OPEN.  See
