\paragraph{SYNTHESIS-0008: recognizable-island quantifier barrier.}
If a language is constant on an infinite uniformly recognizable set $S$,
then every total exact decider has a uniform recognize-first wrapper that is
asymptotically no slower than the recognizer on all of $S$.  Therefore no
formula-intrinsic property containing such an island can by itself force a
pointwise super-recognizer cost in every exact execution.  This exact theorem
consolidates the recent high-treewidth and exponential-rank paired,
connected, and nonpaired clean-core counterexamples.  It does not refute a
worst-case lower bound with quantifiers $\forall A\,\exists F$.  The next
concrete external target is the bit-local FLMV-source reduction to Monotone
3-Sat*-(2,2); arbitrary-exponent bootstrapping and P versus NP remain OPEN.
