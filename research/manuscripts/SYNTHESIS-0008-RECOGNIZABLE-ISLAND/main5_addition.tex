\paragraph{SYNTHESIS-0008: structural pointwise necessity retired.}
For any uniformly recognizable constant-answer island $S$ and any total
exact decider $A$, branch on membership in $S$, return the constant there,
and run $A$ elsewhere.  This SAME\_REALIZATION wrapper is exact and costs
only the recognizer on $S$.  Hence connectedness, nonpairedness, semantic
rank, treewidth, or another intrinsic adjective cannot yield a pointwise
all-decider lower bound while its class contains such an island.  The theorem
does not change the OPEN worst-case UCORE-TIME1 quantifiers.  The fixed-block
H22 localizer is retained; its bit-local FLMV-source first leg and every
separation-bearing bootstrap remain OPEN.  P versus NP remains open.
