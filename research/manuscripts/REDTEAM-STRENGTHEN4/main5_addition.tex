\section{Canonical-byte repair and the surviving WRAPPER barrier}

An independent cross-format audit falsified the exact-byte portion of the
latest ARC adapter.  It used positive-even/negative-odd zig-zag coding, while
the Construction and Recovery encoders use positive-odd/negative-even.  At
ULEB boundaries the sign change can alter byte length.  The old test compared
two routines sharing the same convention and therefore missed the failure.

The repaired endpoint path fixes a total dense CNF1 grammar, exact maximum
and EOF rules, malformed-input rejection, and out-of-range virtual reads.  It
adds one tautological exact-3CNF clause per variable before offset renaming;
this both attains the maximum identifier and guarantees the density needed by
an $O(\log N)$-space parser.  All records then have fixed length, and the
adapter's size, local-access and simultaneous-space bounds are
\textsc{Proved/Exact}.  A deterministic cross-parser test rejected nine
malformed strings and exposed the old global sign reversal.

Primary literature also closes the separate local Cook-stream question.
The classical clause-explicit quasilinear reduction cited explicitly by
Jahanjou--Miles--Viola, followed by fixed three-clause Tseitin blocks, supplies
the required locally indexed exact 3CNF.  Thus the transfer from a completed
joint $(6/5,1/2)$ SAT machine to the FLMV forbidden region no longer depends
on an unspecified serialization lemma.  This is not a SAT algorithm.

The load-bearing WRAPPER premise remains exactly:
\[
 \boxed{\text{From the assumed polynomial SAT solver, construct on every
 source input a favorable phase before any parent-scale semantic call.}}
\]
Discovery, failed alternatives, repeated child executions with multiplicity,
replay, buffers, indexes, parser state and ancestor state all count.  Earlier
RECURCLOSE1, STREAMCOMP1, CYCLEGAIN1 and ORBITBATCH1 results prove conditional
accounting or exact source-backed semantics; none implements this premise.

The proposed hereditary marked grammar remains promise-relative.  Its
aggregate recurrence is favorable after charging multiplicity, but exact
balanced quoting, inherited contexts, dense rebasing, header passes and
malformed-depth caps still require a proof, and no cheap compiler produces
the certificates for arbitrary CNFs.  The parity-star scanner also has an
infinite UNKNOWN family on which its current implementation executes
$\Omega(N^2/\log^2N)$ pair probes; a time cap preserves soundness only by
losing coverage.  Conditional $K$ elimination survives, but $P$ versus $NP$
remains \textsc{Open}.
