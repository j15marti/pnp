\section{Red-team repair of the local endpoint transition}

The exact-byte statement in ARC-STRENGTHEN4 is retired as written.  The
repository's Construction and Recovery artifacts encode signed literals by
\[
 \operatorname{zz}(+u)=2u-1,\qquad \operatorname{zz}(-u)=2u,
\]
whereas ARC-STRENGTHEN4 used the opposite parity.  Its self-comparison test
therefore could not detect that every decoded sign was reversed.  Its empty
case also declared a maximum variable that no literal attained.

The fixed-width idea is repaired by an explicit total language
\texttt{DENSE-CNF1}.  Canonical ULEB integers are required; the declared
maximum is attained (or is zero for the empty formula); maximum identifier,
clause count and widths are polynomially bounded in the complete encoded
length; and exact EOF is checked.  Malformed strings denote NO.  A streaming
parser takes $N\log^{O(1)}N$ time and $O(\log N)$ space.

Given a locally indexed exact 3CNF with $q,C\le n\log^{O(1)}n$, append the
$q$ tautologies $(v\vee\neg v\vee v)$, choose the least $b$ with
$q\le2^{7b-3}$, put $A=2^{7b-3}$, and rename $v$ to $A+v$.  Under the
positive-odd convention every signed literal occupies exactly $b$ ULEB
bytes, every record has length $1+3b$, and the exact maximum $A+q$ is
attained.  The complete output has $n\log^{O(1)}n$ bits and every addressed
bit is computed in polylogarithmic time and space, including EOF/blank
behavior.  This corrected adapter is \textsc{Proved/Exact}.

The formerly open Cook-stream obligation is also discharged.  The classical
local reduction, as explicitly recorded by Jahanjou--Miles--Viola, produces a
quasilinear exact-3CNF whose indexed clause is computable in polylogarithmic
time and logarithmic space.  Equivalently, a densely numbered polylog-uniform
quasilinear circuit can be converted by three fixed clauses per gate and one
fixed output block.  Composing this source theorem with the repaired adapter
gives, on the same charged random-access model,
\[
 L\in\operatorname{NTIME}[n]
 \Longrightarrow R_x\in\texttt{DENSE-CNF1},\qquad
 |R_x|=n\log^{O(1)}n,
\]
with local virtual-input access.  Therefore a joint
$(6/5+o(1),1/2+o(1))$ solver would contradict FLMV Corollary~5.8: at
$c=1.201$, its forbidden space exponent is approximately $0.509108>1/2$.

This closes an endpoint/model guard, not the construction theorem.  Finite
ARC entry still requires one uniform all-length machine deciding every input
in the same total SAT language with simultaneous bounds.  Promise solvers,
per-input portfolio choices, and fallback calls to a polynomial SAT solver do
not supply such a point.  The remaining edge is the all-input favorable
pre-semantic WRAPPER phase; hence $P$ versus $NP$ remains \textsc{Open} and
the elimination of $K$ remains conditional.
