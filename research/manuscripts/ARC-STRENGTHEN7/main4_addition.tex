\section{ARC update: a strict streaming promise transition}

Add the zero-child member \textsc{EXTREME-1} before the corrected charged
controller.  It verifies all-zero and all-one; after either failure it flips
only the at most three variables of one witnessed falsified clause, verifying
each candidate by a full source scan.  It returns TRUE on a verified model
and otherwise UNKNOWN, never FALSE.  With read-only identifier substrings it
uses $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ time, $O(\log N)$ writable bits and no SAT
children.  No assignment vector is stored.

If $D_0^\star$ is the current recursively covered promise and $D_+^\star$ is
its least typed successor closure after adding this terminal, terminal
absorption gives, on one fixed depth-first machine,
\[
D_0^\star\subsetneq D_+^\star,
\qquad (6/5,1/2)\in\mathcal A(D_+^\star).
\]
Strictness holds for the connected sign-balanced family
\[
\Psi_m=\bigwedge_i(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_i)
\wedge(x\vee\neg a_i\vee b_i)
\wedge(\neg x\vee\neg b_i\vee c_i)
\wedge(\neg c_i\vee a_i\vee b_i).
\]
All-one is a model.  Yet the exact old controller declines for all sufficiently
large $m$: the formula is non-Horn/non-Krom; its Horn backdoor number is at
least $2m$; no singleton hits every width-three clause; its sign masks are
incomplete; and every PIVOT either produces cross-block width-four resolvents
or leaves a linear connected child above the square-root cap.

The singleton Horn/dual-Horn component proposal remains CANDIDATE.  Its
finite implementation kept a linear component map, so no half-space resource
promotion is valid.  Empty residual clauses, raw duplicates, component/Horn
buffer sharing and width-four successor typing are explicit remaining duties.

the previous embedding must be re-shielded against near-extreme models before

two-NAE-block repair.  General favorable acquisition, conditional
