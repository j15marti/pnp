\section{A local CNF1 adapter and the exact remaining endpoint guard}

The latest ARC audit closes one part of the model-transfer obligation without
promoting the construction to an unrestricted SAT algorithm.  Suppose a
uniform generator describes an exactly-3CNF $Q_x$ with
$q,C\le n\log^{O(1)}n$ and returns literal occurrence $(j,r)$ in
$\log^{O(1)}n$ time and $O(\log n)$ workspace.  Choose the least $b\ge1$
with $q\le2^{7b-3}$, put $A=2^{7b-3}$, and rename $v$ to $A+v$.
Every zig-zag-coded renamed signed literal then lies between $2A+1$ and
$4A=2^{7b-1}$ and therefore has exactly $b$ canonical ULEB128 bytes.  Every
CNF1 clause record has the fixed byte length $1+3b$.

It follows that a requested CNF1 bit can be generated by arithmetic on its
address and one call to the occurrence oracle.  The complete encoded length
is $n\log^{O(1)}n$, the access time is polylogarithmic, and the workspace is
$O(\log n)$.  An all-input CNF1 machine with bounds $(t,s)$ can consequently
be simulated on such indexed formulas in
\[
 t(n\log^{O(1)}n)\log^{O(1)}n
 \quad\hbox{time},\qquad
 s(n\log^{O(1)}n)+O(\log n)\quad\hbox{space},
\]
on one random-access machine; its work and index tapes remain charged.
This indexed-3CNF-to-CNF1 adapter is \textsc{Proved/Exact}.

The final identification is not yet proved.  FLMV Theorem~3.1 supplies local
access to a bit of its Cook formula, but its stated serialization is not an
explicit clause-indexed exactly-3CNF stream.  Generic repacking of an
arbitrary variable-length stream can require prefix rank/select and currently
costs a linear scan.  The remaining source-checkable lemma is to construct
the oblivious Cook--Pippenger--Fischer tableau directly as a clause-indexed
fixed-layout 3CNF stream, or prove an equivalent local locator.  Until then
the exact CNF1-to-FLMV endpoint transfer remains \textsc{Open}.

The primary endpoint itself remains exact: FLMV Corollary~5.8 rules out
\[
 \operatorname{NTIME}[n]\subseteq
 \operatorname{DTISP}[n^c,o(n^{d(c)})],\qquad
 d(c)=\tfrac12((c+2)/c^2-c).
\]
At $c=1201/1000$, $d(c)\approx0.509108>1/2$.  Hence the completed-phase
$(6/5,1/2)$ target would enter the verified forbidden region once both the
all-input phase and the remaining local Cook-stream lemma are proved.  The
conditional elimination of $K$ is unchanged.  No unrestricted ARC point and
no $P\ne NP$ conclusion follows here.

\paragraph{Stage quantifiers.}
Finite ARC entry requires, at the selected fixed stage, one uniform
all-length machine deciding the same unrestricted SAT language with joint
time and space bounds.  Per-length machines, per-input portfolio members and
promise-only stages do not qualify, even if their union covers all inputs.
