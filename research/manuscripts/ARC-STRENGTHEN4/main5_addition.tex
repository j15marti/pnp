\section{Encoding progress and new hostile controls for WRAPPER}

An exact local adapter now converts a clause-indexed exactly-3CNF stream to
the project's canonical CNF1 encoding.  An offset renaming makes every
zig-zag literal occupy the same number of ULEB128 bytes, so a requested CNF1
bit is produced with one literal-oracle call, polylogarithmic time and
$O(\log n)$ workspace.  The output length grows only by a polylogarithmic
factor.  This is \textsc{Proved/Exact}; it does not supply a WRAPPER phase.
The source-level Cook formula in FLMV has not yet been connected to the
required clause-indexed stream, so the exact endpoint encoding link remains
\textsc{Open}.

The updated portfolio also has a concrete infinite noncoverage control.  For
$m\ge3$, put a complete three-variable parity constraint on every cyclic
triple $\{a_i,b_i,a_{i+1}\}$.  The resulting connected satisfiable 3CNF has
$2m$ variables and $4m$ clauses, no common parity hub, and no strong Horn
backdoor of size two.  Any such backdoor must meet each triple in at least two
variables; since each variable occurs in at most two triples, its size is at
least $m$.  Thus both the parity-star member and the old partial portfolio
return UNKNOWN on this easy affine family.  The first family member has six
variables and twelve clauses.

On the same UNKNOWN family, the current parity-star implementation performs
at least $16m^2$ clause-pair probes.  With encoded length
$N=\Theta(m\log m)$, its rejected-input cost is
$\Omega(N^2/\log^2N)$, above the desired $N^{6/5+o(1)}$ phase budget.
Uncapped parallel portfolio execution does not erase that work.  The member
must be capped, replaced, or optimized under the joint half-space constraint;
a cap loses complete recognition of its asymptotic positive family.

Finally, the present flat marked grammar is not literally recursive: parent
data clauses forbid reserved tag variables 1 and 2, while a child annotation
uses those variables.  Hereditary closure therefore needs a new quoted or
fresh-tag grammar plus charged child renumbering.  Carrying ancestor
assignments as ordinary unit clauses is also insufficient because their
accumulating prefix eventually prevents power shrinkage.  A nested marked
construction remains a promising \emph{promise-domain} candidate if it
residualizes ancestor assignments, preserves widths with duplicate literals
or tautological fillers, rebases identifiers, and validates all descendants
before the first semantic call.  It is not an all-input acquisition rule and
does not establish an ARC point.

The final WRAPPER barrier is unchanged: under the assumed polynomial SAT
solver, construct a favorable all-input phase from source syntax before any
parent-scale semantic SAT call, charging discovery, failed members, repeated
children, replay, buffers, indexes and ancestor state.  Conditional $K$
elimination remains valid after that premise; the premise itself is
\textsc{Open}.
