\paragraph{Review synthesis SYNTHESIS-0007: clean-core transfer budget.}
For a reduction from source length $N$ to canonical Monotone
$3$SAT-$(2,2)$ length $M=N^{q+o(1)}$, setup exponent $a$, workspace
exponent $b$, and virtual-access overhead $\alpha$, a target algorithm with
exponents $(c,p)$ induces
$C=\max\{1,a,qc+\alpha\}$ and $P=\max\{b,qp\}$.
At $(c,p)=(6/5,1/2)$ under ideal other costs, the FLMV curve is reached at
$q_*=1.003836695566892\ldots$, determined by
$11c_*^3-6c_*-12=0$ and $q_*=5c_*/6$.
Thus $N^{1+o(1)}$ overhead fits, but qualitative NP-completeness or an
arbitrary polynomial reduction does not transfer the endpoint.
The exact general-source, bit-local, same-model reduction to the balanced
clean core remains OPEN.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/synthesis/2026-10-11\_1348\_SYNTHESIS-0007.tex}.
