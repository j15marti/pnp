\paragraph{LB-0008 clean-core expander guard (11 October 2026).}
CLEANCORE-EXPANDER-GUARD1 constructs an infinite paired-clause subclass of
Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ from cubic bipartite spectral expanders.  Its primal
graph is the line graph of the source expander and has
$\Omega(N/\log N)$ treewidth, while a perfect matching gives a satisfying
assignment in polynomial time.  Thus high primal treewidth and
$2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$ nominal separator-table mass are not clean-core
hardness certificates and do not force an arbitrary one-bit SAT execution to
instantiate a separator service.  The ARC achievable region is unchanged.
FORCED-NONOPTIONAL-SERVICE1, the $K$-independent compiler, and P versus NP
remain \textsc{Open}.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/lower\_bound/2026-10-11\_0812\_LB-0008.tex}.
