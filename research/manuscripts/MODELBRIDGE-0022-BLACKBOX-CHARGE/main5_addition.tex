\paragraph{MODELBRIDGE-0022: black-box extraction charge boundary.}
For any deterministic adaptive extractor that accesses an exact SAT decider
only through its one-bit answers, the complete query/answer transcript is
identical across all exact implementations.  The extracted object is therefore
source-defined, but the extractor's own work and every oracle invocation are
separate charged executions; they cannot be attributed to the original call
on the source formula.  Thus external black-box extraction does not close the
arbitrary-decider \textsc{same\_realization} bridge.  An embedded selected-output
compiler with an absolute model-matched landing remains OPEN.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/model\_bridge/2026-10-12\_1002\_MODELBRIDGE-0022.tex}.
P versus NP remains OPEN.
