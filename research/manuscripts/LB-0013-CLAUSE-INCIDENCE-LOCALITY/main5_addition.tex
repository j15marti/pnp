\paragraph{LB-0013: clause locality is not incidence locality.}
For a raw length-$L$ occurrence stream with a three-occurrence key at pivot
$p$, the exact deterministic decision-tree complexity of its occurrence
rank is $\min\{p,L-p-1\}$ outside constant-output boundary cases.  Yet on a
Monotone 3-Sat-$(3,3)$ source, two native tag bits per literal make the rank
directly accessible with only constant-factor encoded-length overhead.
Thus the raw scan bound is a black-box composition warning, not a semantic
or arbitrary-decider lower bound.  The OPEN first leg must be bi-local in
both clauses and incidence tags.  UCORE-TIME1 and P versus NP remain open.
