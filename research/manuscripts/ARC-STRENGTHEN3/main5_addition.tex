\section*{ARC-STRENGTHEN3 construction handoff: the relaxed budget}

The construction target can be weakened without weakening the verified FLMV
endpoint.  It is enough, conditionally, to build one fixed uniform exact
all-input completed phase with the following joint worst-case ledger:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{resource} & \text{per input of encoded length }N\\ \hline
\text{explicit child length} & N^{1/2+o(1)}\\
\text{executed child calls, with multiplicity} & N^{3/5+o(1)}\\
\text{all local time} & N^{6/5+o(1)}\\
\text{all peak live local workspace} & N^{1/2+o(1)}
\end{array}
\]
The resulting aggregate executed child volume may be
$N^{11/10+o(1)}$.  Thus the earlier near-linear-volume condition remains a
sufficient route to near-linear time, but is not a necessary condition for
finite entry.

This budget does not make acquisition free.  Local time includes
classification, discovery, failed candidates, certification, generation of
every occurrence, source rereads, replay, combination and cleanup.  Local
space includes transient scratch, all suspended frames, indexes, caches and
a sufficient streaming summary of previous answers.  Children are immutable
full SAT encodings evaluated one at a time.  A descriptor-only child needs a
separate access ledger; per-solver-step overhead $g$ changes the time term to
$(\eta+g)/(1-h)$.  Repeated identical children count repeatedly unless an
actual answer-reuse implementation is proved and charged.

Any same-scale work inside a double wrapper must complete under a
source-checkable well-founded rank and fit the local budget.  Only strict
encoded-length-decreasing phase exits may be recursively inlined.  A
parent-scale SAT query, an unchanged fallback, a growing base case, or bounds
only on the successful route do not meet the contract.

No current WRAPPER, backdoor, disjunctive, orbit-batch, signed-twin,
random-bitstream or typed-cycle implementation establishes this ledger for
all general inputs from the assumption $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$.  The next
construction target is exactly this weaker phase.  P versus NP remains
\textsc{Open}.
