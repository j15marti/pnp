\section*{ARC-STRENGTHEN3: a relaxed finite-entry phase budget}

The near-linear aggregate-child-volume contract is sufficient for the
near-linear route, but it is not necessary for finite entry into the verified
FLMV region.  Specializing the previously proved max-affine recurrence gives
the following exact conditional statement.

Let one fixed uniform exact all-input completed SAT phase on encoded length
$N$ do $N^{r+o(1)}$ total nonchild work, execute at most
$N^{\eta+o(1)}$ explicit child calls counted with multiplicity, and issue only
immutable children of encoded length at most $N^{h+o(1)}$, with $h<1$ and
eventual strict descent.  Suppose the phase's entire peak live nonchild
workspace---transient discovery/generation/replay state, suspended frames,
indexes, caches and prior-answer summaries included---is $N^{p+o(1)}$.
Children are materialized and evaluated sequentially; every same-scale
administrative step finishes under a well-founded internal rank and is
included in the local bounds.  All bounds hold on every true adaptive answer
transcript, and one common uniform error envelope holds at every level.

Inlining the children to a fixed exact base procedure produces one ordinary
uniform SAT machine with simultaneous exponents
\[
 C=\max\left\{r,\frac{\eta}{1-h}\right\},
 \qquad P=\max\{p,h\}.
\]
Correctness is by induction on encoded length.  For every $\delta>0$, a
slack induction in
$T(N)\le N^{r+o(1)}+N^{\eta+o(1)}T(N^{h+o(1)})$
proves time $N^{C+o(1)}$.  Summing every ancestor frame and explicit child
buffer on the depth-first shrinking path proves space $N^{P+o(1)}$.

In particular,
\[
 h=\frac12,\quad\eta=\frac35,\quad r=\frac65,
 \quad p\le\frac12
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad (C,P)=\left(\frac65,\frac12\right).
\]
Yet the executed aggregate child volume may be as large as
\[
 N^{\eta+h+o(1)}=N^{11/10+o(1)}.
\]
This volume is streamed, not stored.  It fits the $N^{6/5+o(1)}$ local-time
allowance.  Storing $N^{3/5}$ answer bits, using an implicit mutable child,
or omitting transient scratch space would violate the contract.

For the primary FLMV curve
\[
 d(c)=\frac12\left(\frac{c+2}{c^2}-c\right),
\]
$c=1201/1000$ gives
\[
 d(c)=\frac{1468676399}{2884802000}
 =\frac12+\frac{26275399}{2884802000},
 \qquad c>\frac65.
\]
The strict margins and FLMV's local SAT transfer therefore exclude the joint
pair $(6/5,1/2)$ in their charged random-access model.  This uses encoded bit
length and one same-machine witness; it does not use the retired unrestricted
cosine endpoint.

The max-affine recurrence and the FLMV target were recovered earlier.  The
new synthesis is the explicit $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ superlinear-volume allowance,
which corrects any reading that near-linear aggregate volume is necessary for
all ARC routes.  No recovered construction realizes this phase on every
general SAT input or derives it from $\mathrm{SAT}\in P$.  Conditional
$K$ elimination is preserved; the separation claim remains \textsc{Open}.
