\section{Portfolio-stable shield and exact fallback localization}

CONSTRUCTION-STRENGTHEN10 generalizes the zero-child extreme terminal and
then proves that this route cannot erase the SAT-hard fallback.  For every
fixed $r$, \textsc{EXTREME-$r$} follows a depth-$r$ search: at each node it
branches only on variables in the first clause falsified by the current
near-extreme assignment.  It accepts exactly the formulas with a model at
Hamming distance at most $r$ from all-zero or all-one, uses at most
$2\sum_{i=0}^r3^i$ scans, $N^{1+o(1)}$ time and $O(r\log N)$ writable bits,
and emits no SAT child.

For $b$ fresh NAE triples let
\[
 Q_b=\bigwedge_{j\le b}(a_j\vee b_j\vee c_j)\wedge
 (\neg a_j\vee\neg b_j\vee\neg c_j),
 \qquad
 R_b(F)=G(\widehat G(\mathsf{Iso}(F\wedge Q_b))).
\]
For a fixed finite portfolio of extreme radii at most $r$ and standard
structural strong Horn/dual-Horn/Krom backdoors of size at most $k$, choose
$b\ge r+1$ and $2b>k$.  Every model retains at least $b$ true and $b$ false
shield coordinates.  Occurrence-private isolation gives $2b$ disjoint
proper shield-clause supports; strong Krom must hit each, while strong Horn
or dual-Horn needs two selected variables per positive or negative triple.
ARC6's old-member failure theorem applies unchanged to $F\wedge Q_b$.
Thus $R_b(F)$ lies in the enlarged UNKNOWN domain $U_{r,k}$ and
\[
 \mathcal A(U_{r,k})=\mathcal A(\text{proper-3SAT})=\mathcal A(\SAT).
\]
For the current operational members $b=2$ suffices; the arbitrary-$k$
statement is claimed only for structural backdoors because size-three
operational branches may use an empty-clause shortcut.

More sharply, a favorable source-first PRE-A phase on $U_{r,k}$ exists iff
one exists on general proper 3SAT, up to common $o(1)$ exponent changes.  The
forward direction composes with the quasilinear polylog-access map $R_b$; the
reverse direction is restriction.  Calling the assumed $N^K$ decider on this
image restores $K$.  Exposing $F$ gives a single child with $(v,h)=(1,1)$.
Padding by exponent $a>1$ and then exposing the core creates typed scale
product $a\cdot(1/a)=1$, or after alias flattening the completed boundary
$F\to F$.  Hence the present terminal/double-wrapper/padding composition
does not construct strict descent.  This is an exact obstruction to that
architecture, not to every future compiler.
