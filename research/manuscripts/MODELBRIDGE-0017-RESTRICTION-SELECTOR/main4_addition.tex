\section{MODELBRIDGE-0017: literal restriction-selector endpoint boundary}
\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT, independent review pending; P versus NP remains OPEN.}
A total polynomial source machine that outputs one literal one-variable
restriction preserving SAT can be iterated to decide SAT.  Conversely, under
$\SAT\in\mathsf P$, one call on the zero restriction selects such a child:
choose zero if satisfiable and one otherwise.  On exact-$(2,2)$ sources the
query has length $N^{1-o(1)}$, so the unknown solver exponent $K$ remains.
Thus literal provenance and one-branch output do not close
\texttt{SOURCE-PASS-ACCESS1}; the selector must additionally be predecision,
$K$-independent, strict-power contracting, hereditary, and fully charged.
