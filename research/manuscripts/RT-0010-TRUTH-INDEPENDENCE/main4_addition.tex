\subsection{RT-0010: truth-independence dichotomy}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, pending independent review.}
Input-independent schedules and branch-free output do not exclude
decide-then-emit: oblivious simulation followed by a fixed multiplexer emits
one constant exact successor in $O(T\log T)$ time and retains the unknown
solver exponent.  Complete semantic noninterference is instead incompatible
with exact closed terminal reconstruction on opposite-answer sources.
Therefore ``truth-independent'' is not an intermediate bridge.  The OPEN
repair is one explicit source identity with a positive, hereditary,
$K$-independent full ledger.  P versus NP remains OPEN.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/redteam/2026-10-11\_1236\_RT-0010.tex}.
