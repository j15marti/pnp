\paragraph{MODELBRIDGE-0013: tagged occurrence localizer and raw-index barrier.}
Darmann--D\"ocker's Theorem~5 gadget, with positive occurrences assigned to
odd clones and negative occurrences to even clones, gives a linear-size
bit-local reduction from incidence-tagged Monotone 3-Sat-$(3,3)$ to
Monotone 3-Sat*-${}(2,2)$: $(n,2n)$ maps to $(15n,20n)$, with
polylogarithmic access and logarithmic workspace.  The printed assignment
using positive clones $1,2,3$ and negative clones $2,4,6$ is literally
inconsistent with exact $(2,2)$ balance.  Without occurrence tags or a
charged index, raw occurrence-rank/successor lookup has an elementary
$\Omega(L)$ field-probe lower bound, so standard occurrence splitting is not
already an FLMV local reduction.  The first leg from the exact FLMV source to
tagged Monotone 3-Sat-$(3,3)$ remains \textbf{OPEN}; arbitrary-exponent
bootstrapping and P versus NP remain open.  See
