\subsubsection*{Explicit DPLL certificates: strictification and ARC scope}
The newest DPLL bridge is exact only after a no-weakening repair.  Berger
et al.'s Resolution simulation counts subsumption leaves at zero cost, so
emitting every displayed leaf as a semantic-CP line violates the proposed
$n+O(|H|)$ budget on redundant split trees.  Maintaining a subclause at each
node repairs the accounting: resolve only when both pivot literals survive;
otherwise reuse the stronger pivot-free parent.  An explicit tree-encoded
DPLL certificate with $n$ charged rule occurrences therefore yields at most
$n$ strict Resolution inferences and at most $|H|+n$ semantic-CP lines, with
all parsing, parent indices, clause storage and output bits polynomially
charged.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for one record per expanded rule
occurrence.  Pointer/macro/DAG certificates and arbitrary one-bit deciders
remain outside the theorem, so the ARC acquisition arrow and P versus NP
remain \textbf{OPEN}.  See
