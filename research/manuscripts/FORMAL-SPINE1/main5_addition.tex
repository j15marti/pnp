wrapper. Accounting identities and finite implementations do not prove
that implication. Alternatively, sound existence transitions between
jointly achievable pairs may reach a verified forbidden region in
finitely many steps; their witnesses need not be identical, but each
must be one uniform all-length machine. A limit of exponent pairs does
not by itself provide such a machine. P versus NP remains OPEN.
