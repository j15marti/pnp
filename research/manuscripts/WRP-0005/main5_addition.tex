For the file-first balanced-pigeonhole member, let $p$ positive rows have
width $h$, let $n=ph$, and let the source contain exactly
$e=h\binom p2=n(p-1)/2$ distinct negative binary clauses.  Under
$p,h\ge2$ and $h/2\le p\le2h$, the exact implication
\[
 e\ge\frac{n^{3/2}}{4\sqrt2},\qquad 32e^2\ge n^3
\]
holds: $h\le2p$ gives $p\ge\sqrt{n/2}$, and $p-1\ge p/2$.
If the negative graph is then verified to be $h$ disjoint $p$-cliques and
every positive row meets each clique exactly once, the formula is a renamed
rectangular pigeonhole instance and is satisfiable exactly when $p\le h$.
These arithmetic and semantic statements are \textsc{Proved/Exact}.
