\section{Near-linear recognition of signed twins on dense inputs}
The 2026-10-08\_0731 DENSE-TWIN construction improves the WRP-0006
repeated-scan recognizer on the gated regime $n^3\le M^2$, where $M$
counts actual encoded bits. Streaming partition refinement by each
clause's positive and negative variable subsets finds exact signed
incidence classes. Only touched blocks are processed; at most $n-1$
splits occur. Variable-indexed arrays and one clause buffer cost
$O(n\log M)$ bits, and total time is $M^{1+o(1)}$, including final
selection, parsing and a constant number of measurement/emission scans.
The constructor chooses one largest class, uses the previously proved
three-state macro, and returns REDUCE only when every ordinary child
has size at most $M^{2/3}$. Otherwise it returns FAIL.
The supported parser and byte-for-byte emitter are in
\path{research/agents/proof/dense_twin_refinement.py}.
A seeded bounded test passed 8,285 checks. A 30,464-bit source produced
304-, 304-, and 96-bit children. An independent-component control failed
the size gate as required. This is favorable charged acquisition on a
restricted family, not recursively closed general coverage; the
all-input constructor and P versus NP remain \textsc{Open}.
