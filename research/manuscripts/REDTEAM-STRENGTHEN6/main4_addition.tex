\section{Red-team correction: uniform promise closure and exact fallback scope}

The preceding ARC6 closure is valid only after making its uniformity
quantifiers explicit.  Fix one finite boundary-type set, one controller, one
common modulus $\rho(N)\to0$, a common strict shrink threshold above a fixed
exact base, and charge peak transient phase workspace as well as retained
continuation/generator state.  If, with multiplicity,
\[
 \max_i\max\{2,|Q_i|\}\le N^{h+\rho(N)},\qquad
 \sum_i\max\{1,|Q_i|\}\le N^{v+\rho(N)},
\]
while phase time and workspace are respectively
$N^{r+\rho(N)}$ and $N^{p+\rho(N)}$, then the least covered promise has one
uniform depth-first decider with
\[
 C=\max\{1,r,(v-h)/(1-h)\},\qquad P=\max\{p,h\}.
\]
The notation must therefore be
$T_{M,D^\star}(N),S_{M,D^\star}(N)$ and the numerical conclusion is only
\[
 (6/5,1/2)\in\mathcal A(\operatorname{SAT}\upharpoonright D^\star),
\]
not a point of $\mathcal A(\operatorname{SAT})$.  Allowing countably many
types with separate typewise $o(1)$ terms is unsound: type $\tau_k$ may have
a finite binary-expansion prefix through length $2k$, while roots of type
$\tau_N$ generate exponential executed multiplicity.

ARC6's Construction8 examples are nevertheless nonvacuous without calling
their growing-identifier encodings constant length: each four-clause
component has the size-two strong Horn backdoor consisting of its two lift
variables, and hence belongs to the existing zero-child base.

The semantic fallback and PIVOT cap survive, but flat-bit local access needs
one global fixed-width renaming before isolation.  Map every old variable
and every fresh isolation variable injectively into one common ULEB-width
bucket; then fixed-record clause arithmetic is valid.  The exact theorem is
scoped to the old ARC5 prefix, GUARD-STRIP, and post-strip
$\{\mathrm{PIVOT},\mathrm{pure}\}$ pair.  It does not cover unspecified
future members.  The empty valid proper formula is a direct TRUE base and
malformed inputs are rejected by the total parser.

The computational summary is also corrected: 3,010 tested pivots account
for 115,943 raw pairs, because the isolated suite contributes 12,874 pairs
omitted from ARC6's combined count.  This does not change the cap proof.
ARC6's final reference should point to its open coverage condition (14), not
to already proved equality (12).  General-SAT coverage, PRE-A and
$P\ne NP$ remain OPEN; conditional aggregate elimination of $K$ is unchanged.
