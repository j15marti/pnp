\paragraph{LB-0012: compatibility rank is not clean-core work.}
For the paired $K_{3,3}$ block in proper unique Monotone
$3$-Sat-$(2,2)$, one $5|4$ variable split has a $32\times16$ semantic
compatibility matrix of full column rank 16 over both $\mathbb F_2$ and
$\mathbb R$.  A conjunction of $k$ disjoint canonical blocks has
compatibility matrix $H^{\otimes k}$ and rank
$16^k=2^{4k}=2^{\Theta(N/\log N)}$ for indexed length
$N=\Theta(k\log k)$.  Nevertheless a fixed perfect matching satisfies every
block, and a uniform canonical recognizer returns the one SAT bit in linear
time without constructing the matrix.  Thus compatibility rank, even inside
the exact NP-complete clean-core syntax, does not force arbitrary-algorithm
extraction, state, memory, communication or time.  The unrestricted
worst-case target remains \textbf{OPEN}; P versus NP remains open.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/lower\_bound/
2026-10-11\_1412\_LB-0012.tex}.
