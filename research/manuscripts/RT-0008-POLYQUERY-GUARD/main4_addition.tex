\paragraph{RT-0008: polynomial-query service guard.}
Independent review accepts TESTS-0004's connected two-pin equivalence after
making evaluator correctness explicit.  Its proposed nonconstant-query repair
is insufficient: a controller with $N^{c+o(1)}$ local work and
$N^{b+o(1)}$ adaptive SAT calls of length $N^{a+o(1)}$ is simulated by a
fixed $M^{K+o(1)}$ SAT decider in
$N^{\max\{c,b+aK\}+o(1)}$ time.  Parity needs all $q$ black-box answers but
has a width-two OBDD with $2q-1$ nonterminals.  Thus superconstant query
complexity is retired as a service repair.  A hereditary source-first
$K$-independent compiler using only strictly smaller SAT channels remains
OPEN.  No ARC point or lower bound follows.  P versus NP remains OPEN.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/redteam/2026-10-11\_1036\_RT-0008.tex}.
