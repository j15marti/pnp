\paragraph{RT-0008: polynomial-query service guard.}
Independent review accepts the connected two-pin equivalence, but rejects
non-$O(1)$ SAT-query complexity as the next WRAPPER resource.  Polynomially
many adaptive queries remain polynomial under $P=NP$ and retain the unknown
decider exponent $K$ as $\max\{c,b+aK\}$.  Parity is a sharp control: exact
black-box query complexity $q$ coexists with a width-two, $2q-1$-node OBDD
and linear aggregation.  The surviving target is a source-first hereditary
compiler with $K$-independent local work and only strictly fractional SAT
channels, plus a lower bound on that same realization.  No contraction,
matched endpoint, or separation follows.  P versus NP remains OPEN.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/redteam/2026-10-11\_1036\_RT-0008.tex}.
