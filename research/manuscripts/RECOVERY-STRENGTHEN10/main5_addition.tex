\section{What the recovered WRAPPER rules can and cannot supply}

The complete mixed WRAPPER ledger is invariant under one initial
quasilinear, locally accessible SAT-preserving virtual source.  Consequently
Construction10's shield does more than preserve language hardness: any
source-first phase on that image with local exponents $(r,p)$, ordinary-child
exponents $(v,h)$, and terminal ledgers
$(w_\tau,g_\tau,c_\tau,d_\tau)$ transfers to general proper 3SAT with the
same fixed exponents up to $o(1)$.  Recursive children then run in the
representation promised by the phase, avoiding unproved repeated virtual
nesting.

The original RECURCLOSE1, CYCLEGAIN1, STREAMCOMP1 and ORBITBATCH1 records are
therefore best understood as closure/accounting theorems.  On a realized
path to an unresolved $N^K$ decider, affine substitution leaves
\[
 A(P)+\Theta(P)K,qquad \Theta(P)=\prod_{e\in P}\theta_e.
\]
Aliases, implicit access and setup/address sharing do not themselves change
the logical scale of the surviving dependency.  Pad/unpad return gain is
one, and every finite closed walk is a product of simple-cycle gains.  The
old rules can charge or preserve a supplied contraction or a supplied exact
terminal; they do not logically construct either premise.

Combining STREAMCOMP1 with aggregate volume gives the reusable bound
\[
 C=\max\left\{1,r,\frac{g+v-h}{1-h}\right\}
\]
when every implicit child access costs $N^{g+o(1)}$ parent time.  The current
$(6/5,11/10,1/2)$ ledger is saturated, so conservative access must have
$g=0$ at exponent scale or be replaced by a proved batching/cache theorem.
ORBIT address deduplication helps only when it reduces actual charged access
or semantic consumer volume.

No general impossibility follows from a coarse label $h=1$: exact recurrences
on $n/2$ or $n-1$ children may still be polynomial.  Such exact-length or
potential-based descent is a legitimate new WRAPPER route but needs full
multiplicity, depth, replay and joint-space accounting.  To finish the
present proof, one still needs on every shielded input either a new $K$-free
terminal or an exact recursively covered child plan meeting
$(r,v,h,p)\le(6/5,11/10,1/2,1/2)$.  P versus NP remains OPEN.
