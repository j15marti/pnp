\section{Full-ledger shield localization and recovered-premise provenance}

RECOVERY-STRENGTHEN10 extends Construction10's shielded-image localization
from PRE-A notation to the complete Recovery9 mixed ledger.  A
SAT-preserving virtual image of length $N^{1+o(1)}$ with uniform
$N^{o(1)}$-time/space source-first bit access preserves local time/state,
ordinary-child maximum and aggregate volume, and every terminal batch/solver
exponent.  Only the initial image is virtual; recursive children run in their
declared representations.  Hence the closed exponents
\[
C=\max\left\{1,r,\max_\tau[w_\tau+g_\tau(c_\tau-1)],
                    \frac{v-h}{1-h}\right\},\qquad
P=\max\left\{p,h,\max_\tau g_\tau\max(1,d_\tau)\right\}
\]
are invariant up to a common $o(1)$.  For Construction10's $R_b$, a
favorable mixed phase on the shielded UNKNOWN image is therefore already a
general proper-3SAT phase.

The recovered closure rules preserve rather than create their premises.  A
typed route with edge intercepts $a_e$ and scale gains $\theta_e$ reaching
the unresolved $N^K$ decider contributes
\[
 A(P)+\Theta(P)K,\qquad
 \Theta(P)=\prod_{e\in P}\theta_e.
\]
Alias flattening composes this pair; streaming changes intercepts but not
scale; legitimate address/setup deduplication cannot delete the final
semantic dependency.  Balanced padding/unpadding has return gain one.  In a
finite graph every closed walk decomposes into simple cycles, so composite
routing cannot manufacture a contracting product if none is present.

For aggregate implicit children, a uniform per-access generator exponent
$g$ changes the conservative closure term to
\[
 C\ge\frac{g+v-h}{1-h}.
\]
Thus the saturated $(C,v,h)=(6/5,11/10,1/2)$ target requires exponent-zero
per-access generation unless a proved batched/cache realization improves the
simulation.  This is exact conditional accounting, not an implementation.

These are interface-provenance results, not SAT lower bounds.  Constant-
factor or additive exact-length descent and input-dependent ranks can be
polynomial although their coarse power label is one; they require a fresh
recurrence and remain open routes.  The missing all-input source-first
construction and P versus NP remain OPEN.
