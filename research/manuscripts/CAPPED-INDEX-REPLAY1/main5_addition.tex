\section{Capped source-occurrence propagation}
CAPPED-INDEX-REPLAY1 adds a complete occurrence index when its declared
index-plus-queue budget permits it. All clauses are initially queued;
assigning a literal requeues only clauses containing its negation.
Acquisition precedes assignments, and an incomplete index is discarded
before charged sweep fallback. No residual CNF is copied.

For proper CNFs, unit soundness and fair event processing give closure
or conflict. One successful phase makes at most
$\sum_C w_C(w_C+2)$ literal reads, including acquisition, and at most
$m_s+\sum_Cw_C$ clause events. This is linear source-probe work at fixed
width, not a complete machine-time or whole-solver bound. Sparse-chain
index payload is of order $n\log n$ bits. Existing ancestors, masks,
source locators, cache and failed attempts remain charged.

source reads fall from 132,860 to 3,065, using 49,061 logical index/queue
bits. This is COMPUTATIONAL evidence for a local tradeoff. General
