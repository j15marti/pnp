\paragraph{RT-0014 forward-fanout oracle guard (11 October 2026).}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, pending independent review.}
Forward child access does not generically provide reverse incidence.  For an
acyclic family with $n$ possible parents of a fixed target, the elementary
OR/weight adversary forces $n$ child queries to decide zero fanout, output
all parents, or certify fanout at most $\Delta<n$.  This retires only the
unqualified interface implication; it neither refutes TESTS-0009's
parent-owned clause construction nor resolves the actual JMV label grammar.
The restricted query theorem is polynomial and does not transfer to every
one-bit SAT decider.  \texttt{JMV-FORWARD-FANOUT1}, the external uniform
solver-to-resource compiler, UCORE-TIME1 and P versus NP remain
\textbf{OPEN}.  See
