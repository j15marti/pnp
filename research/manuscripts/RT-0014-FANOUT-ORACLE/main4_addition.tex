\paragraph{RT-0014 forward-fanout oracle guard (11 October 2026).}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, pending independent review.}
In an acyclic child-only circuit oracle with one target gate and $n$ possible
later parents, deciding zero fanout, enumerating the complete parent set, or
certifying fanout at most fixed $\Delta<n$ has deterministic child-query
complexity exactly $n$.  Hence forward child locality alone does not supply
a sublinear reverse-incidence or fanout certificate, and
\texttt{CHILD-LOCALITY-IMPLIES-FANOUT-CERT1} is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.  TESTS-0009's parent-owned forward-wire
compilation is unaffected.  Source-specific
\texttt{JMV-FORWARD-FANOUT1}, arbitrary-decider SAME\_REALIZATION transfer,
UCORE-TIME1 and P versus NP remain \textbf{OPEN}.  See
\texttt{research/review/reports/redteam/2026-10-11\_1636\_RT-0014.tex}.
