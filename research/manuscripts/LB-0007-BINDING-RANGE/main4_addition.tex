canonical proper-three-CNF pin family must receive at least $n$ bits, and the
bound is tight.  The optimal summary is nevertheless recovered in $O(N)$

family decider need not retain it.  Thus global binding and summary range do
not create a new ARC point.  On arbitrary formulas, lexicographic witness
binding via SAT self-reduction retains the hypothetical exponent $K$.
