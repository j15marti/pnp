\paragraph{MODELBRIDGE-0015: positive-scale black-box calls retain arbitrary wrapper exponents.}
Assume one uniform polynomial-time exact SAT decider exists.  For every
extensional deterministic oracle transducer $E$, fixed charged exponent $c$,
and $\varepsilon>0$, if $E$ issues a query of length at least $N^\varepsilon$
on infinitely many length-$N$ inputs, then an extensionally identical delay
wrapper with delay exponent $d>c/\varepsilon$ forces charged time at least
$N^{\varepsilon d}>N^c$ on those inputs
(\texttt{BLACKBOX-POSITIVE-SCALE-GATE1}, \textsc{Proved/Exact}).  Therefore
no exponent placed before the quantifier over all polynomial SAT
implementations can coexist with positive-polynomial-scale fully charged
oracle calls.  Unit-cost calls change the model; source-only computation does
not force internal exposure; and $N^{o(1)}$-length queries remain an
\textsc{Open} loophole.  A finite audit passed 512 exact rational witnesses,
25,000 adaptive wrapper comparisons, and 40 named hostile controls.
This is not a SAT lower bound.  The arbitrary-decider SAME\_REALIZATION
compiler and P versus NP remain \textsc{Open}.
