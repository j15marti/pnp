\section*{REDTEAM-STRENGTHEN3 construction guard: exclude tautological phases}

The current concrete phase budget allows a zero-child implementation that
simply performs the final $N^{6/5+o(1)}$-time,
$N^{1/2+o(1)}$-space SAT computation locally.  Hence existence of that phase
is equivalent to existence of the endpoint algorithm.  Demanding a positive
child does not repair the definition: a constant or fully restricted child
may be issued and ignored, even with artificial syntactic dependence.

Future WRAPPER candidates should therefore instantiate a source-level
compiler, not merely the extensional phase contract.  The compiler must state
how the code/clock of an arbitrary assumed polynomial SAT decider is used;
forbid direct parent-size SAT decisions and hidden endpoint algorithms;
make the parent answer's SAT-dependent information arise through essential,
strictly smaller, immutable, source-checkable children; and charge discovery,
all failed paths, every executed occurrence, acquisition, bit access, replay,
answer summaries, buffers, indexes and every ancestor.  One size-only
$o(1)$ envelope must apply uniformly at every recursive depth.
This is the recovered CHARGED STRICT SUCCESSOR/PRE-A-MACRO-SHRINK1 causal
condition, not a new semantic label.  The child or macro-decomposition must
exist before the first parent-scale semantic invocation of the old solver.

The chosen SAT syntax must also be model-matched.  Define behavior on every
bit string and either use FLMV's Boolean-formula SAT language directly or
prove a locally addressable Cook--Levin/Tseitin conversion into the exact
CNF/3CNF encoding.  Materializing a near-linear reduction is not compatible
with the desired square-root workspace.

This guard does not falsify recursive composition, conditional $K$
elimination, or the relaxed $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ streamed-volume budget.  It
identifies why the current existential target alone cannot be counted as
progress on the missing compiler.  No present WRAPPER meets the strengthened
source-level obligation on all general inputs.  P versus NP remains
\textsc{Open}.
