\section*{REDTEAM-STRENGTHEN3: the relaxed phase is endpoint-equivalent}

The relaxed parameter synthesis remains correct conditional accounting, but
its existential target is not logically weaker than the endpoint.  Let
$\mathsf{PHASE}$ denote a uniform exact phase with
\[
 h=\frac12,\quad \eta=\frac35,\quad r=\frac65,
 \quad p\le\frac12,
\]
using the complete adaptive, multiplicity, transient-space and strict-descent
ledger.  Let $\mathsf{ALG}$ denote one uniform exact SAT machine on the same
encoding and random-access model with joint bounds
$N^{6/5+o(1)}$ time and $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ space.  Then
\[
 \boxed{\exists W\,\mathsf{PHASE}(W)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists A\,\mathsf{ALG}(A).}
\]
The forward direction is the proved recursive composition.  For the reverse
direction, run $A$ locally, issue zero children, and return its answer.  All
child conditions are vacuous and the local resource floors equal the endpoint
pair.  Requiring one child is insufficient: a constant tautology can be
issued and ignored.

Accordingly, the displayed phase is an endpoint-compatible normal form and a
useful resource budget, but its mere existential construction is not a
weaker intermediate theorem.  After the verified FLMV exclusion, the
implication $\mathrm{SAT}\in P\Rightarrow\exists W\,\mathsf{PHASE}(W)$ is
itself separation-strength.  A genuinely decompositional target must specify
an explicit source-level compiler from each clocked polynomial SAT decider,
forbid direct parent decisions, and require essential SAT-dependent
information to flow through strictly smaller source-checkable children.  A
positive query count or syntactic dependence alone does not suffice.
The recovered CHARGED STRICT SUCCESSOR/PRE-A-MACRO-SHRINK1 guard already
states the needed causal order: construct the contracted ordinary-SAT child
before any parent-scale semantic call.

The endpoint arithmetic remains correct.  The model transfer additionally
requires either the exact FLMV SAT encoding or a local encoding lemma for the
chosen CNF/3CNF syntax: total malformed-input behavior, output length
$n\log^{O(1)}n$, requested-bit time $\log^{O(1)}n$, workspace $O(\log n)$,
and explicit realization on the charged FLMV random-access machine.  The
common $o(1)$ resource envelope must be uniform across recursive depth.
Formal recurrences should use monotone at-most-length envelopes and charge at
most polylogarithmic fixed-tape addressing overhead.

The $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ streamed-volume allowance and conditional $K$
elimination remain valid under their premises.  No qualifying compiler or
encoding lemma is presently supplied.  P versus NP remains \textsc{Open}.
