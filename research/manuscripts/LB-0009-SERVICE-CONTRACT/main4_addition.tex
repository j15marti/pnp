coordinate is one compiled SAT call; an $N^{\alpha+o(1)}$ batch of
$N^{\beta+o(1)}$ residuals retains exponent $\alpha+\beta K$; source plus

exponential table has only the output lower bound of a different task.

not bind the original one-bit SAT execution without a same-execution compiler.
