\section{A realized causal phase on a certified subclass}
\label{sec:construction-strengthen6-arc}

CONSTRUCTION-STRENGTHEN6 supplies an actual PRE-A realization of the relaxed
ARC exponents on a source-checkable subclass.  A raw ordinary CNF may contain
tautological annotations defining a cutset $S$ and ordered disjoint variable
blocks.  The compiler verifies the complete annotation and dry-runs every
assignment to $S$ before its first SAT child.  Under
\[
 |S|\le\tfrac1{10}\log_2N,\qquad b\le N^{1/2},\qquad
 |F_{a,j}|\le N^{1/2}\log N,
\]
it combines residual components by
\[
 \SAT(F)=\bigvee_a\left(G(a)\wedge\bigwedge_j\SAT(F_{a,j})\right).
\]
Complete encoded child headers and repeated occurrences are charged.  The
phase has at most $N^{3/5}$ executed children, aggregate child volume
$N^{11/10+o(1)}$, local work $N^{11/10}\operatorname{polylog}N$, polylogarithmic
nonchild state, and one $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ child buffer.  Thus the relaxed ledger
is realizable without a zero/ignored-child endpoint restatement.

This does not add an all-input ARC point.  The certificate is supplied rather
than acquired, and emitted children need not carry recursive certificates.
A constant-degree expander parity guard leaves an $\Omega(N)$ residual
component after any $O(\log N)$ assignments, so the separator-only route is
not universally applicable.  Cheap all-input acquisition or a non-separator
causal contraction remains OPEN; P versus NP remains OPEN.
