\paragraph{TESTS-0004: connected two-pin service guard.}
For every variable $z$, SAT$(F)$ is the OR of the two genuine restrictions
$F\upharpoonright_{z=0}$ and $F\upharpoonright_{z=1}$.  Decomposing an exact
Monotone $3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ source into connected incidence components preserves
the clean promise, and for every fixed $K\ge1$ the aggregate two-call cost
satisfies $2\sum_i N_i^K\le2(\sum_iN_i)^K$.  Thus a polynomial evaluator for
both nonempty pins on connected clean-core inputs already gives a polynomial
source decider without increasing $K$.  Connectivity is therefore retired as
an intermediate-service repair.  A one-query connected balanced lift remains
open as an existence question, but is unnecessary for this equivalence.
No WRAPPER contraction, strict successor, or lower bound follows.  P versus
NP remains OPEN.  See
