\section{LB-0017: pruned strict contraction carries a linear partial witness}
\textbf{Status: PROVED/EXACT, independent review pending; P versus NP remains OPEN.}
On a proper exact-$(2,2)$ source of length $N=\Theta(n\log n)$, any literal
restriction with explicit residual length at most $N^h$, fixed $h<1$, assigns
at least $n/3-o(n)$ variables.  If the source is satisfiable and the child
preserves the SAT bit, those values extend to a source witness.  Explicit
serialization costs $\Omega(n)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ bits, but a one-bit SAT
decider need not instantiate the pass.  The remaining OPEN target is a
bit-local, hereditary, $K$-independent predecision accessor.
