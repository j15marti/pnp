\section{Batched unit propagation and its clause-order limit}
BATCHED-UNIT-REPLAY1 adds dynamic source-major propagation sweeps to the
existing exact source-backed solver. New forced assignments are visible
later in the same pass. There is no new occurrence index or unit queue;
mask scratch, source access and the inherited index remain charged.
Frontier-first entry and exact bounded sharing are preserved.

The builder's 3,471 solver comparisons pass. Independent tests verify
873 implication-edge permutations plus exhaustive and random Boolean
controls. On the deliberately frontier-capped 256-variable forward
chain, literal reads drop from 261,632 to 2,555; the broader logical
payload charge increases from 1,530 to 2,113 bits.
These are finite operation/payload counts, not machine resource exponents.

For a chain seeded with $x_1=1$, fixed forward sweeps have exact productive
pass count
\[
1+\#\{i:p_{i+1}<p_i\},
\]
where $p_i$ is the source position of implication $i$.
Uniform random edge order therefore needs $n/2$ productive sweeps in
expectation. This kernel retains quadratic scan cost even on shuffled
easy chains; the general solver may avoid it through its frontier.
No all-input favorable time-space pair is added. P versus NP remains OPEN.
