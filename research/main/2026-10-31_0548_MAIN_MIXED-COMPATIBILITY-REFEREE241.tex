\section{Exact compatibility firewall}
For a formula $G(B,W)$ define its boundary projection
\[
 R_G=\{b:\exists w\;G(b,w)\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Shared-interface compatibility]
For every finite $H(B,U)$ and $E(B,V)$ with disjoint private variables,
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(H\wedge E)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R_H\cap R_E\ne\varnothing.
\]
Consequently, the two separate bits
$(\operatorname{SAT}(H),\operatorname{SAT}(E))$ do not determine the label of
$H\wedge E$ on any class containing one pair with nonempty disjoint projections
and one pair with intersecting projections.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A model $(b,u,v)$ of the conjunction places $b$ in both projections.  Conversely,
if $b$ belongs to both, choose its two private witnesses $u$ and $v$; disjointness
of the private variable sets lets the witnesses coexist.  Nonemptiness of two
sets alone says nothing about whether they intersect.  Hence a decision rule
seeing only the two nonemptiness bits gives the same input on intersecting and
disjoint examples and must err on one. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Sound controller terminal]
A mixed-component terminal is sound only if it returns (i) a checked common
boundary assignment with private witnesses, (ii) an exact verified representation
of a nonempty join, or (iii) a checked proof that the intersection is empty.
Otherwise it must use a complete exact fallback.  Discovery, construction,
access, verification, failed probes, switching, replay, and peak live storage of
that object remain charged.
\end{corollary}
This is a correctness firewall, not a size lower bound on every join
representation and not a SAT runtime lower bound.

\section{Independent referee of the current Horn--affine image}
For each proper source clause
$C_j=(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3})$, introduce occurrence-falsity
bits $y_{jk}$ and free properization bits $t_{jk}$.  Put
\[
 H_j=(\neg y_{j1}\vee\neg y_{j2}\vee\neg y_{j3}),\qquad
 y_{jk}\oplus x_i=b_{jk},
\]
where $b_{jk}=1$ for $\ell_{jk}=x_i$ and $0$ for
$\ell_{jk}=\neg x_i$.  Encode each affine equation by its two binary clauses
and replace each $D$ by both $(D\vee t_{jk})$ and
$(D\vee\neg t_{jk})$.  The resulting $T(F)=H\wedge E$ has $n+6m$ variables
and $13m$ proper clauses.

For every complete $x$, the equations uniquely set each $y_{jk}$ to the
falsity indicator of $\ell_{jk}$.  Then $H_j$ is true exactly when $C_j$ is
true, while every $t_{jk}$ is free.  Therefore
\[
 \exists y,t\;T(F)(x,y,t)\iff F(x),\qquad
 \#\mathrm{SAT}(T(F))=2^{3m}\#\mathrm{SAT}(F).
\]
Also $H$ is always SAT by $y=0$, and $E$ is always SAT by choosing any $x$,
its forced $y$, and arbitrary $t$.  This independently certifies the LSPV-3
pointwise, all-source, both-label theorem.  Schaefer's classification supplies
the established context; the displayed reduction supplies the exact project
claim without assuming the conclusion.

The hostile pair uses all eight clauses $C_a$ that are false exactly at
$a\in\{0,1\}^3$ for the UNSAT source.  The SAT source omits $C_{101}$ and
duplicates $C_{000}$.  Both have three variables and eight clauses; both mixed

This reduction is uniform and all-input on proper 3CNF, but it is not the
