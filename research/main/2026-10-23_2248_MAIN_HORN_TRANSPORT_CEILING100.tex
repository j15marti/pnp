\section{Claim card and falsifiable mechanisms}
The fixed language is the canonical Horn-selector image of proper exactly-three-clause CNF.  An input record contains four addressed words (arity and three signed-literal slots, zero when absent), plus two header words; $w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+M+1))\rceil$.  The declared query model charges each literal-word or whole-clause-record read once.  The transport model also charges parsing, source reads, preprocessing, failed attempts, explicit output writes, retained source access, target simulation, witness reconstruction/replay, and peak live space.

Three falsifiable mechanisms were tested before literature review.  \textbf{M1}: the Horn-selector lift might create an occurrence rung whose degree-three side is easy and degree-four side is NP-complete.  \textbf{M2}: the lifted implication chain might transport its $q$-field lower bound through a paid explicit reduction.  \textbf{M3}: even if paid transport fails, the image family might give a direct all-decider target-field lower bound.  M1 and M3 are proved in their declared models; M2 is falsified by an exact negative numerator.

The exact target is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  &\forall A\;\forall q\ge1:\quad
  A\text{ exact on the canonical target language}\\
  &\hspace{2.5cm}\Longrightarrow
  A\text{ reads at least }q\text{ declared target fields on }G_{1^q}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is a deterministic decision-tree statement for one explicit realization.  It is not the desired statement $\forall A\,\forall c\,\exists F:T_A(F)>|F|^c$.

\section{Independent verification of the Horn-selector rung}
For proper source
\[
\Phi=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}(\ell_{j1}\vee\ell_{j2}\vee\ell_{j3}),
\]
introduce fresh selectors $s_{j1},s_{j2},s_{j3}$ and set
\[
H(\Phi)=\bigwedge_{j=1}^{m}\left[(s_{j1}\vee s_{j2}\vee s_{j3})
\wedge\bigwedge_{a=1}^{3}(\neg s_{ja}\vee\ell_{ja})\right].
\]

\begin{theorem}[Lift, extension count, and backdoors; PROVED/EXACT]
$\Phi$ is satisfiable iff $H(\Phi)$ is satisfiable.  For a fixed source assignment $\alpha$, if $t_j$ source literals are true in clause $j$, the number of selector extensions is
\[
\prod_{j=1}^{m}(2^{t_j}-1).
\]
The output has $n=n_0+3m$ variables, $M=4m$ clauses, and $(16m+2)w$ bits.  Under raw restriction, its minimum strong syntactic 2-CNF and Horn backdoors have sizes exactly $m$ and $2m$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every selector triple activates at least one source literal; conversely, any nonempty subset of the $t_j$ true literals can be activated independently.  For 2-CNF, every disjoint positive triple must be hit once and one hit suffices.  For Horn, fewer than two selected variables in a triple leave, on the all-false restriction, at least two positive literals; two hits per triple suffice.  The binary implications are Horn throughout.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp occurrence rung; PROVED/EXACT plus KNOWN THEOREM]
If every source clause has exactly three distinct variables and every source variable occurs at most three times, then $\Phi$ and $H(\Phi)$ are satisfiable.  A witness is constructible in $O(n_0+m\sqrt m)$ word operations and $O(n_0+m)$ live words.  At maximum occurrence four, the canonical Horn-selector language is NP-complete, even for signed exact-$(2,2)$ monotone sources.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any clause subset $Q$, its $3|Q|$ incidence edges meet variables of degree at most three, so $|N(Q)|\ge|Q|$.  Hall gives a matching saturating the clauses.  Assign each matched variable to satisfy its uniquely matched literal.  Hopcroft--Karp gives the stated construction bound.  The degree-four statement follows by applying the answer-blind linear lift to the cited proper-3SAT restrictions; target grammar recognition and inverse source recovery are linear.
\end{proof}

The connected degree-three cyclic source $C_i=(x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1}\vee x_{i+2})$ remains a hostile control: both polarities occur, the lifted formula has minimum backdoors $m$ and $2m$, yet all $x_i=1$ supplies an immediate witness.  Thus linear syntactic backdoor size does not imply exponential continuation.

\section{Exact lifted-chain sensitivity}
Let $F_b$ be the baseline chain on $z_0,\ldots,z_q,u,v$, with four binary endpoint clauses forcing $z_0=1,z_q=0$ and
\[
C_i=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee z_i)\quad(b_i=1),\qquad
C_i=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee\neg z_i)\quad(b_i=0).
\]
Then $F_b$ is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$.  Replace every binary clause $(a\vee b)$ by
$(a\vee b\vee y_C)(a\vee b\vee\neg y_C)$ with a private padding variable, and then apply $H$.  Call the result $G_b$.

\begin{theorem}[Direct target sensitivity; PROVED/EXACT in the declared encoding]
For all $b$, $G_b$ has
\[
n=8q+31,qquad M=8q+32,qquad W=4M+2=32q+130
\]
addressed words, independent of $b$, and $G_b$ is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$.  Relative to $G_{1^q}$, flipping $b_i$ changes exactly two signed consequent words, and the $q$ two-word sensitivity sets are pairwise disjoint.  Therefore every deterministic exact literal-word or clause-record query decider for the fixed canonical target language reads at least $q$ distinct fields on $G_{1^q}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Paired padding is equivalent to its binary source, and the selector lift is equisatisfiable, so labels are preserved.  Flipping $b_i$ changes the sign of the $z_i$ occurrence in each of the two padded copies of $C_i$; after the lift these are two distinct binary-consequent word positions.  No other metadata, identifier, arity, or absolute variable address changes, and different $i$ use disjoint records.

If an exact deterministic decider reads neither word in sensitivity set $i$ on $G_{1^q}$, its transcript is identical on the input with only $b_i$ flipped, although the labels differ.  Hence it reads at least one word from every set.  A whole-record query cannot cover two sets because each record contains a consequent from only one padded source occurrence.  The same adversary gives $q$ sign-bit probes when sign bits are separately addressable.
\end{proof}

Since $N=Ww=\Theta(q\log q)$, this is only
$q=\Theta(N/\log N)$ addressed-word/record acquisition.  It is not an $\Omega(N)$ bit-time result, and it changes if the encoding packs many signs into one machine word.  The family lies in the unrestricted canonical Horn-selector language; the padded endpoint variables occur six times, so it is \emph{not} claimed to lie in the degree-four sublanguage.

\section{Paid-transport annihilation and direct-inclusion salvage}
\begin{theorem}[Write-cost annihilation; PROVED/EXACT]
Any ordinary explicit materialization of $G_b$ writes at least $R(q)=32q+130$ target words.  Transporting only the baseline source bound $L_{\rm old}(q)=q$ through one target call gives
\[
L_{\rm old}(q)-R(q)-H(q)
\le q-(32q+130)<0
\]
for every nonnegative overhead $H$.  Hence the compatible-model transport inequality yields no positive target-time lower bound.  Direct adversary analysis on the already represented target family nevertheless preserves the $q$-field query bound, without increasing its order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The fixed explicit representation has $W=32q+130$ words, each of which must be written before an ordinary target call.  Substitute this mandatory construction cost into
\[
L_{\rm old}\le R+H+Q\tau T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g).
\]
The numerator is negative.  A lazy oracle simulation would avoid output writes only by changing the target-access model and must instead charge each simulated target query and retained source access.  The preceding theorem is an independent direct-inclusion argument, not a rearrangement of the failed reduction inequality.
\end{proof}

This is the first exact ladder reconciliation: the base query bound survives representation-preserving inclusion but paid construction consumes it.  Neither route amplifies beyond input acquisition.  For arbitrary 2-CNF plus $k$ genuine ternaries, choosing one variable from each unhit ternary and branching over the resulting set gives the certified $2^k\operatorname{poly}(N)$ upper bound; no matching lower bound is known.

\textbf{Adopted:} Role~4's lift, Hall boundary, exact backdoors, and degree-four NP-complete classification.  \textbf{Strengthened:} exact target-word count, field/bit/record conversion, direct target adversary, and paid-transport numerator.  \textbf{Downgraded/clarified:} the lifted chain belongs to the unrestricted canonical target, not the maximum-degree-four sublanguage.  \textbf{Rejected:} any inference from NP-completeness, $k=m$ ternaries, or backdoor $m$ to mandatory $2^m$ work.  \textbf{Procedure defect:} predecessor sentinel omission; scientific content retained.

It must start after all $N$ bits may have been read, define its residual/representation measure independently of runtime, and prove either superpolynomial charged semantic compression/compatibility or superpolynomial continuation.  It must survive source-aware recognition, Horn/SCC, affine elimination, matching, extension variables, compact alternate representations, caching/learning, and decide-first normalization.  No additional query gadget can supply this missing arrow because explicit-input query depth is at most input length.
