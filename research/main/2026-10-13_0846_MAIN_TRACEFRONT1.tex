\section{Proof of TRACE-FRONTDOOR-NOSIM1}

Because $(F_n)$ is polynomial-time constructible and canonical, a uniform
machine can parse the size/index parameter, regenerate the corresponding
$F_n$, and compare it with its input in polynomial time.  Define
\[
 A^\star(F)=
 \begin{cases}
   0,&F=F_n\text{ for some }n\in I,\\
   \text{the result of exhaustive search on }F,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Every front-door formula is unsatisfiable, and exhaustive search is exact, so
$A^\star$ is a total correct uniform deterministic SAT decider.  Its execution
on $F_n$ is polynomial in the explicit encoded length $N_n=|F_n|$.

For any total deterministic decider $A$, let $P_A(F,\pi)$ replay a locally
encoded accepting computation of $A(F)=0$.  The verifier runs in time
polynomial in $|F|+|\pi|$; soundness follows from determinism and correctness
of $A$, and every unsatisfiable input has such a trace.  Thus $P_A$ is a
solver-dependent Cook--Reckhow-style refutation system.  For $A^\star$, the
inputs $F_n$ have $P_{A^\star}$ proofs of polynomial length.

Suppose Resolution polynomially simulated these traces.  Translating the
front-door trace would give a Resolution refutation of $F_n$ of polynomial
size, contradicting the stipulated superpolynomial Resolution lower bound.
Hence the simulation does not exist.  The argument uses neither $P\ne NP$ nor
an assumption about the running time of $A^\star$ off the sequence.

For the recovered compiled Tseitin branch, the historical source parameter
counts variables.  The compiler has $v=24n$ target variables and $m=32n$
clauses, while an explicit clause-list encoding has
$N=\Theta(v\log v)$ under the fixed literal-name convention.  The recovered
Resolution bound therefore reads
\[
  \ResSize(F_N)=2^{\Omega(v)}=2^{\Omega(N/\log N)},
\]
which is superpolynomial in encoded bits.  A standard explicit constant-degree
expander construction plus the polynomial compiler supplies the required
uniform sequence.  The proof uses only the sequence and never claims that all
exact-$(2,2)$ inputs are recognizable by the front door.

\section{Why this does not prove a decision lower bound}

The theorem constructs a correct decider that is deliberately fast on the
Resolution-hard sequence and slow elsewhere.  It refutes a universal
\emph{implementation compiler}; it does not produce a polynomial-time decider
for all SAT inputs.  Under the counterfactual $P=NP$, a globally polynomial
decider would likewise induce polynomial traces, but deriving a contradiction
would still require exactly the now-falsified universal promotion or another
model-matched lower bound.  Conversely, a future direct machine lower bound on
the fixed NP-complete exact-$(2,2)$ language would already imply $P\ne NP$ and
would not need a family-broadening step.

The quantifiers that fail are explicit:
\[
 \underbrace{\forall A\in\mathrm{Dec}(\SAT)}_{\text{false transfer domain}}
 \ \underbrace{\exists C_A\in\mathrm{poly}}_{\text{trace-to-Resolution translator}}
 \ \forall F\in\UNSAT:\quad
 C_A(F,\pi_A(F))\text{ is a }\Res\text{ refutation of }F.
\]
The constructed $A^\star$ is a witness against this statement.  Restricting
$A$ to a syntactic algorithm class may make a transfer theorem true, but then
the restriction, its universality, and every representation cost must be
proved rather than assumed.
