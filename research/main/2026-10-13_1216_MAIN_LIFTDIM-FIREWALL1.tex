\section{The no-retuning theorem for the displayed sufficient bounds}
ROUNDINGSQUEEZE3 records the generic dimension condition using the actual
base-variable count \(\nu\), with \(\nu\ge 2r^2t^{c+1}\), and writes a
retuning \(b=t^\alpha\).  Let \(0<\delta<1\).  The recovered sufficient
conditions imply
\[
 \alpha\ge \frac{2(c+1)}{\delta}.                          \tag{2}
\]
For a source-depth exponent \(h\), the same ledger gives the target formula,
known proof, and allowed cap exponents
\[
 q=4\alpha+c+1,\qquad
 u=\alpha(h+4)+h,\qquad
 z=\alpha(1-\delta)(h+c).                                  \tag{3}
\]
The intended supercritical choice requires \(h>q\), while using the displayed
known proof under the cap requires \(u\le z\).

\begin{theorem}[Dimension--cap firewall]
For \(c>0\), \(\alpha>0\), and \(0<\delta<1\), there is no \(h\) satisfying
(2), \(h>q\), and \(u\le z\) with \(q,u,z\) as in (3).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose \(u\le z\).  Expanding (3) and cancelling \(\alpha h\) yields
\[
 4\alpha+h\le \alpha(c-\delta h-\delta c).
\]
The left side is positive, so in particular \(\delta h<c\).  On the other
hand, \(h>q=4\alpha+c+1\) and (2) imply
\[
 \delta h>4\delta\alpha+\delta(c+1)
 \ge 8(c+1)+\delta(c+1)>c,
\]
a contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Displayed numerical choice]
For \(\delta=1/200\), the generic condition (2) requires
\(\alpha\ge400(c+1)\).  Hence \(\alpha=500\) fails already at \(c=3\),
where \(\alpha\ge1600\) is required.
\end{corollary}

This is an exact incompatibility among the recovered \emph{sufficient}
bounds.  It is not a disproof of the source paper.  A specialized lifting
lemma using another structural dimension, a notation correction, or a
tighter source-proof upper bound could evade the firewall.  The correct
status of the concrete recovered instantiation is therefore
\RET{}, while the printed theorem remains \KNOWN{} and a repaired
instantiation remains \OPEN{}.
