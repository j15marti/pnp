\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Authenticated source: explicit binary-encoded state length $M$, state $x_0\in\mathbb F_2^M$, binary $T$, explicit period-$p$ list of affine or SELECT stages, and polynomial-size terminal circuit $h$. SELECT names one old-state coordinate and chooses between two fully specified affine maps of the whole state. Malformed syntax, lengths and indices are rejected. One period is physically $p$ stages. Source length $L$ bounds $M,p,\log(T+2)$; dense-stage application costs $O(M^2)$ bits and direct simulation costs $O(pTM^2+L)$ time, $O(L+M+\log T)$ space on the same machine. No free exponentially large configuration is supplied.

For $m\ge1$, $N=2^m$, use $m$ little-endian counter bits and a carry bit $c$. A period has $m$ SELECT stages followed by the affine reset $c\leftarrow1$. At stage $i$, branch on old $x_i$, perform $x_i'\leftarrow x_i\oplus c$ in both branches and $c'\leftarrow0$ if $x_i=0$ or $c'\leftarrow c$ if $x_i=1$, leaving other coordinates fixed. Both branches are affine maps. At every period boundary, $F(x)=x+1\bmod N$. Starting from zero, all $N$ counter states are reachable; terminal readout $h(x)=[x=0]$ uses an $O(m)$ Boolean circuit.

\begin{theorem}[Exact exponential observation quotient]
Define equivalence of reachable period-boundary states by $x\sim y$ iff $h(F^k(x))=h(F^k(y))$ for every $k\ge0$. Then $x\sim y$ iff $x=y$. Hence every time-independent explicitly enumerated exact quotient preserving the terminal bit for \emph{all horizons} needs at least $N=2^m$ records.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $x\ne y$, take $k=-x\bmod N$. Then $F^k(x)=0$ and $F^k(y)=y-x\ne0\bmod N$. Thus the two future outputs differ, even though both states are reachable from the unique zero initial state.
\end{proof}
This is a representation/output-enumeration lower bound, not an unconditional algorithmic time lower bound: the exact fixed-horizon output from zero is simply $[T\bmod2^m=0]$ and can be computed in polynomial bit time.

\begin{theorem}[Infinite-series and Hankel-rank obstruction]
Let $a_t=h(F^t(0))$. As a formal power series over any field,
\[
G_m(z)=\sum_{t\ge0}a_tz^t=\sum_{j\ge0}z^{j2^m}=\frac1{1-z^{2^m}}.
\]
The $N$ by $N$ Hankel matrix $H=(a_{i+j})_{0\le i,j<N}$ is a permutation matrix and thus has rank $N=2^m$ over every field. Every homogeneous constant-coefficient recurrence generating $(a_t)$ has order at least $N$, and every linear state-space realization $a_t=u^\top A^tv$ has dimension at least $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In row $i$ the unique one is at $j\equiv-i\bmod N$, so $\mathrm{rank}(H)=N$. A recurrence of order $r$ gives Hankel rank at most $r$, while a linear realization of dimension $r$ factors $H$ through $\mathbb F^r$ and also gives rank at most $r$. 
\end{proof}
The rational expression $1/(1-z^{2^m})$ is succinct when the exponent is binary-encoded. Therefore this lower bound prohibits only \emph{small linear realizations and explicit all-horizons quotients}, not succinct rational formulas or general endpoint algorithms. It is a genuine model-restricted asymptotic lower bound from an infinite series.

\begin{theorem}[Refined PSPACE boundary]
Binary-horizon endpoint-bit evaluation for this full one-live-bit-selected affine source grammar is PSPACE-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Membership follows by storing one explicit state and binary step counter. Given an arbitrary deterministic polynomial-space halting TQBF decider, construct its polynomial-size Boolean next-configuration circuit $C_w$ with absorbing halts, $m$ configuration bits and binary horizon $T=2^m$. A deterministic run cannot repeat configurations before halting, so $C_w^T$ gives its acceptance bit. Compile $C_w$ in topological order with one zero scratch bit per gate: an AND gate assigns a fresh target to zero when its first live input is zero and to its second input otherwise (two affine SELECT branches); XOR, NOT and COPY are affine stages. One final affine parallel assignment copies output gates to the current-state register and a further affine stage clears all scratch. Thus one period exactly simulates $C_w$ with polynomial overhead, yielding PSPACE-hardness.
\end{proof}
A uniform polynomial-time exact solver for every such source would imply $P=PSPACE$. If both correct YES and correct NO bits have polynomial-size polynomial-time verified proofs with no false-label acceptance, then $NP=PSPACE$. Mere \emph{global at-most-two accepted labels} is vacuous for a one-bit output and implies neither collapse.

Matched-oracle PFCERT: fix one legitimate oracle $O$ for $U^O$, $V^O$, $P^O$, and $NP^O$, with shortest-description time-bounded $K^{t,O}$. Hirahara's prefix-plus-$O(\log n)$ suffix enumeration relativizes with the \emph{same} oracle and gives $P^O$-immunity of $R^{t,O}=\{x:K^{t,O}(x)\ge|x|-1\}$. Under a BGS collapsing oracle $A$, a polynomial-time $A$-oracle verifier accepting globally at most two $n$-bit labels for each $p$ with $|p|\le n-2$, and covering every within-$t(n)$ length-$n$ $U^A(p)$ output, would define $E\in NP^A=P^A$ with
\[
|E_n|\le 2\sum_{i=0}^{n-2}2^i=2^n-2.
\]
Its complement is infinite, in $P^A$, and by coverage a subset of $R^{t,A}$, a contradiction. This is a necessary nonrelativization barrier, not ordinary universal certificate existence. Syntactic source claims, fixed-PH verifiability, hash freshness, and polynomial line-query access do not supply full exponentially large configuration reachability or global output binding.
