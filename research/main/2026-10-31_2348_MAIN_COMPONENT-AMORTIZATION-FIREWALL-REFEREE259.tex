\begin{abstract}
REF258's canonical branch repair costs exactly 201 variables and 268 clauses.  This run proves the exact certificate-carrying condition under which disconnected satisfiable components can amortize that cost while preserving both SAT and UNSAT answers.  If a valid canonical source is $F=G\mathbin{\dot\cup}P$, the pivot lies in $G$, and every component of $P$ has a checked satisfying assignment, then
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b})
 \iff \operatorname{SAT}(G\!\upharpoonright_{x=b})
 \iff \operatorname{SAT}(R(G,x=b)).
\]
Writing $h=|\operatorname{var}(P)|$, the repaired child has exactly
$(n-h+201,m-4h/3+268)$.  Strict structural shrink begins at $h=204$, but power shrink $n'\le n^\lambda$ requires the retained answer-bearing core already to have at most $n^\lambda-201$ variables.  Thus component peeling does not manufacture an ARC contraction; it requires a near-total certified peel and a charged selector.  Independent replay passed 575,026 checks.  Arbitrary-$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$, FLMV transport, and P versus NP remain \status{OPEN}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact theorem}
Let $F=G\mathbin{\dot\cup}P$ be the disjoint union of incidence components of a valid canonical formula.  Let $x\in\operatorname{var}(G)$ and $b\in\{0,1\}$.  A peel certificate lists one satisfying assignment for every component of $P$, each checked against its source clauses.

\begin{lemma}[Certified component concentration]
Under the preceding conditions,
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b})
 \iff \operatorname{SAT}(G\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}).                 \tag{1}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Because the variable sets of $G$ and $P$ are disjoint, any model of the left side restricts to a model of $G\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}$.  Conversely, join a model of $G\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}$ with the checked component witnesses for $P$.  The union satisfies every clause of $F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Component-amortized canonical re-entry]
Let $R$ be REF258's all-input canonical $q=4$ branch repair.  Under the lemma's hypotheses,
\[
 \operatorname{SAT}(F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b})
 \iff \operatorname{SAT}(R(G,x=b)).                                \tag{2}
\]
If $F$ has $(n,m)$ and $P$ has $h$ variables, then $P$ has $4h/3$ clauses and the child has
\[
 (n',m')=(n-h+201,\;m-4h/3+268).                                    \tag{3}
\]
It strictly shrinks both counts iff $h>201$; since $3\mid h$, the minimum is $h=204$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (2) is (1) composed with REF258's proved equivalence
$\operatorname{SAT}(G\!\upharpoonright_{x=b})\iff\operatorname{SAT}(R(G,x=b))$.
Every canonical component has four literal occurrences per variable and three per clause, so $4h=3|P|$.  Removing $P$ leaves $(n-h,m-4h/3)$; REF258 adds exactly $(201,268)$.  Both inequalities in (3) are strict precisely when $h>201$.  Divisibility follows again from $4h=3|P|$.
\end{proof}

This theorem is same-model and both-label correct.  It does not make certificate acquisition or component choice free.

\section{Power-shortness and selection firewalls}
For fixed $0<\lambda<1$, the variable condition is
\[
 n-h+201\le n^\lambda
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 h\ge n+201-n^\lambda.                                               \tag{4}
\]
Equivalently, the retained answer-bearing core has at most $n^\lambda-201$ variables.  A constant additive saving is therefore categorically weaker than power shrink.

On an UNSAT source at least one retained component must be UNSAT, while every discarded component requires a checked SAT witness.  A policy that always knows which component carries the negative answer is a best-policy oracle.  Discovering components, finding or receiving the witnesses, verifying them, selecting the retained core, and storing or rereading all certificate/source data are charged.  On a connected source $P=\varnothing$, (3) is exactly REF258's growth recurrence.  This is the decisive adversarial control.

Large disjoint easy padding can make a fixed 399-variable child power-short relative to the padded source, for example at $\lambda=1/2$ after sufficient padding.  Such a padding-dominated family is not an all-input ARC theorem and supplies no direct FLMV contradiction.
