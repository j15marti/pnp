\paragraph{Status.}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  This note proves exact structural and
query-model statements, and gives a finite implementation audit.  It proves no
unrestricted deterministic time lower bound for SAT or 3-COLORING.

\section{Mechanisms tested before source review}
We tested three falsifiable candidates: (i) high treewidth after an exact
projection might force expensive residual compatibility; (ii) subdivision or
padding might amplify the fixed-width implication-chain query bound; and
(iii) that query bound might transport through a graph-coloring endpoint with
a positive remainder.  The first is falsified by an explicit matched-label
family, and the latter two fail because linear source construction/recognition
can consume the bound.  The useful survivor is only a hostile control for any
future semantic obstruction.

\section{Declared models and encodings}
Graphs use a canonical adjacency-list encoding with vertex labels
$0,\ldots,k-1$ and sorted edge records.  One machine word has
$w=\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\rceil$ bits; a record read costs one word probe.
For a graph $G=(V,E)$, $\Gamma(G)$ is the standard one-hot 3-COLORING CNF:
one positive ternary clause and three at-most-one binary clauses per vertex,
and three binary clauses per edge.  Thus
\[
 k=|V|,\quad n=3k,\quad m_2=3k+3|E|,
 \quad m=k+m_2,\quad \ell=9k+6|E|.
\]
The fixed-width CNF encoding has $N=(2+4m)w$ bits.  Source scanning,
recognition, auxiliary storage, and output construction are all charged.

\section{Independent check of the role-4 baseline}
For endpoints $z_0=1,z_q=0$ and
\[
 C_i=\begin{cases}(\neg z_{i-1}\lor z_i),&b_i=1,\\
 (\neg z_{i-1}\lor\neg z_i),&b_i=0,
 \end{cases}
\]
the chain is UNSAT iff $b=1^q$.  On the all-one input, any exact deterministic
field-query algorithm must read all $q$ orientation fields: if field $j$ is
unread, flipping only $b_j$ preserves length, variable IDs, clause count,
record width, and every read field, but flips the answer.  This is an exact
$q$-field lower bound.  With $O(1)$ words per fixed-width field it is
$\Omega(q)$ word probes and $\Omega(qw)$ inspected bits only when a field read
reveals its full $w$ bits.  Since $N=\Theta(qw)$, the unconditional statement
is $\Omega(N/w)$ word probes, not an unrestricted RAM/Turing-machine time
lower bound beyond input acquisition.

\section{A matched dense firewall}
For $t\ge4$, let $A=\{a_0,\dots,a_{t-1}\}$ and
$B=\{b_0,\dots,b_{t-1}\}$, and start with $K_{t,t}$ between $A$ and $B$.
Define $S_t$ by adding the three edges
$a_0a_1,a_0a_2,a_0a_3$; define $U_t$ by adding
$a_0a_1,a_1a_2,a_2a_0$.

\begin{theorem}[Matched labels with linear treewidth]
$S_t$ is 3-colorable, $U_t$ is not, and both graphs have
$2t$ vertices, $t^2+3$ edges, and treewidth exactly $t$.
Their one-hot CNFs have identical metadata
\[
 k=2t,quad n=6t,quad m_2=3t^2+6t+9,quad
 m=3t^2+8t+9,quad \ell=6t^2+18t+18.
\]
Moreover the primal treewidth of either CNF lies in $[t,3t+2]$.
Nevertheless, membership in this canonical two-label family and its SAT label
are decidable in $O(t^2)=O(N/w)$ word time and $O(\log N)$ auxiliary bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Color $a_0$ with 0, the other vertices of $A$ with 1, and all of $B$ with 2
to color $S_t$.  In $U_t$, the triangle in $A$ uses all three colors, leaving
no color for any vertex of $B$, which is adjacent to all of $A$.
Both contain $K_{t,t}$.  Its vertex connectivity is $t$, so its treewidth is at
least $t$; the path decomposition with one bag $A\cup\{b\}$ for each $b\in B$
has width $t$ and also covers all three added $A$-edges.  This proves equality.
Contracting each color triangle of $\Gamma(G)$ gives $G$ as a minor, proving
the primal lower bound.  Replacing every graph vertex in the preceding
decomposition by its three color variables gives bags of size at most
$3(t+1)$, hence width at most $3t+2$.  The count formulas follow directly from
the declared encoding.  A sorted scan verifies the bipartite core and compares
the three exceptional edges with the two templates; this also determines the
label within the promised family.
\end{proof}

This theorem retires the claim that dense coupling, large syntactic residual,
or unbounded source/primal treewidth alone forces expensive SAT continuation.
It is a restricted family theorem, not a language lower bound.

\section{Two further controls}
\begin{lemma}[Leaf padding does not amplify]
Attaching any forest to an existing graph vertex preserves 3-colorability.
The added vertices are colored greedily after the core.  Canonical padding is
recognized and stripped in linear source time.  Thus padding can increase
$N,k$, and syntactic residual without increasing semantic difficulty.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[Supplied-decomposition upper bound]
Given a verified width-$\tau$ tree decomposition with $B$ bags, 3-COLORING is
decidable by standard bag-table dynamic programming in
$O(B3^{\tau+1}\operatorname{poly}(\tau))$ time and
$O(B3^{\tau+1})$ bits of conservative live storage.  Constructing or verifying
the decomposition must be charged separately.
\end{proposition}
This is consistent with the classic bounded-treewidth algorithms of Arnborg
and Proskurowski.  It is an upper bound, not evidence that the exponential
dependence on $\tau$ is mandatory for unrestricted algorithms.

\section{Transport ledger}
If a source lower bound $L_{\rm old}(q)$ is simulated using recognition or
reduction cost $R$, other overhead $H$, at most $Q$ target calls of size at
most $g(q)$, and simulation factor $\tau$, then only
\[
 T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g(q))\ \ge\
 \frac{L_{\rm old}(q)-R(q)-H(q)}{Q(q)\tau(q)}
\]
is justified, and only when the numerator is positive.  Materializing a
length-$\Theta(q)$ target or canonically recognizing the embedded chain costs
$\Theta(q)$ words, consuming the chain's $\Omega(q)$ source bound.  Direct
inclusion preserves an $\Omega(q)$ query bound on the same visible fields but
does not increase its order.  The dense and wheel families add no amplification:
their labels are linearly recognizable.  Hence the proposed chain-to-coloring
transport produces no superlinear remainder.

\section{Reconciliation with the projection kernel}
For $F=T(X)\wedge B(X,Y)$, where $B$ is 2-CNF and $T$ has at most $k$ ternary
clauses, the boundary $X$ has size $s\le3k$.  The exact 2-CNF projection of
$B$ onto $X$ has at most $2s^2$ unit/binary clauses.  The current certified
construction uses one SCC computation plus reachability traversals from the
$2s$ signed boundary literals, taking
$O((1+s)(n+m)+s^2)=O(k(n+m)+k^2)$ word time, not the earlier
$O(k^2(n+m))$ bound.  The resulting exact kernel has at most
$k+18k^2$ clauses.  This is a fixed-parameter upper bound and an exact
representation theorem; it is not a lower bound.

\section{Reproducible audit and resource ledger}
The accompanying deterministic Python audit checked $t=4,\ldots,16$, odd
wheels $h=5,\ldots,31$, three padding levels, explicit decompositions, exact
counts, both labels, and every wheel positive-clause deletion witness.  It ran
545 assertions, 398 exact 3-color searches, and found zero failures.  This is a
finite implementation audit only.  The symbolic proofs above establish the
asymptotic restricted statements.

\section{Barrier audit and next lemma}
The construction relativizes and is compatible with a semantic oracle that
simply returns the label; it therefore supplies no nonrelativizing or
algebrizing leverage.  It also defeats wrapper claims based only on large
width, density, clause count, or residual dimension because a charged source
recognizer bypasses continuation.

The highest-value missing lemma is a \emph{charged semantic
compression-or-continuation theorem}: for one fixed NP-complete encoding and
every total uniform deterministic exact decider $A$, define an obstruction
$r_A(F)$ independently of runtime, then prove either costly acquisition or
compatibility construction, or
$T_A(F)\ge2^{a r_A(F)}/N^b$, on inputs with
$r_A(F)/\log N$ unbounded.  It must survive source recognition, SCC/affine and
treewidth dynamic programming, padding, caching, learned constraints,
decide-first normalization, and semantic-oracle leakage.  Every arrow in that
universal statement remains OPEN.
