\paragraph{Opening-acquisition trilemma.}
\texttt{OPENING-ACQUISITION-TRILEMMA1} is \statusproved\ in the declared
deterministic static model, pending independent review.  Let an enriched
opening be $\tau=(c,e,z)$ with commitment $c$ fixed before challenge $e$ and
polynomial-time verifier $V(F,c,e,z)$.  A special-soundness-style fork requires
\[
 V(F,c,e_0,z_0)=V(F,c,e_1,z_1)=1,\qquad e_0\ne e_1.
\]
There are three acquisition routes.  Ordinary satisfying witnesses do not
become conflicting openings without a separate source-matched commitment
theorem.  Exact black-box replay with fixed source, compiler coins,
challenge/query policy, and deterministic one-bit decider has a unique
transcript.  Varying a challenge/query imports an external compiler whose
totality, common-commitment guarantee, costs, and extraction consequence must
be proved; inspecting internal state instead requires a non-black-box model
and wrapper-covariant transfer.

Accordingly \texttt{SPECIAL-SOUNDNESS-REPAIR-AS-INTERMEDIATE1} is
\statusretired\ in its unqualified form.  The smallest repair is
\texttt{EXTERNAL-CHALLENGE-SOURCE-PASS1}, which is \statusopen\ and
separation-strength.

\paragraph{Conditional total-pair search.}
For
\[
 Q(F,c,e_0,z_0,e_1,z_1):=[e_0\ne e_1]\wedge
 V(F,c,e_0,z_0)\wedge V(F,c,e_1,z_1),
\]
if $Q$ is polynomially balanced, polynomial-time decidable, and nonempty for
each promised source, then under $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$ the
lexicographically first tuple is computable in polynomial time by NP prefix
search.  Thus a total polynomially verifiable fork is not by itself an
unconditional lower-bound resource.  If an extractor maps every such tuple to
an unconditionally impossible object, the full implication is already a
$P\ne NP$ proof and must be audited as separation-strength.

\section{Authoritative defense reconciliation --- 2026-10-08}

\begin{warningbox}
\textbf{CURRENT STATUS: OPEN.}  Nothing in this repository proves
$P\ne NP$.  This section supersedes every older endpoint statement, proof
status, and ``verified snapshot'' label below.  The older sections are retained
only as an audit history.  A historical path that is absent from the current

\end{warningbox}

\paragraph{Endpoint correction.}
The STACS extended abstract's claimed universal product exponent
$2\cos(\pi/7)$ was retracted in the published full version.  The established
general Williams product obstruction has exponent
\[
 \varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}:
 \qquad T(N)S(N)=\Omega_{\mathrm{i.o.}}
       \bigl(N^{\varphi-\varepsilon}\bigr)
\]
for polynomially bounded deterministic SAT time and space in the stated
random-access model.  Therefore a general mixed route must satisfy the strict
condition $t+s<\varphi$, not $t+s<2\cos(\pi/7)$.  The constant
$2\cos(\pi/7)$ remains relevant to the separately established very-small-space
time regime, not as a universal product boundary.

The concrete landing $(t,s)=(1,2/3)$ is still forbidden, but for a different
reason: Fortnow's near-linear-time theorem rules out SAT in simultaneous
$N^{1+o(1)}$ time and $N^{1-\varepsilon}$ space for every fixed
$\varepsilon>0$.  Taking $\varepsilon=1/4$ covers
$N^{2/3+o(1)}$ space.  Every endpoint invocation must use actual encoded bit
length, one total uniform deterministic machine, simultaneous worst-case
bounds, and fully charged writable storage and virtual-input access.

\paragraph{Strongest current direct spine.}
Fix one clocked polynomial SAT decider $A$ under the contradiction hypothesis
$P=NP$.  If the still-\textbf{OPEN} CHARGED STRICT SUCCESSOR-$2/3$ theorem
constructs, for every ordinary $M$-bit CNF, an exactly equivalent
self-contained CNF of length $M^{2/3+o(1)}$ in time $M^{1+o(1)}$ and writable
space $M^{2/3+o(1)}$, before any parent-scale SAT call, then a fixed recursion
depth depending only on the fixed clocked machine eliminates its finite time
and space exponents.  The result would be one uniform SAT algorithm at
$(1,2/3)$ and would contradict Fortnow.  The recurrence and endpoint steps are
exact conditional mathematics.  The all-input charged successor construction
is not known.

\paragraph{Alternative typed spine.}
A forward wrapper and an outer recursive wrapper may be composed without an
unbounded recursive operational loop by fixing a finite depth and using the
clocked machine at the leaves.  For a finite type graph, the exact depth-$d$
time recurrence is
\[
 C_i^{(0)}=K_i,
 \qquad
 C_i^{(d+1)}=
 \max\!\left\{R_i,\max_{e:i\to j}
       (w_e+\theta_e C_j^{(d)})\right\},
\]
with the analogous simultaneous-space recurrence.  If every reachable cycle
contracts, these finite-depth budgets converge to the least max-affine fixed
point.  A strict landing $C+\rho<\varphi$, or a landing in another explicitly
cited endpoint, would then give a contradiction.  The favorable all-CNF typed
epoch and its cheap acquisition remain \textbf{OPEN}.

\paragraph{Generated covers and hashes.}
A fully explicit closed generated cover is not a weaker escape hatch.  A
uniform $\widetilde O(m)$ bounded-fan-in verifier for a canonical padded CNF
encoding can be built by sorting literal occurrences by variable identifier,
checking adjacent-value consistency, and checking every clause.  Composing
that verifier with an explicit generator and applying a full biconditional
Tseitin encoding produces one ordinary equivalent CNF at the generator's
charged description scale.  Conversely, a strict successor is a
constant-output generator.  Thus generated cover and ordinary shrinker are
equivalent up to polylogarithmic factors under the closed explicit encoding;
the former output-width condition $2b\le h$ is unnecessary.  Four-, six-, and
eight-hex-digit hash prefixes do not preserve SAT semantics: the finite audit
finds legal opposite-answer collisions at every tested width.  Parallel and
disjunctive wrappers remain useful organizations, but their construction,
Cartesian branching, failed routes, source access, and replay must all be
charged.

The current detailed routes are \path{main4.tex} and \path{main5.tex}; the
defense synthesis is \path{main10.tex}; the compact status ledger is
\path{main9.tex}.  The bounded hostile-control runner is
\path{defense_final_link3_audit.py} with raw output
