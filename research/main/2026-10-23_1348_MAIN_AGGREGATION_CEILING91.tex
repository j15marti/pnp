\paragraph{Global status.}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  This report proves an exact deterministic
field-query lower bound and a linear-size SAT encoding.  It simultaneously
proves that the lower bound saturates explicit source acquisition and does not
yield an unrestricted superlinear time lower bound.

\section{Claim card and mechanisms}
\textbf{Target.} Test whether composition of the fixed-width 2-SAT chain can
amplify its lower bound faster than encoding and acquisition cost.

\textbf{Quantifiers.} For every $p,q\ge1$ and every full binary read-once
monotone AND/OR tree $A$ using all $p$ leaves, construct one width-at-most-three
CNF family indexed by $pq$ orientation fields, prove its exact SAT semantics,
and determine its deterministic exact field-query complexity.

\textbf{Model.} Each orientation is one fixed-width field.  A field query
reveals its orientation bit.  Formula records, identifiers, tags, writes,
parsing, recognition, auxiliary variables, and peak live storage are charged
separately.  The source fields and encoded CNF are the same realization.

Before source review we proposed three falsifiable mechanisms:
\begin{enumerate}
\item disjoint conjunction might give a direct-sum lower bound;
\item guarded selectors might preserve that multiplication through ternary
clauses and the NP-complete 3CNF language;
\item iterating AND/OR aggregation might grow work faster than source length.
\end{enumerate}
The first two succeed exactly in the field-query model.  The third is
falsified: every multiplication is matched by the same multiplication in
independent source fields.  The resulting cost is acquisition order.

\section{The chain baseline}
For block $j$, use variables $z_{j,0},\ldots,z_{j,q}$, force
$z_{j,0}=1,z_{j,q}=0$, and set
\[
C_{j,i}=\begin{cases}
(\neg z_{j,i-1}\lor z_{j,i}),&b_{j,i}=1,\\
(\neg z_{j,i-1}\lor\neg z_{j,i}),&b_{j,i}=0.
\end{cases}
\]
The block is UNSAT iff all $q$ orientations equal one, hence its SAT label is
\[
s_j(b)=1-\prod_{i=1}^{q}b_{j,i}
=\bigvee_{i=1}^{q}\neg b_{j,i}.
\]
All encodings indexed by $b\in\{0,1\}^{pq}$ have equal record counts,
identifier widths, and metadata.  A fixed-width unit can be represented by a
repeated-literal binary clause without changing the claim.

\section{Guarded aggregation CNF}
Let $A$ be a full binary tree whose $p$ leaves are the labels $s_1,\ldots,s_p$
and whose internal nodes are AND or OR.  Introduce an activation variable
$a_u$ for every node $u$.  Add the root unit $a_{\rm root}$.  For an AND node
$u$ with children $v,w$, add
\[
(\neg a_u\lor a_v)\quad\text{and}\quad(\neg a_u\lor a_w).
\]
For an OR node add
\[
(\neg a_u\lor a_v\lor a_w).
\]
For leaf $j$, guard its two endpoint constraints and every chain clause by
$\neg a_j$.  Thus its binary chain clauses become ternary and its endpoint
units become binary.  Call the resulting formula $G_{A,p,q}(b)$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact guarded semantics]
For every orientation source $b$,
\[
G_{A,p,q}(b)\in\SAT
\iff A(s_1(b),\ldots,s_p(b))=1.
\]
If $I_\wedge$ and $I_\vee$ are the numbers of AND and OR nodes, then the
encoding has exactly
\[
n=pq+3p-1
\]
variables and
\[
m=1+2I_\wedge+I_\vee+p(q+2)
\]
clauses of width at most three.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root activation is forced.  An active AND node activates both children; an
active OR node activates at least one child.  An active leaf enforces exactly
its original chain.  Hence any satisfying assignment identifies active
satisfiable leaves forming a witness for the recursive value $A=1$.
Conversely, when $A=1$, activate both true children of AND nodes, choose a true
child at each active OR node, and extend every active satisfiable chain.  Set
remaining activations false.  This satisfies all guards.  The full tree has
$2p-1$ activations, giving $p(q+1)+(2p-1)=pq+3p-1$ variables.  The clause count
is immediate from the construction.
\end{proof}

Every binary clause $(x\lor y)$ can be replaced by
$(x\lor y\lor c)\wedge(x\lor y\lor\neg c)$ with a fresh $c$, and a unit $x$
by its four clauses over two fresh variables.  Therefore the family maps with
constant overhead into ordinary proper 3CNF.  This reaches an NP-complete
language, but only as a specially tagged, easily recognizable subfamily.

\section{Exact query multiplication and its ceiling}
\begin{theorem}[Aggregation ceiling]
For every $A,p,q$ above, the deterministic exact orientation-field decision
tree complexity of
\[
P_{A,p,q}(b)=A(s_1(b),\ldots,s_p(b))
\]
is exactly
\[
D(P_{A,p,q})=pq.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The unique multilinear polynomial for $s_j$ has full degree $q$ with nonzero
leading coefficient.  Induct on the aggregation tree.  For disjoint variable
sets, an AND multiplies its child polynomials, so their top degrees add.  An OR
is $f+g-fg$; its unique top-degree term comes from $-fg$, so the top degrees
again add.  Thus $P_{A,p,q}$ has polynomial degree $pq$.

An exact decision tree of depth $d$ computes a multilinear polynomial of degree
at most $d$: at its root querying $x_i$, write the output as
$(1-x_i)f_0+x_if_1$ and induct on the two depth-$(d-1)$ subtrees.  Hence
$D(P_{A,p,q})\ge pq$.  Reading all $pq$ fields gives the reverse inequality.
\end{proof}

The theorem covers disjoint conjunction, guarded OR selection, and arbitrary
alternation in one statement.  It is compatible with the general deterministic
decision-tree direct-sum theorem of Jain, Klauck and Santha, but is proved here
for a one-bit aggregate rather than a $p$-output relation.

\begin{corollary}[No superlinear transport]
Under a fixed-width record encoding with word width
$w=\Theta(\log(n+m+1))$, the CNF length is
$N=\Theta((pq+p)w)$ and therefore
\[
D(P_{A,p,q})=pq=\Theta(N/w).
\]
Canonical recognition and direct source evaluation take $O(pq+p)$ word
operations and $O(p+q)$ working words.  Thus the exact lower and upper bounds
have input-acquisition order.
\end{corollary}

For a flat arbitrary-order representation, building full indices may instead
use $O(N/w)$ working words; that representation cost does not strengthen the
lower bound.  In the standard SCC implementation, disjoint 2-CNF conjunctions
also take linear total time.  Aspvall--Plass--Tarjan supplies the classical
linear-time 2-SAT baseline.

\section{Charged transport and time--space ledger}
Suppose the source lower bound is $L_{\rm old}=pq$ fields.  Any paid map to the
proper-3CNF target writes $\Theta(pq+p)$ records, so
$R=\Theta(pq+p)$.  The only valid inequality is
\[
L_{\rm old}
\le R+H+Q\tau T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g),
\qquad
T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g)
\ge\frac{L_{\rm old}-R-H}{Q\tau}
\]
when the numerator is positive.  Here no positive asymptotic remainder is
proved.  Direct inclusion avoids materialization, but preserves only the
$\Theta(N/w)$ field bound.

\section{Hostile controls and barriers}
The construction includes the following controls.
\begin{itemize}
\item $p=1$ is the original implication chain.
\item All-AND aggregation is component decomposition; all-OR aggregation is a
guarded selector; alternating trees test nested composition.
\item Renaming, complementation, and canonical padding change neither the
linear recognizer nor the charged source length.
\item Affine/Tseitin families can similarly aggregate many visible fields but
remain source-decidable by Gaussian elimination or a parity checksum.
\item A maximal semantic oracle returns the aggregate label immediately.
Therefore this restricted query proof relativizes and supplies no
nonrelativizing bridge.  Algebraic access can evaluate the displayed
multilinear/read-once source representation; polynomial degree is used only
inside the declared point-query model.
\item A decide-first normalizer under hypothetical P$=$NP still emits a fixed
TRUE/FALSE instance.  Nothing here blocks it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Computational audit}
The accompanying deterministic Python program exhaustively compared the
guarded CNF with the source semantics for $37{,}926$ instances, including
$33{,}123$ SAT and $4{,}803$ UNSAT controls.  It checked exact variable, clause,
and width counts; computed 31 nonzero full-degree coefficients; and independently
computed 31 exact decision-tree depths.  In total, 151,848 assertions passed
with zero failures.  Finite execution verifies the implementation only; the
two theorems above carry the asymptotic claims.

\section{Single next missing lemma}
Independent direct products are now exhausted: they can reach the full
explicit-input query ceiling but not exceed it.  The first plausible
amplification must be a \emph{post-acquisition shared-compatibility lemma}.
For one fixed NP-complete encoding, define a semantic interface independently
of runtime and prove that every uniform exact decider, after reading the source,
must either construct/verify a compressed compatibility summary at
superpolynomial charged cost or perform superpolynomial continuation.  It must
survive the exact projection kernel, component and selector aggregation,
SCC/Horn/affine/treewidth algorithms, caching, source recognition, alternate
encodings, maximal semantic oracles, and decide-first normalization.  The
definition, work inequality, and universal coverage are all OPEN.
