\section{Universal Krom projection ceiling}
Let $F(X,Y)$ be any 2-CNF and let $s=|X|\ge1$. Write
\[
  \Pi_F(X)=\exists Y\,F(X,Y).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Quadratic ceiling; PROVED/EXACT]
$\Pi_F$ has an equivalent auxiliary-free 2-CNF over precisely $X$ with at most $2s^2$ distinct non-tautological clauses. Consequently no recursive composition of 2-CNF mediators can force superquadratic final CNF size as a function of the retained boundary count.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Eliminate each $y\in Y$ by Davis--Putnam resolution. Resolving $(y\vee a)$ with $(\neg y\vee b)$ yields $(a\vee b)$ of width at most two; unit and empty clauses are allowed. Standard extension in the reverse direction proves exact existential projection. After all eliminations, remove tautologies, duplicates, and redundant copies.

Over $s$ variables there are $2s$ units. There are
\[
  \binom{2s}{2}-s=2s(s-1)
\]
non-tautological binary clauses, after excluding complementary-literal pairs. Hence there are only $2s^2$ possible unit/binary clauses in total. Any intermediate syntactic blowup can therefore be compressed to this final bound. An unsatisfiable projection is represented by one empty clause.
\end{proof}

\section{Tight family and arbitrary-width lower bound}
For $p\ge1$, take boundary sets $A=\{a_1,\ldots,a_p\}$ and $B=\{b_1,\ldots,b_p\}$ and one mediator $u$:
\[
M_p=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{p}(\neg a_i\vee u)
\ \land\
\bigwedge_{j=1}^{p}(\neg u\vee\neg b_j).
\]
Eliminating $u$ gives
\[
P_p=\bigwedge_{i,j=1}^{p}(\neg a_i\vee\neg b_j).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Tight quadratic lower family; PROVED/EXACT]
For every boundary assignment $\alpha$,
\[
\exists u\,M_p(\alpha,u)\iff P_p(\alpha).
\]
Every auxiliary-free CNF over $A\cup B$, even allowing arbitrary clause width and signs, that is equivalent to $P_p$ contains at least $p^2$ non-tautological clauses. Thus the universal $O(s^2)$ ceiling is asymptotically tight for $s=2p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A true $a_i$ forces $u=1$, which forces all $b_j=0$; if all $a_i=0$, choose $u=0$. For every pair $(i,j)$, let $\omega_{ij}$ set precisely $a_i=b_j=1$ and all other boundary variables zero. It falsifies $P_p$. Any entailed non-tautological clause $C$ must contain a negative $A$ literal: otherwise set all $A$ variables zero while keeping every literal of $C$ false, obtaining a model of $P_p$ that falsifies $C$. Symmetrically $C$ contains a negative $B$ literal.

If $C$ is false at $\omega_{ij}$, every negative $A$ literal in $C$ must be $\neg a_i$ and every negative $B$ literal must be $\neg b_j$. Therefore the same clause cannot be false at any different $\omega_{i'j'}$. Each of the $p^2$ forbidden assignments needs a distinct falsified clause. The displayed $P_p$ meets the bound.
\end{proof}

Under the fixed-record convention, $M_p$ has $2p+1$ variables, $2p$ clauses, and
\[
N_{\rm src}=(8p+2)\left\lceil\log_2(64(4p+2))\right\rceil
=\Theta(p\log p)
\]
encoded bits. The explicit projection has $p^2$ binary records and
$\Theta(p^2\log p)=\Theta(N_{\rm src}^2/\log N_{\rm src})$ output bits. This is a genuine superlinear \emph{compilation-output} lower bound.

\section{Exact alternate-representation firewall}
The same boundary function has the two-term DNF
\[
P_p\equiv
\left(\bigwedge_i\neg a_i\right)\vee
\left(\bigwedge_j\neg b_j\right),
\]
of only $2p$ literal occurrences, the original $2p$-clause CNF with one retained mediator, and an $O(p)$ scan which tests whether both an $A$ bit and a $B$ bit are true. Hence the quadratic blowup is caused by the simultaneous requirements \emph{CNF} and \emph{no auxiliary variables}. It is not semantic decision hardness.

Role~3's nonlinear selector relation strengthens the restricted output phenomenon: a linear proper-3CNF source can require $h^t$ clauses after auxiliary-free CNF forgetting. Yet it too has a $t$-term DNF, a compact existential CNF, and an $O(th)$ contextual scan. Thus moving from the Krom quadratic ceiling to exponential nonlinear forgetting still does not repair the all-decider arrow.

\section{Independent Role 4 source audit}
Role~4 embeds the mediator into matched mixed 2/3-CNF sources $H_{p,\lambda}$ with
\[
n=10p+9,\quad m_2=10p+8,\quad k=m_3=2p+2,\quad m=12p+10.
\]
Four proper gadgets force blockers $s,t,v,w$ to zero. Then $(a_1\vee s\vee t)$ forces $a_1=1$, so $u=1$ and every $b_j=0$. The final clause is $(\lambda b_1\vee v\vee w)$: positive $\lambda$ is UNSAT and negative $\lambda$ is SAT. The pair differs in exactly one sign word with equal length and metadata.

For the SAT label, each blocker contributes a free private bit, giving factor $2^4$; $a_1=1$ has four filler settings; each remaining free $a_i$ has $3+4=7$ weighted filler settings; and every $b_j=0$ has three. Therefore
\[
\#\mathrm{SAT}(H_{p,-})=
2^4\cdot4\cdot7^{p-1}3^p
=64\cdot7^{p-1}3^p,
\qquad
\#\mathrm{SAT}(H_{p,+})=0.
\]
This independently confirms Role~4's helper accounting. The single changed sign forces one addressed read on the matched pair, not $\Omega(p)$ reads. A uniform template recognizer decides both labels in $O(p)$ words without constructing the projection.
