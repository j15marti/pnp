\begin{abstract}
The first nonminimum quota class left open by REF257 succeeds.  One critical-clause gadget, four fresh balanced variables, and seven clauses restore every Shannon cofactor of a valid D\"ocker duplicate-free proper Monotone 3-SAT-$(2,2)$ input to the same language without changing the branch answer.  For all valid $F$, variables $x$, and $b\in\{0,1\}$,
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(R(F,x=b))\iff\operatorname{SAT}(F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}).
\]
But $R$ has exactly $(n+201,m+268)$ when $F$ has $(n,m)$.  This repairs hereditary grammar, not power-shortness.  Independent U198/V198/Y6 controls and a reduced census of 2,628 layouts support the theorem.  Arbitrary-$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$, FLMV, and P versus NP remain \status{OPEN}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Construction and theorem}

Fix valid $F$, pivot $x$, and branch $x=b$.  The two clauses satisfied by the pivot disappear, leaving four selected-polarity deficit literals $o_0,o_1,o_2,o_3$.  The two opposite clauses become binaries.  Pair the four deficits into two pairs of distinct variables.  Such a pairing exists: each deleted clause has two distinct co-variables, and sharing both would make the two source clauses duplicates.

Use the checked critical pair
\[
C=(x_{194}\vee x_{196}\vee x_{197}),\qquad
D=U198\setminus\{C\}.
\]
$D$ is satisfiable and $D\wedge C$ is not, so every $D$ model falsifies all three literals of $C$.  Orient a disjoint copy so its three forced-false ports have the binary polarity.  Two ports pad the two binaries and the third is $p$.

Add balanced variables $z_0,z_1,z_2,z_3$.  In selected-sign notation add
\begin{align}
&(o_0\vee o_1\vee z_0), &(o_2\vee o_3\vee z_2),\nonumber\\
&(z_0\vee z_1\vee z_3), &(z_1\vee z_2\vee z_3),       \tag{1}
\end{align}
and at the opposite sign add
\begin{align}
&(\bar z_0\vee\bar z_1\vee p),\nonumber\\
&(\bar z_0\vee\bar z_2\vee\bar z_3),
&(\bar z_1\vee\bar z_2\vee\bar z_3).                  \tag{2}
\end{align}
The quota identity is $3c-4q=2+3k$; $(k,q,c)=(1,4,7)$.

\begin{lemma}[Boundary transparency]
For every assignment of the four old slots, (1)--(2) extends when $p=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set selected-literal values $(z_0,z_1,z_2,z_3)=(1,0,1,0)$.  Every clause is then true independently of the $o_i$ and $p$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical branch re-entry]
For every valid canonical $F$, every variable $x$, and each $b\in\{0,1\}$, $R(F,x=b)$ is a valid canonical instance and
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(R(F,x=b))\iff
\operatorname{SAT}(F\!\upharpoonright_{x=b}).          \tag{3}
\]
If $F$ has $(n,m)$, then $R$ has $(n+201,m+268)$.  Hence
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(F)\iff
\operatorname{SAT}(R(F,x=0))\vee\operatorname{SAT}(R(F,x=1)). \tag{4}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Removing $x$ leaves $n-1$ variables and $m-2$ clauses, including two binaries.  $D$ contributes 198 variables and 263 clauses; (1)--(2) contributes four variables and seven clauses.  This gives the stated sizes.

Every old deficit appears once in (1).  Each $z_i$ appears twice per sign.  Each of the three deficit ports appears once: two in padded binaries and $p$ in (2).  All other counts remain two per sign.  Fresh-name separation and the proper old-slot pairing give monotonicity, three distinct variables, and no duplicate clauses.

Every gadget model makes the padding ports false, so the padded clauses equal the raw binaries semantically.  The lemma shows that the closure imposes no old-variable condition.  This proves both directions of (3); (4) is Shannon expansion plus (3).
\end{proof}

This is total after target validation and hereditarily stays in the same language.  It is not hereditary contraction.

\section{ARC/FLMV firewall}
Repeated use obeys
\[
(n_{t+1},m_{t+1})=(n_t+201,m_t+268),                  \tag{5}
\]
so both branches are larger.  In the declared gamma-header, fixed-width identifier encoding, U198 grows from 6,632 to 14,932 bits.  Away from a name-width boundary the constant new records add $O(\log n)$ bits; at a crossover retained identifiers add $\Theta(m)=\Theta(N/\log n)$ bits when $m=\Theta(n)$.  Child length is always $\Theta(N)$, never $N^\lambda$ for fixed $\lambda<1$.

Thus no arbitrary-exponent contraction recurrence or entry into
\[
t<241/200,\qquad s<11642479/23232400
\]
follows.  A succinct accessor still pays source/template/index work and exposes a larger virtual child.  NP-completeness alone does not transfer FLMV's numerical SAT region.
