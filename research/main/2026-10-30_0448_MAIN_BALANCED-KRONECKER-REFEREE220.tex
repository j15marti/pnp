\section{PROPOSE: missing lemma and falsifiers}
The immediate object is the full assignment profile
$\boldsymbol N(a)=(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$, where $N_r$ is the number of
\emph{original} clauses with exactly $r$ true source literals.  LSPV-2
BALANCED-TWO-COORD249 proved that on a sign-balanced ternary source the full
profile is reconstructed from $(N_0,N_3)$ and implemented the corresponding
two-generator Laurent quotient.  The report correctly warned that this does
not automatically reduce sparse support or total SAT work.  Its sentence
that only the two- and four-coordinate representations return the full
histogram is nevertheless falsifiable once arbitrary bounded encodings are
allowed.

This referee tested four mechanisms: (i) exact mixed-radix packing of
$(N_0,N_3)$ into one exponent; (ii) a proof that packing preserves sparse
support rather than contracting it; (iii) a closed count of all algebraically
permitted balanced profiles, distinguished from profiles realized by a
particular source; and (iv) a uniform complete-profile prepass followed by
the same UP--DPLL backend, charging enumeration, decoding, failed work,
witness checking, time and peak allocations.  The representation theorem
succeeds; the speedup interpretation fails.

\section{Original clause extremes before algebra}
For every original clause $C_j$ and signed occurrence $x_{i,j}$, the raw
audit records the local $S_j$ target (all three source literals TRUE), its
inversion $U_j$, the local $U_j$ target (all three FALSE), and its inversion
$S_j$.  These occurrence targets are never assigned independently: every
complete or partial assignment is coherent on the original variable $x_i$.
Ordinary disjunctive satisfaction means $t_j\ge1$, not $t_j=3$.

For every indexed original pair $(C_j,C_k)$ the audit independently
enumerates $SS,SU,US,UU$ over the union of source variables.  It verifies
that $SS,UU$ are feasible exactly when no shared variable has opposite signs,
and $SU,US$ exactly when no shared variable has equal signs.  Indexed triple
targets are checked separately.  The coherent $0^n,1^n$, complements and
one-through-six-variable flip trails retain every $t_j$ and all four
categories.  Every recorded non-tautological source resolvent includes its
falsifying partial cube and exhaustive verification that no completion
satisfies both parents.  None of these diagnostics supplies an answer bit.

\section{The balanced affine plane and its exact lattice-point count}
Call a proper ternary source sign-balanced when each original variable $v$
has $d_v$ positive and $d_v$ negative occurrences.  Then
$\sum_v2d_v=3m$, so $m=2h$ is even.  Every complete assignment makes exactly
$\sum_vd_v=3m/2=3h$ original literal occurrences true.

\begin{theorem}[Exact two-coordinate identities and permitted state count]
For every coherent complete assignment of a sign-balanced proper ternary
source with $m=2h$ clauses,
\[
N_1=h-2N_0+N_3,\qquad N_2=h+N_0-2N_3.                 \tag{1}
\]
Consequently the algebraically permitted pairs $(a,b)=(N_0,N_3)$ are exactly
the integer points
\[
0\le a,b\le h,\qquad 2a-b\le h,\qquad 2b-a\le h,       \tag{2}
\]
of which there are
\[
\boxed{\left\lfloor\frac{h^2}{2}+h+1\right\rfloor}
=\left\lfloor\frac{m^2}{8}+\frac m2+1\right\rfloor.   \tag{3}
\]
This is an algebraic allowance, not a claim that any one source realizes all
of these profiles.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equations $\sum_rN_r=2h$ and $\sum_rrN_r=3h$ give (1) after solving for
$N_1,N_2$.  Their nonnegativity gives (2); conversely (1) reconstructs a
nonnegative quadruple summing to $2h$.  For each $a=0,\ldots,h$, the allowed
$b$ range is
\[
\max(0,2a-h)\le b\le\left\lfloor\frac{h+a}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]
Summing its integer length gives $2r^2+2r+1$ for $h=2r$ and
$2(r+1)^2$ for $h=2r+1$, which is (3).
\end{proof}

For $m=20$, (3) gives 61 permitted profiles.  W1 realizes 24 and SWAP6
realizes 30.  Their different supports reinforce that a universal affine
constraint is not a SAT classifier.  Assignment complementation sends
$(N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)$ to $(N_3,N_2,N_1,N_0)$; coefficient symmetry under that
swap holds for every proper ternary source, not only balanced ones.

\section{One-exponent Kronecker packing}
\begin{theorem}[Bounded full-spectrum univariate encoding]
For the profiles in Theorem 1, put $M=h+1$ and
\[
\kappa(N_0,N_3)=N_0+MN_3.
\]
Then $\kappa$ is injective, with
$N_0=\kappa\bmod M$ and $N_3=\lfloor\kappa/M\rfloor$; equation (1) recovers
$N_1,N_2$.  Thus a univariate monomial $T^{\kappa}$ stores the same complete
profile as $U^{N_0}V^{N_3}$.

For Laurent \emph{differences} $(u,v)\in[-h,h]^2$, the map
\[
\lambda(u,v)=u+(2h+1)v
\]
is injective.  Hence $U^uV^v\mapsto T^{\lambda(u,v)}$ collision-free encodes
every bounded difference profile.  A larger declared range requires a
correspondingly larger radix, which must be constructed, stored and decoded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first statement is ordinary radix-$M$ uniqueness.  If
$u+(2h+1)v=u'+(2h+1)v'$, then
$|u-u'|\le2h$ while any nonzero right-side multiple of $2h+1$ has larger
absolute value.  Thus $v=v'$ and $u=u'$.
\end{proof}

This is the classical Kronecker-substitution idea specialized to the project
object.  It does not contradict the rank-two affine lattice: no fixed
homomorphism from the unbounded lattice $\mathbb Z^2$ to $\mathbb Z$ is
injective.  The packing works because the source supplies a finite bound and
the radix grows with it.  Each packed exponent reaches $\Theta(m^2)$, so its
bit length is $\Theta(\log m)$, the same asymptotic total key length as two
$\Theta(\log m)$ coordinates.  Dense univariate arrays may be worse because
of the enlarged degree.

\begin{corollary}[Sparse-support firewall]
Any collision-free exponent packing is a bijection on the stored support.
It therefore preserves the number of nonzero coefficients.  A direct sparse
product of supports $A,B$ still generates $|A||B|$ candidate term pairs
before combination, provided the radix is chosen collision-free for the full
intermediate sum range.  Coordinate packing alone supplies no asymptotic
reduction in sparse states or pair generation.
\end{corollary}

This corollary independently confirms BALANCED-TWO-COORD249's own finite
observation that its two- and four-coordinate implementations processed the
same 13,868 nonzero term pairs.  The correction is only to exclusivity of the
full-information two-variable representation.  Better cache behavior,
integer arithmetic, dense algorithms or other specialized representations
remain possible and require their own charged analysis.
