The small lifted-PHP obstruction is an \EXACT finite theorem with a 765-case exhaustive certificate; the accompanying implementation is \COMP. \section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
\paragraph{A: direct lifted-CSP to NAE-slice mKW.}
Start with a high-width $4$-ary CSP search problem, apply the de Rezende--Vinyals indexing lift, and map Alice/Bob inputs directly to exact-density SAT/UNSAT NAE formulas so that every target mKW output decodes to a violated base constraint.

\paragraph{B: clique-colouring compiler.}
Use the published reduction from lifted graph pigeonhole to clique--colouring mKW, encode graph $c$-colourability as a SAT/UNSAT NAE formula with one selector bit per potential graph edge, and use equal-size selector pin gadgets to force exact Hamming weight. Falsifier: reverse graph-edge differences create outputs on the complementary pin rail.

\paragraph{C: abstract exact-slice relation encoding.}
Forget NAE semantics temporarily. Characterize exactly when a two-party relation has an output-preserving realization by equal-weight Boolean vectors. Prediction: the condition is a biregular labelled rectangle cover.

\paragraph{D: balanced/symmetric gadget repair.}
If the indexing gadget fails the exact-slice balance condition, search for a symmetric lifting gadget or a balanced restriction of Index whose output rectangles admit the required biregularity while retaining a DAG-like lifting theorem.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
De Rezende and Vinyals prove a nonbinary DAG-like lifting theorem. For a relation $S:\Sigma^n\to Z$, composing with the indexing gadget $\operatorname{Ind}_m$ yields triangle-DAG size at least
\[
\left(\frac{m}{A|\Sigma|\,w(S)\log(mn)}\right)^{w(S)}.
\]
Their Corollary 12 gives the corresponding $m^{\Omega(w)}$ lower bound once $m$ dominates the alphabet, width, and logarithmic factors. They then reduce lifted graph-pigeonhole search to the monotone KW game of clique--colouring.

The reduction is directional. Alice's lifted-index input produces a graph containing a $k$-clique; Bob's table input produces a maximal $c$-colourable graph. Every graph edge present in Alice's graph and absent from Bob's maps to a violated pigeonhole constraint. Edges of the opposite direction are deliberately irrelevant to the source mKW output.

This directionality is the exact feature that conflicts with a naive constant-weight encoding. Wegener's slice-function work already emphasizes that standard monotone lower-bound methods do not automatically survive passage to one fixed Hamming layer. No primary source located supplies a generic output-preserving transformation from an arbitrary lifted relation into a constant-weight monotone KW relation.

Generic low-discrepancy lifting theorems exist for symmetric gadgets such as inner product, but the checked sources lower-bound ordinary communication complexity, not the DAG-like rectangle size needed here to infer monotone circuit size. Candidate D therefore remains a research direction rather than an available theorem.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\subsection{Exact-slice KW embeddings are biregular rectangle covers}
Let $R\subseteq X\times Y\times Z$ be a total search relation. An exact-slice KW realization consists of a coordinate universe $[U]$, a weight $t$, maps
\[
P:X\to\binom{[U]}t,\qquad N:Y\to\binom{[U]}t,
\]
and an output label map $\ell:[U]\to Z$ such that for every $(x,y)$ and every
\[
u\in P(x)\setminus N(y)
\]
one has $\ell(u)\in R(x,y)$. Totality of the target mKW game additionally requires $P(x)\setminus N(y)\ne\varnothing$ for every pair.

For each coordinate $u$, define
\[
L_u=\{x:u\in P(x)\},\qquad
R_u=\{y:u\notin N(y)\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Biregular rectangle characterization]
An exact-slice KW realization is equivalent to a labelled family of rectangles
\[
L_u\times R_u\subseteq R_{\ell(u)}
\]
covering $X\times Y$ and satisfying the exact degree equations
\[
\boxed{\deg_X(x)=t\quad\forall x\in X,}
\qquad
\boxed{\deg_Y(y)=U-t\quad\forall y\in Y.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A target output $u$ occurs exactly when $x\in L_u$ and $y\in R_u$, so output preservation is precisely monochromaticity of each labelled rectangle. Since $P(x)$ has weight $t$, exactly $t$ coordinate rectangles contain $x$ on their left side. Since $N(y)$ has weight $t$, exactly $U-t$ coordinates have target bit zero and therefore contain $y$ on their right side. Target totality is exactly rectangle coverage. The converse reconstructs $P,N$ from the rectangle incidences.
\end{proof}

This theorem separates the lifting issue from NAE semantics. Before one asks whether the bit-vectors are SAT and UNSAT NAE formulas, the lifted relation must already admit this biregular rectangle geometry.

\begin{corollary}[Common padding does not repair imbalance]
Adding a coordinate that is one on both target sides gives the rectangle $X\times\varnothing$. Adding a coordinate that is zero on both sides gives $\varnothing\times Y$. Such common padding preserves the defect
\[
U-\deg_X-\deg_Y.
\]
Hence an unbalanced rectangle encoding cannot be made exact-slice merely by appending common zero or common one coordinates.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Why the natural graph-colouring compiler fails}
A standard graph-colouring CNF can be made uniform in the graph by introducing one selector $s_e$ for every potential graph edge and imposing
\[
s_e\to(\text{endpoints receive different colours}).
\]
The edge status is fixed by a four-clause proper-3CNF pin:
\[
(s_e\vee a\vee b),(s_e\vee a\vee\neg b),
(s_e\vee\neg a\vee b),(s_e\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)
\]
for status one, and the same four clauses with $\neg s_e$ for status zero. The two pin variants have equal size. A standard clause-by-clause reduction to proper NAE-3SAT can preserve this equality and make every other constraint common to the two graph instances.

\begin{lemma}[Local constant-weight reversal]
Let $E(0),E(1)$ be distinct equal-cardinality coordinate sets used to encode the two values of one source bit. Then both
\[
E(1)\setminus E(0)\ne\varnothing,\qquad
E(0)\setminus E(1)\ne\varnothing.
\]
Therefore a directional source relation cannot be preserved by an edge-local output map when its promise contains both $1\to0$ and $0\to1$ changes of that source coordinate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Equal cardinality and inequality of the sets imply that neither can be a strict subset of the other.
\end{proof}

In the de Rezende--Vinyals clique--colouring reduction, valid source outputs are Alice edges absent from Bob. For $m\ge2$ there are valid inputs for which Alice's sparse selected clique has at least one edge missing from Bob and Bob's maximal colourable graph also has edges outside Alice's selected clique. The audit below exhibits a smallest example with one edge of the valid direction and six of the reverse direction. Hence the equal-size selector-pin compiler creates target mKW outputs from both graph directions and is not output-preserving.

This failure is not about the NAE reduction itself. It is already forced by constant weight and directional source outputs.

\subsection{A finite exact obstruction for lifted pigeonhole}
Consider the smallest nontrivial lifted pigeonhole relation
\[
\mathcal R=\operatorname{Search}(\operatorname{PHP}_2^3)\circ\operatorname{Ind}_2^3.
\]
Alice has
\[
x=(x_0,x_1,x_2)\in\{0,1\}^3.
\]
Bob has three binary tables of length two,
\[
y\in\{0,1\}^{3\times2}.
\]
A valid output is a pair $pq\in\{\{0,1\},\{0,2\},\{1,2\}\}$ satisfying
\[
y_{p,x_p}=y_{q,x_q}.
\]

Define
\[
S_X=\{001,010,100,111\}
\]
and the following six Bob inputs, written by concatenating the two entries of tables $0,1,2$:
\[
S_Y=\{000111,001011,001101,100011,101100,110001\}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Finite rectangle inequality]
For every monochromatic rectangle $L\times R$ of $\mathcal R$, labelled by any fixed output pair,
\[
\boxed{2|L\cap S_X|+3|R\cap S_Y|\le8.}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix output pair $pq$ and let
\[
P=\{(x_p,x_q):x\in L\}\subseteq\{0,1\}^2.
\]
For each pair in $P$, exactly one of the two choices of the remaining Alice bit has odd parity, hence
\[
|L\cap S_X|\le|P|.
\]
Every $y\in R$ must satisfy
\[
y_{p,s}=y_{q,t}\qquad\forall(s,t)\in P.
\]
Direct inspection of the six displayed strings, identical for all three choices of $pq$, gives:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
|P|&1&2&3&4\\ \hline
\max |R\cap S_Y|&2&1&0&0.
\end{array}
\]
The four cases give respectively upper bounds $8,7,6,8$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[No nontrivial exact-slice KW realization of $\mathcal R$]
The relation $\operatorname{Search}(\operatorname{PHP}_2^3)\circ\operatorname{Ind}_2^3$ has no output-preserving realization by equal-weight Boolean vectors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume a realization on $U$ coordinates of weight $t$. Apply the rectangle lemma to every target coordinate and sum. By biregularity,
\[
2\sum_{x\in S_X}\deg_X(x)+3\sum_{y\in S_Y}\deg_Y(y)
=
2\cdot4t+3\cdot6(U-t).
\]
The rectangle inequality bounds this by $8U$. Hence
\[
8t+18(U-t)\le8U,
\]
so $t\ge U$. Since always $t\le U$, one has $t=U$. Every target vector is therefore the all-one vector and the target KW relation has no output, contradicting totality.
\end{proof}

This is an abstract obstruction. It applies before requiring target vectors to encode NAE formulas. Therefore no change of NAE gadget can repair this particular lifted relation.

\subsection{What the finite obstruction does and does not prove}
The theorem falsifies a \emph{generic} interface saying that a colourful/index-lifted search relation can always be embedded output-preservingly into an exact Hamming slice. The obstruction already occurs on the smallest lifted pigeonhole instance.

It does not prove that the high-width expander $\operatorname{cPHP}(G)$ relations used in the lifting theorem lack exact-slice realizations. Those relations have different global geometry, and a hard instance cannot contain a separately unsatisfiable constant-size PHP component without collapsing its query width. A new base relation may satisfy the biregularity condition even though the tiny PHP relation does not.

The correct intermediate problem is therefore:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Balanced rectangle design problem.}
Find a high-query-width relation over alphabet four whose lifted communication relation admits a labelled rectangle cover with uniform left degree $t$, uniform right degree $U-t$, and polynomially explicit coordinate labels, while retaining the DAG-like lifting lower bound.
\end{quote}

Only after this balance problem is solved should one impose the additional requirement that the equal-weight vectors be realizable as SAT/UNSAT instances of the NAE critical slice.

\subsection{Possible repairs}
Three repairs remain plausible.

\paragraph{1. A different base relation.}
Search for a high-width $4$-ary CSP search relation whose lifted valid-output rectangles are sufficiently symmetric to admit biregularization. The graph pigeonhole relation is strongly directional and is now a poor default target for the exact slice.

\paragraph{2. A different DAG-like gadget.}
Ordinary query-to-communication lifting is known for low-discrepancy and inner-product gadgets, which are much more symmetric than Index. The checked theorems concern ordinary deterministic/randomized communication complexity, not the rectangle-DAG size characterization required for monotone circuit size. A model-matched DAG-like lifting theorem for a symmetric gadget would be directly relevant.

\paragraph{3. Prove a fixed-weight lifting theorem natively.}
Instead of lifting first and regularizing afterward, formulate a lifting theorem whose communication inputs are themselves constant-weight set systems and whose protocol outputs are directional set differences. This would address the exact-slice geometry at the theorem level rather than through padding.

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
The audit exhaustively checked all
\[
|X||Y|=8\cdot64=512
\]
input pairs for totality. For each of the three output labels and all $255$ nonempty Alice subsets, it computed the maximal compatible Bob side, giving $765$ rectangle checks. The exact inequality
\[
2|L\cap S_X|+3|R\cap S_Y|\le8
\]
held in every case; the maximum was exactly eight and was attained in 72 cases.

A direct graph-reduction control used
\[
x=(0,0,0),\qquad
y=(00,01,10).
\]
Alice's selected graph has three clique edges. Bob's maximal two-colourable graph has six edges not in Alice's graph, while Alice has one edge absent from Bob. Thus both edge directions occur on one legal lifted input pair, confirming the local dual-rail obstruction.
