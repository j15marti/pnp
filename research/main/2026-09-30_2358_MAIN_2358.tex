\section*{PROVED/EXACT}
Let
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2,\qquad C_H=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{1,g_1,\ldots,g_m\}.
\]
Then
\[
\rho(E_H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}(C_H^{\star2})
=\dim\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_i g_j\}.
\]
If \(K_2(H)=\ker E_H\ne0\), Singleton yields a nonzero quadratic annihilator with ANF support at most \(\rho(E_H)+1\). Every nonzero Boolean quadratic has density at least \(1/4\), and the verified constant-density retuning amplifies fixed positive density with constant asymptotic overhead.

For finite accepted set \(\mathcal A\) and exact extensional map \(L:\mathcal A\to\mathcal F\), let
\[
n_v=|L^{-1}(v)|,\qquad \operatorname{Coll}(L)=\sum_v n_v^2,
\]
and
\[
h_2(L)=2\log_2|\mathcal A|-\log_2\operatorname{Coll}(L).
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\boxed{|\operatorname{im}L|\ge \frac{|\mathcal A|^2}{\operatorname{Coll}(L)}=2^{h_2(L)}}.
\]
Thus the existing extractor bad-rank counting lemma implies
\[
\boxed{h_2(L)>\kappa+M}\Longrightarrow
\text{a reachable source object has }\rho=M.
\]
Decorative ignored proof padding multiplies \(|\mathcal A|\) by \(2^t\) and \(\operatorname{Coll}\) by \(2^{2t}\), leaving \(h_2\) unchanged.

The maximum-fiber statistic
\[
h_{\rm eff}=\log_2|\mathcal A|-\log_2\beta_{\mathcal A}(L)
\]
also remains valid, and \(\operatorname{Coll}(L)\le\beta|\mathcal A|\) shows the collision lower bound on image size is at least as strong as the maximum-fiber bound when both are known. Perfect completeness alone does not imply useful accepted entropy: its existential quantifier is compatible with a singleton accepted set.

The proof-length ceiling gives
\[
h_2\le\log_2|\mathcal A|\le L,
\]
so the counting attack requires \(L>\kappa+M\). Under the checked scaling \(L=T\operatorname{polylog}T\), \(m=T^\eta\), \(\kappa=m^{500}r^5\) with fixed-\(\eta\) \(r=O_\eta(1)\), the ceiling rules the branch out asymptotically for \(\eta>1/500\), while \(0<\eta<1/500\) passes this ceiling alone.

\section*{PQ structural toolkit and transfer status}
\paragraph{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER.}
The operation \(C_H^{\star2}\) is literally the Schur square of a binary linear code. Shortening, puncturing, dualization, Schur powers, and the kernel of the degree-two multiplication/evaluation map transfer exactly when their hypotheses hold for the actual reachable code. The latter kernel is exactly the quadratic-relation space. The collision identity above is exact for every finite map.

\paragraph{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE.}
Modern code cryptanalysis shows that shortening plus Hilbert functions, syzygies, and graded Betti numbers can expose algebraic structure even when raw square-code dimension ceases to distinguish. Randriambololona's syzygy distinguisher is an explicit example in the explicit-code model. These invariants are candidates for diagnosing the reachable SU/vMS code, not runtime lower bounds. A saturated raw Schur square therefore retires only quadratic annihilation, not all algebraic structure.

Low-rank matrices in the quadratic-relation space are likewise candidates. The hostile question is algorithmic: if a low-rank relation, filtration, or syzygy is visible, can it be exploited to acquire \(Q\) or reconstruct hidden structure efficiently from the succinct SU/vMS encoder? In PQ cryptanalysis, hidden structure is often a weakness.

An LWE-style architecture suggests replacing forced internal materialization by external extraction from black-box solver behavior. This is only a candidate architecture. Bogdanov--Trevisan rule out broad nonadaptive NP worst-case-to-average-case self-correction unless PH collapses, while Trevisan--Vadhan establish limitations of black-box uniform reductions. Hirahara's later non-black-box work shows that escaping black-box barriers is possible in specialized meta-complexity settings, but supplies no SAT reduction needed here.

Special-soundness/collision extraction is the highest-value reconstruction analogy: seek a theorem that two conflicting valid openings for one fixed vMS commitment yield an explicit locally checkable forbidden algebraic witness. Published resilient partial-single-valuedness is probabilistic over commitments and does not itself provide this pointwise identity.

\paragraph{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Cryptographic hardness, collapse-binding security, random-oracle assumptions, or LWE hardness cannot enter an unconditional P-versus-NP proof. Explicit-code PQ cryptanalysis runtime does not transfer to the succinct SU/vMS representation without a new algorithmic theorem. VLSI area--time, distributed LOCAL/communication cuts, control dissipativity, optimization rank/integrality-gap bounds, proof-system interpolation, and topological obstruction do not imply unrestricted random-access SAT runtime absent a model-preserving all-algorithm transfer theorem.
