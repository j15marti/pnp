\section{Claim card, quantifiers and four falsifiers}
\paragraph{Single target.}
Audit whether a universal adaptive residual-SAT controller can obtain both a
time-competitive and a simultaneous-space-competitive exact policy schedule
without receiving a best-policy oracle, an unknown exponent, or a
cap-respecting complete SAT solver for free.

\paragraph{Claim card.}
For every ordinary CNF source $F$ and every finite public sequence of
deterministic partial policies $P_1,\ldots,P_r$, one fixed deterministic host
runs trials serially from the same indexed read-only source.  Each trial is
given a declared time budget and writable-space cap, and may return only
\texttt{UNRESOLVED} or a SAT/UNSAT certificate checked against the original
source.  Source/index heads, query and child buffers, certificates, verifier
space, policy history, retained state and reset/replay are charged on the same
execution.  SAME\_REALIZATION is the one host trace on $F$; no counterfactual
winner state is substituted for the actual peak.

\begin{description}
\item[F1: maximum versus sum.] Serial rollback must retain the sum of all
trial workspaces.  \textbf{FALSIFIED} under the stated isolation contract:
peak trial space is a maximum, although time is a sum.  A concurrent race is
different and may retain a sum of live states.
\item[F2: winner-space transfer.] A time-competitive restart schedule has the
space of its eventual winner.  \textbf{FALSIFIED}, independently reproducing
LSPV-3's exact counterexample: an earlier silent trial may fill
$\Omega(N)$ cells before a later $O(N)$-time, $O(\log N)$-space certificate
policy wins.
\item[F3: cap implies totality.] Imposing an $N^\sigma$ cap on every
speculative policy gives a total $N^\sigma$-space SAT decider.
\textbf{FALSIFIED}: if no capped policy finishes on an input, totality requires
a different capped complete policy or an uncapped fallback, and the latter
restores its own peak-space exposure.
\item[F4: rollback is free.] Trial termination removes all time costs of
reset, replay or workspace isolation.  \textbf{FALSIFIED AS AN ACCOUNTING
RULE}: the exact ledger has an explicit reset term.  Physical scrubbing can
cost the number of written cells; lazy epochs, indexed jumps or fresh logical
names are alternative implementations whose metadata, address and high-water
space must instead be charged.  No universal reset lower bound is claimed.
\end{description}

\section{Exact serial transaction theorem}
Let the immutable source and its read-only input tape be shared by all trials.
Let $P$ be the maximum persistent writable controller/source-index/history
state.  For realized trial $i$, let $t_i$ be its policy work, $r_i$ its
rollback/reset/reinitialization work, $v_i$ its certificate-verification work,
$b_i$ its maximum live writable policy/query/child state, and $c_i$ its maximum
additional live certificate/verifier/output state.  These quantities include
all relevant work and index tapes and are measured on the same host trace.

\begin{theorem}[Serial certified transaction ledger]\label{thm:ledger}
Suppose trials execute one at a time; after a failed or rejected trial, all of
its source-dependent scratch is made unreachable to later semantics; only the
declared persistent state survives; and a successful answer is committed only
after an original-source verifier accepts it.  Then the realized controller is
sound and its charged resources obey
\[
 T_{\rm host}\le T_{\rm setup}+\sum_i(t_i+r_i+v_i)+T_{\rm commit},
\]
\[
 S_{\rm host}\le P+\max_i(b_i+c_i)+S_{\rm commit}.
\]
If a complete exact policy is eventually scheduled, the controller is total.
The theorem supplies neither a cheap policy nor a cap-respecting complete
policy.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Certificate verification against the immutable source makes every committed
answer sound; \texttt{UNRESOLVED}, timeout and rejected output commit no label.
Serial execution partitions the realized transition trace into setup, trial,
reset/verifier and commit intervals, so their transition counts add.  At every
instant, writable state consists of the persistent controller plus the one
current trial/verifier and commit state.  Therefore its high-water mark is the
maximum, not the sum, of the live trial quantities.  Semantic rollback may be
implemented by clearing, overwriting, abandoning or versioning scratch, but
the chosen implementation's transition, metadata, address and visited-cell
costs are already represented in $r_i,b_i,c_i,P$.  If a complete policy is
eventually reached, soundness plus termination gives total exactness.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Cap-completeness gate]\label{cor:cap}
Fix a cap $B(N)$.  Restricting every speculative trial to $b_i+c_i\le B(N)$
proves a $P+B+S_{\rm commit}$ peak bound only for executions whose committed
answer is also obtained within that cap.  To derive an all-input total
$B(N)$-space SAT decider, one must prove
\[
 \forall F\;\exists\text{ realized cap-respecting checked terminal policy},
\]
or supply a separately proved cap-respecting complete fallback.  If on some
$F$ the controller instead invokes an uncapped complete fallback $A(F)$, the
valid bound is
\[
 S_{\rm host}(F)\le P+\max\{B(N),S_A(N),S_{\rm adapter}(N)\},
\]
not $P+B(N)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:ledger} to all capped trials.  If none commits, a total
controller must continue.  Any uncapped continuation is part of the same
execution and its actual live space enters the maximum.  Omitting it would
replace the realized trace by a counterfactual terminating capped trace.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Relation to the ARC recurrence.}
The proposed conditional space recurrence
\[
 L'\le\max\{1/2,L/2\}
\]
therefore needs an all-input $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ cap-completeness theorem, including
guest work/index tapes, physical child buffers, output certificates, verifier,
source indices and retained ancestors.  The time recurrence must separately
include every failed trial and $r_i$.  LSPV-4's current simulation correctly
warns that a physical guest-workspace reset may add a term depending on
$S_A(M)$ to time.  This report does not prove that every reset has that cost;
it proves only that the chosen cost cannot be deleted from the ledger.

\section{Affine theorem audit and nonlinear controls}
The AFFINE-SMALLBIAS-DESCENT-GATE2 proof survives this audit at exactly its
restricted scope.  For source-independent affine maps $R_Z(x)=A_Zx\oplus b_Z$
with the source $x$ fixed before independent seed $Z$, the corrected target-set
count is
\[
 \log_2 B_N=O((u_N+1)\log(m+u_N+2)).
\]
The source YES distribution has circuit size below the source threshold, and
the small-bias fiber argument compares $R_Z(D_N)$ with $R_Z(U_N)$; it does not
assume surjectivity or uniform target output.  The quantifier and set-size
steps are consistent.  The theorem does \emph{not} apply to source-dependent
or nonlinear maps.  The prior bent-quadratic control already shows that small
linear bias alone does not fool every quadratic output.  Consequently this
restricted MCSP semantic no-go cannot be promoted to arbitrary SAT runtime or
to cap completeness.

\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
FLMV Section 2.1 and Theorem 5.7 provide the \textbf{direct exact theorem
transfer}: the final contradiction must use one total deterministic SAT
machine with simultaneous time and writable-space bounds in their indexed
read-only-input model~\cite{flmv}.  Levin's universal sequential search is

paper supplies this project's deterministic both-label certificate semantics,
ordinary SAT child contraction, or simultaneous FLMV space bound.  Their use

Operating-system or database transaction rollback is likewise only an
\textbf{analogy/candidate}: no physical-memory or recovery theorem transfers
to the FLMV work/index-tape model.  The exact usable statement is instead the
elementary same-model ledger proved above.

\section{Barrier audits and the single next arrow}
The ledger relativizes and algebrizes: semantic or algebraic oracles may make a
terminal policy cheap, which reinforces that no unrestricted lower bound is
proved.  No constructive circuit property is used, so Natural Proofs supplies
no promotion.  DELAY/COPY/RECOMP wrappers remain valid controls on guest and
reset accounting.  Horn, Krom, affine, renaming, padding and duplicate-support
controls remain easy under their native representations.

\textbf{Single weakest OPEN arrow.}
Construct or falsify a uniform, source-computable, all-input partition for
which every ordinary residual has a checked terminal or a genuine physical
$N^{1/2+o(1)}$ ordinary-SAT child, and prove that one fixed serial controller
finds it within the strict ARC time budget while every realized trial,
certificate, guest/index tape, reset and retained ancestor obeys the strict
simultaneous FLMV space cap.  The cap-completeness theorem is a necessary
accounting condition, not the missing construction.  P versus NP remains
\textbf{OPEN}.
