\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\paragraph{Authenticated physical source.}
Let $M=2^n$, with $n\le N$ for source length $N$. Authenticated bytes explicitly encode $Q$ modes, odd slopes $u_1,u_2$, offsets $w_1,w_2$, even query slopes $v_c$, offsets $d_c$, initial compact tuple $s_0=(c_0,b_{1,0},b_{2,0})$, and a complete table
\[
G:[Q]\times\{0,1\}^2\times\{0,1\}\longrightarrow[Q]\times\{0,1\}^2.
\]
At step $t$, positions are $a_i(t)=u_it+w_i\pmod M$ and mode-$c$ query is $q_c(t)=v_ct+d_c\pmod M$. An actual physical RAM tape $X_t$ is initialized from blank in at most two XOR writes. Source reads $r_t=X_t(q_{c_t}(t))$, updates $(c,b_1,b_2)$ by $G$, XOR-erases the two old tokens and XOR-writes the two new tokens at positions for $t+1$. Thus there is one physical point read and at most four XOR cell accesses, plus explicit $n$-bit address arithmetic, per RAM macrostep. Coincident positions use XOR parity. These are not free sequential-head Turing movements; a separate sequential bit machine verifies the certificate.
\begin{lemma}[All-invariant-state source consecution]
For every $t$, every controller tuple and every tape satisfying
\[
I_t(X,b):\quad X(a)=b_1[a=a_1(t)]\oplus b_2[a=a_2(t)]\quad\text{for all }a\in[M],
\]
the actual physical successor satisfies $I_{t+1}$ and
$r_t=b_1[q_{c_t}(t)=a_1(t)]\oplus b_2[q_{c_t}(t)=a_2(t)]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}The point read is exact on each invariant fiber. XOR removal cancels both old contributions, and XOR insertion supplies both new contributions, including overlaps. This applies to every invariant tape, not all arbitrary tapes. It proves syntactic Hamming-support at most two, not sparse coverage of outputs of arbitrary programs.\end{proof}
\begin{lemma}[Source-derived collision residues]
Every mode/token pair $(c,i)$ has exactly one match residue
\[
e_{c,i}=(u_i-v_c)^{-1}(d_c-w_i)\pmod M.
\]
The union $E$ has at most $J=\min(M,2Q)$ residues, and every mode reads zero off $E$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Odd minus even is odd, hence invertible modulo $2^n$. Take the union over ALL possible adaptive modes. Simultaneous matches contribute the XOR of both bits.\end{proof}
Let $K=4Q$. Define the background finite transition $H(s)=G(s,0)$ for off-event times and event map $V_e(s)=G(s,b_1[e=e_{c,1}]\oplus b_2[e=e_{c,2}])$. Repeated squaring of $H$ and composition at the sorted $E$ residues construct a full-period map $R:[K]\to[K]$ and a prefix map $P_r$ for any $r<M$. Because all physical address schedules repeat after $M$ steps, if $T=kM+r$ then
\[
s_T=P_r(R^k(s_0)),\qquad
X_T(q)=b_{1,T}[q=a_1(T)]\oplus b_{2,T}[q=a_2(T)].
\]
