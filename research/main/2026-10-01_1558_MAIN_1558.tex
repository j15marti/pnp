\section*{PROVED/EXACT}
For the declared source-sanctioned Hadamard realization,
\[
K_2(H)=\ker\Psi,\qquad \rho(H)=\operatorname{rank}\Psi,\qquad M=1+m+\binom m2.
\]
Thus \(\rho(H)=O(m)\) requires \(\dim K_2(H)=M-O(m)=\Theta(m^2)\).

Let \(S_v=\operatorname{im}X_v\le V=\mathbb F_2^m\). PAIR2COVER1 proves: if independent directions \(t_1,\dots,t_r\) each occur in legal slices and \(E\) is the set of pairs co-contained in some legal slice, then
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)\ge 1+r+|E|}.
\]
A spanning \(m\)-set whose every pair legally co-occurs gives \(K_2(H)=0\) and \(\rho(H)=M\). Thus \(r+|E|=\omega(m)\) already refutes a claimed global \(O(m)\) rank bound.

WEDGE1 defines
\[
W=\operatorname{span}_vS_v,\qquad U=\operatorname{span}_v\bigwedge\nolimits^2S_v.
\]
All common polar parts lie in \(U^\perp\). Hence
\[
\boxed{U=\bigwedge\nolimits^2V\Longrightarrow K_2(H)=W^\perp},
\]
and
\[
\boxed{W=V,\quad U=\bigwedge\nolimits^2V\Longrightarrow K_2(H)=0,\quad \rho(H)=M}.
\]
Given explicit legal slice bases, these spaces and candidate relations are computable by Gaussian elimination. This is exploitable structure, not hardness.

HADSQ1 remains exact:
\[
d_2(c_{\rm Had})=1+k+\binom k2,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}B_v=1+r_v+\binom{r_v}{2}.
\]
Singleton sparsity, Boolean-quadratic density at least \(1/4\), and constant-density reconstruction retuning remain exact under their stated hypotheses.

NOGUARD1 defines

L_{\rm NO}=\{\langle x,Q,r,u,z,j\rangle:D_j(x,Q,r,u,z)\in UNSAT\}.

If \(L_{\rm NO}\) has polynomial existential certificates and is coNP-hard under polynomial many-one reductions, then \(NP=coNP\). CoNP-hardness of the actual vMS NO family is OPEN. The trivial counterfactual evaluator remains \(U_A=1-A\circ D_j\).

REACQ1 proves that if a deterministic polynomial-time selector \(S\) produces \(y=S(x)\), then with static readable \(x\), discarding \(y\) does not force superpolynomial reacquisition: rerun \(S(x)\). Polynomial coordinates of a deterministic polynomial-time trace can likewise be regenerated in polynomial time. RCCN STORE-or-REACQUIRE remains exact in its own model, but necessity does not imply superpolynomial cost.
