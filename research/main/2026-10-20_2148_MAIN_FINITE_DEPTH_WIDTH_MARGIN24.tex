\section*{Exact finite-depth heredity lemma}
Let $c=1/20$. Suppose a recursive transformation has idealized scales
\[
L_j=N^{2^{-j}}\quad(0\leq j\leq r)
\]
and every descendant frontier has width at most the root width $w$.
Then all levels through $r$ satisfy the scoped eligibility condition
$w\leq c\log_2 L_j$ whenever
\[
w\leq c\,2^{-r}\log_2 N.
\]
This margin is sharp under width persistence.

\paragraph{Proof.}
For $j\leq r$,
$c\log_2 L_j=c\,2^{-j}\log_2N\geq
c\,2^{-r}\log_2N\geq w$. Conversely, if a legal family keeps width
$w$ at every boundary, then eligibility at level $r$ forces
$w\leq c\,2^{-r}\log_2N$. \hfill$\Box$

The $o(1)$ child-length terms require a corresponding asymptotic slack, e.g.
$w\leq(c\,2^{-r}-\epsilon)\log_2N$ for fixed $r,\epsilon>0$.

\paragraph{First-step query ledger.}
With the depth-$r$ margin, the peer enumeration has at most
\[
N^{1/2+o(1)}2^{2w}
 \leq N^{\,1/2+1/(10\cdot 2^r)+o(1)}.
\]
For every $r\geq1$ this is at most $N^{11/20+o(1)}$, hence below the
previous safe $N^{3/5+o(1)}$ envelope. The margin repairs finite-depth
eligibility without worsening the first-step exponent.

\section*{Explicit one-step counterexample}
For any integer $k\geq1$, take $N=2^{40k}$ and $w=2k$. Open
$x_1,\ldots,x_w$. In every block include the proper cyclic clauses
\[
(x_i\vee x_{i+1}\vee x_{i+2})\qquad(i\bmod w),
\]
repeating or padding blocks as needed, and close variables only after the last
block. Every variable occurs in every block, so each internal frontier has
exactly width $w$. The root satisfies
\[
w=2k=(\log_2N)/20.
\]
A square-root child has scale $L=N^{1/2}=2^{20k}$ and threshold
$(\log_2L)/20=k<w$. Therefore the published root promise is not hereditary
even once. This is a counterexample to automatic promise closure, not to the
semantic join theorem.

\section*{Adversarial challenge}
The sufficient lemma is conditional on descendant width not increasing.
Pinned child encodings, locator metadata, and serialization can introduce new
live variables. Thus ARC-INTERFACE-PROJECTION8 has not yet proved even the
narrowed class successor-closed. More importantly, an all-input streaming
normalizer into any such logarithmic-width class would combine with direct
frontier dynamic programming to produce a forbidden FLMV-region SAT algorithm
unless the normalizer itself pays the missing time/space. This is a barrier
derived from FLMV, not a separation.

The MCSP cross-check points the same way: SOURCE-CONJUGACY8 shows that a
nonlinear-looking transition may be efficiently conjugate to affine dynamics.
Any new $R_k$ must establish high ordinary KT after accounting for the source,
keys, conjugacy maps, conditions, output materialization, and random error.
No current artifact does so.
