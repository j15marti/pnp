\section{Exact degree boundary}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT from Brooks]
A finite simple graph \(G\) of maximum degree at most three is 3-colorable
iff no connected component is \(K_4\). Decision costs \(O(|V|+|E|)\) word
operations and \(O(|V|+|E|)\) peak words after charged parsing.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Brooks' theorem says a connected graph of maximum degree \(\Delta\) is
\(\Delta\)-colorable unless complete or an odd cycle. Components of maximum
degree at most two are 3-colorable, including odd cycles and \(K_3\). For
maximum degree three, the only complete exception requiring four colors is
\(K_4\). Conversely \(K_4\) is not 3-colorable. A component/degree scan and
the four-vertices/six-edges test find exactly these components in linear time.
\end{proof}
\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM.} Garey--Johnson--Stockmeyer prove that graph
3-colorability remains NP-complete for maximum degree four. This is an exact
tractable/NP-complete boundary, not a deterministic lower bound.

\section{Growing same-source acquisition family}
Use two header words and \(E\) edge words, each packing its endpoints into
\[
 W=2\lceil\log_2(V+1)\rceil=\Theta(\log N)
\]
bits. Records may occur in arbitrary order but are distinct and loop-free, so
\(N=(E+2)W\). Every validity check and source read is charged.

For block \(i\), use vertices \(a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i,e_i,f_i\). Five fixed records
are the edges of \(K_4\) on \(a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i\) other than \(a_ib_i\).
The special record is \(e_if_i\) when \(\beta_i=0\), and \(a_ib_i\) when
\(\beta_i=1\). Let \(G_p(\beta)\) be the disjoint union of \(p\) blocks.

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT input-query lower bound]
Every \(G_p(\beta)\) has \(V=6p,E=6p\), identical headers, and maximum degree
three, and
\[
 G_p(\beta)\text{ is 3-colorable}\iff\beta=0^p.
\]
On \(G_p(0^p)\), every deterministic exact decider of \(L_4\) must inspect
all \(p\) special packed edge words and at least one bit from each changed-word
support. Hence
\[
 D_{\rm record}=D_{\rm word}=p,\qquad D_{\rm bit}\ge p,\qquad
 p=\Theta(N/\log N).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A zero block is \(K_4-a_ib_i\) plus \(e_if_i\), hence 3-colorable. A one
block is \(K_4\) plus two isolated vertices, hence not 3-colorable. Both have
maximum degree three and differ in exactly one packed word. If the execution
on \(0^p\) omits word \(i\), changing that word leaves its deterministic
transcript unchanged but reverses the label. Changed-bit supports for
different blocks are disjoint. Finally
\(N=(6p+2)2\lceil\log_2(6p+1)\rceil\).
\end{proof}
The bound applies directly to every decider of the fixed NP-complete language
\(L_4\); both endpoints are already valid sources. It is still only
input-acquisition order. The degree-three scan decides the family in \(O(p)\)
word operations, with \(O(1)\) extra words for a recognized template and
linear workspace for a general adjacency structure.
\section{Compatible ordinary-CNF representation}
The parsimonious one-hot encoding gives each graph vertex three Boolean
variables. It has exactly
\[
 n_{\rm B}=3V,\qquad k=V,\qquad m_2=3V+3E,\qquad m=4V+3E,
\]
where the \(V\) genuine ternaries require at least one color, the \(3V\)
binary clauses enforce at most one color, and the \(3E\) binary clauses forbid
equal endpoint colors. Every graph coloring has exactly one Boolean model
and conversely. For \(G_p\), these become
\[
 n_{\rm B}=18p,\quad k=6p,\quad m_2=36p,\quad m=42p.
\]
Writing this CNF costs \(\Theta(p)\) records. Thus paid transport only yields
\[
 T_{\rm CNF}^{\le}(g(p))
 \ge \frac{L_{\rm graph}(p)-R(p)-H(p)}{Q(p)\tau(p)},
\]
and \(L_{\rm graph}(p)=p\) may be consumed by \(R(p)=\Theta(p)\).
Formula-evaluation gates, wires, or switches are not SAT-solving lower bounds.
