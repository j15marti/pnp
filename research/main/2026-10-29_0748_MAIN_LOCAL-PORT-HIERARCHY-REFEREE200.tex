\begin{abstract}
We independently audit the Library Connected Interface archive and two new
LSPV-3 port constructions.  The supplied programs reproduce their finite
claims: the one-designated-port permutation grammar has an exact $2$CNF
projection, while the same fixed 15-variable source has a three-port
projection equal to a genuine ternary clause.  These findings are compatible,
because their retained interfaces differ.  We prove a corrected size lemma:
any bounded-fanin hierarchy that physically retains one nonempty block per
input leaf and per merge has linearly many blocks, even at logarithmic depth,
so it cannot be an $N^\lambda$ child for canonical
$N=\Theta(m\log m)$ and fixed $\lambda<1$.  The lemma does not cover a
compiler that computes and discards exact child summaries; for an arbitrary
zero-port module, that acquisition is already the SAT decision.  The fixed
chain in TERNARY-CLAUSE-PORT162 legitimately escapes this floor by belonging
to a recognizable easy grammar.  Hence exact clause ports and polynomial
primitive-positive definitions are constructive ingredients, but arbitrary
coverage, summary acquisition, recursive physical closure, and strict
contraction remain \OPEN.  No SAT runtime lower bound or general shrinker is
obtained.  P versus NP remains OPEN.
\end{abstract}

\section{PROPOSE: missing lemma and four falsifiable mechanisms}
The load-bearing question is whether PORT-QUANTIFIER-CLOSURE161 and
TERNARY-CLAUSE-PORT162 can be repeated to yield one uniformly constructible,
answer-preserving child of bit length at most $N^\lambda$ on arbitrary SAT.
Before search and build we fixed four falsifiers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Finite-template noncoverage.}  The 81 tables may compile only
the fixed 15-variable one-port grammar, not arbitrary exact22 formulas.
\item \textbf{Interface-arity escape.}  A three-port cofactor may leave the
bijunctive class, invalidating an automatic extension of the one-port $2$CNF
compiler.
\item \textbf{Depth/size confusion.}  A balanced bounded-fanin hierarchy may
have logarithmic depth but still linearly many materialized blocks.
\item \textbf{Hidden acquisition.}  A history-free root summary may be small
only because computing it has already decided the source, as on the fixed
easy chain or through an uncharged semantic oracle.
\end{enumerate}
Mechanisms 1--3 are now proved for the exact scopes below.  Mechanism 4 is an
exact zero-boundary observation and identifies the open constructive burden;
it is not an algorithm-independent hardness theorem.

\section{Independent review of the supplied exact statements}
\subsection{Connected Interface archive}
For a connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF under the stated
static partition, each nonempty private module touches the nonempty global
boundary $Y$, and each boundary variable has four clause occurrences.  Thus
$k\le4b$.  If each private module has a syntactic clause-hitting set $B_i$ of
size at most $q$, then $H=Y\cup\bigcup_iB_i$ hits every clause.  Exact degree
four gives $m=4n/3$, while a variable hits at most four clauses, so
\[
 n\le3|H|\le3b+12bq.
\]
Consequently $b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$ for $b>0$; for $b=0$, connectedness
forces one module and $q\ge n/3$.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for the
declared static hypothesis and is not a runtime lower bound.  The original
program reproduced 4,680 boundary checks, 7,191 local-discovery checks and
139 brute-force comparisons.

\subsection{One-port closure161}
The fixed source has 15 variables and 20 clauses.  Its four occurrences of
the designated $x_4$ port are permuted among $k$ labelled copies, preserving
signed degree.  Conditional on the fully enumerated 81 finite source
profiles, existentially eliminating private variables factors by module and
produces at most three unary/binary clauses per module.  Variable
identification preserves width two.  The resulting $2$CNF has $k$ variables
and at most $3k$ clauses and is exact on every port assignment.

This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for the named generated grammar, with the
finite profile lemma \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL} rather than kernel-certified.
The rerun checked all 81 templates and 700 deterministic signed-token
permutations (171 SAT, 529 UNSAT); independent unprojected MILP controls agree
on one SAT and one UNSAT case.  Its canonical source and child lengths are
both $\Theta(k\log(k+2))$, so it is not a fixed-power contraction.

\subsection{Three-port clause162}
The exact source projection on $(x_1,x_2,x_{13})$ is
\[
 R(a,b,c)=\neg a\lor\neg b\lor c.
\]
It is not bijunctive: $(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(1,1,1)\in R$, while their coordinate
majority $(1,1,0)\notin R$.  Satisfying assignments of every $2$CNF are
closed under coordinate majority, and existential projection preserves that
closure by taking the majority of three complete witnesses.  Hence no
existential $2$CNF represents $R$.  The report's arity-two/arity-three
threshold is therefore \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} under the finite source
cofactor identity.

The linked chain $E_k$ retains this root relation for all $k\ge2$, while
remaining connected strict signed exact22.  Reproduction checked all 455
three-variable source projections, the 60 non-bijunctive ones, all 104 full
MILP cofactors for chain lengths 2--5, and an 18-derived-step Resolution
refutation for the excluded tuple; a separate checker rejected two mutations.
These controls are \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}.  The unpinned chain is always SAT
and its fixed grammar is easy.  Its constant clause summary is a genuine
restricted shrink, not arbitrary-instance coverage.

\section{BUILD: corrected hierarchy theorem and acquisition firewall}
\begin{theorem}[Bounded-fanin materialized hierarchy floor]
Let a final encoded child contain a rooted hierarchy with $m\ge2$ nonempty
leaf blocks, one for each source unit, and $I$ nonempty merge blocks.  Suppose
every merge has at most $r\ge2$ children, every leaf reaches the root, and all
leaf and merge blocks remain physically present in the child.  Then
\[
 I\ge\left\lceil\frac{m-1}{r-1}\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \#\text{blocks}\ge m+\left\lceil\frac{m-1}{r-1}\right\rceil=\Omega(m).
\]
If the canonical source length is $N=\Theta(m\log(m+2))$ and every nonempty
block costs at least one bit, this architecture cannot have output length
$O(N^\lambda)$ for any fixed $0<\lambda<1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The rooted tree has $m+I-1$ child-to-parent edges.  The sum of the internal
outdegrees is at most $rI$, so $m+I-1\le rI$.  This yields the first bound.
For fixed $\lambda<1$,
\[
 \frac{m}{(m\log(m+2))^\lambda}
 =\frac{m^{1-\lambda}}{\log^\lambda(m+2)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Thus even the one-bit-per-block floor eventually exceeds every constant
multiple of $N^\lambda$.
\end{proof}

The theorem is deliberately architectural.  It does not apply when a
compiler computes a parent relation and discards the children, nor when a
single succinct object shares or implicitly generates many logical leaves.
Those are precisely the constructive alternatives that must be charged.

\begin{lemma}[Zero-boundary acquisition is decision]
For an arbitrary Boolean formula $F$, its complete existential summary on
the empty boundary is $\{()\}$ if $F$ is satisfiable and $\varnothing$
otherwise.  Therefore a uniform deterministic algorithm that constructs this
summary for arbitrary $F$ already decides SAT, before any recursive call.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There is one assignment to the empty tuple.  It belongs to the projection
exactly when it extends to a satisfying assignment of $F$.
\end{proof}

This lemma does not forbid a polynomial algorithm; such an algorithm would
prove $P=NP$.  It prevents accounting the history-free root as a free local
operation.  At a nonempty boundary the analogous burden is to compute the
complete extendibility relation, including both SAT and UNSAT fibers.

\subsection{Compact elimination is linear, not a power shrink}
For
\[
 F=R\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^{t}(z\lor A_i)
       \land\bigwedge_{j=1}^{t}(\neg z\lor B_j),
\quad A_i=(a_i\lor b_i),\ B_j=(c_j\lor d_j),
\]
with disjoint surviving variables,
\[
 \exists z\,F=R\land\bigwedge_{i,j}(A_i\lor B_j)
 =R\land\left(\bigwedge_iA_i\ \lor\ \bigwedge_jB_j\right).
\]
The explicit resolvent CNF has $t^2$ width-four clauses, while the displayed
fan-in-two circuit has $4t-1$ gates when the $A_i,B_j$ OR gates are counted.
This refutes inherent quadratic expansion but remains linear in the input
family.  The new standalone control exhaustively verifies all
$16+256+4096+65536=69{,}904$ surviving assignments for $t=1,2,3,4$.

The same program verifies the ternary majority witness and exact hierarchy
counts through $m=2^{20}$ for fanin 2, 3, and 4.  It records the edge-count
floor separately from a level-batching construction, avoiding a false claim
that naive batching is always optimal.

\section{Constructive and separation consequences}
\paragraph{Constructive track.}
Clause-port162 shows that a fixed strict exact22 source can expose a complete
ternary clause relation; compiler224 supplies pointwise entry for ordinary
3CNF; and report198 supplies equality fanout inside exact22.  Together these
are meaningful transport ingredients.  Yet every presently authenticated
physical composition is additive, and closure161's exact projection is only
same-order.  The next constructive lemma must compute a complete joint
summary for arbitrary interacting modules, discard enough history to obtain
a fixed-power child, re-enter a closed grammar, and charge source discovery,
all failed choices, intermediate storage, output, reconstruction, and every
recursive call.

\paragraph{Separation track.}
The Connected Interface bound falsifies universal static logarithmic
boundary/backdoor coverage, and the ternary clause falsifies universal
bijunctive closure at three retained ports.  Neither is an algorithm-
independent SAT lower bound.  Semantic preprocessing, moving separators,
circuits, affine methods, caching, and RAM representations remain legal
escapes.  No tape crossing statement is imported to RAM or unrestricted SAT.

\paragraph{SAT/UNSAT sides and one-program uniformity.}
Closure161's one fixed compiler handles both sides and the rerun includes 171
SAT and 529 UNSAT permutations.  Clause-port162's unpinned chain is SAT; its
UNSAT control fixes the excluded root tuple externally and does not become a
separate solver with a free side bit.  This run establishes no side-specific
worst-case $T_\pm,S_\pm,Q_\pm$, and no independent maxima are combined into a
same-input tradeoff.

\paragraph{Machine ledgers.}
The new hierarchy theorem is an explicit-output length statement, not a time
lower bound.  The finite scripts ran as ordinary indexed-host Python.  We
claim no measured single-tape, two-tape, or fixed-universal-machine exponent.
The loose polynomial simulations quoted in reports161--162 remain upper
constructions on their recognized grammars.  Writable-input, multihead, RAM,
and universal-machine conventions are not conflated.
