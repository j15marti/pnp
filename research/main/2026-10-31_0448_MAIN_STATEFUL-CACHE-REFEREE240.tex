\section{Exact one-hot bit-query theorem}
For even $m$, let $S_h$ be the fixed-width serialized LSPV-4 source with one
marked clause at $h\in[m]$, with label $L(h)=h\bmod 2$.  Relative to the common
unmarked serialization, every nonconstant bit coordinate is localized to a
single clause position: its exceptional value can occur for at most one $h$.

\begin{theorem}[Actual serialized promise query depth]
The deterministic adaptive bit-query complexity of $L$ on
$\{S_1,\ldots,S_m\}$ is exactly $m/2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the upper bound, query one distinguishing sign bit in every clause of one
parity.  An exception identifies that parity; if all responses are baseline,
the unique marked clause has the other parity.  This uses $m/2$ queries.

For the lower bound, answer every query with its common baseline value.  Since
each queried coordinate excludes at most one possible hidden position, fewer
than $m/2$ queries cannot eliminate all $m/2$ candidates of either parity.
At least one even and one odd $h$ remain consistent, so the label is not yet
determined.  The argument is on encoded bits, not on unit-cost clause probes.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Stateful cache collapse]
For the published one-hot child map, after $m/2$ source probes the controller
can retain the label in one persistent bit and answer any number $q$ of
label-dependent child-character requests without rescanning the source.
Hence $q m/2$ is a cold stateless-accessor charge, not a necessary cost of the
universal stateful controller.  Output/access transitions and any materialized
child remain separately chargeable.
\end{corollary}
This does not make arbitrary SAT easy: it exploits a promise grammar whose
classification is exactly the one hidden label bit.  LSPV-4's padded child
remains a valid restricted both-label ordinary-SAT control, but DELAY/COPY
repetition cannot promote the cold rescan product once legal caching is allowed.

\section{Independent star-broker referee}
For a canonical block, combine the four-clause encodings of
$x\oplus a\oplus b=p$, $a\oplus b\oplus c=q$, and the authenticated PIN
$c=r$.  Eliminating $a,b,c$ gives
\[
  x=p\oplus q\oplus r=:\tau.
\]
Conversely, when $x=\tau$, take $a=0$, $b=x\oplus p$, $c=r$ and choose the
two PIN auxiliaries arbitrarily; every core clause is satisfied.  Thus the
boundary relation is exactly $\{\tau\}$.  Independent enumeration found eight
extensions for the feasible boundary and zero for the other in all eight
$(p,q,r)$ cases.

The LSPV-3 counts also check exactly.  With $k$ blocks the source has
\[

\]
and cofactoring one block on $x$ leaves $2(k+1)$ binary clauses from the
$x$-XOR groups plus eight ternary clauses, totaling $10+2k$.  Therefore the
reported $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ child scale is correct on this canonical family.

the complete parent fallback, the descendants are not recursively canonical,
