\section{Exact obstruction and four falsifiers}
The only canonical inputs are valid proper duplicate-free D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) originals: every clause has three distinct literals of one sign and every original variable occurs twice positively and twice negatively. Malformed strings reject. Residuals, circuits and Tseitin encodings are derived noncanonical objects.

The obstruction is an output/state conflation. Exact pointwise continuation equivalence does not force a solver to serialize a fresh residual circuit at every adjacent pin. A retained representation may keep the authenticated source and a changing pin vector, paying later when it is decoded, evaluated, copied or searched. We tested:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(H1)] Every adjacent coherent SET/UNSET/FLIP requires a fresh $\Omega(N)$ residual-circuit rebuild. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the theorem below.
\item[(H2)] Exact continuation equivalence requires semantic equality, assignment recovery, or an injective syntactic pin key. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: source plus coherent pins is already a pointwise exact representative, with no label oracle.
\item[(H3)] Constant-size retained-state updates decide the residual SAT label. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the update represents $F\!\upharpoonright p$ but does not compute $\exists y\,F(p,y)$; same-original F9 one-bit siblings have seven versus zero completions.
\item[(H4)] Logical or physical update distance alone yields arbitrary-machine SAT hardness. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the fixed-layout counts below are implementation-specific and coexist with label changes and unchanged labels.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Exact all-input representation theorem}
For an authenticated source $F$ and coherent partial assignment $p$ to original variables, define
\[
K_{\rm lazy}(F,p)=(F,p).
\]
Its decoder scans every source clause, deletes a clause satisfied by $p$, deletes falsified pinned literals from every remaining clause, and emits TRUE, FALSE, or the ordinary residual $R_p$. Hence for every assignment $y$ to the free original variables,
\[
\operatorname{Decode}(K_{\rm lazy}(F,p))(y)=R_p(y)=F(p,y).
\]
This is continuation equivalence, strictly stronger than equality of the current SAT/UNSAT bit.

For the indexed representation, one paid source scan builds four signed occurrence addresses for each original variable. Each source clause has three fixed slots. Slot state is U/F/T, and a fixed clause summary stores whether some slot is T and the counts of U and F slots. The original source topology remains static.

\begin{theorem}[Exact local continuation update]
For every valid canonical original $F$ and coherent $p$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a one-variable SET, UNSET or FLIP updates exactly one $K_{\rm lazy}$ pin record;
\item in the declared indexed cache, it updates exactly four signed occurrence-slot records in four distinct original clauses and exactly four declared clause-summary records;
\item after either update, decoding is pointwise equal to $F\!\upharpoonright p$ on every future original-variable assignment.
\end{enumerate}
After paid $O(N)$ word-RAM preprocessing, the indexed update uses $O(1)$ word operations because the target grammar has signed occurrence degree four. It retains $O(N\log N)$ bits including occurrence addresses. This makes no $O(1)$ claim for the FLMV tape model: binary addresses, index-head programming, head motion, and tape erasure must be charged under their actual semantics.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first claim changes only one coordinate of $p$. For the second, canonical validity gives exactly two positive and two negative occurrences of the changed variable; clause distinctness puts them in four distinct clauses. SET/UNSET changes U to F/T or back, while FLIP exchanges F and T. Thus precisely those four fixed slots change. In the declared summary, SET/UNSET changes the U count and FLIP changes the F count, so each of the four summaries changes. No other slot or clause depends syntactically on that pin. The decoder identity is clausewise: a T slot makes its source clause true; otherwise the U slots are exactly the surviving literals, and no U slot means the empty false clause. This is exactly ordinary source restriction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Serialization firewall]
A materialized fresh residual or circuit may still require $\Theta(N+|R_p|)$ source reads and output writes. The local theorem avoids that cost only by retaining a source-indexed representation and deferring its use. COPY pays every copied record; RECOMP pays the scan again; DELAY may consume time with no data-bit changes. The theorem supplies neither a free semantic quotient nor a SAT-label oracle.
\end{corollary}

\section{Fully charged work and peak live space}
For a transition sequence $p_0,\ldots,p_q$, the declared indexed word-RAM implementation obeys
\[
T_{\rm idx}=T_{\rm grammar}+T_{\rm source\ scan}+T_{\rm index\ build}
+\sum_{t=1}^{q}\bigl(T_{\rm address}(v_t)+4T_{\rm slot}+12T_{\rm summary\ read}+4T_{\rm summary\ write}\bigr)
+\sum_{t=0}^{q}T_{\rm use}(p_t).
\]
$T_{\rm use}$ is not free: full decoding/serialization scans the source and writes the result; continuation evaluation reads relevant source/pin records; SAT decision may still require exact exponential search. The lazy representation omits the occurrence cache and pays only one pin update, but its use scans the source.

For an adaptive controller $D$, the complete recurrence remains
\[
\begin{split}
T_D(z)={}&T_{\rm grammar,source,index/head}(z)
+\sum_{r\in\mathrm{attempted}(z)}T_{\rm discover,test,fail,switch}(z,r)\\
&+T_{\rm representation,use,semantic,proof,GE/SCC,cache,replay,guest}(z)
+\sum_{y\in E(z)}\bigl(T_{\rm child/output/bit}(z,y)+T_D(y)\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
Peak writable space includes the pin vector, occurrence addresses/cache if retained, rollback stack, every live ancestor, source/index/head state, proofs, equality tables, children and guests. Lazy pins use $O(n)$ trit bits beyond the read-only source; the indexed cache uses $O(N\log N)$ bits under named-record encoding. A DFS rollback frame uses $O(\log N)$ bits per level in the word model. Physical tape costs may be larger. Logical XOR remains offline unless computed and charged by the SAT machine.

The exact gap is unchanged: local conditioning does not bound the number of visited states or the work of deciding any one residual. No well-founded all-input potential sums policy acquisition, failures, switches, exact fallback and successors polynomially. There is no arbitrary correct polynomial decider $A$ transformed into a power-short $\Phi(A)$, and no strictly numerical same-machine FLMV transfer from ordinary SAT to the canonical language.
