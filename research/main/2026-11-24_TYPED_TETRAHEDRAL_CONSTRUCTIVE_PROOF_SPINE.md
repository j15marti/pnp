# Constructive P versus NP: condensed typed tetrahedral proof spine
2026-11-24, Pacific time. Status: OPEN. This is a synthesis and research program, not a claimed resolution of P versus NP. It consolidates the recent user-directed reduced-port, distributed-wheel, guarded exchange, and aggregate-credit work. No existing manuscript or agent assignment is superseded.

## I. Exact source compilation (PROVED)
Given explicitly encoded 3-CNF F, for each clause (a OR b OR c) introduce private u and replace the clause by NAE(a,b,u) AND NAE(not u,c,0). If a=b=0 the first forces u=1 and the second forces c=1; if a=b=1 use u=0; if a differs from b, u=0 suffices. Thus existential projection is exactly the clause, including signed literals. Replace each NAE(p,q,r) by exact-two-of-four B4(p,q,r,t) with private t, since t=2-p-q-r is Boolean iff the sum of p,q,r is one or two. Represent every variable's occurrences by a tree of arity at most four equality vertices; signed wires carry negation, private unary/optional ports and pins are explicit. The source has O(number of literal occurrences + variables) vertices and wires and polynomial encoded size. Satisfying assignments lift both ways by local choices; this is a decision reduction, not a parsimonious counting claim. Every vertex has at most four active ports. Exact equality fan-out is essential to full expressiveness.

## II. Local/regional typed normalization (PROVED within stated domains)
* Fixing B4 ports leaves exact cardinality; projecting one private port yields NAE3, projecting two yields unrestricted binary support. Shared ports cannot be dropped without preserving external compatibility.
* A fixed-parity complement-invariant four-port relation has 16 normalized Boolean signatures, with 0,1,2,3,4 complementary pairs. Aligned two-wire fusion of weighted signatures (a,b,c,d) and (e,f,g,h) yields (ae+bf,af+be,cg+dh,ch+dg), with independent eigenchannels a+b,a-b,c+d,c-d. This closure requires the four-port complement/parity hypotheses and aligned ports.
* Exact-degree matching regions with <=3 external ports have fixed boundary parity; acquire support by <=8 matching calls, including signed normalization. Private odd exact-one rims with three odd inter-port gaps become odd XOR. The matching abstraction is sound only with authenticated source/lift and all external dependencies retained.
* For an even affine boundary relation a+L, put L2=span{v in L: weight(v)=2}. Matching-realizable (in the standard mandatory matching boundary model) iff L=L2, equivalent to independent signed parity blocks. Enumerate cosets of L2 for an exact 2^delta poly(N) terminal, delta=dim L-dim L2. The even-parity qualification matters; odd-arity unrestricted fan-out is not a fixed-parity relation. New affine equations Qx=c give delta'=delta+(t'-t)-rank(Q restricted to L), where t,t' count weight-two blocks. Defect-safe guards annihilate L2. Raw delta is not a monotone universal cost measure.
* Four-state hubs h=(a,b) have 16 possible allowed-state masks. A single mask on two distinct boundary terminals is matching-realizable with optional private vertices: pins/free wires, equality/opposition, signed NAND (a mandatory center adjacent to two boundary terminals, each with an optional private leaf), or contradiction. For transmitting a mask to two regions the relation {(a,a,b,b):(a,b) in S} is matching-realizable iff S is Cartesian A x B (including empty). Necessity: symmetric-difference path switches between two matching witnesses preserve feasibility and change at most two boundary terminals; duplicates must change as pairs. Hence mixed states are required when both coordinates differ. There are 10 Cartesian masks and 6 exceptional masks (two diagonals, four three-state masks). Sufficiency: independent wires/pins.
* This restriction is on an exact ordinary matching representation, not on arbitrary algorithms. Modular rank, positive circuits, raw topology, and explicit cube-cover size are likewise restricted measures, not unrestricted lower bounds.

## III. Exact composition and aggregate accounting (PROVED under promises)
Use matching regions joined by shared hubs. If the residual dependency graph is a forest and each incident region has one distinct terminal per hub coordinate, arbitrary child masks are absorbed by the constant-size gadgets; condition on the parent hub's four states. At most 4r matching calls for r regions (root hub or root region). Glue by authentic shared bits and reconstruct private witnesses. Unrestricted degree-two hubs can be absorbed as independent signed wires even around cycles, as can Cartesian masks by pins and wires. Thus physical cycle rank and global fan-out can be unbounded in tractable families. A supplied b-hub conditioning set reducing the remainder to the promised terminal gives 4^b poly(N); discovery must be charged. Independent sectors need a sum, not product, of costs; shared sectors require exact interface compatibility.

Other established conditional terminals: affine observation-space DP (given low-width decomposition and local evaluators), planar signed pair-energy and guarded quartic reduction, owner/count flow, parity factor and tagged-flow saturation, guarded regional witness exchange. Do not expand implicit regions into full truth tables without charging acquisition. Even product of k exact-one triples requires >=(3/2)^k explicit contained affine cubes, while its symbolic product is linear. Guarded witness cubes only certify sufficiency, not exhaustive coverage.

Use exact partial acquisition: for region i keep witnessed lower rows L_i and possible upper rows U_i, with actual rows in between; compatible private witnesses must be independent given authenticated complete boundary/guard/owner/tag keys. For exact outer join J, J(L)<=SAT(F)<=J(U). J(L)=1 lifts a source SAT witness; J(U)=0 rejects; upper rows unsupported by the global join can retire globally, not be called locally infeasible. DS02 proves a polynomial scheduler given a polynomial explicit row universe, paid row queries and an exact polynomial outer evaluator for ALL masks. DP03 pays monotone updates on a fixed acquired positive compatibility DAG; BS02 supplies a stateful all-mask flow/count alternative (ordinary matching need not admit small monotone AND/OR compilation). S08 gives a uniform-fiber slack certificate for absorbing tagged-flow interiors, not arbitrary-mask persistence. Failed tests are UNKNOWN, not negative evidence. Complete semantic row acquisition for arbitrary regions is not assumed.

## IV. Universal paid contraction (OPEN; principal gap)
Seek a fixed uniform deterministic procedure for every encoded residual. It must either return a verified exact decision, or produce exact child/composition operations with a nonnegative integer credit satisfying
    mu(root)<=N^c,
    sum_j mu(child_j)<=mu(parent)-1.
Every node's total discovery (including failed work), acquisition, verification, source compilation, guarded canonicalization, sharing/cache maintenance, evaluation and reconstruction must cost <=N^d for fixed c,d, with root N. Representations and witness lifts must stay within this bound. Charge total work, peak space, cumulative memory, bit complexity and output; never charge only successful steps. Exact semantics must specify AND/OR or other composition, not simply assert compatibility of children.

Then the expanded computation has <=mu(root) nodes (or O(mu(root)) with a charged positive credit per visit including terminal leaves), and total work O(N^(c+d)). A precise ledger can require every active node credit >=1, terminal processing consumes its credit, and each internal expansion consumes at least one before distributing the remainder. This is a constructive P=NP implication via I. Equivalently, a diagonal hard sequence for a fixed terminating correct algorithm with no polynomial time bound (choose lengths tending to infinity and exponents increasing, absorbing constants) contradicts the universal ledger. The diagonal argument is not a proof of universal progress. Nonpolynomial time for one portfolio would not prove P!=NP.

## Priority next steps
1. Characterize non-Cartesian hub restrictions and collective contraction. Distinguish single-message absorption from duplicated transmission; try exact matching exchange factors and affine/2-SAT composition before branching. Preserve all guard and source correlations.
2. Integrate matching, flow, multisign and nonlinear witness acquisition on identical residuals, with source-checkable exact interfaces. Try to factor irreducible cyclic sectors, not just raw hub counts.
3. Prove or falsify the universal paid progress lemma with infinite SAT/UNSAT controls; test discovery as well as evaluation, use a common charged ledger. Do not infer completeness from an expanding finite portfolio.

## Adversarial controls and research status
High raw quotient defect, large observation width and affine stagnation can coexist with easy SAT (complete tripartite NAE). Independent exact-one regions falsify universal explicit exchange-cube coverage. High-girth and thin tagged-flow counterexamples defeat named restricted closure rules; RT08 has a modular interval escape, not whole-portfolio hardness. Arbitrary reduced matching/synchronization networks encode NP-complete SAT by I, so universal polynomial contraction is the actual mathematical burden. No universal theorem is claimed. The project follows evidence toward either P=NP or P!=NP.
