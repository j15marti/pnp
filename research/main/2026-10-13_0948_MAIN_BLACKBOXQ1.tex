\section{Primary-literature and transfer preflight}
The two obstacles are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an arbitrary one-bit SAT computation need not expose an explicit proof
in a proof system with a known lower bound; and
\item once a proposed extraction theorem ranges only over polynomial SAT
deciders and its conclusion contradicts a hard endpoint, its quantifier may
already encode the desired separation.
\end{enumerate}
Cook--Reckhow supplies the proof-system and simulation framework
\cite{CookReckhow}; Atserias--M\"uller shows that Resolution automatability is
itself computationally difficult under standard assumptions
\cite{AtseriasMuller}.  Feigenbaum--Fortnow and Bogdanov--Trevisan show that
strong black-box/random-self-reduction architectures for NP-complete problems
have conditional collapse barriers in their stated nonadaptive models
\cite{FeigenbaumFortnow,BogdanovTrevisan}.  These papers do not prove the
project's theorem, but they guard against treating the LWE analogy as a free
worst-case/average-case bridge.

\section{Candidate: charged external black-box extraction}
Let $\mathcal K$ be the fixed NP-complete clean-core language under its
canonical complete encoding.  Let $\mathcal H=(H_n)$ be a polynomial-time
constructible UNSAT sequence, with $L_n=|\mathrm{enc}(H_n)|$, having no
polynomial-size explicit witness/refutation in a declared model $\mathcal M$.
For a uniform deterministic exact decider $A$, define
$\operatorname{BBExt}_{\mathcal M}(A)$ to mean that there exists a uniform
oracle transducer $E^A$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item on $H_n$, $E^A$ forms at most $L_n^a$ queries of length at most $L_n^b$;
\item query construction, oracle runtime, answer access, preprocessing,
replay, reconstruction, and output are all charged;
\item if $T_A(m)\le c_A m^{d_A}$, then for fixed constants depending on
$A,E$, total time and explicit output length are polynomial in $L_n$; and
\item the output is a valid explicit $\mathcal M$-refutation of $H_n$.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, if local work is $L_n^e$, the charged composition satisfies
\[
 T_{E^A}(H_n)
 \le L_n^e+L_n^a\bigl(O(L_n^b)+T_A(L_n^b)\bigr)
 =L_n^{O(\max\{e,a+bd_A\})}.                              \tag{4}
\]
Arbitrary fixed polynomial exponents remain polynomial; hiding them does not
create a contradiction or an exponent-independent bound.

Define the proposed universal bridge
\[
 U_{\rm BB}:=
 \forall A\,[A\text{ is a uniform polynomial-time exact decider of }\mathcal K
 \Rightarrow \operatorname{BBExt}_{\mathcal M}(A)].        \tag{5}
\]

\paragraph{Quantifier-collapse proposition.}
Under the explicit superpolynomial $\mathcal M$-witness lower bound,
\[
                         U_{\rm BB}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad P\ne NP.
                                                                    \tag{6}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
If $P\ne NP$, then the antecedent class in (5) is empty because $\mathcal K$
is NP-complete, so (5) is true vacuously.  If $P=NP$, choose a uniform
polynomial-time exact decider $A$ for $\mathcal K$.  If
$\operatorname{BBExt}_{\mathcal M}(A)$ held, (4) would produce an explicit
polynomial-size $\mathcal M$-refutation of $H_n$, contradicting the endpoint
lower bound.  Thus (5) is false.  This proves (6). \(\square\)

The proof also gives
\[
 \exists A\in\operatorname{PolyDec}(\mathcal K)\quad
 \operatorname{BBExt}_{\mathcal M}(A)                    \tag{7}
\]
as unconditionally false once the endpoint is fixed.  The extraction may be
external, adaptive, randomized with zero error, or oblivious to internal
states; none of those changes the explicit-output contradiction.  If the
output is compressed or implicit, the endpoint model changed and its explicit
size lower bound no longer applies.  If $\mathcal M$ is ordinary deterministic
machine time, the needed lower bound is precisely the OPEN separation target.

Because independent red-team review has not yet occurred, the named project
claim \texttt{BLACKBOX-HARD-ENDPOINT-QUANTIFIER-COLLAPSE1} is kept at
\CAND{} despite the complete proof above.  It must not be used as a proved
separation.

\section{Barrier audit and highest-value target}

decider plus an oracle-relative polynomial extractor still composes to a
polynomial process, while the chosen unconditional proof lower bound is not
thereby promoted to arbitrary oracle machines.  No Natural-Proofs-style
constructive large property is supplied.  The theorem is a logical guard,
not a barrier escape.

The proof spine is therefore
\[
\boxed{\text{explicit exact-$(2,2)$ Resolution-hard MU family}}
\quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{\text{arbitrary uniform deterministic time lower bound}}.
\]
The highest-value target remains the direct fixed-core theorem
\[
 \forall A\in\operatorname{Dec}(\mathcal K)\ \forall d,C,N_0\ \exists x:
 |x|\ge N_0\ \land\ T_A(x)>C|x|^d,                         \tag{8}

is equivalent to the desired separation and is \OPEN.  A direct proof for
