\textbf{New verified results.}
PH-PROV-CLOSE1 proves the following closure. Assume \(P=NP\). If \(R(x,w)\) is polynomially
balanced and its graph lies anywhere in PH, then a valid witness is selectable in FP on the domain
of \(R\). The proof uses the prefix-extension predicate
\[
E_R(x,u)\iff \exists v\,R(x,uv),
\]
which remains in PH, together with the collapse \(P=NP\Rightarrow PH=P\).

Consequently:
\begin{enumerate}
\item unique/canonical PH-verifiable provenance is FP-reacquirable under \(P=NP\);
\item fixed-bounded-alternation polynomial-length audit transcripts are FP-selectable under \(P=NP\);
\item any polynomial-time prime/CRT annotation of such a selected realization is also in FP.
\end{enumerate}
PRIME-FNP-CLOSE1 is the NP-level special case.
