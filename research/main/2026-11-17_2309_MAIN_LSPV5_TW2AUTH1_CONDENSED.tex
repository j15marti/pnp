\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Fix $\Sigma=\mathbb{Z}_5$ and $R(a,b)=[(b-a)\bmod 5\in\{1,3\}]$. Authenticate every source byte from a canonical graph encoding (header, ordered vertex table and sorted distinct directed edges). For $G=(V,E)$ let $Z(G)=\sum_{x\in\Sigma^V}\prod_{(u,v)\in E}R(x_u,x_v)$.
Group vertices by equality of their complete incoming and outgoing adjacency signatures. Each directed twin class is an independent set, with uniform interclass adjacency. An isolated class of size $s$ contributes $5^s$. Any nonisolated class has a used-color support $S$ contained in an adjacent vertex's two-element in- or out-neighborhood; for this five-symbol relation the two sets of possible doubletons coincide. There are precisely ten supports: five singletons and five admissible doubletons. Exact assignment multiplicities are $w_s(\{a\})=1$ and $w_s(\{a,b\})=2^s-2$. Interclass arc $i\to j$ permits supports $S_i,S_j$ iff $R(a,b)$ holds for every $(a,b)\in S_i\times S_j$. Summing support weights over compatible support assignments is exactly $Z(G)$, not merely its satisfiability indicator.

Lemma (classical minor argument): an undirected graph has treewidth at most two iff iterative elimination of ANY current degree-at-most-two vertex, with fill between its neighbors, succeeds. If it succeeds the order has width two. If tw is at most two an eligible vertex exists; its elimination is a minor operation (contract incident edge to realize a fill edge or delete the vertex), so tw cannot increase, and induction applies.

Restricted theorem (PROVED/EXACT, pending review): for every original canonical source of length $N$ with directed-twin quotient $Q$ satisfying tw$(Q)\le2$, greedy order discovery and exact integer bucket elimination compute $Z(G)$ in conservative $O((N+1)^{12})$ bit operations and $O((N+1)^5)$ peak bits. Each elimination has at most two remaining neighbor variables, each with ten states, so its table has at most $10^2$ entries and each entry sums at most ten terms. Integer lengths are polynomial since intermediate positive counts are bounded by polynomial multiples of $5^{|V|}$. A verifier reconstructing the source, quotient and order, then recomputing exact elimination, accepts $(G,\pi,z)$ iff $\pi$ is valid and $z=Z(G)$. All accepted orders produce the SAME integer output. This is not a universal arbitrary-Turing-machine terminal certificate.

Explicit family: let $Q_q$ ($q\ge5$) begin with $K_4$ minus edge 03 on vertices 0,1,2,3 and add each $j\ge4$ adjacent to $j-1,j-2$. Its tw is two and its open neighborhoods are distinct for $q\ge5$. Properly three-color it with colors $0,1,2$ and orient each edge according to $R$. Blow each quotient vertex into $a$ independent twins and each edge into a complete oriented $K_{a,a}$. The resulting original source has reconstructed quotient size $q$, is satisfiable, has original treewidth at least $a$, but has polynomial exact-count complexity. For a NO companion, replace five initial arcs by $0\to1,0\to2,1\to2,1\to3,3\to2$. The first three force colors $(0,3,1)$ up to rotational symmetry; the last two have no possible fourth color. Thus exact count is zero for every $q,a$.

Matched oracle: fix one universal oracle machine $U^A$ and superpolynomial $t(n)$ with enough time for polynomial-overhead universal oracle simulation. For every oracle $A$, Hirahara's prefix/suffix proof relativizes to show $R_{K_U^{t,A}}=\{x:K_U^{t,A}(x)\ge |x|-1\}$ is $P^A$-immune. An infinite $L$ in $P^A$ contained in this set would admit a shorter $n-C\log n+2\log n+O(1)$ program by hardcoding the first $n-C\log n$ bits of a length-$n$ element and searching polynomially many suffixes with the same oracle. Let $A=\mathrm{TQBF}$; then $P^A=NP^A=PSPACE$. A polynomial-verifiable oracle 2-list PFCERT covering every $n$-bit endpoint of programs $|p|\le n-2$ and allowing at most two accepted $x$ globally per $(n,p)$ would yield $E\in NP^A=P^A$ with $|E_n|\le2(2^{n-1}-1)=2^n-2$. Its infinite complement is in $P^A$ and contained in the random set, contradiction. All $U$, verifier and complexity classes use the SAME oracle. This does not prove unrelativized certificate existence, nonalgebrization or SAT hardness.

Caveats: polynomial certificate length, initiality, consecution, terminal coverage, certified envelope sparsity, polynomial membership evaluation, global accepted-output multiplicity, and polynomial total explicit proof expansion are separate requirements. No exponential union of individually sparse descriptors may be presumed sparse. DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, RECOMP, affine and compact evaluator controls reject source-appearance hardness.
