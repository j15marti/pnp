\section{Sharp hereditary-boundary implication}
The following theorem composes the square-root block profile reported in
\path{ARC-M22-PROJECTED-CLOSURE177} with the verified growing-depth theorem
\path{ARC-UNIFORM-DEEPENING170}.  It is an implication, not a construction
of the open premises.

\begin{theorem}[M22 hereditary margin]\label{thm:margin}
Fix one uniform reusable typed transformer $\mathcal R$ and a hereditary
domain $\mathcal H$.  On every current ordinary virtual encoding of length
$n$ in $\mathcal H$, assume that one fixed program:
\begin{enumerate}
\item acquires and verifies a valid block ordering in
$n^{p+o(1)}$ work and $n^{\sigma+o(1)}$ writable/index bits;
\item has square-root blocks and two-side boundary
$w\le \kappa\log_2n+o(\log n)$, hence at most
$n^{a+o(1)}$ sequential boundary queries where
\[
 a=\frac12+2\kappa;
\]
\item emits an ordinary child of length $n^{\beta+o(1)}$ per query, with
$\beta=1/2$, using a fixed-degree polylogarithmic bit accessor;
\item pays every source/workspace read, every write including unchanged
writes, indexing, headers, control, failed attempt, compatibility test,
join, and reconstruction; materializing one child at a time costs
$n^{a+\beta+o(1)}$ aggregate work;
\item is exact on SAT and UNSAT inputs, emits only children in $\mathcal H$,
and has an exact join using the same fixed boundary names at every level.
\end{enumerate}
Then uniform depth-first recursion to ordinary length $O(\log\log N)$ gives
one machine on $\mathcal H$ with
\[
 t\le \max\{p,1+4\kappa\},\qquad
 s\le \max\{\sigma,1/2\}.
\]
Consequently this route enters the strict FLMV box precisely when its other
premises hold and
\[
 p<\frac{241}{200},\qquad
 \sigma<\frac{11642479}{23232400},\qquad
 \kappa<\frac{41}{800}.
\]
At $\kappa=1/20$ the time exponent is $6/5$, leaving margin $1/200$;
at $\kappa=41/800$ it is exactly $241/200$, so strict entry fails.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The query exponent is $a=1/2+2\kappa$: there are $n^{1/2+o(1)}$
blocks and at most $2^{2w}=n^{2\kappa+o(1)}$ boundary assignments per
block.  A materialized $n^{1/2+o(1)}$-bit child for every query costs exponent
$a+\beta=1+2\kappa$.  Under the common fixed accessor and hereditary exact
join, Theorem ARC-UNIFORM-DEEPENING170 applies.  At recursive level $i$ the
node-count exponent is
\[
 A_i=a\sum_{j<i}\beta^j
 =\frac{a(1-\beta^i)}{1-\beta}.
\]
Thus the limiting tree exponent is
\[
 \frac{a}{1-\beta}=2a=1+4\kappa,
\]
which dominates $a+\beta=1+2\kappa$.  Ordering acquisition contributes $p$.
Depth-first processing retains one child buffer and a geometric stack of
ancestor frames, giving $\max\{\sigma,1/2\}$ writable/index space.  Every
polylogarithmic factor has one common fixed degree, so the
$\Theta(\log\log N)$-level product is $N^{o(1)}$.

Strict time entry is $1+4\kappa<241/200$, equivalent to
$\kappa<41/800$.  The stated space conditions are immediate.  Exactness
follows inductively from both-label joins.  No time coordinate or space
coordinate is borrowed from another machine.
\end{proof}

If local generation of each written child bit has an additional exponent
$g$, the local materialization term becomes $1+2\kappa+g$ and must be added
inside the maximum.  If finding the ordering calls a parent-length SAT
solver, that call is also charged at the parent exponent and the theorem does
not provide contraction without a separate bound.

\section{Two-level closure-is-not-contraction counterexample}
Let $Y$ have positive supports
$123,145,246,356$ and negative supports $124,136,235,456$.  It is a proper
clausewise-monotone M22 formula with six variables, eight unique clauses,
two positive and two negative occurrences per variable, no complementary
pair, witness $111000$, and 20 models.

Define the deliberately simple restoration operator
\[
 R_0(H)=H^{(1)}\wedge H^{(2)}\wedge H^{(3)}\wedge Y,
\]
where all four variable sets are disjoint and the first three conjuncts are
renamed copies of $H$.  For $H=Y$, both recursive levels remain proper M22,
non-fully-paired, and satisfiable, yet
\[
 (k,m): (6,8)\longmapsto(24,32)\longmapsto(78,104).
\]
Thus exact class closure and label preservation do not imply length
contraction.  This refutes any use of ARC177's restoration property alone;
the small block and boundary premise in Theorem~\ref{thm:margin} is essential.
The counterexample is not evidence against the full ARC177 compiler on its
stated low-width inputs.
