\section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
Four statistics were fixed first: clause-pair overlap type, polarity agreement, same-support multiplicity, and small-core size. A persistent monotone local statistic would falsify the raw conditioned-UNSAT test distribution. A second candidate was to reweight UNSAT formulas if the failure was only moment bias.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
Cavalar--Kumar--Rossman use explicit positive/negative test distributions and bounded-width approximators. Rao's slice-sunflower theorem applies to a uniform fixed-cardinality distribution. Cavalar--Fabris--Mukhopadhyay--Srinivasan--Yehudayoff, ECCC TR26-220 (2026), derandomize robust sunflowers under $O(w)$-wise independent and more generally locally independent, approximately identically distributed marginals. Thus low-order moment matching is structurally relevant, but does not itself give a circuit lower bound.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Fix $q$ and $t=3q$. Let $M=8\binom q3$. The signed permutation group $G_q=(\mathbb Z_2)^q\rtimes S_q$ has nine nonempty orbits on unordered pairs of distinct proper 3-clauses. A pair orbit is determined by $(s,a)$, where $s$ is the number of shared variables and $a$ the number of shared variables on which the falsifying values agree.

\begin{lemma}[Orbit moment identity]
For exact-t formula F, uniform $g\in G_q$, and fixed pair P in orbit O, $\Pr[P\subseteq gF]=c_O(F)/|O|$, where $c_O(F)$ counts O-type clause pairs in F.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}Transitivity on O makes every coordinate pair in O equally likely; averaging containment gives the stated ratio.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Pairwise design criterion]
A distribution $\nu$ on exact-t formulas followed by uniform signed permutation has the same one- and two-clause inclusion probabilities as the uniform exact layer iff
\[\mathbb E_{F\sim\nu}c_O(F)=\binom t2\frac{|O|}{\binom M2}\]
for every pair orbit O. Single-clause marginals are automatically $t/M$.
\end{corollary}

The raw conditioned-UNSAT distribution fails this test. Define $Q(F)=\sum_v d_v^+(F)d_v^-(F)$, a coordinatewise monotone opposite-polarity conflict count. For q=16,t=48, 500 uniform-conditioned UNSAT and 300 SAT samples had means 337.724 and 317.373. A threshold $Q\ge328$ chosen on half the data achieved held-out TPR .748, FPR .213, advantage .535 and accuracy .767.

Linear programming nevertheless found nine sampled UNSAT formulas with positive rational weights summing to one:
\[\left(
\frac{1074105644}{8841649017},\frac{615843518}{8841649017},\frac{1734515486}{8841649017},
\frac{108195384}{2947216339},\frac{969863195}{8841649017},\frac{1545711667}{8841649017},
\frac{1605438695}{8841649017},\frac{961682122}{8841649017},\frac{3300846}{2947216339}
\right).\]
The exact rational weighted pair counts equal the uniform exact-layer target in all nine pair orbits. Each formula has 48 distinct clauses on 16 declared variables and was exhaustively checked UNSAT on all $2^{16}$ assignments.

Since the nine nonempty pair-orbit coordinates sum to one, the affine dimension is at most eight. Therefore, whenever the uniform target lies in the convex hull of UNSAT pair spectra, Caratheodory yields a mixture supported on at most nine formulas. The finite certificate attains that support bound.

\section{ORDER-3 TEST}
Triple patterns were canonically quotiented under variable permutations and independent variable complementation. The pair-matched q=16 mixture had estimated total-variation distance .00838 from 400,000 uniform coordinate triples; an independent 400,000-versus-200,000 Monte Carlo baseline was .00446. Jointly-falsifiable triple fractions were .405982 versus .40594, and all-pairs-overlap triangle fractions .140010 versus .140623. These are \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not exact third-moment matching.

At q=6, all 669,920 coordinate triples were enumerated into 73 signed-permutation orbits. Among 731 sampled UNSAT formulas, the best convex mixture missed the uniform triple-orbit target by .00135 in maximum coordinate. This shows that order three is materially more constrained than order two in the tested pool, not that exact order-three matching is impossible.

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
Uniform-conditioned UNSAT is not locally pseudorandom at pair level, but that bias is not intrinsic to all positive test distributions: convex reweighting removes every pair-orbit bias exactly in the q=16 finite certificate. The new asymptotic target is
\[\boxed{\text{UNSAT exact-}3q\text{ distributions whose slice moments match the uniform layer through }k(q)\text{ coordinates}.}\]
TR26-220 suggests aiming for $k(q)=\Omega(w)$ for the bounded-width approximators used in a sunflower argument, together with the separate positive-spread condition. No such asymptotic distribution is currently proved.

\section{BARRIER AUDIT}
Pairwise matching does not prevent width-3 or wider monotone distinguishers, does not establish minterm spread, and does not supply the approximation lemma. The finite construction is nonuniform. The load-bearing missing statement is an asymptotic pseudorandom UNSAT test distribution plus a model-matched monotone approximation theorem.

\section{NEXT TARGET}
Use signed-permutation symmetrization and convex moment balancing to match all clause-pattern orbits through orders 3 and 4. Seek either a structural orbit whose bias is unavoidable for every UNSAT distribution, retiring the route, or a scalable convex-design theorem placing the uniform slice moment vector in the convex hull of UNSAT orbit spectra through $r=\Omega(\log q)$.
