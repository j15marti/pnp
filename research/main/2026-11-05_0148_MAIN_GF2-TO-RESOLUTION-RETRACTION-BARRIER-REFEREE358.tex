\section{IDEA before search and falsifiers}

The initial hypothesis was a bounded-port byte-normalization lemma: unique paid public-wire owners plus constant-arity consumers should permit dead-payload pruning to polynomial bytes. Search confirmed F1 and F2: LSPV-1/386 already proves bounded-observation compactness in a declared constant-support interface, LSPV-3/416 repairs sparse identifier encoding, and LSPV-2/412 shows that separately projecting correlated DEF gates can destroy the relation $b=x$.  The idea was therefore revised to test F3 and F4 exactly rather than duplicate the conditional compactness theorem.

\section{Exact models and established anchors}

Let $G_h$ be a connected simple bridgeless cubic graph with $h$ vertices and $3h/2$ edge variables.  At each vertex, the parity constraint on its three incident edges is encoded by the four width-three clauses blocking the wrong-parity rows.  An odd total charge makes the $4h$-clause CNF $T(G_h,c)$ unsatisfiable.  With signed binary identifiers its source length is $N_h=\Theta(h\log(h+2))$.

The authoritative project theorem /341 proves in the fixed unit-node calculus that
\[
W_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(T(G_h,c))=4h-1.
\]
Its upper proof pays three AND3 conjunction nodes per vertex to recover the exact local GF(2) row and $h-1$ binary GF(2) additions.  Every edge occurs twice, cancels, and the odd right-hand sides give $0=1$.  This is a native mixed-DAG theorem, not a machine lower bound.

Urquhart's 1987 theorem supplies constants $\alpha>0,h_0$ and an infinite constant-degree expander family such that every ordinary unrestricted shared-DAG Resolution refutation of the corresponding Tseitin clauses has at least $2^{\alpha h}$ lines for $h\ge h_0$.  The proof language has only source clauses and ordinary resolvents; no GF(2), DEF, ER or semantic gates.  This is the exact target of the retraction refuted below.

Haken's separate theorem gives the analogous exponential ordinary-Resolution lower bound for explicit unary PHP.  Cook's polynomial ER PHP proof and the linear mixed Tseitin proof are mandatory strong-system controls.  Efremenko--Garl\'ik--Itsykson prove an exponential lower bound only for \emph{regular} Res$(\oplus)$ and explicitly state that general DAG-like Res$(\oplus)$ was open in their model; the 2026 Braun preprint claims a general DAG-like bit-PHP bound.  Neither theorem is transferred to arbitrary SAT machines or automatically to this different mixed grammar.

\section{ITERATE I: no polynomial GF(2)-erasing retraction}

\begin{theorem}[source-only Resolution retraction barrier]\label{thm:barrier}
There is no translator $\mathcal T$ and polynomial $p$ such that, for every valid original CNF $F$ and every valid $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ refutation $\pi$ of $F$, $\mathcal T(F,\pi)$ is an ordinary Resolution refutation of the \emph{same source clauses} $F$ satisfying
\[
|\mathcal T(F,\pi)|_{\rm lines}\le p(|F|_{\rm bits}+\operatorname{cost}_{R_{\mathrm{mix341}}}(\pi)).
\]
This impossibility holds even if translation time and space are unbounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose Urquhart's cubic expander Tseitin family with odd charge and apply /341's valid mixed proofs $\pi_h$ of cost $4h-1$.  Since $|F_h|=\Theta(h\log h)$, the assumed translator would output ordinary Resolution refutations of size $p(\Theta(h\log h)+4h-1)=h^{O(1)}$.  Urquhart requires $2^{\Omega(h)}$ lines for every such Resolution refutation.  For sufficiently large $h$ this is a contradiction.  The argument does not depend on finding $\pi_h$: /341 explicitly constructs it, and the minimum grants free proof selection anyway.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[normalization classification]
Any advertised ``full original-source-authenticated normalization'' that removes all mixed annotations, auxiliary structure and GF(2) rows and leaves only source clauses plus RES has superpolynomial worst-case blowup.  A polynomial normalization may instead retain typed GF(2), DEF/ER or other strong records; Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier} does not refute such a same-mixed-system compiler.
\end{corollary}

This is an explicit legal family-level counterexample to the \emph{source-only Resolution} version of deliverable (1).  It is not a counterexample to a uniquely serialized consumer-preserving mixed normal form, whose DEF facets, LOCAL exported wires and exact byte selectors remain unsettled.

\section{ITERATE II: consequence for B3 strategies}

LSPV-1/413, from Haken plus its proved additive-RUP-to-Resolution elaborator, shows that any explicit additive RUP or deletion-only DRUP refutation of PHP has superpolynomial output.  Therefore a polynomial-time total SAT decider cannot emit such a log for every UNSAT input, even under the hypothesis $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$: printing the required bytes already exceeds polynomial time.

Together with Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier}, this yields a precise bridge trilemma:
\begin{enumerate}
\item answer bits or raw rejecting traces are not sound native UNSAT certificates;
\item weak RUP/DRUP/Resolution certificates cannot be polynomial on all inputs;
\item strong mixed certificates avoid those lower bounds, but compiling arbitrary polynomial SAT decisions into them requires the still-open same-$R$ p-simulation/reflection theorem.
\end{enumerate}
REFEREE357 proves that, once polynomial encoded proof length is available, $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P$ makes it equivalent to p-optimality and polynomial proof search.  That characterization remains noncircular; no premise here establishes conditional p-optimality of $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$.  Thus same-$R$ B3 is neither proved nor refuted.

\section{Finite controls and physical accounting}

The Python producer and independent no-package JavaScript verifier instantiate K4, $K_{3,3}$, cube and Petersen graphs.  For odd charge they exhaustively obtain zero models; even charge gives respectively 8, 16, 32 and 64 models.  Every one of 448 local truth-table comparisons confirms that four clauses equal its three-edge parity relation.  Row addition ends in $0=1$ exactly for odd charge, and node counts are 15, 23, 31 and 39, matching $4h-1$.  Python rejects five malformed graphs; JavaScript makes 533 assertions.  These checks validate the finite proof skeleton, not Urquhart's asymptotic theorem.

For the expander family, logical mixed cost is $4h-1$ and a sparse explicit mixed proof has polynomially many bits.  Every ordinary Resolution output has exponentially many lines and therefore exponentially many bytes, writes and verification work in any explicit encoding.  A compressed/lazy object is not an ordinary Resolution refutation unless a declared verifier expands or validates it; that work and its access model must be charged.  Translation search, intermediate representations, input reads, construction, checking, output and peak live space are distinct from $W_R$.  The exponential backtranslation cost is not a lower bound on SAT decision time and supplies no FLMV consequence.
