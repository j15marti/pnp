\section{Field-trace firewall candidate}
Fix $r\ge1$ and let $K=\mathbb F_{2^r}$, represented by an explicitly encoded
irreducible polynomial.  Define
\[
Q:K^2\longrightarrow K,\qquad Q(x,y)=xy.                 \tag{3}
\]
After choosing an $\F$-basis of $K$, (3) is a map from $2r$ Boolean inputs to
$r$ Boolean quadratic outputs.  For $\lambda\in K$, let
\[
q_\lambda(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\F}(\lambda xy).     \tag{4}
\]
The nonzero $\F$-linear combinations of the $r$ coordinate forms of $Q$ are
exactly the $q_\lambda$ with $\lambda\ne0$.

\begin{theorem}[Field-trace quadratic firewall]
For every $r\ge1$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is surjective and $s(b)=(1,b)$ is a uniform selector for every
$b\in K$;
\item for every $\lambda\ne0$, the polar form
\[
\beta_\lambda((x,y),(x',y'))
=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\lambda(xy'+x'y)\right)       \tag{5}
\]
is alternating and nondegenerate on the $2r$-dimensional $\F$-space $K^2$;
\item consequently every nonzero linear combination of the $r$ polar
matrices has rank $2r$, while a requested joint right-hand side has a witness
computable with polynomially many field/bit operations.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The selector identity is immediate:
\[
Q(1,b)=b.                                                \tag{6}
\]
Polarizing (4) in characteristic two gives (5), whose diagonal value is zero.
Suppose $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$.  If $x\ne0$, choose $y'=0$ only after selecting
$x'=0$ and choose $y'$ so that
$\operatorname{Tr}(\lambda x y')=1$.  Such a $y'$ exists because
$z\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}((\lambda x)z)$ is a nonzero linear functional:
the finite-field trace pairing is nondegenerate.  If $x=0$, then $y\ne0$ and
choose $x'$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(\lambda x'y)=1$.
Thus no nonzero vector lies in the radical of $\beta_\lambda$.

With the irreducible polynomial and basis encoded, addition is bitwise XOR,
multiplication and trace are polynomial-time operations, and (6) requires no
search through the $2^{2r}$ domain.
\end{proof}

\section{Embedding after Tseitin elimination}
Let $X$ be connected, $T$ a spanning tree, and let the cotree have
$2r$ edges.  Cotree projection is a polynomial-time bijection from the
consistent affine coset $\{x:B_Xx=c\}$ to $\F^{2r}\cong K^2$.  The structured
residual
\[
B_Xx=c\quad\land\quad Q(x_C)=b                          \tag{7}
\]
is solved by setting the cotree vector to the bit representation of $(1,b)$
and lifting through $T$ leaf-to-root.  The projected rank is $2r$ and every
nonzero polar combination has rank $2r$.

An invertible affine change $z=Ay+d$ does not repair the route: compute
$z=(1,b)$, solve $Ay=z+d$, then tree-lift $y$.  Every polar matrix is changed
by congruence and retains rank $2r$.  If $X=K_{s,s}$ with odd $s$, then
$2r=(s-1)^2$ supplies an unbounded-treewidth instance family.  The field
polynomial may be included in the declared structured encoding and checked
for irreducibility in polynomial time; no hidden advice or source witness is
assumed.
