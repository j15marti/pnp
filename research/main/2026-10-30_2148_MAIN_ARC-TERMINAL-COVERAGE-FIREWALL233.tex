\section{Terminal-portfolio coverage firewall}
A \emph{terminal policy} either emits a physically explicit exact ordinary-SAT
child, returns a source-verified exact label, or returns \textsc{unresolved}.
The controller may adapt, cache, recompute and dovetail any finite or polynomial
number of such policies.  A \emph{completed fallback call} invokes the supplied
guest on a physical query and waits for its label.  The statement below does not
apply to a simulation that is safely preempted before completion.

\begin{theorem}[Unavoidable fallback firewall]
Assume $P=NP$ and fix a correct polynomial ordinary-SAT decider $B$ with time
exponent $K_0$.  Let $C$ be a fixed wrapper-oblivious black-box compiler claiming
the time inequality in (1) for every correct polynomial guest.  Suppose that
there are $\eta>\lambda$ such that, for every sufficiently large integer $D$,
infinitely many source lengths $N$ admit an input $F_{N,D}$ on which no terminal
policy returns and $C^{A_D}$ unavoidably completes a fallback call on a physical
ordinary-SAT query $G_{N,D}$ of length
\[
       |G_{N,D}|\ge N^{\eta-o(1)}.                                \tag{2}
\]
Here $A_D$ spends $|G|^D$ dummy transitions and then runs $B(G)$.  Then the
claimed time inequality is false.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose
\[
 D>\max\left\{K_0,\frac{\alpha}{\eta},
                    \frac{\gamma}{\eta-\lambda}\right\}.
\]
The wrapper $A_D$ is one fixed uniform total correct polynomial SAT decider and
has time exponent $K_D=D$.  On the promised infinite sequence the completed
call alone requires
\[
 |G_{N,D}|^D\ge N^{\eta D-o(D)}.
\]
But the claimed exponent is at most
$\max\{\alpha,\gamma+\lambda D\}$.  The choice of $D$ gives both
$\eta D>\alpha$ and $\eta D>\gamma+\lambda D$, a contradiction.
All policy failures and switching before fallback are nonnegative extra work.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Parent-copy fallback]
If an uncovered infinite family inevitably calls the guest on the unchanged
source, then $\eta=1$.  For every $\lambda<1$, certified terminal policies on
other inputs do not establish (1).  The uncovered family must instead be decided
locally, mapped to power-short exact children, or otherwise contracted without
that completed call.
\end{corollary}

This is not a SAT lower bound.  It is a necessary condition for this black-box
architecture and a direct portfolio specialization of the already verified
DELAY-query firewall.  It explicitly permits a compiler that proves the wrapper
irrelevant, preempts it, contracts the fallback itself, or constructs a
source-only solver.  The delay counter needs only $O(\log |G|)$ writable bits;
therefore the theorem attacks the time recurrence and does not by itself enter
the simultaneous FLMV forbidden region.
