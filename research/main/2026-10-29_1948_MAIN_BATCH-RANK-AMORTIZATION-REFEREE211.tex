\section{PROPOSE: the missing lemma and falsifiers}

The current missing lemma was a sound amortized invariant for a reusable summary of
\[
F=A(X,Y)\land B(X,Z),\qquad f_A(x)=\exists y\,A(x,y),
\]
when the retained source may be reread and observations may be cached across several future contexts.  The falsifiable proposal was:

\begin{quote}
For a fixed batch of future contexts, the unavoidable binary information is governed by the number of distinct \emph{batch answer vectors}; in the linear-parity control this number is $2^{\rank W}$.  It is not generally the number of contexts times a one-context cost.
\end{quote}

Four mechanisms were tested against it: (i) arbitrary nonlinear persisted summaries; (ii) adaptive source probes and persistent caching; (iii) complementary XOR facts and duplicate facts; and (iv) representation changes, including retaining the original source.  The resulting theorem survives all four, but only because its machine and transcript hypotheses are explicit.

\section{BUILD: an explicit batch model}

\subsection{Machine and resource ledger}

Fix a finite source class $\mathcal A$ and a fixed ordered batch of future contexts $\mathcal B=(B_1,\ldots,B_M)$.  Each $A\in\mathcal A$ determines a binary answer vector
\[
r_A=(\operatorname{Ans}(A,B_1),\ldots,\operatorname{Ans}(A,B_M)).
\]
The native model is a deterministic indexed machine with binary source cells.  A preprocessing phase, before the batch continuation begins, probes at most $P_{\rm src}$ source cells and leaves a source-dependent persisted state of at most $L$ bits.  The continuation sees the fixed batch, may adaptively probe a total of at most $Q_{\rm src}$ source cells over the \emph{whole batch}, and may cache or recompute without restriction.  All source-dependent side channels---including a head location, a copied cell, a branch-dependent representation, or mutable storage---count toward the persisted state.

For a full execution we record
\[
\mathfrak R=(P_{\rm total},P_{\rm src},L,Q_{\rm total},Q_{\rm src},W,R,H,D;\mathsf{access}),
\]
where $W$ is peak workspace, $R$ communication rounds, $H$ physical head crossings, $D$ cumulative symbol changes, and $\mathsf{access}$ names the source-access model.  The theorem controls only $P_{\rm src},L,Q_{\rm src}$ in the binary indexed model.  Query parsing, output, arithmetic, verification, failed policies, and reconstruction remain in $P_{\rm total},Q_{\rm total},W$ and are not erased by the theorem.

\begin{theorem}[batch answer-vector transcript bound]\label{thm:batch}
Let
\[
R_{\mathcal A,\mathcal B}=\bigl|\{r_A:A\in\mathcal A\}\bigr|.
\]
If the machine above outputs every $r_A$ exactly, then
\[
2^{\min(P_{\rm src},L)+Q_{\rm src}}\ge R_{\mathcal A,\mathcal B},
\qquad
\min(P_{\rm src},L)+Q_{\rm src}\ge
\left\lceil\log_2R_{\mathcal A,\mathcal B}\right\rceil.
\]
More precisely, if $M_P$ persisted states are reachable after preprocessing, then
\[
M_P2^{Q_{\rm src}}\ge R_{\mathcal A,\mathcal B}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The deterministic preprocessing decision tree has depth at most $P_{\rm src}$, hence at most $2^{P_{\rm src}}$ leaves.  Its persisted state has at most $2^L$ possible values.  Therefore $M_P\le 2^{\min(P_{\rm src},L)}$.  Starting from any fixed persisted state, a deterministic adaptive continuation making at most $Q_{\rm src}$ binary source probes has at most $2^{Q_{\rm src}}$ answer transcripts.  The output vector is a deterministic function of the persisted state, fixed batch, and transcript.  Exactness requires different batch answer vectors to occupy different pairs, so $R_{\mathcal A,\mathcal B}\le M_P2^{Q_{\rm src}}$.  Taking logarithms proves the claim.
\end{proof}

This theorem permits arbitrary nonlinear encodings, representations, adaptivity, caching, and recomputation.  Its restrictions are still material.  A $w$-bit cell changes the transcript factor from $2^{Q_{\rm src}}$ to at most $2^{wQ_{\rm src}}$.  A joint solver that already sees $B$ during preprocessing has different quantifiers.  A sequential tape can encode information in head position unless that position is charged.  Sending or storing the original formula is legal and appears explicitly in $L$ and construction cost.

\subsection{Exact linear-parity specialization}

Let the source be $a\in\F_2^b$, and let the fixed batch ask for $Wa$, where $W\in\F_2^{M\times b}$ has rank $r$.

\begin{corollary}[rank lower bound]\label{cor:rank}
Every exact protocol in the preceding model satisfies
\[
\min(P_{\rm src},L)+Q_{\rm src}\ge r.
\]
For the coordinate batch $W=[I_r\;0]$, every integer point $0\le L\le r$ is attained with
\[
P_{\rm src}=L,qquad Q_{\rm src}=r-L.
\]
Thus the bound is exact there even against nonlinear summaries and adaptive continuation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The image of $W$ has $2^r$ vectors, so Theorem~\ref{thm:batch} gives the lower bound.  For the upper bound, preprocessing reads and stores any $L$ of the $r$ requested coordinates.  Continuation reads each remaining requested coordinate once and emits all $r$ answers.  The same single uniform program handles every source.
\end{proof}

For a full-rank batch, $r=b$.  For $r<b$, rank remains a lower bound, but the exact frontier may depend on the geometry/locality of the row space relative to raw coordinates.  That refinement is \status{OPEN}.

\subsection{A concrete falsifier: cross-query caching}

Suppose $M$ parity masks over the same retained $b$-bit source arrive as one batch.  Reading every raw source bit once and retaining it permits all $M$ parities to be evaluated.  Hence total raw source probes are at most $b$, regardless of $M$.  It is false to multiply a valid one-query probe bound by $M$ without proving that probes cannot be shared.

This does not make the whole computation free.  Explicit masks require $Mb$ query-input bits, $M$ output bits, and arithmetic work.  It only retires the claimed product lower bound on \emph{raw source probes}.  In the finite control at $b=64,M=256$, independent fresh evaluation would inspect $6235$ selected source positions, while persistent caching inspects $64$ distinct source cells; the $16{,}384$ mask-input bits and $256$ output bits remain charged.

\section{Contextual value repaired by affine rank}

The notation $I\mid_{v}$ denotes a datum with contextual value subscript; the pipe is neither conditional probability nor divisibility.  Values are relative to a state $s$, known facts $K$, a target batch, and a declared cost model $C$.

Let the current certified source set be the affine space $a_0+U\subseteq\F_2^b$.  For a fixed linear batch $W$, define the independently computable potential
\[
\Phi_W(a_0+U)=\dim W(U).
\]
For certified information $I$, define
\[
v_{\Phi_W}(I\mid s)=\Phi_W(s)-\Phi_W(s\text{ updated with }I),
\]
and separately charge acquisition, verification, and application cost $C_s(I)$.  The ratio $v/\max(1,C_s(I))$ is only a heuristic unless an amortized theorem proves otherwise.

\begin{lemma}[one affine fact has value zero or one]\label{lem:value}
If $I$ is a consistent certified linear fact $c\cdot a=\beta$, then
\[
v_{\Phi_W}(I\mid s)\in\{0,1\}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The updated direction space is $U'=U\cap\ker c$, whose codimension in $U$ is at most one.  The quotient map from $W(U)$ to $W(U)/W(U')$ is generated by the image of at most one lost direction.  Thus $\dim W(U)-\dim W(U')\in\{0,1\}$.
\end{proof}

This potential handles several red-team controls exactly.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Redundancy.} Repeating an already implied fact has value $0$.
\item \textbf{XOR complementarity.} If the target is $u\oplus v$, the separate observations of two shares can have singleton values $0,0$ from the initial context, while their joint value is $1$; after the first share is known, the contextual marginal of the second is $1$.  Therefore ordinal singleton labels are not additive.
\item \textbf{Affine compression.} Row reduction computes $\Phi_W$ and removes duplicates without granting a witness or answer bit.
\item \textbf{Costly high-value information.} A fact of value $1$ may have arbitrarily large acquisition or certification cost; useful progress and the expense of obtaining it are distinct coordinates.
\item \textbf{Negative value.} Under this feasibility potential, consistent restriction cannot have negative value.  Under a cost-aware policy score, however, storing or verifying an irrelevant fact can have negative net score because resource cost rises while $\Phi_W$ does not fall.
\end{itemize}

Pointwise bounded progress does \emph{not} survive arbitrary nonlinear feasible sets.  The LSPV-4 Horn control has a rare branch in which one fact collapses a large support set, so any claim that every Boolean fact reduces log-support by at most one is \status{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}.  Expected information under a stated prior is a different quantity and cannot replace a worst-case endpoint argument.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}

The answer-vector counting idea partially overlaps the previous LSPV-5 M\"obius-dividend accounting and standard one-way communication counting.  The new contribution within this project is the exact amortized source-probe statement with retained-source caching, its rank specialization, and the explicit falsification of query-wise multiplication.  It does not supersede the LSPV-1/2 single-query volume theorem or LSPV-4 sequential travel theorem; those solve different models.

No contradiction was found between the peer theorems after their quantifiers were aligned.  The apparent contradiction---large one-query probe cost versus $b$ probes for many queries---is resolved by reuse: a cached source cell can serve many answers.

The root synthesis should now distinguish three independent axes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item single-query summary/probe volume, controlled for parity by $2^LV(b,Q)\ge2^b$;

\section{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND}

Any claim that $M$ future contexts inherently require $M$ times a one-context source cost is stale without a direct-sum or no-reuse hypothesis.  Any claim that a small summary follows from answer-vector counting must also charge its construction and acknowledge that retaining the original $A$ is legal.  Conversely, the counting theorem alone cannot force a summary exponential in the source encoding length: there are at most $2^{N+1}$ binary descriptions of length at most $N$.

\section{Final claim and next lemma}

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, exact scope.}  In the deterministic indexed binary-source-cell model with at most $P_{\rm src}$ preprocessing probes, at most $L$ persisted source-dependent bits, and at most $Q_{\rm src}$ continuation probes across an entire fixed batch, exact computation of $R$ distinct batch answer vectors requires
\[
\min(P_{\rm src},L)+Q_{\rm src}\ge\lceil\log_2R\rceil.
\]
For a full-rank linear parity batch, the right side is $b$; for coordinate batches the whole line $P_{\rm src}=L$, $Q_{\rm src}=b-L$ is attainable.  Consequently, multiplying a single-query probe lower bound by batch size is \status{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} when persistent caching and retained-source access are permitted.

\textbf{Next specific lemma.}  Prove or falsify an exact generalized-support/locality refinement for rank-$r<b$ parity batches under arbitrary nonlinear fixed-length summaries and adaptive raw-coordinate probes; only after that should an authenticated SAT-context transport be attempted.
