\noindent\textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN.}  This report proves an exact
polynomial kernelization for 2-CNF plus $k$ ternary clauses and shows that it
does not transport the implication-chain lower bound.  It is an algorithmic
upper bound and a transport counterexample, not a superpolynomial lower bound.

\section{Falsifiable mechanisms proposed before source review}
Three mechanisms were tested: (i) existentially project every variable absent
from the ternary clauses while preserving the complete boundary relation;
(ii) compose guarded implication chains in an attempt to make the $k$ ternary
blocks multiplicatively sensitive; and (iii) transport the exact chain probe
bound through the projection while charging construction, target simulation,
retained source access, and peak space.  Mechanism (i) succeeds as a compact
upper bound.  Mechanisms (ii)--(iii) fail to amplify the lower bound.

\section{Declared encoding and resource model}
Use the LADDER87 explicit clause-record encoding.  Variables are densely
numbered after unused variables are removed, every clause has four words
(arity and three signed-literal slots), and
\[
  w=\Theta(\log(n+m)),\qquad N=(2+4m)w,
\]
where $n$ is the variable count and $m$ the clause count.  One addressed word
operation costs one.  Source parsing, reads, implication-graph construction,
failed SCC tests, output writes, renumbering, retained maps, and peak live
space are charged.  No formula-evaluation circuit is reinterpreted as a SAT
solver lower bound.

Let
\[
 F(X,Y)=T(X)\wedge B(X,Y),
\]
where $B$ is 2-CNF and $T$ consists of at most $k$ clauses of width at most
three.  Define $X$ to be exactly the variables occurring in $T$, let
$s=|X|\le3k$, and put every remaining variable in $Y$.

\section{Exact projection theorem}
For satisfiable $B$, define $Q_B(X)$ to be the conjunction of every
non-tautological unit or binary clause over $X$ entailed by $B$.  If $B$ is
unsatisfiable, use a fixed FALSE kernel.

\begin{lemma}[Closure under elimination]\label{lem:closure}
The existential projection $\exists Y\,B(X,Y)$ is equivalent to a 2-CNF over
$X$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Eliminate one variable $y$ by Davis--Putnam resolution.  Delete clauses
containing $y$ or $\neg y$ and add every non-tautological resolvent of a
clause $(y\vee a)$ with a clause $(\neg y\vee b)$.  The resolvent
$(a\vee b)$ has width at most two, and the standard two-direction extension
argument shows that the new formula is exactly $\exists y\,B$.  Repeating for
all variables in $Y$ preserves 2-CNF and yields the stated projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact ternary-boundary kernel]\label{thm:kernel}
For every assignment $\alpha$ to $X$,
\[
 \alpha\models Q_B
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists\beta\in\{0,1\}^{Y}\; B(\alpha,\beta).
\]
Consequently
\[
 F\in\mathrm{SAT}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 T(X)\wedge Q_B(X)\in\mathrm{SAT}.
\]
There are exactly $2s^2$ possible non-tautological unit/binary clauses over
$X$, so the kernel has at most
\[
  k+2s^2\le k+18k^2
\]
clauses on at most $3k$ variables.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:closure}, let $P(X)$ be an exact 2-CNF representation of
$\exists Y\,B$.  Every clause of $P$ is entailed by $B$ and therefore occurs
in $Q_B$ after tautologies and duplicates are removed.  Hence every assignment
satisfying $Q_B$ satisfies $P$ and extends to a model of $B$.  Conversely,
every extendible assignment satisfies every clause entailed by $B$, hence
satisfies $Q_B$.  Conjoining the unchanged $T$ proves equisatisfiability and
preserves both SAT and UNSAT labels.

There are $2s$ unit clauses.  Among pairs of the $2s$ literals, exclude the
$s$ complementary pairs; this leaves
$\binom{2s}{2}-s=2s(s-1)$ binary clauses.  The total is $2s^2$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Charged construction.}
First run one SCC test on $B$.  For every candidate clause $C$, run SCC on
$B\wedge\neg C$, where $\neg C$ supplies one or two unit clauses.  Include
$C$ exactly when this test is UNSAT.  Thus there are at most $1+2s^2$ SCC
tests and word time
\[
 R(N,k)=O\bigl((1+s^2)(n+m)\bigr)=O(k^2N/w).
\]
After dense renumbering, target length is
\[
 g(N,k)=O(k^2\log(k+2))\ \text{bits},
\]
plus a retained $O(k\log(n+2))$-bit source-label map only if witness lifting
is requested.  Materialized output and a reusable SCC workspace use
$O(n+m+s^2)$ words.  Counting the read-only source, peak live storage is
$O(N+k^2w)$ bits.  Streaming the output reduces auxiliary workspace but does
not erase output-write time.

\section{Transport consequence and gradual ladder}
For one target call, the required inequality is
\[
 L_{\rm old}(N)\le R(N,k)+H(N,k)+\tau(N)T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g(N,k)).
\]
The implication-chain baseline supplies only
$L_{\rm old}=\Omega(N/w)$ word probes (or $q$ orientation fields), whereas
$R=O(k^2N/w)$ already has input order even for constant positive $k$.
Therefore the numerator $L_{\rm old}-R-H$ is not provably positive.  The
projection gives no transported target lower bound.  Direct inclusion in a
larger language preserves only the original input-access order and does not
amplify it.

reasons for mandatory $2^k$ work.

deterministic exact decider of the fixed NP-complete endpoint.

Roles 1--3 should replace any residual measure that counts $Y$ or the original
2-CNF bulk by a charged invariant of the projected kernel $T\wedge Q_B$.
The highest-value missing lemma is an all-decider compatibility dichotomy on
the graph-coloring-derived kernels: for every uniform exact decider, either
constructing/validating a compressed representation of $T\wedge Q_B$ or
continuing exact SAT decision from it has superpolynomial worst-case charged
time (or a simultaneously forbidden time--space point).  It must survive
source inspection, treewidth/separator dynamic programming, affine parts,
caching, alternate encodings, and decide-first normalization.  This arrow is
OPEN and is essentially the substantive lower-bound problem, not a consequence
of the kernel theorem.
