\begin{lemma}[Per-mode shortcut fails]
Let $n\ge3$, and define $e_iA_0=e_{i+1}$ if $i$ is even and zero otherwise, $e_iA_1=e_{i+1}$ if $i$ is odd and zero otherwise, with shifts beyond $n-1$ zero. \begin{proof} The individual powers terminate immediately or after one step; alternating $A_0,A_1,\ldots$ produces $e_0,e_1,\ldots,e_{n-1}$, so any common invariant space has rank $n$.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Matched-oracle obstruction (separate from the positive theorem).}
