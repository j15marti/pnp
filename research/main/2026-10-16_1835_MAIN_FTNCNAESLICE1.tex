\section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
\paragraph{A: NAE exact slice as an alternative to the 3-SAT slice.}
Exploit the fact that the folding-tetrahedron clause gadget implements NAE directly. Seek an exact-density NAE family that is NP-complete without padding and lies below a rigorous random satisfiability threshold.

\paragraph{B: even-Fourier/HD collapse.}
Because NAE is invariant under simultaneous complementation, test whether the assignment-by-clause incidence matrix loses all odd Fourier degrees. Prediction: the order-$r$ tensor space is the even Fourier subspace through degree $2r$, smaller than the SAT degree-$3r$ space.

\paragraph{C: asymptotic/infinite moment series.}
Exploit the symmetry of NAE so that the second-moment saddle occurs at overlap $1/2$. Compare this with the ordinary 3-SAT slice, where fixed-order moments do not by themselves isolate UNSAT.

\paragraph{D: transfer only proof-relevant FTNC structure.}
Use the local perfect-matching gadget only through its exact NAE quotient. Retire raw $3^m$ fold-count and order-dependent OBDD width as hardness evidence. Retain linear/4-regular hypergraph incidence and high-girth signed-cover constructions only when they constrain the quotient.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
Darmann--D\"ocker prove that Monotone NAE-3-SAT remains NP-complete when every clause has exactly three distinct positive variables, the formula is linear, and every variable occurs exactly four times. Hence every hard source instance on $q$ variables has exactly $t=4q/3$ clauses.

Achlioptas--Moore show that random NAE-3-SAT and random 3-uniform hypergraph 2-colorability are satisfiable with high probability below a rigorously established density at least $3/2$; their second-moment analysis is symmetric in the assignment overlap. Thus density $4/3$ lies strictly inside a proved satisfiable regime.

Porschen et al.\ and later work confirm that NAE-SAT/hypergraph bicolorability remains NP-complete under strong linearity restrictions, supporting the relevance of the sparse geometric source rather than a dense artifact.

The perfect-matching association scheme is mathematically available, but the FTNC clause gadget has only three local matchings and existentially eliminates to one NAE predicate. Therefore the full matching association scheme is not adopted as a hardness object. The relevant quotient is the NAE constraint system; local fold choices form implementation/witness multiplicity unless a later theorem proves otherwise.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}

\subsection{Exact density-$4/3$ NAE slice}
For each 3-set of variables there are four distinct signed NAE predicates, because
\[
\operatorname{NAE}(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)
=
\operatorname{NAE}(\neg\ell_1,\neg\ell_2,\neg\ell_3).
\]
Let
\[
M(q)=4\binom q3,\qquad t(q)=\frac{4q}{3},
\]
on the subsequence $3\mid q$.

Define the total slice function $V_q:\{0,1\}^{M(q)}\to\{0,1\}$ by
\[
V_q(z)=
\begin{cases}
0,&|z|<t(q),\\
1,&|z|>t(q),\\
1,&|z|=t(q)\text{ and the selected NAE formula is UNSAT},\\
0,&|z|=t(q)\text{ and it is SAT}.
\end{cases}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact-density NAE hardness]
The complement of $V_q$ restricted to the critical layer is NP-complete. In particular the critical-layer UNSAT language is coNP-complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use Darmann--D\"ocker's NP-complete Monotone NAE-3-SAT-E4 source. If every variable occurs exactly four times and every clause contains three distinct variables, counting incidences gives $4q=3t$, hence $t=4q/3$ exactly. Positive NAE clauses are a subset of the signed NAE coordinate universe, so every source instance lies on the declared exact layer. Membership in NP is immediate.
\end{proof}

Thus the same Valiant/Wegener spine applies:
\[
C_{\rm mon}(V_q)=M(q)^{\omega(1)}
\Longrightarrow
P\ne NP.
\]

\subsection{Transfer to the folding-tetrahedron problem}
In the previously established FTNC clause gadget, one tetrahedron has ports
\[
s,\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3
\]
and three legal $K_4$ perfect matchings
\[
M_j=\{(s,\ell_j),(\ell_k,\ell_\ell)\}.
\]
The selected literal is forced true and the remaining pair is forbidden from both being true. Therefore a legal local fold/coloring exists exactly when the three literal values are not all equal.

\begin{corollary}[NAE-to-FTNC semantic quotient]
Replacing each NAE clause by one FTNC clause tetrahedron and using the established occurrence-consistency wiring preserves existence of a solution. Hence the density-$4/3$ NAE hard family maps linearly to a restricted FTNC family.
\end{corollary}

This identifies a proof-relevant FTNC slice: select exactly $4q/3$ signed clause tetrahedra from the possible NAE-coordinate universe and ask whether the resulting folding/coloring instance has no legal solution.

The fold choices themselves are not a lower-bound resource. For one satisfying truth triple, the number of compatible local folds is one or two depending on whether one or two literals are true. Since existentially eliminating the local matching yields exactly NAE, any argument based only on the raw $3^t$ matching-choice count is \RET.

\subsection{Transversal geometry}
For an assignment $a\in\{0,1\}^q$, let $B_a$ be the signed NAE predicates falsified by $a$. Exactly one of the four predicates on each support is falsified, so
\[
|B_a|=\binom q3=\frac{M}{4}.
\]
Moreover $B_a=B_{\bar a}$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact block intersection]
If $d=d_H(a,b)$ then
\[
\boxed{|B_a\cap B_b|=\binom{q-d}{3}+\binom d3.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On a 3-variable support the two assignments falsify the same NAE predicate iff their restrictions are equal or complementary. Equivalently the three coordinates of $a\oplus b$ are all zero or all one. There are $\binom{q-d}{3}$ supports of the first type and $\binom d3$ of the second.
\end{proof}

Thus exact-layer NAE-UNSAT is again a transversal family:
\[
F\text{ is NAE-UNSAT}
\iff
\forall [a]\in\{0,1\}^q/\{a\sim\bar a\},\quad
F\cap B_a\ne\varnothing.
\]
There are only $2^{q-1}$ distinct blocks.

\subsection{Even-Fourier HD hierarchy}
Use $\{\pm1\}$ variables. For a signed NAE predicate on support $\{i,j,k\}$, its falsification indicator has the form
\[
f_C(x)=\frac14
\left(
1+\epsilon_{ij}x_ix_j+\epsilon_{ik}x_ix_k+\epsilon_{jk}x_jx_k
\right),
\]
with $\epsilon_{ij}\epsilon_{ik}\epsilon_{jk}=1$.

\begin{theorem}[Even-Fourier rank]
Let $A$ be the $2^q\times M$ assignment-by-predicate falsification matrix. Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb R}A=1+\binom q2.}
\]
For the row tensor map $\phi_r(a)=A_a^{\otimes r}$,
\[
\boxed{
\dim\operatorname{span}\{\phi_r(a)\}
=
\sum_{j=0}^{\min\{r,\lfloor q/2\rfloor\}}\binom q{2j}.
}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The four sign classes on one support form a Hadamard basis for the constant character and its three pair characters, so the column span is exactly Fourier degrees zero and two. Products of $r$ columns contain only even characters of degree at most $2r$. Conversely every even character of degree $2j\le2r$ factors into $j$ pair characters, each of which lies in the single-column span.
\end{proof}

Hence constant-order HD information has dimension $q^{2r+O(1)}$, and for $r=c\log q$ the dimension is
\[
\exp\!\left((2c+o(1))(\log q)^2\right),
\]
versus the degree-$3r$ hierarchy for the ordinary 3-SAT clause blocks. This makes exact moment/HD searches materially smaller, though not automatically harder for circuits.

\subsection{Depth-two asymptotic lower bounds}
The conjunction of all four NAE predicates on one 3-variable support is UNSAT. Therefore
\[
L_{\rm DNF}\ge\binom{M-4}{t-4}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
\log L_{\rm DNF}
\ge
\frac83 q\log q+O(q).
}
\]

For a monotone CNF clause $Q$ valid on every positive exact-$t$ input, if $|Q|<M/4$ then its complement contains more than three coordinates per support on average; some support contains all four NAE predicates. Since the complement is much larger than $t$, that four-predicate contradiction extends inside the complement to a positive exact-$t$ input avoiding $Q$, contradiction. Hence
\[
|Q|\ge M/4.
\]

At density $4/3$, Achlioptas--Moore's threshold result implies that a uniform random NAE formula is satisfiable with high probability; conditioning on distinct clauses changes probability by $O(1/q)$. Thus
\[
L_{\rm CNF}
\ge
(1-o(1))
\frac{\binom Mt}{\binom{3M/4}{t}}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\log L_{\rm CNF}
\ge
\frac43 q\log\frac43+o(q).
}
\]
Since $M\sim\frac23q^3$, this is
\[
L_{\rm CNF}\ge
\exp\!\left((0.4390850208+o(1))M^{1/3}\right).
\]

The ratio has the exact convergent expansion
\[
\log\frac{\binom Mt}{\binom{3M/4}{t}}
=
t\log\frac43+
\sum_{m\ge1}
\frac{(4/3)^m-1}{mM^m}
\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}i^m,
\]
whose tail beyond the leading term is $O(t^2/M)=O(1/q)$.

\subsection{Why the NAE second moment is cleaner}
For two assignments at relative Hamming distance $\delta$, the asymptotic probability that one random signed NAE clause is satisfied by both is
\[
p(\delta)
=
\frac12+\frac14\left(\delta^3+(1-\delta)^3\right)
=
\frac34(1-\delta+\delta^2).
\]
For density $c$, the pair exponent is
\[
\Phi_c(\delta)
=
\log2+h(\delta)+c\log p(\delta).
\]
Write $x=\delta(1-\delta)\in(0,1/4]$. Then
\[
\Phi_c''(\delta)
=
-\frac1x+c\frac{1+2x}{(1-x)^2}.
\]
Since
\[
\frac{(1-x)^2}{x(1+2x)}\ge\frac32
\qquad(0<x\le1/4),
\]
we have $\Phi_c''<0$ throughout $(0,1)$ for every $c<3/2$. By symmetry the unique maximum is $\delta=1/2$.

At the hard density $c=4/3$,
\[
\Lambda_1
=
\log2+\frac43\log\frac34
=
0.3095710839\ldots,
\]
and
\[
\Lambda_2
=
2\Lambda_1
=
0.6191421679\ldots.
\]
Thus the second moment is centered at independent overlap on the exponential scale, exactly the symmetry exploited by Achlioptas--Moore. This is analytically cleaner than the ordinary 3-SAT moment series.

\subsection{Relation to previous signed-hypergraph/high-girth work}
The previous project construction of high-girth covers with locally identical SAT/UNSAT NAE neighborhoods transfers naturally to FTNC because each NAE constraint is one clause tetrahedron. It is useful as a hostile control: any proposed local-radius, local-motif, or bounded-order folding invariant must fail on such pairs once the girth exceeds the observation radius.

It is not a final hard family by itself. The graph-parity/holonomy examples used there admit polynomial global algorithms, so local indistinguishability alone cannot imply the required circuit lower bound.

A more promising use is to seek high-girth lifts of the NP-hard linear 4-regular NAE source while preserving the density-$4/3$ exact layer. That would combine:
\[
\text{NP-hardness}
+
\text{linear 4-regular incidence}
+
\text{growing local indistinguishability},
\]
and would be directly representable by FTNC clause tetrahedra. This remains \OPEN.

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
A finite exact rank audit for $q=3,\ldots,8$ gave
\[

\]
matching $1+\binom q2$. Tensor ranks for $r=1,2,3$ matched
\[
\sum_{j\le r}\binom q{2j}

\paragraph{Assessment.}
The folding-tetrahedron problem does help the current proof program, but through its NAE quotient rather than through raw fold enumeration. The density-$4/3$ signed NAE slice is arguably a cleaner competitor to the density-3 ordinary SAT slice: NP-hardness lands on the exact layer without padding, the uniform layer lies inside a rigorous satisfiable regime, the assignment-block geometry is complement-symmetric, and the HD/tensor hierarchy loses all odd Fourier degrees.

The remaining load-bearing theorem is still a superpolynomial monotone/set-circuit lower bound. The two most promising next targets are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item adapt SLICESETTRANS1/SLICEBONFERRONI1 to the NAE block system and test whether the even-Fourier geometry yields a mass-production-resistant set-circuit invariant;
\item construct or locate high-girth linear 4-regular NAE hard families, then test whether their signed-orbit moment polytope matches the uniform layer through $r=\Theta(\log q)$.
\end{enumerate}
