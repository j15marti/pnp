\section{Exact obstruction and falsifiable mechanisms}
The only canonical inputs here are valid proper duplicate-free D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) originals: every source clause has three distinct literals of one sign, every original variable occurs exactly twice positively and twice negatively, duplicate clauses are forbidden, and malformed strings are rejected. Residual CNFs, circuits, and Tseitin encodings are ordinary noncanonical derived objects.

The obstruction is a quantifier and representation error: a ternary clause that survives unit propagation in one precompiled auxiliary encoding need not be necessary continuation work for deciding the original source. A paid source scan can discover that its original clause is already satisfied, discard the private clause cone, and compile the exact remaining original-variable continuation function. The following mechanisms were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(H1)] Every precompiled Tseitin ternary surviving original pins represents necessary future decision work. \textbf{FALSIFIED} on 952 source-width-at-most-two F9 pin cubes.
\item[(H2)] Every exact continuation representation must retain all precompiled auxiliary cones. \textbf{FALSIFIED} by the theorem below in the declared source-private architecture.
\item[(H3)] The source-live rebuild is free or decides residual SAT. \textbf{FALSIFIED}: it requires paid authentication, source reads, clause liveness and output writes, and it only represents the residual.
\item[(H4)] A finite or architecture-specific circuit saving yields an arbitrary-machine lower bound or strict FLMV transfer. \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: no such transfer is supplied.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Definitions: decision equivalence is weaker than continuation equivalence}
Let $F$ be any CNF over original variables and let $p$ be a coherent partial original-variable assignment. A source scan forms $R_p$ by deleting every source clause already satisfied by $p$ and deleting every falsified pinned literal from each surviving clause. An empty surviving clause yields the constant \textsc{false}; no surviving clauses yields \textsc{true}. Otherwise $R_p$ is a nonempty ordinary CNF. For every assignment $y$ to the unpinned original variables,
\[
 F(p,y)=R_p(y).
\]
Thus $R_p$ has paid exact \emph{continuation equivalence}, not merely the same current SAT/UNSAT bit. A post-answer constant $0$ or $1$ has the current bit but does not give this construction or its acquisition cost.

Define the \emph{clause-private residual architecture} as follows. Signed original literals and constants are free inputs. Each residual clause $C$ is computed by its own fan-in-two OR tree; no gate is shared between distinct clauses. The clause outputs are combined by a fan-in-two AND tree. Let $L(R)=\sum_{C\in R}|C|$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact source-live residual gate count]
For every CNF $F$ and coherent original-variable pin $p$, source restriction computes an exact pointwise continuation $R_p$. If $R_p$ is nonterminal, the exact number of binary gates in the declared clause-private architecture is
\[
 \boxed{g_{\rm private}(R_p)=L(R_p)-1.}
\]
The compiler takes $O(N+L(R_p))$ word operations after a declared random-access source representation, including a complete source scan and every gate/output write. Constants use zero binary gates. This is an exact topology-specific count and polynomial compilation upper bound, not an unrestricted circuit minimum or a SAT decision algorithm.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $q$ be the number of surviving clauses. A private binary OR tree for a clause of length $k\ge1$ uses exactly $k-1$ gates: $k-1$ gates suffice by a tree, and fewer cannot connect $k$ independent leaves in a fan-in-two private tree. The final binary AND tree uses exactly $q-1$ gates. Therefore
\[
 \sum_{C\in R_p}(|C|-1)+(q-1)=L(R_p)-1.
\]
The source restriction identity follows clause by clause: a clause satisfied by $p$ is constantly true; every other clause has exactly its false pinned literals removed. Scanning all source literal records and writing the resulting private trees gives the stated upper bound. Terminal true/false cases require no binary gate, although discovering them is still charged.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Dead private cones are not necessary continuation state]
If a precompiled source-private circuit has source-clause provenance, every private cone for a clause satisfied by $p$ can be deleted after paid liveness discovery. Rebuilding the conjunction over the remaining clause outputs produces a circuit pointwise equal to $F|p$ on all future original-variable assignments. Auxiliary clauses left by unit propagation in a full-biconditional Tseitin CNF therefore do not by themselves lower-bound exact source continuation work.
\end{corollary}
The corollary does not say that arbitrary shared circuits can be minimized cheaply. Gate sharing, Boolean equivalence, and unrestricted circuit minimization are deliberately outside the theorem.

\section{Charged work, peak space, and exact gap}
One materialized rebuild costs
\[
 T_{\rm rebuild}(F,p)=\Theta(T_{\rm grammar}+N_{\rm source\ reads}+L(R_p)_{\rm writes})
\]
in the declared word model. Sequential tape heads, indirect addresses, variable-length records and index jumps must be charged according to their actual implementation. If rebuild is attempted at many search states, the cumulative cost is the sum over every visited state and may be exponential. For an adaptive controller $D$,
\[
\begin{split}
T_D(z)={}&T_{\rm grammar,source,index/head}(z)
+\sum_{r\in\mathrm{attempted}(z)}T_{\rm discover,test,fail,switch}(z,r)\\
&+T_{\rm rebuild,semantic,proof,GE/SCC,cache,replay,guest}(z)
+\sum_{y\in E(z)}\bigl(T_{\rm child/output/bit}(z,y)+T_D(y)\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
Peak writable space includes all simultaneously live ancestor frames, source/index/head state, residual and gate buffers, proof/GE/SCC arrays, cache/equality records, children, and guests. Materializing the private circuit uses $O(L(R_p)\log N)$ bits under ordinary named-record encoding. RECOMP can avoid retaining it only by paying source rereads; COPY pays for every copied record; DELAY may add time without data-bit changes; DEAD cones may be removed only after their source liveness is acquired. Logical XOR is an offline diagnostic unless its computation is explicitly charged.

The exact gap is unchanged: no well-founded all-input potential bounds the sum of all rebuilds, failed policies and successors polynomially. There is no proof that every valid canonical original reaches a tractable residual cheaply. No arbitrary correct polynomial decider $A$ is transformed into a power-short $\Phi(A)$, and no ordinary-SAT-to-D\"ocker reduction has been matched to FLMV's indexed read-only-input machine with a strict numerical exponent inequality. NP-completeness and linear representation size do not fill that bridge.
