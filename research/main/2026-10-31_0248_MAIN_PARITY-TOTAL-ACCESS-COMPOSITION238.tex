\section{Target, falsifiers, and exact scope}
The active target remains one uniform, fully charged, stateful residual-SAT
controller that turns every fixed total exact deterministic ordinary-SAT
decider $A$ into a same-machine decider entering the actual FLMV forbidden
region.  This run does not introduce another route.  It asks whether the new
LSPV-2 canonical parity child and LSPV-4 paid source authentication satisfy the
already verified complete-total-accessor theorem of MAIN226.

Four falsifiers were fixed before the proof.
\begin{description}
\item[F1.] The peer parity accessor is only a valid-domain fragment, so an
arbitrary guest can distinguish it after an out-of-range jump.  \textbf{FALSIFIED:}
the exact length is computable and the accessor is totalized by returning the
ordinary blank symbol outside the interval.
\item[F2.] Even with a total accessor, the arbitrary fixed guest's input tape
remains an independent OPEN bridge on this family.  \textbf{FALSIFIED:} the
complete total virtual-input theorem composes directly, with all logical-head,
index, source, descriptor, accessor, and guest tapes charged.
\item[F3.] The resulting restricted contraction proves the all-input ARC
recurrence.  \textbf{FALSIFIED as an inference:} a malformed or nonmember
source must use exact fallback, which may run $A$ on the parent of length $N$;
the child family is not closed under the same validator.
\item[F4.] LSPV-4's $q$-block probe lower bound obstructs even the claimed
$N^{11/10}$ setup exponent.  \textbf{FALSIFIED:} the lower bound is
$q=\Omega(N/\log N)$ on the fixed-width encoding and is met by one paid
$O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ scan with $O(\log N)$ retained state.  It forbids
free authentication, not linear-time amortization.
\end{description}

\section{The restricted complete-access composition}
Let $\mathcal P_{b,q}$ be the balanced canonical proper-3CNF source family of
LSPV-4 report268.  It has $q\ge2$ disjoint-private cumulative-XOR components
sharing boundary $x_1,\ldots,x_b$.  Component $i$ has terminal pin
$r_i\in\{0,1\}$ and a private opposite-sign balancing pin.  The latter makes
the native variable-width ASCII-DIMACS block length independent of $r_i$ but
does not change the boundary projection.  A complete canonical grammar scan
validates every original clause and emits
\[
 D(F)=(b,E),\qquad E=\{r_i:1\le i\le q\}\subseteq\{0,1\}.
\]
The LSPV-2 child $G_D$ contains one fresh-private parity chain for each value
in $E$.  Its native DIMACS length is $M=O(b\log b)$ because $|E|\le2$.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted arbitrary-guest parity composition]
Fix any total deterministic indexed random-access ordinary-SAT decider $A$.
Suppose its all-input resource bounds are $T_A(m)$ time and $S_A(m)$ counted
workspace.  There is one uniform same-model machine which, on every valid
$F\in\mathcal P_{b,q}$ of physical length $N$, returns exactly $A(G_D)$ and
hence exactly $\SAT(F)$, on both labels, within
\[
 T(N)=O\!\left(N\operatorname{polylog}N+
 T_A(M)\operatorname{polylog}N\right),
 \qquad
 S(N)=O\!\left(\log N+S_A(M)+\log(T_A(M)+2)\right).
 \tag{1}
\]
The machine is not given $K$, the source label, a best policy, or a source-
dependent advice string.  If $T_A(m)=m^{K+o(1)}$, $S_A(m)=m^{L+o(1)}$, and
$M\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, then the restricted exponents satisfy
\[
 K'\le\max\{1,\lambda K\},\qquad
 L'\le\max\{0,\lambda L\}. \tag{2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The grammar validator scans the complete canonical source, checks every gate,
terminal pin, balancing pin, variable identifier, clause boundary, and header,
and retains $b$, the two pin-presence bits, and positional counters.  It takes
$O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bit time and $O(\log N)$ writable space in the
declared indexed-input model.  Exact XOR truth-table induction gives
\[
 \exists P\,F(\beta,P)
 \iff \bigwedge_i\left(\bigoplus_{j=1}^b\beta_j=r_i\right)
 \iff \exists Z\,G_D(\beta,Z).
 \tag{3}
\]
Thus the ordinary child preserves both SAT and UNSAT, with no answer bit
supplied.

LSPV-2 report268 supplies the exact native-DIMACS length and a symbolic
character accessor.  It locates the header or one of at most two chains,
binary-searches the gate prefix using exact decimal digit sums, serializes only
the selected four-clause gate or pin, and returns blank beyond the exact
logical length.  Its descriptor and scratch are $O(\log N)$ bits and its bit
time is $\log^{O(1)}N$.  Therefore all four hypotheses of the complete total
virtual-input transfer theorem in MAIN226 hold.  That theorem simulates each
guest step and logical input-head/index operation, invoking the accessor only
when needed.  Substituting the validator and accessor bounds gives (1).
Equation (2) follows by $M\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$; the fixed $A$ has constant
$K,L$, so $\log T_A(M)=O(\log N)$.  The program never needs to know either
exponent.  Equation (3) makes the simulated answer correct for the original
source on both labels.\qed
\end{proof}

For fixed $b$, $M=O(1)$ and the restricted family is decided in
$N^{1+o(1)}$ time and logarithmic workspace.  When the measured physical
child obeys $M<N^{1/2}$, (2) is stronger than the proposed conditional ARC
coefficients $(11/10,1/2)$ for this promise family.  This is an easy control,
not a universal theorem.

\section{Why this does not enter the FLMV box}
The validator has three outcomes: certified member with descriptor, certified
malformation of that grammar, or incomplete/failed policy.  Exactness on all
ordinary SAT inputs requires an exact fallback on the latter two outcomes.
Calling $A(F)$ there costs $N^{K+o(1)}$ time and $N^{L+o(1)}$ space and is
exposed by DELAY/COPY wrappers.  No verified argument contracts this fallback.
Also $G_D$ is an ordinary parity-chain child, not necessarily another balanced
$q\ge2$ source in $\mathcal P_{b,q}$, so this construction is not hereditary.
The theorem therefore fills only the restricted guest-input arrow after a
successful source certificate.  It supplies no all-input cover, source-
computable terminal partition, or recursive child theorem.

LSPV-4's decision-tree lower bound remains correct: on the all-even source an
exact policy must inspect a changed position in every independently flippable
pin block.  Its $q=\Omega(N/\log N)$ probes are nevertheless dominated by the
one complete authentication scan already present in (1).  Revalidating once
per guest child would be wrong accounting; retaining the two pin-presence bits
and authenticated grammar descriptor avoids that multiplication on this
source.  Conversely, the reusable descriptor cannot be transferred to a
changed source without checking its provenance.

\textbf{Concrete proved claim.}  For the authenticated balanced canonical
parity family, the verified distinct-pin child and total character accessor
compose with every fixed total deterministic indexed random-access SAT guest
with the simultaneous resource bounds (1), preserving both SAT and UNSAT.
This is a restricted easy-family theorem.  It does not establish an all-input
ARC stage, enter the FLMV forbidden region, or resolve P versus NP.
