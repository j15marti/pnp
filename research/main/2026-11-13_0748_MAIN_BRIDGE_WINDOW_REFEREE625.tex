\section*{Theorem 625.1 (bridge window)}
\begin{theorem}[625.1]
Assume \textsf{(PC)}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\textbf{B}_R\wedge\textbf{LB}_R\Rightarrow \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.
\item[(b)] If $\textbf{LB}_R$ is a theorem, then $\textbf{B}_R\Leftrightarrow \mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$. In particular for $R\in\{$Resolution, Res$(k)$, bounded-depth Frege, Polynomial Calculus, Cutting Planes$\}$, proving $\textbf{B}_R$ is exactly as hard as proving P$\ne$NP; it is not a weaker sufficient bridge.
\item[(c)] (Monotonicity.) If $R'$ p-simulates $R$ on 3-CNF refutations, then $\textbf{B}_R\Rightarrow\textbf{B}_{R'}$ and $\textbf{LB}_{R'}\Rightarrow\textbf{LB}_R$. Thus bridges get easier and lower bounds harder as $R$ strengthens; the spine needs one $R$ in both regions.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
(a) If SAT$\in$P then $\textbf{B}_R$ gives a polynomial bound contradicting $\textbf{LB}_R$; SAT is NP-complete (Cook--Levin), so P$\ne$NP. (b) ($\Rightarrow$) is (a). ($\Leftarrow$) If P$\ne$NP then SAT$\notin$P, so the implication $\textbf{B}_R$ holds vacuously. The listed systems have \textsf{(PC)}-cost lower bounds $2^{N^{\Omega(1)}}$ on polynomial-size 3-CNF families (KNOWN THEOREMS above), so $\textbf{LB}_R$ holds. (c) A p-simulation gives $\overline W_{R'}(N)\le q(\overline W_R(N)+N)$ for a polynomial $q$ under \textsf{(PC)}; both implications follow.
\end{proof}
\noindent\textbf{Red-team consequence for the directive.} The instruction ``prioritize the weakest sufficient bridge'' must be read with (b): for every system whose lower bound is currently provable, the bridge \emph{is} the whole problem. The only nontrivial window is a system at least as strong as the extension rule (EF; this includes $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ with paid DEF, which has polynomial PHP proofs per the corpus), where \textbf{B}$_R$ has a known conditional route (Cook 1975 + Pich--Santhanam) and \textbf{LB}$_R$ is a decades-old open problem. A candidate hard family is useful only if it is a candidate against EF; pigeonhole and Tseitin are not (Tseitin collapses under GF(2)/XOR or extension, as already recorded for /341: $W=4h-1$).

\begin{remark}[free optimal sequence]
The directive's clarification is correct and \textbf{PROVED/EXACT}: for any procedure producing a refutation $\pi_A$, $\min_\pi\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi)\le\operatorname{cost}_R(\pi_A)$, so granting the optimal sequence for free only lowers the quantity being bounded below. But \textbf{B}$_R$ is an \emph{upper-bound existence} statement: a polynomial SAT decider outputs a bit, not $\pi$. The missing ingredient is not search cost but existence of short $R$-proofs, i.e.\ that $R$ p-simulates the trivial proof system ``run $A$''. By Cook 1975 this follows for EF when PV proves $A$'s UNSAT answers sound; no such $A$ is known to exist even conditionally on SAT$\in$P. Status: \textbf{OPEN}.
\end{remark}

\section*{Theorem 625.2 (soundness gate for checker-measured minima)}
\begin{theorem}[625.2]
Let $K$ be a checker and $R$ a sound calculus with every valid $R$-refutation accepted by $K$ at no greater cost. Then $\overline W_K\le\overline W_R$ pointwise and $\textbf{LB}_K\Rightarrow\textbf{LB}_R$. If $K$ accepts a refutation of some satisfiable $F$, then $K$ is not a Cook--Reckhow UNSAT proof system, no upper bound or ``certified minimum'' computed from $K$-acceptance is an $R$-quantity, and $\textbf{B}_K$ is not a statement about any sound calculus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Valid $R$-refutations form a subset of $K$-accepted objects, so the $K$-minimum is at most the $R$-minimum for each UNSAT $F$, and the maximum over UNSAT preserves the inequality. A Cook--Reckhow system must accept only members of the language (here UNSAT); acceptance of a SAT source violates this.
\end{proof}
\noindent Applied to the recovered /341 checker (blob \texttt{fcb7d4ce}): lower bounds proved for it would transfer upward, but its reported ``nativeCertifiedMinimum'' values (47, 55, 63, 71, 79 below) are not values of $W_R$ for any sound $R$. Satisfiable sources lie outside the max in $\overline W$, so the false acceptances do not alter the definition of $\overline W_K$; they disqualify $K$ as the fixed $R$ of the directive until P2-D1 repairs pass.
