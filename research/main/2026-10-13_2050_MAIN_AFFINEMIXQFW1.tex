\section{Exact dense-affine quadratic firewall}
Let $X$ be connected, let $T$ be a spanning tree, and let
$C=E(X)\setminus T$ have even cardinality $d=2t>0$.  For consistent charge
$c$, cotree projection is a polynomial-time bijection from
$\{x:B_Xx=c\}$ to $\F^C$.  Write
\[
 q_t(z)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}z_{2i-1}z_{2i},
 \qquad
 Q_{A,r,b}(y)=[q_t(Ay+r)=b],                              \tag{2}
\]
where $A\in\mathrm{GL}_d(\F)$, $r\in\F^d$, and $b\in\F$.

\begin{theorem}[Affine-equivalent full-rank residuals are uniformly easy]
For every such $(X,T,c,A,r,b)$, a satisfying edge assignment for
\[
 B_Xx=c\quad\land\quad Q_{A,r,b}(x_C)                    \tag{3}
\]
is computable in time polynomial in the full encoded input length $N$ and
polynomial workspace.  The projected rank is $d$.  The polar matrix of the
quadratic part of (2) is $A^{\mathsf T}JA$, where $J$ is the block diagonal
matrix with blocks $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$;
hence its rank is also $d$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose $z^*=0$ if $b=0$, and choose
$z^*=e_1+e_2$ if $b=1$.  Then $q_t(z^*)=b$.  Solve
\[
 Ay=z^*+r                                                     \tag{4}
\]
by Gaussian elimination.  Assign these $d$ bits to the cotree edges and solve
the remaining tree-edge values from leaves to the root.  Consistency of $c$
discharges the root equation, so the returned $x$ satisfies (3).  Parsing,
matrix solving, tree discovery, reconstruction, and verification are all
polynomial in $N$.

The cotree map is bijective, so $\rho=d$.  Polarizing $q_t(Ay+r)$ cancels its
affine part and gives
$\beta(y,w)=y^{\mathsf T}A^{\mathsf T}JAw$.  Congruence by invertible $A$
preserves rank, proving $\operatorname{rank}\beta=d$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Concise dense mixing]
For every $d$ divisible by four, set $u=(1,\ldots,1)$, let $v$ be the indicator
of the first $d/2$ coordinates, and set
\[
 A_d=I+uv^{\mathsf T}.                                      \tag{5}
\]
Then $A_d^2=I$, (2) has an $O(d)$-gate compact circuit, and its polar matrix
has
\[
 \frac{d^2/2+d}{d(d-1)}=\frac{d/2+1}{d-1}\longrightarrow\frac12           \tag{6}
\]
of its off-diagonal entries nonzero while retaining rank $d$.  Nevertheless
the witness algorithm above is linear-algebraic and polynomial.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Because $v^{\mathsf T}u=d/2=0$ in $\F$, the rank-one update squares to the
identity.  Multiplication by $A_d$ requires one parity of the first half and
XOR of that bit into all coordinates.  Moreover
\[
 A_d^{\mathsf T}JA_d=J+uv^{\mathsf T}+vu^{\mathsf T}.
\]
The latter adds all ordered cross-half entries; the $d$ entries of $J$ lie
within halves under the paired ordering.  This gives (6).  The theorem then
supplies the witness.
\end{proof}

This corollary removes a possible input-scale objection: the mixing is a fixed
uniform family with a concise description, not an arbitrary $d^2$-bit matrix.
It also shows why expanded ANF density is the wrong cost measure: the same
function has an $O(d)$ compact circuit and an explicit selector.

\section{Hostile controls and barrier audit}
The theorem permits full source access, arbitrary consistent charges, arbitrary
phase, polynomial workspace, compact circuits, Gaussian elimination, parsing,
and witness reconstruction.  Renaming and phase switches are absorbed into
$A,r$; padding and expanded ANF do not create work.  DEAD/COPY/DELAY may alter
traces without affecting the explicit selector, while INTERP/RECOMP can simply
compute (4).  A semantic or algebraic oracle only makes this easy family easier.
Thus the firewall relativizes and algebrizes; it is not a nonrelativizing hinge.
Natural Proofs is not invoked because no constructive large-circuit property is
used.

The result does not show arbitrary systems of quadratic constraints are easy,
does not address a source-matched conjunction of independent nonlinear
relations, and does not solve $L_{22}$.  Conversely, general MQ algorithm papers,
cryptographic hardness, DNNF size, PC size, pebbling, or one-tape lower bounds
cannot be imported as unconditional unrestricted-time lower bounds.
