\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let the clause-coordinate universe have size $N=M(q)=8\binom q3$ and set $p=q/N$. Let $Y$ be any random positive input supported on the exact Hamming layer $\binom{[N]}q$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-layer singleton spread barrier]
If $\Pr[A\subseteq Y]\le\rho^{|A|}$ is required for all singleton sets $A=\{i\}$, then $\rho\ge p=q/N$. Hence the standard closure/trimming calculation using only a generic $p^{-\ell}$-scale small-minterm bound and the spread factor $\rho^\ell$ has no decaying geometric factor on same-weight positive and negative layers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Since $|Y|=q$ always, $\sum_i\Pr[i\in Y]=q$. Some singleton marginal is at least $q/N=p$, while the spread hypothesis bounds every singleton marginal by $\rho$. Thus $\rho\ge p$. The generic union-bound base $\rho/p$ is therefore at least one before its additional polylogarithmic loss. This falsifies only that proof template, not every possible robust-sunflower argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Rare-pattern detector]
Let $\mathcal P$ be a family of $r$-coordinate patterns and suppose every positive input contains some $A\in\mathcal P$. Then
\[D_{\mathcal P}(x)=\bigvee_{A\in\mathcal P}\bigwedge_{i\in A}x_i\]
is a monotone circuit of size $O(r|\mathcal P|)$ accepting all positives, while for uniform exact-$q$ input $X$
\[\Pr[D_{\mathcal P}(X)=1]\le |\mathcal P|\frac{(q)_r}{(N)_r}.\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $A$, a uniform $q$-subset contains it with probability $\binom{N-r}{q-r}/\binom Nq=(q)_r/(N)_r$. Union bound over $\mathcal P$.
\end{proof}

CRITICALPRISM1 parity/Tseitin positives select four signed clauses on every parity support. Put $S=\binom q3$ and $N=8S$. The monotone same-support detector
\[D_2(z)=\bigvee_{C\ne C',\,\mathrm{supp}(C)=\mathrm{supp}(C')}(z_C\wedge z_{C'})\]
has $28S$ terms and accepts every prism positive. For uniform exact-$q$ input,
\[\Pr[D_2(X)=1]\le 28S\frac{q(q-1)}{N(N-1)}=O(1/q),\]
and the exact repeated-support probability is
\[1-\frac{\binom Sq8^q}{\binom{8S}q}.\]
Thus the prism family is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} for the proposed positive distribution. Clause-minimal UNSAT and a large contradiction core are insufficient; the positives must also look locally ordinary to small monotone clause patterns.
