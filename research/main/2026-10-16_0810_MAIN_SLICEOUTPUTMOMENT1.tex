\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
Bennett's reversible simulation explicitly computes, writes the desired output, and then reverses the computation so that the final configuration retains the input and output but no unwanted scratch \cite{Bennett1973}. This directly validates Candidate A as a hostile control, not as a lower-bound mechanism.

Cavalar--Kumar--Rossman's approximation method replaces small monotone circuits by structured approximators using robust sunflowers \cite{CKR2022}. The 2026 derandomized robust-sunflower theorem proves the required sunflower phenomenon under $O(w)$-wise independence and under certain locally independent, approximately identically distributed marginals \cite{CFMSY2026}. Candidate B/D therefore targets moment order proportional to the eventual approximator width, rather than arbitrary full-distribution equality.

Tensor-product sketches can preserve low-degree polynomial-kernel geometry for a finite point set or a declared subspace in dimensions polynomial in the accuracy parameters and logarithmic in the number of tested points \cite{MSW2019,PhamPagh2025}. They do not automatically preserve convex-hull membership over the entire UNSAT family. Candidate C survives only with an exact lift or an injectivity certificate.

The quiet-planting literature shows that local or low-degree indistinguishability can be designed through balanced clause-sign distributions, including recent code-guided $k$-SAT constructions \cite{Quiet2012,Quiet2026}. Those results concern planted satisfying assignments or restricted distinguishers. They are architectural guidance, not a direct theorem for our UNSAT positive distribution.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\subsection{Output survival makes internal erasure irrelevant}
\begin{theorem}[Output-survival barrier]
For every acyclic Boolean circuit $C(x)$ with $G$ gates there is a reversible realization mapping
\[
(x,0^G,0)\longmapsto(x,0^G,C(x))
\]
using compute--copy--uncompute. Moreover, if a decision is an associative one-bit aggregate of sequential subresults, the computation may retain only the aggregate bit while discarding every subresult after use.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first statement is the exact REVUNCOMP1 construction: compute all gates into fresh targets, copy the designated output, and apply the inverse gates in reverse order. The second is ordinary streaming: update one accumulator and reuse the component workspace. PRODSTREAM1 supplies the exact SAT-product instance. Both are same-realization constructions.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Internal-erasure route retired]
Absence of intermediate information from the terminal state, or destruction of a full answer vector, cannot imply that the final decision is unavailable. Any erasure argument for the monotone-slice route must instead prove that every small circuit is approximately determined by a \emph{source-independent pre-output observation} whose induced positive and negative distributions are close.
\end{corollary}

This is the useful content of the user's remembered result. It does not help by making erasure stronger; it sharply restricts what a valid invisibility theorem must claim.

\begin{theorem}[Retrieval--invisibility dichotomy]
Let $f:X\to\{0,1\}$, let $\mu_+,\mu_-$ be distributions on $X$, and let $\mathcal E$ be a randomized erasure channel from $X$ to a space $Y$. Suppose a decoder $D:Y\to\{0,1\}$ recovers $f$ with distributional errors
\[
\varepsilon_+=\Pr_{x\sim\mu_+,\mathcal E}[D(\mathcal E(x))\ne f(x)],\qquad
\varepsilon_-=\Pr_{x\sim\mu_-,\mathcal E}[D(\mathcal E(x))\ne f(x)].
\]
Then
\[
 d_{\rm TV}(\mathcal E_\#\mu_+,\mathcal E_\#\mu_-)
 \ge
 \left|\mathbb E_{\mu_+}f-\mathbb E_{\mu_-}f\right|-\varepsilon_+-\varepsilon_-.
\]
Equivalently, if a test $f$ is approximated by a decoder of the erased view, its distinguishing advantage is at most the erased-distribution distance plus the two approximation errors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The decoder $D$ is itself a test on $Y$, so total-variation duality gives
\[
\left|\mathbb E_{\mathcal E_\#\mu_+}D-\mathbb E_{\mathcal E_\#\mu_-}D\right|
\le d_{\rm TV}(\mathcal E_\#\mu_+,\mathcal E_\#\mu_-).
\]
Insert these two expectations between the expectations of $f$ and apply the triangle inequality.
\end{proof}

Thus exact retrieval of the final result after erasure forces the erased positive and negative distributions to remain almost as distinguishable as the original problem. A useful invisibility channel must deliberately erase enough that the final answer is not recoverable without substantial error. The remaining proof burden is therefore an approximation theorem for small circuits, not a stronger erasure mechanism.

\subsection{Exact orbit moment polytope}
Fix $q$, $t=3q$, and
\[
M=8\binom q3.
\]
Let $\Omega_q^+$ be the set of exact-$t$ UNSAT proper 3-CNFs. Let $G_q=(\mathbb Z_2)^q\rtimes S_q$ act by variable complementation and permutation.

For $1\le j\le r$, let $\mathcal O_j(q)$ be the $G_q$-orbits of unordered $j$-sets of distinct clause coordinates. For $F\in\Omega_q^+$ and orbit $O$, define
\[
\psi_O(F)=\frac{c_O(F)}{|O|},
\]
where $c_O(F)$ counts the $j$-clause subsets of $F$ lying in $O$. Put
\[
\psi_r(F)=(\psi_O(F))_{O\in\mathcal O_{\le r}(q)}.
\]
For the uniform exact-$t$ slice, the inclusion probability of every fixed $j$-set is
\[
u_j(q)=\frac{(t)_j}{(M)_j}.
\]
Let $u_r(q)$ be the vector with coordinate $u_j(q)$ on every orbit of order $j$.

\begin{definition}[UNSAT orbit-moment polytope]
\[
\mathcal P_r^+(q)=\operatorname{conv}\{\psi_r(F):F\in\Omega_q^+\}.
\]
Define the moment gap
\[
\Delta_r(q)=\operatorname{dist}_\infty(u_r(q),\mathcal P_r^+(q)).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Moment-space characterization]
There exists a distribution on exact-$3q$ UNSAT formulas which, after an independent uniform signed permutation, matches every inclusion moment of order at most $r$ with the uniform exact slice if and only if
\[
u_r(q)\in\mathcal P_r^+(q).
\]
Equivalently, exact matching exists iff $\Delta_r(q)=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed pattern $P$ in orbit $O$, averaging a formula $F$ over $G_q$ includes $P$ with probability $c_O(F)/|O|$. Taking a mixture over $F$ gives the corresponding convex combination of $\psi_r(F)$. Conversely every point in the convex hull is realized by such a mixture.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Moment-space alternative]
For every $q,r$, exactly one of the following holds.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The uniform moment vector belongs to $\mathcal P_r^+(q)$, and an exact $r$-moment-matching UNSAT distribution exists.
\item There is a linear functional $\ell$ on orbit-moment space such that
\[
\ell(u_r(q))>\sup_{F\in\Omega_q^+}\ell(\psi_r(F)).
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
This is finite-dimensional convex separation applied to the compact polytope $\mathcal P_r^+(q)$.
\end{proof}
The separating functional is an exact certificate that the requested low-order pseudorandom UNSAT distribution cannot exist at that $(q,r)$. Its coefficients need not be nonnegative, so it is not automatically a monotone circuit; it is nevertheless the correct dual object for computation and falsification.

\begin{corollary}[Small-support witness]
Let
\[
d_r(q)=\sum_{j=1}^r|\mathcal O_j(q)|.
\]
If exact matching exists, it has a witness distribution supported on at most
\[
d_r(q)-r+1
\]
UNSAT formulas.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For each order $j$, the coordinates obey the affine identity
\[
\sum_{O\in\mathcal O_j(q)}|O|\psi_O(F)=\binom tj.
\]
Thus the affine dimension is at most $d_r-r$. Apply Caratheodory's theorem.
\end{proof}

For $r=2$, the project found one order-1 orbit and nine order-2 orbits. The support bound is $10-2+1=9$, exactly matching SLICEPAIRDESIGN1's nine-formula certificate.

\begin{theorem}[Orbit-space growth]
For all $q\ge3r$,
\[
d_r(q)\le\sum_{j=1}^r\left(8\binom{3j}{3}\right)^j
\le r\left(8\binom{3r}{3}\right)^r
=\exp(O(r\log r)).
\]
Hence for $r=c\log q$,
\[
d_r(q)=q^{O(\log\log q)}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A $j$-clause pattern uses at most $3j$ variables. Label these variables by $[3j]$, order the clauses, and choose for each clause one of $\binom{3j}{3}$ supports and eight signings. This overcounts every signed-isomorphism orbit and gives the stated bound.
\end{proof}

This is the requested asymptotic space for both the sunflower and HD approaches. It removes irrelevant variable labels and exposes the true growth of local signed motif information.

\subsection{HD vector embeddings: search-safe versus proof-safe}
Let $A:\mathbb R^{d_r}\to\mathbb R^D$ be a linear HD/tensor sketch.

\begin{theorem}[HD convex-membership criterion]
Let
\[
V_r(q)=\operatorname{span}\bigl(\mathcal P_r^+(q)-u_r(q)\bigr).
\]
If $A$ is injective on $V_r(q)$, then
\[
u_r(q)\in\mathcal P_r^+(q)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A u_r(q)\in A\mathcal P_r^+(q).
\]
If $A$ is not injective on this span, the reverse implication can fail.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The forward implication is immediate. For the reverse, if $Au_r=A p$ for $p\in\mathcal P_r^+$, then $A(u_r-p)=0$. Since $u_r-p\in V_r$ and $A$ is injective there, $u_r=p$. For failure without injectivity, take $\mathcal P=\{(0,0)\}$, $u=(0,1)$, and $A(x,y)=x$. Then $u\notin\mathcal P$ but $Au\in A\mathcal P$.
\end{proof}

Let $s_r(q)=\dim V_r(q)$. Exact proof-safe compression by a linear map requires $D\ge s_r(q)$, since injectivity on an $s_r$-dimensional subspace is impossible in lower dimension. A randomized approximate subspace embedding can use dimension on the scale $O((s_r+\log(1/\delta))/\varepsilon^2)$, up to the hypotheses of the chosen sketch. By the orbit bound, for $r=c\log q$ the ambient moment dimension is at most $q^{O(\log\log q)}$. Thus HD methods can make the search layer far smaller than the raw clause-pattern universe, but they do not promise polynomial proof dimension unless the actual UNSAT affine span has additional low-rank structure.

Therefore HD embeddings have two legitimate roles.
\begin{itemize}
\item[] \textbf{Search-safe role.} Preserve distances among a finite sampled pool, discover candidate mixtures or dual separators, and compress numerical optimization. Tensor/JL bounds apply here.
\item[] \textbf{Proof-safe role.} Supply a certified subspace embedding with a positive minimum singular value on $V_r$, or lift the discovered certificate back to exact orbit coordinates. Dimension reduction without that certificate can create a false positive convex membership claim.
\end{itemize}
Approximate Caratheodory may sparsify a mixture after approximate membership is known. It cannot establish membership by itself.

\subsection{Signed-orbit flow DAG}
Construct a layered DAG whose level-$j$ nodes are $\mathcal O_j(q)$. A node at level $j+1$ connects to the level-$j$ orbit obtained by deleting a uniformly selected clause and canonicalizing the resulting signed hypergraph. Any distribution on exact-$t$ formulas induces a projectively consistent flow $(\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_r)$ on this DAG. The uniform exact slice gives a known reference flow; an UNSAT test distribution gives a flow in $\mathcal P_r^+(q)$.

This is the useful variation of the old signed-DAG work. The DAG is an exact consistency and canonicalization device. Its raw node count is not a circuit lower bound, because UNIVERSALDAG2 already demonstrated strong continuation sharing.

The asymptotic target is the projective tower
\[
\mathfrak P^+(q)=\{(p_1,p_2,\ldots): p_r\in\mathcal P_r^+(q),\ D_r p_{r+1}=p_r\},
\]
where $D_r$ is random-clause deletion. We seek prefixes approaching the uniform tower through the moment order required by the circuit approximator.

\subsection{Sunflower feasibility cone and contradiction}
Let a presumed monotone circuit have size
\[
S(q)=M(q)^K.
\]
Suppose the relevant approximation theorem reduces it to an observation or moment order
\[
r_K(q)=q^{\beta_K+o(1)}
\]
with approximation errors $\delta_+(q),\delta_-(q)$. Let the stochastic high-girth UNSAT core have
\[
k(q)=q^{\alpha_K+o(1)},\qquad0<\alpha_K<1.
\]
The current planting analysis gives local discrepancy
\[
\eta_K(q)
\le
\exp(-c q^{\alpha_K})+O\left(r_K(q)q^{\alpha_K-3}\right).
\]
Hence the invisibility cone is
\[
\boxed{0<\alpha_K<1,\qquad \alpha_K+\beta_K<3.}
\]
If $r_K=\operatorname{polylog}q$, then $\beta_K=0$ and every fixed $0<\alpha_K<1$ lies strictly inside the cone.

\begin{theorem}[Limit-cone contradiction]
Fix $K$. Assume every size-$M^K$ monotone circuit has an approximation whose effective observation has order $r_K$ and whose errors satisfy
\[
\delta_+(q)+\delta_-(q)+\eta_K(q)=o(1).
\]
If $(\alpha_K,\beta_K)$ lies in the invisibility cone, no such circuit computes the density-3 UNSAT slice for all sufficiently large $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The positive distribution is always UNSAT, while the uniform exact-density-3 distribution is SAT with probability $1-o(1)$. Thus the exact slice function distinguishes them with advantage $1-o(1)$. The erasure-sandwich triangle inequality bounds the same advantage by $\delta_++\delta_-+\eta_K=o(1)$, contradiction.
\end{proof}

This proves a necessary alternative without calculating exact values. Any exact size-$M^K$ circuit forces at least one of:
\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{l}
\delta_++\delta_-\ge1-o(1)-\eta_K,\\
\alpha_K+\beta_K\ge3,\\
\text{the observation is input-adaptive rather than source-independent},\\
\text{or the proposed signed-orbit quotient omits information used by the circuit.}
\end{array}}
\]
In the symmetric case $\delta_+=\delta_-=\delta$ and $\eta_K=o(1)$, necessarily
\[
\delta\ge\frac12-o(1).
\]

\section{WHAT MOVES FORWARD}
The prior erasure work contributes one decisive guard: output retrieval survives both reversible cleanup and sequential answer erasure. The retrieval--invisibility theorem makes the consequence quantitative: any erased state from which the final bit remains recoverable must itself retain essentially the full positive/negative statistical separation. Internal erasure is therefore removed from the positive proof spine.

The surviving proof program is now expressed by two asymptotic spaces:
\begin{itemize}
\item[] the \textbf{UNSAT orbit-moment polytopes} $\mathcal P_r^+(q)$ and gaps $\Delta_r(q)$, which exactly characterize stochastic moment matching;
\item[] the \textbf{sunflower feasibility cone} $0<\alpha<1$, $\alpha+\beta<3$, which characterizes when a planted contradiction is invisible to a source-independent observation of order $q^{\beta+o(1)}$.
\end{itemize}
HD/tensor embeddings become an implementation aid for navigating these spaces, with an explicit injectivity requirement for proof use. Signed DAGs become a projective-flow and orbit-canonicalization tool, not a lower-bound measure.
