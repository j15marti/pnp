\noindent\textbf{Status.}
The minimal-Hall-deficiency lemma, biregular dual certificate, and no-exact-slice theorem for every lifted minimal graph-pigeonhole relation of left degree at least two are \EXACT at their declared models, pending independent review. The expansion/width inheritance uses de Rezende--Vinyals Theorem 28 and is \KNOWN plus an elementary restriction argument. The finite audits are \COMP. Consequently the direct route
\[
\text{high-width cPHP}\circ\operatorname{Ind}
\longrightarrow
\text{exact-Hamming-slice mKW}
\]
is \RET, even before NAE semantics. A different balanced base relation or a natively fixed-weight DAG-like lifting theorem remains \OPEN. No $P\ne NP$ proof is obtained.

\section{IDEAS PROPOSED BEFORE LITERATURE}
\paragraph{A: extend the finite dual certificate.}
Generalize NAELIFTKW1's $\operatorname{PHP}_2^3\circ\operatorname{Ind}_2^3$ certificate to lifted graph pigeonhole relations on arbitrary minimal Hall-deficient bipartite supports.

\paragraph{B: retain high query width.}
Start with the actual $(k,c,d,r,e)$ expander used in the colourful-lifting theorem, pass to an inclusion-minimal deficient left set $S$, and test whether the induced cPHP instance still inherits the $(r,e)$ expansion needed for Theorem 28.

\paragraph{C: special Bob measure from near-perfect matchings.}
Use Hall minimality to match every pigeon except one. Make the unmatched pigeon carry a two-entry mixed table while all matched pigeons carry constant tables. Prediction: this measure gives a uniform dual rectangle inequality for every output pair.

\paragraph{D: balanced-source rescue.}
If A--C succeed, retire the entire Index/cPHP source for exact-slice purposes and search for a relation whose valid-output rectangles admit a biregular cover from the outset.

\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
De Rezende--Vinyals define $\mathrm{cPHP}(G)$ on a bipartite graph $G=((P\cup H),E)$ of uniform left degree $d$. Its search relation outputs a pair of pigeons mapped to the same hole. Their Theorem 28 states that an $(m,n,d,r,e)$ bipartite expander satisfies
\[
\frac{(e-1)r}{2}
\le
w(\operatorname{Search}(\mathrm{cPHP}(G)))
\le n+1.
\]
Their Theorem 11 then lifts width through the indexing gadget:
\[
\operatorname{size}_{\triangle\mathrm{-DAG}}
(S\circ\operatorname{Ind}_M^n)
\ge
\left(
\frac{M}{A|\Sigma|w(S)\log(Mn)}
\right)^{w(S)}.
\]
These are exactly the two published ingredients needed to ensure that passing to a minimal deficient subgraph does not trivialize the source relation.

The clique--colouring reduction in the same paper is directional: an output is an Alice graph edge absent from Bob's colourable graph. NAELIFTKW1 showed that generic constant-weight encodings of such directional differences create reverse-direction outputs and characterized exact-slice embeddings by biregular monochromatic rectangle covers.

Hall's marriage theorem is used only in its standard finite form. The new ingredient below is elementary: inclusion-minimal Hall deficiency has deficiency exactly one and admits a perfect matching after deleting any one left vertex.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
\subsection{Minimal Hall-deficient cores}
Let $G=((P\cup H),E)$ be a finite bipartite graph with every $p\in P$ of the same degree $d\ge2$ and with no matching saturating $P$. Choose $S\subseteq P$ inclusion-minimal such that
\[
|N(S)|<|S|.
\]
Put
\[
s=|S|,\qquad H_S=N(S).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Deficiency-one critical core]
One has
\[
\boxed{|H_S|=s-1.}
\]
Moreover, for every $r\in S$, the induced graph on
\[
(S\setminus\{r\})\cup H_S
\]
has a perfect matching from $S\setminus\{r\}$ onto $H_S$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every $r\in S$, minimality says every subset of $S\setminus\{r\}$ satisfies Hall's condition, so Hall's theorem gives a matching saturating $S\setminus\{r\}$. Hence
\[
|H_S|\ge s-1.
\]
Deficiency gives $|H_S|\le s-1$, proving equality. The saturating matching has $s-1=|H_S|$ edges and therefore is onto $H_S$.
\end{proof}

Every neighbor of a pigeon in $S$ lies in $H_S$ by definition, so the induced graph
\[
G_S:=G[S\cup H_S]
\]
still has uniform left degree $d$.

\subsection{Exact-slice restriction principle}
Recall NAELIFTKW1's characterization. An exact-slice realization of a relation $R\subseteq X\times Y\times Z$ gives a coordinate universe $[U]$, weight $t$, and labelled monochromatic rectangles
\[
L_u\times R_u\subseteq R_{\ell(u)}
\]
with
\[
\deg_X(x)=t,\qquad
\deg_Y(y)=U-t.
\]
Restricting $X$ and $Y$ to arbitrary nonempty subsets preserves these degree equations and rectangle monochromaticity. Hence every restricted subrelation of an exactly realized relation is exactly realized with the same $(U,t)$.

We apply this to
\[
\mathcal R_M(G_S)
=
\operatorname{Search}(\mathrm{cPHP}(G_S))
\circ\operatorname{Ind}_M^s,
\qquad M\ge2.
\]
Restrict every Alice pointer to the first two gadget positions. Fix Bob's gadget entries after the first two positions arbitrarily. It remains to analyze a finite binary-pointer restriction.

\subsection{A universal dual Bob measure}
For each removed pigeon $r\in S$, choose any probability distribution over perfect matchings
\[
\mathcal M_r
\]
from $S\setminus\{r\}$ onto $H_S$; at least one exists by the deficiency-one lemma.

Define a probability distribution $Q$ over Bob tables of length two as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item choose the mixed pigeon $r\in S$ uniformly;
\item choose $M_r\sim\mathcal M_r$;
\item choose an ordered pair of distinct neighbors $(a,b)$ of $r$ uniformly from the $d(d-1)$ possibilities;
\item for every $p\ne r$, set both table entries to the neighbor matched to $p$ by $M_r$;
\item set the mixed table of $r$ to $(a,b)$.
\end{enumerate}
The table values are neighbor indices in the local adjacency lists, equivalently their hole labels.

Let $\alpha$ be the uniform probability measure on the restricted Alice cube
\[
X_0=\{0,1\}^{S}.
\]
Define the nonnormalized Bob measure
\[
\beta=BQ,\qquad
\boxed{B=\frac{3sd}{8}.}
\]
Since $s\ge2,d\ge2$, one has $B>1$.

\subsection{The rectangle inequality}
Fix an output pair $\{p,q\}\subseteq S$ and a monochromatic rectangle $L\times R$ for that output. Let
\[
P=\{(x_p,x_q):x\in L\}\subseteq\{0,1\}^2.
\]
Then
\[
\alpha(L)\le\frac{|P|}{4}.
\]
Every $y\in R$ must satisfy
\[
\Gamma(p,y_p(i))=\Gamma(q,y_q(j))
\qquad
\forall(i,j)\in P.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Four pattern cases]
Under $Q$:
\[
\Pr[\text{compatibility with }P]
\le
\begin{cases}
2/(sd),&|P|=1,\\
1/(sd),&|P|=2\text{ and }P\text{ is adjacent},\\
0,&|P|=2\text{ diagonal},\\
0,&|P|\ge3.
\end{cases}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If neither $p$ nor $q$ is the mixed pigeon, their constant tables are matched to distinct holes, so they never collide.

For one requested cell, a collision can occur only when $p$ or $q$ is mixed. Each event has probability $1/s$; conditional on being mixed, the requested table entry is uniform among the $d$ neighbors, so it equals the other pigeon's matched hole with probability at most $1/d$. This gives $2/(sd)$.

For an adjacent two-cell pattern, one table is required to use both of its entries against one fixed entry of the other table. The table appearing twice cannot be mixed because its two entries are distinct. Thus only the other pigeon can be mixed, giving at most $1/(sd)$.

For a diagonal two-cell pattern, equality would require the two whole tables to coincide coordinatewise (or after swapping). Two matched constant tables use distinct holes, while a mixed table has distinct entries, so this is impossible. Any three edges of the $2\times2$ pointer square connect all four table cells and force a common value, again impossible.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Universal dual rectangle inequality]
Every monochromatic rectangle of the restricted lifted relation satisfies
\[
\boxed{\alpha(L)+\beta(R)\le1.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the four cases above. If $|P|=1$,
\[
\alpha(L)+\beta(R)
\le
\frac14+\frac{3sd}{8}\frac{2}{sd}=1.
\]
If $|P|=2$ is adjacent,
\[
\alpha(L)+\beta(R)
\le
\frac12+\frac{3sd}{8}\frac1{sd}
=
\frac78.
\]
The diagonal case is at most $1/2$, the three-cell case at most $3/4$, and the four-cell case at most $1$ with $\beta(R)=0$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{No exact-slice realization}
\begin{theorem}[Hall-deficiency exact-slice obstruction]
For every $M\ge2$, the lifted relation
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Search}(\mathrm{cPHP}(G_S))
\circ\operatorname{Ind}_M^s
}
\]
has no nontrivial output-preserving realization as a monotone KW relation on one exact Hamming layer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose an exact-slice realization has coordinate universe $[U]$ and weight $t$. Restrict it to the binary-pointer Alice set and the Bob support used above. For every coordinate rectangle, the dual inequality gives
\[
\alpha(L_u)+\beta(R_u)\le1.
\]
Summing over all coordinates and using biregularity,
\[
\sum_u\alpha(L_u)=t,
\qquad
\sum_u\beta(R_u)=B(U-t).
\]
Therefore
\[
t+B(U-t)\le U.
\]
Since $B>1$,
\[
(B-1)(U-t)\le0,
\]
so $t=U$. Every target vector is then all one, and the monotone KW target has no output on any pair, contradicting totality.
\end{proof}

The theorem is semantic and precedes any NAE encoding. No exact-weight SAT gadget can repair this source relation.

\subsection{The obstruction survives the high-width expander source}
Now let the original $G$ be an $(k,c,d,r,e)$ expander of the kind used by de Rezende--Vinyals, with $e>1$. Choose the minimal deficient $S$ above.

\begin{lemma}[Expansion inheritance]
One has $s>r$, and
\[
G_S
\]
is an $(s,s-1,d,r,e)$ bipartite expander.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $s\le r$, expansion gives
\[
|N(S)|\ge e|S|>|S|,
\]
contradicting deficiency. Thus $s>r$. For every $T\subseteq S$ with $|T|\le r$,
\[
N_{G_S}(T)=N_G(T)
\]
because $H_S=N_G(S)$ contains every neighbor of every vertex of $S$. Hence
\[
|N_{G_S}(T)|\ge e|T|.
\]
The left degree remains $d$.
\end{proof}

By de Rezende--Vinyals Theorem 28,
\[
\boxed{
w(\operatorname{Search}(\mathrm{cPHP}(G_S)))
\ge
\frac{(e-1)r}{2}.
}
\]
Consequently Theorem 11 gives, for sufficiently large indexing size $M$,
\[
\operatorname{size}_{\triangle\mathrm{-DAG}}
\left(
\operatorname{Search}(\mathrm{cPHP}(G_S))
\circ\operatorname{Ind}_M^s
\right)
\ge
\left(
\frac{M}{
A\,d\,w\,\log(Ms)
}
\right)^w,
\qquad
w\ge\frac{(e-1)r}{2}.
\]

Thus the exact relations carrying the colourful-lifting high-width mechanism themselves fail the exact-slice biregularity requirement. The finite PHP obstruction was not a small-instance artifact.

\begin{corollary}[Direct colourful-cPHP exact-slice route retired]
The pipeline
\[
\boxed{
\text{expander cPHP}
\stackrel{\operatorname{Ind}}{\longrightarrow}
\text{high-complexity DAG-like communication relation}
\longrightarrow
\text{output-preserving exact-slice mKW}
}
\]
cannot be implemented through any exact-slice encoding of the lifted cPHP relation, regardless of the subsequent Boolean/NAE gadget.
\end{corollary}

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
A dependency-free finite audit generated minimal Hall-deficient uniform-degree graphs and constructed the dual Bob measure exactly from all near-perfect matchings. It checked the sixteen nonempty pointer-pattern cases for every output pair.

Representative supports were:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
s&|H_S|&d&B&\max(\alpha+\beta)\\ \hline
3&2&2&2.25&1\\
4&3&2&3&1\\
4&3&3&4.5&1\\
5&4&2&3.75&1
\end{array}
\]
with zero inequality violations.

The audit also solved the positive dual LP for complete lifted PHP at binary gadget size. For $\operatorname{PHP}_2^3$ the optimal Bob mass was $2.25$; for $\operatorname{PHP}_3^4$ it was $4.5$. The explicit universal construction above gives the same values when $d=|H|$:
\[
B=\frac{3sd}{8}.
\]
These computations are \COMP; the all-size theorem is symbolic.

\paragraph{Outcome.}
Candidate A is \EXACT. Candidate B is \EXACT: minimal deficiency preserves the expander width scale. Candidate C is \EXACT and supplies the asymptotic dual certificate. Candidate D becomes the only surviving lifting direction: the base communication relation must be balanced in a way graph pigeonhole/Index is not.

\paragraph{Next target.}
Search for a high-width base relation whose output rectangles admit a biregular exact-slice cover. Two concrete directions remain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a symmetric two-sided violation relation rather than directional pigeon collisions;
\item a natively constant-weight DAG-like lifting theorem, where the gadget itself produces equal-weight communication inputs and the lifting proof controls rectangle-DAG size directly.
\end{enumerate}
The NAE gauge companion should be retained as a later target distribution, not used to repair the now-proved cPHP balance obstruction.
