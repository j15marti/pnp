The sole hardness target remains every valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker

same-sign literals, every original variable occurs exactly twice with each
polarity, duplicate clauses are forbidden, and malformed strings are rejected.

The exact obstruction is \emph{certificate scope}. A row-XOR contradiction at

UNSAT claim. The SAME\_REALIZATION objects are the original source and coherent
pin map, authenticated source-clause cycle IDs, the explicit GF(2) rows with
choice-dependency tags, the proof provenance, and the charged source/index/work/

\section{Exact prefix-scope theorem}
Fix a valid source $F$, coherent pin map $p$, and an authenticated
source-clause-edge-disjoint ordered odd-cycle family
$\mathcal C=(E_1,\ldots,E_\nu)$ at $\delta=0$. The established theorem writes
the source extensions as the disjoint union of the complete affine systems
\[
 \operatorname{Mod}(F\mid p)=
 \bigsqcup_{(e_1,\ldots,e_\nu)\in\prod_iE_i}
 \operatorname{Sol}_{\GF}\!\left(B\cup\bigcup_iR_i(e_i)\right),
\]
where $B$ contains source-authenticated outside-cycle literal-parity and
preferred-value rows, and $R_i(e)$ is the row set for choice $e$ in cycle $i$.

\begin{theorem}[Scope-safe affine prefix cut]\label{thm:scope}
For prefix $s=(e_1,\ldots,e_k)$ let
$B_s=B\cup\bigcup_{i\le k}R_i(e_i)$. If explicit provenance rows from
$B_s$ XOR to $(0\mid1)$, then every complete descendant of $s$ is
inconsistent. A checker may therefore prune the entire descendant cylinder.
A proof containing a row whose dependency coordinate is greater than $k$ is
not, by provenance alone, a certificate for prefix $s$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every descendant system is $B_s\cup D$ for some later-choice row set $D$.
Any assignment satisfying the descendant would satisfy every row of $B_s$,
contradicting the authenticated XOR derivation $0=1$. Thus inconsistency is
monotone under adding later rows. Conversely, a later-choice row is not a row
of $B_s$ and changes across siblings; omitting its dependency tag gives no
derivation from the prefix equations. The F15 counterexample below shows this
omission is unsound in general. This is a proof-scope condition, not a claim
that all semantically UNSAT prefixes require this particular proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact prefix-free product cover]\label{lem:cover}
For ordered finite choice sets $E_1,\ldots,E_\nu$, a prefix $s$ of length
$k$ denotes a cylinder of size $\prod_{i>k}|E_i|$. Cylinders of a prefix-free
family $\mathcal P$ are disjoint. Consequently $\mathcal P$ covers the whole
choice product if and only if
\[
 \sum_{s\in\mathcal P}\prod_{i>|s|}|E_i|
 =\prod_{i=1}^{\nu}|E_i|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Two trie cylinders intersect precisely when one defining prefix extends the
other. Prefix-freeness therefore makes them disjoint, so their union size is
the displayed sum. Equality to the finite ambient product size is equivalent
to full coverage.
\end{proof}

Together Theorem~\ref{thm:scope}, Lemma~\ref{lem:cover}, complete source-row
authentication and the previously proved affine-union equality yield a sound
UNSAT certificate. A consistent full leaf yields SAT only after an original-
clause witness check. A bounded search lacking either a checked witness or a
full scope-safe cover returns UNRESOLVED.

\section{Physical XOR and full charging}
In the declared FLMV model an index tape is erased in the native jump that it
addresses. Writing $w=8$ one-bits and then reading a delimiter and jumping
takes $T=w+2=10$ steps while cumulative adjacent index-content XOR is $16$
and endpoint XOR is zero. Hence unqualified $T\ge D_{\rm phys}$ is false;
the previously established amortized half-charge survives this trace.
A 257-step read-only DELAY loop has zero data-bit XOR. A changed-then-restored
bit has adjacent total two and endpoint zero. Enumerating six-bit assignments
has offline adjacent logical XOR 120 in binary order versus 63 in Gray order,
while both endpoint deltas are six. These are representation/model
diagnostics, never free solver oracles or arbitrary-machine lower bounds.

Every deployable controller must charge target grammar, pin coherence, cycle
discovery/authentication, failed and successful policy trials, row creation,
source-bit and clause-ID access, index construction/erasure/head motion,
Gaussian operations and provenance, proof or witness output, cache/replay,
guest execution, retained ancestors and simultaneous peak space. Prefix
sharing changes one implementation's paid work; it supplies no free best-policy
oracle. COPY, RECOMP, lazy flags and DELAY remain hostile controls.
