\noindent\textbf{Outcome.}
An independent audit verifies Role~4's exact family and strengthens its
interpretation into a trajectory firewall.  The initial formula has linearly
many genuine ternaries and a linear minimum strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor,
yet a source-certified state transition reduces both quantities to zero in
linear word time.  Matched SAT and UNSAT instances then take different exact
continuations: SCC/witness replay versus immediate conflict.  This falsifies
lower bounds imposed from one initial scalar on all adaptive solvers.  It does
not establish an all-decider lower bound.

\section{Target, quantifiers, and falsifiable mechanisms}
The separation target remains
\[
\forall A\;\forall c>0\;\forall N_0\;\exists F:\quad
 |F|\ge N_0\ \land\ T_A(F)>|F|^c,
\tag{1}
\]
where $A$ ranges over total uniform deterministic exact deciders of one fixed
NP-complete encoding.  The tested solver state is
\[
S_i=(F_i,\alpha_i,L_i,\mathcal C_i,\mathcal M_i,B_i,\mathcal R_i),
\]
containing the current source-linked representation, partial assignment,
learned information, components, cache, remaining budget and selected rule.
The analysis vector
\[
p_i=(n_i,m_i,k_i,h_i,u_i,c_i,\rho_i)
\]
records variables, clauses, ternaries, strong-backdoor size, acquired sound
units, components and recognized rank.  These parameters are defined without
reference to observed runtime.  A policy may use a value only after paying to
recognize or compute it.

Before computation we posed:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{M1:} initial $k$ or strong-backdoor size $h$ forces
$2^{\Omega(k)}$ or $2^{\Omega(h)}$ work for every exact solver.
\textbf{FALSIFIED} by the exact family below.
\item \textbf{M2:} the useful parameter and rule must remain fixed throughout
the run.  \textbf{FALSIFIED}: the tested path moves from raw CNF to certified
units, then to binary CNF, then selects conflict or SCC.
\item \textbf{M3:} a finite portfolio experiment covers all adaptive
deciders.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED as an inference}.  A finite cascade is
an upper-bound algorithm only; arbitrary exact policies, learned states,
restarts and direct decisions remain outside it.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Exact family and initial parameters}
For $q\ge1$ and $b\in\{0,1\}^q$, use variables
$z_0,\ldots,z_q$, $p_0,\ldots,p_q$, $y_0,\ldots,y_q,u,v$.
Let $C_i=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee z_i)$ when $b_i=1$, and
$C_i=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee\neg z_i)$ otherwise.  Define
\[
\begin{split}
B_{q,b}={}&(z_0\vee u)(z_0\vee\neg u)
(y_q\vee v)(y_q\vee\neg v)(\neg z_0\vee p_0)\\
&\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^q(\neg p_{i-1}\vee p_i)
\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^q C_i,
\end{split}
\]
and for every $i$ the four-clause parity block
\[
X_i=(z_i\vee y_i\vee\neg p_i)(z_i\vee\neg y_i\vee p_i)
(\neg z_i\vee y_i\vee p_i)(\neg z_i\vee\neg y_i\vee\neg p_i).
\]
Put $H_{q,b}=B_{q,b}\land\bigwedge_{i=0}^qX_i$.  Then
\[
n=3q+5,\quad M=6q+9,\quad k=4(q+1),\quad
h=q+1,\quad N=(24q+38)w,
\tag{2}
\]
where $h$ is the minimum raw strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor and
$w=\lceil\log_2(64(9q+15))\rceil$.

Each quartet uses one of $q+1$ disjoint variable triples, so every strong
backdoor hits each triple; selecting all $p_i$ proves $h=q+1$.  Binary
resolution of $(z_0\vee u),(z_0\vee\neg u)$ certifies $z_0$; similarly the
other endpoint certifies $y_q$.  Unit propagation forces all $p_i=1$.
Every parity quartet then disappears or becomes binary.  The endpoints and
orientation chain give
\[
H_{q,b}\in\UNSAT\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b=1^q.
\tag{3}
\]

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT restricted trajectory firewall]
There is a total uniform deterministic exact CNF decider $A_{\rm hyb}$ with
a complete DPLL fallback such that, on every $H_{q,b}$, it uses $O(q)$
addressed-word time and $O(q)$ peak live words, including complete input
acquisition, sound pair recognition, implication propagation, clause
restriction, policy checks, switches, SCC/conflict continuation and SAT
witness replay.  Initially $(k,h)=(4q+4,q+1)$; after the paid binary closure
$(k',h')=(0,0)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Index binary clauses by one literal and their companion literals.  The
presence of both companions $x,\neg x$ soundly derives the common literal by
resolution.  A watched occurrence graph propagates each certified unit and
touches each source occurrence only constantly many times.  Restrict all
clauses.  An empty clause is a sound UNSAT certificate.  If the residual is
2-CNF, SCC decides it in linear time; on SAT inputs reconstruct forced values
and replay the witness over the original source.  Otherwise try other sound
recognizers and ultimately exact DPLL.  On $H_{q,b}$ all ternaries vanish
before fallback.  Equation (3) proves both continuations correct.  All
stored clause records, indices, SCC arrays, queue and witness are linear.
\end{proof}

Consequently, for every fixed $a>0,b_0$, a proposed universal inequality
\[
T_A(F)\ge 2^{a h(F)}/N^{b_0}
\quad\text{or}\quad
T_A(F)\ge 2^{a k(F)}/N^{b_0}
\tag{4}
\]
based only on the initial $h$ or $k$ is false: on this growing family the
right side is exponential in $q$ while $A_{\rm hyb}$ is linear in $q$.
This does not rule out a trajectory-wide resource that survives every sound
switch.

\begin{lemma}[PROVED/EXACT adaptive recognizer cascade]
Let $R_1,\ldots,R_t$ be sound partial recognizers, with exact continuation
$D_i$ on the image accepted by $R_i$, and let $D_0$ be a total exact fallback.
A policy that tries them sequentially and runs the first accepted $D_i$ is
total and exact.  If $j$ is selected on $F$, then
\[
T(F)\le C_{\rm parse}(F)+\sum_{i\le j}
(C_{R_i}(F)+C_{\rm switch,i}(F))+C_{D_j}(F)+C_{\rm verify}(F).
\tag{5}
\]
With discarded scratch the peak live space is the maximum live state plus
retained source/cache; if states are retained it is their charged sum.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Sound acceptance makes each selected continuation correct; rejection merely
moves to the next recognizer.  If all reject, total exact $D_0$ halts.  The
time expression enumerates every executed stage, including failed tests and
switches.  The space statement follows from whether state is discarded or
cached.  SAT and UNSAT paths may select different $j$ without being given the
label, because selection depends only on observed state.
\end{proof}

This lemma is an upper-bound closure property, not an assertion that a finite
menu represents all algorithms.

\section{Status, transfer audit, and next lemma}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted:} equations (2)--(5), the state transition,
both-label linear policy on $H_{q,b}$, initial-scalar counterexample and
recognizer-cascade accounting lemma.  \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL:} the 372-check
implementation and finite event ledger.  \textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} linear SCC
2-SAT and the cited algorithm-selection/backdoor frameworks in their own
models.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED:} a single initial scalar or fixed rule as
a universal model of the ideal solver; finite portfolio coverage; transport
without a positive charged remainder.  \textbf{OPEN:} every load-bearing
all-decider arrow in (1).

The switched-systems idea that different modes may require different local
potentials is a useful \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; no Lyapunov or hybrid-control
theorem presently transfers to SAT running time.  SATzilla is empirical
algorithm selection, not an unrestricted complexity theorem.  Blum speedup
is a warning against assuming a universally fastest program, not a SAT
separation result.  Relativization, algebrization and Natural-Proofs escape
remain unproved.

\paragraph{Highest-value next missing lemma.}
Define a source-computable trajectory obstruction
$\mathcal R(S_0,S_1,\ldots)$ independent of measured runtime and unavailable
to the solver until paid discovery.  Prove for every total uniform exact
adaptive decider that either recognition/switching/acquisition already costs
the target amount, or some reachable state has unavoidable continuation
\[
T_A(F)\ge 2^{a\mathcal R}/N^b,
\]
on a growing family with $\mathcal R/\log N$ unbounded.  The theorem must
survive complete source scans, different SAT/UNSAT terminal invariants,
propagation, SCC, Horn, affine elimination, learning, restarts, caching,
recomputation, alternate representations and decide-first normalization.
No such theorem is currently proved.
