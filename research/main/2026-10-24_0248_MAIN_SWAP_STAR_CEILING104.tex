\section{Claim card and falsification targets}
The fixed language is proper, unique signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3-CNF: each
variable occurs in four distinct occurrence positions, two positive and two
negative, and every clause has three distinct variables. The fixed-record
encoding uses two header words and four words per clause, word width
\[
 w=\left\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\right\rceil,\qquad N=(4m+2)w.
\]
One addressed sign-field or literal-word read costs one query. Parsing,
grammar validation, source reads, sensitivity acquisition, failed attempts,
storage, construction, replay and output are charged. The result concerns a
finite promise problem over sign fields, not a RAM or Turing-machine time
lower bound.

\paragraph{Exact quantifiers.}
For every valid source $F$, form the promise consisting of $F$ and all legal
one-swap neighbors having the opposite SAT label. The target is the minimum,
over deterministic exact sign-query trees correct on that promise, of their
worst-case number of queried sign positions. The SAME\_REALIZATION object is
the serialized source sign vector; no digest, witness or neighbor-label oracle
is supplied.

\paragraph{Three falsifiable mechanisms.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{M1, local star amplification:} many opposite-label legal swaps
might force more than input-order work. This is
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}: the exact depth is a bipartite cover number at
most $2n$.
\item \textbf{M2, four-context continuation:} Role 3's exact quartet might
force an internal recursive service. This is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} as
a universal bridge: the calls are optional external calls, their children
leave the narrow exact-$(2,2)$ grammar, and construction is charged.
\item \textbf{M3, sign-edit obstruction:} long repair paths might be mandatory
for deciding the unchanged source. This is \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}:
Roles 1 and 5 give polynomial flows for supplied repair objectives, while
Role 4 gives an opposite-label pair one legal swap apart. Neither edit task
is compulsory for a one-bit SAT decider.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Exact legal-swap star theorem}
Fix a valid source $F$. For variable $x$, let $P_x$ and $M_x$ be its two
positive and two negative occurrence positions. A pair
$e=(p,q)\in P_x\times M_x$ is retained when swapping the two signs preserves
the input grammar and reverses the SAT label. Let
$G_x(F)=(P_x,M_x,E_x)$ be this bipartite sensitivity graph, and let
\[
 {\cal S}(F)=\{F\}\cup\{F^e:e\in\cup_x E_x\}
\]
be the center-plus-sensitive-neighbor promise. Write $\tau(G)$ and
$\nu(G)$ for minimum vertex-cover and maximum matching size.

\begin{theorem}[SWAP-STAR-CEILING104, PROVED/EXACT]
The exact deterministic sign-field decision-tree complexity of
${\cal S}(F)$ is
\[
 D({\cal S}(F))
 =\sum_x\tau(G_x(F))
 =\sum_x\nu(G_x(F))
 \le 2n.
\tag{1}
\]
The equality concerns the restricted star promise. It is neither an upper
bound on SAT for all inputs nor a lower bound on post-acquisition computation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On the execution at the center $F$, every sensitive swap edge must have at
least one endpoint queried. Otherwise the neighbor $F^e$ returns the same
answers on every queried field, so determinism gives the same output on
opposite labels. Hence the queried fields contain a vertex cover in every
$G_x$. Different variables have disjoint occurrence sets, so the lower
bounds add.

Conversely, query a minimum vertex cover $C_x$ for every $G_x$. If all
answers agree with $F$, output the label of $F$. Every offered neighbor
differs on the two endpoints of one edge; the cover meets that edge, so at
least one queried answer differs and the algorithm outputs the opposite
label. Thus $\sum_x\tau(G_x)$ queries suffice on every promised input.
K\H{o}nig's theorem gives $\tau(G_x)=\nu(G_x)$. Finally each $G_x$ is a
subgraph of $K_{2,2}$, so $\nu(G_x)\le2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Local-sensitivity transport ceiling, PROVED/EXACT]
Any lower-bound proof using only the legal one-swap star of a strict
exact-$(2,2)$ source certifies at most $2n$ distinct sign-field reads.
Since a valid input already contains $4n=3m$ signed occurrences, the method
is intrinsically input-order. Under the fixed record encoding this is at
most $O(N/\log N)$ literal-word queries.
\end{corollary}
This ceiling does not say that a general exact decider reads at most $2n$
fields. It says that the specified adversary cannot prove more. Constructing
all $G_x$ also requires sound SAT classifications of neighbors and is not a
free preprocessing service.
