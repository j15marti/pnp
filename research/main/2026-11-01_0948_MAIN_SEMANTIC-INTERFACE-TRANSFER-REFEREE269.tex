\section{Independent source reconstruction}
Let $F_9$ have positive triples
\[

For $t\ge1$, take $t$ indexed copies, and in positive clause zero of block $i$ replace the occurrence of $x_{5,i}$ by $x_{5,i+1\bmod t}$.  Call the result $G_t$ and put $a_i=x_{5,i}$.

\begin{theorem}[Authenticated cyclic semantic-interface lemma]\label{thm:transfer}
Every $G_t$ is a connected valid target original.  Let $W_i(\alpha,\beta)$ count assignments to block $i$'s eight internal variables which satisfy its twelve original source clauses when $(a_i,a_{i+1})=(\alpha,\beta)$.  Then
\[
 \#\mathrm{SAT}(G_t)=\operatorname{tr}(W_0W_1\cdots W_{t-1}).
\]
For the repeated $F_9$ block,
\[
 W=\begin{pmatrix}34&47\\23&36\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \#\mathrm{SAT}(G_t)=\operatorname{tr}(W^t).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cyclic substitution permutes one positive occurrence of each $x_{5,i}$, so exact two-positive/two-negative quotas remain unchanged.  Original clauses retain three distinct same-sign variables and remain unique; the cross-clauses connect consecutive blocks.  Fix a cyclic boundary word $(a_0,\ldots,a_{t-1})$.  After the boundary is fixed, every original clause belongs to one block and internal-variable scopes of distinct blocks are disjoint.  Hence the exact number of extensions is
\(
\prod_iW_i(a_i,a_{i+1}).
\)
Summing over all cyclic boundary words is exactly the trace of the matrix product.  Independent enumeration of $2^8$ internal assignments for each of four boundary settings yields the displayed $W$.  Every counted assignment is checked against original clauses, and no assignment can occur in two boundary words.
\end{proof}

The proof is elementary, but its acquisition conditions are load-bearing.  Replacing an authenticated exact $W_i$ by a structural signature is unsound; omitting source/proof provenance can also make a cached SAT witness or UNSAT proof inapplicable to its original scope.

\section{What the exact transfer does and does not buy}
For a certified cyclic decomposition into $t$ blocks with $b$ shared boundary bits and $s=2^b$ boundary states, exact varying block matrices give
\[
 \#\mathrm{SAT}=\operatorname{tr}(W_0\cdots W_{t-1}).
\]
A conservative charged recurrence is
\[
\begin{split}
T_{\rm sem}(F)\le{}&C_{\rm grammar}+C_{\rm source/index/head}+C_{\rm discover}
 +C_{\rm acquire}+C_{\rm certify}\\
&+\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}C_{\rm local}(i,2^{2b})
 +O\!\left(t,2^{3b}\,M(L)\right)+C_{\rm witness/refutation/output},
\end{split}
\]
where $L$ bounds matrix-entry bit length and $M(L)$ is the paid integer arithmetic cost.  For one repeated authenticated block, binary exponentiation replaces the last product by $O(2^{3b}\log t)$ matrix multiplications.  Simultaneous peak writable space includes live source/index/head state, decomposition and recognition data, local enumerator, matrices, arithmetic carries, proof/cache state, guest state and output.

This is polynomial only when the decomposition is uniformly found and authenticated in polynomial work, local relations are acquired in polynomial work, and $b=O(\log N)$ (or another proved bound controls the table).  No project theorem supplies these conditions on all valid target originals.  Exact fallback still gives a finite recurrence,
\[
 T_D(z)\le C_{\rm all\ paid}(z)+T_D(z\mid x=0)+T_D(z\mid x=1),
\]
with decreasing free-variable count, but this is at most $2^{O(n)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$, not polynomial.  REFEREE268's charged fixed-portfolio scheduler remains valid, but its bound
\(
T_D(F)<4k\min_iT_i(F)+O(k\log\min_iT_i(F))+V_F
\)
does not prove $\min_iT_i(F)\le N^{O(1)}$.
