\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let $\mathcal C_q$ be all proper signed 3-clauses on $q$ variables and $M(q)=8\binom q3$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-density proper 3-SAT]
Proper distinct-clause 3-SAT restricted to exactly $q$ clauses on a declared universe of $q$ variables is NP-complete, already for even $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from Tovey bounded-occurrence proper 3-SAT on $n$ variables. After duplicate deletion, $m\le4n/3$. Set $q=2n$ and add controls $c_1,\ldots,c_n$. Let $d=q-m\le2n$. For each $i$, use two distinct fillers $(c_i\vee x_1\vee x_2)$ and $(c_i\vee x_1\vee\neg x_2)$. Add the first $d$. They are distinct from source clauses and all are satisfied by setting controls to one. Thus the target has exactly $q$ variables and $q$ distinct proper clauses and is satisfiable iff the source is. The construction is polynomial.
\end{proof}

Define $U_q:\{0,1\}^{M(q)}\to\{0,1\}$ by $U_q(z)=0$ for $|z|<q$, $U_q(z)=1$ for $|z|>q$, and on $|z|=q$ let $U_q(z)=1$ iff the selected formula is UNSAT.

\begin{lemma}[Exact Valiant slice]
$U_q$ is a total monotone slice function. The uniform language $U=\{(1^q,z):U_q(z)=1\}$ is coNP-complete.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Below and above the layer monotonicity is forced by constants. Distinct equal-weight vectors are incomparable, so arbitrary exact-layer values are allowed. The complement has a satisfying assignment as a witness on the exact layer, and exact-density SAT is NP-hard by the preceding theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional separation spine]
If $C_{\rm mon}(U_q)=M(q)^{\omega(1)}$, then coNP is not contained in $P/\mathrm{poly}$ and in particular $P\ne NP$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Valiant gives $C_{\rm mon}(U_q)\le O(C_{\rm gen}(U_q))+O(M(q)\log^2M(q))$. Hence a superpolynomial monotone lower bound forces a superpolynomial unrestricted circuit lower bound. If $P=NP$, then coNP=P and the uniform language has polynomial-size circuits, contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constant-weight monotone maps are constant]
Let $\Phi:\{0,1\}^m\to\{0,1\}^N$ be coordinatewise monotone. If $|\Phi(x)|=t$ for every $x$, then $\Phi$ is constant.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every $x$, $0^m\le x\le1^m$, hence $\Phi(0^m)\le\Phi(x)\le\Phi(1^m)$. All three outputs have weight $t$. Comparable Boolean vectors of equal weight are equal, so all outputs coincide.
\end{proof}

Therefore no ordinary coordinatewise monotone projection can transfer a known monotone-hard full-cube function into one exact Hamming layer. This eliminates the most direct CLIQUE/perfect-matching import and forces an intrinsic slice lower bound or a different lower-bound-preserving notion.

For assignment $a$, let $B_a\subseteq\mathcal C_q$ be clauses falsified by $a$. Exactly one signed clause per variable triple is falsified, so $|B_a|=\binom q3$. On $|z|=q$,
\[U_q(z)=1\iff \forall a\in\{0,1\}^q,\;\operatorname{supp}(z)\cap B_a\ne\varnothing.\]
Thus the target is a structured exact-cardinality set-cover function.
