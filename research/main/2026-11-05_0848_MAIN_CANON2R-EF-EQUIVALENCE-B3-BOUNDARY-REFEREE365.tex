\section{IDEA before search and precise falsifiers}

The initial hypothesis was that every explicit, accepted CANON2R refutation
can be compiled rule-by-rule into Extended Frege with polynomial overhead,
while the paid DEF/RES fragment replays Extended Resolution.  The falsifiable
prediction was that the construction would fail if any one paid node could
hide (i) an unbounded truth table or implicit affine batch, (ii) an exported
private LOCAL wire, (iii) an uncharged huge identifier/payload, or (iv) a
semantic equality assertion without explicit provenance.  A fifth falsifier
was that historical /341 might not supply individually selectable paid DEF
facets, blocking the reverse simulation even if the forward semantic
simulation survives.

The single target and quantifiers were:
\[
\forall F\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\quad
 s_{\rm EF}(F)\le p(|F|+W_C(F))
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 W_C(F)\le r(|F|+s_{\rm EF}(F)),
\]
for fixed polynomials $p,r$ independent of $F$, where $C$ is the frozen
CANON2R system and $s_{\rm EF}$ is minimum EF refutation bit length.  The same
original CNF $F$ is used on both sides.  No proof-search time or machine runtime
is inferred.

\section{ITERATE: exact restricted theorem}

Let $C$ denote the exact CANON2R completion checked by REFEREE361--362, with
arbitrary-precision identifiers as required by REFEREE363 and dependency-safe
renaming as in REFEREE364.  Let $N$ be the exact source encoding length and
$q$ the number of distinct paid nodes.

\begin{lemma}[attainment]
For every UNSAT source $F$, $W_C(F)$ is attained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
RES makes $C$ complete.  Every refutation has a nonnegative integer number of
paid nodes, so the nonempty set of costs has a least element.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CANON2R p-simulates and is p-simulated by EF]
There are fixed polynomials $p,r$ such that for every UNSAT CNF $F$,
\[
 s_{\rm EF}(F)\le p(N+W_C(F)),\qquad
 W_C(F)\le r(N+s_{\rm EF}(F)).
\]
Both translations retain the same source CNF.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For the first inequality choose a minimum $q$-node CANON2R proof.  REFEREE363
bounds its canonical explicit bit length by a polynomial in $N+q$; REFEREE364
preserves every auxiliary owner when pruning and alpha-renaming.  Translate
each explicit relation into a Boolean circuit: a clause becomes an OR tree, a
support-at-most-three factor becomes its constant-size truth-table circuit, and
an affine row becomes an XOR tree over its explicit mask.  EF extension atoms
share these circuits.

The rule macros are polynomial and uniform.  Oriented RES is the usual Frege
resolution tautology.  DEF is one binary extension axiom; repeated inputs keep
their ordered positions.  AND3 and LOCAL have at most three original
coordinates, so their checked implication is one of finitely many
constant-variable tautologies and has a constant-size Frege derivation after
substitution.  For GF(2), reassociate the two explicit XOR trees, pair equal
variables, apply $x\oplus x=0$, and retain the symmetric difference and XORed
RHS.  A fixed bubble-sort/reassociation derivation uses at most quadratic work
in the two row lengths.  Summing over $q$ explicit nodes is polynomial in the
canonical evidence length.  The final empty clause, false factor, or $0=1$
row becomes false in EF.

For the second inequality, Cook--Reckhow's ER--EF p-equivalence translates an
EF refutation to polynomial-size ER.  Decompose every ER extension formula
into binary Boolean gates.  One CANON2 DEF node supplies the four individually
addressable defining facets of each gate; every ER resolution line becomes one
oriented CANON2 RES node.  The raw wrapper authenticates the unchanged source.
Thus the paid-node overhead is polynomial.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[polynomial-boundedness and B3 boundary]
The following are equivalent for frozen CANON2R:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\overline W_C(N)$ is polynomially bounded;
\item EF refutations of all UNSAT CNFs are polynomially bounded;
\item EF is polynomially bounded, up to the standard polynomial CNF/tautology
translation.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently CANON2R-B3 is equivalent to
$\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\mathrm{EF}$ is polynomially bounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take maxima over sources of length at most $N$ in the two simulation
inequalities.  Standard Tseitin/CNF encodings transfer between CNF
refutations and tautology proofs with polynomial overhead.  SAT in P is P=NP,
so substitution yields the final equivalence.  Cook--Reckhow supplies a
polynomially bounded \emph{some} proof system under NP=coNP; it supplies no
p-simulation of that system by EF.  Therefore the displayed implication is
not proved by their existence theorem.
\end{proof}

This revises the initial idea in one decisive way: the forward simulation is
robust, but the reverse simulation is legal only for facet-addressable
CANON2R.  Historical /341 says all defining relations are attributed to a paid
DEF and later uses reference the node, yet it never freezes the finite-alphabet
facet selector/export bytes.  LSPV-3/425 independently reaches the same
literal-port versus source-cone fork.  The theorem must therefore not be
promoted to historical $R_{\rm mix341}$.

\section{Lower-bound and transfer consequences}

Haken's PHP theorem is an ordinary shared-DAG Resolution lower bound.  Cook's
short ER PHP proof is a mandatory countercontrol.  Tseitin/GF(2) and published
Res($\oplus$) results remain system-specific.  Under the restricted theorem, a
superpolynomial $\overline W_C$ lower bound would in particular be a
superpolynomial EF lower bound, far beyond current general lower-bound
technology.  Conversely, a Resolution or restricted Res($\oplus$) lower bound
does not lower-bound $C$ because $C$ p-simulates ER and retains GF(2).

The result is a classification theorem, not a separation.  It neither proves
EF hard nor proves the open implication from P=NP to EF polynomial
boundedness.  Pich--Santhanam's additional self-provability conditions cannot
be silently assumed.  No proof-system node bound is interpreted as arbitrary
machine runtime.

\section{Physical resource ledger}

For supplied evidence of $E$ bits, acquisition and materialized output cost
$\Omega(E)$ bit operations.  A direct compiler stores the source, owner maps,
relation circuits and output in $O(E+E')$ simultaneous space and polynomial
time; balanced maps add $O(E\log E)$ bookkeeping before the rule macros.  A
rescanning implementation may trade writable space for polynomially more
time, but cannot avoid output length.  Proof discovery, failed policies and
minimum-proof choice are not charged to $W_C$; any implemented translator must
charge them separately.  No FLMV time/space contraction follows.
