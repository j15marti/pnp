Let X be a distribution on N-bit strings with collision entropy k.
Let S be independent of X and let each b-bit hash H_S satisfy
$\Pr_S[H_S(x)=H_S(y)]\le 2^{-b}+\epsilon$ for all $x\ne y$.
The collision-probability proof of the leftover hash lemma yields
\[
\eta=\Delta((S,H_S(X)),(S,H_S(U_N)))
\le\frac12\sqrt{2^b(2^{-k}+\epsilon)}
+\frac12\sqrt{2^b(2^{-N}+\epsilon)}.
\]
Thus constant advantage eta with k-b diverging requires
$\epsilon\ge(\eta^2-o(1))2^{-b}$.
For d fresh independent stages with lengths
$N=\ell_0>\cdots>\ell_d=b$ and excesses $\epsilon_j$,
write $D=\sum_{1\le j<d}2^{-\ell_j}$ and $E=\sum_{j=1}^d\epsilon_j$.
The same bound holds with $2^{-k}$ replaced by $2^{-k}+D$
and $\epsilon$ replaced by $E$ (likewise for uniform input).
If $b\ll k,\ell_{d-1}$, constant separation requires
$E\ge(\eta^2-o(1))2^{-b}$.
