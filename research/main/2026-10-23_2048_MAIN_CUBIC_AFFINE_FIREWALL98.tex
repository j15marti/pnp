\section{The cubic affine family}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite simple 2-vertex-connected cubic graph, let $g=|V|$ and $e=|E|=3g/2$, and introduce one Boolean variable $x_e$ per edge. For a charge vector $b\in\{0,1\}^{V}$, vertex $v$ requires
\[
  \bigoplus_{e\ni v}x_e=b_v.
\]
Encode a degree-three parity equation by the four proper ternary clauses blocking its four wrong local tuples, and call the resulting proper 3-CNF $\mathcal T(G,b)$. Thus
\[
 n=e=\frac{3g}{2},\qquad k=m=4g,
 \qquad N=(16g+2)w,
 \quad w=\left\lceil\log_2(64(n+m+1))\right\rceil,
\]
under the declared two-header-word, four-word-per-clause record convention.

\begin{theorem}[Exact structure; PROVED/EXACT]
For every such $G$ and $b$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathcal T(G,b)$ is satisfiable iff $\bigoplus_{v\in V}b_v=0$; when satisfiable it has exactly $2^{e-g+1}=2^{g/2+1}$ assignments.
\item If the total charge is odd, the formula is clause-minimal UNSAT. Deleting any one clause leaves exactly $2^{e-g-1}=2^{g/2-1}$ assignments.
\item Its minimum strong syntactic-2CNF backdoor has size
\[
 h=\frac g2=\frac n3=\frac k8.
\]
\item A canonical recognizer and Gaussian-parity scan decide the represented family in $O(g)$ word operations and $O(g)$ peak words (or $O(1)$ auxiliary words when the validated stream order is retained).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The binary incidence matrix of a connected graph has rank $g-1$ over $\mathbb F_2$; its row sum is zero. This proves item~1. For item~2, delete the clause at $v$ blocking a wrong local tuple $a$. Every other local tuple at $v$ is still blocked. Fixing the three incident edges to $a$ leaves the incidence system of $G-v$. Since $G$ is 2-vertex-connected, $G-v$ is connected and its incidence rank is $g-2$. Odd total charge and the wrong parity of $a$ give consistency, leaving dimension $(e-3)-(g-2)=e-g-1$.

A set of edge variables is a strong syntactic-2CNF backdoor exactly when it meets the three-edge support at every vertex, hence it is an edge cover of $G$. A 2-vertex-connected cubic graph is bridgeless, and Petersen's theorem supplies a perfect matching, which is an edge cover of size $g/2$. Every edge covers at most two vertices, so no smaller edge cover exists. Finally the recognizer validates the graph/record grammar and XORs the charge equations; every record and shortcut-discovery step is charged once.
\end{proof}

The theorem retires connectedness, clause minimality, a linear minimum strong backdoor, linear syntactic residual, many models, and bounded degree as universal post-acquisition hardness measures. Cubic expanders satisfy all of these properties while preserving the linear affine scan.

\section{All-decider observation bound}
Changing one charge $b_v$ reverses the SAT label and changes only the four-clause sign-word group for vertex $v$; variable identifiers, record count, width, and all metadata are unchanged. The $g$ groups are disjoint. On every valid family member, if a deterministic exact decider reads no changed sign field from one group, the opposite-label neighbor is indistinguishable. Therefore every such decider performs at least $g$ addressed input-word reads. Equivalently this is
\[
  \Omega(g)=\Omega(N/\log N)
\]
in the declared encoding. The linear parity scan matches this order. This is an unconditional input-observation lower bound, not an unrestricted post-acquisition time lower bound.

For reference only, a shared-NOT, fan-in-two OR/AND formula evaluator uses
\[
  n+3k-1=\frac{27g}{2}-1\ \text{gates},\qquad
  n+6k-2=\frac{51g}{2}-2\ \text{directed inputs}.
\]
This is evaluation circuitry, not a SAT-solving circuit lower bound.

\section{Same-family proof-system firewall}
There are bounded-degree cubic graph families with $\operatorname{tw}(G)=\Omega(g)$. For odd charge, $\mathcal T(G,b)$ is the standard degree-three Tseitin contradiction. The primary theorem of Itsykson--Riazanov--Smirnov gives regular-resolution refutation size
\[
  2^{\Omega(\operatorname{tw}(G))}=2^{\Omega(g)}
  =2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}
\]
up to polynomial factors. Yet the same canonically recognized formulas are decided in $O(g)$ word operations by the charge scan.

Hence the implication
\[
\text{exponential regular-resolution proof length}
\ \Longrightarrow\
\text{exponential arbitrary deterministic SAT-decision time}
\]
is \textbf{FALSIFIED}. A valid transport would need a new simulation theorem forcing every exact decider to yield a regular-resolution refutation with polynomial overhead; no such theorem is established, and the affine decider demonstrates why this demand is substantive. The result does not weaken the restricted lower bound; it blocks its use as an unrestricted machine lower bound.
