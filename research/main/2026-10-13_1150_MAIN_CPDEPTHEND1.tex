\section{A constant-depth backward projection}

Let $\mathcal T(F)$ be the selector exact-$(2,2)$ compiler of CP22LIFT1.
That report proves a literal/constant substitution $\sigma$ mapping target
clauses either to source clauses or Boolean-valid affine inequalities, and
shows that $\sigma$ commutes with addition, nonnegative scaling, and legal
CP division.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{CP22-BACKWARD-DEPTH-PROJECTION1}]\label{lem:depth}
Let $F$ be a nonempty proper 3-CNF occurrence list.  From any dag-like CP
refutation $\tau$ of $\mathcal T(F)$ with $S$ lines and depth $D$, one can
construct a CP refutation $\pi$ of $F$ with
\[
                  S(\pi)\le 16S+O(1),\qquad
                  D(\pi)\le D+4,                                  \tag{3}
\]
and polynomial encoded bit length, time, and peak workspace in
$|\operatorname{enc}(F)|+|\tau|$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Substitute every target line by $\sigma$.  Addition and scaling commute
exactly.  If a legal division has divisor $d$, every parent coefficient is
divisible by $d$.  Identification sums divisible coefficients; replacing a
variable by $0$, $1$, $x$, or $1-x$ changes the constant by a sum of
coefficients divisible by $d$.  Hence
\[
 \left\lceil\frac{b-c}{d}\right\rceil
 =\left\lceil\frac b d\right\rceil-\frac c d,                  \tag{4}
\]
so the projected division remains legal.

A projected target axiom is a source axiom or a Boolean-valid inequality on
at most three source variables.  For $a\cdot x\ge b$, Boolean validity is
equivalent to
\[
                   b\le\sum_{a_i<0}a_i.                            \tag{5}
\]
Scaled Boolean bounds derive the right-hand minimum.  At most three such
bounds are combined; if weakening of the right side is needed, derive
$0\ge-1$ from $x\ge0$ and $-x\ge-1$, scale it, and add it once.  This uses
at most fifteen auxiliary lines per projected axiom and inference depth at
most four.  Replacing proof leaves by these constant dag fragments proves
(3).  Coefficient aggregation and the weakening multiplier have polynomial
bit length by the CP22LIFT1 accounting.
\end{proof}

Thus any CP size--depth theorem for a syntactically eligible source family
passes backward through this compiler, with constant line and depth loss.
The exact proper-3-CNF adapter and parameter conversion for the particular
de Rezende et al. family still require an independent source audit; they are
not silently inferred from the unrelated graph-colouring adapter.

\section{The endpoint audit: one retirement and one viable conditional chain}

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-exponent firewall]\label{lem:firewall}
Suppose a computation-to-proof construction has
\[
 D(\tau_A(x))\le O(T_A(|x|)^a|x|^b)
\]
for fixed $a>0,b\ge0$.  A lower bound
$D(\tau)\ge\Omega((|x|/\log|x|)^\alpha)$ with fixed $\alpha$
can exclude only solver exponents $k$ satisfying $ak+b<\alpha$.
It cannot establish (1), whose $k$ is arbitrary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $T_A(N)\le CN^k$, the construction gives depth
$N^{ak+b+o(1)}$.  A contradiction with the stated lower bound requires
$ak+b<\alpha$.  Choosing $k\ge(\alpha-b)/a$ removes it.
\end{proof}

Consequently, using only the $2.4$ regime as the terminal endpoint for every
polynomial decider is \RET.  This is not a defect in the source theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Superpolynomial endpoint sufficiency, conditional
instantiation]\label{thm:endpoint}
Assume all of the following for one explicit source family $F_N$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item its compiled targets $G_N=\mathcal T(F_N)$ lie in $\mathcal C_{22}$
and have encoded length polynomially related to $N$;
\item every polynomial-size CP refutation of $F_N$ has depth
$N^{\omega(1)}$;
\item for every total polynomial-time decider $A$ of $\Ltwo$, a uniform
charged construction emits from $(A,G_N)$ a CP refutation $\tau_{A,N}$ of
$G_N$ with size and depth polynomial in $N$ and $T_A(|G_N|)$.
\end{enumerate}
Then $\Ltwo\notin\mathsf P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $A$ ran in time $|G|^k$, item 3 would give polynomial size and depth for
$\tau_{A,N}$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:depth} would give a polynomial-size,
polynomial-depth refutation of $F_N$, contradicting item 2 for sufficiently
large $N$.  The exponent may depend on $A$ because $N^{\omega(1)}$
eventually dominates every separately fixed polynomial.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint} is an exact composition lemma, but its
instantiation is \CAND.  Item 2 is the right type of endpoint offered by the
second de Rezende et al. regime after its syntax/parameter audit.  Item 3 is
\OPEN{} and separation-strength.  A deterministic one-bit rejection trace
certifies only what the program did; without a proof of the program's global
correctness it is not a CP refutation of the input.  Proof verification,
proof search, and semantic correctness of an arbitrary solver must not be
identified.  A Cook--Reckhow system tailored to $A$ does not imply a CP
simulation.
