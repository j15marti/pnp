\section{Fixed-slot proper-3CNF transducer}
Assume the checked ORDERED-SEPARATOR-STAGE276 source contract: original clauses have fixed-width records; after choosing an interface assignment $\sigma$, every record in a contiguous block either is satisfied or leaves a residual clause of width $0,1,2$, or $3$; each block occupies at most $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ source bits; there are at most $N^{3/5+o(1)}$ boundary/block children; and all failed candidates, offsets, source scans, and joins fit $N^{11/10+o(1)}$ time and $N^{1/2+o(1)}$ live writable space.

For source record $j$, reserve a disjoint deterministic auxiliary namespace.  Replace that record by exactly eight proper ternary slots:
\begin{itemize}
\item a satisfied record becomes eight independent positive clauses on fresh variables;
\item a residual ternary clause occupies one slot and seven fresh positive clauses pad it;
\item $(u\vee v)$ becomes $(u\vee v\vee a)\wedge(u\vee v\vee\neg a)$ plus six padding slots;
\item $(u)$ becomes the four clauses $(u\vee\pm a\vee\pm b)$ plus four padding slots;
\item the empty clause becomes all eight sign patterns on three fresh variables.
\end{itemize}
Every padding clause uses three variables that occur nowhere else.  It is therefore existentially harmless without using a repeated-variable or tautological source record.

\begin{lemma}[Eight-slot equivalence]\label{lem:slot}
For every source record $C_j$ and coherent interface assignment $\sigma$, the conjunction of its eight slots is satisfiable over the fresh variables exactly when $C_j\upharpoonright\sigma$ is true under the surviving original variables.  The empty residual has no extension.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The width-two and width-one gadgets are the standard existential proper-3CNF expansions: eliminating the fresh signs gives the original residual.  A positive padding clause on private variables always has an extension.  The eight sign patterns exclude every assignment to their three variables, so the width-zero gadget is UNSAT.  Fresh namespaces make the records independent.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Restricted total fixed-slot accessor]\label{th:access}
For every accepted ordered source and declared child $(\sigma,i)$, the eight-slot construction defines a complete ordinary proper-3CNF string $G_{\sigma,i}$ of length
\[
 |G_{\sigma,i}|=N^{1/2+o(1)}.
\]
Its exact length and every symbol at every integer address (standard blank outside the interval) are computable from the indexed original source in $\log^{O(1)}N$ time and $O(\log N)$ scratch, after the already charged ordered-stage metadata acquisition.  Moreover
\[
 \SAT(F)\iff
 \bigvee_{\sigma}\bigwedge_i\SAT(G_{\sigma,i}),
\]
and negating this equality gives exact source-NO correctness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The fixed record width and eight-slot count let the accessor divide an in-range address into header, child-clause ordinal, source-record ordinal, slot, literal field, and field bit.  Because the block is contiguous, source-record ordinal maps by one offset addition to an original indexed record.  Three source literals, $\sigma$, and the record number determine the selected slot and all fresh identifiers.  Fixed-width integer arithmetic and the constant number of $O(\log N)$-bit source fields cost $\log^{O(1)}N$ time and $O(\log N)$ scratch.  Exact length comparison returns blank outside the child.  Eight is constant, and fresh identifier width is $O(\log N)$, so the promised block-size bound yields the displayed length.  Lemma~\ref{lem:slot}, disjoint private blocks, and disjunction over coherent boundary assignments prove both labels.\end{proof}

The polylogarithmic accessor overhead is $N^{o(1)}$ per guest step, and depth-first release prevents sibling-space summation.
