\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
Wegener identifies set circuits on length-$k$ prime implicants as the native monotone model for slice functions. Hiltgen--Paterson show that PI$_k$ set circuits allow strong mass-production sharing, so depth-two expansion lower bounds do not automatically lower-bound arbitrary DAGs. Ellis--Linial supply an infinite sequence of 3-uniform 16-regular hypergraphs with Berge girth
\[
g(v)>\frac{\log v-\log4}{\log30}-1.
\]
Tseitin parity formulas supply the algebraic core, but the clause-minimality needed below is proved directly for prism graphs. Robust-sunflower methods remain relevant only to the later depth-reduction step.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let $N=8\binom q3$, $t=3q$, $\Omega_{q,t}=\binom{\mathcal C_q}{t}$, and
\[
\mathcal U_{q,t}=\{F\in\Omega_{q,t}:F\text{ is UNSAT}\}.
\]
For clause $C$, put $Y_C=\{F:C\in F\}$.

\subsection{Exact set-CNF complexity}
For $3\mid q$ and assignment $a$, partition the variables into triples and include, on each triple, the seven clauses satisfied by $a$. This gives a $7q/3$-clause formula $H_a$ with unique satisfying assignment $a$. Add $2q/3$ further clauses satisfied by $a$, giving an exact-$3q$ formula $G_a$. Let $B_a$ be the clauses falsified by $a$. Choose one added clause $P_a$, and for $C\in B_a$ set
\[
F_{a,C}=(G_a\setminus\{P_a\})\cup\{C\}.
\]
Then $F_{a,C}$ is UNSAT.

\begin{theorem}
The minimum number of bottom unions in a depth-two set-CNF constructing $\mathcal U_{q,3q}$ from the stars $Y_C$ is exactly $2^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The canonical construction is
\[
\mathcal U_{q,3q}=\bigcap_{a\in\{0,1\}^q}\ \bigcup_{C\in B_a}Y_C.
\]
Conversely, if a bottom union $Q$ rejects $G_a$, then every positive swap $F_{a,C}$ forces $C\in Q$, hence $B_a\subseteq Q$. The same $Q$ cannot reject $G_b$ for $b\ne a$, since the unique-solution formula $G_b$ contains a clause falsified by $a$, yielding $B_a\cap G_b\ne\emptyset$. Thus distinct assignments require distinct bottom unions.
\end{proof}
Since $N=(4/3+o(1))q^3$, this is
\[
2^q=\exp\left((\log2+o(1))(3N/4)^{1/3}\right).
\]

\subsection{Exact-density minimally-UNSAT Tseitin minterms}
Let $P_s=C_s\square K_2$ be the cubic prism graph with $n=2s$ vertices and $3n/2$ edges. For every odd charge vector $b$, encode the vertex parity equations by four proper 3-clauses per vertex, obtaining a $4n$-clause formula $T_b$.

\begin{lemma}
Every $T_b$ is minimally UNSAT.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The sum of all parity equations is inconsistent. Delete one clause in the block at vertex $v$, corresponding to one wrong-parity local pattern. The graph $P_s-v$ is connected. Solve all equations except $v$. Any desired wrong-parity pattern at $v$ differs from the obtained one on an even pair of incident edges. A path between their other endpoints in $P_s-v$, together with those two edges, is a cycle-space correction realizing the desired pattern while preserving every other equation. The deleted clause is the unique clause falsified by that pattern.
\end{proof}
There are $2^{n-1}$ distinct odd-charge formulas. Choose $f_n=\lceil(6n)^{1/3}\rceil+3$, set $q_n=3n/2+f_n$, and add a fixed satisfiable formula on the $f_n$ fresh variables with exactly $3q_n-4n$ clauses. This yields exact-$3q_n$ positives $Y_b$.

\begin{theorem}
Every depth-two set-DNF for $\mathcal U_{q_n,3q_n}$ has at least
\[
2^{n-1}=2^{(2/3)q_n-O(q_n^{1/3})}
\]
bottom intersections.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any sound term true on an exact-layer input must itself be UNSAT; otherwise extend a satisfying assignment to an exact-$3q_n$ satisfiable formula. Split a term contained in $Y_b$ into core and fresh-variable clauses. The fresh part is satisfiable, so the core part is UNSAT. Minimality forces it to contain all of $T_b$. A term contained in two positives would force two equal-size distinct Tseitin cores to contain one another, impossible. Hence one term covers at most one odd charge.
\end{proof}
In selector-input length $N$,
\[
L_{\rm DNF}\ge\exp\left(\left(\frac{2\log2}{3}+o(1)\right)(3N/4)^{1/3}\right).
\]

\subsection{Improved high-girth infinite-sequence bound}
Let $H$ be a 3-uniform $d$-regular linear hypergraph on $v$ vertices, $d=16$, with an UNSAT signing and girth $g$. Put $q=2v$, randomly inject the core into $q$ variables, randomly complement core variables, and fill to exactly $3q$ clauses using a satisfiable formula on the complement variables.

If a fixed $\ell$-edge linear hyperforest $S$ has $c$ components, then it has $2\ell+c$ variables. The number of support embeddings into $H$ is at most $v^c(2d)^\ell$, so
\[
\Pr[S\subseteq\mathbf K]\le\frac{v^c(2d)^\ell}{(q)_{2\ell+c}}.
\]
Choose $\ell<g$ with
\[
\ell=(1/\log30+o(1))\log q.
\]
Every UNSAT term contained in a positive formula contains at least $g$ planted clauses and hence a planted $\ell$-subset. Union-bounding over at most $\binom{3q}{\ell}$ subsets gives
\[
\Pr[T\subseteq\mathbf Y]\le\left(\frac{6ed+o(1)}{\ell q}\right)^\ell.
\]
Therefore, along the Ellis--Linial infinite sequence,
\[
L_{\rm DNF}\ge\exp\left(\left(\frac1{\log30}+o(1)\right)(\log q)^2\right)
\]
and equivalently
\[
L_{\rm DNF}\ge\exp\left(\left(\frac1{9\log30}+o(1)\right)(\log N)^2\right)=N^{(1/(9\log30)+o(1))\log N}.
\]
This is weaker than the exact Tseitin DNF bound but uses locally high-girth positives suitable for the deeper-circuit approximation program.

\subsection{Factorial-moment series}
For $D=\bigvee_{i=1}^{L}t_i$ let $R_D=\sum_i t_i$ and
\[
\mathcal M_{\mu,D}(z)=\mathbb E_\mu(1+z)^{R_D}=\sum_{j=0}^{L}\Xi_jz^j,
\qquad
\Xi_j=\mathbb E_\mu\binom{R_D}{j}.
\]
Then
\[
\Pr_\mu[D=1]=1-\mathcal M_{\mu,D}(-1)=\sum_{j=1}^{L}(-1)^{j+1}\Xi_j.
\]
On the uniform exact slice,
\[
\Xi_j=\sum_{|J|=j}\frac{(t)_{|\cup_{i\in J}T_i|}}{(N)_{|\cup_{i\in J}T_i|}}.
\]
The first coefficient gives the depth-two union bound. If $\Xi_j\le\lambda^j/j!$ for all $j$, then
\[
\sum_{j>s}\Xi_j\le e^\lambda\frac{\lambda^{s+1}}{(s+1)!}.
\]
Thus moment matching plus a Poisson-type factorial-moment tail would fool a bounded-width DNF. No theorem presently transfers an arbitrary small set circuit to such a bounded-tail series; mass production is the explicit obstruction.

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
Finite audits passed 5,376 exact assignment/swap checks at $q=6$; all 32 odd-charge triangular-prism formulas and all 768 clause deletions; all 128 odd-charge square-prism formulas and all 4,096 clause deletions; and 44 factorial-series identities. No discrepancy was detected. These runs are computational controls only.

The exact depth-two asymptotic ledger is now:
\[
SC_{\cap\circ\cup}=2^q=\exp(\Theta(N^{1/3})),
\]
\[
SC_{\cup\circ\cap}\ge2^{(2/3-o(1))q}=\exp(\Theta(N^{1/3})),
\]
and the locally high-girth infinite sequence separately yields
\[
SC_{\cup\circ\cap}\ge N^{\Omega(\log N)}.
\]
These do not lower-bound unrestricted union/intersection DAGs. The next target is a restriction or factorial-moment theorem that destroys Hiltgen--Paterson mass production while retaining one of these hard depth-two families.
