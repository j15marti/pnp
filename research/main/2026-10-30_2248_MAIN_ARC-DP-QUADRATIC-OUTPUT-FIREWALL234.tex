\section{Exact quadratic-output theorem}
For $r\ge1$, use core variables $u_1,u_2,u_3$, pivot $x$, and pairwise
distinct gadget variables $a_i,b_i,c_j,d_j$.  Put
\[
 P_i=(x\vee a_i\vee b_i),\qquad
 N_j=(\neg x\vee c_j\vee d_j)qquad(1\le i,j\le r).
\]
Let $H_8$ be the eight sign clauses on $u_1,u_2,u_3$, one falsified by each
Boolean assignment, and let $H_7=H_8\setminus
\{(\neg u_1\vee\neg u_2\vee\neg u_3)\}$.  Thus $H_7$ has unique model
$111$ and $H_8$ is UNSAT.  Define same-gadget twins
\[
 F_r^{\rm yes}=H_7\wedge\bigwedge_iP_i\wedge\bigwedge_jN_j,qquad
 F_r^{\rm no}=H_8\wedge\bigwedge_iP_i\wedge\bigwedge_jN_j.
\]
Every source clause is proper width three.

\begin{theorem}[One-pivot quadratic physical output]
Complete Davis--Putnam elimination of $x$ from either $F_r^{\rm yes}$ or
$F_r^{\rm no}$ produces exactly the core together with the $r^2$ distinct
non-tautological, pairwise non-subsuming resolvents
\[
 R_{ij}=(a_i\vee b_i\vee c_j\vee d_j).
\]
In a fixed-width indexed CNF encoding, the parent length is
$N_r=\Theta(r\log r)$ while the explicit projected child length is
$\Theta(r^2\log r)$.  Converting each $R_{ij}$ to proper 3CNF by
\[
 (a_i\vee b_i\vee z_{ij})\wedge
 (\neg z_{ij}\vee c_j\vee d_j)
\]
is existentially exact and still has $2r^2$ gadget clauses and
$\Theta(r^2\log r)$ bits.  The projection is pointwise exact on every
boundary assignment, $F_r^{\rm yes}$ is SAT, and $F_r^{\rm no}$ is UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The positive $x$-parents are exactly $P_1,\ldots,P_r$ and the negative
parents exactly $N_1,\ldots,N_r$.  Resolving the pair $(P_i,N_j)$ gives
$R_{ij}$.  All four literals are positive and use distinct variables, so no
resolvent is tautological.  Its support identifies $(i,j)$; hence all $r^2$
are distinct.  Equal width and distinct support rule out mutual subsumption,
and the disjoint core cannot subsume them.  Complete variable elimination is
therefore precisely the stated cross product plus the core.

The parent has $2r+O(1)$ clauses over $4r+4$ variables, whereas the child has
$r^2+O(1)$ clauses.  Variable identifiers need $\Theta(\log r)$ bits in the
declared fixed-width encoding, proving the bit bounds.  The two-clause bridge
is satisfiable over $z_{ij}$ exactly when at least one of the four original
literals is true.  Standard complete Davis--Putnam elimination proves
pointwise existential equivalence.  Finally $H_7$ has model $111$ and the
gadget extends it (take $x=1$ and every $c_j=1$), while $H_8$ is already
UNSAT.  \qed
\end{proof}
