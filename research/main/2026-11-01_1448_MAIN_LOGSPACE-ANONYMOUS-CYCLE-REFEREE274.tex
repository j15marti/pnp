\section{Source orbit and repeated-scan recognizer}
In block $i$, F9 has positive supports
$(156),(238),(135),(469),(247),(789)$ and negative supports
$(468),(167),(234),(358),(129),(579)$.  Replace the positive $(156)$ by
$(x_{1,i},x_{6,i},x_{5,i+1})$ cyclically.  For $t\ge2$ the resulting $G_t$
is a valid connected duplicate-free proper D\"ocker Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2)
original with $n=9t,m=12t$.

The read-only input contains the encoded source and coherent pins.  Grammar is
checked in logspace by repeated scans: range/distinctness/monotonicity in one scan,
two-per-polarity quotas by one scan per variable, and duplicate exclusion by
pairwise record comparison.  These scans are polynomial and fully charged.

For each of the two global polarity orientations, begin at source variable ID 1.
Compute its closure under same-sign ``negative'' clause co-membership.  Retain at
most nine IDs; discovering a tenth rejects this orientation.  Closure stabilizes
after at most nine additions and hence a constant number of full scans.  Require
exactly nine IDs, exactly six internal negative clauses, five internal positive
clauses, one outgoing positive clause containing two component IDs, and one
incoming positive clause containing one component ID.  The constant F9 incidence
tests recover local names $x_1,\ldots,x_9$.  Every support is compared with the
template.

The outgoing clause's external ID seeds the next closure.  Verify that it is the
next component's recovered $x_5$.  Repeat exactly $t=n/9$ times, rejecting an
early return and requiring the final successor to be the initial component.
Unique incoming and outgoing incidences make the quotient a permutation; exact
closure after $t$ distinct steps proves that every source component is covered.
No visited-set or global map is stored.

\begin{theorem}[Restricted anonymous logspace terminal]

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The grammar scans are exact.  At an accepted component, the retained closure is
closed under every negative support, so it is the whole negative connected
component; its size and complete support checks establish the unique F9 template.
The incidence reconstruction from GATE310 is local and uses only a constant number
of IDs.  Unique cross incidences give a deterministic successor.  An early cycle
is rejected, while closure after exactly $n/9$ components covers all $n$ variables.
Every clause is thereby checked as one internal template clause or the unique
authenticated cross clause.  Trying both global orientations is complete because
connected monotone sources admit only identity or global sign complementation.

For each block, enumerate the at most $4\cdot2^8=1024$ local assignments to obtain
its four-bit Boolean boundary relation.  Multiply relations in traversal order and
test the diagonal after cycle closure.  REFEREE273's gauge, rotation and
reversal-transpose identities prove independence from local names, start and
orientation.  The live state is two sets of nine source IDs, nine local labels,
constant relations, and logarithmic counters/addresses: $O(\log N)$ bits.
\end{proof}

The implementation's source list and pin dictionary are read-only inputs, not
writable state.  Its Python object footprint is not a native FLMV measurement.
