\section{LITERATURE CHECK AND REVISIONS}
Jukna's corrected monotone switching lemma defines a length measure $\mu:2^X\to\mathbb N$ by $\mu(\emptyset)=0$, $\mu(S)\le |S|$, and monotonicity. A selected input must \emph{respect} the measure: if $T$ is contained in the selected support and $\mu(T\cup\{x\})=\mu(T)$, then $x$ must already lie in that support. The correction is essential for the switching-tree bound. Candidate A therefore has a new model-matched obligation not present in a naive cardinality replacement.

Wegener's slice/set-circuit equivalence and Hiltgen--Paterson mass production remain the controlling deep-circuit model. The latest depth-two report supplies strong DNF/CNF lower bounds, but those witnesses may still collapse under deeper sharing. No literature theorem located converts growing inclusion--exclusion order into a general union/intersection DAG lower bound.

\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
Let
\[N=8\binom q3,\qquad t=3q,\qquad X=\mathcal C_q.\]
For an exact-$t$ formula $F$, its positive support is $F$ itself. For an exact-$t$ SAT formula $G$, its negative support in Jukna's convention is $X\setminus G$.

\subsection{Positive respect-rigidity}
\begin{theorem}[UNSAT support rigidity]
Assume $q\ge5$. Let $\mu_1:2^X\to\mathbb N$ be a length measure respected by every exact-$t$ UNSAT formula. Then
\[\boxed{\mu_1(S)=|S|\quad\text{for every }|S|\le t-7.}\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $T\subseteq X$ with $|T|\le t-8$ and $C\notin T$. Since $\binom q3>3q-7$ for $q\ge5$, there is a variable triple whose eight signed clauses avoid $T\cup\{C\}$. Let $K$ be this complete eight-clause block. The conjunction of $K$ is UNSAT. Extend $T\cup K$ by arbitrary additional clauses, still avoiding $C$, until an exact-$t$ formula $F$ is obtained. Then $F$ is UNSAT, $T\subseteq F$, and $C\notin F$.

Because $F$ respects $\mu_1$, equality $\mu_1(T\cup\{C\})=\mu_1(T)$ would force $C\in F$, contradiction. Hence the integer measure strictly increases whenever one element is added to a set of size at most $t-8$. Starting from $\mu_1(\emptyset)=0$ gives $\mu_1(S)\ge|S|$ through size $t-7$, while the length-measure axiom gives the reverse inequality.
\end{proof}

Thus any non-cardinality behavior on the positive side can begin only within seven units of the maximum possible support length $t$.

\subsection{Negative respect-rigidity}
For an assignment $a$, let $S_a\subseteq X$ be the clauses satisfied by $a$. Exactly seven of the eight signings on every variable triple are satisfied, so
\[|S_a|=7N/8.\]

\begin{theorem}[SAT zero-support rigidity]
Let $\mu_0:2^X\to\mathbb N$ be a length measure respected by every exact-$t$ SAT formula. Then
\[\boxed{\mu_0(S)=|S|\quad\text{for every }|S|\le 7N/8-t+1.}\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $T\subseteq X$ with $|T|\le7N/8-t$ and $C\notin T$. Choose any assignment $a$ satisfying $C$. Since $|S_a|=7N/8$,
\[|S_a\setminus T|\ge t.\]
Choose an exact-$t$ set $G\subseteq S_a\setminus T$ containing $C$. Then $G$ is SAT, $T\subseteq X\setminus G$, and $C\notin X\setminus G$. Since the zero-support $X\setminus G$ respects $\mu_0$, equality $\mu_0(T\cup\{C\})=\mu_0(T)$ is impossible. The same integer-increment induction as above yields cardinality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Full-layer finite-limit narrowing]
If Jukna's criterion is applied using \emph{all} exact-$t$ UNSAT positives and all exact-$t$ SAT negatives as selected inputs, then every admissible pair of respecting length measures coincides with cardinality on the ranges
\[0\le |S|\le t-7,\qquad 0\le |S|\le7N/8-t+1.\]
In particular, the $\Theta(N/t)=\Theta(q^2)$ negative-length scale at which SLICEASYMSERIES1's disjoint-block obstruction must first be defeated lies entirely inside the cardinality-rigid region.
\end{corollary}

This does not prove that every finite-limit application fails. It proves that a genuinely non-cardinality application must either select a proper subfamily of SAT/UNSAT inputs or operate only at near-maximal positive length / near-linear negative length, where the switching correction factors are correspondingly large. The naive ``replace cardinality by a clever global measure'' repair is therefore \RET.

\subsection{Constant-weight literal encodings necessarily expose both polarities}
\begin{theorem}[Balanced-literal code theorem]
Let $\Phi:\{0,1\}^m\to\{0,1\}^N$ have coordinates each equal to a constant, a literal $x_j$, or its complement $1-x_j$. Then $|\Phi(x)|$ is independent of $x$ iff, for every $j$, the number of coordinates equal to $x_j$ equals the number equal to $1-x_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $p_j$ coordinates equal $x_j$, $n_j$ equal $1-x_j$, and $c$ is the constant contribution, then
\[|\Phi(x)|=c+\sum_j n_j+\sum_j(p_j-n_j)x_j.\]
This is constant on the Boolean cube iff every coefficient $p_j-n_j$ is zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Monotone-hardness restriction barrier]
Any injective constant-weight literal encoding in which every source bit matters exposes at least one positive and one negative literal of every source variable. Restricting a monotone AND/OR circuit on the encoded coordinates therefore yields an ordinary De Morgan circuit with both input polarities available. Such a restriction can transfer only \emph{general} circuit hardness, not a known monotone lower bound.
\end{corollary}

This strictly extends the earlier theorem that coordinatewise monotone constant-weight maps are constant: allowing nonmonotone literal coordinates restores nontrivial encodings only by explicitly introducing both polarities.

\subsection{Why the prism-Tseitin depth-two witness is easy for deeper set circuits}
For a cubic Tseitin support, each vertex charge bit chooses one of two four-clause parity blocks. Pick, for each vertex $v$, one clause appearing exactly when $b_v=0$ and another appearing exactly when $b_v=1$. On the restricted family, the corresponding stars are the literals $\neg b_v$ and $b_v$.

\begin{theorem}[Tseitin-family deep-circuit collapse]
On the family of fixed-support cubic Tseitin formulas parameterized by charge vector $b$, the exact UNSAT predicate is computable by an $O(n)$-size union/intersection set circuit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Connected Tseitin is UNSAT iff the parity of the charge vector is odd. The restricted star inputs supply both literals of every $b_v$. Maintain two set-circuit wires representing even and odd prefix parity. Updating by the next bit uses
\[O'=(O\cap\neg b_v)\cup(E\cap b_v),\qquad E'=(E\cap\neg b_v)\cup(O\cap b_v).\]
This uses constant many union/intersection gates per vertex and computes odd charge parity in linear size.
\end{proof}

Hence the exponential set-DNF lower bound from minimally-UNSAT prism Tseitin formulas is intrinsically depth-two. That witness family must not be used as evidence for unrestricted set-circuit hardness.

\subsection{General fixed-support signing warning}
More generally, if a support hypergraph is fixed and each support chooses one of its eight signed clauses, then unions of four coordinate stars recover each sign bit and its complement. Therefore a restricted monotone set circuit can simulate an arbitrary Boolean circuit on the signing bits with only constant-factor input overhead. A deep lower bound obtained solely by restricting to one fixed support would therefore require a genuine general-circuit lower bound for the corresponding signing predicate. The high-girth planting remains useful distributionally, but not as a deterministic hard restriction by itself.

\section{TESTS AND OUTCOME}
The symbolic extension arguments hold for all $q\ge5$. A finite arithmetic audit for $q=5,\ldots,20$ verified $\binom q3>3q-7$, nonnegative SAT-rigidity range $7N/8-t$, and the separation of scales
\[N/(t+1)\ll7N/8-t.\]
No asymptotic statement relies on this finite audit.

\paragraph{Outcome.}
Two tempting repairs are now removed. First, full-layer Jukna measures cannot become structurally non-cardinality at the scales where the earlier cardinality obstruction appears. Second, fixed-support Tseitin/signing restrictions lose monotonicity by exposing both literal polarities and can have tiny deep set circuits despite exponential depth-two complexity.

The surviving routes are narrower: (i) use a proper selected subfamily whose supports respect a genuinely structural length measure and still force a large finite-limit witness; (ii) use the high-girth stochastic positives through approximation/moment arguments rather than as a fixed-support restriction; or (iii) attack the full exact-transversal set circuit / full DAG-like KW relation directly.

The measure must not collapse to cardinality by the two extension theorems, and its respected families must remain locally ordinary enough to avoid the rare-pattern detector. In parallel, test whether moment/Bonferroni overlap bounds can be made gate-stable under a random restriction rather than a deterministic fixed-support restriction.
