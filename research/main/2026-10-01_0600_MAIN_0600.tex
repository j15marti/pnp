\section*{PROVED/EXACT}
Set
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2,\qquad
\rho(H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_i g_j\}.
\]
For any fixed declared legal binary code
\[
c:\mathbb F_q\to\mathbb F_2^N
\]
and legal q-ary orbit tuple
\[
a(v)=(a_1(v),\ldots,a_m(v)),
\]
define on \(D=V\times[N]\)
\[
g_i(v,z)=c(a_i(v))_z.
\]
For each legal source state \(v\), define
\[
B_v=
\left[
\mathbf1\mid c(a_1(v))\mid\cdots\mid c(a_m(v))
\mid c(a_i(v))\star c(a_j(v))\ (i<j)
\right],
\]
where \(\star\) is componentwise multiplication. Then the global quadratic feature rank is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\rho(H)=
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}
\begin{bmatrix}
B_{v_1}\\B_{v_2}\\\vdots
\end{bmatrix}.
}
\]
This is the current architecture-specific structural formulation. It removes the earlier unnecessary Hadamard-only restriction. In particular,
\[
\rho(H)\ge\operatorname{rank}B_v
\]
for every legal \(v\), and one legal slice with
\[
\operatorname{rank}B_v=M
\]
proves \(K_2(H)=\{0\}\) and retires quadratic annihilation for that declared realization.

If the stacked rank is \(r_2<M\), its common kernel is exactly the global degree-two relation space. Singleton then gives a nonzero relation with at most \(r_2+1\) active ANF features. This is existence only: acquisition and exact size normalization remain separate charges.

A fixed permutation of codeword coordinates does not affect \(\rho(H)\): it row-permutes every \(B_v\). Arbitrary nonlinear message recoding or arbitrary invertible mixing of codeword coordinates is not covered because componentwise multiplication need not be preserved.

For the explicitly declared Hadamard realization, the finite-mask results remain exact. If
\[
g_i(s,u)=\langle x_i(s),u\rangle,
\]
then a degree-two pullback in \(u\) is determined by masks
\[
0,\quad e_a,\quad e_a+e_c.
\]
Thus the full Hadamard quadratic rank is computable from a finite stacked weight-at-most-two mask matrix. This is a conditional realization-specific specialization, not a canonical-vMS statement.

The accepted-family fallback remains exact:
\[
|\operatorname{im}L|\ge {|A|^2\over \operatorname{Coll}(L)}.
\]
For homomorphic \(L\) with kernel \(K\),
\[
\min_{|A|=a}|L(A)|=\lceil a/|K|\rceil.
\]
Therefore collision entropy or kernel size alone does not provide verifier anti-concentration. If a legal image contains an affine block of dimension \(r\), then
\[
\rho(H)\ge1+r+\binom r2.

\section*{PQ and cross-domain transfer status}
\paragraph{EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER.}
Schur/componentwise squares are literally the active algebraic object after a code is fixed. The stacked-\(B_v\) identity, quadratic-relation kernel, puncturing/restriction, fixed coordinate-permutation invariance, collision Cauchy--Schwarz, homomorphic minimax bound, affine-block rank bound, and the previously verified shortening-lift criterion are exact in their stated models.

\paragraph{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE.}
Shortening filtrations, low-rank quadratic-relation matrix spaces, Hilbert functions, syzygies, graded Betti numbers, and minimum-rank relation diagnostics may expose structure after raw square dimension saturates. They enter the proof spine only after a SAME\_REALIZATION lifting/acquisition theorem. LWE-style black-box reduction architecture remains a candidate alternative to forcing an arbitrary solver to materialize an internal resource. Collision-extraction, special soundness, and collapse-binding supply a useful two-transcript-to-witness pattern, but no unconditional vMS fixed-coin binding theorem has been established. Every discovered algebraic structure must also be tested as an algorithmic weakness: an efficiently recoverable relation helps acquisition rather than proving hardness.

\paragraph{FAILED TRANSFER.}
Cryptographic hardness, LWE hardness, random-oracle security, and collapse-binding assumptions cannot enter an unconditional proof. VLSI area--time/routing, distributed communication cuts, control/Lyapunov potentials, optimization gaps, topology/singularity/chirality, and proof-system-specific lower bounds do not imply unrestricted random-access SAT runtime absent a model-preserving all-algorithm theorem. GROK/BOOL TransportCert remains an obligation interface; CLAUDE's correction of earlier BOOL arithmetic does not create such a transfer.
