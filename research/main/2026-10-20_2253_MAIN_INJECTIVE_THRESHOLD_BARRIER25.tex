CHMY is a randomized two-sided-error lower bound for a one-tape Turing
machine with one-way read-only input; Corollary 23 permits fixed-oracle queries
under the stated time/query tradeoff, but does not furnish a reduction between
MCSP thresholds
(\url{https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2021.23}).
MMW Theorem 1.2 gives a one-pass search-MCSP streamer with a
$\Sigma_3\mathrm{SAT}$ oracle, time $2^n\widetilde O(s(n))$, update time

length $\widetilde O(s(n))$; it assumes the search target rather than
constructing a total SAT-to-MKTP reduction
(\url{https://doi.org/10.1145/3313276.3316396}).
OPS requires lower bounds for promise Gap-MKtP/Gap-MCSP at every sufficiently
small constant parameter and concludes nonuniform circuit separations, not a
random-access SAT time-space endpoint
(\url{https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v017a011/}).
Ilango's SAT-to-MCSP hardness result is relative to a uniformly random oracle,
so it is not the missing deterministic standard-model $R_k$
(\url{https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1652568}).
FLMV's matched endpoint is for deterministic random-access Turing machines:
for every $c<\varphi$ there is $d>0$ excluding simultaneous SAT time $n^c$
and space $n^d$
(\url{https://doi.org/10.1145/1101821.1101822}).
Hutter's fastest-program construction assumes formal proofs of correctness and
time bounds; it does not uniformly bound the unknown MMW exponent or preserve
the required simultaneous space
(\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0206022}).
Levin-style universal search and Blum speedup likewise do not turn a
proof-certified portfolio into a solver-independent polynomial exponent.
The easy-witness theorem derives easy witnesses from circuit upper-bound
hypotheses and does not prove COMPACT-HISTORY20's open

\section*{Injective threshold barrier}
\textbf{Theorem.}
Let $M=N^{a+o(1)}$ and $a\beta'<\beta$.  For all sufficiently large relevant
$N$, every injective $R_N:\{0,1\}^N\to\{0,1\}^M$ has some $f$ such that
\[
  \mathrm{CC}(f)\leq N^\beta
  \quad\text{but}\quad
  \mathrm{CC}(R_N(f))>M^{\beta'}.
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Choose $\gamma$ with $a\beta'<\gamma<\beta$ and put
$h=\lfloor N^\gamma\rfloor$.  Let $\mathcal F_h$ contain the $N$-bit truth
tables that are arbitrary on the first $h$ positions and zero elsewhere.
Then $|\mathcal F_h|=2^h$.  Every member is represented by the canonical DNF
containing at most $h$ minterms on $\log_2N$ variables, hence has size
$O(h\log N)<N^\beta$.

Let $s=\lfloor M^{\beta'}\rfloor$.  A fan-in-two size-$s$ circuit on
$\log_2M$ inputs has a description using $O(s\log(M+s))$ bits.  Consequently
the number of $M$-bit functions of circuit size at most $s$ is at most
\[
  2^{O(s\log(M+s))}
  =2^{N^{a\beta'+o(1)}}
  =2^{o(h)}.
\]
Injectivity makes the $2^h$ images of $\mathcal F_h$ distinct, so for large
$N$ they cannot all lie in that target low-complexity set.  The claimed $f$
exists. \hfill$\Box$

\paragraph{Corollary for threshold descent.}
For near-linear output length ($a=1$), every strict descent
$\beta'<\beta$ must abandon injectivity or abandon YES preservation.  A valid
many-one reduction may be lossy, but then each fiber that the reduction can
produce must be monochromatic for the source label.  Constructing and
source-checking such semantic fibers without an $M$-bit side table remains
OPEN.  The theorem does not cover the boundary $a\beta'=\beta$ and does not
rule out lossy reductions.

bookkeeping, not evidence for an unrestricted lower bound.

The strongest challenge is constructive: injectivity is only a no-go for one
design class.  A many-to-one map could evade the count if its collisions never
mix source YES and NO instances.  The next decisive test is therefore not
more reversible mixing, but a proposed efficiently recognizable fiber relation
and an adversarial search for one mixed-label collision.  At the equality
boundary $a\beta'=\beta$, logarithmic description factors also prevent the
present counting proof from deciding the issue.

\section*{ARC integration and COMPACT-HISTORY20 firewall}
The scoped ARC handoff reconciles the prior width warning: if
$w(N)=o(\log N)$, then for each fixed depth $r$,
$w(N)+2r=o(\log N_j)$ for square-root descendants $N_j$; a positive density
$w=c\log N$ need not have this property.  This yields a restricted exact
recurrence
\[
t_{j+1}\leq\max(1,1/2+t_j/2),\qquad
s_{j+1}\leq\max(1/2,s_j/2),
\]
conditional on the valid scoped-stream interface.  Because the same promised
class has a direct near-linear/subpolynomial-space algorithm, it cannot be the
load-bearing all-input step into FLMV.  The open arrow is acquisition of that
structure from arbitrary SAT inputs with construction, source access, replay,
and output charged jointly.

COMPACT-HISTORY20 continues to distinguish $\exists W\,\forall i$ from
$\forall i\,\exists W_i$.  Exact $t$-query and some truth-table transition
lower bounds do not apply to explicit succinct transitions; modular increment
and efficiently conjugate affine systems remain fast-forward controls.  No
compact-history observation in this run is promoted to an unrestricted time
lower bound.
