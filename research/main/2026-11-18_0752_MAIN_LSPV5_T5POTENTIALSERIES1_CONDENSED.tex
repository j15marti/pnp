\begin{theorem}[Source-recognized signed potentials]
For any loopless directed base graph $H$, replace each arc $u\to v$ by $u\to v,u\to h_e,h_e\to v$ using a private vertex $h_e$. The exact T5-homomorphism count is $5^{c(H)}$ if the system $\varphi(v)-\varphi(u)=1\pmod5$ on all arcs is consistent, otherwise zero; isolated vertices count as components. This family can be recognized from its unannotated target graph, in polynomial source time.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} The direct arc permits endpoint increments 1 or 3, while the two-step path permits 2, 4 or 1. Their unique overlap is 1 and its unique witness increments are $(3,3)$. Thus all auxiliary colors are uniquely forced and each weak component has five free root shifts if its signed cycle sums vanish. In the expanded source, terminal in/out degrees are even (twice original degrees); auxiliary in/out degrees are exactly $(1,1)$. Classify by these signatures and exhaustively verify a one-to-one bridge auxiliary for every terminal-to-terminal direct arc, with no other arcs. This reconstructs the base graph. Source header, every literal vertex declaration, every sorted arc and all trailing bytes are authenticated before recognition. A graph failing the grammar is handled by a correct generic exponential fallback; no false fast-path answer is issued. A single locally headed deterministic realization on certified sources has conservative simultaneous $T(N)=O((N+1)^7)$ and $S(N)=O((N+1)^3)$ bit bounds, including parsing, failed matches, arithmetic and output.
For an $\ell\times\ell$ right/down oriented square grid, the expanded graph has treewidth at least $\ell$ but exact count five. Reversing one top edge makes the cycle equations inconsistent and gives zero. This shows treewidth alone cannot establish necessary time.
\begin{theorem}[Directed-cycle generating series]
With $a_n=\operatorname{tr}(A^n)$ and $a_0=5$,
\[
\det(I-zA)=1-5z^3-5z^4-2z^5,\quad
\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n=\frac{5-10z^3-5z^4}{1-5z^3-5z^4-2z^5}.
\]
The exact recurrence is $a_n=5a_{n-3}+5a_{n-4}+2a_{n-5}$ for $n\ge5$ and $(a_0,\ldots,a_4)=(5,0,0,15,20)$. With $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, $2^n-4\phi^n\le a_n\le2^n+4\phi^n$, hence $a_n\ge2^{n-1}$ for $n\ge10$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} Since $A=S+S^3$ for the order-five cyclic shift, its eigenvalues are $\omega^k+\omega^{3k}$. The leading eigenvalue is 2 and the others have modulus at most $\phi$. Direct determinant expansion gives the denominator; $5-zD'(z)/D(z)$ gives the numerator and recurrence. This is a solution-count asymptotic, not a machine-time lower bound.
