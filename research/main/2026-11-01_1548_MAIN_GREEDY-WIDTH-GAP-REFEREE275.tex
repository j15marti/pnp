\section{Exact obstruction and fixed falsifiers}
The decision-only priority asks for computational work, not assignment recovery, an injective pin key, or a recoverable syntactic history.  LSPV-2 PAID-INCREMENTAL-BUCKET315 correctly proves that a versioned heap can reproduce its lexicographically tie-broken minimum bucket-union selector without a global rescan.  The exact red-team obstruction is different:
\begin{quote}
Does a cap failure by that paid greedy selector certify that no low-width exact elimination order exists?
\end{quote}
It does not.  Local maintenance can be exact for a heuristic while the heuristic is structurally nonoptimal.

The run fixed four falsifiers before computation.  F1: lexicographic minimum-union width equals optimum on every authenticated residual.  \textbf{Falsified below.} F2: a width-cap rejection is an intrinsic interface-width certificate.  \textbf{Falsified below.} F3: local heap invalidation itself is unsound.  \textbf{Not falsified}; BUCKET315's locality proof is correct. F4: a greedy gap proves hard SAT decision. \textbf{Falsified as an interpretation}: UP-DPLL decides the counterexample in three nodes.

\section{A same-original canonical counterexample}
Let $F_9$ be the valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) original with positive supports
\[
156,238,135,469,247,789
\]
and negative supports $468,167,234,358,129,579$.  It has nine variables, twelve clauses, and seventy models.  Apply the coherent source pin
\[
p=\{x_5=0,x_7=0\}.
\]
No original clause is falsified.  Omitting satisfied clauses, the remaining source-indexed scopes are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
\text{clause ID}&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&9&11\\\hline
\text{scope}&16&238&13&469&24&89&468&234&129.
\end{array}
\]
The residual has thirteen original-source satisfying extensions.

For an active factor collection let $U_x$ be the union of scopes incident to $x$, and call $|U_x|$ its \emph{closed bucket width}.  This equals one plus the current degree in the primal graph whenever $x$ occurs in a factor.  Eliminating $x$ makes $U_x\setminus\{x\}$ a clique.

\begin{theorem}[Greedy width is strictly larger than optimum on a valid-source residual]\label{thm:gap}
On $F_9\mid p$, the exact lexicographic minimum-$(|U_x|,x)$ selector uses order
\[
(1,2,3,4,6,8,9)
\]
with closed widths $(5,6,5,4,3,2,1)$.  The order
\[
(3,2,1,4,6,8,9)
\]
has widths $(5,5,5,4,3,2,1)$, and closed width five is optimal.  Therefore a cap-five greedy policy returns UNRESOLVED although cap-five exact elimination in the second order returns SAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initially the minimum-degree vertices include $1,3,6$, and the declared tie rule selects $1$.  Its neighbors are $\{2,3,6,9\}$.  Filling that neighborhood produces a $K_6$ on $\{2,3,4,6,8,9\}$, so the next closed width is six.  Direct elimination in the second order gives the displayed upper bound five.

For the matching lower bound, the original residual primal graph has an explicit $K_5$ minor with connected branch sets
\[
\{1,3\},\{4\},\{6\},\{8\},\{9\}.
\]
The four singleton sets form a $K_4$; $\{1,3\}$ meets them respectively via $3$--$4$, $1$--$6$, $3$--$8$, and $1$--$9$.  Thus treewidth is at least four, equivalently every elimination order has closed width at least five.  The second order is optimal.  Exact Boolean existential elimination preserves the thirteen-model residual's SAT label, whereas the first policy stops before deciding at cap five.
\end{proof}

This does not contradict BUCKET315.  Its heap and full-rescan selectors agree exactly; both choose the same nonoptimal first vertex under the same tie rule.  What is retired is the stronger inference from \emph{faithful heuristic maintenance} to \emph{intrinsic width}.

\section{What the paid incremental theorem does imply}
The following quantitative repair makes the restricted positive result explicit.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional fully charged aggregate bound]\label{thm:bound}
Consider source-authenticated Boolean bucket elimination on $n$ free variables and at most $m$ initial factors of arity at most three.  Suppose every selected bucket union has size at most $w\ge3$.  With the BUCKET315 versioned incidence heap, omitting optional witness recovery, one implementation has
\[
\begin{split}
T \in O(&N+n(m+n)w^2+n(m+n)w2^w\\
        &+nw\log(nw)),
\end{split}
\]
and simultaneous live storage
\[
O((m+n)2^w+(m+n)w+nw)
\]
bits/words under its declared table/address representation.  A retained complete table proof adds at most $O(n2^w)$ row bits plus provenance; replay may trade that storage for separately charged recomputation.  Hence $w=O(\log N)$ gives a conditional polynomial total-work terminal after paid grammar and authentication.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are at most $m+n$ active factors, because each elimination deletes its bucket and inserts at most one factor; isolated variables can add at most one true constant each.  Every produced factor has scope at most $w-1$ and at most $2^{w-1}$ rows.  Initial heap construction makes $n$ pushes.  At step $x$, only $U_x\setminus\{x\}$ can change key, so total pushes are at most
\[
n+\sum_x\max(0,|U_x|-1)\le nw.
\]
Every stale pop is charged to an earlier push, yielding the heap term.  A deliberately conservative key recomputation scans at most $m+n$ active scopes of length at most $w$, for at most $nw$ recomputations, giving $O(n(m+n)w^2)$.  Each of $n$ projections has at most $2^{w-1}$ result rows, two values of the eliminated bit, at most $m+n$ incident factor tests, and $O(w)$ address work.  Summing gives the projection term.  The space bound stores active tables, incidences, versioned heap entries and current addresses simultaneously.  All source, index, head, proof, stale-entry and output work is included rather than supplied by an oracle.
\end{proof}

The condition is about the order actually acquired.  Theorem~\ref{thm:gap} shows that one greedy cap failure is not a proof that no qualifying order exists.  Computing or certifying an adequate order on every target original remains a paid, unproved selector problem.  Even high true treewidth would constrain this variable-elimination architecture, not all arbitrary SAT deciders.
