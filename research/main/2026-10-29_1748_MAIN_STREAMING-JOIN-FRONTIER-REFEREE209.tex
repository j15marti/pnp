\section{PROPOSE: one falsifiable missing lemma}

REFEREE208 proved that balanced replay removes a polynomial cache exponent on a deterministic path.
The next missing lemma was whether a branching exact-summary tree has a comparable collapse.  Three
mechanisms were fixed before the search:
\begin{enumerate}
\item extend the weighted Sethi--Ullman exchange argument from binary to arbitrary fanout;
\item replace an all-live join by a pointwise streaming fold when child messages share an interface;
\item use high-fanout stars as hostile controls against any representation-independent cache bound.
\end{enumerate}
The first mechanism is correct in its declared model.  The second strictly dominates the first on a
foldable star.  The third falsifies any universal interpretation of the all-live recurrence.

\section{Two declared execution contracts}

Let a node have children $1,\dots,h$.  Child $i$ has a completed output table of $s_i$ bits and
minimum subtree peak $M_i\ge s_i$.  Source access, controller metadata and other work are added
separately; the recurrences here count logical exact-table and join-buffer bits.

\paragraph{All-live contract.} A completed child table remains allocated until the parent join, and
the parent output plus order-independent scratch consume $j$ fresh bits.  For order $\pi$ the peak is
\begin{equation}
 P_{\rm live}(\pi)=\max\left\{
 \max_{1\le r\le h}\left(\sum_{\ell<r}s_{\pi_\ell}+M_{\pi_r}\right),
 \sum_{i=1}^h s_i+j\right\}.
 \label{eq:live}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Streaming common-interface fold.} Every child output is a $B$-bit Boolean relation on the
same authenticated interface.  The parent relation is the pointwise intersection of the child
relations and a local relation.  Reuse the first child output as a $B$-bit accumulator; compute later
children one at a time, intersect in place, and discard them.  Let $K$ be local fold scratch.

\section{BUILD I: exact arbitrary-fanout all-live order}

\begin{theorem}[Weighted all-live ordering]
\label{thm:live}
Under the all-live contract, sorting children by nonincreasing
\[
 d_i=M_i-s_i
\]
minimizes (\ref{eq:live}) over all uninterrupted whole-subtree orders without recomputation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Consider adjacent children $a,b$ after a fixed retained prefix of $p$ bits.  The order-dependent
terms for $a,b$ are
\[
\max\{p+M_a,p+s_a+M_b\}
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\max\{p+M_b,p+s_b+M_a\}.
\]
If $d_a\ge d_b$, then $M_b=s_b+d_b$ and both terms in the first maximum are at most
$p+s_a+s_b+d_a=p+s_b+M_a$, a term in the second maximum.  The final join term is order independent.
Adjacent inversions can therefore be exchanged without increasing peak until the order is sorted.
\end{proof}

This extends the peer's binary theorem to arbitrary fanout, but only inside the same all-live,
independent-parent-output contract.

\section{BUILD II: exact streaming-fold frontier}

\begin{theorem}[Common-interface streaming intersection]
\label{thm:stream}
Assume $h\ge1$ child relations have identical $B$-bit indexing, the first output may be reused as the
accumulator, later relations may be consumed and discarded, and the local relation can be folded with
$K$ extra bits.  If children are evaluated in order $\pi$, then
\[
P_{\rm fold}(\pi)=
\max\left\{M_{\pi_1},\ B+\max_{2\le r\le h}M_{\pi_r},\ B+K\right\},
\]
where the middle maximum is absent for $h=1$.  An optimal order evaluates a child of maximum $M_i$
first.  Total table-combination work is $\Theta(hB)$ and does not depend on the order.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first child is computed with no retained accumulator and its output storage becomes the
accumulator.  During each later child computation, exactly that $B$-bit accumulator remains live in
addition to the child's own peak.  Pointwise conjunction overwrites the accumulator, after which the
child is released.  Thus the displayed peak is attainable.  The first position is the only one not
paying the additive $B$; placing a maximum-peak child there minimizes the maximum over the remaining
children.  Reading and conjoining every table bit gives $\Theta(hB)$ combination work.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[High-fanout exponent separation]
\label{cor:star}
For a star with $h$ leaf children, each having $M_i=s_i=B$, and an independently allocated $B$-bit
parent output, the all-live contract has exact peak $(h+1)B$.  Streaming intersection has peak $B$ for
$h=1$ and $2B$ for $h\ge2$.  If
$h=N^{\mu+o(1)}$ and $B=N^{\beta+o(1)}$, while generation and combination use
$N^{\mu+\beta+o(1)}$ time, the simultaneous auxiliary-work exponent pairs are
\[
 (\tau,\sigma)_{\rm live}=(\mu+\beta,\mu+\beta),\qquad
 (\tau,\sigma)_{\rm fold}=(\mu+\beta,\beta).
\]
Thus all-live weighted ordering is not the global Pareto frontier once an associative streaming join
is available.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Balanced trees.}
For a complete $r$-ary tree of depth $d$ with constant $B$ and the same contracts, the exact peaks are
\[
 M_{\rm live}=B[d(r-1)+2]\quad(d\ge1),qquad
 M_{\rm fold}=B(d+1).
\]
The star is the sharp polynomial-exponent separation; fixed-arity balanced trees differ by a finite
or logarithmic factor, depending on the growth regime.

\paragraph{SAT/UNSAT reconstruction.}
If the final accumulator contains an assignment $x$, rerun each child one at a time to recover and
verify a compatible subtree witness, then check every original source constraint.  If the final
accumulator is empty, a source-authenticated semantic transcript may stream child tables and their
pointwise intersections and be independently replayed.  This can multiply work polynomially but does
not restore the sibling-sum space exponent.  Such a transcript is not automatically Resolution or
LRAT.

\Status{PROVED/EXACT} Theorems~\ref{thm:live}--\ref{thm:stream} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:star} under their explicit execution contracts.

\section{BUILD III: pairwise crossings versus the true lower envelope}

\begin{lemma}[Dominated crossing]
Let three representation costs be $C_1(k),C_2(k),C_3(k)$.  A crossing
$C_1(k_0)=C_2(k_0)$ is absent from the lower envelope whenever
$C_3(k_0)<C_1(k_0)$.  It supplies neither a Pareto point nor a mandatory algorithmic transition.
\end{lemma}
The statement is immediate from taking $\min_i C_i(k_0)$, but it is a necessary firewall for plotted
curves.

For LSPV-2's parity-profile family, with
\[
 D(k)=2w+2^{3k},\quad A(k)=2w+k(3w+1),\quad
 B(k)=2w+5k(w+2h),
\]
the dense/BDD crossing first occurs at $k=3$, where $(D,B,A)=(520,218,47)$ bits.  Yet
\[
B(k)-A(k)=k(2w+10h-1)>0,
\]
and $A(k)<D(k)$ for every $k\ge1$ (directly for $k=1$, and from
$2^{3k}>k(3w+1)$ for $k\ge2$).  Hence the dense/BDD crossing is always dominated by the affine-row
list on this authenticated family.  The crossing is a correct physical comparison between two
encoders, not a point on the three-representation lower envelope and not a solver lower bound.

\section{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT}

\begin{itemize}
\item The all-live ordering is a direct arbitrary-fanout extension of LSPV-3 and classical weighted
register scheduling; no classical priority is claimed.
\item The streaming theorem complements REFEREE208 path replay.  Replay removes retained ancestors;
streaming fold removes retained siblings when the join algebra permits it.
\item The star does not contradict LSPV-3, whose exclusions explicitly include compression and
alternative join contracts.  It falsifies only an overbroad Pareto interpretation.
\item LSPV-2's dense/BDD crossing is numerically correct but is not on the lower envelope once its own
affine representation is included.
\item LSPV-4's stronger parity-refined structural envelope supersedes the older relaxed proxy only
inside its exact residual-hitting grammar.  Neither is an arbitrary-SAT runtime bound.
\end{itemize}
