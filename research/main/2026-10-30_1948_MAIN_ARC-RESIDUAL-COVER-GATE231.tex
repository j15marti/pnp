\section{Exact source-relative cover theorem}
Let $F$ be a CNF over source variables $X$.  For a coherent partial assignment
$\rho$, let $C_\rho(x)$ be its cube predicate and $F|_\rho$ the ordinary
restricted CNF.  For a finite realized family
$\mathcal R=\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_q\}$ write
\[
\Gamma_{\mathcal R}(x)=\bigvee_{i=1}^q C_{\rho_i}(x).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Source-relative residual-cover gate]\label{thm:cover}
For every $F$ and $\mathcal R$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\bigvee_i\operatorname{SAT}(F|_{\rho_i})$ always implies
$\operatorname{SAT}(F)$, with witness reconstruction by restoring $\rho_i$.
\item If $F\models\Gamma_{\mathcal R}$, then
\[
\operatorname{SAT}(F)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigvee_i\operatorname{SAT}(F|_{\rho_i}).
\]
Thus if all children are UNSAT, the source-relative entailment is a sufficient
UNSAT composition certificate.
\item If $\Gamma_{\mathcal R}$ is not a tautology, then there exists a proper
3-CNF source with a unique model outside all cubes, so no source-independent
rule may infer UNSAT merely from all selected children being UNSAT.
Pairwise nonempty intersections $C_{\rho_i}\cap C_{\rho_j}$ do not alter this.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A child model extends with its fixed decisions to a model of $F$, proving (1).
For (2), any model $a$ of $F$ satisfies $\Gamma_{\mathcal R}$, so some
$C_{\rho_i}(a)$ holds and $a$ restricts to a model of $F|_{\rho_i}$; the reverse
direction is (1).  For (3), choose an uncovered complete assignment $a$.
Over at least three source variables, conjoin for every other complete
assignment $b$ the proper clause falsified exactly by $b$ (or use the standard
proper-3CNF chain padding with private variables in larger universes).  The
result has unique model $a$, while every chosen child is UNSAT.  Cubes may be
chosen pairwise compatible while leaving $a$ uncovered, as the next section
shows explicitly.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Charged proof repair]
The entailment in Theorem~\ref{thm:cover} is equivalent to
\[
F\wedge\neg\Gamma_{\mathcal R}\quad\text{being UNSAT}.
\]
A checked source-resolution, RUP/DRAT, or complete DPLL refutation of this
formula repairs the composition.  Its acquisition, verification, source
parents, switching, proof storage and peak live space remain charged in the
report230 quantities $D,H$.  Pairwise overlap data are not such a proof.
\end{corollary}

The criterion is sufficient for decision composition and exact for covering
all source models.  On one fixed satisfiable formula, the Boolean OR of child
answers can happen to be correct even if some other models are uncovered; no
necessity claim is made for that weaker accidental decision equality.

\section{Minimal same-support SAT/UNSAT countercontrol}
For each $a\in\{0,1\}^3$, let $K_a$ be the proper clause falsified exactly at
$a$.  Define
\[
F_7=\bigwedge_{a\ne111}K_a,qquad
F_8=F_7\wedge K_{111},\qquad
K_{111}=\neg x_1\vee\neg x_2\vee\neg x_3.
\]
$F_7$ has unique model $111$ and $F_8$ is UNSAT.  They have the same variables,
seven identical clauses, and differ only by the cover blocker $K_{111}$.

Take
\[
\rho_1=(x_1=0),\quad \rho_2=(x_2=0),\quad \rho_3=(x_3=0).
\]
Every pair intersects in two complete assignments, yet their union is exactly
$\{0,1\}^3\setminus\{111\}$.  All six residuals---three on each source---are
UNSAT.  Therefore the pairwise rule returns a false UNSAT answer on $F_7$.
On $F_8$ the same answer is true, but pairwise overlap still is not its proof:
the verbatim source clause $K_{111}$ supplies
$F_8\models C_{\rho_1}\vee C_{\rho_2}\vee C_{\rho_3}$.

This cleanly separates successful output from a valid certificate.  For $F_7$
the exact repair solves the uncovered singleton child $111$ and finds SAT; for
$F_8$ the source blocker excludes it.

\section{Primary literature and transfer audit}
\begin{description}
\item[Directly transferable.] Cube-and-Conquer explicitly partitions a formula
into cubes before solving the residuals.  DPLL completeness likewise comes from
the two children of every branch covering the parent cube.  This is the same
logical cover condition, though the theorem above is elementary.
\item[Direct proof technology.] DRAT/RUP checking can authenticate an UNSAT
claim or a learned blocker against the original clauses.  A short certificate
does not make its discovery or checking free.
\item[Failed transfer.] Pairwise set intersection, two-pivot deletion totals,
or fewer observed DPLL nodes are not global cover certificates and do not yield
an arbitrary-SAT time lower bound.  Restricted proof-system lower bounds do not
transfer to arbitrary algorithms without a bridge.
\end{description}
