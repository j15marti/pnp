\section{Status, obstruction and falsifiers}

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT restricted structural theorems; COMPUTATIONAL independent
controls; universal complexity OPEN.}  The obstruction is a quantifier jump.
LSPV-1 SOURCE-DISJOINT-BATCH-PROJECTION321 proves that every canonical source
has a uniformly acquired source-disjoint pivot batch of size at least
$\lceil n/9\rceil$.  Tightness of that structural guarantee would constrain
that one compiler; it would not prove that an arbitrary decider must enumerate
the remaining $2^{8n/9}$ assignments.

Three falsifiable claims were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every canonical source has a source-disjoint batch strictly larger than
$n/9$.
\item Tightness of the $n/9$ batch guarantee forces exponential decision work.
\item A 35-gate forward evaluator or a minimum-order OBDD must be acquired to
decide the extremal affine family.
\end{enumerate}
All three are false.  This validates the one-phase theorem's constant while
blocking its promotion to an unrestricted lower bound.

\section{Exact signed affine-plane extremal source}

Let $A_9$ use the four parallel classes of the affine plane over
$\mathbb F_3$:
\begin{align*}
&(123),(456),(789);\quad (147),(258),(369);\\
&(159),(267),(348);\quad (168),(249),(357).
\end{align*}
Give the first two classes positive sign and the last two negative sign.

\begin{theorem}
$A_9$ is a valid connected duplicate-free proper D{\"o}cker monotone
$3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ original.  It has 18 models, its primal graph is $K_9$, and
its largest source-disjoint pivot batch has size one.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each affine point lies on one line in each parallel class, hence twice in
positive clauses and twice in negative clauses.  Every pair of distinct points
lies on exactly one affine line, so all 36 primal pairs are edges and the
primal graph is $K_9$.  A source-disjoint pivot set is precisely an independent
set of that graph and therefore has size at most one.  Direct enumeration gives
18 models.
\end{proof}

For $t$ disjoint renamed copies, the formula remains canonical and its primal
independence number is exactly $t=n/9$.  Thus no theorem over all valid target
originals can guarantee a larger source-disjoint batch.  This product is a
tightness control, not a connected hardness family.

\section{Exact connected affine-lift batch number}

The prior project report AFFINE-LIFT-BVE229 constructs a connected cyclic lift
$F_r$ of $A_9$ on $9r$ variables by shifting one positive incidence between
successive layers.  The family itself is prior work.

\begin{theorem}
The maximum source-disjoint batch of $F_r$ is
\[
 \alpha(F_r)=
 \begin{cases}1,&r=1,\\r+\lfloor r/2\rfloor,&r\ge2.\end{cases}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $r\ge2$, within each layer the only missing primal edges are
$\{p_0,p_1\}$ and $\{p_0,p_2\}$.  Hence an independent set uses at most two
vertices per layer, and a doubled layer must use $p_0$ with one of
$p_1,p_2$.  The shifted clause joins $p_0$ in one layer to both $p_1,p_2$ in
the next, so adjacent layers cannot both be doubled.  Doubled layers therefore
form an independent set in the layer cycle and number at most
$\lfloor r/2\rfloor$.  One safe singleton in every layer plus the pair
$\{p_0,p_1\}$ on alternating layers attains the bound.
\end{proof}

This connected infinite family permits asymptotically $n/6$ simultaneous
pivots, not $n/9$, but still supplies only a one-phase linear reduction.  It
does not make the noncanonical residual hereditary.

\section{Recognition escape and same-original controls}

The extremal disconnected product is easy to decide after paid source
recognition.  Validate the target grammar, construct primal components, and
require every component to be a nine-vertex $K_9$.  Solve each constant-size
component by at most 512 original-source evaluations, join the component
witnesses, and check every original clause.  This accepts arbitrary renaming,
block order, clause order and literal order.  Sorting-based acquisition is
$O(N\log N)$ with $O(N)$ cached words; repeated scans trade higher polynomial
time for logarithmic live indices.  No SAT-label oracle or projection batch is
used.

The producer tested scrambled products through $t=5$.  Although their maximum
batch is exactly $n/9$, the recognizer decides SAT in linear many constant-size
component trials.  This directly falsifies interpreting
$\operatorname{poly}(N)2^{8n/9}$ as necessary work.

On the same connected $A_9$ original, common pins
\[
(x_2,x_3,x_4,x_5,x_6,x_7,x_8,x_9)=(0,0,0,1,1,1,0,0)
\]
give zero models at $x_1=0$ and one at $x_1=1$.  The opposite decision labels
come from the same authenticated source, not two formulas.  The no-import
verifier independently passed 2,141 assertion groups.

The mandatory F9 controls were also reproduced: 70 models, 35 private gates,
depth six, and reduced-OBDD best/natural/worst counts $52/66/80$.  $A_9$ has
the corresponding OBDD counts $42/43/50$ and a 29-gate upper bound in the

\section{Conclusion}

The $n/9$ all-input source-disjoint batch guarantee is exact, but its
exponential enumeration continuation is not necessary decision work even on
the extremal family.  A paid component recognizer decides the tight product in

$r+\lfloor r/2\rfloor$.  This cleanly separates structural compiler tightness
from unrestricted SAT decision complexity.  P versus NP remains
