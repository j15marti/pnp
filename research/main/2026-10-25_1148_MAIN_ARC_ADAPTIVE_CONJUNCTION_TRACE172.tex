\section{Exact adaptive-chain theorem}
Fix a depth $d$.  A deterministic policy may inspect the entire ordinary
source, all retained state and representations, every previous label, and the
source code of its fixed guest.  At stage $i$ it selects an ordinary CNF
$C_{i+1}$, possibly using fresh private names, and forms the actual child
\[
 F_{i+1}=F_i\wedge C_{i+1}.
\]
Shared names retain their identity.  Define $v_i=1$ iff $F_i$ is satisfiable.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive conjunctive transcript capacity]
For every such policy, every ordinary source $F_0$, and every realized
execution,
\[
 v_0\ge v_1\ge\cdots\ge v_d.
\]
Consequently the label transcript is one of exactly the $d+2$ possible
threshold words
\[
 0^{d+1},\ 10^d,\ 110^{d-1},\ldots,1^{d+1}.
\]
Its lossless description therefore needs at most
$\lceil\log_2(d+2)\rceil$ bits.  This remains true when the selection rule
depends on the entire current state and all earlier labels.  For a randomized
policy the statement holds after conditioning on its fixed coin string.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every satisfying assignment of $F_i\wedge C_{i+1}$ restricts to a satisfying
assignment of $F_i$, so $v_{i+1}\le v_i$.  This implication is pointwise and
does not depend on how $C_{i+1}$ was chosen.  A nonincreasing binary word is
specified by the position of its unique $1$--to--$0$ cut, with the two
all-equal words included; hence there are $d+2$ possibilities.  Conditioning
on coins turns a randomized policy into a deterministic one.
\end{proof}

The bound is sharp even for ordinary proper 3CNF.  On boundary variables
$x_1,x_2,x_3$, any support $S\subseteq\{0,1\}^3$ is represented by
conjoining, for each word outside $S$, the unique signed proper ternary clause
falsified by that word.  A literal $\ell$ is enforced with fresh $a,b$ by
\[
 (\ell\vee a\vee b)\wedge(\ell\vee a\vee\neg b)\wedge
 (\ell\vee\neg a\vee b)\wedge
 (\ell\vee\neg a\vee\neg b).
\]
For $d=3$, the fixed sequence
$\neg x_1,\neg x_2,\neg x_3$ and suitable singleton or empty supports
realizes all five threshold words.  A precommitted policy is a special case
of an adaptive one, so no smaller universal capacity bound is possible.

\begin{corollary}[Exact collision firewall]
Let $\mathcal A$ be any finite family of sources and let
$L:\mathcal A\to\{0,1\}^{d+1}$ send a source to the realized label word of
one fixed adaptive conjunctive policy.  Then
\[
 |\operatorname{im}L|\le d+2,\qquad
 \operatorname{Coll}(L):=
 \bigl|\{(F,F'):L(F)=L(F')\}\bigr|
 \ge \frac{|\mathcal A|^2}{d+2}.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The theorem gives the image bound.  If $n_z=|L^{-1}(z)|$, then
$\operatorname{Coll}(L)=\sum_z n_z^2$ and Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$\sum_z n_z^2\ge(\sum_z n_z)^2/|\operatorname{im}L|$.
\end{proof}
This is an EXACT OBJECT/THEOREM TRANSFER of the collision-distribution
accounting requested by the post-quantum technique ledger.  It is a negative
transfer for ARC: the label channel is highly lossy and cannot by itself bind
the source, compatibility data, or a reconstruction witness.

\section{Scope and charged-resource firewall}
The theorem is deliberately narrow.
\begin{itemize}
\item It applies to one realized root-to-leaf chain of actual conjunctions.
It does not bound the transcript of a branching search that explores many
siblings, nor a family of mutually incompatible children.
\item It does not apply to an arbitrary rewrite, disjunctive decomposition,
or reduction child that is not literally an extension of its parent.  The
finite control below replaces an UNSAT formula by a SAT formula and obtains
the forbidden word $01$, confirming this scope boundary.
\item A $\log(d+2)$-bit synopsis is not a free generator.  If labels are
obtained by calls to a guest $A$, every call and every virtual input probe is
charged.  If they are inferred by a direct Horn, 2SAT/SCC, affine, graph-factor
or DPLL recognizer, that actual computation is charged.  Source rereads,
adaptive selection, failed trials, caches, ordinary child headers and bodies,
fresh-name management, joins and reconstruction remain separate ledger rows.
\item Compressibility is not a time or space lower bound.  A solver may avoid
the entire chain, decide the source directly, replay a cached result, or choose
a different representation.
\end{itemize}
Thus the result strengthens Role~3's fixed-path theorem by closing the
\emph{state-dependent policy} loophole for conjunctive descent, while
preserving the larger adaptive and nonconjunctive search space required by ARC.
