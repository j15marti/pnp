\paragraph{Parity reconvergence guard barrier (restricted).}
For the even-parity indicator $E_k$ on Boolean variables, every field of
characteristic different from two has the unique multilinear normal form
\[
E_k(x)=\frac12\left(1+\prod_{i=1}^k(1-2x_i)\right),
\]
whose $2^k$ monomial coefficients are all nonzero.  Over $\mathbb F_2$,
the same indicator is $1+x_1+\cdots+x_k$ and has $k+1$ monomials.
Consequently, a proposed ordinary sparse-monomial PC normalizer that
materializes one canonical guard for all even contexts of the linear-size
two-state parity search DAG incurs exponential guard size outside
characteristic two.  This does not lower-bound arbitrary PC, PCR,
extension, GF(2), or historical mixed refutations.  The historical
$R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ transfer, B3, and P versus NP remain open.
