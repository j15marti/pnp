\section{Status and exact obstruction}

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT only in a declared source-local matched circuit
architecture; COMPUTATIONAL exhaustive controls; universal complexity OPEN.}
The obstruction is a quantifier mismatch: a lower bound on a circuit computing
the full pointwise forward function $C_F(x)=F(x)$ does not lower-bound every
circuit state preserving only $\exists x\,F(x)$.  The projected representation
must be acquired and checked, but it need not compute $F$ on both values of the
eliminated input.

Three falsifiable approaches were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Claim that every first SAT-preserving circuit state must retain the
pointwise function $F$.
\item Claim that the 28-gate F9 forward evaluator lower-bounds every first
decision-preserving circuit.
\item Claim that a 52-node optimal OBDD must be acquired before any exact
source-aware existential projection can be written.
\end{enumerate}
All three are falsified in the declared first-projection model.  No claim is
made about iterated all-input total work.

\section{Exact restricted theorem}

Let $F$ be a valid duplicate-free proper D\"ocker monotone
$3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ original with $m=4n/3$, and let $x$ be an original variable.
Exactly two positive and two negative source clauses contain $x$.  Write their
two-literal remainders as $P_1,P_2,N_1,N_2$, and let $H_x$ be the conjunction
of the $m-4$ nonincident original clauses.  Then
\[
 \exists x\,F
 =H_x\wedge\big((P_1\wedge P_2)\vee(N_1\wedge N_2)\big).
\]
The equality is pointwise on all other original variables: setting $x=0$
requires the two positive remainders, while setting $x=1$ requires the two
negative remainders.

Let $W_x$ be maximum total intersection weight of disjoint same-sign matched
pairs among the nonincident clauses.  Let $d_x\in\{0,1,2\}$ count whether
the two positive remainders share a literal and whether the two negative
remainders share a literal.  In the declared architecture using
REFEREE280's disjoint matched-clause block for $H_x$, local distributive
factors for each remainder pair, one OR, and one final AND, the exact gate
count is
\[
 g_{\exists x}(F)=3m-5-W_x-d_x.
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The matched $H_x$ block has $3(m-4)-1-W_x=3m-13-W_x$
binary gates.  A disjoint pair of positive remainders uses two OR gates and
one AND; a shared literal changes this to one AND and one OR.  The negative
dual uses two NAND gates and one AND, or, with a shared literal, one OR and
one NAND.  Thus the two branch conditions and their final OR cost
$7-d_x$ gates.  Joining that result to $H_x$ costs one AND.  These record
types are mandatory by definition of the declared topology, proving exactness
inside that topology:
$(3m-13-W_x)+(7-d_x)+1=3m-5-W_x-d_x$.  The displayed
Shannon identity proves semantic correctness.  This is not an unrestricted
circuit lower bound. \qed

\section{Charged work, peak space and exact gap}

One projection pays for grammar and source authentication, all four incident
lists, the remaining signed intersection graph, maximum-weight matching,
every gate and source-provenance write, and source/index/head access.  A
controller still satisfies
\begin{align*}
W_D(R)={}&W_{\rm grammar}+W_{\rm source/index/head}
+W_{\rm discover/projection/circuit}+W_{\rm failed/select/switch}\\
&+W_{\rm semantic/proof/GE}+W_{\rm cache/replay}
+W_{\rm child/output/bit}+W_{\rm guest}
+\sum_{Q\in E(R)}W_D(Q).
\end{align*}
Peak writable space is the maximum co-live source parser, incidence lists,
matching graph/state, projected circuit, proof/cache, ancestors, children,
index/head state and guest DELAY/COPY/RECOMP space.  Logical XOR remains an
offline diagnostic unless explicitly computed and charged.

The first projection is polynomial and removes one input, but it produces a
noncanonical circuit/function.  Repeated elimination can duplicate or merge
arbitrary overlapping subfunctions and can be exponential in induced width
or BDD size.  No polynomial bound on the aggregate projection/successor sum
or on acquiring a favorable elimination order is proved.  There is no
hereditary canonical child theorem, arbitrary-$A$ power-short
$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$, or strict same-machine FLMV numerical transfer.

\section{Conclusion}

The 28-gate F9 forward circuit and 52-node best OBDD are not necessary first

P versus NP remain \textbf{OPEN}.
