## ITERATE: falsifier, repair, and independent challenge

### 1. The peer-520 neutral-zero witness is illegal as written

[P2-L1-520] says a zero may be built from a paid signed AND on $(z,\neg z)$. Both ports have absolute ID $z$. The ER2 definition it cites requires two distinct IDs. Thus the displayed gate is rejected before truth-table semantics or proof sharing are considered. Reusing one such zero tuple for both children of an adder also feeds identical absolute IDs into term gates, independently violating the same port discipline. This is a precise failure of the exhibited witness, not a refutation of its asymptotic theorem.

### 2. Legal constant-zero factory

For distinct original IDs $z,a$, introduce paid gates

$$u\leftrightarrow(z\wedge a),\qquad v\leftrightarrow(z\wedge\neg a),\qquad o\leftrightarrow(u\wedge v).$$

Every gate has distinct input IDs and a globally fresh output. From the defining facets, ordinary oriented Resolution derives

$$ (\neg o\vee u),(\neg u\vee a)\vdash(\neg o\vee a),$$
$$ (\neg o\vee v),(\neg v\vee\neg a)\vdash(\neg o\vee\neg a),$$
$$ (\neg o\vee a),(\neg o\vee\neg a)\vdash\neg o.$$

Build three independent copies with outputs $o_0,o_1,o_2$ and represent group zero by $(\neg o_0,o_1,o_2)$. These three ports have distinct absolute IDs. Build a fresh group for every zero leaf rather than sharing one tuple across two children. The extra charge is nine AND definitions and nine RES nodes per zero leaf, hence $O(h)$ over $O(h)$ leaves; it does not change an $O(h\log h)$ bound.
