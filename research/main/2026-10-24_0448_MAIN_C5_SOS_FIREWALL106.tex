\section{Declared equality-SOS model}
For a graph $G$ use Boolean variables $x_{v,c}$, $c\in\{0,1,2\}$, and the
one-hot equality system
\[
C_v=\sum_cx_{v,c}-1=0,\quad x_{v,c}^2-x_{v,c}=0,\quad
x_{v,c}x_{v,d}=0\ (c\ne d),\quad x_{u,c}x_{v,c}=0\ (uv\in E).
\]
A degree-four pseudoexpectation annihilates each axiom multiple of total
degree at most four and is nonnegative on squares of polynomials of degree at
most two.  This is a fixed proof/relaxation representation.  Its degree is
not a machine running-time measure.

On a labelled $C_5$, let
\[
Y=\sum_{uv\text{ a chord}}\sum_c x_{u,c}x_{v,c},\qquad Q=Y-2.
\]
Role 4 supplied explicit ideal terms and a degree-two polynomial $q$ for
\[
Q^2/3-Q\equiv I_L,\qquad 3\operatorname{Av}(q^2)+Q\equiv I_U,
\]
where the average is over ten dihedral and six colour symmetries.  The
independent program reconstructed both polynomials over $\mathbb Q$, reduced
them modulo Boolean, one-hot-exclusion and edge ideals, and verified both
coefficient maps exactly.

\begin{lemma}[Forced five-cycle moment]
Every degree-four pseudoexpectation in this equality encoding satisfies
$\E[Y]=2$ on each $C_5$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Applying $\E$ to the first identity gives
$\E[Q]=\E[Q^2]/3\ge0$.  Applying it to the second gives
$\E[Q]=-3\E[\operatorname{Av}(q^2)]\le0$.  Hence $\E[Q]=0$.
\end{proof}

\section{The exact SAT counterexample}
The predecessor's canonical degree-two functional has, for a five-cycle,
\[
\E_2[Y]=\frac53+\frac{J}{12},
\]
where $J$ sums common-neighbour counts over the five chords.  Every cycle
chord has at least its intermediate cycle vertex as a common neighbour, so
$J\ge5$ and $\E_2[Y]\ge25/12>2$.  Therefore it cannot extend to degree four.

\begin{theorem}[C5 representation firewall]
Canonical degree-two nonextension does not imply UNSAT, graph-colouring
hardness, or expensive deterministic continuation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The bare $C_5$ is 3-colourable.  Exact enumeration finds 30 labelled proper
3-colourings, and every one has exactly two monochromatic chords, hence
$Y=2$.  Their uniform distribution supplies genuine moments of every degree,
while the distinct canonical degree-two functional has $\E_2[Y]=25/12$ and
cannot extend.  Thus nonextension diagnoses the chosen canonical moment
assignment, not the instance label.  The source itself is of constant size
and directly decidable.
\end{proof}

This is stronger than a finite numerical solver observation: the labels,
moments and incompatibility are exact rational statements.  It is weaker
than a SAT lower bound for exactly the same reason: a decider need not build
or extend this moment functional.

\section{Independent audit of the paired controls}
For Role 4's 12-vertex graphs $H$ and $H^+$, exact exhaustive colouring gives
zero and 108 labelled 3-colourings.  Both are 4-regular and triangle-free;
they contain 44 and 28 five-cycles.  The six reported orbit equations have
rank six and force the seven nonedge pair moments to be
\[
(t,6t-2,t,2-4t,2-4t,t,t).
\]
For the induced degree-two Gram matrix $Z$, positivity on
$\mathbf1_{\{0,1,4,5\}}$ gives $t\ge7/18$, while positivity on the all-one
vector gives $t\le1/4$, an exact contradiction.  Therefore $H$ has no
degree-four pseudoexpectation in the declared equality encoding.

The audit made 494 counted exact assertions with zero failures.  It did not
replay the separate 2.67MB, 1,776-square static certificate; that larger
coefficient replay remains specialist evidence rather than an independently
promoted artifact here.  The orbit/PSD proof suffices for the restricted
infeasibility statement.

\section{Quantifier, source, and resource ledger}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT:} the two local polynomial identities; forced
$\E[Y]=2$; the SAT $C_5$ nonextension counterexample; both 12-vertex labels;
the six-equation family; the incompatible PSD bounds.  These quantify over
degree-four pseudoexpectations in the stated equality encoding.

\textbf{KNOWN THEOREM:} triangle-free maximum-degree-four 3-colourability is
NP-complete \cite{MP}; moment/SOS relaxations form an optimization hierarchy
\cite{Lasserre}.  NP-completeness supplies reductions, not unconditional
superpolynomial deterministic time.

\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL:} a standard-library Python audit used exact rational
arithmetic, made 494 assertions, and completed in about 21 seconds.  Peak
space was not instrumented.  It read the finite graph descriptions and did
not use a SAT or SDP oracle.

\textbf{RETIRED:} canonical nonextension as a label/hardness measure; fixed
core replication as amplification; low-degree SOS obstruction as an
unconditional all-machine time lower bound without simulation.

\textbf{OPEN:} an asymptotic family requiring growing SOS degree after all
source-aware shortcuts; more importantly, a theorem covering every total
uniform deterministic exact decider.  No claim is made about formula
evaluation gates, SAT-solving circuits, ETH, SETH, cryptography, or oracles.

\paragraph{Highest-value next missing lemma.}
Fix one NP-complete encoding and a source-computable obstruction $r(F)$
independent of runtime.  Prove a charged all-decider dichotomy: for every
uniform exact decider $A$, either acquiring/constructing a shortcut costs the
claimed amount, or after that shortcut $A$ still pays
\[
T_A(F)\ge 2^{a r(F)}/N^b
\]
on a growing family with $r(F)/\log N$ unbounded.  The theorem must be
representation invariant or explicitly simulate every decider with a
positive transport remainder.  C5-SOS-FIREWALL106 shows that failure to
extend one canonical pseudoexpectation cannot serve as this $r$.
