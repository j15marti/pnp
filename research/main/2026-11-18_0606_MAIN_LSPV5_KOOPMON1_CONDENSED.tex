\begin{proposition}[Exponential monomial closure with compact endpoint]
For each $k\ge1$, a degree-two nonlinear ANF source with $2k+1$ state bits and $O(k)$ ANF terms has exactly $2^{k+1}$ features in the closure above, but has a polynomial-time source-derived endpoint evaluator for every binary-encoded horizon.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let state bits be $y_0,\ldots,y_k$ and fixed controls $c_0,\ldots,c_{k-1}$. Take $y_i'=y_{i+1}(1+c_i)$ for $i<k$, $y_k'=y_k$, $c_i'=c_i$, with output $y_0$. At time $t\le k$ its output polynomial is $y_t\prod_{i<t}(1+c_i)=\sum_{S\subseteq[t]}y_t\prod_{i\in S}c_i$, containing $2^t$ distinct monomials. The exact monomial orbit has $1+\sum_{t=0}^k2^t=2^{k+1}$ features including the constant. Yet the same endpoint is computed by the factored product at $t=\min(T,k)$ in $O(k+\log(T+1))$ bit operations. Thus ANF orbit growth and increasing polynomial degree are NOT lower bounds on arbitrary endpoint algorithms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Matched-oracle PFCERT impossibility, conditional verifier schema]
Let $U^A$ be one efficient universal oracle interpreter and $t(n)=n^{\omega(1)}$. Suppose a $poly(n)$-proof-length $poly(n)$-time $A$-oracle verifier is complete for outputs $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ of every $|p|\le n-2$ program $U^A(p)$ running within $t(n)$, and globally accepts at most two distinct $x$ for each fixed $(n,p)$ over ALL proofs. Such a schema cannot exist with the same oracle $A=\mathrm{TQBF}$ supplied to interpreter, verifier and all algorithms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $E^A$ be accepted $(n,x)$ for some $(p,\pi)$. At length $n$, at most $2(2^{n-1}-1)=2^n-2$ strings are accepted, since there are $2^{n-1}-1$ programs of length $\le n-2$. Completeness places every complement string in $R_{K^{t,A}}=\{x:K_U^{t,A}(x)\ge |x|-1\}$. Since $E^A\in NP^A$ and $P^{\mathrm{TQBF}}=NP^{\mathrm{TQBF}}=PSPACE$, its infinite complement is in $P^A$. Hirahara's polynomial suffix-search proof relativizes with this SAME oracle and an efficient universal interpreter: given a short prefix of one accepted length-$n$ random string, enumerate $O(\log n)$ suffix bits with the $P^A$ decider; the resulting $A$-program prints a member of the infinite complement within $t(n)$ steps from fewer than $n-1$ bits, contradiction. This excludes a collapsing-oracle schema, not ordinary universal PFCERT existence or nonalgebrization.
\end{proof}
