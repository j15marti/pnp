\section{Primary-literature preflight}
The two obstacles checked first were the probability-normalized spread input and the
model boundary between named proof systems and arbitrary computation.

Mossel, Niles-Weed, Sun, and Zadik define an $R$-spread probability measure by
\(Pr[S\subseteq\mathbf A]\le R^{-|S|}\) and prove that a Bernoulli-$p$ set hits a
supported member with probability at least $0.9$ for
\(p\ge C\log k/R\) \cite{spread}.  This is a direct theorem transfer to the row-set
measure used by ROUNDINGSQUEEZE5.

De Rezende, Fleming, Janett, Nordstr\"om, and Pang prove supercritical trade-offs in
Resolution, semantic Cutting Planes, monotone circuits, and Weisfeiler--Leman, using
model-specific lifting theorems \cite{tradeoff}.  Their results support the source
width--depth family and semantic-CP/triangle interfaces.  They do not assert that an
arbitrary deterministic SAT computation emits a proof in any of these systems.

Thus the spread theorem and the source proof-complexity theorem are
\emph{directly transferable theorems}.  Treating their conclusion as an unrestricted
machine-time lower bound is a \emph{failed transfer}.

\section{Promoted theorem and exact scope}

\begin{theorem}[No-weakening spread lift; independently audited]

\[
 b\ge3,quad 1\le w\le\nu,quad
 0<\delta<1-\frac1{\log_2 b},\quad
 b^\delta\ge64C(w+1)\ell h.                              \tag{2}

\[
 s\le\tfrac12 b^{(1-\delta)w}

and depth $d$ solving $\operatorname{Search}(F)\circ\operatorname{IND}_b^\nu$

\[

of dense fibers, saved strips, full-image rows, or the extracted proof.
\end{theorem}
