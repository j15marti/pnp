\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}
A source of explicit length $N$ describes an $n$-bit clock ($n\le N$), a write-once indexed tape of $2^n$ bits, a power-of-two $d\le N^2$, and functions $f,g$ that are XORs of polynomially many affine-system indicator predicates. At step $a$ a pairwise-disjoint affine guard chooses a pointer $h_j(a)=A_ja\oplus b_j$. A \textsf{RANK} pointer has $\mathrm{rank}(A_j)\le\lfloor\log_2N\rfloor$; a \textsf{MASK} pointer has $A_j=I$ and $0<b_j<d$. Every active guard is source-checked to imply $h_j(a)<a$. The machine reads $T[h_j(a)]$ (or zero if no guard), sets $y'=y\oplus f(a)\oplus T[h_j(a)]$, and writes $T[a]=g(a)$.
\begin{theorem}[Source-checkable future-read observer]
Let $A_S$ be the union of the global images of all \textsf{RANK} pointers and the terminal-probe locations. Then $|A_S|\le N^2+N$. The set
\[Q_a=([\max(0,a-d),a)\cap\mathbb Z)\cup(A_S\cap[0,a))\]
has $O(N^2)$ cells, covers every active read, and satisfies $Q_{a+1}\setminus\{a\}\subseteq Q_a$. Define
\[Y(a)=\bigoplus_{v<a}\left(f(v)\oplus\bigoplus_j[G_j(v)]g(A_jv\oplus b_j)\right).\]
The inductive invariant $y=Y(a)\wedge\bigwedge_{i\in Q_a}T[i]=g(i)$ has polynomial descriptor, polynomial-time source evaluation, at most $O(N^2)$ tape-value queries against arbitrary supplied full configurations, initiality, consecution and exact terminal coverage. A polynomial verifier comparing a proposed output to $Y(2^n)$ and source-computed probe values accepts exactly one distinct output over \emph{all} proof strings for every valid source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
To check backwardness, $h(a)\ge a$ splits into one equality case and $n$ cases for the highest differing bit, each a solvable affine linear system. A rank-$r$ affine map has exactly $2^r$ global image values, enumerable from a Gaussian basis. For a mask $0<b<d=2^L$, $a$ and $a\oplus b$ agree on all bits at positions at least $L$, so strict backwardness implies the read is within the last $d$ cells. Every subsequent needed old cell was already tracked, and newly added cells are freshly written. The source prefix is a parity sum of polynomially many affine-system indicators after composing $g$ with each affine pointer. Partition $[0,a)$ into at most $n$ dyadic cylinders; parity of an affine system's solutions is one iff it is consistent and has rank $n$. Gaussian elimination yields $Y(a)$ in polynomial time. The next update therefore gives $Y(a+1)$, and all tracked cell equalities persist. Terminal probes are permanent anchors. The verifier recomputes the only possible output, so any different asserted output is rejected.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Restricted minimal future-read frontier]
For local-equality invariants of this form requiring $y=Y(a)$ at every phase, every already-written cell read again in the future must be checked at phase $a$. In particular a pointer $h(t)=t\oplus2^{n-1}$ on the upper half of the clock has $2^{n-1}$ future-live cells at midpoint, even when the source terminal answer is trivially zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Flip an omitted future-live cell in an arbitrary phase-$a$ configuration. It passes the local checks, is never rewritten, and eventually either violates a newly imposed cell check or changes the accumulator at its first subsequent read. Hence induction fails. At midpoint, the exhibited pointer will read each of the first $2^{n-1}$ cells once. This only obstructs \emph{local point-query} invariants, not succinct source-level proofs.
\end{proof}
