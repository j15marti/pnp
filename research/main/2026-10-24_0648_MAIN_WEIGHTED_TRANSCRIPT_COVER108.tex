\section{Target, quantifiers and three falsifiable mechanisms}
The target was a trajectory-sensitive obstruction that permits a uniform exact
solver to change representation, parameter vector and rule at every state.  It
must charge source acquisition, rule selection, switching, failed attempts,
continuation and peak live space, and it may not reveal the SAT label.

\paragraph{M1: weighted conditional cover.}
Opposite-label sources consistent with the observed transcript define a
minimum-cost distinguishing cover.  Each source observation can decrease this
potential by at most its own cost.  \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} below.

\paragraph{M2: summing residual potentials.}
The values of a large remaining-work potential may be added over successive
states to obtain superlinear or exponential work.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED}:
the valid accounting telescopes.  A $q$-field all-uniform input has potentials
$q,q-1,\ldots,0$ but costs only $q$ unit reads.

\paragraph{M3: SAT/UNSAT need the same state invariant.}
\textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED} on the exact unit-free quotient family below.  The
uniform SAT sources have cover $q$, while every mixed UNSAT source has cover
two.  A restricted policy stops on the first observed polarity disagreement;
full-language recognition and fallback remain fully charged.

\section{Weighted adaptive transcript theorem}
Fix a valid encoded domain $D$, language $L\subseteq D$, and a finite declared
source-observation schema $\mathcal A$.  Action $a\in\mathcal A$ returns
$g_a(x)$ and costs $c(a)>0$.  Internal computation, caches, learned clauses,
restarts and representation switches reveal no source response unless an
action is charged.  Fix input $x$.  At transcript $\sigma$, let
\[
Y_\sigma(x)=\{y\in D:L(y)\ne L(x),\ y\text{ gives the same responses as }x
\text{ to all actions in }\sigma\}.
\]
For $y\in Y_\sigma(x)$ define
$E(x,y)=\{a\in\mathcal A:g_a(x)\ne g_a(y)\}$, and let
\[
\tau_c(\sigma,x)=
\min\left\{\sum_{a\in H}c(a):H\subseteq\mathcal A,
H\cap E(x,y)\ne\varnothing\quad\forall y\in Y_\sigma(x)\right\},
\]
with value zero when $Y_\sigma(x)$ is empty.

\begin{theorem}[Weighted transcript cover; PROVED/EXACT]\label{th:cover}
For every total deterministic exact decider $A$, every input $x$, and every
reachable transcript $\sigma$ on $x$, the cost of future distinct informative
source observations is at least $\tau_c(\sigma,x)$.  If the next new action is
$a$ and the new transcript is $\sigma a$, then
\[
0\le \tau_c(\sigma,x)-\tau_c(\sigma a,x)\le c(a).                 \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
\tau_c(\varnothing,x)\le \sum_{a\text{ newly queried on }x}c(a)
\le \sum_{a\in\mathcal A}c(a).                                  \tag{2}
\]
Once all relevant source responses are known, this potential is zero and
supplies no post-acquisition computation lower bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a completed exact execution has queried no action in $E(x,y)$ for some
opposite-label $y$ consistent with the current transcript, determinism gives
the same future source responses, state trajectory and output on $x$ and $y$,
a contradiction.  Hence its future new actions hit every $E(x,y)$, proving the
first claim.  Querying $a$ deletes precisely those remaining edges containing
$a$.  A minimum cover after the query, together with $a$, covers every edge
before it, giving the upper inequality in (1); deleting edges cannot increase
the optimum, giving the lower inequality.  Telescoping (1) proves (2).  Cached
reads and internal rule changes leave the consistent-source set unchanged.
\end{proof}

The theorem covers heterogeneous bit, word, record and source-oracle costs
when those are the declared actions.  SIMD, compressed-oracle or advice models
require their own action schema and simulation.  It is an exact input-access
lower bound, not a lower bound on unrestricted deterministic continuation.

\section{Unit-free equality-quotient family and exact asymmetry}
For $q\ge2$ use source variables $x_1,\ldots,x_{3q}$ and the binary equality
backbone
\[
B_q=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{3q-1}
[(\neg x_i\vee x_{i+1})\wedge(x_i\vee\neg x_{i+1})].
\]
For orientation $b_i\in\{0,1\}$ let $T_i(1)$ be the all-positive clause on
$x_{3i+1},x_{3i+2},x_{3i+3}$ and $T_i(0)$ the all-negative clause.  Put
$F_{q,b}=B_q\wedge_iT_i(b_i)$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact labels, backdoor and weighted covers]
$F_{q,b}$ is SAT iff $b$ is uniform.  It has $n=3q$, $m=7q-2$, no unit
clauses, and minimum raw strong 2-CNF deletion backdoor $h=q$.  In the
orientation-field query model with costs $c_i>0$,
\[
\tau_c(b)=
\begin{cases}
\sum_i c_i,&b\text{ uniform (SAT)},\\
\min_{i:b_i=0}c_i+\min_{j:b_j=1}c_j,&b\text{ mixed (UNSAT)}.
\end{cases}                                                     \tag{3}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$B_q$ forces all source variables equal.  All positive triples require their
common value one and all negative triples require zero, proving the label.
The $q$ ternary supports are disjoint, so a strong deletion backdoor must meet
each and one variable from each suffices.  At an all-one orientation, flipping
any one field produces a mixed opposite-label source, so every singleton is a
difference edge and every field is required; similarly for all zero.  At a
mixed orientation the only opposite-label orientations are all zero and all
one.  Their difference sets are respectively the positive and negative
positions, which are disjoint; the cheapest hitting set chooses one field from
each set, proving (3).
\end{proof}

Paid SCC computation on $B_q$ identifies one positive and one negative
equivalence class.  Every ternary then becomes a unit on one representative.
Thus a total exact hybrid---charged source validation, SCC quotient, reduced
SCC, SAT witness replay, and exact fallback outside the recognized class---runs
in $O(q)$ word operations and $O(q)$ live logical words on this family.  This
extends QUIET-EQUIV67 from one negative terminal control to all $2^q$
orientations and gives an exact SAT/UNSAT transcript asymmetry.

\section{Independent audit and ladder transport}
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL.} A deterministic standard-library audit exhaustively
tested all orientations for $q=2,\ldots,8$ with nonuniform positive costs.  It
brute-forced every weighted cover, checked formula (3), checked every prefix
of every natural-order transcript against (1) and (2), and verified the
restricted adaptive policy.  Four $q=1024$ controls covered both uniform SAT
labels, an early mixed UNSAT switch (two orientation reads), and a late mixed
UNSAT switch (1024 reads).  Total: 8,710 assertion-accounted checks, zero
failures.  No random seed was used.  Python RSS was not measured; all reported
counts use the stated logical source-event basis.

The transport implication is exact but negative.  A weighted transcript cover
can preserve a charged source-query lower bound under a model-matched
simulation.  Since its total budget is bounded by the cost of reading every
declared source action, it cannot by itself yield work exceeding input
acquisition.  Explicit reductions still obey
\[
L_{\rm old}\le R+H+Q\tau T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g),
\]
and only a positive numerator $L_{\rm old}-R-H$ transports.  For these linear
source bounds, explicit construction can consume the entire numerator.

\paragraph{Highest-value missing lemma.}
After the complete encoded input is available, define a representation-neutral
state/trajectory object independently of measured runtime and prove that every
total uniform exact decider either pays superpolynomial acquisition,
recognition and switching cost or reaches a state with unavoidable
superpolynomial continuation.  The statement must survive SCC equivalence,
Horn and affine rules, learning, backtracking, restarts, decomposition,
memoization, rereading, compact representations and direct decision.  No
source-only transcript cover can fill this gap because it is zero after full
acquisition.
