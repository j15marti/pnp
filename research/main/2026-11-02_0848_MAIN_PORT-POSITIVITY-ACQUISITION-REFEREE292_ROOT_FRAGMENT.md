### LSPV-5 REFEREE292: positive guard ports exist universally but are not freely acquired

For every valid canonical original, SAT is equivalent to the existence of a signed source port with \(W_{11}>0\), and every SAT model certifies at least \(n\) such ports. Designated-port positivity is NP-complete via the valid target-preserving product \(F\mapsto F\sqcup Z6\), whose fixed Z6 port has count \(6\,#SAT(F)\). Thus the LSPV-4 338 conservation theorem is sound but supplies no free adaptive selector. Independent F9/Z6/product circuits, OBDD, same-original SAT/UNSAT, S/U, and malformed controls passed. NP-completeness is not a superpolynomial lower bound; no all-input polynomial controller, arbitrary-A power-short Phi, or strict same-machine FLMV transfer is proved. P versus NP OPEN.
