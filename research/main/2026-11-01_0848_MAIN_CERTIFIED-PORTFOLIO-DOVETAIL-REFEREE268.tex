\section{Certified finite-portfolio theorem}
Fix a source encoding $x$ and $k$ public deterministic policies.  Every
policy starts from blank writable tapes, reads $x$ through the declared input
interface, and either remains unresolved or emits a SAT assignment or a
complete UNSAT certificate.  A checker $V_x$ accepts only certificates scoped
to the original source.  Let $T_i(x)$ include grammar/source access,
recognition, failed internal trials, certificate construction and output.

\paragraph{Theorem (fixed finite oracle elimination).}
Run each policy from scratch at budgets $1,2,4,\ldots$, one policy resident at
a time, discarding every unfinished state.  Put
$T_*(x)=\min_i T_i(x)<\infty$.  The first valid result is returned after
\[
 W(x)<4kT_*(x)+O(k\log T_*(x))+V_x,
\]
and peak writable space is
\[
 \max_{i,b\le2^{\lceil\log T_*\rceil}}S_i(x,b)
 +S_V(x)+O(\log k+\log T_*).
\]
No best-policy index or runtime is advice.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Let $j=\lceil\log_2T_*\rceil$.  Some policy completes in stage $j$.
All allocated policy steps before and in that stage are at most
\[
k\sum_{r=0}^{j}2^r<k2^{j+1}\le4kT_*.
\]
The scheduler pays one dispatch/reset record per attempted run, hence
$O(k\log T_*)$.  A result is accepted only after the original-source checker
runs, so an incorrect or incomplete output cannot terminate the controller.
Because unfinished states are discarded and only one policy is resident,
workspaces are maximized rather than summed.  Source rereads and replay occur
inside each budget and are therefore included. \hfill$\square$

\section{All-input recurrence and explicit missing implication}
For a fixed portfolio the exact controller bound is
\[
 T_D(F)\le 4k\min_iT_i(F)+O(k\log\min_iT_i(F))+V_F.
\]
Adding complete fallback makes $T_*(F)$ finite on both labels, but gives only
\[
T_D(N)\le O\!\left(k\max_{|F|=N}\min_iT_i(F)\right)+V(N),
\]
which may be exponential.  The missing P=NP-style implication is
\[
 \exists c\;\forall F\in L_{\rm D\ddot{o}cker}:
 \min_iT_i(F)+V_F\le |F|^c.
\]
No verified structural terminal, favorable-policy disjunction, finite test,
or trivial DPLL completeness proves it.  The ARC alternative still needs a
power-short fully charged universal $A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ for unknown exponents
and a strict numerical same-machine FLMV transfer.  Neither is proved.
