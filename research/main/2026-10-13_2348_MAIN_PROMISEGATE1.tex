\section{Primary literature and transfer classification}
Cook's theorem gives NP-completeness of Boolean satisfiability and therefore
NP-completeness of Circuit-SAT under standard encodings \cite{Cook}.  Its
complement Circuit-UNSAT is coNP-complete.  This is a direct theorem transfer:
the reductions below use the very same circuit truth predicate.

Tseitin's definitional translation replaces circuit gates by linearly many
local clauses \cite{Tseitin}.  Full bi-implication gate clauses uniquely fix
every auxiliary value after the primary inputs are fixed.  That exact
definition property transfers; proof-complexity lower bounds for Tseitin
contradictions do not transfer to unrestricted witness-search time.

Parsimonious reductions are the correct structural analogy: the repaired
extension gives a bijection of satisfying assignments.  Counting preservation
or binding alone is not hardness.  The post-quantum ``unique opening implies
binding'' analogy is a \textbf{FAILED TRANSFER} here because the opening is
uniformly computed.  Schur powers, relation ranks, syzygies, and LWE-style
worst-case/average-case architecture are \textbf{ANALOGY/CANDIDATE}; no exact
object correspondence supplies the missing all-program lower bound.

\section{Two exact recognition obstructions}
Let \textsc{EvenCircuit} contain circuits $c:\F^n\to\F^v$ satisfying
$\bigoplus_i c_i(u)=0$ for every $u$.  Let \textsc{InversePair} contain pairs
of circuits $P,J:\F^d\to\F^d$ satisfying
$J(P(z))=P(J(z))=z$ for every $z$.

\begin{theorem}[promise-recognition firewall]
Both \textsc{EvenCircuit} and \textsc{InversePair} are coNP-complete under
polynomial-time many-one reductions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Their complements have a polynomial witness: one input on which parity is odd,
or one vector on which either composition differs from the identity.

For hardness, let $C:\F^n\to\F$ be a Circuit-SAT instance.  Define
\[
 c_C(u)=(C(u),0).
\]
Then $c_C\in\textsc{EvenCircuit}$ exactly when $C$ is identically zero, i.e.,
when $C$ is unsatisfiable.

For the second language set $P=\mathrm{id}$ on pairs $(u,t)$ and
\[
 J_C(u,t)=(u,t\oplus C(u)).
\]
The circuit $J_C$ is itself an involution, but it is an inverse of $P$ exactly
when $C(u)=0$ for every $u$.  Thus Circuit-UNSAT reduces to each validity
language.
\end{proof}

Consequently, the general PERMEXT1 phrase ``the recognizer checks the
even-charge output contract'' or verifies an arbitrary declared inverse is not
a proved polynomial recognizer.  This does not invalidate its semantic theorem
on promised valid records.  It retires only the unrestricted off-image claim.

\paragraph{Smallest repair.}
Use the fixed Gold circuit $P_d(z)=z^3$ for odd $d$ over a canonically encoded
irreducible modulus, and derive its inverse exponent from $d$.  For each graph
component, encode free charge outputs $r_1,\ldots,r_{v-1}$ and define
\[
 c_v=\bigoplus_{i<v}r_i.
\]
Parity is then true by syntax, not by an unverified semantic promise.
Connectivity, spanning-tree validity, odd cycle rank, circuit syntax, and
polynomial-basis irreducibility are all decidable in polynomial time.  Unknown
keys or malformed records are rejected.

\section{Repaired unique-extension neutrality theorem}
Let $E(F)$ be the repaired record compiled from a source CNF $F(u)$.  It
contains the source literally, public circuits $b(u)$ and parity-by-construction
$c(u)$, a connected typed graph $X$ with a declared spanning tree and cotree
of odd size $d$, and full definitional clauses.  Its semantic core is
\[
 G_F(u,x)=F(u)\land[B_Xx=c(u)]
                \land[(\pi_Cx)^3=b(u)].                 \tag{1}
\]

\begin{theorem}[charged search equivalence]
On repaired records, projection and lifting are uniform polynomial-time
inverse maps between satisfying witnesses of $F$ and $G_F$.  Explicitly,
\[
 z=b(u)^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3},\qquad
 x=\operatorname{Lift}_{c(u)}(z),                       \tag{2}
\]
followed by topological gate evaluation.  Hence source witness search and
target witness search on the compiler image are polynomial-time equivalent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For odd $d$, cubing permutes $\mathbb F_{2^d}$ and (2) is its unique inverse.
Fixing cotree coordinates and an even charge leaves exactly one assignment to
the declared spanning-tree edges: solve leaf-to-root.  Full bi-implication
gate clauses then force exactly one gate trace.  Thus each satisfying $u$ has
exactly one complete target extension.  Conversely every target witness
contains and satisfies the literal source conjunct $F(u)$; projection returns
$u$.  Parsing, field exponentiation, tree lifting, gate evaluation, witness
writing, and verification are polynomial in the explicit record length.
\end{proof}

This independently confirms the mathematical core of PERMEXT1 after the
recognizer repair.  The general semantic promise version remains valid, but
only the repaired typed language has the claimed polynomial off-image
behavior.

\section{Exact short-program budget}
Write
\[
 B(n)=b_0+\lfloor\log_2(n+2)\rfloor.
\]
Suppose the fixed decode/simulate/lift wrapper adds $c_0$ program bits.  A
typed semantics-neutral padding field is parsed and charged.  Choose padded
length $N'$ satisfying
\[
 N'+2\ge 2^{c_0}(|F|+2).                               \tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 B(N')\ge B(|F|)+c_0.                                  \tag{4}
\]
For every eligible source program $p$, the target wrapper $\widehat p$ obeys
\[
 |\widehat p|\le|p|+c_0,\qquad
 t_{\widehat p}(E(F))\le t_p(F)+\operatorname{poly}(N').\tag{5}
\]
Taking the minimizing source program gives the exact upper-bound form
\[
 H(E(F))\le H(F)+\operatorname{poly}(N').               \tag{6}
\]
The fixed $q(N')$ term in the definition of $H$ is absorbed into the displayed
polynomial.  Equation (6) does not itself bound the ratio $H(E(F))/H(F)$ by a
constant, so it does not refute every proposed growing-factor inequality.  It
does prove that this residual layer contributes no more than a charged
polynomial additive overhead on the compiler image.

For a polynomial-depth decoded hierarchy with $D\le N$ and per-layer cost
$O(N^k)$, the total extension cost is $O(N^{k+1})$.  Finite existential
iteration is not confused with an unbounded-depth uniform construction.

\section{Decoder trichotomy and falsification first}
The current corpus now requires three cases to remain distinct:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Typed image.}  A target-only decoder recovers the embedded source
and metadata; Theorem~2 and (6) apply.
\item \textbf{External reduction.}  The reducing algorithm retains metadata;
source-to-target witness transport is valid, but a target-only UWI program
cannot use uncharged per-instance side information.
\item \textbf{Untagged orbit.}  An automorphism can exchange candidate source
blocks, so a covariant canonical orientation is impossible.  This is not

block-swap invariant, $S^{-1}=S$, and the all-zero assignment is a model under
every variable renaming.
\end{enumerate}
Thus ``a canonical decoder fails'' and ``finding one witness is hard'' are
logically independent.  ORBITDECODERFW1's theorem is adopted at its stated
\CAND{} status pending separate promotion.
