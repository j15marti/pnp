\section{CONSTRUCTION OR PROOF ATTEMPT}

The loop executes one XOR store \(X_{A(u)}\gets X_{A(u)}\oplus B(u)\) at every \(r\)-bit index \(u\), in order. No unverified protected writes, early exit, or coefficient changes occur. Define
\(Q_R(X)=\bigoplus_a X_a\prod_{i\in R}a_i\) for \(|R|\le s\), with \(q_R^0\) computed from sparse initialization. Already-known cancellation gives full-loop neutrality when \(E+sD<r\).

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial phase correction]
For any \(T\subseteq[r]\) and \(0\le z\le2^r\), the parity
\(P_T(z)=\bigoplus_{u<z}\prod_{i\in T}u_i\)
is computed with \(O(r^2)\) bit operations. Moreover, at every intermediate machine state with exactly \(z\) indexed stores committed,
\[
Q_R(X)=q_R^0\oplus D_R(z),\qquad
D_R(z)=\bigoplus_{u<z}B(u)\prod_{i\in R}A_i(u).
\]
For constant \(s\), the entire symbolic correction vector is computable in polynomial time and space in \(N\), conditional on being given the actual frozen coefficients.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Partition \([0,z)\) into at most \(r\) aligned dyadic intervals. For a block with \(k\) freely varying low bits, a monomial sums to zero unless it contains all \(k\); otherwise multiply its included fixed bits. Inspect at most \(r\) coordinates per interval. Handle \(z=2^r\) separately. Expand each product of at most \(s\) address ANFs and one mask ANF into at most \(N^{s+1}\) pre-cancellation terms, and apply the prefix algorithm to each. Initiality holds at \(z=0\). An XOR store changes \(Q_R\) by exactly \(D_R(z+1)\oplus D_R(z)\), and private operations leave it unchanged. At completion, degree \(E+sD<r\) implies \(D_R(2^r)=0\). Controller coefficient acquisition and actual execution may be exponential; the polynomial bound is for a supplied-state symbolic query.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[No-snapshot parity obstruction]
For an unrestricted Boolean circuit \(C(u)\), deciding whether a complete fixed loop with constant mask one preserves all degree-\(\le1\) memory moments is \(\oplus P\)-hard.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let \(d=r+1\), \(A(u)=(C(u),0,\ldots,0)\), and \(B(u)=1\). The constant moment changes by \(2^r\bmod 2=0\); all coordinate moments except the first have zero change; the first changes by \(\bigoplus_u C(u)\). Thus neutrality is precisely even parity of satisfying assignments, a \(\oplus P\)-complete decision problem. Address collisions cancel by XOR. This is not an unconditional time lower bound.
\end{proof}
