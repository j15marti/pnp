\paragraph{Global status.}
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT, black-box route lemma; COMPUTATIONAL, finite controls; not a SAT lower bound.}
P versus NP remains \OPEN.  This run falsifies one proposed inference in the
all-input adaptive-decomposition spine: exact fallback and a reachable call to
an arbitrary unknown-exponent polynomial guest on a $\Theta(N)$-bit residual do
not yield strict exponent contraction.  It does not rule out a different
controller with power-short children, independent-component factoring, or a
direct uniform polynomial solver.

\section{Exact obstruction and falsifiable approaches}
The obstruction is whether a source-authenticated adaptive controller can turn
``exact fallback guarantees completeness'' into a nontrivial polynomial
total-work theorem without a favorable-policy oracle.  Four concrete claims
were attacked:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a selector may hand an arbitrary correct polynomial guest a
$\Theta(N)$-bit child and nevertheless obtain strict unknown-exponent ARC
contraction;
\item exact fallback plus finitely many local terminals implies polynomial
total work;
\item logical XOR distance lower-bounds DELAY, read-only RECOMP or shadow COPY;
\item finite F9/Y6/F15/U198/V198 coverage supplies an all-input potential.
\end{enumerate}
The first three are falsified at the stated scopes.  The fourth remains an
invalid extrapolation: these tests are controls, not a universal cover.

\section{Near-linear guest re-entry lemma}
Let $D^A$ be a uniform source-authenticated controller.  Suppose that on an
infinite family of reachable legal source states of encoded length $N$, an
actual path constructs or exposes a residual $y_N$ of length
$M(N)\ge cN^\lambda$ and waits for a correct guest $A(y_N)$.  Fix any correct
polynomial decider $A_0$.  For every fixed integer $q\ge1$, define the uniform
correct decider
\[
 A_q(y)=\textsc{Delay}(|y|^q);\ A_0(y).
\]
Then the charged total time on that path obeys
\[
 T_{D^{A_q}}(N)\;\ge\; C_{\rm grammar}+C_{\rm acquire}+C_{\rm select}
 +C_{\rm emit}+T_{A_q}(M)+C_{\rm verify}+C_{\rm output}
 \;\ge\;M^q=\Omega(N^{\lambda q}).
\]
Thus $\lambda=1$ preserves the arbitrary unknown exponent $q$ and is not a
power-short ARC transformation.  If instead a faithful lazy child interface
services $Q(M)\ge M^q$ child-bit requests by a full $\Theta(N)$ source rescan,
then $C_{\rm access}=\Omega(NM^q)$.  Materializing the child pays at least $M$
writes and retains those bits concurrently with its index and guest workspace;
streaming can avoid retention only by paying the ensuing access/recomputation.

The quantifier is deliberately narrow and exact: this lower bound applies when
the reachable computation actually makes and waits for this guest call.  A
controller that recognizes and bypasses the call is not covered.  Consequently
this is a black-box architecture obstruction, not a mandatory lower bound for
all target-language algorithms.

\paragraph{Fallback recurrence.}
For children $R_j$ the honest recurrence is
\[
 T(R)\le C_{\rm scan}+C_{\rm acquisition}+C_{\rm selection}
 +\sum_j\bigl(C_{\rm encode,j}+T(R_j)+C_{\rm check,j}\bigr)
 +C_{\rm failed}+C_{\rm output}.
\]
An exact fallback makes this recurrence finite.  If its number of leaves or
its own time is exponential, completeness alone does not make the sum
polynomial.  A separate all-input potential or solved aggregate recurrence is
still required, including transitions where $\delta$ increases and policy
attempts fail.

\section{Connected canonical family and paired exact controls}
The producer begins with the valid duplicate-free proper monotone F9 source and
forms a chain of $t$ disjoint variable blocks.  Across each boundary it swaps
one positive occurrence between the two first positive clauses.  Every
variable still has exactly two positive and two negative occurrences; all
clauses retain three distinct same-sign literals; clause duplication is
absent; and cross-block clauses make the primal graph connected.  Under the
shifted F9 SAT pin pattern, source and residual fixed-width lengths are:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{r|rrrrrrr}
$t$&1&2&3&4&8&16&32\\\midrule
$N_t$&220&496&736&1120&2512&5584&12306\\
$M_t$&88&184&268&400&880&1936&4242
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Hence $M_t=\Theta(N_t)$, with observed ratios converging near $0.34$.  This is a
structural counterexample to treating ordinary residual formation as
power-short.  The chain may admit other decomposition, so it is not claimed as
a hard family.

On the same original F9 source, exhaustive enumeration gives 70 root models,
two models under $x_1=x_2=0,x_8=x_9=1$, and zero models under
$x_3=0,x_6=x_7=x_9=1$.  The UNSAT cube's 32 completions each carry an original
falsified-clause ID.  Exact encodings are $N=220$, $M_{\rm SAT}=88$ and
$M_{\rm UNSAT}=78$ bits.  The no-producer-import verifier checked 75 assertions.
Y6 independently gives root/SAT/UNSAT counts $20/6/0$; F15 retains the
two-model SAT cube.  Five malformed F9 encodings are rejected.

The separate F9/F15 affine verifier passed 18 checks: F9 outside-cycle parity
is consistent but all three cycle choices refute; F15 has exact nonoverlapping
model coverage and a failed leaf with SAT siblings.  The fresh large replay
rejects all 64 U198 boundary rows in 152 module calls and checks the complete
V198 witness against all 264 original clauses.  These controls do not establish
hereditary closure or an all-input runtime theorem.

\section{Charged DELAY/COPY/RECOMP and XOR controls}
For F9 SAT, $q=1,2,3$ yields respectively 88, 7,744 and 681,472 DELAY steps;
full-parent RECOMP costs 19,360, 1,703,680 and 149,923,840 source-bit reads.
For F9 UNSAT the corresponding values are 78, 6,084 and 474,552 delay steps,
and 17,160, 1,338,480 and 104,401,440 reads.  COPY writes 88 or 78 child bits;
with the fixed child index its displayed peak is 95 or 85 writable bits before
adding selector, proof, guest and head state.

Logical residual endpoint XOR is zero in all these protocols.  DELAY and
read-only source traffic may perform arbitrarily much work with no data-bit
change.  COPY may write a shadow representation while the source-indexed
logical residual is unchanged.  Binary index cells can change and then be
restored, giving zero endpoint XOR but positive adjacent physical delta.  Full
XOR tracing remains an offline diagnostic unless its own reads/writes are
charged.

\section{Scope, heredity and remaining gap}
The originals in the connected chain are target-valid; their ordinary
residuals are noncanonical.  No paid target-valid reencoder is proved here.
The delta-zero affine terminal, grid terminal, K4 cut, matroid components,
Horn/2SAT/XOR closures and typed DAG remain useful restricted actions, but no
effective selector has a proved polynomial all-input aggregate recurrence.
Likewise no arbitrary-$A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ power-short theorem or strict numerical
same-machine FLMV transfer is available.  P versus NP remains \OPEN.
