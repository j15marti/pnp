\section*{Primary-source model check}
McKay--Murray--Williams define search-$\mathrm{MCSP}^A[s(n)]$ on the full
$N=2^n$-bit truth table: output an at-most-$s(n)$ AND/OR/NOT/$A$-oracle
circuit, or correctly report that none exists.  Their Theorem 1.3 states that
the absence of a one-pass deterministic $\operatorname{poly}(s)$-space,
$\operatorname{poly}(s)$-update algorithm for some constructible $s\ge n$
and $A\in\mathrm{PH}$ implies $P\ne NP$; their Algorithm 1 prints its circuit
at the end.  Thus a promised easy slice does not refute or prove the full
hypothesis.

Cheraghchi--Hirahara--Myrisiotis--Yoshida instead prove a randomized
two-sided-error $N^{1.99}$ lower bound for MCSP at a larger constant threshold
in the one-work-tape plus one-way read-only-input model, while citing a smaller
threshold where an $N^{1.01}$ lower bound would imply $P\ne NP$.  No threshold
transport is supplied here.  Oliveira--Pich--Santhanam require, for every
sufficiently small $\beta$, an $N^{1+\varepsilon}$ circuit lower bound for
$\mathrm{GapMCSP}[2^{\beta n}/(cn),2^{\beta n}]$ to conclude
$NP\nsubseteq P/poly$.  Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas concern
deterministic random-access SAT time-space, a fourth model.  These endpoints
must not be merged.

\section*{Two-sided sparse-stream escape theorem}
\paragraph{Theorem (\textbf{PROVED/EXACT}, scoped).}
Let $T$ be the lexicographically streamed truth table of
$f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$, $N=2^n$, and suppose the algorithm is given $K$
with
\[
 k:=\min\{|f^{-1}(1)|,|f^{-1}(0)|\}\le K.
\]
In the fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit basis with constants admitted as
zero-size inputs, a deterministic one-pass, end-output algorithm retains at
most $2(K+1)$ addresses and outputs a circuit for $f$.  For $k\ge1$ that
circuit has at most
\[
 n+k(n-1)+(k-1)=kn+n-1
\]
gates.  Its writable workspace is at most
\[
2(K+1)n+2\lceil\log_2(K+2)\rceil+n+O(\log s)
\]
bits, its per-symbol update work is $O(n)$ bit operations, it reads exactly
$N$ source bits, and its post-stream serialization is polynomial in the
circuit-description length.  Consequently, if $s\ge n$ and
$n+Kn-1\le s$, this promised slice has an $O(s)$-space,
$\operatorname{poly}(s)$-update search algorithm.  The $k=0$ constant cases
are immediate; without free constants they use only $O(1)$ additional gates.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Maintain capped lists $L_0,L_1$ of the positions of each bit value, stopping
each list after its $(K+1)$st entry and setting an overflow flag.  At end the
promise guarantees that at least one list did not overflow; choose a shortest
intact list $L_b$ of size $k$.  If $b=1$, output the DNF consisting of the
$k$ minterms indexed by $L_1$.  If $b=0$, output the CNF consisting of the
$k$ maxterms, each false at exactly one address in $L_0$.  Share the $n$
input negations.  Each term or clause uses $n-1$ binary gates and their
top-level combination uses $k-1$, proving the count.  The list and counter
sizes give the workspace bound.  Direct evaluation proves semantic equality.
\hfill$\square$

\section*{Strongest falsifier and allocation consequence}
The specialist's zero-prefix family has
$\binom{N/2}{k}$ completions, yet all those completions can be represented by
their $k$ exceptional addresses.  The theorem now shows the symmetric failure
near the all-one table.  Therefore neither a large version space nor an
exception count forces large terminal workspace: a lower-bound proof must
separate states that require genuinely different valid outputs or rejection,
not merely different source strings.  \textbf{FALSIFIED/RETIRED:} support-only,
co-support-only, and raw completion-count arguments as direct proofs of a
full search-MCSP streaming lower bound.
