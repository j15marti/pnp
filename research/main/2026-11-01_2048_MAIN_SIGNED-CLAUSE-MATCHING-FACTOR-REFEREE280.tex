\section{Obstruction and falsifiers}

The exact obstruction is architecture escape.  LSPV-2 first reduced F9's source-private
35-gate evaluator to 34, then to 33 gates by grouping only clauses that repeat a signed
two-variable pair.  These are correct minima only in their declared architectures.  A
forward-evaluator lower bound cannot be promoted while additional polynomially discoverable
factoring, alternate orders, affine/Horn/2SAT methods, source-aware SAT engines, and
DELAY/COPY/RECOMP guests remain admissible.

This run tested four claims:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the 33-gate F9 evaluator is stable under broader two-clause Boolean factoring;
\item only repeated two-variable pairs give polynomially discoverable sharing;
\item the broader factoring changes the original truth function or loses clause provenance;
\item a smaller evaluator supplies a smaller existential SAT decision bound.
\end{enumerate}
The first two are false; the third is refuted by identities and exhaustive checking; the
fourth remains unsupported.

\section{Maximum-weight signed clause matching theorem}

Let $F$ be any valid proper duplicate-free monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) original with $m=4n/3$
clauses.  Form separate positive and negative clause-intersection graphs.  An edge joins two
same-sign clauses when their variable supports intersect, with weight equal to the
intersection size.  Properness and duplicate-freedom imply weights are one or two.  Let
$W(F)$ be the maximum total weight of a matching in the disjoint union of these graphs.

\paragraph{Theorem.}
In the architecture allowing disjoint matched clause pairs to use the four circuits below,
unmatched clauses to use their ordinary two-gate evaluator, and all group outputs to be
combined by a binary AND tree, the exact optimum is
\[
       g_{\rm match}(F)=3m-1-W(F)=4n-1-W(F).
\]
The representation is uniformly acquired in polynomial work without a SAT-label oracle.

\paragraph{Factor identities.}
For pairwise distinct residual variables as required by the original clauses,
\begin{align*}
(a\vee b\vee c)(a\vee d\vee e)
 &=a\vee((b\vee c)(d\vee e)), &&\text{four gates},\\
(a\vee b\vee c)(a\vee b\vee d)
 &=(a\vee b)\vee(c\wedge d), &&\text{three gates},\\
(\bar a\vee\bar b\vee\bar c)(\bar a\vee\bar d\vee\bar e)
 &=\operatorname{NAND}\bigl(a,(b\wedge c)\vee(d\wedge e)\bigr), &&\text{four gates},\\
(\bar a\vee\bar b\vee\bar c)(\bar a\vee\bar b\vee\bar d)
 &=\operatorname{NAND}(a\wedge b,c\vee d), &&\text{three gates}.
\end{align*}

\paragraph{Proof of architecture optimum.}
Two ungrouped clauses use four local gates and consume one gate in the final AND tree: five
gates.  A matched weight-one pair costs four and saves one; a weight-two pair costs three and
saves two.  For a matching $M$, unmatched clauses cost $2(m-2|M|)$, groups cost
$5|M|-w(M)$, and the $m-|M|$ outputs cost $m-|M|-1$ AND gates.  The total is
$3m-1-w(M)$, minimized precisely by maximum-weight matching.  Exact signed occurrence quotas
let the intersection graph be acquired from the two same-sign occurrences of each variable;
sorting source-ID pairs costs $O(m\log m)$ word comparisons and a standard weighted matching
algorithm is polynomial.  Grammar, source/index/head reads, graph construction, matching,
gate output and provenance writes are not free. \qed

This exact optimum is only for the declared disjoint-pair architecture.  Overlapping
multi-clause factors, arbitrary Boolean networks, OBDDs, d-DNNFs, SDDs, affine structure or a
direct SAT solver may do better.

\section{Charged work, peak space and exact gap}

At a controller state $R$ the complete recurrence remains
\begin{align*}
W_D(R)={}&W_{\rm grammar}+W_{\rm source/index/head}+W_{\rm discover/compile/match}
+W_{\rm select/switch/failed}\\
&+W_{\rm proof/GE}+W_{\rm cache/replay}+W_{\rm child/output/access}
+W_{\rm guest}+\sum_{Q\in E(R)}W_D(Q).
\end{align*}
Every attempted representation is charged, not only the winning 28-gate circuit.  A naive
materialized clause-intersection graph and matching workspace use $O(m^2\log m)$ bits;
bounded-degree edge generation and streamed/recomputed matching can reduce space only by
paying the corresponding source rereads and computation.  Peak is the maximum simultaneous
parser, graph, matching, circuit, proof, cache, ancestor, child, index/head and guest space.
DELAY adds time without semantic progress; COPY writes are charged; RECOMP trades storage for
rereads; logical XOR is OFFLINE.

The forward circuit evaluates one supplied input in $O(g)$ work.  It does not find an
accepting input or certify that none exists.  No potential bounds the sum over all adaptive
trials and successors polynomially for every canonical original.  No representation here is
a power-short hereditary canonical child, and no ordinary-SAT-to-target locally addressable
reduction plus strict numerical same-machine FLMV transfer is supplied.  That is the exact
remaining gap.
