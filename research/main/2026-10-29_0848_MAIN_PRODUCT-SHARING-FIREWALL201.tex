\begin{abstract}
We independently reproduce the Connected Interface audit, the LSPV-3
MULTIPORT-PRODUCT163 construction, and the LSPV-2 sequential-frontier
controls.  We then give a concrete falsifier to the proposed direct-product
route to an arbitrary-SAT one-child contraction.  If every occurrence of a
proper 3CNF is replaced by a fresh variable, the clauses become independent
nonempty ternary relations and the result has exactly $7^m$ models, even when
the source is UNSAT.  Restoring the semantics by explicit pairwise equality
requires the equality graph on the $d_v$ copies of every source variable to
be connected, hence at least
\[
  \sum_v(d_v-1)=3m-n
\]
links.  Spanning trees attain this bound and recover the source pointwise.
For the eight-clause full-cube UNSAT control, the independent product has
$7^8=5{,}764{,}801$ models and requires 21 equality links.  With the concrete
15-variable/20-clause multiport block and the report198 two-swap equality
gadget, this instance expands to 435 variables and 580 clauses.  The result is
\textbf{PROVED/EXACT only for explicit occurrence copies joined by pairwise
equalities}.  Higher-arity consistency, shared-variable encodings, semantic
preprocessing, and computed history-free summaries may escape.  Therefore it
is an architectural coverage/representation firewall, not a SAT runtime lower
bound or a general contraction impossibility.  Effective arbitrary-instance
summary acquisition and P versus NP remain OPEN.
\end{abstract}

\section{PROPOSE: missing lemma and four falsifiable mechanisms}
The missing constructive lemma is an unconditional, uniform, answer-preserving
map on a closed encoded class containing SAT that emits one child of length at
most $N^\lambda$, $0<\lambda<1$, with all discovery, construction, access and
reconstruction costs charged.  Before search and build we fixed four tests.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Independent-product collapse.}  Disjoint ternary clause ports
may remember each clause but forget all shared-variable consistency, making
every unpinned product satisfiable.
\item \textbf{Paid occurrence consistency.}  Reintroducing one logical
variable through pairwise equality may need a connected graph on all its
occurrence copies and hence linearly many links in aggregate.
\item \textbf{Materialization floor.}  A bounded-fanin physical circuit or
gadget hierarchy may have logarithmic depth yet linear size.
\item \textbf{Semantic-oracle escape.}  A small history-free summary may
avoid physical sharing only by computing a semantic relation whose zero-port
case is already the SAT decision.
\end{enumerate}
Mechanisms 1 and 2 are proved exactly below; mechanism 3 was proved in
LOCAL-PORT-HIERARCHY-REFEREE200; mechanism 4 is an exact acquisition warning,
not an algorithm-independent hardness theorem.

\section{Independent reproduction of the supplied evidence}
\subsection{Connected Interface audit}
For the stated connected proper strict signed exact-$(2,2)$ 3CNF partition,
each nonempty private module touches the nonempty global boundary $Y$, and a
boundary variable has four clause occurrences.  Thus $k\le4b$.  If $B_i$ is a
syntactic clause-hitting set of size at most $q$ in private module $i$, then
$H=Y\cup\bigcup_i B_i$ hits every clause.  Since exact degree four gives
$m=4n/3$ and one variable hits at most four clauses,
\[
  n\le3|H|\le3b+12bq.
\]
Hence $b+q\ge\sqrt{n/3}-1/4$ for $b>0$; for $b=0$, connectedness gives one
module and $q\ge n/3$.  This is \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} for the declared static
syntactic hypothesis, not a runtime bound.  The unmodified audit reproduced
4,680 boundary checks, 7,191 local-discovery checks and 139 brute-force
comparisons.  All 130 sampled exact22 sign variants were SAT; that final
statement is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not a universal theorem.

\section{BUILD: the product--sharing firewall}
Let $F=C_1\wedge\cdots\wedge C_m$ be a proper 3CNF on the $n$ variables that
actually occur.  Let $d_v\ge1$ be the number of occurrences of variable $v$,
so $\sum_vd_v=3m$.

\begin{theorem}[Occurrence-disjoint product]
Replace every literal occurrence in $F$ by a fresh Boolean variable, retaining
its sign.  Call the resulting formula $F^{\mathrm{split}}$.  Then
\[
  \#\mathrm{SAT}(F^{\mathrm{split}})=7^m.
\]
In particular $F^{\mathrm{split}}$ is satisfiable for every source $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Properness gives three distinct occurrence variables in each clause.  A
three-literal clause has seven satisfying Boolean rows.  Different clauses in
$F^{\mathrm{split}}$ use disjoint variable sets, so choices multiply.  Thus
the global relation is the Cartesian product of $m$ seven-row relations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tight pairwise-sharing count]
Suppose shared-variable semantics is restored solely by adding pairwise
equalities among the $d_v$ explicit copies of each source variable $v$.  The
equalities force all copies of every $v$ equal only if at least
\[
  L=\sum_v(d_v-1)=3m-n
\]
pairwise links are present.  Equality spanning trees use exactly $L$ links and
the resulting formula is pointwise equivalent to $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Form a graph whose vertices are the $d_v$ copies and whose edges are the
pairwise equalities.  If it is disconnected, assign one connected component
zero and another one, satisfying every edge while not making all copies
equal.  Therefore the graph must be connected and has at least $d_v-1$ edges.
Summing gives $3m-n$.  Conversely, a spanning tree makes equality transitive
across all copies.  Every source assignment has one lifted assignment, and
every assignment satisfying the trees descends to one source assignment;
clause truth is preserved occurrence by occurrence.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of the lower bound.}
The theorem quantifies over explicit occurrence copies joined only by
pairwise equalities.  It does not lower-bound higher-arity consistency
relations, circuits with genuinely shared input wires, algebraic elimination,
semantic preprocessing, moving interfaces, or history-discarding summaries.
It therefore cannot establish an unrestricted SAT lower bound.

\subsection{A complete UNSAT falsifier}
On $x,y,z$, include one clause falsified by each of the eight assignments:
for assignment $a$, use $x$ when $a_x=0$ and $\neg x$ when $a_x=1$, and
similarly for $y,z$.  Every assignment falsifies its indexed clause, so the
source is UNSAT.  Splitting its 24 occurrences produces eight independent
clause relations and exactly
\[
  7^8=5{,}764{,}801
\]
models.  Each of $x,y,z$ has eight copies, so equality trees use
$3(8-1)=21$ links and restore UNSAT.  This is a specific falsifier to the
claim that independent exact clause ports already preserve arbitrary SAT.

The dependency-free reproducer checked the eight source assignments, 64 local
clause rows, 800 deterministic random proper 3CNFs and 27,680 source
assignments.  All identities passed.  These controls are
\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}; the two theorems above do not depend on them.

\subsection{Concrete exact22 cost, not universal gadget optimality}
Compose one 15-variable/20-clause MULTIPORT block per source clause with one
report198 two-swap equality gadget per pairwise link.  The explicit counts are
\[
 V=15m+15(3m-n)=60m-15n,
 \qquad
 C=20m+20(3m-n)=80m-20n.
\]
For the full-cube control this is 120 base variables plus 315 equality-gadget
variables, and 160 base clauses plus 420 equality-gadget clauses: 435 variables
and 580 clauses total.  This is a \textbf{PROVED/EXACT construction count} for
these named components, not an optimality theorem over all exact22 gadgets.

For canonical bounded-occurrence encodings with $n=\Theta(m)$ and
$N=\Theta(m\log m)$, the concrete physical composition has $\Theta(m)$
constant-size blocks and hence remains $\Theta(N)$ up to encoding constants;
it is not an $N^\lambda$ child for fixed $\lambda<1$.  This conclusion is
architecture-specific.

\section{SAT/UNSAT separation and one-program uniformity}
The constructor is one fixed program; it is not given a free answer-class bit.
It parses, splits occurrences, and builds the same equality trees on SAT and
UNSAT sources.  On the SAT restriction, every source witness lifts uniquely
through the trees.  On the UNSAT restriction, the tree equalities prevent the
spurious independent clause choices.  Removing the equalities makes both
restrictions SAT, so the failure is not side-specific.  The constructor does
not discover whether the source is SAT; any subsequent decision cost remains
fully chargeable.  Thus short certificates on one answer class do not create
two independent solvers.

\section{Transfer audit and post-quantum prompts}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Directly transferable theorem.}  Connectivity of a pairwise
equality graph is exactly the condition for forcing all explicit copies equal.
Tovey's occurrence-copy architecture is relevant precedent, but our count is
proved directly.
\item \textbf{Useful analogy requiring a bridge.}  DNNF decomposability and
communication rectangles explain why disjoint products are easy to combine.
A new representation theorem would be required before their lower bounds
apply to the present arbitrary-SAT target.
\item \textbf{Failed transfer.}  Circuit routing congestion, tensor-product
rank, Schur-square growth, or cryptographic binding do not by themselves
lower-bound semantic SAT summaries.  The present product has conspicuous
structure that helps an algorithm rather than certifying hardness.
\end{itemize}
No Schur-square, low-rank quadratic relation, collision entropy, or
worst-case/average-case theorem supplies the missing arbitrary-instance
bridge in this run.  Those prompts remain analogies/candidates only.
