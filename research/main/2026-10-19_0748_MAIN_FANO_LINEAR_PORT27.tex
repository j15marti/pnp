\section{The Fano equality gadget}
Let the endpoint variables be $a,b$ and let $u_1,\ldots,u_5$ be fresh.  Define
$\mathcal E(a,b)$ by the seven constraints
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\operatorname{NAE}(a,\neg b,\neg u_1),&
\operatorname{NAE}(a,u_2,u_3),&
\operatorname{NAE}(a,\neg u_4,\neg u_5),\\
\operatorname{NAE}(b,u_2,\neg u_4),&
\operatorname{NAE}(b,u_3,\neg u_5),&
\operatorname{NAE}(u_1,\neg u_2,u_5),\\
&&\operatorname{NAE}(u_1,\neg u_3,u_4).
\end{array}
\]
The unsigned supports are the seven lines of the Fano plane.

\begin{theorem}[Fano equality, \EXACT]
The existential projection of $\mathcal E(a,b)$ onto $(a,b)$ is equality.
Each equal row has exactly five extensions, every unequal row has zero, every
variable occurs exactly three times, and every two distinct clause supports
intersect in exactly one variable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The support and occurrence statements follow directly from the seven displayed
triples.  In the coordinate order $(u_1,\ldots,u_5)$, the satisfying extensions
are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(a,b)&\text{extensions}\\ \hline
(0,0)&11000,10100,11100,11110,11101\\
(0,1)&\varnothing\\
(1,0)&\varnothing\\
(1,1)&00010,00001,00011,01011,00111.
\end{array}
\]
Substitution into the seven NAE predicates verifies every listed row and
excludes the remaining $118$ complete assignments.
\end{proof}

An exhaustive canonical search quotiented each signed clause by simultaneous
literal complementation and considered every linear-support clause set on
$a,b$ plus at most four auxiliaries.  No equality gadget with positive equal
fibers was found.  With five auxiliaries, the displayed seven-clause gadget
was found after 60 search nodes.  This is a \COMP{} finite minimality result,
not a symbolic lower-bound theorem.

\section{A complete linear-support compiler}
Let $F$ be a proper 3-CNF with $m\geq1$ clauses and $n$ nonisolated source
variables.  Create one Boolean copy for every literal occurrence, so there are
$3m$ occurrence variables.  Create one gauge copy $g_i$ and one splitter $s_i$
for every source clause.  For clause $i$ with copied signed literal values
$A_i,B_i,C_i$, emit
\[
\operatorname{NAE}(A_i,B_i,s_i),\qquad
\operatorname{NAE}(\neg s_i,C_i,g_i).
\]
Connect consecutive occurrence copies of each source variable by one fresh
Fano equality gadget.  Connect the $m$ gauge copies by a Fano equality path.
There are
\[
E=(3m-n)+(m-1)=4m-n-1
\]
equality gadgets.

\begin{theorem}[Linear-port compiler, \EXACT]
The resulting signed NAE formula $L(F)$ is satisfiable iff $F$ is satisfiable.
It has
\[
\boxed{N_L=25m-5n-5}\quad\text{variables},\qquad
\boxed{M_L=30m-7n-7}\quad\text{clauses}.
\]
Every pair of distinct clause supports intersects in at most one variable and
every target variable occurs at most seven times.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each Fano gadget enforces equality of its endpoints.  Hence all copies of a
source variable agree and all gauge copies equal one bit $g$.  Complementing
every copied source value when $g=1$ reduces each frontend to its $g=0$ case.
For $g=0$, existential elimination of $s_i$ gives exactly
$A_i\vee B_i\vee C_i$.  Thus a target witness projects to
$x_j=X_j\oplus g$, and every source witness extends.

The variable count is $3m+m+m+5E$ and the clause count is $2m+7E$, yielding
the displayed formulas.  Distinct frontends use distinct occurrence, gauge,
and splitter variables.  Within one frontend the two clauses share only
$s_i$.  Within a Fano gadget every two clauses share exactly one variable.
Different equality gadgets share at most one endpoint, and a frontend meets
an equality gadget in at most one copied endpoint.  Hence the whole support
hypergraph is linear.

Choose copy paths, so an occurrence or gauge copy belongs to at most two
equality gadgets.  It then appears in at most $2\cdot3+1=7$ clauses.  A Fano
internal variable occurs three times and a splitter twice.
\end{proof}

For a satisfying source witness $x$, let $h_F(x)$ count source clauses whose
third literal is true and whose first two literal values differ.  The two
global gauge values, five extensions per equality gadget, and local splitter
table give the exact fiber
\[
\boxed{\#\pi^{-1}(x)=2\cdot5^{,4m-n-1}\cdot2^{h_F(x)}}.
\]
Thus
\[
\#SAT(L(F))=2\cdot5^{,4m-n-1}
\sum_{x\models F}2^{h_F(x)}.
\]
The fiber is explicit but assignment dependent.

\section{Polynomial Calculus projection}
Use the standard two-forbidden-assignment polynomial encoding of each signed
NAE predicate.  Set every gauge copy to zero and every copied occurrence of a
source variable to that source variable.  For each frontend set
\[
s_i=(1-A_i)(1-B_i).
\]
For a Fano equality gadget whose endpoints both map to $q$, use the canonical
extension
\[
(u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4,u_5)=(1-q,1-q,0,q,0).
\]

\begin{theorem}[All-field PC/PCR retraction, \EXACT]
For every field $\mathbb F$ under the stated encoding,
\[
D_{\mathrm{PC},\mathbb F}(F)
\leq2D_{\mathrm{PC},\mathbb F}(L(F))+4.
\]
The same degree bound applies to PCR after mapping complement rails
consistently.  Independent proof-complexity review remains pending.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch with explicit axiom images]
Every substituted target variable has degree at most two.  The first frontend
NAE predicate becomes a Boolean-ideal consequence.  Of the two forbidden
monomials for the second frontend, one vanishes and the other is exactly the
source clause polynomial
$(1-A_i)(1-B_i)(1-C_i)$.  The canonical Fano extension makes all seven Fano
predicates true on both Boolean values of $q$, so their substituted axioms are
bounded-degree consequences of $q^2-q$.  Target Boolean axioms have substituted
degree at most four.  Expanding substituted multiplier polynomials simulates
each target inference with degree at most twice the target degree; the
bounded-degree Boolean repairs give the additive four.
\end{proof}

This theorem transports lower bounds only inside PC/PCR.  Conneryd et al.'s
all-field graph-colouring lower bound becomes relevant only after verifying a
constant-width source adapter.  It does not imply unrestricted SAT time.
