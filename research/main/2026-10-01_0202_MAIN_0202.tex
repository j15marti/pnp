\section*{PROVED/EXACT}
Let
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2,\qquad C_H=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{1,g_1,\ldots,g_m\}.
\]
Then
\[
\rho(E_H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}(C_H^{\star2})
=\dim\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_i g_j\}.
\]
If the quadratic evaluation kernel is nonzero, Singleton yields a nonzero quadratic annihilator with ANF support at most $\rho(E_H)+1$. Every nonzero Boolean quadratic has density at least $1/4$, and the verified constant-density reconstruction retuning handles such fixed density at constant asymptotic overhead.

For every finite accepted set $\mathcal A$ and map $L$,
\[
\operatorname{Coll}(L)=\sum_v |L^{-1}(v)|^2,\qquad
|\operatorname{im}L|\ge\frac{|\mathcal A|^2}{\operatorname{Coll}(L)}.
\]
If $L:G\to V$ is a homomorphism with kernel $K$, then
\[
\operatorname{Coll}_{\mathcal A}(L)=\sum_{d\in K}r_{\mathcal A}(d),
\qquad
r_{\mathcal A}(d)=|\{(a,a')\in\mathcal A^2:a-a'=d\}|.
\]
These are exact finite-map/group identities under their stated hypotheses.

\paragraph{New exact minimax correction from LSPV-4.}
Let $L:G\to V$ be a homomorphism of finite groups with kernel $K$, and let $A\subseteq G$ have $|A|=a$. Then
\[
\boxed{\min_{A\subseteq G,\ |A|=a}|L(A)|=\left\lceil\frac{a}{|K|}\right\rceil.}
\]
The lower bound follows because each image fiber is a kernel coset of size $|K|$; sharpness follows by filling as few kernel cosets as possible. For $G=\mathbb F_2^\ell$ and rank-$r$ $L$, the strongest image lower bound obtainable from only $|A|$ and $r$ is therefore
\[
|L(A)|\ge\left\lceil |A|2^{-(\ell-r)}\right\rceil.
\]
Combining this with the established extractor bad-rank threshold shows that cardinality and kernel rank alone can force a reachable full-quadratic-rank object only if
\[
\boxed{|A|>2^{\kappa+M+\ell-r}.}
\]
This is sharp at the level of those parameters.

\paragraph{Consequence for collision entropy.}
The collision identity remains exact, but it does not itself create anti-concentration. At fixed $|A|$ and $|K|$, the worst case packs accepted objects into the minimum number of kernel cosets. Therefore any improvement over the minimax image bound must use additional verified structure of the actual accepted set, such as affine constraints plus a controlled kernel intersection, pseudorandomness across cosets, local-test equations, or another explicit anti-concentration theorem.
