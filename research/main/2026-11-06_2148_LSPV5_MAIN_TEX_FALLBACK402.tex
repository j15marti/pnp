\paragraph{Restricted algebraic branch-guard lemma (LSPV-5).}
Let two Polynomial Calculus refutations of $\Gamma\cup\{x=0\}$ and
$\Gamma\cup\{1-x=0\}$ have line counts $L_0,L_1$, monomial-occurrence
sizes $S_0,S_1$, and degrees $d_0,d_1$.  Multiplying the first proof by
$1-x$, the second by $x$, and using $x^2-x=0$ gives a PC refutation of
$\Gamma$ of length at most $3(L_0+L_1)+1$, size at most
$4(S_0+S_1)+2$, and degree at most $\max(d_0,d_1)+1$.  In PCR, path
guards are monomials, so an explicit context-expanded decision tree can
be flattened with polynomial overhead in the displayed tree certificate.
This does not bound an unlabelled reconvergent shared DAG, authenticate
historical $R_{\mathrm{mix341}}$ rules or weights, establish an all-mixed-rule
lower bound, or prove B3.  These transfers and P versus NP remain open.
