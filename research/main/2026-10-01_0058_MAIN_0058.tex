\section*{PROVED/EXACT}
Let
\[
M=1+m+\binom m2,\qquad
C_H=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{1,g_1,\ldots,g_m\}.
\]
Then
\[
\rho(E_H)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}(C_H^{\star2})
=\dim\operatorname{span}\{1,g_i,g_i g_j\}.
\]
If the quadratic evaluation kernel is nonzero, Singleton yields a nonzero quadratic annihilator with ANF support at most \(\rho(E_H)+1\). Every nonzero Boolean quadratic has density at least \(1/4\), and the verified constant-density reconstruction retuning handles such fixed positive density at constant asymptotic overhead.

For every finite accepted set \(\mathcal A\) and exact extensional map \(L:\mathcal A\to\mathcal F\), with fiber sizes \(n_v\),
\[
\operatorname{Coll}(L)=\sum_v n_v^2,
\qquad
h_2(L)=2\log_2|\mathcal A|-\log_2\operatorname{Coll}(L),
\]
and Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
|\operatorname{im}L|\ge
\frac{|\mathcal A|^2}{\operatorname{Coll}(L)}
=2^{h_2(L)}.
\]
Thus the established extractor bad-rank counting lemma yields
\[
h_2(L)>\kappa+M
\Longrightarrow
\text{a reachable source object has }\rho=M.
\]
Perfect completeness alone supplies no useful lower bound on \(h_2\).

\paragraph{New exact kernel-energy specialization.}
Suppose, additionally, that the ambient proof/source objects lie in an abelian group \(G\) and the actual map is a fixed homomorphism
\[
L:G\to V,\qquad K=\ker L.
\]
For
\[
r_{\mathcal A}(d)
=
|\{(a,a')\in\mathcal A^2:a-a'=d\}|,
\]
one has the exact identity
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Coll}_{\mathcal A}(L)
=
\sum_{d\in K}r_{\mathcal A}(d)
}.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{
|L(\mathcal A)|
\ge
\frac{|\mathcal A|^2}
{\sum_{d\in K}r_{\mathcal A}(d)}
}.
\]
If \(\mathcal A=a_0+U\) is affine, this specializes exactly to
\[
\operatorname{Coll}_{\mathcal A}(L)=|\mathcal A|\,|U\cap K|,
\qquad
|L(\mathcal A)|=\frac{|\mathcal A|}{|U\cap K|}.
\]
These are unconditional finite-group identities under the stated homomorphism/affine hypotheses. No theorem currently establishes those hypotheses for the vMS accepted family.
