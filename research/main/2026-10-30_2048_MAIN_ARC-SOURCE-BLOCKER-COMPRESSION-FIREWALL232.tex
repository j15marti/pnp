\section{Exact source/blocker theorem}
Fix an explicit CNF $F$ of encoded length $N$ and clauses
$D_1,\ldots,D_r$ satisfying $F\models D_i$ for every $i$.  Put
\[
 H=F\wedge\bigwedge_iD_i,qquad B=\bigwedge_iD_i.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Source-preserving versus blocker-only]
The following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H\equiv F$.  In the declared append-only explicit encoding, the source
bits occur verbatim inside $H$, so $|H|\ge N$.  Therefore $H$ cannot be a
physical child of length at most $N^\lambda$ for any fixed $\lambda<1$ and
$N>1$.
\item $F\models B$.  Hence $\operatorname{UNSAT}(B)$ certifies
$\operatorname{UNSAT}(F)$ and $\operatorname{SAT}(F)$ implies
$\operatorname{SAT}(B)$.  The converse implication
$\operatorname{SAT}(B)\Rightarrow\operatorname{SAT}(F)$ is false in general.
Consequently $B$ alone is a relaxation or proof endpoint, not an exact
ordinary-SAT child without a separately checked completeness/equivalence
theorem.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $D_i$ is true in every model of $F$, giving $F\equiv H$ and
$F\models B$.  The length statement follows from the declared representation,
which contains the complete encoding of $F$ before the appended clauses.
If $B$ is UNSAT, no model of $F$ can exist.  The counterexample for the missing
converse is given in the next construction.  No claim is made about an
arbitrary semantic re-encoding that has independently proved exactness and paid
construction/access costs.
\end{proof}

This theorem separates three objects that the ARC recurrence cannot merge:
a globally valid learned consequence, a complete proof endpoint, and an exact
smaller ordinary-SAT instance.  The first need not be either of the latter two.

\section{Matched proper-3CNF counterfamily}
For $b\ge1$, partition $3b$ variables into triples $X_j$.  On each triple take
the seven proper ternary clauses falsified by the assignments other than
$111$, then duplicate the clause falsified by $000$.  Let $S_b$ be the
conjunction over the $b$ blocks.  It has $8b$ clauses and the unique model
$1^{3b}$.  Let $U_b$ replace the duplicate in the first block by the missing
clause falsified by $111$.  Then $U_b$ is UNSAT.  Both sources have exactly
$8b$ clauses and the same unsigned clause-support multiset.

Both sources entail the same blocker conjunction
\[
 B_b=x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_{3b}.
\]
It is satisfiable, with unique model $1^{3b}$, so it cannot distinguish the
source labels.  Moreover, for every $i$, the residual under $x_i=0$ is UNSAT
on both sources and the source-only assumption-discharge theorem yields the
valid unit blocker $x_i$.  These $3b$ cubes cover every assignment except
$1^{3b}$.  Exact evaluation of that one uncovered completion returns SAT for
$S_b$ and UNSAT for $U_b$.  Thus globally valid blockers and exact residual
answers still require either a complete top-level proof or an exact uncovered
fallback.

This is the precise repair to the invalid shortcut: retain $F$, produce and
check a complete proof that the learned clauses refute it, or prove a separate
answer-preserving re-encoding.  None is free.

\section{Independent peer audits}
\paragraph{LSPV-1 ARC-AFFINE-CUBE-COVER261.}
The restricted counting theorem is correct under its stated raw-syntactic
cofactor hypotheses.  In one parity block, fixing $0,1,2,3$ variables leaves
respectively $(c,v)=(4,4),(2,2),(1,1),(0,1)$ residual clauses and compatible
models.  Hence $v\le2^{c/2}$ and a source-relative cover by children with at
most $s$ residual clauses obeys
\[
 q\ge4^b/2^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}.
\]
For explicit source length $N=\Theta(b\log b)$ and fixed $\lambda<1$,
$s\le N^\lambda=o(b)$ gives $q\ge2^{2b-o(b)}$.  The inference is restricted
to raw cofactors; the same affine source is solved directly in polynomial time,
so no arbitrary-algorithm lower bound follows.

\paragraph{LSPV-2 and LSPV-3.}
LSPV-2's source-only DPLL assumption discharge is sound: a completed NO branch
can export a source-derived clause, but acquisition may be exponential and the
source-preserving formula does not shrink.  LSPV-3's parity-moment camouflage
calculation is also sound: a connected cubic incidence matrix has rank $q-1$,
so all proper subsets of syndrome coordinates are uniform and the first
$q-1$ moments coincide across charge parities.  A signed-source scan plus
Gaussian elimination is a paid easy escape; moments are not a decision oracle.

\paragraph{LSPV-4 cover gate and FLMV attribution correction.}
The reduction $H\mapsto F_H$ proving satisfiable-source backbone recognition
coNP-hard is correct.  More importantly, the original FLMV Theorem~3.4 does
\emph{not} state a generic arbitrary-child total-accessor simulation.  It
constructs a particular Cook-style formula for a nondeterministic multitape
computation and explains how each requested bit of that formula is recomputed
in polylogarithmic time and logarithmic space.  The project's total virtual-
input head-shadow lemma may stand as an independently proved lemma under its
declared accessor hypotheses, but root MAIN must not attribute that generic
statement to FLMV Theorem~3.4.  FLMV Theorem~5.7 and its forbidden region are
