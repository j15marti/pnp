\section{Status and exact obstruction}

\textbf{PROVED/EXACT first-step theorem; COMPUTATIONAL exhaustive controls;
iteration and universal complexity OPEN.}  The exact obstruction is loss of
the source occurrence quota.  Each original variable occurs twice positively
and twice negatively, so its first Davis--Putnam elimination has a $2\times2$
pair bound.  The projected CNF is ordinary noncanonical mixed 4-CNF; later
variables need not retain two occurrences of either sign.

Three falsifiable mechanisms were tested.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A first existential projection must increase the source clause count or
must first acquire the full forward circuit.
\item The $2\times2$ candidate-resolvent quota persists under iteration.
\item Every elimination order has the same charged candidate-pair cost on F9.
\end{enumerate}
All three are false.  The first-step bound is useful, but it is not a
hereditary all-input complexity theorem.

\section{Exact first-step theorem}

Let $F$ be a valid duplicate-free proper D"ocker monotone
$3$-SAT-$(2,2)$ original with $m$ clauses, and let $x$ be an original
variable.  Davis--Putnam elimination of $x$ considers exactly four
positive--negative source-clause pairs.  After discarding tautologies,
duplicates and subsumed clauses, the result $E_x(F)$ satisfies
\[
 E_x(F)\equiv \exists x\,F,\qquad
 |E_x(F)|\le m,\qquad
 L(E_x(F))\le 3m+4,\qquad
 \operatorname{width}(E_x(F))\le4,
\]
where $L$ counts literal records.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Exactly two clauses contain $x$ and exactly two contain $\neg x$.  Remove
these four clauses and resolve every positive clause with every negative
clause.  There are $2\cdot2=4$ candidate pairs.  Each resolvent deletes the
pivot and contains at most four other literals.  Hence at most
$(m-4)+4=m$ clauses and at most $3(m-4)+4\cdot4=3m+4$ literal records remain.
Tautology deletion, duplicate deletion and subsumption only decrease these
quantities.  The standard Davis--Putnam resolution-elimination identity gives
the pointwise equality with $\exists x\,F$. \qed

This proof uses no assignment recovery, injective pin key, SAT-label oracle,
forward-circuit lower bound, or post-answer constant circuit.

successor sum, favorable-order discovery, failures before selection, or the
width/occurrence products after the noncanonical first projection.  It gives
neither a polynomial-total-work all-input solver nor a universal
arbitrary-$A$ power-short $A\mapsto\Phi(A)$ construction.  No strict numerical

\section{Literature and implementation audit}

Queries covered Davis--Putnam elimination, bounded variable elimination,
occurrence-product and clause-growth bounds, elimination-order sensitivity,
project-local exact phrases, and organization \texttt{openai} code search.
E\'en and Biere's bounded variable elimination specializes the established
resolution method and deliberately prevents harmful formula growth:
\url{https://doi.org/10.1007/11499107_3}.  The original Davis--Putnam source
is \url{https://doi.org/10.1145/321033.321034}.  Current implementations
explicitly expose the same engineering issue: SATCH implements bounded
variable elimination
(\url{https://github.com/arminbiere/satch}), while OR-Tools bounds the
positive-times-negative occurrence product and weighted clause/literal growth
(\url{https://github.com/google/or-tools/blob/stable/ortools/sat/sat_parameters.proto}).
Project-targeted indexed search found no prior exact $m,3m+4$ first-step
statement; OpenAI-targeted searches found no relevant SAT implementation.
Search absence is not novelty evidence.

\section{Conclusion}

The source quota provides an exact, cheap first projection: at most $m$
clauses and $3m+4$ literal records.  It does not survive projection.  F9
already exhibits six and twelve-pair later steps and an exact 9-versus-35
order-cost gap.  Forward gate counts, fixed-order OBDD size and post-answer
constant circuits establish neither an all-input algorithm nor a lower bound.
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.
