Exact overlap counting for first-generation proof-carrying forbidden cubes is

assignment.  Therefore exact overlap is not a free clause-equity surrogate.

\section{Claim card and falsifiable target}
\textbf{Target.}  Falsify the implicit step ``sound forbidden cubes plus
exact non-double-counting gives a cheap operational progress measure.''
The exact prefix is: for every finite connected simple graph $H$, construct one
specified source $F_H$ and its first-generation source-parent resolvents; then
classify the exact cardinality of their union.  Source formula, resolvents,
certificates and finite strategy runs refer to the same realization.

\textbf{Declared models.}  The theorem is a discrete counting reduction.
Finite execution uses deterministic CPython on an indexed in-memory clause
array, recording clause scans, literal reads, probes, memo states, elapsed time
and simultaneous \texttt{tracemalloc} peak.  These counters are not silently
transferred to a tape lower bound.  The source may be retained, and a solver is
free to skip exact overlap counting and switch to Horn or 2CNF.

\textbf{Mechanisms tested.} (M1) deduplicate resolvents and count their exact
union; (M2) compile the cube union into a graph/2DNF representation; (M3) switch
to Horn/2CNF before search; (M4) use complement profiles, exact clause equity,
or raw pair weights to choose actions.  M1--M2 expose a counting barrier;
M3 is the easy control; M4 is diagnostic only.

\section{Signed occurrences, complement profile and RUP cubes}
Let $F=\bigwedge_j C_j$.  The occurrence $x_{i,j}$ denotes variable $x_i$
appearing in source clause $C_j$, not a fresh variable.  Its sign is
$\sigma_{ij}\in\{+1,-1\}$, its preferred literal-satisfying value is
$b_{ij}=(1+\sigma_{ij})/2$, and its status under a full assignment $a$ is
$\mathrm T$ or $\mathrm F$.  Under a partial assignment $p$ it is
$\mathrm T,\mathrm F$, or unset.  These fields are separately emitted by the
reproducer.

For proper ternary $F$, write $N_h(a)$ for the number of source clauses with
exactly $h$ true literal occurrences.  Full complementation satisfies
\[
N_h(\bar a)=N_{3-h}(a),\qquad
E(a)=N_0(a)+N_3(a)=E(\bar a),\qquad
D(\bar a)=-D(a),
\]
where $D=N_3-N_0$.  These are exact diagnostics, not SAT/UNSAT oracles.

For opposite parent occurrences
$(x\vee A)$ and $(\neg x\vee B)$, the non-tautological resolvent is
$R=A\vee B$.  Falsifying $R$ makes the first parent unit $x$ and the second
unit $\neg x$, so unit propagation conflicts.  Thus $(\neg R)$ identifies a
whole impossible completion cube and $R$ has a two-parent, one-pivot RUP
certificate.  This is ordinary resolution \cite{DavisPutnam}; no novelty is
claimed for the rule or for RUP checking.

\section{The graph-indexed Horn family}
Let $H=(V,E_H)$ be a connected simple graph, $|V|=n$, $|E_H|=m$.
Introduce one selector $x_e$ per edge and define
\[
F_H=\bigwedge_{e=\{u,v\}\in E_H}
 \bigl[(x_e\vee\neg u\vee\neg v)\land
       (\neg x_e\vee\neg u\vee\neg v)\bigr].                 \tag{1}
\]
Every source clause is proper, ternary and Horn.  The incidence structure is
connected when $H$ is connected.  Each selector has one positive and one
negative occurrence; graph variables occur only negatively.  Consequently
the complete set of non-tautological first-generation opposite-occurrence
resolvents is exactly
\[
R_e=(\neg u\vee\neg v),\qquad e=\{u,v\}\in E_H.              \tag{2}
\]
There are no hidden graph-variable pivots.  Each falsified cube is simply
$u=v=1$, with all other variables free.  Moreover
\[
F_H\equiv\bigwedge_{e\in E_H}R_e.                            \tag{3}
\]
The all-zero assignment is an immediately verifiable witness.  The source is
therefore easy even before invoking the general linear-time Horn algorithm
\cite{DowlingGallier}.

\begin{theorem}[Exact union classification]\label{th:hard}
Given a connected proper Horn width-three source of the form \emph{(1)},
computing the number $U(F_H)$ of full source-variable assignments contained
in the union of the forbidden cubes \emph{(2)} is \#P-complete under
polynomial-time Turing reductions.  Explicitly, if $I(H)$ is the number of
independent vertex sets of $H$, then
\[
U(F_H)=2^m\bigl(2^n-I(H)\bigr).                              \tag{4}
\]
SAT of every source in the family is nevertheless trivial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A graph assignment avoids every forbidden cube iff its set of true vertices
is independent.  Selectors do not occur in (2), giving the factor $2^m$ and
(4).  The union count is in \#P: guess one full assignment and accept exactly
when a canonical scan finds at least one falsified $R_e$.

Valiant proved \#P-completeness of counting models of monotone 2CNF
\cite{Valiant}.  Complementing its variables identifies those models with
independent sets of the clause graph.  To force connectedness, from any simple
graph $G$ add one universal vertex $w$ to obtain connected $H$; then
$I(H)=I(G)+1$.  One query for $U(F_H)$ therefore recovers
$I(G)=2^n-U(F_H)/2^m-1$ with polynomial arithmetic.  Finally (1) is Horn and
the all-zero assignment satisfies it, so this counting classification does
not make its SAT decision hard.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Firewall.}
\#P-completeness under the cited reductions is not an unconditional proof
that the function is outside FP, and it says still less about arbitrary SAT
algorithms.  It proves that any algorithm which insists on exact global
overlap has taken on a standard counting task that an answer-preserving Horn
solver need not perform.

\begin{corollary}[Distinct-cube false optimism]
For the star $H=K_{1,r}$, all $r$ resolvents are distinct.  Summing individual
uniform cube weights gives $r/4$, while their exact union density is
\[
\frac12(1-2^{-r})<\frac12.
\]
The ratio diverges.  Deduplication therefore does not repair additive pair
weight.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The union requires the centre to be true and at least one leaf true.  The
formula follows immediately.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact clause equity and criticality}
Recall
$Q_j(F)=|\{a:a\models F\setminus\{C_j\},\ a\not\models C_j\}|$.
For edge $e$ let $t_e(H)$ count vertex subsets whose induced edge set is
exactly $\{e\}$.  Directly from (1), for either parent clause of $e$,
\[
Q_{e,+}(F_H)=Q_{e,-}(F_H)=2^{m-1}t_e(H).                    \tag{5}
\]
Indeed, falsifying one parent fixes $x_e$ and selects both endpoints; its
sibling becomes true, every other edge resolvent must remain true, and the
other $m-1$ selectors are free.  Since the two endpoints alone witness
$t_e(H)\ge1$, every source clause is critical.  Thus large exact $Q_j$, many
distinct RUP cubes, and a \#P-complete exact-union statistic coexist with an
$O(N)$ syntactic witness.  Clause criticality is not decision difficulty.

\section{Adaptive representations and charged finite controls}
The source/resolvent DAG admits three faithful representations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item retain the $2m$ ternary Horn parents;
\item store the $m$ proof-carrying binary clauses (2), obtained after $m$
opposite-pair checks;
\item retain the graph and answer the SAT decision by the all-zero witness.
\end{enumerate}
All preserve the exact source answer.  Only exact cube-union cardinality needs
the hard counting query.  No width-four clause is silently converted to 3CNF;
the present resolvents already have width two.

\paragraph{Same-run resource ledger.}
For (1), occurrence indexing and pair discovery are $O(N)$ and $P=m$; storing
all resolvents takes $O(m\log n)$ bits.  Source-retaining Horn recognition and
witness verification are $O(N)$ indexed-RAM time with $O(N)$ source footprint
and logarithmic counters.  The explicit finite exact-overlap enumerator costs
$O(2^n m)$ time and $O(n+m)$ live words.  These are alternative executions,
not independent minima combined into a fictitious pair.  On a sequential
single tape, source rescans and head crossings must be charged separately;
this report proves no tape lower bound.  Communication rounds are zero.

\paragraph{DUPLICATION / CONTRADICTION AUDIT.}
The sound whole-cube resolution lemma, complement identities, repeated-cube
overcount and distinct-grid overcount were already present.  This report's
new restricted contribution is the graph-indexed Horn reduction: the exact
union itself is \#P-complete although source SAT is trivial, together with
(5) and source-parent RUP provenance.  It does not supersede the peers' UP
closure theorems.

\paragraph{WHAT CHANGED RELATIVE TO ROOT MAIN.}
Exact overlap is now classified as a potentially hard optional statistic,
not merely an overcounting correction.  The main synthesis should prefer
proof-carrying local cubes plus a representation switch; it must not require
global union cardinality from every solver.

\paragraph{ADOPTED / REJECTED / RETIRED PRIOR COMPONENTS.}
Adopt occurrence indexing, canonical non-tautological resolvents, RUP
provenance, contextual UP effects and fully charged discovery.  Reject exact
global overlap as an automatically computable potential.  Retire raw pair
weight, distinct-resolvent count, $Q_j$, or complement asymmetry as general
SAT difficulty measures.  Preserve Horn, 2SAT and XOR switches as mandatory
easy controls.

\paragraph{STALE OR OVERSTATED CLAIMS FOUND.}
Any statement that exact deduplication/union accounting repairs clause-equity
optimism without computational cost is overstated.  Conversely,
\#P-completeness of the optional count must not be promoted to an
unconditional runtime lower bound or arbitrary-algorithm bridge.  The family
is not strict signed exact-$(2,2)$.

\paragraph{Ranked five-role obligations.}
(1) characterize a polynomially computable \emph{marginal certificate}
potential that never asks for exact global union size; (2) prove a whole
adaptive branch-DAG upper bound on a syntactically recognized class, charging
all failed switches; (3) seek source-faithful connected strict22 SAT/UNSAT
controls; (4) keep Horn/2SAT/XOR recognition and source retention legal;
(5) only then ask whether a coverage theorem reaches arbitrary SAT.

\paragraph{Next specific lemma.}

prove an exact overlap-and-marginal dynamic program with construction,
