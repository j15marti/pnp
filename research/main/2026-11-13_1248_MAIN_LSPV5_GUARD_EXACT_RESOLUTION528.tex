\section*{Main result}
\begin{theorem}[528.1, PROVED/EXACT]
Let $F$ be an unsatisfiable CNF, $g$ a variable not in $F$, and $S_{\mathcal P}(\cdot)$ the minimum number of
Resolution inferences in a DAG-like ($\mathcal P=\mathrm{dag}$) or tree-like ($\mathcal P=\mathrm{tree}$) refutation.
Then $S_{\mathcal P}(G_g(F))=S_{\mathcal P}(F)+1$. Moreover every minimum $G_g(F)$-refutation contains exactly one
inference whose pivot is $g$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Upper bound.} Replace each source leaf $C$ of a refutation of $F$ by $\neg g\vee C$. Since $g$ is fresh, every
$x$-resolvent of lifted parents is the lift of the original resolvent (no new clash, no tautology), so the same DAG
(or tree) derives $(\neg g)$; one further inference with $(g)$ gives $\bot$.

\emph{Lower bound.} Let $\pi$ be a $G_g(F)$-refutation and $\rho=\{g\mapsto1\}$. By induction along $\pi$ assign to
each line $D$ with $D|_\rho\neq1$ a clause $D'\subseteq D|_\rho$ derivable from $F$, using at most as many new
inferences as there are non-$g$-pivot inferences among $D$'s ancestors (counting shared ancestors once in the DAG
case). Sources: $(\neg g\vee C)'=C$; $(g)|_\rho=1$. For an inference with pivot $g$, say $D=\mathrm{Res}_g(A,B)$
with $g\in A$, $\neg g\in B$: $A|_\rho=1$, set $D'=B'\subseteq B\setminus\{\neg g\}\subseteq D$ (no new inference).
For pivot $x\neq g$ with $D|_\rho\neq1$: both parents restrict to non-$1$; if $x\notin A'$ set $D'=A'$, if
$\neg x\notin B'$ set $D'=B'$, otherwise $D'=\mathrm{Res}_x(A',B')\subseteq D|_\rho$ (one inference; $A',B'$ cannot
clash on a second variable since they are subclauses of $A,B$). The root gives $\bot'=\bot$. Hence $S_{\mathcal P}(F)\le
|\pi|-k$, where $k$ is the number of $g$-pivot inferences. Finally $k\ge1$: $(g)$ contains no other variable, so it
can be a parent only of a $g$-pivot inference; if $k=0$ then $(g)$ is unused and $\pi$ refutes
$\{\neg g\vee C\}_{C\in F}$, which is satisfied by $g=0$, contradicting soundness. The tree case is identical because
the construction never duplicates subproofs. For a minimum $\pi$, $|\pi|=S(F)+1$ forces $k=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[528.2, PROVED/EXACT, Resolution only]
The guard map is cost-neutral for the worst-case objective: with $N'=N+O(m\log n)$ bits,
$\overline W_{\mathrm{Res}}(N)+1\le\overline W_{\mathrm{Res}}^{\rm guarded}(N')$ and conversely. Guarding can neither
manufacture nor hide Resolution hardness. In particular the /592 DP identity
$R_{\rm guarded}=R_{\rm strict}+1$ is the DP-path shadow of an all-proof identity; and since the DP count
$R_{\rm strict}(F_m)=80m-161$ (observed, not proved for all $m$) is an \emph{upper} bound on $S(F_m)$, odd-charge prism
remains an easy family ($S=O(m)$), unsuitable for P2-B1, exactly as /592 states.
\end{corollary}

\textbf{Scope firewall.} The lower-bound half uses closure of Resolution under restrictions. This fails or needs a
fresh proof for systems with extension/definition rules (a definition $y\leftrightarrow(g\wedge x)$ restricts to a
different definition whose re-introduction is a paid step) and for redundancy rules (PR/DRAT-style systems are not
closed under restriction in the same way; Buss--Thapen). So Theorem 528.1 does \emph{not} transfer to the historical
mixed calculus $R_{\rm mix341}$, its DEF/XOR/AND3/LOCAL-CONVERT rules, or /386 native acceptance. It is a
same-calculus statement about ordinary Resolution with an explicit, different source $G_g(F)$; the source-binding
gate P2-D1 is untouched.

\section*{Bridge quantifier firewall (KNOWN THEOREM consequence)}
For $R=$ ordinary Resolution, $\overline W_{\rm Res}(N)$ is unconditionally superpolynomial (Haken 1985; Urquhart 1987
Tseitin). Hence the boxed bridge $\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P\Rightarrow\overline W_{\rm Res}\le\mathrm{poly}$ is
logically equivalent to $\mathrm P\neq\mathrm{NP}$ itself: it is a reformulation, not a weaker step (world-ledger
lesson). For any fixed $R$, the bridge says ``$\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}$ implies $R$ is polynomially bounded'', which
(Cook--Reckhow) is at least as strong as needing $R$ to be the proof system made p-bounded by $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$;
no fixed natural system is known to have this property. The bridge is therefore informative only for an $R$ strong
enough that no superpolynomial $\overline W_R$ lower bound is known, and for such $R$ (e.g.\ Extended Frege) the
hardness side is wide open. Restriction-closed systems that allow Theorem 528.1-type reductions are exactly those with
known lower-bound technology, and that is why they cannot carry the bridge. Status: P2-B2/B3 OPEN.
