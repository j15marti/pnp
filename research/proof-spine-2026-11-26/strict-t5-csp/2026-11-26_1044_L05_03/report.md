## 2. Polynomial identity invisibility (proof by interpolation and constructive countermodel)
Let F be any field. On the Boolean cube reduce polynomials modulo z_i²-z_i to unique multilinear representatives. Let b be a physical center with each bundle constant equal to any macro solution of the chosen subsystem. Every physical word within Hamming distance <=d *in each bundle* of b has the same majority values and is an exact model. Translate b to zero by replacing coordinates with z_i or 1-z_i, an invertible Boolean substitution that preserves total degree and degree bounds within each bundle.
For polynomial P of degree <=d, assume P vanishes on this exact subsystem. Its translated coefficients a_J satisfy P(1_I)=sum_{J subseteq I}a_J for every I with |I|<=d. These inputs are among the product of allowed balls. Induction on |I| gives all a_J=0, over any characteristic. Alternatively choose a minimal nonzero coefficient a_J; evaluate at 1_J, all strict-subset coefficients zero, contradiction. Hence no nonzero degree<=d identity survives. Stronger: for polynomials of degree <=d separately in each bundle, evaluate all I with |I intersect bundle|<=d and induct on total cardinality; again the entire coefficient array vanishes. This stronger identity statement concerns a potentially huge symbolic space, not a polynomial algorithm for enumerating it.
Consequences: every proper subsystem of NO, even V-1 equations, yields no nontrivial identities of the stated degree in original physical coordinates. Full YES has same property. Any iterative relaxation extracting only these identities from proper subsets and combining them linearly cannot decide NO unless it adds new operations. Not a claim that arbitrary-degree or extended-variable polynomial calculus cannot decide, nor that an arbitrary proof line's support must obey this bound. The degree claim does not cover gate-wire coordinates where the majority output is directly exposed, or additional physical pins (which can force macro bits). Exact guarded retraction below handles pins separately. For d=floor(log2 V), even logarithmic-degree identities of this form are blind with polynomial encoded native length. This is the substantive correction to lower-bound arguments relying on ever-increased local algebraic order.
