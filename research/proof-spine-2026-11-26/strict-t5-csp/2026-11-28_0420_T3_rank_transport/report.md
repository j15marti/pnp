## 2. Scoped amortization theorem (author proof)
For fixed d, V_d(r) is the space of multilinear Boolean polynomial functions of degree <=d in r coordinates. W is a verified vanishing subspace on the exact current residual; maintain Phi=D_d(r)-dim W+r, D_d(r)=sum_{j=0}^d binom(r,j).
A sound independent export strictly decreases Phi. For an admitted exact affine chart restriction x=a+B y with B full column rank s<r (verified all residual solutions lie in its image, and exact residual replacement), substitution L:V_d(r)->V_d(s) is degree-nonincreasing and surjective: a left inverse expresses every y coordinate as an affine function of x on that image, so every degree<=d monomial in y has a degree<=d preimage. Thus dim L(W)>=dim W-(D_d(r)-D_d(s)); for W' containing L(W), D_d(s)-dim W'<=D_d(r)-dim W, and Phi drops by >=r-s. Keep canonical source/guard identities and charge substitution, verification and lifting. Coordinate projections that are existential rather than certified affine restrictions do NOT qualify automatically.
On each fixed chart with initial degree <=4, full Boolean PC4 closure by kernel multiplication and Gaussian elimination is finite polynomial work for fixed d=4, even counting all unsuccessful tests (at most D_4 independent rank additions). A source-derived binary export can be selected deterministically by testing all O(r^2) signed pairs for membership in W4 and nonmembership in W3; also test units and constant. The row proof DAG is retained and checked. No promise a productive export exists. In an admitted sequence of fixed-degree closure epochs and exact affine restrictions, the entire unsuccessful and successful search overhead is polynomial in original N, provided the chart and all explicit retained polynomials/maps remain polynomial-size: there are at most D_3(r0)+r0 charged successful epochs and at most one failed full scan per unchanged state. ## 7. Tag-preserving transport through nonaffine fusions (author proof)

For t explicitly acquired affine literal tags, attach to each local option (s,u) where s is its syndrome and u the vector of its tag contributions. A fusion is exact XOR sumset in the augmented space, not merely syndrome projection. Every untagged affine filter depends only on s, and commutes with projection pi(s,u)=s: pi(A+B)=pi(A)+pi(B); pi(A intersect pi^{-1}(C))=pi(A) intersect C.
