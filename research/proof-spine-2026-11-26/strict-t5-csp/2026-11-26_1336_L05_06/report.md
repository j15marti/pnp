## 4. Replica C: adversarial reconstruction and proof-hardness collapse

### 4.1 Pair-only compression cannot do the job
If one simply deletes a pair inequality from the complete graph on the H positive groups and uses no other constraint except inequality/equality grouping, choose the two missing-edge endpoints equal and all other groups distinct from them and each other. There are H-1 used labels; this passes every retained edge yet fails exact raw support. Every edge is necessary for this pair-only scheme. Moreover any loop-free graph whose H-coloring is impossible has a vertex-minimal induced non-H-colorable subgraph, min degree >=H (otherwise extend an H-coloring of the rest), >=H+1 vertices and >=H(H+1)/2 edges. This holds even with added auxiliary label vertices in the loop-free inequality-only class. Equality can be contracted, but a self-loop is an immediate contradiction and excluded from this scoped bound. No conclusion for arbitrary nonlinear gadgets or decision time.

### 4.2 Sorting compresses exact support but destroys intended proof hardness
The new negative compact source has a uniform polynomial-size standard one-hot CNF resolution refutation, with weakening and DAG sharing, width O(ell). Here is a complete constructive proof, including acquisition.

Local semantic consequence lemma: for CNF on q Boolean variables, an entailed clause D can be obtained by exhaustive binary splitting under falsifying assignment to D. Every complete leaf violates a known clause, weaken it to the full blocking clause of that assignment (including D); resolve sibling leaves upward until D. At most O(2^q) resolution/weakening steps, width <=q. Each leaf checks an actual premise, no semantic oracle. Weakening can be eliminated by keeping subclauses and skipping redundant inferences; polynomial ordinary resolution upper bound unchanged.

Fix a legal frame g. Denote by M_g the conjunction of its five positive one-hot anchor indicators. Under M_g, output word j low bit b is represented by native indicator of color g(1); domain gadget gives exactly Boolean {g(0),g(1)}. Let L_{j,v} be the conjunction of the ell signed Boolean bit indicators expressing value v. The mode pin forces t=1 under M_g. Each adjacent strict-order test (including conditional last) consists of O(ell) native gate variables and clauses, two output words' Boolean domains, shared frame, constants and mode. Include the actual NOT/domain definitions of any reused complement wires, even when declared earlier. Scope is still O(ell), since each gate has constant expansion; no need to traverse the sorting network upstream. By local lemma derive for every j=1..H and pair u>=v the clause NOT M_g OR NOT L_{j-1,u} OR NOT L_{j,v}. One may take q<=1000(ell+1) one-hot variables as a loose fixed bound from the gate list, each less bit iteration uses fewer than 60 new native variables plus input domains and constant/conditional wires. Enumeration time O(2^q poly(N))=H^{O(1)} poly(N) per clause, polynomial despite huge constants; the number of target pair clauses O(H³). This proof generation is explicit, not executed in full native form.

Inductively derive D_{j,v}=NOT M_g OR NOT L_{j,v} for all v<j. For j>=1 fix v<j; for each possible previous u=0..H-1 either u<j-1 and earlier D_{j-1,u} exists (weaken by current blocking literals), or u>=j-1>=v and the local strict-order clause exists. In both cases obtain NOT M_g OR NOT L_{j-1,u} OR NOT L_{j,v}. Resolve over all ell previous word bits by binary elimination, leaving D_{j,v}. For j=H all H values excluded; resolve over final word bits to obtain NOT M_g. Ten gauges exhaust all legal frame models; finite 25-bit frame truth-table lemma derives contradiction using the ten clauses NOT M_g. Width O(ell) and total proof size/time poly(H) with one fixed exponent; the final ordered-word proof layer itself uses O(H³) lines. All comparisons use actual Boolean bit indicators, not assumed target values. R conversion's forbidden-triple CNF union equals the C forbidden-triple CNF, so the same proof applies to converted R.

Over any field, translate resolution/weakening clause to product of falsifying literals. Multiplying by missing literals, simulated by variable multiplication and linear combination, gives common product times x and (1-x); sum yields common product. Tautological clauses follow from Boolean x²-x=0; constants and disjoint pivots handled identically. Degree <=width+1 up to a fixed constant, size polynomial after paid expansions as monomial count <=2^{O(ell)} per local clause. Thus native compact PC refutation degree O(ell) over any field. This is unconditional for compact encoding. Previous original-degree lower bound Omega(H/ell) was conditional on Braun 5.7, not independently checked; if valid, exact compression cannot preserve it. No new lower bound on original native Res(xor) claimed.

There is also a direct algebraic reason the old local polynomial substitution fails: with t=1 and every raw assignment (not just models), canonical sorted first low word's most significant real bit equals product of the P raw high bits, because minimum >=H/2 iff all real labels >=H/2. Sentinel never smaller. Its unique multilinear degree is P over every field. This is for the canonical extension on ALL raw words, not a lower bound on arbitrary extension schemes over only feasible inputs. It shows that the previous degree<=2ell pair-local substitution is not maintained by this exact sorter. Do not assume lower bounds transfer merely by exact semantic retraction.
