## 1. Scoped exact grammar theorem and indexed acquisition
Let F be a polynomial-size explicit Boolean table CSP over current named coordinates, max factor scope width <=7. For a pivot v whose complete bucket contains at most two factors and total union U has <=7 coordinates, define local slot order U=(v, sorted others). Key K consists of |U| and ordered pairs (each factor's slot map, exact allowed word list), sorted as a multiset. Every factor uses its own slot map, and different original IDs may be replaced only by bijective slot renaming preserving aliases and relative signs already incorporated into table supports. Exact projection is Q(y)=OR over the two v bits of AND of all bucket factors. The same key implies pointwise same local relation in all slots and therefore identical projected support; cache the support, not a global witness or a proof with the old original IDs. Restore each occurrence's actual coordinate map and exact parent IDs. No external dependence on v allowed, including guard, retained predicates or reconstruction obligations; guard restrictions must be applied or explicitly included. Here projection occurs before guarded splitting. Both directions: old witness projects to a permitted row; each permitted row extends by testing both v bits, followed by reverse reconstruction. Each accepted pivot retires one variable and replaces at most two factors by at most one, so representation doesn't grow beyond original fixed width. A repeated table alone does not imply equal boundary binding.

Indexed implementation: maintain incidence sets per carrier, eligible bucket size 1 or 2 and union <=7, priority (bucket size, original ID). Removing/adding one bounded bucket changes only incidence sets of its at most seven union coordinates; update only those, with versioned lazy heap entries. At most O(V+F) initial and subsequent eligibility updates and incidence modifications, because every successful pivot retires a coordinate and each step has bounded arity. Reject high-degree bucket by maintained cardinality BEFORE materializing it (v2 correction); otherwise a high-degree hub could hide quadratic work. At fixed width, local key length, truth support and table calculation are bounded constants; use deterministic balanced dictionaries/sets and heap, not assumed constant-time hashing. O((V+F) log(V+F+2)) indexed operations for admitted phase, each on polynomially many O(log N)-bit IDs (a conservative polynomial bit simulation suffices); O(V+F) entries including lazy stale records, trace and occurrence-specific reverse maps. Explicit normalizer, parsing, authentication, verification, guards, failed other tracks and reconstruction remain charged separately. No universal admission statement. One can bound memory by polynomial and same-execution cumulative memory by T*S, but physical peak/CM not instrumented. Numeric potential V+F drops on every admitted pivot; it is not a universal UCAC credit for branches. Key cardinality even bounded by a constant for fixed local arity, which is not a decision algorithm: E/B and 3SAT already have finite relation languages. Falsifiers: alias loss, omitted incident constraint or guard, sign mismatch, stale heap entry. Separate XOR(v,a) and equality(v,b) are feasible, alias a=b makes them contradictory; executed both. No inference from grammar equality alone.
