## 4. Restricted lower bound, independent geometric and communication methods

Balanced vertex partition A,B has |A|,|B|>=k^2/3. Let mixed rows contain both colors. If at least k/3 mixed rows, horizontal cut >=k/3. Otherwise if no full A row, |A|<k^2/3, contradiction; same for B. Full row of each color implies every column mixed and vertical cut >=k. Thus number q of crossing edges >=ceil(k/3). This proof holds for every balanced partition, no fixed-seam assumption.
