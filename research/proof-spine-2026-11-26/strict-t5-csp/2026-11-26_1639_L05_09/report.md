## B. Irreducibility and separated-space theorem
Use the actual proof of Mikša–Nordström, arXiv:1505.01358v1, Theorem3.8, Definition3.20, Lemmas3.15,3.26,3.27 and Theorem4.9 (published JACM2024 DOI10.1145/3675668). For FPHP_G take E all row/hole pair clauses, U one positive pigeon clause per row; V_v includes all edge variables of rows neighboring hole v. They establish respectful expansion (s,1,0,E), overlap d<=4 and a linear reduction operator R_G on multilinear polynomials: for term t reduce modulo the ideal of E plus positive row clauses in Sup_s(t); for degree<=s/(2d) this support has <=s/2 rows (Lemma3.26), operator annihilates every degree-bounded PC derivable polynomial, by their axiom and multiplication invariance proof (Theorem3.8, Lemma3.15). It is crucial to use the operator itself, not merely the numerical lower bound on refuting 1. Order monomials by degree then lex, with every raw pivot variable larger than every raw nonpivot variable.

For a selected free monomial t=product_{i in S} x_(i,j_i), construct A's cube with H=Sup_s(t). Every generator of that local ideal vanishes on each cube corner, hence every polynomial in the ideal vanishes there. If t were the leading term of a polynomial in the local ideal, restrict to cube variables u_i=x_(i,j_i). The coefficient of product u_i is nonzero: terms of lower degree cannot contain all k independent toggles; any degree-k term with a pivot would be larger than t, and every other degree-k nonpivot term omits a selected toggle or includes a fixed bit. Thus only t contributes to that coefficient. A multilinear polynomial with a nonzero coefficient cannot vanish on the entire Boolean cube (Möbius inversion, any field). Contradiction. Hence t irreducible and R_G(t)=t. This proves R_G is identity on the linear span U_k of all such degree-k monomials, even though each monomial uses a potentially different local cube.

Quotient by row parity x_(i,p_i)=1+sum_{j!=p_i} z_(i,j) over F2. Keep every substituted row/hole exclusion, each a disjunction of <=2 affine equations; positive row clause becomes tautological. Q has q variables, J=O(h) clauses, width<=2; exact lift since odd row plus at-most-one forces exactly one. Take standard PC base of Boolean equations and these quotient clause polynomials. Read quotient variables as original nonpivots. Each quotient axiom is implied by raw constraints on at most two rows (<=8 raw variables). A degree<=8 raw PC derivation follows by truth-table interpolation: sum full assignment indicators for the polynomial's nonzero entries, each such assignment violates a local source clause and its indicator is a product of that clause polynomial with remaining literals; Boolean identities permit multilinear reduction. Multiplication by 1+x is linear combination of multiply-by-x and original. Thus any degree-B PC consequence from quotient lifts to raw degree<=max(B,8).
