## B. Exact twin-quotient connecting lemma

Necessity count selected arcs and occupied vertices. Sufficiency choose d_i distinct actual vertices in B_i; partition them into outgoing groups of sizes f_ij and independently into incoming groups of sizes f_ji. For each quotient arc choose any bijection from corresponding outgoing to incoming groups. Completeness guarantees each actual edge exists. Each chosen vertex has one incoming and one outgoing edge, yielding disjoint directed cycles with exactly s occupied vertices. No pooled fractional or signed flow as a Boolean witness. Partition and exact complete/empty adjacency can be verified by explicit pair scans O(t^2) plus supplied source checks; identical in/out neighborhood classes are polynomially acquirable on the supplied retracted graph.
