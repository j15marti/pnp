## Proof and accounting
Clause C becomes p_C=product of its false-literal indicators, positive literal x -> 1+x, negative -> x; p_C=0 iff C true. For each retained row p_i of degree e<=d, take explicit monomial multiples m*p_i with deg(m)<=d-e, reducing squares. Every such row vanishes on every solution. All coefficients in GF(2). Sort square-free monomials by degree, then fixed order. Gaussian elimination with high-degree pivots first produces a basis. Its low-degree pivot rows span the intersection of the generated row space with the affine subspace: a combination containing a high-degree pivot row has a noncancelled highest pivot, since distinct pivots. Each output affine row is replayable as XOR of explicitly indexed parent multiples, without free semantic implication. If 1 appears, contradiction. Otherwise if x_j+q=0 is nonconstant affine, substitute x_j=q into *every* retained equation, including degree>D_acquisition (here quartics). For every surviving word y, the unique extension x_j=q(y) satisfies old rows iff it satisfies substituted rows. This proves both directions; q in current surviving chart, replay reverse in reverse order. Substitution of affine polynomial into degree<=D polynomial has degree<=D after Boolean reduction. Each original row gives one new row; delete zero/duplicate rows only. No arbitrary constraint deletion or count-preservation claim beyond bijection on this pivot. Conditional consequences remain within the exact guard, not global literals.

At most n successful pivots. At most M_D=sum_{k<=D}binom(n,k) coefficients per row. At most m M_d generators per epoch, each with <=M_d coefficients; dense GF2 elimination and provenance (explicit bit vectors of length <=m M_d) take polynomial bit time and storage, e.g. O((m M_d)^2(M_d+m M_d)) loose bit operations per epoch with elementary addressing overhead, plus polynomial substitution O(m M_D n) term contributions, canonical sorting/dedup, source maps and certificate output. Charge up to n+1 epochs (including final failed search), all other capped lanes, exact guards, proof replay, reverse maps, random access simulation, peak space and cumulative memory <= serial T*S. This is a fixed polynomial for admitted phase in original N; no bound on number of subsequent guards/branches absent separate global credit theorem. If D,d grow with input, polynomial bound does not follow. No claim of complete degree-d polynomial calculus saturation; static generator span is a restricted search. Falsifiers: invalid multiplier, erroneous XOR combination, omitted quartic, out-of-guard substitution, incorrect reverse map or nondecreasing dimension. Independent trace replay rebuilt each monomial via product of variable images; both parent combination and each replacement checked. No arbitrary-decision lower bound from degree plateau.
