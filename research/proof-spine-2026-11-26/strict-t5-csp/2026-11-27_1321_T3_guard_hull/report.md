## Target and new theorem
On a fixed explicit affine carrier A of dimension n over F2 containing every possible solution of a certified residual, suppose k independently checked conditional refutations forbid affine guards G_i (intersected with A). All guards retain their source and reconstruction maps. Let S=A minus union_i G_i. For k fixed, or k<=c log2 original N, uniformly compute the exact affine hull of S, or certify S empty, in polynomial aggregate bit time in original N provided the chart, guards and their checked certificates have polynomial size. This computes ALL affine consequences of these exclusions alone, not all affine consequences of the nonlinear residual. No assumption of polynomial acquisition of the guards. For each additional affine condition H use exact integer inclusion-exclusion
C(H)=sum_{J subset [k]} (-1)^|J| 2^(dim(A intersect H intersect all G_j)),
with zero for inconsistent intersections. Gaussian elimination on explicit binary rows, n+O(k) bits per signed integer intermediate, exact count. Coordinate prefix conditioning produces a witness in S intersect H in at most n+1 extra count queries when positive. Begin with p in S; maintain basis W of attained differences q+p. For each basis vector a of W^perp, query C(a*x=1+a*p). If positive, recover actual q, adjoin q+p and recompute W^perp. If all queries zero, S subset p+W; all retained witnesses are in S so aff(S)=p+W. At most n rank increases and O(n^2) count queries including extraction; each query 2^k GE calls. Deterministic certificate: exact count identities, witness words, independent rank verification and zero opposite-halfspace counts. This is a genuine interface to contextual affine contraction, providing exact forced rows from partial obstruction coverage before the final contradiction. All original carrier equations, guard maps and conditional refutations are paid dependencies. When guard-row union rank r small, an alternative is enumerate 2^r quotient words and expand kernel, not all 2^n source words. For exactly five guards each standalone count has at most 31 nonempty intersections, but extracting all affine consequences may need multiple such counts.

If S nonempty, choose p in S and one violated affine equation per nonempty guard. Their opposite equalities define a flat containing p and contained in S, codim<=k in A. Thus |S|>=2^(n-k) (or >=1) and hull codim<=k. Uniform random full carrier words give hit probability >=2^-k conditional on the supplied nonempty S, not a bound on discovering good refutation guards. Deterministic count-prefix extraction derandomizes this PARTICULAR sampling task. Brouwer (1986) separately shows an irredundant full cover of a binary affine carrier by k affine sets has common direction intersection of codimension <=k-1. Use as known theorem, not a new all-source result. No free global coverage test for arbitrary many guards.

## Falsifier and repair
A single forbidden codimension-r affine flat G removes points but need not yield any affine contraction (for r>=2 complement spans the carrier). More strongly a nonzero Boolean polynomial vanishing on A\G must have degree at least r: invertible affine coordinate change reduces G to x_1=...=x_r=0 and does not increase degree; unique multilinear Boolean representation is product_i(1+x_i) times a nonzero polynomial in remaining coordinates, degree >=r. Thus even a verified exclusion may provide zero new fixed-degree row credit in the relaxation, for growing r. This is a refusal of a specific credit rule, not a lower bound on proof length or all deciders. Compact product-circuit representation of guard indicator is an immediate bypass, but aggregate circuit acquisition/rewrite/equivalence/branch credit remains OPEN. Other falsifiers: empty carrier, duplicated guards, zero opposite count required for hull row, opposite-value witness must actually survive, mismatched source/guard IDs rejected. Independent quotient enumeration checks actual benchmark cover.
