## 3. Direct semantic measure proof, then constructive resolution projection

For any Boolean predicate f on edge assignments, define mu(f) as the minimum number of original odd-charge vertex equations whose conjunction implies f. Entire system inconsistent, so minimum exists. Every proper vertex subset is consistent: the incidence rows over GF2 are independent on proper subsets, since any nonempty proper sum is the nonzero cut incidence vector by connectivity. Therefore mu(false)=v, mu(true)=0. Each input axiom pulled back via phi has mu<=1. Logical consequence of two predicates has mu<=sum of their mu, and weakening is nonincreasing. For any refutation, apply this to the pullback of each proof line: resolution is truth-preserving on all valuations, hence on all phi(x), even though phi(x) need not satisfy the source. First line with mu>=v/3 has mu<2v/3 for sufficiently large v. Let S be a minimizing vertex subset for this line. Every edge e crossing S must belong to semantic support of that line's pulled-back predicate f. Otherwise remove its unique endpoint u in S; minimality gives assignment satisfying S without u but falsifying f. Flip e if needed to satisfy u, preserving all other equations in S and f. Contradiction. Thus |support(f)|>=|delta S|>=h v/3. A width-w clause involves at most w indicators, each depending on at most9 original bits, so support <=9w. Hence EVERY refutation has width >=h v/27. This argument does not use actual proof length, circuit structure, or affine-hull approximations. In particular no balanced gate/context overlap assumptions.
Complementary constructive transfer: for each proof clause D take exact truth-table CNF of D(phi(x)) on union of its actual dependency sets, omitting tautologies; at most 2^(9w) blocking clauses. For each input axiom, its pullback follows from either zero equations (tautology) or one original parity equation, and can be derived by a truth-table resolution on the union of support and that equation's <=8 variables. For each resolution/weakening step, use union of parent supports (and child support if weakening introduces vars) of size <=18w, and derive each child blocking clause by expanding to full assignments, weakening an already-falsified parent blocking clause, then resolving on forgotten variables. For a weakening the same bound holds using parent+child. This constructs a source refutation of width <=max(18w,9w+8,8), including empty clause.
