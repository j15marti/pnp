## Connecting theorem to T2 affine orbit
Let the two forbidden events E_i be affine sets in the full local binary chart, both affected by toggling v (i.e. the unit direction e_v is not in either nonempty event's translation space). Then each fiber over V contains at most one forbidden bit per event. Write E_i over the projected affine domain D_i as v=f_i(y), with f_i affine (solve an equation with v coefficient 1). The projected forbidden event is exactly
D_1 intersect D_2 intersect {f_1(y)+f_2(y)=1}.
Proof: both v choices must fail; no individual affected event can block both, so exactly opposite bits must be forbidden by the pair. Conversely opposite bits cover the whole fiber. Thus Gaussian elimination on the two systems and the opposite-orientation equation computes this projection without truth-table expansion. If one event invariant and the other affected, only the invariant event's projection can block both bits; if both invariant, union of their projections is the result and must not be treated as an affine intersection.
