## 3. New scoped dual activation and owner rigidity theorem
Let V be an explicit finite-dimensional F2 monomial coefficient space containing constant e=1, W span of verified vanishing rows p_i, with e not in W. The affine space of refusal functionals is
  D(W)={lambda in V*: lambda(e)=1 and lambda(w)=0 for all w in W}.
It is nonempty, dimension dim V - dim W - 1. If lambda exists it is a certificate that linear combination alone cannot refute, not a Boolean witness or a refusal of polynomial calculus multiplication. If a verified new row p has lambda(p)=1, that particular refusal is eliminated and p is outside W. For all p outside W, either p is in e+W (terminal e in W+span p), or the new dual space is an affine codimension-one slice of D(W). Thus in a fixed chart and degree/monomial space a successful independent row earns exactly one rank credit, or a terminal. At most dim V such credits, no claim of universal acquisition. A certificate functional on a smaller explicit monomial universe may be extended by zero on new monomials for a particular comparison; that does not bound all expanded-space blockers or reset credits. Guard lift must preserve soundness; representation changes/branches need separate transport or accounting.

For a system with a constant proof, write row coefficient map M:F2^m -> V, choose any alpha0 with M alpha0=e. Every certificate coefficient vector is alpha0+ker M. For owner i, every span proof uses i iff alpha0_i=1 and every basis vector of ker M has zero i-th coordinate. Equivalently e not in span of rows excluding i, iff there exists lambda_i with lambda_i(e)=1, lambda_i(p_i)=1, and lambda_i(p_j)=0 for j!=i. Necessity follows by applying lambda; existence by separating e from remaining span and using the full proof. One augmented elimination records the basis and dependencies; applying different RHSs/backsolving yields all owner-dual certificates without independently rediscovering full proofs.
