## Exact proof
Signed BFS assigns each port p representative r(p) and phase s(p) with x_p=y_r XOR s(p); check every signed edge and E equality, inconsistent cycle is a contradiction. Native type and all port/simple graph checks. B rows are exact integer sum two, duplicate signed rows and their global complement are identical relations. Global simultaneous bit complement preserves all E, B and edge constraints; fixing any representative to zero preserves feasibility at EMPTY, not arbitrary absolute guards. Chosen gauge by maximum row frequency then smallest ID.

If bit v occurs in exactly one live factor F(v,X), for any conjunction G on other coordinates, exists v (F(v,X) AND G)= (exists v F(v,X)) AND G. Compute support by exact enumeration at arity <=4; witness recovery tests both bits in reverse retirement order. For a distinct singly occurring B4 slack this specializes to NAE3 since 2-sum(other three) must be 0 or 1. Repeated signed coordinates are handled by truth table rather than assumed NAE. No claim of uniqueness for general projected factors. For each forbidden assignment, its full blocking clause is valid; delete literals until inclusion-minimal while remaining forbidden. All minimal forbidden subcubes yield exactly the factor (any invalid word extends a forbidden minimal pattern; valid words satisfy all). Arity constant, enumeration <=3^4 patterns plus support checks. Thus no implicit semantic oracle.

For each pivot v, positive bucket (v OR A_i), negative bucket (NOT v OR B_j), replace by all non-tautological A_i OR B_j. Forward implication immediate; conversely if both values of v fail, some A_i and B_j false, contradicting their resolvent. If one side empty, choose v to satisfy other bucket. Reverse reconstruction is polynomial. Implementation accepts only total bucket clauses <=8, <=16 cross pairs, resolvent width <=4 and strictly fewer unique resolvents than removed bucket clauses. Therefore no blowup; at most O(N) successful retirements, O(N) clauses, O(N) candidate pivots per round. Termination of this preprocessing follows without any unproved universal progress. For unresolved branch tree, UP is sound and both bits exhaustively covered; proof leaves conflict. Branching may grow exponentially.
