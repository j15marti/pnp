## B. Exact all-field and all-small-modulus theorem (integral cokernel)
Let A be integer incidence matrix 3h by 4h of base triples, b=all ones. Present abelian group G=Z^(3h)/im(A) by generators a_i,b_j,c_k and relations a_i+b_{i+d}+c_{2i+d}=0 for d=0,1,2,3. Indices cyclic. Subtract successive d equations: Delta b_{i+d}=-Delta c_{2i+d} for d=0,1,2. In particular d=2 at i and d=0 at i+1 give Delta b_{i+2}=Delta b_{i+1}. Hence all Delta b=t, all Delta c=-t (d=0 and1 cover every residue); h t=0. Then b_j=b_0+jt, c_k=c_0-kt, a_i=-b_0-c_0+it. Conversely these assignments in Z^2 direct sum Z_h satisfy all relations, including wrap by h t=0, and generate that group. The maps are mutually inverse, proving G=Z^2 direct sum Z_h, not merely a quotient bound. RHS class [b]=sum_i(a_i+b_i+c_i)=h(h-1)/2 t=(h/2)t, nonzero. Special column class also (h/2)t. This proves Ax=b has no signed integer solution and class is precisely order2 in Z_h.

For ring Z/M, solvability iff [b] lies in M G (equivalently b in im A + M Z^(3h)). Thus iff gcd(M,h) divides h/2. For h power2, iff h does not divide M. Every modulus M<h passes, and any fixed finite collection of moduli fails on sufficiently large h. Over F_p prime fields consistency follows because gcd(p,h)=1 or 2 divides h/2.
