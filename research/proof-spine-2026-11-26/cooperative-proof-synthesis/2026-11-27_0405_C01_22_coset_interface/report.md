## Theorem: coset join
For every exact cut support D subset Q* in binary coordinates over GF(2), choose subspace H of its translation stabilizer {h:D+h=D}; D is exactly union of disjoint cosets a+H, A one canonical representative each. Empty D retained separately. Stabilizer is a subspace by composition of translations. For a nonempty explicit quotient A, every period belongs to {a+a0:a in A}; test all such translations on all A, build basis of passing shifts, lift to H. Canonicalize by linear remainder; no enumeration of preexisting H. At most |A|^2 membership checks plus polynomial matrix, dictionary and bit work; if initial support large, acquisition is not free.

At parent cut, by C01-21 exact gluing, child supports (A+H) and (B+K) must agree on child intersection I. Denote restriction maps L,R and parent linear evaluation maps P,Q constructed by annotated Gaussian decomposition of each parent basis observation. For each representative pair (a,b), write children a+u,b+v, u in H,v in K. Solve M(u,v)=L(a)+R(b), M(u,v)=L(u)+R(v). If RHS outside image M, entire coset pair impossible. Otherwise choose particular solution (u0,v0). Its parent image is the single coset P(a+u0)+Q(b+v0)+J, with J=(P,Q)(ker M). J is independent of the representative pair; compute it once by Gaussian elimination. Necessity/sufficiency follows because all matching child solutions are precisely the affine solution fiber, and child witnesses glue uniquely on the intersection. Bucket representative a by remainder of L(a) modulo im M and b by remainder of R(b). Equal keys iff entire pair is compatible; use deterministic sorting for worst-case guarantee, not assumed constant-time hash. Collect one canonical parent representative per distinct coset, optionally find extra translation periods of quotient set. Guard fixed throughout; no marginal or affine-hull substitution. Root empty support refutes; a nonempty root admits global witness by replay of coset pair, solving for selected parent key, local row pointers and matching lift. Witness reconstruction not implemented this run (UNSAT focus).
