## 1. Protected cylinder certificate (derived from C05-08, extended to changing frontiers)
Use C01-08 exact model: each factor i on subspace P_i of GF2 form space L permits explicit actual evaluations R_i, with source row and guard witnesses. A global solution is one linear functional satisfying all tables. Affine coordinate offsets retained in source conversion. Full factors impose nothing. A rank2 factor with 3 words forbids exactly one evaluation f_i; any nonzero d in P_i can be prescribed value 1+f_i(d), which guarantees factor satisfaction regardless of other coordinates. Other nonfull factors are mandatory core factors.

For any factor region J and protected subspace W, acquire core C subset J and one d_i in P_i per nonfull exterior factor i such that d_i are jointly independent modulo W+U_C, U_C=sum_{i in C} P_i. These are source-checkable Gaussian rank and table certificates. Then its EXACT attained response on W is a cylinder:
  E=W intersect U_C;
  R_J|W = {w in W*: w|E in R_C|E}.
Necessity by restriction. For sufficiency pick an actual core functional agreeing on E, glue with w on W+U_C, then prescribe the opposite forbidden value on each independent exterior d_i. Extend linearly to L. Full factors automatically satisfied. No marginal inference or all-model/count preservation. Even if w has a very large free part, no enumeration of that free part is required.

Acquisition: include all other nonfull factors in mandatory core, protect W0=W+their span. Use C05/X01 independently reconstructed shortest alternating-path independent transversal search for remaining rank2 three-word factors, each with its three nonzero directions. Start empty; BFS from every unmatched factor, inspect vector coefficients on current reps modulo W0; outside span is endpoint, otherwise follow every selected owner with nonzero coefficient. Shortest path replacement preserves independence (triangular coefficient matrix on path, new endpoint outside span). At failure reachable factors form additional core, every vector in their planes lies in W0+span of their selected reps; exterior reps remain independent modulo W+U_C. Directly recheck final rank certificate, so no unverified optimum premise needed. Finite O(s) augmentations, each inspects at most 3s candidate vectors with paid Gaussian operations. All full factors can be skipped. No assertion that acquired core always small.

## 2. NEW changing effective frontier theorem
Fix ANY acquired factor order of length s. A_j=sum of prefix planes; B_j=sum of suffix planes; W_j=A_j intersect B_j. For every prefix independently acquire protected cylinder certificate above on W_j, let E_j=W_j intersect U_Cj. Store ONLY the attainable evaluations S_j on E_j. Then exact dynamic program works even if W_j has linear dimension and E_j is small, and E_j may change nonmonotonically.

For step j let P=P_j, enumerate u in S_(j-1), v in R_j, and w in E_j*; retain w precisely if the three assigned evaluations on E_(j-1), P and E_j are jointly Gaussian-consistent. No arbitrary choice of independent pairwise gluing: solve all three together. Necessity by restricting actual prefix witness. Sufficiency: W_j subset W_(j-1)+P (C01-08 identity), so consistent u,v,w extends to W_(j-1)+P, giving some full prior boundary t restricting to u. Cylinder certificate makes t attainable by actual earlier prefix, and A_(j-1) intersect P subset W_(j-1). Glue to v; new prefix witness attains w. This proves equality of computed S_j with actual projection inductively. Reapply cylinder certificate at that layer to interpret EVERY full W_j extension of each attained w as attainable. Given full t_j on W_j from suffix, scan u in S_(j-1), v in R_j, find joint consistency of t_j,u,v; existence follows from cylinder invariant and exact transition. Extend to W_(j-1)+P and choose full t_(j-1) via Gaussian (free coordinates zero). Continue reverse. Store original row v each step. The recorded row evaluations glue inductively to a full original functional; verify every original tuple occupancy and guard. This is exact reverse replay, not fixed-pointer reconstruction.
