## Named load-bearing step
For one exact source chart and a split coordinate x, let P_d be square-free Boolean polynomials over F2, W0,W1 verified vanishing-identity spaces on complementary guards x=0,1, represented in the other r-1 coordinates. Let tau extract homogeneous degree d coefficients. A pair (p0,p1) has a degree-at-most-d unconditional lift iff tau(p0)=tau(p1). The unique Boolean lift is p=p0+x(p0+p1). Proof: restriction at x=0,1; square-free normal form makes terms containing x disjoint from those not containing it, so degree <=d iff difference has degree <=d-1. Each feasible word is on exactly one guard, hence p vanishes. Rank of this entire compatible lift space is dim W0+dim W1-dim(tau W0+tau W1), by rank-nullity and injectivity of restriction on Boolean functions. Gaussian kernel computation gives basis and checked parent combinations. Can transport a heterogeneous guarded proof only after its exact source/guard adapter and identity independently check. With d fixed, D=sum_{j<=d} binom(r,j)=poly(N) for r<=poly(N), dense rank work O((m+D)D^2) bit elementary XORs up to index/metadata overhead for m polynomially acquired rows; retain O(D^2+mD) bits and parent data polynomial. Include source verification, failed attempts and branch acquisition in the same budget; no oracle. This is not a bound on discovering W0,W1 universally.
If an admitted lift is outside existing W, global Phi=D-dim(W)+r drops by one. Keep both branch searches as paid speculative work; do not grant two fresh copies of Phi. Use a fixed B(N) epoch budget, reset only after checked strict decrease; Psi=(B+1)Phi+b, b remaining credit. Rollback never refunds discovery/check work or credit. With a polynomial initial potential, bounded epoch and universally available productive events this telescopes; the final availability premise is OPEN. Counterexample to unconditional transfer: p0 a degree-d monomial independent of x and p1=0, lift has degree d+1. W0=W1=0 produces no gain. Nonaffine chart maps can increase degree and are not covered. No claim of branch completeness, arbitrary polynomial chart changes or unrestricted hardness.
