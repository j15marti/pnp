## Named theorem: monotone bounded-width learning epoch credit
For any explicit Boolean CNF C on n variables, fixed integer k, let U_k be all non-tautological canonical clauses of width at most k, including empty. M_k=sum_{j=0}^{min(k,n)} 2^j binom(n,j). Preserve original clauses (which may be wider); no deletion or new variables in this phase. Learn only sound RUP or exact resolution clauses in U_k; deduplicate. For a deterministic epoch containing at most B(N,k) charged operations/jobs, including every failed guard probe, root closure, implication import, candidate selection, checking and messaging, advance to a new epoch only if at least one previously absent clause was added. Stop UNSAT on empty, or UNKNOWN on exhausted credit/fixed point. Let a be number of present U_k clauses at epoch START and r the remaining epoch credits (0<=r<=B). Phi=(M_k-a)(B+1)+r. Within epoch each paid operation reduces r by 1; at restart a increases by at least 1, r resets to at most B, hence net decrease >=1. Numeric initial Phi <= (M_k+1)(B+1). This is a scoped global allowance, not a proof that an untested oracle can produce useful clauses. With k fixed (not O(log N)), B polynomial, explicit deterministic bit algorithms for membership, UP, resolution and verification, original size poly(N), cost polynomial including peak and cumulative space. For k=2, M=2n^2+1; one epoch testing all single/pair guards, plus extracting at most n implications per singleton and checking them, has polynomial bit-cost. No branch reset, no free dedup/semantic equivalence. If using k growing logarithmically, M can be quasipolynomial, unlike bounded bucket width 2^k. A budget exhausting UNKNOWN cannot become SAT absent proved uniform negative completeness. Separate earlier bounded bucket credit and optional fixed-depth all-child branch credit; sum/multiply fully charged, not parallel span. Arbitrary imports of wider clauses need their own fixed universe/credit and cannot silently replenish this one.

RUP implication clause (-l OR q) justified by unit propagation under l deriving q; assuming l and not q makes the propagation trace inconsistent. A failed guard yields its negation clause. Unit closure is polynomial for explicit DB. Width-bounded resolution closure is a known restricted proof search (Ben-Sasson/Wigderson). The queue exhaustion -> productive contraction arrow remains OPEN; finite epoch closure does not decide arbitrary SAT.
