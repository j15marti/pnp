## Exact selector projection lemma
For any Boolean factor conjunction F, let S be k distinct internal selector variables constrained exactly-one, let H be all factors touching S, and K the other factors. For each i restrict H to one-hot e_i; G_i(x)=H(x,e_i). Then exists S F iff K AND OR_i G_i. Forward unique active selector; reverse choose the witnessing i. Copying/substituting incident circuit of length L gives O(kL) gate/representation bound up to index costs, no boundary-word enumeration; repeated arbitrary circuit projection can multiply sizes, no universal aggregate bound. All guards touching S must be included in H. Special admitted star: only additional nontrivial incident constraints are implications s_i -> literal l_i (one distinct literal per selector, duplicates allowed), with all l_i outside S. Projection exactly clause OR_i l_i. Derive it by k ordinary resolutions of the at-least-one clause with the implications. At-most-one is needed for the stated source but not this negative deduction. More general s_i -> conjunction of literals projects to OR of conjunctions. A failed external assignment yields a necessary clause by choosing one false literal from every conjunction and resolving k times. This is a paid local certificate, not universal acquisition of a global refutation.

## Hostile control / barrier
For k one-hot selectors require s_i -> (a_i AND b_i), with all 2k external variables distinct. Projection is OR_i(a_i AND b_i) of linear circuit size. Its exact auxiliary-free CNF needs >=2^k clauses: each maximal false word has exactly one 1 per pair, there are 2^k; if one necessary clause were false on two distinct such words, their coordinatewise OR would still falsify the clause yet satisfy the projected DNF, contradiction. Exhaustive k=2..6 checked 32,192,1024,5120,24576 high-level tests and 4,8,16,32,64 maximal false words. This falsifies unrestricted polynomial explicit CNF projection for this representation class, not decision hardness; compact DNF bypass. Other falsifiers: selector constraints or guards omitted; unsound resolution step; assuming ancestry is minimum; cap failure interpreted as NO. No native high-arity transfer claimed.
