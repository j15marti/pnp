## 1. Greatest matching autarky for mixed explicit tables (PROVED scoped)
F is conjunction of explicit nonempty distinct-row Boolean relations R_i on scopes P_i of distinct variables. An eligible edge i-v exists if assigning some bit b to v guarantees R_i regardless of all other variables of that factor. Check by counting rows with that bit: count=2^(|P_i|-1). Store one such b. Use all actual variable incidences Inc(v), not just eligible edges.
A matching-autarkic factor set C has an injective matching saturating C to eligible variables v with Inc(v) subset C. Assign each matched variable its eligible bit. Each factor in C is satisfied independently of all remaining variables and no retained factor touches a pinned variable. Consequently the retained conjunction equals existential projection onto remaining variables; every residual solution lifts by those pins, every original solution projects to a residual solution. Not all original witnesses, weights or counts are preserved, nor are pins necessarily semantic forced facts.
Algorithm: initialize C to all factors. Build eligible graph on C, retaining a variable only if all its incidences belong to C. Find maximum matching. If saturates C, return matching. Otherwise direct eligible edges factor->variable and matched edges variable->factor; reach from all unmatched factors. Remove reached factors from candidate C, recompute graph and matching. Repeat.
Proof of greatest set: suppose safe S subset C. Let A be reached factors and M_S a saturating internal matching for S. No reachable variable is unmatched in the maximum matching M (otherwise augmenting path). For any i in A intersect S, the variable M_S(i) is reached, and M matches it to a reached factor in S (all incidences inside S). This induces an injection A intersect S -> A intersect S, hence a bijection. Thus each reached factor of S must be M-matched through an internal variable. It cannot be an unmatched root, nor the first factor of S on a path from an outside root, because the predecessor matched variable has all incidences in S and its preceding factor already lies in S. Contradiction if intersection nonempty. Pruning never removes a safe factor set; final C itself safe and contains every safe set. At most f+1 maximum matching computations for f factors. Eligibility can be checked by explicit row scan and O(arity)-bit integers (no exponential cube enumeration). Exact lifting, strict descent of factor count, polynomial in explicit table length. This is an extension/implementation of matching-autarky reasoning, not a novelty claim for autarkies generally.
Adversarial distinction: OR(x,y) and equality(y,z) permit deleting OR by x=1, but equality has no eligible variable. A reach rule that traverses all incidences rather than eligible edges can wrongly delete OR from candidates when y was matched. All incidences restrict eligibility of variables in the candidate; they must not be confused with directed alternating arcs.
