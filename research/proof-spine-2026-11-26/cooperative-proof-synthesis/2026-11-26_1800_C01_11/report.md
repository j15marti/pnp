## 1. Exact affine-event elimination
Let F be an explicit conjunction of complements of nonempty affine events E_i={x:A_i x=b_i} over GF(2), each written as independent rows. Remove inconsistent events; a rank-zero nonempty event certifies NO. Literal guards are part of this exact residual. Let R=sum_i rank A_i, d=rank of all their rows. The state is the actual event list, immutable original source/guard map, coordinate transformations and replay stack; not merely a span or marginal supports.

For any direction v nonzero on at least one event row, split x=y+t v with a chosen coordinate y_k=0 and v_k=1. A touched event has exact form G_i(y) AND t=l_i(y), where G_i is a conjunction of r_i-1 affine equations and l_i is an affine bit. Untouched events remain on y. Eliminate t by adding one forbidden affine event for each touched pair i<j:
G_i(y) AND G_j(y) AND l_i(y) != l_j(y).
Proof: given y, t is impossible precisely when active events forbid both bits, equivalent to a pair forbidding opposite bits. Conversely absence of all pair conflicts leaves at least one bit available. Store original touched events and substitution; reverse choose a bit not forbidden. Canonicalize by Gaussian elimination, discard inconsistent events, deduplicate identical ones. Each new event rank <=r_i+r_j-1. The span after projection is contained in the image of the old row span intersected with v-annihilator: equivalently each projected row is the restriction to y of an old row combination annihilating v. Hence d decreases by at least one. This is an affine analogue of Davis–Putnam elimination, not a general SAT terminal.

## 2. A paid potential that permits row growth

In particular R<=R0+B d0. Every unit equation substitution or certified Rado peel that strictly decreases R without increasing d also decreases Phi. At most d0 dimension-reducing pivots, at most Phi0 other strict decreases, no branching. History size bounded by polynomial times number of rounds. If all three touched events have rank <=2, their total replacement rank <=9; actual increase <=3 (for three positive ranks, sum replacement ranks <=2 sum original ranks-3). Thus B=3 suffices for that step class; B=R0 permits higher-rank steps after checking actual row growth. ## 3. Dual signatures: complete discovery under a bounded-rank promise
Let L:GF(2)^R -> form space send row-index unit vectors to their written rows. Take a basis k_1..k_h of ker L. Row index j gets signature sigma_j=((k_a)_j)_a. An event-row evaluation mask u is attainable by a direction v iff u is orthogonal to ker L, equivalently XOR of sigma_j over indices with u_j=1 is zero. Indeed row evaluation image is (ker L)^perp. For every event of rank <=r enumerate its nonempty row subsets, retain their XOR signature and exact mask. With at most s(2^r-1) entries, a zero signature gives a one-touch direction, equal signatures from distinct events give two-touch, and signatures a,b,a XOR b from three distinct events give three-touch. Solve all original row evaluations against the mask by Gaussian, verify actual touched events, project exactly and check the tariff. Every attainable nonzero mask supported on <=3 eligible events has precisely such a decomposition, so exhaustive lookup is complete for accepted directions on those eligible events. If a block exceeds r it is not eligible for this scan; if capped, UNKNOWN. Unfiltered first candidate can be found by pair enumeration and a dictionary; if acceptance filters require trying all matches, conservatively charge O(L_entries^3) plus per-candidate Gaussian/projection. For r fixed (or O(log N) with proven aggregate entry budget) polynomial. No free hash or collision oracle.

## 4. Falsifier: rank-safe flat consolidation is not universally possible
Take three rank-two events on six independent bits t,a,b,c,u,v:
E1: a=1,t=0; E2: b=1,t=u; E3: c=1,t=v.
Project t. The forbidden retained set B in GF(2)^5 is union of the three pair conflict flats:
(a=b=1,u=1), (a=c=1,v=1), (b=c=1,u!=v).
Trigger words of weight <2 are absent. Each weight-two trigger word has 2 bad (u,v) words; trigger 111 has 3, for exactly 9 points. For any affine flat contained in B, its trigger projection is an affine subspace contained in {110,101,011,111}. These four points do not form an affine plane (their XOR is nonzero), hence projection dimension <=1. Each fiber over a trigger has <=3 points, hence affine fiber dimension <=1. The flat dimension is <=2. Every forbidden flat in any exact union cover thus has at most 4 points and codimension >=3. At least 3 flats are required to cover 9 points, so minimum summed rank is >=9, attained by the three pair conflicts. Input total rank is 6. No equivalent conjunction of affine-event complements on the same retained coordinates has total rank <=6, despite one exact elimination and a cheap SAT witness. This falsifies universal rank-nonincreasing consolidation of a specified pivot, not all possible pivot directions or extended formulations or arbitrary deciders. Our additive tariff pays the unavoidable +3. Independent enumeration tested all nine 8-point subsets of the forbidden set; none is affine.
