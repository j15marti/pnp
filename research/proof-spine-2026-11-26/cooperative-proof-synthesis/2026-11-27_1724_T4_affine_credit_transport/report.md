## Theorem
Let A be a nonempty affine Boolean carrier of dimension r, containing all feasible words of the active exact residual. P_d(A) is the vector space over F2 of functions represented by multilinear polynomials of degree <= fixed d on affine coordinates of A. Its dimension D_d(r)=sum_{j=0}^{min(d,r)} binom(r,j). W is a *certified* subspace of P_d(A) whose elements vanish on all feasible words. Keep one live carrier and define Phi(A,W)=D_d(r)-dim(W)+r. An independently certified new polynomial outside W strictly lowers Phi by at least one. An invertible affine chart change transports W isomorphically and preserves Phi. If H subset A is a nonempty affine subspace certified to contain every feasible word, restrict all W to H and retain the exact restricted residual and inverse maps. The restriction rho:P_d(A)->P_d(H) is onto: every affine coordinate on H extends to an affine function on A, so degree <=d extension exists. Substitution and Boolean reduction cannot increase degree. It induces a surjection P_d(A)/W -> P_d(H)/rho(W). Thus Phi(A,W)-Phi(H,rho(W)) = dim ker(rho)-dim(W intersect ker(rho)) + r-dim(H), nonnegative and strictly positive for a proper affine restriction. No reset of rank credits is needed even if rank drops. If H is empty, terminal contradiction; if 1 is in W, terminal contradiction. If a certified impossible guard is a hyperplane within current A, its opposite hyperplane contains every feasible word and can be contracted. No analogous single affine complement for arbitrary codimension >1. All conditional deductions retain exact source guard and map. No arbitrary existential/non-affine map transfer: e.g., y=product_i x_i may take degree-one y to degree r. Unrestricted branch duplication fails the potential: W=0, d=1, parent Phi=2r+1, two hyperplane children sum 4r-2 > parent for r>=2. These are scoped refusals, not decision lower bounds.

If each productive event can be acquired and verified in a bounded epoch of B(N) polynomial bit operations, then (B+1)Phi + remaining epoch credit telescopes; consume one per failed job, reset only at a strict decrease. Fixed d, r<=poly(N) and polynomially sized proof objects yield a polynomial conditional total work allowance for the single carrier path, including affine substitution and Gaussian rank computation on O(r^d) monomials. For dense matrices, deterministic bit arithmetic naive polynomial bound suffices (e.g. O(D_d(r)^3) per rank operation, polynomial substitution via expansion of each degree-d monomial). No free advice, oracle, or chart acquisition. A full branch tree requires additional paid credit, all obligations preserved; inverse chart maps polynomial size in a polynomial number of epochs. Peak polynomial representation under stated assumptions, cumulative <=time*space in serial bit model.
