## 1. Two-sided / multi-region protected contraction
Partition active nonfull factors into regions I_j, A_j=sum P_i for i in I_j. Protect W_j=A_j intersect (Q+sum_{k!=j} A_k). Suppose in each region we have a certified core C_j and one safe direction d_i per exterior factor i in I_j minus C_j, jointly independent modulo W_j+U_j, where U_j=sum P_i for i in C_j. Full factors may be removed without certificates. Then ALL exterior directions across ALL regions are jointly independent modulo Q+sum U_j.

Proof: any putative dependence writes sum_j (u_j+sum_{i in I_j-C_j} alpha_i d_i) in Q, u_j in U_j. Isolate each j. Its bracket lies in A_j and in Q+sum_{k!=j} A_k, hence lies in W_j. Local independence forces every alpha_i=0, for each j. Contradiction to a nonzero dependence. This is stronger than pairwise disjointness. Necessity: any original solution restricts to a core-union solution. Sufficiency: every core-union evaluation compatible with the protected Q evaluation extends by prescribing every exterior representative its safe value; Gaussian extension supplies all remaining values and actual row witnesses. Exact existential retraction preserving all retained core and Q evaluations, not global model counts.

For two sides and Q=0, W=A intersect B, E_L=W intersect U_L, E_R=W intersect U_R. The core-union interface is EXACTLY Z=E_L intersect E_R=U_L intersect U_R. Thus existence of a common actual word on Z suffices and is necessary. Restrict actual core supports to Z, intersect, glue actual core functionals by Gaussian on their common subspace, then extend all exterior directions. Do not independently pick arbitrary full W representatives or assume a fixed backpointer works. Concrete abstract control: left x=0 plus NAND(y,u), right y=0 plus NAND(x,v); raw shared rank2, left/right essential rank1, joint rank0. Core constraints together fix x=y=0; arbitrary local words 01 and 10 cannot simply be glued. Exact lift tested.

This theorem does NOT by itself give cheap acquisition of arbitrary exact projected supports on Z or a valid recurrence over many recursively branched cuts.

## 2. Global least-core dominance (new scheduling consequence)
Let H be mandatory indices and I eligible indices, H and I disjoint. In quotient by Q+span(H), write rho(S)=rank(Q+span(H)+sum_{i in S}P_i)-rank(Q+span(H)), f(S)=rho(S)-|S|. f is submodular. Its minimizers closed under intersection/union, so unique least minimizer M exists. For any T subset I-M, f(M union T)>=f(M) implies rank gain >=|T|. Apply Rado to written spanning direction lists in quotient by Q+span(H)+span(M); independent representatives for ALL remaining factors exist. Thus H union M itself is a valid global peel core.

Conversely let H union D be ANY valid protected peel core, with independent exterior directions modulo Q+span(H)+span(D). For T subset I-D, f(D union T)>=f(D), because the representatives for T are independent modulo that span. By submodularity, f(M intersect D)<=f(M)+f(D)-f(M union D)<=f(M). Hence M intersect D is a minimizer; leastness forces M subset D. In particular, global least core is contained in the union of EVERY valid regionwise core certificate from section 1. This is a theorem about a particular certificate class, not semantic irreducibility or all algorithms.

Paid acquisition: maximum independent transversal of eligible written direction lists in quotient by mandatory/protected span, using shortest alternating augmentation as in C01-09/C05-09. Let R be factors reachable from unmatched owners at termination through nonzero coefficients on selected representatives. No vector outside their span can be reached, or an augmenting path exists. Every candidate direction of R lies in span of selected representatives of R modulo the protected mandatory space. Therefore rho(R)=#matched in R and f(R)=-#unmatched (all unmatched in R), the global minimum since every S contains its own matched reps. Any minimizer must contain all unmatched and must contain every dependency neighbor of a factor in it (its rank equals the number of selected reps in it, so independent global reps outside it cannot enter its span). Thus R is exactly least minimizer M. Directly recheck rank certificate. At most |I| augmentations, each with polynomially many written vector/bit Gaussian operations. No free rank or SAT oracle.

FIXPOINT COROLLARY: drop full factors and retain only H union M. On this exact residual the same least-core operation returns ALL remaining nonfull factors: f restricted to subsets of M has unique least minimizer M. For ANY partition and acquired protected certificates of the stated kind, section 1 and dominance imply no more factor deletion. In particular two-sided protected effective rank equals ordinary cut rank on the least-core residual, at every cut. Repeating the same certificate method with more replica layouts cannot further shrink this residual; a genuinely new exact transformation must change the factor system. This is the principal negative result. It does not preclude low ordinary width in a different order, affine-coset representations, matching, flow, pivots or other terminals.

## 3. Productive alternative: basis-independent two-touch pivot
Use explicit conjunction of avoided affine events F_i=(A_i x=b_i), with independent written rows in each event; all affine offsets and guard equations retained. C05-09 supplies exact event-pivot idea. Here prove efficient complete discovery of a direction touching at most TWO events, independent of the chosen coordinate basis. Direction v is nonzero on at least one event row and annihilates all rows of other events. For candidate D of one/two event indices, concatenate all row vectors as columns of linear map L: GF2^R -> ambient form space. Let K=ker L. Let J(D) be their row-index block. A direction exists supported on D iff rank(L)>rank(L restricted outside J). Rank-nullity gives exact drop
  rank(L)-rank(L_out) = |J|-dim(projection_J K).
Compute one Gaussian dependency basis of K (retaining original row-index coefficients) per round. Each row index has a signature column of its coefficients over this kernel basis. Projection dimension is rank of the signatures for J. Enumerate at most s+s(s-1)/2 candidates and check the displayed rank drop. A positive drop yields an actual v: impose zero evaluation on every outside row and evaluation one on any row of D outside their span, solve by Gaussian. Verify touched set directly. This is complete for one/two-event directions, not just written coordinates, and polynomial in the encoded current row list. General fixed d candidate enumeration is polynomial with exponent depending on d, not a log-d theorem.

Exact elimination: choose a coordinate k with v_k=1, write x=y+t v with y_k=0. Substitution is bijective between x and (y,t). Untouched events do not depend on t. Each touched event is a trigger B_i y=c_i plus t=l_i(y), obtained by one pivot row and Gaussian cancellation. With one touched event always choose t avoiding it. With two, replace them by ONE forbidden affine event: both triggers hold AND l_1(y) differs from l_2(y). In that case and only that case both bits are forbidden. Canonicalize inconsistent events away, rank-zero consistent event rejects. Replacement rank <=r_1+r_2-1. Thus the total written independent row count strictly decreases; rank itself may grow but no exponential table materialization needed. Reconstruct with either t compatible with all touched events and invert x=y+t v. Ordinary rank-one event gives unconditional equality, substitute and reclose; global generalized Rado peel also decreases written row count. A scheduler alternating these operations makes <=initial row count successful rounds, with all failed candidate scans polynomially bounded. Source adapter for arbitrary native owner degree: exact integer one-hot prefix constraints (s_prev+x=s_next), endpoints 0,1, rows 000,011,101; parity quotient plus forbidden word yields O(N) initial rows and exact original witness lift (C05-09 derivation checked algebraically, new tests below use archived exact regional chart and bounded-rank table adapter instead). No proof of universal availability of a two-touch direction; native residual tests explicitly stall. This is an exact productive module, not a total algorithm.
