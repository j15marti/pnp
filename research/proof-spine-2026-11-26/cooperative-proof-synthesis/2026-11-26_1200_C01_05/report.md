## 1. Phase equations and exact pair elimination (PROVED)
For region i let e_i=0 for L or 1 for U. Its B and C occupancies equal t_i, and A consumption is e_i+t_i. Every shared B/C owner enforces t_i XOR t_j=1. The resulting colored multigraph on regions is the union of a B perfect pairing and a C perfect pairing, hence disjoint even cycles, including length two with parallel edges. Store original owner edges; graph simplification only for checking bipartite parity consistency. Unavailable local phases give exact pins; inconsistent pins/odd XOR cycle reject. This alone does not imply exact A compatibility.

A double-A seam occurs when two distinct A owners are precisely the two A ports of each of two regions i,j, and no third region touches either. Acquire the exact set of compatible (t_i,t_j) by enumerating at most 9 pairs of original support rows and testing complementary A occupancy at both actual shared owners. Retain a pair of original witnesses for each allowed phase pair. This is an exact existential projection of those four A incidences, disjoint among such seams. The integer identity is e_i+e_j+t_i+t_j=2. LL forces both t=1, UU forces both t=0; LU permits only (0,1),(1,0). Empty compatible set rejects, singleton gives two exact pins, both options give a complement edge. Remove the two regions' A obligations and the two discharged A owners, but retain their B/C phase vertices, edges, original IDs and witness maps. Their remaining interaction is precisely the phase pair support; no other original owner can be affected. This is exact in both directions, not a retraction preserving every internal witness or count. Apply unconditional parity closure after acquiring these seams and before deciding residual.

In each merged free phase component choose a bipartition color s_i, t_i=z XOR s_i. Every original B/C cycle has equally many s=0,1; every removed seam of a free class has opposite t and therefore removes one of each. A singleton seam pins the component, so cannot violate the free-class invariant. Thus active (not discharged) factors in each free class have equal s counts. A free class with zero active factors is unconstrained after verified seam compatibility, select either phase. With two active factors they have opposite phases. More than two is a long free class. ## 4. Rigorous obstruction to generalizing pair flow without phase treatment
A free class of even active size >=4, all factors with both local phases, has constant total rank. Any two exact bases in opposite phases differ in local cardinality by one at EVERY active factor. Choose a factor losing one element going from base X to base Y and an element x in X\Y there. Its local selected set in Y is a subset of X at that factor, because possible quotas are 0/1 or 1/2 on two ports. Thus no y in Y\X belongs to the same factor. Exchanging x with any such y changes counts in exactly two factors, cannot remain in X phase and cannot reach Y phase (>=4 count changes). Violates matroid base exchange. So no exact single partition-capacity base representation on the same incidence set. This is representation-specific; nonmatroid support can still be solved by phase seams, bounded phase enumeration or other structures.
