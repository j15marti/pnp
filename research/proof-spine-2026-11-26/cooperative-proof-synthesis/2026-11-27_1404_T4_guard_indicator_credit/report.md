## Main theorem: fixed-chart semantic novelty implies rank credit
Let A be an explicitly acquired affine carrier containing every solution of an exact guarded residual F. Work in fixed r free binary coordinates z, with fixed original-coordinate lift. Every checked conditional impossibility F and G_i -> false implies the Boolean indicator I_i of G_i vanishes on every feasible word. If G_i is affine of relative codimension q_i, use independent normal equations ell_j(z)=b_j, so I_i=product_j(1+b_j+ell_j(z)) in R=GF(2)[z]/(z_i^2-z_i), degree <=q_i. An empty guard has zero indicator. For fixed d, D=sum_{j=0}^{min(d,r)} binom(r,j). Store a coefficient basis M of proved vanishing polynomials of degree <=d. If q_i<=d and G_i contains a word not in any previously excluded guard, and M consists only of identities obtained from previous guard indicators by sound addition/multiplication, every old basis row vanishes at that word, but I_i=1. Thus I_i is independent and increases rank by >=1. Even without testing semantic novelty, direct exact row reduction checks rank credit. Other independently acquired vanishing identities can be stored, but then novelty relative to their common zero set (not just the guard union) is needed for the implication. Constant 1 in span refutes F. Discard dependent rows, never credit them.

For a fixed polynomial epoch allowance B(N), charge every candidate, failure, verification, branch and handoff within that epoch. Let a=rank at epoch start and b=remaining work credit. Phi=(D-a)(B+1)+b. New epochs require increased rank. Every charged operation or restart strictly decreases Phi; initial numeric value <=(D+1)(B+1), polynomial for fixed d and r<=poly(N). Dense row representation and exact Gaussian rank operations have polynomial bit cost, no precision oracle. Each certificate check must fit charged epoch. Expenditures on chart acquisition, initial proof DAG, memory and reconstruction are separate polynomial allowances, not free. No chart resets, fresh variables or branch copies without a separate global transfer theorem. If exhausted, UNKNOWN, not SAT. Point guard example: a single excluded word in r>d coordinates has nonzero indicator of degree r (unique multilinear representation), and no nonzero degree<=d polynomial supported only on that word. Thus strict exclusion alone does not imply rank progress for arbitrary high-codimension guards. This is a restriction, not an unrestricted decision lower bound.
