## Connecting lemma: affine syndrome-tree
For any finite explicit support system on a consistent affine slice x=x0+V, let W_i <= V* be the span of its factor observations (canonical masks), and R_i subset W_i* its exact attained assignments after sign transport and row filtering. Each original global assignment restricts to a tuple of these functionals. Let E=direct_sum_i W_i and S:E->W=sum_i W_i be addition; K=ker S. A tuple of local functionals is a functional lambda on E. It comes from a global assignment iff lambda vanishes on K: necessity by linearity, sufficiency by descent through E/K isomorphic W and extension to V*. This is exact jointly, not a moment relaxation. Source witness lift: extend linear functional by Gaussian solve, then reconstruct group selectors and native block matching using original witness maps. Not executed on native YES cases (UNSAT focus).

Choose spanning tree T on factor indices, weight dim(W_i intersect W_j). For edge ij identify same vector w in its two summands, giving element e_i(w)+e_j(w) of K. All edge relation subspaces are independent: at a leaf only one edge contributes, so a vanishing sum forces that edge vector zero; peel tree. Their span K_T has rank sum of edge weights. Complement basis k_1,...,k_delta of K modulo K_T acquired by Gaussian elimination. Thus delta=sum_i dim W_i - dim W - sum_edges dim intersection >=0. Maximum-weight spanning tree minimizes delta over trees. At delta=0 agreement on tree separators suffices for globally compatible assignments. More generally each row contributes delta-bit syndrome (evaluation of its component on k_l). Root tree; dynamic program indexed by separator observation values, store exact attainable XOR sums of syndrome contributions in subtree. Child joins require equality of separator and combine XOR. Root accepts iff syndrome 0 attained, otherwise certified NO. Induction on subtree proves both directions. No counting oracle; states are Boolean existence sets, not exact counts. This is a linear-algebra extension of junction-tree gluing; novelty relative to all literature not asserted.
