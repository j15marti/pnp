## Connecting theorem
Let B be satisfiable 2CNF on d variables, with implication digraph G (including units as doubled literals). For ANY set P of pinned literals, B AND conjunction P is inconsistent iff there exist p,q in P (possibly equal) with a directed path p -> not q in G; paths of length zero permitted. Necessity of inconsistency from a path is immediate. Conversely let U be union of forward reachable vertices from pins. If it contains v and not v, choose paths p->v and q->not v; reverse-complement second gives v->not q and concatenation proves criterion. If U consistent, set all its literals true, complements false, and retain any satisfying assignment of B on remaining variables. Every clause with a forced false literal has its other literal forced true by closure. Every other clause is either already true or unchanged under this assignment. Thus all pins extend. Also follows by exact existential projection of 2CNF using binary resolution. This gives exact local support for each conjunction term, not global joint support across unrelated factors.

Keep compact groups D_i=OR_j AND_{l in T_ij} l after exact one-hot selector projection (C01-15), plus B. Under guard g add units g to B. If B inconsistent refute. Otherwise remove precisely terms T with a pin-pair path conflict. If no term survives a group, refute. Any unit or binary clause C meeting every surviving term is necessary under guard: each surviving branch pins at least one literal in C. Import C locally and recompute B reachability. All guard-local imports preserve necessary consequences, not an assertion that all surviving terms coexist. Repeated batches have finite unary/binary universe. If guard closure refutes, export clause OR_{p in g} not p unconditionally (relative to original fixed guards); rerun unguarded closure after each import. A single DNF plus 2CNF is decided exactly by checking all terms; many groups only a sound partial refuter. Source adapter and every projected group preserve existential decision; witness lift chooses a surviving original one-hot selector for any actual completion, not just a relaxation witness. No counting claim.
