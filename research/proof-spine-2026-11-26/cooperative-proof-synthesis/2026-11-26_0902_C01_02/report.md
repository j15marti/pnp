## 2. Genuine prime geometric sources
For positive integer k not divisible by3, V_k={(a,b,c) integer : |a|+|b|+|c|=k}. Triangulate each of the eight octants by elementary unit triangles on its triangular lattice. This is a closed triangulated sphere (octahedron with frequency k); faces number 8k^2 and vertices 4k^2+2. Color a vertex by (|b|+2|c|) mod3. Within any face, neighboring vertices differ by transferring one absolute unit between axes, so colors differ, and every face has all three colors. Cyclic axis permutation adds or subtracts k mod3 to color, hence color populations equal. Create one actual tuple for every elementary face, with original element and tuple IDs. Face interiors are disjoint; drawing a tuple center with three arcs to its vertices gives planar bipartite incidence. Thus these are genuine native P3DM sources, no extra promise imposed on the original decision problem. Owner degrees 4 at the six poles, 6 elsewhere; tuple degree3.
All three simple pair projections connected: every deleted third-color vertex has link cycle alternating the other two colors, allowing any primal path segment through it to be replaced by a path along that link. Nonpole owner in either retained color has 3 neighbors of the other retained color, for k>=2. Hence all simple degree-two charts fail on intact source. Incidence graph has no one- or two-edge cut. Each primal edge has two length-two paths through its incident face centers. Removing <=2 incidence edges can destroy both paths for at most one primal edge: both removed edges must be in two faces sharing that unique edge. All other primal adjacencies survive, and deleting at most one edge of a triangulation leaves it connected (every edge on a cycle). Each tuple center still has >=1 incidence. Thus entire graph remains connected. This proof excludes two-incidence seam acquisition, not all regional decompositions or all algorithms.

## 3. Thirteen-fold face refinement lift (PROVED scoped)
A finite local certificate supplies an infinite constructive bypass. For any closed properly three-colored triangulation with a vertex-disjoint facial-triangle cover, subdivide each triangular face into a frequency-13 triangular lattice. Assign every original vertex to its selected covering macroface. Assign every original edge to exactly one adjacent macroface; if an adjacent face belongs to the cover, give the edge to that face (at most one adjacent face selected because cover faces vertex-disjoint); otherwise choose lexicographically first incident face. Within each macroface, require coverage of every strictly interior lattice vertex, all 12 interior vertices of each owned edge, and all three corner vertices iff selected. No other vertices may be covered by that macroface. The nine needed local configurations are: all eight edge-ownership masks for nonselected face; selected face with all three owned edges. All nine have explicitly verified covers. Let local barycentric coordinates be nonnegative integers (a,b,c) summing to13. A patch certificate is a list of elementary triangles; checker verifies pairwise L1 distance2 for the three lattice points of each triangle, no repeated vertices, and exact equality with the assigned vertex set. There are 66 strictly interior vertices. The nonselected patch uses 22+4s elementary triangles if s owned edges; the selected patch uses35. Constant exact search macro.py produces all nine certificates; refinement.py independently checks exact sets and local adjacency. Complete patch lists in patch_certificates.json. This is an exhaustive finite certificate of a fixed gadget, not an empirical asymptotic inference.
Map each local coordinate triple (a,b,c) to a*u+b*v+c*w where u,v,w are the coarse vertices; this is in the scaled refined lattice for the octahedral case. More generally use the standard barycentric identification along shared coarse edges. Interiors are private, each edge-interior vertex assigned to one face, each old corner assigned to exactly one selected face. Thus union of local covers covers every refined vertex exactly once. The coloring extends consistently by the weighted sum of original corner colors mod3, since13=1 mod3 and shared-edge barycentric coordinates agree. Every refined elementary face is rainbow.
