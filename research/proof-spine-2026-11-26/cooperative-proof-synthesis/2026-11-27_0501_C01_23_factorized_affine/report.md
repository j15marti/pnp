## Connecting theorem
For each cut retain exact support D=o+H+sum_i D_i over GF2, with H a linear subspace, each D_i finite nonempty explicit set containing zero (translation can absorb an offset). Sum denotes independent choice and XOR, not necessarily a direct sum or unique representation; do not infer cardinality as product when maps overlap. Empty distinguished separately. Leaf acquired support is exact. C01-21 child interface maps L,R for intersection and P,Q for parent are well-defined. Combine two child offsets and all finite factors after mapping their rows to (c,p), shared compatibility and parent contributions; combine both affine subspaces as common slack. Write compatibility as M u = c0+sum c_i, output p0+sum p_i+V u. M,V explicitly linear, slack coordinates u. Choose a linear quotient map pi with kernel im M and linear map J from full syndrome space to slack coordinates such that M J c=c when c in im M (pivot reduction with annotations, zero on a chosen complement). Transform each finite row (c,p) to (pi c,p+V J c); same transform for offset. Thus exact compatibility is sum pi c_i=pi c0 and parent support is corrected offset plus sum corrected p_i plus V ker M. Proof forward: all compatible u=J(c0+sum c_i)+ker M; linearity distributes corrections. Reverse: any retained finite choices meeting quotient equation and any V ker M contribution have a compatible u, thus child witnesses glue by C01-21. Preserves decision and exact joint support. On fixed source guard, no conditional facts exported unguarded.

Group projected syndrome spans using a basis selected from their actual row vectors. For each factor connect its vertex to owners of basis columns appearing in coordinates of any of its projected rows. Distinct connected components have disjoint basis-coordinate sets, so their spans form a direct sum. If target outside total span, NO; else decompose target by those coordinates. For each component enumerate only its finite group choices that sum to its assigned target; collect their joint corrected parent values into one finite factor. If any component empty, join empty; otherwise choices of component contributions are independent even if parent coordinate images overlap. This is the genuine hybrid: Gaussian elimination changes compatibility domains, thereby exposing independent finite groups; exact relational factorization can further split any materialized finite factor along fixed output coordinate blocks. An explicit support factors across a split iff size equals product of projection sizes, since support contained in product.
