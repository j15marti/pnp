On taking a new pending clause, visit each older processed clause with an opposite pivot, then mark/index the new clause exactly once. Each unordered parent pair and pivot visited at most once, at most Q=W*binom(M,2). Process every candidate including tautologies, duplicate and over-width results, charging attempts even when no narrower output. Canonical clause insertion at most M. FIFO or width-priority scheduling fair if run to completion; fixed W gives fixed polynomial bit cost in original N (chart coordinates and bit lengths polynomial; deterministic sorting/trie lookup, explicit indexes, bounded subset tests, polynomial work for each pair; no free oracle). Subsumption-aware insertion optionally skips a new clause if a previously admitted subset already exists. Never remove the earlier proof record. At uncapped saturation, for any width-W resolution derivation from eligible clauses a subsuming clause is available by induction: if a parent subsumer omits the resolved pivot it subsumes the resolvent; otherwise resolve the two subsumers, whose resolvent is contained in the original non-tautological resolvent, and is either admitted or itself subsumed.
