## 2. New family, exact nonlinear seam
Let m>=5 odd, k>=2; m disjoint k-bit blocks X_i and M_i=product_j x_ij. Each edge of a cycle has exact relation M_i XOR M_(i+1)=1. Encode each edge by k^2 positive binary clauses (a OR b), a in X_i,b in X_(i+1), and one all-negative clause on all 2k bits. The positive clauses are equivalent to M_i OR M_(i+1), since if both blocks contain a zero the corresponding pair is false. The long negative clause excludes both monomials equal 1. Thus exact relation, not relaxation. There are mk variables and m(k^2+1) clauses, explicit input size O(mk^2 log(mk)). No extension variables. Changing the final seam to equality gives a YES neighbor (two implications, each a length k+1 clause); alternating all-one/all-zero blocks give an explicit checked witness. For NO, XOR of m equations cancels every monomial twice and leaves 1=0. This is a sparse degree-k factor-ANF certificate of size O(mk log(mk)) bits, after exact source verification; degree can grow, but sparse monomial representations remain compact for this family.

## 3. Degree-locality refusal theorem (new scoped result)
Let H be ANY bipartite subset of edge factors, and work over F2 on square-free Boolean polynomials in the original mk bits. Every polynomial q of total degree <k that vanishes on the solutions of H also vanishes at the global all-ones word. Proof: choose a bipartition of H (including isolated vertices arbitrarily); fix blocks on one side to all ones, allow every block on the other side to range independently over all 2^k-1 words other than all ones. Every such joint assignment satisfies H. For any proper subset S of a k-bit block, XOR over all 2^k words of the product over S is zero (2^(k-|S|) even); excluding all ones gives XOR one. Thus XOR evaluation over this odd-size product set agrees with evaluation at all ones on every degree<k monomial, including constant, and by linearity on q. All q vanish on that product set, so q(all ones)=0. No enumeration required for this symbolic argument.
Every proper edge subset of the odd cycle is bipartite, including m-1 edges. Consequently even the complete collection of degree<k consequences acquired from ANY proper subsets of original edge factors (not just one edge, not just quadratic linear span) has a common all-ones model. Algebraic closure of those equations alone, including multiplication, cannot produce constant one. This is a degree-vs-source-locality obstruction, not a lower bound for global semantic extraction (full source UNSAT implies all equations), guarded closure with incompatible pins, extended variables, higher-degree axioms, general resolution, or arbitrary algorithms. Ordinary degree-bounded PC on the explicit CNF additionally cannot use long input axiom of degree 2k at a lower cap; that observation by itself would be trivial and is not the new theorem. Native compiler may expose extensions; no transfer of this refusal to native STRICT-EB4-v1 asserted.

## 4. Exact compressed quotient and logarithmic-depth composition
Replace each block by one phase bit y_i=M_i. Full original seam incidence accounted for. Both phases have lifts, e.g. all zeros and all ones. Every solution projects and every quotient solution lifts independently. The exact joint compatibility problem becomes y_i XOR y_j=1; a path of length L gives endpoint parity L mod2. Compose adjacent paths by XOR in balanced tree O(log m) depth, O(m) aggregate phase operations plus source recognition, proof recording and witness checks. Cycle parity gives terminal. ## 5. Complementary width-two RUP escape, no extension variables
A stronger surprise: the degree/locality obstruction does not defeat narrow guarded DAGs. On a path X0-X1-X2-X3, assume a in X0 is zero and d in X3 is zero. Positive pairs force every bit in X1 and X2 to one; the negative X1-X2 seam conflicts. Thus (a OR d) is RUP. Induct along odd paths of length L=3,5,...,m-2: previously derived positive pairs from the starting bit to the block at distance L-2 force that whole block to one; a zero destination bit forces the adjacent block at distance L-1 to one. Their negative seam contradicts. This learns positive pairs at distance L. On the length-m closed walk, the starting bit is the destination bit: one assumption forces adjacent blocks all ones, giving a RUP unit. Repeat for all 2k variables of two adjacent blocks, then their long negative seam yields the empty clause. Every added row has width <=2, though original clauses may be long. No auxiliary y_i in this proof. The construction generates at most 2k*((m-3)/2*k+1)+1=O(mk^2) learned rows, each with <=2k+1 ordered unit reasons. O(mk^3 log N) certificate bits and O(mk^3) literal reads in the reason replay, plus parsing/indexing/source recognition and polynomial deterministic dictionary overhead. This is a uniform family-specific proof, not universal width-two RUP completeness. The deterministic checker verifies each ordered reason as unit or final conflict from actual earlier clauses. ## 6. Scoped stochastic and derandomization consequence
Let n=mk, Q=1+2n+4 binom(n,2), all consistent signed guards of size<=2, including empty. Sample uniformly from Q and test exact UP on current monotone clause database; admit any new RUP row once. Given the explicit ordered proof above, at any unresolved history the first absent proof row has all earlier rows present and its negated guard triggers UP. Thus conditional probability at least 1/Q of acquiring that missing row or reaching a terminal. Extra learned clauses cannot invalidate prior RUP. At most L proof rows must be acquired, with expected <=QL trials, or L*ceil(Q ln(L/delta)) trials using stage union bound; implementation should use an explicit rational bound/integer upper bound, all RNG bits, finite cap, and verification charged. Deterministic enumeration of Q guards repeated until fixed point gives polynomial acquisition even without knowing the odd-cycle order. This is a proved probability floor for this family only; no randomized trials on the new controls, no claim of universal progress. It shows how exact proof existence of this special grammar plus finite candidate universe bridges stochastic acquisition to deterministic P for the family.
