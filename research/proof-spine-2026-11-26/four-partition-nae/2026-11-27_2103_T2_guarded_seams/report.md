## 1. Exact mixed fibre elimination
Let s be boundary selector bits, x transport bits, and suppose all factors in a region are exactly equations A_i x = f_i(s) over F2, with coefficients independent of s. Other constraints, if present, must be entirely on s or otherwise preserved outside this elimination; no eliminated variable may have an unaccounted incident factor. For each selector assignment, feasibility of Ax=f(s) is equivalent to L f(s)=0, where rows of L form a basis of the left kernel of A. Proof: a linear row reduction on A and symbolic right-hand side yields pivot rows and zero rows. The latter are exactly the compatibility constraints; if they vanish at s, set free transport bits arbitrarily and back-substitute. Necessity follows by multiplying by L. This is an exact projection and a constructive witness lift, not a parity relaxation of an arbitrary nonaffine factor. Keep full guard and original variable maps.

A single row reduction handles all selector words at once. With t supplied selectors and t=O(log N), explicit truth-table columns of size 2^t are polynomial; if local RHSs have explicit degree <=d for fixed d, instead use the square-free ANF basis of D=sum_{j<=d} binom(t,j). XOR row operations preserve degree and can be performed on dense coefficient rows; O(m^2(n+D)) elementary bit-entry operations is a safe elimination bound for m rows, n transport variables (plus polynomial indexing, parsing, reconstruction and verifying source tables). No need to enumerate 2^t when degree fixed. The projected equations on s may be nonaffine and NP-hard. A terminal is certified if any resulting row is the constant 1=0, or an independently verified guard cover excludes all s. A polynomial number of fixed-size selector-set candidates can be acquired by exhaustive enumeration (e.g. all pairs); for growing unknown sets, acquisition is not automatically polynomial. No coefficient matrix depending on s is silently handled by this lemma. Source recognition and complete-incidence condition are essential.

Connected graph corollary: one edge bit per edge, one parity equation per vertex, RHS f_v(s); every edge occurs twice. Summing all rows gives XOR_v f_v(s)=0. This condition is sufficient for a connected graph by a spanning-tree leaf elimination, or rank |V|-1 of its binary incidence matrix. Hence exact conditional boundary response with no need to enumerate internal transport states. This is a standard linear-algebra fact applied to our mixed seams, not a new unrestricted complexity theorem.

## 4. Growing-selector symbolic cancellation, no boundary-word enumeration
Partition floor(m/3) disjoint triples of vertex rows; attach separate (a_j,b_j) to each triple. f1_j=a_j b_j, f2_j=a_j(1+b_j), f3_j=a_j+epsilon_j, XOR_j epsilon_j=1 for NO, zero all for YES. Remaining rows ordinary parity zero. Now t=2 floor(m/3) grows with input, not constant. Each local exact violation function g_i(x,s) is the XOR of three transport edge bits and its RHS, of degree <=2. Every edge occurs twice, every nonlinear term occurs twice, and every selector linear term cancels. Thus XOR_i g_i=constant 1 for NO. This supplies a deterministic degree-two Boolean linear-span proof for this family, without even guessing a selector partition. It does not need the full joint separator table. For YES all-zero witness. Local affine hull still cannot detect contradiction: nonlinear RHS factors have full affine hull (vary x within even-parity directions; a nonzero transport-parity coefficient would make the nonlinear RHS affine in s, impossible). The only remaining local linear equations are pure zero parity or f3; all-zero x and a_j=epsilon_j satisfy those. Thus no pure local-affine contradiction. This is a restricted polynomial calculus/ANF cancellation, not novel general algebraic proof theory.

Acquisition groups source clauses by exact scope, enumerates only each width<=5 local table, computes its violation ANF by Möbius transform, XORs sparse monomial keys, independently checks each local polynomial truth table and every monomial multiplicity.
