## 1. Exact series-elimination identity
A matched owner at v is represented by OR of parity equations x_v xor x_w = q over its outgoing arcs. One matched owner per representative. Normalize duplicate equal arcs; two opposing arcs to the same w give TRUE; self arc parity0 TRUE, parity1 FALSE (delete). Empty remaining owner FALSE, absent owner unconstrained. Do not delete protected variables. Suppose nonprotected v occurs in only one other distinct owner u, with normalized arc u->v label p; v's own nonempty owner is OR_j(x_v xor x_wj=q_j), no self arc. Other alternatives of owner u are A, independent of v.
Then exists x_v [(A OR (x_u xor x_v=p)) AND OR_j(x_v xor x_wj=q_j)] equals A OR OR_j(x_u xor x_wj=p xor q_j). For A true, choose v to satisfy any own arc, possible for every external word; otherwise substitute the required v=x_u xor p. This is pointwise exact, not only satisfiability preservation. If v has no own owner, its single predecessor can always satisfy its arc, and both can be eliminated with u kept as a free variable. If no predecessor, v's nonempty owner can always be satisfied and removed. Empty owner refutes globally; tautologies removed with provenance. If composition produces self arcs or opposing duplicates re-normalize. Reverse lifting: if original A true choose v to satisfy its own row, otherwise take required phase; verify original rows in reverse elimination order. All other clauses/guards independent of v, hence exact existential substitution. Number of arcs nonincreasing: replace one predecessor arc plus all v arcs with at most the v arc count, plus unchanged A. Each step removes v and one matched row (or more if tautological); retain unmatched owners unchanged. Record consumed original owner IDs and arc origins; certificate is replay of local identities. Direct scan at most O(N) eliminations, each polynomial in N, fixed polynomial total time/space even with full history and failed scans. No claim of general availability of this rule.

Independent algebra view: eliminate v from the Boolean relation by its two possible values; if A true both branches jointly cover all outer words since any own parity alternative can be satisfied by one phase; if A false only one phase remains and all outgoing alternatives are translated by XOR. Equivalent to bounded-occurrence CNF resolution after converting each signed NAE owner into its two complementary clauses, but proof above independent of importing external complexity theorem. This changes the representation before Gaussian/SCC or frontier processing; arbitrary multiple incoming owners cannot be merged separately.

## 2. Merge-excess interface theorem
For original normalized nontrivial strongly connected component S (>=2 vertices) of matched free representative digraph, let E be every protected entry within S, and B all outgoing boundary variables/pins. Define internal arc surplus b=|A(S,S)|-|S|, counting distinct directed internal neighbor arcs after normalization. Strong connectivity gives each vertex internal indegree >=1; consequently at most b vertices have internal indegree >=2. Retain R=E union all original vertices of internal indegree >=2. If R empty retain one arbitrary vertex as anchor (or use established unguarded even-directed-cycle test instead). Every vertex in S minus R initially has exactly one internal predecessor and no external reference or guard. After a series elimination, each surviving vertex's distinct predecessor count cannot increase: remove predecessor v, possibly replace it with u, and dedup, while any tautology or lost owner only deletes arcs. Thus every vertex in S minus R can be eliminated in any order, using the zero/one-predecessor cases. If contradiction occurs return FALSE. Preserve all actual outside variables and unmatched owners. Remaining representative constraints count <=|R|; no increase in arc count. For nonempty E, at most b unprotected variables remain (R minus E); for empty E use prior rootless/zero-entry extension theorem if applicable or count the added anchor, <=b+1. Singleton SCC handled directly. This is an exact symbolic interface: enumerate only those <=b internal surviving phases, NOT boundary words, and OR the resulting conjunctions after substitution; leave entry and exit literals symbolic. Formula size/time <=2^b poly(N) for nonempty E, <=2^(b+1) poly(N) with optional anchor. Negate for exact negative-compatibility predicate. Each surviving row remains a parity disjunction with representative retained or fixed; no uncharged existential oracle. A word admits extension iff it satisfies at least one symbolic branch. Distinguish the cost of representing each interface from solving their unrestricted joint conjunction. If every SCC has b<=c log2 N, fixed c, and there are O(N) SCCs, independent summaries cost fixed polynomial TOTAL, not product, but joint decision still open. For b=0 and nonempty E, no enumeration: at most |E| signed NAE constraints, all interiors eliminated even with arbitrarily many entries. If R empty/closed, handle as above; not a universal single-NAE theorem. Mapped macros may remain cyclic across entries; do not apply acyclic token DP without checked ordering. General merge-surplus acquisition is polynomial for supplied matching; existence of universally low surplus or low frontier not proved.
