## 2. Constructive and adversarial tracks: guarded Hall subclass is NP-complete
For each original variable x make copies x_0,...,x_3, one per original partition. Each original triple is copied on its three occurrence IDs; assign all these new owners color2, as they are pairwise disjoint. For every x and p modulo4, create fresh intermediary u_(x,p) and two fresh protected variables q1,q0, respectively pinned 1,0. Add positive triples NAE(x_p,u,q1) color0 and NAE(u,x_(p+1),q0) color1. Exact local truth table of available u for (a,b)=(00,01,10,11): {1},{0,1},empty,{0}. Thus projected relation is a<=b. A directed cyclic chain forces every x_p equal and u=1+x_p. Conversely for any original assignment assign all copies equal, all intermediaries opposite, pins as given. Every link satisfied and each original owner's truth exactly retained. This is an exact adapter, not a supplied SAT witness. All clauses linear: unique anchor and intermediary IDs, neighboring links share only a copy, original copied owners share no variables and meet any link in at most one copy. Each variable occurs at most once in each assigned color (3 used colors, fourth free). The construction is explicit O(n) objects O(N polylog N) bit cost.

H has 4n copies+4n intermediaries+8n protected anchors=16n variables, m+8n owners. The guarded extension problem on this explicit positive linear partially four-colored Hall-admitted class is NP-complete by the reduction from F and NP verification. ## 3. Exact twelve-copy completion (independent geometric track)
For any positive linear 3-uniform partial four-partition instance H with each variable occurring at most once per color, construct 12 disjoint copies indexed (l,j) with l=0,1,2 and j in GF(4). Represent field elements 0,1,alpha,alpha+1 by bits 0,1,2,3; addition XOR and alpha multiplication [0,2,3,1]. For each original variable v missing in color p in GF(4), add four filler triples {v_(0,j),v_(1,j+p),v_(2,j+alpha*p)} in color p for j in GF(4). Each missing color is filled exactly once at each copy; every color is now a perfect partition, no repeated triples. Each filler involves distinct layers and copies. Two fillers on distinct v are disjoint. On same v, same p disjoint; different p share at most one point: knowing two coordinates uniquely determines j,p, using invertibility of 1,alpha,alpha-1. A filler meets any copied owner in at most one variable because it contains at most one variable from a given copy. Copied owners linear by original property. Thus full source native positive, linear, exact4, four supplied partitions.

Exact projection onto central copy (0,0): any model of completion restricts to H there. Conversely extend any assignment z on H by z in all four layer0 copies and its complement in layers1,2. Every copied owner has the same truth as its original; every filler is mixed. This proves exact projection for every assignment, including guards placed only on central copy. No claim that arbitrary completions equal the phase section, only existence suffices. Native completion 12|V(H)| variables, 16|V(H)| clauses, O(N) size apart from indices. ## 4. Corrected free-Hall alternating core theorem (PROVED WITH SCOPE)
Given any actual finite signed NAE region, with protected set B and free variables I, every constraint touching I included, no unsupported interior guard, take maximum matching M of owner rows to free columns only. Let U be unmatched owner rows. Traverse row->all incident free columns, column->its matched row, starting at U. Let S reached rows, K reached free columns. No reached column unmatched, by maximum matching augmenting path criterion. Every matched row reached has its matched column reached (it is reached through that column), and every reached column has matched row reached. Therefore |S|-|K|=|U|=m-|M|. Every S row uses only K or B. Every outside row is matched to an outside free variable; distinct representatives. No outside free variable occurs in S. This construction is polynomial and requires no guessed separator or region.

For outside matched owner with representative v and representative sign s0, form arcs v->w for other occurrences, parity 1+s0+s_w; owner is satisfied iff one arc's equation holds. Treat K union B as terminal roots, also any unmatched outside free variable as a root. Build graph with outgoing arcs only for outside matched owners. For every sink SCC not a root, all its outgoing arcs internal and matched representatives inside. Necessity of a balanced simple directed cycle: follow any chosen satisfied arc in sink. Sufficiency: choose one balanced cycle and consistent phase; reverse BFS extend within SCC then all upstream vertices, holding roots K,B fixed (unmatched free roots arbitrary). Every vertex reaches a sink. Signed loops, parallel arcs retained; singleton matched row with no alternative fails. Decide sink by RST even directed simple cycle recognition, subdividing parity0 edges to length2, parity1 to length1, preserving loops (or handle loops separately). This is known deterministic polynomial recognition, not double-cover closed-walk parity reachability. Witness acquisition by paid edge deletion self-reduction if needed. Thus if an outside rootless sink is NO, whole region NO for every protected word. Otherwise outside rows extend every assignment satisfying S on K union B, so exact full boundary support equals that of S. External guards on B preserved, and other guards may be retained only if they mention retained variables or have separately proved exact adapter. This is a stronger acquisition mechanism than enumerating separators: matching finds maximal reachable deficient block relative to the chosen matching without searching. Not a novelty claim over matching-autarky/Rado style literature; exact graph-cut guarded form reconstructed here. If S empty, solve all rootless sinks and discard every matched owner, so arbitrary many protected words all extend or none; this is compatible with C08-20 free-only Hall case.

Native obstruction: for raw degree4 unguarded source, every outside free variable has all four owners outside S; if r outside owners and v outside variables, 4v<=3r while r<=v by outside matching, so v=0 and r=0. Thus this procedure cannot shrink raw native full components. On compiled H, free defect m and reachable core exactly all original rows and links; optional tails peel but hard core stays. Universal effective-density reduction remains OPEN. Fixed-poly total for matching, traversal, at most O(N) RST sink calls, source maps and verification; successful outside elimination never increases occurrences, <=O(N) rounds if combined with other productive reductions. No counting oracle. Charge failed jobs and witness lifts; polynomial peak S and cumulative <=(T+1)S for same serial run. No proof of universal progress.
