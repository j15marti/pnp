## A. Constructive track: canonical Hall core
Let A be normalized NAE owner IDs, X variables, B protected variables. Form bipartite incidence graph from owners to distinct unprotected variables only. For S subset A let d_H(S)=|S|-|neighbors(S)\B|, delta=max_S d_H(S). By Hall or matching minmax, delta is number of unmatched owners in any maximum matching M. Start from all unmatched owners, traverse owner->every unprotected neighbor (matched edges may also be traversed), variable->its matched owner. Let U reached owners, V reached unprotected variables. No reached variable unmatched (augmenting path contradiction), all V matched to reached owners, all reached matched owners matched to V. V=neighbors(U)\B. Hence d_H(U)=delta. Every other maximum-deficiency set S includes all unmatched owners and all its unprotected neighbors are matched to owners within S: |S|-delta matched owners in S is lower bound and equals its neighbor count; equality forces both assertions. Thus alternating reach from every unmatched owner stays in S. U subset S for all maximizers. U is the UNIQUE inclusion-minimal maximizer of d_H, independent of which maximum matching was acquired, with original IDs preserved. This proves exact structural reconvergence across different matching replicas on the SAME incidence and guard. It is not canonical semantic equivalence for distinct residuals.

Every exterior owner in A\U is matched to a distinct representative variable outside V union B. No variable outside V union B occurs in U or the additional guarded factors. Core remains U plus all guard factors on B. This is a deterministic polynomial matching and BFS, not an attained-syndrome count.

## B. Global parity-cycle track: universal extension or rejection
For each exterior signed NAE owner i, choose the representative variable u fixed by the matching, with its unique normalized sign s_u. Add a directed arc u->v for each other variable v in owner i, label w=1 XOR s_u XOR s_v. An arc encodes precisely x_u XOR x_v=w (its two literals differ). Any satisfying assignment allows selection of one such outgoing arc per representative, and conversely this selection plus its satisfied equations satisfies every exterior clause. Nonrepresentatives have no outgoing arcs; in particular V union B are roots.

Necessary: each sink strongly connected component consisting of representatives only must contain a simple directed cycle with XOR of labels zero. A model gives a selected successor for each rep; a closed finite component necessarily contains a cycle of selected arcs, whose equations telescope to XOR zero.
Sufficient: choose one such cycle in each representative-only sink SCC, and use each sink nonrepresentative as a root (possibly prescribed arbitrary value from the protected/core assignment). Every vertex can reach a chosen cycle or root. Reverse BFS chooses one outgoing arc decreasing distance for every representative off chosen cycles. Components of selected arcs are rooted trees or trees into chosen simple cycles. A zero-XOR cycle admits either binary seed; all tree values are fixed by propagating back from their root/cycle. Since no core/protected variable is a representative, this construction extends EVERY assignment on V union B, including any joint guard, without modifying one bit. Nonrepresentative unprotected variables not assigned by core may choose arbitrary seed.
Consequently the exact exterior projection onto V union B is either empty or the entire cube. If any terminal SCC fails cycle test, original formula UNSAT independent of core. Otherwise F SAT iff retained core+guard SAT; witness map is constructive. For signs, replace each label0 arc by a private two-edge directed path, retain label1 arc as single edge (parallel arcs can be handled by private 2/3-edge paths with same parity if necessary). Every simple directed cycle in transformed graph corresponds to a simple directed cycle in original and length parity equals XOR label. Invoke Robertson-Seymour-Thomas (RST) 1999 deterministic polynomial even-directed-circuit decision; recover chosen cycle by greedy edge deletion with <=E oracle calls to this known polynomial algorithm, then edge-minimal feasible graph has exactly the cycle and isolated vertices. No free NP oracle. Mere even closed walk or parity-lift reachability does NOT suffice. Applies to signed NAE of arbitrary explicitly written arities when Hall holds, even with unrestricted variable occurrence, no need for C08-07 sign splitting or degree3. If U empty, this is an outright polynomial terminal subject to the independently retained guard. If |V union B|=O(log N) and guard evaluation polynomial, exact core enumeration is polynomial. More generally it is a strict, sound peel whenever exterior nonempty and feasible.

## C. Adversarial reconstruction: raw four-partition barrier
Raw F, B empty: each variable degree4, owner degree3, so for any variable set X' there are 4|X'| incidences and >=4|X'|/3 adjacent owners. Hall saturates all n variables. Let U,V above. Outside owners and variables have equal cardinality because all exterior owners matched outside, all variables saturated, all V matched inside U. Every outside variable has all four incidences with outside owners (none adjacent U); outside owners have only three incidences each. Thus 4|X\V|<=3|A\U|=3|X\V|, forcing no exterior variables or owners. Raw Hall core is ENTIRE F; delta=m-n=n/3, not the owner-plane deficiency n/3+1. This is a structural barrier, not unrestricted hardness. It holds for all raw genuine sources. Fano 7 clauses/7 vars has full Hall matching and empty core but exterior test rejects, so matching alone insufficient. Odd inequality triangle has even closed walk by repetition but no even simple circuit; signed balanced triangle is SAT. Arbitrary extra guard on an eliminated variable cannot be ignored; it must be protected first or transformed exactly.
