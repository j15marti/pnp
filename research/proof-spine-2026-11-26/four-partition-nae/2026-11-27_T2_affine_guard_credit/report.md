## Exact lemmas

1. For a variable v with all incident factors gathered, let U be their combined scope minus v. The factor R_U(b)=exists v AND(incident factors) replaces exactly those factors. All other factors omit v. Thus the global decision is preserved, and a retained local witnessing bit reconstructs v in reverse elimination order. There is no degree-two occurrence restriction. If |U|<=4, at most 32 full local words need testing, with the cost of reading every incident factor charged. Intersect factors with identical scopes. Use one verified global complement gauge before projecting distinct variables; complement symmetry discharges the root pin. A degree cap is a representation policy, not a completeness theorem.

2. On a fixed chart with explicit Boolean relations of arity <=k (fixed), enumerate local parity masks and collect every affine equation constant on the currently allowed rows, retaining the original guard. Their conjunction A is a necessary condition for any solution. For each local row, test compatibility with A by exact GF(2) elimination with its pins; equivalently use the projection of the affine solution space onto that scope. Remove only rows that are incompatible. All true solutions survive every deletion. Recompute local equations and forced bits after productive changes. For fixed k, table enumeration is polynomial; each productive round deletes an allowed row or fixes a new bit, or records a contradiction. Thus closure terminates in polynomial aggregate cost on fixed scopes. No claim that the affine approximation equals the nonlinear solution set. An empty relation or inconsistent affine equations certifies NO. A nonempty fixed point does not certify YES.

3. A non-resetting ledger across exact representation contractions. Fix constants k,w,D,c, let n be the number of original port coordinates (<=N), and require the live chart coordinates to be identifiable surviving original coordinates, with certified reverse maps. Define M as a uniform polynomial upper bound on the sum of (a) all possible forbidden-row slots over subsets of <=k coordinates, (b) all non-tautological clauses of width <=w, and (c) the dimension of multilinear GF(2) polynomials of degree <=D. For example take M=sum_{i=0}^k 2^i binom(n,i) + sum_{i=0}^w 2^i binom(n,i) + sum_{i=0}^D binom(n,i). Maintain a canonical monotone logical ledger on a fixed guarded chart: a counts distinct forbidden-row slots, distinct acquired clauses and independent polynomial rank, hence 0<=a<=M. Never discard an acquisition for credit purposes on the same chart; redundant constraints can be represented by checked references. If a representation switch does not strictly reduce chart dimension r, it must retain the logical ledger or be charged as nonproductive work. No credit for an unverified or duplicate deduction. With L=1+r(M+1)+M-a, a new certified entry decreases L; an exact elimination reducing r by >=1 decreases L even if the ledger is reset. Let B=1+n(M+1)+M.

For a binary orientation search of maximum depth H=floor(c log2 N), a live branch with remaining split budget h has credit P(h,L)=B(2^{h+1}-2)+L. The sum of credits of both children after a split is at most 2B(2^h-1)=B(2^{h+1}-2), strictly below the parent by at least L>=1. Local productive deductions decrease the credit; a sound terminal removes a positive credit. A sum over every live branch, or fixed/polynomially many explicitly initialized tracks, is bounded initially by poly(N). All branches, failed attempts, projection discovery, map checks, communication, arithmetic bits, shared proof references, storage and reconstruction are charged. If between decreases/terminals the algorithm has a fixed polynomial discovery/checking cap q(N), the aggregate capped execution is polynomial, with peak storage polynomial (explicit bounded chart, certificate and live tree sizes), cumulative storage bounded by total bit time times peak storage. This is an accounting theorem for admitted transitions, NOT a proof of availability of a productive transition or coverage of all NO inputs. A failed cap returns UNKNOWN. It does not license acceptance as YES. Arbitrary new coordinates, degree increases, scope increases, resets or additional branches require a new accounting proof. No global semantic oracle.
