## 2. Exact chart reachability in the ambient cube (PROVED)
For each owner i=(a,b,c) with original ordered IDs, L_i(x)=(x_a+x_b,x_a+x_c) over GF2. For a retained owner set T, an orientation chart at x consists of y with L_T(y)=L_T(x), under any actual retained guard. With no extra guard, y+x lies in span of the indicator vectors of the connected components of the primal graph formed by T (isolated vertices included). This is C08-03 exact equality kernel. For T a complete partition P_j, the components are its disjoint triples; thus a legal move flips any subset of whole triples of that partition. For repeated moves each preserving at least one full partition, the unconstrained reachable words are precisely x+span of all clause indicator vectors: necessity by summing moves, sufficiency by expressing any displacement in the row basis and executing those flips. Gaussian elimination acquires this ambient reachability and an explicit path with at most n single-triple moves, all original IDs. Actual guards and intermediate satisfaction/defect restrictions can reduce reachability, so ambient membership is necessary only for constrained reachability. In affine guard x0+W, use span of (ker L_Pj intersect W) instead; if only endpoints in guard each move then sufficiency for ambient guarded path, not intermediate NAE restrictions. No new guard is imposed on source.

Cyclic family C_p for every prime p>=5: n=3p, variable types A_i,B_i,C_i over Z/p; partition s=0,1,2,3 contains (A_i,B_(i+s),C_(i+2s)). Every variable once per partition. A-B and B-C pairs distinguish s by difference s, A-C by 2s, injective mod p. Thus linear. Any two partitions' union connects all A vertices via alternating A-B-A steps of difference s-t, which generates Z/p. B and C all incident, so every pair union connected. Consequently any chart retaining >=2 partitions consists of only x and its global complement (unguarded). No violation change possible by such moves.

Algebraic independent method: over GF2, row nullspace is precisely the 2D constant-by-type space with A+B+C=0. Let shift T on p coordinates; rows s=0 imply A=B+C, s=1 imply (I+T)(B+(I+T)C)=0, s=2 imply (I+T)^2(B+(I+T)^2 C)=0. Combining gives T(I+T)^3 C=0. Because p odd, vector space splits into constant vectors and coordinate-sum-zero vectors; I+T invertible on the latter since only constants fixed by T. Hence C constant, then B and A constant. The same argument with T-I and T+I in any field of characteristic not dividing p gives constant-by-type kernel for three consecutive shifts: from s=0, A=-B-C; s=1, (T-I)(B+(T+I)C)=0; s=2, (T^2-I)(B+(T^2+I)C)=0. Difference yields T(T-I)^2(T+I)C=0; on nonconstant subspace T+I invertible for odd p in char !=2 (in char2 equal T-I), as T^p=I and -1 not a pth root except char2. Therefore C constant, B and A constant. In GF2 rank n-2, exact ambient chart orbit characterized by parity(A)+parity(B) and parity(A)+parity(C) invariant. This is not a plateau connectivity result.

## 3. Exponential explicit chart-cover obstruction on easy connected sources (PROVED)
For each A word of length p, B all 0 and C all 1 gives actual SAT model. Any chart preserving the complete observations of at least one partition has at most one member of this witness subcube: two distinct such words differ only in some A variables, whereas displacement preserving one full partition must be constant on each triple, which includes B,C with zero displacement. Consequently any explicit cover of ALL actual satisfying assignments by such charts, even adaptively chosen and mixing partitions, needs at least 2^p distinct charts. For charts preserving >=2 partitions this also follows from component connectivity. This is a representation cardinality obstruction even when the exact chart support can be cheaply acquired. It is not a lower bound on SAT decision, on arbitrary compact circuit/DAGs, or on the number of charts needed to find one witness. Indeed the family admits trivial explicit SAT witness and source-only polynomial ternary nullspace witness acquisition. Its actual Boolean affine hull is full: varying each A bit independently while B,C opposite, and cyclically exchanging the roles, gives every standard coordinate difference. Under one fixed complement anchor, complement each flip pair as needed, obtaining full affine hull in that guard. Thus certified unconditional affine closure cannot shrink it, even if complete, and raw full-owner deficiency n/3+1. No implication about all nonlinear closure methods. This directly falsifies a proposed universal polynomial explicit all-model cover by fixed complete-partition charts, not existential classification by other means.
