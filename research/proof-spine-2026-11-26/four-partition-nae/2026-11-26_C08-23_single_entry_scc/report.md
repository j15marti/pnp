A matched owner is satisfied iff at least one arc equation holds. Unmatched free vertices have no owner or outgoing arc. Take SCCs of free graph. Entry set E(S) consists of vertices of S occurring in any unmatched owner or in an owner represented outside S. Proof: if all x,a,b,q equal, first row forces y opposite, second forces z=x, third fails.
