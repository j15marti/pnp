## Theorem: first-new-unit trigger
On a fixed explicit CNF F (including its fixed base guard), let U(a) denote the consistent unit-propagation closure after assuming literal a. Assume root and singleton UP do not contradict; ignore already root-fixed variables and complementary pairs. For a pair a,b, if U(a) union U(b) is consistent, falsifies no existing clause, and no unsatisfied clause has exactly one still-unassigned literal, this union is itself a consistent UP-closed assignment containing a,b. Therefore UP(F,a,b) cannot contradict: by induction every propagation is contained in that union. Equivalently every genuine pair-UP conflict must trigger either an opposite literal, a directly falsified clause, or a new unit. The first two tests alone are incomplete. Example F=(-a OR -b OR c) AND (-a OR -b OR -c): both singleton closures merely set their assumptions, union consistent and not directly falsifying, pair UP contradictory. Opposite-unit test catches this example but is also insufficient for longer implication chains. RANDOM initial chart (all ternary clauses) gives zero productive candidates in both tested direct-union and opposite-unit lanes, yet has 26 pair-UP conflicts.

Exact reverse index: enumerate free signed literals L, compute U(a) for every a, and build bitset F_l of assumption indices b such that -l belongs to U(b). For a fixed a, conflicting union candidates come from OR_{q in U(a)} F_q. Only inspect clauses touched by a falsified literal in U(a), using occurrence index. If a satisfies clause and combined closure consistent, clause cannot trigger. Let R be its residual literal set after deleting falsified literals of U(a). Direct conflict candidates from intersection of F_l for all l in R; possible unit at p in R from intersection over R minus p, excluding indices with p true (complement of F_{-p}). If a newly triggered clause were untouched by U(a), the other singleton closure alone would have had the same conflict or unit, impossible for consistent saturated singleton closures. Thus this screening has no false negatives for pair-UP conflicts, though it may have false positives, each verified by indexed actual pair propagation before export. Pair candidates deduplicated, and only new clauses admitted. Sparse variant enumerates set bits in the combined trigger mask rather than scanning every pair index. Original coordinates and guard unchanged. Do not reuse closure after database update without recomputation or validated incremental maintenance.

Each certified failed guard {a,b} yields clause (-a OR -b) by RUP. Each failed singleton yields unit (-a). Scalar independent checker replays negated clause and extracts original reason clause indices; monotonicity makes a batch of independently checked clauses valid even when verified sequentially. Under matched deterministic candidate order and batch12, skipping only provably non-conflicting pairs preserves the exact output of exhaustive pair probing. Independent exhaustive indexed initial audit: 3120 consistent distinct-variable guards on each RANDOM chart, 508 eligible,26 conflicts, zero false negatives. This census uses the same indexed propagator, not an independent full second implementation; learned proof separately checked by scalar repeated UP. Guarded Fano 84 consistent pairs,42 eligible,0 conflicts; all128 full words independently checked NO. Trigger and even full unary/binary RUP saturation cannot refute Fano from its initial CNF. Prior width4 resolution and other methods can. No universal fixed-width guarantee. At most M productive epochs and one terminal/stall epoch; each rebuild charges all singleton propagation, incidence, bitset operations (at most 2n bits), all eligible pair tests and false negatives audit if performed. Reason DAG per RUP step <=n+1 distinct reason clauses before recursively referencing earlier proof steps. All failed tests and reconstruction charged.
