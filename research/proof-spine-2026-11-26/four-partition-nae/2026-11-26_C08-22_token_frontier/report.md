## 2. Exact parity-demand DAG terminal (PROVED WITH SCOPE)
Let M be ANY verified matching of actual residual owner rows to distinct FREE variables, not necessarily maximum. d_M=m-|M|. Each matched owner with representative v of literal sign s_v is satisfied iff at least one other occurrence w of sign s_w satisfies x_v xor x_w=1 xor s_v xor s_w. Construct directed arc v->w labeled this bit; arcs to fixed roots have known phases. Each unmatched free variable is a root without outgoing arcs. Empty/single-literal NAE immediately NO; eliminate tautological opposite repeats or normalize identical repeats before admission. This report uses distinct-variable 2/3-NAE, so each matched vertex has one or two outgoing alternatives. Pins are not free representatives. Keep actual row provenance.

Assume the directed graph on free vertices (arcs into fixed pins terminal and evaluated immediately) is acyclic, with verified topological order pi. For each unmatched owner select an arbitrary literal as pivot and branch on which of its at most two other literals differs. Choose pivot bit, if not already fixed, in two ways, and impose the resulting two endpoint phases. Thus at most 4^d_M initial seed jobs, each with <=2d_M free demands, less when pins are endpoints. Check all pins and merge or reject duplicate demands. Every satisfying assignment has one of these choices; every accepted seed satisfies its unmatched owners.

For a seed, state is a partial map of pending vertices to required bits, with original IDs. Empty state succeeds. Process pending vertex v of least topological rank; remove it. If v is an unmatched free root, its demand is fulfilled and no successor is required. Otherwise try each actual outgoing parity arc v->w; add demand x_w=x_v xor label, or evaluate fixed w immediately. If w already pending with opposite bit, reject this branch; same bit merges. Recurse; if all choices reject, this state rejects. Every new free demand is later than v. No processed vertex can be reached by any still-pending demand, since future processing ranks strictly increase. Consequently different histories with identical pending maps have exactly the same feasibility continuation. Memoization of full maps (not marginal reachability or hashes alone) is exact. Necessity: a solution of the original formula extending the seed supplies a satisfied arc at every visited matched vertex. Sufficiency: a successful path fixes mutually compatible demanded bits and a satisfied arc for every visited matched vertex; all unvisited free vertices can be assigned in reverse topological order, choosing any arc toward its already assigned successor or fixed pin. Unmatched roots choose arbitrary bits if never demanded. This cannot alter a visited bit or violate any previously satisfied owner. Unmatched owners were satisfied by seed. Thus the procedure is sound on both labels and preserves exact guarded decision and witness lift.

State size never increases: one pending token removed, at most one added. At most sum_{j<=2d_M} binom(n,j)2^j <= (2n+1)^(2d_M) distinct maps. Each state scans at most two arcs; use deterministic sorted exact keys/dictionary or bounded trie with polynomial overhead in original N, and polynomial-depth history for reconstruction. Seed enumeration factor 4^d_M. Time 4^d_M (2n+1)^(2d_M) poly(N) = XP in d_M, not FPT in d_M alone. This proves exact polynomial decision for any fixed d_M on an acquired DAG, not polynomial for d_M=O(log N) using this first bound.

Stronger frontier bound: for cut p of pi define W_p as the union of free unmatched-owner endpoints later than p and all free heads later than p of arcs whose tails are at or before p. Let omega=max_p |W_p| including p=-1. After processing any demanded vertex p, every pending vertex belongs to W_p. A never-processed seed state belongs to W_-1. Each W_p has at most 3^omega maps (absent, bit0, bit1), so number of distinct states <=(n+1)3^omega, even if counted at multiple cuts. Time 4^d_M 3^omega poly(N), with one fixed polynomial exponent independent of d_M,omega. This is FPT in d_M+omega, and polynomial under a fixed logarithmic tariff 2d_M+omega log2(3)<=c log2 N if acquisition, adapters and all other work also have fixed polynomial cost. No free extension/count oracle or semantic equivalence check. Negative DAG certificate: all initial choices are enumerated or rejected by explicit pin conflicts; each reached state has all actual successors rejected, with conflict or terminal status; check transitions in decreasing topo progress. A recorded witness history can be replayed only if needed; check all source constraints. Decision, witness and counting remain distinct.

Further hybrid at the cyclic obstruction: define the active cone C as the free unmatched-owner endpoints and all free vertices reachable along matched arcs. C is closed under free arcs, and all unmatched owners use only C and pins. Outside C every owner has a distinct representative if it is not unmatched (all unmatched retained). Treat C, pins and unmatched roots as roots for outside extension. Every remaining rootless sink SCC must have a balanced simple directed cycle, and this condition is sufficient for extension of EVERY compatible core word, by choosing cycle bits and reverse reachability. This is the C08-21 signed cycle theorem, applicable for arbitrary matching, with deterministic RST even-cycle test after parity subdivision. Rootless infeasible sink gives whole NO; otherwise discard outside owners exactly and run demand DP on acquired acyclic C. No actual RST backend executed in this run; Fano control uses bounded direct enumeration. If acquired feedback vertex set of size f breaks cycles on active cone, dematch its representative rows, retain all those owners as unmatched, and rederive cone or use full DAG. d_M increases by at most f; no guard or clause is deleted. Verify DAG and recompute omega. Finding such set or matching under a uniform budget is a separate acquisition problem. Enumeration of n^f is not polynomial for f=Theta(log N).

Acquisition caveat: a matching induces an acyclic representative digraph on free vertices iff it is uniquely restricted in the bipartite free-incidence graph (no alternate matching on exactly the same matched endpoints). Directed cycle of representatives is exactly an alternating cycle, and the symmetric difference of two perfect matchings on these endpoints decomposes into alternating cycles. Thus optimizing acyclic matching is not a free operation: maximum uniquely restricted matching is NP-complete on general bipartite graphs (Golumbic-Hirst-Lewenstein 2001). No proof of that hardness for this restricted native source, nor claim that optimization is needed to solve it. Try paid greedy candidates, small dematching sets, and cycle terminal; return UNKNOWN on cap. The next missing dependency is a uniform small acquired effective frontier/feedback tariff or complementary terminal for arbitrary NO.

## 3. Separation from ordinary CNF matching deficiency (PROVED)
Write a NAE owner as its positive clause and complemented clause in ordinary CNF. In a normalized residual with distinct scopes and no tautological/duplicate CNF clauses, after substitution of fixed pins, an owner with no fixed pins yields two CNF rows; with pins all of one literal phase yields one; opposite fixed phases satisfy owner and it can be dropped. Let w_i be number of surviving CNF rows, with the same free scope as that owner, for retained owners. Then
d_CNF=max_{S subset owners}(sum_{i in S} w_i - |Gamma_free(S)|).
Reason: Hall maximum deficiency is max of clause-subset size minus variable-neighbor count. For each selected owner, adding all of its surviving CNF copies adds no neighbors, so the maximum is attained by whole copies. If duplicates collapse or scope changes, recompute from actual CNF and do not apply this identity blindly.

Szeider's Theorem 5 (ECCC TR03-002 revision1, 7 Feb 2003) bounds ordinary CNF SAT by O(2^d_CNF n^3) with witness or regular resolution refutation. The hypothesis is not NAE owner deficiency. For raw native unguarded n variables, m=4n/3, maximum matching covers all variable columns by Hall: each variable subset X has 4|X| incidences, each neighboring owner at most3, hence |neighbors|>=4|X|/3. Thus d_free=n/3, d_CNF=2m-n=5n/3 (simple linear native, no duplicates). Stronger constant-d_free / unbounded-d_CNF family in section 4 disproves any functional transfer of the raw parameter alone, without disproving a different NAE FPT algorithm.
