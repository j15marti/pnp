## 4. Native density obstruction, PROVED
For a region with i internal variables, e>0 boundary incidences and m owners, native exact degree4 implies 3m=4i+e. Single-anchor Hall requires m<=i+1, thus i+e<=3. If i>=1 then i<=2 and m<=3, contradicting four distinct owners of an internal variable. Therefore no nontrivial direct native region admitted. Closed native components have m=4i/3>i. In distinct signed 3+/3- logical cap image, 3m=6i+e implies 3i+e<=3, impossible for i>=1 with e>0; closed components again surplus. Cap+anchor portfolio has no universal progress. For t anchors counting allows i+e<=3t native, 3i+e<=3t logical; independent relative anchor phases introduce guards not covered by theorem.
