## 2. A. Forced support propagation and exact lean classification (PROVED)
Let R be any satisfiable complement-invariant subformula of F, and suppose every R-model satisfies x_u xor x_v=t. If a global autarky assigns u but not v, overwrite its assigned coordinates in a model w of R and in its complement. Both overwritten words satisfy R (touched owners already satisfied by assigned opposite pair; untouched owners unchanged). They agree at u but disagree at v, contradicting the forced equation. Reverse roles similarly. Thus every autarky support includes both or neither. Applies to overlapping satisfiable regions with independently verified parity deductions, regardless of SAT status of F. If R itself has no model, direct UNSAT instead. For a rigid cap with exactly two complementary full models, every autarky either assigns all its variables or none; if assigns all, must choose one of those two models.
Suppose a partition of variables into disjoint acquired rigid caps, each with explicit internal owners and exact models w_i and complement (singleton with no internal owner allowed). Replace x_v by z_i xor w_i(v), retain every external owner's original positions and signs. This is a bijection on actual full models; autarky supports correspond exactly by the preceding theorem and assigned opposite-pair semantics. Do NOT simplify a tautological external owner away when reasoning about autarkies: an external clause can still constrain support. Additional guards are outside this unguarded support equivalence.
If every external owner has at most two distinct phase variables, exact maximal autarky and lean kernel are polynomially acquired. Single phase with all signs same: false, forbid its selection; mixed signs: no restriction. Two phases, repeated phase a twice, singleton b: if repeated signs equal, any touched owner requires both phases assigned, with parity z_a xor z_b=1 xor s_a xor s_b. If repeated signs opposite, full clause is tautological but support implication b selected => a selected. Form connected components of the first (parity) edges. Mark a component bad if it has inconsistent parity cycle or a forbidden singleton. Reverse-reachability from bad components along support implications marks all blocked components. Every autarky excludes these components (support closure plus bad cannot be assigned). Conversely choose all unblocked components and any consistent parity value in each. No support implication to blocked remains; this assignment satisfies every touched owner. Therefore unique maximal support is exactly the union of unblocked components; removing touched owners leaves the lean kernel (any additional autarky would compose to a strictly larger original one). Full formula SAT iff there is no bad parity component. A cycle xor1 or false unary is an explicit refutation. Support implications do not assert value equality. This is an exact characterization only of the stated two-phase quotient regime.
Cost: graph parity and reverse reachability linear in acquired quotient; verifying acquisition and lift charged separately. For remaining 3-distinct-phase factors, no universal terminal claimed. A scoped extension: greedily take maximal disjoint triple packing, let B be union of its vertices; B hits every triple, size <=3 times minimum hitting set. Enumerate actual B assignments; each residual becomes 2CNF (pinning one NAE literal gives a binary OR or smaller factor) together with already affine pair constraints. If |B|<=c log N fixed c, 2^|B| paid SCC checks give complete polynomial SAT/UNSAT terminal; for UNSAT retain contradictory paths for every covering guard. This extension is proved as a conditional algorithm but not implemented in the present audits.
