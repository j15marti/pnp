## Named lemma: polynomial orientation-clause acquisition for a paid finite vocabulary
For any explicit CNF F on n<=O(N) variables of polynomial literal length, fix an explicitly enumerated polynomial-cardinality set V of non-tautological clauses of polynomial length. Starting with F, repeatedly scan V and insert any absent C for which unit propagation on current F plus all negated literals of C reaches contradiction (reverse unit propagation, RUP). Stop on empty clause or a complete scan with no insertion. Every addition is entailed: if a satisfying assignment falsified C, it would satisfy the contradictory assumed formula. UP contradiction is monotone under addition of clauses. Hence the fixed point contains every clause derivable by any RUP sequence whose added clauses lie in V. Proof by induction over such a sequence: all earlier additions are present at the fixed point, so its next eligible clause must already be present. This proves exact scoped completeness, not completeness for all UNSAT.

Let M=|V|; potential is number of as-yet absent candidate clauses, initially <=M. Each successful addition decreases it, and a failed full scan terminates UNKNOWN unless an independently certified decision is available. At most M+1 scans, O(M^2) candidate tests, each UP <=n+1 full rounds of scanning original and learned clauses, polynomial in N and M. Verification runs one UP test per logged addition, no semantic oracle. With width<=2, M<=1+2n+4 binom(n,2), so fixed polynomial (e.g. O(N^9 polylog N) deterministic bit time using conservative sequential lookup, O(N^2 log N) live clause storage plus input and reconstruction; CM <= T*S in same serial implementation). Original-source parsing, equality BFS, exact bounded-arity projection and reconstruction cost polynomial by prior proof; fixed finite replicas multiply bound by constant. The experimental caps are much lower and do not establish exhaustive saturation.

New guard-basis extension: given a uniformly acquired set S of s<=c log2 N original residual variables, V can include every clause consisting of an arbitrary noncontradictory guard negation on S and at most one additional literal outside S. M<=3^s(1+2n), length <=s+1, hence fixed polynomial for fixed c. Includes all guard-only clauses. Every UP-conflict orientation tree whose decisions stay in S can be converted into RUP additions in V: for a conflict leaf add the negation of its path; sibling guard clauses resolve to the parent guard clause (assuming its falsity makes opposite pivot units); recurse to empty. The saturation algorithm discovers such a proof without being given the tree. Conditional unit facts are stored with their exact guards. This also admits guarded lemmas reused across orientations, not only tree proofs. The S acquisition must be paid; merely proving depth O(log N) with changing pivots is NOT enough to enumerate all possible guards in polynomial time (general enumeration n^{O(log N)}). No universal guarantee S exists, nor that a fixed-width derivation is sufficient. The polynomial potential bounds exhaustion of this vocabulary, not unresolved-state global progress. No claim of novelty for RUP itself or bounded-width proof search.
