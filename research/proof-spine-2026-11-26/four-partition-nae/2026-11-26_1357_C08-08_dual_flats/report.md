## A. Independent constructive track: rainbow forest rank
Let r(S) be rank over F2 of all pair differences of owners in S. Each component of retained S spans the even-parity space on its vertices (by paths), so r(S)=n-c(S), including isolated vertices. d(S)=|S|-r(S), D=d(E)=n/3+1. Define for temporary removed T, g(T)=D-d(E-T)=|T|-c(E-T)+1. For nonempty T, each component K has b_j=|K|-3 times number of retained owners of color j in K. Hence b_j>=0 and b_j congruent |K| mod3; positive total b_K>=3 by original connectedness, so 3|T|=sum b_K>=3c, g>=1 and D uniquely maximizes d. At g=1 every cap has exactly 3 boundary incidences, all in one color. Contracted incidence quotient connected; each cap incident only to that one color, so all removed owners have same color. In particular singleton cuts have g=1, c=1.
Create 3 labeled possible edges per owner, its three variable pairs; keep owner IDs even if pairs coincide in other settings. A rainbow forest chooses at most one edge per owner and is acyclic. Graphic and partition matroids on 3m elements; Edmonds deterministic matroid intersection gives maximum size R(A) using owners A. Minmax: min over edge subsets B of graphic rank(B)+partition rank(edges(A)-B). Let S be owners with all three edges in B. Graphic rank(B)>=r(S); partition rank complement=|A|-|S|; equality attained by taking all edges of S. Thus R(A)=|A|-max_{S subset A}d(S). This is the Rado rank of independent representatives of owner planes. No SAT oracle, chosen forest is not claimed a satisfying coloring. R(E)=n-1. For T subset E define
rho(T)=|T|-R(E)+R(E-T)=min_{U superset T}g(U).
This computes the monotone closure of the deficiency-gap function by a standard polynomial rank oracle (also a dual Rado-matroid rank). Our implementation uses shortest alternating paths, from unused-owner edge to graphic-addable edge; exchange from outside edge to selected edge on its unique forest path, then selected edge to another edge of its owner. Reuse maximum forest after deleting an owner (drop its edge, at most one augmentation needed). Rank queries are deterministic, independent of truth assignments.

Min over supersets rho is nondecreasing and nonnegative. For minimizers U>=T,V>=W, g(U)+g(V)>=g(U union V)+g(U intersect V)>=rho(T union W)+rho(T intersect W). rho(T+e)<=rho(T)+1 by extending a minimizing U. Hence normalized integer rho is a matroid rank independently of the Rado derivation. Can evaluate rho directly by deterministic submodular minimization (IFF), with g values computable via connected components; no free oracle. This is an alternate proof and algorithm.

g(cl(T))=rho(T)<=g(T).
