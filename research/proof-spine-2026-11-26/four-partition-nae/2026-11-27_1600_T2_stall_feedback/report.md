## PROVED WITH SCOPE: fixed guard-width RUP closure is polynomial
For n variables and original L literal occurrences, fixed k gives M=sum_{i=0}^k 2^i binom(n,i)=O(n^k) possible distinct noncontradictory guards, each producing a unique non-tautological learned clause. Maintain an append-only database, deduplicate normalized clauses, and restart complete guard scan after each successful admission or terminate on a full scan with no admission. At most M+1 scans, each at most M guards. Naive UP costs O((n+1)(L+kM)) literal visits per guard (n+1 full sweeps). Hence O(M^2(n+1)(L+kM)) discovery visits, plus polynomial exact indexing, proof replay, arithmetic, source authentication, guards and storage. For fixed k all are fixed polynomial in original N; reason traces have <=n assignments and a conflict, and M learned clauses. Sequential cumulative memory <= product of polynomial time and peak space. A valid SAT witness may be checked on original input; a root UP contradiction refutes, and all other stalls UNKNOWN. This is a finite-width proof system, not a universal decision algorithm. It is not direct width-k resolution because a RUP check may use arbitrary original or intermediate width. Proof DAG dependencies can be deep even with binary guards.
Falsifier: for fixed k, on k+2 variables take all 2^(k+2) clauses excluding each complete assignment. UNSAT; under any guard of size <=k each unfulfilled clause retains at least 2 free literals. No UP starts or conflicts, so guard closure adds nothing. Other algorithms may easily decide these instances. No unrestricted P!=NP inference. Also native LP/affine/flow saturation can be nonterminal; repeated clauses/renaming can alter heuristic schedule; source-only verified projection does not prove universal small chart width. No need for a final branch tree is not a resource lower bound or universal coverage.
First open arrow: prove every native NO either admits a polynomially acquired chart and bounded-guard closure or some complementary spine uniformly meets a fixed polynomial total deadline, with proof of credit conservation and all branch obligations. Current finite traces do not establish that. Next lemma: find structure-driven RUP guard acquisition avoiding quadratic pair scans and identify a complementary proof lane for width-k stall families.
