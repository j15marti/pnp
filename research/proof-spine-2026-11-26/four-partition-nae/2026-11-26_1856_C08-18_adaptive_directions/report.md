## 5. Further adaptive quadratic directions (PROVED)
Given a KNOWN Boolean quadratic q(z), define alternating bilinear polar form B_q(u,v)=q(u+v)+q(u)+q(v)+q(0). Its restriction to affine hyperplane h*z=e has quadratic part zero iff B_q vanishes on ker(h) x ker(h), independent of e. For rank0 q already affine; for rank>=4, restriction to codim1 cannot have zero rank (adding one row and column raises rank by at most2). For rank2, write B_q=a tensor b + b tensor a with independent rows a,b spanning polar rowspace. Restriction vanishes iff h is a nonzero vector of this two-dimensional rowspace: if h belongs, projection of ker h to (a*z,b*z) has dimension1; otherwise projection surjects to both bits, hence nonzero. Thus any known non-affine quadratic has at most three rank-one guards making it affine; discover by Gaussian rank. For any polynomially acquired list of known quadratic combinations this produces a polynomial guard portfolio, possibly containing directions outside original pair observation list.
