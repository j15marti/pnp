## 2. Exact negative interface circuit (PROVED)
Let F(I,B) be a region of signed NAE triples, with protected B and internal I disjoint; explicit fixed pins substituted or included as unary clauses. All owners contain at most two internal literals (or have been exactly reduced to that form). Any other guard touching I must be a supported conditional 2CNF factor, or explicitly retained/decline; arbitrary interior affine equations are not automatically 2CNF. External boundary guard G(B) remains unchanged. For fixed b, split each NAE row into its two complementary ordinary CNF clauses. A clause is either satisfied by its boundary literals or becomes a clause of at most two interior literals. The conjunction of negations of its boundary literals is the activation predicate. For a binary clause l OR r active under predicate g, insert arcs not-l -> r and not-r -> l with activation g; unary l gives not-l -> l; empty clause contributes g to boundary-only failure E. Multiple edges use OR. Signed/repeated literals preserve identity; tautologies may be discarded. Thus evaluation at b gives exactly the ordinary implication graph of F(I,b).

Track A: construct shared Boolean circuit for R^(0)_uv = [u=v] OR active direct edge, then R^(k)_uv = R^(k-1)_uv OR (R^(k-1)_uk AND R^(k-1)_kv). By induction on allowed intermediate vertices, evaluates to reachability for every single common b. Cycles are allowed, no path enumeration. Return D(B)=E OR OR_x(R_(x,not x) AND R_(not x,x)). Aspvall-Plass-Tarjan 1979 Theorem1 gives D(b)=1 iff no internal extension. Feasible relation is NOT D. Circuit uses O(N+|I|^3) binary gates when input literal occurrences explicit, logarithmic-bit addresses; for supplied exact edge predicate circuits charge their full sizes. This preserves full joint support, not marginal reachability under separate guards. If feasible b needed for reconstruction, evaluate guards, solve ordinary 2SAT, verify and lift prior maps. We do not search SAT instances as research lane, only use completeness to justify sound refutations. No universal circuit-tautology oracle. C07-10's bounded guard expansion is not needed, but its certified 2CNF join can be tried only if the actual circuit admits a paid exact 2CNF summary.

Backdoor extension (symbolic proof only): if some original NAE triples are fully internal, greedily pack pairwise variable-disjoint fully internal triples until maximal. Let p be the attained packing size, U union of these triples, <=3p variables. Every fully internal triple meets U by maximality. Branch on all U phases (actual guards retained); each leaf conditionally 2CNF after fixing U,B. For each b no extension iff EVERY U branch is NO, including contradictory guards. AND all leaf negative circuits; time and size <=2^(3p) poly(N) including paid discovery, no free hitting-set oracle. If p<=c log2 N, fixed c, representation polynomial in N. No claim that such p universally small or that the circuits' joint unsatisfiability is easy; arbitrary interior nonbijunctive guard may invalidate admission. Greedy packing bound not empirically evaluated here.

Track C adversarial CNF proof before affine guard (pins retained): D_L has precisely 2^L inclusion-minimal true words in forward coordinates, picking one of two complete path pairs each stage.
