## B. Protected quotient direct-sum theorem
For ANY partition C=disjoint union C_j of remaining factors let U_j=sum(P_i:i in C_j). Assume the images (U_j+W)/W form a direct sum, including possible zero summands. Define S_j=U_j intersect W. Let R_j be the exact set of linear functionals on S_j attained by local assignments on U_j satisfying every factor in C_j. Store a local extending functional for each attained word. Then the EXACT attained protected response of the original region is:
    {f in W*: for every j, f restricted to S_j belongs to R_j}.
Necessity by restriction. Sufficiency: choose local witnesses g_j agreeing with f on S_j. On W+sum U_j define g(w+sum u_j)=f(w)+sum g_j(u_j). Any zero linear combination has each u_j in W by directness modulo W, hence the value is zero by agreement. Thus g is well defined. Extend through exterior reps by the independently checked Rado certificate and then linearly to L. This simultaneously reconstructs all original tuple IDs, with no marginal witness assumption. Different S_j may overlap arbitrarily; constraints use ONE common functional f, not independent local words.

Acquisition: reduce all plane vectors modulo W, compute an independent basis of their joint quotient span. Express each plane's generators in that single basis. Make a bipartite graph of factor nodes and basis-coordinate nodes; connect a factor to every coordinate appearing in either generator. Connected components partition factors; every component uses disjoint basis coordinates, guaranteeing directness. Independently verify sum of component quotient ranks = total quotient rank. Isolated factors whose quotient image is zero are legitimate. This is sufficient, not a promise of universally small components, nor a claim of canonical minimum decomposition. Obtain S_j by Gaussian intersection. If r_j=dim U_j <= fixed c log2 N, enumerate at most N^c actual basis words per component, retain a single actual lift per projected word. This is a paid exact support table, with no free extension/counting oracle. The total is O(sum_j 2^r_j poly(N)), not 2^dim W. Rank cap failure is UNKNOWN.

For two arbitrary regions G0,G1 use W=span(G0) intersect span(G1). Apply the theorem separately on both sides. Both regional assignments glue iff the conjunction of all their exact S_j tables on the SAME functional on W is satisfiable; pairwise intersection suffices for exactly two spaces. In general >2 regions use joint span directness certificate, not pairwise intersections. Affine tables can be identified by exact support cardinality equal to 2^h after computing their affine hull dimension h. Add their actual equations. Nonaffine response with hull dimension 2 and exactly three attained words is an exact rank-two forbidden-word factor after those equations, following C07-07's argument, and admits another paid Rado core contraction. Other tables remain exact and can be solved by a capped joint span enumeration or declined. These operations preserve genuine compatible lifts even when W has linear rank, with no enumeration of W itself.
