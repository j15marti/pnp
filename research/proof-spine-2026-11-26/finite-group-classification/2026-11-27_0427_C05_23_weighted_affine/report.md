## A. Exact minimum-weight native synchronization
Let A be the original incidence matrix restricted to retained original IDs, target 1, and let F={x in {0,1}^m: Ax=1 mod 2, additional certified affine guards}. Inconsistent affine system is NO. For any x in F, each owner has positive odd integer occupancy. In each color, sum occupancy=|x|, so |x|>=n. Equality implies every owner has exactly one, and conversely every exact cover is a weight-n word. Thus native decision under the guards is EXACTLY min_{x in F}|x|=n; min>n refutes. Unlike C05-22 synchronized chart feasibility, there is only ONE original tuple bit per ID, hence no independent projected witnesses. This standard weight observation alone is not a polynomial algorithm. A positive word of weight n may be checked on all original IDs. Over arbitrary prime p the occupancy lower-bound and weight-n equivalence also hold (1 mod p means positive), but the following cancellation and graph reduction specifically use binary affine forms; no claimed prime-field extension.

Gaussian elimination gives x_i=a_i+f_i y over F2, y a free parameter vector. Group coordinates by identical nonzero linear forms f, counting p_f uncomplemented and q_f complemented occurrences. Zero forms are constants. Within each class the integer weight is q_f+(p_f-q_f) f(y). Equivalently offset min(p_f,q_f) plus positive cost |p_f-q_f| times z_f, where z_f=f(y) if p_f>=q_f and z_f=1+f(y) otherwise. Balanced classes p_f=q_f disappear from the objective completely. Constant bits contribute their exact weight. Let L be sum of constant 1s and class offsets; C=b+D the exact simultaneous affine image of surviving z coordinates. Then
 min |x| = L + min_{z in C} sum w_f z_f.
This follows pointwise, and every image z has a Gaussian lift to y and the same original objective. No independence assumption on the unbalanced classes. L>n by itself is a polynomial UNSAT certificate. If all classes balanced, every affine word has identical native weight even if nullity is linear. Costs are positive integers <=m. Recompute after any new guard; an old cancellation is not a license to omit guards.

## B. Exact weighted syndrome quotient and graphical hybrid
For any parity-check map B with kernel D, impose Bz=Bb. Identical nonzero columns s are grouped. An even group parity has minimum cost zero (empty), an odd group parity has minimum cost the cheapest coordinate in that group, since costs nonnegative. Zero columns may be set to zero. Thus the exact optimum is min cost subset of distinct syndromes s summing to t=Bb, with cost c_s=min corresponding w_f. Choose a basis of these columns by Gaussian elimination and express every column and t in it. For each column connect all basis coordinates in its support. Components separate EXACT syndrome equations and costs: every column lies wholly in one direct-sum coordinate block, and target splits uniquely. On block j with q_j distinct columns and rank r_j, d_j=q_j-r_j. Solving the affine parity equation on class bits and enumerating 2^d_j words gives the exact optimum. Work is sum, not product; all blocks' minima add. The optimum is not a count.

Improved graphical terminal: in the chosen basis classify weight-1/2 columns as edges to a common ground vertex or edges between two coordinates. Higher-weight columns are exceptions. Let h_j=rank of exceptional columns (<=d_j since the r_j basis columns have weight one). Compute exact DP E(s) giving min cost of a subset of exceptional columns with XOR syndrome s: start E(0)=0, update E'(s)=min(E(s),E(s+column)+cost). At most 2^h_j attained keys; each represents actual choices, not nominal masks. For each attained s solve minimum-cost T-join of graphical edges with odd vertices t+s and ground parity chosen to make total even. A disconnected component with odd target parity is impossible. Compute shortest paths and min-weight perfect matching on odd vertices. Proof: any feasible edge subset decomposes into trails pairing odd vertices plus cycles; shortest paths and matching cost <= its cost. Conversely XOR of paths from a perfect pairing has required boundary and costs no more than their sum, since weights are nonnegative. Hence equality of optimum. Minimum over s of E(s)+Tjoin(t+s) is exact. Lift by DP predecessor, graph paths/XOR, cheapest original class coordinate, Gaussian image inversion and native ID check. Positive witness construction follows this proof; this run's implementation computed costs, did not implement full positive lift. Negative certificate can be verified by replaying exact min computation; no independent blossom dual certificates emitted.

Time poly(N) sum_j min(2^d_j,2^h_j) if both parameters acquired and cheaper terminal chosen before enumeration; conservative poly(N) sum_j (2^d_j+2^h_j) if both executed without caps. Capped versions: fixed polynomial bound on total actual DP transitions, subset words, matching calls, graph construction, failed branches, arithmetic, and replay. For fixed c, sum_j 2^h_j<=N^c suffices; likewise d. Graph weights/path sums have O(log N) bits, integer matching deterministic polynomial, Gaussian elimination and original labels polynomial. Stream blocks; memory O(poly(N)*max 2^h_j) for hybrid, cumulative <=time*peak. A cap failure UNKNOWN unless known disjoint block costs plus L already exceed n. This is deterministic, not approximate or counting. No universal bound on h_j or paid basis producing it; general minimum-weight affine solution is NP-hard by the native P3DM adapter above, so unrestricted shortcut is precisely a substantive open problem.
