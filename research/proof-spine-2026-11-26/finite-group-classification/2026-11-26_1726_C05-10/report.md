## Theorem (exact conditional moment admission)
Use native exact-one prefix adapter: s_prev+x=s_next, endpoints 0,1, relation {000,011,101} equals even parity minus 110. For arity <=3 use direct exact table. Substitute guards, solve affine equations and retain each forbidden consistent flat F_i; remove inconsistent flats, reject universal bad event. Parameterize remaining free coordinates by a common GF2 functional x. Additional affine guard H must be consistent or reject directly. All counts below are over uniform x in H; compress to span of bad-event rows when desired after substituting H. This is exact decision support, not counting original matchings after existential projection.

Let J be ANY chordal graph on m bad-event indices, with fixed maximum clique size k. Define integer P_J(x)=sum over nonempty cliques C of J of (-1)^(|C|+1) times indicator(x in intersection_{i in C} F_i). For fixed x retain exactly the active indices. The induced graph is chordal. A perfect elimination order of that induced graph charges each nonempty clique to its first vertex. For a vertex with t later neighbors, those neighbors form a clique, and total signed contribution is (1-1)^t, one iff t=0. Removing a simplicial vertex with later neighbors cannot disconnect its remaining component, since its neighbors are mutually adjacent. Thus the number of vertices with no later neighbor equals the number of nonempty components. Hence P_J is a nonnegative integer, ZERO iff no forbidden event holds. All correlations are on the same x, not independent marginals.

Both moments are exactly computable: each term is an affine intersection, and any intersection (including products of two clique terms) is an affine system. Its conditional probability is zero if inconsistent, otherwise 2^(-rank increase over H). Let a=E[P], v=E[P^2]-a^2. If a<1 then a zero exists. If a=1 and v>0, a zero also exists: otherwise integer P>=1 everywhere, forcing P=1 identically. If v=0 and a>0 then P is a positive constant, so NO. If a=0 then all points are valid. Other cases UNKNOWN. This is deterministic exact admission on both labels, not a random search or a count of the union. It holds after any consistent affine guard and any exact previous pivot whose witness lift is retained.

Constructive lift: choose each free coordinate bit by conditional moments on the two halves. If mean<1 choose a half with mean<1. If mean=1 and variance>0, choose a half with mean<1 if present; otherwise both means equal1 and at least one half has positive variance (law of total variance), so choose that half. Preserve invariant until singleton, where integer P=0. Fixing all free coordinates gives genuine satisfying functional. Gaussian extension, stored native prefix map and previous exact pivot or Rado certificates give actual original tuple IDs; verify integer coverage. NO requires exact zero variance positive mean, not failed cap.

Forest special case k=2: P=number of active forest components, mean=sum_i Pr(F_i)-sum_{ij in J}Pr(F_i intersect F_j). A maximum weight spanning forest minimizes mean over all forests (Hunter 1976 union upper bound). Pair weights are exact rank counts, acquired by Gaussian elimination, not sampling. At mean=1 the variance test is a strict extension of the usual strict union bound. Chordal expansion: start with maximum forest; scan all missing edges once, accept only chordal graph with clique size <= fixed k. Every accepted edge weakly decreases P pointwise (active graph gains edges), so mean cannot increase. No guarantee of universal admission.
