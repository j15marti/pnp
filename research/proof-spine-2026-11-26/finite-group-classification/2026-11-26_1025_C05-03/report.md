## 2. Native geometry and acquisition
V_k={(a,b,c) integer: |a|+|b|+|c|=k}, elementary triangular faces in the eight octants; m=8k^2, |V_k|=4k^2+2. Color (|b|+2|c|) mod3. Every elementary face is rainbow. Cyclic permutation of axes shifts all colors by +/-k mod3, so populations equal when 3 does not divide k. Face centers joined to vertices certify planar incidence. Native triple IDs are never replaced by mere pair marginals. From encoded intact source infer k from m, build primal adjacency, check six degree-four poles, all other degrees six. Six BFS determine opposite poles at distance 2k, others at k. For chosen positive coordinate pole distance to v is k minus signed coordinate: each edge changes coordinate by at most one, and a path transferring units through another axis achieves this length, also for negative target coordinate. Recover coordinates, check exact vertex bijection and exact face-set equality with generated V_k. A signed axis permutation preserves geometry. Keep actual original face->ID map. Degree pattern alone is insufficient. Recognize the intact source before applying literal guards; do not assert that arbitrary incomplete sources can be recognized without original provenance.

## 3. Constructive all-frequency theorem
Let r=|a|+|b|. A latitude ring r>0 has 4r vertices. North port P_r^+={(r,0,k-r),(r-1,1,k-r)}; south port is its full negation. At each pole select the elementary face covering pole and P_1. A north three-ring band at r consumes P_r (already covered), covers all remaining vertices in rings r,r+1,r+2, and covers P_(r+3) on outgoing ring. Normalize its full rows to c=1,2,3 with partial outgoing c=0 on frequency r+3. Translate c by k-r-3 to actual north; negate all coordinates for south. This preserves elementary triangles. For k=1,2 use direct fixed covers. For k=1 mod3, k>=4, use central block full rows 0<=c<=3 minus P^+_(k-3) and P^-_k on equator. For k=2 mod3, k>=5, central rows -1<=c<=4 minus P^+_(k-4), P^-_(k-1). Starting at both poles, step r=1,4,7,... until these stopping radii. All stopping radii equal 1 mod3. Bands, poles and central assigned vertex sets partition V_k exactly: incoming ports excluded, outgoing ports covered once, equator appears once. Both directions of the local feasibility check use actual elementary triangles inside each assigned set. The construction is a source YES terminal, not a claim that all covers respect those fixed ports.

### Exact finite transfer proof
For any of the normalized strips, let w be the number of full latitude rows z, and R=min_z(k-|z|). In each open quadrant (signs sx,sy), central angular columns j=|b|=2,...,R-2 contain one vertex (sx*(k-|z|-j),sy*j,z) per full row. Remaining corner neighborhoods, partial outgoing and removed ports have constant size per corner for w<=6; all ports are outside the middle columns. Elementary triangles wholly within these columns join consecutive columns and adjacent rows. Put u=2^i,v=2^(i+1) for adjacent row bits. If |z_(i+1)|>|z_i| (radius decreases), local triangles have left/right masks (v,u|v),(u|v,u); otherwise (u,u|v),(u|v,v). Enumerate all disjoint subfamilies of at most 2(w-1) triangle choices for a column pair. Record exact covered masks (L,Q), retaining a local witness. Transfer T[p,q]=1 iff some such subfamily has L=full XOR p, Q=q. Here p is occupancy from the preceding column, q occupancy passed forward. Boolean matrix multiplication represents exact existential composition, not a hull or marginal. For the three row lists the following finite equalities were checked by complete enumeration of these local choices and exact Boolean multiplication:
- w=3, z=[1,2,3]: 8 states, 6 nonzero entries, T^3=T^5;
- w=4, z=[0,1,2,3]: 16 states, 13 entries, T^2=T^5;
- w=6, z=[-1,0,1,2,3,4]: 64 states, 54 entries, T^7=T^10.
Multiplication by further powers proves the periodicity at every subsequent exponent, not empirical extrapolation. Each quadrant contains R-3 middle columns, R-4 transitions. Retain finite template triangles outside the middle and crossing into its first/last columns. On increasing R by d, translate left retained triangles in a by sx*d, right in b by sy*d; all near-axis corner triangles move together and remain elementary. Their occupied endpoint masks in the middle are unchanged. Exact equality of the relevant Boolean powers guarantees a sequence of internal transfers with those masks. Backtrack and use the stored elementary triangles for each transition. Four quadrant interiors are disjoint, with retained corners and endpoints already coordinated. Thus all original owners in the enlarged strip are covered once.

Finite template checks: bands r=1,...,8; for r>=7 choose r0=7+((r-7) mod2). R-4>=3. Central k=1 mod3: k=4,7,10; for k>=10 extend base 10 by multiples of3, with R-4>=3. Central k=2 mod3: k=5,8,11,14,17; for k>=17 extend base17, with R-4>=9. Two full sphere certificates k=1,2. All 18 finite certificates generated by exact frontier search (3779 memo states, 628 actual triangles) and verified against actual assigned vertices and face set. ## 4. Exact cyclic frontier and guarded variable-seam join
Complementary method for any fixed-width block with the same geometry: split into four corner bags and angular columns. If R<5 the entire block is bounded-size; otherwise columns b=2,...,R-2 and constant-width corners. Cyclic order: east corner, (+,+) columns ascending, north corner, (-,+) descending, west corner, (-,-) ascending, south corner, (+,-) descending. Every elementary triangle occupies at most two adjacent bags cyclically, checked by code; bags bounded in size for fixed row count, including permitted partial rows. Sort vertices inside each bag. Process earliest uncovered vertex, branch only among actual triangles containing it, whose other vertices are later and unoccupied. Memoize (index, future occupied bitmask) and clear processed bits. At any cut occupied future vertices lie in current/next bag, plus the final bag if a triangle crossed the cyclic wrap. Thus at most O(N*2^C) reachable states for an absolute fixed C. Each branch has bounded degree, exact existence and witness reconstruction by induction on the earliest remaining vertex. Code uses O(N)-bit masks and copied witness tuples; charge polynomial overhead. Removal of already-covered boundary vertices and literal tuple deletion cannot increase frontier width.

Now partition intact source into disjoint unmodified latitude blocks (pole singleton, triples of rings, central 4 or6 rows, mirrored south blocks and pole). Every triangle is internal or crosses one adjacent seam. At pole seams allow all four actual faces. At every other seam allow crossing triangles only within a fixed angular window |b|<=w0 near positive a on north, negative a on south. w0=1 executed. There are O(w0+1) such faces per seam; enumerate all internally disjoint subsets, including empty, storing actual owner-ID occupancies. This is a finite explicit local state set, not enumeration of all global seams. Compile original true pins by deleting all competitors, false pins by deletion; conflicting pins reject. For a true pin excluded by chart, the other incident triangles cannot satisfy it, so chart infeasible, not native NO. For each block and pair of neighboring seam states, check disjoint occupancies, remove covered owners and use the exact cyclic frontier method on allowed internal faces. Store a local witness iff feasible. A path DP joins these binary tables exactly, retaining one predecessor and actual witness per reachable right state. Every chart cover gives seam states and local witnesses; conversely a common seam sequence and the disjoint local witnesses cover all original owners exactly once and satisfy all guards. This proves both directions for the restricted chart, including all correlations, not just separately attainable boundary masks. O(k D^2) local queries where D is an absolute constant for fixed w0, no product across seams. This is exact for every permitted chart guard, and source YES whenever a lift verifies; chart NO is UNKNOWN at source level unless exhaustiveness separately established. Intact source theorem does not claim completeness under arbitrary deletion guards.

## 5. Same-source Hall checks and adversary
On intact geometry each selected YZ pair has two X labels (adjacent triangular faces). Its owner-edge graph H has n edges and n vertices. A connected component with e edges,v vertices admits edge-to-vertex matching of size min(e,v): orient tree away from root for e<v, or a spanning unicycle toward distinct vertices for e>=v. Thus deficiency equals number of tree components, including isolated vertices. Feasibility iff all components unicyclic. In the planar degree2/3 YZ graph, complement of a perfect matching has two paths (four degree1 endpoints) plus cycles. Dual nesting tree of cycle regions with two path marks has component cycle rank deg+p-1; unmarked leaves correspond to tree components. Hence all cycles separate the two paths iff deficiency zero (C05-02 scoped topology). Independently compare Hall matching rank and actual dual topology on acquired projected matchings. On twelve frequencies k<=17 constructed deficiency zero. Ten independently weighted PMs at k>=4 have positive deficiencies 1,4,1,6,6,9,7,15,15,16. One constructed PM at k11 has a complementary cycle, which separates the paths; others in this batch have none. Do not infer Hamiltonicity is necessary.

Adversarial one-deletion guard: forbid the prescribed north pole face. Fixed-port chart rejects, but reflecting a previously constructed cover by (a,b,c)->(-a,-b,c) provides an independent genuine source witness. Variable seam chart repaired this guard at 15 tested frequencies. This falsifies unqualified fixed-port completeness under even a single native deletion. It does not falsify all constant windows or all deciders. Also construct a genuine SAT/NO neighboring pair by keeping only a selected face t and an alternative j at one owner, forcing another witness face q incompatible with j by its independent anchor, then deleting t. Positive original cover retained, negative initially has no empty row but unit closure contradicts. These are closure-easy NOs, no hard UNSAT claim.
