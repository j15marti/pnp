For each of up to six chart copies (I or J, XY/XZ/YZ), project the corresponding region tuples to a labeled bipartite graph on two owner sets. On I the deletion mask is 1+P u; on J it is P u. The exact affine image C=c+D is obtained by applying this one linear-affine map to the same base point and kernel basis, NOT by taking independent affine hulls of each chart. Take disjoint union G of the chart graphs, with no real edges between chart copies. A mask z admits a perfect matching in G minus z iff all the individual charts admit a matching at those SAME correlated masks. ## B. New factorization theorem (Gaussian circuits change matching domain)
For ANY explicit affine-mask matching instance (G,c+D), choose parity-check syndrome columns b_i in an ambient vector space (canonical reduction modulo D suffices). Select an independent basis among the columns. For each nonzero column, express it uniquely in that basis and link coordinate i to the basis-coordinate IDs in its support. Add all real edges of G to this auxiliary undirected graph Q. Zero columns have no syndrome links. Let V_j be connected components of Q. For each j, the span W_j of columns in V_j is spanned by a subset of the chosen basis, and the subsets are disjoint. Therefore sum W_j is direct. Equation Bz=Bc holds iff sum_{i in V_j} b_i z_i = sum_{i in V_j} b_i c_i for EVERY j. Thus C is exactly the Cartesian product of its projected affine charts C_j, even if the input rows look dense. No real edge crosses these blocks. Consequently a joint matching exists iff every (G[V_j],C_j) is feasible. A failed block certifies entire joint relaxation NO. A positive block witness lifts by concatenation, and then solving the original affine image map; no common native tuple-selection witness inferred.

Construct Q by Gaussian elimination and fundamental-support links, polynomial in total explicit bits. Compute each local quotient d_j by C05-21. Deterministic work poly(N) * sum_j 2^d_j (not product), for polynomially many chart copies/cuts, including candidate generation, elimination, graph building, failed matching tests, labels, certificate replay, storage and reconstruction. A fixed max d_j<=c log2 N gives a uniform polynomial bound; or cap aggregate sum at N^c. Space polynomial with streamed patterns; cumulative <= time*peak. In a capped NO search a proved empty local block is sufficient even if other blocks UNKNOWN. This is exact independent decomposition of the relaxation, not universal native acquisition. Guards recomputed in the original affine slice; factorization remains valid under arbitrary certified affine guards, but old components need not remain independent after guard addition.

## C. Duplication barrier and adversarial check
If every observed source coordinate is copied at least three times into the stacked joint mask (possibly complemented), every nonzero image direction has weight >=3. Hence no weight-one/two period generators; d=dim D. In particular the full six charts copy every owner occupancy four times; naive fusion sacrifices ALL pair-generated compression. In contrast for exactly two chart views, a direction supported on two owner coordinates appearing once stays weight two, and a single coordinate appearing twice can also generate a pair. This is why sparse chart selection or syndrome-block factorization is necessary. More chart constraints strengthen the semantic relaxation but may worsen the representation cost. No unrestricted complexity lower bound.
