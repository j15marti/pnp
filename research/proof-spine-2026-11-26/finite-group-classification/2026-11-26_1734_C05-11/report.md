## 1. Bounded-score moment terminal
Reconstruct exact native prefix adapter: s_prev+x=s_next over integers on bits, with endpoints 0,1; three-variable relation {000,011,101} is even parity minus 110. Gaussian and unit-flat normalization preserves all attained assignments and original tuple lifts. In a common normalized free affine chart, let F_i be forbidden events and J any chordal graph on their indices. Alternating clique indicator sum P equals the number of components of the active induced graph by a perfect elimination ordering. P=0 iff all original factors satisfied. From one vertex in each active component obtain an independent set in J, so 0<=P<=M=alpha(J). For a forest, M acquired by repeatedly taking a leaf (or isolate) and removing it and its neighbors: exchange any selected neighbor for the leaf, preserving optimality. General chordal version similarly uses simplicial elimination. Compute mu=E[P] and nu=E[P^2] exactly by Gaussian intersection counts, under a consistent common affine guard.

SAT if nu-(M+1)mu+M>0 (M>0). The polynomial Q(t)=(t-1)(t-M) equals M>0 at zero and <=0 at every positive integer up to M. Positive expectation forces an actual zero. Maintain positive expectation under successive conditional halfspaces (one half must be positive); at singleton it yields an original satisfying evaluation. This strictly extends mean<1 or mean=1 positive variance: nu>=mu and equality mean=1 gives nu>1. UNSAT if for some integer j>=1, E[(P-j)(P-j-1)]=0. This polynomial is nonnegative on every integer and positive at zero; zero expectation forces P in {j,j+1}, hence excludes zero. Extends zero variance to nonconstant adjacent positive support. For M=0 immediate YES. For M>=1 choose j=max(1,floor(mu)) truncated at M; if an adjacent positive support exists this choice detects it.

These two tests are optimal given only two moments and a range 0..M, over arbitrary probability laws on the range: the feasible moment pairs form convex hull of (i,i^2). A zero-free law exists precisely in hull of i=1..M, whose upper edge is nu=(M+1)mu-M (M=1 handled directly) and lower chain is inherited from full hull. Thus positive strict certificate excludes all zero-free laws. Zero mass is impossible precisely on faces of full hull not containing the zero vertex, namely lower-chain edges joining consecutive positive integers (including endpoints). Interior admits a law with positive zero mass by extending a short segment from origin through the pair inside the polygon. This optimality concerns arbitrary laws, not extra structural or dyadic information. No novelty claim for the discrete moment problem itself (Prekopa 1990).

Solve rational LP on p_0,...,p_M >=0 with sum=1 and moment equations. min p_0>0 implies YES; max p_0=0 implies NO. ## 2. Exact rank-cut contextual joins with partial tables
Partition factors into an ordered list of regions, U_j the span of rows used in region j. P_j=sum_{i<=j} U_i, S_j=sum_{i>j} U_i, W_j=P_j intersect S_j. W_0=W_b=0. At each region query one common affine guard giving a functional on W_{j-1} and W_j simultaneously; incompatible guards are NO. Define actual relation R_j of pairs of these restrictions for some functional satisfying all regional events. For a path of actual transitions choose local witnesses x_j. Inductively the prefix functional on P_{j-1} and next local functional on U_j agree on their intersection (contained in W_{j-1}), so glue. Moreover W_j subset W_{j-1}+U_j: write w=p+u, p belongs to P_{j-1} and, because w belongs to S_j and u belongs to U_j, p belongs to S_{j-1}. The gluing thus retains the claimed new boundary functional. Necessity by restriction. Induction yields a global functional on all rows; extend by Gaussian elimination, reverse adapter to original tuple IDs. This preserves all simultaneous cut evaluations, not just decision. Neighboring pairwise intersections alone are unsound: span(a), span(b), span(a+b) have pairwise zero intersection but evaluations 0,0,1 do not jointly extend.

Sharing under identical guard can add YES cells and remove NO cells, never promote unknown. Reachability reclosure after imports. No multiplicative global state product. ## 3. Adversarial fixed-order obstruction
For any fixed h>=2, use independent h bits and one affine parity bit chosen so total Hamming weight of h+1 bits is respectively even or odd. Score is number of true bits, i.e. edgeless forest potential of h+1 affine halfspace events. Under uniform h-bit input both distributions match every moment of orders <=h: any h of the h+1 bits are uniform independent for either parity, and powers expand into monomials using <=h distinct bits. Even case admits zero, odd case does not. Same M=h+1. Thus no fixed-order moment-only classification of arbitrary affine event systems is complete; Gaussian instantly distinguishes these controls. This is NOT an obstruction for already-closed native P3DM, not a native hardness family, and not a general time lower bound. At h=2 explicit sets of three rank-one lines give scores [0,2,2,2] vs [1,1,1,3], equal mu=3/2,nu=3. Verified orders 2..8.
