Define V_k={h: deg(F(x+h)+F(x))<=k}, V_-1={h: difference identically zero}. Begin V_(d-1)=F2^r (constant F handled separately). For h,u in V_k, D_(h+u)F=D_h F(x)+D_u F(x+h); translation preserves homogeneous degree k coefficients of polynomials of degree at most k. Therefore the degree-k coefficient map on V_k is linear, and its exact kernel is V_(k-1). Compute it on a basis, perform Gaussian elimination and keep kernel basis with source-direction witnesses. Repeat k=d-1,...,0. L=V_0, H=V_-1 are respectively the constant and zero derivative subspaces, not all semantic automorphisms of the joint zero set. On L, D_hF is a linear map A h. Choose a checked complementary subspace with basis C, let B be basis of L. Then F(Cs+By)=F(Cs)+A B y. Left-nullspace constraints exactly characterize feasibility in s, and Gaussian elimination reconstructs y; each fibre has 2^dim(H) responses. Fixed-degree explicit substitution and derivative expansion require polynomial bit work (each derivative of a monomial expands into at most 2^d terms; at most r basis probes per level; coefficient vector dimension at most number of output rows times sum binomial(r,j), j<=d; all integer bit masks are length O(r)).
