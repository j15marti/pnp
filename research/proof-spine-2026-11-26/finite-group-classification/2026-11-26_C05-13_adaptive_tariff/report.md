## Theorem A: learned integer tariff
For each matching M of the labeled X,Y projection let d(M)=c(M)-1 in Z^n, where c is the vector of Z multiplicities. Native solution iff d=0. Let R be the set of unoriented rational lines spanned by nonzero attained d, represented by primitive signed integer vectors. For every fixed integer c>=0, there is a uniform deterministic capped algorithm that returns exact native cover count and lifts an original witness (if positive) whenever |R|<=N^c; otherwise it may still finish or return UNKNOWN. Here N is original explicit bit length, not number of free bits. No promised access to R.

Proof of separation: given k already acquired nonzero representatives, group each d by its last nonzero coordinate i. Process i from 1 to n; each vector in bucket i excludes at most one integer value for w_i, since its dot product with w_j, j<i, is known and d_i!=0. Choose smallest allowed integer in [0,|bucket_i|]. Then all k dot products nonzero; sum_i w_i<=k and each w_i<=k. This is a constructive integer hyperplane-avoidance argument, not probabilistic hashing. In F_p for p>max(n,k) the same proof gives a residue avoidance rule, but the integer proof does not require field arithmetic.

Proof of learning: in the slice w dot c(M)=sum w, count matchings using weighted planar signed determinant with original labeled parallel edges. For each z test whether a matching in the slice omits z by deleting all edges bearing that label. If slice empty, native NO. If no omission count positive, every matching in slice is native; its count is exact. Otherwise reconstruct a bad projected matching by conditioning one X row at a time on an original tuple edge while retaining the same price and missing-label guard. Its defect vector is nonzero and orthogonal to current w. Its normalized ray is new because all previous representatives have nonzero dot product. Add it, discard the obsolete price (NOT the original guards), build a new separating price. All native covers always remain. Each failed stage acquires one new attained direction. Cap k at N^c, with one final purity test; exhaustion returns UNKNOWN. Nonzero d has at least one -1, so primitive normalization is harmless. Exact slice purity is sufficient, not necessary for SAT. A native witness may also be accepted immediately when verified, but the implemented lane tests exact count purity rather than this shortcut.

Coefficient extraction: let B=2^b>m^n with b=1+n*ceil(log2(m+1)), m original tuple count. Evaluate determinant of signed projected biadjacency with labeled weights B^w_z, sum parallel terms, take absolute value. All matching terms have consistent sign; base-B digit at sum w counts the slice with no carries. Every weighted degree <=nk. Entries and minors have O(n b k+n log(m+n)) bits; using fraction-free elimination and ordinary integer arithmetic gives a polynomial in N,k. Per iteration at most 1+n+(m+1) determinant calls for purity and reconstruction, O(nk) small-integer work for separator, plus parsing, planarity, signing, guard replay, original witness checks. Initial and subsequent sound local closure and failed lanes must also be charged. At k<=N^c, time and peak storage polynomial; storing learned rays O(nk log n) bits; cumulative storage <= time*peak. No free SAT/counting oracle. Hereditary under deletion guards since retained matchings are a subset, and native covers surviving guard are preserved. No claim for arbitrary nonlocal guards without adapter.

## Theorem B: a native obstruction to ALL polynomial-range additive slices, not only fixed power sums
For every k construct k full 2x2x2 cube triple blocks (8k tuples, 2k owners per color) and connect them in a chain by swapping the X-owner incidences of identical tuple IDs 0 (even boundary) or 7 (odd boundary) in neighboring cubes. Embed each cube as corner-to-face-center incidence on the sphere (six owner face centers); splice edges between components in exposed faces. This preserves planarity inductively. After removing the at most two spliced X incidences within each block, Y/Z incidences still connect all corners and both X nodes have surviving edges; cross-splices connect the blocks. Thus connected. The four choices of uniform complementary pairs (u,7-u), u=0..3, across all blocks are actual full covers even after the swaps: X swaps permute already selected endpoints if the swapped tuple type is selected. Consequently every tuple is supported and avoidable, so even complete sound forced-literal closure cannot eliminate any tuple on these unguarded inputs.

Fix ANY of the three matching projections. In each block use the four tuple IDs with kept-coordinate patterns 01 and 10, each at both omitted-color values. They avoid 0 and 7, so none is spliced. They admit two good pairs (both complementary full triples) and two defective pairs (the omitted label is repeated, respectively the lower and upper label). All four choices satisfy the kept owners exactly, independently across blocks. Every combination of good pairs is a native full cover.

Let arbitrary per-tuple additive tariffs take values in an abelian group G; assume every native cover has common target. This implies the two good pair totals in each block are equal g_j (hold other blocks fixed). The sum of the two bad pair totals equals 2g_j because the same four tuple IDs occur. Thus bad deviations are +a_j and -a_j, even if the tariff is not a function of the omitted owner alone. Independent local choices realize every signed sum of the a_j, with defect equal to the number of nonzero signs. If |{sum_{j in S}a_j:S subset [k]}|=L, there is a fiber of at least 2^k/L subsets. If all pairs in this fiber differ in at most h positions, choosing one center bounds size by sum_{i=0}^h binom(k,i). Whenever 2^k/L exceeds this ball, two subsets differ in more than h positions. Their difference chooses a real projected matching with the EXACT original tariff target and >h omissions. This also holds for adaptive tariffs on a final slice that retains ALL original covers; use product group for the final combined constraints.

For every fixed polynomial bound L<=N^C and natural explicit encoding N=O(k log k), and any fixed alpha<1/2, eventually h=floor(alpha*k) is permitted because the ball is <=2^{H(alpha)k} with H(alpha)<1. In particular a final polynomial-range additive slice retains linearly defective matchings in EVERY projection. Bounded maximum defect cannot be universally acquired solely by such tariffs, even after perfect forced-literal closure. With r integer tariffs having per-tuple magnitudes polynomially bounded, subset-sum range <=(poly(N))^r; constant defect requires r=Omega(k/log N). This includes all prior fixed modular/power-sum filters with polynomial combined attainable range. Does NOT exclude huge groups with sparse attainable ranges beyond the assumed bound, arithmetic circuits that compute coefficients without enumerating ranges, nonadditive predicates, adaptive branching that drops some native covers with charged coverage, or structural retractions. No lower bound for arbitrary deciders. Explicit easy bypass: block-local exact DP. Retain two adjacent block choices (each a subset of its 8 tuple IDs), enforce Y/Z in the block and check the preceding block's X owners once both neighbors are known. All X constraints touch at most three consecutive blocks. O(k*constant) transitions, polynomial integer count bits; guarded witness replay. Native planarity and DP are independent of tariff. This is why the obstruction must NOT be promoted to P!=NP.
