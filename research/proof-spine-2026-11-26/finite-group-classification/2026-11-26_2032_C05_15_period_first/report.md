## Theorem A: Hall-certified periods without response enumeration
Let E be all allowed labeled edges of a bipartite projection. Let L map label vectors to a finite abelian group G (explicit finite-field coordinates suffice). Let S={sum_{e in M} L(e_z): M perfect matching}. For h in G nonzero, define J_h={e in E: some f in E with same endpoints and L(e_z(f))-L(e_z(e))=h}. If E minus J_h has no perfect matching, then S+h=S. Indeed every M contains e in J_h; replacing that single labeled edge by f keeps all endpoints matched and all original deletion guards, producing response s+h. Thus S+h is contained in S; translation is bijective on finite G, so equality. No claim that the numbers of witnesses in translated fibers are identical. A Hall witness A subset left, with |N_{E minus J_h}(A)|<|A|, verifies hitting. A vertex star contained in J_h is a cheap singleton-side version. Test by ordinary maximum matching, extract witness by alternating reachability, verify every alternative edge and Hall deficit on original IDs. Merely finding one parallel pair does not establish hitting.

Binary specialization: initial labels e_z; each accepted h is difference of two class-unit labels, so quotient merges two owner classes. After each verified period, recompute candidate switch sets using quotient labels. If current target class parity is in attained quotient support, reverse the merge: lift an attained matching for the quotient response, and, if its previous response differs from target by h, replace a certified edge. With multiple merges, reverse in order. Thus support-exact quotient, no enumeration, at most n-1 merges. The accumulated kernel H is a true translation subgroup of the original S (induction: if S is H-invariant, a period of S/H lifts to a period of S). Owner-class target bit is size mod2. Reacquire or revalidate after original guard changes. For general F_p the same one-way replacement and finiteness give an additive period, quotient by span(h) without enumerating p; exact count acquisition for large prime quotients remains open. No need for a large-prime oracle.

## Theorem B: exact affine response hull from allowed cycles
Choose one labeled perfect matching M0; contract its matched pairs and direct each nonmatched edge from its left pair to its right pair. Delete cross-SCC edges (cannot occur in any permutation cycle), retaining loops for parallel alternatives. Every directed simple cycle in retained graph can be flipped against M0, giving an actual matching difference; every other matching is a disjoint collection of these cycles. Directed cycles span the binary circulation space: in each SCC choose a positive integral circulation by summing directed cycles through every arc. Any signed integral circulation (in particular oriented undirected fundamental cycle with coefficients +/-1) becomes nonnegative after addition of a sufficiently large multiple of this positive circulation. Both nonnegative circulations decompose into directed cycles by following positive arcs and subtracting a cycle minimum. Subtract and reduce mod2. The GF2 incidence-kernel of original multigraph maps bijectively to contracted cycle space (each M0 edge coordinate uniquely determined by parity of incident nonmatched edges; contraction enforces the two endpoints' parity agreement). Therefore the affine hull of actual parity responses is p0+V, where V is the image under omitted-label XOR of the ordinary undirected cycle space of allowed labeled graph. Compute with a spanning forest: tree vertex potential is XOR of edge labels on root path, each non-tree edge fundamental cycle image is pot(u)+pot(v)+label(e). Parallel edges remain distinct. Gaussian basis acquisition polynomial; no enumeration of directed cycles or actual responses needed. This hull is NOT actual support. Quotient labels can be used directly. If target outside the hull, NO; otherwise choose pivot coordinates injective on V, so equality of these r=rank(V) observations is sufficient for equality of whole attained response. ## Theorem C: transport/compress a negative observation certificate
Suppose S+H=S and F is any explicitly given linear observation map. Let Q have kernel F(H). Then
S intersect F^{-1}(F(t)) is empty iff S intersect (QF)^{-1}(QF(t)) is empty.
Necessity of feasibility in the coarser slice immediate. Conversely F(s)-F(t) in F(H) implies some h in H has F(h)=F(t)-F(s), hence s+h in S and F(s+h)=F(t). In binary case Gaussian produces Q and its independent rows; rank of observation space decreases by dim F(H) when F is surjective onto its stated rank coordinates. This preserves emptiness (not counts) without requiring full support enumeration. In native class-merge quotient, for observation subset J of original owner unit rows, invariant combinations are exactly the whole owner classes contained in J. The same argument works over any finite field with a verified period subgroup. Exact conditional extension: for guard on responses Pp=Pt with Ph=0, if every matching in that guard hits J_h, then its guarded support has period h; can verify hitting by an exact signed determinant zero count in the guard on E minus J_h when its rank has paid log cap. This conditional lemma proved symbolically, not implemented. Do NOT inherit periods across deletion/pin changes or arbitrary observation guards. A quotient row is itself a necessary native observation even without the period proof; periods supply a sound exact search-domain retraction and guide which multi-owner observations to offer.
