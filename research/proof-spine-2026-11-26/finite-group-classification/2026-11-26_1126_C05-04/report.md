## 2. Direct exact global join on two-color regions (PROVED)
Suppose an acquired partition of live source tuples into regions; each owner either private to one region or shared by any number of regions. Each region has ports in at most two colors and <=2 ports of each color; region tables are exact, with actual witness per word under local literal guards. Empty table or isolated mandatory owner rejects. Missing color fixes local tuple count, hence fixed port counts for all colors. Each region has <=2 Boolean choices. Any relation on <=2 Boolean choices is exactly 2CNF (one forbidden-word clause per missing assignment). Write occupancy of each port as constant or literal of these choices. For each shared owner prohibit simultaneous occupancy by any two distinct regions via binary clauses. This alone normally lacks the at-least-one condition when more than two regions meet at a hub. But each region has fixed total port consumption k_j, and native exact feasibility requires sum_j k_j=number of shared owners. Check this equality once; failure means native NO. If equality holds, every 2SAT model has <=1 occupancy at every owner and exactly that many total occupancies, so EVERY shared owner is occupied once. This supplies exact joint compatibility regardless of hub degree, cycles, region count, or width of the interaction graph. Reverse lift from actual local witnesses is disjoint at private owners and saturates shared owners. Forward source cover yields satisfying bits and required budget. No multiplicative global state enumeration. O(N^2) binary owner clauses plus local factors; SCC and original witness verification. Direct solution on ring source compared with generalization below.

## 3. General conservation-priced bijunctive theorem (PROVED)
Remove the above shape restriction on local tables. Let R_j be arbitrary nonempty exact attained boundary relation on actual shared owner incidences in region j, and H_j its bijunctive hull defined by all unary and binary projections, i.e. conjunction of every valid unary/binary clause. No claim H_j=R_j. Let I(o) be regions incident to shared owner o. Introduce a separate occupancy bit for each incidence. Join all H_j; enforce exactly-one when |I(o)|=2 by 2CNF, and at-most-one when |I(o)|>2 via pairwise exclusions. Private owners already handled. For each shared owner acquire rational price w_o, and local constants q_j, with:
  (i) sum_(o in j) w_o u_o = q_j for every u in R_j;
  (ii) sum_(o in j) w_o v_o >= q_j+1 for every v in H_j minus R_j;
  (iii) w_o>=1 for each |I(o)|>2 (prices of exactly-one owners unrestricted);
  (iv) sum_j q_j = sum_o w_o.
This is an explicit rational LP after enumeration of actual table and hull. The exact decision proof is a conservation argument. In any relaxed 2SAT model, sum_j w dot v_j >= sum q_j, with strict excess if any fake local word is used. On the other hand the same sum equals sum_o w_o times its occupancy, <=sum_o w_o: owners of degree2 exactly occupied, all other owners at most once and strictly positively priced. Equality (iv) forces equality on both sides. Thus NO fake word and NO missing high-degree owner can occur. All relaxed models lift exactly, not merely existence of a favorable 2SAT model. Forward direction immediate. If the hull formula itself is UNSAT it also proves original NO without price; implementation conservatively returns UNKNOWN before SCC on unpriced cases. LP infeasibility does NOT prove source NO.
The theorem includes Section2 with all w_o=1 and q_j=k_j if the budget holds. It also handles nonbijunctive local supports under exact price certificates, including some C01-04 four-port (2,1,1) L/U tables. With bits (a0,a1,b,c), L={0,13,14} has one extra hull word 12, U={1,2,15} one extra word 3. For a complete chart with all shared owners degree2 and equal global color populations, balance (iv) automatic; if a common set of lambda signs gives every full L/U factor strictly positive excess, scaling ensures unit margin.
