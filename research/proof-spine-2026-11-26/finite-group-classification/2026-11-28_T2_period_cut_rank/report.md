## Exact theorem
For nonempty finite dictionaries S_i in F2^r and local periods H_i={h:S_i+h=S_i}, let H=sum H_i. The quotient of sum membership by H is exact. Lift any quotient solution by selecting dictionary preimages, then express target discrepancy in the tagged generator basis, assigning each term to its owning block. All corrected choices remain in their dictionaries. For explicit S_i of m_i options, test at most m_i candidates (anchor xor each option) against all m_i options; sort membership checks give polynomial bit work in explicit representation. Verify generator closure and retain tagged Gaussian elimination. No enumeration of the global group. This is an extension/analysis of existing period quotient methods, not a novelty claim for period quotients.
For a cut P|Q use centered U_P=sum span(S_i+a_i), U_Q similarly. Write H_P=sum of periods on P and H_Q on Q. H=H_P+H_Q, H_P subset U_P, H_Q subset U_Q. Thus pi(U_P) intersect pi(U_Q)=pi(U_P intersect U_Q): if p+q=h_P+h_Q, then p+h_P=q+h_Q. Exact rank loss lambda-lambda'=dim(H intersect U_P intersect U_Q). Hence no unchanged cut grows; only shared period directions reduce interface rank. This does not assert that unrelated charts, reordered cuts, or arbitrary substitutions have monotone cut rank.
