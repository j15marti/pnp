Source: explicit P3DM on equal disjoint owner classes, distinct original-ID tuples, planar incidence; original-ID deletion guards. Remove one owner color Z and suppress tuple nodes to obtain a planar bipartite multigraph. Parallel labeled edges retained, or aggregated by sum of weights. Every projected perfect matching uses n actual tuples; D=#missing Z owners; exact cover iff D=0.

Theorem 1: assign nonnegative polynomial-range integer prices w_z. All native covers have weight K=sum_z w_z. A weighted Kasteleyn determinant counts labeled projected matchings in this exact slice: evaluate at integer B>m^n with F(B)=sum_M B^(sum w_z(e)); all coefficients are base-B digits without carry (absolute signed determinant with consistent planar signs). Extract K digit. Matrix/determinant integers have polynomial bits for polynomial degree. Let Z0=count in slice, A=sum_z count with z omitted, C=A+2 sum_{z<z'} count with both omitted. If Z0=0 source NO. Otherwise A/Z0 and C/Z0 are exact first/second moments of D. For proved upper bound M on D, C-(M+1)A+M Z0>0 implies YES, and C-(2j+1)A+j(j+1)Z0=0 for integer j>=1 implies NO. Condition on original labeled edge choices to preserve positive quadratic sum and reconstruct a cover. Otherwise UNKNOWN. The prior guard remains present in every query. No general exact count oracle.

Theorem 2 (bounded-defect exact filter): suppose ALL matchings in an exact-cover-preserving slice have D<=d, fixed. Verify by zero count under omission of every d+1 subset, at most binom(n,d+1) queries. Choose prime p>max(n,d), label owners 1..n. Missing and extra owner multisets have equal size D<=d. Force equality to cover targets for first d power sums modulo p, using exact integer sums of residues z^j mod p encoded simultaneously in radix R=n(p-1)+1, previous price in higher digits. Newton identities in F_p imply identical missing and extra multisets (all 1..d invertible), impossible unless empty since their supports disjoint. Consequently one weighted determinant digit gives the exact original cover count. Recover original witness by edge conditioning with positive count. Fixed d and prior polynomial-range tariff: O(N^{O(d)}) bit work; charge all queries and big integer arithmetic. This is stronger than existence of ONE near-cover; all projected matchings in admitted slice satisfy the bound. No universal acquisition.

Adversarial: Prouhet partition of 0..2^(d+1)-1 by binary digit parity has equal power sums through d (product(1-t^(2^j)) has root multiplicity d+1 at 1). Pair the two sets as Z labels of full native cubes; connect cubes by planar X-edge splices exchanging equally selected or equally unselected tuple incidences. Replicated complement pairs give actual exact covers supporting and avoiding every tuple. Select local IDs 0,6 in every cube: exact X,Y matching, doubles one label set and omits the other. Therefore fixed raw power-sum tests alone fail on connected planar native family. Also defeats modular congruences, not necessarily exact sums of residues or adaptive graph-dependent prices. No P!=NP transfer.
