## Scoped theorem
For nonempty exact response R subset F2^b define D={u+v:u,v in R}. Projection to F2^b/K injective on R iff K intersect D={0}. Proof: collisions are precisely nonzero differences in K. Translation stabilizer H={h:R+h=R} is different from collision-free K, and a nonzero H direction cannot be in K. Exact section maps each admitted quotient word back to its unique boundary word and a checked interior witness. For complete incident bucket plus optional boundary-only factors, replace region by quotient domain and substitute section into every exterior consumer; this is equisatisfiable with lift by stored maps. No free dropping of external orientation. Exact root variable elimination gives a local nonrenewable credit decrease if representation and reconstruction remain inside the global cap. Walsh autocorrelation of indicator 1_R equals inverse transform of squared Walsh coefficients; correlation count=0 iff direction missing from D, count=|R| iff in H. Explicit table cost O(b 2^b) integer additions plus squaring O(b)-bit values; only polynomial in original N for appropriately bounded acquired table. Greedy K extension checks (h+K) intersect D empty; no maximum-dimension claim.

Stronger sparse algorithm: enumerate distinct nonzero differences M<=|R|(|R|-1)/2. Choose a parity row by method of conditional expectations, fixing its b coordinates in order. For every unseparated nonzero difference, a uniform random row has dot product zero with probability 1/2. After partial row assignment the probability is 0, 1/2 or 1 depending on whether any unassigned difference coordinate remains. Use exact doubled expectations; choose lower score, ties 0. At most floor(M/2) differences survive each row; repeat until none. Hence <=ceil(log2(M+1)) rows, <=b by independence (each new row separates a difference annihilated by all earlier rows). Cost O(b M log(M+1)) elementary tests conservatively, plus O(|R|^2 b) bit construction and polynomial bit overhead; no ambient 2^b table. For acquired polynomial-size explicit support this gives O(log N) quotient coordinates, not a claim that arbitrary exact boundary support can be acquired or all consumers recomposed cheaply. Sparse controls b=32,128,512,2048 and m=4,16,64 had verified injectivity; 2048/m64 required 9 rows, 15,953,920 scoring tests, outside optional 10^7 diagnostic cap (secondary sparse synthetic test, not a native NO benchmark). No universal contraction availability.
