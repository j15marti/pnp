## Guarded nonlinear swap theorem
Let a checked guard-fixed restricted clause set be preserved by a signed transposition of two unfixed variables. The involution applies only inside the guard; elsewhere identity. Unit-propagated facts outside the moved coordinates remain fixed. Verify the restricted clause-set permutation, not semantic equivalence. Unsigned swap under one variable g gives a'=a+g(a+b), b'=b+g(a+b), generally quadratic. For p=A+aB+bC+abD with coefficients independent of a,b, exact conditional swap pullback is p+g(a+b)(B+C), degree<=deg(p)+1; exact lex section mapping of the pair uses e=g*a*(1+b), a'=a+e,b'=b+e, giving p+e(B+C), degree<=deg(p)+2. These are syntactic certificates; keep degree increases charged. General guard degree k increases respective caps by k or k+1. For multiple verified lex breakers, common global lex minimum of feasible set survives all, but breakers are not logical consequences and must not be imported into an unbroken chart as vanishing identities. No promise of finding lex minimum cheaply or discovering arbitrary semantic automorphisms. For UNSAT semantic stabilizers are trivially all transformations of the empty solution set; this experiment only tests structural automorphisms.
