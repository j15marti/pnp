## A. Exact factorization and parity support learning
Given an ordinary perfect matching M0 of the labeled X,Y projection (if none, native NO), contract each matched X,Y pair, direct every available projected edge from its X pair to its Y pair. Relative to M0, every perfect matching is a disjoint permutation of pairs, so each used edge is inside one SCC; conversely any interpair edge on a directed cycle is attainable by flipping that cycle. Parallel labeled edges inside a pair are individually attainable. Hence discard all cross-SCC edges, and every projected matching factors exactly as an independent product of local perfect matchings of balanced SCCs. Original tuple IDs retained. Removing Z from planar incidence and suppressing degree-two tuple nodes preserves planarity. This decomposition is polynomial and needs no supplied chart.

For a local matching of r edges, define p(M) in F_2^n as XOR of the unit vectors of its Z labels. In the global product every matching selects exactly n tuples, so XOR of all local words equals 1^n iff every Z multiplicity is odd, hence >=1, hence exactly 1 by total n. Locally popcount(p)<=r, with equality iff no label is repeated. Thus exact parity support is enough, even without full multiplicity vectors; filter by popcount r for squarefree local tables. Every witness of a retained word is squarefree. This is exact under original deletion guards, not an affine relaxation. For NO, no witness is needed from a failing global join, but local table entries are backed by original matching IDs.

Theorem A (fixed polynomial capped exact parity learning): for each local component and arbitrary explicitly given linear map L:F_2^n -> F_2^b, the attained Lp table, its exact matching counts and local lifts can be learned in deterministic poly(N,K) bit work whenever there are <=K distinct attained image words. K may be a fixed polynomial in N; larger supports return UNKNOWN upon cap exhaustion. No free count oracle. Proof:
1. A planar Kasteleyn signed biadjacency determinant has a single base coefficient sign s0 for all labeled perfect matching monomials. Use simple planar projection signing (parallel terms with same endpoints have the same base sign and add), then attach edge character (-1)^(a dot L e_z). Keep the fixed sign s0, NEVER absolute value of a signed character sum. The determinant times s0 is sum_M (-1)^(a dot L p(M)). This remains true under any deletion of original IDs. Recover count of each q-bit linear hash H L p via 2^q determinants and inverse integer Walsh-Hadamard transform (divide by 2^q). Counts are nonnegative integers.
2. Given k known distinct image words, let D be their pairwise differences, with multiplicity. Choose a linear form separating >=half of unresolved pairs: group each difference by its highest nonzero bit, set bits of the form from low to high, choose the bit that makes at least half of that bucket's final parities 1. Later bits cannot affect settled buckets. Repeat on remaining differences. At most ceil(log2(binomial(k,2)+1)) rows suffice. Thus hash has <=O(k^2+1) buckets, deterministic polynomial acquisition, no random perfect hash assumption.
3. Compute complete hash distribution. Any nonzero bucket not represented by a known word yields a new word by self-reduction on original matching edges. Otherwise every occupied bucket has exactly one known representative. For each of b coordinates, append that coordinate as a hash row and compute distribution. If any bucket has positive count at the opposite bit from its representative, acquire a new word in that joint bucket by conditioning original edges row by row while retaining the same hash/coordinate guards. At most m candidate edge queries suffice. If all tests pass, every actual word agrees in every coordinate with its bucket representative; table complete and bucket totals are exact counts. Every failure learns a genuinely new attained word. At k=K do final completeness check; if new word still required, UNKNOWN.
4. The coefficient sign and row conditioning use original labels. Arbitrary L only changes the sign attached to each edge. Matrix entries at most m in absolute value, determinants and fraction-free minors O(N log N) bits by Hadamard (intermediate arithmetic polynomial), transforms add O(log K) bits. Per stage O((b+m+1)(k^2+1)) determinant evaluations plus explicit hash construction, table/guard replay, integer transforms, signing, preprocessing and verification. Summed through K stages and <=N factors, polynomial for fixed original-input cap K=N^c. Store local table and one original-ID lift each, charge peak and cumulative space (<=time*peak). All failures and other lanes count. No claim that local support is universally bounded, nor exact native counting from a support-only quotient.

Independent local method: learn full multiplicities by integer tariff separating known vectors (last-nonzero difference bucket avoidance, sum w<=binomial(k,2)), then univariate/bivariate signed determinant coefficient extraction, checking every hash bucket and each coordinate. This was implemented independently of Walsh acquisition. It also learns a new vector per incomplete stage and is polynomial in N,K with larger integers. Walsh can be strictly smaller in representation, and has only +/-1 per-tuple weights rather than packed exponent weights.

## B. Exact joint period quotient and matroid terminal
Let S_i be complete squarefree parity tables (or raw parity tables for the XOR-only formulation); empty table gives immediate NO. H_i={h:S_i+h=S_i}, a subgroup of F_2^n, computable by checking each candidate h=s+s0 (<=|S_i|) against all S_i. Let H=sum H_i. Then the sumset S=sum S_i is invariant under every element of H: decompose h=sum h_i and translate each local choice. For quotient pi by H,
1^n in S iff pi(1^n) in sum_i pi(S_i).
Necessity immediate; sufficiency because quotient equality gives an attained sum differing from target by h in H, and all translations in H preserve S. Gaussian elimination builds quotient and can decompose h into local periods, so exact lifts are possible. This is a support equality, not equality of counts. Explicit DP of quotient sumsets under fixed poly(N) cap; if cap exceeded UNKNOWN. It is sufficient if intermediate attained quotient sets remain polynomial. Can further split independent quotient coordinates using actual support/dependency structure, but no general universal factorization theorem asserted.

Feedback extension (proved symbolically, not implemented): if an accumulated exact response sum A is H-invariant, A+B=pi_H^{-1}(pi_H(A)+pi_H(B)). Learn the next matching factor's quotient image directly by Theorem A, without learning raw parity or multiplicity vectors. Use raw parity for this query; do not infer squarefreeness of an individual response from a quotient word. Global XOR correctness still enforces all owners exactly once. Once an actual new sumset quotient is acquired, extract its additional translation periods, lift them to the accumulated invariant subgroup, and continue. Sound exact NO under a fixed aggregate cap. This permits already-certified affine regions to reduce the cost of acquiring later factors. Finding a favorable order/period group in arbitrary prime residuals remains open.

Matroid terminal: use the squarefree tables as rank-r_i bases on copied ground elements (i,z). Recognize matroid basis families by the finite basis-exchange axiom over the complete explicit table; independence oracle is subset containment in an actual listed basis. Direct sum of recognized local matroids and partition matroid enforcing at most one copy of each Z owner. By ordinary two-matroid intersection, size n common independent set iff native cover, since sum r_i=n. Edmonds rank cut r_1(A)+r_2(E\\A)<n is exact NO certificate, with rank computed by max overlap with listed bases per factor and distinct owner count. For a partition-matroid local table, find exchange graph components, check constant per-component rank and exact cardinality product of binomial choices. Then simple maxflow suffices; group-to-owner edges and demand capacities; deficit cut certifies sum group demands > number of reachable owner labels. This special terminal implemented; general matroid intersection proof only, not implementation. Do not assume arbitrary local tables are matroids; two native countercontrols below. No approximate count or triple-matroid oracle used.

## D. Falsifier for universal small local tables in all three projections; balanced seam bypass
Mixed chain: 3k cubes attached to the same core. Boundary j uses color floor(j/k) in {X,Y,Z}, j=0..3k-1; type 000 for even j,111 for odd j, core uses ID0. Same planarity, cut balance NO, pair closure and GF2 arguments apply for each color, as above. In any two-color projection, each kept-color boundary joins the allowed graph via cross edges; original block K2,2 with parallel edges remains internally connected after deleting up to two spliced edges (their parallel alternatives remain). Boundaries in omitted color do not connect projected blocks. At least k consecutive cubes lie in one allowed SCC in EACH projection. Fix core matching avoiding tuple0, and independently select good/bad pairs avoiding 000/111 in each of these cubes. All are actual projected matchings, and choices produce distinct parity words on the omitted labels. Thus some local component in every projection has >=2^k attained parity vectors, while N=O(k log k). Universal bounded local parity table conjecture for any of three projections is FALSE. This does not rule out quotient-feedback, implicit representations, or an unrestricted decider.

Balanced exchange seam theorem: partition original incidence into connected balanced pieces (each piece has equal numbers of its three owner colors). Suppose the piece adjacency graph is a tree, each seam consists of two crossing incidences of the same color in opposite tuple-to-owner directions, and reversing these swaps gives valid local source pieces with original IDs. Each seam separates balanced sides. Native exact cover implies equal bits on its two incident tuples by the preceding cut balance. Conversely local exact covers of reversed pieces with those equalities give a native cover upon restoring all swaps. All original tuple guards lift exactly. Verify rewiring, distinct tuples, planarity and balance of every piece. Acquire a valid such tree by greedily scanning original two-edge cuts in each current piece, require both new pieces balanced, crossing orientations correct, and keep endpoints of every earlier seam on the same child side. Each split raises piece count, <=N; previous adjacency tree remains a tree. At most O(N) stages, O(N^2) pair tests per stage with polynomial connectivity checks, no assumption that the greedy tree is optimal. If source admits no such split, retain prime piece. Actual implementation scans bridges after removing a first edge. 
Exact negative join under local solver cap: root piece tree. Each child subtree transmits only subset of {0,1} feasible for its seam tuple. Intersect each received subset with the corresponding parent tuple's unary pin set, including all other original guards. For each of two parent-seam bits, solve the local reversed source with these unary guards, applying native closure after each import. Root one call. By induction, all children are independent once seam bits fixed; local arbitrary correlations handled by the local solver. At most 2*pieces-1 guarded decisions; if any unknown needed, propagate UNKNOWN, not NO. For NO it is enough that an unguarded piece is already NO. Exact local cap can be Fourier-period, matroid, or bounded native exhaustive search when leaf size logarithmic; charge all work, cuts, failed attempts, reconstruction. No universal cut existence. In the mixed chain greedy acquisition actually found all planted seams and isolated the eight-triple refutation.
