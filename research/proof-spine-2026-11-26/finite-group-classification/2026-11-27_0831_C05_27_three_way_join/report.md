## Exact three-way join theorem
For every explicit owner-choice chart as above and every binary tree of disjoint leaf groups, let a_I be the sum of actual chosen anchors and U_I the span of all row differences in I over a supplied prime field. For each region I retain T_I, precisely the actual PACKING occupancy rows u with t-u in a_O+U_O, O=complement(I). Empty table proves native NO because any full cover would induce a retained row. A nonempty interior table is not proof of a cover.

For children L,R of I, write outside O. Compute T_I by joining rows u in T_L and v in T_R with (i) u and v physically disjoint and (ii) t-u-v in a_O+U_O. Output u+v, an actual binary union. Exactness: every packing in I decomposes into disjoint child packings. The outside affine membership for the parent implies each child's outside membership because the sibling's actual row belongs to its affine hull. Conversely any emitted pair is a real compatible packing on disjoint factors satisfying parent membership. At the root the outside is empty, so retained target iff native cover. One representative per occupancy is enough for decision/reconstruction because all future constraints depend only on the occupancy and factor partition; absence of a representative ID does NOT certify deletion. Retain original IDs in witness or use reverse replay. Rebuild under guard changes; no claim of arbitrary provenance queries or counting.

Define r=dim(U_L+U_R+U_O), w=dim(U_L+U_R)+dim(U_O)-r and j=dim(U_L)+dim(U_R)+dim(U_O)-r. All can be acquired by polynomial Gaussian elimination on original row differences without enumerating partial assignments. The affine pair space of (u,v) in (a_L+U_L) x (a_R+U_R) satisfying t-u-v in a_O+U_O is empty or dimension j: linear map (u,v) -> (u+v) mod U_O on U_L x U_R has rank r-dim(U_O). Also j=w+dim(U_L intersection U_R) by dimension identity. It is not generally bounded by w alone. Each child lies in its own cut fiber automatically for such a pair. Since a w-dimensional affine subspace of F_p^d has a projection onto w independent coordinate positions that is injective, it contains at most 2^w binary points. Apply the same argument to the pair space in F_p^(2d). Thus |T_I|<=2^w and number J of distinct retained child row pairs passing (ii), even BEFORE disjointness, <=2^j, independent of p. The general modular-only method instead gives p^j (2^j when p=2). No claim of binary occupancy in a general quotient coordinate system; apply the bound in physical coordinates only.

Key acquisition: let q be a linear quotient map with kernel U_O, and goal=q(t-a_O). Bucket child rows by q(u) and goal-q(v); count exact candidate pairs J=sum_k |L_k||R_k| before expansion. This is not a count of covers. Enumerate only matching buckets, reject physical overlaps, deduplicate. Cost per node poly(N,log p)*(|T_L|+|T_R|+J), including deterministic sorting, membership, original row reconstruction and keys, with polynomial-bit counts and witnesses. Total bit work poly(N,log p)*(1+sum_nodes 2^j_v) for given tree, plus paid acquisition. Space at most poly(N,log p) times total table rows and keys on live recursion, or store all tables under same aggregate bound. In particular a supplied tree of logarithmic j and prime bit length polynomial in original N gives a deterministic polynomial terminal. No enumeration of p-sized alphabet. If only max edge cut width w_max known, j<=w_L+w_R<=2w_max (pair space projects injectively to two child cut fibers). This refines naive Cartesian pair upper bound but does not prove universal low-width acquisition.
