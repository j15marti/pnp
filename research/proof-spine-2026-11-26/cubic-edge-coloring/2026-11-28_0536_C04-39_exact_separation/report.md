## New scoped lemma: local moment separation
For a regional support R on V and a known family of moments T subset V, define vector a(z) of monomial evaluations including the constant 1. A proposed moment packet m extends to a nonnegative normalized regional distribution iff m lies in conv{a(z):z in R}. If not, an inequality p=a·c exists with p(z)>=0 for every z in R and c·m<0, by finite-dimensional separation. The constant coordinate absorbs the threshold. Normalize max |c_i|<=1. Rational LP maximizes separation (equivalently minimizes c·m). With explicit support, rational m, and bit-polynomial LP, acquisition/verification polynomial in explicit representation; with w<=O(log N) a single region's rows are polynomial, not the number of all width-w regions. Every accepted separating region removes that exact old packet, preserves every true witness; neither strict dimension loss nor universal contradiction follows. For a fixed width, candidate universe polynomial and never readmit the same scope, but it can saturate feasible. No credit reset across representations proved.
