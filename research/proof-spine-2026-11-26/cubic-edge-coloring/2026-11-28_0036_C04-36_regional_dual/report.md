## 1. Theorem and hypotheses
Given finite Boolean constraints and any polynomially acquired regional cover, let R_U be the exact joint support of a specified subset of original factors on U (or a certified outer support). Introduce p_U(a)>=0 for a in R_U, sum_a p_U(a)=1. For each shared subset T and word b, equate the marginal of every region containing T with the first such region, using exact common original coordinates. A global solution gives delta measures, so LP infeasibility soundly refutes the original constraints, even if regional owners overlap. No claim that feasibility implies global feasibility for a cyclic cover. In particular, no private interior variable is required for an informative regional LP. With fixed width w and polynomial number of acquired regions, total columns <= L*2^w, equations <= L*3^w, and integer coefficient bits polynomial (all entries 0, +/-1); a uniform rational LP algorithm can decide feasibility in polynomial bit time in the explicit matrix. Also polynomial for w=O(log N) when region count, acquisition and row maps are polynomial. No claim that enumerating all log-width scopes is polynomial. Practical HiGHS solve below is not a proof of that runtime bound.

By Farkas, infeasibility of Ap=b, p>=0 yields rational y with A^T y>=0 and b^T y<0. Scale to integers. Equivalently assign each region a constant c_U and each shared subset/word a signed transfer between two regions, added at one endpoint and subtracted at the other. For each allowed row a in every used region require c_U + sum incident signed transfers evaluated at a >=0. The total over a hypothetical global witness is sum c_U<0, because all transfers cancel. Contradiction. This is a local integer checker, not a floating-point certificate. Overlap is harmless for this NO implication; do not add overlapping original energy counts as if disjoint. Exact full support is sufficient but a certified upper support also works. If regional cover has a checked running-intersection tree and consistent normalized marginals, a global witness can be constructed by choosing a positive root row, then positive child rows with the parent overlap word (positive marginal ensures existence). This terminal needs the actual regional acquisition and lift maps; no universal decomposition promise. Falsifiers: incorrect support, inconsistent original-ID mapping, negative local row score, nonnegative total constant, or feasible LP on NO showing incompleteness. First four verified on these two proofs (corrupted sign rejected); fifth remains a general limitation. DISJ: no uncharged branching; no DTISP, configuration, relativization, algebrization or Natural Proofs separation inference.
