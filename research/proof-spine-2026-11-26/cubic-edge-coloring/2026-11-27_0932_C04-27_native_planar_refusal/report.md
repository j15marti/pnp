Signed exact balance matching theorem: after exact parity normalization and optional certified constant substitution, let each row require exactly k_i true signed occurrences (including repeated root occurrences), with every unresolved root occurring at most twice across all retained rows. Build a port vertex per occurrence and k_i private slot vertices per row, each connected to all its ports. A port matched to a slot denotes true physical bit. A root with two occurrences of equal polarity connects the two ports directly; with opposite polarity add a private midpoint adjacent to the two ports. The former forces equal, latter opposite. A singleton occurrence attaches its own dummy vertex. Let K=sum k_i and h=number of opposite-polarity pairs. Singleton dummies whose ports are true are free; their number has parity K-h since equal pairs contribute 0 or 2, opposite pairs 1. Add one extra dummy if K-h odd; clique all dummies. Then the remaining free dummies have even cardinality and can be paired. Every satisfying row assignment lifts to a perfect matching; conversely each perfect matching supplies exactly the required signed local occupancies and shared roots. No planarity required.
