## A. Matching changes the probe domain
For each of the 32 even subsets J of the six labeled ports, demand precisely these ports have color 1. Reject coincident demanded endpoints. Remove their incident vertices from the graph used for perfect matching. A perfect matching of the remaining internal graph is necessary and sufficient for a color-1 edge class with that boundary pattern (other ports excluded). If no matching exists, delete all orbit words in which any color has precisely this port subset. For a found matching restore the full pole and remove its color-1 edges and selected semiedges. Each internal vertex has degree 2; components are cycles and paths with two unselected port ends. If every cycle has even length, choose one alternating coloring of each cycle and independently alternate both phases of every path, giving <=2^3 actual boundary words, with actual internal witnesses. Reject odd-cycle candidates, but do NOT reject the pattern on this basis. In a bipartite pole all cycles are even, so every feasible pattern is an exact positive hit condition. These operations acquire lower L and upper H with L subset S_A subset H. For fixed k=6, 32 maximum-cardinality matching calls, O(poly(N)) graph work, at most eight phases per matching. Optional additional force/exclude-one-edge matching witnesses require O(n) calls per pattern; never infer coverage of all matchings. Ordinary matching is not joint coloring.
