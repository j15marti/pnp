## A. Greatest full-Kempe-cube kernel
For each orbit word w and color pair a,b, pair all ports colored a or b by any perfect pairing P. Let C(w,a,b,P) be the orbit set obtained by independently swapping a,b on each paired pair of endpoints. Every actual nonzero-coloring induces a disjoint two-color path pairing; swapping any subset of its paths is a proper coloring. Therefore every actual support S satisfies: for each w in S and each color pair, some entire cube C lies in S. This is a necessary condition, not a realizability theorem. It includes free wires. Call such masks closed. A union of closed masks is closed (each included word retains its original cube). For arbitrary A subset U, iteratively delete every word lacking a full contained cube for some pair. At fixed point K(A), each remaining word satisfies the condition. By induction no closed subset of A loses a word in any deletion round. Thus K(A) is the greatest closed subset of A, unique and independent of deletion order. At most 31 strict rounds; each word has 3 pairs, <=15 possible pairings and <=8 swaps. For variable k the cost is exponential/superexponential in k and is not declared polynomial. For any known actual S, Q_real(S)=union of actual disjoint contexts is bounded by Q_sample(S) subset Q_real(S) subset K(U minus S). If bounds agree, exact contextual signature certified. If K(U minus S)=empty, S intersects every nonempty actual context. Does not decide whether S itself is realized by an unexamined region.
