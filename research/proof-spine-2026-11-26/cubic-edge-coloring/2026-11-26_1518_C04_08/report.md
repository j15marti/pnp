## A. Five-pole support is a bridgeless graph of singleton pairs
A proper Tait coloring has odd multiplicity of each color among five labeled boundary incidences, by summing the count of incidences per color over cubic internal vertices and cancelling internal edges twice. Thus the multiplicities are (3,1,1). Up to one global permutation of three colors the boundary type is the unordered pair {i,j} of singleton-color ports. There are exactly 10 types, indexed lexicographically by unordered pairs of [0,1,2,3,4]. Represent exact support S as an edge set of K5, not an affine hull.
For each attained {i,j}, choose its witness. Swap the majority color and the singleton color at i along their alternating path starting at port i. Every internal vertex has degree two in this two-color subgraph; there is exactly one singleton port and three majority ports in it, so the path ends at some k outside {i,j}. The resulting proper witness has singleton pair {k,j}. Likewise swap starting at j to get {i,l}, l outside {i,j}. Hence each nonisolated vertex of S has degree >=2. In a graph on five vertices with no degree-one vertex, every edge lies on a simple cycle: if a bridge existed, each of its two sides must contain a cycle (otherwise a tree side has a leaf of original degree one, including the singleton-side case). This requires >=6 distinct vertices, contradiction. Therefore every nonempty actual S is a union of simple cycles. The same argument works on disconnected poles and free wires, because alternating paths may be free edges. In witness acquisition for actual connected sides, every path is paid O(n).
There are 10 triangles, 15 four-cycles and 12 five-cycles on five labels, total 37. Each has an exact small cap, by relabeling:
- triangle: a single cubic vertex with its three ports plus one isolated free edge joining the other two ports; the two singleton ports lie among the vertex's three ports and any of their three pairs is attainable.
- four-cycle: three-vertex path, each end vertex carrying two ports, central vertex one. Majority must appear once at each end and at center; singleton pair consists of one port from each end, giving all four K2,2 pairs.
- five-cycle: pentagon with one port per vertex. Enumerate its five internal edges over 3 colors and require three distinct incidences at each vertex. The singleton pairs form a five-cycle (adjacent port pairs in cyclic order). Relabel to any desired cycle.
Directly enumerated all 37 caps and verified supports. Cap internal order <=5. Joining two poles yields proper coloring iff their singleton-pair supports intersect, since global color permutation aligns any common type.

## B. Exact cycle signature and six finite representatives

A NO answer for cap C means C is wholly disjoint from S; their union is Q(S). If all queries are NO, S is empty: any edge of K5 belongs to some cycle. No free exact support oracle. For any admissible outside support T, the union-of-cycles property yields
 S intersect T = empty iff T subset Q(S).
Proof: if disjoint, every cycle of T is a cycle in K5-S, so every edge belongs to Q(S). Conversely Q(S) disjoint S by definition. This also covers empty T. Thus equality of Q is sufficient for exact compatibility against every unguarded cubic five-pole, even if the supports themselves differ.

## D. PRIME5-TARIFF
For a five-bond, s is odd, and s>=9, n>=2s. At most 37 children of order <=s+5 and one child <=n-s+7. A conservative budget even permits 38 smaller calls. Since
 n^32-(n-s+7)^32 >= (s-7)n^31,
 38(s+5)^32 / ((s-7)n^31) <= 266*(7/9)^31 < 1,
the integer potential obeys
 38(s+5)^32+(n-s+7)^32 <= n^32-1.
Here (s+5)/(s-7)<=7 and (s+5)/n<=7/9. Thus all child sizes strictly decrease and aggregate potential pays every node and failed exact query. Earlier 2/3/4-cut recurrences independently rechecked at their smaller exponents; since normalized child sizes are <1, increasing exponent to 32 preserves strict sum decrease. For decision mode use C04-07 six small queries and one large on four-cuts, exponent 10; witness mode uses three small and one large, exponent 8. Disconnected components are conjunctions and sum powers strictly less than parent for a nontrivial split. Bridges are impossible in proper cubic edge coloring by parity. Loops reject. Parallel pair is 2-cut or bridge unless the constant triple-parallel component. Thus terminal simple connected bridgeless leaves have no nontrivial 2/3-bond, no four-bond with both sides >=6 and no five-bond with both sides >=9.
