## New theorem: exact contextual scope erasure
Let F=H AND S(B) be an explicit Boolean table CSP, H all retained factors, B the target scope. Let M be any certified superset of the projection of feasible H assignments onto B. For C a subset of B, define Good=projection_C(M intersect S), Bad=projection_C(M minus S), treating S as a set of B-words. If Good and Bad are disjoint, replace S by the indicator of Good on C while retaining H. For every assignment satisfying H, its B-word is in M. Its membership in S is determined uniquely by its C-word, so H AND S equals H AND Good pointwise on the original variables. Conversely, when M is exact, a collision between Good and Bad proves no C-only predicate works relative to H. When M is an overapproximation, collision only refuses this certificate. No existential witness is lost and no external occurrence is deleted. Any already eliminated private roots must have recorded reverse maps.

Pair context R(A): M is all B-words whose overlap restriction to I=A intersect B belongs to projection_I(R). Compute by scanning R then S tables; no enumeration of A union B. This costs O((2^|A|+2^|B|) poly(w,log N)) to test all singleton deletions for fixed w=max factor arity, with explicit tables already paid. Multiple contexts R_i: intersect these projected masks. Every H-witness survives all, so use the same exact replacement criterion. No claim that intersection of projections equals the true projection of H. If all good/bad fibers collide, return UNKNOWN. Sequential replacements valid by pointwise equivalence even if a context is later replaced; preserve exact version order and reverse maps. 

For fixed m factors of max width w, potential L=sum factor arities <=mw, each replacement drops at least one incidence without adding factors or increasing arity. A full multi-context sweep costs O(m^2 2^w poly(w,log N)), at most mw productive sweeps plus final scan, with polynomial space, replay and reconstruction, and cumulative memory bounded by same deterministic T*S. If w=O(log N), m polynomial in original N and explicit tables, initialization and prior chart acquisition polynomial, this is polynomial. No universal progress or initial width guarantee. DISJ: pointwise equivalence, no branches. DTISP/configuration lower bound not claimed; relativization/algebrization/Natural Proofs not used. Counterchecks: inconsistent private-root maps, overlap collision, altered guard, malformed tables, source-only saturation.
