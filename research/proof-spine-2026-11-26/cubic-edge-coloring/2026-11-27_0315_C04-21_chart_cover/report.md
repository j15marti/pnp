## C: infinite native falsifier to unconditional polynomial affine-chart coverage
For k>=0, start with Petersen graph, replace one edge by a series chain of k copies of the cube graph with edge (0,1) removed; use two degree2 endpoints as connector ports. All vertices degree3, graph simple connected, n=10+8k. Guard excludes all k+1 connecting edges. Each cube region has a two-edge cut. In any cubic proper 3-edge-coloring, each color has boundary count congruent to region order modulo2, so both boundary edges same color. Contract each two-port region to an edge; a coloring would give a Petersen coloring, impossible (Petersen PM complements always two 5-cycles). Conversely any original coloring lifts by choosing a cube coloring with removed edge of the required color. Thus even unguarded G_k is NO. The 2-edge-cut contraction is a polynomial bypass; not a hard decider family.
With connector edges excluded, ordinary PMs factor into Petersen-minus-edge and k cube-minus-edge components.
