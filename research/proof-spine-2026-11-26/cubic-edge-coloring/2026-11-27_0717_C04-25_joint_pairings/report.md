## Exact joint signature theorem
For a cubic multipole with b labeled boundary ports, a proper three-edge-coloring induces a word w of actual color labels (not quotient by color permutation) and three simultaneous pairings p12,p13,p23: for each two-color subgraph, its boundary ports with those colors are endpoints of disjoint paths. Keep one whole tuple (w,p12,p13,p23) from one coloring. For disjoint regions with labeled wire incidences, join two attained signatures iff colors agree on every glued wire. For each color pair, form the multigraph of its pairing edges and its newly glued wires. Internal glued endpoints have degree two, surviving ports degree one. Every path projects to its two surviving endpoints and closed circuits have no surviving port. Retain the three resulting pairings together with the remaining word. This computes exactly the attainable joint signatures of the union: forward follows by contracting original bicolored paths, reverse follows by choosing a witnessing coloring on each disjoint region and identifying equal-colored ports; the resulting coloring realizes precisely the computed connectivity. No independent choices across color pairs. Multiple wires retain distinct IDs. The same argument permits self-gluing with matching word labels. Fixed matching guards can be checked locally by color1 membership of each original edge, including seam edges, before any join; arbitrary nonlocal guards need a separate adapter. No guard-dependent swaps are assumed. For unguarded or locally guarded decision, word-only join is already exact and cheaper; joint signatures are needed only if subsequent path-sensitive deductions require those actual joint observables. An upper approximation may safely refute on emptiness, but must never be asserted attainable.

Bound: at most 3^b (max(1,b!))^3 signatures, 2^O(b log b); naive pairwise join square of this bound times poly(b). Given a merge tree with b<=C log N/log log N, explicit local tables acquired within a fixed poly(N) cap, and O(n) merge operations, total work polynomial. Charge table discovery, matching calls and failures, comparison/sort/dedup, wire maps, verification, witness pointers, original bits. A witness can be reconstructed by following retained child records and leaf assignments; no exact counting is inferred from deduplicated signatures. Source-preserving terminal for bounded width; no guarantee of acquiring suitable trees for all graphs.

## Native marginal counterexample
Take the open region of three consecutive star blocks (12 internal vertices) in the J7 source, vertices 0..11, ports ordered left leaf 1,2,3 then right leaf 9,10,11; left edges cross the final twist, but local order uses actual endpoint. Use zero-indexed port positions 0..5. At fixed word (1,1,2,2,3,3), let A=((0,2),(1,3)), A'=((0,3),(1,2)), B=((0,5),(1,4)), C=((2,4),(3,5)), C'=((2,5),(3,4)). Exactly three triples occur: (A,B,C'), (A',B,C), (A',B,C'). Phantom (A,B,C) lies in the Cartesian product of the three pair projections but does not occur. Separate native edge-color CSP replay, with six port colors pinned, found exactly three colorings in 39 recursive states and traced all original paths, confirming these triples without using the join. Thus independently attained Kempe pairings cannot be joined as exact semantics even on this small source-realizable residual. This is a falsifier of marginal equality/positive realizability, not of sound upper-bound pruning, and not a hardness lower bound.

## Acquired three-rail negative terminal
For the four-vertex star block, denote left/right color triples l,r and the spoke triple s. Each (l_i,r_i,s_i) has all three colors, and s is a permutation. Hence monochromatic l is impossible. If l contains all three colors, r is obtained by an even 3-cycle of l; if l repeats a and has singleton b, r has singleton b and repeats the other color c. This is the classical Isaacs proof of flower snarks (not a new theorem). Turn it into an acquired two-channel negative certificate: for arbitrary chain of three-input/three-output multipoles with verified upper tables satisfying these same local laws, all-distinct parity is preserved across blocks and toggles by the parity of the seam permutation; repeated-color state preserves singleton and toggles the repeated color across blocks, independent of seam permutation. For an odd number of such blocks and odd product of seam permutations, both disjoint classes impossible. No exact full support required for this implication, and local guards can only shrink support. More generally, for acquired upper tables preserving the classes and with verified fixed per-block parity shifts, sum each shift with seam shift; if each class contradicts closure, refute. Exact word transfer with original labels remains available when this abstraction cannot refute. Generalizing to arbitrary multipoles requires a proved upper table, not just parity of port counts.

Native graph acquisition without supplied hub partition: for each of n possible hubs, order its three neighbors and try the 8 choices of their incoming neighbors other than hub. Their remaining neighbors uniquely give outgoing leaves. For a true three-rail ring of k>=3 star blocks, the next three leaves have their next hub as unique common neighbor: outside hub each rail neighbor in adjacent columns touches exactly one of these three leaves. Advance, preserving order and incoming edges. Reject repetition or ambiguous next hub; at closure check seam permutation, initial incoming edges, all vertices visited and the ENTIRE original edge set equals the reconstructed edges. At least one seed is correct on the native family, any accepted representation exact even on arbitrary inputs. O(n) seeds each O(n) bounded-degree local steps, final deterministic sorting and indexing poly(N), conservative O(n^3 log n) bit operations under explicit arrays (naive scanning also polynomial). This is a recognition/refutation terminal, not a general cubic solver. For true J_k with odd k and odd twist, return NO, otherwise this negative terminal may return UNKNOWN. Exact word transfer uses at most 3^6 boundary words, O(n) constant-size table joins, original ID reconstruction; both channels require only two finite invariant cases.
