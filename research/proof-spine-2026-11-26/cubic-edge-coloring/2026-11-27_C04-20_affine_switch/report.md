## A: exact matching-switch chart
Delete X and every competing edge incident with an edge of P; reject inconsistent P. Remaining allowed graph A has maximum degree two. Its components are paths, cycles or isolates. Odd components cannot have PM; even paths have one PM (alternating from endpoint), even cycles have precisely two alternating PMs. Hence exact ordinary PM family is Cartesian product of one binary switch t_j per even cycle, plus fixed edges F. Include all unsupported edges in X (ordinary forced-information closure, derived exactly from chart, no general matching oracle). This preserves all PMs of the guard, and the cycle bits reconstruct original IDs. In particular, if X after closure is not bipartite, refute. Otherwise fix base bipartition b on its components; full complement labels are b(v)+phi_comp(v) over GF2.

## B: exact affine slices and rank contraction
For each switch j, let M_j^0 and M_j^1 be the two matchings on its cycle. In variables (phi,t), form affine slice U_j^h with t_j=h and for every e=uv in M_j^(1-h), impose phi_comp(u)+phi_comp(v)=1+b(u)+b(v). All other nonmatching edges are in closed X. Hence good matching iff intersection over j of U_j^0 union U_j^1 is nonempty. Proof forward: take its complement bipartition and bits; backward: reconstruct matching from bits and use resulting phase labels on every nonmatching edge; alternately color each even complement cycle. Unlike local signed-bundle clauses this enforces cycles of arbitrary length exactly.

For any acquired affine overapproximation R (initially full space), compute B_j^h=R intersect U_j^h by Gaussian elimination. If both empty refute. If one empty import other exactly. Otherwise import affine hull of their union, represented by intersection of their augmented equation rowspaces. Justification: a nonempty affine system's augmented rowspace is all its valid affine equations (homogeneous annihilator after translation). An equation holds on union iff holds on each branch. Can acquire intersection by tagged Gaussian elimination (track contribution from first rowspace while reducing stacked rows; zero dependencies produce common rows). No enumeration of phase words. Reclose after every round of sound imports; strict rank gain or saturation, at most d productive rounds, d=#phase components+#switches=O(n). If contradictory system, NO sound. If stable nonempty, do not infer feasibility in general.

Exactness criterion: two nonempty slices lie in distinct parallel t_j hyperplanes. Their union is affine iff their direction spaces are equal, equivalently rank(B0)=rank(B1)=rank(hull)+1. Necessity: in an affine union, intersections with the two parallel hyperplanes have identical direction and half its size. Sufficiency: if the directions agree, union is coset of their common direction enlarged by the difference of representatives. Empty-branch case also affine. Rank test gives polynomial acquisition, no actual counts. At fixed point all hulls contain R (equal to R because subsets of R); if each is exact, all points of R satisfy the source. Otherwise let b be number of remaining non-affine switches; enumerate ONLY those switch bits and Gaussian-test R intersect selected slices. All other constraints already hold throughout R; thus exact in 2^b poly(N), regardless of total switches or phases. If b <= c log2 N for fixed c, a polynomial terminal. This is not a universal bound. Additional negative pair clauses, when used, need separate exact handling (each edge is fixed or a single switch literal; pair clause is a union of two literal hyperplanes, can include in affine closure but count all remaining non-affine constraints for exact branch terminal).

Take affine hull of union of nonempty branches by repeated equation-rowspace intersection. Empty window certifies NO. For fixed k this is polynomial; no global switch enumeration. Include all singleton and pair windows in synchronous scans; this genuinely imports matching-derived exact local support into global affine equations. All good solutions survive, and all-variable support is not replaced by hull in exactness claims. First exact reconstruction using independent exhaustive reference on same source.
