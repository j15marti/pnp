## 1. Exact balanced-support classification and new pointed obstruction
Use canonical boundary words ordered lexicographically, six positions, color labels 1,2,3 normalized by first appearance, each color occurs an even number of times. U has 31 words, 15 all-222. The C04-11 ten-set S=1553138944 has states 112233,112332,121323,121332,122133,122313,123132,123213,123231,123321. Its complement within the balanced words consists of 112323,121233,122331,123123,123312. Those five perfect pair partitions cover each of 15 unordered port pairs once: a 1-factorization of K6. Every pair belongs to exactly two states in S. The graph on these ten states, adjacent when sharing a pair, is the Petersen graph (independent Python isomorphism test; explicit adjacency in independent JS finite_result). This graph is connected and has an odd cycle.

Constructive/contradiction proof, no graph search assumption: let F={e1,e2,e3} be a missing partition, and take any actual coloring of an assumed S-realizing pole whose boundary state contains e1. There are exactly two such S states. Consider the two colors of the other four ports. Their alternating paths must pair endpoints of DIFFERENT colors, since a path joining equal colors permits a swap yielding a forbidden (4,2,0) word. There are two cross pairings; swapping one path gives the third pairing distinct from the current state and the chosen path pairing, with e1 fixed. Hence the only acceptable actual path pairing is {e2,e3}: the missing partition must be the path pairing, not the resulting boundary type. The same holds fixing e2 and e3. If a graph were embedded in a disk with all six semiedge endpoints on its boundary, every such pair of paths is vertex disjoint and cannot cross. All five missing partitions would be noncrossing perfect matchings of the six cyclically ordered points. In a noncrossing matching each chord joins odd and even positions (otherwise an odd number of endpoints lies inside one arc and cannot be paired internally). But their union covers K6, including same-parity pairs. Contradiction. No planar disk realization, for any order. This is NOT impossibility in general planar embeddings with ports on different faces or nonplanar graphs; the CEC source is arbitrary.

Additional symbolic matching/Kempe count condition: in any realization of S, for an adjacent pair of states sharing e, switch the two-color path containing the least of the other four port indices, then globally renormalize colors. The actual path pairing is forced as above. Reverse with same port and pair of colors corresponding to the two non-e colors, path unchanged by switch/renaming. Gives a bijection of canonical boundary colorings, so all ten counts are equal by connected Petersen graph. For a bipartite realization with all six ports on one side, fix an arbitrary order of edge incidences at each internal vertex; sign of a coloring is product of local permutation signs. Internal vertex count is even (3n=2m+6), so global color renaming preserves sign. A two-color path between same-side port vertices has odd number of internal vertices, and switching reverses sign. Thus signed counts alternate sign along Petersen edges; odd cycle forces them all zero. Each actual canonical word count must be positive even, at least two. This excludes any candidate with sign-coherent canonical colorings, not arbitrary bipartite poles.
