## Theorem and falsifiers
Let R_U be exact feasible local assignments, p_U nonnegative normalized distribution, m_U(T)=sum_{a in R_U} p_U(a) prod_{v in T}a_v. For any common interface I, full word consistency is equivalent to consistency of all m(T), T subset I, including empty normalization. Indeed the word indicator at pattern b is prod_{v:b_v=1}x_v prod_{v:b_v=0}(1-x_v); expand by inclusion-exclusion; converse sums word probabilities. For each nonempty T shared by k_T regions a star of k_T-1 equations suffices; no separate equalities for each of 2^|T| words. At degree k use only |T|<=k. This is a relaxation (unless all overlaps have width <=k); singleton and pair marginals can agree without full joint feasibility, e.g. uniform opposite parity classes on three variables. Every global satisfying assignment gives a feasible point; no inference from numerical infeasibility alone. 
By Farkas, a finite regional moment LP is infeasible iff there exist rational local polynomials f_U supported on its shared moment coordinates and constant, degree <=k, with f_U(a)>=0 on every exact local allowed row and sum_U f_U identically a negative constant. Forward: dual normalization and signed moment multipliers; backward: sum expectations and use consistency. For certificate checking allow all local scopes, verify original owners and maps, evaluate all locally allowed words and sum global monomial coefficients. Scale rational coefficients to integers, or use exact fractions. Global polynomial equality is over Q, not GF2 and not a PC proof. If region cover admits a checked running-intersection tree whose edge intersections have width <=k, the k-moment LP is exact: choose any positive root tuple, recursively choose positive child tuple agreeing on overlap (equal word probability ensures existence); tree consistency implies global agreement, and full scope covers every source constraint. Reverse witnesses reconstruct original bits. For fixed explicit dictionary L with max region width w, normalization+moment rows <= L sum_{i<=k} binom(w,i); columns <= L 2^w. For all-overlap version use k=w (or maximum shared intersection). If L poly(N), w=O(log N), rational matrix and exact bit-polynomial LP are polynomial; rational dual has polynomial bit size by determinant bounds. This does not guarantee acquisition of dictionary. Fixed polynomial dictionary admits nonrenewable region/moment credit per novel admission with failed attempts paid; no reset on replay.
