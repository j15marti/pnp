## Theorem: negative Kempe pairing closure
For any unguarded b-port cubic multipole, let W be the parity-compatible boundary equality words modulo simultaneous S3 color permutation. Actual support S is a subset of W. For w in W and colors a,b, let L be the boundary indices with colors a or b. Enumerate every perfect pairing p of L. Write O(w,a,b,p) for the set of canonical boundary words obtained by independently exchanging a,b on the endpoints of each selected pair of p (all subsets of pairs). A real coloring realizing w has its a/b subgraph decomposed into cycles and paths; at each internal vertex exactly two incident edges have these colors. Paths pair the boundary ports, including free edges. Swapping any subset of these disjoint paths keeps a proper coloring. Thus for every w in S and every color pair, some p has O subset S. No assertion that pairings for different colors can be chosen independently to construct a coloring.

Given any certified upper bound U containing S, delete w if for at least one color pair no pairing has O subset U. Repeat to fixed point K(U). By induction no actual state can be deleted. Each productive round deletes at least one type. The map is monotone; the result is the greatest subset of U satisfying the pairing rule: every fixed subset survives every round. K is idempotent, and repeated verified negative-cap imports with reclosure preserve S. If cap C has exact type set D and the glued graph R+C is certified NO, delete D from U_R (both directions of gluing equivalence follow by simultaneous color permutation on one side and identical port order). Therefore K(U_A) intersection K(U_B) empty implies source NO. This is a sound negative-only hybrid, not an assertion of exact support or universal sufficiency. Arbitrary color/matching guards may break Kempe invariance; a singleton pinned actual word can be pruned, so do not use without a guard-preserving swap theorem. The cap type sets are exact; upper sets need not be. No counting of colorings is used.

For b=6 there are 31 types (1+15+15), <=15 pairings per color pair, <=8 subsets per pairing. Constant-size proof replay per iteration, <=31 productive iterations. For general b direct enumeration has cost bounded by poly(b)*|W|^2*max(1,(b-1)!!)*2^ceil(b/2) with suitable even pairing bound (use b! instead for odd b), hence polynomial for b=O(log N/log log N) for closure alone; cap acquisition and decomposition width are separate. Sound on all permitted inputs; failed cap calls never delete a type.
