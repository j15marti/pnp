## Theorem A (all pair-splits iff affine consistency)
Let L(G) be the native signed transport/E4 equalities with B4 replaced by XOR of its four ports =0. For any choice sigma of one of the three partitions of the four ports into two pairs at each E4, replace E4 by equalities on those pairs, retaining each B4 exactly. Call the relaxed network G_sigma. Then for every strict unguarded source G,
  L(G) feasible iff for ALL sigma, G_sigma feasible.
Moreover if L(G) inconsistent, polynomially acquire a sigma and an odd-sign connected component certifying G_sigma NO. If L(G) consistent, a single affine witness certifies survival of EVERY sigma; given any sigma, a feasible relaxed witness can be acquired in linear graph operations.

Proof (constructive): view each split E as two degree-two vertices, B unchanged degree four, edges with signs and original ports. Each resulting component is even-degree. Summing the satisfied edge equations modulo two, or summing the original affine witness over all ports of that component, gives XOR of edge signs zero: E pairs sum to zero, B parity zero. Conversely, for each component with even sign sum, take Euler circuit. Pair consecutive arrival/departure ports at each traversal of a vertex; at E2 impose equal bits; at each B4 impose opposite bits in both visit pairs. Traverse edges applying the given signs and at B visit toggle. Each B visited twice, E2 once, so closure holds exactly when the sum of edge signs is zero. Produces all original signed edge equations and B exact two, and all selected E pair equalities. An odd sign component gives necessary contradiction by summation. Isolated empty components irrelevant.
Proof (dual converse): an inconsistent GF2 system has a sum of original affine equations yielding 0=1; use E star equalities, B parity, signed edge equations. Let F be the set of selected signed edge rows. At every B either all four incident edges or none are selected (B row used or not). At E the number of selected incident ports is even because equality rows have even support. For each E choose a partition whose pairs are each monochromatic in membership in F (possible for 0,2,4). Then F is a union of complete connected components of the split graph: all incident edges at each resulting node are in F or none. Sum of signs of F is 1; hence at least one component has odd sign sum. This sigma is acquired from Gaussian elimination with tracked row provenance, no enumeration. A checked dual or affine witness decides whether the whole local projection family can refute. The proof does not assert source feasibility when all relaxed instances survive. Arbitrary extra pins/guards not covered.

## Theorem B (all local even delta-matroid relaxations)
Let R be any even delta-matroid relation on the four ports containing E4 = {0000,1111}. Since 0000 is present all words have even Hamming weight. Let H be the graph on four port indices whose edges denote the weight-two words in R. Symmetric exchange applied to 0000 and 1111 in both directions requires H and its disjoint-pair image (each edge mapped to its complementary two ports) to have no isolated vertices. If H has no two disjoint edges, its edges are pairwise intersecting; a pairwise-intersecting simple 2-set family is contained in a star or a triangle (choose two meeting edges; an edge not containing their intersection must join their two other endpoints, and then no edge leaves that triangle). A triangle fails the first no-isolate condition; a star fails the second. Thus H contains complementary edges. R includes the four-word pair-split relation formed by those edges and 0000/1111. Every pair-split is itself an even delta-matroid (two independent equality pairs).
For ANY independently chosen even delta-matroid superset at each E, the network contains a pair-split relaxation and is feasible whenever L(G) is consistent. The converse uses Theorem A's rejecting split. This covers local matching-realizable relaxations (known matching-realizable => even delta-matroid, Kazda/Kolmogorov/Rolinek, arXiv:1602.03124 journal version, Def.16 and discussion; no planarity needed). Also holds if B is independently enlarged. Does NOT exclude coupled multi-region gadgets, arbitrary alternative encodings, exact joint joins of different projections, or a polynomial solver. Intersecting two distinct E4 pair-split relations gives E4 exactly; joining them with shared original bits restores hard joint information, not independent feasibility. No unrestricted lower bound.
