## Proof A: matching-domain odd-cycle polynomial
For a perfect matching M let x_e be its Boolean indicator. For simple odd cycle C of length 2r+1, h=|M intersect E(C)|<=r. On the perfect-matching domain define P_C=sum_{j=0}^r (-1)^j sum_{S subset E(C), |S|=j, S matching} product_{e in S} x_e. Evaluated on M this equals sum_{j=0}^h (-1)^j binom(h,j), hence 1 iff h=0, else 0 over integers and any field. In a cubic graph C is a component of G-M iff h=0: all cycle vertices have exactly two complement edges and those are the cycle edges. Thus all P_C=0 are jointly necessary and sufficient for even complement on actual perfect matchings. For 5-cycles degree2, 7-cycles degree3. For fixed degree d enumerate odd cycles of length <=2d+1; in cubic graph bounded-degree DFS costs O(n*3^(2d+1)) up to canonicalization. Multilinear monomial variables indexed by edge sets size <=d; conflict monomials zero; optional exact monomial support via matching after requiring edges, at most O(m^d) queries. Multiply local sum incident x=1 by monomials degree<=d-1 and odd-cycle P_C by monomials degree<=d-r, reduce x^2=x. Linearized inconsistency sound; consistency UNKNOWN, not a realizability claim. O(m^d) variables and polynomially many rows for fixed d, dense elimination and provenance polynomial. Tests at d=2 over F2/F3, 3M sparse coefficient-update cap per run. Every actual matching checked for identity on all odd cycles <=7 (Dot8 12 cycles, Dot0 14, Dot1 12, Petersen12, K4 4). ## Integrated C: first-hit guard partition
Order original edges e_1..e_k of chosen odd C. Valid children (P union {e_i}, F union {e_1,...,e_(i-1)}). These guards are disjoint and cover exactly those guarded M hitting C. Missing-C fiber is universally invalid. Test each child matching feasibility before recursive acquisition; choose among witness odd components by number of feasible child guards. Retain failed candidate work and all rejected child certificates. For every matching, guard indicator g_{P,F}=product_{e in P}x_e product_{e in F}(1-x_e). Exact identity on matching domain: g_parent = g_parent P_C + sum_i g_child_i. Hence any finite all-NO tree supplies an exact disjoint cover 1=sum_internal g_parent P_C on perfect-matching domain, since all terminal guards contain no matching. This is a compact arithmetic circuit identity, not a count oracle and not a rank-one relaxation. If a guard is feasible, compute union of support by requiring each edge and testing matching. Edges absent from every matching form graph of max degree2; any odd cycle in it is a common odd complement component and is a terminal. Conversely common odd cycle implies all its edges absent from matching support. Exact single-guard common-cycle certificate acquired with <=m matching queries, no short-cycle assumption. Production algorithm can also check forbidden/required closure by <=2m queries; these logical forced facts preserve the entire guard fiber.
