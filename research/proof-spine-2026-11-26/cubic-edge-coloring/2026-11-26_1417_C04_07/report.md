## A. Exact four-pole structural facts
For a four-edge bond separating connected sides, each side has even vertex count. In a proper boundary coloring the count of each color on the four semiedges is even: sum its incidences over vertices, cancel internal edges twice. Up to global permutation there are only four types: 0=1111, 1=1122, 2=1212, 3=1221. All boundary edge IDs and order are retained, including repeated internal endpoint vertices.
Every nonempty attainable support S has at least two types. In a witness choose port0 color and another boundary color (if all equal, any other color). Follow its two-color alternating path from port0 to another boundary port; internal degree two in the two-color subgraph. All four ports lie in these two colors. Swap along the path. If initial type 0, this produces a pair type. If initial pair type, swapping two equal endpoint colors produces type 0; swapping two unequal endpoint colors produces a different pair partition. Witness remains proper, only O(n) traversal.
For each of the six unordered pairs of distinct types construct an exact cap. Pair {0,i}: join the two couples of boundary ports of pairing i by two edges, with zero added vertices. Pair {j,k} with both nonzero: let i be the missing nonzero type; attach the two couples of i to two fresh adjacent cubic vertices. Distinct incident colors require unequal within both couples and the same unordered color pair at the two fresh vertices, exactly types j,k. Parallel edges retain distinct IDs; loops mean impossible. Each cap query tests precisely whether S intersects the requested pair; a proper closed coloring restricts to a pole witness.

## B. THREE-PROBE-FOUR-BOND (witness-producing version)
Query the smaller side with cap {0,1}. If NO, query {2,3}; if NO again, S empty. If YES, S exactly {2,3} by the two-type lemma; one Kempe exchange gives the second witness. Otherwise first query YES yields type t. Query any pair not containing t. If NO, S is exactly the complementary pair and one exchange gives its other witness. If YES gives u distinct from t, query the pair consisting of the remaining two types. If NO, S exactly {t,u}; if YES, retain three actual distinct witnesses. No fourth witness or exact large support inferred. Every NO must be exact, never cap exhaustion.
Glue the larger side using exactly one capped coloring query. For exact two-type smaller support use its exact pair cap; YES lifts by stored compatible witness and one global color permutation, NO excludes any common type. For at least three actual smaller witnesses, use a square cap on larger side, whose attainable types are three of the four (in cyclic port order 0,1,3). Verify square by direct alternation or explicit enumeration of its four internal edges. Any nonempty larger support has at least two types and intersects both the cap support and stored three-set. Thus larger query NO iff larger pole has empty support; YES restricts to a witness of a type r. If r missing from stored smaller witnesses, perform one alternating-path exchange on the larger pole; its different type must be stored. Align colors and glue; verify original IDs. This is existential decision equivalence and reconstructive sufficiency, not exact preservation of all proper boundary words or any coupled guard. The old CEC04 report already had the square relaxation and six-pair alternative; new contribution is at most three adaptive witness queries and the following fixed aggregate recursion.
Decision-only version: query all six smaller pair caps. All NO means empty. If nonempty and some pair NO, S is exactly its complement. If all YES, S has >=3. One larger query using pair or square cap as above decides parent without witness-producing oracle. Leaf decision does not automatically supply a class-preserving witness self-reduction; distinguish the two versions.

## C. PRIME4-TARIFF (uniform conditional reduction)
Let n parent vertices, 6<=s<=n/2 the even size of smaller side of a four-bond. At most three recursive queries have size <=s+2; the one larger query has size <=n-s+4. All sizes strictly smaller. Since n^8-(n-s+4)^8 >= (s-4)n^7, and
3(s+2)^8/((s-4)n^7) <=12(2/3)^7 <1
(using (s+2)/(s-4)<=4 and (s+2)/n<=2/3), integer potential pays
3(s+2)^8+(n-s+4)^8 <= n^8-1.
This charges unsuccessful and successful calls alike, without sharing. For six smaller decision-only queries use exponent ten: 24(2/3)^9<1. Thus even without acquisition of a small/logarithmic separator side, the entire split tree contains at most n^8 (witness version) or n^10 (decision version) nodes. At each node discover cuts by enumerating subsets of <=4 current edge IDs, traverse components and verify actual connected sides, O(n^5) graph operations. Disconnected components are independent conjunctions, sum powers <=parent power-1 when nontrivial. Bridges reject by parity. At a 2-bond cap each side by one edge; children of sizes s,n-s, same parity and glue by permutation. In bridgeless graph ports on a side cannot coincide at a vertex (remaining edge would be bridge), loops reject. At a nontrivial 3-bond cap by one new vertex each, children s+1 and n-s+1, s>=3 and both odd. Two children satisfy (s+1)^8+(n-s+1)^8<=n^8-1 by n^8-(n-s+1)^8>=(s-1)n^7 and ratio <=2(2/3)^7. Same for exponent ten. Three distinct boundary colors glue by permutation. Parallel-edge pair in a connected nonterminal cubic multigraph creates a 2-cut or bridge; triple parallel is direct constant YES. Loop directly NO. Small constant cases solved by enumeration. A leaf may have no nontrivial 2/3-bond and no 4-bond with both sides >=6; simple connected and bridgeless, square sides remain possible. Define this precise terminal promise rather than claim fully cyclically five-connected or large girth. For absence of lower cuts, a cyclic cut of size four with both sides >=6 has connected sides: a disconnected side would require at least three edges per component by lower connectivity. Thus this is a structural reduction genuinely beyond C03-06. 
The decision language on these terminal graphs is NP-complete under polynomial-time Turing reductions (membership NP; arbitrary cubic coloring reduces by above); a polynomial-time terminal decider would imply P=NP, and P=NP implies it. Witness version conditional on a fixed uniform polynomial witness routine for terminal inputs. Alternatively, if all terminal atom orders <=b log2 N for fixed b, exhaustive local color assignment O(3^(3b log2 N/2)) gives unconditional polynomial admission. Large unresolved leaf returns UNKNOWN unless a sound terminal routine supplied. No claim of a uniform terminal solver. No guarantee singleton-flow witness survives cap queries, so do not import the C03 prime-singleton promise. No arbitrary pins beyond one globally permutable final edge.
