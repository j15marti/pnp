## A. Polynomial acquisition theorem
Let F(x)=sum_{j=1}^r c_j 1_{A_j}(x) on V=F2^d, integer coefficients, A_j nonempty affine flats with explicit linear systems. No positivity is required for period discovery; exact count interpretation requires certified nonnegative F. Define unnormalized Walsh transform H(a)=sum_x F(x)(-1)^(a dot x). If A=p+K, its transform is |K| (-1)^(a dot p) 1_{K^perp}(a): characters cancel in pairs outside K^perp. Thus H itself is an explicitly computable signed-flat dictionary with <=2r terms (split K^perp by a dot p=0,1), integer bit growth <=d+log r. No 2^d-dimensional transform is materialized.
For any affine query G, E(G)=sum_{a in G} H(a)^2 = sum_{i,j} h_i h_j |B_i intersect B_j intersect G|. Compute exactly by Gaussian elimination. Nonnegative energy is zero iff G contains no nonzero coefficient. Let U be span(supp H). Starting from U0=0, take a basis of its annihilator. If every associated opposite halfspace a dot b=1 has zero energy, support is inside U0. Otherwise choose a positive-energy halfspace; greedy coordinate halfspace refinement preserves positive energy and acquires one actual frequency outside U0. Add that frequency and repeat. At most d independent additions, O(d^2) energy calls (scan at most d annihilator rows and at most d coordinate refinements per round). This is deterministic, exact, polynomial in explicit r,d, coefficient bits, not a value-oracle property tester.
Fourier inversion and orthogonality prove F(x+h)=F(x) for all x iff a dot h=0 for every a in supp H. Thus P=U^perp is the complete maximal linear period group. The minimal dimension of any exact linear-observation representation is dim U: if F factors through L, ker L consists of periods, so rank L>=dim U; the observation map x->(u_i dot x) for a basis of U attains equality. It is not necessarily the smallest flat dictionary or arbitrary circuit.
Construct a linear section of observation map by solving the independent rows with free coordinates set zero. Restrict each original affine term to the section, then lift to original observations. This yields <=r canonical terms and exactly the same F, proved by period invariance and independently checkable by squared difference from C06-18. If dim U<=c log N, its entire quotient table can instead be evaluated in polynomial time. The same energy method enumerates Fourier support up to a declared cap by binary prefix tree traversal; zero-energy nodes terminate; <=O(d(s+1)) queries if s actual nonzero frequencies. Beyond cap UNKNOWN, not a zero function. Inverse sparse character representation is exact with common denominator 2^d; do not replace by GF2 arithmetic. This latter sparse enumeration is a mathematical corollary, not implemented.
All claims extend to an affine guard G by first parameterizing it with a particular point and independent directions, restricting each term, and performing the calculation on that domain. Periods are contextual, not globally valid or logical unit assignments. Nonnegative count period implies support period, converse false: F=1+1_{x=0} has full support but zero nontrivial count periods. F=0 has all periods; constant positive is not a refutation.
