## Theorem 1: two-copy energy with no product expansion
F(x)=sum_z prod_i f_i(x,z), x in F2^d, z private; factors explicitly supplied integer tables on named subsets of Boolean coordinates (or acquired with paid exact evaluations). Let H(a)=sum_x F(x)(-1)^(a.x). For G=a0+L, E(G)=sum_{a in G} H(a)^2. Expanding and using character orthogonality,
E(G)=2^dim(L) sum_{x,y : x+y in L^perp} F(x)F(y)(-1)^(a0.(x+y)).
Expand F(x)F(y) into two copies of original factor network, summing independently over their private variables. This is exact over integers; no approximate FFT, sampling, Boolean-semiring cancellation or product dictionary construction.

For acquiring span(supp H) one only needs guards with some prefix of the d frequency coordinates fixed to p_j, and at most ONE extra parity b.a=beta. Remove the fixed-coordinate part of b and adjust beta to beta'=beta+sum_{j in P} b_j p_j. If b'=0, return zero for inconsistent guard and otherwise discard redundant equation. Write e=1 when b' nonzero, else0. The annihilator of the homogeneous guard is parametrized uniquely by h=sum_{j in P}u_j e_j+t b', t in F2 if e=1, else t=0. Thus:
E(G)=2^(d-|P|-e) sum_t (-1)^(beta't) sum_{x,z,y,w} prod_i f_i(x,z)f_i(y,w) prod_{j in P}(-1)^(p_j(x_j+y_j)) prod_{j notin P}1[x_j+y_j=t b'_j].
Private coordinates have no equality constraint. For fixed t, pair each original Boolean coordinate with its counterpart as one four-state variable. The original interaction graph is unchanged: factor scopes unchanged and guard weights unary on paired variables. Thus any verified elimination order/decomposition of width w gives a scalar contraction using at most 4^(w+1) entries in a bag and poly(N) such contractions/operations per query. At most two t branches; exact integers. Every intermediate magnitude bounded by 2^(2n) times product of squared factor maxima (with possible harmless constant factors for t and Walsh scaling), hence O(n+sum_i bitlength(max(1,|f_i|))+d) bits. All original factor evaluations, pairing, decompositions, table operations, guards, failed scans, independent reference caps, reconstruction, indices and integer bits charged. Storage poly(N)4^(w+1); cumulative <=(T+1)S on same execution. For a fixed c, if treewidth <=c log N on supplied exact representation, use Korhonen arXiv:2104.07463v3 2-approximation in 2^O(k) n time, width <=2k+1, k=ceil(c log2 N); acquire and verify order, then polynomial total bit time. Approximation driver not executed; experiment uses a deterministic min-fill order with checked width, not a universal approximation guarantee.

## Theorem 2: complete period acquisition and quotient gluing
Start with U=0. If total energy is zero return U=0. For each basis vector b of U^perp, test E(b.a=1). If all zero, support lies in U and stop. Otherwise fix an attained frequency bit by bit using exact nonnegative prefix energies retaining b.a=1; positive energy on parent means at least one child is positive. Add actual frequency to U. At most d increases and O(d^2) queries, all of precisely the form in theorem1. Then P=U^perp is exactly maximal translation-period space by Fourier inversion. Any linear observation map through which F factors must have kernel subset P; rank(U) is optimal. Count function can be evaluated on a linear section using the original exact factor DP; materialize a quotient table only if 2^rank(U) fits paid cap. For regional joins use C06-19 exact fiber multiplicity, not independent child multiplicities. This is a genuine hybrid: acquisition occurs on the unexpanded product and changes the join's observation domain. Ordinary bounded-width counting can already decide these full instances; this theorem addresses representation acquisition and sharing, not an exclusive decision class or speedup. For general affine-scope factors and guards, a naive coordinate choice may inflate width; no arbitrary low-width assumption.

## Symbolic extension: affine-owner cut trees
For factor scopes W_i subspaces of a common dual space W, let W_S=sum_{i in S}W_i and I_S=W_S intersect W_complement. On a supplied binary leaf tree of width max dim I_S=w, root I=0, leaf K_i complements I_i in W_i. For internal children A,B, J=I_A+I_B contains I_parent. Choose K_parent complement of I_parent in J. Inductively W_S is the direct sum of I_S and all K_v in its subtree: private complements from W_A,W_B are independent modulo J, since any relation forces each back into W_A intersect(W_B+W_out)=I_A or I_B. Consequently the K_v at all nodes form a direct coordinate basis of W. A leaf tensor evaluates f_i on K_i and I_i values; a parent tensor accepts compatible child I-values iff they agree on I_A intersect I_B, then enforces their induced linear functional on J restricts to parent I and physical K. The product network has exactly one consistent set of internal labels for each global functional on W (unique gluing), no extra multiplicity. Local tensor dimensions O(w+max_i dim W_i) bits, tree structure, exact integer arithmetic. Pair tree bonds and physical coordinates and apply same prefix/one-parity unary guard method. Thus spectral period of full product is acquired with 2^O(w+max leaf rank) poly(N) time for supplied decomposition (and known branch-width acquisition C06-17 where applicable). Private coordinates can be summed if retained observation space is spanned by selected K-basis coordinates; arbitrary retained affine subspace requires a new adapter and can raise width. Symbolic only, not implemented or independently reviewed. This avoids conflating coordinate graph width with affine subspace cut width.

## Adversarial alternative: actual local projections and rank-two hull
For indicator S=intersection_i pi_i^{-1}(T_i), define actual T'_i=pi_i(S). Then S=intersection_i pi_i^{-1}(T'_i): one direction obvious, reverse follows T'_i subset T_i. Hence P(S)=intersection_i pi_i^{-1}(P(T'_i)). Using periods of allowed local T_i instead can be strictly wrong. Example on F2^2: T1={01,10,11}, T2={00,01,10}; each trivial period, joint support {01,10} has period 11. With empty joint support every direction is a period. This is a concrete falsifier for naive factorwise period propagation.
For nonempty S defined by avoiding rank<=2 nonempty affine flats, if exact affine hull x0+D of S is acquired, all projection periods are determined: on a rank2 scope W_i, if pi_i(D) has dimension0 or1, actual projection equals its affine hull; if dimension2, actual projection must consist of the three allowed words, as two points span at most dimension1 and one forbidden word is excluded. A three-word subset of F2^2 has no nonzero period. Rank1 scopes have singleton projection. Thus P(S)=D intersect kernels of pi_i for precisely the rank2 scopes with full-dimensional projected hull. This is exact and polynomial given the true nonempty affine hull. It is not a decision from a hull relaxation, not valid for arbitrary higher-rank projections or weighted counts, and not a free method of obtaining the hull. After exact full affine-hull closure, a nonempty raw defect product with spanning normal scopes has no nontrivial period in the effective chart. This identifies where intermediate marginalization is necessary to obtain useful compression.

## Universal obstruction is an exact equivalence, not a separation
On any nonempty native formula use the ungarded original parity chart Ax=1 mod2, always nonempty because x=all ones. Write x=x0+B t and F(t)=prod_i(1-1_{H_i}(t)); each defect flat H_i has codim<=2. On the all-ones assignment F=0 (at least one original clause); F is an indicator of exact satisfying assignments on the chart. Hence span(supp Hhat)=0 iff F constant iff F identically0 iff native UNSAT. Therefore a uniform polynomial algorithm acquiring complete periods of arbitrary compact products of these rank<=2 defect complements would prove P=NP. An unrestricted exact total-energy algorithm has an even stronger consequence: by Parseval E(F2^d)=2^d sum_t F(t)^2=2^d #SAT(F). Cai-Fan arXiv:2303.16705v1 proves #P-hardness of counting cubic planar X3C, the same native source. Universal exact energy would give FP=#P (counting functions, not class identity P=NP). These are reductions, not a proof that either algorithm is impossible. Necessary distinction: exact zero test vs numeric energy vs period basis vs support-only period vs exact count period.
