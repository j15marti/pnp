## Replica A: exact constructive separator algorithm
Write pairwise energy E(p)=sum_e c_e(p_u XOR p_v), both values rational with polynomial encoding; loops constant and parallel costs aggregate pointwise. Every cut energy has global flip symmetry. For a region with at most three retained boundary phases S, exact value f(s)=min_internal E is also flip-invariant. Empty/one boundary: constant. Two: f00+(f01-f00)(p1 XOR p2). Three: write a=f010-f000, b=f001-f000, c=f011-f000. Then f=f000+w12(p1 XOR p2)+w13(p1 XOR p3)+w23(p2 XOR p3), with w12=(a+c-b)/2, w13=(b+c-a)/2, w23=(a+b-c)/2. The four normalized words exhaust the flip orbits. Negative coefficients allowed: shift constants and use nonnegative penalty preferring inequality instead of equality. Retain one actual minimizing internal phase witness for each normalized word; complement the witness if the receiving boundary's first bit is 1. These are exact min-cost summaries, not affine hulls, not counts.

Acquire a clique separator K of size <=3 by enumerating all O(v^3) candidate subsets in lex order, testing clique and disconnected complement. Disconnected graphs use K=empty. A subproblem has one boundary clique S (initially empty). Because S is a clique, S-K lies in at most one component of G-K. Pick one different component O, or if S subset K choose any component to retain and a different O. Put B=O union K, A=V minus O. Assign every cost involving O to B, all other costs to A. Keep zero-cost virtual clique edges on K in B for structural checks; never double-charge the original K-K edges. Recursively compute all normalized K values for B once. Replace it in A by the exact constant+signed clique terms. Recursively compute the S table on A once. Every old boundary word lifts by selecting the stored B witness on the actual K word, using complement when needed. Soundness follows by disjoint interiors and exact cost equality; completeness follows by restricting any original minimizer to A and B. The recursive tree is not replicated per boundary word. Failed leaf admission returns UNKNOWN, not a negative decision. The first implementation omitted zero-cost K edges in B and hit a boundary-not-clique assertion; fixed and rerun.

Charge: preserve the structural clique edges even when their cost is zero. The potential is the number of unordered nonadjacent vertex pairs. At a split, no nonedge can be duplicated because the overlap K is a clique; at least one nonedge crossing between distinct components vanishes. Sum of child potentials <= parent potential-1. Hence at most binom(v,2) internal nodes, at most that+1 leaves. O(v^5) separator tests across tree, each polynomial adjacency/connectivity work. Each atom makes <=4 terminal calls, never product over ancestors. Each recurrence has a constant number of rational additions/subtractions and divisions by 2 per effective edge (plus aggregation over polynomially many terms); denominator exponent and numerator bit length increase by at most polynomial across the polynomial-size tree. More directly each operation adds at most logarithmic bit growth in its polynomial fan-in or 1 for doubling, so polynomial total bit lengths. Store up to four witnesses per node (O(v) bits each, plus IDs and costs) or backpointers. Reconstruction, all failed checks, closure and source verification polynomial on the same execution. Peak and cumulative memory polynomial (cumulative <=time*peak). No free global count: at most four actually evaluated normalized words per seam, and paid exact atom solver; nonplanar log-size enumeration explicitly capped. The atom admission criterion is conditional on the acquired decomposition, not existence of some favorable decomposition.

## Replica B: global matching and native algebraic construction
For a planar leaf, choose an embedding of its actual graph. For each edge choose preferred parity b_e minimizing its two costs; w_e=absolute difference. Let y be the set of violated preferences. A phase solution exists for a chosen y iff the parity vector b XOR y has even sum around every face boundary. Face boundaries generate the binary cycle space (bridges appear twice); thus y is a T-join in the geometric dual, T=faces with odd preferred parity. All edge costs w>=0. Dual shortest-path distances on T plus min-weight perfect matching find exact min T-join: any T-join decomposes into paths pairing odd vertices and cycles; conversely XOR of shortest matched paths is a T-join costing at most the matching value. This proves equality of minima in both directions. Ignore dual loops and retain minimum-weight parallel dual edge with original ID. Recover primal phases by BFS of prescribed parities. To compute a normalized boundary word, impose its <=2 relative parity preferences on existing boundary clique edges with penalty larger than total edge cost variation. Every boundary word extends to phases, so optimum obeys these penalties; remove them when reporting cost. All graph costs scaled to integers before matching; scale has polynomial bit length. Weighted matching is polynomial (Hadlock 1975 planar MaxCut; Edmonds–Johnson T-join). No appeal to arbitrary MaxCut tractability.

Native connected stress family: b blocks of six variables and six rows. In block j row i is cyclic triple (i-1,i,i+1) mod 6, all IDs offset by 6j. For consecutive blocks swap the middle incidence of row 1 in left block and row 0 in right block. This is a planar connected 2-edge splice of planar hexagonal-prism incidence graphs; each endpoint retains degree three. Row 0 and row 1 are independent coordinates in the original left-nullspace (period-3 words with XOR of first three bits zero), so deleting either/both leaves row rank four. Therefore the unmodified rows imply every original homogeneous row, and the modified rows impose only equality between outgoing residue-1 coordinate and next incoming residue-0 coordinate. In each block all homogeneous coordinates are (t_j,a_j,t_j+a_j,t_j,a_j,t_j+a_j). The b-1 independent equalities a_j=t_(j+1) leave b+1 dimensions. Let phase vertices 0..b+1, with t_j=p_j XOR p_(j+1), a_j=p_(j+1) XOR p_(j+2). Indexed edges are [(j,j+1),(j+1,j+2),(j,j+2)] repeated twice per block. This chart is exactly the kernel, verified via row rank and each original row, not guessed from SAT solutions. All-one is the affine base. Original guards retained as penalties. The chart is path-square with b consecutive triangles; all unguarded preferences favor inequality. Every disjoint triangle requires a removed vertex for balance; triangles at positions 0,3,... are disjoint. Deleting phases 2,5,... removes every triangle and leaves a path of retained adjacent vertices with distance<=2 edges, hence bipartite. Thus minimum global balance deletion is ceil(b/3)=floor((b+2)/3), unbounded linearly in original n. The phase graph is planar and also solvable by previous matching/width terminals; no claimed superiority over all existing lanes. Independent direct path DP stores the last two phases and appends the next bit with its exact incident costs. Two-state-bit table, O(b) transitions and actual lift. This is the separate global lane on the identical native residual, not a different source. In an unguarded loop-free graphic kernel chart, every native row is a triangle, so any acquired proper 4-coloring yields SAT by partitioning colors 2+2; no proof source incidence planarity implies planar or 4-colorable chart. A loop may force pairwise constraints; all-loop row rejects.

## Replica C: independent adversarial reconstruction
Exhaustive source Boolean scan for n<=18, independent Gaussian kernel enumeration for larger small nullity, with cap on words; compare exact penalized minima and original witness. At large nullity return UNKNOWN in reference lane. For any four-port star with equal preference and unit edges, eliminate the center. Effective cost f(p)=min(t,4-t), t=number of 1 boundary phases. Its Walsh degree-four numerator is sum_p (-1)^t f(p)=4, so no pairwise XOR cost representation on those same four boundary phases, even with signed rational weights and constant. This is a representation falsifier (auxiliary vertices may retain the star); no decision lower bound. Arbitrary K5 chart with all ten triangles, each edge occurs in three clauses, is a nonnative-planarity control: n=10 and impossible exact-one integer occupancy, loop-free graphic kernel (triangles generate cycle space). Incidence graph not planar: bipartite, 20 vertices, 30 edges, no 4-cycles, hence planar girth-six upper bound 27 edges. Thus the native planarity hypothesis cannot simply be omitted. K5 control symbolic; no assertion that it falsifies the native implication.
