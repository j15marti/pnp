## A. Exact unit principal-pivot split
Permute a diagonal-one index to first position, Q=[[1,r^T],[c,B]]. At that coordinate either y_i=0 or (Qy)_i=0. In the first case remaining equations are S(B), lifted by (0,z). In the second y_i=r^T z, remaining equations are S(B+c r^T), lifted by (r^T z,z). Thus S(Q) is exactly the union of the two linearly lifted child supports (overlap allowed). The span is the sum of the lifted spans. Both children may be computed by the C06-15 reciprocal-SCC exact-span terminal; an unknown child is not a refutation. Scan at most d pivots. With one omitted flat, combined span containment is exact. With multiple omitted flats, it is sound to certify each lifted child hull contained in SOME omitted flat, potentially different flats for the two children. This is sufficient, not necessary. Never infer union containment from arbitrary hull substitution. No unlimited recursive branching is claimed.

## B. Protected parity-module theorem (exact attained projection)
Let G be a nonempty proper group with incoming and outgoing coefficients uniform on G for every outside vertex k: Q_ik constant over i in G and Q_ki constant over i in G. Additionally require every normal of every omitted target flat to be constant on G. Replace G by z_G=XOR of its coordinates. Outside equations then depend on G solely through z_G. Given outside values, the common incoming contribution to each local row is h. Internal equations are exactly S(Q[G]+hI). Define A_h to mean some internal support has odd parity. For z_G=0 use the zero internal assignment, always feasible. For z_G=1 require A_h. At least one A_h is true: choose any i, singleton e_i is feasible for h=Q_ii. If both labels permitted, the new row is zero. If only p permitted, the new row has diagonal p and uniform outgoing coefficients, encoding z_G=1 implies h=p. Outside rows inherit the uniform incoming coefficients. Store actual odd witnesses for each permitted h; reconstruct zero or stored witness as appropriate, in reverse contraction order. This proves BOTH inclusions of the exact projected support, and every original target-flat membership is unchanged by protection. Valid for arbitrary number of omitted flats; no claim to preserve multiplicities or counts. For symmetric internal Q[G] with constant diagonal alpha, sum local equations to obtain (alpha+h) parity=0; singleton proves odd iff h=alpha. This recovers the simple twin/group contraction.

Acquire candidate groups without a supplied partition: for each equal-target-signature pair, repeatedly add outside vertices that distinguish two current members in incoming or outgoing coefficients. Abort if target signatures conflict or group becomes whole. If a proper protected module contains the seed pair, closure remains inside it; this discovers a proper module whenever one exists. It does not guarantee discovery of an admitted/certifiable larger module when its minimal closure fails the local solver. Check exact group conditions. For each candidate query local odd support twice, using singleton, disjoint weak components, reciprocal-SCC span, or single unit split into reciprocal-SCC children. Odd linear functional nonzero on actual span iff some actual generator is odd; retain witness. If unknown, do not contract. Each accepted contraction strictly decreases dimension. This hybrid changes global domain using the local algebraic split rather than running independent solvers side by side.
