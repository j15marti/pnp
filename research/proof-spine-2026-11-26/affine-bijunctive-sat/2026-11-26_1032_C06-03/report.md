## 2. Triple-token adapter and exact median localization
Construct a multiset E of indexed nonzero vector tokens: for rank-two factor f take three distinct tokens with vectors a,b,a+b; unary factor takes one token a. Identical vectors from different factors are parallel elements, not merged owner IDs. Let V_S be the span of token vectors on S. For any cut S|E-S define W_S=V_S intersect V_(E-S), lambda(S)=dim W_S. Root any subcubic leaf tree by subdividing an edge (trivial small cases separately). Place a rank-two factor at the unique median of its three leaves, and a unary factor at its leaf.

At any cut, if a factor median is on S side, at least two of its tokens lie in S; these span the whole factor plane. Thus a factor assigned to any rooted subtree is a genuine constraint on V_S, even if its third token is outside. At a rank-two median with incident branch spans V1,V2,V3, one token lies in each branch; since their vectors sum to zero, each lies in that branch's intersection with the other two. Hence all factor inputs can be read from incident boundary functionals, not hidden private variables. This handles arbitrarily many factors assigned to one median at cost polynomial in their explicit count. No need to force the three tokens to remain contiguous in the acquired decomposition.

## 3. Exact join and proof
For every rooted subtree S store all attainable linear functionals p:W_S -> GF(2), with one backpointer per admitted value. Attainable means there exists a linear functional on V_S satisfying precisely the factors whose assigned nodes lie in S. Store values on a computed basis only; an empty boundary has one possible state, not necessarily attainable. At leaves try both scalar values, check local unary factors and restrict to W_S.

Internal node with child token sets S1,S2 and exterior O: let V1,V2,V0 be their spans, B1=V1 intersect (V2+V0), B2=V2 intersect (V1+V0). For each pair of child states p1,p2, impose their basis evaluations as linear equations on an ambient functional. Reject if inconsistent; otherwise derive their unique combined functional on B1+B2 by Gaussian elimination. Check all median factors of this node using this combined functional. Indeed the two tokens in the children are in B1 and B2 (the third lies in O), and the third direction is their sum. The parent boundary W=(V1+V2) intersect V0 is contained in B1+B2: if w=v1+v2 in V0 then v1=w+v2 belongs to B1 and v2=w+v1 belongs to B2. Restrict to W and admit that state. The artificial degree-two root has no rank-two median.

Soundness: pick actual child extensions on V1,V2 promised by the admitted states. They agree on V1 intersect V2 since this intersection lies in B1 and B2 and joint equations were consistent. Their unique linear sum extension to V1+V2 therefore exists, satisfies all child factors and the checked median factors, and has the recorded parent restriction. Completeness: any genuine functional restricts to child states, jointly satisfies all the equations and median factors and is retained at the parent. At root, acceptance iff the empty state is attained. Follow backpointers to recover leaf-vector evaluations, solve these linear equations to extend to the full phase space (free directions not represented by E can be set arbitrarily), undo Gaussian parameterization, and verify all original rows and guards. This also gives a contradiction proof: a missed global witness would restrict to admissible states at every node, contradicting the rejected root. The audit independently brute-checks the full attained state set at every node, not just final labels. Do not replace nonaffine support by its span.

## 5. Source-native cut identity (second algebraic method)
Before adding nonlocal guard rows or dropping factor planes, take native parity matrix A with each clause row indexed. Partition clauses into L,R. Let X_L, X_R be the variable-ID sets incident to respective sides, S=X_L intersect X_R. In the free-space dual identify direction space with Q=GF(2)^n / row(A), with ell_i the class of unit vector e_i (this is canonically isomorphic to restrictions of coordinate forms to ker A). Because clause parity gives ell_i+ell_j+ell_k=0, full native clause plane span is exactly span of its three directions. Write P_L,P_R for image spans of X_L,X_R. Then

P_L intersect P_R = span{ell_i : i in S}.

Inclusion from right immediate. For reverse, if image(a)=image(b) with a supported in X_L, b in X_R, then a+b=r_L+r_R with r_L in span of left rows and r_R in span of right rows. Thus a+r_L=b+r_R is supported in both X_L and X_R, hence in S, with the same quotient image. This proves exact intersection, not just an upper bound on rank. Local guard rows supported on their side can be incorporated identically. After removing tautological/irrelevant factor planes the cut width can only decrease for that fixed coordinate chart. Extra nonlocal parity guard rows may change the quotient; use perturbation bound below, not the unguarded identity blindly. In particular, the relevant interface can be much smaller than the physical separator cardinality. Grouping clause tokens according to any clause tree with maximum boundary-direction rank w gives a token tree of width <=max(w,2), by expanding each factor leaf into its at most three tokens. This establishes a precise structural parameter on native input; finding all-input logarithmic cuts remains open.
