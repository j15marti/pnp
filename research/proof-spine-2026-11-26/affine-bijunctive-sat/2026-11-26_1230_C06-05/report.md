## Track A: exact affine-to-signed-cut compilation
Gaussian elimination either detects inconsistency or supplies x0 and a full row rank K with row span ker B. Recognize graphicness of K by Bixby-Wagner graph realization on the basis-normalized binary matrix. Construct graph incidence D indexed by the same coordinates. Verify row span(D)=row span(K) by Gaussian elimination. Binary graphic representations have identical dependence space after normalization: a basis becomes I and every nonbasis column is the indicator of its fundamental cycle's tree path; loops are zero columns. Thus all and only affine witnesses are x=x0+D^T p, p a vector of vertex bits. With weights c_e >=0, objective is exactly sum c_e [p_u XOR p_v != x0_e] (ordinary integer sum). Each graph phase produces an actual affine witness and every affine witness has a phase preimage. This is a cut-space chart, not an affine hull of nonlinear satisfying assignments. The source occupancy threshold is applied to the exact optimum. Nonnegative weights have polynomial bit length.

Loops contribute c_e x0_e independent of phases. For parallel edges on u,v, let W0,W1 be sums of weights preferring XOR 0,1. Their aggregate penalty at phase XOR z is W_(1-z). Replace by constant min(W0,W1) plus one edge of weight |W0-W1| preferring the heavier side (drop zero). This is pointwise equality; reconstruct every original coordinate from the original D and x0, not the reduced graph. Split components after aggregation, optimize independently, sum constants and minima. Choosing a different affine reference only switches signs by a vertex gauge, leaving balance deletion size invariant for fixed graph and weights. This can remove entire conflicting seams without any branching.

## Track B: exact acquisition by signed iterative compression
For signed edges target b_e, balance means there is a gauge g with g_u XOR g_v=b_e on retained edges. BFS propagates and detects inconsistent signed cycles. Given a deletion set S of size h+1, enumerate 3^|S| possibilities assigning each s either deleted, phase 0, or phase 1. Let F be deleted vertices of S, P the retained pinned ones. Reject a choice if |F|>h or a retained S-S edge conflicts. G-S is balanced by premise; compute gauge g there. In switched coordinates q_v=p_v XOR g_v all its edges require equality. Each edge from a pinned vertex in P to v in G-S forces q_v to a known bit. Let A0,A1 be vertices forced 0,1. An extension exists after deleting Z in G-S exactly when no surviving connected component intersects both A0 and A1. Overlap A0∩A1 requires deleting that vertex, not treating it as an impossible terminal.

Find minimum such Z by directed vertex-split mincut: each v has vin->vout capacity 1, adjacency arcs vout->uin and reverse with capacity M=|V|+1; source->vin for A0 and vout->sink for A1 capacity M. If a separator exists of size <=h-|F| the cut returns it by the crossing split edges. Take h<=|V|, otherwise delete all directly. Infinite arcs cannot cross a feasible cut of capacity <=h. Conversely any separator gives a cut of capacity at most its size, by reachability after its split arcs removed. Every surviving component can be assigned the forced bit or arbitrary bit if unforced. Undo gauge. This proves the exact compression criterion both constructively and by contradiction: a valid smaller solution has some enumerated intersection and pin pattern, and its remaining deletion is a separator, so a minimum cut cannot exceed its budget. No guess of unacquired structure.

Acquire from scratch by adding vertices one by one. An old valid deletion set plus the new vertex is a valid set for the next prefix. If size exceeds h, compress as above. Failure on any induced prefix proves no size-h deletion of the entire graph, but NOT UNSAT of original source. A BFS check can avoid unnecessary compression. Total O(|V|3^(h+1)) mincut calls plus polynomial graph and gauge work. All choices processed sequentially. Use polynomial bit mincut (e.g. Edmonds-Karp). This is a direct signed adaptation of Reed-Smith-Vetta iterative compression, not reliance on a randomized OCT kernel. A separate derivation using subdivision of equality edges into two inequality edges also reduces signed balance deletion to OCT: replace any deleted subdivision vertex with one original endpoint, at no greater size. The direct method handles terminal overlap explicitly.

## Track C: exact objective after acquired deletion
For every one of 2^|S| assignments on the acquired deleted vertices, gauge G-S. Edges within it now impose equality with nonnegative disagreement weights. Edges to pinned vertices supply unary costs for q_v=0/1. Edges within S contribute a constant. Standard s-t cut with source-side q=0, sink-side q=1: bidirectional arcs w for equality edges, source->v with capacity unary cost for q=1, v->sink with capacity unary cost for q=0. Thus every cut is an actual phase assignment with exactly that objective, and every assignment yields a cut of that cost. Take minimum across all pin words, undo gauge, evaluate original coordinate IDs and verify original affine rows and objective. The min over words is disjunctive, not addition of alternate solutions. Independent lower bound: any global optimum induces one enumerated word and one cut, so no returned value can exceed it; every returned candidate is feasible, so cannot be smaller. Exact nonlinear decision follows by previous threshold.
