## A constructive saturation theorem and exact lift

If z=1 and d_i=1 for some i, select b_i=1 only, take a_i=0 and a_j=d_j XOR [j=i+1] for j!=i. All local a,b compatible. For d=0 the equations imply a_i+b_i=b_(i-1) over GF2, and b_i=1 forces b_(i-1)=1. Thus b is constant around the ring; b=0 gives z=0, b=1 gives z=m mod2. Both lift with a=0. Therefore the full attained support is GF(2)^(m+1) for odd m, and exactly GF(2)^(m+1) minus {h} for even m. In particular rank m+1>m, so full Rado transversal certificate impossible, even though odd ring saturates. No replacement of support by hull. All d=0,z=1 even jobs are genuine guarded NO, affine-hull feasible. No count claim.

## C adversarial reconstruction: no proper group has a period
Let S_K=sum_{i in K} S_i for proper nonempty K. Induct on |K|. Select start i in K whose predecessor not in K (exists since proper cycle subset); only S_i contributes coordinate e_i. Thus the e_i=0 slice is T=S_(K-i). Slice e_i=1 is (e_i+T) union (e_i+e_(i+1)+h+T). Inductively T has trivial stabilizer (base T={0}). Two translated copies are distinct, so slice 1 strictly larger than slice 0. Any period must have e_i bit zero, preserve slice 0, hence belong to stabilizer of T and equal zero. Thus ALL proper sums, including arbitrarily large ones, have no nonzero translational period. Every nonempty proper group spans nonzero space, so none is full affine support. Even full support also has trivial period when m even, since exactly one point missing. Odd m full support has rank m+1 and saturates only globally. Exact Rado+period algorithms on ANY partition into proper groups cannot take a nontrivial saturation/period step: a Rado basis would force an affine support in a proper union, impossible; full union rank m+1 exceeds group count <=m. Further every group K has at least 2^|K| attained words, by choosing only 0/e_i. Any binary materializing merge tree of all m blocks has a root child of size >=m/2, hence at least 2^(m/2) rows; no fixed-polynomial explicit-table cap suffices along this family (even sparse encoding N=poly(m)). This is only a falsifier of restricted period/Rado plus explicit joins, not compact DP or all deciders. It also refutes necessity of any bounded-order exact affine saturation in these charts. ## SCC syndrome-walk lemma
For any satisfiable 2CNF H, condensation of literal implication graph gives complementary SCC pairs. A satisfying word selects one true SCC per pair and is closed along implications. Between any two actual satisfying words x,y, choose a sink among SCCs true in y and false in x in the induced DAG. Its outgoing neighbors are already true in x: all are true in y, and no outgoing differing SCC by sink choice. Set this SCC true and its complementary SCC false. No new violated implication from remaining true SCC into newly false SCC, because contraposition would force a false complement among outgoing neighbors. No edge to own complement can occur with chosen SCC true in y. New assignment is satisfying and one pair closer to y. Repeat at most n times. Hence solution graph under complementary SCC flips connected.

Let Lx be arbitrary k-bit syndrome, and w_P be XOR of columns L_i for variables represented by pair P. An SCC flip changes syndrome by w_P. Pair P flexible iff both phases are attainable; from one base witness, test opposite phase by one 2SAT solve with unit pin. All varying pairs acquire in polynomial time. Every solution syndrome differs from base by sum of flexible pair labels; conversely for each flexible P a path to opposite phase includes one flip of P, yielding two actual witness syndromes differing by w_P. Therefore attained-syndrome affine hull is EXACTLY base+span{w_P: flexible}. This is polynomial acquisition of hull for arbitrary k, not full support. For k=1 it gives exact membership: if base wrong and any flexible pair has label 1, construct path until parity flips; if none exists parity invariant and NO. O(n) 2SAT calls plus path construction O(n(n+e)) graph scans and polynomial bit costs. This independently reconstructs a special case of known Brakensiek–Gopi–Guruswami 2SAT-MODG (Prop 5.2); their general rank-k algorithm n^O(k) not polynomial for k growing logarithmically. No novel claim for one-parity tractability. For k>=2, H=not(x AND y), identity syndrome has attained support {00,01,10}, hull all four; arbitrary hull membership is false. No counting, no unrestricted implication. For binary quotient with arbitrary guards, only exact tested unit/implication closure imported.
