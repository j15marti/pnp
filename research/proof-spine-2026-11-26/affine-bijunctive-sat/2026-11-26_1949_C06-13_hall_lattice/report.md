## All-clique LP domination

P = {0<=x<=1, Ax=1, sum_{v in K} x_v<=1 for every acquired clique K}, with additional certified linear constraints. Every Boolean solution lies in P. Consider any admitted chart of exactly-one groups and slots partitioning choice occurrences, whose capacity inequalities are valid rows of P (no duplicate use of a variable within a slot unless coefficient accounting explicitly certified). A point of P maps to a fractional left-saturating matching by giving each chart edge the corresponding choice value; repeated variables in different groups are copied with the same value. Hall deficit U gives |U| <= |Gamma(U)|, contradiction. More generally bipartite matching integrality implies an unsupported edge has zero weight in every fractional matching, and mandatory slot is occupied with weight one. Thus optimizing each variable and each acquired clique sum over P subsumes all such matching pin and mandatory-slot deductions without enumerating partitions. Parallel-edge slots may be handled with separate choice edges. Rational LP is polynomial in input bit length. Preserve actual discrete constraints; no claim for semantic slots outside enumerated cliques or unbounded-degree guarded quotients. This is a genuine acquisition bypass for a specified certificate class.
