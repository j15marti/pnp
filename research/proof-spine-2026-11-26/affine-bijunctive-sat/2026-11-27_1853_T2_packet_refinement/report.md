## 1. Scoped theorem: fixed-dictionary independent correction credit

Verify both exact local coverage and absence of allowed words in G by exhaustive local evaluation. Then F is equivalent to the conjunction of all g=0. Outer carrier O is necessary unary/binary projections and local affine identities; retain exact source, not just O. Use a fixed dictionary K, no resets. At epoch t let W_t be span of active correction indicators of degree <=d, with d fixed; original source satisfies all. If a candidate x satisfies O and every active packet, yet fails source, some missing packet g evaluates to 1 at x. Since every w in W_t evaluates to 0 at x, g notin W_t. Thus adding g gives strict independent rank increase and decreases Phi_aff=D_d(r)-dim W_t+r (D_d=sum_{j<=d} binom(r,j)); adding all violated packets also gives at least one rank increase. Candidate is an explicit independence witness, independently checked in polynomial time under fixed d and bounded chart. For a fixed explicit dictionary of K packets there are at most min(K,D_d(r)) successful nonterminal epochs; if exact source SAT check passes, verify source reconstruction; if checked outer contradiction, sound NO. ## 2. Cross-scope cancellation identity
