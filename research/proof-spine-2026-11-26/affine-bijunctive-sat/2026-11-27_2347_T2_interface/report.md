(i) a degree<=d Boolean polynomial h is identically 0 on L and 1 on R;
(ii) affine hulls of v_d(L), v_d(R) are disjoint;
(iii) the system v_d(w) dot c = 0 for L and =1 for R is consistent.
Proof: (i)=(iii) by evaluation. If hulls intersect, applying h to their common affine combination yields 0=1. If inconsistent, Gaussian elimination gives a dependency XOR of rows with zero feature vector and RHS 1. Constant coordinate forces even total cardinality, RHS forces odd right cardinality, hence odd left cardinality. These are affine combinations witnessing intersection. Empty regional support is a separate terminal. A dependency with <=D+1 rows exists by selecting independent rows and one dependent inconsistent row.
