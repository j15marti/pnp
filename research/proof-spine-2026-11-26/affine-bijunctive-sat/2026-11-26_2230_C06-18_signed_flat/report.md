## A. Exact affine-interface counting theorem
For region S use W_S and interface I_S from C06-17. Instead of the attained support table, define f_S(b) to be the NUMBER of functionals on W_S that avoid every owned defect and restrict to b on I_S. This is nonnegative integer valued. Represent it by a signed dictionary f_S=sum_j c_j 1_{A_j}, where A_j are affine flats in I_S^* given by canonical reduced binary augmented row systems, c_j are signed integers. This is an equality of functions on the finite Boolean domain, not formal polynomial equality over GF(2). Intermediate terms may overlap and have negative signs; their sum is the exact count. At a leaf the allowed functional set is all W_i^* except the one forbidden functional; use the difference of these two indicators, then project to I_i^*.
At a join with children A,B, let L be the linear space of pairs (b_A,b_B) agreeing on W_A intersect W_B. The parent map p:L->I_P^* exists by I_P subset I_A+I_B and is well defined by agreement. For each compatible pair there are exactly f_A(b_A) f_B(b_B) distinct glued functionals on W_P; no two different pairs or different child functionals give the same parent functional. Thus f_P(b)=sum_{v in L:p(v)=b} f_A(v_A) f_B(v_B). For each term pair intersect its inverse images with L (Gaussian elimination), obtaining an affine set C or empty. An affine map on a nonempty affine C has constant fiber cardinality 2^(dim C-dim p(C)) on its image. Replace the pair by coefficient c_i c_j times that power of two on the affine flat p(C). Canonicalize by full affine RREF, sum equal-flat coefficients, remove zero coefficients. This is exact including arbitrary deficiency and asymmetric interactions, and all joint attainable behavior is encoded by positive count. Root I_J=0, so the exact total is zero iff UNSAT. No duplicate multiplication of counts from integrating over hidden W_P instead of L. Coordinate variable elimination is the same construction on a chart with explicit scope: multiply signed flats by intersecting, sum out a variable by projecting and multiplying by 2 only when that coordinate is unconstrained inside the term. Include every variable exactly once. Any explicit affine guard is an indicator factor or an equivalent quotient, never dropped.
If every intermediate dictionary and raw candidate pair count (including failed/zero candidates) is bounded by fixed poly(N), this terminal is polynomial even at large interface rank. It needs a verified schedule and any rewrites actually acquired in that budget. It is not a general symbolic counting oracle. Without rewrites, total absolute weight grows at most 2^O(N^2) for a tree of O(N) nodes with each fiber exponent O(N); coefficients therefore O(N^2) bits. With verified rewrites charge their actual written bit lengths and cap those too. With P total charged term operations, dense rank/project and deterministic sorted maps give poly(N,P) bit work and space; cumulative memory <= time*peak. Witness retrieval when positive is possible on retained exact summaries by prefix conditioning compatible child-pair count functions and descending; not necessary for NO. Do not infer decision lower bounds from dictionary size.

## B. Semantic equality and guard recycling (hybrid interface)
For any signed dictionary F and affine query G, M_F(G)=sum_j c_j |A_j intersect G| is acquired by polynomially many Gaussian ranks. If F>=0 pointwise (as certified by exact count construction), M_F(G)=0 iff G meets no attained support. Without nonnegativity, use the following unconditional semantic test:
F=H on G iff sum_{x in G}(F(x)-H(x))^2=0.
The latter is an integer double sum over pairwise affine intersections, polynomial in supplied dictionary sizes, bit length and dimension. This verifies a candidate compression or merge WITHOUT enumerating its wide boundary; it does not discover a short candidate for free. A signed function 1_{x=0}-1_{x=1} has zero mass but is nonzero, so arbitrary signed total-zero cannot certify equality. Similarly arithmetic mod2 can erase even positive counts; this method uses exact integers, not GF2 counts.
Given nonnegative F, positive query mass yields an actual boundary point by greedy coordinate pins with positive mass. Start with one attained point, form the annihilator of the span of differences to acquired points, test each independent annihilator equation's opposite affine halfspace. Any positive mass supplies a new actual point outside that span. At most d rank increases, <=O(d^2) mass queries with greedy extraction and fresh elimination suffice to acquire a spanning actual basis (or certify empty). The resulting affine equations are all and only the affine identities of the actual support; this is necessary information, NOT a replacement for the count function. These are sound unconditional guard imports from a contracted region.
For an imported normal space E subset I_S, the C06-17 quotient identity gives a decrease of exactly dim E at the S|outside cut. For any other cut not splitting S, E is contained in one side's normal space U, so lambda'=lambda-dim(E intersect V)<=lambda. Within S cut width may increase, so do not assert monotonicity there. Keep contracted S as its exact count factor restricted/reparameterized to the acquired guard, rather than re-enumerate its interior. This is a scoped guard-width recycling theorem, connecting C05-16 observation feedback and C02-19 contextual period work through the verified affine-count representation; no cross-source matching adapter claimed. Semantic support equality is not the same as count equality, which is sufficient but may be stronger than required.

## C. High-interface guarded native NO family and three method replicas
Two input blocks of k bits each. On each side, two AND chains compute the two block products, in forward order on side A and reverse on side B. Internal wires distinct; four output bits a,b,a',b'. Explicit guard a+b+a'+b'=1 mod2. By induction a=a', b=b', hence NO. The guard couples the two regions but is not an unconditionally derived native clause; preserve it exactly. Every gate z=xy has two disjoint rank2 bad flats: (z=1,x=0) and (x=1,y+z=1). Check all eight words. For each defect (u=p,v=q), native prism atom has six cyclic triples on doubled observations (1+u+p,1+v+q,1+u+v+p+q). All native clauses have parity1 and are exact-one iff the bad event is avoided. Anchor prism for each chart variable consists of doubled (x,1+x,0); its clauses always satisfied, and explicit guards uniquely express all other original variables. Include original IDs, native clauses and guard rows, exact obs expressions; incidence cubic, planar verified with NetworkX. The four-output odd guard is a separate exact affine row. N is actual original encoding, not just interface rank. Before guard there are 6k-4 chart coordinates, after guard d=6k-5; native variables 6(6k-4+8(k-1))=84k-72. For the cut separating the two gate regions, their pre-guard normal spaces span all their respective wires and share precisely the 2k input normals. The guard normal is in their sum but neither side. Quotient formula gives cut width 2k+1. Anchor events are empty. This is a specified cut, not an optimal branch-width lower bound; other decompositions and the structural invariant bypass it.
