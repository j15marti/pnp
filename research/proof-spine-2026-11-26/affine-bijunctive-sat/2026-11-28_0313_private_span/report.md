## Simultaneous exact projection theorem
After signed equality union-find and B4 parity/difference closure, parameterize the affine carrier by free coordinates. Every remaining constraint is a predicate on affine forms. Partition forms into blocks so each constraint lies in one block. Let L_i span the linear parts in block i, S_i=L_i intersect sum_{j!=i} L_j, P_i a linear complement of S_i in L_i, and W=sum_i S_i. Then sum_i L_i = W direct sum (direct sum_i P_i). In any relation w+sum_i p_i=0, isolate p_i. All other terms including W lie in the outside span for i (S_i by definition), so p_i belongs to S_i intersect P_i=0. Thus all private terms vanish, then w=0. Spanning follows by local decomposition.

For each block enumerate exactly those local words consistent with its affine dependencies and predicates; project onto a basis of S_i with affine offsets retained. Store one representative per allowed image. A global assignment to W satisfies all projected tables iff the original system is feasible. Necessity by restriction. Sufficiency: chosen local representatives determine linear functionals on L_i agreeing with the global functional on S_i; the direct sum lemma combines them simultaneously; extend to ambient coordinates and solve original carrier equations, then lift every native port. No marginal-independence assumption. Active dimension decreases by sum_i(dim L_i-dim S_i). Fixed t<=c log2(N+2) local forms per block makes enumeration, GE, projection, verification and reconstruction polynomial in original N, for fixed c. Straightforward dense arithmetic/scans give a conservative per-stage O(N^8 2^t) bit bound; not a measured clock. No assumption of universal positive private rank or small acquired blocks.

## Permanent rank-or-clause budget and exact terminals
In a fixed shared-coordinate chart, keep independent affine rows, fixed form labels and permanent admitted implication arcs. Learn all local affine equations and missing binary patterns from exact filtered support, add affine-equivalence/complement implications, and extract forced units/equalities via reachability and SCC. These deductions are sound. Let d denote active affine dimension, m<=M0 a fixed polynomial original-N bound on simultaneous form labels, M=4M0^2, and a the number of admitted arcs in the chart. Global potential Phi=d(M+1)+M-a. An arc admission lowers Phi; an independent restriction within the active span lowers d; exact private-span projection lowers d. A chart reset with strict d decrease can lose at most M arc admissions, still decreases Phi by at least one. Same-dimensional changes must transport labels/admissions or use a separately paid neutral-step budget. This is one global nonrenewable reserve, not a new polynomial allowance at each projection. Failed attempts need an independent fixed progress deadline; credit alone does not prove one.

A fixed point is not a decision. Exact terminals: inconsistent affine equations, empty table or contradictory implication SCC gives NO; verified native witness gives YES. If all filtered tables are affine and their complete affine hull is imposed, GE decides. If all exact tables and carrier are bijunctive (in common variables), or the bijunctive table forms are independent, 2SAT decides. If C is a vertex cover of the graph joining every pair of arguments of each table, enumerate assignments to C: each residual predicate has at most one remaining argument, so yields unary affine restrictions or a contradiction; GE decides each case and reconstructs. This is polynomial for |C|<=c log N; a greedy maximal matching supplies a 2-approximate cover. Logarithmic active rank is another exact terminal. The implemented rank terminal uses cap12; general cover and affine-only recognition were not separately implemented.
