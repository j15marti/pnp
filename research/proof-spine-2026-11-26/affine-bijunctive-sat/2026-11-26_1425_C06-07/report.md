## A. Constructive exact algorithm
Project each actual local row through L_i, the original columns on that block, preserving one preimage per image. Choose actual base s_i in the nonempty projected support S_i. Let U_i=S_i+s_i, which contains 0, and let t=d+sum s_i. Feasibility iff t belongs to sum U_i. For K subset of block IDs let W_K=span union U_i over i in K and r(K)=dim W_K.
Form a labeled ground set (i,u), u in U_i minus zero. Matroid 1 is binary linear independence of vectors u; matroid 2 is at most one element from each block i. Edmonds' finite matroid intersection theorem and augmenting algorithm give maximum common independent set size
t_K=min_{H subset K} (r(H)+|K-H|).
Derivation: apply rank minmax to the labeled ground set. In a minimizing partition, move all elements of a block into the linear-rank side whenever all of its elements are already there; conversely if the block contributes to the partition-rank side, remove its remaining elements from the linear-rank side, not increasing objective. Thus a minimizing partition can be chosen as a union of whole blocks, yielding the formula. Empty/zero blocks harmless (can put them on linear side for free).
If t_K=r(K), let M be the selected basis. Every w in W_K has a unique subset of M summing to w. In each selected block choose its actual alternative row when its vector occurs in the subset, otherwise choose the base row; in all other blocks choose base. Thus every w actually attained; reverse inclusion trivial. This is exact affine saturation by independent switches, not hull substitution. It proves decision and lift, not equal multiplicities or min-cost optimization.

## B. Global rank characterization and acquisition
Define f(K)=r(K)-|K|, submodular. Let J be the inclusion-wise largest minimizer over all block subsets. Minimizers are closed under union/intersection by submodularity. For H subset J, f(H)>=f(J), hence r(H)+|J-H|>=r(J). Matroid minmax gives t_J=r(J), so J saturates. For any K that saturates by independent transversal, f(H)>=f(K) for all H subset K. Submodularity gives f(J union K)<=f(J)+f(K)-f(J intersect K)<=f(J), so K subset J. In quotient by W_J, for every nonempty K disjoint J:
r_quot(K)-|K|=f(J union K)-f(J)>0 by maximality. Thus no remaining subset has a full independent-transversal basis. This is an exact negative certificate for THIS operation only.

Acquire f_min=t_all-m from one ordinary matroid intersection. For each i, contract W_{i} by Gaussian reduction of all remaining columns; minimum f over subsets containing i is r({i})-1+t_remaining-(m-1), with t_remaining computed on those quotient supports. Include i in J iff this equals f_min. Union of those witnessed minimizers is the largest minimizer. A final intersection on J supplies the actual independent basis. If t_all=r(all), all blocks saturate and shortcut. Each query has explicit finite independence oracles. No submodular minimization oracle is assumed. ## Native infinite family and limits
A six-variable native atom has rows C_i=(i-1,i,i+1) modulo 6, a planar hexagonal-prism incidence graph. Subtract adjacent real occupancy equations: x_i=x_{i+3}; exactly one of x0,x1,x2 is 1. The three complete assignments (bit masks 9,18,36) are verified exhaustively on 64 words. Take m disjoint copies with original IDs 6j+i. Add explicit affine equality/disequality guards x_{j,0}+x_{j-1,1}=d_j (mod 2, cyclic indices); incidence graph of the original source remains native planar cubic, augmented guard graph is not claimed to have the native promise. Each block projects to U_j={0,e_j,e_{j+1}}, base residue2; actual local witnesses fixed. For m>=3 every individual support has odd cardinality 3, no nonzero translational stabilizer. Every distinct pair sum has cardinality 7 (planes share one standard basis vector) or 9 (disjoint), again odd, so no pair can have a nonzero period. Yet one independent attained vector e_j per block spans GF(2)^m. Thus every RHS d feasible with x_{j,0}=d_j, x_{j,1}=0, x_{j,2}=1-d_j and period-3 copies. Matroid method acquires this lift without enumerating any 2^m joint states. Direct simple source witness also exists; not claimed harder than other solver lanes. This family extends projected-period contraction by simultaneous independent switches, not only pair merging. On actual chart all 2^m syndromes attained; individual rows and pair sum counts do not suffice to infer this.

Adversarial raw-native obstruction (symbolic): split a native unguarded formula into one exact-one block per clause on separate incidence bits. Enforce equality of the three occurrences of each variable by two independent binary equations. For any nonempty set K of clause blocks, the span of their local differences before equality projection has dimension 2|K| (even parity of each triple). Kernel of equality projection on that space corresponds exactly to homogeneous parity solutions supported on original variables all three of whose occurrences lie in K. There are a<=|K| such variables by counting incidences. If a=0 kernel dimension0; if a>0 at least one nonzero parity row restricts them, so q_K<=a-1. Thus projected rank r(K)=2|K|-q_K>|K|.
