## Result and scope
For a CNF residual F=E(z) AND C(v,z), take the complete incident bucket C=AND_i(v OR A_i(z)) AND AND_j(!v OR B_j(z)). Define f(z)=AND_j B_j(z), with empty conjunction true. For every boundary assignment z, if f(z)=1, v=1 satisfies C; if f(z)=0, some negative clause forbids v=1, so a feasible fiber must have v=0. Hence C(f(z),z) iff exists v C(v,z). This is a constructive canonical Boolean section, not a forced equality where both values are allowed. If a subset of C explicitly defines v=f, every full solution satisfies that equality; in that case exact substitution into every exterior consumer of v is legal. Without uniqueness use all incident constraints or an explicit residual condition retaining the alternate fiber. For a single negative row f=B_1, substitution yields A_i OR B_1, exactly ordinary resolution elimination.

The CNF of an explicit AND gate v=AND_h l_h is (v OR !l_1 OR ... OR !l_k) plus each (!v OR l_h). Scan original rows for this pattern or its polarity dual. Substitute the small CNFs of f and !f into every incident row, retaining exterior rows and deleting only tautologies/duplicates. The reverse map evaluates f at a reconstructed boundary; it need not enumerate support. This is known preprocessing/Skolem logic, not a newly discovered general SAT algorithm.

Scoped accounting theorem: choose a fixed cap L=N^a for the entire explicit residual and map/certificate storage, never resurrect eliminated variables, and scan a deterministic polynomially bounded candidate set (all direct gates of degree <=3, all single-sided buckets, all pair resolvents). Charge each scan, normalization, rejection, replay, and output in the original N. At most n<=O(N) strict elimination events occur, with potential n minus number of eliminated original variables. Within the explicit cap even naive enumeration, candidate construction, deduplication and syntactic replay have fixed polynomial bit cost (variable labels O(log N); pair generation quadratic in row count; copying/sorting all output rows still polynomial). Reverse maps can be retained for each accepted event and evaluated in reverse order, with polynomial aggregate storage and verification. No branch or reset is covered. A stall is not a decision. Universal availability of a bounded-growth elimination or complementary terminal remains OPEN. Full affine span is not an obstruction to a nonlinear interior section.
