## Theorem: CONDITIONAL-NONLINEAR-RESPONSE-CREDIT (scoped saturation)
For any explicit bounded-width Boolean factors, with r original chart variables and m factors, let P be any sound local forced-bit closure, here repeatedly retain support rows compatible with current pins and force their unanimous coordinates. Soundness by induction. For unassigned pivot v, independently close contexts v=0 and v=1. Both contradictory means root contradiction. One contradictory means the other pivot value is forced. If a coordinate u is forced to x on v=0 and y on v=1, learn u=x when x=y and u xor v=x when x differs from y; the latter is two binary clauses. If only one context forces u, learn the corresponding binary implication guarded by that context (v OR u=x for v=0; NOT v OR u=y for v=1). If neither forces, acquire nothing. This does not assert that arbitrary consistent local assignments extend to actual solutions. Every added clause is true of all root solutions because the two cases exhaust pivot values, and unchanged factors still impose joint support. Reclose after each batch. Independent replay checks each clause against both closed contexts of the pre-batch database, using explicit row filtering and separate unit propagation. A contradiction closes the original gauged problem and hence the original source. Reverse bucket extension is by checking both pivot bits against all consumed factors, no free witness oracle.

At most 2r+4 binom(r,2)+1 distinct unary/binary/empty clauses, numeric polynomial. At most O(r^2) productive batches, <=2r closure calls per batch plus final no-progress sweep: O(r^3) calls. Each closure has <=r pin-increasing rounds, scanning O(m+r^2) factors of at most 2^w rows with w fixed (<=6 after admitted bucket elimination). Thus conservative O(r^4 (m+r^2) 2^w w) row/bit-inspection scale, with polynomial overhead for explicit O(log N)-bit IDs, comparison, storage and deterministic tape simulation; fixed polynomial in original N for admitted explicit bounded-width inputs. Initial bounded bucket normalization and checker are polynomial, since each pivot removes a variable, creates at most one factor and examines <=128 words; trace and reverse maps polynomial. One serial lane at a time, total work adds, no parallel span claim; storage polynomial, cumulative memory bounded by product of same execution time and peak storage. No universal progress guarantee: all eight sign clauses on three distinct variables are UNSAT but after any single pin the residual two-variable clauses force no bit by local support, so all single-bit probes stall. This is a general explicit-CNF control, not a native E/B lower bound or failure of GE/2SAT after a stronger transformation.
