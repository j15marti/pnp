The distinct feasible C are a linearly independent function basis for their span: evaluate on actual words C_i, order by increasing cardinality, matrix e_Cj(C_i) is triangular with ones on the diagonal. ## 3. Infinite simple-cubic parity separation for fixed-width-two RUP, and native cheap bypass

XOR of vertex equations is 0=1 because each edge occurs twice. With at most two initial signed edge assignments, unit propagation cannot contradict. If the edges are nonincident, every vertex has at most one assigned edge and there is no unit. If incident, the unique common vertex forces the third edge (parity gives exactly one continuation); all three other endpoints are distinct by simplicity, each has one assigned incident edge, and propagation stops. No starting units, no clause of size<=2 is RUP, so full width-two RUP saturation is at its initial fixed point.
