## A. Signed-pair distance and acquisition
For each coordinate x_i choose a Boolean flip r_i and write x_i=y_i XOR r_i. For source literal l on i define p_l(r)=1 iff it becomes positive, i.e. r_i=0 for positive l and r_i=1 for negative l. A clause C is Horn after the flip iff for every distinct pair l,m in C, (NOT p_l OR NOT p_m). Remove tautological clauses and repeated literals first; empty clauses immediately NO. Define S(F) to be the multiset of these 2-CNF sign constraints, indexed by original clause and pair. Let delta=min_r number of violated pair constraints, and k_H=min_r number of non-Horn source clauses. For width<=3, each non-Horn clause contributes 1 or 3 violated sign pairs, so k_H <= delta <=3 k_H. Exact sign recognition at delta=0 is the classical renamable-Horn reduction; this is a parameterized application to our projected chart, not a new claim about classical Horn SAT.

Multiplicity-safe conversion to ordinary Almost-2-SAT: for every occurrence of a sign clause (a OR b), create a distinct private z and two clauses (a OR z), (NOT z OR b). If the original pair is true some z satisfies both; if false at least one must be deleted, and deleting either one suffices. Thus a flip r violating <=k pair occurrences exists iff at most k clauses can be removed from the resulting ordinary, duplicate-free 2-CNF to make it satisfiable. Proof by restriction of any surviving satisfying assignment, and conversely choosing private z per occurrence. Apply Razgon-O'Sullivan deterministic Almost-2-SAT (arXiv:0801.1300v4, O(15^k k m^3) for m clauses, outputs deletion set). Then run SCC on survivor, restrict to r, and re-evaluate every original sign pair. Cap at k=ceil(c log2 N) for fixed c, charge all trials and bit encoding. If no such r, UNKNOWN from this terminal, not source UNSAT. In particular a source satisfying assignment is not a satisfying sign assignment and vice versa.

## B. Disjoint Horn branch theorem with exact tariff
For a fixed acquired r, let p_C be number of positive literals in flipped C. If p_C<=1 retain C. Otherwise order the positives l1,...,lp and partition by the first true positive among l1,...,l_{p-1}, or the case that all those p-1 positives are false. In a first-true branch impose the corresponding prefix-false/true units and drop C (satisfied). In the final branch impose prefix-false units and retain all negatives OR l_p, a Horn clause. The p branches are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive, with each equivalent to C under its guard. Cartesian product over bad clauses produces Horn formulas (including all retained good clauses and all guards as units). Horn least-model forward chaining decides each. All branch contradictions certify exact source NO, and any successful branch lifts through the flip and actual local witnesses. This is exact decision, not counting; Horn least model alone is not a count. Number of branches B=product_{C:p_C>=2} p_C <=2^{sum_C binom(p_C,2)}<=2^k (since p<=2^binom(p,2)). For width3 B=2^{b2}3^{b3}. Charge every candidate, including inconsistent/repeated branch guards; no free count oracle. Almost-2-SAT acquisition plus exhaustive Horn leaves takes (15^k k+2^k) poly(N) bit time, not N^k. When k<=c log N the entire procedure (on admitted paid regional representations) has one fixed polynomial exponent. Fixed k=0 admits ordinary Horn, but an UNSAT CNF without unit or empty clauses cannot be renamable Horn (all-zero assignment satisfies each Horn clause with at least one negative). Thus this lane is particularly useful for remaining non-Horn clauses after unit closure; renaming alone cannot refute that particular postclosure promise.

## C. Contextual decomposition and genuine hybrid
A regional table of size7 cannot be made bijunctive by any invertible affine transformation, has full affine hull and trivial period; nevertheless it can be Horn in the acquired coordinates. Projection can convert a nonlinear rank2-affine private gadget into a signed Horn clause, which changes the representation and allows the sign terminal. On an explicit shared-coordinate separator X of q bits, branch on all 2^q assignments, simplify exact CNF, form actual connected components of residual clause-variable incidence, and apply the above sign terminal separately to each component. For NO, each separator branch needs at least one sound refuted component; others can remain UNKNOWN. For complete decision on admitted executions, every component must be decided unless another refutes the branch. The total tariff is 2^q times the SUM, not product, of per-component costs (plus all separator/decomposition acquisition, projection, guard substitution, SCC, source verification and reconstruction); polynomial if q and every admitted delta are O(log N), even if their sum grows linearly. This holds without claiming that such a separator always exists or is polynomially acquirable. At a new affine guard, redo exact restriction and basis tests; arbitrary parity guards are not units. A guard cannot be omitted from a Horn leaf. Disjoint repetition of any unsatisfiable unit-free length>=2 core forces global delta at least number of copies, while componentwise terminal remains easy; add binary connectors for connected high global delta, still no hardness inference. General 3-CNF is encoded by the private rank2-defect gate below, so universal success of this fixed representation class cannot be presumed.
