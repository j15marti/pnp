## Scoped theorem: conditional affine join
In a verified exact residual F, let d explicitly selected bits form exhaustive disjoint guards. Under each guard, apply sound occupancy/unit closure, extract all affine equalities from each explicit allowed-row table, and solve their joint linear system over GF2. Each feasible guarded assignment lies in its affine relaxation A_g; an inconsistent relaxation refutes its guard. Empty union implies UNSAT. Otherwise take p from any nonempty A_g; the affine hull of the union is p+span(all directions in all A_g and all offsets p_g+p). Every equation annihilating this direction span, with RHS evaluated at p, is sound unconditionally in F; Gaussian elimination computes a basis. Verify a candidate by deriving it from the row span of every nonrefuted guard, independently checking local table masks, propagation and inconsistent guards. Charge discovery and verification. With d<=c log N, polynomially many guards and explicit bounded-arity factors, the procedure is polynomial in N for each split. A polynomially sized list of splits is also polynomial. Full all-pairs probing is polynomial but only the top10/45 pairs were tried here. On explicit tables of unbounded width, mask enumeration costs exponential in width; no succinct-table promise. If actual equation substitution increases factor width, retain explicit XOR or cap and charge the exact replacement. Independent rank gain contracts affine free dimension, but raw volume is not a polynomial potential. No universal theorem that some log-sized split yields rank gain. No all-decider lower bound.
