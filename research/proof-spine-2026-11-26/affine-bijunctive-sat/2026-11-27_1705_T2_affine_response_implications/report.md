## Theorem 1 (bounded joined affine response implication closure)
Given exact Boolean factor tables on bounded scopes, take each table and every pairwise exact natural join whose union scope has at most fixed k bits. For each nonzero linear form a on a scope, define the virtual bit y_a=a*x over F2, indexed by its original carrier mask. For every two forms (a,b), evaluate all allowed local words. Each absent pair (u,v) implies (y_a!=u OR y_b!=v). Thus add the two implication edges y_a=u -> y_b=1-v and y_b=v -> y_a=1-u. Both directions of a path between signed responses force a*x + b*x=u+v; a path to its own complement forces a*x=1-u. A strongly connected component containing both values of one form is contradictory. All edges have an exact original-table truth checker. Gaussian elimination on extracted equations detects additional contradictions or certified rank gain. Reapply GAC with the acquired equations, retaining original constraints. The implication graph is a relaxation, not an exact representation of arbitrary source feasibility: virtual response bits have linear dependencies not enforced by ordinary 2SAT. No model of the relaxation is claimed to lift unless checked.
For F factors, r carriers, maximum scope w=max(k, original explicit arity), there are O(F^2) joined tables and O(F^2 2^w) forms, O(F^2 4^w) candidate response pairs, O(F^2 8^w) local truth tests. SCC is linear in graph size; naive reachability for every response has polynomial cost, as does GE on r-bit vectors and independent path replay. For fixed w, or explicitly bounded w=O(log N) with all work charged, one pass is polynomial in original N under a polynomial representation promise. No need to enumerate all 2^r forms. A successful independent equation reduces affine free dimension by one; does not imply immediate scope contraction. Repeated acquisition has a polynomial rank-increase count only if the intermediate representation and costs remain polynomial, and if each nonterminal round produces new rank or other certified bounded credit decrease. ## Theorem 2 (exact pair-scope refusal for strict three-clauses)
A clause on three distinct variables excludes one word on its scope; in a larger union space of dimension n, its forbidden set is an affine codimension-three flat of size 2^(n-3). For two clauses with different three-variable scopes, let A,B be their forbidden flats. Any absent response pair for two independent linear forms would give a codimension-two flat H contained in A union B, of size 2^(n-2). Since |A|+|B|=|H|, equality requires disjoint A,B and H=A union B. Within H, A and B would be disjoint affine hyperplanes, hence parallel: distinct direction hyperplanes in a finite affine space intersect (solve two independent linear equations). But their directions are the kernels of distinct coordinate-scope restrictions and thus different, contradiction. Dependent forms yield only tautological incompatibilities or unary restrictions, and a unary forbidden fiber is even larger. A single strict three-clause cannot forbid a rank-two fiber by cardinality. For two distinct clauses on the SAME three-variable scope, their two forbidden points form a line, a codimension-two affine fiber. Its annihilator has rank two with three nonzero forms, giving three equivalent pair descriptions. This proves the exact limitation of this one/pair join response acquisition on strict 3CNF; it is not a SAT decision or lower bound.
