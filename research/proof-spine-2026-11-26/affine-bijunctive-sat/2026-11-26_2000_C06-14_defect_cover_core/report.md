## Exact defect-weight identity
For x satisfying Ax=1 mod2, each clause has weight1 or3. Let D count weight3 clauses. 3|x|=n+2D, hence SAT iff min |x|=n/3 over the affine chart. When 3 divides n, D is multiple of3. ## Minimum Rado core theorem and constructive acquisition
For each nonempty flat H_i, W_i is its normal space and c_i:W_i->F2 the linear functional giving RHS of its defining affine equations. For K subset I set r(K)=dim sum_{i in K} W_i and f(K)=r(K)-|K|. For ANY global minimizer J of f, the union covers iff union over J covers. More strongly every chart point avoiding J admits an extension with the same observations on W_J=sum_{i in J} W_i that avoids ALL flats.
Proof: for K subset I\J, f(J union K)>=f(J), hence dim of projected sum of W_i modulo W_J >= |K|. Use Rado on the projected bases (distinct labeled elements, linear and partition matroids) to choose one independent projected normal per outside group. Lift each selected vector a_i to its actual basis normal with certified RHS c_i(a_i). Impose a_i*t=1+c_i(a_i), along with the prescribed observations on W_J; independence modulo W_J ensures consistency. Reconstruct by Gaussian elimination, avoiding each outside flat and preserving every core observation. No marginal/affine-hull substitution. The projection may discard outside assignments but preserves decision and allows witness lift. Converse immediate. The minimal core J need not itself cover; if J empty then no cover.
Acquisition: t(I)=max size of independent partial transversal of normal bases = min_{K subset I}(r(K)+|I\K|); min f=t(I)-|I|. Rank is submodular; intersection of minimizers is a minimizer. The unique inclusion-wise smallest minimizer J consists exactly of indices i for which min f on I\{i} is strictly larger than min f on I. Thus m+1 ordinary matroid-intersection calls plus rank checks; one additional intersection on quotient bases obtains explicit independent extension certificate when desired. All input normal bases are size<=2. Canonicalizing full AFFINE equations (not just normals) is essential to remove true duplicates; provenance of all originals retained. Choosing largest minimizer instead can retain all spectators in the examples below.
Conditional universal shape: if F is UNSAT then min f<0 (otherwise empty is minimizer). If an affine cover is irredundant, I must be the unique minimizer, since every minimizer is a subcover; thus for each proper K, r(I)-r(K)<|I\K|. In particular r(I)<=m-1 and no event owns a unique normal dimension when m>1. This independently recovers Brouwer (1986) theorem and his proper-subcollection lemma, not claimed novel. Strict deficiency alone is NOT sufficient: H1={t1=0}, H2={t2=0}, H3={t1+t2=0} covers F2^2; change H3 RHS to1 and point11 escapes, same rank geometry.
## Tight deficiency-one next step
If smallest minimizer J has f=-1, every proper subcollection K of J has f(K)>=0 (else proper minimizer, contrary to minimality). Rado gives a constructive escaping assignment for every deletion of one group. Thus if J covers it is an irredundant cover, r(J)=|J|-1. These deletion witnesses are not a NO certificate: three distinct singleton flats in F2^2 leave one uncovered point, yet deletion witnesses can each be the removed point.
For any actual avoiding t, the normals a in W_i with a*t != c_i(a) span W_i: they form a nonzero affine coset of a hyperplane, whose differences span the kernel, plus one outside vector. In codim2 this is two of three nonzero normals; codim1 one. Therefore any independent-representative-capable fixed subcollection remains capable using actual violated normals at t. For deficiency-one J, fix any omitted owner j. SAT iff some independent transversal of nonzero normals of all other owners yields, after solving their opposite RHS equations, an observation word on W_J avoiding H_j. Forward: choose violated normals from t by Rado; reverse: the basis avoids all retained and the omitted flat by test. This removes the need to guess the omitted owner and establishes an exact one-basis orientation problem. Acquiring the successful basis without exponential exchange/search is OPEN. Rank tests alone cannot settle signs.
