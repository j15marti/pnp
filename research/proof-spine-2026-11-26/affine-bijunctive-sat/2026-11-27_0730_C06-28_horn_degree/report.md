## A. Constructive theorem
Let H be explicit Horn CNF on n variables; M its actual model family. If Horn propagation refutes H stop NO. On M use the GF2 basis e_C(X)=1[C subset X] for attained closed models C. Closure c(S) is least H model containing S, or inconsistent. Product e_C e_D=e_c(C union D), zero if inconsistent. Inclusion-ordered evaluation matrix has unit diagonal, so this is exact function algebra, not formal polynomial equality. Independently reconstruct from C06-27.

Define V_d=span of e_c(S) for |S|<=d, omitting inconsistent seeds. These are a subset of independent basis keys, including constant e_c(empty). Thus dim V_d=D_d<=sum_{j<=d} binom(n,j). Acquire by enumerating all seeds of size<=d+1 and running polynomial Horn propagation (or lazily acquire overflow keys after preacquiring V_d). Store canonical n-bit model masks. x_i V_d subset V_(d+1), since if C=c(S), c(C union {i})=c(S union {i}). Full closed model may have large cardinality; seed degree, not model cardinality, controls the bound.

Let ell_j=b_j+sum_i A_ji x_i be seam violation functions, zero precisely when corresponding seam holds. For d>=1, R_0=span(ell_j) subset V_d. Iterate
 R_(k+1) = (R_k + sum_i x_i R_k) intersect V_d
with exact GF2 row reduction in V_(d+1). To acquire intersection, give all overflow keys larger pivot IDs than V_d; insert basis vectors of R_k and x_i times each basis vector. Gaussian elimination with high pivots first yields basis for intersection from precisely the low-pivot rows. This allows cancellations across different products, including cross-variable cancellations. Do NOT truncate away terms before cancellation. A genuine hybrid: Horn canonical multiplication changes the domain of linear algebra; dynamically acquired vanishing identities change the next round's row space. All rows vanish on the exact joint models, by induction. If 1 lies in R_k, NO. If fixed point without 1, UNKNOWN, never SAT or UNSAT. At most D_d strict rank increases; no enumeration of 2^rank seam products or actual Horn assignments. The fixed point is exactly least subspace of V_d containing seam violations and closed under this combined multiply/cancel rule, by monotonicity and induction. The algorithm pays all attempted products, including failed high-degree rows.

## B. Complementary proof and integration
Let Q_J=prod_{j in J}(1+ell_j), the exact seam acceptance indicator. Define P_J=1+Q_J. For |J|<=d, P_J is in saturated R: P_empty=0, P_(J+j)=P_J+ell_j+P_J ell_j; latter multiplication is a linear combination of P_J and x_i P_J, and resulting degree<=|J|+1<=d when used. If the conditional subsystem is NO, P_J=1 on M. If affine function a vanishes on H and seams J, a Q_J=0, so a=a P_J. The latter is a linear combination of P_J and x_i P_J, and the result is in V_1, so falls into R by the combined cancellation rule (even if intermediate seed-degree d+1). Thus for d>=t, saturation subsumes C06-27 fixed-t conditional affine feedback, including repeated imports: any previously acquired affine row is in R and can participate in the same induction. This is a mathematical inclusion of derivation systems, not a measured speed improvement. Exact joint support never replaced with hull. At d=n, V_n is the entire function algebra; closure under coordinate multiplication gives the ideal generated by violations. For UNSAT, P_all=1 gives refutation. That class is exponential; no fixed degree bound follows.

At a nonrefuting fixed point E=R+sum_i x_i R satisfies E intersect V_d=R. Since 1 notin R, there exists a linear functional on V_(d+1) with L(1)=1 and L(E)=0 (extend a basis of E by 1, then to ambient). This is a polynomially checkable obstruction to this specific saturation step, not a real model or nonnegative probability. It can be acquired by Gaussian elimination, not executed in this run.

Native degree bridge: when H consists solely of negative binary exclusions, the Horn model sets are independent sets of a conflict graph. Every admissible seed is itself closed. e_C is ordinary squarefree monomial indexed by independent C. In standard Boolean polynomial calculus over GF2 with seam linear axioms, conflict quadratic axioms and x_i^2+x_i axioms, every R_d step lifts to degree<=d+1: multiply each row by variable, then remove conflicting monomials via a conflict quadratic times remaining variables, and repeated-variable monomials via Boolean axioms; each reduction has degree<=d+1. Combine the cleaned rows to cancel overflow, as recorded. Conversely a PC derivation of degree<=d projects to R_d by induction on axioms, additions and multiplications, since all projected intermediates are in V_d and the other axioms become zero. Therefore for native negative-binary charts, if minimum standard PC refutation degree is D_PC, successful saturation degree d implies D_PC<=d+1, and PC degree<=d implies saturation success. ## C. Adversarial falsifier, same-source proof
On the exact archived guarded core, three disjoint groups A,B,C each have odd-parity rows. Every triple selecting one from each group is forbidden by H. Independently prove NO by choosing one true per group. Static degree-two products of original seam rows do not refute in the executed cases. Dynamic d=2 gets the contradiction symbolically: for each c in C, multiply ell_A by x_c (degree2), then by each variable of B. Every triple term x_a x_b x_c vanishes in the Horn quotient, leaving the necessary products x_b x_c. Sum those products with x_c ell_B to derive x_c=0. Sum with ell_C to derive 1. This yields a two-round derivation on unchanged source guards, without expanding spectator seam products. In the initial source representation the earlier quadratic/SCC closure already refutes every archived case, so no claim of new postclosure-hard cases or exclusive solver coverage.

General obstruction for every fixed d: take d+2 disjoint pairs, each odd parity, and negative Horn clauses forbidding all 2^(d+2) selections of one variable from each pair. All selections forbidden => NO. Every subset of at most d+1 variables is H-admissible and closed. Therefore V_(d+1) and its multiplication from V_d are identical to those on unconstrained Boolean cube. On the latter the affine seams have a witness; evaluation at that word annihilates every R round and sends 1 to1. Thus saturation at d provably stalls on these NOs.
