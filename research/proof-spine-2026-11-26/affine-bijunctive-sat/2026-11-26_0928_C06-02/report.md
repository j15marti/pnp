## Proof 1: independent small-seed reconstruction
Use SCC 2SAT to acquire a model w of H, reject if none. Rename x=w XOR y; all zero now satisfies H. Normalize to positive implications i->j, negative pair exclusions and negative pins. Row reduce A and reject inconsistent equations, translate target t=b XOR Aw in independent coordinates; a_i is column i. A model's true set is forward closed and violates no prohibition. Acquire every singleton forward closure C_i, discarding those violating H. Union of any set of feasible closures is implication closed and respects unary pins; it is feasible iff every pair of closures is compatible (all remaining prohibitions binary). This also gives certified unconditional forced zero for seeds with infeasible closures; preserve guard context. Can rerun after any paid source/linear pin and SCC probes; do not claim complete semantic forced-literal closure.

For any attainable t choose a minimum-cardinality true set U attaining t. Condense positive implication SCCs, so U is an upward-closed union of SCCs. Let B be its minimal SCCs in reachability order (no incoming from another SCC in U). Their column-sum weights are linearly independent: otherwise remove all source IDs of a nonempty dependent subset of B. No retained SCC implies one removed; no negative prohibition becomes false upon deletion; syndrome unchanged, strict reduction of cardinality, contradiction. Thus |B|<=r. Selecting one original ID per minimal SCC gives U as union of at most r actual singleton closures. This independently reconstructs C06-01's polynomial-indicator/triangular argument, without treating polynomial functions as formal identities.

## Proof 2: oriented conflicts and exact overlap separation
On feasible seed IDs build two undirected graphs F and O. F has edge ij iff C_i union C_j violates H. O has edge ij iff C_i intersect C_j contains an original y-coordinate with a_i nonzero (use the coordinate's own column, not seed's column). Shared coordinates with zero column may be ignored for syndrome accounting but not feasibility. Retain all original IDs. F and O may share edges. Compute degeneracy d of F by repeated minimum-current-degree removal, orient each edge earlier->later; outdegree <=d. Compute D=max degree of O. Both graphs and orientation acquired in poly time by scans.

For a red/blue coloring of feasible seeds, retain red vertices that have no outgoing F-edge to ANY red vertex (including vertices later discarded). The retained set K is F-independent: any retained edge's tail would have been discarded. Compute connected components of O[K]. For each component J define actual true-set U_J=union_{i in J} C_i and label s_J=XOR_{x in U_J} a_x. All U_J feasible; any subset of them is jointly feasible, since all retained seeds pairwise F-compatible. Distinct components share only zero-column original coordinates; for any chosen set L, label(union_{J in L} U_J)=XOR_{J in L} s_J. This last identity holds for arbitrary multiplicity of zero labels. It does not rely on pairwise intersections merely summing to zero.

For each coloring solve the linear system with columns s_J and target t by Gaussian elimination. This uses the span of a family proven to realize EVERY subset sum, not the affine hull of arbitrary syndrome support. Reconstruct selected component union (ordinary union, NOT XOR of original assignments), undo w and check H, original rows and chart lift. Per-color work is polynomial even in r; no enumeration of 2^r syndromes needed. If all colors fail, reject only after full covering family has been processed.

Completeness: take the <=r seed set S from Proof1. S is F-independent. Prescribe S red, every out-neighbor of S in F blue, and every O-neighbor of S outside S blue. These sets are disjoint, total at most k=min(number of seeds, r(1+d+D)). A covering family for k prescribed bits contains such a coloring. Every S seed is retained, and S is a union of whole components of O[K], because no red outside O-neighbor exists. Selecting exactly those components attains t. Thus every genuine solution is captured and all generated witnesses are genuine. This is a constructive proof; assuming a missed attainable target directly contradicts the covering property and Gaussian exactness.

Cover acquisition, independently restated from C06-01: n0 seeds, m=k+ceil(log2(max(1,n0)))+1, q=2^m. Construct GF(q) by enumerating monic degree-m polynomials and trial division by monic polynomials through floor(m/2); deterministic 2^O(m) poly(m), standard existence of irreducibles. Enumerate a,b in GF(q), color index i by fixed nonzero binary linear functional L(a*b^i), 0^0=1. For any nonempty set I of at most k distinct indices, average character bias over a,b is at most (n0-1)/q by root bound on nonzero polynomial sum_{i in I} b^i. Expanding prescribed t0-bit pattern indicators gives its probability >=2^-t0(1-(2^t0-1)(n0-1)/q)>0. Thus all patterns occur. Empty graph handled directly. Stream pairs, no free advice. For k=0 no target other than zero (or n0=0); direct test possible. Cover size q^2<=2^O(k) poly(N). No reliance on external arithmetic results.
