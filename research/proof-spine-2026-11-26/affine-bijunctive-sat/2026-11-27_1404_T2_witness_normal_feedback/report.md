## Mathematical result
Work in free coordinates of a verified affine carrier A=F2^r (translate and parametrize if necessary). Let G_1,...,G_k be explicit checked affine forbidden regions in A; the actual solution set is contained in U=A minus their union. Remove empty guards first (or treat their inconsistent defining equations as usual). If some G_i=A then U is empty. Exact counts of U intersect any additional affine system H follow by inclusion-exclusion over 2^k systems, each evaluated by rank and consistency via Gaussian elimination. Counts have O(r+k) bits even during signed accumulation. If U is nonempty, extract a lexicographic witness u by r prefix count queries. For each G_i select one defining equation a_i x=b_i violated at u. The common flat L={x:a_i x=a_i u for all i} contains u and lies in U. Therefore every affine equality of U has normal in span(a_i), and codim aff(U)<=k. Enumerate its at most 2^k-1 nonzero normals a; a x=a u holds on U iff count(U intersect {a x != a u})=0. Reduce accepted rows to basis. U is exactly its affine hull iff count(U)=2^(r-rank). The hull alone is not exact unless this equality holds. All counts, witness construction, candidate enumeration and rank checks require O((r+2^k)2^k poly(N)) deterministic bit operations, assuming explicit polynomial-sized carrier and guards and verified dependencies. This is polynomial even for k<=c log_2 N, fixed c; experiment caps k at five. Peak and cumulative storage for this standalone module polynomial using sequential rank computations. Does not charge unbounded discovery of parent refutations as free. A known stronger cover theorem (Brouwer 1986, primary PDF https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/2507/2507D.pdf, theorem p315 independently read) states that any irredundant binary affine cover of a flat has common-direction codimension at most k-1; our witness-flat lemma concerns the nonempty complement and is proved directly.

If U nonempty, a verified surviving word u either satisfies all exact residual constraints (then reconstruct/check original witness) or violates some bounded-arity factor f. Pin its local coordinates to u to obtain a new excluded affine guard P (certified by one table lookup plus the source derivation of f). It contains u. For width w, L intersect P has codimension <=k+w, so it removes at least 2^max(0,r-k-w) words and strictly decreases cardinality. Thus local guard acquisition can always succeed in the scope, but raw volume is exponentially large and k cannot be reset without preserving constraints. No universal rank decrease: disjoint forbidden patterns x_{3i+1}=x_{3i+2}=x_{3i+3}=0 leave a nonaffine full-dimensional complement (each factor's nonzero 3-bit words affinely span F2^3). Adding such constraints to an independent UNSAT core yields a scoped unproductive choice on an UNSAT context. Does not falsify all adaptive algorithms. First OPEN: universally acquire a bounded batch producing terminal or strict global rank/other polynomial credit decrease, or prove charged alternative portfolio coverage. A rank decrease can pay at most r rounds, but neither bounded acquisition nor nonaffine backlog progress is established. Preserve dense XOR rows explicitly, not exponential table expansion.
