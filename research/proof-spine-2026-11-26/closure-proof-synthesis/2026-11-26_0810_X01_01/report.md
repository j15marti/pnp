## 2. X01-A: semantic collision budget and minimum witness layer
Let C be a division-free arithmetic circuit with s gates over any field F, computing P(x_1,...,x_d). On {0,1}^d it agrees with Boolean f. Let B be any set of b variables such that P is individually of degree <=1 outside B, AFTER cancellations in its output; no claim gates satisfy this. Construct
 Q(x) = sum_{u in {0,1}^B} P(x_outsideB,u) * prod_{i in B}(u_i x_i+(1-u_i)(1-x_i)).
This is multilinear and agrees with P on the Boolean cube. Uniqueness follows by evaluation at 0/1 subsets and induction (or Boolean Mobius inversion); valid in all characteristics including 2. Thus Q is precisely the unique multilinear representative, size O(2^b(s+b+1)) (count input variable/scalar nodes if needed; unused ones omitted). Each specialization is a copy of the original circuit with B inputs replaced by constants. This proves the interpolation lemma for a certified/existential B; discovering B or certifying output degree after cancellation is separate, no PIT oracle. Even existence of such B suffices for a nonuniform circuit contradiction.

Let r be a positive integer, and f(1_S)=0 for |S|<r. Coefficient of squarefree x_S in Q equals sum_{T subset S}(-1)^(|S|-|T|)f(1_T). For |S|=r only T=S may contribute. Hence homogeneous component [Q]_r is exactly the sum of squarefree monomials for accepted words of weight r, coefficient 1 each. No all-witness counting or OR-to-sum conversion is used. Degree r extraction works over every field by representing each gate as the vector of components degrees 0..r; additions componentwise and products truncated convolution. O((r+1)^2) arithmetic gates per original gate, no bound on original intermediate degrees needed. With Boolean arithmetization constants 0,+/-1, construction uses polynomial-bit gate indices and copies, not evaluation of enormous field coefficients. Total size O(2^b(s+b+1)(r+1)^2); bit work for circuit construction polynomial in that size plus explicit constant encoding. Arbitrary free field constants are NOT a uniform bit-computation model. This is a conditional nonuniform lower-bound adapter, not a Boolean decision algorithm.

Controls on the same adjacency variables:
- f_PM is existence of a bipartite perfect matching. It is uniformly P by augmenting paths, and f_PM(1_S)=0 for |S|<m. At weight m exactly the permutation matrices are accepted, so [Q]_m=perm_m.
- f_HC is existence of directed Hamiltonian cycle. At minimum weight m, the adjacency support itself is a single directed cycle; each is one permutation with one cycle, no factor m from starting vertex, no division by m. Therefore [Q]_m=hc_m. Allow loops for all m, single-cycle convention for m>=2; finite small exceptions irrelevant.
- f_exactHC accepts iff the ENTIRE arc set is one directed Hamiltonian cycle. Uniform P: verify indegree=outdegree=1 at every vertex, follow successors from vertex 1, check one cycle. Same minimum layer hc_m. Hence the hard symbolic witness polynomial is not evidence that its associated Boolean decision problem is hard. Likewise f_exactPM. A raw nonmultilinear output need not have the right lowest degree: x^D agrees with x for all Boolean inputs but [x^D]_1=0 if D>1. High-degree collisions matter even if only the minimum layer is desired.
Proof by contradiction: if the external Anthropic stated bound m^(c(log2 m)^(1/3)) holds over a fixed field of characteristic not 2, then every polynomial-size circuit representing f_PM on Boolean inputs has semantic collision count b >= c(log2 m)^(4/3)-O(log m) = Omega(log^(4/3) m). Same conditional conclusion for the easy exactHC checker over any field, using HC bound. Since known easy Boolean functions already violate a universal O(log m) collision guarantee, a generic Boolean-to-arithmetic low-collision compiler would contradict the external arithmetic bound independently of P vs NP. OpenAI's claimed eventual all-fixed-exponent exact lower bounds similarly imply b=omega(log m) for any fixed polynomial size cap; its stronger manuscript quantitative exponent would yield b=Omega((log m)^2/(log log m)^2), only conditional on that distinct stronger statement. No claim these external proofs have been independently reconstructed. Characteristic 2 permanent excluded, hc still applicable by source claim.
This is a precise decision-sensitive falsifier of a generic collision transfer. A bridge specific to ordinary HC existence (not the easy exact-support verifier) could still imply P!=NP if derived from its hypothetical P algorithm, but such a bridge is OPEN. No free access to coefficients, counting oracle or arbitrary equivalence test.

## 3. X01-B: independent reconstruction and strengthening of L02 grid-overlap bound
Reconstructed L02-01 (report at 72ccd6f62aeab428a28e1af6f6eaa7e83d7faf62, blob fcf5949a778e7a14929d1e410947f6bb46992054). Its source reduction is valid: one row per Boolean variable W=E, one column per clause S=N OR satisfied_literal_at_cell(W), north 0/south 1 (padded columns 0), west/east free on active rows. k=max(2,n,m) after parsing/remapping variable IDs. Each local table lists the 16 possible port words satisfying rule. Induction on rows/columns gives both directions; empty clauses fail, tautologies handled. Explicit O(L²) bits and deterministic polynomial compilation in encoded 3-CNF length L. This authenticates arbitrary-table grid CSP; no uniform fixed local relation-language hardness asserted. NP verifier scans the actual indexed edges and tables. Boundary pins can be absorbed into tables.

Model: fixed k>=2, circuit computing full grid decision predicate; restrict to singleton local tables with fixed-zero external boundary. Leaves are arbitrary one-block predicates/constants; AND/OR fan-in two, no internal NOT (negated block predicates allowed). A syntactic scope S_g is all leaf block IDs below gate g. A true trace is unfolded, selects one true child at OR and both at AND. Actual occurrence scope A is the union of its trace leaves. External context leaf scope C is union of leaves outside that occurrence in the trace; context predicate is conjunction of those leaves for the chosen OR paths. Define J=S_g intersect C, using SYNTACTIC gate scope and actual outside context, not immediate child overlap. For each accepted singleton input assume SOME balanced true occurrence with k²/3<=|A|<=2k²/3 and |J|<=b. We remove L02's smooth-OR requirement without increasing its rectangle count.

Read-saturation lemma: every true root trace of a valid singleton input includes EVERY grid vertex block. Otherwise modify that block's singleton word at any internal incident port (k>=2 ensures one). All other words and boundary stay fixed, so all trace leaves remain true while shared edge equality fails, contradicting circuit correctness. Thus root actual scope is full. For any occurrence A, S_g minus A is contained in C and hence in J, because every block read at root occurs inside or outside. Follow largest actual-scope child at AND and the selected child at OR until crossing below 2k²/3. At AND the largest child's scope has at least half the parent scope; at OR actual scope is unchanged. Finite acyclic descent from full root gives balanced actual occurrence. This existence does NOT imply small J.

Safe grouping lemma (new nonsmooth proof): group accepted singleton inputs by (g,J,z) where z is their actual singleton block words on J and the group includes at least one balanced low-J occurrence. Choose ONE representative actual scope A* from a balanced occurrence in that group; this fixes a balanced partition even if other true occurrences of g have different actual scopes. Since S_g minus J subset A* subset S_g, all S_g outside A* lie in J. For the inside rectangle factor take assignments to A* agreeing with z there, for which g evaluates true when S_g minus A* is set by z. For the outside factor take assignments to V minus A* agreeing with z there, such that some context with intersection J has all its remaining leaves true when C intersect A* is set by z. This union is safe because C intersect A* subset J. The whole assignment agrees on J and makes g and the chosen context true. Substitute a true trace of g at the distinguished occurrence only; if the same DAG gate appears elsewhere its original context leaves still hold. Hence the product contains only accepted inputs. Every input represented by this label lies in the product, even if its actual trace scope differs from A*. Empty products ignored. Thus at most s sum_{j<=b} binom(k²,j)16^j balanced rectangles suffice. No enumeration or acquisition of traces asserted. This is a combinatorial cover proof, not a polynomial circuit compiler. L02's smooth version is independently valid and is a special case.

Any balanced cut has at least ceil(k/3) crossing edges: either >=k/3 rows mixed, or fewer mixed rows occupy <k²/3 vertices so both shores have a full row, producing a vertical crossing in each column. Sound rectangle must fix every crossing bit (cross-splicing disagreeing endpoints gives NO). With M=2k(k-1) independent internal bits there are 2^M valid singleton input words, each rectangle contains <=2^(M-ceil(k/3)). Therefore
 s * sum_{j<=b} binom(k²,j)16^j >= 2^ceil(k/3).
For polynomial s, b=Omega(k/log k); for fixed per-gate overlap superset J0 of size b label by z on J0 and get s16^b>=2^ceil(k/3), hence Omega(k). Can also apply to fraction of inputs with low-overlap accepted occurrence. Lower bound is on restricted representation size/context overlap, NOT arbitrary time. Bova et al. IJCAI2016 Theorems1,6 support DNNF rectangle technique; our block read-saturation argument is independent and specialized.

Adversarial control: singleton equality predicate is uniformly O(N polylog N) by reading each block, testing adjacent port bits and boundary, with unique physical assignment. This direct computation is also its own initial exact forced-information closure; no hard singleton instance. For fixed circuit, immediate AND-overlap <=2 suffices for compact edgewise checking (conjoin constant-size two-block equality predicates), but cumulative outside context can be large. No all-input compiler from arbitrary P decider to low-J representations established. Nonuniform circuit simulations preserve gate count, not this special overlap property. Negations may be pushed to leaves with O(s) dual-polarity gates but do not create low J. Per-instance decide-first charts likewise do not supply a fixed all-input circuit. Full row-frontier DP for arbitrary grid remains O(k²2^k poly(N)) bit time, exact original edge witness, matching the scoped exponent of decomposable representations. It is not a lower bound on that DP or on general algorithms.

## 4. X01-C: exact contextual projection admission is coNP-complete even for one edge and known SAT
This addresses C01's context-certified demand projection (CPS19 original blob 574c43d85de0d7207597fefeebeb4694f082e1d2) on the SAME arbitrary-table grid source as X01-B, not an unverified transfer to P3DM. For Boolean edge x, independently project both incident tables existentially onto other ports, producing G. Let F be original. Always projection_x(F) subset Sol(G). Exactness iff every assignment satisfying G has at least one common x completion. A countercertificate is a complete retained assignment satisfying G and failing both x=0 and x=1. Polynomial verification; exactness in coNP.

Reduction from 3-CNF UNSAT with >=1 variable and clause (standard harmless padding). Compile the row-variable / column-OR grid as above; now append TWO horizontal guard rows a,b. In each original clause column the first guard row updates S=N OR (1-a), second S=N OR (1-b); lower rows copy vertical value and bottom pins 1. So projected grid is satisfiable exactly when each clause is true or a=0 or b=0. At column m+1, adjacent vertically to one another, end guard rows in A and B, sharing one physical vertical bit x. Table A has north/east zero, west=a, south=x, condition (not a OR not x). Table B has east/south zero, west=b, north=x, condition (not b OR x). Active variable rows end with a sink allowing free west and other ports zero, all unused cells all-zero, top0 on original clause columns, lower vertical copies and bottom1, west free for variable/guard rows, other boundaries zero. k=max(3,n+3,m+1). Every bit and table is explicitly encoded. Original F allows all original variable assignments if a=0 or b=0, with an x satisfying the two implications. It excludes a=b=1. Independent projection removes both implications entirely; G additionally allows a=b=1 exactly on original SAT witnesses. Both F and G are always SAT with explicit efficiently constructed witnesses (a=b=0, choose original variable word all zero and x=0, propagate accumulators). Thus projection exact iff original formula UNSAT. Both directions and a concrete lift; O(L²) encoded grid size. The physical x can also be treated as a dummy unconstrained shared bit after projecting tables, without affecting retained assignments. No free equivalence or SAT oracle. This is a uniform many-one reduction to the admission problem, not to the decision of F (which is easy YES).

Stronger local-support control: every extra assignment of G has a=b=1 and all original clauses already true. In both guard rows all original column accumulators are already1. Change a to0 in one copy, b to0 in a second; choose x=1 in first, x=0 in second. Both are F solutions, with same original variables and all vertical accumulators. On retained edges only one horizontal guard row changes in each copy. Every retained local table word of the extra assignment appears in at least one of these two original solutions after projecting x (each local table touches at most one guard signal). Therefore exact unary support and even exact projected individual-factor support are IDENTICAL for F and G for every source input, including SAT/UNSAT neighbors. Full unary/local marginal saturation cannot establish joint equivalence here. On a=b=1 guard, exact-one of x demands 0 vs1 gives immediate conflict; full binary consequence learning can retain not-a OR not-b, resolving this construction. Hence not a barrier to CDP with explicit retained nogoods or complete forced-binary consequences, not a post-entire-portfolio hard NO. At stagnation transmit the exact clause, not a guessed pin. Earlier one-guard variant also works but its escape bit is semantically forced; two-guard version avoids that limitation and proves identical individual local support. No transfer claimed to restricted homogeneous tetrahedral languages.

Consequences: an always polynomial algorithm answering whether all opposing demands are redundant in arbitrary explicit grid contexts would imply P=NP (via UNSAT). This does not itself prove P!=NP. Exact CDP is still sound with sufficient efficient certificates and can safely keep all opposite-demand nogoods when their explicit encoding is affordable. No existential projection of one variable may silently omit its conflicting fibers. Proved scoped obstruction targets completeness of an admission oracle, not cooperative coverage.
