## Scoped theorem: compositional credit for bounded-width root-derived certificate agenda
Let m <= N be normalized variable count, w a fixed positive constant. U is all non-tautological clauses of width <=w on the original representative namespace and fixed checked gauge, including empty; K=sum_{i=0}^{min(w,m)} binom(m,i)2^i=O((m+1)^w). Use one exact canonical clause node per (full source ID, checked gauge, clause); a branch-conditional fact cannot use this root key until its assumption is discharged. Store first accepted proof derivation and every parent reference. Enumerate every potential unordered pair of distinct admitted clauses and pivot at most once, as second parent is admitted, with canonical ordered job key. Even rejected/tautological/overwidth candidates consume jobs. Number of pair/pivot jobs <= m K^2, including checking potential pivots. Each new clause adds at most O(mK) such jobs, and each attempted job has polynomial checking, key comparison, bit encoding, edge and memory cost. Use deterministic ordered arrays or tries, not a unit-cost semantic hash. Initially reserve K insertion slots and mK^2 candidate slots, plus fixed polynomial communication/checking credits for r fixed replicas and rK accepted-node subscriptions; rejected imports must have their own pre-reserved job slots, no unlimited retries or uncharged messages. Queue empty is a terminal of this restricted saturation procedure (UNKNOWN if no contradiction). The integer potential is remaining reserved job slots; every completed paid job decreases it and no dynamic job generation replenishes it. Polling and scheduling implement bounded queue operations; no indefinite empty polling. Total work and peak live storage bounded by fixed polynomial in N for constant w,r, including parsing, verification and explicitly charged transmission; cumulative memory <= total bit-time times aggregate live storage on same execution. Sound: each accepted clause is a root consequence under gauge. Complete for every source having a width<=w resolution derivation over that normalized root source: induction on a topological derivation shows all premises eventually admitted and the corresponding pivot job executed, so empty found. No assumption of universal bounded width. This is a scoped reconstruction of bounded-width saturation plus cooperative accounting, not a new unrestricted theorem.

For any verified DAG with U nodes, E reference edges, and total serialized proof length L, a once-per-physical-instance checker using topological order costs poly(N+L), not unfolded tree size. Multiple receivers still pay physical checks; source/guard mismatch invalidates reuse. A local decrease in unresolved variable count is insufficient: full binary branching on k irrelevant variables gives 2^k leaves even when an easy contradiction exists. Restricted branch-tree hardness does not transfer to all algorithms. Universal polynomial productive admission (or other terminal) is the first OPEN arrow.

## New scoped proof transport: resolution to shared Boolean syzygy
Work in F2[x]/(x_i^2-x_i). Failure of clause C is f_C=prod_{l in C}(1-truth(l)). For parents P=A or v, Q=B or not v and non-tautological resolvent R=A or B, f_R=f_A f_B=f_B f_P+f_A f_Q, because (1-v)+v=1 and repeated factors are idempotent in the Boolean quotient. Every proof node can be replaced by two multiplications and one addition of prior expressions times balanced tail products. Thus from a checked DAG with S nodes and total literal length L, produce a shared arithmetic circuit of O(S(w+1)) gates by direct per-edge tail products (the actually executed construction), coefficients bits, output 1 for empty; input axioms each appear only as failure indicators in the derivation, and linear combination identity follows inductively. Here w is the maximum clause width. A stronger O(S+L) gate bound, when L is the sum of clause lengths over unique proof nodes, follows by computing all prefix and suffix literal products of each clause by a balanced parallel scan in O(width) gates and O(log(width+1)) depth, then obtaining every pivot-deleted tail from two such products and a multiplication; this optimization was not executed. For proof depth D and maximum clause width w, gate depth O(D+log(w+1)) with bounded fan-in; no universal logarithmic proof depth. To flatten the linear recurrence in the Boolean quotient, write y=A(x)y+b with strictly triangular S x S matrix; doubling powers and partial geometric sums gives y=(I+A+...+A^{S-1})b. At most O(log S) matrix products, O(S^3 log S) binary gates and O(log^2 S+log(w+1)) depth (each matrix product sum has O(log S) binary depth). This latter parallel depth theorem is symbolic, not executed; all matrix work paid. It does not give O(log N) binary depth unless additional structure is proved, and does not establish exact polynomial identity outside Boolean quotient or translate an arbitrary decision algorithm into a formal arithmetic circuit.
