## Named lemma: PROOF-LEAF-MULTIPLICITY-TO-PAID-DEFECT-COVER
Let P_j be an acquired checked resolution refutation, with leaves either hard axioms (including a correctly discharged complement gauge) or clause C_l. Attach to each nonhard leaf a nonempty set O_l of DISTINCT original B IDs such that H and satisfaction of each individual owner v in O_l imply C_l. Full normalized clause identity is sufficient but not necessary; the independent checker verifies local truth tables in exact signed components. Every hard-feasible assignment x (gauged by complement if necessary) falsifies a leaf of each resolution refutation: otherwise all leaves true imply by induction all nodes true, contradiction at empty clause. Every owner in the falsified leaf support is defective. For D(x) = original defective owners, necessarily
    D(x) in intersection_j union_{nonhard leaf l of P_j} { D : O_l subseteq D }.
This is a monotone Boolean formula on defect indicators, obtained without unfolding the proof DAG. It is a necessary condition only, not sufficiency for existence of an assignment with a given defect set. In particular if one proof has min_l |O_l| >= k, energy >= k. For exact energy k and all leaf supports at least k, D must equal one listed size-k support in each proof, so sparse intersection suffices without enumerating all binom(m,k) choices. Distinct owners are required; repeating the same owner or a clause cannot increase the energy lower bound. Duplicate B predicates can legitimately have distinct original physical cost owners. Reuse across gauges is sound only when complement preserves the objective and discharges each gauge; conditional proofs require their actual guards.

Alternative deletion-context proof: for any D containing no listed support from one proof, choose one surviving owner per leaf outside D. The same resolution skeleton is a refutation when the B constraints in D are relaxed. If a listed support is contained in D, the supplied support list cannot certify that particular leaf; other proofs or unlisted owners may bypass it. Never infer SAT from lack of survival. Multiple proofs intersect the residual candidate sets. For fixed integer k, explicitly enumerate at most sum_{i<=k} binom(m,i) deletion contexts if needed, and mark survival of leaves by checking owner sets, inference survival by AND of parent masks, alternative checked derivations by OR (within a fixed acyclic derivation graph). This evaluates a scoped proof-survival certificate family, not semantic completeness. On a fixed acquired DAG of S nodes, E edges, L total owner references and Q contexts, this costs O(Q(S+E+L)) elementary mask entries, plus deterministic sorting, comparisons, bit encodings and full verifier/communication costs. For fixed k and polynomially acquired S,E,L it gives a fixed polynomial aggregate bound; no free discovery, no restarted budget per context. Sparse k-pair lists used here avoid the full Q enumeration. Numeric credit for each (node, context) and each parent/reference check is initialized once and consumed at most once per fixed agenda; rejected/failed acquisition separately reserved. Physical verification per import is charged. This lemma is a scoped representation theorem in the spirit of core-guided MaxSAT, not a new claim of universal tractability.

## Genuine hybrid: exact chart projection of sparse allowed pairs
Let F be an exact min-sum residual chart with nonnegative integer cost tables, each factor carries disjoint original B owners, collectively a partition. Exact variable elimination merges all factors containing the eliminated coordinate, computes each boundary minimum over both bits, records an attaining bit, and combines like scopes by addition. Reverse elimination reconstructs original bits. By reverse induction on bucket operations, each final factor's table value equals the sum of original costs on its own owner set for the reconstructed assignment: grouping adds disjoint owner costs; every bucket replacement includes all occurrences of its eliminated variable and recorded argmin realizes its value. Thus there is a consistent global reconstruction that realizes each final factor's cost, not merely the total. This is the necessary map for importing proof-leaf restrictions.

If all chart costs are even, a chart word of total cost 2 has exactly one positive factor, with cost 2. Its reconstructed defect pair must be on every parent proof list and both original owners must lie in that factor's owner set. Therefore only factors containing an ENTIRE permitted pair and attaining 2 require a threshold chart refutation. This is stronger than requiring intersection with a union-of-core owners; do not drop exact owner pairs to unary marginals. If chart has a cost-2 entry but no allowed pair fully contained, the entire single-positive-factor chart is excluded by the source proof family. Zero cost is excluded by source root refutation. To exclude cost <=2 check every eligible remaining single-positive case. Finite coverage of these cases is not universal polynomial refutation.
