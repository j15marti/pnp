### A. Exact universal saturation characterization and hardness
For a fixed label tuple p, let C(p) be the distinct vertices chosen. XOR sum of edge fibers is the coordinate subspace on C(p): choose any one occurrence of each desired coordinate and set other choices zero. Sum of f filler supports is exactly the Hamming ball of radius f (every sum has weight <=f and every such word uses each nonzero coordinate once). Therefore the complete attained support is
 S(p)={b: |supp(b) minus C(p)|<=f}.
It is the whole ambient space iff |C(p)|>=d-f; the same condition suffices and is necessary for just the all-ones target. Min_p |C(p)| = vertex cover number tau(G), since every selected vertex set covers each edge, and any cover supplies a choice for each edge. Thus universal protected saturation, or even FOR ALL locally attained label tuples EXISTS exact witnesses for one fixed all-ones target, holds iff tau(G)>=d-f. Reduce complement of vertex-cover decision (tau(G)>k), 0<=k<d, by f=d-k-1. Explicit polynomial expansion with four rows per edge and d+1 rows per filler. Failure certificate is a label tuple with fewer than d-f distinct selected vertices, polynomially verifiable. Hence the universal test is coNP-complete on this exact chart family. This does not prove it takes superpolynomial time; a polynomial algorithm would imply P=NP. It does not falsify the sufficient adaptive span-intersection certificate X01-13. Each edge region's two span lines have zero intersection, so that certificate cannot eliminate d directions when f<d, even in many cases where universal saturation is true. This is an inherent completeness limitation of that certificate, not evidence against existential decision. Universal saturation is not a necessary precondition for a productive decision step.

### B. Polynomial existential decision without universal recognition
Given target support T, make a bipartite graph from edge regions to their allowed endpoint coordinates in T (respect unary label pins). Let nu be maximum matching cardinality. Then exact feasibility iff
 |T|-nu <= f.
Necessity: take any actual witness. Every target coordinate not matched to a filler contribution must have at least one edge region contributing that coordinate. Distinct such coordinates require distinct contributing edge regions, since a region emits at most one unit. At most f target coordinates can receive any filler contribution. Thus a matching of size >=|T|-f exists, even allowing cancellations. Sufficiency: choose one actual endpoint/one-hot row for every matched edge region, any permitted zero row for each unmatched one. Assign remaining target coordinates injectively to fillers, rest zero. This gives exact sum b and legal independent label tuple. This also works more generally for explicit independent local tables with possible 0 and any subset of standard basis units, with the only requirement that after the independent allowed-label restrictions each free region still has some zero choice. One may simply include all regions including fillers on the left and require full matching to T. Fixed actual rows may be removed and their XOR moved into b; do not apply the matching lemma if free restrictions remove all zero rows, or if relational guards couple protected labels. Thus this is a new exact chart terminal, not universal recognition or general guarded CSP tractability.
