## New theorem: exact Hall slack
Let V be the original owner set, E the currently authenticated allowed labeled triples, H a subset of one shore, Y a subset of another, and B a set of blockers disjoint from H and Y. For every t in E incident to H, require t meets Y union B (original X01-20 certificate condition); K=Y union B. Thus w(t)=|t intersect K|-|t intersect H|>=0 for every allowed triple (including triples not incident to H). Every old certificate satisfies this, with |H|>|Y|. More generally any disjoint H,K with nonnegative weights suffices; the cardinality deficit is not needed for conservation.

For ANY exact disjoint partial cover P, let U be the uncovered owner set, and R the allowed remaining tuples contained in U (possibly additionally soundly pruned). For ANY exact extension F of P, F partitions U, hence
  sum_{t in F} w(t) = |K intersect U|-|H intersect U| = s(U).
Every summand is a nonnegative integer. Therefore:
(1) s<0 excludes the entire fiber, even if not all B occupied or not all H uncovered;
(2) for s>=0 every t with w(t)>s is forbidden in that fiber;
(3) s=0 permits only zero-weight tuples, and s=k permits at most k positive-weight selected tuples (not a bound on total candidates).
The old Boolean cube B all occupied and H all uncovered implies s<=|Y|-|H|<0. Thus new rule subsumes it. Certificate source verification scans every native allowed triple; compute weights independently. If another partial cover shares occupancy, residual completion constraints are identical for native exact cover; guard-local tuple restrictions must also be preserved. After selecting a forced tuple, update U and recompute s; after deletion reclose. No need to reconstruct which previous tuples produced U for negative decision. Do not import a conditional deletion globally without guard discharge.

Independent algebra track: with incidence matrix A, row multiplier lambda=1_K-1_H, w=A^T lambda>=0 and Ax=1_U for residual incidence, so w^T x=lambda^T1_U=s. This is a nonnegative linear certificate; all these inequalities already hold on any feasible fractional exact-cover LP, so adding them alone cannot improve the full native LP feasible set. They can improve inexpensive discrete local closure and avoid repeatedly solving LP. Generalization to any integer lambda with nonnegative weights is valid, but no universal acquisition algorithm for useful lambda is proved. Conditional minimum-cost perfect matching on any two-shore projection, with edge cost the minimum w over its currently allowed tuple labels, yields lower bound on extension cost, hence optimum>s refutes. This latter weighted-matching bound is proved by projecting any true extension, not implemented here. It is not a proof of sufficiency when optimum<=s. For a bounded joint guard, the sum of weights of fixed disjoint tuples cannot exceed current slack. Bounded k does not alone make enumeration polynomial when k grows; charge binomial candidate count. Stronger native LP may supply rational Farkas dual with checked bits, but not used here.
