## Guard-preimage credit theorem
Fix Boolean affine parent chart A of r coordinates, V=P_{<=d}(A) squarefree polynomial feature space, dimension D=sum_{j<=d} binom(r,j), fixed d. For each of t guards covering all feasible parent points, use a verified affine restriction rho_i:V->V_i on a child chart containing the feasible points of that guard. Let W_i be an acquired vanishing subspace certified on that guarded residual. Then W*=intersection_i rho_i^{-1}(W_i)=ker of the stacked quotient maps pi_i rho_i vanishes on all feasible parent points.
Proof: any feasible point lies in at least one guard and has the corresponding child chart representation. Every p in W* restricts there to an identity in W_i, hence evaluates to zero. Affine substitution followed by Boolean reduction does not increase degree. If 1 belongs to W*, contradiction. Missing cover invalidates inference (guard x=0 permits x, not a root identity). Do not require child closure charts to cover the whole ambient cube; they must cover feasible points in their guards.
For current globally certified W0, credit decrease is dim(W0+W*)-dim W0, not sum of child ranks. Fixed-chart potential D-dim W0 nonnegative and nonincreasing. With polynomially many guards, bounded explicit charts, fixed degree and certified acquisition, dense linear algebra conservatively O((tD)^3) binary operations and O((tD)^2) storage; add source verification, substitution construction, guard coverage, acquisition, failed work, all branch costs, communication and cumulative memory. No universal acquisition claim or branch-credit theorem follows.
