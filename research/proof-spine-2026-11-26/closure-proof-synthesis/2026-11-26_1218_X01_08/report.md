## Theorem 1: exact affine cut
Reject inconsistent affine system by Gaussian elimination. Let W be coefficient rowspace, b its induced linear RHS functional, W_I and W_E the pure-side subspaces, and choose a basis C complementing their direct sum. Compute via elimination in both column orders. Let r=dim W-dim W_I-dim W_E. For x on I and y on E satisfying all pure-side rows, the full affine system holds iff C_I x=b_C+C_E y (field GF2). Proof: all original rows lie in the span of these pure and complement basis rows; hence necessity and sufficiency. A dependence of the projected complement rows modulo pure I rows would give a nonzero linear combination in W_I+W_E, contradiction; similarly E. Thus for the pure affine spaces alone each side attains all 2^r syndrome values (shift by RHS). Actual exact-cover supports may be smaller. The rank is basis invariant and may be much smaller than written crossing-row count.
