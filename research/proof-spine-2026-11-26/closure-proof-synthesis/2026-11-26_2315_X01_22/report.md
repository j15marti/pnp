## New theorem
Let U be the uncovered owner set, u its complement occupancy bit vector, lambda=1_K-1_H from a source-checked nonnegative native weight certificate w(t)=sum_{v in t}lambda_v>=0. For every disjoint partial exact cover and exact extension F, sum_{t in F}w(t)=s(u)=sum_v lambda_v(1-u_v). Let S be any exact or conservative set of attainable occupancy masks under the same original guard, contained in an acquired affine chart u=o+Bz over GF2, z in GF2^d, B full column rank. Write a_v for the vth row of B, chi_a(z)=(-1)^(a dot z). Then exactly
  2s(o+Bz)=c+sum_{a != 0} C_a chi_a(z),
  C_a=sum_{v:a_v=a} lambda_v (-1)^o_v,
  c=sum_v lambda_v + C_0.
Group equal signatures before taking absolute values. Therefore U_char=c+sum_{a !=0}|C_a| is a valid upper bound on twice the slack for ALL points of the chart, hence also S. It never exceeds the coordinatewise independent upper bound U_axis. No actual-attainment assertion from affine hull. If U_char<0, the entire chart has no extension. If 2w(t)>U_char, tuple t is forbidden in every complementary residual where it is still available. Do not delete already occupied tuples or import guarded facts into unrelated source. If some unit tuples are forced subsequently, original upper bound remains valid for remaining weight because those forced tuples have nonnegative weight; reapply exact closure.

Rank-two refinement: for distinct nonzero a,b,a+b, the three character signs multiply to +1. If their preferred signs (signs of the three nonzero coefficients) multiply to -1, their exact maximum contribution is sum of absolute values minus twice the smallest absolute coefficient. Proof: at least one preferred sign must be violated; flipping precisely the cheapest is feasible since a,b independent, and the third sign is determined. If preferred signs multiply to +1, all can be realized. A deterministic collection of disjoint coefficient-term triples gives a valid sum of these local maxima and leftover absolute values even if their underlying coordinates overlap. The sum may exceed the true maximum, never undershoot it. More generally partition coefficient terms into bounded-rank groups, enumerate each group only with a proved paid rank cap; sum maxima. No need for exponential whole-chart enumeration.

Exact period criterion: on the full affine chart, translation by h in parameter space preserves s for every z iff every a with C_a !=0 obeys a dot h=0. Sufficiency is termwise; necessity follows by multiplying the difference by chi_a and summing over z, using pairwise orthogonality over the rationals/reals. Thus the exact period subspace is kernel of the active signature matrix, acquired by Gaussian elimination. For several certificates, use intersection of their kernels. This establishes invariance of weight only; a decision-equivalent quotient also needs the separate exact attainable-support stabilizer and lift. On a strict subset S, the full-chart period is only a sufficient weight-invariance test, not necessarily all possible periods. An affine hull is not automatically attained. Exact slack profile may be represented on the smaller quotient for each certificate independently, with no joint witness claim. Distinguish C02 minimum selected-weight lower profiles from this maximum remaining-slack upper profile. For rejecting a whole chart s<0 one needs an upper bound on max s, NOT min s. Hostile slack values -1,+1 demonstrate the logical error. For deleting a tuple use the same upper orientation. Gaussian describes the chart but does not optimize it.
