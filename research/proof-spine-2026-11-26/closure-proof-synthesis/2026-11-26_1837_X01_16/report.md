## 2. Exact mixed-shore conservation
Let x_t in {0,1}; impose all owner parity equations sum_{t incident v} x_t = 1 mod 2. For one owner-pair projection B,C, find a perfect matching or reject NO. Every connected component has equal shore sizes. A selected triple occupies exactly one B and one C in the same component. For each component independently choose either whole shore, and impose only its integer exact-one occupancies. Then the component contains k selected tuples for k owners on either shore. Every opposite-shore owner has positive odd occupancy; total k forces all exactly one. Summing over components gives n selected tuples. All n third-color A owners have positive odd occupancy, so all exactly one. Converse immediate. No positivity relaxation or counting substitution. Can choose cheaper shore after actual parity parameterization and canonical event dedup; take minimum over three projections and all/single-color alternatives. Every retained exact-one is expressed by excluding all pairs of simultaneously selected incident bits, since parity already enforces at least one. Rank<=2 events before subsequent transformations; duplicates and inconsistent events removed. Per-component selection minimizes its local row sum, not necessarily global post-dedup optimum. With degree-two/three owners the raw degree-three count on both balanced shores is identical; savings are not guaranteed, and can come from Gaussian redundancy/dedup. This works in the standard bit model, not just planar sources. Polynomial acquisition scans all owners and pairs, charges all three charts. No new NP-completeness assumption.

## 3. Attained-syndrome private block compiler

For proposed touched set J, compute K=common kernel of rows of all untouched events; choose basis plus complementary space y. For touched event i the fiber on private t in K is defined by L_i t=b_i+B_i y. For q touched events, any minimal cover of K has private codimension <=q-1 for each participating fiber: choose a private point of that member, for every other member an opposite defining equation satisfied there; their intersection has codimension <=q-1 and is contained in the chosen member. Screen higher-private-rank events for coverage only, retaining every original event for replay.
Stack private rows L of surviving events; let Q be a basis of the left kernel. RHS h(y)=b+B y. If Qh=Qh', then h+h' is in im(L); a translation of t bijects all fibers simultaneously, hence identical union coverage. Crucially, enumerate only the actual affine image Qb+im(QB), of dimension eta=rank(QB), not every nominal dependency syndrome. For each attained syndrome sigma acquire any representative h with Qh=sigma; test cover by inclusion-exclusion on surviving events, with each intersection count either zero or 2^(dim K-rank). If covered, exclude the exact affine event Q(b+B y)=sigma. This projected conjunction is exact in both directions, including empty fibers and rank-zero contradiction. ## 4. Matching-component frontier theorem
For each actual B-C projection component, take local indexed triples. Impose parity on its B,C owners and private A owners (those occurring only in that component). Parameterize by Gaussian elimination, nullity h_j. Enumerate all local affine words under a paid cap; retain one actual witness for each attainable XOR syndrome on shared A owners, requiring exact occupancy on one B or C shore. Conservation forces the other shore exact. Across components the XOR of protected syndromes must equal all ones on shared A. Together with private A parity and total n selected tuples, this is equivalent to global native cover. No shared marginal product or lost third-owner correlation. Reverse witness lift concatenates actual local triples, then checks every original owner exactly once.
Discover a component order by minimizing next frontier cardinality (deterministic greedy, polynomial per scan, no optimality guarantee). F_i consists of shared owners appearing both in processed and unprocessed components. Store exact attainable XOR words on F_i, with already closed owner parities fixed to one. On joining a table, XOR the words; require one on every owner whose last component has been processed; restrict to F_next and deduplicate exact states with witness backpointer. Induction gives exact prefix semantics, empty support is certified NO, terminal empty interface with an attained word is YES. Cap exhaustion is UNKNOWN. If q_i=number of attained frontier states and s_j=table size, charged total candidate enumerations sum_j 2^h_j, join transitions sum_j q_{j-1}s_j, times poly(N) overhead for bases, graph construction, scans, comparisons, bitstrings, replay and verification. Store at most polynomial size if both sums and peak table sizes are polynomial in original N. Three projections and all failed lanes must be charged in a single original-N budget. A sufficient bound is h_j and frontier widths O(log N) with polynomially many components; actual attained-state caps can be smaller. This is a conditional polynomial terminal. No proof every residual admits it.
