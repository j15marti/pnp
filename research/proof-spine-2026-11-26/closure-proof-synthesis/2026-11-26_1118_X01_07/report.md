## L2: EXTREME-IMAGE-TARIFF (new)
For every monotone f on a full Boolean cube, any monotone multioutput circuit map M (s gates, m output membership coordinates) satisfying f=F∘M on every input, let Y be the set of distinct exact-four images of componentwise minimal YES inputs and Z the set of distinct exact-four images of maximal NO inputs. Write u=M(x). Then
f(x)=A(u) AND [B(u) OR OR_{y in Y} AND_{i:y_i=1} u_i],
f(x)=A(u) AND AND_{z in Z} OR_{i:z_i=0} u_i.
For the first identity, any YES x contains a minimal YES x0. M(x0) meets A; if overweight then B(u), else exact-slice y<=u. Converse uses monotonicity of F and F(y)=1. For the second, a NO x lies below maximal NO x1. If A(M(x1)) false, A(u) false; otherwise B(M(x1)) false since x1 rejected, hence exact-slice z and u<=z, falsifying exclusion clause. Any YES satisfies A and cannot be below a rejected z. Empty OR/AND use constants. No witness count oracle or free image enumeration.
Nonuniform circuit existence bound (A,B fixed thresholds per 16-bit block):
C_mon(f)<=s+O(m(1+min(|Y|,|Z|))).
Thus for bipartite matching on n by n adjacency, Cavalar et al arXiv:2507.16105v2 Thm1 gives L(n)>=2^{n^{1/3-o(1)}}; both cardinalities >= (L(n)-s)/(K m)-1 for a fixed implementation constant K. For polynomial-size s,m, both must be 2^{n^{1/3-o(1)}} up to polynomial factors. Applies to arbitrary depth, nonlocal monotone reductions, even off-promise output. Does not rule out such a map, nor supply a monotone lower bound on F or an all-decider transfer. Existence bound is nonuniform, not acquisition of Y,Z. The same conclusion would not follow for arbitrary nonmonotone M. First lower OPEN arrow: superpolynomial monotone lower bound on actual total F, or another all-decider bridge. Arithmetic claims not needed.
