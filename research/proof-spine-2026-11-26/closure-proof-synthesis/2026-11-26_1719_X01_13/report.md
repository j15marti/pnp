## 1. Adaptive span-intersection theorem
For region i, let S_i(p) be its nonempty exact attained syndrome fiber under a locally attained protected label p. Let
W = span of all s+t with s,t in the same fiber of the same region.
V_i = intersection over attained p of span{ s+t : s,t in S_i(p) }.
Suppose an independent basis of W can be chosen with each vector drawn from a distinct V_i, at most one per owner. Then for EVERY tuple of locally attained labels p_i,
sum_i S_i(p_i) = sum_i a_i(p_i) + W,
where each a_i(p_i) is any attained reference. This is an exact sumset statement, not a relaxation and not a preservation of solution multiplicity.

Constructive track: obtain each fiber's affine-direction basis from differences relative to one reference, intersect the subspaces by Gaussian elimination, and solve finite linear/partition matroid intersection on the union of written bases of V_i (at most B*d elements, B regions). A full-rank transversal is enough. If not full rank, do not contract on this certificate. Every vector in a V_i need not itself occur as a pair difference.
Global track: fix ANY permitted label tuple (do not enumerate tuples). Let J be owners selected by the certificate. For every T subset J the actual pair-difference sets D_i(p_i) of those owners have union span containing their |T| independent selected V_i directions. Rado's independent transversal theorem therefore supplies actual independent representatives from these D_i(p_i), one per owner, forming a basis of W. Reacquire this actual transversal AFTER the labels are known, by polynomial matroid intersection on actual pairs, storing their original row witnesses. Choose any base point of each selected pair, and any row in each other fiber. Their sum differs from the reference sum only in W. For any requested target in the coset, solve a linear system for which independent pair choices to flip. These flips stay within the selected actual fibers and preserve all original labels. Conversely all actual sums belong to the coset by definition of W. Thus exact projection modulo W preserves every label tuple and enables reverse actual witness reconstruction, including outside syndrome terms. Keep original target, row IDs, partition, labels, and external constraints.
Adversarial track: a four-state tetrahedron {0,e1,e2,e3} has full span of differences but does not fill GF2^3; one owner cannot supply three independent representatives. Full rank and one-per-owner are necessary premises of THIS sufficient certificate. No direct use of a span vector as a witness move. Do not drop the complete interface or use pooled labels. Partial rank does not justify contracting arbitrary W.
Integrated procedure: apply prior exact unary/conditional affine row closure; run actual-difference and period probes separately; import verified span-intersection certificate and quotient; reclose projected tables and apply capped exact DP to remaining target. Reverse actual adaptive pair acquisition and solve discrepancy only after choosing the final labels. No independent global SAT calls. This extends X01-12 strictly. Existing common actual difference and common period certificates remain valid alternatives.
