For each candidate C retain unit propagation under negation of C, a candidate-local assignment, per-row counts, queue and reasons. A prior propagated literal remains a sound implication after a clause addition. At insertion inspect the new row under the retained assignment; update through occurrence lists for subsequent assignments; propagate until fixed point or conflict. By monotonicity of sound unit propagation, the retained closure equals reset closure (or both conflict), including conflicting assumptions. Each variable assigned at most once per context; each incidence inspected at insertion and at most once on assignment; each row becomes unit/conflicting only boundedly often, with stale queued work charged. If epochs scan all candidates, at most K candidate-admission epochs or L nonempty row-admission batches. Thus O(K(L+m+K)) elementary indexed operations is a conservative total bound, including failed probes and registration/epoch scans, not just successful deductions.
