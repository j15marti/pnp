Define s(a+b)=s_i(a)+s_j(b). Well-defined because any two decompositions differ by an element of intersection, whose values coincide. Conversely restriction recovers each child. A functional on W_i+W_j extends to the ambient binary vector space by basis extension, hence the union table for the conjunction of the two local constraints (in the same affine guard) equals this join, not just a relaxation. With upper-support child tables the result is an upper support, not claimed exact. Sort/index both tables by intersection-basis evaluation key k. Exact output size J=sum_k n_i(k)n_j(k); evaluate it before expanding the join and return UNKNOWN if the output cap would be exceeded. Cost poly(N,dimension) times (|R_i|+|R_j|+J), including deterministic sorting, field operations, basis conversion and witness pointers; no enumeration of all 2^dim(W_i+W_j) functionals is logically needed. ## Connecting lemma 2: cross-intersection defect
For a third space W_k let D=((W_i+W_j) intersect W_k), E=(W_i intersect W_k)+(W_j intersect W_k), g=dim D-dim E >=0. Given pointwise pairwise compatible functionals s_i,s_j,s_k, first glue i,j to s. s and s_k agree on E by linearity. A basis of D/E therefore gives exactly g additional independent linear compatibility equations to enforce global gluing of all three. For every basis representative u=a+b in D, equation s_i(a)+s_j(b)=s_k(u) is necessary and jointly sufficient. Gaussian elimination produces representatives and decompositions in polynomial bit time from bases. Necessity of g in the unconstrained functional space: choose s_i=s_j=0 and choose s_k vanishing on E but arbitrary on a complement of E in D, then extend to W_k. All pairwise comparisons hold, each defect coordinate can vary independently. Thus g detects hidden compatibility dimensions, not just geometric overlap. Equivalent dimension calculation g=rank(W_i+W_j)+rank(W_k)-rank(W_i+W_j+W_k)-rank((W_i intersect W_k)+(W_j intersect W_k)).
Rank gain is NOT a guarantee of productive deletion for constrained local tables, nor of global LP consistency. A genuine hybrid uses gain to change the local representation by selecting exact pair joins, then full-key support peeling followed by character-connected LP. Valid point masses survive each admitted join and deletion. LP infeasible with exact dual implies native NO. A feasible LP or capped join is UNKNOWN.
Hostile controls: W_i=<a>, W_j=<b>, W_k=<a+b>, bits 0,0,1 give g=1 and pairwise agreement but no common functional. Four 1D spaces <a>,<b>,<c>,<a+b+c> with independent a,b,c and bits 0,0,0,1 have g=0 for every triple but no global extension. Even ordinary coordinate triangle constraints a=b,b=c,a!=c have all g=0 and pairwise consistent uniform marginals, no global point. Therefore no universal completeness from maximizing g, and no all-decider transfer. Equality, Gaussian and matching cheap bypasses remain legitimate. Bounded-width DP and compact circuits need not represent full joins.
