## Theorem: guarded defect-support-safe proof transport
Let a fixed exact nonnegative integer energy chart have factors F_i and disjoint original owner blocks. Under the same checked guard, translate every positive table row of factor i to a forbidden-assignment clause, label it by singleton owner i. (This is a zero-energy CNF, not an energy-preserving CNF MaxSAT transformation.) For each RUP learned clause c, independently replay UP on not c using specified earlier parent rows. Store S_c as union of their owner sets. Induction shows c is a consequence of the original zero clauses in S_c under the guard. If D is a set of relaxed owners and D intersects S_c trivially, c may be used under D; it is NOT in general a consequence when a parent is relaxed. Removing proof nodes requires removing descendants or rederiving them. Exact guard/residual adapters required, no transport by matching names. If UP proves conflict from the surviving rows, no assignment can have all its positive-energy factors in D. In particular, for charts checked to have only even costs, a positive energy <=2 assignment must violate exactly one factor of cost 2, or have zero energy. Enumerate only factors admitting cost 2, enforce every other factor at zero, retain target factor rows <=2, and reject that possibility with a checked contradiction. A root refutation plus all such exclusions proves E>=4; lift any matching upper witness through exact min-sum traceback. Do not assume evenness for arbitrary E/B inputs.
General fixed-k threshold: if every positive cost >=q>0, energy <=kq implies positive-support size <=k. For fixed k the contexts D of size <=k number Q=sum_{j<=k} binom(m,j). Retain rows with source supports disjoint from D, apply UP separately or in exact bitplanes, and check exact costs of surviving assignments. Each refuted context excludes all positive supports contained in D. For variable k Q need not be polynomial. A single root proof is not generally complete in relaxed contexts; an unavailable support does not falsify a clause semantically.

## Exact context masks and future saturation
Represent each literal's certified forced truth across Q fixed contexts by a Q-bit word. An original or replayed learned row has an activation mask A_C. For clause C=(l_1 OR ... OR l_t), set dead contexts by A_C intersect all forced-false masks B_{-l_j}. Force l_i on A_C intersect all B_{-l_j} for j != i, ignoring already dead contexts. Also mark dead whenever opposite forced literal masks intersect. All operations componentwise implement ordinary sound UP; monotone fixed point equals independent UP on live contexts and conflict detection on the rest (first missing rule argument). Prefix/suffix intersections compute all leave-one-out products in linear Boolean-word operations in t instead of quadratic; for short clauses their constant overhead can be worse. This is exact compression of the context computation, not of the energy values.
New proposed extension with a proved conditional resource ceiling: replace one selected support per learned clause by a context-validity mask. For each fixed-width candidate c, run the exact batched UP on not c, and OR its certified conflicting contexts into its validity mask, retaining a checked derivation DAG or replayable scans. For fixed w and k, M=sum_{j<=w}2^j binom(n,j), at most MQ new clause/context bits, at most MQ+1 full sweeps, polynomial per sweep in explicit rows, n, M, Q and mask bit length. At most 2nQ new propagated literal/context bits per fixed probe. Every failed probe charged. This establishes polynomial acquisition of this specified fixed-chart fixed-width fixed-support saturation, not completeness. Context-specific admissible certificates can be retained without enumerating every possible original support antichain. Must separately pay chart creation, guard transport, evidence, memory, reconstruction and nonaffine changes, and not silently restart credit. New context-saturation loop not implemented in FP59; first missing implication: every unresolved NO admits a polynomially acquired terminal or productive contraction beyond this restricted closure. Width barriers apply to fixed unchanged CNF, not unrestricted algorithms. No P!=NP inference.
