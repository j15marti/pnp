Track 1 (linear flow): correction f xor f' is conserved in both bits. At least one bit on e is nonzero; its support is Eulerian and e belongs to a cycle therein. Any alternate path between original 0 and 1 must traverse >=2 original stretched edges, each shortest passage 3L+1 (L+1 seams and L internal paths of length2), hence shortest cycle through e is 6L+3. Thus changed support >=6L+3 without assumptions on direction or intermediate states.

Track 2 (exact weighted closure): summing all 4 pole vertex equations cancels internal edges; two seam colors equal for EVERY conserved flow, and proper seam colors nonzero. Therefore every proper source coloring induces proper core word. Given p->s!=p, correction boundary delta=p xor s nonzero. Some binary correction coordinate has 1 on both boundary edges, hence internal support includes a port-to-port path (by parity of each component). At least two internal edges necessary. If only two changed internal edges, path length2 with both labels changing by delta (conservation); at each port the initial changed internal edge must be s because other nonseam color is delta and would become zero. At the common intermediate vertex two original s edges contradict proper initial internal coloring. Thus >=3 internal changes. Explicit alternating path above attains exactly3. Unchanged seam admits 0 changes. Chain tariff precisely 4L+1 for changed color, 0 otherwise. Original e must change, and conservation at each of its two endpoints forces at least one other incident edge to change; those two are distinct. Triangle witness attains 2 chain changes, giving exact 8L+3. Connected-support lemma: retaining only the edge-connected correction component containing e leaves conservation intact because correction components share no vertices; other components can revert to f, which has no other zero. This does NOT imply small support.
