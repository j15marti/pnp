## Named obligation: certified defect-budget acquisition
Let exact nonnegative integer residual cost factors f_i on Boolean carrier scopes partition original B-owner IDs, with checked min-sum contraction and reverse argmin map. Original optimum equals min_x sum_i f_i(x). If each entry in each attained table is a multiple of d>0, the residual optimum is a multiple of d. This divisibility is tested on actual tables, never assumed on arbitrary native E/B input. For zero support, every nonzero row is forbidden; direct CNF and full support propagation are independent representations of the same relation.

**Defect-budget coverage lemma (PROVED WITH SCOPE).** For m factors and fixed nonnegative integer k, sum f_i(x)<=dk iff there is a nonnegative integer allocation (b_i), sum b_i<=k, with f_i(x)<=d b_i for all i. Forward choose b_i=f_i(x)/d; reverse sum. At most binom(m+k,k) allocations, polynomial in m for fixed k. No counting oracle. If zero energy is already refuted and k=1, it suffices to check factors having at least one row of cost d: permit that factor <=d, force all others to zero. For every total assignment satisfying a chart, the reverse argmin map realizes the total cost on original B rows. Every zero-infeasible subset C of factor IDs forces sum_{i in C} f_i >= d. Thus the sole positive factor in any energy-d word must lie in C, or in the intersection of all such checked cores. This can shrink the entire disjunction without changing decision semantics. If all eligible charts are refuted then min >=2d. General fractional packing: for checked zero-infeasible cores C_j and rational lambda_j>=0 with sum_{j:i in C_j}lambda_j<=1 for every i, E>=d sum_j lambda_j, by multiplying and adding the core inequalities and using nonnegativity. All arithmetic and support checking paid; no universal core acquisition asserted. This is a native specialization of core-guided weighted optimization, not a claim to invent the general idea.

**Peer owner-core transfer.** Under same authenticated source and complement gauge, attach to each forbidden-word clause its source B owner (one valid owner suffices if identical). Carry unions of owner sets under resolution, and at each conflict collect antecedent supports used by UP. Exhaustive binary tree leaves cover all assignments; union of their supports yields a sufficient source B subset for zero-infeasibility, with gauge discharged by complement. If an exact energy factor has zero cost, its original B-owner set is satisfied by the joint reverse reconstruction (the original cost IDs form a disjoint partition). Hence selecting all residual factors intersecting the peer B-core must form a zero-infeasible subset. Independently re-prove the selected subset in energy-table coordinates before use. This is the connecting lemma changing acquisition domain; it does not require transferring a peer's bare UNSAT label or conflating zero support with full energy equality.
