## Mathematical result
Let S_i be the original zero-energy words of factor i. For a region U of carrier IDs, take ALL original chart factors whose scope lies in U, and enumerate exact nonnegative energy sums. A row a of any contained factor i with no zero-energy extension to U can be excluded from S_i. This is sound for original feasibility: every global zero witness satisfies every contained original factor. No modified positive-energy value is asserted. Disjoint owner IDs and the min-sum reverse map are preserved. Under the complement gauge, any original witness has a complemented representative satisfying the gauge; reconstruction lifts a feasible projected assignment. A row exclusion never assumes the other regions' constraints hold at positive energy.

Define a fixed occurrence-star consistency syndrome: for each retained variable v connect its first factor occurrence to each other occurrence. Every equality edge gets one GF(2) coordinate, and each local factor row contributes its bit at every incident edge. A choice of one allowed row per factor is a global assignment iff its summed syndrome is zero. Fix the linear local encoding L_i, and define D_i=span{L_i(a)-L_i(b):a,b in S_i}. For a nonempty filtered S'_i subset S_i, D'_i subset D_i, independent of chosen anchors. For any FIXED block partition P,Q, U_P=sum_{i in P}D_i and similarly U_Q; width lambda(P)=dim(U_P intersection U_Q)=dim U_P+dim U_Q-dim(U_P+U_Q). Thus lambda'<=lambda. Strict decrease at a measured cut iff the new intersection is a proper subspace of the old, not merely when ambient rank falls. An empty support is an immediate contradiction. Exact target update after anchor change is essential: target equals XOR of all chosen anchors; if inconsistent with aggregate span, affine contradiction; otherwise exact compatible local-option recomposition still needed. All new local affine equations can be extracted by testing masks against all surviving words; dependent equations must not be counted as rank. Any fixed finite tree of cuts admits nonresetting sum-of-cut-width credit on the fixed chart, but neutral refinements can consume work and changing chart/tree requires a separate ledger. This theorem is direct linear algebra, not novelty relative to rank-connectivity theory. It is stronger operationally than reporting a reduced total rank and does not claim a small global tree.
