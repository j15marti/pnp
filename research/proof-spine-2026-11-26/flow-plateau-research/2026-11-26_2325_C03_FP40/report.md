If A forces this square to 2, refute since F3 squares are 0,1. Test forced value over ALL A, not one relaxed point. Repeat after every 128 chart visits and at pass end, until rank stationary. Thus a local zero square changes the domain of subsequent global chart images. This complements FP39, not an asserted performance improvement over its unavailable code.
