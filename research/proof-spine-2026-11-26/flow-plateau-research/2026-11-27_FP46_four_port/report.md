## Exact adapter
Normalize guard and acquire supported graph S by one perfect matching test and one test per allowed edge with endpoints removed. If empty matching family, NO. Connected components V_i of S factor the guarded perfect matching family. Use original G[V_i] for complements, including unsupported internal edges; retain each crossing EDGE as a distinct port even when two ports touch one vertex. Every component has even order and an even number of ports. For a local perfect matching M_i, the complement consists of cycles and port-to-port paths, including a one-vertex path with two ports. Reject if an internal cycle is odd. Label each port by the binary color of its crossing edge (colors 1,2, not vertex bipartition phase). Each path of v vertices pairs its ports with equation x_a XOR x_b=v mod2. Closed even cycles and these paths account for every vertex, hence the XOR of all port colors is 0. Retain the union R_i of these exact pairing relations over all local PMs. Original guarded coloring iff one port word per factor lies in R_i and crossing-edge bits agree. Lift by choosing attaining M_i and alternating the other two colors along paths/cycles. This is not an affine-hull substitution.

## Three complementary tracks
A: exact matching gadget compilation. Nonempty relations for 0 ports are {empty}; for 2 ports equality; for 4 ports each pairing contributes exactly 4 distinct words, all of even weight, and union is complement-closed. Thus size 4,6,8 only. Size4 = one pairing relation; realize equal port pair by one edge, XOR1 by flipping one port's interpretation. Size6 omits complementary even words a,a+1111; flip by a, obtaining exact weight2. Use four port vertices and two private vertices adjacent to all four, so a matching exists after deleting external-used ports iff exactly two remain. Size8 = all even words, use K4. Equality across factors is direct joining edge if port interpretation flips agree; if they differ subdivide joining edge once, requiring exactly one end used externally. Every port vertex either matched internally or externally. Exact in both directions by deleting external-used ports; recover original word and its local witness. O(n) gadget vertices and edges after acquisition. Empty R_i refutes immediately.

B: stronger affine sufficiency in PORT-COLOR coordinates (not arbitrary vertex-phase or arbitrary affine guards). For every factor with <=4 ports, consistency of seam equations and the affine hulls of R_i implies exact compatibility. Proof: 2-port and size4 relations are exact pairs. Suppress these pair wires, retaining XOR signs, and check closed wire cycles for contradiction. Remaining flexible factors (size6 or8) have four ports and hull=even parity. Flip size6 by omitted even word; size8 may voluntarily use weight2. Every remaining vertex is degree4 in a multigraph, loops permitted, with signed wire equation x_p XOR x_q=s_e. Summing the hull equations gives XOR of wire signs zero in each connected component. Take one Euler circuit per component, pair the two ports visited consecutively at each vertex, and require opposite bits within each pair. The unique circuit of wire and pair steps is consistent: XOR of wire signs plus number of pairs is zero, since number of pairs=number of wires=2*vertices. The resulting port word has exactly two ones at every flexible vertex. Undo flips and lift. Thus Gaussian inconsistency is not just sound but complete for this scoped class. This is an existence/constructive lift, not equality between each local hull and exact support. Euler implementation not executed; independent matching compiler and Gaussian were executed.

C: independent direct native edge-color replay, using exhaustive small allowed-component PMs for support and direct proper edge-color recursion on original internal edges with pinned port colors, independent of pairing derivation. Also full capped source PM/complement reference on small controls. New six-port counterexample: C6 prism, forbid all six rungs in the prospective zero matching, hence each cycle is one supported factor. Its exact R is union of equality-pair subspaces (01,23,45) and (12,34,50), 14 words. Bits numbered 0..5, a=33 and b=12 attainable; differing positions {0,2,3,5}. For i=2 flipping i with j=0,3,5 yields 36,45,5, all absent. Even delta-matroid symmetric exchange fails.
