## A. Exact ternary chart
At each v use a bijection c_{v,e} from its three incidences to F3. On ordinary vertices c=f-1; at defective endpoints use increasing incidence IDs. Any permutation of F3 is uniquely g_e=s_v c_{v,e}+t_v with s_v in {1,2}, t_v in F3. For edge uv, t_v-t_u=s_u c_{u,e}-s_v c_{v,e}. Root t=0 per connected component (global translation of all three target colors preserves properness); use a spanning forest to express each t as a linear form in s. Nonforest edges give A s=0. Conversely any full-support sign vector in ker A yields a consistent proper edge coloring by tree lift. Map back to original F2^2 labels 1,2,3; these satisfy D and have no defects. Exactly 3^c original proper colorings per sign word, distinguished by root translations. No guard on target colors assumed; arbitrary pins can break root translation and need a separate adapter. Chart construction polynomial, no satisfying coloring used.

## B. Gaussian closure -> 2SAT
Compute RREF over F3. Each pivot s_p=L_p(x) where free coordinates x_j are in {1,2}; all L_p must be nonzero. Zero form certifies NO; singleton nonzero form imposes no restriction. A two-variable homogeneous form a x+b y is nonzero iff a x=b y (nonzero F3 has exactly two elements). Substitute y=(a/b)x in all forms and retain reverse sign map. Repeat to fixed point, at most n-1 merges. Any produced zero form is a sound NO certificate. All substitutions are unconditional. This is an exact hybrid, not parallel unrelated solvers.
Acquire a set B of remaining free coordinates hitting every residual form all but at most two of its nonzero coordinates. Greedy repeatedly selects a coordinate in the greatest number of currently long forms; deterministic ID tie break. For each of 2^|B| sign words on B, each remaining nonzero form is affine in at most two sign bits. Enumerate its <=4 assignments, forbid those giving zero using a clause of size <=2 (empty clause if fixed zero). Solve conjunction by SCC 2SAT. Replay all substitutions, pivots, tree offsets and original labels. Thus deterministic exact decision and witness on admitted sources in poly(N)*(1+2^|B|) bit work, polynomial working space with serial branches; if |B|<=K log2 N for one universal K, polynomial. ## C. Rank/geometry and exact trellis alternative
Let U be unsigned vertex-edge incidence over F3. A vector in ker U has incident triple either all equal or all distinct. In the same local charts it is uniquely s_v c+t_v allowing s_v=0. The map ker U -> ker A onto signs has kernel exactly the c componentwise constant edge-label vectors, so dim ker A=m-rank(U)-c=m-n+b-c, where b counts bipartite connected components (left kernel y_u+y_v=0). In a connected cubic graph bipartite gives m-n+1-1=n/2; nonbipartite gives m-n-1=n/2-1. Thus raw dimension is linear even on easy families. 
For an ordering of the n columns a_i of A, let P_i and Q_i be spans of prefix and suffix, and w_i=dim(P_i intersect Q_i). Enumerate reachable partial sums of selected nonzero signs, retaining only those in Q_i; combine identical sums, keep witness predecessor and optional integer multiplicity. Future feasibility depends only on the exact sum; this is a joint support join, not affine hull substitution. At most 3^w_i retained states, total poly(N)*3^max w_i, bits of multiplicities <=n+1 (sign choices). Greedy ordering minimizes next exact width using paid rank computations, no optimality asserted. For connected G and both induced sides S,T connected, w=|delta(S)|+b(G)-1-b(S)-b(T), b(H)=1 iff connected H bipartite. Proof: codewords supported in S correspond bijectively to unsigned-incidence solutions on internal edges of G[S], since all edge labels touching T are constant (subtract that common offset); dimension |E(S)|-|S|+b(S). Same for T. w=dim C-dim C_S-dim C_T for C=ker A, giving formula. No logarithmic-width theorem follows.
Additional exact contraction: two nonzero parallel A columns a,ka with free signs have attainable sum equal to full span(a). Quotient this span and eliminate both, lifting from the two-sign lookup; zero column free. Repeat only under exact support; this may be useful after regional decomposition. On simple 3-vertex-connected G no initial column is zero or proportional to another: a dependency supported on <=2 vertices gives a nonzero sign word in ker A; all other vertices form a connected induced graph and force their common constant edge value; each exceptional vertex has at least two incidences to that complement, hence must also be constant in a valid F3 triple. Contradiction. This defeats that initial contraction on prime sources, not unrestricted algorithms.
