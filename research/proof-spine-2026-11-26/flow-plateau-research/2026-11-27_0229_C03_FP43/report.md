**Proof.** For a pair h,f partition colorings by equality vs disequality. Translate h to 0; reflect if necessary so f=1 in the disequal case. Required membership masks at (h,f) of the three color matchings are (11,00,00) and (10,01,00) respectively. Compute exact F3 linear spans H_i of ordinary matchings in each mask; empty family refutes case. Lovasz span acquisition polynomial over a fixed finite field after ordinary required/forbidden deletion. Restore forced coordinates via common residual star sum, or single complete matching if no residual vertices. Optional fixed-r odd cycle filters use union of spans over hit tuples. No filter needed in the four reported hybrid refutations.

Join p_i in H_i simultaneously with common scalar t, Bp_i=t*1, p0+p1+p2=t*1. Let y=p1-p2-((p1-p2)_0)*1. Impose R y=0 and require F(y) and t nonzero. Actual colorings lift with t=1 and original y up to reflection; homogeneous constraints preserve that symmetry. On each joint linear space, if a required form vanishes identically, reject case. If a,b,a+s*b are required nonzero, s=+/-1, import a=s*b over F3. Close until no independent equality remains. Project surviving spaces to y; their sum contains all actual colorings, so annihilator gives common necessary equations. Both cases refuted -> NO; equal case refuted -> new required nonzero difference; unequal case refuted -> equality. Reclose after every import. Never claim a span is exact support. K4 with matching vectors M1,M2,M3 permits p0=M3, p1=p2=2M1+2M2,t=1 in the equal branch for the two edges of M3, but y=0 violates required nonzero forms. Linear consistency alone not sufficient; if actual y satisfies all vertex requirements and By=0, y itself is a proper coloring regardless of pseudo p.
