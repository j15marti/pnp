## New cooperative theorem
Suppose verified local transfer polynomials q_R sum identically to -1, and q_R is nonnegative on all zero-energy assignments of factors contained in R. For candidate single-defect factor d, define
m_R(d)=min q_R(a) over R words with all contained factors at zero except d (if contained), which must have cost exactly two. An empty domain refutes d. Otherwise any global E=2 word with defect d satisfies -1=sum q_R >= sum m_R(d). Hence sum m_R(d)>-1 refutes d, with no double counting of owner energy: it is an inequality about transfer scores, not summing owner costs. If chart has only even nonnegative costs, E=2 must have one such d. Exact integer coefficients suffice here; rational extension by common denominator. A selected dual packet is verified on all ORIGINAL zero supports, not a restrictive pseudodistribution. Fixed explicit region width O(log N), polynomial count, polynomial bit-size coefficients and polynomial acquisition/check of chart/dual imply polynomial candidate screen.
