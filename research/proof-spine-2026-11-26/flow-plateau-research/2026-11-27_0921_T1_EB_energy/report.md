## Exact energy theorem
For every consistent signed equality network, graph traversal gives x_p = y_{r(p)} XOR s_p, retaining original port IDs. Contradictory equality cycles are terminal NO. For unguarded networks simultaneous complement preserves all signatures and energy, so one root can be normalized to zero; here highest-frequency B root port 10, gauge only, not source advice. Under arbitrary guards do not use that gauge unless guard symmetry or coverage is proved. Define E(y)=sum over original B vertices of 1[sum of its four physical bits !=2]. Then feasibility iff min E=0, all costs nonnegative integer <= #B. Keep duplicate rows as separate costs, even when their feasibility constraints coincide.

If current factors partition original cost IDs and v occurs in factors I, let S be their joint scope minus v. Replace exactly those factors by g(y_S)=min_{b=0,1} sum_{i in I} f_i(y_S,b). Other factors do not mention v, hence for every assignment to remaining variables min over v of previous energy equals new energy. Record both candidate sums, argmin (choose smaller bit on tie) and original B-ID list. Composition gives exact reverse reconstruction. This is known min-sum bucket elimination; adaptation is native signed port provenance, three-port energy formula, and independently replayed concrete certificates, not a novel general DP theorem.

Three-port specialization: min over a private fourth bit of weight-p balance violation equals p*[the remaining three physical bits all equal]. Indeed sum 1 or 2 permits unique slack, sum 0 or 3 does not. Signed and repeated ports are evaluated on the actual root assignment. Private means no other live factor uses the bit except the explicitly grouped copies. Two NAE3 factors of positive weights p,q sharing one private bit with opposite signs minimize exactly to min(p,q)*[A=B=C=f] over the four outer bits: if A=B then first demands z=!A, if C=f second demands z=C; conflict iff all four equal. Otherwise satisfy both; on conflict choose which weight to pay. This works without guessing a z. All 8 private-slack and 16*9 weighted split cases executed separately per input.

Corollary for the pinned compiler: for any original truth assignment relative to anchor, after optimizing all introduced variables and respecting all E/sign equations, native B energy is exactly 2 times the number of unsatisfied original clauses. Duplicates contribute weight two. Each long-clause existential chain has min false ternary count zero if source true, otherwise one: for all-false originals choose chain auxiliaries all zero, first ternary false, all later true; for satisfied source choose the standard satisfying chain assignment. Short repeated-literal padding preserves violation; empty clause gives opposing repeated units, exactly one false. Independent clause auxiliaries compose. This is a statement about this compiler, not free inverse recognition for arbitrary native input.

GAC pins preserve zero feasibility, not necessarily the positive optimum. Both tested inputs have no initial pins besides the symmetry gauge, hence the positive lower bound is valid for the full equality-consistent source. For future inputs with additional pins, report only guarded positive optimum unless cost-equivalence proved; unconditional zero-feasibility refutation remains sound.

## Resource ledger and first unresolved arrow

Each new table has <=2^w rows; evaluating both bit values costs O(N 2^w) factor evaluations per elimination, plus paid scans and index operations; keeping source-ID partitions and reverse records costs at most O(N^2 + N 2^w) indexed words. One conservative straightforward deterministic bit bound for all scans, copies, reclosure, replay and log operations with w<=C log N is O(N^6 2^(2w)) including simple sequential-access overhead (not optimized).
