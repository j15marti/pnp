## Named theorem and proof
On the exact FP51 min-sum chart with hard signed edges/E and complement gauge port10=0, costs F_i nonnegative even integers with disjoint original B-owner sets. Let I be a cluster of distinct factors, U their union scope, and define h_{I,i}(a)=min_{x_U:x_{S_i}=a} sum_{j in I} F_j(x_{S_j}) for i in I. Then any global assignment with row a at i has energy at least h_{I,i}(a). A cluster lower bound is min_a h; a row lower bound is max over clusters containing i (NOT sum over overlapping clusters). With clipping at 4, exact min-sum commutes with clipping, hence preserves threshold <=2 tests. If h_{I,i}(a)>0 and original row cost zero, the row may be excluded in zero-energy decision only; if h>=4 it is excluded for total energy<=2. Do not impose zero-energy deductions in positive-energy optimization. If for every original row a there exists an extension with all other factors j in I at zero cost, h_{I,i}(a)=F_i(a), without clipping. This gives a finite explicit survival/refusal certificate for all tested clusters. Independent verification uses Cartesian factor-row joins or the explicit extension witness. For fixed max factor count t, union width w, polynomial chart size, discovery O(m^t 2^w poly(N)) with fixed t,w (or w=O(log N)); integer arithmetic exact. Credit for unique threshold-row exclusions at most 2 sum_i 2^|S_i| in this fixed chart; no credit for duplicates or resetting chart. Universal acquisition and transport across nonaffine transformations not proved. Exact local joins and projection are standard inference, not a new universal algorithm.
