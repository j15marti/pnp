## Exact theorem
For r supplied original odd simple cycles C=(C1,...,Cr), define P_C as all perfect matchings intersecting each cycle. Every color class of a proper 3-edge-coloring lies in P_C: its complement consists of even cycles and cannot contain an odd cycle. Under required/forbidden edges P,F, P_C(P,F) equals the union of ordinary guarded matching families P(P union {e1,...,er},F) over ei in E(Ci). Reject conflicting pins, delete required endpoints and forbidden edges, solve ordinary perfect matching, lift. Overlap irrelevant to existence and affine span, not counting. At most m^r queries, or L^r if cycle lengths <=L. For nonempty families, acquire exact F3 affine hulls via Lovasz finite-field matching-span theorem on residual graph, intersect linear span with vertex-star sum=1, lift fixed coordinates, take affine span of union. Empty residual unique matching. This is polynomial for fixed r, NOT counting.

Imports: if P_C({e,f},empty) empty, y_e-y_f !=0; if P_C({e},{f}) empty, y_e=y_f. If P_C({e},empty) empty or P_C(empty,{h}) empty, NO. If H p=b on P_C(empty,{h}), both complementary color classes avoid h and their signed incidence difference equals y-y_h*1 over F3; hence H(y-y_h*1)=0. All cycle constraints are universal to every color class; never apply a single-color guard symmetrically without justification.

On B y=0 over F3 (unsigned incidence), required vertex difference l_v nonzero exactly excludes all-equal vertex colors. If l_a+s*l_b is proportional to required nonzero l_c on current linear space, s=1 or 2, import l_a=s*l_b; otherwise two nonzero F3 values could cancel, contradicting the required nonzero sum. Every independent linear row reduces dimension. Pair bans add required nonzero forms to dictionary, exposing new lines.
