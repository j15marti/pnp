## New theorem: exact energy lift
For every assignment z of a C04 graph constructed from CNF F, set a(z)=1_(E minus M(z)). Then
min_{b in ker D} Psi(a(z),b) = number of false clauses of F at z.
Proof of general fixed-a reduction: let E0={e:a_e=0}; put j_e=1+b_e on E0. Feasibility of filling b on support(a) is exactly even demand on each support connected component (incidence image is even vectors, tree solution). Summing original cubic degrees on component C gives parity |delta_E0(C)|=|C| mod2. Thus contracted j has odd vertex set T={C:|C| odd}; its size is Psi. Loops can be omitted; disconnected components have even T cardinality. Conversely lift any T-join by solving the binary incidence equations on each component; minimum T-join via shortest-path metric PM. On C04 graph each false clause supplies two odd components, so any T-join needs >= |T|/2 = #false clauses edges. The fixed allowed K2 for each false clause directly joins its own two odd components. Those K2s are disjoint as terminal pairs and form a T-join attaining the lower bound. No hidden cost for b: choose b=1 on M except these K2s, solve along each even-demand complement cycle, verify D b=0 and exact defect. Both directions proved. The entire guarded optimized flow landscape is thus precisely the unweighted MaxSAT energy, not just zero/nonzero. Guarded minimum defect zero is NP-complete via standard distinct-variable 3SAT (pad short clauses and duplicate clauses to achieve d>=2); bounded-switch local search is not thereby a general decider. No unrestricted lower bound or Boolean/arithmetic transfer.

## Infinite NO plateau family
r>=3 ring switches x0,...,x_(r-1), auxiliary free bit z. Formula has 8 clauses on (x0,x1,z) excluding all eight words (constant violation cost1), four 3-clauses per ring edge excluding each word with x_i != x_(i+1) regardless of z, and four clauses excluding x0=0 regardless of x1,z. Retain each physical clause even if literal strings repeat. All clauses contain three distinct variables. n=80r+240, m=3n/2, exactly 2^(r+1) ordinary guarded PMs. By the energy theorem, f(x,z)=1+sum_i [x_i != x_(i+1)] + [x0=0]. Every assignment is guarded NO. At all-zero x, f=2, independent of z; at all-one x, f=1. Every nonempty proper subset of real ring bits flipped from zero makes at least two disagreements, so f>=3. The component of the all-zero point under nonincreasing moves flipping <r real switches is exactly its two auxiliary-bit variants. Escape to a lower value requires flipping all r real bits in one move if nonincrease is required. This is a linear-radius barrier in SWITCH coordinates, not a lower bound on graph edits, algorithms, or arbitrary fixed-b/fixed-(a+b) directions. With uphill steps allowed the barrier is exactly one defect unit (flip consecutive bits starting at x0). No universal strictly productive bounded-switch move, even on guarded NOs. These instances are easy by clause/affine closure and not post-full-portfolio hard.
