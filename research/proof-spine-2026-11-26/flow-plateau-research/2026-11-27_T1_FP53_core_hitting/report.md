## Target and exact lemma
STRICT-EB4-v1, exact hard signed transport and E-equalities, integer B occupancy. In a certified min-sum chart, assume nonnegative integer costs f_i on disjoint original owner sets, all divisible by d>0. A symmetry pin is justified only by hard-carrier invariance. Write P(x)={i:f_i(x)>0}. At energy <=dk, |P(x)|<=k. If a guarded set C of zero-cost constraints is infeasible on the hard carrier under G, then G implies sum_C f_i>=d, hence P(x) intersects C whenever G holds. Only jointly applicable guards may be combined. To exclude all supports inside any proposed block D, refute the zero constraints outside D (under the same guard). An independently verified proof restricted to a core C disjoint from D excludes EVERY support P with P intersect C empty, including every P subset D. This is stronger than individually testing singletons. Feasibility of the relaxed system outside D is NOT a threshold witness: evaluate the actual full energy, and if necessary constrain retained factors to their exact cost budget. Cap failure is UNKNOWN.

For fixed k, enumerate at most sum_{j<=k} binom(m,j) potential support sets. For each support candidate P, collect the guards of all certified cores disjoint from P; if their union covers the hard carrier then P is impossible. If all guards are affine predicates in a shared affine carrier and their restricted linear forms span rank t, acquire that rank and row-basis by GE. Parameterize the attained syndrome image; it has 2^t elements (if carrier nonempty). For each syndrome, determine all applicable guards, eliminate P if it misses any corresponding core. Count <=2^t * sum_{j<=k} binom(m,j) live pairs (syndrome,P). A new verified core obtained under the corresponding entire syndrome and disjoint from a live P eliminates at least that pair. Thus the screening and its progress potential are polynomial for fixed k and t<=C log_2 N, assuming polynomial acquired evidence/number of tests, and polynomial factor representation. No free acquisition oracle. The experiment used 3 or 4 distinct pin coordinates, not a general affine-rank implementation. For five affine guards alone, union cardinality may also be calculated from 31 GE intersection counts. Arbitrary guard-cover checking is coNP-hard even for conjunctions of Boolean pins: take independent hard bits and one guard for the falsification of each CNF clause; cover iff the CNF is UNSAT. This is a scoped coverage-task obstruction, not an arbitrary-decider lower bound; do not require arbitrary covers in the solver.

Complete core duality (support count only): P intersects all zero-infeasible subsets iff zero constraints outside P are feasible; if those are infeasible the whole complement is a disjoint core. Full core acquisition can be exponential, and a surviving support may require costly factors. Universal polynomial negative coverage, energy threshold completeness for variable k, and universal E/B contraction remain OPEN.
