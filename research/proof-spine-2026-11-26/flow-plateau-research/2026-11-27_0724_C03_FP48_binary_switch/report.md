## Native three-switch obstruction to universal binary completeness
Three C48 cycles at original offsets 0,48,96 and a reservoir C96 at offset144. For each j=0..7 with binary triple b, allocate local occurrence pair in cycle i at ports 6j+b_i,6j+b_i+1. Form three cyclic clause wires from the second port at i to the first port at i+1 mod3, one sign-one (last wire) and two direct. Pair the eight sign vertices by four allowed K2 edges. Connect each of the 32 spare ports per real cycle, sorted, to successive reservoir ports 144+3j+i. Only cycle edges and four K2 edges allowed in M. Simple connected triangle-free, n=248. Exactly 16 guarded PMs (four binary factors). For every real switch triple the corresponding occurrence circuit is an original odd 7-cycle, independent of reservoir choice; all 8 real triples fail. No unary or binary partial odd-circuit conflict. Proof: a real factor's alternate local pairs never connect reserved ports of different clauses; inactive occurrences lead into spare ports and unpinned factors. With two real factors pinned no whole clause circuit closes, and reservoir unpinned. With reservoir plus one real pinned every reservoir pair joins ports of distinct real factors, so no closed circuit can contain only those pinned factors. Remaining flexible factor quotient connected in either case; total even sign parity and even pinned-factor parity imply conditional affine parity feasibility. Thus even the stronger full conditional affine hull on every one/two switch probe is consistent. All 32 probes pass. Binary closure cannot refute; 8 triple pins on the same three real factors refute all choices. This FALSIFIES universal binary closure completeness, even with only two PMs per nontrivial factor. Fixed three-variable full exclusion is sound and polynomial by scanning O(r^3) subsets; no claim arbitrary 3CNF resolution is polynomial. This hostile source is easy by exhaustive reference (16 PMs), not an all-decider LB.
