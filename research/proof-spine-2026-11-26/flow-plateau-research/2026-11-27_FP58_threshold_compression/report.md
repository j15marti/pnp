## 1. Exact threshold theorem
Let all cost tables be nonnegative integers, with hard signed equality enforced first. Use E=sum_i F_i. On the two pinned native charts only, every factor cost is even (checked during FP51 chart reconstruction), so use scaled costs E/2. Do not assume evenness for arbitrary STRICT-EB4-v1 inputs; K5 control below has energy1. For integer threshold K>=1 set c_K(a)=min(K,a). Then c_K(min(a,b))=min(c_K(a),c_K(b)), and c_K(a+b)=min(K,c_K(a)+c_K(b)), by nonnegativity. Inductively EVERY row of a fixed min-plus computation commutes with clipping. On an exact complete elimination, c_K(min E) preserves whether min E >= t, equivalently min E < t, for every t<=K. It does not distinguish min E=K from min E>K. A decoded witness below K must have exact original energy below K if reconstructed through exact buckets; verify original constraints and energy. If split buckets used, result is a lower bound only; no relaxed witness is a source witness.
For a fixed split plan (mini-bucket elimination), consume each factor exactly once and eliminate v independently in each group of its current incident factors. Since sum min <= min sum, resulting constant L<=min E. Every row of clipped calculation matches c_K of exact numeric RELAXED calculation. A coarse threshold-one result zero implies threshold-two result zero on same plan: there is no benefit in increasing precision alone on this plateau. If coarse is one, selectively compute threshold-two. This is standard bounded valuation/min-plus arithmetic and mini-buckets, adapted to energy chart/guard semantics; not claimed original unrestricted complexity theory.

## 2. Exact two-bitplane representation
For scaled cost a in {0,1,2}, encode A=[a>=1], B=[a>=2]. B implies A. Saturating sum with another cost planes (a,b) gives A'=A OR a, B'=B OR b OR (A AND a), using old A in second equation. Min of two rows is AND of threshold planes, since both rows must meet a threshold for their minimum to meet it. For an entire truth table store each plane as a bit vector. Lift a narrower scope by inserting/duplicating missing-variable coordinates; use standard bit spreading with masks. On scope union U of size w, planes have 2^w bits. For eliminating variable j, intersect with shifted copy, mask zero-coordinate rows and compact; proof: each output bit is AND of the two rows with identical other coordinates. Exhaustively checked 862 small mask expansion/elimination cases per run, all intermediate plane rows against separately computed numeric rows on both inputs (JOINT14242, RANDOM65224 at K=2). Exact values above threshold cannot be recovered from the planes: preserve original input or exact residual trace. BigInt width charged; not unit-cost unbounded arithmetic. For explicit scopes and width w=O(log N), fixed polynomial bound for constructing, computing and checking each plan; arbitrary width not assumed cheap.

## 3. Monotone split-copy synchronization theorem
A fixed mini-bucket plan has a precise relaxation semantics: when eliminating v from multiple groups, introduce one independent choice of v for each group. The min over the expanded assignment space is exactly the computed relaxed minimum (induct on ordered elimination; each factor's occurrence of v is associated to the unique group that consumes it at its elimination). There is a diagonal embedding of every original assignment setting all copies equal. Synchronize any set S of split variables by enforcing equal choices of all their copies, via exhaustive guards b in {0,1}^S. Evaluate SAME plan under those pins (restrict choice at each elimination of v in S), then take minimum over all guard bounds. This is exact minimum of a smaller relaxation. Thus L_S<=L_T<=min E for S subset T. When T contains every split variable, all original variable choices are consistent, and L_T=min E. With threshold cap K, same statement after clipping. Branch cells that already meet K need not refine, but keep their guard and certificate in exact cover.
Let s be count of split variables, w=max union width. A fixed plan can be constructed in poly(N) for bounded explicit scopes, number of groups <= removed factors, total steps O(nm). Full synchronization with fixed schedule uses at most 2^s times polynomial overhead times 2^w; hence polynomial if s+w=O(log N) with a fixed constant and the plan/selection/acquisition itself is uniformly polynomial. Numeric costs have O(log original total cost) bits, guard/map and reconstruction charged. This is a conditional terminal, related to bounded-width backdoors. It does NOT prove that every input has such a plan, that it is discoverable, or that selective refinement gives polynomial total leaves. A decrease in unresolved threshold leaves is not automatically a global UCAC potential decrease when splitting creates children. Numeric potential cannot be reset. First OPEN arrow: polynomially acquired split-consistency repair or complementary checked productive step on every reachable NO state, with globally paid branches, imports and failed work. Scope-specific affine quotient credit not used across nonaffine energy splits.
