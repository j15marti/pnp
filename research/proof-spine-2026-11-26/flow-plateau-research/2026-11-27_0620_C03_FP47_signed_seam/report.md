## Theorem 1: signed-pairing class is closed under exact regional gluing
Take two disjoint factor scopes (or one factor with a self-wire) and impose any set of disjoint wire equations x_a XOR x_b=s. Join their exact tables and existentially delete the wired ports. For each selected local pairing alternative, the union of pairing edges and wire edges is a multigraph of degree at most two. Every component is a path with two remaining ports, or a closed circuit. A closed circuit is inconsistent iff XOR of its edge signs is 1; discard that alternative. Every consistent path projects EXACTLY to one signed pair equation on its endpoints, with sign equal to XOR along path. Conversely a satisfying endpoint word extends by path propagation and arbitrary root bit on consistent closed circuits. Thus the new table remains a union of signed perfect-pairing spaces. If all original alternatives had fixed total parity p_i, the new common parity is XOR_i p_i XOR XOR_wire s (sum all bit equations). Zero-port nonempty factor has the empty word; empty table refutes. This proof does not enumerate pairing alternatives in production: exact word-table join/filter/project suffices. Retain a child witness pointer per attained output word. Multiple parallel wires and loops are not deduplicated.

In particular a six-port factor with an acquired self-wire becomes a <=4-port factor. Two six-port factors sharing >=4 wires merge to <=4 ports; sharing 3 wires merges to 6. Exact paired factors may be eliminated by path composition, potentially creating self-wires at previously exceptional factors. This is a genuine domain-changing hybrid: affine fixed-pair equations expose which nonlinear table ports must be joined BEFORE re-taking affine hulls.

## Theorem 2: signed four-port terminal
Nonempty uniform-parity complement-closed union of signed pairing spaces on four ports has size 4,6,8, since each alternative contributes four and any union is complement-closed in a parity class of eight. Size4 is one exact signed pairing. Size6 omits a complementary pair a,a+1111; XOR translate by a to obtain exact weight2. Size8 translates by any word of its parity class to all even words. For two ports the relation is a single exact signed pair; zero ports trivial. Suppress all exact pairs and reject inconsistent closed wires. Remaining flexible factors are 4-regular vertices, possibly loops/multiple edges. Translate their ports as above; translated hulls are even parity. A consistent hull assignment implies XOR of wire signs zero in each connected component. Choose an Euler circuit and require opposite bits at each successive port pair visit. The combined circuit has XOR wire signs plus 2 per vertex, hence zero; propagate bits. This gives exactly weight2 at every flexible factor, hence an actual compatible word. Undo translations and lift child pointers. Therefore for this class affine consistency is equivalent to exact decision, including odd original parity after seam contraction. Alternatively compile size6 to 4 ports joined to 2 private vertices (exactly two internal matches), size8 to K4, and exact pair to an edge; opposite interpretation across a seam uses a subdivision vertex. Matching compilation is exact, but Euler or gadget not needed in the family test, which reduces to signed cycles. No arbitrary-arity affine sufficiency.

## Theorem 3: guarded native connected NO family, and falsifier of global six-port affine sufficiency
For even r>=2, use r C6 factors with vertices 6i+j; only cycle edges allowed in M. Pair C6 factors i,i+1 for even i using a K2 (vertices 6r+i,6r+i+1) whose two endpoints attach by forbidden edges to ports 0,1 of their respective cycles. This forces signed self-wire x0 XOR x1=1 in each C6. Ring seams connect port2_i to port5_(i+1), and port3_i to port4_(i+1), indices mod r. On i=0 insert one endpoint of an additional K2 into each ring seam (vertices 7r,7r+1); its internal edge is allowed, all other seams forbidden. This inserts a sign1 into each ring. Graph simple, cubic, connected; n=7r+2,m=3n/2. All allowed edges supported; exactly 2^r guarded PMs, from two matchings per C6 and unique K2 matching. Each C6 relation R=A union B, A pairs (01,23,45) equal; B pairs (12,34,50) equal; |R|=14 and aff(R) is even parity (two rank3 subspaces intersect in rank1). All K2 factors exact signed pairs. Hull feasibility: after self-wire substitution each C6 requires XOR of ports 2,3,4,5 equal 1. In connected ring graph incidence equations are consistent because sum RHS=r mod2=0 and inserted wire signs sum 0. No unary pin.
Exact contraction: self-wire x0 XOR x1=1 removes A, projects B to x2 XOR x5=1, x3 XOR x4=0. Now first ring XOR signs r+1 odd, second ring XOR 1 odd. Contradiction. All local tables before contraction nonempty. Removing the extra K2 and using direct seams restores even ring parity, a minimal soundness control. This is a guarded source (not necessarily unguarded NO). Raw six-port affine sufficiency FALSIFIED. The triangles at each self-wire force edge01 into every even-complement PM, independently yielding complement cycles of sizes 2r+1 and 5r+1. This is an easier structural bypass and is a certified initial forced-information closure if that rule is enabled. Thus the family is not hard post-full-closure, no exclusive hybrid advantage over that structural rule.
