## B. Global charge and prime characterization
Use integer charge q=n-2 for connected loopless cubic piece of n vertices. At 2 split (n1+n2=n), sum child q=q-2. At 3 split (child sizes n1+1,n2+1), sum child q=q. Every child strictly smaller (nontrivial 3-cut sides odd and >=3; 2-cut sides even >=2). Terminal n=2 consists of three parallel edges, trivially colorable. No n=2 leaf is generated by a 3 split. Let I2 be number of 2-splits, L+ positive-charge leaves, L0 zero-charge leaves. Telescoping q_initial=sum leaf q+2I2; hence L+ + I2 <= (n-2)/2, and L0<=2I2 for n>=4. Thus L<=n-2, number of nodes <=2n-5 (for n=2 handle separately). Early failed branches can be counted against the complete structural tree. Total leaf vertex mass sum n_i=sum q_i+2L=O(n). Therefore for any fixed c>=1 the sum of vertex-scale leaf tariffs O(n_i^c) is O(n^c). For original bit length N and explicit normalized IDs, encoded child sizes O(n_i log N), so query sum O((n log N)^c), modulo a fixed encoding polynomial; all cap construction and discovery separate. Unlike a defect-count potential, q accounts for both children when a cut duplicates a defect. At most O(n) internal splits, each O(n^4) adjacency-list operations via exhaustive triples and traversal; conservative polynomial deterministic bit overhead (e.g. O(N^8) with direct scans). Store all piece graphs and maps O(n^2 log N) bits with dense renumbering, plus polynomial indexing buffers; sequential queries, peak is storage plus maximum query space; cumulative memory <= total time times peak on same run. Actual reference search is not this hypothetical bound.
No bridge, no 2-bond, no nontrivial 3-bond at leaf. Parallel pair would induce a 2-cut around its endpoints unless the graph is the two-vertex terminal; triple parallel is entire connected piece. Hence leaf simple. Any cyclic edge cut of size <=3 yields a nontrivial such bond (for <=3 sides connected by bridgelessness), contradiction. Thus nonterminal leaf is cyclically 4-edge-connected. Triangle implies nontrivial 3-cut unless K4, but squares can survive. K4 and bipartite leaves have direct terminals (perfect matching plus alternate even complement), although general matching implementation was not executed in audit. No joint logarithmic-girth/prime claim.
