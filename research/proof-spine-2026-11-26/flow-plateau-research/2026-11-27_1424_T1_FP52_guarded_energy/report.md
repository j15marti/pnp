## Claim
On a hard E/edge-consistent chart with nonnegative even integer min-sum factors f_i and disjoint original owner sets, suppose a verified guarded core C satisfies sum_{i in C} f_i >= 2 on guard G. For energy <=2 under two simultaneous verified guards, the single positive factor must occur in the intersection of their eligible core factors (cost table includes 2). An empty intersection refutes energy <=2 under their joint guard. If an exhaustive set of such guard intersections is acquired and each energy<=2 chart independently rejected, global minimum >=4 follows, with complement gauge justified by symmetry. Nonempty intersection is necessary, never sufficient. More generally the positive factor set hits every applicable verified core; fixed k positive factors can be enumerated in O(m^k) branches only if k fixed and the downstream checks and total acquisition are paid. Conditional core intersections alone do not imply zero infeasibility. For an energy bound, guard coverage must cover the HARD affine carrier (or exact projected energy chart), not merely the zero-energy solution relaxation. If a carrier used soft constraints, escaping it has to be charged separately. These claims follow directly by summing nonnegative even factors and exact argmin reconstruction. Proof system scope only.
