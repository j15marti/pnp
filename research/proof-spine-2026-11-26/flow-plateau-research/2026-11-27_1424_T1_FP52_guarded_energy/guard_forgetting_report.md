# FP52 addendum: guard-forgetting avoids a Cartesian branch tariff

PROVED WITH SCOPE (author audit). Given two exhaustive partitions of the hard energy chart into checked guards G_i,H_j, let C_i,D_j be their zero-infeasible core factor sets. At energy 2, a unique positive factor p exists, and p belongs to C_i intersect D_j for the actual joint region. For fixed G_i, it follows that p is in the union over compatible j of C_i intersect D_j, rather than requiring an independent downstream solver call per joint cell. Further remove a candidate if no cost-2 row of its factor is consistent with all joint pins. Taking the union of surviving candidate indices over all compatible cells gives a sound unconditional necessary restriction on the sole positive factor. Proof: the actual assignment belongs to a joint cell, its positive factor hits both cores, and its own row is one of the tested rows. This establishes safe existential projection of the candidate ID, not a witness or sufficient condition. If a core was verified in original owner coordinates, transfer via the exact min-sum argmin reconstruction as in FP51. No assumption that core intersection itself is inconsistent. This is a polynomial screen for a constant number of acquired guards, with all downstream searches still charged.
