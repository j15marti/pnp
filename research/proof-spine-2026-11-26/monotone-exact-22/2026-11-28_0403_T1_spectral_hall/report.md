## Mathematical result
Let a candidate set of disjoint positive demand clauses have interiors occurring positively only in their own demand and otherwise only in binary clauses containing the negated interior literal. The complete incident clause set is mandatory, with no missing constraints. In any model choose one true interior variable in each demand and set the other interior variables false: all outside clauses are unchanged, all incident negative occurrences can only be helped. Form the graph on interiors using negative pair clauses between distinct demands. Its connected components are proposed bins. Verify that each component is a complete multipartite graph across distinct demand owners (same-demand edges irrelevant to selected-one witnesses). Verify every member of a bin has the same set of external trigger literals, i.e. l blocks the bin precisely when an incident clause (!v OR !l) is violated by choosing v. Retain the original exterior clauses and map each demand/bin edge to an original interior option. Under these checks, feasible words of the region are exactly those for which a matching covers all demands in the unblocked bins. Reconstruction sets one option per matched demand true, other interiors false. Check all original clauses after lifting. No assumption about independent boundary selectors.

Let d>0 demand count, q bin count and k=q-d>=0. **Clone certificate for robust slack:** for every demand i, match the d demands plus k clones of i, each having exactly i's neighborhood, to q bins. All d matchings exist iff every nonempty demand subset S has |N(S)|>=|S|+k. Necessity: choose i in S and include all k clones; Hall applies. Sufficiency: any nonempty subset of cloned graph maps to original subset S and has at most |S|+k members; Hall applies. Each certificate checks each assignment of a demand/clone to an incident bin, injectivity, full coverage. Matching generation is polynomial even for arbitrary k<=q; no subset enumeration or min-cut numeric precision. The d matching witnesses have O(dq log q) bits. With robustness, arbitrary signed OR triggers yield exact interface sum_j OR_{l in T_j} l <=k. If at most k bins are disabled Hall survives; if more, cardinality alone prevents covering d. This extends prior robust-shell work with a direct discrete matching witness and a sufficient source recognizer, not universal discovery.

**Compositional terminal:** for k=0 the exact interface is unary clauses negating all trigger literals. For k=1 it is the 2-CNF of (!l OR !t) for every pair of triggers on DIFFERENT bins (tautologies may be discarded; equal triggers yield units). Both directions follow because >1 blocked bin is equivalent to a pair of true triggers on different bins. Any number of disjoint-interior certified shells of slack 0/1 can be projected into one shared exterior 2-CNF, even with arbitrarily overlapping boundary variables, provided the full incident clause sets are consumed once and the other shell interiors are not used as external boundary. Recompose independent matching witnesses under the same exterior assignment. Additionally one robust shell of slack <=c log2(original N) with arbitrary signed OR blockers is handled by the previous exact Almost-2SAT reduction (hard trigger implications, one soft unit per bin; hard clauses protected by k+1 private two-clause paths). Ramanujan-Saurabh O(4^k k^4 ell) implies fixed polynomial total terminal on this admitted class, not executed FPT code. Multiple distinct positive slack>1 budgets cannot be replaced by their sum. Partial recognized regions may be eliminated with a preserved interface, subject to representation and global-credit accounting; cannot independently combine overlapping interiors.
