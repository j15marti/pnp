## A: exact regional table and local retraction (constructive)

Rank-two row has three allowed local functionals on its plane P_i (offsets determine forbidden state). All original rows remain obligations for final reconstruction.
For a region A let U_A=sum of its P_i, V_A=sum of the exterior planes, B_A=U_A intersection V_A. The exact regional response S_A consists of all functionals on B_A attainable by some functional on U_A satisfying its rows. This is not its affine hull. Given an exterior satisfying functional on V_A, extension to the region exists iff its restriction to B_A is in S_A: equality on the intersection is precisely the necessary and sufficient condition for a well-defined sum functional on U_A+V_A; extend to the full chart. Actual local witness retained. If S_A is empty, reject. If S_A is full, retract. If S_A is affine, compute its exact defining equations and retain them as unconditional global facts, then retract. ## B: exact tree DP and width monotonicity (global algebraic)
Let a rooted binary tree have leaves indexed by all original rank-two planes (rank-zero/one also allowed). For node A, store exact support on B_A. At a leaf test its at most four local functionals directly. At a binary node with children C,D, combine two attained functionals on B_C,B_D iff they agree on intersection, i.e. are consistent under Gaussian elimination. U_C intersection U_D is contained in BOTH child boundary spaces. Therefore their actual interior witnesses glue to a functional on U_C+U_D. Moreover B_A is contained in B_C+B_D: if v=u_C+u_D lies in exterior V_A, then u_C=v+u_D lies in V_C, and similarly u_D lies in V_D. Thus the parent boundary evaluation is fully determined by the two child boundary values. Converse by restriction. At root B=0, nonempty iff SAT.
