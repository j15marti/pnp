## New connecting lemma: five-core affine guard cover
Let F be a fixed authenticated source under immutable base guard, let A be an affine carrier containing every possible solution, let C_i be original constraint subsets under a common hard background, and let G_i be affine guards on A. Suppose checked conditional refutations establish background AND C_i AND G_i is UNSAT for i=1..5. If A is covered by union of G_i, background AND union(C_i) AND (the constraints certifying A) is UNSAT. Proof: any hypothetical solution has a point in A; it lies in some G_i, contradicting the corresponding checked core. Coverage is exact without enumerating A: inclusion-exclusion over at most 31 nonempty subsets of five guard indices; each intersection is an affine system, count zero if inconsistent else 2^(d-rank). Charge GE, bit lengths O(d+log 31), source/guard mapping, full conditional refutations and checker costs. This supplies a sound merge rule even when every individual track has only a guarded contradiction and no root contradiction. It does not require guards to be disjoint, but requires common actual coordinates and guards and carrier containment. If guards cover only a relaxation subspace with missing solutions, theorem fails; no merging across distinct instance IDs. This is a fixed-five symbolic result not executed on nontrivial benchmark guard covers here. No guarantee such guards are acquired, nor that the merged core is smaller. If distinct inclusion-minimal unconditional cores, their intersection is satisfiable relative to the hard background: intersection is proper subset of at least one core. Majority is not generally safe; RANDOM witnesses show this.
