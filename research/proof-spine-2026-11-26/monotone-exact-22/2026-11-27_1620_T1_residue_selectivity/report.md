## 3. New sparse-support acquisition lemma

Let a checked signed integer sum of exact-one rows be sum_v a_v x_v = b > 0 with Boolean variables. Let P be the variables with strictly positive coefficients. At least one variable of P is true: otherwise the left side is nonpositive. For each v in P, let N(v) contain w for which an original checked binary clause forbids x_v=x_w=1. Every w in the intersection of the N(v) is forced false. Indeed, under x_w=1 all positive terms vanish, contradicting b>0. Empty P gives an immediate contradiction. This is an exact conditional-to-unconditional deduction in the current certified guard, not a probabilistic prediction. The empty-support case must be handled explicitly.

Recognize exact-one rows by one positive clause and every corresponding negative binary pair. Enumerate singleton rows and distinct three-row sums with signs (+,+,-), deduplicate positive supports, iterate shortest exclusion list first, verify exact sources, and reclose after each forced unit. For fixed row count three, bounded coefficients and explicit chart, this enumeration and checking is polynomial in encoded input. It does not prove every NO instance admits a productive triple or that all charts can be acquired and contracted in polynomial aggregate cost.
