## Scoped structural obstruction and repair
Theorem: In a signed 3-CNF with every clause containing three distinct variables and any two clauses either have underlying triples intersecting in at most one variable or are exact sign complements on the same triple, pure resolution with learned width <=2 cannot initiate a non-tautological inference. Proof: on different triples, resolving on their sole shared pivot leaves four distinct variables; on the same complementary triple, both remaining variable pairs are complementary and yield tautology. Duplicates of a clause do not resolve. Thus no admissible new row exists at intake, and by induction no later one can arise. Fano NAE3 (both all-positive and all-negative clauses per line) is an explicit unsatisfiable control; every pair of lines intersects at one point. Combinatorial proof of non-two-colorability: at least four points have the same color. If a same-color four-set contains a line, done. Otherwise its six pairs have their line's third point in the three-point complement. For a fixed complement point, its lines through that point partition the other six into disjoint pairs, hence at most two pairs from the four-set. Exactly two for each complement point, using all four same-color points; the remaining line through it contains the other two complement points. This complementary triple is a line, also monochromatic if the other points have opposite color; if the original color has at least five points, a same-color fifth point in the complement extends one of its two pairs into a monochromatic line. Therefore no two-coloring exists. Scope: only pure width<=2 learned resolution on the untransformed clauses; not arbitrary preprocessing, affine constraints, SAT deciders, RUP, or unrestricted P!=NP.

On a fixed m-row scoring table strict positive integral make-minus-break flips decrease the count of violated rows by >=1 and therefore terminate in <=m flips. This is not an UNSAT certificate or global polynomial potential across table growth. With fixed width w and r coordinates there are at most sum_{j=0}^w 2^j binom(r,j) distinct non-tautological clauses; append-only unique learned rows give a polynomial *numeric* credit for fixed r,w and bounded scans, but saturation can stall on NO formulas, and coordinate/guard changes must be charged. Flips must actually change proof acquisition or branching to be a hybrid.
