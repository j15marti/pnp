## Theorem A: at most r closure generators
Gaussian-reduce affine rows; inconsistency is immediate NO. If H inconsistent refute by SCC. Otherwise acquire actual model a by 2SAT. Set z=y XOR a, so H'(z) has all-zero model. Every non-tautological clause now is a negative unit, a negative pair (conflict), or implication z_i=>z_j (possibly tautological). Collapse SCCs of the positive implication graph. Each SCC has label equal to XOR of A's columns for its member bits; target t=b XOR Aa. Assignments correspond exactly to forward-closed SCC sets with no violated negative unit/pair. Choose an inclusion-minimal such set S of target syndrome if one exists. Let M be source SCCs of the induced DAG on S. If |M|>r, some nonempty subset J of M has XOR of its SCC labels zero. Removing J leaves a forward-closed set: no retained selected predecessor of a removed source; conflicts/negative units remain satisfied. Syndrome unchanged, contradicting minimality. Thus |M|<=r, and reachability closure of M is exactly S. Enumerate all <=r seeds (one representative per SCC or all bits conservatively), close under implications, reject unit/pair conflicts, test actual syndrome. Sound and complete without supplied minimal model. The count is sum_{j<=r} binom(n,j), not merely 2^r. This independently derives a more specific generator bound within the known GF2^r modular 2SAT n^O(r) regime (Brakensiek-Gopi-Guruswami, arXiv:1902.04740v1 section 5.2, Proposition 5.2 and following paragraph). No novelty claim for the broad complexity class. If r=1, at most n+1 candidates suffice; r=O(log N) is only N^O(log N) in general. The all-zero reorientation is essential; dropping positive-positive constraints without it is unsound. Decision and witness lift, not counting.

## Theorem B: exact SCC/row-space hybrid before generator enumeration
In each iteration test H by 2SAT; acquire actual forced bits via opposite-unit tests and equivalent/opposite literal SCCs. Substitute only certified values and signed identities in H and A; retain reverse maps. Gaussian-reduce A. For each free coordinate i and pair i,j, test whether e_i or e_i+e_j belongs to row space of A. If it does, use the same row combination on b to obtain a certified unary or pair XOR. Import into H as 2CNF, reclose, and substitute. In a consistent affine system the RHS is independent of the chosen combination. Each productive import can be immediately eliminated by pinning or merging at least one free coordinate, hence <=n productive iterations. Reject on inconsistency. All transforms are exact, not affine hull replacement. If no more productive imports and rank<=fixed r0, use A. If larger, UNKNOWN, not NO. Explicit row-space tests O(n^2) per pass, no SAT/equivalence oracle. This extends the earlier <=2 *unfixed* cycle-bit observation: signed SCC substitution can cancel long rows and combinations of long rows may produce binary consequences.
