## New theorem: rank of each necessary guard in a constant-size cover

Let A be a nonempty finite affine space over F2 of dimension d, and let nonempty affine subsets G_1,...,G_k cover A irredundantly (no proper subcollection covers A). Then every relative codimension codim_A(G_i) <= k-1. Proof: irredundance gives a private point x_i in G_i outside every other G_j. For each j != i, choose one affine equation defining G_j relative to A which is violated by x_i (such an equation exists). The intersection H_i of the opposite equations contains x_i, is disjoint from each other G_j, and therefore is contained in G_i by coverage. H_i has relative codimension at most k-1. Hence codim_A(G_i) <= codim_A(H_i) <= k-1. For k=1 the result is immediate. Empty guards can be discarded; an empty carrier must be handled separately. Tight for d>=k-1: prefix partition x1=0, x1=1 & x2=0, ..., x1=...=x_(k-2)=1 & x_(k-1)=0, x1=...=x_(k-1)=1. All private regions are nonempty and final two codimensions are k-1.

Corollary: any cover by at most five affine guards has a covering subfamily whose individual ranks are at most four, and whose combined normal span has rank at most 20. No need to retain a high-rank guard in a minimal five-cover. For coordinate pins on a common free-coordinate chart with r polynomially bounded coordinates, the entire noncontradictory rank<=4 guard vocabulary is sum_{j=0}^4 2^j binom(r,j)=O((r+1)^4). More generally if all candidate equations come from an explicitly acquired dictionary of M affine directions (both right-hand sides), enumeration of <=4 independent equations costs O((M+1)^4) candidate descriptions, with Gaussian normalization charged; rank is relative to A. If candidate guards have coordinate descriptions in dependent original coordinates, reducing relative rank gives a subset of their equations with the same guard. For a dictionary or coordinate vocabulary, exhaustively try every candidate in each fixed method under one fixed original-N polynomial cap; collect checked conditional contradictions; test every <=5 candidate subset by at most 31 affine counts. Enumeration costs O((M+1)^20) subset trials, not a semantic SAT oracle. All guards and rank reduction must be source-checkable. If the fixed-budget acquisition procedure always finds such a cover or another globally charged contraction on every reachable NO state, this closes the guard-selection combinatorics, but that antecedent remains OPEN. The theorem does NOT guarantee any cover by guards that have efficiently obtainable conditional proofs, nor an efficient direction dictionary for arbitrary affine forms. There are exponentially many possible directions. No extension to arbitrary nonaffine guards. No universal result.

The proof is an elementary private-point argument, related to irredundant affine-subspace covers, not a claim of priority. Literature: Pete L.
