## L1. Exact rank-two forbidden affine fibers
Each native row a+b+c+d=2 is equivalent to even parity a+b+c+d=0 over F2 AND not all four equal. Under even parity the latter is exactly (a+b=1 OR a+c=1) over F2. Add all native even-parity rows and certified affine guards to affine system L. Gaussian elimination obtains consistency and the dual quotient V=(F2)^n/rowspan(L), with affine offsets retained by reducing augmented equations. Each row's forbidden fiber is the simultaneous vanishing of its two affine differences. If their linear parts have rank zero or one, explicitly examine the at most two possible values of the remaining form: reject empty, add a forced affine equation, or remove tautology. Iterate to fixed point; every added independent equation reduces primal dimension by one, so <=n productive sweeps. Remaining rows have two independent directions u,v; record plane P=span(u,v) with three nonzero elements, actual offsets and original row IDs. This is EXACT, not an affine-hull relaxation; all nonlinear forbidden fibers retained. Witness solve Gaussian leaf and verify source rows. This is the mobile-neuron representation modulo all acquired parity, not just signed DSU pair identities.

## L2. PROVED: rank-hitting backdoor acquisition and exact polynomial subclass
For arbitrary such closed affine chart let k* be minimum dimension of a subspace W of V with W intersect P nontrivially for EVERY remaining plane. Construct greedily W=0, scan planes in fixed source order, and whenever P intersect W=0 add the whole P to W. Membership test uses all three nonzero directions; 2 independent directions added. If t planes selected, they form a direct sum of dimension 2t. Every possible hitting subspace W* contains a nonzero vector from each selected plane; these t vectors are independent. Therefore t<=k*, acquired rank r=2t<=2k*. The completed W hits every plane. No optimization oracle. Alternatively, when P is missed, branch on adjoining ONE of its three nonzero directions, decreasing remaining rank budget by one. If a rank-k solution extending current W exists, at least one branch stays inside it. Conversely a successful branch explicitly hits all planes. At most sum_{j=0}^k 3^j search nodes; exact optimal acquisition by iterative budgets costs O(3^k* poly(N)). Each candidate W is represented by an actual basis, not 2^k entries.

Enumerate all 2^r assignments to a basis of acquired W, with actual original affine offsets accounted for. In each child affine quotient every old plane has rank <=1: some nonzero direction has been fixed, and remaining constraints become exact affine conditions or contradictions. Iterated rank-zero/one closure plus Gaussian decision and lift suffices. Every original solution appears in exactly one basis assignment, and every accepted leaf lifts to an actual original solution. Total deterministic bit time O(2^(2k*) poly(N)) greedy, or O(3^k* poly(N)) optimal acquisition followed by 2^k* leaves. The implemented greedy lane returns UNKNOWN above a fixed cap (20 for finite tests); theoretical polynomial lane uses fixed C log2(original N) cap and fixed exponent. Additional unary/affine guards project the old W to a space of no greater rank, hitting every surviving rank-two image (a hit killed by projection would cause that plane to lose rank). Thus hereditary under guards when original chart and basis are retained, including rank-decreasing closure. No claim that fresh greedy ranks monotonically decrease.

Exact additive component tariff (symbolic, not implemented): choose a basis of span of all remaining plane directions in V. Express the two generators of each plane in these basis coordinates; connect all coordinates occurring in their combined support. Partition by connected components of this acquired coordinate graph. Each forbidden fiber depends on exactly one component. Every assignment to these independent basis coordinates extends to an original affine-system solution, by Gaussian elimination, so solve components independently and glue by one affine lift. Cost SUM of component search costs, not product. If all component optimal ranks are <= fixed C log N, sum is polynomial in original N (at most O(N) components). This may be a coarse partition, not a universal small-rank assertion. Inspired by C02-02 exact projection-before-splitting; no direct P3DM result imported.

## L3. PROVED: exact linear native virtual hub construction and bypass
For four boundary variables p0,p1,p2,p3, introduce six fresh edge bits e_ij on K4, and for each vertex i a native row p_i+sum_{j!=i}e_ij=2. Summing gives even boundary parity. Conversely selected edges must have degree 2-p_i: if all p zero choose a Hamiltonian cycle of K4; if all ones choose a perfect matching; if exactly two ones choose a Hamiltonian path whose endpoints are those two. All even words extend, all odd words excluded. This is exact existential projection, with a constant-size explicit witness lift; not just the necessary parity hull. Source recognition: four rows each with three pairwise distinct internal globally degree-two variable IDs linking to the other three rows; four distinct external IDs; no overlaps of removed internal IDs/rows with other gadgets; no external guard on eliminated internal IDs. Can recognize by incidence scan, verify all conditions, and replace by parity equation, store source-to-row lift. Tests use empty guards for projection; arbitrary guards touching interiors require new analysis.

## L4. PROVED obstruction: minimum linear backdoor rank equals vertex cover on an easy EX22 family
For any finite simple graph G, discard isolated vertices or keep them free. Introduce anchor a, vertex bits x_i, private w_ij per edge, native row (a,x_i,x_j,w_ij). Even parity uniquely expresses each private bit over F2; all a,x_i free in parity kernel. Independent dual forms e_i=a+x_i span a coordinate subspace, and edge plane span(e_i,e_j). All rows rank2, so initial affine closure makes no nonlinear deduction. For any vertex cover S, span{e_i:i in S} hits each plane. Conversely intersect any hitting W with span of e_i (cannot increase rank, preserves all hits). Row-echelon basis using the e_i coordinates: every nonzero vector of W has its leading coordinate among pivot coordinates. For every edge, some nonzero vector supported on {i,j} lies in W, so a pivot lies at an endpoint. Thus pivots form vertex cover of size dim W. Consequently k*=tau(G) exactly. Minimum rank acquisition decision is NP-complete for this subclass (vertex cover Karp 1972, certificate basis rank and all plane hits polynomial). These instances are ALL satisfiable by a=0, x_i=1, w_ij=0. More generally pinning anchor value converts each projected NAE triple to a binary OR or NAND, with uniquely reconstructed private bit, so this family has a trivial two-case 2-SAT decision algorithm under arbitrary original unary pins on a and vertex IDs (private pins require retaining exact restrictions, not ignored). On k-by-k grid, even k, tau=k^2/2 by disjoint matching lower bound and checkerboard cover upper bound. Plane-support interaction is connected in the natural e_i basis. Raw greedy rank k^2 in tested grids. The algebraic backdoor tariff can be exponential even on easily solved connected instances; exact small-rank acquisition is not universally required for SAT. This is an obstruction to the chosen operation, not unrestricted runtime. An alternate 2-SAT track solves this same source. No claim all possible decomposition bases force high rank in each independent block; global k* is exact.
