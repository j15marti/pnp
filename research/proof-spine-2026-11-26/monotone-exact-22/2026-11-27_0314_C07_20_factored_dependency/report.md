## L1 PROVED: fixed-literal all-context circuit for a moving binary core
On a verified graph disequality chart (or explicit binary forbidden-pair CSP, owners of at most three colors), use a fixed Boolean proposition p_(v,c) for each original owner/value. The opposite literal is always logical NOT p_(v,c), NEVER another color. The ambient context G includes immutable actual pins. Independent input flags g_(v,c) indicate that a temporary pin v=c has been imposed. They are not color assignments themselves: 0 means not imposed, not v!=c. Inconsistent guard sets are refutable. For each owner/value define deletion bit D(v,c) initially OR of flags pinning v to another color, including ambient constant pins. For every round define forced bit J(v,c)=AND_{a!=c} D(v,a). A neighbor forced to c deletes c by disequality. For general binary table, remove a value if every support in its neighbor has been deleted; equivalently conjunction over the support list. For a certified earlier nogood h, if all pins other than (v,c) are forced, delete c; if all pins forced, set failure. Domain empty sets failure. OR each newly derived deletion into previous deletion. At most 3n strict pointwise deletion rounds (n owners); unroll 3n synchronous rounds, which is correct simultaneously for all flags. This is a monotone circuit for domain closure. Contradictory guard inputs are accepted as failures; no unsupported nonrefutation means YES.

Construct fixed implication graph (6n literal IDs) by including:

- actual static pair incompatibilities (for graph, NOT p_(u,c) OR NOT p_(v,c));
- unit NOT p_(v,c) activated by D(v,c);
- positive pair p_(v,a) OR p_(v,b) activated by deletion of the third c.
For a previously certified nogood and chosen two distinct remaining pins, include its negative pair clause activated by AND of the forced bits of every other pin; analogous unit/full conflict rules. Library chronological certification and same original IDs mandatory. All active clauses are sound consequences under the actual flag set; no complement depends on a chart that may disappear. Graph edge activation is OR of generating clauses' activation. For any given flags, form reachability by Floyd-Warshall over monotone Boolean gates: R^0_ij = edge activation OR identity; R^k_ij = R^(k-1)_ij OR (R^(k-1)_ik AND R^(k-1)_kj). Its invariant is reachability using internal vertices among first k. Terminal fails iff domain/active nogood failure OR any R_(p,NOT p) AND R_(NOT p,p).
