For any simple 4-cycle Q, telescoping color differences give sum_Q epsilon_e d_e=0 mod3. The integer sum is even and lies in [-4,4]; the only such multiple of 3 is 0. Therefore every real coloring satisfies the integer square relation and its mod4 consequence

For triangles, the corresponding sum of three signed differences is odd and divisible by 3, hence +/-3. All three traversal differences equal. For e,f on this triangle, z_e+z_f mod2 = 0 if traversal orientations agree, else 1. Collect actual source-derived square rows and triangle pairs. ## L3: winding conservation across arbitrarily many layers (known family, reconstructed proof)
Literature: generalized Mycielski graphs of odd cycles are 4-chromatic (indeed circular chromatic number 4), not new. Our concrete algebraic acquisition and source adapter use the following direct identity rather than a preloaded graph embedding. Let k>=5 odd, L>=2. Vertices (i,h), i mod k, 0<=h<L, apex a. Edges: base cycle (i,0)-(i+1,0); between each successive layer both diagonals (i,h)-(i+1,h+1) and (i+1,h)-(i,h+1); apex adjacent to all top-layer vertices. Define oriented integer chains B=base forward cycle; S_h=sum of forward i-increment diagonals across layers h,h+1 in BOTH directions of layer movement. The following actual oriented four-cycles give identities in free edge-chain group:
  base: [(i,0),(i+1,0),(i+2,0),(i+1,1)] summed over i equals 2B-S_0;
  interior: [(i,h),(i+1,h-1),(i+2,h),(i+1,h+1)] summed over i equals S_(h-1)-S_h for 1<=h<=L-2;
  top: [(i,L-2),(i+1,L-1),a,(i-1,L-1)] summed over i equals S_(L-2).
Adding yields exactly 2B, with all diagonal and apex edges cancelling. If a proper coloring existed, every actual square would have integer winding sum zero, so 2 sum_B d=0. Yet the sum over the k odd base edges is odd; contradiction. Even just mod4 gives 0=2 mod4. The square-only F2 algorithm therefore refutes the family without the layer map. Verification of coefficient cancellation performed independently on exact same graph IDs; rank-combination certificate found by Gaussian and checked. Odd girth is min(k,2L+1) by cited Proposition 1, distinguish from ordinary girth (4). This gives scalable high-odd-girth native NO controls but not universal coverage.
