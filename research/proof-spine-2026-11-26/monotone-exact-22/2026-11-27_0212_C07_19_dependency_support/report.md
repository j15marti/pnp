## L1 PROVED: reason transport and two-path conflict extraction
Fix original formula F, immutable root context G and previously certified negative library L. Each record in L means F AND G AND h is inconsistent, with chronological proof. Work on verified explicit graph disequality chart with constant domain q=3 (extension to explicit binary tables via support explanations straightforward but not implemented). Input P consists of owner=value pins. Store a sufficient subset W(v,c) of P for every deleted value c of owner v: under F,G,W(v,c), v cannot equal c. Reasons may refer to earlier certified L; these are global conditional nogoods under G, not additional assumptions. Root deletions have empty support relative to G. Imposing a pin gives its singleton support for all other colors. If a variable becomes singleton a, the union of reasons for its other colors proves v=a. Disequality to a neighbor deletes a with that support. A nogood becomes unit after every other pin is forced true; union the reasons of those forced pins and use the earlier certified nogood. An inactive nogood cannot reactivate as domains shrink. Every reason references only previously acquired facts. If domain empty, union all its deletion reasons; if nogood fully active union the forced-pin reasons. By induction every union is sufficient, including empty guard if derivation is unconditional.

If domain closure does not refute, form the binary core of exactly two-valued owners. Represent each pair of surviving values as opposite literals. A forbidden pair yields two implications. Their support includes all reasons excluding the third color at BOTH endpoints; for learned nogoods also union the reasons fixing all other pins true. This step is essential: complement of an owner=value proposition is only its other remaining value after the excluded third value is certified. Under the support of an arc, any actual solution has at most the chosen two options at each endpoint and obeys the implication. It need not attain both options. A path is valid under union of its arc supports. SCC conflict yields actual directed paths l to not-l and not-l to l. Under union S of the two path supports, at least one of l and not-l must hold (both endpoint restrictions are certified), and whichever holds implies its opposite, impossible. Thus F AND G AND S inconsistent, S subset P. No SAT claim from absence of conflict; omitted nonbinary conditions are relaxed ONLY for negative soundness. This is a connecting lemma: domain explanations change the labels on the implication graph, whose negative certificate then changes the learned library and future binary cores. It is an adaptation of classical conflict analysis, not a novelty claim for the general principle.
