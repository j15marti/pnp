## L1 PROVED: dual-corridor series terminal under correlated 2CNF context
Admit a verified native-to-phase chart under actual retained guard. Shared boundary variables x use one fixed independent Boolean coordinate dictionary. All private constraints, once x is fixed, are directional implications between Boolean phase variables, with source p0 pinned1 and sink pL pinned0. Other constraints consist of exact exterior 2CNF G(x). Private implication graph is a series of blocks from p_(i-1) to p_i, each a union of two internally vertex-disjoint simple corridors sharing only their endpoints; different blocks share only the serial endpoints. Edges may be active in either or both directions, with explicit conjunctive literal guards for each forward arc; each corridor is traversable forward exactly when the conjunction g_i or h_i of its actual forward edge guards holds. Reverse guards may be arbitrary as long as exactly evaluable under the fixed boundary and no extra arcs/obligations; they cannot create an alternate simple source-to-sink route. The assertion extends to one corridor by treating the missing corridor as false. Empty conjunction means true. Contradictory conjunction means false. Native adapter must verify all these hypotheses; arbitrary native residual need not admit the chart.

For fixed x, extension iff no directed path from source to sink: necessity by implications, sufficiency by setting exactly the reachable private vertices to one. Because the serial cuts are articulation interfaces and each block has only its two corridors, directed source-sink reachability is exactly P(x)=AND_i(g_i OR h_i). Repeated boundary IDs, repeated literals and correlated G are retained; no independence hypothesis. Write g_i=AND A_i, h_i=AND B_i for lists of literals. By distributivity g_i OR h_i = AND_{a in A_i,b in B_i}(a OR b). Hence P is 2CNF of size at most sum_i |A_i||B_i|, with usual constant handling.

Universal extension within the actual G-context holds iff G AND P is unsatisfiable, one 2SAT call (including vacuous G=empty support). Ordinary existence of an extension is G AND NOT P satisfiable, NOT the same test. For every stage and every a in A_i,b in B_i, test G AND NOT a AND NOT b by 2SAT. A successful call gives a compatible boundary assignment with both corridors inactive at that stage; BFS gives a full private witness, then lift through verified native adapter. Conversely any accepted boundary prevents a stage from transmitting and falsifies at least one literal from each corridor, so one of these calls succeeds. Empty true guards admit no failing pair; single corridor handled by enumerating a falsified literal. Explicit boundary contradictions detected before the lane. All negative decisions are exact for admitted charts. No free SAT oracle, no sampling in production terminal. A polynomial conditional response (circuit P) alone is not enough for arbitrary global conjunction; here the actual contextual decisions are proved.

Given a candidate chart with original row maps, check disjoint interiors, serial overlaps and row adapter in polynomial time; the underlying simple phase graph recognition can use articulation blocks, checking each block is a two-route cycle with the specified terminal pair and no extra edges. Polynomial chart verification is not universal native-to-chart discovery. ## L3 FALSIFIED: polynomial exact flat path-guard cover from bounded geometric width
For k serial diamond blocks, let independent actual forward labels x_i,y_i each enable one corridor. Under empty exterior context, P_k=AND_i(x_i OR y_i). Phase graph has a path decomposition of width two (bags containing two serial endpoints and one interior corridor vertex); this is a statement about phase graph, not a claim of width two for complete native incidence graph. P_k has 3^k accepted blocking assignments. A conjunctive literal guard implying P_k must force x_i=1 or y_i=1 for each i. Hence it covers at most one of the 2^k one-hot-per-stage blocking words; at least 2^k such guards are necessary, attained by enumerating route choices. For arbitrary affine guard, its satisfying set S is an affine subspace contained in P_k. Each coordinate-pair projection is an affine subset of a three-point set, of dimension <=1. S injects into the product of its projections; |S|<=2^k. Any exact union cover needs >=ceil((3/2)^k) affine guards, even after arbitrary invertible coordinate change. Same elementary product bound as C02-09, applied to actual reachability via explicit native adapter. This is a bound for flat union representations of the BLOCKING predicate, not for the extension response NOT P_k or for arbitrary decision algorithms. P_k has k binary clauses, and NOT P_k has k two-literal conjunctive disjuncts. L1 is an inexpensive bypass.
