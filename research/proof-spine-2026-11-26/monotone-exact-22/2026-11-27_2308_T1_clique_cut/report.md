## Weighted regional cut lemma
Let T be a selected family of signed NAE triples with distinct variables in each triple, and with no unordered variable pair repeated across selected triples. Let E contain the three pairs from each triple. For a pair e=(u,v) from a triple of polarity mask p, define y_e = x_u XOR x_v XOR p_u XOR p_v, as an integer 0 or 1. Every feasible word has sum_E y_e=2|T|. Source tables must verify NAE exactly; extra constraints may be ignored for this necessary condition.

Let R_j be a family of clique regions in E (each edge of a region must be owned by E; regions may overlap). If phases within R_j are q, and b_j is the number of edges whose original polarity difference disagrees with q_u XOR q_v, then U_j=floor(|R_j|^2/4)+b_j bounds sum of y_e on R_j for every word. Under a guard with compatible fixed phases one may sharpen to the maximum of k(|R_j|-k) over the permitted count interval plus b_j, provided the guard is verified. Different regions may have different gauges; do not assume global phase consistency.

For any nonnegative integer weights a_j and positive integer M with c_e=sum_{j:e in R_j} a_j <= M, multiply the individual inequalities by a_j and add (M-c_e)y_e <= M-c_e. The exact necessary condition is
  2M|T| <= sum_j a_j U_j + sum_e (M-c_e).
Strict reverse inequality is a sound negative certificate. No multiplication of unknowns beyond their local Boolean meaning; no optimizer or global assignment search needed for replay. Rational weights can be cleared to integers and their bit lengths charged. The ordinary edge-disjoint regional bound is M=1, all a_j=1. This proof is elementary and is not a novelty claim against Max-Cut clique inequalities or Goodman/Ramsey double counting.

Fixed region width k and polynomially many original chart variables give a polynomial-size candidate family: at most sum_{i<=k} binom(n,i) scopes. Test exact pair ownership and local gauge, charge all enumeration and arithmetic. For coherent K5s (U=6, ten pairs), use all recognized K5s with unit weights and M=max(1,max_e c_e). This has integer certificate length polynomial for fixed k=5. If uniform positive load M covers every edge, RHS/M=0.6|E|=1.8|T|<2|T|. Thus a regular overlapping K5 cover suffices even if disjoint greedy packing fails. Do not assume arbitrary NO has such cover. Weighted allocation can be optimized as a separate rational LP with explicitly replayed capacities; not executed here.
