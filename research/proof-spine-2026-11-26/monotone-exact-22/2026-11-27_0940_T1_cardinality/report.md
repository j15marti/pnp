## Theorem: weighted affine fiber hole absorption (symbolic)
Let independent, disjoint-coordinate factors have exact attainable syndrome supports A=(a+P) minus optional hole u, B=(b+Q) minus optional hole v in F2^r, with verified actual support, and P,Q explicit subspaces. Arbitrary binary linear coefficient maps from local coordinates allowed (not arbitrary integer-weight subset sum). Set d=dim(P intersection Q), t in a+b+P+Q. Exactly 2^d pairs (x,y) in full cosets satisfy x+y=t, by affine fiber translation. Define alpha=1 iff u exists and t+u in b+Q, beta similarly for v, gamma=1 iff both holes exist and t=u+v. Inclusion-exclusion gives number of surviving distinct syndrome pairs M(t)=2^d-alpha-beta+gamma. These are support pairs, NOT number of native assignments. Outside sum coset M=0. If d>=2, every target survives. If d=1, at most one target fails: when both holes exist, missing target is u+v+h for unique nonzero h in intersection. If either absent, full. If d=0, unique full pair; removal of either hole deletes an entire translated coset, not just one word. Hence exact hereditary full-or-one-hole closure under positive-dimensional overlap, with polynomial GE acquisition and reverse extension. For d>=2 find at most three candidate fiber pairs by taking a base solution and distinct nullspace translations, since at most two pairs forbidden; d<=1 test all 1 or 2. For a general linear image of full-or-one-hole local coset, nontrivial kernel makes image full (at least 2 preimages); injective map preserves optional hole. Cost polynomial in input+verified support descriptor size. For a weighted generalization with explicit hole lists of sizes h_A,h_B, M(t)=2^d minus number of actual forbidden first/second options plus intersection count; if 2^d>h_A+h_B, full, construct at most h_A+h_B+1 pairs. Do not assume a huge implicit hole list cheap; weights here mean explicitly mapped binary syndrome coefficients and hole counts. Arbitrary binary-encoded integer weights not covered.

Independent Python test: all ordered subspace pairs in ambient dimensions 1..4; optional hole normalized by translation, all target pair counts exact: 28,316,5980,197596 checks (203920 total). Positive intersection closes, zero intersection counterexample P=span(e1), Q=span(e2), remove zero in each: only e1+e2 survives from four, not full-or-one-hole.
