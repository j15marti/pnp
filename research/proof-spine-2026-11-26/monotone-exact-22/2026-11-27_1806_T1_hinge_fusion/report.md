# T1 Monotone Exact-22: cross-scope activation and a width-two obstruction theorem

2026-11-27 Pacific. P versus NP remains OPEN. Scope: fixed-width RUP acquisition, exact native projection and finite benchmark controls. No public release.

## Main new result: activation, not merely accumulation

For a CNF with no unit or binary clauses and every clause of width at least three (distinct variables), a two-variable guard on variables that never co-occur in a clause cannot start UP: no clause has two falsified literals, hence no clause can become empty or unit. This holds for every choice of signs. More generally if every clause has fewer than |C|-1 falsified literals and is not already satisfied, UP has no initial move. A cross-scope-only width-two probe rule is therefore blocked at intake on such sources. A learned unit or binary can change this condition. The useful scheduling rule is to acquire a small local certified seed, then rescan nonlocal hinge guards rather than persist with a stalled cross-scope method.

**Counterexample to universal width-two RUP completeness, even with same-scope pairs.** Fano-plane NAE: seven points, seven triples, each point on three triples, every pair on a unique triple and any two distinct triples meet at one point. Encode every triple by its positive and all-negative three-literal clauses. Under a zero- or one-bit guard no unit arises. Under a two-bit guard on distinct points, opposite colors force nothing; equal colors force the third point of the unique line to the opposite color. The three assigned points lie on one line; all other lines contain at most one of them. UP then stops without contradiction. Every non-tautological width <=2 RUP candidate has one of those guards. Thus no first new such clause exists, even if one allows all pairs, repeated scanning and unlimited selection time. UNSAT: a color class of five or more has at least ten pairs on seven lines, so some line is monochromatic. A class of size exactly four with no monochromatic line occupies six different lines twice (six pairs), exhausting its twelve incidences; the seventh line is entirely the other color. All colorings fail. For a linear 3-uniform NAE hypergraph, the same local no-start argument applies to two-pin guards (pairs not sharing a line have no propagation). Finite independent audit: 99 guards (empty, 14 singles, 84 pairs), 4,978 literal visits, zero UP conflicts, maximum one forced bit; exhaustive 128 complete assignments, zero solutions. Fixed-width learned clauses admit a polynomial numerical credit D_w(r)-rank/count on a fixed chart, but finite credit does not imply any productive admission. Fano proves a fixed-point obstruction to width-two RUP, including arbitrary guard-selection policy, not to algebra, joins, extensions or general decision. The next target: prove a polynomial acquisition theorem for a complementary seed from another mathematical method when all narrow guards stall, using cross-scope affine/cardinality identities or exact small interface joins, or exhibit an infinite simultaneous-stall family for precisely specified bounded rules. Universal seed acquisition, universal global aggregate progress, uniform worst-case bit bound and independent full-proof audit remain OPEN. No public pvsnp write.
