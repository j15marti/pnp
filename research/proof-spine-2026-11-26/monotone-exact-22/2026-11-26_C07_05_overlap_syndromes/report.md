## L2 PROVED: maximum-overlap forest and residual deficit
For arbitrary finite family of rank-two planes P_i in the dual free coordinate vector space V over F2, let W=sum_i P_i, r=dim W, and C=direct sum of copies P_i. The sum map phi:C->W is surjective, dim ker phi=2m-r=D. A vector of local functionals is an element of C*. It is the restriction of a common functional on V iff it annihilates ker phi: sufficiency by well-defined factoring through W and linear extension to V, necessity by summing evaluations of each relation.
Give edge ij weight dim(P_i intersection P_j); select maximum-weight forest of positive edges, via pairwise intersections and Kruskal. For each chosen edge embed a basis of the intersection diagonally into its two summands of C (sign irrelevant over F2). These are in ker phi. They are independent by leaf induction: only the leaf's incident edge contributes to its own direct summand, so all coefficients of that edge vanish. Remove leaf and repeat. Thus the tree span K has dimension w=sum edge weights. Extend its explicitly acquired basis to ker phi by Gaussian elimination on the source direction matrix. Exactly delta=D-w extra basis relations E are needed, delta>=0. Among such forests maximum weight minimizes delta, NOT necessarily across arbitrary regional grouping or nonlinear transformations. No hidden dependence omitted. Every edge has at most 2 equality tests on local states. Original coordinates and affine offsets are preserved. This is a scoped forest gluing theorem, not a lower bound on other charts.

## L3 PROVED: exact syndrome forest DP, with faster optional convolution
For each allowed local state s of P_i, define its delta-bit contribution sigma_i(s) by evaluations of the E basis relations on that summand. A choice of states passes all original compatibility equations iff it passes each tree edge equality and XOR_i sigma_i(s_i)=0. Root each tree. At each vertex and each of its <=3 states store exact attainable XOR support of all subtree contributions. For every child, take union of child support sets whose child state agrees on the edge, then XOR-sumset with accumulated parent support. Do same across forest components. Root union contains 0 iff original source SAT. Proof inductively constructs compatible local witnesses; no lost correlations since each tree subtree meets its exterior only through its parent edge plus the exact full syndrome. Backtrack predecessor states and syndromes, solve original affine system with acquired evaluations, verify all original EX22 rows and guards. If 0 absent after full exact DP, NO. If cap exceeded, UNKNOWN, not NO.
At most 3 states per node and O(m) merges; naive pairs cost O(4^delta poly(N)), including repeated failed state attempts. Faster symbolic variant (NOT executed): boolean XOR-sumset of supports f,g via exact integer Walsh-Hadamard transform, pointwise product, inverse and threshold >0. Each input indicator is 0/1, transform abs<=2^delta, products <=2^(2delta), inverse numerator <=2^(3delta), bits O(delta+1); reset to indicators after each merge. O(delta*2^delta) integer operations per merge; storing intermediate tables lets one reconstruct by scanning at most 2^delta candidates for one desired XOR per merge. Counts here are pairs of syndrome values, not counts of satisfying assignments. Uniform bit bound O(2^delta poly(N)) and peak O(2^delta poly(N)); naive implementation stores full partial witness arrays and pays their polynomial size, so same bounds with 4^delta time. Cumulative memory <=(T+1)S on the same sequential execution. Parsing/ID normalization, affine reductions, O(m^2) pair tests, edge sorting, tagged Gaussian elimination, failed queries, storage, reconstruction and verification all paid; all coefficients are bits except exact small integers in optional transform. No uncharged sharing, canonicalization or recomputation. For any fixed C, admit delta<=C log2(max(2,N)) before allocating table, uniform polynomial across original bit lengths. No unrestricted coverage claim.
Hereditary scoped extension: keep the old chart and forest when adding affine guards. First test consistency of additional guard linear forms G with right-hand sides in old affine coordinate space. A functional on W extends to satisfy these guards iff it agrees on W intersection G with prescribed values (adjust origin offsets); proof by well-defined sum and extension. ## L4 PROVED: source-acquired direct-sum factorization before forest tariff
Select any basis of the 2m copied directions' images. For every nonbasis coordinate, tagged Gaussian elimination gives its fundamental dependence on basis coordinates (one relation including that coordinate). These D relations are independent (each has unique nonbasis pivot) and span ker phi. Connect all copy IDs within each relation support and connect each neuron's two coordinates; take connected components. Every full dependency is a sum of relations internal to these components; hence ker phi is direct sum of component kernels. Equivalently the spans of the component image forms form a direct sum, since a relation between distinct components would decompose into within-component relations, making each component sum zero. Thus locally attainable functionals can be chosen independently and glued globally; all nonlinear rows wholly contained in one component. No original variable IDs need be split or identified incorrectly: actual affine lift on original input restores joint witness. This proof is an independent Gaussian/direct-sum track on the same native charts. Compute each component maximum forest deficit delta_j and solve separately. Total O(poly(N) * sum_j 2^delta_j) with optional fast variant, or O(poly(N)*sum_j 4^delta_j) direct. Peak polynomial times largest table, cumulative <=time*peak. Global delta can be large; max_j delta_j<=C log N suffices. Factor acquisition O(N^2) explicit union work plus Gaussian elimination and original chart preprocessing. For independent factors no product of exponential state counts; actual aggregate work is sum. Additional nonlocal guards require recomputing factors or accounting for cross-component syndrome; do not assume independent thereafter.

## L5 falsified universal admission on raw authenticated hard family
For private completion of linear positive NAE3-E4 on n original IDs, m=4n/3, each plane consists of three pair differences in original variables; no pairwise overlap since triples share <=1 ID. r=n-c where c is number of primal connected components (spanning-tree differences give the even subspace in each). Thus w=0 and delta=2m-r=5n/3+c. Earlier algebraic leaf peeling cannot make global delta logarithmic: m-r starts n/3+c, never decreases on permitted peel; remaining delta=m'+(m'-r')>=n/3+c. This is only a raw-chart obstruction, not a theorem that direct-sum components all have high deficit, or that semantic contraction or alternate chart fails. Additional example anchored k by k grid of private-completed edge triples (a,u,v,w_uv): common basis of all differences a+u solves by C07-04 2-SAT, but all planes form one direct-sum component, m=2k(k-1), r=k^2, forest w=m-1, delta=m-r+1=(k-1)^2. Thus no general lower bound from large delta. For overlap forest with large absorbed dependency D: star of rows (a,b,u_i,v_i) distinct private rim endpoints gives r=m+1, w=m-1, delta=0. Cycle of linear triples on rim vertices plus one private original leaf per triple: r=2m-1, w=0, delta=1. These controls have arbitrary rank. Forest topology alone is insufficient; complete additional syndrome is required. No arbitrary-decider exclusion.
