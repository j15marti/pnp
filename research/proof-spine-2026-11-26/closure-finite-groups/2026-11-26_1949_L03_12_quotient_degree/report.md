## A/B: exact filtered quotient theorem
Let B_n=F2[x_1,...,x_n]/(x_i^2-x_i), V_D the subspace of squarefree polynomials of degree <=D. Take consistent affine equations L and arbitrary generators q_i of degree e_i<=D. Gaussian elimination selects free original coordinate set J of size d and expresses each pivot x_j as an affine form a_j in the free variables. The substitution pi is a degree-nonincreasing ring homomorphism because every Boolean-ring element is idempotent. Its restriction V_D(n)->V_D(d) is surjective (free variables are actual original coordinates). Its kernel is exactly K_D=span{m*l: l in L, m squarefree monomial, deg(m)<=D-1}. For the nontrivial containment, replace pivot variables one at a time in each monomial of any f. Each difference is (x_j+a_j) times a product of at most D-1 variables/affine forms. Expand and Boolean-reduce; the row x_j+a_j is a linear combination of original L. Thus f-pi(f) lies in K_D. Conversely each product vanishes on the affine chart. No exponential truth table computation.

Define W_D=K_D+span{m*q_i : deg(m)<=D-e_i}. Define U_D=span{u*pi(q_i) : u free monomial, deg(u)<=D-e_i}, charging ORIGINAL degree e_i, even when pi(q_i) drops degree. Since pi(m) has degree <=deg(m), and every u is already an original monomial, pi(W_D)=U_D. K_D subset W_D, hence W_D=pi^{-1}(U_D) within V_D. Exact rank and refutation identities:
dim W_D = sum_{j<=D} binom(n,j) - sum_{j<=D} binom(d,j) + dim U_D;
1 in W_D iff 1 in U_D.
The second follows either by applying pi or by lifting the free-coordinate multiplier sum and subtracting 1, which belongs to K_D by the kernel theorem. All coefficients and pivots have original provenance. This is a filtered static Macaulay/Nullstellensatz span result modulo Boolean identities, NOT a proof about all polynomial calculus derivations or all deciders. If chart degree drops, allowing larger multipliers based on new degree gives a different stronger chart span; no equality asserted for that modified tariff. In this particular residual every nonzero projected generator has degree 2, so the implemented actual-degree chart agrees with original-degree tariff. Soundness even for the stronger chart variant follows by evaluating every derived equation on any putative original witness, which must map to the exact chart. No witness counting assumption.
