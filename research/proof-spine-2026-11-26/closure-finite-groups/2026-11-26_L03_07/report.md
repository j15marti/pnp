## Track A: collapse a query complete for all positives
Lemma: if a coordinatewise monotone map R has A(R(0))=1, then A always holds. If B(R(0))=1, F(R(x))=1. Otherwise R(0) is exact-four. If G(R(0))=1, monotonicity gives F=1 for every x. If G(R(0))=0, F(R(x))=1 exactly when at least one output coordinate originally zero at 0 changes to one. Reason: with no such change the entire image equals R(0); with such change monotonicity creates overflow. Hence F(R(x)) is a constant or OR of the monotone coordinates zero at 0. For positive literal maps this is a single positive clause (possibly empty). In a nonuniform circuit lower-bound argument the one fixed baseline answer is just a fixed bit, not an assumed free uniform algorithm. In the actual audit baseline G=0 by explicit row contradiction and executed arc closure. Any uniform use of the general lemma must pay for acquiring the baseline answer or have a certified baseline label.

If a positive literal query is complete on all matching YES inputs, it cannot have A(R(0))=0 for n>=14. Choose 13 zero membership coordinates of one deficient cell at 0. Set the distinct source variables among these coordinates to zero, at most 13. The n cyclic permutations are edge-disjoint; fewer than n removed edges destroy at most that many permutations. Choose remaining permutation and its graph, hence actual source YES with same deficient cell and F=0, contradiction. This is a direct construction, not an oracle. General version: zero certificates of size <=d_0 at 0 suffice if 13d_0<n. Initial certificate fact concerns original source IDs, not joint marginal support.

## Track C: independent maximal-NO classification and adversarial reconstruction
For every maximal negative graph M of Match_n, Hall supplies S of rows, with <=|S|-1 neighbors. Choose T of columns outside the neighbors with |T|=n+1-|S|. All of S x T are missing. The graph K_(n,n) minus S x T is still negative, so maximality gives equality. Conversely this graph is negative, and adding any single missing edge (i,j) yields a perfect matching: use (i,j), biject S\{i} to columns outside T, biject rows outside S to T\{j}; counts agree. Thus every such rectangle is a distinct minimal edge blocker, and count M(n)=sum_{s=1}^n binom(n,s)binom(n,n+1-s)=binom(2n,n+1)=Theta(4^n/sqrt(n)). For each positive clause Q_E accepting all matchable graphs, E hits all perfect matchings. If Q_E rejects maximal NO M, E is contained in its missing rectangle. Every proper subset of that rectangle fails to block a matching (the explicit one-edge lift), hence E equals the entire rectangle. Such a clause rejects at most one maximal NO. This classification was independently checked by exhaustive permutation enumeration and maximality on all 16+512+65536 small adjacency inputs; no enumeration of large maximal NO family.

## Integrated theorem 1 (PROVED WITH SCOPE, sharp)
For n>=14, suppose Match_n(x)=AND_(i=1)^q F(R_i(x)), where each R_i is an arbitrary-sized positive literal map to fixed target grid dimensions. Then q>=binom(2n,n+1), independent of map size. Proof: every query complete on all YES; Track A excludes deficient baselines and reduces each to constant1 or positive clause. Track C requires distinct query for every maximal NO. Equality attained: for each Hall rectangle S x T take a copy of the original all-zero adjacency grid (all masks exactly4, G=0); make optional 0110 at each core position in S x T depend positively on its original adjacency bit, all others fixed. F on this query equals OR of those edges. The conjunction of all queries is precisely Match by Hall. No source word or witness inferred from F shortcut; underlying G witness is separate. This is a sharp lower bound on a RESTRICTED nonadaptive conjunction oracle model. The exponential family is a symbolic construction, not claimed to be acquired in polynomial time or executed at large n. If allowing monotone coordinate circuits with local 0-certificates, each complete query similarly collapses to one counted monotone circuit; use Cavalar et al. theorem 1 for a superpolynomial total gate-size bound, not the exact number of queries (a single query may contain nonlocal monotone logic).

## Track B: global restriction, and integrated theorem 2
Let Match_n(x)=H(x,F(R_1(x)),...,F(R_q(x))) where H is a fixed monotone circuit and every coordinate of R_i is implemented by a counted monotone circuit. Let I={i:A(R_i(0))=0}, q0=|I|. For each i in I select one deficient cell, 13 zero coordinates there, and valid zero certificates at 0 on original adjacency bits (all pins zero). Z is the UNION of the pinned edge IDs, not sum of overlapping certificates. A positive literal coordinate has a zero certificate of size <=1; for a monotone d-junta it is <=d. On any input with x_Z=0, every i in I outputs F=0. Let P be a perfect matching of the complete graph avoiding Z, if it exists; acquire by ordinary matching. Let V_Z be a minimum bipartite vertex cover of the graph consisting precisely of pinned edges Z, acquired by a maximum matching plus alternating reachability (Konig). Write t=|V_Z|. Remove every matched pair of P meeting V_Z, at most t pairs. On removed pairs fix the matching edge to1, all other incident edges to0. Leave the remaining m-by-m submatrix freely variable, m>=n-t. No Z edge lies inside this submatrix (cover property), and no Z edge is fixed to1 (P avoids Z). Thus restriction is consistent with every zero certificate. Any perfect matching of the restricted graph must use the isolated fixed pairs; conversely any matching of free submatrix extends by those pairs. Exact identity Match_n restricted = Match_m.

All deficient queries now zero; all remaining ones simplify by Track A to constants or ORs of their already-counted monotone coordinates, after the same literal restriction. Therefore a circuit for Match_m exists of size O(S+M), where S is total outer and preprocessing circuit gates/wires, M is total number of explicit output membership coordinates. No F gates remain. The fixed baseline bits are legitimate nonuniform constants; we do not claim a uniform procedure finding them. Apply Cavalar et al. ECCC TR25-102 revision1 theorem1, page1, to this actual Match_m, obtaining O(S+M)>=2^(m^(1/3-o(1))). In particular if S+M<=n^C for one fixed C and n large, every selected certified kill set Z either blocks ALL perfect matchings (then contains some full Hall rectangle by Hall) OR has vertex cover size t>=n-(log n)^(3+o(1)). Derivation: if avoiding P exists, m>=n-t and m^(1/3-o(1))<=O(log n), hence m<=(log n)^(3+o(1)); bounded m is harmless. The conclusion is for any supplied/explicitly acquired valid certificate union, not free acquisition of minimum certificates.

Corollary: with 0-certificates size <=d_0 (d_0>=1), if 13d_0 q0<=n/2, then |Z|<=n/2, an avoiding cyclic perfect matching exists, t<=n/2, m>=n/2; no polynomial-size reduction of this model. This bounds the number of deficient queries, not all queries, and can be stronger using actual overlap/cover. Even arbitrarily many repeated queries killed by a small cover do not help. The test is polynomial in explicitly supplied map/certificate descriptions and n if coordinates are literal/junta or zero cert proof already provided. For arbitrary circuits, zero certificate verification/acquisition need not be easy; this is a semantic necessity, not a free oracle. When Z blocks every PM, restriction cannot give a free matching subproblem; no contradiction. Explicit isolating-row controls demonstrate this boundary. No claim that this dichotomy rules out all polynomial nonlocal monotone reductions.

## Further attack at stagnation and unrestricted bypass
Each source bit x_e itself is one positive-literal F query: all-zero adjacency grid plus optional 0110 controlled by x_e in a single core cell. It equals x_e by baseline NO and overflow. Thus n^2 such queries, NONE deficient at zero, followed by an ordinary polynomial-size matching circuit compute Match. The arbitrary Boolean outer computation is exactly the operation omitted by the monotone theorem; this explicitly falsifies the unrestricted extension even with simple local maps and zero deficient queries. Alternatively one nonlocally computed coordinate Match(x) implemented by deterministic augmenting-path algorithm triggers overflow in a fixed baseline, so even a functionally monotone map need not have a small MONOTONE implementation. Do not charge matching as an impossible gate just because the monotone circuit is expensive. All these maps are total-function F uses, not necessarily exact-four promise maps.

Reconstruct the actual all-decider conditional bridge separately: if G on exact-four inputs has polynomial-size ordinary circuits, use dual rails to move NOT to input literals; on a weight-four 16-bit block, NOT x_j is threshold >=4 of the other 15 coordinates. Substitute monotone threshold circuits for those negative rails. On D the resulting monotone circuit Q is correct. A AND (B OR Q) is exactly F on ALL inputs, including outside D. Ordinary deterministic P algorithm implies such polynomial-size circuits via standard computation unrolling; G lies in NP by explicit edge-word certificate, so P=NP implies polynomial monotone circuits for F. The reverse small nonuniform circuits do not imply uniform P. The new theorems exclude limited reduction mechanisms for proving a lower bound on F, NOT F itself. No lower bound against all monotone circuits for actual F established.
