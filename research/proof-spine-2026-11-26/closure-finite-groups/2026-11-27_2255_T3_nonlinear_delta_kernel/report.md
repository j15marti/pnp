## Exact theorem
Let V_d(r) be the square-free Boolean ANF space of degree <= fixed d over F2, and W a supplied certified subspace of identities on a fixed exact chart. Let T be an explicitly verified bijection of r-bit coordinates with coordinate ANFs of degree <= fixed e, mapping an exactly equivalent chart to the original chart. Define L(p)=p composed with T and H(p)=projection of L(p) onto monomials of degree >d. L is injective because T is surjective. The complete degree-safe subspace is ker(H restricted to W), with dimension dim W - rank(H|W). Compute it by coefficient expansion (idempotent Boolean variables, cancellations over F2) and Gaussian elimination tracking source combinations, then transport its basis. For fixed d,e and polynomially many supplied rows, there are O(r^(de)) possible monomials; substitution, rank and provenance bit-cost are polynomial in original N if the map, chart, certificates and their checking are already polynomially acquired. No semantic oracle. This is a filter on supplied identities, not proof acquisition. A transformed identity is not a root identity until the inverse/map and scope are verified; do not count old identities twice or reset credit. If T is an automorphism of the same feasible set, L(W) consists of valid identities on that set; independence relative to the existing root span still requires exact rank comparison. A coordinate change need not be a symmetry.

For a controlled swap T of a,b by g distinct from both, let sigma swap a,b. Write p=p0+g p1 with p0,p1 independent of g. Then L(p)=p0+g(p0+sigma p0+sigma p1). For p of degree <=d, the only degree d+1 coefficients come from the degree d part of p0+sigma p0. Thus degree preservation is equivalent to invariance of the homogeneous degree-d part of p0 under sigma. On the full V_d(r) the high-part map has rank binom(r-3,d-1) (zero if out of range): each orbit of two monomials a*S and b*S, |S|=d-1, S excludes a,b,g, contributes one independent output g(a+b)S. Monomials containing both or neither contribute zero. Explicit counterexample p=a*c for distinct a,b,c,g: L(p)=ac+gac+gbc, degree3. For two separately degree-unsafe rows ac and bc, their sum is safe; discarding individual high-degree rows misses this identity. For a guard g=1, the already guarded polynomial g*p transports as g*sigma(p), with no added degree (use its g-free representative); for g=0, (1+g)*p is unchanged. This is not valid for unrelated guards without checking. A reversible circuit with arbitrary degree has no fixed e promise; prior coNP exact-recognition barrier remains. No unrestricted lower bound.
