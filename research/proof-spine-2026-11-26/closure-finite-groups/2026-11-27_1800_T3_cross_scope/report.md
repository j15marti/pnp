## Exact pair fusion (proved)
For Boolean factors R on A and S on B, let f,g be their squarefree GF(2) violation indicators in the Boolean ring of A union B. Let h be a projected forbidden word indicator (or any polynomial function). h vanishes on R join S iff h(1+f)(1+g)=0 iff h=hf+hg+hfg. This is pointwise equality and hence equality of unique multilinear coefficient vectors by Boolean Mobius transform. Every local identity is checkable within O(w^2 2^w) bit operations up to indexing, where w=|A union B|; the products can have degree w. An added clause is necessary, not an equivalent replacement of the parents. A projection may eliminate a variable only after complete incident bucket inclusion or a separate interface theorem.

### Separator-one exact compilation lemma
Suppose A intersection B={v}, both factors are individually satisfiable under the negation of a proposed clause D on their union, but the join is not. Then the partial assignment does not pin v (otherwise both extend the same pinned value and can be combined). Each factor's nonempty set of feasible v-values must be disjoint, hence the sets are opposite singletons. The first factor therefore entails C_A OR v and the second entails C_B OR not v, with C_A,C_B subsets of D restricted to private variables. Minimize each entailed clause to a prime implicate; v cannot disappear because that would contradict separate satisfiability under the guard. Resolve on v. The resolvent is a subclause of D. Conversely every such resolvent is a valid join consequence. Thus one cross-parent resolution round, using complete local prime implicate lists, captures every genuinely joint forbidden word up to subsumption. For a fixed maximum factor scope w, acquire lists in O(6^w poly(w)) time per factor and enumerate pairs of prime clauses in O(9^w poly(w)) time per overlapping factor pair, plus certificate checks. This remains polynomial for w=O(log N) with a fixed constant and explicit tables. For k>1 shared variables, separately feasible boundary sets may be disjoint without either forcing a bit; exact boundary enumeration costs 2^k per query. No global guarantee of small separators or productive pair fusion. A bounded-width learned-clause potential is polynomial but can stagnate.
