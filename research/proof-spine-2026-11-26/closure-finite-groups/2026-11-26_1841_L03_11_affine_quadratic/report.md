## A: exact local algebra and standalone closure
Over F2, use Boolean multilinear ring (x²=x). For each nonempty table R of size <=4 acquire a basis of all polynomials of degree <=2 on the four original port bits vanishing on R, by nullspace of evaluation matrix with <=4 rows and 11 columns (constant, four linears, six pair products). This represents R EXACTLY: choose r0 in R. Affine dimension d<=3. If |R|=2^d, R is its affine hull, represented by affine equations. Otherwise R is affinely independent: for |R|=3 d=2, for |R|=4 d=3. In affine coordinates z on the hull, R={0,e1,...,ed}; its exact additional constraints are zi*zj=0 for all i<j. Extend those affine coordinate forms to the ambient four variables, giving degree<=2 polynomials. Thus all local degree<=2 vanishings have exactly R as their common Boolean zero set. Empty table directly rejects. All pins/guards are linear and retain their original IDs. No counting or equivalence oracle. Arc support closure deletes only unsupported rows. Standalone affine hull closure uses only affine vanishings and boundary pins. Standalone raw span uses the F2 linear span of all local degree<=2 vanishings plus pins, with no multiplication or substitution; 1 in span certifies NO. These are different deliberately restricted methods; raw span is NOT full polynomial calculus.

## B: hybrid affine retraction and global contradiction

This is exact because the chart parametrizes the entire affine solution space bijectively, not an approximation to it. The exact residual is the common zero set of the substituted quadratics, with original assignment reconstructed as a+Bz. Gaussian elimination in coefficient space of degree<=2 (at most 1+n+binom(n,2) monomials) can now cancel quadratic terms from distant original cells. This is not asserted equivalent to full degree-2 polynomial calculus (multiplication of nonlinear rows is not implemented). ## C: constructive adversarial native family, same-source comparator
Take left and right boundary cells on row floor(k/2) as two AND gates. Three internally vertex-disjoint paths connect them: top perimeter, bottom perimeter, and middle horizontal row. Each AND gate uses two perimeter path inputs and one middle-path output, pinning its unused exterior port to0. Wire cells have equality of their two active ports, except the first internal middle-row cell has inequality. Pin one unused port of every wire to its canonical inactive value (0 except south exterior1); other unused port free. Nonpath cells pin first two NESW ports to canonical values, leaving two free. Every table exactly four rows. All inactive pins agree, and active local projections each contain both values. Thus arc closure does not eliminate an active value. AND relation on three active bits has full affine hull: (0,0,0),(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(1,1,1) are affinely independent. Standalone affine hull is consistent (the three paths have two equality transports and one odd complement transport, no active cycle in the system of affine equalities). Any two distinct cells share at most one edge variable; every quadratic monomial with two distinct variables therefore belongs to at most one local table. In any raw combination cancelling all quadratic monomials, each cell's own combined polynomial must already be affine; it is a local affine vanishing and cannot yield a contradiction because the affine hull system is consistent. Raw span misses all k>=3. This proof remains true after arc closure: active supports and gate relation remain intact, inactive restrictions consistent.

Hybrid changes the domain of the coefficient span: chart identifies both upper-path inputs as a, both lower-path inputs as b, and complementary middle-path outputs c and 1+c. The two original gate vanishings become ab+c and ab+c+1. Their sum is1, certifying original UNSAT for every k>=3. No enumeration or search for a favorable chart; any exact affine chart gives the same cancellation. A second direct negative proof follows by deterministic path transport: both outputs must equal ab but the middle path requires inequality. This is a genuine hybrid, not a union of two solvers. It excludes an entire affine fiber with a two-row contradiction. The currently unhandled cheap operation for lower-bound transfer is nonlocal affine coordinate change followed by cancellation of equal nonlinear observations.

The source is not claimed to survive every possible polynomial closure. It survives initial arc, full local affine hull and raw degree<=2 linear span; the hybrid is specifically designed to bypass them. A simpler specialized gate-recognition or bounded-width solver may also decide it. Equality, affine and compact-circuit controls are intrinsic to the example. Perfect matching is a known polynomial terminal even with hard monotone representations; no new matching reduction or experiment here. No assertion of superiority to all solvers.
