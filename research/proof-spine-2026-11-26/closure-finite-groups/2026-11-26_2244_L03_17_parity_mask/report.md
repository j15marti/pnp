## Source, quantifiers, falsifiers
Ordinary explicitly encoded CNF, not a claim of NP-completeness for the constructed subclass. Start with functional graph pigeonhole formula F(G), simple bipartite graph, left row exactly-one, right hole at-most-one, all row degrees >=2, hole degrees >=2, maximum degree d fixed. NO by Hall (our examples n rows, n-1 holes). Enumerate original edges e=1..q. Replace every x_e by parity of b fresh shares y_(e,j) with ID e+jq, j=0..b-1. For each original width-w clause, encode each full share assignment falsifying that clause by the corresponding blocking CNF clause. Exactly 2^((b-1)w) distinct clauses of width bw. Define F_b. For every original assignment x, exactly 2^((b-1)q) lifts, and EVERY lift satisfies F_b iff x satisfies F; any lift preserves the original failed-clause identity. This is exact existential projection, not a free SAT oracle or a count-based decision inference. For any fixed n, parent b=4 and guard y_(e,j)=0 for j>=r, r=1..4, exact original-ID restriction yields F_r; every stage independently NO. Standalone growing n,b input bit length N includes all explicit expanded literals, indices and guards. Falsifiers: incorrect parity fiber, premature unit propagation at fewer than 2b-1 pins, symmetry restriction losing a satisfying orbit, or inferred arbitrary-decider hardness despite cheap Hall quotient. 
Named obligation: can increasing local refutation depth and known exponentially hard shared proofs enforce standard-machine time after exact source-acquired symmetry contraction?

## A: local blindness and restricted lower bound
All clauses in F_b have width >=2b, distinct variables. Under any <=2b-2 original share pins, every surviving clause retains at least two unassigned literals. UP cannot make a single inference or detect contradiction. Thus any fixed depth k of ordinary branching + UP, including bounded-depth failed literal probes on the unchanged formula, can be defeated by b with 2b-2>=k. This does NOT include arbitrary learned clauses, Gaussian, changing coordinates or additional terminals. Every unshared UP tree has at least 2^(2b-1) leaves since all its paths must reach depth >=2b-1 before UP can do anything. This bound alone is polynomial in n if b=ceil(log2 n); do not call it a superpolynomial time bound.

Stronger known proof-system bound: Miksa/Nordstrom 2014 Theorem9 states that for fixed d>=3 and 0<delta<1/2, almost all d-regular bipartite graphs on n+n vertices have |N(S)| >= (d-3/2+delta)|S| for |S|<=epsilon n. Use d=4, remove one hole. In original graph boundary (unique neighbors) >=2|N(S)|-4|S| >= (1+2delta)|S|; removing one hole costs at most one, so new boundary >=2delta|S| for every nonempty S in range. Minimum remaining left degree3, right degree4. Miksa/Nordstrom JACM2024 Theorem5.8 gives PC degree > (2delta epsilon n)/(2d) and expanded PC/PCR size exp(Omega(n)); Theorem3.14 plus standard width-size bound gives exp(Omega(n)) resolution size (full matching of right into left survives from regular base). Setting all non-representative shares zero recovers F(G) exactly; restriction of resolution or PC/PCR refutations cannot increase size (satisfied axioms become zero, others restrict to original axioms; Boolean/complement variables pinned consistently). Hence F_b requires at least exp(Omega(n)) expanded resolution and PC/PCR proof size even with sharing. No claim of multiplying the lower bound by mask depth. For b=ceil(log2 n), d<=4, F_b has O(n^5) clauses, width O(log n), N=O(n^5(log n)^2) explicit bits under binary or DIMACS indices, hence bound superpolynomial in N. For every fixed k, infinitely many such NOs defeat k-depth UP and have shared-proof size superpolynomial, while the algorithm below decides them in P. This asymptotic statement uses the existence theorem, not measured expansion of test circulants.

## B: source-acquired symmetry quotient (no graph labels supplied)
General lemma on a normalized CNF with distinct literals, duplicate clauses removed. Bucket variables by exact unsigned clause-occurrence signatures. In each bucket choose one representative r. For every other variable s, verify that complementing BOTH r and s preserves the clause set exactly (scan affected clauses and test full signed-clause membership; injective involution so inclusion suffices). Failed checks are not imported. For all accepted s, fix s=0 simultaneously, leaving r free. Every satisfying assignment can be carried to this chart by applying the verified flip (r,s) once for each s initially equal to1. Earlier fixed s' remain fixed because flips affect only their own s and common r. Representatives of different buckets disjoint. Hence F is satisfiable iff restricted F is satisfiable. These are symmetry-break choices, NOT unconditionally forced logical assignments. Witness lift from chart to source is the identity with zero pins; reverse projection toggles representative by parity of removed variables. For guarded F|g, perform on the residual with original guard pins already fixed, never flip a fixed variable; exact only under g. General arbitrary CNF symmetry checks and restriction polynomial, but not guaranteed useful or complete. 
For this particular mask the buckets are exactly the original blocks: all b copies occur in exactly the same clauses; distinct edges in different rows distinguished by a row ALO clause, and distinct edges in same row distinguished by a hole-conflict clause with a different row because hole degree >=2. All even pair flips preserve each clause fiber. Therefore every block contracts to one representative, yielding exactly original F(G), up to original representative names. This recovers a (b-1)q-dimensional symmetry orbit of size 2^((b-1)q) by (b-1)q verified generators rather than enumerating assignments. It does not count all SAT witnesses or assert semantic uniqueness of solutions.
Recognize resulting functional PHP directly: all-positive clauses partition variables into rows; cross-row negative binary conflicts form hole cliques; connected components give hole IDs, validate distinct rows inside each component, then check EXACT expected set of row/hole exclusions, reject any other clauses. Graph simple. Find maximum matching via augmenting paths; alternating reach from unmatched rows gives Hall certificate S,T with |S|>|T| and every edge from S ends in T. Check from source. Sum original row at-least-one and hole at-most-one inequalities to contradict. Import only existential quotient (with verified orbit map) into matching and reapply UP; no actual forced pins claimed. A general algorithm returns UNKNOWN if recognition fails; on this entire admitted family it decides correctly in polynomial time, including feasible controls. No arbitrary SAT coverage.

## C: independent exact parity-fiber reconstruction
A second method uses occurrence buckets but NOT pair-flip verification or the original intended graph. Normalize/deduplicate all clauses. Each clause must contain all variables of every block it touches, which follows by signature but checked. Project the clause's falsifying share word onto block parities, obtaining a blocking clause on representatives. Group original clauses by projected blocking clause. A complete parity fiber for support B has exactly 2^(sum_(i in B)(|B_i|-1)) possible distinct signed clauses. The observed clauses are distinct members of that fiber by construction; equality of count certifies EXACT completeness. If every observed fiber is complete, F equals the pullback of projected CNF on every assignment, not just equisatisfiable. Missing fiber entries cause rejection, never assumed by interpolation. This proves a second source-acquired quotient with polynomial acquisition using only explicit counts and integer bit lengths O(N); absent parity words impose no restriction. It is valid for arbitrary normalized CNF satisfying the check; no general equivalence oracle. General proof on same source is independent of the symmetry-generator proof. Both methods agree on all admitted instances and match an independently generated base. This is a stronger audit than merely comparing cardinalities of projected domains. The fiber method itself may also feed Hall, so no exclusive hybrid advantage. Minimal correctness control: (a OR b) AND (!a OR !b) admits double flip and either value of a; setting a=0 is NOT a logical consequence, only a SAT-preserving representative choice. Deleting one source expanded clause breaks its fiber: strict fiber checker rejects and generator checker accepts only verified remaining flips. Damaged formula is not asserted UNSAT and excluded from research NO set.
