## A: constructive native wall and exact witness lift
Define a connected active graph H_k on all cells: every horizontal edge is active; vertical edge from (r,c) to (r+1,c) is active if c=0, c=k-1, or r+c even. All other edges/ports inactive. Every vertex has active degree d in {2,3}: interior non-boundary columns have one vertical incident edge and two horizontals, left/right sides have two vertical edges and one horizontal (corners degree2); top/bottom internal columns have at most one vertical. Every active edge lies on a cycle: a horizontal edge is on a cycle using the two side columns and an adjacent row, and a vertical edge is on a cycle with another vertical in that row gap and the horizontal paths in the two rows. All edge IDs and embedding retained.

Choose the first inactive port at each vertex in NESW order. Pin it to the canonical inactive-edge value 0, except south exterior ports have canonical value1. All other inactive ports of this vertex are unconstrained. Define R_v^b by parity of the d active incident bits equal to charge b_v, and the chosen inactive pin. This contains 2^(d-1)*2^(3-d)=4 allowed words exactly. The two charge masks at each cell are disjoint, distinct, and each membership coordinate is 0, b_v or NOT b_v. Inactive pins and exterior values are consistent since the canonical assignment uses one value per edge. Thus all charge vectors map to legal exact-four tables and F=G on every image. The existential active constraints are precisely Tseitin parity equations on H_k.

For a constructive witness, set all non-tree active edges0 and inactive edges to their canonical values. Acquire a spanning tree with original edge IDs; in postorder assign each parent edge the residual charge at the child, XOR that charge into its parent. This satisfies every nonroot equation and the root iff the sum of charges is even. Check all original table words and exterior values, not merely the active relation. Odd root residual certifies infeasibility. This is a uniform polynomial exact algorithm; adjacency-list operation count O(V), bit-array time polynomial even on a sequential multitape TM. Charge recognition: every table must equal one of the two canonical masks (compare all 16 membership bits); no unrecognized instance is labeled by this terminal.

Local arc closure cannot refute any charge image: initial pins/exterior affect only inactive ports. For each active degree >=2 and either value of any active bit, some allowed local assignment to the other active bits satisfies the parity. Fixing more inactive values never couples to active parity. At the fixed point every active edge retains both values, and canonical inactive values survive. For even charges, every active edge truly has both attainable values globally by XORing any active cycle through it. For odd charges, this local closure is incomplete; global XOR is the certified contradiction. No claim about surviving full Gaussian closure. After the global contradiction/witness, the integrated run terminates, not a new open residual.

## B: global algebra and guard-preserving distribution theorem
The XOR of all vertex equations is zero on the left; the right is the global charge parity. Conversely the tree lift proves sufficiency. For each proper subset U of vertices, any charge restriction to U can be completed to even parity (and separately to odd parity), using at least one free vertex. Hence all proper subsets of vertex constraints are jointly satisfiable irrespective of odd full charge, and all proper source-block marginals of the two uniform distributions D_even,D_odd on complete legal images agree exactly. In fact, conditional on any fixed proper charge guard leaving r>=1 free coordinates, the induced distributions are uniform on the two opposite parities on those r coordinates. Every proper subcollection of the remaining coordinates has exactly the same marginal under the two distributions, including all overlapping individual membership bits.

For any real polynomial P in membership bits of total degree <r, substitute the source literal map and multilinearize on the free charge cube. Every monomial touches fewer than r distinct free charge bits, so its expectations agree under conditional D_even and D_odd. By linearity E_even P=E_odd P. But a pointwise approximator of the 0/1 decision with error epsilon<1/2 on both classes must have E_even P >=1-epsilon > epsilon >= E_odd P, contradiction. Therefore approximate degree >=r on every such guard. Degree r suffices on this subfamily using the product of (1-2b_v) times the fixed parity sign, shifted and divided by2. At the empty guard this gives approximate degree >=V=N/16 for actual F on all encoded inputs. This is a degree bound, not a size bound. Signed literal projection preserves degree, not monotone gate polarity.

A one-sided sound positive DNF term accepting any legal even image must fix every free charge bit: if it omits a cell, flip that charge to obtain an odd legal image satisfying the same term. Since the two masks in each cell are disjoint, a satisfiable term that fixes all r bits accepts just one free charge vector. A sound DNF covering fraction 1-delta of even images therefore needs >=ceil((1-delta)*2^(r-1)) terms. For exact DNF take delta=0; dual positive CNF clauses that reject no even image cover at most one odd charge each. The corresponding lower bounds hold for actual F, and for any extension with the same labels on this support. They do not apply to arbitrary AND/OR DAGs or two-sided approximate DNF without an extra argument.

## C: independent adversarial separator and high-width proof barrier
Choose one canonical membership rail e_v for charge0 and another o_v for charge1 (take the all-zero active word with the fixed inactive value, then flip one active port). On legal images, these rails are complementary. For disjoint subregions with even/odd rails (E1,O1),(E2,O2), merge by E=(E1 AND E2) OR (O1 AND O2), O=(E1 AND O2) OR (O1 AND E2). These are ordinary monotone gates in the original membership coordinates. A balanced tree has O(V) gates and O(log V) depth; exact on the entire distribution, no off-promise reliance, negation gates, free oracle or SAT preprocessing. This adversarial construction disproves the generic implication from all-proper-marginal equality, linear approximate degree, or exponential sound DNF cover to superpolynomial monotone DAG size. The currently unhandled cheap operation is complementary rail composition and reuse: high-degree global correlations can be computed with O(V) Boolean gates. The final parity separator does not compute F outside this family.

Stronger counterexample on a total monotone function: define the exact-four promise language H to accept iff each table is exactly one canonical mask and the charge vector has even parity. This is an ordinary polynomial-time promise predicate. Define e_v and o_v now as the conjunctions of the four membership coordinates of the respective masks. On the exact-four slice these indicators identify membership in the family; if neither matches at a vertex, both rails are zero and remain zero through merge. Let C be the resulting even rail. Then the upper completion A AND (B OR C) is monotone and has O(N) gates on ALL membership inputs, including off-promise inputs, yet has all the preceding DNF and degree lower bounds. This is an explicit counterexample to a representation-independent transfer based on those properties. It does not settle the actual F.

Further complementary attack after stagnation: contract the horizontal edge inside every adjacent column pair (2j,2j+1) of H_k. Every consecutive row has at least one vertical edge in every pair (by the checkerboard rule). After contraction and parallel-edge removal, the rectangular k by k/2 grid is a minor. Its treewidth is at least k/2-1: in its square k/2 subgrid use the connected row-column crosses, pairwise intersecting; every set of fewer than k/2 vertices misses some row and column, hence some cross. In a tree decomposition the connected bag subtrees of all crosses pairwise intersect, so tree Helly gives one bag hitting every cross. Thus tw(H_k)=Omega(k). Width of the full native graph is also high, but width alone is not a time bound.

For a FIXED charge input, encode the native four-port tables by the twelve clauses forbidding disallowed words (one clause per word) and include exterior units, shared edge variables. Pin all inactive edges to their canonical values. Surviving non-tautological clauses at each vertex are exactly the standard CNF encoding of its active parity equation (duplicates may be removed). This is an exact variable restriction, not a reduction requiring an oracle. For even charges, any DNNF for the fixed native edge-witness relation restricts to a DNNF for the satisfiable Tseitin formula. For odd charges, any regular-resolution refutation restricts without size increase to a regular-resolution refutation of the Tseitin CNF, by dropping satisfied clauses and bypassing resolved assigned pivots (or keeping a subclause of the surviving resolvent). Theorem 4.1 and 4.3 of Itsykson, Riazanov, Smirnov, SAT 2022, yield 2^Omega(tw(H_k)) = 2^Omega(sqrt N) respective lower bounds. Their theorem 3.1 also bounds OBDD conjunction/reordering refutations on the restricted Tseitin CNF; this report does not require a native OBDD proof replay. These are KNOWN THEOREM applications, not a new proof of the underlying theorems; exact adapter proved here, independent review pending. For conventional CNF variable-ID encoding O(N log N) bits, express the bound in that encoding as 2^Omega(sqrt(N_CNF/log N_CNF)). These DNNF/refutation objects are NOT circuits deciding satisfiability from table membership input bits. The cheap tree algorithm on the identical source witnesses the gap. Historical TSEITIN-EXPANDER-PAIR83 established related parity/core bypass for nonplanar cubic CNF; the present exact-four native adapter, guarded degree/DNF separator and high-width planar proof-system comparison are the additional scope, not a priority claim.
