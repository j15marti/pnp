Suppose (i) x0 in L, (ii) conv(P) intersects {Ax=b}, (iii) differences u-v of elements of P with Au=Av span ker_Q A over Q. If w annihilates kernel (g=0), w is constant on Ax=b and fractional witness in conv(P) puts c inside interval. Otherwise some spanning move d=u-v has nonzero integer cost, divisible by g. Thus width >= |w.d| >= g, so closed interval contains an integer congruent to c modulo g. This is unconditional for all w, not just bounded coefficients, and includes adaptive scalar choice on unchanged source. It says nothing about iterative cuts changing P, guarded subproblems, nonlinear costs, or exact scalar supports. No Markov basis connectivity assumption: rational spanning suffices.
