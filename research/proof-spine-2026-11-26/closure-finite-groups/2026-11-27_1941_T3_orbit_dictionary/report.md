## Exact theorem and guard stabilizers
For an explicit factor CSP F on r Boolean coordinates, each factor R_i subset F2^{S_i}, arity <= fixed k, define H_i={h:R_i+h=R_i}. This is a vector subspace: 0 is present, and composition of two translations is translation by their sum. Enumerate 2^k local masks and check every allowed row (bijection makes inclusion equality), obtain H_i^perp by local parity tests, and embed its equations into the full variable list. The common factorwise translation group H is exactly the kernel of the resulting homogeneous binary system. It can be acquired by deterministic Gaussian elimination, with no semantic automorphism oracle. For affine carrier A={x:Lx=a} and guard G={x:Mx=b}, add Lh=0 and Mh=0 to restrict to the stabilizer H_AG. For a nonaffine guard, this formula does NOT apply; preserve that guard or acquire its exact translation stabilizer separately. A syntactic factorwise group is not necessarily the full semantic automorphism group.

Let t=dim H_AG, choose t coordinate positions P on which a basis restricts invertibly, and row-reduce the generator matrix B (columns) so B_P=I. For any x in A intersect G, c(x)=x+B x_P (GF2) has c(x)_P=0, is in the same orbit, and is the unique such point: any two representatives differ by Bz with z=0. Thus F(x)=F(c(x)), and feasibility is equivalent to the section F|x_P=0 within the original context. One solution lifts by inclusion, all orbit members lift by Bz. Exact solution count, if asked, is 2^t times section count; decision does not require enumeration. Represent dictionary by at most r*t bits. No exponential orbit enumeration; polynomial explicit bit arithmetic. Enumerating local masks and checking rows costs O(m 4^k poly(k)); dense elimination and systematic generator extraction take polynomial bit time (e.g. O((m2^k+r) r^2+r^3) elementary bit operations, plus explicit input/reverse map and indexing). Explicit local tables and original IDs are charged. No claim of arbitrary semantic canonicalization.

## New credit transfer and affine guard pullback
For a certified degree-d vanishing span W on a dimension-r affine carrier, restrict W to a section of codimension t, expressed in independent affine coordinates. Restriction from Boolean polynomials of degree <=d is onto the section's degree <=d space: affine coordinates on the section extend to affine linear forms on the ambient carrier. Its kernel dimension is D_d(r)-D_d(r-t), where D_d(r)=sum_{j=0}^d binom(r,j). Hence dim W' >= dim W - [D_d(r)-D_d(r-t)]. With Phi=D_d(r)-dim W+r, Phi' <= Phi-t. This is valid even though the section omits solutions: exact orbit equivalence, not an erroneous claim that all original solutions lie in the section, certifies the decision and reconstruction. Evaluate and verify via polynomial-size coefficient matrices for fixed d. Only genuine acquired stabilizer directions receive this credit; no resetting after equivalent coordinate changes, branches, projection or failed acquisition. Those other changes still need global charge.

A guard P(y) on the section pulls back exactly to P(c(x)), a conjunction of affine equations when P is a coordinate pin conjunction. A chart clause with w literals lifts to a root exclusion with indicator product of at most w affine forms (degree <=w), independently of orbit size. This gives a bounded-degree bridge to T4's affine identity ledger. For the one-dimensional complement group, relative original port tests x_v XOR x_g=b are the pullback. No absolute bit pin is asserted unconditionally. Restricted cores retain all signed transport/equality constraints, and complement preserves any selected subset of B constraints. Thus a local chart contradiction with a checked original B support implies the root affine exclusion using just that support.

## Infinite obstruction to translation-only completeness
For the pinned compiler on any 3CNF with three distinct occurring variables per clause, every normalized B factor has four distinct carriers (A,B,z,slack or not-z,C,anchor,slack). On exactly-two-of-four, any odd-weight translation breaks even parity, any weight-two mask maps the corresponding legal word to zero, and weight four is global complement. Literal signs conjugate by translation and do not change this stabilizer. Therefore each factor enforces equality of the four translation coordinates. Every clause connects both triples through z and every second triple through the common anchor; all occurring carriers are connected. Consequently H has dimension one, regardless of source formula size or label. After fixing the anchor, H_AG is trivial. Ordinary proper 3SAT is NP-complete (standard padding can ensure distinct variables). The compiler's guarded chart remains NP-hard in this class. Thus a polynomially acquirable translation dictionary is useful preprocessing but cannot itself prove universal contraction; trivial stabilizer is compatible with both labels and arbitrarily growing NP-hard input class. This does not imply P!=NP or exclude other affine/permutation symmetries. Distinguish exact restricted factorwise result from semantic group recognition. No new native family registered/executed in this run; analytic statement covers the pinned compiler family.
