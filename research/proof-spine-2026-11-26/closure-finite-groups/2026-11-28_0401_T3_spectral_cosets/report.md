## Proof
For a bounded-arity conjunction of factors R_i on I_i, a proposed affine chart C=a+im B is contained in the joint relation iff each projected chart a_I+im B_I is contained in R_i. Compute the local projected basis by GE and enumerate at most 2^arity points per factor, independently of dimension of C. An affine guard Ax=b adds checks Aa=b and AB=0. Thus polynomially many proposed generator extensions can be checked in polynomial work in original N for bounded arity, given a verified seed and explicit directions. For existential projection retain checked interior witnesses. No complete cover or seed acquisition theorem follows.

For explicit support S in GF(2)^k define F(u)=sum_{x in S}(-1)^(u dot x). Its translation stabilizer is exactly the annihilator of span{u:F(u)!=0} by character inversion. Autocorrelation A(h)=|S intersect (S+h)|=2^-k sum_u F(u)^2(-1)^(u dot h). Greedy chart extension uses exact A(h) order, checks independence and every point of C+h in uncovered support, removes C when done. Disjoint coverage checked by enumeration. With k<=c log(N+2), all directions, failed work, WHT and replay polynomial (conservative O(2^(3k) poly(k,N)), integer magnitudes <=2^(3k)). Not a guarantee for unbounded k or an optimal cover.

On overlapping scopes I,J charts a+B u,b+D v glue iff B_overlap u+D_overlap v=a_overlap+b_overlap. GE gives particular solution and nullspace. Joined dimension d1+d2-rank of this matrix, exact point count 2^dimension, since both bases injective. Retain parameter maps for reconstruction. Disjoint covers produce disjoint joined cells; cost polynomial per pair, cumulative pair count not yet bounded.
