## 2. Two canonical completions (PROVED)
Let G be exact ordinary grid satisfiability on arbitrary tables. It is monotone in each membership bit. D is the product of exact-four masks. Let A mean every table has at least four entries, B mean some table has at least five entries. For any predicate h:D->{0,1}, define h_lo(z)=1 iff exists x in D, x<=z and h(x)=1; h_hi(z)=0 iff exists x in D, x>=z and h(x)=0. Both monotone, both equal h on D since D is an antichain, and every monotone extension H satisfies h_lo<=H<=h_hi. On G|D, lower completion L=A AND G: if A and G, choose one actual allowed word at each cell from a witness, retain it plus any three other allowed words. The result is an exact-four subinput with the same witness. Conversely legal subinput YES implies A and G. For upper completion U=1 iff NOT exists dominating legal NO: if A false, extend each deficient table to weight4 unless another table is overweight; in that mixed case there is no dominating legal mask, so the *literal maximal extension* h_hi can equal1 even with A false. Important correction: L02-03's F=A AND (B OR G) is a convenient upper completion within the region A, with F=0 outside A; it is the greatest monotone extension subject to rejection outside A, NOT the globally greatest extension on all malformed mixtures. For A true, F agrees with h_hi; for A false and B false, global h_hi may be 0 or1 depending on attainable dominating NOs. No global maximality claim for F. This does not affect L02's valid compiler: if A false reject, if A and B true accept, otherwise all blocks exact-four. On D any input complement is threshold4 of other15 bits; dual rail simulation yields O(s+N) positive gates for size-s ordinary circuit, with F wrapper and fixed polynomial bit overhead. Lower and upper completion are different targets.

## 4. Unconditional easy-predicate counterexample on identical native grid
Let C=(B0 minus 0000) union {0110}, an exact-four mask. On D define H=1 iff all padding is precisely the above fixed masks, each core cell is B0 or C, and C cells form a permutation matrix. This is a P predicate (even O(N) ordinary validation) with H implies G on D: actual prefix witness uses 0110 at each chosen C, never the removed word. On positive projection any perfect matching produces such a legal submask: place C exactly at its selected cells, B0 elsewhere. Conversely every accepted submask gives a matching. Hence H_lo restricted to SAME projection equals Match. On arbitrary full masks H_lo itself is in P: check padding contains required mask, compute possible B0/C inclusion bits per cell; reject neither, force C at C-only cells, reject conflicting forced choices, remove their rows/columns, solve remaining bipartite matching using C-admitted cells; B0-only cells disallow selection, both allow choice. This is exact because all unselected cells must admit B0 and selected ones C. No free witness. Use augmenting paths, original prefix lift and actual selected legal submask. H_lo is monotone and P, yet has the stated superpoly monotone lower bound.

H admits an explicit positive circuit on D: test mask equality by AND of its four membership bits; under the promise inclusion equals equality. For every row/column OR over the candidate selected C position AND all other positions B0, require every row and column test and all fixed padding mask tests. O(n³) bounded fan-in gates (or prefix/suffix sharing O(n²)). Wrap A AND (B OR H_positive) to obtain a total monotone polynomial circuit agreeing with H on D, rejecting outside A. Consequently even an already positive polynomial circuit on D cannot always be transformed to a polynomial monotone circuit for its canonical lower completion. Contradiction: compose such generic transformation with explicit H circuit, then restrict by positive matching projection, violating known bound. This is a stronger unconditional control than an argument conditional on P=NP. It does not exclude a special compiler for actual G if such a theorem is separately proved. ## 5. Explicit Hall-guard repair tariff (PROVED scoped)
Maximal NO graphs for bipartite perfect matching are exactly K_{n,n} minus S×T, where S,T nonempty subsets of opposite sides with |S|+|T|=n+1. Hall: for any NO choose deficient S, choose T of size n+1-|S| from complement of neighbors. Then graph lies below the corresponding maximal NO. The stated missing rectangle is deficient by one, and adding any one edge u in S,v in T permits matching u-v, biject S\{u} to all columns outside T (equal counts), and all rows outside S to T\{v}; hence maximal. Distinct rectangles give distinct maxima. Number M=sum_s binom(n,s)binom(n,n+1-s)=binom(2n,n+1)=Theta(4^n/sqrt n). For n>=2 all maximal NO graphs nonempty. Given any monotone extension of G|D, every projected matching YES is forced accepted by legal witness submask. If every projected maximal NO is forced rejected, all projected NO are rejected by monotonicity, so the matching lower bound transfers. But F accepts every maximal NO. If one repairs F by conjunctively adding one down-set-exclusion guard per chosen maximal NO, namely OR of membership coordinates absent in that actual mask, then each guard excludes precisely its own maximal NO among the projected maxima. Every guard is sound on actual G YES since ordinary grid monotonicity prohibits a YES below a NO. Therefore all M explicit clauses necessary and sufficient for this particular repair on projection; no arbitrary-circuit lower bound from guard count alone. This exposes exactly why free all-Hall guards are impermissible and why hard NO examples outside promise do not establish a lower bound on F. For polynomially many explicitly supplied guards the wrapper remains polynomial overhead; no claim this computes all of G. Alternative compact exact positive repair would already compute matching on projection and is excluded by theorem1. Ordinary nonmonotone repair via matching is polynomial. No unrestricted separation.
