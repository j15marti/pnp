## New scoped theorem: unrestricted guard-cover acquisition cannot be replaced by a free width-two affine coverage test

Let A=F2^n, and explicit guards G_i each specified by at most two affine equalities. Deciding whether their union equals A is coNP-complete, even when every guard has codimension two (allow signed affine equations). Membership in coNP: an uncovered point is a polynomially checkable witness. Reduction: take 3SAT with three distinct variables per clause (NP-complete). Add shared f and fresh z per clause. Interpret physical source literals A,B,C relative to f. The clause holds iff NAE4(A,B,C,f), iff exists z with NAE3(A,B,z) and NAE3(not z,C,f). For every NAE triple (u,v,w), its violation guard is u+v=0 and v+w=0 in F2; linear parts independent because the three coordinates are distinct. Signed literals give affine constants. The union of violation guards covers the entire physical assignment space iff there is no assignment satisfying the NAE conjunction, iff the original formula is UNSAT. Polynomial reduction. This is a theorem about the abstract supplied-guard cover subproblem, not about the difficulty of every E/B decider or every reachable guard collection. In particular five guards still cost at most 31 exact affine intersection counts.

A constructive tractable escape: if all distinct nonconstant affine test linear parts (after parameterizing the common carrier and merging identical forms, including opposite constants) are linearly independent, their values range freely over a Boolean cube. The complement of a guard with <=2 tests is a clause of width<=2 in these coordinates. Test coverage by exact 2-SAT on all complement clauses; a surviving assignment lifts via GE. Constants and contradictory guards simplified exactly. Polynomial acquisition of the linear chart and rank check suffice; no independence assumption for arbitrary inputs. With dependent tests the 2-SAT chart must respect their affine compatibility, and the preceding reduction explains why ignoring it is invalid.

Concrete minimum-cover obstruction: the seven Fano triples [123,145,167,246,257,347,356] in seven Boolean variables; each monochromatic triple gives a codimension-two homogeneous guard. Exact enumeration of all128 assignments finds none uncovered and ten assignments exclusive to each guard (so any subset of six, hence five, misses points). This is an explicit finite counterexample to universal sufficiency of five supplied guards, not to adaptive acquisition of new guards, alternative terminals, or cooperative progress. Five-lane execution does not imply five-guard cover. Repair by independent-test 2-SAT when recognized, or by charged bounded-size exact inclusion-exclusion and guard forgetting; universal affordable escape remains OPEN.
