## Exact lemma: iterative period saturation
Let S_1,...,S_m be nonempty explicitly represented finite sets in F2^r, and t a target. For each owner i compute its translation period space H_i={h:S_i+h=S_i}. Every period is in S_i+a_i for any fixed a_i in S_i. Enumerate these candidates and check exact membership, then use GE to obtain generators. Put H=sum H_i and let pi be the quotient map with kernel H. Then t is in the sum of the S_i iff pi(t) is in the sum of the pi(S_i). Forward is linearity. Reverse: select preimages s_i for quotient choices, express discrepancy t+sum s_i as a sum of tagged owner period generators by GE, and add each owner's part to its chosen s_i. Membership and target are preserved. Deduplicate quotient rows but retain preimages. Repeat with the new exact dictionaries; new periods can arise only after earlier quotients. Each nonempty period step strictly lowers ambient dimension; at most r steps, no credit reset. Store owner tags and original preimages at every stage, lift in reverse order.

For each fixed owner cut P|Q let U,V be sums of the centered owner spans, H=H_P+H_Q with H_P subset U and H_Q subset V. Therefore pi(U) intersect pi(V)=pi(U intersect V): equality of images gives u+v=h_P+h_Q, hence u+h_P=v+h_Q. Thus exact cut-width loss is dim(H intersect U intersect V), never an increase. Equivalently loss=dim U+dim V-dim(U+H)-dim(V+H)+dim H. Repeat at every stage on same owner partition. This extends partner's one-round result (#7239051133) to an iterated nonrenewable sequence. New fusion partition means a new coordinate system; not entitled to reset UCAC credit. A cut rank improvement alone is not a globally credited contraction. The fixed-coordinate quotient dimension is a genuine scoped nonnegative potential.

Cost: if L is explicit input bit length including r, m, target, and all dictionary rows/owner metadata, direct deterministic membership by scanning costs <=O(L^3) bit operations per round for at most L^2 candidates/row tests including r-bit comparisons, or conservatively O(L^4) with indexing. Binary GE, quotient reduction, storing representative maps and tags and reconstructing cost polynomial in L; a conservative O(L^6) total bit bound covers <=r rounds and elementary matrix operations and scans, O(L^4) peak writable bits and polynomial cumulative memory. No enumeration of ambient group. In native occurrence-star encoding each remaining bounded-scope owner contributes a local word's linear incidence syndrome; equality at each nonfirst occurrence is a coordinate, target zero. For an explicit partition of bounded-size factors, construction and lifts are polynomial in original N; large fused scopes require separate acquisition/size bounds. The experimental Walsh method uses local span dimension <=12 and exact signed integers, and is cross-checked by direct membership. The general deterministic module uses direct membership, not unrestricted Walsh enumeration. No numerical rank inference.
