## Theorem and obstruction
For a fixed chart of r Boolean variables and m bounded-width factors, let dictionary D of distinct non-tautological unit/binary clauses plus empty clause. |D| <=1+2r+4 binom(r,2). A bounded local exact joint-support deduction is sound: every global solution restricts to an allowed word of the selected parent conjunction, so exclusion of a pair word is a valid consequence. Each new row decreases |D minus admitted| by 1, and every unsuccessful full epoch is charged and then stops. For fixed scope bound b, enumeration of O(m^2) candidate unions, all currently admitted parent binary rows, all 2^b words and candidate exports is polynomial; O(r^2) productive epochs. No variable resurrection, no resetting the dictionary. The procedure is not universally complete. RANDOM has 183 distinct triple scopes, one duplicate group containing two forbidden words of the same parity, all others one. A proper triple with <=2 same-parity forbidden words has full pair projection. Two distinct such groups sharing one variable have full pair projections across their join: fixed cross pair extends through either shared value since both original pair projections are full. If they share two variables, a fixed pair in their union either lies inside a factor (extend the other by full shared-pair support), or fixes both private endpoints. The latter leaves four shared words; at most three forbidden by the two groups combined when at most one group has two forbidden words. At least one survives. Disjoint groups also have full pair projections. Thus a class with at most one double group of this type, all other proper triples single forbidden words, gives a complete structural refusal for this exact two-factor pair-export rule; binary path and resolution cannot start.
