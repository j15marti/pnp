## Exact central-extension theorem
Admit coordinate group G=F2^a × F2^c with group product
(u,z)*(v,w)=(u+v,z+w+B(u,v)),
where B:F2^a × F2^a -> F2^c is a supplied bilinear map. Bilinearity gives associativity by expansion; identity zero, inverse (u,z+B(u,u)). This is a class-at-most-two central extension, not a claim that all class-two groups possess this chosen coordinate form. The explicit structure coefficients and charts are checked, no exponential multiplication table is required. Each chart reads any sequence of affine labels (u_i(x),z_i(x)) on the same primary Boolean vector x; repeated variables, different orders, complemented bits and constants are allowed. Initial state identity, fixed terminal group element for each chart. Affine guard Ax=b permitted; no arbitrary nonlinear omitted constraints. An exact verified source adapter is required, otherwise UNKNOWN.

Induction gives terminal coordinates U=sum_i u_i and Z=sum_i z_i+sum_{i<j} B(u_i,u_j). Thus abelian coordinates are affine, central coordinates have Boolean ANF degree<=2 under x_i²=x_i. Preserve all primary IDs and chart witness maps. This formula holds separately in each order without commuting anything. A permutation changes the central phase by the sum of B(u_i,u_j)+B(u_j,u_i) over inverted pairs (derive via adjacent swap or subtract the two expansions). In contrast to L02-25 quotient, this keeps the phase rather than killing it.

Collect all terminal abelian equalities and original affine guards; solve by Gaussian elimination. If inconsistent refute. Otherwise x=x0+Lt with independent t in F2^d and exact one-to-one lift. Substitution preserves degree. Central requirements become equations q_j(t)=0. Row reduce their squarefree coefficient vectors, discard zero and detect constant1. If linear combinations produce new nonconstant affine equations, include them in the chart and substitute again, at most n independent dimension decreases; each step exact. ## Exact Fourier count and proof
For each lambda in F2^r evaluate the integer Walsh sum S(lambda)=sum_{t∈F2^d} (-1)^{sum_j lambda_j q_j(t)}. Pointwise identity 2^(-r) sum_lambda (-1)^{lambda·q(t)} equals 1 iff q(t)=0 and zero otherwise. Consequently exact count of common primary words C=2^(-r) sum_lambda S(lambda). Since lift is exact, C=0 iff UNSAT for admitted source. A nonzero count proves feasibility for exact adapter but no positive research track; if using a sound relaxation it means only UNKNOWN. No arithmetic reduction modulo a field for the outer sum: cancellations are signed integers.

Compute every quadratic Walsh sum deterministically with no oracle. If a cross term t_i t_j has coefficient1, write q=xy+xL(v)+yM(v)+R(v), where L,M affine and R quadratic in remaining bits. Direct summation over x,y gives 2(-1)^{L(v)M(v)} (sum over y kills x≠M). Thus S(q)=2 S(R+LM) on d-2 variables. Boolean reduction uses t_i²=t_i. Iterate until affine; sum is zero for nonconstant affine function and ±2^(remaining dimension) for constant. Each elimination takes O(d²) coefficient operations with dense representation; at most d/2 eliminations. Signed totals require O(d+r+1) bits. A fixed r, or r<=c0 log2 N for one fixed c0 and fixed-polynomial chart acquisition, gives deterministic polynomial bit time. More generally exact enumeration 2^r times polynomial; not universally polynomial. This is elementary quadratic character-sum computation, not a novel general quadratic counting result. Integration contribution: exact order-sensitive group-phase compiler + count on original shared domain; avoids both explicit tuple frontiers and destructive commutator quotient. ## Barrier / first open
An unrestricted number of these constant-size group charts already expresses arbitrary Boolean quadratic feasibility, with repeated affine labels. For each quadratic q=0, build one word of central products: A^x B^y A^x B^y gives C^(xy); C^x gives linear term, C gives constant. Concatenate and require identity. Every label depends affinely on one primary bit or constant; private states lift uniquely. An arbitrary 3SAT instance has polynomial-size Boolean circuit with AND and NOT gates. Introduce gate-output variables, impose z+xy=0 for AND, z+x+1=0 for NOT, output=1. Thus unbounded-many-chart group-word feasibility is NP-complete (membership by direct evaluation, polynomial native CNF adapter). This does NOT establish hardness for two read-once charts in this particular group; indeed fixed number with fixed c is polynomial under verified affine adapter. Nor does it prove that every NO instance needs high r after an arbitrary transformation. First OPEN: uniform acquisition of polynomially many exact regional charts with total remaining nonlinear constraint rank O(log N), or complementary refutation, for general native grid NO. Next lemma: interaction-rank factorization of the quadratic equation family, allowing independent local clusters of many equations without paying the product of all Fourier characters; seek source-realizable counterexample where all local clusters overlap and no affine elimination lowers rank. Do not infer unrestricted hardness.
