## Mathematical result
Let A be a nonempty explicitly given affine carrier over F2, dimension d in free coordinates, containing all solutions of the current guarded residual. Suppose k affine guards G_i inside A are independently certified impossible for that residual. Set S=A minus the union of G_i. This is a relaxation of the actual solution set, not an exact projection of it. Empty guards can be discarded; if S empty, the residual is refuted. Otherwise choose p in S. For each nonempty guard choose a defining equation a_i.x=b_i violated by p. Then H={x in A : a_i.x=a_i.p for every selected i} lies in S and contains p. Therefore codim_A aff(S)<=rank(a_i)<=k; indeed every normal to aff(S) lies in span of the selected a_i. This extends C07's private-point argument for irredundant full covers (#7228164030) to partial covers. No peer theorem is assumed: the preceding direct proof suffices. The bound is tight for k distinct coordinate hyperplanes whose complement fixes k coordinates.

For each affine query Q, exact |S intersect Q| is the signed sum over 2^k subsets J of |A intersect Q intersect all G_j, j in J|, evaluated by binary GE (zero if inconsistent, otherwise a power of 2). Use one initial count and d successive coordinate-prefix counts to find p, if one exists. This p is only a carrier survivor, never asserted a witness to the original CSP. Enumerate at most 2^k normals in the span of chosen a_i (deduplicate by GE); retain u iff count with u.x!=u.p is zero. Their equations define exactly aff(S), as H contained in S proves no omitted direction can be constant on S. Rank reduce output. Thus O((d+2^k)2^k poly(N)) bit work, including GE, coefficient and query representation, counting arithmetic (O(d+k) bits for intermediate sums), construction, verification, and all failed normals. Space polynomial for k<=c log N and d, guard encodings polynomial in original N. Can stream intersections; cumulative memory bounded by product of this bit-work and peak space. Does not require bounded individual guard codimension. For fixed five, at most 31 nonempty intersections plus the carrier per query. Rank credit decreases by the number of independent new equations, not by the number of excluded assignments; do not replenish credit by changing coordinates or allocating branches.

Independent proof tracks: constructive survivor-cell argument; dual annihilator containment from H subset S; adversarial exhaustive comparison of general affine guard families (seed 20261127, 810 tests, 51,124 carrier assignment checks, 49,958 candidate normal checks, 7,309 count calls, 108,645 intersections, zero discrepancies). 84 empty and 726 nonempty; 447 nonempty full-affine-hull survivors and 279 nontrivial affine restrictions. Includes inconsistent guards, duplicates, cover controls and tight codimension examples. The latter are mathematical controls, not additional SAT research instances. This does not bound guard discovery or imply that new rank credit always exists.
