## Named theorem: disjoint complete hub-bucket sewing
For any finite CNF F on Boolean carrier IDs, let B_p be ALL current clauses containing p or not p. Partition B_p into clauses p OR A_i and not p OR D_j. Tautologies can be dropped and identical clauses deduplicated. Let R_p = conjunction over every pair (i,j) of A_i OR D_j. If one polarity has no clauses, R_p is true. For every surviving assignment x, existential p B_p iff R_p. Constructive proof: if all A_i are true choose p=0; otherwise choose p=1, and every D_j must be true by a resolvent with a false A_i. Necessity follows by checking the two values of p. Subsumed resolvents can be dropped without changing the conjunction.
Now select a set P of pivots with pairwise disjoint clause buckets, equivalently no active clause contains two chosen pivots. Let F0 be all clauses outside the selected buckets. Conditional on any surviving word x, each B_p depends on x and only its own pivot p. Hence existential P F = F0 AND product over p in P of R_p, pointwise. All overlapping surviving hubs use the SAME x; no marginal Cartesian-product assumption. One may reconstruct all removed bits in reverse round order with the constructive rule; selected pivots within one round do not depend on each other. If a guard mentions a pivot, include it as a clause (or substitute it first); the gauge restriction is maintained throughout. This is safe guarded sewing, not a proof that all shared wheels have a usable elimination pivot.
Corollary: fixed cap on pair count (36 here), surviving bucket scope (8 here), width of replacement (5 here), and nonincreasing clause count imply a fixed polynomial acquisition, verification, storage and reconstruction bound for a deterministic maximal batch process. Initial prime clauses per B factor are obtained by checking at most 3^4 patterns against at most 2^4 legal words. Complete clauses suffice, prime minimization is an optional exact adapter. At most O(N) active clauses and carrier IDs; at most O(N) rounds, each candidate examination and conflict detection can be done by naive scans in O(N^2 polylog N) bit work with deterministic sorted structures; generous O(N^4) preprocessing+sewing bound using ordinary explicit storage and bit-index arithmetic, no assumptions of optimal hashing. At least one carrier retires on productive rounds; count of clauses never increases. Recording each complete bucket and reconstruction data costs polynomial total; verification may reconstruct all buckets independently by scanning every active clause. Peak storage polynomial, cumulative memory bounded by product of charged time and peak storage. Potential Phi=number of unretired carriers strictly falls during accepted batches, but no proof that Phi falls at every unresolved state; final branching must pay all children. For a given explicit proof tree of t nodes, replay polynomial in N+t, not in N alone without a bound on t. No free SAT/count/equivalence oracle. This is a specialization of known Davis-Putnam variable elimination and bounded variable elimination (BVE), not a novelty claim for resolution.

## Falsifiers and first open arrow
1. Omitted incident clause: (p OR h) AND (not p OR h) entails h; dropping one side makes projection incorrectly true at h=0. Checker requires all incident clauses.
2. Overlapping eliminated pivots: (p OR q) AND not p AND not q; independent marginal elimination of p or q without retaining the other resulting condition can incorrectly erase the contradiction. No selected pair may share a clause.
3. Shared surviving hub: two local nonempty supports {h=0} and {h=1} have empty join; local nonemptiness does not certify extension. Our resolvents retain the common h.
4. Fixed pair cutoff not complete: CNF containing every sign pattern on every triple of n>=5 variables is UNSAT with no units, yet each variable has 4*C(n-1,2) positive and negative occurrences, so every product exceeds 36; this is a refusal for the stated filter only. The formula has cheap local eight-clause refutations. Equality, affine, small-scope and compact circuit shortcuts remain valid; no perfect-matching control run or unrestricted transfer. First open arrow: on every unresolved native residual, acquire a safe complementary terminal or a productive batch/guard decomposition within a single fixed aggregate polynomial credit. In particular, no bound on the subsequent branching tree size. DISJ sanity: local independently satisfiable components factor only on disjoint private variables; shared hubs retained. Relativization/algebrization/Natural Proofs: no all-decider separation or claimed barrier evasion. Simultaneous DTISP and deterministic configurations: no machine-model lower bound, no inference from storage counts to time. No publication gate satisfied.
