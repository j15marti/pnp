## A. General multi-hub saturation
Let U=direct sum U_i, actual base sets S_i subset U_i (with stored lifts), independent hub groups H_j subset U, all explicit. For each target t in U, choosing hub vectors h_j requires remaining t_i+sum_j pi_i(h_j) in S_i for all i. The missing set D_i=U_i minus S_i, not affine relaxation. Define f_ij=max over r in U_i of |{h in H_j: r+pi_i(h) in D_i}|. For one-hole S_i and injective projection on H_j, f_ij<=1. Constant projection is not an influencing hub; absorb constant and ignore that edge. If an ordering of hubs assigns every base component to its last influencing hub and sum of f_ij over components assigned to j is strictly less than |H_j|, every target t admits an actual joint choice. Proof: choose hubs in order; at j all other influencing hubs of assigned components have already been chosen. Each assigned component forbids at most f_ij choices, their union does not exhaust H_j. Pick an allowed choice. Previously settled components have no still-unselected influencing hub. At end choose base local words from exact S_i. Thus entire U attained, and quotient/retire block with actual lift. The same argument works with arbitrary finite additive groups and explicitly attainable tables, not merely GF2. For supplied base/hub partition and explicit maps, verify direct sum via Gaussian, derive projections, compute f_ij when base spaces small/explicit or given certified upper bounds. Greedy peel a hub with sum of f over its current incident unassigned components <|H_j|, delete those components, reconstruct in reverse; monotone deletion ensures success if such a peeling order exists. If a component untouched and has holes, do not claim saturation. Does not assume global universal acquisition of a base decomposition. For q=3 and at most two injective last-owner obligations this extends earlier triple saturation; simple union bound/degeneracy is related to correspondence coloring, not a claimed new general coloring theorem.

## C. Parity at every step: exact affine look-ahead and limitations

Equivalently every row in annihilator W_suffix^perp has correct parity (alpha·z=alpha·b). At each step join actual choices, discard only states failing these checks. Retain whole compatible state, not its hash or parity value. If all prefix states excluded => NO. Full witnesses never pruned. At final suffix empty, z=b mandatory.
