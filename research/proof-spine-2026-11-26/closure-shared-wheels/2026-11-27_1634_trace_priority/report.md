## Proof
For a non-tautological deduplicated clause set F and complete Boolean word a extending the current guard, define U_v as violated rows containing v in either polarity, B_v as rows whose sole true literal belongs to v. Flipping v changes violated count by |B_v|-|U_v|. For an opposite-polarity pair with pivot v, its resolvent is false at a iff one parent is in U_v and the other in B_v. Indeed the nonpivot literals must all be false; precisely one pivot literal is true. Conversely both remaining literal sets are false in those two rows. The resolvent cannot be tautological. Thus enumerate exactly the false-at-a resolution candidates via U_v x B_v; deduplicate by pivot and parent identity, not by unverified semantic equivalence. A strict single-flip local minimum with at least one violated nonempty clause satisfies |U_v|<=|B_v| for every permitted free v. For any such v with U_v nonempty there are at least |U_v|^2 parent-pivot pairs, not necessarily distinct resolvents or newly learned clauses. At guards, use only free variables; if an entirely pinned clause is violated it is already a certified contradiction. On width-three inputs each such resolvent has width <=4. No claim for width two or for universal productive contractions.
For a clause of distinct variables, k=sum(s^2), signed sum t=sum(s), each incident flip gives delta k=+/-1 and delta t=sign(l)*delta k. The violation and satisfied signed-zero counters update exactly from the changed rows. A sweep of all candidate flips reads O(L) incidences rather than O(nL), where L is current literal count; a strict-descent walk has at most M accepted moves for fixed M rows. Snapshot/replay costs O(L) per accepted move; trace storage O(M^2) rows, candidate scans O(M L^2) with fixed-width arithmetic/indices and polynomial overhead. These are scoped bounds on the heuristic/one sweep and not on residual DPLL. Guard facts remain guarded. Each accepted resolvent and final tree checked independently by an alternate UP implementation. Candidate ranks depend on the number of trace states in which the resolvent is false, so the earlier feedback-independence observation no longer applies. Falsifiers: no width-two candidate; duplicates/subsumption; score descent with no proof; finite budget exhausted.
