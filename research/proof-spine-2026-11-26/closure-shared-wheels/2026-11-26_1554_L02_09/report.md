## A. Constructive boundary formula
After deleting pinned internal edges and shifting each vertex baseline b_v by the XOR of its fixed port values, retain only exceptional words compatible with the pins. Let C be a component in the graph on constraint vertices with free internal edges; its external ports P_C occur just once. Let B_C=XOR_{v in C} b_v (shifted charges). For each fixed boundary assignment y the internal graph is unchanged, all its exposed edges are now pins. Reconstruct L02-08: if a satisfying assignment has >=2 exception vertices, a shortest path between a nearest pair has baseline interior. Flip its free internal edges, toggling the parity at its endpoints and preserving it at internal vertices. Both endpoints enter full baseline parity, all other words remain accepted and guards stay fixed. Iterate until 0 or 1 exceptions. XOR of vertex equations shows the number of exceptions modulo two is B_C XOR XOR_{p in P_C} y_p. In particular, every y with XOR y=B_C has a baseline witness, by tree leaf elimination (non-tree edges set 0). Includes isolated vertices and empty boundaries.

For each actual compatible exceptional word w at v, define Q_{v,w}(y) as conjunction of:
1. y_p=w_p for exposed ports at v;
2. for each connected component D of C-v, XOR_{p exposed in D} y_p = XOR_{u in D} b_u XOR XOR_{e from v to D} w_e.
Parallel edges each counted once; scopes have no repeated row within one factor. Empty XOR is 0. Word w includes original fixed pins, so no missing conditional facts. The component signature is exactly
R_C(y) = [XOR_{p in P_C} y_p = B_C] OR OR_{v,w exceptional and compatible} Q_{v,w}(y).
For each candidate, own vertex word fixed and other vertices baseline; equations in every D are necessary by summing and sufficient by tree elimination with all fixed incident bits. Candidate w has opposite shifted parity automatically. Thus soundness and completeness, including actual witness maps, without acquiring any unknown satisfying assignment. Empty boundary components are independently tested; if one is infeasible the whole relation is empty. Unused boundary rows remain free. Conjoin component signatures, never enumerate their Cartesian product. This is a compact OR-of-affine-systems response, not necessarily affine, not a disjoint cover, and not exact counting. L02-08's decision algorithm is a special case with all boundary fixed.

## B. Algebraic route and precise representation obstruction
The incidence matrix of a connected undirected graph over GF2 has columns e_u+e_v, image all even vectors (spanning tree suffices). Hence the baseline condition is exactly the single displayed parity equation on the boundary; imposing an exceptional local word deletes v, leaving block-diagonal incidence systems, whose consistency is exactly Q. This independently reconstructs each affine signature. For q exposed ports in one component with q>=1, the baseline supplies 2^(q-1) jointly attainable words regardless of exceptions. This is a compact implicit representation, not compulsory enumeration.

**Fanout firewall.** No finite network of these admitted degree-two factors realizes E_q={0^q,1^q} on q>=3 distinct exposed ports, even with arbitrary internal unary pins, auxiliary vertices, nonplanar wiring or cyclic shared hubs. If the two constant words are attainable, every component is feasible; if the exposed ports lie in more than one component (including free unused ports), combine the appropriate component witnesses for a mixed word. Otherwise the unique exposed component contains its entire parity class, at least four words, impossible. Arbitrary terminal bit complementations merely change the target to two complementary words, same argument. EQ2 itself is admitted, so binary equality alone does not extend fanout. Independent switching proof from a witness: if an exposed connected component has an exception, choose a shortest path from any exposed port to the nearest exception with baseline interior and flip the port and internal edges; only that one exposed bit changes, endpoint enters baseline. If no exception, flip a path between two exposed ports, changing two boundary bits while preserving all internal parities. For all q>=3 in one component these are mixed words. This proof does not assume that a generator reveals a baseline witness. A single vertex incident to both ports permits the same two-bit switch.

**Quantitative mixed-gadget bound (symbolic only).** Permit h arbitrary nonadmitted factors of arity<=4, all other factors admitted; each internal variable still occurs at most twice and external once. Any exact E_q gadget for q>=3 satisfies q<=4h. Fix the complete local words of all h bad factors from a 0^q witness, deleting them and pinning all their incident row values. The remaining good components have independent boundary signatures. A good component with >=2 exposed ports supplies at least two words (even if no other words), contradicting exact E_q because other exposed bits can be held fixed if it is a proper subset; if it contains all q it supplies >=4 words. Hence each remaining good component touches at most one exposed port. A component containing one exposed port must be adjacent to a bad factor by an originally unpinned internal edge, or its two values from the original all-zero/all-one witnesses would be independently interchangeable, producing a mixed word. Distinct such components require distinct bad-factor incidences. Each exposed port at a bad factor consumes another distinct bad incidence. Free unused exposed ports impossible. Count q<=sum of bad arities<=4h. This bounds only the specified representation; it neither says these bad factors are expensive nor excludes alternative globally computed transformations. ## C. Adversarial hardness transition and controls
Occurrence-three generalization of L02-08 is NP-complete even when every exact group is a single signed clause of length 2 or 3. This is the classical Tovey bounded-occurrence reduction with extra scope hygiene: for each occurrence of original variable x create a fresh row x_i, pad to at least 3 copies for variables with one/two occurrences; add directed cycle of binary clauses (not x_i OR x_{i+1}), cyclic indices. Every copy participates in at most one original ternary clause and exactly two binary implications. All scopes distinct (cycle length >=3 and fresh original occurrence IDs). Directed cycle forces all copies equal (a 1 forces every successor 1, or all are 0). Every original witness lifts by copying, every new witness restricts to the original. Each signed clause omits exactly one word, hence contains the entire opposite parity class. The arity is <=3, no row pins, initial unit closure unchanged, full single-factor unary support and affine hull over any field (cube minus one word of dimension >=2 contains an affine basis, by translating/complementing and choosing a word at distance >=2 from forbidden word). Encode as native exact-four grid above; k=O(m+n) for m clauses and n nonempty variables, original encoded input L, k=O(L), output O(L^2) bits with explicit witness map. Membership in NP by verifying physical labels. This is a known bounded-occurrence hardness theorem instantiated with explicit native grid and exact group promise, not a new general complexity separation. Universal polynomial algorithm for this occurrence-three subclass would imply P=NP. A local equality replica gadget preserving the degree-two admitted language is impossible unconditionally by the firewall. A global decision-preserving polynomial adapter would imply P=NP, not ruled out here.

Hostile scalable controls: q blocks on variable x and private pairs (a_j,b_j). Block 0 contains all eight forbidden-word clauses; each other block contains seven, allowing only 000. Split actual occurrences and add cycles, as above. NO due block0; replace one literal cell in the column forbidding 000 of block0 by constant-1 table, one-table neighbor, same dimensions, all-zero copied solution. Both labels have no unit consequence initially. Raw primal incidence cycles are not proof of hardness; source-acquired equality cycle quotient and small-component DPLL solve these easily. This is not post-full-portfolio hardness. No generic matching reduction or proof-complexity transfer.
