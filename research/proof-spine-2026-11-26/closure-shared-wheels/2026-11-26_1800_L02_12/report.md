## Hard fixed points after both passes

## Complete global equality acquisition is coNP-hard even on satisfiable inputs
Take any 3-CNF Phi, fresh a,b,g, constrain g=a xor b, and for every clause C add (NOT g OR C), arity4. Group scopes if necessary; compile to native masks as above. Always satisfiable: a=b, g=0, arbitrary Phi variables. Both values of a and b separately attained (00 and11). If Phi SAT then choose g=1 and either differing pair and lift using a Phi witness. If Phi UNSAT, every solution has g=0 and a=b. Hence testing whether a=b in EVERY satisfying assignment is coNP-complete (promise reduction to always-satisfiable native instances; complement has certificate original satisfying assignment with a!=b). This does not assert unconditional superpolynomial time.
