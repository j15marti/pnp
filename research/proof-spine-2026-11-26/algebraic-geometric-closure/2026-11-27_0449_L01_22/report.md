## B: exact intersection-frontier theorem
Let f_1,...,f_q be explicitly acquired sparse Boolean-valued character dictionaries on V*=GF2^d, coefficients in GF(p) for fixed odd prime p (GF3 in audit). Each f_i depends only on the linear functional z restricted to U_i=span of actual nonzero frequency keys (including zero harmlessly). A finite-dimensional binary subspace carries all possible restrictions of ambient z, since any functional extends. For cut i define L_i=sum_{j<=i} U_j, R_i=sum_{j>i} U_j, W_i=L_i intersect R_i, b_i=dim W_i. W_0=W_q=0. Store exactly the attainable boundary keys K_i in W_i^*: those restrictions of some ambient z satisfying f_1=...=f_i=1. A key is an evaluation on a computed basis, not a list of separate marginal observations. All spaces and intersections acquired by Gaussian elimination.

Initialize K_0={empty}. Given a in K_(i-1) and candidate b in W_i^*, test whether some ambient z has both restrictions a,b and f_i(z)=1. First Gaussian-eliminate the two sets of restriction equations on ambient z. Inconsistent pair => absent transition. Otherwise express each frequency key u modulo the span of restricted rows, multiplying its coefficient by the known sign of the eliminated row combination; collect identical quotient keys in GF(p). This is the exact restricted character dictionary on the remaining free parameters (the nonpivot coordinates span the quotient). Independence of characters follows from orthogonality and invertibility of 2 in GF(p). Thus restricted dictionary nonempty iff some z has f_i=1. This checks every coefficient, not modular counts. No need to enumerate the full local rank of f_i.

If transition succeeds, retain b in K_i. Necessity: a true prefix witness passes the test. Sufficiency (gluing): a previous witness defines a functional lambda on L_(i-1); the transition witness defines mu on U_i+R_i. These two subspaces intersect exactly W_(i-1). They agree there by a. Hence they define a unique joint linear functional on their sum, by lambda(l)+mu(r), well-defined because intersection agreement, and it extends to ambient V*. It satisfies all prior factors and f_i. Its restriction to W_i is b because W_i is contained in R_i. Thus no global correlations are lost. If K_i empty, entire conjunction absent, sound NO; if K_q nonempty, joint compatibility exists, and on admitted exact source a tour.
