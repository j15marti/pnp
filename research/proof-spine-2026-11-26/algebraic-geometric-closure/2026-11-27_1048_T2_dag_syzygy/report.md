## Named lemma: POINTWISE-DAG-FIRST-FAILURE
For every finite Boolean constraint system with base failure indicators f_b in R=F2[x_1,...,x_n]/(x_i^2+x_i), suppose an explicitly encoded acyclic derivation supplies indicators h_i, in topological order, with final h_t=1 and local verified identities
    h_i = sum_b q_ib f_b + sum_{j<i} q_ij h_j.
Each coefficient q is an acquired Boolean-ring circuit, or an expression constructed from a verified bounded local truth table or hinted unit trail. Then a flat ideal certificate exists:
    P_i = product_{j<i}(1+h_j),
    1 = sum_b f_b (sum_i P_i q_ib).
Proof: h_j is idempotent, so P_i h_j=0 for j<i. Multiplication of each local identity by P_i removes its derived predecessors. The first-failure indicators P_i h_i are mutually disjoint, and sum_i P_i h_i = 1 + product_{i<=t}(1+h_i) =1. Summing gives the stated certificate without recursively substituting predecessor coefficients. The identity is pointwise on the Boolean cube, not a formal identity over an ordinary polynomial ring unless Boolean equations are included and their lift charged.

Local constructions: for a consequence whose failure h implies at least one parent failure a_j on every Boolean assignment, h = sum_j h a_j product_{k<j}(1+a_k). In particular, an exact existential bucket projection failure implies parent failure at the actual eliminated bit, including for either bit. A locally entailed forbidden-word clause likewise has a pointwise parent implication. For reverse unit propagation, target failure h is the negated-clause guard. With chronological propagated true literals L_j, reason indicators a_j, and conflict indicator a_*, the local identity is
    h = sum_j h (product_{k<j} L_k) a_j + h (product_{k<=u} L_k) a_*.
Under the guard and previous true literals, the reason is false exactly when L_j=0. Under all units the final conflict is false. Telescope. Invalid hint, guard or missing parent is rejected. A learned clause may be a parent only after independent checking. Projection and literal substitution preserve original IDs, or carry a checked reconstruction map. Local implications for table transformations refer to original coordinates, not free independent assignments of eliminated variables.

Gauge: the augmented proof uses base failure x_g for gauge x_g=0. Multiply the resulting identity by 1+x_g to kill that term. Complement all original bits to obtain the complementary branch, and add. Native B/E/XOR constraints are complement invariant, giving an unconditional certificate. For direct port-level lift, signed BFS/DSU paths express each port as its representative bit plus phase. On the parity paths the discrepancy from that expression is the XOR sum of edge and E equality violations. Substituting into the at-most-four-bit balance failure polynomial telescopes one argument at a time, with those discrepancies as factors. This gives a polynomial-size local lift, not an executed materialization of an expanded port circuit.

Cost theorem: let S be total actual encoded size of the acquired proof, local representations, and coefficient circuits. For explicitly encoded table width w<=c log N, table enumeration and prime-implicate acquisition cost poly(N) 2^{O(w)} (including 3^w partial words). A RUP step with supplied chronological hints is checked in polynomial time in encoded trail and reason lengths. Fixed binary-fanin balanced products for all P_i (even computing separately O(t^2) gates), local literal products and final sums give polynomial(N+S) circuit size and O(log(N+S)) evaluation depth **when local indicators and coefficients are built from explicit tables/clauses/hinted products of polynomial length**. Arbitrary supplied circuits of unbounded depth are not covered by this depth claim. Coefficients one bit, no field extensions, no uncharged multilinearization or expanded monomial representation. Width/peak gate count polynomial(N+S). A compilation-stage potential is the number of remaining operations in a precomputed bounded balanced product/sum construction and structural verification schedule; it decreases by one per charged operation. It is polynomial in original N only if S already has a uniform fixed polynomial bound in N. Failed discovery, all branches, and acquisition of S are outside that compilation credit and must be charged separately. Exact finite precision; uniform finite algorithms, random-access indices charged log(address range). No attention oracle used. Evaluation depth is not discovery depth.

Falsifier to automatic extension to RAT/DRAT: start with empty CNF and add unit x by vacuous RAT (no negative pivot occurrence). This preserves satisfiability but excludes x=0, and 1+x is nonzero in the Boolean ring, not in the zero ideal. Thus the pointwise local implication hypothesis is essential. A satisfiability-preserving rule is not automatically an implication. A verified existential witness adapter or separate extension-variable proof is needed. Reference: Cruz-Filipe et al., Efficient Certified RAT Verification, arXiv:1612.02353v2, 2016, section 2 (RUP/AT and RAT). This is a refusal of a specific transfer, not a lower bound on decision.
