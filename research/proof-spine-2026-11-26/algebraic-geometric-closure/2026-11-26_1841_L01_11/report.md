## A: constructive matching support and guarded enumeration
Represent directed cycle covers as perfect matchings between original source rows and destination columns. Find a matching sigma by augmenting paths; absence proves NO. Relabel destination j by sigma^-1(j) in an auxiliary exchange digraph only: i->k if original arc (i,sigma(k)) is present and k!=i. Every cover is sigma composed with a permutation, whose nontrivial cycles are vertex-disjoint directed cycles of this digraph. Conversely any collection of vertex-disjoint exchange cycles gives a cover. An original nonmatching arc is supported iff its exchange arc lies in a directed cycle, equivalently both endpoints share an SCC. Remove unsupported arcs, recompute forced singleton rows/columns and original reachability; all removed arcs are impossible in every HC. A mandatory cover arc is a singleton after this closure. This is exact cover support, not exact HC support. If resulting original graph is not strongly connected, NO. Sign(sigma) and all cover signs refer to original vertex labels.

## B: global determinant parity fiber
KNOWN: Robertson-Seymour-Thomas (1999) gives polynomial recognition and construction of a Pólya signing of a 0-1 matrix, if one exists (Section 1, 1.1, Section 9). Let A be the availability matrix and S its entrywise signing by +/-1, with all nonzero determinant terms of S having the same sign. Then C=|det S| is the number of covers over the integers, and D=det A is their signed sum. The exact number of covers of the only possible HC sign h=(-1)^(n-1) is
 B=(C+hD)/2.
Both determinants are exact integer calculations; this identity is not evaluation in characteristic two and division by 2 is exact integer division. A signing persists under every deletion guard, so no repeat recognition needed. For an arbitrary supplied signing, verify via the known even-directed-simple-cycle algorithm: relative to a reference matching sigma, give exchange arc i->k weight 1+delta mod 2, where delta is 1 iff S[i,sigma(k)] and S[i,sigma(i)] have opposite signs. The ratio of determinant monomials after flipping a simple exchange cycle is (-1)^(sum weights-1). Thus uniform sign iff no even-weight directed simple cycle. Subdivide weight-0 arcs once using private vertices and retain weight-1 arcs to reduce to even-length directed cycle detection. Do not confuse simple cycles with closed walks. If no matching, immediate NO. Alternatively use RST recognition/construction directly. General algorithm not executed here; explicit family signing proved below.

## C: adversarial native family and independent reconstruction
Take r=6t, n=3r, IDs 0,...,n-1, sigma(i)=i+2 mod n. Partition rows into consecutive triples; pi maps a->a+2, a+1->a, a+2->a+1 for anchor a=3j. Add availability arcs (i,sigma(i)), (i,sigma(pi(i))) and for anchor a also (a,sigma(a+3 mod n)). No loops: displacements +2,+1,+4,+5 mod n (n>=18). Original graph strongly connected: +2 arcs form two parity classes, and +1 arcs from both classes connect them. Every arc is in some cover, and avoidable; no unary cover support deletion. Exchange graph is precisely disjoint directed triangles, with an additional directed ring through their anchors. Any directed simple cycle either is one local triangle or the entire anchor ring: a path entering a triangle off its anchor must return to that anchor before it can use any ring edge. Hence all covers are either any subset of independent triangle flips (2^r) or the single full ring flip (1). Each triangle flip is even as a permutation; sigma has two cycles and positive sign (n even); the r-cycle ring flip is odd. All 2^r positive covers have wrong HC sign. Give one anchor ring arc (0,5) a minus sign and every other arc plus. The negative cover contains this arc; no other cover does. Thus every signed determinant term is positive and exact count is 2^r+1. B=1. The ring cover follows residue classes mod 3 in sequence 0,2,1,0; after three steps advances 9 mod n, so number of cycles is gcd(r,3)=3. It is not HC. This proves NO for every r=6t without enumeration; r=6 and 12 independently enumerated using ordinary row matching DFS under explicit finite node cap. This is a post-matching-support NO, not a proved hard instance for arbitrary algorithms. Correct sign is necessary, not sufficient. A one-cycle input is the minimal falsifier of an unsound NO test.

## Integrated second hybrid: exact contraction of the hostile fiber
Replace every base vertex by four labeled vertices a,b,c,d with internal arcs a->b,a->c,b->c,c->b,b->d,c->d. External arcs enter a and leave d only; no additional internal arcs. The cycle-cover choices inside each block must be one of a-b-c-d or a-c-b-d: if b,c form a 2-cycle there is no available outgoing arc for a. Each external original arc d_i->a_j is preserved exactly. Every cover projects to a base cover with the same cycle count, and each base cover lifts in exactly 2^n ways. The expanded graph retains strong connectivity and all arcs supported/avoidable. For n=18, B=2^18, so count-cap 1 fails; both signs remain. A valid NO proof on the quotient lifts to the expanded source by the two-way cover map. Acquire the disjoint four-vertex blocks by scanning exact local degree and incidence patterns, verify no cross-boundary arcs other than d->a, record original IDs, contract and reclose. This changes the determinant's domain, rather than demanding a bigger counting cap. Signed exterior edges retain their labels; a new signing may instead be reacquired on the quotient. Both internal traversals have the same sign contribution; for a general number of blocks the HC target sign changes by the same subdivision parity factor. No need to assume numeric equality of signed counts before/after contraction. Greedy recognition may fail on unrelated graphs, in which case return UNKNOWN, not NO. For this explicit expanded family the recognition is successful and source-verifiable. Independent hostile test demonstrates uncontracted cap failure, not a hardness result.
