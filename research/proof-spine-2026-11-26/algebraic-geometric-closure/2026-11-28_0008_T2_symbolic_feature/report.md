## Scoped theorem and obstruction to explicit cover acquisition
If an exact regional boundary relation is the union of K certified affine cubes x=a+Bt, then imposing h(x)=y on each cube is equivalent to coefficient equality after substitution in the Boolean ring. For fixed d and polynomial ambient dimension b, each affine substitution produces at most sum_{j<=d} binom(b,j) terms per degree-d monomial after square-free reduction. A dense polynomial algorithm constructs the coefficient equations and solves them with GF2 elimination, polynomial in b and K for fixed d, including row bits and verification of the algebra given the exact cover. Acquisition and exact cover certification are separate obligations.

A general representation firewall: take m independent 3-bit AND graph factors (x_i,y_i,z_i) with z_i=x_i*y_i. The four local words 000,010,100,111 are affinely independent in GF2^3; no affine subspace of dimension 2 is contained in the local relation. If an affine subspace A is contained in the product relation, each block projection has dimension <=1, so dim A<=m by injectivity of the product projection. Each affine piece contains <=2^m words, while the relation contains 4^m. Thus every exact explicit union of affine subspaces contained in this relation requires >=2^m pieces, even though input CNF has 3m constant-width clauses. This refutes a universal polynomial-size explicit affine cover guarantee in this representation; NOT a decision lower bound. No new instance IDs asserted for this symbolic family.

A factorized escape is exact: the local evaluation space has independent basis 1,x,y,z, each degree <=1; every local monomial reduces to one of those (xy=xz=yz=xyz=z). Product tensors of these basis elements are independent on the full product, and a degree<=d input monomial reduces to a tensor with <=d nonconstant factors. Conversely every such tensor is realized at degree<=d. Hence exact feature rank is sum_{j<=min(d,m)} binom(m,j) 3^j, polynomial at fixed d. This offers an explicit polynomial filtered tensor representation even when every exact affine cover is exponential. For overlapping factors, the same conclusion does not follow because local choices may be incompatible. Next falsifiable lemma: efficiently acquire and certify factorized filtered bases on joined regions with polynomial joint separator responses, rather than require polynomial explicit covers.
