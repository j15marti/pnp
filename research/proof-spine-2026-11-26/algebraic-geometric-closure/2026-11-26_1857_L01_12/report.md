## Track A: native matching and small closed subsets
Let C be the exact set of cycle covers in the target HC permutation sign h=(-1)^(n-1). A proper nonempty vertex set S is closed in cover p iff p(S)=S. Delete every arc from S to outside S; bijectivity implies no arc enters S from outside in any retained cover. Thus the corresponding event E_S is the exact set of covers in C with closed S. A tour can never lie in E_S. An intersection of events is represented by the union of their deletion sets; no overlapping witness count is guessed. Fixed-size subsets up to k can be acquired in O(n^k) for fixed k (k=3 in the executed audit), retaining only positive events if desired. Each subset is checked against source IDs; an absence of one or all events is not a global SAT conclusion. Matching closure may be applied to any deletion guard without changing cycle covers.

## Track B: exact signed determinants and Gram certificate
KNOWN (Robertson, Seymour, Thomas, 1999, Annals 150:929-975, Section 1 lines 48-50 and 1.1, Section 9): polynomial recognition and construction of a Pólya matrix for a 0-1 matrix when one exists. For a verified signing S of A, every nonzero signed determinant monomial has the same sign. Therefore C_total=|det S|, D=det A, B=(C_total+hD)/2 is exact integer cardinality of the target parity fiber. This persists under any edge deletion, with the original n and h unchanged. Unverified/nonexistent signing => UNKNOWN, not NO. Every event intersection count follows by deleting corresponding arcs and repeating this pair of integer determinants.

For events E_i, let M1=sum |E_i| and M2=sum_{i<j}|E_i cap E_j|. Pointwise for activity a=number of events containing a cover, a-binomial(a,2)<=1_{a>0}. Summation gives M1-M2<=|union E_i|<=B. Equality M1-M2=B proves NO. Higher fixed even truncation is also sound, via finite binomial identity, at q^O(d) charged intersections. No additive count inference without overlap correction.

Stronger linear certificate: vectors f_i=1_{E_i} in Q^C, constant v=1. Form integer Gram G_ij=<f_i,f_j>=|E_i cap E_j|, b_i=<v,f_i>, and augmented Gram H=[B,b^T;b,G]. Over Q (equivalently R for rank), Gram rank equals vector-span dimension by positivity: a rational linear combination has norm squared zero iff it is zero. Thus rank H=rank G iff v belongs to span{f_i}. If an uncovered cover exists, all its f_i entries zero while v entry 1, impossible. Return NO on this equality (or B=0); otherwise UNKNOWN unless a complementary exact certificate succeeds. May export rational coefficients alpha satisfying G alpha=b and b^T alpha=B, giving sum over C of (1-sum alpha_i f_i)^2=0. No arithmetic-field reduction: over finite characteristic the Gram implication would not generally hold. Necessity not claimed for coverage with fixed-degree features. Generalize features to products of up to fixed d distinct event indicators, which still vanish on uncovered covers. Their Gram entries are exact intersections of <=2d original events, so O(q^(2d)) determinant pairs and polynomial exact rational rank suffice for fixed d. For d=1 use q(q-1)/2 plus q and base count. A degree-q inclusion-exclusion expansion is NOT free. A polynomial number of features can certify a union even if B exponential. Exact count over the retained set is a scoped Pfaffian property, not an arbitrary HC counting oracle.

Each cover must traverse a-b-c-d or a-c-b-d: if b,c formed a separate 2-cycle, a would have no allowed destination. Contracting this region gives exactly a base cover and preserves cycle count; each quotient cover has exactly two lifts. Repeat k times nested at entry 0, n=9+3k. Recognition scans exact internal/outer incidence, including all six internal arcs, no other internal arcs, all internal signs + for this audit; record union of original IDs. The quotient preserves HC in both directions, also target odd-cycle-count parity. Each expansion multiplies all target and total cover counts by 2 and leaves sign uniform up to a common factor (-1)^3. For arbitrary graphs, reacquire/check signing on quotient and stop if unadmitted; do not assume a greedy pattern is universally available. Any quotient closed subset lifts to the union of its original fibers: a cover projects to a closed subset iff that lifted subset is closed. Thus every event intersection count and Gram matrix entry is multiplied by 2^k in this family. Complete original cover support and target support persist; since the base has all arcs target-supported and strong connectivity and both internal choices possible, target-support union remains strong and no universal cut appears.
