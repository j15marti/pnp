## A: construct a star interlace representation
Start with an arbitrary 3-uniform exact-cover instance: universe m=3q labeled elements, list T_j of distinct-element triples, t triples. Karp 1972 problem 17 perfect 3-dimensional matching is a restriction (use three disjoint labeled copies of its ground set and retain its allowed tuples); hence X3C is NP-complete. Handle empty universe separately. For each element e create a chord c_e and one occurrence chord l_(e,j) for each T_j containing e. Use consecutive nonoverlapping circular segments: c_e, l_1,...,l_r,c_e,l_r,...,l_1. Chords within a segment have exactly the star interlace edges center-leaf; no other intersections, including between segments. There are s=2(m+3t) chord endpoints, labeled by their circular positions; C is the increasing cyclic permutation. Let p=C composed with all center transpositions. Each triple T_j has its three occurrence transpositions tau_(e,j); their product k_j consists of three disjoint transpositions, and supports of different k_j are disjoint. Actual chart P(x)=p composed with product_j k_j^x_j for x in {0,1}^t. Centers are fixed selected, not free. All chart words attained in the native adapter below.

## B: exact nullity and occupancy

Independent derivation: invariant bit vector y on permutation cycles changes along C by z_i=y_a+y_b at each selected chord endpoint and zero otherwise. Summing along arc from a_i to b_i gives z_i plus the crossing z's. Consistency equivalent to A[U]z=0; arbitrary starting bit adds one dimension. No generic symbolic Tutte substitution. For each element, d_e=sum_{j:e in T_j}x_j is an integer. The selected center and d_e leaves give K_(1,d_e). If d_e=0 rank0, nullity1; if d_e>=1 rank2, nullity d_e-1 (over GF2, indeed over any field for this symmetric block). Direct sum gives
cycles(P(x))=1+sum_e |d_e-1|.
Consequently one cycle iff every element occurs exactly once; this is an exact identity, not merely global sign or a count of matchings. Uniform polynomial reduction from X3C to the problem of achieving a single cycle by independent triple-transposition groups, even on star interlace graphs. Membership NP by verifying phase word and permutation. This proves NP-completeness of explicit triple-switch chart language. For each nonfixed i add arcs i->u_i,u_i->v_i,v_i->p(i), and g(i)->v_i,v_i->u_i,u_i->p(h(i)). Only these arcs. The exact local paths yield options (i,p(i)) and (g(i),p(h(i))), no other endpoints.
