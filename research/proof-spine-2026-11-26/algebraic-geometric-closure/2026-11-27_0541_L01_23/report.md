## A: constructive output-sensitive acquisition
Given explicit sparse Boolean-valued character dictionaries f_i over a fixed odd finite field (GF3 executed), z in GF2^d, U_i span of nonzero frequency keys, W_i=(sum_{j<=i}U_j) intersect (sum_{j>i}U_j), K_i exact attained prefix keys in W_i^*. Do not scan all 2^dim W_i keys. For each a in K_(i-1), impose its linear equations on z. Traverse a binary prefix tree of evaluations on a computed basis of W_i. At each node test consistency of old key plus partial new key by Gaussian elimination; if consistent, restrict f_i dictionary by reducing frequency keys modulo the equation span and multiplying by known signs, combining identical quotient keys. A nonzero dictionary iff some extension has f_i=1: character orthogonality with invertible 2^d in odd characteristic. Discard impossible prefixes; extend both bits only at nonzero nodes. At a leaf retain exact new key; union by deterministic deduplication. No free oracle. This is identical local semantic transition to L01-22. Gluing proof: prior witness functional on L_(i-1) and transition witness on U_i+R_i agree on W_(i-1), hence extend jointly, and W_i lies in R_i; necessity immediate. Every successful prefix has a successful leaf. For L leaves, at most 1+2 b_i L checks including failed siblings, where b_i=dim W_i. For no leaves, root only. Hence total queries <= sum_i |K_(i-1)|(1+2 b_i |K_i|), with all duplicate leaves counted by this upper bound. Polynomial bit time if acquired dictionaries and actual K_i sizes are bounded by one fixed polynomial in original N, regardless of b_i. Cap intermediate work and all states; failure returns UNKNOWN. This is a conditional output-sensitive refuter, not a universal bound on K_i.

## B: global tree alternative and exact join
For a supplied binary tree, W_v=U_v intersect U_out. At leaves acquire attained keys with the same prefix oracle. At children l,r, W_l intersect W_r = U_l intersect U_r: each member of U_l intersect U_r occurs outside both children, and the reverse inclusion is immediate. A pair of attained child keys glues iff it agrees on this intersection. Also W_v subset W_l+W_r: any u in W_v decomposes u=l+r with l in U_l,r in U_r; l=u+r is in W_l and similarly r in W_r. Thus parent key determined uniquely by a compatible pair. Compute and hash/sort exact intersection keys, join only compatible pairs, deduplicate parent keys. Cost polynomial per acquired leaf-prefix test plus per actual compatible pair (bounded by |K_l||K_r|); all failed sorting and verification paid. No scan of all parent keys required. This proof is symbolic; the executed tree lane exploits the direct-sum structure of copies, using local full 8-point tables and zero-width joins; not general driver implementation.
