## A. Conditional mincut from exact countable fiber
Let C be exact remaining target-sign cycle covers (odd number of cycles), after excluding already acquired closed-proper-subset events E_i. Every original HC remains in C. B=|C|. For a guard g of at most fixed h compatible arcs, C_g=C intersect g; B_g=|C_g|. Set capacities w^g_uv=|{p in C_g:p(u)=v}|, exact integer counts by the L01-13 signed determinant/verified bounded-overlap IE method when the original matrix has a certified Polya signing and selected events have overlap <= fixed d. Sign uniformity persists under deletions/pins. With no signing or overlap proof, this count lane returns UNKNOWN. Every row and column sums to B_g, so every cut has equal in and out capacity. Compute minimum cut over nonempty proper sets by root-to-each-other-vertex maxflow and taking a complement when necessary. Edmonds-Karp uses polynomial augmentations independent of capacity magnitude. Only rooted cuts excluding fixed root are acquired, and they exclude tours.

For a cut S, let X_S(p)=number of successor arcs from S to its complement. For every cover, X_S is a nonnegative integer and X_S=0 iff p(S)=S. Therefore
 |E_S intersect C_g| >= B_g - sum_(u in S,v not in S) w^g_uv.
No estimate of sign cancellation, no independence assumption. This lower bound is valid also on a restricted guard; E_S itself is an unconditional necessary exclusion (no Hamiltonian tour closes any proper S), so its *unconditional* removal from C is sound, but the measured bound is only the mass known within g. In particular a guard of mass p with conditional expected crossing <=1-delta removes at least p*delta of C. Empty guard recovers the simple marginal mincut heuristic. Use exact residual count with E_S appended and verify all new (d+1)-wise intersections empty in the original target fiber; otherwise decline the update and return UNKNOWN or use the support-only lane. Negative guard pins are never exported as independent positive deductions.

Progress theorem (PROVED WITH SCOPE): Fix integers a>=1,h>=0,d>=1 in advance. At each nonempty residual, scan all guards of at most h arcs (including empty) and exact mincuts of their counted capacities. If some guard has B_g-mincut >= B/N^a, acquire that cut, provided overlap remains <=d; a cap or gap failure is UNKNOWN. Each successful iteration decreases B by at least B/N^a, hence after at most Q=ceil(N^a n^2)+1 successful rounds, B<1 since B_initial<=n! and ln(n!)<n^2. The rule gives deterministic polynomial total work on the class of NOs where a qualifying guard exists throughout this *actual* selected trajectory and overlap condition persists. It is not a proof every NO admits such a guard. At most O(Q (m+1)^(h+1)(Q+1)^d + (Q+1)^(d+1)) determinant-count pairs (m original available arcs), including failed scans, closure, event intersection counts and incremental overlap checks. All graph construction, guards, maxflows, arithmetic bits (n! and polynomially many IE terms), and memory charged. No hidden enumeration of full cut space or use of true HC search.

## C. Stronger connecting lemma: sign-free exact support

Reconstruction (PROVED WITH SCOPE): For any directed availability matrix A and requested permutation sign h in {+1,-1}, find any bipartite perfect matching sigma, treating rows as tails and columns as heads; no matching => false. If sign(sigma)=h, true. Otherwise form exchange digraph D on row IDs, arc i->j iff i!=j and A[i,sigma(j)]=1. Any allowed permutation tau has tau(i)=sigma(rho(i)) for a permutation rho. Its nontrivial cycles are vertex-disjoint simple directed cycles of D, and sign(tau)/sign(sigma)=sign(rho)=product over its cycles of (-1)^(length-1). If signs differ, rho contains an odd number of even-length cycles, hence at least one. Conversely flipping sigma along any even-length simple directed cycle changes its sign and preserves a perfect matching. So requested sign is attained iff D contains an even directed simple cycle. Use polynomial RST decision, or acquire a cycle by repeated edge deletion with the same paid decision if a witness is required. No counting oracle, no signed determinant cancellation, no finite-field PIT or Pfaffian signing required. The same algorithm applies to every deletion/pin residual, with original n and HC sign (-1)^(n-1) unchanged. This is an elementary reduction to known algorithms, not a priority claim.

## E. Hostile infinite fixed point for the sign-free guard rule
The sign-filtering improvement is not universal. Reconstruct L04 separator family as an explicit ordinary graph: take bidirected clique U of s>=8 vertices, plus s+1 pairwise disjoint bidirected lobes L_i of b>=8 vertices, with all arcs in both directions between U and every lobe and no arcs between lobes. Removing U from a single Hamiltonian cycle leaves at most s paths, each contained in a single lobe. There are s+1 lobes, impossible. This proves NO for all s,b, not a finite guess.

Any compatible set of at most two pinned arcs extends to a cycle cover of EITHER permutation sign. Its components are one/two directed paths or a fixed 2-cycle; close each path by inserting one fresh U vertex, available since at most four endpoints and two added vertices. Cover remaining U vertices by one cycle (>=2 remaining) and each remaining lobe by one cycle (>=4 remaining). Toggle parity by splitting one remaining lobe into two cycles of length>=2. Thus every arc is target-supported, and after any single pin e=(u,v), every compatible second arc is target-supported. The support graph after pin is just the source with competing row/column arcs removed, strongly connected: take z in U outside {u,v}; all other vertices connect to/from z, and u->v->z and z->u->v connect the pinned endpoints. Consequently no ordinary or sign-filtered one-pin support deletion can occur. In fact these graphs are nonsignable: pick six distinct vertices within one lobe, split into three row vertices R and three column vertices C, choose each of 3! bijections R->C with one fixed C->R bijection; cover other vertices independently (remaining lobe >=2). Across these six covers every arc appears an even number of times, yet three relative permutations are odd, giving the same mod2 inconsistent signing equations. All details are symbolic, no large-instance exhaustive enumeration executed. A degree-based separator recognizer can identify universal U and count components of G-U in polynomial time, proving NO on this family. Thus this is a fixed point of one-pin support, not an unrestricted all-decider or whole-portfolio lower bound. It also illustrates why universal coverage needs complementary regional demand certificates.
