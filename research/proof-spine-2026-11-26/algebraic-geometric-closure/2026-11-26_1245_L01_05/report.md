## A. Constructive acquisition using actual verifier discrepancy (conditional)
Fix F=F_p with a fixed prime p (also Q with exact rational arithmetic, see ledger). Assume D; since ordinary directed HC is NP-complete, every explicitly polynomial-time-verifiable existence predicate below can be decided using a uniform polynomial reduction and D. To recover a witness for such predicate use bit-prefix existence queries (O(v log v) bits for a suffix). This is not an assumption of a free rank, PIT, counting or equivalence oracle. Before acquisition, test root nonemptiness and apply exact unary support/mandatory-edge closure with guarded existence queries. An empty relation gives zero circuit. Retain forced edge factors and original labels. Repeat closure for actual prefix representatives, after deductions and at stagnation; conditional facts attach to the actual prefix, not to arbitrary linear combinations. Root deductions are unconditional relative to the input guard. This optional extra processing is charged below and does not require discovering any satisfying assignment in advance.

Let B_i be a list of actual prefixes whose residual rows form a basis, C_i a list of suffix pivots of equal length, and A_i=M_i[B_i,C_i] nonsingular. Root nonempty gives B_0=[empty], choose one feasible suffix via paid queries. Given B_(i-1), the at most v*r_(i-1) children bj (b in B_(i-1), j in [v]) span M_i: restriction of a row to suffix words starting with j is a linear operation, so restriction preserves every previous row relation. For each candidate a, solve c A_i=M_i[a,C_i]. The proposed coordinates are unique (zero vector when basis empty). Ask whether there exists suffix z with
 V(az) != sum_b c_b V(bz) in F.
The verifier evaluates all displayed terms on explicit words and checks exact arithmetic. Its size polynomial in N,r, and the coefficient bit lengths. If no, the row belongs to span. If yes, recover a specific z by prefix search, append a and z, and recompute the inverse. Schur complement is exactly the nonzero discrepancy, so the enlarged square submatrix is nonsingular. Retaining only independent candidate rows loses no span. Thus induction acquires exactly r_i independent rows, not all literal residuals. This is a paid implicit coefficient-matrix rank construction, not a novel claim about the standard rank characterization of ordered ABPs.

At each layer define T_i(j)[b,b'] as coordinates of residual bj in the B_i basis. End nonempty means rank at final cut is one, basis row has value one at empty suffix. With T_i(X_i)=sum_j x_(i,j) T_i(j),
 P = e_1^T T_1(X_1) ... T_v(X_v) e_1.
For each word w, its coefficient is obtained by successive restrictions, hence is exactly V(w) including zero for invalid words. Conversely every coefficient one is an actual tour, not a modular count of a class of tours. Each labeled word determines a unique squarefree monomial. This proves the identity over any field where the bases exist; acquisition is established for fixed F_p or Q. Arithmetic linear combinations may have negative coefficients and cancellations. No individual nonzero state is automatically a feasible residual and numerical evaluation at all ones in positive characteristic may vanish even on YES. Decision/witness lift remains the original guarded D and actual-word verification, not nonzero modular counting. A separate weighted variable t with nonnegative integer weights <=B permits dense coefficient arrays with an extra O(vB+1) factor; no claim when B is given in binary but superpolynomial in magnitude.

## B. Necessity and cost
Any row-ordered set-multilinear ABP with width w_i at cut i gives a separated sum P=sum_{j<=w_i} L_j(X_<=i) R_j(X_>i), by factoring at the cut. Coefficient flattening is a sum of w_i rank-one matrices; rank <=w_i over every field, even with signs/cancellation. Conversely A gives width r_i at all cuts. Same necessity for any exact separated factorization at the same cut, regardless of how individual factors are computed. This is a necessary and sufficient dimension in this model, not all arithmetic circuits.

Ledger: <=v^2 r candidate rows total; O(v log v) D calls per discrepancy witness, so O(v^3 r log v) decision calls for acquisition (including no answers). Query verifier explicitly contains O(r) row tests, rational/fixed-field arithmetic, candidate labels, graph, guard and prefix. Each NP reduction and actual D cost polynomial in these lengths; no unit oracle timing. ## C. Unconditional all-field obstruction on one orbit of HC residuals
Take v=2k, k>=2, complete loopless digraph, first k row vertices A, other k B, both labeled [k]. Equivalently use full directed bipartite graph for this fixed row order. Restrict prefix a to a bijection f:A->B, suffix to a bijection g:B->A. Then M_k[f,g]=1 iff g f is a k-cycle. Under every f this suffix relation is the same template up to renaming the domain B (and hence original suffix variable labels): R_f(g)=R_id(g f). All k! prefixes in this selected submatrix are extendable and their residuals constitute ONE permutation orbit. No assumption of cheap instantiation or shared arithmetic factors.

Let tau=(0 1 ... k-1), and H be the subgroup generated by pairwise disjoint transpositions (0 1),(2 3),... . |H|=2^floor(k/2), and each h=h^-1. Choose prefix f in H and suffix g_h=tau h^-1. If h=f then g_h f=tau, so YES. Otherwise h^-1 f contains at least one transposition (2j 2j+1). At 2j+1 its image under tau h^-1 f is tau(2j)=2j+1, a fixed point. Therefore not a k-cycle. This constructs a literal identity submatrix of size 2^floor(k/2) in M_k. Its rank is that size in every field, so
 r_k >= 2^floor(k/2)=2^Omega(sqrt(N))
 for complete digraphs under every fixed row order. This is an unconditional source-native rank obstruction, independent of the external arithmetic claims. Same cut and same prefix residuals as L01-04, but stronger resistance to linear span/parametric merging, even signed exact cancellation. Proof by contradiction: any separated factorization with fewer terms would factor identity through smaller dimension, impossible. Every asserted YES uses explicitly labeled f,g_h; each off-diagonal selected graph with exactly these successor arcs is NO, despite nonzero in/outdegree at all vertices. Unary support at root of complete graph never forces an edge; within each prefix, the forbidden return edge g(b)=f^-1(b) is a conditional fact, so closure preserves the distinctions. If a preprocessing algorithm chooses a different representation, no rank-to-time inference is justified. On complete bipartite graph numeric count is k!*(k-1)! and decision is YES; these are cheap. One residual orbit does NOT imply a small row-separated linear-state circuit. It does not falsify an unrestricted arithmetic circuit compiler that uses nonseparated gates.

## D. Adversarial controls and honest scope
Perfect matching: on complete n-by-n matrix, at cut i=k, select one prefix injection per k-column subset S, and a suffix injection onto its complement for each S. The compatibility submatrix is identity of size binom(n,k), so row-ordered formal permanent rank is exponential over EVERY field. Yet ordinary matching decision is polynomial; in F_2 the same formal permanent equals determinant, with polynomial-size general arithmetic circuits. Exact witness verification is also polynomial (for both HC and matching). Hence rank does not imply general arithmetic hardness, much less Boolean hardness. Equality control: polynomial prod_j(x_(j,0)y_(j,0)+x_(j,1)y_(j,1)) has rank 2^k across X|Y yet O(k) gates by interleaved local factors; equality decision is trivial. Affine/parity controls are likewise allowed direct linear algebra; no universal low-rank fixed-order claim. Conversely on an abstract guarded word relation V(a,b)=a dot b mod 2 (k bits each), at central cut over F_2 rank=k, though 2^k distinct rows including zero. At earlier/later cuts rank <=k+1 by expanding the bilinear sum; all-word coefficient relation linear over F_2 but not affine in the combined bits. This is a compact-circuit control, not an ordinary HC reduction. It demonstrates genuine compression beyond literal equality in the same linear-state construction. Bounded-width independent matching blocks have many tours but admit local product factorization; do not infer work from multiplicity. No sums of overlapping track decisions are used.
