## Theorem A: native PC degree pullback

Let F be any width <=3 CNF without empty clauses, padded by repeated literals as in compiler, and E(F) its strict E/B image. For the ternary source chart, work over Boolean multilinear quotient over a fixed field K. Native initial equations are exact local violation indicators (or local equations vanishing on every locally satisfying word), including signed edges and E equalities. Use AR03 Definition 2.1 PC: linear combinations and multiplication by a variable; degree charges multiplication premise degree +1, plus degrees of premises. Let d_native denote degree of a PC refutation of this native system. Then PC degree on F is <=2*d_native for K=GF(2) (also any char2 field) and <=3*d_native for arbitrary fixed K. This is a proof-model relation, not a decider reduction from arithmetic lower bounds.

Proof: In gauge f=0, write a,b,c for literal truth bits. Define z=(1-a)(1-b), w0=a XOR b XOR z, w1=(1-z) XOR c. The first local B4 word (a,b,z,w0) always has weight two. The second word (1-z,c,0,w1) has weight two iff a OR b OR c is true. In the all-false case it is (0,0,0,0). Define source original carrier x_i=x_i, z/w values as above, global f=0, and signed occurrence ports equal carrier bit XOR occurrence sign. Every equality carrier copies one value; each K5-minus-edge wire vertex has value of its second endpoint (sign carried on first edge). All E and signed edge constraints therefore hold identically on every Boolean source assignment. Every native port is a Boolean function of at most three source variables, and over char2 the formulas above have degree <=2. Unique multilinear representative of a function of at most 3 bits over arbitrary field has degree <=3. B exact violation indicators become identically zero on first B copies and the source clause violation polynomial (1-a)(1-b)(1-c) on second B copies. For other allowed exact local vanishing axioms the image is zero or a scalar multiple of the same clause polynomial, because only the one all-false literal word can be violated (tautological or repeated cases reduce correctly). Substitution is a ring homomorphism because all images idempotent. Every addition line maps to a valid addition. A variable multiplication line maps to multiplying a preceding polynomial by a degree<=r polynomial (r=2 or 3); expand this constant-size factor as sum of monomials and derive by successive multiplications by original variables and linear combinations. Inference degree <=r*(deg(parent)+1) <=r*d_native; initial source clause polynomials also lie within this bound (native initial degree included). Last 1 maps to 1. This proves theorem in AR's exact multilinear PC. If using ordinary polynomial ring with explicit Boolean axioms, additionally charge reduction of squares; standard termwise reductions use degree at most unreduced product degree, or conservatively allow r*(d_native+1). No free new high-degree extension variables, no hidden counting oracle. For fixed degree the pulled-back dictionaries and verification remain polynomial; no time bound on arbitrary high-degree expansion. For JOINT's long clauses, this bound applies to its ternary split chart, not automatically to its unsplit original source; exact split equivalence checked separately.

## Theorem B: bounded-scope cooperative obstruction

Let local premises f_j be exact failure indicators on scopes contained in a common union U of w Boolean variables. A polynomial h supported on U that vanishes on their joint satisfying set has the pointwise identity h=sum_{a in {0,1}^U, h(a)!=0} h(a)*delta_a*f_{j(a)}, selecting one failing premise j(a) on each such word, with delta_a the assignment indicator. This follows by evaluating each word separately; exact in Boolean quotient over every field. Construct each summand by multiplying a previously derived f_j by literals and adding. At most O(2^w * poly(w,number of parents)) local acquisition/checking work, inference degree <=w+1 with AR convention, since all intermediate multilinear supports stay within U. This includes bounded-width table joins, projections and local annihilator extraction when the entire proof union (not merely output scope) is bounded. A RUP consequence with at most k guard literals and source/learned clauses of width <=b has a resolution derivation of width <=b+k (derive guarded units from propagation reasons using previous guarded units, resolving until conflict), and polynomial calculus simulation of degree <=b+k+1. Bound actual guard degree and cumulative chart pullbacks, not just terminal output degree. Resolution identity from previous run: for complementary literal factors P=(1+x)M,Q=xN in GF2, NP+MQ=MN. More general fields use the usual signed false literal factors; same identity. All conditional proof premises must have been established or guard-discharged in the original ring within a common degree cap. A finite or even polynomially many lanes sharing such deductions can be concatenated topologically, retaining degree cap D (number of tracks does not multiply degree). This does not include arbitrary flow, CP, matching, arbitrary guarded maps, unbounded guard width or extensions unless an independent low-degree translation is established.

Known dependency: Alekhnovich/Razborov, Lower Bounds for Polynomial Calculus: Non-Binomial Case, March 13 2003 author version https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~razborov/files/misha.pdf, Definition 2.1, Corollary 4.7, section 4.5 uses related local substitution in EPHP. For fixed k=3, fixed density Delta=6, random clauses chosen independently from signed distinct-variable triples with replacement, refutation degree Omega(n) over any fixed field with probability 1-o(1). Expected witnesses 2^n(7/8)^(6n)=(2*(7/8)^6)^n, tends to 0; Markov => UNSAT whp. The intersection of events has probability 1-o(1), so arbitrarily large NO instances exist (not an explicitly generated growing hard family). Compiler uses O(n) vertices and edges for m=6n; exact ASCII edge-index bit length N=O(n log n) and Omega(n log n) on these outputs; hence native PC degree Omega(n)=Omega(N/log N), by Theorem A, for any fixed field, with stronger factor 2 transfer in char2. This falsifies universal sublinear-degree completeness of a portfolio whose entire accepted proof DAG has a checked same-field degree-preserving simulation. More lanes alone cannot escape. Does not prove exponential work for all proof representations, let alone P!=NP. PC lower bound does not directly constrain compact high-degree circuit certificates, unrestricted matching/cutting planes, extended reasoning, mixed-field imports, or unrestricted Boolean deciders. A fixed finite set of independently PC-refuting fields also faces a simultaneous high-degree family by finite union bound; combining facts across fields is a different system and not ruled out. Perfect matching/FPHP are controls: polynomial decision can coexist with restricted proof complexity. No assumption of unrestricted hardness from representation.
