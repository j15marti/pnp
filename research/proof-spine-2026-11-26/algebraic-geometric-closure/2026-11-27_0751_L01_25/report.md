## A: exact existential projection without key enumeration
Let U be a binary observation space, W a subspace, choose complementary D with U=W direct sum D (Gaussian elimination on explicit bases). Work on U*, coordinates w in W*, h in D*. Let f(w,h)=sum_{a,b} c_(a,b) chi_a(w)chi_b(h), over GF(p), p odd. Group each distinct hidden index b into a row g_b(w)=sum_a c_(a,b)chi_a(w). By orthogonality over the field (2^dim D invertible), f(w,.) is identically zero iff every g_b(w)=0. Therefore the exact projection is {w: at least one g_b(w) nonzero}. A single average g_0 is insufficient in positive characteristic. If f is itself a vector of functions representing a support by nonzero evaluation, apply the same operation to each component, then concatenate the rows. This preserves existential semantics exactly.

Collect the distinct boundary frequency columns and row-reduce the coefficient matrix over GF(p). A row basis has precisely the same simultaneous zero locus as all input rows because each lies in the span of the other collection. Thus at most min(number of nonzero hidden rows, number of distinct boundary columns) independent rows survive. For s total input nonzero coefficient cells, there are at most s rows and s columns; Gaussian basis costs O(s^3) field ops plus sorting and key conversion polynomial in N and s. Potential densification to s^2 cells is charged. No field extension, summation of counts or enumeration of 2^dim W. This is support representation, not exact counting.

## B: join, closure, and width tariff
On same W, two vector predicates F=(f_i),G=(g_j) are jointly nonzero exactly when some f_i*g_j is nonzero pointwise (field has no zero divisors). Form all sparse XOR convolutions, then take a coefficient-row basis. If all rows are zero, there is no compatible key. For different observation spaces, embed both into their sum; functions depend only on their own subspaces. Agreement on complete intersection permits a common extension by linear-functional gluing. Project onto parent interface by A. Tree induction proves exact existential support, retaining original nonlinear predicates or a certified exact replacement with provenance. For input total coefficient cells s,t, the raw pair-product cost is at most st coefficient-pair ops; at most st nonzero cells before row compression. Dense row reduction on up to st columns/rows costs O((st)^3) field operations; storage O((st)^2) cells. Account for Gaussian coordinate transformations, sorted lookups, field arithmetic, raw and failed products, scans and temporary buffers. If cumulative input/output/intermediate matrix dimensions and acquisition costs are bounded by fixed N^C, total work polynomial, with one fixed exponent, across a paid O(N^C) tree schedule. There is no proof of universal representation bound; an explicit table of all possible seam keys can be exponential even for f=1, represented by one row/one coefficient. Conversely repeated products can grow rapidly, even when a compact circuit exists.

Exact cut-width tariff: for quotient pi by learned normal space H in ambient frequency space and L,R any cut,
lambda' = lambda + dim(H intersect (L+R)) - dim(H intersect L) - dim(H intersect R).
This follows from dim pi(V)=dim V-dim(H intersect V) and the intersection dimension formula. In particular define cross=dim(H intersect (L+R))-dim((H intersect L)+(H intersect R)); overlap=dim(H intersect L intersect R). Then lambda'=lambda+cross-overlap, giving the exact change (not just upper bound). Side-local H has cross=0; unrestricted learned normals may increase width. This part symbolic, not separately benchmarked.
