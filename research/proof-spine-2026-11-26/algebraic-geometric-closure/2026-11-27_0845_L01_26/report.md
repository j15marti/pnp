## A: scalar contraction with no modular cancellation
Let U be the binary observation space, W a subspace, and z a uniform linear functional on U. For a 0/1 predicate f, define conditional message m_W(w)=E[f(z)|z restricted to W=w]. This is C(w)/2^(dim U-dim W), where C(w) is an integer in [0,2^(dim U-dim W)]. Set M=2^max(1,d)+1, odd and larger than all possible C. Work in R=Z/MZ (not necessarily a field). Powers of 2 are units, so the residue m_W(w) is zero iff C(w)=0. All coefficients have O(d+1) bits. Fourier inversion on GF2^d is valid over R because the character orthogonality matrix has inverse 2^-d times its transpose. Projection simply discards every frequency not in W, in ambient coordinates; basis conversion and membership use GF2 Gaussian elimination. This follows by averaging each character on the kernel of the restriction map. No enumeration of fibers, no need to discover primes. At root multiply the constant by 2^d modulo M and choose least nonnegative residue: this equals the exact count on the original d-dimensional chart, including unconstrained coordinates. The output count is chart words, not automatically source witnesses unless the map is bijective. Signed general polynomials do not satisfy the nonnegativity premise. The earlier vector-support theorem remains useful for such functions.
