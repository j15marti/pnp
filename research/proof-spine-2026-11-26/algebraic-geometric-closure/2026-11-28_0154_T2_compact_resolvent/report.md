## Exact symbolic response theorem
For a bucket (v OR A_i) and (!v OR B_j), retain all nonincident rows; existential v is exactly all non-tautological resolvents A_i OR B_j. Reverse lift: if some A_i false choose v=1, if some B_j false choose v=0; never both because the matching resolvent would fail, otherwise arbitrary. No full boundary-word enumeration. For clause C, define p_C=product of falsity indicators over GF(2) Boolean quotient x_i^2=x_i. Let R=A_i union B_j without tautology. Then p_R=p_{R\\A_i}p_{v OR A_i}+p_{R\\B_j}p_{!v OR B_j}; expand and reduce square-free, gives ((1+v)+v)p_R. Degree of intermediate <= |R|+1, even when output degree |R|. For a further monomial multiple of degree<=d, lifted proof can use d+1; charge signs and expanded monomial count. Per fixed d features O(k^d), clause-pair generation O(m^2 k) before dedup. No uniform repeated BVE growth bound.

## Exact unate degree-d basis theorem
If all occurrences of each variable in the compact response have one polarity, complement those coordinates so every clause forbids an all-one set. Feasible support is a downset D. Every monomial whose support contains a forbidden scope vanishes on D; the remaining monomials of degree<=d are independent because evaluate on their support words, yielding a unit triangular subset-incidence matrix ordered by cardinality. This is complete for degree<=d: precisely the span of forbidden monomials is the vanishing subspace. Enumerate O(k^d) supports and test containment, not 2^k carrier assignments. Affine sign change preserves degree. Mixed signs invalidate downset reasoning; every tested native region was mixed. This is a scoped exact rank acquisition theorem, not universal. Single-clause test controls and proof: positive/negative uniform signs gauge to downset, empty scope kills all supports; overlapping mixed signed clauses can force consequences not given by individual forbidden supports (e.g. (x OR y),(x OR !y) imply x); conflicting pair illustrates why unate premise needed. No independent general implementation tests beyond symbolic checker.
