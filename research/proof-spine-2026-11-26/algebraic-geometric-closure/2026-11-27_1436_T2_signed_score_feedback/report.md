## Signed count bits

For literal l, v(x)=x, v(not x)=x-1; b=v^2 is exact truth bit. Q=sum b, D=sum v. Q>0 iff clause satisfied, and (Q+D)/2, (Q-D)/2 are counts of satisfied positive and negative literals. Neither aggregate alone preserves which variable is true. In the Boolean ring over F2 let e_r be elementary symmetric polynomial in the b_i. At Boolean point, e_r=C(Q,r) mod 2. The coefficient of t^(2^j) in (1+t)^Q = product_{j:Q_j=1}(1+t^(2^j)) over F2 equals bit Q_j. Thus the first two count bits are e_1 and e_2. B4 exact weight two iff e_1=0 and e_2=1 (0 and 4 both have low two bits 00 and both must be excluded). On width-three clause Q>0 iff e_1 OR e_2, equivalently e_1+e_2+e_1 e_2=1. A third bit for Q would be e_4, not e_3. Wider count bits can have high degree and expansion cost; no free enumeration. All 256 signed B4 word cases and 64 signed 3-clause word cases checked.

## Fixed-chart moment-table feedback theorem

Given an explicitly acquired exact Boolean table residual with n retained coordinates and total R0 allowed rows, maintain the degree <=2 polynomial space over F2 in the Boolean quotient, dimension D0=1+n+binom(n,2). For each local relation, acquire its degree <=2 annihilator basis by Gaussian elimination on local monomial evaluations. Store their global span V. Multiply every acquired affine relation by each Boolean coordinate, reducing squares x_i^2=x_i. Add sound consequences until affine-multiple saturation. To test a table row w, append equations x_i=w_i and x_i x_j=w_i w_j for its coordinates to the linear system V in formal moment coordinates. If the resulting system entails constant 1=0, no actual solution can have that row: evaluation at any such solution would satisfy each equation. Delete only those rows. Recompute local annihilators and repeat, retaining all previous valid equations; after a deletion, a polynomial vanishing before also vanishes after. At constant contradiction or empty table, reject. Otherwise a fixed point is UNKNOWN, not SAT. Under a guard every inference remains guarded. For each round, the independent replay generates a row-oriented local kernel, checks vanishing on the current table, recomputes a fresh span with affine multiplication, and replays recorded row deletions via full augmented elimination. The discovery quotient reduction is not claimed complete; a missed deletion is allowed. Independent replay verifies each attained deletion.

Potential Phi=D0-rank(V)+sum |T_i|, initialized <=D0+R0. Rank additions and row deletions each strictly decrease it; they cannot replenish within this fixed chart. At most D0+R0 productive credits, and a final no-progress scan, with at most D0 distinct pivot additions and at most R0 row deletions. Each scan is polynomial in explicit table size, n and D0; dense exact GF(2) elimination needs polynomial bit work and polynomial live storage, hence cumulative memory polynomial for this admitted representation. Fixed number of guards and fixed-width projection steps also polynomial with explicit bounded acquisition. Neither arbitrary useful guards nor arbitrary chart size/width nor future representation changes have been universally bounded. This is not universal P=NP.
