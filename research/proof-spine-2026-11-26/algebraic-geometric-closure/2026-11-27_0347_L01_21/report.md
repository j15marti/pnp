## B: deterministic polynomial exact joint residue decision (fixed parameters)
Let x=a+Bz, z in GF2^d, be a nonempty exact Boolean affine chart with known original coordinate map and guards. Let p be a fixed odd prime, r and D fixed positive integers. Let F_1,...,F_r be explicitly given integer polynomials of degree<=D in t Boolean coordinates (or their sparse character expansions), targets b_i in GF(p). Claim: decide whether any actual chart word satisfies all F_i(x)=b_i mod p in deterministic polynomial bit time, regardless of d. This is exact for the modular relaxation, not general integer equations or HC.

Use characters chi_u(z)=(-1)^(u dot z) in GF(p), u in GF2^d. Character product chi_u chi_v=chi_(u XOR v); sum over all z chi_u chi_v=2^d if u=v else 0, by pairing on a nonzero direction. Because p odd, 2^d invertible: the characters form a basis of functions on GF2^d with values in GF(p). This is the finite-field version of ordinary Boolean Fourier orthogonality, independently proved here. No evaluation on off-Boolean field points.

Write x_j=(1-(-1)^a_j chi_{B_j})/2, where B_j is original coordinate's observation row. Substitute into each F_i-b_i; collect coefficients of equal d-bit keys by XOR. Every degree-D monomial contributes at most 2^D terms. A t-coordinate polynomial with polynomially many monomials thus yields a sparse dictionary G_i with at most s_i<=2^D times input monomial count terms. Over GF(p), Fermat gives exact pointwise indicator I_i=1-G_i^(p-1), taking values 0,1. Define H=product_i I_i. This equals one exactly when every modular condition holds. Products are in character algebra, not free formal multilinearization: for each pair of existing dictionary terms XOR keys, multiply coefficients mod p, accumulate, remove zeros. By character independence, H vanishes on all Boolean parameters iff every dictionary coefficient is zero. Therefore empty dictionary iff no joint residue witness. Nonempty dictionary means modular witness exists, not a tour. Test ALL coefficients, not just constant. No counting oracle.

Cost: sequential dictionary multiplication has at most sum_i O(p*(1+s_i)^(p-1)) pair operations for powers, then O(r*product_i (1+s_i^(p-1))) for joins, or conservatively O(r p (1+max_i s_i)^(r(p-1)+1)). Each d-bit key, exact mod-p coefficient, comparison, storage and deterministic sorting/aggregation is polynomial in N; use conservative fixed exponent. For fixed D,r,p and polynomial explicit input, polynomial time and space; if parameters grow, enforce cap on actual products and return UNKNOWN, never infer NO from an aborted dictionary. Source admission, Gaussian elimination, failed guards, original ID map, integer coefficient reduction, comparisons, verification and reconstruction all charged. Peak<=poly(total work), cumulative memory<=time*peak on same execution. For arbitrary supplied sparse character dictionaries, D not needed but their acquisition cost must be proved. Optional guarded closure: for a nonzero H, test H*x_j and H*(1-x_j) by the same two-term character convolution; a zero product discharges that phase pin from the modular relaxation and hence HC. This computes exact unary support of the actual modular chart at O(t*|H|) additional pair operations plus key cost, no assumption of independent compatibility.
