It is not the full attained weight set.

## Independent method: mod4 polarization
For x=x0 XOR sum of selected directions, use (-1)^x_j=(-1)^x0_j product_i (1-2 t_i v_ij). Expanding integer weight modulo4 gives
 |x|=|x0|+sum_i t_i [|v_i|-2|v_i intersect x0|] -2 sum_(i<k) t_i t_k sum_j (1-2 x0_j)v_ij v_kj (mod4).
 Terms of degree>=3 vanish. Therefore constant weight modulo4 iff every linear coefficient divisible by4 and each pair of directions has even support intersection. Necessity follows evaluating zero, singleton, then pair parameter choices, so independent reconstruction without HNF. Thus every parity-feasible Boolean assignment has weight 1 mod4; required q=3 is 3 mod4. The contradiction refutes the native graph despite both original integer and fractional feasibility.
