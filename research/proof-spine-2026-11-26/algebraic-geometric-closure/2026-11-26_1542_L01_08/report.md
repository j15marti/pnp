## A. Constructive exact normalized HC verifier
Introduce n^2 bits t_(k,i) for position k occupied by vertex i, and n(n-1) original edge bits e_ij. Require exactly one vertex per position and one position per vertex using a positive clause plus all pairwise negative clauses, t_(0,0)=1, exactly one outgoing e per row, and for k modulo n and i!=j the clause (!t_(k,i) OR !t_(k+1,j) OR e_ij). Every satisfying assignment corresponds bijectively to a normalized directed tour: positions are a permutation anchored at 0, the implications force each successor edge, and the outgoing exact-one constraints prohibit extras. Conversely each tour uniquely determines all positions and edges. No existential multiplicity. O(n²) bits, O(n³) clauses and literal occurrences, O(n³ log n) encoded list bits. Assign weight x_ij to selected e_ij=1 and 1 to every other label. The exact assignment polynomial is H_n. This retains every graph mask through setting x_ij=0 for unavailable edges.

For any CNF F with m Boolean bits and q clauses (including empty and tautological clauses), construct simple graph G: literal vertices L_i^0,L_i^1 joined; one private vertex P_i adjacent to both; one clause vertex C_j adjacent to all literal vertices that satisfy its clause. Private/clause vertices are mutually nonadjacent. Assign weights z(L_i^1)=x_ij for original edge bits, 1 otherwise; z(L_i^0)=1; z(P_i)=z(C_j)=-1. In I_G(z)=sum_{S independent} product_{v in S} z_v, fix the selected literal vertices. At most one per pair. Each unused pair's private vertex contributes 1+(-1)=0; each unsatisfied clause vertex contributes 0 in exactly the same way. Every valid complete assignment has all auxiliary vertices blocked and contributes its exact monomial once. Therefore the specialization of I_G is H_n over Z and hence every commutative ring. This proves both directions of the surviving weighted contributions and no overcount. Equivalently, choose the first available auxiliary and toggle it, a sign-reversing involution; no enumeration is needed to prove the identity. In characteristic two signs coincide but paired contributions sum to zero. This is a cancellation map, not a support-preserving map.

## B. Global algebraic replica: bipartite incidence and homogeneous dual-rail mask polynomial
Construct B with left vertices V(G), right vertices E(G), each right vertex adjacent to its two endpoints. Define the homogeneous two-label polynomial
P_B(a)=sum_{S independent in B} product_{v in S} a_(v,1) product_{v not in S} a_(v,0).
For original vertex v set a_(v,0)=z_v, a_(v,1)=1. For edge vertex h set a_(h,0)=1,a_(h,1)=-1. For fixed selection C of original vertices, each edge vertex whose endpoints are both absent is freely togglable and contributes zero; otherwise blocked. The surviving original selections C are exactly vertex covers of G; their complements are independent sets, with weight product z_v over v not in C. Hence P_B under this projection equals I_G and under A's weights exactly H_n. This direct dual-rail incidence identity is the same cancellation principle as Briquel–Koiran MFCS2009 Lemma2, with explicit HC verifier and no divisions or field restrictions. No novelty priority asserted.

Define a total Boolean label-availability decider D_B(a) on 2b bits, b=|V(B)|: is there an independent set whose chosen label at each vertex is available? Exact formula
D_B(a)= AND_v(a_v0 OR a_v1) AND_{uv in E(B)}(a_u0 OR a_v0).
Necessity: no missing pair, no adjacent forced-one vertices. Sufficiency: select exactly those vertices lacking their 0 label, all others choose 0. Uniform linear indexed scan and explicit witness. All Boolean inputs, YES and NO. Empty guard every label supported (empty set or singleton). Under arbitrary masks close by checking empty pair or adjacent forced-one; then remove 1 from neighbors of forced ones. No other forced values: any remaining permitted singleton-in can be added to the forced set. This is complete unary support closure on B, preserves IDs and actual witnesses; do not import source mask facts as target pins unless supported by a Boolean adapter.

Every accepted availability vector has >=b ones. At weight b precisely one bit per vertex is available; acceptance iff these labels describe an independent set. The unique multilinear representative ML(D_B) over any field has zero coefficients below degree b, by Boolean subset Möbius inversion; its degree-b homogeneous part equals P_B since no lower Boolean support contributes. Thus [ML(D_B)]_b=P_B, a formal identity over every field. D_B is an explicit depth-two monotone AND-of-2-OR formula of O(b+|E(B)|)=O(n³) size and can itself be arithmetized with AND as multiplication, OR(a,b)=a+b-ab in O(n³) gates and formal degree O(n³). That arithmetization is not necessarily multilinear.

## C. Adversarial replica: even exact weighted optimization does not bridge
For arbitrary binary-encoded signed integer unary costs c_v(0),c_v(1) and availability masks, exact minimum cost and witness of B can be obtained by min cut. Flip labels on the right: z_l=s_l, z_r=1-s_r. Each edge imposes z_l<=z_r. Choose source-side=1. Normalize each unary cost by subtracting its minimum, and use arcs source->v of capacity c'_v(0), v->sink of c'_v(1) in the flipped labels. Each implication arc l->r has capacity M=1+sum_v |c_v(0)-c_v(1)|. Add capacity M to the unary arc penalizing a forbidden label. Any feasible cut costs <=M-1 after normalization; any violation costs >=M. Min cut returns exact feasible optimum and witness, or no feasible assignment. Polynomial bit time by Edmonds–Karp or any standard polynomial max flow; capacities have O(L+log b) bits for max unary bit length L. Thus masks, arbitrary unary minimum cost, threshold queries and optimal witness extraction are all in P; lexicographic tie-breaking using O(b)-bit powers of 2 also in P. Exact prescribed weight equality and multiplicative signed partition sums are NOT claimed easy.

If any general polynomial-size compiler applied to these deciders to obtain their minimum-degree multilinear component, A+B would turn its output into polynomial-size unrestricted arithmetic circuits for H_n over the same field using only variable/constant substitutions. Conditional on either external HC arithmetic lower bound, this compiler cannot exist even nonuniformly, even in F_2. In characteristic zero a support-preserving nonnegative projection to H_n would instead imply HC in P: for a source mask set original variables zero or one, forbid target labels mapped to zero, permit the others, and D_B is true iff a projected monomial survives. More generally the same implication holds for any explicitly given cancellation-free support adapter with both directions, regardless of field. Our signed projection fails this property: even an empty HC edge mask leaves the empty independent set of B compatible with all the nonzero mapped constants/labels except possibly original-side labels zero (choose no original vertices, all right labels zero if compatible with nonzero original-side 0 labels; alternatively choose ALL original-side vertices and NO right vertices, whose weight is 1 under the projection). Indeed that latter assignment always survives as a term, canceled by another term in the polynomial when H_n=0. Thus decision does not follow from this projection. This is the first exact missing arrow, not a Boolean separation.

The above HC identity avoids this problem. Easy exact-tour verifier is also P; the full monotone mask extension of B is stronger than verifier-only control.
