## Target, model and falsifiers
NO-SEMANTIC-TRANSFER: Can genuine whole-input YES/NO decision plus bounded-weight isolation recover formal witness polynomials with polynomial circuit size? Ordinary directed HC on v labeled vertices, loopless simple directed graphs, explicit adjacency bits N=v(v-1), normalized successor permutations forming one cycle. PM on n-by-n Boolean bipartite adjacency. Deterministic uniform bit model for algorithms; formal nonuniform division-free binary arithmetic circuits over specified fields for algebraic bounds. No supplied witness, no promise of few cycles. Falsifiers: (1) a native chart mask with unmatched HC/PM witness multiplicity; (2) colliding Birkhoff-adjacent permutations whose union has extra tours; (3) an isolation family below the coloring inequality that isolates every YES graph; (4) a polynomial formal compiler that survives the easy PM determinant control under the claimed permanent lower bound. Scope of (4) conditional, not a disproof of HC-specific transfer.

## A. Parsimonious native matching cube (proved)
For n>=2, take rows r_i and n disjoint directed rings c_(j,i), i,j in Z/nZ, v=n(n+1). Fixed ring arcs c_(j,i)->c_(j,i+1), fixed exits c_(j,i-1)->r_(i+1); optional entries r_i->c_(j,i) exactly for M_ij=1. No other arcs. Every Hamiltonian cycle must enter each ring at least once, from a row. Exactly n row departures and n rings imply one entry per ring. After entry c_(j,i), visit every vertex of that ring before leaving (no further entry), hence follow the fixed ring direction and exit at c_(j,i-1) to r_(i+1). The distinct ring choices are precisely the matching permutation sigma with M_i,sigma(i)=1. Conversely each such permutation concatenates these n uniquely specified traversals to a Hamiltonian cycle. Starting at r_0 gives one canonical lift per permutation, not v rotations. The map works for all masks including NO, not just selected witness graphs.

Set fixed-arc variables to 1, nonarcs to 0, optional entries to x_ij in HC_v. The resulting FORMAL polynomial is perm_n, coefficients one, over every field. This is the HC-to-permanent specialization, not a claim that the permanent projects to unrestricted HC in the reverse direction. On this entire native deletion cube, exact ordinary HC decision is nevertheless in P by bipartite matching. An easy total Boolean extension rejects outside the syntactically recognized chart. On the complete chart every native arc is selectable and avoidable: entry and associated exit selected iff matching uses ij, corresponding omitted ring arc iff matching uses ij. This improves L01-02 controls from sparse agreement to exact unrestricted mask semantics WITHIN a polynomial-size native chart, not the entire unrestricted HC universe.

Certified initial/after-deduction/stagnation closure: run matching, if infeasible return NO; for each entry test matching with forced and forbidden entry. These are exact support/forced statuses, mapped to entry, corresponding exit and complementary ring arc. Maintain original IDs and explicit guards; unsupported choices removed, forced ones recorded; iterate on any new guarded restrictions. No assumption of free full unary closure on unrestricted HC. For two adjacent permutation supports, both complete lifts survive this closure and their weight equality survives if forced arc constants are paid.

## B. Universal isolation obstruction (proved using KLR Theorem 1.8)
On the same native chart give each arc integer weight w in [0,W]. Write B=sum of all ring-arc weights and a_ij=w(entry_ij)+w(exit_ij)-w(omitted ring arc_ij). Each lifted tour weight is exactly B+sum_i a_i,sigma(i). In the Birkhoff graph, permutations are adjacent iff their relative permutation consists of one nontrivial cycle and fixed points. Union of their matching edges has singleton common edges and exactly one alternating even cycle. It admits exactly two matchings, hence exactly two original HC lifts on the corresponding graph. Thus any fixed list of q weight maps isolating a unique minimum on EVERY nonempty native mask must assign different weight-signature vectors to adjacent permutations. Otherwise the two tours tie on every map. Same necessary condition for exact detection by OR of nonzero weighted cycle polynomials over F2: two equal-weight tours cancel identically, including all coefficients, on every track.

Kane-Lovett-Rao (arXiv:1702.05773, Theorem 1.8) bound independent sets by n!/2^((n-4)/2), so at least 2^((n-4)/2) colors. Signature range <=(vW+1)^q since each tour uses v arcs. Consequently
  q log2(vW+1) >= (n-4)/2.
In particular W >= (2^((n-4)/2)-1)/v if q=1; q=Omega(n/log n)=Omega(sqrt(v)/log v) for W=v^O(1) along v=n(n+1). For differing bounds use product of (vW_l+1). Also the variable part of each pullback lies in [-nW,2nW], giving optional stronger range 3nW+1. This is a fixed-weight/track cardinality obstruction, NOT an arbitrary decision lower bound. Integer magnitude is not bit length. Exponential degree obstructs dense interpolation, not necessarily small formal circuits. q lower bound remains polynomial. Odd characteristic: the two-tie polynomial has coefficient 2, so the cancellation obstruction is F2-specific; the unique-minimum obstruction is field-independent. Adaptive graph-specific weights escape (acquire a witness and weight its edges 0, others 1); chart PM does this uniformly in P. Collision discovery in the audit was enumerative, not claimed polynomial.

## C. Conditional decision-sensitive bridge: acquire a universal isolation family
For ordinary v-vertex HC, m=v(v-1), let q=m+1 independent lists of m weights each uniform in [1,2m]. Isolation lemma: holding other weights fixed, for each arc at most one of its 2m possible weights ties the best set containing it with the best set excluding it. Multiple minima imply some arc exhibits such a tie; union bound <=1/2. Across q independent maps failure on a fixed nonempty mask <=2^-q. Union over <=2^m masks <1, hence a universal list exists. This existence is nonuniform and not an algorithm.

Assume an actual uniform polynomial-time correct ordinary HC decider D. For a graph and weight list, find minimum weight by polynomially many threshold decision calls: weighted HC existence at threshold is NP, with explicit bit-weight sum and permutation certificate, hence reduce to ordinary HC by standard polynomial reductions. Nonuniqueness at optimum is likewise an NP query using two distinct normalized tours. A bad mask is YES on which all q maps have nonunique minima. Given D, verifying badness is deterministic P, with all q tests paid. Existence of a bad mask for a proposed list is therefore NP and decidable in P by the same D and reductions. Consequently validity of a full list is P; existence of a completion of a partial list with valid range and no bad mask is NP, hence decidable by D. Use a unary vertex count and fixed-length binary encodings of weights, reject malformed entries. Greedily fix O(q*m*log m) bits with a paid extension query each, then test full validity. Existence above ensures one completion; the output is a uniform polynomial-time acquired list conditional on D. All nested reductions have constant depth, fixed polynomial size, and fixed exponent if D has fixed exponent. No randomized search or free NP/coNP oracle. The argument applies equally to PM if P=NP but PM's own P algorithm does not alone decide all the NP list-validity predicates.

For each list define P_l(t,x)=sum_C t^w_l(C) prod_(e in C)x_e. On any Boolean mask the NO case makes all coefficients zero; unique minimum yields coefficient 1 in any field. Thus exact decision is OR of coefficient-nonzero predicates. Over fixed prime field F_p use 1-prod_(l,k)(1-c_(l,k)(x)^(p-1)) on Boolean masks. This corrects overlapping votes; addition of track values is not OR. The argument does NOT acquire circuits for the coefficients c_(l,k), or the witness sum; the assumed Boolean decider does not supply them. This identifies the first missing arrow rather than completing transfer.

## D. Adversarial arithmetic control on the same matching cube
For PM on n^2 bits, the analogous nonuniform universal isolation lists exist with q=n^2+1,W=2n^2. Let d_(l,k)=[t^k] det(x_ij*t^w_l,ij). Degree in t <=nW, each coefficient homogeneous degree n in x. Division-free Berkowitz computes determinant over any commutative ring; simulate over F_p[x][t]/(t^(nW+1)), replacing each ring gate by coefficient addition or naive convolution with polynomial overhead. Thus all d_(l,k) have jointly polynomial-size formal circuits. NO gives all zero, and isolated minimum gives +/-1, nonzero in every characteristic. Exact Boolean PM on every mask is represented by
  Q(x)=1-prod_(l,k)(1-d_(l,k)(x)^(p-1)).
Over any fixed prime p, Q is an actual polynomial-size formal circuit of polynomial degree, evaluated correctly on the whole Boolean mask cube. For p>2 every nonconstant term has degree >=n(p-1)>n, so [Q]_n=0 as a formal polynomial. Yet [ML(Q)]_n=perm_n: by Boolean Möbius inversion, all masks smaller than n are NO, and the n-edge YES masks are exactly the matchings. Hence ordinary homogeneous extraction alone is invalid even with exact NO semantics and polynomial-degree arithmetic circuits. If the claimed permanent lower bounds are true (p>2), a general poly-size multilinearization compiler for these exact Boolean circuits would contradict them. In p=2 permanent=det, so no corresponding permanent hardness inference. Via A, this is also a native HC restriction with hard (conditional) symbolic witness polynomial but a cheap exact decision algorithm. Control does not assert a cheap exact formal circuit for HC on the entire ordinary HC cube. Easy minimal-witness verifier (one outgoing/incoming per vertex and one-cycle traversal) is another independent P control with degree-v HC witness polynomial after ML, as reconstructed previously.
