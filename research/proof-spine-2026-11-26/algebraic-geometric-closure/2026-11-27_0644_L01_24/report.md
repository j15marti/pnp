## A: exact constructive spectral affine hull
Let F be any explicitly encoded field of odd characteristic with polynomial-cost exact arithmetic (GF3 executed), z in GF2^d, chi_u(z)=(-1)^(u dot z). Let f(z)=sum_(a in S) c_a chi_a(z), with distinct keys and nonzero coefficients. If S empty, f identically zero by orthogonality (2^d invertible). Otherwise let X={z:f(z)!=0}. For any u and bit epsilon,
 u dot z=epsilon for all z in X iff chi_u f=(-1)^epsilon f
 iff c_(a xor u)=(-1)^epsilon c_a for every a (missing coefficient zero).
Necessity pointwise, sufficiency divide by f(z) on its support, and character independence translates equality to coefficients. Fix a0 in S. Any qualifying u lies in a0 xor S, because translating the nonzero coefficient at a0 preserves nonzeroness. Test those <=|S| candidate shifts against both signs; checking a in S suffices because translation injective and a successful check implies equality of supports. Thus acquire ALL affine equations valid on X in O(|S|^2) coefficient comparisons/dictionary lookups and polynomial bit operations in encoded keys/field. Deterministic sorted lookup avoids probabilistic hashing assumptions. Take their independent GF2 basis. Their simultaneous solution is precisely aff(X): annihilator of span of pairwise differences with RHS evaluated on any x0 in X; no need to acquire x0 to compute equations. For any explicit interface W, take linear combinations of equations whose normals lie in W by Gaussian elimination; these are exactly the affine hull equations of the attained restriction to W*. No enumeration of 2^d points or even actual interface keys. In particular f=1 has 2^d attained words but one coefficient and zero nontrivial equations. This is an exact hull, NOT actual nonlinear support.
