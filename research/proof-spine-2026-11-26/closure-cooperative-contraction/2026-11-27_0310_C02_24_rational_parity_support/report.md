## Connecting theorem: maximal fractional support then parity closure
Let A be the 0/1 incidence matrix with one column per retained row, one equation per block (exactly one row) and per physical coordinate (exactly one occupancy). Let b=all ones. Every cover has x in {0,1}^R with Ax=b over integers. Conversely such x is a cover. Rational polytope P={x>=0:Ax=b} is bounded by group equations. If P empty, a Farkas certificate y with A^T y>=0,b^T y<0 refutes every cover. If feasible, acquire S={j:some x in P has x_j>0} by exact rational LP. A certificate for removing a subset D is A^T y>=1_D,b^T y=0, since any nonnegative feasible x obeys 0>=sum_D x_j. A^T y>=1_D is componentwise. No numerical status alone accepted. Signed rational y allowed.

**One-optimization support acquisition (theoretical, not implemented):** if A has m rows, every positive coordinate maximum is attained at a vertex and is at least 1/m! by Cramer's rule on an independent square 0/1 submatrix (zero coordinates fixed). Average at most R such vertices, one for each possible positive coordinate; the resulting point has every supported coordinate >=epsilon=1/(R m!). Maximize sum_j z_j subject to x in P,0<=z_j<=epsilon,z_j<=x_j. All supported j can reach epsilon simultaneously; unsupported j cannot. Every exact optimum therefore has z_j=epsilon exactly on S. Bit length epsilon O(log R+m log m), LP size polynomial. For D=complement S, solve one further rational LP to get the dual certificate A^T y>=1_D,b^T y=0 by strong duality; if infeasible use a Farkas search. Alternatively use repeated ordinary LP maximizing sum of as-yet unobserved coordinates; every productive call acquires at least one positive coordinate, at most R+1 calls; final zero objective gives a dual for all remaining coordinates. Exact acquisition is known polynomial-time rational LP, not an oracle and not a claim for numerical simplex implementation.

On identical row universe reduce Ax=b modulo 2. Gaussian elimination can refute if inconsistent, delete rows forced 0, and if row forced 1 delete its other group rows by original exactly-one condition. Affine feasibility alone not exact integer feasibility. Exchange only these certified deletions with rational track, recompute both on restricted universe, repeat. Each nonterminal productive round deletes >=1 row, no reintroductions, so at most R+1 rounds. Each retained solution was feasible before and every full source cover survives; if a purported lost cover exists, its original indicator violates either a verified inequality or a necessary parity equality. Strict potential R. Supports computed from LP and GF2 are not actual joint integral support. Recompute geometric tree and frozen affine interfaces only after this closure, invalidating stale table caches. The implementation repeats table-level rational/parity closure but does not rerun full original native matching GAC after each row deletion; no stronger closure claim.
