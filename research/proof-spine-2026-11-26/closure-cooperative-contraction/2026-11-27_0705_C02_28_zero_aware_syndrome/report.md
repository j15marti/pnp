## Scoped theorem
Fix a guard and explicit disjoint blocks D_i with alternatives labelled by original row IDs and occupancy vector v(a). Every native cover selects one alternative per block and has total XOR t. For native owner partitions this follows from one tuple per owner and all-ones occupancy on the other shores; conversely full parity and total mass imply exact cover for the unfiltered independent owner choice representation. Here only necessity used. Fix a paid linear projection pi:F2^d -> F2^r, L=2^r, projected target tau. Define f_i(z)=number of labelled alternatives with key z. Keep duplicate keys with their multiplicities; count relaxed tuples, NOT native covers. For character u, W_i(u)=sum_a (-1)^(u dot pi(v(a))). Store Z(u)=number of zero W_i and P(u)=product of all nonzero W_i (empty product 1).

For arbitrary acquired subset I of blocks, the exact complementary distribution c_I(s) counts choices of all blocks outside I with XOR s. Its transform is zero if Z minus number of zero factors indexed by I is positive; otherwise it equals P divided by the product of nonzero factors indexed by I. All divisions exact, including negative factors. Inverse WHT divides by L. This is proved by expanding the character of the sum, multiplying independent sums, and using orthogonality of characters: sum_u (-1)^(u dot z) equals L for z=0 and zero otherwise. Thus every joint choice on I with key k is impossible if c_I(tau xor k)=0. A single negative key excludes its entire fiber, without generating choices on I. It is a symbolic negative predicate, not a free enumeration of all attainable keys or all subsets I. Nonzero count cannot certify native YES.

Deletion of a labelled alternative with key z updates every W_i(u) by subtracting (-1)^(u dot z), removing its old contribution from P or Z and inserting new contribution. Exact zero-aware invariant handles factors changing sign or passing through zero. For fixed chart, explicit total initial alternatives M, g blocks, h queries I_j and total U deleted alternatives, indexed arithmetic operation bound O(L[(g+sum_j 1)r+g+U+sum_j |I_j|]+M+output/input). More explicitly initialization O(M+g L (r+1)); deletion O(L) per known option plus polynomial ID lookup; complement query O(L(r+|I|+1)), then each acquired concrete key query O(1) indexed after encoding. No inverse of zero. Every product magnitude <= product_i max(1,|D_i|) and transform intermediates <=L times that; bits O(r+g log(M+1)). Storage O(gL) coefficients plus option dictionary, one complement table at a time, logs and IDs. Schoolbook signed arithmetic suffices for deterministic polynomial bit time when r<=c log2 N, M,g and number of charts, queries, options and failed operations polynomial in original N with fixed exponents. Tape indexing simulation incurs polynomial overhead; on same run peak O(N+T), cumulative O(T(N+T)). Fixed chart across deletion-only passes: total U<=M, no spectral reset charge hidden. Edge deletion changes a factor only if the edge is an internal option of a fixed pair block; do not pretend arbitrary cross-block edges are represented by independent factors. Fresh chart/guard/partition pays full initialization. Cap each query before execution and return UNKNOWN at cap. This does not prove a polynomial number of useful charts or branches.

## Genuine hybrid and counterchecks
Pack disjoint pairs of owner groups using a paid deterministic greedy scan of retained edge density. A pair-factor alternative is an actual retained edge labelled by both source rows with XOR of their labels. Unpaired groups retain their options. Every cover survives because its selected rows are adjacent in every retained pair and the pairing is disjoint. Thus count relaxation is tightened, without increasing chart rank; at most R^2 explicit alternatives overall. Alternative global proof: suppose a genuine cover were lost; its selected edge in some block would have been excluded by a sound certificate, contradiction. Path imports of exact zero complement fibers are sound; reapply closure. Exact root count zero or empty required pair => NO. Pair packing does not assert global pair compatibility. Overlapping factors would double-use group choices and invalidate product independence.

Hostile theoretical controls: one factor with all nonzero vectors in F2^(r+1), target0, every rank<=r linear projection has nonzero kernel and hits target, although full target absent. This is an explicit exponential-size factor in r, not a superpolynomial lower bound or falsifier of every O(log N) chart. Two distinct alternatives with identical key yield count2; mod2 zero would be unsound. Equality/affine and matching terminals remain cheap independent bypasses; no claim to replace bounded-width exact DP or compact circuits.
