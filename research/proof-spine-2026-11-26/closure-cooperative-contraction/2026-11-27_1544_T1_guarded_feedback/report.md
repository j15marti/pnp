## Scoped budget theorem
For each acquired guard over r Boolean carriers, retain a fixed original-ID universe, M initial clauses, fixed learned width w, and at most q persistent trajectories. Let K=sum_{i=0}^w 2^i binom(r,i). Accept distinct checked clauses only; at most K admissions. Each admission increases each trajectory's unsatisfied count by at most 1. Each strict flip decreases it by at least 1, so total flips <= q(M+K). Maintain exact k and signed t per row, update only incident rows when a variable flips; candidate scores are exact by summing row changes. One complete candidate sweep O(L+r) indexed operations for L literal occurrences; bit/index arithmetic polynomial. Persistent tested pair/pivot dictionary gives <= O(r(M+K)^2) candidate checks under a static clause universe (algorithm must use dictionary, not an assumption about prior measured runs). Failed candidate checks, admissions, maps, scheduling and verification are polynomial for polynomially acquired fixed guard family and fixed w. No restart/credit reset. This bounds feedback, not acquiring productive guards or the total exhaustive tree. Guard import only from checked F AND G entails C, lift to F entails NOT G OR C; direct import under H only if H entails G. Disjoint prefix guard coverage checked exactly, not favorable child. Countercontrols: residue collision, local minima not contradiction, incompatible guard mixing (x=y with opposite x pins), width4 regression.
