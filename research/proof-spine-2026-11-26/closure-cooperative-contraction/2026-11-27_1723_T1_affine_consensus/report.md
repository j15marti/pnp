## Lemma 1: affine consensus
For a fixed acquired chart of r Boolean coordinates, guard G and a pivot x, soundly propagate each of G AND x=0 and G AND x=1. Let L_b be the augmented row span of all acquired affine equations a.z=c under branch b. Every row of L_0 intersection L_1 holds under G, by exhaustive alternatives. Compute the intersection as (L_0^perp + L_1^perp)^perp over GF(2). If one branch is certified impossible, the entire surviving span holds; if both impossible, G is impossible. This is complete for shared consequences of the two acquired affine relaxations, not for all logical implications of the original residual. Extraction from explicit nonempty local tables uses span of row differences and nullspace, not global word enumeration. A fixed chart bank accepts independent equations only: at most r consistent rank increases and one terminal inconsistency. A deterministic pass tests at most 2r branches; at most r+1 passes including the final failed pass, if every nonfinal pass increases rank. Explicit polynomial tables and compact GF(2) equations (or polynomial-size ternary parity chains) give a fixed polynomial total bit cost per acquired guard, including failed probes, checking and representation. No expansion of the probe universe to new auxiliary variables or resetting rank credits. This bounds saturation, not universal productive guard discovery.
