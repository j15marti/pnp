## Theorem: certified factorwise translation stabilizer
For explicitly represented Boolean factors R_i on V_i, compute H_i={u:R_i+u=R_i}. This is a subgroup of the binary additive space: 0 acts identically, translations compose, each is its own inverse. Test every local translation by exact row membership, enumerate annihilator or obtain by GE. Intersect the lifted equations H_i^perp in chart coordinates to obtain H, adding homogeneous restrictions preserving the exact guard (for affine guard Lx=b, impose Lu=0). For nonaffine guards a separate stabilizer proof is needed. Complexity polynomial for arity a=O(log N) since exhaustive local translation and row comparisons cost at most 4^a times polynomial overhead; space, basis extraction and bit arithmetic charged. All original factors and maps must be acquired within budget.
For complete separation of factor regions L,R with shared boundary B, b=|B|, let K=projection_B(H), rank t. Both existential regional supports are K-invariant: translate any witness by a preimage in H, which preserves every relevant factor and the guard. Each response is constant on K-cosets. RREF gives canonical keys on b-t nonpivot coordinates. An exact dictionary of at most 2^(b-t) keys preserves joint support, not independent marginals. If both regional witnesses meet at one representative they glue since all shared coordinates are in B. For another word in the same coset lift a basis combination back through H and translate both witnesses. Conversely any global witness restricts to an accepted key. Regional H_L,H_R can also be computed independently; intersection of projected stabilizers is the projection of simultaneous regionwise stabilizers when interiors are disjoint and the guard is fully accounted for.
Fixed separator: one-time translation preprocessing P(N), plus 2^(b-t) times the actual regional solve, proof checking, indexing, witness and recomposition cost. With polynomially many paid separators, b-t<=c log2 N, polynomial per-query costs and representations, this gives fixed polynomial aggregate work and memory. The dictionary credit 2^(b-t)-checked distinct keys strictly decreases only when a previously unchecked key is verified; it does not pay for unlimited failed searches or reset across changing separators. Not a universal acquisition theorem. No claim for weighted counts unless weight invariance is checked.
Falsifiers: unverified local translation; omitted boundary edge/scope; guard not preserved; invalid response/witness; uncharged regional solving. Example barrier on a fixed abstract decomposition: two equal-chain regions on all b boundary variables, with one disequality in the second. Their common translation stabilizer has rank1; effective width b-1 despite only two support words per region and an easy affine contradiction. This is not native hardness. It shows fixed decomposition orbit rank alone cannot establish universal logarithmic effective width.
