## 2. Summable residual theorem (proved, conditional admission)
Let an algorithm have one exact current residual, polynomial-size representation and provenance in original N, and an acquired positive integer measure m_j bounded by poly(N) that counts remaining free Boolean variables (or a native tuple count bounding them). Every nonterminal accepted operation is an equivalence preserving both labels and actual witness lifts, not a necessary relaxation. All attempts at a round, including unsuccessful discovery, table acquisition, verification, arithmetic bits, reclosure, canonicalization, access, cache operations, and provenance maintenance, cost at most P(N)m_j^a for fixed polynomial P and fixed a>=1. No free global SAT/equivalence oracle. Charge reconstruction by the same bound or a fixed polynomial separately. A fixed-clock partial portfolio may return OPEN; universal admission is a separate premise.
Suppose m_(j+1)<=rho*m_j+e_j, fixed rational 0<rho<1. Then induction gives m_k<=rho^k*m_0+sum_(j=0)^(k-1) rho^(k-1-j)e_j. Two sufficient uniform defect hypotheses:
(A) e_j<=C log_2 N. Above m>=2C log_2 N/(1-rho), m'<=theta*m, theta=(1+rho)/2<1. After O(log N) admitted rounds, stop with O(log N) free bits, solve exact kernel by enumeration in fixed polynomial time.
(B) e_j<=C m_j^alpha with fixed 0<=alpha<1. Above threshold m>=(2C/(1-rho))^(1/(1-alpha)), m'<=theta*m; constant-size exact terminal below threshold. Hence total accepted-round work <=P(N)m_0^a/(1-theta^a) plus final kernel and reconstruction, even as the formal infinite geometric series converges. All constants fixed uniformly, not fitted to each instance. Direct proof uses decreasing geometric majorant. Minimal-counterexample proof under universal admission chooses least m above terminal threshold; its acquired exact child has strictly smaller m, hence decides by induction, lifting gives contradiction to failure. This proves termination only under universal productive-step hypothesis.
For simultaneously evaluated disjoint owned local regions of sizes s_i with sum s_i<=C0 m and local total work <=P(N)s_i^a, sum s_i^a<=(sum s_i)^a gives same aggregate bound. A local enumerative kernel with b_i<=c log_2 s_i free bits costs at most s_i^c tests and can be included in a fixed a. Fixed original-N logarithmic kernels may instead be charged through P(N) with polynomial number of local calls. Every candidate/failed route must be included. Overlapping regions require a paid ownership/composition proof; otherwise do not use this additive bound. This theorem concerns a SINGLE exact successor or explicitly additive local operations, not arbitrary branch trees: dependent recursive children need C02-01's charged sum-of-powered-measures or an exact joint summary. Short parallel span alone insufficient.
Useful structural sufficient criterion: a polynomially acquired set of simultaneously composable disjoint regions contains at least delta*m mass; their replacements consume at most (1-epsilon) times that mass plus C m^alpha new free variables. Unmodified interior stays. Then rho=1-delta*epsilon and (B) applies. This permits many distinct contractions and growing but sublinear absolute interface defects. It does not establish acquisition, disjointness, or uniform density. Lipton--Tarjan balanced planar separators alone give O(sqrt m) boundary VERTICES, not an O(sqrt m)-bit-work exact compatibility oracle: explicit masks can require 2^O(sqrt m) work. No inference from geometric shrinking alone.

## 4. Core-density padding barrier (proved)
Convergence to a small FRACTION of the input is not the same as convergence to a log-size or otherwise tractable residual. Fix any epsilon>0. Pick fixed integer h>=1 with 1/(h+1)<=epsilon. For arbitrary native P3DM input with m>=1 triples inflate all tuples d=h*ceil(log_3 m) times (compute ceiling by integer multiplication, handle m=0 directly). M=m 3^d lies in [m^(h+1),3^h m^(h+1)]. The reduction is uniform polynomial in original encoded length for fixed h, including all fresh owner IDs and planarity-preserving construction. Each resulting instance has degree-two signed quotient at most m<=M^(1/(h+1))<=M^epsilon<=poly(N)^epsilon (in particular M<=N' for explicit new encoding). If the signed graph is contradictory it immediately rejects; to state the promise uniformly use its graph-component count before parity consistency, always <=m. Exact solution equivalence established above. Thus the native P3DM promise class of inputs with degree-two graph component count <=M^epsilon is NP-hard for every fixed positive epsilon, by reduction from the published broad source. No range-membership recognition or narrower unrelated source hardness required. This is NOT an unconditional lower bound on decision time; it says a polynomial solver for that promise would already imply P=NP. It directly exposes why arbitrary relative compression, even to an arbitrarily small polynomial power, cannot be asserted to establish tractability. Geometric levels contract rapidly yet stop at a potentially arbitrary original source core. Exponential brute-force in M^epsilon is not polynomial merely because the density tends to zero. In contrast, the acquired absolute O(log N) class bound really pays for enumeration. Independent C02-01 history-budget warning remains relevant for actual branching, distinct from this nonbranching native example.
