## Persistent-probe theorem
Fix a finite explicit CNF chart, guard and named n-variable set. For constant width w let K=sum_{i=0}^w 2^i binom(n,i). Enumerate canonical non-tautological candidate clauses. For each C test unit propagation under its negation (RUP); cache a failed nonconflicting closure, assignment array and cursor of installed rows. Appended clauses only, no deletions or unverified change of chart. Old derived facts remain consequences; process new rows, then incidence lists of newly false literals until closed or conflict. Every possible newly unit/conflicting old row must contain a newly false literal. Thus closure remains exact, by induction and confluence of unit propagation. A conflict licenses C; a nonconflicting closure is not a SAT witness. Fresh and persistent arms use identical candidate order, root checks and sweep logic. Every probe assignment changes from unassigned at most once. Each clause is inspected once at insertion for each retained probe and at most its width times on false-literal triggers. Each inspection costs at most its length plus constant. Let Fmax contain original rows plus at most K admissions. Total indexed discovery cost O(K*(n+sum_{r in Fmax}(1+|r|)^2)+K^2), including failed and successful scans, metadata, at most K+1 sweeps and root checks. Memory O(Kn+L+wK+K w) indexed entries where L is initial literal count, plus proof/index metadata. Deterministic array/trie (or slower linear lookup) and bounded binary identifiers provide polynomial bit overhead. Independent full-scan RUP replay polynomial too. If original chart acquisition and all mapping costs are polynomial in original N, this fixed-width lane is polynomial; no claim of polynomial chart acquisition on arbitrary sources, semantic progress after saturation, or permission to replenish credit on new guards. CEGAR correction: monotone verified clause admission uses one of K credits; persistent failed states are sparse correction consumers. Falsifiers: deletion, changed guard, unverified chart alias, non-UP contradiction requirement. No SAT oracle, no inference from timeouts. This is not a universal UCAC theorem.

## Growing restricted stall and source-only cross-scope escape
Generate nonzero vectors of F2^d, each unordered pair lies on unique triple {a,b,a xor b}, each triple encoded by opposite 3-clauses. For d>=3, n=2^d-1, m=n(n-1)/6. Two assigned points of same polarity force at most the third of their unique triple to opposite polarity; no other triple shares two points with this triple. Thus UP does not conflict under any <=2 pins, and root width-two RUP has zero admissions. Every single NAE triple has full affine hull and full binary projections. This does not show a global affine or all-lane fixed point. Independently recognize opposite-clause triples, consistent signed gauge by graph traversal, and pair cover. For normalized y_i in {-1,+1} each triple implies y_a*y_b+y_a*y_c+y_b*y_c=-1. Sum all rows: (sum y_i)^2=n-2m=n(4-n)/3<0 for n>4. This is an elementary counting obstruction for coherent signed Steiner triple systems, not a claim of novelty or arbitrary native extraction. Same-scope hulls reveal no contradiction; cross-scope quadratic cancellation does.
