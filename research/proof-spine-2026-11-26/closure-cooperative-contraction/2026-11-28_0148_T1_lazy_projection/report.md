## Scope and proof

For one complete Boolean interior bucket, F=E AND all (v OR A_i) AND all (!v OR B_j). Begin with E and no correction clauses. Refuting any strengthened relaxation E AND S, where S consists of valid pair resolvents, refutes F. Given a relaxed model, if no positive/negative tails are simultaneously false, choose a compatible v and verify the original. Otherwise take a false A_i and false B_j and add A_i OR B_j. This is non-tautological and not already active because the relaxed model satisfies all active clauses. Each admission consumes one unused parent-pair credit. At most p*q corrections and p*q+1 solves for a fixed bucket; parent scan, correction verification and represented formula size polynomial in the explicit chart length. No enumeration of 2^boundary-width words necessary for this mechanism. Repeated bucket changes, branch credits, and the aggregate cost of all relaxed solves remain separate obligations. Standard Davis-Putnam elimination and lazy constraint generation, not a universal completeness claim. Small NO/YES controls required respectively two/one corrections.
