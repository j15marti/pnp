## Theorem: all fixed-k group-pin signed supports with a paid full-run ledger
Relax Boolean feasibility to signed integer equations Ax=b (not nonnegative ILP). Compute Smith decomposition D=UAV, U,V integral unimodular, D diagonal of rank r. If a nonzero diagonal fails divisibility of (Ub)_i or a zero row has nonzero (Ub)_i, refute. Otherwise set z_i=(Ub)_i/D_ii for i<r, z_i=0 after r (zero-based), a=Vz, K=V[:,r:]. Exactly all signed integer solutions are a+K Z^(R-r). The equality follows by the bijection x=Vz, not by rational Gaussian elimination alone.
For a subset J of groups, let I be ALL their coordinate indices, and e a joint one-hot word choosing one retained row in each group. Then an integer extension exists iff
e-a_I belongs to K_I Z^(R-r).
Acquire column Hermite basis H of that projected lattice once per J. Test all attainable local row choices (not all Boolean assignments to I) using exact rational coordinates relative to the independent columns of H. Rational coordinates must exist and be integral. A negative test can be independently verified without trusting positive HNF generation: choose a left inverse L on independent rows, check H L K_I=K_I and L K_I integral. If H L(e-a_I) differs from e-a_I or L(e-a_I) has a noninteger coordinate, the pin is impossible. Empty rank handled by testing e=a_I. Check U A V=D, unimodularity and source row IDs. Full equality of the projected lattice follows from a correct HNF; the containment checks alone suffice for safe deletion.
For each row j in any tested J, if no joint one-hot word containing j passes the test, delete j. This is stronger than ordinary coordinate pin x_j=1: the latter permits other signed coordinates of the same group to cancel. Every actual cover would give a passing joint word, since its remaining coordinates are integers. Therefore a first lost cover is impossible. An empty group proves NO. Positive tests only indicate relaxation feasibility.
Independent constructive route B: delete all columns I from A, acquire column lattice of A_complement once, then query whether b - sum of the selected columns belongs to that lattice. This is equivalent by splitting Ax into I and complement; no global normal form required. It is the independently executed reconstruction used in the audit. Each method can choose the cheaper acquisition route.
Combine with exact rational support elimination C02-24: after a verified dual deletes rows, rebuild integer lattice and group pins on that restricted row universe; after integer deletions, rerun rational support and forced overlap closure. A singleton group forces that occupancy; incompatible rows in other groups are impossible. Never enlarge row universe or transfer guards. Every productive iteration removes at least one original row. Reclose before each import. The prototype does not re-run full original tuple-level matching GAC after each row deletion, only forced row-overlap closure and the rational/lattice passes.
