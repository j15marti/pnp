## Lemma A: affine orbit fusion
Let F be any Boolean feasible relation invariant under translation by a known vector u. Let G={x:Lx=b} be a checked forbidden guard, rank k. F disjoint from G implies F disjoint from G+u={x:Lx=b+Lu}. If v=Lu=0 this is the same guard. Otherwise pick j with v_j=1. The union G U (G+u) is EXACTLY the affine system (L_i+v_i L_j)x=b_i+v_i b_j for i != j. Proof: these equations say Lx+b is either 0 or v; conversely both syndromes satisfy the equations. For independent rows rank k-1, so one guard equation is removed and one degree of its Boolean indicator is saved. Even inconsistent/redundant equations are safe by the displayed equivalence (rank statement assumes nonempty independent row form). Complement u=all ones is a symmetry of all original E/B constraints, and of their exact existential projections; arbitrary base guards may break this symmetry and must be checked separately. The union of two parallel cosets is affine over GF(2), not over arbitrary fields. Exact finite audit: 400 random small linear systems, 26302 pointwise checks, including redundant/inconsistent systems.
If k>=2 and G fixes k independent coordinates, the minimum nonzero degree of a Boolean function vanishing on the remainder after exclusion of a single guard is k (indicator). For its two disjoint parallel complement translates, the union has codimension k-1 and minimum degree k-1. Proof: invertible affine coordinates preserve degree both directions. A function supported on the forbidden codimension-h flat has unique multilinear polynomial equal to its h-factor indicator times a function of free coordinates; if nonzero, degree >=h. This is a refusal for that relaxation and degree, not for the original formula or arbitrary proof systems.

## Lemma B: complete fixed-degree consequence extraction on acquired affine remainders
Let a relaxation R containing all feasible words be an explicitly acquired union of q affine cells in r common Boolean coordinates. Fix constant degree d. D=sum_{i=0}^d binom(r,i). For each cell a+span(v1,...,vk), form degree-d monomial evaluation rows at a+sum_{i in I}v_i for |I|<=d. The span of those rows equals the span of evaluations at ALL cell points. Proof: each coordinate of the evaluation row is a polynomial of degree <=d in the k free coefficients. Its coefficient indexed by J is the GF2 sum of evaluations at all subsets I of J, hence lies in the span of sampled rows. Every evaluation is the corresponding sum of these coefficients, so the reverse inclusion follows. The nullspace of the aggregate row matrix is exactly all degree<=d vanishing polynomials on R. Deterministic dense GE O(q D^3 poly(r,log N)) bit work and polynomial memory, including generating rows, provided q,r polynomial in original N and d fixed. Complement of a fixed number h of known affine guards in an affine carrier admits <=max(1,r)^h affine cells by partition at the first failed independent equation, discarding inconsistent cells by GE. This costs polynomial for fixed h, not unbounded accumulation. Symmetric guards may be fused before partition. A nonzero identity gives an independent rank credit only on the fixed monomial universe, with all guard and parent proofs retained. Arbitrary nonlinear constraints or unacquired cells are not silently enumerated. 200 seeded affine image tests x four degrees = 800 exact rank comparisons against full evaluations.
