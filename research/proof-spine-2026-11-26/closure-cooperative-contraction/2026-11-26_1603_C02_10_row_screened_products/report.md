## 2. Exact representation and constructive proof
For fixed acquired factor order, let W_i be actual variables occurring on both processed and unprocessed sides. Let S_i be exact attained evaluations of W_i from processed factors. Maintain partition into pairwise disjoint nonempty coordinate blocks U_j of W_i and exact supports C_j, each represented as a union of affine cosets a+H over GF2. Invariant S_i=Cartesian product_j union C_j, with every point extendible to a prefix witness. This is stronger than disjoint marginal projection: independence is constructed by induction, never inferred from marginal cardinalities or pairwise independence. Empty frontier starts with the singleton empty assignment. If any component becomes empty, NO.

Next exact factor scope X, allowed actual row r on X, new frontier Z. Touch every block meeting X; unchanged blocks are disjoint from X and cannot lose an old variable at this step unless that variable is in X. Let U be union of touched blocks and Y=(U union X) intersect Z. For each touched block U_j and row r compute
    D_(j,r) = projection_(U_j intersect Y) {x in union C_j: x restricted to U_j intersect X equals r there}.
Gaussian restriction of each coset followed by linear projection gives one exact coset or empty. Normalize locally by canonical offset/basis, containment deletion and merging two distinct cosets with identical direction (their union over GF2 equals the enlarged coset). This never adds words. If D_(j,r) empty for any touched j, discard r. Otherwise compatible outputs for this row are exactly the direct Cartesian product of D_(j,r), plus new retained coordinates (X minus U) intersect Y fixed to r. Variables of different touched blocks disjoint, and actual r fixes their entire overlap with the new factor, so independent old witnesses can be glued. No extra equality across blocks is silently lost. Union these products over actual rows, normalize globally, and put the result in one new block on Y (discard Y empty once feasibility established). Keep untouched blocks separately. Every new prefix witness restricts to an old product and one actual row, so converse holds. No need to find minimum product partition or affine cover. This is row-first restriction before multiplication; old product across all blocks is never materialized. When factor touches no block the empty Cartesian product is the singleton, and a nonempty row yields its retained fixed values.

Backward reconstruction: given actual target frontier z at current layer, check candidate r agrees on retained X. For each touched old block independently seek one old coset point satisfying shared row coordinates and retained target coordinates. Two explicit affine linear restrictions solved by Gaussian; if both prescribe a retained overlapping coordinate, their values must agree. Scan old records until a compatible point is found; all independent points can be united, and unchanged blocks take their same target values. At least one r exists by forward invariant. Store actual row witness and continue backwards. Even when final normalization merged records, find an actual predecessor; no per-point table or SAT oracle. Verify all original integer owner obligations, IDs and literal pins. Contradiction lane: first lost prefix of an original satisfying assignment impossible by exact row restriction, independent lift and projection; false YES would yield a verified invalid original witness, excluded by gluing and final checks. Cap failure is OPEN not NO.
