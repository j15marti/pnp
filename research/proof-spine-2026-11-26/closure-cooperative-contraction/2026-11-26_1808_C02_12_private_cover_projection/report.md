## A. Private-block projection theorem
Let the current exact residual have d binary coordinates. Acquire a k-dimensional subspace K on which every row of all untouched events vanishes. Extend a basis to an invertible coordinate chart (t,y), t in GF(2)^k, y in GF(2)^(d-k). q events touch K, each having rows A_i t+B_i y=b_i. The other events are exterior, independent of t. Row-reduce the private columns separately in each touched event. Obtain r_i independent private equations L_i t=h_i(y), and exterior-only trigger equations G_i(y)=g_i. For fixed y the event fiber is empty if a trigger fails, and otherwise a coset of codimension r_i in K.

**Essential-cover bound.** Suppose at most q affine fibers cover K. Remove redundant fibers to obtain an inclusion-minimal subcover of size a<=q. For each member F_i choose a point x_i in F_i but in no other subcover member. For every j!=i select one equation of F_j violated at x_i. Its opposite equation defines a hyperplane containing x_i and disjoint from F_j. The intersection of these a-1 hyperplanes is nonempty (contains x_i), has codimension at most a-1, and is disjoint from all other members. Since the subcover covers K, this intersection lies in F_i. Therefore codim(F_i)<=a-1<=q-1. This is an elementary direct argument, also valid as a contradiction against an essential high-rank fiber. Hence for every y one may ignore r_i>q-1 when testing whether the union covers K. DO NOT drop those original events in witness replay. The bound is tight: the disjoint fibers x1=0; x1=1,x2=0; ...; x1=...=x_(q-1)=1 cover GF(2)^(q-1) irredundantly, with maximal codimension q-1.

Let s<=q events remain after screening, let L stack their independent private rows, and let delta = rows(L)-rank(L), the dependency dimension. For each nonempty subfamily J of screened events, stack L_J and compute a basis Q_J of the left kernel. All selected fibers are nonempty iff all their exterior triggers hold. Their stacked RHS h_J(y) determines the syndrome Q_J h_J(y). Two RHS vectors with the same syndrome differ by an element of im(L_J); common translation of t bijectively maps their full unions. Thus cover status depends only on the dependency syndrome, not on private points. Enumerate the 2^delta_J syndrome patterns and acquire representative RHS by Gaussian elimination. For each, determine if the union covers K by inclusion-exclusion:
|union F_i| = sum_(empty != I subset J) (-1)^(|I|+1) |intersection_(i in I) F_i|.
Each intersection size is zero for inconsistent equations, else 2^(k-rank(stack_I)). All integers have O(k+q) bits. This is paid local affine counting, not an unrestricted counting oracle.
For every covering pattern sigma, output one affine forbidden event on y containing the selected exterior triggers and Q_J h_J(y)=sigma. Discard inconsistent outputs, deduplicate exact canonical rows. A rank-zero nonempty output forbids all y. If no touched events, eliminate the unconstrained directions directly. Proof of projection equivalence: if a compiled event holds, that subfamily covers K, so no private extension avoids all original events. Conversely if there is no extension, choose a minimal covering subfamily, which survives the rank screen and is represented by its actual syndrome and triggers. Thus the resulting conjunction of complements of affine events on y is exactly the existential projection of the old relation, with no loss of guard correlation. Untouched events are copied unchanged in their new coordinates.

Bound: delta_J<=delta by monotonicity of row-dependency dimension under adding rows. There are <=(2^s-1)2^delta candidate patterns and at most 3^s 2^delta inclusion-exclusion subset tests (looser bound 2^(2s+delta)). Per test is polynomial bit work in explicit representation. Since delta<=s(q-1), q<=C sqrt(log2 N) for fixed C suffices for polynomial local compilation regardless of k. More generally q=O(log N) and delta=O(log N) suffice. k may be as large as the polynomial-size residual dimension. The actual output rows, triggers, rank bases, failed candidates and all intermediate patterns must be charged.

**Exact replay.** For a retained y satisfying the compiled relation, compute uncovered count using ALL q original fibers, including screened ones, by inclusion-exclusion. Count is positive. For each successive private bit, restrict it to zero, compute uncovered count in that half by the same method, select zero if positive and otherwise select one. Every chosen branch retains an uncovered point. After k bits the extension is verified against all original rows. At most (k+1)(2^q-1) intersection evaluations plus polynomial linear algebra and verification. Reconstruct backwards along all accepted transforms and then through original native adapter. This is constructive, deterministic and not a free SAT oracle. Positive count is used only locally.

## B. Discovery and immutable full-run tariff
For a proposed event set S, compute K as the simultaneous nullspace of every row outside S and a complement by Gaussian elimination. The actual touched set may be smaller than S; recompute and verify it. For fixed q, enumerate all subsets of size <=q in polynomial time (or use the coordinate support lists employed in the experiment). For growing q, admission requires a separately polynomially acquired candidate list; enumerating all q-subsets is not automatically polynomial.
Independent complementary one-direction acquisition uses row-dependency signatures: flatten event rows, compute a basis of the dependency kernel, form a signature per row. A nonzero evaluation mask corresponds to a realizable direction iff the XOR of its signatures is zero. Enumerate nonzero local masks in low-rank events (2^r-1 each). Extend C01-11's two/three-touch scan to four-touch by pair/pair signature matching with distinct event IDs; also retain one-, two-, and three-touch cases. Every support of size <=4 consisting entirely of eligible ranks occurs, provided the scan is completed. Enumerating duplicate/collision matches can take O(L^4) for L mask entries, not guaranteed O(L^2); all are paid. These sets are candidate regions for the exact nullspace acquisition, not assumed independent variables. Maximal private-block choice can fail the tariff even if a smaller choice would pass; no universal progress from discovery alone.

Fix immutable polynomial B>=0 and initial R0,d0. Let R be total written independent equation rows of the normalized forbidden-event list and d its parameter dimension (unused directions counted). Reject attempted projection unless output R'<=R+B k. Rank-one forbidden equations are equivalent to a single forced affine equation with opposite RHS; closing them by Gaussian substitution cannot increase R and strictly decreases d whenever nontrivial. Phi'-Phi <= -(B+1)k+Bk=-k.
Hence sum of eliminated dimensions <=d0; R<=R0+B d0, at most d0 productive rounds. This generalizes C01-11's additive one-direction tariff to multi-direction contractions.
