## A. Constructive theorem
Partition one owner shore into blocks of at most two owners. For each block enumerate original allowed labeled choices, one tuple per owner. Reject internal overlaps. For each remaining choice P, delete covered owners and incident triples; if any of the three remaining two-shore bipartite projections lacks a perfect matching, reject P. Store occupancy word of other two shores and actual original tuple-ID witnesses. Matching is necessary for extension, not sufficient. Every native solution restricts to retained rows in every block. Conversely if one row per block XORs to all-ones in 2q coordinates, choose stored witnesses. Each other shore has q total selected incidences and q odd positive occupancies; hence every occupancy is one. Group shore covered exactly once by construction. Thus membership in the retained block sumset is equivalent to the native decision, although individual retained rows need not extend. This is a genuine adapter: matching changes exact decision-equivalent factors on which C02-19 contextual period contraction and exact quotient-key DP operate. Native filtering is completed before any quotient; no subsequent native query on arbitrary quotient words. Fixed-block product acquisition <=m^2 per chart (sum of products for disjoint owner pairs <=m^2), polynomial matching and verification. More generally any acquired partition with polynomial sum of candidate products works; logarithmic block size alone does NOT imply that bound.

## B. Complementary resource/contradiction theorem
From a failed complement matching obtain Hall H,Y in uncovered shores by alternating reachability, H larger than Y. Let B be all occupied owners of P and K=Y union B. For each original allowed t, w(t)=|t intersect K|-|t intersect H|>=0: if t meets H, either touches B or its projected neighbor lies in Y. Every exact cover has sum w=s=|K|-|H|. Same source guards must hold; verify each coefficient directly. For any current factor row r with a set of native lifts, store l_j(r)=minimum total native weight under certificate j over its lifts. These are separate coordinatewise minima, not a joint attaining witness. For each certificate j delete a row if l_j(r)+sum over other factors of min l_j > s_j. If all factors' minima sum exceeds s_j, NO. Every true exact cover survives because its own lifts bound the minima and sum to s_j. For a quotient map pi, transport by l'_j(y)=min_{pi(r)=y} l_j(r); for a merge, l'_j(z)=min_{r1+r2=z}(l_j(r1)+l_j(r2)) over retained pairs, then apply contextual filters. This remains a valid negative bound even if weight is NOT invariant on cosets. Proof by induction on images of any original cover, or contradiction to its exact occupancy conservation. If all quotients separately satisfy C02-19 exact support period hypotheses after filters, decision equivalence survives as well: filtering cannot invent a solution and every original solution retains an image. Arbitrary quotient only gives a necessary relaxation; no positive conclusion. Native witness reconstruction after weighted quotient not implemented; no multiplicity preservation. Exact weighted-budget decision in a generic problem requires a weighted period or additional lift proof (counterexample A={0:0,1:1}, B={0:0}, target1,budget0, quotient by 1 spuriously passes).
