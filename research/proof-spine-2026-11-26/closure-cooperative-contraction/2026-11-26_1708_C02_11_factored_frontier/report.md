## Theorem 1: exact nested frontier transformation
A typed node on coordinate scope U represents a set of full Boolean words on U. Nonempty affine leaf is a+H over GF2 on U; empty relation is a separate sentinel. Union children have identical scope; product children have disjoint scopes with union U. Empty scope singleton is multiplicative identity. All nodes immutable, DAG acyclic, syntactic hash-consing only; compare exact keys (not collision-prone hashes alone). A node's relation depends only on its stored content/scope, NOT source-history labels. Keep provenance via historical roots and actual original row witnesses; do not assume a merged node retains a universal fixed backpointer.
Define T_(M,r,Y)(S)=projection_Y{x in S:x restricted to M equals r}, with M,Y subsets of U, r an actual assignment on M. For affine leaf Gaussian elimination gives inconsistency or exact affine coset; for union apply to each child; for disjoint product apply to each child with M,Y restricted to child scope. These identities follow by existential projection distributing over union and restriction/projection distributing over genuinely disjoint products. Empty child makes product empty. A common immutable factor C of every alternative can be extracted by distributivity:
  union_i (C x D_i) = C x (union_i D_i).
This is valid only for disjoint scopes and the SAME exact C; equality of syntactic canonical IDs is sufficient but not necessary. A factor may itself have exponential support and arbitrary nested nonlinear structure. Exact affine normalization merges distinct same-direction cosets over GF2 or deletes contained cosets; no approximate hull. No need to expand any product of leaf choices. For an explicit nonempty relation R on disjoint X,Y, |R|=|proj_X R| |proj_Y R| iff R is their product, because R is contained in that product. Scan any proposed split in polynomial explicit-row work. For a single affine coset the analogous check is rank(H)=rank(proj_X H)+rank(proj_Y H). Not valid to multiply overlapping scopes or assume pairwise marginal independence: even parity on 3 bits has every pair full but 4 rather than 8 joint words.
A next exact factor on M with actual rows r and new frontier Z=(prefix vars union M) intersect suffix vars transforms old frontier root S on W to
  S' = union over r [T_(M intersect W,r,W intersect Z)(S) x singleton(r on Z minus W)].
Each retained old assignment has a genuine prefix witness by induction; actual r fixes all overlap, so witnesses glue. Conversely any satisfying prefix induces precisely an admitted row and old word. This yields exact forward support. No branch on a potentially enormous support or cover product. For reverse replay with full current frontier word z, scan actual r agreeing on M intersect Z, restrict previous root on (M union Z) intersect W to prescribed values, keeping its whole scope W. If nonempty choose one word by descending union to any nonempty child, product to each independent child, affine leaf to its offset. Existence follows from forward invariant, not from fixed representative backpointer. Keep row's actual original tuple witness and replay to empty start, verify all owner obligations and literal guards. If exact root empty at any stage return NO; caps return OPEN. Contradiction lane: choose first prefix losing a full satisfying witness, then the transform/gluing identity restores its restriction, contradiction. A spurious YES would imply a failed original witness check; exact backward gluing excludes this. No decision/counting equivalence: nondeterministic union may overlap; summing cardinalities not justified.

## Theorem 3: explicit infinite family defeating flat screened products
Same abstract hostile Boolean family from C02-10: k local factors R_i on (x_i,y_i,z_i), R_i={0,1} x NAND(y_i,z_i), NAND relation={(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)}; one explicit one-hot factor on all x_i; then full unary factors on every y_i,z_i to keep them in frontier. Initial factors exact independent blocks. At one-hot join, every actual row fixes x_i, and x variables are forgotten at this layer. Each row has EXACTLY the same output product NAND^k. Its attained support size is 3^k. Every affine coset contained in NAND^k projects onto at most 2 points per pair (projection affine cardinality power of two, cannot have 4), so contains at most 2^k points. Thus any explicit flat exact affine cover has >=ceil((3/2)^k) records even with overlapping records. C02-10 row-first enumeration of two local cover choices per block constructs k*2^k candidates. In contrast, directly certify split {x_i} vs {y_i,z_i} by six=2*3 actual rows (no solution oracle); store separate full-x affine leaf and two-record NAND union. Condition every x_i and forget it, retaining identical immutable NAND factors for every one-hot row. The common product needs O(k) active nodes/arcs. Each of k explicit rows can be screened by O(k) factor visits; identical resulting product is shared. Subsequent unary steps update at most one block each; scanning k child pointers per step plus reverse replay costs O(k^2) primitive operations and O(k^2) total stored history arcs, apart from polynomial bit operations and row/closure work. This is a constructive algorithm using explicit local split, not an assertion that arbitrary normalizers must discover it. Executed implementation instead recursively factors identical child IDs and normalizes local affine leaves, without supplied split. All phases can remain independent even when the previous chart incidence graph linked their scopes. Native realization of this abstract high-fan-in hostile family is NOT established. Existing native controls below are distinct, have exact source adapters, and are also tractable by earlier terminals.
Reciprocal series: sum k^p/(3/2)^k converges for every fixed p>=0 for the representation bound. For the explicit O(k^2) algorithm, normalized actual work on dyadic k has sum T(2^j)/2^(pj) convergent for sufficiently large fixed p (bit work polynomial in original explicit length). For quadratic primitive envelope, unweighted reciprocal also converges; hence even this convergence cannot distinguish polynomial work from exponential representation. Neither series establishes lower bound on unrestricted deciders. Pairwise-independent parity is the adversarial false split; independent set audit below checks it.
