## Exact terminal A: equal-space hole coalescence

Solve the linear system including every retained guard: x=x0+Kz; inconsistent system is NO. Each pair has affine observation map (a z+alpha,b z+beta), with homogeneous row space S. Rank zero: reject iff constant pair zero. Rank one: test its two words, producing a tautology, contradiction or one affine equation. Rank two: choose canonical basis of S, translate nonzero-pair restriction into exclusion of one of the four possible basis words. Intersect all masks having identical S. A mask of size zero is contradiction, one is a point, two a line, three a puncture, four a tautology. Replace point/line by exact affine equations and recompute GE; retain all remaining punctures and original IDs. Induction proves equivalence under the same guard, not just a relaxation. A consistent productive import cuts latent dimension at least one because a nontrivial linear form was constrained in current free coordinates. Thus at most initial nullity productive rounds and one final pass. All canonical comparisons, duplicate masks, unsuccessful tests, reconstruction and GE cost poly(N) with explicit arrays. A checker reruns the deterministic passes or checks the logged source IDs and row combinations. No ancestry assumption. No general semantic forced-information completeness.

For one reciprocal lift of each root plane (u,v), the children are (a,b+u),(b,a+v) with fresh independent private coordinates a,b. For parent coefficients (x,y) and target private values (s,t), the child coefficient pairs are (y+s,x),(x+t,y). Nonzero children exclude exactly (0,s) and (t,0) from parent words. At zero private target these coincide, otherwise two distinct exclusions leave exactly two allowed values, hence an affine constraint. Under arbitrary additional affine guards a lower rank can occur, but a formerly affine constraint cannot become nonlinear. This is an ancestry-free GE/mask realization of the independently checked T4 ancestry-mask identity; one lift with q zero-private roots leaves at most q rank-two observation spaces. Iterated explicit reciprocal forests also preserve the linear nullity (two new independent private rows per expanded owner, net two new columns); direct GE recognizes this without guessing the forest.
