## Lemma FPR4 (exact four-port replacement)
A region J contains indexed tuples, with all incident nonboundary owners private (no outside incident tuple); all private owners are obligations, including isolated ones if any. Boundary ports a,a',b,c have colors A,A,B,C, respectively. Each attainable mask R is a subset of the boundary owners covered by disjoint local tuples that cover every private owner once and respect every local literal guard. Acquire one witness for each feasible mask. For private counts pA,pB,pC and bits a,a',b,c (abusing owner names), every mask satisfies
pA+a+a'=pB+b=pC+c.
Empty support implies native NO. A nonempty support is always exactly realizable by a distinct-triple rainbow gadget of at most FOUR tuples, with new private owners, in a disk with cyclic port order a,b,a',c (or its reversal).
Classification proof: if b,c fixed, the number of selected A ports is fixed; thus support singleton or two choices exchanging a and a'. If b or c varies, both must vary together by conservation, hence pB=pC, b=c=q. Put d=pB-pA. Then a+a'=d+q; variation of q forces d=0 or 1. For d=0 support is a subset of {empty, abc, a'bc}. For d=1 support is a subset of {a,a',aa'bc}. Other cases already singleton/exchange. Exactly 26 distinct nonempty subfamilies arise over all feasible private counts (differences bounded by 2; direct finite enumeration corroborates).
Construction:
1. Singleton mask: for each selected port, force it with one tuple containing two fresh private owners of the other colors. Unselected ports receive no incidence. Empty mask uses zero tuples.
2. Fixed b,c and A exchange: private B*,C*, tuples (a,B*,C*) and (a',B*,C*) choose exactly one; force b,c individually when selected. At most 4 tuples.
3. d=0 support containing empty plus one or both triples: use a fresh B' and fresh A*,C*. Add wire tuples (b,A*,C*), (B',A*,C*), then (a,B',c) and/or (a',B',c) according to support. The private A*,C* force exactly one wire tuple, and private B' must be covered. Therefore the local coverage bit at b equals the core use of B'. The wire is equality, NOT complement. This prevents duplicate triples with outside even if the same three boundary owners already form a tuple. Core choices exclude one another by B',c. At most 4 tuples. If empty excluded, case 1 or 2 applies.
4. d=1 support with both possible q values: introduce private B*=p,C*=q'. Include singleton tuple (a,p,q') iff {a} allowed, and (a',p,q') iff {a'} allowed. Include BOTH tuples (a,p,c),(a',b,q') iff all-four allowed. A singleton consumes both private owners and conflicts with both all-four tuples; the latter two must be chosen together if neither singleton chosen. Thus exactly the desired support, at most 4 tuples. All triples have fresh private IDs, avoiding duplicates.
Planarity: cases 1/2 use disjoint local stars and a narrow two-terminal wire strip. Case 3 two core centers form the b'-center-c-center cycle with a,a' on opposite sides, then extend b' along the b strip. Case 4 draw the cycle t_a-p-t_a'-q'-t_a, with t_a west, t_a' east, p south, q' north. Put (a,p,c) southwest and (a',b,q') northeast; each connects its nearby A port, private vertex and nearby C or B port, outside this cycle. Outer port order is a,b,a',c. Delete unwanted tuples; planarity remains. No disk guarantee for other cyclic orders. For an arbitrary candidate region, one can also check planarity of the whole resulting incidence graph in polynomial time and decline if nonplanar; preserve fixed cyclic attachment where a later module requires it, rather than inferring its embedding from unconstrained planarity. Any guard on an old local tuple is discharged into the exact table, not forgotten or reused as a new pin. No cross-region nonlinear or affine guards admitted by this lemma.
Forward and reverse: outside choices interact only via the actual four occupancy bits. Replace the original local cover by gadget witness of the same mask; replace any gadget cover by stored original witness. Exact-one obligations and all literal guards remain satisfied. For any admitted mask, both old and new private counts plus the mask counts are constant across colors, so their difference is a common integer across colors; equal global part sizes are preserved. Distinctness is preserved by fresh private IDs. Store boundary IDs, all local witness IDs and reverse substitutions. Preserve explicit owner universe; a zero-degree owner must cause rejection, never be erased by constructing the owner list from live tuples alone. The replacement preserves existential decision and witness lift, not counts or all original witnesses.

## Full-run tariff FPR4-T
For any fixed c>0, inspect components after deleting any four owners of pattern A,A,B,C in the current incidence graph, with actual boundary exactly those four; also allow separately authenticated geometric candidate regions. If region has 5<=|J|<=floor(c log2 N), enumerate all its subsets (or use a sound fixed bounded exact backend); acquire exact support and witnesses, reject empty, otherwise replace by <=4 tuples if planar and permitted embedding. Candidate enumeration uses O(N^4) cuts and O(N) components per cut; if geometric candidate lists are polynomial, charge their actual acquisition. After each accepted replacement reapply polynomial certified exact-one, matching support, and affine consequences where available; preserve lifted guards, never assume full semantic forced closure is free. At stagnation reapply those and return OPEN unless an independent exact terminal decides. At most m_initial<=N accepted steps because active tuple count strictly decreases. No branches are sent to the global solver for the up to 3 alternative masks: the gadget represents all of them in one native residual. Each exact local kernel costs <=N^c poly(N), including failed acquisitions and candidate testing. Thus local lane T<=N^(c+6) P(N) for a fixed polynomial P(N) covering all per-candidate work, bit-model simulation, closure, and final reconstruction. One clock starts at original N; no replenishing the root budget on re-encoding. Active owner count <=3m after isolated-owner checks; fresh IDs and total history polynomial (at most O(N) successful substitutions, at most three witnesses each of O(log N) IDs for log kernels; constant bounded patch backend may have larger fixed constants). Peak S polynomial; cumulative memory <=(T+1)S on same serial execution. If all calls end at certified terminals then exact decision; otherwise partial lane only. Contradiction proof: first excess accepted replacement beyond m would require nondecreasing tuple count; every accepted replacement deletes at least one. A first excess failed query in a round would require a candidate outside the finite acquired list. Both contradict implementation; unlike unrestricted recursive ORs, mode enumeration is internal, not multiplicative. No semantic equivalence oracle, no count oracle, no preloaded witness.
