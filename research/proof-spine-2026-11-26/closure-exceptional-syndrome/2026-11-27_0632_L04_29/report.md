## 1. All-mincut acquisition theorem
For any connected undirected multigraph Q on b>=2 vertices with positive global edge mincut lambda, there are at most b(b-1)/2 distinct unordered global minimum cuts. Proof: a random uniform edge contraction process preserves any specified mincut through a stage with j vertices with conditional probability at least 1-2/j, since all contracted singleton degrees >=lambda and total edges >=j lambda/2. Thus that cut is the final two-vertex partition with probability at least 2/[b(b-1)]. Sum over disjoint output events. Randomness is used ONLY to prove the combinatorial bound, not in the deterministic algorithm.
Fix vertex 0 on the first side to avoid duplicate complements. Prefix-assign original vertices to sides; when both sides nonempty, add source/sink edges of capacity lambda+1 to all fixed vertices on their respective sides. A min s-t cut <=lambda iff an actual global mincut extends the prefix. If false, prune. If neither side has been excluded yet, retain the single all-first-side prefix at each depth, and reject the all-first-side leaf. Every other surviving prefix extends a leaf. At most O(b^3) visited prefixes including failed children (depth b times O(b^2) leaves). Global lambda is acquired by pairwise flows or standard mincut; in test subclass lambda=6 verified by pair flows capped6 plus degree6 upper bound. For fixed lambda=6 each extension query is capped7 integral augmentations on O(b^2) matrix entries, so O(b^5) elementary operations total. Store O(b^3) bits for explicit masks, polynomial. No all-subsets scan, no probabilistic decision.
This differs from pairwise mincut threshold: pairwise values alone do not identify nontrivial minimum cuts when every pair has the same value. Enumerate BOTH sides of each cut, group by cardinality d; for each d check whether all d-sized sets form a disjoint partition. Only polynomially many candidates; do not solve general exact cover of overlapping cuts for free. If a candidate passes, restore balanced cross rows and apply cycle-deficit; failure UNKNOWN.

## 2. Same-source hard-for-old-threshold family

Module construction explicitly: block i uses channels i mod4, i+1 mod4, i+2 mod4; its three heads are 3i+j; for each channel connect predecessor occurrence to its same-channel head or the cyclic next head of that triple. Thus each bipartite matching block has exactly two matchings, differing by a 3-cycle. Reference identity choice gives four cycles; every local cover has even cycle count >=2.
Connect modules by cubic prism on k+k vertices, edges of the two k-cycles and vertical matching. Each module uses distinct local tails 0,1,2, one per incident prism edge; exchange complete outgoing lists on the two tails for each prism edge. Exactly 3k disjoint transpositions. This produces n=24kr completed vertices, 8kr independent switches, 2^(8kr) covers. Every resulting cover has odd cycle count because 3k odd, and c>=2q-3k=k>=3 by exact row-exchange split/merge bound. Native instance has n+1 vertices. ## 3. Cut proof and exact acquisition without module metadata
Build quotient Q with one vertex per head triple and one directed arc per original tail, from its head triple to the block of its allowed successor heads. Three incoming/outgoing arcs per quotient vertex; symmetrize retaining each original arc as one undirected edge. Before exchanges each module is union of four directed channel cycles. Any nontrivial cut of a module splits at least three cycles: otherwise two monochromatic channels share a block and must be on the same side, and every block meets those channels. Hence each split module contributes >=3 outgoing cut arcs.
For a cut splitting t modules, one row exchange reduces the directed cut by at most one, and reduction requires both its modules split (verify using membership of exchanged tails and target blocks). For t=1, no reduction; t>=2, number of internal prism edges <=floor(3t/2) and <=1 if t=2. Therefore directed cut >=5 when t=2 and >=ceil(3t/2)>=5 when t>=3. If t=0, cut capacity equals prism cut, at least3: both horizontal cycles split =>>=4; exactly one split =>>=2 horizontal and >=1 vertical; neither split but opposite sides =>k>=3. A cut of global min capacity can split at most one module. If its side has exactly d=4r head groups (one full module size), no module can be split, since whole modules contribute multiples of d and a proper nonempty part is strictly between 0 and d. Thus the complete catalogue of d-sized minimum-cut sides consists precisely of original modules. Their disjoint partition is identified by size scan without knowing d, k, labeling, or the prism.
