If B_j+p=B_j for a nonzero joint period p, quotient every B_i and A by span(p). Original attainment implies quotient attainment. Conversely choose an original allowed target lifting the attained quotient target, then original local representatives of each quotient choice. Their total differs from that target by 0 or p.
