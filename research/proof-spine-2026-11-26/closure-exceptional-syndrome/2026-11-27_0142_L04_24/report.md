## 2. Subtour-to-wire fractional transfer theorem
For any simple cubic completed graph, a probability distribution over degree-two factors containing mandatory root wire r gives x_e and vertex row probabilities (singleton regions). Define transition capacities as expected paired edge incidences at each original vertex. For any wire subset X excluding r, at each cubic vertex place that vertex on majority side of its three incident wire memberships; mixed vertex has unique minority wire e. A factor selects exactly two distinct incident wires, so crossing contribution is precisely indicator minority wire selected, expected x_e. Pure vertex contributes zero. If the vertex majority split is nontrivial, each edge crossing that split is minority at exactly one endpoint, so transition cut capacity >= x(delta S). If all vertices majority outside X, every wire in X is minority at both ends, cut capacity =2 sum_{e in X}x_e >=2x_e for each optional demand; no singleton region can have all three wires in X. If all majority inside X, root wire is minority at both ends, giving capacity>=2 x_r=2. Thus x(delta S)>=2 for all nontrivial original vertex cuts implies every optional and mandatory rooted wire-flow cut in L04-23. Does not imply converse or integral feasibility. Maxflow/mincut provides polynomial verification; for exact integer counts scale by number of factors, O(k)-bit capacities in this family. No adding independent commodity demands.
