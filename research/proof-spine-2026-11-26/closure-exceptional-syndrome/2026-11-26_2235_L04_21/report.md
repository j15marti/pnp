## 1. Exact graph-cut representation
Let T be a nonempty exact or conservative upper set of partial pairings on four distinct original ports, each row one pair or two disjoint pairs. Assume a retained double row d={(a,b),(c,d)} exists; it need not be attained for a safe upper-chart version, but exact capacity is relative to the retained table. Let f(X)=max over rows of number of pairs crossing X. Define q=f({a,b}) in {0,1,2}; complement symmetry makes f({c,d})=q. Singletons and triples have f=1 because the double covers all ports; empty/full have 0. The two mixed 2/2 partitions have f=2 since the fixed double crosses twice and no row can cross more. Define M(X) as cut count for the fixed double; S(X)=min(|X|,4-|X|). Then for all 16 subsets
2 f(X) = (2-q) M(X) + q S(X).
Proof by cardinality and the three complementary two-versus-two partitions, just listed. Both summands graph-reducible: matching edges weight 2-q, one auxiliary center joined to each port with undirected edge of weight q, center side chosen to minimize. The expression holds for every retained table containing any double, including multiple doubles (then q=2). Consequently f is symmetric submodular, and the sum F over all blocks is exactly half an ordinary weighted cut after minimizing independent auxiliary centers. Submodular proof also directly follows from singleton/triple value1, pair value <=2, two pair-partitions value2; any nonnested pair of subsets checks by sizes. Stronger than general SFM: mincut of explicit O(b+w) vertices/edges for arbitrary threshold, not just <=1. Ports are original wires; repeated interblock edges retained as parallel gadget edges, no loss of original indices. Fix a target wire x inside and forbidden wires Z outside by connecting Z to sink with capacity > total ordinary weights. If x in Z impossible; otherwise solve exact mincut, then recover X by projection. For threshold1 only, integer scaled cap2; three augmenting flow units already exclude threshold, so stop at3 with sink edges cap3. At most three BFS augmentations; if final residual BFS exhausted, cut and flow certify optimum. For exact arbitrary threshold/full minimum use ordinary integral maxflow with total capacity O(b), polynomial. No SFM implementation needed.

## 2. Faster exact threshold-one quotient
Select one double row per block and form multigraph J0 on wires, edges the local pairs. Each wire occurs in precisely two blocks, so degree2, union of cycles. For every X, cut(J0,X)<=F(X) and is even. Thus F<=1 implies X is union of J0 components. Contract those to nodes. Each block's chosen double consists of two within-component pairs; if they lie in distinct components i,j, give auxiliary edge weight f(the two ports on i), 0..2; loops irrelevant; weight0 omitted. For any union of components this graph-cut cost equals F by direct local equality. Hence all F<=1 separations reduce to ordinary positive weighted graph cuts <=1. In a possibly disconnected positive graph, enumerate connected components and, for every weight1 edge, components after deleting that edge. Every generated set has cut <=1 (verify directly); for any arbitrary cut X with cost<=1 and any wire x in X, the component containing x in graph with its at most one crossing positive edge removed is a generated subset of X. Thus union of allowed generated subsets exactly equals union of all capacity<=1 cuts avoiding forbidden Z, although the algorithm does not explicitly list all such cuts. Can use linear bridge algorithm or repeated component scans, the latter executed. No requirement of unique double row for theorem; code checks uniqueness only to recognize earlier family.

## 3. General bounded-port acquisition without submodularity
For any nonempty retained charts of maximum boundary d, integer F<=k implies at most k positive-capacity blocks. Enumerate subsets H of <=k blocks, omit their pair-union edges from global equality graph J (all attainable/retained pair edges at other blocks). Components must be monochromatic for any cut with zero capacity outside H. There are at most dk components touching ports of H. Enumerate all 2^(dk) membership patterns on these components, forming X0 with all other comps outside; verify actual F(X0)<=k. Every other component individually has F=0 (it touches no omitted block and is closed under all other pair edges). For each valid pattern retain X0 and separately retain each such free component. For fixed forbidden Z and x, any witness X avoiding Z gives one H, pattern and either X0 containing x or free component containing x, both subsets of X; hence exact union of all possible excluded wires can be obtained by checking the polynomial-size family, without enumerating all unions. This is not exact representation of all cuts as individual candidates. Complexity O((b+1)^k 2^(dk) poly(N,L)) where L total chart encoding; fixed k,d polynomial, including k=1. For k>1 cut discovery alone is not a negative path certificate; old parity logic needs <=1 or additional exact constraints. For large d or k no universal polynomial claim.
