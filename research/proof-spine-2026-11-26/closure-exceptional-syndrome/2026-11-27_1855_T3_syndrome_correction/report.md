## 1. Syndrome sketch theorem
Let m explicit Boolean violation indicators e_i(x) (zero exactly if the corresponding row is satisfied), label i with distinct nonzero a_i in GF(2^s), 2^s>m. Use linear syndrome H e=(sum e_i mod2, sum e_i a_i, sum e_i a_i^3), total 1+2s bits. Two supports of weight<=2 with same sketch differ by a nonzero support of weight<=4 and zero syndrome. Odd size impossible by parity, size2 impossible by distinct labels. For four distinct labels a,b,c,d with a+b+c+d=0, the cube sum is (a+b)(a+c)(b+c), nonzero. Hence kernel minimum nonzero weight>=6 and injectivity on weight<=2. The proof is exact finite-field arithmetic, a special case of BCH-type moment checks. For general m, find irreducible polynomial degree s=ceil(log2(m+1)) by deterministic enumeration and trial division by monic degree<=s/2 polynomials; polynomial in m. Construct labels directly, no discrete-log primitive. Build sparse dictionary of 1+m+binom(m,2) supports or scan, polynomial work and memory. Decoding is valid only with a certified weight<=2 promise. Zero syndrome is necessary for solution, not sufficient. Nonzero syndrome refutes a candidate, not the entire source. Arbitrary dense collision and an attained false zero shown below.
Exact compression lead: on a fixed guard/carrier, represent each e_i as its unique squarefree Boolean ANF coefficient row p_i (bounded original width for this experiment). Let W=rowspan(P), R=rank(P). H P has rank h. Greedily add p_i only when outside current span; after all m candidates, exactly R-h additions suffice and are necessary for any linear full-span reconstruction from H P using scalar correction rows. Necessity by rank bound; sufficiency because all original rows reduce to zero against resulting basis. Store parent coefficient combinations and check both span inclusions. Then for every Boolean x, all original e_i recovered as linear combinations of sketch and corrections; in particular zeros equivalent. This proves exactness even with nonlinear local rows and original joint compatibility, not merely matching coordinate marginals or cardinality. Linear lower bound applies to reconstruction of full row span, not arbitrary nonlinear decoder or semantic zero-equivalence on actual errors. Fixed degree d and r explicit variables give D_d(r) monomials, dense rank computation and replay poly(N) for fixed d and polynomial explicit m,r; construction and evaluation paid, no claim this solves feasibility. For arbitrary guard restrict rows exactly (degree preserved under affine restriction, not generally nonlinear). This is the same mathematical principle as a linear basis compression of polynomial constraints, no novelty claim for ordinary coding or GE.
