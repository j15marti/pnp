## A. Deterministic guard mass and its limit
For any finite nonempty relaxation R contained in affine carrier A (containing all actual solutions) of a NO instance, suppose the explicitly acquired forbidden-row guards G_1..G_M cover A on that instance: each candidate assignment violates at least one retained original constraint. For native B4 each has at most ten forbidden consistent words (E and signs already closed), for a fixed width-w exact chart at most 2^w. The property holds equally for any probability distribution supported on R. Sum_i |R intersect G_i| >= |R|, so max mass >= |R|/M. In a uniform random word of R, one violated local guard is found with certainty by testing all constraints; a uniformly random guard index has joint hit probability >=1/M; an index with >=1/M mass exists. No claim that this discovers nonlocal contradictions or reduces the original constraint count: these guard exclusions restate known factors. With a paid exact counter, evaluate every M candidates against current exclusions, greedily take largest positive mass. For a fixed M, no productive index used twice, at most M updates. Independent multiplicative bound |R_t| <= 2^r (1-1/M)^t <=2^r exp(-t/M), M>=1, gives at most floor(M r ln2)+1 steps for zero if the same coverage/representation persists. This is a polynomial progress-count bound, not a numeric polynomial volume potential: |R| can be 2^r. For at most five stored exclusions, each evaluation has <=32 affine intersection counts (prefix pins also affine). If a fifth addition does not empty R, extending inclusion-exclusion to arbitrary list length does NOT preserve polynomiality. No free counting, chart compression or universal productivity implied.
Barrier: for arbitrary CNF F, G_i is unique falsifying subcube for each non-tautological clause (empty clause covers whole space); R is SAT(F). Exact counting is #SAT, even width3 #3SAT via parsimonious encodings. Append independent fresh contradiction z and not z to create known NO H; use only F guards. Intermediate remainder emptiness/nonemptiness still gives arbitrary UNSAT/SAT, with free z factor2 in count. Native version: compile H by pinned exact adapter, take signed E/edge carrier, exclude exactly local forbidden rows of B owners belonging to F, ignoring B rows of the two contradictory units. By compiler lift/projection, those retained constraints are feasible iff F is feasible (fresh auxiliary/slack variables for ignored constraints are free, common complement anchor consistent). Original full H is NO with explicit contradiction. Thus any unrestricted polynomial exact remainder nonemptiness oracle for all such certified partial ledgers implies P=NP, even under NO-only inputs. Does NOT prove a good algorithm must visit such ledgers, or establish P!=NP. Native transfer symbolic; no new native instance IDs registered or generated for this reduction.
