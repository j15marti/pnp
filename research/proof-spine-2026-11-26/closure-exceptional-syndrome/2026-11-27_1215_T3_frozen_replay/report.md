## FROZEN-RUP-CREDIT and conditional depth
Fix one explicit chart F with r variables, and let pool U consist of all non-tautological clauses of width <=k (including empty), k fixed. Size M=sum_{j=0}^k 2^j binom(r,j). For current D, each candidate C is eligible iff unit propagation on D and negation of C contradicts. Eligibility is monotone under adding clauses: a prior unit derivation can still be reproduced or encounters an earlier conflict. For a frozen batch evaluate all chosen candidates against the pre-batch D, then add only independently checked eligible clauses. Induction proves every learned clause entailed; empty is a contradiction. The full pool saturation either reaches empty or certifies no more eligible unadded clauses, relative to this exact rule and chart. It does NOT certify satisfiability on refusal. Each successful epoch decreases integer credit M-|D intersection U| by at least 1; at most M productive epochs and at most M(M+1) probes, including a final failed pass. One naive UP check uses <=r+1 scans of all clause literals; with binary IDs, input/trace size poly(N), and fixed k, total bit cost polynomial. A conservative polynomial can also be obtained with serial tape rescans of the polynomial-size explicit tables. The executed priority variant checks singleton guards first, pairs only if no failed singleton, skips already root-assigned variables and existing clauses. Its finite refusal means only that precise test rule stalled; no completeness asserted for general residuals. No recharge across a changed chart: a universal recurrence needs a separate bound on chart count, map size and all children.

For a supplied finite candidate list L, maximal frozen rounds define D_{t+1}=D_t union all clauses of L eligible in D_t. By monotonicity, any legal schedule restricted to L and the same base has its attained set after t rounds contained in D_t (induction). Thus earliest maximal rounds minimize number of frozen rounds to attain any target in this fixed list. Does not minimize arbitrary proof length or physical time, nor bound depth by O(log N). Proof discovery is charged; no free candidate list.
A supplied RUP proof can instead be verified at logarithmic Boolean depth without evaluating its dependency chain. For each addition C, provide distinct negated guard pins, ordered implications (literal, earlier reason clause index), final conflict clause. For each local reason check its reference precedes current layer, target literal occurs exactly once, and each other literal has an opposite assignment at a strictly earlier local rank (guard rank -1). For final conflict require all literals false at final rank. Check unique assigned variable, all ranges, clause syntax, complete reference coverage and presence of empty. Induction on local rank proves contradiction under negated C; induction on outer layer proves every addition from base. Given fixed-width padded binary record arrays (not an assertion about parsing arbitrary JSON in NC1), all these finite predicates can be implemented with uniform bounded-fan-in Boolean comparisons, parallel scans over records, balanced AND/OR reductions. For total explicit record bits P, size poly(N+P), depth O(log(N+P)), no advice or semantic oracle; all-pairs checks avoid free random-access hardware. A serial implementation remains polynomial in explicit P. If P is polynomial in original N, depth O(log N) and aggregate work polynomial. This is verification of a supplied chart/certificate, not proof discovery, and excludes full native projection preprocessing depth. It is related to GRIT/LRAT hint verification, not a claim of priority for proof hints. Exact integers and bit indices; no floating attention. Actual JS checker serial (not circuit implementation). Falsifiers: future clause reference; within-layer dependency; inconsistent duplicate assignment; missing unit reason; wrong chart, gauge or source; corrupted future reason rejected for both cases, forged empty rejected for RANDOM.

## Scoped refusal and algebraic bypass
For each fixed k>=0 take w=k+2 distinct Boolean variables, and the complete explicit CNF of all 2^w width-w clauses (one clause forbidding each total word), equivalently both XOR(x)=0 and XOR(x)=1 encoded with forbidden full words. UNSAT because every assignment falsifies a clause. For any consistent guard of size <=k, every unsatisfied surviving clause retains >=2 unassigned variables, so UP has neither units nor conflict. Thus even full width-k RUP pool has no new clause in the first epoch and stays fixed forever, yet exact Gaussian elimination on the two recognized parity rows refutes immediately. For explicit CNF, recognition by full-word coverage costs O(2^w w) plus sorting, polynomial in its own encoded length; no assumption of a free inverse chart. Executed w=4..9 with k=2, all fixed points, 32..162 probes, 528..83456 scans. These are secondary derived CNF chart controls, not claims about native source-only closure after an arbitrary compiler or about 3CNF width preservation; no new benchmark registry entry or unrestricted lower bound. Falsifies only universal progress of that fixed pool on general explicit charts. GE bypass and compact circuit controls refute decision-time transfer. No inference from growing w to superpolynomial unrestricted time.
