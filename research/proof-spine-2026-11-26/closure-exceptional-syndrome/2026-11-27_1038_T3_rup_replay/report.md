## Lemma SUPPORT-TO-RUP
For every explicit Boolean table R on w distinct coordinates, take every inclusion-minimal partial assignment a with no extension in R. Emit clause C_a = disjunction of negations of its pins. Every R-row satisfies every such clause. Every rejected total word contains a minimal forbidden assignment, so conjunction is exactly R. If current pins P have no support in R, some minimal forbidden a is contained in P; its clause conflicts. If P permits at least one row but variable v=b has no support, a minimal forbidden assignment contained in P union {v=b} must include v=b. Its clause is unit on P and forces v=1-b. Conversely every unit or conflict from these clauses is a genuine coordinate-support consequence. Iterating across all factors yields the identical least support closure (unless conflict) as Boolean unit propagation. This is local exactness, not global completeness. Ternary enumeration in numeric order finds minimal forbidden words by checking all one-pin deletions (each has lower numeric index and has been processed); at most 3^w words and 2^w rows, each test O(w) bits before indexing. Thus O(6^w poly(N)) for polynomially many factors, polynomial for fixed w or w<=c log2 N with fixed c. Do not assume such a factorization is universally acquired.

Given explicit finite support-closed binary refutation tree T with L nodes, postorder emit clause negating path decision guard g. At a conflict leaf UP under g conflicts by lemma. At an internal node, both child clauses are already entailed and are mutually conflicting under g (unit v and not-v); if a child clause was strengthened by dropping pins, it is either already conflicting or remains unit. Thus parent is RUP. Root empty clause. Every accepted RUP deletion of a guard literal is verified against current clause DB; may stop at first empty clause. At most L additions, O(L r) literal occurrences for r original residual coordinates. With M total base literal occurrences and stored explicit proof size P, naive sequential bit scanning and O(r) rounds per check gives a conservative poly(N+M+P+L) replay bound; e.g. O((N+M+P+L+1)^6) with scans and O(log)-bit index charges. Discovery of the tree, projection trace, and any minimization probes is separate. No free semantic oracle. For each fixed bounded-width/trace-size cap the replay is uniform. Depth alone does not bound L; polynomial depth permits exponential tree. Scope excludes arbitrary guard or semantic equivalence oracles.
