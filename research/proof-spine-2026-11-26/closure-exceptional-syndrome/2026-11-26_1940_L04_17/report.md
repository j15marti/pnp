## 2. Global odd-mincut acquisition: a nonoverlap theorem
Root at original vertex r. Let F be all nonempty subsets not containing r with exactly three undirected boundary edges. Since graph is 3-edge-connected, all these are global minimum cuts. F is laminar (disjoint or nested). Direct proof: suppose A,B cross with four nonempty sectors X=A∩B,Y=A\B,Z=B\A,W=V\(A∪B). Cut submodularity and posimodularity give 6>=d(X)+d(A∪B)+2e(Y,Z)>=6 and 6>=d(Y)+d(Z)+2e(X,W)>=6. Hence both opposite edge counts zero and all four sector cuts have size3. Remaining edges go around sector cycle X-Y-W-Z-X. The adjacent edge-count sums at each sector are3, so opposite sides have equal counts; d(A) is sum of two equal opposite sides, even, contradiction. Since r lies outside both cuts, every nonlaminar overlap would be crossing. Cubic graph has every nonroot singleton in F. Region tree by inclusion, maximal proper children partition each nonsingleton; root V with children including {r}. Number of proper regions <=2n-3 (laminar forest on n-1 singleton leaves). Original edge IDs retained even if quotient becomes multigraph; no invented adjacency.

Acquisition algorithm: for every pair of distinct original edges, delete them and DFS from r, verifying connectivity, find all bridges by lowlink. Each bridge gives exactly a 3-edge cut in original graph, oriented away from r; dedup actual vertex list. Conversely any cut of size3 appears when two of its edges deleted; third is bridge. Thus all and only cuts acquired. No supplied decomposition, no exponential enumeration. O(m²(n+m)) DFS scans plus explicit candidate list extraction, sorting, verification, dedup and tree construction; m=3n/2. Conservative O(n^5 log n) elementary bound including these extra costs. Verify actual boundary and laminar/partition conditions before any use. This is an elementary reconstruction of an established odd-mincut structural fact, not novelty claimed for the cut theorem.

## 3. Exact endpoint-free three-port retraction

If R singleton, zero-length internal path connects any distinct pair of its external edges. A global Hamiltonian path with both endpoints outside R must enter/exit R an even positive number of times. At most 3 boundary edges, each used at most once, hence exactly2. It restricts to a single spanning path in R. Therefore empty T_R forces at least one global endpoint in R.

For nonleaf R let children partition it, each with exactly three boundary edges. In an endpoint-free spanning traversal of R with specified external ports a,b, adjoin external terminals to those two edges. No global path endpoint is within any child, so every child has exactly two selected boundary edges and is a contiguous spanning path. Contract children to vertices, retain original multiedge IDs.
