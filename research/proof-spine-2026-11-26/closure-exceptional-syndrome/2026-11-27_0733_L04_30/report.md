## A. Constructive exact paired-transposition adapter
Acquire the connected components of the tail/head bipartite graph and verify C6 or K2. Each C6 has exactly two perfect matchings; every arc belongs to exactly one of the two. Arc pins delete rows locally, with conflicts refuted immediately. Singleton rows become fixed, all original IDs retained; this is exact degree/matching forced closure, not completeness of general Hamiltonian forced information. Select a baseline cover p, honoring pins; every free block has disjoint tail triple (a,b,c) ordered so that its alternate changes successors by right composition with (a b c), i.e. a->p(b), b->p(c), c->p(a). Disjoint blocks independent for covers, not for connectivity.
Insert a new vertex t on the baseline arc b->p(b), so P(b)=t and P(t)=p(b). Replace the rotation by the two DISJOINT transpositions of successor rows (a t) and (b c). Off means neither applied; on means both. On gives a->p(b), b->p(c), c->t->p(a); off gives the original baseline upon contraction. No dummy-only orbit: t has predecessor b or c and its successor is original. Thus each legal configuration contracts bijectively to one original cover with exactly the same cycle count. All free blocks disjoint, and every guard is absorbed in p or its pair list before construction.

## B. Global interlacement and deterministic delta-parity
P may consist of c0 cycles. Insert two fresh vertices per joining transposition, one into a selected first baseline cycle and one into a different baseline cycle. Use distinct new vertices for each join; multiple insertions on one arc form a chain. They are disjoint from all switch chords. Let J be the c0-1 joining transpositions. Right-composing P with all J merges baseline cycles into one cyclic permutation sigma. For every legal set T of switch transpositions (both or neither per block), sigma J T=P T since all transpositions are disjoint. The joining dummies contract on restoring J, leaving only the original switch subdivisions. All legal covers and their original successor maps are preserved.
Build binary alternating interlace matrix A on all chords, crossing iff their endpoints alternate along sigma. Cohn-Lempel equality, Traldi arXiv:0903.4405 Theorem 1, gives c(original cover)=1+nullity_F2 A[J union T]. Thus HC iff some principal submatrix indexed by all J plus whole switch pairs is nonsingular. This is stronger than global parity, a necessary rank bound, or any mincut partition.
To force J without an oracle, append one isolated zero row/column d_j for each j in J. Call augmented matrix B and D0 the set of all these new indices. Form the explicit linear delta-matroid M(B) twist D0, with feasible sets F=S symmetric-difference D0 where B[S] nonsingular (empty allowed). No nonsingular S contains a zero row, so all d_j belong to every F. Partition the ground set into the original switch pairs and the pairs {j,d_j}. A feasible F that is a union of pairs exists iff J subset S and S uses whole switch pairs. Equivalently the minimum number of pairs split by F is zero. This is exactly the linear delta-parity problem on a represented alternating GF(2) matrix, not a guessed determinant identity or a parity-of-count test. Geelen/Iwata/Murota JCTB88(2003)377-398, Theorem3.1 and Section5, gives deterministic augmenting path optimization in O(s^4) operations over the field (Section5.2; Section1 explicitly includes characteristic two with zero diagonal). Starting feasible set D0 is supplied. A positive optimum proves original NO; no exponential search or PIT/randomness needed. Their algorithm was read, not independently implemented or built this run. This is an exact reduction to a known polynomial theorem, pending independent review of adapter. All prior C6 families are now in a common polynomially decidable subclass, even if no useful mincut is found. Do not infer novel unrestricted Hamiltonicity or U_h solution.
