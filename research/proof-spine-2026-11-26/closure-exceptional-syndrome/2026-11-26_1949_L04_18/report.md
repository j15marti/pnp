## B. Channel-cover necessary condition and infinite prime UNSAT family
Take any partition into certified four-port blocks with the exact chart above. Wire all ports by original edges, no internal endpoint in ordinary block. Build group graph on two group nodes per block; each group has degree two via original external edges, hence components are cycles (parallel edges possible). Call their IDs channels. Form auxiliary graph K with channel vertices and one edge per original block between its two group channels, permitting loops/multiedges in general. For any hypothetical global spanning path on >1 block, every block uses >=1 external wire, and all used channel labels form a vertex cover of K. Along original path list external wires in order. Consecutive wires passing through an endpoint-free block belong to the same channel by A. Any label change occurs through a block containing an endpoint and follows its corresponding K edge. Even multiple visits to such a block add at most one distinct channel-connection edge. The graph of active labels is connected by this sequence and has <=2 distinct edges, hence <=3 vertices. Therefore tau(K)>3 implies NO. This is an upper constraint on number of active channels, not a charge for overlapping original regions. Enumerate every subset of <=3 channels and check cover; O(k³ b) basic operations (can test triples alone for k>=3 by monotonicity). No unknown row may be treated as impossible.

Construction for every m>=5: K=complete simple K_m, one H block per channel pair. For each channel order incident blocks cyclically by other channel index, connect outgoing group port to next group's incoming port. Quotient Q on blocks is simple 4-regular (two channels share only one block), connected. Every cut of Q crosses an even number of edges of each channel cycle. A nontrivial cut cannot cross only one channel: all other channels monochromatic and share pairwise blocks, so their colors agree and every block on the putatively split channel also has that color. Thus Q 4-edge connected. n=8 choose(m,2)=4m(m-1); tau(K_m)=m-1>3; exact source NO independent of trials.

Expanded cubic G has no nontrivial <=3 edge cut. H is connected, every edge in a 5-cycle: (2,3,4,9,7), (2,3,8,5,7), (3,4,9,6,8), (5,7,9,6,8). Thus any split H contributes >=2 internal crossing edges. If a cut splits >=2 blocks, >=4. If none, Q cut >=4. If one split block v and unsplit blocks on both sides, let a count cross edges between unsplit sides and b,c be Q edges from v to each side, b+c=4. Q cuts give a+b>=4 and a+c>=4, so a>=2; plus >=2 internal =>>=4. If all other blocks on one side, other side X subset of H has full cut size equal to Petersen cut d_P(X).
