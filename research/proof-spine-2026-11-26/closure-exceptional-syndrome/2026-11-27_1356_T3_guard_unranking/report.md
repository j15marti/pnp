## Exact affine-remainder rank/unrank theorem
Let A subset F2^r be explicitly defined by affine equations, containing all potential solutions of current authenticated context. Let G_1,...,G_k be explicitly defined affine guards certified impossible for that context, k<=5 fixed. For any prefix P (coordinate pins or additional affine equations),
R(P)=sum_{S subset [k]} (-1)^|S| count(A intersect P intersect intersect_{i in S} G_i).
Each term by exact Gaussian elimination: inconsistent ->0, consistent rank h ->2^(r-h). R(P) counts the surviving RELAXATION, not actual satisfying assignments. R(empty)=0 is sound contradiction only if all guard refutations and carrier containment checked. If R>0, a lexicographic witness can be obtained without enumerating affine cells. Given integer j in [0,R), for each coordinate i evaluate R(P and x_i=0). If j is smaller choose 0; otherwise choose 1 and subtract that count. The child sets disjointly partition the current remainder, so inductively the rank stays valid and the leaf is the unique j-th surviving word. Inverse ranking sums zero-child counts at each 1 bit. This gives exact lex rank/unrank and any nonempty remainder witness after at most r+1 count calls. Arbitrary affine bit extraction: if R(P and ell=b)=0, every actual solution must obey ell=1-b (unless R(P)=0 already terminal). All arithmetic uses <=r+O(k) bits even for intermediate signed sums. With q total explicit equations across A, guards and prefix, one GE count costs O(q r^2) bit operations with direct dense row XORs and ordinary indexing (plus conservative polynomial tape-access simulation); unranking costs O(2^k(q+r)r^3) bit work plus input and certification; polynomial when k fixed and chart dimensions/encoding polynomial in original N. At most 32 systems per count, no oracle or enumeration of 2^r words. Store one system, prefix, counts and proof references, polynomial space; serial cumulative memory bounded by (T+1)S on same execution. Discovery of guards and certificates separately charged. This is an exact known counting-to-unranking pattern (Jerrum/Valiant/Vazirani 1986 related general random generation); scoped constructive extension to shared affine-guard ledger, no claim of novelty in general sampling.

For exact uniform randomized selection of a relaxation word, choose uniform j with ceil(log2 R) independent fair bits, reject j>=R; conditional on success j uniform, each attempt succeeds >1/2 (R=1 direct), expected <2 trials, cap t fails with probability <=2^-t. No conclusion about uniform sampling of actual solutions or productive guard probability; deterministic j=0 is available but may not be productive. The first audit.js PRNG sampled via modulo (biased for non-power-of-two R); not claimed uniform. verify.js independently tests dense affine guards with seed 20261127, 120 cases, 10680 brute words, all 1922 valid ranks matched exact lex order, 81453 system counts and 833724 row-XOR events. Counts for empty guards, inconsistent systems and overlapping guards included. Exact source-specific first/last survivors after hybrid also checked. Falsifiers: omitted intersection, wrong signs, incompatible guard maps, rank out of range, R=0 with witness. The method does not prove a polynomial global potential: numeric volume is exponential, and repeated batches or residual changes can recharge local credits. Unrestricted k not covered.
