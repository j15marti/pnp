# AB01 | Affine-bijunctive SAT: exact deletion-track restoration and the one-rank-gap barrier
2026-11-25 11:32 Pacific | PROVED/EXACT scoped; COMPUTATIONAL controls; universal P versus NP OPEN. Independent review pending.

## Target, language and source compiler
Take explicitly encoded GF(2) equations Ax=b and ordinary 2-CNF. Require, in the stronger matching promise, that every variable occurs in at most one binary clause (different endpoint names, no shared endpoints). Invalid encodings rejected. NP membership by checking every affine row and clause. For arbitrary proper 3-CNF F, for every owner C=(l1 or l2 or l3) introduce fresh selectors s1,s2,s3 and private literal copies o1,o2,o3. Add affine equations s1+s2+s3=1 and oi=li(x); add binary clauses (not si or oi). The binary layer is literally a disjoint matching. A legal word selects at least one true literal in every owner; conversely select exactly one true literal per owner for a lift. This is an existential projection with potentially multiple lifts, not count preservation. The entire construction and both maps have polynomial bit cost; the sparse encoding uses O(n+m) names/rows and O((n+m)log(n+m+2)) bits. Thus the precise stronger language is NP-complete, even with affine rows of width <=3. No restricted DET-SYNC theorem is needed or transferred. Falsifiers: a spurious source projection, a missing true-source lift, an incorrect affine-basis restoration rule, or a claim that bounded missing witness rank implies uniformly cheap acquisition.

## Theorem A (PROVED): exact restoration by actual lifted witness bases
Let F be any such system, R be F with k specified obligations (clauses or affine equations) deleted, and W be an actual witness list for R. Suppose the feature vectors nu_k(w), consisting of all squarefree Boolean monomials of degree <=k including 1, for w in W span those of every legal word of R over GF(2). Then F is SAT iff at least one actual word in W satisfies every deleted obligation. Proof: if v is a model of F, choose a true literal in each deleted clause at v and the affine Boolean truth indicator of each deleted equation. Their product is a Boolean polynomial of degree <=k, equal to 1 at v, so its linear functional on nu_k is nonzero on some supplied basis word. That word satisfies every deleted obligation. The converse is direct verification. Empty relaxed relation needs its independently certified empty span, not a mere empty sample. For k=1, a complete actual affine witness basis suffices, because the falsification set of a clause is affine (all literals false), and a deleted affine equation has an affine falsification set. This is even valid for arbitrarily wide OR clauses. For k fixed the list has at most sum_{j=0}^k binom(v,j) words after Gaussian basis selection, where v is encoded variable count. Evaluate the original constraints on each listed word, not on arbitrary affine combinations of words. Work polynomial once complete actual basis acquisition is charged; no 2^k enumeration of literal choices is necessary in the evaluator. If R is empty, there is no extension. If basis completeness is unavailable, failure to restore means UNKNOWN.

An exact source-verified complete basis from ANY single deletion track decides the original system, both labels, regardless of all other tracks; only one such successful acquisition is needed. This directly formalizes independent alternative-track coverage and extends ARC1's degree-t lifted-witness consumer. All comparisons are guarded by the deleted-owner set; deductions valid on R are valid on F, but a witness for R is not valid on F without restoration. Intersection of necessary affine hulls is sound, not a proof of existence.

## Theorem B (PROVED): target-specific rank acquisition NP-complete even with one missing rank
We refute the inference that an actual witness span only one dimension short of the ambient affine space has a uniformly polynomial exact completion merely because the gap is one. Let x in GF(2)^n be source bits and y in GF(2)^k be fresh controls, k>=3 fixed (k=3 sufficient). Write g(y)=sum y_i. For each proper source triple C and each odd-parity p in GF(2)^k form the widened OR owner
    D(C,p) = (OR_{i=1}^k [y_i != p_i]) OR C(x).
Compile this owner exactly using private selectors and literal copies as above with sum of its k+3 selector bits equal to 1. Wide parity can alternatively be replaced by a private width-three XOR chain with unique affine lift; the code uses the wide row. Denote the conjunction of these affine equations A and matching clauses B by R. Add two fresh variables a,b with affine rows a=g(y), b=g(y); keep these rows in A. The restored clause is (a or b), using two fresh endpoint names; B plus this clause is still a disjoint matching.

Projection of R onto (x,y) is exactly {g=0} union {g=1 and F(x)}. For g=0 every odd p differs from y, so every D holds for arbitrary x; for g=1 the owner with p=y enforces every original C, and all other p-owners are automatically true. The local selectors lift iff each D holds. Thus R is always SAT; the restored system is SAT iff F is SAT. Actual witness lift and source recovery are explicit and polynomial.

Let M=m*2^(k-1) be the number of widened owners, L=k+3, and D=n+k+M(L-1) the dimension of the nonempty ambient affine solution space A, including the uniquely determined copy coordinates a,b. The hyperplane H=A intersect {g=0} has dimension D-1. We can construct in polynomial time an actual witness pool whose affine hull equals H:
1. Lift the zero projected word using any true position in each widened owner. Lift each unit source displacement and each even control displacement e_1+e_i, i=2..k. Their differences span the projected tangent hyperplane.
2. For each widened owner and each position j != 1, construct an even-parity control word and source word making its positions 1 and j both true. At most two control bits are specified, so k>=3 leaves a parity-adjustment bit. Source literals use distinct names. Set any unspecified source bits to zero. Every owner then has at least one true literal.
3. Lift this same projected word twice, with identical choices in all other owners, selecting just position 1 or just position j in this owner. Their difference is exactly the private selector vector e_1+e_j (and zero copies), so these pairs span the complete kernel of projection restricted to A.
Together these verified actual words span the projected tangent and its kernel, hence all of H, without assuming H itself consists of legal witnesses. Gaussian elimination can extract an affinely independent sublist of size D. This known witness pool is obtained without solving F.

If F is UNSAT, aff(models(R))=H. If F is SAT, a single odd-guard witness extends that known hull to all of A. The rank is therefore exactly D-1 or D, and deciding whether the supplied known hull needs one more dimension is NP-complete as a promise search/decision problem (yes certificate a verified witness outside the supplied hyperplane; promise checked for this reduction by its explicit construction). Computing the full exact affine hull is NP-hard; proving the missing equation g=0 is coNP-hard. This hardness applies to restoration of just ONE binary clause with a supplied actual witness basis spanning a certified ambient codimension-one hyperplane. It is not hardness of the bare rank operation or of a guaranteed complete basis. The missing-rank parameter here is ambient witness-hull gap, NOT the RT31/RT38 owner-transversal deficiency or DET-SYNC rank parameter. A parameter-only guarantee for this rank acquisition would resolve P=NP, not justify importing a restricted determinant terminal.

All individual coordinates of x,y and the original selector/literal-copy coordinates (before a,b) take both values among actual g=0 witnesses. For selectors, choose the corresponding position true and alternatively another co-true position; for literals, the even guard projects freely onto any one control. In fact every prescribed subset of fewer than k control bits and any source bits extends to an even guard, so all such projected coordinate tables are completely uninformative about the missing parity. k can grow up to O(log source length) with polynomial compilation, though k=3 suffices for hardness. This is a statement about specified projections, not all semantic deductions on auxiliaries. The additional a,b can be forced in the UNSAT case; the restored clause immediately enforces g=1 by paid affine substitution, which simply exposes the original NP-complete source. No claim of absence of all possible inference shortcuts.

## Paid closure, exact scope and resources
Initialize Gaussian affine parameterization, matching-layer unit implications, and rank-zero/one forbidden-pair closure. In R every ordinary owner pair has rank two even when restricted to H: its copy varies with the underlying projected literal and its selector can independently vary in the ambient selector parity kernel. Thus no rank-zero/one packet supplies a new equation; the unguarded matching layer has no units. For restored clause a or b, a=b implies a=1, hence g=1, reclose, then each original pair still has rank two on the affine odd chart. These are syntactic paid closure facts, not semantic closure of the joint system. On known witnesses, H is computed and rank checked by Gaussian elimination, then every possible additional affine necessity of R (relative to A) is either equivalent to g=0 or is trivial. At stagnation exact target acquisition of that equation would require deciding F. This is why the missing witness must not be replaced by an affine-hull assumption. Conditional even/odd guards retained; no unconditional use of odd-guard failures.
