## Theorem 1 (exact projection and reconstruction)
On an actual consistent affine chart, normalize each forbidden affine packet to independent rows; remove inconsistent packets, reject whole-space forbidden packets. Let P_i be its dual coefficient span. Let v be any nonzero free primal direction annihilating all protected observations. Choose a complement and write z=y+t v. Untouched owners (P_i annihilates v) remain unchanged. For a touched owner i, row operations choose one row with evaluation 1 on v, subtract it from every other such row, and express the forbidden condition as
G_i(y)=g_i, t=p_i(y)
with affine p_i. At fixed y, avoidance is either vacuous or forces t=1+p_i(y). Therefore conflict between i,j is exactly the forbidden exterior affine packet
G_i(y)=g_i, G_j(y)=g_j, p_i(y)+p_j(y)=1.
Construct all pairs, discard inconsistent packets by Gaussian elimination, and retain all consistent ones (including a possible whole-space forbidden packet). Remove touched owners. This is the exact existential projection: for any y obeying all surviving/created packets, the active owners demand at most one common t; choose it, or choose either when none active. Every original word projects, and every exterior word lifts by testing activations, restoring the affine chart and verifying source. No synthetic hull words or count-preservation claim. For rank-two parents the new packet has rank <=3, but repeated eliminations can raise rank; cap by ambient dimension and keep explicit row bases. Exact under additional guards annihilating v; arbitrary other guards cannot be discarded.

## Theorem 2 (cycle-rank charge)
Let I be the normalized parent owners, delta=max over S subset I of |S|-dim U_S (empty included). Define simple conflict graph K on touched owners, edge ij iff the pair conflict packet is consistent. Keep one labeled packet per edge for accounting even if equal; exact duplicate deletion can only reduce maximum deficiency. Let beta(K)=|E|-|V|+c(K) (isolated vertices included). Let delta' be the maximum deficiency of the projected labeled owner set. Then
delta' <= delta+beta(K).
In particular if K is a forest, delta'<=delta. This includes every direction affecting at most two owners, and some directions of arbitrarily large support.

Proof: fix any projected owner subset consisting of untouched owners A and selected conflict edges E'. Let B be the endpoints of E', and c' the number of nonempty components of (B,E'). Identify exterior dual rows with the annihilator of v. For each touched i, its activation rows span P_i intersect ann(v), and the chosen pivot row r_i has r_i(v)=1. For a consistent pair packet, its row span is generated by both activation spans and r_i+r_j; these span (P_i+P_j) intersect ann(v), because the full sum is spanned by those generators and r_i. The RHS is irrelevant to rank once consistency has been checked. The union of selected conflict row spaces contains each incident activation span and each edge difference r_i+r_j. Add one pivot per connected component; by path sums, recover every r_i, hence all U_B. Together with unchanged U_A this proves projected rank >= dim U_(A union B)-c'. Thus projected deficit <= |A|+|E'|-dim U_(A union B)+c' <= delta+|E'|-|B|+c' <= delta+beta(K). If E' is empty the same inequality holds with B empty and c'=0. Maximize. A rank-zero consistent packet already means UNSAT; the numerical bound also holds prior to rejecting it.

This is a one-step inequality on the actual chart, not an unconditional bound after arbitrary unrelated guards or newly learned affine equations. Additional affine restriction of rank q can increase deficit by at most q for a fixed owner registry; unit elimination with a retained unit owner can be charged more tightly. Recompute delta after each paid closure. In particular we do not claim that the entire forced-probe-and-elimination loop globally preserves initial delta.

## Theorem 3 (uniform polynomial admitted terminal and discovery)
For a forest K, the number of new packets is |E|<=|T| (strictly less if touched set T nonempty); hence the total owner count never increases in the elimination step. Every accepted step drops actual free dimension by one, so at most initial d steps. At most m^2 candidates per direction; Gaussian consistency, canonical basis, graph DFS, and beta computation polynomial in explicit N. Can find ANY direction supported on at most two owners: enumerate unordered pairs/singletons/empty owner sets J, take nullspace of all rows outside J and all protected observations, and pick a nonzero direction when available. Zero-support directions are free. This is polynomial and complete for support<=2, not for all forest directions. For larger supports test a deterministic polynomial candidate set of free-chart basis directions and pair sums (or supplied verified candidates); reject failed attempts, never treat failure as UNSAT. A successful candidate removes a dimension. Run paid rank-zero/one Gaussian normalization before attempts, after every accepted move/deduction and at stagnation; additionally test both coordinate pins using only the sound same closure, exporting the opposite only when contradiction certified, and restart. Preserve actual guards and maps. Can add AB03 cancellation on rank-two charts, but not required for soundness; arbitrary-rank packet closure is not the full semantic forced-information oracle.

After contraction, use AB06 exact core decision if residual core rank <= c log N, or independent exact terminal. If no terminal is admitted, return UNKNOWN, not NO. If all d directions disappear, decide both labels and reconstruct in reverse. Owner count <= original m after accepted forest steps and normalization (no learning that adds owners); each packet has <=d independent rows of <=d bits; at most O(d) saved stages. ## Explicit obstruction and scope of Szeider transfer
The five two-dimensional row spaces in GF(2)^4 with generators (bit masks) (1,2),(4,8),(5,10),(6,11),(7,9) form a spread. Each distinct pair spans the whole dual space. Hence all proper owner subsets satisfy rank-Hall, full set deficit1. All-zero forbidden RHS gives UNSAT: their primal annihilators are also a spread, covering every vector (each nonzero belongs to exactly one). For every nonzero v exactly four owners are touched. Any two touched independent row planes have jointly consistent activation equations and opposite pivot demands: their four independent equations can realize opposite forbidden t values at the same exterior y. Thus conflict graph is K4, beta=3, for every v. This refutes universal existence of a forest-eliminable direction on raw strict deficiency-one charts, not on the full portfolio or after arbitrary closure; indeed XOR of the five rank-two forbidden indicators is identically one and AB03 can reject this UNSAT geometry. Flip one RHS in the first owner for a SAT neighbor. The earlier Szeider ordinary clause-variable deficit splitting theorem does not transfer merely by substituting linear row rank for variable incidence. Our exact forest criterion is a sufficient tractable reduction, not a universal deficiency-decreasing split. Bounded delta alone remains an open algorithmic parameter here.
