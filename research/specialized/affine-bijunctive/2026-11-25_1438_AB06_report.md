## Theorem A: exact maximum-deficiency core, arbitrary guards
After Gaussian parameterization use dual row space V=GF(2)^d. Owner i forbids a consistent affine flat a_i z=p_i,b_i z=q_i; P_i spans its rows. More generally each owner may forbid a single explicit affine packet A_i z=b_i: normalize its equations to an independent basis, remove inconsistent packets (vacuous avoidance), and treat rank-zero consistent forbidden packet as UNSAT. Define U_S=sum_{i in S} P_i and r_W(S)=dim((U_S+W)/W) for any protected observation row space W. Define f_W(S)=|S|-r_W(S), delta_W=max_S f_W(S) (empty set included). Let C_W be the intersection of all maximizing owner subsets.

1. f_W is supermodular (linear dimension submodular), so maximizers are closed under union and intersection; C_W is the unique smallest maximizer. It depends only on geometry and W, not signs or RHS. Rado defect formula: maximum number of independent distinct-owner representatives of projected row sets equals |I|-delta_W. One may derive from ordinary Rado by adjoining delta_W common independent dummy directions; Hall holds after enlargement, and at most delta_W dummy representatives chosen. Use partition/linear matroid intersection on tagged rows. Original row basis of each P_i suffices, since their projections span the same space. Each row represents the sufficient equality opposite its forbidden RHS, so any independent chosen representatives can be satisfied simultaneously. For rank-two packets may use all three nonzero rows, with their opposite forbidden values. O(m) matroid intersection calls find C_W: compute delta_W then scan owners, deleting one when max deficiency in the remainder stays delta_W. Deleted owners can never become necessary; once deletion fails no subset of the current remainder without that owner maximizes. This yields the unique least maximizer. delta=0 gives empty C, even if nonempty tight sets exist.
2. For any X outside C, max deficit gives
r_W(C union X)-r_W(C)>=|X|.
Thus outside owners have full independent transversal modulo U_C+W. Acquire it once, via matroid intersection. Every consistent assignment of values on U_C+W satisfying core owners lifts by solving their observation equations and the selected outside equalities together by Gaussian elimination. The independence ensures no conflicting RHS for any compatible core/guard word. Every full model restricts to a valid core word. This proves exact both-label decision and witness lift for every sign choice and every consistent guard on W. No synthetic feasible words. For a fixed guard on W enumerate at most 2^(r_W(C)) core quotient coordinate assignments, check core constraints, lift. r_W(C)=|C|-delta_W. Total time 2^(|C|-delta_W) poly(N), polynomial space, including discovery and verification. Contradictory guard is NO without enumeration. Under polynomial cap a missed admission is UNKNOWN.

Arbitrary packet rank extension is symbolic: an independent row basis per forbidden affine packet provides the same rank and one sufficient opposite equation per row, so no need to enumerate its exponentially many nonzero directions. Exact only for a conjunction of individual forbidden packets, not an arbitrary XOR of indicator functions or semantic guard registry. ## Theorem B: nesting and shared exact target projection
If H subset W, r_H(S)-r_W(S)=dim(((U_S+H)/H) intersect (W/H)) is nonnegative and monotone in S. Explicitly equals dim((U_S+H) intersect W)-dim H. Thus f_W=f_H+g with g monotone. For A=C_H, B=C_W, supermodularity gives f_H(A union B)-f_H(B)>=f_H(A)-f_H(A intersect B)>=0. Adding the monotone g, A union B is a maximizer of f_W and equality forces A intersect B a maximizer of f_H; minimality of A yields A subset B. Also delta_H<=delta_W<=delta_H+dim(W/H). This extends AB05's one-guard unique tight support to arbitrary deficit. All tracks protecting subspaces of W share a common exact contraction and outside representatives; no promise of small largest core.

For full feasible set X, let pi_W map a word to its actual linear observation functional on W. The exact image is
pi_W(X)={t in W*: exists u in U_C* satisfying core owners and u|_(W intersect U_C)=t|_(W intersect U_C)}.
Indeed consistent u,t extend to a functional on the sum U_C+W; apply Theorem A. Therefore the projection is a union of actual affine fibers parallel to ann(W intersect U_C) inside W*. For each feasible core observation u (enumeration 2^dim U_C) form its target fiber; duplicate restriction signatures may be merged. Retain the actual u and the same outside representative equations for every lift. This is exact joint image, not merely affine hull; no independence of separate guards assumed. Boolean Mobius inversion proves these evaluations suffice. Retain actual lifted source words if constructing a degree-h evaluation basis. Time 2^dim U_C * N^{O(h+1)} and polynomial space with streaming; full image may be exponential and is not explicitly enumerated outside the fibers. If dim U_C=O(log N), arbitrary-rank W is admitted. ## Counterexample to universal small core
For d>=4 take coordinate variables on a simple cycle and add one chord. For each edge ij forbid x_i=x_j=1. The d+1 owner planes are span(e_i,e_j). Whole set deficit1; every proper subset has at most as many edges as incident vertices, since deleting any edge of the theta graph destroys one of its two independent cycles (remaining components have at most one cycle each). Thus C_0 is all d+1 owners, r(C)=d unbounded even at delta=1. All-zero and all unit vectors are models, so the complete affine hull is ambient and no nontrivial sound affine closure or coordinate force can shrink it. This family is ordinary Horn/2SAT and trivially easy; not a hardness result. Shows this contraction parameter alone cannot prove universal logarithmic work. On a simple cycle without chord, delta_0=0 and C_0 empty, but any nonzero h in coordinate span gives C_<h> all d owners: every proper nonempty edge subset is a forest with rank>=|S|+1. Even a single added guard may enlarge the core by arbitrarily many owners. A joint portfolio must decide when sharing actually helps, not assume small rank growth bounds core growth. These infinite controls proved symbolically, not separately executed.
