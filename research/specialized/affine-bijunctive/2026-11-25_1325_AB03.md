## Theorem 1: exact cooperative affine/quadratic normalization
Fix an actual affine guard A (including original rows and all genuine pins). Parameterize A by x=a+Dz, with z in F2^d and independent columns. Each ordinary clause has two false-literal affine functions f_i(z),g_i(z); its violation indicator is p_i=f_i*g_i. Work in the Boolean ring z_j^2=z_j. Write p_i=q_i+ell_i with q_i squarefree homogeneous degree two and ell_i affine. The system is p_i=0 for all i.

Represent coefficients in an explicit degree<=2 monomial table. Gaussian eliminate the q_i rows, carrying ell_i and owner-index provenance with them. Each zero quadratic row with nonzero affine remainder gives a necessary affine equation ell=0 (a constant 1 certifies inconsistency). Add all such equations to the actual guard, reparameterize, repeat to fixed point. No restriction is guessed. Each nontrivial consistent round strictly lowers affine dimension, at most n rounds. All model sets are preserved, including under any supplied guard. At a fixed point each linear combination of p_i with zero quadratic part vanishes identically on the chart. Thus selecting any subset of original p_i whose quadratic parts form a basis gives an exact equivalent conjunction on this chart: the omitted equations differ from their corresponding combinations only by identically-zero affine terms. This is stronger than checking only individual rank-zero/one observation packets. It is not complete semantic closure. Example xy=0 and (x+1)y=0 jointly force y=0, although both original products are individually nonlinear.

Let r=dim span{q_i} at a nonempty fixed point. It is invariant under invertible affine reparameterization because restriction induces a linear map on quadratic functions modulo affine functions. It is not the rank of any particular alternating matrix.

## Theorem 2: actual basis acquisition in 2^r poly(N) for each fixed feature degree
At a nonempty saturated chart with r>0, choose any original clause with nonzero q_i. Its factors are nonconstant affine functions and their linear parts independent. Partition the chart into two disjoint children: f_i=0, or f_i=1 and g_i=0. Their union contains exactly the parent models. Both children are affine restrictions, and the chosen quadratic becomes zero under either. Restriction induces a linear map on the parent's quadratic quotient by affine functions. The chosen nonzero q_i belongs to its kernel, hence the child's quadratic span has dimension <=r-1. Subsequent closure restrictions cannot increase it. Inconsistent children are rejected by Gaussian elimination. Reapply normalization initially, after each deduction and at stagnation before branching. All branch-derived equations stay conditional on the exact branch guard. After at most r choices along any path, the live rank is zero; saturation implies every original clause then vanishes identically. The original chart is covered by at most 2^r disjoint affine terminal charts (at most 2^(r+1)-1 search nodes including rejected children). Any terminal anchor lifts via the recorded parameterization and is verified against all original constraints.

For an actual degree-t feature-span basis, t fixed, in every terminal affine chart enumerate z with Hamming weight <=t (include zero). These are actual legal words, not arbitrary combinations of legal words. A degree<=t Boolean polynomial vanishing on them has all coefficients zero, by triangular evaluation on subsets and induction. Affine substitution preserves degree<=t. Thus the union of these actual words spans the full degree-t evaluation image of the original mixed problem; retain independent vectors with actual word provenance by Gaussian elimination. A supplied target polynomial of degree<=t is nonzero on the feasible set iff it is nonzero on at least one retained word; all recovered words and restored source obligations are verified. Empty model set is certified by exhaustive admitted rank tree, not sample emptiness. For t fixed the exact acquisition time is 2^r poly(N) (degree-dependent exponent); decision alone may stop after first witness. This combines ARC1/AB01 with a genuinely mixed residual, without assuming semantic rank acquisition. When r=O(log N) this is polynomial. This also supplies deterministic fixed-degree identity testing on the actual model set. Arbitrary Boolean-circuit identity tests and unbounded degree not claimed.

An adaptive factor choice may remove much more than one independent quadratic at a time. Star family p_j=z_0*z_j, j=1..d-1 has rank d-1, yet branching on z_0 yields two affine leaves: z_0=0 or z_0=1 and all other z_j=0. This yields an actual full affine hull with only three tree nodes; no need to enumerate 2^(d-1) tracks. To remain inside disjoint matching promise, replace each occurrence by a fresh private copy tied to its latent coordinate by an affine row; the quotient system and witnesses are unchanged.

## Theorem 3: bounded deletion cannot universally rescue this rank criterion
Let R be obtained from F by deleting k binary clauses and/or affine rows; retain actual common guards. Let A_F and A_R be the respective fixed-point charts from Theorem 1. If A_F is nonempty, A_F is contained in A_R. Proof by induction over closure rounds for R: initially containment holds; each derived affine consequence on the R chart is a combination of retained violation polynomials. Its restriction to A_F is affine, hence identically zero by the fixed-point property for the full F. All R affine deductions therefore hold on A_F, even if it has no actual model. This proves containment and excludes a conflicting R closure when F closure is consistent. Restrict the R quadratic span to A_F. Its image has dimension at most r_R. Restore at most k deleted binary products, increasing dimension by at most k; deleted affine rows are already enforced on A_F. Consequently
  r_F <= r_R + k.
For constant k, a large saturated nonlinear rank cannot become logarithmic solely by removing k constraints. This is a genuine obstruction to universal low-rank deletion portfolio admission, not a lower bound for adaptive rank branching, AB02 width, Horn, matching or other tracks. Example: for all pairs i<j impose z_i*z_j=0. Every product has independent quadratic part, rank binom(d,2); normalization forces nothing and actual words 0,e_i span the entire ambient affine space. This SAT family is connected on latent variables, can be compiled to disjoint binary matching by private copy equalities, and is trivially Horn. Thus universal logarithmic initial rank is false even on easy SAT inputs, while adaptive branches and other tracks remain legitimate. No universal rank or arbitrary owner deficiency transfer.
