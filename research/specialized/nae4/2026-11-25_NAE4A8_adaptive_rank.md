## 1. Exact quotient width (PROVED)
For subspaces U,V,W of finite-dimensional F2 space E let pi:E->E/W, w=dim(U intersect V), k=dim W. Rank-nullity applied to each of U,V,U+V yields w'=dim(pi U intersect pi V)=w+dim(W intersect (U+V))-dim(W intersect U)-dim(W intersect V). Thus w-k<=w'<=w+k: the middle positive term dominates each individual negative term and is <=k. With W=U intersect V, w'=0. This is minimum rank among ALL W producing w'=0, since k>=w. It can be acquired by polynomial Gaussian elimination (no target). Projected coupling can increase: U=<e1>,V=<e2>,W=<e1+e2>. For fixed owner split, enumerate all 2^w values of an intersection basis. Under each guard the remaining spaces are independent; solve both regions, combine consistent local linear functionals via h(u+v)=h_U(u)+h_V(v), extend to original space by elimination. Exact SAT iff some fiber has both feasible sides. For supplied exact child solvers cost sum over all fibers of their actual work plus 2^w poly(N); no hidden compatibility oracle. Witness lift polynomial for each successful pair. Fixed guard only; no assumption all fibers SAT.

## 2. Adaptive rank budget theorem (PROVED)
Define beta(F)=minimum over subcubic owner trees of maximum cut intersection dimension (unshifted branchwidth), with width zero on at most one owner. Quotient sensitivity for every fixed cut implies beta(F)<=beta(pi F)+k by lifting an optimal quotient tree. If child owner groups have direct-sum spans, the beta of union is at most maximum child beta, by connecting their trees: internal cut intersections unchanged, cross-group intersections zero. If removing owners of total rank r, reattaching their leaves to a tree costs at most r in width (any old cut gains at most r; new cuts wholly among reattached owners have rank <=r). These three facts apply with arbitrary basis changes and choices of owner tree.

Consider a structural decomposition certificate for a raw NAE4 source: at each node choose a consistent guard of new rank k_v on the current quotient, eliminate only owners whose original rank-two plane has dropped rank in the cumulative quotient (possibly solve resulting rank-one equations and charge their independent rows), and split remaining owners into groups with direct-sum observation spans. Require all surviving groups recursively represented, eventually terminal beta<=b. Choices may depend on earlier guard RHS; on a SAT execution choose a successful guard at every node and represent every conjunctive child. Let K be the maximum cumulative guard rank along a root-to-leaf path (including closure deductions), each guard lifted to original dual space along nested quotient maps. For any cumulative rank K' choose pivot coordinates J with restriction of its rowspace to J injective. The quotient is injective on vectors supported outside J, so each original owner that loses rank touches J. Since each original variable belongs to four owners, at most 4K' original owners have dropped rank. Along any path all deleted owner IDs distinct and have lost rank under final cumulative W, so at most 4K deleted on that path, each of quotient rank <=1 when deleted. By combining the three width inequalities inductively, beta(F)<=b+max_path sum_v(k_v+r_v) <= b+5K. This bound also covers leaves with no remaining owners; reattach eliminated owners and use zero-width empty arrangement. If all owner IDs retained, beta(F)<=b+K. A single successful structural certificate suffices for bound, so arbitrary adaptive RHS choices do not evade it. Balanced binary splitting reducing owner counts by fixed factor <1 and constant new rank per level gives K=O(log m), hence beta(F)=O(log m) for bounded-width terminals. The theorem does NOT constrain arbitrary semantic contractions, rank-two owner removal justified by nonlinear deductions, duplication/replacement with new factors, high-width terminals, or SAT shortcuts. If arbitrary owner deletions are allowed, use total ranks charged instead of 4K. This is a structural necessity, not a runtime lower bound.

## 3. Explicit counterfamily for adaptive low-rank balanced separation (PROVED)
L>=5, group G=Z_L x Z_L; variables A_i,B_i,C_i, n=3L^2. Four partitions indexed by shifts s in {(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)}, each containing (A_i,B_{i+s},C_{i+2s}) for all i. Each perfect; pairwise linear since s,2s differences distinct for L>=5 and pair differences determine s. SAT by A=0,B=1. Full semantic affine hull: fix any two groups to opposite colors and flip any one variable in remaining group; all unit differences appear. Thus no unconditional nontrivial affine equation exists on these SAT instances before guards; complement normalization costs one rank. Each owner quadratic part is independent because unique pairs (A5 method).

Graph H is union of triangles from each owner; its cycle matroid represented by edge differences. For each owner the three edge vectors span exactly its rank-two plane. Refine any owner tree by replacing each leaf by a three-edge subtree: old cuts have matroid connectivity lambda=intersection dimension+1; internal cuts have lambda<=3. Thus bw(M(H))<=max(beta(F)+1,3). H bridgeless (every edge in a triangle); use Mazoit–Thomasse equality of graph and cycle-matroid branchwidth. Contract each edge A_i B_i (from zero shift), discard C vertices and unwanted edges; shift (1,0),(0,1) yields square L x L grid as a minor. Robertson–Seymour grid tangle gives graph branchwidth >=L by tangle/branchwidth duality, so beta(F)>=L-1. Consequently any certificate in section 2 with fixed b requires K>=(L-1-b)/5, Omega(sqrt n); cannot satisfy constant-rank balanced logarithmic depth. With retained planes stronger K>=L-1-b. This falsifies universal low-rank-only adaptive separation even on easy SAT inputs, not all heterogeneous terminals. In ordinary explicit encoding N=Theta(n log n), sqrt n is superlogarithmic in N.

Genuine UNSAT neighbor family: disjointly add the nine-point affine plane with its four slope partitions, then swap A_(0,0) and one variable of a plane owner in partition zero. Perfect partitions preserved; new cross-component pairs fresh, same-component pairs subsets of old, so linear. Other eleven plane owners remain, UNSAT: each monochromatic pair belongs to exactly one of twelve lines; any two-coloring of 9 points has at least 16 same-color pairs, but with <=1 bad line at most 14 (=11+3). Grid minor of size L-1 survives by using coordinates 1..L-1 in both dimensions. Thus high width occurs for both labels. No claim that this family is hard to decide (local plane core easy); no claim about affine closure of UNSAT family. SAT family is tripartite, so simple SAT terminal bypasses width barrier.
