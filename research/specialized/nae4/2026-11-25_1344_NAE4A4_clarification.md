# NAE4A4 clarification | residual batch semantics
The phrase “A path cannot revisit a clause node” in section 4 of the report is too strong and is withdrawn; the subsequent qualification in that section is the intended result. In the selected-variable graph the paths visit each VARIABLE vertex at most once. In the residual circulation network a clause node has two independent incoming-to-outgoing transitions (the selected opposite pair, and the leftover variable/quota pair). Following a selected-variable path can therefore revisit a CLAUSE node through its other transition. Each path still determines one directed hub-to-hub closed walk without repeated arcs. The walks are arc-disjoint, partition the residual arcs, and every subset of whole walks is a legal unit augmentation from the same anchor. The count n'/3 and the exact phase-chart projection hold. A closed walk may split into simple cycles; to preserve the fixed chart, all pieces must be toggled together. No NP-hardness result for a single simple cycle on each side is asserted. This clarification leaves the fixed-side acyclic chart theorem, source maps, and tests unchanged. Closure tests iterate full parity scans to a fixed point, rather than restarting a scan after each individual join; each accepted join is used immediately by subsequent owner scans. The reference UNSAT controls are not full compiled UNSAT searches.
