## 1. Exact rank terminal (PROVED)
On the guarded B chart each occurrence is a signed phase. For remaining NAE(a,b,c) use Boolean ANF q=(1+a+b)(1+a+c) over F2, reducing z_i^2=z_i. Eliminate on the quadratic coefficient columns; zero quadratic rows are affine consequences of the conjunction. Solve and substitute, repeat until no further nontrivial affine equations or contradiction. Every successful round reduces dimension, so at most d rounds; inconsistent constants reject. At the fixed point r independent quadratic rows suffice, since dependent rows reduce to zero or already-enforced affine rows. Preserve all chart/anchor guards. With d free coordinates the exact number is Z=2^{-r} sum_{lambda in F2^r} sum_z (-1)^{sum lambda_j q_j(z)}. For a quadratic with a mixed term x_i x_j write q=x_i x_j+x_i A+x_j B+C, where A,B affine in other coordinates; summing x_i,x_j yields 2(-1)^{C+AB}. Repeat, or if affine return zero for a nonconstant linear part and signed 2^remaining for a constant. Polynomial per character, exact integer accumulator O(d+r) bits. Deterministic time 2^r poly(N), polynomial peak space with streamed characters. Prefix pinning and recounting at most d times yields witness; original chart/source lifting is polynomial. This is a scoped exact terminal, not a general P algorithm; r=O(log N) suffices. Further decompose independent phase/constraint components; cost sum of 2^{r_i} poly(N). Source compilation and explicit ANF substitutions polynomial (O(d^2) monomials per row), time/space/access, all unsuccessful prefixes and integer arithmetic charged; cumulative memory bounded by time times peak bits.

## 2. Linear nonlinear-rank obstruction on actual compiler image (PROVED)
For any p>=7 use A_i,B_i,C_i, i mod p, four partitions (A_i,B_{i+j},C_{i+2j}), j=0,1,2,3. Each partitions all 3p variables; pair uniqueness follows because j,2j differences are distinct modulo p. Connected by A_i--B_i--A_{i-1}. Each group can vary freely when the other two groups are fixed to opposite colors. In particular for each source coordinate the difference of two witnesses equals its unit vector, so affine hull is full F2^{3p}. The 4p original NAE polynomials have independent homogeneous quadratic parts (each consists of three private pair monomials). A4 canonical lifts obey the explicit external source clauses and every literal occurrence equals its source-coordinate value with the appropriate sign. Let L be any affine domain containing all canonical lifted witnesses, with chart guard. Restrict to their affine hull L0, on which those affine occurrence identities hold. The affine source projection L0 -> F2^{3p} is surjective. Pullback is injective modulo affine functions (compose with an affine right inverse). Thus these original external rows remain independent modulo affine functions on L0 and hence on L. Sound affine restrictions retaining the canonical witness family cannot reduce residual quadratic span below 4p, even with full semantic affine closure. This is linear in source/compiled vertex count and Omega(N/log N) for ordinary explicit indexing; not a runtime lower bound. Nonlinear rewrites, SAT-only discovery and structural factorization can bypass this obstruction. No claim that the family defeats every chart or solver.

## 3. Exact anchored conflict-cut translation (PROVED)
At anchor y all original clauses except one are NAE. For a good clause with minority m and majority a,b, set t_v=x_v XOR y_v. Preserving the clause iff R(m,a,b): t_m=t_a OR t_m=t_b, equivalently edges ma,mb cannot both cross the cut. For original linear instance the two edges per good owner are distinct across owners, so conflict graph is exactly a matching on host edges. The bad clause is NAE of its three repair bits, requiring at least one designated pair separated. Try three pairs. In a supplied B chart add equalities t_u=t_v for selected edges, contract them if desired, maintaining owner identities/parallel edges; loops and duplicate support choices simplify. Every cut is an actual repaired word, lift via x=y XOR t and circulation quotas. The unrooted CF-cut result of Kalyanasundaram-Kumar does not guarantee a designated pair is separated. The raw ordinary host has 2(4n/3-1) edges, exceeding 2n for n>3; after contracting B chart one must recalculate multiplicity/redundancy. Do not import simple-graph density theorems blindly. A4 skeleton projected via its exact wires/fillers is already a DAG of good support obligations: selectors s_j -> X_i,Y_i; Y_a -> X_b,X_c; X vertices sinks. Exceptional owner NAE(s0,s1,s2). Thus acyclicity alone fails as universal terminal on this compiled source core.

## 4. Rooted single-obligation DAG terminal (PROVED)
If each variable has at most one good obligation R(v,a,b) (possibly singleton support) and the support digraph v->a,b is acyclic, deciding if s,t can differ is equivalent to two vertex-disjoint directed paths from s,t to unrestricted sinks (paths of length zero allowed). Necessity: follow a same-color successor until a sink; opposite paths disjoint. Sufficiency: assign entire paths opposite colors; color other sinks arbitrarily and remaining vertices in reverse topological order, copying one successor. Split vertices with unit capacities; super-source connects s,t, all sinks connect to super-sink; two unit augmentations suffice. Recover paths and check all obligations and bad clause; at most three root pairs. If no such flow for all pairs, exact UNSAT for this guarded subclass. After local deductions run sound unit/affine closure again and recheck class, keeping guards. Contracted same-color equalities can be safely simplified; arbitrary pin sets or cycles/multiple obligations require separate proof. Time polynomial, graph O(N) and standard integral max-flow; adjacency matrix implementation in audit is quadratic space; witness lift is explicit.

## 5. NP-hardness even with two multi-obligation centers and acyclic support (PROVED)
Given genuine NAE4 source, introduce s,t,w and for each source owner (a,b,c) fresh u_c,v_c. Good constraints R(w,s,t), and R(s,a,u_c), R(u_c,b,c), R(t,a,v_c), R(v_c,b,c). Exceptional NAE(s,t,w). These are precisely Boolean betweenness constraints; all good constraints admit all-zero anchor. Bad owner plus R(w,s,t) forces s!=t (and any w then works); complement to s=1,t=0. The first two clause constraints extend iff at least one source variable is 1: if a=1 take u in {b,c}; otherwise force u=1 and need b or c=1. Symmetric for zero with v. Thus exact source NAE equivalence and constructive polynomial witness lift and projection. DAG order w -> s,t -> auxiliaries -> source (also s,t -> source). Only s,t center more than one obligation. This is an NP-completeness result for auxiliary rooted support DAGs, NOT a claim that these particular graphs have the original degree-four/linearity/chart promise. In particular the parameter number of multi-obligation vertices cannot suffice alone unless P=NP. The relevant obstruction is many simultaneously coupled obligations even at two centers. Under branch guard s=1,t=0 every good relation has full affine hull over its free coordinates, and no ordinary unit propagation forces a source or clause auxiliary variable; full semantic reasoning is not ruled out. The A4 skeleton is another direct acyclic hardness instance with larger multi-center count. Do not infer unrestricted separation.
