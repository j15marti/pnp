## A. Exact side compilation and affine hull theorem (PROVED)
Pair any two partitions P,Q. Build bipartite clause nodes L,R plus hub h. Each actual variable v is a distinct L->R arc with [0,1], each P quota h->L and Q quota R->h has [1,2]. Coordinate pins replace variable interval by [b,b]. Optionally fix actual quota arcs to 1 or 2 as conditional guard. Feasible integer circulation iff side NAE word obeys guards; quota values equal owner sums. Every word uniquely determines quotas; reconstruct directly, verify all original source constraints on acceptance. Standard lower-bound feasibility via super-source/sink maxflow finds integral anchor f or proves infeasibility. All capacities integral, unit-width or fixed; total lower-balance demand O(N). No source solving in compiler.

For every nonfixed arc, residual orientation at f is forward if lower bound attained, reverse otherwise; exactly one residual direction. Let E* be residual arcs with endpoints in the same strongly connected component. Other arcs are invariant over all feasible integer flows: any flow difference is a nonnegative integral circulation on residual arcs, hence a sum of directed cycles, and a cycle cannot cross SCCs. Conversely each E* arc lies on a directed simple cycle, which is a legal unit augmentation of f. This determines every fixed variable and fixed quota, even under coordinate guards.

Let D be the GF(2) incidence matrix of the active residual multigraph on E*. The exact affine hull of feasible arc vectors modulo 2 is f + ker D, with zero coordinates on all other arcs. Proof: every actual difference decomposes into residual directed cycles, hence belongs to ker D. Conversely for each strongly connected component choose one root and directed simple paths P_v root->v and Q_v v->root. For each vertex take closed walk W_v=P_v Q_v, for each arc e=(u,v) take W_e=P_u e Q_v. For a directed cycle C, over GF(2), sum of W_e over C plus sum of W_v over vertices of C equals C. More generally for an undirected GF2 Eulerian edge set, at each vertex indegree and outdegree mod2 agree, so the same identity uses W_v for vertices with odd indegree, and yields that edge set. Every closed directed walk decomposes into simple directed cycles, each individually feasible at the SAME anchor. Thus a polynomially constructible collection of actual single-cycle augmentations spans the entire affine hull. Parallel arc IDs kept distinct. No claim that their arbitrary XOR is feasible.

Projected variable hull has a particularly simple exact description: pin each invariant variable; for each invariant quota arc impose XOR of its three incident variables equals its anchor quota modulo 2. No further equations are needed. Given such a variable word in the affine hull, assign free quota-coordinate parities as the corresponding XOR; then every clause node balances mod2 and hub balance follows from summing clause-node balances. Thus full incidence-kernel extension exists. This characterizes all affine equations supported by that side with coordinate/actual quota pins, not just singleton marginals. For unguarded genuine sides complement of any feasible anchor toggles every variable AND every quota (1<->2), so every arc is active and projected affine hull is the entire Boolean cube. Thus even the COMPLETE side affine hulls carry no unguarded information across sides. This is an algebraic obstruction, not computational hardness.

## B. Actual witness basis and one-owner restoration (PROVED)

Discovery O((V+E)V) total closed-walk arc occurrences, simple path/SCC discovery, cycle decomposition and Gaussian reduction all polynomial; full raw witness storage polynomial even without streaming. This directly answers AB01's request for COMPLETE actual degree-one witness bases, rather than guessed points in a hull.

If one deleted owner g=NAE(a,b,c) is the only missing constraint, its bad set {a=b=c} is affine. Every actual basis word violates g iff the entire hull lies in its bad set, iff every side word violates it. Therefore testing at most n+1 REAL witnesses decides whether restoration is possible and yields an actual original witness when it is. Likewise a deleted synchronization equality has affine bad set, so the same test applies to that single restored obligation provided all other constraints are already enforced by the flow chart. One may instead use the exact affine equations to choose a separating actual cycle.
