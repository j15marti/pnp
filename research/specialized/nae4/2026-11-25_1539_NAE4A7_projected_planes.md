## 1. Exact dual-plane compilation
Over F2, let E=F2^d be the space of coefficient vectors (dual to free phase assignments z). Clause NAE(a,b,c) is exactly (a+b,bad=0) OR (a+c,bad=0) in the sense that its pair of differences must not be (0,0); for signed or affine phases, write a+b=f(z)+alpha and a+c=g(z)+beta, so the forbidden observation pattern is (alpha,beta). Owner plane U_i=span(f,g) has rank <=2. Compute its allowed local linear functionals by a truth table of at most four entries, including rank-zero contradictions/tautologies and rank-one equalities. A word z evaluates a linear functional on every plane. Conversely a functional on the sum of all planes extends to all of E and yields a word by solving binary linear equations; hence exact SAT equivalence, polynomial compilation and witness lift. If using fixed B chart, all conclusions remain under that chart guard, and reconstruct original bits by A4's affine map. For original positive source d=n, f=e_a+e_b, g=e_a+e_c. Coordinate pins or parity equations can be eliminated by Gaussian substitution; retain guard provenance.

## 2. Exact boundary gluing and charged dynamic program (PROVED)
For a set S of owners let U_S=sum_{i in S}U_i. Functional h on U_S is feasible iff all local restrictions satisfy their owner truth tables. Two feasible functionals on U_A,U_B combine iff they agree on U_A intersect U_B; define h(a+b)=h_A(a)+h_B(b), well defined by this agreement. Choose any subcubic decomposition tree on owner leaves. For oriented edge with side S define W_S=U_S intersect U_complement(S), and table of feasible h|W_S. Leaf table computed by <=4 assignments. At an internal node with disjoint child owner sets A,B and outside O, W_A and W_B share U_A intersect U_B, and W_{A union B} is contained in W_A+W_B: any w=a+b in U_O has a in U_A intersect (U_B+U_O), b similarly. Join child tables by agreement on intersection and restrict combined functional to parent interface. This is exact even when observation vectors are dense or when shared variable counts overestimate rank. Enumerate all functionals on W_A+W_B (dimension <=2w), check child restrictions, record one parent pointer per surviving interface value. Root outside empty, nonempty root table iff SAT. ## 3. Raw positive NAE rank has exact graph interpretation (PROVED)
Within any owner subset S, create undirected triangle edges for each triple, on all n vertices including isolates. Span of pair differences consists exactly of vectors of even weight in every component, by spanning-tree paths. Its dimension is n-c(S), c(S) #components. Thus interface width for S and complement T is n-c(S)-c(T)+c(S union T). Make bipartite multigraph of components of the two sides; each original variable is one edge. Its connected components are exactly those of union, so width equals its cycle-space rank. This is analogous to A3's comparison graph but not its fixed-pair chart: it measures actual original observation subspaces. Works for raw positive planes, not arbitrary chart-substituted dense forms without recomputing rank.

## 4. Linear backdoors and simultaneous coordinate planes (PROVED)
Choose rank-k matrix L, enumerate Lz=b, solve fibers by Gaussian elimination. Each observation plane becomes its image in E/rowspan(L) after restriction, plus affine offsets. If every fiber has a recognizable polynomial terminal, cost 2^k poly(N) plus charged discovery. We consider a specific direct terminal: after quotient, every distinct rank-two plane is spanned by two members of a single coordinate basis; each local forbidden pattern becomes one 2-CNF clause. Rank-one constraints must be treated as mandatory basis lines or eliminated first, zero-dimensional rows evaluated directly. Recognize coordinate-plane property by collecting distinct rank-two planes (merge duplicate subspaces but retain all local constraints), their three nonzero vectors, and shared lines occurring in two different planes. Each shared line must be a basis element since intersection of coordinate subspaces is coordinate. Include explicit rank-one lines when requiring those as coordinate too. If mandatory vectors dependent reject. For each plane extend its mandatory subset to a basis, arbitrarily choosing private vectors. Each private vector lies in only one distinct plane and is not mandatory. The resulting union spans all owner planes and has fixed cardinality independent of local choices. A common coordinate basis exists iff this union is linearly independent; extend it to ambient basis. Gaussian elimination is polynomial. In the audit the rank-two-only recognizer was tested; the rank-one extension is symbolic. This is a sufficient exact tractable language, not a characterization of all instances reducible to 2-SAT by arbitrary preprocessing.

Static-backdoor obstruction: for raw genuine NAE4 with n variables and m=4n/3 owners, let W be any rank-k observation space. Choose k pivot coordinates J such that restriction W -> F2^J is injective. Quotient map E -> E/W is injective on vectors supported outside J. At most 4k owners touch J. Untouched owner planes remain rank two and pairwise intersect trivially because original hypergraph linear. Also making every plane rank <=1 directly requires k>=n/3, since any perfect partition supplies n/3 direct-sum planes, and rank drop requires W to meet each nontrivially. ## 6. Dynamic CSP and two-sided messages (PROVED)
Compute exact messages for both directions of every decomposition edge by bottom-up then top-down sweeps. For leaf i, the complement-side message on W_i tells which local observations extend to all other owners. Intersect it with the local allowed truth table to obtain full-instance projection on U_i. This is exact forced-information closure for each leaf plane; every fixed value of a linear form in that plane is a certified consequence in the current guard. Empty full-instance projection means UNSAT. Removing one owner replaces local relation by the full truth table on its unchanged span. It restores SAT iff complement-side table nonempty; more generally any tightening/relaxation/replacement within the same span is decidable from the complement table without solving the whole instance. All one-owner deletion tests and local projections obtained in total 2^{O(w)} poly(N), not m full solves. For local relation updates with fixed span and tree, update leaf-to-root messages in time 2^{2w} poly(N) times number of affected nodes (O(log m) on balanced tree); refresh reverse messages on demand or charged full two-pass sweep. Deletions invalidate consequences using deleted owner; retain them under their original guards or recompute. Do not export them into relaxed CSPs. For a tree rotation ((A,B),C)->(A,(B,C)), only one internal owner split changes; exact message relations on unchanged splits remain identical. Recompute both directions on changed split and affected witness pointers. If owner spans themselves change, boundaries elsewhere can change; recompute unless invariance proven. Candidate rotation ranking by sum or max interface dimensions is computable in polynomial time without running expensive candidate DPs. A strictly decreasing integer sum of ranks allows O(m*d) accepted rotations with polynomial scanning, but can stop at a nonoptimal local minimum. No universal logarithmic width or global acquisition guarantee.
