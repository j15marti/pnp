## 2. The exact two-side anchor and chart theorem (PROVED)

Pair the partitions into A=(P0,P1) and B=(P2,P3), or either other pairing. Construct for each side a cubic bipartite graph with triple nodes on the two shores and one edge per original variable joining its two owners. Hall follows by counting the three incidences at each vertex; a polynomial bipartite perfect matching exists. Set matched variables to 1, all others to 0. Obtain actual anchor words a,b, each satisfying its respective side, exactly one 1 per triple. No target solution is used. The same representation is the unit-width circulation of S21 with quotas [1,2], but here matching suffices for initialization.

For each owner i, use its side anchor and let E_i={e_i^0,e_i^1} be the two opposite-bit pairs in its triple. Introduce one binary selector s_i choosing one pair. Let G(s) be the selected graph on the original variables. The following equivalence holds for ANY independently acquired valid side anchors, not merely some favorable anchors:

    F is SAT iff there exists s such that G(s) is bipartite.

Forward: the two opposite pairs of any satisfying source coloring in a triple intersect the two anchor-opposite pairs (two 2-subsets of a 3-element pair catalogue). Choose a common pair per owner. The actual source coloring is a bipartition of G(s). Reverse: a bipartition makes a selected pair opposite in every triple and hence satisfies all source NAE constraints. All choices and lifts are explicit. This is an exact union of charts, not a claim that a single chart covers the models. A fixed chart pair can be glued in linear graph operations by BFS; any conflict is an explicit odd cycle. The family of selectors is still exponential.

This supplies a uniform bridge between BALANCED-17 and S21 without an unknown target or an enumeration of circulation words. A joint chart witness gives the common circulation target in S21. Given such a target, S21's synchronized cycle-batch repair works; the proof here does not assert that discovering a favorable selector is free or that each chart exchange decreases disagreement.

## 3. Exact implicit odd-cycle clauses (PROVED)

Consider the graph containing both edges of every E_i, with labels (i,j). For every simple odd cycle C containing at most one edge from each owner, write

    L_C(s) = OR_{(i,j) in C} (s_i != j).

Cycles using both alternatives of an owner can never occur in a selected graph and impose no restriction. Let L be the conjunction over all eligible cycles, potentially exponentially many. For every s, L(s) holds exactly when G(s) is bipartite. Thus L is an exact existential selector formulation of the NP-complete source. Given s, BFS either produces a source witness or an actual violated L_C in polynomial time. An odd cycle contains distinct selected owner labels. No cycle enumeration or SAT oracle is needed. Every learned clause is source-authenticated in the SELECTOR representation; under an anchor change, retain its original edge labels and translate only if all edges remain selectable (otherwise the conflict is automatically avoided). Do not treat selector clauses as unguarded source-variable consequences.

## 4. Polynomial lazy renamable-Horn terminal (PROVED)

Let F_seen be an initially empty set of learned cycle clauses. Use the convention that signed integer literals +i and -i denote s_i and not s_i (indices start at one). A renaming f complements selected s variables. A literal with truth sign p in {0,1} is positive after renaming precisely when p XOR f_i=1. Every pair of distinct literals in a learned clause contributes the 2-CNF renaming constraint (f_i=p_i OR f_j=p_j), excluding simultaneous positivity.

Maintain all such pair constraints P and a satisfying renaming f, initially zero. Solve the renamed F_seen by its Horn least model and translate back to s. If no model, return certified UNSAT (a subset of L is UNSAT). If BFS colors G(s), return verified SAT. Otherwise learn the violated odd-cycle clause. If it is Horn in the current renaming, retain f: the new Horn least model must strictly increase coordinatewise, unless the new formula is inconsistent, since the old least model violates the new clause. If the new clause is not Horn, its pair constraints include at least one previously absent violated constraint. Recompute a renaming by 2-SAT on P. If no renaming exists, return UNKNOWN, never UNSAT on that fact alone.

There are O(m^2) possible distinct signed pair constraints, so only O(m^2) renaming resets. Between resets the least model increases at most m times; each stage learns at most m+1 clauses before solving or resetting. Thus O(m^3) oracle/learning rounds, O(m^4) literal storage conservatively, and polynomial aggregate deterministic bit work including scans, SCC, failed probes and verification. If the full implicit L is renamable Horn, no renaming failure is possible and this procedure decides both labels exactly. On other inputs it is sound SAT/UNSAT-or-UNKNOWN. This is an application of MFC-5, not a new unrestricted identity test.

## 5. NEW: implicit deletion-backdoor discovery with single-exponential cost (PROVED conditional parameter bound)

Define k* as the minimum number of selector variables whose removal from every eligible L_C (erase their literals, not the whole clauses) makes the entire implicit L renamable Horn. This is a deletion backdoor, stronger than just saying each of several successful charts is easy.

For a supplied cap k, solve Variable Deletion Almost 2-SAT on the polynomial learned pair-constraint formula P: find a set B of at most k variables such that deleting all P-clauses incident to B leaves a satisfiable 2-CNF; acquire its renaming f outside B. This is exactly recognition of a deletion Horn-renaming backdoor for F_seen: erasing B from each learned clause leaves only the pair constraints on its other variables. Use the standard deterministic 2^{O(k)} poly(N) backend (Parameterized Algorithms, Cygan et al., Theorem 3.12, 4^k poly; alternatively Razgon–O'Sullivan clause deletion reduction: replace each variable's two literals by positive rails p,q, enforce not(p and q) once, replicate each translated original positive clause k+1 times; deleting at most k consistency clauses permits the deleted variables to have both rails true. Nondeleted variables can choose one true rail. This is equivalent and uses the O(15^k k m^3) clause-deletion algorithm). For a standard backend with decision only, the deletion witness can be recovered by ordinary iterative decision-to-search on the explicit deletion instance with polynomial additional calls; or use the constructive published algorithm. No implicit full cycle catalogue is required.

Fix B,f and examine all 2^{|B|} actual selector assignments a on B. In each, simplify F_seen under a, rename outside B, and repeatedly compute its Horn least model. A verified graph coloring terminates SAT. An inconsistent learned branch is soundly rejected. On a violated cycle, add its entire clause and all pair constraints globally. If erasing B from that clause is Horn under f, continue the branch: its least model strictly increases or the branch becomes inconsistent. (The clause was violated, so no assigned literal can have made it true.) Other branches already certified inconsistent remain so. If the erased clause is non-Horn, at least one new pair constraint is violated outside B. Restart with a new deletion-backdoor/renaming computed on the enlarged P. If the backend reports no B of size k, return UNKNOWN. If all B assignments are rejected, return exact UNSAT.

Bound: O(m^2) resets; at most 2^k(m+1) conflict/terminal tests per stage. Each reset calls the 2^{O(k)} poly(N) backend. The persistent learned clause set has at most O(2^k m^3) clauses of length <=m; deduplicated P has O(m^2) pairs. Thus T=2^{O(k)} poly(N), with explicitly bounded storage of the same order, no exponentially many implicit cycles generated for fixed or logarithmic k. At k>=k* every learned P has an admissible backdoor, so the algorithm is complete. For k=O(log N) the entire acquisition, not just replay of a supplied backdoor, is polynomial. One can also discard redundant learned clauses but no such saving is required. For arbitrary k the time is not a polynomial bound. The algorithm may terminate SAT/UNSAT even when k<k*; UNKNOWN is not an UNSAT claim.

This is the precise combination of the four-group reduction, adaptive puncturing and guarded Horn-cycle learning. The new open structural condition is not existence of a finite number of types, but a uniform logarithmic bound on k* for some efficiently acquired representation, or another certified terminal.

## 6. A caution and useful structural theorem: maximal phase contraction is not witness acquisition (PROVED)

After removing one whole partition, the three-group hypergraph has n triples and degree three at each variable. In each connected component let E_i again be the two opposite pairs under ANY actual three-group anchor. There is a polynomially discoverable choice of one pair per triple whose graph is connected within every original hypergraph component.

Proof: the candidate pairs of a triple form a connected star. For any subfamily I of retained triples, its candidate graph has components with v_j vertices and e_j triples. Each triple uses three distinct variables, each variable has degree at most three in I, so v_j>=e_j. Equality means every incidence in the full three-group source at every vertex of that component belongs to I and remains inside it, hence that component is an entire original component. With c0 original components and c(I) candidate components (including isolated vertices), c(I)<=n-|I|+c0. The graphic matroid rank of the candidate union is n-c(I). The maximum-size independent rainbow forest formula (Rado/matroid intersection, one edge per color) is min_I(n-|I|+n-c(I)), hence >=n-c0; the full graph rank is n-c0, so equality. Ordinary matroid intersection constructs that forest, and choose either available edge for each still unrepresented owner. This preserves the connected components and the anchor coloring. No target solution is needed.

Consequently the maximum contraction strategy achieves one phase per three-group component. In a connected three-group source it makes the residual nonlinear hypermatching h=0 automatically. But it preserves only the anchor and its complement, so it can fail to extend even on a SAT four-group source if the anchor violates the omitted group. This fixes the interpretation of BALANCED-17: small h ALONE is not favorable-skeleton acquisition. Need compatibility coverage as well. An existential h=0 feasible skeleton for a SAT source follows by using the unknown full witness as anchor and this forest theorem; it does not yield a discovery algorithm.

For two partitions every anchor admits an acyclic chart: chosen edges form a maximum-degree-two graph. On a cycle, switch any owner's selected edge to its other anchor-opposite pair. The third variable has no selected edge of that partition, degree <=1, hence is outside all cycles. The switch opens the cycle without creating another. Iterate to a forest with n/3 components. NAE4A4 proves the stronger fixed-chart acquisition hardness with canonical source lifts on a compiled family. Thus even a supplied acyclic chart cannot universally replace joint compatibility. The connected three-group theorem here is symbolic; no matroid-intersection implementation was executed in this audit.

## 7. Cooperative nonlinear and topological terminals (exact interfaces, not universal coverage)

On any chosen side chart, its actual source variables are affine phase literals x_v=a_v+z_{C(v)} over GF(2). An omitted NAE(u,v,w) has violation polynomial

    (1+x_u+x_v)(1+x_u+x_w).

Apply AB03: Gaussian-eliminate the quadratic coefficient rows carrying affine parts, impose any resulting affine consequences, and reparameterize until saturated. This preserves all actual guarded models, and at most n affine dimension decreases occur. At rank rho of the remaining quadratic span, split on one live factor f: f=0 or f=1,g=0. The chosen quadratic vanishes on each child; restriction is linear on quadratics modulo affine forms, so rho strictly decreases. Hence decision/witness acquisition is O(2^rho poly(N)). This is a separate exact terminal when rho=O(log N); the input chart and guards stay attached, and chart UNSAT does not imply source UNSAT unless exhaustive coverage is proved. AB03 also acquires actual fixed-degree feature bases at this cost. BALANCED-11 algebraic-seam DP and AB05 guarded Hall localization can process compatible local relations with small interfaces; no claim of universal small width or rank. RT39 gluing is an exact alternative on appropriately dual strict deficiency-one charts, with its aggregate job count still OPEN.

Use actual two-partition circulation anchors, all three pairings, polynomially clocked cycle/backdoor solver, chart quotient/rank solver, topology/flow terminals, and direct witness verification cooperatively. Persist learned source-certified consequences, preserve guards, never discard source-valid alternatives on heuristic weight alone. Only conclusive source-wide UNSAT, or UNSAT from a provably exhaustive union, can reject before a proven universal witness cutoff. Local chart failure triggers retry. ## 9. The strongest conditional end-to-end theorem and the remaining gap

Fix a uniform deterministic witness program W consisting of polynomially charged cooperative rounds, with actual NAE4 input, no instance-dependent advice, and source verification of every candidate. If there exist absolute constants C,c such that W finds a verified source witness in C(N+2)^c bit steps on EVERY satisfiable NAE4 input, then clock W at that budget, accept only verified witnesses, and reject otherwise. This decides the NP-complete source in P, hence P=NP. No general UNSAT proof system and no reconstruction of all solutions are needed. This is the constructive analogue of the project's UWI witness-cutoff equivalence. Conversely P=NP implies a uniform polynomial witness finder, not necessarily the present restricted W. The theorem is conditional, not evidence that its premise holds.
