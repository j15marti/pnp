## 2. Exact nonlinear fusion and high-boundary projection (PROVED)
For nonempty lists of literals A,B and free private pivot x, existential NAE(A,x) AND NAE(B,not x) is exactly NAE(A,B), assignmentwise even when boundary variables overlap. If A or B mixed, its owner places no requirement on x; otherwise common colors a,b require x=1-a and x=b, compatible iff a!=b. If pivot literals have same sign, complement all literals in second owner (NAE invariant). If pivot is negated in first, rename its Boolean value. A witness lifts by testing two pivot values, constant choices and linear owner scans. This rule is exact for signed arbitrary arity, not just ternary.

For an explicit region of signed ternary owners with three distinct variables per original owner, let private variables be unpinned, appear nowhere outside region (including guard/side-chart constraints), and occur at most twice within region. All remaining variables boundary, possibly shared arbitrarily. Build owner multigraph with each degree-two private variable an edge. Fuse spanning tree edges in each connected component. Non-tree private edges appear twice in the fused literal list, degree-one private variables once. A component with m owners has m+2 remaining literal occurrences, since each fusion removes two. If no remaining private variables, projection is one signed NAE on boundary; repeated same literals can be deduplicated, opposite literals make it tautological, and singleton/empty NAE is false. If any private variable remains, the fused list has >=3 occurrences and a private variable occurs at most twice, hence there is another distinct variable. If opposite literal pair occurs the relation is tautology. Otherwise choose values for all remaining private variables arbitrarily except one, then set that one to oppose a literal on a different variable. Thus every boundary assignment extends. This includes cyclic components and components with a degree-one private variable. Sign-frustrated cycles do not invalidate this theorem because residual private values, not necessarily alternating selected pairs, are used. No boundary enumeration needed. Apply all components together: private sets disjoint, so boundary feasibility is conjunction of their projected signed NAE relations. Reverse fusion records to recover every original private word needed for one witness. Verify all original owners and guards. Polynomial discoverable for a supplied region or by scanning globally free low-degree coordinates; not a claim every input has a large eligible region. For arbitrary-width residual owners use the single fusion identity and explicit remaining-list checks; do not blindly apply the ternary m+2 corollary.

Tree with distinct m+2 boundary variables: exactly two complementary forbidden assignments, all proper coordinate projections full, hence impossible to express by conjunction of proper-support constraints on the same boundary variables without auxiliary variables. In dual difference space it is one forbidden point of rank m+1. This illustrates a compact high-dimensional relation, O(m log N) encoded literals vs explicit 2^(m+1) dual truth table. It does not imply exponential decision time. Unlike A8, this operation deletes rank-two original owners and replaces them by a new higher-rank owner, so the A8 retained-plane bound does not apply.

## 3. Exact degree-d projection and minimal direct degree-four cost (PROVED)
Generalize to d owners incident on a single free pivot, one occurrence per owner, each remainder nonempty. Each owner restricts x to a nonempty subset of {0,1}. The intersection is nonempty iff every pair intersects (binary Helly). Thus exact elimination produces one pair-fusion signed NAE constraint for each pair of owners, normalizing pivot signs pairwise as in section 2. Check all original owners to choose x on lift. d=2 gives one replacement; d=3 gives three; d=4 gives six. Duplicate or contextual redundancies may reduce some instances; arbitrary degree d explicit cost O(d times total incident literal count) before simplification, no hidden boundary enumeration.

For raw positive linear NAE4, every variable x has four incident owners NAE(x,a_i,b_i), i=1..4, with eight distinct neighbors. The exact standalone projection is the conjunction for i<j of NAE(a_i,b_i,not a_j,not b_j). In CNF these are precisely 12 width-four clauses forbidding partial assignments with one complete neighbor pair 00 and another complete pair 11. A partial boundary word has no extension iff it already contains such a conflict: if not, choose x opposite the common color of all fully monochromatic neighbor pairs (if none, arbitrary); every partially assigned pair can be completed to satisfy its owner. Hence the 12 conflict patterns are exactly the minimal infeasible partial assignments, and the corresponding clauses the prime implicates. Each has a private violating word: make its two specified pairs monochromatic in opposite colors and all other pairs mixed. An arbitrary implicate is false only on a set of words fixing at least one such conflict pattern, since its falsifying partial assignment is infeasible; no single non-tautological implicate can reject private violating words for two different patterns. Therefore every exact CNF representation without auxiliaries on these eight boundary variables needs at least 12 clauses; every signed-NAE conjunction on those same variables needs at least six owners, since each NAE expands to at most two CNF clauses. Original local region used four owners/eight CNF clauses. Thus direct raw degree-four elimination is intrinsically expanding in these representations, even before repeated elimination. This is a local exact projection lower bound, not an unrestricted algorithm/representation lower bound, not a claim about auxiliary variables, affine observations, global-context simplifications or alternative semantic contraction. It applies on every genuine NAE4 source including DS17 output.

## 4. Hall-deficit conservation under two-owner fusion (PROVED)
Connect to DP11/AB07. For an explicit signed-literal registry (no dense guard mixing x), define U_o as span of pair differences of variable coefficient vectors in owner o, ignoring signs in the span but retaining them in the relation. Let D=max_S (|S|-dim(sum U_o)), including empty S. Fuse owners i,j sharing a private x (other lists nonempty), retain merged owner even if its actual sign relation is tautological at this accounting stage. For any subset including both old owners, remove x and replace by merged owner. Its span rank drops by exactly one: U_i+U_j is spanned by differences among the union of variable names including x, and the x-coordinate-zero subspace is exactly the new owner's difference span. All other owners lack x. Their inclusion preserves the same rank decrement. Owner count also drops by one. For a subset containing exactly one old owner, remove it; its pivot difference contributes an independent dimension beyond all other owners, so deficiency cannot decrease. Subsets containing neither are identical. Every new subset lifts to an old subset of equal deficiency. Hence D_before=D_after. Subsequent genuine tautology deletion may reduce D, but mere tree fusion does not guarantee a Hall-deficit reduction. Dense row-space guards must not be assumed to satisfy pivot independence. When the contracted system has D=0, a polynomial linear/partition matroid intersection chooses one nonzero actual difference row per owner, all independent; set each row to make its pair unequal and solve simultaneously. For wide NAE use O(arity) base differences, as some base difference must witness satisfaction, and all span dimensions preserved. This yields SAT and explicit lift, not UNSAT on failure. A5/A7/A8 terminals can also consume exact new factors only where their arity/language supports are validated. No universal admission theorem.
