## Run Summary
Asymptotic, evidence-driven review of P versus NP. No assumption of equality or separation. Focus on lower-bound candidates via hierarchies, magnification, and circuit bounds; consistency checks across complexity universes. Survey of ECCC reports through TR26-271.

## Key Findings This Run
- Confirmed best explicit general-circuit lower bound remains \(3.5n - O(\log n)\) (Mishra--Santhanam, ECCC TR26-260, 19 October 2026). No subsequent general-circuit improvements identified in ECCC through TR26-271 (23 October 2026).
- Magnification thresholds of Atserias--Müller (arXiv:2503.24061) remain the sharpest known general magnification: slightly superlinear lower bounds on sparse NP approximation problems imply fixed-polynomial formula lower bounds for NP.
- The vanishing-ratio formalization holds: for every fixed \(\epsilon > 0\),
  \[
  \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{3.5n - O(\log n)}{n^{1+\epsilon}} = 0.
  \]
  No amplification pathway from the linear regime to any fixed superlinear threshold is known.
- Hierarchy theorems continue to yield only internal separations. No cross-resource forcing of P \(\neq\) NP arises.
- Multi-universe consistency reconfirmed: no contradictions forced in P=NP, P\(\neq\)NP, RP=P, BPP=P, ZPP=P, P=UP, MA=AM, or related settings from current bounds, magnification thresholds, or repository ceilings (charged-transport inequality, adaptive-trajectory firewall, degree-boundary, Horn-transport).
- New relevant literature: ECCC TR26-270 (Li) characterizes fragments of prAM whose derandomization reduces to Range Avoidance (containing prMA, GNI, certain GapSVP); TR26-269 settles nondeterministic communication complexity of graph connectivity at \(\Omega(N\log N)\); TR26-271 improves polynomial approximation bounds for discrete logarithm. None transfer to superlinear general Boolean circuit lower bounds for explicit functions.
- AC\(^0\) improvements (TR26-248, TR26-261) and near-maximum bounds for restricted classes remain compatible with the open status of general-circuit superlinearity.
- Repository investigations continue to impose ceilings that constrain structured reductions without closing the asymptotic gap under either resolution.

## Status
P versus NP remains OPEN. Evidence continues to indicate persistence of the linear-versus-superpolynomial (and linear-versus-slightly-superlinear) gap rather than its closure. No new asymptotic contradiction identified.
