Assume an all-input transformation producing \( \le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} \) children of length \( \le N^{\lambda+o(1)} \) with local work \( \le N^{1.1+o(1)} \) and live parent space \( \le N^{0.2+o(1)} \), \( \lambda=1/(5K) \), \( \zeta=0.1 \). Then the derived exponents satisfy
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2.
\]
Direct evaluation shows \( 0.2 < \beta(t') \) for every finite \( K\ge 2 \) (e.g., \( \beta(1.2)\approx 0.511 \), \( \beta(1.3)\approx 0.326 \)). The pair therefore lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region. The transformation remains unproved.

**Lemma (Classical Self-Reducibility Cost — PROVED/EXACT).**

The classical self-reduction that fixes variables one-by-one issues \( \Theta(n) \) queries on formulas of size \( \Theta(N) \). The aggregate time is therefore \( N^{1+K+o(1)} \). For every finite \( K\ge 2 \) this exceeds the local-work budget \( N^{1.1+o(1)} \).
