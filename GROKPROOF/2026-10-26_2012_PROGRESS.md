## Status
P versus NP remains OPEN. The strongest missing proof step is the universal all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver A into a simultaneous resource profile forbidden by the FLMV (or Williams) bounds, with every resource fully charged.

## Next Decisive Lemma
