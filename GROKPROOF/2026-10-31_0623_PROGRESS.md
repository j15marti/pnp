**Lemma (Numerical Membership — CONDITIONAL).**
Assume an all-input transformation producing at most N^{1-λ+ζ+o(1)} ordinary SAT children each of length at most N^{λ+o(1)}, with total local work N^{1.1+o(1)} and live parent space N^{0.2+o(1)}, for λ = 1/(5K) and ζ = 0.1.
Then the derived simultaneous exponents satisfy
t' ≤ 1.3 - 1/(5K), s' ≤ 0.2.
Direct substitution into β shows 0.2 < β(1.3 - 1/(5K)) for every finite K ≥ 2. Hence the derived profile lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region.
The transformation itself remains unproved; the lemma is therefore CONDITIONAL on its existence and correctness under full charging.
