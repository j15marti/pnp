### Build / Formal Observation
**RESTRICTED Lemma (Circuit-cut cost barrier).**  
Assume a uniform family of circuits \(C_N\) of size \(S = N^{K+o(1)}\) that correctly decide ordinary \(N\)-bit SAT. Any cut of boundary width \(w\) induces at most \(2^w\) residual SAT instances. For the residual length to be \(\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) with \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), one needs \(w = O(\lambda\log N)\). Locating a cut whose residuals are ordinary CNF of that length, verifying semantic equivalence (or sound one-sided implication), and combining answers requires, in the worst case on expander incidence graphs, time at least \(\Omega(S^{1/2})\) or an equivalent parent-sized access in known cut-finding algorithms. Consequently the local-work budget \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) is violated for every finite \(K\ge 2\) unless a specialized cut oracle of sub-budget cost exists. This does not refute the existence of some other transformation, but rules out naïve circuit-cut selectors.
