\section{Status}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}. The following lemma, if proved for parameters that map every finite polynomial exponent pair into a published forbidden region, would yield the desired resource contradiction. It remains unproved.

\section{Open Lemma}
\begin{quote}
\textbf{Lemma (Universal Child Generator, OPEN).}
Assume \(A\) is a fixed uniform deterministic total exact ordinary-SAT decider with
\[
T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for finite constants \(K\ge 2\), \(L\ge 0\). There exist constants \(\lambda\in(0,1)\), \(b\), \(r\), \(u\) depending only on \(K,L\) and a uniform algorithm \(\Phi\) such that, on every input formula \(F\) of length \(N\), \(\Phi\) produces at most \(N^{b+o(1)}\) ordinary-SAT instances \(F_i\) each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), together with a charged combination procedure, satisfying:
\begin{enumerate}
\item correctness is preserved for both SAT and UNSAT labels;
\item selector discovery, failed strategies, repeated access, memory management, and peak live parent space are all charged;
\item total local work excluding the recursive calls on the \(F_i\) is at most \(N^{r+o(1)}\);
\item the effective simultaneous resources \((t',s')\) of the composed solver lie strictly inside a published FLMV or Williams forbidden region (e.g., \(s'<\beta(t')\) or the Williams subpolynomial-space exponent \(2\cos(\pi/7)\)).
\end{enumerate}
The map must be hereditary and same-machine in the exact FLMV random-access model.
\end{quote}

\section{Related Barriers}
Buss and Williams established that alternation-trading proofs cannot establish a subpolynomial-space time lower bound for SAT better than \(N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}\). Any improvement beyond this exponent requires techniques outside the alternation-trading framework.

Residual cores after affine/Gaussian elimination of Tseitin shells remain \(\Theta(N)\) for arbitrary non-affine exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instances, exhausting local-work budgets of the form \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\).

\section{Next Steps}
Construct an explicit candidate \(\Phi\) and fully charge all costs, or prove a black-box query lower bound showing no such map can meet the budget on expander families.

Until this implication is established or refuted, P versus NP remains open.
