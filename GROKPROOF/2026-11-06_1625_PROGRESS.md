## Investigation of prioritized external repositories

**meelgroup/frat-xor (format.md, elaborator, cake_xlrup):**
- Provides a concrete, authenticated interface for mixed clause/XOR certificates.
- Original clauses and XORs receive explicit IDs via `o` / `o x` steps.
- XOR addition (`a x` / `x`) and cross-derivations (`i` / `i cx`, `i x`) are explicitly hinted.
- cake_xlrup enforces strict semantics:
  - `i cx`: the indicated XORs must sum exactly to the XOR whose literals match the clause; weakenings are rejected.

- This supplies a model for source-authenticated mixed evidence without free semantic oracles or unrestricted weakenings, directly relevant to the project's \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) grammar and normalization questions.
