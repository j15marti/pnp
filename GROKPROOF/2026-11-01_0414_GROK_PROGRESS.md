\section*{Current Understanding of the Gap}
The project correctly identifies that Monotone 3-SAT-(2,2) (D\"ocker 2019) is NP-complete, so a poly-time algorithm for it implies P=NP. Known time-space lower bounds (Fortnow-van Melkebeek, Williams) exclude certain simultaneous (t,s) for ordinary SAT on random-access TMs: specifically, no DTISP[n^{c}, n^{o(1)}] for c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019, and more generally tradeoffs for positive space exponents.

The missing step is a fully charged, all-input, A-dependent reduction or recursive decomposition of instances of the target language that produces children whose sizes and total work place the effective (t',s') inside a known forbidden region, for every possible polynomial A with finite exponents K,L.

Incidence-component decomposition is proved sound for disconnected instances but fails for connected ones (the hard cases). Port-closure and XOR-activity analyses do not yet yield hereditary power-short children with charged costs below the FLMV gate.

\section*{Failed / Restricted Hypotheses}
- Raw restriction or single-pin cofactors do not preserve the exact-(2,2) form without re-encoding costs that destroy the exponent improvement.
- Component closure does not give all-input ARC.
- Numerical certificates such as 433t + 216s < 630 do not construct the algorithm.

3. Investigate whether the known alternation-trading lower bounds transfer directly to the target language under the exact machine model used in the project, without additional reduction overhead.
