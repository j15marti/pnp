\subsection{Proposed Falsifiable Mechanisms}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Self-reduction portfolio charging.} Standard self-reduction of SAT produces a decision tree of depth equal to the number of variables, each node invoking \(A\) on a slightly smaller instance. For exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 the variable count is \(\Theta(N)\). Charging every invocation, even with memoization of identical subinstances, produces total time at least \(N^{K+o(1)}\) on hard instances unless massive sharing collapses the tree to polynomial size. Falsify by exhibiting a uniform memoization schedule whose charged live state remains \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\) while recovering the answer inside local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\).
\item \textbf{Independent residual cores after affine elimination.} After eliminating a linear-size expander Tseitin shell, the residual exact-(2,2) core of length \(\Theta(N)\) is equisatisfiable and remains NP-complete. Any portfolio of children of length \(\le N^{\lambda}\) must either query \(A\) on a parent-sized residual or maintain a live cut of size \(\Omega(N^{\delta})\) with \(\delta>0.2\). Falsify if a fixed uniform grammar of nonlinear extensions or relational joins reduces every such residual to a polynomial portfolio of \(N^{\lambda}\)-size ordinary SAT children inside the local budget.
\item \textbf{Certificate volume under van Melkebeek incompatibility.} Under the near-boundary seed \(T_A\le N^{1.802+o(1)}\), \(S_A=N^{o(1)}\), construct a co-nondeterministic companion from adaptive UNSAT certificates of aggregate volume \(V\). Falsify if a uniform certificate grammar achieves \(b<1.40082175\) and verification exponent \(r<1.80182175\) on every exact-(2,2) instance.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Formalization}
\begin{remark}[Self-Reduction Charging Barrier]
Let \(A\) be a uniform SAT solver with \(T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}\). Standard self-reduction by successive variable fixing produces a binary decision tree of depth \(\Theta(N)\) for an \(N\)-variable exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance. Even after identifying identical residual formulas, the number of distinct residuals encountered on hard expander-based instances is exponential in the cut size. Any schedule that materializes or queries \(A\) on residuals of length \(\Omega(N^{\lambda})\) with \(\lambda\ge 1/(5K)\) either exceeds local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) or requires live parent space exceeding \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\) to store the active frontier or memoization table. Thus ordinary self-reduction does not realize the conditional ARC contraction.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Adaptive Charged Residual Barrier (strengthened)]
Let \(F=\mathrm{Tseitin}(G,c)\land Q(B)\) with \(G\) a linear-size expander and \(Q(B)\) an arbitrary exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance of encoding length \(\Theta(N)\). Gaussian elimination eliminates the affine shell in \(O(N)\) time, leaving an equisatisfiable residual of length \(\Theta(N)\). Under black-box or adaptive access to \(A\), any procedure that must discover or verify a semantic child of the residual either submits a parent-sized instance (incurring time \(N^{K+o(1)}\ge N^{2+o(1)}\)) or maintains live intermediate state of size \(\Omega(N^\delta)\) for some \(\delta>0.2\). In either case the local-work budget \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\) are exceeded for all finite \(K\ge 2\). The same obstruction applies to self-reduction frontiers whose active cut exceeds the space budget. The Williams product bound supplies an independent obstruction for certain residual-query schedules whose time--space product falls below \(\Omega(N^{1.618-o(1)})\).
\end{remark}

The conditional numerical membership remains PROVED/EXACT. The universal all-input fully charged transformation remains OPEN.
