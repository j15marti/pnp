**Lemma (Black-box Child Generation Cost — RESTRICTED).**  
Any procedure that, under black-box access to a SAT solver \( A \), outputs \( M \) distinct ordinary SAT instances each of encoded length \( m \) incurs writing cost \( \Omega(M\cdot m) \) (plus any subsequent verification). Setting \( M = N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} \), \( m = N^{\lambda+o(1)} \), \( \lambda = 1/(5K) \), \( \zeta = 0.1 \) yields cost \( \Omega(N^{1+\zeta}) > N^{1.1} \) for all finite \( K \ge 2 \). Consequently pure black-box batch generation is excluded from the target budgets.
