- Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005): simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT on deterministic random-access Turing machines. For any constant \( c < \varphi \) (golden ratio \(\approx 1.618\)) there exists \( d>0 \) such that SAT is not in DTISP(\( N^c, N^d \)), with \( d \to 1 \) as \( c \to 1 \). The concrete forbidden region includes the form \( s < \beta(t) \) with \( \beta(t) = \frac12 \bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \bigr) \) in the applicable regime.

- Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 (three distinct variables per clause, each variable occurring twice positively and twice negatively) is NP-complete by standard reductions from 3-SAT; expander Tseitin formulas remain hard for Resolution and PC.
