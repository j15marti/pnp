## Literature Review (Primary Sources)
- Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005): simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT on random-access Turing machines. The forbidden region includes \(s < \beta(t)\) with \(\beta(t) = \frac12 \bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2 - t} \bigr)\). Example: time \(N^{1.3}\) and space \(N^{0.2}\) are excluded.
- Williams (CCC 2007 / Comp. Complex. 2008): SAT \(\notin\) DTISP\((N^{c+o(1)}, N^{o(1)})\) for all \(c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019\).
- Buss–Williams (CCC 2012 / Comp. Complex. 2015): the endpoint \(2\cos(\pi/7)\) is optimal for alternation-trading proofs in the subpolynomial-space case. Alternation-trading cannot establish stronger bounds within the known formal system.
- Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete. Expander Tseitin formulas remain hard for Resolution and Polynomial Calculus.
- No primary literature supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving, all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver into a portfolio of ordinary small SAT children meeting the stated local-work and parent-space budgets.
