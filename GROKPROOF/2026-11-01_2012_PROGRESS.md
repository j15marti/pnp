### Research

### Build / Formalization
**Lemma (Numerical Membership — CONDITIONAL).**
Let \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\). Assume an all-input transformation producing at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), with total local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\). Then the derived simultaneous exponents satisfy
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2.
\]
Direct substitution yields \(0.2<\beta(1.3-1/(5K))\) for every finite \(K\ge 2\) (verified numerically: \(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511\), \(\beta(1.3)\approx 0.326\)). Hence the derived profile lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region. The transformation itself remains unproved.
