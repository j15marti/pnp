## Key Findings This Run
- Best explicit general Boolean circuit lower bound for a function in P remains $3.5n - O(\log n)$ over the $B_2$ basis (Mishra--Santhanam, ECCC TR26-260). No improvement identified in subsequent reports through TR26-276 or in available literature as of 13:29 UTC on 26 October 2026.
- Magnification (Atserias--Müller, arXiv:2503.24061) continues to show that slightly superlinear ($n^{1+\epsilon}$) lower bounds on sparse NP approximation problems would imply fixed-polynomial formula lower bounds for NP. The ratio of the best linear bound to any such threshold vanishes:
  \[
  \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{3.5n - O(\log n)}{n^{1+\epsilon}} = 0
  \]
  for every fixed $\epsilon > 0$. No amplification pathway from the linear regime is known. This asymptotic negligibility reinforces the distance from current explicit bounds to any forcing threshold.
- Hierarchy theorems yield only internal separations (P $\subsetneq$ EXP, NP $\subsetneq$ NEXP, etc.). No asymptotic forcing of a cross-resource separation between deterministic and nondeterministic polynomial time.
- Multi-universe checks reconfirmed: no contradictions arise in the P=NP, P$\neq$NP, RP=P, BPP=P, ZPP=P, P=UP, or MA=AM universes from current unconditional bounds, magnification thresholds, or repository transport/firewall ceilings. Range-avoidance characterizations of AM fragments (Li, ECCC TR26-270), improved discrete-log approximation bounds (TR26-271), algebraic-geometric coding advances (TR26-272), CSP results (TR26-273), noncommutative identity testing (TR26-274), determinantal limits (TR26-275), and ReLU learning algorithms (TR26-276) remain compatible with both resolutions and do not transfer to general-circuit separations.
- Repository charged-transport and adaptive-trajectory ceilings continue to show linear acquisition costs consumed by construction, yielding non-positive numerators and no residual superlinear remainder.
- Related consistency results (Atserias--Müller on Gödel incompleteness and consistency of EXP $\not\subseteq$ P/poly with certain bounded arithmetic theories) do not force unconditional separations.
