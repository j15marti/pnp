## Literature Review
- Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005): SAT cannot be solved in simultaneous time \(N^{t+o(1)}\) and space \(N^{s+o(1)}\) whenever \(s < \beta(t) = \frac12 \bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \bigr)\).
