## Proposed Mechanisms
1. Source-normalized variable-block self-reduction: partition variables into \(\Theta(N^{1-\lambda})\) blocks of size \(N^\lambda\), generate one child per consistent partial assignment on a block, charge discovery of consistent assignments via unit propagation or limited resolution fully against the local budget.
2. Charged one-sided nonlinear projections with verification via short resolution proofs of UNSAT(\(F \land \neg G\)), limited to depth \(O(\log N)\).
3. Near-boundary UNSAT certificate portfolios under the seed \(T_A \le N^{1.802 + o(1)}\), \(S_A = N^{o(1)}\), using the sum-power bound \(\sum m_i^d \le M^{d-1} V\).
4. Live-space accounting for Pippenger-Fischer circuit simulation of the decision circuit \(C_{A,N}\) before any conditional transfer of circuit lower bounds.

## Research
Primary literature (Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas, JACM 2005; Williams, CCC 2007) reconfirmed as strongest simultaneous time-space bounds. Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete by standard reductions preserving bounded occurrences. Self-reducibility trees require exponential exploration in the worst case without additional structure (e.g., P-selectivity). No new universal low-cost decomposition found. van Melkebeek two-algorithm incompatibility remains conditional on near-boundary seeds.

## Build / Test
Independent numerical verification reconfirms that the conditional profile \(t' \le 1.3 - 1/(5K)\), \(s' \le 0.2\) lies inside the forbidden region \(s < \beta(t)\) for every finite \(K \ge 2\).

For the restricted Horn case, unit propagation and resolution yield a linear-time solver independent of \(A\). On expander Tseitin instances, Gaussian elimination leaves a residual of size \(\Theta(N)\). No algorithm or full lemma constructed for the unrestricted case.
