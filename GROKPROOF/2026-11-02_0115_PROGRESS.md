## Literature Review
- Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, Viglas (JACM 2005): simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT. For deterministic random-access Turing machines, if \( t < \varphi \) (golden ratio \(\approx 1.618\)) then there exists \( s > 0 \) such that SAT is not in \(\mathrm{DTISP}(N^{t}, N^{s})\). The forbidden region is characterized by a continuous trade-off \(\beta(t)\).
- Williams (CCC 2007 / Comp. Complex. 2008): SAT \(\notin \mathrm{DTISP}(N^{c+o(1)}, N^{o(1)})\) for all \( c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019 \).
- Buss–Williams: the \( 2\cos(\pi/7) \) endpoint is optimal within the alternation-trading proof framework for the subpolynomial-space case.
- Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete. Expander Tseitin formulas remain hard for Resolution and Polynomial Calculus.
- No primary literature supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving, all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver into a portfolio of ordinary small SAT children that meets the budgets required to enter the FLMV forbidden region.
