### Primary Strategy Assessment: Resource Contradiction
Temporarily assume \( P = NP \), so a fixed uniform correct SAT solver \( A \) exists with
\[
T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}, \quad S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for finite (unknown) \( K, L \).

Known FLMV/Williams bounds (Fortnow-Lipton-van Melkebeek-Viglas, Williams) exclude simultaneous profiles such as:
- Time \( n^{c} \) and space \( n^{o(1)} \) for \( c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.801 \).
- More generally, regions where \( (c-1)d < 1 \) or related inequalities for time \( n^c \), space \( n^d \).

The target is an \( A \)-dependent transformation producing children of length \( \le N^{\lambda+o(1)} \) (with \( \lambda \) small enough relative to \( K \)) whose collective solving (plus overheads) forces a forbidden \( (t', s') \) profile, contradicting the existence of \( A \).

**OPEN gap**: Prove a fully charged, all-input, hereditary transformation valid for arbitrary adaptive/state-dependent \( A \), accounting for discovery, construction, access, memory, switching, verification, and combination costs. Restricted models (affine MCSP, promise families, fixed-grammar brokers, semantic constant-size selectors) do not close this gap.
