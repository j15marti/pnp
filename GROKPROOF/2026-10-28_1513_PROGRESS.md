## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).** Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) with \(T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}\) for unknown finite \(K,L \ge 2\), construct or rigorously refute a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) (with \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\), \(\zeta = 0.1\)) such that the satisfiability status of \(F\) is recoverable by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) using live parent space at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), with every ancillary cost (discovery, verification, failed strategies, switching, memory allocation, combination) fully charged, thereby yielding a simultaneous profile forbidden by the FLMV theorem.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and P versus NP remains open.
