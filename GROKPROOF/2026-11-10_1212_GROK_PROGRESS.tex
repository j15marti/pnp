\section{Open Need: Model-Preserving Bridge}
A pebbling lower bound on a certificate DAG or computation graph yields a lower bound only for algorithms that must materialize an equivalent DAG. For an arbitrary SAT algorithm \(A\) running in time \(T(n)\), one requires a theorem of the form:

\begin{quote}
If \(A\) decides the fixture family in time \(T\), then there exists a certificate (or simulation transcript) whose pebbling cost is \(O(\mathrm{poly}(T))\).
\end{quote}

Absent such a simulation or extraction theorem, the fixture bounds do not separate P from NP.
