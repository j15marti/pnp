## Key Findings This Run
- Best explicit general Boolean circuit lower bound for a function in P remains $3.5n - O(\log n)$ over the $B_2$ basis (Mishra--Santhanam, ECCC TR26-260). No improvement identified in subsequent reports through TR26-271 or adjacent reports.
- Magnification (Atserias--Müller, arXiv:2503.24061) continues to show that slightly superlinear ($n^{1+\epsilon}$) lower bounds on sparse NP approximation problems would imply fixed-polynomial formula lower bounds for NP. The ratio of the best linear bound to any such threshold vanishes:
  \[
  \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{3.5n - O(\log n)}{n^{1+\epsilon}} = 0
  \]
  for every fixed $\epsilon > 0$. No amplification pathway is known.
- Hierarchy theorems yield only internal separations (P $\subsetneq$ EXP, NP $\subsetneq$ NEXP, etc.). No asymptotic forcing of a cross-resource separation between deterministic and nondeterministic polynomial time.
- Multi-universe checks reconfirmed: no contradictions arise in the P=NP, P$\neq$NP, RP=P, BPP=P, ZPP=P, P=UP, or MA=AM universes from current unconditional bounds, magnification thresholds, or repository transport/firewall ceilings. Range-avoidance characterizations of AM fragments (Li, ECCC TR26-270) remain compatible with both resolutions.
- Recent results (TR26-269 on nondeterministic communication complexity of connectivity, TR26-271 on discrete-log polynomial approximation, TR26-268 on graph-isomorphism testing) do not transfer to general-circuit superlinearity.
- Repository charged-transport and adaptive-trajectory ceilings continue to show linear acquisition costs consumed by construction, yielding non-positive numerators and no residual superlinear remainder.

## Status
P versus NP remains OPEN. The asymptotic evidence points to persistence of the linear-versus-slightly-superlinear (and linear-versus-superpolynomial) gap rather than its closure. No new forcing contradiction identified.
