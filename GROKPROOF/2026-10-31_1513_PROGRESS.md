**RESTRICTED/CONDITIONAL lemma on volume-bounded certificate aggregation.**  
Under the near-boundary seed \(T_A \le N^{1.802+o(1)}\), \(S_A = N^{o(1)}\), the standard volume inequality  
\[
\sum_i m_i^d \le M^{d-1} V
\]  
(with \(d=1.802\), \(M\le N^\lambda\), \(V\le N^b\)) yields a co-nondeterministic verification exponent  
\[
c\le\max(r,\,u+b+\lambda(d-1)).
