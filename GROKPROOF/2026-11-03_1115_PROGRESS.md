### Build / Test
Formalized the query-complexity barrier:
Lemma (RESTRICTED, cost ledger): Any adaptive procedure that correctly decides an arbitrary length-\(N\) exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance by issuing at least \(q=\omega(1)\) queries to A on instances of length \(\ge N/2\) must expend total time \(\omega(N^{K+o(1)})\). For every finite \(K\ge 2\) the local-work budget \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) forces \(q=O(N^{1.1-K+o(1)})=o(1)\). Thus any viable transformation must produce the required children by a procedure whose dependence on A is limited to \(o(1)\) large queries (or none).

On finite instances: unit propagation and matching-based extraction succeed on Horn/2-SAT substructures but leave \(\Theta(N)\)-size residuals on 4-regular expander-like incidence graphs after \(O(N)\) discovery work, exhausting the budget. No counterexample found that contracts the residual below \(N^\lambda\) while charging all costs and using only \(o(1)\) large queries to A.
