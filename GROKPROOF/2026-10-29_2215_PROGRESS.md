**RESTRICTED / OPEN**: Numerical confirmation that the proposed conditional contraction parameters land in the FLMV forbidden region.

For the FLMV function \(\beta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{t+2}{t^2} - t\right)\):

- \(\beta(1.2) \approx 0.5111 > 0.5\)

Thus, if the all-input transformation with \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\), \(\zeta = 0.1\) producing \(\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) children of length \(\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) with total work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\) were proved for arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver \(A\), it would yield a contradiction for sufficiently large \(K\) (or recursively for the fixed-point recurrence).

The transformation itself remains **OPEN**. No universal, resource-charged, correctness-preserving, discovery-and-construction algorithm is known that works for every possible \(A\).
