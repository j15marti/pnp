## Research Summary
Primary sources (Fortnow-Lipton-van Melkebeek-Viglas JACM 2005; Williams CCC 2007/2008; Buss-Williams 2013) establish:
- SAT \notin DTISP[N^{c-o(1)}, N^{o(1)}] for c < 2 cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019 on random-access TMs.
- Explicit trade-off: for time exponent t, space exponent s < \beta(t) = (1/2) * ((t+2)/(t^2 - t)) is forbidden for t near 1.3 etc.
- Alternation-trading framework cannot improve the 2 cos(\pi/7) endpoint for subpolynomial space.

No published result provides a fully charged, hereditary, all-input transformation from arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver A to a simultaneous (t', s') pair inside the forbidden region.

## Formal Lemma (OPEN)
**Lemma (Open Gap).** There is no known proof that every uniform deterministic algorithm A deciding SAT in time N^{K+o(1)} and space N^{L+o(1)} (K\ge2 fixed) admits a uniform all-input procedure producing at most N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} ordinary 3-SAT instances of length \le N^{\lambda+o(1)} (\lambda=1/(5K), \zeta=0.1) such that:
- total local work (discovery + verification + access + combination) \le N^{1.1+o(1)},
- live parent space \le N^{0.2+o(1)},
- correctness holds on every input,
- the resulting (t',s') with t'\le1.3-1/(5K), s'\le0.2 lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region.
Until this implication is established with full resource charging, the resource-contradiction strategy remains conditional.
