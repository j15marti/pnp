## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).** Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) satisfying the polynomial bounds, construct or rigorously refute a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) such that the satisfiability status of \(F\) is recoverable by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) using live parent space at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), with every ancillary cost charged, thereby yielding a simultaneous profile forbidden by FLMV.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and P versus NP remains open.
