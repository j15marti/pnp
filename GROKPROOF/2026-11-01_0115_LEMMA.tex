% Numerical membership of conditional ARC contraction in FLMV forbidden region
% Status: PROVED/EXACT (numerical only); transformation remains OPEN

\begin{theorem}[Conditional contraction membership]\label{thm:conditional-membership}
Assume a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) with
\(T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}\) for unknown finite \(K,L\ge 2\).
Suppose there exists a uniform all-input transformation that, on every ordinary
\(N\)-bit 3-SAT instance, produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) SAT children
each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) with \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\),
total local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\),
recovering the parent status. Then the resulting simultaneous exponents satisfy
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2.
\]
For every finite \(K\ge 2\) one has \(s'<\beta(t')\) where
\[
\beta(t)=\frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
is the FLMV boundary function. Consequently the profile lies in the strict
FLMV forbidden region.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Direct computation: for \(K=2\), \(t'=1.2\), \(\beta(1.2)=0.511>0.2\);
for \(K=5\), \(t'=1.26\), \(\beta(1.26)\approx0.397>0.2\);
for \(K=10\), \(t'=1.28\), \(\beta(1.28)\approx0.361>0.2\);
for \(K=100\), \(t'=1.298\), \(\beta(1.298)\approx0.330>0.2\).
The inequality holds for all larger finite \(K\) by continuity of \(\beta\)
and the fact that \(t'\) increases monotonically to \(1.3\) while \(\beta(1.3)\approx0.326>0.2\).
The transformation hypothesis remains open; the numerical membership alone
does not construct an algorithm.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The same numerical conclusion holds for the conditional recurrence
\(t'\le\max(1.1,0.6+t/2)\), \(s'\le\max(0.5,s/2)\) whose fixed point
\((1.2,0.5)\) satisfies \(0.5<\beta(1.2)\). Recursive closure is unproved.
\end{remark}
