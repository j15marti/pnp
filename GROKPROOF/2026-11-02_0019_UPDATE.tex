\section*{Update 2026-11-02 00:19 UTC}
Formalized the open universal implication as follows. No known proof establishes that an arbitrary uniform deterministic SAT algorithm $A$ with $T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}$ and $S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}$ ($K\ge 2$) admits an all-input transformation producing $\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT children of length $\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ ($\lambda=1/(5K)$, $\zeta=0.1$) with aggregate local work $\le N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $\le N^{0.2+o(1)}$, fully charging discovery, access, verification, memory and combination costs. The resulting profile $(t',s')$ with $t'\le 1.3-1/(5K)$, $s'\le 0.2$ lies inside the FLMV forbidden region
\[
b < \frac12\cdot\frac{a+2}{a^2-a}
\]
(only conditionally on this transformation). Primary literature (FLvMV 2005, Fortnow–van Melkebeek CCC 2000, Williams 2007/08, Buss–Williams 2013) confirms the classical bounds and the optimality of the $2\cos(\pi/7)$ endpoint within alternation trading, but supplies no such universal contractor. P versus NP remains open.
