\section{Conditional Numerical Membership (PROVED/EXACT)}

Assume a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) with
\[
T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}, \qquad S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for finite \(K,L \ge 2\).

The conditional contraction parameters \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\), \(\zeta = 0.1\) yield a target profile
\[
t' \le 1.3 - \frac{1}{5K}, \qquad s' \le 0.2
\]
(assuming the unproved all-input transformation of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) local work and \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\) live parent space).

\subsection{FLMV Forbidden Region}
By Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (JACM 2005), in the random-access Turing-machine model, SAT cannot be solved in simultaneous time \(N^{t}\) and space \(N^{s}\) whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \right)
\]
for the applicable range of \(t\).

\subsection{Numerical Verification}
For every finite \(K \ge 2\), \(t' = 1.3 - 1/(5K) \le 1.3 - 1/10 = 1.2\).
Direct evaluation shows \(\beta(1.2) > 0.2\), and the inequality holds strictly for all larger \(K\) (smaller \(t'\)).

Thus the target profile lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region.

\begin{remark}
This establishes only numerical membership of the \emph{conditional} profile. The transformation itself, including correctness, discovery, construction, access, memory, and combination costs on every input, remains OPEN. No contradiction is obtained until the transformation is proved or refuted.
\end{remark}
