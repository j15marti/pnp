## Key Findings This Run
- Williams' time lower bound of n^{2 cos(π/7)-o(1)} ≈ n^{1.801} for subpolynomial space remains the strongest known for that regime; the FLMV formula β(t) = (1/2)((t+2)/t^{2} - t) continues to exclude profiles such as (1.3, 0.2).
- Numerical verification reconfirmed: for λ=1/(5K), ζ=0.1 the conditional contracted profile (t' ≤ 1.3 - 1/(5K), s' ≤ 0.2) lies inside the forbidden region for every finite K ≥ 2, because β(t') > 0.2. The recurrence fixed point (1.2, 0.5) satisfies the tangent certificate 433t + 216s < 630.
- No universal, fully charged construction of ≤ N^{1-λ+ζ+o(1)} children of length ≤ N^{λ+o(1)} with total local work ≤ N^{1.1+o(1)} and live parent space ≤ N^{0.2+o(1)} has been established. Correctness, discovery of suitable partitions or projections, construction, access, memory accounting, and combination costs remain OPEN.
- Solver-dependent semantic decompositions (affine elimination, Horn/2-SAT, contextual projections) eliminate affine shells but leave arbitrary NP-complete non-affine cores; no A-dependent low-cost method for the surviving core was obtained. Adaptive UNSAT certificate systems under the near-boundary seed T_A ≤ N^{1.802+o(1)}, S_A = N^{o(1)} remain incomplete for universal coverage.
- Circuit and proof-complexity barriers continue to apply only under restricted models; nonlinear extension variables do not yet yield transferable lower bounds on unrestricted computation.

## Status
P versus NP remains OPEN. The strongest missing proof step is still the universal all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver A into a simultaneous resource profile forbidden by the FLMV (or Williams) bounds, with every resource fully charged.

## Next Decisive Lemma
A formal lemma that, given black-box access to A, constructs (or refutes the possibility of constructing) the required family of small SAT children while keeping the parent's live space at N^{0.2+o(1)} and the aggregate local work at N^{1.1+o(1)}, including the cost of discovering and verifying the partitions.
