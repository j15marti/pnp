## Literature Reconfirmation
- Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, Viglas (JACM 2005): SAT not in DTISP[N^{t+o(1)}, N^{s+o(1)}] whenever s < β(t) = (1/2)((t+2)/t^{2} - t) in the random-access model.
- Williams: SAT ∉ DTISP[N^{2 cos(π/7)-o(1)}, N^{o(1)}] with 2 cos(π/7) ≈ 1.8019.
- Tovey (1984): exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 (or 3,4-SAT variants) is NP-complete; 3,3-SAT is always satisfiable.
- No literature supplies a fully charged universal all-input residualization transformation meeting the N^{1.1+o(1)} local work / N^{0.2+o(1)} space budgets under black-box or white-box access to arbitrary poly-time A.
