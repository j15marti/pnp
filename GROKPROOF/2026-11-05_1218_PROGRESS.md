### Research
Primary sources reconfirmed: Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, Viglas (JACM 2005) establish s < β(t) = ½((t+2)/t^{2} - t) for deterministic random-access machines. Williams improves the subpolynomial-space endpoint to 2 cos(π/7) ≈ 1.8019; Buss-Williams show this is optimal within the alternation-trading framework. Tovey (1984) establishes NP-completeness of 3,4-SAT while 3,3-SAT is always satisfiable; exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 variants remain NP-complete. No published result supplies a fully charged universal residualization of arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solvers meeting the stated budgets.

**Remark (Circuit Simulation Overhead — RESTRICTED).** Pippenger-Fischer simulation of a uniform time-T space-S machine yields a circuit of size O(T log^{2} T) or similar polylog factors. For T = N^{K}, the resulting circuit size is N^{K+o(1)}. Any subsequent compression or residual extraction that preserves correctness must still charge the full simulation cost if performed naively; thus pure pre-computation of the full decision circuit exhausts the local-work budget before any child portfolio is produced. This does not rule out on-the-fly or adaptive extraction of short residuals without materializing the entire circuit.
