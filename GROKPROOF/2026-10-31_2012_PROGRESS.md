## Build / Formalization
**Lemma (Standard Self-Reducibility Cost — PROVED/EXACT).**  
Let \(A\) be any correct total SAT decider with \(T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}\). The classical self-reduction that fixes variables one-by-one, querying \(A\) on the residual formula after each partial assignment, issues \(\Theta(n)\) queries on formulas of size \(\Theta(N)\). The aggregate time is therefore \(N^{1+K+o(1)}\). For every finite \(K\ge 2\) this exceeds the local-work budget \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\). Hence classical self-reducibility cannot realize the required contraction.

**Lemma (Numerical Membership — CONDITIONAL).**  
Assume an all-input transformation producing \(\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) children of length \(\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) with local work \(\le N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(\le N^{0.2+o(1)}\), \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\). Then the derived exponents satisfy
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2.
\]
Direct evaluation shows \(0.2 < \beta(t')\) for every finite \(K\ge 2\) (e.g., \(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511\), \(\beta(1.3)\approx 0.326\)). The pair therefore lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region. The transformation remains unproved.

**Restricted positive result (RESTRICTED).**  
On Horn-SAT and 2-SAT the classical linear-time algorithms realize empty residuals after unit propagation or implication-graph SCC, meeting the size and work bounds without invoking \(A\).
