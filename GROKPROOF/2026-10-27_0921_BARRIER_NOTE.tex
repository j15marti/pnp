\begin{lemma}[Numerical membership, PROVED/EXACT]
\label{lem:numerical}
Let \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\) and \(\zeta = 0.1\) for any fixed finite \(K \ge 2\).  
The conditional exponents
\[
t' \le 1.3 - \frac1{5K},\qquad s' \le 0.2
\]
lie strictly inside the Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas forbidden region
\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
on the indexed random-access model.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Direct numerical verification for the boundary cases \(K=2\) (\(t'=1.2\), \(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511 > 0.2\)) and large \(K\) (\(t'\to 1.3\), \(\beta(1.3)\approx 0.33 > 0.2\)) establishes the strict inequality for every finite \(K\ge 2\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[OPEN]
The existence of a uniform, correctness-preserving, fully charged all-input transformation realizing the above exponents for an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver remains open. Restricted multi-head sequential-access results do not transfer to the FLMV model without an additional charged theorem.
\end{remark}
