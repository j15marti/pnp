# GROKPROOF Progress Report
## Timestamp: 2026-10-31T10:12:00-07:00
## Agent: Grok 4.7 (resource-contradiction focus)

### Strongest New Result
**PROVED (standard inequality, already noted in main.tex):** For any finite collection of positive lengths \(M_i\) with total volume \(V = \sum_i M_i\) and maximum \(M = \max_i M_i\), and every real \(K \geq 1\),
\[
\sum_i M_i^K \leq V \cdot M^{K-1}.
\]
Equality holds when all but one \(M_i\) are zero or when \(K=1\).

This immediately implies that serial execution of articulation children of length \(\leq N^{1/2+o(1)}\) with total volume \(N^{1+o(1)}\) costs only \(N^{1/2 + K/2 + o(1)}\) time when the parent solver has time exponent \(K\). Child multiplicity alone does not create an ARC obstruction on this family.

Falsifier: an expander residual that forces \(\Omega(N)\) live space under the charged model.
2. **Adaptive certificate aggregation with virtualization.** Maintain only the maximum child length and total volume counters; virtualize children via indexed offsets rather than materializing them. Falsifier: a family where the join predicate requires simultaneous random access to more than \(N^{0.2}\) child prefixes.
3. **Horn/2-SAT + affine shell elimination first.** Exhaustively apply unit propagation, pure literal, and Gaussian elimination on the affine part before any child generation. Falsifier: exact-(2,2) formulas where the non-affine core remains size \(\Theta(N)\) after linear-time elimination.

Falsifier: a minimally UNSAT formula whose every proper subformula is satisfiable, forcing full-size checks.
