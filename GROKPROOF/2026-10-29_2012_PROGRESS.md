\subsection{Formalization}
\begin{remark}[Adaptive Charged Residual Barrier]
Let $F=\mathrm{Tseitin}(G,c)\land Q(B)$ with $G$ a linear-size expander and $Q(B)$ an arbitrary exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance of encoding length $\Theta(N)$. Gaussian elimination eliminates the affine shell in $O(N)$ time, leaving an equisatisfiable residual of length $\Theta(N)$. Under black-box or adaptive access to $A$, any procedure that must discover or verify a semantic child of the residual either submits a parent-sized instance (incurring time $N^{K+o(1)}\ge N^{2+o(1)}$) or maintains live intermediate state of size $\Omega(N^{\delta})$ for some $\delta>0.2$. In either case the local-work budget $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $N^{0.2+o(1)}$ are exceeded for all finite $K\ge 2$. Thus this family of semantic decompositions cannot realize the required contraction.
\end{remark}
