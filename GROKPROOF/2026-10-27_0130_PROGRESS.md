## Proposed Falsifiable Mechanisms
1. **Black-box query decomposition via adaptive branching on fixed-width windows.** Falsifiable if an expander-based instance forces query cost exceeding \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) while preserving correctness.
2. **Affine shell elimination followed by solver-dependent core projection.** Falsifiable if the non-affine core requires parent-sized invocation of \(A\) on a positive-density set of inputs.
3. **Certificate-based co-nondeterministic companion under near-boundary seed.** Falsifiable if universal low-cost certificate coverage fails for some minimally UNSAT family.
4. **Circuit simulation of \(A\) with Pippenger-Fischer compression.** Falsifiable if the resulting decision circuit falls outside known conditional exclusions only after uncharged preprocessing.

## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).** Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic SAT solver \(A\) with \(T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}\) for unknown finite \(K,L\), construct (or rigorously refute) a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) (\(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\)) such that the SAT/UNSAT status of \(F\) is correctly recoverable from the children by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\), live parent space is at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), and all ancillary costs are charged, yielding a simultaneous profile inside the FLMV forbidden region.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication to an impossible resource profile) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and P versus NP remains open.
