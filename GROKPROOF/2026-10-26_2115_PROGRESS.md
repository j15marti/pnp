## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).**  
Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic SAT solver \(A\) with \(T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}\) for unknown finite \(K,L\), construct (or rigorously refute) a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) (\(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\)) such that:
1. the SAT/UNSAT status of \(F\) is correctly recoverable from the statuses of the children by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\);
2. live parent space is at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\);
3. all discovery, verification, access, memory, and combination costs are charged to the stated budgets;
4. the resulting simultaneous profile on the children lies inside the FLMV forbidden region.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication to an impossible resource profile) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and \(P\) versus \(NP\) remains open.
