\section{Known Unconditional Lower Bounds}
The strongest unconditional circuit lower bounds for explicit functions remain modest relative to the thresholds needed to separate P from NP.

\begin{itemize}
\item For general Boolean circuits over the \(B_2\) basis, the best known lower bound for an explicit function in P is \(3.5n - O(\log n)\) (Mishra--Santhanam, ECCC TR26-260, October 2026), improving the previous \(3.1n-o(n)\) of Li--Yang. The proof uses refined gate elimination with reparameterisation on an underlying independent parameter space, applied to affine dispersers of dimension \(O(\log n)\). For the \(U_2\) basis the best remains \(5n-o(n)\). No subsequent improvements to general-circuit bounds for explicit functions in P have appeared through ECCC TR26-277.
\end{itemize}

\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
for the applicable range of \(t\), and in particular cannot be decided in time \(N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}\) and space \(N^{o(1)}\) (Williams). These are the strongest published simultaneous bounds used in the present investigation.

\section{Conditional Resource Contraction}
Under the temporary assumption that a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) exists with
\[
T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for unknown finite \(K,L\ge 2\), the parameters \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\) would, if an all-input transformation of the stated budget existed, yield a simultaneous profile
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2
\]
that lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region for every finite \(K\ge 2\). The recurrence
\[
t'\le\max(1.1,0.6+t/2),\qquad s'\le\max(0.5,s/2)
\]
has fixed point \((1.2,0.5)\) satisfying the tangent certificate \(433t+216s<630\).

\section*{Conclusion}
The evidence from hierarchy theorems, circuit lower bounds, magnification thresholds, and barrier results points consistently toward the difficulty of proving either equality or separation by currently available asymptotic techniques. The quantitative gap between the best explicit general-circuit lower bound (\(3.5n-O(\log n)\)) and the thresholds that would resolve P versus NP remains substantial and unclosed. Conditional numerical contractions enter known forbidden time-space regions but do not establish the required universal transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver. No contradiction has been identified that forces one resolution in the universes examined. P versus NP remains open.
