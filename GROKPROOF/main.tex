\section{Purpose and methodology}
The goal is to follow available evidence toward asymptotic (rather than hard algebraic) lower bounds, using known theorems from the literature, while systematically searching for contradictions in assumed universes such as P$=$NP, P$\neq$NP, RP$=$P, P$=$UP, P$=$BPP, P$=$ZPP, MA$=$AM, and related collapses.

Work proceeds by:
\begin{itemize}
\item Surveying primary literature on circuit lower bounds, barriers, hierarchy theorems, and algorithmic methods for lower bounds.
\item Distinguishing restricted models (e.g., AC$^0$, ACC$^0$, monotone circuits) from general Boolean circuits and uniform Turing machines.
\item Identifying candidate asymptotic phenomena (e.g., superlinear circuit size, superpolynomial time, communication, information complexity) that might scale.
\item Auditing for relativization, algebrization, natural proofs, and model mismatches.
\item Recording only what is supported; retiring routes that produce contradictions with known theorems or easy controls.
\end{itemize}

\section{Known lower bounds and barriers (literature summary)}
\subsection{General Boolean circuits}
The best known explicit lower bounds for general circuits over the full binary basis remain linear:
\begin{itemize}
\item Blum (1984): $3n - o(n)$.
\item Find--Golovnev--Hirsch--Kulikov (FOCS 2016): $(3 + 1/86)n - o(n)$ for affine dispersers (in P).
\item Li--Yang (STOC 2022): $3.1n - o(n)$.
\item Mishra--Santhanam (ECCC TR26-260, 19 October 2026): $3.5n - o(n)$ for an explicit function $F \in$ P. Technique: gate elimination with reparameterization on independent parameters rather than direct substitution, simplifying prior proofs. The bound holds for any affine disperser of dimension $O(\log n)$; Xin Li's construction supplies such an explicit disperser in P.
\end{itemize}
Almost all functions require $\Omega(2^n / n)$ size (Shannon). Superpolynomial explicit lower bounds for NP functions remain \OPEN{}. Superlinear bounds for NP are also \OPEN{}. The 3.5n result is for a function in P; it does not yet yield an NP lower bound. Asymptotic scaling of the reparameterization (independent-parameter space dimension) beyond constant-factor coefficient improvement remains \CAND{}.

\subsection{Restricted circuit classes}
\begin{itemize}
\item AC$^0$: exponential lower bounds for parity, majority (H\aa{}stad, Yao, etc.). The classical parity barrier of $2^{\Omega(n^{1/(d-1)})}$ for depth-$d$ was broken: OpenAI (2026) gave an explicit function in P requiring $2^{\omega(\sqrt{n})}$ for depth-3 OR-AND-OR circuits; Chukhin (ECCC TR26-248, October 2026) broke it for every constant depth $d\ge 3$ with the trilinear function $\sum_{i,j} a_{i+j} x_i y_j \bmod 2$, requiring $2^{\Omega((n\log n / \log\log n)^{1/(d-1)})}$ size. Subsequent improvements (arXiv:2610.27127) reach $2^{\Omega_d((n\log n)^{1/(d-1)})}$ for several explicit functions in P.
\item Monotone circuits: exponential for clique (Razborov, Alon--Boppana).
\item ACC$^0$: Williams (2011/2014) showed NEXP $\not\subseteq$ ACC$^0$/poly via algorithms-to-lower-bounds (faster ACC-SAT implies lower bounds). Strengthened by Murray--Williams, Chen et al. to quasipolynomial and sub-half-exponential sizes, and average-case versions (Chen--Oliveira--Santhanam).
\item TC$^0$, depth-2 threshold, etc.: many open.
\end{itemize}
These restricted lower bounds do not transfer directly to general circuits or uniform P vs NP; the barriers prevent naive lifting.

\subsection{Barriers}
\begin{itemize}
\item Relativization (Baker--Gill--Solovay 1975): oracles with P$=$NP and P$\neq$NP.
\item Natural proofs (Razborov--Rudich 1997): combinatorial properties useful against P/poly would yield PRG distinguishers, conflicting with crypto assumptions (or even some unconditionally for weak classes).
\item Algebrization (Aaronson--Wigderson 2008): arithmetization techniques are insufficient for P vs NP, P vs RP, NEXP vs P/poly, etc. Recent extensions (Chen--Hu--Ren 2025) to more classes.
\item Resource-bounded unprovability (Okamoto--Kashima): certain asymptotic lower bounds unprovable in PTM-$\omega$-consistent extensions of PA.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hierarchy and diagonalization}
Time hierarchy theorems give strict separations with more resources (e.g., DTIME$(n) \subsetneq$ DTIME$(n^2)$). Linear-time versions of P $\subsetneq$ NP exist in restricted senses (Paul--Pippenger--Szemer\'edi--Trotter). These relativize and do not resolve the polynomial case. Recent fine-grained improvements (Alman--Williams, October 2026) refute some textbook hypotheses for 3SUM and APSP but do not resolve P vs NP.

\section{Universes and contradiction searches}
We systematically examine assumed equalities/inequalities for internal contradictions with known theorems.

\subsection{P $=$ NP universe}
Assumes every NP language has a polynomial-time algorithm. Implications include collapse of PH to P, existence of efficient algorithms for SAT, factoring (under certain reductions), and many optimization problems. No absolute contradiction with known theorems is known; it is consistent with current evidence but widely believed false. Cryptographic collapse and the non-existence of one-way functions would follow under standard assumptions. The linear circuit bounds for functions in P are compatible (since the hard function is itself in P). The new AC$^0$ lower bounds for functions in P are likewise compatible. \OPEN{} for contradiction.

\subsection{P $\neq$ NP universe}
The standard conjecture. Consistent with all known lower bounds and barriers. No proof exists. \OPEN{}.

\subsection{RP $=$ P, BPP $=$ P, ZPP $=$ P}
Derandomization conjectures. Impagliazzo--Wigderson: BPP $=$ P if E requires exponential circuits. Williams-style methods and hardness-vs-randomness tradeoffs make these plausible but unproved. Recent work (Doron--Moshkovitz--Oh--Zuckerman, ECCC TR26-082) improves PRG parameters under nondeterministic hardness; no result yet recovers near-optimal PRGs under pure deterministic hardness. Linear circuit bounds for P-functions do not contradict derandomization (they are too weak). Related to P vs RP which algebrizes. \OPEN{}.

\subsection{P $=$ UP, MA $=$ AM, etc.}
Unambiguous polynomial time, Arthur--Merlin. Related to graph isomorphism (now known in quasi-P) and other intermediate problems. No known contradiction forcing separation or collapse beyond known results. \OPEN{}.

\section{Asymptotic directions under consideration}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Gate-elimination refinements and independent-parameter reparameterization (building on Mishra--Santhanam 2026 and predecessors) for slightly stronger linear bounds; the reparameterization idea is a \CAND{} for further incremental gains but unlikely to reach superlinear without new ideas. Formal asymptotic scaling of the independent-parameter space (e.g., if the parameter dimension grows mildly with $n$) remains \OPEN{}; a super-constant coefficient or $n\log n$ term would be significant but currently unsupported.
\item Algorithms-to-lower-bounds extensions: faster SAT or $\#$SAT algorithms for larger circuit classes (beyond ACC) implying stronger separations (e.g., against TC$^0$ or general circuits). The constant-depth breakthroughs show that barrier-breaking is possible in restricted models via sophisticated restriction and switching arguments.
\item Fine-grained and communication-complexity transfers: missing-string, XOR-missing-string communication problems yielding new algebrization or direct lower bounds.
\item Information-complexity or reconstruction-based arguments that survive hostile controls (Horn, 2-SAT, affine, bounded treewidth).
\item Resource-bounded consistency and unprovability boundaries: what asymptotic statements are consistent with weak theories. Consistency of EXP $\not\subseteq$ P/poly with certain bounded arithmetics has been shown (Atserias--M\"uller, October 2026 talk).
\end{enumerate}
All remain \CAND{} or \OPEN{}.
