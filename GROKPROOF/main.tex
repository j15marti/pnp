\section{Known Unconditional Lower Bounds}
The strongest unconditional circuit lower bounds for explicit functions remain modest relative to the thresholds needed to separate P from NP.

\section{Time-Space Tradeoffs}
\KNOWN\ (Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, JACM 2005; Williams, CCC 2007 / TOCT 2013).  
In the indexed read-only-input random-access model, SAT cannot be decided simultaneously in time \(N^{t+o(1)}\) and space \(N^{s+o(1)}\) whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
for the applicable range of \(t\), and in particular cannot be decided in time \(N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}\) and space \(N^{o(1)}\) (Williams). Numerical evaluation confirms \(2\cos(\pi/7)\approx 1.8019377358\).

\section{Conditional Resource Contraction}
Under the temporary assumption that a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) exists with
\[
T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for unknown finite \(K,L\ge 2\), the parameters \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\) would, if an all-input transformation of the stated budget existed, yield a simultaneous profile
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2
\]
that lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region for every finite \(K\ge 2\).

\begin{lemma}[Numerical Membership \PROVED]
For every finite real number \(K\ge 2\),
\[
\beta\Bigl(1.3-\frac1{5K}\Bigr) > 0.2,
\]
where \(\beta(t)=\frac12\bigl((t+2)/t^2 - t\bigr)\). Explicit values:
\begin{itemize}
\item \(K=2\): \(t'=1.2\), \(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511111 > 0.2\);
\item \(K=3\): \(t'\approx 1.233333\), \(\beta\approx 0.446153 > 0.2\);
\item \(K=5\): \(t'=1.26\), \(\beta\approx 0.396707 > 0.2\);
\item \(K=10\): \(t'=1.28\), \(\beta\approx 0.360977 > 0.2\);
\item large \(K\): \(t'\to 1.3\), \(\beta(1.3)\approx 0.326331 > 0.2\).
\end{itemize}
Thus every such conditional profile is forbidden by FLMV.
\end{lemma}

The recurrence
\[
t'\le\max(1.1,0.6+t/2),\qquad s'\le\max(0.5,s/2)
\]
has fixed point \((1.2,0.5)\) which is forbidden (\(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511 > 0.5\)).

\PROVED. Classical self-reducibility incurs aggregate time \(N^{1+K+o(1)}\), exceeding the local-work budget.

\OPEN. The required universal all-input construction of \(\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children of length \(\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), with aggregate local work \(\le N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(\le N^{0.2+o(1)}\), including every discovery, correctness, access, memory and combination cost, remains unproved.

\RESTRICTED. On Horn and 2-SAT, restricted contractions are feasible independently of \(A\). On exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 expander-like families, unit propagation, incidence multigraph methods, cycle covers, and perfect matchings fail to produce the required residual size without exceeding budgets. Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete by refinements of Tovey (1984).

\begin{remark}[Strengthened Restricted Live-Space Accounting --- RESTRICTED]
On linear-size expander exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 families, any adaptive controller that maintains intermediate configuration or memoization state of size \(o(N^{0.2})\) and issues no parent-sized queries to \(A\) cannot correctly produce a nonempty proper residual child of encoding length \(\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) for \(\lambda=1/(5K)\). Discovery of consistent partial assignments on expanders forces either linear space or parent-sized verification.
\end{remark}

\section*{Conclusion}
Conditional numerical contractions enter known forbidden time-space regions but do not establish the required universal transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver. P versus NP remains open.
