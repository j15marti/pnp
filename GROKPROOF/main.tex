\section{Executive Status}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}.  
No unconditional polynomial-time algorithm for SAT, nor an unconditional super-polynomial lower bound for all deterministic algorithms, has been established.  
The primary research spine under active investigation is an ARC/FLMV simultaneous time--space contraction: transform an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver \( A \) into a simultaneous resource profile lying inside the Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (FLMV) forbidden region.

\section{Known Results (PROVED or KNOWN)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas (JACM 2005): For deterministic random-access Turing machines, SAT cannot be solved in time \( N^t \) and space \( N^s \) whenever \( s < \beta(t) = \frac12\bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\bigr) \) in the applicable range.
\item Williams: SAT requires time \( N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}\approx N^{1.8019} \) on machines with space \( N^{o(1)} \). Buss--Williams show this endpoint is optimal within the alternation-trading framework.
\item Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 (three distinct variables per clause, each variable exactly twice positive and twice negative) is NP-complete.
\item Numerical membership: the conditional profiles \( t'\le 1.3-1/(5K) \), \( s'\le 0.2 \) satisfy \( s'<\beta(t') \) for every finite \( K\ge 2 \).
\item Classical self-reducibility incurs \( \Theta(N) \) parent-sized queries and cost \( N^{1+K+o(1)} \).
\end{itemize}

\section{Load-Bearing Open Lemma}
\textbf{Universal all-input fully charged transformation (OPEN).}\\
Given the code of an arbitrary fixed polynomial-time SAT solver \( A \), produce at most \( N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} \) ordinary SAT children, each of length at most \( N^{\lambda+o(1)} \), such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item aggregate local work (including all discovery, verification, access, memory, and combination costs) is at most \( N^{1.1+o(1)} \);
\item live parent space is at most \( N^{0.2+o(1)} \);
\item the resulting simultaneous profile lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region.
\end{itemize}
The transformation must hold for every ordinary \( N \)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance and for adaptive/state-dependent algorithms.  

A successful construction would yield an unconditional contradiction with the FLMV/Williams bounds under the random-access model. A rigorous refutation would close this particular route.

Additional open items: authenticated historical \( R_{\mathrm{mix341}} \) byte grammar for LOCAL/DEF, exact minimum on the bridge control (mixed 35-node tree still possible), genuinely superpolynomial full mixed \( \overline{W} \) on a certified hard family, and the noncircular same-calculus B3 implication \( \mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm{P}\Rightarrow\overline{W}_R \) polynomial.
