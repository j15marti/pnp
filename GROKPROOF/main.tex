\section*{Living Proof Status: Restricted Affine-Cover Sharpness and Guarded Controls (2026-11-07)}

\subsection*{Summary of most recent corpus (commits through 2026-11-07T21:19:20Z)}
The latest verified artifacts advance LSPV-1/486 (restricted affine-cover sharpness for independent AND gates) and concurrent LSPV-2/476 (independent signed Tseitin pure-cofactor controls). Earlier same-day work includes guarded-branch controls (LSPV-1/483), restricted Res(XOR) transfers, and safe-range audits. These remain strictly scoped to declared fragments, circuits, or grammars. No result establishes a superpolynomial lower bound against arbitrary SAT algorithms, a full native \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) authentication, or an all-input same-machine residualization into the FLMV forbidden region.

\begin{theorem}[486.1, PROVED-RESTRICTED]
Let \(X = (u_1,v_1,\dots,u_g,v_g) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{2g}\) and \(y_i = u_i v_i\). For any covering \(\mathcal{H}\) of the full Boolean cube by nonempty affine subspaces (overlaps permitted) such that each \(y_i\) is affine on every cell of \(\mathcal{H}\),
\[
\dim H \le g \qquad\text{and}\qquad |\mathcal{H}| \ge 2^g.
\]
Both bounds are tight (coordinate splits achieve equality). Independent exhaustive enumeration for \(g=1,2,3\) confirms the dimension bound on all affine cosets. This is a circuit-only modeling control; it does not bound mixed-refutation size, suppress shared DAGs or legal shortcuts, or yield a SAT decision lower bound.
\end{theorem}

\subsection*{Strict controls and assumption-scope}
Finite controls confirm source authentication, polarity preservation, gadget projections, and affine-cell rank on small instances. Semantic weakenings are polynomially simulable, but syntactic authentication of the full mixed rule set (Resolution, AND3, GF(2), LOCAL-CONVERT, paid definitions, branching, and assumption discharge) remains open. Restricted-subgrammar hardness does not extrapolate to the full calculus.

\subsection*{Open needs and external clues}
The load-bearing open obligations remain:
\begin{itemize}
\item Exact native authentication of mixed \(R_{\mathrm{mix341}}\) rules without semantic oracles (P2-D1). Superpolynomial all-rule minimum lower bound in the full authenticated calculus (P2-B1).
\item Noncircular B3 compact-witness / prefix-search transfer (P2-B2).
\item All-input, solver-dependent, fully charged residualization of every polynomial SAT algorithm into the FLMV forbidden box on the same native model (P2-A2).
\end{itemize}

External references supply models for mixed clause/XOR certificates, verified checkers, shortest restricted proofs, and formal resource accounting:
\begin{itemize}
\item meelgroup/frat-xor processes XOR-aware UNSAT proofs, translating FRAT-XOR into XLRUP with a cake_xlrup formally verified checker. CryptoMiniSat generates input proofs. Relevant for clause/GF(2) certificate interfaces and source-clause/parity authentication.
\item tanyongkiam/cake_lpr provides LRAT/LPR proof checking through CakeML, with source and formal proofs in CakeML/cakeml/examples/lpr_checker. Separates untrusted producers from verified checkers; final planned release announced 1 Nov 2026.
\item peitl/short-proof is a SAT-based tool computing shortest Resolution proofs by encoding “Does this formula have a Resolution refutation of length at most \(s\)?”. Closest match to minimum-derivation-cost experiments within Resolution (and specified QBF variants), not the full mixed calculus.
\item cslib-community/complexitylib (Lean 4) and uds-psl/coq-library-complexity supply formal definitions, time hierarchy, NP-completeness, Cook–Levin, and resource analyses for machines and lambda calculi.
\end{itemize}
These tools authenticate specific systems or establish minima within declared proof systems. Neither establishes a lower bound against arbitrary SAT algorithms. For the project, the value is explicit treatment of inference rules and evidence.

\subsection*{Status}
P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}. No unconditional decision-time lower bound, ARC edge, or universal residualization has been established. The restricted affine-cover sharpness, Res(XOR) backtranslation, syntactic local mixed-to-Res(XOR) transfer, guarded branch controls, and signed Tseitin pure-cofactor controls are recorded as supporting evidence only. Finite controls confirm the stated lemmas but do not constitute native FLMV resource verification for unrestricted solvers.
