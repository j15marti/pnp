\section{Purpose and methodology}
The goal is to follow available evidence toward asymptotic (rather than hard algebraic) lower bounds, using known theorems from the literature, while systematically searching for contradictions in assumed universes such as P$=$NP, P$\neq$NP, RP$=$P, P$=$UP, P$=$BPP, P$=$ZPP, MA$=$AM, and related collapses.

Work proceeds by:
\begin{itemize}
\item Surveying primary literature on circuit lower bounds, barriers, hierarchy theorems, and algorithmic methods for lower bounds.
\item Distinguishing restricted models (e.g., AC$^0$, ACC$^0$, monotone circuits, fixed-width acquisition, charged transport) from general Boolean circuits and uniform Turing machines.
\item Identifying candidate asymptotic phenomena (e.g., superlinear circuit size, superpolynomial time, communication, information complexity) that might scale.
\item Auditing for relativization, algebrization, natural proofs, and model mismatches.
\item Recording only what is supported; retiring routes that produce contradictions with known theorems or easy controls.
\end{itemize}

\section{Known lower bounds and barriers (literature summary)}
\subsection{General Boolean circuits}
The best known explicit lower bounds for general circuits over the full binary basis remain linear:
\begin{itemize}
\item Blum (1984): $3n - o(n)$.
\item Find--Golovnev--Hirsch--Kulikov (FOCS 2016): $(3 + 1/86)n - o(n)$ for affine dispersers (in P).
\item Li--Yang (STOC 2022): $3.1n - o(n)$.
\item Mishra--Santhanam (ECCC TR26-260, 19 October 2026): $3.5n - O(\log n)$ for an explicit function $F \in$ P. Technique: gate elimination with reparameterization on independent parameters. Holds for any affine disperser of dimension $O(\log n)$; Xin Li's construction supplies an explicit one in P.
\end{itemize}
Almost all functions require $\Omega(2^n / n)$ size (Shannon). Superpolynomial explicit lower bounds for NP functions remain \OPEN{}. Superlinear bounds for NP are also \OPEN{}. The 3.5n result is for a function in P; it does not yield an NP lower bound. Asymptotic scaling of the reparameterization beyond constant-factor coefficient improvement remains \CAND{}.

\subsection{Restricted circuit classes}
\begin{itemize}
\item AC$^0$: exponential lower bounds for parity, majority (H{\aa}stad et al.). Improved: OpenAI (2026) $2^{\omega(\sqrt{n})}$ for depth-3 OR-AND-OR for a function in P; Chukhin (ECCC TR26-248) broke the barrier for every constant depth $d\ge 3$; Li--Zhong (ECCC TR26-261 / arXiv:2610.27127, 19 October 2026) further improve to $2^{\Omega_d((n\log n)^{1/(d-1)})}$ for three explicit functions in P (inverse trace, Goldreich--Tal trilinear, OpenAI polynomial evaluator), with short-point-list CNF decomposition. Also improved correlation bounds and some upper bounds.
\item Monotone circuits: exponential for clique (Razborov, Alon--Boppana).
\item ACC$^0$: Williams (2011/2014) NEXP $\not\subseteq$ ACC$^0$/poly via algorithms-to-lower-bounds; strengthened by Murray--Williams et al. to quasipolynomial and sub-half-exponential sizes.
\item Depth-2 threshold circuits: Chen--Tal--Wang (ECCC TR26-039) $n^{2.5-\varepsilon}$ for a function in E$^{NP}$.
\end{itemize}
These do not transfer directly to general circuits or uniform P vs NP due to barriers. Short-point-list decompositions are \CAND{} for further restriction arguments but confined to constant depth.

\subsection{Barriers}
\begin{itemize}
\item Relativization (Baker--Gill--Solovay 1975).
\item Natural proofs (Razborov--Rudich 1997).
\item Algebrization (Aaronson--Wigderson 2008); extensions by Chen--Hu--Ren (2025).
\item Resource-bounded unprovability (Okamoto et al.); Atserias--M{\"u}ller (arXiv:2604.25251, April 2026): consistency of EXP $\not\subseteq$ P/poly with $S^1_2$. Related consistency results for NEXP $\not\subseteq$ P/poly with $V^0_2$ (Atserias--Buss--M{\"u}ller) and stronger variants (Thapen 2025).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hierarchy and diagonalization}
Time hierarchy theorems give strict separations with more resources. Linear-time versions of P $\subsetneq$ NP exist in restricted senses (Paul--Pippenger--Szemer{\'e}di--Trotter). These relativize and do not resolve the polynomial case. Fine-grained improvements (e.g., Alman--Williams for 3SUM/APSP) do not resolve P vs NP. Recent ECCC TR26-268 (Dror, 22 October 2026) gives a linear lower bound on testing graph isomorphism in the bounded-degree model, relevant to intermediate problems but not resolving P=UP or similar.

\section{Repository restricted models and asymptotic implications}
The j15marti/pnp corpus (root \path{main.tex} as of 2026-10-23, including BACKDOOR-PRODUCT-FIREWALL97, DEGREE-BOUNDARY96, PALETTE-SOURCE-CYCLE56, SELECTOR-GRAPH-BOUNDARY95, EXCEPTION-KERNEL94, and predecessors) establishes many \PROVED{} restricted results:
\begin{itemize}
\item Fixed-width implication chains yield exact $\Omega(N/\log N)$ acquisition bounds in declared models, with positive-remainder charged transport inequalities, but pure query amplification beyond input order is retired.
\item Independent products, disjoint components, guarded selectors, shared-interface tables, relation-algebra widenings, exception kernels, and selector-graph boundaries all collapse to input-order acquisition or admit linear or polynomial charged recognizers (e.g., affine checksums, 2-CNF projections, $O(N + 3^c p)$ for exceptions, $O(ed^2 + gd + d^c(\cdot))$ for feedback vertex sets).
\item Domain-two arbitrary binary CSP compiles to 2-SAT; domain-three inequality is graph 3-coloring, yet supplied feedback sets yield exponential-in-$c$ but polynomial for fixed $c$ algorithms. Matched table scans meet the field-query lower bounds at input order.
\item DEGREE-BOUNDARY96: Maximum-degree-three 3-colorability is linear-time decidable by $K_4$-component detection (Brooks); maximum-degree-four is NP-complete. A $p$-block equal-metadata family forces $\Theta(N/\log N)$ packed-edge-word queries on the SAT input for every exact decider of the fixed language, matched by a linear scan. Direct inclusion preserves genuine all-decider acquisition but does not amplify beyond input order.
\item BACKDOOR-PRODUCT-FIREWALL97: Shared-switch families replicated in $p$ variable-disjoint components yield proper 3-CNF with minimum strong syntactic 2-CNF backdoor exactly $h=p$. The product is SAT exactly when all $p$ component parities are even, so a charged recognizer and source scan decide it in $O(pq)$ word operations. With fixed $q=3$, $h=\Theta(N/\log N)$ while decision remains input-linear. Pointwise differential coverage forces $\Omega(N/\log N)$ word acquisition, matched by the scan. Paid materializing reductions have nonpositive transport remainder. Thus even a linear minimum backdoor, essential ternaries, and disjoint-product amplification do not force $2^{\Omega(h)}$ work. Direct inclusion preserves but cannot amplify the input-order bound.
\item Many structural features (treewidth, density, cycle rank, syntactic backdoors, feedback vertex set size alone, degree bounds) do not force unrestricted superpolynomial time due to efficient source-aware algorithms.
\end{itemize}
These support the asymptotic observation that structured instances often admit polynomial (even linear) algorithms despite NP-completeness of the language, consistent with the difficulty of proving uniform lower bounds. No transfer to unrestricted Turing-machine time for arbitrary NP languages is established; all such routes remain \OPEN{}. The charged positive-remainder inequality continues to block naive amplification. The next target in the root document is a non-product semantic obstruction $r(F)$ with charged acquisition-or-continuation inequality $T_A(F)\ge 2^{\Omega(r(F))}/N^{O(1)}$ surviving known shortcuts.

\section{Universes and contradiction searches}
\subsection{P $=$ NP universe}
Compatible with all known theorems: linear circuit bounds for P-functions (now 3.5n), strengthened AC$^0$ bounds for P-functions, derandomization conjectures, consistency results with weak theories. Cryptographic collapse would follow under standard assumptions, but no absolute contradiction. The restricted acquisition ceilings in the repository are upper-bound phenomena and do not force a contradiction. \OPEN{} for contradiction.

\subsection{P $\neq$ NP universe}
The standard conjecture. Consistent with known lower bounds, barriers, and the existence of efficient algorithms for many structured NP-complete instances. No proof exists. \OPEN{}.

\subsection{RP $=$ P, BPP $=$ P, ZPP $=$ P}
Derandomization conjectures (Impagliazzo--Wigderson et al.). Plausible via hardness-vs-randomness but unproved. Linear and AC$^0$ bounds for P-functions are too weak or orthogonal to force contradictions. Related to algebrizing questions. \OPEN{}.

\subsection{P $=$ UP, MA $=$ AM, etc.}
Related to intermediate problems (e.g., graph isomorphism in quasi-P; TR26-268 gives linear lower bounds in bounded-degree testing model). No known contradiction forcing separation or collapse. \OPEN{}.

No new contradictions identified in any examined universe this run. Consistency of EXP $\not\subseteq$ P/poly with $S^1_2$ (Atserias--M{\"u}ller) and related NEXP results remain compatible with both P$=$NP and P$\neq$NP under resource-bounded logics. Repository restricted bounds do not interact with these consistency statements to produce contradictions. BACKDOOR-PRODUCT-FIREWALL97 further illustrates that linear backdoors need not force exponential work.

\section{Asymptotic directions under consideration}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Gate-elimination refinements and independent-parameter reparameterization (Mishra--Santhanam) for stronger linear bounds; superlinear remains \CAND{} / unsupported. Successive reparameterizations preserving affine-disperser properties for mildly growing dimension are under examination.
\item Algorithms-to-lower-bounds extensions beyond ACC (Williams-style).
\item Short-point-list decompositions adapted beyond constant depth (Li--Zhong); model mismatch for general circuits.
\item Fine-grained / communication transfers.
\item Information-complexity or reconstruction arguments surviving hostile controls (as emphasized in the repository).
\item Resource-bounded consistency boundaries (Atserias--M{\"u}ller et al.).
\item Interaction of short-point-list or top-down techniques with the repository's charged transport inequalities for model-preserving transfers.
\item Potential asymptotic accumulation from slowly growing feedback-vertex-set, exception-kernel, degree-boundary, or backdoor-product parameters; currently matched by linear scans.
\end{enumerate}
All remain \CAND{} or \OPEN{}. Repository evidence retires several candidate amplifiers as unrestricted mechanisms. Direct-inclusion and product families preserve acquisition order without amplification.
