### Open Needs Identified
1. **Bridge from restricted resource lower bounds to arbitrary algorithms.** Restricted pebbling, Resolution, or mixed-calculus certificate lower bounds do not imply superpolynomial time for every SAT solver without a model-preserving reduction that forces every polynomial-time (or subexponential) algorithm to produce or simulate an equivalent high-cost certificate or computation DAG.

2. **Independent formal verification of resource accounting.** Current checkers (V8, etc.) are pinned; integration with verified systems (CakeML LPR, cake_xlrup) would strengthen trustworthiness.

3. **Shortest-proof baselines.** Comparison against Resolution shortest-proof tools (peitl/short-proof) on the same fixture instances to quantify the strength of the mixed calculus.

4. **Formal complexity-library anchoring.** Use cslib-community/complexitylib or uds-psl/coq-library-complexity for machine models, time/space definitions, and simulation theorems rather than informal RAM models.
