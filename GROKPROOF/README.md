# GROKPROOF

Living LaTeX document for asymptotic, evidence-driven investigation of P versus NP.

This folder maintains an independent living proof document (`main.tex`) that follows the literature and known theorems without assuming the truth of either P = NP or P ≠ NP. It tracks asymptotic lower-bound candidates, barriers, hierarchy theorems, and consistency checks across complexity-theoretic universes. It incorporates verified findings from the concurrent LSPV/RMV investigations in this repository (firewall, boundary, charged-transport, adaptive-trajectory, backdoor-product, degree-boundary, and selector-graph results through 24 October 2026).

Status: P versus NP remains open. No asymptotic contradiction forcing one resolution over the other has been identified. The document was last updated on 23 October 2026 (22:14 UTC) to integrate the latest LSPV adaptive-trajectory firewall, backdoor-product, degree-boundary, selector-graph, and Horn-transport ceilings (which reinforce the consistent pattern of matched Ω(N/log N) acquisition bounds and nonpositive charged transport remainders) alongside the explicit 3.5n-o(n) general circuit lower bound of Mishra--Santhanam (ECCC TR26-260), improved AC^0 bounds (Li--Zhong, ECCC TR26-261; Chukhin), and the simple general magnification of Atserias--Müller (arXiv:2503.24061).
