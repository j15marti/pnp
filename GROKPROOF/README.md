# GROKPROOF

Living LaTeX document for asymptotic, evidence-driven investigation of P versus NP.

This folder maintains an independent living proof document (`main.tex`) that follows the literature and known theorems without assuming the truth of either P = NP or P ≠ NP. It tracks asymptotic lower-bound candidates, barriers, hierarchy theorems, and consistency checks across complexity-theoretic universes. It incorporates verified findings from the concurrent LSPV/RMV investigations in this repository (firewall, boundary, and charged-transport results through October 2026).

Status: P versus NP remains open. No asymptotic contradiction forcing one resolution over the other has been identified. The document was last updated on 23 October 2026 to incorporate the explicit $3.5n-o(n)$ general circuit lower bound of Mishra--Santhanam (ECCC TR26-260) and the simple general magnification of Atserias--Müller (arXiv:2503.24061), alongside prior 2026 literature on AC$^0$ improvements, almost-everywhere ACC$^0$ lower bounds, and Thapen consistency results.
