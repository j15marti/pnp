\section{Latest Restricted Result (TMR-1/039 / GUARDLIFT071)}
For any UNSAT CNF \(F\) on private variables \(Y\) and \(k \ge 3\) shared variables \(X\), the guarded joint formula \(G_{F,k} = A_{F,k} \land B_k\) satisfies:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{RES}}(F) \le S_{\mathrm{RES}}(G_{F,k}) \le q_k \, S_{\mathrm{RES}}(F) + k^2,
\end{equation}
where \(q_k = 1 + \binom{k}{2}\), \(S_{\mathrm{RES}}\) is the minimum native shared-DAG Resolution inference count, the unique Craig interpolant of \(A\) versus \(B\) is exactly the exact-one predicate \(J_k\), and \(J_k\) is non-Schaefer yet has circuit size \(O(k^2)\).

For the odd-charge Tseitin family on \(H\)-vertex cubic expanders with \(k = H\), this yields a restricted exponential native Resolution lower bound of \(2^{\Omega(H)}\) on a formula of encoding length \(\Theta(H^3 \log H)\), paired with an easy interpolant. Finite executable checks confirm the construction and lifting for small cases (160\,244 assertions, zero failures).

This does not transfer to arbitrary deterministic SAT machines, does not produce a superpolynomial fusion-safe initial potential, and does not resolve P versus NP.
