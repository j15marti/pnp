\section{Resource Contradiction: Adaptive Residual Barrier and Source-Normalized Transfer}

The all-input ARC/FLMV contraction remains unproved. Primary literature reconfirms the FLMV simultaneous bounds $s < \beta(t) = \frac12\bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\bigr)$ and the Williams subpolynomial-space endpoint $2\cos(\pi/7)\approx 1.8019$. No new primary source supplies a universal fully charged all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver $A$.

\subsection{Proposed Falsifiable Mechanisms}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Adaptive residual core with memoized boundary cuts.} Any adaptive realization of $A$ that processes a residual non-affine core $Q(B)$ of encoding length $\Theta(N)$ after linear Gaussian elimination of a Tseitin shell must charge live intermediate state of size at least the boundary cut or repeated parent-sized queries. Falsify by exhibiting a uniform adaptive schedule that reduces the residual to $N^{\lambda+o(1)}$-size ordinary SAT children at total charged cost $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $N^{0.2+o(1)}$ on a positive-density family of expander Tseitin plus exact-(2,2) instances.
\item \textbf{Source-normalized one-child reduction overhead.} An explicit child of physical size $N^{\alpha+o(1)}$ transfers target exponents $(t,s)$ to source $(\max\{r,\alpha t,h\},\max\{u,\alpha,\alpha s,v\})$. Falsify if a uniform on-demand access implementation absorbs the $\alpha$ storage floor at polynomial native access cost for arbitrary SAT without exiting the local budget.
\item \textbf{Williams product obstruction on residual schedules.} The Williams time--space product bound $\Omega(N^{\phi-o(1)})$ with $\phi$ the golden ratio may exclude residual-query schedules whose product falls below the bound even if individual exponents avoid the FLMV region. Falsify by a uniform residual-handling procedure whose charged product remains strictly below the bound while recovering the SAT answer inside $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ work.
\item \textbf{One-sided nonlinear projection verification.} Sound abstractions $G$ with $F\Rightarrow G$ require verification of UNSAT($F\land\neg G$). Falsify if a fixed grammar of inexpensive proof-carrying rules generates complete coverings for all exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instances inside the stated resource bounds.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Formalization}
\begin{remark}[Adaptive Charged Residual Barrier (reconfirmed)]
Let $F=\mathrm{Tseitin}(G,c)\land Q(B)$ with $G$ a linear-size expander and $Q(B)$ an arbitrary exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance of encoding length $\Theta(N)$. Gaussian elimination eliminates the affine shell in $O(N)$ time, leaving an equisatisfiable residual of length $\Theta(N)$. Under black-box or adaptive access to $A$, any procedure that must discover or verify a semantic child of the residual either submits a parent-sized instance (incurring time $N^{K+o(1)}\ge N^{2+o(1)}$) or maintains live intermediate state of size $\Omega(N^\delta)$ for some $\delta>0.2$. In either case the local-work budget $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $N^{0.2+o(1)}$ are exceeded for all finite $K\ge 2$. Thus this family of semantic decompositions cannot realize the required contraction. Source-normalized transfers impose an additional storage floor of $\alpha$ that cannot be absorbed without a native access-time tax that itself must be charged.
\end{remark}

The conditional numerical membership remains PROVED/EXACT. The universal all-input fully charged transformation remains OPEN.

\subsection{Next Decisive Lemma}
\textbf{OPEN Lemma (Universal Charged Contraction).} Construct or refute a uniform transformation that, given black-box or adaptive access to any fixed polynomial-time SAT solver $A$ with unknown finite exponents $K,L\ge 2$, on every ordinary $N$-bit exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance $F$ produces at most $N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT children of length $\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ such that SAT$(F)$ is recoverable by a local procedure of total charged cost $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $N^{0.2+o(1)}$, thereby yielding a simultaneous profile forbidden by FLMV (or by the Williams product).

Until this (or an equivalent unrestricted implication) is established, no contradiction is obtained and P versus NP remains open.
