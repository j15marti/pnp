## Literature Review
- Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, Viglas (JACM 2005): SAT cannot be solved in time \(N^{t}\) and space \(N^{s}\) whenever \(s < \beta(t)\) for the indicated function in the random-access model (for \(t\) below the golden-ratio threshold in the asymptotic sense). The explicit form used is \(\beta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^{2}} - t\bigr)\).
- Williams (CCC 2007): improves the subpolynomial-space endpoint to \(2\cos(\pi/7)\approx 1.8019\).
- Buss–Williams: the \(2\cos(\pi/7)\) endpoint is optimal for alternation-trading proofs in the \(N^{o(1)}\)-space regime.
- Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 (three distinct variables per clause, each variable occurring exactly twice positively and twice negatively) is NP-complete by standard reductions from 3-SAT that preserve the occurrence bounds up to polynomial blow-up.
- No primary source supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT algorithm into a portfolio of ordinary small-length SAT instances meeting the stated size, time and space budgets on every input.

## Proposed Falsifiable Mechanisms
1. **Cost-accounting barrier on residual cores after affine elimination.** On Tseitin formulas of expander graphs, Gaussian elimination removes the affine shell in linear time, leaving an arbitrary NP-complete residual \(Q(B)\). Falsify the contraction if the description size or black-box evaluation cost of every useful residual is forced to be \(\Omega(N^{1-\lambda})\) on a positive-density family, exhausting the \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) local-work budget.
2. **Discovery/verification overhead for adaptive children.** Any procedure that queries A on candidate sub-instances or partial assignments must charge the time to generate, store and verify each child against the parent formula. Falsify if a concrete adaptive branching schedule on ordinary 3-SAT can be shown to stay inside the \(N^{1.1}\) work / \(N^{0.2}\) space envelope while recovering exact satisfiability on all inputs.
3. **Circuit-simulation margin exhaustion.** Convert the decision procedure of A into a uniform circuit family via the Pippenger–Fischer simulation; charge the resulting size and evaluation depth before any conditional circuit lower bound is applied. Falsify if the simulation overhead already places the resulting profile outside every FLMV-forbidden cell for the target exponents.
4. **One-sided contextual abstractions on minimally unsatisfiable instances.** Exact equivalence is unnecessary for UNSAT. Sound projections \(R_B(z)\) may be checked by a short proof-carrying deduction. Falsify if every minimally unsatisfiable exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 formula forces at least one context whose correctness check requires a parent-sized SAT call.

## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).**  
Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) satisfying \(T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}\), construct or rigorously refute a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) (with \(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\)) such that the satisfiability status of \(F\) is recoverable by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) using live parent space at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), with every ancillary cost charged. The resulting simultaneous profile must be forbidden by FLMV.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and P versus NP remains open.

## Formal Numerical Verification
