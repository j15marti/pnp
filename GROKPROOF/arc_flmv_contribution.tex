\section{ARC/FLMV Resource Contradiction (Conditional)}

Assume for contradiction a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) with
\[
T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}, \qquad S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for finite constants \(K,L \ge 2\).

The FLMV simultaneous lower bound asserts that no deterministic indexed random-access Turing machine solves SAT in time \(N^{t+o(1)}\) and space \(N^{s+o(1)}\) whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12 \Bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \Bigr).
\]

A conditional contraction with parameters \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\), \(\zeta = 0.1\) would produce at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) children of length \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) with total local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), yielding the simultaneous profile
\[
t' \le 1.3 - \frac1{5K}, \qquad s' \le 0.2.
\]
Direct evaluation shows \(s' < \beta(t')\) for every finite \(K \ge 2\).

\begin{theorem}[Numerical Membership]
\label{thm:numerical-membership}
For every finite \(K \ge 2\), the pair \((1.3 - 1/(5K), 0.2)\) lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(t' = 1.3 - 1/(5K)\). Then \(1.2 \le t' < 1.3\). Direct computation yields \(\beta(1.2) \approx 0.511 > 0.2\) and \(\beta(t')\) decreases continuously but remains greater than \(0.32 > 0.2\) throughout the interval.
\end{proof}

The required all-input transformation that realizes the contraction while charging every discovery, verification, access, memory, and combination cost remains OPEN. Consequently no unconditional contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained.
