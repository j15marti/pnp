## Literature Review (Primary Sources)
- Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005 / ECCC): simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT. Forbidden region \(s < \beta(t)\) where
  \[
  \beta(t) = \frac12 \Bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \Bigr).
  \]
  Example: simultaneous \(N^{1.3}\) time and \(N^{0.2}\) space excluded.
- Williams (CCC 2005 / Comp. Complex. 2008): SAT \(\notin\) DTISP\([N^{c+o(1)}, N^{o(1)}]\) for all \(c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019\).
- Buss–Williams: optimality of the \(2\cos(\pi/7)\) endpoint within the alternation-trading proof framework for the subpolynomial-space case.
- Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete. Expander Tseitin and graph-colouring formulas remain hard for Resolution, PC, and PCR.

No primary literature supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving, all-input transformation from black-box access to an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver into a portfolio of ordinary small SAT children meeting the stated ARC budgets.

- Restricted proof-system and circuit bounds do not transfer to unrestricted computation.

## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).**  
Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) with \(T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}\) and \(S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}\) for unknown finite \(K,L\), construct (or rigorously refute) a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) (\(\lambda=1/(5K)\), \(\zeta=0.1\)) such that:
1. the SAT/UNSAT status of \(F\) is correctly recoverable from the statuses of the children by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\);
2. live parent space is at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\);
3. all discovery, verification, access, memory, and combination costs are charged to the stated budgets;
4. the resulting simultaneous profile on the children lies inside the FLMV forbidden region.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication to an impossible simultaneous resource profile) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and \(P\) versus \(NP\) remains open.
