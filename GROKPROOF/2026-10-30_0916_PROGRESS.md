\subsection{Formalization}
\begin{remark}[Charged Portfolio Discovery Barrier]
Assume $P=NP$ and a fixed uniform SAT solver $A$ with $T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)}$ and $S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}$ for finite $K,L\ge 2$. Any all-input transformation that produces $\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}$ children of length $\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$ with $\lambda=1/(5K)$, $\zeta=0.1$, total local work $\le N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $\le N^{0.2+o(1)}$ would place SAT inside the FLMV forbidden region. Discovery of the child portfolio, verification of equisatisfiability, repeated access, memory management and combination costs must all be charged to the local budget. No uniform procedure meeting these simultaneous constraints on every ordinary 3-SAT or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instance is known. Affine elimination followed by residual submission exhausts the budget on expander-plus-core instances. Thus the required contraction remains unrealized.
\end{remark}

The conditional numerical membership remains PROVED/EXACT. The universal all-input fully charged transformation remains OPEN.
