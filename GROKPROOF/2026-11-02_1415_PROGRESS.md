## Literature Reconfirmation

- Williams (CCC 2007): SAT ∉ DTISP[N^{c+o(1)}, N^{o(1)}] for all c < 2 cos(π/7) ≈ 1.8019.
- Buss–Williams: the 2 cos(π/7) endpoint is optimal within the alternation-trading proof framework for subpolynomial space.
- Self-reducibility of SAT yields sequential length-decreasing queries, but the cumulative space and sequential time costs do not automatically yield a simultaneous low-space portfolio of parallel small children meeting the N^{1.1+o(1)} / N^{0.2+o(1)} budgets for arbitrary K.
- No primary source supplies a uniform, fully charged, correctness-preserving all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver A into the required child portfolio.
