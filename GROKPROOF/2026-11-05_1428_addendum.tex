\section*{Resource Contradiction Status (2026-11-05)}

Assume for contradiction a uniform polynomial-time SAT solver \(A\) with
\[
T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)}, \qquad S_A(N) \le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for finite \(K \ge 2\). The Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005) forbidden region states that no deterministic random-access machine solves SAT in time \(N^t\) and space \(N^s\) whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) = \frac12 \Bigl( \frac{t+2}{t^2} - t \Bigr)
\]
in the applicable range. Williams (CCC 2007) strengthens the subpolynomial-space endpoint to time \(N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}\).

A conditional contraction with parameters \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\), \(\zeta = 0.1\) would produce at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), aggregate local work at most \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\), and live parent space at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), yielding the simultaneous profile
\[
t' \le 1.3 - \frac1{5K}, \qquad s' \le 0.2,
\]
which lies strictly inside the forbidden region for every finite \(K \ge 2\). Numerical verification confirms membership (e.g., \(\beta(1.2) \approx 0.511 > 0.2\); \(\beta(1.3) \approx 0.326 > 0.2\)).

The required all-input transformation—covering discovery, correctness, access, memory, and combination costs for arbitrary (including adaptive) polynomial-time \(A\)—remains unproved. Restricted attempts (literal partitions on expanders, free residual contraction after affine elimination, unit-propagation or incidence-graph cycle covers on exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) fail to produce sublinear residuals within the local budget. Classical self-reducibility incurs parent-sized queries.

Thus the implication from the existence of \(A\) to a contradiction with the FLMV/Williams bounds is conditional on an open lemma. P versus NP remains unresolved.
