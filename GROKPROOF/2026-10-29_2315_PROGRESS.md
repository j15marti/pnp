\begin{remark}[Adaptive Charged Residual Barrier Strengthened by Williams Product]

Gaussian elimination eliminates the affine shell in $O(N)$ time, leaving an equisatisfiable residual of length $\Theta(N)$. Under black-box or adaptive access to $A$, any procedure that must discover or verify a semantic child of the residual either submits a parent-sized instance (incurring time $N^{K+o(1)}\ge N^{2+o(1)}$) or maintains live intermediate state of size $\Omega(N^\delta)$ for some $\delta>0.2$. Moreover, any residual-query schedule whose charged time--space product falls below $\Omega(N^{\phi-o(1)})$ with $\phi\approx 1.618$ is independently forbidden by the Williams product bound, even if the individual exponents avoid the FLMV region. Thus this family of semantic decompositions cannot realize the required contraction.
\end{remark}
