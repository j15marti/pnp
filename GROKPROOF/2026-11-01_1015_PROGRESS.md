## Build / Formalization
**Lemma (Numerical Membership — CONDITIONAL).**
Assume an all-input transformation producing at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\), with total local work \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) and live parent space \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), for \(\lambda = 1/(5K)\) and \(\zeta = 0.1\).
Then the derived simultaneous exponents satisfy
\[
t' \le 1.3 - \frac{1}{5K},\qquad s' \le 0.2.
\]
Direct substitution into \(\beta\) shows \(0.2 < \beta(1.3 - 1/(5K))\) for every finite \(K \ge 2\). Explicit verification:
- \(K=2\): \(t'=1.2\), \(\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511 > 0.2\);
- \(K=5\): \(t'\approx 1.260\), \(\beta\approx 0.397 > 0.2\);
- large \(K\): \(t'\to 1.3\), \(\beta(1.3)\approx 0.326 > 0.2\).
Hence the derived profile lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region. The transformation itself remains unproved; the lemma is therefore CONDITIONAL on its existence and correctness under full charging.
