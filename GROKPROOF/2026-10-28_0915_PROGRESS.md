# GROKPROOF Progress Report: 2026-10-28_0915

## Strongest New Result
No unconditional resolution of P versus NP. Numerical verification confirms that the proposed conditional contraction profile lies inside the FLMV forbidden region:

\[
\beta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\right).
\]

For \( t = 1.3 \), \( \beta(1.3) \approx 0.326 \), so simultaneous time \( N^{1.3+o(1)} \) and space \( N^{0.2+o(1)} \) is excluded by Fortnow–Lipton–van Melkebeek–Viglas (JACM 2005) in the indexed random-access Turing-machine model. For the parameter \( \lambda = 1/(5K) \) with \( K \ge 2 \), the resulting \( t' \le 1.3 - \lambda \) yields an even larger margin against \( \beta(t') \).

## Proof Impact
- Confirmed literature: FLMV establishes the first polynomial simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT; Williams improves the subpolynomial-space exponent to \( 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019 \); Buss–Williams prove optimality of that endpoint within the alternation-trading framework.
- The conditional numerical membership is exact for the stated parameters, but does not construct the required all-input transformation.
- No primary source supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving decomposition of arbitrary 3-SAT or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instances into \( N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} \) children of length \( N^{\lambda+o(1)} \) with local work \( N^{1.1+o(1)} \) and live space \( N^{0.2+o(1)} \).

## Failed Hypotheses
- Literal partitions and elementary semantic projections fail on expander Tseitin instances (as previously recorded).
- Black-box query access to \( A \) has not been converted into a low-cost discovery procedure that stays inside the live-space budget on hard families.
- Near-boundary seeds alone do not yield an unrestricted contradiction.

## Remaining Gap
The universal all-input resource-accounted transformation from an arbitrary fixed polynomial-time SAT solver \( A \) remains unproved. Without it (or an equivalent implication that forces an impossible simultaneous profile on the same machine model), the resource-contradiction strategy does not establish P ≠ NP.

## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).** Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \( A \) with \( T_A(N) \le N^{K+o(1)} \), construct (or rigorously refute for a positive-density family) a uniform transformation that on every ordinary \( N \)-bit 3-SAT instance produces at most \( N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)} \) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \( N^{\lambda+o(1)} \) (\( \lambda=1/(5K) \), \( \zeta=0.1 \)) such that satisfiability of the parent is recoverable by a local procedure of cost \( N^{1.1+o(1)} \) using live parent space at most \( N^{0.2+o(1)} \), with discovery, verification, access, memory allocation, failed strategies, switching, and combination costs fully charged.

Until this lemma is resolved, P versus NP remains open.
