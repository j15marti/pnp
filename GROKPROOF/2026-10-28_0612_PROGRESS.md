## Literature Review
- Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek, Viglas (JACM 2005): simultaneous time-space lower bounds for SAT in the indexed random-access Turing-machine model. The forbidden region is \(s < \beta(t)\) where
  \[
  \beta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\right).
  \]
  Numerical confirmation: \(\beta(1.2) \approx 0.511 > 0.2\), \(\beta(1.298) \approx 0.330 > 0.2\).
- Williams (CCC 2007 / Comp. Complex. 2008): SAT \(\notin\) DTISP\((N^{c+o(1)}, N^{o(1)})\) for all \(c < 2\cos(\pi/7) \approx 1.8019\).
- Buss–Williams: optimality of the \(2\cos(\pi/7)\) endpoint within the alternation-trading proof framework for the subpolynomial-space case.
- van Melkebeek survey (ECCC 2007): unified treatment of time-space lower bounds; two-algorithm incompatibility results.
- Pippenger–Fischer (JACM 1979): oblivious simulation of multitape TMs by circuits of size \(O(T\log T)\).
No primary literature supplies a universal, fully charged, correctness-preserving all-input transformation from an arbitrary polynomial-time SAT solver into a portfolio of ordinary small SAT children meeting the stated local-work and live-space budgets.

## Next Decisive Lemma
**OPEN Lemma (All-Input Resource Contraction).** Given black-box access to any fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver \(A\) satisfying the polynomial bounds, construct or rigorously refute a uniform transformation that, on every ordinary \(N\)-bit 3-SAT (or exact-(2,2)-CNF-3) instance \(F\), produces at most \(N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}\) ordinary SAT children each of length at most \(N^{\lambda+o(1)}\) such that the satisfiability status of \(F\) is recoverable by a local procedure of cost \(N^{1.1+o(1)}\) using live parent space at most \(N^{0.2+o(1)}\), with every ancillary cost charged, thereby yielding a simultaneous profile forbidden by FLMV.

Until this lemma (or an alternate universal implication) is established, no contradiction with known lower bounds is obtained and P versus NP remains open.
