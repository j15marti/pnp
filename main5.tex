    \paragraph{ARC-STRENGTHEN8: exact-size split acquisition.}

    and certifies $\sum_i(m_i/N)^{6/5}\le 99/100$ with integer arithmetic.

    $N^{1/5+o(1)}$ workspace; sequential child emission is implemented.
    Conditional geometric-moment closure permits constant-ratio descent
    even with coarse power label $h=1$, provided physical composed access,
    retained state and successor coverage are charged.

    368-bit children rejected by the same acquirer. Explicit child buffers
    are linear; streaming does not supply cheap random access.
    The bounded semantic test passes 256 formulas and 1,536 emitter checks.
