\section*{What is proved in a declared model}
\begin{itemize}
\item OFFPATH1, as adopted by MAIN0901, is an interface theorem: identical static and
realized compiler views cannot select a response from two disjoint legal off-path sets.
It is not a SAT lower bound, and it relativizes.
\item PATHSTRAT1 remains path-faithful polynomial-horizon staticization in
\(\mathrm{FP}^{\mathrm{SAT}}\), hence in FP under \(P=NP\), only under its stated
stage hypotheses. SOURCESTOP1 remains the corpus statement that the source layer does
not yet define \(W_{\rm exact}\).
\item RETURNSQUEEZE1 is conditional exponent accounting. If a legal route obeys
\(H_T(K)\le A+\beta K\) and a uniform return reduction scales length by
\(N=n^{\lambda+o(1)}\), then \(K'\le C_T+\lambda\beta K\), which is contractive only
if \(\lambda\beta<1\). Full-input carry forces \(\lambda b\ge 1\) on the direct route.
Generic Cook--Levin/Robson self-encoding of the improved computation is correctly
retired as the missing contraction. The return reduction itself remains OPEN.
\item DELAYOR1 proves DELAYCOEF1: an extensional blackbox transformation that actually
calls a DELAY-wrapped exact decider \(A_L\) at scale \(n^{\delta-o(1)}\) cannot have a
uniform contraction coefficient below \(\delta\). RESTRICTCOVER1 is the subcube-cover
bound \(m\ge n-\log_2 q\). The literal Williams OR packing followed by a generic return
to CNF is FALSIFIED/RETIRED as the missing polynomial-exponent contraction. A
nonblackbox source-specific bridge is not ruled out.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Literature check}
Williams, STOC 2024, does prove a self-improvement theorem for Circuit-SAT,
\#Circuit-SAT, and quantified Circuit-SAT in a large-circuit regime, and the published
abstract records consequences toward ETH rather than a P-versus-NP theorem. The
DELAYOR1 transfer is therefore an analogy: ordinary CNF return does not inherit the
same-representation self-modeling used in that paper. The Williams SAT time-space
product \(t(n)s(n)=\Omega(n^{2\cos(\pi/7)-\varepsilon})\) is a known restricted lower
bound. Using it as a fixed-point screen is CONDITIONAL until \(C_T\), \(\lambda\),
\(\beta\), and the space floor are source-certified. Santhanam--Srinivasan is a
functional representation barrier, not a decision-runtime barrier; the report already
keeps that distinction.
