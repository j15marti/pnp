\section*{What is proved in a declared model}
\begin{itemize}
\item LEGALFLAT1 remains PROVED/EXACT under polynomial-time legality, verification, and a
polynomial-size strategy evaluator: existence of a source and winning strategy has the form
\(\exists R,S\;\forall C\;W(x,R,S,C)\) with \(W\in P\), hence lies in \(\Sigma_2^P\).
This is a local closure, not acquisition, and not a theorem that an arbitrary static SAT
decider evaluates \(S\).
\item PBDP1 remains a hostile representation control. Explicit prime-product expansion is
FALSIFIED as a standalone lower-bound mechanism.
\item ENVELOPE1 is PROVED/EXACT as an accounting theorem under its declared route
hypotheses. For one fixed deterministic exact SAT decider of exponent \(K\), reconstruction
time is at most the minimum of the direct-simulation route, the RAMCOMP1 extension-search
route, and an independently acquired evaluator route:
\[
T_{\rm rec}(N)\le N^{\min\{\max(a,q+bK),\;\ell+c_0K^2,\;\max(f,c_FK)\}+o(1)}.
\]
Alternative routes are not composed. The bound does not multiply \(K\) by adaptive depth
and does not force acquisition. Application to the actual vMS/SGL map is CONDITIONAL
until source-level parameters are certified.
\item RAMDOM1, read from the post-14:12 LSPV-3 note, is PROVED/EXACT only inside those
same hypotheses. Assume \(c_0>0\). The nested RAMCOMP1 exponent is strictly smaller than
direct substitution exactly when
\[
\ell+c_0K^2<\max\{a,q+bK\}.
\]
The note gives the corresponding finite interval in \(K\) and states that past a finite
threshold the direct branch is at least as cheap. It does not permit varying or inflating
\(K\), and it does not certify \((a,q,b,\ell,c_0)\) for the vMS/SGL map. It is not a SAT
lower bound.
\item D3CAL1 is a PROVED/EXACT finite control. With three variables and simple exact-3
clauses, every formula with fewer than eight distinct clauses is satisfiable, and the
unique eight-clause formula is unsatisfiable. The earlier eight-node ordered membership
DAG counted selection of that unique set, not intrinsic SAT-solving complexity.
\item UNIVERSALDAG2 is PROVED/EXACT for one fixed clause order only:
\(D_{\rm ord}(3,8)=8\) and \(D_{\rm ord}(4,8)=2107\). It is not a minimum over orders,
not an FBDD minimum, and not an unrestricted hypergraph-DAG minimum.
\end{itemize}
