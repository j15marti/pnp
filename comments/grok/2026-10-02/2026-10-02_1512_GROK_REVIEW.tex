\section*{What is proved in a declared model}
\begin{itemize}
\item LEGALFLAT1 remains PROVED/EXACT under polynomial-time legality, verification, and a
polynomial-size strategy evaluator: existence of a source and winning strategy has the form
\(\exists R,S\;\forall C\;W(x,R,S,C)\) with \(W\in P\), hence lies in \(\Sigma_2^P\).
This is a closure, not a separation.
\item ENVELOPE1 remains PROVED/EXACT as an accounting theorem under its declared route
hypotheses. Alternative routes are minimized, not multiplied. Application to the actual
vMS/SGL map is CONDITIONAL until source-level parameters are certified.
\item RAMDOM1 is PROVED/EXACT under the ENVELOPE1/RAMCOMP1 hypotheses and \(c_0>0\).
The nested route strictly improves on direct substitution exactly when
\[
\ell+c_0K^2<\max\{a,q+bK\}.
\]
The comparison splits into a finite region \(1\le K<\sqrt{(a-\ell)/c_0}\) when \(a>\ell\),
and, when \(\Delta=b^2-4c_0(\ell-q)>0\), the open interval between the roots
\(K_\pm=(b\pm\sqrt{\Delta})/(2c_0)\), intersected with \(K\ge 1\). Outside that finite
region the direct branch is at least as cheap. RAMDOM1 does not vary or inflate \(K\).
\item D3CAL1 remains a PROVED/EXACT finite control: on three variables, every simple exact-3
formula with fewer than eight distinct clauses is satisfiable, and the unique eight-clause
formula is unsatisfiable. UNIVERSALDAG2 remains PROVED/EXACT for one fixed clause order only:
\(D_{\rm ord}(3,8)=8\) and \(D_{\rm ord}(4,8)=2107\).
\end{itemize}
