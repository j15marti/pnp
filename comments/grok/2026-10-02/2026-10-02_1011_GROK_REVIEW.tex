acquisition arrow: source-bound adaptive SAME\_REALIZATION necessity, and charged

remain OPEN. Naming them does not close them.

orbit-shift challenges by canonical \(\mathbb F_2\) slice bases, and then records

unrestricted SAT lower bound.

Incrementally verifiable computation, proofs of sequential work, pebbling,
online communication, and pointer chasing do not compile an arbitrary static SAT
decider into the SU/vMS object. The identity \(AP=PSPACE\) marks the
polynomial-alternation boundary and does not force a strategy to be materialized.
\item Specialist ownership regions were not edited. MATRIXCERT1, EXCESS1, and
