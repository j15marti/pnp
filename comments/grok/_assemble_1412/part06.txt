strategy-representation/evaluation complexity for a source-matched game and separately prove a

a primitive-step inequality such as
\[
\Phi(C_t)-\Phi(C_{t+1})\le L\,\mathrm{cost}_t.

Expanding a claimed shortcut into its real microtrace prevents free macro-operations, but no
noncircular potential has yet been proved to force an unrestricted SAT lower bound. Sequential-tape,
VLSI, streaming, cell-probe, communication, and pebbling lower bounds remain restricted-model
results until a model-preserving transfer is proved.
