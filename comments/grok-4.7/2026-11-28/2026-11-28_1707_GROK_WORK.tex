\section{Work: the PC4 status of the T3 triple-packing family}
\subsection{Setting}
$H$ is a 4-uniform hypergraph on $[n]$. For each edge $e$ write
$x_e=\prod_{j\in e}x_j$ and $c_e=\prod_{j\in e}(1+x_j)$, both multilinear over $\F_2$. The
\emph{grouped} axiom is $p_e=x_e+c_e$, which has degree 3. The \emph{raw} axioms are $x_e$
and $c_e$, the falsity indicators of the two NAE clauses. $W_d(\mathcal A)$ is the T3 ``full
bounded multilinear closure'': the least subspace of $V_d$ that contains $\mathcal A$ and is
closed under $p\mapsto x_ip$ whenever $\deg(x_ip)\le d$. T3 \S2 proves that the kernel
algorithm computes it exactly.

\begin{theorem}[\lab{PROVED/EXACT}]\label{thm:enc}
For every 4-uniform $H$ and every $d\ge4$,
$W_d(\{p_e\})=W_d(\{x_e,c_e\})$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$p_e=x_e+c_e$ lies in the raw span. Conversely, take $a\in e$. Then $x_ax_e=x_e$ and
$x_ac_e=0$ because $x_a(1+x_a)=0$, so $x_ap_e=x_e$, which has degree $4\le d$ and is an
admissible product. Also $c_e=p_e+x_e$. Each initial set therefore lies in the closure of the
other, and closures are monotone and idempotent.
\end{proof}
\noindent So T3's grouped and raw PC4 runs explore the same space. Their different capped
ranks (2456 versus 2453 at $n=18$) reflect order and cap timing only. Two independent
implementations agree: both encodings give identical ranks and verdicts.

\begin{remark}[\lab{PROVED/EXACT}, elementary]
$W_d(\mathcal A)\subseteq W_d(\mathcal A')$ whenever $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal A'$. Hence
the minimal refuting degree of $H$ is at most that of any sub-hypergraph, and it is at most
$n$, because $V_n$ is everything.
\end{remark}
