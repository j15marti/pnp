\paragraph{Gap.} The 19:21 KST log FALSIFIED the T2 universal polynomial-ceiling lemma for clause-level moves and left ``GE moves'' OPEN. Current T2/T3/T4 lanes and the new mandate depend on modules that mix clause reasoning with exact linear (parity) quotients. We settle the most common engineering form (GE on parity constraints present in the input, as in CryptoMiniSat-style static Gauss--Jordan) and isolate what remains.

\paragraph{System.} Let $F=A\wedge X(L)$ where $L$ is a system of $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear equations, $X(L)$ is its canonical CNF encoding (each equation of arity $a$ encoded by its $2^{a-1}$ clauses), and $A$ is any CNF. \emph{$\mathrm{Res}{+}\mathrm{GE}_{\mathrm{in}}$} has as axioms the clauses of $F$ and, at unit cost, \emph{every} clause $C$ with $L\models C$ (this dominates anything Gaussian elimination on $L$ can output, including all reason clauses), plus resolution and weakening. Size $=$ number of lines.

\begin{lemma}[Restriction lemma --- PROVED]\label{lem:restr}
If $\rho$ is an assignment to $\mathrm{vars}(L)$ satisfying $L$, then every $\mathrm{Res}{+}\mathrm{GE}_{\mathrm{in}}$ refutation of $F$ of size $S$ yields a resolution refutation of $A|_\rho$ of size $\le S$. Hence $S_{\mathrm{Res+GE_{in}}}(F)\ \ge\ \max_{\rho\models L} S_{\mathrm{Res}}(A|_\rho)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
(1) Every $L$-implied clause $C$ is satisfied by $\rho$: write $C=C_1\vee C_2$ with $\mathrm{vars}(C_1)\subseteq\mathrm{vars}(L)$ and $C_2$ disjoint from $\mathrm{vars}(L)$. If $\rho$ falsified $C_1$, extend $\rho$ by any assignment falsifying $C_2$ (possible: $C$ non-tautological, else trivially satisfied); this extension satisfies $L$ (which only mentions $\mathrm{vars}(L)$) and falsifies $C$, contradicting $L\models C$. (2) Each clause of $X(L)$ is $L$-implied, so also satisfied. (3) Standard: applying $\rho$ to every line of a resolution/weakening derivation, deleting satisfied lines and bypassing resolution steps on assigned variables, gives a valid derivation from the restricted axioms of no larger size, ending in $\bot$. The only surviving axioms are clauses of $A|_\rho$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[PROVED $+$ KNOWN THEOREM]\label{cor:php}
Let $A=\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n$ and let $L$ be any \emph{homogeneous} (all right-hand sides $0$) system on a set $Z$ of $k\le n/2$ PHP variables, of any arities and any number of equations. Then every $\mathrm{Res}{+}\mathrm{GE}_{\mathrm{in}}$ refutation of $\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n\wedge X(L)$ has size $\ge S_{\mathrm{Res}}(\mathrm{PHP}^{n-k'+1}_{n-k'})=2^{\Omega(n)}$ for some $k'\le k$ (Haken 1985; Beame--Pitassi 1996 for $2^{\Omega(n)}$).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Take $\rho=0$ on $Z$ (satisfies homogeneous $L$); by Lemma~\ref{lem:restr} it suffices to bound $S_{\mathrm{Res}}(A|_\rho)$, and further restrictions do not increase resolution size. Let $I_0$ be the pigeons touched by $Z$, $k'=|I_0|\le k$. Greedily give each $i\in I_0$ a distinct hole $j_i$ with $x_{i j_i}\notin Z$ (possible since $i$ has $\ge n-k$ non-$Z$ holes and $<k$ are already used, and $n-2k+1\ge1$); set $x_{ij_i}=1$, all other variables of pigeon $i$ to $0$, and $x_{i'j_i}=0$ for $i'\ne i$. All clauses touching $I_0$ or the holes $j_i$ are satisfied (no clause is falsified), and the residual formula is exactly $\mathrm{PHP}^{n-k'+1}_{n-k'}$ on the untouched pigeons/holes. Apply the known lower bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[scope]
(a) If $L$ is unsatisfiable, $\mathrm{GE}_{\mathrm{in}}$ refutes $F$ in one step; the lemma is vacuous there, as it must be (e.g.\ Tseitin, where GE is the polynomial algorithm and resolution is exponential by Urquhart). So GE genuinely helps \emph{only through} the interaction of $A$ with assignments to $\mathrm{vars}(L)$; the lemma quantifies this. (b) Non-homogeneous $L$: the same proof works whenever some $\rho\models L$ has its $1$-entries forming a partial pigeon--hole matching. (c) Input-free variants: on $\mathrm{PHP}^{n+1}_n$ itself syntactic XOR detection finds nothing (COMPUTATIONAL check below), so a static-extraction GE module is idle and the solver is plain resolution on that input.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Consequence for the project (CONDITIONAL on modelling).} Any decider whose UNSAT runs are certified by resolution steps interleaved with Gaussian elimination on input-extracted parities (including all reason clauses GE emits) needs $2^{\Omega(n)}$ time on the explicit polynomial-size family of Corollary~\ref{cor:php}. Thus the 19:21 KST FALSIFIED verdict for a universal polynomial ceiling extends from clause-level moves to clause-level moves $+$ static GE. The modelling condition is that the module's UNSAT certificate is expressible in $\mathrm{Res}{+}\mathrm{GE}_{\mathrm{in}}$; lanes that build \emph{new} parities from derived clauses (dynamic XOR extraction, exact quotient discovery as in \texttt{bc04b91}) are not covered.

\paragraph{What stays OPEN.} Resolution with GE over parities extracted from \emph{derived} clauses, or with extension variables: such hybrids are (CANDIDATE, not proved here) simulated by Res($\oplus$)/ResLin, for which only tree-like, regular (Efremenko--Garl\'ik--Itsykson, SICOMP 2025), and depth-restricted (Efremenko--Itsykson; Bhattacharya--Chattopadhyay, STOC 2026) lower bounds are known; super-polynomial lower bounds for general dag-like Res($\oplus$) are an open problem. So no ceiling-refutation for those modules can be imported from known theorems today; equally, no positive claim follows.
