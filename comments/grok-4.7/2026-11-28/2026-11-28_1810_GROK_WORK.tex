\paragraph{Setup.} The C04-36 LP for a cover $\mathcal U$ (sets of variables) has variables
$p_U(a)\ge 0$ for $a\in R_U$ (the exact joint support of the factors whose scope lies in $U$),
$\sum_a p_U(a)=1$, and equal marginals on every shared subset. Tseitin system on a connected
graph $G=(V,E)$ with charges $c:V\to\F$ and $\sum_v c_v=1$: variables $x_e$, constraints
$\sum_{e\ni v}x_e=c_v$. This is UNSAT, and the CNF companion (for cubic $G$) has 4 width-3 clauses per
vertex with the same factor supports. Write $\delta(W)$ for the edge boundary and
$h(G)=\min_{0<|W|\le|V|/2}|\delta(W)|/|W|$.
For a region $U\subseteq E$ let
$B(U)=\bigcup\{W\subseteq V: W\ne\emptyset,\ |W|\le|V|/2,\ \delta(W)\subseteq U\}$.

\begin{lemma}[\lab{PROVED}]\label{lem:B}
If $|U|\le w$ and $|V|\ge 4w/h(G)$ with $h(G)>0$, then $|B(U)|\le w/h(G)\le |V|/4$, in particular $B(U)\ne V$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each member $W$ satisfies $|W|\le|\delta(W)|/h\le w/h$. For members $W_1,W_2$,
$\delta(W_1\cup W_2)\subseteq\delta(W_1)\cup\delta(W_2)\subseteq U$, and
$|W_1\cup W_2|\le 2w/h\le|V|/2$, so $W_1\cup W_2$ is again a member and has size $\le w/h$.
By induction the union of all members, $B(U)$, is a member, so $|B(U)|\le w/h$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[\lab{PROVED}; EB-REGLP-1]\label{thm:main}
Let $G$ be connected, $\sum c=1$, and $\mathcal U$ \emph{any} cover (any acquisition rule, overlaps
allowed) in which every region satisfies $B(U)\ne V$. This holds in particular when every
$|U|\le w$ and $|V|\ge 4w/h(G)$. Then the C04-36 regional LP, with consistency imposed on
\emph{every} shared subset, is feasible with a rational solution, although the formula is UNSAT.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $r\in V$ let $\mathrm{Sol}_r$ be the solution set of all vertex equations except $r$. The
rows $\{\mathrm{row}_v\}_{v\ne r}$ are independent, since $\sum_{v\in W}\mathrm{row}_v=\delta(W)\ne 0$
for $\emptyset\ne W\subseteq V\setminus\{r\}$ when $G$ is connected. So $\mathrm{Sol}_r$ is a nonempty affine subspace, and $\mu_r$ denotes the uniform distribution on it.
For each $U$ choose $r(U)\notin B(U)$ and let $p_U$ be the marginal of $\mu_{r(U)}$ on $U$.

\emph{Support.} If all edges at $w$ lie in $U$, then $\{w\}\in B(U)$ (with $|V|\ge 2$), so
$r(U)\ne w$ and $\mathrm{row}_w$ holds on $\mathrm{Sol}_{r(U)}$. Hence $p_U$ is supported in $R_U$.
Normalization and nonnegativity are clear.

\emph{Consistency.} Fix $T\subseteq U\cap U'$. The marginal of the uniform distribution on an affine
space onto $T$ is uniform on its projection, because all fibres are cosets of one subspace. That projection is cut out by the
implied forms supported in $T$: those $\ell\in\mathrm{rowspan}\{\mathrm{row}_v\}_{v\ne r}$ with
$\mathrm{supp}\,\ell\subseteq T$, with their constants. The row span over $v\ne r$ is $\{\delta(W):W\subseteq V\setminus\{r\}\}$, which is the set of
\emph{all} cuts, because $\delta(W)=\delta(V\setminus W)$. So the set of implied forms on $T$ does not depend on
$r$. For a cut $D=\delta(W)\subseteq T$, $D\ne\emptyset$, exactly two sides realize it
($W$ and $V\setminus W$, because $G$ is connected), and $\mu_r$ assigns $D$ the constant $c(\text{side}\not\ni r)$.
Let $S$ be a side with $|S|\le|V|/2$. Since $D\subseteq U$ and $D\subseteq U'$, we get $S\subseteq B(U)\cap B(U')$. If both sides have size $|V|/2$, both lie in $B(U)$, which would give $B(U)=V$; that is excluded. Hence $r(U),r(U')\in V\setminus S$, and both constants equal $c(S)$.
Equal forms with equal constants give equal projections, hence equal uniform marginals.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[\lab{PROVED} given \lab{KNOWN THEOREM}]
Let $G_n$ be cubic with $h(G_n)\ge h_0>0$. Random cubic graphs satisfy this with high probability
(Bollob\'as, \emph{The isoperimetric number of random regular graphs}, European J.\ Combin.\ 9
(1988)), and explicit expander families exist. For the Tseitin CNF on $G_n$ ($N=\Theta(n)$ variables),
\emph{no} C04-36 regional cover with all regions of width $\le h_0 n/4$ refutes, whatever the
acquisition schedule, region count, or overlap. In particular width $O(\log N)$ and the blind
width-5 pair-union rule fail for all large $n$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Scope and limits]
(i) Gaussian elimination (the affine lane) refutes these instances in polynomial time. This is a
statement about one lane, not a lower bound for the portfolio or for algorithms. The
mandatory early forced-affine closure in AGENTS.md would catch Tseitin before the LP runs.
(ii) The result concerns the CNF-level factor LP; transfer to the native STRICT-EB4 compiled
encoding (auxiliary variables) is OPEN.
(iii) Random 3-CNF, the family where the affine lane fails: a degree-$2t$ SoS pseudo-expectation
satisfying the constraint axioms restricts, on any $|S|\le t$, to a genuine distribution
($\tilde E[\mathbf 1_{x_S=a}]=\tilde E[\mathbf 1_{x_S=a}^2]\ge 0$, sum $1$). These restrictions are
mutually consistent and supported on $R_S$, so it yields a feasible width-$t$ regional LP for
every cover \lab{PROVED, elementary}. Combined with \lab{KNOWN THEOREM} Grigoriev (2001) and
Schoenebeck (FOCS 2008, ``Linear level Lasserre lower bounds for certain $k$-CSPs''), which give
$\Omega(n)$-level Lasserre/SoS solutions for random 3-XOR/3-SAT with $\Delta n$ clauses, this gives
\lab{CONDITIONAL}, on the exact quantitative form of that citation, which I did not re-derive: for fixed density, whp every regional cover of width
$\le c_\Delta n$ yields a feasible LP. The width-5 refutation of RANDOM-3CNF-40 is therefore a
finite-size effect and cannot be extrapolated. Polynomial-calculus degree lower bounds for random
3-CNF (Ben-Sasson--Impagliazzo; Alekhnovich--Razborov, \lab{KNOWN THEOREM}) rule out the affine/PC lanes separately.
Whether \emph{deduction exchange} between these lanes escapes both bounds is \lab{OPEN}. It is not covered
by either theorem.
\end{remark}
