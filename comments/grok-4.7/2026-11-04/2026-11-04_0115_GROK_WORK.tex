\section{Gap chosen and work}
\paragraph{Gap.} LSPV-3 365 bounds the local cost of $F_{G,k}$ by $O(hk^3)$, against a source floor of only $m-1=\Theta(hk^2+k^3)$, and it marks the all-graph upper bound CONDITIONAL. \paragraph{Setting.} This run uses exactly the LSPV-3 365 setting: $G$ is simple, cubic and 2-vertex-connected, with $h$ vertices and $|E|=3h/2$ edges; exactly one vertex $v_0$ has charge $1$; the sources $S_{v,a,\mathbf j}$ are as in LSPV-3 (1), with $m=m(h,k)$; and ER2 is the signed fragment of the unchanged $R_{\rm mix341}$. In ER2, a paid fresh definition $u:=a\wedge b$ of distinct earlier positive variables authenticates the slots $(\neg u\vee a),(\neg u\vee b),(u\vee\neg a\vee\neg b)$, and each strict binary Resolution step costs one. Write $T(G,s)$ for the ordinary Tseitin CNF, which has $4h$ width-3 clauses on edge variables $y_e$.

\begin{lemma}[Linear chain extraction; PROVED/EXACT]\label{lem:chain}
For each edge $e$, $k-1$ paid definitions and exactly $3k-6$ strict Resolution nodes derive all $(\neg g_e\vee z_{e,j})$, $1\le j\le k$, and $W_e=(g_e\vee\neg z_{e,1}\vee\cdots\vee\neg z_{e,k})$. This tightens LSPV-3's $(k-2)(k+3)/2$, with equality at $k=2,3$ and a strict improvement for $k\ge4$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Define $t_2:=z_1\wedge z_2$ and $t_j:=t_{j-1}\wedge z_j$ ($3\le j\le k$), and set $g_e=t_k$. (a) $D_{k-1}=(\neg t_k\vee t_{k-1})$ is a slot, and $D_i=\mathrm{Res}_{t_{i+1}}(D_{i+1},(\neg t_{i+1}\vee t_i))$ for $i=k-2,\ldots,2$, which is $k-3$ nodes. (b) $(\neg t_k\vee z_k)$ is a slot; $(\neg t_k\vee z_j)=\mathrm{Res}_{t_j}(D_j,(\neg t_j\vee z_j))$ for $2\le j\le k-1$, and $(\neg t_k\vee z_1)=\mathrm{Res}_{t_2}(D_2,(\neg t_2\vee z_1))$, which is $k-1$ nodes. (c) Start from the slot $(t_k\vee\neg t_{k-1}\vee\neg z_k)$ and resolve successively on $t_{k-1},\ldots,t_2$ with $(t_i\vee\neg t_{i-1}\vee\neg z_i)$ and finally $(t_2\vee\neg z_1\vee\neg z_2)$, which is $k-2$ nodes. All variables are distinct, so no resolvent is tautological. The total is $3k-6$ for $k\ge3$; for $k=2$ every target is a slot, and $3\cdot2-6=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Eliminate-then-convert blockers; PROVED/EXACT]\label{lem:block}
Fix $v$ and a forbidden tuple $a$ with $p$ zero entries. From the $k^p$ sources $S_{v,a,\mathbf j}$, the clauses $W_e$ and the implications of Lemma~\ref{lem:chain}, the blocker $B_{v,a}$ is derived with $\sum_{r=1}^{p}k^r+(3-p)k$ strict Resolution nodes. Consequently a charge-one vertex costs $k^3+k^2+10k$ and a charge-zero vertex costs $3k^2+9k$. LSPV-3 had $k^3+7k^2+4k$ and $3k^3+3k^2+6k$; in particular, charge-zero vertices drop from cubic to quadratic in $k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The order is reversed relative to LSPV-3: zero groups are eliminated \emph{first}, while the clauses still contain their $\neg z$ literals. Take the last remaining zero group $e$. For each tuple of the other remaining indices, the $k$ clauses $C\vee z_{e,j}$ ($j=1,\ldots,k$) share a common part $C$. Resolve them successively against $W_e$ on $z_{e,1},\ldots,z_{e,k}$. This takes $k$ nodes and leaves $C\vee g_e$. $C$ mentions only other edges, so nothing is tautological. Levels cost $k^p,k^{p-1},\ldots,k$. The single remaining clause is $\bigvee_{a_i=0}g_{e_i}\vee\bigvee_{a_i=1}\bigvee_t\neg z_{e_i,t}$. Each $\neg z_{e,t}$ is replaced by $\neg g_e$ by resolving with $(\neg g_e\vee z_{e,t})$; the merge leaves one copy, for $(3-p)k$ nodes, and the result is $B_{v,a}$. Totals: at $v_0$ the forbidden tuples are $000$ ($p=3$) and three with $p=1$, giving $(k^3+k^2+k)+3(k+2k)$. At a charge-zero vertex there are three tuples with $p=2$ and one with $p=0$, giving $3(k^2+k+k)+3k$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lift sandwich; PROVED/EXACT for this family]\label{thm:sandwich}
Let $\pi$ be any ER2 refutation of $T(G,s)$ with paid cost $c(\pi)$. Then
\[
m(h,k)-1\;\le\;W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_{G,k})\;\le\;W_{\rm ER2}(F_{G,k})\;\le\;m(h,k)+\Delta(h,k)+c(\pi),
\]
where $\Delta(h,k)=15hk-\tfrac{23}{2}h+k^2-2k+1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The lower bound is LSPV-3 365's theorem (minimal unsatisfiability and at most two premises per paid node). It is imported, and its indispensability witnesses were independently rechecked by computation (Section~3). For the upper bound: the blockers $B_{v,a}$ over all $v,a$ are exactly $T(G,s)$ with $y_e$ renamed to $g_e$. Rename $\pi$ accordingly, with its fresh extension variables renamed beyond all $g_e$; this preserves every signed step and definition. The cost is then $\frac{3h}{2}(k-1)$ definitions plus $\frac{3h}{2}(3k-6)$ chain nodes (Lemma~\ref{lem:chain}), plus $(k^3+k^2+10k)+(h-1)(3k^2+9k)$ blocker nodes (Lemma~\ref{lem:block}), plus $c(\pi)$. Subtracting $m=k^3+3k+(h-1)(3k^2+1)$ gives $\frac{3h}{2}(4k-7)+k^2+7k+(h-1)(9k-1)=\Delta$. Finally, ER2 is a fragment of $R_{\rm mix341}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fixed-graph asymptotics; PROVED/EXACT]
For every fixed $G$, $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_{G,k})=m(h,k)\,(1+O(1/k))$ as $k\to\infty$. A finite pure-Resolution refutation of $T(G,s)$ exists by completeness, and $\Delta=O(k^2)$ while $m\ge k^3$. The minimum cost of this family is thus determined to first order by the number of sources, unconditionally and without LSPV-3 357.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[All graphs; CONDITIONAL]
$W_{R_{\rm mix341}}(F_{G,k})\le m+O(hk)+W_{\rm ER2}(T(G,s))$ unconditionally. If $W_{\rm ER2}(T(G,s))=O(h^3)$ (LSPV-3 357, still awaiting independent replay), this becomes $m+O(hk+h^3)$, improving LSPV-3 (4) from $O(hk^3+h^3)$. Separately, Tseitin formulas have polynomial-size Frege proofs \cite{Urq}, and extended Resolution p-simulates extended Frege \cite{CR} (KNOWN THEOREMS). These give a polynomial bound via standard ER. Transferring it to the positive-input AND2 grammar of ER2 is not verified here.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[Restriction to Tseitin; PROVED/EXACT, with KNOWN THEOREM consequence]
Under the restriction $\rho$: $z_{e,j}=1$ for $j\ge2$, $F_{G,k}|_\rho$ is exactly $T(G,s)$ on the variables $z_{e,1}$. Since restrictions do not increase Resolution refutation size, and Tseitin CNFs on cubic expanders need size $2^{\Omega(h)}$ \cite{Urq,BSW} (KNOWN THEOREMS), the AND$_k$ lift is exponentially hard for pure Resolution for every $k$. By Theorem~\ref{thm:sandwich}, in ER2 its cost is at most the source floor plus $O(hk)$ plus the cost of $T(G,s)$ itself.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A source with a positive literal $z_{e,j}$, $j\ge2$, is satisfied by $\rho$. The surviving zero-group index choices are $j=1$, and the one-groups shrink to $\neg z_{e,1}$. What remains is exactly the $4h$ Tseitin clauses, and duplicates collapse.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Consequence for the project (FALSIFIED/RETIRED, narrow).} The hypothesis that AND$_k$ substitution \emph{amplifies} the difficulty of Tseitin in $R_{\rm mix341}$ beyond the source floor is FALSIFIED: the excess is at most $\Delta+W_{\rm ER2}(T(G,s))$, and $\Delta$ is linear in $hk$. Any superpolynomial candidate built this way must already be hard through $T(G,s)$ in ER2. That is the classical Tseitin question, not a new lift effect. Whether $W_{R_{\rm mix341}}$ is monotone under restriction (which would give $W_R(F_{G,k})\ge W_R(T(G,s))$) is \textbf{OPEN}, because the closure of LOCAL-CONVERT and the other mixed rules under restriction has not been checked.
