The current lower-bound transport ladder has one exact base rung.  For the
fixed-width implication-chain family
\[
F_b=(z_0)\wedge(\neg z_q)\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{q}C_i(b_i),
\]
where $C_i(1)=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee z_i)$ and
$C_i(0)=(\neg z_{i-1}\vee\neg z_i)$, the formula is UNSAT exactly when
$b=1^q$.  Every orientation bit is sensitive on the all-one input, so every
deterministic exact decider in the declared field-query model reads all $q$
fields.  Equal-length one-bit flips supply SAT controls.

For a paid reduction with costs $R,H$, at most $Q$ target calls of length at
most $g$, and simulation factor $\tau$, the only valid transport is
\[
L_{\rm old}\le R+H+Q\tau T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g),\qquad
T_{\rm new}^{\le}(g)\ge\frac{L_{\rm old}-R-H}{Q\tau}
\]
when the remainder is positive.  The explicit-input query ceiling is the
number of fields, so direct products and padding cannot amplify this route
beyond input order.  For 2-CNF plus at most $k$ ternary clauses,
$O(2^k\operatorname{poly}(N))$ is a certified upper bound, not a lower bound;
$k=O(\log N)$ is polynomial, while unrestricted $k$ reaches NP-complete
3-CNF.  Proving unavoidable post-acquisition internal work for every uniform
exact decider is the next load-bearing theorem.
