\textbf{Status: P versus NP remains OPEN.}
This document does not assume the truth of either equality or separation. The constructive target is one unconditional, uniform, answer-preserving, recursively closed physical child of length at most $N^\lambda$ for a fixed $0<\lambda<1$; no such all-input SAT map is known. The separation track retains published time--space lower bounds and conditional ARC/FLMV arithmetic, but its all-input transformation also remains open. The OpenAI one-recursive-call quantum architecture uses target-dependent Boolean-oracle contents that it does not construct classically, so it is architectural inspiration rather than a SAT algorithm. Local projection examples have both quadratic auxiliary-free CNFs and linear circuits and therefore prove neither inherent expansion nor general contraction. A connected proper-3CNF table image establishes that all-boundary lossless detached summaries sometimes require \(\Omega(N/\log^2N)\) bits, while that same easy image admits a uniform answer-preserving \(N^{3/4}\) child and a genuine second level. Inside the exact22 class, special source-occurrence replacement gives exact finite equality fanout, but each copy adds 15 variables and 20 clauses. A retained bounded-fanin port hierarchy has linearly many physical blocks even at logarithmic depth. An occurrence-disjoint product of $m$ exact clause ports is always SAT with $7^m$ models; restoring repeated-variable semantics by explicit pairwise equality takes a tight $3m-n$ links, so that physical route also remains linear. A four-pin online continuation family now gives an exact firewall: source-free context response needs $t$ bits, while the unaugmented decision family has one state and a uniform constant YES child.
