\section{SLICE-UNSAT monotone lower-bound branch}

A new lower-bound branch has been opened around fixed-weight clause-presence slices of
\(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n}\). Let \(N=8\binom n3\) index the proper signed 3-clauses and let
\(U_{n,t}\) agree with clause-presence \(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n}\) on the Hamming layer
\(|z|=t\), with value \(0\) below the layer and \(1\) above it.

The attraction is an established bridge rather than a new solver-to-circuit compiler:
Valiant's slice-function theorem shows that negations can be removed from circuits computing
Boolean slice functions with low overhead. Consequently, a superpolynomial monotone-circuit
lower bound
\[
C_{\rm mon}(U_{n,t})=N^{\omega(1)}
\]
for an explicitly computationally hard slice would transfer to unrestricted Boolean circuits,
provided the exact theorem hypotheses and the hardness/padding reduction for that slice are
verified.

This branch must not infer slice hardness from full-cube monotone hardness. Known slice
phenomena show that canonical monotone functions can become easy on a fixed Hamming layer.
The load-bearing obligations are therefore:
\begin{enumerate}
\item prove an exact fixed-clause-count hardness/padding lemma for a useful \(t=t(n)\);
\item reconstruct the applicable full-cube monotone lower bound for clause-presence
\(\mathrm{UNSAT}_{3,n}\) and identify the first step that fails after conditioning on \(|z|=t\);
\item attempt a repair using the current local-pruning, pair/triple-correlation,
outside-mediator, and low-degree externally mediated clean-core decomposition;
\item red-team every candidate lemma against easy fixed-weight slices, Horn/2-SAT,
Tseitin/parity, bounded treewidth, padding/renaming, and independent-product controls.
\end{enumerate}

The exact-algorithm Liu/chain branch and this lower-bound branch remain logically separate.
They may share structural lemmas, but the former seeks an improved deterministic upper bound
while the latter seeks a lower-bound invariant on an exact clause-count layer.
