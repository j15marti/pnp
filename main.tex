\section{Master contradiction architecture}

The project still uses a contradiction framework:
\[
P=NP
\Longrightarrow
\text{deterministic polynomial-time SAT/search machinery}
\Longrightarrow
\text{one \same\ structural or reconstruction object}
\]
followed by an independently justified lower-bound step. The proof is complete only if the
resource lower bound applies to the same actual computation and survives arbitrary implementation
changes.

The shortest surviving structural spine is:
\[
\boxed{

\to

\]

% BEGIN LSPV-1 OWNED
\subsection{LSPV-1 --- legal orbit slices, exterior span, and repeated-root correction}

\textbf{Status: \statusopen.} The active object is one declared source-sanctioned SU05/Hadamard
realization, not a claimed author-unique vMS bit-level encoding.

For one legal MV/LDE line, ORBITSLICE1 identifies the nonzero-seed slices as cyclic windows of one
binary label sequence. Writing the window columns as \(c_{s,j}\in\Ftwo^m\), define
\[
W_R=\operatorname{span}_{s\in R,j}c_{s,j},
\qquad
U_R=\operatorname{span}_{s\in R,\;j<\ell}c_{s,j}\wedge c_{s,\ell}.
\]
SYNCWEDGE1 proves \(\dim U_R=\operatorname{rank}_{\Ftwo}\mathcal W_R\), using only same-shift
wedges. DIMSUM2 strengthens the quantitative falsification criterion to
\[
\boxed{\rho(H)\ge 1+\dim W_R+\dim U_R.}
\]
Thus \(\dim W_R+\dim U_R=\omega(m)\) on one \same\ legal family already refutes a global
\(O(m)\) product-span theorem; full exterior saturation is unnecessary.

DIAGCONV1 and SUPPORTK1 retain the exact convolution/support formulation
\[
U_L^\perp\cong S\cap\ker\widetilde\Phi.
\]
ROOT2ADIC1 corrects the spectral branch. For the ORBITSLICE1 orbit
\[
N=p^d-1
\]
with odd prime \(p\), \(N\) is always even. Writing \(N=2^\nu N_0\) with \(N_0\) odd,
\[
\boxed{z^N-1=(z^{N_0}-1)^{2^\nu}\quad\text{over }\Ftwo.}
\]
Hence the binary cyclic algebra is repeated-root and nonsemisimple. The conditional ordinary-DFT
formulas in DIAGCONV1/SUPPORTK1 requiring odd \(N\) are unreachable for this \same\ orbit and are
\statusretired\ for the active path. Their exact block-circulant and support-intersection identities
remain valid.

After scalar extension, the correct local factors have the form
\[
K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2^\nu}),
\]
so multiplicity-aware Hasse-jet/generalized-DFT coordinates, or direct \(\Ftwo\) elimination, are
required. Ordinary root values discard nilpotent information and cannot determine the supported
kernel.

\begin{warningbox}
The finite SU05 Eq.~(7) controls in \path{results/raw/product_span/} frequently exhibit maximal
quadratic rank, including all tested nonconstant Boolean-grid leaves in the recorded finite cases.
Those experiments are \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not the exact reconstruction-compatible vMS theorem.
Repeated-root structure is efficiently exploitable algebra and is not itself a runtime lower bound.
\end{warningbox}

\paragraph{Hasse-jet construction and test.}
HASSETEST1 proves that the multiplicity-aware transform is an exact algebra conjugacy:
\[
J_\omega(fg)=J_\omega(f)J_\omega(g)\pmod{\epsilon^{2^\nu}}.
\]
For \(N=2^\nu\), the transform is the invertible mod-2 Pascal/Hasse map at the sole root \(1\).
A new reproducible experiment independently compares direct synchronous wedge rank, supported
cyclic-correlation rank, and Hasse-jet operator rank. They agree in all exhaustive \(N=4\) and
seeded \(N=8\) controls. In the exhaustive \((N,m,k)=(4,4,3)\) battery, full exterior rank
\(6\) occurs in \(2016/4096\) words. Therefore repeated-root/nilpotent structure alone does
\emph{not} force \(O(m)\) exterior span; that generic route is \statusretired.

The local rings have the exact nilpotent filtration
\[
J_\omega\supset\epsilon J_\omega\supset\cdots\supset
\epsilon^{2^\nu-1}J_\omega\supset0,
\]
which makes the local Hasse operator lower-triangular by derivative order. This is a useful
source-specific diagnostic, not a hardness theorem.

\paragraph{Source-typed LDE update.}
LDELEAF1 rechecks the 2025 vMS source. In the PCPP branch,
\[
h=m^{100},\quad r=\min\{2^j:h^r\ge |y_i|\},\quad \Delta=hr,
\]
and $p$ is the smallest prime in $[\Delta^{100},2\Delta^{100}]$. The generator verifies $y_i$
with probability one, forms its canonical LDE $\widehat y_i$, and invokes RMV on it. RMV
enumerates every MV line and every local-extractor seed. The bit-level SU05 code remains a
realization parameter; Hadamard is source-permitted but not uniquely fixed.

A new source-typed leaf battery reconstructs canonical Boolean-grid LDE restrictions before applying
the declared SU05/Hadamard orbit. Every tested nonconstant leaf has
\[
\dim W=m,\qquad \dim U=\binom m2,
\]
while constant controls are deficient. This is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}, not asymptotic. It reinforces
that canonical LDE structure alone does not supply the desired $O(m)$ contraction.

One correction is required: the 2025 vMS source states $h^r\ge |y_i|$, not strict inequality.
Therefore the earlier strict-padding phrasing is stale. Nonconstant-leaf existence is exact when the
padded proof contains both zero and one; the equality/all-one full-grid control remains open unless
the PCPP architecture excludes it.

\paragraph{Corrected rank-transition update.}
RANKTRANS1 independently audits the leaf-rank calculation and withdraws two erroneous tiny examples
stated only in an unverified chat turn; no artifact containing those values entered the repository.
The corrected exhaustive small-grid sweep finds genuine exterior-rank deficiencies when $p$ is
relatively close to $h$, but no deficiencies in the tested grids once the field is moderately larger.
For example, deficiencies occur at $h=5,6$ with $p=11$ and at $h=7$ with $p=11,13$; they disappear
in the tested cases by $p=13$ for $h=5,6$ and $p=17$ for $h=7,8$. This is \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL};
no threshold theorem is claimed.

Coding-theory literature also blocks the shortcut that a large field implies maximal square dimension:
GRS codes retain highly structured small Schur squares. The active question is specific to the binary-labeled,
same-shift SU/Hadamard orbit. The actual vMS separation
\[
h=m^{100},\qquad \Delta=hr,\qquad p\ge\Delta^{100}
\]
is therefore a plausible quantitative hypothesis, not a theorem.

\paragraph{Immediate target.}
Formulate and attack a binary-label shortening lemma under explicit $p,h,m$ separation. Search first
for exceptional LDE leaf families. It is enough to prove $\dim U=\omega(m)$ on one source-legal
\same\ family; maximal exterior rank is unnecessary. Boolean source degree remains \statusopen.

\section{Track D --- primitive traces, BOOL, and falsification infrastructure}

\subsection{Primitive-trace battery}

A valid computational potential must be independent of unknown optimal remaining runtime and satisfy
a primitive-step inequality such as
\[
\Phi(C_t)-\Phi(C_{t+1})\le L\,\mathrm{cost}_t.
\]
Expanding a claimed shortcut into its real microtrace prevents free macro-operations, but no
noncircular potential has yet been proved to force an unrestricted SAT lower bound. Sequential-tape,
VLSI, streaming, cell-probe, communication, and pebbling lower bounds remain restricted-model
results until a model-preserving transfer is proved.

\subsection{BOOL and historical invariant search}

The migrated GROK BOOL program is now primarily a falsification catalogue. Numerous candidate
predictors through C79 were broken by Schaefer islands, Gaussian elimination, compact
representations, padding, or representation-relative escapes. CLAUDE's independent review found a
factor-of-four arithmetic slip in one total-influence identity and a literature mismatch, but neither
changed those negative conclusions.

The durable lesson is
\[
\boxed{\text{semantic or representation complexity}\ne\text{forced machine runtime}.}
\]

Complete TASK-001 source typing for one reconstruction-compatible line. Instantiate the exact
\(a_t\), compute \(W_R\) and \(U_R\), and search for a full stacked minor or an explicit common-kernel

\item \textbf{P2 --- source-match reconstruction.}
Certify \(q(t)\) and \(S_{\max}(t)\) for the actual vMS reconstructor; then compare SGL and Viola
routes with all compiler exponents charged.

\item \textbf{P3 --- arbitrary-algorithm bridge.}
If Track A yields a nontrivial structural theorem, determine whether ACQ1 or another charged
extraction theorem genuinely forces it on the same solver realization.

\item \textbf{P4 --- rerun-resistant causal falsification only.}
Do not extend Search--G with more precommitment terminology unless a concrete audit survives static
reread and a rerun oracle for the selector. A clean failure should retire the remaining causal
hardness candidate.

\item \textbf{P5 --- barrier hinge.}
For any surviving proof spine, state exactly which implication is nonrelativizing and why the same
line is not recovered by algebraic oracle access.
\end{enumerate}
