\begin{itemize}

\section{Time-Space Tradeoffs}
\KNOWN\ (Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, Williams).  
In the indexed read-only-input random-access model, SAT cannot be decided simultaneously in time $N^{t+o(1)}$ and space $N^{s+o(1)}$ whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
for the applicable range of $t$, and in particular cannot be decided in time $N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}$ and space $N^{o(1)}$ (Williams). These are the strongest published simultaneous bounds used in the present investigation. Numerical evaluation confirms $2\cos(\pi/7)\approx 1.801938$. Buss--Williams established that this endpoint is optimal within the framework of alternation-trading proofs for the subpolynomial-space case.

\section{Constructive Single-Child Route and Oracle Firewall}
\PROVED\ (elementary sufficient theorem, peer review pending).
Let $\mathcal C$ be a closed encoded class containing an NP-complete language.
If one fixed deterministic total uniform algorithm physically produces, on
every valid nonbase $x\in\mathcal C$, one answer-preserving child
$G(x)\in\mathcal C$ with $|G(x)|\le |x|^\lambda$ for fixed
$0<\lambda<1$, and all construction/access cost is $O(|x|^c)$, then
\[
 T(N)\le N^c+T(N^\lambda)=O(N^c).
\]
Fixed bounded bases therefore give SAT in P.  Bare existence of such a map is
a reformulation rather than progress: if SAT were already in P, a decider
could output one of two fixed YES/NO bases.

\FALSIFIED\ (theorem transfer).
At OpenAI \texttt{math} commit
\texttt{7ef5c0cc5ca4b0e072b7a050fca09ab24bc6fa3a}, the unitary-synthesis
preprint proves a uniformly generated polynomial quantum skeleton with one
smaller quantum call per level, but the target chooses unrestricted Boolean
oracle contents.  The source explicitly gives no efficient classical
constructor for those contents and states that the generator never computes a
table entry.  The quantifiers $\forall x\,\exists f_x$ alone give no
classical upper bound: a one-query skeleton can use the hindsight bit

polynomial evaluator $E(x,q)=f_x(q)$, or a uniformly materialized and charged

\section*{Conclusion}
The constructive and separation tracks now meet at the same missing discipline: an unrestricted transformation must be uniform, correct on both answer classes, physically encoded, recursively closed, and fully charged on one realization. A valid power-short single-child constructor would put SAT in P, but neither the OpenAI target-dependent quantum oracle nor the selector/projection family provides it. Published hierarchy and time--space theorems remain valid barriers, while conditional ARC/FLMV arithmetic still lacks its all-input machine-producing edge. Restricted representation, backdoor, proof-system, tape, or RAM results do not transfer automatically to ordinary SAT. The joint grid/source-support family shows that large structural measures can coexist with cheap authenticated transfer; the reuse theorem shows that compact current state need not pay for future authenticated updates; Report 191 shows that fixed-boundary exact projection is idempotent; and Report 192 shows that even all strict boundary marginals can erase a decisive global correlation. A successful moving-interface proposal must therefore pay for complete joint summaries, their effective acquisition, source authentication, physical re-entry and a polynomial-value descent rank. The latest port audits separate a closed saturated relation from a true open interface: the triangular one-foot splice is idempotent, while the authenticated two-swap construction exactly transports and fans out a source variable inside exact22. The latter still adds a fresh fixed guard per copy, so it is a compiler upper bound rather than a contraction. The physical truth-table source gives a representation-independent floor for detached lossless boundary relation summaries while also admitting an explicitly charged answer-only single-child shrink on a restricted easy family. No verified argument currently forces either equality or separation. P versus NP remains open.
