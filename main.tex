\section{Known Unconditional Lower Bounds}
The strongest unconditional circuit lower bounds for explicit functions remain modest relative to the thresholds needed to separate P from NP.

\begin{itemize}
\item For general Boolean circuits over the $B_2$ basis, the best known lower bound for an explicit function in P is $3.5n - O(\log n)$ (Mishra--Santhanam, ECCC TR26-260, October 2026).
\end{itemize}

\section{Time-Space Tradeoffs}
\KNOWN\ (Fortnow--Lipton--van Melkebeek--Viglas, Williams).  
In the indexed read-only-input random-access model, SAT cannot be decided simultaneously in time $N^{t+o(1)}$ and space $N^{s+o(1)}$ whenever
\[
s < \beta(t) := \frac12\Bigl(\frac{t+2}{t^2}-t\Bigr)
\]
for the applicable range of $t$, and in particular cannot be decided in time $N^{2\cos(\pi/7)-o(1)}$ and space $N^{o(1)}$ (Williams). These are the strongest published simultaneous bounds used in the present investigation. Numerical evaluation confirms $2\cos(\pi/7)\approx 1.801938$. Buss--Williams established that this endpoint is optimal within the framework of alternation-trading proofs for the subpolynomial-space case.

\section{Constructive Single-Child Route and Oracle Firewall}
\PROVED\ (elementary sufficient theorem, peer review pending).
Let $\mathcal C$ be a closed encoded class containing an NP-complete language.
If one fixed deterministic total uniform algorithm physically produces, on
every valid nonbase $x\in\mathcal C$, one answer-preserving child
$G(x)\in\mathcal C$ with $|G(x)|\le |x|^\lambda$ for fixed
$0<\lambda<1$, and all construction/access cost is $O(|x|^c)$, then
\[
 T(N)\le N^c+T(N^\lambda)=O(N^c).
\]
Fixed bounded bases therefore give SAT in P.  Bare existence of such a map is
a reformulation rather than progress: if SAT were already in P, a decider
could output one of two fixed YES/NO bases.

\FALSIFIED\ (theorem transfer).
At OpenAI \texttt{math} commit
\texttt{7ef5c0cc5ca4b0e072b7a050fca09ab24bc6fa3a}, the unitary-synthesis
preprint proves a uniformly generated polynomial quantum skeleton with one
smaller quantum call per level, but the target chooses unrestricted Boolean
oracle contents.  The source explicitly gives no efficient classical
constructor for those contents and states that the generator never computes a
table entry.  The quantifiers $\forall x\,\exists f_x$ alone give no
classical upper bound: a one-query skeleton can use the hindsight bit
$f_x(0)=L(x)$ for any language $L$.  A valid classical repair needs one fixed
polynomial evaluator $E(x,q)=f_x(q)$, or a uniformly materialized and charged
oracle circuit; then ordinary composition is polynomial.

\PROVED\ (restricted representation audit, peer review pending).
For
\[
 F_t(z)=\bigwedge_i(z\vee A_i)\wedge
        \bigwedge_j(\neg z\vee B_j),
\qquad
 \exists z\,F_t=
 (\bigwedge_iA_i)\vee(\bigwedge_jB_j)
 =\bigwedge_{i,j}(A_i\vee B_j),
\]
with disjoint two-literal $A_i,B_j$, the auxiliary-free projection has
$t^2$ prime-implicate clauses, while a shared fan-in-two circuit has $4t-1$
gates.  Full Tseitin re-entry has $12t-2$ clauses and remains
$\Theta(t\log t)$ bits, the same exponent as the original selector.
Reintroducing one selector can reproduce the original encoded instance exactly.
Thus compact representation is useful but supplies no fixed-power, recursively
closed shrink on this family.  Exhaustive checks covered 69,904 assignments;
finite evidence remains \textbf{COMPUTATIONAL}.

\OPEN.  The decisive constructive arrow is an unconditional, answer-oblivious,
all-input child constructor with physical encoded shrink, effective choices,
closure at a second genuine recursive level, and charged failed attempts,
storage, access, and model transport.  A local failure or a restricted
representation lower bound does not imply P$\ne$NP.

\section{Conditional Resource Contraction}
Under the temporary assumption that a fixed uniform deterministic total correct SAT solver $A$ exists with
\[
T_A(N)\le N^{K+o(1)},\qquad S_A(N)\le N^{L+o(1)}
\]
for unknown finite $K,L\ge 2$, the parameters $\lambda=1/(5K)$, $\zeta=0.1$ would, if an all-input transformation of the stated budget existed, yield a simultaneous profile
\[
t'\le 1.3-\frac1{5K},\qquad s'\le 0.2
\]
that lies strictly inside the FLMV forbidden region for every finite $K\ge 2$. Independent numerical verification confirms the membership (e.g., for $K=2$, $t'=1.2$, $\beta(1.2)\approx 0.511 > 0.2$; for large $K$, $t'\to 1.3$ and $\beta(1.3)\approx 0.326 > 0.2$). The recurrence
\[
t'\le\max(1.1,0.6+t/2),\qquad s'\le\max(0.5,s/2)
\]
has fixed point $(1.2,0.5)$ satisfying the tangent certificate $433t+216s<630$.  

\OPEN. The required universal all-input construction of $\le N^{1-\lambda+\zeta+o(1)}$ ordinary SAT children of length $\le N^{\lambda+o(1)}$, with aggregate local work $\le N^{1.1+o(1)}$ and live parent space $\le N^{0.2+o(1)}$, including every discovery, correctness, access, memory and combination cost, remains unproved. Numerical membership alone does not establish a contradiction. Primary literature supplies no such universal fully charged transformation. Exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 is NP-complete, while Horn and 2-SAT are in P and may serve as restricted test cases. Reconfirmed 2026-10-29 00:30 UTC: no new primary source closes the transformation.

\RESTRICTED. On Horn and 2-SAT instances, polynomial-time algorithms exist independently of any hypothetical solver $A$. Thus restricted contractions are feasible in principle without black-box access to $A$, but this does not transfer to the NP-complete case.

\FALSIFIED\ (restricted model). Literal clause partitions fail on expander graphs. After Gaussian elimination of a Tseitin affine shell conjoined with an arbitrary non-affine core $Q(B)$, the surviving core retains an encoding length that can be $\Theta(N)$, forcing parent-sized queries to $A$ that exhaust the local-work budget $N^{1.1+o(1)}$.

\begin{remark}[Residual Core Size]
Let $F=\mathrm{Tseitin}(G,c)\land Q(B)$ where $G$ is an expander of linear size and $Q(B)$ is an arbitrary exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 formula on $|B|=\Theta(N)$ variables. Gaussian elimination eliminates the affine shell in linear time and produces an equisatisfiable residual instance whose encoding length remains $\Theta(N)$ whenever $Q$ is not itself reducible by the same linear algebra. Under black-box access to $A$ the residual must be submitted essentially intact.
\end{remark}

\section{Proposed Mechanisms (2026-10-28)}
The following falsifiable mechanisms address the open all-input transformation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Explicit cost accounting for black-box discovery and verification of partitions or projections on ordinary 3-SAT instances; falsify if any concrete uniform procedure meets the $N^{1.1+o(1)}$ local work and $N^{0.2+o(1)}$ space bounds on a positive-density family of hard instances.
\item Residual core analysis after affine/XOR elimination on Tseitin formulas conjoined with an arbitrary non-affine core $Q(B)$; quantify description size and evaluation cost under black-box $A$. The high-treewidth affine shell disappears linearly, but $Q(B)$ can remain an arbitrary NP-complete instance of parent size $\Theta(N)$.
\item Circuit-simulation overhead via Pippenger--Fischer before transferring any conditional decision-circuit lower bound; falsify if the simulation exhausts the margin to the FLMV region.
\item Near-boundary UNSAT certificate portfolios for the seed $T_A\le N^{1.802+o(1)}$, $S_A=N^{o(1)}$; construct minimally unsatisfiable exact-(2,2)-CNF-3 instances forcing aggregate verification outside the van Melkebeek window via $\sum m_i^d\le M^{d-1}V$.
\item Black-box query complexity lower bounds for discovering useful semantic children on expander Tseitin instances.
\item Adaptive reconfiguration and state-dependent overhead accounting when the solver $A$ is adaptive or memoizing.
\end{enumerate}
No mechanism has been established as a fully charged universal transformation.

\section*{Conclusion}
The constructive and separation tracks now meet at the same missing discipline: an unrestricted transformation must be uniform, correct on both answer classes, physically encoded, recursively closed, and fully charged on one realization. A valid power-short single-child constructor would put SAT in P, but neither the OpenAI target-dependent quantum oracle nor the selector/projection family provides it. Published hierarchy and time--space theorems remain valid barriers, while conditional ARC/FLMV arithmetic still lacks its all-input machine-producing edge. Restricted representation, backdoor, proof-system, tape, or RAM results do not transfer automatically to ordinary SAT. No verified argument currently forces either equality or separation. P versus NP remains open.
