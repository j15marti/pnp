\paragraph{Structural status.}
FULLPAIR6 remains \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} only in its finite declared slice hypotheses:
for independent legal directions \(T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_r\}\), complete legal pair co-occurrence
is recoverable by rank-membership tests and PAIR2COVER1 yields
\[
\rho(H)\ge 1+r+|E_{\rm full}|.
\]
PAIRDEG1 is now adopted at the same finite fixed-realization scope. With pair multiplicities
\(\mu_{ab}\), \(I_a=\sum_{b\ne a}\mu_{ab}\), and maximum positive local multiplicity
\(\lambda_a\), it proves
\[
d_a\ge\left\lceil I_a/\lambda_a\right\rceil,\qquad
\rho(H)\ge 1+r+
\left\lceil\frac12\sum_a\left\lceil I_a/\lambda_a\right\rceil\right\rceil.
\]
Thus \(r+\sum_a I_a/\lambda_a=\omega(m)\) would be a sufficient asymptotic structural
condition on the exact SAME\_REALIZATION family, but that condition remains \textbf{OPEN}.
No theorem yet gives this growth, Boolean source degree, acquisition, or unrestricted runtime
hardness. MATRIXCERT1, EXCESS1, EXCESSDUAL1, TURANEX1, HADCOEF1, BILINREC1,
DIGCORR1, SPARSEEX1, and COVERBREAK1 retain their previously declared scopes. The earlier
Gram-to-characteristic-two signed-lift route remains retired.

\paragraph{Causal status.}
HEC/CRS-PPC/CCSPC remain a specification of the acquisition problem, not a lower bound.
STRATFLAT1 and LEGALFLAT1 remain local closures: transcript-dependent legality does not
escape polynomial-hierarchy flattening when legality, verification, and a polynomial-size
strategy evaluator are polynomial time. No theorem forces an arbitrary static SAT decider
to expose or evaluate such a strategy. TRACECLOSE1, REACQ1, PH-PROV-CLOSE1, ACR1,
SBACR1, EVACR1, STRATFLAT1, LEGALFLAT1, and PBDP1 therefore remain hostile controls
against uncharged provenance claims.

\paragraph{Reconstruction and exponent ledger.}

\[

\paragraph{Literature and representation guard.}
Agarwal--Ben-David--Blais (2026) give an information/amortized-size equivalence and
success-conditioned direct-product theorem for randomized decision trees. Capelli et al.\
(SAT 2026) show that TDDs generalize OBDDs and give FPT-size TDDs for bounded-treewidth
CNFs; Berkholz--Micun--Razgon (2026) give quasipolynomial TDD-to-OBDD simulation.

inside a stronger circuit-lower-bound argument. Garlik--Gryaznov--Ren--Tzameret (2026)
obtain strong Weak Rank Principle lower bounds in PCR over \(\mathbb F_2\), but this remains

Cascudo's cyclic-code square results are an exact componentwise-product-code correspondence
and an easy-structure control, not a SAT lower bound. Efremenko--Garg--Oliveira--Wigderson's
arithmetic rank-method barrier is mandatory hygiene for generic flattenings, but it is not a
proved obstruction to the project's specific source-typed characteristic-two certificates.
These results are model boundaries and hostile controls, not unrestricted SAT transfers.
Pebbling, pointer-chasing, online/streaming, proof-complexity, communication, and
cryptographic sequential-memory lower bounds remain model-specific absent a charged
SAME\_REALIZATION compiler.
