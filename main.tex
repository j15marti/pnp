A direct fixed-language separation requires every correct total uniform
deterministic decider to fail every polynomial worst-case time bound:
\[
 \forall A\text{ deciding }L\;\forall k\in\mathbb N\;\forall C>0\;
 \forall N_0\;\exists x:\quad |x|\ge N_0,\quad T_A(x)>C(|x|+2)^k.
\]
Hard inputs may depend on the decider. A direct non-P proof for one fixed
NP-complete language needs no extra broadening step. It need not use a
universal source-code compiler or a general circuit lower bound.

The separate common-input UWI benchmark is
\[
 H(F)=\min_{|p|\le b_0+\lfloor\log_2(|F|+2)\rfloor}
                   (t_p(F)+q(|F|)+1).
\]
Polynomial boundedness on all satisfiable inputs is equivalent to P=NP in
the retained model. This is an equivalence, not a lower bound. A common-input
obstruction must defeat every eligible short program on the same satisfiable
input; it is not the preceding algorithm-dependent quantifier.

The active residual is
\[
 G=(B_Xx=c)\land Q(u,v,x),\qquad x=a+Lz,
 \qquad \exists x\,G\iff\exists z\,Q(u,v,a+Lz)
\]
after checking consistency. Parsing, elimination, every remaining free
variable, source access, compact algebra, reconstruction, verification,
output and encoded bit length are charged. Polynomial workspace is allowed;
program description length is not memory.

The latter arrow remains open. The alternative growing-factor criterion is
\[
 s_j=|F_j|+2\to\infty,\quad s_{j+1}\le As_j,\quad
 H(F_{j+1})\ge H(F_j)r(s_j)/B,\quad r(s)\to\infty.

\section{Barrier and representation ledger}
A successful witness computation need not encode all solutions in DNNF.
DNNF, PC/PCR, Resolution, pebbling, one-tape, black-box, communication and
expanded-ANF bounds require a model-preserving transfer before they can bound
unrestricted computation. Existence, acquisition, verification, evaluation
and existential search are separate resources.

Projected rank, polar rank, polar-kernel dimension, fiber count, equation
count, collision entropy, table size, and essential conjunctive factor arity
are not unrestricted runtime measures. CORRELATIONELIM1 gives high-arity
relations with linear circuits. TWOTREEBRIDGE1 gives two small tree-summary
circuits whose joint existential search is NP-complete, but the conjunction
and each supplied-assignment evaluation are still small and easy.

Horn, dual-Horn, Krom, affine/Tseitin, bounded full-instance treewidth,
independent products, padding, renaming, complementation, compact algebra,
DEAD/COPY/DELAY, INTERP and RECOMP remain hostile controls. A small-treewidth
affine carrier need not bound the full coupled graph. Auxiliary variables,
other representations and source-aware recomputation remain legal. The current
upper bounds survive stronger semantic and algebraic access. No
nonrelativizing or nonalgebrizing separation hinge has been established;
Natural Proofs is not invoked. No ETH, GRH, cryptographic or random-oracle
premise is treated as unconditional.

\paragraph{TWOTREEBRIDGE1: exact charged cofactors.}
For a three-valued variable $v$, form three circuits $H|_{v=c}$ and their OR.
If the original circuit has $s_0$ independent and $s_1$ dependent gates,
retained support outputs and the result use at most $s_0+3s_1+2$ gates;
at most $3s_1+2$ new gates are allocated in the existing pool. Reverse
selection returns a valid shared color. This resolves the previous one-variable,
three-summary construction. Repeating it gives only an upper bound
$S_{j+1}\le3S_j+2$, not unavoidable exponential growth. Physical archives
and all index/access costs remain charged.

\paragraph{TWOTREEBRIDGE1: two-tree bridge.}
\CAND{}, complete proof and executable reduction, independent review pending.
Split each source vertex into occurrence copies. Enforce consecutive-copy
equality by a diamond with edges $xa,xb,ab,ay,by$. Partition its edges as
$\{xa,ab,by\}$ and $\{xb,ay\}$, putting each source edge between its two
reserved occurrences in the second part. The first part is a path forest;
the second is a matching with attached leaves. Connect each forest's
components by private two-edge paths to a private hub. Every existing forest
coloring extends, so the two trees admit compatible colorings exactly when
the source graph is 3-colourable. Both directions have polynomial witness maps.
Shared tree vertices need not be leaves; the previous leaf-count theorem
is not invoked outside its hypothesis.

Each individual tree has a uniformly constructible linear-size boundary
summary. The conjunction of the two summaries is also small. Their shared
existential search is a fixed NP-complete problem with a total encoding:
eight-bit header, unary universe size $h$, two membership vectors and two
adjacency triangles, total length $9+h^2+2h$. Malformed inputs reject.
This is a complete reduction theorem, not a proof that the problem is outside P.

\paragraph{Assuming P=NP: audit of the proposed contradiction.}
The predicate EXT(original input, partial coloring) is in NP. Under P=NP a
fixed polynomial decider for it can be unrolled and the original input
hardwired, yielding one polynomial-size general circuit for all partial-coloring
extension queries on that input. Witnesses follow by at most three queries
per vertex. This conditional construction avoids recursively multiplying
representation sizes and preserves the same original input. It supplies no
DNNF restriction, small-space or near-linear time bound, so no retained
restricted-model lower bound contradicts it. This is a hypothesis audit,
not a new unconditional algorithm or a renamed separation lemma.
