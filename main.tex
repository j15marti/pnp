\section{Current target and exact quantifiers}
For satisfiable encoded formulas, the retained universal-work benchmark is
\[
H(F)=\min_{|p|\le b_0+\lfloor\log_2(|F|+2)\rfloor}
       \bigl(t_p(F)+q(|F|)+1\bigr).                         \tag{1}
\]
Polynomial boundedness of $H$ on all satisfiable inputs is equivalent to
$P=NP$ in the declared project model.  This is a transfer equivalence, not a
lower bound.  Its obstruction has common-input quantifiers: for each budget a
single satisfiable $F$ must defeat every eligible short program.  This differs
from a direct decision-language lower bound, where the hard input may depend
on the algorithm.

The active structured object is
\[
G=(B_Xx=c)\land Q(u,v,x),
\qquad x=a+Lz,
\qquad G\equiv Q(u,v,a+Lz).                                \tag{2}
\]
All parsing, decomposition discovery, Gaussian elimination, circuit access,
candidate testing, reconstruction, verification, and encoded bit length are
charged.  Full source access, arbitrary polynomial workspace, compact
circuits, and input-assisted witness lifting are permitted.

\section{Shortest current proof map}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Affine base---\EXACT.}  A consistent Tseitin system has a uniform spanning-forest witness algorithm.
\item \textbf{Residual contraction---\EXACT.}  For residual edge support $R$,
\[
\rho=\dim\pi_R\ker B_X=|R|+\operatorname{cc}(X)-\operatorname{cc}(X-R),
\]
with all isolated vertices counted.
\item \textbf{Upper-bound escape---\EXACT.}  Feasible projections form an affine space of $2^\rho$ points and extend in polynomial time.  With $h$ other Boolean variables the bound is $2^{\rho+h}\operatorname{poly}(N)$.
\item \textbf{Cotree relocation---\EXACT.}  Cotree projection is a polynomial-time witness bijection.  Arbitrary SAT placed there is relocated, not amplified.
\item \textbf{Field/polar firewalls---\EXACT.}  FIELDTRACELIFT1 proves that $Q(x,y)=xy$ over $\mathbb F_{2^r}$ has a uniform selector although every nonzero scalar polar combination is nonsingular.
\item \textbf{Polynomial-automorphism firewalls---\EXACT.}  BIJECTIVEQFW1 and QUADSHEAR1 give quadratic permutations with charged polynomial inverses.  POLARKERNEL1 recovers the hidden shear quotient from raw ANF.
\item \textbf{Zero-kernel singleton firewall---\EXACT.}  For odd $d\ge3$, $Q(z)=z^3$ on $\mathbb F_{2^d}$ has $K_Q=0$, singleton fibers, and inverse $b^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3}$.
\item \textbf{Planted even-cube section firewall---\EXACT.}  For even $d\ge4$, $Q(z)=z^3$ has zero polar kernel, proper image, and three-to-one nonzero fibers, yet a source-matched request admits the explicit polynomial section $z=s_d(u)$.
\item \textbf{Target-only cube selectors---\EXACT{} / \CAND.}  CUBEROOTSEL1 is \EXACT{} for $\nu_3(d)=0$.  VALSUBCUBEFW1 is now \EXACT{} for every fixed $\nu_3(d)$.  The uniform extension to $3^{\nu_3(d)}=O(\log d)$ is \CAND{} pending independent review.
\item \textbf{Amplification---\COND.}  If satisfiable $F_j$ satisfy $s_{j+1}\le As_j$ and
\[
H(F_{j+1})\ge H(F_j)\,r(s_j)/B,\qquad r(s)\to\infty,
\]
then $H$ is superpolynomial.  No verified construction supplies this premise.
\item \textbf{Unrestricted lower bound---\OPEN.}  No theorem forces every eligible short program or uniform decider to pay superpolynomial cost for the nonlinear residual.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Barrier and hostile-control ledger}
\begin{itemize}
\item DNNF, PC/PCR, Resolution, pebbling, single-tape, communication, and expanded-ANF lower bounds do not transfer to unrestricted witness search without a model-preserving theorem.
\item A successful trace need not represent all models.  Program description length is not workspace.  A fixed percentage overhead is not a fixed exponent gap.
\item Horn, dual-Horn, Krom, affine/Tseitin, bounded treewidth, independent products, padding, renaming, complementation, compact representations, DEAD, COPY, DELAY, INTERP, RECOMP, and field/trace/power selectors remain mandatory controls.
\item Projected rank, polar rank, polar-kernel dimension, fiber size, collision-effective entropy, nonlinear equation count, and graph treewidth are not unrestricted runtime measures.  CUBEPERMFW1 defeats $K_Q=0$ plus singleton fibers.  PLANTEDCUBEFW1 defeats the same conjunction with a proper image and nonunique fibers when a source-witness section exists.  CUBEROOTSEL1 and VALSUBCUBEFW1 further show that erasing the source root does not help on $\nu_3(d)=0$ or any fixed valuation; VALSUBREVIEW1 extends the algorithmic firewall to $3^{\nu_3(d)}=O(\log d)$.
\item Current firewalls relativize and algebrize; no nonrelativizing hinge is known.  Natural Proofs is not invoked here.
\item Cryptographic hardness, ETH, random-oracle assumptions, and average-case MQ evidence are not unconditional lower bounds.
\end{itemize}

\section{LSPV-1: lower-bound specialty}
RANKNONLINFW1 established
\[
\rho=|R|+\operatorname{cc}(X)-\operatorname{cc}(X-R)
\]
and the charged $2^{\rho+h}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ enumeration upper bound.
Large projected rank plus nonlinearity is \RET{} as a sufficient
all-algorithm premise.  The conditional growing-factor theorem is retained,
but no construction supplies its required $r(s)\to\infty$.

BIJECTIVEQFW1 and PLANTEDCUBEFW1 give complementary \EXACT{} firewalls.
A quadratic triangular permutation has a charged polynomial inverse despite
singleton fibers and full projected rank.  For every even $d\ge4$, cubing on
$\mathbb F_{2^d}$ has a proper image, three-to-one nonzero fibers, and zero
scalar-polar kernel, yet the source-matched residual
\[
 F(u)\land[B_Xx=c]\land[(\pi_Cx)^3=s_d(u)^3]
\]
has the polynomial section $\pi_Cx=s_d(u)$ followed by the tree lift.
These structural properties are therefore \RET{} as sufficient work
premises when an explicit section exists.

CYCLOROOTFW1 adds a representation-driven counterexample outside the
logarithmic-subfield window.  For
\[
 d_a=2\cdot3^a,\qquad
 f_a(X)=X^{2\cdot3^a}+X^{3^a}+1=\Phi_{3^{a+1}}(X),
\]
the Frobenius-order calculation
$\operatorname{ord}_{3^{a+1}}(2)=2\cdot3^a$ makes $f_a$ irreducible and the
residue of $X$ an element of exact order $3^{a+1}$.  It is therefore a
public cubic nonresidue.  For
$2^{d_a}-1=3^{a+1}m$, $3\nmid m$, a promised cube $b$ is selected by
$y=b^{3^{-1}\bmod m}$ and testing
\[
 yX^j,\qquad 0\le j<3^a=d_a/2.
\]
Recognition, exponentiation, the linear-size correction search, and the
Tseitin forest lift are polynomial in encoded length.  Hence unbounded
$\nu_3(d)$ and $3^{\nu_3(d)}\gg\log d$ are \RET{} as stand-alone hardness
premises.  The theorem is \CAND{} pending independent review; its
1,210-assertion audit through degree 162 is \COMP{}.  Evidence:
\path{research/agents/lspv-1/2026-10-14_0107_LSPV-1_CYCLOROOTFW1.tex}.

This does not supply a selector for arbitrary opaque field representations
and does not solve any conjoined source SAT instance.  The growing-factor
construction, unrestricted all-program lower bound, and P versus NP remain
\OPEN{}.

\section{LSPV-2: tests specialty}
COTREELIFT1 independently proves the cotree witness bijection and the honest
upper bound $2^{\rho+h}\operatorname{poly}(N)$ when $h$ external bits remain.
Its exhaustive controls also minimize the first shared-variable failure:
fresh-edge lifting does not generalize automatically.

HIERLIFT-TEST1 independently proves the decoded unique-extension composition
rule: a polynomially decoded hierarchy of polynomial depth whose layers have
uniform polynomial-time unique extensions admits source-witness lifting in
source time plus polynomial reconstruction/output cost.  Its finite audit
passed 3,619 assertions and reconstructed 256 complete hierarchies.  Thus
explicit polynomial-depth iteration of uniformly easy extensions is
\RET{} as a sufficient residual-only amplifier.

CUBEROOTSEL1 now attacks the stronger planted-preimage-erasure repair.
For even $d\ge4$ with $6\nmid d$, write
$m=(2^d-1)/3$ and $e=3^{-1}\pmod m$.  Every promised cube
$b\in\operatorname{im}(z\mapsto z^3)\subseteq\mathbb F_{2^d}$ has the
target-only selected root
\[
 R_d(0)=0,\qquad R_d(b)=b^e\quad(b\ne0),
 \qquad R_d(b)^3=b.
\]
Repeated squaring computes this selector in polynomial time, and recomputing
the cube recognizes off-image inputs.  Public invertible affine input/output
conjugations preserve the selector.  For the $K_{s,s}$ carrier one may choose
odd $s$ with $3\nmid(s-1)$ and $d=(s-1)^2=O(n)$, giving an infinite
polynomial-size typed subtype with target-only cube selection followed by
the spanning-forest lift.  The symbolic theorem is \EXACT{} after independent LSPV-3 review; the
finite audit is \COMP{}.

The audit passed 27,810 assertions: 5,896 image targets, 11,784 off-image
rejections, 1,296 affine-conjugate targets, 540 complete Tseitin witness
reconstructions, and 862 cases where the selected root differed from the
erased planted root.  Exhaustive degrees $4,8,10,14$, actual square cotree
dimensions $4,16$, disconnected/isolate graphs, YES/NO charges, renaming,
complementation, malformed encodings, and reducible/odd-degree controls were
covered.  Therefore planted-preimage erasure alone is \RET{} as a repair
when $6\nmid d$.  When $6\mid d$, this monomial exponent selector does not
exist, but that is only an \OPEN{} algorithmic case, not a lower bound.
General nonlinear residuals and the unrestricted all-program lower-bound
arrow remain \OPEN{}; P versus NP remains open.

POLARKERNEL1 independently confirms QUADSHEAR1 and its typed Tseitin lift as

FIELDTRACEFW1 and CUBEPERMFW1 separately retire zero polar kernel, including

ORBITDECODERFW1 separates unique extension from canonical source orientation.
For the quadratic involution $S(a,b,c)=(a,b,c\oplus ab)$, the relation
$y=S(x)$ is invariant under swapping the source and target blocks.  The
general orbit obstruction remains \CAND{} pending independent review, but
the ambiguity is not hardness because $S^{-1}=S$.  Thus a universal decoder
for arbitrary untagged presentations and hidden orientation as a sufficient
hardness repair are \RET{}; declared tags and charged metadata remain legal.

VALSUBCUBEFW1 independently verifies CUBEROOTSEL1, so its target-only exponent
selector for even $d$ with $6\nmid d$ is now \EXACT{}.  It also proves the
stronger fixed-valuation theorem.  For each fixed $a=\nu_3(d)\ge1$, put
$r=2\cdot3^a$.  The subfield
$\mathbb F_{2^r}\subseteq\mathbb F_{2^d}$ has the same $3$-primary
multiplicative order as the ambient field.  Its basis is the kernel of
$z\mapsto z^{2^r}+z$ and its constant-size enumeration yields a cubic
nonresidue.  An explicit Adleman--Manders--Miller correction then selects one
cube root from the target alone in $\operatorname{poly}(d)$ time.  The
general theorem is \EXACT{} after independent LSPV-5 synthesis review; its
finite audit is \COMP{}.

The audit passed 7,659 assertions in 1.702 seconds for $d=6,12,18,36$,

PERMEXT1 makes the residual genuinely source-matched.  For a source CNF
$F(u)$, public $b(u)$ and parity-valid $c(u)$, a connected graph $X$ of odd

\[
 G_F(u,x)=F(u)\land[B_Xx=c(u)]
              \land[(\pi_Cx)^3=b(u)].
\]
On a promised-valid record, every source witness has exactly one complete
extension,
\[
 z=b(u)^{(2^{d+1}-1)/3},\qquad
 x=\operatorname{Lift}_{c(u)}(z),
\]
so the semantic witness bijection and polynomial source-witness lift remain
\CAND{} pending independent review.  The finite PERMEXT1 audit is \COMP{}.

PROMISEGATE1 proves that arbitrary declared even-charge and inverse-circuit
contracts cannot simply be checked in polynomial time: the corresponding
universal validity languages are coNP-complete.  Its repaired subtype fixes
the Gold circuit and generates the last charge bit as the XOR of the others.

CHECKABLEPERM1 audits the actual PERMEXT1 graph parser.  On the smallest tested
$d=3$ record, the legacy parser accepted seven malformed tree/cotree or graph

off-image-recognition claim is \RET{}, without retiring the semantic theorem
on valid records.  A strict recognizer now checks endpoint safety, simple
edges, cycle rank, an in-range disjoint tree/cotree partition, and that the
declared tree is spanning.  The symbolic recognizer and unique tree-lift proof
are \EXACT{} (independent review pending); the deterministic audit passed

Thus source dependence, shared variables, nonlinearity, singleton fibers,

themselves yield extra unavoidable residual work.  The unrestricted lower
bound and P versus NP remain \OPEN{}.

\section{LSPV-5: synthesis specialty}
The logarithmic-subfield theorem covers even degrees with
$3^{\nu_3(d)}=O(\log d)$ and is \CAND{} pending specialist review.  If a fixed $C$ satisfies
\[
 3^{\nu_3(d)}\le C\log_2(d+2),
\]
then the fixed subfield has at most
$2^{2\cdot3^{\nu_3(d)}}\le(d+2)^{2C}$ elements and the correction list has
at most $C\log_2(d+2)$ entries.  One input-driven algorithm is consequently
polynomial on this entire promise window.  This logarithmic-subfield theorem
is \CAND{} pending independent specialist review.  It permits unbounded
$\nu_3(d)$ and shows that the unbounded-valuation condition alone is not the
correct remaining boundary.
