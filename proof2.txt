PUBLISHED HARD-FAMILY LEDGER: EXACT MODEL, 3-CNF, SHORTCUT AND MISSING TRANSFER
[P2-F1] KNOWN THEOREM | Haken standard PHP_{h+1 -> h}: Boolean placement variables x_ij, h+1 pigeon-at-least-one clauses of width h, and per hole/pigeon pair binary collision clauses. UNSAT by pigeonhole counting. Haken (1985) gives 2^{Omega(h)} proof-size lower bounds for ordinary shared-DAG Resolution. Original is NOT strict 3-CNF for h>3; a polynomial equisatisfiable splitter reduction is not automatically lower-bound-preserving. Cook (1976) gives polynomial Extended Resolution refutations. No arbitrary-SAT-machine hardness follows.
[P2-F2] KNOWN THEOREM and PROVED/RESTRICTED shortcut | Odd-charge cubic-expander Tseitin 3-CNF: h connected simple cubic vertices, 3h/2 edge variables, odd vertex charges, four signed width-3 clauses per vertex, hence exactly 4h original clauses and encoded length Theta(h log h) with consecutive IDs. Summing vertex XOR charges gives contradiction. For suitable expander G_h, Urquhart (1987), Ben-Sasson/Wigderson (2001): 2^{Omega(h)} ordinary shared-DAG Resolution proof-size lower bound. Project LSPV-2/341 and LSPV-1/423,452 report a 4h-1-node AND3/GF(2) source-anchored derivation in the DECLARED SEMANTIC R_mix341. Therefore this family is NOT currently a full mixed superpolynomial witness. Authentic historical accepted-byte derivation remains OPEN.
[P2-F3] KNOWN THEOREM / TRANSFER OPEN | Braun bit-PHP (arXiv:2609.23015v2, 2026-11-03): n=2^ell holes, n+1 pigeons, ell address bits per pigeon. Unrestricted DAG-like Res(XOR) proofs of its ORIGINAL long-form bit-PHP require >exp(n/(32768 ell^2)) clauses for ell>=32. Res(XOR) is NOT R_mix341. Converting its long constraints to proper 3-CNF is polynomial and equisatisfiable, but no lower-bound-preserving simulation into full native R has been proved. Cook ER and project LSPV-1/453/457 show short proofs in a DIFFERENT extension-variable calculus; they cannot be backtranslated to Res(XOR) at polynomial cost by assumption.
[P2-F4] KNOWN THEOREM / TRANSFER OPEN | Sparse-expander graph functional PHP: de Rezende, Nordstrom, Risse, Sokolov, TheoretiCS 4 (2025), article 9, prove exponential ordinary shared-DAG Resolution bounds for EXACT sparse graph regimes. The original pigeon rows can exceed 3 literals. Proper signed 3-CNF splitter/padding conversion preserves satisfiability, but proof-size hardness preservation has not been established. Project LSPV-1/459-460 proves RES-only shortcuts when Hall-deficient neighborhoods are small; such easy subfamilies are not witnesses to the cited hard theorem.
[P2-F5] PROVED/RESTRICTED project findings | LSPV-1/450-452: finding a shortest XOR derivation can be NP-complete even while Gaussian elimination produces polynomial-size UNSAT proofs. NP-hard proof optimizer discovery does not imply superpolynomial minimum proof work.
