\paragraph{New T5 constructive theorem.}

\[
  \ell+q+2\sum_i t_i\equiv0\pmod5.
\]
For \(\ell\ge4\), choosing \(t_i\) on the first four edges covers all five residues; for \(\ell=3\), each \(q=0,1,2,3\) has a closing choice. Each attached tree extends with increment \(+1\) or \(-1\) along its arcs. The native edge-minimal unsatisfiable oriented \(K_{2,3}\) (five vertices, six arcs) has cycle rank two, showing the at-most-one-cycle satisfiability boundary is sharp.

\section{CURRENT TMR-5/098: SAT-only intrinsic T5 critical witnesses and undirected girth}
\label{sec:tmr5-098-critical-girth}
\noindent\textbf{Status: exact finite obstructions and source-aware algorithms PROVED; high undirected-girth critical DAG existence PROVED from Erd\H{o}s; unrestricted machine lower bounds OPEN.}
Throughout this section, \emph{girth} is the length of the shortest undirected cycle of the underlying graph; it must not be confused with \emph{directed} girth.

\paragraph{A native five-arc critical UNSAT DAG.}
Take vertices $0,1,2,3$ and arcs
\[
 E(H_0)=\{0\to1,0\to2,1\to2,1\to3,3\to2\}.
\]
Each T5-homomorphism of a transitive triangle $a\to b\to c$, $a\to c$ has $(x_a,x_b,x_c)=(t,t+3,t+1)\pmod 5$. The triangles $(0,1,2)$ and $(1,3,2)$ require simultaneously $x_2-x_1=3$ and $x_2-x_1=1$, so $H_0$ is UNSAT. Deleting the five edges in displayed order yields respectively $10,10,5,10,10$ satisfying assignments. Exhaustive verification certifies minimality: this is not an attached fixed UNSAT gadget.

\paragraph{Arbitrary-orientation paths have an exact $\mathbb Z_5$ transfer formula.}
For a simple path $v_0,\ldots,v_\ell$, write $\epsilon_i=0$ if $v_i\to v_{i+1}$, $\epsilon_i=1$ if $v_{i+1}\to v_i$, and $q=\sum_i\epsilon_i$. Every satisfying increment has the form
\[
 x_{v_{i+1}}-x_{v_i}\equiv1+\epsilon_i+2z_i\pmod5,\qquad z_i\in\{0,1\}.
\]
Hence the number of internal assignments connecting colors $a,b$ is exactly
\[
\boxed{
 T_{\ell,q}(a,b)=\sum_{t=0}^{\ell}\binom{\ell}{t}
  \mathbf1\{b-a\equiv\ell+q+2t\pmod5\}.
}
\]
For $\ell=3$, precisely residue $1+q$ is forbidden, and equal endpoint labels always extend. For $\ell=4$, every endpoint difference has a positive count in $\{1,1,4,4,6\}$; for $\ell>4$ every endpoint difference remains reachable. Thus \emph{no connected edge-minimal UNSAT T5 oriented graph can contain a path of four or more arcs whose internal vertices all have undirected degree two}: remove a path arc, retain the satisfying endpoint colors and extend the complete path, contradicting UNSAT.

\paragraph{Source-aware linear-time theta witness algorithm.}
For a theta graph, that is, internally disjoint oriented paths joining two terminals, compute each path's five-residue endpoint support $S_i$ from the exact formula. The graph is SAT precisely when $\bigcap_iS_i\neq\varnothing$. A satisfying assignment can be constructed in $O(n+m)$ time by choosing a common endpoint residue and setting the specified number of bits $z_i=1$. For a designated deleted edge, omit its broken path in the intersection and extend both dangling arms from their prescribed endpoint colors. This directly produces SAT witnesses for every edge deletion on native critical theta examples.

Every edge-minimal UNSAT theta has all paths of length at most three, because length-four paths impose no endpoint restriction, and has at least one path of length at most two, because every length-three path supports equal endpoint labels. Therefore it has an undirected cycle of length at most five, a sharp ceiling. The sharp nine-vertex example has paths
\[
0\to2\to1,\qquad 0\to3\to4\to1,\qquad
0\to5\to6\leftarrow1,\qquad 0\leftarrow7\leftarrow8\leftarrow1.
\]
It has eleven arcs, undirected girth five, no satisfying assignments, and all eleven edge deletions are SAT, with exact counts $180,180,60,60,60,60,60,60,40,40,40$. An additional seven-arc \emph{DAG} critical obstruction on six vertices uses paths $0\to2\to1$, $0\to3\leftarrow1$, $0\to4\to5\leftarrow1$, has girth four and SAT deletion counts $50,50,50,50,20,20,20$.

\paragraph{Existence of arbitrarily high \emph{ordinary}-girth critical DAGs.}
For each $g\ge3$, Erd\H{o}s' primary undirected high-girth/high-chromatic theorem supplies a simple graph $U$ with $\operatorname{girth}(U)\ge g$ and $\chi(U)\ge6$ (Canadian J. Math. 11 (1959) 34--38, \url{https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1959-003-9}). Direct all arcs in increasing vertex order. The result is a DAG yet is T5-UNSAT: a T5 homomorphism would supply a proper five-coloring of $U$. Delete arcs until UNSAT is edge-minimal; choose the unique UNSAT connected component and remove isolated vertices. This produces a connected, intrinsically edge-minimal UNSAT DAG $H_g$ with undirected girth at least $g$. It is an \emph{existence} result and supplies neither a polynomial-time/size generator nor a lower bound on SAT-deletion witness search. In particular, it is not a source-preserving NP-hardness reduction.

\emph{Critical literature audit:} Kayll--Morris (Contributions to Discrete Mathematics 18 (2023) 234--243, \url{https://doi.org/10.55016/ojs/cdm.v18i2.73457}) explicitly defines \emph{girth} via shortest \emph{directed} cycle (their Section 2, p.~237); its theorem alone cannot establish underlying undirected girth for DAGs. The Erd\H{o}s theorem is needed above.

\paragraph{Structural consequence, not a time bound.}
Every connected edge-minimal UNSAT T5 graph is undirected 2-vertex-connected and has minimum degree at least two: independently SAT blocks may be rotated to match at a cut vertex, while a leaf can always be extended. Every connected deletion $H_g-e$ admits at least five global color rotations and each witness violates the restored arc. For $g\ge7$, suppress degree-two paths of length at most three. The remaining simple core has minimum degree three and girth at least $h=\lceil g/3\rceil$, because longer degree-two paths are excluded and a core cycle of length $k$ lifts to a cycle of at most $3k$ original arcs. The elementary BFS Moore bound yields
\[
|V(H_g)|\ge3\cdot2^{\lfloor(\lceil g/3\rceil-1)/2\rfloor}-2.
\]
This lower bounds \emph{input size} for given girth, not the running time of a witness algorithm.

\section{CURRENT TMR-5/097: minimum-order OBDD firewall on universally satisfiable T5}
\label{sec:tmr5-097-all-order-obdd}
\noindent\textbf{Status: PROVED for deterministic five-way symbol-order decision diagrams; finite reproduction completed; external review outstanding.}
For a bipartite undirected graph $H=(U,W;E)$, orient all edges from $U$ to $W$.
The resulting \emph{native} T5 instance has no directed walk of length two and
is always SAT by the uniform assignment $x(U)=0$, $x(W)=1$.
Nevertheless, write $F_H(x)=\prod_{u\to v}R(x_u,x_v)$ for its complete
satisfying-assignment indicator. If $\Delta$ is the maximum undirected degree,
then \emph{for any symbol-variable order}, putting the first half of
vertices in $S$, every ordered deterministic decision diagram for $F_H$
has width at least
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{width}(F_H)\ \ge\
   2^{|\delta(S)|/(2\Delta^2)}}.
\]
This is a rank obstruction to \emph{representing the entire solution set},
not a decision-time lower bound.

\paragraph{Direct full-rank T5 minor.}
An induced matching of size $k$ crossing the variable-order cut
exists by greedy extraction, because selecting an edge $uv$ forbids
at most $2\Delta^2$ further crossing edges incident to
$N(u)\cup N(v)$. Hence $k\ge|\delta(S)|/(2\Delta^2)$.
Fix all nonmatching endpoints to the universal SAT labels 0 on $U$
and 1 on $W$. Allow each selected $U$ endpoint to take labels
$\{0,3\}$ and each selected $W$ endpoint labels $\{1,3\}$.
The identities $R(0,1)=R(0,3)=R(3,1)=1$ preserve every
nonmatching constraint, including those with fixed vertices.
As the matching is \emph{induced in the entire graph}, no other edges
join any two selected endpoints. Each matched edge contributes
\[
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det Q=-1,
\]
or its identical transpose. The restricted prefix/suffix matrix
is exactly $Q^{\otimes k}$, with rank $2^k$ over every field.
Therefore the full assignment indicator has at least $2^k$
distinct residual functions under that symbol ordering.

\paragraph{Explicit superpolynomial and expander exponential consequences.}
For the $d$-dimensional undirected hypercube $H=Q_d$, let
$N=2^d$ and $\Delta=d$, with parts determined by Hamming-weight
parity. The elementary edge-isoperimetric inequality
$|\delta(S)|\ge\min\{|S|,N-|S|\}$ yields, at any balanced prefix,
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{width}(F_{Q_d})\ge
  2^{N/(4d^2)}=2^{\Omega(N/(\log N)^2)}}.
\]
A self-contained induction proves the isoperimetric inequality:
split $Q_d$ into two $(d-1)$-cubes of size $M$,
with subset sizes $a,b$. The boundary is at least
$\min(a,M-a)+\min(b,M-b)+|a-b|$, which is at least
$\min(a+b,2M-a-b)$ in the two same-side and one mixed-side cases.
For any standard constant-degree bipartite edge-expander family
with expansion $h>0$, the same argument yields
$2^{hN/(4\Delta^2)}=2^{\Omega(N)}$ width for \emph{every}
symbol-variable ordering, despite the linear-time SAT witness.

\section{CURRENT TMR-5/096: $\mathbb Z_5$ gauge equivalence and cubic grid DAG/cyclic twins}
\label{sec:tmr5-gauge-grid-096}
\noindent\textbf{Status: PROVED, scoped mathematical theorem with independent exact-count corroboration; external review pending.}
Write $S=\{1,3\}\subseteq\mathbb Z_5$, so the $T_5$ arc
$u\to v$ means $x_v-x_u\in S$. Since $-S=S+1$,
a reversed arc is equivalent to shifting its allowed
difference by $+1$. Give every underlying edge $e$ a reference
direction $u_e\to v_e$ and record an orientation $O$ by
$t_O(e)\in\{0,1\}$, with $1$ meaning reversed. If
\[
\phi(v_e)-\phi(u_e)=t_{O'}(e)-t_O(e)\pmod 5
\]
for every edge, the map $x\mapsto x+\phi$ is a bijection
between the solution sets of the two orientations.
A potential $\phi$ exists exactly when every signed cycle sum
of $t_{O'}-t_O$ vanishes in $\mathbb Z_5$; a spanning-forest
traversal plus chord checks decides this \emph{equivalence condition}
in $O(n+m)$ time, not general T5 satisfiability.

Let $A$ be the adjacency matrix of $T_5$ and let
$P_{ij}=1$ iff $j-i=1\pmod5$. Then
\[
 A^{\mathsf T}=AP,\qquad AA^{\mathsf T}=A^{\mathsf T}A,\qquad
 M(w)=A^\ell P^b
\]
for a path of length $\ell$ with $b$ backward arcs relative
to its traversal. Because $(A^4)_{0,*}=(4,4,1,6,1)$,
every private path of length at least four, regardless of its
internal orientation, imposes no existential endpoint
restriction. It may still carry nonconstant counting weights.

\subsection*{Unbounded-treewidth cubic bipartite two-connected pair}
For each $h\ge2$ take an $h\times h$ grid and replace every
cell $(r,c)$ by an undirected 16-cycle on
$v_{r,c,0},\ldots,v_{r,c,15}$. Add a rightward arc from the
left cell's port 0 to its right neighbor's port 5 and a
downward arc from each upper cell's port 10 to the lower
cell's port 15. In $D_h$ orient all 16-cell cycles
cyclically $v_i\to v_{i+1}$, including $v_{15}\to v_0$.
In $D'_h$ reverse the first 15 arcs in every cell, leaving
$v_{15}\to v_0$ and all connectors unchanged.

The common undirected graph is bipartite by local-index parity,
2-vertex-connected and has maximum degree 3, with
\[
 N=16h^2,\qquad M=18h^2-2h,\qquad
 \beta=M-N+1=2h(h-1)+1.
\]
Contracting each 16-cycle yields the $h\times h$ grid,
so treewidth is at least $h$. The digraph $D_h$ has
exactly $h^2$ strongly connected 16-vertex components,
while $D'_h$ is acyclic since each cell becomes acyclic
and connectors point only right/down.

Set $\phi(v_{r,c,i})=i\pmod5$. Reversed edges have
potential difference $+1$; unchanged edges have zero
difference because all connector ports are multiples of five.
The gauge identity therefore gives the exact all-input
solution-set bijection
\[
 \boxed{\#\operatorname{Hom}(D_h,T_5)
      =\#\operatorname{Hom}(D'_h,T_5)}.
\]
Both orientations have explicit linear-time SAT witnesses:
define $g_i=i\pmod 5$ for $0\le i\le14$, $g_{15}=2$,
and $x(v_{r,c,i})=r+c+g_i\pmod5$. Along the internal
cycle, fourteen increments are $+1$ and two are $+3$;
horizontal connectors are $+1$ and vertical connectors $+3$.
Thus $x$ satisfies $D_h$ and $x+\phi$ satisfies $D'_h$.
