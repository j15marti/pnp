\paragraph{Primary exact classification (KNOWN THEOREM).}
$T_5$ is a loopless tournament with directed triangles
$0\to1\to2\to0$ and $0\to3\to4\to0$.
The semicomplete-target dichotomy of Bang--Jensen, Hell and
MacGillivray (1988, DOI \texttt{10.1137/0401029})
therefore proves $\operatorname{CSP}(T_5)$ NP-complete.
Independently, every endomorphism of a loopless tournament is
injective (otherwise one directed edge maps to a loop), so $T_5$
is its own core. It has indegree/outdegree two at each vertex and
is not a directed cycle. The smooth-core dichotomy of
Barto--Kozik--Niven (2009, DOI \texttt{10.1137/070708093})
independently verifies NP-completeness and the associated absence
of a tractable Taylor-polymorphism explanation for this core.
These cited original theorems, not static rank or an unchecked
SAT gadget, justify the classification.

\paragraph{Signed-path saturation (PROVED/EXACT, scoped).}
For a source path of length $t$ with $p$ forward and $q$
backward traversals ($p+q=t$), target increments are
$s_i(1+2e_i)$ modulo $5$, where $s_i\in\{1,-1\}$ and
$e_i\in\{0,1\}$. Endpoint differences form
\[
  \{\,p-q+2j\bmod5:-q\le j\le p\,\}.
\]
There are exactly $\min\{t+1,5\}$ residues. In particular
\emph{every} four-edge oriented path allows every ordered
pair of endpoint labels, independent of its orientation.

\paragraph{Girth-seven path-consistency obstruction
(PROVED/EXACT existential, external review OPEN).}
Let $G$ be any loopless orientation of a simple undirected graph
of girth at least $7$. For each pair $u,v$, let $M_{uv}$ be the
actual target endpoint relation of the unique shortest
undirected path if its length is at most $3$, equality when
$u=v$, and $\Sigma^2$ for distance at least $4$ or infinity.
Every $M_{uv}$ is nonempty and lies inside the initial
(2,3)-path-consistency relation. For every third vertex $w$,
\[
 M_{uv}\subseteq M_{uw}\circ M_{wv}.
\]
If the two shorter-path lengths sum to at least four,
composition is full by the preceding lemma, including when
one argument was already full. If their sum is at most three,
their concatenation is the unique short $u,v$ path up to
immediate backtracks: a second simple path would yield an
undirected cycle of length at most six. Every backtrack can
be inserted while preserving any valid endpoint labels,
since every target vertex has incoming and outgoing support.
Thus $M$ is a nonempty post-fixpoint of the path-consistency
operator. All monotone revision rounds preserve $M$ and
cannot empty a pair relation.

By Erdos's high-girth/high-chromatic existence theorem
(1959, DOI \texttt{10.4153/CJM-1959-003-9}),
there exist finite graphs with girth at least seven and
chromatic number at least six. Orient their edges arbitrarily.
They do \emph{not} map to the loopless five-symbol $T_5$
(any homomorphism would be a proper five-coloring), yet
binary path consistency does not reject them.
Disjoint copies yield infinitely many false positives.

nonconstructive existence theorem, not a tested efficient
uniform source generator. These sources might have cheap
source-recognizers; no mandatory general solver work follows.

\section{Sharp unpinned $C_5$ to $T_5$ edge-gadget minimum /073}

\[
P(K)=\{d\in\mathbb Z_5:\exists\text{ an auxiliary labeling
satisfying all arcs with }x_u=0,\ x_v=d\}.

If $P(K)=\{1,4\}$, then $K$ needs \emph{at least

\paragraph{Complete one-auxiliary classification.}
With no auxiliaries, constraints between $u,v$ project to
$\mathbb Z_5$, $S=\{1,3\}$, $-S=\{2,4\}$ or $\varnothing$.

vertex pair force $\varnothing$. Otherwise each endpoint
can leave $z$ free, require $z$ to be its outneighbor,
or require $z$ to be its inneighbor. If fewer than two

\[
\{0,2,3\}\text{ (same directions)},\qquad
\{1,2,4\}\text{ (}u\to z\to v\text{)},\qquad
\{1,3,4\}\text{ (}v\to z\to u\text{)}.
\]

support with $S$ or $-S$, never yielding $\{1,4\}$.

target color has both incoming and outgoing neighbors.

\paragraph{Theorem A (PROVED/EXACT, elementary; review OPEN).}

and \(\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\); the latter is false. \(\square\)

\exists\text{ correct poly-time }A:\ \mathrm{Sound}_{A,n}

\paragraph{Theorem C, black-box bridge collapse (PROVED/EXACT, elementary; TMR-5/060; review OPEN).}
Fix \(R\) whose proof relation does not depend on an oracle (resolution,
polynomial calculus, cutting planes, Frege and EF qualify). For an oracle \(O\) let
\(B^O(R)\colon\mathrm{SAT}\in\mathrm P^O\Rightarrow\overline W_R(N)\le\mathrm{poly}(N)\).
A \emph{black-box} (relativizing) argument for \(B(R)\) is one that
establishes \(B^O(R)\) for every \(O\).
(i) If \(B^O(R)\) holds for some \(O\) with \(\mathrm{NP}\subseteq\mathrm P^O\),
then \(\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\) unconditionally, hence \(\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}\).
(ii) Therefore a black-box argument for \(B(R)\) already proves
\(\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\).
(iii) For every \(R\) with a known lower bound \(L(R)\), \(B^{\mathrm{TQBF}}(R)\)
is false, so no black-box argument for \(B(R)\) exists.
(iv) For every \(R\), the pipeline consisting of a black-box proof of \(B(R)\)
and a proof of \(L(R)\) is inconsistent.
(v) The same holds for algebrizing arguments, using any low-degree extension
\(\widetilde{\mathrm{TQBF}}\).

gives \(\overline W_R\le\mathrm{poly}\). This statement contains no oracle and
so holds in the real world. A polynomially bounded refutation system places
UNSAT in NP (Cook--Reckhow 1979). For (ii) and (iii) take \(O=\mathrm{TQBF}\);
then \(\mathrm{NP}\subseteq\mathrm{PSPACE}=\mathrm P^{\mathrm{TQBF}}\). Part (iv)
follows from (ii), and (v) holds because the extension answers every Boolean
TQBF query. \(\square\)

\paragraph{Authentic model and two-level decomposition.}
Take a fully authenticated root-reachable acyclic binary-gate source of
length \(L\), with \(n\) original input identities and \(g\) original
gate identities, all arbitrary Boolean binary truth tables allowed.

Each original input has one READ owner, each gate one GATE owner,
and every original shared reference occurrence is a paid REF.
Let \(H\) be the resulting DAG of \emph{distinct component
dependencies}, \(Q=|S|+1\). Restrict to authentic \(H\) with at
most two distinct predecessors per target. Put
\[
 Z=\{s\in S:\ s\hbox{ has at least two distinct consumers in }H\},
 \qquad z=|Z|.
\]

unique distinct consumer, hence one owner in a second-level binary tree.

duplicate that child's \emph{computation}; the references themselves
are counted. This distinction also preserves repeated-child and
noncommutative-gate semantics.

gate and input is evaluated/read once. The instruction totals are
