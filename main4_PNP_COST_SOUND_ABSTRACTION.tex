\begin{theorem}[Source-authenticated semicomplete-module exact quotient /076]
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite explicitly enumerated loopless directed source
without repeated or opposite arcs. Let $M_1,\ldots,M_q$, $q\ge2$, be the
connected components of the undirected graph whose edges are the unordered
pairs with neither directed arc present in $G$.
Suppose that each two distinct $M_i,M_j$ are connected by all
$|M_i||M_j|$ possible arcs, identically oriented.
Then the full five-symbol homomorphism count can be computed in polynomial
bit time and polynomial peak writable space, and an explicit
assignment-preserving, complete, five-way \emph{ordered} decision diagram
can be constructed in polynomial time and space.
If $q>5$, the count is zero. Otherwise its OBDD width is at most
$46\cdot10^{q-1}+1$, its node count at most
$n(46\cdot10^{q-1}+1)$, and it has exactly $Z(G)$ accepted
five-way paths and $5^n-Z(G)$ rejected five-way paths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A vertex outside $M_i$ sees all of $M_i$ in one direction. If that
vertex has target color $b$, all colors in $M_i$ lie in the two
in-neighbors or two out-neighbors of $b$ in $T_5$.
Both possible two-element sets have the form $\{a,a+2\}$.
The ten possible nonempty exact color supports therefore comprise
five singletons and five such pairs. A singleton support has weight
one if and only if $G[M_i]$ has no arcs.
For a two-color support, the restricted target has only one
directed arc. Thus any internal vertex with positive in- and outdegree
rules that support out. Otherwise every internally nonisolated source
and sink has a forced color, while each of $z_i$ internally isolated
vertices has either color. Its exact-support count is $2^{z_i}$
if at least one arc is present, or $2^{|M_i|}-2$ if none is present.
For any two module exact supports, all complete cross arcs hold if
and only if every cross product pair satisfies $R$ in the
authenticated quotient direction. Summing products of these
internal weights over the at most $10^q$ disjoint support tuples
counts each original satisfying assignment exactly once.
If $q>5$, selecting one representative per module forms a
$q$-vertex tournament, whose homomorphism to a loopless tournament
must be injective, impossible for $T_5$.

For the complete OBDD, query all vertices module by module, retaining
the at most ten realized supports of each completed module.
For the active module retain the colors observed so far and the
ten-bit mask of full-support templates still compatible with each
read vertex's source-derived unary forced source/sink color.
If the observed set exceeds two colors or is a forbidden pair, reject.
At module completion, require its actual support template to survive,
and check its complete cross product against earlier realized supports.
Then reset the local masks. Before seeing any active-module color
there is one state; after seeing a single color there are at most
$5\cdot 2^3$ candidate mask states; after seeing an allowed pair,
at most five states. Hence there are at most $46$ nonreject
local states and $10^{q-1}$ completed-support tuples.
One rejecting sink per layer consumes all remaining five-way inputs.
The construction is deterministic and thus preserves pointwise
decisions and both original-input assignment counts.
The parser explicitly authenticates every vertex and arc before
building the complement graph and checks every uniform cross join.
Counting uses at most $10^5$ support tuples on the only potentially
satisfiable $q\le5$ instances; the explicit OBDD uses polynomially
many layer nodes, and each exact integer needs $O(n)$ bits.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Feedback-edge exact representation and counting /074]
For every finite explicitly vertex-and-arc enumerated T5 CSP source,
there is a uniform deterministic source-authenticating algorithm
computing the cyclomatic number $f$ of its underlying simple graph,
constructing a complete original-variable five-way OBDD with
\[
 \mathrm{width}\le 5^{2f+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil}+1,\qquad
 V_5\le n\bigl(5^{2f+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil}+1\bigr),
\]
and returning both exact accepted and rejected original-assignment
multiplicities, which sum to $5^n$. The same source has an independent
exact counting-only algorithm taking $O(25^f\operatorname{poly}(L))$
bit time and $\operatorname{poly}(L)$ writable space. For
$f=O(\log L)$ both computations have polynomial bit cost, with a
constant in the polynomial exponent depending on the logarithmic
parameter bound. A pointwise equivalent 3-bit-code Boolean OBDD
requires at most seven binary internal nodes per five-way node.
An auxiliary-free CNF uses $3n$ Boolean variables, exactly
$3n+15m$ listed clauses of width at most six, including harmless
tautological forbidden-pair clauses for loops.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Parse every vertex record and every directed arc; malformed and
truncated sources are INVALID, while loops and opposite arcs
are valid and can be UNSAT. Thus $n,m\le L$.
A source-derived spanning forest has exactly $f$ feedback edges.
Let $S$ comprise their endpoints, of number $g\le 2f$.
Deleting $S$ leaves an induced subforest. Read the vertices of
$S$ first and recursively read the centroids of each component of
the remaining forest, processing each child component contiguously.
Every centroid removal leaves subcomponents of size at most half
the original size, so the recursion depth is at most
$h=\lceil\log_2(n-g+1)\rceil$. At any query prefix, every
previously read vertex adjacent to a future unread vertex belongs
either to $S$ or to the at most $h$ active centroid ancestors:
no completed child component has an edge to a later sibling.
The deterministic layer-dependent live-frontier colors are therefore
at most $g+h$ five-valued coordinates.
At each new query, check every original directed arc whose second
endpoint has just been read, including self-loops; discard a prefix
on failure but continue its remaining original queries along one
reject-completion state per layer. Two nonrejected prefixes with
identical retained colors have exactly the same remaining truth
function because all old constraints have been tested and all
crossing constraints refer only to retained colors. This proves
the pointwise cofactor congruence, width, node and accepted/rejected
multiplicity bounds. The three-bit conversion uses a complete
7-internal-node decoder with codes $5,6,7$ rejecting.
For independent counting, enumerate the at most $5^g$ assignments
to $S$, test arcs internal to $S$, and form all induced unary factors
between pinned and unpinned vertices. All remaining binary factors
lie on a forest, and each 5-color tree dynamic program computes
the exact partition function using integer addition and
multiplication. The product over forest components, summed across
pinned assignments, is exactly the number of satisfying complete
source assignments. Streaming assignments keeps this calculation
in polynomial writable space with $O(n)$-bit integers.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact orientation-robust transfer count /073]
Let $A_{xy}=R(x,y)$ and $B=A^{\mathsf T}$.
For each substituted edge with orientation bits
$b_1,\ldots,b_4$, its pinned-endpoint marginal is the
ordered product $W_b=\prod_{j=1}^4(A\text{ if forward, else }B)$.

column sums $16$ and determinant $16$. For an undirected
skeleton path with $e$ edges, the exact T5 count is
$5\cdot16^e$, regardless of the orientations. For a cycle
of $e$ edges, the count is $\operatorname{tr}(\prod_i W_i)$,
using $W_i^{\mathsf T}$ for a reverse traversal.
For a balanced two-forward/two-reverse cycle,
\[
 Z(C_e)=16^e+2q_e,\qquad q_0=2,\quad q_1=7,\quad
 q_e=7q_{e-1}-q_{e-2}.
\]
The total count is the product of component counts,
with factor $5$ for an isolated vertex.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both $A$ and $B$ are circulant, commute and each has determinant
$2$ and all row/column sums $2$. Each four-factor product has
determinant $2^4=16$, and direct cyclic convolution of the
five coefficients verifies positivity for all five possible
numbers of forward arcs; every row/column sum becomes $2^4$.
Eliminate the private vertices to obtain exact five-by-five
factors. The open-path count is
$\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}(\prod W_i)\mathbf1=5\cdot16^e$.
Closing the endpoints gives the matrix trace.
For the balanced factor $W=A^2B^2$ the eigenvalues are
$16$ once and $(7\pm3\sqrt5)/2$ each twice.
The two nontrivial conjugate eigenvalues have sum $7$ and
product $1$, giving the displayed exact integer recurrence.
\end{proof}

with \(k\) vertices and \(e\) edges. Replace each edge
\(\{u,v\}\), \(u<v\), by a private three-interior-vertex path
\(u,t_1,t_2,t_3,v\); each of its four arcs can point independently
in either direction. The resulting directed source \(G\) has
\(N=k+3e\) vertices and \(M=4e\) arcs.

\(a,b\in\Sigma\), there exists a legal coloring of the three
internal vertices. Consequently all members are satisfiable,
and a valid certificate is constructible in polynomial bit time.
Writing \(W_{\{u,v\}}=A^fB^{4-f}\), where \(f\) is the number
of forward arcs on the path, the exact original-coloring count is
\[
  Z_{T_5}(G)=
  \sum_{\sigma:V(H)\to\mathbb Z_5}
  \prod_{\{u,v\}\in E(H)} W_{\{u,v\}}(\sigma(u),\sigma(v)).
\]
When every path has exactly two forward and two reverse arcs,
\[
W=A^2B^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
6&1&4&4&1\\1&6&1&4&4\\4&1&6&1&4\\
4&4&1&6&1\\1&4&4&1&6
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad\det W=16.
\]

\(Z_{T_5}(G)\) is \(\#P\)-complete under polynomial-time Turing
reductions, even when \(H\) has bounded degree. The decision
promise remains identically YES.
\end{theorem}

coefficient vectors in \(\mathbb Z[z]/(z^5-1)\) are
\(z+z^3\) and \(z^2+z^4\), respectively. Counting internal
colors of an oriented path is exactly five-by-five transfer-matrix
multiplication. For \(r=0,1,2,3,4\) reversed arcs, the five
row-zero vectors of \(A^{4-r}B^r\), respectively, are
\[
(4,4,1,6,1),\ (1,4,4,1,6),\ (6,1,4,4,1),\
(1,6,1,4,4),\ (4,1,6,1,4).
\]

\section{Source-derived decomposition and liveness}
Let $S$ consist of original vertices occurring in at least two gate operand positions; repeated operands count twice. Write $k=|S|$ and $M=n+g$. The output pseudo-use is only a liveness convention, not an extra operand occurrence. In an acyclic single-output root-reachable DAG the root is not a shared predecessor.

Set $Q=S\cup\{o\}$. For each target $t\in Q$, expand its original predecessor tree only until a shared predecessor is reached, replacing that occurrence by a cache-reference leaf. The target itself is evaluated, not replaced by a reference. An original shared input has a one-leaf READ component. Let $T_t$ be this local expression tree, $D(t)\subseteq S$ the set of distinct referenced shared identities, and $R$ the total number of REF occurrences over all components. Define the core graph $H$ by $s\to t$ exactly when $s\in D(t)$. Distinct core edges are used for dependency and consumer counting, but every repeated REF occurrence remains an instruction.

\begin{lemma}[Authentic partition]
Every original input has exactly one READ owner, every original gate has exactly one local component owner, and $R\le2g$. The total fresh-input and reference-leaf occurrences are at most $n+2g$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every nonshared vertex has at most one outgoing operand occurrence. Following its unique consumer therefore reaches a unique first target. Distinct components cannot contain the same nonshared vertex. Each shared vertex is evaluated at its own target and otherwise appears only as a reference. Each reference leaf is associated with a genuine original operand occurrence, so there are at most $2g$ such leaves. This is not expansion of a shared DAG into a full formula.
\end{proof}

For each local tree use the Sethi--Ullman recurrence: a leaf has label one, a binary node with labels $a,b$ has $\max(a,b)+[a=b]$, and the larger-labelled child is evaluated first. Restore the original operand order at noncommutative gates. Let $r$ be the largest local root label. The usual leaf induction gives
\[
 r\le 1+\lfloor\log_2(n+2g)\rfloor.
\]
The tree-scheduling recurrence is known prior art, not a new general DAG-allocation theorem \cite{sethi,appel}.

Choose a deterministic topological core order $\sigma$. For a completed prefix $I$ let
\[
 F(I)=\{s\in I:\exists t\notin I,\ s\in D(t)\}
       \cup (\{o\}\cap I),\qquad q=\max_{I\text{ prefix}}|F(I)|.
\]
Thus $q$ counts simultaneously live target values, not all $k$ shared vertices and not all wires. At component completion, decrement each distinct predecessor's remaining component-consumer count. DROP it only when that count becomes zero. STORE the new target value after these drops. Finally OUTPUT and remove the root value. Every REF, STORE and DROP is an actual charged instruction.

\begin{theorem}[Liveness-parameterized all-step compiler]
For every authenticated source and every uniformly constructed topological core order, the emitted program has exactly $n$ READs, $g$ original GATEs, $R$ REFs, $k+1$ STOREs, $k$ DROPs and one OUTPUT. Hence its instruction count is exactly
\[
 I=n+g+R+2k+2.
\]
It evaluates every original gate once, reads every original input once, and preserves every original intermediate gate value and both output labels. A conservative logical Boolean cache-plus-stack bound is
\[
 W=q+r+1.
\]
There is a fully constructed complete labelled OBDD $B(C)$ with
\[
 V(B(C))\le n2^W,\qquad B(C)(x)=C(x),\qquad \#B(C)=\#C.
\]
For every field $K$ and each prefix of the discovered input order $\rho$,
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_K M_{\rho_{\le j}:\rho_{>j}}(C)
 \le \#\{\text{reachable states at boundary }j\}\le 2^W.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initially both stack and cache are empty. Before component $t$, every $s\in D(t)$ has already been computed and still has unfinished consumer $t$, hence is present. Each READ introduces its original input bit; each REF copies a present authentic value; each original gate applies its actual truth table. Structural induction on the local tree and then the component order preserves all original source values. After a local tree finishes, its result is on the stack. All REF occurrences of that component have finished before any DROP. A value is removed only if no later component references it, and STORE transfers the result into its designated cache slot. This establishes the liveness invariant $F(I)$ and both terminal outcomes.

The local stack uses at most $r$ bits while the old cache has at most $q$ values. Drops cannot increase storage; a store transfers one stack result. The additional unit in $W$ is conservative staging slack. Actual copied REF values in the scratch stack are counted in addition to their one original cached identity. Public addresses, counters and source storage are charged separately below. The partition lemma gives the exact original-operation counts; every shared target is dropped once, and only the root is consumed by OUTPUT.

Split the public instruction list at its $n$ READs. At a fixed program counter, the cache key set and stack depth are known from the compiled program, independently of assignment values. Therefore an inter-READ state is precisely its live Boolean tuple, not a tuple of all allocated or expired shared slots. From the real initial tuple, breadth-first construction executes the actual intervening instructions on each hypothetical next input bit. Equal successor tuples may merge, but both 0- and 1-labelled arcs remain even if their destinations coincide. There are at most $2^W$ tuples per input layer.

Induction on all instructions proves pointwise source/target equivalence. Because the program reads each original input exactly once in a fixed discovered order, complete labelled paths are in bijection with original assignments. Exact integer propagation through both arcs preserves their multiplicities, proving the count. After a prefix the tuple determines the complete suffix function, so the assignment matrix has at most $2^W$ distinct rows. This proves the rank bound over every field, without equating rank with necessary unrestricted work.
\end{proof}

The compiler uses a source-derived topological fallback on a general core DAG; it does not claim an optimal order or small $q$ there. Original input labels and all $n$ variables are retained, with no auxiliary-variable growth. The serialized OBDD has exactly two labelled arcs per internal node. Physical input seeks in the discovered order are not free.

\section{A polynomial class with linearly many shared vertices}
\begin{theorem}[Recognizable binary in-tree core]
Suppose the constructed core $H$ has at most two distinct predecessors per target and exactly one distinct consumer per shared target, with the root having none. These conditions are checked on the authenticated source. Then a uniform larger-label-first core postorder satisfies
\[
 q\le1+\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor,
\]
and therefore
\[
 W\le3+\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor+
             \lfloor\log_2(n+2g)\rfloor,
 \qquad V(B(C))\le8n(k+1)(n+2g).
\]
The complete construction and exact count have polynomial time and polynomial peak space in source length, without a supplied schedule, even for $k=\Theta(M)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root reachability and acyclicity imply that the checked core is a rooted in-tree, allowing unary vertices. Give a core leaf label one, a unary target its child's label, and a binary target the usual Sethi--Ullman label. Execute the larger-labelled child subtree first. Completed results of that subtree are needed only by their unique parent, so after the parent component has consumed them they can be dropped. Induction shows that the maximum completed-component frontier is the root label: with two child labels $a\ge b$ its peak is $\max(a,1+b)$, and with one child it is unchanged. A label-$h$ binary tree has at least $2^{h-1}$ leaves, which are among the $k+1$ targets. Combine this bound with the local scratch bound and the previous theorem. All recognition, labels, ordering and last-consumer counters are computed from original records in polynomial preprocessing time. The same-realization ledger below makes the full polynomial claim explicit.
\end{proof}

This strictly enlarges the earlier certified domain when combined with the general fallback and the old small-$k$ test. Bounded $k=O(\log L)$ remains sufficient even when $H$ is not a tree, since $q\le k+1$. Many-share tree cores add a new recognized family. Its three-bit block-ordered binary FBDD conversion has at most
$7n5^k$ internal nodes and rejects illegal codes $5,6,7$.
A parsimonious direct CNF uses exactly $3n$ Boolean variables and
$3n+15|E|$ emitted clauses of width three or six.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cover definition leaves no arc within $I$.
Fixing an assignment $a$ to $C$ therefore leaves independent
residual choices, exactly $|D_v(a)|$ for each $v$.
Every full source assignment has exactly one cover restriction;
hence the stated disjoint weighted sum is exact.
Read $C$ first, followed by $I$. Reject a violated internal
cover constraint immediately. Each residual node at level $t$
has state $(t,a)$; good colors advance to $(t+1,a)$ and bad
colors reject. On every accepting path all original symbols are
queried. There are at most $5^k$ nonreject states per layer,
giving $n5^k$ nonterminal states. Rejecting a branch at stage
$t$ represents exactly $5^{n-t-1}$ original completions; this
accounts for all rejecting multiplicities as well.
Replace each five-way node by a full seven-internal-node
three-bit tree; only codes $0,\ldots,4$ continue to the
corresponding five-way child. For CNF, ban three illegal codes
per symbol and the $15$ forbidden endpoint pairs of each
arc, without auxiliary variables. The encoding is bijective
on accepted source assignments.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Mixed structural counterexample, T5/077]

leaf $v_S$ for every subset $S$ of at least two hubs; place
$(u,v_S)$ for each $u\in S$. For $k\ge6$, let $U_k$
add the $15$ arcs of a transitively oriented tournament on
six hubs. Both have $n=2^k-1$ and underlying $2$-vertex-connectivity,

\[
f(F_k)=(k-2)(2^{k-1}-1),\qquad f(U_k)=f(F_k)+15.
\]
Yet $F_k$ is satisfiable and $U_k$ unsatisfiable, both with
cheap source-verifiable certificates. Writing $M=2^k-k-1$,
\[
Z(F_k)=5\cdot2^M+
5\sum_{a=1}^{k-1}
 {k\choose a}\,2^{2^a+2^{k-a}-k-2},
\qquad Z(U_k)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every leaf is adjacent to at least two hubs and every two
hubs share a leaf, so deletion of any single vertex leaves
the undirected graph connected. Different leaves have different
neighborhoods; distinct hubs likewise do not have equal directed

and every hub is nonadjacent to some leaf, making the
nonadjacency graph connected. There are
$M=2^k-k-1$ leaves and
$k2^{k-1}-k$ original arcs. The cover and feedback formulas
follow. Assign color $0$ to all hubs and color $1$ to all

they differ by $\pm2\pmod5$, and none otherwise. The
compatibility graph on five distinct colors is a five-cycle
and has no triangles. Thus contributing assignments are
either monochromatic (five choices, with two colors on each

(five unordered pairs). For a given unordered pair and a

exactly $2^a+2^{k-a}-k-2$ leaf subsets are monochromatic
and hence have two choices; all other leaf subsets have
one. Summing gives the count formula.
\end{proof}

\section{T5/078: exact local-mask frontier, 12 versus 32}
\label{sec:t5mask078}

\paragraph{Object and scope.}
We study the original five-symbol tournament constraint
\(R(x,y)\iff y-x\bmod5\in\{1,3\}\) on authenticated,
complete source graph encodings. Every input source is a graph;
neither ternary clauses nor external unary relations are assumed.
The representation target is a complete, source-assignment-preserving
five-way OBDD and its parsimonious Boolean analogue.
This section bounds the information needed by a very particular
local propagation interface, not arbitrary-machine computation.

\begin{theorem}[Sharp forward-checking domain alphabet]
Suppose each unassigned vertex starts with domain \(\mathbb Z_5\)
and is filtered only by binary beat edges to already assigned
vertices. The reachable domain masks are exactly
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{FC}}
=\{\varnothing,\mathbb Z_5\}
\cup\{\{a\}:a\in\mathbb Z_5\}
\cup\{\{a+1,a+3\}:a\in\mathbb Z_5\}.
\]
Thus \(|\mathcal D_{\mathrm{FC}}|=12\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every forward or reversed arc to a singleton assigned color
imposes one of the same five two-element masks
\(B_a=\{a+1,a+3\}\). Distinct masks \(B_a,B_b\)
intersect in at most one element. Arbitrary finite intersections
are therefore full, empty, a singleton, or a mask \(B_a\).
All cases occur using already queried palette vertices.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Palette and unassigned-tree construction.}
Adjoin the literal five-vertex tournament \(T_5\) as a source
subgraph, and query its five vertices. Every homomorphism
of this palette to \(T_5\) is a cyclic shift, so there are
exactly five gauge branches. In a fixed gauge \(p_a=a\),
the two source arcs
\(p_{a-1}\to v\) and \(p_{a-3}\to v\) pin
an unassigned \(v\) to \(a\), without adding any
unary constraint to the source language. Define
\[
U(S)=\{x:\exists y\in S,\ R(x,y)\},\qquad
V(S)=\{x:\exists y\in S,\ R(y,x)\}.
\]
They are implemented by a single new vertex and edge, and
intersection is implemented by identifying the roots
of independent gadgets. After removing the assigned palette,
all resulting unassigned graphs are trees.

\begin{theorem}[No proper 12-state quotient of full AC]
In this palette-rooted class, every one of the
\(2^5=32\) unary support masks occurs as the exact
root domain left by full bidirectional arc consistency.
A local support-register abstraction that is exact under every
subsequent palette-pin test must distinguish all 32 masks.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let \(P_a=\{a\}\) be the two-arc pin above, and \(F=\mathbb Z_5\).
The following eight expressions generate orbit representatives:
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
\varnothing & =P_0\cap P_1,\\
\mathbb Z_5&=F,\\
\{0\}&=P_0,\\
\{0,2\}&=U(P_3),\\
\{0,1\}&=U(U(P_2))\cap U(U(V(P_0))),\\
\{0,1,2\}&=V(U(U(P_1)))\cap U(U(V(P_0))),\\
\{0,1,3\}&=U(U(P_2)),\\
\{0,1,2,3\}&=V(U(U(P_1))).
\end{array}
\]
Cyclic translations supply the other 24 subsets.
These eight representatives and translates partition the
32 possible masks into the empty/full singleton orbits
and six nontrivial orbits of size five.
The gadgets are tree structured after the palette query.
On trees, full arc consistency leaves exactly the labels
extendable to total solutions, by the classical
tree-CSP consistency result \cite{freuder1982,dechterpearl1987}.
Thus the root domains coincide with the displayed expressions.
For two distinct masks \(S\ne T\), take
\(a\in S\triangle T\) and apply the two-arc
pin test to \(a\). This continuation distinguishes the
two masks. This is a conditional lower bound on the
local \emph{unary-support message alphabet} only.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Small falsifier and general-machine counterexample.}
After pinning \(x=0\), append \(y\to x\) and
\(z\to y\). Full AC gives \(D_y=\{2,4\}\)
and \(D_z=\{1,3,4\}\), so the 12-mask forward
alphabet is not AC-closed. Conversely, all 32
root-mask gadgets become trees after a constant-size
palette is queried. Exact tree DP decides and counts
them in polynomial time. Hence even attainment of
all 32 local masks does \emph{not} yield a source
runtime or unrestricted FBDD lower bound.

\section{T5/079: two-terminal relative-gauge signatures and a cheap mixed family}
\label{sec:twoterminal079}

\paragraph{Candidate and source model.}
The only input is a byte-authenticated directed $T_5$ graph,
$R(x,y)=[(y-x)\bmod5\in\{1,3\}]$; the source does not contain
an annotated separator, palette orbit, witness, or variable order.
Construct $m\ge1$ copies of the literal five-vertex tournament $T_5$,
all sharing the same oriented source arc $u\to v$ and otherwise
disjoint. In each copy the distinguished ordered edge has cyclic
template difference $d_i\in\{1,3\}$. The source graph contains
$n=2+3m$ vertices and $1+9m$ arcs. A source-derived recognizer scans
candidate ordered arcs, deletes their endpoints, verifies that every
remaining component has three vertices, authenticates every induced
five-vertex tournament by at most $120$ permutations and checks
that the original arc list has exactly the specified structure.
Neither the list $(d_i)$ nor the separator is advice.

\begin{theorem}[Exact two-terminal palette-edge sum]
Let $F_i(a,b)$ count valid assignments to the three private vertices
of copy $i$ conditional on $x_u=a,x_v=b$. Then
\[
 F_i(a,b)=\mathbf 1\{(b-a)\bmod5=d_i\},\qquad
 Z(G)=\sum_{a,b\in\mathbb Z_5}\prod_{i=1}^m F_i(a,b)
  =\begin{cases}
    5,&d_1=d_2=\cdots=d_m,\\
    0,&\text{otherwise}.
   \end{cases}
\]
Both satisfiable and unsatisfiable members remain fully
arc-consistent with all five labels present in every unary domain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A homomorphism between two loopless tournaments on five vertices
is injective, hence bijective. The $T_5$ automorphisms are exactly
the five translations $z\mapsto z+t$: after the image of $0$
is fixed, its directed-neighborhood pattern determines all
remaining images. This is also a finite, exhaustively verified
template identity. Fixing the images of $u,v$ therefore permits
exactly one extension if their difference has the source edge's
template orbit, and no extension otherwise. Distinct copies have
disjoint private vertices. Summing the product proves the formula.
Initially every source arc has a support for every endpoint
color, so ordinary unary arc consistency removes no value,
even for the mixed-orbit unsatisfiable case.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp two-channel counting interface]
For an arbitrary $T_5$ source subgraph $H$ with two distinguished
boundary vertices, let $F_H(a,b)$ be the exact integer number of
extensions of those fixed boundary labels to the private vertices.
Then
\[
 F_H(a+t,b+t)=F_H(a,b)\quad(t\in\mathbb Z_5).
\]
The exact count signature thus has at most five independent
relative-difference entries $F_H(0,d)$, not $25$ independent
absolute-color entries. If $u\to v$ is a source arc, then
$F_H(0,d)=0$ outside $d\in\{1,3\}$.
The two glued-palette orbit types produce the linearly independent
indicator signatures of $d=1$ and $d=3$; hence a two-dimensional
linear compositional count interface is both sufficient and
necessary on their span.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The simultaneous shift of every private and boundary color
is a bijection on assignments, because every beat constraint
depends only on relative differences. The source arc restricts
the two remaining differences, and the preceding theorem
realizes the two standard basis signatures. The linear dimension
claim is only about exact compositional \emph{count messages},
not bits of arbitrary nonlinear encodings or lower bounds on
adaptive programs. A pointwise source-assignment-preserving
decision diagram must additionally track enough absolute phase
information to evaluate its unqueried source variables.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constant-width complete OBDD and Boolean transfer]
There is a polynomial-time source-derived exact compiler for
this recognizable family which constructs a complete five-way
OBDD of width at most $626$ and at most $626n$ internal nodes.
It reads all original variables on every path, preserves
the acceptance of every original complete labeling, and
counts rejected as well as accepted assignments exactly.
The three-bit-per-symbol binary OBDD has at most $7(626n)$
internal nodes, and the parsimonious direct Boolean CNF has
exactly $3n+15(1+9m)$ clauses without auxiliary variables.
The graphs are $2$-vertex-connected, have no directed false
twins, have undirected vertex cover $2+2m$ and feedback rank
$f=6m$, all while being source-recognizably easy.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assign $u,v$ first, then read the three private vertices of
each recovered copy contiguously. At every active layer the
state consists of at most $25$ boundary color pairs and at
most $25$ prefixes of the current block, plus a single
reject-only state. Thus width is at most $25^2+1=626$.
Every failed arc continues along a complete rejecting
suffix, so no original assignment's multiplicity is dropped.
For Boolean translation, replace every five-way node by
a complete seven-internal-node, three-bit lookup tree,
rejecting codes $5,6,7$. Independently, forbid three
illegal binary codes per vertex and the $15$ forbidden
ordered color pairs per beat arc. No auxiliary labelings
are created. Each block induces a $K_5$ in the underlying
graph, with the blocks intersecting only in $\{u,v\}$.
Removing any one vertex leaves the graph connected.
The shared pair is a genuine two-separator when $m\ge2$.
Every block requires at least two of its three private
vertices in a cover if the common endpoints are covered,
which gives the stated optimal cover. Direct edge counting
gives $f=6m$. The source recognizer certifies the decomposition
before the compiler uses it.
\end{proof}
