\begin{theorem}[Source-authenticated semicomplete-module exact quotient /076]
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite explicitly enumerated loopless directed source
without repeated or opposite arcs. Let $M_1,\ldots,M_q$, $q\ge2$, be the
connected components of the undirected graph whose edges are the unordered
pairs with neither directed arc present in $G$.
Suppose that each two distinct $M_i,M_j$ are connected by all
$|M_i||M_j|$ possible arcs, identically oriented.
Then the full five-symbol homomorphism count can be computed in polynomial
bit time and polynomial peak writable space, and an explicit
assignment-preserving, complete, five-way \emph{ordered} decision diagram
can be constructed in polynomial time and space.
If $q>5$, the count is zero. Otherwise its OBDD width is at most
$46\cdot10^{q-1}+1$, its node count at most
$n(46\cdot10^{q-1}+1)$, and it has exactly $Z(G)$ accepted
five-way paths and $5^n-Z(G)$ rejected five-way paths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A vertex outside $M_i$ sees all of $M_i$ in one direction. If that
vertex has target color $b$, all colors in $M_i$ lie in the two
in-neighbors or two out-neighbors of $b$ in $T_5$.
Both possible two-element sets have the form $\{a,a+2\}$.
The ten possible nonempty exact color supports therefore comprise
five singletons and five such pairs. A singleton support has weight
one if and only if $G[M_i]$ has no arcs.
For a two-color support, the restricted target has only one
directed arc. Thus any internal vertex with positive in- and outdegree
rules that support out. Otherwise every internally nonisolated source
and sink has a forced color, while each of $z_i$ internally isolated
vertices has either color. Its exact-support count is $2^{z_i}$
if at least one arc is present, or $2^{|M_i|}-2$ if none is present.
For any two module exact supports, all complete cross arcs hold if
and only if every cross product pair satisfies $R$ in the
authenticated quotient direction. Summing products of these
internal weights over the at most $10^q$ disjoint support tuples
counts each original satisfying assignment exactly once.
If $q>5$, selecting one representative per module forms a
$q$-vertex tournament, whose homomorphism to a loopless tournament
must be injective, impossible for $T_5$.

For the complete OBDD, query all vertices module by module, retaining
the at most ten realized supports of each completed module.
For the active module retain the colors observed so far and the
ten-bit mask of full-support templates still compatible with each
read vertex's source-derived unary forced source/sink color.
If the observed set exceeds two colors or is a forbidden pair, reject.
At module completion, require its actual support template to survive,
and check its complete cross product against earlier realized supports.
Then reset the local masks. Before seeing any active-module color
there is one state; after seeing a single color there are at most
$5\cdot 2^3$ candidate mask states; after seeing an allowed pair,
at most five states. Hence there are at most $46$ nonreject
local states and $10^{q-1}$ completed-support tuples.
One rejecting sink per layer consumes all remaining five-way inputs.
The construction is deterministic and thus preserves pointwise
decisions and both original-input assignment counts.
The parser explicitly authenticates every vertex and arc before
building the complement graph and checks every uniform cross join.
Counting uses at most $10^5$ support tuples on the only potentially
satisfiable $q\le5$ instances; the explicit OBDD uses polynomially
many layer nodes, and each exact integer needs $O(n)$ bits.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Feedback-edge exact representation and counting /074]
For every finite explicitly vertex-and-arc enumerated T5 CSP source,
there is a uniform deterministic source-authenticating algorithm
computing the cyclomatic number $f$ of its underlying simple graph,
constructing a complete original-variable five-way OBDD with
\[
 \mathrm{width}\le 5^{2f+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil}+1,\qquad
 V_5\le n\bigl(5^{2f+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil}+1\bigr),
\]
and returning both exact accepted and rejected original-assignment
multiplicities, which sum to $5^n$. The same source has an independent
exact counting-only algorithm taking $O(25^f\operatorname{poly}(L))$
bit time and $\operatorname{poly}(L)$ writable space. For
$f=O(\log L)$ both computations have polynomial bit cost, with a
constant in the polynomial exponent depending on the logarithmic
parameter bound. A pointwise equivalent 3-bit-code Boolean OBDD
requires at most seven binary internal nodes per five-way node.
An auxiliary-free CNF uses $3n$ Boolean variables, exactly
$3n+15m$ listed clauses of width at most six, including harmless
tautological forbidden-pair clauses for loops.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Parse every vertex record and every directed arc; malformed and
truncated sources are INVALID, while loops and opposite arcs
are valid and can be UNSAT. Thus $n,m\le L$.
A source-derived spanning forest has exactly $f$ feedback edges.
Let $S$ comprise their endpoints, of number $g\le 2f$.
Deleting $S$ leaves an induced subforest. Read the vertices of
$S$ first and recursively read the centroids of each component of
the remaining forest, processing each child component contiguously.
Every centroid removal leaves subcomponents of size at most half
the original size, so the recursion depth is at most
$h=\lceil\log_2(n-g+1)\rceil$. At any query prefix, every
previously read vertex adjacent to a future unread vertex belongs
either to $S$ or to the at most $h$ active centroid ancestors:
no completed child component has an edge to a later sibling.
The deterministic layer-dependent live-frontier colors are therefore
at most $g+h$ five-valued coordinates.
At each new query, check every original directed arc whose second
endpoint has just been read, including self-loops; discard a prefix
on failure but continue its remaining original queries along one
reject-completion state per layer. Two nonrejected prefixes with
identical retained colors have exactly the same remaining truth
function because all old constraints have been tested and all
crossing constraints refer only to retained colors. This proves
the pointwise cofactor congruence, width, node and accepted/rejected
multiplicity bounds. The three-bit conversion uses a complete
7-internal-node decoder with codes $5,6,7$ rejecting.
For independent counting, enumerate the at most $5^g$ assignments
to $S$, test arcs internal to $S$, and form all induced unary factors
between pinned and unpinned vertices. All remaining binary factors
lie on a forest, and each 5-color tree dynamic program computes
the exact partition function using integer addition and
multiplication. The product over forest components, summed across
pinned assignments, is exactly the number of satisfying complete
source assignments. Streaming assignments keeps this calculation
in polynomial writable space with $O(n)$-bit integers.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact orientation-robust transfer count /073]

\[
 Z(C_e)=16^e+2q_e,\qquad q_0=2,\quad q_1=7,\quad

\section{Source-derived decomposition and liveness}
Let $S$ consist of original vertices occurring in at least two gate operand positions; repeated operands count twice. Write $k=|S|$ and $M=n+g$. The output pseudo-use is only a liveness convention, not an extra operand occurrence. In an acyclic single-output root-reachable DAG the root is not a shared predecessor.

Set $Q=S\cup\{o\}$. For each target $t\in Q$, expand its original predecessor tree only until a shared predecessor is reached, replacing that occurrence by a cache-reference leaf. The target itself is evaluated, not replaced by a reference. An original shared input has a one-leaf READ component. Let $T_t$ be this local expression tree, $D(t)\subseteq S$ the set of distinct referenced shared identities, and $R$ the total number of REF occurrences over all components. Define the core graph $H$ by $s\to t$ exactly when $s\in D(t)$. Distinct core edges are used for dependency and consumer counting, but every repeated REF occurrence remains an instruction.

\begin{lemma}[Authentic partition]
Every original input has exactly one READ owner, every original gate has exactly one local component owner, and $R\le2g$. The total fresh-input and reference-leaf occurrences are at most $n+2g$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every nonshared vertex has at most one outgoing operand occurrence. Following its unique consumer therefore reaches a unique first target. Distinct components cannot contain the same nonshared vertex. Each shared vertex is evaluated at its own target and otherwise appears only as a reference. Each reference leaf is associated with a genuine original operand occurrence, so there are at most $2g$ such leaves. This is not expansion of a shared DAG into a full formula.
\end{proof}

For each local tree use the Sethi--Ullman recurrence: a leaf has label one, a binary node with labels $a,b$ has $\max(a,b)+[a=b]$, and the larger-labelled child is evaluated first. Restore the original operand order at noncommutative gates. Let $r$ be the largest local root label. The usual leaf induction gives
\[
 r\le 1+\lfloor\log_2(n+2g)\rfloor.
\]
The tree-scheduling recurrence is known prior art, not a new general DAG-allocation theorem \cite{sethi,appel}.

Choose a deterministic topological core order $\sigma$. For a completed prefix $I$ let
\[
 F(I)=\{s\in I:\exists t\notin I,\ s\in D(t)\}
       \cup (\{o\}\cap I),\qquad q=\max_{I\text{ prefix}}|F(I)|.
\]
Thus $q$ counts simultaneously live target values, not all $k$ shared vertices and not all wires. At component completion, decrement each distinct predecessor's remaining component-consumer count. DROP it only when that count becomes zero. STORE the new target value after these drops. Finally OUTPUT and remove the root value. Every REF, STORE and DROP is an actual charged instruction.

\begin{theorem}[Liveness-parameterized all-step compiler]
For every authenticated source and every uniformly constructed topological core order, the emitted program has exactly $n$ READs, $g$ original GATEs, $R$ REFs, $k+1$ STOREs, $k$ DROPs and one OUTPUT. Hence its instruction count is exactly
\[
 I=n+g+R+2k+2.
\]
It evaluates every original gate once, reads every original input once, and preserves every original intermediate gate value and both output labels. A conservative logical Boolean cache-plus-stack bound is
\[
 W=q+r+1.
\]
There is a fully constructed complete labelled OBDD $B(C)$ with
\[
 V(B(C))\le n2^W,\qquad B(C)(x)=C(x),\qquad \#B(C)=\#C.
\]
For every field $K$ and each prefix of the discovered input order $\rho$,
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_K M_{\rho_{\le j}:\rho_{>j}}(C)
 \le \#\{\text{reachable states at boundary }j\}\le 2^W.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initially both stack and cache are empty. Before component $t$, every $s\in D(t)$ has already been computed and still has unfinished consumer $t$, hence is present. Each READ introduces its original input bit; each REF copies a present authentic value; each original gate applies its actual truth table. Structural induction on the local tree and then the component order preserves all original source values. After a local tree finishes, its result is on the stack. All REF occurrences of that component have finished before any DROP. A value is removed only if no later component references it, and STORE transfers the result into its designated cache slot. This establishes the liveness invariant $F(I)$ and both terminal outcomes.

The local stack uses at most $r$ bits while the old cache has at most $q$ values. Drops cannot increase storage; a store transfers one stack result. The additional unit in $W$ is conservative staging slack. Actual copied REF values in the scratch stack are counted in addition to their one original cached identity. Public addresses, counters and source storage are charged separately below. The partition lemma gives the exact original-operation counts; every shared target is dropped once, and only the root is consumed by OUTPUT.

Split the public instruction list at its $n$ READs. At a fixed program counter, the cache key set and stack depth are known from the compiled program, independently of assignment values. Therefore an inter-READ state is precisely its live Boolean tuple, not a tuple of all allocated or expired shared slots. From the real initial tuple, breadth-first construction executes the actual intervening instructions on each hypothetical next input bit. Equal successor tuples may merge, but both 0- and 1-labelled arcs remain even if their destinations coincide. There are at most $2^W$ tuples per input layer.

Induction on all instructions proves pointwise source/target equivalence. Because the program reads each original input exactly once in a fixed discovered order, complete labelled paths are in bijection with original assignments. Exact integer propagation through both arcs preserves their multiplicities, proving the count. After a prefix the tuple determines the complete suffix function, so the assignment matrix has at most $2^W$ distinct rows. This proves the rank bound over every field, without equating rank with necessary unrestricted work.
\end{proof}

The compiler uses a source-derived topological fallback on a general core DAG; it does not claim an optimal order or small $q$ there. Original input labels and all $n$ variables are retained, with no auxiliary-variable growth. The serialized OBDD has exactly two labelled arcs per internal node. Physical input seeks in the discovered order are not free.

\section{A polynomial class with linearly many shared vertices}
\begin{theorem}[Recognizable binary in-tree core]
Suppose the constructed core $H$ has at most two distinct predecessors per target and exactly one distinct consumer per shared target, with the root having none. These conditions are checked on the authenticated source. Then a uniform larger-label-first core postorder satisfies
\[
 q\le1+\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor,
\]
and therefore
\[
 W\le3+\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor+
             \lfloor\log_2(n+2g)\rfloor,
 \qquad V(B(C))\le8n(k+1)(n+2g).
\]
The complete construction and exact count have polynomial time and polynomial peak space in source length, without a supplied schedule, even for $k=\Theta(M)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root reachability and acyclicity imply that the checked core is a rooted in-tree, allowing unary vertices. Give a core leaf label one, a unary target its child's label, and a binary target the usual Sethi--Ullman label. Execute the larger-labelled child subtree first. Completed results of that subtree are needed only by their unique parent, so after the parent component has consumed them they can be dropped. Induction shows that the maximum completed-component frontier is the root label: with two child labels $a\ge b$ its peak is $\max(a,1+b)$, and with one child it is unchanged. A label-$h$ binary tree has at least $2^{h-1}$ leaves, which are among the $k+1$ targets. Combine this bound with the local scratch bound and the previous theorem. All recognition, labels, ordering and last-consumer counters are computed from original records in polynomial preprocessing time. The same-realization ledger below makes the full polynomial claim explicit.
\end{proof}

This strictly enlarges the earlier certified domain when combined with the general fallback and the old small-$k$ test. Bounded $k=O(\log L)$ remains sufficient even when $H$ is not a tree, since $q\le k+1$. Many-share tree cores add a new recognized family. No efficient optimizer for arbitrary reconvergent cores has been proved.

\paragraph{Infinite witness family and honest compact control.}
Take any binary tree with $n\ge2$ original input leaves. Replace each internal operation on $a,b$ by the original three-gate module

 p=a\land b,\quad z=a\oplus b,\quad t=p\lor z.

Each child result occurs in two original operand positions. Thus $g=3(n-1)$, $k=2n-2$, and $H$ is the original binary tree; each local module has scratch label three. internal $T_5$ onto $T_5$ is injective and hence an automorphism.

the five translations $a\mapsto a+t_i$.

potentials if and only if every signed fundamental-cycle sum is zero,
leaving precisely five choices for the global translation.
The accepted original assignments are five complete words of length $n$.

each accepted word and a rejecting state, continuing to query every
remaining variable even after rejection. This yields width at most

\section*{TMR-4/083: Known NP-completeness of the five-symbol target; exact palette-backdoor compiler}

\paragraph{Scope and literature correction.}
The sole binary target is the loopless tournament
$T_5=(\mathbb Z_5,R)$, where $R(a,b)$ holds exactly if
$b-a\bmod 5\in\{1,3\}$.  The fixed-template problem
$\operatorname{CSP}(T_5)$ is, in fact, \emph{already known to be
NP-complete}. This is a consequence of Barto, Kozik and Niven's
2009 theorem on digraphs without sources or sinks
\cite{bartokozikniven2009}, independently stated as Theorem 15
in Larose's survey \cite{larose2017}. It was not proved by
the finite experiments below and must not be misrepresented
as an original NP-completeness proof or as a proof that
$\mathrm P\ne\mathrm{NP}$.

Here is an exact verification of the theorem's hypotheses.
The five-symbol relation has ten arcs, with indegree and
outdegree two at every vertex, so the target is smooth.
Every two distinct vertices of the source tournament
are joined by one directed arc. A self-homomorphism
must therefore be injective, since mapping the arc to
a single vertex would require a loop. Thus every
self-homomorphism is bijective and $T_5$ is its own core.
The core cannot be a directed cycle, since its outdegree
is two rather than one. The smooth-core dichotomy therefore
implies NP-completeness of this \emph{exact} directed graph
homomorphism problem. Exhaustive inspection of the $5^5=3125$
self-maps gives exactly five endomorphisms, all cyclic
translations; this finite inspection is corroboration,
not the hardness proof.

A polynomial-time preprocessing filter proves that the
NP-completeness still holds when the source input must be
loopless and have no opposing arc pair. Any graph with a
loop or opposing arcs is necessarily UNSAT for this loopless
tournament, so replace such a graph with a fixed six-vertex
oriented tournament, also UNSAT because six mutually adjacent
vertices cannot map injectively to five. Other graphs
are left unchanged. This maintains YES/NO for an ordinary
many-one reduction. It does not show NP-hardness on the
additional degree, density, connectivity or prime subfamilies.

\paragraph{Candidate D4-T5-PALETTE-BACKDOOR083.}
Find a source-induced five-vertex tournament isomorphic to
$T_5$, called a palette $P$. Its homomorphisms are precisely
five rotations. If an outside vertex is adjacent to exactly
four palette vertices, its image must be the color of the
unique missing palette vertex: all other colors would map
one source arc to a forbidden loop. Checking its four arc
orientations may instead reject the instance. A vertex
adjacent to all five palette colors is likewise impossible.
Let $Q$ be the remaining outside vertices with at most
three palette adjacencies, and let $q=|Q|$.

Every satisfying coloring is determined by a common
translation, the $q$ unpinned values, and the uniquely
forced pinned values. Each literal source arc is then
checked, with no uncharged propagation. Consequently
\[
 Z(G)=5\sum_{z\in \mathbb Z_5^{Q}}
 [\,\text{all source arcs satisfied under the normalized
 palette and forced pins}\,].
\]
Searching all five-element palette candidates costs at most
$O(n^5\operatorname{poly}(L))$ bit operations, for full
canonical byte length $L$; residual exact counting costs
$O(5^q\operatorname{poly}(L))$. There is no external
palette oracle, hidden fixed order, or omitted integer
bit cost. The construction is a restricted parameterized
algorithm, polynomial when $q=O(\log L)$; arbitrary

Query the five palette variables first, then the $q$
exceptional variables, then the pinned variables. Maintain

original values of the exceptional variables seen so far.

 \operatorname{width}\le5^{q+1}+1,\qquad

\section{T5/084: tight block-respecting Boolean transfer and domain-mask commutation}
\label{sec:t5block084}

\paragraph{Precise candidate D4-T5-BLOCK-REVERSE-084.}
Let $G$ be authenticated from its complete canonically enumerated
source bytes, with $n$ original variables taking values in
$\mathbb Z_5$, and $R(a,b)$ holding when
$(b-a)\bmod5\in\{1,3\}$. A complete $5$-way adaptive read-once
branching program (FBDD) queries each of the $n$ original
variables exactly once on every root--terminal path; the next
variable may depend on earlier answers. A $3$-bit Boolean FBDD
is \emph{full-decoder block respecting} if a queried variable's
three code bits are read contiguously on every valid-code path,
each original variable occurs in exactly one such block,
and any invalid code rejects. The Boolean bit order within a
block may depend on values already read. These requirements
are stronger than being an arbitrary Boolean FBDD.

\begin{theorem}[Quantified two-way block transfer /084]
Let $S_5(G)$ be the minimum number of nonterminal nodes of a
complete $5$-way FBDD for the original $T_5$ predicate and
let $S_{\rm blk}(G)$ be the corresponding minimum in the
full-decoder block-respecting Boolean model for the direct
three-bit code $000,001,010,011,100$. Then
\[
 S_5(G)\le S_{\rm blk}(G)\le 6S_5(G).
\]
The Boolean predicate rejects invalid codes $101,110,111$.
Both directions preserve all $5^n$ original-input terminal
decisions and their exact accepting and rejecting multiplicities.
If $Z(G)$ is the native accepted count, the Boolean predicate
accepts exactly $Z(G)$ of the $8^n$ code words and rejects
exactly $8^n-Z(G)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Replace each original $5$-way query node with the following
six-node $3$-bit decision tree. Query the most significant bit,
then the middle bit. Under prefix $00$ query the final bit to
choose original ports $0$ or $1$; under $01$ choose ports $2$
or $3$; under $10$ choose port $4$ or the rejecting terminal;
under $11$ reject without asking the final bit. The original
FBDD is complete, so every potentially accepting code path
validates all $n$ code blocks, even when an original source
constraint was already decided by an earlier assignment.
The replacement creates six internal bit nodes per original
node; along valid-code paths each block reproduces exactly one
native transition. Invalid codes reject, proving the forward
size and multiplicity statements.

In the reverse direction, at each recognizable Boolean
phase-root evaluate the three-bit subdiagram on the five valid
words and replace the whole block by one five-way query
on the same source variable with these five continuations.
All invalid words reject by hypothesis. The block property
ensures that none of the five continuations leaves its
source variable's block prematurely. Any adaptive ordering
of the blocks remains read-once and complete. Charge one
native node per Boolean phase-root, at most the original number
of Boolean internal nodes. Original assignments and their
terminal decisions are pointwise unchanged.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Six is tight for an independent decoder interface,
not for global FBDDs.}
If five original continuation ports denote five pairwise
different residual predicates and invalid codewords must
reject, a source-independent three-bit \emph{decision tree}
requires at least seven leaves: five distinct valid leaves
plus at least two invalid leaves, since three invalid codes
cannot form one Boolean subcube. Thus at least six binary
internal nodes are necessary; the construction attains six.
The authentic source consisting of one directed arc $x\to y$
gives five distinct residual suffix predicates after reading
$x$, so the distinct-port hypothesis is source-realizable.
This local bound must not be promoted to a global minimum
for arbitrary interleaved FBDDs or deterministic machines.

\section{T5/085: Cost-sound live-open-symbol transfer for arbitrarily interleaved bit FBDDs}
\label{sec:t5live085}

\paragraph{Exact model.}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be authenticated original directed source bytes
with literal vertex identifiers $0,\ldots,n-1$ (so $L\ge n$),
$|V|=n$, and $f_G:[5]^n\to\{0,1\}$ its exact beat-relation predicate.
A full three-bit Boolean word is valid only when each three-bit block
encodes one of the five symbols $0,\ldots,4$; all other code words reject.
Let $B$ be an explicit, validity-sound, acyclic deterministic read-once
\emph{bit} FBDD on $3n$ bits, with $s$ reachable nonterminal nodes.
No ordering or block-respecting assumption is placed on $B$.
For a bit node $v$, let $\mathrm{Past}(v)$ be the union of source-symbol
identities with any bit previously queried on some root-to-$v$ path.
Let $\mathrm{Future}(v)$ be the source-symbol identities with a
bit tested at $v$ or any descendant node, and set
\[
 p(B)=\max_v|\mathrm{Past}(v)\cap\mathrm{Future}(v)|.
\]
This is a \emph{potential} live-overlap parameter computable by forward
and backward bitset propagation on the explicit DAG.

\begin{theorem}[Parameterized exact reverse simulation /085]
There exists a five-valued deterministic at-most-once FBDD $A$
with at most $s6^{p(B)}$ nonterminal nodes such that
\[
 \forall x\in[5]^n,\quad A(x)=B(\mathrm{code}(x))=f_G(x).
\]
In particular, the accepted and rejected original \emph{full assignment}
multiplicities are exactly $Z(G)$ and $5^n-Z(G)$. An explicit
transduction from a verified bit FBDD runs in
$O(s6^{p(B)}\operatorname{poly}(n+\log s))$ bit time and peak writable
space, beyond the cost of authenticating $G$ and producing/verifying $B$.
No polynomial-time general source-to-$B$ construction is implied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each bit node $v$, memoize the pair $(v,M)$ where $M$ records
the exact five-valued colors of previously queried source symbols
which still belong to $\mathrm{Future}(v)$. If the current bit belongs
to a symbol in $M$, follow the dictated bit edge without a new source
query. Otherwise, query that original five-valued variable once,
branch to all five colors, and cache its color only as long as some
remaining bit of it can occur in the suffix. The DAG invariant and the
bit-level read-once condition ensure no source variable is queried
twice along a transduced path. Induction on the remaining bit DAG
proves pointwise equality. Each cache domain is a subset of
$\mathrm{Past}(v)\cap\mathrm{Future}(v)$, with six options (unset or five
colors) per potential entry, yielding at most $6^{p(B)}$ states per
bit node. State merging, cache keys, discarded entries, and five-way
fan-out are explicit, never free.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Assignment multiplicities and completeness caveat.}
The constructed FBDD is \emph{at-most-once}, not necessarily complete.
If an accepting path queries $d$ distinct original variables, it
represents $5^{n-d}$ full inputs, and distinct root-to-leaf paths
represent disjoint cylinders. Hence the exact count is
\[
 Z(G)=\sum_{\pi:\,\mathrm{accept}}5^{n-d(\pi)}.
\]
A depth-indexed dynamic program on the DAG computes this quantity using
exact integers; the rejected count is $5^n-Z(G)$. Interpreting the
number of accepting terminal paths as $Z(G)$ would be incorrect.
Requiring \emph{complete} query-once paths, as in the earlier /084
submodel, may require explicitly tracking the set of queried original
variables and incurs additional potentially exponential state cost.
The stated bound applies only to at-most-once FBDDs.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Then the unqueried suffix predicate is
$\bigwedge_{i\in S}(\neg m_i\land\neg\ell_i)$, since $100$ is the
only valid code with high bit one. Different subsets yield different

the accepting sink; all others require distinct nonterminal

\begin{theorem}[Exact cycle-gauge transfer /086]
Choose any spanning forest of the underlying undirected multigraph
retaining each original directed arc as a distinct edge. Let

\[
 Z_{T_5}(G)=5^c

\]

\begin{proof}

\section{TMR-4/094: Exact decision certificates from original $T_5$ source bits}

The actual source decision function for an original $n$-vertex loopless
directed graph is
\[

\bigwedge_{i\ne j}(\neg e_{ij}\lor
((x_j-x_i)\bmod5\in\{1,3\}))\right].

The full source has $M=n(n-1)$ independently addressable arc bits,
including both directions for each vertex pair. A correct deterministic
\emph{bit-probe source decider} must answer correctly on every one of the
$2^M$ input matrices, not just on the all-zero graph. Its restricted
per-instance query count on the empty source is
$C_{\mathrm{SAT}}(0^M)$. Computation, source parsing and hashing are
accounted separately and are never assumed free.
\begin{theorem}[TMR-4/094A: tight full-source bit certificate]
For all $n\ge1$,
\[
\boxed{C_{\mathrm{SAT}}(0^M)=n(n-1)-
\left\lfloor\frac{2n^2}{5}\right\rfloor.}
\]
The conclusion is an exact source-bit query lower bound, not an
unrestricted Turing-machine time lower bound.

\begin{proof}

transcript and let $H$ contain \emph{all} unqueried possible arcs.

the loopless target tournament permits exactly one orientation
between each pair of differently colored vertices and no arc between
equal colors. Therefore
\[
|E(H)|\le \sum_{a<b}n_an_b
=\frac{n^2-\sum_{a=0}^4n_a^2}{2}
\le\left\lfloor\frac{2n^2}{5}\right\rfloor.
\]

query precisely its forbidden arc positions, and return YES with $h$
when every queried bit is zero; all unread completions preserve this
witness. On a nonzero queried bit, read the remaining source and use
exhaustive original five-color search. This is a single uniform
all-input correct deterministic decision algorithm, with the exact
claimed query count on the zero source.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Unique successor triangle]
Let $\Sigma=\mathbb Z_5$ and
$R(a,b)\iff b-a\bmod5\in\{1,3\}$. Put
\[

\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both differences $v-a,z-a$ are in $\{1,3\}$.
If equal, $R(z,v)$ is a forbidden loop.

\section{Randomized source-probe cost and encoding firewall for $T_5$ (TMR-4/096)}

\paragraph{Source model and a genuinely all-algorithm lower bound.}
Let $R(a,b)$ mean $(b-a)\bmod5\in\{1,3\}$. For $n\ge2$
encode directed graphs by the $M=n(n-1)$ independently addressable
ordered-arc bits, charging one unit for each bit read. Let $E_n$
be the empty digraph and, for each unordered pair $p=\{i,j\}$,
let $B_p$ have exactly the reciprocal arcs $i\to j$ and $j\to i$.
Because $R$ is an asymmetric loopless tournament,
$Z(E_n)=5^n$ whereas $Z(B_p)=0$.

\begin{theorem}[Two-sided-error randomized bit-probe barrier]
Every possibly nonuniform randomized adaptive algorithm deciding
the original $T_5$-CSP on all $n$-vertex ordered-arc matrices with
per-input error at most $\varepsilon<1/2$, even with unbounded
computation between probes, satisfies
\[
 \mathbb E Q(E_n)\ge(1-2\varepsilon)\binom n2.
\]
For $\varepsilon=1/3$, the bound is $n(n-1)/6$ source-bit reads.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Couple runs on $E_n$ and $B_p$ using the same random tape $r$.
Until the empty-run examines either bit belonging to $p$, the
two executions and their outputs agree. Let $S_r$ be the set of
unordered pairs touched by the empty-run and $a_G(r)$ its
acceptance indicator on graph $G$. For each $p$,
$a_{E_n}(r)-a_{B_p}(r)\le[p\in S_r]$.

\paragraph{Original-source factorization (PROVED/EXACT, restricted).}
Let $G$ be an authenticated original $T_5$ source with a supplied and
source-verified interface $Z$ of $q$ shared vertices. After fixing
$z\in\mathbb Z_5^q$, suppose every other vertex is in exactly one local
private set $Y_{rb}$, and all original arcs lie within the scope of
some block $F_{rb}(Z,Y_{rb})$. The partition and cross-block-arc
exclusion are checked from encoded $G$, with parsing and discovery charged.
Define $N_{rb}(z)$ to be the number of original local assignments
satisfying that block. By independence of distinct private sets,
\[
 \boxed{\#T_5(G)=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z_5^q}
                 \prod_{r=1}^{R}\prod_{b=1}^{B_r}N_{rb}(z).}
\]
This identity counts all original accepted paths. Rejected original
assignments are retained, and a Boolean summary is
$h_{rb}(z)=[N_{rb}(z)>0]$. Therefore
\[
 G\in\mathrm{SAT}\iff\exists z\ \bigwedge_{r,b}h_{rb}(z),\quad
 G\in\mathrm{UNSAT}\iff\forall z\ \exists(r,b)\ [N_{rb}(z)=0].
\]
The latter does \emph{not} imply $\exists(r,b)\forall z$.
For a concrete counterexample, the two rows
\[
 (u\to v)\wedge(u\to a),\qquad
 (v\to u)\wedge(v\to b)
\]
share $u,v$ and have disjoint private vertices $a,b$. Each row separately
has ten feasible shared assignments, each with two private extensions,
yet the conjunction is UNSAT since $T_5$ has no opposing arc pair.
Among 25 shared assignments, exactly ten satisfy only the first row,
ten only the second, and five neither. Removing $v\to u$ yields a
SAT control with exactly 40 satisfying full assignments on four
original vertices. Independent Python and Node.js tests reproduce both
counts, original-source SHA-256 values and row histograms.

\paragraph{Three-valued sound specialist and wrong-path guard.}
A deterministic per-$z$ specialist emits
$\mathrm{REFUTED}(z,r,b,\pi)$ only after validating $\pi$ as a sound
refutation of the actual residual block, or
$\mathrm{EXTENSION}(z,y)$ only after checking all local witnesses and
every original arc. Otherwise it emits UNKNOWN. An untrusted suggested
index is not an answer. A deterministic complete SAT decision seed can
give total fixed-$z$ Boolean answers, but its UNSAT bit is not a short
independently checkable negative certificate. Standard search
self-reduction from a decision-only seed may require a further
$O(s)$ child calls per block. Deterministic complete global decision
must account for all $5^q$ interface assignments unless a sound
symbolic compression is actually constructed.

\paragraph{Exact resource-envelope firewall (CONDITIONAL).}

 T_z\le T_{\mathrm{parse}}+T_{\mathrm{discover}}

\]

\section{Exact-one T5: private-follower component quotient and global-row barrier}
\label{sec:t5exactone099}
Fix the loopless tournament $R(a,b)$ iff $b-a\pmod5\in\{1,3\}$,
and $Q(h,u,v)=R(h,u)\oplus R(h,v)$.
All three original variable names in a clause are distinct; repeated assigned
letters are allowed, and a non-win includes a tie. All statements are
about an authenticated explicit list of original ternary clauses.

\paragraph{PROVED/EXACT: component-fiber quotient.}
For every head variable $h$, form an undirected follower graph $H_h$ with
edge $\{u,v\}$ for each clause $Q(h,u,v)$. An odd cycle in $H_h$
proves UNSAT. Otherwise choose a bipartition $(L_C,U_C)$ for each
nontrivial connected component $C$ of every $H_h$.
Given a protected shared interface $Z$, retain the variable set
$S$ consisting of $Z$, all head variables, and each follower occurring
under at least two distinct heads. Every other active follower is
private to one component. Let $i$ be the number of unprotected isolated
original variables. For a retained assignment $\alpha\in\mathbb Z_5^S$,
write $[P]$ for the indicator of predicate $P$. Define
\[
\begin{split}
F_C(\alpha)=\sum_{e=0}^1&
\prod_{v\in L_C\cap S}[R(\alpha(h),\alpha(v))=e]
\prod_{v\in U_C\cap S}[R(\alpha(h),\alpha(v))=1-e]\\[-2pt]
&\quad\cdot(2^e3^{1-e})^{|L_C\setminus S|}
(2^{1-e}3^e)^{|U_C\setminus S|}.
\end{split}
\]
Here $R(\alpha(h),\alpha(v))$ denotes its zero-one truth value.
Then the exact number of satisfying original extensions of $\alpha$ is
\[
A(\alpha)=5^i\prod_C F_C(\alpha).
\]
Exactly $5^{n-|S|}-A(\alpha)$ extensions reject. The total accepted
count is $\sum_{\alpha}A(\alpha)$; the total rejected count is
$5^n-\sum_\alpha A(\alpha)$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Every follower edge requires opposite Boolean win-bits relative to $h$.
A connected bipartite component has only the two global orientations of
these bits. For fixed orientation and head letter, an eliminated follower
has independently exactly two winning or three nonwinning choices.
Retained followers impose the displayed indicators. The orientations
have disjoint accepted full-letter extensions because the component
has an edge. By the retention rule, eliminated followers belong to
only one factor; multiplication therefore counts full original
extensions exactly once. Each isolated unprotected variable contributes
five choices. Complementary rejecting counts follow by partitioning
the original $5^{n-|S|}$ extensions. The quotient does not claim to
retain pointwise identities of eliminated assignments in a
polynomial-width decision diagram.

\paragraph{Exact row join and global shift orbit.}
For private-disjoint rows sharing $z$, evaluate the above factors
with protected interface $z$ and sum any retained nonshared letters to
obtain $A_r(z)$. Then
$\#F=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z_5^{|z|}}\prod_r A_r(z)$.
This is an exact evaluator, not a free materialization of exponentially
many shared states. Because simultaneous translation of all letters
by $t\bmod 5$ preserves $Q$, if $S$ is nonempty the total count may
be computed by fixing one retained symbol to zero and multiplying by
five. This saves a constant factor only; with a fixed external $z$ no
unilateral shift is permitted.
