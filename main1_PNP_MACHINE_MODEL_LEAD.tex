\begin{theorem}[C1-T5-ORIENTED-CACTUS-BLOCK-COUNT081]
Let $G$ have no loop or opposed arc pair, and suppose the
underlying undirected graph is a cactus. Let $c$ be its
weak component count, $b$ its bridge count and let
each cycle block $j$ contain $p_j$ agreeing and $q_j$
opposing arcs relative to one cyclic traversal. Define
\[
 Q(p,q)=\sum_{i=0}^{p}\sum_{j=0}^{q}
 \binom pi\binom qj
 {\mathbf 1}\{p-q+2(i-j)\equiv0\pmod5\}.
\]
Then
\[
 \#\mathrm{Hom}(G,T_5)=5^c2^b\prod_jQ(p_j,q_j).
\]
Each $Q(p,q)>0$ whenever $p+q\ge3$.
In particular every orientation of every simple cactus
is satisfiable, with a polynomial-time exact counter
and witness extractor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An agreeing cycle edge changes a label by $1$ or $3$,
and an opposing edge by $-1$ or $-3$ modulo $5$.
Selecting the absolute increment $3$ on $i$ agreeing
and $j$ opposing edges gives displacement
$p-q+2(i-j)$ with the binomial multiplicity above.
Thus $Q$ counts extensions when the cycle's initial
vertex has any prescribed label. The integer $i-j$
ranges over every value in $[-q,p]$. When $p+q\ge4$,
this interval has at least five consecutive residues,
so zero displacement occurs. For $p+q=3$, the
cases $p=3,2,1,0$ admit respectively
$i-j=1,2,-2,-1$. Hence all factors are positive.
Cactus blocks meet their predecessors in at most
one articulation vertex. The translation automorphism
$x\mapsto x+k\bmod5$ shows that the number of
extensions does not depend on the attachment color.
An oriented bridge contributes two choices and
a cycle $Q(p,q)$. Choose five root labels per
component and multiply the block factors.
Dynamic programming on five residues backtracks
a satisfying assignment.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[C1-T5-C5-BIJECTIVE-EDGE: parsimonious four-arc reduction, /072]
Let $C_5$ be the undirected loopless five-cycle with adjacency
$b-a\bmod5\in\{1,4\}$. For every canonical simple undirected input
$H$ with $n$ vertices and $m$ edges, one source-authenticated uniform
compiler produces a directed $T_5$ instance $D^*(H)$ on exactly
$n+2m$ vertices and $4m$ arcs, with an explicit bijection between
the original $C_5$ colorings and the complete $T_5$ labelings:
\[
 \boxed{\#\operatorname{Hom}(D^*(H),T_5)
 =\#\operatorname{Hom}(H,C_5).}
\]
The reduction preserves both labels, is \emph{parsimonious}, and has
serialized binary output length $L_D\le8L_H$ in the grammar above.
Its unique extension map is translation-equivariant over $\mathbb Z_5$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each source edge $e=\{u,v\}$, $u<v$, create fresh vertices
$z_e=n+2e$ and $w_e=n+2e+1$, and append the four arcs
\[
  u\to z_e,\qquad u\to w_e,\qquad w_e\to z_e,
  \qquad w_e\to v.
\]
The first three form the TMR-2/068 unique-successor triangle.
Indeed the first arc requires $z_e-u\in S=\{1,3\}$,
whereas the path $u\to w_e\to z_e$ requires
$z_e-u\in S+S=\{1,2,4\}$ modulo $5$.
Their intersection is $\{1\}$ and its sole two-step summand
is $3+3=1\bmod5$. Hence those three arcs hold if and only if
\[
 z_e=u+1\bmod5,\qquad w_e=u+3\bmod5,
\]
with exactly one internal assignment. The final arc then demands
$v-u\in3+S=\{4,1\}$, which is exactly the $C_5$ edge condition.
Thus the edge-gadget completion vector, for endpoint differences
$d=0,1,2,3,4$, is $(0,1,0,0,1)$.
For every $H$ coloring, assigning the two forced labels on
each edge yields the unique $D^*(H)$ labeling; conversely every
target labeling restricts to a proper source coloring and forces
all edge auxiliaries. All auxiliary names are disjoint, and
isolates and the empty graph retain their correct counts.
The maps are mutually inverse, also under simultaneous translations
of all five-valued labels.

The new vertices and arcs are lexicographically sorted and
serialized using $w_D=w(n+2m)$. The exact target length is
$L_D=n+6m+2+8mw_D$. Since $m\le n(n-1)/2$ for $n\ge2$,
$n+2m\le n^2$ and $w_D\le2w(n)$.
Therefore $L_D\le8L_H$; $n\le1$ is immediate.
A fixed conventional multitape machine authenticates all source
bits, creates records, sorts and outputs the exact target encoding
in polynomial bit time and space.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[C1-T5-DAG-SOURCE-HARDNESS: parsimonious NP-hardness on directed acyclic inputs, /078]
For every canonically encoded finite simple undirected graph $H$,
the same exact four-arc $C_5\to T_5$ compiler above produces a
\emph{directed acyclic graph} $D^*(H)$. Its complete
$T_5$ labelings are in bijection with all $C_5$ colorings
of $H$. Consequently, even restricted to valid
directed acyclic input graphs, deciding
$\operatorname{Hom}(-,T_5)$ is NP-complete.
For $n\ge1$ original vertices, the target admits an explicit
strict topological rank with maximum at most $3n-1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each undirected source edge is canonically oriented $u<v$ for
construction purposes, with private vertices
$w_e=n+2e$ and $z_e=n+2e+1$. Define the rank
$\phi(i)=3i$ for original vertices and
$\phi(w_e)=3u+1$, $\phi(z_e)=3u+2$.
On the four arcs
$u\to z_e,\ u\to w_e,\ w_e\to z_e,\ w_e\to v$,
the ranks strictly increase: the only nontrivial comparison
is $3u+1<3v$, using $u<v$.
Thus no directed cycle can occur, irrespective of the
undirected cycles of $H$. For $n=0$ the target is empty
and acyclic.

The preceding four-arc theorem already proves that
$u\to w_e\to z_e$ together with $u\to z_e$
uniquely forces $(w_e,z_e)=(x_u+3,x_u+1)$
modulo five, and that $w_e\to v$ then permits
$x_v-x_u\in\{1,4\}$ modulo five. Its extension/restriction
is therefore a bijection, with exactly
$n+2m$ target vertices, $4m$ arcs and
$L_D\le8L_H$ complete encoded bits.
The Hell--Ne\v{s}et\v{r}il graph-homomorphism
dichotomy makes undirected $C_5$-coloring NP-complete.
The demonstrated uniform many-one reduction targets
only directed DAGs; polynomial witness checking and
a paid topological sort establish NP membership.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[C1-T5-BIPARTITE-DAG-TRIADFREE-HARDNESS: simultaneous parsimonious restriction, /080]
For every canonically encoded finite loopless simple undirected graph
$H$ with $n$ vertices and $m$ edges there is a fully charged
deterministic compiler to a directed graph $D_{\mathrm{bt}}(H)$ with
exactly $n+3m$ vertices and $5m$ arcs.  The underlying undirected
graph of the output is bipartite and hence triangle-free, and
the output orientation is acyclic.  The complete five-symbol
witnesses are in a bijection with undirected $C_5$ colorings:
\[
 \boxed{\#\operatorname{Hom}(D_{\mathrm{bt}}(H),T_5)
  =\#\operatorname{Hom}(H,C_5).}
\]
Consequently $\operatorname{CSP}(T_5)$ restricted to inputs that are
simultaneously directed acyclic, underlying-bipartite and triangle-free
is NP-complete.  No input of this promised form contains a
transitive-triangle seed for the /075 forced-triad contraction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $S=\{1,3\}\subseteq\mathbb Z_5$.  For each original edge
$e=\{u,v\}$, $u<v$, create private $a_e,b_e,c_e$ and append
\[
 u\to a_e,\quad a_e\to b_e,\quad b_e\to c_e,\quad
 u\to c_e,\quad c_e\to v.
\]
The four arcs ending at $c_e$ require both
$c_e-u\in S$ and $c_e-u\in S+S+S$.  The three-step sum
has support $\{0,2,3,4\}$ with multiplicities $(3,3,1,1)$
on those residues, respectively.  Thus
$S\cap (S+S+S)=\{3\}$, and the only summand triple
producing residue $3$ is $(1,1,1)$.  The unique possible
labels are
\[
 a_e=x_u+1,\quad b_e=x_u+2,\quad c_e=x_u+3
 \pmod 5.
\]
The remaining arc $c_e\to v$ holds if and only if
$x_v-x_u\in3+S=\{4,1\}$, exactly $C_5$ adjacency.
All internal vertices are private, so the forced assignments
give a global witness-level bijection, even for isolates and
the empty source.  The local endpoint extension vector in
difference order $0,1,2,3,4$ is $(0,1,0,0,1)$.

Set $\phi(i)=4i$ on original vertices and
$\phi(a_e)=4u+1,\phi(b_e)=4u+2,\phi(c_e)=4u+3$.
Every constructed arc strictly increases $\phi$, since
$u<v$ for the terminal arc.  Hence the source digraph
is acyclic.  The partition consisting of all original vertices
and all $b_e$, versus all $a_e,c_e$, is a bipartition:
each arc crosses it.  Thus no undirected triangle occurs
and every triangle-driven quotient operation is vacuous
on this particular image.

The original source encoding length is
$L_H=n+m+2+2m w(n)$, where
$w(n)=\max(1,\lceil\log_2\max(2,n)\rceil)$.
The output length is exactly
$L_D=n+8m+2+10m w(n+3m)$.
Since $m\le n(n-1)/2$ and $n+3m\le2^{3w(n)}$ for
$n\ge2$, while $n\le1$ is immediate, the
output width is at most $3w(n)$ and
$L_D\le15L_H$.  A sequential source-authenticated
compiler charges unary/binary parsing, unique variable
allocation, sorting, all addresses, buffers, full output and
witness-verification access.  It achieves simultaneous
conservative deterministic
$T=O((L_H+1)^4)$ time and
$S_{\mathrm{peak}}=O((L_H+1)^2)$ writable bits.
Malformed source words are mapped to the fixed NO
instance $D_{\mathrm{bt}}(K_3)$, which remains a bipartite
DAG and is unsatisfiable since $C_5$ contains no
triangle.  Three-bit witness codes $5,6,7$ are rejected.

The original Hell--Ne\v{s}et\v{r}il theorem makes
undirected $C_5$ coloring NP-complete; the demonstrated
polynomial-time exact reduction proves NP-hardness under
all simultaneous source restrictions.  Direct finite-witness
checking proves NP membership.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Independent classification and template structure.}
The Hell--Ne\v{s}et\v{r}il (1990) undirected $H$-colouring
dichotomy makes $\operatorname{Hom}(-,C_5)$ NP-complete
because $C_5$ is loopless and non-bipartite.
The proved bijective four-arc reduction and direct polynomial
certificate checking therefore classify the \emph{exact}
$T_5$ problem as NP-complete.
Independently, Bang--Jensen, Hell and MacGillivray (1988)
classify any semicomplete target with two directed cycles
as NP-complete. Here the triangles are
$0\to1\to2\to0$ and $0\to3\to4\to0$.
Any endomorphism $T_5\to T_5$ is injective because
identifying two tournament vertices would send an arc to a loop.
Its five automorphisms are precisely cyclic translations:
after fixing vertex $0$, the two outneighbors and two
inneighbors are distinguished by their internal arc.
This proves the core property, but not a full
higher-arity polymorphism classification.

\begin{lemma}[C1-T5-PARSIMONIOUS-CNF: reverse Boolean compiler]
Every canonical directed $T_5$ source with $n$ vertices and $m$
arcs, including loops and isolates, has an exact-count-preserving
width-at-most-three Boolean CNF with precisely
$q=8n$ declared Boolean variables and
$c=20n+15m$ raw clauses:
\[
 \boxed{\#\operatorname{SAT}(F_G)
        =\#\operatorname{Hom}(G,T_5).}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Create one-hot bits $X_{i,a}$, $a=0,\ldots,4$,
and three successive functional OR-prefix bits $Y_{i,j}$
for each source vertex $i$.
Ten binary clauses forbid two simultaneously true one-hot bits.
Each of three successive OR gates is defined by its three
standard Boolean equivalence clauses, followed by the final
unit-in-the-OR-chain clause $(Y_{i,3}\lor X_{i,4})$.
These are $10+9+1=20$ clauses per vertex and force
exactly one label, with one uniquely determined auxiliary extension.
For each source arc $(i,j)$ emit all 15 binary clauses
$(\neg X_{i,a}\lor\neg X_{j,b})$ for disallowed $(a,b)$.
The 25 ordered pairs have exactly ten allowed ordered pairs.
Hence satisfaction agrees pointwise with $R$ and
the unique auxiliary extension proves the exact count.
Loops are UNSAT, as required; duplicate Boolean clauses
may be retained without altering the raw clause tally.
\end{proof}

\section{Physical semantics of the fixed formal finite-stack machine}
\begin{lemma}[C1e-STACK-PHYSICAL: uniformly charged fixed-machine simulation]
Fix any $M:\mathrm{TM2ComputableAux}(\mathrm{Bool},\mathrm{Bool})$
with $\forall i,\ \mathrm{Finite}(M.\mathrm{tm}.\Gamma_i)$.
There is one ordinary deterministic finite-alphabet, locally headed
multitape machine $U_M$ and constants $a_M,b_M$ such that for every
input word $x$ of length $B$ and every $t$ for which the pinned
$\mathrm{TM2OutputsInTime}(M.\mathrm{tm},x,\mathrm{some}(y),t)$
holds, the \emph{same complete physical run} of $U_M$ on the
read-only source word $x$ outputs exactly $y$ with
\[
 T_{U_M}(x)\le a_M(B+t+1),\qquad
 S_{\mathrm{peak},U_M}(x)\le b_M(B+t+1).
\]
These bounds include initialization, input copying/reversal, all
stack accesses, finite-control lookups, output copying/reversal and
completion. This is a fixed-machine simulation theorem and does
not independently verify the source theorem's Lean proof.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The pinned Mathlib \texttt{FinTM2} structure supplies a finite number
$K$ of stacks, finite label type $\Lambda$, finite internal state
type $\sigma$, a fixed main label and a fixed statement tree
$m(\lambda)$ for each label. The pinned \texttt{MainTheorem} adds
finite alphabets on \emph{every} stack, not only its input stack.
Every finite-state function inside a statement is thus a fixed
finite lookup table. Let $L$ be the maximum number of syntactic nodes
on a root-to-leaf path across the finitely many $m(\lambda)$.
Both $K$ and $L$ are constants of $M$, independent of $B,t,x$.

Allocate one writable tape per stack with its head immediately after
the present top symbol and a bottom marker. A \texttt{push} writes
one finite-alphabet symbol and moves its head right; a \texttt{pop}
moves left and erases the top (or reads the bottom marker if empty);
a \texttt{peek} visits the last symbol and returns to the head.
Each operation needs bounded local tape steps. The other
\texttt{load}, \texttt{branch}, \texttt{goto}, \texttt{halt}
operations change only the fixed finite control. Initialize the
input stack from the actual read-only word using a linear temporary
copy and reverse sweep; the other stacks start genuinely empty.
This costs $O_M(B+1)$ physical steps and $O(B)$ writable cells.

In Mathlib's \texttt{TM2.step}, \texttt{stepAux} evaluates only
one path down the fixed finite statement tree until the first
\texttt{goto} or \texttt{halt}. Hence each formal step costs
$O_M(L)$ physical actions, and at most $Lt$ new stack symbols
can be pushed over $t$ steps. The total live stack contents are
bounded by $B+Lt$. The pinned \texttt{haltList} requires exactly
the designated output list, empty nondesignated stacks, and restored
internal state. Copying the output stack into the ordinary output
tape, including order reversal if required, costs
$O_M(B+Lt+1)$. Counting temporary buffers and all initialized
cells yields both bounds on the identical execution.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[J56: charged uniform recovery of a fixed-size junta]
For each fixed integer $w\ge1$, an explicit evaluator for every Boolean
$f:\{0,1\}^{\ell}\to\{0,1\}$ depending on at most $w$ coordinates permits a
deterministic exact $O(w2^w)$-gate fan-in-two circuit reconstruction using at most
\[
 2\ell\sum_{j=0}^{w-1}\binom{\ell-1}{j}+2^w
\]
evaluator calls and polynomial additional bit work.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each position $i$, query $f(1_S)$ and $f(1_{S\cup\{i\}})$ for all
$S\subseteq[\ell]\setminus\{i\}$ of size at most $w-1$. A relevant position
has a witness in which only its at most $w-1$ other relevant coordinates
might be nonzero, so some tested pair changes. Every irrelevant position
leaves all tests unchanged. Reconstruct the truth table over precisely the
discovered support by at most $2^w$ extra paid evaluations, then build its
Boolean Shannon expansion. Each call actually computes $f$; there is no
free support-location oracle.
\end{proof}



\begin{lemma}[C1f-JMV-VECTOR-SUPPORT: one batched low-weight suite]
Let $\ell,b\ge1$, $0\le w\le\ell$ and
$F:\{0,1\}^{\ell}\to\{0,1\}^{b}$ be an explicitly evaluable
total Boolean vector function. Assume that \emph{each} output
component depends on at most $w$ input coordinates, with no
supports supplied as free advice. Exactly
\[
Q(\ell,w)=\sum_{j=0}^{w}\binom{\ell}{j}
\]
nonadaptive \emph{whole-vector} evaluations suffice to recover
every support and every restricted truth table, and to compile
a pointwise identical shared fan-in-two circuit with at most
\[
3b(2^w-1)+\ell
\]
noninput NOT/AND/OR gates. No additional oracle evaluations
are needed after support discovery.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Query the full vector once for every Boolean address of Hamming
weight at most $w$, retaining all $bQ$ answer bits. For each
component $F_j$ and coordinate $i$, compare the stored values at
$1_S$ and $1_{S\cup\{i\}}$ over all $S$ not containing $i$ with
$|S|\le w-1$. If $i$ influences $F_j$, some setting of its at
most $w-1$ other relevant coordinates witnesses a change;
setting all irrelevant coordinates to zero supplies exactly
one of these charged query pairs. Irrelevant coordinates never
produce a change. Thus the discovered support $R_j$ is exact.
Every assignment to $R_j$ has at most $w$ ones and its value
at zero elsewhere was already queried; it fills the entire
$2^{|R_j|}$-entry component truth table without a new query.
A binary Shannon tree needs at most $3(2^{|R_j|}-1)$ AND/OR
gates, and at most $\ell$ negations are shared across all
components. Sum these bounds.
\end{proof}

\section{C1-T5-UNIFIED-SHORTCUT-QUOTIENT /082: exact two- and three-path rigidity}

\noindent\textbf{Status and source authentication.}
Fix $S=\{1,3\}\subset\mathbb Z_5$ and $R(x,y)\iff y-x\in S$.
Retain the authenticated, sorted, low-first input word of length
$L=n+m+2+2mw(n)$, with $w(n)=\max(1,\lceil\log_2\max(2,n)\rceil)$.
Each witness has precisely $3n$ bits with all decoded labels in
$\{0,1,2,3,4\}$. Malformed words are INVALID, whereas valid loops
and opposing arcs have count zero. This section is an exact
source-to-source preprocessing theorem, not a universal SAT solver.

\begin{lemma}[Shortcut rigidity for the exact $T_5$ relation /082]
Suppose $G$ has a path $x\to a\to b$ and the shortcut $x\to b$.
Every satisfying assignment obeys
$a=x+3,\ b=x+1\pmod5$.
If instead $G$ has the path
$x\to a\to b\to c$ and shortcut $x\to c$, then every satisfying
assignment obeys
$a=x+1,\ b=x+2,\ c=x+3\pmod5$.
The vertices in each motif are distinct.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every directed arc has modular increment $1$ or $3$.
For a two-arc path, the sums are $2,4,6$; only $6=1\bmod5$
is in $S$, so both increments equal $3$.
For a three-arc path, the sums are $3,5,7,9$; only $3\bmod5$
is in $S$, so every increment equals $1$.
The shortcut itself is an original source arc, so all equations
are logical consequences of the original CSP.
Length four already admits ten allowed increment sequences and
does not yield analogous uniqueness from path length alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform authenticated shortcut quotient /082]
On an arbitrary valid directed $T_5$ source $G$, enumerate every
two- and three-arc path with the corresponding start-to-end shortcut.
Insert all forced differences from the previous lemma into a
source-derived modular-equation system. If inconsistent, there are
no original satisfying assignments. Otherwise let $k$ be the number
of resulting components, and choose potentials $p_v\in\mathbb Z_5$
so every constrained original label has the unique expression
$x_v=t_{[v]}+p_v\pmod5$ for one free label
$t_{[v]}\in\mathbb Z_5$ per component.

For every original arc $u\to v$, if $[u]=[v]$, require
$p_v-p_u\in S$. If the two classes differ, write
$i<j$, $\delta=t_j-t_i$, and impose
\[
 \delta\in
 \begin{cases}
 S+p_u-p_v,&[u]=i,\\
 p_v-p_u-S,&[u]=j.
 \end{cases}
\]
Intersect these masks on each unordered pair of quotient classes.
An empty mask means count zero. Otherwise the resulting quotient
has at most $n$ variables and each pair-mask cardinality is at most
two. Its satisfying assignments are in explicit bijection with
those of $G$, so
\[
 \boxed{\#\operatorname{Hom}(G,T_5)=
       \#\operatorname{Solutions}(Q(G)).}
\]
There is a uniform sequential bit-machine compiler with
simultaneous conservative
$T=O((L+1)^7)$ time and $S_{\rm peak}=O((L+1)^3)$ writable bits
for the \emph{entire preprocessing and quotient output}.
The quotient may remain NP-complete to solve.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Scan actual source arcs and ordered tuples; each derived equation
holds in every original solution. A weighted modular component
data structure detects contradiction or gives a unique
potential for each vertex relative to its component's free root.
For each original arc, the displayed mask follows by substituting
$x_v=t_{[v]}+p_v$ in its original beat predicate. Conversely
every quotient solution satisfies every source arc under the
unique lift. Hence the maps are mutually inverse even in
disconnected and zero-solution cases. A sequential realization
can stream $O(n^4)$ motif tests into a polynomial-size potential
structure, then form pair-mask intersections and output them.
It does not have to store all motif tuples. Addresses, input
authentication, contradictions, arithmetic, output and retained
work are charged in the same physical time-space ledger.
\end{proof}

\]
\end{lemma}
