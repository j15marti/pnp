## 3. Obstacles and revision

**Obstacle 1 (pure-function collapse of complex-analytic/factorization residual).** The complete configuration at the crossing, any Gap Hamming completion, factorization-norm or trace-norm value, analytic certificate (polynomial division remainder, Jensen bound, Hadamard interpolant), approximate equality predicate, and any verification threshold are pure functions of the original ordered input blocks L_π, R_σ and the fixed deterministic machine A. By PURE-FUNCTION-UNIVERSAL-COLLAPSE1 and prior post-crossing residual collapses (including spectral/cut-rank, two-way, unshardable-code, process-matrix and tree-code), the entire complex-analytic/factorization residual is jointly recoverable by free local solves and deterministic whole-run replay within a single authentication-and-comparison pass. No information outside the original input and A is introduced; the norm and analytic objects are efficiently computable functions of the residual bits already determined by the blocks. Factorization norms and Gap Hamming completions re-encode aggregates or approximate statistics of the map lists but do not certify exact equality on adversarial (incompressible) permutations without retaining or re-accessing the source data. Approximate equality is insufficient for exact UNSAT certification on the diagonal family.

**Obstacle 2 (charged transport of residual symbols).** Realizing the factorization-norm comparison, analytic certificate verification or approximate residual equality check still requires the head (or message) to transport the residual map-difference information (or its norm/analytic signature) across the cut. Because the maps are incompressible, the information length remains Θ(r) (or Θ(N) in the encoding) in the worst case; free analytic methods and norm evaluations solve local algebraic or analytic fragments but do not compress the global incompressible difference. Even exact factorization-norm thresholds or Gap Hamming completions do not eliminate the need to co-locate or transport the incompressible information for exact decision. Free random access removes only local charge. Complex-analytic residual transport does not reduce the asymptotic charged transport of the global residual map across the cut.

**Obstacle 3 (static ceiling and matching upper bound).** Theorem 2 continues to bind: Dh ≤ N²/8 and Dh² ≤ N³/27. The matching upper bound (eq. 41) realizes the quadratic/cubic orders on the family by authenticating and comparing the maps in one pass. Complex-analytic/factorization residuals do not force additional independent work beyond what is already paid; they re-encode the comparison differently (approximately or via norms).

**Revision.** The free resources resolve every local fragment and every pure-function complex-analytic/factorization residual, but leave the global map-equality as the residual compatibility obligation.
