\section{PROPOSE: one missing lemma, falsifiable mechanisms}
\noindent\textbf{Question.} Prior ARC229 absorbs all exact two-variable (and other bijunctive) separator messages into local 2CNF; LSPV-4 HYBRID-BAD-MESSAGE228 and ARC232 enumerate the \emph{union} $E_t$ of all variables participating in exceptional child messages. Can a smaller, polynomially discoverable set of \emph{shared pins} eliminate every ternary exceptional message, even when $|E_t|=\Theta(N/\log N)$? Can this yield a new polynomially decidable certified hereditary restriction, with full discovery and memory charges?

\noindent\textbf{M1 (pin-to-binary).} Fixing any one coordinate of an arbitrary ternary relation makes each residual relation exactly 2CNF-definable. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} (Lemma~\ref{lem:pin}).

\noindent\textbf{M2 (message hitting cover).} A common set $X_t$ hitting all non-bijunctive ternary child scopes permits $2^{|X_t|}$ local 2CNF-plus-affine calls, plus charged $3^{|X_t|}$ cover discovery, rather than $2^{|E_t|}$ enumerations. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} under the tree hypotheses (Theorem~\ref{thm:cover}).

\noindent\textbf{M3 (hereditary syntactic cover).} Covering \emph{every} ternary child separator rather than just currently non-bijunctive ones is stable under deleting owned source constraints while preserving the certified bag directory. \textbf{PROVED/EXACT} in that explicitly fixed-directory sense (Corollary~\ref{cor:hereditary}); no arbitrary decomposition discovery is asserted.

\noindent\textbf{M4 (unrestricted improvement).} Bounded edge width three and fixed local affine rank alone force a polynomial controller. \textbf{FALSIFIED as a sufficient theorem}: prior ARC230 already proves that the supplied width-three, rank-one star class is NP-complete. Our cover parameter can be unbounded; this does not prove $P\ne NP$.

\section{Definitions and exact source semantics}
Let an explicit or polynomial-time discovered module tree $\mathcal T$ have $k\le\poly(N)$ bags $V_t$, with each original typed constraint owned exactly once and its entire scope contained in its owner's bag. The bags containing any variable are connected in $\mathcal T$ (running intersection). Local owned constraints comprise 2CNF and at most $e_0$ \emph{linearly independent} affine equations, with $e_0$ one fixed integer. Inconsistent rows are recognized. The source authenticator checks IDs, signs, multiplicities, complete row coefficients, ownership, connectedness and full scope containment. $D(N)$ denotes the charged cost of this check plus any asserted polynomial decomposition discovery. Every parent-edge separator $S_t=V_t\cap V_{p(t)}$ has size $s_t\le3$, except the root with $s_r=0$. Larger separators are not handled by this theorem.

Let $R_t\subseteq\{0,1\}^{S_t}$ be the exact relation extendible to all owned constraints in the subtree rooted at $t$. Once exact child tables are computed, a child relation $R_u$ is \emph{good} if it equals the conjunction of all its valid unary/binary clauses (a decidable constant-size test for $s_u\le3$). Otherwise it is \emph{bad}. A bad child necessarily has $s_u=3$. Define the bad-scope hypergraph
\[
\mathcal E_t=\{S_u: u\text{ child of }t,\ R_u\text{ bad}\},\quad
h_t=\tau(\mathcal E_t),\quad
E_t=\bigcup_{S\in\mathcal E_t}S,
\]
where $\tau$ is the minimum hitting-set cardinality. The \emph{full} set $E_t$ may be much larger than $h_t$ and is never silently replaced by the maximum single-edge separator. We distinguish the syntactic cover $h_t^{\rm all}=\tau(\{S_u:|S_u|=3\})\ge h_t$.

\begin{lemma}[Ternary one-pin closure]\label{lem:pin}
For \emph{every} Boolean relation $R\subseteq\{0,1\}^{\{x,y,z\}}$, every $v\in\{x,y,z\}$ and every $a\in\{0,1\}$, the pinned relation $R|_{v=a}$ on the remaining two coordinates is definable by at most four clauses of width at most two. The empty relation is represented by the empty clause.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate each of the at most four missing tuples $(b,c)$ of the two retained coordinates. Forbid $(b,c)$ using the binary clause whose literals are positive when the forbidden bit is zero and negative when it is one. The conjunction excludes exactly the missing tuples. If no coordinates remain, an empty relation is the empty clause. The statement is an exact truth-table identity; it assumes no parity structure.\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Charged three-port cover discovery]\label{lem:search}
Given an explicitly enumerated family of scopes, each of cardinality exactly three, a hitting set of size at most $h$ can be found, or proved nonexistent, in $O(3^h\poly(N))$ time and $O(h\log N+\poly(N))$ bit workspace. A fixed deterministic order and iterative deepening find the minimum $h$ within the searched cap.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the first uncovered triple $\{a,b,c\}$. Every hitting set contains one of $a,b,c$; branch on the three possible choices and decrement the budget. The recursion tree has depth at most $h$ and at most $1+3+\cdots+3^h$ nodes. At each node, scan the source-verified scope directory to find the first uncovered edge. Iterative deepening repeats at most a geometric series of the final exploration. The active recursion stores at most $h$ names, plus the directory.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact adaptive ternary-message cover]\label{thm:cover}
On the verified tree grammar above, there is one uniform deterministic controller that constructs all exact $R_t$, decides SAT/UNSAT, and on SAT reconstructs and source-checks a complete witness. It uses a bounded-cover search and local pinned fixed-codimension 2CNF--affine tests, with
\[
T_{\rm RAM}\ \le\ D(N)+\poly(N)\sum_{t\in\mathcal T}
\left(3^{h_t}+2^{s_t+h_t}Q_{e_t}(N)+2^{s_t}\right),\qquad
Q_e(N)=\sum_{i=0}^{e}\binom{N}{i},
\tag{1}
\]
and polynomial peak RAM space when $\max_t s_t\le3$, $\max_t h_t=O(\log N)$ and $\max_t e_t\le e_0$ fixed. More explicitly, stored membership tables use at most $\sum_t2^{s_t}$ entries; temporary branching/pin buffers, source indexing, rejected trials, joins, solver workspace, re-encoding, backtracking and witness reconstruction are included in the displayed polynomial factors. The theorem is restricted to \emph{recognized} trees, not arbitrary CNF.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct bottom-up. By running intersection, each child subtree meets its complement only on the child-parent separator; hence its exact feasibility table suffices for gluing. Compile every good child table into exact valid unary/binary clauses, as certified by checking all eight assignments. Find a hitting set $X_t$ for the other child separators by Lemma~\ref{lem:search}. For each requested parent separator assignment $\beta\in\{0,1\}^{S_t}$ and assignment $\alpha$ to $X_t\setminus S_t$, pin all those coordinates. Each bad ternary child table now has at most two free coordinates by the hitting property, so Lemma~\ref{lem:pin} compiles it into 2CNF. Good tables already compile and remain 2CNF after pinning. The conjunction of these clauses with the original owned 2CNF clauses and the original affine rows is exactly the desired local feasibility condition, with affine codimension at most $e_t$ (pins are 2CNF unit clauses). Apply the known deterministic fixed-codimension solver. A successful local assignment extends into each child subtree by induction; failure of all $\alpha$ proves the parent row infeasible. Every $\beta$ is enumerated, so the computed table is exact.

There are at most $2^{s_t+h_t}$ solver calls at node $t$, including unsuccessful calls, and at most $3^{h_t}\poly(N)$ cover-search work. Child lookups, validation, table emission, intermediate encodings, and all paths are charged in $\poly(N)$. Store membership tables and either polynomially sized backpointers or recompute successful local assignments in a second bottom-up trace (at most one additional charged local search per visited bag). Finally verify the reconstructed witness against every original owned source constraint, using source ID and sign. UNSAT is determined by emptiness of the root's zero-arity relation, without an externally supplied answer-class bit. Source parsing and full peak indexed memory are polynomial since $k\le\poly(N)$, $2^{s_t}\le8$, $e_t$ fixed and $h_t=O(\log N)$.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Deletion-stable certified restriction]\label{cor:hereditary}
Fix a verified bag directory with $s_t\le3$, and require a source-independent set $X_t^{\rm all}$ of size at most $H$ to hit \emph{every} three-variable child separator. Keep the directory fixed when deleting any subset of original owned clauses/affine rows. The resulting class remains within the same local rank cap $e_0$, separator cap 3, and syntactic cover cap $H$. For an explicitly supplied directory of encoded length $M$ with $H\le c\log_2 M$ and fixed $c$, exact decision remains polynomial in each descendant's total source-plus-directory length. All cover witnesses can be rediscovered by $3^H\poly(N)$ search, not assumed as free advice.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Deleting constraints neither changes the directory/separators nor increases the coefficient rank of the retained original affine rows. The syntactic three-edge family and its fixed hitting set stay unchanged, even when a previously good semantic table becomes bad after deletion. Since the directory remains part of every encoded descendant, $M$ is a lower bound on its length. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:cover} using $h_t\le h_t^{\rm all}\le H$; construction and search are polynomial in that physical length. No closure under \emph{re-optimizing arbitrary new decompositions} is claimed.\end{proof}

\section{Exact countercontrols and constructive separation of cost bounds}
The prior ARC230 reduction (not new here) maps every proper signed 3CNF clause $(\ell_1\vee\ell_2\vee\ell_3)$ to a rank-one XOR3 leaf with private bits $a,b,c$ satisfying $a\oplus b\oplus c=1$ and implications $a\Rightarrow\ell_1$, $b\Rightarrow\ell_2$, $c\Rightarrow\ell_3$. The leaf projection onto its triple is precisely the clause, which is non-bijunctive. A root bag containing all source variables and no owned constraints yields a verified width-three star. This class is NP-complete by uniform 3SAT reduction and membership in NP; it falsifies a polynomial implication from width-three adhesion plus rank-one local affine alone, \emph{not} the full-interface or bounded-cover theorems.

For each $q\ge2$, use $q$ such rank-one clause leaves, all containing a common original variable $z$ and otherwise disjoint names. Then $h_{\rm root}=1$ and $|E_{\rm root}|=2q+1$, with $N=\Theta(q\log q)$ for a canonical explicit signed-identifier representation. Add root unit clauses to produce matched SAT/UNSAT cases: $(z\vee a_0\vee b_0)$ and $(\neg z\vee a_1\vee b_1)$, $a_0=b_0=a_1=0$, and set $b_1=1$ (SAT) or $b_1=0$ (UNSAT); remaining clauses $(z\vee a_i\vee b_i)$ are harmless. One uniform controller tries $z=0$ and $z=1$, at most two SCC solves, then checks the full returned witness. The raw exceptional-union enumeration has a \emph{proxy} of $2^{2q+1}$ trials; the new certified hitting-cover algorithm has two. Neither is a lower bound on all algorithms, since direct clause inspection also recognizes this easy family.

For a disjoint collection of $q$ ternary OR clauses, the syntactic and semantic hitting number is $q$, but each clause is independently satisfiable. This is a hostile control against interpreting the new cover parameter as an intrinsic runtime lower bound. The program returns UNKNOWN when its configured cap is too small, rather than inventing an UNSAT decision.

\section{Time-space curves, crossings and separate models}
Write $N$ for physical encoded bits, $T$ for \emph{same-run} time including all failed work, and $S$ for simultaneous peak storage. Only within one declared model define $t=\log_N T$ and $s=\log_N S$. For a fixed node let $u=|E_t|$, $h=h_t$, $s_0=s_t$, and $Q=Q_{e_t}(N)\poly(N)\ge1$ encompass local-call and compilation charges. Ignoring the common source-checking factor, the two comparable \emph{algorithmic upper-bound work proxies} are
\[
W_{\rm union}=2^{s_0+u}Q,\qquad
W_{\rm hit}=3^h+2^{s_0+h}Q.
\tag{2}
\]
Their continuous equality threshold is
\[
 u_*(h,Q,s_0)=\log_2\big(2^h+3^h/(2^{s_0}Q)\big),
\quad
\frac{\partial u_*}{\partial h}
=\frac{2^h\ln2+(3^h/2^{s_0}Q)\ln3}
{(2^h+3^h/2^{s_0}Q)\ln2}\in[1,\log_2 3].
\tag{3}
\]
For the primitive $Q=1,s_0=0$, the first integer union-size point with $W_{\rm union}\ge W_{\rm hit}$ is $u=3$ when $h=1$ (since $2^3\ge3+2$). For fixed $Q,s_0$ and $h\to\infty$, $u_* =h\log_2 3-s_0-\log_2Q+o(1)$ and the derivative tends to $\log_2 3$. If $h=\gamma\log_2N$, $u=\delta\log_2N$, and $Q=N^c$, the illustrative normalized time exponents are
\[
 t_{\rm union}=c+\delta+o(1),\qquad
 t_{\rm hit}=\max\{\gamma\log_2 3,\ c+\gamma\}+o(1),
\tag{4}
\]
with all constants model-native. On a star with $h=1,u=2q+1$, the right side of (2) is polynomial while the raw union proxy is exponential in $q$. A budgeted adaptive scheduler may cap cover discovery by the direct-union budget and choose the cheaper certified route; it does not obtain an uncharged \emph{minimum} of two methods.

For comparison, the distinct strict signed exact-(2,2)-3CNF static-interface \emph{necessary structural} inequality $n\le3b+12bq$ yields the separate illustrative proxies $\log_2T_{\rm proxy}=b+q$ and $\log_2S_{\rm table}=b$. In the active continuous region $q=n/(12b)-1/4$,
\[
 E'(b)=1-\frac{n}{12b^2},\quad
 E''(b)=\frac{n}{6b^3}>0,\quad
 b_*=\sqrt{n/12}.
\]
The discrete minimum at $n=1200$ is 20 at $b=9,10,11$; the first permitted $q=0$ and hence first \emph{log-proxy} equality is $b=400$. Neither decomposition realizability nor any SAT runtime lower bound follows. The two problems' input classes and their $b,q$ versus $h,u$ parameters must \emph{not} be conflated.

\noindent\textbf{Model firewall.} Formula (1) is an indexed-RAM algorithmic upper bound with finite-access tables and word-size charges subsumed in $\poly(N)$. A read-only-input multi-tape TM, a single shared read-write tape counting the input in its footprint, a native two-writable-tape machine, and a fixed universal interpreter have distinct input/head/space conventions and transport costs. This report proves no numerical exponent transfer to them. In particular, a one-tape crossing lower bound is not a RAM or unrestricted-SAT lower bound. A standard polynomial simulation is enough to retain \emph{polynomiality} on the restricted class, provided all simulated source/choice access is charged; native exponents require a separate implementation.
