THEOREM BONFERRONNI-MOMENT
Let mu_plus, mu_minus be distributions on {0,1}^M matching all squarefree
coordinate moments through degree K.
Let H be a monotone DNF with terms A_1,...,A_m of width at most w.
Let X be the number of satisfied terms.
For every even R with R*w <= K,

|Pr_mu_plus[H=1] - Pr_mu_minus[H=1]|
<= E_mu_minus[ C(X,R) ],

and the same R-th factorial moment occurs under mu_plus.

Proof:
factorial moments through R are linear combinations of coordinate containment
moments of unions of at most R terms, hence degree <= Rw.
Bonferroni lower/upper partial sums through R and R-1 are identical under the
two distributions; interval width is E C(X,R).

UNIFORM EXACT-SLICE SPECIALIZATION
For U_{M,t},

E C(X,R)
=
sum_{I subset [m], |I|=R}
(t)_{|union_{a in I} A_a|}/(M)_{|union_{a in I} A_a|}.

LITERAL SUNFLOWER COROLLARY
If A_a=C union P_a, |C|=c, |P_a|=d, petals pairwise disjoint, then for even R
with c+R*d <= K,

acceptance gap
<= C(m,R) * (t)_{c+R*d}/(M)_{c+R*d}.

INTERPRETATION
Low-order fractional-design feasibility is not SAT-specific. The same
order-three convex-hull phenomenon appears for a polynomial-time
Gaussian-elimination predicate.
Therefore growing fractional designs can be only one ingredient of the SAT
lower-bound program; the load-bearing SAT-specific step must enter through
core/minterm geometry or a set-circuit approximation theorem.

LITERATURE STATUS
Wegener: set circuits are the natural exact-slice model; standard monotone
lower-bound methods do not directly transfer.
CKR 2022: robust-sunflower approximation applies to general monotone DAG
circuits, using closure plus trimming.
Beimel-Nir 2026: multislice lower bound is for formulas over multislice gates,
not unrestricted monotone DAG circuits.
Krieger 2007: prime-implicant incompressibility covers restricted
bounded-depth/multilinear models, not general DAG circuits.
No existing set-circuit-to-sunflower-DNF decomposition theorem for arbitrary
DAG sharing was located.
