THEOREM SLICE-PSEUDO-LP
Uniform exact-t slice U_{M,t}. For fixed local order k and epsilon, Definition 2.2 of ECCC TR26-220 reduces exactly to a (k+1)-variable LP after:
1. exchangeability symmetrization;
2. multi-affine reduction of all allowed product measures to corner biases p(1±epsilon);
3. grouping corners by j = number of high-bias coordinates;
4. matching normalized factorial moments r=0..k.

Target normalized moment:
m_r = ((t)_r/(M)_r)/p^r, p=t/M.

Corner-type normalized moment:
A_{rj}(epsilon)
= 1/C(k,r) * sum_l C(j,l) C(k-j,r-l)
  (1+epsilon)^l (1-epsilon)^(r-l).

Feasibility iff A w = m, w_j>=0, sum_j w_j=1.

THEOREM SLICE-PSEUDO-LOWER
d = (M-t)/(t(M-1)).
Any (k,epsilon)-pseudo-independent representation of U_{M,t} satisfies:
epsilon^2 >= (k-1)d.
For k=2 equality is achievable, so
epsilon_min(2)=sqrt((M-t)/(t(M-1))).

Necessary parameter condition for TR26-220 Theorem 2.3:
epsilon <= gamma/w
implies
k-1 <= gamma^2/w^2 * t(M-1)/(M-t).
With k=Theta(w log(1/delta)) from the sunflower corollary, necessary:
w^3 log(1/delta)=O(t)
up to theorem constants.
