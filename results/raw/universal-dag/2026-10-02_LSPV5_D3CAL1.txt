D3 CLAUSE-COUNT CALIBRATION
Date: 2026-10-02
Status: exact finite result under simple exact-3-CNF, three distinct variables per clause.

For n=3 there are exactly 8 possible distinct full 3-clauses. Each clause falsifies exactly one of the 8 assignments.

For exactly m distinct clauses:
m=0..7: every formula is SAT. Therefore the minimal SAT/UNSAT classifier is a constant SAT terminal; no decision nodes are required once the promise n=3 and exact m<8 is known.
m=8: there is exactly one distinct formula, containing all 8 clauses, and it is UNSAT. Therefore the minimal classifier under the exact-m promise is a constant UNSAT terminal. A membership-input ordered construction may contain bookkeeping nodes, but these are not intrinsic solver nodes under the promised formula family.
m=9: impossible without duplicate clauses. If duplicate occurrences are allowed, duplicate deletion adds no semantic constraint; the instance reduces to a formula with at most 8 distinct clauses.

Thus D(3,m) is a degenerate saturation control, not a meaningful growth curve. The first nontrivial clause-count curve is D(4,m).

Important correction: a prior ordered membership-DAG count of 8 nodes for n=3,m=8 measured representation/bookkeeping for choosing clauses, not the minimum solver under the exact formula-family promise. The intrinsic promised classifier is constant because there is only one formula.

P versus NP remains OPEN.
