without the empty clause certifies that no resolution refutation of width <=W exists.

Literature alignment:
Ben-Sasson and Wigderson relate resolution width to proof size and use expansion to obtain
exponential resolution lower bounds.
Chvatal-Szemeredi and subsequent work establish exponential resolution lower bounds for
random k-SAT at fixed unsatisfiable densities.
Carenini ECCC TR26-158 (2026) proves exponential lower bounds for k-DNF resolution on
random 3-CNF at every constant density above the elementary first-moment UNSAT bound,
for k=O(log n). For 3-SAT the elementary first-moment density is
ln(2)/ln(8/7) = 5.190893069684433.

Interpretation:
The finite experiment shows the onset of a genuine proof-system resource, not merely solver
runtime: constant width 3 begins to fail first near alpha=5.25 and, as n increases, also
fails at alpha=6 and alpha=8. This is qualitatively consistent with the asymptotic random-CNF
resolution literature. It remains a resolution/Res(k) statement, not an arbitrary SAT
algorithm lower bound.
