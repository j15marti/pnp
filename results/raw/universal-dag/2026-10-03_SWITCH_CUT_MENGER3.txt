Model:
Passive monotone two-terminal contact scheme for the exact-weight-eight promise classifier D(n,8).
Each controlled contact is labelled by one clause-presence bit x_c and conducts iff x_c=1.
Clause labels may repeat on multiple contacts.
Fixed always-on wiring/junctions are free and may be contracted.
The output is SAT iff s and t are connected.
Complement/normally-closed contacts are NOT allowed in this theorem.

Definitions:
M(n)=8*C(n,3) clause labels.
U8(n)=number of UNSAT promised inputs =
 C(n,3)+268*C(n,4)+3030*C(n,5)+7800*C(n,6)+5040*C(n,7).

Define tau(n) as the least integer d such that:
C(M(n)-d,8) <= U8(n).

PROVED/EXACT cut-label lemma:
Let K be any s-t edge cut in a correct passive monotone contact scheme and let L(K)
be the set of distinct clause labels appearing on contacts of K.
If |L(K)|=d, then every weight-eight input using only labels outside L(K) leaves every
contact of K open and therefore disconnects s from t.
Hence all C(M-d,8) such inputs must be UNSAT.
Therefore C(M-d,8) <= U8(n), so d >= tau(n).

Every s-t edge cut has at least tau(n) contact edges.
By edge-Menger, the network contains at least tau(n) pairwise edge-disjoint s-t paths.

Every clause label c belongs to an UNSAT exact-eight formula: take all eight sign patterns
on c's underlying variable triple.
Therefore no s-t path can contain only one controlled contact labelled c, because that
negative promised input would activate it and create a false SAT path.
After contracting fixed always-on wiring, every s-t path contains at least two controlled contacts.

Therefore:
C_contact(n,8) >= 2*tau(n).

A one-occurrence/read-once switch network is impossible for every n>=4.

Asymptotic sharpening:
Let r=M-tau. Since C(r,8)<=U8(n), C(r,8)>=(r/8)^8, and U8(n)<49n^7:
r < 8*49^(1/8)*n^(7/8) <13.013*n^(7/8).

C_contact(n,8) >=2M-26.026*n^(7/8).

C_contact(n,8)=2M-o(M)=16*C(n,3)-o(n^3).

Two-switch-path sparsification control:
For D(4,8), exhaustive construction finds, for every one of the 96 conflict pairs,
a legal weight-eight SAT input having that pair as its unique conflict pair.
Therefore a parallel bank of two-contact paths cannot omit any conflict edge:
all 96 pair paths are individually essential in that subclass, requiring 192 contacts.

Limitations:
This theorem is only for passive monotone contacts. Complement contacts, active/sequential
relays, general Boolean circuits, branching programs, and RAM algorithms are stronger models.
No unrestricted SAT-time lower bound follows.
