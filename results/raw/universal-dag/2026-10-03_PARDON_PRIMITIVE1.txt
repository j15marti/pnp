Literature verified:
1. John Pardon, "Universally counting curves in Calabi--Yau threefolds" (2023):
   global curve-enumeration invariants are determined by local curves.
2. Kai Behrend, "A Pardon Algebra for Zero-cycles" (arXiv:2604.05534, 2026):
   Definition 1.10 defines Pardon homology as a colimit over families.
   Pullback-related representatives become the same element.
   Product is induced by disjoint union of families.
   Proposition 1.11 gives a graded commutative/cocommutative Hopf algebra.
   Definition 2.8 identifies multiplicative theories as group-like.
   Remark 2.13 calls primitive theories "connected theories".
   Proposition 2.14 gives an exponential/logarithm bijection between primitive and multiplicative theories.
3. Schmitt's incidence Hopf algebras and later graph Hopf-algebra work provide the established combinatorial analogue:
   disjoint union is product; coproduct is restriction/splitting; primitive generators capture connected structure.

Computational analogue tested:
We do NOT claim the actual Pardon algebra transfers to computation.
For a promise function and a split of its source coordinates, form a binary continuation matrix M whose 1-entries are legal UNSAT completions.
Use:
  rho(M)=rank_F2(M),
  kappa(M)=log2 rho(M).
Rank is multiplicative under Kronecker product, so kappa is additive:
  kappa(M tensor N)=kappa(M)+kappa(N).
This is the scalar analogue of taking logarithm of a multiplicative/group-like quantity.

Wrapper test:
On the natural D(4,8) 16|16 clause split, the k=4 continuation block has:
  shape 84 x 84
  ones 112
  rank_F2 56
  distinct nonzero row signatures 56.
COPY (duplicate all rows), DEAD (append 17 zero rows), and a tautological branch duplication
all leave rank exactly 56.
Thus kappa=log2(56)=5.807354922... is invariant under these finite semantic wrappers.
This works because the object is already a semantic continuation relation; it is not a statement
that raw DAG syntax automatically quotients wrappers.

Independent-product test:
The D(4,8) k=2 block has rank 16.
Its explicit Kronecker square has rank 256.
Therefore:
  kappa(M)=4,
  kappa(M tensor M)=8.
The primitive-like log score exactly removes multiplicative product inflation by converting it to additivity.

Hostile controls:
6+6-bit parity continuation matrix:
  rank_F2=2
  kappa=1.
Thus a function with arbitrarily large ordinary decision depth can have constant primitive-like score.
6+6-bit equality matrix:
  rank_F2=64
  kappa=6.
The score therefore measures cross-cut semantic distinction, not decision count.

D(4,8) natural split decomposition:
k=0: rank 1, 1 connected support component, complete bipartite.
k=2: rank 16, 16 components, each K_{1,4}.
k=4: rank 56, 56 components; all are complete bipartite:
  16 K_{2,1}, 16 K_{1,1}, 16 K_{1,2}, 4 K_{1,4}, 4 K_{4,1}.
k=6: rank 16, 16 K_{4,1} components.
k=8: rank 1, one K_{16,1} component.

D(5,8) natural 40|40 triple split:
k=0: rank 1.
k=2: rank 28, 28 complete-bipartite components.
k=4: rank 144, 144 complete-bipartite components.
k=6: rank 36, 36 complete-bipartite components.
k=8: rank 1.
Every support component in every tested block is a complete bipartite graph.
The largest k=4 components have at most 22 support vertices.

Interpretation:
This is a useful negative result for the fixed-m=8 hardness route.
The Pardon-style "connected/primitive" decomposition does not reveal one large irreducible obstruction.
Instead the continuation relations decompose into many small local biclique pieces.
The equality rank = row-type count = connected-component count is explained by this decomposition.
This is consistent with the known polynomial conflict representation of D(n,8).

Most important conceptual correction:
Taking a primitive/logarithmic part is NOT sufficient for wrapper invariance by itself.
If a syntactic wrapper W is treated as an independent multiplicative factor, log(F*W)=log F + log W:
the wrapper remains as an additive primitive contribution.
Pardon-style wrapper invariance therefore requires TWO stages:
  (A) a colimit/quotient identifying certified semantics-preserving realizations;
  (B) primitive/log extraction to remove independent-product inflation.
The actual Pardon construction has an analogous ordering: the colimit relations are built into H_* before the Hopf-algebra primitive/multiplicative analysis.

Candidate computational architecture:
  raw realizations
    -> certified semantic/refinement colimit quotient
    -> product by independent composition
    -> continuation relation / multisign representation
    -> multiplicative invariant rho
    -> primitive score log rho.
