Negative-star architecture lower bound:
There are 64 star-path/contact incidences.
A contact can be shared only by the two stars corresponding to the endpoints of its Q4 edge.
If a shared occurrence appears at positions p and q on the two four-contact paths, DAG splicing creates paths of lengths p+4-q and q+4-p.
Since no safe all-negative conjunction has length <4, p must equal q.
If p=q is 2 or 3, splicing creates a four-negative set that is not a vertex star; exhaustive classification shows the only safe all-negative size-4 sets are the 16 stars.
Hence a shared contact can occur only in position 1 or position 4.
Each of the 16 star paths can share at most two incidences, so at most 16 of the 64 incidences can be saved.
Thus this architecture needs at least 64-16=48 controlled contacts.
The construction attains it.
