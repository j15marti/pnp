No information outside the original input and A is introduced; the rank-driven objects are efficiently computable functions of the residual bits already determined by the blocks. Spectral embeddings and cut-rank re-encode aggregates of the map lists but do not certify exact equality on adversarial (incompressible) permutations without retaining or re-accessing the source data.

**Obstacle 2 (charged transport of residual symbols).** Realizing the spectral comparison, rank cut, private-row filter or syndrome verification still requires the head (or message) to transport the residual map-difference information (or its rank/syndrome signature) across the cut. Because the maps are incompressible, the information length remains Θ(r) (or Θ(N) in the encoding) in the worst case; free GE solves the local linear algebra but does not compress the global incompressible difference. Even exact cut-rank or private-row ablation does not eliminate the need to co-locate or transport the incompressible information. Free random access removes only local charge. Rank-driven transport does not reduce the asymptotic charged transport of the global residual map across the cut.

**Obstacle 3 (static ceiling and matching upper bound).** Theorem 2 continues to bind: Dh ≤ N^{2}/8 and Dh^{2} ≤ N^{3}/27.
