\textbf{No unconditional super-polynomial lower bound for Circuit-SAT is claimed; P versus NP remains OPEN.}

\section{Goal and method}
Build circuits step by step (gate by gate, wire by wire), track the minimal number of
gates/wires needed at each step, grow the number $n$ of inputs, and track what any
decider for $\SAT$ must pay as a function of the parameters $(n,s,w)$:
inputs, gates, wires. Models: fan-in-2 gates over the full basis $B_2$ unless stated.

\section{Level 0--1: structural (wire) lower bounds}
\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] A fan-in-2 circuit whose output depends on all $n$ inputs has $s\ge n-1$ gates, hence at least $2(n-1)$ gate-input wires.
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Every input needs a directed path to the output; contract the DAG of relevant gates: each fan-in-2 gate merges at most two components, so connecting $n$ inputs needs $\ge n-1$ gates. \qed

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT, read-all bound] Any deterministic decider for $\SAT$ given the circuit as a read-only list must probe $\ge n-1$ gate descriptions on the instance family $C_n=\bigwedge_{i}x_i$ (balanced AND tree).
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} $C_n$ is satisfiable. If gate $g$'s type is never probed, replace it by the constant-FALSE gate: the probe transcript is identical but the new circuit is unsatisfiable. \qed

So the input-size bound is $\Omega(s)$ with the wire count $w=2s$ as parameter; this is the trivial floor of the ladder.

\section{Level 2: black-box (evaluation-only) bound}
\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] Any deterministic algorithm that only evaluates an unknown $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ needs $2^n$ evaluations to decide satisfiability in the worst case (adversary answers 0 until one point remains).
\end{prop}
This does not transfer to the circuit-given model: reading wires gives structure. Gap between $\Omega(s)$ (circuit given) and $2^n$ (black box) is exactly where the open problem sits.
Best known upper bound for general circuits remains $2^n\cdot\mathrm{poly}(s)$; improvements $(2-\varepsilon)^n$ are ruled out only \emph{conditionally} (SETH-type hypotheses, CONDITIONAL, not used as proof).

raw data: \texttt{results/raw/circuitsat\_min\_gates\_B2.json}; runtime $<10$ s). Number of functions by minimum fan-in-2 $B_2$ gate count
(constants and literals cost 0; negation free):
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lccccc}\toprule
$n$ & 0 gates & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4\\\midrule
1 & 4 & 0&0&0&0\\
2 & 6 & 10 & 0&0&0\\
3 & 8 & 30 & 114 & 80 & 24\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
Totals $4,16,256$ check. For $n=3$ the maximum cost is 4 gates (8 wires) and all 256 functions are covered. $n=4$ requires a smarter search (naive BFS exceeds the 30\,s rule; see plan).

Base case: for $s\le1$ gate, $\SAT$ is decided in $O(1)$ time by checking the gate type (only the constant-FALSE gate is unsatisfiable).
