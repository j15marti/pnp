\section{Goal and method}
Goal: a lower bound for \CSAT{}, possibly parametrized. Method: climb a ladder of instance families
$\mathcal F_{n,m}$ ($n$ inputs, $m$ gates, $w$ wires, fan-in $\le 2$ over the basis $B_2$), at each rung recording
(i) the minimum number of gates/wires a circuit in the family must have, and (ii) the best provable bound on the
cost of deciding satisfiability in a fixed model. Parameters tracked: $n$, $m$, $w$, fan-out $k$, depth $d$, treewidth $\mathrm{tw}$.

\section{Rung 1: unique-solution circuits and the wire bound (PROVED/EXACT)}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:and}
Let $C$ be a fan-in-2 circuit with $n$ inputs, all relevant to the output (the output is not constant in any input).
Then $C$ has at least $n-1$ gates and at least $2(n-1)$ gate-input wires; equality holds for the AND of $n$ literals.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Relevant inputs must be connected to the output; the underlying graph restricted to the output's cone is connected, with $n$ source
nodes and $g$ gates of in-degree $\le2$, so edges $e\le 2g$, and connectivity of $n+g$ nodes needs $e\ge n+g-1$. Hence $2g\ge n+g-1$, i.e.\ $g\ge n-1$,
and $e\ge n+g-1\ge 2n-2$. The balanced AND tree attains it.
\end{proof}
The AND of $n$ literals has exactly one satisfying assignment, so this is the smallest nontrivial unique-\CSAT{} rung: $m=n-1$, $w=2(n-1)$.

\begin{theorem}[Wire-reading bound]\label{thm:read}
In the adjacency-list model (the algorithm reads the circuit by probes of single wires/gate types), any deterministic algorithm deciding \CSAT{} on
circuits with $w$ wires makes $\Omega(w)$ probes, even restricted to AND-trees of literals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $n/2$ variables, each appearing positively at exactly two leaves of an AND-tree; the instance is satisfiable. Flipping the polarity of any single
leaf makes it unsatisfiable ($x\wedge\neg x$). There are $n$ such unsatisfiable neighbours. A deterministic algorithm that never probes leaf $j$'s polarity behaves
identically on the base instance and on the instance with leaf $j$ flipped, so it errs on one of them. Hence it probes every leaf: $\ge n=\Theta(w)$ probes.
\end{proof}
\emph{Caveat.} This is an information bound (input must be read), not a computational lower bound; it is the $\Theta(w)$ floor of the ladder.

$m$ (size) & $2^n\cdot m$ brute force & $\Omega(m)$ read (Thm.~\ref{thm:read}), PROVED\\
treewidth $\mathrm{tw}$ & $2^{O(\mathrm{tw})}m$ by DP over a tree decomposition (KNOWN) & $(2-\epsilon)^{\mathrm{tw}}\mathrm{poly}$ excluded under SETH (CONDITIONAL; cited from memory for CNF-SAT, to re-verify)\\
$n$ (inputs) & $2^n m$ & $2^{o(n)}$ excluded under ETH (CONDITIONAL)\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
Unconditional circuit-size lower bounds for explicit functions are only linear: roughly $3.1n$ over $B_2$ (Li--Yang, 2022; KNOWN THEOREM, verify exact constant).
So an unconditional parametrized lower bound of the form $2^{\Omega(\mathrm{tw})}$ or $\omega(m)$ time for \CSAT{} is OPEN.
