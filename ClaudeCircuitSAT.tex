\section{Program}
Build circuits bottom-up: $C_{n,w}$ has $n$ inputs and $w$ wires (fan-in $\le 2$ gates, so $w\le 2g$).
Track (i) $W(f)$, the minimum wire/gate count to \emph{build} $f$; (ii) the cost $T(n,w)$ of deciding SAT on a circuit of that shape.
A lower bound may be parametrized: $T(n,w,k)$ for $k\in\{\text{treewidth},\text{depth},\text{fan-out}\}$.

\begin{prop}[PROVED/EXACT] $\mathrm{AND}_n$ in fan-in-2 gates needs exactly $n-1$ gates ($2n-2$ wires).
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Upper bound by a tree. Lower: the output depends on all $n$ inputs; a fan-in-2 circuit whose output depends on $n$ inputs has at least $n-1$ gates, since the graph restricted to the output's cone is connected on $\ge n+g$ nodes with $\le 2g$ edges, and connectivity needs $\ge n+g-1$ edges, so $2g\ge n+g-1$. \qed
