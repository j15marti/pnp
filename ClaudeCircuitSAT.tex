\section{Goal and method}
Ladder: start from the simplest circuit-SAT-type problems, track the exact wire count (gate inputs; fan-in~2, unbounded fan-out, basis $B_2$) of the minimum circuit at each rung, then increase the number of inputs $n$ and the structural complexity, recording where proven bounds stop. Wires $=2\cdot$gates for $B_2$ circuits with all gates fan-in 2.

\section{Rung 1: connectivity lower bound (PROVED/EXACT)}
\begin{proposition}
If $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ depends on all $n$ inputs, any fan-in-2 circuit computing $f$ has $\ge n-1$ gates and $\ge 2(n-1)$ wires.
\end{proposition}
\emph{Proof.} Every essential input has a directed path to the output; the underlying graph restricted to inputs and gates must be connected, so with $g$ gates of fan-in 2 and $n$ inputs we need $2g\ge g+n-1$ edges to span $g+n$ nodes, i.e.\ $g\ge n-1$. \qed

Tight for: a single clause $\bigvee_i \ell_i$ ($n-1$ gates; its SAT instance is trivial), parity ($n-1$). Hence the first genuine content of the ladder is the gap between $n-1$ and anything larger.
