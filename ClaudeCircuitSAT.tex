\section{Model}
A circuit $C$ over $n$ inputs is a DAG of $g$ fan-in-2 gates from the full basis $B_2$ (negation free); \emph{wires} $w(C)=2g$ (in-wires; fan-out unrestricted). $s(f)$ = min gates, $w(f)=2s(f)$. Circuit-SAT$_{n,g}$: given $C$ with $\le n$ inputs, $\le g$ gates, decide $\exists x\,C(x)=1$. Parameters: $(n,g)$, possibly also fan-out/depth.

\section{Step 0: tiny cases}
\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, connectivity]
If $f$ depends on $n$ inputs then $s(f)\ge n-1$, so $w(f)\ge 2n-2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} The graph (inputs plus gates, one edge per in-wire) must connect all $n$ relevant inputs to the output, so it has $\ge n+g'-1$ edges on the $n+g'$ nodes of the cone, while edges $\le 2g'$; hence $g'\ge n-1$.\end{proof}
Tight: $\mathrm{AND}_n$ chain/tree uses exactly $n-1$ gates.

\begin{theorem}[PROVED/EXACT, probe lower bound]
Any deterministic algorithm deciding Circuit-SAT on instances given as gate lists must read $\ge n-1$ gate records, on the family $\mathrm{AND}_n$ chain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} The chain is satisfiable (only $1^n$). Replacing any single gate by constant $0$ gives an unsatisfiable instance; an algorithm skipping gate $i$ answers identically on both. \end{proof}
This is only $\Omega(m)$ (reading the input); it is the trivial baseline against which anything real must be measured.

\section{Step 1: exact minimum sizes (COMPUTATIONAL)}
Script \texttt{results/circuitsat/mincirc.py} (BFS over sets of computed truth tables, shared gates allowed, $B_2$; $n=3$ ran in $<30$ s).
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{@{}cccccccc@{}}\toprule
$n$ & \#functions & $s=0$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & $\max s$ \\\midrule
2 & 16 & 6 & 10 & -- & -- & -- & 1\\
3 & 256 & 8 & 30 & 114 & 80 & 24 & 4\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
So $\max_f w(f)=2,\,8$ for $n=2,3$ (max wires $=2\max s$). The 24 functions needing 4 gates for $n=3$ (e.g.\ parity-like/majority-type) are the first witnesses of ``fresh wires beyond connectivity'': connectivity gives only $s\ge2$.

\section{Known bounds to build on}
\st{KNOWN THEOREM} Counting: almost all $f$ need $\Omega(2^n/n)$ gates (Shannon). \st{KNOWN THEOREM, recalled, not re-verified this run} gate elimination gives $\approx3n$ for explicit functions in $B_2$ (Li--Yang, STOC 2022: $3.1n-o(n)$). \st{KNOWN, conditional} SETH implies no $O(2^{(1-\epsilon)n}\mathrm{poly}(g))$ algorithm for CNF-SAT; this is the natural parametrized target ($2^n\cdot g$ vs.\ $n,g$).
