\section{Model and plan}
Circuit $C$: $n$ inputs, $s$ fan-in-2 gates over $B_2$, wires $w=2s$. CircuitSAT asks whether some $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ gives $C(x)=1$.
Plan: (i) fix the cost of \emph{building} circuits wire by wire; (ii) add inputs one at a time and track minimal size/wires;
(iii) turn construction costs into a bound on \emph{deciding} satisfiability, as a function of $(n,s)$ or another parameter.

\section{Step 1: construction cost (PROVED)}
\begin{lem}[connectivity]\label{lem:conn}
A $B_2$ circuit whose output depends on $k$ inputs has $\ge k-1$ gates and $\ge 2(k-1)$ wires.
\end{lem}
\begin{proof} The $k$ inputs and $s$ gates form a DAG whose output reaches each essential input. Each gate adds one new component-merge at most (two in-edges), so connecting $k$ sources to one sink needs $k-1$ gates. \end{proof}

\section{Step 3: a decision lower bound linear in the instance size (PROVED)}
Model: random-access machine reading the circuit as a list of gate records.
\begin{thm}\label{thm:read}
Every deterministic algorithm for CircuitSAT on instances with $s$ gates must read $\Omega(s)$ cells in the worst case.
\end{thm}
\begin{proof}
Let $k=\lfloor s/4\rfloor$. Gadget $i$: gates $N_i=\neg x_i$, $g_i=x_i\wedge h_i$ with $h_i\in\{x_i,N_i\}$; output is an AND-tree over $g_1..g_k$. Satisfiable iff all $h_i=x_i$. Let $I_0$ have all $h_i=x_i$ (satisfiable) and $I_j$ flip only $h_j$ (unsatisfiable). The cell storing $h_j$ differs only in $I_j$ and cells are distinct across $j$. If the algorithm on $I_0$ reads fewer than $k$ cells, some $h_j$ cell is unread; then $I_j$ yields the identical transcript but the opposite answer. Hence $\ge k=\Omega(s)$ reads.
\end{proof}
Parametrised reading: the bound is in $s$ and independent of $n$ here ($n=k$ is tied to $s$). Dependence on $n$ with $s\gg n$ is the missing part.

\section{Step 4: parametrised upper bounds that constrain any lower bound}
\begin{itemize}
\item KNOWN: brute force $O(2^n s)$. CONDITIONAL (SETH): no $2^{(1-\varepsilon)n}\mathrm{poly}(s)$.
\item KNOWN: circuit treewidth $w$: $2^{O(w)}s$ by dynamic programming. So a $2^{\Omega(n)}$ lower bound needs instances of treewidth $\Omega(n)$; the hard parameter is not width.
\item KNOWN: $s=O(n)$ with bounded fan-out/depth classes lie inside SAT of CNF form; no improvement over $2^n$ known.
\end{itemize}
