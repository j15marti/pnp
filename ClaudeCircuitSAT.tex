\paragraph{Status.} \textbf{OPEN.} No super-linear unconditional lower bound for Circuit-SAT (general circuits, general algorithms) is claimed. Labels: PROVED, KNOWN, COMPUTATIONAL, CONDITIONAL, CANDIDATE, OPEN, RETIRED. Everything below ``Ladder'' is PROVED only for the stated restricted object.

\section{Setup}
A circuit $C$ has $n$ inputs, $s$ fan-in-2 gates over $B_2$ (negation free), hence $w=2s$ wires (fan-out edges counted at their sink). $\mathrm{SAT}(C)$: $\exists x\in\{0,1\}^n\,C(x)=1$. Parameters: $(n,s)$ jointly; the natural bound shape is $T(n,s)\ge f(n,s)$. Known upper bound $O(2^n s)$; \textbf{CONDITIONAL} (SETH) $2^{n-o(n)}\,\mathrm{poly}(s)$ is necessary for $s=\mathrm{poly}(n)$. Unconditional general-model lower bounds beyond reading the input are OPEN.

\section{Ladder (wire-by-wire accounting)}
\begin{prop}[Connectivity, PROVED] If $C$ depends essentially on $n$ inputs then $s\ge n-1$, i.e.\ $w\ge 2(n-1)$ wires; equality is attained by a tree of $n-1$ gates (e.g.\ an AND of literals, which has exactly one satisfying assignment).
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} The output cone is connected and every input is a leaf; a connected DAG with $n$ sources and in-degree $\le2$ has $\ge n-1$ internal nodes (each gate merges at most two components). \qed

\begin{prop}[Probe lower bound, PROVED, model: wire-probe] Let the algorithm access $C$ only by probes ``who feeds gate $g$?''. Deciding SAT needs $\ge s_0$ probes on the family with $s_0$ gates below.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} Let $C_0=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}x_i$ implemented as gates $g_i=\mathrm{AND}(x_i,x_i)$ feeding an AND-tree ($s_0\approx 2m$ gates). $C_0$ is satisfiable; for each $j$, $C_j$ replaces $g_j$ by $\mathrm{AND}(x_j,\neg x_j)$, making it UNSAT. An algorithm that skips probing $g_j$ answers identically on $C_0$ and $C_j$. So all $m$ leaf gates must be probed: $\Omega(s)$. \qed

\emph{Caveat:} this is a reading bound, not a computational hardness bound. It is the base rung of the ladder; the open question is whether rungs can be raised above $\Theta(s)$ for $T(n,s)$ in general models.

\section{Parametrized targets}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Width/treewidth $k$: $2^{O(k)}s$ upper bound (KNOWN dynamic programming); candidate lower bound $2^{\Omega(k)}$ under SETH (CONDITIONAL, KNOWN-style).
\item Fixed fan-out $d$: study how the minimal circuit for hard instances must duplicate wires; the repository's fan-out/locality material is a source of CANDIDATE bridges.
\item Gate-elimination: best known explicit-function bound is $\approx 3.1n$ gates for $B_2$ (KNOWN; Li--Yang 2022); these bound circuit size of functions, not SAT-solver time, so transfer is a FAILED TRANSFER unless a solver-to-circuit reduction is supplied.
\end{enumerate}
