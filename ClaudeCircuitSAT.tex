\section{Model and parameters}
A circuit $C$ has $n$ inputs, $g$ fan-in-2 gates (full binary basis), one output, and $w$ wires
(edges). Fan-in 2 gives $w\le 2g$. Circuit-SAT: given $C$, decide $\exists x\; C(x)=1$.
Candidate parameters for a parametrised bound $T(n,g,w,\mathrm{tw},\dots)$: $n$, $g$, $w$, treewidth/cutwidth of the wire graph.
\emph{Upper bounds (KNOWN):} $O(2^n g)$ by enumeration; $2^{O(\mathrm{tw})}\mathrm{poly}$ by dynamic programming over a tree decomposition.
\emph{Unconditional white-box lower bound:} only $\Omega(g)$ (input must be read). Anything stronger is OPEN
(SETH would give $2^{(1-\epsilon)n}$: CONDITIONAL).

\section{Step 1: wire lower bound for dependence (PROVED/EXACT)}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:wires}
If a single-output fan-in-2 circuit depends on all $n$ inputs then $g\ge n-1$ and $w\ge n+g-1\ge 2n-2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof} Every input and every gate except the output has an outgoing wire into a gate, giving $w\ge (n+g)-1$ (distinct source nodes). With $w\le 2g$ we get $g\ge n-1$, hence $w\ge n+g-1\ge 2n-2$. A binary AND tree attains $g=n-1$, $w=2n-2$. \end{proof}
The \emph{needle} $N_a(x)=[x=a]$ ($n$ literals ANDed, negations absorbed in the full binary basis) therefore has exact complexity $g=n-1$, $w=2n-2$ (a function with a unique satisfying point depends on all inputs).

\section{Step 2: black-box lower bound (PROVED/EXACT, model-restricted)}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:needle}
Any algorithm that learns about $C$ only through evaluation queries $x\mapsto C(x)$ needs $2^n$ queries in the worst case to decide Circuit-SAT, even when promised $C$ is $N_a$ or the constant $0$, with $g=n-1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof} Adversary answers $0$ to every query $x$; after $2^n-1$ queries one point $a$ is unqueried and both $N_a$ and $0$ are consistent. \end{proof}
\textbf{Caveat (failed transfer).} A white-box algorithm reads the AND tree and solves $N_a$ in $O(g)$. So the black-box bound measures only
\emph{information hidden by the encoding}, and says nothing about P vs NP. The right parameter for a white-box bound must
measure interaction among wires, not their number: $N_a$ has $\mathrm{tw}=1$.
