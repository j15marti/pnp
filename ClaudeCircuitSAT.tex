\section{Goal and method}
Goal: lower bounds for Circuit-SAT, possibly parameterized by $(n,m,k)$ =
(inputs, gates, a structural parameter such as width/treewidth/fan-out).
Method (ladder): start from the simplest circuits, track the \emph{wire ledger}
$W(f)=2\,s(f)$ (fan-in-2, so wires $=2\times$ gates) of the cheapest circuit per function, then
raise $n$ and record how the ledger and the \emph{decision-time} bound move.
Two distinct quantities must never be conflated:
(i) \emph{construction cost} $s(f)$ of circuits (a circuit lower bound), and
(ii) \emph{decision cost} of SAT on a given circuit (an algorithmic lower bound). Rung data below is (i); (ii) is the actual target.

\section{Proved elementary rungs}
\begin{lemma}[Connectivity, PROVED]\label{l:conn}
A fan-in-2 circuit with a single output depending on all $n$ inputs has $\ge n-1$ gates and
$\ge 2(n-1)$ wires. Proof: the dependence graph is a connected DAG with $n$ sources and one sink;
a tree combining $n$ leaves by binary nodes needs $n-1$ internal nodes.\end{lemma}
\begin{lemma}[Input-reading, PROVED]\label{l:read}
Any deterministic algorithm deciding Circuit-SAT for circuits with $m$ gates must read
$\Omega(m)$ symbols of the description in the worst case. Proof (adversary): take
$C_0=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m'}(x\wedge\neg x)$-style chains where each gate $g_i$ is an AND of $x$ with a
copy of $\neg x$; $C_0$ is UNSAT. Redirecting any single unread wire of $g_i$ to a fresh
constant $1$ yields a SAT circuit that agrees with $C_0$ on every read position; an algorithm
that skips a wire answers wrongly on one of the two. So all $\Theta(m)$ wires are read.\end{lemma}
These are trivial (linear) bounds: they set the baseline of the ladder, not progress toward P$\ne$NP.

\section{Parameterized view}
Known upper bounds frame what a lower bound must beat (KNOWN):
exhaustive $O(2^n\,m)$; treewidth-$k$ circuits solvable in $2^{O(k)}\,m$; so any parameterized
lower bound for fixed $k$ must be $\ge$ linear and a bound of the form
$f(k)\,m^{c}$ with $c>1$ would already be a nontrivial theorem.
Under ETH/SETH, CNF-SAT needs $2^{(1-\varepsilon)n}$ (CONDITIONAL; not unconditional).
Candidate parameter ledger: $(n,m,\text{tw},\text{fan-out},\text{depth})$.
Natural next unconditional target (OPEN): a gate-by-gate \emph{incremental} bound
$T(C_{j+1})\ge T(C_j)+\delta_j$ where $C_{j+1}$ adds one gate; no such $\delta_j>0$ beyond $O(1)$ is known.
