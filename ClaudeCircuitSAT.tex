\section*{Status}
Unconditional super-polynomial lower bounds for Circuit-SAT are \textbf{OPEN} (equivalent in difficulty to P vs NP territory; repository corpus agrees: P vs NP OPEN). Below: only what is proved or computed.

\section{Model}
Circuit $C$: $n$ inputs, $m$ fan-in-2 gates (any of the 16 binary functions), one output; wires $w=2m$ (gate input pins). Problem $\mathrm{CSAT}(n,m)$: is there $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ with $C(x)=1$? Parameters $(n,m)$; a bound may be $f(n,m)$.

\section{Level ladder (wires built up step by step)}
\textbf{PROVED (L0, connectivity).} If $f$ depends on all $n$ inputs, any circuit for $f$ has $\ge n-1$ gates, hence $\ge 2(n-1)$ wires (each input must reach the output; a fan-in-2 DAG with a single sink connecting $n$ sources needs $n-1$ merges).

\textbf{COMPUTATIONAL (L1--L3).} Exact minimum gate counts by BFS over circuits (\texttt{experiments/claude\_circuitsat/exact\_min\_gates.py}, raw: \texttt{results/raw/claude\_circuitsat/exact\_min\_gates.json}). For $f$ depending on exactly $d$ of $n$ inputs:
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{c|cccc}
$n$ & $d=0$ & $d=1$ & $d=2$ & $d=3$\\\hline
1 & 0 (2 fns) & 0--1 (2) & & \\
2 & 0 & 0--1 (4) & 1 (10) & \\
3 & 0 & 0--1 (6) & 1 (30) & 2--4 (218)\\
\end{tabular}\end{center}
(entries: min gates over functions; count of functions). Matches L0: $d$-dependent needs $\ge d-1$, tight at $d\le3$ for parity-type; worst case at $n=3$ is 4 gates (8 wires). Note: this is \emph{circuit size of a function}, not yet SAT hardness.

\section{Decision-time bound (PROVED, trivial but unconditional)}
\begin{prop}[Input-reading] In the random-access model where $C$ is given as a list of $m$ gate descriptions, any deterministic algorithm deciding CSAT must read $\Omega(m)$ gate descriptions in the worst case.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof sketch.} Take $C$ = AND of all inputs chained through $m$ gates; changing one unread gate to the constant 0 flips satisfiability, so an algorithm that skips a gate errs on one of the two instances. $\square$
This is a bound in $m$ only; it yields no dependence on $n$ beyond $m\ge n-1$.

\section{Parametrized view}
Upper bound $O(2^n m)$ (brute force). Conditional: under SETH, no $O(2^{(1-\varepsilon)n}\mathrm{poly}(m))$ algorithm (\textbf{CONDITIONAL}, known). Unconditional $2^{\Omega(n)}$ time is \textbf{OPEN}. Candidate parametrization to study: bound in terms of $(n,\ \text{treewidth } k)$ -- CSAT is solvable in $2^{O(k)}m$; a lower bound $2^{\Omega(k)}$ would be conditional (ETH).
