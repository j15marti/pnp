\paragraph{Status.} P versus NP remains \textbf{OPEN}. No super-polynomial lower bound for Circuit-SAT is claimed. Labels: PROVED/EXACT, KNOWN THEOREM, COMPUTATIONAL, CONDITIONAL, CANDIDATE, OPEN, FALSIFIED/RETIRED.

\section{Program}
Build circuits gate by gate (fan-in~2, basis $B_2$ of all binary gates), count gates $g$ and wires $w=2g$ (+ output tap), for $n=1,2,3,\dots$ inputs. Track (i) the minimum size $C(f)$ of circuits for hard functions, and (ii) the cost of deciding satisfiability of a circuit with $n$ inputs and $m$ wires. A lower bound is stated as $T(n,m)$, possibly parametrized by $n$, $m$, depth, or treewidth $k$.

\section{Ladder of exact facts}
\begin{description}
\item[L0 (PROVED/EXACT)] Any circuit whose output depends on all $n$ inputs has $\ge n-1$ gates, hence $\ge 2(n-1)$ wires (connectivity: the output DAG must reach $n$ inputs through fan-in~2 nodes). $\mathrm{AND}_n,\mathrm{OR}_n,\mathrm{XOR}_n$ attain $n-1$.
\item[L1 (PROVED/EXACT)] Reading bound: deciding Circuit-SAT for a circuit with $m$ wires needs $\Omega(m)$ time, since an unread gate can be changed to flip satisfiability (adversary argument on a gate feeding the output through an otherwise satisfiable path).
\item[L2 (COMPUTATIONAL)] Exhaustive search over all circuits ($n=3$): minimum $B_2$ gate counts of all 256 functions are
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lccccc}\toprule gates & 0&1&2&3&4\\ \midrule \# functions &8&30&114&80&24\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
so $\max_f C(f)=4$ for $n=3$ (e.g.\ majority). Script \texttt{experiments/circuitsat/min\_gates.py}; data \texttt{results/raw/circuitsat\_min\_gates\_n3.json}. Extension to $n=4$ needs checkpointed chunking (state space too large for one 30\,s run).
\item[L3 (KNOWN THEOREM)] Counting: most $n$-input functions need $\Omega(2^n/n)$ gates (Shannon); explicit best $B_2$ lower bounds are $\approx 3.1n-o(n)$ (Li--Yang 2022 style improvements; verify current record before citing). Hence wire-counting alone gives only linear bounds for explicit functions.
\item[L4 (KNOWN THEOREM)] Time--space: SAT is not in $\mathrm{DTISP}(n^{c},n^{o(1)})$ for $c<2\cos(\pi/7)\approx1.8019$ (Fortnow--van Melkebeek, Williams). This is the strongest unconditional deterministic bound of the parametrized time--space type.
\item[L5 (CONDITIONAL)] SETH $\Rightarrow$ no $O(2^{(1-\varepsilon)n}\mathrm{poly}(m))$ algorithm for CNF-SAT; ETH $\Rightarrow$ $2^{\Omega(n)}$. Parametrized by treewidth $k$: SAT in $2^{O(k)}m$, and SETH rules out $(2-\varepsilon)^k$.
\end{description}

\section{Parametrized target (CANDIDATE)}
Define $L(n,m)=$ minimum steps to decide satisfiability of circuits with $n$ inputs, $m$ wires. Known: $L=\Omega(m)$ (L1), $L=O(2^n m)$. Gap target: any unconditional $L=\omega(m)$ for general circuits in a fixed machine model is already beyond L4 only for restricted space; the open question is whether wire-structure (e.g.\ fan-out $\ge2$ reconvergence) forces work $\Omega(2^{\epsilon k})$ in treewidth $k$ without SETH. OPEN.
