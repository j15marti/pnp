\section*{Status}
\textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN.} No unconditional super-polynomial lower bound for Circuit-SAT is claimed. Statuses: PROVED, KNOWN, COMPUTATIONAL, CANDIDATE, OPEN.

\section{Goal and plan}
Circuit-SAT$(n,s)$: given a fan-in-2 circuit with $n$ inputs and $s$ gates, decide whether some input gives output $1$. Plan: climb a ladder $(n,s,w)$ ($w$ = wires), tracking for each rung the minimum wires/gates needed to \emph{build} the instance family and the best provable cost of \emph{deciding} it. The lower bound sought is parametrized, $T(n,s)$.

\section{Rung 0: building cost (wires)}
\begin{prop}[PROVED] A circuit whose output depends on all $n$ inputs has $\ge n-1$ fan-in-2 gates and $\ge 2(n-1)$ wires; $\mathrm{AND}_n$ meets this.\end{prop}
\begin{proof}The underlying undirected graph must connect the output to all $n$ inputs. Each gate adds at most one new connected component merge per input wire pair, so $g$ gates join at most $g+1$ sources: $g\ge n-1$; wires $=2g$.\end{proof}

\section{Rung 2: deciding cost}
\begin{thm}[PROVED, black-box] Any deterministic algorithm that decides Circuit-SAT using only evaluation queries needs $2^n$ queries in the worst case, even when $s=n-1+O(n)$.\end{thm}
\begin{proof}Adversary answers $0$ to every query. After $2^n-1$ queries, the instance ``$x=a$'' ($a$ unqueried; an AND of $n$ literals, $\le n-1$ gates) is consistent with all answers, as is the constant-$0$ circuit, which has the opposite answer.\end{proof}
Consequence: any better bound must inspect the wiring (non-black-box). Randomized: $\Omega(2^n)$ by Yao; quantum $\Theta(2^{n/2})$ (Grover/BBBV) black-box --- KNOWN.

\paragraph{Parametrized upper bounds (KNOWN).} Brute force $O(2^n s)$. Treewidth/pathwidth $k$ DP: $2^{O(k)}\mathrm{poly}(s)$. Hence a parametrized lower bound can only be $2^{\Omega(\min(n,k))}$-type; matching it for general circuits is SETH-flavoured (CANDIDATE/CONDITIONAL: SETH gives no $(2-\epsilon)^n\mathrm{poly}(s)$ for CNF-SAT, hence for Circuit-SAT).
