\section{Plan}
Build up from the simplest circuits, track gates $g$, wires $w$ (fan-in-2 basis, $w=2g$ plus output wire), inputs $n$, and the cost of deciding satisfiability.
Parameters: $(n,g,\text{fan-out},\text{depth})$. The bound is allowed to be parametrized, e.g.\ $T(n,g)$.

\section{Ledger of exact small cases}
\begin{prop}[PROVED, trivial] AND$_n$ has $n-1$ fan-in-2 gates, $2(n-1)$ gate-input wires, and exactly one satisfying assignment. Any algorithm that only evaluates the circuit as a black box needs $2^n$ evaluations in the worst case (adversary answers 0 until the last unqueried point). Reading the circuit needs $\Omega(g)$ time. These are query/reading bounds, not bounds for algorithms that inspect structure.\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[COMPUTATIONAL] Exact minimum number of fan-in-2 gates (all 16 binary gates, free constants) over all $n$-input functions, by BFS (\texttt{research/agents/claude/circuitsat/mincirc.py}):
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lll}\toprule $n$ & functions & max min-gates\\\midrule 2 & 16 & 1\\ 3 & 256 & 4\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
For $n=3$ the distribution of minimum gate counts $0,1,2,3,4$ is $5,33,114,80,24$ functions. (The generic Shannon bound is $\Theta(2^n/n)$.)\end{prop}

\section{Known barriers/results to build on (KNOWN, cited from memory; verify before use)}
Gate elimination gives $\sim 3n$ (Blum) to about $3.1n$ (Find--Golovnev--Hirsch--Kulikov) for explicit functions; these are circuit-size bounds for functions, not time bounds for SAT. Circuit-SAT hardness under ETH/SETH is conditional. The repository's time--space material (AGENTS.md; world ledger rows L13, L34--L37) bears on SAT time exponents in small space.
