\section{Model and the ladder}
Circuits: DAGs of fan-in-2 gates over the full binary basis $B_2$ (all 16 operations, negation free); size $=$ number of gates $g$; \emph{wires} $=2g$ gate-input connections (each input/gate may fan out). Ladder rung $(n,\mathcal F)$: all functions of $n$ Boolean inputs in a class $\mathcal F$; tracked quantity: $C(f)$ and wires $2C(f)$.

Ladder of problems (simplest first): single clause $\to$ conjunction of clauses $\to$ 2-CNF-SAT verifier $\to$ 3-CNF verifier $\to$ circuit-evaluation verifier. For a fixed formula $\varphi$ on $n$ variables, the \emph{verifier} $V_\varphi(x)=\varphi(x)$ is a function with $C(V_\varphi)\le O(|\varphi|)$; SAT itself asks for $\exists x\, V_\varphi(x)$, whose lower bounds are query/size questions for a \emph{solver}, a strictly harder object. We keep the two apart.

\section{Proved basics}
\begin{lemma}[connectivity, \EXACT]
If $f$ depends essentially on all $n$ inputs then every circuit for $f$ has $g\ge n-1$ gates, hence $\ge 2n-2$ wires.
\end{lemma}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} The circuit's underlying graph restricted to the cone of the output must be connected and contain all $n$ inputs; a connected graph on $n+g$ nodes has $\ge n+g-1$ edges, while edges $=2g$, so $2g\ge n+g-1$. \qed

\section{What is known at scale (\KNOWN, to be re-verified against primary sources)}
Gate elimination gives explicit functions in $\mathrm{P}$ with $C\ge 3n-o(n)$ in $B_2$ (Blum; later improvements to about $3.1n$ by Find--Golovnev--Hirsch--Kulikov and Li--Yu); nothing superlinear is known for any explicit function, and for NP-complete problems the best general circuit bounds are linear. Natural-proofs, relativization and algebrization barriers apply to any route via random-like functions.
