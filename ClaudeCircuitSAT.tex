\paragraph{Status.} \textbf{OPEN.} No superlinear lower bound for Circuit-SAT is claimed; P versus NP remains open. Labels: PROVED/EXACT, KNOWN THEOREM, COMPUTATIONAL, CONDITIONAL, CANDIDATE, OPEN, FALSIFIED/RETIRED.

\section{Goal and method}
Goal: lower-bound the size (gates; wires $=$ 2$\cdot$gates in the fan-in-2 basis $B_2$) of circuits deciding Circuit-SAT. Method: a ladder of instances, from the simplest circuit-SAT upward in number of inputs, tracking at each rung (i) the exact minimum size where computable, (ii) the best proved lower bound, (iii) the wire connections forced.

\section{Rung 0: the generic wire bound (PROVED/EXACT)}
\textbf{Lemma 1.} If $f:\{0,1\}^m\to\{0,1\}$ depends on all $m$ inputs, every $B_2$ circuit for $f$ has $\ge m-1$ gates and $\ge 2(m-1)$ wires.\\
\emph{Proof.} The underlying graph of the circuit must connect all $m$ inputs to the output; a tree with $m$ leaves and fan-in 2 has $m-1$ internal nodes. \qed

\section{Rung 1: one gate, two inputs (PROVED/EXACT)}
Instance: circuit $g=\mathrm{op}(x_1,x_2)$, $\mathrm{op}$ given as its 4-bit truth table $t_{00..11}$. Then $\mathrm{SAT}_1(t)=t_{00}\lor t_{01}\lor t_{10}\lor t_{11}$. It depends on 4 inputs, so $\ge 3$ gates ($\ge 6$ wires) by Lemma 1; the OR-tree achieves $3$. Hence $C(\mathrm{SAT}_1)=3$ exactly (COMPUTATIONAL check: all 15 nonzero tables satisfiable, essential variables $=4$).

\section{Rung 2: two gates, two inputs, fixed wiring (COMPUTATIONAL + Lemma 1)}
Topologies: $g_2=\mathrm{op}_2(g_1,x_1)$, $g_2=\mathrm{op}_2(g_1,x_2)$ with $g_1=\mathrm{op}_1(x_1,x_2)$; the ops are the $8$ input bits. Exhaustive evaluation: $\mathrm{SAT}_2$ is true on $220$ of $256$ inputs and depends on all $8$ bits, giving $C\ge 7$ gates, $\ge 14$ wires. Upper bound: OPEN here (not computed).

\section{Calibration: exact $B_2$ complexity of all 3-variable functions (COMPUTATIONAL)}
Exhaustive enumeration of circuits with $\le4$ gates over $x_1,x_2,x_3$ yields all $256$ functions. Number of functions of exact size $0,1,2,3,4$: $5,33,114,80,24$ (max size $4$). Provides ground truth for later SAT/exact-synthesis extensions.

\section{What is known, and the wall}
\begin{itemize}
\item KNOWN THEOREM (from literature, not re-verified this run): best explicit $B_2$ lower bounds are linear, about $3.1n-o(n)$ (Li--Yang, STOC 2022), after $5n-o(n)$ for the \emph{$U_2$} basis (Iwama--Lachish--Morizumi--Raz). Gate elimination has a known linear barrier.
\item The ladder above only reaches Lemma-1-type bounds, since fixed-topology Circuit-SAT is a tiny-function family. These bounds are linear in the \emph{description length}, which is not a lower bound on general Circuit-SAT for a circuit of size $s$ in terms of $n$.
\item The genuine target: lower bound $s(n)$ for the family $\mathrm{CSAT}_{n,s}$ (input: description of an $s$-gate circuit on $n$ wires). Description length $\Theta(s\log s)$; Lemma 1 gives only $\Omega(s\log s/\ \log)$-type linear bounds.
\end{itemize}
