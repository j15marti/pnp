\section*{Status}
\textbf{Unconditional superpolynomial lower bounds for Circuit-SAT, and $P\neq NP$, remain \OPEN.}
Labels: PROVED, KNOWN, COMPUTATIONAL, CONDITIONAL, OPEN, RETIRED. Nothing below claims more than its label.

\section{Method}
Circuit-SAT$(C)$: given a fan-in-2 circuit $C$ over $n$ inputs with $w$ wires, decide $\exists x\,C(x)=1$.
Ladder: for each $(n,g)$ ($g$ = gate count, $w=2g$ wires) record (i) the minimum number of gates/wires
needed to build each target function, (ii) the cheapest known and provable cost of deciding satisfiability.
Parameters: $n$ (inputs), $w$ (wires), $k$ (treewidth / cutwidth of $C$) --- a bound is \emph{parameterized}
if it has the form $T(n,w,k)$.

\section{Proved basics}
\begin{prop}[connectivity, PROVED]
A fan-in-2 circuit whose output depends on all $n$ inputs has $\ge n-1$ gates and $\ge 2(n-1)$ wires.
\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} The sub-DAG reaching the output must connect $n$ input nodes; each gate merges at most two
components, so $n-1$ merges are needed. \qed

\begin{prop}[query bound, PROVED, trivial]
Any algorithm that accesses $C$ only as a black-box evaluator needs $2^n$ queries on the unique-satisfying-input
family $C_a(x)=[x=a]$ (worst case over $a$). This is a black-box bound only; it says nothing about algorithms that read $C$.
\end{prop}
The white-box version (reading the $w$ wires) is exactly where the open problem lives.

\section{Repository cross-check (2026-10-28)}
The \texttt{j15marti/pnp} repo's root \texttt{main.tex} also states P vs NP \OPEN. Its proved items are restricted:
sequential-access two-way/low-crossing-cut theorems for easy-promise instances, explicitly not extending to indexed RAM
or ordinary SAT; the ARC/FLMV time--space contraction route is OPEN. Usable here: the cut/crossing viewpoint is the
same wire-congestion quantity tracked in this ladder (wires crossing a cut $\leftrightarrow$ the parameter $k$); transfer to
a Circuit-SAT bound would require a theorem for unrestricted algorithms and is \OPEN.
