\section{Goal and method}
Build Circuit-SAT bottom-up: fix fan-in-2 circuits over the full basis $B_2$ with $n$ inputs and $m$ gates (wires $=2m$, plus output). Track, per level $n=1,2,3,\dots$, the minimal gates/wires needed, and the resulting solving lower bound, as a parametrized function of $(n,m)$.

\section{Proved facts}
\begin{prop}[connectivity] A fan-in-2 circuit whose output depends on $n$ inputs has $m\ge n-1$ gates.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof.} The sub-DAG reaching the output must connect $n$ input nodes; a connected graph on $n+m'$ nodes needs $\ge n+m'-1$ edges, while fan-in 2 gives $\le 2m'$ edges, so $m'\ge n-1$. \qed

\begin{prop}[read lower bound, parametrized] Any algorithm deciding SAT for circuits with $n$ inputs and $m$ gates (given as a gate list) must examine $\Omega(m)$ gate descriptions.\end{prop}
\emph{Proof sketch.} Take $z_0=x_1\wedge\neg x_1$, $z_i=z_{i-1}\wedge x_2$, output $z_m$: unsatisfiable. Changing any single $z_i$ ($i\ge1$) to $z_{i-1}\vee x_2$ makes the output equal $x_2$ on suffix, hence satisfiable. An algorithm skipping gate $i$ errs on one of the two instances. \qed\ (PROVED; trivial, linear only.)

\section{Parametrized lower-bound picture}
\begin{itemize}
\item Unconditional: $\Omega(n+m)$ (Props.~1--2). KNOWN: no superlinear bound for general Circuit-SAT; best time--space results are Williams-style, no deterministic SAT algorithm runs in time $n^{c}$, $c<2\cos(\pi/7)\approx1.8019$, with $n^{o(1)}$ space (Williams, 2008), and Buss--Williams show alternation trading cannot exceed $2\cos(\pi/7)$.
\item Conditional: ETH/SETH give $2^{\Omega(n)}$-type bounds in $n$ for $m=\mathrm{poly}(n)$; the parameter is $n$ (FPT view: $2^{n}m$ is the brute-force upper bound).
\item CANDIDATE next step: count wire-sharing (fan-out) needed by a family with unique solution and track whether a ``sequential revelation'' argument forces $\omega(m)$ work as $n$ grows; $n=3$ data shows min size growing as $n-1$ for unique-SAT, i.e.\ no hardness yet at small scale.
\end{itemize}
