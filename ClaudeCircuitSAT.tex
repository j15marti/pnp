\section*{Status (strict labels)}
\textbf{P versus NP remains OPEN.} No unconditional super-linear lower bound for
Circuit-SAT on general models is claimed. Labels: KNOWN, PROVED, COMPUTATIONAL, CANDIDATE, OPEN.

\section{Goal and method}
Goal: a lower bound for Circuit-SAT, possibly parametrized, e.g.\ in $(n,m,k)$ =
(inputs, wires, structural parameter such as treewidth/depth).
Method: build circuits from the simplest case upward, tracking at each step the
minimum number of wires/gates and the number of inputs, and record what
a SAT-solver must at least read/decide.

\section{Step 0: trivial and parametrized facts}
\begin{prop}[KNOWN/PROVED] Any algorithm for Circuit-SAT on $m$-wire inputs needs $\Omega(m)$ time
(it must read the circuit; flipping one unread wire's gate changes satisfiability for suitable instances).
\end{prop}
\begin{prop}[KNOWN] Brute force gives $O(2^n m)$; under SETH no $O(2^{(1-\varepsilon)n}\mathrm{poly}(m))$ algorithm exists
(CONDITIONAL, not a lower bound). Parametrized: circuits of treewidth $k$ are solvable in $2^{O(k)}m$ time, so any
hardness must come from large treewidth/pathwidth.
\end{prop}

\section{Step 1: exact minimum circuit sizes, small $n$ (COMPUTATIONAL)}
Script: \texttt{research/agents/claude/circuitsat/mincirc.py}. Exhaustive search over all
straight-line programs with 2-input gates (all non-degenerate binary gate types). Counts of functions by
minimum gate count $C(f)$ (wires $=2C(f)$, fan-in 2). Convention: negated variables are not free (cost 2), so
rows for $C=2$ at $n=2$ are $\bar x,\bar y$; constants and variables cost 0.
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}\toprule
$n$ & $C=0$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4\\\midrule
2 & 4 & 10 & 2 & 0 & 0\\
3 & 5 & 30 & 117 & 80 & 24\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
Totals are $16$ and $256$ (checked). Max $C=4$ for $n=3$ agrees with the known value. Each new input raises the
worst-case minimum size; this is the data layer for the wire-tracking ladder, not a lower bound on SAT.

\section{Ladder plan (to be built hourly)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item $n=4$: exact $C(f)$ via SAT-based synthesis (chunked, $\le$30\,s per run); tabulate by class.
\item Wire-count ladder for structured families (AND, XOR, MAJ, parity chains): minimal wires per added input.
\item Gate-elimination for the \emph{verification} side: KNOWN $\ge 3n-o(n)$ gates for explicit functions
 (Blum; Find--Golovnev--Hirsch--Kulikov $3.011n$; Li--Yang $3.1n-o(n)$). Transfer to SAT \emph{solving} time needs a new theorem (OPEN).
\item Parametrized target (CANDIDATE): bound for restricted algorithm classes (DPLL/resolution) in terms of
 $(n,m,\mathrm{tw})$, e.g.\ resolution size $2^{\Omega(\mathrm{tw})}$-type results, as honest unconditional baselines.
\end{enumerate}
